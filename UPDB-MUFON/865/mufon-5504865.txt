{cak} saw bright red and bluish green lights, in a triangle changing flight patterns. it stopped and did a 180` direction change several times then it descended out of view

At around 9:15 we were just checking on our dogs and locking up the gate for the night, when I was closing the gate, I looked towards the south sky and noticed 2 bright lights. One red and the other bluish green,I thought it was a helicopter or plane so I checked to make sure that I had latched the gate and when, I looked back at where the object was, I noticed that there were no blinking lights, as there normally are on planes and such. All of a sudden, it turned to the west in a very fluid motion so I called my husband who had the binoculars and told him to hurry. I was trying to record it with my phones camera but he got a much better view with the binoculars. What he saw through them was what he described as a rack of "billiard balls". With the bottom right "ball" being red and the rest being bluish green then more reds appeared along the bottom. And then as it started to descend, it changed into 2 "balls" of light, one being red and the other one blue. It moved very irratically but very fluidly at the same time. From side to side, up then down and then it just finally descended out of view. I did get some video from my phone but it isn't very good. All you see is a pulsating light and a lot of talking.
