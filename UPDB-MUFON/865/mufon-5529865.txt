HUGE, black triangle moving very slowly from west to east in broad daylight.

I am only doing this because I cannot fathom why no one else has. HUGE, BLACK, isosceles triangle with the nose tipped up a bit was moving a snails pace above a cloud during rush hour traffic. It was coming from the West moving East, casting a shadow over the cloud, occasionally peeking out. This object was literally blocking out the sun. Rays of sunshine were highlighting the edges of the craft, it was so big I cannot imagine I was the only person to have noticed it, I went into the bank... came out it had only moved a little but was much less visible. There were also 3 helicopters moving in and out of the cloud.
