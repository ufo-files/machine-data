In France in the army.i was driveling down highway then I woke up and was still driveling but there was a very large craft hovering over my car shrouded in mist my first thought was I was in a toll booth area. Just when I knew what it was it went straight up and in two seconds it was the size of a pea that1s when I lost sight of it. When it was hovering it was lit up all over with single multi colored lights. Forty five years later my ear became inflamed on the lobe I ask my wife to look at it. She said there was something in it and pulled out two approx one inch long round objects slightly larger than a wooden pencil lead dark green in color with a black stripe around it like a barbers pole. The first thing I said was throw them away. Why I don,t know it was like I was programmed to discard them. The objects were straight and I never felt them or had any idea they were there.
