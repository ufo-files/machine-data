Saw a bright orange (star-like) object flying through the sky (it was traveling slowly) flew in a straight direction and then flew upwards and disappeared . There weren't any lights blinking like a plane and it didn't make any sound.

While driving back from work. I happen to look up at the sky ( it was a very clear night and there weren't too many stars out). I saw a bright orange star like object in the sky , it was moving quite slowly (it appeared to be flying at the speed of a helicopter). First I thought it was just a plane but there weren't any lights blinking and it also didn't make a single sound.
I was driving for about a minute and realised it could be something else. so I pulled up on the side of the road and noticed someone else looking at it too. We both stood there are watched it fly sideways and then ascend upwards and slowly disappear in the sky. 

It just looked like a bright orange object.. it didn't loose its intensity, it was bright and then suddenly disappear.
