{nd} strang cigar shaped objects,2, not much sound at all moving very slowly no wings very shiny running together

We were on the back deck and I looked up and seen two (2) crafts of some kind fly in side by side at the same angle going the same direction but the crafts had no wings. They looked kind of like a rockets, with fire coming out the back, but they were not going up at all....flying parallel to the ground staying side by side. Little to no noise at all. The speed made me think of the Goodyear blimp.
