{nd} A black triangular mass with three lights

We were headed to a poker game going east on Brewer Drive on the night of January 29th, when I noticed three very bright lights in the sky. At first we thought it was a blimp because it was moving slow and it was the NHL Allstar weekend downtown. As we got closer to Nolensville Pike, I knew it wasn't a blimp. It definitely wasn't a plane, it was moving too slow. we turned south on Nolensville Pike toward Old Hickory Blvd. I told my buddy to stop the car and we got out to see it better, there was no noise whatsoever. It was triangle in shape and the lights were pretty bright. It seemed to hover for a minute and then started moving very slow to the north. Now this is a busy area of Nashville, so I was very surprised to see an object like this in such a populated area. We lost sight of it as we were driving on. I'm a huge airplane guy, and can tell you right now it wasn't a plane or a helicopter. I don't know what it was, but the experience was pretty amazing!!!
