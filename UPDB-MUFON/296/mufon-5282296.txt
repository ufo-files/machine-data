Triangular. Stationary. Lights noted, but not super bright. Like TR3B Astra.

While traveling on I-185 towards Greenville one early morning around 0200 in approximately 2017, I witnessed a large triangular object hovering above the roadside near the Donaldson Center. The object was dark with lights, although not super bright. I immediately believed the object a UFO. The object was motionless and hovered. I believed I was crazy in seeing this, but my work partner also confirmed seeing the object and verifies the details I noted. Now, years later, I believe the object was consistent with the TR3B Astra. Visual was lost after driving past.
