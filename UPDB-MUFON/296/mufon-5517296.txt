(gs) UFO spotted July 8, 2012 in Michigan

I live in Eastpointe Michigan and I went outside at 3:34am July 8th, 2012 and saw what i thought was a satellite as it didn't have any flashing lights and moving quicker than a plane. It become the brightest thing in the sky next to the moon. But then as quick as it got bright it started getting dim again. Then it just disappeared in front of me.
