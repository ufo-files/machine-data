{nd} Solid Black Triangle

I didn't smoke in my house so I went outside to have a cigarette late one night. I was looking towards the city when I saw a light moving from the North East to the East over it. I thought it was a plane at first but something was off about it. So I stood there and watched as I smoked. This is where the story gets strange, it was like it knew I was watching it. It stopped moving to the East and turned West to come towards me. At first I was happy thinking I am going to at least see what it is. It slowed to a crawl as it got closer to me and when it was directly above me it stopped and just hovered there. It was so huge it blocked out the star light and all I could see was this huge black triangular shape hovering above me. It made no sound at all, It did not displace the air, nothing just absolute quite. It was solid black and from below it I could not see anything identifying about it, no lights or markings, just black. It hovered there for I don't know how long. Then it just disappeared, I was left standing alone on my back porch holding a cigarette butt. I don't know how I lost sight of it, I don't remember, just next thing I knew it was gone. I didn't say anything cause part of me thought this has to be some kind of secret military plane and some crew member thought it would be funny to mess with my head. But I can't forget it, god knows I have tired.
