{dr}Starlike glowing orb. Mostly white with a rotating mix of red, blue, and green. Completely stationary and above cloud level.

I was home sitting in my chair. I leaned back and looked out the window at the stars. One star I noticed seemed to be pulsing more than any of the others. Given that I started seeing some red and green colors I thought it was a plane. However I looked at it for 2 minutes and it did not move what-so-ever. The orb was completely stationary, however the light revolved around itself and changed colors in a quick and fluid way. A cloud bank moved in and covered up the object after a few minutes and I lost sight of it.
