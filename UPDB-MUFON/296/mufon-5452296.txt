Shape shifting satelite

I was watching the sky for satellites when a prominent light came from the eastern horizon at a good speed. But as it made it's way directly above me it slowed to a stop. I had a small but high powered telescope that fit on my phone. I was able to take a handful of pictures. It sat above me for at least an hour. Then slowly moved to the west
