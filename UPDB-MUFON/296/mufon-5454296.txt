{tjd} A red light, large enough to be comparable to the star in the the background, raced across the sky in a straight path then abruptly changed direction

A solid red object raced across the sky in a straight path. Large enough to be comparable in size to a bright star that was higher in elevation, but in the background of the night sky. It continued in a straight horizontal path until it was almost under the star with zero sound and then stopped and then shot up towards the star in a vertical movement and then disappeared completely! Attached are two videos from two different phones at the same location at the same time. Unfortunately Our videos ended before it made the directional change and disappeared. The whole sighting was about two minutes long for us. Very exciting!
