ufo sighting witnessed by several

we had just turned right off Leeville Pike onto Hwy 109, headed up to the 1st traffic light to turn left. Was sitting at light waiting when we,noticed something in the sky to our right make a sudden movement, like a sharp directional movement. There was 3 of us in our truck, it was going in front of us when all the sudden it took off faster than Ive ever seen anything move. There was also a car in the lane next to us, a man & a woman, when I looked at them, (my husband who was driving & our 21 yr. old daughter, saw them looking too) they were watching it too, still sitting in the lane next to us and the light had already turned green for them! It was going SO fast that in just a second, it went into a cloud.
