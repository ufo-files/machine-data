On May 27,2016 at 0025hrs: I was in my bed watching TV when I heard my dog starting to start growling. As I got up to look out my window I saw green lights in the sky traveling towards me. Its speed was approximately 60 mph. IT appeared to be approximately 1000 ft high. As it got closer I could see the objects shape was cylindrical. There were numerous green lights around the perimeter of the front of the craft that were pulsating like a movie marquee. Also the front of the craft was inverted with visible ribbed supports. The rear had red lights. It traveled from west to east.
