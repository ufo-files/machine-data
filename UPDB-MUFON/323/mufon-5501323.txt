{nd} Orange-ish oval or sphere traveling NW at a constant speed.

We were on the deck talking and watching the fireworks in the neighborhood. We observed a single orange-ish oval or sphere traveling at a constant speed. We initially noticed it to the West Southwest of our house and it was traveling Northwest. I thought it might be an airliner on approach to MCI but it made no noise at all and it was much lower. It was glowing orange, not landing lights or point source of light and no red/green wing-tip lights or strobes. As it progressed to the NW it did not vary in size or intensity. This was observed by 4 adults for approximately 2 minutes. The time was 11:10pm. The night was clear and calm.
