{td} Flashing lights, 3 different objects, erratic flight pattern

Standing on my back porch smoking as I do before I go to bed & talking to my husband on the phone when I looked in the sky and noticed a red, green, and blue flashing objects through the tree line opening way out in the sky. I talked to my husband and explained what I saw & after remaining stationary for a few minutes it then moved erratically through the sky. It moved in almost a circular pattern. The flashing then faded into the distance until I couldn1t see it anymore. After about 10 minutes, I assumed maybe it was a drone or something. Then I continue looking and another TWO appeared in the distance and remained stationary for about 10 minutes before stopping abruptly and then they were gone. 10 minutes after those disappeared I could see a plane flying very very fast in the sky near where I spotted those lights. Then the original flashing object popped up and went erratically in a circular motion again before ascending into the sky and again stopping abruptly. I am honestly astounded. We immediately looked online to see if I was crazy for seeing these things and we actually watched a news clip from a news station a few towns over (blue springs, Missouri is where that story was covered a few months ago but I hadn1t previously heard of this incident until now) where the lights they described were almost identical to what I saw. After about an hour I looked out again and nothing is there anymore and I can1t see anything out of the ordinary. Very very strange for someone who has never seen anything like this before. I heard nothing in the sky, except when the one airplane flew quickly across which was basically a jet sound. Definitely odd.
