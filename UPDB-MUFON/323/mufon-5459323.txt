{cak} I witnessed an orb floating along the second story windows of the houses on the opposite side of our street

What I remember is that it was a ungodly hot and humid summer night and I couldn't sleep because of this. The bathroom was at the front of the house and the bathroom window was open. I got a drink of water and from my peripheral vision saw something. I went to the window and saw the orb hovering at the second story level of the houses on the other side of the street. The street was on a hill, and all the houses were attached townhouses or what were called row homes at the time. The orb slowly moved following the second story windows going downhill staying level with the windows. All the houses on that street had the same floor plan so in the front of the second story of the house was the master bedroom, a hall, and the bathroom. The orb itself was very bright, round, and made no sound, and it struck me at the time that it was too small to be a ship, but rather some kind of surveillance device. I really thought that it was a " UFO peeping tom." I watched for some minutes and then went back to bed. I told no one because I was 12 years old at the time and knew no one would believe me. I was well into adulthood when I finally told a therapist who believed that I had at least saw "something." Only a few close family members know my story, and this it the first time I have shared this in writing or on the Internet. Please treat this with respect.
