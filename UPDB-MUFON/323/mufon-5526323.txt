Very strange bright flashing chrome object

While sitting in a parking lot I noticed a flashing/reflective object at about 10,000 feet making a slight arc as it was falling-flying towards the ground. As it approached a treeline about 3 miles away it leveled off and flew above the trees at about 50-100 feet for about 1/4 mile. It then stopped, flew backwards for maybe a few 100 yards. Then continued forward out of site as it flew above the treeline going east near Franktown area. About 5-10 seconds later I could see it back at about 10000 feet or so about maybe 5-8 miles away and watched it go out of site towards the East. It was very bright, looked like something that was chrome plated, possibly triangle shaped, spinning and reflecting the sunlight. It was flickering-flashing at a consistent rate of speed. It was a perfectly clear sunny day with light winds. I have no idea what it was. It was not a plane, helicopter, balloon, nor remote control plane.
