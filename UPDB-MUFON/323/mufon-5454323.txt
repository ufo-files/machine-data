Straight line of lights that couldnt be picked up on camera

Me and my girlfriend saw a straight row of 10 moving lights evenly spaced traveling at the same speed (fast) across the sky. After the first 10 anothe 10 came with the 11th trailing behind. It caught up to the last one information passed it a little bit and then dropped back to the end of the line again showing the same distance as the others. After that group of 11 past we saw another group of 10 and shortly after another group of 11. We rang my mother in law to see if she could see them and she agreed she could. I then rang my brother and his girlfriend on face time and they 2 could just about see the odd one on the phone. I rang my mum and dad who only live local and they too saw them but not in formation. I then rang my friend and he too saw them in formation. This went on for round 1 hour.
