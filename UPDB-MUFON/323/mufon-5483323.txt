{jpg} Driving west on hwy 212 noticed the stars

On May 25 around 10:55 PM I was driving west on highway 212 when I noticed that the stars were out despite the clouds and lightning to the south, west, and north of me. To the west northwest of me I noticed two bright stars then one became very bright and flashed and seem to to turn at a 901 angle and head straight north. The other star remained the same and did not change brightness or direction. The light that got brighter and change directions disappeared as soon as it headed north.
