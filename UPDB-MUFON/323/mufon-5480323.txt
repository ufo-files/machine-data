{cak} A very bright, soundless, multi-colored triangle

I was actually out on a date that night. We had driven up the seawall on Horsefall Beach to the North Jetty before it started to get dark. We started to drive from the North Jetty and were chatting with a huge, soundless, multicolored triangle passed right over us. My date did not see it until I had turned around to my left and yelled at him to see it. He missed it because he was driving. It freaked me out because we are right across from an airport and this wasn't an airplane. We have huge United prop planes that come in and they are very loud! This triangle had come from the direction of the ocean, over us and toward the sand dunes. I was shook up about it, but my date kept driving and totally forgot about it. Later as we left the beach and toward the sand dunes we lost our electricity more than once. It was very dark that night so I was pretty sure something was up. I know this has taken me awhile to report. But it is interesting to note that this object was bright, multi-colored, quiet and came from the ocean. Also, that it was across from the North Bend airport. I know that the traffic control tower reports things like this as 'targets' I was tempted to call, but I get enough eye rolls when you talk about UFO's. Seen more than one I can't explain and I believe em! The picture submitted is the one taken before we left the sea wall down to the beach. Just before it showed up.
