{nd} line of glowing balls single file following-the-leader traveling slowly from west to east

Arrived home and exited my vehicle. I glanced up at the stars and was shocked to see several very bright ones off to the west. As I looked at them I realized they were moving in my direction. At first they were just above the horizon, but in about 30 seconds or so, they got close enough to me that I grabbed my phone and tried to video them. The quality of the picture is very poor, and does not appear nearly as bright as they were to the naked eye. They did not blink like aircraft but were a steady amber light. They seemed to follow single file slightly back and forth like follow-the-leader. As they got closer, I ran into the house and woke my wife, telling her there was a "flock of UFO's outside. She ran outside with me and we watched as they flew nearly overhead and moved off the the east-north-east direction. We heard a plane noise getting louder and turned to the south spotting a plane approaching. As it got louder and closer it had lights out on its wingtips and flashing belly lights and was moving in the direction the balls had exited from. It appeared to be twice there size but at about the same altitude. I would say three to five thousand feet up, but far less than say twenty thousand feet like an airliner. These were much lower than an airliner.
