Only star in the sky making its way West.

My brother and I were a few miles from town driving home at about 7:00-7:30am. The sky was totally black not even the moon could be seen. Then above the car was something glowing. It was to low to be a star. I couldn't think what it was other than a UFO. It was glowing brightly and moving West slowly as if observing the area. I decided not to worry about it as we weren't bothering it. We lost sight of the object when it dissapeared into the sky.
