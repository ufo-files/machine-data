{jpg} UFO sighting south of Jordan Valley off Hwy 95

We saw 3 bright orangish yellow lights in the sky. One appeared, then the next directly under it & then the next directly under it. All three were the same exact size & shape, like a triangle shape with rounded edges & large in size. Much larger than the surrounding stars. First my husband saw bright lights in the sky & was literally speechless as he pointed in the direction they were saying he saw something in the sky. I looked out my passenger window in the direction he was pointing and then it happened again, shortly after he saw it the first time. They stayed lit while each one appeared & then disappeared all at once together like a cloaking device covered them up. They never moved, just hovered high in the sky. Much larger than an airplane. We pulled over to continue watching but they never appeared again.
