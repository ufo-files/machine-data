Silver disc shape, with windows and lights flashing

In 1968 I lived in Grand Junction Co, on the [exact location details have been moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] I was 13 my brother 17 we were going to walk to the local book store on 7th and Main Street to buy a six pack of soda, the store was about two blocks away from where we lived we had just walked out of our home as we were crossing the street we heard a whistling type of sound that was coming from above us it was around dusk I would say around seven or eight in the evening still could see very well it was in the summer time so the days were long, as we both looked up my brother said to me do you see what I see and I said yes, then he said “it’s a flying saucer” !! and he grabbed my hand, and we just stood there looking at it in amazement. When we looked up at it the whistling sound stopped, It must have been around 5 minutes at the most that it stood there hovering I remember it was disc shaped, silver in color very shiny and had windows and you could see light coming from them, I remember trying to see if I could see anyone looking out but I didn’t see anyone, I remember it had lights on the under part that were flashing in a circular motion 
When it took off it flew southward it was gone in a flash when it left, I will never forget that experience for as long as I live
We wanted to report it but because we didn’t want to be ridiculed we decided not to report it, my brother said that. no one would believe us anyway, so we never did report it all these years until now the only ones who ever heard about what we saw that day was close friends and relatives.
