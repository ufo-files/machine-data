{dr}During and after Fireworks Display NON Standard Aircraft lights were brought to my attention. Def Triangle Shaped. Made that determination after Object turned.

During the Fireworks display at Bush gardens in williamsburg VA. I witnessed what appeared to be a triangular shaped object over the fireworks display. Object had a series of sequential flashing Lights along what appeared to be one of its edges. Def not Standard Aircraft strobe style flashing lights. These were larger light sources. Object appeared on one side of the Fireworks display, behind and above. Moved from Right to left. Once Display ended. Object Made a very sharp turn and went in a reverse direction. This is went I made the determination that it was triangular. No Sound was emitted from it. Object headed way from the area and disappeared behind the distant trees.
