I was waiting for Even to take him to work at the bakery. I was in my car facing west looking at the sunset. It was light out side. Right in my field of vision, there was a sudden appearance of the bright ball streaking down with things breaking off of it and the things went out and the main body just went to the ground. I though it was a crashing plane. It was in front of the setting sun and it was still very bright. The object went down below the horizon.
