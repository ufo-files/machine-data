{jpg} Hard to describe. It was kind of gray/silvery, but changed shape and color. It was fuzzy to look at.

My husband and I were in our back yard. We were just relaxing when I noticed something in the sky. I pointed it out to my husband, but he couldn't see it. I found this incredibly odd. 

As I looked at it, it seemed like a metallic balloon at first, but then I realized it wasn't moving with the wind and wasn't changing altitude. It simply stayed in the same place. The more I tried to focus on the object, the more it seemed fuzzy. The object seemed to change shape as time passed, changing from a sphere, to 6 ovals attached in the center, then back to a sphere. As it changed shape, there seemed to be a colored reflection from the edges - seemed violet/blue. 

Both my husband and I are former military and trained in air threat identification. I've seen many things in the sky, but nothing like that before, and nothing like that since this event.
