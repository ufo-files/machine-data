{nd} 10 objects flew East to West, then swift vertical ascent until no longer visable.

I was walking my dog on a dark street, Sunday evening May 19, 2013 near Columbus, OH. A bright orange/yellow object caught my eye, in the sky to the East. I like watching planes so I kept looking at it. In next 30 seconds 5more orange/yellow objects appeared, moving in the same direction, EAST to WEST, as the first object. The last 2objects were side by side instead of in a straight line. They all seemed to be pretty evenly spaced. I watched as they moved through the sky and I tried very hard to hear anything, something that would tell me that these were planes, but I heard nothing. They seemed pretty low in the sky as well as the appearance was larger than most planes I see fly overhead. In another 30 seconds my gaze had followed the six objects West, I looked East again and saw 4more objects, same size, same color, same flight pattern as the first 6, moving across the sky. I was completely fascinated and then quickly thought, pictures, I need pictures. I snapped a few high def pictures with my iPhone. I was only able to capture 5 of the first group of 6 and then the final group of 4 and a short video. After I stopped the video I looked to the first group again and saw the lights ascend, swiftly, and then completely dissappear. I shifted to the last 4 and watched them more closely and they did the same thing. While I was taking pictures and especially during the video I became self conscious about any light I was shining on myself and briefly thought, "I should get inside". I did go inside and have been reluctant to say anything because Im pretty sure people will think Im a little crazy but I know what I saw and what I didn't hear.
