{dr}White orb ascending from clouds to seaplane

I was at an airshow and I was recording a Martin Mars Water Bomber with my iPhone6. The plane flew over the airfield and then back out over a nearby lake. While I recorded the video I did not observe anything out of the ordinary. Later that evening as I was reviewing what I recorded throughout the day I noticed in this video a white orb falling from the clouds above the plane. It appears the orb falls to the plane and not past it. My first thought was a possible daytime meteor, but now after watching the video 100+ times I'm now calling it a fallen angel. 
Watch closely at the 35 sec. mark.
