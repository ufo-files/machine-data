Green Fireball across night sky

I was working overnight at the bar in Brooklyn. A friend was stopping in, as we were driving the next day to a wedding out of state. It was around 3:30AM, and he was having a smoke and I was sitting on the front steps, looking out over the park. Just then a MASSIVE green fireball streaked across the sky. I have seen lots of shooting stars before, they have always been quick zips across the sky that leave thin white streaks behind them. This was much much larger, with a much thicker tail. I would guess that if I hold my fingers out at arms length and measure across, the streak was roughly 5 inches long, and as I said, just wider across than a normal shooting star. And the trail in the atmosphere was distinctly green, sort of an emerald green glow. I tried to point it out to my friend, but by the time he craned around to look, the trail had faded. The next day I looked on gothamist and gawker and google, all in hopes of finding anyone reporting the same event, as when something happens in NYC, it's often seen by a lot of people. All I saw were a series of reports on green fireballs in the southwest states that same night.
