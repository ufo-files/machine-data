{jpg} 4 Bright lights in a line

I was driving east down route 724 and as I pulled up to a red light at the Bethel Church Road intersection, I looked out my driver's side window and saw 4 bright white lights in the sky. They were in a straight line, but the line itself was at an angle, the line was at an almost diagonal angle, with the lower end being on the right hand side from my viewpoint. The traffic light turned green almost as soon as I saw the lights in the sky and I could not safely look out the window again. By the time I could-- maybe 30 seconds later, I no longer saw the object. I first thought the object was a low flying plane as there is a small airport nearby, but none of the lights were blinking and it was flying at a diagonal angle as opposed to horizontally, like most planes. The two passengers in the car (my parents) did not see the object but suggested it was a reflection in the window of something, but my window was down. They then suggested it was the lights from the nearby nuclear power plant, but those lights are blinking and were farther away than the lights I saw. I did not hear any noise associated with the lights. The lights seemed to fly similarly to an airplane but I did not get the best look. The lights were flying above the treetops, but not very far above. When I got home, I saw my boyfriend had texted me about the same lights and he happened to be in the same area at the same time I saw the lights. We both confirmed that we saw them at 8:43pm. He also said that he saw a different light in the sky get brighter and brighter and then it disappeared in the same area at about 8:45pm.
