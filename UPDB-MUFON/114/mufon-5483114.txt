{nd} Brilliant light. Steady low. High speed. Sped to horizon. Appeared overhead less than 5 minutes later.

Sitting on a deck viewing the ocean in Buxton NC with girlfriend. Chrystal clear night, before the moon rose. very clear. Was the brightest object in the sky, with the next brightest object being Mars. No strobing, blinking or sound presented.
