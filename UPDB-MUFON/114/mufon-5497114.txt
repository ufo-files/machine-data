Giant iridescent green dome at Holloman Air Force Base NM

My father was stationed at Holloman Air Force base from 1974 to 1977 where he worked as a tech sergeant in a building directly off the flight line. One night during an intense thunderstorm, typical of the monsoon season my brother David and me were watching the lightning. All of a sudden a GIGANTIC emerald green dome appeared where the flight line and desert beyond would be. Our jaws dropped and we faced each other with complete disbelief on our faces. The half dome had to have been over a thousand feet tall. How the semi circle was possible by all the Physics we understood struck me even then at the age of 12. I was a great fan of Star Trek but this was real, actually happening. It must have been well over 1000 feet high. To accurately show you what it looked like please look at this red half dome over Atlanta, Georgia and imagine it a brilliant emerald green against a very dark sky. https://pbs.twimg.com/media/BpaYCgNIAAAzRTG.jpg:large It lasted about a minute then disappeared. Whatever was going on that night at Holloman Air Force base is a mystery to me. We did not tell our father because he was good at ridiculing stories of ours, just something that was a reality in our family. What we saw that night cannot be explained, how can light be contained within a semi circle? It was not a rainbow, it was 8:30 pm in a thunderstorm. I feel fortunate to have seen such a bizarre sight. Thank you.
