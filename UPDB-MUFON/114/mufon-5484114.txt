{nd} Pulsating light in the sky, stationary at first then slowly drifting towards the east. Lost behind clouds.

I live in Queens, NY. On April 8, 2016 at about 10:30 PM, I was closing the bedroom window getting ready for bed, looked out the window and noticed a bright pulsating light in the sky. At first I thought it was a star then I realized this was first blinking (non-airplane type blinking) and pulsating more and more. I grabbed by video camera to record and each time I zoomed in, object seemed to be omitting different types of lights, almost looked like beams coming from it. At one point, looked like shooting beams coming from it then after a while, started to drift behind clouds. I lost it for a minute then regained observation. Recorded about 20 minutes of this object then lost it behind clouds again. I never regained visual of the object. It DEFINITELY was not a plane, not the moon, not a drone or helicopter. I showed it to my girlfriend who doesn't believe in any of these things and she was freaked out by this video.
