Saw a massive black sphere following behind an A380 commercial airliner

I went outside the front of the house to look in the mail box in the afternoon, and noticed a large A380 commercial airliner flying low above the suburb following the flight landing path to the east. Following behind its tail was a massive black sphere traveling at the same speed as the airplane. The object would of been at least 15 meters across, and was probably at least 10 meters behind the A380.
After it went out of view behind some trees, I looked across the street to the neighbours house noticed that they were looking at it as well.

The sphere was dark black with a slight shine on its surface, with no other features on it.
