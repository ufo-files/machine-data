Witnessed a triangular shaped craft. Completely silent, did not lose altitude.

At exactly 7: 20 pm. We witnessed a small craft moving very slowly towards us. Maybe 400 yards above us. It had 3 white slightly yellow lights. 2 in the back one in front. Looked like a triangle. Had a flashing red in the middle. Since it made no sound I thought maybe it was gliding. I ran thru the apt to the north side of the building. It was still silent and did not lose any elevation. I have lived near an air force base most my life. I have seen a lot of planes, jets, drones and helicopters. Anything moving that slow would lose altitude very quickly. Looked like maybe 45 mph. I’ve seen predator drones within a few 110 fr of me and they made noise. A lot quieter than maybe a sesna but still made noise. I’ve seen fast lights in the past. Nothing ever that close. It’s 9:08 and I’m still shaken up a lil. Thank you for your time. [removed name/cms/fgc].
