{jpg} Cigar shaped silver craft moving

I saw as I was driving a silver cigar shaped UFO. It was moving the same direction I was driving and was headed as I was North. I first went through a check list in my head, like, must be a plane looked and found no wings, no lights visible, craft appeared smooth and metallic in color. It moved fast, this was seen over a snow covered open field. I tried while driving to open my phone and fumbled with it quite a bit coz I was excited over what I was seeing. By the time I got the phone set up I looked for it but it was gone. So I have no pictures to show what I saw, but I saw it and it was nothing I have ever seen or like the normal sky crafts we usually see.
