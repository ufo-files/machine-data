We took one of my horses for training to Toomsuba, MS, in February 2012. While being evaluated in a large open arena, during completely clear and sunny weather, I took photos of the horse moving in the arena, which we looked at that evening. Two of the photos showed a strange object high up in the sky (above the head of the horse), which seemed to suddenly appear and move with incredible speed across the horizon, only to disappear after the camera took the next photo. This object moved so fast that we did not see it with the naked eye, only on the photos later on. The camera used takes 30 frames a second and that is why the two photos showed the object. 
The attached photos show the sequence of events, demonstrated also by the swishing tail of the horse.
