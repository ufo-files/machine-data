Craft hovered and zipped away.

I was walking home from a friends house through a field.I was on my way back home to go to bed.When I had seen a violet colored glow in the corner of my eye and decided to look upwards.Sense I live near a-lot of military bases I just assumed it was another black project.But the way it was hovering and the length it hovered I knew it wasn't a black project.I stood in the field transfixed for at-least 1 hour 10 minutes just looking.I felt like it was scanning me or something to that effect.I had some sort of paralysis that made me unnerved like I couldn't move even if I tried.Then after I was able to regain my constitution and be able to move.The violet glow intensified then it was as if it went from stationary to at-least mach-4 if not faster.When I got home it took me at-least a week to finally be able to sleep.To this day I still have nightmares from this event.
