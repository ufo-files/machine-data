{nd} STRANGE MORNING LIGHT

THOUGH MOVING VERY SLOWLY, NOTICED AN ODD "CLOUD" OR SOMETHING IN THE EARLY MORNING SKY (4 AUGUST). ASSUMED IT WAS A JET. LOOKED A FEW SECONDS LATER AND IT DIDN'T SEEM TO MOVE VERY FAR.

THIS STREEK OR CLOUD TOOK MY EYE BECAUSE OF IT'S COLOR AND THE FACT THAT IT SEEMED TO MOVE SO SLOWLY. BEING SO HIGH UP I ASSUMED IT WAS A PLANE YET THERE WAS JUST SOMETHING ODD ABOUT IT.

FINALLY GOT MY CAMERA AND TOOK A FEW SHOTS FROM A WINDOW. LAUHED INITIALLY AS I THOUGHT IT WAS JUST A PLANE. PUT THE THE SHOTS ON MY COMPUTER AND FOUND WHAT WAS NO PLANE BUT SOMETHING ELSE ENTIRELY.

HAVE INCLUDED A FEW PICTURES. WHATEVER THIS WAS, IT LOOKS HUMAN-MADE BUT WOULD VERY MUCH LIKE TO KNOW WHAT IT IS. I ENLARGED THE ONE SHOT AS I'M SURE YOU HAVE DETERMINED.
