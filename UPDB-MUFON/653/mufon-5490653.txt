{nd} Object chasing the ISS

I was watching the ISS make it's way across the sky. Another bright object came into view at a different trajectory but much faster (at least 3X) but on an intersecting path to the ISS. It was apparent it was at a slightly higher altitude. At the point where it appeared it was going to intercept the ISS it stopped short and disappeared. It reappeared seconds later and moved directly away, the light gradually dimming until non-visible.
