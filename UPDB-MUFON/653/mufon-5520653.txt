3 orange-red orbs

I saw three orange-red glowing orbs, about a half mile off on the Eastern horizon.. maybe 500 feet up. They were moving in patterns around eachother sort of like when you are mixing cups around on the table with a ball onder one of them.. Kind of a figure eight pattern with three orbs. They would also move and then stop and hover for a while in other patterns. This lasted for only a few minutes and then they faded away. They did not fly off... they just dimmed out and were gone. I had a couple people with me when I saw this, but at this time none of us had a camera.
