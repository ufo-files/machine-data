{kt}I was in 2nd grade. Saw this object while on school ground at recess. Appeared out of the clouds, hovered, was spinning, disappeared back into clouds after about 30 seconds

I was at recess on our school ground when I noticed this object come out of the clouds. It was a partly cloudy day, the sun was shining when I saw it. 4 others in my class also saw it but I can't remember who they were. I thought at first it was the moon, because it had a patchwork of dark areas just like the moon. But this round object was spinning as it came out of a cloud, hovered for about 30 seconds then receded back into the cloud. I realize I was young when I sighted this thing but it left quite an impression. I reported it to my teacher and was pretty much blown off.
