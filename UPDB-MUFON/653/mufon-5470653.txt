{jpg} 2nd Sighting of Pulsating Red Orb

I went onto the patio to have a cigarette and looked into the sky. I immediately noticed a large red orb hovering and pulsating a white light. The white light was as if flashing in a pattern, yet would also randomly start flashing very fast, then returning to a pattern and repeat the cycle. What has my attention is that I saw this exact object doing this exact thing in the exact colors and location just the other morning a few hours before the 5.8 mag earthquake up north! Just like before, I watched it for about two minutes before it faded out and disappeared. I tried getting video and pictures the other day, but couldn't get my phone to focus on it. I felt safe the whole time as if it were friends here to protect and maybe warn me of something still unknown. However, I can definitely draw pictures of it.
