1 Blue sphere splits into 2 orange/red spheres.

My wife and I got home from a friends house about 2 in the morning and we went out onto the back porch and it wasn't but a few seconds later, I looked up and over the trees there was this blue ball of light flying west and I told my wife to look and then as she saw the blue light, it started splitting into 2 balls and almost instantly turned and orange / red color as it kept the same speed and kept going across the sky til it was out of visual range. it never made any noise or anything. I will never forget it.
