{jpg} 2 clouds in a cloudless sky, clouds upside down mushroom then both changed shaped at the same time to almost identical shapes. Looked unnatural photos showed dark spots above clouds.

On beach around 330 pm. No clouds in the sky. Saw boats on horizon and then looked up and saw 2 strange shaped clouds that looked like an upside down mushroom with a circle in the mushroom head. Seemed unnatural for a cloud. Then both small clouds changed shape at the same time. Very strange. Shape changed to a more natural small cloud shape. Again no clouds anywhere in the sky except these 2. Took photo and didn't think anymore about it until I got home and looked at the photo and zoomed in. Saw black dots over both clouds and some around the cloud.
