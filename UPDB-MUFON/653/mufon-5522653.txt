White smokey rectangle shaped orbs at tree level

Between 8 pm and 8:40 PM while outside a friends house in Baden, PA. observed 5 low flying (tree level) White smokey rectangle shaped orbs. The orbs were flying in a circular pattern while blinking on and off. Every now and again they would leave their usual flight pattern and circle by themselves. After 40 minutes, A helicopter shining a spotlight appeared over the ridge and the objects disappeared (Blinked out). This event was witnessed by at least 5 people.
