Bright star-like light, stood there for over 20mins

About 10:30 on Aug 9th I sat on my bed to watch TV. I looked outside and saw what I first thought was a star. It wasn't high up in the sky it was more like along the horizon. A few minutes later I saw some airplanes pass by, I believe they were below this star (I'm not 100% sure). Planes flying by is normal as I see them every 30mins or so. Then I noticed that this star was very bright unlike I saw before. So I looked outside and looked for stars to compare to and it was very bright to the point I thought the light was from a tower or powerline. Then when I paid more attention to this object, I believe I was able to see a blue pulsing light behind it which meant I was looking at the front of the object. I convinced myself this was just a bright star and went to bed. One hour later I woke up because of the heat, it was a hot night, I went to pee and when I returned I noticed the light was gone. The next day I looked in that direction, and there is nothing obstructing the view. No mountains, towers, powerlines, nothing. 

There is a picture on twitter from someone who took a picture of a UFO from Vancouver Washington about 3 weeks ago. It looks exactly like what I saw.
