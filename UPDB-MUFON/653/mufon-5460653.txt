Strange, sliverly white long line of lights in the sky - with no noise.

Strange silvery white long line of straight lights in the sky (like a stretched-out pearl necklace). Appeared to be very high up, in a south easterly direction, and moved off towards the east. Initially I thought it was a meteor (for the first couple of seconds) - then realised it was not. I ran into the house to call other family members, who initially did not respond. After a couple of quick: “Come look at this!”, they responded. By this time, it had moved along a straight trajectory towards the east. My wife then sent me to get a camera and to wake up one of my sons, who had gone to bed. By the time I had returned, it had moved out of sight. I then, trying to think of a rational explanation, wondered if it was the contrail of a jet liner being lit-up in some strange way. So, I ran in and checked the website flight radar - there were no planes showing anywhere over the entire area and the nearest ones were in the north, over the peak district on the approaches to Manchester. Also, there was no moon. I then waited until a jet liner did appear in the sky and it looked nothing like it - and obviously had additional lights (some coloured and could be heard.
