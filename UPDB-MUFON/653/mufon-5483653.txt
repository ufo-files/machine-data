{nd} soft glowing object moving silently and very erratically.

Neighbor called and stated that there was something strange in the northern sky. It was a glowing reddish orange object, which to the naked eye was just a strange light. After using binoculars, we could see that it had an oval or disc shape and emitted a soft glow. Certainly not a blimp, because of the way it jigged in the sky.At first, we thought it might be a helicopter, but it was totally silent, and demonstrated flight characteristics which, to us, are impossible. it would hover stationary, thin move up, down, horizontally and diagonally at incredible speeds. It was demonstrating movements as if a laser was being jigged in the sky. The sky was clear at the time and there was nothing but forest in the area. This happened on both the 5th and 6th of may at approximately 9pm.
