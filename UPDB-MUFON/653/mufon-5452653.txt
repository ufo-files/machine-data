Captured Photo of 4 Glowing or Lighted Objects Flying in Formation Over Liberty, MO

On Saturday May 16th I was at the Liberty, Missouri town square taking some pictures of my classic car, which just so happens to be a 1964 Ford Galaxie. The space inspired name of my vehicle is merely coincidence. At aprox. 9:00PM CT I took 7 photos of my car from the same angle. After I looked at the images, I noticed the last picture, which was taken at exactly 9:02PM CT had 4 glowing objects in the sky. 3 of the lighted objects appear to be flying in formation together, while the 4th object is just outside the group of 3 flying objects. The 4th object appears to be either larger or closer and also appears to have some patterns to the lights that are glowing on the exterior. I am making this report, because I thought these flying objects in the sky looked similar to other images that I have seen reported as UFOs. I captured these images on my smartphone camera and I have attached the RAW digital photo file with the objects located right of center of the image in the sky.
