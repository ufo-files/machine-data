At 9:20 (dark outside) I went to have a cigarette outside. From the steps of the home I was looking up at the sky watching the lights of a plane going by (leaving my line of sight heading east) heading east). Seconds after I had moved my eyes to a tree in the yard I noticed in the sky a very fast moving thin blue-green-orange tail with a whiteis glowing cone shaped "head" heading to the right (I was facing south). It was skinny like when you see a meteor shower, or a shooting star, but it was much lower (as low as a visible airplane), but this did not seem like a natural pehnomenon-trajectory of this object was straight. It was definitely not a plane, or helicopter (nothing about it resembled aircraft-no sound - long tail, glowing colours, shape). It was very interesting. I observed it for roughly 3 sec. when it was just gone (I had eyes on it the entire time). I'm posting to see if it was seen by others in the Edmonton area. I've never seen something like this before. I've seen meteors over Edmonton twice, plenty of shooting stars, and meteor showers but this "sighting" was very cool.
