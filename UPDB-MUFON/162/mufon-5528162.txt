blue light in the western sky

my friends and i were at the Cobalt in Woodland Hills, California leaving a shoe, when all of a sudden i saw a huge blue light in the western sky. It hovered for a moment, then shot to the right and disappeared. It wasn't a plane because it moved so fast, and it shot across the sky so fast , never ascending or descending. It just simply vanished into thin air.
