Bright sphere, undetermined distance, rays of light, disappeared, reappeared, split in two, then three, turned into small clouds, more clouds appeared then all dimmed and faded away.

Aprox 11:45 AM 24 Nov 2017. Driving South on Hwy 3. Winkler would have been about 10 miles S/E and Morden 10 miles S/W of us when we spotted the sphere.
Sky was clear, some cloud on the horizon.
We observed a bright sphere in the sky, might have been reflective. Sphere would have been the size of a penny. it had what appeared to be beams of light emitting at an angle to the West from the top and bottom of the sphere. One beam each from the top and bottom of the sphere.
We watched it for about 5 minutes but couldn't tell if it was moving. We did not seem to draw any closer to it during the 5 minutes we observed it. We were driving at 60 mph.
It disappeared suddenly. A few minutes later a bright light appeared in the sky, no beams this time, and appeared to be fuzzy in nature. It remained in sight then suddenly split into two equal parts, then 3. The 3 lights were in a line running East to West. All three parts turned to small bright clouds, then more small clouds appeared suddenly around them. These clouds would have been about the size of a silver dollar. After a few minutes all three bright clouds dimmed and disappeared. In another few minutes the other clouds disappeared as well.
