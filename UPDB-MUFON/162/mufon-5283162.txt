Tic tac looking object

I was at the park with my daughter and my bro and I was observing the sky and looking at how beautiful it was in the night , then I suddenly seen this tic tac, pill looking object and thought it was very strange, it looked just like the 2004 incident with the air force pilots catching unidentifiable phenomenon on their live footage, in their jets, the exact same , no means of propulsion from what I could tell , no visible lights just the whole thing light up very bright , the second I go to take my phone out to record it all of a sudden just shot like a rocket and disappeared so quickly it left me feeling weird.
