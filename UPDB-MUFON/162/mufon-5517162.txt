{flg} Glowing Green Object going S to N over treetops

On the night of June 5, 2012 sometime after 10 PM, I was standing in my sons frontyard, facing the West when something caught my eye, I looked up and over the tree tops I saw a glowing green object going from left to right (S to N). It was maybe half a mile away, really hard to say the distance. It lasted just a few seconds. My son (age 31) looked up and saw it as it went behind the trees. It wasnt falling from the sky or being shot up, just going straight. 

Ive been looking online since that night to see if anyone reported seeing something like this. Someone finally directed me to this website.

Does anyone have any idea what it was? 
 
This is the first time Ive seen anything like this and now I find myself looking up at the sky every time I go outside.
