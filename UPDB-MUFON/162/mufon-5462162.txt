{tjd} Looked like a chrome M&M

Driving home from Harrisburg, PA on route 11&15 north, about halfway between Harrisburg and Liverpool PA, we spotted a shiny object in front of us. It was stationary at about a 60-degree angle off the horizon near power lines crossing the Susquehanna River. We watched it for several minutes as we drove along going toward it. It was a clear and sunny day and visibility was excellent. At first, we thought it was a blimp, but as we got closer it was obviously not a blimp as I've seen many over the years, and it was definitely not any aircraft I've ever seen. I spent 4 years in the air Force during the Vietnam war and literally saw hundreds if not a thousand different flying aircraft from a U-2 to a B-52. It looked like a chrome M&M as seen from the side, like a slightly flattened oval. It just sat there motionless all the while we drove and watched it. No movement whatsoever. I even said to my fiancee that we should stop and take a photo, but we didn't. To this day I don't know why we didn't stop and photograph it. It was absolutely beautiful.
