Moving MilkyWay

My husband and I were in our driveway at 9:27pm on May 7th, 2021 in Alton, NH, when a sparkling dusting of lights (Similar to what the Milky Way looks like only a condensed and moving version) caught our attention - it slowly moved from the Southwestern sky towards the South eastern sky, it lasted about 4 minutes. When it was over, I immediately ran in and searched what the phenomenon could have been, since I couldn't find it on my night sky app, and I couldn't film it -because it was too far away for my cell phone to pick up, I figured I would be able to find a reasonable explanation - Here some things I thought it could have been: The missing Chinese rocket hitting something and causing it to shatter and sparkle, the Starlink 24 satellites, (but from the videos I have seen there was no sparkling, no swath of moving stardust looking thing - in those videos. We have no idea what we saw. Maybe it was our cold atmosphere mixed with the Starlink 24 satellites shining off of whatever - but I cannot say that I can believe that because it was such a large swath of dust, sparkling lights, and stars moving in a smooth silent motion - disappearing just as ominously as it appeared.
