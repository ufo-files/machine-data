saw a object this morning as i delivered papers

i was delivering the daily paper this morning and as i put the paper in the last box for the development i was in, i saw a red light in the tree's where there is no red light normally. as i was heading out of the development i was looking to my left and right because there are allot of deer around and i didn't want to hit one. as i looked left i saw through the trees a red/white light that isnt there normally. it was about 100-150 feet in the air and about 300-400 feet away.as i turned onto a crossroad i saw the object again at left/center and it was changing from red to light red/white. i came to the intersection of 169th street and turned right where i lost sight of it. i realized that when i got to hwy 4 where i had another paper to deliver i would be able to see it again so i raced to get to hwy 4. as i turned left onto hwy 4 i saw the object again but it was on my right and allot farther away now so i sped down 4 to deliver the paper as i was watching this thing. i threw the paper in the box and continued down hwy 4 as i watched the object. i stopped and watched as it flew away then i turned around and want back to delivering the rest of my paper

i know it wasnt an airplane or helicopter because the thing didn't have any of the white and green strobelights that planes/choppers have. this thing was GLOWING light red/white and then red.
