Extremely bright square object with a straight line trajectory towards circular opening in the sky.

I was in Myrtle Beach driving and its motion caught my eye. I had no idea what it was but it was so different I had to photograph it. It was a square object moving in a Southerly direction at 1 oclock in my visual field. The object was extremely bright, moving rapidly towards the cone shaped area in the sky. Since I was in traffic I did not have the time to track it further. Later when I looked at the photograph I thought it was so unusual I needed to report it to you.
