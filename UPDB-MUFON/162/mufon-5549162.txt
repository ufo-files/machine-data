Clear night. Bright neon green light in southwest sky. Shape of a hot dog or long balloon in the southwest sky.

Standing on my back deck on a mild, absolutely clear August night I witnessed a bright neon green light flash in the southwest sky. It had the shape of a hot dog or long balloon, the type used for making figures. It acted like a falling star in motion although the color was stunning. A shade of green so unique and beautiful. I've never seen anything like it before or since. The distance made it too difficult to discern whether it was an actual object or an event caused by an object. A few days later the Quad City media were reporting crop circles that were discovered in a cornfield near Coal Valley. I have seen comets and falling stars and know that this was different. This did not burn out like the usual falling stars do.
