Shimmery orb watching at close range.

I was outside in my backyard working on my old car with my brother. My 2 kids were standing on back steps watching us. My brother and I were talking to each other when this shimmering orb, about the size of a softball, suddenly appeared between us around chest level. I asked what that was. We thought maybe a camera flash, or someone shinning a mirror at us. We watched as it circled us then it stopped right in front of my daughter face. I screamed, "you can't see that light in front of your face?" "What light!" She said. I asked her but she could not see it. Neither could my 6 year old son next to her. The orb looked liquid like. Kind of like you can put your hand through it and it would not come out on other side. It flew by some trees, then we run in the house. Next, it came through the wall into the house where we were sitting in the living room. My brother and I knew it was a UFO. I prayed that it would leave and shortly after that, it flew through my bedroom wall back outside. Creepy!
