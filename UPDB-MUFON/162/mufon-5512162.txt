(GS) I saw a translucent rectangle flying S to N approx 8pm 3/12/13 Huntington Beach

I went outside to look at the stars about 7:55pm on Tues night 3/12/13 in Huntington Beach. I hadnt been watching for more than 2 minutes when I saw a weird object pass overhead. It went from the South to North. I could see through it or it was cloaked somehow. It was a rectangle. I have no idea what it was. It was approx. 20,000 ft up and about thumb wide at arms length. I have an aviation background about my entire life so I know what Im looking at most of the time. This was amazing.
