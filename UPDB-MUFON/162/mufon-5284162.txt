Craft resembling bright satellite disappears from view.

Observer witnessed a bright craft with a magnitude resembling the ISS move through the sky, with a solid, non-blinking light. The object was first spotted at approximately 6:36 pm in the SE sky moving over the tree-line in a straight line, from south to north. The object travelled in a straight line until it was nearly due east of the observer, when the observer pointed an astronomy laser pointer directly on the object (after confirming that there were no signs of the object being a conventional aircraft). Directly after the observer pointed the laser pointer on the object, the object faded until it completely disappeared from sight. The observer stated that it was a clear sky, and he could clearly see stars in the same area of the sky as the object disappeared, indicating that the object was not obstructed by cloud cover. The moon was also clearly visible in the same area of the sky as the object disappeared from view. As soon as the observer spotted the object he began recording it with a smartphone camera, which will be included with this report.
