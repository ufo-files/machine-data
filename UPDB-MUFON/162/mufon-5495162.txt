{dr}Glowing orb with energy field moving incredibly fast no sound and sudden change of direction then dissapears

At 9:45 PM PST, an object was observed travelling from the SE at high rate of speed and observed by two people who were "sober" and not impaired in anyway. Object appeared to be bluish white with energy field that appeared to be a halo but did not have any trail of light or debris. Object was observed from sea level on a clear night however, the object appeared to be travelling through clouds. Again, the optical effect of the halo around the object (orb) distorted any details of shape or marking of the object. Unable to determine rate of speed of the object and altitude however, the object appeared 25 degrees of horizon travelling a very rapid rate of speed with an approximate observation time of 15 seconds and object making immediate change in direction at 45 - 90 degrees to the East and was no longer observable (disappeared from sight). Object did not emit any sound what so ever considering object's rate of speed and assumed low altitude. This object did not flash or have any aviation lights of different colors, same colors. The object was unidentifiable with any aircraft observed to date. Object was not a shooting star or falling star, meteorite or any other object falling to Earth.
