Sir/ Mam.
         evry year, for the last 9 years,myself along with my youngest son spend two weeks fishing in the area of E B campbell hydro electric station, usually during early to the middle of August ,it is just good father son time. the species we fish for ( walleye ) bites better at night, so we allways fish from early in the evening untill sometimes , 1 am, there is a small lodge and campsite nearby and we allways stay at the lodge . when i was in high school i worked at the same lodge for two summers, so, in one way or another this place has allways been a part of my life, i was born and raised aprox 70 miles south of the lodge to the south east in a place called porcupine plain, so for me, going there from alberta is going home.

aprox at 11 pm , i just happened to look up, why, i have no idea, what i seen was three bright distinct circles , for lack of better discription, i can say each circle looked like pools of flowing mercury , the circles were surounded by a single white aura , encircleing all three of the pools of flowing metalic light, i looked down , i was scared, i looked back up again, the pools still there, i did not want to scare my son, i turned to him and said, lets go , the fish arent biteing. we grabed our gear and walked back up the hill to my truck. i looked back and now had a good view of this thing from the side, god it was huge, it was shaped like half a blimp with a flat bottom from the bottom three tubes protruded from it, the tubes had like a corrugated apperance, the object never moved, and as far as i know was still there when i left , i never said a word to my son and i dont think he noticed the object when we left and drove back to the lodge. in order to do this, we needed to drive directly under the object, it had no interest in us or what we were doing,

 the next morning i wanted to ask the lodge owner if he had ever seen anything in the area before. but i could not find the courage .
