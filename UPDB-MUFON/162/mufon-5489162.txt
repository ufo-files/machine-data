{cak} Like blue aluminum disc flashing symbols floating like a baloon

Observed by entire crowd at drive in theater,first noticed it over lake Erie floating like a balloon toward me. Came right in front of movie screen and hovered flashing symbols similar to hieroglyphics. felt like it was directed right at me but I did not understand the symbols. after ten minutes or so hovering it rose slowly then whipped away so fast could not tell direction. was in The Lorain Journal the next day cut it out and saved it then lost it.
(50 witnesses)
