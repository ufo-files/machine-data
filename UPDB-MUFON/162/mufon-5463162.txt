{rjl} Three large, very bright amber orblike lights appeared in a wide V formation in the sky ahead of me, then disappeared together after several seconds

I was driving north on Hendricks and nearing 56th when I looked up and saw three very bright, large amber lights sitting stationary in the sky ahead of me. Much larger than stars but not the "aspirin" choice above, which was the closest of the offered choices. They were each probably more like 1/8" at arm's length. My impression was that they were a couple of miles ahead of me and several hundred feet off the ground, and probably covered a good half-mile from east to west. They were each identical and appeared as bright as a streetlight seen from ~100 feet away, so were in actuality probably much larger than a streetlight. They were evenly spaced and in a wide-V formation, so also separated by some space. Couldn't tell if it may have been one object displaying 3 lights, but appeared to be 3 separate lights and I didn't see any structure/craft. Immediately it dawns on me that I'm having a great sighting at the moment, and it felt very much like it was a display just for me to see, especially considering it was directly ahead of me and absolutely nothing around - no cars, no planes, just these three lights that appeared hovering ahead of me. I'm hoping someone else saw them, however, and also makes a report. I knew the sighting would not last long so I just gazed at the lights in wonder for a few seconds, and then they all just blinked out together. I immediately pulled over and called my daughter to tell her what I'd seen.
