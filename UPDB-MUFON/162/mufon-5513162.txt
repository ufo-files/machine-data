{cak} low, slow flying diamond shape. a white light on 3 points 4th point glowed from interior light orange

Pulled into my mother in laws driveway about 6:30 pm, I got my son out of his car seat and was holding him in my arms. He looked up and said "airplane" I looked up and saw an object flying low directly above us. I said to my mother in law look at that. It looks weird. She said oh yeah it does. We started to walk up her front walk I was behind her and my son and I kept looking up at the object. I stopped and stood still to watch for a moment. I observed the object was diamond shaped, had a white light on 3 of its points a blinking red light in the center and the 4th point was glowing orange, as if it were an interior light illuminating the whole point and bright enough to see a distinct diamond solid shape. I turned to go inside and my mother in law handed me a bag of rock salt for her driveway. It was covered in packed snow/ice. I stopped to watch the object again and realized it was completely quiet. It was at the same height of what the local helicopters fly and no noise. I watched it until it went behind the trees and house across the street. It flew low, and slow in a straight line to the west. Towards Jeanette.
