orange orb flying in the sky

It was around. 9:20 in the evening when me and 3 of cousins were grilling outside I noticed an Orange light in the sky flying I would say in a southeastern direction. We seen it get bright then fade then brighten again. We live close to dulles airport where planes frequently land and take off, but this was no airplane for sure. The light did not flicker like an airplanes strobe light . And we could also see that it did not fly in a straight path. We watched it move across they sky for a good 2-5 minutes then as a plane in the sky approached, it just disappeared
