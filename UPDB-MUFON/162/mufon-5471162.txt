{dr}Bright single solid car light, zooming across sky. NE straight-line direction with minimal 2-3 degree slight direction change towards NEE.No blinking lights, no trails.

My aunt was driving over the Main Street Bridge in Downtown when she saw a something zooming across the sky. "What's that?," she cried. Sitting up towards the windshield, I saw a solid bright light over Webster Bank. The light seemed to be at the sky level small airplane fly. The light looked like a plain light when it flies directly towards you, but this object was flying away from us at a NE direction. The light passed over New Brite Plaza and appeared to slightly/minimally shift towards a NEE direction. The trajectory I determined from looking at a digital map is from the plaza going towards Fairview Cemetery towards Newington and beyond. We took a right onto East Main Street and turned left into the driveway. By that time, which was about one minute after first spotting the light, we lost sight of the light due to a building. If looking at the bright yellow moon on the horizon, it was to the left. About how many feet to the left, I cannot make a proper estimate in pure recollection. My first reaction was deductive reasoning, as it would be foolish to report a natural or man-made occurrence. However, I truly believe that we saw a UFO. Extra terrestrial, who knows? But a UFO, indeed it was.
