Early morning the object was much brighter than in the video

It was early morning and i was standing on the road side just looking up at the sky as i do most mornings when i noticed a bright object making its way across the sky.
At first i thought it was a plane but as i watched it for a couple of minutes I've seen planes before so i know what there trials look like i began to think it was more than just a plane perhaps a satellite or an asteroid but that's when i started recording for a few reasons.

1)The objects trial varied only slightly and did not remain in the atmosphere like planes.
2)It seemed to be far higher than normal.
3)The total time i watched it was about 5 minutes and i thought the object would have burnt up or bounced off by then.
4)But it seemed more controlled

As i continued to watch this object the brightness began to intensify as though it was hitting the upper atmosphere as it looked like it was making some kind of breaking manoeuver.
As i watched it began to slowly break away and head into space.

Given that this was the first time i got something like this on camera i was quite thrilled so i ask that you watch the video and decide for yourself.
