Thought it was a very brigth star at first but it began alternating colors between red, blue and green and it hovered at exactly the same spot for about an hour. Had it been a star it would have appeared to have moved in that span.

My brother in law and I both witnessed a very bright light in the west that was brighter than other stars. We both assumed at first it was a star but then it began to alternate colors between red, green and blue. At times the whole object would appear white and then begin changing colors. We watched the object for nearly an hour. Then it just disappeared. We did not witness it move. It was as if someone just turned the lights off and it was suddenly not there.
