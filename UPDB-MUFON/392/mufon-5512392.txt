heading home from work, at around 22:55pm 25 December 2011, noticed a star that seemed to stand out from the others, this light promply vanished, at 23:15pm something made me look back. towards a playing field, it was dark for the time of evening, and hardly any traffic and no air traffic at all. 
looking towards the field an lighted object came over the field at flight path height, what made this object odd or stand out was the fact there was no noise, it flow over heading out to the north sea, about a minute later a second object came over at almost a chase or pursuit speed on the same flight path as though it was chasing the first object.
these objects were both flashing in a orange and red color and both made no noise.
