{tjd} Crash landing of ufo with what looked like boomerang with lights. Believe military jet or drone came to investigate

My husband and I were driving home around midnight through Fort Ripley, MN. We have seen things over the years in the upper part of MN as well as once in our home town. We both saw this boomerang type object coming down at an angle fast towards the highway but you could tell it was falling fast. It was storming and a crack of huge lightning, pink flash of light and a glow of pinkish gold was a constant. We took an exit to see if we could catch whatever it was. Unfortunately my phone didn’t cooperate and you can hear me groan on video but I believe the three dots to the bottom right corner of my video is a partial of the craft but in my excitement could be reflection. The earth was glowing for a while and it was difficult to find through the trees and we ended up going back to 371 but as we hit the on-ramp what looked like a small plane or jet or drone came flying over low and I could just barely make the outline. We headed home convinced it was not of this world. Could have been struck by lightning.
