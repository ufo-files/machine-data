my brother and I was standing in front of our house waiting for a ride to our boy scout meeting when my brother said look ,I look up and there was a ship hovering over top of us right above the maple tree in front of our house.The ship didn't make a sound,it had lights on the bottom of it .The ship was at least 50 feet wide.Days later my sister and I was watching TV when we heard glass brake upstairs ,so we ran to the neighbors and the neighbor came over to the house to check the house to find nothing broke.I asked my brother years later if he remembered it and he said no.I believe that something happened to my brother and sister because they were never the same after that.
