Bright object seen in sky which split into 2 during day

I was with friends on Christmas Eve sat by the bay having a picnic with our kids. As I looked up I saw an object in the sky and started recording it. 

It was moving in various directions and seemed to be moving of its own accord. It would also return to its original location and was moving against the wind. 

It would also split into what looked like 2 objects which moved together and then would switch back to a single object.
