Bright light traveling across the sky like a satellite, but brighter, faster, and closer. Then got brighter and flew into space

I walked outside with my wife to smoke a cigarette and looked up at the stars. I noticed in the SW west sky in the direction of the Oregon coast range a light similar to a satellite and pointed it out to my wife. It was brighter than a satellite, appeared closer than one also, and was faster. It traveled from SW to NW, appearing to follow the earths curvature. After it passed the Big Dipper it paused and got brighter, almost as if it pointed it's light towards us. Similar to someone pointing a flashlight towards you. The pause was only for a second and then it appeared to fly away. Not flying away as if it flew away in the distance, but straight up, as if it went into space, faded, and then disappeared.
