Four 12-years children saw object from 5m distance on the 5m altitude. Object was hovering over treetop.

Four 12-years children saw an unknown object on the distance of 5m and it was hovering over treetop approx. 3 minutes.That evening they laid down on the beach and about 2230 they saw unknown object not so far away from them (5m). Object was hovering over treetop very slowly with intensive pulse light fluorescent green colour on the part of object. It was hovering 100m and when came near a high tree, object leaned out, lost a strong glow, and fluttered in the night sky at the speed of the meteor. After that my daughter which is one of the 4 children came at the home earlier than normal but with fear and very upsetting. She could not explain what she saw. I went to the beach and I have not seen anything. Tomorrow during the day I talked with all four children but with each child alone. They all explained what they saw and everyone, without the help of others, drew what he saw that night. Sketches are very similar, colors also, match the dimensions of the object. From this I concluded that the children saw the object of material nature and that they had never seen such a thing. The following things can be seen from their sketches: 1. The object is cone shaped 2. The dimensions of the object are 40-50cm in width and 60-80 cm in height 3. The object is dark with part around the whole object fluorescent green color that is somewhere more or less glossy 4. At the top of the object is the brightest fluorescent green light as well as on the same colored rays below the object, which were about 1cm wide and seemed like a gel mixture 5.
