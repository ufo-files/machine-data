{jpg} Very Bright and large stationary light low in the skyline, early evening. Other stars were barely visible, did not move or flicker. Took pics.

Early evening (7:30 pm), I was throwing pizza scraps to the brim in canal off back deck. Bright white star-like light was at/slightly below tree line looking from lowland level. Star-like object direction was west, exactly. Looked for other stars overhead but they were dim, barely visible. Watched what appeared to be a large star for 25 to 30 minutes. It did not move and gave me a strange feeling like it was headed right for us. Went in house, got phone, zoomed in and took a couple photos. Stayed outside for another 20 minutes, seemed like it was moving closer and getting slightly larger but did not change direction. Went back inside to put children to bed,thought about taking more pics without zooming in. Came outside again at 8:45 and it was gone.
