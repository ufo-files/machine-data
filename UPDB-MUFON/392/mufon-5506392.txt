{nd} 3 triangle craft at a hover no sound i called carswell a f b

I was 11yrs old I felt strange and went out side. the night was clear and calm. I looked up and saw them 3 triangle shape objects. they just stayed in one place.I looked at my watch it was 9 pm. I must have watched them for 20 min. I then went inside and called careswell afb. they said it was a hoax. two myaterias showed up at the door about 10:15 pm that night. thst scared me to death. I kept quite till now.
