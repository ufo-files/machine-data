{rjl} A friend and I were walking by the shoreline of the beach, when suddenly we saw this mysterious object ride from the water into the sky.

A friend and I were going for a walk at the beach around 11-11:30 pm. When we were close to the shoreline we both saw this red flare-like light rising from the water as if it came underneath the ocean or it looked like someone had shot a flare gun in the distance on a small boat. There were no boats around. We knew this because the object was very close to us. We didn’t even see anyone at the beach but us. This red light that left a trail behind like a small fire ball slowly raised to the sky as it expanded in size and form. It looked like this giant orb with white glow around it as it had a shape of a torpedo. Suddenly it stopped from going straight to the sky and started moving sideways. We were both freaked out and confused to what we were seeing. I’ve seen many weird/unidentified objects before but nothing ever compared to this. My friend and I took out our phones and recorded it as much as we can until it vanished into the sky. Being freaked out we both left the beach and went home. Till this day I have forgotten about the video and am currently trying to find it. My friend in the other hand I haven’t spoken to but I will be reaching out for that video.
