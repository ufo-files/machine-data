My wife and I observed saucer shaped pulsating objects in northern sky. Difficult to determine size or distance. Could have been very large which means they could have been near the east central Minnesota/Canadian border. They left at a remarkable speed. I had just become employed by city police department. Felt duty bound to make report. Initially contacted Intl. airport MSP. Within minutes a ranking military officer from Duluth base called. He asked questions and said they had not observed anything on radar. Later I learned of several events on August 27, including Salida Colorado, Bensalem Pennsylvania, Warren Minnesota etc. I became astonished at the date/event ratio. I began to look into the subject, traveling to these areas, making contacts, where possible to ascertain facts. Mr.Edwards from Salida passed on, Phil Schneider is dead, Al Bielek died, so I called Ken S. from MUFON and began dialogue. Intending to interview Dan Akroyd.
