{dr}5-7 glowing orbs moving in an M shaped formation. Descended below trees. One emerged and two more joined formation. Then gone.

My family and I were out to look at Jupiter and the moon with a telescope. At 9:00 PM my daughter said "what is that" as she pointed to 6-7 glowing orange orbs moving in perfect formation. My wife is ex military and a pilot and we just discussed the lighting on commercial aircraft before the sighting. We instantly knew this was nothing like we had ever seen. The objects descended below and behind the trees so we went to the side of our property for a better view. One object reappeared shortly joined by two more in a triangular formation before basically vanishing. Total time 10 minutes. I had everyone go inside and draw a picture of what they saw. Four witnessed the event.
