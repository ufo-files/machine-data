Golf ball like object seen hovering, no sounds

I was at at a golf course [item moved/CMS/nd] on Sat 4/14/12 (approx. 3pm)and one of the guys I was playing with just hit his drive and he said "I popped it up".... So I looked up and I saw this white object that looked like a golf ball. And I looked at it and looked at it and it didn't move. Mind you it was really windy(25-30 mph gusts) and not a cloud in the sky. Yet this object did not move or even make a noise. So I turned to the rest of our group and I said jokingly " hey I thought that was your ball" As I pointed towards the object in the sky. Everyone stood and stared at this object not knowing what to say or think....I hesitated and then I said "I didn't think you could see Saturn today."..As we were golfing next to an airport it was quite clear this was not a plane or helicopter. As it had no obvious sign of direction, did not appear to make a sound and did I mention it looked like a golf ball. We proceeded down the fairway and it did not move, much like you would expect from looking at the moon. When we got to the green (aprox. 1 minute later)we all looked around and it had vanished. It was no where to seen...we looked and looked as if expecting to see that it was just a ballon or blimp or anything. But it was gone........
