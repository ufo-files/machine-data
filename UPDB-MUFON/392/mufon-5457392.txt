It was a sudden flash projecting in the sky

I just came out of the famous Hanuman setu temple back gate of the city on the Choti Diwali Festival yesterday and as I was waiting for my Cab , my eyes turned left towards the metro line above and just above it a strange object rushed extremely fast through the sky with a greyish and whitish trail as well as a same coloured cloud around it.
I with my mom and brother immediately pointed them to look over it but it vanished within 4 secs till I made them look at it.
The object vanished and I initially thought of a firecracker rocket but it wasn't as it was ascending upwards with no colour except a grey and white cloud and trail.
It was my first experience of an UFO ,the cloud formed was lit by it but got vanished as the object disappeared.
