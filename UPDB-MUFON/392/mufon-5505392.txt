{cak} 5 reddish, orange lights traveling in a straight line, no sound, disappeared one at a time overhead.

While traveling west on a roadway in Wilmington N.C., I saw in the distance what I thought were two helicopters. I noticed the objects were heading towards me and once I knew they were not helicopters, I pulled off to the side of the road and got out of my car. I observed not two, but five round, reddish orange lights moving in line one right after the other. They were spread apart but do not know how far. They went directly over me and I could not hear any sound. Once the first object passed over me, it disappeared. The other objects disappeared at the exact spot as the first. It was a clear night, the moon was almost full, and I could not see any clouds in the sky. Lived here my whole life and never seen anything like this before.
