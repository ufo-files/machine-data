Seven hovering lights {had what looked like chem trails behind them} slowly moving toward each other - {djs}

I was traveling from Livonia to Plymouth heading west on 96 toward 275. It was around 7:45. I witnessed exactly seven (that were observable to me) lights that appeared stationary however they had what looked like chem trails behind them. That being said, the "chem trails" were not what you would typically observe from an airplane, they were about 1.5 times the width of the light itself. We are near DTW, however I was heading west, and I realized that each light was slowly headed toward the others. It appeared they were soon going to line up perfectly (which would not happen given there were no other airplanes visible in the sky, they were in VERY close proximity to one another, and that number of planes would not fly toward each other. I continued to watch from 96 to 14, then unfortunately I had to turn on Sheldon which blocked my view. I immediately parked my car and took my dog (who was traveling with me) for a walk to get a better view. Once I parked and got around trees and houses, I was still able to observe one of the lights. They were still moving very slow but completely down, toward the horizon. Given the area I was in, this was only observable for another 45 seconds to a minute. I continued walking in hopes of other sightings, to no avail.
