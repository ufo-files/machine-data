It looked like a giant string of lights or a ‘train’.

A giant ‘string’ of about 50 lights moving high and fast in a perfect line. Star like but very bright. The line seemed to have a pattern of 3 space 4 space 3 space 4 and so on. It moved from the horizon in the west toward the southeast sky above and then seemed to fade out or disappear into the clear sky in order , not all at once. Almost like a train going into a tunnel. We could still see stars at the spot where the lights disappeared. If I had to guess I would say it was a mile long but very high up. The lights did not blink at all, but were like very bright stars. Almost twinkled. We pulled over and tried to get a picture but my phone camera wouldn’t pick it up. The whole thing lasted about 2-3 minutes.
