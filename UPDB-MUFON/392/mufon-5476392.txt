{cak} Orb came over the hills of Pacifica/San Bruno (Sweeny Ridge area trail) straight towards our location

The video I am attaching has strong language so just a heads up. I was hanging out with a family member up on a landing on the second floor of a building watching fireworks since it was New Years Eve.
