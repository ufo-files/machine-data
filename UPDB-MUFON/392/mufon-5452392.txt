Black box with fins

I was walking to the local bar as I did every night... as I walked over the stone bridge spanning the river tees I heard a fairly loud humm.. i looked in the direction of the sound and saw a matt black shape hardly visible against the night sky only 20 ft away. I stopped to observe and it as it drifted at about 5mph in a westerny direction.. by the time it was 40ft away it was no longer visible and i walked on
