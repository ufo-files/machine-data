{cak} I seen a light thought it was a plane. It got closer rolled my windows down no sound. Hovered then left

I seen something in the sky, from far away above the orchard next to la Rosa school. I was at the stop light turning left onto Eastgate, I kept looking to the right to see this odd light I thought was an airplane. As it got closer I was scrambling for my phone to get a pic. I rolled my windows down. It was totally silent and hovered over my car for about one second it was giant. I was frozen. Then all the the sudden at rapid speed it took off over our houses in the direction of the air port. I haven't seen any thing like that before.
