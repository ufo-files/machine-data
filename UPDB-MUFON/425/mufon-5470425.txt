I was taking out the trash around 6:30pm this evening when I looked up and saw a vey bright star like light shining above me to the west; it stood out because it was brighter than the other starts in the sky and it also had a misty fuzzy cloud like aura around it. I took out my cell phone camera to take pictures of it while it sat there motionless. I did not see any pulsating or blinking lights like you would normally see on an aircraft. It just sat there hovering like it was suspended in midair. Then as I was re-positioning my cell phone to take more pictures it began to slowly move sideways to the left in a southeast direction. It would move slowly then stop, move again slowly then stop. Then it moved again and picked up speed and as it moved it created a plume of vapor trail behind it like the contrail of a rocket, with an arrowhead shape and there was a low rumble sound off in the distance as well; it continued moving towards the southeast for a few more seconds then it blinked out like a flash and the vapor contrail dissipated and the object was no longer visible. I did manage to get about 10 pictures of this object before it disappeared but I must apologize for the low resolution image quality as the camera on my phone is not the greatest. This is not my first sighting of this kind since I've lived here, there have been several others in the past.
