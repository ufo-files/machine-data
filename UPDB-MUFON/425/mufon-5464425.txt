{cak} Very large round disc shaped object with a black bottom and large lights around the edge

I was five years old. In bed sleeping next to my grandfather. He was to my right and the wall to my left with a window. It was a warm night. The window was open and I was awake and playing with a jumping spider that was crawling on the outside of the screen. I was poking at it. Then I remember a massive (so big I couldn't see all of it) black-ish bottomed saucer-shaped hovering object very close above our home. The outter edge of the craft had red-ish, white-ish, and possibly blue-ish lights. This thing was huge. I was not scared at all. That is all I recall. I don't recall seeing it show up or leave. Just that I saw it. That is it. My first recollection of seeing ufo's.
