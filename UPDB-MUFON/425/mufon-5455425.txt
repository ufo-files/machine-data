{jpg} Craft looked like it was observing NASA's Solar Orbiter just after takeoff

My husband & I were watching NASA's solar orbiter takeoff on Sunday, February 9, 2020 in the evening from our home in Barefoot Bay. We can see NASA's launches pretty clearly. The rockets, etc. come up over the houses/trees when we look North. We can usually view the rockets, etc. for about 15 minutes as they fly across the sky (usually going South in an arched fashion). I try to take photos, but sometimes the objects are too small. I did take phoso of NASA's solar orbiter. When I downloaded the photos to my laptop, I noticed a cylindrical cigar-shaped object below and to the left/West of the orbiter. I left the photos exactly as I took them, except for two. I copied the photos of the object that appeared to be observing NASA's orbiter as it took off and ascended. I just enlarged two photos to get a better look at it. I could not explain that object and it looked like a UFO. It was not a plane or drone. If anyone else has photos or can explain this object, please let me know.
