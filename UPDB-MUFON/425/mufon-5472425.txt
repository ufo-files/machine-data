UFO hovered for extended period of time.

Just about to go to bed and looked outside. Saw some unusual lights in the distance, above the treetops. The lights were not moving so it couldn't have been a plane. There were approx 13 rectangular windows, standing on short side. The largest in the centre and progressively smaller extending to left and right. From the central window there was a light of varying colours that rotated and pulsed. We were not successful in getting pictures due to the distance. The object did not change locations. It stayed there for so long...we observed for about 45 minutes, that we got tired and went to bed.
