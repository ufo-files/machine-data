{jpg} Intense, large bright light with unexplained behavior.

Very large, very bright orange-red light (about the size of a full moon) suddenly appeared closely above the hill directly beyond my backyard. It hovered for a few seconds, then ascended 3x its previous altitude, in a straight line and hovered for a few seconds, then ascended 2x higher, in a straight line and hovered for a few more seconds, then ascended 2x higher again, in a straight line, and hovered, (each time getting smaller in size) then quickly shot upwards diagonally, on a 45 degree angle and vanished. Conditions: 100% clear sky, Milky Way visible.
