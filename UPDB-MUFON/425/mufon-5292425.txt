We were camped up the cliff in a cave after my dad's funeral. The thing flew toward Mt. Index, across the Skyhomish valley then stopped in from of us.

The object had a red light and and a yellow light. Could not see the exact shape. It flew slowly towards Mt. Index. Then towards Stevens Pass, back towards the place we were on the cliff. We had a small fire. The thing stopped in front of us, felt like 20 .minutes. then it went straight up and disappeared. It did not make any noise. We thought it was a helicopter but there was no sound.
