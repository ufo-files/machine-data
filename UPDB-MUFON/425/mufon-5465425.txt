Mr. D. is retired from a supervisory position in the construction of a high security facility (such as Taverny). Mr. D., apart from the feeling that he has always been "different" and waiting for a particular event, has also for a long time believed to have lived 2 previous lives. One in a country bordering the Mediterranean, and the other in a small village in the center of France (which he has located on a map), this last life having materialized in a kind of small manor in bad state, often bordered by the mists of a small river. More than ever, he feels this feeling of sadness that seems to inhabit this thing and its immediate environment. The car is still rolling, slowly, all headlights on, nothing happens.

It is at this moment that the 4 occupants of the car, while the edge of the flying disk is in line with their vehicle, see their conscience anesthetized. Nobody, but really nobody remembers anything.

Missing time
At one point, the witness regains consciousness. What happened ? Where is he ? 3 minutes have passed. He does not remember anything. He feels that 3 minutes have passed, but no proof. The car is in line on the road, the engine is running, the thing has completely disappeared. The 4 occupants regained consciousness: a distance of 1.2 km was traveled by their car. How? Mystery. Complete misunderstanding, still to this day. One certainty then becomes necessary for the witness: he was no longer able to drive his car, he had lost consciousness, he had been emptied of everything. But what has happened?
