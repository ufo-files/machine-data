{rjl} Orb sighting

I spotted a single round reflective orb moving from North to East. After a few minutes it joined up with 6 - 8 other orbs in the sky slowly moving further East. At different points during the sighting I viewed them in triangle formation and three in a straight line one over the other until they went out of view.
