{dr}Every Thurs. bet. 7 and 9 for past 3 wks. 2-3 bright hovering objects

For the last three Thursdays between approx. 7:00 pm and 9:00 pm my son and his neighbors have observed two ufos in the sky. They have bright orange lights which go from very bright to non-existant. They make no sound and either hover in one spot or move across the sky, sometimes slowly and sometimes very fast. They are in the shape of a "V" from what he can tell. He called me last around 7:00 pm and said they were back again. This time I drove to his house and observed them myself. I found them to be everything that he claimed. His neighbors had been watching these crafts just as he had been. I took a picture since I brought my camera. Looking through the camera viewfinder they appeared as just kind of light dots. When I uploaded the photo to my computer they definitly appeared to have a "V" shape. If you want to see them, come by next Thursday or give me a number I can call when they arrive.
