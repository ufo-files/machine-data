Bright white boomerang pass over our heads with no sound moving really fast and hover over 70 east freeway shooting a beam of white light onto the freeway for a few minutes then that quick beam of light off. Then move up then a 90 degree turn left then made a right 90 degree turn then shot straight up into space. My supervisor look at me and said he was ex LT in the Air force and there's no conventional Craft can move that fast and make no sound making 90 degrees turns like that that he knows of. I told him I agree. He told me if we go back into this building and tell these people what we saw they will laugh at us. So we went back to work from our break and we never spoke of it again.
