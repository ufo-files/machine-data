{dr}Watching fireworks show with 4 obvious disc shape objects looking down at the fireworks show changed color from white beam to red, merged into one and left.

My mother and I were sitting on the back deck watching fireworks show tonight 7/4/14. Over the east sky of Denver there were 4 disc shaped objects hovering over the largest fireworks as if watching. They hovered a few moments then seemed to merge into one object and disappeared as fast as they arrived. They stayed in place about five minutes .
