The security camera detects motion and sends a series of photos to my email. I found these photos strange as the light appears to be hovering right over the front of my truck. Where the object is over the truck there is a block wall so no light could be coming through from the other side or the road. No one is aware of the camera so I don't feel anyone was playing games with it. I went out and tested it the next night with a mag light to see if I got the same effect. I did not, not near bright enough or large enough. I think this is from something hovering. There are lights way in the background from other houses but they should be much higher in the photo. They also don't show up this bright or big on the camera. The only other possibility for this photo is a UFO.
