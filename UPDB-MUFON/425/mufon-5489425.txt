{nd} Looked like a star but 3-5X larger blinking, but changing color and moving in circular patterns

Upon returning from shopping and eating (no alcohol consumed) I noticed a very bright star in the sky, after further observance I realized it was moving but in a path of no commercial air vehicle. I called my husband over who has served in the military and is VERY familiar with air crafts and he at first dismissed it, I began recording on my Samsung S6 showed him the video and he was speechless, for over 4 minutes I recorded the object in what seemed like an erratic dancing pattern across the night sky, getting brighter then dimmer and disappearing. This isn't the first time I've witnessed an object in this area (not exact location but near by). Please have a look at the video and let me know your thoughts, there are some bugs flying across the lense and you will hear the pool pump humming and cut off in the background, the rest are crickets and other night creatures, I wish I had better equipment (watching the video on my phone you can clearly see the stars vs the object).
