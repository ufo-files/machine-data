Bright hovering object in sky

My wife brought me outside to ask my opinion on what I thought this object was. This is not the first time we had witnessed peculiar lights in this area. I went outside to see, and it was as bright as any star I had seen (in fact, much brighter), and it hovered quietly. I originally thought this to be a star, but it was far too bright. I went back inside, grabbed my camcorder, and filmed it for several minutes. I videotaped the spot I was standing as to give judgement to my point of distance. Seeing as how it was pitch black, it's hard to estimate distance on the film. I sat the camera on a fence post to insure stability, and zoomed in and out on the object a couple of times. I wanted to make sure EVERY thing I was seeing was captured in every point possible. After a few minutes, my wife grew nervous and asked me to come back inside. I came back outside minutes later to retreive some firewood, and the object was gone.
