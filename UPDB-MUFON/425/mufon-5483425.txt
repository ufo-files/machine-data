Six white orbs changing shape

On April 11th 2016 at approximately 7:45 p.m. I witnessed and videotaped 6 objects while videotaping the contrail of a high flying aircraft. I was at work looking SE from South Burnaby BC. It was during the daylight and was sunny with cloudy periods. The objects are described as white in color and flashing in and out of existence while constantly changing shape and illumination. There appears to be a dark eye in the center of them that were looking around...no s**t... In the captured video there are three pairs of two of these objects. I will include a copy of my video for your records.

PS If you need help finding them, the first two are near the top left and move to the right behind the branch then change direction. The most obvious two start in the right and end up in the center of the frame and directly below the aircraft. The third pair are to the left of the two below the aircraft and pop out of the clouds briefly...
