Large color-changing orb hovering over neighbors roofline

We walked out the front door about 8:50 pm on Sunday night and saw this large glowing orb, which changed between a burnt-orange and whiter color hovering in the distance above the telephone lines. The sky was overcast, and this was the only object visible in the sky. This was visibly larger and brighter than any star normally would be and did not "fly" across the sky with blinking lights like we've seen airplanes do. After a few minutes, the light dimmed and then was not seen again.
