i saw a large orange glowing moving object over our nieghborhood

oct 31st 1977 i was about 10 years old.i had just come home from football practice.i was late coming home and all my friends had already been out for halloween.i went to catch up.i ran down my street vesper lane to the corner and made a left to start trick or treating.as i made the left and came down the street i saw in the sky heading from my left to my right a large glowing sun like ball in the sky moving over the nieghborhood.i stopped and watched.it disappeared over the trees.i ran back to my house never finishing going door to door because i was scared and didn't want to go down the street into the direction of the light
