1> I WAS WORKING ONBOARD A MERCHANT SHIP (OIL TANKER)AS A TRAINEE NAVIGATING OFFICER WE HAD DEPARTED FROM U.K IMMINGHAM AFTER LOADING CARGO AND PROCEEDING TO HOUSTON. WE WERE IN THE MID ATLANTIC OCEAN WEHN I WAS HAVING A CHAT WITH THE THIRD MATE IN HIS WATCH AT THE BRIDGE.
2> THE OBJECT WAS FIRST NOTICED BY THE LOOKOUT SEAMAN WHO WAS ASSISTING THE THIRD MATE IN HIS WATCH LATER HE BROUGHT IT TO OUR NOTICE.
3> I WAS HAVING AN IMPRESSION THAT IT MIGHT BE A NAVAL HELICOPTER SINCE I COULD BARELY SEE A RED AND A GREEN SIDELIGHT. BUT MY SUSPICION GREW A BIT LARGER A HELICOPTER IN THE MIDDLE OF THE OCEAN?? IF THERE WAS A SEARCH AND RESCUE OPERATION GOING ON IN THE VICINITY WE WOULD HAVE GOT A DISTRESS MESSAGE ON OUR GMDSS SYSTEM WHICH I COUNTER CHECKED NO MESSAGES WERE RECEIVED AT THAT TIME
4> I COULD JUST SEE A LIGHT AT FIRST AT ABOUT AN ALTITUDE OF 45DEG THE I DECIDED TO HAVE A LOOK THROUGH THE BINOCULARS THAT TIME I COULD SEE RED AND GREEN SIDELIGHTS I THOUGHT IT WOULD BE A HELICOPTER THEN WE KEPT A WATCH ON IT FOR ABOUT 10 MINUTES THEN I STARTED LOSING ALTITUDE RAPIDLY NOW IT WAS ABOUT 10DEG THEN IT SHOT ALTITUDE AGAIN RAPIDLY WITHIN 5 SECONDS THAT TIME I REALIZED SOMETHING WAS WRONG I WAS UNDER THE IMPRESSION MAYBE THE AIRCRAFT MIGHT BE DITCHING WE EVEN MONITORED THE VHF CHANNELS FOR ANY DISTRESS MESSAGES NONE WERE RECEIVED. WE CALLED UP THE CAPTAIN HE CAME UP AND OBSERVED THE OBJECT AND WENT BACK IN HIS CABIN. AFTER ABOUT HALF AN HOUR WE WERE THROWN IN FOR A SURPRISE THE UFO STARTED TO CHANGE ITS LIGHTS AND WAS MORE VISIBLE THROUGH THE BINOCULARS NOW I COULD MAKE OUT A ROUGH FIGURE I COULD SEE A TRIANGLE SHAPED OBJECT WITH A BRIGHT GOLDEN YELLOW LIGHT PULSATING THIS KEPT ON TILL 03:30HRS ON THE SECOND MATES WATCH WITH THE UFO LOOSING AND GAINING ALTITUDE MANY TIMES.(WEATHER WAS QUITE GOOD WITH NO SWELLS, VISIBILITY WAS ALSO APPROXIMATELY 10 NAUTICAL MILES AND THERE WAS A SLIGHT MOONLIGHT)
5> I WAS AWARE THAT UFOS EXISTED BUT TO SEE ONE WAS A MIND BLOWING EXPERIENCE FOR ME I WAS HAPPY AS WELL AS UNEASY TO SEE A UFO IN THE MIDDLE OF THE OCEAN.
6>WELL I WASN'T THERE WE THE OBJECT WAS LOST THIS HAPPENED IN THE SECOND MATES WATCH AROUND 03:30HRS.
