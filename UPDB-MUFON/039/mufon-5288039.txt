Unknown blue/green lights in sky

I walked onto my balcony on the evening of April 27, 2021 and sat down to enjoy a beautiful evening. I sat and looked east toward the rising moon. It was then that I noticed approximately 10 blue/green glowing tic-tac-shaped lights in the sky mostly to the left (north) of the moon. I watched for several minutes. A couple of the lights would blink off then back on. I took a couple of photographs with my iPhone. I decided to go inside and get my binoculars to see if I could see more detail. When I came back out with my binoculars all of the lights were gone and did not reappear to my knowledge.
