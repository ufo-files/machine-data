{nd} 3 spheres hovering in a triangle form over Santa Monica Pier America

On the 20th of September 2014, I was at Santa Monica Pier in Los Angeles in holidays from Australia. I have now come home and found on my mobile phone, photo camera and video camera these three round spheres in triangle form in the blue sky. One appears to be reflecting the sun more than the other two. I remember not seeing anything flying in the sky at the time and it was a beautiful clear day. You may need to zoom in to notice the spheres but once you find them you will see the three spheres clearly.
