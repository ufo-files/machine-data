{nd} Orange-Red lights to the east fading in color from orange to red heading west.

I seen some lights rise above the treeline to the east of my house. the lights were initially orange and i assumed they were fireworks, that was until they started heading my way and more of them appeared. as they got above me they 1 by 1 disappeared. the lights started orange and turned red. at one point i seen 3 of them form a triangle so i though for a seccond maybe this is military or some type of test rocket, i stopped to listen and heard nothing! i ran inside to find camera and naturally the battery was dead. i rushed back outside to see the last few lights head my direction and fade overhead. less than 5 minutes later there were atleaste 3 airplanes flying over head in the same direction.

these lights moved slowly, did not move in 90 degree angles but did display formation/color changes and all disappeared at roughly the same location.
