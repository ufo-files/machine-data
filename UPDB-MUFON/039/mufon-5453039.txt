Dark shape that flew across my plane of sight

I was watering my garden, in the last evening . The sun had set, so the light to the west toward the mountains was dimming. The dark shape cauugt my eye... thinking it was a large bird or bat, but flew to straight .. not a drone , didn’t hover and had no lights. So I watched.. it flew from the north to south over our town, and straight out of my sight. No sound, no lights... a dark shape with an angle tilt in the direction is was headed.
