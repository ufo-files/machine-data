{nd} Very large, glowing green/blue, eye shaped light, appears, hovers and dives

I was riding my motorcycle heading south on the 71 freeway heading from Pomona, CA to Corona CA. There were no clouds in the sky and it was very clear. On the motorcycle I can see everything. I had on my clear goggles that allow meto see everything without wind or debree bothering my eyes.I was about 5 to 10 miles miles outside of Chino Hills well before the 91 freeway and the damn in Corona. All of a sudden to my right at about 45 degrees a glowing light green blue shape appeared in the sky without any sort of entrance trail like a shooting star. It appeared, hovered for a split second and dove toward the earth but disappeared before hitting. It had an eye shape to it and was a very strange light glowing green. I have never seen a light or a green color like that. It was not a comet or falling star. Comets and falling stars don't just appear, hover and then dive.
