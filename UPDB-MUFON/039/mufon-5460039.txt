{rjl} 2 metallic, oval ahaped objects flying slowly over Downtown Excelsior Springs, MO

I was volunteering for a small concert event in Excelsior Springs, MO. Which is not too far from Kansas City. After I was done getting a large, heavy beam up for lighting systems, I noticed 2 objects moving very slowly north. I watched them for at least 15 minutes before leaving the area to return home at Excelsior Springs Job Corps. Campus. 

It was too large too be a balloon, and too slow to be planes as well, so I really didn't know. Speculation on it from the NWS says it was a weather balloon, but I'm not so sure.
