{nd} My friend and I just saw what I can only describe as a white, flat, rod shape object in the sky, across the street from me. It did NOT look like a plane. I live in Whitefield NH.

My friend and I were sitting on my porch, across the street from the water fountain in Whitefield. I observed a white, rod like, misty object (that looked like a small piece of "cloud"), slowly going across the sky. I asked my friend if she was seeing the same thing I was... and she said yes. We watched it go across the sky and disappear into a cloud. It was too high up to be a plane. The cloud was small and dissipated rather quickly, after the object entered it.
