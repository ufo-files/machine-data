Simply stepped out onto my patio, looked up and there it was. It was a bright orangeish- yellow globe. I thought it may have been a chinese latern but, the wind was at my back and the object was moving towards me. It had spikes like those of a compass. Straight line. Excitement no fear. Though afterwards very, very agitated. Very little sleep that night. I did get my cell phone and was able to snap to photo of it. The object simply just vanished in a heartbeat before my eyes. Thanks for being here, It's difficult to speak to others about an event of this nature. I'm not very tech savvy and don't know how to post my photo. What a great start to my new year. The best to you all at MUFON.
