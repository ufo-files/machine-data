{jpg} Driving to work and noticed a strange light in a triangular shape toward the horizon. Took my cell phone and took pictures and a short video with my Samsung Galaxy s8.

I was on my way to work the morning of August 31, 2018 @ 625 in the morning. I stared at the sunrise as usual because I have to stop a damn red light, right before i get on the freeway to work.
