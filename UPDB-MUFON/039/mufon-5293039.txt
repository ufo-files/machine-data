Yellowish orange pulsating sphere

My father and I were driving South on the freeway I 25 in colorado sometime around 10:00 p.m. just South of Colorado Springs maybe 15 or 20 miles traveling at approximately 75 miles an hour when to my right which is the direction West I saw a yellow Orange white extremely large pulsating glowing sphere hovering over the Rockies mountain range. After watching this for 10 minutes or so these beams of red lights shot down from what I can see as no point of origin no where near the glowing sphere we were seen in the background but these lasers or red beams of light where coming from a start point out of mid air shooting down to the ground coming backup at the same angle and disappearing parallel to the point it had started from originally. We heard no sound of any kind and I asked my father to pull over because I have a disposable camera in my luggage I wanted to get to and stop so I could get the best photographic evidence possible but as I was extremely interested and adventurous in to pursuing this he was very very frightened and something about it told him not to stop. We continue to drive South on I 25 and until it left our line of sight
