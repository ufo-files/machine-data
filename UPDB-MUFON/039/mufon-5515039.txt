{cak} Multiple flying unfamiliar objects sighted in air on clear cloudless night

1. Where were you and what were you doing at the time?
Walking out on back porch for some air at my house
2. What made you first notice the object?
Blinding light hovering in air close to ground 
3. What did you think the object was when you first noticed it?
A star, or UFO, and then it began moving
4. Describe the object and its actions and motions in detail.
After noticing the first object move, my eyes adjusted to the dark and I saw a virtual light/air show of glowing, blinking and moving objects in the sky of various shapes and sizes.
5. Describe your feelings, reactions and actions, during and after sighting the object.
I paced, stared up at the sky, checked the time (which apparently was affected - a moment after seeing the clock say 11:44, it changed to 11:56)
6. How did you lose sight of the object?
I turned my back to the objects and went inside once I started to sense fright.
