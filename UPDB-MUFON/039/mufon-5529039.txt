In the eastern sky low on the horizon there was something star like but was flashing lots of colors and it was in a location where there are normally no stars, the colors were visible to naked eye and object stayed stationary in the sky

I was walking outside and happen to notice a very bright star like object in the due eastern sky low on the horizon just over top of a ridge over the tree tops, it was a good distance away, but the colors flashing made me think it was an aircraft but it was stationary, and I knew it wasn't a star because no star or planet would be in that location and wasn't there next night. In staring at the clear sky longer I noticed 5 more of these objects,these others were much higher up in the sky stretching westward, but if you drew a line connecting the dots so to speak you would have a hexagon. So there were 6 of these in total. Each flashed the colors the same way and were visible to the naked eye. Each was stationary in the sky that night. As late as 11:30pm I was still seeing them before I finally went to sleep. Next night the 9th I did not see it anymore, none of them. Tonight I am seeing 2 of them higher in the eastern sky, also clear night.
