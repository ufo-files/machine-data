Image of a chemtrail with anomaly

As I am concerned about the environment, in particular Chemtrails I am often on the look-out for strange cloud formations etc. If it's nice weather and I'm sitting out in the garden I often take photos of the sky.

Here we have a nice expanding chemtrail which I shot back in 2014. The original frame size is 3872x2592. I was planning to send the image to a friend so I reduced the size to 1000 px for email transfer. When I zoomed in even further I noticed a Black line and thought it was dirt, but the line could not be seen on other images. I thought perhaps it was a bug or a bird as the aperture value was 1/80s @ F8.0 On closer inspection the line looked as if it was partly in the chemtrail, thus the same height as the cloud and not below it.

The image has not been edited or manipulated in any way, only slight contrast enhancement to make the chemtrail stand out a bit more.

I did not see anything at the time, so cannot give you any actual details such as direction or speed. On the original full size image this line can hardly be seen at all, only when one zooms in. Therefore it must be far away, or extremely small, and at that height and distance I doubt the camera would record a flying bug. 

Perhaps the image may be useful to you.
