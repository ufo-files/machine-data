{cak} A solid blue, reflective sphere. No apparent openings, propellors, or lights.

On Sunday,July 13th 2014, I was standing outside in my driveway talking to a relative I haven't spoken to in 22 years.My peripheral vision caught something above the treetops about two houses down. I looked up and saw a solid metallic looking blue sphere was flying above the houses and treetops in a straight line heading south right toward my direction.The object slowed down as it crossed over my street. This allowed me to get a very good look at it. It had no visible openings, propellors, and was completely silent. As I attempted to activate my phone camera, the object quickly picked up amazing speed and continued south along the same very straight path. I watched it until it disappeared from my view. It was too far away to get a picture once I was ready. I noted the time immediately it was 6:57 p.m. I immediately ran inside to tell my family what had happened. My sister and I went back outside and witnessed the strangest cloud formation either of us had ever seen. We were both moved to tears by what appeared in the photos we took. The craft wasn`t in the photos, but what the photos showed was not apparent to our naked eye. Two police cruisers came down our street right then, and one officer stopped and asked us if we had seen or heard anything strange. I didn`t mention the ufo, as something within me felt it unwise. What an amazing day..one I will never forget. I have included a photo of the strange cloud formation and double rainbow that appeared afterward, as somehow I feel that it`s pertinent.
