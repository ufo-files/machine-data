Pulsating Orange Orb

While out for an evening walk I observed a pulsating flame-orange coloured light, traveling slowly from north to south, apparently following the course of the river Nore. This orb of flight would periodically grow from a pinpoint to someone the size of your small finger held at arms length. 

Very eerie, was low, seemed to be dipping in and out of cloud. When visible below the clouds it appeared to be a light underneath an object of some type, with the light surrounded by an aura or mist. 

This was not a Chinese lantern or firework, nor was it a searchlight playing out on clouds, it grew and diminished considerably in size when moving. Not much wind on the night, visibility good, low cloud. 

Checked flightradar at the time, no flights listed within 50 miles, no stars visible due to cloudy conditions.
