bright red circle

My husband had called me on the way home from work around 10:50PM and said that he saw 7 red, circular lights in the northwest sky. I thought he was just being stupid and joking until I came outside and saw them myself, except there were only 2. They just kind of hovered in the sky and made a little triangular motions; moving left to right then upwards every now and then. They were there for maybe 5 minutes or a little less. And then each of them took off towards East and West directions. They were too fast to be any kind of human aircraft we know of. My brother-in-law lives about 2 miles down the road and my husband was talking to him while they were still there and he said that they were moving towards his direction, and saw them when they left. He said they might've been military aircraft, but there was NO noise to them at all. That's what we all couldn't understand. And with the fact that they were so fast made us question whether or not they were UFOs.
