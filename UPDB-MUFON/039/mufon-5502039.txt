{cak} strange triangular, perimeter dark shaped craft

I was walking off the porch at 8 to 8:10 p.m on April 15, 2014 and looked up and seen the airplane then saw a bright object following behind the airplane. I took one photo. I only witnessed this event for maybe 30 seconds. When I looked at the photo and zoomed in on the craft that's when I realized what I was looking at was not just a orb of light. It was a strange dark triangular, pyramid shaped craft.
