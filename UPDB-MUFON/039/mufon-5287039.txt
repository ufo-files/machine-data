Another sighting of the strange pulsating orbs/lights on way to the Treasure Island.

I was on my way to the casino again and in the exact same spot that I've witnessed these things on several occasions in the past, there they were again. At first there was only one object in the sky. Almost immediately after noticing the light in the sky, the light split into two lights. This is the first time I saw it do that. At the time, there was a partially clear sky to the NW (direction of the lights) and there was a thunderstorm to my SE that was moving out of the area. I only mention this because when I left the casino at around midnight, the sky was completely covered with clouds and the lights where still there in the exact same place. There has to be other people that have seen these lights. I really want to ask the people that live on that road what they think of the lights because they have to see them all of the time.
