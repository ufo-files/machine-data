Very fast shiny chrome aircraft no wings

I had just finished mowing my backyard and was looking it over when I looked up and in the sky and saw an extremely shiny metal aircraft (the shine is extraordinary) flying across the blue sky. At first I thought it was just an airplane but then my eyes noticed that it didn’t have any wings. I thought my mind was playing tricks on me because I couldn’t process what I was looking at. It moved so extremely fast across the sky that I couldn’t get my phone out in time to take a picture. It didn’t move through the air like an airplane does and its course didn’t seem budge at all which stood out. The shine and the way that it moved is why I am contacting Mufon.
