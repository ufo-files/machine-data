I worked on the outside with the house that night. then the girlfriend came out and talked for a while before she said, what is it? I looked up and thought it had to be a small small plane, but after a few seconds the meaning changed. I couldn't see the wings, no color and no sound. Just something that reflected the sun as if it were of shiny steel. It flew slightly lower than small planes I have seen over the same place but it made no sound. It flew in a straight line, a little closer to us and from us. I have seen over the years several types of aircraft with propeller and jet engines as well as helicopters flown over this area year round, but this one was completely different. I managed to get two pictures, but when I got on camcorder it flew over the mountain. I was sure it had to be some plane, so it took some time for me to pick up my cellphone. But this must have been the strangest plane I've ever seen. It would be fun to know what this is, my reason says a plane but it was totaly quiet. If it was a plane i would have heard the engines even if it had been idling because it was close. In the pictures it looks like it is further away and white in color, but it did not fly very high over the mountain and you could see the sun flashing throughout the hull.
