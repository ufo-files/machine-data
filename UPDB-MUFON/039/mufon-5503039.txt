Cigarette shaped object hovers over ocean then leaves at speed.

My grandma woke up as her husband went to do milking at around 0600 hrs when she witnessed a cigarette shaped object in the twilight sky. She stared at it for a while and eventually it turned and left -at speed. This was also witnessed from Onerahi (further north) and was published into the local newspaper.
