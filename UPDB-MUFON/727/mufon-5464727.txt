{tjd} Six fireballs evenly spaced with flame tail on all 6 going upward at 45% angle then peal off to left in a random pattern and disappeared behind tree line.

This incident happened Friday, November 2, 2018 at 7:30 pm in the sky over New Braunfels, Texas. My husband saw the large fireballs out of our high windows. He came quickly to me and told me to look out the window, i did and saw 6 fireballs. He went out the back door onto our deck. 
I followed him out onto the deck and we both saw the 6 large fireballs in a row then they began to roll off to the left then they scrambled. They were asending and had fire tails then they hesitated and decended behind the trees. Did not see fire tails as they were decending. We waited a while but we did not see them any more.
