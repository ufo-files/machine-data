Strange rainbow coloured patch in sky first drew my attention.

While walking my dog, had an urge to look up to the sky. Noticed high altitude coloured patch of sky, after some time this loosely formed a halo type broken coloured ring. 180 degrees to the first noticed coloured patch that first drew my attention, a mirror image patch was observed.
Grabbed my phone (S7) and took a few pics.
It was only when I got home and put the photos on my desktop that I noticed the strange object that had appeared. 
I had not seen this at the time because of the bright glare from the sun.
