{cak} Light Flew across the Sky

Driving North on Highway 127 a little after 10:00PM, no other vehicles in sight. An Eastbound light appeared up in the sky in front of me. It was visible for a couple seconds, disappeared for a half second, then reappeared for a second or so until it seemed to go over the horizon. The light was very far away. It was the same size as the stars, but it seemed like the edges were sharper. There was no tail, it wasn't like any meteor I've seen. Just a distinct light zinging across the sky. This is the only time I've seen something in the sky and immediately thought: "I just saw a UFO."
