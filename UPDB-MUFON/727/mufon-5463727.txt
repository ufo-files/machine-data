{tjd} I saw a bright GREEN baseball sized object ascending in an arc

I was driving my truck to my bar at approximately 6:35 am. I was at the intersection of Wright Street and Hull Avenue facing East when I looked up in to the sky and noticed a shooting arc of light ascending. It was at a curve. The light was solid GREEN and bright. It disappeared quickly. 

I was looking at the rooftop of the bar, which has Christmas lights on it, but were not displayed, probably because of a timer. It certainly wasn't an aircraft. I thought perhaps it was a shooting star, but the green glow was very brilliant. Perhaps a flare? This is in the city, so I didn't think a flare would be going off. 

I was startled by viewing the object. My girlfriend was in the passenger seat, but didn't see it. She wasn't looking in the direction. I was turning North onto Wright Street to pull into the bar's back parking lot. It descended into an arc and the green light was opaque and bright. It lasted a few seconds. 

I lost sight when it seemed to disappear or disintegrate. It reminded me of a falling star, but the color was green.
