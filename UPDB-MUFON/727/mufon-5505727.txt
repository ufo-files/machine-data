{rjl} 4 lights in same path started red in color ending white and then disappeared.

My wife and I were sitting on the porch of our house facing west when from out of the NE sky we saw 2 lights one apparently on the same path going SW and about 15 seconds later 2 more lights/fireballs followed the same path. It was probably about 1 min from the time we saw the first lights that started red in color changing to orange, yellow, then blue to white and finally disappeared. They flew in a mostly straight direction but seamed to change to more westerly towards the end where they disappeared. Did not seem like other meteors I have seen, they did not seem to loose elevation, like they were flying in the air rather than falling. I have never seen anything like this except on tv/internet videos. I would love to know what they were.
