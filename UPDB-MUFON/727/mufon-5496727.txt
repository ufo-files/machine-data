Traveling to work with my partner by car.noticed a very bright star.kept watching as it seemed to move slowly heading south on my route to work.observed it continuing same direction and then it stopped and changed shape and direction.

On Tuesday morning 13th January I left home for work with my partner at 7.30am.i travel same route daily at around same time.we had started our journey coming out of our estate in where we live in walls end Newcastle upon Tyne.we drove down Broomfield avenue heading toward the coast road when I noticed a very bright star to my right.it looked a bit bigger than the other stars around but a lot brighter.we adjoined the coast road heading south toward the1058 which takes you to Newcastle town centre.i looked at the star again and thought that looks as though it is moving.i asked my partner to have a look and if she thought it was moving too and she replied it's probably a helicopter or an aeroplane.we continued watching it as we travelled we merged onto the 1058 and could see that the star was actually heading the same way but also followed the same contours of the dual carriage way.we passed the junction at benfield road and noticed the star looked as though it was getting bigger.we were travelling at roughly 10 to 15mph having to stop for the other traffic in front of us as this road is usually very busy.we were approaching a set of speed cameras near the next junction to chilling ham road when the star stopped in mid air.we were both sure it was a helicopter as it resembled the same sort of movement as it hovered over the main road ahead.we were stationary behind traffic when the star started to get bigger,it then looked like a big white diamond with hazy edges.on each end I noticed lights faintly blinking.one was green on one side the other red like an aeroplane.it stayed there motionless as it seemed to be observing the traffic and surroundings.it then suddenly started to move heading back toward our direction which would of been north.i asked my partner to try and get a photo of it but she did not have her phone on.i said I've got mine try and get a picture of it.the traffic started to move again as I struggled to get my phone from my pocket.  The diamond came closer but looked to stay the same size and then emerged to be an aeroplane which was around 2 to 5maybe hundred feet up. My partner couldn't get my video on my phone to work as I was saying get a photo come on quick before we move on.the aeroplane stopped motionless again,I was looking out of my screen and could only view the under belly of the craft and noticed it had about five dome shaped round lights which were quite big and never see on other aeroplanes.they were a creamy white and had a strange glow to them.the traffic started to move again and my partner never got the chance to take a photo as the craft started to move off slowly getting out of view as we had to speed up and carry on our way.my partner gave up trying to get a photo and said that she wasn't quick enough.i focused on the road ahead as she said that the aeroplane was still there in the distance.we arrived at work and talked to our work colleagues of what we had saw. My partner said she thought it looked like a big red orange fire ball a total different opinion to what I saw which is very strange.
