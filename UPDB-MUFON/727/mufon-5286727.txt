Glowing fluorescent red triangle with rounded points traveling silently from west to east over Kenora On.

Standing in my backyard having a cigarette just after midnight when I saw a glowing fluorescent red triangle with rounded points flying from west to east high in the atmosphere and omitted no sound. From my perspective the ufo appeared to be the size of a penny at arms length, did not blink or pulse and did not change direction.
