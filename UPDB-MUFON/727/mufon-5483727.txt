Over Building I Saw A Triangle Object flying

At 24th April 2016, I and my friend we are going to our homes. The time is 22:15 at night (GMT +2 GR Timezone). In a moment when I saw up to the sky, one Triangle object passed over building. At this moment I think that it is a Military Vehicle. The Uknown Flying Object had a Triangle Shape. Two sides of this object had lights both (have a lot of lamps in both two sides). Only one side don't have light. The only I can saw that it going straight. When I saw the object, I was feel a bit nervous and I tell to my friend and they don't believe and when I arrive to my home, I told the event to my parents. I missed to take my smartphone to take a snapshot because the object was moved a bit fast. When it was passed the building I was loose it because the other buildings it's a bit taller.
