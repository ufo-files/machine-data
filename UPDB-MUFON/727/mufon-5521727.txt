Stationary Fireball in Sky two days in a row

I went to meet my parents for dinner in Clermont and am from Kissimmee. Last night my Dad meet some neighbors on a hill to watch all the fireworks from the theme parks and all saw several orange fireballs in the sky almost above them around the same distance a jet would be. Ive seen some UFOs before (Zig Zag across the sky and crazy bright lights) so I believed him and am mindful of it. Before I left I looked things up on Twitter and YouTube and saw a small response to what he saw last night, with a few videos posted and posts on twitter about it. When I left it was dusk and saw a bright orange looking fireball to the east, to me it looked around the downtown area or past it, but it was low and the trees easily blocked it until I was up on a hill. As I continued to drive down 27 to reach 192 I saw it a few more times above the trees. I stopped once and got out Google Sky and checked, it was not a star, and no other stars were visible along the same level. I may have also saw another one to the south-west, but wasnt sure and couldnt look in two directions at once while driving. Compared to the photos I saw on twitter/videos on youtube, it is a similar object in the sky.
