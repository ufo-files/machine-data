Strange object seen during lunchtime floating around in the sky

So yesterday, my boyfriend and I were pulling up to our place, it was about 12:45 and I saw this...i dont know this object, to the south of us. I went to third floor of our building to get a better view and It continued to gradually move west, changing from solid black to very very bright, like it was catching the reflection of the sun. The sun was so bright i couldn’t see my screen to keep it in focus. After i took the video, I saw TWO of the same thing! The other was to the right and higher than the original object. I ran inside my place to grab my sunglasses but then it was too late, i couldn’t find them again. I’m sorry that the video is mostly junk, I’ve just never seen anything like that.
