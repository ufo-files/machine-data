Elongated, elliptical object pale silver-platinum color flying west to east at what appeared to be a high altitude.

North Dayton, Ohio, 8:59 pm EDT July 5, 2020. 88 degrees F. Sparse clouds to southwest, northwest and east; rest of sky clear, no cloud cover. Very light wind, 2-3 mph est. at ground level. 

Taking photos of cloud formations at sunset using iPhone and facing southwest; turned camera straight up to take a photo of the sky above me when noticed small object moving smoothly and rapidly west to east in left peripheral vision. Assumed a plane, attempted to record it using iPhone camera but do not believe it showed up on either the video or still images I attempted to take of it. The video attached shows me panning around the sky trying to find it.

Object remained a solid, opaque, highly-shined metallic pale silver-platinum color — or else reflected sunlight and appeared that color. An elongated elliptical shape solid in appearance without any lights or other observable features. 

Alternately watched it and tried to photograph it as it continued course. 

It appeared to me as an amateur observer to be quite large and moving rapidly at a very high altitude. I watched it for approximately 60 seconds before it was no longer visible to me.

Cell phone uncharacteristically froze multiple times just after sighting.
