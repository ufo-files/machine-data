At the time of sighting I was driving my van, and first noticed a very bright and blazing blue light that crossed the road I was driving at about 3 kms from me and approx. 100 feet high and about travelling approx. 80kph.
I first thought it was a light plane going to attempt an emergency landing.
It then suddenly veered to its left and was flying towards me but now over a field adjoining the road and getting lower all the time to the ground.
I stopped the car, grabbed my mobile hone and was prepared to dial emergency 000.
As it flew past my vehicle I could clearly see its shape, the body of a small aircraft but no wings. The blue light on the front was still blazing brightly but no other lights.
When in full view it appeared to turn hazy & it was hard to see whether the color was black or dark grey. It was a full moon which was behind the craft.
I was quite relaxed and not nervous during the entire sighting which totaled about 6 minutes.
As it passed by (no more than 100 feet from my position) I alighted from my vehicle, went around the back of it and looked to the sky where the object appeared to go. But there was not s sign of a anything in the entire sky.
I drove home and told myself it had not happened for 48 hours and got on with my daily routine.
After that time I wanted to tell everyone in the world what I saw.
