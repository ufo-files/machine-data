Sighting when I was 10

I was 10 when my mom and I went to pickup my older brother from work as we weregoing home I saw a light in the sky it approached our car and followed usstaying about 100 to 150 ft. behind and above us. When we apprached traffic itmoveed away and when we left the populated areas it returned. It was round andit had a bulge on top it was shaped like a derby hat it had lights on the edgeand windows on the bulge.
