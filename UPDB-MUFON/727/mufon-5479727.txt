{nd} white orb flaming across sky then separates into 3 orbs and hovers

I was walking my dog outside just after 6:00 pm and saw something out of the corner of my eye and looks directly upwards. I then noticed a bright white teardrop shaped flame/glow moving from East to West pretty slowly. Once I realized I had no idea what this was I got my phone out and recorded a short video of the light moving across the sky. When the light moved behind the trees, I stopped the video and ran to the other side of them along the road, taking about 15-20 seconds, filming the second video where the white orb had turned into three stationary orbs that were continuously glowing. These three lights then seemed to slowly fade out of sight, i am not sure if they were getting closer to the ground or just out of visual range.
