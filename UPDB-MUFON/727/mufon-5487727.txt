{jpg} Seen two bright lights hovering in the sky

My fianc1 and I were heading north on I-57 north in the Country Club Hills town of Illinois at about 430 am. First we noticed the moon was to the west and was very big and extremely yellow in color. Then we noticed two bright lights in the sky to the east. They were very bright and pretty close together. They didn't look far enough to be stars. They seemed to be helicopters at first guess. But we don't believe that to be the case. They just seemed different. One was bigger and brighter than the other. I started to record the occurrence on my phone. After watching the video I noticed orbs surrounding the objects. The objects didn't seem to be moving at all. They just sat there hovering way up in the sky. We kept driving to our house so we lost sight and never observed a flight pattern or the objects leave the sky. In the video towards the end there is a glimpse of the big yellow moon.
