{rjl} Object flew directly overhead, no wings, blue and white lights up front, shine on underbelly, afterburner-like effect to the rear without the sound or varied intensity or uneven light effect.

I had taken my dog out for his nightly potty break (about 9:15 pm) when I noticed a very low flying "plane" with intense lights heading directly towards me form the south. As I watched it get closer, it became obvious that there were no wings. It was not silent but instead emitted a low-toned, very steady hum. There were no windows present, no landing gear or wheel wells that I could make out. The best I could describe it would be to say it was a military-like jet without wings, landing gear, sound, etc. The rear resembled dual, very intense afterburners, although there was no fluctuation in light, such as bleed-off. Also missing, from an afterburner sense, was the obvious lack of noise and the lack of fire/heat release. I also have trouble calling them simply lights, as they seemed more like a power source. The underbelly was a dull grey and was brightened somewhat (maybe from glow from the front lights). The craft continued north for around another 10 seconds or so before turning northwest, with no obvious tilt, as one would expect from an airplane. The craft was slender in form and looked to be approximately 50 feet long. What struck me as odd is that I didn't think about what I saw until the day after and that I went to bed as if nothing out of the norm had occurred. In fact, I remember dismissing it so fast that I didn't wait to see it disappear out of sight. It shifted northwest and I simply went inside.
