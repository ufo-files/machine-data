As passenger in BA 0965 between Amsterdam and Clacton and over North Sea, fast moving star below other aircraft

I am both Pilot and Aeronautical Engineer, and work in the Space and aviation industry. Often I have observed traffic in the same airspace, therefore I know of the affects of light.

I was flying as a passenger in window seat 14F travelling from Hamburg to London Heathrow airport. Over the North Sea, mid way between Amsterdam and Clacton upon Sea, a bright pin prick of special light (10 x brighter than a star or satellite), was seen proceeding in a exact opposite trajectory at constant speed and at a altitude of 36,000 feet the same at BA965. It was a pure crystal light, like seen in a prism or glass. Since it was mid way over the sea, I quoted UK time.

There were many other aircraft higher and lower in the same airspace, going different directions, but this object was flying faster. It was definitely not a aircraft or other flight vehicle, or their lights. The best description would be a bright star, but with a pure crystal or diamond quality of light flying in a straight line at high speed.

I would even suggest that the UFO made the effort to mesh in with other traffic in the airways, over the North Sea in order not to conflict with other traffic. 

I am 200% sure it was not a aircraft, or anything from this world.

I would like to suggest that many other Pilots and passengers would have seen this UFO
