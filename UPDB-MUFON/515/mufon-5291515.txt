Rapidly descending light

I was travelling west towards Hudson WI from my house in Troy. I noticed a rapidly descending point of light in the direction of the MSP Airport; I often see aircraft in the area, but this was moving very quickly and in an unusual path. The light stopped and suddenly split with 2 additional lights moving away from the central light, which remained stationary. I immediately called my wife and told her about what I was seeing. I watched them travel until I reached my destination. I came out 5 minutes later and could not see the lights anymore. The lights appeared to be very far away, likely beyond MSP Airport, but I was unable to tell. I originally thought it was a plane, but it travelled in a very strange way.
