{dr}GLOWING YELLOW/ORANGE SPHERE

I was outside star gazing when I noticed a glowing yellow-orange sphere like object traveling at a high rate of speed from the NE to the SW. I originally thought it was a the ISS. Then realized it wasnt due to speed and color. I was naturally in awe because I had never seen anything like it. the object appeared to increase in color brightness and altitude during its path SW, then disappeared into the black of night
