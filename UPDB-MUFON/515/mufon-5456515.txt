{cak} White pulsating thing

West southwest there is something in the sky. I first saw it at 6:05 and I thought it was a really big, bright star. It is bright yellow white and undulates and flickers wildly. I used my sky map app to see if it was venus and there was nothing there on the map. This object is silent. It isn't coming from space. It's close.
