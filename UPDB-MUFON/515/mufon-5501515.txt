{dr}Observed while driving past Argon national lab. The object was roughly above the labs property

On the morning of July 1st, 2014 I was heading north on Cass Ave in Willowbrook Illinois to get on the highway to go to work. At the time I was driving past the property that Argon National Lab is located on. I first noticed the object because it was reflecting the sunlight in a way that seemed to make it glitter; easily visible against the clear blue sky. At first I thought the object was a plane but thought it was strange that it was reflecting light in the way it was. Upon further observation, I determined that the object didn't seem to be following any sort of linear path, but instead seemed to be maintaining its position for the entire duration of the observation. The object appeared to be a thin elliptical shape that was spinning along its horizontal axis. This is not my first time witnessing a strange object like this, however it is my first time seeing something like this by myself. I wasn't scared but instead felt curious and wanted to keep watching it to see what it did. After watching it for less than a minute, I decided to call a family member and tell them what I was seeing. Almost as soon as I had that thought, the object instantly vanished from sight leaving no trace that it had been there.
