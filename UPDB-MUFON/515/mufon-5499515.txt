{nd} Red orb flashing lights

Wife and I witnessed a single red orb with white pulsating lights. White lights appeared to revolve around the red light. The object looked the same color as the 12 or so red orbs that I witnessed last November. I was hesitant to pay further attention as I saw white lights pulsing to a slow rhythm. I assumed passenger jet at first. I continued to watch because the object appeared stationary. The rhythm of white lights increased sort of randomly and over the course of about five minutes the rhythm had increased to a constant glitter. The object stopped for about two minutes and hovered over the Oakdale area (again). It was heading slowly towards the WNW until it disappeared over the horizon. I took a video. Haven't checked it. Likely too far and too dim but I'll post back if interesting apart from the commentary : )
