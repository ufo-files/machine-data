On back porch with spouse hoping to see fireworks. Noted bright star through palm frond. Then saw red light and thought it was a plane. Observed red light circling white several times. Then red light took off quickly to the right, returned to white light, circled it again, then off to the left. White light moved some, but not as much as red. Red then seemed to head west to horizon and disappeared. White one then turned red and appeared to have bluish center. It moved back and forth across the sky, then to the horizon. It came back two more times, but alone both times. Spouse and I both tried to get video on our phones, but very difficult. I was nervous because my phone has a light and I didn't want to be noticed by these possible ships. Also, at one point the second ship appeared to change shape, it almost looked like it had wings with the red light outlining them and it appeared to be floating gently like a parachute. Each time this would disappear, it was to the west.I realized the color scheme sounds coincidental, but that is what we both saw, and it was not fireworks. There was no airplane or jet noise. Once again, apologies for poor video quality. And, yes, we know there is a big military presence here, but this seemed very out of the ordinary. Thanks, hope you hear from other people and this helps you.
