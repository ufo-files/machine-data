Blue light

My husband and I were traveling from Mission Viejo to Kake Elsinore at about midnight on Hwy 74. We first noticed blue light about 50 feet to our north through the trees, possibly on the ground. As we continued, the light seemed to be pacing us, and moving closer to the highway. After about 5 miles, we pulled over, turned off the engine and opened the windows. And heard absolutely nothing. We had thought it might be a helicopter. The light was moving closer and then we were spotlighted. That spooked us and we took off and sped toward Lake Elsinore. We were paced by the light for miles. Just before the overlook the light disappeared. A few miles later we were in Lake Elsinore. We did not see the light again.
