{rjl} Black triangle with a red and green light on either

I was driving from my dad's house to my mom's house. When I got very near my mom's house I saw a bright orange light over some condos. Maybe a couple thousand feet above it there was a fainter yellow light. As I drove a bit more I had to stop in the middle of the street and watch. It was moving very very slowly and almost right towards my car. I was too in shock to get my camera. As it got closer I noticed that there were also green and red lights on this thing. I figured it was a drone but it kept getting bigger as it got closer. It must have been 100 feet across which is not a drone. It was triangular with a couple of pointed edges on the back. It made a faint humming noise as it passed and I just watched it until I couldn't see it anymore. My estimation for speed is about 40 mph.
