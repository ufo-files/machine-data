2 white objects seen together leaving each other

My daughter said to me to come look at this and when I did there were 2 white objects horizontally eclipsing each other, at the moment sight was achieved they left each other and moved away from each other in a completely straight flight pattern away from each other. We kept sight of them until another one showed up from original point but only one this time going in the same direction as one of the others. By the time it was out of sight another spawned again from that point but going opposite of previous. Best way to describe it is a slightly rounded rectangular object that didn't have external lights but illuminated bright white with a slight break in the middle. No emissions and absolutely no break in it's slow flight patterns. I know it wasn't a drone nor plane, this was something I've never seen before in my life. I told my daughter (7) to get my phone but she only got mommy who was half asleep (note, it was day time out).
