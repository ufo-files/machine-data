Star-like object in Nasca

The sky was clear. I was in Majoro in the field, near the airport, with my dog, when I saw a star-like object passing from West to South East. And I said to myself: "this is another ufo pretending to be star" (because in Nasca we see star-like objects without movement that suddenly start to fly, and also the contrary, star-like objects flying and then remaining without moving in one place). And this time I decided to follow it with my eyes. It was as if they were reading my mind because at this moment it changed the color to yellow-amber, and continued to fly in the same direction until it was not visible.

Past week I was with my sisters and saw another object more glowing one glowing passing South East to North West, also in Majoro
