{jpg} Many oscillating lights, fast up and down motions, took off west to east under power-no sounds. I'm retired LE and a former MUFON member.

Saw craft hovering due west of Hot Springs. It was emmitting green, white, yellow red and blue pulsating lights. Initally it was hovering but then it began almost a dance up and down at incredible speeds before going back to a hovering pattern. After 20 minutes the craft under control moved from the western sky to the east. It seemed that during this movement the lights were mainly red and white with the other colors not present. We then lost sight of the craft behind trees on the eastern horizon. About 5 minutes later that craft, or another one, was seen in the western sky in the exact same location as the initial sighting. We lost sight of it shortly, possibly due to cloud cover.
