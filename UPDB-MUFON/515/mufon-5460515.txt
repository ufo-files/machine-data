Saw a light brighter than Venus moving from West to East north of the road. No flashing airplane lights, too bright to be a satellite and it didn't fade. I filmed it in comparison to stars, much brighter and larger, and passing behind shadowy trees. It's probably one line of trees there so you could see it beyond them. Smelled a strong, musky smell, like what I think is an elk smell, whether that's right or not, however stronger, so I stepped closer to my vehicle. Had seen a full grown grizzly bear on the road miles before, and filmed that with my cell phone. Had time to experiment with three cameras, finally filmed it. I've seen this before around the area on several occasions around twilight or later, it was past twilight. Stars werent full intensity, but visible. You have permission to reprint this, however I can't grant all rights to video. Saw another brighter one towards 1 AM, however it wasn't visible as long, I wasn't on a mountain at the time, and I was tired. First one looked like the moon color, just larger than Venus. The second looked like a blue white bollide, too slow for a shooting star though. Not as good an observer or at filming for the second one, too tired. Different location, same region. Not sure if the first one changed direction slightly, I was concentrating on filming. None of the abrupt movements I've seen a few times elsewhere.
