{ck} UFO fly over while waiting for fireworks show

While me and girlfriend waited for fireworks show in Meridian from our street we observed from the North below high clouds comes a fiery orange round light that randomly grew/diminished in intensity moving at a steady pace, maybe that of a helicopter and generally around the same height they fly if they are local. As it flew directly overhead noticed no sound at all from object. Object was not "floating" as a balloon, not being "carried by wind" but on a direct heading south. As the object headed away the light either diminished in intensity or was no longer visible as much from the rear of the body of the craft an we observed a dark grey or black body to the craft. The shape was hard to pin down as the edges were not smooth like a disc or egg. It was kind of squarish in shape but with protrusions round about. We watched it continually from start to finish maybe 4-6 minutes until it decreased in size in the far away dusk sky.
