Orange ball decending slowly and diagonally in the southern sky

At 11:03 my husband texted me to come outside and see something. It took me a minute to get outside. I observed the orange ball moving slowly across the sky, diagnoally, from about 45 degrees to about 10 degrees. It was at least 4 times larger than mars, which was also visible in the southern sky that night. I was able to observe it for another minute or two. From where my husband said he first observed it to where it shrunk and faded out was about the length of my hand, with fingers spread out. I looked down at my cell phone to check the time that it disappeared at 11:06. It was red-orange, had no trail, and only shrunk and faded in the last second that it was visible. It seemed to move slowly compared to a meteor, which I have observed before. It was bigger than anything I have ever seen move across the sky and prominently colored quite differently. It seemed very far away, past the mountains visible from where I was standing. It passed behind a tree that is at least 100 yard away from us. I repositioned myself and was able to keep observing it until it disappeared.
