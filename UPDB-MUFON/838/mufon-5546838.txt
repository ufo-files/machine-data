CIGAR SHAPED OBJECT SEEN IN SKY ON A CLEAR NIGHT

WHEN LEAVING THE ADDRESS LISTED ABOVE WITH MY SIGNIFICANT OTHER AS A WITNESS TO THIS SIGHTING, LOOKING UP IN THE SKY AS I ALWAYS DO I SAW A CIGAR SHAPED SHINNY OBJECT . WITH NO WINGS. IT TRAVELED FROM THE SOUTH TO THE NORTH AT A VERY SLOW PACE. THERE WAS NO SOUND, THE OBJECT APPEARED LOW ENOUGH FOR US TO HEAR ANY NOISE HOWEVER THERE WAS NO SOUND.AFTER THREE MINUTES THE CIGAR SHAPED OBJECT DISAPPEARED. WE FELT EXCITED UPON VIEWING THE OBJECT. DISAPPOINTED WE DID NOT HAVE TIME TO TAKE A PICTURE OF WHAT WE OBSERVED.
