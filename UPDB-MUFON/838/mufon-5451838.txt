Not a Firework - Yellow Orb flashed lighting up the sky

There are many fireworks in the sky these days near our home in Huntington Beach. My fiancé and I were sitting together with one of our dogs - I was facing south and she was facing East. 

Out of the corner of my eye I saw a bright flash so I kind of braced myself for a large boom that I was expecting to follow. But I didn’t here the boom so I got nervous and remained quiet. 

After a minute or so of neither one of us talking. She asked if I saw that. I immediately said yes and it scared the hell out of me. I asked what she saw and she said it was a perfect bright circle that flashed and went away. 

This flash was not a firework as it lit up the entire sky without any noise. I would call it a flash as it was gone in an instant. I recall a bright orange/yellow light and she recalls a bright white/yellow perfect circle. She said it resembled the sun. 

We continued to look where the flash came from with no additional sighting. She didn’t see what appeared to be a black cloud for a moment after the flash but I could not see the dark cloud. Our dog did not seem phased by the flash, but there was a near by dog that started barking it’s head off immediately after the flash and the dog did not stop barking. 

I checked with others in the area but I have not found anyone else that saw the flash. I went on twitter to see if anyone else reported the incident but didn’t find anything from the event. I did however find an older twitter post from Oklahoma that captured something similar if not identical to what we saw.
