{cak} Round Metallic Ball stationary then spend away small vapor trail no sound speed of light

In September 2001 I had just finished building our new home and had just had the gutters installed. It was a Saturday morning around 10 to 10:30am our house faces East, the gutter man and his nephew had come to collect his money. He was sitting in his truck writing out a receipt, I was standing to the left of his open truck door and his nephew was standing to my left he and I facing west were looking that my house. The sun was at my back and there was not a cloud in the sky and the sky was a dark blue. The object was observed floating and it was a large round metallic ball. It seemed to have lines (maybe windows) it then hit the throttle and and sped away leaving a small vapor trail that instantly evaporated, estimated speed Northern California to Alaska 2-3 seconds It's size was around 2 to 3 large houses as the object sped away, I turned to my left and said "Did you see that" to the nephew, he had an amazed look on his face with his mouth open. He could not speak, he only shook his head YES. The object made no noise! When I first saw the object I could not believe my eyes, I thought it was a movie or a cartoon that was happening. This event has haunted me for years, I did not know who to report it to. After seeing MUFON on TV, I'm glad I can report this to someone and get this off my mind
