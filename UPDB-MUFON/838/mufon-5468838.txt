{jpg} 2 lights in sky. With binoculars; I saw them change like kaledidescope lights

Awakened by neighborhood animals & my dog in house . Dogs were barking, cows mooing, donkeys bellowing. Took our dog outside to see what was going on & saw 2 bright lights. We went back inside to get binoculars & saw the 2 lights side by side with binoculars. The light on the right was changing colors in a sphere like a kaleidoscope. The light on left only changed to bright white. They flickered & danced for about an hour. We got creeped out & went back to bed.
