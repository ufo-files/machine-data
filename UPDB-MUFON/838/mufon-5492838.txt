{dr}Seen red ball come over house, descend about tree level than go over house across street

Lived in two story house, blinds pulled up. I was standing up looking out window when I seen this red ball come over the house descented about tree level traveled across the street and then flew up and over the house across the street and disappear into the night. I ran down stairs, told my room mate to come outside because I just seen something that was amazing. She wanted to know what it was. I told her it was a red ball that came over the house than shot over the house across the street. I went outside because I thought something might be following it. I stay outside about a half hour, but, nothing. I was excited and scared at the same time. It did remind me of something I seen years before. In 2013 I seen something at night in the sky, about 11:00 pm in Fullerton, Ca. Where I now live, it was a white light traveling north. It had no flashing light like a plane, just a white dot going across the sky. As I was following it I notice this wave like thing waving like a flag, however it was clear but I could see the sky waving where it was at, almost square like. When the light came to it both the light and the object disappeared. It seem like it was a mother ship waiting for the other craft to arrive so they could depart, which they did. I told my family but they said I was crazy.
