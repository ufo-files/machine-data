{jpg} Bright light is merged with smaller light and disappear.

Saw what I thought was an airplane at cruising altitude coming from the south towards the southeast. Except, there were no blinking airplane type of lights. It was solid white. It suddenly stopped and another white light appeared. This light was not initially noticeable. It just 'turned on'. The second light merged into the first primary light and then it flew away.....VERY quickly. I've never seen anything disappear that fast.
