sighted at fraser island while looking at the stars

At the time of the sighting we were looking at the stars on the deck of a holiday house on the back beach of fraser island. We first saw a very bright light hovering it seemed. So i ran inside and grabbed the camera. By this time it had gotten closer and was changing colours around the edge. It then ejected 3 smaller rounded objects which took off at such speed from it. It travelled probably a few kilometers and returned to the object that it had come from. Then it seemed to have just vanished. Never thought much about it as we have had synical responses from people. But i believe what i saw and photo graphed. These photographs were taken over the ocean on the eastern side of fraser island . Ive unfortunatly lost the negatives and have just recently found the photos again. So i am hoping for some indentification to what i saw. Thankyou.
