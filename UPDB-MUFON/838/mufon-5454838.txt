2 white lights/objects side by side

Unsure if it was a transparent object with 2 white lights, or reflecting the sunlight at 2 spots. OR if it was 2 white objects/lights flying side by side. As they/it flew NE, the "lights" got closer together and disappeared in the distance.

Unknown how high the object(s) was/were above. 

I thought it may be birds, but there was no flapping, they seemed too high for birds, and there were only 2. Then I thought perhaps they were white jets. 

Flight path was consistent speed, no vapour trail(s), no "flapping" like a bird. 
Very white in colour, like a star, no flashing, consistent colour. Went across the sky in about 10 seconds.

It was a clear blue-sky sunny morning at 10:30am March 9 2020 - flew over Hillsburgh, Ontario SW-W to NE. Was not a direction I would usually see a plane fly.

A few minutes later I heard what sounded like an aircraft. I looked to the SW and there was a plane flying SE to NW. I assumed the sound was coming from the Aircraft, not the UFO I saw earlier.
