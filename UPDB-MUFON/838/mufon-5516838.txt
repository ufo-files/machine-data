{dr}Glowing blood red orb with orange at bottom

Driving near my house on horizon above tree line on hill Glowing blood red orb with orange at bottom. The object flew straight for about 45 seconds made about a 10 degree turn to the left and started to climb toward the cloud deck. It was still below the cloud deck and it started to fade then disapeared before reaching the cloud deck. There was no sun light at this time and no marked lights that a plane would have. I saw the object and pulled off the road and my wife and I watched it for about two minutes until it disapeared. It seemed to be flying from south to north along the direction of state route 88 in Pittsburgh.
