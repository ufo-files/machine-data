{nd} Four flashing lights in the sky, equidistant to one another, on Allentown/ Bethlehem border

Was leaving a friends house this evening at about 5:22 pm. We were in the front yard facing the West. My roommate noticed four objects high in the sky, and they moved, nearly overhead. At first I thought they were planes, but they remained equidistant from one-another, and moved in a single file. They did not have lights like an airplane, nor could I discern any shape, just that they had brighter-than-starlight flashes of light that seemed to flicker yellow/orange. They moved West to East, and we observed them for several minutes. The neighbors pulled in a few doors down, and exclaimed at seeing them, as well. They had been tracking them on the drive home. The objects seemed to disappear one by one, one was still barely visible behind a cloud when I got into the car. We could no longer see them when I drove away at 5:27. The objects moved fairly steadily, similar to planes moving at a high altitude.
