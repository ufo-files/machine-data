We were sitting outside at out picnic table talking and watching a couple of airplains flying by when we noticed the objects flying through the sky, they did not look like airplanes because they did not have blinking lights like the airplains we were watching had. The first one we saw was going from north to south, flying about the same speed as an airplain but it looked like a brite white ball of light, and we could see an airplain at the same time. Thats how we knew their was a difference. As the object got to a certain point south of us it dissapeared. The second object was coming from north northeast going south southeast at the same rate of speed , but this one was a ball of red light. The third and fourth ones we seen looked like the first one and heading in the same direction north to south, and it was a bright white light like the first one. They too also dissapeared when they reached a certin point.
