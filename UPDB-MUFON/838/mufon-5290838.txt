Color Changing Rainbow Light in Night Sky

Out walking my dog around 8:30pm. Looked up and noticed a bright light behind some clouds. Initially thought this light was the moon and clouds were just passing over it. The light was blue and that was what was confusing to me (determined it wasn’t the moon at this time). I stopped and then saw that the light was changing colors. This light was stationary and was visible for over 1/2 hour. The light was not close to any tall buildings or bridges but was just on its own pulsating a rainbow of colors. No noise. No indication that it was being projected. Was high up in the sky which made me think it was definitely something in the atmosphere.
