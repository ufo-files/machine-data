I was using the bathroom when lightning caught my eye through the window shade. So i opened the window shade. Then looked out the window facing west, when a green ball of light caught my attention. I could not identify the green ball of light. I saw the ball of light hovering over large a metal building. Approx 1/4mile away. I watched it hover left to right over building in a north to south direction. Then the ball of light rose up above building then back level with the building, hovering almost ground level in a north to south direction. I was memorized by the strange weird green ball of light. Ball of light was visible for approx 20 seconds. Finally the green ball of light faded away suddenly and was gone.
