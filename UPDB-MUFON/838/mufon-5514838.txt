(GS) two small objects at high altitude closely following each other

It was 7:33 P.M. I had gone out on to my deck to observe the space shuttle go by when i noticed two lights at high altitude following one another. at first i thought it could be satellites but then the one in the rear began to glow and pulsate very brightly.I continued watching until they were out of sight about 1 minute. then the shuttle went by.these objects appeared to be higher than the shuttle.
