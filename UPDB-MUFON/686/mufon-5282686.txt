What looked like burning orbs of fire moving from south to north

Witness what appeared to be glowing reddish/orange orbs moving in a straight line across the night sky from south to north. the orbs would appear one at a time from the south and follow one behind the other heading in a straight line to the north. Some of the orbs would speed up and pass some other orbs that had become stationary. As the orbs moved on they would fade out and disappear. At the time of the event an airplane past through the flight path of the orbs and kept going. When the orbs were overhead they looked to be on fire like a fireball. We witnessed at least 15 of these orbs over an approximate 20 minute time span.
