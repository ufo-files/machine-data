{nd} Hills of Austin, Tx

My mother had just put me to bed. My aunts and uncles were all at a cabin we went to every year. My mom and them were having a a seance, I overheard. I was in bed for a while when I got scared. I got up and was going to get my mom right next door. When I opened the door I saw multi- colored lights hoovering over hills in the distance. I actually froze. I was terrified, I stood there just staring at it. I was too scared to move. It just hovered.Made no sound. I don't remember what gave me the courage, but I finally ran to get my mom, tried to get her to come look at it real fast, but, of course she didn't believe me and took her time going outside and didn't see it.Unfortunately I did not see it leave. Now much older regret not waiting.
