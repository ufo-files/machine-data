{rjl} Saw orangish/red ball, it was dark outside watch for a few minutes til disappeared over the power lines

I was in the house watching T.V. with my family. My youngestsister Lucy, went outside to make sure all the animals were locked up for the night. She came running in the house, screaming. Dad, dad, there's a orange ball in the sky. 7 family members ran out to look at the orange ball in the sky. My grandmother who was living with us at the time. Who was die hard Catholic. Started screaming, that it had something to do with the devil and ran back into the house. We didn't own any binoculars. So, my dad ask lucy to go get the rifle that had a scope on it, so he could look at the object. The orange ball dissappeared and then a form started taking shape. It looked like a saucer with yellowish windows around it. It was following the power lines, which crossed in front of our house. At that time my dad was still looking through the rifle scope and I started yelling shoot it, shoot it. We watched it go through the neighbors field hooveing over the power lines. The power line goes over the mountain and it followed the power line over the mountain and couldn't see it anymore. My dad told us not to talk to anyone about this, because he thought everyone would think our family was crazy. Out of 7 of us that witness this. There are only 3 of us still a live today. Myself and my to youngest sisters. I called both of them, to make sure it was alright with them to contact your organization.
