Was watching some small cloud formations when I noticed a strange object hovering that appeared to be standing still. I got my wife and friend and our son to look at it when my friend ran to get binoculars and a telescope. We begin studying the object as it changed from what appeared to be a cicular object but it changed to a triangle shape then to a square with what looked like 4 cicular shapes within inside. We notified WVLT about the object and emailed them pictures of the object. After about an hour and a half the object slowly disappeared over the horizon.
