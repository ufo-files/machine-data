Equilateral Triangle dark grey, no lights at extreme altitude.

Equilateral Triangle dark grey, no sound and no lights at extreme altitude. I would estimate it was as high as any cruising commercial jet and maybe even higher. It was huge. I would think up to 1,000 feet across, but as with the altitude, it's hard to say exactly how big because of the distance involved. It made an instantaneous 30 degree change in direction after flying on a straight path, then continued to fly straight until it was out of sight. The whole sighting lasted only about one minute. It was a very clear night and stars and planets were visible as well as air traffic that is common in the area, the Vancouver International Airport (YVR) is close by. Unfortunately I did not have the opportunity to get a photograph, my cell phone was in the house and my camera as well at the time of the sighting. All I can say is I have no idea what it was and that I never expected that I would see anything like that in my life. I was just out looking at the stars on a warm summer evening.
