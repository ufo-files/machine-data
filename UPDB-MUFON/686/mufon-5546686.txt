saw disc hoovering in sky emitting lights from the center of disc approx. 200 feet in air

Myself,my brother and my cousin went for a walk about 10:30 pm on approx June 10th 1973, after a short walk we noticed a disc hoovering approx. 200 feet above us and about 100 feet in diameter emitting blue, yellow and pink lights from the center of the disc, with an approx diameter of the lights being 20 feet. All three of us starred for several seconds when suddenly the disc shot stright up disapearing in the sky. The disc was silent with no wind or sound coming from it. We believe this UFO traveled faster than the speed of light.
