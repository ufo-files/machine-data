Saw 5 objects in the sky. Solid red lights. They had an odd formation. No sound. Moved up and down before disappearing into the clouds.

I was baking cookies with my son. I then stepped outside for a cigarette. I sat down on my step and then noticed bright red lights in the sky. At first I thought they were planes but then quickly realized they were way to close together to be planes. I then stood up and walked to get closer to the objects to get a better look. The sky was dark. Immediately I noticed 5 objects moving across the sky. They were a solid red color moving with no sound and made a strange formation in the sky. Three of them then made a triangle like shape. The last of them 5 objects moved further. Then on object one I noticed a grey like shape under the red solid light. The objects moved in a way that was not a plane. I immediately pulled out my phone to record th the objects.The red nights did not show up in the video as there was too much street lighting. I remember saying out load "this is so weird". I felt scared a but but also in awe as I've never seen anything like it before. Before the first object disappeared it moved down and the up rather fast. I stayed outside until all objects disappeared into the clouds.
