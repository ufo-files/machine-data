I was riding my motorcycle on the hwy outside wickenberg, AZ when I notice what looked like an airplane white strobe, then much larger red light appeared in its place. Then at the same time first light disappears another red light appeared some, maybe miles, from first light. This was a sequence of probably 4 lights moving equal distance from previous light location. This occured straight in front of me about 5 mile out 30 degrees from horizon. When last light went out another set appeared directly off my left still high in the sky and did the same action. After the last light went out I did not notice any more action. The strobe caught my attention and I thought it was an airplane, but when the first red light came on it was much larger and I found that curious. It held my attention and that is why I noticed the sequence of red lights poping on and off across the sky. Later I arrived in Wickieup, AZ I stopped at the gas station to ask if anyone had heard of sightings of lights in the sky and a man instantly reacted and explained what I saw before I described this to him. He saw them in the same area some 60 miles back down the road I saw them, with the exception that he saw white lights instead of the red I saw. I considered this a UFO because Ive never seen any lights behave like that. Then the other curious thing was the instant relocation of the event maybe 10 or 20 miles across the sky. I consider that this might be a military op because of the small white strobe that could have been responsible for red light action across the sky.
