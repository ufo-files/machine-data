(GS) Red pulsating hovercraft in the Miami night sky

I was with some friends at a friends house when all of a sudden one of them shouted "look up in the sky...what is that!?!" I looked back into the sky and it appeared to be a flying vessel of some sort pulsating red. We were all very confused and some were even frightened. One of my friends even mentioned it could be a chinese lantern but this couldnt be so as it was moving very very quickly and side to side. I was very scared at first but then eager for an alien invasion. I spoke with a co-worker today about it and he said he saw it last night too as well as 2 weeks ago.
