I witnessed falling glitter over trees

There was a storm in the vicinity of Gassville Arkansas. The event lasted approx. 30 minutes. The shining glitter floated down as if to settle over the trees foliage. A couple of years later I saw a science documentary on ball lighting. I attributed this event, and excused this sighting due to ball lightning until today. 

They say, (scientist), ball lighting only last seconds, not minutes. 

This event was life lasting, and I will never forget it.
