Very bright white lights

Hello I am a avid sky watcher from Lawrence Massachusetts having seen multiple strange craft over the last 10 years not all in Lawrence some in Methuen and some over the line in Salem NH and last night at dusk i observed a craft traveling southeast to northwest it was approx 500 ft of the ground and traveling at a slow steady pace it was in the shape of a blimp only about one quarter of the size with two dull white lights the two lights taking up almost the whole length of the craft the front light was stationary and non blinking the back light pulsated. No apparent wings and absolutely no noise what so ever!!!       and then at 10pm when i was going to pick up my daughter from work in a area with many people lots of lights and a lot of noise!!! i noticed two more white lights only these two lights were very bright and both stationary very low to the ground i got very excited with this craft it was just sitting there hovering the two lights were appeared to be connected to the same craft although the craft wasnt view-able the lights didn't give off or reflect light it bugged me that nobody else noticed it i guess that the occupants new that the area was a Government hand-out Latino area were nobody cares about anything The craft took off in the blink of an eye and then stopped and hovered again for only about 5 seconds before taking off for good Sorry i couldnt set up my camera fast enough
