{nd} Bright yellow-orange pulsating light emitted 2 or 3 objects, same color, but smaller, then disappeared.

I was on my deck beside my apartment after work one night. I got off work at 11 PM, so it was around midnight. I saw a VERY bright yellow-orange light pulsating in the sky to the south. I have no idea how far away it was or what altitude it was at. I had no idea what it was. I am a pilot so I know more about things that fly than the average person. It pulsated 4 or 5 times then it ejected 2 or 3 smaller similar colored objects and then it just disappeared. I was dumbfounded! I knew I had seen something extraordinary, but to this day it concerns me as to what it was.
