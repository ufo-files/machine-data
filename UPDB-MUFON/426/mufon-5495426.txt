{cak} Several Ships

My family and I were outside and saw a bright light in the sky. We watched it and several smaller lights came out of it. They had blinking colors and one flew directly over us and we were able to see a round object with three white lights and a red flashing light. They made little to no noise except the close up one and it made a bit of a swishing noise. They are still in the sky as I write this and are continuously circling the sky. At first I thought they were just stars. I feel anxious right now and was excited while I watched it.
