A large number of small star-like objects moving quickly in random directions, sometimes following one another at high speeds.

I was on my balcony last night around 10:30 pm, smoking a cigarette, and noticed a small streak of light go across the western sky/horizon, probably above the Pines/Michigan City area in Indiana. So I watched a waited for more- but noticed something way more awesome! There were 20+ of these objects scattered in that direction only(west). They were FAST and could stop on a dime, make a 180 turn at high speeds... it was just WAY out of the ordinary. Last night was cloudy, I couldnt see the stars but I could definitely see these tiny objects chasing each other through the sky. I stood there and watched this for at least 30 minutes, with my mouth hanging wide open and wondering- WHy isnt anyone else outside right now?! Id totally ask them if they saw it too I was home alone and got a little startled when I thought I saw one of the objects come in my direction... once I saw the mass of the object coming closer and growing- which was like a shadow- I quickly went inside grabbed my keys and left. That was all I wanted to do with it...so that was the last I saw of it. No ET meetings for me, thank you!
