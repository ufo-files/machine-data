{cak} Arrived at pond with friend, Witnessed glowing orb hovering at tree line. A few minutes later, it shot toward us, blinding us with bright light.

In the mid-summer of 1992, I had just got out of work, and I drove home, picked up a neighbor friend, and drove out to the pond behind our trailer park. As soon as I shut off the engine, we both noticed a glowing orb(It seemed to be a round or oval object, but all you could see was an intensely bright, orb shaped light.) at the southern tree line, which is the way the car was facing. We both watched it for several minutes as it hovered, moved several tree widths in both directions, ascended up above the trees, descended just below the tree line and back up again, obviously under intelligent control. It seemed as though it knew we were watching it. After a few minutes of this, we were both taken by surprise when it seemed to shoot directly at us, reaching us in a split second, and instantly blinded us both with an intense bright white light. After just a few seconds, our eyesight seemed to adjust normally as the light disappeared. We got out, but found no sight of it. A similar encounter happened a few nights later at the same location, with some of our other friends as witnesses, which I will submit as a different encounter. I realize this happened a long time ago, and really doesn't help much, but I figured it might as well go on record.
