{rjl} Supplemental report to original report 2010 sighting

I was living in a house in Aloha, Oregon, and made a report about a ufo that overflew my home heading from a SW area and made a 90 degree turn to the East (no sound what so ever, just a faint glow) I truly wasn't sure what I was seeing. What made me report it back then were the two fighter jets that came out behind it hitting what looked like afterburners and also turned slightly on an obvious intercept. That is all I can remember, you have the original report. This supplemental report is about what happened a day after reporting it. I received a phone call that I will always remember as the strangest call I ever heard. Besides a faint clicking sound , a deep voice came on the phone identifying themselves as someone from the JPl saying they were interested in what noise if any it made and several other technology questions and said they may send someone out to speak with me from the government. I didn't say anything at the time cause I had difficulty believing if I was in danger or not do it took a while and for that I am sorry. I was a police officer and not likely to fantasize.
