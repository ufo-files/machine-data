{rjl} Large beam of light that not like a search light.

My son and I would look out his bedroom window every night before bed. Off in the distance I saw the light. A light that I have never seen before, not then and not now 30 some years later. It was very white and a perfect cylinder shape. Stationary and never moved its position. I called to my husband to witness what I was seeing. We called the news team to see if anyone else had called to report the light. They took down my information and said that they would call us back ... They never called and nothing was reported on the news or in the paper for weeks to follow. When I tell the story to people they say that it was probably this or that... I can't explain what I saw but I do know that I feel a connection to it, something inside me saying it was more than just a beam of light. My sons are grown now but I still watch the night sky. Maybe someone else saw this light that night or maybe I was the lucky one.
