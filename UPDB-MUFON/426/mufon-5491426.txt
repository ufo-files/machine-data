Weird ball hovering

I was stationed at FOB Wolverine and I was in charge of the USO. I stepped outside the doors to smoke a cigarette and noticed over the mountain there was a weird ball just hovering at around 6am. As I smoked I watched as the object moved around. At first it moved from side to side and then moved up and down. After watching it for a few more minutes if appeared to move around in almost a figure 8 shape. I noticed it over a period of 4 days. After noticing it after 2 days I decided that I would go ahead and bring my camera along with me (canon 60d slr). I snapped a few pictures and then as I finished my cigarette I went back outside. I stepped out again an hour later and the object was gone. But over the period of about 4-5 days the object would hover and make figure 8 shapes in the same spot at around the same time each morning. Eventually it stopped and I never saw the object again.
