Golden crosses in the sky

During a hot July night in 2018, I decided to take some fresh air and to look out of the window. It must have been 11:20PM. Because of the street light almost directly across the street, I usually can't see the stars but I decided to take a look around me anyway.

Much to my surprise, I saw two golden crosses on my left. I know they look nothing like golden crosses according to my own photos but that's how they appeared. They came from south and travelled to the north. The striking thing about these crosses is that they sleemed to move slowly at first, much like a normal aircraft would from that distance but they would speed up or slow down randomly in an unnatural way: as if they didn't need time to accelerate or decelerate.

I observed them for approximately 10 or 15 minutes before deciding to take photos with my crappy cellphone camera before they left my line of vision.
