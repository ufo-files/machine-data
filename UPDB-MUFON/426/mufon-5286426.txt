Saw huge spaceship when leaving dance recital spring of 1962

Date was spring of 1962 - We were driving from Pacific Junction Iowa to Council Bluffs to a dance recital for my cousin. It was a early spring night and clear night but we were leaving dance recital hall in Omaha and driving to cousins house for refreshments after the recital. Maybe was 9:30pm driving down 13th Street in South Omaha going towards South Omaha bridge to cross into Council Bluffs. In car were my parents, a neighbor lady and her son and me. I saw over the top of a warehouse building a spaceship as big as a city block. I was so excited, I was hanging out car window pointing up in the air for other motorists to look up. I was yelling at my dad to stop the car. He did not but my mother and the neighbor boy saw it. It maybe stayed stationary for 30 seconds. I got a good look. Then it moved up at super speed until I think I could see it amongst the stars. When we got to cousins house in Council Bluffs, the boy and I sat out on the porch steps and we could still see it - maybe 30 - 60 minutes it was still there. Most people would laugh at my story so have only told it a few times.
