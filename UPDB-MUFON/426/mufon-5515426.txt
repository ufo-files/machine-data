Huge object with bright glowing green light, decending at a very fast speed.

I was walking home with my girlfriend, when we stopped to decide if we should get some food or not, when all of a sudden a huge glowing light came across the sky. Cars stopped to look also. At first i thought maybe it was an asteroid, but then two other things looked like they had been ejected from the primary object, trailing behind the biggest one. We both stood there and watched then i ran down the road to see if i could see the object, unfortunately i was blocked by buildings. We both felt quite strange after this event.
