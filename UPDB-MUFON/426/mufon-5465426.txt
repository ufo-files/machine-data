{jpg} Thought it was a star.

I was outside, early AM, taking out the trash. I tend to keep an eye on the skies. I looked up and noticed something didn't seem right because I could see 4 stars that were in a perfect line with each other and I didn't recognize that formation. As I was looking at it trying to figure it out, one of the "stars" took of at a very high rate of speed, in a north-western direction. It must have been climbing at the same time as the light got smaller and smaller until it disappeared. The skies were completely clear and visibility was very good. I have very good long distance eyesight but in just a few seconds, it went from the brightest "star" in the sky to completely gone. After it was beyond my ability to see anymore, I just looked up and said thank you. I've been hoping for something to happen that would solidify my belief that there are others out there.
