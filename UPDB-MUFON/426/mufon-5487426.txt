3 strange reflective shapes in sky, In a triangular formation, the top two shapes disintegrated into the atmosphere over time as the bottom shape moved in a north western direction

At 5:11pm Saturday, 7th of September, I was driving my car on the way to the supermarket in Victoria , Gozo , when I looked directly up in the sky just above the road, something caught my attention. I saw 3 objects that were in a triangular pattern, they seemed to reflect light, they were almost silverish in color, but the bottom one looked a bit orange. They were shaped what looked like a long oval shape but each one was stationary at a 45 degree angle. As I stopped the car to watch this phenomena , I realized the two shapes at the top of this triangular pattern were fading, and at the same time, the bottom shape of this triangular formation was moving ( I really cannot state how fast, but if they were large objects, as they seemed to be, the bottoms one must have been moving quite fast, perhaps as fast or faster than a modern airplane) and continued to a north western direction, toward cloud-cover, at which point I had lost track of the object, but not before taking photos, with my mobile phone camera, ( which are not very clear, but you can definitely see a pattern in the sky if you look closely)
