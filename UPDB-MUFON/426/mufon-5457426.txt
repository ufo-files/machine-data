{rjl} Bright orbs circling each other

I was sitting in my car on a phone call when I noticed a single very bright light. I noticed it hadn’t moved at all in a minute or 2 and that’s when I saw a plane go by and noticed the plane’s lights were nowhere close to being as bright as the light I was seeing. Then I saw another light come out from the bottom and stay in a line below the first light. They stayed still in this formation for about a minute until the second light from below circled around and went to the top of the first light. The lights continued to stay in that position until it the 2nd light proceeded to circle back to its original spot. As it was circling back down, that’s when I hung up my call to take a picture. Unfortunately I didn’t get the chance to take a video of what was happening, and I called my brother on FaceTime to show him what was happening. By the time he answered the second light disappeared. Shortly after we watched the first original light disappear as well. We didn’t see any other objects in the sky. Only the bright orbs.
