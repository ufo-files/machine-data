{tjd} Missing time

My husband and I were driving at night on a back country road wen I asked him if he saw what I saw He said he didn’t see anything, however I saw a large stationary object just above the trees in front of us hovering. The next thing I remember is asking him and we were driving in a different direction looking for it. No idea of time passed or how we got down the road. We turned around to look for it but he still to this day has no memory before that and we couldn’t find anything even though, in my mind, it had just happened. I’m not wanting any publicity and am actually embarrassed that it even happened. I’m kind of in the public eye because of my current occupation where we live in Kentucky and I’m not sure it would be good to bring up
