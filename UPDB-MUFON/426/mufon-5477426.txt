pictures of a piramid shape object taken at Uxmal mexico

1. I was on vacation in Uxmal,Mexico taking multiple pictures of the different structures.

2. I never noticed anything until I got home from vacation and I was viewing my pictures.

3. The object looks like a cloud, but when I applied a program called DStretch to the picture I noticed a pyramid shape object.
DStretch is a tool for rock art researchers who wish to enhance images of pictographs.

4. I didn't notice anything at the time I took the picture.

5. After I applied the program to the pictures I was really surprised I never noticed anything when I took the picture. I went back to the original picture and I was able to see the pyramid shape object without the Dstretch program1
