It disguises itself like a star

I see this or these things a lot i only ever see one at a time but I see it often, mostly it travels from south to north sometimes north to south,early last week I tried to put a laser pointer on it and it actually headed east for a little and as I hit it with the laser a couple of times it stopped dead for a few seconds then started to move west which is where I was again i put the light on it and it stopped again. I did not use the laser after that as it just sat there silently and i felt like it was looking for the source of the light i watched it for 5 to 10mins and then i went in to my home. Then on Saturday night I went out to the back garden and i noticed a very bright flash in the sky it was like about 10 to 15 times the size of a star it flashed really fast and bright so I kept watching the sky and then there was another it was like something moving around randomly and then after 6or 7 times and not seeing anything in between the flashing this light like a star moves from roughly where the last flash was and head's south to north for a few seconds and stops nearly directly above me.i see these lights on most clear nights at certain times.
