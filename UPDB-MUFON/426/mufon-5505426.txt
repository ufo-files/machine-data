I was putting my baby in the car and noticed something shining in the clouds. I watched the craft move in and out of the clouds going sideways. Then it would stop and hover start shining again and go back into the clouds. It seemed like it would go backwards in the clouds because the clouds were moving but that craft would come back out in the same spot it went into the clouds. Every time it started moving towards the clouds the bright light would go away. And it seemed like the whole craft would glow not just a few lights on it. When it would come back out of the clouds it would be dark colored kinda like the clouds. I watched it for about 7-10 minutes and then it went into the clouds and didn't come back out.
