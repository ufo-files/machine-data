We were watching a hawk fly above us, when I saw something unusual

We were observing a red tailed hawk fly above us and as my eyes followed, I suddenly saw 4 strange object flying in formation. I got my husbands attention and he was perplexed by them as well. We often have military jets fly above us in formations and they make a lot of sound. These had no sound and were moving very quickly and didn’t look like any jets we’ve ever seen. As we continued to watch we realized there was a weird dusty like cloud they were heading to and suddenly there were around 30 more of these things. They were flying in formations of 4 and making fast turns like downward and upward and it affected the dust like weird cloud as they flew. It also appeared like they would disappear in this cloud. Then they’d reappear. There was not a cloud in sight today and as we continued to watch these strange formations we got the binoculars out and observed the center was a darker color and the wings or whatever they were were a lighter color. By the time I went to go get my camera or phone, they had literally disappeared before our eyes and didn’t return. I wish I had evidence to give of what we saw because we ruled out everything else and would be seriously
Surprised if no one else saw this. I’ve seen strange things before at night but never this many and during daylight.
