3 of us sitting in the backyard. I was faceing east, They come up fast, stop, and a bright flash. Cruzed alittle ways stopped and flashed again. Like the flash of a camera.

We had 4 sightings on 6-1-12 all evening and through the night. Me and two others were in the backyard star gazing. When I said theres another one! Two of us saw the first flash, all three of us saw the second flash. It was a dull object that flashed twice. They came from the east and then after second flash shot north.
