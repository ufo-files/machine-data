Started as a bright spot in the sunset and than it just faded away in same position

I was sitting in a bench with my girlfriend, watching the sunset a couple of meters from the beach, skies were clear, place was packed of people walking by, taking pictures as the tide was higher than usual, i even could make out some stars as the night falls.

But in the horizon (as seen in the picture) i noticed this bright white dot above this hill, kinda big to be a star, did not notice at first, but after a couple of minutes (4min i guess) it slowed faded out, at the same position as i saw it first, took maybe 10sec to fade out/disappear.

I just took a photo because it got my attention, wish i could have recorded.

It is not the first time i see a light like this around that area, there are helicopters flying by sometimes, but it is different, this ones does not blink, does not move, does not make any sound, it is stationary.

Kinda next to the ground of where the light was, there is a small army base, but i don't know what is the purpose of it.
