bright star to the naked eye, but a cluster of lights thru telescope

I was at Lake Havasu looking at stars thru my telescope, when I noticed with the naked eye a bright star low on the southern horizon. After locating it with the telescope I was astonished to see it was actually a cluster of lights. It then started moving west to east and I was able to track it thru the scope for about 2 minutes til it faded away in the eastern horizon. This was also witnessed by my wife and daughter who also described it as 6 or 7 brighter lites on the leading edge in the shape of a boomerang, followed by about a dozen dimmer lites close behind which were clustered together. It was traveling much faster than any of the airliners which fly overhead, and seemed to be more distant and outside of our atmosphere. I've been looking for reports and wonder if anyone else may have seen this.
