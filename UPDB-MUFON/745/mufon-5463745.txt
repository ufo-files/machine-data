{tjd} Top quadrant of moon, maybe 2000 feet from the moon. a black chain of rectangles stretched across the moon

6:54 PM , 12/22/18, I was driving home, I was on FM 2664 driving easterly.and looked up at the moon. The moon was so bright and I felt compelled to look at it.

I glanced down to make sure I didnt run off the road. I slowed down and looked back up and then I thought "what the hell is that?" when I saw the line. 1/5th the distance from top of moon was a flat black thick line. 

I didnt know what it was, was black and I realized it was too sharp to be a cloud, sharply defined.

As my eyes focused it was rectangular shapes, almost all the same size, hooked together by smaller connectors. It stretched over the moon where I could not see the ends.It slowly descended, took about 4 to 6 seconds, then dropped below the moon and I could no longer see it.

I felt really strange when I saw it, elated, and when I got home I felt like I had to report it, like it was my "duty", like I had a responsibility, a burden, but not to humans. I also felt shocked like after a really good or a really bad event. 

It descended past the bottom of the moon and was no longer visible.
