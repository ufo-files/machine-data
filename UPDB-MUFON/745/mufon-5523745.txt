Flashing lights now in formations

For the past 3 to 4 months blinking colored lights in sky. At first just saw a couple then over a period of a couple weeks started seeing more. Now the are in triangle formations. there have hardly moved in three months there are at least 12 of them now. Can see them ever night they are noticeable before the stars are. There flash in colors at same time. I love to go out and watch them. My son Koty said he thinks they are so cool to watch flash. We wish we had a better telescope to see them better. you can see the color change with your bare eyes. I do not have a camera that can get a picture that far away.
