{dr}2 bright white flashes 20 seconds apart, way way up in the sky, higher than planes can fly and the flashes looked miles apart.

I was starting my day at 3:35 AM like I normally do, stepping outside to feed the stray cats and critters that live here in the countryside. As always the first thing I do when I step outside is look up into the night sky and look up at the stars. I glanced up at the sky and was immediately welcomed by a bright flash of white light. Not knowing what it was I kept gazing to see if there where any tell tale signs of what it was. It was so high in the sky I couldn't imagine it being a plane and there was no sign of any sort of tail. Just a bright white flash of light then gone, until about 20 seconds later when I saw the same thing again. This looked a little higher in the sky and looked to be what I imagine miles away from the first. I would assume if it was as high as it looked it would have been hundreds of miles away. As with the first flash I gazed into the sky hoping I could make heads or tails of what it was, but nothing, just darkness. I can't say what it was for sure, I've been watching the sky for many years and have never seen anything like this. Unfortunately this wasn't an event I could have gotten a photo or video of, it happened so quickly.
