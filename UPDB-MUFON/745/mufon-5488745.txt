{jpg} Blue and red lights, then only red lights. Diamond shape.

My daughter, son and I were outside looking at the blood moon toward the East. My son turned toward the West and said "look at that plane coming in". I turned and looked and said "wow, look at that"....the lights were very bright , like a cars halogen headlights. Glowing white/blue and red lights too. We watched these lights come toward us, and then stop at the tree line at the neighbors yard. The lights hovered for approximately 25 seconds, then the blue lights turned off and it was 4 bright red lights hovering. Approximately 20 seconds after that, the blue lights came back on and they started to travel steadily West, while the lights were turning slowly counter clock wise.
