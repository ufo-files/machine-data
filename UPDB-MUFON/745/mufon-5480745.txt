{rjl} watching stars, four enterd vision in formation then split in 4 separate directions

My wife, mother in law and I were sitting looking at the lake (Goose Creek) watching boat lights cruise by no wake in the dark. We were also looking for iridium flares and satellites in the dark sky in front of us and above. My mother in law had a star phone app so we were looking at Mars, Saturn and Antares to our right and trying to find the constellations. Just to the left of Mars, 4 white stars in a square formation suddenly appeared. I asked my mother in law what those were and her reply was "I don't know". Just then they split in 4 different directions, 3 to the left and 1 to the right at a very fast speed. These were still at satellite altitude or higher.
