Controlled flight path

I went to let my dogs out before bed,i opened back door and noticed the clouds were unusually low i sat on a small stool and i was thinking if i climbed onto shed i could touch the clouds they were hanging down so low,it was then that i seen what i can only describe as a white ball it was very close to me i was confused it was rooftop height and travelling in straight line.there was no sign or sound of a motor and no other way it could naturally stay afloat.i was frightened as i could not make sense of what i was seeing,despite the time i could see it very clearly it was bright white but i think it was more reflective than glowing.it travelled slow enough for me to have a good look before it glided between 2 houses where it disappeared from my veiw.I had to keep telling myself i did see it as it was hard to rationalise.
