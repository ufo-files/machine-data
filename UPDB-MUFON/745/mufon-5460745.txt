{rjl} I witnessed a bell shaped orange glowing object over a house rooftop. There was no sound.

At around 10:00 pm I went outside and witnessed a glowing orange, bell- shaped object over the roof of the house next door. It was like hot orange embers from a fire. I looked for any lines of a door or any other markings. There were none. It never moved in any directions, it was stationary. There was no sound when coming or going. I also saw the same thing again in September making it a total of 2 sightings.
