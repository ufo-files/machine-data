Giant Cigar shaped object with lights

I went outside to take my brother his kneepads walked over to his property it's dark out and we're talking and that we were talking about SpaceX and I happened to look up because I was thinking all you can see the stars are so bright tonight the sky is so clear. When I looked up I Saw where I would guess would be a craft in the air at least a quarter-mile long moving slowly across the sky it had lights all the way down it and my brother his wife and her friend were out there and we all looked up and saw this thing together it was like deer in the headlights. We probably had our eyes on it for a good 15 seconds my brother yells out everybody get a camera, he runs back to his truck calls out his phone I had left my phone at the house and the light started to fade. Even though the lights were fading you could still see the silhouette of this giant ship it looks like what I would describe as a cigar-shaped form when the lights were out it looks sort of grayish on the dark background of the sky it was actually blocking out stars as it was going and then I lost side of it.
