{nd} Observed solid white non pulsating light at dusk. Moved South to North very rapidly, very high, no noise, 30 degrees above horizon South to approximately 45 degrees north in less than 2 seconds, then faded out.

My wife and I were talking on the back porch at dusk. Stars had just become visible when I noticed a bright white round star-like object to the south. It traversed over head to the North with no sound, at what appeared to be substantial altitude, in an arc covering about 60% of distance horizon due south to horizon due north.The light did not very in intensity, until the end of my observation when it abrubtly faded out. I am near the Northern approach for Wright-Patt AFB and Dayton International AP and have never seen any aircraft move with this apparent speed. There were multiple observable vapor trail's in the area at the time and the object produced none, nor did it have any apparent particle or heat trail.
