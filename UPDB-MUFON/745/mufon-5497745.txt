{rjl} Hovering saucer gave out a beem of light, then light disappeared then flew off

While starting my car at 5:33am this Friday, Nov. 21st, 2104, something made me look straight up into the night sky. Directly above my head was a very bright light. The beam seemed to be shinning at a 45 degree angle to the SW of my location. The beam was only admitting light for about 20 seconds. In that time I could see that there was an object connected to the light. I estimated the object to be about 500 feet or so in the night sky. The sky was clear and many stars were visible giving somewhat of a reference to how high in the sky the object was. I live in S.E. Minnesota. The light slowly dimmed, and at this time I could see the craft that was admitting the light. It was saucer shaped and about 50-75 feet circumference. With the lights of the town, the object was low enough I could see it. I thought maybe it was brownish in color, but after thinking about it, the craft could have been reflecting back the orange colored street lights. The craft hovered for about a minute and then very slowly it started to move to the east. Made no sound and there were no other lights on the craft that I could see. The craft moved off not moving any faster than about 20 mph. I watched it to the point I no longer could see it which was about a 3 minute time span.
