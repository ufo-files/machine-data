{rjl} Bright light hovering in single location for over hour before stars/planets visible.

During one of our events today we spotted this oval shaped object in the sky around 6 pm, possibly a little earlier than that. We became fixated on it as the show went on, but it did not move at all. The stars were still not visible, nor were the planets. In fact, the sun was still up. In the attached videos, the size is a bit deceiving. It looks to be tiny, however viewing with your naked eye it appears to be about 2-3 times the size of what jupiter looks like currently. It also appears this same object was viewed in Swarthmore PA, which is about the same latitude as where we were. Distance was difficult to estimate without knowing the size of the object. It was bright white and did not become dimmer until about 45 minutes after the sun had went down. There were no stars in that position on my star app, and if it were, it would have gotten brighter. The only thing I cannot tell you is when it got there...we just noticed it about 6pm, and from then on it did not move at all.
