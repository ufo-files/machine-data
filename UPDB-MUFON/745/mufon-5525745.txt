3 orange lights in triangle formation

I was standing in my driveway facing North, approx. 10:45, with 4 other people (all witnessed the same thing) 3 orange lights appeared traveling south/southwest. Lights were moving very slow, not blinking, stayed in a triangle formation for a good 20-30 seconds, disappeared behind cloud and did not come out. This happened on July 4th but absolutely were not fireworks.
