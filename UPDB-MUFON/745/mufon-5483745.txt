Red, green, white light non moving stationary above Bolton Ontario. Somewhat like star but pulsating.

I was sitting in my bedroom watching TV and I look out the window and clearly see a fairly bright light much like a star pulsating, with red, green, and white colours flashing randomly. This is not the first time I see it, as it might be a star but because it is pulsating clearly I am suspicious that it is not. I did not lose sight of the ufo as I fell asleep. I understand people might think it is a star but if they were to see it they would be mystified how brightly it flashes in the clear night sky.
