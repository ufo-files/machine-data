20 or more star like objects flying in a formation

We saw at least 20 star like objects flying across the sky from SW to SE in a type of formation. During the event the objects started to just turn off when they reached the SE side of the sky. There was no sound that we could here.
In the middle of all the objects there were two lights that were a lot closer then the other lights, one was red and the other was blue. The red and blue lights seemed to be in line with each other meaning one light was in the SE direction and the other light was behind it towards the SW. The video was taken with a NOTE 10 and we only caught eight of them on the film. Maybe someone can figure out what we saw.
