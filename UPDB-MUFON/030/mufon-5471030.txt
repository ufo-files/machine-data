{cak} saw several orange orbs hovering low in the sky, my first experience seeing what i thought was fake star, when it shape shifted into an old type of airplane

my sister and i was filming what i believed to be fake stars in the sky over the summer, they were orb like, orange in color pulsating and spinning, noticeably not what i know stars to look like, however we couldn't ID the objects. there were a few in the sky that we film, and when i reviewed the tape this evening i realized there were other orb like object that we recorded flying around and below the main orange glowing lights in the sky. we are filming for several minutes, the objects just hovered about 27-33 degrees on the horizon. After like 5 or so minute the orange light started to lower its altitude, you see the light bend a few times as it morphed. it started to change shape, it turned into an airplane, a real large airpLANE with LED ish glowing type of modern airplane lights, the plane was HUGE. when the plane started to shape shift you then start to hear the hum change into, what sounded like a propeller driven aircraft, and as it flies over it sounded so low, and not appropriate for the type of plane that was over head.
