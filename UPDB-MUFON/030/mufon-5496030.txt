{cak} hovering with many lights and then flew away slowly

I was driving to work on 435 E and noticed large object with many lights hovering at a distance. Object began to fly to my left as I began to merge on highway 71 S. I observed the bottom of the object, which appeared to be triangular in shape with one blinking light in the center and multiple lights along the edges. As it changed directions the object no longer seemed triangular but more like a cross or a capital T with the upper part of the T being the back. I have grown up in a military family and I have never seen any military craft with this many lights on it.I ,along with other motorist were travelling around 70mph but when object past over highway, everyone slowed down to about 40mph to observe it.
