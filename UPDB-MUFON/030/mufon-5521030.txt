Saw hovering object with sequentially flashing lights around belt

I was sitting on my back porch with friends and family and happened to glance up through the trees branches. I saw a multicolored belt of lights flashing sequentially from one side to the other and then back again. It hovered in place. At the same time, I saw the lights of a plane pass across my field of vision in front of the hovering object, so I knew it was definitely not a plane. The object, still hovering in the space place, began to slowly fade. It did not decrease in size as if it were moving away, but rather faded into black.
