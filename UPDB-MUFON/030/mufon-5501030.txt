{cak} Initially flashing. Seemingly shifting on video. 2 pics. Hovering then sped away

On Sunday, June 8th 2014, while traveling south on Main St. West Haven, Ct, I initially noticed unusual contrail formations. I proceeded to videotape the footage at 11:13 am. At which time, two flashing objects at the top of my field of view appeared and disappeared quickly. They then shifted position drastically. They went from 3 o'clock and 9 o'clock to 6 o'clock and 12 o'clock and back within a second. I then stopped the car, stepped out, aimed my camera in the direction of the aerial illuminations and took two photos at 11:14 and 11:15. I later discovered two dark disc shaped objects appearing to hover and seemingly unrelated to the contrail laden sky, a potential third object is possible due to the disc shaped hole in the cloud, within the first picture. The second picture taken immediately after shows one clear, dark, disc shaped object appearing to accelerate to the east. A faint contrail or atmospheric disturbance is evident. Furthermore, the leading edge of the object gave off a yellowish orange glow. Perhaps a coronal discharge from a potential electrogravitic propulsion system, however, this is only speculation. I only noticed these details sometime later while zooming in on the photos.
