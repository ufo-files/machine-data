Large classic Saucer shape right above my house.

I first saw what I would describe as a spotlight but instead of looking up it was coming from the sky downward. It moved in a circular pattern for a few seconds. Suddenly a very large classic saucer shape ufo came through the clouds directly over my house. It hovered about 15 feet above my two story home. It had square type window or shutter type openings on the bottom with a circular middle. The square shutter type windows would open like slats going around the bottom and it would move a few feet. It did this about 5 times before doing it the final time when it took off at an enormous rate of speed. I got the impression it knew I saw it and it wanted me to see how it was moving. I felt as though when the shutter slats opened it was taking air in and using the air to move forward. I am not sure why I felt this way and it is hard to explain in words. I did get a really good look at it and it was reflecting the sky so that you could see all of its definition but it looked like sky at the same time. It was profound for me as I have never seen anything like that before. It was right above me for what felt like a long time but was actually only 30 seconds to a minute. Thank you for letting me share this as its a kind of lonely feeling having seen it but not really being able to speak about it. I dont have any drawings but could if I was asked to. It was very large and low after it came down from the sky.
