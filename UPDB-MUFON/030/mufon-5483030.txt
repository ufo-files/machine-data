{nd} Bright light in sky, white light portal spinning

I was outside smoking a cigarette. Was looking into the sky and all of a sudden this large, very bright pure white light suddenly appeared in the sky. The light was so bright that it lit up the house like daylight. I almost dropped my cigarette and for a second I was scared for my life, I thought I was going to be ubducted or something. It was a large, round circle with the center looking like a portal from a science fiction movie. It was spinning also. Hard to describe. Very beautiful. 
Lasted about 3 mins then slowly faded away. On the internet a lot of people saw this and some got it on video, but from a greater distance. This was definitely some type of portal. No craft or entities were seen. This event is know as the Nov. 7, 2015 Southern California light. The media and the military stated they were testing some type of missile from a submarine. This is impossible, because if it was a missle then I would have seen the trail as it went in the sky. It just suddenly appeared out of no where and then disappeared. There was a faint blue light that remained for another 5 minutes or so.
