I was dropping off my friend at home and when I drove away down the road toward the Mississippi river in Fridley MN I notice lights over the river I thought it was maybe landscapeing lights from the homes across the river but it wasn't. It was like white lights above the river it was formed like a triangle with a light at each end of the triangle shape. its was about maybe 40 to 50 feet above the river. When I first notice I was going west bound towards the river and towards the lights that I seen then I turn south bound and it was followed me for about a half mile and the half mile that I was going south it was hovering over the river following me. then I turn east bound and it was still following me at that time it was hovering over houses about 15 to 20 feet off the houses. I went east bound for about other half mile maybe then I turn north bound and soon as I turn north bound jetted off so quick that it was gone in a split second.
