{jpg} A Bright but Translucent Craft Moving Away and then Towards Me

I was watching television when it started to act up with static, broken images and loss of sound as if some form of interference. At approximately 8:59 I saw a light as if someone was outside with a flashlight and it caught my attention. At 9:00 PM I walked onto my balcony and looking up at the almost full moon I saw a large craft in the Chevron or triangular shape with three lights at each point. It seemed translucent from my vantage point. It seemed to disappear after about 5 seconds or so. Ten minutes later at 9:10 PM I felt the urge to go back outside onto the balcony. This very bright and intense light appeared from behind some clouds and came towards me from east to west. At the time there were planes taking off from D/FW airport from north to south which is their flight take-off path and this craft went right across the sky directly in their flight path. It seemed to be low in the sky as it came towards me so I was preparing to hear the sound of a helicopter but there was no sound at all even when it traveled directly in front and above me. The interference continued until about midnight when it completely stopped. I feel that it was the same craft that I saw going and then coming towards me.
