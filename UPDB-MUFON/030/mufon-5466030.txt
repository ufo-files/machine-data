{jpg} hovering over US Capital

Was on Vacation traveling through Washington DC in July 2018.. after leaving the american indian museum, and walking into the park between the washington monument and US Capital Building, I noticed something sharply reflecting the sunlight in my eyes, almost like a watch or something like that. I only had a cheap disposable camera with me from the CVS but managed to snap a few photographs before it quickly shot away into the distance without a sound. Only one photograph came out, but since it was on film i do have the negative.
