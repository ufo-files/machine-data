{jpg} Observed exceptionally bright stationary light while driving home on I-90. The light appeared to be emitted from inside the object rather than a beam.

I was driving home Northbound on I-90 after an evening work function. Shortly after passing the SR-91 exit, I observed a bright light in the Northeast direction not far above the treetops. I didn’t think much of it in that it was in an area where I have frequently observed aircraft on approach to Cuyahoga County Regional Airport in Richmond Heights, OH.

As I traveled North, the light didn’t appear to be moving, but was getting larger and brighter as I headed toward it.

I noted the time to be 7:57 PM and estimated It had been a minute or two since first seeing the light. The light remained steady and bright and apoearing not to move, which isn’t unusual seeing aircraft approaching the airport. 

View of the light was obscured by treetops as I drove and the topograghy changed on the road.

Shortly after exiting the I-271 North express lanes onto I-90 North, the light hadn’t moved past me and appeared to be in the same spot and size and intensity.

Between mile markers 192.3 and 192.4 I was paralell to the light on the right, just North of the Chagrin River and 400 to 800 yards off the highway and could see the light was coming from inside an object, like the effect of looking at a lit house window at night. There also seemed to be s dark, shadowy almost stealth bomber triangular shape to it with some very small lights on what or where wings would be on an aircraft. 

This close view only lasted 10 or 15 seconds, with the entire encounter lasting 5 minutes or so.
