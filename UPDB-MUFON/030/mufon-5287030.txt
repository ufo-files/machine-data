Strobing object slowly flies NW, disappears then reappears flies incredible pattern, stops then flies away at incredible speed

I live in the mountains of Crested Butte CO and have often seen anomalous things in the sky for the past 6 years. Sometimes I film/photograph things when the appear closer than normal. On this particular spring nite I saw this bright object in the sky that was strobing, changing colors and moving. I set up my phone to record on a tripod on my patio which faces west. I actually live at the base of the ski resort and if you listen to the video you can hear the snow CAT machines beeping in the background. 
The object slowly flies away to the NW at a slow speed until it seems disappear. I continued recording and after about 10 seconds of darkness it reappears in a very strange way, flew crazy fast and stopped for a second kind of right in front of me and flew away so fast that it’s hard to comprehend. All is caught on video.
