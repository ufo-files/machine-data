(GS) Noticed light in sky, it changed direction and had several sequential

At approximately 5:00 am January 16, 2013, while standing in my backyard, I noticed a light with the intensity of a star traveling from the south east toward the north west at a constant rate of speed. I initially thought it was a "satellite"(constant rate of speed, looks like a star, moving in a straight line across the sky). After about 8-10 seconds the object immediately changed direction heading south. A second or two later the objects light intensity increased by 10 to 20 times brighter, what I can only explain as a "burst". This "burst" continued every 2 seconds or so until it traveled past the roof line of my house and out of view. I thought about it all day and decided to file a report.
