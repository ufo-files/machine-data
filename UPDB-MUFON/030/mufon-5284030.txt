Fleet of ransom shaped objects appear over Dallas Skyline

On Sunday Night , November 21, 2022, my neighbor and I went up to the roof for about two minutes after realizing how cold it was we returned back inside. For the short time we were up there we noticed a bizarre funnel shaped cloud pouring right into the Dallas Skyline. He snapped a few pictures of the cloud. The next day I asked him to send me the photos and this is when we realized the odd objects that appeared in the sky. They seemed to be hiding behind the cloud. We did not notice them at the time when we were on the roof. Only in the photos did we see what was in the sky.
