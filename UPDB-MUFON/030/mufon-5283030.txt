Object with red and white flashing lights with a triangular shape

When going to work on approx 10/28/21 I observed an object with white and red flashing lights. It was in the distance but clearly visible in front of me while I was driving. I though it was a helicopter as it hovered as I proceeded west north west. However, it moved to the south in a constant altitude and as I moved closer, it appeared to be triangular in shape. I observed the object as I continued to drive and tried to make sense of it as a helicopter but it had no search light and appeared stationary until my driving route and safety concerns would not allow additional observations. Over the course of ~ 1 1/2 weeks, I observed the object in a similar location when I was not driving to the west and north west of me. The object was in the distance and at no point flew overhead so I never got a close view of it as with the first night. On subsequent occasions, the object appeared to have an erratic movement with a slow movement in a direction but then followed by fast movements appearing in a different location. Thankfully, I no longer see the object. On the first night when driving to work for my night shift, I did experience a brief loss of time. Concerning this, I was driving and just remember driving then being in a daze briefly while continuing to drive. I am not sure that this was out of the ordinary, but I continue to recall the incident
