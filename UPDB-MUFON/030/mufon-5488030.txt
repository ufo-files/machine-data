{cak} very large , very bright . no sound just sitting low tree top level.

My cousin and I were hunting deer in fall of 1983 Park Falls Wisconsin, Price county evening, dark out side, we were heading east back to Park Falls when we both noticed a very bright light ahead of us . we proceeded down the county highway and we are getting closer, and the light is getting bigger, now we are directly underneath the object and its really close to us 50 ft approximately. We were both shocked at what we were seeing. I pulled to the side of the road shut the truck off and just was amazed at what we were seeing. We were both scared but I could not just keep going I had to stop. We watched for 3 to 5 minutes it seemed like then it slowly just started moving to the west in the same direction we had just came from. In 1983 we had no cell phones cameras were not that advanced as they are now and we didn't have one. On our way home we talked about what had just happened and decided not to say any thing to anyone for the reason it was just to unbelievable, so we didn't hear of any other reports about this and kept silent for many years and only spoke of this between the two of us. Mind you, we were just 19 or 20 years old at the time, but we both seen the same thing. We today still have no answer of what it was. I would like to add, after the fact, when we first seen the light it was 2 miles ahead of us prior to stopping.
