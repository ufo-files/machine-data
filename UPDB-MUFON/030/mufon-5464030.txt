{jpg} Saw bright green-white object falling at a 45 degree angle.

I was in the Martha Lake area around 18:50 PST and saw a bright green-white object, like a rocket falling from the sky at a 45-degree angle. It was a big orb-shaped object with a tail. At first I thought it was a shooting star, but it was way too large and greenish. Then, I thought it could be a crashing airplane, but it was all lit up and too small to be an airplane. I assumed it could have been a meteor or space junk falling from the sky, but I didn't see anything about it online or in the newspaper. It all happened so fast, I didn't have time to take a picture or get video. It was about 2 or 3 seconds before it shot out of my eyesight.
