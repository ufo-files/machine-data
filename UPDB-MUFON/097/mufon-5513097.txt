(GS) Multiple series of orange lights moving slowly and steadily over hwy 59 south near the Galleria in Houston

I was driving home from work on US Highway 59 near Hillcroft 
I saw cars stopping and pulling over to the side of the freeway taking cell phone pictures of orange lights flying in formation on 1/13/2013 at about 7:30 PM. After seeing waves of these lights I knew it was not airplanes, there was no noise. These lights were flying in a northwestern direction towards Houston. I was anxious and a bit nervous. I knew there were UFOs. I tried to OnStar Bluetooth phone to call my parents but it was not working. My cellphone did work without the OnStar, however.
I pulled over and got several photos on my cell phone. The lights eventually disappeared from sight. As I said, these were not airplanes, they made no noise and kept coming in formations of 3 and four lights.
