Orange circle moving slowly through sky

Was bike riding late at night, saw an orange glow in the sky. Look closely and saw a circular orange ball the size of a big star. It moved slowly and straight like a sattelite but it was moving a bit quicker than a satellite usually does. It glided very smoothely through the sky dimming out occaisionaly then the light just went out. I was a little spooked when I saw this object, i thought it could be a sattelite but I have never seen one with that vivid of an orange color. Especially going over our small town.
