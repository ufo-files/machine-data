Sitting outside when it shot across the sky turned around then dissappeared

It was early friday morning, i was talking with my friend on his back porch. I looked up and saw what looked like a very bright star, it was still then started moving to the east. it went a pretty long distance then did big a U-turn as it finished the turn it vanished not to be seen again. the sighting lasted for around 10-15 seconds. Our reaction was like "what in the hell" we started laughing because it just blew our minds. how could something be still start moving at a high rate of speed do this huge turn then just vanish as soon as it finishes the turn.
