Giant metal grey triangle ship over my head in the East of France during August 2003

The sighting took place in the month of August 2003 in the village Rizaucourt-Buchey in France in the afternoon. 
I was sitting outside on the grass and observing the clouds while listening to music on headphones.
Suddenly, out of nowhere, an enormous triangular shaped metal object appeared in the sky, right above me, at a very low altitude (slightly higher than the top of the church nearby). The size was probably half a football stadium. 
The colour was dark grey, metallic, with no noise, no smoke, no lights, no bolts, no windows, just a piece of metal which looked like a ship. I definitely thought it was a UFO.
I stood there for a few seconds and ran into the house to call a family member, and when i came back outside (about 10 seconds later), it had disappeared, with no noise, no traces in the sky. 
There is no other evidence than what I saw for these few seconds. 
I was feeling quite excited by what I saw but we never able to explain it. 
It would be interesting to not that there is an Military airport nearby with regular jet exercises happening in the sky in this vicinity.
