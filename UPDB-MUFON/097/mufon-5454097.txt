Bright lights in sky. Initially thought to be drones 3 yrs ago, no idea now. I've seen the parades of orbs going across the sky, the floating orbs that dim and brighten, the weird diamond shape floating thing that rotates and seems to shoot out beams of light, I've seen everything recently reported on this site. I've had them follow my dads van from Bremerton to Tacoma before, never once breaking away from my view the entire trip. Literally keeping the same exact speed, elevation, everything. One time I watched it circle above us the entirety for a whole trip to Olympia. And it wasn't just us moving creating the illusion of it moving with us, when we stopped at street signs or traffic lights, it would keep moving. Sorry if I'm rambling, it's just up until a month ago it seems no one was really reporting all this stuff and now everyone is reporting the same things that I've been seeing for a while now and I'm excited.
