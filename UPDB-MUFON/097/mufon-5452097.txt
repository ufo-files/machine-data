Two Lights brighter than the sun appeared outside of my plane window and rose in elevation before disapearing.

On Alaska flight 2925 between Las Vegas Nevada and Portland Oregon on 6/17/20. soon after reaching altitude a passenger sitting in the opposite aisle made a comment that she saw very bright lights out of her window. Several minutes later a single light appeared outside of my window at a lower elevation. It rose to a higher elevation while getting brighter and brighter. a Second light of equal brightness appeared next to it and they stayed viewable before disappearing.
