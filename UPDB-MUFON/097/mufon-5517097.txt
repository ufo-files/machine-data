{dr}Bright red reflective orb traveled slowly and silently over my head during broad daylight.

It was 8:55 pm in Pocatello, Idaho and I was on the golf course with my friend. There were only a couple other people on the course at the time. I looked to the sky above the treeline on the far edge of the course at a bright red light that caught my eye. I knew right away it wasnt a plane or anything else I had ever seen before. The surface of the object appeared to be some type of red reflective foil. It moved slowly but steadily in a straight line overhead, completely silent. We lost sight of it when it dissappeared behind the trees. I would estimate it was at least 500 feet overhead and from my perspective about the size of a pea. My friend and I are usually pretty skeptical, but what we saw was the strangest thing either of us have ever seen.
