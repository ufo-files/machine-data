Triangular craft with two Orange lights underneath that moved

Myself and my husband were outside as it was quite hot that evening. We were chatting and about to go in when my husband asked what's that. I looked to where he was pointing and it took me a second to see what he saw because it was so dull. I noticed there was an area of the sky that was blacked out where I'd previously seen stars as it was perfectly clear that night. The shape of the blackness was triangular and I realised it was,a craft of some description. It was huge and completely silent. There was no movement of air or any sound. It was low and then we saw two Orange lights appear. They seemed attached to the craft yet they seemed to move around each other underneath and were definitely not fixed. We both instinctively knew it wasn't anything usual and didn't seem man made. It disappeared out of view beyond our house. I've seen lots of pictures of stealth aircraft but none that match this in either size or exact shape.
