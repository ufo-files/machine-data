Light under moon

This morning 12/12/11 at 7:30 a.m. I was driving north on Waverly Road. The moon was still bright and visible and under it was an object that seemed to be just hanging there. The object was all lit up; one bright light. Airplanes and jets are not typically all lit up. There was no contrail. As I continued North, the object moved quickly upward Northward and from my perspective was above the moon by that time. It still did not look like an airplane or jet. I lost sight of it after almost hitting another car. When I arrived home I looked up and saw a jet traveling south with a visible contrail and much higher up in the sky that this object that I saw was.
