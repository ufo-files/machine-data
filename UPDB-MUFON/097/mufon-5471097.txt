{cak} Red orange firey light slowly heading northwest, then stopped hovered, went straight up and disappeared

My husband and I were walking outside our apartments to go do laundry. I saw a bright fiery light coming towards us (north) in the night sky. It was overcast and this was the only light in the sky. It almost looks like a helicopter that was on fire that was flying really low. It got brighter as it came closer. My husband thought that it was a meteor but it was moving too slow. It quickly changed directions and started going north east. Then it suddenly just stopped, and hovered in the same spot for about 35 to 45 seconds. Then it went straight up, and just disappeared. It didn1t make any kind of noise or sound at all.
