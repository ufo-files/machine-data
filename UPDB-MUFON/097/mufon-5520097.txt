It looked like a football shape silver in color then became transluscent

Myself, my sister and her husband were traveling west on I-70( not sure of the nearest city or town we were close to, anywhere between Madison and Pittsburgh) on March the 10th 2012, heading to Wheeling West Virginia. At first my brother-in-law (driver) pointed out that he saw something to his left, directly over the tree line but when we passed, it wasnt there.  We really didn't think much of it because me or my sister didn't see anything.  But maybe thirty miles down the road, me being in the back seat I just happened to glance out the window to my right and just above the tree line a silvery football shape object was hovering, As we continued to travel west the object seemed as though to become reflective till I could no longer see it. After seeing the object Im still in a state of maybe confusion, not sure of what I actually saw, and not knowing exactly how to process the sighting.
