{cak} V shaped, solid black, amber red lights, no noise

I know this was so long ago, but I thought perhaps it can contribute some sort of mapping of that time.

I was at Schmid's Marina in New Baltimore, MI on October 5th, 1997 at 2am. My husband and I were there at our boat and met another couple at the Marina we were staying at. It was a clear night. We were sitting around a fire pit, they were drinking, I was not, as I can't tolerate it. It was so pretty out at that time and I was sort of bored so I looked up as I saw something from the corner of my eye moving across above the trees. It was shaped more like a V or boomerang until it turned on its side, then it looked more like a triangle. I think at first, since it was black against the night sky I thought it was boomerang shape. It was slow moving and no noise and heading south towards Anchor Bay when it suddenly stopped, turned on its side and headed west. It traveled extremely fast and was out my sight within about 5 seconds. I told the others and they didn't believe me, accusing me jokingly that I was drunk, too. I shrugged it off mainly because Selfridge Airfield Base is about 3 miles away.

For years, I thought I had seen the Stealth Bomber (B2). It wasn't until the Documentary on Sling, called Phoenix Lights (Beyond Top Secrets expanded version) that I realized what I saw. It was when the documentary showed the B2, the shape, color and noise, it hit me like a ton of bricks, it wasn't the B2 at all.

At the time, I wasn't scared, just curious. I never told anyone, I didn't want anyone to think I was drinking or telling me I was making it up. At the end of the documentary I told my 30 year old son, who I think believed me.
