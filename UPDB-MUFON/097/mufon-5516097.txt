{nd} Looked Like A, Slow Moving Star.

I Was Laying Down on My Front Lawn, Looking at the Sky, When I Noticed a Bright Moving Object, Moving Directly from South to North. I Watched it for About, One and a Half Minutes. It Suddenly Stopped and Went Directly the Path it Came From. After Another Minute and a Half. It Simply Disappeared. I Was In Utter Amazement, Of What Happened. This Thing Must Have Been Huge. Because I Think the Light I Was Seeing Was a Reflection from the Sun, And It Was Total Darkness at the Time. I Was Very Reluctant to Report It! And Didnt! I Didnt Tell Anybody What Happened.
