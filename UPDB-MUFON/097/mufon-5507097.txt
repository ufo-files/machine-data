{nd} Three bright lights lined up in a horizontal row.

I was traveling on highway 11 W. Heading towards the city limits of Bristol, TN. I observed what I believed to be a helicopter traveling across the night sky. It was moving at a decent speed and had a bright light. Catching my attention, I scanned the night sky. Shortly after, I saw three bright lights that appeared to be in a horizontal row. At first I thought they were lights from a radio tower or something. I thought this because they appeared to be stationary and not moving. They didn't flash or anything. Just three bright lights lined up in a horizontal row. As I got closer to the lights; I noticed there was no radio tower under the lights. I wasnt too emotional just curious as to what they were. After I passed them, I pulled over at a gas station. I got out of my car and looked up but they were nowhere to be found.
