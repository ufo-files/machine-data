{kt}Rapidly blinking red and blue light, would go dark intermittently.

I went out for a smoke in the backyard of my home in East Vancouver. I happened to look up towards the SSW and noticed a light in the sky. It was a blue/bluish white with some less pronounced red. The light was blinking very rapidly (think strobe light) and would frequently go dark/vanish. It's movements were very peculiar, as it was moving at a steady pace but would frequently and fluidly change direction very quickly/easily. I could not imagine anything from my experience that could look and move in the manner this object did. I stood there fascinated and observed the object for approximately two minutes as it moved closer then away and left and right repeatedly throughout the event. Eventually, it went beyond the tree line, and I was still able to see the lights flashing intermittently through the trees. Then it was completely out of sight.
