{kt}i saw a ufo on route #4 in the early evening of march 5 1978 over the road on rt/4 and the unh horse barn on that evening . went home and call local police and the pease air forcebase about my sighting

i was returning from a appt. in concord n.h. i had gotton a ride for a women that worked with the c.a.p. office in somersworth n.h./she had business with the state office of some kind. i remember her licsense plate read HOUNDS on it but i dont remember her name. we had just left the lee traffic circle heading east passed durham nh / to get me home when we saw the craft. i new right away that it was something id never seen before it was very large and had lights that seemed to revolve/rotate on the underside but the upper part of the craft seemed to be sationary only the underside of the craft was moving /the lights as i recall were red and possibly green or blue .it was in and open area of durham n.h with the the unh horse barns just behind it and the rt#4 which we were traveling i dont really remember for sure but one of the lights were defintitly red. as i looked a the craft it occurred to me that the lights were moving with the bottom half and appeared to be switching from one side to the other side of the ufo but i realized that they were just rotating on the under side . this craft was very large and it looked like a feeris wheel on its side ,it made no autable sound other than a low electrial sort of hum and current you could feel on you skin and it kind of reminded me of the sound a jump rope makes when it breaks the air around it as you are jumping that low whooping sound but not really a sound maybe a vibration. when i got home i called the dover pd and they laught at me, so i called the pease air force base i portsmouth n.h. they told me that it was probably a helicopter. i also called the durham police station and when they took my call they( the dispatcher) didnt want to talk becauce they(other officer/police employees) were witnessing the ufo themselves and she wanted to get off the phone and see the ufo for herselves. at the time this occurred i didnt have a phone in my apt. i had to use my neigbors phone. they were a husband /wife and child that lived right next to me . the husband was in the air force and worked at pease air base  after i made the calls from his phone he was call back to the base for some purpose unknown to me at the time and that he would not discuss with me because i asked him if it had any thing to do with what i saw . he would not give me an answer and left to report for work. but it seemes very odd to me. i still wonder about this and would love to talk with other who might have seen the same thing.
