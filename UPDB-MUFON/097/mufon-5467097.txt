Multiple ufos following the I.s.s. And appearing in and out of clouds and blinking

Watching the I.s.s. Live feed and cam 1 was set to looking downward to the earth, flying past Cuba heading towards Spain, crossing the Atlantic ocean. I began to see a little redish dot that was flying along and below the space station so I started the screen record..
By around 5 mins there were multiple orbs and they were different colour, I thought it may have been light reflections but they were disappearing and reappearing from cloud formations and blinking. 
I often watch the live feed and so it's not unusual for me to see unexplained objects in the fees but these were clearly shadowing the I.s.s and at one point were flying in a triangular formation.
