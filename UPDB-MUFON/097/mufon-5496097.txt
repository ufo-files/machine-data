I took my dog out to go to the bathroom around 11 p.m. as was waiting for her. I looked up I seen traveling southwest to northeast a glowing reddish orange ball. It appeared to be moving across the sky at about the same speed you would see a small plane moving. There was absolutely no sound. It just looked like a glowing orange beach ball flying over my head. It was flying in a straight line at about 1500 to 2500 feet. There was no sound no wings no tail. Just a reddish orange glowing ball. I watched it for 2 to 3 minutes. Till it flew out of view. I know I haven't been able to stop thinking about it.
