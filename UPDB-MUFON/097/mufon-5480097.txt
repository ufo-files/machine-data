{rjl} Saw aprox.5 object in formation heading north,called wife to observe.

I was standing on my front porch and looking at the sky when I observed aprox.5 what appeared to be round objects in a chevron formation going from the south to north toward the San Gabriel mtn.s..I watch for about 2 minutes and decided these did not look like planes,they had no wings so I called my wife to come and look..we both watched for about 3 minutes till they crossed over the mtn.s and out of site..no lights,no noise
