{jpg} Two Green Orb Lights traveling in opposite circles meeting in the center with a flash of light

Standing on the back porch looking to the north on January 28, 2017 at 12:10am. Observed at least 2 oblong oval greenish orb light objects traveling in opposite circles. They traveled in separate opposite directions in a circular pattern meeting briefly together at a center point. The objects had different trajectories. When they met together at the center point there was a brief flash of green light. There was no audible noice. They traversed in the sky for about 10 minutes then disappeared. Caught the images on digital camera at 12:10, 12:11, 12:12 am cst. but will not post them on the sight. Have no clue what they were. Fascinating.
