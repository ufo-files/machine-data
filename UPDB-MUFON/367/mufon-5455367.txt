{cak} Moving lights

Feb 16th 2020 5:55 am
Saw moving lights moving by the Big Dipper. They looked like a star from one of the stars of the Big Dipper. What was strange was I counted thirty-five. There may have been many more because I didn't catch it from the start, I don't believe. I woke up and went out on the back deck for a smoke. I had my back towards the lake and watched a plane go by. I remember thinking how early they must have took off and wondered how cold it was up there because it was cold. I turned around and was looking at the Big Dipper. I was half way done with my cig and tilted my head back just a tad and noticed a moving light, it didn't register until I seen a second one then a third and I started counting. I got to thirty-five and a cloud slowly moved in from the west. I stopped counting and just watched. They moved kinda quick. Perfect straight line and perfect space between them. I bet I could have held a ruler up and they would have lined with the ruler. I held my index and pinky up and the spacing was even. Would like to know if radar picked it up. There was no sound.
