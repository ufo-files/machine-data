{tjd} Looked like a rocket with a cloud around it, changed direction and disappeared quickly

While driving southbound on Highway 101 we saw several beams of lights with clouds around them and behind them, Two of them disappeared quickly upon first sighting, the third continued to be visible for several minutes and change direction from Northwest to westbound after about two minutes. The third shot away quickly but left the trail of clouds for another minute or two before they disappeared also. Almost looked like a supersonic rocket but it changed direction and disappeared too quickly, also didn1t make any sound.
