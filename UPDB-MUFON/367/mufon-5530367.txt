BBQing outside w/ my cousin and looked up and saw 3 white spheres in a large triangular formation.

I was at my cousin's home in Deansboro, NY. We were bbqing an amazing London broil on a nice sunny night outside near his garage for dinner, 7pm. I look up to the sky (because I often do when outside) and say "What are those!?" We see three separate small white orbs moving silently and effortlessly in a straight line through the sky in a large triangle formation, maybe 200 - 300 ft in the air. They weren't moving very fast, but weren't moving slow either. I first thought they were maybe balloons, but the formation they were in I didn't think balloons could maintain in the sky and they didn't even change elevation. They didn't change direction or speed at all and remained in a perfect large triangular formation. My cousin kept telling me they were airplanes way up, but those definitely were not airplanes way up or even hot air balloons (no wings, no markings, nothing emitted, no nothing, just 3 perfect spheres)...they were just moving along like nothing was going on. I kept watching them till I couldn't see them anymore because of the trees and hills. I wish I had my camera on me so I could provide evidence, but my camera was at home w/ my fishing tackle. Never seen anything like that before and to this day can't think of what they possibly could have been other than a UFO. Maybe surveillance drone, I have no clue, there's nothing much for our gov't to survey here in Utica, NY.
