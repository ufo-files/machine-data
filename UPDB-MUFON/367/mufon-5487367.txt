{nd} Observed in area

I was not there. This happened to my friend, his wife and daughter and another man in the area. They were outside their house in the morning and looked to the North. Nearby was this large saucer shaped object hovering several hundred feet above the ground. They could see multicolored lights around the rim flashing on and off. 
they heard a humming noise. The UFO hovered there for a few minutes and then flew rapidly to the East over the mountains toward the Sacramento Valley. My friend called the Air Force and they knew nothing about it. Several days later my friend asked another man who lived in the area if he had seen anything strange in the sky and the man said yes but I was not going to tell anyone it. I knew both of these men. Both hard working Loggers. The wife and daughter still live in the area.
