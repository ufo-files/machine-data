{rjl} Saw object that hovered but heading away from nuclear test facility.

Myself and two friend was going down country road along the Ohio river. I was sitting in back seat when the driver said "look at that plane, it's just hanging there". I scooted over and it was just hanging there. It looked odd that all the windows were mirrored out. As we passed the object it blasted a huge green (odd flouescent green) ball of fire out to the ground. This green fireball was 100 ft in diameter. When it hit the ground it flash up like a huge explosion. But it made no sound. Nothin! Right after the explosion and the most unsettling thing was when I turned my head to look forward, all three of our heads turned at the exact same time. We continued to our destination and carried out normal conversation but never spoke of our encounter. NEVER! The girl that was in the passenger seat died several years ago and we never said a word to each other about it. Worst part is, I got word she wanted too see me. She was on her deathbed. I'm 65 and sick. Wanna tell my story.
