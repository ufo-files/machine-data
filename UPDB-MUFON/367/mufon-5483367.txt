At about 10:45pm in Tacoma, WA, I was traveling west along 74th, and coming up on S Tacoma way, I crossed after the light turned green, it was about 100 meters past it I saw a bright red light over the power sub-station about 1/4 mile ahead on the left, off the road. It was a small spherical object about 8-10' in Dia. It glowed a brilliant red. I saw what looked like a bolt of lightening coming into the craft, which I believe it was taking power from the transformers at the station. My car quickly died, all electronics in it too including my flashlight, CB and watch. I observed the object for about a min. when it stopped taking power, then sped off silently in a NW heading. I was coming from work that night, I work in the electronics field and had just graduated college in same area. I theorize it emanated an EMP, or Electro-Magnetic-Pulse that dampened electric fields around so nothing worked, even the traffic lights flickered. It was a very frightening experience and I kept silent of it until now. I was awed at first seeing this thing, after reading of them, it was my first UFO exp. When it left the vicinity, my car started and I left for home quite shaken I had trouble driving the last mile.
