{jpg} Spinning, bouncing, solid Cross amid thunderheads

Waiting for ride outside business office complex. Large thunderheads against very blue backdrop of sky. I frequently look skyward when hanging outdoors, because I've seen ufos in daylight several times in the last several years in the City of Las Vegas & each ufo has been unique in shape and flight activity. At first, I thought this object might have been a bird (dark color) & wondered how it was so high up near the clouds. Then noticed it was spinning, sunlight reflecting off some parts of its surface as it spun and bounced. It was quite beautiful. The way it moved reminded me of a spinning top. I took 3 photos w/my cell phone. However, I don't know how to get the pics uploaded from my phone to my PC & I don't have internet via phone. The photos show a neon green spinning object in one pic; a pure blue half moon shape in the next; and a small very white colored solid looking oval shape in the last pic. These were all the same spinning Cross object that didn't look like a Cross in the pics. The pics were taken seconds apart. The same pattern of distinct cloud formations in each shot shows the path of the object. It looked playful(?), like it was spinning and bouncing around having fun. I lost sight of the object as it spun off to the northwest, and out of my view.
