Witnessed a bright orange orb moving from south to north a very high rate of speed. No sound detected on a warm still, clear night. Witnessed with two other people.

Was sitting around a campfire at a campground by Pigeon Lake , Alberta with two family members. One of them said "look, a meteorite". We looked and immediately realized this was no meteor. We saw a bright orange orb, spherical in shape, moving at a rate of speed unattainable by aircraft that I have seen. It was silent and moved in a straight line at a constant speed. We three agreed that we have never seen anything like it or even remotely like it. It traversed the sky from horizon to horizon, south to north in about 20 seconds. The sky was clear and warm with little or no wind.
