Three objects flew from NW to S steady line about 30 seconds apart

Standing along Exmouth waiting for the bus facing SW towards the border,when I ended up seeing three long missle like looking planelike images dark gray in colour. The wide view of the sky allowed me to view each flying duration for about 6 to 8 seconds, appearing in the distance. Trees & buildings closer to me limited my seeing where they actually came from and where they were headed. I didn't really think anything when I noticed the first one. Just instantly thought how odd to see an F15 fighter jet fly over so low and no noise. Then I was thinking how neat to see that but then I questioned myself...was that a military plane? Next thing I knew I saw a second one flying along the same path roughly 30 seconds later. They didn't fly very fast just a steady paced glide almost. I tried to listen for the noise and couldnt hear anything other than the cars going by along the street. F15s make loud noise & don't fly that low. If I didn't know better they could've been low enough to fly under the bridges or just overtop. Then to my surprise I saw a third and then my eyes were glued to the sky to see if there would be another. No more appeared. I'm a skeptic but I sure will say this really intrigued me to investigate if any planes were in the area etc. I could find nothing & found nothing of any other people reporting to see them. I was excited & yet shook up about the whole thing & cant get the images out of my mind what I had seen.
