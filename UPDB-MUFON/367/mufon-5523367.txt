About a dozen lights in clusters traveling from north to south in the eastern sky at about 2 o'clock in the sky. when we Saw for about 2 minutes then turned corner, looked, then they were all gone.

It was just around 8pm on Oct. 14th driving east from downtown Lawrence, KS to the fairgrounds.Traveling on 19th street, my friend and I saw in the sky in front of us, (East) a cluster of lights traveling North to South. There was a larger group of lights followed by a smaller group. They were in a random group not in a pattern. It looked like they were moving moderately across the sky. When we turned a corner and looked up again, they were gone. They were yellow, white round lights. Could they have some kind of weather balloons? We both thought there were too many to be planes.
