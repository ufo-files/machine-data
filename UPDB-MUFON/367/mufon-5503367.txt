It came from south, was heading north

Me and i my mother were at the balcony smoking cigarettes and i usually stare up in the sky. Then suddenly i saw this object moving from south. I said to my mother "hey, that one is moving". We both saw it. First i thought it was a satellite because i have seen satellites before. After a few seconds the object started to turn northwest. After that happened i told my mother "this aint no satellite", neither was it a plane. At one moment the object disappeared for a second and came back while the object was turning until it vanished entirely from our view because it moved right over the building we were standing.
