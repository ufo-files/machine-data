My mother, father and my self at 10 years old were coming back from visiting my relatives in Salineville, OH.. On Route 30 west of New Alexander, OH my mother told my dad that she sees something coming our direction. She continues to watch it as it got closer. Then she told dad pull off to the side of the road. we got out and observed this triangular shaped vehicle pass over us. NO sound just three white lights, one on each corner. As it passed over it blocked out the sight of stars above. Coming out of the south going north it continued on until it was out of sight
