Lights observed

Saw 4 or 5 objects moving quickly. My first thought was that I was seeing F-16s out of Buckley AFB as 2 followed the first one in formation with another trailing a few seconds behind. 

I lost sight of the first object in the clouds and turned my attention back to the other 3 I could still see. I started to get curious about the blinking lights as they didn’t match nav lights I’m familiar with. The main “body” of the object and a second smaller light on the left hand side would blink opposite. At this point an object passed directly over me and it appeared to be fairly low and moving quickly but was silent. This was the point at which it occurred to me that I wasn’t observing F-16s at a distance. 

I got my phone out to try and get some video of what I was seeing and only managed to capture one for several seconds before disappearing. There’s prominent lense flare at the end of the video that shouldn’t be confused with the objects.
