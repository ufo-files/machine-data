{jpg} 8-10 objects just south of lunar eclipse flying in circle formation, then each one separated from group flew in spiral motion & disappeared

I was outside of my apartment with my dog viewing the lunar eclipse and trying to take pictures of the event. Below the moon appeared a circle of lights, approximately 8-10 of them. I thought they were birds at first but they were too high in the atmosphere and these things were glowing white a little. There was a bit of light cloud cover so they may have been behind the clouds. They were in a circle formation, flying higher, then one by one they peeled off from the group, flew in a spiral like a number 9 and disappeared. I waited to see if they would come back, but I didn't see them again. The whole event lasted less than a minute or two, then denser clouds rolled in and the moon wasn't visible any longer so I went back inside. 
My feelings were those of disbelief and curiosity. I think I said aloud "holy ****, do you see that?" to the dog. Afterwards I had a little trouble falling asleep because while I'm not a believer, I was also concerned that they saw me seeing them and I felt like I may be "in trouble" for seeing them.
