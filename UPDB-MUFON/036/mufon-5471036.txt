{cak} Bright glowing craft made 90 degree turn - then shot up toward NW of town

Was driving along north mountain road near first baptist church ten minutes to 11pm, when I saw an odd shapped bright object hovering above the tree line right above a ridge. The object moved slowly and then made a 90 degree turn and shot off in the north west direction of town. Way too fast to be an airplane and was not a shooting star either. It was probably about 500-800 feet from the tree line. The light made no noise and there did not seem to be any smoke or anything coming from it. I did notice the screen on my cell phone went blank and my battery died. The phone had at least 60% battery life in it at the time.
