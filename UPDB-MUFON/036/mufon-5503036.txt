{kt}Looked like a shooting star with a long tail but was way to low to be a star.

I was standing in my backyard enjoying the cool air and looking up at the stars when a ball of light with a long tail trailing behind it making no noise streaking over my yard never changing direction or altitude all I could do was freeze, I couldn't move I was aww struck and could feel every sense in my body just go crazy like when there is extreme danger around. after about a second or two the light just seem to dim out and it wasn't visible anymore, I didn't see any smoke or any kind of cloud that would have been left behind in it's trail like a rocket would. Once it was gone and my body stopped tingling I composed myself and I dismissed it as a shooting star but now that I think about it there is no way it would be that low to the ground.
