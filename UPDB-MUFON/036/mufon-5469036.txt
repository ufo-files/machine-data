{cak} Observed Silent, fast moving white orb with long, glowing tail from SSE to NNW.

On back deck of residence at approximately 2045 on 19 April 2018 while having a (real tobacco) cigarette, observation as per "Short Description" entry was made. Sighting was made against the backdrop of a very dark sky, no moon, but with some stars in evidence. As object was between those stars and myself, my first thought was "meteor". However, having observed these on prior occasions, this one was somewhat slower and, it appeared to have a flat, terrain following trajectory as it crossed my line of vision from SSW to NNE neither increasing nor decreasing altitude which I would not try to estimate as I had no way to judge its distance from me.
