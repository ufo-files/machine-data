{jpg} Saw cbemical trail come out of nowhere and ascend

I was parked on the southwest part of Wichita Falls when I saw a bright white chemical trail that appeared out of nowhere below some clouds and start climbing straight up. You don't see planes acting this way unless they are in an exhibition of sorts. I got my phone out, zoomed and focused it to take a few photos of the object at 652pm. After I took a few photos, I then turned phone onto video mode at 654pm to follow object into the clouds. The whole event was about 3 minutes in duration. It did not reappear after attempting to drive in westerly direction. I reviewed the photos and video afterwards. The first two photos sent in showed some type of distortion around the object. The video possibly showed a bluish object on top of the chemical trail, IDK. If this is found to be something worth posting, I will say these recurring events are overwhelming.
