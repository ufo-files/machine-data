(GS) Bright light over gravel pit/state land hovering then changing direction and flashing away

This occured while I was driving south to I-94 through the waterloo rec area, and just a few miles before the freeway, while traveling east about to turn south just after 11pm. I first disregarded the extremely bright object as I initially thought "Radio tower light". I then noticed it changed direction abrubtly to the south leaving behind a slight streak of orange/fiery light before randomly pulsating in intensity. I witnessed this for just a few moments, then it returned near its original position very quickly and gained altitude. Very quickly it darted off into the North/Northeast so fast that it was almost instantaneously. I am reporting this because I saw another sighting of a similar sounding object just east of where I saw this one posted on this website and it literally made me flush white and feel sick when I read the report. The waterloo rec. area will have my eyes in the sky from now on when driving through there.
