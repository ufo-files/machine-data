{nd} Saw a brilliant golden/yellow flash 3 times.

ON February 20, 2017 at about 9:30pm I was camping at the Vogel Canyon Recreation Area, about 10 miles south of La Junta, Co. It was very cloudy that night, and few stars were visible. I was watching a plane (?) come from the west as it went in and out of cloud cover. Right near it, I saw 3 brilliant golden flashes in succession. It was very strange since it was a heavy cloud cover and I could see very few stars/planets shining through. The flashes were very, very bright....brighter than anything else in the sky. I watched that area of the sky for over half an hour afterwards and saw nothing. I don't think I have ever seen such a bright light in the sky....ever. Except the sun.
