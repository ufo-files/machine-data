I took multiple pictures of a Blue Green glowing cylinder objects moving across sky

On Nov 16, 2020 at about 6pm I was riding in a golf cart down my street. I was taking pictures of the crescent moon in the post sunset sky; the sky was not quite dark yet. When I got home and looked at the pictures I saw in addition to the crescent moon two strange objects in all of the pictures. The objects glowed blue-green and were cylinder shaped right next to another in some pictures, but like a boomerang in one picture. From the first picture to the last, 5 total - they were taken in succession less than a minute of total time, in the pictures, the objects moved from the right to the left, which was north to south as I was facing west. I didn't notice the objects when I took the pictures, but only when I got home and looked at them, I zoomed in on them and took screenshots to see the object better. I transferred the pictures to my laptop at about 10:50 pm the same day and reported the incident on the MUFON website. I uploaded 10 files 5 original jpg's and 5 screenshots of the same pictures zoomed in.
