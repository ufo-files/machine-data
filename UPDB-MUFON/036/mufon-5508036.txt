{cak} Large fleet of orange orbs over St. Louis

As reported to ASD by phone today: 
I was near the Art Palace Museum at Forest Park in St. Louis in front near the statue of a king - this is at the top of Art Hill. I turned and saw orange lights in the sky - a lot of them - waves of them flying in loose V shapes with 10-15 lights in each V shape and moving very fast. There was no sound and they were high in the sky - perhaps at 2,000 feet. I could see the lights start at the horizon in the distance and move across the sky towards downtown St. Louis. I watched them for about for 30 minutes. Someone else was nearby - I could hear them talking but did not see them or talk to them. The objects came from the direction opposite of downtown St. Louis (west?), and they flew in a straight line. I saw jets flying over the area earlier that where very loud. These were much faster than jets. It would take a hand held at arms length to block out one of the lights. I would say there were 10-15 lights in each V pattern, and many V patterns flew over. I did not have a camera with me at the time. I saw another similar report so someone else must have seen this as well.
