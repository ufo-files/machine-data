As I looked up at them there was a super bright light, which i figured was Venus or Jupiter, and then i thought it was an airplane when i saw the blinking lights. The lights flashed blue and green in conjunction with thewhite light. While i stared at it it never moved, in fact it didn't move from the position it was in for a few hours. The other heavenly bodies moved as they should but this light did not move. I could see it from my living room window.
