4 Bright red/orange orbs, flew straight & disappeared

Sitting with some friends at coffee when one of them facing me said "Hey, whats that?" and pointed to something behind me. I turned around to see what was clearly a helicopter or airplane flying but surrounding it a little further in the distance were 4 "orbs", glowing orange/red. Looked like they could have possibly been in a v formation, but not a part of one unit, and the angle at which we were looking at them made it also difficult to tell if there was a true formation in the flying pattern. The orbs did not hover but rather continued to move in a straight line and then one at a time each orb slowly dimmed out until none of them could be seen anymore. This happened in about a minute time span, maybe slightly more or less. Nothing more was seen and the helicopter/airplane just continued on its way. 

I was present during the first Phoenix lights in AZ and saw them first hand. Although these did not hover or seem to be in a clear flying formation, the color and shape seemed very similar to the first sighting in 1997. 

I am not a huge UFO person and I am not a person who would typically seek out this kind of site, however this is not the first time I have seen this in the last couple years and it frequently seems to be unreported, as if no one else saw it. So, I thought I would throw this out there and see if anyone else saw what I saw. I was at McClintock and Southern when it happened in Tempe.
