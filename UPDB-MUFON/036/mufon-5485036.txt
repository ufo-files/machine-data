{rjl} Taking pictures outside of sky. Downloaded to computer and noticed triangles on several images.

I went outside to the back deck to take pictures of an incoming storm to the west. I turned to take some pictures of the sky to the east which was more calm. I took about 100 pictures using a Sony Digital Camera. I did not notice anything out of the ordinary so I went back inside and downloaded the images to my laptop. I was showing the pictures to my daughter, and immediately she noticed several frames had transparent, triangle shaped images. They were not cloud-shaped triangles but rather very distinct, triangle/tetrahedral shapes that looked the same color of the sky. I am attaching one of the images that I did only minor enhancements to, such as reversing the image to negative so that the blue sky appears brighter and the white clouds appear darker. Then I colorized the image. I have about 12 images with the triangles in them, the original digital copies. The one attached shows what could be three triangles, in a group, and possibly four-sided, such as a tetrahedron. Closer look shows light or white areas on the triangles which could be windows. There is a dark area to the upper right of the image that looks like a face, but that is just the reverse value of the clouds.
