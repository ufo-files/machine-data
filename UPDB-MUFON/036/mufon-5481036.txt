While walking on a abandoned railway line myself and a friend observed a bright orange sphere that seemed to be hovering over the tree line it moved in rapid horizontal and vertical movements and seemed to disappear into the night sky.

While walking south on a abandoned railway line approximately 4 km north of the town of Lindsay Ontario around 8:30 pm my friend and I spotted a bright orange glowing sphere shaped object that appeared to be hovering over the tree line. 

At first we thought the object was a low flying aircraft but it remained stationary with no noise or flashing lights we scanned the sky for other moving aircraft and their was none. We observed the object for about 10 minutes while doing so the object seemed to pulsate and flicker.

We continued our walk and buy 9pm we had returned to my truck took off our day packs stored them in the backseat and while exiting the parking lot of the conservation area we noticed the object again . This time the object had traveled to the south east part of the sky and was at a much higher altitude again we scanned for moving aircraft and their was none. The object made a series of rapid horizontal and vertical movements its bright orange glow seamed to fade out as it appeared to be drawn into the night sky I checked the time and it was 9:15 pm. 

Once the object disappeared we drove home to Lindsay and arrived at 9:30 pm.
