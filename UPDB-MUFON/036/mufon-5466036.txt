Cylindrical with lights surrounding perimeter. Blocked the stars while passing over

On the night of September 03, 2018, my family and I (6 adults) were having a campfire in the backyard at my son's house in Devon, Alberta. We had been by the fire for several hours, and the 3 men were having "spiritual" discussions, which included talk of UFOs. Women were having a different discussion (as is often the case lol). I went behind a shed to urinate, and saw a bright light flash through the sky from east to west. It did not appear to me to be a normal piece of debris, and I wondered about it for a minute. I went back to the fire and told the boys what I had seen. I sat down in my chair and (by the way the stars were as brilliant as I have seen in years that night, and we were commenting about it throughout the evening) looked straight up into the night sky. I immediately saw a "craft", floating silently from northwest to southeast, in a straight line. It had 4 dimly lit reddish orange lights on each side, and was "cigar" shaped. Shocked, it took me a second or two to yell at my son to "look".....and he did. He then watched it with me, as it continued to move in a straight line. Our field of vision (between trees) was about 40 to 50 feet, before it moved behind trees and was out of sight. My son is an artist, and next day drew what he saw. It is exactly what I saw.
