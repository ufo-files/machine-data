Bubble like Orb

Around 4:30 I walked outside on my porch. It was still bright outside and I noticed multiple 'Chem Trail' plains flying far into the distance. After a minute of watching I see what could only be described as a bubble floating off into the distance. It was much bigger than the planes and looked as though it was around the same area as them. I turned around to get my husband and looked back real quick before getting him and watch the 'Bubble' float further away and then it was gone in an instant.
