First sighting was SSE traveling in an apparent straight line WSW. At first thought it must be a plane or helicopter. Ruled out helicopter due to no ssound no rotors. Ruled out plane last because no wings or tail structure or blinking lights. It had the appearance of two balls stuck together moving together as if stuck together. Thought it was possible balloons but have seen balloons in flight there was no tumbling or elevation change. It seemed to emit a blue silver gliow. As the sun was low in the west I thought of reflection possible but there no glints or flaring. Everyone seemed to agree it did not appear normal. It continued on the same straight line path without interruption until it was out of sight.
