{rjl} Two red-orange lights observed flying in formation in night sky above hill, then flying out of sight into stars.

My friend and I were driving to his house when we saw two red-orange lights hovering in the sky. They at first appeared to be right over a hill we were passing, but then seemed very far away. As we drove, pulled over to watch them and saw another person pulled over. I exited the car and paced when the person in the other car asked me "what do you think that is?" I told him I didn't know. As I went back to watching the UFOs, I noticed they were moving away at an alarming rate, orbiting each other and moving back and forth. Eventually, they left our field of vision.
