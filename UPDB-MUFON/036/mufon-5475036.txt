{cak} Moving, flashing white light in W sky stopping then moving E.

I woke up early aprrox 4 am. Went to bathroom. Went into West facing office to look out the window as we've seen weird lights in the past so it became a habit to do so. Noticed a bright white flash in Western sky then it dimmed. It slowly tracked East and flashed again. It then stopped. It slowly moved again and would randomly flash about every 10 seconds or so. Got my wife to look out to have a witness. She also wondered what it could be. Saw an awesome shooting star about 4:30 am as well. I went outside and watched it in the Eastern sky for a few more minutes as it slowly moved in a North-Northeast direction. It was very strange as it seemed to move randomly and very different from the half dozen or so satellites that we also observed. It almost seemed under intelligent control. The only camera we have is our cell phones which are useless in low light conditions.
