{jpg} Driving down the interstate and watched this object go by

It was the middle of the night, I was driving from Bismarck To Fargo for my brothers wedding. It was a bright moon lit night. So bright you could turn off your head lights and drive. Out of the corner of my eye I saw a huge object. I leaned forward in the car almost touching the steering wheel as I watched this giant object go by. It didn’t have a light on it and as it crossed my path all I could think was that huge, giant object is in no way aerodynamic. I was coming up on an exit and was so excited by what I just witnessed I pulled off and stopped the car and got out. That’s when I noticed there were two airplanes, I thought to be jets, in the path of where that thing I just saw was. I was so pumped up I wanted to stop other cars in the area to see if they saw it. But I didn’t, and I never told the story until now. The object was enormous and just drifted by, I didn’t hear any noise. I’m not sure what I saw and UFO’s were the farthest thing from my mind but that thing was huge like watching a building go by.
