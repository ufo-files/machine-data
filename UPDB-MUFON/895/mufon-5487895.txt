Mexico City Pyramids Trip, UFO appears in picture, did not notice object until that night at hotel while looking at cell phone pictures we took

We were spending the day at the Teotihuacan Pyramids in Mexico City. While walking to the Pyramid of the Sun my wife and step daughter climbed one of the small temples to our left side. I stayed below with our baby in the stroller. I snapped a picture of them with my iphone and didn't really examine it since we were probably going to take many more. That night while back at the hotel I was going over the pictures and noticed an object in the sky. I estimate the clouds were less than 2000 feet from the ground from experience as a private pilot. When we got back home to Arizona my wife zoomed the picture in on her computer. I am attaching the picture and the zoomed in copy.
