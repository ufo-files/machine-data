Slow moving Chevron

I was an amateur astronomer Living in Clark County, Wisconsin. I was using my 10 inch Newtonian telescope. I stepped away from the ocular when something caught My eye Coming from the southwest sky. It was very high in the sky. It was a clear crisp night. Stars were brilliant. I observed a dark chevron shaped craft with lights forming the outline on the bottom.
It moved quietly and slowly directly overhead and proceeded from the southeast to the North west until the lights faded out of sight. Presumably it moved up. I never saw it again.
