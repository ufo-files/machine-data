Triangle Object traveled from east to west over over valley

Observed a dark triangle object with 3 lights, one at each corner, moving slowly from east to west, almost silently down the valley. Did not hear any sound until I went outside. Then could hear a very low pitch almost imperceptible "werrrrr, werrrrr werrrr" sound, as it passed down the valley. I watched the object as it moved down the valley for about 20 seconds when it started to rise slowly and then just shot straight up into the sky. . Total horizontal observation was not more than about 20-30 seconds, once it went verticle, it accelerated and disappeared.
