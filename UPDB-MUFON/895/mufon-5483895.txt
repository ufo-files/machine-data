boomerang shape no lights fast

Lying awake in bed just looking at the sky. I saw something moving really fast got out of bed before it was out of view watched it zoom in a straight path before it made like a u turn and disappear. The object made me think of a glider. I realised it was moving too fast to be an aeroplane. Then when I saw the uturn I realised it wasn't a plane. It was quite fascinating to watch and it happened so quickly. Wish I had had the time to grab my phone to record it. Got me excited to see it I woke my partner who wasn't too amused at being woken up. Would love to see more so I will keep an eye out in future.
