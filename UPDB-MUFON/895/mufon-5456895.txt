{tjd} Bright Light

On Sunday November 24th, 2019 at 20:08 My dad, and my uncle and I, were on top of Colonel Pauley Dr, in the Belle area of Kanawha Country in West Virginia. GPS coordinates reading upon arrival to view the sky. 265° W 38°13'0" N 81°30'10" W. 1,380 ft elevation, current weather conditions temperature was 35° and clear. While upon the top of the mountain we witnessed a white bright light that was in a spherical shape illuminate above the tree line about 100-150 feet.  It suddenly appeared in the Northeast sky traveling to the Southwest then done a zigzag pattern (left to right) then then suddenly disappeared to the Southwest horizon.  There was no sounded that between the three of us could recall hearing. The whole duration lasted 3-4 seconds. First thought it was a shooting star, however, after observing a shooting star right after, we determined that what we saw was not a shoot-star do to the brightness, and did not have the characteristic of a shoot star as it did not leave a trail or stay in a straight line.
