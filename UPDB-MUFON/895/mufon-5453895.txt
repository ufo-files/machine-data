Black streak in the sky

Driving into Winnipeg at around 8:30 in the morning on Dec 14th, There was a big black streak in the sky, not moving. I thought it was a contrail at first, but it had no beginning or end and no sign that it was dissipating. It just hung there for about 15 min then it was gone.
