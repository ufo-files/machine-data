I am finally attempting to relay the facts of someone's viewing of a UFO in approximately the middle of 1971. I was on active duty status at a radar site on the US/Canadian border when a woman called into our site, relaying her seeing a UFO hover over her house and scare the daylights out of her, her children and their dogs. Since I was on duty that evening, I was able to verify that an unknown object (blip) was viewed and followed on my radar screen for approximately one half hour. So, I have kept this secret all these years and feel I need to let your organization know what the Air Force is doing regarding UFO's and their own people.
