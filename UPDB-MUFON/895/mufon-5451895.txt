First started as a blip in the tree line, then the blip started moving across the field, over two fences and beside my family barn

At 1:17am I was looking out my window towards the back field, facing north/north east. In the upper corner of the field I saw a blip of white light and then it disappeared, a few seconds later it reappeared a couple dozen feet diagonally down left, closer to my house, it continued to do so until it appeared at the barn near our house. It then repeated the pattern once more. After this I’ve begun to experience many cases of Déjà Vu and neighbour dogs are extremely agitated sounding.
