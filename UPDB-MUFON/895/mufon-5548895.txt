Eight foot glowing white sphere suddenly switched on and slowly floated through the woods at five in the morning. Witnessed by my son and I.

My son and I went out deer hunting before dawn and split up to go to our separate spots, about a half mile apart. Shortly after I got settled, a glowing white sphere suddenly switched on in the shallow valley below me and slowly meandered around the trees until I lost sight of it as it floated deeper into the woods and then over a pond. The sphere was about eight feet in diameter and hovered about five feet off the ground. When I reunited with my son, several hours later, he described the same object to me without me having said a word to him.
