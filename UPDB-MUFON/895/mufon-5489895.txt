I was out in my backyard at around 5:30pm smoking a cigarette when something caught my attention out of the corner of my eye. What I observed was a white/cloud like color sphere object moving west to east. I was only able to see this object for like 2 secs before it went into a cloud. I waited to see if it would come out the other side because this object looked like a white/cloud color perfect sphere and I though this was odd. I waited for about a minute to see if it would come out the other side but it never did. I first thought it might of been a balloon but at the level of the cloud that it went into, it would of have to been a pretty large balloon to appear about the size of the object I saw. It could of been a weather balloon but it wasn't gaining altitude and was traveling west to east at about 300mph. I found the object very odd to see in the sky and that's what made me search online to see if anyone else saw this object but I couldn't find anyone. I wouldn't of came onto this site if I didn't think it was strange to see. In my 27 years on this earth I've never seen something that perfect of a sphere flying and be the same color of the cloud it went into.
