{rjl} 4 BRIGHT LIGHTS WITNESSED ON A SILENT NIGHT

I just got off work got out of my truck and noticed something up above me and wasn't sure what it was, first I thought it might be balloons .note it was 4:35 am clear night (the day before it rained in California this year), I watched the four very bright yellow orange lights. the object appeared to just stay in one position, as if it were watching me. strange feeling for maybe 10 mins. or more. I took a few pictures and a short video of this thing , I wasn't sure but I think I was actually viewing a UFO. it made no noise, it was a very quite morning, so I know it wasn't a plane or helicopter. I was standing watching this thing in awe. I had chills from the top of my head down my spine to the bottom of my heels very strange feeling. I watched this thing then it turned in a south eastern ward direction very slowly so I figured it would be moving away from me. so I got into my truck, shut the door and briefly turned away from it and looked to insert my key in my truck ignition then when I looked back at this thing it was completely gone, vanished. I turned off my truck stepped out again and it was no where to be seen, now this was all in just seconds of me getting in my truck??? I got out walked around for another 10 minutes or so and saw nothing. it was completely gone? I've never been a believer of UFO's but after this incidence I do believe we are not alone!!! I went home and woke my girlfriend and told her about what I had witnessed.
