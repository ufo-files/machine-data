{tjd} Glowing beam trail light that zigzag across night sky.

Camping in the Grand Canyon National Park, AZ Sept. 18th, 2019. Approx. 8-9 PM while viewing the Milky Way Galaxy I saw a glowing beam light directly over head that zigzagged across the sky towards the North. As it moved from point A to B, it would fade and become bright again when it changed direction. Watched it zigzag across the night sky for about 30-40 seconds before it disappeared.
