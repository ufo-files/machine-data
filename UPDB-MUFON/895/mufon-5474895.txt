{rjl} I was taking a picture of church peaks before evening mass and saw nothing - later when viewing pic 3 orbs were above church

December 24, 2014 my husband and I made our way to St. Thomas of Villanova for midnight mass. Being our first time at the beautiful church I had to snap a picture before going inside. I quickly looked up and snapped one picture. It wasn't till after mass and we returned home did I look at the picture and saw the three lights. I showed my husband and he was equally puzzled since we both had not seen the lights while walking up to the church. As the photo shows there were other people around but no one seemed to notice the lights.
