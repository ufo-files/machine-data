{nd} 3 Unmarked Black (otherwise normal looking) Helicopters

Was Traveling west on Queen Blvd, Stopped at the traffic signal on Pacific Ave. My Girlfriend and I witnessed 3 Unmarked Black helicopters Travel South to North at Very low altitude and at a moderate to "awkwardly fast but slow enough not to look out of place" Speed. The Helicopters where Very close and I have great eye sight and for the life of me couldnt make out ANY marking on any of them. They were very normal looking Helicopters, Not unlike an older military chopper (one that a kid might draw if he was a talented artist). 
I cant remember ever in my life seeing 3 choppers flying in formation, I have also never seen an unmarked black helicopter, seeing 3 of them that low to the ground was really out of place for my area.
