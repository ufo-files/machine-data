{cak} 2 bright red and white objects seen over Medford Oregon moving at high rates of speed with amazing directional changes.

On June 15th 2018 at 10:30 PM Pacific time, my wife and I were in our backyard in Medford Oregon. We were facing North, Northeast at 13 degrees. In the distance was a bright red light with a bright white light above and below. I1d estimate it to be 3 to 5 thousand feet in the air and maybe 10 to 15 miles away. Difficult to judge distance without anything to compare it to. As we were watching the lights move our direction, another exact object come out of nowhere at an incredible high rate of speed and started traveling in the same direction right next to the first lights we saw. Suddenly the two broke away from each other and made turns left, right, up and down then returned next to each other. The one light flew away at very high rate of speed going Northeast. The other light turned about 90 degrees and disappeared in about 2 or 3 seconds. The objects were able to go forwards, backward, up, down and side to side. There1s no plane that could make theses flight changes and no helicopter that could fly that fast or be seen at the distance they were from us.
