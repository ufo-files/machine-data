Saw a “shooting star-like” activity

My family and I were welcoming our oldest daughter home from her trip and were talking outside with the porch lights off when our youngest saw something in the sky that was lower than an airplane should fly. We live between two major airports in DFW and are familiar with the flight patterns and flight ceiling of the airplanes landing and taking off when she saw an unusually low object slowly approaching our neighborhood. It was very slow and low then hovered in spot for about a minute or so. Then we all saw a “shooting star” and then the object flew close where the shooting star was. It hovered there for about 5 minutes then BLINKED OUT. This object did not have the colored navigation lights that blinked like an airline jet would. We could only see white light that did not pulsate.
