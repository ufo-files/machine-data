ufo hovering over cemetery and bayou

It was halloween night i was 6yrs old my grandma, aunt and brother just walked out our house to trick or treat. Before we crossed the bridge we saw an Ufo hovering over the cemetery and bayou. It was big disc shape with bright orange lights around it, there was no noise, and in minutes after starring at it, it turned off its lights and shot straight up in the air in seconds. I was so young i just felt very excited to see it and then carry on to get candy. As for my family they were very intrigued by it. There was other witnesses that night behind us. 
My grandma did take pictures with a disposal camera but they came out blurry and now they are gone don't know what happened to them.
When we tell other people they don't really believe us but we know it did happened. 

I recently watched Hangar 1, which prompt me to tell my story.
