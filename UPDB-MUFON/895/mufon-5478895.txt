A Hovering UFO

I went out to bakery , early at evening. I Used to check the sky all the time to search for UFOs. Suddenly I saw a light above my head that come closer and the makes more distance to high. At first looking I thought it is lantern but The out light of this UFO was changing randomly from silver to orange. It was flying stationary and I stayed and observed it, it was cloudy and this UFO was the only light out there. I started to capture video but unfortunately I ran out of storage on my mobile phone, suddenly it changed the speed and direction and flied to the east. I chased it by my car but it changed the direction again to North-East and did speed up and I have lost it like it has been disappeared from the sky.
