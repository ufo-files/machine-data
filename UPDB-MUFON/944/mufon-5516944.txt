{nd} ufo in daylight?

I was walking home early eve on 7/25/2012 about 6:30 pm in central Ohio, when I spotted something silver & shinny to the left of me (the northern sky view). At first glance I thought it to be a plane, but there was no sound & it just hovered. There was an plane that was in the west flying south & the object did not share any characteristics of any aircraft Ive ever seen. Curious I stopped walking & watched it (trying not to blink, Lol) & it seemed to just float, no movement that I could see. After about 5 minutes of observation & skepticism I felt that I could not "explain" &/or define what the object was. I watched it for another 2 minutes, then it flickered and was gone... So my question is did anyone else see this in or around Ohio on Wednesday July 25th 2012??? Sorry I don't have any pix or vids, it was daylight & I tried to get a pic but the object didn't show up on my cell cam.
