Luminous orbs

Several luminous orbs (~30+ over the course of an hour and a half) made straight flight paths from the west to the northeast over the sky at various speeds and various altitudes. Most were spotted using night vision goggles and a handful of them were able to be seen by the naked eye. The brightest one could be viewed with the naked eye and glowed a faint red as it transited across the sky. Another one appeared to the southeast and appeared to raise straight up in altitude, growing further away as it traveled upward. In another instance, two of these luminous orbs traveled at the exact same speed about 5 degrees behind the leading orb in seeming formation as they transited across the sky. These orbs would not disappear beneath the horizon, but would rather blink out, as if turning off.
