Solid soft white ball

My family and I just got back from the movies and I was sitting on my deck and my 7 yr old daughter was sitting on my lap and she said "look daddy a shooting star." I looked up in the sky facing the north west and I seen a low flying approximately 40-50 feet above tree top level. A solid white ball a little bigger than a basket ball. I turned my radio off to hear if it was making any sound I did not hear a sound. I watched it come straight at my house for about a 30 seconds and then I went into the house and got my camera a shot a short video of it. It flew right over the house and continued south east. I watched it until it went out of view of my camera I lost visual on it. 
About a week and a half or two weeks later I was on my deck with a friend and we were talking and I noticed a small light move in my left peripheral vision. I said to my friend that I thought I seen something and we looked but did not seen any thing. There were allot of clouds that night. Then about ten seconds later we seen it. It was a small light that turned really bright it was almost a intense spot light. Then it turned into what I call a "disco ball" This went on for about five to ten seconds then it disappeared. I have no idea what it was but it has been bothering me every since I seen it.
