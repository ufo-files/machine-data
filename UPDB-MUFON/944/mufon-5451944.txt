Moving reflective and/or pulsating orb

I was sitting outside on my patio with my partner when we noticed an orb, about the size of a bb at arms length, maybe a little smaller, in the northeast sky and slowly moving in an easterly or southeasterly direction. There was fine, somewhat transparent cloud cover and the object appeared to be within the clouds (sometimes more visible than at other times). It also appeared to be reflecting sunlight and/or pulsating - hard to discern which. If I had to pick a color, I would say it was more white than silver. The time was 3:02pm. I videoed the object with my iPhone and was actually able to see it afterward (it's hard to pick up) and take screen grabs. It was clearly spherical (or round) without any indication of wings or fuselage. There was no noise that I could hear (it was far away), nor was there any contrail.
