I Saw Same Vermont UFO

I wanted to wait until another Vermonter reported what I saw in Wocester, Vt. on the evening of the 24th.....I saw the same thing. A round ball of VERY bright light headed due north. This was a bit after 9 o'clock. I remember initially thinking it was a shooting star but there was cloud cover. I had commented to my girlfriend that I couldn't see any stars because of it. Although it was moving fast, it wasn't as fast as a shooting star.....and it was below the clouds. What was very strange for me was, after watching it cover about 3/4 of the sky-----out go the lights. Nothing. Like someone flicked the switch. I am retired Law Enforcement....30 yrs. This, just like it was for the woman who wrote earlier....was exciting to see.
