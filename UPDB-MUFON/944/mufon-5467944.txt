{cak} Red/white Bright star like object moving fast towards the NE approx 2 miles away from us

At approximately 10pm a star like object passed by while myself and one other person were closing up the shed. We first noticed it while it was directly north of us. There are several helicopters, private jets, small sesnas that fly over the house on a regular bases. At first we thought it may be a helicopter the light was so bright. But we concluded that there was no noise and couldn't be that. I suggested later, but there was no wind and after later checking the 2mph wind in the area was heading se. The object shined like a star and would randomly project a red light in between the white gleam. The entire sighting lasted about 2 min. It moved at a speed that was faster than any plane / helicopter we've ever seen before (daily air traffic). Lastly, as it made it's way farther away from us the light dimmed substantially within a matter of seconds and then it was simply a grey spec in the sky that eventually vanishes before our eyes. On the drive to work this morning I noted 3 black helicopters taking off in unoson from ft Lauderdale executive airport heading east around approx 930am. I didn't think about this detail until your questionnaire prompted us to specific if we see or saw helicopters prior to event. I would not classify this as a fireball as the color that projected from this object almost had a laser like quality to it. I'm still sceptical if maybe it wasn't a lantern. But the mechanic that was with me assured me that a lantern would not be able to move that quickly when there was virtually no wind.
