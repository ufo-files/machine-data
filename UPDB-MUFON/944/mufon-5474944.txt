Most bizarre thing I've ever witnessed

I was watching the clouds move rapidly like I've never seen. I started seeing theses little orbs in the sky everywhere over the ocean and just dropping . Almost like they lost power they would drop like a crash. Then I witnessed at first I thought three objects in the air moving to west slowly . They were too far to record from my view so I got as close as I could . I managed to capture them in broad daylight moving in formation very slowly almost at a hover. I couldn't believe my eyes I believed in it but I always wanted proof and now I have it . The clouds were thick, shorty after I lost sight of them due to visibility.
