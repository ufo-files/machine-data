An intensely bright egg shaped light in the NW above a band of clouds caught my eye as I turned off to the road home. I turned on Indian Hill Road heading west and it was just to the north of the road in the far distance. It's size and brightness caught my attention. I assumed it was a plane from the airport but when it remained in place, very bright and very large until drove in my drive, I was considering other things (weather balloon - but seemed far too large for that). It glowed but it did not seem reflective like with other weather balloons I have seen and was a single light so did not seem like a plane and the size was troubling. I could still see it as I parked in my drive. I went into the house to get a pair of binoculars but when I came back out it was gone; did not see where. There was a band of clouds just above the horizon. I have never seen such a large and bright egg shaped light before.
