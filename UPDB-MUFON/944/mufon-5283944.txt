The object was directly over NAS-CC. I was getting out of my car at my apartment and felt like something was in the air over me. I looked up and saw a triangular object hidden in the clouds. There were few clouds at that time but what there was were floating on by but this cloud formation stayed stationary. The photos I took do not show the object as I saw it but look fuzzy (cloudy) I took the photos but had to go inside, clouds passed under the object and the sky clouded up. I have no idea where it went but suspect it went straight up and out of the area. My skin felt dry and static feeling but did not last longer than a few minutes.

The file photo at first is hard to spot as the triangle is in the blue of the clouds but quite large. It's almost hidden in plain sight. It is in the center of the photo. If there were occupants inside this triangle, they had a perfect view looking down at NAS-CC.
