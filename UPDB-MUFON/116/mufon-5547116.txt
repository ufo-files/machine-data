Red, Yellow, White lights flashing on craft in the nights sky

Well I was driving home when I seen what seemed to be a stationary craft, that's what immediately caught my eye. Then when I looked close the light scheme was not anything I had ever seen before on a plane at night, it was red, yellow and what looked to be white one right after another. I called my friend a mile away he stepped out and spotted the object as well, but in a matter of 15-20secs it was out of sight. Then about 10mins later the same friend as well as another went back out to take a second look, this time they spotted two of these crafts with the same lighting. I have seen similar reports, this is very interesting.
