{rjl} Ufo was Circular with four white dome lights on bottom

The circular shaped object came into view as me and my brother played in the backyard. The object was so close, an adult could have touched it if standing on the house. When the object came into view, my brother was terrified and screaming for it to go away. However, I remember feeling as though the object was playful and I was screaming for it to land. It drifted slowly over our heads within around 20 ft off the ground. It was a vibrating sound and continued over the house. My bother says that it jetted up into the air, but I dont remember that part. We were both witnesses to this amazing event. The object had 4 larg domed lights on bottom that had a soft milky white glow. I have told this story to mufon before. Looking on google earth at where this event took place, there is an apparent trail over the tree where the path of this object was. It is all dead. If these trees were tested, they may still hold evidence of the craft. As for me, it has haunted me my entire life. We never tell people of this event, because they laugh or brush it off. It was such a significant event, I felt as though I should not tell people that wouldnt understand.
