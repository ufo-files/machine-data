Clear blue sky few clouds glow attracted attention and lack of movement though moved from west to eastern horizon in about twenty minutes so very slow movement.Looks different when enlarged notice four dark areas around object which forms letter H.

I was going out of my front door.I am 66 years old and have seen strange objects before [most of the time without having a camera]i am a keen aircraft show visitor so unlikely to be fooled by conventional aircraft.Looking up the glowing object did not appear to be moving so i continue to look.
My first thought was that it was not conventional whatever it was.The object appeared to be high in the sky and glowing with my naked eye i could only see that much,i went back indoors and got my didital camera with a 50x zoom.zooming in the object appeared to form the letter H,the object moved very slowly from directly above me to disappear over the trees Eastward.From my past experience of sightings my feelings are always the same ...raised hairs so to speak and to be honest a little excited.Lost over the Easterly horizon.
