Two pulsating orbs

Sitting in my hotub facing east at approximately 1:30am I witnessed 2 orbs between the gap of my house and the neighbors for about 15 seconds. They both were flying next to each other, then one slowed slightly before increasing its speed moving to the front of the other than underneath very quickly. They pulsed with a glow that I’ve never seen before.Seemed to be a sequence or random from red,orange,yellow all blending into one.Then they increased speed and continued past where I couldn’t see. I had my wife go to the front of the house to look because I’m disabled. She couldn’t spot them 30seconds after on a clear cold night.Just gone. I really don’t care what people think,I know what I saw .
