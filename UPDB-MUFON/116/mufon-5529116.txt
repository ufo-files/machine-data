Star like streaks of light

I was enjoying a beautiful night under the stars. Noticed two weird streaks of light within a 20 minute period approx 8pm Saturday night. Very fast moving. First in the west sky and one in the east. The first one I thought must have been a shooting star. Appeared to me for approx 10 seconds, shot across the sky with a white light and white trail and then vanished. When I noticed it again later in a different spot I thought that was quite a coincidence.
