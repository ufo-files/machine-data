Bright orb light moving erratically on mt Shasta

Object was a bright circular light that appears to be moving towards the group. The light was solid and never changed brightness. For the first 3 minutes the aspect of the object never changed as it appeared to be moving towards us, then it changed and moved to the left and appeared to lower by the mountains. No noise was ever observed and to object appeared to wobble very quickly. The group of observers were not drinking or under influence of drugs. Most of the group were skeptics and are well educated in science including one PHD candidate. One member was a recently retired Naval Commander.
