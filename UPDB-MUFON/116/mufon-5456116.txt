I had been watching the sky, we get a lot of helicopters due to sikorsky being nearby. I was facing east and moved my eyes to look northeast and I saw a thick, silver cigar shaped object in the sky There was also a black ring towards the front of the object. It was heading east when all of a sudden it was hidden behind a tree and when I saw it again it was going northeast. It got lost behind a tree again and then I couldn't see it after that.
