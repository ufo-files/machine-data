{rjl} 3 reddish/orange orbs hovering, pulsating, moving independently of each other

Walking up driveway at 1003pm on 8/15/14 when I observed three red orbs to the east through treeline. Observed these orbs three different times over a 15 min period. Hovered, pulsated, moved slowly up and down and left to right. They eventually faded out at different times. This is the 3rd time these objects have been observed this year in this area, 4/20, 7/4, and 8/15. I have video/pics from last two incidents. Chinese lanterns? I think not. Some kind of drones perhaps, but from where?
