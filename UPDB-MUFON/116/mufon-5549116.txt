glowing yellow -orange coloured ball/star like

Walking dog around park (the sky is always busy with air traffic LAX and Torrance airports)but I see approaching from the south at an estimated height of 6-8000 ft a glowing yellow/orange ball of light. The color of the light was different to those forward facing aeroplane headlights (not as bright. After about five minutes of walking, I stopped, to see if the object was moving. It was, but very slowly. For a moment from side to side, but it continued on its path northward NNW. It also seemed to gain some altitude before eventually disappearing in the light haze of an almost full moon night. Its journey that I viewed, took approx. 20 mins.to pass over my view of the night sky. Even a high altitude aircraft would have passed this area in about 5 mins. My pleasure of viewing, as I have already said, was about 20 mins.
