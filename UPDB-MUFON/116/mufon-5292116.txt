The lights changing direction

At 8:11 pm on August 30th I stopped outside of my residence to photograph rainbows over the horizon. This was facing to the Southeast and away from the sunset. During this time I noticed three illuminated orbs moving separately, though not across the sky like a plane or helicopter. No wing or tail lights were visible. They were under cloud cover at the time (as you can see in the photos) from the storm passing through. The objects spread and then resumed A tight formation before disappearing from view. Total duration of the siting was app. 2 minutes.
