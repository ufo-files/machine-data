{nd} I was in my driveway looked up and saw a round grey object with yellowish lights on top

I was out on my driveway at 7:15PM on January 2, 2016. The sky was star lit and clouds were maybe at 25,000 to 30,000 feet. I looked up towards the North. I saw an aircraft flying from the NE going SW. It appeared to be going the speed of a jet plane or a little faster in a strait line. At first I thought is was an airplane, however it did not have the lights that airplanes use. At that point I knew It was a UFO. There was no sound at all. The UFO appeared to be round in front and a flat rear and had a dome with yellowish lights, some seem to pulsate and some weren't. The body of the craft was greyish and was fuzzy on the edges. I only had around 10 to 15 seconds to observe it until it flew out of sight. When I observed the object I did not feel anything. However, I did not sleep well that night, I had nightmares all night ( that does not happen to me ever). While I fill this report out I am getting chills and goose bumps up and down my back. I am a relatively fearless person what happened to me afterwards was strange.
