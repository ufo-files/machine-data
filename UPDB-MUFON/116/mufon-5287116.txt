Nest camera, front porch,2 lights in unison,one single dot and a small ring spinning

While reviewing the previous nights Nest camera alerts I came across two unusual lights that moved in sync. They were both small. Looked like they were projected lights but no source would have been possible. The lights moved back and forth, sometimes under the porch roof sometimes out in the open. The larger of the two resembled a wedding band in shape with paired vertical features that were curved and separating the top and bottom of the band. Like a carousel with no interior.

The lights stayed for approximately 45 minutes in all and then were gone. Lights were not seen before or since. 

While viewing enlarge the images and you can clearly see the circular,spinning band.
