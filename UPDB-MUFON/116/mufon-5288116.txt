Multiple white lights following similar NON linear pathq

Last night I got home late. I live on 4th floor facing North. It was midnight and I saw a light like a landing light coming from the North-Northwest. Then there were two, like maybe one behind another appearing to be coming South toward me.. Then they became parallel and I could not tell how far away they were. I have a radio tower about one mile North/NW of me with red flashing lights that they transitioned. 

It looked like maybe a huge aircraft with lights on the wingtips. Then it started banking to my left which was West. Then I saw several more coming and at one point in about a one minute period there were 7-10. I tried to video with my phone but was unable. There were at least 12 and maybe 15 white lights that all followed the same path but made turns and seemed to "swirl" or cluster toward each other like bees swarming a nest and then slowly faded out as though they were getting farther away. I cannot find anything online. I knocked on my neighbor's door and he saw the last two for what it's worth.

Never saw anything like that before. Wish I could have recorded but I will keep an eye out.
