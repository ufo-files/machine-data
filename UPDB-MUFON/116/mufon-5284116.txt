UFO (or drone?) above shopping plaza

I was taking my daughter to a job interview and we witnessed this object hovering in the sky. It was pretty close to us, and we saw it clear in the daylight. It didn't look very large, I imagine a couple feet or a little more across perhaps, had a dull orange color, and was donut shaped. There were no lights, no sound, and appeared to be maybe turning/rotating, and definitely wobbling in place. If I remember correctly, the temperature outside was moderate, and there was a little breeze. 
 I did take a very short video, but I wanted to walk my daughter into the store, and by then time I came out, it was gone. I thought it was probably a drone, so I kind of talked myself out of taking it more seriously, which I've now come to regret. Because watching it land/leave may have settled the issue. 
  I am attaching a screenshot of the object, as I'm having trouble uploading the video form my phone to my laptop. If you find this report worthy of your time, I can try to get you the video. 
Note: there were also a couple very fast little round objects zipping through the video which I did not see at the time. I'm guessing they are bugs, but maybe not.
