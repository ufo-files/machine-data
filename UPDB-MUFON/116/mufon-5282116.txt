Machine between two slats in the sky

I was 4 & half years old, playing outside at the front corner of my parents' house w/my best friend Michael. We were playing with matchbox cars on the ground when I stood up a minute. I looked up & saw dark machinery right above me, just barely moving eastward. But it was like looking through two slats because if you moved to the left, it disappeared. Straight, it was there. Moved to the right, it disappeared again. Straight, it was there. It was like looking underneath a car without any identifying parts. I yelled for Michael to look but he ignored me. I yelled again but he wasn't moving so I left my spot & shook his shoulder, "You've gotta see this!" He finally got up & I stood back in my spot & looked everywhere but never found it again. Later I told my Mom what I'd seen but she ignored me too. Then I had 2 very distinct & vivid dreams of a kind I'd never had before or since. 1)I dreamed I was petrified, lying on a table in a dark room. I dreamed my grey/white striped cat was touching my eyes & I couldn't move or speak. Then my cat turned into a grey alien & I was startled awake, hyperventilating. (I never saw anything remotely like what I'd seen until I saw the cover of Whitley Strieber's book Communion). 2) I dreamed that my Mom asked me to call my brother in for supper so I go outside & get on my "tricycle" (this tricycle had no wheels, just handle bars & a body to sit on), turn a key & am lifted up in the air just above my house's rooftop & glide across the street, over houses & see my brother playing baseball with the neighborhood kids. I call him in for supper & leave. I was 4 & a half. I'd never been on top of our roof & yet I had a perfect vision of not only our rooftop but of multiple rooftops in our neighborhood. And the weird thing is that it felt like something I'd done a hundred times before. Like it was a chore.
