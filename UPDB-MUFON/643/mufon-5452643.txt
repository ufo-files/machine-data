photo taken on flight from oregon to Iowa

I was going through my old iPod touch a while back and came across this picture I had taken out the window on a flight from Oregon to Iowa Aug. 26, 2014. I had never really had the chance to look back at the pictures so I was surprised to see what looks like two smoke(?) trails passing between the cabin and wingtip of the plane and then making a diving right turn. I never saw this at the time of the photo, but considering how long it takes for a camera to take a picture this thing traveled what looks to be a great distance in the blink of an eye. when you follow the trails you can see they come to some bright silver looking object.
