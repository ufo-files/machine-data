{rjl} Flying pill shaped object Cincinnati

I was on my to work, and was stopped at a traffic light, i noticed the object moving. At first I thought it was a plane but it had no wings, than maybe a blimp no too small. it was pill shaped, white with a dark band from top to bottom. It seemed to be coming in for a decent and was moving at a good rate of speed. I thought this was odd because there are not landing fields in the Norwood OH area. It was not very high up and i lost view behind the near by building. I estimate the size as 30 foot long by 10 foot high. I was very interested in the object it seemed to move slow and silently but my next turn was away from its path.
