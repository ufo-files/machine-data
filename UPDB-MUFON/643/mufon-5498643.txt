Silent triangle craft flew directly over head with big light on the bottom centre of craft

Me and five others were outside in the summer and i just glanced up into the sky and a triangular craft flew over head and over the horizon in less than a second. It was mostly black with a sort of large glowing egg shape in the center of the bottom of the craft. I didn't notice anymore details as it happened so fast. I felt as though it was nothing at the time, but a year or so on i still have no idea what it was.
