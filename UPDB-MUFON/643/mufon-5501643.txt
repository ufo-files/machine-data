I was closing my curtains and looked out my open window. Saw what appeared to be an airplane at first, but crossed the horizon much too rapidly and the two glowing orbs were very large compared to the other 6-7 aircraft in the area. I watched it cross the entire horizon in less than 2 seconds without sound. It stopped on the eastern horizon and went straight upwards very rapidly, then descended at the same incredible speed with no pause between the rise and descent. Then went to the horizon. I called my wife to come quickly to the front yard to look, but by the time we got outside and got our bearings it had vanished.  NO BS..!!!
