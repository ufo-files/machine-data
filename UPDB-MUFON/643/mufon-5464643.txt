{jpg} At sea, observed 3 objects in v shaped formation, north bound.

No wings were seen no noise. Just an observation, no real attention was made. A moment later, one object backed off the formation at a high rate of speed, Almost instantaneously. My attention immediately changed. The object moved back from the other three without making any turns. Just fell back.Next thing I could not believe. The object went straight up for quite a distance but was visible. The same as a star in comparable light. It made an instant 90 degree right turn. Went off in a southern direction. Then at a high rate of speed made another 90 degree right turn, instantly. Flew off again at a rate of speed I could not believe. Turned right again instantly met up with the other Two in the same previous position it left. Didn't slow down just stopped. I was in the NAVY and worked around large cargo planes and on a flight deck with high speed jets.No aircraft could make these maneuvers or pilots endure that kind of g forces. I watched this incident as well as my wife. The formation went behind a small puffy white cloud. I tracked the line of flight waiting for their exit from the cloud but they never came out. Never saw them again. [name removed/CMS/jpg]
