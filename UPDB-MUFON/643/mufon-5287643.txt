At first I thought it was a helicopter in an open field. It did not make a chopping sound - more like a hum

Around August 1990 Louisville KY, I was in my apartment near hurstbourne lane and I heard and saw something in a vacant field about 500 feet away. I thought it was a helicopter it was there what seemed like 2 hours. It would move along the tree line (slowly) with rapid crisscrossing white search lights. The object would ascend @30 degrees and descent with visible red lights. There was no chopping sound only a loud hum. After about 2 hours it turned towards where I was standing (2nd floor apartment balcony). The object passed over my apartment @ 100 feet above and 100 feet away. It passed slowly and appeared larger than what I saw in the field. It was grey - flat bottom with a long green steady light and silent. After it passed by I heard 3 sonic booms and saw an orange ball of light in the sky.
