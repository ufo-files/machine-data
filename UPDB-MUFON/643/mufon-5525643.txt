Silver cigar floated across the city and then disappeared

I live on a hill, in an area where I have an impressive view of the city and a window that takes up my entire wall. I was sitting on my bed on my laptop. Around 7:00 pm, I glanced out my window and saw what I initially thought was a plane, because I saw a light on it's tail end. I got up to get a closer look and watched it fly over the city slowly before stopping in midair and disappearing. It hovered for maybe a minute before completely vanishing; it didn't get smaller like it was going off into the distance, it just vanished.
