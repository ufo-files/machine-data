Two bright lights in NW sky looking like super bright stars, then dimmed and started moving SW together slowly, no sound

4:30 am, took the dogs out like I do most mornings and I always look up since I've had three other sightings in my life starting in 1973, 76 and New Years day morning in 2017. When I went outside I looked up and saw the normal star filled sky, but then looked left through the trees because the light sources were much brighter then anything else. I moved around the tree for a better look and as I said to myself that they were abnormally bright stars they dimmed to the brightness of the other stars and they both started moving to the SW and as far up in the sky they were, they were probably going fast but didn't seem so to me on the ground. The thing is, is that when I first saw them they seemed fairly close, like 4000 to 5000 feet in the air. The problem here is that I saw them bright and close and I looked down for a second and looked up and that's when the light was dimmed so I don't know if they took off out to space before they started moving across which caused the light to seem dim because they were like it seems in space when they started moving, or, they actually dimmed the lights when I mentioned how bright they were. It was 15 below zero so I didn't follow them as long as I wanted to. I felt like they were waiting for me. I will take my phone with me from now on. The reason I don't go ga ga and do nightly spotting is that you don't find them, they find you. I will try calling them in tomorrow morning, it's not going to be as cold. Thought about calling the local airforce base but I don't want them messing up the works.
