At approximately 8:08 pm, EST, February 17, 2012, I witnessed the recurring Bright White light that I have observed at least 5 times previously. As I stepped onto my front porch I spotted the light above the western slope of the same nearby mountain where this event had occurred in the past; the light appeared to be approx 400 - 500 above the horizon in the Northwestern Sky (almost directly below Venus). As I continued to observe, the light began moving horizontally very slowly in a Northerly direction and at the same time the altitude was dropping slowly. This horizontal and vertical movement continued until approximately 8:24 pm. During this period of time I observed that the light dimmed and brightened 3 times. By 8:41 the light then began to descend more quickly and by 8:54 pm it had dropped below the tree-line.
