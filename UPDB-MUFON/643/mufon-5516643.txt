(GS) Large bright orb decending through clouds

I was pulling into my work parking lot admiring the sun rise and as I began turn into the parking lot I noticed a bright orb above some cloud cover approximately 65 degrees above the horizon. I looked at the road to ensure I wasnt going to hit anything and looked back up. The orb was brighter and was descending through the clouds. I could see the brightness as one cloud completely enveloped it. As it passed through the orb was extremely bright and large - almost flame like, but with a definite 3-d definition to it. It was a yellow / white color (I am somewhat color blind, but am pretty certain this was the color). It appeared to me to hold the same characteristics as a large falling star, but was moving slowly and had no tail. I looked down at the road again to pull into a space and looked back up and it was gone. I searched the sky for another 10 minutes and couldnt locate anything out of the ordinary. As I think back on it the one thing that kept coming to mind was how real it was. Not like I just caught a glimpse of a small light briefly in the sky. No, this was as real as the clouds and trees around me. Though I was an unknown distance away from it, I had the impression it was large in size - not real small. It was a bright, glowing orb with light that fluctuated (not a rhythm like pulse).
