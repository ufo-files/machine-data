{ck} Observed an orange red light in the sky moving south to north no sound didnt move like an aircraft

While walking in my neighborhood with my girlfriend we witnessed a reddish/orange light in the sky about 10:05 PM 7/3/12. We were facing east and the object came from the south heading north. It varied its speed and actually stopped and hovered it moved up and down slightly and moved on a curve. It appeared to begin moving away from us to the northeast and then the light disappeared. When we arrived home we kept looking to the northeast and saw it again off in the distance moving non linear and then it disappeared into a tree line. We noted the time and the fact there was no wind at the time and commented on the fact there was no sound from the UFO. Did anyone else report this?
