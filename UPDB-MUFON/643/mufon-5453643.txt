{rjl} two round lights buzz truck and zoom away

Here's an old sighting that I finally got around to reporting. I was 12 yrs. old going buck hunting for the first time with my brother. It was really early in the morning and we were driving on a back road in the middle of nowhere. I can't remember where we were exactly because it was a long time ago. So it's dark and quiet before sunrise and all of a sudden we see two whitish-yellow circular lights flying downwards coming straight towards us. it's hard to say if it was one craft or two because all we saw was one light fly synchronized in front of another. It/they moved fast and when they got directly over the truck (about 50 ft. above) I watched them accelerate and zoom they were gone back up toward the sky! They/it made no sound at all.
We drove on discussing what it could have been. We agreed we never saw anything like it. As we drove a little further we saw the woods to the right all lit up but had no roads leading to it from the road we were on. We just kept driving until we got to our spot. I fell asleep against a tree while my brother tried to push deer to me.
I will never forget that. One night in my adult life I thought about it and called my brother and asked if he remembered it. He sure did. My father and Uncle also had a strange sighting while hunting but thats another story
