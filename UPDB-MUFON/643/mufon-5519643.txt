3 spheres swooping around each other over Portland International Airport

Me and some of my family were driving to church on Marine Drive around 7:00 P.M. and I was looking out the window, when i noticed 3 red colored spheres in the vicinity of Portland International Airport. At first i believed them to be helicopters, but a helicopter could not replicate the rapid changes in direction these objects made. I kept my eyes on them, and they were swooping around each other, almost like a dance, getting close then moving back out, always smoothly but rapidly. We turned off of Marine Drive continuing to our destination and I lost sight of the objects.
