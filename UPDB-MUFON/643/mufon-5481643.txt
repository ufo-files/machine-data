{dr}Massive flash of orange light in a cloud, LOW to ground...then a massive blue flash. The blue pulsating light beams in circular motion.

Myself and 5 people where standing next to my home on a pathway. We were all talking at out of nowhere there was a soundless HUG orange flash in the sky in the clouds, very low, barely above homes. There was no sound. It was not lightning or fireworks. Then about less than a minute later a Blue flash of light occurred, then several blue pulses of light in a circular motion in the sky. All 6 of us just starred at eachother and could not understand what we just saw. MASSIVE, LOW, and NO sound in clouds...the weather was CLEAR, no reason for the clouds.
