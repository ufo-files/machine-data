The lights did not blink, just very bright. It moved over my house then disappeared in the distance NW. We checked to see if any blimps or planes were in our area that night and there were none. Also on 12/4/2015 while riding with my father on HWY 13 AT DUSK AROUND 5:15 PM North out of town we both saw a very bright star like object sitting stationary in the North Eastern sky. Very strange lights and happenings occur in this area yearly at about this same time. December seems to be a great time to see UFO'S here.
