{rjl} 25-30 Moving Lights - West to East

While on the deck I looked up and happened to notice an object moving. Assuming it was a jet or satellite I didn't pay much attention. Until another, then another, and another kept appearing approximately nw to se in the path before disappearing. Clouds were rolling in and out, this probably went on close to an hour, I'm guessing 25+ were what we say. All I can assume is this is part of the new Starlink satellite system... which is fine, but it was quite unnerving actually seeing some weird things going on in the sky. 5 Witnesses, neighbors and my wife.

We tried to get cell phone video but none had good enough resolution. I'll keep an eye out for awhile, if they are satellites we'll probably get some sort of "yes, this is Elon Musk's plan" thing. I can't imagine 10000 up there if this is what it's going to look like.
