{nd} Pulsating, rapidly changing trajectory but moving in one general direction (like a hummingbird), quickly changed colors

Noticed a bright light about 9:45 pm. I called my wife and she had seen it thinking it was a shooting star, until it stopped not too far from us. It was like a bright pulsating star. It made very quick changes in direction like a hummingbird, but eventually went back to its general west bound direction. There was no noise and was a delight to see it change directions as it did.
