{nd} Walking late at night witnessed something strange in the sky took a short clip before it vanished

Walking my dog last Saturday night (sept 6th) it was about to storm and there was lighting in the sky I was looking at that's when I noticed this bright object hovering in the distance I stopped and observed it for a few seconds before it started moving very strangely and stopping in mid air that's when I pulled out my phone and started recording for about a minute and a half then it changed directions and dissapeared behind a tree. At first I thought it could of been a helicopter cause it flashed red a couple times but not the way an aircraft should. My feelings were that this could be authentic and I should put it in the hands of the right people.
