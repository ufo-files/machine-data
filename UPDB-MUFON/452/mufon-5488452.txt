I WATCHED FOR APPOX 90-100 MINUTES TILL MY NECK ACHED. THIS HAPPENED  ABOVE THE BIG DIPPER. THE TRAVEL OF THE OBJECT WAS THE WIDTH OF BIG DIPPER IN THE BLINK OF AN EYE. MOST OF THE MOVEMENT WAS AS HIGH AS THE 3RD STAR OF THE HANDLE AND OVER TO THE RIGHT TO A DISTANCE NO FURTHER THAN THE BOTTOM STAR OF THE DIPPER(RIGHT). IM NOT TOO SURE OF THE DATE BUT I SPENT THE FIRST FULL WEEK AT THE STURGIS RALLY.THIS HAPPENED 4-5 DAYS LATER. IVE BEEN THINKING ALOT SINCE THEN.
