{cak} Saw Cylindrical object move from right to left, hover, then descend.

Myself, Dad, and various brs, sistrs were in backyard playing w/Frisbee, when I looked up and saw a cylindrical object flying from N to S in Western sky. I told others, and they saw it too. It proceeded on course till it was over Englund AF base to the S of Alexandria. stopped in mid-air, then changed directions toward the ground, I lost sight after it reached the line of trees and levee along the Red River.

This was about when I was 14 or 15. We had just moved to Pineville from Fla.
