{tjd} Circular object lights rotating around outer cicular dimension of object

This event occurred on 7/15/1973, in Gorham, Maine.
Sleeping outside for 13th birthday with brother, when older brother and friend showed up about 1 -1 1/2 hrs after sunset.. We heard a low humming sound coming from NW moving SE in straight flight path approximately 100ft above treeline headed toward Scarborough. Observe large circular object/craft, approximately 50-70ft dia, black circular center, dark grey around black center circle. Overall the craft was very dark blending into the night sky.  Outer perimeter had various color lights rotating around craft, as in a systematic pulsating meaner. Event lasted about 15 seconds
Older brother stated, it had to be a helicopter. I dismissed this premise, stating helicopter blades don't have numerous different color lights on the end of the blades. Noting there was no forward light, like a headlight or obvious green/red forward/rear and/or side side lights - like a boat or aircraft would have.
After being ridiculed, dropped issue but this event always stuck with me, such that I remembered over 46 yrs, it was on my birthday in 1973. 
I can re-affirm to this day, it was not a helicopter. Noting that there was no "chopping" sound a helicopter makes, lights were not normal to know aircraft, and low altitude. 
Do to increase in recent disclosures of UFO (2019), I thought I would share my experience, in which I never shared to anyone since that night.
