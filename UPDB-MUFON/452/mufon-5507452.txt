{rjl} 2 glowing discs low in western sky

At 6:52 p.m. 27September in Naperville Illinois, I noticed one, than two objects low on the horizon. One object, glowing yellowish/white, in the west, much larger than Venus. It faded in and out, from extremely bright, to dull, to invisible, and then back to extremely bright. 2nd object higher in the sky in west as well, about 10 degrees to the SW of first object. This object appeared briefly, moving east slowly, and than move off West very quickly. The first, brighter object, stayed stationary for about two minutes then scrambled west quickly. Very large objects, but far away as well. This was around sunset, but were not satellites or planets- much more luminosity than Venus or Mars.
