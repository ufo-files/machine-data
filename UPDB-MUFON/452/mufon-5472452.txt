{cak} Two bright yellow lights with one red blinking light, may have been a drone

I was driving down a road when I saw two bright lights in the field to my right. They didn't appear to be moving like a plane and were brighter/larger than lights coming from a plane at night. I followed them for a half mile until I got to the curve in the road that ends near the ocean. I turned my car around and the object was hovering in front of my car staying somewhat still. It had two bight yellow lights and a small red blinking light in the middle. At first it was hard to tell if it was a large aircraft far away or a drone close up. I think it may have been a drone. I turned my car around and the object was flying to my right above the treeline alongside my car. As I turned down a driveway the object continued and flew northwest over the woods. I didn't hear any loud noises coming from the object (I was in my running car though). I've heard that nice drones are quite loud and it is also illegal to fly drones out of eyesight in Maine. If it was someone's drone they were not within eyesight of it as it flew over a field, woods and houses.
