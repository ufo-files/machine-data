{rjl} Flying North, then changing directions

I pulled into my driveway facing south and was on the phone. Got out of truck looked north into the sky and seen a blue/white object flying from south to north direction. The color caught my eye and thats why I stopped to watch it. As I was watching it stopped and made a sharp right turn and told my girlfriend that its a UFO and for her to come look. She tried saying it was a plane. My hands were full and was also on the phone so was unable to take video right away. Went inside and put the items down, went back out and started to watch again calling my girlfriend back out to watch it as it made sharp turns. I started to record it and at 1 time it went behind the roof of a house then shot out the west back into sight, then made a swift direction change really fast and went northeast and disappeared really fast.I was only able to record about 1:09 of it. I tried to focus at one point and couldnt focus from my phone very well.
