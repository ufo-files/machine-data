Worm/snake like smokey white tube that formed into a concave white object

This originally appeared as a smokey/cloud-like worm/snake about 1000 feet above. Best described as a tube suspended in the air moving around in a thermal rise. I've seen WalMart bags flying above the city on many occaisions, this was like a WM bag in a tube form 100 feet long and emitting smoke or gas. I was driving east on McDowell. I turned to park and get pics. Then I noticed the "tube-snake" no longer had a tube shape, but was now more like a shallow bell shape. Took a pic, then another and in the second one, it was already gone.
