Large square bell shaped UFO

I was driving towards Foxholes coincidently listening to a podcast about UFO's when a large black object was in the sky ahead, The best way to describe it would be you were trying to draw a bell but had to use straight lines to do so. It was solid black and the size of a large helicopter. It floated left and suddenly changed direction floating diagonally right. it then went down and left and just vanished out of sight. 

It was solid black with no detectable markings.
