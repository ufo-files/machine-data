{rjl} Hovered over Moon then took off after a couple seconds

I was in my backyard viewing the sky and moon with my telescope approximately around 17:00. What made me notice the object was its shape it seems to look like a Bio-hazard symbol. At first i thought it was just dirt on my lens but i barely had the switched out form the store therefore, i keep on viewing the object. The motion of it was stationary until it eventually turned out to have a other object come out of it. Also in the shape of a circle. both where stationary over the moon until they took off. I was impressed by how quickly it took off. Eventually i lost sight of the object/s when they flew away from the moon.
