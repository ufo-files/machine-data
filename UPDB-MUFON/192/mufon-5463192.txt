3 Lights. Left Green, Right Red, moving white light between.

I never believed in UFO's. I'm still not sure I do.
I do not rule out other intelligent life. I'm just not convinced that its local. 

Sometimes I wonder if I have ever encountered it on this planet - a philosophical debate for another time.

The problem I have is that I have seen something distant in the sky.

A green light on the left, red on the right. In between was a white light moving up and down, wobbling.

I dismissed this sighting, because I thought it was a drone, or some such. The distance into the nights sky suggested it would have to be a pretty big drone.

I forgot about this for several weeks before confiding in 2 friends.
They had been keeping a similar sighting, minus the white light, to themselves too. Fearful they'd be ridiculed.

I still cannot explain this sighting and wonder if there is some plausible explanation ? Part of my thinking is because there were 2 sighting, mine and that of 2 friends, there IS a plausible explanation.

I can't fathom it right now.

This all happened over a year ago now.

This is the first time I have mentioned it online.
First time I have had the guts to do so.

I am more than happy for someone to debunk this. If a plausible explanation exists - happy to hear it.

I have no footage, it happened in a short time. I was in too much disbelief to reach for a recording device. Fumbling in my mind to compute it as normal.

Writing this down for public consumption makes me nervous. I checked in the other 2 people before posting this.
