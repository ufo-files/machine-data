Fireball in low sky

Fireball with orange & red tail close to object , streaming blue & green, passed over house across street in a direction from NNE travelling SSE below cloud. I thought it was a meteorite but very big and low. We drove down toward the direction it appeared to be going, but no sound of impact or bright glow on impact. Basically disappeared. What prompted me to wright in is, Ohio appears to had similar witnesses at the same time & date.
