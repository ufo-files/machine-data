I was walking into my parents backyard from the alley when I noticed a fireball that seemed sentient in the sky..it was slightly above tree initially and it was moving left..it looked translucent on the right side like it was gas emitting from it. I felt like it was trying to communicate with me just by the feeling that I felt. I wasn't scared but I dialed my dad who was inside the house to come look at it but it slowly AND hurriedly moved upward in the process until it disappeared. It was remarkable how fast but slow it seemed to be moving..Earlier in the day I must say that I was having a lot of deja vu.
