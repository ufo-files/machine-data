Was driving from Raleigh towards Durham and first thing I noticed was a flat cloud really low in the horizon, I was having funny thoughts to myself , saying wow someone could easily hide in these clouds, next thing I saw was an airplane landing towards the Raleigh Durham airport and another one taking off to god knows where. The third object I saw was this flat jet black disk hovering under this one flat cloud.It was like the craft or thing was using the cloud as cover. I had to rub my eyes to believe. i was going to stop to record and talk pictures but there was a time of the day where everyone was rushing home at very least 85 mph. It was 4 :13 when I checked the clock this was happening, I certainly will never forget.
TO better describe it looked like one big frying pan without a handle. jet black , no lights, it was day light . It was navigating towards the right side and I was able to see it for around 30 seconds.
