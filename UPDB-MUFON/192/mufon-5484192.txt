{nd} 2 pale white lights in a line as if part of a larger object. The lights passed over myself and my house, then disappeared.

April 5, 2016 at approx. 855pm EDT, I was outside of my home walking my dog. It was a very clear night and I live in a development just outside of the city of Thomasville, GA. So the city lights do not effect the night sky. I was facing east, then turned to the west (the rear of my property overlooking a pond) then I noticed 2 pale white lights in the sky. One was following the other as if they were a part of something larger. The lights did not blink, this is what really got my attention, I listened for the sound of a plane or helicopter. Nothing. The object was completely silent. It moved very smoothly over me and my home from West to East. Slightly changing course to the ESE and then back to the East. The object was not very high, I was low enough to almost make out a shape, but I couldn't say for sure what shape. It never stopped moving at a pretty low rate of speed until the lights suddenly disappeared maybe a 1/4 mile away. I did notice that something had gotten the attention of several dogs in the direction the object was moving as many of them began to bark shortly after the lights disappeared.
