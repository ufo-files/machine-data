Blue semi solid transparent object went across the car

We were driving in a westerly direction at about 8pm at night. It was dark and had no street lights. There were no other cars in close proximity.
I noticed a blue feature that appeared misty but also seemed solid. It went across the car from left to right at a relatively fast pace and went in front of the car. It seemed as though we would hit the object but as we drove into it, nothing happened. 
As the light from the car hit the object, it did not appear to absorb or reflect light. The object did not change colour even when the light hit it.
