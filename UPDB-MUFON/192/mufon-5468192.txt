STAYING AT GRANDMOTHERS ON THE FARM.IT NOON TIME BECAUSE GRANDPARENTS TOOK NAP AT NOON ON THE COOL FLOOR.NO AC IN 1962.AS THEY NAPPED I GOT UP AND WENT OUT THREW THE SCREEN DOOR AND ONTO THE NORTH PORCH. THE OLD PORCH IS A DULL DRIED WOOD COLOR. I WENT DOWN THE PORCH STEPS WALKING WEST ALONG THE OUTSIDE OF THE OLD HOUSE TO THE WEST CORNER OF THE HOUSE. I REMEMBER TURNING AND LOOKING TO THE SOUTHERN SKY AND STOOD IN THE VERY QUIT NOON HEAT OF A COUNTRY FARM AND WATCHED THIS ROUND DOMED TOP GREY ARE SILVER OBJECT HOVER OVER THE PASTURE AND MOVE VERY SLOWLY TO THE EAST FOR I THINK 1-2 MIN.AFTER THE SIGHTING I HAVE NO MEMORY OF GOING BACK TO THE HOUSE ARE NO MEMORY OF ANYTHING AFTER THAT SIGHTING.I WANT TO BACK UP A LITTLE AND EXPLAIN THAT EVERY MOMENT FROM WHEN I WALKED OUT ONTO THE PORCH TILL I SAW THE OBJECT IS ETCHED IN MY MIND ,EVERY OBJECT ALONG THE PATH I WALKED IS ETCHED IN MY MIND.I REMEMBER THE OLD TATTERED BRICKSIDING NAILED TO THE HOUSE ALONG THE PATH,,THE WOODEN BOARDS THAT WERE LAID ON THE GROUND TO WALK ON CAUSE THE YARD WOULD BECOME MUDDY IN THE RAINY TIMES. THOSE BOARDS ARE VIVID IN MY MIND AND ALSO THE DRIED CHICKEN FEET PRINTS LEFT FROM A PREVEOUS RAIN,,ALSO AS I WALKED ON THAT PATH THE OLD BARNYARD WOODEN FENCING HAD THIS GRASS ARE FUNGUS GROWING ON THE OLD BOARDS,ALL THESE THINGS I RECALL LIKE A PHOTO IN MY BRAIN ON THE PATH UNTIL I SAW THE OBJECT. AGAIN AFTER SEEING THE OBJECT I CANT RECALL ANYTHING ARE EVEN BEFORE I WENT OUT THE SCREEN DOOR I CANT RECALL ANYTHING,JUST THE WALK OUT THE HOUSE TO THE WEST CORNER AND SEEING THE OBJECT.I WAS 45 YRS. OLD WHEN I SAT AND MADE MYSELF THINK ABOUT THAT KODAK MOMENT IN TIME. I THOUGHT SOMETHING REALLY HAPPENED WHEN I REALIZED I HAD NO MEMORY BEFORE ARE AFTER THE SIGHTING.I KNOW I SAW SOMTHING REAL TO HAVE THIS PHOTO OF THAT MOMENT IN TIME STAY WITH ME ALL THESE YEARS. I DO NOT EXPECT ANY INVESTIGATION,TO MUCH TIME HAS PASSED. I WAS JUST A 5 YR. OLD. JUST WANTED TO TELL SOMEONE.IF YOU CHOOSE TO CONTACT ME IT WOULD BE OKAY. AGAIN IF YOU DO NOT ITS OKAY TO.
