very bright reflective surface of object along side of jet aircraft

I was 8 miles SE of Arnold Ne. talking to a friend that had just arrived to assist with working some cattle. Time was close to 8:45 a.m. central time, sun had been up about 45 min. I was hearing a jet aircraft passing over head and I always try to look up and see if it is commercial or military. I found the plane directly above and slightly to the East, some where in the 75 to 80 degrees angle, moving in a straight East direction. After finding the jet I notice a very shiny object off the left wing and slightly to the back side of the wing tip. My first thought was a F-16 or some other fighter jet along side the the large jet. I could not distinguish any wing, or fuselage of this object, just smaller round object with the intense sun glint coming off the surface. I observed the object for a few seconds and then it moved from near the left` wing, off around, and very close to the back side of the jet to the right side, then started to move right in a S.E. direction gaining separation from jet. The object had moved off to the right some distance when I decided to run to my truck and retrieve binoculars which I did not have this time. Jet and object had moved off to the East and no longer in sight. Object had to be seen by crew with it's close proximity to the jet. If it wasn't suppose to be there they had to be nervous of it being that close.
