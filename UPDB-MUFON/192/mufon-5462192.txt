It shows from space, 5x ORBS' on earth, and in NW South Africa.

The small town is called Boshoek, near [40km] the international resort of Sun City in North West South Africa.

There are 5 ORBS's. One is underneath a tree, one is close-by to farm buildings, two is almost on top of each other and one is some distance away.
