Hovered low ,then moved east less than a city block & hovered silently.

I was leaving my friends house one block north of the parkway ,their street is next to the parkway with the parkway wall separating their yard from the parkway. I turned right onto Lake Avenue & took another quick right onto the Ontario Beach Parkway, even before the first ramp to exit (which would have brought me around to where I started but I was intending to drive approx. 30 miles),I immediately saw this object just hovering there across the parkway just somewhat above tree level. It wasnt very large but was low & close. It was black, somewhat disc shaped but appeared with rounder edges with a dome-like look & something else on it like maybe a landing leg or as I can discribe shaped like a grasshopper leg (for lack of a better way to discribe)& that was coming off of the side & also black. There was,from what I could see lights from top to bottom in a row with 3 rows. I didn't know what to think as I stared in disbelief as to what I was seeing. I pulled over briefly & kept asking myself what is that? Although there was traffic passing by at the intersection behind me, I then realized that no one else was near me on the parkway at that moment as I was curious if anyone else was seeing what I was seeing. I then thought that Id better get out of there & exited on the nearby ramp which brought me back to where I started & ran in & told my friends. They could not catch a glimpse as the parkway wall way blocking the view. I once again had to go my route which I did almost immediately but when I got back on the parkway it was gone. I drove & didn't look back. This is my 5th sighting with one other sighting while driving the parkway last year but that one was green paneled disc & lit up with green lights & fell like a falling star in the distance but kept falling & hovered long enough for me to see it then raced off.
