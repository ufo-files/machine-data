{rjl} Comet-like aircraft, split into two individual aircrafts and both changed direction.

I was walking around the neighborhood, it was starting to get darker outside and i tend to look at the night sky frequently. I saw what i thought to be a comet, but it seemed to be under the atmosphere... As i watched it, it split into two separate comet-like flying air crafts, or so i believe, and then both changed direction at a 90 degree angle and both went separate ways. As i watched them fly off they kept going at the same direction as when they split off, and then about halfway across the sky to the treeline, they dissepated into thin air. Never seen anything like that before. Immediately i went to the house and searched around on the internet for reports of comets or other celestial objects splitting in two like that and could not find anything.
