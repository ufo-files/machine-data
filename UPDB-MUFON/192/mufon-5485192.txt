I walked out of my house in plantation florida and to my far left saw a very large oval shaped object with lights on it in the sky hovering in what appeared to be a static position. I was going with my mother to pick up prescriptions from walgreens and as we were driving we both looked at it trying to make out what it could be.. it was clearly not a plane or star or any other mundane object. My mom guessed it could be a helicopter but it seemed far to large and the wrong shape to be a helicopter. We looked at it as much as we could during the trip until it was blocked by trees. When we got back to my house it was no longer where I had originally seen it indicating it moved.
