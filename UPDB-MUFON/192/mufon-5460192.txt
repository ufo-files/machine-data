{cak} Sighting

My daughter and I were sitting outside when an orb of light appeared in the sky out of nowhere. It was pulsing, flashing,and changing colors. At one point it seems like it exploded and then returned to an orb. It seemed to float but also made rapid movements. Eerily quiet and mesmerizing. Then it moved toward the trees and disappeared. It lasted approximately 3-4 minutes. Through our clamoring and fumbling we managed to get a 52 second video.
