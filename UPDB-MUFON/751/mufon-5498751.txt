{nd} Shaped like teardrop on its side slowly moving and stopping and hovering

I was at home about to watch a TV program. I was compelled to grab binoculars and look out window. The initial view was of just twinkling stars - changing colors from white, green, blue, red, purple. The large object appeared from behind trees in the distance (approx. over Eastside San Jose) and hovered for about a minute. I immediately sensed that this was NOT A NORMAL everyday aircraft. It was moving South to North along the East San Jose foothills. It moved extremely slow and would stop and hover from 30 seconds to a couple minutes each time. Looking through binoculars - It was a teardrop on its side 1 round at front and pointed in back. It was illuminated in front half 1 I could see metallic reflection. The back end had red, blue/green, and white lights. There were other aircraft in the area that were clearly smaller as if they could fit inside this huge object. When they looked like they might be going towards it the large object would brighten and flash 3 large red lights in triangle formation - two red lights on bottom and one on top. It travelled into my site and travelled out behind trees, poles and houses. It had to have come from Gilroy area (South) going North towards Oakland/San Francisco. It must have been at least 20-25min's in my sites until it went passed trees and houses and I lost it but believe it kept going.
