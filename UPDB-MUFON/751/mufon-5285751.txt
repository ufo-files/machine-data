one night driving north on Loop 338 noticed flashing lights on what looked like a carnival ride (tilt a whirl) flew straight up and shot off to the NE

At approximately 9:00pm my wife and I were traveling on Loop 338 in Odessa Texas. As we topped an overpass I pointed to my wife the flashing lights in the distance and said that must be the carnival that was in town. The lights were flashing blue,red,yellow and green lights on a carnival ride called the tilt a whirl. It was tilted at 45 degrees and soon turned vertical. Next was unusual as it went strait up, climbing to maybe 1000 feet. At that point it crossed the horizon in the blink of an eye and left a bright silver trail for a few milliseconds I asked my wife what she had seen and she said the same thing. I told her we had to go home as this was surely on the news so we turned around and headed home. As we pulled into our driveway, we could see three what looked like military helicopters flying at low level in the direction the craft had traveled. Nothing was ever mentioned on the news. FYI the craft was to the west of the loop, the carnival was to the east of the loop.
