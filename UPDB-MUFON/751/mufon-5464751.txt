{tjd} An orange yellow light very high zig zagging going fast slow like how a bee fly's.

My wife and I saw an orange yellow colored light in the sky. It was a solid light no flashing just seemed to have a pulse so slightly. It was moving left to right zig zag motion like how a Bee moves through the air. It was going fast then slow but constantly moving. There was bright flash and the light moved in a horse shoe pattern back into it's flight path. There were planes east of this object. The light was way higher in altitude then the plane and moved a lot faster then the plane. We kept watch the light till it just disappeared. A couple minutes later my wife says there it is. It was traveling a different direction then before but still flying wobbley. When it lined up with a star it vanished.
