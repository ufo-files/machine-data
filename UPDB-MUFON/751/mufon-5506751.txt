{cak} Two orange dots moving slowly, without sound, left to right.

In Huntington WV, I went outside with my son at 11 pm to get my cell phone from my car when we noticed a small orange light moving without sound from left to right (North to Northeast) in the sky. A second orange light appeared approximately one minute afterwards. The objects seemed to disappear after we lost sight of them due to tree cover. The sky was cloudless and we had an excellent view of the event.
