Fireball spotted north west of our patio. Witnessed by 5 people total. Multiple pics and video taken.

July 4th 2020 Around 1115 pm, 5 total witnesses sitting on back patio spotted orb or fireball traveling toward us from the north west. Watched object for maybe 2 mins before it disappeared. Within a few mins, the object returned slightly to the west approx same distance away and on same path toward us. Watched the object again for approx two mins and it once again disappeared by fading out. Again a few mins later, witnessed it again in roughly the same place traveling this time to the north. Watched it for a few mins before it faded away again. On last account, one witness saw another object smaller in size but white in color traveling out of the tree line toward the fireball, it faded out approx half way from where it was first noticed and the fireball. Multiple video and pics of object available.
