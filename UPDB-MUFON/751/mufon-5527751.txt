oddly moving light that disappeared rapidly

My family and I returned home from school/the grocery store, and as I was getting groceries from the car and taking them into the house, I looked up at the stars, like I always do. I am a lover of the night sky, and it draws me. As I was grabbing a bag of groceries, I noticed an odd star that started moving at a constant speed and moving directly upward. It moved almost like the speed of a plane, but it was just a star (or so I thought at the time). However it moved upward in a straight line. It was too high up and quiet to be a helicopter and it moved too much against the earths rotation to be a satellite. I watched the light move upward for about 30 or 40 seconds then headed in to drop off the groceries. When I came back out to get more bags, about ten seconds or less later, the light was gone. I looked all over and couldn't find anything moving in the sky. I thought it was odd, because at the speed it was moving, ten seconds later I would still have been able to see it. I had always been curious about UFOs, but that night I was certain.
