Bright white light observered ascending into the sky slowly in the east from the 5th floor of a central Edinburgh office block.

While working on my night shift; I observed a bright white light in the distance ascending into the sky slowly in the east (towards Leith and the east coast) from the 5th floor of my central Edinburgh office block. I have a very clear view of the entire east skyline and can slowly watch the sun come up from these windows. I observed this object 2 days in a row at around the same time from around 05:30 until after 06:00. The object followed a different path on both days it was observed, but started its ascent from beyond where I could see. It slowly ascended up and then off to the right (South East) at a visible speed before disappearing. This sighting took place on Thursday 27th and Friday 28th of August and was not visible on the first 3 nights (Monday, Tuesday & Wednesday) of my shift. When one of the security guards arrived in the office at this time; I asked him to observe the object I was watching and we both agreed this was no star as it was moving rather fast. The security guard commented that the object must be quite large as it was rather bright and far away. I tried to take a photo with my mobile, but could not get the object to appear on the photograph. I managed to capture one picture that was zoomed in, but the camera just can't capture the clarity and lighting of the image you are observing.
