Large object about 100ft or so off ground.

ws riding my snowmobile , going to a friends house about 5 miles away , not sure of the date may have been dec or febuary, turned the corner on to the powerline trail and it was like i was in a vacuum ,kind of strange feeling , couldnt hear the sled running lights went out , i stopped and knew it was running then i looked up and could make out the outline of a large sort of trianglr shape , no lights just black as it moved over head lasted 1-2 min , as it passed i could hear the sled running again , but i watched this thing as it went under the powerline then over the hill and disappered was huge , what i remember was archs off the powerlines to the top of the object i wish i could remember the exact date because others had talked about seeing something similar, but that was a long time ago , i was 15-16 years old so most didn't listen
