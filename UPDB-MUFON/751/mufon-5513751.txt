(GS) small round lights just after midnite of the new year

My boyfriend and I were at at friends house for a New Years Eve party. We were standing to the back of the friends apartment has a cigarette near the lake. I looked at the water and saw red dots reflected from the sky. I turned up to the sky, and saw 6 sometimes 7 red dots hovering then moving in a line up over the building and stopping. There were definately 6 spheres to close together to be an airplane. The 7th one was blinking and disappearing and reappearing. They all stopped abovethe roof of the building, the 7th one blinked 3 times on, off, on, off, on, off. The other 6 flashed on, then off one time- and they were gone consumed by the darkness. ( for 3 years i never knew i could report this!!! ) I really wish i would have gotten photos
