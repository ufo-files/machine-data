{cak} I counted 47 craft moving from west to North East.

I counted 47 craft that appeared just South and West of Venus, over a period of approximately 17 minutes. The brightness was that of a star. They appeared to materialize from a very faint light to the brightness of a star on the belt of Orion.and then proceeded to move in a North Easterly direction. The first 12 where evenly spaced apart under Venus, and then the others that appeared, moved in the same direction but some were above Venus.
There wasn't any sound that was made.
