{tjd} Brilliant white light moving at incredible speed

Driving westbound on state route 39 a couple miles east of Walnut Creek, Ohio at 7:11pm on October 5th, 2019. Sun setting, but still very much light out. Brilliant white, star like light, about two to three times the size of a star, moved from roughly south to north starting from the direction of the sun to our slight left given our slightly northwest heading. The speed was incredible, faster than any jet, but slower and travelled farther than a shooting star, which we've never witnessed during daylight hours. Shined brightly, despite having the sun behind it, so I don't believe it was light reflecting from the sun given our vantage point. Witnessed it for several seconds before disappearing behind trees. A few seconds later, we obtained a view through a break in the trees, but it was gone by then.
