Red light moving slowly at 12:15 Jan 1 2012

I was closing curtains in my bedroom to go to bed and noticed a slowly moving red light in the sky. It was a steady red light with nothing flashing from it and moving so slowly that I ruled out an air plane. It was much too high in the sky to be fireworks from festivities. I thought meteor but do meteors move so slowly??? Anyhow, my wife also witnessed it and we watched for about 3 minutes and it dissappeared either under the thick cloud cover or just vanished.
