Did not see it until reviewing photos on home computer.

My wife and I were on a one day tour of London in May 2013 and I was simply snapping photos and a few videos of the landmarks. We were unaware of their presence and did not see any UFO's. To my knowledge nobody near us saw anything unusual. When we returned from the trip I loaded the photos in my computer and reviewed them for the first time. One of the photos was of a passenger plane coming out of the clouds near a high rise building I believe to be called the shard. I enlarged the photo to get a better view of the plane for a possible enlargement and noticed a bright object between the plane and the building. It appeared to me to possibly be a UFO so I continued to examine more closely the other photos I took in London and found a few more objects. There were some larger clouds that appear to have unusual well formed shapes that I cannot say are UFO's. I did accidently shoot a video of the Tower Bridge with an object moving in the sky above it. Again, I did not see it at the time. I attached some of the photos below and am not sure if I have some others as well.
