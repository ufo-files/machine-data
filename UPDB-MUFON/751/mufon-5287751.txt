Saw an orange light approaching from west. Flew overhead light gone just round black shape the flew east totally silent.

It was May 22nd 2021 approximately 9:04 pm. I was outside with my dog Bandit waiting for him to do his business when I saw an orange light approaching from the west at about 1000 to 1500 feet. As it flew overhead I could not see the orange light anymore but could see the black circular shape of the object. There was no sound at all and it flew at a steady speed. From first sighting until it disappeared in the clouds to the east was approximately 10 seconds. The sky was overcast but the object was below the clouds until it disappeared in them to the east.
