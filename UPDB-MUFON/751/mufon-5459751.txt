{rjl} like nothing we ever seen

I picked my son up after work at 2 am and on the way home, he noticed and pointed out a UFO. No question about it. We watched in and out of the clouds and light fog for a little less than 30 seconds. It was on a slight decent heading south east. From our vantage point it could have been over Plant City Florida area. It was really hard to tell but it looked huge. I guess the best way to describe it is that it kind of looked like a comet because all the lights were facing "backward" They looked more like bright beams of light or some sort of energy and not "tails" of broken light like a comet. The lights were definitely fixed on the object and stayed the same exact length and intensity over the duration of the sighting. The shape was the most striking. It looked like it could have been shaped like a pencil....very long with 2 other pencils connected to it but kind of set toward the aft of the craft. At the end of each pencil was the light beams. This may sound strange but it almost seemed like the lights only showed when they were actually in the cloud/haze. Its so hard to explain. It wasnt moving in an real extraordinary way or speed but was going fast looking for its sheer size and length. The lights/beams or whatever they were white with kind of a very faint green tint unless the color was from the haze. It was not a normal plane, period. No anti collision lights , just the lights or whatever it was facing backwards behind it. Definitely not a comet either....not fast enough for a comet and lasted too long and was too "stable" in its appearance. I did open my window and heard no sound at all. We got to the next traffic light and there was a car at it so they probably seen it too. We also passed a Pasco Co Sheriff near wiregrass mall about 60 seconds before the sighting and Im certain he/she seen it.
