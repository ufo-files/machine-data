Hovering ball of light

I was heading westbound on Grand River west of the airport on my motorcycle. The sun was shining appox in the southwest. I don't know why but this bright light caught my eye. (there were high thin clouds but the object was below them) I first thought that it was the headlight of an airplane landing but it seemed much brighter, almost like a welding torch, being that the sun was bright and I had sunglasses on. This light hovered, got even brighter then fizzled out leaving a silver/grey ball that then faded out in about 2 seconds. I was amazed that that silver/grey ball was left behind. I have no idea what that could have been. The time is within a minute or two as I was cutting it close with my daughter coming home from school
