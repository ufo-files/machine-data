White light orb flying W to E and faded out above me

I went outside to look at the stars and noticed a white light coming over the mountain top to the west. It was flying in a straight path towards the east. At first I thought it was an airplane but then noticed no flashing lights. I started video with my iPhone 11, trying to keep it centered in frame the best I could. When I zoomed in on the object, it appeared to be a white orb and seemed to fluctuate occasionally between appearing solid and transparent. I followed it’s path with the camera until it went transparent again and then faded out to nothing almost directly above where I was. It was pretty exciting!
