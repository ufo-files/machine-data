Observed UFO at home

We have a penthouse and was enjoying the sun. We see a lot of sky in our place and I regularly observe the various planes and objects that fly in our area in Vancouver. At the listed time I noticed an object that seemed out of place in the sky so I observed it. It seemed very high and seemed like a satellite but in the day light. There was no emissions. And I casually watched it fly over top of us. As I continued to watch it was eventually South East and stoped. That’s when I New it might be something unusual. My wife was with me and I told her then that I was following an object that seemed out of place. She grabbed the binoculars and it clearly was not an aircraft or drone. It was spherical, bright metal chrome, seemed stationary, and could observe sometype of lights, at times red. It was hard to tell because of its hight, distance, and lack of foreground objects but it appeared to lower in the sky. Knowing it’s distance and the lack of zoom on my camera I assumed it wouldn’t come out clearly in a picture, yet I took one any way. I was shocked that when you zoom into the picture there is some form that can be seen.
