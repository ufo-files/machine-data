{jpg} Several orbs with yellow lights came from east, hovered, then quickly moved north

My husband and I were walking tonight and saw 5 orbs come from the East, hover together, and then take off north. They moved back and forth and some up and down before taking off to the north. This occurred around 9:30 tonight. Around 9 just my daughter and I were walking and we saw one orb. Right away we observed that the light, which was yellow, and the object's movement seemed unusual. We didn't think much about it. But then my husband and I saw several more orbs and that's when we started to conjecture about what they were.
