Orange Orb Flying Low Over Carol Stream Illinois

I pulled into my driveway at exactly 7pm and went to the back of the car to take out groceries. I've seen (and previously video taped) a sighting so I am now inclined to always look up. From the back of my car I saw a low flying orange ball or orb that was moving very fast over my culdesac. It made no noise and moved in a very smooth line from south to north. After it was over my home it either disappeard into night clouds or vanished all together. I tried to record on my cell phone, but it wouldn't pick anything up. There were planes flying well over the orange ball, so it was clear to me that this wasn't a plane. Someone also reported seeing the same thing in Bellview Illinois a couple of hours later (on another site). Very weird.
