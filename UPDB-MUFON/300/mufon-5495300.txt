Oblong box shape with three observable colored lights, white and two blue. Craft was fast and noisy and flew from south to southwest before going out of sight.

Craft first heard then seen at around 10.50 pm Monday 23rd March in Airdrie, Scotland. Shape was that of an oblong box with three discernable static lights, white on the left, blue in middle and blue on right. 

Craft was moving fast because when first observed through living room window in the sky, was gone by the time observer opened front door. Craft was very noisy, which alerted observer to look out of south facing window in living room. 

Was definitley NOT an aeroplane. We are directly in the flight path of constant planes returning to Glasgow airport in the south west or Cumbernauld in the north. We also have the odd helicopter buzzing around (Police or Power coys) this craft was totally distinct from these.
