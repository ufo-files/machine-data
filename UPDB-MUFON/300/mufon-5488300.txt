{nd} Circular object spotted north east overhead traveling around 50 mph darted southwest and out of sight, dull bluish glow around disclike object

I was traveling south in my vehicle on Seymour RD in Montrose, MI, between lake RD and the Montrose Orchard. Around 8:50 PM EST+- 2 minutes. To my south west, I saw the underside and outline of a circular disc like object nearly overhead. The disc was dull colored, barely reflecting the ambient light in the night sky, the object had a very faint pinkish glow around it , it was traveling I would estimate 60-70 mph west for approximately 1-2 seconds then darted off at an extremely high rate of speed to the south west out of sight. Approximately 3-5 seconds later I saw a 2nd object exactly like the first, traveling in the same manner and flight path as the 1st object. Then 15-25 seconds later, I spotted a 3rd object 1000 -1500 yards more southerly, exactly like the 1st and 2nd traveling very close/ parallel to the 1st and 2nd objects flight path. All objects were observed from my vehicle, I was traveling approximately 45 MPH in a southward direction. I would estimate the craft to be roughly 100-200' in diameter. I was extremely excited/ shocked to see this sort of event. I have never seen anything remotely close to this in the 30 years of my life. I just continued traveling south on Seymour rd. another quarter mile, then turned into Riverside Market.
