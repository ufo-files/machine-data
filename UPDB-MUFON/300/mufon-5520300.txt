Strange moving lights in the sky of Miami

At a bit passed 8pm I went out for a walk with my fiance. The skies were clear and we walked together and talked until we arrived to the gyms door area. As I was going to say good bye before my work out I looked up in the sky. I saw immediately many lights at least 5 or 6 moving fast and chasing each other. They were not flying in a straight line and they were not airplanes. I pointed them to my fiance who happens to be an airline pilot and he also saw them. He said, "they must be satellites". I said they cant be satellites, there are too many and they are moving in irregular patterns. After no more than 15 seconds all of the lights vanished. I looked and try to spot them elsewehere in the sky but I didn't see anything else like it. We did see seconds later 2 planes flying from different directions and altitudes but definitely not related nor similar to the lights we saw previously.
