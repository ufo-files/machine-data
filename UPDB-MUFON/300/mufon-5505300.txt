{cak} close encounter of 1st kind

Myself and 3 friends were stargazing N.W of Normal, Il. looking east. Summer of 95 or 96. We noticed a "star" dropping in elevation until just above horizon. Next it started moving towards us until eventually coming very close to the car. Appx. 50-100 feet into field from road and 25-50 feet in air. No sound from object and flight path was from east to west. Object remained at constant brightness level. Size of object was similar to a large car or truck Once past us it picked up speed until out of sight. Estimate: moving 30mph until even with us and then faster after it past us. Felling a bit nervous after we headed home right away. Multiple sightings on multiple occasions in the sky but only this one time did we get the flyby. Gave phone interview with mufon today as well.
