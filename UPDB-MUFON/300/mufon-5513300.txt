{dr}A single light. Looked like a plane or helicopter light at first until we realized it had no lights by the wings like airplanes do... It was a single light... Moving all over a times, spiraled at one time..then ascending to nothing.

At around 2am my cousin and I went outside to get away from a house full of people. We lived in Jackson in a wooded area. We sat in the car listening to music and talking about girlie stuff. We were just talking, listening to the radio, and looking at the sky when we saw a "star". I cant even remember who noticed it first, but then it looked kind of close so she said "I think thats an airplane".i said.".no it cant be...there arent any lights on the side."
 Then we thought maybe it was the angle .. It was an airplane that was facing our direction and thats why we couldnt see the side lights maybe... We watched it because it just seemed "off" or strange somehow.then it darted diagonally upward ... i guess you could say a 2 oclock direction from where it was before that.... then darted toward to the left. it then made a spiral .. And we were both like omg! Its a crashing helicopter!.. But then it just stopped and hovered. We then realized theres no way a helicopter can move that fast in the distance it would have travelled that far up in the sky... It moved WAY faster than any airplane could. ...then it went diagonally across as fast as a shooting star moves and disappeared completely.
