{nd} Glowing light foul smell for very long period of time this was in very remote area

Our family was on our annual camping trip pulled off highway to camp for night on very UN traveled road shot some rabbits while driving in for future meal stopped made camp while mother was making sandwiches for dinner myself and my dad cleaned rabbits we noticed a foul smell not to bad at first but got very bad as night progressed so bad made me sick to my stomach went outside camper to use bathroom noticed glowing light behind camper was not there when we stopped no cars or town for miles in this area in morning no smell no animals no reptiles nothing touched rabbit guts that I walked out of camp and through as far as I could they were still in the paper sack very odd being in the desert
