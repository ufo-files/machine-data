I was driving. This is the second time I have seen a similiar craft over this exact highway. There were hundreds of other cars, no one else seemed to notice it. It was hovering, not moving at all, only about 50-75 feet above the highway. I passed directly under it. There was dull greyish lights in sort of a peace sign type formation, without the circle, underneath. The 3 lights were very bright. I couldn't believe other drivers were not distracted, I almost side swiped another car looking at it. After I got off the highway and wrapped around the exit ramp, I could still see it in the distance. About 5 minutes before that, i saw what I thought was a drone in another area. This one had green and red lights on it and was also hovering, but it was over a tree line. It was smaller than the one I saw directly. I was driving so unfortunately I could not safely take a picture of video.
