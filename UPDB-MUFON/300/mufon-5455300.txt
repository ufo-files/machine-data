{cak} Golden Yellow lights in diamond shape hovered in sky, much larger than star

Four people, adults in 60’s and 70’s, saw NW 30-degree azimuth in night sky over [location removed] on February 22, 2020. It was like a huge star in the sky but with several distinct lights. Possibly four lights in a diamond shape with two lights trailing. Looked like a star low in the sky at first, but much larger. We could see it from the car as we drove four or five miles back to home also in the same town. The object did not move. Stayed in the same place as we watched. It was not a star – we could see stars above us in the sky clearly. These lights were golden in color. We got out of the car and still could see the light clearly. Our neighbors were frightened and went inside their house. We went out in our car to try to find it, driving toward Lake Nasworthy. But the light had vanished. It was just gone. We saw it clearly, and it was very large, for about 20 minutes. Then it was just gone
