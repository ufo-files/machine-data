Saw a silver orb in the sky while driving to work. Disappeared in a very specific way in front of my eyes.

I was traveling on Kernsville Rd in Orefield PA when I noticed (I drive constantly so i always notice things visually) a silver orb in the sky, small to me but I have no idea how large it would be. What made this interesting to me, also scary, was that while i was looking at it, it just like, ah, vanished slowly? Imagine somebody turning down the opacity of an image from 100 to 0 within a 2 second period. I have never seen anything like it. I fully watched it slowly disappear in front of my eyes. I have seen weird things in the sky and could just chalk it up to perceptive error or...i simply don’t know. THIS though behaved in a way I can’t explain and i’m still weirded out by it. It was a very short sighting and It did not appear to move in any direction (that i know of) , but it DID disappear in the way described. I don’t think i’ve ever considered submitting anything ever relating to...this, to anyone or anywhere. this though, I feel almost obligated.
