Colors pulsed up & down & up on object

I was talking out trash at the Travel Center where i work and i looked up and saw this object in the distant sky pulsating and flashing all these different colors. I immediately radioed in to my coworker to come see this thing. We both tried to capture good pictures of the anomaly but albeit the camera on either of our phone wasn’t powerful enough to get a perfect photo, but what we did capture was a short video and a couple pics in which you can clearly see this thing. We observed it for probably 15 min or so but abandoned the observation to return to work.
