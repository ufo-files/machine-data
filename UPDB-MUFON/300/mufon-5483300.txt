Wednesday and Thursday mornings between 3:40am and 5:00am, I photographed some very interesting objects in the sky. I was in my backyard when I saw them. I would guess I saw at least 12 different 1OBJECTS1 flying high in the sky over Boulder, Colorado where I live. It is entirely possible that some of them may have returned and where aware of my observance. I often see 1lights1 in the sky, this is not unusual for me, so I was looking for them, but this time, I decided to try and get some photographs. What came out is very interesting indeed. I will send you three of the most interesting photos taken with my Canon Digital T6i with a 75-300mm zoom lens with an iso of 6400 at 1/4 second exposures. Because this is handheld, the images show a trail and the shake of my hands, but what is interesting is the glow that emanates from the objects themselves as you will see. Most of the objects traveled in a straight line, some would slow and seemed to hover near my position. I couple times I have seen objects that seem to dim out completely after I notice them and both nights I saw in the northern portion of the sky a craft that intermittently blinked with high intensity which is why I noticed it and then it would go dark to a point where you had to strain to see it. I felt I guess a slight nervousness the entire time. I would watch each object until I could no longer see it or get a clear photo of it.
