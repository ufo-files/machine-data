{cak} lights above huron south dakota

i was standing outside of my house smoking a cigarette. i noticed 3 lights in the sky to my N NE direction. they was hovering in one area stacked on top of each other. the lights would move up and down really fast. i noticed to the west that there was the same lights that appeared. they didn't move at all. while observing the lights to the west, i noticed another set of lights the same color that was doing a cartwheel motion going from the west to east directly above me. the lights would flash. it disappeared a few times reappearing a long distance away finally disappearing for good. i looked back to the north NE and notice balls of white light shooting from the lights. there was one ball of white light that absorbed back into the three lights. what threw me for a loop was the night before, i had observed the same set of lights on the other side of town.
