{cak} Orb shaped erratic flight

Observed floating in what appeared to be air updrafts, rapid position changes, move south, then back North and finally south to South east. Movements sometimes rapid, sometimes hovered. When it was relatively stable, there were sudden bright bursts on same horizontal plane a distance away. Glowed. At first thought it was just a plastic bag floating on wind drafts until it went into cloud banks and came out again. Both vertical and horizontal movement. We are near the airport in Denver, less than 20 miles, but this was not in any of the same take off of approach patterns I have observed.I
Mother was first to observe. Eyesight not the best so she called me out of the house, i, in turn called out my 25 and 16 year olds.
The clouds today are wispy and scant yet it seemed to take cover in them several times. We attempted to film with phones but difficult to see in playback because of the brightness of the day.
