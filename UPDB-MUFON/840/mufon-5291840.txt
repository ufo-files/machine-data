UFO SIGHTING, Friday, September 18, 2020, Port St. Lucie, FL, 8:58 PM

It was almost 9:00 PM and I had gone outside to our lanai to latch the two screen doors of our in-ground pool enclosure. It was cloudy and there weren't many stars in the sky. As I was walking over to the screen door facing the west side of the pool I looked up and saw this huge object moving very slowly towards the NW. It had bright, white blinking lights and made no sound at all. It looked like there were about 10 or 12 white, blinking lights on each side flying in a V-formation, like some birds do. But two of these bright, white blinking lights at the bottom of the formation on each side were a little off center and slightly to the outside of the formation. It looked like it was flying a bit below the altitude of a commercial plane. I was looking at it for about 20-25 seconds and then it seemed to fade slowly into the clouds. I ran to the front of my house hoping to get a better view, but by that time it had completely disappeared. First time in my life that I've experienced anything like this. I was in awe and amazed at what I was seeing. It happened so suddenly and it faded so quickly that I didn't even think of getting my cell phone to take a photo until I had gone over those moments later in my mind. Now I wish I had taken a photo as verification, but I wanted to document this sighting anyway. And I wanted to do it with a reputable organization.
