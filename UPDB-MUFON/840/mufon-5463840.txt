{rjl} Eerie and ominous

I was simply walking down the street and enjoying some scenery when a strange light flashed across my eyes. I was inclined to look up at the sky and saw a shape-shifting, color changing orb in the sky. It darted around, moved in strange ways, bending physics and other basic laws of the universe like it was nothing. Then it stopped. I could barely see two eyes staring at me from inside. It descended toward me and everything was a blur. I only remember a few things. When it left, the ground was distorted in every way possible.
