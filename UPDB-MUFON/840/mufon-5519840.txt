Bright glowing orange light, hovered then dissapeared

I was on a jog (I like to take my jogs late at night) in Garden Hill Ontario. I was selecting some tunes on my Ipod and when I looked back at the road I saw a light in the distance. I figured it was just a light as it looked to be at a regular height for a street light. It was far in the distance above a private airport which doesn't get any use after it gets dark. It was 11:45 P.M. The light then began to slowly raise up over the airport, the airport had no lights on. after about 10 minutes of it raising up I turned to make sure no cars were on the road behind me, when I turned back it was completely gone. 2 Mins. later 3 helicopters flew by the area where the object was. They were fairly high up. After these 3 choppers I saw no more aircrafts in the sky for half an hour.
