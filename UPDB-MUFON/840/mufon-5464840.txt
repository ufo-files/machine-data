{tjd} Looked up in sky and thought airplane, but it wasn't moving and observed for maybe 10 minutes.

My husband and I were on vacation in Hot Springs, AR. On 10/27/2018 at around 7:15 pm, we were leaving our cabin which was in a remote area about 10 minutes outside of Hot Springs. I looked up above the cabin and thought I saw an airplane, but it wasn't moving. After observing it for about a minute, I grabbed my cell phone out of my purse which was in the seat of the car, and began taking pictures. My husband asked me what the heck was taking so long, so I had him get out of the car and look to see what he thought. He was as baffled as I was. I then took a video. After turning off the video, two planes came into view going from my left to right. I was wondering how close they were to the object, but it was too hard to tell. Unfortunately, I did not get that on video. We watched it for another minute or two, and then left. When we returned after dinner, we didn't see anything unusual. My husband checked the next morning with a compass, and the object was at 150 degrees east/southeast.
