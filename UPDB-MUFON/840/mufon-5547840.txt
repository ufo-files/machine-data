Flying disc glides slowly overhead. Two friends confirm sighting and agree on description.

Our alternative education school was taking a "lantern walk" in which everyone walks with lanterns and sings songs. I cannot remember exactly how I came to notice the object, but it may have been the sound it was making. A quiet buzz or hum perhaps. When I eventually did notice the object I assumed it was a passenger plane. Then i realized there were many clues all telling me it was something else. First, it was a disc. I could tell its shape because it was outlined with small lights, similar to those seen on an airplane. Then I realized how low it was. It was either very low or very big which, either way, means it was not an airplane. It was also moving at a very slow, constant speed. I do not remember what I saw when it left my field of vision. It may have simply passed over some trees out of my line of sight. After it was gone, I went to a couple of my friends to see if they had seen it. They said they had, and a few days later we decided to draw a picture of what we had seen. We all agreed; we had all seen the same object. While I was watching the object I don't think I really knew what to think. I had never really thought about UFOs or really the unknown in general. I will definitely not forget what I saw.
