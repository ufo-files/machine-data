Disc like UFO, with white lights going around it

Me and my mom were returning home from a wedding while in the high way.

My mom tells me that there is a light in the mountain and tells me to check what it is. When I look I don't see anything right away, but then i see these five white lights that seem to belong to something. I would say it's a plane, but there were no flashing lights. The five lights went in circles, like they were on a disk shaped thing.

The UFO didn't seem to move much and the lights were going around slowly. The shape of the object could barely be seen, but the lights kept going around in circles. I wasn't scared, even though all my life I've been afraid of aliens. I was more amazed and exited and happy to see one.

At first I couldn't believe my eyes, my mom had seen it and so had I. What surprised me was that my mom was acting normal (she said she's seen something like that before).

I lost sigh of the object when some mountains went in the way, later on I saw it pretty far away and then it faded out of sight.

This is the first time I have ever seen a UFO and I am 100% sure that it was nothing made by man.
