{nd} at first appeared to be a small cloud, but then realized there were no clouds in the sky

I was just leaving for work, approximately 6:15am on Nov 4th,and as I drove along in the neighborhood (NE Mesa, AZ), the object caught my attention. I'm not sure why I even noticed it, but at first I thought it was a small cloud. It hit me, though, there was not a cloud in the sky. Maybe it was the contrast of a perfectly clear sky and then this shape. I watched it the whole drive out of my subdivision trying to figure out what it was. I don't believe it moved, but I was moving so not sure. It finally hit me that this might be a UFO, so I took a picture as I was leaving my subdivision. I didn't think to zoom in before taking it, so it's difficult to see unless you zoom. It's in the middle of the photo, about 2/3 of the way up. After taking the picture, I continued onto work, so didn't see what happened to it. There is nothing that is really spectacular about this sighting, except I can't let it go, I just can't imagine what it could be if not a UFO, hence this report.
