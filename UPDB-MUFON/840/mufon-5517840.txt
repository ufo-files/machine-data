{nd} A large diamond or saucer shaped craft appeared twice within 20 minutes. It made a furocious sound, had many white lights around its exterior and had a red light on the top of it.

Myself and two of my sons were sky watching when a silvery saucer or diamond shaped craft appeared from the northwest heading toward the west fairly fast. Because of the surrounding trees we could only observe a few seconds of its passing. It made a very loud growling/hovercraft-like/jet-like sound as it traveled very low considering its size. My son, a veteran, said it sounded like a fighter jet but it didn't look anything like one.
We don't know what what this object was but we think it made two passes 15-20 minutes apart following the same exact flight pattern.
If its a military vehicle what the heck is it doing in a residential neighborhood at 11:00p.m.? And if it wasnt military...what was it?
We lost site of the object when its flight pattern placed it behind a neighbors pine tree.
