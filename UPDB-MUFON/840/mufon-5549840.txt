Triangular-shape changing object, dull amber color

My wife and I, and a friend were waiting outside at a Carrabbas restaurant inGreensboro, NC. We were sitting outside on a bench and I noticed the object tothe north.(7:20PMET) My first thought was, ok, its probably just an airliner,or a small plane. As it got closer, I felt pretty certain it wasnt a normalaircraft. The object at the distance was light amber colored/goldish yellow, very faint. It was moving ESE, unknown speed. I held my hand up to it withthumb and pinky finger extened it and it move from the thumb to the finger inabout 4 seconds, not sure how fast that is, but thought you guys could give anestimate. As it came close to over head I could make out a structure,it had whatlook like a cluster of small spheres within it moving in and out,within thecraft, no noise and a very faint glow around it. I attracted a crowd of peoplearound me who also saw it, and one lady even let out a startled yelp. It movedoff to the southeast and eventually was out of view.
