{jpg} Walking dog,saw 30 red&yellow saucers go from north horizon to east. No sound. To other witnesses

At 9:15 PM 3/8/18 I WAS walking my dog in Newtown,Ct. I looked up & saw an orange looking object at airplane level. But their was no lites blinking. I looked down at my dog,then back up, & there were 4!!! I then ran & got my neighbor, knowing I needed a backup witness. Another lady dogwalker joined us, & we watched 30 of the same type aircraft come up from the northern horizon, at regular intervals, slowly arcing to the east. The her last one was twice the size of the rest.ALl were silent, and no other aircraft were around them.It all took at least 20 min. We were all amazed & I called the Newtown police, & 3 cars responded, but the event was over, I didn't think to call during it!!!
