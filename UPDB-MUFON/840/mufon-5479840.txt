{dr}saw 3 objects fly in a strait direction, lost sight of them, reacquired sighting coming straight at us & passed within 30 feet.

I was at a friends house in the back yard with my friend and his parents. Totally clear night (no clouds at all) saw 3 disc shaped objects in triangular formation flying in a straight line. Lost sight of the objects for 2 or 3 seconds & when we regained sighting the objects were coming straigh at us. When the objects went by us they were a little off to our right. There was a slight humming sound (you would never hear anything if there was distance to the objects) & within 2 or 3 seconds you could smeal ozone in thr air. We were standing 10 to 20 feet below the crest of the hill & when the objects passed the crest we lost sight of them. The sighting was reported to the police dept. & found out that other people had seen the objects. My friends name was "B". Don't know the parents first names. Using Google Maps I think I can identify the exact location that we were standing in at the time of the sighting.
