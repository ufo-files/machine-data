Straight, single-file line of 20+ star-like objects pass overhead.

At about 6.15 a.m. (still dark) I was putting out the recycling rubbish. The bin is opposite my house door [due east], on the other side of a narrow drive (see photos). The bright star, Vega (?), caught my eye for some reason and as I looked up I noticed there was a stream of white, star-like lights passing directly overhead, in a due west to east direction. They were one behind the other, in a dead straight line, equidistant and totally silent. Impossible to gauge the altitude. I'm guessing 3-500 feet. Some appeared to have a curious wobble. There must have been at least 20/25. It was partly cloudy but I had a clear view in an arc of about 20/30 degs. I woke my wife to have a look. We went outside and they were still passing overhead. My wife witnessed and counted the last 8. They disappeared into the cloud cover. Interestingly, there appeared to be quite a few dimmer lights around just after the sighting (they looked the same as satellites do - tiny, dim pinpricks of light). I only took two photos - to be honest, it was dark so I didn't think they'd develop. I transferred them onto my laptop out of curiosity more than hope. The original photos are numbered 1a and 2a. Brightness enhanced photos are numbered 1b and 2b. I also include day time photos of the scene. Strangely the photos of the objects seem to suffer from what appears to be camera shake, whereas the stars picked up do not. However, I'm no expert! . Camera is a Fujifilm X30. They seem attracted to me and my wife - sighting number 5!
