Two silver discs hovered. Sudden zig-zag movements in sync. Then slow flight south.

After a day-time hail storm, Brisbane 1977/78 I think, I was outside with a friend in the front yard. "Felt" my name called, and a distinct impulse to go to the back yard. 

My friend was the first to spot the two discs when we got there, pointing up to them. The sky was very blue and very clear with no clouds. Two silver discs exactly the same reflected the sunlight as they seemed to rotate, hovering fairly high in the sky. Typical saucer shape, but with no dome (same shape above and below the middle). 

They stayed there for some time, before suddenly zig-zagging then abruptly stopping at a lower point in the sky. Their movements mirrored each other. After some time of hovering (roughly 15 minutes in all) a black, rather shapeless blimp appeared between them. This black ship/helicopter/plane/whatever it was pulsated red light. Once this appeared, all three slowly moved southward, and out of sight. 

There were a few neighbours who also witnessed this.
