Cigar with Chemtrails

Very odd morning, waiting for the bus I noticed 5-6 chem trails running parallel in-between a clearing of the clouds, rather odd, however, a small Cigar shaped 'object' was stationary, it had a thin smokey trail behind it. Strange because the Cigar was very small and thin, I doubt it was a cloud because all the other clouds where large and bulky.

I am not concluding that this was Extraterrestrial but it seems interesting to see Parallel trails and some kind of UFO.
