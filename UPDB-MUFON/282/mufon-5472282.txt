I was wide awake early saturday morning. Went on the porch for a smoke. Looked up in the sky where I usually tend to and saw two bright non flashing lights. Simular to stars, but slightly brighter. They caught my eye becouse they were both traveling north at a high rate of speed north. They had a good distance apart, but were going the same exact speed. They were way to high to be airplanes and were going too fast. As they passed over they both started to dim and I lost sight.
