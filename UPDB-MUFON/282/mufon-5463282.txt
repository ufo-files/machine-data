{cak} Two light orange-red spheres flying SE in a smooth linear fashion approx 4-5 times faster than commercial jet.

I was taking my dogs out in the backyard to go to the bathroom and while I waited for them I was looking up at the stars. I didn1t see anything out of the ordinary for the first five minutes and then out of nowhere I saw two orange-red orbs appear from the northwest heading directly southeast in a very linear, smooth trajectory. They appeared to be almost transparent with a muted orange color and orb shaped. They seemed to be cruising at an altitude below 5000ft and were moving at least four to five times faster than a commercial jet. I followed them across the sky for 15-20 seconds until they were beyond the horizon.
