{cak} Triangle in shape with two very bright headlights.

Date unknown @ 2002 At 11:30 pm. While standing on my deck, I witnessed an aircraft approaching my home from around 10-20 ft above the trees. I yelled for my two kids to come see. The craft was directly above our house around 40- 100 ft above and silent. It had two very bright front lights. As it hovered (1 minute) it took off in a flash. It went from west to south. It disappeared above my neighbors tree. The sky was clear with many stars. The craft was silent with no air pressure. It looked just like a stealth bomber.
