3 bright red/orange lights in the sky above Vancouver, Wa

The 3 lights were glowing orange/red and traveling in a line or triangular shape, then lined up and one by one ascended and kind of evaporated straight up into the sky in front of us. The fist one we saw was alone, then the second two appeared. They hovered there for about 5 minutes, and it was like they were watching us watching them. Very strange. We took pictures, but by the time we got the cameras out, the first one had ascended already. We got pics of the last 2.
