Small glowing orb

My dad and I were walking out to check on some trees, when all of a a sudden when we were near the first tree, a bright, small (around the size of a basketball) yellow-white glowing orb/circle flew out above and past a shop, flew right by us, then once it hit the treetops it disintegrated/disappeared. We looked around but could not find any remains of the object. What really got us was how it just disappeared once it hit the treetops, just like poof, gone. We investigated and tried to reason out what we saw, trying to make an excuse for what the object was, but no excuse fits what happened, so we figured that, well, it might have been and probably was,a UFO.
