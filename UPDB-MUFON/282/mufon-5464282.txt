{rjl} Steady starlike light, no sound, faded while still fairly close... estimated at less than a half mile.when it vanished

My family and I were headed home after grocery shopping. I noticed 2 stars above, southeast, and to the right of highway 111. They appeared to be spreading apart. I pointed out what I was seeing to my wife and she agreed they were seperating. One was actually a star, but the second was moving. It did not flash, twinkle, or pulsate. It was only white in color. We opted to pulled to a safe location off highway 111 and I began filming with my cell phone. As I zoomed in, I realized we had driven under this light as it was now northwest of us. It made no sound and eventually faded to darkness. This was not because it was too far away to see as it was less than a 1/2 mile away when as if someone turned the dimmer switch slowly to the off position. We both felt excited.
