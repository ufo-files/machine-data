Saw silver white object while driving, my son took random pictures while moving. it vanished in last picture.

I was driving my son to a basketball game and noticed this object that looked like a star. There were the normal jets we always see crossing deep in the distance leaving their exhaust trails behind them. The sun was starting to set, I thought the object was not normal, so I told my son to take a few pictures of it. After the last picture he took of the object, he said it was gone. I looked in the area where it was, and sure enough, it was gone. These pictures were taken within 20 seconds for sure. There were still a few jets left, but no where close to where the object was sited by my son and I.
