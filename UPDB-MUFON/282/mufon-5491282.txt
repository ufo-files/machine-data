{cak} Small, fast moving object seen over Boise.

While sky watching from Camelsback Park I witnessed a small, circular, white object move extremely fast across the sky from the NE to SSW in about 6 seconds. Craft was above the cloud line but of unknown altitude. Object moved from one section of the sky to the other too quickly to be a satellite, but considerably slower than a meteor and left no trail. No noticeable noise was associated with its movement.
