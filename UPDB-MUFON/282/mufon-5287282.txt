May 5, 2021 Santee, CA I was inside watching the camera monitor and TV when I saw it appear on the East monitor, I turned down the TV. It came down from above the cloud line and flew overhead making a sound we did't hear at first. But afterwards I found another camera with a mic had picked up its sound, I was amazed, it was very low and we had to amp it up to hear it. A 3D analysis it peaked at 98Hz and was between 20-200Hz. I understand why we didn't hear it now.
It was not a bird or bat in had an engine we didn't hear from inside. And it looks like it has wings and is glowing so its not a plane either so I'm guessing. It is also Absent in some video frames. I don't know what it was.
