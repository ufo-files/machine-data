{dr}Re-occuring phenomenon. Seen 4 different days out of last 5. Satellite or meteor like but can change speed and direction in an instant.

Follow-up to Case #75502 and an ongoing event since first seen again this year on March 27th 2016. They are still appearing regularly albeit fewer in number. I only witnessed 2 last night around 10:00pm. I was unsuccessful in trying to video and photograph these things - the tripod was too wobbly and the camera is not designed for the type of night sky filming. The following new details maybe helpful. The constellation that the objects most often appear in is Canis Major. The bright object I thought was Jupiter is actually the brightest star in the northern hemisphere sky, Sirius. I need more eyes looking for these objects I call "Sbrats." I have provided a constellation map which should have enough details to allow others to look for these things. Unlike satellites and meteors the Sbrats appear to maneuver as well as shoot-off in a straight line at incredible speed.
