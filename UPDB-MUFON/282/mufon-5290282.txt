This sighting was an USO not a UFO

The USO was back in 1997 whilst working on Holme-Next-To-Sea Nature Reserve on the north Norfolk coast, UK (TF 714 449) towards the latter half of August. I was at the time a years trained observer of birds and indeed was working as an assistant warden of the reserve at Holme-Next-To-Sea. I had just finished a circuit of the reserve looking for birds and making sure visitors were behaving when at the top of the sand dune, in front (north) of the visitor centre I spotted a disturbance in the sea just off-shore bearing in mind the tide was way out at the time (early to mid-afternoon). I observed the object with a pair of Carl Zeiss BGAT 10x40 binoculars and the weather was good with bright sunshine.

Being someone who is his own worst enemy when comes to observing things, rationalising things first; I proposed to myself that maybe the object was a cetacean (whale etc.), jet ski or power boat with a person behind or even a school of fish but all of these were instantly discounted. The sea was boiling, some 20-30 feet across, circular if looking from above with a small black ‘post’ sticking up from the centre. The USO was slowly moving eastward along the coast and was observed for about 5-10 minutes. By way of comparison in size, there were people scattered on the vast expanse of sand some near the shoreline some 100-150 feet from this object.

In the end and although pulled I decided to put it down to experience and never mentioned it to anyone. It was only after watching a UFO program a few years ago (about 2015-2016) where a USO was photographed off Stockholm, Sweden depicting an area of water that looked as if it was boiling with a small black post sticking up from the centre that I privately exclaimed, “That’s what I saw!” that I now believe I had seen a USO in that instance.
