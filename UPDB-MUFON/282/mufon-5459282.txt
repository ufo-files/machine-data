{cak} Ufo came and went quickly

I was close to highway 33 in Ventura,CA. It was a little bit past ten and I was outside smoking looking in the sky. Then I noticed a new bright star in the SW sky. I watched as it went from standing still to slowly moving north. It was about 30 to 45 degrees from level and in direction of the islands over the ocean. After slowly moving north for about 8 seconds it changed direction and went slightly west but mostly vertical. It was going so fast after it changed direction it looked like it was stretching or it left a trail of light. About 30 seconds later I was still looking in that area when from the south a single plane was flying through same area. I assume it was a fighter because of the sound and its speed but can't say for sure.
