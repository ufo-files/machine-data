Light seen in sky, too high for drone, move abruptly, in a flight path for planes landing

While walking to the shops at 20:38, I noticed a strange object in the sky, it was too fast and agile to be a helicopter, and moved across the sky far too fast to be a drone, if it was a drone, it could have to be capable of covering miles in minutes and flying in excess of 1km off the ground. I record a video with my phone with UHD settings, and kept the street lights in frame as much as possible to help show its speed. I then returned home to grab 50mm 30x binoculars. When I returned to the spot 5 minutes later it was heading away, a distance had doubled by this time, and it was ascending at the same time. When my binoculars lost sight of the object, it was over the cloud cover, which would have been 2km this night. The object would needed to have been over 10km away from me at this point, it's lights barely visible. There was 2 solid red lights on it, and a flashing blue light. The object was above a path used for commercial aeroplanes coming into land, planes always travel from the East Westwards and turn north to come into land, however they always travel a constant speed, this object turned over it's path multiple times and darted around before departing. I could probably do the trigonometry maths to determine exact angles and possible distances but the margin of error would be high.
