{jpg} Thought it was a satellite

The objet moved north to south. We were watching satellites traverse the sky. At first we thought it was a satellite until it got directly overhead and stopped. It emitted Avery bright beam of light that shown down on us. Bright enough to cause a shadow. It the started moving again and just disappeared. We were shocked at first then a little scared. We were at our camp house in the country very alone out there. We did not notice any loss of time.
