Sightings of UFO in 1992, Angola, Benguela City

good morning, this sighting took place in Angola, Benguela City in 1992, i was 11 years old at the time, and i witness at least 3 sightings, but i remember at the time family members, friends and local news reporting more sightings everyday, for 3 or 4 months straight, first time that i witness i was playing football and all of a sudden everyone started to look at the sky and i saw a object hovering and with a weird directional change of path, i was in owe and i couldn't take my eyes of the object, it was beautiful and scary at same time, i remember older people taking strange conclusions about it, calling the children to get out of the street, the second time was at night and were two objects and they had some lights on it, i have now 38 years old, and with time our memories change real events, but i believe that with some research MUFON can find the truth of my report, there was some local interviews on local Angola television with testimony of pilots and others, a curious and coincidental event was, when the sightings ended the violent civil war returned ending a 2 Years peace resolution.
