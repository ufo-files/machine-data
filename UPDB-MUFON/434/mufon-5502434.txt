{rjl} My mother- a christian woman - pointed it out to me and was quick to dismiss it as remote controlled. I think otherwise.

My mother, who is a very Christian woman was looking outside the window, when she called me over and pointed out to me a classic saucer-shaped object hovering in the sky. It had lights that seemed to be spinning around the outside bottom edge of the craft, with more lights toward the center that were stationary, I can't remember exactly what color the lights were as it was about 8 or so years ago (I am 23 now) but I do remember seeing piercing white lights on it, however that's wasn't the ONLY color observed. It was hovering rather low, and my mother was quick to dismiss it as remote-controlled, she doesn't believe in UFOs, and I hadn't been educated on the subject but I knew that they were supposedly controlled by things not of this earth. I didn't think much of it after she said it was remote controlled. But knowing what I know now, I'm convinced it could have been a real sighting. My first reaction when I saw it was a wave of shock over my body, I simply didn't know what I was looking at. I watched it fly away, picking up speed as it did. It was traveling pretty fast, but not fast enough to automatically know it's not human-made.
