small white orb, appeared semi-transparent like a balloon

The orb was observed well overhead and slightly to the northeast above the ocean. It was not moving at all. After observing through binoculars for awhile, we returned to fishing. Periodically we looked up, and the orb was still there. By late afternoon, approx 5pm, we realized it had moved slightly and was now more to the SW, however we believe that may have been due to earth's rotation. There was strong trade winds but the object was not affected at all by winds, so although it looked like a balloon, it could not have been. I noticed in the late afternoon when some clouds appeared that it was above the clouds, which passed under it temporarily blocking our view as it passed. Therefore the orb must have been quite large as the clouds were fairly high above us. Unfortunately it was too high against the bright sky for our phone cameras to get a shot.
