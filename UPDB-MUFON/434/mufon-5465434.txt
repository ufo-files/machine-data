{jpg} 8-10 lights in a triangle formation, then out of formation then back in formation

I was looking at the night sky 10-4-18 @ 8:57PM with a friend. I look at the sky 4-5 times a week before I go to bed and have good vision. I can see satellites clearly at night moving. I was focused on a couple stars when I saw approx 8-10 lights in triangle formation high in the night sky for about 2 minutes. They were in a perfect triangle formation coming up from the horizon to nearly overhead then they slowly came out of formation to almost a circular formation then suddenly within 2-3 seconds back into a perfect triangle formation. I followed as long as I could see before they were out of range. I know they were not Canadian geese or birds as I know what they look like and their formation. They also were going too fast for geese. I am in the desert near Palm Springs CA and the sky is generally clear. I have been looking into the night sky all my life and know what I saw. At first I thought they were geese when I first saw them but realized they were not when they came closer and were in a perfect triangle.
