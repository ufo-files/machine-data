{rjl} Line of 20+ lights traveling SW to NW

About 6 AM a line of lights ( over 20 ) traversing the sky. The North Star was visible. For several minutes, a series of lights moved from the south going north. The lights were traveling in a straight line. Sometimes two or three together, then a gap, then one. This continued for several minutes. Long enough for me to go inside and get my phone to try and film them. I was unable to get them on video
