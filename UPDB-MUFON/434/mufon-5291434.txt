2 Jets and 2 balls. Newcastle upon tyne.

Two jets were seen to be chasing two round, silver 'crafts' across the sky in Newcastle upon tyne, before they all disappeared into the clouds. I tried to get it on video but the recording isn't very good because I was frantic trying to catch them on video...you have to zoom in to see them. I saw them plain as day with my own eyes. They were shiny and round like golf balls.
