{dr}40-50' wingtip to wingtip. observered 3 round depressions on vessel. silent and Slowwww. no lights at all. other witnesses

I was standing behind my work taking a break when my coworker said whats that and pointed up. Upon first oberservation, i thought it was a flock of gulls or pelicans. Then it went over the street and the lights from the lightpoles lit up the underside of the craft. We could see it plain as day. It was completely silent with no lights visible. Id estimate its speed at about 20mph. It was flying from south to north between the ocean and the intracoastal waterway. Once it was out of the reflection from the light, it vanished into the night. Had we not been looking up we would have never known it was there. It is definately something that ive never seen except in scifi movies.
