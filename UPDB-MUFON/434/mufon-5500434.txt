{nd} Not sure what it was. Thought it might be toy or drone, but it would have been big.

In Perry Hall working on laptop from my car. Saw it out of the corner of my eye. Big lightning storm moving in from west when it appeared in clear skies of east. I thought it was a toy remote control airplane, but it would have been very large at the apparent distance, and it climbed straight up at the end. Wasn't sure if an RC airplane could do that. I videotaped it on my cellphone but didn't realize it was still in view when I stopped recording. Later when showing the footage to a .mil friend of mine, I noticed it reappeared at the end going straight up behind a tree - hadn't noticed at the time. .mil friend said it didn't look like a drone. Just curious what it was - was interesting to watch.
