{cak} Six lights that hovered as two triangles in formation.

My girlfriend and I were walking along the Edmonds ferry terminal when my girlfriend looked SE and said "What is that?" I looked where she pointed and saw two triangles in the sky rotating toward us in formation. I asked her to start using her smartphone to record the event which lasted about 5 minutes. The lights just vanished over us.
