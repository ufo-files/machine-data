Driving east bound on a local street mysellf and two others witnessed a group of lights that appeared moving towards us in a westerly direction , there were airplanes on the usual North westerly flight path climbing out of MIA airport and the airplanes were above these lights. I decided to pull over and the three of us got out the car , as they got closer I realized that these were not aircraft but some kind of ORB . They were bright as what a flare would look like however there were climbing steadily in a westbound heading and began to get closer together they then headed north bound then faded above us , there were no clouds around them them simply faded and vanished all at the same time. I can compare them to when you stere up a fire and the hot embers rise .. I am a trained observer and work around aircraft all the time and I know that these were not any kind of helicopter or airplane.
