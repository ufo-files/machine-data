Military Jet chased a blue light across the sky.

Had walked out side my house to have a smoke and check out Mars in the night sky. My house faces east so I was standing
looking in that direction.Mars was very bright in the sky.I saw a very bright fireball and streak travel across the sky from south to north. Assumed it was a meteor that I had heard there was alot of this time of the year. I saw one the previous week also about the same time.
 I suudenly caught something out of my eye and looked to the north. I observed a object emitting a blue light moving at a high rate of speed toward the south east. At the same time I heard a jet engine and observee a military jet with a flashing red light on the tail in pursuit of the object. I am retired military and live very close to an military airport so I know what one looks like and sound like.This plane was obviously chasing the blue light and was in after burner mode. I oserved the plane for about 20 secounds and I could tell that it was probably going to catch the blue light unfortunately my neighbors big tree was in the way and I lost sight of both of them.
