{cak} I took a picture with my camera phone of the Capital building in DC not really expecting to capture a photo of UFOs.

I was attending a rooftop party with my girlfriend and her friends when I decided to take a photo of the view. I saw the Capital building where congress works so I snapped a photo with my camera phone and posted it to Facebook not really thinking anything of it. I checked my notices and saw that a friend noticed the 6 to 8 lights in formation over or just beyond the Capital building. He said in my post "UFOs". I was confused and looked at the picture up close and saw what I had taken a picture of. I was shocked and amazed. Yes, there were airplanes around but not flying overhead. DC has restricted airspace. Could have been Military aircraft but I doubt that. Just looking off into the distance in the direction of the Capital building I didn't notice the lights at all. Perhaps the city lights drowned them out. But from the looks of it the orbs of light are pretty big. The image was not altered in any kind of way.
