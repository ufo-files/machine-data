Flaming ball of light appeared over treeline slowly ascending and eventually turned into an oblong like shape and disappeared

My girlfriend and I were sitting outside on the back porch of my brothers house and she asked me, "What is that in the sky over there?" and pointed towards the West. I looked and at this point there was an orange glowing sphere in the distance flying straight up from the horizon. At first it made me think that it was a rocket ship taking off in a far distance but there wasnt a trail behind it and we do not live beside any government or privately owned space centers that I know of. My girlfriend is beyond excited, yelling "What is it? What is it?" and I do not respond out of disbelief of my eyes. Moving slowly, it eventually turns into an oblong teardrop shape. We both watched as it ascended into the sky and eventually disappeared.
