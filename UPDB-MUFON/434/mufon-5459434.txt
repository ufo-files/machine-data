Two cigar shaped ufos

My mother took me my brother and two cousins on a holiday to crail . The four of us were messing around on the shore when for some reason I looked right above us and saw a massive silver cigar shaped craft above us. Also above that one was another just a bit higher up. They both were stationary above us and as I pointed and said look we all experienced a black for about 5 seconds then it went light again. In the black I was still concious but could not see anything or hear just black and like floating.. but me personally didnt feel afraid then all of a sudden we were back on the shore with light as normal.. it happened to all of us and still talk about it till this day.. my mum can still remember us all running into the caravan and saying it went all black.. Ron halliday put the story in his book .Scotland's ufos.. he wanted me to be hypnotized to see what happened in the black but i was afraid. But now I would do it.. I know it was along time ago but its still fresh in all our memories.. I'm not joking it wasent a plane or anything . It was two silver grey cigar shaped ufos.. it was like being switched off for five seconds..
