It was moving from north to west(Approximate). It was moving at quite a bit of speed. It barley had a blink or pulse while it moved. I watched it as much as I could Until I couldn't see it anymore. About 5 mins later in about the same place I first saw the first light, another started moving in the same direction but not the same spot, this one was lower in the horizon but had same movement, etc as first light.
On a good dark night you can watch lights move all over the horizon. Sometimes you can see better with your peripheral vision. I am going to try to get a good set of binoculars and try to get a better view. When we use to live in Greenbriar County WV we could really see things moving in the night sky.
