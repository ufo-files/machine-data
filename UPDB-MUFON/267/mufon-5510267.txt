(GS) Sighted 3 red lights, object appeared to be gliding, in 1 sec. it looked like a star going out into space.

I was taking a walk down my driveway one night in July around 10:15 to 10:30 pm on a Wednesday night 2010 sometime during the middle of the month. On the way back up the driveway I noticed 3 red lights in the night sky coming down from the Pittsburgh area. I knew right away that it wasnt a jet because of the red lights and the way it was gliding. The red lights on each end were slighly smaller than the middle one. And I would say that the craft was of good size. As large as a Jet or bigger. The red lights I would say were probably 12 to 2o feet in diameter, estamating from the distance that I saw the craft. For it was about the same as a flight leaving the airport. Also no sound. I was the only one that saw it. But it was the speed in which it left our atmosphere. It moved so fast I never saw it take off and I was watching the whole time. Two to three sec. later I caught it moving out into space and it looked like a tiny star.
