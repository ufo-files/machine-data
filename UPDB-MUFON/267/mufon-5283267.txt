Moon like light, following treeline

It was about 6:30pm, December 23,2021, my friend and I were driving back home. When we pulled into the apartments we noticed a bright moon like light over the tree line. I pulled out my phone to use my star chart application. Nothing was coming up where that light was coming from, no star or planet name appeared. We parked got out of the vehicle and I started taking pictures of it, we followed it as it pulsated on the left and right side. I zoomed in some of the pictures and you could see an aura or a mist come off of it. We watched the moonlike light as it descended into the tree line. We checked days after at the same time to see if it would come back. We saw nothing else.
