Saw an object that looked like the full moon slowly move into a position then cross the sky in one second and fly into a cloud and dissapear.

I went across the street to get my mail, one beautiful clear summer night,(from a community mail box) and while retrieving my mail from the box, I noticed movement from above. What initially appeared to be the full moon ,was an object, glowing white like the moon, except it was solid white , with no grey or dark places on the surface , like the full moon displays, moving slowly , like a top fuel dragster staging toward the starting line. Then , the object went across the entire sky ( from southeast to southwest) in one second , and flew into a small vertical shaped cloud and didn't come out of the other side. Ironically , a little further west , was the real moon , and it was not full on this night , but perhaps just a quarter moon , and it was dark orange on this night. The entire incident lasted about 1 minute. I was initially frozen in place , realizing I was witnessing an authentic ufo incident, but when in ended so abruptly , I knew that this was a situation that I may well have been the only witness , and I really didn't want to be. So , thats why I really didn't care to make a report ,because I couldnt record or photograph the object , and the date and time wasnt as important to me as actually witnessing the event for myself , so I let it go. I guess if I thought Bill Birnes was going to interview me , I think I would have risked the negativity that comes with the reports.
