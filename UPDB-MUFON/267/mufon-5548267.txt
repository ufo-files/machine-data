3 ball like orange objects in triangle formation heading north-northwest.

i was watching fireworks from my driveway looking to the south, most of the fireworks have stopped but a few were still going off.i happened to look to the south west when i saw these strange lights that were not there a moment before,the objects travelling slowly in a north by northwest direction in a large triangular pattern, much higher than the fireworks, then one "the western most point" seemed to disappear. then the other two shortly afterward, seemed to head up, one at a time towards the atmosphere until they either went out of sight or disappeared. i tried to capture them on my cell phone video camera, but was unable to see them on the screen. what do i think they were? definately not fireworks or airplanes.
