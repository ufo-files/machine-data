{nd} Outdoors taking a landscape photo, craft appeared in photo when reviewing the image

As I held the camera up to take the picture the sun was in my eyes and I couldn't get a clear view of the screen and took the picture. I than got in my car and headed home once I made it back I took a look at the photo and was as shocked as could be when I saw this craft looking object on the side of the screen
