(GS) Walking dog and watching a jet land saw orange ball out of corner of eye, then fireballs....

I was walking my dog about 6:10pm on 11/13/12 and was watching a jet land, as I like to do, and noticed an orange ball out of the corner of my eye. It had no blinking lights so I knew it wasnt a plane and it followed the tree tops...I watched it for about 5 seconds and when I turned to leave saw several "fireballs" come out of the atmosphere. I stood there in awe watching as one, then three were what looked like they were :"flicked" out of the atmosphere...I ran to get my camera and got a pic and video but it doesnt do justice to what I saw...they were following each other once they came into the atmosphere and were slow moving...I ran around one of the condo buildings to video but they were heading west. Whatever I saw was beautiful and lasted about 5 minutes.

To answer the questions on the left sidelink:

1. Walking my dog
2. I was watching a jet land but something moving in the sky to the left caught my eye
3. I thought it strange that it was orange and had no blinking lights like a plane
4. When I saw them come into the atmosphere, I tought they moved together like bubbles but in a line
5. Seeing what I saw at first was surreal and I stood there dumbfounded until I thought to go get my camera, then while trying to get pictures, my heart was pounding and I forgot all about how to ISO my camera!! After, when I calmed down, I thought how beautiful the orbs were!!
6. I followed them as they headed west.
