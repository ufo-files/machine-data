Arrived in my laneway and looked up and saw lights hovering in the back of my house. Thought they were helicopters in search for someone but when I got out of my car, no noice whatsover and they were huge.

I arrived in my laneway (farmhouse) at 9 am and when I drove down the laneway towards the house, I saw two large ships hovering over the tree tops at the back of my house where the forest is. I thought at first they were helicopters searching for someone but when I got out of the car, there was no noise and they were extremely large and almost looked like an apartment building due to all the lights. There were two. No one was home and I could not find my house keys due to the fact I had changed purses and went to the garage to obtain my spare key so I could get my camera but I guess my daughter had taken it, as usual. My husband was at a hockey game with our son and would not be home until 11 pm and there was no way I was sticking around because I was very frighten. I took my cell and took a picture but all I could see were the lights and circles on the camera. I still have the cell but don't know how good the photo is. All I can say is I got out of there as fast as I could due to the fact I was frightened and went to visit a friend as I was very shaken up until my husband came home. I got home after eleven and was still pretty shaken up but it was gone by then, thank goodness.
