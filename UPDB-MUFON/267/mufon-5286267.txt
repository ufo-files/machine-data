Disc like shape, constant light

Drinking my usual 3pm coffee on my deck and looking at phone. Glanced up and saw a light to the west. Thought some plane had on landing light on, as I live close to a small airport. Didn’t pay much attention, until it got closer to me. Maybe 500’ agl. Kept coming closer and was very large and all of a sudden it was going south. No noise, speed ?? 60 mph. Turned south about 1/4 mile from me and I ran to west house corner to see it until it disappeared behind trees. It was unbelievable. I never saw anything above the light, just the light. Maybe 150 feet in size.
