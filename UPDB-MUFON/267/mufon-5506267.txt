{cak} Red star like object accelerating roughly 10 times faster than conventional aircraft.

Arrived home at about 2:25am, parked the car and looked up at stars. The object was red and starlike, moving roughly 10 times faster than an airplane. My first thought was that it could be a satellite. The object was traveling north, and slightly shifted northeast during its travel. I knew it could not have been an airplane, because I have never seen an airplane move that fast through the sky at night, nor during the day. I ended up losing sight of the object, due to it going too far over the houses and continuing its path.
