I was sitting on my deck watching the skies. It was a clear and sunny evening. There were plenty of birds flying around at this time. All of a sudden I noticed this fast object glide a straight across the sky directly above me in a unwavering fashion. I took a closer look, thinking it was a fast flying bird. It was a round, basketball size type sphere! At that point I freaked. I immediately got out of my chair and stood up for a closer look. It continued to fly pass me and over my house. I then ran around my house to see the object. As I got around my house, it was standing still above the tree line. It did not move and I watched it for about 1 minute. I then ran back into my house to get my camcorder. When I got outside with the video camera, it was gone.
