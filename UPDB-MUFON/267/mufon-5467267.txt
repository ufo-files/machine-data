While driving to Winter Garden Village, to pick up my grand-daughter from work, I was stopped in traffic, I noticed a pulsating light that changed colors hovering in front of and a bit above my vehicle. It was round and pulsated colors changing from yellow to white to red continually. I pointed it out to my grand-daughter who rode along with me to pick up her sister. I opened the sunroof of my car and asked her to take some video of the object with her cell phone. It seemed to be about the height that a helicopter would be and stayed in the same position. Then it quickly moved a few degrees south, hovered for about a second then traffic began to move and when I looked up again, it was gone. My grand-daughter who took the video said it quickly flew north and disappeared. The sky seemed clear but, when I looked up to see which direction it was traveling it was nowhere in the sky.
