6 or 7 bright stars moving over house, blue sky sunshine, formed a V then faded into distance

Myself and my son were reading in the garden when I happened to look directly above and saw 6 or 7 what looked like a constellation of bright stars moving over the house.
They were totally silent.
They then formed a V with one of them staying outside the V formation.
The V broke up and 2 of them seemed to interact with one another before they faded into the distance.
My son tried filming them on his phone and got some grainy footage from which we managed to obtain some stills.
We live on an aircraft flight path with the airport about 2kms away 
We have no idea what we saw.
