White yellow ball of light quickly descends over hill.

I lived right next to the Yakima Training Center facility (our rural, dirt road abuts their fence. Sometimes personnel did PT running on this road. One night a friend and I were driving home and I saw this bright moon-colored light. As soon as I pointed it out to my friend it disappeared down into or behind the hills and canyon separating my house from East Valley/Yakima. Directly on the other side is a small dam and power generator station for the Bureau of Reclamation where I eventually worked. The direction of the light would have been a bit east of there though, where the bureau does have a small repeater station atop the hill for weather data telemetry, other city entities also broadcast from there.
