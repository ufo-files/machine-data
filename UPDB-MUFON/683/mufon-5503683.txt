{nd} large reddish orange light, seemed lower than the stars. got smaller rapidly then disappeared without seeming to move.

I was in the backyard at about 8:45 p.m. putting the dog out. i was looking up at the stars as i like to do and saw a reddish orange light in the western sky that was about three times larger than the north star. it seemed to be pulsating as i could see a glowing like effect from the sides. I looked at it for about 45 seconds to try to figure out what it was then went to get my husband but he had gone back upstairs. i went back outside and the light was still there for about another fifteen to twenty seconds then it started to get smaller very rapidly and went out completely without appearing to move. there were planes in the skies all around but nothing that looked even close in nature to the object. the light appeared to be closer than the stars in the skies.
