Strange star drops outta sky stops then starts changing colors then travels south after crazy flight pattern

Ok so my friend and I were walking to taco bravo late at night to get some food and there was a strange blueish star that seemed a lil to bright so we kept watching it as we were walking..after about 5 mins of this thing being dead still it shoots outta the sky to a low altitude what appeared to be over stevens creek area..It was big in the sky and bright then started changing colors and flying up and down in mishappen circles rather quickly then it started flying off towards the south relatively slow compared to how fast it was moving first...People pulled over that seen it and we talked to some of them cause at first we were skeptical as to what we were seeing..lotsa people saw this thing including the taco bravo workers..on our way back home we passed several younger spanish people running around with a video camera i guess trying to get footage of it but it had long since gone.We don't speak spanish so i have no way of knowing if they taped it before it dissapeared..But 1 thing is for sure it was something amazingly fast at first when it dropped outta the sky and there for sure had to be radar contact from san jose airport cause its right next to where we were at viewing this thing. I tried to record it with my cell camera but it looked so terrible through my cheap cell i just stopped cause it didn't show anything.But I have seen youtube footage from the same night and people are commenting about it on youtube but there has never been any mention in papers or anything an i was sure this would be...I see airtraffic all the time and nothing ever ever even resembled this things lighting flight pattern or speed..there's video out there i know it and several people saw this thing including san jose police..I seen 1 officer driving down saratoga slow down and observe it as it flew past but he didn't stop for whatever reason..
