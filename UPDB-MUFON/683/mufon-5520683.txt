Triangular Object Changing Bright Colors With Strange Sounds

Taken sometime between 1:00 and 1:45am Wednesday morning, my friend Victoria talked to someone at Luke AFB and they said they had a medical emergency and that they did not have anything in the sky. On the display of the camera, it appeared to be triangular shaped. Seeing it wit your own eyes without the camera, it changed colors and moved within a half a centimeter radius, going in circles or going up and down and side to side. Which you can see is what its doing when zoomed in. You can see at one point how far it is above the street light and by the time we were ready to finally settle in for the night, it almost at street light level. We were zoomed in at 35x at most points during the video. The static noise is the camera zooming in and out. Also take not of the strange whirring/humming sound in the last 10-15 seconds of the vid. There were no cars near our subdivision and the sounds intensity goes up and down. It kind of sounds like the "strange sounds" in the "Strange Sounds In Conklin, Alberta Jan 12th 2012", YouTube link [Link moved to FI section/cms/tg]. Its not as intense, but definitely sounds similar.
