{cak}Flashing colored lights changed to one bright light Changed direction abruptly any times hovered also

Cuyahoga Falls Ohio. It was sometime in the late 70s at my JR. High school at night. My friends and I were at an event, either a baseball game or possibly fireworks in the summer. We stayed on the hill after the event and most people were gone. My boyfriend pointed up in the sky and said look at that strange object. It seemed to be pretty far up in the sky, but still close enough to see multicolored lights flashing in a pattern around the object. It appeared to be disc/saucer shaped by the light pattern.  It sped across the sky too quickly to be an airplane, changing directions abruptly. Then it would stop and hover. When it hovered, the colored lights would stop and a large bright white light would shine out. It did not create a beam, just a bright light in the sky. This bright light would standstill for maybe 10 to 20 seconds, then it would turn off and the flashing colored lights would come on and it would zip across the sky in various directions. This whole event lasted somewhere between 5-10 minutes. It just disappeared or zipped away.  
 I called down at the police station to see if anyone had reported seeing anything and We waited over the next week or two to see if there were any other people reporting sightings in the newspaper. Never saw any reports.
