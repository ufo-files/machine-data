Unusual looking craft flying horizontally/low, then hovered, then disappeared.

I was traveling on 81N just a mile prior to Exit 313 when I saw an odd craft passing over 81N heading west. It was white in color, a smooth cylinder shape with slightly bulbous ends and appeared to have fuzzy edges, about the length of a small airplane . It was flying low and initially I thought it might be an airplane because there is an airport in town and maybe it was taking off... but it was not a plane and it stayed the same height, flying horizontally. I expected to see it accelerate however it slowed down, hovered for a bit, then disappeared. I kept looking for it as I was driving but it was nowhere to be found, however I did see a helicopter in the distance but nearby traveling north after it disappeared. It was a sunny day, with bright blue skies and no clouds. Shortly after I saw a black military looking plane that also disappeared, not sure if they are connected but it was odd.
