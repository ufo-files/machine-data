(GS) 5 orbs moving from east to west in straight line path, saw first 3 @ 2035 1 @ 2040 1 @ 2045

Was leaving my apartment to walk my dogs, when I got to the end of the building a light in the sky caught my eye. I saw three orb shaped objects approx ten feet in diameter and approx seventy feet in the air travel in a direction from east to west. I watched as they disappeared in the west. I then changed my direction and went to the rear of the building where I saw another orb move in the exact same manner and direction. I called my wife to come outside and look with me and she then witnessed the fifth and final orb move in the same manner and direction.
