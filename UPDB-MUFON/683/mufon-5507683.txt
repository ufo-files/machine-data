{nd} A mysterious light over the Sandia Mountains on September 22, 2013.

I saw a mysterious light over the Sandia Mountains on September 22, 2013. I observed this from Rio Rancho, NM. I first saw it at about 11 pm on the 21st, and thought it was just a bright star. But by 6 am I noticed it had become a lot brighter and could not be a star. It had not moved for over 6 hours. It was also witnessed by two other security officers. We used Google Sky to determine that the only thing close was Mars, but it was way too bright to be Mars. I had work to do and could not continue to observe it.
