A few UFOs are seen every night and at night only over the sea near St. Maarten in the Dutch West Indies. They are on the side of the island facing St. Barths and appear to be a few miles away.
St. Barths is 14 miles away and is a good marker for distance.
They appear as tri-colored flashing "Christmas lights" and go brighter and dimmer sometimes. Always white, green-blue and red rotating colors.

This report is updated because I heard a great description of what I see in this video:
Michael Schratt at the Secret Space Program Conference, 2014 San Mateo
 (at the 48 min. mark)
http://youtu.be/pFWza6LTMrY

He is talking about the, presumably man-made, "flying Dorito".

If you are in St. Maarten and on the Atlantic side of the Dutch half of the island look for these around 9 p.m. - 4 a.m. local time.
Look over the sea toward St. Barths (town lights show it).

They can be seen by the naked eye and look different from stars because of the 3 blinking colors. They move very slowly and are usually in a different 'spot' in the course of an hour or two. They don't move far but they move.
