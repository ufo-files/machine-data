{rjl} A trail of lights on a downward descent like a comet or massive arrow in formation

I witnessed a trail of lights of varying sizes and levels of brightness spaced apart but all in a perfectly linear formation. The lights when viewing the group of them had the appearance of some sort of an arrow with a larger lighted object in the front and a trail of smaller lights, leaving their own trails, in a perfect line behind the primary object. The travelled at a downward slope across the sky and they moved at a very smooth, moderate and consistent pace that looked effortless and quite beautiful. I could hear nothing unusual but there was some static on the music I was streaming via my cell phone.
