Approximately 9:18 p.m. EST ,I walked outside of my house and turned to the South Western night sky and noticed a very bright orange sphere hovering and then moving a little and then back to hover mode. There was an incoming plane in the spheres direction and as soon as the plane was close to it, it just dissapeared. I took video of it as best I could on my cell phones video. I thought at first it might be a chinese lantern or something but lanterns don't move like that. I thought about contacting the police but then I thought to myself, what if the police show up at my door and arrest me for prank calling the dept. I felt very weirded out by this. I surely hope someone else in the Florida Pinellas and Pasco county area saw this as well.
