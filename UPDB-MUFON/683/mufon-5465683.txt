On this beautiful afternoon of September 17, 2018, I was going to make pictures in old Quebec.

While passing by the parliament I noticed 2 people in a basket, they seemed to be doing the maintenance of the Tourny fountain. I found the scene inerrant enough to make some video images. So I set up a tripod.

It was only when I got home in front of the computer that I was surprised, at one point there was something that passes very quickly through the sky, a white ball that had the color of a cloud, its passage through the image only lasts a fraction of a second, so impossible to see on the spot with the naked eye.

In slow motion mode I saw the path of the white ball better, I noticed that it was not perfectly linear. 

My curiosity led me to do tests with FCPX filters, one of the filters gives the impression that the ball passes behind the clouds in the distance, if that is the case and well this thing should be huge. 

The ball came out of the image on the right.
