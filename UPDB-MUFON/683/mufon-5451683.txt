Sighting

I went outside to enjoy the twilight and noticed these lights. So I grabbed my phone and started taking video. There were two objects. The first hovered for a short time and took off after the second one arrived. The second one flew in quickly, hovered for a minute or so then expanded and took off. You will notice in the video that the UFO expands from a smaller white light to a expanded red light with a whiter light in the middle. I uploaded some screenshots taken from the video which show the un-zoomed light then zoomed in on the expanded red light.
