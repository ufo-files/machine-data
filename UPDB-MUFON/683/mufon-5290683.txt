Possible Fast Movers

I recently downloaded pictures from my camera to my computer. I had not done this in a long time. Among the pictures, I found several that I had taken in 2016. 

When I finally looked at the pictures, I noticed on a couple of pictures that the camera captured more than my eyes had seen at the time. We were driving on Missouri Hwy 367 heading East towards Alton, IL.  I saw 2 airplanes and 3 contrails and took a picture of them.  I take a lot of "sky" pictures. I sent the picture to a MUFON friend who suggested these could be "Fast Movers".

Details....the picture was taken through the windshield of the car. There was nothing on the windshield. On the top left of the picture, you will see reflections of the camera. In the middle of the picture, very lightly, you can see part of the camera too. The bottom left has part of a bridge. In the middle, you can see the 2 plane contrails that I saw at the time. In the bottom right, there are 2 round disks with some kind of trail. I did not see these when I took the picture.
