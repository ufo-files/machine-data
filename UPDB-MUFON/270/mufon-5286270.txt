Round/Oval, black object observed briefly, disappered behind cloud

My family was camping in Porcupine Mountain State park for two weeks spanning July 4th, 2017. We rented a pontoon boat from a near by business to spend the afternoon of the 4th on Lake Gogebic. The weather was nice, mostly clear with some puffy clouds. We had the lake to ourselves, as there were very few other boats on the lake. We spent the afternoon just puttering up and down the lake. At one point I leaned back and happened to look into the sky and saw a small all black round/oval object moving around under the puffy clouds, then it curved up and around behind a cloud. I did not see it again. I had asked my brother in law if he had happened to see it, but unfortunately he did not. He thought maybe it could be a drone, which I consider a possibility, however the height seemed to be way above the normal operating height of a drone.
