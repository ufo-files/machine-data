{jpg} Sighted a disc shaped object with large red light in the center and 2 white lights to either side moving east toward me while walking my dog.

I left my house to walk my dog on 12-30-2017 at 8:25pm walking west, I usually look to the sky's for meteor showers, shooting stars. I saw at about a 45 degree angle on the horizon between myself and South Mountain to the west an unusually bright and large red light with two smaller white lights to either side forming a disc shape. I stopped and observed it moving to the east toward me, I decided to call my daughter to see if she could see from her location east of me a few miles (she couldn't) I then observed the object stop and hover in place then start moving south. I attempted to take video but was unable to open video mode only a time lapse photo was taken (will need to review). My dog was getting agitated and whining then the disc went from being large and visible to accelerating to the southwest at such a rate of speed it went to a pinpoint and gone. I'm retired Air Force and that was not an aircraft from this earth, I have never seen anything like it in my life before, truly amazing. My dog seems to be bit lethargic after getting home and I have a very bad headache, not sure what to make of that as of yet.
