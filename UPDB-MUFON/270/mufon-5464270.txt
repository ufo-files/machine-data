Outside my house in Pittsburgh. Looked up. Saw three white orbiting shapes, very high up, white, circular, with what appeared like a blur around the entire periphery like how it looks when a helicopter is in motion and its a blur. At first two were very close together, almost like they were about to touch. Then they didnt and went in different directions. I walked a little while and looked up and they were still there. Then they went away.
