In 2013 my girlfriend (at he time), her family, and myself went to the pueblo reservoir for her birthday. We spent 3 days there and on our last night there I saw multiple crafts hovering around and on the reservoir as well as around our camping grounds. There were bright flashing (pulsating) with humanoids standing on the water, pot a potty, and picnic tables in the area. I sat and watched them watching me for about an hour before I went to sleep.
