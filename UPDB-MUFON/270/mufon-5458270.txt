{jpg} Witnessed slow moving bright Orb or seemingly circular object that disappeared into space quickly

My friend and I were sitting outside on a very clear night, when we were both in disbelief as we watched a bright shining, slow moving object moving across the sky. As we live by the airport we often see planes, even satelites and whatever this was, it was no plane or known man made craft. I began filming the unknown object, that was similar to a miniature full moon, to the best of my ability using my phone, we watched as it moved across the sky West to East for about a minute. Then without making a noise it vanished into space and was gone, almost like a shooting star, in a second or less it was no longer visible in the clear dark sky. I am unsure if the object was in fact circular or if we were only seeing the light it emitted. I have always been a skeptic of UFO accounts, but now after this my eyes are open. It was flying at a similar altitude as a plane then shot up into space at a 45 degree angle and appeared further away than a star to the point it was no longer visible. I think this is the second time witnessing this object, but the first time I thought it was my perception and I discounted the event to some rational explanation. Now after this I am not alone and WE ARE NOT ALONE.
Side note: Cigar shaped object with similar flight behavior witnessed by at least one other person during the daylight. Same day at 3PM same exact same area code. Coincidence?
