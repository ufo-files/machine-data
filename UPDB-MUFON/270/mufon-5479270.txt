Erratically moving star-like light quickly changed direction, then shot across the sky

I was attending an open-air Church service at Bracken Ridge, Brisbane on Christmas Eve. My father and I observed a light in the sky that looked exactly like a star. However, this star moved erratically, moving in many different directions, so that its movement simply could not be attributed to anything like a plane, helicopter or satellite. There were no other vehicles in the sky at the time. After moving around erratically for probably 10 seconds, the 'star' very quickly shot straight across the sky and disappeared from sight. My father and I both agreed this was a UFO. I have observed a very similar star-like UFO with the same movement years before in the same area.
