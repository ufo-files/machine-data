5 objects in the sky

1814 on 1/8/2011. I had just pulled into my driveway and glanced towards the southwestern sky, about 200 degrees magnetic. I saw 5 round objects in the sky flying at a few thousand feet. They were following eachother and were far too close for aircraft arriving or departing Portland Jetport.  None had appropriate lighting for an aircraft and all were moving at a high rate of speed due south east towards Biddeford/Saco.  Their direction of travel was not an apparent straight line, but more like left and right. I lost sight of them when they went behind the clouds. It was too dark for my cell phone camera to record them
