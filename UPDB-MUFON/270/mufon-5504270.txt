{cak} Silver object flying approx 200-300 mph, had no wings

I was watching a contrail being made in a N/S direction, but I could not see the plane. making it. All of a sudden the forward progress of the contrail just stopped. I looked ahead of it to see if the airplane was visible, it wasnt. So I slowly scanned the sky turning back to where I first observed the contrail, I then observed something very shiney flying N/S, It's flight path would be about 200 yds due West of where the contrail was observed.

The interesting thing about this object is that at first I thought it was an airplane, but there wasnt any wings. I asked my wife to take a look and she confirmed it did not have any wings and that she didnt know what it was. I followed the flight path of it going in a Southerly direction until I could not see it any more. I was sort of dumb founded. I thought to myself, did I just see a UFO ?
