{tjd} Dark Boomerang shaped object no lights, no sound, traveled N to S until behind tree line.

Friend went outside to smoke, immediately called me outside. Looked up in the sky to see a very large dark boomerang shaped object gliding from North to south in the night sky. Was very slow moving, almost as a bird gliding until I could no longer see it due to trees. No sound, completely quiet, no lights. It was curved or round on the front and shaped like what I can only describe as a boomerang. It blended in with the black of the sky, but had some lighter coloring along the front edge. They were not lights, but maybe reflecting. I have tried to think of anything known that it could have been, and came up empty. Have never seen anything like it before.
