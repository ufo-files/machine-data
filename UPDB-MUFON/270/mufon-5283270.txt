Hovering light over Deming NM

I'm a delivery driver in the southern new Mexico region. over time you tend to see strange things on the road. but this time. I was making a delivery, driving from Las cruces to Deming. on the mountains just before you get to Deming. I saw what i thought was a strange looking radio or flight tower. but as i was passing the mountains i realized the object wasn't moving with the mountains as i passed. i got all the way to Deming and as i got into town i watched the whole time and it was a stationary large chrome object with no attachments, no sound or anything. a few other people where stopped looking at it.
