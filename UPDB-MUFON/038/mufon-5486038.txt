{nd} I saw a bell shaped object with white,yellow,blue,green,and red lights hovering and then shot off.

I was with my older sister driving towards Bellefonte, Pa. we were going to pick up my other sister from after school activity. We went around a turn at the outskirts of Axeman on state route 144 about 1.5 miles outside of Bellefonte. We drove up to 2 other cars that were stopped on the road. 4 or 5 people were out looking at the UFO. We got out and watched it for around a minute or so. I thought that it looked like a Mercury space capsule with lights. The object was just over the tree line right in front of us. It was bell shaped tilted to the left. It was spinning very fast. the bottom had a row of white to yellow lights. The top had red,blue,yellow,and green lights. It hovered for awhile then it did a zig zag movement and shot out in a western direction. It moved at speeds that did not exist in any airplane book I read. I did not talk to the other people that were there nor did I know them. I did remember that it looked funny with the others could not take their eyes off of it. 20 years later I was talking about this incident with my sister who is 12 years my elder, and she said I was lying, it did not happen, I did not see anything. I was 10 years old.
