{cak} Star like object in daylight almost overhead

Had just moved into a new apartment complex in Bothell, WA. The place was still under construction and this sighting took place during the afternoon on a Friday. We were in the last occupied building that was complete and the sighting lasted well over the weekend. My wife and I saw a blue sphere gliding I would guess about 5 to 700 ft above our heads heading west out over the Sound. Then we were visited by four greenish brown orbs above the tree tops. I saw what resembled a Christmas ornament that goes on top of the tree, pure silver to chrome in color. The ship that I am uploading I like to call the mother ship because while we witnessed all the orbs and others she stayed high above us and at about a 75 degree angle to our sight the whole time. My wife prior to this had not witnessed anything except what I showed her on video. Talk about excited. That weekend really changed our lives because we feel they put on a show for us. We had just arrived in Bothell for me to start a new job in Richmond and it was their way of welcoming us to Washington.
