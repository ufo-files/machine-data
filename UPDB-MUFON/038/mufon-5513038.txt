(GS) Blue light arcing a short distance and then disappeared.

My husband & I were taking my mother to Sea-Tac Airport at 5:30 in the morning of December 18, 2012. I was driving north on 1-5 and looked towards my left as we approached the Kent area and saw a blue flash of light that arced in a northerly direction and then disappeared. I would estimate that this object was southeast of the airport.
