I was driving to work and saw this thing to my left.

I was driving to work about 6:30am. I saw these very bright lights in the sky to my left. The white ones were much brighter than an airplane. I then saw red lights and yellow lights. They were blinking off an on in a random manner. I could not make out the shape of what this thing was. But it was not an airplane because my car got beside it and then passed it. There was no sound. Other cars around me were slowing down--so I know they could see it. I stopped at a red light and "stared" at it as hard as I could. It was just hovering and bright white lights going off on, then yellow, then red. I had the distinct feeling that I was being watched or observed. I lost sight of it by driving on into work. It was creepy. I felt uncomfortable all day....like a weird feeling. I felt like I was in a fog or something.
