{rjl} Was taking look moon photos and saw star change colors and pattern.

I was on my balcony taking pictures of the Blood Moon with my Canon 7DII, 100-400mmLII with tripod. I saw a bright star to the bottom right of the moon and was using it to be in the photo composition. I then notice it started flickering and it changed from white to blue. I then directed my camera to it and at first it had white swirls on the blue then it had changed to a face. I posted the images on Facebook and the photo of the face was pixeliized. I reposted it then it was scrambled again. I tried to find the pictures on my hard drive and they are now missing. Luckily I had downloaded them to my phone. The pictures are in order.
