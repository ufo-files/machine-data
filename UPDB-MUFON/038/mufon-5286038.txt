Unknown erratic moving blue object in S/SW sky above Yamhill County-Seen from Newberg, OR

I noticed a blue pulsing light when I was out at 10:00 giving our dogs a potty break. I often look into the night sky. Tonight, I noticed the blue object at about 20 degrees in the southern sky. I was looking to the left of my house at the time. As I watched, it darted around in a small area there. Then, it went an incredible distance, high above me, north towards me at about 70 degrees. Next, it stopped and went back to its original location, lower in the horizon and was gone. This last action was done very fast.  I called my wife out to watch, but by the time she got out, it had disappeared. I continued to look for it over the next 20 minutes, but I did not see it return.
