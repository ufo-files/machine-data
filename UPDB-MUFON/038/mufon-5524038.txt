Very red light ( like the intensity of a laser) over our house pulsating and moving extremely slowly before disappearing.

I was outside with my son and we noticed a red light in the sky over our house. It was too large to be a star or planet and the color was spectacularly red! I grabbed my video camera and recorded it for several minutes- the camera was clearly showing it recording however the tape is blank with the exception of a few seconds Wich is puzzling. Observed it moving very slowly in a north or northeast direction. Then after pulsating more with a very large bright one it disappeared
