Flashes of light moved around randomly in a small area close to the stars Acrab and Jabbah. In the Southern sky 35 degrees above horizon.  This same exact incident repeated itself when I went skywatching again the next night on 7/21/17 @ 10:30. First nite observation flashes in area above ( north when looking south) of star Jabbah and following nite to the west of star Acrab.
