i was 15yrs. old on my way home from a friend house,in 1984,i was riding my mopad home and i was coming up rawsonville rd. heading home.when my bike just cut off i look down to see what was going on and i tryed to start it while i was still moving but it wouldnt start.then i notice all these cars pulled over on the side of the road and as i looked at the cars i stopped and looked all the way up till i noticed a dark very hugh disk shape object hoovering over the street light about 15ft. above the street light with different color lights going around it.blocking out the sunset and casing a shadow over the entire road right over rawsonville rd. and huron river drive,right where it turn into texttile rd.i have never been more afraid in my life. i watched it for a couple minutes and then it took off toward the east so fast i couldnt believe it. i mean as quick as you could blink your eye it was gone...i notice a trail fell off what look like spakle falling to ground but they landed over the woods that was there 30yrs. a go now there is some auto shop.the area is different now but after the ufo left my bike started right up i spoke with a few people who was pulled over but never got a name or anything.i hurried home and told my mother who laughed at me along with my friends.i was so angry i called belleville police station that day and spoke to some lady officer,i told her what i saw and she told me she been getting lots of calls tonight and can tell me what i saw was a helicopter.i was so upset i never talked about it again.
