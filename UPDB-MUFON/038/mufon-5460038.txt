{rjl} Very loud sounding, like a helicopter. Bright white light stationary approximately 200 ft. In a valley directly behind my home. Quickly disappeared.

I was awoken in the middle of the night because of an extremely loud helicopter or plane engine sound. I got up and looked out my bedroom patio door. I saw a very bright white light that was intermittently flashing. It was a large lighted area. I saw no shape, the lights were very bright. I live on a hill with a valley out my back patio. The bright light shown in the valley, approximately 100-200 ft from my patio door. The loud jet sound continued & suddenly it was gone. I can’t recall how it moved away.
