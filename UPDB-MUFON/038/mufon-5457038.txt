{rjl} Bullet shaped objects in flight

4-5 pulsating bullet shaped lights flying in a perfectly straight line from one side of the sky to the other - they moved too slowly to be a meteor but too fast, straight, and silent to be an aircraft. They were spaced oddly, some closer together, some farther apart, but their spacing was maintained the entirety of their flight path until I lost sight (dipped below tree cover). Totally silent. Entire family witnessed the event or I would’ve thought I was imagining things.
