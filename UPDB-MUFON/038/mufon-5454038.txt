{jpg} 2 night event and 1 day event over 3 total days

The first 2 nightly events began on April 7, 2020 and continued the next night. On the first night I observed a star-like object to the west of my apartment. It caught my attention because it seemed to be flashing more than an average star twinkles. It remained stationary for as long as I watched it. I was on my back porch smoking a cigarette at the time and when I finished, I went back inside. At that time, I considered that it could have been an asteroid or another object spinning in space which seemed to explain the blinking and why it was stationary. Then, on April 8, 2020 my wife went outside to smoke a cigarette at about 0030 and she saw an object. She beckoned me outside to look at it. This time, there were 2 objects to the north-west. The second object flew away before I was able to get it on video. The first object was bigger and had the same characteristics as the object I saw on the 7th. It was flashing more than a star twinkles. This time, it also appeared to be drifting to the north-west. I was able to capture a picture and a video. We observed the object for about 20 minutes before we went inside to go to bed. It was still in the sky when we went inside. The next day, April 9, 2020, I saw a cylindrical object in the same part of the sky, at 1130, moving from the north-west to the south-west. I took a photo of it. My wife also saw the object. At first we thought it was a plane. However, I noted it wasn't making a sound and it wasn't leaving a contrail. 10 minutes after that object was out of viewing range, a plane flew by on the same course. I noted to my wife that we could hear the plane and it was leaving a contrail. I took a picture of the plane as reference.
