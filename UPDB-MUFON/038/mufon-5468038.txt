{jpg} Red light caught my eye as I was gazing up b4 getting into vehicle

At approximately 1040pm (central time) I was gazing up at the sky before getting into my truck to go to work for the night. I saw a red blinking light slowly floating downward towards my lilac bush. My first thought is "cool...a lightning bug"....then it abruptly shot straight up. Very quickly and very high. Its light blinking as erratically as its movement in the sky before it took off in a northwest direction where I lost site of it due to my really tall tree in my yard. My hands were full so I couldn't get to my phone fast enough to take a video. The whole incident lasted about a minute. I did happen to have a wooden dowel rod in my hand that I used as a pointer to help me track it in the sky as I was yelling for my husband to "come check this $!#& out". He was locking up his shop (located on the other side of the tree where I lost site)...sadly, he saw nothing. I know it wasn't a conventional aircraft because they don't blink or move the way this light was. I also know it wasn't a lightning bug...for the same reason and they glow orange in my area.
I would love to know what it was that I saw and will be making more time for sky gazing moving forward.I see cool atmospheric things regularly, but this one has me stumped.
