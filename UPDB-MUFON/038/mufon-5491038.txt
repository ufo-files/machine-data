I am a former U.S Army Indirect Fire Infantryman I have two suspected sightings to report on 2 different dates. The first sighting was on the first date. I was traveling in my car and I witnessed what looked like a firework or a flare It may of been a meteorite, It was heading on a horizontal path and I saw it for a few seconds before it went over the treeline it was headed from right to left and had a shimmering trail like a firework. The second date was July 12th 2015 on or around this date. I witnessed a star like object that was high altitude and possibly in orbit that looked like a moving star it moved for about a inch of my fingers with ( holding my hand in the sky ) and it disappeared. It was nright and looked like star.
