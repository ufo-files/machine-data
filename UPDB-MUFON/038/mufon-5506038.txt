{rjl} Bright light followed by paralysis and nonverbal communication. Spirit floated through rooftop.

I was lying in bed sleeping. I was awakened by an intense light outside my window. I sensed an intelligent presence that wanted to communicate with me. I wanted to communicate with the presence. My body became paralyzed and felt cold. I felt my spirit leave my body and ascend through the rooftop. I have no recollection of what happened beyond the rooftop. Later I felt my spirit being returned to my body. I felt the presence of two observers that disappeared. When I awoke I felt disoriented. I sat up and thought to myself, "I remember nothing." When I put my feet on the floor I felt like the force of a hundred suns had landed on my head. I placed my hands on my head and screamed, "Jesus Christ!" I had never experienced such a headache! I was amazed that I could stand. On my skull there were two grooves that had not been there the day before. I felt like a had literally been through a wringer.
The next day, my coworkers asked me if something was wrong. I said no. They asked me if I had ever seen a UFO. I said no. I didn't believe in UFOs. My neighbors told me that they had heard screams from my apartment and wanted to know if anything was wrong. I said no. Following this experience I became more withdrawn and depressed than usual.
