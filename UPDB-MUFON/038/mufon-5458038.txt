{rjl} Glowing Red Triangle flew right up the beach

My newlywed wife and I were stargazing on a sand dune with a clear sky when we noticed a brightly glowing red triangle come flying up the beach and continued over us. It looked slightly longer than wide and it was glowing bright red all over like a charcoal briquette or like a steel object heated to glowing by a blowtorch. It flew smoothly at maybe 1000 to 3000 feet like it was either observing or just flying directly somewhere. I jumped to my feet in shock and awe and asked her if she was seeing it and she was. It flew out of sight within maybe a minute so it seemed like it was going about as fast as a prop airplane would at 1000-2000 feet (but no sound)which made me believe it was 30-40 feet long. There were no drones in 1989 and it glowed brightly all over so how would you fake that exactly? (and why fake it for a few people to see it fly down the beach?). I was shaken to my core thinking "So it's true-they are here and they're real! but she said "ah-it's probably just some military plane" and so I gradually accepted her view and even forgot about it. Years later after I got to know how closed-minded she is, I realized she was wrong. I have had two more sightings since that day and I know we are not alone.
