{dr}At approximately 9:30 pm last night 5/28 at my home in Chandler, ten orange orbs.

Last night, 5/28 at about 9:30, I was sitting outside at my home in Chandler Arizona. I was facing south. An orange light appeared to " be turned on" near the backside of south mountain right at the Estrella Stargate. There were about ten of them. They appeared one at a time, silently. They seemed to travel towards me and then to the west . I gave them the peace / victory sign and said " I love you". I don't know who they are but I wanted to be friendly. They looked like they were within a few miles. They had to have crossed the I10 also. I did not take pictures, I figured it would be on the news, still waiting.
