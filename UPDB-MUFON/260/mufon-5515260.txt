{nd} 4 lights traveling at a very high, higher than planes travel, slowly moving

My girls were outside enjoying dinner when they looked up and saw 4 lights moving slow across the sky and huvering at some points. Girls ran indoors to get myself, my father and brother. We all saw the 4 lighted objects moving thru the sky, too high to be planes or any other helicopter. Lights were pulsating across the bottom of the aircraft. My father who worked for an aircraft company knows that this was not an airplane.
