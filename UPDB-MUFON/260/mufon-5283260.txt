Black stationary object observed against dusk sky.

Observation took place at Harquahala Mountain trail head, at dusk. While scanning the western horizon, with binoculars, a black dot was noted in the sky. The object did not reflect light or emit light. It was flat black. Three individuals observed the object through binoculars. The object was stationary. After five to ten minutes the object was gone. Several photos were taken using a cell phone through the binoculars.
