Grey object first sighted east of Cassiopia I originally thought it was a satellite.

I was out on my patio which faces north and first saw a grey streak with an elliptical path from east to west at mid horizon. Then about 30 seconds later I saw another object which I first was a satellite which was only about as half bright as the stars. The object was first sighted to the right of Cassiopia but then turned in a southerly direction and then made a very tight 360 degree turn to the right and then flashed off to the west on approximately the same trajectory as the first object. There was no sound and I thought the objects were quite high.
