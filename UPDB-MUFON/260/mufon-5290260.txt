Approx 15-20 orbs within thin overcast making a perfect figure 8 over and over, Track did not change

I am writing this because I noticed another report from CT describing same thing. About 430 or 5 am on Jan 6th, I noticed a bright light in the sky while looking out my balcony door. I went out and realized it was just an airplane. Then I noticed lights in the southern sky just inside a thin overcast moving very rapidly in a perfect figure 8 pattern. The lights were equidistant, visually about an inch apart. The exact same pattern continued for several minutes and I estimated the pattern covered about a mile if projected on the ground. I'm a former USAF fighter pilot. These were not airplanes. The speed and tightness of the figure 8 couldn't have been made by an aircraft. No sound. Due to the haze layer, I could not see the shape/structure of the orbs.
