{rjl} Two Bright Orbs in Eastern and Northeastern Sky over Scottsdale, AZ

I took video on the evening of April 10th, 2019 of two seemingly stationary yet quite bright round objects in the Eastern and Northeastern skies above Scottsdale, AZ. They both were at about a 45 degree altitude from the horizon, but separated from each other by about 40-50 degrees. The sighting was at about 7 p.m to 7:15 p.m. local time.

There were no other stars out at all at that time, and only the moon was visible high in the Western sky in regards to other celestial objects. Again--No planets nor stars were out yet, but shortly after taking the video, the typical dusk-emerging objects began to show themselves. I am a frequent observer of the skies at dawn, dusk and at night—these two bright objects didn’t correspond to any planets or other objects with which I am familiar. Once the night sky started to take shape, these two objects slowly faded from view.

I found nothing online of any other accounts of a similar nature at that time, on that day; and I didn’t see any SpaceX or otherwise launch events that may have explained the objects. I have two videos of the objects, but the file size is quite large. If someone wishes to see the video, I'd be happy to send it, but for now, the .mov files are over 100MB, which would take some work to get uploaded on my home computer.
