{tjd} UFO Over Walmart

On Friday, 11/23/2018 at 5:43 a.m., I saw what appeared to be a large white orb hovering high above the ground. The object didn't move! It just sat there! It was above the Walmart in Commack, NY and I observed it for an extended period of time. It appeared star-like but was WAY bigger than any other star or planet in the sky. As an astronomer and aircraft aficionado, I can tell you that this was not anything I have previously seen. I also thought it may have been a helicopter but it was silent. There were no effects that I was aware of but there were other witnesses.
