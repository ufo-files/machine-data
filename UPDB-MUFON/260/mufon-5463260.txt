Flashing lights and coloured objects observed near the moon

I was in my back garden taking photos of the moon as i do quite often. As i zoomed into the moon i noticed flashing lights close to it. I took many photos and about eight videos over about five hours. Some videos have just a few flashing lights but one has about fifty flashing lights. These are just a few of the photos of the coloured objects i took. I was amazed how good they were when i put them onto my computer. In the weeks leading up to this i took photos of red and blue lights on the shaded part of the moon.(only cropped altered light and contrast to see them better).
