I was sitting on my back porch at approximately 9 PM There are a thicket of trees to my right. I was looking to my right when I saw a medium grayish brown elongated object, at least 300 feet long, drift by just below the tree line. It took 3-5 minutes to drift by over several houses & then passed by. It had a line of white & orange lights running along the side. I have observed two other dissimilar objects at different times & dates with different descriptions. I am right next door to Bucks County. When I first saw the object my jaw dropped & I just sat there motionless. Unfortunately, I don't have a camera so I could not take a picture.
