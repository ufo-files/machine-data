Cigar / Long thin craft sighted in Allyn WA

Witnessed a long thin cigar shape / thin cylinder / bar shaped craft fly directly overhead at 4:50 AM. The whole thing was bathed in very bright white light but appeared to have round slightly lighter areas down the length of the craft. Appeared to be at a very high elevation meaning this thing would have to be extremely large. No blinking lights and in my opinion far to large to be any type of airplane. Appeared very solid like a bar of bright light and did not look anything like a meteor / falling star. Took about 30 seconds to travel from horizon to horizon. Just enough time to pound on the window and wake the wife so she could catch a glimpse. Only caught a poor picture of it as it was nearing the horizon. It does not show the actual shape well at all due to the location when photographed.
