4 reddish orange objects appearing and disappearing in the sky around midnight between Nothing, AZ and Wikieup, AZ

Driving to Las Vegas from Phoenix on I-93 and a little time after passing Nothing, AZ, me and my girlfriend were looking to the west and saw a stationary reddish orange diamondish looking light under the moon and then disappear, a few seconds later it reappeared with another light and stayed there for about 20 seconds and then disappeared and soon after three more appeared to the left of the moon and one was off to the side and zoomed over to the other three and then all of them disappeared at once and never came back. This all took place within a span of about 2 minutes or so around 12:30 at night. We have never seen any kind of light or object that looked like this in the sky before. It was exhilarating and was something we will never forget!
