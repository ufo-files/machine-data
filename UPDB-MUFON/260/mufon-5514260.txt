(GS) Saw two objects flying east to west swaying left to right emmitting rainbow colors

While I was sitting next to my goat barn stargazing I noticed a light moving from west to east..It was cycling the colors of the light spectrum..About one or two seconds repeating these colors..As it was traveling East it was going in a non-linear fashion swaying left to right..As I kept watching something strange occurred..The object stopped right next to a cloud for about 10 seconds then moved east again then stopped by another cloud then continued going east..Before it went out of view another identical object came from the west where I saw the first..The second object did not stop but did waver left to right same as the first object as it flew..When I first saw the objects I thought they were airplanes but realized they were not because of the colors they were emmitting and the way the first object stopped by two clouds..I had a weird feeling these things were alive because of the way they flew and checking out the clouds as if it were looking for something..Like a fox stopping by every bush looking for food..I lost sight of both objects as they continued on their easterly course...Strange indeed....
