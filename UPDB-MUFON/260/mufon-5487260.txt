{nd} IN THE SUMMER OF 2011, WHILE FISHING ON THE RIVER AT TWILIGHT, I SAW A DARK TRIANGE RISE FROM THE TREE TOPS.

FILLED OUT AT MUFON HQ, WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18809, ON 11/11/2015
------------------------------------------------------------------------
IN THE SUMMER OF 2011, WHILE FISHING ON THE LOCHLOCHNEE RIVER AT TWILIGHT, ABOUT 8:30PM, I SAW A DARK TRIANGLE RISE FROM THE TREE TOPS. I WAS FACING NORTH WHEN I SAW IT. THE TRIANGLE HAD NO LIGHTS AND MADE NO SOUND. I WATCHED IT RISE STRAIGHT UP FOR 4 OR 5 MINUTES. IT HOVERED REALLY HIGH UP AND THEN SOME WHITE LIGHTS CAME ON. AFTER A MINUTE OR TWO I SAW A FLASH OF LIGHT AND THE CRAFT SHOT STRAIGHT UP, MAKE A RIGHT TURN LEAVING A TRAIL OF LIGHTS BEHIND IT.
I FELT I SHOULD REPORT THIS AFTER I SAW A ARTICLE IN THE TALLAHASSEE DEMOCRAT NEWSPAPER DESCRIBING WHAT I SAW.
