Floated up and down in wave like pattern parallel with horizon

I woke up and went to the bathroom and when I was done I walked to the sink to wash my hands. The sink is closer to the window and on my way there I noticed a light that I thought may be ball lightning, moving higher than the tree line at the top of a mountain about a mile away. When I first spotted it the object was already moving slowly in a wave-like pattern just above the tree line. It glowed very light blue, almost white and was very bright. It was oval shaped, I could not make out any details because of the brightness. I felt my hands go numb and I was very scared, almost terrified. I might have held my breath for the duration of the event. I just stood about a foot away from the window watching in awe, frozen on the spot. I lost sight of the object when it spontaneously and instantly sped up to a very high rate of speed and left a line of light the same colour and was gone in a fraction of a second. My information is not real because I do not wish to be contacted, but my siting is real and I wished to report it but don't want to be accused of being insane.
