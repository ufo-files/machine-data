A Triangle sighting over Walmart

7/7/08 10:05pm-ish 
A Triangle sighting... Tampa Palms (North Tampa) Florida on Bruce B. Downs Super Walmart.

I was walking out of WALMART, and I saw a bubble... so I thought. At first it seemed to be about 25 feet off the ground and about 50' ahead of me. I kept walking toward it to where my car was... and as I stared at the bubble I wondered "how would ONE lone bubble survive out here too long? I walked... expecting to see it burst any second. It didn't. As I watched it... I then saw it wasn't a bubble at all as it started to get almost above me. I stood next to the car. 

It had been cloudy and drizzy... and there was a low canopy of clouds. I noticed it had two round looking lights and a lighter third. Nothing blinking. The lights were very clear... not like you'd expect headlights on a plane. I listened for sound... and heard nothing. Then I suspected I was seeing something unusual. I then checked... and TRUE nothing blinking... and watched and made study that the lights were not REAL bright but were very pronounced... without halo or atmospheric glaring quality. The blueish roun lights seem to be directional... as like laser light travels, defined. Then as it started to get over Walmart... and by then I knew it was not a plane or chopper. I hoped it was a Black Triangle and I watched to see SOMETHING that would reveal it. Then... it slowed down very gradually... and before it had a chance to STOP... it ascended straight up. I appeared to have traveled fast UP than it had since I spotted it. As it moved up, the position of the lights stayed fixed... meaning that it didn't turn nose up to ascend. It went up what looked like around 1000' until it pierced the cloud cove and disappeared. No noise.I detected no SHAPE to it... but the lights suggested a triangle. A plane climbing like that would have made noise. This thing moved silent and very SMOOTHLY. It matched the Triangle video taped at Zephyrhills a couple months ago... VERY close... minus the blinking read light. I'm glad I saw it... because now I know they are real. I immediately text messaged a friend to time stamp the sighting.
