{jpg} Red orbs hit with spotlight. Three orbs formed a triangle. Shut off electricity. Oil unit howled and moaned and pipes rattled under the ground.

Me, my son, and nephew were camping outside on a clear night. I bet them I could get the attention of a UFO. They did not believe me so we went into the back yard area. We live in oil fields in North West rural Gallatin County. I did see three objects, red/orange in color in a triangle. They went dark or dim and dissapeared. I seen one fly back across tree tops, behind the pond and oil platform. I hit it with a spotlight and it lit up and got bright and seemed to get closer. It then went dim. My oldest son got scared and ran inside screaming. Two more came out behind it making no sound. Orange red and the dimmed. My nephew stood there with me scared and shaking. My spotlight was out at this point. Orbs dimmed and dissapeared still making no sound. I caught a glimpse of one moving quick to the east. The electricity then went out, and the oil rig lost power. It made a howling, screeching sound, and the oil pipes or pipeline under the ground rattled like an earthquake for a few minutes. Power came back on after ten minutes. I see these orbs out here on any given night. Some are fast movers that zig zag, and sometimes they move like they are on a solid trajectory. Always in threes it seems like and like to form triangles.
