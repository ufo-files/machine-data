stationary ball of light

My wife and I were eating dinner near the window, which overlooks the Tualatin Valley, and suddenly she turns to me and says, "Whoa! What is that?". As I turn and looked, I saw what appeared to be a perfectly spherical ball of bright white light, hovering stationary approximately 15 to 25 degrees above the southern horizon. The ball was 3-4 times larger than a bright star in diameter, and appeared to be perfectly stationary. The sphere hovered in place for about three minutes, remaining mostly bright white, although it would briefly transition to a yellow/amber color, before switching back to bright white. After about three minutes, the ball of light suddenly dimmed in intensity to approximately 1/4th of it's original brightness. At this point, the object appeared to drift slowly west for 45 seconds before it suddenly disappeared. No remnant of the ball of light was seen again after this. The sky was completely overcast, so what we saw was below the cloud deck. The described object appeared to be hovering over the Beaverton, Tigard area, possibly a little further south and/or west. The sphere was well defined and no FAA/Aeronautical lights could be seen by either witness. No sound was heard from the object either. Neither witness had previously seen anything like this in the sky before. The sudden disappearance of the brilliant ball of light was abrupt and perplexing. All of these events were observed before it got dark at 7:53 PM PST from the west hills of Portland, OR.
