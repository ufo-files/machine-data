When we were 14 years old, my friend and I both saw a triangular shaped craft hovering over the Al State Park fishing Pier. We were sitting on the grass/sand line about 25-30 yards to the West of the pier. The craft was large- the size of a small building. It was elevated a couple hundred feet above the pier. To me it was hovering very low, considering the size of the craft. The craft hovered for about 30 seconds. We watched the lights (red,orange,and white) underneath blink randomly. The craft never made a sound. Suddenly, the craft elevated and took off to the East , following the coast line. It disappeared in almost a blink of the eye. I being 14 at the time wasn’t familiar with aircraft and their abilities. I called it a stealth bomber because it is the closest shape description I could give at the time. It wasn’t until years later I was telling the story to a more savvy aircraft enthusiast. At that moment I realized it wasn’t a stealth bomber, it was an aircraft that could not be explained. 
To add to the experience, the day after the sighting, I had developed huge red circles on my legs. A Dr said I had been attacked by sand fleas. I believed the Dr because the circles itched and it made sense because I had been sitting in the sand. The bites/marks went away after about a month- seemingly long for a flea bite to exist. They were large like a silver dollar large. I have never experienced a reaction to an insect bite like that since.
