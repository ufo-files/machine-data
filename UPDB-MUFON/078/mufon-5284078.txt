Amazing light show traveling at unbelievable speed.

A few years ago myself, and two other observed what appeared to be a line of planes traveling at a very fast rate. All we could see was lights. After traveling in a straight line they began to form a huge circle and hovered for a couple mins. They then broke up and the lights disappeared all but two. The two lights remaining soared the sky at a unbelievable rate of speed going back and fourth. The lights eventually disappeared. We were all amazed at what we were witnessing! It was def nothing we’d ever seen before!
