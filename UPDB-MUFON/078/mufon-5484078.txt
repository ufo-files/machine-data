On April 6, 2016, at 5:53am in downtown Cleveland, Ohio, I was walking from the parking garage over to my work and I noticed some lights in the sky. I looked up facing west and I saw an Unidentified flying object hovering in the sky. When I first saw it I thought it was a helicopter or something, but I kept watching and it never moved. Then a few minutes into the recording, an airplane flew past and it still didn1t move and I could tell then that it wasn1t a helicopter or plane. It was large and black with red and white lights. The red light was blinking continuously. I was recording for approximately 11 minutes and 23 seconds and it never moved. A little while after it headed west in a straight formation until it wasn1t visible any longer. I was baffled and surprised. I was wondering what it could have been. There were others looking in disbelief also wondering what it was.
