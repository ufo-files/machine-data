{cak} I was cleaning my roof and saw a shiny disc going east and then a second one joined it from above.

We have a flat roof on one section of our house and I go up weekly to clear off the acorns and leaves, twigs from our side yard oak tree, at this time of year.It was about 6:00 pm as I was looking up at my Citizens Band antenna to make sure the top was still clearing the low branches of our oak tree, when I noticed a shiny disc shape in the northwest traveling quickly to the east, from my vantage point.( It was over the city of South Bend,it seemed.) I see many commercial jets and helicopters flying over weekly and monthly but this object was different. The shiny disc had no tail section that I could see at its far distance.It was reflecting sunlight off its silvery body. It was travelling quite fast from the west and, from northwest of me to northeast of me. It was moving earthward during its course at a very slight angle. Then , I was surprised to see a second shiny disc object approaching the first one from above and behind . I thought maybe it was 2 fighter jets joining up or on a close collision course. Then, the second object leveled off beside and slightly above the first disc as they both continued eastward parallel to each other and disappeared beyond and behind the trees lining my high bank side of the St. Joseph river.I am a freelance photographer and only wish I had taken my video camera on the roof this afternoon.I would have had great video with my 72X power zoon capabilities. Of course, I normally wouldn't have used it while cleaning my roof off. That's how it happened..... and all in about 1 minute of observation.
