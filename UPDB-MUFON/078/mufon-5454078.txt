{jpg} Partial Spherical Shaped Object seen in early morning, glowed orange, no other discernable features

While commuting in to work in Fresno I was looking westward I saw something in the sky that looks like the crest of the sun through clouds. It had been raining all night and into the early morning. The object itself appeared it would have been spherical if the clouds had not been there blocking the other part of it, what I could see it looked like the sun cresting over a hill, or a sphere with the upper 1/3rd of it being seen, this thing radiated a yellow-orange light, and had no visible structural features. It slowly faded out of light until I could not see any of it anymore. Also none of the light seemed to reflect off any of the clouds around or behind it. The entire sighting lasted approximately 10 seconds. I was doing approximately 65 miles an hour and I doubt the camera on my iPhone would have picked it up adequately.
