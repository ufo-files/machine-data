{jpg} Multi colored star

On 5Aug17 at about 0100-0130 hours, in Yellowstone National Park, Mammoth Hot Springs campground.

Looking at stars through binoculars, observed a star sized object, at about a ten o'clock angle, to the NW.

It was changing colors, and sometimes wobbling, but like the stars near it, to far to focus on. I went back and forth between the object and a similarly sized star near by, with my binoculars. Changed colors,(primary) from red, green, yellow, blue, and did not travel. I didn't think to watch for a pattern. I fell asleep after about thirty minutes observation. Tried to see it again the following night, nothing.
