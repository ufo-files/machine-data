{cak} Super bright pulsating light that remained pretty stationary for over 30 min then drifted W

I was walking my dog and saw what looked like an airplane landing light but it remained stationary. I went home and got some binoculars and it appeared as a pulsating white light and after a while it showed red and orange then back to white. I watched it for about 40 minutes before it drifted slowly west and behind some trees on a hill. It was 220 degrees from Cedar City, UT
