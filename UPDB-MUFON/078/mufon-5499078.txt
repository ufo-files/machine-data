{rjl} saw disc shaped object in the sky as I drove from Rhode Island to CT on I 95

Saw disc/ saucer shaped object in sky as I ascended an uphill grade on I- 95 in RI heading to CT. It caught our attention because it shone in the sun. It reflected the light of the sun. We had no other thoughts as to what it was because of the way it disappeared. It was there and then it was gone. Blew my mind. I'm sure of the date because my wife and I wee on our way back to CT from Cape Cod after our honeymoon. The previous week was very rainy but on the date we returned, it was a brilliant June day.
