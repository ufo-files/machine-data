A glowing sunlike object appeared to shoot across my line of vision for a split second from East to West over my town.

I was standing in the rear garden of my house facing North Sky Watching at about 9.15pm. Night sky was dark and a bit cloudy but could see many stars and what appeared to be a Satellite pass from North to South in the East sky. I see satellites several times every night so know what they look like. A short time later, as I looked slightly east a glowing orb-like object suddenly appeared from nowhere directly in front of me in the sky at a 45 degree angle. It was so bright I could not miss it. It appeared for just a split second, travelling at a very high rate of speed from right to left (East to West) Then it was gone! No lights, sound, emissions, etc. It was my first sighting of a very bright glowing gold orb-like object. I had that WOW! feeling during and after seeing it. I always question what I see afterwards as one can easily be mistaken. I will add to this event: the night before in the same area of night sky I saw a sunburst ( almost like a supernova) It appeared only one time. Four months before, I was standing in the same place at about the same time when what appeared to be a white/grey triangular or flat shaped craft glided at high speed past me from East to west. I caught a glimpse of it as it passed only to disappear as quickly as it appeared. No lights, sound, or emissions. It was as if it was "teasing' me - showing itself to me but only for a split second! This craft was in my opinion a definite UFO encounter! I hope this information is useful to you.
