{cak} Saw 2 or more objects hovering less than half mile from my position. Looked like plates with a light on top.

I walked outside at about 11:10 for a night smoke and saw some kind of blinking light in the distance to the west, about a half mile from me. At first I though maybe it was a plane taking off because theres an airport within 30 minutes of my house. But all of a sudden this light just stopped moving, almost instantaneous to when I started looking at it. It just hung in the sky, almost like it was watching me. I couldnt move my body or take my eyes off of it. I started to hear this beeping noise in my head, and it started to scare me. For some reason I looked in the rest of the night sky to see if there were any other craft, and I saw one more similar object to the north, farther up in the sky. How far Im not sure. This one was also moving before I looked at it, I could tell for sure. But within a few seconds of my noticing the object it stopped moving as well. I got the same feeling as I had staring at the other one. I broke eye contact and went inside to get my phone to get a picture of the objects, but when I came back outside they were both gone. They both looked to be of a dinner plate shape, and they both had a red/blue pulsating light on the top (?). It scared me deeply to be back outside, and I had a feeling of dread for some reason. Im not sure why. Im typing this about 15 minutes after the sighting and I don't feel safe doing it.
