3 basketball size objects joined

In evening on arriving at my house I saw at the edge of my wooded property an object consisting of three bright blue glowing lights about the size of basketballs
They we're in the shape of a pyramid
Two on the bottom and one on top
The surface of the balls were faceted like a gemstone
It sat motionless for at least 15 minutes. I tried to take pictures but surprisingly it did not show up
It was a little dark by then I was scared to go closer to it and went in the house
