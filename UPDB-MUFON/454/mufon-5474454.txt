I looks over toward the Brooklyn Museum, then I saw a glowing object pass by rather quickly faster than a plane. I yelled for her to come out fast. It was still moving through the sky and she was unclear what it was, at that exact moment, and meteor flew across the sky. I live close to JKF and LGA flight patters and I have been here for over 4 years. I see everything from helicopters, planes, private jets, baloons, drones, trash bags floating about..... this was none of those. Nothing I have ever seen like this.
