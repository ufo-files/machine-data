Was driving south on Interstate 71 toward Cincinnati, saw light; later discern three lights in triangle pattern hovering near two radio/TV transmission towers.

I was driving south on Interstate 71 toward Cincinnati about 8 p.m. as I returned to work from home. As I was driving (between mile markers 5 and 2), I noticed what looked like a "new" white radio tower light up ahead in the vicinity of two tower lights that are red in color, which I am accustomed to seeing. As I got closer, I realized the light, which actually discerned into three small lights in a tight triangular pattern, was not atop any kind of tower whatsoever and was in fact not moving at all, hovering instead. The triangular object was to my right as I traveled south, dark against the dusk sky. I simply continued driving, glancing up often as I drew nearer and ultimately passed the object on my right. After I realized it wasn't a tower light, my second thought was maybe a stealth bomber, since the shape would lend itself to that type object.

I also know it wasn't Venus, which was in the western sky at sundown.
