{jpg} Object first looked like a huge star. I took some pics and downloaded them to find that it was string shaped and changing.

It was about 12:30 am. I was walking my dog and I looked up at the sky (as I usually do) and saw what I thought to be a huge star, double the size of a normal star, and it was pulsating red. It seemed to be the same distance as some of the other stars but I really couldn't tell because I don't really know how to calculate that.

After a few minutes, I took my dog inside and got my cheap little CoolPix camera, to see if I could capture the image. I figured if it was a star, I would only see a white spot in the shot. But I saw something very different. (See pictures) A string-like object, changing shape.

It seemed to move very slowly, just like a star would. When I first saw it, it was slightly to my left, as I stood at my front door. A couple of hours later, it was over my apartment building.

The next night (which is the night that I'm writing this), I went out to see if it was there again but it wasn't. I should add that on both nights, the sky was clear so if it was there tonight, I'd have seen it.

Wish someone could tell me what that could have been. I noticed as I looked through the reports, that there were a couple of sightings in neighboring states that posted similar reports.
