{jpg} Dark Object hovering and another one moving very fast with a slight directional change

Driving in the california desert right on highway 78 at the Ocotillo Wells airport, my friend was taking pictures using her iphone 7 burst mode, after we arrived at Borrego Springs, we were amazed at what was captured by the phone. 
She first noticed the dark object, which seemed to move up and down and hover around, and then after a closer look, she spotted the second object which is smaller and after watching the video we could see it moved extremely fast from left to right, first appearing to the right of the frame, then it changed direction slightly and appeared right under the dark object and then it appeared to the right of the frame.
