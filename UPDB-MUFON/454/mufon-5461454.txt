Transparent light anomaly

Was trying to snap a few photos of the International Space Station as it made it's fly over here in South Mississippi last night at 2044 hours (4-9-18). The ISS was moving 28 degrees above WNW and ending 50 degrees above S. As I was setting up I notice two transparent object moving from NE to W at a significant rate of speed about 15-20 degrees above the horizon and looked like they where trying to pass one another and seemed to be in a tight formation. Before I could get my camera turned around they where gone. I can say with 100% certainty that they where not any type of aircraft that I have seen fly over by house in the pass,as there was no sound or lights and where moving way to fast .
