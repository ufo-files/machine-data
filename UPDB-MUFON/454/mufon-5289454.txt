11 orange yellow glowing orbs in a line

I was traveling on Hwy 54 with friends I was in the driver-side back seat. We were going toward Holts Summit and had just crossed the Missouri River bridge and had passed under the Hwy 63 over pass, I looked out the window to my left and saw a string of 11 orange yellow orbs they look like they were connected and had an orange line above and below the lights that seemed to be holding them together. I couldn't believe my eyes, I didn't say anything to my friends, I thought my friend who was driving relatively fast, may drive off the road. So I kept my mouth shut. I thought to myself, am I seeing a reflection of lights off the window? I looked to my right no car there and we where in the left lane, so no car there, and no lights in the car that it could be, and then I saw a thin wisp of a cloud pass in front of it. Then it just vanished.  
  I drew a picture of what I saw too.
