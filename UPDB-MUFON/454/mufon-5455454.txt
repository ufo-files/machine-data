{jpg} Two boomerang shaped craft observed.

I was on my way home from work around 7:45 pm. While driving west on Hwy 50 between Silver Springs and Stagecoach I noticed 5 while lights in a perfect row just off to the right side of the freeway maybe 500-1000 feet high. I then noticed two more lights to the left and slight below. all of the lights were very bright and very crisp. The lights were getting closer quickly as I drove. I turned off a side road and drove almost directly below the lights. When I stopped I noticed the five lights created a delta wing with a flashing red light in the middle. The other two lights off to the left turned out to be another delta wing exactly like the first. Both wings then slowly banked 90 degrees and headed North. I continued to observe them for a couple more minutes until they slowly disapeared. I was not able to get any photos but someone must have also seen this.
