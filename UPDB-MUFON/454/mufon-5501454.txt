Stopped car to photograph tree and sunrise later observerd object in 4 photos

I was driving my car with a friend as a passenger in the countryside early morning, just as the sun was rising. I saw a beautiful tree I wanted to photograph so I stopped the car and walked into a field to take some photos of the tree with the sun rising in the background. I took my photos and returned to my car and resumed driving. I was completely unaware that I had photographed anything unusual and had not seen the object at the time. It was only when looking at my photos again sometime later that I noticed the strange light in the clouds in four of the photos. 

When I first noticed the object or light in my photos I thought I had captured a star still shining bright before the sun had completely risen.Then on closer inspection and enlarging the object, zooming in on my phone, I realized it is a very unusual object that seems to be hovering in the clouds. The colours are most unusual and the four photos were all taken in quick succession at 7.27am. I realized by studying the photos that the object moves across the clouds and then returns to its original position very quickly. Due to the great distance between myself and the object and seeing how bright it is I would guess the object to be quite large. 

I do not know what the object is, and don't know how to explain what I'm looking at. I have shown these photos to several friends and they too cannot explain what the object is.
