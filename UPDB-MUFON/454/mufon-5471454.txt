{rjl} Two sphere-shaped orb moving very quickly, left a strange aura/cloud

On December 22, 2017, around 5:30pm, (give or take a few minutes) a possible UFO was sighted. In Southern California, in the city of Pasadena (91106) 2-3 blocks North of Lake Avenue and the 210 Freeway, me and 2 of my roommates saw a cloud-like aura suspended in the air about half a mile or less from our distance, althought depth was hard to tell given the night-time and the cloud-like substance making it hard to tell its exact location.
Immediately after seeing the aura, we witnessed two sphere-shaped objects creating this aura-like trail. One object was traveling extremely quickly and it seemed the trail and aura was being emitted from this first object. It had an unwavering light and was heading roughly 170 degrees South of North.
There was also a second object following the same path as the first object. This object was traveling at a slower speed than the first object and also was pulsating as opposed to unwavering. This second light seemed to disappear after the "aura/cloud" was created.
The first object continued to in the same direction, emitting a bright light trail behind it. The degree above the horizon was immeasurable at this time.
No sound was heard at all and there were others in the neighborhood looking at the events as well.
