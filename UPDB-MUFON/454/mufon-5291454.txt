3 Objects Appear in Storm Clouds

Saw several dark objects appearing and disappearing within storm clouds. Thought my eyes were playing tricks on me so I took a picture with my cell phone. When I zoomed in on the pictures I discovered 3 dark objects in and around the cloud. Way to high to be store bought drones or kites...Actual size about 1/4 of a Boeing 737- 200. My impression was that they were observing our area while camouflaging in the clouds..
