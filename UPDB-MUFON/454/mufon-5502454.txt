{rjl} Large orange flaming ball gliding silent across clear sky

Was at a park with my girlfriend talking, noticed a super bright orange ball. Thought maybe a plane. Was very large, a solid orange glow like a fireball. We watched it glide across the clear sky without a sound. Saw planes fly by before and after, like any normal plane with white and blinking lights. This was MUCH bigger. There is also a meteor shower tonight but flew across the entire sky unlike any meteor I've seen before. We both watched it the entire time. It started to get smaller and almost looked as if it went up into some clouds that I could not see because of the darkness. Neither of us had seen anything like it before and have seen plenty of meteors, planes, helicopters, etc.
