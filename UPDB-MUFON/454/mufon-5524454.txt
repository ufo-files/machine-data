2 bright white objects, oval shaped, flying very close to each other.

I was outside watering the plants on my balcony. I looked up at the horizon and I saw 2 bright white objects, oval shaped, one of them seamed different, longer and bigger, flying in a very close formation. I observed them for a few seconds and I was thinking in the same time if I have time to get dressed, grab my camera and try to capture them on film. The objects were moving in a fast straight pattern. I went inside, grabbed my camera, got dressed and run outside. I had to go in a three clear spot to be able to spot the objects again. I tried to capture them on film but I've stopped after a few seconds because I was loosing sight of the objects and I didn't have time to focus on them. They got pretty far in a matter of seconds. So I switch my camera of photo capture and I snapped a few pictures of the objects. They broke formation and were getting distance between them when I was able to photograph them. Immediately after that they disappear. Not by distance like furthering away, they just disappeared like dissolving very fast in the sky. I have the original un-cropped photos that I cant send you for analysis. I really regret that I lost time, amazed by what I was witnessing and I lost the opportunity to snap photos when the two objects were so close to me and to each other and more detail could be observed.
