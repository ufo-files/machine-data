{cak} Extremely slow moving craft moving at tree top level

It was evening. I was a passenger in a car exiting the West Rock tunnel on the border of New Haven and Woodbridge, CT. The object was traveling from the SE as it came across Rte 15, a 4 lane highway, heading toward the NW at treetop level over the tunnel. No sound at all, moving extremely slow.  White perimeter lights. A chevron shape. Watched it move over the highway, our car and then the tunnel. It’s width spanned the width of the highway then some.  This is a long time ago and cannot be investigated at this time, however, I never reported it and would like to report it now for the record.
