{nd} Burnt orange light, fairly big size, went towards airplane, got smaller and disappeared very quickly

At 10pm May 25th in Kewanna Indiana as I was going out to the car I looked up to see a good sized orange light lingering in the air... It started moving slowly so I ran in to get my husband so I had a witness and knew I wasn't crazy. He came out and it was still there. It started moving fast and changing directions from Northwest to north east and went towards a plane and started getting smaller and smaller and itching about 45 seconds after the orange light started getting smaller, it completely disappeared. I was pretty much in awe. It was not the first time if seen weird things in the sky.... This however is the very first time my husbands witnessed such a thing. I had serious goosebumps.
