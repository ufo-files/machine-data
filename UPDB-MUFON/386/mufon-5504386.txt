{nd} I saw a slow flash of light come from a star. Emitted other objects that turned to mist and moved hyperspeeds

Went outside at 8:00 to take dog out. I saw a slow flash of light come from a small dim star or what looked like a star. About 10 mins. later it did it again. 5 mins. later it did it again and then it emitted other objects. One of the objects turned into a misty disk shape and separated from the star like object then took off to the north very fast and left a trail that disappeared quickly. It flashed slow again. My friend came outside and saw it flash again right after I said something. Then what looked like a star traveled west to the east and then about 10 mins. Another one from north to south. The star flashed again and then another separate one. Looking up from my view it was right under Orion. I see lights and have had alien contact before and I feel that this is them letting me know they are around. There is a port and I thought might be a drone but I watched for about an hour. The flashing ones looked like small stars and the others were very fast. I have seen similar but never saw one go that fast before. They are still outside right now.
