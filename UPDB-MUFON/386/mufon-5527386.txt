Multiple "watcher" ufo sighting(20)

I pulled to the side of road near Mankato. March 15-2011 at 9:11 pm. I began filming the following footage. Some of these watchers were stationary but many were "movers." Some of these watchers became blinkers, but most remained in watcher form. Those are the ones on this piece of video. I saw approx. 20 watchers, and 15-30 blinkers, but it is hard to calculate amounts. I simply know that they are plentifull. I posted some of the footage on my youtube channel, [Youtube channel name remopved/cms/tg]. This phenomenon appears to be getting quite serious. I stopped filming while the entities were still in view, as my battery went dead.
