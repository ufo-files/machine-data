{jpg} Very brief rectangular light hovering above neighbor house and then took off.

I was in my bed, looking at my phone in the dark with my blinds open, it was a full moon so it was a bright night. All of a sudden my room filled with light, a bright flash. Thinking it was lightening I looked out my window and noticed a rectangular light hovering a couple hundred feet above the fence in my back yard. At first I thought it was a helicopter with a spot light looking for someone, but there was no sound and it was kind of wobbling. The light was very bright and I as I tried to figure out what it was it made a weird quick curve upward and vanished with this blue trail. It did not fade gradually like a airplane would or a shooting star. It was like a vacuum sucked it into the sky.. When I saw the object I was confused, but when I noticed it was hovering, my heart was pounding and was like a deer in headlights. When it disappeared my arms and legs got goosebumps and I was in shock. I was scared and light headed. The object took off in flight and disappeared.
