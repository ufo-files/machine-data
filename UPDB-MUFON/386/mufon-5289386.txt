DEFINITELY NOT A PLANE

My friend and I were sitting by the Meramec river in the suburbs of St. Louis. We were in her jeep with the windows down. It was nearly dead silent. All we could hear was the soft ripples of the water. We had literally just been joking about what if we saw a UFO, and then about 10 minutes later, at around 10:40pm, we saw an aircraft coming from the west. At first we thought it was a plane (obviously), but as it got closer above us, we realized it was not a plane at all. It made absolutely no sound. Planes have sounds, and this aircraft produced not even a light hum or buzz, and it was flying close to the ground. The lights on the aircraft were orange, red, and green, all flashing randomly. As it was above us, the bottom was an indescribable color (almost like a taupe color or a dull copper). The components that made up this aircraft looked seamless. There was no certain shape. It looked NOTHING like a plane. We have a video but it is very low quality and it doesn’t do the actual sighting justice.
