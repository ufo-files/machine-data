{cak} Observed a very large rectangular object

I'm only reporting this event as a result of all sightings of this size do not have the same signature that I encountered.
I did not observe the object initially although I could hear it. The sound was very low pitched almost inaudible however you could feel it once you heard it. You could tell that it was in the sky which was pitch black with no moon on that night. I kept trying to see in the sky the by sound and eventually I started to see stars disappear as the object past in front of the star. This object was enormous in size and took 10-15 minutes before the sight and sound was lost. It looked to me as if this object was studying or observing the landscape of our planet. It never change direction or speed.
The object I observe was very low flying , at a very low altitude
