I was on patrol with the NSDF on exercise Hammel as a medic I was contracted for the job and when I first saw the black Tear drop object. I pointed it out to the SGT at the time who told me not to worry. I could tell it wasn't a normal type of air craft as it had copper colour lights and moved in a very random way unlike a military helicopter or aircraft. We did have access to helicopters but we got told at the start of the exercise that Helo's won't be operating. So it couldn't of been them after we walked down a dirt road past a few homes we watched the black object land vertically in a field. Once again the SGT said don't worry but shortly after 3 MH-60 Military helicopters come flying over our heads with the search lights on as the lights went over the object. I could see a reflective black surface with 2 I would describe as port windows and 3 Small lizard type people with huge brown eyes and a pale green skin one member of the group fired a shot from his rifle and that caused them to I could only describe as teleport back into the craft. Shortly after it shoot up into the sky and was just gone. I tried to get photos but wen i retuned to the camp they made us hand over all electronics and told us to go to the hospital.
