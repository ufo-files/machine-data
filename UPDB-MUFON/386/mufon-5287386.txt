Group movement was instantaneous change of positions

This report is for two sightings, actually, which took place in New England. I can't remember exactly where, but possibly Virginia. I was in undergraduate school at the University of North Carolina School of the Arts and on the Senior Rep Tour performing at various venues. At two times within a two-week span that Fall, I witnessed small groups of ovals which seemed to be luminescent and colored light grey-green/blue. Their movement was remarkable in that they would "flit" from one spot in the sky to another, nearby, almost as if they were projections of light - no movement from spot to spot could be perceived, only the staring and ending positions. Then, all at once, they silently "whooshed" at a steep angle up and away in an instant.
