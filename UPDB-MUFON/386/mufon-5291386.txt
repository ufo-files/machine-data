Orange light in OH sky

We did not have our phones during this event. We can not provide pictures.

The object was spotted around 2130; it "winked" into existence, as if it has suddenly turned on its lights. It remained visible as it continued its course, South to North, perhaps following the path of the Ohio River a few blocks away from our house. The moment lasted less than five minutes. It "winked" out and we saw no additional activity after that.

Its altitude was between 700-1000 ft; it moved along, sometimes seeming to momentarily hover. Our best estimate is that it moved no less than 25 mph and no more than 50 mph. It was a brilliant orange-red light that shone steadily. We couldn't ascertain any shape. It made no sound.

This was not an airplane or helicopter. It is plausible that this was a drone. However, a drone of that size requires a Remote Pilot Certificate from the FAA. If a drone, the pilot violated the FAA 's Small UAS Rule by operating in a careless manner (no lights or beacons before and after the drone traversed its course).
