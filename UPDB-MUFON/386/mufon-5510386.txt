(GS) White Light UFO that changed direction

It was about 10:35 pm when I noticed a White light in the sky to the south. It behaved just like a satellite, except it made a slow turn that I cannot possibly believe a satellite could make given that much distance. About 5 minutes after noticing the light in the sky, it was toward the east-north-east, and I stopped watching it after it got to about 15 degrees above the horizon east-north-east of me.
