Streak of green light from west horizon to east horizon over JFK airport.

Sitting on my couch with a view out of my window, I saw a green streak of light faster than any shooting star and with much longer, GREEN trail of light. Flew from west to east right over JFK airport. Streak remained for a few seconds. I went to Craigslist and made a post about the occurrence. A reply from Boston came within minutes. Someone saw the same thing. A report I saw around August 31 on MUFON's web page reminded me to post this occurrence.
