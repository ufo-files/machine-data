'Invisable' triangular UFO

I was traveling along RT322 in Atlantic Co., NJ when at around 4:40 PM I saw some distance ahead of me a moving distortion in the (fairly solid & darkish) clouds that appeared as a very fast large 'cloaked' triangle shape moving at high speed. It appeared several times larger than I would expect a passenger jet to appear at that distance. I was traveling east to west, the distortion moved from north to south. It was in my field of view for not more than 2 seconds. It was like there was a 'cloaked' large triangular 'flying wing' turned on its side. Think of the distortion of the jungle in the movie Predator when the alien moved while cloaked. That is how a part of the clouds looked. I thought, "What the hell was that!"

It is the first time I have seen something that I can not explain. I don't believe it was 'alien', but something natural & outside of my experience. I would love to know just what it was.
