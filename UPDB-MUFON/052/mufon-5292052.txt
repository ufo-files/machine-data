3 white points of light in triangle formation blocking out sky behind it

My son, Ethan and I (Kevin) were watching the sky for satellites. We observed the triangle formation moving slow, about 20 degrees above horizon. The center of the formation blocked out the sky behind it. We both saw it block out a star that it passed in front of. The 3 points were made of white points of light, with the lights not blinking at all. Due to houses and trees, we could not tell how far away it was from us. The duration of our sighting listed around 75 seconds. We tried alerting the other people in the house of what we were seeing but they had already gone to sleep.
