Exact location : 43,3'38.29" N 82,34'53.47"W 2 miles east of Leamington, Ontario, Canada.
I was alone sitting on ground on back lawn playing with my puppy. I had a feeling that someone was looking at me. I looked around and directly to my west approx. 200 feet away and about 100ft up as a large silver object.
I was an avid reader and knew this was an UFO. It made no noise-it was lined up North/South. Looked like a plane with no wings, nor tail fin nor engines and had multiple rectangular windows. There seemed to be movement in those windows--something opaque. I did not move just stared at it. I was not afraid just interested. After about 10 minutes it slowly turned from one end facing South towards the West. It was moving faster & faster--never any noise and when was quickly gone.
