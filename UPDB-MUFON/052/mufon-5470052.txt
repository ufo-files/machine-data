{td}I saw a cylinder shaped object flying straight followed by 5 spheres with no flight path

I was on my porch and told my wife an airplane was going fast. She said it was not a plane and had no wings. I put my glades on and saw it did not have wings. It did not leave a vapor trail and was shaped like a blimp. After watching it for maybe 15 seconds five round objects appeared behind it flying in no particular pattern. They dissapered about 3 times and would just reappeare and fly all over without a pattern. This went on for approximately 3 minutes.Then everything dissapered. A few minutes later a jet flew to there approximate location and went the same path they were headed before disappearing.
