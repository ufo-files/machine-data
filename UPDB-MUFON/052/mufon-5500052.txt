{rjl} Glow/burning cylinder moving at rec plane speed. 2 mins

I was at work @10pm east on august 13 2014. I looked to the horizon north and saw a glowing red object. I didn't think much of it at first because there were other planes flying. However as the object got closer it's shape was undefined. By that I mean it didn't look like a plane at all. It looked like a flying cylinder of fire. Red and orange in color and moving at a similar speed as the planes in the area. It stayed on course moving south east for @ 2 minutes before disappearing. Not behind a tree or building or behind a cloud but just gone. It's size remained fairly constant so I don't think it got out of sight, but it was night and I was in a small valley. Another person witnessed this and I have a short video.
