{rjl} Horizontal cylinder reflected sunlight, moved across sky, and disappeared

I was driving on the southbound highway leaving Grand Rapids at around 3 pm. That's when a shiny object caught my eye. It was long and laying horizontally, so I thought it was probably a plane. But, the shining of reflected sunlight was so bright I did not think it could be a plane. I also saw no tail or wings on the craft. This is when I locked my focus on it. It was slowly moving across the sky as it continued to shine. After about 25 seconds, the shine began to dim and I could see better the outline of a typical cigar shaped craft. As this was happening, the ship disappeared in a baffling way. The reflection from the sun went away, and the dark form beneath seemed to close in on itself while at the same time blotting out of vision before totally dematerializing. I was shocked when I saw this happen. I searched the sky around where the object disappeared but I could not see anything. It just vanished. I kept scanning the sky and I continued to tell myself that it was not an airplane. I believe that I am right and may have expereinced a brief connection between realities/dimensions. I am curious to know if anyone else on the highway saw the object as well. I hope you enjoyed hearing my experience.
