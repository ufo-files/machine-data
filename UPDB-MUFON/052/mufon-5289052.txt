Large, slow moving fireball gliding though night sky. Continued to appear to get smaller and then changed direction and disappeared

My neighbor, husband and daughter were outside talking. My daughter asked what is that thing, and I said plane, then she said not that, that big orange thing. So when we all looked it was a large glow like a fireball slowly gliding toward our direction. It was getting smaller as is was gliding. It reached an area above the trees and turned direction, it was getting smaller and then it just disappeared. It was fascinating to watch.
