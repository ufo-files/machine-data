UFO fleet over Burbank CA

Walked outside for a break approx 2:30 am when I clearly saw several ships hovering in the air above me. The looked like lit christmas trees standing upright blinking with red, white and blue lights. Very bright and they were too close for comfort maybe 7-800 feet up.I could count a total of 7 ships. They moved slowly and separately in different areas around me. I called for co-workers to come out and they came within a minute and thank god saw the same thing confirming I have not lost my marbles. I have seen 2 similar ships very close before but these looked larger in size and more lights on them. It is difficult to explain but I could feel that these were not earthly so to speak. This being very close to St:Josephs hospital which is always busy there must have been more people that saw this than us ( we were a total of 8 witnesses) I had to go back in to work but as I did they were still there hovering. I saw them close to 50 min.
