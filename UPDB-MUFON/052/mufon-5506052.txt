{rjl} 1 light appeared from the North, traveling South, which then broke into three lights that formed a triangle shape and continued Southward in a triangular formation.

We were laying out in the front yard looking at stars at around 1am, noticed a light that appeared to be a bright satellite coming from the North and travelling in a Southward direction. The light abruptly changed directions multiple times including 90 degree angle turns, indicating that it was not an orbiting satellite. We were very excited by this fact, and continued to watch the light as it travelled South, looking for changes in altitude and more change in direction. After watching this light for about another minute, two more lights, coming from different directions in the sky and rapidly changing direction, joined the first and after much changing of flight paths, all three arranged themselves in a triangular formation and continued Southward as the first light had been doing. This sighting was not of a single craft with multiple lights, but of three separate crafts that formed a triangular flying formation. We watched the formation continue South until it was obscured by city lights.
