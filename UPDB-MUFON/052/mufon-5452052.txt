Very bright, but small intense red light in front. Small, dark object attached to light that I couldn't see until it passed me. Close, fast, silent.

I was out front of my house watching a line of storms moving in. Just as the front edge of storm wind started hitting, I noticed a small, but very bright, red light emerge from over my neighbor's roof. This light appeared to be the size of a baseball and it moved very fast (200mph+). It was at a distance from me of no more than 500ft. It's altitude was about the same - 500ft. It looked like it was just staying in front of the gust front. The light wavered and it was completely silent. As it very quickly moved across the sky, as it passed me on my right, I noticed a small dark object attached to the light (the light was on the very front). The object looked ovalish at 1st, but then slender the farther away it got. And, it was absolutely silent. 
It moved in a straight line with no deviation until it went behind neighbor's house and disappeared. I was still able to see the red light even as it passed, even though not as vibrant, the entire time it was in my view (which was only 12-15 seconds). I managed to take a picture of the object as it was about perpendicular to me. It was too fast to capture more. In the picture, it almost looks like it has a trail behind it, but this was not visible at the time. The trail may just be the front edge of the storm clouds. I took the picture as soon as I could and the object is visible (even though small) just left of center. It looks distant in the picture, but again, it was very close to my position. I have added two close-ups to evidence.
