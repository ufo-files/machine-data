{rjl} Tons of lights in sky

Driving home on 202 from Gray to Windham and saw a cluster maybe 40-50 lights pulsating and fading in and out of the sky traveling towards the ocea from the west. Lasted a few minutes and lights blinked out. I'm certain these weren't Chinese lanterns. I launched some at my daughters wedding three years ago and these were brighter, faster, and seemed to have organic qualities.
