(GS) 2 diamond shaped objects

I walked out of Walmart. Put grocieries in truck. I was taking the buggy to the return area when I noticed one diamond shaped object in the sky.It wasnt moving. It glowed a redish-orange color in a steady light patern;like an LED strip. Seconds later a second object of the same description appeared rigth beside the first. one would disappear then re-appear...then the other...and they began to "dance" eside each other...then at once they both disappeared. 

When I saw the first one I immediately knew it was not a normal aircraft. I just stopped in the middle of the parking lot...unable to move. Just starring. When the second appeared I was literally frozen in place. I wasnt afraid. Just frozen. Ive never seen anything like it. I was on the phone with my boyfriend and described to him what I was seeing as it happened. There was no one near me so I don't know if anyone else saw or not.
