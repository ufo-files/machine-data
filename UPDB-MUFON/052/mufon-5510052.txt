{nd} Bright star like object went directly overhead then changed to orange-red and shot off disappearing in the SE sky.

I was in my back yard sky watching as I do every night. I often see things that could be satellites or ufos. but this time it was much closer or larger and brighter than any I have ever seen. Brighter than aircraft landing lights that are usually in the area. when it got over me straight up I observed it with my binoculars to be sure it was not a plane. It was a bright blue-ish white slightly pulsating. As it moved it seemed to be on a jittery path, changing speed and direction kind of like a jitterbug on the water. After it passed overhead and was far to the southeast it quickly turned red-orange and got smaller and dimmer until it was out of sight. At that time the full moon was low and a bit to the north and blocked by the house next door so it did not interfere with my observation.
As the object was overhead I felt energy, a kind of excitement not usually felt during my other nightly observations.
