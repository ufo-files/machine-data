Three intense red lights moving slowly to the East, then North East

it was December 31st, 2021. The time was 11:45 PM, only 15 minutes for the new year. I went to the balcony at the north side of the 9110 Fontainebleau Building to see the new year fireworks. Looking up, we noticed three intense red lights moving very slowly to the east in the night. At first we thought it was some kind of fireworks, but they were definitely not. They were much higher than the fire works and kept moving very slowly, maybe because of the altitude. We are somewhat close to the Miami Airport. The airplanes, when passing every day near our home, are about 1,000 feet high, sometimes much higher. An airplane was coming from the airport, but instead of staying on course and gain altitude, like they all do, the airplane stayed very low and turned in the direction of our building, coming very low and passing over our heads with tremendous noise. We supposed that maybe the pilot saw the three red lights and was trying to avoid them.
The three red lights continued to move very slowly, the one heading the three, began taking a slight turn to the northeast. The other two, also very slowly, followed it, turning very slowly in that direction, following the first one. There was absolutely no noise coming from them.
The three red lights appeared to be three independent UFO's.
