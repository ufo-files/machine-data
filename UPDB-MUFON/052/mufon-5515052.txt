{cak} Many objects/lights hovering, to a semi-formation, some lights blinking

Out on the balcony for a family river trip. I was first to notice slowly/moving small cluster of lights in distance atop low mountain range. About 10 family members came out & also witnessed the "floating" objects/lights. Te lights seemed too far apart to be 1 object, they kept to a mostly straight formation path, some drifted. Altitude seemed too high for helicopters (it was quiet, we heard no noise from them, but we were a fair distance away). Also seemed to be moving too slow to be aircrafts. Some others also had slow-constant, flashing lights. They faded into distance as if increasing altitude. Initially thought it might be fighter jets practicing formations, but again, seemed like they were moving too slow to be a jet.un
