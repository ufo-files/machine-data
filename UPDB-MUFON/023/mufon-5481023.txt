{nd} IT HAD BEEN FLOODING IN MY BACK YARD SO I TOOK A PICTURE, WHEN I DEVELOPED THE FILM A LITTLE MAN WAS IN THE PICTURE

FILLED OUT AT MUFON HEADQUARTERS BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER, 18809, WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE. SEPTEMBER 8TH, 2016
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
WITNESS SAYS:
IT HAD BEEN RAINING AND MY BACK YARD WAS FLOODING. THE BUSINESS AT THE BACK OF THE YARD WAS CAUSING THE FLOODING SO I WAS TAKING A PICTURE TO PROVE IT.
WHEN I GOT THE FILM DEVELOPED I SAW A SMALL MAN IN THE PICTURE, HE HAD HIS HANDS UP OVER HIS HEAD, HE APPEARED TO HAVE A POUCH AROUND HIS WAIST AND SOME KIND OF WRAPPINGS AROUND HIS LEGS. I HAVE HAD THE PICTURE FOR 15 TO 16 YEARS AND MY CHILDREN SAID I SHOULD REPORT THIS ALIEN TO MUFON.
I'M NOT SURE THE MONTH OR YEAR, BUT IT WAS 15 TO 16 YEARS AGO.

WHEN FI CONTACTS THE WITNESS THE PICTURE CAN BE TURNED OVER BY AN AGREED METHOD, MAIL OR PERSONALLY. WITNESS WANTS THE PICTURE RETURNED AFTER THE INVESTIGATION IS FINISHED.

RESEARCHED SHOWED A TROPICAL STORM CALLED LESLIE DID HIT FLORIDA ON OCTOBER 4TH OF 2000 CAUSING FLOODING.
