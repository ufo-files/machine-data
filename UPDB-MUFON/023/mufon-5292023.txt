Speedy Tic Tac Sighted

On 8.11.20 I noticed a white object traveling at tremendous speed over a long distance across the sky traveling NE to NW. There are nearby airports and this object had no wings, no propulsion and no sound which is what caught my attention. This object appeared solid white like a Tic Tac. The sky was clear with few clouds. After this sighting I searched and found a video sighting from Albany, NY taken on 8.10.20 which is close in resemblance to my sighting.
