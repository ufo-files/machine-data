Three small white orbs flew over the home, no sound, about , this is a guess, 5,000 to 8,000 feet, In a triangular formation at very high speed with no sound. The objects changed direction and as they did so the right object changed direction just hesitant of the other two then went back into formation. The objects continued on In a north easterly direction and went out of site In approximately 30 to 45 seconds.
