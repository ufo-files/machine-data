I was driving through Crawford notch and I saw a 747 sized tube with no wings or tail slowly descend behind mount Willard. I lost it as soon as I saw it because as I was driving my perspective got blocked by the trees around the road and when I had a view of the area again it was gone. It made no noises that a landing 747 would have made.
