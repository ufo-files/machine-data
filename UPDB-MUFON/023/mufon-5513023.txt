(GS) A slow - medium moving fireball like object with no tail (contrail). No speed or altitude change. Constant like a private jet would move.

I was letting my dog out before I left for work around 18:00 eastern time and looked up at the sky as I always do. I noticed this fireball type object slowly moving across the nearly clear sky at a steady speed. I used my iPhone to snap a couple of pics then a few seconds of video. 
 I thought it was a jet completely engulfed in flames but it continued on its path with no noise or change in altitude or direction. No contrail or streak following it.
