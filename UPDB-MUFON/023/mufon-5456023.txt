{cak} Bright yellow light made several

While stationed at Lowry Air Force base in Denver, Colorado, in late 1966 (~November), I was undergoing electronic Weapons Control Systems Mechanic training for the F-4 Phantom fighter plane on night shift. While on a break from class at ~3 AM, our group of trainees was just outside the training center, when all of a sudden, one of the other Airmen yelled "Look up in the sky". When looking up, I observed a small yellow "ball of light" quickly flying in a "square" pattern overhead at a significant elevation. This light completed ~3 travels around the square pattern in a matter of several seconds, then rapidly flew off to the east, over the horizon.
