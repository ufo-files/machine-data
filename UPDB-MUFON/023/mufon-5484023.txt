{cak} Starlite object moving North NE, faded to red dot and disappeared

Out gazing and letting the dog. Clear warm evening. Watching the air traffic. Noticed a star about the size of Sirius with a similar magnitude brightness slowly moving above me. Aligned a power pole to make sure it was in fact moving across the sky, it was....Watched it for a few minutes debating on grabbing my phone to record it. Just as I was going to grab my phone it disintegrated within a second to a very small red ball and completely disappeared.
