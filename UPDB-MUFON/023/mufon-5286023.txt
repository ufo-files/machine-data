Apparently spherical object emitting bright light for hours

On July 4, around 10:00 PM, about 10 miles south of Peyton CO, I observed an apparently spherical object in the sky, at approx. 60 degrees elevation, and glowing brightly at near minus 3 (-3) magnitude.  
I am both a professional (I’ve taught college-level astronomy) and an amateur astronomer. I have a number of telescopes including a 16” Newtonian (which I designed and built) and a 22” DOB, and have spent hundreds of hours observing the night sky.  This was clearly not a ‘normal’ astronomical event. 
Thie object was ‘spewing’ some kind of emission (see video) and it alternatively accelerated in different directions and then hovered, in approximately a 10 degree area for OVER FOUR HOURS.
