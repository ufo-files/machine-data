20-30 Orange/Reddish Orbs Moving in straight line at high speed

There was about 80 people at a birthday party(approx 40 adults). Location was Carpentersville. The lights came to our attention because someone in the crowd said that it was weird how many airplanes were above. At that moment alot of people looked up and people commented that it was not airplanes. They were reddish/ orange orbs with even spacing between them. They were moving at a speed faster than an airplane. The lights did not blink or flicker. People also notice that there were also search lights pointing in the opposite direction of these orbs. They emmitted no noise. Video will be posted shortly. Several people joked that they were UFO's while some mentioned that they were those small candle "hot air ballons." They were moving E to W.
