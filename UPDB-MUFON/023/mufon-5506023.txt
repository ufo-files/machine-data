Walking to the car after food shopping I notice the nearly full moon. I took out my phone to take a picture of it, shot the picture, and also hit the button to make a video. When I looked at the picture I saw a light beside the moon I had not noticed. I then saw that a video had been made too. I checked it to see if the orb appeared, and it did. In the video the orb moved rapidly up in an arcing manner and then sharply down disappearing behind the moon. I went back to the still shot and zoomed in as much as I could; it looks like something with a distinct shape.
