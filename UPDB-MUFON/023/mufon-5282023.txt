Egg shaped craft pic taken outside of Amtrak train in Colorado.

On May 27th 2019 I was on a trip to Grand Junction Colorado on a Amtrak train, we were going between several canyons on the train and i decided to take pictures of the scenery. As I was taking photos I happened to notice something flying along the rim of the canyon we were in and noticed a egg shaped craft white in color flying with looked like a complete smooth surface and no visible windows.
