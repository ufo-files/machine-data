{cak} looked like a star in sky, perfect circle of light around me and friend, 1 hour loss of time

Me and my friend were walking down the road at about 2 am. I was 14, he was 15. A bright light shines on us. We both thought a car was coming down the road. We looked behind us and there was nothing but the light was still there. I still remember clear as day seeing a shadow on his face from the brim of his hat. I don't know what made us both look at the same part of the sky, but we both looked at the sky. We both seen a light that vanished seconds after we seen it. It looked just like a star in the sky and then just vanished. 

After, I thought if we were abducted, maybe when they put us back we were looking in the same direction of the craft because of them putting us back. We didn't know what to think. We continued on our walk. We walked less than 30 mins and then back. We left around 1:30. We didn't get back until 3:30. An hour just gone. The truly crazy thing is when I was young, too young to remember, my father (a very honest person) seen a chevron shaped craft at our house (practically same location) and it hovered for a bit before taking off very fast. It rattled the windows at our house, and our friend about a mile away said her whole house shook too. People as far as 20 plus miles away we know heard it along with thousands we don't know. I was told over 1300 people's called into local airport [location removed] to see if they had anything on radar and they said no. I don't know if it is the area I was living in or if it has something to do with my family, but two truthful sightings in the same area within 10 years happened to me and my father.
