Dull object on a flight path, then stopped and hovered over the motorway.

Last night i was commuting home after being in Birmingham with my friends, on the motorway i looked up and saw two BRIGHT red lights, at first i thought it is a large drone or a plane as this is a common flight path for planes. I slowed down and observed further and realized it was neither of the two due to the sizing. There was two red lights each side that seemed like they were brightening, the UFO came down the motorway about 20 feet towards North and then it turned and went in the other direction. When i looked behind in my rear view mirror it had completely disappeared.
