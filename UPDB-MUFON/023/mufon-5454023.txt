{jpg} 30-50 lights consecutively flying

I was on the phone with my brother who said something crazy was going on in the sky, and to go check it out. When I walked outside, I initially noticed a plane and could hear it as it passed. (Very common) When I looked up I noticed a ball of light, It was as bright as any star in the sky, not as bright as the brightest, and not as dim as the dimmest, except it was in our atmosphere. If I had to guess, knowing planes over our home normally fly around 30,000 ft these objects were closer to 90,000+. These objects just kept coming in a line, traveling directly over our home heading southeast. They passed fairly quickly but the shocking part was the amount of them. We attempted to get footage but everything was just black in the screen. If we had to guess it went on for at least 10 minutes, there were at least 30, but as many as 50, they seemed to be perfectly spaced apart. At that height and the amount of ground they were covering they had to be traveling at a tremendous spread. We attempted to call in the report, but didn’t leave a message. It honestly looked like a military invasion, it sounds weird... but that’s what it looked like
