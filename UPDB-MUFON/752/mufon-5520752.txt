3 bright orange lights

I was travelling home from work along the A363 Bradford Road at about 7.30pm, when I noticed 3 bright orange lights above the hill to my right. I was on my mobile at the time, letting my girlfriend know that I was on my way home. I told her that they were visible above the woods on the hill opposite, and that they appeared to be stationary. I could see a plane in the distance in the vicinity of Bristol Airport. I explained that the lights were bright orange and were far larger than the plane's lights and so gave the impression of being much closer, although there was little to gauge perspective. 
As we discussed them, my phone suddenly turned itself off and when I looked back at the lights, they had disappeared. The plane's lights were still visible in the distance but there was no sign of where the lights had gone. Two of the lights were closer to Bath (North) and the third was about 3 or 4 times the distance that separated the other two but towards the south. 
If they had been military helicopters, I am sure that I would have heard them.
