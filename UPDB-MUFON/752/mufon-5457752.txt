{tjd} 25'-30' silver disk small bubble top & bottom

Had been working in my shop which is in my back yard. Walked out of the shop app. 4' and stoped. Just taking a break. Beautiful day. On wind. Very few clouds. I looked back over the shop and saw the object moving very slowly to the west. No, doubt in my mind what it was. No cell phone in hand and camera in the house with batteries out. With no pictures to show who will believe me. I watched the object untell it went behind some trees. I wanted to follow but the direction it was going there was no way.  Felt the same before, during and after.
