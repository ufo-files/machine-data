{cak} two star like bright objects almost overhead head ne direction 3-5 degrees apart

Went outside at 8:20pm eastern time. Out backdoor to take trash out to storage bin. Stood out on back deck two very bright star like objects est -3 magnitude caught my eye almost directly overhead. Even though my eyes were not adjusted to the dark these star like objects were about 3-5 degrees apart. I could clearly see moving in a ne direction and headed directly through the big dipper handle from my view point in southern michigan. Near the tri state are where indiana, ohio, and michigan meet. Objects looked brighter than ISS and reflective in color. 2 objects brightness quickly deminished in big dipper area until no longer seen.
