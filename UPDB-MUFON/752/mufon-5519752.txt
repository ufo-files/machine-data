Orange orbs hover over Lake Ontario

2 orange lights hovering over lake ontario. They did not move at all. This is the 2nd time we have seen them. The first time was November 3rd (3 lights that time). This time we only saw 2 lights. They were very bright when they were on, though they would fade out, much like a light would on a dimmer switch. They came on and off over a 3 hours period of time. At one point quite a few planes were in the sky flying towards it. It got brighter and brigher and then faded out. It would stay out for 20-45 minutes at a time, and reappear for 5-30 minutes at a time. It was in exactly the same place it was in on November 3rd. There had been strange clouds in the sky March 29th over the water. When I saw the strange clouds I wondered if I was going to see the lights again... and sure enough I did.
