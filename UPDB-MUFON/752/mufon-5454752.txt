{jpg} stationary light in sky, took picture - it disappeared - 10 minutes later back where it started

Went out to Nightcheck the horses, saw a light in the sky (which has been there for a few weeks, same spot), not as high as the airplanes that go over ahead, and the round light typically stays stationary. Since I've seen this light for a number of nights I thought to video tape it, as soon as I started filming the light, it started to move off to the North a bit and start to move farther away to the west. I had never seen it move in any of the prior sightings. So I stopped filming and tried to take a picture. As soon as my flash went off, the object completely disappeared. So I went down to the barn to feed and water the horses, about 10 minutes later on my way back to the house, I noticed the light was back in the same spot where I always see it.
The following night on the way to the barn, I see the light again, and thought about filming it again, but figured I would as soon as I finished with the horses. When I came out of the barn about 15 minutes later the object was gone.
