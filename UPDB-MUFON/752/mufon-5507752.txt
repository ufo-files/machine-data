{kt}direct contact with non-human entities

I walked out to my car and as I approached my vehicle I began to feel very uncomfortable. I felt as though there was something near by watching me. I looked around and saw a human-like figure about 4ft tall standing motionless about three yards from the drivers side. It was somewhat transparent but I could easily make out its form. It had a small body with a large head with a distinct cleft running front to back on the top of its head. It didn't move as I approached it. I then got in my car and drove away.

Ive had many other encounters with creatures like this and beings of other sorts.
