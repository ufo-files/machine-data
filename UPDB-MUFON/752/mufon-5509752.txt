{cak} large red/orange light moving slowly in my direction

Outside watching fireworks. Looked away from fireworks for a second or so then turned back toward the same SW direction and noticed very large red/orange ball shaped object with hazy outline heading straight toward me. No idea about identification but first impression was a stray bit of fireworks but speed was too slow and it had a flight path. Object was way too bright to be a plane/planet or anything that I had ever seen. Was moving at a steady pace/not very fast and about the altitude of a small plane. Appeared to possibly be pulsating but not sure about that. It then took a wide left turn, maintaining the same speed. Once this was accomplished, it began to fade in and out then quickly disappeared from site.
