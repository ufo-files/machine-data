{cak} We saw a bright light that lit up the sky and then the sky was filled with reddish orange orbs that traveled South to North a few hundred feet above the trees.

We were leaving the house when we saw a bright light over the trees. At first we thought a plane crashed but there was no sound or fire. Then there was these bright orange orbs that ascended into the sky an traveled in controlled flight about 400 to 500 ft. above the trees. There had to be about at least 100 orange orbs in flight. There was no noise produced by these objects. The experience was quite unnerving. We did capture on video. (5 witnesses)
