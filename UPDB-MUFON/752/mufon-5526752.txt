Large teardrop shaped object descends directly over cell towers followed by military craft

I witnessed a very bright object descend directly down over an area near Hogan Rd on the eve of April 29th, right after 10pm. I was parked on Town Center pkwy facing the direction of Beach Blvd. I saw a large object, like an upside down cone with a bright yellow-white light go directly down over the cell towers. The object then disappeared behind trees, but then I noticed what I can only describe as a military plane or heli-craft that I have never seen in flight. It was circling the area where I last saw the object. I then tried to follow the craft and noticed a news van was also looking for the object. They soon gave up chase for no reason. I went to the site of the towers the next day and noticed it is also a group of satellites and communication for radio, tv, etc. The object I saw was not a lantern, as suggested by local news. It had to have a span larger than the aircraft I saw afterwards and was brightly lit object that lit up the sky and it descended DIRECTLY down in a controlled fashion.
