Lights shining in my windows from the outside, came inside.

July 19, 2018 the power went out at 18:49 until some time after 22:00 (10pm). I went to bed at dusk. About 9:20 I thought a utility truck was coming up my long drive, because lights were reflecting in my bedroom window and I got up to meet them, but they were somehow already on my porch with flash lights, I thought. Inside I followed them going around the porch on the outside, and as I walked toward the kitchen, two orbs about knee high, one slightly higher and more in front than the other came into my kitchen through the walls in front of my dishwasher.
As I focused on them, I felt they knew I could see them and the top one winked out and almost instantaneously the second lower one winked out.
I was never afraid and I do not think they were spiritual entities looking to communicate or connect with me. I really have no idea who or what they were. I just know I witnessed them being here. Why did they come here when all the power was out in this region? Did anyone else experience this then?
