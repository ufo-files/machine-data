{jpg} Was watching blood moon and spotted and heard something unusual.

I was standing in my driveway watching the blood moon outside when a loud buzz and humming noise started. Then out of the right side a bright light appeared. It came forward to about 500 ft away from me but clearly enough to see that this wasn't anything like an airplane or jet. I watched for about two minutes or so when it disappeared with a flash of light.
