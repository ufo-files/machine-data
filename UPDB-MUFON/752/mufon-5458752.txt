{cak} Strange plane? No wings, no sound invisible mid section could see blue sky has it passed over.

Was watering plants in my backyard and heard a plane that sounded low. Looked up and yes saw a plane very low and heading in a odd direction from West to East . I live South of Chicago so I often see planes on a north to south pattern so this was odd. The plane was low enough to see its colors , was red & blue southwest flight i thought maybe?? Then maybe 5 minutes later another plane , same kind red & blue very low and loud which is what made me look up again also from West to East ?? I now was looking up into sky and saw this white long cigar/ tic tac shaped wingless, soundless, plane??? My little dog was outside with me and I spoke to her saying "what is that girl ?" I wish my phone was in my pocket but wasn't . It traveled slowly from North to south in a straight line. I couldn't take my eyes off of whatever it was. I have never seen anything like this. It seemed almost invisible in the mid section. This lasted less than a minute before I lost sight in the treeline. After searching online I discovered a video cigar/tic tac ufo in the UK. This is the same thing as I witnessed. This event happened on August 1st 2019 at approximately 4:15pm on a beautiful summer day. I live about 200ft from a nuclear power plant.?? Makes you wonder.?
