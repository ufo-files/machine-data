Very high altitude, stationary, flashing object.

Stepped outside for a smoke. I scanned the sky for aircraft and stars and identified Taurus, Orion and Betelgeuse. Suddenly it looked like Gamma Tauri the second star to the right of Alderbaran gained strengt and flashed 2 times with dobble the brighness of Alderbaran. Each flash came on slowly and dimmed slowly. My son also witnessed this strange phenomenon. Whatever this was , it definately was not in the atmosphere. There was no movement of this light it was stationary all the time.
