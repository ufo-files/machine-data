{dr}I saw a bright white light to my left and it flew across my path to my left at a high rate of speed

I was driving to work on Leeville Pike going towards Nashville. When I got to an area that is being cleared off because houses are going in, so there are no trees there to obstruct my view. I saw the brightest white light I have ever seen. It was white not yellow white like a halogen light and it wasn't a bluish white like a L.E.D light it was white white. It was approximately 351 above the horizon about 1 mile away. At first I thought it was a plane or a helicopter because they have landing lights in the front but as it crossed my path the lights should have disappeared because the angle would have changed but the light was constant from the front, side and back. The object traveled approximately 3 miles in about 15 seconds.
