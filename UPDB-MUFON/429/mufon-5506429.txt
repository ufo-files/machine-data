{rjl} Driving SB on I-5 by Millersburg noticed bright hovering light then its path went South hovering over empty fields

I was driving Southbound on I-5 near Millersburg, Oregon. Noticed a very bright light hovering near the old torn down Wah Chang plant. It proceeded to move southbound over Albany paralleling I-5 stopping to hover over some empty fields south of Albany. Time of day was 5:40 pm weather conditions were clear and cool  No rain clear skies with stars visible. Object was not a plane or blimp made no sound continued its path south by southwest. I stopped my work truck on the shoulder of I-5 near Seven Mille lane overpass to try yo take a photo of object. Too dark for any detail other than a white blip on iPhone camera. Object hovering was a very bright White light with an occasional bright emerald green appendages lighting/flashing short on top of object longer on bottom of object. Saw no evidence of aircraft flashing lights on wings or bottom besides object hovered multiple times. Planes don't. Got back in truck proceeded SB til I exited on Hwy 34 towards Corvallis. Objects pathway was SW towards Corvallis but stayed SE of the city. I arrived at my destination stopped and watched for 3-5 min pulled out iPad to try to get a better photo but not much better than the phone version. Pulled out a pair of binoculars to see if I could see any detail of object. It appeared to be maybe 1/4 to 1/2 mile away. Object still a very bright light TOO bright for a star not a landing or spot light. Bright kinda pulsing out of focus but had the bright emerald green lights in the shape of a X. Short on top longer on bottom. Only lighting up occasionaly. After my 3-5 min I had a short conversation with another driver in the same warehouse parking lot who also had been watching it. Weird huh? Been watching it for several minutes thing seems to just float up there silently. I said I know  I watched it basically parallel the freeway before it veered toward Corvallis.  He said its just weird s**t! He left. I finished checking my work tuck back into the dock. Come out from work/office about 20 min later could still see object but at a much further distance down over empty farming fields. Headed back home towards Salem. Time was 620pm.
