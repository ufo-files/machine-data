three lights forming a triangle

we saw the three lights heading south east at a slow pace,and we thought it was a plane heading in to cork city.This happened while i was walking with my wife on the beach at Youghal. After a while we realized that these lights were not a plane as there was no sound whatsoever even when they passed overhead,As we watched the triangle accelerated to an enormous speed and disappeared in a matter of seconds,heading south east and vanished into space.I have never seen anything accelerate so fast ,and I realised that what I saw was something of a unknown phenomena.
