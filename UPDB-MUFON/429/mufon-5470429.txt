{jpg} Evening walk. Fuzzy orb light in west, plumes top & bottom. Appeared stationary, moved rapidly overhead to south. Plume appearance changed then vanished.

Evening dog walk, 6:25pm PT. 10 miles south of LAX. Happened to glance up, sighted fuzzy orb light in west, with plumes top & bottom. Thought it was an aircraft but aircraft lights in sky were in sharp focus. Object oppeared stationary, so took sightings with Distant Planets and Skyview Apps - nothing on sky maps. (pointed toward Aries-Perseus) Object then moved rapidly overhead on curved trajectory to south, with twin vapor trails similiar to an expended rocket booster. Guess it was quite high in the sky, no apparent sound. Unusual flight path for rocket as local launches are typically are viewed in the west moving north to south. Called spouse who witnessed the object from residence. Object vanished leaving plume then looked like expended booster then vanished in the south leaving vapor "ring."  Have very faint image of distant plume ring which does have a dark shadow similar to an elongated Nissan logo. Didn't seem worth posting.
