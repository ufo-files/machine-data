phoenix lights

Me and several friends where outside talking when a brilliant orange colored fireball flew across the sky,at first i thought it was a meteor as we have very many of those in the desert i live about 45 minutes away from phoenix so i see much more because city lights don't wash out the sky,but this fireball flew completely horizontal not at a angle like falling meteors it continued that way for quite awhile until it just seemed to disapear being 18 at the time i really didn't pay much attention to it until about 30 minutes later when my brother and friend shouted "look" the way he shouted really startled me it took a few seconds to scan he sky but than i spotted it,the object was made up of about 6 o 7 lights shaped in the form of a triangle slowly moving above the municipal airport now i know what i saw and so did my brother and several others and those werent no military flares we stood there in total disbelief it was so close. it continued off in a westerly direction toward california until i lost sight of it it was wierd because it kind of hummed and the air became kind of tense i cant say what it was but i know it couldnt be man made
