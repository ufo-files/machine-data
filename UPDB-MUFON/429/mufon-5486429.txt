{rjl} Multiple Light over Charleston I-26

Multiple lights spotted over I-26 in the North Charleston area. Up to 6-10 lights viewed in low cloud cover with an almost orb like glow. Triangle formations made up the arrangements and they seemed to cover about half a miles distance in length. Lights were all moving from NW to SE and some moved faster and came extremely close to each other. My viewing time was probably for only 10 seconds driving on the highway but it was pretty incredible. They way some accelerated close to one another made me considering ruling out helicopters. This area is close to the Charleston airport and military bases which is something to consider. Since I only had exciting seconds to view the objects I wanted to see if anyone else saw these lights.
