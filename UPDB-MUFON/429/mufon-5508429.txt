Flashing light making random changes in flight path. Frequency of flash changed often. Another light spotted to south near RAF base.

I was walking home from the park on a monday night, roughly 9.30 - 10pm. I spotted a strange light, at first I thought it was a star but it began to flash which made me think it was a plane. Then the flashing kept changing randomly from very fast to very slow. The object was making sharp turns and looked like it was floating and wasn't moving very fast. I watched it for a few minutes then it moved east out over the north sea. I continued home and when I was looking out over the River Tay to the south towards Leuchars Air base where I saw another light which flashed it made a few turns then headed out East.
