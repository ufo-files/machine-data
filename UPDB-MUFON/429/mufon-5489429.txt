{nd} V shaped boomerang , appeared white

We only saw it for several seconds before it absolutely unequivocally DISAPPEARED in MID AIR. It did not fly away...it DISAPPEARED like a magic act. It was there and then it was GONE. There were other people on the highway so I can only assume that we were not the only ones who saw this...
