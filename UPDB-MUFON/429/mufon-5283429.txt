Witnessed a huge round

We were hanging ot by the water and i witnessed a few lights pass bye and i figured it was people on kayaks with flashlights on their head but it was strange since it was late and never seen kayakers on the hudson river at night. 

I randomly turned around and saw my girlfriend at the time jumping up and down and pointing out towards the water (manhatan side) so i look and witnessed this huge round object it was bright but not bright white more like the color of a street light when it gets old yellowish. The object was round and bigger than both buildings (on the right) combined. 

I turned around speechless to see my girlfriend and we were both shocked. I turned back around to see the object and it made 3 flashes and disappeared. 

Our friend that was with us never looked up and when he heard us screaming trying to explain to him he was shocked. I wanted to call the police to report it and they didn't let me thinking the cops might think we are crazy...

In one of your shows you showed a very similar object to what i saw and also now learned not far from the location there is a large powerplant.

After watching your show that it said they seem to show up near our powerplant i figured id share this story.
