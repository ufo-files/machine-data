{nd} Large, very bright orange-yellow light moving slowly ona straight line.

This morning I was walking in my neighborhood at 6:30am, it was dark out. I looked up at the moon and saw what I thought was a huge, very bright star, on the left side of the moon, looked back down, kept walking. When I looked back up, I noticed the light was higher than the moon, so I kept watching it. It was moving east very slowly, I thought 'maybe it's a plane?' But I noticed there was no sound, I stopped and I kept watching it until it disappeared behind the clouds.
