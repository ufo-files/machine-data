my fiance' and i were setting off fireworks, just like all our neighbors. one of our neighbors set off some big fireworks. both me and my fiance' saw what we thought at first to be burning embers of a firework. but as we watched it, it did not burn out and sort of "cruised" in the sky. my fiance' went to grab the camera, but by the time he got back it had burned out. we were in mt. pleasant, south carolina at the time and it was around 9:30 p.m. i have always been fascinated with space, and this really kind of scared me. i yelled for my fiance', and panicked. i did not lose sight of the object, it just vanished.
