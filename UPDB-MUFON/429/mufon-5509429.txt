(GS) Ball of fire flying across the sky.

Saw an bright object isln the sky. Lights started out very bright and then started pulsating across the sky. Started out from SE and went N/ NE and then veered NW where I lost it in cloud cover. The object covered a very long distance in a very short amount of time. I recorded this event and have video as well as two pictures. It was a bit after 10 at night on the 12th of July 2013. I look at the sky a lot but this object was so bright when I first went outside it shocked me. Next to the moon, it was the brightest thing in the sky. I lost it after a minute or so in so e cloud cover to the NW. The only way I can describe it is a fireball the way it shined.
