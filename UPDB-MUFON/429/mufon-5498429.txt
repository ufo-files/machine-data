{rjl} saw two glowing orbs with a white line between them,as it got closer it changed to a circle, one orb moved around the circle to the other orb, the circle began to rotate,the other orb moved about one quarter way around the circle and disappeared

Oct.5 2014,Sunday morning a little after 8:00am was on the carport with a cup of coffee. I heard something howling in the woods in the front of the house. The neighbors dogs were raising sane. I went in the house and walked out the front door and let my dog out in a fenced in area. I was standing on the front porch looking toward the woods, when I first noticed it in the sky, I thought it was a jet contrail (a little short one). but as I kept looking at it I noticed it had two glowing orbs on each end of it, also it was not moving like a jet contrail would following a jet. The sun was to the right of it, from the position I was standing I was having to look into the sun. There was not a cloud in the sky other than that. I had my cellphone in my pocket, I walked out on the sidewalk to where a tree was blocking the sun and started taking pictures. While I was doing that it seemed to be floating are drifting closer to me. As it got closer I noticed it was taking the shape of a circle with the two orbs directly across from each other one was at 12:00 and the other at the 6:00 position. Then the orb at the 12:00 position moved around the circle to the 6:00 position. At that point I noticed that the whole circle was starting to rotate clockwise. It seemed as if a small cloud(for lack of a better word)started to form and rotate to about the 9:00 position, the orb that was at 6:00 moved around the circle and disappeared into the cloud. Then another orb appeared in front of the circle it went into the circle between the 7:00@8:00 position and disappeared in the same small cloud, the orb that had moved first from 12:00 to 6:00 also disappeared. At that point the circle itself seemed to go through the small cloud that had formed and then started dissipating. I took 20 pictures of it in about 3 min. I don't know how long it had been there before I saw it. The picture number081549 has the orb in front of the circle, my camera was zoomed out if you zoom in and look in front of the circle about halfway to the tree you should see it. The next picture 081600 shows the orb entering the circle between 7:00@8:00.
