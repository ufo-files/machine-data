At most 7 Black objects at a time just below clouds

My son and I noticed at least 7 black orbs at a distance that seemed to be moving just below the cloud cover. They could possibly be weather balloons but I didn't think so many would be launched in the same area, typical ones I've seen here have also not been black. They would move closer, then further away quickly, disappear and then more would appear within seconds. It was at quite a distance, so it definitely was not regular balloons.
