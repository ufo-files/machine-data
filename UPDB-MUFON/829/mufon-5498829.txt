{cak} Historical case 1965 Loose Park, Kansas City, Mo.

Reporting witness related the story told to him by his father Robert that occurred in 1965 at his residence near Loose Park, KCMO after dark. Robert and his neighbor were outside when they saw an object in the night sky. They both could see panels on the object. The object made no sound. When the object passed overhead, Robert phoned the police department to report it. Robert was informed by the police department that there was a bi-plane flying around with advertisments on it. This made no sense to the two witnesses as it was low enough to see that it did not have any wings, engine sound, or lights.
