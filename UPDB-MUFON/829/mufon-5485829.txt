{cak} 5-6 orange spherical lights traveling NE, then vanishing

Was headed to store at roughly 1900 hours on January 14th, 2016. As I was creating a hill in my neighborhood and started to descend I noticed an uneven string of orange lights in the sky. I immediately stopped and rolled my window down so that I could take video. I followed the lights and tried to zoom in. As the lights moved across the sky, I attempted to follow them in my car, but when I reached a road to head North on u pursuit they all suddenly vanished at the same time. They had a drifting like lotion, with 2 out ahead of roughly 3-4 others and followed by 1 straggler. They almost had a Chinese lantern like feel, but were too large (in my estimation) and they were also moving at a faster rate of speed than one would expect a paper lantern to float through the sky. They made absolutely no sound whatsoever
