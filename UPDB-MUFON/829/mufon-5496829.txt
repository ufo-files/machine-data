We noticed a star-like light in the sky but it's movement was highly unusual. We were walking home in our neighborhood when we noticed a very bright star. At first we thought it was a star or plane but then we took a couple of steps and the light tracked us very quickly across the sky. We took a few more steps and it tracked us again, moving across large areas of space almost instantly. The object then moved away at high speeds, blinked, and disappeared in plain sight. We were skeptical at first, but the rate of speed which the object moved across the sky was difficult to understand. The object left us confused and even afraid.
