We were visiting with some friends on our backyard patio when one of my friends indicated a bright light travelling towards us from the east.  It was moving at a constant rate of speed (not excessivley fast) - and then made a turn to the north.  After a minute it began gaining altitude and disappeared towards space.  It was dusk and the light was a very bright white.
