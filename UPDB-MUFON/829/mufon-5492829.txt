{cak} was star gazing with a friend and and an object drifted over head from east to west was blocking out the stars some very dark features apparent. no sound no lights just blocked out a swath of the stars. very clear skies no clouds before or after,

a friend and I were on the roof of my house star gazing very clear night.and from the east we saw the stars being blocked out by a moving object as it got over head shape and size (hard to tell altitude) became apparent. but i can say this about the size it was at least two or three fists held arms length wide. it blocked out a swath of stars.we did not know what to think of it at the time we got excited went down and told my mother we were not, to say shook up but excited.over the years thinking about it I assumed it was military considering we were next to lackland air force base in san antonio, but now i know it could not have been. when we saw it we watched it move from east to west at a steady rate slowly over head then the stars came back as it went out of sight guessing twenty to thirty degrees above the horizon.
