UFO sighting with specific observations and documentation of incident immediately following

After viewing HANGAR 1 I decided to submit my personal experience to MUFON.

On 25 March, 1989 at 2355 (11:55 PM) I stepped outside, looked up, and observed bright six-sided orange lights traveling in a northwest direction. These lights illuminated steadily, neither pulsating, flickering, or blinking. The sighting lasted approximately 5 seconds, being ended when my field of vision was block by the roof as the object passed overhead. This sighting appeared to be of one object at low altitude rather than seven objects in formation at high altitude. The object appeared to be traveling approximately four or five times faster than a light aircraft at low altitude (2000 - 2500 feet). Absolutely no sound accompanied this sighting.

I distinctly remember making the following observations during this sighting:
 1. The clarity of the orange lights and surprise at being able to clearly observed the shape of each light so distinctly (there was no 
    diffusion of light - the lights did not glow.
 2. The shape of the object would have been a perfect "V" but for the absence of one light.
 3. The noticeable lack pf sound emanating from this object as it passed directly overhead.
 4. The speed at which this object traveled.

Immediately pursuant to this sighting I called the local police to report what I'd seen. They recommended I notify Andrews Air Force Base which I did. 

Personal background:

1. A 20 year retired Navy enlisted veteran whose career field was Cryptologic Technician with a TS/SCI security clearance.
2. A retired Federal employee after a 13 year career at the National Security Agency (NSA) as a Senior Telecommunications Officer with a TS/SCI security clearance.
3. A retired Computer Sciences Corporation (CSC) employee after an 8 year career as a contractor to the National Security Agency as a System Architect/Principle Leader and Test Manager.
