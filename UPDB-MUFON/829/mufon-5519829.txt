The object was white rectangular, then object disappeared right in front of my eyes, white blur as the object disappeared. Acceleration was 1 of second.

Location: Naples-aerial location was north of the Naples airport.
Approx date & time: Nov of 2009, 10:30 am, very clear morning after a cool front moved in, visibility unlimited and no clouds.
Observation:
While driving home from morning errands, drove into Lakewood Blvd., very slow speed limit of 25mph, I observed three objects in the sky, two where planes, very common occurrence, the third object was not. I saw the object as clear as the planes but the object lacked wings and airspeed was negligible.
The object was white, very white if that makes any sense, rectangular and on the surface of the object where some geometrical shapes, like rectangular with a slight shadow from the angle of the sun on the whole object. After noticing this object was not a plane, balloon or space station (way too close for that), I stopped the car got out and the object disappeared right in front of my eyes after another 15 seconds, without a blink. The distinct effect I saw was a white blur as the object disappeared. I would say the acceleration happened 1/8 of second. Absolutely incredible! I feel 100% that this wasn1t a human observation error. I am a believer of scientific evidence and thinking process.

Please let me know if anyone has seen such an object.
