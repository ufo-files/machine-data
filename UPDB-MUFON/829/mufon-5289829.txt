Cross like star

I went outside and looked up like i usually do to look around the sky, and saw a light to the south that originally looked like a plane, we see them all the time in the area, but this one stopped and hovered for about 30 seconds. Then changed direction and came north towards me for about 10 seconds, then changed direction and went back east. The light that it gave off looked star like, and it does in my video, but to my eyes, it looked like light in the shape of a cross just gliding across the sky. It seemed to pulse randomly and get dimmer and brighter a few times, but eventually lost it in my eye sight.
