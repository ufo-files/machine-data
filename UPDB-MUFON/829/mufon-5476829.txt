{jpg} Super bright orb appeared over Gulf of Mexico off Galveston Island. Silent, steady course, seemed to shift shape in video I took (possible focus adjustment), low parh, went behind building and never saw it again. Video/still shots, not recognizable

On 3rd floor balcony facing south, at baymont Inn and Suites. Was using sky map app to identify a star. Bright light distracted me, looked south, southwest and saw extremely bright light coming onshore from gulf of Mexico probably 100-200 feet above water. When closer and no sound, no pattern or blinking light, switched to camera and 2 quick pictures. Then video. It was silent, brighter than anything I've ever seen in the sky, seemed to shift shape momentarily about at the shore, and then straight path took it behind another building and it never was visible again. This was not a helicopter or airplane. Don't know if crash, land, stealth, cloak or something else. The video and pics are clear evidence and also edited one to see shape by eliminating all other light sources and using negative effect to "see object ". It's clearly not a human machine.
