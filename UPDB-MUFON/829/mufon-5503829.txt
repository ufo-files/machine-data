{dr}Dark triangular object with red flashing lights on base of triangle

I was driving home from work and saw two blinking red lights in the sky and thought that it was a low plane because the dupage county airport is nearby. Then I thought it was two planes flying side by side. I pulled off on a side street and put my window down and stuck my head out and noticed the dark outline of the triangular figure. It looked almost like a B2 stealth bomber. I couldn't see the whole figure against the dark sky but I could see the flashing red lights on the base of the triangular figure. The object made no noise as it flew slowly northwest. I lost sight of the object behind the trees and houses after about 3 minutes.
