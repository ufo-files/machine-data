This may have been a large aerostat

This may have been a military aerostat but I travel this road several days per week and have not seen it in the past. I only saw it for about 10 seconds while driving westbound on Route 101. Though it was stationary when I saw it, I lost it in the trees and could not find it again though the avenues of observation should have allowed it. It was not the moon as I could see the moon as well. The sky was clear and there were no other objects in the air. My assessment is that it is terrestrial but it was very large and not a normal appearance.
