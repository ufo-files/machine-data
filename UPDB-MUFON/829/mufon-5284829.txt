I was in the park watching birds with my binoculars and I was looking at some birds circling on the thermals above me. I noticed that there were two white spheres that initially appeared to also be circling with the birds but they had no wings so I tried adjusting the focus of my binoculars to see if they were different birds that were perhaps higher in the sky but they were definitely spheres. They stopped circling and moved to hovering next to one another, then they shot off in opposite directions, hovered a greater distance apart from each other, and then simply faded away. Importantly, I was able to see the birds without looking through my binoculars but I couldn't see the spheres at all without my binoculars.
