three dull orange lights in a v shape

My neighbor and I were outside with a campfire in her backyard. She was telling me they were getting new eaves on the house. We were looking up at it when three dull orange lights in a V shape went over the house, South to North. The lights did not remain consistently apart, but the central light moved first west, closer to the one, then east closer to the other. All were simultaneously moving. As they went maybe 1/4 mile, they came closer together, all three, and then disappeared. No sound. The fact they were dull or blurry looking didn't occur to me until later. It was a crystal clear night and all the stars were very clear and sharp. This was not a plane and the moving of the location of the light was not due to perspective. I have a Master's degree in fine art and know what perspective does to things in location as they move.
