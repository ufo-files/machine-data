{jpg} It looked like an upsidedown acorn

My family and I were the returning from store when we noticed this glowing light in the sky that was moving erratically and it caught my eye. I told my son to get my binoculars and camera and I started to take photos and a video. I decided to call the police and informed them of what a I saw, about 20 minutes later they arrived and saw it but said it was just a weather balloon and asked if they could take my camera and I told them no. as they left my house and went across the street they took are neighbors camera and never returned it. Later on in the week I went to my camera to look at the photos and my camera hasn't turned on since but I still have the memory card and found the photos.
