When I was 10 years old I had missing time

There was apparently over 500 witnesses that saw the craft in the sky that night in Perth, Western Australia. My brother was training for soccer at the time and his whole team saw the craft. I remember it flying directly towards me and then I have no memory of it departing. When I went inside the front door, my family told me that I was missing for at least 15 minutes and that the neighbour had come past where I said I was sitting but I was not there (right near the front door). I remember the object appearing fairly close to me, spinning with various lights flashing and changing colours.
