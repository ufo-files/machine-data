Cylindrical object in night sky

We were driving home from visiting family heading south on IN Highway 11 when I spotted an object in the sky. It appeared to be traveling northbound but changed direction to the northwest as we got closer. It then appeared to hover over a cornfield near the Jackson County/Bartholomew County line. It also rapidly dropped altitude as we got closer to it to maybe 200 feet or less. I asked my wife if there was a tower in that area and she stated no. It was large and had lights shining from the underneath. I saw multiple colored lights. It had 1 main bright light and then multicolored lights around it. My wife was driving and wasn't able to get as good a look as I did but she said it looked like green rays of light were shining down with red lights in front of them. She thought it was the size of a helicopter however it made no noise.
