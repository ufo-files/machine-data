{td} Random photo taken of sun and noticed object when looking at photos later

I was at the pool at the ritz Carlton Orlando grand lakes and the sky was virtually cloudless. It was around 3:30 and I just took a random photo of the sun aiming my camera without looking. Later on that day I was looking at the photo and noticed what appeared to be a purple disc like object with a beam shining and what looks like a window. I had taken a few other photos of the sky at the same time but this was the only where the object appeared. I am also including the other photos of the sky to emphasize how clear the day was.
