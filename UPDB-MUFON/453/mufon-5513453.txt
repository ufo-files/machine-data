very bright light (star-like) red coloured cross the sky from N to S-E/E.

I was checking my mail box at the entrance of the building where I live when I noticed a very bright red light crossing the sky. My wife was in the balcony at the 3rd floor and I immediatel told her to watch the sky.
She immediately saw the light. I took my smartphone and start recording a video (poor quality). When the light went no more visible to me I asked my wife to keep follow the light in order to report me later what it have do (change directions, change color or other, but it did not).
The light hide under some clouds, after it crossed the sky from W to E/SE.
I did not know what it could be. Im totally sure its not a plane since there is civil airport at few km from where I live and I KNOW how airplane are in the daylight and in the night. Maybe it could be a meteo baloon but my supposition is only becouse I do not know meteo baloon and I don't how how they are. It was very fast to be a wind-moved ballon : it seemed to be some sort of vehicle.
