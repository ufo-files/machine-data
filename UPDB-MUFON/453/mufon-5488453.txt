{nd} I saw a UFO

In the summer of 1984 myself and a girl I new (Jackie)were in a field at Tom Sawyer state park at about 10:00 PM (Dark) when off in the distance we saw an object coming our way. The object approached us quickly and passed us directly over head. It was directly over head, and continued on a path away from us. It was moving very fast and not making a sound. it was very large close to the size of a football field. What stood out was the sight of about 30 to 50 white speres thought were bouncing along behind the craft. This sight has been in my memory since the night I saw it. also a Jefferson county police car came flying into the parking lot after the craft had passed us. The whole event lasted 15 to 20 seconds and the craft must have covered many miles in the time of our first sighting and it moving out of sight. I have wanted to report my sighting for many years and never got around to it. I know this happened many years ago and most likely will not help in your investigations. I believe the local news reported that a Russian satellite had passed over Louisville that night. What I witnessed was no satellite, it passed directly over my head, I could not of seen it any better, it ws very big.
