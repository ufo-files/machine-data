Unusual occurance in broad day light.

It was approximately 1740 and my spouse and I were out for a bike ride. We were waiting for the crosswalk light to change so that we could proceed across the street. We were looking up at the moon and noticed this object. We weren't sure if it was a plane at first,and it didn't look like any we had seen before. It appeared as if it slowly moving then hovered. It shot straight up in the air, and back down again, we looked at each other as if we were unsure if we had actually seen what we did. We watched it for another minute and it shot to the right and then straight a head and out of our vision. It was shiny with what looked like blue on it. There was no sound, no emissions. It lasted for about 1 or 2 minutes. We couldn't believe we were seeing this in broad daylight. I went to take a picture with my phone, but by the time I got it out, the object was too far away, and it was gone! We were in awe and in disbelief, we had to look at each other and ask each other if we were really seeing what we were seeing.
