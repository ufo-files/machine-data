I saw a star like object at first then it became a large glowing orb

On Jan. 11, 2012 I went into my backyard with my dogs at 6:52am so they could do their business. I always look up into the sky looking for planes or possibly a ufo. I never was lucky enough to see anything until this morning. I was looking in the eastern sky and saw what I thought was a star at first. Then I noticed it was moving in a southeast direction. So I thought it was a plane, but it didn't have any port or starboard lights. It had been moving for about 6 to 7 seconds when all of a sudden it changed into a very big glowing orb. The size change was about 20 times its orignal size when I first saw it. It was still heading southeast when it disappeared. The whole sighting lasted 15 - 20 seconds. I kept watching for a while after that. But it never re-appeared. It was the most exciting expirence Ive ever had except for when I was in the Navy in the South China Sea Gulf of Tonkin in 1973. Thats when I saw my first ufo. I had been trained to spot all kinds of planes and I was on watch between 00:00 and 04:00. I spotted a bright white light doing a zig zag pattern then it shot across the sky zig zaged again and shot back where it had started and then shot off into space and disappeared. Thats why I know what I saw was not a plane on Jan. 11, 2012
