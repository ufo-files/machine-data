Moved slowly. With binoculars I saw teal colored round objects connected together to another white object, maybe 2 white objects. Changed forms.

We live in Euless, Texas, a suburb of Dallas. 
My husband was (Commercial Pilot) sitting outside on phone and noticed a white cloudy object in the sky and watched it moving for about 10 minutes while on phone. He realized it couldn’t be a plane, air balloon or balloons tied together. He went in house to tell me he thinks he sees a ufo. I took my phone with me and recorded it. When I zoomed close the object was not steady. My hand was steady, but object kept moving back & forth. Through phone, it was just like a white moving cloud. In person, I could see a white object changing forms, moving a little slower than a plane would that high. Husband took a zoomed picture with ipad. I grabbed binoculars and saw something I had never seen before. It was about 8 teal circles connected together and a black dot where they connected. They were all connected to one, maybe two white object that were bigger than the circles. They didn’t appear to be round. When they changed shapes they remained connected to the white object(s). I felt something incredible because it was not normal to see something like this. We both knew we had witnessed something incredible, like maybe a ufo, but still were not sure as we thought it was only in movies.
