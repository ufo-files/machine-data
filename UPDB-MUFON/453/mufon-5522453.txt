Floating in my pool. Saw what looked like cloud. Had defined shape. Like stingrays rear half. Vanished .

I was in my back yard, floating in my pool. A hot summer afternoon, clouds were of course forming. I saw something between two of these clouds, which seemed to be a cloud itself, but it had a more defined shape. It was, as best I can describe it, like the rear half of a stingray. (Triangle with a long point.) I watched it for less than a minute, I guess. The clouds on either side continued building and morphing. The "object" vanished in a way I would describe as similar to "cloaking" ships seen on television.
