Strange stationary light flashing randomly then disappearing

It was around 6pm sunset at Aardvark beach near Coyote Point, San Mateo, CA. Facing north past Oakland I saw a bright light low in the sky, thinking nothing of it at first because SF airport and others are nearby and it's a busy area for air traffic. It was flashing like a plane would, but after observing it with my full attention I realized it wasn't moving. My gut told me to take out my phone and start recording but this thing was really far away and I thought it was a plane. I couldn't see an outline of whatever it was. It was very bright and quite far from the airport. Plane lights at that distance would be very small, this was maybe 15-20 times the size, and very bright. The flashing became random. not consistent with that of a plane. Hard to tell how far away it was, maybe 10 miles or more from where I was standing. At least over 6k feet in elevation,( I could be wrong) it was stationary next to a cloud relative to where I was standing. , after observing it for about 20 seconds it stopped flashing. Another 20 seconds or so before it suddenly disappeared. It didn't turn around or fly off, it just vanished. I knew the spot where it disappeared in the sky because of the cloud. I kept my eyes fixed in that area thinking maybe the light would turn around of come back, but nothing happened. There was nothing there.
