{cak} orbs circling then stopped and disappeared like eraser on blackboard

I had just arrived home from picking up my son from elementary school at 3:30pm. My son got out of the car and looked up and asked me "what is that" I looked up and saw an airliner in the distance and I said "that is an airplane" he responded "no not the airplane, that" he pointed away from the airliner and I saw 5 orbs circling each other. 2 orbs were white and 3 were silver. after a short while they stopped in mid air and then they began to disappear as though they were being erased from a black board. distance was about 200 feet linear and about 6 stories above the houses on our street. my son is 6 and I am 69. probably not good witnesses. just reporting what we saw.

can't tell you more because total event time about 2 min. i was in awe and feel dumbfounded. my son just went about his routine and it did not seem to be anything out of the ordinary.
