Seemed to be inside a small cloud and radiated yellow light. Then the light seemed to dim

I was returning home from school headed north on Peroia street and turned west onto Mississippi avenue. As I headed west i spotted a small cloud that seemed to be glowing in the top left corner. The glow was a yellow almost like that of a wooden pencil. My Dad also saw the cloud and said that it seemed to get dimmer. I didn't get a chance to see if it moved or not but it didn't move while I was watching it.
