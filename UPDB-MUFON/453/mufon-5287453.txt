I was out front of the house and my friends called to me and said come look at this and there it was on it's way.

I was about 12-13 years old and it was summer. My mom, dad and I were visiting our neighbours 2 houses down and it was just a normal visit as always. Food, beer, playing on the street with my friends. A friend came out front and frantically called to me to come and see this thing. I ran to the backyard and everyone was gathered at the back fence hanging on and stretching out looking to their right. Behind our houses was our elementary school and yard. No houses and dim lighting so it's quite dark. There is a cat-walk that extends the whole length of the track of homes. I got to the fence and looked to my right and watched a small disk like object heading towards us as if following the cat-walk. It moved slow and steady. It reached us and we all had a front row seat. I could've easily peddled a bike faster than it was moving. Colourful lights surrounding its brim and metal. Nothing special apart from it was not a dream. It travelled the whole length of the cat-walk and that was it. I remember turning to face the house to look and see my parents wondering should I run inside and tell them to come and look but I didn't want to miss a thing. Some years later we were all chilling and I brought it up and asked if anyone remembered. All the guys said no but Nonie said, "Michael, I remember". I was so relieved to know I wasn't the only one who remembered.
