My friend an I were camping in the Massanutten mountain range when I noticed what first appeared to be a small private plane traveling from the south to the north just to the west of me. As I observed the object, it moved erratically up and down as it traveled in a northerly direction. I at first thought perhaps the plane was hitting serious turbulence but then realized that if indeed it was, the distance involved in the up and down movement would have either destroyed a small plane or at the very least kill the occupants. 
 We then saw the craft continue this way north almost out of sight and then heard saw, and then moments later heard, a jet traveling in the same direction. About the time we heard the jet, we noticed the object now traveling north and to the east of us.
 After carefully evaluating what I witnessed, it is my opinion that there is no way any man made craft that I know of could have maneuvered in that manner.
