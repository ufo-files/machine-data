UFO southern Oklahoma. A circle inside a circle, glowing yellow

Okay, so My husband and I were lying in the back of the truck on 6/1 about 10:30 pm watching the sky like we do when we notice this ball of light floating across the sky pretty fast. Once we noticed it it slows down right infront of our gaze to an almost stop and slowly began this dim to very bright yellow light glowing thing and I know it sounds wild but almost as to say “I see you” then went dim again. Oh it’s not over, then it turns around and goes past us the other direction and at this point I’m out of my shock and am trying to find my phone to record. This is the aftermath.
