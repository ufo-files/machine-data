{nd} I lay down on the grass to relax and I immediately saw this craft directly above me. It did not have the shape of an airplane and flickered constantly. It seemed to hover, then steadily moved southwest. During that time, it constantly flickered.

For some reason, I felt compelled to lie down in the freshly mowed grass at my parents' house on a beautiful October afternoon. As I looked up at the sky directly above me, I saw this object and immediately could tell that it was not an airplane. (A couple of airplanes flew by during this time and this object behaved and looked nothing like them.)

At times, I wondered if it was a star, but it moved too fast through the sky and was too bright and changed colors too often to be a star.

It was highly reflective (mostly white/silver light at first)and seemed to move slowly toward the south/southwest. I called my partner over to look at it and he agreed that it appeared odd. We each saw it change colors several times and there seemed to be a grey cloud surrounding it. At one point it turned an electric blue-green color (that was beautiful) and eventually as it moved further southwest, we could no longer see it.

I was pretty much in awe of the experience and I feel that the operators of this object/craft wanted it to be seen.
