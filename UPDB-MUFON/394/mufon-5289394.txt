A bright light hovering over London changing direction very fast

I was working in Fulham in January 2020
I was filming the view from on top of a building when it was still dark early in the morning . A bright star like shape was travelling fast in different directions over London . Then there was a loud bang . Then it disappeared. The video photo I have put on here works wen you hold your finger down on screen and after a couple of seconds you hear a loud bang .
