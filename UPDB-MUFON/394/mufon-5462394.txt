Hovering Ufo Beaverbank, Nova Scotia.

Me and a friend were on our way to explore an abandoned RCAF radar base in beaver bank nova scotia when we spotted this object hovering down the road from us! I thought it was the top of a cell tower or something but we realized it had no base, it was just hovering. We then saw a couple of flashes beside it and instantly started recording. we were exhilarated! 
we would have pulled over and gotten a longer clip of the object but we were being slightly tailgated and didn't have a great place to pull off too. when arriving at the entrance to the base we noticed a no trespassing sign which means it was private property for sure, however, we took the factor that we are in the middle of nowhere into account and decided to head in regardless. after gearing up and getting ready to check it out a car pulled up behind us and told us to leave. I doubt that this has anything to do with the ufo but knowing it is a retired RCAF radar base is a little weird.
