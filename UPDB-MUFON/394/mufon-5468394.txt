{rjl} Strange Melted Aluminum found

In January 0f 2012 my friends and were repairing a off road buggy up in the mountains near my home. I found what appeared to be melted Aluminium. Back then I thought it strange then because there didn't appear to be any kind of crash or fire any where in the area. Let alone enough fuel to make a fire hot enough to melt Aluminum.
Fast forward several years and I was watching a UFO show where a ufo witness had discovered metal that he had seen ejected from the craft he witnessed. My mind immediately went back to the metal we had discovered in the mountains because it looked almost identical to what I had found. Not having anymore of the metal we originally found I decide to go back and see if I can find more.

I had my son drive me up to the site in his 4x4. I found more of the metal. All of witch was found where it appeared to have been dropped in a molten state(see pictures). Now I found several different types of metal this time one of which had some sort of screw embedded in it wile it was molten. This was sort of a let down for me thinking that it was from something known.

I had spent several days trying to find some sort of fiery crash in the area ever but was unable. I put the parts into a box and forgot about them until recently when I found them wile cleaning. I had been wanting to show someone in the know on the subject but had never gotten around to it until now.
I'm sure it's probably nothing. But there is that part of me that wonders.
I know this isn't related to a sighting of a fling object. But i figured if anyone would know someone interested in checking out what I found it would be you guys.
Thanks for taking the time to humor me by reading this and looking at my pictures.
