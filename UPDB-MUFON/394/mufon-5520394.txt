Was watching a jet fly when suddenly, a white light appeared and charged the jet extremely fast. Missing it from above, it halted almost immediately and hovered. It soon disappeared.

Was watching the skies as usual while the family was driving us to go eat, focusing on a jet at the time. It was almost directly above me when I saw a white light suddenly appear in front of it. It accelerated with starting speed and missed the jet from above, slowing and halting in probably 1 second flat. It hovered as I sat bewildered. I wanted to get a size comparison of the light itself and the jet, but at the time the jet had moved on behind the roof of the car, so I had to crane my neck to see it. By the time I looked back to find the light, it had disappeared and I didn't see it again afterwards. From my memory, it was probably either the same size as the jet, or a little over half the size.
