{jpg} What we first thought to be a large amount of bright stars begin to fall from different points in the sky into a straight line formation and take off.

50 to 80 star like objects fell from above into a straight line formation. Other star like objects came from further away to catch up to the formation. 6-10 star like craft came from what seemed to be far away to join the fleet. Then blasted off into space at the speed of light. Leaving one craft that floated from our far left in the sky to far right at a slow speed then hovered for several minutes before vanishing over the Mtn top just as a very distinguishable air plain flew into sight. We witnessed the bright orbes brighten up then dim to a dull light. It was truly the most amazing thing I've ever seen!!!
