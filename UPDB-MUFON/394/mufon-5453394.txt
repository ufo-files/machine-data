String of about 12 lights that appeared star-like and moving across sky in straight line

My wife was looking up towards the big dipper as we were walking. She noticed a vary bizarre string of about a dozen lights that appeared to be stars. The odd thing was that they were all in a perfectly straight line at various intervals and they were moving from Northwest to Southeast very fast. I was able to get my phone out and videotape part of the movement. I caught the tail end of the lights as they disappeared. They had to be moving very fast to cross the entire sky in one minute or less.
