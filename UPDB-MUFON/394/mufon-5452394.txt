Silver reflective sphere

Was having lunch and just happened to look up across the St. John's river to see a silver sphere punch through the clouds leaving a trail where clouds were disturbed. Object was moving incredibly fast and was out of sight within a second or two, heading towards the Atlantic ocean. Object left disturbance in clouds.
