Unusual bright white shaped light

Outside observing sky on clear night. 30% moon rising in the west. Facing SE at 120 deg. at est. 50 deg angle over fixed point - tree. At ~21:35, a very bright white "wink" caught my attention, appeared to have a shape, triangular, much larger than the stars in view. I locked in on the location, approx. 5 seconds later, another "wink", exact same location. Continued to observe; approx. 40 min later at ~22:05, witnessed the exact same "wink" at 180 deg. SE, but appeared to illuminate a bit more suggesting maybe object was closer. angle, trajectory in sky was about the same at 50 deg. Starred intently in the S / SE locations for another 30 minutes with no additional observation; sky was cluttered with light pollution and moon to the west. This was quite unusual, I spend a lot of time starring into the sky, never seen anything like this before. The brightness of the light stood out; like laser white, and shape plus the time and movement between observations.
