{dr}Two red/orange, triangle lights, no sounds (motor/engine) hovering .

My husband was out walking the dog by the county fairgrounds in Santa Rosa when he called me and told me to go outside and look in the sky. He said that two red/orange, triangle lights, no sounds (motor/engine)appeard out of no where.  When I got outside, in front of my house I saw both lights side by side hovering above my house. Then one light slowly moved north in an upward direction till out of site. The 2nd light then moved slowly NE upwards till out of site. I have no idea of what they could have been. They seemed to be to close together to be normal aircraft. The where no motor or engine sounds.
