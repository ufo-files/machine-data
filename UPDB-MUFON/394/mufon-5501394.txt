{dr}UFO flew over house and disappears

I was sitting on my back porch watching the 4th of July fireworks in the area when my father and I spotted the object. It had a red glowing upper portion and what looked like a yellow orange fireball underneath. It reminded me of a paper lunch bag. with a candle in it but that's as close as I can relate it to a known object. It flew from north east to south west.. When I first noticed it the object looked about 500 feet away from me and was a couple of hundred feet in the air. It was hard to estimate the size of the object but my father who is a trained pilot estimated it at about 30 feet across. From my vantage point the object appeared to be vertical in shape with the red portion on top. At first I thought it was an errant or stray firework from down the street but I quickly realized it was nothing of the sort. It was moving slowly over the house without changing direction and it made no sound at all.. after it flew slowly over the house it continued in the same direction at the same speed. Once it was about a mile past the house it appeared to ascend then it disappeared. Later last night. after the sighting I was listening to Coast to Coast A.M. and a gentleman from South Carolina phoned into the radio show at about 4 in the morning to report an identical object he saw there last night flying fro the same direction and flying in the same direction.
