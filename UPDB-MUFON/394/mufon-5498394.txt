Went out with dog as my daughter got on her bus. Bus pulled off. I looked up as usual. The was a silver craft with the sun reflecting off of it. No sound at all. It came from the east heading west. Kind of wobbled a little, like from side to side. Had a pale pinkish light underneath that was a continuous light. It was closer than they usually get. Like maybe 200 feet above the tree. It looked to be about the size of a small car. It was oval shaped. The sun was really bouncing off making it shine like polished silver. I stepped in the door to grab my camera, like 2 seconds and it was gone when I got back out. They always go straight up and disappear soon as they get right above me. It feels like they are teasing me. I could not get a pic this time. It was different from the ones I usually see.
