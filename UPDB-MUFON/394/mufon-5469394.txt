{td} Looks like Skywriter or jet entrail

Was driving car North to South on Franklin Avenue from Kingsland to Centre Street. At approx 100 degrees N to South angle from North horizon saw what looked like a sky writer entrail but very short. (80 to 40 if you limit to 90 degrees like in the form above) Over approx 4-6 minutes it moved south across the sky slowly approx 40 degrees and appeared further on the Southern Horizon.

Assumed this was possibly related to the renetry of the Shenzhou Chinese space station as most likely.

Next likely would be an actual jet or plan entrail that just happened to be short but moved faster than usual across the sky perhaps due to jet stream.

Also perhaps related to reentry of humanity star satellite or maybe just a meteor that broke up.

Reporting as this may help astronomers determine the path of Shenzhou or Humanity star on their re-entries to the Earth's atmosphere using this information. DId not take a picture.... other's probably did at other points along the path.

I never lost sight, and consider this an astronomical observation. No emotion except the same I get everytime I look at the sky, but was hopeful this will prove useful to science.

No surprising motion, except the speed seemed a little faster than normal for an entrail thus it might be orbiting debris... and thus part of Shenzhou or Humanity star as they come down to earth.
