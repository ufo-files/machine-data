{nd} On our way home from school summer and there it was over a building.

My friend and I where coming home from high school in June of 1977. We where both seniors it was around 11:30 AM we started to come down the hill in our village ( Wappingers Falls N.Y.) to our right was the Library beautiful building very old and there it was the Jupiter 2 from Lost in Space,we started flipping out and then it started to move the same way we where going we stayed with it for only a short time and then lost it. We looked at each other and said don't bother to tell anyone they won't believe us. I'm 57 years old and never told anyone,it's been 22 years since I seen my friend,we new each other since the 6 grade we where best friends we went to all our proms togethere he married his high school sweetheart. Well that's our story.
