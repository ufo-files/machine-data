4 lights 3 bright white 1 red very slow moving very low no noise after attention was drawn to it it took off.

On 16 November 2011 in the process of moving to a new house down the road at 8:45 ish pm Counter part and myself seen a car sitting on a twp road then we noticed a object with 4 lights 3 white one red in a triangle formation either hovering or moving extremely slow. As we pulled into the entrance to Mid-Ohio race car course the object began to move. It moved between us and the twp road. To me when the object seemed to notice it was drawing attention to itself it accelerated off quickly. We chased it down the road then it disapeared. After picking up another counter part we seen the object again moving very slowly across the sky. Object then did a real quick u-turn and proceeded back over mid-ohio race course track. It seemed to be drawn to this spot. This was the last we seen of it. During this time there was no sound coming from object.
