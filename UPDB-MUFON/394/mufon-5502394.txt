{nd} UFO sighting in Melrose Park, IL

It was a nice day to throw a party for my young nephew. Here we were, outside, nighttime, and having a great time with the family out in the backyard. When suddenly, I hear one of my family members call my name to show me a mysterious object in the sky. Soon after, most of my family members gathered into a small crowd and stared at the object. We were all freaking out. Questioning this odd and weird object. All of a sudden, while we kept watching, it started to hover around. It was moving upward and a bit downward. This went on for at least three or so minutes. It was blue, green and red. The lights kept changing, blinking, flickering, and I can't even explain it now. We had never seen anything like this. This was not a plane. It was unlike anything I've ever seen before. Even my brother-in-law who is easy to explain things and distinguish whether it was a plane or something else, couldn't find a simple explanation. My whole family was shocked yet intrigued. It then descended and disappeared. My little cousin was scared. I had to comfort her. This is what happened last night. I read a person's UFO sighting on here from Zion, Illinois. He saw something similar to what I did. I'll never forget it. Thanks for reading.
