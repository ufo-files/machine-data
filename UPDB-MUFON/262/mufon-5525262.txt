*Two silver discs flying low to the ground

Two silver discs flying along close to the ground....(we could view it from our van out the driver's side...while driving towards OKC)...(going east)...husband saw it first and we watched it quickly change direction and went straight up quickly....could no longer see it...as it went from view very fast...but husband stuck his head out the window to look up...and saw a vapor trail that was being left behind as the two spheres ascended into the sky....(it was yellowish in color) (we had been down to the Texas state line to visit family...and it was mother's day...and we were returning home in the wee hours of the morning....and that was a Sunday...we were shocked to see something so strange and foreign to us....I wondered if it was some kind of new ammunition (hah) being tested....as they were round, shiny, and like giant dishes...flying parallel to the ground...then they suddenly rose and flew as if towards 2 o'clock..(to illustrate using a clock...as I do not know degrees)..lost sight of the objects as we were driving down the highway and it was necessary to stop looking (for my husband) in order to maintain control of the van...it looked to be flying from a westerly direction...over open fields which had small trees on them....and maybe livestock...and then it ascended as it got into a populated (housing) addition...it was in the edge of a town, but out in the country.they looked to be at least a quarter of a mile from where we were driving...but we were the only ones on the highway at the time, since it was early morning...and we had come from a virtually unpopulated area....brushy terrain, no homes...wide open spaces..
