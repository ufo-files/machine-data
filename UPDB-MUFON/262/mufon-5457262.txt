{jpg} UFO was above for minutes. Left in a second

My brother and I were waiting for our cousins to arrive from Mexico. The street was very quiet. No cars no people. We looked up and saw it. It was just there, just above the tree covering our heads. My brother told me to hide so I hid under the managers station wagon. My brother was between a wall and a trashcan. He got a better view of it. I tried to sneak a peak and my brother told me to not look at it. Then he told me it was gone and let's go to the house. 
Black little helicopter came from down the street and stopped and turned they're spotlight on the playground next door. Big apartments next to us. Big playground.
