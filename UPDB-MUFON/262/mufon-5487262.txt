{nd} Couldn't describe its Something I've never see in my life

I was leaving home with my mother around 8:30 am when I see a plane pass by on final approach to the airport, when I looked up I see a bright, shine metallic kind of object and I asked myself what could that be, I started looking at it and I realize that's neither a star nor a plane so kept looking, my mother was in the car and I said to her "mom get out of the car and look at this" but by the time she is out of the car the object had disappear. At that moment the sighting only lasted a few secs so we got in the car and left. When we get back to the house a little after 11 am I look up again and there it is only this time my mother goes back into the house and get the binoculars. I looked up and I finally see the object triangular with three white glowing lights under and then changing shape and position but still static, however in my mothers opinion it looks like a cilinder, she at some point sees a red light. We kept looking at the object and for the next hour and 10 mint but during that time my father comes and also witness the event. After sometime we lost sight of the object because it got cloudy but it moved and saw it again for like 10 more min. The clouds rolled in again just to lose sight of but at the end a saw it one last time but with big difference that this time there were two instead of one just to disappear between the clouds. To be honest I don't know what to think of it but it was really impressive since is the first time something like that happens to me.
