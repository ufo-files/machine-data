{rjl} Dim lit orb that flashed VERH bright at random (no pattern) shot out beams or flames on both sides then disappeared.

On 8/19/17 I was on my front porch facing East in Clayton NC on a clear night at 9pm. On any given night I see airplanes , both civilian and military , helicopters and satellites passing overhead. Directly in font of me over some trees I saw a dim lit orb, then it pulsed to a very bright orb. It did this 4 or 5 times without any pattern, maybe once every 15 to twenty sceonds then approximate once a minute. It appearing to be stationary. I had no idea what this was as it did not have the characteristics of any plane or helicopter or satellite that I have ever seen. Then as it glowed very bright again it shot out beams of what I can only describe as white/ red flames out of both sides. They flared out approx 5 times the width of the orb then the orb went dim until it disappeared. I have seen satellites ,even the ISS pass overhead and at times glow bright as the solar panels reflect sunlight but this was not the same. It looked as if this orb was going to explode when the beams shot out of both sides. When they shot out it had almost the characteristic of intense electricity as the beams fizzled. When I saw this I felt very strange,almost dizzy. I'm not sure if it was because I have never seen anything remotely close to this and felt as if I was witnessing something extraordinary or if the energy affected me in some way. There was no sound until the energy beams came out and it sounded like static/electrical fizzle. The energy beams only lasted about 10 seconds but the entire sighting from start to finish was about 10 minutes. I remained outside for several hours but did not see it again.
