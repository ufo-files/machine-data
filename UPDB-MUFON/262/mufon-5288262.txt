Disc flying belly foward.

I was outside about 9 50 pm stargazing when I noticed a red and white blinking object approaching from the south-east . I'm a private pilot and this looked a bit odd so I retrieved my binoculars for a closer look. I live approximately 2 miles north of the airport and approach control for the military base Ft Sill, Ok. I was amazed to see the craft flying in a belly forward attitude and on the belly I saw a complete circle of lights. The lights (about 10 to 12) were strobing back and forth from red to white. It maintained a steady speed and course until it was out of sight to the north west about 5 to 7 minutes later. OK, now for the part I'm not 100% sure about. So the craft appeared to be a disk flying in a vertical configuration with the bottom of the craft aimed forward so the ring of lights was facing forward in the direction of travel. However, after the craft past and I was looking at the back side, I thought I could see cutouts of some sort that allowed me to still see portions of the blinging lights. About 5 minutes before my observation, I had been watching an army helicopter that although isn't unusual, it did seem to do a rather high altitude hover for several minutes before, as I watched, it made its approach and landed at Ft Sills airport.
