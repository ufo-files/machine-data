I saw a bright white egg shaped light moving rapidly across the sky.

I was relaxing in my friends pool. I was facing south with my back against the side of the pool. I was looking at lightening bugs in the backyard and then looked up and noticed a bright egg-shaped light. It was moving rapidly across the sky in a northeastern direction. It was gone in less than a minute. The object did not have any blinking lights and no wings. I was not sure what to make of it. After it was gone, I thought I saw something amazing. My wife and my friends were inside at the time of the event. There were no witnesses to my knowledge. I live near an airport and see aircraft everyday. I have never seen anything like this before. It was pretty spectacular.
