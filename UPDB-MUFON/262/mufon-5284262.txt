Three parallel lights in the sky. 2 joining together.

There were 3 lights in the sky, horizontally parallel and roughly equally spaced from each other from my vantage point. 1 of the lights was fainter than the other 2. The 2 brightest lights soon merged together which is when I realized these weren't stars, airplanes, radio tower lights, etc. Soon the fainter light disappeared although I did not see it depart. Then the brightest light (which was formerly 2 lights) began to move to the south and disappeared behind the silhouette of the trees. One thing to mention is that the Silver Spring Naval Surface Warfare Center is in the same direction that the lights were viewed. Not sure if this is connected.
