Black Triangle UFO Slow Hovering

I was outside in my yard on a full moon night looking at the stars. Was watching one particularly pretty, when I happened to look to the right and saw moving stars. Then I realized that that they were actually lights on a black triangle going to the right. A few were red lights and they were dimmed to the same brightness as the stars around it. I compared it to any planes or ships Ive ever seen. It was going too slow and too close to be a plane. Used to be a flight attendant so Ive been around a lot of planes. Tried to listen for sounds but it was hard to tell if it was emitting any because of the night sounds of the town. Then I ran inside to get my boyfriend, but I knew it might be gone into the fog by the time I returned. And it was.
