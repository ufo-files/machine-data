{dr}Candles with flickering Flames

It was late in the evening and I was about to cross the street, to Chelsea Piers; when something in the sky caught my eye, it was a candle with a flame in the sky. One flame, turned into two more candles that flickered as flames in the sky. The shape, had been taken as a tri-angle.
These candles had flames, that was floating above the Hudson River. There was no sound, and I could see the sky, in between the candles. There wasn't any solid, in between the candles, no metallics.
 The colors of the flames were orange, and this tri-angle floated north, above the river. The odd thing was that there was but one man whom had jogged pass me, before I saw the flames. And for a nice evening, there weren't other people around and no traffic to speak of. ( for New York, that's odd )
 Before I crossed the street to get closer to the water, I took out my flip phone. I knew the phone would light up when I opened it. I did this to communicate with the craft, but nothing happened, I got no response.
 The candles were about 300 feet above the water, the diameter of the were about 100 feet from each other, it was huge. 
 The flames, flickered softly as it floated up the Hudson. I can say that I was unafraid of what I saw, in fact I felt happy. 
The flames were of an orange color, and they just flickered softly as they floated north up the Hudson River. I watched it just continue up the river north bound.
 I was amazed, when I saw these candles in the sky. When I saw the one candle turn into two more candles and form the shape of a tri-angle I was elated. What was this thing? Just candles with flames on them floating over the river. These candles were uniform, in the shape of a tri-angle.
