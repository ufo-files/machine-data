A sphere of light that did not emit sound

Suddenly it disappeared and after a few seconds, it came towards me from the northwest and it passed right over me shining as powerful as a star .. I tried to photograph the object but the camera did not respond, and that was fully charged .. The object was Lost in the distance to the east and around 1min. He reappeared in the South, but in a static way; after a few minutes the object disappeared and appeared in the same direction but at different distances; Something out of this world.
