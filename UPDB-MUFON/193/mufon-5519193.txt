3 bright yellowish lights appear in night sky

Driving home tonight (4-25-12) from Savannah, GA to Augusta. We were past Statesboro heading towards Waynesboro in US 25 North. Head of us we saw 3 lights (above the tree line) ALL IN THE STRAIGHT ROW up in the night sky. They were bright yellow and glowing. It appeared like a jet coming into a landing. One light, then 2 lights and 3 lights. All lining up in a row. There are NO airports out here. Cotton fields and dark. Then one light shot off, 2nd cirlced around and took off and the 3rd one disappeared. I laughed and told my son I bet those were UFOs. We came home and searched internet and noticed others have seen "weird lights" in the same location.
