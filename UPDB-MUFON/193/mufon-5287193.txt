5 lights at an angle V-shaped with black outline

At 10:20pm on Tuesday April 2, 2019, driving westbound and stopped at a stoplight when I saw 5 bright, round, white-gold lights in a V-shape formation above the tree line of a small neighborhood. It looked like 2 lights on one side/angle, 1 on the tip/point, and 2 on the other side. Very bright, clear white lights. This formation was gliding low like an incoming plane, but we were not anywhere near close enough to the airport for it to be as low as it was. It made no sound. It was floating/gliding eastbound. I continued looking at it as the stoplight changed and I drove closer. The body of the object looked like a parallelogram-type shape, but it appeared as just a shadowy dark outline...I would almost describe it as matte black. There was definitely some sort of dark material/solid substance to the object that blocked out the stars. The object continued to move eastbound slowly, and before it was out of my sight as I was driving, I saw it stop very smoothly and hover in place over some trees.
