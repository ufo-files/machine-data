Silver Orb spotted outside Flagstaff

It was the size of a volleyball with a shiny silver surface. It came from the North over highway 89 southbound into Flagstaff. It flew overhead roughly between Milepost 431 and 430.

At first I thought it was a Mylar balloon until I noticed it didn't have a tarnished surface and there was no design on the surface. Before it crossed the traffic median and over Highway 89 North, it slowed down, made a J like turn and proceeded NE.

I was driving, so unfortunately I didn't take a picture.
