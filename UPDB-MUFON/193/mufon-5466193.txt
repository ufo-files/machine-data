{td} Bright light with short rays coming off, much larger than a star or plane. Stationary, then turned red and descended out of sight-line.

While watching TV, approx. 9 pm, I looked out of the back doors and saw a large bright light in the sky. It was much larger than a star or a plane and had short beams of light coming from it. It was stationary for around 20 min, during which time we went outside to look at it. It then turned red and descended out of sight-line.
