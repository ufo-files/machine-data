1993 I was in high school at the time and my friends and I were on a mountain top area of what they call a park near Rancho Buena Vista High School in Vista ,Ca.We had a getoblaster radio at the time light had no shine to it I mean like a headlight would.It was bright by not shining at anything if that makes sense.They were all side by side then as it got really close to us about 50 ft away above
