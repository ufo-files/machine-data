{nd} Orange glowing object floated across sky

On the night of July 4, 2014, about 45 minutes or so after the fireworks, I was sitting on my porch when all of a sudden, I noticed a pretty large Orange glowing object, floating across the sky. It came from the north/west, heading south/east. At first I thought it to be fireworks, but it wasn't. I stepped off the porch and stood on the sidewalk watching this object. As it headed south/east, it gained altitude, then changed to two red lights, one at the top and one at the bottom. It then disappeared behind the tree tops. There was no sound.
