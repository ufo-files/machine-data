Green light changed to 3 in a triangle accelerated and stopped instantly. Changed direction, accelerated instantly then went from view.

I took the dog out to the back garden before we went to bed, I always look at the sky while he does his business. I noticed a bright green light flashing in a NE direction it was completely stationary and I assumed it was a hovering helicopter as they are quite common, although I couldn't hear one. As I watched it appeared to change to three green light in a triangle, they now seemed to be more pulsing than flashing. The object then suddenly accelerated in NW direction. It appeared to travel a few miles in around a second and stopped instantly. At this point I started to shout for my fiancee to come, but she didn't hear me. The object then appeared to get brighter and became a single larger light. It stayed there for about a minute or so then accelerated back in the direction it came and continued out of sight behind the houses. It was the way it moved that was unnerving, it instantly accelerated and instantly stopped, I have never seen anything move that way. I came straight in and told my partner, my hands were shaking and I felt quite unnerved by the whole event. 
I used the compass app on my iphone to estimate the directions and angle.
