{nd} glowing orange/white/pink/yellow orb floated through the sky (like a high altitude plane), quickly changed direction and faded away

I was taking a photo at night of a rare car when I noticed in the north east distance there was a strange pulsating orb traveling south. at first I just assumed it was a plane... but as I kept looking I realized it was round and just sort of floating. it caused a strange low frequency humming in my ears as it neared. it quickly changed direction and headed east towards the mountains and faded away. tried to take a photo or video but my phone would not do it. the guy in the car I was photographing also could not get his car to start. both my phone and his car worked fine after the orb disappeared. he said he wanted nothing to do with "this s**t" and took off.
