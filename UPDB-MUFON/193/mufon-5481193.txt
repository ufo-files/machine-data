It was ongoing, A very bright light like the sun coming through my window of my bedroom. I could see many tall shapes disapating through my window as well as small ones & then a large one sat down on my bed & stuck a needle into my leg.

It was an on going occurrence. I would smell the scent of sulfur & always upstairs where the 2 bedrooms were. Covering my head with my blanket but leaving a place to see - I'd hear a hum or weird buzz and then the brightest light....I saw several large cloaked somethings going towards the the window where the brightness was & dissipate into it. This particular night, one sat very deliberately on the side of my bed & stuck a needle into my leg. I prayed as I was scared. During the time that followed, more somethings continued towards the window & continued to leave. The small bedroom had a blue light shining into it. 2 days later 2 men came to my house when I was alone & asked me questions - told me not to tell anyone about their visit. When I saw them checking the ground at my side yard, they told me to go inside or I'd be in trouble. So I did. Later when my parents returned, I mentioned it & checked my yard. Seeing small circular depressions in the grass, I told my parents & immediately my dad cut the grass so none would be seen. Interestingly, my dad's hair would stand straight up when he got to certain areas of the yard. Again I was told not to say anything to anyone. We lived there until I was 20 years old & had several experiences. To this day, I believe it was a pickup point with a live-in sentry.
