{cak} Star-like UFO over the bronx, New York

But before I decided to close the window because it was getting cold in the room and plus I never leave my windows open. As I was about to close the window. I notice a star in the sky. This star wasnt like the other ones. This one was much much brighter and bigger, plus it was in a part of the sky that in all of my years living here. Ive never had seen a star that big and bright there. Thats what really caught my attention. I was calm not excited normal.So I open the window completely to get a better view of it. Then I decided to take pictures and video of it. So I took out my Fujifilm finepix 18x camera to take pictures. The first couple of pictures I took without the tri-pod, but then decided to put the camera on its tripod to take better steady pictures. So I took a few pictures, then decide to take a video of this star. So I did about 7-9 minutes of video I believe. After I had taken photos and a video of this star. I closed my window and pull down the venetian blinds stored the camera away and went to bed. I didn't think about it. I decided to check the pictures and video the following day. So thats what I did. The following day I downloaded the pictures and video into my computer and saw what I had capture on video and on photos. I forgot to mention the object after I closed my window it remained there.
