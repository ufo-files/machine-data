{dr}Strange orange orbs/fireballs hovered over town. disapear simultaneously

I was just pulling into the parking lot at work when I looked up and saw 4 distinct orange fireballs/orbs. There was a very thin cloud cover and these objects were well below the clouds. I could not believe my eyes so I walked into work. I came 5 minutes later to double take what I had just saw and I was amazes. I watched these things for about 10 minutes until I looked away for maybe 2 seconds then looked up and they were gone simultaneously. At work I went up tothe top floor (3 story building) and witnessed 2 of the lights again but this time they hovered over the airport. I didn't watch them for long they moved very slowly almost mistook them as a light until they moved. I went downstairs to get my phone to attempt a picture but they were gone by the time I made it up. So I went outside to see and found only one orb this time and it was over the ocean. I watched this one until it just disappeared without a trace.
