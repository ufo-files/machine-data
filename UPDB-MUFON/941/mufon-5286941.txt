Constant presence of UAP / UFO above Johnson Ranch/San Tan Valley Az

I began seeing what appeared to be a dim cloud of distant lights in the sky that appeared to be shimmering similar to the way water reflects the sun and shimmers. I had seen this happen before and wanted to try and get it on video. I had a hard time believing what I was seeing. As I was recording, a black truck with an elderly couple got out and let their dogs out, the dogs and the couple noticed me and turned their attention to the sky. About the same time, one of their dogs looked at the sky and barked, but then began whimpering and barking intermittently, while crouching in a submissive way and looking at the sky. We were all looking at the sky. Then a red car with a middle aged man inside pulled up and parked and made no contact with the rest of us, he just looked at the sky and started walking towards the shimmering greyish objects. 
I looked back in the sky and hoped my camera caught something, but wasn’t sure. I then heard a commotion and looked to see the elderly couple (who I believe were there to take their dogs for a walk) collecting their dogs in a hurry and speeding away. It distrurbed me to see the dogs reactions to the objects, so when the elderly couple left, I got out of there as well. I reluctantly described the incident to my girlfriend a few days later. Since that time I see them on a regular basis (basically, a few times per day, every 48 hours). I have numerous videos of them.
