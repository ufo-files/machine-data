we were watching American planes that were returning from the war in Yugoslavia, we decided to go to a better vantage point on a road atop a hill over looking the Bras D'or Lake in Cape Breton Nova Scotia, after a little while we noticed a sphere or bubble shaped object hovering above the water, at first we thought it could be a helicopter, from our distance we couldn't see a tail even with binoculars, after a short while it began moving in such a way that we knew it couldn't be a helicopter, it was too fast and was doing loop de loops, the winds didn't seem to affect it. eventually it went out of our site behind Chapel Island , we couldn't find a place where we could see it again.
we were totally befuddled by it's motions, my colleague was an ex US marine and he said he never saw anything like that before.
