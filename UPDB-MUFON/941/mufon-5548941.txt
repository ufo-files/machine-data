It was a star like light

There were four of us sitting on the deck of my brother Pats house looking to the west when a star suddenly dropped down about 6 inches in the sky then at a 90 degrees angle it went south at a high rate of speed out of sight. We all looked at each other and made sure that each of us had seen the same thing it was that strange that it had caught each of our eye.
