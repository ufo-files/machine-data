{rjl} 20-30+ high flying rapid moving lights

Points of light moving rapidly at what appeared to be very high altitude in a straight NNW trajectory. Each traveling with a short distance between them, they travelled mostly singularly but several did move in groups of two or three in a tight formation. Estimated 25-30 or more of these objects were observed over the course of 5-7 minutes by 4 individuals.
