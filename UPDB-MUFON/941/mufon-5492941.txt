{cak} The craft was hovering, south of town, slowly went south.

I was driving home from work, and in the distance I saw red light. It suddenly dawned on me that there were no towers in that area. I was on the phone to my sister and as I got close to town, I could make out that it wasn't a tower, but a craft in the air. I told my sister that I think I was looking at a ufo! She told me to go home and go to bed. At this point, it wasn't moving, but as I went under it, it began moving south. There was a red light in the center and two white lights on two of the corners, but the corner in the front had no light. I checked the newspapers for days after that and of course saw nothing. I was northbound at the time and it was dark out, so I didn't see it very long....just long enough to change my life.
