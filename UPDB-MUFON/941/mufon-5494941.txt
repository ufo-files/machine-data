{nd} Went to my car to get something and looked up and I saw a white ball then it changed colors

It was around 9:00pm, I went outside to get something out of my car. It was a clear night and I always look up at the sky. I looked up and saw a white ball. When I first saw it, it looked like a snow globe. Then it changed to orange, red and yellow in color. I had my cellphone in my pocket so I took it out and started recording it. The object was just hovering slowly to the north. If you look closely near the end of the video, you can see a airplane's light blinking and I think you can see some stars.
