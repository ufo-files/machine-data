{rjl} UFO fly over on a clear night sky

walked out of my apartment complex about to get into my car for practice. Looked up to admire the stars during this clear night. While looking up I saw a seemingly solid black, triangle, object go across the sky. It appeared to be fairly large with a wing span similar to a large plane. About 5 small and dim lights ran down each of the sides making an upside-down "V". The apex of it had one extra light ahead of it. All the lights moved in perfect unison making me believe it to be a one solid object. The lights on it were no brighter than the stars around it so it was difficult to see, but it did appear to block the light of the stars as it passed over head. It seemed to be around 100-150 ft off the ground and made no noise as it flew over.
