Star-like moving south-east

This evening I was returning from my evening walk along a country road hear in the mountains (2400'), when I happened to look up at the moon and Jupiter (I believe) and saw a star-like object move silently and steadily southeast for around 30 seconds until it started to go behind the tree-line, at which time I ran down the road and got a few more seconds view. When I first saw it, it was near the moon and proceeded to go between the moon and jupiter. There was no flashing or change of course and it had almost the luminosity of jupiter. The color was very close to that of jupiter as well. I watch the sky often and am very familiar with satellites, planes, etc. and this immediately struck me as something I have never seen before. My wife and child were with me and saw this as well.
