{cak} Orange Glowing Star-like object tracked by Jet at low speed

Tonight was the third time this month that I have witnessed an Orange Star-like or Planetoid-like object cruising the skies of Saratoga County between the hours of 9:30 -10:30 PM. Tonight at 9:57 PM as I was returning home, I exited from the car and heard an aircraft/jet which sounded to be at a lower than normal elevation, as I looked up and focused on the jet, I noticed that it was tracking an Orange Glowing star-like object that was golf ball sized from the ground. The speed of the jet seemed to be slower than usual and it tracked the UFO keeping a consistent distance at what appeared to be 12" from my perspective. they traveled in a straight path from South to North running slightly west of the Northway I87. I was reaching for my phone to take a video but did not have the time to get the capture because I didn't want to take my eyes off the event, it was over within several seconds. I saw the same Orange UFO traveling North to South at approx 9:40 PM two weeks earlier, and after posting a video to fb, 2 other people contacted me and one had seen it in Saratoga Springs on South Broadway and another in Ballston Spa. The night before this sighting, I caught a quick glimpse of a Sun-like whitish gold large sphere stationary low in the sky hovering above and overpass partially behind some pines, I was traveling in a car and could not stop to investigate.
