{tjd} White pencil shaped object. Thought it was an airplane until I saw no wings or tail structure.

6/23/19 2:05pm Driving north on 131 towards Grand Rapids (between Door and Wayland) I viewed an all white object about .5 miles ahead flying quickly in the northwest direction. At first I thought it was a basic, white airplane until I saw it rapidly change course to a eastern direction. As it did so it changed speed to very slow rate. It almost hovered above 131 it was moving so slowly. I waited to see a tail section or wings as it turned and nothing was reflected, yet the sun clearly reflected off the entire body of the object. The object had no windows or anything recognizable as a commercial aircraft. I’ll admit it was a slight distance away, but the movement of the object was most peculiar. Unlike an helicopter in movement as well. 
I watched it head in an east, northeast direction from 131 until it was obscured momentarily by trees. When I passed the trees, the object was no longer visible anywhere on the horizon.
The sighting lasted approximately 2 minutes.
