{dr}Saw bright, apparently flaming object in sky over Holmes Beach for about 3 minutes.

We were having dinner at Skinnys Bar on Gulf Drive and noticed through the window a strange lighted thing in the sky. It seemed to be moving from west (over the Gulf of Mexico) inland. It looked somewhat like a plane only much closer and bigger, and it was moving more slowly. Part of it or all of appeared to be on fire. We watched it for 2-3 minutes, went inside to get a camera, and then it was gone. Neither of us could identify it although we agreed it was not a plane. We thought it might possibly have been a flaming lantern like some weve seen in Thailand, but it was very high up and lasted a long time. Anyone else see it?
