{rjl} Three lights in triangle.One dim as a star.One the brightest white I've ever seen. Last a bar. Randomly went on and off.

Was in backyard having a smoke.In the distance to my left plane's were making runs at Warren Grove bombing range.I looked up and overhead were three lights in a triangle. They randomly came on and off.One dim as a bright star.Another the brightest white light I've ever seen in the sky.(These two would come on for awhile then go out.)The third was a long bar of light that just quickly flashed when it came on. There was no sound.And after several minutes the lights just stopped.I couldn't tell if it was one large object or three separate ones.It shook me up and I wondered if it could be related to the fighters at the range. Decided to report it when I saw an article in the paper about triangles being reported over the Jersey Shore.
