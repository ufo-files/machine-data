{dr}At the beach at night we witnessed a rainbow

Around 8pm Christmas Eve we stopped at the beach on Palm Beach Island for a brief moment. In the time between 8-9pm we witnessed a star like light appear far out over the ocean traveling north like an airplane. It was pulsating white, blue, red, orange and was not on a stable path. It then slowed down, and hovered for a brief moment before traveling backwards. Once we recognized it was something else, it started dancing in the sky in all directions, speeding and slowing down. The size of a bright star. It disappeared eventually and left us mesmerized.
