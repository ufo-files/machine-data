Dull metallic grey saucer shaped ship with bottom bubbled cylinder

I was only 11 years old at the time, my mother was a second witness at the time but she has since forgotten. The ship was a dull metallic grey, not shiny. It was saucer shaped with a shallow dome on top and what seemed like a cylinder extending from the bottom with at least 4 half circle domelike projections on the cylinder. When I first noticed the craft it was northeast of me moving southward, the craft rotating as it flew. It suddenly stopped in place and tilted about 45 degrees rotating faster as it remained in place before shooting off north at incredible speed. Halfway through the experience I dragged my mother over to look because she didn't believe me and it left her speechless. Shortly after the UFO left the area I saw two either F16s or FA18s fly in the direction of the UFO at full speed. I included a rough sketch of the UFO
