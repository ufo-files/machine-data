Alien like being choking me

A being come toward me from my living room very quickly stands by my head next to my bed. Looks right at me and then begins to choke me. I can not scream or talk. I can not breath my arms are moving wildly. Then it is gone. The being was hairless had large eyes but not abnormally so. About 3-4 ft tall large forehead. I had been sleeping, at first thought it was a dream but the surroundings were identical to my home.
