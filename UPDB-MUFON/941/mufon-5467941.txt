{cak} Falling star came in view then 2nd star followed. Third star came in from different direction. First star blew up. third star stopped. Second star shot off ,backwards. Then seconds later, Third star shot off backwards.

This happen around 1973. Near Lubbock texas. I was Walking home on lone street , looking at stars, on a very clear night, tons of stars. Saw a falling star going to the left.then I realized a second star was following same path, but faster. While I was trying to figure that out , a third star came in from a different direction, going toward first star. First star exploded ! The third star stopped. Second star stopped for a split second then shot off backwards . Then third star waited,like 3 seconds then also shot off backward . I kept looking in the area but didn't see any other movement. 
It took me a while to figure out what had happened. Even after all these years it still plays in my head.
