Two or obsess flying beside me

Two or obsess flying beside me there were two orbs one small one bigger bouncing around in the air not too high off the ground not that far in front of me. They seem to hover and then zigzag from one place to another for a significant amount of time. Their glowing light would expand upon movement and they would be star like when they were stationary. I think they were Trying to talk to me.
