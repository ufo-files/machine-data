{cak} Korean War military base

Back in the Korean War, my grandfather was stationed in Kentucky as an army painter and cartographer. He told me that one night he went out of the barracks for a brief moment and saw red and green auras flying through the sky at surprisingly fast rates. He called all the other enlisted men out of the barracks and they all watched in amazement as the objects buzzed through the sky. He never explained more than that about the crafts themselves. When my grandfather went to go tell the general, whom he was very close with, what had occurred, the general said, "you're crazy, and you never saw a thing," then abruptly dismissed him.
