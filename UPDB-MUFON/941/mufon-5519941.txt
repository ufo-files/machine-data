1st one red light that seem to bring out 2 more

The first red light flashed and stayed to the east of us and then there apeard a 2nd light and then a 3rd. The lights went north and south seeming to almost become one and then shoot in another direction away from other light. This has happened for the last three nights. They seemed to be flying over the Columbia river from Wallula jct. to the Snake river. This lasted for over two hours and we called another person whom lived at a higher location in the area, they witnessed 4 red flashing lights. From our location we could only see 3 at any given time. 
I know these are not planes because what plane would go back and forth? There was many trains that go by and when the train came thru area the lights seemed to be further away. And after train passing the noise in area became silent. These lights had no sound.
