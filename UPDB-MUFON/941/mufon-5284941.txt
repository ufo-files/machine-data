Bright red light turned into a white light and moved erratically

I first saw it bright red in the sky (at 11:20 PM) with the intensity of a star. It then turned into white light as it descended in altitude, which I could tell due to the chevron orientation of what I assume to be exterior lights. It then began making very erratic movements; it would stay still in the night sky then move a random direction at around the speed you could see a satellite move, then stop, then begin again in a new direction a few seconds later. It continued this for at least 30 minutes. Then I saw a very small white light appear close to it which lasted only a few milliseconds. At which point, it appeared to gain a tremendous altitude, at which point the pulsating exterior lights seem to only be a star in the sky. I could still make it out through the erratic yet much less pronounced movements. At the time of writing, it still has not left my view.
