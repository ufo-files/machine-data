{jpg} Stationary foggy star observed. Looked away for 2 seconds, foggy star rapidly moved south. Saw star between mist and mist trailing.

I walked to the car and looked up at the stars as I always do. The night sky was black, crisp, and filled with stars. I noticed one star was oddly foggy looking. I wondered what would be causing the blurred misty look. I pointed the star out to a friend who was with me. I had to remove something from my car, so I took my attention away from the star for a few seconds. My friend gasped at something in the sky that caused me to look up. I saw the star traveling south. I observed as I watched the star disappearing, that the star was between the mist and the mist was trailing. The star disappeared before the mist. I looked back up where the star was and there nothing at first. Suddenly a crisp star appeared in the same spot and disappeared.
