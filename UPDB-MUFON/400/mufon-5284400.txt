Multiple sets of flying crafts flying soundless and fast above the atmosphere from W to E 1direction. Sets of 2, 4, and 12, in V formation.

Wednesday (November 3rd 2021) early morning, between 12:30am and 4:30am, we saw multiple sets of flying "crafts" (spheres) flying across the sky. We were using Gen 4 7 Bravo Nightvision Goggles when we noticed the first set of 4 crafts flying close together. They were moving fast in a West to East direction without any sound. They seemed to be going above the atmosphere, but definitely faster than an airplane or satellite. A few minutes later, we saw a set of 12 crafts flying in a V-formation going the same direction as the first set. Most stayed in formation however we could see some wavering a little which made us realize they were individual crafts. These sets of 2, 4, and 12 kept coming every few minutes. Overall we must have seen about 50. When looking at the sky without the Nightvision goggles, we could barely see them. They seemed to have a golden glow around them with a faint spiraling trail around them.
