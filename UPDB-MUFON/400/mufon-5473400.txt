{jpg} Bright silent light when zoomed in on video object flips

My son and I were observing the sky when we saw a bright light coming towards us and fly over our heads. It made no sound. He told me to film it so I did. When I zoom in on the video we can see the object appear to be flipping. The object also does not fly in a straight path it sort of bounces. We thought that's a UFO! We watched it as far as we could but then it sort of disappears. In the video you can see the moon and then a small glow moving. Beware there is a swear word at the end of the video.
