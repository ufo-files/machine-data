Driving to work noticed lights

As I topped the hill on Fairview Rd. just pass Rock Bluff Rd. toward Gold Point Circle S. on my way to meet my carpool to work. I notice three perfect circles of light moving across the black sky. It was moving from the Chester Frost Park / Middle Valley area toward the wooded area around Gold Point Circle / Chickamauga Lake. 

At first I thought the light were an airplane. They looked to be about three to four hundred feet in the air. They were moving too slowly to be an airplane. I also noticed right away that none of the lights were flashing. Airplanes have red and green flashing lights on them. The lights were much larger than airplane lights. They looked almost like flood lights, but none of them put out any type of beam, they way a search light puts out a beam that you can see from the side even if it isn1t pointing at you. 

I got to look at it for about a minute. I spent most of that time trying to rationalize in my own mind what I was seeing. As Fairview curves to come to Gold Point Circle, I lost sight of it.

On my way home, it was dark. I drove the long way around to drive back down Fairview the same way, so I could be sure that I had not mistaken the lights of a new cell tower for the object I saw. I saw two airplanes flying on a similar route, but they were much higher and the lights in no way resembled the lights that I1d seen in the morning. There weren1t any lights in the field of view that I had as I topped the hill.
