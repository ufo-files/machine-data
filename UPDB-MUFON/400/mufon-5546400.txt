bronze colored triangular shaped fast and no lights

On 04/27/10 the pensacola news journal stated the space station would be visable at 849pm. On that night it was clear with no clouds and full moon and the station would be traveling from southwest to northeast. As my girlfriend and I stood in my backyard looking southwestern we saw the space station and observed an object traveling with it at the same speed stopped then turned south for 10 seconds then disappeard. The object was bronze triangular shaped with no lights. Neither of us could identify it and I emailed. NASA with negative results.
