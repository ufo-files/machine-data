large tic tac shaped over a radio tower, proceeded over highway then vanished after ascending a bit

Our family was traveling on highway 84 out of Utah towards Idaho. We saw a radio tower on a bare dry hill and above it was a large whitish silverish tic tac cigar shaped object. It went straight in a slow manner over the highway and ascended up a little bit and one more ascend and then it was gone as in vanished from it's spot. It was odd when going straight in an exact speed. Methodical in movement as if being driven. Pearl white to silver when it turned..like a cement truck turns. No wings, no propeller, no sound - but we were in the car. My teen in the back seat spotted it first. We couldn't explain it.
