(GS) Observed two fiery orbs with trails. One hovered for about 2mins. Both ascended into thin air.

I was sitting on an upper deck of at a friends house in Worthington, OH when we observed two fiery orbs slowly moving thru the sky. At first we thought that the objects were planes, but we noticed that the objects appeared to be engulfed in flames and both had tails. It was too bright to be a star and didn't move through the sky quickly like a comet. The orbs moved very slowly. One of the orbs hovered for about two minutes and only began moving once the other orb got next to it. They ascended into the sky until they were no longer visible.
