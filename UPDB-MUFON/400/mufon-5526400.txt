Bright red glowing object in the sky that dimmed and flew away quickly

My boyfriend and I were driving to my house in a town called Port Republic, NJ. (small town) My boyfriend noticed first and I noticed about two seconds afterwards. It was a bright red glowing object in the sky. It was really bright and there were no flashing lights like a plane or anything like that. I hit the brakes to get a better look and try to get a picture but just as I did that the object stopped glowing so bright and became a dull red dot. Then took off and disappeared behind the tree line. I couldn't quite tell how high up in the sky it was. I'd say it was higher up than a plane, but my boyfriend thinks it was lower. He did see it first. I was truly freaked by this.
