White cigar stopped and hovered under cloud cover

White cigar shaped objective with a supposed reflective surface observed moving in a straight path at decreasing velocity and then stopping and hovering in a fixed spot under cloud cover at the same elevation. Object had no detail, no wings nor visible appendages.

Object was observed on Coniston road, Ramsgate, CT11 at 6PM while on a bus ride home. Object caught my eye as a white speck appeared under cloud cover and moved abruptly before slowing pace and remaining stationary. Gaze was kept on the object - I initially paused and squinted to check, had good vision and could see the distinct silhouette of the cigar shaped object. Bus began moving and the object remained stationary but was removed from my view.
