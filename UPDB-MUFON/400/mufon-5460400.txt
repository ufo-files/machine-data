4 orbs of light, two white, one blue, one red/orange. Orbs appeared to be attached to a larger object that can't be seen in the night. After a couple of minutes it shot off across the city(port Isabel,tx) out of sight into Mexico in a couple of seconds. A few minutes later we saw two jet military airplanes with green lights coming from the north heading in the direction of the object.
