Large disc like object with 6 yellow fixed lights hovered around 100-200ft above us for around 1.5 mins than took off at a surreal speed.

My son and I were watching some people play soccer in Quite, Ecuador when we noticed a few folks looking straight up. When we looked up we saw a large metallic disc with 6 lights approximately 80-100ft in size (similar to a C-130 in size) hovering around 150-200ft above us. I went to take a pic, but my phone was not responsive. It hovered for around a minute and a half than moved slightly forward before taking off straight up at an ungodly speed. I am a special operations veteran and familiar with most of the military and commercial flying capable vehicles...this was nothing like I have ever seen before.
