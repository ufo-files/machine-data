3 objects flying in triangular formation

I was traveling east on Western Row Road approaching the intersection of Western Row Road and Snider Road. My wife and I both seen the objects ahead of us in the sky. I guessed the objects were no farther away than a mile and no higher up than maybe 700 to 800 feet. We were able to guess the height by using the WLW radio tower across town as a reference. The objects appeared to be 3 dark objects roughly round in shape with a pulsing flickering orange/amber light at the top. The objects were moving in a triangular formation in a northerly direction with 2 objects in the lead and one trailing. The 2 lead objects were moving erratically forward and back in a horizontal manner while still maintaining a northward direction. At first we thought the objects were helicopters but realized they didn't have the telltale shape of an aircraft. I turned Left/North onto snider road to try to keep the objects in sight but they gained elevation and were obscured by the clouds before they reached the WLW radio tower.
