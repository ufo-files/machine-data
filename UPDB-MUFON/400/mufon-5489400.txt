It was October 1977 and I was standing in the city park of blue grass iowa at around 10 oclock at night. I happened to look up into the night sky and there to the northwest sat 2 lights. like headlights of a car. no movement whatsoever and i immediately just knew it was not an airplane or helicopter. it was as if a car was sitting up there. it wasnt that high up, maybe 500 feet of so. the park was filled with other kids, we were drinking, having fun that night. i kept pointing up at it but nobody else seemed interested or concerned. i couldnt stop watching it. those 2 lights just sat there completely motionless which i knew wasnt right. even a helicopter would wobble a little bit. and i knew they werent stars either. the other thing is i felt a strange feeling of being observed. so after maybe 10 minutes i noticed other lights from the east going towards where these "headlights" were and i could tell it was a plane. as the plane started to get near the ufo the 2 lights just blinked off. at about this time it was time to go since i was spending the night at my friends house who lived there in blue grass. my buddies had to pull me away because i was so excited. they told me not to go into the house and say anything about seeing ufo's since we wernt supposed to be drinking. i'll never forget that night and i've always thought i was the only one who really believed what i saw that night until I found this a few years ago which substantiates what i saw since it was the same exact month and year. i would really like to talk to whoever this woman is if she's still alive. here is the link to the story i found:
