Approximately 46 lights fly over in straight line

My wife and I observed 2 lights fly over Side by side, then approximately 43 more lights evenly spaced followed the same path. One then approached from the north toward other objects, then changed direction almost 90degrees to follow other 45 lights. They disappeared over mountains when we lost them. It was the most incredible thing I ever saw!
