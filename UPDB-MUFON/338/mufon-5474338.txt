Dozens of ufo's flying over NOS primavera sound festival in Porto, Portugal

I was attending the NOS primavera sound festival in Porto. A friend pointed me out all these pulsating lights flying over the festival. I started recording at once. We had never witnessed anything like this, the sheer number of ufo's gave a very unsettling feeling and we were all very puzzled.
