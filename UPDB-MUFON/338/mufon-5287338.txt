On the night of June 5th I was stargazing outside my guard shack and noticed a bright green white and orange light rocket past my field of view

I proceeded to look in its direction, only to watch it change directions suddenly, heading from SW bearing to S immediately, and in the blink of an eye, it was gone. It was too low flying to be a plane, helicopter, drone, and it was moving near the speed of light as far as I could tell. It was in and out of focus within seconds, leaving behind only a bright flash of lights for a brief second, as it rocketed across the sky, at around 3500-4000 feet elevation, and did not decrease or increase in altitude during observation.
