bright object changing direction in sky

I was walking my dog around 1 am on the 25th of May, when I saw a light appear in the sky, coming in my direction from the northwest. It was a clear night, and i watched it for a while trying to figure out what it was and if there was any sound coming from it, thinking it was a plane. When i heard nothing, I decided it was pretty odd so I began recording as it neared the zenith of its path relative to me. 

It was similar in brightness to Jupiter, but whiter. I repositioned my phone just in time to catch it abruptly change direction and then quickly fade/dim, taking on a reddish hue as it did.
