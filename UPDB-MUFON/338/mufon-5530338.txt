Star like object ascends in the night sky.

It was a very clear, calm Sunday night, with sky visibility celling at 30,000 feet or above. I was sitting on the front porch observing the night sky when i noticed various star patterns. One pattern directly in my line of sight were "3 stars" at about an altitude of 12 to 15,000 feet. These lights were similar in shape, color, brightness and were level and fixed in the sky in a straight line. No clouds or atmospheric interference was present. I then observed what I thought to be a star, the left one of the (3) slowly and constantly rose from a stationary point up northward. I stood up and watched as it rose out of sight. There were no strobe lights, beacons, sounds or color other than what appeared to look like a star. This was not a plane balloon, helicopter, flare or lighter than air raft.
