My daughter and I were driving home from a concert in Plymouth NH, about 10 pm, we both saw a long cigar shape object descend in a couple seconds into the trees to our left. A few hundred yards away. No sound, the brightness of the solid silver almost fluorescent light was striking. It was horizontal when it came down. There are too many woods here, no way to know for sure where it landed, sorry to say. It was unforgettable.
