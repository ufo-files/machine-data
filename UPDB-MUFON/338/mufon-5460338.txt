noiseless vanishing star

Right after picking up my dog's droppings and while walking back to my house I looked up to the sky and saw a star as bright a Jupiter which was still for about 4 seconds, then began to move in a westward direction. As it began to move it began to dim until it disappeared. 
All of the incident went noiseless.
