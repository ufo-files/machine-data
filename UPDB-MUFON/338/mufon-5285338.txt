Sighted object moving across sky at very high altitude

I was in the pool and sighted object in the southeast a 737 passed between it and me so it had to be at a very high altitude the 737 was at least 12,000 or higher. I yelled at my wife to get her phone and hurry. By the time she came the object had crossed to the Northwestern sky and was barely observable because of the sun. It had traveled in a fairly straight line from southeast to northwest then changed direction to due north. She snapped 3 photos which are attached.
