yellowish bight,yet foggy circular light

My husband and I were sitting on the roof of the house and we were watching the stars and watching airplanes in the distance (approx 10 pm central time). We were out there for an hour or so total. At 10:26 pm we noticed a yellowish/orange bright ball of light appear out of nowhere,just above the tree lilne in the front yard,the object seemed to move a little closer in our direction (we were north of the object). At first we though it was an air plane but noticed it had no blinking lights and the light was too large to be a plane that far away. I would say it was atleast a mile or two away. The object quickly veered eastward then faded away. IT DID NOT FADE INTO THE DISTANCE, it faded as if a battery lost its power. We were very baffled and tried to find logical explanations for what we had just seen..We sat on the same spot of the roof for the next 3 nights and every night between 10:25 pm and 10:35 pm we saw the same thing appear. It would just come out of nowhere then within 5 or 10 seconds it disappears. Its NOT CELL PHONE TOWERS OR SPOT LIGHTS OR PLANES OR HELICOPTERS, even planes that far away can be heard from the ground, we heard NOTHING when the light appears. The object also moves in different directions, but always fades away to the east.The 3rd night we saw this object we managed to get a picture but its so far away that you cant make out any detail. It looks like an orange/yellow ball of light when magnified. We have no idea what this object is but we have NEVER seen anything like it. We are going to try to get video of it tonight. We have looked at some ufo websights and there was a video from kingsport tennessee that looks almost identical to what we are seeing.
