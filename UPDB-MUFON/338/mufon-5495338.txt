{cak} Bright light going down then steep dive scattered. Video

I was driving home on a neighborhood road. There was a bright light much brighter than any airplane I've ever seen and white in color. It appeared to be a shooting star or some space related thing. The bright light descended for a little then the bright light shut off and there was just red and blue lights going in a very unsteady flight pattern. The video couldn't capture the UFO after bright light shut off. No special feelings, just a sense of anxiousness because of what I had observed. I lost sight by turning into an area where trees covered the object and didn't see it again.
