{nd} Silver Object Hovered In western sky at dusk for approximately 1 minute, then vanished. object reappeared and flew across sky.

On property throwing frisbee to dogs. Object was bright. Thought it was a star at first but it disappeared. Considered it was the ISS once it was moving however looked online and it was not over the U.S.A.
