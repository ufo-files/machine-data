Unidentified bright orange objects that were filmed

On August 16, 2020 at approximately 9:00 pm CST my wife and I observed a total of seven (7) bright orange, round objects in the sky travelling from NW to North and eventually disintegrating. They were in the approximate flight path that we see daily near Midway Airport in Chicago. We filed all seven (7). Three (3) of the seven (7) were filmed together. they appears out of nowhere and disintegrated out of in the same manner they originally appeared. We also have an airplane in one of the videos to show the difference between the flashing lights of the plane and the steady orange light from the unidentified object. With the naked eye we could not see anything other than steady orange lights however, on video, it is clear that the objects are perfectly round and seem to have circular lights on the undersides of the objects.
