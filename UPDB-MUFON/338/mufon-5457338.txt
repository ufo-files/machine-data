{tjd} Witnessed bright light with helicopters. Helicopters disppeared but light appeared to follow us changing to green orange and white with small orbs..

As we were travelling back roads after a thunderstorm, we noticed several helicopters following a ball of light at telephone pole height. It was travelling perpendicular to the road we were on. The light started changing shape to a dark object with green, orange and white lights. The helicopters disappeared but the object followed us at tree top height with lights flashing on the way. It followed us until we approached the nearest town and then veered off into the distance.
