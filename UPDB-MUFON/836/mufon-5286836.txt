Tic tac shapes objects made triangle shape in the sky

This is our fourth sighting at this location. We were camping onLake Michigan this past week hoping to see UFO activity and we were not disappointed. Friday night the sky had cleared and my son and I decided to step out onto our camp beach to stargaze when my son noticed several UFOs making shapes in the sky. It looked like the stars came alive and put on a little show for us. It was absolutely silent... and the area is one of the darkest places on earth because of its location on Lake Michigan. The show lasted only 10 minutes because my son was "getting nervous" and wanted to go back to camp. 

This camp is very remote and planes are easily noticed when they are in the area due to the noise they make. These objects were completely silent and had no discernable lights. These objects and variants of these objects appear regularly. I have a photo of my first encounter, but I only had my Iphone which was impossible to get evidence with. I am returning in July with friends to document these objects in a more detailed fashion.
