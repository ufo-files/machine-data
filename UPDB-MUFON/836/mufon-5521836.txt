2 hovering lights over Lafyette area.

I was driving at the time, I forget which road it was but it was in Westminster heading North. I noticed the objects because they were brighter than airplanes and seemed to be hovering over the ?Lafyette? Area. I thought it was just an airplane with the airport near by but these objects were brighter than the stars and airplanes Im used to seeing. The objects didn't do anything they just seemed to hover there, the less brighter one higher the brightest. I don't really have any feelings about what I saw because Im not sure what it was...plane? Flares? Helicopter? Im just not sure, if anything Im just confused. I lost sight od them after turning onto 120th east bound to go home.
