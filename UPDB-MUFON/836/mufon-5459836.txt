Hovering orb, then suddenly accelerated at very fast speed

I was observing Jupiter in my telescope on my balcony when i caught a glimpse of something in the corner of me eye. I looked an noticed this Bright orb just hovering to the North East of my position. I quickly pulled out my phone and started recording it. It made small sudden movements back and forth and then suddenly accelerated straight up very quickly and faded out of site.
