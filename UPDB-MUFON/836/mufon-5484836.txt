{jpg} Orbs flying off like birds, suddenly changed shape and direction, some disappeared, some wobbled and were gone.

I was washing the windows and saw what I thought was birds flying and hovering. There were about five or six doing semicircles and up very high. I noticed some puffs of cloud nearby and saw that the objects were higher than the clouds. I said aloud to myself, how did they get so high? Then they rapidly started flying in different directions, by now I could tell they were not birds and were probably too big to be any birds I've seen. Two flew toward the sun till I couldn't see them no more and they were orbs that didn't change shape. The others flew in different directions, one descended and seemed to change to a cigar shape. It flew west to east and behind a small cloud then seemed gone. Three minutes later another one appeared from the west going east. I got out the binoculars and watched it closer until it got to the same point in the sky, it appeared to be changing color then it was gone for only a few seconds then it was there again going the opposite way. It did seem to be coming toward me for a few moments but eventually disappeared behind some clouds in the west part of the sky without reappearing. When I thought it was coming toward me, I felt a little worried, but thought whatever happens, happens.
