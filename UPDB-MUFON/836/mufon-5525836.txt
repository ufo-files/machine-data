3 orange/yellow lights in triangle

My Daughter was taking out the trash and ran in the house yelling for me to come outside to see something. I ran out to see three bright orange/yellow lights hovering in the sky. I yelled for my Husband to come out and see too. As he came outside they were dropping toward the ground behind the trees VERY SLOWLY! We watched them until we couldnt see them anymore. We stayed outside for about 10 min to see if it was going to come back up but never did...that we saw.
