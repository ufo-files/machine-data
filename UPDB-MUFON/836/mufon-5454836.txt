{jpg} object went into cloud

I was home for lunch took dogs outside . Clear blue sky no clouds .
Heard strange noise like a train or something heavy flying over head, looked up to see giant metal object go into a cloud. It was like I could see through it but there was something there. Shape long , not like airplane just long. 
I was on the phone at the same time talking to my son and told him what I was seeing. 
The object never came out of the cloud , could hear the noise but stayed in cloud. Cloud moved slowly away.
