Pulsating sphere 30-50ft in diameter over beach town

UFO sphere pulsating red, orange and yellow over residential area of Mexican beach town. At first just hovering, but then began to move slowly in a straight line with altitude staying constant (approximately 300-500 ft off the ground). It did seem like something was dripping off of it, almost like molten metal. It moved silently over the roof tops leaving the Playa del Carmen area. It headed south towards Tulum, which has Mayan ruins and temple right on the beach.
