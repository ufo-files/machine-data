i was on my balcony ... exactly 11:45 pm.. July 29th ... from a distance i had witnessed a bright star light object in the sky.. there were no aircrafts or lights in the sky . the overhead light coming from west to east was moving fast over a long distance in the shape as if you were going to draw a checkmark on a piece of paper. The beginning of the checkmark when pencil touches the paper coming towards east and then at same speed would be the long part of the checkmark.
I felt over welmed and surprised by the event... my reactions were very reactive towards something I never believed in before . The object moved so fast away it literally did not give me time to blink...
