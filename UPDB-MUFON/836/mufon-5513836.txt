A Triangular Ball of wavering light moving across the sky and then vanished

Just saw a triangular ball of yellow light in the sky moving at a speed faster than a plane. The shape of the light was not exactly triangular, but somewhat appeared to be so. No sound was observed. The light glowed and did not blink as would appear when a plane went across the sky. Surprisingly in the pictures I took with my mobile camera, the object appears to be changing shape!! Please see the pictures for yourself. In couple of the pics, you will also notice the side of a tree, so this is not a hoax. I tried to record a video, but in the excitement, I did not press the record button looks like :(. But thankfully, the pics have come out well.
