I was having a bon fire with the family when I first saw the object. I called it to the attention of my father. He then turned around and when he realized the odd movement and lack of blinking lights (as seen on commercial plains, jets, etc...), he then took out his phone and began to try to record what we were seeing, onto video. I too, took out my phone and tried recording. We saw 3 unidentifiable lights that night, in different sections of the sky, one after another. I am not sure what the object was, but to the naked eye it appeared as just one solid white light, upon reviewing the video we realized it was pulsating different colored lights. I tried recording one but it would not appear on video. My dad captured the video seen here. After the experience we then noticed an abundance of jets streaking through the sky in what looked to be an attempt to find the object we had just saw. Usually we see one every few hours in the part of Ohio Im from. In this night we counted 10 at least in the sky at one time, all moving quite rapidly.
