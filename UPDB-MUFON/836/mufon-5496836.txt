{nd} Three lights approached high in the night sky, arced and departed to the East as the lights faded

Arrived at home, exited the truck and saw what I thought was a couple helicopters flying in loose formation over the city of Bend. Walked towards the house and looked up again to see three objects. Watched them for awhile until I realized there was no rotor noise so they couldn't be helicopters.  I went inside to bring my girlfriend out to see them. When I returned, the first two were starting to head to the East. The light seemed to fade until they dissappeared. This took about 2 1/2 minutes from the time I saw them till the time they dissappeared. About this time my girlfriend came out, and was able to see the third object glowing brightly. We watched it until it began to change direction and head East like the first two. The light stayed bright for another minute or two as it got farther away,then faded. There was never any sound, never any flashing lights. Sky was clear, no wind.  Total time observed was 4 minutes.
