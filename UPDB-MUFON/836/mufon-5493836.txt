{rjl} Saw blue white light that changed directions four (4) times.

I was at home, in lemoore, ca. I had gone outside to get some air. I looked out toward the east side of the valley and started tracking what I thought was a jet from the nearby naval base, naslemoore, going north at a high rate of speed. There was a flashing a blue light only, which was odd. It then changed direction instantly to the south. No arced turn just a 180. I climbed up onto the roof, hoping not to lose it below the horizon. It was nearly out of sight when it changed direction again, heading what appeared to be straight toward my location. I walked up to the highest point of our roof, and the object passed by about 100 yards away, at about 50 feet above an apartment complex. It was then I could see or observe the following: it was about 70 feet long, saucer shaped, dull in color, had a small soft white light dome in the top center. The dark blue light was "orbiting" or circling the dome, which from a distance had made it look as if the object were flashing a blue light. The only sound it made was the object cutting through the air, like a glider would make. It then crossed the railroad tracks over the coors warehouse, turned briefly toward the west while rising about another hundred feet, and shot up like a bullet to what appeared to be the middle star in Orion's belt. A neighbor down the street saw it as well. No one believed us.
