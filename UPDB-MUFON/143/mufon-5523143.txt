4 lights moving W-SW to E-NE

My wife, son and I observed 4 glowing reddish-orange lights moving toward Lansing from the west-southwest. One was ahead and seemed to slow and move upward, then it disappeared. The first light was followed closely (maybe 1/2 mile behind) then came two lights moving side by side together. They all seemed to come to the same location and disappear. We thought that maybe they were weather ballons. They were absolutely silent.
