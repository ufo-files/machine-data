Triangle shaped hovering craft; red, white, blue lights not flashing

I was driving in to workout at gym at my place of employment; the time was 4:58am and it was a clear morning with a crescent moon in the east. I turned from Torrey Pines Road onto La Jolla Village Road going east. At the next intersection, La Jolla Scenic, there was something hovering over a grove of trees and condos. It was about 100 to 200 feet in the air, had red and blue lights with a few white ones. None of the lights was flashing and they appeared in a triangle shape. Instinctively, I rolled my window down and peered at it as I drove past (there were no other cars on the road). I could not hear anything or feel anything. Once past La Jolla Scenic I was committed to that direction, so I made a right onto the next available street (Villa La Jolla) and drove into a parking lot that was east of the lights. However, I was now down a hill and could just see the lights through the top of the trees.
