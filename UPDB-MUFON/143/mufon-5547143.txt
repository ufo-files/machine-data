This time, I was alone. I was expecting nothing. After about an hour or so of standing outside (alone) my wife decided to head to the store. A few minutes after she left one of them showed up a few thousand feet up or so and moved along the sky and disappeared. 20 more minutes go by and as I looked up I saw one high and to my south (light up and then disappear) really quick, but headed back in my direction. I got my camera ready, but what was about to happen; in all honesty I was not ready for. Within just a few seconds, very close the craft lit up out of nowhere. I started filming, which is difficult when they fly directly above you, as it went out of view over the tops of the trees in my yard, I ran out to finish filming it as it left the area. I was a little shook up this time, mostly because they surprised me. After returning from a short break, several minutes went by and to my west, one lit up out of nowhere for a split second and out of of the corner of my eye to the south I filmed another craft, very beautiful and brightly lit slowly traveling just above the tree lines. After these two, I stopped for the night, I had enough for one evening. :)
