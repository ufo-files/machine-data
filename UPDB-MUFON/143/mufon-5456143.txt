{jpg} Three fast moving shiny objects shaped by long pie slices with no antennas, or anything on them

Looking with my binoculars at the Kinkaide fire in the north from our hill I scanned the valley east and happened to see three strange shiny objects shaped like long pie slices racing south together from north to south along the Laguna area below our hill. Just as I noticed them the front one turned itself around and pointed north as it traveled south. Then one of the objects disappeared-, poof, gone. Then theyy were quickly beyond my ability to see them.hidden by trees. I have no idea how large they were. They seemed to bounce on the air currents like they were made of mylar.
