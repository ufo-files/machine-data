Hi. I have had this image for a few years and it's stored on the hard-drive of an older computer that I have. I was in Grad school in Pasadena and drove home to my kids in Westlake Village about 60 miles away. When I stopped at Sprouts to pick up dinner, there was a rainbow. So I took a picture of the rainbow. When I went to look at the picture, there was a strange object in the rainbow. It looked like a crystal plane with no wings. Right away, I was like - that is a ufo, because it was not visible to the naked eye. It's very cool. I got a fuzzy feeling when I took that picture. I have the original, but these are photos I took from the screen of my old computer. Oh, There are also these weird arrow looking things at the tail.
