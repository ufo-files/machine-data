5 White lights travelling on the same course and speed

At roughly 0455 I observed what appeared to be a satellite travelling from NNW to SSE. Behind the first light appeared a second light on the same course and what appeared to be the same speed. The measured distance between the objects, from my view was about 3 inches. After the second light appeared a 3rd, with a total of 5 lights appearing travelling on the same course with roughly the same distance between between all of the objects. Again, they just all appeared to be satellites, just a white point of light. There was no sound or navigational lights observed. Thank you to MUFON.
