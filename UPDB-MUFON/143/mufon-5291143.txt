Looked like a star moving at satellite speed, then changed direction several times and then changed course

Standing on my deck at 3:30 am, I saw something that looked like a star but it was moving at a south by southeast direction 120 Degrees approx. once it got directly overhead, it zigzagged 3 or 4 times. Then it changed direction to approx 65/70 degrees Northeast. With my binoculars, I could make out a triangular shape with approx 4/6 lights on one side and one in the middle of the object. It stayed on the Ne direction till I lost sight of it. Total event last 2/3 minutes.
