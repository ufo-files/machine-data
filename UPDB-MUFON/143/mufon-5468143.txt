{jpg} Extremely large boomerang object traveling at rapid rate of speed. Lit up 7 lights simultaneously for approximately 1 second (2 times).

I was leaving the bowling alley with my husband and as I was about to get into the passenger seat of our car, I observed an extremely large "V" shaped UFO traveling southwest at an alarming rate of speed. From my viewpoint and the altitude of the object, I estimate it to be approximately 50-100 times larger than a modern sized airplane. The object had seven lights-three on each line of the "V" and one light where the lines intersect. The lights turned on in sync for approximately one second (the lights were white and dim). The lights turned on and off two times before I lost sight of the object. The object was moving at a rapid rate of speed (I only observed it for approximately 8-10 seconds before I lost sight of it). The object was completely silent. I did not hear any noise of an engine or acceleration sounds as the object traveled. From my viewpoint, I observed the bottom or underside of the object. The object appeared to be completely black in color except for the seven lights. I observed this object on June 2, 2018 at 11:55 PM.
