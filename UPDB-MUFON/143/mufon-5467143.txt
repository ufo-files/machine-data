{cak} Saw Tall Creature / Entity

## Pulled off exit 20 southbound, our car came to a stop I was driving my fiance was in the passenger seat. Put left blinker on and looked both ways. As I looked to my right I saw a tall slender figure all black with stick like features. No detailed features... the entity was about 30 to 50 feet away and in the middle of the road. It appeared to be about 10 feet tall. As we began to turn my fiance did a double take and saw it as well. I asked her what she saw...she explained exactly what I saw. By this point we had made the left turn, we decided to turn around ..we drove back and there was nothing there. We both felt like we were being observed. This event happened at 12:15 a.m. ##
