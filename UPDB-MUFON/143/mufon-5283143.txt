Observed pair of white lights that later moved from south to north revealing a long triangle shape object.

It was a clear night just past twilight when I observed two white lights moving very slowly from south to north. No other lights were observed. When the pair of lights were nearly overhead a triangle shape object blacker then the night sky suddenly became visible. 
The object did not match known aircraft that I know of. The object seemed to be very high and large.. Note New Orleans International Airport is some ten miles away.
