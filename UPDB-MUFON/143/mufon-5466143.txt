{td} Rapid, blinking, blue light - in sky for significant amount of time

Boyfriend was on a quick trip to grocery store, less than a mile, and video phoned about a blinking blue light in the sky that seemed to be stationary. I was able to see the light on the phone even though the call quality was dark. Went outside with binoculars and looked north and saw the same light. It is visible to the naked eye. It is bright blue. Observed with binoculars and did not see a body/aircraft around it, above it or behind it. It is too high in the sky to be attached to a tower and we have never observed it there before. It has been in thee sky for half an hour or more. No sound and does not appear to be moving. If any movement at all, it is perhaps hovering to some degree. Other stars in the sky are quite clear so there is not significant cloud cover. This is observable even though there is light pollution in the area. We are going out driving to see if we can observe it from elsewhere as it is still in the sky as I type. We have video but hoping to get more.
