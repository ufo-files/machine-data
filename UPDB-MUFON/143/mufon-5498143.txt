I was leaving work peddling on my road bike headed south down Allen street at the intersection of Allen street and Delancey Street I first noticed a dark colored or black aircraft no larger than a commercial airplane in my southern view of the sky. I can remember some red and green lights on the craft some constant others blinking. The aircraft had to be over the Chinatown area when I first spotted it hovering above at building height in the sky. What made this aircraft stand out from the norm was that it made no sound, had no emissions and moved in ways that was not practical to any other type aircraft I had ever seen before.
