A glowing white ball shot over the house far too fast to be a plane and much bigger than a common meteor

A glowing white ball of light shot over the house around midnight on July 15. It was viewable through the eight foot skylight over the bed. It did nothing but shoot from the northeast to the southwest in a straight line. The orb was moving far too fast to be a plane and was perfectly silent. I thought it might have been a meteor, but it was much larger than the common “shooting stars” that zoom overhead now and then; the size is really the main reason I thought of reporting it. I’m not trying to make any claims about it but thought if anyone else had seen it, my report might add to the body of knowledge (if any) regarding the fast-moving glowing orb I witnessed last night.
