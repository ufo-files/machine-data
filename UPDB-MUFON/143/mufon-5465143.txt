{rjl} Making a video of the moon when objects appeared

I am a Certified Professional Photographer (certified by the Professional Photographers of America, PPA). On the night of October 15 2018 I started production of a short film I am producing by filming a sequence of the moon from the deck of my home. The camera I am using is a Nikon P1000 which has a lens capable of 3000mm optical performance (not digital). I had extended the lens to its maximum optical performance and started the moon sequence. The camera was mounted to a heavy duty tripod. This is a basic shot; the moon enters the frame and is filmed until it exits. During the filming I noticed an object appear in the frame but put it off as an insect. After filming, I went into my house to my office where my computer is. I ejected the SD card from the camera and inserted it into my computer and opened the file. I was excited to see the quality of the image that was shot in 4K resolution. As the opening sequence started and the moon came into frame, I noticed an object appear to shoot from behind the moon followed by another object which appeared to be chasing it or following it. A few seconds later the large object comes into view from deep space and appears to follow a trajectory at an angle to the top of the moon.

Time to first objects: 44 seconds
Time to second object: 53 seconds
