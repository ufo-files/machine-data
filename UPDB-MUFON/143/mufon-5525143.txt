A dozen or more lights

Lights were over the Peacock mtn. lots of people watching cars driving to get closer. Everyone was excited. Lights zooming back and forth, hovering, getting extremely bright, then dim. Like fighting or playing in the sky. One by one they got dim and moved off toward the north.
