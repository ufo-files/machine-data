Three lights.

IN KOREA APPROXIMATELY 1957 AND TEN YEARS AFTER MY FIRST SIGHTING. CLOSE TO PUCSON, KOREA, OBSERVED 3 LIGHTS AT DIFFERENT LOCATIONS IN THE SKY COME ON LIKE A LIGHT COMES ON. THE OBJECTS MOVED AT A HIGH RATE OF SPEED IN A STRIGHT LINE TOWARDS EACH OTHER. THE LIGHTS STOPPED AFTER MEETING, SQUIGGLED AROUND AND DEPARTED IN THREE DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS, AND VANISHED.
