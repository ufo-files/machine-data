{nd} Orange sphere, little larger then a basketball, moved along ground till it came to road we were on, up over trees, power lines crossed road back down to ground and off to NW.

My friend and I were returning from a meeting in Fredonia. We had drove toward Rt. 13 and had just emerged from a forested area to a cornfield, which had been harvested earlier. As I looked to my left to talk to my friend I saw an orange sphere off to the north approximately 500 feet away traveling to a point on the road we were headed down approximately 200 feet away. My friend, after seeing my attention had changed from him to something outside the window had looked also in the same direction. We both watched it travel across the road. I watched it till it went out of sight and his attention went back to the road. It appeared to be a little larger then basketball. It had an orange glow to it and was about a foot to 18" off the ground. We had a view over it as we were headed down a hill. It came to the tree lined road, never changed speed, which appeared to me to be traveling about the same speed as us, maybe 35 mph and went up and over the trees and the road, back down to the ground, changed direction and went off to the NW. The glow from it never seemed to light up the ground or anything else, which I would have thought it would having been so close to the ground. I then asked my friend "Did you see that?" His reply was "Don't ever ask me about it. I didn't see anything." It was a wet night with some rain sprinkles. It had rained earlier in the evening.
