{flg} Large Chinese Lantern Shape crossing over all of St. Louis in Minutes

We were on the porch with clear skies and what looked like a huge chinese lantern or a balloon on fire traveled in an arched path from East St. Louis to West St. Louis in a matter of minutes. We had no idea what could be that large and go that fast. There was a helicopter stationary in the area and the craft was much larger. I got part of it on an iphone video but it only looks like a white dot in the video. Completely freaked us out. My friend said it had two beams that came out of it before it disappeared on the west side. I didn't see that part as I was trying to take the video and a tree in the front yard was in the way and I lost it after that.
