(GS) Reflecting light of multiple colors. Object changed shape and form.

I was looking out the window at the stars and sky when I noticed an object that appeared to be moving closer and closer. I thought it might be a helicopter as I live close to a hospital that receives alot of air-lifts. 
After a few minutes it seemed to move further away and continued to cast a mulitude of colors and seemed to change in shape and size. 
I grabbed my nikon and set it in a stationary position and adjusted the settings to night and shot a few photos. 
The photos didn't show much until I zoomed in and to my surprise I had captured what I thought appeared to be very interesting.
Included are the photos I took. If the photos cannot be magnified when you view them let me know and I can try to blow-up the images and repost.
