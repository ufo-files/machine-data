Me and my family was in our backyard enjoying the afternoon when my grandson said look grandpa there's a eagle. I looked up and saw a eagle soaring what look like about a 1000ft in the air. We watched the eagle soar for a while when I spotted a 2nd eagle even higher in the air. I showed my grandson where to look and then I saw a very bright object above the 2nd eagle, it was round in appearance. flying from directly over my head at a very high altitude heading south. Me and my grandson both watched it for about a minute before it disappeared in the distant. 20 minutes later the object came back heading north in the same path it had come . This time it was a lot lower in altitude I estimate about 2000 or 3000 ft above the ground. It traveled north and stopped and hovered, south of me. from my angle it appeared to be at about a 40 degree angle. the object stopped and hovered for about 1 minute and a second object appeared coming from the south. it headed north and stopped next to the first object side by side. they were hovering for about a minute when the one on the left started to travel southeast. the other headed south. During this time I took several pictures with my phone and camera. when both objects had left about 10 minutes later I saw a 3rd object this one a lot lower in altitude heading south a very fast pace. I observed the third object with binoculars and it appeared to be silver in color reflecting the evening sun. It travelled at fast rate and was out of sight in about 15 seconds.
