connected orbs rotating and changing the shape of the connection

I was very late getting going in the morning and when I exited to the car from the garage when I noticed very far in the distance there were some very odd lights I have never seen previously anywhere. I didn't have the time to look and was so rushed I had to leave while these lights were still rotating and changing shapes but never moving its position in the sky..
I don't know why I took only 2 pictures but I did take the 2. The camera I used has an effective focal length of a 520mm telephoto lens and I must have been more concerned with watching these lights rotating in unison as a string, the 2 pictures I did take were likely within a couple seconds of each other.
It was Dark, 630am, kind of a mist like an ice fog in the sky and the ambient street lighting pretty much erased the stars from the sky with this slight haze to the sky.
I lived in the south east end of Barrie and I would estimate these lights could have been as far away as Bradford or Newmarket.
I have never been able to figure this one out, perhaps you can?
