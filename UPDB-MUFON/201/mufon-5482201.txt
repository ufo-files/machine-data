Discovered in picture taken at dusk of parking lot N of Lynnhaven Mall

My fiance' took a picture of the last rays of sunlight in a strip of shops just N of the mall. It wasn't until I was looking at the pictures that I noticed a diamond shaped series of lights, 3 white/blue and one at the tip was reddish. It was so far off, we never saw the object, but captured it on film.
