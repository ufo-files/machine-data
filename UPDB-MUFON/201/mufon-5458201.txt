I was walking my dogs after work about 0030 next to a small lake and open area and was looking at the sky for the location of the moon as I do every night. . When I looked to the right over the lake I noticed what I thought was a plane. but it was barely moving extremely slow and if it was practically standing still.. It was just above the treetops.As it floated past me I noticed it was triangular in shape with a blue light on each tip and the fascinating  part was the flat bottom of the object was entirely covered in large lights which did not throw a beam on the street and the object made no engine noise. I thought it was part of the military because it was heading in the direction of the airport. This object was the size of a commercial plane . I was unable to visualize the top of the object so I don"t know if there were windows.It did appear to be flat.There were no visible descended wheels.
