Grey disc that changed shape

Whilst walking through the park with my mother she directed me to the sky and said what’s that ? We saw a triangular shaped object move very slowly in our direction with no noise.  As it did so the front edge of it was wobbling like in a haze and it gradually changed shape to a disc. 

You could see what looked like two tiers and on the second tier were what looked like windows. 

We stood and watched it for approx 4 minutes. It was very low and emitted no noise at all. 

We watched as it slowly crossed where we were and went off into the distance at the same speed. 

We had never seen anything like it and both remarked what a strange feeling we had as we watched it. 

This was before the 1980s movie “Hangar 18” but we both remarked later when seeing this movie how much the UFO looked like what we had seen
