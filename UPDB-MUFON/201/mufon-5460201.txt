{cak} Fireball/ multiple objects

At 320 am i went outside to have a smoke after getting home form work. I looked to the west and about 20 to 30 degrees above the horizon saw what i thought was a moonset. It looked beautiful so i watched and quickly realized it wasnt a moonset because the object started to glow red/orange, brighten, rise on the western horizon, and appear to grow rapidly. I thought it 
Could be the moon behind the clouds but it also appeared that other much dimmer objects approached and joined the now very bright, glowing light just above the treeline. I took two pictures and sprinted upstairs to get my binoculars. I searched for about 1 minute before i found them and ran back downstairs to find the western sky completely dark and quiet. I took another photo from the same spot to show my vantage but theres nothing. I have no idea what i saw at this point so i decided to report. The light is in the center of the attached images
