{cak} giant (size of large building) 4 sided imperfect pyramid shaped/ silent/ hovering/ reflective dark surface (like tinted glass)

When I was around 13 or 14 years old my older sister and I drove into town to get groceries. We lived in a rural area in Oregon in between Portland and the coast and were driving along why 8 between Gales Creek and Forest Grove. The day was completely clear, no clouds and in the early afternoon. As we rounded a bend, I looked up and saw the huge pyramid shaped structure hovering in the sky above a field to the left of the car. It was huge (when looking out of the front windshield the bottom of the object hit mid-windshield and in order to see the top I had to lower my head and look up). 

It was silent and hovering stationary and did not move. I did not look away or speak except to momentarily look over to my sister who I could see was looking at it and then back to the road as she was driving. I was in a frozen or stunned state. I did not look away from the object as we drove under it and then passed by it and I looked at it through the back window as we drove away and we went around another bend. It took about 6-8 mins to drive toward under and past the object.

As I could no longer see the object I asked my sister "did you see that?".. she said "yes" I said "should we go back?".. she said "no" and we sped home. I told my parents and my dad went to go see if he could see it- he did not. I remember drawing a picture of it- but it has been lost. Over a surprisingly short period of time I began to forget about it and even now have a hard time remembering... I remember not quite being able to hold on to what I was seeing as it was so unfamiliar to me.

When I entered my early 20's I started thinking about the experience more. I learned of this website and decided to record what I had seen as I have looked on the internet and have not seen anything credible like it. I can only hope one day I will learn what I had seen.
