{cak} White/Yellowish interior glow with a blue aura arount it, After a few left to rights it Decended to the ground

I was standing at the corner of Felton Fair shopping center at the front of the closest store to Mt. Hermon Road looking West towards highway 9 with the Bonnydoon mountain behind. I saw what possibly appeared to be a plane slowly moving north a little bit above bonnydoon mountain from my angle. Object was about 3/4-1.5 miles away aprox. What caught my eye is when it stopped for a few second then moved south then to the north a few times as it descended slightly sometimes hovering for a few seconds in between. At one point when it stopped the blue aura went away and then returned, it seemed to be a perfectly round sphere type object. After the object went north to south it stopped for another few seconds and then dropped strait down to the ground out of sight into the trees. It appeared to have landed on the far side of highway 9 maybe about where the end of cooper street ends or at-least somewhere in that general area. There seemed to be a helicopter to the north of where this strange object went down and the helicopter was no more then 500ft above ground, it could have been a small plane as well, did not have a lot of time to look at it because it went out of sight and I heard no noise from either of the objects. There is a possibility that it could have been a RC helicopter with a weird light attached to it, but it sure did not seem like one. Was definitely not a plane, real helicopter or anything I am used to seeing and it did seem to be intelligently controlled. If anyone else saw this or knows what this was please post what you know or saw, I would like to know what this was. One other wierd thing is that I seemed to forget about this event until later the same night when something that reminded me about it triggered the memory, could have just been me forgetting about it I dunno.
