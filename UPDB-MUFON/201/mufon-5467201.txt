{td} Yellow orange glowing spheres over Peavine Mountain

Saw five glowing objects over Peavine mountain at 10:30 PM on July 14th, 2018. The objects appeared to be attracted to a person shining a spotlight from the ground near the peak of Peavine mountain. My wife and I witnessed these objects from our second story bathroom window as we were getting ready for bed near Robb Dr Reno, NV. The spotlight shining from the ground first drew our attention towards Peavine and the subsequent viewing of the objects which we believed were ETVs. Three of the glowing objects hovered over Peavine for roughly 20 to 30 minutes. These UFO1s where initially white in color with flashes of brighter white light. One of these three appeared to possibly be two UFO1s close together. These glowing objects gradually changed color to yellowish orange, with one object changing all the way to red and eventually disappearing in place. The other two objects eventually started moving in the direction of downtown Reno, blinking different colored lights with one of the two objects changing to a glowing blue. After these initial objects had left, there was one more object observed briefly as it blinked into the area and quickly blinked back out. This last object was whitish in color, and higher in the sky than the other three / four. The object that turned red and dissapeared in place also appeared to be wobbly when it was hovering. My wife and I were both amazed at what we saw.
