Three objects in formation over US HWY 17

On the night of Friday, September 25, 2021, while driving down US Highway 17, away from Saint Simons Island heading towards Brunswick, I saw three bright shining objects flying/hovering in apparent formation. The three objects formed a roughly diagonal line, sometimes slightly curved. The leftmost object was the highest with the object in the center slightly lower and the furthest right object the lowest. I called a relative who lives about 20-30 minutes away from my location at the time. Upon stepping outside, said relative was able to see all three objects in formation as well. Each of the objects appeared basically silver from further away, but as I got closer I could see that each of them appeared to have flashing red and green lights.
