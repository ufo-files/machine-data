2 small lights travelling in different directions, both appeared to be satellites

I went outside to star gaze as i usually do almost every night, as soon as i looked up i saw what i believe to be a satellite traveling across the sky East, i then noticed a second light traveling towards the object South, when the 2 lights met in the sky the light traveling south made an abrupt 90 degree turn and started following the object in front, the objects did not blink, moved like normal satellites (except for the 90 degree turn) and appeared to be very high up like normal satellites would, the 2 nights before this sighting i also saw the ISS twice at 9.07pm both nights, as the sky was the clearest i have seen it as far as i can remember, i lost sight of the objects when they passed over my house and i didn't attempt to go out the front to watch them further as at the time my feelings were that they were just satellites but soon after i asked my father if he believes a satellite can make an abrupt turn and his opinion was no.
