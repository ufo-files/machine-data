{cak} 3 orbs with trail moving South to North at sunset 11/16/12. Slow speed. Parallel to ground and equidistant from each other.

Pulled into my driveway at 5:05PM on 11/16/12. Sun was setting and it was particularly impressive. Looked to the West and noticed 3 glowing orbs moving at a slow rate of speed from South to North. They all moved at the same speed and seemed to be moving parallel to one another and fairly parallel to the ground (no climb or drop). I thought at first that I was seeing vapor trail from commercial jets from Pittsburgh International Airport, but it seemed odd to me that they were traveling so close to each other at the same speed. I did notice other vapor trails from obvious commercial jets throughout the sky traveling in many different directions. The three orbs appeared larger than jets and also appreared to be traveling slower than the immediate airtraffic. Received a cell call from ex-wife from Natrona Heights (15 miles away) asking me if I saw the three lights. She indicated that there were numerous people pulled over in Heights Plaza parking lot looking up at these three objects. 

The orbs never changed speed. They did not grow or shrink in size. The trail was brighter and shorter than that of a jet. Color was a combination of red, orange, yellow and white. Orb was predominately white with hints of yellow, red and orange. The trails were predominantly orange with hints of white, red and yellow.

I watched the objects for approximately 10 minutes as they moved north and faded from view. 

I was aware of possible meteor activity predicted for the weekend. My initial thoughts after ruling out commercial air traffic was meteor activity. It just seemd odd that these orbs traveled so slowly and in set formation. Without a real frame of reference, I thought that the orbs were somewhere in the vacinity of the North Side of Pittsburgh.
