stationary global shape with intensely bright fluctuating white, red and green lights at about 8,000 feet

1. Outside on front porch.
2/3. We are always checking out the skies. Saw a very bright collection of lights and we waited for it to move to see if it was an aircraft or possibly a high-flying helicopter. When the object remained stationary for several minutes we went inside to get our 20x50 binolculars.
4. The object did not move and has not moved in over an hour. As I write this it can still be seen with both the naked eye and in much more detail with the binoculars.The object has remained stationary. It appears to be a black sphere covered in primarily white lights. Red and green lights are also interspersed with the white ones. Lights go on and off seemingly at random. The lights are so bright it is difficult to focus on the object itself. My husband checked the local air traffic control feed and there were no flight plans that would indicate normal air traffic in the area for the time period.
5. I don't know what to make of this object. I have seen a lot of strange aircraft, weather balloons, drones and helicopters in my life and this is not any of those things.
6. I didn't lose sight of the object - it's still there as I write this.
