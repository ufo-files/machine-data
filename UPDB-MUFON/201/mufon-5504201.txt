Strobing orbs in close proxiemty,photographed and filmed after being followed.Appeared to be observing the viewer.Close enough to throw a stone at.

UFO filmed between Arnprior and White Lake Ontario Canada on Nov 5th 2013 at approximately midnight.Coming back from Ottawa heading home the owner of the film took the usual back road route.Noticing to his right was an unusual bright flashing strobe beaming in plain view about 15 meters from his car.The object seemed to be be moving in the same direction he was going.Slowing down to observe the the light he came to stop.The object stopped aswell.He got out of the car to start filming it.The object appeared to to be at a standstill hovering soundlessly at about eye level directly 15 meters from where he was standing in the adjacent field.He said it seemed to observing him as he was observing it.After filming and taking several photographs he hopped back in his car and slowly proceeded to drive away only to notice it was following him.After driving about a quarter mile with the object traveling parallel to the right side of his car he then turned on to the nearest gravel road and witnessed the object veer across a corn field over the road then in behind a farm.He then stopped the car got out and heard what sounded like a huge sonic boom and immediately began to film again.Now filming the object hover over the farm,he noted that it seemed to have an usual interest in that general area.
