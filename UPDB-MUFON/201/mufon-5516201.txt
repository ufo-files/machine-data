I noticed the UFO when I was taking photos of these birds, from the balcony of the hotel by the lake Llanquihue in Puerto Varas, Chile

I was on holidays with my wife in the south of Chile. We were staying at the hotel called "Caba1as del Lago" in Puerto Varas, that is by one of the biggest lakes in Chile (lake Llanquihue).

After having a late lunch at the hotel, we went out to the balcony to admire the view of the lake & the volcanoes that surround it. I took a few pictures of the lake & the city, and then decided to photograph the birds on top of the roof. Checked the pictures I had taken & noticed the object, immediately recognized it not being any plane, helicopter, etc.

Zoomed into the object on the camera & clearly saw it was a UFO.

Showed the photos to my wife who was amazed & surprised.

The position of the UFO would have been high above the lake.

I then tried to see if the object was still around, but could not see it. Amazed at the pictures of the UFO & excited, started to show off the photos to the waiters & other people in the hotel (all were just as excited & surprised as we were).

I've always been interested in UFO's & count myself very lucky to have my own authentic UFO photos :)
