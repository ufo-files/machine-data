Star like point in the night sky moving in a straight line then slowing down, eventually coming to a complete stop and proceeding opposite direction

I was in a field up the road with two of my friends when we looked up towards the night sky and saw that there were thousands of stars viable because it was pitch black in the field. I spotted what I thought at the time was a satellite, star like, moving in a straight line across the sky towards the East at a casual speed, when then It started to slow down, then I thought it might be a helicopter but then realized it cant be at there is no noise and they cant travel that high. It eventually came to a complete stop for about 2 seconds then started moving in the opposite direction. So we new it was not a plane, helicopter or satellite so we don't know what it could of been. Reason I did not report this sighting sooner is that I didn't get around to doing it ,saying to myself ''ill do it tomorrow'' then forgeting about it.
