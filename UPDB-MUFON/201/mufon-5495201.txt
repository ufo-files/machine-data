{rjl} Looked out the window at night, then I saw a large glowing object hovering above the trees.

I was in my living room one night, I looked out the window and saw a strange object hovering over the trees by my house. It made no noise and it glowed. The object was circular and as large as a car. It was yellowish in color. I immediately screamed. My father was awakened and ran outside within 2 minutes to look for himself but the object was gone. I had never been so scared in my life. I was paralyzed with fear and that is why I was afraid to go outside to look for the object myself.
