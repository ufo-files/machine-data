{dr}The 2nd biggest ufo I've ever seen.

I was standing outside on the porch. Looking at the sky as I always do on clear nights. Ever since moving to San Diego I have witnessed hundreds of ufos. This one I had to report. It was flying west from the east. Then it hovered I was looking at a rooftop of a the house to make sure it wasn't moving past certain point. It hovered for a few minutes. Then it got amazingly bright. It had looked like 3 lights in one. Then it vanished but was like it left behind a redness in the sky for brief second. There was no blinking lights whatsoever. About 2 months ago some friends and I were outside and I noticed hundreds of them in daylight. I see them so often in San Diego and have plenty of pictures to share. They must like the weather too. The most memorable ufo experience was when I was a child in Byron Georgia. I was at my grandparents and all the family was there. It was night time. We were out in the street and a huge ufo landed on the railroad tracks. I remember it just like it was yesterday. It was oval and had lights in the middle round ones with separation in between. It must of been 2 regular sized blocks away. My uncles ran to see it up closer and it flew away. Ever since I've seen them. None like the detail of that first one and the one I saw tonight.
