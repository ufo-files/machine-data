Object appeared unexpectedly while I intended to photograph Las Vegas' fireworks display.

Each year Las Vegas puts on a fantastic fireworks display along the Las Vegas Strip. Each casino/hotel sets off their own display so I decided to drive west of town to get a panoramic view of all the displays on my camera. Photo was snapped with a Sharp Digital Camcorder Model VL-Z5 set at infinity. The craft appeared without notice. I snapped this photo and lost track of time. I seriously do not recall any other details. This is the most detailed photo of a UFO I have ever seen. I would refer to this craft as dome shaped. Because it contains almost every color of the rainbow, I call this craft the "Rainbow Transport". This is very hard for me to admit, but I feel that I experienced the sensation of "missing time" and do not recall any additional details. The craft appeared above me about 200 ft. high and 100 yards away. I do not recall the craft leaving my sight.
