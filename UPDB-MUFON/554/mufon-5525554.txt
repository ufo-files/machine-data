triangle object flies directly over house

I was outside having a cigarette, and i was gazing at the sky as always. I was facing the southeast direction, and noticed a triangle object flying silently over the house, coming from directly behind me. It was pretty low to the ground, and was black with 3 white lights (one in each point of triangle). It blocked out the stars as it flew over. I simply watched the object fly slowly & silently over my home and off into the distance. I must also say that over the past 3 years i have seen many strange lights and/or objects flying over the texas and arizona skies. As soon as i saw this object, i was amazed because I have heard of other people telling about triangle-shaped craft and have never encountered one. I wasnt nervous or anxious, just very curious & excited.
