Traveling towards Heaton from wallsend across the Coast Road i saw what appeared to be three lights moving as one in a straight vertical line, blue and pulsating.

While coming home with my partner in his brother's car from his father's home in Wallsend last night i was in the rear right passenger seat looking out of the window when i observed what appeared to be 3 orbs of blue light flying together in a vertical line pulsating slowly at first i was not sure what i was looking at but when i was able to focus on it's shape it faded out. It could only have been in my view for between 3 and 7 seconds before it was gone. That same evening i saw 2 helicopters heading in that direction.
