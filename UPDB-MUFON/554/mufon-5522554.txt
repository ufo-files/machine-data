Gold coloured disc moving.

I arrived home at 8 and decided to put out rubbish bags before settling in for evening, so I went straight out to backyard;, I looked up towards the southwest and noticed a gold coloured disc, I thought it was an helicopter but it had no flashing lights and no sound, It moved slightly to the south and stopped, it remained stationary, then it moved again towards the west and again stopped .Suddenly it moved upwards until it was a dot, then disappeared. I watched for a few moments then put the bags in the bins. As I turned to go back to the house I looked up and it had reappeared, it was just hovering, I rushed into the house and grabbed a camera it was still there but looking through the monitor I couldnt see anything. I took the picture, suddenly the disc assended straight up and disappeared. I downloaded the pictures in the camera and looking at the black picture noticed a mark in the centre. looking at an enlargement it has an obvious shape, I knew without any doubt that I was looking at a very unusual object fron the way it moved, hovered and disappeared.
