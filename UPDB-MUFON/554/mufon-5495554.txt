{nd} Bright white

Me and my wife were outside around 8pm testing a new telescope and binoculars we just bought. the sky was clear with no clouds. Around 10pm, my wife says to me "I think I just saw a shooting star, but it's weird because is was to bright and bigger than a star, it moved fast and then stopped before turning off" when I look up to see what she was talking about, in the area she saw the light we saw 3 orange/red dots "turning ON" forming a perfect triangle, it hovered for a few seconds before slowly started to move from North to South. Once it passed in top of my house I was able to see better the triangular shape but the middle was transparent, you could see stars thru the middle of the 3 lights. What ever it was, it was very low and it was big, made no sound.
