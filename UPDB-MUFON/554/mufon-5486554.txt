{cak} Saw what looked like a flame that had a shadow of a person toward the front of the flame

It was late summer in 1956. I was 8years old. I was sitting on the back porch after everyone else went inside. I noticed what looked like a flame moving across the sky, which looked really cool. As I watched the flame it moved in a straight line from my right to my left. as the flame was straight out in front of me I saw what looked like a person in the front of the flame, who I waved at and he waved back. the flame continued to move to my left. As it got a little past me the flame then shot straight up into the air, which I thought was pretty cool. When I went in the house and told my dad and uncle about what I saw, they told me it was swamp gas. Being 8 years old what did I know. However that night while my brother and I were sleeping on the sofa bed, something woke us up. At that time there was a person standing next to the sofa bed. As this scared me and my brother, I stood up on the sofa bed which made me about 6 feet tall, which would have made the person about 7 or 8 feet tall, I still had to look up at the person. At that time not knowing who this was I told my brother to run to our dad's bedroom and I followed him out of the room. I could not see the person's face as he was wearing a hooded robe that covered his face. I have never had another encounter. But I have been looking for ufo's ever since. My brother did not see the flame, but he did see the person in the room.
