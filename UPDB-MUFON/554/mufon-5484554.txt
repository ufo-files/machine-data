{cak} Unlike anything I've ever seen or experienced.

We were on our way to celebrate St Patty's day. First noticed by my girlfriend she pointed up into the distance of the sky and said look a UFO.
I at first glance of the object thought that this had to be an air plane because what else could it be. It was slowly moving to the right and it looked white then it went completely red and abruptly turned left and spun going into the opposite direction. It went up and down very fast. Next it ascended up and swirled around again. Went right again and left then went down in the distance. My girlfriend ran off to try and see more but was unsuccessful. I got my neighbor as a witness. It lasted only a few minutes but after it was over approx 5 minutes after coming from the north to the south was an airplane. Low flying non commercial airplane. It had two very bright searchlights on the front. It went into the direction where the object was in the sky it was very low flying and then disappeared in the same direction that the UFO was seen. It was an amazing experience one I can not get out of my mind.
