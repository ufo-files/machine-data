{cak} 2 bright reddish/orange fireballs

Walking to my car in the evening to go to the store. I turned and looked over my house and saw 2 slow moving, low altitude craft. It was dark so I have no specifics other that they were both redish orange in color, pulsating, slow moving. Very bright, like 2 fireballs in the sky. Making no noise. I went back in my house to get my family. My daughter filmed these on her phone until both disappeared, possibly in to cloud cover.
