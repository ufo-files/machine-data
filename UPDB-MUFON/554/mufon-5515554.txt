This happened in 1979, virginia St. Reno Nevada. I had taken girls to the rollerskating ring in Reno. On our way home(Carson City), while driving I saw a light to my right (Virgina St.)I had about 5 girls in my car. It was a larger light different so I watched it and I was still driving. When it went above us it made no sound and I will tell you it wasnt very high above us. The girls in the car were looking out the back window asking me what it was, i remember telling them I didn't know. This object was just above the light post that were posted on both side of virginia street. So much has changed since then. From what i can remember virginia street was four lanes on both sides, this thing was larger than the road. I went to work the next day and I told my boss what we saw, he ask me to draw it for him. I remember it having symbol on the bottom of it. Egyptian symbols. I was 27 at the time and now i am 60. The object wasnt round nor was it a boomarang shape. My friend was behind me in her car, when we got home we talked about it but I don't think she remembers now. (she also had girls in her car)

The witnesses were about 10 years old at the time. I just wanted to tell you my story. I worked at the National Guard as a state emply. of Nevada.
