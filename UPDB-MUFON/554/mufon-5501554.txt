Bright fuzzy orb no nav. lights straight flight the 60 degree angle up an gone above ocean

My wife and I were sitting on the patio of the Ocotal Beach Resort restaurant watching the sunset, after the sun went down we sat for a while and at around 20:00 hrs I looked back over to the east toward our Condo and a bright Reddish Orange fuzzy glow appeared over the top of the hill close to the entrance to Ocotal beach, my first thought was an aircraft but it lacked Navigation light and I could not see any wing structure, it looked to be about 1000 ft ASL it continued on a straight course up the center of the bay with absolutely no sound at all, it was doing approximately 100 mph until it reached the middle of the bay then increased in a blink of an eye to jet speed were it went out over the ocean for about a mile or so and then shot up through the clouds at about a 60 degree angle and was gone, Liberia airport is close by but this was on no flight path that I have ever seen a plane use inbound or outbound from the airport
