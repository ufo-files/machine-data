{rjl} Bright orange Orb

At 8:55 pm I spotted a bright orange orb in a SSW direction through my neighbors trees. After a double take I moved position to get a better line of sight. It was moving horizontally towards the east with a very sight descent. Its seemed to cover a lot of ground rather quickly, but not darting.  I can't even roughly estimate its speed or size since I am not familiar with such an object , and do not have an accurate sense of its range. A bright glowing orb is difficult to discern. No perceivable sound. I called over my girlfriend to bring her phone which she did record the end of the event. It continued east , located in a south east position in the sky. It started to climb a little, while slightly turning south where it seemed to greatly increase speed. Its brightness increased, dimmed, then disappeared. My girlfriend took the camera off because she thought it was over, but it reappeared for a moment which she does catch. What is incredible to us is we had seen what we thought to be a ufo 2 weeks prior but dismissed it. That object as well as this one both disappeared in the same location of the sky. The observation from 2 weeks ago was also an orb but came from the east , brightening, disappearing, reappear, descending , disappeared to reappear in a different location, pulsed while descending, then seemed to turn green as it took off and a final dissapearenc. 

For the date and time listed on this tagged sighting There were no other aircraft seen in the sky at this time and zero cloud cover.
