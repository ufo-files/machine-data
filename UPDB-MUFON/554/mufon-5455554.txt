MY FAMILY OF 15 PEOPLE WAS VACATIONING IN A BEACH HOUSE, WHEN MY BROTHER AND MOTHER, WHO WERE OUTSIDE ON THE SIDE OF THE HOUSE BY THE GATE AND THE SIDEWALK, SAW THE FOUR RED LIGHTS OUT OVER THE OCEAN. IT WAS JUST AFTER SUNSET, SO IT WAS A CLEAR, PERFECT DAY AND EVENING.
MOTHER CAME IN AND ALERTED ALL OF US TO COME OUTSIDE AND SEE THE LIGHTS. MY BROTHER WAS ALREADY VIDEO TAPING THE LIGHTS.
AT FIRST WE SAW A FEW LIGHTS AND THEN MORE APPEARED.
AT FIRST WE THOUGHT THE LIGHTS MIGHT BE OIL DERRICKS OR A BOAT ON THE WATER, BUT WHEN THE LIGHTS MOVED UP AND DOWN AND MORE LIGHTS CAME AND THEN CAME TOGETHER, WE KNEW IT WAS SOMETHING ELSE. 
WHEN THE LIGHTS CAME TOGETHER, THEY WERE ABOUT THREE FINGERS APART USING THE RULE OF THUMB.
WHEN THE REDDISH ORANGE LIGHTS CAME TOGETHER THEY COULD HAVE BEEN AS LARGE AS A BATTLESHIP. WE GUESSED IT WAS MAYBE A MILE OFF SHORE TO THE SOUTH. 
I WAS IN THE NAVY AND THESE LIGHTS WERE NOT A SHIP. 
THE LIGHTS JUST FADED FROM VIEW, I DON'T BELIEVE WE HAD FOG THAT EVENING.
