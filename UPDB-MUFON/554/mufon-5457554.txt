{cak} Saw a huge craft with 3-4 light beams of 100 feet+ shooting out of the top

Me and my fiancé were driving home from Dairy Queen at 9 pm. Driving down a straight road to our home and she saw it first. Looked huge and square. Had 3-4 light beams shooting up from the craft. Like tractor beams but 3-4 of them and facing straight up like 100 feet above it. Looked like a very strong light where you could clearly see the beams of light. Saw the beams shoot up while we were driving. Never seen anything like it. Watched for 2 minutes while we drove home. Parked at house and went back out to street and it was gone. Clear skies that night and full moon. Next day there was black military helicopters flying around the neighborhood
