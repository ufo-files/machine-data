Bright orbs ascended in the sky, flashed, disappeared, then chevron shaped craft flew from orb flash west to east.

I was sitting on my couch when I looked outside to see a bright, white, flare-like light ascending over the tree line west of my house. The trees were about a half a mile away. The light appeared midair, then ascended what I can only assume was a few thousand feet. As it ascended, it grew in brightness and in width, appearing like a bright star, then flashed brightly and vanished. About a minute or so later, I could see flashing lights that looked like an airplane coming from that direction. As it travelled towards my house, I noticed that it was oddly shaped like a chevron, but I didn’t really give it much thought until it happened twice more, each time the same. The craft were anywhere between 500ft-mile above ground. The fourth time, the flare was bright red/orange, and the craft that flew overhead was much larger/lower to the ground. I grabbed my binoculars before it came, so I could look at the next one, and it was shaped like a black chevron with 6 blinking white lights down the wings with a bright red light at the peak. The lights would travel up the wings headed to the peak, and the final light would flash red. 6 white down each side simultaneously, then a dull red glow, and this would repeat as it flew. After the red light flare, there were 4 more white flares that resembled the red one only smaller and higher up. After the last one flew over, a helicopter came from the same direction. They all came from relatively the same point and travelled the same direction each time. I’m still watching for them as I write this, so there could be more. I’ll try to catch a picture if I can.
