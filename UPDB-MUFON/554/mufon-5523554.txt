Saw shape high in the sky speed across the entire night sky.

was looking up at the stars from my driveway in the night sky and within 15 seconds i saw what i thought right away was a shooting star so i was able to lock in to watch it. it was a shape. circular with 2 flat sides, was a hazy pink glow on the surface, way, way up high in the sky, it sped from east to west, no tail. what seemed like 5 seconds was probably more like 2-3. "i had the feeling of the phrase "i know what i saw" i lost sight as it sped all the way out of sight to the west horizon.
