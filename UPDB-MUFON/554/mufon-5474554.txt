{dr}5 Bright lights hovering in sky, helicopter moving towards them then lights disapeared

I was driving West on Route 50 almost to the Bay Bridge when my wife and I noticed 3 bright lights hovering in the sky to our left side (south). I exited onto Romance Rd and saw another car pulled over with their hazards watching the lights. I pulled behind them and then we noticed there were five lights. We then saw a helicopter moving towards the lights (it was much less bright and had flashing navigation lights). I pulled the car back onto the road to drive closer to get a better look at what the lights were. I turned right onto Irene Way where trees then blocked our view. I made a U-turn and went back to the main road where then the lights were all gone except the helicopter had turned around and was headed back in the direction it came from. I took pictures that just look like lights in the sky but one of them either shows some trails from the lights or more likely a photography abnormality.
