{dr}Observed very low flying triangle craft.

On the night of August 28th,2014 @ approximately 10-10:15pm I was walking out of my mother's house,as I turned left out of the driveway something caught the corner of my eye immediately I looked up thinking it was a bright shooting star and what I saw blew my mind! There's was a dense cover of low clouds, completely overcast as far as you could see at what I would estimate to be maybe 500 ft elevation and this craft was just breaking through the clouds when I saw it and it came right over top of me slowly but seemed to be picking up speed like it had been possibly hovering in the cloud cover but as it went right over top of me I saw a black triangle structure but looked more elongated then the standard triangle shape I now know as a "Tr3-B" but it looked to have two almost jet looking openings in the rear that completely ionized the air into this beautiful blue streak/trail across the sky that lasted up to 10-15 seconds before dissipating. It was completely silent and it ascended back into the cloud cover then once again descended back below the clouds and by this time it had went from being right over my head maybe 500ft to I'd say easily 1000 ft away and ascended the last time and never saw it again. I immediately ran back in the house and was so excited I could hardly speak and just said colored pencils, markers! I just saw a ufo! To my mother. Now its been a few years and have no idea where the drawing went but I have no problem drawing another of what I saw it's burnt into my head.
