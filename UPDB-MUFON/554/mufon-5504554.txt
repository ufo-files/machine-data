{kt}Driving south on Maybank Highway .This light suddenly appeared in the sky ahead of us. It was stationary and silent

I was driving south on Maybank Highway aprox. 6:30 pm. This incredibly bright and oddly shaped light was suddenly just there in the sky to the right of the road. At first I thought it was a helicopter with a search light but neither my son nor myself could see anything behind the light. The was no sound either. (my window was down). The light was pointy and shaped like the star in nativity scenes. I felt like it was pointed at me. It was in veiw for 3 to 5 minutes. After we passed it my son could still see the light he said it was on the front of a funny shaped plane. The wings of the plane were at the back. We stopped at the car wash for change. I was very nervous. We were at the intersection of Main rd and Maybank Hwy at 6:40.We did not reach the city limits of Hollywood until 7:30 or so which bothers me greatly.
