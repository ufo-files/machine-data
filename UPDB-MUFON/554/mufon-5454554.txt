{rjl} Small UFO found while taking 2 pictures back to back within 4 seconds.

On October 21 2019 at 2:22pm I was on the job at a customer's house in Haywood county NC. This customer happens to live at 4000 ft in elevation on a mountain in Maggie Valley NC. I was there to preform a annual service as usual. As I pulled up to the house that sat on the ridge I couldn't help but to look out at the beautiful views of the October fall foliage on all the surrounding mountains. It was at this time I decided to get my phone out and start taking pictures to show family and friends the beautiful colors of the mountains. So I took a picture with the phone in the upright position and then I turned my phone the opposite way and took the second picture. Then I commenced to start my service for the customer. Later that evening when I returned home from work I wanted to show my family the pictures I had taken earlier in the day of the fall foliage and mountains. It was then when showing the pictures to the family that I zoomed in and out of the two pictures and saw something (UFO) different in one that wasn't in the other. So the first picture that was taken in the upright position had a UFO in it when I zoomed in and the second picture that was taken horizontal didn't have the same UFO in it and it was taken 2 seconds after the first picture. I could see for miles this particular day and NEVER before or after the picture taken did I see ANY know flying objects nor did I hear anything. At 4000' in elevation in the remote NC mountains you can hear a mouse pee on a cotton ball. The UFO in my picture looks like a saucer/ tic tac shiny top dark bottom. In one picture and not the other taken seconds later. Very strange! This isn't the first sighting I ever seen. Would love to have answers.
