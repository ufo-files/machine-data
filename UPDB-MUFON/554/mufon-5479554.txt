Initial bright light shot down split into two bigger even brighter lights. One descending at 45 degree angle the other going straight down

Big bright indigo/white light in the sky appeared. Was facing west at the time. It then shot downward at a 45 degree angle split into two different larger lights then winked out. Both lights had white centres surrounded by Blue, violet, and indigo. Lasted all of a second. Though it is a flight path it was definitely too low in the sky and took up too much space to be a plane. Was not a helicopter either as there was no sound and again was too big of an object relatively speaking. Was not a fire work as there was no sound and the explosion of light was not big enough and no one was lighting fireworks over west Calgary at 430 in the morning.
