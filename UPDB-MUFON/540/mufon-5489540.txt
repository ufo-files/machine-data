IN SEPT. OF 2013 MY HUSBAND SAW A SHADOW FIGURE NEAR OUR SHED ON OUR RANCH, WHEN HE SHINED THE FLASHLIGHT AT IT THE FLASHLIGHT WENT OUT AND WHEN IT CAME ON THE CREATURE WAS GONE. THE NEXT DAY WE FOUND OUT GOAT, BY THE STREAM, CUT FROM NOSE TO TAIL WITH LASER LIKE PRECISION . WE LIVE ON A RANCH OUT SIDE GILROY AND MANY WEIRD THINGS HAVE BEEN HAPPENING. WE HAVE SEEN STRANGE ANIMAL TRACKS, HEARD WEIRD NOISES AND LOST MANY ANIMALS. THE GOAT WAS NOT A NORMAL ANIMAL KILL. IT WAS CUT WITH LASER LIKE PRECISION AND ONLY THE HEART WAS MISSING. THE INTESTINES WERE NEATLY PILED UP NEXT TO THE BODY. THE DAY BEFORE MY HUSBAND WAS BEHIND OUR SHED,WHERE WE KEEP THE ANIMALS AND SAW A SHADOWY BROWN FIGURE LOOKING AT HIM. HE COULD SEE NO FEATURES, BUT THE OUTLINE WAS OF A TALL 6' CREATURE WITH A HUNCHED POSTURE. HE HAS SEEN HIM THREE TIMES, THE LAST TIME A FEW DAYS AGO. THAT'S WHEN I DECIDED TO CONTACT MUFON. WE HAVE CALLED THE POLICE ABOUT THE DEAD ANIMALS, BUT THEY WERE NOT ANY HELP.
MANY WEIRD THINGS HAVE BEEN HAPPENING HERE.
