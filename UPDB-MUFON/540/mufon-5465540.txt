{dr}light that split into 2 then 3 then back to 2 then 1 repeatedly.reported on tv news,10 yrs later,the Bulletin sun. mag.

on a hot summer night in 1969,all of the kids on my street were outside playing. When my friends & I came outside,the kids seem to stop & looked at us on the porch of my house.I found out that they were looking above my house & watching the light in the sky. It made no noise except when it split into 2,then another black line came down the center & it split into 3. This went on for about 1/2 to 45 min.s I called my sister who lived about a half hour away 7 she & her husband saw it. It was on the 11pm news & Larry Kane or Tom Snyder reported it. They said don't call the police or tv stations. That it was just gas from the Marcus Hook plant in SW phila. However ,10 years later it was in a sunday Bulletin magazine feature promoting Close Encounters of the 3rd kind film. It was called close encounters of the phila. kind. They said it was never explained. My sister,neighbor & 1 other person saw it. I reported it to mufon several years ago but never heard back or anything. The witness are dwindling in number. Thank you.
