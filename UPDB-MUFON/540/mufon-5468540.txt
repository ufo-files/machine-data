{cak} OBJECT FLOATING OPAQUE, SQUARES WITHIN GET SMALLER TOP TO BOTTOM, 2 OBJECTS BECOME 4, 2 VERY FAST LIGHTS

ON MOTHER'S DAY 2018 AT APX. 6 AM MY WIFE CHECKED MOVEMENT ON OUR RING DEVICE AT 4:33 AM. WHAT WAS RECORDED WAS A NEIGHBOR'S CAT SLEEPING ON A CHAIR. ABOVE AN UMBRELLA WE COULD SEE 2 FLOATING OBJECTS THAT CHANGED DIRECTION SLIGHTLY. THEY KIND OF SHIMMIED THEN WERE JOINED BY TWO OF THE SAME. AT THIS TIME THE CAT SENSES SOMETHING. BEFORE OBJECTS MOVE UP AND OUT OF CAMERA VIEW OBJECTS SEEM TO ELONGATE AT BOTTOM LIKE A NEEDLE. NOW CAT IS STANDING ON CHAIR LOOKING ACROSS POOL THEN THERE IS A VERY FAST STREAM/S OF LIGHT COMING FROM ABOVE TO POOL WATER (NO DISTURBANCE OF WATER CAN BE SEEN) STREAM/S OR CAT ACTIVATE FLOOD LIGHTS AND CAT IS SPOOKED ANS RUNS AWAY. THE OBJECTS APPEARED TO GLOW AND WERE KIND OF CIGAR SHAPED I GUESS. WHAT APPEARED TO BE INSIDE WERE DARKER SQUARES THAT WERE STILL BRIGHT. OBJECTS APPEARED TO BOB UP AND DOWN AND MOVED AWAY. I DON'T KNOW WHY BUT INSIDE OF OBJECT REMINDED ME OF A HAIR FOLLICLE. WE HAVE NO IDEA WHAT THESE COULD HAVE BEEN BUT WE THOUGHT IT WAS WEIRD AND IT PEAKED OUR CURIOSITY.
