{cak} Traveling south on highway 33 entering town of Taft, observed three orange lights in sky traveling south east.

I was returning home from night shift work along with co workers Jose [removed name] who I was car pooling with, Jose was driving his SUV and I was sat in the front passenger seat of his vehicle. As we were traveling south down highway 33 we neared the north end of the town of Taft, I noticed three bright orange lights in the sky. I first thought the lights were mounted on oil rig equipment but noticed as we traveled south on the highway the lights were moving in the same direction. I pointed the lights out to Jose and he drove his vehicle to the albertsons supermarket parking lot in the town of Taft were I had left my vehicle, we pulled into the parking lot I got out of the vehicle and started to film the lights as the passed behind the strip-mall which I was standing in front of, unfortunately the flood lights on the building obscured the lights of the UFO so my phone didn't capture what we were viewing. As we were watching this event unfold I noticed a police officer also watching the lights, he then entered his SUV and I shouted over to him did he see the lights, the officer drove his vehicle out of the parking lot and made a gesture to me I believe acknowledging he had seen the same lights. As I was doing this Jose was still watching the lights as the disappear into the low cloud cover. All the time this event unfolded there was no aircraft sounds, engine or vehicle sounds other than Jose vehicle and the police vehicle. The UFO made no sound and glided by us very slowly.
