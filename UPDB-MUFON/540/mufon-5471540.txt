Saw light above me moving over my house and joined by five other lights heading east between the 2 towers

I was online in my bedroom late at night and decided to go outside for a smoke. I looked up at the stars above me and noticed a white ball of light slowly moving over the house. I went into the garden to see it over the rooftop and caught sight of three more coming from the north. These joined the first I observed, Joined by two more coming from the south. All met together between the two blocks of flats and slowly headed east. 
All this occurred in silence, apart from the faint rumble of traffic on the ring road half a mile away. 
Seemed like they1d finished their work, agreed to meet over the estate and slowly sauntered off home. Quite comical really. Especially all six meeting between the two tower blocks and slowly moving off into the distance.
