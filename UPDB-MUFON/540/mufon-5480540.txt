{jpg} Was out front with telescope with mom when saw large Ufo with 13 lights. I was also able to call my stepdad from out of the house to observe the craft

I was outfront with my telescope and my mom and when I first saw the UFO my mom was looking at Saturn (south) while I was stargazing looking north. When I understood that I was looking at a large UFO (2-3 seconds) I grabbed my mom off the telescope and pointed at the UFO. She instantly saw the UFO and started yelling in excitement. After both of us watching the craft for about 20 seconds i banged on the window for my stepdad to come outside and he came to also see the craft. We watched the UFO hover over our heads heading south until it faded off to nothing. There were a total of 13 lights that were lit up on the craft (6 on each side of a V shape and one at the point of the V). Another side note was that the craft was slowly spinning.
