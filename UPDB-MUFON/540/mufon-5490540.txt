{nd} HUGE triangle 6 light perfect formation with lights that dimmer-ed on and off in a pattern

My girlfriend at the time, her 2 kids and I were coming from dinner and driving down the street when she noticed the huge lights in the sky. At first it looked like a planet but it was to big, to bright and there was 3 in a perfect vertical line. Then while we were driving 3 other lights appeared that dimmer-ed on in a pattern and then we noticed 6 huge lights that made an enormous perfect triangle. I was to stunned to get my phone out to take pics and a video. All 4 us were watching for a good 5 mins till we made a stop then the lights dimmer-ed in a specific pattern off. I realized it was gone and I had no evidence!
