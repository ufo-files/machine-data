{nd} A red and yellow flashing object like a plane on fire streaked across the sky then disapeared.

I was walking easterly from my driveway to the back house after 9:00pm or so and noticed a flashing reddish-yellow light in the low SE sky. I thought it may be a plane on fire but it kept a straight horizontal path. It appeared to slow down for a second and then kept streaking on its path. I ran in the front house got my binoculars and ran back outside. It was still there on its path traveling west. I was facing south as I looked in the binoculars and saw a fuzzy orange oval-like object. It began to dim its light. I looked again with my own eyes but it was gone. I didn't get creeped out about it just curiously fascinated. I know what I saw even if It didn't get reported on the news.
