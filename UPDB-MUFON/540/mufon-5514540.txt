{dr}large object decended behind treeline

I was going to work about 10am, headed north on howell bridge road almost to the ramp to access I575 south, when I saw what looked like a large paper airplane desceding stright downward beyond the treeline just ahead of me, i drove to the next road and heard nothing and saw no signs of a plane crash, such as smoke or other indicators. The object was as big as a house, didn't see anything else and headed to work, when I got home that night around 7:30 pm, I saw and heard several helicopters around town. not sure if they were related to the sighting from that morning.
