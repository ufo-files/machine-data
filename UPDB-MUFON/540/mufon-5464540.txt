A light flashing very brightly in sequence fast then slow

I was standing in my garden having a ciggerette looking at the stars, noticed a few planes then noticed this moving flashing light I watched it for a few seconds and noticed the lights were in a sequence, one slow flash then one quick flash. There were no clouds. It was moving very slowly. I also saw the ISS it was a lot slower than the ISS. I called my partner to have a look and told me to grab my phone to record it. On the video you can see how bright the moving flash is as you cannot see any stars in the video or planes but can see this bright flashing moving light.
