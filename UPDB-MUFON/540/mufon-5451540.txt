Object was in the same field of view as the moon

I was taking picture and videos of fireworks. I was also taking pictures of the full moon. There were objects close to the moon that I thought were reflective orbs. One was, but the other one was odd. It looked like a solid object. What was really odd was the fact that I could see it in the view finder, but not with the naked eye. The picture will bear witness to what I was looking at.
