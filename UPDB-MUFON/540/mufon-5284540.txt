Three black Orbs flying through gray clouds, two flew by and went behind clouds, one coming in and out of clouds

Noticed a small black dot in the sky and assumed at first it was possibly a group of hawks at a high altitude, but then I noticed they moved way too rapidly. Two black orbs flew through the clouds out of sight at a high rate of speed, then the third black orb ascended downward and back upward and went through the passing rain clouds then it came back towards the North from the south, rapid changes in movement, then went out of sight. I was only able to see these objects by my tinted brow on my windshield, I could not see them through my clear lower windshield because of the bright grey sky. The objects would have about been the size of a small fiat car or a engine block. I witnessed these objects above the Channel 2 tower on Brookside in Tulsa, oklahoma
