{kt}Perfect formation, silent, no definitive structure, clear evening sky, four witnesses. Over a lake

I was out on Orchard lake fishing, when a friend pointed out "UFO". There were three orb/saucer like vessels traveling in a perfect triangle formation not skipping a beat. They went from one end of the horizon to the other silently and slowly. Until they were out of sight. There were four witnesses on the boat. Two being skeptics one more than the other. One orb disappeared. Maybe due to distance or relocation. It defiantly gave me a chilling feeling to see. Which everyone knows you do not get from airplane. UFO is a vague phrase to me, could be government spy planes. I do not know. But was weird.
