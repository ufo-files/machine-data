Medium size triangular shuttle flew overhead and sat still for about 5 min. Then took off and while it was a few copters showed up.

I was outside playing for a while as I hear a loud buzzing and vibration. As I look up I see a triangular object overhead and it was a grey/black color and looked very unusual compared to human built objects. I though it may be a military shuttle or something, but it was very unusual, it dashed overhead then stopped hovering for about 5 minutes but as the helicopters showed up it started to leave and at lightning speed dashed off leaving a trail of what I thought may be smoke. After I saw it I fell alittle woosy and got a minor headache but it vanished quickly. I lost contact as soon as it flew off.
