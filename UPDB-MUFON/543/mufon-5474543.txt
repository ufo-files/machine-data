{dr}Glowing Orb that faded in and out

I was letting my dog out at 4:30AM. I looked up and seen a very bright light over the roof of the house next door. It looked to be at least one mile out. It was a lot brighter then all the stars in sky. Then I noticed a plane in the sky. The plane was further then the orb but it passed behind it. The orb then faded out and then it came back before it faded out for good. The color of the orb was yellow and there was no sound.
