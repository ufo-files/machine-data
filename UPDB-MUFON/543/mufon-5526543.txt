While out, my friends sayy one flashing light in the sky. When recorded, many lights appeared on video.

I was at work while this happened. My roommate called and said he was having a smoke outside when he noticed a non-moving sparkling light in the sky. He called me to tell me about it. I told him to grab my digital camera with recording feature and get some footage. Him and another friend watched as more lights appeared flashing different colors and moving in different formations. This was only happening in the view-screen of the camera. They said they could still only see the one with their naked eye. After watching this on the computer even more lights appear and disappear doing all sorts of flashing, color changing and maneuvering, but never actually leaving. They recorded for about 23 minutes until the memory card was full. They also took a still.
