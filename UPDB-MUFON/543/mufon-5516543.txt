(GS) White ball of light traveling from west to east

I was outside on my back deck having a cigarette and looking up at the sky / clouds. I was facing east. There was a jet that came from DTW flying from south to north. As I observed the jet I noticed a bright white ball shaped object to the south of my location it was higher then the jet and slower traveling in a straight line from west to east. I called my wife outside who was in the kitchen. We watched it disappear into the clouds. So however high up the clouds were tonight that was the altitude. I checked WDIVs website because they usually tell us if the ISS will be passing by. Nothing was found. Checked the ISS website and it was nowhere near my location. After checking the reports on here this sounds exactly like the object that was reported today around the same time over New Philadelphia, OH.
