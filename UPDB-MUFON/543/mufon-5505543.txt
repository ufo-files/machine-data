{nd} 4 Glowing Hovering lights

Tuesday Jan.7 2014 @ 7:56PM I live near Schenpf Farms and saw them in the East toward Florance. There were 4 orange/red orbs that were big enough to lite up the clouds behind them and they hovered not moving in any direction. From the left one would fade out and then one would appear in front of the other three to the right. Watched them do this 2 more times till they all faded out at the same time. Did I mention they did this in a horizontal line with out loosing or gaining altitude. I wish I had my camera. The whole thing didn't last for more than a minute from when I had noticed it.
