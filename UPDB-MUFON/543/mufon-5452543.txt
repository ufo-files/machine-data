- I can see brands of jets flying into DCA from my vantage point. These planes were just dark with no apparent markings. (observed through a telescope)
- Large planes do not fly/tow banners
- This small object behind the second plane glinted/reflected light
- All normal air traffic in the area was stopped from going into DCA (flight path would be right-to-left over the Potomac. (Need secondary verification with someone knowledgable with flight tracking history: May 2, 2020 11:57AM)
