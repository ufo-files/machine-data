{dr}Shiny non-winged object flying South-North in a straight path Seattle area

My daughter was laying on her back in the yard looking up and asked me what that was flying over the house. I was BBQing and looked up after a few seconds and noticed was looked like a long cyliner shaped object with NO wings flying at a fairly fast rate in the clear sky. It was traveling South to North passing over the Woodinville, WA area. I never changed path or altitude for as far as I could see it. Again it had no wings, it had a reflective character to it and silent. 

I know there have been some reports of many orbs on July 4th in the area, but was curious if anyone knows what I saw. I typically see aiplanes fly East to West at a fairly low altitude always emitting noise. This was a non-typical flight path and silent.
