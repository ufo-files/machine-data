{jd} I saw a teardrop object fall from the sky.

I was sitting on back porch having a cigarette and I was just looking around and I happened to see a white teardrop object drop out of the sky. It looked like it went straight down in front of the two story house behind me. I thought it could be a firecracker but it didn't make any noise. Right after it disappeared I saw a reflection on the balcony fence of the house. that only lasted about 3 sec. I went inside to tell my Husband and we went back outside to see if maybe there were meteor showers going on. It was partly cloudy so you couldn't see that many stars.
