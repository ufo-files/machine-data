Dark shape with bright corner light and thin lights around two visible sides. Could have been a triangle, disc or square.

Object hovered and moved sporadically around the sky like a cursor when using a computer. At one point it got stationary appeared to begin vibrating and dozens of pinkish orange dots appeared at different points in the sky around it and it seemed to absorb them. My wife and I watched it for well over an hour. We shined a bright spotlight on it several times and I’m not sure but it seemed to react especially right after I turned the light off. Could not be identified as any celestial body in either of our stargazer phone apps.
