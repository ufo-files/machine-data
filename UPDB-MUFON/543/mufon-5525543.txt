Silent steady moving, too bright for twilight hour than any thing I have ever seen. Some radiant shimmering.

I looked up at the north sky from my yard and seen an unusually bright light in the sky and mentioned to my wife how bright it was for twilight. Then I said I could hear an engine running. Then again I thought that it was a motorcycle passing, and after the motorcycle had passed the object was silent as it passed with no noise at all. We watched it for about what seemed to be 2 minutes. It was heading south east at a steady rate until out of sight. It was very bright for twilight. With some shimmering. I have spent many hour of my life watching the sky and have never before seen anything like this. I have seen meteors, space station, comets, northern lights, and satelites. But nothing like this.
