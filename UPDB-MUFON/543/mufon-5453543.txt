Had this sighting when I was out fishing with a friend on baker lake. We were the only people on the lake that evening. The sun had just started setting when I noticed a ball of blue light ascend from the tree line across the lake in a slow fashion. As the sun began to set further it begin moving very erratically, in a diagonal direction. If you zoom in on the video you can see a pulsating blue aura around it as it accelerates. At one point it seems to have attempted to disappear which I’ve seen them do many times before, but fails. I felt very calm as I’m pretty custom to seeing this exact UFO but have never gotten a chance to capture it on video. If you zoom in on the video you can get a very clear picture of what’s going on.
