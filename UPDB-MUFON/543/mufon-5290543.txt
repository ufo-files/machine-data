Boomerang shaped stationary light

Arrived home, it was dark. Got out of car and looked up at the moon. I often do. To the lower right was a orange red boomerang shaped light with a smaller red ''reflection" or light above it. I watched it for a while and it did not move. It was cold, about 32 degrees and breezy..so I didn't watch long..maybe 5 minutes. It did not move. I took a picture with my phone. Went inside..15 minutes later it had not moved. Checked on it through the window..about an hr. from the time I first saw it..still had not moved. The moon moved in the sky..but the light seemed to stay and not change size or position. A few minutes later when I looked it was gone. The boomerang shape did not show up on the picture I took. It looked more like a small object in the pic. When I enlarged it looked like an object surrounded by clouds I am including the pic and the enlarged picture. It did not appear to be a cloudy sky...there were many stars that were visible. I wish I had gotten the moon in the pic. I don't know what to think of it. I have never seen anything like this before. Would love an explanation if there is one.
