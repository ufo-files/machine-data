{cak} Object seen was photographed with 4 military aircraft in same picture, and was also sent to Langley AFB.

To whom it may concern: I was standing just outside Hancock airport and near a Toyota dealership and sign when I observed something I thought was unusual in the sky. I happened to take a picture with 4 military Thunderbirds flying in formation past me heading North perhaps circling around the airport at the time. The object seemed to have an unusual shape and I could not identify it even almost 20 years later. This was the same day that an F-117 flew at the Syracuse airport, went to Maryland and crashed. I also have a picture of that plane as it was circling around Hancock to leave for Maryland. I have what I believe is the original negative, camera specs, 4 x 6 photo, with several 8 x 10 enlargements and a thank you letter from a military source. I merely considered myself lucky if I could get the unknown object with the 4 planes in the same photo which I did. I lost sight of the object after I took the picture. The number of witnesses could be several if the pilots of the 4 Thunderbirds are counted also. Maybe even the airport control tower or radar people from the airport could be considered as well.
