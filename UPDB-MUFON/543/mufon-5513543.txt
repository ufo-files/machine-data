{dr}Lights faded in and then were Observed hovering then changing spots with eachother.

At about 6:45 US mountain Standard time on Saturday, December 22nd, 2012, I observed a light(about the size of an asprin at arms length)fade into view above Greeley, Colorado. I got my sister and as we went to the window, a second light faded in, much like the first. As we were watching them, they started to pulsate randomly. I went and got my mom, and when we came to the window again, a third light had appeared. They faded in as 1 (left) 2 (right) and 3 (Center). Light 1 and Light 2 both faded out half way, then proceeded to switch places, then fully appeared again with the original brightness. Then Light 3 dropped unexpectedly almost to the horizon. then just as quickly as they had appeared, Light 2 Faded, then Light 1, then Light 3. the time that Light number three, the last, faded out at was 7:04.
