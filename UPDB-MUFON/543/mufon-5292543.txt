14 red lights. 2 at a time. Space between them

2 At a time. Like in pairs. Altogether there were 14 red lights. 2 would come from the tree area and went NW. A short time 2 more would come out. We counted 14 lights. No sound. They went up in a 30 degree (guessing) and them disappeared. Dont know if they flew behind a cloud. Strangest thing i ever saw. I reported it to our county sherriff dept which a deputy came to the house but by the time he got there they were gone. I took a movie of then but when i played it back there were not there. When i took the movie they were in the camera but not there when i replayed it
