I saw a silver sphere flying in the sky.

I was inside near a window. It was around noon I turned toward the window looked up at the sky moving towards me in a curved line about 50 to 150 feet in the sky not more than I saw something black on the front of the sphere then it spun in a circle without moving from the spot it was in. I saw it for a few seconds then for reasons unknown I looked away. When I looked back it wasn't there. When I first saw it I thought it was a satellite or camera. It was about a foot to 15 inches in diameter 
inches in circumference. I remember thinking as though I was being watched I stopped moving for a brief moment then walked to the window to get a better look for a few seconds before I turned away.
