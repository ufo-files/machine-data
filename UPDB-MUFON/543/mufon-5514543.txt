Fast moving star like object zig zagging.

I stayed behind at work on the 02/11/12 to do some vehicle repairs when I heard a noise outside the rear of the building, I opened the shutter door and checked out the car park but nothing was there? Outside the rear of the premises is not lit very well and I could clearly see the stars and noticed a plane coming towards my way with what seemed to be landing lights glowing, at the side of the plane moved a very fast star like object. It came right over the top of me very high up and changed direction zig zagging about in impossible manoeuvres then shot of so quick in the direction it was first heading. I would really like to find an explanation of this. The strange thing is I have seen this before about 14/15 years ago at a BBQ of my brothers where a star like object zig zagged about and then shot off. This is almost unbelievable but I know what I saw.
