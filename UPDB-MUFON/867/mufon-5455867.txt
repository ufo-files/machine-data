{rjl} Bright stationary light in the sky for over an hour

I took my dog out for a walk right before dinner. As I was closing my front porch gate, I noticed a Very Bright object in the sky to the west. It was just a solid bright white light stationary in the sky This object was 10x's brighter that any star in the sky. It took me about 15mins to walk my dog and the object remained in the same spot. I went inside my house to grab my phone which had a night photo mode so I can capture a good picture of this object. I took a photo of the Orion constellation in the sky as a reference to how bright this object was compared to the stars in the sky. I also took a photo of the moon in the sky as another reference to the object. The object remained in the sky in the same spot for approximately 1hr 5mins. I went in to eat dinner which only took 15mins, when I went back outside the object was gone. I took another photo of the sky where the object should gave been but wasn't. I also blew up the photo of the object and it's got a pretty interesting shape to it compared to a picture blown up of a star in the Orion constellation that I took as the reference.
