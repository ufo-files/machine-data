Two v wing silent flying objects

While looking at the full moon I witnessed a large plane flying from the east. I heard it first. Then as i was looking up, i saw the first object slowing coasting directly overhead i watched it fly north to south for about 10 to 13 seconds and then it disappeared. As i was talking to my housemate saying what is that what was that a second much larger one with more lights passed over at a little higher altitude. Both objects crossed the plane's flight path relatively close. About a minute later the sound of a helicopter could be heard. The helicopter was following the same exact flight path but a much higher altitude that the silent things . The first one reminded me of a glider the way it looked like it wasnt supposed to be up there without an apparent engine. There was no sound.
