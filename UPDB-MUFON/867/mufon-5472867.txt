{cak} Speeding object becomes stationary.

Was sitting in my back yard watching for the Orionids, when I spotted what appeared to be an aircraft or brightly illuminated satellite coming out of 036 degrees, on a heading of about 250 degrees. No position lights or rotating beacon were visible, although I assumed nav lights would become visible before it passed overhead -- except it didn't pass overhead! As I'm watched the object's approach, it came to an abrupt stop in the northeast sky. I watched for a few minutes, but the object remained stationary, looking like a bright star. I look down at my phone, and when I looked back again, it was gone!
