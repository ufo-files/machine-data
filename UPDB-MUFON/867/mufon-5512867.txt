Cigar shaped object with intense front- and backlight

I was next seated to my friend in the car he was driving, we have been working and he brought me back home. As usual I do look at clear night sky and first I saw a intense red light in the sky, my friend saw it too, he stopped the car and we watched out of the front window of the car and we saw a cigar shape - distance from ground to object unknown - it wasnt a plane or a helicopter, they usually have to blink here in switzerland and it wasnt a known noise to hear when I open the window of the car, it was like a hovering hum. It was a straight flight of the object, it seems for a second it would like to stop but it flew on - the backligt was in a intense blue/white. I never saw an object like this before in our night sky.
First I thought its a Star, then a airplane and then a helicopter - but it was none of them.
Since I feel that there is "more" outside our planet I regulary watch the night sky - so I still hope that we gonna meet other life forms.
The object just get out of our sight then - during of this event; aprox. 3min
