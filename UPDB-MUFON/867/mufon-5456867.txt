{tjd} Lights in the sky hundreds

Straight line of lights appearing out of the sky flying.. Over 100 of these ufo lights in a row some staggered, some together, some stopped. A brighter light then comes from the opposite direction and stops in the middle of the line and stays there. Lasts about 40 minutes as one by one disappeared. 5 witnesses to this encounter at about 5 am on 11/25/2019.
