{rjl} I was taking photos of airplanes and noticed an orb in one of the photos

I was driving in the far east phoenix valley observing the sky around 9 am. About twenty planes came from east of San Tan Valley omitting heavy trails. I decided to snap some photos with my Samsung Galaxy s6 edge phone camera. Observing the photos later I noticed an orb in one of the photos. When I zoom in on the orb I can clearly see it has five white lights on it.
