star moving iradic in zig zags and circles shooting up and down in small space in sky

It looked like a large star in the sky. My sister and I went to go watch the meteor shower Wed night at newport beach. We noticed a large star that was actually moving irradicaly in the sky! NOT Stationary! It was shooting up and down what appeared to be miles at a time. It was changing colors from blue to yellow to what appeared to be red on top and it would get brighter and the dimmer. It also moved in huge zig zags and figure 8's and did a downward spiral when ever a plane flew below its path. We atched it perform manuvers over an hour and a half. we wanted to see it fly off but it was getting real late so It was still active at 3:45 am when we left it was witnessed by others on the beach as well
