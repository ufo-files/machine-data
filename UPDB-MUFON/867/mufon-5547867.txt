There appeared to be three at the time. They were disc-like shaped and had lights on them.

I was in the car at the time, travelling to Belgium. It was early and quiet in the morning. I was looking out of the window, when I realised there were three strange disk-like shapes in the sky. Those objects weren't moving and were pretty big for a bird or a plane, so my thought was it had to be UFOs. I was really excited by this phenomenon. I decided to take a photo to observe it more closely. Looking back I saw there were also lights on them. Two UFOs were close together and one in a distance. The one in a distance was more detailed. As I kept driving on, I lost the view of the objects.
