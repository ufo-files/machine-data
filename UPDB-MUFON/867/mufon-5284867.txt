We saw what looked like a sideways building shaped like a boat descending over the treetop.

Around 10:30 pm Saturday October 2nd we were driving NE going home. To the right over the treetop was a huge sideways building looking thing descending over the treetop NE. I saw 8 lights on all the corners. There was what looked like a cabin or cockpit of some kind on the other side in the back. It was slowly descending super close but not a sound. Exactly the opposite of sound, an eery silence. We followed until we had to turn but went down another road to try to find it but it was gone. When we got home my phone was on 1% which was weird because last I checked it was fully charged and I had not touched it all night. Within an hour I completely forgot about it until the next day when my partner brought it up to family. I normally would have been telling everyone and talking about it all night and all day so that was weird. My uncle had my partner and me draw what we saw separately without us have even talked about it together and we both drew almost the same thing.
