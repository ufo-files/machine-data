single flickering blue light became 3 (triangle) caught my attention while watching fireworks display in the distance (Nerja center)

Definite triangular craft overhead which would follow a pattern of shooting toward the oceans horizon at insane speed, looping back to the exact same spot initially spotted, then hovered. Began as a single blue light when I first noticed it in my peripherals while observing Nerja's firework display in the distance from a balcony. I thought I was losing my mind so I called my sister and she saw what I was seeing. It then became two, then three, but would move as one object (triangular). I could not make out whether it was transparent or just dark in colour as it was almost midnight. After about 20 minutes of observing the phenomenon, we decided to head to Nerja center for drinks and food, walking there for about 20-30 minutes while observing the ufo continuing it's path / pattern. We had dinner, and about an hour later headed to the beachfront where it was STILL up there! It was happening for as long as we observed before heading to bed - which was at least 3 hours. While on the beach we noticed at least 100 more people on the pier / ocean observation platform of which most were pointing up at the sky - yet I am yet to ever find anything on this sighting since the occurrence! It was incredible - non threatening, no noise, but many, many people saw this. 

While on the beach we saw one two or three more flashes which seemed disk like, which emitted a beam at one point.
