{jpg} UFO EMERGING FROM LA GUARDIA AIRPORT

ONE SUMMER EVENING IN 1968, 17 YEARS OF AGE AT THE TIME, I WAS WITH MY IMMEDIATE FAMILY IN THE PARK FACILITY WHICH FACED REAR OF MY PROJECT BUILDING. IT WAS JUST ABOUT 7:00 TO 7:30PM AND I WAS SEATED ON A SWING FACING SOUTH. TO MY LEFT (EAST ) WAS MY BUILDING SOME 20 STORIES HIGH, TO MY RIGHT WAS THE PARK AND THE PUBLIC SCHOOL, TO THE FRONT, LOOKING SOUTH WAS MY FAMILY CHATTING ON THE BENCHES, MY MOTHER AND FATHER, MY UNCLE AND WIFE, AND 2 COUSINS . . . I COULD ALSO SEE THE DISTANT EMPIRE STATE BUILDING ( THIS WAS AT 137TH STREET IN THE BRONX ). I AM AND WAS AN AVID AIRCRAFT ENTHUSIAST PLANING TO BECOME A PILOT IN THE USAF. AS I WAS SWAYING IN THE SWING A BRIGHT LIGHT, COMMENSURATE WITH A STAR LIKE VENUS SIZE AT ARM1S LENGTH CAUGHT MY ATTENTION AS IT APPEARED FROM THE SIXTH LEVEL STORY OF MY PROJECT TO MY LEFT OR THE 10:00 O1CLOCK POSITION. THIS WAS THE ROUTE THAT AIRCRAFT COMING FROM LA GUARDIA AIRPORT FREQUENTLY TOOK. THIS TIME THE FAMILIAR AIRCRAFT LIGHTING WAS NOT PRESENT, INSTEAD , IT WAS A BRIGHTLY LIT STARLIKE SPHERE. I FOLLOWED IT AS IT MOVED FROM EAST TO WEST IN A RISING ELLIPTICAL MOTION OR PARALLEL TO THE EARTH CIRCUMFERENCE AT APPROXIMATELY TWICE THE SPEED OF A JETLINER ON TAKE OFF PATTERN SOME 300 KNOTS. . . THEN . . . AS I WAS TRYING TO DISTINGUISH SOME DETAIL OF THE OBJECT IT BEGAN TO FADE OUT AT AROUND 11:00 TO 11:30 O1CLOCK POSITION. AT THE SAME TIME IT BEGAN TO SLOW DOWN UNTIL IT CAME INTO A HALT !. I WAS MESMERIZED. . .THAT WAS NO HELICOPTER, MUCH LESS ANY AIRCRAFT THAT I KNEW OF THEN. . .I IMMEDIATELY BEGAN TO SHOUT TO MY PARENTS TO COME AND SEE WHAT I WAS SEEING . . .INITIALLY THEY JUST WENT ON CHATTING , BUT I GOT THE ATTENTION OF MY UNCLE WHO BEGAN TO WALK MY WAY. . .I JUST COULD NOT / WOULD NOT TAKE MY EYES OFF THE OBJECT THAT NOW LOOKED LIKE A BLACK HOLE AGAINST THE CITY NIGHT LIT SKY. WHEN MY UNCLE WAS SOME HALF WAY TO ME, I IMMEDIATELY SAW TO MY AMAZEMENT HOW THE SPHERIC OBJECT LIT UP AGAIN AND SPED OFF IN A SOUTHWARD DIRECTION PERPENDICULAR TO ITS ORIGINAL PATH AT A TREMENDOUS IF NOT INCREDIBLE SPEED UNTIL IT WAS OUT OF VISION RIGHT AFTER IT WENT PAST THE EMPIRE STATE BUILDING. I LATER IN RETROSPECT CALCULATED THE OBJECT AT SOME 100 FT IN DIAMETER AND AROUND 15 TO 20,000 FT IN ALTITUDE. IT THEN SPED OFF INSTANTLY AT AROUND 2,000 KNOTS. 
IN 2007 I WAS DOING SOME SEARCH ON THE INTERNET AND AN ARTICLE APPEARED ABOUT SOME ANIMAL MUTILATIONS THAT WENT ON THE FLUSHING MEADOWS AREA OF QUEENS. WHEN I READ THE ARTICLE I IMMEDIATELY ASSOCIATED THE INCIDENT WITH MY CE-3 EXPERIENCE. NUMEROUS ANIMALS WERE BLOODLESSLY DISSECTED IN THE FLUSHING MEADOWS ZOO AND IT COINCIDED WITH THE DATE OF MY EXPERIENCE. 
TO THIS DAY I OFTEN THINK ABOUT THAT SIGHTING AND EVER SINCE I BELIEVE IN EXTRATERRESTRIAL LIFE.
