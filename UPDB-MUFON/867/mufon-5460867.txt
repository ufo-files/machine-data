{cak} It was a square shaped craft that reassembled a giant drone.

I witness all kinds of "interesting" aircraft from my backyard late at night when I am smoking a cigarette, as it faces Alliance Airport. I've been caught off guard once or twice witnessing something I cannot explain and fumbled for my phone each time to try to capture it on camera, but it's been to quick. However what I witnessed this morning made me literally jump out of my seat, gasping and vehemently trying to record what I was seeing. I heard it before I saw it then I saw a craft taking off like any plane would, but this thing didn't have wings, rotating blades or anything I would normally associate with a aircraft. It was square shaped and looked flat looking up, with large, blinking lights on each end. It almost looked like an enormous drone, but wasn't hovering it was moving and fast. I honestly don't know how to describe it but it was something I have nothing to compare it with and I have seen all kinds of different planes, jets, helicopters, etc living as close to the airport as I do. I tried to get it on camera unfortunately what I did get is the last few seconds of it before it disappeared over my home. The video does no justice to what I heard and witnessed.
