{cak} Bright stationary red glow that slowly dissipated

Returning from picking up a child at a class, out of habit I looked south to take a look at Jupiter but saw sparse cloud cover and a bright red glow roughly where Anteres would have been at that time. Surprised, I pointed it out to my son and we watched it, thinking it would move closer or further away and we'd figure out what it was. It stayed stationary, obscured by a layer of clouds and slowly dissipated.
