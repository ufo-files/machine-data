{nd} strange huge light found over Idabel OK moving at irregular speeds

I was on my way from picking my girlfriend up from work when we get to a stop sign i notice a very bright light hovering above the old wood shop in our town. The light was so bright and big that it could not go unnoticed. So i get my camera out on my phone and started to record the orb. Soon as i start recording it starts moving at swift soft speeds but somehow even though it looks like its traveling slow its actually hard for me to keep up with it as im driving at speeds up to 80 miles an hour it progressively speeds up beyond my recording abilities and disappears behind the trees where i lost the craft as it goes through the clouds and disappears. Chills go down my body as i think to my self was that an actual ufo that i just saw. Stunned by the event i have decided to turn this video in for experts to examine.
