Oval shaped UFO, which was Grey Metallitic in colour.

I saw a UFO on Holiday in June 2001, Thailand's Unesco Sukothoi World Heritage Site. It appeared as a star like object which was oval in shape under close scrutiny. I took a photograph from distance with no telephoto lens. The object on the photgraph appears oval in shape. It could not be a meteor as there is no trail in the sky. There was no airplane traffic in the sky. It is not Venus as it is oval in shape. A Loud electrical humming emanated from the craft and filled the air.
