{cak} Observed via ISS application on my Samsung PAD

At 6:41PM AZ time, I opened the NASA ISS Application on my Samsung Tablet and noticed 2 white orbs/lights while the ISS station was directly over the starting of FKA USSR. In the screen shots that I captured 1 of the 2 lights remains stationary on the left while the ball of light on the right ejects to the right and maintains straight path leaving the stationary orb remaining to its left. Then all of the sudden the ISS feed went down.
