Orange/Red orbs flying

3 orange red Orbs came over the house from the se. They were in a triangle formation but like they were rotating around then all of the sudden split way out from each other then came back and formed into a single light. I couldn’t get my phone to focus so I ran in and got my mother out of bed and told her and she ran out and when I pointed to the orb it was traveling e as we watched it slow down to almost a hover it split into two orbs and then streaked across the sky and disappeared. Not 3 minutes later another orb came from the e and stopped then disappeared. I pulled up sky view on my phone and the orbs went directly toward the constellation of Capricorn. The other orb came from Capricorn.
