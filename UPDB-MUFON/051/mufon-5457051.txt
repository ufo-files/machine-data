{cak} It looked like a reflective orb.

My buddy and I were driving down Canterbury Rd and I just happened to look up and and straight out the window in front of me was a reflective orb. I thought that it must be pretty large because it looked like it was pretty far away but it was still a pretty good size. At first I thought that it was a reflection of something in the vehicle, so I looked out the side window and it was still there. As soon as I told my friend about it I looked back and it was gone! No vapor trails, nothing!
