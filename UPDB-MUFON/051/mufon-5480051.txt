Three Craft During 3 Minutes

On Monday September 26, 2016 at 3:15 pm, I decided to take some random videos over the treetops behind the house which overlooks Blue Springs Lake. This is because I had once again been noticing fleeting and very momentary fractions of a second of movements up, down, right and left. If one pays attention, this occurs daily.

I took six random videos over a span of five minutes with each one varying in length. In three of the videos I found evidence of craft moving through the screen. 
1	The first video was taken at 3:15 pm and lasted 17 seconds, and showed a black sphere moving from right to left in the lower quadrant of the screen above the tree tops.
1	The second video was taken at 3:17 pm and lasted 22 seconds and showed a faint grey sphere moving upward on the left side of the screen.
1	The third video was taken at 3:18 pm and lasted 12 seconds and showed a faint grey sphere moving from left to right at a slight angle in the upper quadrant of the screen.

Video #1
The craft in this video was the clearest of the three and very obvious as a black round spherical craft. It moved slowly compared to the other two craft as it lasted 3.56 seconds as it moved from the right to the left side of the screen from an elevation of 201 above the lake from 3401 to 3331.. The distance travelled, assuming that it was around 1 mile away, calculates to a distance of .15 miles in 3.56 seconds. The speed computes to be 151 mph. It dropped 3.51 from right to left in its trajectory across the screen. Interestingly, the distance that it travelled from first observation to probably hitting of land or water is about .86 miles. It just so happens that there is a small unnamed lake due west of the initial sighting that is .87 miles away. This most likely was its destination.
                
Video #2
The craft in video # 2 was a light grey sphere that moved upward at NNW or 3341 and travelled a distance of .1 mile in .73 seconds. This converts to 493 mph.
              
Video #3
The craft in video # 3 is at an 181 elevation above the lake travelled from left to right on the screen a distance of .19 miles in .37 seconds. This converts to 1,868 mph.

Discussion:
In a span of only 3 minutes, three different types of craft were captured on video each travelling in a different heading and at different speeds. This was due to my curiosity. I suspect however that if videos had been taken for several hours, much more would have been revealed.

From speeds of 151 mph to over 1868 mph, the three craft obviously had their own agendas. The craft in videos #2 and #3 were similar in size and type-- being light and somewhat hazy grey spheres. It is very difficult to obtain any clarity beyond what is in the photomerged frames above. The black round craft from the first video however was able to be fairly accurately resolved into its shape as a sphere with clear lines. 

The craft appear to be unknown aerial vehicles, demonstrating speeds (493 mph and 1868 mph) which defy those of known human-made small aircraft. For example the top speed for a Piper Cub is 87 mph, while the top speed for a Cessna 182 is 173 mph. This analogy is despite the fact that none of the craft in these videos even remotely resemble human-made small aircraft. 

The probable target of descent of the black spherical first craft being only .86 miles distant makes the speed of 151 mph illogical if it were a normal and known aircraft such as a Piper Cub or Cessna 182. (A Cessna averages 72 mph at landing, and a Piper at about 60 mph). 

All three craft either appear to be headed to entering a lake, or have departed from a lake. The first craft appears to be descending straight toward the unnamed lake behind the Lakewood Oaks Golf Clubhouse. The second craft appears to be departing from 3341 azimuth, or the westernmost portion of Blue Springs Lake. The third craft appears to be ascending to the right from the unnamed lake behind the Golf Clubhouse. 

Origins & Destinations:
1	The origin of the black sphere in the 1st video is unknown. Its destination appears to be the small unnamed lake west of highway 291, at 3101 azimuth from point of observation.
1	The origin of the ascending grey sphere from the 2nd video appears to come from the westernmost area of Blue Springs Lake at 3341 azimuth from point of observation.
1	The origin of the grey sphere from the 3rd video, since it has an angle of climb not unlike that of the angle of descent of the black sphere from the 1st video1could be from the same small unnamed lake. The angle of descent of the first craft was 3.51 while the angle of ascent of the third craft averages to about 41, close enough for a margin of error.

Summary:
1.	Three craft were seen in a span of 3 minutes.
2.	Two of the craft may have been coming from and going to the same location.
3.	The speed of descent of the black sphere entering the unnamed lake was much slower at 151 mph, but the craft that seemed to be departing from the same area was very fast at 1868 mph. This would be logical to conclude about such movements.
4.	As of this report, five bodies of water within a 5 square mile triangle have been reported to be the origins and destinations of many craft. 

These lakes include:
1	Blue Springs Lake. 
1	Lakewood Lakes. 
1	The unnamed lake in this report (just west of Highway 291 and behind the Lakewood Oaks Golf Clubhouse)
1	The unnamed lake .3 miles west of Blue Springs Lake1s middle and Western shore.
1	The unnamed lake at the Bass Pro Shops mall west of the intersection of Highway 291 and 40 Highway.

Conclusion:

The fact that the speeds of other two of the craft were 493 mph and 1868 mph are proof that none of them are normal human-made craft. The shapes of these three craft being spherical are also not shapes of human aircraft. The origins and destinations of the craft appear to be lakes, and not airfields. Therefore it is reasonable to conclude that none of the three craft in these videos are of human origin.
