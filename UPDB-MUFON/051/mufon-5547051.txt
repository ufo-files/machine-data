Saw star-like object move at a high rate of speed.

I went outside to my driveway and looked out in the distance towards Richland, WA. I saw a bright star-like object that seemed to move from left to right in a rapid motion. The star-like object was over Richland, WA. The object began to move foreward towards Pasco, WA in the direction of my house in a very swift and fast movement. As the object got closer to flying over my house I could not hear any engines or helicopter blades coming from the oject. The object then flew over my house at an altitude of approx. 29,000 ft. I lost sight of the object as it flew into the horizon. The object had not strobing or flashing lights like a plane or helicopter would. It did not make any noise.
