{jpg} Boomerang Shape Flying over San Diego.

8:25pm. San Diego. I was out smoking, looked up and saw faint stars in a V-shape. Then I realized it was moving. No lights (on). No sound. Just east of San Diego going north. Approximately 1 mile above. Barely visible. I only saw it because the 5-6 lights under the object were reflecting the city lights. As I focused more, I could see the outline. A slender, long boomerang. Rounded point front. Tapered "wings" Observed it for about 20 seconds until it faded out of sight.
