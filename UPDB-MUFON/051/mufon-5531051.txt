Explosion over Santa Cruz Mountains.

A large white object entered the atmosphere very fast and exploded in the air over the Santa Cruz mountains, just west of Loma Prieta. There was no big sound, just a muffled pop. When it exploded concentric rings came from the center in a plane. They were all colors of the rainbow, and I could feel them pass through me because I was at the same elevation as the event. It felt like vibrations, or if wind could pass through you. Roughly 3000ft. I was looking out the window over a scene of the whole mountains south of the highway 17 over pass and Monterey Bay, and it was broad daylight. The news reported it as a meteor. I believe it was a ufo crash. There were no visible debris. And it came in realllly fast!
