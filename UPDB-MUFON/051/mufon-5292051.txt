Crescent shaped orange orb

On August 24th 2020 at 10:01 pm, My wife and I witnessed an unusual crescent shaped orange orb or light outside of our home. The object was about treetop height and located in the south west sky. We originally thought it was the moon. However as I was attempting to take a video of the object it disappeared. The moon that night was located in the south east sky. There was also a “mist” or something surrounding the orb and it seemed that the orb would disappear within the mist. I did manage to take a photo of the object.
