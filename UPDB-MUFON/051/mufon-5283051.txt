Silent, large dark object observed

Bill was sitting outside with his wife at 10:30 pm. He saw a large, dark object moving silently east to west overhead towards Luke Air Force Base. The object was large enough to block out stars. When Bill called the police, the police gave him air traffic control. Air traffic control said, "We have stuff that doesn't make any noise. They were supposed to have their lights on."
