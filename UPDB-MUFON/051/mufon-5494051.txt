{cak} saw bright object in sky,cloud formed about 20 feet from object then cloud moved to object and the cloud broke up object was gone . sky filled with planes and helicopters, they left and object shot up from ground went south.

I was with my friend Bill and he dropped me off at the end of my road where I lived, a short walk to the house about 200 yrds. I was walking down my road when I noticed a bright light in the sky. It was not moving or making any sound. It was about a half mile away, hovering over a wooded area. It was very bright, you could tell it was not a plane or helicopter. As I walked down the road I saw a cloud form about 20 feet from object then the cloud moved to the object. So I ran into the house to wake up my parents and sister. When we came out of the house the cloud was breaking up and the object was gone. My family did not stay outside very long but I did and about 10-20 min. later planes and helicopters filled the sky, they were everywhere, flying all around. I sat outside watching and waiting, I just knew it was not gone. And about 15 min. after sky was clear it shot up from the ground, it was the same size but not as bright. It was in the same place as before, when the cloud covered it and it went to the south. This all took about 2hrs. My friend Bill was home watching a ball game on late, when he saw it out his picture window.
