Tic-tac shape to east of Denver/aurora around 2pm 11/22/21 with 2 accompanying orange orbs

At my friends house in aurora, the power went out, so we went outside to see if the neighbors also had issues, and then remained outside since the weather was nice.
As I pointed out the third low-flying large jet in a matter of seconds to my two friends, we all noticed a white object appear seemingly above and to the east of the jet. 
We were in Aurora colorado looking northeast but almost straight up as the jet passed overhead. After the jet passed the object, we continued to watch it as it faded out of view towards Denver international Airport. 
The object seemed to bob or float at times, stand perfectly still, and rotated or possibly extended moving parts. The air traffic, which was heavy at the time (more than 4 prop planes and/or jets at various altitudes in the visible sky simultaneously) seemed to avoid the area of sky near the object. 
Beginning north-northeast, the object traveled south and further east in a drift or indirect fashion. At times, it appeared to twist or rotate, but one friend thinks it may also have been extending and retracting wings. 
Still appearing white/gray, with light reflecting off, the odd floating motion, along with definite pauses, confirmed to us that it wasn't any conventional aircraft we were familiar with. The entire ordeal lasted between 5-10 minutes, and halfway through the sighting, there were two balls or orbs of light that appeared suddenly close to where the initial object had, and floated/flew quickly above and below the object in the same heading. 
The two smaller objects or lights started as white and turned orange as they passed the tic-tac shape and shortly after simply vanished. It may be worth mentioning I saw a drone or very strange aircraft the night before that came from the direction that all three of these objects eventually vanished. I've been told there's an air force or army base of some kind in that direction. East-southeast of Aurora.
