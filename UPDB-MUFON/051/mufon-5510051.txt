9 reddish-orange orbs flying high-speed over north Lethbridge, Alberta and heading east

On the night of June 22, 2013 at approximately 11pm, while observing the super moon, my wife & I noticed 9 reddish-orange orbs flying east at high-speed over north Lethbridge, Alberta. Theses were not flares, airplanes or helicopters. The objects made no sound and were in scattered formation (altitudes ranged from the lowest orb flying approx. 20,000 to the highest being approx. 40,000 feet)for about two minutes until forming a triangle formation pattern and then disappearing straight up into the stars. The whole event lasted about 3 minutes.
