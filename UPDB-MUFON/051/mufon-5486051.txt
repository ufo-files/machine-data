{nd} Thought it was Chinese lantern or embers at first. Flew silent opposite normal wind direction. Disappeared.

Standing outside at a neighbors fire pit. Just hanging out. Hubby saw it first, so 3 of us looked up. We all thought it was a Chinese lantern or an ember from the fire. But then it just stayed in place for a bit. As we watched it, it flew in a straight line to the south, moving kinda fast. Then it hovered, blinked a few times and disappeared. Never made a sound. It was too low in the sky to be moving as fast as it was. Plus, there was no wind. We just watched it go by in disbelief.
