{jpg} a fireball fell straight down at inhuman speed

sitting on my porch at about 12:30-12:40am, and out of the corner of my eye, I observed an unmistakably bright white missile-like projectile fall straight down to the surface from maybe 10,000 feet in a matter of about two seconds. No following noise or return from surface. At first I thought it might be a ballistic warhead, and started to wonder if I should evacuate, but it was not. I lost sight of the sighting over buildings and trees. About 30 seconds later, I observed an aircraft entering landing pattern at my local airport. Nothing on local news
