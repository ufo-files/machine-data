A star-like object that hovered in a stationary position then suddenly shot off at high speed horizontally to the west.

I was on a local bus that stopped in the city centre. I looked out of the window and noticed what looked like star up in the clear blue sky. It was a sunny day with no clouds. The object was stationary and then it suddenly shot off to the west horizontally at high speed. I knew then that it could not have been an aeroplane or a drone because it moved too fast. It was a UFO in my opinion.
