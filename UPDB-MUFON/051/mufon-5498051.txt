Well I heard a buzzing sound so I looked up for aircraft, I then saw this object in the sky hovering. It had 4 red lights at first. After a while Two of the red lights started blinking. After ten on/off blinks the two red lights on that side went out and 4 yellow lights replaced them ( http://www.nuforc.org/index_files/image014.jpg ). The craft then started defending 10-20 feet and went in an octagonal pattern and then ascended into the clouds when sighting was over.I did not loose sight of it until it went into a cloud. The object made me have no sense of time. The UFO was sky coloured where there were no lights.

I was going swimming at the time, so I had my glasses off; I could not see very well but could make this out.
