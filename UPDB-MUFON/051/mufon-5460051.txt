{rjl} Phenomenon in Night Sky

During the night of May 21, 2018, probably sometime between 2 and 4 a.m. I took my dog out for potty break. I always look up at the stars, and this night I knew I was seeing something I had never witnessed before. I ran back into the beach cottage to grab my cellphone and took two pictures.

I have studied the pictures closely with a magnifying glass and can see objects which resemble toy spinning tops. There are many objects and lights seen throughout what I describe as a con trail, with a heavy presence around a large phenomenon in the middle lower portion.

I was standing on the elevated porch of a beach cottage in Emerald Isle, NC when these pictures were taken, within a few seconds of each other. In both pics you can see the electrical wire in the foreground. In one pic, the overhand of roof is visible. Two most visible objects are located far left, top and bottom of S-shaped con trail. 

Can someone help explain what I captured in these pictures?
