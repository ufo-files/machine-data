silent red orb in sky

me and my two kids were outside watching the roof looking for evidence of bats leaving, a little problem that i have now. it was dusk and the sun had set however there was still some light in the sky. my daughter noticed a red light that appeared over the tree line to the south.
the object glowed redish orange left no trail and had no sound. it traveled to the north across the sky. as it traveled it was a steady glow but would flare out randomly. there was also a small white light that would appear randomly from within the object.. it traveld in a constant speed from the first appearance to the north three quarters across the sky. then it just dissapeared. the sky had no clouds no other aircraft were in the sky. and it was quiet. the whole thing only lasted a few seconds, my daughter who is still young was not as nervous as my son, but we all knew what we saw. i was a aircraft mechanic in airforce, this object had no collision beacons, and the speed that it traveled with no sonic boom,engine sound tells me that it was something other than normal aircraft.
