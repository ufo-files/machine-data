Saw 5 red orange stars which slowly faded away at different times

I was in the car being driven home by my father. It was about 10:09pm, eastern time April 7th 2012. We turned the corner and I looked at the moon and saw five red-orange star like objects surrounding it. I pointed them out to my father, who saw them as well. I was confused as to what it could possibly be and i tried to keep my eyes fixed on them as my father continued to drive. Some 30 seconds later, I got out of the car and I could still see 3 of the stars left. They were no longer near the moon but they appeared to be unmoving. As I watched them, i realized they were fading away, each at a different rate. Even after the phenomenon was over, I was unsure as to what it was that I actually witnessed.
