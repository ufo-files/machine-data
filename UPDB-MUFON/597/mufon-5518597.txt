Strong Floating Blue Light

My friend and I were driving back to my house on a main road of our town. He was actually first to spot this and quickly as I looked to our right I spotted it too. It was a bright blue light that was floating in mid air above the road we looked down. Street lights we on in the vicinity and we saw nothing that could explain this or that was connected to the light. We drove past as the light didn't flicker or fludder, we quickly went back to check on what it was, but when we returned the light was gone.
