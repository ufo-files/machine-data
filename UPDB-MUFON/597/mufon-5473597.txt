{rjl} Fireball w/ smoke and flame trail

My wife and I were soaking in the warm pool in Calistoga, CA, facing north. The time was summer with a clear blue sky. 

Suddenly, a glowing object passed in front of us from our left (west) to our right (east). It appeared to be a fireball with a trail of flames and gray smoke swirling directly and strictly behind the fireball. The smoke and flame trail did not pass beyond the width of the fireball. it was maybe 5 times as long as the diameter of the fireball in front. We heard no noise and felt no heat from it. It moved about as fast as a jet liner landing at an airport, and about that high and far away from our vantage point. It did not swerve or vary from a straight-line path as it disappeared over the horizon to our right. We saw no evidence of a crash landing. It just passed in front of us and was gone. 

The flyover event took less than 20 seconds or so. The pool had a dozen or so people in it, and they saw it, too. One man commented that it must be a meteorite and went on soaking. My wife was present and she saw it, but does not now have any recollection of the event.

This exactly like the fireball I saw in 1988 that I reported in case 86767 but without any visit from a helicopter.
