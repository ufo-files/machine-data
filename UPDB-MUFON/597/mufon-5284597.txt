Orangy Red Fireball with a black object on the back coming from the west heading east in a straight line moving very fast. Then disappeared no clouds?

Standing on driveway with my dog, approximately 7:30 PM on Wednesday October 20th. Turned to the South to look down Echo Park Circle, saw an orangey red fireball in the sky coming from the west heading east moving fast. Noticed bright colors and the black object on the tail end of the fireball. Appeared to not be falling from sky had a directional purpose. Then disappeared. No clouds were present in path of the object.
