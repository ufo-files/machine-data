A bulbous disk UFO that looked like chrome with no windows and no lights

My wife and I saw this object at the same time while driving north up Semoran Blvd. I noticed it first and told her to look. I told her it looked to be over our apartment at the time. It hovered for several minutes while we were stopped at a light before taking off straight up and disappearing.

My wife (now ex-wife) has completely shut this memory out of her mind and it actually became an issue during our divorce.

For me, it was yet another confirmation that I, we, were being tracked.
