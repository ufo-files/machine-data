2 orbs over Nanaimo harbour, one flying low and the other much higher. Low orb changed directed and went near the cloud level with the other orbs and then out of viewing distance.

My wife and myself were just about sit down for the evening and my wife noticed 2 orange glowing orbs outside our back window, we both rushed to the deck and tried to take video but were unsuccessful but was able to take a picture that shows 2 orbs in the distance but captured on a cellphone so not the best quality in a rush. The orbs were moving at a slow pace one was quite low to moving over the Nanaimo harbour while the other was near the cloud line and moving in the same direction. After reaching the southern end of downtown Nanaimo the low flying orb changed directions and flew high up to the second orb the followed the same path going east or south east and then out of viewing range. 2 adults saw this clearly from the deck for approx 5 or 6 minutes before the event ended.
