{jpg} North on 95 ,before the 124 exit , object traveling east to west , two really bright lights with other smaller lights , no strobes

Going north on 95 in Maine and coming up on the 124 exit . The wife and I saw an disk shaped object traveling east to west . It flew right over the interstate at about 200 to 300 feet . The object had two really bright white spotlights with other smaller white lights and the body of it was glowing white . We only had it in view for about 15 seconds or so . It was about a 1/4 mile away when we spotted it in the cloudy sky . But it either shut its outer light off or just shot up and out of there . 

Time was about 6:50 pm . 

We have seen other objects before , just surprised to see one again .
