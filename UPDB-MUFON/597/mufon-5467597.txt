White disc like saucer hovered then flew over head with airplane close by.

I was walking towards the bus stop next to the ruby Cantonese when i hear a deep low pich hum from above. I see an airplane first, then notice that there is a white saucer flying alongside, leaving a trail just like the plane. It then slowed down a bit, before hovering, with its white reflective body reflecting the morning sun. It appeared to be rotating as the light hitting my eye varied. The pulsating light i observed led me to conclude that the craft span as a means of propulsion. It then continued to follow closely to the plane before disappearing NE from my field of view. I try to tell my family and friends but nobody believes me. I personally believe the craft i observed was a government project to produce efficient rotary propulsion aircraft for ground surveillance and spy operations. Seems valid enough to believe to me. I get some are sceptics but just open your minds a little, and truth will set you free.
