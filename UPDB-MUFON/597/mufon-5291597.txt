Orbs of light spotted in the sky over the course of a month {sometimes the object appears to explode in the sky, other times I have seen the object move straight up and out of sight, once I witnesses the object move through a blue circle in the sky}-{djs}

The events happened over the course of a month or so. Was able to witness the object about 5 times a week for several weeks. Multiple videos/pictures and close to 100 other witnesses of the object. Bright light appears in blue skies. Object sometimes moved slowly across the sky ( about 15 degrees in 15 minutes) almost always moving east to west. Object witnessed through binoculars and telescope. Hangs around for about 15 minutes then leaves. Sometimes the object appears to explode in the sky, other times I have seen the object move straight up and out of sight, once I witnesses the object move through a blue circle in the sky and disappear. 

I contacted the local news after the first few sightings but did not get a response. Also spoke with a police officer about the object who was sitting in his car in the parking lot while object was above. All my employees witnessed the object at least once. My wife (who was very skeptical) saw the object with me on several occasions including once with our friend who was visiting home after graduating with a doctorate. Several strangers also joined me at a local park (dodge park in sterling heights) to take their own pictures of the object.

Completely mind blowing.
