{nd} While driving saw4 white lights 2 red lights gone!

While driving to work on Pioneer road 2 miles east of cedarburg wi at about 4:45 am under a clear early dawn I was looking up at the stars and noticed 4 "stars"that formed a rectangle. It didn't look natural. It caught my attention so being the only car on the road I slowed down to 35mph and divided my attention between the road and these lights. In these short minutes of this observation, as the sun was slowly rising allowing more light in the sky was when I could see the outline of the craft with the 4 white lights. One in each corner. I was getting excited. As I took my eyes off the road the last time and the timing for this was perfect I looked up and saw the 4 white lights (one in each corner of this rectangle) turn off while simultaneously 2 red lights flashed at the center of this obbject and it was gone! It was a really cool experience.
