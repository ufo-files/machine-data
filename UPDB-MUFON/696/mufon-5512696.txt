{cak} 3 bright intense lights that formed a large triangle object with no sound, hovered and then flew northbound over SR 630

Reportee advised above Chief Vincent DEgidio of New Middletown Police that on Sunday Feb 3 at app 1705hrs he and his family were traveling on SR 630 in New Middletown when they observed three intense bright white lights slowly traveling app 1500 feet in the air. The sky was overcast however as they approached the objct they observed a very clearly shaped object described as a triangle. The object stopped in the sky emitting no sound and then a couple minutes later flew off in a nortbound direction. Reportee then notified New Middletown Police on Feb 6 of the sighting. Reportee did not want to leave his name or contact information.
