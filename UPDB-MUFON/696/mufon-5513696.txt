White ball going very very very fast

I never saw it live, I first saw it when I uploaded my daily pics that evening. I saw it immediately. A white ball streaking across the sky at a tremendous speed in Essaouira Morocco on April 1, 2010 during the day.

My camera says it was taken at 3:29:08 but I didn't have the timezone set for Morocco, it was still set for my country so it was probably 11:30:08

I'm including the original photo and then a cropped version. I enhanced the pixel count and color to make it stand out more so you can see it better.

I don't know what the photo is, but it was moving at a tremendous speed. Maybe a meteorite. Maybe something else. Don't know.
