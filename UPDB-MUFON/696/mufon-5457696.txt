I have noticed this object on one of the photos that I took in Morocco.

I have been looking through the photos that I took while on vacation in Morocco and in one of these photos I have noticed an object in the sky above an building. I have taken 3 photos in rapid succession of the same scene, about 1 sec appart. The object was present only in second photo, which means that it was there for less than 1 sec, which means that it moved very fast. In large magnification of the photo it can be seen that it moved from left towards right. The exposure was 1/250 of the second.
