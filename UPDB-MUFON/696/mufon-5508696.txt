{kt}Object appeared and moved in a rapid W path 2 times

When I was approximately 7 or 8 years old, my father, brother, uncle and I were on the rooftop of my building looking at fireworks across the city. It was then that all four of us looked slightly upwards and saw the object move from left to right in a "W" pattern (sharp 90 degree angles). The object disappeared and then reappeared and repeated the same motion another sequence and then disappeared again (not to return). We all saw the object and commented on how fast it did what it did and that we didn't know what it was or at least what we knew what it wasnt... My uncle has since passed but my father, brother and myself still vividly remember the incident and feel that we (the US) or other countries do not have the technology to move at the speed or direction that this object moved.
