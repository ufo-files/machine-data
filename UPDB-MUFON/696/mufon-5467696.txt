Round object moving in perfectly straight line, followed half a minute later by exactly the same.

Weather was hot. I sat in garden watching planes, I am used to doing this. As I watched an object came from my left approx north. It was not a plane, it appeared to be spherical and silver mainly but possibly black at rear, travelling in a dead straight line towards the south, with no sound at any time. It was slightly windy so I don't believe it could have been a bubble or balloon etc. Distance is very difficult but much closer than high planes. I viewed for approx 30+ seconds when I lost sight of it. Strangely about 30 seconds later I saw exactly the same thing over again.
