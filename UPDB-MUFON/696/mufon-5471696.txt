{cak} Large red ball chased by black hawk helicopter. Flew right over my house. Not last sighting . Others different shapes.

First sighting in 1996. Hugh red glowing ball flew directly over house. Chased by black hawk helicopter from fort campbell. Ky. The ball was acting like it was playing with the helicopter. We moved to pleasant view 2 yrs later and we have no trees. Open space. We have seen several objects since. Twice the ball was close enough to send a large beam of light. It's as if it saw us on back porch in hot tub. It flashed the beam 4 times. My husband never believed in UFOS . Now we both look for them. Several times I have seen small shadows in my bedroom. He said he's seen several also. No sound from any of them. I thought it was ghost in my home. Don't know what to think.
