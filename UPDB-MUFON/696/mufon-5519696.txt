Dark gray to black oval or blimp-shaped, traveling against the wind

My fiance and I were enjoying the sunset on his patio overlooking the hills. We noticed a small, oval or blimp-shaped "thing" traveling over the top of the hill in a north-west direction. We ruled out it being an actual blimp because of its rather speedy movement and the fact that it was going against a brisk wind. I watched it for about a minute or so till it disappeared into the clouds and setting sun. It seemed to be climbing at a small angle, and it may have wobbled a little on its pretty much steady path and/or hovered a bit. It was so tiny to my vision that even with my glasses on I couldnt be sure.

We watch the skies in all weather and are familiar with the birds and planes we frequently see. This "thing" didn't seem all that extraordinary, but it WAS an anomaly, so Im reporting it in case it is of some value to your organization.
