{rjl} Around 1964 playing with my cousins and seeing a bright light hover, move than shoot out of sight.

I just wanted to make this as part of a collective record. I remember as a kid around 1964 my brother and cousins were playing behind my grandmother;s house in Linton, IN. I remember looking up and seeing a really bright flickering light. It seemed odd because it seemed to have color to it as opposed to stars. It seemed to simply be there then it slowly started to move across the sky. Shortly after it started to move it shot up at an angle with incredible speed. As an adult this to me is significant in that back then there was nothing in the atmosphere. It was very high up so there was no light pollution creating an effect. There was no explanation for this event. I am an ordained pastor and retired psychotherapist with my forte in PTSD and crisis/war trauma counseling. I believe in life on other planets because there is no other explanation for what I know I saw. I am now 61 and wanted to put into record. thank you
