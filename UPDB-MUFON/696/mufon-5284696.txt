Delta shaped craft

My wife and I were outside our apartment with some friends, as we were walking towards the apartment door I caught some motion in the corner of my sight and when I turned to view what it was I immediately gasped and said " WHOAH, look!" . It was very last second however, my wife managed to get a last second glimpse of what I was looking at but to male sure I wasn't losing my mind, I asked her to describe exactly what she saw, she described it exactly as I saw it. On a straight flight plan headed SE from Duenweg just outside Joplin MO we could see a delta shaped craft with 7 lights extending in a delta formation, the craft had no form of visible propulsion, the craft made no audible sounds of any kind. The skies were completely clear absolutley no clouds despite it had just raind the day before amd were expectimg more rain the following day. One odd feature to note about the craft was this odd haze underneath the craft as if it were hitching a ride on a bed of misty fog. It was on a pretty direct route coming from the direction of Kansas city, 2 hours north of Joplin MO. We viewed the craft in the eastern skies with a SSE heading. The crafts body was easily distinguishable even with the haze it was riding on and against the night sky. Unfortunately we were not camera prepared for this moment so we don't have any picture or video proof, but something tells me this won't be the last time we see something like this. Very cool sighting, I hope everyone gets to see at least one in their life!
