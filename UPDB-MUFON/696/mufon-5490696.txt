one of my favorite things to do is look up at the night sky in hopes that I see another extraterrestrial aerial vehicle. At 2:30am MDT I was sitting on my chair with the satellite tracker trying to view when the International Space Station would pass over my location. I looked at my clock on my taskbar and then seen on satellite tracker that I had 6 more minutes to wait until ISS is above me.  As I checked the time a streak caught my eye so I fixed my gaze upon it. Pointed my bright LED flashlight at the object and flashed it several times hoping the occupants would notice me and swing around to have a look. I lost view of it after it went past my garage for about 5 seconds. I live across from the National Air Guard base so I see military aircraft flying daily at the same altitude. This object was about 500 ft in the air and less than a mile from me.  I believe 1/4 mile is an accurate distance from me. It had to have been traveling supersonic speeds. However, it was silent.  It was triangle shaped but I can definitely tell where the front of the vehicle was as opposed to the sides.  It was a dirty white color and the sides had a dark goldish (rust) with brown fins.
