{nd} bright green light came straight down from the sky

At 10:17pm on Saturday April 5th me and my girlfriend were coming back from dinner at a steakhouse in the neighboring city of portage Indiana. While traveling west down 700 north/Cleveland avenue toward our town of Hobart I caught something out of the corner of my eye in the southwestern sky. At first I thought it was a falling star but when I actually looked at it, I knew it wasn't. It was bright green(like a traffic signal), it was coming straight down from Very high in the sky and it also slowed down as it came closer to the earth. It looked like it was probably around 5 miles away. I didn't see it land or crash or anything cause I lost track of it behind the tree line. Definitely interesting.
