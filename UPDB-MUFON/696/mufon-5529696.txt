Seen a star that diminished

ON 10/9/2010 approx. 21:15 My brother-inlaw, 12 year old son, My sister, and I were viewing Jupiter with my telescope. My sister was looking at the time in the telescope, I was looking around to see what else we could look at. I noticed a bright star and stated that I don't remeber that star we should look at that. The star was brighter than Jupiter. My son and brother in-law turned to look, at that time the star started to slowly get smaller until it was gone. by the time my sister looked it was already gone. We could still see all the other stars close to the area and knew it could'nt be the very light cloud cover that was moving in. The star never came back. This all took place within 1min and 30 seconds from the time I spotted it till the time it was gone. It did not move across the sky it seemed to be traveling outward. This is strange, something I've never seen before and can't explain it.
