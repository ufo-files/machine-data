The object flew from the west. Was shaped like a white ball. From the ball disconnected another ball of the same size. Which flew abruptly in the northern direction. The main ball-object continued to fly east. Not yet reaching the zenith point, the object took a diamond-shaped shape. At the edge of the diamond-shaped object have flashed sequentially of light white lamps. Around the object was a purple glow, similar to the northern lights. At the zenith point, the object paused and then slowly flew to the east. Witnesses were many, because everything was happening over the station square near the railway passenger station.
