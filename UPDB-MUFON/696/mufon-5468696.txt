{td} Star-like object rapidly descended toward horizon initially like shooting star then changed course

My wife and I were sitting on our hotel balcony at the Best Western Plus Beach Resort on Fort Myers Beach. We had been sitting on the balcony for roughly 25 minutes looking out to the horizon and the clear night sky over the Gulf of Mexico. At approximately 11:28 p.m. I was looking in the southern direction roughly 45 degrees above the horizon when I caught view of a star-like object falling toward the horizon. I pointed my wife to the object in time for both of us to witness the object change course from S to SE at roughly 20 degrees above horizon before disappearing completely from view. The object traveled much faster than any airplane or commercial airliner but slower than a typical shooting star. Our initial reaction was excitement from seeing a "shooting star" and then to shock and greater excitement to have witnessed the object change direction while moving at a rapid speed. The entire sighting occurred over a span of ten seconds.
