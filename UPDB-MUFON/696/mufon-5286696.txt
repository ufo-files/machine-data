UAP hovered,

I saw a green tic-tac UAP hovering between 1000 to 2500 ft AGL to the south of me. It hovered for about 30 sec from when I spotted it. Then rapidly flew to the East for approx 500 feet, then accelerated directly west and disappeared in milliseconds.
It was like the craft hovered, backed up to the East, then flew West at a speed that is unlike any craft I have ever seen.
I immediately contacted Luke Air Force Base tower because this caft was in their control space. They claimed to have not seen it on any system.
I couldn't be the only person to have seen it on that day. Weather was sunny and no clouds. Craft was not in any known aircraft flight path.
