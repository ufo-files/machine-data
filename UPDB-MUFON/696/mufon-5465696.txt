We were in the car driving home, to the left of us there was an unknown flying object in the sky.

1. We were driving home in our car on the motorway. We were about 5 minutes from home.
2. There were three bright flashing lights to the left of us and my dad (who was driving) pointed it out. 
3. We didn't know what it was at first, so my mum and I continued to watch it's movements since we thought that it looked very strange.
4. At first it was hovering quite far off of the ground. And then the lights started flashing. It very quickly descended, and stayed there for about 10 seconds before flying very quickly upwards, making no sound at all.
5. At first my dad said to my mum and I, "look, up there in the sky" so we both looked and couldn't quite figure out what it was. We then decided to watch it, soon realizing that it was a disk shape and was tilted (we could tell by where the lights were). I then knew it was something much more than a helicopter or an airplane.
6. It vanished suddenly.
