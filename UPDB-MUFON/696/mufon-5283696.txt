White tic tac shaped object in broad daylight following jet

Witnessed a plane flying a grid like pattern all morning littering the clear sky with what some wild call chemtrails. Heard loud boom (assuming sonic blast). Looked to sky to try amd find the jet that caused the noise. Located jet and watched with binoculars trying to id plane as it was clearly not the plane from earlier that day. As I panned across the sky with binos observing the jet and the contrail behind it I noticed another white tic tac/oval like object following the jet. The object made no noise and left no mark the jet was climbing very quickly the object kept up with jet effortlessly and eventually they climbed into the clouds l.
