{dr}Observed Disk Shaped Sphere with Dual Pulsing Beams

I was at the end of my Driveway looking for the Cat to bring inside for the night. I almost always look at the Stars when I am out at night and noticed one that was Brighter & Pulsing. At first I thought it was a Plane but after just a few seconds I discarded that thought. I have no Idea what it was but my best description would be a Hovering Sphere that tapered at the ends with 2 "Pulsing Beams" (I guess would be the best way to describe them) Which were coming down & away at approx. a 45 degree angle...1 on either end of the Main Object. The Beams went from Not Visible to fully extended in 3 separate, equal length, segmented pulses and then repeated. When the Beams when fully extended they were about 1/2 again longer that the diameter of the Main Object. Full Cycle for the beams to extend was about 3-4 seconds.
They would do a varied amount of cycles and then quit only to start again after about 1 minute. 
I have done a lot of Skydiving during the day & night, so I have been in and around a good deal of Conventional & Military Aircraft and this was something I have never seen before. I observed it for about 10 minutes and took a photo and a couple videos on my Phone. Unfortunately, I had to use Max Zoom and between that & the hand movement at that setting it is pretty lousy Video. After about 10-15 minutes I went in to wake up my wife to show her. I was gone about 5 minutes and when we came back out it was no longer visible.
