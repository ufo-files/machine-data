1)	Location: Covert Township Park
Date: July 10, 2010  at 10:30 pm
This observation was the weekend following 4th of July. While camping at the park, my 3 children, their spouses/friend, and my grandchild went down to Lake Michigan to play flashlight tag. About 10:30, my girlfriend and I went down to check on the kids and see the lake. After a few minutes they decided to head back to the campsite to make s1mores. My girlfriend and I stayed to watch the fireworks from St. Joseph. While standing on the landing of the stairs to the beach. A 1Cigar Shaped1 thing appeared right in front of us. It was between the Lake and the sand dune. It was close enough to see bands around it. The bands were 8-101 wide with something like rivets. This thing was about 40 ft long and 15ft tall. It made no noise. It seemed to be coming from the water. I observed it for 20-30 seconds. It disappeared as quickly as it appeared.
