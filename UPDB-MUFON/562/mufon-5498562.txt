{jd} Pulsating stationary red/blue/white single source for 15 min, then incredible acceleration

on the way out to the barn tonight, in the southern sky a little to the east, I noticed a stationary spot in the sky, alternating red, blue, white, a single source, not like a plane's lights, although that's what I thought it must be. There are lots of planes in the sky at night, see those all the time, but this was different. I noticed it wasn't moving...or if it appeared to move, was in very small circles...got out the binoculars, held them as steady as I could against a tree, and watched this thing for over 15 minutes, and it never really seemed to move, the lights just kept oscillating red, blue, white. Then, in a %$#& instant, and I mean, in a half-second, it became a pinpoint, way far off. I could barely make it out, but could still see a faint color change taking place, unlike the stars around it. Tried to keep focused on it, but it disappeared or I lost track of it. Stayed out there for a while hoping for another look, but the clouds moved in. It was somewhere between aspirin-sized and star-sized when the event began, ended up being star-sized before disappearing.
