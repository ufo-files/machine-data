World Series Game 5 Citi Field, Flushing NY approx. 10 PM EST 11/1/15 facing west saw a green glowing object in sky above stadium moving west to north and quickly disappeared. My son standing next to me turned to me and was about to say something and I interrupted and asked "Did you see that?" He responded in the affirmative. The next day I googled to see if there were any other witnesses since we were at a game with 45,000 people but couldn't find any reported sightings.
