(GS) Amber orb appeared in the Western sky.

After hearing of reports of something in the western sky. I scanned the evening sky with my binoculars. While viewing a orange amber light suddenly appeared. The amber orb was circular and bright and about the twice the size of the planet Jupiter if viewing through the naked eye. It appeared over Mexico or some miles from the Rio Grande/US MX Border. The event lasted for about 10 seconds and then the object disappeared.
