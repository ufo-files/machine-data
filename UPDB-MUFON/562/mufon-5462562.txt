{cak} Three pulsating orbs in triangle formation, but video only depicts one

1. I was in my room playing Everquest, a MMORPG.
2. I felt a sudden urge to open my curtains and look outside.
3. An alien craft of some sort.
4. The three orbs pulsed in synchronicity and they rotated clockwise
in a triangular formation. After approximately 40 minutes they lined up vertically and traveled south until they went out of sight.
5. I was at first shocked, but became thrilled because I have never seen anything so bizarre before. I told my parents to look outside, and my dad grabbed a camera and started filming while my mother and I watched the orbs rotate. I felt excited throughout and after the encounter, because I felt like some sort of message was being conveyed, but I wasn't sure what kind of message.
6. They traveled out of sight slowly.
