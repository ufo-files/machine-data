Reddish Orbs Seen During Fireworks Show in Anacortes, WA

My family and I were watching the fireworks show here in Anacortes, WA on July 4th, 2011 and at approximately 10:00 PM we saw a large orange orb in the sky above us. At first it appeared to just be hovering stationary, but then we saw it move slowly to the north. Approximately 20 minutes later we saw another red orb in the sky which separated into two red orbs, one orb dropped a sort distance and then they both slowly moved off toward the northeast. The orbs were visible for several minutes and gradually faded off as they moved away.
