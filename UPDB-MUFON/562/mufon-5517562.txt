{flg} two glowing orbs

On July 4th approx. Around 9:30-10:00 in (Oregon City) I was looking up in the sky for morters people were lighting off... I then noticed right away that there were two large redish orange glowing orbs side by side just hovering silently. I then told my girlfriend and her friends about it and they didn't seem to care! Or they must have thought I was crazy. I then continued to stare at these orbs and the one to the right moved on top of the other and continued hovering, then the one on top went out like a light and vanished then about 30 seconds later the second orb vanished as well.... I hope someone else saw this so I can have some kind of proof that Im not crazy or making this up.
