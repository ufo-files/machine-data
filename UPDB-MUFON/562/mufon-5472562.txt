Was driving over Hoan Bridge in Milwaukee, WI., this morning (which I do everyday) when I observed an airplane that had just taken off from the airport headed east over Lake Michigan, a bright star-like object caught my attention. I kept my eyes on the object for about 30 sec - it did not move, just hovered in some clouds, the light appeared to get brighter, then just disappeared. I do want to note that the sun was behind the object which again, was in a cloud, yet it was still illuminated.
