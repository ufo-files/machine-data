(GS) All I saw was grey in my rear view window on my way to the market.

When I parked my car at the market, I took out my iPod and started recording this circular grey blotch in the sky. It was surrounded by the calmest sky. It didn't belong there- especially with the sun shinning. I went into the market to shop. When i came out, it was gone. When I got home, I watched it and noticed the flying objects. No big deal... It was probably shinny birds.
