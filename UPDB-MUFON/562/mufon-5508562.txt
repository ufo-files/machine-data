{kt}HOOVERED OVER HOUSE, THEN MOVING SNAIL PACE UNTIL OUT OF SIGHT

ON THE NIGHT IN QUESTION, MY NIECE AND MYSELF DECIDED TO TAKE THE BINOCULARS AND GO OUTSIDE TO LOOK FOR POSSIBLE UFOS. STANDING IN THE FRONT OF THE HOUSE ON THE CONCRETE WALKWAY, WE WERE TALKING QUIETLY, AND SEARCHING THE SKY. WE WERENT OUT THERE LONG WHEN I WAS LOOKING TOWARD THE SOUTH IN THE SKY. ABOUT THEN SHE HAD SAID TO ME THAT SHE REALLY WOULD LIKE TO SEE ONE. AS I WAS TURNING MY HEAD LEFT TO RIGHT, MY EYES STOPPED ON THE UFO HOOVERING ABOVE THE BACK OF THE HOUSE WHERE I LIVED. IT WAS AS IF IT APPEARED THERE OUT OF THIN AIR. JUST AS CALMLY AS IF I SAW THEM ALL THE TIME, I TAPPED HER ON THE RIGHT SIDE AND JUST AS I STARTED TO SAY, YEAH JEA, I, I STOPPED BECAUSE I SAW IT, AND I SAID LOOK. WE WERE BOTH IN AWE AT WHAT WE WERE SEEING, LOOKING AT EACH OTHER WITH OUR MOUTHS WIDE OPEN, IN A FROZEN STATE. IT ONLY LASTED WHAT WE THINK WAS SECONDS. SHE THEN WENT TO THE RIGHT SIDE OF THE HOUSE, AS I WENT FURTHER INTO THE YARD, STILL LOOKING UP. I COULD SEE THAT THE SURFACE OF THE CRAFT WAS A PATTERNED SURFACE, THROUGH THE BINOCULARS. WHICH I KNEW RIGHT AWAY THAT I DIDNT NEED TO USE. AND THAT I COULD ACTUALLY SEE IT BETTER. MOVING SLIGHTLY TO THE RIGHT, AND TURNED VERY SLOWLY TO THE LEFT IN A SNAILS PACE, AS TO LINE ITSELF UP TO THE DIRECTION IN WHICH IT WAS GOING. IT THEN WENT OVER THE TOP OF THE NEIGHBORS TREES,(TO THE RIGHT OF ME) AT THIS TIME I HAD WENT OUT INTO OUR STREET, STILL WATCHING IT MOVING SLOWLY IN THE DIRECTION OF THE END OF THE STREET. OVER TOP OF THE HOUSE THAT SET THERE AND THE TREES. I WATCHED IT UNTIL I COULD ONLY SEE THE LIGHTS. I THEN WENT OVER TO MY NIECE JEANNIE, AS SHE WAS COMING TO ME. I SAID JEAN, DONT SAY ANYTHING ABOUT WHAT YOU JUST SAW, LETS GO IN THE HOUSE, YOU GET IN ONE AREA AWAY FROM ME AND ILL GET IN THE OTHER AND WELL BOTH DRAW & WRITE WHAT WE HAD SEEN. AFTER WE DID THIS, WE THEN COMPARED WHAT WE HAD WRITTEN AND DRAWN. IT WAS NO REAL SURPRISE TO US THAT WHAT WE HAD DRAWN WAS EXACTLY THE SAME. OF COURSE WHEN WE TOLD THE OTHERS WE KNOW, THEY LAUGHED AND THOUGHT WE WERE CRAZY. WE DIDNT CARE THOUGH BECAUSE, WE KNEW WHAT WE HAD SEEN, AND NOTHING OR NO ONE
COULD CHANGE OUR MINDS. LATER ON, NOT FOR SURE WHEN IT WAS, THE TREES IN THE NEIGHBORS YARD STARTED DYING AND THEY HAD THEM CUT DOWN, THEY WERE HUGH TREES. NOW, IM NOT SAYING THIS IS WHAT CAUSED THEM TO DYE, BUT, YOU CAN USE YOUR OWN JUDGEMENT. MAYBE IT WAS, AND MAYBE IT WASNT. I DO KNOW ONE THING FOR SURE, WE BOTH SAW A U.F.O.
