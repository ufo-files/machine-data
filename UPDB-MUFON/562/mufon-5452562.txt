Observed single object with flashing white light disappear, then three smaller lights shot off in different directions

While on a night walk I observed a black craft approach at 50° from the NW. The craft was silent and was flashing a bright white light that appeared to rotate around the exterior of the craft, like a lighthouse. The object flew directly above me and further to the SE, before disappearing in mid flight. Immediately three orange/ red balls of light shot out at incredible speed in all different directions from the point in the sky where the initial object was observed disappearing. 
Following that sighting, I stood in my driveway flashing my LED headlamp up at the sky in rhythmic numerical patterns, counting from three flashes, up to seven flashes. Immediately following my light display, a red orb could be seen moving S to N at very high altitude. I repeated the numerical pattern with my headlamp several times, and EVERY TIME an orb could be seen. In total I repeated the experiment 4-5 times, with positive sightings every time. The orbs all moved S-N, from 90° to 45° relative to the horizon.
