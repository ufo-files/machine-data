I saw really bright white light,

I was looking at the stars, I see lots of satellite movement, but this was pure really bright light, too close to a star. Just kept moving westerly as I was looking at it., then as it moved over me it looked like it had wings slightly out from the body of the thing. Then it disappeared when about quarter of a mile away, not because it had travelled too far in the distance. I had no photo evidence of it. Just my own eyes.
