{tjd} An orange orb was sighted heading in a straight line from South to Northwest.

Me and my dad were out by our bonfire when I looked up and saw what I thought was a hot air balloon that was going to the NW. I told my dad about and he saw it then too, he said it couldn’t be a balloon. We then got up and walked to the top of small hill to follow the object. The orange object was moving fast, not blinking, and was one single blob of light. This is when I took out my phone and recorded a video of it. The object did not blink as it moved out of sight. It was one single orange blob. The weather condition was no clouds or wind. The object did not give out any sound as it went by. 

Around 10 years ago, there was a report in our town of an incident almost exactly like this that occurred at very closely to the same date and time as what we saw last night. The link provided is the report.
