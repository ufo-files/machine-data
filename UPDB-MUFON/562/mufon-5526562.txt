Multiple Reddish Orange Lights, small, compared to larger Base?, square, red and blueish lights

about 10:20pm, I was bringing my brother home from work (northampton to southampton). On Rt. 10 where Pomery Meadow Rd. meet, I first noticed a pair of orbs flying in the same direction (towards Westfield) in unison with each other, lost them over some trees, got to my brother/parents house, dropped him off, (he did not see them, I at first wasnt thinking anything of it, just some planes..). they live right on Rt.10,dropped him off and I pulled out of their driveway, back towards Easthampton, I took a right onto Clark St.(Southampton), a right onto East St. Then I noticed a large craft with red and blueish lights hovering over a field on Pleasant St. So I took that left, I was amazed, It was making a not too loud humming noise. I kept driving because I admit, I was kinda freaked out, this is my first UFO sighting. As I kept driving I saw the same orbs as before, atleast, I think they were the same ones. I slowed down to get a better look, as one of them seemed to be tailing me, everytime I looked out my driver side window, it was right beside me probably a couple hundred yards away. It stayed with my until I reached the center of Easthampton, I then lost it. I will say, as I looked at it out my window, I had an amazed look on my face, a big smile all the way home, I kinda felt it was following me purposely.
