{dr}Hovering craft surrounded in mist resembled a boomerang with reddish orange lights

On September 3, 2011, I was attending the 1st Annual Triad Music Festival at the Greensboro Grasshoppers Stadium. A little after 9:00pm, musician Lupe Fiasco took the stage. Shortly into his set all the electricity cut off for a few minutes Then me, my friend and others standing around us look up to see a cloud of mist right above our heads. In the mist you could see a dark boomerang shaped object with small reddish orange lights on it. The object just hovered there for a minute or two and was completely silent. We were speechless at first, almost in disbelief and I tried to take a picture but my phone was almost dead. Other people around us noticed it and tried to take pictures as well 
and we all said the same thing "UFO". As soon as the object appeared it disappeared and the electricity came back on. The next day there was a report that others had reported the same sighting in Greensboro but the news said the military issued a statement that they were doing military exercises last night in the Greensboro area. My best friend and I didn't believe it for a second and we knew what we saw was a UFO.
