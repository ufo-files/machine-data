Did not see them in the sky, only after viewing the pictures did I spot them.

I took these photo1s on 26 February 2009, in Philadelphia, Pa. I was trying to prove that you can make out an object in the sky taken with cheap (iphone) technology. I wanted to know why these ufo photos are always so grainy and suspect. And thought, if you practiced shooting planes and helicopters with decent equipment, you should get a pretty good picture.  As I stepped out of my work place that day, I saw a copter hovering above the expressway and decided to test my idea. 2 days later while watching UFO Hunters I remembered the photos. To my surprise I had captured these other objects. One directly above the copter, the other to the left at about 11:00. I took 2 shots one after the other.  I thought it might be a bird at first, but it should be smaller than the copter. Or a plane taken at an angle, that distorted its characteristics, could be! 22 years in the Navy and 5 aircraft carriers, I think I should be able to tell the difference. So what do you guys think.
