{nd} came in from the ocean over trees

the end of the isle of palms.before wild dunes was built was a culdesac.we were facing south looking at the long road ahead to see if anyone was coming.we were on a dead end.talking then we saw a very bright light hovering above the surf and was coming in through the treetops.silent.but a huge search light on top of the trees coming straight at us.we left in an instant and never looked back. it made no sound.we could hear the surf just fine.this happened in the years of 74 75 76.to this day i believe.sincerley [Item moved/CMS/nd]
