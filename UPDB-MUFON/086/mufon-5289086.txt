One pulsating light on a northwest flight path.

At 2015 hours on March 18, I went outside to the east side of our hangar to take a break and drink some water. Skies to the east were clear with no clouds-temperature was around 48 F, and winds were light. At a 45 degree elevation, at my 2 o'clock, I saw a object approaching from the south east on a heading of roughly 290 degrees. The object was slightly smaller than an aspirin held at arms length and was deviating from its flight path, left and right, by several degrees. The speed approximated that of a helicopter approaching the airport.at 100 knots. No aircraft anti collision lights were seen and rarely do aircraft approach the airport from that quadrant as our runway headings, from the east, are 280 and 250. My observation lasted for a minute before I realized this was not an aircraft or satellite as its flight path deviated very slightly left to right, no collision lights were evident, and it would occasionally pulse unlike a landing light. I took out my iPhone Max Pro 12, dialed up the magnification, and took the attached photo. I looked at the photo, looked back up, and the object was gone. From the initial sighting to disappearance, the object had traveled from my 2 o'clock to 12 o'clock position with a flight time of about 75 seconds slightly lowering in the 45 degree initial sighting elevation as it was approaching the airport.. Altitude unknown as I have no size reference and the sighting was at an obtuse angle but I would estimate it was below 8000 feet AGL? There was no sound affiliated with this sighting and the airport was quiet at the time of the sighting with no jet departure noise.
After the sighting, I checked my Flight Radar 24 app but no aircraft were in that south east quadrant of the controlled airspace although there was a flight from San Francisco to London at 39,000 feet headed in a north east direction-90 degrees to the flight path of this object. In addition, I checked my GOSATWATCH app and noticed that an Ariane 40 R/B shared the same trajectory as this object but was much farther south at the time of this sighting. 
The submitted photo is unmagnified but when magnified there seems to be a faint structure behind the glowing/pulsating orb. On April 28th of 2020,at approximately 2200 hours, three of these flew over me at low altitude heading northeast.
I am a former Recon Marine, have been a pilot since the eighties, and am a science teacher with 28 years in our District. This is my third such orb sighting in the last six years.
