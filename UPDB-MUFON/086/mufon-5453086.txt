Craft was white and red no windows or sound

A prox 60 feet in length height of trees two colours, hovered in front of us at 70 mph for at least one minute, we were heading north, the craft then went east dropped down and disappeared . Then with in 20 seconds it came back and did the same as before hovered in front of us for about one minute same height as before then took of in to the sky . As far as we could see in 1.5 seconds , it was summer very light our windows were down , I was driving a lorry at the time , this is something I will never forget as long as I live ,
