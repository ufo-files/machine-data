{tjd} flight of 50 lights in straight line formation

At 4
4 45 am March 2, 2020 I was sitting in my living room with windows facing east. Clear night with no cloud cover. Lights similar to a plane began crossing the sky from southwest to northeast following directly behind each other. I thought it was a flight of planes, But there was a large number of lights, after approx 20 passed I stepped to the window, and saw a continuing line. I then stepped out side for a better look. The lights were appearing in the sky from a fixed point a half mile south and 2 miles east of my residence. They seemed to materialized from a single point and follow fairly close behind each other. They appeared as a long line of light as they traveled north east in a straight line. I'm only contacting you because of the manner in which these lights appeared from a single fixed point in the sky. Maybe our country has tech that allows our planes to do that. But I heard no sound like jets or props. There were a total of 50 or more lights.
