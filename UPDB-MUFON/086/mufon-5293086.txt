Triangular pattern captured in nightlapse footage twice.

On the night of July 16th 2020 I set up my go-pro to capture star coverage for a film project I've been working on. During the night-lapse you can clearly see seemingly unnatural multi-directional cloud covering followed by a triangular light formation moving from the South West. The unidentified object passed behind tree coverage, something either alerted or spooked an outside cat who then knocks over the go-pro before the object can move further into view. However shortly after an identical triangular pattern of lights is seen moving from the North East in the opposite direction of the initial path. The battery dies shortly after. 
The initial UFO can be seen in the attached video around 58 second in the top right corner, and then again around 1 minute 5 seconds in the lower left corner.

The trees in the video are just about 90ft tall for reference.
