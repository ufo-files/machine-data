ok it was about 1999 or 2000 i was in the poccanos working a carnival we shut down for the night and me and 4 friends met at my buddys trailer to hang out and have a couple drinks we went in sat down at table the door was left open to get air.before anyone could crack a drink open i saw a bright blue light shinning in the door i asked my friend if someone still had a ride or game open and lit up he said no everything is off so we went outside to make shur and everything was off someone looked up and started pointing not saying a word we all looked up and saw a bright blue ring glowing around a saucer shaped craft just hovering there we all looked at each other and made shur we were all seeing the same thing when we looked back up there was such a bright flash of light we could not see for a couple seconds when i could see again all there was was a blue trail of light going off into space.we kinda said we would never talk about it cause who would believe us.lol  but a couple days later i remembered when i was a small child my mom came into my room to tuck me in when she closed door i saw a pair of big green eyes looking at me from corner of room i pulled covers over head and dont really remember anything after that.but that is my story and i am convinced what i saw was not of this world.nothing we have can move in the blink of an eye from a stand still let alone hovering in the silent mountain night air....no noise at all.
