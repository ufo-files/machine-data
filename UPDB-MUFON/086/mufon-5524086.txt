I saw a green glowing light in the night sky in Phoenix tonight. At first I thought it was a green firecracker, but it was flying horizontally. It didn't last long, less than a minute. I've never seen anything like it before. It looked like a green fireball and dissappeared into thin air. I couldn't think of a practical explanation for the object I saw. It was fairly close to where I was standing outside in the backyard. I later looked online and thought maybe it could be a military flare, but I don't know of glowing green military flares. I felt confused and wanted to make sense of what I saw, but I couldn't. I was at my parent's house, but I was the only one outside who witnessed this event. I wished I had it on video but it happened so fast.
