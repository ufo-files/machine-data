{nd} Saw reflective craft while commercial fishing on Georges Bank

While commercial fishing on Georges Bank on a 120' dragger, myself, three other crew members and captain saw a UFO. The craft was silver/white reflective and shaped liked old fashioned barbells like what strong men lifted in old circuses. Two spheres connected by a bar or tube. The scale of spheres to bar was just like the old barbells as well. It was impossible to judge size as we'd no idea how far away the object was. We all got a good look for perhaps 2 or 3 minutes. The captain lifted a camera with zoom lens and the moment he started to aim for a photo the craft sped off upward at perhaps a 70 degree angle so fast as to seem to vanish instantly. No idea what it was but we all saw it and it was very odd. Absolutely a UFO, no doubt about it. We all agreed to tell no one as we were afraid of being ridiculed.
