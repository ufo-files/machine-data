Two sets of 15-20 lights travelling east in a uniform fashion. Steady speed with equal spacing aside from a few larger gaps.

Looking south, I spotted an object what I thought was a satellite. This object was followed by 10-15 same size objects travelling eastward at a steady rate of speed. Spacing was somewhat uniform.
