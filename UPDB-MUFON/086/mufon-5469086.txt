{cak} Blinked on twice, three times in a row.

On March 16th, 2018 at approx. 9:00 PM, I went outside of my home and happened to look North in the sky and within 2 minutes, I saw what appeared to be a star. The object then blinked at me two times and when it did, I saw that it looked like an oblong-shaped object. The object blinked twice within a second then turned itself off. Then the object blinked twice within a second again and again, turned itself off. It blinked twice again and turned itself off. It then traveled West and disappeared.
