{rjl} Orb circling

Walked outside of my apartment going to my car, to go to a gas station. Approaching my vehicle I noticed a light above me. Having my phone I took a short video of a orb of light circling. At first I though it was a beacon of light hitting the clouds at the right angle to make the object look like it was an orb. However I ruled that out after watching it for a few minutes. I know it was not an aircraft or helicopter, based of my knowledge of being an air traffic controller for the air force, the way the light looked and they way it moved. The video was poor due to the iPhone 4's ability and the fact that I was tracing the movement of the object, instead of keeping it stationary. After about five minutes I decided to continue what I was doing losing interest. Coming back 15 minutes later, it was gone.
