{rjl} Glaring orange orbs estimated at 5-10 feet in diameter taveling in a pair shifting relative to each other, then a single, then another pair (video).

I was sitting on my patio facing NW listening to nearby fireworks but unable to see most of them due to trees. I checked weather radar because I could see lightening thru the trees some distance away (30 miles or so east of my location). I noticed I only had 5% charge left and turned phone off. within seconds I saw a pair of glaring orange orbs through the trees headed in my direction. They obviously weren't fireworks. My phone was still shutting down when they came over the treeline and I'm guessing were 100 feet away traveling E to NW at approximately 500 feet altitude. I ran up the cul de sac and watched them travel over the horizon. I'm thinking, "damn, missed opportunity." I'm walking back down the hill and saw another single headed my way, same flight path. I booted up my phone and managed to get a few seconds of video as that one followed the first two. I then said to myself, " Please let there be more..." Sure enough, I saw 2 more through the trees but further away following a more northerly track in the same general direction as the first 3. I was able to video those two. They were not as bright and were an estimated 500-1000 yards away from me, same altitude. Video exceeds MB sending to Mufon, stop by and you can see it.
