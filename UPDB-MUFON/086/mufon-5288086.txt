Bright coppery yellow pulsating light moving from east to west

Went outside for a smoke. Was looking up, noticed a bright light in the east. Glowing a reddish orangey yellow. Round fuzzy diamond shape. Moving out of the ordinary flight paths of the planes. Noticed it was moving at a moderate speed but not consistently. Wavered up and down but stayed more or less on a straight western path. Light seemed to pulse gently but never went out entirely. Never heard any sounds whatsoever.
Had a weird tingly sensation the entire time I was watching it.
