{rjl} Unknown super fast bright light passing close to the moon.

Was a passenger on a vehicle traveling east on the Long Island Expressway by the town of Westbury when a bright light zoomed at a an excessive on the right side of the moon. The object didn't pulse, didn't increase in brightness like a typical meteor or disintegrate leaving a trail of lights like a meteor and the light was as bright as the moon. If it was a meteor, I'm sure there would be more reports about it but I haven't heard anything so far.
