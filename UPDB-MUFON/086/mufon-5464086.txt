I had just gotten home from school, and when i climbed out of my car, i looked to the east, and no more than maybe 500 feet away, no more than 30 feet in the air, i saw an odd flying craft. It was T shaped, a cylinder with a saucer on top, and maybe 10 feet in height. It made no noise as i watched it ascend vertically,and then it began moving horizontally eastward. It stayed perfectly upright, and had green lights. it moved slowly, i watched it for a long time, probably 2 minutes before it disappeared over a house. It did not occur to me to be a UFO, i did not feel uneasy seeing it. I calmly watched it fly away wondering if it was a neighbor's drone, but only much afterward thought it might be unusual--in fact, i almost totally forgot about it, i didn't remember to mention it or think about it until hours later.
