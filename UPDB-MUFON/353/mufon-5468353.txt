{rjl} Witnessed Object above cities north side, then object moved above city

I was riding a bus from Chicago's Edgewater neighborhood to city center when I observed an object, initially thought to be airplane, then realized no tail fin, object was stationary (did not resemble blimp), appeared to be flat possibly cylindrical. I checked flightradar24 app, as I'm an aviation enthusiast to see if it may have been a plane but none showing on their radar in the area. I was on a bus travelling down Lake Shore Drive near Belmont Harbor object appeared to be west approximately 1 mile, tried to take picture but speed of bus and trees moving into frame made it impossible. Moved around to make sure was not light reflection inside bus and could still see object. Looked down to text friend about what I was seeing and when I looked up, object was gone. I scanned the sky and noticed same object now over Chicago's Loop. Again confirmed was not a reflection, again tried to get picture/video but speed of bus/obstructions made it impossible. Initial reaction was shock and excitement when I realized this may not have been an airplane but something else. Ending reaction was disappointment that I was unable to get a picture or video.  At this point we were in the city and unable to see object further.
