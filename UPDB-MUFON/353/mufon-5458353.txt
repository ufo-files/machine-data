I saw the object appearing like a very bright star moving from south to north at approximately 60° above from the west horizon. Then it got really bright moved off at a different angle fast really bright and disappeared in a flash going into a little pin point of light and faded away. I thought it might’ve been an Iridium satellite but I checked the database and there was none anticipated. Truly a sight to see. I watch satellites all the time. It was brighter than the ISS and then the flash was huge when it disappeared in a little pinpoint of light.
