High altitude craft moving East with constant 90’ left hand turns

Shortly after viewing the ISS another object with a white light was sited probably close to the altitude of the ISS. This new object had a straight trajectory and would make left hand turn followed by straight and left etc. I’m a private pilot and do not know of any aircraft known to the public which is capable of this maneuver. Continued for app. 5 mins before going over the horizon.
