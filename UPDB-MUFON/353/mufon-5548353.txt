Traveling S IH-35 when object appeared on my right side of vehicle.

I was traveling S.IH-35 near Mcneil Rd. on my way to work at U.S. postal facility. I noticed a bright object approximately 30 meters to my right and parallel to my window. I did not think; I just observed the object and still have no idea, what it was. The object was a bright, approximately 12-16" in diameter. I first noticed a downward motion and then an object traveling parallel(eye level/same speed) to my truck for several miles. After the fact, I reviewed the incident; comparing it to a meteor:color,speed,trails,motions. The object then moved slowly down and to the right behind a tree line.
