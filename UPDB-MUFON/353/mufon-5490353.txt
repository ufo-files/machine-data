In 1960 I decided to join the army in Cuba I was enthusiast with the Revolution and to consider you fit you had to climb 3 times the Turquino Peak 6002 meter above sea level. In the 2nd journey to the peak at 5:20 am we observed downward because we where at least 4000 mmeter high, a couple of very bright lights a dozen or so that where maneuvering down in a valley toward the coastline, at first some of my companions said that it was probably some secret weapon brought from the USSR. But I knew that what those lights were doing, defys all phisical laws that I knew. They were ascending and descending each one of them by themselves, there was no unity, they look like bees landing on flowers, but at a speed that left no doubt it wasnt human made machines. There where around 800 witness that saw all that. after 20 min. checked by watch they disappeared just as they made their appearance. It was a great experience in my life. I am now 70 years old and still have this memory as fresh as that day. Later on I realize what a jerk I was joining this army that has cause so much suffering to my people. I was wise enough to escape from that atrocity in my twentys. thank you
