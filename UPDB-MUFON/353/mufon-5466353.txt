{rjl} Taking a sunset picture, never saw object until the camera review, looked up and nothing was there.

I was taking a sunset picture like I almost always do on my lanai in Napili, Maui when I saw a blurb in my picture review on my camera, I did not see anything thru my viewfinder as I was shooting. I looked up and saw nothing in the sky. We are currently in hurricane watch conditions from Lane with winds at 20 - 25 mph at the time, so I don't think it was a drone or a weather balloon because how could it disappear so fast, maybe 5 to 10 seconds elapsed from shooting to looking up. In the picture the wind is blowing right to left. Upon downloading my shots I noticed the blurb and assumed it was a large bird, some trash blowing (doubtful) or possibly something on my lens. No, no and no. I zoomed in and saw a round disc with a V shape cut from one side, greyish - green. The photo was taken at 1/1000 of a second, a setting I used in the high winds to 'freeze' the trees. I have the original raw file still. I then posted the pictures when I girl I know posted these in response, a photo from Oahu that shows a very similar object. I'm truly bewildered by what I saw. The 1st shot is a cropped sunset shot showing the object in relation to the surrounding area. #2 is zoomed in from the original shot. #3 came from Oahu and #4 is zoomed in on that shot.
