{dr}Bullet shape, big green lights maybe 1 or 2, lower than a plane flies, moving slow then super fast and just vanished .

Heading east on a side street connecting with Watson road on my way home from work. Listening to music, I approached the stop sign across from Adams furnace on Watson. There's an auto shop to my right and an out of business Peruvian stone shop to my left. Approximately 11:40-11:45 pm. As I was about to turn right onto Watson heading south, I looked up, not sure if the light caught my attention. It was slow moving for like a second or 2. I thought it was a plane or jet but it wasn't shaped like anything I've ever seen and the color of the light was so unusual and so pretty. It was like a greenish neon blue but dull and bright at the same time. There were maybe 2 lights on the craft. The color of the craft was a light and dark grey. It seemed to be flying too low to be an aircraft and heading in the opposite direction of lambert airport. It sped up so quickly and just disappeared completely out of site. I have never seen anything like this in my life but am so in awe of what I just saw. I know St. Louis is close to Belleville, IL where Scott Air Force base is located, and could have been a jet or whatever, but it was shaped like nothing I've ever seen and the color of those lights was so intense and how it moved. It was so graceful. Just thought I'd put this info out there as I am baffled and curious more than ever. Appreciate your time reading this. Thank you.
