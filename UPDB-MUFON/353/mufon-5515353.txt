{nd} A red glowing ball rose up and disappeared behind tree. when chased to look it was totally gone

I was sitting on my back patio near cornhusker highway having a cigarette. When I looked up, I saw a red ball of light ascend from behind a building; Im thinking it was in the parking lot, which is out of sight line from where I live. It ascended in an arcing manner. I said "no way," and pursued my sight-line on foot because the object had gone behind the leaves of a tree in our backyard. It was totally gone. No noise, no trail, no nothing.
