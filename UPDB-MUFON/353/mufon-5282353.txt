Shooting star like

On March 15, 1994 [name moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] and I were on the Navajo Reservation near Blue Gap, Arizona driving on a dirt road about 15 miles from the highway near some power lines. I saw what appeared to be a shooting star from the west. We stopped our vehicle to observe it to watch it go down. We saw the object stop abruptly in its path and we could see different colored lights flashing around it. Red, blue, green and white lights. The object would descend and ascend moving up and down in one movement so that when it ascended it went up so high it twinkled like a star but then it would descend and we could see its round disc shape and the lights flashing around it. This lasted for about 3 minutes and then the object shot off at rapid speed to the south and we never saw it again. As it shot off to the south I said did you see that and [name moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] said yes. We never reported this sighting because we knew no one would believe us.
