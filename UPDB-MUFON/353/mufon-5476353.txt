{rjl} Bright streak of white light behind orb. Huge, fell like falling star. Then 5 or 6 bright pulses of light behind clouds.

My 15 year old son and my 10 year old son were sitting in the parked truck using their phones on our ranch road about 9:45 pm. This road is 20 miles from any town. A huge bright white orb suddenly fell like a falling star. Streaked in an arc across the distant horizon. It was very large. It fell below the mountain horizon and the clouds in the distance (15+miles away). They looked at each other and said "did you see that?". They were very scared so they drove off. They looked back and saw a huge pulsing/beating white light behind the thin clouds. It pulsed 5 or 6 times. There were large streaks of light radiating from the pulsing light. My younger son thought it was a huge meteor. My older son thought it was a ufo. They were very scared and their hearts were racing as they told the story to us just a few minutes after.
