In the mid nineties I was walking across the street to work going east. It was 10 to 6 am and the sun was half an hour from rising, as it was winter. As I crossed the street, I caught sight of what I thought was a large [falling star] It seemed to have the same speed of the average meteor. It was coming in at a 45 degree angle from the SE and straight east of my line of vision it did a slight curve upward to a 2"0clock angle at the same speed.It paused a half sec.,Then shot off straight north and was gone in a second, reminded me of star trek warp speed. I only told my family about this and they thought i was loco. So, I never mentioned it again. Could it have been a meteor skipping across the upper atmosphere?
