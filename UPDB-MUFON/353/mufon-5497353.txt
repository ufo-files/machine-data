{cak} Very bright pulsating light (3 sets of 4 pulses). 2-3 times brighter than any star in the sky. The light remained perfectly stationary in the same NW location. The pulses grew in intensity from beginning to end of each set.

Two of my friends and I were out late in the evening watching the Perseid meteor shower, looking towards the northwest. The meteors were pretty scarce but suddenly a bright light appeared that was so unusually bright that it caught our attention. The light appeared and grew in intensity until it was 2-3 times brighter than any star in the sky. It held that intensity for a few seconds and then slowly decreased from intensity until it disappeared. This pulsing repeated three more times and then there was a period of perhaps 8 seconds of darkness and then the pulsing repeated low to high intensity again but the shocking my thing to us to as that instead of moving along a track in the sky (as you would expect a plane or satellite to move) these pulses appeared in exactly the same position. There were in total, three sets of four pulses which were separated by the approximately 8 second intervals. 

The intensity of the light, the metronome (like) pulsing and the fact that it remained stationary was so unusual that none of us could come up with any possible explanation. It was a mind boggling combination. We still talk about it and I decided to file a report in hopes of ci find an explanation or someone else who has seen this phenomenon.
