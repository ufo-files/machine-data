Multiple translucent discs seen flying in groupS of 3 and appearing to land within large rectangular shaped craft hovering above Wellington city

At 11am 27th July 2018 I was a passenger in a car vehicle, driven by my friend, back into Wellington City from Eastbourne.
As he drove I noticed a large rectangular shaped cloud in the distance clouds, directly over the commercial and government area of Wellington. I took a number of photos of the large strangely shaped cloud and didn’t think much more about it.

Later that day when flicking through the photos we noticed a number of oddities that we did not see with naked eye, namely several different photos showing groups of 3 glowing, translucent seeming dome/discs in formation dropping down from above, and all heading towards the same direction... the middle of the large rectangular shaped cloud. Then disappearing.

The scenes were captured in ‘live’ mode and this we understand now is why we are able to view the discs, and also why they appear to be jumping about - as they would appear if moving much faster than our eyes would see?

The image in which we pass under a bridge is particularly relevant as the discs go behind the bridge, unlike how a reflection, glare or other such thing would appear. Similarly, there are images showing discs coming from top left into the rectangular shape, as well as top right.
