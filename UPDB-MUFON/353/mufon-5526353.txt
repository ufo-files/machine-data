Small star-like object moving at a moderate rate of speed high in the sky

I was standing on my balcony looking up at the sky straight up above my head I saw a star formation Alphekka, and then it looked like a star broke free. I first thought it was just a blur, or a plane, but then it flew in a pattern unfamiliar to planes, it was erratic in nature, it paused, and then continue to move north through Hercules, toward Vega and disappeared. It didn't gain intensity, it stayed a faint brightness, but remained visible for almost 2 minutes, and then it disappeared. I felt like this was my first actual sighting, that I wanted to share with a community. After it took me an hour to gather my thoughts, and decide that I had to tell someone about it. I went back out to look and I didn't see anything like what I saw before.
