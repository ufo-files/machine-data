{cak} 3 bright red/orange spheres heading ne very fast

My wife returned home from work and we went on the porch to have a cigarette. Looking over the treetops, I saw a bright orange/red sphere moving in a straight line north east, moving very fast. The first thing I noticed was no sound. I thought they might be an airplane, but there was no noise or blinking lights. Shortly thereafter, roughly 5-10 seconds, I saw a second object appear and travel along the same line. After 30 seconds, a third object followed the same flight path. I watched the 3rd until it disappeared.
