We were standing on the deck smoking, my daughter had brought our grandson to the farm for the weekend. We noticed a light in the distance flying up and down kind of bouncing across the sky. We had a million candle watt power flash light my daughter said hey do an sos signal to that light so we did the ship dropped below the trees came back up and flew right at us! It flew over the north side of the farm then flew over the barn! It was flat black we did not need binoculars to see it. It could have landed on the barn with no problem. It then changed direction and headed toward us 40. On the back was an octagon shaped light it was glowing red orange and you could see fluid moving in it. My daughter left and said she followed it until she turned off in Danville Indiana on to 39 it flew toward Plainfield Indiana. We see ufo's there all the time!
