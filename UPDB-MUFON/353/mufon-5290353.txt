there were 3 moons that night

one night in november of 2004 i was travelling eastbound on hwy 417 in ottawa, ontario. i'm a truck driver and was there to deliver a load. i noticed that there were what appeared to be 3 moons that night. the second moon was approximately 1/3 the diamater of the moon and the third was approximately 1/2 of the second moon's diameter. they were in a straight line with the moon on a 45 degree angle as i looked at it that would be southeast if you were looking at a compass. i thought at first that maybe my eyes were playing tricks on me because there was a lot of traffic and it seemed that no one else saw it. i got to the customer and noticed it was still there. i went to bed and got up around 3am and it was still there. in the morning when i went to our yard it was daylight but i could still see the moon and the objects were still visible. i never saw anything like that again and never heard of anyone else with a similar sighting. at that time i did not yet have a camera on my phone so i have no pictures or video evidence
