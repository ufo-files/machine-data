UFO over Ottsville, PA

We've seen the same large object every clear night for the past 5 weeks. The object appears star-like at dusk and then descends from altitude and hovers/moves to the right until it's out of view from our back yard, typically over a 2-3 hour period every night. On 6/12/21 we witnessed dozens of smaller very bright tick-tac shaped object moving fast across the sky in sorties of 5-6 each, in single-file/straight-line formation moving from E to W. We must have seen 8-10 sorties go by and they all disappeared in the vicinity of the larger object. They appeared to be independently controlled - one came up fast from behind and fell into formation before they disappeared behind a cloud.

Also, the larger object is the same one we witnessed (and reported to MUFON) one year ago over a 3-week period in June 2020.
