Two white tandem lights

While walking my dog this morning around the block I was heading south when I noticed what appeared to be two brite stars in the pre-dawn sky. The sky was very clear and these lights at first appeared to be stars but they were in motion coming toward me traveling in a northern direction. They moved in tandem as if at opposite ends of a blimp or something but at one point directly overhead I could view another star between them. They were traveling at a constant speed and took about 2 1/2 minutes to completely disappear into the northern horizon. I kept looking for flashing lights or red lights that would indicate a plane but did not see any. I also listened for engine noise of any sort but heard nothing.
