{jpg} 4 lights in the sky- excitement in the sky - SE Phoenix

On Sat Sept 26th 2015 at aprx 918pm AZ time. Myself and a friend were sitting outside and we both saw, a box shape, with 4 glowing lights on it appear in the south east sky it did not come up from the ground, but just came from out of nowhere, went up for a few sec, then moved left to the east and then just disappeared in the sky. It was only there for about 8-10 sec, could not get a picture, almost seemed to grow in size slightly as it moved left before it was gone. It almost looked like it was on fire but it was the lights. Nothing else to report on it.
