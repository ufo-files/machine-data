{nd} Picture taken of sunrise and craft was in it.Not visible to naked eye.

My 18 year old son went out on side porch in Higganum Ct. early before work at 5:40 am on Oct. 31, 2016 and liked the way the sky looked. So he took a picture for his girlfriend. After taking the picture my son realized there was some type of craft in the picture that was invisible to his naked eyes. The lights were directly over the church steeple across the street from our house. He looked up at the sky and still saw nothing then came inside to show me the picture. He was pretty confused and surprised at what was in the picture. He felt it was alien and after looking at other real pictures of craft I felt this needed to be shared with others.
