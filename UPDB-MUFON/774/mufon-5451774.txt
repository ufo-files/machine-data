Watched an orb move higher in the air. Took out phone and snapped a picture and went to switch to camera my phone crashed and the object vanished.

I was crossing the steel bridge in downtown Portland, Oregon on the local light rail train. The sunset was beautiful so I went to gaze at it's beauty when I overheard the guy next to me gasp in awe. Following his gaze and I saw what he was staring at. It was a whitish orb but with what looked like a row of lights or engines, I couldn't tell. I quickly pulled my phone out and snapped not only a gorgeous picture of downtown Portland, but also a picture of the craft. I looked down to swap to video and my phone crashed. I looked up and watched as it bolted a short distance to where it then completely vanished. After getting home and examining the picture closer you can clearly see what looks to be lights or large engines. They did pulse a bit so I'm not sure.
