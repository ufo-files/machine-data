{jpg} Observed a white orb moving west to east above home for 2-3 minutes; rapidly took off until it disappeared after passing over home.

On Saturday night I was enjoying an outside fire with my mother and father-in-law when all of the sudden I spotted a glowing white orb about 500-700 ft. above the water, which is west of my in-laws house by about 300 ft. The object was much lower than a satellite and moved at a much more rapid pace than airplanes. The object moved west to east above the house for a total of about two minutes until it all of the sudden accelerated at a very fast pace and disappeared from sight. Both my father-in-law and mother-in-law also observed the object and asked "What was that?" due to the object's unnatural movements that were not like that of an airplane, satellite or shooting star. The object was a solid white orb and did not blink like airplanes do during flight. While the object was still in sight, I immediately turned on my Skyview app to try to identify the object but it did not register. One thing I found interesting was that the International Space Station was directly over my in-laws when the event happened, according to my Skyview app. It was unlike anything I've ever seen before.
