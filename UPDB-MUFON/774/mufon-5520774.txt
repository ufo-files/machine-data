UFO over ships in Singapore Harbor

I was watching the history channel tonight and I saw a picture that looked alot like mine so I thought that I would follow up. I have a professional camera that I was taking pictures with on top of the Sands Hotel in Singapore. I saw a black spot in the sky over the harbor. I took bunch of closeups of the object. Thought it was a little weird and went about my way walking around the hotel observation deck. About 20 min later I saw that it was still there so I took a few more as the sun was setting and a couple wide angle to show distance from me. I only had a 300mm lens with me at the time and saw another guy with a huge lens. I showed him the object and ask him to take a picture. He said "that is a UFO... We see them all the time- I have a bunch of pictures of them." Then he walked away. Then it got dark and I could not see it any longer. No lights, at that time. I went back and looked at my pictures and zoomed them in at 16MP. It was a black object and had orange, green and blue lights or reflections in it. It almost looks like there is a face on the bottom of the object.

I do not have the photos handy right now but if you are still interested I can pull them out of my archive. They will all be time stamped and show the camera info.
