{cak} Watched them in the sky around the moon

My fiance and I went out on our balcony to watch the blood moon event. When we noticed what we thought was a star. As I said look there is only one star out it moved. We started watching it as it went from one side of the moon to the other. Then three more appeared. They all were hovering around the moon and circling it. We watched for at least an hour but had to go in to get to bed. We did get pictures and one video before we went inside.
