Intense bright red/orange/yellow lights hovering above trees past a field

Was out smoking a cigar and saw incredibly bright lights through the trees of the neighbor's woods. They were very, very bright through the thick trees. Went inside to grab my phone, and recorded 10 minutes of them. As I went to inspect closer to the road, I found I could see them more clearly above and past the other neighbor's field.

They seemed far away but quite large. Much, much, much brighter than stars, or perhaps even a bright moon, and quite orange-red, though I think the color changes. Not sure how many there were, but it seems to be more than one or two. The video I took is 4 GiB and my upload speed is trash being rural.

I have a hard time imagining nobody else saw this although it was in the middle of the night. -Very- noticeable and attention-grabbing even to someone with very poor vision.
