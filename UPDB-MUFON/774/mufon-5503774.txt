Giant cloud hides UFO as it lands on Punta Leona early Feb 28

At least 10 different people saw this incredible event. Later we went as a group and found the landing site with bare foot prints and heavy holes that looke as if metal rods were poked into the earth. Some testimonies are on video, spanish language.
Intense colors filled all shadows and surfaces when the lights flashed silently from inside the cloud. 1.5 inches of rain fell in the immediate area in 10 minutes, which is in a bottle waiting for analysis.
Pictures from the landing site and HD video terstimonies plus a recreation from my POV on the balcony are online. I was unable to use my camera during the event as I was clearly told in my head "It is not important to film this but only to see it with full attention, leave your camera behind, it will not work anyway". Similar feelings stopped the neighbor from across the street from filming at the same moment. We all went bact to sleep before the UFO activity had finished, although it lasted only for another 5 minutes, ending on a blue light tone flashing alternatively with red.
I know of at least 10 witnesses to the lights appearance and numerous subsequent lights of a smaller nature have been observed on at least three seperate occasions. I have been out to investigate them as I have seen them and they regularly are accompanied by a power outage.
