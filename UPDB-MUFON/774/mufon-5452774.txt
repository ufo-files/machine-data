Red light moving phenomenally fast

I'm British but was visiting Croatia. I hadn't had a drink because everything was shut but was out late and was lying on the monument to the sun listening to the sea organ at around 2.30 am with two guys I met in my hostel after we stayed up chatting and decided to go for a walk. I saw a red orb move erratically across the sky towards the south east across the coast. I suspected a drone at first but it shot off northwards at a phenomenal speed and vanished from sight. I believe from researching afterwards that an island north of Zadar is a UFO hotspot. I live on a flight path in England so I'm familiar with seeing planes, this was unbelievably fast. Its speed and direction wasn't consistent enough to be a shooting star or meteor - both of which I've witnessed in real life. I've also seen drones used professionally as I work for a fire service that uses them. This was way too fast. The other guys missed it, they were both Spanish speakers and chatting away in Spanish while I was resting. Very beautiful town, lovely sunsets!
