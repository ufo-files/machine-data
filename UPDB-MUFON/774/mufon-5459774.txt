{rjl} Two selfies taken back to back. The second has an undeniable bright disc

Basically, an old friend and I were hanging out and she decided to take a couple selfies of us. In the first selfie it is just us two in a parking lot with houses and a night sky in the background. In the second picture (which was taken basically at the same time) there is an undeniable, very bright tic tac shaped disc in the background. It has different layers to it and as the layers go on you can see the brightness gets less intense but still has a white color. I DID NOT SEE IT IN PERSON, but I have had these two photos for over a year now and everyone I have shown them to have not been able to figure it out. It looks extraterrestrial from an average persons point of view. Can’t wait to share.
