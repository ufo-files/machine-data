Two friends and I had just changed the brakes on my Nissan Maxima in Stover, Missouri. We then began our drive back towards Jefferson City, Missouri. I was driving and they were passengers. Minutes into our drive, I noticed a light in the sky from the corner of my eye. It completely frightened me. I had never seen anything like it before. My initial reaction was to accelerate the vehicle, which I did. My friend noticed my anxiousness as I swerved on and off the road at a high rate of speed on this desolate and winding road. As I hollered, [item removed/CMS/jpg] , my friend attempted to steer the car wheel. I was not looking at the road. I was focused on the gigantic object hovering over our heads just to the left of my vehicle. I didn't know what it was. I had never seen anything like it. The object was the shape of a "remote control" for a television. It was GIGANTIC. Along the rim of the object there were white and yellow/orange lights that alternated. The object was appearing to come from within a dark cloud that had opened up like a doughnut. I call it a cloud but it was much too dark to be any cloud I have ever seen. It was if the object was coming through a worm hole. I could only see half of the object but judging from the half I could see, I knew it's entire size was that of a football field. It wasn't motionless. The front of the craft was going in a circular motion while going up and down simultaneously. I continued be in disbelief. My friend in the passenger seat did not look towards the sky, he was busy fighting to keep the car on road by steering the wheel which I was ignoring (but pressing the gas firmly) My other friend in the back seat said his window was foggy. As I looked back onto the road, my friend in the back seat is heard saying, "you see that?".....I turned again and the object was gone. My friend said he saw a bright flashing light but no object....But, we all witnessed the dark, black cloud in the sky that did not disappear. The craft that came from the middle of it was gone but the dark cloudlike image stayed beside us all the way back to Jefferson City, Mo.
