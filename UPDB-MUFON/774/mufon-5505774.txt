{rjl} Orange ball of light hover then changes directions

Wife, son, and I were in our home and my wife was letting the cat in from outside and she noticed an orange ball of light in the sky. It would move from west to east and then west again. I will attach the video that was taken. I tried zooming in and out, which is the cause of the shakiness in the middle.

I tried taking pictures of the object, but the camera wouldnt focus.

We have seen these lights many times south of Maricopa, AZ, but their usually more southwest of our home. This is the first time I saw them directly south of us.
