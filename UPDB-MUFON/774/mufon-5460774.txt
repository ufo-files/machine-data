I was on a Southwest flight last Friday, May 17th from MSP to STL. Beautiful day not a cloud in the sky. I was sitting in the first row middle seat. I looked out the window because another passenger was pointing out the object and when I looked out I saw a black disc off in the distance roughly a mile away if I had to guess. I was hovering horizontal for awhile and was, what seemed like, flying alongside the plane. As I am looking out of the window, 2 things happened.
1) the object became vertical - literally vertical 
2) right at that time a black trail shot out from under the plane and straight towards this object. 
Multiple people, including myself were startled and made a noise as if they were frightened though when this trail shot out from under the plane, the plane did not move at all, no turbulence or anything that seemed to upset the air flow of the flight.maybe 30 seconds or so after this happened the plane seemed to turn towards this, whether it was to get a better look I don't know.Once the trail made it to the object it seemed to absorb it and shortly after it disappeared. I don't know what it was though I can tell you, I was in the USAF an guarded U2 planes overseas and I have never seen anything like what I saw last week. To top it all off, as I was walking off the plane I asked the pilot if he saw that trail shoot out from under the plane and towards the object to the east? He looked at me, smiled and said "I dont know what you are talking about." No shot, he knew exactly what i was talking about. .
