{dr}Light in sky making abrupt turns and moving at high speeds. Moved in zigzag pattern.

My friend and I were out camping one night and we were looking at the stars while talking about something. Then I noticed a light that seemed to be moving, about the size of a star. I got out a monocular to see it a little better and we watched it for about 20 or 30 minutes before losing track of it after I looked away to look for my notebook to write down the date and other information. It made several abrupt turns and seemed like it was moving rather fast, faster than a plane would be moving at roughly the same altitude. At first we thought it may be a drone or a plane, but who would be flying a drone at 3AM? There are also no military bases close by.
