silent triangle light formation

As my daughter and I were soaking in the hot tub, we noticed a strange looking light in the east sky. As we observed this light, it grew into two lights. At this point I thought, this is a plane coming right at us. However, as it progressed, I knew, this was not a plane. It was very low and silent as it went right over head,not to mention, it was moving way to slow.As it was directly on top of us, a third light was present. Two in the front and one in the rear. It was in the shape of a triangle, with the flat part of the triangle being the front of the craft. We continued to watch it, excitedly, until it continued west out of sight. If only I had the DVR out with us!
