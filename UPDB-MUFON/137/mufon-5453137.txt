Hunting UFO's and recording the Sun

I keep finding UFO's during the day. I have long understood them to be telepathic, and perhaps they know I am watching them because I seem to find one in almost every picture I take. I was rapidly taking pictures on my canon t5i and when some birds flew into the frame I decided to follow them and use them as focal points so that I could focus the depth of field against the sky. I found orbs in every single shot. Along with a strange artifact that seemed to pop into the frame for a millisecond and then vanished. There was no other bird around when this happened and I have the flight path of the bird in every frame to show you the before and after. The camera must be able to pick these objects up. I have a feeling they are interdimensional and not mechanical.
