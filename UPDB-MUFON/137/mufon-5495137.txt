on Saturday night 3/28/2015 at 9:33 pm i was standing outside in front of my house and i looked up at the sky as it was a clear night.i then saw a bright spherical light at the 12 o'clock position i first thought it was a satellite i noticed it was traveling from south to north in a straight line. at 11 o'clock position it suddenly got brighter and larger, at 10 o'clock position it dimmed back to its original size and brightness. a few seconds later it dimmed to a dot and then vanished.it was not a meteror or satellite as i see them often.it made no sound or emission, it was a clear night i did observe a military jet traveling in the same direction about 2 minutes later. i wanted to get my phone to take a photo of it but was afraid i wouldn't have time to go back in the house and retrieve it in time.
