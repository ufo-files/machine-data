anecdotal sightings

Along with several friends sat on the sidewalk to rest after playing all afternoon and talk. We spent an hour talking about UFOs, and then another 30 minutes talking about the pyramids. Most of my friends was about 11 years, my older friends were between 15 to 16 years. When our mothers called us to go home, some look at the sky and some of my younger friends said it was small flying saucer, old friends said it was a plane or a helicopter, but then stopped the flying object noiselessly, and when all my friends saw the flying object that rose vertically until it was lost quickly in less than a second.
