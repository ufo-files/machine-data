Three round dots inline

Went outside in my backyard. Looked up at the sky which is common for me. After a second or so I looked to the Northwest and saw at first two dots back to back moving what looked to me to be east. They were too close to be airplanes or helicopters. I Immediately went and got my wife. When we looked up in that same direction there were now three objects. All inline and back to back. The were moving east at a steady rate. We watched these objects move across the sky until we were blocked by a tree. We moved a few feet to see them come from behind the tree and they didn't. They disappeared in a second. This was unbelievable to see. But my wife and I are convinced we witnessed something that was not normal.
