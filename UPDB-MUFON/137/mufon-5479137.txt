{cak} One orange glowing orb was hovering over the ocean. One more appeard. Then a third. They then vanished and re-appeared miles down the beach. No sounds heard.

I was on my week long vacation at N. Myrtle Beach in SC, USA. My cousin and I were walking on the beach one night and noticed a bright orange-yellow ball in the sky hovering maybe a few miles away over the ocean. It then disappeared for a few seconds and then re-appeared miles down the beach to the south. It stayed in one place every time after re-appearing and seemed to be the same distance offshore always. After maybe 5-8 minutes it disappeared again and re-appeared in the first place we saw it originally. It hovered there for a few minutes and then another orb of the same color and size seemed to come out of the first orb and it hovered beside the original orb to the right of it. A few minutes passed and a third orb appeared in the same exact manner as the second but it hovered to the left of the original orb. They were all a uniform distance from one another and just stayed in one spot for a few minutes. They then vanished and we never saw them again.
