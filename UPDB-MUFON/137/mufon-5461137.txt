Well the F-22s I observed the 21st were pursuing a UFO as I came to notice. The pictures I am posting show the craft in seperate places in each picture. The pictures were taken 1 after the other . The craft made the persuing aircraft look ridiculous as it maneuvered away from them effortlessly.
