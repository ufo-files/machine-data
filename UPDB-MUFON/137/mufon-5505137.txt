bright orb hovering infront of left wing of airplane, sudden descent, leaving trail

Flight to Cancun, window seat on left of plane, directly adjacent to left wing, looking out window, approximately 30 - 45min prior to landing, roughly 10:45pm (local time); saw very bright orb in front of left wing about 1 metre from tip. At first I thought it was the brightest star I had ever seen, realized it couldn't possibly be a star as it was too close and far too bright with no defined perimeter. I couldn't take my eyes off of it, I wanted to tell my travel companion but I didn't want to look away to tell her. Orb stayed in same position to aircraft for several seconds and suddenly descended straight down extremely fast leaving a tail of white light. after the orb has disappeared from sight I kept looking for it, I then looked around the plane to see if anyone else seemed sort of stunned and excited. I turned to my friend, with an odd look on my face I suppose because she immediately asked me "what? what's going on?" I excitedly told her what I had just seen.
