{td} Looks like very bright star going left to right, up, down with small stars emanating.

I was in my kitchen when I saw the object in the window in the Southern sky (high up). I thought it was a star, but it was very bright and large. It moved erratically right to left and up and down. It appeared to be sending out small faint stars on and off and even a beam or two. I went outside to view it and it did the same thing. It's still there.
