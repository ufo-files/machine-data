{cak} Ball of bright white light zoomed passed my house the change direction and after 19 minutes faded into a black hole in the sky.

I was on my way home and as I got closer to my house I noticed a ball of bright white light move very quickly past my house then changed direction and went to the mountains in the distance and hovered. The light pulsed a few times and after about 5 minutes it pulsed very quickly and what was very bright faded into what looked like a black hole. The stars behind the object were not there and then when it disappeared the stars reappeared. I saw something like this a couple of months ago this one was brighter and it changed direction but they both times went over the same mountain and hovered. Both times they just got bright white and just disappeared into what I can say looked like a black hole. I truly wish I knew what these two objects were. I am thinking of setting up a camera outside all night to see if I can catch something on camera. This is really bugging me and I can only hope more people see these things and report them but some may think it's just a shooting star but they are not. Shooting stars they did not burn out like regular shooting stars this is truly puzzling.
