{cak} Driving to the gas station.

My friend and i were driving to the gas station, when i saw the first light. This is not the first time we saw the lights, so we slowed down to investigate. The lights were in a triangle formation not moving at all. We went to the gas station and on the way back home we saw four more lights, we watched them hang in the sky for about ten miles worth of driving. At one point we asked the lights jokeingly to do something cool, and almost instantly one light got bright amd blipped out. After that the remaining lights fadded out but never moving. We witnessed them outside of findlay ohio facing due north. I hope other people have seen them. Keep looking up ~matt
