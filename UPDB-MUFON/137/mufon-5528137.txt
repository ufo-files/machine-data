Photos of UFO - did not witness

Working on a site taking photos of landscape because of Fall colors. Did not see the object until afterwards when viewing photos. Based on the series of photos, which were consecutively taken, the object appears in three frames. Knowing how the photos were taken, it appears the object descended in the first frame and then assumed a horizontal flight pattern. time between photos was in a matter of 15 seconds. Based on this time frame, the object would have to of been travelling much faster than conventional means, therefore would not be a plane or flying creature. Photos have been mosaiced together; first is the descending object; the second is the object in horizontal flight. Both photos are zoomed in. Second photo frame of reference determines the object is approximately 1/2 to 1-mile from my vantage. It definitely assumes a classic saucer shape in second photo.
