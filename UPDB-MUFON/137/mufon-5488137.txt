{cak} V shaped object with faint lights heading south from Freer TX

At 8:45pm after returnign from dinner I observed an object south west of Freer TX. The lights were faint and in a distinct v shape. At first I thought it was a falling star but is was moving a little slower. Then I noticed that it was not just one light, but rather a series in a distinct formation. The entire thing went from mid sky to the horizon south of freer in 3-5 seconds. No blinking lights and no sound.
