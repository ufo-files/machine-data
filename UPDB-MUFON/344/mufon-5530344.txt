Bright light that interchanged colors hovered for hours then disappeared.

Was inside the house, went outside as the sun was starting to set to have a cigarette. As I look up I see a red flash streak the sky. This was not a shooting star I said to myself. So as I look up, I then see (2) very bright objects in the sky to the left of where I saw the red streak.
