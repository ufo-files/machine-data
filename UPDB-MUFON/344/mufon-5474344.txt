{cak} Possible visitation/Abduction?

My encounter was not a sighting but an experience. I was about 8 years old at the time living with my mother. I had what at first felt like a strange dream. I was in a room on a table with aliens who were doing an experiment/or test. This is the weird part. When I awoke I felt I was supposed to check my belly button. When I did I found a single seed/dry plant that was like wheat; inside my belly button. I pulled it out and had the sense it really happened/wasn't a dream. The picture I attach is the closest plant I could find that was like the one I found, but is not the exact plant. Again, I was younger at the time. 

As time when on I just rationalized it as a dream, but that never explained the plant part. Maybe this is useful to you or maybe it's nothing. I just felt the need to share the crazy experience, and wondered if you have ever heard of something like this happening before. It was very strange. Thank you for your time.
