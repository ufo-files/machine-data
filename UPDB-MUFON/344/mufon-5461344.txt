{cak} Saw an orb that divided in to two and then three separate orbs and came back into one.

At 2:43 AM on April 17, 2019, I took my dog outside to potty. I was looking up at the stars and enjoying the serenity of the early morning hours. The moon was bright, and the sky was clear. 
A large, glowing, orb appeared in the sky. At first, I thought it was a meteor, but it divided into two and then into three orbs. They lost brightness as each orb broke off and when they came back together their collective brightness grew.
