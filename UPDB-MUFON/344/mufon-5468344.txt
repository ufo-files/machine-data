{rjl} Triangle ufo with three white lights and a pulsing red light.

While traveling northbound on I-89 on the interstate in Vermont. My girlfriend and I were remarking on the lights that at the time became more apparently a triangle formation. Because of the distance we had traveled the Ufo's actual area in the sky became very apparent as we continued closer and closer our angle to look at the lights got steeper and steeper. It was spotted at 5/24/18 at 11:45 pm. From what I saw it was very capable at flying at low heights and could change direction easily. It was over 10ft by 10ft. Possible had thermal imaging as it moved away as we started taking pictures, it made no sound, and had a range over 15 miles.
