{jpg) Lights

Traveling northbound on Mountain ave and observed 4 strobe like lights appear in the sky. They were each equidistant from one another and spanned the night sky. Probably a mile or so. They flashed together in unison about 3 times and as soon as they appeared out of nowhere, they disappeared. No change in direction.
