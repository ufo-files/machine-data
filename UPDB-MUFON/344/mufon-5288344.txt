Three Bright Objects Either On The Moon or Near Moon!

On 25 April, I was taking photos of the Full Moon when I noticed three bright objects in a perfect line. I've never seen these lights there before and they were just odd. The shape also reminded me of Orion's belt. Prior to 25 April, these lights were not there, and so far, haven't seen them since. Doing some research, found out that they are a Transient Lunar Phenomenon.
