{cak} a line of equally spaced high flying craft in a parade of sorts all going exactly the same speed in perfect line

We had a few people in our town witness a pink object over the coast and flying over ocean shores. The one witness took amazing pictures of it and clear as a bell. I was up at 430 ish in the morn laying in bed the night after the pink sighting . I looked out my skylight from bed to see what appeared to be the 'international space station' which we've seen a bunch . It passed by the skylight so I got out of bed and went out on my deck to see not one , but six lights apparently as far up as a satellite but very bright. They were flying in perfect distance from each other at the same speed apparently. Clear as a bell out and saw them very clearly. They had to be traveling extremely fast and in my view faster than the international space station moves. They flew from over the ocean from the west to the east over the WA olympic peninsula . They continued until out of sight. no blinking or flashing just very bright like the INSS reflecting the sun but 6 of them in line . an hour and a half later the resident who took pics of the pink ufo the night before posted new pictures she took of the pink ufo 6:22 this morning on facebook ! Hopefully she will report and post the pics she took of the pink one. It has seven pink lights and the outer body is pink glowing. Ill leave it up to her to report that .
