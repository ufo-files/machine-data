I was out with my dog before leaving for work and I looked up and saw an object hovering in the sky. to the right of the object the moon was out and bright. I thought maybe it was Venus, I looked at it and took several pics of it but with the digital camera I couldn't get a close enough shot. The object looks like a small cloud on what would be a cloudless morning. I snapped a picture of the moon also, I will send all that I have. I am sure the object was there longer then 5 minutes, but I had to leave for work. Also on Thursday, May 22nd, about 6:15 - 6:20 A.M.
