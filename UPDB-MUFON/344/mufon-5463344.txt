{cak} OBSERVED SPHERE LIKE OBJECT IN SKY WHILE CAMPING AT HUNTING CLUB IN RURAL GEORGIA. OBJECT PULSATED, CHANGED SHAPE AND COLOR , FLASHED AND MOVED LEFT AND RIGHT , UP AND DOWN .

On January 5th around 8:30 pm my 15 year old son and I were camping out at our deer hunting club in Taylor County Ga off of Highway US 80 . The area is mostly forest land between Butler Ga and Talbotton Ga and at least 10 to 12 miles away from each town with no light pollution and clear skies. 

While we were sitting in our truck charging our cell phones and surfing internet my son noticed a bright light in the sky . At first I thought it was maybe a planet or star but then it began moving from side to side , up and down and in circles . The object was also changing colors from white to a bluish color and then to sort of a reddish to gray and was pulsating , going dim to bright and changing shape from a sphere to a kind of fuzzy donut looking disk. 

We both began filming it with our cell phones and all the while talking with excitement as we watched this thing . We couldn't believe what we were seeing. At one point the object moved so quick it startled us both and was amazed how fast it moved. We watched this thing for about 30 minutes until 3 or 4 planes entered the area at once and it simply disappeared in a blink of an eye. We talked about what we had just witnessed till late in the night and ended up oversleeping the next morning to hunt . 

I don't know what it was we were looking at but i'm pretty sure we don't have the technology to build something that could do what this thing was doing , especially the speed and sudden stops.
