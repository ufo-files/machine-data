{rjl} Hovering craft approximately 4mies due west of Sky Harbor airport.

Went to watch the sunrise from the top of South Mountain. Shortly after arriving noticed an object hovering approximately 4 miles due west of sky harbor airport. Object was approximately 5-6 miles from my location. Thought it might be a plane from the airport but after watching it closely for a few minutes realized it was just hovering slowly back and fourth. Believe it was cylindrical due to it changing from being elongated to circular. Noticed as it turned the sun would occasionally flash of of each end. Noticed a jet leaving sky harbor west bound and shortly after a small plane ( Cessna) flying west bound also which I got in the video. Not sure what the object is. Thought it odd to see something just hovering and from the distance it must be quite large...
