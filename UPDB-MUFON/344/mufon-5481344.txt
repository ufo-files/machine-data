I was outside the house on the third (top) floor looking to the east and i saw in my periferal vision a large object approaching, it was already at an angle of 45 degrees when i spotted it and it took less than 5 seconds to fly over the house . A large cross of white lights (like a constellation) flew over very fast without making any sound. It was roughly 8 to 10 times the width of the moon and appeared to be far away because the lights were very dim like a constellation or a sattelite but it moved way to fast to be a sattelite. It came in from the north and i watched it as it flew over my house, i ran through the house to have another look at it which took maybe 4 seconds but it was gone.
