{rjl} While trying to take a photo of a unusual helicopter a bright red object suddenly appears in the sky.

On the evening of August 24, 2019, was sitting on my front porch as usual. Around 7:55 P.M. I heard the sound of a helicopter. It sounded like it was flying very slowly. Soon a helicopter flies directly over my house from the West. The chopper was dull grey in color and had no markings that I could see. It moved slowly from the West. It moved toward the South descending behind the trees across the street. I quickly ran to get my camera to get a photo but it vanished behind the trees. I later heard a helicopter to the South. I did also see one but it was too far away for a shot.

Around 8:22 P.M. I heard a helicopter flying over the Southfield freeway. I got up to see if I could get a look and a photo. It was flying from the South to the North. I took a photo of it. While I was taking the shot, a bright red object appears in the Northern sky just to the East of the chopper. It was red and bright. It kinda "jumped" into view and hovered there for about 2-3 seconds. Then I didn't see it. I was trying see if it was another airplane or chopper. Around 8:25 P.M. it reappeared. It moved quickly from the West to the East a few feet and stopped dead. I thought by that time this is not an aircraft. It hovered again for about 3 seconds and then it vanished. I didn't observe again.

I was able to take three photos of the object. It was so far away that the movement of the camera blurred the images a bit. I am attaching them.

I kinda felt that the object may have been "checking me out."
