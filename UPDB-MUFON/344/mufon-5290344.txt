New Years Eve sighting. Glowing orange light viewable in the Eastern Sky at around 11:40pm and again at 12:00.

Witnessed an Orange glowing light(star like) in the Eastern sky above Beavertown, PA at approx. 11:40pm. The light was moving slowly West as confirmed by movement next to stars and power lines. The direction was against the wind as confirmed by nearby chimney smoke, visible clouds that moved East. The night sky was brightly lit by a 97% waning gibbous moon. Very clear sky. The light appeared much larger and brighter than any star in the sky by about 3-5x. The light then stopped got very dim then brighter again and then disappeared out of view. Witnessed by 4 people. 

20 minutes later at 12:00 ball dropped for the new year. I went outside to see if nearby fireworks were visible for the new years celebration. Again in the Eastern part of the night sky was the orange light pulsating about 5 times and disappeared. Witnessed by one other person.

Zoomed in photo, original photo, short video clip all from first sighting event. Supplied by the other witnesses to the sighting.
