orange disk above trees at night

driving home one night approx 9-11pm probably 1994 or 1995. My two sleepy children in the back seat. We are heading S or SE on Minnesota Bluffs Drive, passing Quail Ridge Playlot on my right. Above the trees, I see a disk shaped orange thing, emitting light from it's perimeter . It hovers for a few seconds, then takes off at a speed and angle unlike any plane, helicopter, or anything I'd ever seen , what seemed like into the stars.
