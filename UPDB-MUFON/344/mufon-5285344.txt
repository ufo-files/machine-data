Bright Orange flaming Orb moves across sky.

At approximately 8:04 pm, PST, I just happen to look up and saw a bright orange flaming orb moving from the southwest to the southeast over the golf course practice range. I appeared to be tens of thousands of feet above the ground like a lear jet, based on its speed and size. it resembled a matching being lit, but it was bright orange instead of blue. I seemed to be spewing 360 degrees of bright orange as it moved. Compared to most high flying jets, it was much larger. I watched it through some tree limbs and repositioned myself past the trees to get an unobstructed view, while recording it on my IPhone 6S. As it approached Jupiter and the moon, it slowed to a stop just above and east of the moon. It stayed stationary for about 30 secs and the light got dimmer and dimmer until it vanished. I didn't know if it was moving away from me or simply stopped. The light was twice the size of Jiupiter and other people at the location saw it. I heard comments from those attending a rooftop function comment on it. I know I am not the only one to have seen this. There were no news stories of the event. i didn't believe it was space debris entering the atmosphere because it came to a controlled stop. I recorded about 2 mins of video on my iPhone 6S, which did not capture the bright orange glow of the object and the auto focus feature was not optimal.
