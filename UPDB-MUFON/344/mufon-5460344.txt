I was outside looking at the stars a little after 4 a.m. when I noticed a "star" descending at what appeared to be an extremely fast speed in the eastern sky before stopping abruptly. Two other objects, similar in appearance, appeared and began "flying" in large circles within one another's arcs. The first object then shot up at a 20 degree angle very fast before stopping abruptly. The other two came to a stop, forming a straight horizontal line with the first object. The first object and the one on the far right then "merged" with the one in the middle. The now single object shot up in the sky so fast it was impossible to see after the first millisecond. We have an old Navy/NSA base -- Sugar Grove -- not far from here, in eastern West Virginia, and years ago I witnessed what I assumed were military aircraft on maneuvers in the area, traveling in unusual (compared to a commercial jet) flight paths at tremendous speeds, but this was nothing like that. It would be like comparing a slug to an F-15.
