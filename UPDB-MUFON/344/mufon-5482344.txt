{cak} 4 - 6 unfamiliar objects in the sky with unusual flight patterns

While standing out side for a smoke, I observed, at first 2 bright orange objects in the sky heading south to north. They abruptly changed direction towards the west. Less that 1 min later, 4 more objects appeared and seemed to be scattered. There did not appear to be a flight pattern. I often see helicopters, as I live a block from the hospital. I am familiar with their flight patterns and directions. I have also seen countless planes, as I live on the coast. I have never seen anything like this, as the flight patterns where not typical.
