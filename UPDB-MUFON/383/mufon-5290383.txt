Silver “Pizza Hut roof” shaped disc, wipe-like disappearance, followed by shifting red-orange beam shapes

I was a preteen or tween around the time, in the car with my family heading home to st Mary’s county from Bowie on I-495. It must’ve been in the first 30-45 minutes of the trip, late afternoon. Seated back, left side, looked out the left window and there it was hovering, perfectly still for a minute or two. I was completely speechless, and wanted to tell my family to look but I feared it would be gone as soon as I said something and get in trouble for waking my sleeping sister and stepmom over nothing. I tried to capture footage on the only device on hand, my 3DS, but the object would not appear on the screen. Then the object appeared to be wiped away left to right, dissolving into the blue sky. Afterwards I was glued to the sky, and then appeared seemingly out of no where, two or three of a very strange object. They were like two red orange beams hinged on a point, and seemed to rotate around this point creating different angles, L, and V shapes. They shifted and crossed eachother for minutes and soon disappeared midair just as they had appeared.
