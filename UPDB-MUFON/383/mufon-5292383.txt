A very strange shaped object, turning and twisting,it moved straight across our eyeliner, across the sky, twisting as it went.

Myself, my partner and mother (she doesn’t want to be named) were sitting in her garden celebrating her birthday, it was late afternoon, early evening. The weather was humid and over cast. 
My partner suddenly said “what’s that?” we glanced to where he was pointing, way in the distance we saw a black object moving across the sky towards us.. as it got closer we could see that it was twisting but we couldn’t accurately make out the shape. We continued to watch it move across the sky closer to where we were sitting. We started to speculate that it was maybe a balloon.. or a light air craft, which it certainly wasn’t, no wings, no engine, no sound. 
It continued moving across the sky twisting but appearing to stay at the same altitude at a steady pace. As it twisted the sun shone onto one side and it reflected, looking a silvery colour. 
I started to film it on my iPhone travel across the sky whilst we got some binoculars. By the time we got the binoculars the object was very far away and I couldn’t make out any details through them. 
The object continued on, not dropping not rising on a trajectory until it went out of site. 
We all had a mixture of thoughts, a balloon a light air craft.. but it looked very very strange, I immediately thought it’s a UFO. 
I’d be interested to know what others think.. is it a balloon..?
