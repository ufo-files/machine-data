three orbs flying south to north, then turning north/west and fading out.

I was walking from my car up my walk to my front door when I noticed 3 bright orange/red lights flying south to north(which is against the normal flow of air traffic). the lights were in a straight line and seemed to turn towards the west a bit over the ocean and as they were turning the first one faded and disappeared, then after a few more seconds the last one faded and then the last continued on N/W until it faded from view as well. I called my mom out as it was happening, but she caught the end of it and wasnt very impressed.
