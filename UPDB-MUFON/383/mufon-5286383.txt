Lights Hovering over West London

I finished work and around 6pm, on my way to my car, I observed which in first instance looked like a Lazer show. This is in very close proximity to Heathrow airport.

I observed these lights and I was surprised, the speed was astounding, to confirm this, I took several videos. This was happening for almost 2 hours while I was there. I asked my brother to witness it as well. Someone told me this is a lazer light. But lazer light comes with a trail from point A to B. There was not trail just some random light traveling crazily. In one of my videos there was even a Plan clearly flying next to it, proving these lights wasn't a plane.
