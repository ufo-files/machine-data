{rjl} Two Glowing Lights

When driving to Troutlake, WA while driving my boyfriend and I saw two huge glowing red lights. Cars were already pulled over on the side of the road observing the phenomena. My boyfriend said he could see the outline of a structure for each objects. It was the brightest most vibrant glowing red lights I have ever seen. They hovered in the distance on a ridge side by side. Suddenly, the object on the right took off and the second followed. They did a game of "cat and mouse" then suddenly they both took off at break-neck speeds. Mind you this location was close to ECETI ranch in WA which is a well known observatory for regular UFO activity and is considered a "UFO Hot Spot". The objects were too far away to capture on my phone.
