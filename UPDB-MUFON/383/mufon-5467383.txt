{jpg} White object hovering at 30-40 degrees to the north of Ft. Collins. Multiple witnesses.

My wife and I were on a walk at 8:15-8:45pm on Wednesday, July 11th 2018 when my wife noticed a white object hovering at around 30-40 degrees above the horizon to the North of Ft. Collins, CO. The object was round in shape and clearly not a plane or a star. The sun was just setting but the object was brighter than any star/planet that is visible at that time of day. It didn't appear to give off any light but was stunningly white and moved very slowly, if all during the time of our observation, which lasted approximately 15 minutes. 

The object was also observed by family members who live 9 miles to the north of us. It was much higher from their perspective and was observed moving slowly to the west.
