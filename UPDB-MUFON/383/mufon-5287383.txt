V Shaped, black, silent moving aircraft with 5 round dim lights

While walking my dogs at night, I saw what looked like 5 dimly lit ducks/geese flying my direction. Once flying directly over me, I realized that it was a 40-50 foot v-shaped aircraft with 5 light brown/yellow and evenly spaced lights on the bottom. It was blacker than the night sky, moved without sound and at about 15 miles per hour, above the treetops. The total visible distance (path) was approximately 100 yards before it was out of my sight.
