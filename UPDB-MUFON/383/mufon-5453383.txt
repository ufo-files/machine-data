Formation of 6-10 starlike lights in formation.

Roasting marshmallows over fire pit. Happened to glance up: up ahead looking North-northeast over Army post, saw 6-10 starlike lights flying in a strait line formation that seemed to disappear as they decended towards horizon line. Thought at first they were sattelites or noiseless aircraft. About 30 mins later looked back up to see a star brighter than Venus that then flared up like a flashlight bulb burning out.. then was gone.
