{jpg} Large glowing white orb moving over lake East to West

Watched large white glowing ball move east to west over lake. News reported on it the following day. Others saw it and fire department looked for boat in distress due to other calls.

It was about 11pm when I looked out to the lake from my camp and it caught my attention.

Walked out to the deck and watched it slowly glide close to surface of water until I lost sight of it.
