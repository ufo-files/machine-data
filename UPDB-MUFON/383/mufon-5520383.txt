Green ball of light

I was heading down Huntingdon ST just after I turned off Aramingo AVE at about 132am and the area of sky above the street ahead of me a Huge, green ball of light was going straight then it went up and then down and angled to the left, but it was so fast and it disappeared. I stopped to see if anyone else seen anything, but it was late and no one was around and as I drove around I tried to find it again, but nothing.
