I am 33 now and when I had the obduction with the entity I was about 10 or 11. I remember I think it was about 12:00am or 1:00am I can't be sure. It started to get a little humid and there was an entity over me looking strait at me. I remember it being very tall and its eyes where pitch black and not shaped like tear drops like a lot of people describe but more human like but bigger. All I remember was I was Scared. I don't remember how long it looked at me or anything or if it took me. My mind is blocked and I don't remember. I remember that I was so scared that I started to cry and it left very quietly and quickly and I was not sure how it got into my mothers home.
