{jpg} Observed a VERY VERY bright 'star', much brighter than ANYTHING in the sky. Then it started moving.

Walking home from work I get to a turn in the road on Third st, near the church, above the foundation trail, and we(me my brother and his girlfriend) see a very very bright light, brighter than ANYTHING in the sky. It started moving, almost coming towards us. Then stopped almost completely turned around and went the other way. Slowly dimming until you couldnt see it. It was white and it was spherical. It was lower than normal planes fly, and too big to be a drone. And it didnt make a single sound. About 2 minutes later... We see the SAME object, again directly in front of us, however heading about SW at this point. It stopped moving and just hovered above us, at least 500ft above us, for about 30 seconds. Then headed SW again, this time much more quickly, and sometimes faster, in spurts. As it moved the light started to dim as if it was moving out of our atmosphere. This object did not move like a plane. It did a complete 180 in a matter of seconds..... And it was dead quiet. It also never moved in a straight line. It was like it didnt know where it was going. As it was flying away the second time, a plane was flying towards it. (Flashing red and white lights, and obvious plane roar.)
