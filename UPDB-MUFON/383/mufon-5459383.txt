Lights going multiple angles at night.

At the Cabin on a sunday night in Lake of the Woods, Canada. Saw a light hovering above water doing different angles and directions not known from any current aircraft. There are no planes or helicopters ever in this area & it was past midnight. Night as clear as ever. 

Closer it got to the cabin, our lights started flickering rapidly. We face the water looking directly south. I saw a shadow of the floating object and it looks narrow horizontally with one light in the middle glowing. As the lights flickered (they never do), i got major chills and went to get a water from the mini fridge 2 feet away and i look back and it was completely gone. It lasted about 20 minutes. Not a plane. No helicopter & who would fly a drone at that time of night on a monday morning? 

There was a famous UFO sighting at Falcon Lake decades ago which is 15-20 mins down the road & the lake next to that (West Hawk Lake) is know to me a meteorite made lake. So it makes you wonder what lures them to this area. This is my first sighting.
