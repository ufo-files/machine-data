Black cigar shaped objects passing across the surface of moon and between earth and moon.

9/14/2021 8:20 p.m.-9:10 p.m. five separate black cigar shaped objects traveling from north to south across the face of the moon. I observed them for close to 45 minutes and in the time period a minimum of five different objects passed with a constant rate of acceleration across the face of the moon. The objects varied in size and I was also monitoring the sky looking for satellite activity, which none were observed in this airspace throughout the observation time. 
9/17/2021 7:30 p.m. -8:32 p.m. four separate black cigar shaped objects were observed traveling in multiple cardinal directions, north to south, east to west. Constant velocity and moved out of the area quickly.
8:42 p.m. observed a bright flat from an object passing overhead and when I searched the sky for the object which was moving it totally disappeared. 
9/17/2021 7:15 p.m. observed a bright orb traveled across the the horizon from south east to north east traveling in a straight line with a constant velocity. It was observed through a set of binoculars and it had a brilliant white to yellow brightness.
