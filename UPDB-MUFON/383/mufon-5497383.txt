{cak} Large Dull then Shiny Object Above Clouds

Back when I was living in Springfield Massachusetts, four of us were in a Jeep traveling East on the 20 just crossing the bridge over the Connecticut River. The Jeep had a soft top with the back door or hatch missing. Myself and one other were sitting face-to-face sideways in the back. I was behind the driver. The day was cloudy with only several small places to see the blue sky. I loved the outdoors so I was always checking the sky for the weather and our chance to play golf, baseball, or something. The clouds were maybe 400 feet higher than us. The opening could be considered nearly round and about 50 - 75 feet at it's largest diameter. As we were crossing the bridge I was looking up through one of the openings in the clouds. Through the hole I saw part of an object. It appeared to be on the other side of the cloud. At first I thought it might be the front or rear of a deridgable. It was grey in color, dull and smooth. I called attention to the other dude in the back and he looked at it and just stared back at me in bewilderment.1I started shouting to the driver to stop the car and pull over. As soon as we got onto the on-ramp for the next freeway we were able to pull over. We all got out of the car and stared at it. By now the clouds had exposed it a little bit more, but nothing could be made out any more than a dull oblong shape. Then, the object appeared to be struck by the sun and it glistened like we were looking at the end and bottom of a very shiny, metallic object. There were no seams, windows, wings or anything. A few minutes later the clouds covered it completely.
