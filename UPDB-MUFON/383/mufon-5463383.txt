{cak} Sitting by lake. Attention drawn up. Noted silent dark Triangular craft

Afraid they would think I was seeing things. I was about 16 yo and did not even tell my Mother until we got back home and saved newspaper for time we were gone showed a drawing of craft seen in Hot Springs Arkansas while we were there. Unknown if had real missing time, but the next memory was after we arrived back home in Baytown Texas. This was in the Summer of 1965 possibly 1966. Drawing was in either the Houston Chronicle or Houston Post that existed at that time. I have in 20-30 years after suddenly noticedtriangular shaped missing skin and flesh on arms that were bloodless and painless with no knowledge of how they happened. I am unique in the fact that possibility not probability of being abducted is not scary. Since my Tubal Ligation in 1978 I have had very vivid dreams of having babies - one a girl.
