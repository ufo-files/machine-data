{jpg} Flying Orb in the Pacific Ocean --Mexico

I was on vacation on the Crown Princess in April of 2015 taking a cruise along the Mexican Riviera. On our way back to the Port of Los Angeles I decided to take a video with audio on my cell phone to show family...I was on the deck (as I was frequently) Turned my camera on to video and began my video, we were in the middle of the ocean with NO land in sight. With my eye I saw nothing while I was videoing...it wasn't until I reviewed my video did I see this bright Orb like shape fly out of the ocean. It shot out of the water and ascended then just disappeared. Again we were in the middle of the Pacific Ocean with nothing around us but Water ! I did not see this while videoing. The video I am submitting is the original...nothing has been tampered with...I am STILL very amazed that while shooting this I saw nothing but the wake and the water...Any help would be greatly appreciated and letting me know what I saw on my video !
