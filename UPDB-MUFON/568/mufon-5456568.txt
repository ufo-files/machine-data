{cak} Like a round silver mirror.

As I was driving South toward home, I caught a reflection in the sky. It was a brilliant reflection that suddenly vanished. Other than turning at the angle that caused the reflection, there was no further movement. It was only a split-second moment for me, so I have no further details about it. I can speculate and rule out many things. The reflection was more like a flash. Has there ever been an instance of an icy cloud, shaped like a coin, that would reflect sunlight for a second then immediately melt? As in, flash melt?
