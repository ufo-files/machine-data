{rjl} Round, white, pulsating, hovering object.

Around April of 2012 between 6-7 in the evening, I was in the back yard and happen to look up in the sky when something caught my eye. It looked like it was pulsating and very strange to me as I have over 17 years of aviation experinse. I was very unsure of what the object was, so I quickly ran inside and grabed my camcorder and began recording the object. It seamed to just hover for a while and than began moving from a north west direction to a northern direction. I remember thinking it was very high in the sky because it was well above the hight of some birds that were flying in the sky as can be seen in the video. Please note that I did not have a tripod and it was very difficult for me to keep track of the object. I tried to zoom in as best as I could with out distorting the object. After several attempts to zoom and focus on the object, I lost sight of it as it seamed to fade away into the sky and I zoomed away. I remember feeling over whelmed and surprised at what I had just witnessed and seen.
