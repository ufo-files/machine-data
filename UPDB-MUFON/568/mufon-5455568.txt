{tjd} Large craft over interste 35 between Edmond AND oKC

My mom, brother and I were headed to OKC to see my dad at work. While driving down the interstate we saw a bright light from a large object on the west side of the freeway. It travels slowly a cross the highway with no sound. We could see the underside of the craft which had many features underneath and bright lights on the bottom. The object reminded us of the mellinium falcon from Star Wars in the way the hull looked. It moved slowly until it got ai mile or two on the west side of highway, then took off at incredible speed to the southeast.
