diamond/pentagon shaped large object hovering at low altitude.

At around 2.30am i was in my bedroom on my laptop. i noticed a bright orange light getting more intense outside my window. i glanced over and seen not one light but five, in a pentagon-type shape. the light at the top was the most intense of the lights and burned a brighter orange colour than the rest. at first i thought it might be the moon or stars but when i went to my window to investigate i noticed they were all attached to an extremely large craft , maybe quarter to a half mile wide which was hovering just above the clouds, and seemed to be behind the house across the road from me. i rushed downstairs where my mother and brother were still awake and told them . as we all looked out the lights began to fade until they were no longer burning but the outline of the craft and the lights could still be seen. i was extremely agitated and scared as i knew this was not something man-made. as we watched it fade the outlines of the lights began to drift in opposite directions to each other until they were no longer visible. at time of sighting 3 dogs in the house started barking which is unusual for them.
