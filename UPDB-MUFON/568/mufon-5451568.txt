UFO hovering, flashing light of different color, disappears leaving a trail

it was a hot summer day and I was playing outside with my best friend. We both were 9 years old. While we were doing stupid stuff and playing on the field (potato fields with lots of greenhouses) both of us eventually looked up into the sky (don't know why we did that, maybe there was some kind of sound or it was plain instinct) and saw a disc shaped object. Well,I guess it was disc shaped, we only saw the bottom of it but it was atleast round bottom. It was hovering in the sky not moving for about 1 minute. If I have to guess I'd say it was about 1-2 miles above us. Lights began to flash in a circular manner. Blue,Green,Yellow-Orange-reddish faster and faster ending with a white flash. I only registered a small twitch in a direction and it was gone leaving only a small but visible trail in the direction it has twitched before. I was baffled and looked at my friend asking him if he just experienced the same as I did. Yes, we both saw the exactly same thing happening
