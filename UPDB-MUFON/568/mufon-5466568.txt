{cak} White cross shaped object appeared clear sunny sky in Charleston WV near Capitol buillding

On March 16th 2017, I was standing on Kanawha Boulevard, Charleston WV at the Capitol building, on the "river"side of the street.
I looked up and saw a white cross-shaped object high overhead. I thought it was a high altitude jet, until i realized it did not have the silhouette of a jet, and it was stationary.

As i watched, it rotated in a clockwise direction, then turned back. It darted to the northwest, stopped and stayed there for awhile. then, it darted back to the southwest and stayed there.
it darted to its original position and hovered there for a while, and then seemed to "zoom" up up up and disappeared. I can't say that it didn't just "wink out" it was moving so fast.

There were no other objects in the sky, no con-trails or other smoke, emissions.
