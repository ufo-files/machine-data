{jpg} Stationary object

I live in Colorado Springs, home of many military bases. I see all kinds of aircraft. It might be our military, but what I saw was not an aircraft like any other. It was shaped like a cylinder or oblong box hovering in one place for hours. It seemed to have jets of air or fuel keeping it stationary. Couldn't tell how high or size because of distance. Just hovered, I went inside, came out later and it was gone. I have also seen many aircraft here, but they are probably ours. It's a strange place here, so many secrets and rumors, hard to find the truth. I don't really want to get serious here but it was weird enough I thought you might want to know. P.S. Native Americans know we are being visited, and have been for a long time before white people came here. Where did the Anasazi go? The Mayans, Chaco canyon?
