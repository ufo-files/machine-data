Golden Object above Berlin

I saw with a friend in a very high altitude by trying to make some photos from the television tower of Berlin a golden object. It moved slowly to the right and then to the left, but most of the time it seemed to be stationary. I tried to zoom this object with my camera and could make some photos of it. It seemed to be very small. My friend saw it too with the help of the cameras zoom.
