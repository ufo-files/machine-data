Triangle shaped craft

In June of 2020, after dinner, my sister and I had walked out to my back patio on a clear night. Being in a neighborhood you still have a good view of the stars at night. This was appx 2330. As we are stand looking at the sky, the first thing that caught my attention was a single light moving from south to north. First thought was, that plane is very low and slow! Doing a double take I realized there are no typical lights as planes have. Looking behind the light I began to see that the stars we were looking at were disappearing. Then the back corners lights came into view. You could see the triangle shape by the 3 white lights. It slowly moved from south to north almost directly overhead. I quickly looked at my sister and back up. I asked her if she is seeing that, she replied, “yes”! And almost as quick as she answered, it was gone in a linear motion towards the north, as we stood looking at each other....speechless.
