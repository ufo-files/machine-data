3 UAP lights seen in Aurora Borealis outside Whitehorse, Yukon Canada.

Driving to Alaska from Kirkland, WA. 98034 (near Seattle) in route to Anchorage, Alaska. Outside Whitehorse, Yukon in Canada my wife, daughter and I started to see bright white lights as the Aurora Borealis started to be seen in the night sky. This was the first time we have seen the Aurora. It was so bight I could see in the dark. In the most active area of the "aurora" you could see 1 then 2 then 3 bright white lights moving around. I started taking photos with my cell phone. I took 4 shots and when you enlarge the image, they are triangle shape.
