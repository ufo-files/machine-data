I was driving home from picking up my wife up from work when I noticed some lights out of the corner of my eye. At first I thought it was just a reflection on the window as it was night time. Once I turned and saw the bright lights in the sky I stopped driving to look at them. I pointed at them and told my wife to look, she immediately said "what is that". The three lights were dimming and reappearing for about 30 seconds when the first one split into two lights and disappeared. The middle light dimmed and reappeared a few more times then just disappeared. The third light dimmed and reappeared a few more times before it split into three lights that all disappeared.
