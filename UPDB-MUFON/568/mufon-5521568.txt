Flickering flame partially surrounded by red film/glass/plastic

I was getting out of my vehicle and taking groceries into my home when I observed an object just below the cloud cover, that made no noise, covered about 10 miles in less than 2 minutes, and flew in a straight path. The objects appearance was a flickering bright white flame with a red lens surrounding the bottom of it. The flame would flicker at a slow steady pace (like observing a large fire from a distance) although the wind should have had a greater affect considering the speed at which it moved. There were no flashing or pulsating lights that would make me think it was an airplane, helicopter, weather balloon, or satellite. It flew from a Southwesterly direction to a Northeasterly direction, flying just under a small amount of cloud cover, and disappeared into the clouds just above it. Prior to New Years 2012, I had never seen anything in the sky that I could not explain away. So far, this year, I have seen 3 different objects on 3 different nights/days that I have no explanation as to what they were.
