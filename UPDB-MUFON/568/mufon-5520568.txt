Feb. 21 2012 is the 7th time I have observed this Brilliant light moving NW thru the sky and slowly descending and disappearing below treeline/mountain slope.

Feb. 21 2012 is the 7th time I have observed this Brilliant light moving NW thru the sky At 6:50 pm, I first saw the light slightly North and very low on the horizon a great distance beneath Polaris. As the light traveled in a NW direction, at approx. 8:45 it began to slowly descend and at 9:04 it disappeared below the treeline/NW slope of a nearby mountain.
