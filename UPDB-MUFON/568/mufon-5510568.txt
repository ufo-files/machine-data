While traveling west on rt 34 in Derby Ct 6-1-2013 appox 22:20 pm I saw two headlight objects hovering in the air. I was comming home for the evening driving my car. I first noticed this object because of how bright it was and the fact that it was just hovering in the same spot in the sky. I certainly did not know what it was which was why I just kept looking. The object looked like two basketballs in the sky brightly lit with a cloud of light around it, it was hovering. I had no immediate feeling just wondering what the heck it is/was. I lost sight of the object after pulling into driveway when I got out of the car and looked for it it was gone.
