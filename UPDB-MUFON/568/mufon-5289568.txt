UFO sighting directly above my house

On the evening of February 22, 2021 at7:29 pm as I looked into the sky on a crystal clear night directly straight above my head I saw two lights appeared at first to look like two stars but I noticed both of them were moving. I continued to watch and caught some video. 

In addition I observed other white objects in the same area traveling straight and level at a high attitude to include one that appeared to have a orange glow to it.

Note: media video #3 the large white object in the video is the moon as I’m describing the orange glow object passing to the moons right side.
