semi-white on bottom and chrome reflection on top, approx. size would be that of a volkswagon

I was on the phone with an attorney and looking out the bedroom window of my second story apartment. I noticed an object that I first thought was a very large bird moving south to north approx. 1/4 mile away and approx. 1500 feet in the air, as it got closer I realized that it was not a bird nor a plane. The object moved in a straight line across the sky, it started out slow moving and then fast and back to slow. The object did not spin, rotate or change altitude while it moved. I lost visibilty when it flew into a cloud formation approx. 2 miles away. I was in shock at what I was seeing and couldn't believe it was so visible in the daytime sky, I made several explictives to the attorney I was on the phone with and ran to get my wife up from bed in hopes of seeing it again with an witness; the object never returned. The object was moving in the direction of the international airport in kansas City Missouri, it was low enough to have enough visibilty to see that it was not any kind of commerical or military aircraft.
