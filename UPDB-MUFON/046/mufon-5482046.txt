I was coming from the grocery store. A large storm had just passed over. I took my phone out and started to take pictures. I noticed a cloud that had straight lines. I thought that was strange. I took a picture of it and followed it as far as I could. It started to get dark so I went on home. A couple of days later I looked at the picture again and enhanced it. I see in the right of the pictures object over a larger object that looks like a long line of windows with light emitting from them. It looks like it's two objects to me. I was alone in the car so I couldn't take as many photos as I would have liked to. Plus my daughter was sick at home and I had to get back to her. There was no after affects from this but wondering was it two or one large ship.
