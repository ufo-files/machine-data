{nd} video taping a military heli to check cell capabilities, seen the object in playback.

I wanted to check the video capabilities of my Krazor cell phone. I was sitting in my garage when I heard the military Boeing CH-47 Chinook helicopter coming. I figured it would be a good time to test the video with the zoom. I taped the heli for a short duration & when I played it back I saw the object.
I went in the house & downloaded the video to my computer so I could slow & pause the capture.
The object appears out of nowhere goes in a straight line under the heli, then slows in a U-turn around the front of the copter, then does an unbelievable acceleration behind it & disappears. The weird part is before the object appears, there is a distortion in the video before it appears. I have seen weird things in my life, but this is the first I captured it on video. I was in shock of how this object moved & changed course with rapid acceleration.
