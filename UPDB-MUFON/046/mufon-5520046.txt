luminous orange sphere with white pulsating center floating above trees then moving across road and slowing moving up into sky and disappearing

Driving through Gilead, Maine on 3/17/12 at 7:45 pm, I observed a small luminous orange sphere with whitish pulsating center, floating just above trees on side of road, then it crossed the road and started slowly upward into sky and became dimmer and dimmer until light disappeared. It did not make any sound. Appeared to emanate its own light. I observed one just this back in late 1980s in Harpswell Maine along the edge of the water. Same color and behavior, this one cruised just over treetops, staying same altitude for quite a while as it followed the coastline towards Brunswick.
