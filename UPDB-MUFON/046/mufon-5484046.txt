{cak} Thought it was a bright star began to move thought it was a plane but didn't appear to be.

I'm a Canadian truck driver. Stopped at the Sandstone rest area in Michigan. Got out of my truck and looked up to see if the sky was clear. I noticed what i thought to be Jupiter and beside it was a bright star. That star began to move so I thought it was an airplane. The object only moved a few degrees to the north slowed then there was a bright flash and was transformed into a much smaller greenish object that moved very fast upwards getting smaller and smaller till it disappeared. there were no other objects visible. When I came out of the eest area building I looked back up into the sky to see if anything else was going on the only thing were stars and what I'm assuming was Jupiter. Then I bunked down for the night.
