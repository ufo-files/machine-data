Light in the sky

We were out spot lighting on the lake. We seen a light on side of the lake, while we were on the other side. The light looked to be like a big spot light from a helicopter. But it looked like it came from the clouds. Then lower it self down to the tree line. Once it hit the tree line it turned that whole side of the lake bright as day? It then started to move straight up to the clouds. Then was gone? The strange part to this was that we were not the only ones to see it that night. There were two other boats out on the lake that night that seen it? We were able to record a light video clip and take picture. It was the strangest thing I had seen out on the lake.
