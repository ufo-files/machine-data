We were just sitting there looking up at the moon when all of a sudden this light just appeared out of nowhere in the sky below the moon, to the East. It was about 11:23PM. My first instinct was that it was a shooting star and was about to say, 1woah a shooting star!1 but it proceeded to continue moving across the sky so I had said nothing. It was oval in shape, about 5 times bigger than the brightest star in the sky. It was a faint orangish color and moved with an incredible speed East to South direction. It appeared to move in a straight line for about 10 seconds and then just disappeared. There were no clouds in the sky it could have disappeared behind nor did it just disappear out of distance, it just disappeared. It is hard to explain what it looked like. It didn1t appear solid, it didn1t have blinking lights like a plane, it was fuzzy orange and seemed to wobble. It did not leave a trail behind it. It was almost as if the orange color from the moon reflected onto this object. We had seen airplanes in the sky before and after the incident, and this thing moved with incredible speed, way faster than an airplane. Before I had said anything my boyfriend was like 1what the f*** is that?!1 1Did you just see that thing zoom across the sky!!
