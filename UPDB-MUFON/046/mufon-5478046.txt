{cak} there were 3 UFO's moving in directions our planes can maneuver, then one went into the larger one.

It began around 10:30 pm on 11/03/2003, I went to empty the trash and something made me look up. And as I did, I saw 3 UFO's hovering over each other and it seemed like they were playing. The larger UFO stood still for a while and the 2 smaller ones maneuvered around, in, out, backwards, forwards, up & down in patterns that our planes can't mechanically complete. I couldn't believe what I was seeing, so, I ran in the house and got my exchange student, both of my daughters, and my camcorder and we all rushed outside to take another look. One of the smaller ones disappeared into the larger UFO and to this day it's an amazing memory! (I would say that the UFO's were out in our view for about an hour because I have 2 short tapes, one around 10:30 pm and another around 11:46 pm.)

I turned on my camcorder 2x's, and I have a few minutes of video of the objects, and still have them in my possession to this day. I have taped over some of it, but there's still a few minutes of tape. My tape is in Hi-8 and I've been reluctant to send it anywhere in fear of loss or damage. I know there were many more that saw what we did that night, so, I'm glad I have video of it and will gladly let anyone copy it.
Thank you for spending the time to read our experience.
