{jpg} Thought it was birds at first, no way was it birds, the objects were in a formation that suddenly changed direction and form but uniformly.

I was on the football field at Shawnee Mission Norths stadium with about 17 other kids hanging out. We were playing a band game called "lap tag" nothing strange observed. I hear someone say, "Look satellites" after looking at them and everyone else no longer caring about it, I thought it was just birds, but how unusual they were flying, because no wings were flapping at all. The formation was sort of how you would see birds in a form, however after heading south closer to the football fields direction, the formation abruptly formed to the east and headed off until I couldn't see a trace of it. No noise was heard and no light emission from where I was located. I tried to find it after losing track of it but it had been completely gone. I was in shock to say the least, I asked my friend D if he saw it and he didn't seem too phased but agreed that he had seen it.
