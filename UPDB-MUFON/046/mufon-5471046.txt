I saw a green-blue star-like flash that darted N-NE then faded within seconds of its ascent!

At home, unable to sleep, skywatching out the bedroom window. Unable to miss, bright blue-green light about one mile away. Immediately thought it was a shooting star until I realised it ascended out of the atmosphere, opposed to descended towards earth. Caused a feeling of anxiety. The round-shaped object was floating for around 5-10 seconds, before darting from north to north-east (leaving a light-coloured trail) before ascending out of our atmosphere!
