{dr}4-5 objects swirling around each other near what appears to be the edge of space

My Daughters and I were camping near Beale Air Force Base. I ASKED MY OLDEST DAUGHTER TO JOIN ME AWAY FROM THE FIRE LIGHT. Approx. 11:pm my daughter and I witnessed 4-5 star-like objects swirl around each other and go bright and dim. Random pattern, as if 5 distinct objects. Lights get bright and big, then get small, sometimes disappearing and showing back up within a second or two. We watched until my Daughter was "Creeped Out". She would not stand with me and watch because of the nature of the event. She and I still talk about it regularly. Not my first time, but hers...
