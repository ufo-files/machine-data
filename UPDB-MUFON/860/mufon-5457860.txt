Scary

Me, my sister and several friends were metal detecting around a new building site when my sister noticed a shooting star, I looked up and said straight away that it was no shooting star, the object broke into two pieces, one went one way and the other came towards us, suddenly the object stopped and we noticed it was a flying disk, a typical flying saucer. We ran away and called my mum and a neighbours mum who thought we were being silly until they saw the craft. We all stood in amazement and shock as the object, slowly spinning with a dull hum came towards us, stopped and went left of us towards the woods and went down below the tree line, it then came back up and came back to where it started, It hovered again turning slowly then rose up again and then flew off at an amazing speed and disappeared
