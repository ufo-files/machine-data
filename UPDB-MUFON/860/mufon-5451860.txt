Lightbulb like lights, very bright orange in perfect straight line over Marana

I came back from Casa Grande to my home by Tangerine and Twin Peaks in East Marana area and while on the driveway I noticed bright lights to the right of me. It was already dark and our neighborhood has no street lights, I was surprised by the long line of bright lightbulb like lights. When I realized that they are not very far and don't resemble any airplanes or helicopters I called my husband who was in the garage. He came out to the driveway but those lights "turned off" one by one in a very rhythmical way. Then they turned on in a different configuration and dissapeared very quickly. We all saw it including my daughter. It stopped for about 15min and then the show continued leaving us mesmerized. We saw it the next day but it was further away. Two days later I saw the same lights for less than 30seconds over the Dove Mountain area.
