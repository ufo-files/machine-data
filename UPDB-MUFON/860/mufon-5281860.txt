Saw a huge cigar-shaped object in the sky while driving along Neely Ferry Rd. in Simpsonville, SC

I was driving along Neely Ferry Rd. in Simpsonville, SC on Friday morning, 4/08/22, and noticed a massive cigar-shaped object in the sky to the south. It seemed to be hovering and perhaps slowly ascending. I would judge it's altitude to be about double what one sees when they are observing a plane cruising near an airport, for instance. The object's surface was a dull, whitish gray color with what appeared to be several bands or ridges at either end of the "cigar" shape. It must have been quite large because it was very high in the sky and still appeared to be about double the size of a commercial jet. I've never seen anything like this hovering in the sky and wondered if it was some kind of massive drone or perhaps a blimp. I tried to photograph it via my phone, but the glare of the clear, bright, sunny sky turned the image to a blur. Anyone else in the vicinity would have spotted the object clearly, as well, had they been looking up.
