on the evening of July 4th 2015 around 9 30pm as I was watching fireworks that a neighbor was putting on a show from across a canal my mother and I witnessed 2 circular objects in the sky watching the fireworks they hovered for about 3 minutes then slowly went off in the distance. We did not hear any motor sounds I ran for my I pad to take a picture then they were gone. I contacted the police dept to ask if any helicopters were in the area and they said no.
