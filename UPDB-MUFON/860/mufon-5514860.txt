(GS) Looked like it was reflecting sunlight - almost looked like a star

I was in the backyard smoking a cigarette. And happened to look up into the sky. At first I thought I was looking at an airplane that was just reflecting light, but then I noticed that it wasnt moving and hovering with the light cloud cover. I ran inside to get my camera and it was still there when I went back out. I don't know how long it had been there before I saw it but it was still there when I finished taking pictures and went back in. Shock and awe were my reactions because I had never seen anything quite like this before. But then I remembered a dream I had as a teenager about seeing stars in the sky during thee day...
