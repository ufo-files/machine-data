Orange balls flying southeast with drastic silent maneuver

Two orange balls flying southeast witnessed while pointing out the belt of orion. They were silent. They abruptly separated from their formation, then returned to formation to the exact distance away from each other and continued south east toward lower manhattan. Two witnesses at 1 AM. Clear skies. No sound at all from the craft.
