{cak} 1961 Albuquerque UFO photos, 5 photos taken by Capt. of the US Air Force

So earlier today, October 2nd 2014, my wife and i were going through some of her grandmother's old belongings, we found a 50 year old photo album full of photos from around the time my wife's mother was born, they were living in Albuquerque because her husband was a Captain in the US Air Force, so these photos are all from 1961 through 1965. So we started looking through this photo album, and all of a sudden we flipped the page and there are five beautiful photographs of my wife's grandfather in civilian clothing pointing up at the most immaculate day time disc i have ever seen. After the five UFO photos there are a few pages of airplanes, helicopters, hangers, and him sitting at his desk in the base he worked on, talking with several other officers. When I saw these photos, I was dumbfounded. Just because of the context of them within the whole photo album gives these much more credibility to me. We are not looking for any kind of publicity, i think you will see these are just some very special photos, and we want to share them with others so we can try to put a few more pieces together of this mystery.
