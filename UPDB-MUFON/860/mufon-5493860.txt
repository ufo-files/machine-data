{rjl} UFO Flying in Formation

Returning from a family vacation in the North Carolina mountains in 2001, I suddenly sighted a group of five objects, flying in a V formation, over a marshy area off of Interstate 95 South in South Carolina. It was on a late afternoon in July in full daylight. The objects were parallel with our vehicle when I first became aware of them and were progressing South. They appeared to be smooth oval shapes with a brushed chrome finish (metallic, but not shiny). There were no markings or protuberances of any kind visible on any of them. They were completely silent and moving at a relatively low altitude. They appeared to be in no hurry. They were, however, moving faster than our 65 mph. I continued to watch the craft until they finally disappeared over the horizon. I will never forget this encounter. It took me totally by surprise, and I was immediately aware that these were NOT "normal" vehicles. There was no evidence of any type of conventional propulsion. There was no evidence of propulsion at all. They were enormous, smooth, metallic eggs gliding through the sky. I felt incredibly fortunate that I had the privilege to witness this evidence that we are NOT alone.
