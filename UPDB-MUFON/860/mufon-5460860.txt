{cak} Large Round Object with estimated 8 strong light beams in front and one green light in the rear.

A friend who would rather remain anonymous and I where traveling east on the I10 at about 2AM. He drove as I sat in the passenger seat and his mother and father slept in the back seats. We were traveling at about 80MPH. At a distance we saw several headlights traveling South. At the moment we thought it was a group of vehicles traveling on another road. As we came closer to cross the headlights we realized the lights seem to be hovering above what we thought was a road. As we got even closer we noticed the object was traveling pretty slow. It made a maneuver that I thought would be impossible for an airplane to make at that slow speed. The object changed direction traveling north. After a couple seconds it hit a very high speed and disappeared in about a second. It took a while to process what we saw and then I asked my friend what he saw. He described the same thing I experienced.
