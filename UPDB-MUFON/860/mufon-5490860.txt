{jpg} Small, white geometric shaped (dodecahedrons) about twice the size of soccer balls flying above houses and trees.

I was driving down David Drive in Metairie, Louisiana and I saw to my right, above the houses and trees several (6 - 9) of these small, white, geometrically shaped UFOs (possibly dodecahedrons, about two or three times as big as a soccer ball) flying along at a seemingly casual, slow pace, possibly a pace slow enough to allow them to observe the land and sites below them. I was surprised to see that none of the other drivers around me seemed to notice the UFOs. The UFOs did not seem to be glowing or have lights, but they were seemingly silent and the material they were made of was reminiscent of the white material that things like the ship from Star Trek, Star Wars, and others, this white material that ships from those shows/movies are made of. I would note that these things did not seem scary to me and that these UFOs, in some weird way, seemed like they were a bunch of friends flying along to just check out the area. They were flying in a direction that put them on a path where they gradually got further and further away from me and I lost sight of them. Also, this was a very long time ago, but I hoped that maybe someone else saw similar UFOs either around that time (1992 or so) or since then and maybe had something to share about them.
