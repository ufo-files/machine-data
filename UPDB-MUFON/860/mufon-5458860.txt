{cak} Red flashing light in a sequence was mostly stationary, but at one point demonstrated a falling leaf pattern.

I stepped into the backyard with my dogs, and was star gazing due south on a very clear night when a stationary red object (at about a 45 degree angle) caught my eye. The 3/4 moon was to the left and a bit lower than the object. A bright planet (either Venus or Jupiter) was to the right of the object. The brightness of the nearby moon and planet created such a bad glare that it was very difficult to view the object with binoculars. The object appeared to be about the same size as the bright planet, and was blinking in a sequence. First it looked like a single solid, circular red light; then it looked like two red circular lights fused together; then after that, a smaller green light blinked. All the while, the object was stationary, so it was definitely not a plane (also no white lights were seen.) I've never seen a helicopter remain at a fixed point for as long as the object was still. At one point during the 20 minutes of observation, I saw a falling leaf motion, and it startled me a little bit. That was when I rushed into the house to get binoculars, which didn't help me see any additional details of the object. At one point, I had to go back into my house again; but when I went back outside, the object was gone. I heard no noise, have never seen anything like this before, and I have no idea how far away the object was.
