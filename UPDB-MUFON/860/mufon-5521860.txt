I saw a silvery type of disc in the sky. Very large. Very fast.

I was driving home from work with my sister. It takes us an hour to get home. I don't remember exactly where we were when I saw it. It was so fast that my sister didn't see it. It looked very large and it was like it came from behind us and directly above us and I saw it in my site above us for about 2 seconds. It is hard to describe but I was driving.
