Strange lights at sundown

About 8:30 PM, my partner and I are at the dog park with another couple watching our dogs play together. The sun was already set, completly clear sky, no stars or anything else in sight. A bright white flash of light caught my eye, it looked like a single search light or high beam. By the time I looked, it was already fading, as if it had been shut off. After the white light dimmed, 3 smaller, dimmer green lights in a triangle formation became visible. The craft completely disappeared into the atmosphere. There were absolutely no clouds in the sky, airplanes usually do not disappear like this thing did. Whole sighting lasted no more than 5-8 seconds, lady from other couple we were standing with saw it as well. Wow!
