{rjl} Stationary light that quickly moved and changed directions many times.

Walked outside to walk my dog late at night and saw a what looked like a star that was oddly bright red and stationary. This was unlike anything I have ever seen at that altitude (Seemed like it was a close star in the sky) but at least 20,000 - 30,000 ft in the skyline. Suddenly, the "star" turned green and continued to be stationary ( 4-5 seconds) before it began moving westerly quickly, stopped and became stationary again for 1-2 seconds, then turned red again then traveled east quickly, stopped and was stationary again for 10-15 seconds or more. Then became and stayed green for several seconds as it stayed stationary. It then reverted west again for a short time then immediately fell (or traveled) south down past the horizon out of sight. I have never gone out at night and witnessed in the high sky (where one would look at the stars) and see a definitive RED star (like the color of a red light at a traffic light) just sitting in the sky. As I looked and asked myself, "what is that!?", I realized it was very unusual. That is when it turned green and I became fixated on watching it as long as I could track it. I have lived here at this address for two years and have never seen anything like this in the sky or star gazing. It was a very unusual experience.
