Silvery object (larger than conventional aircraft), dissapears behind cloud.

At approximately 3:18 pm was leaving work. As i neared Parkway i glanced up and to my right and noticed a fast moving Bright object approximately 1-2 miles up. Object appeared to be Disc shaped and traveling at least twice as fast as a conventional jetliner. I initially thought that it was an airliner as i have seen them shine in the bright sunlight. However this object appeared to be approximately twice the size of a 747. Sun was bright and sky was deep blue. A few small thick white clouds were scattered about. The object traveled what looked to be a couple 100 yards and straight into one of these clouds. I watched for object to emerge so as to make a positive ID but the object did not emerge. I continued to observe this cloud for another 5-6 miles travel time with nothing emerging from it.
