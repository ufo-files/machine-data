Suspected Sattelites, but nine one after another

I would assume I saw satellites but there were nine of them. Looking up at Orion's belt I see a light (like an average star) fade in and travel south-west for 2-3 seconds before fading out. Less then a minute later I see another, exact same location and direction. And then there were three... They started getting dimmer and I counted nine. And then I went outside to also check out how bright Sirius was, no more satellites. Used Stellarium and Celestrak to try to identify the satellites, still a mystery to me. I guess it could have been a set of jets following each other??
