Hovering spherical shape with bright lights in a cross formation

Observed an object in the sky while on my phone, noticed the light's were not moving as in hovering, however there was NO sound, my friend and I watched and listened for an engine note there was none it was silent, the light's were not a normal plane formation, they were in a cross formation, it hovered for about 1and a half minutes then flew off at great speed and left NO contrail, it moved very fast, faster than normal planes that fly overhead, it then elevated higher very fast and then in a cloud?? It disappeared, it wasn't a normal cloud it was grey and covered the object and it just disappeared. My friend and I thought it was a plane initially, however when we noticed that it was hovering I thought it may be a helicopter however it was silent and the lights are not what you usually see on aircraft or helicopters, we just watched it hovering and then just blitzed off at a phenomenal speed, much faster than planes, also I thought if it was a helicopter it would tilt first before moving, this object didn't it just began moving fast, I was on the phone to my brother so I told him I would call him back, I got my camera up and took a couple of photos but it is a poor camera and couldn't get it in proper focus, my friend and I watched it ascend sharply and a cloud came over it and it disappeared in a instant.
