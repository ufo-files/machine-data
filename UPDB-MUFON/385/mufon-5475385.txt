The object under the moon is moving at a high speed to the right in a zigzag pattern.

We were sitting on the back balcony as we always did with my sister. I always liked watching the sky. I especially like to watch the moon. Then, suddenly, under the moon, the object, like a "~", sometimes went too deep and sometimes straight, with a sky speed more than the first I saw. I could not follow the object because the balcony ended there. The object is exactly as I pictured it. He could not make any noise, he was not doing anything else. I'm pretty sure it was not a plane like a plane in the sky at any minute in the sky. I also had a very strange dream when I slept 2 hours later. I had two white, unicorn, winged, very bright light horses in my dream, and these horses were approaching me kindly and trying to tell me something as if they wanted to touch them. I saw the same dream 3-4 times a few hours a night during the night. But I was awakening when I would touch them and tell me something. I hope it's not useful. Thank you.
