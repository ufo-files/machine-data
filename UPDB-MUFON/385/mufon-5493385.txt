It was almost dusk. I went to the backyard to bring the cat in, I was standing on the lawn, calling my cat,he just sat there staring up to the sky.I felt heat, not burning hot ,but heat blowing down on me, I looked up, and saw a large black object, hexagon shape,different color lights,just hovering there. I froze for don't know how long, but I remember telling it that I didn't want to go with them (?)why I said this I don't know, I regained myself grabbed the cat and headed for the back door,as I started moving so did the object, slow at first,I ran in the back door and yelled for my little brother to run outside and look up,( as the direction was towards the front of the house)I ran out front as fast as I could only to see it disappear on the horizon, my brother never saw it.I was very agitated and upset, my mother said she would call any bases in the area. I calmed down as she made calls,and was told there were no tests being done in the area and they had no other reports,I just sat on the couch and stared into the sky. Then around 10-11pm 2 cars pulled up in front of my house, 2 men knocked on the door and asked if they could talk to me. they had me draw what I saw and asked me what seemed like a million questions, told us not to worry, and basically forget about what I saw.Although I never forgot it , it took me many years before I told anyone else,afraid my friends would think I was crazy. I just recently told my other brother. Now that people believe, Im not afraid to tell my story. Im sorry I cannot remember the address of the house.I was about 12, it was around 1976
