{nd} Green Roman candle shape object in the night sky breaks into two objects

I was driving down Dodson Ave and when I came to the stop sign at Dodsone & N Bedell is when I noticed a glowing green roman candle like shaped object moving across the sky leaving behind a trail as a roman candle would streaking towards the sky but this was moving horizontally and then broke off into two objects both moving similar and in the same direction until it just instantly disappeared. No trace, no trail, nothing. And there was no sound at all during this event or other objects in the sky (planes,jets, helicopters).
