{nd} Me and my son saw a glowing orb while fetching the nights fire wood

Me and my son went out just before bed to get the nights fire wood . We have a wood stove and it was starting to get late . We went to close the animals in the barn down for the night when we noticed a large very bright glowing thing in the sky . The size was about as big as my little fingernail at arms length. We watched it to see if it was moving and it did descend and ascend slightly and move slightly side to side . Then it started to get smaller until it was a small star like dot before it was so far away it faded out . It leaving took about 5 seconds . I explain it to people like taking a mag light and focusing the beam to a point . We were both excited about this and talk freely to friends and family .
