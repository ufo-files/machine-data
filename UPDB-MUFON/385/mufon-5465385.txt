{tjd} saw neon-green UFO while in passenger seat on 101 freeway while looking out the window it came out of fog then went back into fog

It was the summer of August 1981. I was home from college for the summer. I was in the passenger seat of a vehicle driving north on highway 101 in Ventura. It was dark, approximately 9:30 pm. We were near the Johnson Road off ramp. I looked to my right. The fog was really low to the ground. All of the sudden the fog parted and there was a very large, neon green ship angled toward the ground. The ship was surrounded by long neon-green vertical cylinders and it was pulsating. I saw it for about 10 seconds then the cylinders spun counter-clockwise and the ship backed up into the fog bank.
