{tjd} Gold UFO.

I stepped off our front porch to view the night sky. I immediately spotted a large object in southern sky. It hovered or was stationary for a good five minutes. The object had a winged projection on its right side. ( my left). Very bright gold or white color. Distance of object from where I stood, at least a mile. Size description: using my pinkie finger, halfway down between the first phalange. After 5 minutes, the object moved to the left and then behind the trees. I continued to watch the object when it changed direction and headed north ( east to me). I noticed it gave off a white light as if a search light. This happened twice. The object then appeared to become a heliocopter or lite plane. Exterior lights of red, green and blue. No noise. I watched the object until I could see no more. The apparent size: a nickel.
I'm not sure of the elevation ( degrees).
