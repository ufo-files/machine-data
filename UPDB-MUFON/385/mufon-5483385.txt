{jpg} Witnessed a large reddish orb in the sky that was not behaving like a star or plane. Changing course slightly yet significantly

I step out onto my porch at approximately midnight January 9th 2016 to witness what can only be described as a strange yellowish orb in the sky. It was moving all over the place and could not have been a plane. I wake my sister to see for herself and all of a sudden more appear in the sky and then the woods behind the house. I couldn't count there were so many. I tape my iPhone 6S to my chest and set out to investigate further. In this video I walk down to one area of the woods were I capture numerous oddly behaving lights in the woods as well as the larger orb in the sky. There were many other orbs in other parts of the woods that I would attempt to investigate and get closer to. I couldn't get close though. They always disappeared before I got close enough to tell what was the source of the light. Some of the lights even seemed to be connected. See for yourself. Video is 100% raw. No editing, no nothing.
