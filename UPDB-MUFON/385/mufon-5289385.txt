Giant black cross in Illinois

Incident occurred mid September 2019. I had just walked into my back yard to take my dog out when I noticed a weird humming noise coming from the south/southwest. As I watched the sky in that direction after about 30 seconds I observed a black shaped flying directly towards my home. It was traveling in and north/northwesterly direction. As it slowly flew above me maybe 75ft above tree level I could clearly see it was shaped like a giant black cross. It was massive easily stretching across my property. I could not see any lights, windows, etc, just a huge black cross. I continued to watch it fly till it disappeared beyond the trees.
