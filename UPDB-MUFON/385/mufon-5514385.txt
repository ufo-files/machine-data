(GS) orange light traveling at high speeds in a straight line then hovering before vanishing.

while walking along a local street heading back from my girlfriends house with a good friend, we had just happened to look up to see what would be the most amazing light in our lives. Right at that moment our heads dropped never taking our eyes off the glow it seemed to hover for a split second and then just disappeared. after the object disappeared we both discussed what we had just seen. it was not like anything we have ever seen before it had no tail like a shooting a star, it made no noise and had no other lights to signify it was anything like a plane or helicopter and it was traveling faster than any object we have ever known but seem to be able to stop instantaneously hovering for a split second before vanishing as quickly as it appeared.
