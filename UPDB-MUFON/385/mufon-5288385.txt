Erratic flashes in the sky

Throughout the night I had seen three red only slow flashing slow moving aerial craft S coming E while out for smokes during the evening. Maybe not relevant but they didn’t appear to be like airplanes because they only had really slow pulsating red lights. They were on a direct path. The last red one I saw appeared to head east and then stop and gain altitude and then stop but I just went inside because I was cold and assuming i was only seeing their red navigation lights for under 10,000 feet. I didn’t get a video of that which was at 12:17 Newfoundland time AM. I came inside and shortly later turned off the lights with my friend and began looking from my front window in the east direction. Seeing all the same sky, at 1:02 am NL time, I saw flashing around exact near Altair and tarazet. I could see flashing. Then I took another video and this one picked up what I could see and even more as they flashed at one fixed point but very erratically.
