{cak} It hovered, pulsated, and steadily started to ascend

I was in the back room of my house winding down for the night about to go to bed. Suddenly, I saw these two twirling lights outside my window. Because there are woods behind my house I figured it was probably some kids trying to mess with me with a flashlight. In fact, my dogs noticed the lights first. After that, I went outside and to my amazement, there were two orbs tracing around my window and then they started to ascend and hover over my privacy fence. In the video, the double oblong fixture is actually my back den window, and the reason I kept going back to it is so that if someone were to watch this video they could tell the proximity the orbs were from my house. As they hovered over my fence, they warped shapes, sometimes even blinked in and out at times disappearing. It looked to me as if they were feeding energy off one another, or maybe it was some weird form of communication. The ironic thing is, my dogs who are usually rambunctious, went stoic and stood side by side not taking their eyes off the lights. I actually have a picture of that as well. I'm not trying to gain anything from this video other than a logical explanation. Granted, I do live in Columbus, Georgia which is about 40 minutes from Ft. Benning's military base. I've shown people this video who have worked for the government before and they all said the same thing, that this is nothing they have ever seen in the military. I would be thrilled for someone to take a look at this video and after watching it in it's entirety, please help explain this bizarre event. Thank you for you'll's time.
