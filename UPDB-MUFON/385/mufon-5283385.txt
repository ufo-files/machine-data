Two Discs fly south past KSC then accelerate at a phenomenal rate in another direction

I was standing on the beach outside of my friend's condo on a clear night. His condo was maybe 1500 feet south of Jetty Park in Cape Canaveral, FL, which is just minutes away from Port Canaveral, Cape Canaveral Air Force Station, and Kennedy Space Center. One of the Space Shuttles was on the pad, being prepared for a night launch. (https://www.nasa.gov/mission_pages/shuttle/shuttlemissions/archives/sts-113.html)
I spotted two orange discs travelling from the north, parallel to the beaches, heading south. I observed them for maybe ten seconds, before they appeared to rotate on their sides, nearly 90 degrees from the previous orientation.
Instantly, they accelerate back towards the north at a phenomenal rate, like a ship in a sci-fi movie or show would be heading into warp-speed or hyperspace.
I'm not sure exactly how close they were because they were pretty distant, and I couldn't hear any noise. There was no signs of propulsion or any type of control surface.
