{jpg} First of my 3 UFO sightings, 4 foot craft outside bedroom window

In late May 1966, I was laying in bed reading 'Exploring Mars' about the earlier Mariner space probes and how we were going to use the same technology to go to the Moon and land humans on Mars in the mid-1980s. 

The neighborhood was new and the lot next door was vacant. The lots were all about 1/2 to 3/4 acre. My bedroom side window faced East and I could watch planes take off and land at Bradley Field (now Bradley International Airport). Being a model plane builder and aircraft junkie, I knew what Boeing 707 and 727s looked like. 

I'm not sure why I looked up from the book, but I saw a craft about 20 feet high and perhaps 100 feet away (see sketch). In a moment, it shrunk to a small red dot, then disappeared. My next thought was,"Where did you go?" "Are you coming back?" Somewhere in the back of my head I heard the words "We will be back later."Being an impatient 8 year old, I asked "When?" and got no answer.

I immediately called my parents in, told them what I saw and asked them if they had seen a spacecraft/ My Mom told me there is no such thing and that I must have seen an airplane. She took the book away and told me she hoped I wouldn't have any bad dreams. She was convinced my imagination was working overtime. My Dad wasn't so sure. I checked the window every night for several weeks and saw nothing except airplanes. I even told some of my friends. At least they believed me.
