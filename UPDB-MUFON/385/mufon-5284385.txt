Another white object visible to my naked eye

While driving out to Slide Lake in the Gros Ventre, the road makes a left turn and and soon as I went around the corner I spotted the first object floating in the sky. 
It was white and tubular shaped. It’s speed was slow enough that even though it didn’t seem super high I was able to watch it for around 2 minutes. Of course that’s when it did’nt just flat out disappear 
I was initially driving but was able to get to a clear high spot on the road super fast and then I hopped out hoping that I would still be able to film the object. While driving I saw a “shadow” to my right that was super fast and I did’nt see it again during that 2 minutes. If you slowly track the object it’s going to flat out disappear but I was able to track it still when it would come back from disappearing as it would look like a flash. After driving down to the lake I continued to take photos and I spotted a black object in the waters reflection but you could not see it in the air. After going through the photo I spotted a second black object that as you watch it splits into 2 objects and then back to 1. I’m including the original photo but also a screen record so you can see what I’m seeing also in my Live Photo’s.
