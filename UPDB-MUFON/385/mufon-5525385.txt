While painting the house roof and looking north,I glimpsed a very rapidly moving, black pipelike object moving from west to east.I did have a camera with me but didn't have the time to get a picture as the transit lasted just a few seconds. I was left with an after image of the black pipe which lasted for about fifteen to thirty seconds.On each end of the object I noticed cube like bump outs on opposite ends and on opposite sides.
