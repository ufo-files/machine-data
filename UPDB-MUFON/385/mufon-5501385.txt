I went to the end of my driveway to see if rain was coming. There was a thunderstorm nearby. I spotted a small white dot in the clouds to the west. It appeared stationary. It was clearly inside the clouds but could be seen briefly in the open. After a while, I was sure it was a round solid sphere. Below its location is a large hospital with a helicopter pad that is frequently used. Its not an object I have ever seen in my life however, it could have been a balloon I suppose. It did have an unusually white glow. I lost sight of it as the clouds obstructed the view once again. My gut feeling tells me its there to observe, whether man made or otherwise.
