Hovering craft with bright lights over Fresno 3/6/2012

Driving to Fresno on Tuesday, March 6, 2012 at approximately 10:40 pm, several of us saw pulsating red lights hovering near the intersection of Dickenson and Jensen Avenue. We drove toward it on our way home but the sporadic display of aerial lights floated away in a Northeast direction. Once we got to the city, we lost sight of the craft but saw it reappear hovering just Northeast of Fresno, hovering over the ridge of the Sierra Nevada mountain range. It didn't make a sound and continued to pulsate erratically. We used our digital camera but the sky was too dark and the craft was too distant. At least ten individuals simultaneously witnessed this phenomenon. The craft was about the size of two football fields when fully lit but could morph into a third of its size at will. We watched in shock and amazement. We braved the cold until 1AM, watching it hover and change its light pattern.
