I have thought about this night quite often, it was of June 1988 
My cousin and I were watching a late night movie, 
I went into the bathroom around 11:15pm because I heard a strange noise.
I looked out the bathroom window and saw a rather large craft hovering above the powerlines 
I went back out to my cousin and said, I believe I just saw a UFO .
We both decided we would go out to the front yard. And see if we could see anything.
We walked out into the middle of the road and were absolutely stunned.

Above us was a small craft about the size of a car ( x2 )
They was in the shape almost like an ice cream cone the front of it had neon coloured lights flashing , the underneath of the small craft was one Bright White light.
The lights along the front of the craft were flashing. ( coloured )
We both stood on the road what seems like ages but obviously was not so long within a few seconds, They hovered above us just watching us and we could see there
Silhouettes..
They left with like with a stream of light in the distance.
I think this often.
