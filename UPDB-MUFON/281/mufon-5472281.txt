{rjl} Group of lights playing in the sky, tumbling around each other.

Driving North on 185th Ave, my wife pointed out a group of lights, possible reflection of sunlight off the objects, which were ahead of us and underneath the cloud canopy. There were more than six objects rotating, diving, climbing around each other. They were an unknown distance from us but directly ahead of us. My wife felt that they were similar to objects which we had seen 12/2016 in Central Oregon. 
I felt that they might have been birds at first, their flight almost seemed as a flock of seagulls playing but they were reflecting light and almost glowing. Then I thought they seemed like gliders but there are no fields near here and I have never seen gliders in this area.
While I pulled into the turn lane to a parking lot Northwest of Walker Street, my wife took out her cellphone and shot a video of almost 15 second duration. As I turned into the lot, she mentioned that the car that had been in front of us in the regular traffic lane had stopped in traffic and the driver was staring at the objects as well, until it seemed they remembered that they were driving.
Once we parked and got out to take a better video, all of the objects had disappeared. She felt, as last December, they showed up when she was feeling particularly "down" to remind her that they were here.
