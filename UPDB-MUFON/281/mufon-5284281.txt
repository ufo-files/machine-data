Two very intensely bright coppery objects followed each other in sky then got close to each other, rose, and disappeared.

At about 7pm on Monday, Nov8 2021 I was outside a store on Airport Road in Allentown, PA walking my dog. I looked up at the sky where I'm used to seeing the usual airplanes and such as it's by an airport but two lights caught my eye. I've never seen something with this kind of glow. It was like a copper ball was being lit up by a spotlight. Very different color than any airplane lights I've seen or am aware of and the light itself was very intense to the naked eye. There was no blinking on oscillating of the light. The objects were completely silent, following each other, and kind of floating in a way. They'd move together at the same speed, then one would catch up to the other, they'd both slow down, almost to a stop at one point. You can hear a prop plane come and go during one of the videos. The lights seemed to change direction, gain altitude, then disappeared one after the other. I've been watching the skies for a long time, spotted and confirmed the space station and other satellites, etc but have never seen anything like this. The event lasted about 5 minutes. The video where they're closer together is the end.
