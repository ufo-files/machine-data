{jpg} Orbs hovering in clouds.

I was with a passenger in my truck when she tapped my shoulder and said "Look there!" We had just come to a stop at a stop sign. I saw clearly a couple of silver/gray orbs floating in and out of a large cumulus cloud. I tried to film the event with my phone, but I was too late. They suddenly disappeared.
