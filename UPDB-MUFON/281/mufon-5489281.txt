{nd} 2 photographs show 3 daytime glowing objects far away

I just revisited the 3 photos of the 3 objects that were taken with my cell phone in 2013. I have been watching Unsealed: Alien Files and remembered I had these 3 photos with 3 unknown objects in them. At the time I took the photos I had not noticed the objects were there until 3 weeks later when I looked at the photos for the first time since they were taken on May 17, 2013. I did not report them at that time. I first thought they were smudge marks or something. My best guess is the 3 looked like the space shuttle, a fighter jet and an orb. My feelings are that they could be UFOs. 
The timeline to keep in mind is that when I took the photos of my friends sailboat on May 17, 2013 I had not noticed the 3 objects. I finally looked at the photos a few weeks later (June or July 2013) and saw the 3 objects for the first time. I did not report the incident at that time. I have not looked back at them until 2 days ago, 9/8/2015.
I have lived on a houseboat on a river on the Gulf Coast for the past 2 and 1/2 years and frequently sit on the back of the houseboat and look south across the river. On the other side of the river is 6 miles of state forest lands and then the Gulf. I have frequently (4-5 times)seen what looks like a glowing fire across the river and into the swampy land before you get to the Gulf. I always attributed the glowing to the forestry service conducting controlled burning of the woods. About a year ago I was looking in the direction of the glowing area and a bright light rose from the glowing area, hovered 2 seconds and then sped away instantaneously. I did not have a camera with me. All of these sightings have probably been 2-3 miles away.
I have attached the 3 photos taken that day. The best photo ends in _980 and clearly show 3 objects in the sky. _388 clearly show 2 objects and the 3rd object not as bright. _563 seems to show 3 similar shaped objects in the water at the bottom of the photo.
