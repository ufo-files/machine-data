{rjl} about 5 or 6 orbs, came in thru window bounced around over me then left

My boyfriend, at the time,& I had come in from a party, & were laying down. It was about 2a.m. 6 glowing, yellow & orange orbs came in my window in a straight line. They were the size of a grapefruit. When they came in, they formed a circle and were bouncing up and down, still in a circle. While every other orbs were up, the others were down. They circled around about 2 or three times, then left in a straight line. The exact same way they came in. I asked my boyfriend if he had seen it, thinking I was seeing things. He said,"Yes, I saw it, but I was drinking. You don't drink, and you saw it?" I answered yes, but have stayed puzzled all of these years.
