(GS) Orb light that flashes brilliant white

I was getting ready for bed. I laid down and was looking out the window above my bed and say a brilliant flash of light. It disappeared then a few seconds later I saw the flash again. I got up and looked out the window closer and saw a star like object moving at a high altitude from south to magnetic north. I ran downstairs and out to the front porch and it was directly overhead. The sky was clear and the stars were out. It appeared to be high in the sky. It grew brilliant white again and then it was gone. I went around to the backyard to see if I could find it again but it just wasnt there anymore. It was star sized and when it flashed it grew to the size of a dime. The flash lasted a few seconds each time. I am an amateur astronomer and have never seen anything like that before.
