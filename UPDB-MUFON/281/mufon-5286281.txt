Two triangular objects with a white light on each corner and a red pulsating light directly ion bottom center. Objects were following each other…

Approx. 10:15pm on 7/28/21, I saw two triangular objects with a white light on each corner and a red pulsating light directly on bottom center passing directly overhead of me. 

Objects were following each other by about 700 to 1000 ft. There was no noise at all emanating from these crafts… They were directly overhead when the lights caught my attention. 

I watched for a few seconds and realized that these two objects appeared to be the same as the ones I reported a couple of years ago. I ran to get my phone to try and capture them on video but by the time I had returned they had gone behind the tree line next to my property. 

These objects were no higher that 1000 to 1500 feet up, however, they made no sound at all and were traveling fairly slow, 50 to 70 mph at most…. Normally, I can hear the jet engines of a commercial airliner crossing over well over 10 to 20,000 ft. with no problem…. 

As the objects moved past my property I saw a small plane, possibly a turbo prop, coming from the east and it was at least a mile away and I could plainly hear its engines as it crossed the sky… 

I could barely make out the triangle shape of the crafts as the 3 white light had somewhat illuminated the shape…
