{rjl} Lights in parking lot at Vons started blinking erratically so I took a video. On the video, three red lights appear above Vons and form a triangle.

I went to Vons (grocery store) and the lights in the parking lot started blinking/flashing erratically almost pulsating. I thought it was odd, I’ve seen lights burn out but this wasn’t like that these lights were flashing as if being manipulated. I thought it was strange so I took a video of it. On the video, three red lights appear in the sky over Vons and zoom over the store, then come together and form a triangle shape; appearing to move in sync with each other or perhaps it is one single object. While I was recording I did not see the red lights in the sky. It wasn’t until I watched the video that I saw the lights on the video. There also appears to be greenish blue lights that appear in the parking lot at the same time the red lights are in the sky. Not sure if that’s related or just a reflection of the blinking Vons lights. Anyway the whole thing was quite strange. It was strange that nobody walking around the parking lot seemed to notice or care. No one even looked up at the flashing Vons lights.
