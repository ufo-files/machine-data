Ufozmankato youtube witness of two high altitude watchers, on skyflash

Upon leaving Mankato heading south, I noticed two higher altitude orbs, which I call watchers. I video'd them for a bit, looking for movement. I did get a surprise "skyflash" by a nearby watcher while taping. It was a nice surprise, as it was my first captured skyflash, although skyflashes are very common as of late. I continued on and taped probably five low altitude watchers which were under 100 feet, to my best estimations, and numerous blinkers. Some of the blinkers were spraying at low altitudes. One was leaving a contrail at 4000 ft, according to the rangefinder. Very unlike conventional craft. These particular watchers did not move while I taped them, but did later move slowly to the west, until I lost sight of them.
