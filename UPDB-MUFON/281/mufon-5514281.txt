(GS) looked like a large flying snowcone, green on the cone, red on the front

my husband and i were just getting home from walking our dog that we do every night. i was a few feet ahead of my husband and dog, almost to the street we cross to go to our house. i happened to be looking in the right place at the right time. it was clear and cold out. i saw the object fly by very fast and i immediatley said to my husband that i saw a ufo. i explained that it looked like a large snowcone, green and red. it flew west to east above houses but not above the trees. i got a good look at it before it was out of sight. i knew what i saw and yelled to my husband what happened. we stayed out a bit and i showed him exactly where i saw it and the direction it was going. we looked around for a while then went inside.
