Me,my fiance,and my mother stepped outside my mother's apartment to wait for a cab. We had our dog with us so I walked her out into the yard to do her business when I looked up and saw 2 bright glowing yellow/orange orbs in the sky almost directly above us. I got my fiance and my mom's attention and pointed them out. My fiance handed me our phone so I could try and record them. When I pressed the button on my phone to stop recording an error message popped up that I have never seen on my phone before that said CAMCORDER FAILED then some error code. I should mention that later that night when we got home I recorded our dog for a couple minutes to see if it would work and it did. So whatever happened to our phone I believe was caused somehow by the craft we saw. This happened just last week on New Years Eve 2020 between 7:00 and 7:30pm.
