Long flash of light with no sound over Missile sight in Montana on a clear night.

My friend and I were U.S Air Force Security Personnel doing security for a Missile Silo near Grassrange, Montana. It was about 2 A.M. and we were slowly eating away at our 12 hour shift. We had been out there for a few days and it was forecast to stay clear and cool, I was eating dinner while my friend caught up on his book. All of the sudden the whole area as far as we could see lit up bright with a white light. It lasted long enough for us to look up and see the surroundings lit up as if it were day time, and the it was gone. We grabbed our rifles and radioed HQ about what we saw. We asked the HQ Controller to rewind the footage of the sight to see if he saw what we saw while we secured the perimeter. As we met up at the entrance, the HQ Controller radioed back that he saw nothing on the cameras. We got back into our vehicle and continued our night. A while later my friend reported seeing a red glow overhead that I only briefly glimpsed. After that we saw nothing for the rest of the stay.
