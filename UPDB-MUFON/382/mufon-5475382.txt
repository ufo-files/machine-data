{nd} Giant hovering fireball like object above major event venue. No event taking place at time. Confirmed not moon, stars, etc.

While driving home from work I witnessed a massive deep red to gentle orange fireball like object. This was floating/ hovering over a popular venue. However, there was no event scheduled for the night, nor any for a week prior, or for over a week into the future. Thinking it was the moon i searched for it in the sky, and saw it behind me. Due to the objects size, color and position the moon was immediately discounted as a cause. It was far larger than any known aircraft, and thee is no possibility it was a planet, as the sky was cloudy, and covered in city haze (even normally seen stars and ISS not visible). Also in the vicinity is a major casino with lights that are often reported as UFO's, but they look nothing like what i saw, along with being a few miles to the west of where this object was. Slowly as I approached my home the object seemed to fade away. Unfortunately due to the cloud cover I could not tell if it disappeared from view because of the clouds, or action caused by the object itself.
