BLACK DIAMOND

BLACK DIAMOND SHAPED CRAFT HOVERING OVER THE ROAD ON ORLANDO DRIVE IN STEPHENS COUNTY, TOCCOA GEORGIA. THERE WERE LIGHTS AT EACH POINT OF THE DIAMOND. NO SOUND FROM THE CRAFT. I WATCHED IT HOVER FOR MAYBE 3 OR 4 SECONDS THEN WHEN I DROVE CLOSER TO GET A BETTER LOOK, IT ASCENDED UPWARD AND MANUVERED ITSELF OVER AND BEHIND THE TREELINE IN THE NEIGHBORHOOD I WAS DRIVING THROUGH.
