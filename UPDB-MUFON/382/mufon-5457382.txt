{tjd} Object flew East towards plane heading south dropped elevation 90 degree angle straight down forward then up 90 degrees to previous elevation avoiding

I was on the porch smoking a cigarette when I looked up at a plane which was flying south away from me. Then I noticed a non blinking set of lights which moved much faster heading east at the same altitude. Before the object came in contact with the planes right side it stopped then dropped downward at a 90 degree angle below the flight path of the plane and went east again underneath the plane until it was beyond the left side of the plane then went up again in a 90 degree angle to its previous altitude and continued to fly east for a longtime until it changed to a south east detection then dissapeared at the horizon line. During this time I was growing plants at the house indoor in a hydroponic system and 1:30am was one of the feeding times which took 45 minutes to complete. I would stand outside and take a break at this time regularly and witnessed multiple identical crafts that would make a similar path however this incident there was a plane which confirmed my suspicions that these crafts were much faster than planes and didn't blink and usually made angled course changes always toward the same direction dissapearing at the horizon line/tree line southeast of my position. Initially I was skeptical these were UFOs until this incident. When I saw it manuever around the plane I was shocked and a little frightened. I witnessed these objects at least 1 or more times a week until my grow was done in August.
