{nd} UFO was hovering in distance with another UFO closer to me (witness).

I was outside my house in the backyard taking a smoke break. It was night and there were some clouds but I was still able to see the stars. As I was stargazing I was also watching the airplanes in the sky. Five minutes into my smoke break and stargazing, I noticed an object in the far distance that was brighter and more colorful than the stars or airplanes. I stopped and observed it, seeing if its flight pattern was normal. It wasn't. It hovered and it was oval shaped with livid lights, it ascended then descended in an vertical direction. I was frantic because it didn't make any noises like the other airplanes nor did it fly like one. Then closer to me in the sky was another irregular fly craft. It was a glowing orb and it made impulsive and turbulent moves in the sky. It went behind the cloud, it advanced to the other unknown craft, or hectically flew around like nothing I had seen. It then jolted to the other object and it went dim. I didn't know what I saw and that's what frightened me the most. I ran in to tell my step mom and boyfriend and to grab my iPod to get evidence but the original object was already gone and my family laughed it off. It was the first time I ever saw something like this and it wouldn't be the last time either.
