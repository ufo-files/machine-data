Dark shape in sky

Late last night I stepped out onto the balcony just outside my bedroom. The sky was beautifully lit up by the moon. As I moved my vision from left to right, I could see in my peripheral vision a big black L-shaped object. I got such a distinct fright that I immediately jumped back into my room. Then focused my vision directly in that direction by ducking down to look out. When I did that there was nothing to be seen. I once again looked slightly to the left and saw the dark shape out of the corner of my eye. I focused in that area again and saw nothing. But now I was adamant to keep my focus in that area. I stood looking in that same spot for over 30 minutes but saw nothing else. 
I felt I had to report this as I havent been able to find anyone else with a similar experience. Something is up and I can still feel the fright I felt when I saw that shape out of the corner of my eye. It was huge and black. Just stationary sitting there. Very surreal in deed. Thanks for reading
