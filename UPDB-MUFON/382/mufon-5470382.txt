{jpg} Strange Anomoly Photographed in Deming, NM Backyard

My real-estate agent, Micki Shillito of United Country Realtors observed the phenomena and photographed it in her back yard in Deming, NM at 5:00 AM on May 19, 2017. Below is the information she provided me and is unedited:

"I let my dogs out in the morning anywhere from 5 to 6 o'clock. I started to notice to the east (northeast) what I thought was a star sitting kind of low and with no other stars in the sky. That was the unusual part to me that it was the only light in the sky. I took a couple of pictures because it was an odd shape. When I enlarge the pictures it became apparent that there were several shapes in this light/star. Additionally, it seemed to move back and forth on some occasions. I'm guessing that I took these pictures for about three or four months when the 'star' disappeared. Have not seen it since that time." 

"When I took the particular photo you have I was not aware of anything else in the camera view other than that star, the Gazebo, the trees. It wasn't until I fanned back thru the pictures I took that morning that I noticed the white filmy images. It was pretty clear to me that they were inside my yard and not in the sky."
