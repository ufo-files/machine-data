{tjd} Bright solid white led light.

A little after 8 pm. Just got home from shopping. Got out of the car. Looked up at the stars because it was a clear night. Noticed a bright white light going across the sky with no sound. The light made me think of a bright white led flashlight. It came across from North to south in the sky at a slow steady speed. We watched it for about a minute and then it turned to a red dim light and then disappeared.
