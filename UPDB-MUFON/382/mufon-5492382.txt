Driving home with my friends from the lake early morning, there was three of us in the vehicle, when in the blink of an eye the highway was bright as day light then dark by the time we could look up to see what was going on the object had passed over Junction City, we could see it in the distance it was pretty far off from us but it stopped moved left to right so fast that we all became overcome with fear and a feeling of we just seen something we shouldn't of, we all went to the radio station and got the attention of the late night DJ we told him we seen something and his reaction was what color was it we told him and he stated it had been seen over several towns in KS, OK, CO, and MO. I wished I could of gotten pictures of it though. Ever sense then I'm always looking up at night it was one of the events in my life I cant forget
