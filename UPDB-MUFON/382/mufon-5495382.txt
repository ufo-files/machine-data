{cak} I was in sleep paralysis as it came hovered through my window

I had gone to bed around 10pm that night. I have many dreams every night, but on this night I could not remember one dream. I was awakened from sleeping as a blue/white sun like orb hovered through my closed bedroom window. During this time my eyes opened wide. I could not move, I could not speak. I was in sleep paralysis as all I could do was look at this brilliant blue/white sun like orb. It hovered in through my closed window from the west, stopped directly in front of my bed and only hovered about 4 feet off the ground. The color was amazing but I was in deep fear as I could not move or speak, only stare. It hovered for around 15 seconds until I finally awakened. Upon waking, the orb was gone, without a trace of being there except within my own mind. Since this experience, I've had roughly 15 earthquake & tsunami dreams within a 6 month span. I've never dreamed of these events ever before. I had only lived in Long Beach, Washington for 2 months when this experience took place and in those 2 months, and before the experience, I found much information about the Cascadia Subduction Zone that I previously did not know about. UFO's have been known to study earthquake prone areas before & after major events happen. They have been seen around Chili Subduction Zone quakes & around the Japan 2011 great quake. Due to this experience of the orb, I am having a tendancy in believing the great Cascadia quake is close to happening. People are ultimately unaware of this great quake in the PNW & maybe, just maybe because I knew about the information, & because I was new to the area, it, the orb, came to visit me. Since the experience I have decided to move away from the quake & tsunami prone areas and continue to have these dreams. I have not seen the orb since moving away from the longest beach in the world.
