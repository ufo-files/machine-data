Returning home on Christmas eve during heavy snow with intermittent periods of no snow, went to the rear west window to turn on back lights and noticed a brightly lit object with six or seven lights in a row, looking gold and white. The clouds had broken just enough to show it against the darkened sky. Time was 9:10. Sighting lasted about 7 seconds but it then disappeared behind more clouds. Gold and white lights in alternating pattern in a straight, not blinking. Size of myu fingertip from first knuckle to the end of finger. No sound, opened window to listen, quiet This was about five seconds after it disappeared. Too close not to hear a plane or copter. No chance to aim and start a cell phone flick, cell phone was in car.  Similar object sighted October 19 and reported here, traveling in the same direction.
