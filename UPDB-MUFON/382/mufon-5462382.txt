{rjl} 3 bright lights in triangular shape, hovering above trees for approximately 15 minutes.

In approximately August of 2008 my wife (then girlfriend) and I were sitting in the kitchen of her mother's house where we lived. We had lived there for well over 4 years at this point so we were very familiar with the area as it was a rural setting outside of city limits and very few neighbors. As we sat at the kitchen take, next to a large Bay window I noticed that it was quite bright outside. This was unusual as it was night and no real reason for there to be any amount of light outside of the home. I stepped outside, alone at first, and noticed just above the tree line, about 50 yards away, 3 triangular lights seemingly hovering over the trees. 2 lights approximately 10 feet apart on bottom and a single light above the two. At this time I rush inside and urge my wife (girlfriend) to come outside and see what is happening. She steps outside with me and sees the same thing. After a few moments in astonishment we decided to grab the phone to get video/pictures. However, for whatever reason we were not able to capture any images as the phone was not working normally as it typically worked. There was no sound whatsoever, we just stood, v wondering aloud what it was that we are looking at. After several minutes the lights began quickly fading. Not dimming, but getting further from us. It was only a matter of seconds before they were gone. At that time we began calling our friends and family (phone now working normally again) to inquire if they had also seem the lights... They had not. To this day my wife and I share the story with our children and it is something that we will likely never forget.
