Irregular Pulsating orb crossing over frankfurt airport

Since I got my desk right up my window, I've literally seen a million planes, at least a thousand helicopters and hundrets satellites (satellites not from the inside) passing over me.

This time was wierd because this object was a relatively bright pulsating orb moving north east in a linear fashion. If it was rotating space debris, it could have caused the effect I've seen but there are two factors against this theory:

  It was moving as if it were in low earth orbit (350-600km). If it were slower it would've been in a higher orbit. It was the Novermber 14th 2020 at 3AM. There is no sunlight at that time at that altitude in the northern hemisphere.

  As it approached the horizon, you could see it through (?) the clouds. I'm really not sure about that one. But it appeared to be in front of the clouds, hence in the atmosphere but I didn't want to believe that at that time. You still could see it as it was 10° over the horizon.

It made no maneuvers, no noise and it really scared me the first 30 seconds before I went: probably just space debris. Now after further analysis, I'm 50% UFO 50% Space junk.
