Bright yellow/orange light with zero noise travelling across the sky in a West to SSW direction. Approx x2 size of typical 747, no blinking lights, but too bright to be a satelite

Observed from my rear garden in Ealing. I was testing some new binoculars and observed the very sudden appearance of this bright yellow/orange light from the West. 

It travelled to a South South West position before going out of view. It travelled at a very steady, but high, speed. The light did not flicker or blink, and was much much larger than any star or satellite (I was observing this with the naked eye, not the binoculars.

I got the strong impression that the craft knew that I was observing it.

I have observed other aerial phenomena in this area of sky before that indicate sensitivity to meditative thought.

I will be posting this on other forums to share the experience.
