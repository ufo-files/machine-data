{rjl} bright white light that changed colors and shapes

I was driving to work 5:30-6:00AM. I observed a bright white light to the east. It moved south following a parallel path to my vehicle. After about 10 miles I stopped to photograph the object with my phone. I thought it might be a military helicopter with a search light, or an airplane with its landing lights on, however there was no flashing lights like on an aircraft. I lost sight of the object when I turned west and it continued south. I forgot about the incident until later that week, then I checked the photos more closely and found something very interesting. The photos showed the object changing shape and color multiple times. I did not see this at the time. I only saw a large bright light.
