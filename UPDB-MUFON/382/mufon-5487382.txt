{nd} initial 3 lites triangular formation changing directions, then a 4th appeared

I was driving north on loop 1 around 6:30 pm and looked to the NE over downtown, I noticed the first three lights in the sky in a loose triangular formation they were very bright, brighter than stars or planet, there was an over cast sky, so there were no stars visible. As I kept driving and looking I noticed that a fourth light had appeared slightly away from the initial three lights, they had almost a fuzzzy, sparklish light source to them. All four light continued to move in a random "falling/dancing" movement to them. Initially I thought they might be skydivers, but considering the overcast sky,and the area they were over, that's not a "normal" area for skydiving. I was surprised at what I was seeing and could not believe it. I eventually lost sight behind the trees as I kept driving North on loop 1 around West 6th street.
