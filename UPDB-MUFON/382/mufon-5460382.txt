On Sunday June 2, 2019 at approximately 11:00 am I witnessed my first daytime UFO. A glider was flying at an altitude of approximately 5,000 to 6,000 feet and at a distance of two miles in a controlled thermal chasing pattern. The glider came out of a turn from north to south, and was well silhouetted against a large Grey and white Cumulonimbus cloud approximately two miles long and 3,000 feet tall. There was clear blue sky above and below the cloud at the time of the sighting. I estimate the cloud bottom was at least 8,000 feet. Cloud color was bright white on top, and gray across the lower side and darker on the bottom.As the glider made the final turn from due north to the southwest in a graceful turn and then settled at and altitude of 5 to 6 thousand feet and an estimated speed 40 to 50 mph. At this point I had been watching the glider for at least 30 seconds, and I was enjoying and reminiscing the experience I had in my first glider ride. The glider now heading south was about half way across the width of the cloud, when a dark round or square object bigger than a basketball fell from the upper part of the cloud. This caught my eye, and I was amazed to see a second object the same size as the first, fall in the same way the first did. The second object fell below the glider and kept pace in level flight. The first object then dropped in behind the glider, and at the same altitude kept pace with the glider. The pilot would have to look 180 degrees behind him to see these objects. The three of them traveled together for another 5 seconds, and then the object following the glider stopped, and the shot straight up into the cloud. The second object made an instant 180 degree turn and shot straight up into the cloud. The glider continued southbound probably unaware of the two visitors. You may call me any time. Thanks
