{rjl} Sitting out smoking and seen two orange lights fly over with in 1 minute of each other.

I was on the phone with my husband and smoking a cigarette when I seen a orange object come over the treeline I did not have my glasses on but it didn't make any sound as it went over. I told my husband that it was strange about 30 seconds later another orange object appeared going the same exact way, this time I got my glasses and I looked up at the orange object and little sparks of fire were coming out the end of it and then the went over the building. The feeling I had after was I know I saw something that wasn't a plane or something I have seen before.
