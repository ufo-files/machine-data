Unknown object fell from sky

At approximately 10:25pm Tuesday, July 26th I exited the front door of my home which is located in east-central Indiana; the front door of which faces north. Immediately as I exited my home I saw a bright light which was of a single color in appearance (Yellow), this light was larger in relation to the background stars and fell from the sky in what I would personally and possibly naively speculate to be a normal rate of descension. Upon falling the object made no sound and appeared eliptical in its descent... I believe that the object itself was spherical or circular, but that in its descending motion appeared eliptical to me. This lasted just 15 or 20 seconds... the object from what I could tell continued to fall to Earth, but my view was obstructed by the house accross the street, I walked to my driveway after I lost sight of this to try and find a better vantage point, but by that time the object was gone... I heard no sounds in relation to it and could see no visual reminance of the object by looking to the horizon. I wonder if anyone else has seen this or anything similar in the area recently... I live in a pretty small community which is always pretty quiet at night, however in the past 2 days upon sitting on the front porch at night I have heard a lot of air traffic ( helicopters to be specific ) which is odd.... I wonder if there is any correlation between that and what I saw.
