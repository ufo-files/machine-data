Moving string of lights, (thirty objects one following the other) - (djs)

I was outside talking to my brother and our nephew, my nephew says hey what’s that in the sky. My brother and I looked up and there was at least thirty objects one following the other moving from west to the east. I tried to catch them on camera but couldn’t pick them up on the screen. The objects approached to mostly straight south from us and either turned straight south or disappeared. We thought there was a thin cloud that blocked our view but there was a jet that came from the East that stayed in view through the area where we were trying to maintain sight on the objects that we were watching.
