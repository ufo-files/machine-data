Wife and I observed red light above us (again) before it ascended and disaapeared

On January 18 at 7:06 PM I stepped out of our 5th wheel when I observed a glowing red light hovering to the north at about 1,000 feet. It made no sound or emissions. I beckoned to my wife to come and watch. We both observed it for about a minute before it ascended and disappeared. I have bought a new camera and came close to getting a photo. If it keeps showing up, I will get a picture of this craft.
