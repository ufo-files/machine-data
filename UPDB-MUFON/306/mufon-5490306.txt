{cak} The triangle was hovering over a house on a small hill flashing it's lights.

My brother and his fiance and I were driving back from their work in Sonora towards angels camp. Along highway 49 as we came into a low ground I noticed light emanating around the next bend. As we came around the bend we saw a black triangle hovering over a house in a small hill pulsating it's lights. We pulled onto the road that lead to the house and had enough time to get out and stare for at least 20 seconds at it. It was about 400 feet away and the craft was about 120 to 150 feet long with white bright lights on each corner of it. the craft was covered in what appeared to be heat waves that hot summer asphalt gives off. After the twenty seconds passed the craft descended below the tree line to the west of the house. We then turned around and took multiple back roads to try and find it but it was gone.
