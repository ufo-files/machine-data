{td} 5 lights traveling from North to South

Husband and I were out boating in Bellingham Bay , I said to husband look at the lights. 5 of them the size of light bulbs going to the North to the South at a very modest pace, Watched them for 15 minutes before we docked. Other people at dock said they saw them also. Friends meet us there as they said yeah they thought they saw something too. WEIRD nO ONE COULD GET THIER CELL PHONE TO WORK TO TAKE A PICTURE.
