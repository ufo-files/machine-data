{rjl} two lights came into view, hovered then burst away

I was letting my dogs out before going to bed and I always make it a habit of looking into the night sky. I saw two very bright balls of color changing light and I immediately dismissed these as planes. But after watching them for a minute they were noted to be simply hovering. Then a minute or so later they both shot straight up and out of view. The rate of acceleration was amazing. I felt totally uneasy. I tried to call my wife from my cell phone and it,showed no signal and was not working properly. I had to take the battery out of my phone and upon turning it back on the battery was dead. It was at least at 50% battery at the time of the event.
