Hold up a UK 2 pence piece in your fingers at arms length and view it from the side at around 5 - 7° .

This is the shape of two objects that 'flew' silently over the rooftops a couple of weeks ago.

I did report this but I may not have completed the form correctly and it did not seem to register my report.

Anyway, I was at my back door and a movement caught my eye to the top right.

I turned my head and had to really try to focus on an object the shape if which I described at the start.

The sky way grey and pretty bland, cloud cover being quite low.

The object was just a few shades lighter than it's surroundings so I saw it as soon as it came past the neighbours chimney stack.

As I mentioned, I could barely make it out gut as my eyes got focused another object the same shape, half the size and half the luminosity became visible following behind.

Both 'craft's came from roughly SW and went approx. ENE from my position though this is just based on the orientation of my house.

They did not emit any noise, light or anything else. They travelled in a straight line and traversed the visible area (half a mile or so?) in around three seconds and it was just pure chance I that saw them.

Everything about them is not what I expected to see from a 'UFO'.
