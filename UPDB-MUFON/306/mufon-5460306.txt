Unidenified Submerged Object

I was watching the History Channel Program " Unidentified" episode 1 about the UFO they encountered off the coast of Baja in 2014. I encountered a USO in the exact same location while serving aboard the USS Flasher SSN 613 in 1976.I was a sonarman and I recorded the incident on 1 inch tape. I have kept this secret for over 40 years because I told by commanding officer that we could never speak of this. We recorded everything while underway and when we recorded any unusual sounds or submarines we would submit our tapes for further analysis to the Navy and they would let us know what we had recorded, In this case we were told to shut up about it. This thing was moving at speeds underwater in excess 80 knots and was running circles around us. There was no torpedo or surface vessel that could move at those speeds. The sonar system we were using at the time, the BQQ-5, which was built by IBM and was the most advanced sonar system in the world at the time. After watching the program I felt compelled to reveal the secret that I have kept for so long. I hope this information can find its way to Elizondo and Delong so that I can provide more details.
