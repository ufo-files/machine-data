-Pulling into my driveway at home.
-I glanced up and saw the sky swirling.
- It seemed somthing big was going to enter our atmosphere.
- By the time I came out with my camera I saw a large fireball and saw what looked like a piece of it breaking away from the left corner of the fireball heading South, that being the 2nd object.
-Exhilarating, excited, calm. Called for my wife several times!
-The fireball disappeared behind the home next door into the Gulf of Mexico I would assume.
The 2nd object I followed after losing track of the fireball looked like an aircraft I had never seen before. I showed the video to a neighbor who was a master mechanic for an airline for 35 years retired, who claimed he had never seen an aircraft like this in his lifetime! That 2nd object flew at a slight upward angle and I lost it behind the trees across the street.
