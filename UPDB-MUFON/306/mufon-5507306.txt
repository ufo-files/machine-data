{cak} White/red light hovering in the sky, moving abruptly up, down, and in circles

Came out to the porch, noticed what seemed to be a moving star. Realized by movements and change in light it was not a star. Could not have been a jet/plane or helicopter with the speed and directional change. It would change from bright white light to duller red. Emitted way looked like red sparks. Seemed to be encased in a black cloud but it is a crystal clear night. It moved in vertical circles but would also fall and rise very abruptly. Generally stayed within the same area. Had a very strange feeling about the object that it was not man made. Watched it for close to 2 hours with it ascending but not too terribly much.
