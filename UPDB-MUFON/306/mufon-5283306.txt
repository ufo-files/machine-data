White Cigar shaped craft at low altitude moving slowly overhead

I was driving my car North/Northwest on McClellan ave (M-553) starting to go down from the plateau into NW Marquette when I noticed through the top of my windshield a white cigar-shaped craft, with semicircular ends and straight top and bottom edges, slowly passing directly overhead. I intentionally slowed to a stop and watched in awe as it slowly (maybe 150 knots, about the speed of a jetliner on landing approach) passed overhead and continued in a straight path without deviations heading North out across Lake Superior. It soon grew smaller in the distance until I could no longer see it. The sighting lasted about two minutes. It appeared to be a few hundred feet above me, but hard to tell size and height without any reference points. From my perspective, it was about one foot long and grew smaller as it moved away. The weather was cold and clear and the visibility at that time was unlimited and I saw nothing else in the sky. This is a rather old report, but I have finally decided to make this report to MUFON. I never talked with other locals about this so I do not know if any others also saw this phenomenon. I also forgot to take any pix with my cell phone as I was awe-struck and never thought to do that. I found the timing unique as President Obama was in Marquette that day making a speech.
