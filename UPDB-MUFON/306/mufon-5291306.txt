10 to 15 fluttering lights flying in a path.

I was in my backyard getting some fresh air since the weather was nice. I was looking at the stars when all of the sudden I noticed these 10 to 15 white lights moving in the night sky. At first glance I thought it was an airplane flying low. I realized it was silent and had more lights than an airplane. 

It was moving in a straight line but the lights were all individually moving in different directions. The moment felt surreal because I knew it wasn't an airplane or anything I'd ever seen in the sky. The lights flew north until I couldn't see them anymore.
