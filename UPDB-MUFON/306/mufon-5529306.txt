All lit up like a light bulb, slow pace, no blinking lights like a plane, No sound, going North-East then disappeared very fast.

I was outside of my workplace and noticed a bright light in the sky with a co-worker. It looked very different to me so I grabbed my binoculars out of my car to look at it closer. This "object" was shaped as a cigar, all lit up like a light bulb in color. There were no blinking lights like an airplane has. I stared at this for 10 minutes trying to figure out what it is. It slowly moved across the sky, heading North-East. All of a sudden it stopped for about 3 minutes at a stand still. With my binoculars glues to my eyes, I stood in one spot staring at this weird thing not moving in the sky. All of a sudden, it took off, with an enormous amount of speed, into the clear dark sky. I stood there waiting for it to come back but it never did.
