Stationary diamond, dark shades of grey, no lights

At around 3am, I got up to use the bathroom as I couldn't sleep. I walked across my living room and looked out of a skylight at a clear sky, stars and a bright moon. I then noticed directly straight up, what appeared to be a dark diamond shaped object, made up of maybe 4 different angled sides. I felt that I was looking directly up at its underneath. It sat motionless in the sky for at least a couple of minutes, totally static, totally silent, and no lights.
I went to the bathroom for a minute or so, came back to the window and looked up, and had gone. 
A mutual friend who lives a couple of streets away also saw the same object. Apparently it just vanished, very quickly.
