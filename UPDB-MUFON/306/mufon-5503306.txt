It was night, thought a bird, hovering high speed, mmoving up n down like 500 - 100mts ht. pulsating. Not sure if only one or many coz once the light goes then comes from another place.

I was sleeping above our building with my family and noticed my motorola C131 was vibrating. Woke up and saw it switch off and switch on by its own. Not knowing what to do, I have removed battery and checking. I noticed that something strange in the sky. Some object with bright light which I can say is the perfect white color, was hovering. I couldn't see the object but the light. It was moving with a great speed like some 500mts in 2-3secs and then the light goes off. Then after few seconds it comes from another direction. It was changing the height of flight from 500-100mts approximately leaving a white trail of light for a couple of seconds.

I called my dad sleeping next to me and showed him. He's not interested to see. I got busy with my phone and also watching the lights. After an hours time it went OFF.

Next day, I explained my dad but he was not interested for he thought I would have seen some bird. That day night I stay awake and the light again started around 2am IST. This time my dad too saw and said, it could be the devil he saw in his 18s and his father (my grandfather) said it's a devil which hovers and laughs weird sometimes and it's not new but have to be aware as they can attack when alone.

After some 30-45mins another helicopter joined the area when the object hovered for a couple of minutes and then stopped showing OFF.

Amazing thing was 4 fighter jets (my knowledge by then) with big noise has circled that space for nearly 5 times and then gone. That is when even my dad stood up and started watching at the sky.

I have witnessed the incidents for many times for another 6 months and then I had to move to another city.
