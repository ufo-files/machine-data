I saw a light above the clouds moving in a circular motion across a span of 6 miles

It was 9:50 PM on 2/4/11, It was a cloudy night, and I was driving to Olney, MD to go to work. At the intersection from Fieldcrest Road to Route 108 I noticed what at first, I thought was just a spotlight hitting the cloud cover above. The light was going in a circular motion that looked much like when you see a spotlight at a construction site being projected in the air. I thought nothing of it, stopped at the traffic light and I turned right on 108 to head towards Olney, MD. After driving for a minute, I noticed the light was in front of me again and I could see it moving in the same circular motion, covering a lot of ground as it made each complete circle and at the same time moving forward with each circle it completed, heading in the same direction towards Olney, MD. 

I put down my windows and heard no sound from above. The clouds seemed to be kind of low so my only guess is maybe a helicopter could have been above it is shining a spotlight down and moving it in a circular motion while it was traveling the same direction I was traveling. However, I do not feel this is the case since looking at the oval shaped light it seemed as if it was glowing and having some sort of mass rather than being just a point of light being projected from above. I really do not know what I saw however its characteristics such as the way it moved across the sky was significantly different from a plane or a helicopter. Why would an aircraft make spiral like motions to move from point a to b? The object finally went out of my sights as it headed towards Sandy Spring, MD. At that time, I had turned into work. It was certainly an odd thing to see.
