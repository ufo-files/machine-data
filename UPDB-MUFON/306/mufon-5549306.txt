disc moving in a vertical position across sky, no sound

I was helping a friend with some yard work by holding a garden hose to put out any fires while he used the grinder to remove some old bolts from a length of fence in his large lot. This gave myself time to watch the sky and at 5:14 pm while looking south and scanning to the east I saw this object approching at a high rate of speed about 1500 to 2000 feet up. I called for my friend to look but he was between some pine trees and the fence and could not hear me over the grinder so did not see it. The object was moving against the wind. It was a clear/slightly cloudy day. I only managed to get one picture and my memory card was full. Camera was at 3x optical zoom
