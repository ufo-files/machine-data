{nd} flashing orb lowered into pensacola bay

WHILE SERVING IN US NAVY ABOARD USS SAIPAN AIRCRAFT CARRIER ON DUTY TRAINING PILOTS,ON MIDNIGHT WATCH WITH OFFICER OF THE DECK,WE BOTH OBSERVED THIS INCEDENT.NONE OF WHICH WAS ENTERED IN SHIPS LOG BECAUSE OF FEAR OF RIDECULE.WE BOTH WERE LOOKING OUT OVER THE BAY AND NOTICED THIS BRIGHT ORANGE OBJECT APPROCHING THE SHIP ABOUT 45 DEGREES OVER THE HORIZON,IT APPROCED VERY QUICKLY SIMPLY STOPPED AND HOVERED FOR A MINUTE OR TWO AND LOWERED ITSELF INTO THE BAY GLOWING AS IT DISSAPEARED UNDER THE SURFACE OF THE WATER.IN DISBELIEF OF WHAT I HAD JUST WITNESSED I ASKED THE ENSIGN I WAS ON DUTY WITH WHAT DID WE JUST SEE ? AND SHOULD IT BE ENTERED INTO THE SHIPS LOG? HIS REPLY WAS WE DID NOT SEE WHAT WE HAD SEEN AND TO ENTER IT WOULD CAUSE DETREMENT TO BOTH OUR CAREERS.
