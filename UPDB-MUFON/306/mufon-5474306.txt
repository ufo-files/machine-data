1. I was traveling south on the 99 freeway with my wife heading back to Fresno from Stockton. 

2. While I was driving, I notice some strange lights in the middle of the air and I thought maybe it was a kite with lights, but there was no wind and it did not look like a weather balloon.

3. At first I thought it was a car dealership advertisement blimp, but there were no signs on it.

4. It looked like a large, glowing, dancing centipede. What I thought were the legs looked like it was swimming in the air.

5. I asked my wife if she saw it and she said yes then she started recording it. During the ordeal, I was amazed because never in a million years I thought I would see a UFO. Then we just continued home.

6. Our 11 month old daughter was sleeping and we did not want to stop because it was getting late.
