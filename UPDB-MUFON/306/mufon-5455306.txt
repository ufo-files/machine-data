Appeared to be satellites in a straight line formation horizon to horizon.

They were too high to see anything other than the light reflected as by a satellite. They were in a straight line formation horizon to horizon, traveling from approx. S.W to N.E. From my vantage point there were 12 at a time visible. The travel rate was close to the same rate of travel as a satellite as observed from the ground. My location is in a valley so the horizon here is from about nine thirty o'clock to two thirty o'clock. This procession lasted four to six minutes, appearing over the S.W. horizon and disappearing over the N.E. horizon until the last one was gone. I was too intrigued to think of counting until close to the last of the procession but I estimate with the number visible at one time and the rate of procession, there were at least 24 and maybe up to 36 that I saw. They were already across the horizon when I noticed them so there's no way to get an actual total count. Satellites? Doubtful. UFO'S? Maybe. I'd just like to know.....
