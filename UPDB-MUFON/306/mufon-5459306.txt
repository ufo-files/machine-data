{cak} Red orb on dirt road

Me and my father were coning back late from car races in cereal Fla. When we were about to arrive at relatives house we were staying on vacation we saw a basketball sized red glowing orb. It was in the dirty road right off the east coast of lake Monroe. It hovered 10 to 20 feet in the air About 30 feet directly in front of our vehicle, 6 to 8 feet from the woodline on tyre right side of the road a few feet beyond the driveway we were about to turn on. We observed the glowing red orb and stopped and watched. My father saidsomething like "what the hell is that" and we watched it for 10 to 25 seconds. It then shot southeast into the woods at a very high speed. We then returned to my aunts house and found that relatives were still up and were hearing animals in the woods making lots of noise. I don't knife if this is related. It was in 2001 so we didn't have camera phones. I believe it was most likely unknown natural phenomenon but after researching all the years since,that it could be a being,drone,ship or something we can't imagine.
