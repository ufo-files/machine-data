Huge Citing Very High Up

I looked up from my computer and saw a flash of something shiny in the sky very high up. There was a cloud and as I watched the object got covered by the cloud. The cloud took about 5 minutes to move away and when it did I saw that two more objects had appeared. They were in a triangle formation. Soon more and more lights kept pooping up. At one time I counted 14 but its possible there were more. They were flying very slowly and in between another constantly changing formation. The only formation that remained was the original triangle shape. These objects all appeared white/ silver and round but they were very far away. One of them appeared an orange-osh color and flew much faster and in a lot more directions than the others. Very slowly they moved towards the south east in the sky. During the siting an airplane flew by and I could tell the plane was flying significantly lower and faster than these objects. Seeing them made me very excited, Im obviously very interested in this phenomenon, and I could easily dismiss any other possibility. Planes would never fly this closely to each other or hover in a the air like this. They would never switch directions. They could not have been balloons because they were far to high in the sky, and they were too slow moving to be any thing the air force.
