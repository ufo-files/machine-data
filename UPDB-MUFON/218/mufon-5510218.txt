{dr}Two UFOs in Oregon?

At 11:20AM, Pacific Coast Time, on June 10, 2013, I was outside in the backyard (in Oregon) taking pictures of my nephew while he was playing. The day was sunny and calm. I don't remember seeing any planes or helicopters or hearing any either. Today, on June 17, 2013, I was going through my photo gallery and I saw that in one of the pictures there appears to be two objects above a house roof. When I zoomed in, I couldnt identify either object. I think one might be a helicopter and the other is possibly a bird, but Im not sure. A friend suggested that I submit the picture to an organization that might be able to sharpen the picture and bring out the details.
