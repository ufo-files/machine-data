{nd} In the night sky over Ft. Gordon, Ga. I was in training at this time.

I saw a very bright light (star like) very high in the sky. It looked like a star but it was moving. It was moving in a straight line for sometime when it began to zig-zag across the sky for very large distances. The it came back on the same line it had been flying and began the move straight again. The it dropped like a stone for a great distance and then it rose back to the same spot it dropped from. It began to fly straight again and then it took off at a very high speed straight up in the sky. I had reported it to the OD and they laugh at me.
