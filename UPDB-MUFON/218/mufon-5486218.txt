{dr}I came out of my house to cross the street to see a friend when it just appeared over me and stayed awhile while it lit up everything around me

I was coming out of my house around midnight to visit with a neighbor across the street on the corner. All of a sudden with no noise an Unidenif. UFO swooped down above me. It stayed over me for some time and light up everything to where I could see the fence, flowers, cars and stairs going up to my neighbors house. I could not yell, and just felt frozen. Then my friend came out because of all the light, and ran down the stairs to me. We just watched in awe. Then it began to teeter very slowly up over her roof top and stayed a few mins more and again teetered slowly and just swooped off very very fast and looked like a mist or vaper left behind as it was leaving. We then went into the house and called the US Air Base closet to Los Banos, Ca. and five unmarked cars showed up within the hour. They took me in the house and asked questions with a very large recorder. Then they took me outside where the Air Force men had telescopes set up and werelooking at the sky. They let me look, and we saw a few of the UFO's playing tag with each other. They looked like stars, but were not. The Los Banos Newspaper has a write up about other people who had seen the UFO's. I believe it was either 1971 or 1972. I really need to talk to someone about what happened to me after this incident. It is a story like no other I have ever heard.
