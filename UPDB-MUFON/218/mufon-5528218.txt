This event was captured on my cell phone. I was taking a picture of the beam of light coming through the clouds not the object in the pic.

I was on my way to Nashville, Tn from my home in Bruceton, Tn. It was an overcast day as you can see in the Picture. I was traveling south on hwy 641 in benton co when this beam of light came through the clouds of course I thought at the time it was the sun breaking through. I thought this would be a cool picture so I took out my cell phone and shot the picture never thinking nothing of it other than it being a nice picture. This happened approx. 4-5 yrs ago. Today on 1/12/11 for no good reason while watching a program on TV that had absolutely nothing to do with UFOs I thought about this picture and how i would like to have it as a background Picture for my desktop. So I sent it to my email and downloaded it. When I first saw it enlarged on my computer I thought it was a speck of dirt on the screen then after close inspection of it I now think I may have inadvertently caught a UFO in the picture. I may be wrong but I don't think so. Take a look and with better software than I have maybe you can figure out what it is.
