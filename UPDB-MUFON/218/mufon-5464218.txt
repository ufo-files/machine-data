{tjd} Two bright star like objects went from stationary to slowing moving together to stop to start moving fast and then blinked out.

I was in my garage watching the news ( my son was playing video games on living room tv) and I walked outside onto our back patio to smoke a cigarette. I noticed what I thought were 2 stars that were brighter than the rest and they were stationary. Then they started to slowly move towards Wilmington NC from my house in Leland. At first I thought I was just seeing things. They were moving very slowly but remained exactly the same distance apart. Then they stopped for a few seconds. I was knocking on my bedroom door which also exits onto our back patio to get my wife's attention so that she could see this. She got there too late because right after I was done knocking, the two bright stars started to rapidly move in the same direction while staying the same distance apart the whole time. After a few seconds of acceleration they simply blinked out.....and they were gone. I've always been a believer in UFOs and alien life elsewhere but I've ALWAYS been skeptical about people who claim to see these things. But finally I actually saw something that I can't explain. We live just several miles from ILM Airport in Wilmington, NC and are very accustomed to seeing planes at night coming and going but this was indeed different. I'm typing this with very nervous excitement because I was completely blown away by something that only lasted about 30 seconds and I've NEVER reported anything like this. I'll be watching the local news tonight to see if there were any other reports of what I saw.
