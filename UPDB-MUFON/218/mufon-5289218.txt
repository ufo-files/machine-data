Cigar craft with lights down the side

I was walking my dog in the grass near the Selah WA track at approximately 5:00 a.m., it was very dark and clear skies. I looked up and saw a long cigar shaped object with lights going down the side. It appeared they were windows, because I could see that it was not one solid light, but had spaces between each light. I would say there were approximately 10 windows. There was no sound, and speed was about the same as an airplane.  I fumbled for my phone and took a picture, but by that time it was far away, but it does show a long shaped lit up object in the dark sky if you zoom in.
