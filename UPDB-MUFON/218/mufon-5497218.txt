A star like light caught my interest because it appeared backlit. I called my man out to see it because it looked like it was ascending. It is brighter than Sirius and originally about 35 degrees above the horizon; it's currently 90 above us. We were making dinner no drugs or alcohol were involved. At first I thought it was the iss but the hovering, random seeming lefts and rights quickly took that off of the maybe list. It was still hovering over 3 hours later.
