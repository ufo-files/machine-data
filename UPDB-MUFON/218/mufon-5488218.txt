{cak} Saw a strange light orb flying around and filmed it

My two friends and I went out on the balcony of our dorm to shine laser pointers at people, but one of my friends noticed a light flying around. It was about a mile away flying above a neighborhood. We watched it's erratic path for a while and then I decided to record it. I tried to film it once, but it disappeared for a few minutes. Then I tried to film again and while filming the light returned and I caught it on tape. That's what's in my video. Shortly after the video, the light dissappeared and never came back.
