Saw objects floating/flying brightly in the sky emitting fiery sparks all around them, and then descended.

Was outside with with my family, my oldest daughter yelled, startled by 3 floating discs with sparks emitting from all sides and leaving a slight trail as they slowly moved/hovered through the sky, sometimes they were bright and you could clearly see a disc, sometimes they faded or disappeared completely while others reappeared, there were more than 3 it seemed but usually no more than that were visible at the same time, they flew in a diamond formation of sorts but changed formation frequently, they came to a point and then descended very intentionally but slowly, we could see their lights through the woods till it came to ground level. We were not able to go pursue and see what it was though.
