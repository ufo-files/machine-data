White or silver cigar shape with red lights hovering over Lummi Reservation- 4th of July. Unexplainable movements, also disappeared and reappeared.

4th of July fireworks were going off everywhere.
I could see red lights hovering in the sky way above the fireworks. it also made this back and forth or circular movement that is impossible for any aircraft I know of. I also saw a plane coming towards it, it blinked out, became invisible, Then reappeared- this actually happened several times. I honestly felt like it was watching me and I did not want to get my phone/camera out, or call attention to it. I didn’t want my young child to be scared. I just watched it for quite awhile, when our family firework show was over, I asked my sister to take a look- she said “whoa, they are hovering there!” 
I went from front yard to back yard with my dog and thought “it’s watching me” as soon as I had the thought it brightened its lights directly at me. Went back to the front of house to pack up my daughter and dog and head to hotel room. My sister said it quickly dropped behind the tree line.
