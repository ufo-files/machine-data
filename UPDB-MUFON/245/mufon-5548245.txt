Multiple People saw what at first appeared to be a star suddenly move and stop at super speeds

I was at a church camp in central Oregon lying in a feild under the stars one night at about 11 p.m. A bunch of us were outside that night but most had already gone to sleep. As i lie there on my back way out over the tree tops i saw what at the moment seemed to be a super bright star. As I looked at it, it jumped probably 100 miles almost instantly then jumped in a different direction. I felt bad waking anyone up so i let the people sleep. Before long i heard some people a few rows down talking about this thing that would jump in seemingly random directions every five seconds or so. so i walked to them and watched it with them. We concluded it was very far away probably 100 to 150 miles away and was moving far to fastto be a helicopter which is what they thought it was at first sight. After probably half a hour it just dissapeared instantly gone like it had never been there.
