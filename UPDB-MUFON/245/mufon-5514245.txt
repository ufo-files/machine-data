(GS) Daughter and I witnessed line of lights tilted 10 degrees flying across sky

My daughter and I both witnessed a line of 4-7 orange lights, seemed connected, equally spread apart, vertically lined up but tilted at an angle, flying just above horizon, flying west to east. We were driving south on Cartersburg Road, from Danville to Plainfield. There was an airplane at a higher altitude flying farther east. As we watched, the lights went out/disappeared, starting from the closest light to the ground, and moving up in order to the top, and all lights were gone/extinguished. Drove the road twice more, in day and night, looking for anything that could make the lights, and found nothing.
