{dr}My wife and I saw 6 red orange orbs floating but controlled pattern while on our deck.

Just before twilight on a overcast evening, my wife and I had just finished walking our dog and my wife sat down on a chair on the deck. I was standing across from her and looking across the road when I saw two strange large red orange lights rise from behind the white pine trees in the southern part of the sky heading in a south west - westerly direction. These orbs moved in a floaty but controlled way, but unlike any aircraft I know. As they traveled, they seemed to have subtly moved around as they headed on their trajectory. These two orbs were followed by four more in an identical way. My wife and I were completely amazed. I captured this event on my iPhone. My wife told me that she saw two of them move together then apart as they were departing. It was difficult to believe what we were seeing. To our surprise, 10 minutes after the fist sighting, we saw two more of these same orbs acting in a similar manner to the first sighting and traveling in the same direction. These orbs were below cloud level and disappeared by fading into a small dot into the cloud covered sky.
