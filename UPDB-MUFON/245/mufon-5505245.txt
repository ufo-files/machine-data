{rjl} About 12 lights in a circle, would have been size of moon at 12 o'clock position in its orbital path

Was looking over an apartment at all the stars. From my point of view, Jupiter and Sirrus were to my left. I was facing SE (mostly East). At first sight, it was like an esclipse of the sun without the flares of light. The lights appeared somewhat like streaks then because focused. Was in a complete circle of about 12 lights with the horizontal plane (East to west) being the brightest. All lights were faintly shimmering. It did not move in any way and was there for about 2 minutes. My friend who was standing there (on his cell phone) also saw this when I pointed to it. The sky was very clear, no clouds at all. The object appeared over an apartment such that I had a very clear view of this object well over the roof top. I am quite certain of what I saw. I had just arrived at this location, had not been drinking or otherwise impaired. I clearly saw this circle of lights and have no doubts what so ever this was nothing made by humans that I know of. I couldn't tell distance therefore the size but it was about how a full moon would be at the top of its skyward path over head. This went on for about 2 minutes. I glanced away, looked back and it was gone. Didn't have time to shoot a picture with my cell doubt it would have shown up.
