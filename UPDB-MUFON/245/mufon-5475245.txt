3 orange lights flying in triangular form.

I sat on the balcony on Saturday, July 1 at 23.I looked at the sky to see if there were stars.
Three lights of orange color appeared on the roof of the next school.They are in the shape of a circle and they fly in the northeast direction in the form of a triangle.Two of the lights left their places a little.They came back to their old places.Everything was so fast. It gets very close.
