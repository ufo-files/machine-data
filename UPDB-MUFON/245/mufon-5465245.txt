{tjd} White Light in the distance that became a dark rectangle as it went overhead

Witness first saw the object as a bright white star like object in the distance which became bigger and bigger as it approached and floated with the wind directly over head at less than one thousand feet. Appeared to be dark black or brown, rectangular in shape with 4 smudge pots in the four corners with Flames.
