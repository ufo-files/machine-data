Orange ball of light blinks in and out.

Was traveling back from Anderson, heading West on S.R. 26 when I noticed an unusual orange ball of light about 60 degrees above horizion in the western sky, slighty north of 26. It started out soft and fuzzy, got bright and then popped out completely. Then it popped back in brightly for a second or two and then disappeared for good. It never moved from the same spot. There were no clouds in the sky, the sky was clear and the temp was approx 60 degrees with a slight breeze.
