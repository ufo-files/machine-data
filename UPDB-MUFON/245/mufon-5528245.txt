circle in the north east sky going around in a circle repeatadly, went counterclockwise

walked out the back door to check on *(Name deleted--CMS/sg) who was out in the barn feeding. Happened to look up and there was this circle in the north east sky going around and around counterclockwise. Cloud cover was medium. It seemed to be very close to our farm. I went to get Gary and we watched it. We could see no light tail coming from the ground. We thought maybe it was a spot light but is was above the lower clouds and we could not see any light trail going through the clouds or coming up from the ground. I tried to take pictures but it did not show up on my 35 mm.It would change circle patterns from more east then go back west.The circle would sometimes have a little blip in it like a stutter. At the end it moved towards the north and got dim. We even called our neighbor to see if he saw the same thing and he did.
