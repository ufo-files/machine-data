saw a cigar shaped object in broad daylight

As I live by an airport I look up alot as planes are arriving....I was with my fiancee and we in the car heading towards the airport and over the bay near the Navy base was a cigar shaped object....white and silver in color...reflective but not too bad as it was not overly sunny. The object did not have wings,tailfin or lights......it moved faster than a normal aircraft into a single cloud and never reappeared until a half mile later and then was on opposite side of car....no noise was heard....I see alot of planes everyday as I work at night...this did not have the characteristics of a normal craft...I was absolutely floored and this event has lead me to believe for sure that we are not alone.....This also witnessed by my fiancee.....
