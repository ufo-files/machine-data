{nd} Bright lights hovering in the sky uniformed pattern

Traveling to dinner, my wife and I observed a few bright lights in the sky. As we traveled closer we noticed that the lights were not moving over head as a normal plane would, they remained on one side of the highway. Traveling farther down the road we observed that there seemed to be no tail as a normal plane, it was more of a boomerang shape. As we slowed the car we both stared up at the object still remaining motionless in the sky with bright lights which seemed to be three on both "wings". As we traveled by the object we noticed it still had not moved. We lost sight of the lights a few moments later as we traveled farther down the highway. Discussing the thing we saw in the sky, we turned off the highway and stopped at a red light only to have the object come over the hill at a high rate of speed and swoop over the road and continue heading west toward the river. My wife and myself where concerned as we have never seen anything like this before
