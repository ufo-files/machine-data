{tjd} 3 STAR LIKE OBJECTS IN PERFECT PYRAMID SEQUENCE WITH DISTANCE IN BETWEEN .

MY WIFE AND I LIVE IN THE RURAL COUNTRY ON A SMALL FARM ABOUT 10 MILES FROM THE NEAREST TOWN. WE ARE LOCATED PROBABLY 35 MILES FROM FORT BRAGG ARMY BASE IN FAYETTEVILLE,N.C. IT WAS SEPTEMBER OF 2018. I DO A LOT OF CATFISHING AT NIGHT SO I AM FAMILIAR WITH THE NORMAL STAR CONSTELLATIONS AROUND OUR SKY FOR THAT TIME OF YEAR. MY WIFE AND I STARTED NOTICING 3 STARLIKE OBJECTS IN THE WESTERN SKY BEHIND OUR HOME EVERY EVENING PROBABLY FIRST IN THE MONTH OF MAY OR JUNE AND THEY WERE IN A PYRAMID FORMATION WITH A SMALL DISTANCE BETWEEN EACH OF THEM IN WHAT APPEARED AS THREE DIFFERENT LIGHTS ALL THE SAME DISTANCE APART TO FORM A PYRAMID. IT DIDN'T MATTER IF IT WAS CLOUDY OR RAINY OUTSIDE, THEY WERE THERE EVERY NIGHT. THE LAST TIME WE WITNESSED THEM , IT WAS ABOUT 9:00 P.M. VERY DARK OUTSIDE AND WE GOT IN MY TRUCK TO GO TO TOWN AND GRAB A LATE BITE OF SUPPER AND AS WE EXITED OUR DRIVEWAY THEY ADVANCED FROM THE BACKYARD TO THE FRONT OF US IN THE SKY ABOVE THE ROAD WE WERE ON HEADED NORTH WEST TO TOWN. I ASKED MY WIFE WAS SHE SEEING THIS AND SHE SAID YES AND AS I MOVED FORWARD UP TO 60 MILES AN HOUR THEY REMAINED IN UNIFORM AS A PYRAMID BUT TOOK OFF AT AN INCREDIBLE AMOUNT OF SPEED UNTIL THEY WERE COMPLETELY OUT OF SIGHT. WHAT BLEW MY MIND AND STILL TROUBLES ME TO THIS DAY IS HOW THEY SEPARATED IN DISTANCE, MIGHT I ADD EQUAL DISTANCE TO HUNDREDS OF MILES APART AT THE EXACT SAME TIME AND MAINTAINED THE PYRAMID SHAPE PERFECTLY UNTIL THEY WERE NO LONGER VISIBLE. A FEW NIGHTS LATER WE HAD ONE LIGHT FOLLOW US IN FRONT OF COURSE TO TOWN AGAIN ABOUT 3 MILES AWAY AT ALL TIMES UNTIL IT WENT OUT, CAME BACK ON WHEN WE WERE ON THE TOWN LIMITS THEN VANISHED AS QUICK AS IT CAME. I CANNOT EXPLAIN THESE EVENTS BUT THEY ARE TRUE AND I CAN ONLY IMAGINE IT IS SOMETHING MILITARY OR TOTALLY FROM ANOTHER, HIGHER INTELLIGENCE THAN OF WHAT I KNOW OF. IT IS ABSOLUTELY THE MOST EERIE, UNEXPLAINABLE EVENT THAT I HAVE EVER ENCOUNTERED IN MY LIFE.
