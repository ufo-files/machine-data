3 white spheres moving silently across a clear blue sky from NW to SW. all perfectly in straight line, moving at same speed, kind of a

My friend and I were standing at the top of the driveway at Keith Dr near 8th Ave in Vancouver, BC. It was about 3 pm, approximately, February or early March 2016. It was a clear sunny day. I noticed a white orb sauntering into the sky over the mountains to the north, coming from the east. It was about the size of a cockpit and moving about the same speed as an aircraft readying to land at YVR. Then another orb appeared behind the first one about 50 feet. It was identical in appearance and motion, moving at the same speed at the leader sphere. Then as my friend and I stood with gaping mouths, another white orb appear about 50 feet behind the second orb. The three orbs moved silently and calmly across the sky from NE to SW and we watched until it disappeared behind trees. The three orbs were all identical, same size, same height, white, no lights and at no time did their speed or course alter. They just mosyed quietly to the SW, uniformly. We had no camera, unfortunately.
