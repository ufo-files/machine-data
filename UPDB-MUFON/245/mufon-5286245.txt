Green lights forming a grid and seeping into my windows

I was 7 years old and couldn’t sleep. I remember hearing a loud noise approaching and I thought it was a helicopter, but then I saw the green lights. I hadn’t known helicopters to have green lights, so I thought something was wrong. The lights came in both my north and west windows in a grid like form, squares cut out of the green. I opened my door and ran to our sun porch which led to our backyard. I opened the door to catch the green light against the outer wall, and then disappear. I never saw the lights again.
