{rjl} Witnessed a very bright object in the clear sky.

I saw the object In March 12, 2015, at 7:50 PM above Manhattan, NY. I was home. I looked out of the window and noticed unusual helicopter activity in the sky. I saw three or four helicopters and a plane and a very bright light. 

I kept looking because I was first intrigued by the number of helicopters but then I noticed helicopters were moving but the light didn't move at all as if it was frozen. That night the sky was clear, no single cloud, I could see the stars however they were too small to be captured on a video or a picture with my phone. I recorded few videos and took pictures and uploaded on this website. The light of the object was very strong and bright, it did not flash, it gave a feeling as I am looking at a light bulb. I took binoculars and the light kind of hurt my eyes like if I was staring at the light bulb.

During entire hour the object was there, the helicopters and the plane circled around the object (the object was above them). And when the object disappeared, only one helicopter stayed on guard but was present another half hour and then also left. The object was very big in size and it was visibly higher than helicopters and plane. During one hour it moved slightly from the west to the east and a bit on the south, and its moving I could only notice because of the tree in front of my windows (each time I was checking on the object I saw it moved just a bit). I lost its sight when I went to check on it again and I noticed it was already gone.

On the video the flashing and moving lights are a helicopter and a plane. NON flashy bright light that stays still light is a UFO.
