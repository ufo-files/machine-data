I went outdoors to smoke a ciggarette. As I looked towards the northeastern skys I noticed a small but brilliant light headed at high speads towards the horizon. At roughly 70deg the object made a high speed maneuver towards north. At first, I thought perhaps the object may have been a day-time falling star, untill it changed direction. Then I thought perhaps it was a neighbor with an R.C. plane, untill it went through a high altitude cloud. As it dove, it went from glowing bright- yellowish orange, to a reflective silver color. No sound was heard. The color change is what initially shocked me. Then to see the object pass through high altitude clouds... 
Roughly three seconds later, as I stared in a mild state of excitment and shock, the object reapeared through the clouds, hung for but a moment, this time appearing in a much more uniforn blackish-gray circular shape, seeming much further away, then, within a fraction of a second, the object withered into the distance of the sky, around the bank of clouds from which it had previously broke.
