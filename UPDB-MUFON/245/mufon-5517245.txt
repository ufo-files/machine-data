(gs) Red-orange star-like objects over Fannin County, TX

My grandson called me and told me to look at the sky towards the south. I looked up and saw at least 8 red-orange objects that looked like stars or bright planets. They were moving at a brisk pace. They changed paths,directions, and 1 changed shape so it looked like a red centered clear globe. Some formed an L or V shaped pattern depending on ones viewing angle. Then they continued in a Northwesterly direction and disappeared. I wasnt sure what they were. I thought aircraft but they were silent and changed formations too quickly. I came in and did a search for red lights over Texas and saw that there had been similar sightings in the southern part of the state. I felt that they were very extraordinary. Earlier in the afternoon, the city of Bonham experienced cellphone outages, ATM problems, and difficulty accessing the internet.
