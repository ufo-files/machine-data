We have witnessed these disk types in the sky for over a week. To begin with I thought it was satellites, but there are lots of them every night. They form a line or a triangle in sets of 3. 
They move up, down, left to right at a speed! They hover & move again. 
They flash various colours.
2 nights ago we walked our dog, there were at least 30 in the sky. They were moving in perfect harmony.
I’ve noticed my dog’s behaviour has completely changed. He’s anxious & waking at night but won’t go outside. Other people have also been saying their animals are acting strange. 
This has become a nightly thing. Tonight we saw them again, this time there were around 5, my daughter captured a video.
