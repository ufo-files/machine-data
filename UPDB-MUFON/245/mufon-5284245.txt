Was in backyard and seen strange shape hovering in and out of cloud

I have 3 sightings the very first one was when I was in my backyard on the phone with my friend planning a bbq. I so happened to look up at the sky at a fluffy cloud. That’s when I seen an object in the shape of half a square [ floating in and out of a cloud. I couldn’t make out what is was. I called my wife to come and look. And I said “look what is that” she said “l don’t know is that what you called me out here for” I took pictures with our camera which she had later sold and cleared memory card. The 2nd time I seen something was in 2010 driving on my route to Arizona. I seen a fireball in the middle of a valley as I kept driving the highway the fireball then turned into 3 fireballs not moving. I couldn’t understand what I was looking at maybe a flair but it wasn’t descending. On my way back it was no longer there. The 3rd time was just resent at 4:19 am 3/17/2021 three lights just sitting in the sky like an airplane but it wasn’t moving. I took a picture and you can see the lights but the pic is grainy but I got it. Since my first experience I can not stop looking up at the sky. I am searching like wise stories .I know I’m not the only one that has seen such things. thank you for taking the time to read my story … [name removed/CMS/fgc]
