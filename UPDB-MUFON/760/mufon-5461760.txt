{rjl} Very bright neon colored bluegreen. Appeared and immediately shot down vertically and then disappeared

I was driving home at night and was just looking up at the moon. Then out of no where a glowing bluegreen ball appeared so fast and descended downwards going at speeds inhumanly possible. Then disappeared! Totally visible for only about three seconds and the whole thing was over. I thought I was going crazy but there is no way that it could of been anything else! I felt sick to my stomach afterwords and had to call my boyfriend otherwise I would of cried. Very non threatening. But I still had never seen anything like it before and couldn’t think of any other explanation!! I made sure to note the time.
