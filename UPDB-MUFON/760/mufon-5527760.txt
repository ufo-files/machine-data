my younger brother & i walked our dog across fields in medway where we lived. As we walked we saw a large bird dead on the grass a crow or hawk size? we looked up and there was a silver kind of shimmery like oil on water colour disc shaped craft it had a dome shape on top of the disc and several lights around the bottom of the disc which looked kinda white/silver.we could not believe what we saw and i called the dog back and put him on the lead.We watched it for about a minute as it stayed still with no noise quite low ,kind of low helicopter height in the sky.Then as it moved so rapidly,in the blink of an eye,to the right where it stayed moitionless for about a minute or so . we continued to stare in disbelief when the craft rapidly (again in the blink of an eye )so fast and fluid movement shot upward in a straight line out of sight and was gone. We stood for a while longer looking to the sky but it was gone. the day was bright and clear with no clouds and it was about lunchtime. We where both a little shaken and scared by the enormity of what we had seen so rushed home to tell our mum who told us to never tell anyone so we never mentioned it again apart from between my brother and i sometimes have said" do you remember. . .when on our own. i would dearly love to know if anyone else saw the same thing? It was roughly august 1981 and the place was lordswood medway,it was fields and woods but i believe houses are now built on the land?
