Long, non-reflective ruler shaped object spinning from E to W made a right turn like a boomerang moved back to E and disappeared

I woke up and went to the kitchen downstairs to drink some water, I checked the time on my microwave's clock when it was 5:07 AM. I looked at the stars for few minutes as I always do. The sky was clear and I still could see the stars, even with my neighbors' outside light on. Suddenly (around 5:10 AM) I noticed something skinny and long moving towards my direction from E to W which I thought was a big bird, maybe a Canadian goose for the size. However, the movement pattern didn't look like flapping wings, so I thought it was struggling with the wind. As it got closer I realized it was a solid ruler shaped object. It was spinning slowly enough that I could identify its shape. It was non-reflective and had no lights. I am not quite sure about the color, but it could be something between dull white or light grey. It lasted 20 seconds or so, but after making a sharp right turn it sped up to the same direction it came and disappeared when it was about at the same spot it first appeared. It made me think of a huge boomerang, not in shape but in trajectory. I kept looking the sky for few more minutes in hopes of seeing it again but the only thing a noticed was a satellite going West. Unfortunately I didn't have my phone with me when that happened. I think it was clear enough to be recorded on camera.
