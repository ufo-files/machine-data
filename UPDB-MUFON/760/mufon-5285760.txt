Colorful craft emerging from the clouds

I was lost in a neighborhood out in Stanley on my way to pick up my daughter from a football game at East Gaston High School. It was getting dark. Around 7 pm. I stopped on a street, got out of my car, looked up and this huge thing was coming out of the clouds. I thought it was Air Force One at first but it was too big to be an airplane. It was moving really slow too. It looked like a whole world billowing out of the clouds. My phone and car dashboard lights had started going crazy a few minutes before. One of the reasons I stopped. I stared at it for a minute, got in my car and left. I thought the world may be ending and I hurried to get me daughter.
