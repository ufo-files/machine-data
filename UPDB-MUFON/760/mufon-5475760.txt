{rjl} Came down and leveled and turn back up

I had bought a new time lapse video camera and was letting it run out my back door all night long and down load it the next day. I had watch it a few times with out seeing anything. I then slowed it down as much as i could and noticed the objects almost in a vertical dive then level off and then back up almost vertical when a third object came up from below with them. The rectangular objects had four equally spaced lights. I know what planes and jets look like in the night sky and these were not normal.
