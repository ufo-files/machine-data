It was about 20:00 hours on a weekend in October of 1992.I just got out of workof a Store in Altamonte Springs.I was about to cross the parkinglot where myhusband was waiting,when I noticed something very large moving in the sky abovethe store.The sky was overcast and somewhat cloudy.The Object was very big,atleast football field size.It had greenlights in a v-shape fom front to back.Itappeared to be black,coudnt tell the exect shape.It was going very slowandwithout any sound.There just seemed to be some vibrations coming from it.Itwas also flying very low.It was no blimp or any such thing.I don't know what itwas,but I never seen anything of that size before.My husband was in theAir-Force,so I got a pretty Idea about what is what.
