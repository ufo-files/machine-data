{jpg} Like a bright star glowed,hovered and then went straight down very very fast.

Plumas Lake 
  My wife and I were returning home on the freeway just before 11pm we both saw what looked like a bright star white with a little blue. it hovered for a few seconds and then zipped downward at great speed.It went straight down and not at an angle. We could not tell how far away or how big it was but it was the weirdest thing i've seen in the sky for a long time.
