Bright amber lights in sky disappeared.

I was driving on I-64 west toward Corydon, Indiana when I spotted a bright amber light or (collection of lights that were close) hovering over Corydon. This is on one of the flight paths to the Louisville airport, but was much brighter than landing lights besides being a different color. I would estimate that the light(s) was/were 4 X brighter than landing lights. It was early dusk, but one could still see quite well with no clouds and excellent overall visability. When I first saw it, the distance was about 10-12 miles. I was very curious about what it was and it seemed to hover about 800 to 1000 feet up. As I got to about 2 miles of Corydon, the light disappeared while I was looking at it. No plane, no nothing. I looked at the area so long I almost hit a another car. Any known aircraft would have been easy to spot. Strangely afterward I forgot about the experience for a time.
