{jpg} Very high bright star-like objects traveling first straight then turning. Approx 7-10 spotted during 2 hours time.

We were preparing for a camping event coming up on Aug 21-23, and had built a fire where we were resting and looking at the stars. We noticed a bright star-like object moving from the NW to SE very quickly. 
Once it was out of sight, another appeared. This one did not move in a straight line, curving to the east, then turning back to the SE. 
Another one appeared in the SE moving North that vanished. 
We saw what looked like a passenger plane fly over with blinking lights. Then approx 20 min after the plane was gone more star-light objects appeared, one after another. There was 2 in the sky at once at one time moving slightly different paths. 
I wasn't sure but what they may have been satellites, but they were moving extremely fast, and when one took a sudden 45 degree turn, I no longer knew what we were seeing. 
This is not unusual here. I see these lights all the time. I saw them again on August 22 during the camping event, 2 weeks later. 
For the record, no one was drinking or using illegal substances or mind altering drugs. There were about 40 people here on August 22, and I'd say about half of them saw something high in the sky that night if they were paying attention.
