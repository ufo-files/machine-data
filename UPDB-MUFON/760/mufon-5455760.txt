{rjl} Disk ufo

Walking late at night with a friend, A disk shaped UFO, with mult- colored lights around its perimeter, appeared directly overhead, hovering just above the tree line. It made no sound. I remember staring up at it for several minutes. My next memory is quickly walking home. My friend and I didn't talk about it many years, but I remember being terrified for years of lights flashing at night. when we did discuss it, 15 years later, my friend had memories of us seeing something strange in the sky and watching it approach us. I do not. Nor do I remember watching it leave. about an hour of time is missing and the memory of the incident itself vanished from both our memories for many years until a random conversation brought it Back. We've only discussed the event once in 30 years.
