{dr}White Cylinder Object that appears to change shape!

Last night 7/31/2017 I spotted a White Cylinder object heading from the North West to West in a straight-line. There was no noise coming from the object, and the lights did not blink. After taking the picture zooming in on the object, the object appears to change shape and looks like a face. You can see a face in the picture. Very strange.
