(GS) Saw object that appeared to be a cluster of balls or a tubular fabric object

On my commute home, I saw this strange object flying toward me at a relatively high elevation. At first I thought It might be a flock of geese. It was not; rather, it was a singular object made of circular, joined balls or tubular material with a shifting rough "v" shaped tube shape. The top of the "v" always faced upwards at different angles. I thought initially it might be a motorized parachute or parachutist with a collapsed chute in distress. I stopped my truck to better view the object. It was not a traditional motorized chute, hang-glider or other type of object. It "flew" in gentle arcs from SW to the North. I took several I-phone photos which show the "v" shape, but little else. As it went out of sight in the North (towards Cartersville), I saw a small Cessna 150 size aircraft fly between my position and the object. The object made no sound ...
