{dr}Black object invisible to the human eye and not seen until picture was reviewed

There was a storm front and I decided to take a few pictures of the two fronts meeting. I took several pictures observing the clouds by eye and through the display on my camera and didn't see anything odd. After I pulled them up on my phone and started to look back at them I noticed this black object seeming to come out of the clouds in one picture and in a different area in the sky in another. I and my wife were both outside and didn't see this object until looking at the photos.
