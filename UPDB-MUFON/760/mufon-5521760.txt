Three orange lights moving in a direct line NE to SW over southeast PA

Eastern PA - standing outside BSing with neighbors after midnight - had three lights come over one after the other traveling on a heading of about 110 degrees. (NW to SE)

was an orange light - looked to be moving more like a satellite than an airplane - Im usually pretty good at IDing aircraft - this had no strobes or nav lights, just an orange glow.

had one come over, a few seconds later a second - then a third.... COULD have been an iridium flare but, Ive never seen three in a row.... none predicted for the time (or any other passes that are known).

not moving overly fast (meteor - Ive seen a bunch of those) and Im 100% sure it wasnt an aircraft - the third one looked to "burn out" but, the shape was still easily made out in the sky for a bit....
