{jpg} Boomerang shaped object sighted above Rio Verde Foothills.

I walked outside with my wife to catch some fresh air. We always look at the stars outside as we are in a rural area. At first, the object appeared to be a large star. Upon closer observation we noticed a shape and that the bright lights were part of a shape with other smaller lights along the edges. They were pulsing red, green, and some blue. We watched for over one hour then went inside. When I went out again to observe, approx., one hour later, the object had vanished.
