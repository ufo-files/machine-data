The Object was black/Dark Grey in the Sky at first I thought it was a hang glider

I was driving in my car in slow traffic up Lenton Blvd Nottingham (Inner City), Nottingham - In the car was my 9yrs old son and mother, as I was driving I spotted what I thought was a hang glider in the sky but it was too high up to be one and we live in a city? I pointed it out to my mother and son who thankfully also saw it, it was sort of hovering but then moving very slow so not a military plane. I did think it could be a drone, its shape was sort of triangle/Hang glider shape but it must of been a big object because it was just under the clouds but very high up. We only saw it for about a 1 min as there are some big buildings and I did drive in the direction it was heading but could no longer see it.
