Sightings of bright lights over my village, after coming home from mountain biking!

One evening after coming back home from mountain biking, my friend and I stopped off on kellet road, looking over carnforth, Lancashire, and you can see pretty much as the eye can see. We saw bright lights over carnforth, my friend said it’s helicopters, I said no way, that’s miles away and too bright! I’ve seen loads of raf planes training whilst out on the mountains. But this was something that has bugged me for years? I will never forget it! Hope my friend can concur? Bob? You remember don’t you?
