{cak} Awoke to a snap sound, blue rod 6

We were asleep, I awoke to a snap or pop and heard the fridge die down; I thought I smelled electricity and fearing a fire, sat up but before I could rise up, a blue rod of light came straight through the house, it entered through a window and exited another in a straight path approx. 45-50 ft. in length, it was 6 inches in width and I felt compelled to run my hand through it but didn't. It had a beginning a middle and an end, I watched the end go on out. I ran to a side window to look out and a second rod ran through, it took about maybe a minute each. Ran to the back of the house, where it came in and observed a big pulsating ball which was rolling but going nowhere, in the back yard. Called the electric co. next morning and asked if an electrical event happened, they called back end of the day and said 2 more houses a mile from mine had the blue rods go through same time. No one reported the white ball. They suggested it was ball lightning, so I looked it up but this moves and our sighting was rolling but stationary. It faded out after a while then regenerated slightly then disappeared.
