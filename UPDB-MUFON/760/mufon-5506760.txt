{cak} Orange orbs

My boyfriend and I were driving down the hills of woodland. I noticed a "triangular" shape of orange glowing lights I had never seen before. Looked almost as they were hovering or moving very slowly. It was over cast outside and raining. Recently went dark out. They were under the clouds, but too high to be electrical power lines. I was just staring at them, until we eventually drove to where we couldnt see them any longer.
