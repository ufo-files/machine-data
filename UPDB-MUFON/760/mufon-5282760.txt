Brief sighting of unusual-looking object

Traveling north on Interstate 75 just north of Hwy 80 exit in London, KY. Observed a large white object approximately 1/2 mile away just left of interstate and approximately 200-300 feet above tree-tops. Object seemed to hover for 2-3 seconds then began descending slowly to the left (from my vantage point) in a a strange motion slower than that of a plane approaching a landing. Seconds later when I passed the location I saw the object it was gone. There is no way an object that large shouldn’t have been visible once I got parallel to where I’d seen it. Nothing but open pasture. There is an airport in London, KY but it was 1-2 miles away and we’ll behind me to the right. The object I saw was losing altitude as though coming in for a landing and could not possibly have been aiming for the London airport.
