A hovering orange-red light with slight left to right movement then ascent like a like a candle going out

My mother and I were getting ready to get into my car to go to a new years eve party and we noticed the light towards the east just hovering with a slight left to right movement. I used my cell phone to take a video of it. It continued hovering then it seemed to ascent up and east. There were no blinking lights such as in helicopters and planes, just seemed like a flickering light like that of a candle.
