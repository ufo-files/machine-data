{cak} 2 Super Bright White Led lights blinking downward

I live in La Habra Heights California. The two super bright large white led like lights were blinking from the bottom of the craft. When I noticed it, it was unlike any aircraft I have seen before. I live under the flight path to LAX and this was definitely much more south than the flight path for LAX. It flew in a westward direction with a no sound and decented where I believe would be the pacific ocean. I immediately looked and the flight path to LAX to compare its path and lights. The planes that followed were much higher in altitude, and lights were much lighter in luminosity. The craft I saw had no red blinking faa lights as well. It was quite obvious to me that this was no airplane. I could see no shape except for the extremely bright large white led blinking together. I have no explanation for what it was as it slowly decended toward the west, yet south of LAX.
