Four lights in a row

My husband and I were on our way to a friends house. He was looking up at the sky and said, "Look baby, a UFO.", We parked the car so I could get a better look. I got out my cell phone to take a picture, and as soon as I got out of the car and pointed my phone at the UFO it disappeared. A few minutes after we saw a blinking red light and what appeared to be 2 jets. I didn't believe in Aliens until last night. We live on a back road, and its normally people out in the middle of nowhere that get abducted, I'm a little afraid that is going to happen to us.
