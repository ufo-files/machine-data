{jpg} Looked like stars moving in different directions and would appear and dissapear out of nowhere. Some got much beighter and then dimmer

The first one that we noticed looked like a possible satelite. But then it stopped and begun moving backwards. Then we noticed more, sometimes multiple at once that would move across the sky and suddenly dissapear around the same area in the sky. Some would change direction, and one got VERY bright suddenly like a glowing planet then it would dim back down and then get bright again as it moved. We spotted atleast one every few minutes for over an hour. All going different direcrions but seeming to dissapear in the south west region of the sky. They didn't blink like the airplanes were doing. They would just grow brighter and dimmer in no particular pattern. There was multiple times where we witnessed small flashes of light in the sky as if a star exploded for a second and then it would be gone. Also witnessed bright flashes that occurred twice as if lightening. The entire sky lit up, but there were 0 clouds.
