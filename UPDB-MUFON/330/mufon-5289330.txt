V-shaped formation of lights at high altitude flying north

V-shaped formation of lights flying at what appeared to be very high altitude going north. 5 or 6 solid lights (not flashing) unlike the aircraft with navigation lights seen at lower altitude in the same area. Moving at a similar speed to satellites. Looked similar to the Little Dipper in terms of size and brightness. 

Only observed for 5-10 seconds before the stop light turned green. Perfectly clear night without cloud coverage.
