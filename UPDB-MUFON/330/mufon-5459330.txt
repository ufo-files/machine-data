{cak} 2 bright objects flying in the Northern sky, in an Easterly direction.

On my way to work I was going North on Noland Road, 2 or 3 blocks South of 23rd St. when I happened to look up and see the two objects flying ahead of me in an Easterly direction. They were extremely bright like sun reflecting off of chrome. They seemed to be in formation at first but started cavorting before just disappearing out of view. I was driving so was not able to get a picture but was able to observe them for a few seconds. They were going rather fast.
