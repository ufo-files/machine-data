White orb

I was in my backyard, when I saw a white orb flying southbound, at about 400ft high. There was no sound from the object. It looked baseball sized, glowing white, and although it was a little orb, I could follow its path from above me to about 5km away, when I lost visual contact. Unfortunately, I didn't have my phone at the moment of sighting to film it.
