{rjl} A silver orb going back and forth under a giant, white oddly shaped cloud. The sky was totally clear blue except for the odd cloud.

I was driving back home from a visit with my sister. It was late evening but the sun was still out and the sky was a brilliant blue. A huge white, billowy cumulus cloud was in the south west, all by itself in an otherwise clear sky. It was like a big thunderhead, but very white. It caught my eye as I was driving, and I stopped the car alongside the road for a few minutes to look at it. Then a small flash of silver appeared under the cloud. I thought it was a small plane at first, glinting in the sun. But as I watched, I saw it was round, and was not moving like a plane. It moved slowly forward, then went backwards pretty fast. It stayed still for a moment, then went forward a again, like it was measuring the cloud or something! As I watched, it went up into the cloud for a second, then back down under it. I had to go on home, so I do not know how long it was playing around that strange cloud. VERY ODD EXPERIENCE! It was far enough away that I couldn't have taken a picture even if I had my camera with me. I am just curious if anyone else saw what I did that day Tuesday, June 16, 2015 in Bon Aqua, TN...
