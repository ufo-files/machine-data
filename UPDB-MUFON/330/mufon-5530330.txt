Bright white light.

I awok eat 3.15am as my yougest son was crying for a milk, after this I could not sleep so decided to stand at my window and have a cigarette, as I was standing there I saw two planes fly over from north to south, just after the second one passed bright white glowing light passed from south to north over my house, I watched it trying to determine what it was, it was definitely inside our atmosphere as it was lower than the two planes had been, it was quite fast moving but not fast enough that I lost site of it quickly, I watched it on it's way when it turned and started heading north-west. I was only able to watch it for 5 minutes as it then disappeared behind the houses and trees.
