{rjl}Egg Shaped Object Over Phoenix, Daylight

Around 3:30 pm on August 23rd, 2018 two friends of mine (an educated housewife and her executive husband)and their two teenage children were visiting from Seattle. I am a college instructor.

We were having a pool party in my backyard when my female friend and I simultaneously spotted an erratically moving, white, egg shaped object. We agreed it could not be a balloon, her husband said it was way to high to be a drone. It was high up easily in airplane altitude. It eventually hovered for about 5 minutes, then it deliberately traveled in a straight line to the south, then to the northeast and eventually went higher and higher until no longer visible. Two airplanes flew over the city at a lower altitude than the object at the time.

The four of us all saw it. My friend and her 13 year old daughter and I felt curious and elated to see it. Her husband and 15 year old son looked at it, but were not particularly excited by the event. Everyone agreed it was in fact a UFO sighting. None of us had ever had a UFO experience before.
