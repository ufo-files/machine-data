Took 3 pictures while in parking lot, the 3rd one had an orb, or, circle shape in it. Didn't see it when I took the picture.

My husband and I stopped at the Enroute in King City on our way home from Pearson International Airport. The caliper on the driver's side brake had seized and needed checking. While my husband threw a hammer to it, before it got too cool, I took my camera from the car and snapped 3 photos of the sun's rays between and beneath the storm clouds brewing. The first 2 I took were at 4:30pm. The third was at 4:33pm. I looked at the pictures after we got back on the road. I wanted to see how they'd turned out. The first two photos were just the clouds and sun rays. But the third was different.That's when I saw the orb in the attached digital photo. I knew it wasn't the moon, because it was 4:33 when I took it,and, we were in the second night of the full moon, so it would not be anywhere near the sun. I would like to know what it IS though. Also, my flash was turned off while I took the photos. And I saw nothing at all in the sky while taking them.
