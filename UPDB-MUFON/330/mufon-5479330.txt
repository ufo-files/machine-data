{jpg} UFO noticed when viewing cloud photos on cellphone

It was morning and I was at home with my husband standing in my porch in Rio Rico, AZ, taking time-lapse pictures (GIF file) of the clouds trying to capture the soft misty rain falling with sunshine. The lapse was 15 seconds between pictures. I stopped the camera after three lapses.
I then viewed the GIF picture file to see if showed the rain, but it didn't.
I then viewed the individual pictures and noticed a blue-green orb with 4-5 dots of light in the photos in a SE direction.
I didn't notice the orb with the naked eye as I was taking the pictures and I have no idea what it is. It looks semitransparent.
The first picture shows the sun behind the clouds and to the right, an orb partially obscured on the top by the clouds, with one of the dots of light shinning thru the cloud.
The second picture shows the orb lower and to the right from original position over the horizon, still partially obscured by clouds, with 4 of the lights visible.
The third and last picture shows the orb against a clear spot of blue sky, with 4 lights visible;it has now moved NE across and under the layer of clouds.
I looked for the object after seeing it in pictures but nothing was visible to the naked eye.
I have uploaded the phone pictures to my computer to send with this report.
