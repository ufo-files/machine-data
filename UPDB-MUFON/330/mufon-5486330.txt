Three red lights on the sky

I was smoking on my balcony when I saw three red lights on the sky. They didn't move for some minutes, but then they started to move very fast to all directions. They stayed on the sky for about a half and an hour, and they changed their positions very fast a lot of times. Finally, they dissapeared. I'm so sorry because I don't have any photo of them.
