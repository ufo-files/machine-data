{rjl} Noticed lights in sky that didn't make sense. Definitely not a plane or helo.

Been witnessing this object for some time. Have witnesses that have seen it. Watched the object through binocs twice. The lights seem to circle the object changing from red and green and add white after a while. One night while observing through binocs it emitted a white beam. We think it may be a govt. drone but not sure. Several of us witnessed a bright white light fly just above our house at an inedible speed one night that was also reported in other towns the same night hundreds of miles from us. The next day the local reporters and papers said it was a meteorite. Not so! I have no idea what this object is I've been watching, but it seems to be 'hovering' over Formosa Plastics alot and south east of our home. Are we crazy?? I know what a satellite, plane, and helo looks like at night...and this is not one of those. Like I said, I'm not the only one to see it. And it's way too low to be a star. I wouldn't think a drone would have lights? Not looking for fame, just want an explanation.
