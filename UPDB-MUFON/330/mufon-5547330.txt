Strange bright orb like light that slowly dissipated into small dim lights.

Last night at around 10:30 PM CST, I was in my back yard situating things in preparation for the anticipated storms on 6/2. While gathering toys, I took note of a lone star in the NE sky. A few moments later, I noticed 2 "stars" in that same area, but quickly realized the second star was bigger, brighter and moving. It was moving at about the same rate of a commercial plane, but there were no beacon lights and it was traveling on a course not often traveled by aircraft. Out here, most commercial aircraft travel East/West or West/East, but almost never North/South as this was. 

This craft appeared to be heading South or South East. What was strange, the large 1orb like1 light slowly dissipated and then two very faint lights were visible - one appeared to be red the other blue. This was NOT an aircraft turning off the landing lights, as they didn't quickly fade out. This was a process were the light slowly reduced in size over about 3-5 seconds. Once the "main" light was out, I was able to track the object across the sky for a few more moments, but it was very hard to see it continuously. Every few seconds I could pick back up on the movement a little further south in the sky. I ran to get my camera and when I returned I was unable to pick up the movement anywhere. Not sure what it was, but it really didn1t look like a commercial aircraft for many reasons. Just thought I'd share. Thanks!!
