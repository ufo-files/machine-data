{tjd} Stationary white light with one beam downward, then three beams upward.

This occurred on Sunday,October 7, 2018 at about 7:26 pm. I live in the Outer Sunset District of San Francisco, CA. I just finished dinner and stepped out on the front porch,facing south, for a bit of fresh air and take a look at Mars. Being a sky watcher, that is normal for me. However, this time, I noticed a long shaft of light coming from this large white light. My first impression was an airplane light beam, but this looked unusually different, yet beautiful! Mars was far to its left. I took my small binocular and could not see a structured object, only this bright light with the beam casting downward and slanting toward the left. Nothing was moving until very slowly it sailed to the right. I hurried to grab my 7 x 35 binocular and saw, again, no solid structure. However, by now three light beams were directed upward! One in the middle of the light and two longer beams on the side; slanting outward. I was mind boggled as I watched it finally moving very slowly south and disappeared behind the apartment across the street. The pretty beams of white light were still in the air. I took no photos, since my camera batteries were dead.

I know aircraft and this was not a plane. My house is under the flight paths of San Francisco International and I see planes all the time. This experience left me speechless and I was beside myself for the rest of the night, nor could I sleep. I know that other witnesses must have seen this as there is a traffic lane on our Great Highway. So, in reporting this, other witnesses may come forward knowing that they are not alone.
