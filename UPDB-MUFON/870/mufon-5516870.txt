{dr}Two very dim lights hovering, and then travelling S to N. Dramatic changes in speed, color and brightness.

I was walking through our field, and listening to my ipod when I noticed two very dim lights in the sky. Im not sure why I even noticed their presence honestly. A moment later the two lights began to move south to north at what appeared to be a very high altitude, judging from the nearby aircraft visible at the time. One of the lights turned red while the other became noticeably brighter. Both objects seem to slow for a moment, but at that moment I ran for the house to try to get a second witness. Unfortunately I wasnt able to locate the objects when I returned with a second person and had to play the whole thing off as shooting stars to avoid ridicule.
