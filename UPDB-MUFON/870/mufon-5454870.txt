When I noticed it I was running to the gas station. Took a video, sent it to my boyfriend. Phone was at 15% battery. When I got back (not even 10 mins later) I flipped my phone screen to the front facing camera since it was behind me and sent him another video. Phone was at 14% battery. In the less than 1 minute drive to our driveway after I sent the second video, my phone died. He came out with me and we watched it. He took some pics. We walked to the end of the street by the highway and the entire perspective changed. If you're into art, you know the further things are away from you the closer they look together and as you get closer they look further apart. This is backwards. You'll see that in the pics, there's a telephone pole and a large pine type tree for reference. We walked back. Noticed it changed again. So we walked on the same side of the street we were standing on while by the highway taking pics. My boyfriend's battery was at 28%. Walking back the second time his phone just powered down. He turned it back on right away and the battery was at 15%. When we got back to our driveway he took a picture and then it died completely. He went in to put his phone on the charger and bring me my phone (which I ran in to charge as soon as I got home) and I stayed watching it. You can see the big dark pine tree in the pics. In the short time he was inside it dropped lower and moved behind the pine so I kept having to move so I could still see it. He came out in time to see it drop behind the main tree line. We walked back down again but it was so far down we could only catch glimpses between the trees and not enough to get another pic of.
