A thin, hard to observe line with some thickness.

On 5-16-2021 at approximately 11:30 pm I was sitting in my backyard watching the sky. I noticed off in the distance I could see what looked like a long line slightly lighter than the background sky. I could only observe it in my peripheral vision. At first, I didn’t notice any movement however, after a few seconds I discovered it was moving in an Easterly direction. 

For several minutes I thought I was experiencing some kind of illusion caused by an unknown natural event. My impression was it was a reflection from an overhead powerline. (But it wasn’t). I continued to monitor it alternating between direct and peripheral vision. 

It kept moving until in was in direct line of sight. At that time, I noticed I could now see it by looking directly at it. It appeared to be over a mile away, perhaps as much as 10. If I were to have put my hand up for comparison, the object would be about 3” long. This would make it exceptionally large. 
I watched it for a total of maybe 3 – 4 minutes until it was blocked by my neighbor’s house. I never saw any navigational lights; nor could I hear any sound. It was too dark and too far away to see any details. It continued at a moderate speed (Perhaps the same as conventional aircraft) and didn’t make any unusual or extreme movements. 

While I have been an avid sky watcher for over 60 years, I have never noticed anything like this before. It definitely was not a traditional aircraft. My best guess was this might have been Space X’s Starlink satellites. It did not appear to be a line of lights, but rather a single object. That being said, it was faint and some distance so I am not sure.
