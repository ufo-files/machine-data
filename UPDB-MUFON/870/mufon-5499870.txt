{jd} Left Girl friends house traveling home saw blinding light lost around 1 hour of time

Durring high school I would travel about 25 mins to and from my girlfriends house every evening. on week nights i was required to be home no later than 22:00. Therefore i would leave no later than 21:10 to ensure i was home in timely manner. One evening (around August i Believe) I was traveling south bound on Rt 62 Graham Station Rd. around "Brinker Holow" when all of a sudden i was forced to come to a screeching halt due to a blinding white light filled my windshield. i wasn't able to see anything other than the light. i remember having to close my eyes due to the brightness. next thing i remember im about two miles from where the sighting and my phone is ringing. i answered it and it was my father. he was yelling at me wanting to know where i had been. i was very confused because i was on time, or so i thought i looked at the clock as i started to argue with him and the time was in fact about 23:15. at that point i was convinced my self that i in fact left late without realizing it. once i too the lashing from my father over the phone i saw that i had message after message from my girl friend and at this point she was accusing me of cheating. i waited until i got home and swore that my phone had in fact died and i couldn't find the charger and had just got it charged. i was so confused although never thought of it as a ufo sighting. honestly the sighting wasn't even very prevalent in my mind until a few weeks down the road. i then started day dreaming of the event and more and more came to me. now today im having dreams that i contribute to that night on rt 62. what i saw that night had to be from elsewhere.
