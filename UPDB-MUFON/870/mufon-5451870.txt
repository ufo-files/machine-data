Hovering Object sending out beam of light.

I was on my way to work at about 5:25AM. I turned right off of Hwy 441, onto Homer Rd. when I noticed a light in the sky that appeared to be shooting a beam of light towards the ground. Within 20 seconds I pulled the car over to take a picture. I took two pictures. One with the flash on accident, and another without. The object continued to hover. I watched for about five minutes, but was late for work so I eventually moved on. The object appeared to be roundish from my point of view, however, when I zoom in on the picture I can see that the beam of light is elongated, and possibly saucer or cigar shaped. This is very near I-85. Over the six years I've been with my job, I've had numerous sightings of unknown objects on or near I-85, between Lavonia, GA and Commerce, GA. My co-worker also took an amazing picture of an object that she says she sees regularly. With her permission, I've attached her photos as well. These were taken within a few miles of Commerce, GA, again very near I-85.
