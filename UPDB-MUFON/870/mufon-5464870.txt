{tjd} I had called the day before and greys came. I had a book on the occult.

I was in bed around 11pm-12mn had been asleep when about a dozen small greys filed into my room and surrounded me.They levitated me off the bed and through a back window. I recall looking down at the driveway as I moved over the yard and then I saw a hovering saucer craft above me with lights around its periphery and an opening in the center that was lit. A light beamed out of it and surrounded me and I began to rise toward the saucer. I then understood I was being taken and became fearful. I called on Jesus and immediately I started to go back down and then across the yard , over the driveway and through the window to my bed. As soon as I was in bed the greys all filed out of my room and they and the craft were gone. I them thanked the Lord for rescuing me and went back to sleep. I disgarded the book on the occult where I read how to call the saucer in.
