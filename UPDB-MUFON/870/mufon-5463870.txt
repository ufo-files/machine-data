{rjl} Hovering in the direction of Serious

I was up early in the kitchen. It was very bright outside, and I thought to look at the moon, the brightness was not coming from a moon, but from a strange star in the SSE sky heading in the direction of the Serious Constellation. It got very bright, I grabbed my iPad and started to take pictures. The light changed from white, to blue green when I tried to zoom in although it didn1t show up well on photos. I watched it until it faded away into the sky. 

After the sighting I felt unnerved by the whole thing. Never saw a light do that before.
