{rjl} Me and multiple witnesses saw three large and bright lights in a triangle shape over lake erie

We were on vacation with family in cabins by Lake Erie. My husband was on the pier fishing and I was sitting on a bench watching. Suddenly he pointed out strange lights in the clouds above us. It looked like three lights in a triangle shape moving in a slow pattern at first then moving differently but still at a slowish pace. We stared at them for a good few minutes. He got somewhat of a video on his phone but it1s dark and mostly hard to see. The lights disappeared and then my mother in law came out and sat by me. We told her about the lights and then they came back for a few seconds. They were dimmer but not in a triangle shape anymore. We only saw one or two lights but she witnessed those with us. My husband is Air Force and we have never seen planes or drones or anything similar to what we were seeing before. We also ruled out spotlights over the lake because of where the lights were located so close to us. There1s sometimes a single spotlight we see but it1s much farther away (across the lake). The next day we were talking to the owners of the cabins because they live right next door and they saw the same thing we did. They have been living there for probably 40+ years and have never seen anything like that either-and living there that long they of course know what spotlights or anything else looks like. Inverting my husband video and zooming in the next day gives us a little more perspective. When the color is inverted you can see the lights movement a little better.
