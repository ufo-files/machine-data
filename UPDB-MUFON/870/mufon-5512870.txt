{nd} 2 Bright red/orange light hovering over the house

My husband was outside when he called me to see a bright red/orange light which looked like a fireball coming towards the house. I stepped outside to see the light as it hovered over the house. Looked around and saw planes. This was not a plane. As the light hovered over the house it made no noise. Followed the light towards the front of the home as it quickly disappeared. Approximately 2 minutes later, a second light appeared. I closely payed attention, no noise once again. The light was moving from south east to western direction. We were close to the light but it was so bright that we could not see any details of the object. The second light moved in the same exact location. At first we thought it was a plane but this was no plane. 

My husband and I did not feel scared at all but we knew that what we saw last night was not common.
