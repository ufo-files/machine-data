{jpg} 5 glowing red orange orbs flying in formation then vanishing

On July 4th 2015 my family and I were outside popping a few ground fireworks , since no aerial fireworks were allowed in our area because of all the woods. After we were done me, my wife, our 3 kids my father and mother and 2 neighbors witness one glowing orb appear in the sky moving slowly right over us, then a second later another appear then another till there were 5 total moving in a straight line very slowly after about 20 seconds the first one to appear vanishes then the others vanish in the same order they appeared while moving in a nw direction.
