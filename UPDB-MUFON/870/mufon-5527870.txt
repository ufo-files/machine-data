STOPPED TO PHOTO ROCK FORMATIONS

MY WIFE AND I WERE ON OUR WAY HOME FROM LAS VEGAS, NV AFTER A BOWLING TOURNAMENT. WE DROVE. STOPPED TO TAKE PHOTOS OF INTERESTING ROCK FORMATIONS. I WAS TAKING THIS PHOTO WHEN THE OBJECT CROSSED MY FIELD OF VISION. IT WAS SO FAST THAT MY CAMERA WAS ONLY ABLE TO CAPTURE THE BLURR IN THE LT SECTION OF MY PHOTO. THERE WAS NO SOUND. NOTHING WE HAVE IS THAT FAST WITHOUT SOUND OR EXHAUST TRAIL. I WAS EXTREMELY IMPRESSED WITH THE TECHNOLOGY OF THOSE THAT IN CONTROL OF THIS CRAFT. IT WAS GONE AFTER I LOOKED IN THE DIRECTION OF IT'S FLIGHT PATH. WOULD LOVE TO SEE THIS CRAFT AGAIN. THERE WAS NO FEAR. ONLY AWE.
