2 unknown cigar/comet shapes seen in western sky from Norman OK sunset

Jan 31 at sunset in western sky over Norman. At first I thought it was two comets in the sky with one being closer and larger looking and one to the right of it that was smaller and farther away appearing. I say they looked like comets because of the dark reddish color they glowed although there was no discernible tail on either and it wasn’t possible to tell which direction they were going. When I photographed them the smaller of the two didn’t show up and the larger showed up as a pale white line looking shape while everything else in the photo showed up the correct color. I first saw them as I was driving to the store and after not being able to get a good photo due to power lines drove home, took these shots, and had my grandmother in law come look at it and verify I wasn’t seeing things. We watched it until it passed over the horizon out of seeing. Very strange and had to of been others.
