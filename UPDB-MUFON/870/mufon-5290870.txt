Small glowing yellow orb in hospital room

I was recovering from a spinal cord injury in Tucson AZ in the first quarter of 2017. During that time, I had a roommate for 2 weeks that claimed he talked to aliens that appear to him in different forms. One night when the curtains were drawn to separate us, he was talking quietly to something. I thought he was crazy. As he was talking, a small orb floated over to my side of the room and back, like it was checking on me. It was a perfect sphere with a glowing corona-like aura that was yellow. I was just confused and curious. I didn’t say anything to my roommate until the next day, and he said the orb was communicating with him and that they were talking about me. The orb told him I would regain 75% of what I lost in my spinal cord injury. I have no idea what to believe, but I know this orb experience drives me crazy, because I want to know what it was! I want to know if my roommate was being truthful or not.
