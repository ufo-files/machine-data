While standing outside my friends house a group of 5 were enjoying the nice cool evening. One of us spotted what appeared to be an orb shaped object moving fast across the sky. There was a flame coming from the top of the craft and was easy to identify as the craft was low and there was no flashing pattern to the light. It was clearly a flame, we all saw it. Shortly after we spotted anther craft identical to the first following shortly after the second one faster than the first as if it was trying to catch up. before the orbs had faded into the night a plane had started to approach the same area where the orbs were but we're gone by the time the plane had made it in the same area
