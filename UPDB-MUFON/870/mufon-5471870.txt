{dr}Six White lights. Hovered 30' above a bridge with a vehicle under it. Casually flew away and descended further into a forest.

I was in my living room preparing to head to my friends house across the street to spend the night. My friend was with me in the living room but went outside before me. I heard him frantically calling my name and to "get my ass out here". When I left my house he was about 200' away at the entrance to our subdivision. I ran to him. He pointed at the craft and said "Don't ever forget this." I immediately knew it was a UFO in the traditional sense. It hovered 30' over a bridge in a field adjacent to our subdivision that was not yet developed. There was a car parked under the vehicle with it's headlights on. The craft had 6 white lights and just hovered there. I cant recall if it spun or not. About 30 seconds after watching the craft hover it descended to the East into more dense forest at a very slow rate of speed. The vehicle then drove away the opposite direction.My friend and I ran to his garage to grab a baseball bat and golf club and into the forest to find it. We found nothing. The next morning we described the event to my friends mother. She accused us of lying. She thought we had read the newspaper from the day prior our sighting which described the craft had been sighted in our County.The descriptions were identical. I have tried to locate the article in the local newspaper (The Macomb Daily) past issues but they are not available online prior 1994. The event occurred in 1992. I'm hoping that by reporting this maybe the driver of the vehicle or anyone that may have read the article would have more info.
