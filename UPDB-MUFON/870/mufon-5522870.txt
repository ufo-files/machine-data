Tonight while walking the dog saw this very bright object, sphere-shaped, brighter than Jupiter. Moving the exact same course as before. Whipped out the flip-cam and filmed it as it crossed the sky.

Silent. No navigation lights. Not an airplane and not a satellite. Ive observed satellites before with my eyes alone, through binoculars and through my night vision scope. WAY too bright to be a satellite. Not descending. No characteristic flashing of meteor grazing the atmosphere. No sonic boom. 

Lost sight of the object at the tree-line.
