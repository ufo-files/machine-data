500' DISTANCE BRIGHT WHITE LIGHT PULSED COLORS

I camping, there is nothing manmade in that area I saw the light. I came outside of my tent to tinkle at 5:30 am. I saw a bright light and thought "That's a low airplane." I did my deed then felt wide awake, so I sat near the low ambers of the fire from the night before. I looked up again and noticed this super white LED like light had not moved at all. It was so close 300-500' upward. After starring at it for at least 5 minutes. I noticed slight auras coming off, blue, red, yellows but mostly white. There was no sound other than crickets. I'm thinking there's not a street lamp over there? I pulled out my phone to record and take photos. I had a sense of calm and to this day believe it told me to go back to sleep. As to this day I'm still baffled that I didn't wake friends in their tents, something I would of done. Why I put myself back to bed instead of staying longer? It was in the same exact spot after 15 min when I went back inside my tent. I woke up feeling like I had internal hemorrhoids that affected me for several days thereafter. I felt too embarrassed to really share or empathize what I experienced with about 15 friends that camped. I was shocked at what I saw when I cropped images later that morning. The next night I woke up in the middle of the night again, but too scared to peak outside of the tent and haven't ever returned to that great camping spot.
