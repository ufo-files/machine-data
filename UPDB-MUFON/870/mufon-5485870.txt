{cak} Multiple bright lights transformed light emission randomly and a perfect triangle of light that was drifting

I went out on the patio to smoke at 11:30 and looked up to admire the stars. I noticed one light was fluttering light, then I observed it making small directional changes and then could tell it was hovering, moving like a drone. Then I noticed at least 4 other lights doing the same thing. They appeared to be at different altitudes and were above me, to the south, the NW, all over. For about 30-45 minutes planes would fly by the brightest light and it would make aggressive moves toward the planes as they flew by. The first few planes appeared to stay there course but the few curved as if to avoid the light. The light also appeared to transform and separate top to bottomwith a solid middle, then change back to one light randomly. The most unusual behavior was that there was a perfect triangle configuration with three lit points that drifted over a couple hours and the one bright light appeared to be drifting with it like it was guarding the triangle. I had trees to reference to gauge the movement. After a couple hours I went inside because I got too cold.
