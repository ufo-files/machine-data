(GS) Triangular shaped silent craft with 4 red flashing lights in back crusing in straight path over my apartment.

My boyfriend went to throw out the trash at around 7:40 and he was taking long to come up. 5 to 10 minutes had passed so I went to the front on my porch upstairs and looked for him. I saw him talking to the neighbor so I started looking in the sky. I notice a lot of planes flying above for like 5 minutes. Then I looked at my boyfriend again and looked up in the sky and saw no planes. Then I saw this dark triangular shaped object slowly fly over my apartment complex, between 2 trees and then out of view. There were 4 blinking red lights on the back of the craft. I was amazed because I heard nothing from it. It was sort of like cruising over. It was in my view for a good minute maybe. So then I ran downstairs to my boyfriend and neighbor and told them what I saw. I asked if they saw anything or heard any plane and they said no, so I know I wasnt trippin when I didn't hear anything come from this thing. It was huge, too huge to be a plane flying that low and silent. The other planes I saw flying around were higher up and their size didn't compare. I got this adrenaline running thru me when I saw it, like, am I really seeing this. I am amazed still, like 2 hours later. Ive attached a drawing (very poor drawing)of what I saw.
