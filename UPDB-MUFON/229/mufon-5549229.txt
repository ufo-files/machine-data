Observed bright round shaped object in sky moving fast and dissapear behind the full moon.

While patroling the parking lot last night at the Holiday Inn in Barrie I looked up in the sky to observe the full moon. I observed a bright object circular in shape moving rapidly across the sky from North to South almost in line with the moon it then dissapear behind the moon.The time was about 05:04am I waited for some time and did not see this object reappear in any direction. It was too high to be a aircraft or the space station that i do believe past by later on at a lower degree to the moon and it was not nearly as bright as the circular object I had observed.
