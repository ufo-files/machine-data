{nd} Thought I was looking at stars, one flashed very bright and then was gone.

I went out in my yard early in the morning while still dark to listen for Elk bugles. I was looking up at the stars because I live in the country and have no light contamination. Looking at a small cluster of stars when one flashed like a camera flash, then it was gone. The object was in space, it was not close.
