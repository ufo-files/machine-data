{dr}UFO spotted in Bridgeport WV 10/27/14

Driving through downtown Bridgeport between 8 and 9 pm on October 27th 2014 when noticed a UFO like object hovering over the Bridgeport Fire Dept. or slightly behind it, the object was grey in color triangle shaped and had 3 lights closest to each tip the lights were white. It appeared to be larger then a golfball but smaller than a basketball. It then flew off and headed towards the Grafton WV area. Radio in car was on so were not able to hear any sounds.
