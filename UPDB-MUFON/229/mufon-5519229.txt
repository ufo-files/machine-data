wife and I saw two green lights above us

My wife and I was laying on the trampoline in the back yard watching for the meteor shower and she said look look. I figured it was a meteor but when I looked I observed two dim green glowing lights going backwards and forwards. It made no sound that we heard and it was only a couple hundred feet in the air. We watched it for about 30 secs until the lights was gone. I never could make out an object just the two lights which I figured was the bottom of the craft since it went directly above us. The lights seem to be oval shape with black waves through them. Glowing dimly and pulsating.
