Grey upside down teardrop shape dull metallic

It was a large grey dull upside down teardrop shape object observed while we were driving and was hovering near art. 1 in Middletown, DE on April 24, 2021. At first my husband thought it was a hot air balloon but it was completely closed in with no windows or anything. It was just hovering up in the sky around the tree top line. I noticed all the other cars on the road didn’t stop or break or anything. I felt fear of the object for some reason. It was strange and I really want people to know about it.
