{nd} 5 blinking red orbs moved at different velocities high above a mountain range.

I walked out of my house which is approximately 1 mile from blue mountain, which is a strip of mountain along the Appalachian mountain range. I looked up in the sky and saw a few blinking red lights. Myself and two othes witnessed this. I watched as a few red lights turned into 5. The highest one was changing direction while one in the back caught up and stopped right beside another red orb. These objects were changing speeds and direction independent of one another. I have it all recorded on my cell phone.
