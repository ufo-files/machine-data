{rjl} Huge V (assume triangle) over codo building no sound no motion

I was walking dog late (fell asleep earlier) across the street from Condo building (40 floors) There was snow storm in progress. I looked up and above the building saw a huge V with a bright light at the tip and two bright lights (all round) on the underside under the blowing snow... I did not stay and watch what it did as it was very late (Jan 21-22 midnight) and very cold. I went to the doorman and told him and he said someone else had told him the same thing.
