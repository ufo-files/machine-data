traveling home from work

Myself and a friend of mine was returning home from work on 3/8/2012 around 1030pm we noticed an object off in the distance that appeared to be a white light. We thought that it was to bright to be an cell tower and appeared to be hoovering. As we got closer it began to move towards us as it got closer we noticed that it was a diamond shape and had red, green, and white lights on it, it also made no noise as it passed over us. We have seen this for the past week or so around the same time of night and same location ,we are just curious as to what we are seeing
