{td} Incerted triangleof hovering then moving light balls.

At start, I was sitting on my couch watching tv and the front door was open for ventilation. I saw a light in an otherwise black sky. Initially only observed a hovering ball of light, thought it was a star but it shifted side to side frequently. After a few minutes, two smaller craft joined formation in an upside down triangle. All 3 moved around slightly but kept the formation and slowly moving in a northeast direction. Hovering, twinkling, shifting. At one point a plane or helicopter flew toward them but changed course quickly. Due to light pollution, no videos could be taken and only one crude photograph. The objects eventually faded from view. I was shocked when they first started moving and kept observing. My feelings are of shock and bewilderment at the possibility of having just had a legitimate ufo experience.
