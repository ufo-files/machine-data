July 4th,2009 approximately 10:45pm
After observing the Bethlehem, PA area fireworks display, my 2 guests and husband were sitting on the back deck of our home. My sister-in-law was the first to observe the crafts, and called my husband's, her brother's, attention to it. My husband then came to our back door to get my attention. I walked out on the back deck, and saw the crafts immediately. The four of us stood there shocked for several minutes. I ran out front, through the house, but the crafts were not there. The crafts had an aura around them with a single red light in the middle of their belted centers; in the area similar to a belt buckle. The crafts flew slowly, but "chased" after one another like children playing tag, and much lower in the sky than an airplane or the stars. The UFOs proceeded to fly off in a East South Eastern direction. We were all stunned and scared by what we had never before experienced. I/we am/are now a believer/s!!!!!!!!!!
