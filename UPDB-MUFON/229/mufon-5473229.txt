Went to observe the heavens with new Bushnell Night vision camera. The object wasn't visible with the naked eye. We still aren't sure of what we were seeing. On July 10th at approximately 10:40 we had observed a spherical object that blended in with the stars; it was as if it were see through. The shape of that prior object was like looking at a sphere with a rotating ring around it. The outer ring rotated in a clockwise direction, while the sphere rotated in a counter clockwise direction. There were structure's on the object that were clearly mechanized. The object we witnessed on September 30th was also spherical. both objects also had given off no sound. The July object had flown over our heads while we were outside our home.

The object of September 30th behaved in an erratic manner. It changed direction suddenly; it also hovered when I pointed a laser toward it. The feelings we experienced was one of wanting to study it more.
