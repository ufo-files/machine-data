{cak} Brilliant, almost florescent orange - appeared to be rotating with cone like protrusions entire circumference

The above orb, ball object entered my bedroom window frame from south to north (I have a 1,000 ft panoramic view of downtown Cincinnati about 3 dues west miles out of downtown skyline, object flew at horizontal at around 1,500 ft - looked to be traveling at 100 miles per hour and decelerated to a dead hoover, north east view for me - over the valley in between downtown and my high rise residence - appeared to be about one mile away - had to be as big as a school bus. It was absolutely brilliant orange glow and rotating - after hoovering for maybe 5-10 seconds, it began to travel northeast, moving away from me and dimming until it disappeared completely. I am a lay person science nut, read theoretical physics, L. Suskind, Lawrence Krause, etc, cosmology junkie, an atheist and a skeptic. Only the 1997 Phoenix Lights intrigued me - but this was like nothing I've seen - it wasn't a meteor or firework, certainly not a drone or aircraft - I've seen plenty. This had no trail and trajectory was 90 degrees to surface - no descent or ascent - but velocity, 100 mph to 0 - then changed direction - at hoover, I ran to get to get my smart phone - had the flash on which reflected in the glass window - and by the time I adjusted everything it had already dimmed - I chose to run to the window first so I wouldn't miss or lose any personal observation - All I can say is WOW!
