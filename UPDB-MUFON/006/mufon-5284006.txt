Seen Over Raleigh

Driving around Raleigh after eating some dinner. 
I saw something that looked like 5 shooting stars. 
I was excited and they were moving very quickly. Then they turned and bunched close together. They then appears to turn into one large object with lights underneath. 
I thought it was airplanes for a second but it hovered in one spot for a little bit while my parter, kids, and I looked at it and tried to drive back around to see it.
