{rjl} Driving down from Asheville with the Franklin Ford dealership to our right. Saw mercury/chrome like triangle, super reflective.

When we were coming towards the Ford Dealership, we saw a weird object maybe 200 feet above the ground hovering, it was extremely close, and had like a chrome/liquid kind of mercury like reflectivity to it. It was almost like it was invisible due to how reflective it was, but it was definitely a triangle type shape. It had strange looking hieroglyphs on the side of it. I swear this is true, I could not take a picture of it with the camera phone. It would switch from video to camera every time I would click to take a picture and then back again. We were in shock. It then shot away shortly afterwards. I understand this is here-say, but some friends told me about a story of a mutual friend and his mom seeing a ufo right near the ford dealership many years earlier. Don't know what to make of it all.
