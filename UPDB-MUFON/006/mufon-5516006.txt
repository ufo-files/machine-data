A LONG TRAIL OF VERY BRIGHT LIGHT MOVING AT A PNEOMENAL SPEED

i HAD JUST COME OUT OF MY CONDO TO WALK MY GERMAN SHEHERD. I LIVE ON THE NORTHEASTERN SIDE OF BOGOTA AGAINST THE MOUNTAIN. HE STOPPED TO SMELL A PARTICULARLY ATTRACTIVE SPOT OF GRASS AND I JUST HAPPENED TO BE LOOKING AT THE SKY OVER BOGOTA.

A SHARP BRILLIANT LIGHT SHOT DOWN FOR A VERY SHORT DISTANCE, TURNED SHARPLY TO THE RIGHT AND THEN HEADED WHAT SEEMED A NW DIRECTION AT A PHENOMENAL SPEED LEAVING A BRILLIANT TRAIL OF LIGHT BEHIND.

ON THE FRONT PART OF THIS BLAZING LIGHT THERE SEEMED TO BE A SPHERICAL SHAPE OF AN AQUA COLOR AND BORDERED IN THE FRONT BY A DARKER BLUISH GREEN.

ALL OF A SUDDEN EVERYTHING DISAPPEARDED AT THE SAME TIME. jUST AS IF NOTHING HAD BEEN THERE AT ALL. IT JUST HAPPENED SO FAST THAT i KNOW THAT IF I HADN'T BEEN LOOKING AT THE SKY AT THAT PARTICULAR MOMENT I NEVER WOULD HAVE SEEN IT.

I WAS MISTIFIED! I KNOW I HAVE NEVER SEEN ANYTHING LIKE IT AND I COULD FIND NO REASONABLE EXPLANATION FOR WHAT I HAD JUST SEEN.

I WAS A MARINE STATIONED AT THE MARINE AIR BASE OF CHERRY POINT, N.C. IN 1965 AND I HAVE SEEN ALL KINDS OF AIRCRAFT FLYING DURING THE NIGHT HOURS. NOTHING LIKE THIS.

MY REACTION WAS TO MAKE A DRAWING OF WHAT I HAD JUST SEEN AND EMAILED IT TO MY SONS
