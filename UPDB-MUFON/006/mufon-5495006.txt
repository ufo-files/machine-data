We live in Columbia, MO. At about 22:30hrs my son noticed what he discribed as what looked like a super nova. An object that brightened and then faded out in the NW sky. He pointed out the area to me and we watched it for about 15 to 20sec. The object then appeared again, closer to us but still in the NW sky. It looked like a star. When it faded the second time my son said that he thought it moved off in a NE direction after it faded. We were both excited and would of liked to film something but the whole event took less than a minute.
