{tjd} 24 hour time-lapse video of full moon on Easter with 2 moons https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y5L2kNu8bYs

On Easter Weekend, I set up an ipad using an acrylic cook book holder to stand it up for a 24 hour time lapse video to film the full moon with rising tide. I also filmed it upside down but found a program to flip the video right side up. That is when I saw the flash of two moons zip off the screen and my first thought that is could be a lens flare. Then I discovered how to make a you tube video - never done this before - and take snap shots of the 2 moons so I could analyses frame by frame. Then I posted is the video of the two moons on Easter on you tube. Yes, there is a large military installation near by.
