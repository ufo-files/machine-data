Felt compelled to take a picture of the moon that morning and noticed a light between the moon and morning star.

I like taking pictures of the moon, and stars. And this morning I had a weird feeling to go take a picture of the moon. Between the morning star and the moon, I noticed a new light. I assumed maybe it was a plane or helicopter but I wanted to get a good picture of whatever it was anyway. This time it didn’t seem to be a plane…or a helicopter. The light on the ground is our local quarry, and I cannot tell if this is hovering above the quarry or miles away from it. But wherever it is, it was large. I tried to get a snapshot of it after the video when the sun began to rise, but when I focused and shot the picture all I got was a blur and then it was completely gone. I love watching the skies and have been waiting for this forever, but I’m still not satisfied with this and will keep looking lol. I want to one day get a completely clear shot of something fantastic. The included attachments are of the video I took, the photo I took of it before it vanished and screenshots of it after I zoomed into the video and tried to investigate it myself.
