Saw and large silver object in the sky different from a star, no movement. From it sprang 4 smaller objects off in all directions.

In Maine, looking toward the West at nighttime while walking the dog on a clear night. The object hovered; it was in space, not near the Earth. I stared at it for a few minutes because it was silver, appeared metallic. It burst with 4 other, small objects that went off to what we would describe as NESW, but in space, I don't know the language to use. The "Mother" ship stayed for a second more and flew off.
