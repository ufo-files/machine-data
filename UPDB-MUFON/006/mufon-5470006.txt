Black triangle pass in west east direction

Got on the balcony and noticed up a comet like iregular trace, then it appeard from nowhere black triangle about the size of 2 jumbo jets, or a playground, without any sound and passed from west to east in direct. It was so black, darker then the night sky, trianglular form with orange lights alongside the edges. No lights in the middle. Light had no any glow they have been so straight like comeing out of a tube. The event lasted about 2 seconds, the hight of the object could have been about 500 meters, like the airplane in the landing and the speed maybe twice. The first tought was if sides of triangle were the same and so it appeared. It was not an airplane as they do not have lights alongside, it had no body. 

Next day have called the civil air traffic control to check, they said there were no any flights in the area at that time.
