Triangular, object possibly cloaked, blocked out the stars, 5 very dim red lights

I was in my girlfriends driveway, replacing a speaker in my car after my work shift. It was very cool that night, and the sky was quite clear. I was watching the sky periodically as I worked, I had seen 2 shooting stars and was watching for more, when I seen a strange movement on the horizon. I was trying very hard to figure out what would be flying so low at that time in the morning (approx. 3am). It flew in my general direction, and as It came closer I realized this wasn't one light but had unveiled itself as 5 very very dimly lit red lights (similar to the red light in the cab of a police car while trying to be stealthy). This object was moving @ the same elevation as the surrounding mountain tops. Couldn't see a chassis or frame, but the object blocked out the stars it moved under and the lights were in a triangular formation. It was dead silent. Couldn't hear any bugs or crickets because it was chilly and nothing was very active outside. The object flew over my head and continued moving towards town. This encounter took about 15 seconds, possibly more. My blackberry was broken so I wasn't able to snap a picture. I was very much in awe following the event, but suddenly a feeling of dread overcame me. I woke my girlfriend immediately, told her what had happened, and decided I was done and laid in bed wide awake for hours after. Very crazy experience.
