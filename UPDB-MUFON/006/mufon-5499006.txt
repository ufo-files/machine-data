This is the first time using a computer so please excuse my mistakes. a summers evening around 9pm I was with a friend sitting on a hill at a park looking at thestars. We saw 3 bright stars high in the sky over Lake Erie.They moved in zigzag path, we watched them for about 20 minutes. It was time to head back home. The walk home was through empty streets.This area was full of factory buildings, 5 to 6 storys high. I felt like someone was watching me,I looked up and saw a huge copper color object hovering about 10 feet above the 6 floor building. There was no wind or sound,no odor.There were rectangular windows around it. I tried to see if I could see inside but it was just black inside. Im looking at it thinking if they take us up in this my mother is not going to believe me and I will be late getting home. So we started to run home I had my father come outside to see it. By this time the 3 were high up in the sky over downtown. Now they just looked like stars but moving crazy patterns. I have played this over and over in my head and told many people the story. I feel blessed to have seen this
