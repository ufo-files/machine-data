{cak} bright red-orange light, much larger than the front light of a plane, slow moving, much like a fire ball. it moved past the sky

I walked out of my house and walked 20 feet to my car then saw a light in the night sky. Thought for a moment it was the headlights of a plane, like Ive seen before but it was much larger than that. If I held my hand up, it would be about the size of my pinky nail, and did not have any blinking/flashing lights that I could tell. The object seemed very high up. It was red-orange. It did not seem solid, like it was a fire ball. moving from the north-west to the east. lost it after it moved past the tree line. then less than a minute later a second one appeared right where the first one was spotted(same look). watched this one go past in the same path as the last one then it seemed to stop and began dimming to a faint red color and vanished shortly before reaching the tree line in the distance. I was kind of alarmed when the second one showed up and I waited for a 3rd for a couple of minutes but I had to get to work.
