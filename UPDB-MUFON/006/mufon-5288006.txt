Triangular object approximately 500 feet above us. Driving at idle speed object would turn as we turned left and right.

Triangular shaped object approximately 500 feet above the vehicle that my best friend and I were in. We were driving slowly around our home town of Buffalo ND when for some unexplainable reason I decided to put my head out the passenger side window and look up. I saw a very large triangular shaped object with very large curved corners . It traveled at the speed  as our vehicle and would rotate left or right duplicating our left and right turns in the vehicle. Observed for nearly 5 minutes. It had no sound or lights. It was light Gray and each of the 3 sides was approximately 400 feet in length.
