{jpg} UFO sighting 1969

I was looking out the window into the sky when suddenly in the middle of my viewing area appeared an object. It was saucer shaped and it seemed to me made of a material that glowed, or gave off a light (different from being lit). It hovered for a few moments then moved very fast, very quiet in a reverse "s"" shape. I lost sight of it because of a tree in the way. My overall impression as soon as I saw it was that I never saw anything like it, it was radiating a light and it was close enough I should hear it but it was quiet. Also I never saw anything move is such a tight space so smooth and so fast.
