UFO seen following a plane at very high speeds. It glowed a reddy/orange colour. Very surreal.

On the 9th of August 2011, at roughly 21:50, i observed what seemed to be a UFO. I was in my bedroom looking out of the window into the sky and i noticed a plane fly by at regular altitude and speed. Just as it escaped from my line of sight, a red/orange, pulsating/blinking object appeared out of nowhere at roughly the same altitude but it's speed was nearly UNBELIEVEABLE. It must have been travelling atleast 4 or 5 times quicker than the plane that just flew by. 2000-2500 mph would be a rough estimate of it's speed. It looked as if it followed the exact same flight path of the plane (South East). It flew in a straight line as if it was chasing the plane for some reason. It flew in a way that seemed too perfect. It felt like a dream or like i was imagining it. I dismissed it like it meant nothing but earlier on today (10th of August 2011) my friend informed me that he also saw a UFO at the same time on the same day. I told him to describe everything about it and from what i gathered from him, it must have been the same light i saw the previous night. I realised i had to report it to clear my mind but i know i'll probably never know the truth...
