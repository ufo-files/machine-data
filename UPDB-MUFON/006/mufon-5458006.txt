{rjl} Tic Tac hovering right below the edge of a cloud.

As I was leaving work on Saturday February 9, 2019, at 8:20 am, I saw a "tictac" shaped object hovering right below a cloud to the NE of a warehouse. As I got closer, I pulled into a parking lot, made a u-turn and parked looking straight ahead but at a side view of it. I tried to get a picture of it but it was to high for my phone to pick it up. I thought about getting out and trying, but since it was only 22 degrees out I knew there was no way I could hold my hands still enough and not shake. I watched for aproximately 4-5minutes and then proceeded on. I drove about another 1/2 mile and turned up a side road to get a different view of it.(This was as if you were looking at the end of a tictac). It was just stationary, all white, no widows or wings. After a few more minutes and then really thinking about it I got the "willies" and drove home.
