I went outside to get the mail when I saw about 5 to 10 lights flashing very fast and very close together above the tree tops a few miles away, towards the East.They would flash very fast about 4 times, then another would flash. They were very close together. I tried to video them but couldn't see the lights on the screen. I went inside to get my wife's phone hoping it would pick them up and when I came out the lights were gone. Some clouds or fog was moving in so they were probably just covered up.
