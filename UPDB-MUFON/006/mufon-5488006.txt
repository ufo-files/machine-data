{rjl} Two white orbs seen flying slowly in tandem SE over Winter Park, then Orlando

I was playing Frisbee around 4:40 p.m. Sunday, Oct. 11, 2015, at Blue Jacket Park in a large field in Orlando. My Frisbee buddy said, "What are those?" and I turned around and looked north-northeast toward Winter Park and saw two white orbs against a cloudless blue sky, perhaps a mile away, maybe 1,000-2,000 feet up and moving slowly in tandem (perhaps 50-100 mph, like a very slow airplane's speed) to the ESE. We watched for perhaps 10 minutes till the orbs reached the edge of sight on the ESE horizon, probably over Orlando's Avalon Park area at that point, headed in general direction of Cape Canaveral. My iPhone and my friend's iPhone were in our cars a few hundred yards away, and we did not get them for fear the orbs would be gone by the time we had the phones.
