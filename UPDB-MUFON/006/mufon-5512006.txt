{dr}Bright red flashes of light that lit up the whole sky

Myself and a friend were outside in my backyard working. It had become dark outside by the time we were done. As we talked there was a sudden bright red flash of light that lit up the sky facing west. About 5 minutes went by and there was another bright red flash like the first one then another bright red flash about a min later. The flashes were very bright and filled the whole skyline to the west.
