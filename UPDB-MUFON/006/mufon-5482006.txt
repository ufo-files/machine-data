I have witnessed naziz trust me they exist. Now i want to tell someone who deals in this matter. They are among us. I even know the name of their new commander. They eat human flesh.

I was barely 5 years old. As we were a middle class family, i was sleeping in my garden on a wooden plank. Suddenly in midnight i was waken up by some humming noise. My father was working on a night shift. My mother was in a deep sleep. I observed 3 light orbs in the sky. They were circuling around. I was paralised by fear. Suddenly something lifted me above the ground. Absorbed into the darkness in the middle of three orbs. My eyes opened and i regained consiousness inside that vehicle only to find myself on a disection table with a nazi officer laughing and saying something in german or some other language. What i noticed was a white face, an ss insignia on arm, and a very big injection in his hands. A doctor like person was also standing besides him with red eyes. I couldnt move myself. Later i dont know why they let me live left me where they picked me and last thing i remember is that the aircraft slowly disappeared in night sky. I can still feel them. Later in my life when i came to study i read about haunabeau then i realized that they were germans. How i know their leader name it is another story.
