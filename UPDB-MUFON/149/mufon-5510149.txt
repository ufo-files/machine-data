{dr}Acorn shape dull metal with windows hoovering for 30 min

On 6/17/13 at 9:20 was looking up at sunset when a bright object in the night sky caught my attention. At first glance it looked like a star; but the object appeared much closer. Object had an erratic twinkle effect. It wasn1t until I looked through my rifle scope that I was able to determine that this object was not a star. While looking through the scope I was able to see a pewter colored craft that was classic 19501s shaped saucer/acorn. The craft had about 8 illuminated windows and was hovering for about 25 minutes until it took off in a NE direction towards Thomaston/ Litchfield Ct.
