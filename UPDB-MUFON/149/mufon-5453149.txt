Observed a bright light that hovered and had rapid short movements. Observed a smaller light exit and interact with another bright light.

Around 10 Pm, I went to the front door to make sure it was locked. I looked out the window and observed a very bright light just above the tree top. It was white and sparkled. There were no other lights associated that would indicate a plane or helicopter. I was facing the NW direction. It was stationary and then it moved slightly. The movement was sudden as was the stop. I called my wife who also observed it. It continued to make slight movements in a NW direction. At one point, it seemed to be coming closer to my position. After around 15 minutes, it began to move further away. At that point, I observed what looked like a smaller light exit the larger light. It moved in a southerly direction. Another bright light appeared and the small light circled it. The second bright light moved to a position that was below the first light. The small light moved from one to the other. It also would vanish and reappear. I then stopped observing as the lights went below my observation.
