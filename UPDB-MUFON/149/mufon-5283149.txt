Comet shaped gray object changed shape, hovered for minutes then dissolved.

Gray comet shaped object seemed large in the sky, I kept looking at it to determine what it was. Have seen many planes, blimps, balloons, smoke, and other objects in the sky near Nellis airforce base, but this baffled me. How could a smoke residue cloud get denser after hovering for minutes???
