{dr}Looked like a plane or an asteroid was about to crash

My mom and I were driving on the freeway where the 210 meets the 118 in the San Fernando Valley of Los Angeles, CA. In the sky we noticed what appeared to be a plane landing (it was dark out, we just saw a white light), but we noticed the object was moving FAST. Too fast. I didn't think much of it...but then a trail of what looked like flames burst out behind it. My mom and I gasped thinking we were about to witness a plane or asteroid crash land. The flame quickly went out and the object went back to looking like a white/blue circular light. It was then out of our sight.
