Object in Nasca

When I was in Nasca, I saw for several days, or weeks, what seemed to be a planet, a bit orange. It was perhaps at 45 degrees looking to the Nasca Lines and the sea. I believed it was a planet.
Suddenly, one night, after having seeing it daily, perhaps at 20:30 or more, it descended vertically and made a perfect 90 degrees angle, then horizontal, to the left. Then it made another 90 degrees angle for a vertical descend, until it disappeared at sight because of the trees. I was in Majoro, Nasca, SO of the Nasca Lines.
I saw it doing this for 2 or 3 weeks, approx 3 hours without moving and then descending.
