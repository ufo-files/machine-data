{dr}Starlike Object Moving Across The Sky

While walking to work at a commercial airport, I noticed a bluish/whitish light moving across the night sky from north to south. I was facing west. The time was almost exact to 5:20am when I started taking video.

I took out my Google Pixel phone to record the object. The video is very grainy because I just got the phone and did not learn until today how to adjust the settings. 

The bright object seen at first is the planet Jupiter. I am basing this off Google Sky Maps which I used after the object/light disappeared. As I adjust the video to obtain better focus, you will see a starlight object moving across the night sky. It's not moving very fast. It goes under Jupiter before it disappears.

The object did not display any pulsating red beacon light normally seen on aircraft nor did I view the standard anti-collison/navigation lights. It just remained a constant steady bluish/whitish color.

I don't know if this was a satellite but I do know it's not the ISS. It is not observable for the next several days. The video does not do it justice but as I wrote before, I just got this phone so I am still learning how to use it. I really wish I had the proper settings when I made the video.
