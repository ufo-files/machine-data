5 circle objects in formation one slightly bigger than others

I saw one dark circular object in the bright cloudy sky. When I ran upstairs to look out of the sky light there were 4 smaller ones behind. Dark & solid - appeared heavy or metal. Flying over head from South to North in one steady direction but appeared to be spinning. All 5 were in a clear group but maybe 50 meters apart.
Disappeared into the clouds. 
There were birds in the sky at the time - crows, seagulls, pigeons which put it into perspective that they were not birds.
To dark & solid to be balloons that high in the sky & wouldn’t have stayed in a group/formation unless tied. There was no string or ties.
My phone battery died seconds before I saw it - which was the only reason I saw them as I stopped reading.
On the attached map the direction of flight would have been Allerton to Baildon
