{nd} Taking cloud pics. Noticed object in the frame later

I had parked my vehicle at our assigned parking deck. It was a day after the storm so the sky was cloudy, and I decided to take some pictures with my phone. Since I was facing the sun, I took the pics not really looking, and when onto class.
I noticed the object after I had posted the picture on my Instagram account. I zoomed in and tried to to make out what it was. It's a round, dense circle. I even tweaked the settings, after zooming in, so as to get better resolution on the object. 
I have never seen anything like it in all the years I've been photographing.
