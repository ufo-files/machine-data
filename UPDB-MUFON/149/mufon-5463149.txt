Followed us Stopped overhead when we did Shot off into space in 1.5 s

Driving back from Fredericton saw 3 lights in triangle shape following us. Passenger asked me to pull over. We were on the Peterville Hill, Gsgetown Base. After getting out my passenger pointed up. What do you think that is? It has been following us. We heard noise, felt no air wash, felt no heat, could not see the body of a craft. Three lights hovering.  The lights got bigger it went up, the lights got smaller it came down. It did this several times. A guy stopped curious to see what we were up to.He asked, Did you see a moose? I just pointed up. He was gone like a jack rabbit. The craft did its up and down a couple more. My passenger asked, Do you think it is a helicopter? I explained no noise no wash this close, looks like a UFO. The lights got bigger, joined into one big circle, went up abruptly, made a high orbital arc, and was gone out of sight in about a second.

When I was home I called the Saint John airport to see if anything was over the Peterville Hill. He said he observed nothing. He did not take anyinfo from me.
