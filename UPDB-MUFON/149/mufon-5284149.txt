Multiple small spinning lights converging to make a large object

Evelyne and I were on the terrace of the second story home. Had just finished dinner when we both noticed at the same time, spinning blue and red lights, At first I thought they were drones in formation but no. They were apart from each other then they converged to outline a larger craft in a stacked double diamond pattern. One on top of the other. We watched it for a few minutes before the clouds obscured it. We were looking in a west direction
