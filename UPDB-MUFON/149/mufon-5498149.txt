{dr}Observed two bright lights in distance to North coming towards me, as they approached and flew overhead noticed they were black triangles with light in middle, silent

I was standing out on my deck in Roanoke Va,the evening was cold and clear it was around mid-night in Feb. 2008. I observed two very bright lights in the distance to the North coming towards me. I thought at first they might be helecopters or something, but there was no sound. As they came closer and flew slowly overhead several things struck me as odd. First of all these were two black triangular shaped objects that glided perfectly silent above my house with a very large ball of light in the center of each. This light emintated (as best I can describe) sort of a static type of electricty of different colors. There were no other lights of any type on the objects (such as blinking running lights) normally seen on most aircraft. Again; these craft were perfectly silent and passed directly above me as they moved to the South and dissapeared. I lost sight at that time due to other houses and mountains in the way of my view. I thought it was quite incredable but also realized it could have been some type of stealth aircraft, although i've seen plenty of stealth aircraft and none looked like these perfectly black triangles.
