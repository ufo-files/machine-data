{jpg} A OBJECT IN SPACE THAT FLASHED TWICE THEN MADE A 45 DEGREE TURN

On the night of June 2nd 2018 I was sitting in my yard at 10 pm looking at the sky when I had seen a small flash that caught my eye traveling north as i was looking right above me. for a sec i thought it was a small Satellite but it was moving to fast and at a altitude of maybe a few hundred miles it was up there for sure and not in low earth orbit. as I watched it cruise it flashed one more time and then about 10 sec later it took a hard left turn to the west and slowed down a bit and then it turned again to the south and that's when I lost sight of it . so I watched the sky for about a hr after that and about 10:40 a bright light came out of the east at a good speed heading northwest and a lot brighter and lower than the first object .it kinda looked like the iss by the way the light looked and speed but i checked my app on my phone to see where the iss was in orbit and it wasn't even close it was on the other side of the globe . I see lots of things here in rhode island from the loud boom in oct 2012 to a bomerang shape hovering over my neighbors house to a red triangle lighted craft. I am a sky watcher and what I noticed here in the north from spring to fall is when we tend to see most of the ufo's around here but no sighting here in the winter . it's weird
