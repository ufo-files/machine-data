UFO Chico, California OCT/2020

Sitting outside while visiting family in Chico california, me and 1 other person look up and see these things moving through the night sky. It was a pretty clear night some mixed cloud cover but that was about it. We thought it was possibly a plane at first sight, but as we looked closer they were literally balls of light about the size of a star in the sky moving from right to left closely following one another. Within the span of 5 or so minutes, these balls of light would suddenly stop. They would come together and then split and continue to move on their way. I honestly don’t think it could’ve been a satellite or plane due to the nature of they acted and moved about the sky. I will mention that this happened on October 9, 2020 and it was the night of planet Mars being super close to us until some time in 2035? It has taken me a minute to come forward with this account because I have been second guessing myself and have constantly told myself that there is just no way that I am seeing what I am seeing, but the more I think about it, the more I know I was not just seeing things. I will go on to state that no I was not intoxicated or on any mind faulting drugs.
