My boyfriend AND I saw a crazy unknown flying object in Summerlin, Las Vegas at approximately 9pm Monday, 10/1/12. It was a star-like object. At first we assumed it was a satellite or star, but then it stopped and hovered in one area for about 30 seconds and then started going in sort of diagonal directions from southwest tonorthwest and so on and then just flew farther until it vanished. We completely felt like it was in our atmosphere, and also felt like it was aware of us watching it. I have always been a skeptic, but now I am a complete believer!!!
