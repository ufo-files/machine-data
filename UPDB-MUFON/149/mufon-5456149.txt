{jpg} Four to five equally-spaced planet or star-like objects moving slowly SE in a line

The sky was cold and clear this evening as viewed from my car. I was traveling southbound on M-19 between Memphis and Richmond (Michigan) when I looked ahead and to the left (which would be SE) and became aware of a line of starlike objects moving SE at low speed. They were equally spaced and of constant brightness. They reminded me of satellites but I thought it was extremely unlikely to have so many, all with equal spacing. They gradually continued SE until I lost sight of them. Local winds were from the NW so I suppose they could have been "sky lanterns" released a few minutes apart and allowed to rise to altitude and drift downwind towards the SE.
