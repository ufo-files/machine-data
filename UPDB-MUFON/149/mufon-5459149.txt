{rjl} Transparent blue/white orbs, some with red eyes moving about yard

As reported to ASD by phone: I was getting ready for bed when I noticed movement in the backyard of my family's house where I was visiting in Kansas City, Missouri. I looked out the window and saw at least 10 round glowing objects moving around the yard and in front of the bushes and trees. They were somewhat transparent as I could see the trees through the objects. They were slightly larger than a softball, all the same size, but some also had two red "eyes" for lack of a better term. I don't know if they were actually eyes or not or what these objects were or what they were doing. After about 30 minutes they all moved out of my sight and did not return.
