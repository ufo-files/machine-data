Driving on the high way at 110 km/h, ufo flying above at 100-105km/h.

I was driving home on this Holiday (24th june, Saint-Jean). This was on a long weekend. 

I left Saint-Jean-Port-Joli at midnight and heading home in Riviere-du-Loup. 

While heading east, I saw that Strange yellow dot doing a patterns in the sky. The pattern was doing the same pattern in the sky (going left and right, up and down). (file: 1.png). 

Later, around Notre-Dame-du-Portage, the UDO was hovering above us at a very close range. The UFO was just above trees and was going a little slower than us. It was really big, really really big, shaped like a triangle. In the center, there was a big red light. On the 3 extremities, a white light that was doing back and forth quickly like when someone is shaking a light fast in front of you. The UFO was above us for 1 minute. (file 2.png)

When arrived home, we were able to see the same yellow dot as the first step. It was doing the same kind of pattern as the step 1. (file 3.png)

Bonus information: There is a small Airport really close to the second encounter. https://www.google.ca/maps/@47.7727891,-69.5740381,15z?hl=fr (file 4.png)
