Witnessed a unidentified flying object. We could not make out a shape.

My family and I observed a aircraft of some sort flying over our home at 9:00 pm DST. The aircraft that flew over did not have exhaust, no sound, no lights underneath blinking, just a steady bright light ahead of it. It traveled at a slower speed of a large jet line carrier, therefore the sighting lasted at least 3 mins. We watched as it flew directly North, towards another aircraft, which had all the tail signs that it was a jet. I have reached out to the FAA, they did respond with a response that NO aircraft was reported or detected flying in our area for the time and date I reported. I was directed to contact the military, I have done so, with no response. Unfortunately we did not have our phones to record anything. My husband and 14 year old daughter watched.
