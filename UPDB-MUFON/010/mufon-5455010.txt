{tjd} Glowing Green/White ovals over West Seattle

Just before 18:00 I noticed two brightly glowing greenish white ovals. The light had an unusual brightness. Two objects were slowing flying in meandering fashion in the northern direction. Thinking they might be regular aircraft i watched them for a minute or two, then ran for my camera when one of the objects changed direction and flew south under the other object. This was obviously very unusual. Filmed both of them and decided to focus on one the north bound one for a total of about 44 seconds until it left my field of view. The objects had a red light that flashed, but they were not typical running lights.
