A large ball off light with a plane like trail behind it although I was certain it was not a plan falling from sky then becoming brighter until it detached from the trail and drifted downwards, then completely disappeared.

I was with my dad getting a tyre changed on his wagon in the Adams hill area of Woodgate Valley, I got bit bored of waiting for the tyre to be changed so sparked a cigarette and wondered about the curb looking at Facebook on my phone,then my attention turned to the sky as a group of ducks flew over head, it was then I noticed a bright white light falling from the sky leaving an odd trail behind kind of like a plane would, but very different it certainly was not a plane also there was no sound at all, then I watched in amazement as it got brighter and fell faster so with my phone in hand I started taking pictures and continued to observe, eventually the white light detached from the trail and became brighter then all of a sudden it dissappeared it was brilliant!!
