Cigar shape flying at airplane altitude

I was driving down the highway 280 southbound when I saw what appeared to be a plane high in the sky. I wouldn’t have paid it any attention, but I had seen 3 discs flying just a month earlier and I’ve been more aware of the skies. As I looked at what I thought was a plane, it flashed white light from grey. I looked closer and it didn’t have a tail wing or horizontal wings. It was a cigar shape. It flashed again as it flew up against a soft whispy cloud (whispy meaning it was a diffuse cloud that was about 50% see through, not dense or completely blocking the blue sky behind it). It flashed again and then disappeared. I thought perhaps it disappeared behind the cloud, although it wouldn’t be possible since the cloud was essentially see through, so I waited while watching fixed at the flight path where it should have been based on its previous movement and speed. It didn’t emerge from the whispy cloud as it should have. I thought perhaps it had changed directions and was flying away behind the cloud, but as the cloud disappeared, there was no plane behind it.
