{cak} Orb fell from sky like shooting star but didn't disappear; shot back up from same way it fell.

I'm driving late one evening around 10:30-11:00 PM. I see a shooting star fall from the sky but the star didn't burn out and there was no impact. The star/orb shot back into the sky the same way it fell. This all happened in like 2 maybe 3 seconds, very quickly. I don't know the distance between myself and star, I would guess well over a mile. This happened in between Selmer and Bethel Spring, Tennessee. This sighting occurred 06/07. Disbelief and wonder were my thoughts-
