Silent triangle craft with glowing fusealage. Moving less than 100 knots

I was out walking on my local beach near Dublin Ireland at approximately 18.30 mid April 2018 . My dog had just died a couple of days earlier so I was a little sad and reflective . About halfway into my walk I stopped to take in the beautiful view from the beach. My mind was drifting , thinking of nothing in particular , when suddenlly a voice spoke to me in my head. It was as clear as day . It was like someone speaking to you on a podcast or something , through headphones . It said Look........Up . So I started to look and kept moving my head until I saw it directly above my head. There was full cloud cover at around 7,000 ft except for a perfectly round hole directly above my head. In t I saw a triangle shaped craft .Reletive size was around one inch at arms length .In the backround was the moon perfectly framed in the circular hole along with this craft. I tried getting a picture but dropped my phone and saw that I would only have a very short time before the craft went back into cloud cover again so I decided just to take it all in. Event lasted 15-20 secs .I would never have seen it only for being told to Look....Up . Craft had no wings No rotors and made no sound. Also travelling too slow to stay airborne . Glow around craft was like it had created it own energy field. Definitely not a commercial aircraft . I estimate it was around 35-45 ft in size
