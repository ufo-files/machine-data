(GS) Blue Flash in sky. No cloud cover.

I was driving to work. I was on the Nevada side of Lake Tahoe heading North about 2 or 3 miles South of Sand Harbor on highway 28. From the Northwest, my left and foreward, I saw a blue light come up from behind the mountains. It was bright enough that I could not see the road and had to turn my head. I never closed my eyes and could see the blue light on my passenger seat. It lasted only a second or maybe even a fraction of. My first thought was lightning, I loooked out at the sky thinking I hadnt heard of any storms coming in and saw only stars on a clear night sky. No clouds. Then I realized that lighting isnt blue. I asked coworkers if they had seen the same flash I had seen and it appears that I am the only one that witnessed this. Also, when the light came up, it seemed a dome shape.
