Large triangle craft

Large reflective black craft of triangular shape sighted over Wavertree. At least a 100ft in diameter. The underside of the craft had 3 large blue coloured lights more or less positioned in each corner. Craft drastically changed direction from descending to around 350ft to above 500ft in less that 5 seconds before fully ascending at great speed into the sky.
