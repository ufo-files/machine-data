On March 11, 2012, approximately 100 UFOs witnessed by 3 individuals in Whitestone, Queens, NY.

On March 11, 2012, I witnessed over 100 UFO1s with two of my friends. We were on 6th Avenue and Powells Cove Blvd in Whitestone, Queens, New York. My friend was having his car repaired at an auto shop nearby. It was a clear, sunny afternoon between 3:57pm and 4:27pm. We were located approximately 6 miles from LaGuardia airport.

I saw over 100 white dots in the sky, so I used my binoculars to view these dots. These dots were actually shiny, dark silver-colored UFO1s (they looked like silver balls). They were at an altitude of approximately 10 to 12 km and were stationary. Every 2-3 seconds, these UFO1s changed their shapes into triangles, circles and an arrow-like shape. They all had a size of about 30 to 40 feet. 

My friend saw two larger UFO1s, approximately 90-100 feet in width. These were located at an elevation lower than the 100 or so UFO1s. These two UFO1s were 7 to 8 km away. These objects also had a shiny silver color and did not move. However, one of them had a green light on its side, which flashed every 2 seconds and this object was triangular in shape. We believe that these two larger UFO1s may have been motherships.

After 20 minutes, the two larger UFO1s slowly began to drift to the East. The smaller ones followed. They went towards Yorktown, NY/ Hudson River Valley.

To view one or two UFOs, I would think is unusual, but likely (which I have seen last year). However, to see over 100 UFOs together, is amazing!!
