6 circular lights in a circular rotation moving from side to side then vanishing

I was outside on a partly clear night letting the dogs out when I happened to see the object hovering in the sky between cloud cover, it just caught my attention. I first thought it was a projection, but it being so cold and in an area where projections wouldn't make sense, I immediately knew it wasn't one, as there was no trail of light from projection, and why would it be such a weird looking projection? Could not be. It had 6 bright white lights and was formed in a circle shape, and they would move slowly from side to side, maybe 50 feet in between. It lasted for about 8 or so minutes and then just disappeared. I've never seen anything like it before and am still shocked what I saw because I never thought anything like it would ever happen to me. Strange for sure, just vanished.
