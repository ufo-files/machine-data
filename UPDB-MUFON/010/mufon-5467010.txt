{cak} A long silvery cylinder with 4 orange lights was stationary for ~ 5 minutes in the western s sky, then spun and abruptly left

This happened back in the mid-seventies - it11s been so long that I can11t remember exact date and time. I had taken a small spotting scope over to my neighbor to show her the moons of Jupiter. While she was looking through the telescope I noticed the object in the western sky. At first I thought it was a plane coming toward me because it did not appear to be moving. I knew the lights were wrong for a plane. I realized it was something strange so I moved the scope onto the object. I could see a silvery cigar shape and the four orange lights but no other details. At that time it disappeared from the scope view and I looked up to see it spin abruptly and disappeared to the west. I knew it was no plane, planet, or weather balloon. My neighbor only saw it briefly.
