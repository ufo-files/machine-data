craft moving southeast across sky

Sitting in the backyard, enjoying the stars in the clear sky, I saw what appeared to be a cluster of stars start moving across the sky. The lights were as bright as the stars so at first it appeared as a cluster of stars started moving across the sky. As it picked up speed, I realized it was actually moving and was a craft. We live close to a small municiple airport, but I heard no engine noise. It had more lights on it than an aircraft and the lights were only white. The lights were not real bright so they blended in to the stars. Based on the lights not being real bright, I realized what I was watching was probably further away than I first thought and therefore probably a lot larger as well. For reference, at arms length the object appeared to be about 2.5 - 3 inches across. I watched it cross the sky in a southeastern direction.
