(GS) Greenish glowing object ascended over treeline, formed a diamond shape, then circled back around to previous flight path

I was walking outside facing east, and witnessed a glowing orb shape in the sky. It came up passed the treeline, stopped, and formed a diamond shape. It circled back around behind the trees after its formation. It had a greenish tint, but a prominent white glow. I basically stared at the object and observed it making an elongated diamond formation. My first reaction was that of surprise, but then I felt excited that I got to see something like this. Next, confused. Then, reluctant to go to sleep.
