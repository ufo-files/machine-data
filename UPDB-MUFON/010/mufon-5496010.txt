{cak} saw 2 objects less than quarter mile south of my house approx 150 and 300 ft above trees

On Friday 13, February 2015 I was sitting in my living room when I noticed that my son turned into our driveway and stopped his car up by the gate approx 300 ft east of the house. He sat there bout 10 minutes or so. I thought it was a bit unusual but I have seen him drive past the house down into the pasture checking out deer or coyotes etc. so I didn't think much of it. After a bit he came into the house and said, Dad you need to come take a look at this; so I got up and we went outside. He pointed to the south of the house and about 300 yards or so away were 2 objects, one a diamond shaped object about 300 or so feet above the ground and the other a thick donut shaped object about 150 feet up and maybe 100 feet west of the diamond shaped object. Both were a dull orange color. The diamond shaped object seemed to "sparkle" while the donut remained a dull orange the entire time we observed it. I went back into the house and grabbed some binoculars hoping to see more detail but they just made the objects brighter. After approx 10 minutes or so we noticed the larger object, the diamond shaped one, started moving very slowly to the east. Soon it was behind the trees. At that time we moved to the front or east side of the house. We couldn't see it at this point but could see the glow behind the trees so we moved out by the road in front of the house just as the object moved east out of the trees. As it moved across the road it started to rise. By the time it had crossed the road it was maybe 700-800 feet up. it sat there maybe 10 minutes or so. At this point we noticed a plane maybe 5 miles or so east of the house moving north to south and it was at this time that I noticed we could hear the plane but there was absolutely no noise coming from either object we were looking at. The donut shaped object had not moved at all. After about 10 minutes of hovering above the trees east of our county road the object started moving east again climbing to about 1000 ft then it started moving north, climbed to about 1500 feet, and started flashing lights and we watched it move north til it was out of sight. The donut shaped object never moved at all and eventually seemed to fade away. After a few more minutes we went back into the house. The diamond shaped object was maybe the size of a 727 and the donut shaped object was about one third that size. I spent 27 years in the military and have lived all over the world I was part of the 3rd marine air wing and so was around aircraft most of my life and I have never seen anything like what I saw that night.
