2 clusters. First cluster with 2 objects, 2nd cluster with 3 objects (one was a different shape)

Relative rung to tell me to look out of the window towards a bridge in my hometown (relative lives on the same estate)

Looked out of the upstairs window and observed two clusters of objects in the sky. The first cluster had two, dark sphere like objects. Didn't seem to have propulsion at all, almost like it was floating. These were travelling side by side. 

2nd cluster was about 1/2 a mile behind and was three objects. One object was triangular in shape and dark in colour (nearest thing I can describe it as is a stealth bomber shape) 

the other two objects within that cluster were spherical like the first cluster.

Observed them for over a minute until they disappeared into the clouds.

Not sure if this is related but earlier in the day (about an hour before the sighting) we observed two bright flashes of blue light, about 30 seconds apart. We just assumed they were lightning but we both commented on it as it is very unusual to have lightning in January in Wales..

We tried to take a picture but the objects were not visible on the camera. My relative did watch them through binoculars but couldn't see anything other than shape and colour.

I personally couldn't hear any sound emitting from the objects but my son (the 2nd witness) heard a high pitched ringing in his ears and felt a little dizzy afterwards.
