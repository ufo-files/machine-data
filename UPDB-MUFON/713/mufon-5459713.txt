{rjl} Sphere hovering above fireworks, disappeared then reappeared

Round white sphere instantly appeared above area where fireworks were going off (viewed just east of SPS parking lot viewing area on Highland ave.)
The sphere appeared for a minute or so then disappeared for a minute and then reappeared for another minute or two. The object was perfectly round and bright white and moved slightly from side to side. It finally seemed to fly away from us to the east. A helicopter passed by afterwards offering contrast to the bright hovering object we had observed; the glowing white orb was nothing like the helicopter. which was very identifiable. Hundreds of people were watching fireworks in this parking lot and someone else had to have seen this curious object.
