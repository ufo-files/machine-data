I was awakened about 01:00 am by lights outside that I thought were landing gear lights on a small aircraft. After seeing the lights about six times I left my room in the basement and saw approx. eight white lights arcing above the trees,etc.

I was sleeping in my grandparents basement as I was helping them gather hay on their actual farm a few miles away.
I first saw a white light outside my basement window that I thought was landing gear light for a small plane,etc. but after I saw it approx. six times I went to the main floor kitchen to look out the window.
I saw several white lights arcing above the trees and sometimes flying in various formations,etc.
I was amused and continued to watch for approx. 15 minutes and went back to bed.
To the best of my recollection I did not see them disappear before I quite watching.
I did not wake my uncle as he works late and gets up early and my grandparents were quite elderly.
