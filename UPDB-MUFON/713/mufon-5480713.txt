'Black Something' appeared underneath a rainbow

It's the morning of Saturday 21st June 2014, I'm outside taking photos of a beautiful rainbow that is south-west and I'm standing on the verandah of the front side of the house I'm renting. After taking 9 photos on my smartphone, I went back inside, downloaded the photos on my computer to look at later. When the time came to look at the rainbow, I noticed in the first photo shot a 'black something' underneath the rainbow and in the photo taken two shots after shows the 'black something' in the top right hand of the photo. There would have been four shots altogether, but the second shot had been a blurr and the third shot had been just blackness and both were deleted immediately. It could be a UFO or a military aircraft, whatever it is, the times between the first shot and fourth shot were fast: first shot of 'black something' occurred at 7:37:10am and the second shot of 'black something' occurred at 7:37:46am, so it takes 'black something' 36 seconds to get from underneath the rainbow to the sky that is top right hand. The first photo shows the 'black something' underneath the rainbow and it is like a round or half moon shape that has motion lines underneath it and it could be a cylandrical craft with a window with the motion lines being the pattern of the craft, then the second photo shows the 'black something' up in the sky, further west, back-on and like a trail is seen behind it, but just barely. I felt excited to have shot my first UFO.
