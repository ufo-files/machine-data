Small bright object straight up from me.

While watching an outdoor movie in Tent City, Nha Trang, Vietnam, the movie stopped, lights went out and soldiers were looking up and pointing. I looked up and observed a small point of light moving quickly and making 90 degree turns at high speed. After a few minutes it zoomed straight up and disappeared. The lights came back on and then the movie Compat I believe, Started. There was about 50 +- soldiers watching at the time. I was in Co.A, 504th MPs. 1966-67. This happened June 1966 but I was unable to put that in the date occurred box.
