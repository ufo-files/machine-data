{rjl} Strange Lights Flew Right Over Town

I was traveling North on i-69 from Jefferson to the Coldwater Rd exit at around 10:00pm. At first, I saw an aircraft of some kind with blue lights on each end cross over the freeway going south just over the Chevy Dealership by Costco. At first I thought it was just some kind of small drone. As I got closer to Coldwater, I realized that this object was actually a lot larger and now flying east. It was lighting up red and blue and white. I exit at Coldwater and I-69. I headed south on coldwater towards coliseum. At Coldwater, I took a left and headed towards PFW. At that time, the object was off on the right hand side (south) of Coliseum. I live in the area around PFW and as I pulled into the parking lot of my apartments this object flew right over me. As I looked up I could see the bottom of the object lighting up light blue, red, pink and green with white lights on the end. I had my camera and was only able to catch it flying away. Did anyone else end up seeing this? IT had a deep humming that really confused me too.
