{dr}Bright orange orb

Was looking for the dog opened the door to see if she was outside saw this bright glowing orb as soon as I stepped out. It was moving side to side slightly like it may have wobbled. I called my wife to see it. The got in the car and drove to the barn to get a closer look. while I was on the way to the barn trees and a building blocked my view. When I got to the barn the object had moved about a half mile west. I returned to the house and it was back in the place it began. I then told my wife to watch it while I went back to the barn by then it had moved back to the west about a half mile then it vanished. When I got back I ask her how it moved she said it looked like it had just got lower. I tried to take a picture but the camera wouldnt focus on it. I think it was to bright.
