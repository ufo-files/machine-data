During an evening cookout,campfire, fireworks by the pond, on September 5th 2018, three of us saw pulsating lights that would appear to slowly drop down out of the night sky but staying within a small range. It would disappear and then reappear in a different location lower to the ground in the slow descent. This happened mutimrs in the same area of the sky. We were identifying constellations when we noticed it. It would disappear close to the horizon and the reappear hanging around the treetops then drift down. The pulsating colored lights alerted us. We watched airplanes in the sky also but the UFO was not close to the planes. The planes moved horizontal the UFO moved vertically within a small range. Thought at first it was a Satellite.i immediately started to take video as the guys kept watch where it was. Light would emit and light up the sky. It was not heat lightening.The guys were interested in the lighting up of the sky, I was interested in where it was and it's movement. We were not frightened but continued sitting in our seats. Itdid not come toward us but stayed in the same location multiple times and would float down and then repeat emitting light to light up the sky.After an hour of viewing we went back to roasting chestnuts and making smores.
