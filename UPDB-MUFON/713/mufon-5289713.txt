UFO with spinning lights explodes in a silent flash

On February 8th, 2021 at exactly 10:35 pm, I observed 2 lights that were flashing and stationary/hovering over a meadow at the rear of my house. I viewed the lights through a 2nd floor window with a view that was unobstructed. 
  Approximately 1 second after I made eye contact the flashing lights rapidly began moving toward my location. Because of prior UFO activity I already had a Nikon camera with a 3000mm telephoto zoom lens positioned in the window and the window screen had been removed, I immediately began to record the lights movement. Because the lights moved exactly the same way and the distance between them stayed the same I felt that both lights were attached to the same object. One light was white and the other looked to be a greenish yellow.  Even though the lights were different colors they both exhibited the same characteristic. 
Each appeared similar to a spinning atom or electron. As the video approached the 20 second mark the lights dipped slightly, then suddenly and silently exploded/disintegrated in a low intensity flash. Even while dissipating the spinning atom type appearance continued outward to nothing. After the flash the meadow went dark. No aircraft of any type were visible. No moon was visible. The video will be sent separately. I contacted the local television station KTVZ and was advised that no UFO's had been reported to the station. The entire sighting lasted approximately 25 seconds.
