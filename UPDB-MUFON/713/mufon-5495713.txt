{dr}Captured in random cloud photo

My husband and I were driving North on Hwy 95 in Melbourne, Florida, in November 2014. We were South of Patrick AFB/Cape Kennedy area. I took two pictures of clouds with my cell phone from the vehicle only because the clouds interested me. One photo at 9:59am and again at 10:00am. This was 18 days before the scheduled Dec 5 Mars test launch at Cape Kennedy which actually took place Dec 6. I did not see the formation on the second photo until I downloaded them to my computer in January. I was intrigued but assumed there is a non-UFO explanation. A friend urged me to send the photos to you. I did not observe any activity at the time.
