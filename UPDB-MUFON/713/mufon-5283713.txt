Low flying orange starlike pulsing orb hovered and then extremely slowly moved to the east until I could no longer see it due to tree obstructions.

This was a low flying orange starlike pulsing orb seen at 4am from my second story south facing window approximately 1 mile to my south. The sky was very clear and I spent a few minutes looking at the stars from my window before looking away for approximately 90 seconds. 
When I looked back out the window, this object was suddenly present, hovering approximately 300ft above the treetops, again, approximately 1 mile away. It did not have blinking lights such as an airplane, helicopter, or drone might have. It was too low to be a satelite. It hovered before moving extremely slowly. The object was starlike, but orange/red, and after hovering in place for approximately 1 minute, it began moving very slowly to the east for approximately 5 inches of my field of view (it took perhaps 60 seconds to traverse those 5 inches of my view) before disappearing behind trees that then obstructed my view. 

I saw this kind of object on 1/11/15, without movement, in nearly the same vicinity as the object in this report (I had journaled about the first sighting, so I was able to find the date).
