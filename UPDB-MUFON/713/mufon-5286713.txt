Red/Orange Orb heading North, then Northeast.

Light first appeared in the South moving directly towards us. Wife collected cell phone to record.
Moved at a consistent speed directly overhead and then when North of our location moved to Northeast and disappeared as though light had been switched off.
Appeared about the size of a lit cigarette end at arms length, then when passing above, seemed to show a cylinder shape until direction change.
No sound. No navigation lights at all - Just an orb moving about the speed of a jet liner.
Unable to determine altitude and accordingly cannot estimate size.
Inbound comets travel much faster and leave a trail - this was not observed.
No conclusion, but definitely not an airplane, nor helicopter, nor anything with known propulsion, though clearly was able to navigate and must have some sort of propulsion.  No noise. No strobe. No navigation lights..
