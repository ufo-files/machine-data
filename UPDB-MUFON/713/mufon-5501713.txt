{rjl} Fleet formation of wingless craft while photographing sunset with phone.

I was waiting in the car while my husband ran into the store. I often take photos in the evening of the sunset. I usually have my camera with me, but used my phone. Recently, I've been capturing crafts in the evening that are wingless or could be drones, I suspect (when there is just 1 visible). I did not notice the fleet until I got home and put the photos on my computer. I usually have this weird feeling that I will capture something on film. (Yes, I know, it is not film-I am dating myself). I did not notice the fleet with my eyes while in the parking lot. My reactions to this fleet sighting seem to be increasing my anxiety, as this is not the first time for me. I did not lose sight of the fleet because I could not see it until I got it on my computer. But, it must have come and gone quickly because the fleet is not visible on any other photos taken.

To really see the fleet, you need to zoom in, and/or enlarge the area to the left of the lightpole.
