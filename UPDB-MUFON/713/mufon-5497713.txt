{dr}Bright Orange Light

I was driving west on Lynnhaven Parkway when I noticed a steady light source with brightness of a streetlamp that appeared to be hovering over a neighborhood near Salem HS at low altitude. Light then began moving slowly and crossed over Lynnhaven Parkway. The light dimmed slightly then intensified and it accelerated very quickly out of view. I tried to take a picture with my smartphone camera but it didn't appear in the viewfinder. It 100% was not a plane/helicopter/meteor.
