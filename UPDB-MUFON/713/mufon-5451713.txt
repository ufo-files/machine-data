White cylinder almost as wide as it’s length. Solid. Hovered between 2 lg clouds then turned to enter cloud cover. No sound!

My sister and I were just leaving her house and it was a cloudy day with huge white, fluffy clouds. I glanced up at the clouds and saw a short cylinder hovering between two clouds. The clouds were moving east to west north west and the cylinder turned slightly from my being able to see the only the 
long side to seeing both length and diameter and it flew into the western most cloud and disappeared. No sound, markings, entities or source of propulsion was seen. When it disappeared it was moving only slightly faster than the clouds. Odd. ..
