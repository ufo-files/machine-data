White Sphere in Sky over Colorado Springs

On the morning of July 11, 2020 we were performing a photoshoot at the top of Palmer Park in Colorado Springs. While taking pictures we caught a white/silver sphere in the sky. We were taking pictures on a Sony a7iii in continues mode with 3 consecutive pictures, each taken less than one second apart. The sphere appeared in the middle image and not on the first or last image.
