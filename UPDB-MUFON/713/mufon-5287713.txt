observed black chevron while mother and I were traveling to Morris illinois from dwight illinois to have dinner at Rockwell Inn

On or around mid november 1996 my mother and I were traveling form Dwight Illinois to Morris Illinois to have dinner at the Rockwell Inn. North of Mazon, north of Reed Road, at some high tension power lines, I observed what I thought were lights traveling in formation - they tracked each other precisely and were moving very slowly west over the power lines, in a triangle formation. I had my mother pull over and we observed this for a couple of minutes. I saw that it was a dark triangular/chevron shaped object because it would obscure the stars when it moved, but the actual structure was just black, not really "visible". We watched this for a minute or so. I felt at the time and still believe that it was following the power lines. We talked about this occasionally, and she corroborated events.
