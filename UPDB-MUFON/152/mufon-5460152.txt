{rjl} 2nd sighting, I saw 3 discs together go down and slide in to trees, just of road between Sparr and Ft McCoy.

1st sighting about 1969, returning from Orlando YMCA to FTU. Just as I needed to slow down to turn off 4 lane road, I had to go under part of UFO, center underneath opening width of 2 lanes. Overall ship width was more than 4 lanes. I looked up and saw many beings in the top globe like part, busy working on various panels, I wanted to visit with them but was running late getting back to the dorm by curfew. I got back okay, but do not remember how presumed I drove it. I forget about this event until something triggers the memory.
2nd sighting after I was out of school and teaching. While looking out the home window one evening I saw 3 ships that looked just like the 1st one I saw. They were close together, went down to the ground and into a clump of trees and disappeared. I tried to report it but found no one interested in the report.
