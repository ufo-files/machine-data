FILLED OUT AND FILED FROM A PAPER REPORT SENT INTO MUFON HQ ON 2/27/2020

I WAS IN MY BACKYARD HAVING A SMOKE 15 TO 20 MINUTES AFTER SUNSET I LOOKED UP AND I SAW A PRESSURE WAVE (ONE SHAPED 5 TO 6 WAVES MOVING FROM A SHARP POINT).
WHEN I LOOKED UP IT LOOKED LIKE A BOAT ON THE WATER IN THE CLOUDY FOG LAYER WHICH ABOUT 3,000 FEET
WHEN I FIRST SAW IT IT LOOKED LIKE A BOAT ON THE WATER WAVE GOING OUT FROM A POINT BOTH WAY'S 5 TO 6 WAVES. I LOOKED BEHIND THE SHARP POINT TO SEE SOMETHING CLOAKED LIKE STAR TREK I LOOKED RIGHT THROUGH WHAT EVER IT WAS.
IT WAS MAKING A PRESSURE WAVE IN THE CLOUDS THE CLOUDS WERE GONE WHERE IT HAD PAST THREW THEM FIRST SIGHING IT SHIMMERED LIKE IT WAS CLOAKED SECOND SIGHTING I SAW A BACK OF A BOOMERANG SKY BLUE LIGHT FLASHED AFTER TRACKING IT AND MOVING MY HEAD BACK WHERE IT STARTED IT WAS LIKE I SKIPPED THROUGH THE AIR
I WAS BLOWN AWAY BY THIS NEVVER SEEN ANYTHING LIKE IT MOVING VERY FAST NAD TO MOVE MY HEAD TO FOLLOW IT.
IF THERE WERE NO CLOUDS I WOULD HAVE NEVER SAW IT SO FAST IT FLEW OFF. IT MEASURED 30 X 100 ' VERY SHARP POINT 4 TO 5 WAVES.
