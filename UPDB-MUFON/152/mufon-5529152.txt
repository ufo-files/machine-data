Bright lights, lowered in sky then hovered and took off like a sling shot.

At about 1:30 am I was laying in bed watching t.v. Out of the corner of my eye I saw bright lights out of my bedroom window. When i looked over the lit up object was dropping in height very quickly. I thought i was seeing a plane crash. It looked unussually close for a plane and was so loud I could hear it. I stood up and got closer to the window and it stopped dropping and hovered for a couple seconds. I noticed its ring of lights and its strange, hollow-like (saturn-like), shape. Within seconds it took off into the fog like a slingshot and I could see the lights disapearing in the distance. Once i realized what i just saw i woke up my boyfriend and brother in a panic. I went over it in my head trying to find another explanation, but there isnt one. It seemed like it came out of a movie and i couldnt believe what i had just saw.
