Amber colour, silent, very large & appearing low, travelling east to west slowly then sped off at an unbelievable speed & was gone.

Very clear night, went out to look at the starts, walked out on our back deck and looked up and from the east just above the neighbours house my husband & I saw what appeared to be a luminous amber boomerang shaped object.I immediaely called my husband to come and look. I knew it was a UFO but my husband wasn't sure. It appeard extremely large and low but we couldn't establish really how high up or close it was. It travelled very slowly and silently over our heads and then the next house to the left. It suddenly sped up travelling west then vanished out of site. We both decided to go into the house and each separtely draw what we saw and then compared sketches. Our drawings were almost identical.
