{tjd} Red Orange flaming like craft flew by bedroom window...I ran outside to see it zig zag, hover, then dissapitate.

I was laying in bed watching tv. I have a long bedroom window that gives me a nice view of the night sky. We live near an airport and often see planes on their path to the airport. I noticed a flying craft but it look nothing like planes i've observed before. It was larger than the planes and was a red/orange pulsing craft that almost looked like it was pulsing flames. It was redder (and some black) at the center with a surrounding orange transparent color that changed in shape...the center stayed same. It went out of view of my window and I ran outside to see if I could spot it. It did not head to the airport, which is south west of my house, it swung east, changing direction and altitude while doing so until it stopped and hover in the sky. It pulsated while hovering. I had yelled for my wife to come out while I was observing and she saw the zig zag and saw it hover. She ran in to get her phone, which is the video we posted, it just hovered. I lost sight of the object as it just seemed to shrink and dissipate.
