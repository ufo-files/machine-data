{cak} Multiple 2 point starlike lights hovering in grid pattern over Florida Coastline

I often set out on the beach at night and for the past few years shortly after the sun goes down within an hour multiple star like lights will seem to appear out of nowhere more or less winking in. All the lights are composed of two points of light next to each other and the altitude is way too low for them to be stars. They hover there stationary for a few hours and normally since I do not want to spend the night on the beach I will leave before I know where they go to. I have seen the lights further inland but it is not as easy to see because of the traffic and city lights. I have seen some of them winking out and where they go I do not know. I have seen a few that after hovering for about an hour will slowly move off to the left or right. I do not believe that they are stars because they are all twin points of light close together. I was curious if this was a phenomenon that was only occurring in Florida. I went up to Dayton, Ohio to visit relatives for a week and made it a point to observe the night sky there and saw the same lights only not as many. I saw a film clip of when the small fleet of flying objects flew over the White House shortly after World War Two and they did a close up shot. The objects had 2 points of light just like what I have seen. My question is If there is a grid of craft hovering over us what is their purpose? Some nights there are just a few other nights there are a few hundred. For any one who wants to check it out clear nights are best. On some overcast nights you cant see anything. Would send photos but don't have the equipment. Just had to tell some one who can appreciate my message. Thanks
