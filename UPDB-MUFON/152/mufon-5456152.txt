{jpg} Craft was concave underneath with bright golden-white circular light in middle.

The object was first spotted coming over top of a stand of pine trees directly above us. Underneath the object and in the center was a circular frosted looking glass that was very bright and looked like swirling golden waves or flames. The craft appeared be made of a cork like material. As it cleared the trees it lowered in elevation. It was going very slow and did not change speed. It was as if it was looking for a place to land or was looking for something. It continued on the same path at a very low altitude
( 50 - 100ft) and lost to site.
