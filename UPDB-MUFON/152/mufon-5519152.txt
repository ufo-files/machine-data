There was a very large red light in the sky which seemed to fly very quickly in an arched line into the distance.

I was lying in bed last night, facing the window, and through my blinds I saw a bright red light. When I opened the window the light was headed north in an arch. It was visable to me for about 45 seconds to 1 minute before it dissapeared over the horizen. At first I thought maybe it was a meteor, or some kind of firework or flare. 

I called my boyfriend over to the window and he thought it was very strange as well. Ive never seen anything like it before and I havent been able to get my mind off it today, trying to figure out what it was. 

If I held a baseball up to the light it would cover it, maybe just a bit smaller. Either way it was so far up that it must have been very large! It was a round, red light with red beams or glare coming off. It seemed high up and then slowly arched its way down to the north until I couldnt see it anymore.

The object did not skip around, it simply glided through the sky in one smooth motion. 

I still think this is very strange, and like I said I have never seen anything like this. Im just hoping maybe someone saw the same thing as us.
