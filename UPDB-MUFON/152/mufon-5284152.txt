Looked like a star or plane light

I was walking out of the office door to my car when I noticed a really bright light in the sky. When I noticed it at first, I thought it was a star as it looked about right height for one. As I was getting in my car, the "star" seemed to get a little larger and brighter. I thought then it was possibly an airplane as it started getting bigger and brighter. It had a shiny aura around the "star" as the light intensified. It got really bright and then it started getting dimmer, then all of the sudden, it seemed to collapse upon itself and it was gone!
