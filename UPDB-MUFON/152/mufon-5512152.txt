(GS) bright star like heading north. Blinked once and then faded away

I was looking for the meteor, that was to pass along the horizon last night, While on my front porch I happened to look up a little higher and noticed a star like light that was moving really fast toward the north. At first I was surprised to see a "flying star" but realized it wasnt a star. I thought , How amazing this sighting was. AS I watched I thought it as big a the star venus It blinked once and then just faded away. I only observed it for about 10 seconds but that was a long 10 seconds.
