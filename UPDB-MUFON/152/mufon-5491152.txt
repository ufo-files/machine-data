{rjl} Bright yellow, circular, blinking light. Travels in a straight line then fizzles, disappears.

I was in my kitchen around 9 o'clock at night when I looked out the window and spotted this bright yellow, circular blinking light in the sky. It immediately struck me as odd and so I watched it as it came closer. It was larger than a star. I could see no actual object, just a bright blinking light. It traveled in a straight line. I told my partner about it but they didn't show much of an interest. I continued to watch. It blinked and traveled for about six minutes and then suddenly it started blinking faster and faster then it fizzled and was gone. I mentioned it again to my partner once it disappeared. And have mentioned it again just recently. This happened about a month ago and it's still fresh in my mind - I'd never seen anything like it before.
