Glowing, hovering jellyfish like object.

My father was stationed at Wildflecken, Germany which was a very isolated US Army base which had been a secret SS training camp under Hitler. The base itself is very small and only those stationed there know much about it. It is located in NE Bavaria and at the time was within 10 km of the East German border.

Over the course of the 2.5 years that I lived there, I often found myself woken up at odd times of the night and on a few of these occasions would look out the windows to witness odd lights in the sky close to our apartment building and believe that I experienced "lost time". The area surrounding the base is primarily rural farm and woodland with minimal air traffic due to it's proximity to the Iron Curtain.

In late summer or early autumn of 1990 I woke around 2-3am and looked out the window on a clear, moonlit night to see a large, jellyfish like object above the treeline near my apartment building. The windows in my room were very large and afforded a full view of the object which had light blue, white, and purplish color illumination. I recall feeling unusually calm and at ease dispite what I was witnessing. I remember staring at the object for what seemed like a very long time, however, I do not recall ever having fallen back to sleep. I woke up the next morning without any indication that I had left my bed or sustained any physiological damage.

I understand that it has been several years since this even occured but I have a photographic memory and will never forget what I saw.
