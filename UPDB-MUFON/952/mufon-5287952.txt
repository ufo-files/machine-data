Moving horizontal line moving East. Formation of lights tightly packed to form an almost perfect line moved as a formation West to East. High Altitude

Noticed a line of light in the sky in the NorthWest section of the sky. I thought it may be light from our driveway lamp reflecting off the power line to the house, but then noticed it was too far from the house to be that cable. Also, the line was moving East. As the light moved further East, I called over my mother and sister to verify that I was really seeing this 2 inch horizontal line in the sky move East. Everyone was shocked when they saw it. It was 9:35pm when this happened, and it lasted about 5 minutes before it was out of sight towards the Eastern horizon. The crazy thing about it was that it looked like a line near the horizon, but when it was closer to overhead it appeared as a group of individual lights moving in a very tight formation, packed in like fighter jets or train cars. 

My sister drew a rough drawing of the cigar shaped string of lights. Like I said before, it was at most 2 inches long in the sky, looking like a horizontal line, or cigar shaped, white light object in the sky. . Not like StarLink, which spaces it's satellites several miles apart. I've attached the drawing to this report.
