Red and white lights moveing in a straight line ,jumping back and forth

while driving from Bristol Tn. To Kingsport Tn, on a country road, we were starteld by a bright flash of light in the sky some 100 ft in front of us and 50 to 75 ft of of the ground. The lights never focused on us but slowley moved from right to left in a jerkong way that looked like it was one place and then in an instant it was forward or backward of the original position. We pulled off the road and watch it for about 15 minutes until it went off in the distance. Just as were pulling back onto the road several helecopters went in the same path as the lights did.
