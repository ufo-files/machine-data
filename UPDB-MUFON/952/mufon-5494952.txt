Parked car sighting. we were just approaching our car and a UFO left behind a factory

Pinaroo, Victoria, near Western Hwy. Waiting on shops to open and my husband and I certainly saw a lolly or I think more like a bucket shaped aircraft or who knows what it was, leave from behind a warehouse. It went up super slow and was a little bit blurry. We called a person to look but their eyesight was very average and they thought it was a cardboard box, but deep down I think they were a bit spooked. The aircraft thing wandered about the sky. It dipped and weaved a small amount. I mean, it could have been some industrial thing or I guess some huge plastic bag, but it moved alone and against the wind easily. Besides, it looked metal to me.
