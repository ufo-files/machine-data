{jpg} Sighted during drive home

While driving home, my fianc1 and I noticed a bright orange light that "turned on" in the sky. It stayed stationary at first and then moved quickly in a line. Then the object was close enough to see triangle shape. It had an orange light on each of it's corners. The object then moved south of us and then the lights turned off and it was gone. Lasted about 5 minutes. Gave us both chills and we had ear aches afterwards.
