Manasota Key,FL

This was seen over the gulf from the Lock & Key resturant in Manasota Key,FL. It looked like a ball of fire in a hot air ballon, although the people that I was with that night said they don't fly hot air ballons at night for lack of proper night landing gear. It was there for several minutes just floating around very quietly and then just was gone, only to reappear a few minutes later. This was very strange to see so I took a quick video on my Blackberry although the quality is poor. I recently moved here this past june & I must say this is not the first time I can mention a sighting. I know of people close to me that have witnessed events from my native Long Island,NY.
