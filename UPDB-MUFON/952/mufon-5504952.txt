{nd} Object appeared above mature pine trees and house

Me and my brother were getting golf balls from the wildwood golf course on hole number 12 a short par three hole with a pond between the tee box and green. We had been there for close to an hour and we both noticed some type of large craft that slowly appeared from the SE moving from the green towards the tee box. It made no sound and was hovering, moving very slowly towards us as we were in the water getting golf balls. At the time there was a newly constructed house just behind the green and the craft had moved directly over it as it approached us. Once we seen it we hurriedly left the water and ran across number 13 fairway towards our house behind number 16 green, approximately 1/4 mile away. The craft filled the entire opening above the green making no noise and moving nothing in the trees or the ground. The craft was disc like with windows or openings, it was not spinning. I told my brother there are times that I think we possibly could have been picked up by the craft.
