A black orb hovering in the sky along I-40. It was erratically flashing a white light.

I was in the backseat of a car driving along I-40 West from Greensboro, NC to Asheville, NC when I saw a black ball in the sky at around 60 degrees. It was flashing a white light very erratically. I kept my eye on it as we past it until it was too far behind me and directly in the sun. It seemed to be moving slowly east, in the opposite direction of the car I was in. 
This lasted for a short period of time. I saw it for maybe ten seconds before I could no longer view it. I could not determine altitude, size, or distance from the car, but it seemed to stay at the same height. 
We were soon pulled over for speeding. Could it have been a drone used to monitor speeding traffic?
