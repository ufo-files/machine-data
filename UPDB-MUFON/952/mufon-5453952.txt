{cak} Saw as I was coming home from the east

Went out for pizza at about 8:30 and on the way he coming from the East on Delaney Rd when I caught site of it. Thought it was helicopter searching for someone because I could see a beam of light shining just behind the barn. But as I got closer the two red lights were stationed perfectly apart even from the one Bright beam. I was on the phone with a friend of mine but as I got to the end of the driveway I could see it was no a man made craft. Dropped my phone and lost the call I had on line. But I picked up the phone tried to call my partner but got no answer he was playing music loud couldn't hear the phone everyone next door was at work. But after about ten minutes the white beam turned red then started hoovering of to the east as it was elevating up into the night sky then it just slowly flew away. Never thought I would ever see anything like that still no one believes me but I know what happened at my yard that night.
