I was laying outside at night in the grass watching the stars like I sometimes do when Im bored or upset. I noticed a bright orange/reddish ball of light that I happened to notice moving funny and oddly large to be anything Ive seen before, like a planet on its closest nights, but bigger than that by far. The light was a steady glow, no pulsing or anything. It moved somewhat slowly way above me from the SW toward the SW. It would then stop (yes, just hang there) and turn a sharp change in direction (not like a loop, but like a corner), and it did this a few times. It would change direction quickly, but then move slowly. It then zig zagged a few abrupt times and shot off out toward the NW. It was way above me, so I couldnt see fine details, but I have NEVER seen a plane or even bird move that quickly on a direction change. My Father had actually seen the same thing (come to find out when I went inside and told him) but years ago when he was alone driving down a back road. He said the one he saw was seemingly following him from above. When he stopped the car and got out to see what the [Item Moved/CMS/nd] he was whitnessing, it stopped, hovered a momnent, then shot off. This was in 2006 or 2007, June. Sorry I cant remember the year any better. I felt like my heart was going to burst, my throat was clogged with wonder and excitement of curiosity, and I must say, it changed my opinion on what may be around us without our knowing. I would love to experience it again. Even at a distance, it was one of the most beautiful speciticals I have ever seen.
