{tjd} Was in back of home looking for meteors and first saw cloudiness beyond neighbors 80 ft. tree, then color changes, then a black

Was on back side of my house looking south for meteor showers away from street lights at front of house; no clouds; moon was in the east. I was out there about 5 minutes when I began to see an unusual "cloudish thing" through the branches of a large tree, half in and half out beyond the branches of the tree, which was about 80 feet away in neighbor's yard. I began thinking, "Where did this cloud come from?" It began to have colors, red and other faint colors. Within 2-3 seconds, the "cloud" disappeared. I then saw a black, tube-shaped object. In size, it was maybe a little larger than than if I spread out my thumb and index finger and held them at arm's length. It looked totally solid and black. I watched through the tree branches as it slowly moved to the east. It was up about 35 degrees, and lower than the top of the tree. I heard no sound from the object. It seemed like it was 1/4 to 1/2 mile away. I am near two freeways, I-75 & I-696; I could hear sounds of traffic on the freeways. I have no idea where the object came from; it seemed to appear from nowhere. I lost sight of it as it continued to the East.
