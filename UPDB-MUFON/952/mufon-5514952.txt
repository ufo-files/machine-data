(GS) 3 SMALL MEN WERE STANDING AT THE FOOT OF MY COUCH, THEY PARALYZED ME AND TELEPATHICALLY TOLD ME IF THEY COULD DO THIS TO ME THEY COULD DO THIS TO OUR NUCLEAR WEAPONS

I HAD BEEN SLEEPING AND GOT UP AND WENT TO LIE DOWN ON THE COUCH.
JUST THEN I BECAME PARALYZED.
I TRIED TO OPEN MY EYES AND WITH GREAT DIFFICULTY
DIED GET THEM OPEN ALITTLE BIT AND SAW THREE LITTLE MEN STANDING AT THE FOOT OF THE COUCH.
THEY WERE WEARING SKIN TIGHT UNIFORMS AND HAD BLACK, REPTILIAN SKIN.
ONE OF THEM WAS MISSING HIS HEAD, ANOTHER WAS MISSING HIS FEET, BUT ONE WAS ALL THERE.

THEROUGH T;LE[ATHY THEY TOLD ME THEY COULD PARALYZE OUR NUCLEAR WEAPONS.

THEN I WAS PUT TO SLEEP SUDDENLY WHILE FEELING SHEER TERROR.

JANE A.
