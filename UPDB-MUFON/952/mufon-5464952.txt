{cak} I saw a ufo and an alien.

I was sleeping one October night. It was a full moon. I heard a whirring, humming noise coming from outside. When I woke up, I saw a UFO coming toward my window from the woods to the side of my house. I observed a beam of light that had an alien-like creature that landed in my yard. It looked at me and I thought it looked like a teenage alien. I watched as the UFO sucked the alien creature back up in a beam of light. I was excited, scared and worried. I did not think my parents would believe me. I watched the UFO out my window until it flew out of sight. When I went back to bed, I could still see a light but the UFO was gone.
