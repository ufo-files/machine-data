Two luminous spheres in the sky looking like stars during the day

On November 22, 2012, I was lying in a hammock in my backyard. It was late afternoon, it was still daylight, and I was looking at the sky when I saw a "star" almost in the zenith (~ 90 degrees). It was a white object, like, in fact, a "star" and was apparently immobile. It was impossible to know its altitude, but it seemed far away. I found it strange to see a "star" at a still bright time, so I took my Canon SX-40HS camera, which has a powerful 30x zoom, to try to see this phenomenon better. Then, at 7:19 pm (it was actually 6:19 pm because we had summer time), with the maximum zoom, I took a first picture of this object (IMG_2174), which appeared as a small white shiny sphere. A few seconds later, I noticed from the camera's viewfinder that the object moved and a slightly smaller object came out of that first object. They then started to move away from each other, at the same time that they started to move in a curve, clockwise, in relation to each other. I managed to take two more pictures (IMG_2175 and 76) of the two objects moving away and moving (7:19 pm and 7:20 pm). it lasted about 1 minute. At 7:20 pm the smaller object disappeared and I took one last photo of the larger object (IMG_2178), which was still moving slowly. And then, that last object also disappeared, like the other. I couldn't find an explanation for that. And I have seen images of UFOs on television programs that are very similar to what I saw.
