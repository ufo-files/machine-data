{cak} None moving, two parellel/perpendicular red-orange spheres hovering stationary.

I was coming home from a basketball game we were right by my house and my dad told my friend and I to look up at the sky towards the SE. We all looked up out of the car windows and saw 2 stationary red-orange spheres kind of star-like but bigger. They were just hovering there. Then I go and get out of the car and so does everyone else and they were gone. We waited and looked around for about 5 minutes but they vanished. My friend took a video but then when we went back to look at it the phone said that there was no more memory.
