{nd} Glowing dull red, dimmed to grey, very slowly wobbled away.

I was walking back from the gas station, saw what I thought was Mars. Circle or sphere glowing a dull red, hovering in place. As I stopped and stared at it, the red glow dimmed away, leaving the craft looking a grey color. It slowly and steadily drifted over all the apartment complexes on my road, wobbling slightly (best way I can describe it) as it went, until it was out of sight. I tried to take photos and video with my phone but its a total piece. Zoomed one picture where *something* showed up, but it looks really bad.
