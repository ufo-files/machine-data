On that day, a man was buying chewing betel/paan from my aunt's shop. I was sitting there in the shop doing idle talk with her. The man was waiting bus for home. When the bus came, as soon as he got up, he said "that's colorful plane!"
At first he might be thinking it was the aircraft as the state's only airport is just few km away from my residence.
Me and my aunt came out of the shop and stared at the object together. Three of us were stunned to see the L shaped or cylindrical craft flying 1undisturbed1 in parallel path.
