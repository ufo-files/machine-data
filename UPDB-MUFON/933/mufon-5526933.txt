LOOKED LIKE A VERY BRIGHT STAR IN NIGHT SKY (VERY BRIGHT)

MY WIFE AND I WENT WALKNG WE WALKED OUT OUR FRONT DOOR @ 8:00PM ON THE DOT AND THE FIRST THING WE DO EVERY NIGHT IS CHECK THE SKIES, WE SAW AN UNUSUAL VERY BRIGHT STAR LIKE OBJECT IN SOUTHEAST SKY WE STARTED WALKING AND NOTICED THIS THING STARTED MOVING TWORDS US WE THOUGHT IT WAS A PLANE AND WE WERE LOOKING @ LANDING LIGHTS BUT THIS THING TURNED AND THE LIGHT WAS STILL VERY BRIGHT IT NEVER DIMMED LIKE LANDING LIGHTS IF A PLANE WERE TO TURN AWAY FROM YOU. ALSO NO AVIATION LIGHTS. WE SAW IT GO OFF INTO THE DISTANCE AND STOPPED AGAIN WHERE WE COULD BARELY SEE IT WE STARED FOR A GOOD 10 TO 15 MIN. AND WENT ON WITH OUR WALK. THIS THING WAS DEFINATLY NOT A PLANE AND IT WAS BIG WHATEVER IT WAS. WE SEE STRANGE THINGS ALMOST EVERY NIGHT AROUND 8:00PM , KEEP YOUR EYES TO THE SKY THEY ARE ALL AROUND.
