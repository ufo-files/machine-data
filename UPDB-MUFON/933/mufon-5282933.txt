Lights moving in synchrony and then fast change of direction

Sitting in our family room, looking south at 10pm, my wife and I saw 2 odd lights approaching quickly over the mountain tops. We live on a hill with extensive views.  The lights were spread slightly apart with one being significantly brighter. They stopped forward progress then one light abruptly circled the other and then the both turned south and shot over the horizon at an incredible speed
