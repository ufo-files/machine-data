My wife and I were driving home from her parents house when we noticed a strange low flying object that seemed to go from a hovering stationary position to picking up speed very quickly. We followed the one until we were right under it. My wife was terrified it was just above the tree tops in a highly populated area on the northwest side of Chicago. It looked like a military drone. It soon picked up speed and altitude and we thought it was gone. We then noticed 4-5 more almost circling our neighborhood. We live about 3 miles from ohare and noticed no air traffic flying into the air port. These drone like crafts were only flying from the west coming away from the air port. We did see one green or black helicopter in the distance but it only stayed around for a couple minutes. These crafts were very quiet. The planes by us are always very loud. It was very strange I never experienced anything like that before.
