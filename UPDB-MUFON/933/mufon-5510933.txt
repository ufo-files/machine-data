(GS) 2 bright lights that faded out independently

On May 8th 2013 I was walking out of my local walmart in Sebring, Fl headed towards my car. I walked out the doors of the general merchandise side of the building and looked before crossing into the parking lot. Shortly after getting into the main drag coming into the store something told me in the back of my head to look up. Just as I did I happened to notice 2 extremely bright yellow, yellow/orange lights in the sky. Im not sure how high into the air they were but they were side by side, towards McDonalds across Hwy 27. Then out of the blue the one on the left just faded out kind of like someone turned it off with a dimmer switch, and a few seconds later the other one did the same thing. Thats when I happened to notice that I was not alone in viewing this, about 10 other shoppers in the parking lot had noticed it as well.
