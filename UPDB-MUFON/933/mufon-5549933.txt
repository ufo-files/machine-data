A nearly blind man observed an apparently odd red light near his rural home andcalled his wife out. She viewed a bulbous disc with a flat top and bottom (i.e.,like inverted bowls) moving very slowly below treetop level between 25 and 50yards away. A series of rectangular windows in its upper and lower hemisphereswere separated by narrow black partitions. Within the upper portion, a red lightslowly brightened and dimmed. A red band of steady red light separated itshemisphere.The object continued to move slowly into the distance until lost from view.Duration estimated to be 15 minutes.
