Two large glowing orange spheres over residential street

While walking at night (9 p.m.), my husband and I saw two large glowing orange-yellow spheres over residential street. They were silent, and seemed to be hovering over a house. They headed east and seemed to move up and down and side to side as they traveled. The light was bright and kind of fluttery. It was a very clear night. We did not notice any wind.
