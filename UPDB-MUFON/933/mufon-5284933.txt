Triangular shaped object ,red center light with 3 orange lights on each side in V shape

Star gazing on 10-4-2021 at 5:10 am . Suddenly noticed a very large object come over the horizon with 7 lights ,one red,in the center and 3 orange on each side. First thought was a plane but there was no sound. Came directly toward my house , moving at a very moderate to slow speed. The closer it got , I couldn't get over the size and no sound. Larger than a C-17 and no sound. Flew directly overhead . Dark shell and the seven lights. As best as I can describe it was similar to a boomarang.
Made a gradual turn to the east out toward the ocean. I heard a whir or low vibration as it was almost out of sight , but not loud. Hard to believe something that size ,less than a mile high , could move that slowly with no sound.
