{nd} Observed triangular craft, ascended a very high altitude in a split second, then made an abrupt turn

While driving home around dusk we observed odd white lights hovering in the sky at a low altitude maybe 500- 1000 feet. Several other drivers noticed and pulled over/slammed on their breaks, we continued driving however to try to get to a better spot. Upon getting closer to the object, you could see three distinct white lights forming a triangle shape, with a faint triangular outline that blended in with the sky but you could tell there was something there. The object hovered in place for maybe a minute, and then it shot up vertically into the air at an incredible speed, ascending maybe 30,000 feet in half a second. Then made an abrupt right angle northerly, and disappeared.
