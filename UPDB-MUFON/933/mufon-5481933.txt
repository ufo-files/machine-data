Small but fast light seen in night sky, emitting barely any light, starts at the North west then slowly descends diagnolly towards the south east

The time it started was around 11:36. We had just come from dinner with a couple of good friends. We were in Vienna, Austria when it happened and I was pretty surprised to see so many stars for a city's night sky. A few minutes later, we saw a strange, small light going at pretty incredible speeds for a spacecraft. First we thought it was a satellite or an airplane but then it started making curves along the way towards the south east. After a few minutes, my friends went back inside the house (we were staying with them) but I decided to stay a little longer to see what was going to happen with the spacecraft. After a few seconds, out of a sudden the object started shaking around wildly in all directions with a brighter light than before and then the object just disappeared like that. A few seconds later, I just stood there staring into the night sky without really any sort of emotion. It was as if nothing at all happened. After the event, I was still not as petrified as I thought I would be in this sort of event.
