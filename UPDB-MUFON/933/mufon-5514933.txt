(GS) Many Reddish Orange Lights in somewhat of a Triangle Formation.

Me and my wife saw this also, we were heading down 2nd ave got to pierce st. intersection and notice them I stopped the car short in disbelief and could not belieave what we were seeing, here is what we saw, We went for coffees , heading towards turkey hill, we live on 2nd ave in kingston so we get to the intersection of 2nd ave and pierce street, and straight in front of us is about 8 lights in the sky about I would say at a 45 degree angle from our car up , not in any formation but I would say they were in the shape of the number 7 with about 3 lights at the top then 1 in the middle then 3 more then one at the bottom, they were so distinct that I screeched the jeep to a stop and got out in the middle of the road to look at them, then one by one starting from the bottom they disappeared slowly, I swear on my dads grave we saw this
they were so bright We each do not have smart phones or any phone that would take any decent picture so we did not get a picture, unfortunately.
