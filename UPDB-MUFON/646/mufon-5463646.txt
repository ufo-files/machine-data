Looked like a shooting star but greenish and changed direction like a reverse tick

I was on my balcony taking with my friend on a call and I was looking out into the sky because you could see the stars, it was summer so it was nice and I could hear the crickets. I did this every night for a week so everything was going as it usually did. I saw this flash of light in the sky, it was blueish green at its most bright part and changed direction so it looked like a reverse tick (✔️). It was gone in 2-3 seconds, the whole path disappeared into the air like it never happened. The speed at which it travelled was almost impossible or the speed of light since I thought it was a shooting star at first but they don1t change direction nor are they green.
