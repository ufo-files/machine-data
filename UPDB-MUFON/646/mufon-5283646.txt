giant sphere

But with it you could see the surface of the sphere, only what i could say what it was, like seeing oil on water reflecting the sun all the different colors. it was in the sky over and hour. i had to take my date home, when we got to her house we try to call the Arlington airport but line was busy. never heard anything about for years. i have since retired from a defense com. i wish not to say. i know a little about aircraft and rockets since i was a inspector. i always wonder if anyone else came forward with the same story. i heard of a story about what it might have been but the person said he would deny that he ever told me the story.
