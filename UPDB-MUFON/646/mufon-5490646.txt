Hovering in view of my Son's window.

My 15 year old son actually sent me this photograph, in fact a series of them. There were a number of planes in the south as there were fireworks that night, and amateur pilots sometimes come to watch them, but he observed this object in the opposite direction and unlike the planes because it was: in the other direction, hovering, and did not display coloured navigation lights as the planes did. I was out, and he was really surprised by it, and started sending me pictures to see what I made of it. I think he felt kind of spooked by it, although he was not really sure why. He's a practical, smart kind of kid and not prone to flights of fancy or easily frightened. After a few minutes it just disappeared. I was not far away and tried to see the object at the same time but the area I was in was heavily bordered by trees.
