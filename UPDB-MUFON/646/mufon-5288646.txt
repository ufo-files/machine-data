ball of light

On July 22, 2015
At 9 o'clock in the evening, as soon as I reached the roof, I saw a ball of light in the direction of 75 degrees east, which was fixed.
It shone with different lights and there was a green halo around the object.
It was twice the size of Venus.
Suddenly, the object became brighter and larger, about the size of a full moon.
Then a black tunnel came out of the object, twisted, and moved forward in the sky until it was 20 degrees away from the object.
Then suddenly the object jumped in the tunnel and went through the whole tunnel at high speed and came out from the other side. Then it made another 45 degree jump and the sky jumped and disappeared.
In the second jump, a white trail formed behind the object.
I fainted when I looked at the object.
I heard a strange voice in my head that said: We will not forget you
