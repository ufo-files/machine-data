Just past Midnight April 7, 2014. I was as usual, standing on the 2nd floor balcony/walkway right outside my front door. I star and sky gaze. Noticing something coming from the right(West) and alignment with the length of the condo building amazed me. As it got closer I saw and counted THREE(3) white lights on each point, of what appeared to be a triangle. ONE (1) red light was located in the middle of the three lights of the triangle. Red light pulsated - not fast, it went on... off... on... off...  It was close and over-head. If it were flying higher or more over the building I could not have spotted it. It continued moving in a straight line and then turned and went south proceeding over the Gulf. I watched until it was out-of-sight. A sketch was sent via text and email to Lechner - MUFON. A powerful experience that gave me the chills and I felt that whatever hair was on my body was standing on end.
