{td} Observed object hovering for 2 hours before it took off and disappeared

Walked outside my house approximately 9 in the evening April 15th and I could see with the naked eye a flashing in the sky observed for a few minutes called my children my son and daughter both came out my son with binoculars to get a closer look and we were amazed it hovered there for 2 hours Boomerang shaped flashing green red blue lights. We stayed outside and talked and the object stayed in the sky was not a plane helicopter nor anything else known to any of us. After a couple hours we took a break came in for something to drink when we came back outside it was gone no sign of it anywhere there was no noise nothing.
