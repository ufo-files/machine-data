{cak} At first it was radiating like a star over the clouds

In this area I have always seen strange objects but it had been a while since anything had happened. After eating dinner I step outside to view the clouds for about twenty minutes or so, and before I knew it behind some clouds I noticed this object that looked like a star but it was rotating because one moment you saw it shine off and on in the same place for about 20 minutes or so. Mean while commercial planes are flying under it as they flew westbound and other's northeast but this object still looked way higher then the jets passing by. Before I knew it was gone with the clouds but then as I'm talking to myself as to what this object is it appeared again. This time it's above me and as the sun is setting I began to see a little red on it but honestly don't know if the red was coming from the setting of the sun. 15 minutes after my wife came out to tell me where going home, I said in my mind "see you soon" but when I got home and looked up it was nowhere to be found.
