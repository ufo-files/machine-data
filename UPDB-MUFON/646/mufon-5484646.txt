Cloud-like form passing between 2 clouds with dark winged shape inside.

On April 28 2015, during a vacation at the Melia Cayo Guillermo hotel in Cuba, I was relaxing in one of the sun chairs near the exterior pool after supper. It was shortly after sunset, the sky was still lighted and a few clouds were visible in the sky. As I was relaxing watchig the sky above, a cloud form shape appeared between 2 stationnary clouds. This one was travelling straight. As the form passed over me, I could see some kind of wing shaped craft inside. The craft was brighter, but the wings were not engineered like common plane wings. The looked more part of a triangular shaped craft and had some indentations around them. The sighting lasted about 30 seconds, the object disapeared behind another cloud formation. I did not even had the time to show it to my girlfriend who was resting on another chair near me. I could not hear any plane sound around and that's what caught my attention. It felt to me that this craft or whatever it was, was travelling with a kind of cloud shield to avoid beeing seen from the ground. Back at home, I still had some questions about what I've seen. I did some reaserch on the internet and found 2 similar cases in the MUFON files. 

I also found a video on youtube with the title [Title removed/cms/tg]. And the same strange cloud form was filmed hovering around clouds in the sky.
