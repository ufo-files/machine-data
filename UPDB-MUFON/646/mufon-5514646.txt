{nd} observed 7 white orbs in triangle formation then broke up and formed a broken line

My wife and I was traveling east on I-69 got off on exit 153 and went south then back west towards house, when we saw seven brights lights in a triangle formation moving towards us. Then they broke up and scattered out of formation about 30 to 40 deg. above the west sky. they were moving slowly up,down,back and forth for about 10-15 min. then slowly disappered. they appered twice the size as mars or venus this all happened 7:40 to 7:55
 Our neighbors and a number of people around our lake seen this also, also reported it to the lapeer county sheriff dept
