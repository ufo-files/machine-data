My son and I were looking out the second story bedroom window when we noticed slow moving bright light moving from left to right very slowly. When a plane appeared the object would go dark and disappear, then reappear after plane vanished. I grabbed my digital camera and stated taking pictures. When I took some pictures the object was there, but when I looked at the camera it was not there. This happened several times. I called Union City Police to report it. They gave me the phone number to Mufon. I called and made my report. The Mufon Representative seem to try to debunk what I saw. He said that I was seeing Venus or a plane. I told him that I was a former Navy veteran aircraft mechanic and I know the difference between a plane and venus. I got furstrated and said ok, without telling him that I had pictures.
