Up right cylinder- pulsating & then a patterned blinking that moved from the north towards Ronan MT, to the east over the Mission mountains.

Driving home from Missoula MT, to Saint Ignatius MT two family members witnessed a up right cylinder glowing & pulsating then would blink in a pattern. It traveled from the North from around Ronan MT to above the Mission Mountains in Saint Ignatius at 6:22pm on February 10, 2022 it was visible 20+mins in the daylight.
