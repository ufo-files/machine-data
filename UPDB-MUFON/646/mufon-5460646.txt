{rjl} I witnessed about 100 orbs, in a line,,

It was 11:30pm, on May 24, 2019.. I had just layed down, in bed.. I looked straight up, from window, next to my head..  I could see, what first looked like a meteor, but quickly noticed, something is odd, about this meteor.. It was moving at the speed of an passenger airline.... The line of orbs was about 10deg long, most of the orbs, were up at the front of line, and the number of orbs dropped off, the further back in the line... The orbs appeared to be stationary in the line of orbs.. It looked like a meteor, slowed down in time... It continued NE, until it was out of sight..
