{rjl} Highly Reflective Object Moving At About 80 MPH

The witness stated that he was walking about one-half mile from his home when he saw a highly reflective/oval object moving from the Northwest to the Southeast. His impression was that it was coming from the Duane Arnold Energy Center and passing over Marion,Iowa which is only about two miles from Rockwell International. Chinook helicopters were reportedly seen traveling the same flight path two hours later. The next day, two Chinook helicopters were seen traveling the same flight path.
