triangular craft dark with 3 high intensity white lights on each corner

whilst driving home approximately 2 miles east of the village of glencoe me and my friend noticed what firstly appeared to be a very bright white light heading west towards us at a rather slow speed i found this strange as it is not an aircraft flight path so we assumed it was a helicopter but as we continued to approach each other the one white light soon became three in the shape of a perfect triangle at this point we new this was no helicopter as it continued towards us at a steady pace the three light intensified in brightness almost blinding i then stopped my vehicle with mountains on both sides of me in close proximity and i started to flash my headlight constantly and we both put our windows down and could hear no sound but as i continued to flash my lights the craft was now almost overhead stopped and made an unbelievable left turn not a sweeping turn the now visibly triangular craft dark in color turned on the spot and headed off north the three white lights went of and three dimmer red lights started to flash and it unfortunately disappeared out of our site due to the close proximity of the mountains i would like to add the sky's were clear and a bright full moon was visible and its shape was easily identified on the moonlit sky i can categorically say this was no aircraft i have ever seen making such manouvers with no sound i would estimate it was roughly two to three hundred feet up and each side roughly eighty to a hundred feet my friend said it was if they seen us stopped to look and turned away from us quite unbelievable if i hadn't seen it with my own eyes
