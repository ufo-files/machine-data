Randomly Blinking Light

At 2:37AM EDT I was looking at the sky directly overhead. Caught a flash of light lasting a few tenths of a second. I stared intently in the same area and over the course of about a minute and a half, I saw approximately 6-7 more flashes. It was a yellowish gold light, roughly equal to a medium bright star. There were light clouds to the west but none in the area I was viewing. This light moved some distance between flashes in random directions but stayed within an area roughly the size of 3-4 full moons. It did not seem to be following a linear path. I live near a medium airport and look at planes all the time. This was not a plane nor helicopter. Also no air traffic here at this time of night. I’m a satellite spotter and am always looking at the sky. This was not a satellite. I fly drones, and this did not appear to be a drone. There were no marker/nav lights visible. It was a single faint light which flashed on for just an instant, and after an irregular delay of about 10-15 seconds would flash again. My phone camera is not up to the task of capturing such a thing and I opted to observe rather than try to get a (bad) photo. As I said, I look at the sky nightly, and this was unlike anything else I have seen. It didn’t move like an object in orbit (ie in a linear way.) It didn’t move like a plane. A drone could move in such a fashion, but the behavior of the light was not typical of a drone.
