{rjl} Bright circular light, first thought it was a star but it moved, then hovered and moved occasionally for more than 1 hour. Have still photos and 30 minutes of phone video.

Outside my house at 8 pm I noticed an unusual star constellation of 1 bright and 4 smaller stars. Then I saw it was moving and thought it was a plane. The one bright star, circular and bright white, made almost a 90 degree turn. Then it hovered. Now I thought it was a star or Venus. But I had seen it move. So I watched for several minutes until it moved again almost fluttering like a butterfly. I took 6 photos with my cell phone. The object appears star-like in several. Then zooming in I took several less clear photos of something that was obviously circular. I saw the object moving with my naked eyes so turned on the cell phone video 4 or 5 times for a total of about 30 minuets. I braced my elbow on a chair so the video is steady and caught the movement many times. I zoomed in and caught more movement. The zooming caused a momentary camera movement and the object moved out of frame several times causing me to recenter the image but the vast majority of the movement was by the object. I even have a sequence in which a plane crosses the screen closer to th camera, indicating the distance of the object and the steadiness of the camera. After an hour I went to bed. Key points. It was not a plane or helicopter and it remained in the same portion of the sky for at least an hour. It looked as bright as Venus but was a complete circle. It moved many times and moved up, down, left and right and diagonally.
