Did not see live. Found in photograph after downloading to computer.

I took a scenic photo from the highway pullout using about a 35mm lens on a Nikon D7200. The UFO was located over the Pacific Ocean, roughly at an altitude of about 500-600 feet. Probably about 1/2 to 3/4 mile from me based on having seen hang gliders from this location.

Recently got the MUFON email about T.L. Keller's photo of a UFO over Stillwater, MN - Case # 106985. That photo matches my photo. Interestingly, my photo was taken on August 9 and Keller's photo on August 20, both in 2018.

Note the superstructure, termed a 'cupola', in Keller's report. It is similar to my photo. I thought the "lines" of the cupola may be structural parts of the "cockpit" of the craft.

I interpreted the main body of the craft's shape as wider on the top half and canting inward on the sides to a narrower bottom.
