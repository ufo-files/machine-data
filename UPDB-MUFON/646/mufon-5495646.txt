{dr}two arch shapes glowing near each other about 6 lights each

my husband and I were out on our back deck just at dusk..we live in a rural area with hills and mountains...looking to the North we saw two objects in the sky...both were the same hovering near each other...arch shaped with 5 or 6 lights pulsating...I ran in to get the binoculars and when I came back they were gone...less than a 30 second observation but we both saw them and they just disintegrated or disappeared. It was dusk and the skies were clear...Not the first sighting out here after being in the same house 25 years, but definitely the closest and the clearest. These were NOT planes...they were there one moment and gone the next....Ive been periodically going out and looking in that direction and have seen nothing.
