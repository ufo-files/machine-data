{td}high altitude white light flashing approx. every 3-4 seconds

I was letting the dog out to go and I always look up at the stars and to see if there are any aircraft in the area. There is a flight path almost directly over my house and commercial jets pass over every few minutes so I am use to seeing conventional craft. I saw a flash of light to the southeast in the upper right portion of Orion. As I did so I also noticed a jet passing below Orion, moving to the west southwest at a much lower altitude. The flashing light, which flashed approximately every 3-4 seconds, at first did not seem to be moving in a specific direction, but moved around randomly in a small area. Upon noticing that there were no red or green lights like normal craft, I stepped inside, grabbed my binoculars and stepped back out to observe this anomaly that appeared to be at high altitude. The binoculars showed the same as I could see unaided. A single bright flashing light, but by this time it was moving in definite eastish direction. It moved slower than commercial jets I have seen at similar altitudes. I was slightly excited to be observing something out of the ordinary and lost sight in the binoculars a couple of times. I then got it in my sights, and as it moved eastward, though still high in the sky it started to become dimmer (last 2 flashes) as if it was increasing in altitude rapidly, then disappearing completely.
