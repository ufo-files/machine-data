{rjl} Phoenix lights craft seen in day light.

I was out exploring off road and took a break on the Honeymoon trail. 1/4 mile away to the east is a 500 foot ridge. I was looking up the ridge to see if any mountain goats were on it. Suddenly appearing in the sky above the ridge was a Chevron shaped craft coming from behind me. It actually just appeared in the sky and moving lightning fast then going behind the ridge. Because it appeared in the middle of my view indicates to me it slowed down enough for my vision to see it. It was the classic Flying V with 3 belly lights on each leg. Point, pointing forward. It was a considerable distance away and yet still looked gigantic compared to jet airliners I've seen at that distance.
