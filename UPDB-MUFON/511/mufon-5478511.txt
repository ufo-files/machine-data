{rjl} Saw a moving object that stopped and then went in a 90-degree Direction then It sped up and disappeared.

In August of 1993 I was on a date with a woman I had meet a couple of days before. After we had dinner she drove us to a secluded rest stop about 20 to 30 minutes north of downtown Spokane, WA. We Parker car she put the roof down on her convertible and we got in the backseat, I don't remember how long we were there but I was amazed how many stars I could see because there wasn't any "light pollution". I was looking straight up and noticed (what I thought at first was a shooting star) a star or baseball light bright light moving diagonally from the top right down to the bottom left of the night sky. Then it stopped for about 1-3 minutes then it went in a completely different direction (901 from the direction it came from) speed off and vanished. As soon as we lost sight of it both of us had goosebumps, our heads felt "fuzzy" and we left the area immediately and went back into town. Before what I saw I had a photographic memory and then afterwards about a day later parts of my short term and long term memory were and still to this day are blank. I'm including a drawing of what I saw and how it moved.
