We were observing the Jupiter/Saturn conjunction and were looking off to the Western sky. The moon was right above us. No street lighting present and no outdoor lighting. Neither were there any autos anywhere nearby. There were a number aircraft at much lower altitudes than this light appeared to be. 

When looking at it with the naked eye it looked cigar shaped and had yellow or orange lights on either end and one in the middle. Then it looked more rounded. Bright white white. Through the binoculars it looked jagged but essentially circular.

We started observing the planet conjunction around 6:30pm and happened to look directly overhead and saw this object apparently not moving. It did seem to drift a bit very very slowly as though keeping station. We did not have the binoculars with us at this time and had to leave to perform some chores. At about 715 to 7:20 pm I went back outside with high power binoculars and the object was still in the same position with the light side of the moon facing away from it.  It still seemed to drift just to keep station. I took the attached pictures and enlarged one.

The object looks more like a group of closely aligned roundish very silvery white balls when viewed this way. I am hoping I'm not the only one who saw this or that there is a reasonable explanation for it. I had to leave again so I do not know what happened to the object.
