{rjl} white orb making figure eights, in and out of a cloud top, observed it for 15 minute maybe more

I was at home, it was close to sundown. After playing in backyard I was laying on my back in the grass and had been watching these tall clouds that developed to the east of town. The sunset had turned them into a magnificent cloud formations, anyway i notice at the top of one of these clouds, there was an orb. It made figure of 8's, in and out of the cloud top. It could have been 20,00 ft up and 6-8 mi. away. I thought it was kite, we had always flown them as kids. I watched it for fifteen minutes at least, could not take my eyes off it. It was far away my eyes couldn't focus on it after awhile moving in and out of the cloud. Ive never stopped replaying that moment in my head, i'll never forget it.
