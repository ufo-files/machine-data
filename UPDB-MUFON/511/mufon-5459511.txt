{tjd} Communication with ufo in the sky.

I was 16 or 17 years old. I was sitting outside of my mother's apartment playing with a new army flashlight I just bought. This light was meant to signal aircraft from the ground and could change between the colors Red, Yellow, Blue and Green.
I was playing with this flashlight when I saw an "airplane " flying at a low altitude over my house. I flashed a random sequence of colors up towards this craft. That's when it stopped on a dime mid air. Like a strobe, this craft flashed back the colors I flashed to it. Its neutral color was white, like a really bright star, but it was much, much lower than a star. 
I was stunned and flashed another sequence of colors. It immediately repeats the colors again. We did this for what seemed like a fairly long time. Then I realized what I was doing, and what I might cause so I got spooked and quit. This did happen.
