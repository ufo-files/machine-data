Just a very slow moving ball of light

On february 27th 2018 I was just coming home from working the night shift doing security at around 6:30 am and as I exited my truck I caught a glimpse of a bright ball of light slowly moving to the north north east , there was no sound coming from it . I quickly grabbed my camera that I always carry with me cause I am a freelance photographer and started shooting video and at that time is when I heard a faint helicopter sound and noticed that this ball of light was being followed by a helicopter , I have seen plenty of Satellites and airplanes but this was not anything I have ever seen .
