{nd} Triangle shape brightly lit up no sound traveled straight N fast

Outside looking at stars facing south. Saw object triangle shape all lit up heading north. There was no sound as it glided past heading north. It moved at a fairly quick speed. As I tried to follow it's direction it seemed to vanish. This happened at 9:57 pm. I know the time because as I came into the house the tv show Vikings had just began. I have seen ufo's in my area in the past but never filed a report. I knew what I saw and know it was not a bird or airplane. It gave off it's own light. I was excited when I saw it and disappointed when it seemed to disappear. I immediately came into the house and told my boyfriend what I saw. I continued to look for it but did not see it again.
