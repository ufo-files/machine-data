{kt}Orange lights in the sky Ufo

On July 6th at approximately 930pm, my mother and I were driving the car down the road and in the dark sky we saw about 10-14 rectangular very orange glowing beams that hovered in pairs. We stopped in the road and just watched in shock. We watched for about 4 minutes and the all disappeared as the seemed to be going more north.
