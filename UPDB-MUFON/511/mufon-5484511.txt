It is now 2016 but since 1982 I have wondered if I had a very vivid dream or not. I cannot remember the month only that my son was just over two years old. I went to bed as normal but was woken by an intense light, I was communicating with something but unsure what, I found it friendly and I had a sense of ease, I felt that this went on for quite a while, but they what ever it was, then focused on my son, my reaction was disturbing to them and they were gone like a flash, I do remember that when they were gone I found I was in my sons bedroom. Even after twenty four years I still remember and am just as confused now as then. I obviously have had dreams before and after but never quite recall the whole dream, certainly not as detailed as the 1982 episode, Any idea why. I cannot remember the date or month but think it was feb/march of 1982.
