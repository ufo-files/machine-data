6 lights in a row, with directional change and then leaving one at a time in rapid succession

I was sitting outside of my home, watching the stars as I usually did at night. I noticed what I thought was an odd line of stars, which were a yellow/orange colour all spaced equally apart from one another. After I had focused my attention on them, I watched as one slowly started to move off to the side and then slowly moved forward and vanished. After the first left, the next did the exact same pattern as the first. Within two minutes (give or take) they had all disappeared after following the same pattern. While I was watching it, I couldn't believe what I was seeing. After they had left, I sat in a daze and then went inside with a knot in my stomach- realizing I had just seen something similar to when I was a child.
