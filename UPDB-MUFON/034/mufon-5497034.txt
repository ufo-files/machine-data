As I was driving down the road, I observed approximately 5 amber colored lights, east of my location. The lights were evenly spaced and traveling at a steady rate west bound. I stopped my vehicle and got out to try to take video and pictures. I noticed that there was no sound coming from the objects. I couldn't tell how high they were or any type of shape or form, just lights. I noticed a light or two fade out then reappear but am unsure if that was the objects going behind cover of some sort, like clouds or trees etc. The objects continued west until I couldn't see them behind the trees. I was thrilled to see an unidentifiable object. I contacted the local police dispatch and they had not received any reports of what I had seen.
