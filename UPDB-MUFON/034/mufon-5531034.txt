Horizontal cylinder with approx. 10 round white lights in a row left to right about 2 thousand feet in altitude hovering.

I was just driving home from Manhattan, (NYC) going Northbound on the Palisades Interstate Parkway. I was descending a steep hill just approaching the New Jersey / New York border when I saw a long horizontal bank of round white lights in a row about 2000 feet in the sky. At first I thought it was a new building or athletic field in the hills in front of me. I then realized as I was going down hill that it was in the sky. There were no hills there. The object was in a hovering / still position. As I was going down the hill focusing on the row of lights in the sky I suddenly saw a bright yellow contained flash around the object and the bank of lights was gone. When the flash happened and the object was gone I realized I probably had just seen a UFO. The flash as I said was just contained around where the object was in the sky, the whole panarama of sky didn't light up like it does with regular lightning. That's when it hit me that this was something very very strange. Like nothing I had experienced in my short 55 years here!! I was and I'm still very exited and a little un nerved by what I saw. There were no other "lightning" type of flashes for the duration of my time on the parkway (approx. 15 minutes). I just wanted to stop the cars in front of me to ask those drivers if they saw what I saw. It looked a lot like the recent sighting in China I believe that stopped air traffic for a while. Very strange!!
