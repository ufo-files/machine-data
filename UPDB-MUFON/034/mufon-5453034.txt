Objects flying in column at extreme high altitude

At 2132 on the evening of 4 May 2020, I observed what I first thought was a satellite at very high altitude. The first moving object I sighted appeared directly below Venus. I watched as the faintly glowing object faded and about the same moment it faded another appeared below Venus. Objects continued to appear in column evenly spaced for another 8 minutes. Each new object appeared to be in the exact same path as the objects ahead. I think more than a dozen objects were moving in this column from North to South. The objects appeared faintly below Venus and then faded out like a distant satellites. The speed of the objects was very similar to the speed I have observed with the ISS. The skies were clear but I was in a well lit area in a stadium parking lot. No blinking lights or exhaust trails were observed.
