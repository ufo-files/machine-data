On October the 6th, 2018 at approx 6:45pm I witnessed something strange in the sky, I was outside waiting for a meeting to start and it appeared, I looked around to see if anyone else was around to see it as well. All of a sudden I heard a voice holler "did you see that?" I knew then that I wasn't loosing my mind. The best way to describe it was that it was glowing a neon green color and was cylinder in shape. Anyone that is familiar with Glace Bay, it appeared out over the water in the area of the Bayplex, and it then took a path that had it head straight over towards Donkin, it had a high rate of speed and then it disappeared. Myself and the other witness attended our meeting and brought it up, and naturally we were riddled with "yeah yeah gowan, yeah right". The following week, we attended our meeting and an apology was given, as the father and a brother of one of our members witnessed the exact thing in Baddeck, the same night. There may be a perfect explanation for what was in the sky that night, I just wanted to share my story in case others seen what we have and possibly submitted their story so that there is more evidence of something certainly in the sky that night.
