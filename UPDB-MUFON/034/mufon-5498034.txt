Observing the crckers

I was bursting crackers on the eve of Diwali from my rooftop. I then saw some sources of lights coming from the west. After nearing me the stopped and started hovering around the place. I shot few rockets into the air two of which were absorbed by it.It kept on hovering there for 1.5hours.
