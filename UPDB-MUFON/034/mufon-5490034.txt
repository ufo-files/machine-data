{rjl} UFO moved silently. Red stationary light with translucent body that changed shape.

I was out on a balcony overlooking the Gulf of Mexico with my wife and two other family friends. One of my friends shouted "Is that a UFO?" Where pointed towards the SW. We all looked up and saw this UFO seemingly glide to the SW/W with no sound. There was a red dot in the middle of the UFO that did not blink. The outer edges of the UFO seemed to be wobbly and there seemed to be no solid state, it was always changing its shape. Imagine a amoeba flying, that's what i could describe the shape as. Around the solid red light, the color was white, but it was very translucent. I could see through it and it was going in a straight direction and was following the beach line until it disappeared behind a condo complex.
