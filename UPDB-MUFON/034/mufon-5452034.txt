Zig zag object

In the mid 60's I was about 13. While watching the local weather man Dan True channel 7. He reported on live TV there are UFOs in the area I immediately went outside to look for myself standing in the middle of the street looking to the West I soon saw a light zigzagging coming towards our town the next thing I noticed it stopped put sing to be a block away I can only see three lights red white blue I could also hear a humming sound which I could not identify at my age later I would find out electromagnetic. I also don't believe the vehicle was zigzagging I think it was the lights that were moving back and forth. I looked back to the West and saw another light approaching a single white light I looked at the UFO and again looked at the white light one more time looked at the UFO and it took off to the east again appearing to zigzag about a minute later the white light was actually a fighter jet from the Amarillo air Base at the time going full afterburners trying to catch this object.
