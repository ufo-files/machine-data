Two bright head lights like a car, that came on and off repeatedly. Just west of MSP Airport. It hovered for 5 mins then flew mostly south then turned and seemed to go north for about 10 min slow and low. It was dusk and there was red light on the back we could see when it turned. My thought was a police drone. It seemed to close to the airport for safety. We witnessed planes flying there routes at the same time. The object slowly made its way north and slightly west which eventually put it behind a tree and that was the end. A bit strange. I took video with my phone but it was worthless compared to the eye.
