{dr}Observed bright flare like silent object that changed shape and hovered towards me

I had dinner with a friend at the Rosemary Hall dining room. It was late March perhaps early April. We left shortly after dusk on a Sunday night. We had a 9PM curfew.i don't recall running which suggests that we had time. So I place the event at 7:45-8:00 ish. The dining hall is at the top of a hill adjacent to soccer fields and some classroom buildings. We observed a bright ball of light hovering over the Winter Exercise building. We both noted that it was odd and then continued on our way home. We paused again as we notice that the light had changed. It was no longer a shapeless ball of light bit had flattened into a disc shaped object such that if you were to place both of your forefingers so that the tips met and the thumb tips met the shape formed would be almost exactly what I observed. The object changed orientation. I noticed red and green lights rotating horizontally in a counter clock wise direction( if you were looking down on it). The craft began to move silently in our direction. No noise at all. This was not a plane, helicopter, or anything I had ever seen. We watch it come directly at us until panic set in and we sprinted back to the dining hall. We dragged the custodian out to see what we saw. We were not inside for longer than a minute. We returned it was gone. Mind you it was clear spring night. Stars were visible. From the soccer field we had a planetarium like view of the night sky. I have kept this relatively quiets for decades. Wondering is others witnessed this event.
