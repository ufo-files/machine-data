{flg} cross shaped object in NW sky. At first thought it was a bright star. dimmed quickly.

I was in the house about 9 - 9:15 PM on the night of July 21, 2012 when my mother started to yell for me to come to the rear patio. when I went I saw what I perceived as a star or planet in the sky. It was very bright. I looked for my binoculars and when I returned it had been hidden by the clouds and was barely visible. It was cross shaped and stationary in the sky. It could have been a comet.  I was curious if anyone else had seen something similar in South Texas.
