I finished a bike ride around 8:31 pm on December 11, 2016, turned around and saw about 10-20 Chinese Lantern-style objects in the sky, moving east to west. I have seen one similar object before, on August 26, 2013, over eastern Tallahassee, and reported it here. This time, 50 to 60 of these streamed by over a period of about 8-10 minutes, sometimes in bunches, sometimes singly. I recorded about three minutes of video on my phone camera. Unfortunately I missed recording a small propeller plane flying directly parallel to these lights, heading towards the Tallahassee Airport, I'd guess. The plane was basically at the same speed and altitude as these lights, and would show that these lights were moving faster than the wind. Also, the wind was flowing south or southwest at a slow speed at the time, not west, so the evidence points to these not being actual Chinese Lanterns. They don't seem to be drones since they made zero noise, and it would be probably illegal and not easy to fly dozens of drones from horizon to horizon.

Phone was a Sony Xperia Z5 Compact. I also have GPS data of my bike ride, showing that it ends at 8:31 PM. I also have audio recordings of phone calls with my brother taken during the event, but those will stay private, unless really needed.
