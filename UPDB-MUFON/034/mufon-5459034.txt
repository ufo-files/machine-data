{rjl} Lights moving around in a circular pattern just inside the clouds

As reported to ASD by phone: I was getting something in my car when I noticed lights moving around in a circular pattern just above the clouds. I could not tell if they were connected to a craft of some type or if they were independent objects, but they stayed in the exact same circle for approximately three minutes. I went inside to get my wife so she could see it and when we came back outside the lights were gone. There was no beam, just the lights themselves. There was no sound whatsoever, which I found odd since they seemed close enough that there should have been a sound if it was a conventional aircraft or even a drone. I've never seen anything like it - this was very strange. If I held my hand up it would have just covered the lights.
