{cak} bright light in day time sky

my wife spotted the object first --- we were in our lounge chairs in our yard observing the clouds because it had stormed off and on all day and the formations were interesting and pretty --- i thought it might be a satellite --- it was round and moved out of cloud cover on a straight line --- we were waiting for the sun to stop reflecting off the object so we could make out what it was but it wasnt the suns reflection --- the light being given off by the object was its own --- the object disappeared into the distance --- i felt like maybe this was the first ufo i have ever seen
