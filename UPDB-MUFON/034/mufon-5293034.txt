I was driving home from Knoxville around 10pm and saw a strobe light in the sky. I was on Hwy 11W going home.

I was heading home to Rutledge when all of sudden I looked up to my left and this strobe light was in the sky. I kept watching the object and tried to see where it was going as I kept my eyes on the road while I was driving home. When I got to Rutledge High School I pulled into the parking lot and turned around to follow the light. I drive back down 11W and when I got to the graveyard where my best friend Gena is buried I pulled into the drive around and parked in front of her grave. The reason I pulled into the graveyard is that the lights seemed to be coming closer to the trees. The moment I pulled into the graveyard, the lights started getting closer and as I watched they were right above the tree line and I got scared. I felt like I couldn't move and I don't remember anything else, but when I came to myself I drove home as fast as I could. When I got home I looked at the clock on my living room wall and it said 12 am. I couldn't sleep all night because I couldn't explain what happened from 10pm until 12 midnight. Where did those 2 hours go and what happened during those 2 hours. I have been scared to say anything because I thought that people would call me crazy. The encounter happened a very long time ago. My son and daughter are grown now and he wasn't a year old when this happened. My son is 18 now and my daughter is 29 and weren't involved in this encounter.
