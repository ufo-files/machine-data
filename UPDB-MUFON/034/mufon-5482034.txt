object was a Phoenix Changed to a ball of light

My wife and I were on our honeymoon at adventura spa palace, Riviera Maya Mexico . During a Lunar eclipse on the beach a brightly lit, multi colored outline of what looked to be a phoenix appeared right over our heads. It appeared to be transparent with brightly lit colored dots. It turned its head to the left and looked right at us. After hovering for a few seconds it took off in a ball of light ,straight out over the ocean into the stars. Right after it left us, another couple was walking down the beach and we asked them ," did you see that?" They confirmed they did. At that point we shook our heads in disbelief and continued to walk down the beach.
