It was a Friday evening,it was just me & my cat.After locking up,I was on my way to bed,I went to close the landing window upstairs(it was a humid night).as I did I gasped at the stars,it was a clear & beautiful night.Three very large/bright stars caught my attention as they seemed so close to me & so large.I then noticed that they were in a triangle shape...a large triangle shape.I thought they were just large planets or something,until I noticed that they were moving along(they had been stationary to start with).Just to the right of this a slightly lower down I saw two circular Orb shapes that were white but with a large reddy purple light on top.These were both moving,infact they were moving/dancing around eachother.At first I thought they were aeroplanes/jets on the Gatwick flightpath but the lights were wrong ie.there was no visible green light or white light just the red light & it was not flashing on & off in sequence as all aircraft do.Also they were definitely doing some kind of weird dance like movements around eachother.If these had indeed been aircraft there would almost certainly been a collission/crash with serious consequences. I then noticed that up above the triangular lights shape there was another of these white orb shapes with reddy purple light on top that was making odd up & down movements...moving but not travelling in a straight line.These 3 white & red Orbs all appeared to moving along at the same time as the large triangle.When i say triangle I mean the three bright star like lights at each point.You could see the sky through the triangle shape...but I got the feeling of an outline of a large object too. I thought this all very puzzling & kept on staring at it to try to make sense of it.I feel absolutely sure that these objects were not stars like the others in the sky that night.They just seemed so very close to me & too large...also they were definitely moving along & the other stars in the sky were not.So it`s unlikely that it was the wind moving the clouds around & giving that effect.I was just very puzzled by it & still am when I think about it.After 5 or 6 minutes they moved out of sight & I decided to go to bed.I lay there awake thinking about this for quite some time...I had not been drinking & I do not take any substances!! Sorry if this account is long winded & sounds a bit confusing....I have tried to write it exactly as it was & how I felt at the time about seeing it. I don`t have any footage or a photo as my camera really does not seem to cope with taking footage at night in the dark.Nothing really comes out when i do film at night.
