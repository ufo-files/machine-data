{cak} Just a light

Me and some family members were trying to see Jupiter and it's moons. I looked up and seen what looked like the size of a small star moving very slowly going North. I was trying to get everyone to see it but it was so high up they couldn't see it in time. Then it just disappeared. I kept looking for it to move again but nothing. It was gone. I didn't see it again.
