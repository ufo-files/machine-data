{rjl} Cylinder moving methodically NW, not fast, abrupt change to E - Vanished from site followed my 2 military jets heading NW

My wife and I were sitting on our front porch, clear blue skies and sunny - Something caught our attention as we looked to the SE - It was obviously not an airplane, it was coming towards us in a NW direction - I am a private pilot with 1500 hours - I would put it at about 2500 feet or less - As it turned to go E we could see that it was in a cylinder shape, a short fat cylinder, not elongated - While we could clearly see it's shape, it had an odd translucent quality to it, although if I had to scale it, I would say it was more solid than translucent , there was perhaps a halo around it as well - We watched it turn to the E and head away from us - Right after it turned E,, immediately after two Military fighter jets came on the exact same NW path, they did not turn at the sam,e point the cylinder turned, they kept going, it appeared almost as though the cylinder wanted to evade the jets and turned away from them - The jets really caught our attention, when we looked back to the cylinder, it was gone from view and then the jets were gone - It was unreal, Judging from the size of the jets, I presume F-16's, in close formation - the size of the cylinder would have occupied the same space, length wise, as the two jets in formation outside wing of jet 1 to outside wing of jet 2, which would make it large - It was not darting across the sky but moving methodically - I have flown next blimps and this was not a blimp, it disappeared too quickly - I have had at least 4 other sightings, all of which defy explanation
