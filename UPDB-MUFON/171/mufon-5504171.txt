I was sitting outside along with another employee and the security guard waiting for the other employee to get picked up. I was just looking up at the stars and I noticed a really bright star. It then started to fade away. I watched it fade for a bit and then I asked generically "are you seeing this". The other employee didn't look up but the security guard said yes. I hadn't even said specifically what I was asking about. I then asked asked him what he saw and he said he saw a bright star go away. I was pumped. I thought I witnessed the last breath of a supernova. I've been checking the web for any news on supernovas for tonight but nothing has been mentioned so I expanded the search to "bright light fading March 15" and other variations but still no results. I'm now posting my experience with MUFON because I once noticed an object streak across the entire sky and they quickly called me and informed me that it was most likely a satellite. I'm hoping they will call again with an explanation for this sighting.
