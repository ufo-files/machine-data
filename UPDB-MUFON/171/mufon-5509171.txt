Was driving North on I65 at about 1030am South of Seymour and observed a white pill shaped object up and to the left in my front window. It was going West and It turned reflected turned again and reflected. Then it started going from West to East and picking up speed I looked down to note the time and looked back up and it was gone. I looked everywhere in the sky and saw nothing. There were no clouds in the sky. Ive never seen anything do that before. I drive the route every Saturday and every other Sunday. Im also prior Military and seen nothing like that.
