{tjd} A star like object dancing in the sky. Red light minimal, spinning in circles, quick sharp movements

Aprox 9:20pm my husband went out to the garage and looked into the sky. He had me come look. Just above the treeline was what looked like a bright star. It was a crisp clear night at very cold all stars and the milky way were visable. The "star" started to pulsate. It was not a star. It moved, first slowly and in circles then quick jumps, it was dancing like a flame. It would take a few minutes of a break and then be active again for about 30 seconds then another break. There were red streaks almost like light at a few points. It bounced and spun in circles. For nearly 2 full hours we watched it till fog moved in and got too thick. We took one digital phone and attempted videos. We were shocked, goose bumps, felt cold and sick at moments. We were crying mesmerized by what we were witnessing.
