Orange non blinking orb and seperate formation of white flashing lights w Starlike object

Since having a sighting in my backyard over Memorial Day weekend, I have been watching the sky at night. I had observed the same pulsing/strobing star that floated over my neighborhood that weekend, now in the distance on several occassions. However, last night I saw something different. I observed some flasing lights over my treeline and called my boyfriend out, who also observed them. We then lost sight of them, so I went around to the front of the house to take a look. At this lime I saw an orange ball moving through the sky. It did not blink or make a sound. I watched it come across our entire block, but then it went behind a tree and never emerged on the other side. At which time we noticed an airplane in the direction the ball had been heading. We eventually went back inside, but I kept popping back out to check the skies. At one point I saw a flashing light dancing around the pulsing star that was in the distance. I called my boyfriend back out. The single light was then joined by two more flashing lights and they danced/flashed around for a few minuted until they lined up in a straight line directly below the pulsing star. This alignment seemed to cause a reaction in the star object of a bright red strobe. The small lights then moved off in seperate directions and disappeared. The Pulsing star object became more distant.
