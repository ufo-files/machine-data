{td} White star-like object moved across sky; photo shows saucer shape

After 3 years researching these objects, noticed a large number overhead since the 4th of July fireworks season, seeing 6-10 per night. Outside with 7x31 binocular with video camera/night vision capability I was looking for objects. I had just finished capturing one, when another white one came from the SE, and was moving in a straight-line to the NE (Az 126 deg, Elv 90 deg; final Az 46 deg, Elv 45 deg). Does not appear to be moving in video because first seen overhead and moved in a straight line until out of sight. This object was also moving very slowly. Altitude as the others, about 2-5 miles, in the high plane area. Cannot see LEO commercial satellites because my skies are not dark enough and need binoculars for LEO objects which are at least 50 miles up. Object was spinning and tumbling, as can be seen in the video. Object first seen in Aquila. Brightness about +2 magnitude. Apparent size slightly larger than star-like. Appears reddish in video but actually white to the naked eye. Smaller red objects are stars.
