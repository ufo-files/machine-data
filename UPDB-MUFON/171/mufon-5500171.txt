{cak} Bright orb in the sky observed, then shot off in a fast speed

Me and my brother were outside our house at around 10:30 pm on Aug 14th 2014 when we observed a bright light in the sky moving entirely to fast to be an airplane the orb was moving in a straight line from west to east I did not believe at first this was a ufo until I seen the object streak across the sky at a unbelievable speeds then disappeared and also before we witnessed this object the sky was empty then as the object disappeared the sky filled with black helicopters and airplanes and they were all flying in formation which strikes me as odd.
