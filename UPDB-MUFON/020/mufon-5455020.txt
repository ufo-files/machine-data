{tjd} 20 + star-like lights moving in a line from approximately west to east

My son Michael was outside and called me and his girlfriend outside because he saw 3 to 4 star-like lights moving across the sky in a row. I went out to look then immediately ran back in the house to turn the porch light off. The three of us then stood and watched as star-like lights seemed to fade into view in the approximate direction of Venus (which we could see brightly in the sky), move across the sky from approximately a westerly direction toward the east then fade out of view almost directly overhead. At lease 4 or 5 were in view at a time and were very high up. We watched this parade of lights across the sky for about 10 to 15 minutes before there were no more lights blinking into view. At arm's length, they were about 2 inches apart, all moving at the same speed. There were a total of approximately 20 or more in total. Isabella also saw a light move away from the parade of lights to the south. We also observed what looked like a separate single light moving quickly across the sky from west to east which we believe was a satellite. The lights that paraded across the sky were similar in brightness to the various constellations that were also clearly visible from where we were standing. Unfortunately, it was too dark and the lights were too high in the sky to get a good video or photograph so we do not have any visual evidence.
