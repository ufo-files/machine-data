My son and I were stargazing in hoopa California at 10pm 6/11/14. I pointed out a satellite to him when a second light sped up along side it slowed down to pace the first light. The first light pauses for a moment speeds up and stops in a jerky motion. Second light speeds up and arcs into the clouds. I observe the first light moving across sky for awhile longer. Both son and I observed a light earlier coming from the opposite direction moving across the sky in a slalom pattern assumed it was a satellite.
