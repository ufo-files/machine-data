{rjl} Reflective orb hovering over the mountains to the west

I was driving home from picking my nephew up from school at around 3:15 pm. As I was driving west, I noticed what I thought was a plane, but realized it was stationary. It was a reflective orb hovering about 20 degrees above the horizon to the west above the cascade mountains. I pulled over and watched it for about 5 minutes and it hovered the whole time, flashing periodically like it was spinning, then vanished. No flight path, no movement. Just vanished. I drove home and went into the backyard to see if I could see it again to take a picture but it's gone.
