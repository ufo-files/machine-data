LARGE V SHAPEWITH STRUTTS A GOOD 120 FEET LONG 3 BLUE LIGHTS I FRONT 2 BACK

The craft was about 120 ft long i could see the struts quite clearly 1t was the shape of electrical pylons on its side / as it passed over there was no noise'' i could see part of blue light /disc upfront '& TWO LIGHT BLUE LIGHTS AT THE REAR '1 ON EACH STRUT;PRIOR TO THE CRAFT PASSING ;I WAS LOOKING OUT OUR DINING ROOM WINDOW ; WE LIVED ON CASHMERE HILLS QUITE HIGH UP ''I WAS LOOKING NW/TO WEST'I SAW A QUARTER MOON SHAPE GLOWING 'HIGH UP BUT ABOUT EYE LEVEL ''TO WHERE I WAS SITTING'AFTER VIEWING THIS OBJECT FOR ABOUT 20 MINUTES I ASKED MY WIFE TO GET THE VIDEO CAMERA , I FILMED IT FOR TEN MINUTES 1T SOMEHOW TURNED TO A CIRCULAR LIGHT ''JUMPING ALL OVER THE PLACE ..I PLACED THE CAMERA DOWN TO VIEW IT MANUALLY AGAIN''IT SEEMED TO HAVE AN OBJECT BELOW IT TRAVELLING IN A CIRCULAR MANNER'AFTER A FEW MOAS I LAY THERERE MINUTES I SAID TO MY WIFE IT'S GETTING BIGGER 'THERE FOR WHAT EVER IT WAS WAS'COMING OUR WAY I WENT OUT SIDE ''OUR HOME WAS L SHAPED WITH A POOL WITHIN THE L ; I STOOD THERE TO VIEW IT THEN I WALKED TO THE BACK OF OUR HOME . WE HAD ROCK FACE AT AN ANGLE OF ABOUT 30 DEGREES. THIS IS WHEN THIS CRAFT PASSED OVER 1T WAS HUGE TRIANGULAR SHAPED WITH LIGHT BLUE DISCS ON REAR &APART BLUE DISC IN FRONT ;BECAUSE OF THE ANGLE THIS IS ALL I COULD SEE .THERE WAS NO NOISE .THE STRANGEST THING I CANNOT IMAGINE IS 'I WAS THE ONLY ONE TO REPORT IT .OR SEE IT ; MY WIFE REMAINED INSIDE .SINCE THEN MY HEALTH HAS BEEN BEEN A PROBLEM .I GOT IN TOUCH WITH A LOCAL U.F.O.CLUB HERE. AFTER A COUPLE OF WEEKS I WAS VISITED BY THREE MEN IN SUITS . THEY INTERVIEWED ME E.T.C. THEY SHOWED ME DIFFERENT LITRATURE ON UFOS ''HAD NO EFFECT ON ME UNTIL ONE GUY PUSHED OR THREW A BOOK WITH AN ALIEN HEAD ON THE REVERSE . WHEN VIEWING THIS IT FELT LIKE SLOW MOTION . AS IT PROCEEDED TO COME ACROSS THE TABLE'THEY THEN ASKED ME TO LAY ON A BED .THEY SAID I WAS BURN'T ? THEY PROCEEDED TO MAKE GESTURES WITH THEIR HANDS AS I LAY THERE'A PUSHING MOTION'I HAVE NOT SEEN OR HEARD FROM THEM SINCE. BUT HAVE HAD EXTREME THINGS HAPPEN IN MY LIFE SINCE THEN .I AM NOW 71 YEARS OLD .OF VERY SOUND MIND . .
