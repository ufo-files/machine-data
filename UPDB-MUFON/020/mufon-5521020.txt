Trail of very strange light

We were on a porch when we saw a bizarre light beam in the sky. It was too dark to see what was actually causing the trail, but it was far too wide to be from an airplane or other common aircraft. It was very straight and orange-ish with blue undertones. It "ate itself up" rather quickly, but we got to see it for almost a minute. I felt excited to see it because I am a firm believer in extraterrestrial visitors, but I had never seen anything with my own eyes.
