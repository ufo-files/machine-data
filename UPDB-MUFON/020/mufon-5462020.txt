{jpg} Sighting from an airplane out the window

I was flying from Alaska to Florida in the end of June or early July 2016. I had layovers in seatac and o'hare. Flying into ohare it was a misty over cast day and I noticed out of the portside window about a mile or more away a large dark object sitting stationary in the sky at about 1500ft or so. It was longer than it was high by a ratio about 10:1 with a upper and lower appendage and giving the distance I figured it was it had to be at least 7 or more times as long as an air plane. I never reported it because who would believe me, I just chocked it up as I was privlaged to see it. I'm only reporting this now because I just learned a lot of other people have seen things in the sky around ohare and I thought it was interesting. So if anyone else saw this around the same time, there is something to it. NEVER SAW ANYTHING IN THE SKY LIKE IT! kinda gave me a creepy feeling..
