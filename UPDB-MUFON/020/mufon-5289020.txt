Meteor like “ufo” becomes two then disappears [looked like a space capsule reentering the atmosphere}. - {djs}

I work at a golf course in royal oak, MI. I was on the 9th green almost in the exact middle. I can’t remember the exact time or date now, but it was July or so 2019 sunrise around 5:45-6ish. I remember it was a red sky to the East just before the sun came over the horizon and still sorta like blue/purpleish like could almost see some stars if the city didn’t drown them all out. By chance I look up to the west and maybe 5 ft from my view above the tree line maybe a few thousand feet up but still far below the clouds I see what looks like a space ships flame with the tip (the end) facing north, and it was traveling south/southwestish, it almost looked like a space capsule reentering the atmosphere and it’s got the flames all around it, but it’s traveling the way I described above.. I watched for maybe a second and waved to my coworker to look but he missed most of it, I never took my eyes off. After tracking this object for maybe 2-3 seconds (stayed on the most straight line/plane I’ve ever seen something travel) the flame disappears to nothing then two white orbs split in a perfect “V” shape and after maybe .5-1 seconds later they just poof disappear and that was it. My coworker says he saw the orb at the end but doesn’t know if there were one or two when I asked him a few minutes after it happened only because I repeated everything I just saw to him like I just described to you. Not sure what it was not saying it’s anything crazy, but ever since It’s just had too many connections to what I’ve heard from others story’s. Just thought I’d share and see if anyone else in the area may have seen the same thing.
