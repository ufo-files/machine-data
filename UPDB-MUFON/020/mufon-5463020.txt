{jpg} Fusha ball of light followed me in my Car ..it spoke to my mind.

about 10 p.m. at night. August 1992
I was on my way to return a video to Block Buster's, in Las Vegas NV , 
I was driving West on Tropicana Blvd when out of the corner of my eye, I saw something.I was aware of something. I looked out my side car window, and saw a pink light the size of a softball. It was following along side of me. Moving at the same speed as me. I thought I was dreaming, but I knew I was wide awake. I pulled into the shopping center parking lot which was mostly empty. I had just crossed over S.Jones Blvd 
I turned my car to face South, the Pink Light, which was hovering in one stop directly in front of me.
I asked it out loud "What do you want to show me"
Instantly, 3 white lights dropped down from the bottom of the Pink orb.
Each white light was smaller than the Baseball size pink orb.
They were the size of golf balls.
The 3 lights were lined up next to each other, the same amount of space between each one.
One by one the white lights fizzled down towards Earth and disappeared.
When the last white light had disappeared, the Pink light got smaller and smaller until it was a white Star. I kept looking at the white star transfixed until my mind fully comprehend That They Are Always with me. in our skies. To protect us. It was in my mind, till I acknowledge thier presence, is always Here. When I verbally spoke out loud. I acknowledge you. Then it disappeared.
I had no fear. I felt they were here to protect us in the future. They made sure I Knew They we're always with me.
