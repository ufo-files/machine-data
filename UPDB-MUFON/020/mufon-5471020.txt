{cak} Object sighted flying southeast over I-25 near Waldo Canyon Road exit south of Santa Fe, NM

My husband and I were driving from Albuquerque to Santa Fe. At the Waldo Canyon exit, we both witnessed a triangular object, surrounded by bright green lights, flying at about 100 mph across the Interstate in a southeasterly direction. It was on an aerial path until it arced and appeared to be on the verge of falling. It then disappeared from view. The event lasted around 10 seconds.
