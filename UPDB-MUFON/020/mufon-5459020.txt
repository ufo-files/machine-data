{rjl} Silver orb came through atmosphere

We were outside in the back yard and I was looking up and I saw it just show up like it came through the atmosphere. It just sat there hovering for about a half hour. It slowly changed position within a half hour moving North Western in direction. At least two jets passed it during the event, but it was undaunted by their presence. At the end of the event, it looked like it flipped and took off disappearing the same way it appeared.
This happened last Summer also and it appeared in the same place.
