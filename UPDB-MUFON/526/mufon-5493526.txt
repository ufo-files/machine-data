{dr}Mainly orangish glow..perfectly ascending arc pattern...13+ units evenly spaced

Walked out of friends house. Entered my car parked on street. Started car...then in the air over the street in front of me, was mainly orangish blob...semingly fairly low (hundreds of feet high maybe)...heading southeast, then asscended very gracefully up and then southwest in arc pattern at an even non-accelerating speed. When the second one appeared, got out of my car...watched it follow the exact same path with identical speed...then watched several more. Because of trees, once each glob passed clearly in front of me, the most I could see at *one time* once they diverted from SE path in front of me to ascending South/Southwest away from me...was approx 5...again, evenly spaced....each evenly paced...no speed varisation between any of these globs. Richards Gabeur Air Force Base (supposedly many years closed) is approx 3 miles SW of the location I made this viewing. The closest airport that might have radar of my location would be Lee's Summit (Mo) airport...approx 15 miles east. The experience made me smile and get goose bumps. Totally exhillerating...it finally happened to me!!
