{jpg} Looked like trying to look through a jellyfish in the night sky.

I was living in San Antonio, TX on the Northwest side near UTSA in a subdivision called College Park. That Summer the city was experiencing a water shortage so water rationing was being enforced. In order to water your lawn you could only do it by hand with a garden hose. So late one night around 10 or 11 PM I was in my front yard watering my grass by hand. The cul de sac I lived on had one street light which provided plenty of light to see by so I didn't have my porch light on. It was a beautiful warm clear night so I happened to look up to admire the stars. This was when I noticed there was something wrong. Looking straight up the stars seemed blurry. The only way to describe it is; like trying to look through a clear jellyfish. Or heat waves in the Summer over a long highway in extreme heat. I kept looking at it and then I would look down thinking it was my vision. But when I looked up again it was still there. I then looked out in a different direction instead of up and things looked normal. This was when I felt uneasy and decided to go in the house. The only people I mentioned this incident to were family and close friends. At that time I didn't know who or if I should report it. At the time it was so surreal.
