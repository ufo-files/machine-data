{jpg} GoPro Photo for Time Lapse of the Sunset (5 Sec Intervals) / Object In Photo

I was using my GoPro to make a time lapse movie of the sunset in an open field. There were some ducks flying in the area attempting to land in a nearby pond. Additionally there were two commercial aircraft in the distance leaving a trail which I though would look cool once I got the movie together. A couple of days later I decided to put the movie together and I was going through the pictures to make sure I didnt have any of those ducks in the photos. I noticed on G0028388 a small rectangular object that looked to be reflecting light from the sun. At first I thought it was one of those ducks flying around but then I zoomed in to 400 percent and seen that the object had angles to it.?? I did some playing with the photo using different color filters and come to an initial conclusion that it was not a duck in flight. G0028389 then shows a small white spot and under different filtering shows to be some sort of object that moved slightly to the left. G0028390 same thing. Photos were taken in order 5 seconds apart. Not sure what to think of it all. Just wanted to share it with you. G0028391 is one of the ducks flying around. The Sun is reflecting just right to make it look as if it has a spotlight.
