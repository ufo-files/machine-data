{jd} Hovered then slowly headed east then it shot like a bullet into the clouds. Boomerang looking object did not make a sound and did not leave a exhaust trail.

I was outside in the garage getting ready to work on my sons car. I was standing smoking a cigarette before I entered the car and I happened to look up in the sky ( which I often do since my childhood encounter). I noticed a black object in the north eastern sky at first I thought maybe it was an airplane till I noticed it was hovering and the shape. The shape looked similar to a V but more like a fighter. I know USA does not have any aircraft like this I said to myself. I continued to watch it and it just sat there hovering it did have what looked like a beam coming down from it but I couldn't tell. It was a partly cloudy day but the sky seemed very blue more so then normal for some reason. I saw clouds pass above it. I watched it for 20 minutes before it slowly turned and headed east then shot like a bullet up into the clouds faster then anything I have ever seen faster then any airplane we have far as the military or civilian goes.I made no sound what so ever no smoke from the engines or anything.I was kind of excited about seeing it I know they exist and seeing it just means they are getting more and more frequent.
