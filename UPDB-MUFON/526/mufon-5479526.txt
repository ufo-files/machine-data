triangle shaped craft changing direction above me and 2 friends before zipping off into the sky almost straight upwards at high speed

i was with 2 friends walking along an old railway track beside what was to become greenlands avenue after the building work was later started then completed . we saw a triangle object darting around the sky making no sound we could hear, it hovered above us for a few seconds before zipping up into the sky at high speed and disappeared at altitude . a few years later and I would of said it was the American stealth bomber , except for the erratic movements and the speed at which it accelerated . I didn't report this at the time because I was afraid I'd be judged as crazy despite there being 2 close friends as witnesses . I only got confidence to report after some time watching the show on blaze and got the confidence to share this . I've wondered for many years about this . it was a funny experience and one me and my friends still talk about (though only between ourselves) . I felt very shocked by what I had seen and it lead to me suffering severe anxiety which I was diagnosed with in 1993 . I wonder if my experience had something to do with my anxiety that I still suffer with today
