3 orbs filmed from airplane over CA I believe

I was traveling to LAX from DFW and within 45 min of landing in LA via plane ride I saw these 3 orbs off in the distance. I immediately grabbed my phone to record. They appeared close to a body of water, above the clouds. You can see the light reflecting off the top of the clouds. Very large orbs in my opinion and different shapes. Possibly a very small 4th light if you pay close attention. I can’t wrap my head around what would be that large and bright above the clouds! It was an early morning flight before the sun was up so absolutely no possibility of sun reflection of any sort.
