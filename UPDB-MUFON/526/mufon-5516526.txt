(GS) blue flashing light doing antigravity dance

One of my roommates was letting the dogs out back at around 2:15 AM on 7-26 when she saw this in the sky. She woke my other roommate and me up and all three of us watched it for minutes. It was about five miles or so off I would guess, and I would say it was hovering around 1000-2000 ft. I cant be sure, just my unqualified estimate. I would put it just west of the I-17 between Bethany Home and Thomas. Maybe closer, but definitely not farther. It looked like a really bright LED that would dim to a small starlike white, and then itd get bright blue again. The lights didn't follow any pattern, appeared random, or perhaps it was somehow related to its movements. The only thing I could think of that could match this things maneuvers is an RC helicopter. But it seemed much too fluid and controlled... and theyd have to be controlling the lights too if it was RC. It seemed anti-gravity like. 

Heres the thing, a couple months earlier I saw the same thing way off to the North East (probably near Cave Creek) at around 4:30 AM as I was leaving for work. I didn't have time to stand there and watch it, but it gave me motivation to buy a camera so next time I could film it if I see it again. So I had the camera that morning of 7-26, and I tried filming it a couple times. I didn't record much because it was very dark, and I didn't think the camera was picking it up very well, which it didn't, but it did record better than I thought it would. Anyway, a few minutes after I gave up recording, the light descended until we couldnt see it anymore through the trees and houses.
