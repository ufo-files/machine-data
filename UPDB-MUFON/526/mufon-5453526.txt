{rjl} CE5 Contact

Three of us went out onto the beach of East Long Island NY and after about 40 minutes of following the CE5Contact app instructions we decided to leave bc it was too cold. We packed up and then I looked up and there is was! It was an angled saucer shape like a forward slash on a keyboard, about two thumb lengths & width over the bay maybe 1/2 mile away. Metallic, reflective type craft, clear as day just in-line with the star line for about 7 seconds, drifted West to East very slowly b4 it faded away or became more transparent and then vanished. It was mind blowing to say the least.
