{cak} Dog found old strange tibia bone, partly petrified

A friend had a bone in his fishing boat that he used to "thump" rough fish over the head to kill. I examined the bone and it has very unusual characteristics.. so I purchased it from him and set out on the quest to determine what type of animal it may have originated from.

After submitting pictures to many contacts in the paleontology, archeology, and museum fields.. none offered any explanation. They were baffled. That is why I am contacting MUFON.

Initially I thought it was a human shin bone (tibia). Now I have no idea. Squatch, humanoid, or prehistoric hominid idk.
