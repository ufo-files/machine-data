Blue orb with lighter blue fuzziness around main orb

I was letting my dogs out for the morning outing (4:45 a.m. on 11/20/2011)when I looked up in the sky and quickly noticed a blue orb. Before I could yell for my Husband to come outside to look at this thing it suddenly dropped down silently and disappered behind some trees. Two of my three dogs took off in the orbs direction and one of my dogs turned around to hide behind me. 
  I knew that it could NOT be the moon as I could see the moon to my right. This thing was half the size of what the moon appeared to be that morning and I know it was not a star. The stars where somewhat covered by clouds. This orb was very bright, blue with a fuzzy or hazy outer layer.
  I kept looking for whatever it was but never saw it again. It went down behind some trees, and on the other side of these trees is a lake. This all took place between 7 to 10 seconds.
  I have lived here in this area for 10 years and have seen other strange lights, orbs and flashes of lights from unknown objects but this is by far the closest I have ever been to whatever it was.
