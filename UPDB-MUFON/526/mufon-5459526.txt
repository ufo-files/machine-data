{tjd} Bright Yellowish Sphere followed by bright green light and bright blue light

A large bright glowing Sphere slowly moved across the sky over our camp site at United Campground , . It took about 25 seconds to traverse our view area. It was followed by an Airplane seeming to be over 5000 ft altitude. As soon as we lost view of the Sphere, on the same path appeared a very bright Green light moving on the same path. About 30 seconds later a very bright Blue light appeared on the very same path. None of these had any type of blinking lights We watch the sky most nights using our Sky Watch I PHONE app. We have seen the Hubble and International space station many times. We have NASA connections and take this very serious. I was shocked to find no reports of this the next morning. As always happens my wife could not focus her IPhone camera , and our 3 Binoculars were in the Motorhome, Once in a lifetime missed.
