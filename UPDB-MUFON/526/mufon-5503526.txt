{nd} orange light moving rather quickly in straight path then suddenly disappeared, then did the exact sequence once more from east to west

I was in my backyard working on my back building on a ladder when I first spotted the orange pulsating bright light coming from the east flying directly over my house rather quickly in a straight path, with no noise, it started descending and then suddenly disappeared in the west still in the air. I had yelled for my wife to come outside and told her to hurry and run. It had disappeared by the time she got outside. I started telling her about it when another orange pulsating bright light from the exact same location in the east was flying overhead, with the same fast speed, same path and suddenly disappeared in the same location in the west (exactly as it had done the first time). This happened approximately within 2-3 minutes of each other. Total time occurrence from the first siting to the second one disappearing was approximately 7 minutes. There was no other plane traffic in the area at the time.
