Over the course of the past 6 months I have had numerous sightings

Nearly any clear night I can go out around 11 p.m. to midnight and view light flares that sporadically come from the same spot in the sky. And I have seen an inordinate amount of satellite sightings, sometimes 5 - 10 per hour, about 90% of the time going in a north/south direction. Another coincidence is I see many of the flares of light happen in the Big Dipper/Polaris region of the sky and this odd number of satellites seem to always appear and disappear into this same region. One Light flare I witnessed about half a dozen flashes of light, then 20 light orbs appeared in the same moment from nearly the same point in the sky one travelled directly south and the other directly east from the same areas of the flares. After another series of these light flares, two bars of light appeared from it and travelled southwest. If you want to witness these strange events come to my house after dark and I will show them to you personally as it is nearly a nightly event. I would file more reports but it takes far too long.
