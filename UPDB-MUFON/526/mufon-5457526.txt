{cak} Glowing Green Orb

Saw a BRIGHT GLOWING GREEN, and I mean bright green object the size of a kernel of corn in sky go flying very fast below trees. This was seen by at least 8 others in Park Hills, Bonne Terre, Festus, Potosi, Hwy 221, Terre du Lac. All saw same thing. No trail coming from it. Was not burning like a meteor, and going more than the 67 km that meteors were supposed to travel that night. No sound, no report of large meteor strike, it was still a huge object when it went below tree line. All witnesses are strangers and all saw same thing.
