Oblique object (covered stars in its path) moved with lightly audible turbulence and a short white burst.

I heard a sound like a jet plane coming from the southwest, and looked into the sky to see what originally looked like a falling star. Upon noticing that the short burst of light emitted from the object had no trail, passed somewhat overhead, made a noise seeming to originate less than a mile away and briefly covered stars in its path after the burst I realized it was likely an experimental craft. It seemed to be moving at 700-1500 mph.

I don't believe it was a colony craft or military prototype; the speed and apparent altitude correction rule out resonance induced 'gravitational propulsion' and the lack of multiple thrust points rules out relativistic magnetic vibration. Because the light seemed to originate from the entirety of the body, I would postulate it was a saucer craft utilizing magnetically/electrostatically directed circumferential corona discharge. The whitish light matched the upper ends of the emission spectrum for this phenomena, so I would further postulate that the body contained an advanced triboelectric material storing a large potential and some mechanism for controlling its distribution.
