Black Chevron Shaped Craft Silently Moving Above Tree Line

Evening walk to local ice cream store with friend. Both he and I stopped walking independently after sensing static electricity sensation on skin. Light audible sound of static heard clearly in ear canals. Looked 360 around, down & up for explanation. Observed directly above head, moving south to north at pedestrian speed, large black craft. Forward edge was chevron shaped with light emitting downward from evenly spaced recessed lights extending both east and west. Estimate 5 or 7 lights total, set back from leading edge 20 feet. Surface was black and clearly visible.Estimated elevation 60 feet. Craft moved slowly and silently north over adjacent single story retail store structure. Atmospheric anomaly and visibility ended simultaneously. Clear, starry night, but was unable to view the craft further once no longer directly beneath, even though we knew the position it would logically occupy.
