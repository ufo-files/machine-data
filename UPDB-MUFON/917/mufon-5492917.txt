{cak} Was with my dad swimming at a lake when we both saw the UFOs.

My mother, dad & I were at a public swimming lake near Otatha KS, around the early 1960's. My dad & I were in the water. I had swam out to the dock at the end of the corded off area. When I came up I turned to the beach where my mother sat. That's when I saw the 2 large silver saucer shaped objects hovering above yet just past the beach. My dad came up next to me in the water & I pointed to the objects. We both watched them for a moment till they made a sharp angle turn & shot off, disappearing in an instant. We never reported it. I'm haunted by wat we saw. Just hope some day I will have answers before I die.
