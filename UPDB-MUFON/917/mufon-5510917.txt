(GS) sq 3 tired item on top my yardy copper iluminated

Looked out west kitchen window 9:00 PM saw 3 tiered object on top my yardy, 2 feet sq, maybe slightly taller than 2 feet. Each tier had 3 ovals of illuminated, brushed copper appearance. Wasnt shiney, wasnt lit up, I thought illuminated, not pulsing, just there. Didnt have sound, no movement. The framework around the bottom similar to leggos, little sections with holes spaced evenly on each piece. It sat flat on top yardy all 3 tiers faced west, tilted up at an angle, heavenward. Right by edge of street back property, I thought someone had set whatever it was there. From window looked for anyone around. No one.  Hurried to door to go outside, unlocked inside door, was about to open outside door I stopped.  Was afraid to open that door! Went back to window and watched for minute or 2. Went into living room to think what to do and when I came back was gone! Sure wish I had kept on watching but didn't know it was leaving, didn't know what it was! Next day looked around yardy and its top. Nothing left behind. No pattern in the dust on yardy top. I immediately drew a picture which Im sending along with this article. After I had time to think about it I thought
it was either sending a signal to mark this spot, or, was receiving something, possibly energy for traveling.
