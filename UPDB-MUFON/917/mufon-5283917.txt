Huntington WV

Observed a rather bright light in SW sky from a rural address. I love watching the sky, astronomy, etc. This is the first unexplainable light I've ever seen. I noticed it looked strange and called for my boyfriend. I asked him if it was blinking kinda yellow and red. He replied yes. I commented that I was going to search planets in the sky tonight and turned my phone on. Then decided to wait. Turned it off, looked back up and it's gone. Neither of us could see it anymore. So I sat outside to adjust my eyes back and it was never there again. Yes, I'm aware several planets are out tonight, and considered it was Venus. My judgement, based on sheer amateur studying astronomy for years, is that it wasn't Venus. It only took two seconds to disappear.
