Appeared from a distance of about 1 mile. Hovered, then moved silently at a low rate of speed from southwest to northeast.

I was in my front yard with my wife and two children around 8:30 pm. We were kicking a soccer ball around when a light in the sky got my attention. This light was orange/red and seemed to pulsate. It remained stationary a few moments and then slowly started moving to the northeast. The object was about 300 feet above us and traveled silently. I couldn't make out a distinctive shape of the object because when it would pulsate, it distorted it's image like a halo or something. It was definitely an uncomfortable experience for my children as I was trying to come up with a logical reason to explain this phenomena. We all four watched this thing slowly move over us and continue to travel until the light couldn't be seen any more. The whole sighting lasted about four minutes and left me scratching my head as I tried make sense of what we just saw.
