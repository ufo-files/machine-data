Noticed a blue-white flash in the Northwest out of the corner of my eye, upon looking I noticed another blue-white flash and then the object dimly lit up and began to descent lower to the horizon.

At around 11pm, I was sitting out on my apartment patio smoking a cigarette, when I noticed a blue-white flash out of the corner of my eye towards the Northwest. I stood up and stared in the direction that the flash came from for about 1 minute or so, then I saw another blue-white flash that looked like it was coming from a star-like object. I continued to focus my eyes directly where I witnessed the two flashes. Another couple of minutes went by when I noticed a dimly-lit, star-like object appear and begin to slowly descend towards the horizon. All the while there were maybe two or three commercial planes and/or jets in the sky to reference to the anamoly. The object slowly descended out of view behind the apartment building across and to the side of my patio.
