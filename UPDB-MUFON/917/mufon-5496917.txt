{nd}round object zoomed close then retreated several times. A mist, steam or smoke radiated from it constantly from all around the edge & periodically from the side facing us. a friend & I were going to my home after school. It 0ccoured near the northern

edge of Terre Haute, Ind. Incident lasted perhaps one half hour. The object was still present when we left the area. Never seen anything like it before or since. We did not report it for fear of being negatively labeled. Two other guys in our class also seen the object and did report it to the police. The locol newspaper did mention it in the morning edition the next day. Both of the two reporting witnesses names were given by the newspaper. I will provide a sworn statement to the above if requested. I solemnly swear that at the time of the incident, the witness with me nor I had any discussion with the two reporting witnesses before, during or after the event concerning the sighting. The two reporting witnesses were at the extreme southern edge of Terre Haute. I don't know if they sighted the object before or after my friend and I seen it, or if they seen it at the same time as we did. The orange "ball" started at the size of approx. a tennis ball or golf ball, then zoomed toward us until it was the approx. size of a large grapefruit or a little smaller than a basket ball. Black or dark grey smoke came or radiated from it. It came forward then retreated back to its original position very quickly several times, every ten or so seconds. We had no idea what the object was or is. Neither of us felt fearful or any physical sensations, only a great curiosity at the wonder of it. I don't understand why everyone else did not see it. We were only about two blocks from a busy highway. The incident sure opened my mind to the subject of UFOS.
