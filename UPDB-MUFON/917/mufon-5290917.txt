Red light moving across sky high altitude

Traveling North on I-95 on Cypress Creek Road exit witnessed a red light moving across the sky. The path was slow, and it left a red trail. It almost looked like an airliner on fire, but it completely disappeared. Two witnesses observed the same object in complete profundity. No It wasn't a rocket, missile, or asteroid because it was moving slowly. It was high altitude. Not a flare because it appeared to be moving from the west to the east. No clue what that was. Was in too much shock to think to pull out the camera. Then it vanished.
