Light orb landed in front of me

Witnessed a bright orb, about the size of a large tire or wheel. It was transparent, like a giant bubble . It traveled over my home. At first I thought it was a mylar balloon but as it came over head and quickly dropped, it shrank in size. It shrank to the size of a tennis ball and dropped abruptly into my neighbors yard. I literally watched it land. I went up to look for it and there was nothing there. Nothing at all. I was shouting to my husband.
