Night was really dark and there weren't really any stars out because there was lots of light at this concert. An airplane sized white dot shows up above the concert area, as bright as an airplane in the sky at night, and flew around for a few seconds before making a sharp 90 degree turn, pausing for a moment, and then shooting out of sight in a NW direction. My fiance and I only noticed it at first because it was moving around in an odd fashion and we didn't say anything out loud until it made that sharp turn and just shot off into the night. 

All I said was, "What the hell?"

He didn't even look at me when he said in a quiet voice, "You saw it too. S***."

We consider ourselves to be rational people. We thought it was just another airplane UNTIL it made that sharp turn. I felt sick, not because I was afraid, but because I knew what we saw was unusual and that it was not possibly powered by man because they would die turning and stopping at that speed. We didn't tell anyone else because, well, there's still that stigma. We will never forget that night.
