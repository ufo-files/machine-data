Strange slowly moving light there on 4/18 and again 4/20

At approximately 11PM on the 18th of April, 2011, I was outside with four other guys. Three of them were looking up towards the southwest at an unusually bright star. They noticed it while staring at the full moon towards the south. This "star" appeared to be 2-3 separate lights at an extremely high altitude (or in space) as there was a lot of atmospheric distortion. From the my location the light was Northwest from Sirius towards M47. The relative brightness in town with maximum light pollution was probably a -2.6 (as bright or brighter than Venus on its close approach to Earth). When mapped with a sky map the light appeared to be directly edge on with the disc of the milky way. The light was moving counter-wise compared to the surrounding stars. The light or light(s) appeared to be attached to a fixed object and change from a white to a blue to a reddish color. However it was NOT blinking like you would see on any civilian helicopter or airplane and due to the close proximity to an international airport it would not be feasible to suspect a military aircraft which was somehow hovering. The speed was also too slow to be a satellite (going on my experience here).

The light was visible for several hours on the 18th and again on the 20th. On the 19th they were in no way visible from my location. 

I have checked the path of the ISS, all planets, all artificial satellites, and anything else I can consider to be the source of this light/lights and I am coming up empty handed. The closest object I found would be a Cosmos Descent craft 482. 

end result is I have no clue what it was.. Did anyone else see this? perhaps someone with a telescope handy to get a closer view on this object?
