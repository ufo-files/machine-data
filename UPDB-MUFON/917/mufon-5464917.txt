Many Oblong bright lights in a straight line . Moving toward me, went sideways in both directions, then retreated from the original. Position.

I was looking out of my living room window when I saw this oblong object with lights all across the front high in the sky coming toward me, the lights were very bright, and the object moved from side to side a few times then seemed to retreat backwards all light still shining bright it was very large, much bigger than an airy plane. This was in the 19601s in north East England. I have not told many people about this as most people think you are crazy if you say you saw a UFO. 
Years later in the USA . I also witnessed something like a Comet coming down behind Double Header mountain in Colorado. That I had time to run in the house , grab my camera, and take it1s photograph. I submitted that photograph to the Denver museum of science. I still have a copy of the photograph.
