{kt}several reflective orb like flying objects hovering and random movement. Visible then not with reflective characteristics

Driving through Grants Pass Oregon we spotted several hovering orbs above the hills outside of town. We pulled over and attempted to record objects with our phones. During this time we spotted several objects in different directions from our location. Three objects hovered in a northernly directions, one to the Northeast and approx 5-7 in the southwest. They appeared to be silver in color and reflective. They came in and out if visibility . We attempted to film and captured some rough images. We then drove across the street to the nearby Goodwill store to get binoculars. When we came back out with the binoculars the objects were gone.
