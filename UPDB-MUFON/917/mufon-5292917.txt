Orbs hovering and moving in all directions every night for 4 nights and counting

I first noticed a sound, which may have been unrelated but it got me to check the front door camera which picked up what appears to be a ball of gas that moves in all directions and is able to hover effortlessly in the air. The orb moved around my front porch for a few minutes then disappeared. This occurred several more times throughout the night and every night since. I even caught a few glimpses of it in daylight which show a pinkish almost flesh color to the orb. The orb moves like there is a front to it, so it rotates and turns when it changes direction. In once scene it almost appears to move like a snake or a fish wriggling its body back and forth. At times it appears to materialize through the wall. One clip it goes directly over the camera and for a brief second it almost appears to be body shaped with arms and legs. The visibility of the object seems to be variable in that sometimes it reflects more light than others. I have two scenes where we see the object with the sunlight hitting it from behind and it doesn't quite cast a shadow but it does seem to be absorbing some of the light as its color changes. In a couple scenes there appear to be more than one object. I have video recordings of roughly 40 instances of it appearing in the past few days and to my knowledge its still here.
