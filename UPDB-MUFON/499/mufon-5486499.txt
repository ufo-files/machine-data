{cak} Standing with friends in yard.Saw a green ball of light on ground.Looked up,saw green orb and it was gone.

I had been out with 2 lady friends to some isolated juke joints,in Macon and Tallapoosa,and Lee counties.We had several beers apiece over the course of about 7 hours.We were not drunk.I had maybe 6 but over as i said 7 hours.We came to my place a small mobile home in a clearing surrounded by woods.My driveway was about 100 feet long.Upon stopping i was standing outside the camero saying goodnight and i turned to the right laughing at a joke and saw a ball of green light on the ground in a unplanted corn field coming fast toward us.I instinctively looked straight up an saw a emerald green orb moving at a tremendous speed about 300 feet above the ground.It was by us and gone in a second orless.I said "did you see that".She had not because she was inside the car.Neither lady saw it.I did.No sound.Not anything.The orb was emitting the light to the ground,though i only saw the light in the field because as i said i looked up and saw the orb and it was gone.I have no physical proof.I just know what i saw.The ladies when told laughed it off.I do not know what it was but i did see it.It was totally quite out because of the hour and semi remote location.The orb had no sound,not even a swish.It was traveling at a tremendous rate of speed.
