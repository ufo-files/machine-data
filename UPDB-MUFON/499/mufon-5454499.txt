{rjl} I saw a object that didn’t look like anything I ever saw.

A object that had bright lights. The lights were so bright all I could see was a vague part of the object it also appeared to blend in with night sky. The lights randomly change colors all of colors of the rainbow, they would be one color for about 30 seconds then go out for 5 seconds before coming on in a different color. When I first saw it it was descending from the sky right over Chenney Mountain. When it got close to the top of the mountain it stopped midair and started going East. By time it passed over my head I noticed it appeared to be quite low to the ground and made no noise at all. A side note here my 6th grade teacher owned a cattle ranch in the area I lost sight of the object. When I got to school the next day he said some of his cattle had been dicected and were dead. He said it was not the first time this had happened.
