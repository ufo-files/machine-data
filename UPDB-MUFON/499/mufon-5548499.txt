About every other night for 3 wks., we see these "things"around our area,sometimes up high, sometimes very close

I have already made reports of 3 sightings,2 of which were pretty close to us, there are re-occurring events , now taking place about every other night,(with the exception of the 2/3 nights of rain lately,no sightings in the rain)I'll tell of the one's I wrote down... on the 16th of March at approx. 11:42p.m. my husband & I decided to go out front and look at the stars, but it was too cloudy to see any,so we watched the clouds pass over the moon ,husband not impressed was ready to go back in, as he stepped onto the porch I noticed this thick white line over top our house,looked like a light I thought ,& said what's above our house right here, husband looks & says it looks like a jet line or power line lit up,but we don't have a power line there, he agreed & as he said that , it totally disappeared all at once , not a portion at a time like a jet line would evaporate, but like a switch just turned it off.We agreed that that was very odd,and continued looking around the sky then.To the S.W. of our house we noticed something flashing in the sky,watched it for a few min. & went inside.Husband thought nothing of it & went to shower, I got binoculars out to look at it... it was definately hovering,it didn't get smaller or larger,was approx. the size of a penny through binocs. & had many lights flashing off/on like tree lights blink,all at diff. time sequence.Had my teen come look he could make out colors,red,blue,mainly & bright white big light in center.I lined it up w/our utility pole,it wasn't moving...stayed there for almost exactly an hour and then just dissappeared like it turned off it's lights.
 Then, on 3-20-08,the other sighting that lasted awhile, 3am exact, dogs had been quiet all night til now,barking ,whimpering,wouldn't go after whatever it was they saw/heard, like they normally would,so I looking around the property and all around,don't see anything out there,til I look up...right out to the north ,above the house,there's one of them again, I get the binocs. to look closer, same red,blue rows of lights as before w/white light in middle, white light goes off while I'm watching, then another white light comes zig zag blipping across in front of the other,it moved great leaps between lightings,coming from east,where I see 2 more ,all hovering but the one zig zagger, then another in the north near first one.Now there's like 5 of these things,just hanging out around my house.The one to the east is closer to my house it looks to have a white light on the end or on top ,it kindof looks like a cockpit but larger ,can't tell for sure,some of them have red light in center w/more blue around perimeter,some red there too,& they all look like triangles from the position of lights.Took photos but they didn't show anything oddly enough. They stayed & I watched till 4:07a.m.,then I decided to go to bed,so I got ready & came back for one more look(dogs both calm by this point) and "they" were all gone...just gone,they sat there that long & I walk away for 5 min. & they disappeared, I even ventured outside to see if they just moved,they were nowhere in sight.I don't know who could do anything about it but for someone who's always been intrigued by ufos...I'm a little freaked out & would like someone to do something,if possible.These are definately something & I see them very well through binocs., they are real and here at my location.
