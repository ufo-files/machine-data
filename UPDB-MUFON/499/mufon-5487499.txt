{dr}Single light came together with other lights

I went out back of my house to check out the meteor shower. Now since i live in the NE of the country I always see planes in the sky. At any givin time there can be at least a half dozen at the same time all over the sky. So I'm watching for the meteors and a very dim light catches my eyes. No strobe or green and red wing lights. I can tell its really high in the sky. Its moving way faster than a jet would fly. It stays in an easterly direction for about 2 min or so then stops. Now it gets really strange. Where it stops it forms a perfect triangle with 2 other lights that weren't moving at all. Then those lights start moving away from each other at the same speed but are forming a bigger geometric shape. Like a pentagon. The whole thing then rotates clock wise for a few seconds stops and another light drops out of the shape and moves south at a high rate of speed. What the hell did i just witness???
