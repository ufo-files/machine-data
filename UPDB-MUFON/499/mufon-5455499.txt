Saw large white light flashing intermittently and dancing around in the sky

I saw a large white star like object dancing around in the sky. It appeared to be bouncing up and down, then darting off to the sides slightly. There seemed to be no pattern to the movements, just random darting around a small area. This went on for 15 minutes or so, then I could no longer see it due to cloud coverage. At this point I went to my kitchen and witnessed an even larger orange light which disappeared within a few minutes of me being in the kitchen. There was no cloud coverage on this area of sky, which is why I found it so strange. This second object seemed much larger and further away.
