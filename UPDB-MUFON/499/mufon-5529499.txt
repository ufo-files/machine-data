two star like objects moving in unison across the sky

I went out on my deck at 8:00pm and began to observe the moon in relation to jupiter. I noticed two stars at an even distance to jupiter, as if there were three stars lined up at equal distance. I closely watched these to stars and it seemed they were moving ever so little that i stood there watching for a few min., before i was certain they were not stars. At the moment i concluded this, the two star like objects turn at a 45 deg. angle, pass below jupiter and as the last star passed jupiter, they stopped and slowly faded to a red, then dissipated.
