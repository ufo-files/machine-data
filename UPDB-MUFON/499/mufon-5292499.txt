An armada of unknown objects

Myself and 5 other people wittnesed an string like group of about 15 to 18 objects with red, green and white lights. Not like typical aeronautical lights at all. There was one lagging behind that moved oddly as well. It stopped for several minutes after the others left. It looked like a star then resumed in the direction other larger group with the colors returning. The objects were moving very fluid like in a tight proximity of each other. Nothing manmade could make these movements. All 6 of us were convinced we had wittnesed something completely unknown. The movements defied physics of our current technology.
