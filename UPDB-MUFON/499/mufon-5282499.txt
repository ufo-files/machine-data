Line of Light Stationary.

Was driving to work just before 6am, approximately 40km to the west of Saskatoon, SK Canada and notice light over Saskatoon. Thought it was a shooting star at first and noticed it was not moving. Watched for approx. 5 mins and took 30 sec video and pic. Finally I had to keep driving to get to work and when I looked back approx. 2-3 mins after driving south, the object was gone. I did not see it leave.
