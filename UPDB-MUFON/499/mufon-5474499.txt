{dr}Round. It had at 3 stories and windows. No lights and no sound. Just sat there just above a house.

Left a high football game. Driving east about one mile from football game I saw something above a house. I was the only car on the road that was very odd. No traffic. I drove to the front of the house there it was. It was huge. About two and one half football fields in size. I stopped my car. I sat there watching it. No sound and no movement. I was so scared. I couldn't take my eyes off of it. For some reason I opened the car door and got out. I am not sure how long I stood there. I could not move. All this time no cars at all. 8:30 to 9:00 it wasn't real dark you could see around you. There were no outside lights on. There was no inside lights anywhere I could see. Then all of the sudden I heard a voice say leave right now. It was like I woke up. I got into my car as I was leaving it was still there. I flew home which was about ten miles away. More 2 hours had past. I was looking at it for 2 hours. I just remember, looking at it. I believe I was in some kind of a trance.
