{jpg} 3 lights in formation strangely bobbing and changing direction followed by what looked like a shooting star

Sunday night at about 1025pm I was sitting on my front porch having a cigarette. I saw something out of the corner of my eye which at first thought was just birds, but as I got up I realized they looked like 3 stars moving in a slow strange formation. They would Bob up and down in formation almost like the points on a wave function, two would bounce up and one down, two would Bob down and one up. They were slowly moving left two right across the sky to my north in this strange bouncing formation. After about a minute they stopped and hovered in a V or triangle formation. After hovering there for a couple seconds they formed a tighter formation and accelerated to the east. I watched them disappear quickly into the distance over about 15 seconds and not more than 5 seconds after they completely disappeared from sight there was a very bright and defined shooting star between the direction I saw them disappear and myself. They way they moved and they height they were at convinced me it was no helicopter or plane. It was dark and at high altitude and they travelled fairly far out of sight so I don't think they were drones. Afterward I got pretty tired and slept for 13 hours but I don't know if it was connected but it was a little out of the normal for my sleeping schedule.
