Pyramid-shaped object was ascending when first seen, then hovered and moved away.

I was outside putting stuff in the car, when a pyramidal object seemed to take off from the street. Initially, I saw it from the corner of my eye, but was disturbed to find a large object hovering over my house. I could not see the pavement from where I was, but it was likely on the ground. Once the object was in the air, it hovered for around 10 seconds, then zoomed off to the south-east. There seemed to be writing on the object, which looked like hieroglyphs. The street looked to be unaffected. I do not know if there were any other witnesses. I was very startled, almost dropping the bag I was holding.
