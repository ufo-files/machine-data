Recorded a video and a picture of a UFO

About 7:30, on October 5 2020. A friend of mine text me, to go look up in the sky towards behind my house. I went outside to see what he was talking about. I saw a set of about 7-8 mildly blinking lights. Next to each other, evenly spaced apart, in a curved shaped. Hovering what seemed to be right above our back yard. I took a couple steps back to get a better whole view. I ran inside to get my mother and ran back outside. When I went back outside, there lights where farther away. And there was another set of lights. These were a set of about 4-5 lights. In similar formation, same color blinking lights. After about 5 minutes, the second set of lights disappeared. Thinking maybe they're flares. I got on my motorcycle and started driving towards their direction. When i got to the stop sign at end of my road. I decided to snap a picture. Instead I recorded a short video. And then I took a picture. Accidentally leaving the flash on, as soon as I took the picture, the lights disappeared. I drove towards the lights general direction. Thinking if they were flares. I should, maybe see some emergency lights or something. But no dice. Didn't see anything. Not one thing. Nothing.
The lights seemed to stay hovering around a stationary elevation.
