{nd} Fast unknown craft cruising with random blinking lights sometimes stationary lights

Roommate and I were outside smoking when I looked toward the nails to the east and thought I saw a plane but it was going as fast a fighter jet. It blinked like it was a plane but it started blinking at random intervals. Sometimes all red for a brief second. No sound was heard and so my roommate and I bolted to our car to chase after it. As soon as we hit the main road going East, it was already gone. It's a quiet night and not a single sound in the air. Earlier in the day I saw about 4 pairs of what looked like f16's flying doing runs. So the sound of the engines are fresh in my mind. Nothing. Almost as fast as a fighter.
