{dr}Ball of light passing airplanes.

I was walking my dog around 10pm. Everything was calm and normal. I look up to look at the stars and a ball of light flies overhead making the passenger planes in the distance look as if they were standing still. It was bright white, no sound or engine sound like the planes that fly overhead. Completely silent. I call my buddy and told him and he said he saw it as well as his friend about 20 miles west from me. The next morning f18 hornets are circling the area and social media has no idea why the jets are out.
