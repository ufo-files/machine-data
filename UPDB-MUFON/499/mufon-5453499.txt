Saucer shape,dull metalic, black blemishes on bottom

Summer of 1971 at CFB Clinton Ontario Canada, base was closing down, late morning early afternoon, hot southern Ontario day ,blue sky no clouds, outside playing with other kids and my dad was also outside with other parents , not really sure the exact date but maybe a weekend 
since my dad and other parents were home, someone shouted to look towards the school, that were we saw the low flying dull metalic saucer with black blemishes on the bottom, i climbed the porch roof and watched it float from the school to the middle of the base were the radar tower was, stopped there for a second or two, then disappeared straight up in a second, remember my dad came home and told me he has been told not to talk about it, funny this incident has never been told after so many witnessing this and flying so low in broad daylight,
