{cak} It was what we thought was a star until it moved

I was with a friend at a dead end by the bay in East quogue in New York. It was around 10 at night and we were both looking at the same spot in the sky. We were looking at a star and what we thought was a star next to it moved. It moved to the left for about 3 seconds and then stopped. It hovered for about five to seven seconds and then it took off so fast all that was left behind was a glowing white Trail. The only way I can describe it is the way the ship in Star Trek would take off and leave that bright flash and then a trail of white glowing debris. It was the most incredible thing I've ever seen in my life and I will never forget it for as long as I live. There is no way it was an airplane a helicopter or a drone whatever this thing was was not of this Earth. I would love to know if anyone else has ever experienced or seen anything like this before.
