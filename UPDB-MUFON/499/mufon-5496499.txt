4 orange spheres. 3 staked 1 above the other. 4th stacked in front of center 3 spheres.

These 4 objects were orange and about the size of the moon. Full moon that night. 3 were stacked one on top of the other and the 4th was in front of the center Sphere. They did not move and I can,t really recall how they disappeared. They were just gone when I went back to watching TV. I just noticed them thru my window when I was watching TV and just stepped outside to look at them. In my excitement I completely forgot about my movie camera and camera which I always have with me. I saw these outside of my home in the early evening of October 2012 These objects did not move while I was looking at them. I was very excited.
