{nd} Saw strange vapor formation in sky; used binoculars to examine. 3 sphere like objects observed with vapors radiating off.

We were driving North on M52 when we spotted a strange cloud like shape in the sky. When I saw it, I decided it looked far too odd and thick to be a jet chem trail; it also wasn't trailing very far behind the object but instead seemed to surround the object creating it. Today an asteroid was supposed to come close to Earth and we therefore pulled safely over near Jerusalem road to pull out our binoculars. When I first examined the formation through my binoculars, it was immediately apparent that there were three distinct, thick vapor trails. It struck me as extremely odd; it looked like a "hand" was coming out of the sky with three dull, sphere like objects at the head of each trail. They were slightly staggered. I could actually see vapors rolling off of these objects; there was a pretty distinct aura around each. We were debating if it could be jets in the beginning, however an air liner passed over the formation during the sighting; the air liner was closer in our field of vision; the jet liner was dwarfed in comparison to this formation. I distinctly remember feeling nauseous when first viewing the formation through the binoculars as it looked incredibly foreign; the objects were moving at incredible speed in an Eastward direction. We lost sight of the object as it was rapidly moving East and eventually became too blurry to view through the binoculars.
