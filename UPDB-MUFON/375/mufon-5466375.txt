A dark disk flying through the evening sky quite fast and disappeared in the clouds

I was standing on my balcony, smoking a cigarette and watching a bat flying around some metres away. In the background I did notice another dark flying thing.

At first I thought it was a bird, seconds later when it came closer I thought it was a plane.

The object at first seemed simply dark (maybe black or grey, which is hard to tell in the evening sky) and had a round shape, which I could observe when it was at the closest distance to me on its flight. It did not emit or reflect any light and did not make any sounds.

When I thought that this wasn't a bird or a plane (no wings on it, round shape), I walked down on the balcony, so that I could observe it further.

The object flew a straight route, roughly the flight route from Bremen to Hamburg but much faster than airplanes. It didn't make any turns or changed its speed, finally vanishing in a group of clouds.

I also realized that the bat had been gone and didn't return until the object had passed some seconds before.
