{tjd} At my workplace on a cloudless day I viewed what appeared as a blimp shaped cloud. Then it shrunk down to a perfect circle of fire.

On a perfectly clear day after entering the front entrance gate of my workplace, Pratt and Whitney Aircraft, I proceded to the parking lot to the rear of the building which was on my right. As I turned to the right behind the building and then to left to where I park, I saw this pastel pink cloud like blimpshaped object totally by itself hovering over, which was then an airfield. My windows were down and when I approched my coworker which was standing on my left I stopped. We both watched for only minutes. It then shrunk down to a perfectly shaped circle of a very bright light. It was as if I was then looking into the exhaust of a fighter jet. That only lasted for a matter of seconds and then it just disappeared.l
