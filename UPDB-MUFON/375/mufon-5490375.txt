{cak} I SAW SOMETHING I CAN NOT EVER FORGET

I WAS SITTING ON MY FRONT PORCH IN [location removed]. IN LATE FALL. I WAS ON MY SECOND BEER WATCHING THE AIR CRAFT APPROACHING THE GLIDE PATH TO SEATAC.      
WE HAD TWO TREES ONE ON EITHER SIDE OF THE YARD, I LOOKED UP AN OBSERVED A LARGE TRIANGLE WITH A LIGHT ON ALL THREE POINTS. IT BLOCKED ALL IMAGES BEYOND ITS SHAPE IN THE DARK.LIGHTS STARS ETC. I SAW IT PASS BETWEEN THE TWO TREES AS I HAVE REFERENCE. I CALLED TO MY WIFE WHO ALSO WITNESSED A SUDDEN STREAK OF LIGHT MOVE EAST IN THE SKY. WE LIVED CLOSE TO THE [location removed] AND NOT FAR FROM THE BASE. PROBABLY NOT EXTRA T.
