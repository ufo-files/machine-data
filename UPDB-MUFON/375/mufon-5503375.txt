{cak} 3 Bright Lights Show Up Out of Nowhere While I was standing On My Deck Looking Over Black Lake

So I went into my house to turn off all the outside lights really fast and watched the ufo flying still toward me a very quiet still. As it approached over my head at about 800' to 1,000' over my head I could see 3 round bue/white lights and a triangle aircraft with no sound what so ever.
It flew Fast, Quiet and defiantly wasn't any aircraft I've ever seen while I was an MP in the Army.
The feeling I had was a different feeling, that I've only felt a couple times in my life. Right before I seen it, I thought about a ufo and a knot in my stomach. A nervous feeling. I don't know why I went outside to pray and than see this ufo?
