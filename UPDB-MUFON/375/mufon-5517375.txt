(gs) Two Orange Spheres seen within 10 minutes apart. First one disappeared

I was outside with my 23 year old nephew. We were sitting on the steps in front of my place. We both had 10x50 binoculars mounted on tripods. We were sky watching. 
At about 10:30 pm, I spot a glow round sphere moving north. It is moving away from us. It just seemed to appear....it glowed bright orange. It seemed to be accelerating. It went for about a mile northward and dimmed and disappeared. It was probably about 4,000 feet in altitude. It was about 4 or 5 miles away from us.
The second one came alone about 10:42 pm. It was almost straight above us when we saw it. It was moving very slow when we first saw it. It had a slight orange white glow to it. It was up higher than the first one. This one was at about 8,000 ft in attitude. Within seconds of me getting it in view with my binoculars....... it speed up and continued to speed up for a few moments. It wasnt an instantly fast acceleration, but it got up to a very fast rate within a short distance. It was impressive. Then it seemed to maintain this speed. It disappeared over the horizon. We saw this object for over a minute, but less than two minutes. This object moved in a straight line path and went north northeast. 
Neither of these objects had any blinking light, no FFA marker lights. They glowed brightly, a solid steady light. No sound was heard. 
We have both seen these types of objects a few times and some were much closer than the two we observed on this night.
