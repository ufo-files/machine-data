Saw 2 lights in the sky with Air Force jets in air.

I was in my pool, no lights, I heard several jets from Luke afb fly over my house. I am retired Air Force so I always look to the sky. I wasn’t looking for anything but just enjoying the air show. I saw something in the sky with 2 lights that was stationary. I was watching it, the object did not move. I was trying to figure out what it was when more jets from Luke afb came flying over, still the object did not move. After the jets left the area, the object in the sky started moving in reverse. I wanted to get my phone to record it but I wanted to see what it would do and didn’t want to miss it. I stayed outside for another 30 minutes but it was gone unfortunately. I’ve never seen an object fly backwards like it did.
