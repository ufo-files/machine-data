Approx. 20 reflective objects circling themselves in sky and suddenly disappearing

Was outside appart. block when saw approx. 20 white objects circling each other about 1,000 feet from ground. Not birds. Not balloons. Objects did not appear to be troubled by the wind. They kep circling each other (like a turning wheel shown horizontally). Just had time to get phone out of pocket and snap 2 photos. Coincidentally, it is at that time that objects stopped circling each other, broke in various directions and literally disappear as if they had never been there in the first place. Objects were clearly visible to the naked eye, although phone-photo does not give justice to that. Birds or balloons would not have simply vanished in the blink of an eye.
