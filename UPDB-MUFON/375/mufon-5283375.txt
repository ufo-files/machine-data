Strange red and green blinking lights that ascended to the sky in linear motion, moved abruptly, and than hovered over the city

My business partner and I were sitting in our company van in a parking lot over in the west loop area of the city around dusk and I noticed these strange red and green lightings that ascended to the sky in a straight vertical motion. The lights were blinking in a 1.2.3 pattern and changing from green to red. The lights created a horizontal shape and moved in straight vertical motion then abruptly moved east and then ascended in another vertical motion, the lights than hovered over the city. I was confused about what I was seeing and thought maybe it was a drone. I've never seen a drone with these types of lights or move this way. In the moment I convinced myself that it was just a drone and I was being silly, but now I'm not so sure, it was strange. I wish I took a video!! We didn't stick around much longer and than drove off.
