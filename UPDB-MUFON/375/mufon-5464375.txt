{rjl} I saw to fighter jets chasing some orb in the ski.

I was sleeping in bed, when I heard loud buzzing like very a close airplane. So I peeked out my backdoor and I saw two fighter jets chasing a greyish orb. The orb was moving at extreme speeds and seem to jet smoothly ahead the jets, until out of sight. Sadly I didn't get the chance to record it, but the next day it was on the Washington post.
