{dr}UFO before Space Shuttle Launch

Evening before Space Shuttle launch I observed an object in the sky over the Space Center area. I called my husband that was a Launch Manager and he told me to video it and he would look at it when he got home, I took our VHS camera out back door (in Port St. John) and video taped it. It had a fixed area it was floating in with flashing lights and then it suddenly took off toward the West and I tried to follow it the best I could with the camera. I had seen this afterwards several times before during and after a Space Shuttle Launch. My husband was concerned because Russia and China had ships in Cape Canaveral waters during each launch and he thought it could be a drone of some type watching the launches. I also was curious about the colored lights on it. If it was a type of drone this was back in the 1980's before we even had them.
