I have had multiple ufo sightings most in New Jersey in the late 1970's. However I also had a sighting while camping in the Sierra mountains west of Bishop Ca. in 2009. These sightings in New Jersey range in duration from a mater of seconds up to 20-30 minutes where I witnessed a hat like craft that was hovering less then 500 feet off the ground. It appeared to have windows and the windows changed colors from blue to green. It appeared to be in trouble as it had what appeared to be sparks coming out from under it. The sparks were of various colors. I observed this for 20-30 minutes. I have also witnessed them suddenly appearing in the sky as dots that looked like stars all in a straight line with more dots forming in the line. Suddenly they accelerated so fast from one side of the horizon to the other, faster then our eyes could tack and were gone. I have also seen them appear as what looks like a star then suddenly accelerate at extremely high rate of speed. They then abruptly change direction at extreme angles. This area in New jersey is nothing but farmers fields surrounded by forest. The sighting up in the mountains was quite different where my wife and I observed a v shaped flash of lights that last for only a couple seconds. after about 10 seconds it flashed again. After about ten minutes it happened again. It appeared to be over the desert south east of Bishop.
