Two Star-like Objects Observed

On a particular clear night I went to my garage to retrieve some tools, and as I was walking back to my house, I noticed two bright stars perpendicular to one another just below Jupiter. Besides being bright or brighter than Jupiter, something about them really caught my attention. I had been stargazing lately, but I hadn’t noticed these two stars whenever I was viewing Jupiter and Saturn earlier in the week. So I took out my phone, and opened my SkyView app to identify these two stars. To my frustration I wasn’t able to find them on the app. I could clearly make out Jupiter and Saturn’s locations, but these two stars were a mystery. I was getting aggravated, so I looked down for a moment to get my bearings straight with the SkyView app. Just as I looked back up to try again I suddenly realized that the two stars were gone. Two stars as bright as Jupiter vanished. They were as stationary as any other star in the night sky. I had seen many planes in the area, but these were too bright and stationary to be planes. Satellites could be ruled out as well because of this same description. I really wish I could know what I had seen.
