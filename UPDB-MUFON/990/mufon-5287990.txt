Cylinder shape

Was traveling north on highland av at about 5:41pm. Was come to a traffic light and was looking up at it when I noticed the sunlight reflect off something in the sky. Saw the object traveling north/east, didn’t appear to be a plane but shaped more like a cylinder. I rode to the first side street which was about 300-400 feet from the traffic light. Ride about another 75-100 feet and stop at a point where I could get a clear vision of it at this point I got my phone and started recording. It was about twice as for from me then when I first saw it. I recorded until I couldn’t see it anymore.
