At a little after 3am the night of June 20, 2020, I stepped out onto my bedroom terrace at our summer home in Northeast Harbor, Maine to observe a crystal clear night sky. I did not go back into the bedroom to retrieve my iPhone to attempt a video because I was afraid I would miss something.
