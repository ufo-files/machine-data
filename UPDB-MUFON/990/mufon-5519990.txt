While looking at the stars, I noticed 2 stars were moving at a high rate of speed. These objects appeared for about 5 secs. Then disappeared.

I was standing outside looking up at the stars. I was looking at the big dipper (northeastern from me) and noticed a star like object moving at a high rate of speed across the sky. It had no tail and no sound> I observed the object for about 5 seconds until it just disappeared. About 10 seconds later, another star like object appeared in the northwestern sky and was also moving at a high rate of speed and also disappeared. The first object was moving from north to south, the second was moving from northeast to southwest. I observed each object for about 5 seconds before they both disappeared.
