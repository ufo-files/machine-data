3 bright lights, rotating a triangular position, maintaining position in sky.

On the morning of Thursday January 16th 2020 while driving my eldest daughter to highschool, we saw 3 strange lights in the sky that did not behave like regular aircraft. Three bright lights that held a triangle shape. First the one on the left dropped lower and went to the middle and the middle light replaced the left light. The movements were very quick and too fast to be a commercial aircraft. Then the lights formed an upside down triangle then they rotated again quickly. In the 20 mins I took to drive there and back towards my home the lights remained in the sky. Meanwhile we did observe planes flying as normal and advancing in the sky towards north however these 3 lights did not advance forward or backward and remained south of my location. Once my daughter arrived at school, a friend of hers who was waiting at the bus stop also witnessed the strange bright lights.
