A triangle shaped craft in 2 different parts of town 3 minutes apart

I was in town at the Subway getting food, I went to leave the parking lot and head towards stop and shop and the dana woods area, I noticed a large triangle looking craft with red and orange flashing lights hovering over town with no apparent flight pattern. I drove to the area of Checkers and the State Schools and saw the same craft now hovering above Checkers, I observed this craft skeptically and watched for about 5 minutes, the craft made fluid right and left hand turns and changed its flight path very quickly, I turned around to follow and observe it and it raised to a high enough altitude and I lost sight, I experienced radio interference in my vehicle as long as it running kind of funny.
