{cak} orangenorthbound light moving at rapid speed made a rapid 90 degree left turn and hovered, then faded out

Arden Arcade, Sacramento CA. At approximately 7pm PDT a bright orange light with a more yellow hue than a sodium vapor light moved rapidly across the sky at high altitude and made a rapid tight banking 90 degree turn to the west where it decelerated and hovered. It remained visible hovering for approximately 10 seconds before fading out ... like an ember cooling. Not landing lights on a plane or copter - too bright and too orange. No red/green white safety lights. I saw a similar craft over a year ago in October of 2017 with the same color.
