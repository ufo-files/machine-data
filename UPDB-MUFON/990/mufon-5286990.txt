Extremely bright large constant and flashing lights

While working as a remote camp lead for a Discovery Channel production a coworker and myself witnessed something unexplainable from the top of Mt. Ryan north of Fairbanks Alaska approximately 10 miles off of the Steese Highway. We had wrapped up production and myself and a coworker were rigging down the camp and preparing to pack out the next day. I was outside the cook tent burning trash when the valley in front of me lit up like daylight. Above the ridge line across the valley was a big very bright white light. It seemed to be maybe a few thousand feet above the mountains. It was so bright that it lit up the inside of the cook tent and got my coworkers attention. He came out and we both observed. Then multiple smaller bright white lights appeared and flashed on and off all around the big light. Absolutely no sound. Earlier in the week we had fighter jets fly through the valley and the noise was thunderous. This went on for quite some time. The lights would vanish and then reappear up and down the ridge line for approximately thirty minutes. We took video on photos but they didn’t turn out very good. You can tell there’s lights but nothing close to what we witnessed.
