{cak} pulsating light zig zagging / hovering and changing color

Little after 2am stepped outside on to the back deck to check the temperature.Pulsating, moving light caught my eye just below the treeline. I could see it through the trees.

Colors changing, sometimes getting brighter. Would dim, then get very bright and flash multiple times and colors randomly, then dim for about 20-60 seconds, and start flashing again. It moved up, down, and further South, and ended up about 20 deg higher later on.

Movement very "bumble bee like" in how it zig zagged up/down/left/right, then held still, then moved and hovered.

Observed for about an hour.

Set up telescope and through the scope it appeared very prism-like with more colors visible through the scope.

As it seemed to move further up / farther away over time. As it got further away movements were less bumble bee random zagging and more hovering/smaller jerky movements. Was able to keep it in the scope.

Side note:
We did see two "shooting stars" from the meteor shower last night but they were in a different part of the sky and unrelated.
