Orange glowing spheres in formation hovering than shifted shape

At 7:15 pm on November 23 2021 we witnessed 7 glowing orange spheres hovering in a Vshape formation at treetop level NE of my front porch about 150 yards away and about 250 yards in the sky. They stayed hovering about 2 minutes until slowing moving East as they changed formation into a rectangle. We tried to follow them but could not reach them and they just disappeared. As we came home 3 more appeared but this time single.
