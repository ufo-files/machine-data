three bright lights in a triangle

Driving to work. To the right hand side (East) I notice three very bright lights, equally spaced apart in a triangle shape. The apex of the triangle was closest to me. At the time of video one of the lights vanished to the right at a very high speed. When I pulled over to view the two lights reminded perfectly still. I knew these were not aircraft due to the lack of anti collision light, and wing navigation lights. The lights were large and produced an intense light. Suddenly the light to the right moves incredibly quick to the object on the left before returning to its original position. Once I continued to drive, I could still see these lights, still no movement. After about then minutes one light vanishes leaving one left. The last light remained still but seemed to become less intense. This light eventually travelled vertically at a very very quick speed. Faster than any aircraft.
