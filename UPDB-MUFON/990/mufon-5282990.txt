Orbs and/or Strange White Lights

I took my dog out for his nightly walk and noticed (2) two "orb like" lights in the sky. I took my dog inside and then went back out into the field to confirm what I was seeing. I could not make sense of what I was seeing, so captured a video on my phone. Also, I opened the blinds so my girlfriend could view the lights. It was cold and she did not want to stay outside. She too confirmed that she had never witnessed anything like this before. I hope we can get some answers. I have not shared this footage with anyone else.
