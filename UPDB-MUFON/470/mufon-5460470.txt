{tjd} Bright white light, like Jupiter in night sky, moving descending NE, very fast !

On the night of May 30th 2019 myself and a coworker were working construction on I-10 in Baldwin Fl. and driving next to the highway headed east. We saw a very bright white light, about the size of Jupiter and just as bright , as it is seen in the night sky.  About 30 degrees up in the ESE sky moving at a high rate of speed in sort of an arc downward in a NE direction and it dissapeared below the tree line but looked much further away at that point, like it was headed out over the ocean. We only saw it from 2 to 5 seconds or so, and he said "Did you see that?" , I said "yes", he said "what you think that is?"

Granted,  Cecil Field, NAS Jax,  Grumman St. Augustine were pretty much all in that direcion. But we are accustomed to seeing the aircraft, and hearing them, and aware of their speeds and flight patterns, as well as the F15's out of the ANG station at Jax airport.  But this didn't match any of them and was going way to fast.  It didn't "streak" like a meteor, or have a "beam" like a plane headlight.  I have been close enough to a meteor to not only see it but hear it burn by me, from a basketball size burning orange, to whiter baseball size then exploding into bits.  This looked further away than the bases, way too fast for a plane, and not fast enough to be a meteor.
