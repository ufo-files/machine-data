{nd} Five bright solid yellow orbs with red translucent glow surrounding the yellow center.

My husband and I were outdoors in our lounge chairs looking at the stars. It was ten minutes after midnight when these bright glowing orbs came out of the south heading north. There were four orbs lined up following each other in a straight line and spaced evenly apart. They were brilliant solid yellow in the center with a red translucent glow surrounding the center. All were the same size and looked identical. We live in an area with a lot of trees. The orbs where low but above our tall oak trees and disappeared going north beyond the trees. Suddenly a fifth orb came out of the south following the same path and looked identical to the other four. There was no sound. We looked for a structure that the lights might be attached to but they appeared to be independent of each other. It is hard to determine the speed. We watched them for about 45 seconds. I am the same person who reported the two solid orange orbs in November 2015. In both cases the lights were brilliant in color and moved almost the same path/direction, height and speed. The orange orbs were smaller in size than the yellow/red orbs.
