I was driving from Savannah to Statesboro. I noticed a black object in the sky far off in the distance didn't really give it much thought. The object from my view seemed to be almost directly in between the two highways. It was not until I drove closer that I started to become curious. The object was directly over my car and was slowly spinning. The object was black, daisy shaped, and had long cut in grooves. Yhere was no sign of a portal on the bottom. The object then moved toward the tree line slowly. The hieght of the object was right over the tree line.
