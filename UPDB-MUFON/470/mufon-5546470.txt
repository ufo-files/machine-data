I saw a metal object in the sky during a storm when lightning struck.

I was driving home from the store at around 6:30 pm. It was very cloudy & dark out, with a storm coming on. As I was driving down Highway 204, a bright flash of lightning brightened the whole sky and right in front & above me I saw what looked like a metal object in the sky. There were no lights on it, & it was silver in color. The lightning flash lasted about 2 seconds. After it was dark again, I didn't see anything, but I slowed down to see if I could see it again. Lightning flashed again about 30 seconds later, but this time the object was gone. I'm positive about what I saw. It appeared to be cigar shaped, but it could have been a classic disk shape, with the middle being thicker than the ends. Because I only saw it briefly, it didn't appear to be moving, just hovering. I didn't hear anything. I believe it was maybe about 1/4 mile away, so it wasn't just a dot in the sky.It seemed pretty big to me. I was shocked. If it was a known aircraft, there would have been lights. I only saw it for about 2 seconds, but that image is burned into my mind.
