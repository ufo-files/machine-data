Two meandering star-like points of light, followed by three straight line points of light following

Standing at my back door at just before dawn. Looking north north east. Happened to see two meandering points of light heading north east. These were not in a straight line of flight but were making slow tight circular maneuvers. Their illumination was not constant; they slowly lightened to greyish white then darkened. Within ten seconds the objects were followed by a formation of three of similar in appearance craft. These did not detour from their exact straight line and spacing and they were following the two other craft. This remained visible for around 30 seconds until the neighborhood trees obscured further viewing. There was no sound. The objects appeared to be moving at about commercial jet speed and height. Though the height is difficult to judge. (If we were to say the objects were jet sized, I'd put the distance from me at around 20 miles or more, and 5 miles in altitude or more.)
