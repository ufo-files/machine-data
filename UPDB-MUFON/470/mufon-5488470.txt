{nd} Over a few seconds, everything, wind, insects, wildlife, buildings, went absolutely still and silent. Then a bright green light filled the air itself all over coming from everywhere at once.

We were out on the back porch of our apartment late one night in November 2014. Suddenly, everything got very, very silent. The wind stopped blowing, the insects stopped buzzing, birds stopped chirping, animals stopped rustling, even the surrounding apartments and businesses went silent. Then, filling the air from every point around us all at once came a bright green light, lasting for a few perceived seconds, but we both felt as if we had lost time. Then the light faded and we heard a loud BOOM. Our vision and hearing was affected for a short time afterward, all the power in the area was out for a short time, and everything stayed eerily quiet until some time later.
