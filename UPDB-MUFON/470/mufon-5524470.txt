This was not an illumnate kite or meteor

I am an American engineer who has lived in Beijing for the last 15 years. My wife will not let me smoke in the house so I went to our back porch, lit up and looked at the sky since we are all amature astonomers.

I noticed a bright object travelling over our compound in downtown Beijng from the North West towards the South East. I had my son and daughter come out and we observed the object moving. It was less than a minute from the first appearance and they saw the object for the last 30 seconds before it went completely vertical and disapperared.

There was no sound and it was very bright. A meteor would move faster and they typically have a tail. There was none for this object. We are used to the local Chinese flying kites at night with lights attached and I can say frankly that this was not a kite.

The object was spherical with a yellow/white hue. About the size of a golf ball.

I do not know the altitude but this was real and not fake. I am 58 years old and not prone to fantasy but this was out of this world.

I am glad that the children saw it since it will help to spark their interest in science more.
