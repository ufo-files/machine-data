Blue-White lights seen through clouds, very fast, looked like meteor

While traveling South on Marsh Hill Rd in Orange, CT, approaching Rt 95 intersection, I saw through spotty rain clouds a bright object that was moving extremely quickly. The blue and white lights illuminated the clouds as it travelled through them. The angle of its path looked like a meteor. I watched the lights move across the horizon from left to right, disappearing into dense clouds and the tree line. Total time was approx 3-5 seconds. A few seconds after I lost sight of it I saw a bright flash of light from the direction the lights were traveling, much like a camera flash. I saw this from the corner of eye as I was looking forward driving south.
