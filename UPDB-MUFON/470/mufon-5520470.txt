Two objects one triangle/boomerang shape flashing blue/whit/red lights and one bright orange sphere

Myself and my flatmate were stood on our balcony in Manly (which faces South West towards Mosman / Sydney CBD) after a night out and we were looking out at the stars when we noticed an oject which was flashing. Our immediate thought was that it was an aircraft (plane or helicopter) but after 5 mins or so it was still stationary in the sky and it seemed to be turning around. Then we noticed another much smaller bright orange orb to the right. When the larger object turned around there was a clear triangle/boomerang shape which was flashing blue/white/red lights.. The smaller sphere started flashing back. They seemed to be commnicating to each other. We watched this for probably up to 40 mins at least. I actually have never believed in ufos but my flatmate saw exactly the same. To see other eye witness accounts for the same evening which are near on identical reaffirms that we did see something unusual!
