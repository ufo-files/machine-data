{cak} i was photographing a century plant with a camera. when printed, an object appeared.

i was photographing a century plant for my neighbor. it was a clear day,i was snapping photos from different angles. neither me or my neighbor saw anything in the sky. when i printed the photos, something appeared next to the top of the plant ( the century plant was about 35 feet tall ). it was a blurred grayish "thing". it was not in any other photo. i studied it long and hard, and decided it was not a jet,blimp or weather balloon.i have no idea what it is or was.
