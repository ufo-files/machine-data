UFO hovered above light post and moved to the left over some houses and eventually above the forest.

I was taking my family trick or treating in Falconbridge, ON and as we were driving we witnessed a UFO hovering about 10 feet or so above a light post and I stopped and began to film it as it hovered in a stationary position. The UFO had a red light on it that seemed to be rotating and would turn to white and back to red. The craft also had white lights that seemed to pulsate al around it. I watched for several minutes as it slowly moved down Edison street in Falconbridge towards the highway and it stayed very low above houses and eventually became stationary over the woods off the road. I watched for about ten minutes total but had to continue trick or treating as my kids were getting restless and my wife a little nervous.
