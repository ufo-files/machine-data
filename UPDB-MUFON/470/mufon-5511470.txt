{nd} Very Large triangular gray object that was stationary during the day clear skies by dte 2 witnesses

My wife and her friend where watching the kids swim in the pool on a clear day no clouds when they noticed a huge gray triangular object hovering in the distance. They both did not understand what it was and never seen anything that could stay still in the air it was larger then a 747 and made no noise. My wife ran inside to grab a camera and came back out and was about to take a pic when it moved for a second then disappeared. My wife is a teacher and her friend never told me about it until yesterday. She said they did not want to tell anyone because no one would believe it.
