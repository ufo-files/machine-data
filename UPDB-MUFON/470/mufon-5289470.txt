Minor UFO sighting

I was camping just outside of Price Utah on my way to Moab. I was looking at the stars at about 11:00pm. This was two years ago so it’s hard to remember exactly how the lights on the UFOs were structured (I don’t remember if they were flashing or solid). But about 1000ft up there were flashes of light that moved at incredible speeds to converge in a triangular formation. More flashes of light came and joined the formation, I think the total must have been 5 ufos all hovering equidistant from each other, then one by one the individual UFO would leave the formation. You could see that they accelerated immediately away from the formation at an incredible rate of speed. It was not a plane, satellite, or a reflection of light. I’m not that into conspiracies, but the most logical explanation is that they were UFOs. The sighting lasted for about ten minutes in total.
