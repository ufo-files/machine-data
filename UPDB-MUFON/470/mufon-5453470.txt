{rjl} November Sighting Mobile, AL

On November 10, 2019 I was standing in my backyard with my wife when I looked up in the sky and saw a very bright object in the sky that caught my attention. It was late afternoon around 4:30, and when I first saw the object I thought it was a very bright star just coming out in the evening sky. Then I realized that the object was moving and it was flying in our direction. It was moving very slowly maybe about the speed of a balloon floating through the sky. As it crossed over my house from the back yard to the front yard just north of my location I began taking pictures and video with my iphone. After I reviewd the pictures you can see some sort of what looks like electrical beams being emitted from the object. I went to my front porch where I have a huge unobstructed view of the sky, I can see for miles. After watching it float in a southeastwardly direction for a few minutes I decided to go inside to get a drink thinking at the speed the object was moving I would still have ample time to come back outside and continue watching it. Maybe 5 minutes later I returned to my front porch and looked up in to the sky and the object had disappeared.

I found MUFON through research I was doing on the internet via a news channel interview from Gulf Breeze, FL. On May 13th [name removed/cms/rjl] reported to WEARTV channel 3 news a sighting that he said he had reported to MUFON. George was able to capture a video of the object and when I saw his video I knew immediately that it was of the same exact object I would see 6 months later 80 miles west. There was no doubt in my mind they were the same object after hearing his description of it. Bright very bright. I would suggest comparing other reports and pictures from the Gulf Coast area of the US in the last 2 years or so. I bet we see this craft again.
