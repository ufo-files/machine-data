I was in the front of my house getting the mail out of the street side mail box. You could hear fire works going off so I looked up and saw a orange orb slowly floating from the North/West to South. It was moving to slow to be a airplane and there was no sound what so ever. I know it wasn't fireworks. I then ran in the house to get my cell phone. I went out onto my patio to try and take video of the orb. It was gone. You can hear the fireworks and the traffic behind my house. There is a main road right behind my house. Then I saw two orange orb's moving in a southerly direction they maintained a equal distant's from one another. I was able to get video of the two before they got to far away to be seen in my video. The next thing I new there was another single orb heading from the North/West to the South. I was also able to video this orb. When the orb got to be overhead it disappeared. No other orb's were witnessed. I then went inside my house to transfer the video's from my cell phone to my computer.
