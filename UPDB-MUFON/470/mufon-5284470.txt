A triangular shaped object with lights on it at the bottom flew overhead,with no sound it did not look like anything I had seen before.

We were out on the banks of the Puget sound in Edmonds Washington as we heard the Aurora Borealis was going to make an appearance.
I was looking up at the night sky and a triangular vehicle with lights on the bottom flew over.
The lights were dull but I could make out the shape.I dont know how high it was but it was quite large .If it was a plane it would have been flying low but there was no sound.It was visible to me for about 20-30 seconds.I dont think anyone else noticed it as it was not very bright it sort of merged in with the stars and the night sky.
I rationalized it may have been a drone or a military plane but I dont know,maybe others saw it.
