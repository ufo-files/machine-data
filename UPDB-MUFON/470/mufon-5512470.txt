{dr}Shape changing UFO South San Jose

About 6:15pm, 2/22/13 in South San Jose we saw a black rectangular line in the sky. I was getting the car keys from one of the witnesses and noticed the object just over their right shoulder in the sky. We thought it was a stray balloon at first but quickly realized it wasnt. It was moving South, slowly and in a steady path. When the other 2 witnesses saw it, it seemed to turn and look oval and then triangular. It was solid black on the 3 sides in color except the middle which looked see-through. One side was not as straight or as wide as the other 2. One witness said it looked like a person was in the middle. It seemed to turn and spin slowly as we looked at it. I turned the car off, and listened for any sounds coming from the direction of the object and there were none. There were no airplanes, helicopters, birds, etc. It was not a balloon simply for the deliberate path it was taking. It wasnt floating or moving in the wind. We watched it for a good 5-6 minutes and it started moving farther away towards Los Gatos direction. We all felt a little scared. We had no idea what it was. I was kind of frozen watching it. I had dreams about it the same night. We watched it as we were walking towards our stairs and when we looked back for it within about 2 seconds and it was ascending and then it was gone. It was the darkest black object Id ever seen.
