{jpg} UFO hovering over power lines

While finishing up making a table in our barn, I noticed strange lights above the power lines in the field across from our house. There seemed to be three extremely large “discs” floating above the lines. I walked down the driveway to get a closer look, but they were still too far away to make too much out. The lights were very bright and pulsed together in a whole array of colors, ranging from white and red, to blue and green. All the ships appeared black and moved together in sync. I went to take a video, but found both my phone and smart watch were frozen. When I looked back up, I caught them moving at a high speed to the west and once they were out of sight my phone unfroze and worked perfectly. I have never felt fear like I did when I seen those ships.
