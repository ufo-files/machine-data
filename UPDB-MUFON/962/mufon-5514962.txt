{cak} Strange orange/yellow fireball

While returning from a evening ride around Salt Fork State park and approaching the small town of Center Ohio We noticed an odd orange/yellow light in the sky to the southwest of us. It was traveling from the southwest to the northeast at a quick pace. The light appeared to flicker like the flame of a candle. I rolled down the passenger side window as the light crossed over the road ahead of us but we couldnt hear anything over the sound of our tires on the roadway as we were unable to pullover. The brightness of the object decreased as it traveled away from us and eventually faded out somewhere over Salt Fork State Park. There were no noticeable navigation lights like you would see on a normal aircraft.

We reported another strange light/object near this area last October
(MUFON Case Number 32970).
