{cak} Round shape disk

Saturday night at 7:30 PM, I was driving N/B on the 110 fwy near downtown L.A. when I observed what appeared to be a UFO just hovering near downtown L.A. approximately 3-4 miles away. I was amazed at how nearby planes and a helicopter seem to just pass below it without notice. I was able to get a few shots as I slowed for my exit at 39th st. from the fasttrack lane. Upon reaching the intersection of Figueroa st. and exposition blvd. I could still see it so I took another picture as I made a left turn w/b on exposition. At the first stop I came to at Vermont and Exposition bvd. I excitedly looked at the last picture I took. It was blurred from the overhead street lights. I could not see anything of what I had seen. That Monday I told my wife what I had seen. She asked if I had taken any pictures. I told her I had but they did not come out clear. She asked me to text them to her anyway. Upon examining the rest of the pictures I discoverd that two of them had captured the object as well as a plane just below it to the left.
