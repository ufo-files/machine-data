Several lights/orbs in sky visible in daylight from backyard, some moving slowly others moving rapidly

Just before 6pm last Sunday I was outside in my backyard and looked up to see an object in the sky to my east apparently reflecting light. I initially thought it was a party balloon or something but decided to take out my phone and record it anyway. As I looked at my phone screen I noticed other objects traveling rapidly around the original object so I continued filming. I took two recordings and in certain parts you can see objects shoot into and then out of view. I didn’t notice all of the additional objects shooting around initially, the one bright light was what originally got my attention. The main object would intermittently dim completely and I would lose track of where it went momentarily then it would reappear a few seconds later. It slowly traveled from east to southeast on a non-linear path until I eventually had to go inside. I went back out a little later but didn’t see anything except for a plane which I recorded for reference and to compare to the other videos (last video). After slowing the video down it’s clear that there were several other objects I didn’t even notice at the time. I wish I would have stayed outside longer and recorded more video.
