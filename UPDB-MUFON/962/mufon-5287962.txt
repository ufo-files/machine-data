A UFO flew from west to east

My two friends and I witnessed a UFO 5/16/21. Austin and I were hanging out in my Steves shop. When decided to call it a night around midnight we exited the shop and stood remarking at the beautiful night sky. Austin pointed out the Big Dipper and shortly afterwards Steve or Austin spotted a large long light in the northwest sky moving from west to east at a slow steady rate of speed. At first I thought it was a shooting star but I soon realized it was moving much too slow and what I first thought was a comit tail was the same thickness as the front. It was glowing white but there were red, green, and blue pulses of light randomly appearing along it's length. When it reached the Northeast sky a horse next door got spooked and started whinying and then a second horse further down the hill did as well. Steve captured a good photo and I captured a not so good video and photo. This was very large and moving very slowly and deliberately across the sky. This is my second sighting, the first being about 10 years ago.
