{cak} was in the hospital parking lot looking west at some weird looking clouds or jets with my daughter turned my head and saw a ufo

I was going down town to pickup the mail with my daughter. After we came out of the post office and got in the truck she noticed something in the sky to the west and asked me to go across the bridge for a better look. I did and you still couldn't see what she had seen very well so I turned around and headed back towards the post office and up the hill to the hospital parking lot. We were looking at these strange clouds/jet trails. I'm still not sure what they were because a jet leaving a long trail crossed paths with one. They were in the sky for awhile .  My daughter was positive they were meteors or ufos.  Well I turned my head away a saw a silvery, sort of cigar shaped object in the sky to the north. It was moving to the southeast.  The object seemed to be thinner in the middle. I called to my daughter to look and grabbed my phone and took a picture before it disappeared. I took the photo and lowered my phone and couldn't find it anywhere in the sky. My daughter stayed outside while I ran inside the hospital to pick up a prescription. I told the two people inside what I had seen and the guy said he had videotaped lights in the sky on the other side of town but hadn't shown anyone. He ran outside to have a look at the clouds/jets/ufos my daughter was looking at. She also videoed and took photos of what she was looking at. Technically I guess there was two sightings.
