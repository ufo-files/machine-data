{dr}bright blueish white light, hovered, then moved to southeast, turned & moved away from Earth rapidly

I was on my front porch, facing west. I was noticing the thin cresent moon & observed what i thought to be an unknown triangular constellation. The triangle base was aprox. 20 degrees above the horizon & tip aprox. 40 degrees. I asked the person with me what constellation it was. As i pointed it out, the lower right "star" started moving. It went from west slightly northwest, to a point aprox. 45 degrees toward the south, slightly southeast. At that point it turned, changed from a bright blueish white, to a amber, orange & accelerated away from earth staying at the aprox. 45 degree point in the sky. We both watched as the object went further & further away to the point of past the furthest visable star in aprox 8 to 10 seconds!
