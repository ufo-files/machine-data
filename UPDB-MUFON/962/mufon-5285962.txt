Saw flashing lights while working security

I was working a security assignment in Denver. Had walked out on to the top level of the parking garage to begin my walk down to monitor for car thefts. Something caught my attention that turned out to be blinking lights above the highest set of clouds. It only hovered in the same spot and I did not ever see it move. While I don't think it is extra terrestrial in origin, I am positive that it was not a plane, and I have never seen helicopters go that high before. I got a 29 second video with my cell phone, since it only captured the blinking lights I stopped the recording. By the short time that I looked back up to the spot where it had been while filming, it was then not in sight any longer. I showed it to a couple friends and they had no idea what it was, and then I forgot about it, until just going through my old photos and videos on my phone today. Decided to send it in just in case it is noteworthy?
