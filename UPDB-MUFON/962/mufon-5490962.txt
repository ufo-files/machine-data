{jpg} 3 Hovering Objects in Elk Grove, CA

My friend and I were going back to my house in Elk Grove, when I got distracted by these 3 lights that looked unusual. I ask my friend do you see that? We were almost to the house and we were still watching the lights. My friend thought they may have been lights from a tower, but there are no towers in that area. I was thinking they may have been drones. But why would there be 3 drones hovering. I did not hear a sound come from the lights. I was able to snap off a few pictures. When we got closer to the house the lights were disappearing behind the tree coverage. I just don't know what to think.
