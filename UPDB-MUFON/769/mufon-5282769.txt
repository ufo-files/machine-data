Many different flashing lights meet, merge, and ejects others.

My son and I took our usual dog walk, around 8:20 PM. As we rounded the corner we saw a large white ball, stationary and hovering about 200 feet from the ground. There was a smaller red ball moving around it.
A yellow ball sat flashing over the trees to the west, it started to head towards the larger white ball.
As the yellow ball approached, the larger white ball ejects 3 or 4 smaller white balls.
The yellow ball and the white ball merged for a short period, when the yellow ball split from the white ball, it appeared to glow much brighter, and with a strong electrical appearance, with a thin blue perimeter, it then headed east.
The smaller red balls following shortly after, in the same direction.
The larger white ball, rotated in some manner, like it became a crescent, and headed west with an arching turn, it headed south.
