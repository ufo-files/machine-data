V shaped objects traveling in vicinity of KRAL

Sighted approx 1830, in the sky apparently South West of Riverside Airport, observed three inverted V shaped objects travelling roughly North to South. Objects appeared relatively large, possibly a few thousand feet in altitude. Objects would stagger left to right as they travelled South, and would bank with those turns. They would then appear to hover, and then the lights they emitted extinguished. In one case, after the lights emitting off of the V shape shut off, I could barely make out a much smaller object, which turned East and disappeared quickly from my visual range. It did not appear to be travelling very fast, it was just too small and not sufficiently illuminated for me to keep visual contact with it.

The lights emitted from the V shapes were quite strong, color wise somewhere around the vicinity of 3000 degrees Kelvin, with the most prominent source being at the vertex of the V shape. The rest of the "wings" were also illuminated with slightly dimmer lights. 

The attached image is from a sighting in Indiana which very closely resembles what I saw last night.
