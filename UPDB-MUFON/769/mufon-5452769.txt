Bright object moving fast across the sky

This week I have had three sightings off my back deck. First was on a cloudy night and I could see three lights coming through the clouds in a triangle shape about the size of a football field. Two nights later I saw what reminded me of an huge version of an old flashbulb it blinked bright like a flash and disappeared. Two nights later (last evening) I had my camera ready this one was up really high. I thought it was a satellite but it was to low and bright for that. It wasn’t flashing in real life but it looks like it is on the video I took.
