{cak} I saw a silvery disc like shaped object hovering in the sky, turning and changing color

I was walking my dogs around 8 30 am in  when I spotted an object in the sky hovering and changing color as it spun around on its own axis. It went from bright silver color to dark. It made no sound. I saw no wings or rotor. I watched it for about 5 minutes as I continued walking but after I passed under a big tree I could not see it again. The object was clearly metallic and controlled in some way as it was spinning slowly. It was not something I had seen before. It was not an airplane or blimp or helicopter or drone. It was hovering at an altitude of maybe 1000-2000ft.
