{rjl} Large oval object blocking out the stars

While watching navigation lights from several small planes fly around Mount Diablo I noticed a large circular disk above the mountain. The disk appeared at least a mile in diameter if not several miles in diameter. It hovered over the center of the mountain for several minutes before drifting southward at approximately 60 mph. I observed it moving from Mount Diablo to about Altamont Pass where it disappeared.
