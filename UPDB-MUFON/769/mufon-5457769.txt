{tjd} Walking to van and noticed stationary lights.

Walking back to vehicle after getting coffee and noticed a bright, reflective, stationary object in the sky. There wasn’t a cloud in the sky and immediately started to get footage. It was too bright out to see the object through my screen so I just pointed toward the object and recorded some footage. After seeing the first object, I notice another object nearby. I caught footage of both objects in subsequent clips. I ended up taking about a minute and a half worth of footage. Upon reviewing my footage after entering my vehicle and being able to clearly see what I had captured, I noticed in the first clip, two other objects entering the frame at extremely high speed. One zips straight past the stationary object while the other makes a ninety degree turn and shoots straight up almost intersecting the stationary object and out of view. I did not see this in real time while shooting footage since it happened so fast. The other clips are not as exciting but one has a jet that may be helpful for altitude reference and size comparison.
