{dr}Object was just above high voltage lines traveling above them.

A local sixty year old male farmer/businessman was driving north on SR-127 through the small town of Tamms, Illinois early one morning when he glanced to his right (east) and observed what he described as a metallic object traveling at a slow rate (approx. 15mph) of speed just a few feet above high voltage lines located approx. 200 yards from the roadway.

The witness stated he could clearly see the object as it maintained its rate of travel. Witness also stated he slowed his speed to match the object1s speed and maintained eye contact for several moments until it made a right angle turn in order to stay above the power lines that now traveled eastwardly away from the Town/highway/witness. Witness never lost eye contact until he lost sight of the object as it now traveled east still above the power lines.

The object was described as being spherical in shape, approx. 121 to 151 in length and approx. 101 in height in the middle. The object was metallic grey in color with a smaller dark grey band around the middle as if the top and bottom were joined at that point (Equator like band). He also stated that there appeared to be an antenna that resembled an old type t.v. antenna protruding from the top/rear of the object that wasn1t more than a few feet in height. 

The witness is a lifelong resident and a well-respected member of the community. I also went to the location described and drove the same route the witness stated he drove and his account/description matched my observations.
