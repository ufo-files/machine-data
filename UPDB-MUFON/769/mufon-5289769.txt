Looked same as ISS when it passes over - but there was a stream of 50 - 60 lights randomly spaced but in exact same orbit.

For years on clear mornings in Vancouver, I have enjoyed watching the ISS pass over based on email alerts provided by NASA.
Today was garbAge day so I was outside and I always look up often - especially if sky is not overcast.
I tend to look up in the same NW direction as the ISS approaches from - just out of habit ( there was no ISS alert this morning).

I looked up and did notice what looked like the ISS - a bright white light moving fast NW to SE in a very straight steady pace. I was not that surprised as I have seen lots of other satellites in orbit over the years.
What happened next was a real surprise - as I kept watching this "satellite" I was quite astonished to see 50 or more exactly the same following this first one ...randomly spaced but close to each other in the exact same trajectory! 

Once previously I saw a light following the ISS in a fly over wasn't sure what it was - but this was something else altogether.
