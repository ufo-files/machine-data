Bright light moving slowly, no sound

Out on my deck taking night shots of the sky. I do this sometimes on clear nights to capture pictures of meteors. My camera pointed to the north east away from street lights, 3 second exposures at 10 second intervals. A very bright light came into view to my left from behind a tree and slowly moved across the sky to my right and out of sight behind our forest.
Images from my Nikon D810 with a 60mm 2.8 lens, ISO 1600. There have been 4 sighting of these objects from my deck within a year. Two due south, one to the east and this one in the North East. I've been living here and taking sky shots since 2008 never saw any object in the sky that I could not identify. All kinds of military and non-military aircraft including an low flying F16 over our farm and for remembrance day this year 6 WW2 aircraft in double V formation. I was getting some work done in the yard when they passed over, missed photo op.
