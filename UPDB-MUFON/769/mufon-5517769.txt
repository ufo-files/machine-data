{nd} Wife & I Came Outside to see the current ISS Flightover S. Florida

We live in South Florida and went out at about 9:25 PM EDT to see the ISS fly over South Florida. We had been outside less than a minute when my wife called out, "There it is," and pointed in a northerly direction from the front of our home. Having used the "Heavens-Above.com" web site, I had not expected the ISS to be located here. I turned and raised my 7x35 binoculars. 

We saw a somewhat square/oval object with no regular aircraft anti-collision lights. Instead, it had hues of red, green, yellow, and blue. It was traveling at a very high rate of speed, but at an altitude I would estimate to be between 5K-10K feet. There was absolutely no sound whatsoever. Even with the binoculars, it was difficult to hold it in focused view long enough to truly define its characteristics.

As soon as it disappeared from sight, we turned around to see the ISS passing over the area. I have seen the ISS several times before, and this was definitely following the course predicted by NASA on "Heavens-Above."

My wife had never before seen a UFO and doesnt hold an opinion one way or another. I had seen one when I was about 12 years old, in my hometown, outside of Boston MA, that was also seen by hundreds of other observers and even discussed by a local on-the-air radio personality as the phone calls flooded his switchboard.

I cannot say for sure what we did see, but I can definitely state that we did not see (or hear) a standard aircraft, i.e. jet, prop, helicopter, etc.
