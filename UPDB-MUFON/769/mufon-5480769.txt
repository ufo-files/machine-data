{rjl} saw a light as bright as the sun, a short distance to the north of it. It hovered for a time before moving west.

I was heading west along 78th street when I notcied a very bright light in the sky, near the sun. I assumed it was perhaps a chrome airplane, maybe reflecting light from the sun, but it did not apear to move. Vertically, it was roughly in line in the sun and horizontally it was a few degrees to the left. Though much smaller, it was still larger than any other star and was about as bright as the sun. I had some trouble taking pictures of it intitially, but that was likely due the proximity of the object to the sun, rather than any anomalous phenomena. After blocking the sun with a tree, and zooming in, I was able to take a few shots of the object. I continued to walk down the road and when I was about 100 meters from Harlem ave. the object began to move west untill I lost sight of it. It should be noted that while to my perspective it appeared to be one object, it may have been two as shown in my pictures.
