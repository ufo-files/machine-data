{dr}Glowing orb

Driving toward Myrtle beach about 30 miles north near the town of Aynor I noticed a bright reddish orange orb appearing stationary under the cloud cover. It was just after sunset and there was a solid cloud cover @ approx 2000ft, no stars or moon visible. The object came into my view and disappeared after about 45 seconds. Was not able to keep it in view the whole 45 seconds due to tree line and buildings along the route. Driving about 45-55 mph. I realize there is not much information here but I want to report in case there were other witnesses which might have more complete details and thereby have some corroboration.
