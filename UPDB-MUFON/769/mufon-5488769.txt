{jpg} Driving to work, I noticed some cool looking clouds and a bright object going up from below clouds.

I was driving to work at 730am and noticed some interesting looking cloud formation and what I thought might be an airplane ascending towards the clouds. I wanted to take a pic but had to wait until I was stopped at a light. It was only about a minute later, but I didn't see the object when I took the picture. Because traffic was backed up,i was able to take another photo.This time I saw the object above the clouds. The next time I was able to look at it, the object was gone. I looked at the photos when I got home from work and zoomed in on the object I had thought must be a plane. It just looked like a bright bullet shaped light when it was below the clouds. When I zoomed in on the photo it looks nothing like an aircraft, but looks like a yellowish rectangular. Curious if anyone else has photographed something that looks like this.
