Red cigar-shaped object

I was standing on balcony and smoking. My attention was drown by bright red thin cigar-shaped light on the sky, casting several short beams at one end. At first I thought it is a plane but it was standing still for short time. The object started to move linearly toward east by continuous speed, much faster that airplaine would, then stopped, retured toward west, moved east again and get out from my sight behind trees.
