{jpg} Accidentally photographed spheroid over street sign in Hollywood. I did not notice it at the time.

[Link moved to FI Section/CMS/jpg]

I was stopped in traffic and took the opportunity to photograph Griffith Observatory in the distance. Only later, while editing the photos for my blog, did I notice the object. It is odd that in the first two shots the object is directly in my line of sight of the Observatory, yet I did not see it; also, it is the only thing that is blurred out while everything else is in sharp focus (perhaps it was invisible to the naked eye?). In the final shot it seems to be shooting away, so fast that it seems to be flattened in the direction of motion. I am a hobby photographer with a special interest in clouds (member Cloud Appreciation Society), and there are other possible UFO shots on my blog, if you are interested. This is just the most intriguing case, because of its accidental and seemingly mundane nature.
