Morphing gold object flying above then headed straight down

Sitting outside in Beverly Hills with my friend watching the cloud, suddenly this hold reflective object comes flying over it, almost looked like the size of a very large balloon. We watched it fly and morph into strange shapes including what looked like a boomerang shape, and it almost looked like it had three red lights on the bottom of the boomerang. Watched it descend over the hill behind my friend house and change direction to fly straight down until we couldn’t see it anymore. Going maybe 30 mph and I’m broad day light , sun was out.
