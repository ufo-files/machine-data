Triangle with flashing red and white lights hovering over lake. Very clearly observed.

On the night of 11/1/21, a very clear night, a triangle was observed with flashing red and white lights traveling SW over the Peaks of Otter Lodge at an altitude of approximately 4000 ft. There was no engine noise. My daughter and witnessed this and observed that the lights did not seem like typical aircraft lights.

On the morning of 11/2/21 my other daughter awoke me as she was hurrying to a large bay window that faced a lake right next to the lodge, telling me to wake up and look at the UFO hovering over the water. I jumped out of bed to see a triangle with flashing white and red lights - possibly the same triangle from the night before - hovering over the lake. It was still dark, and as I fumbled for the camera, the craft left the location. She said that the craft quickly ascended and travelled over the mountain SW behind the lake without making any noise.
