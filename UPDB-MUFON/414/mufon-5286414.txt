Fast moving abrupt moving light

Observed fast moving white light at first approaching low in the horizon, at first I thought it was a satellite as similar size/brightness but somwhat faster. It then made a rapid direction change. I had called my family to watch which they witnessed with me the object continue to make rapid changes in direction and speed whilst it changed from white to orange to a deep red.
At one point it disappeared for a moment and come back. The changes in colour seemed to correspond to the acceleration. No sound was heard, it was a very warm night with no clouds anywhere. No navigation lights or flashing lights we're seen. My wife commented that she thought she could see smoke or a haze around it
