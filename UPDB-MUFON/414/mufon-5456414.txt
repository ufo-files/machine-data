{rjl} 3-5 lights were observed hovering about a field and treeline

Driving home around 7:30pm my children and I saw several bright, stationary lights hovering in the air over Route 80W near Hope. We continued to see them through Hope and White Township over Routes 521 and 519. My young son tried to snap a photo while we were driving past a wheat field. We were too freaked out to stop and get a better photo, but you can see the 3 distinct lights in nearly a straight line.
