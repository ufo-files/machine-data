My family and I were traveling west on US-270, we observed a large bright light to the southwest. We then observed a smaller light move from the larger one at a high rate of speed moving north/northwest. We pulled over and watched as 8 more for a total of 9 smaller lights flew to the north/northwest at very high speeds. Some of the smaller light would change in brightness. Shortly after the 9th light we began to observe known aircraft in the same vicinity as the smaller lights, they also were flying in at a high rate of speed in the same direction as the lights. The whole event took place over a 30 minute period. Aircraft that seemed to pursue the unknown lights came from the east overhead toward the west.
