It looked like a shooting star (very bright) was traveling straight,then made an S turn and dissapeared

I was outside having a cigarette on my front steps, around the end of January or the begining of February (I appologize I can't be more accurate with the date) just after midnight. I was just looking at the sky and the stars when I saw a what I thought was a shooting star, except instead of remaining in the usual straight/arching path it was following, it made a very quick abrupt turn into an S shape and then dissapeared. The entire incident was over in a matter of seconds. The object looked very much like a shooting star, I couldn't identify any shape but it was very bright (I would say at least twice as bright as any other shooting star I have ever seen). The trail of the object seemed to stay in the air for a brief second or two after it had passed and then dissapeared as well. I have no idea what that was, natural? unatural? no freaking idea, but the moment it happened I was a little spooked out which quckly changed to confusion as I tried to figure out what I had seen.
