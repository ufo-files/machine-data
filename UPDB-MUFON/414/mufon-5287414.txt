Extremely brilliant! Hovering without movement or sound. So bright as to to cast rays through trees like the sun shining from behind the clouds.

I took a walk with my friend Ruth about a half mile up a familiar dirt road leading away from a cabin where we and several of our friends were staying.
Ruth and I were engaged in some deep conversation yet I still half-noticed what seemed like a set of car headlights approaching from the east casting its beams onto the hillside pine trees and growing larger as they got nearer. Then I realized that this was impossible as there were no roads intersecting ours!!!
We then walked about a 100 feet or so to investigate this light now inside the thickly sloped woods. A huge brilliant, quiet and hovering sphere was lighting up the woods so brightly that it cast beams like the sun shining from behind a cloud! Instinctively I picked up a couple of small rocks and a stick and threw them in its general direction and simultaneously shouted "Hey! hey!" as if to scare it off (!!!) At that moment the hair on my body raised and I "heard" an inner voice telling me to get the hell out of there quickly! Ruth and I ran the entire way back to the cabin to relate what happened to our friends. Of course they didn't believe us and teased us mercilessly. A hunter in our group told us that it was that new 1,000,000 watt light bulb campers use!
