{dr}All in straight path, orange like fire orb, last one shot off to another direction

My husband was going outside to throw out the trash and saw what he thought was a plane. Then he noticed several fire ball like objects in a straight line in the sky. He ran in to get the rest of the family to come outside and look at them. There were six of them in the sky all traveling one behind each other. We watched them for about 5 minutes and each one just dimmed out and disappeared. The very last one shot off quickly into another direction and disappeared. We have never seen anything like this before. It was dark outside at 7:20 PM at night. They did not have any noise.
