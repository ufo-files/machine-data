Visualization of UFOs over the skies of Munich-Germany.

All of the sudden a big storm was approaching the north part of Munich. Huge clouds were being enlightened from the inside in consequent light beams, causing many thunderbolts to hit the earth. When abruptly, at aprox. 5 km of distance north-west of Munich, I spotted 4 strong light sources in the sky, they were perfectly arranged in symetry, giving the form of a diamond. The stronger the thunderstorm was, the more intense they seemed to be. After aprox. 1 minute a fifth point of light appeared down under the other lights, building the form of a cross. seconds later they just dissapeared from the sky...I couldn't take a picture nor a film...i didn't have the time and didn't wanted to miss the end.
The cross was showing its surface center (considering if I join the light spots to an imaginary geometrical figure) towards me.
