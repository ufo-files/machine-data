Glowing starlike objects in a high elevation “arch” line generallly grouped in threes travelled into my line of sight crossing W to E at high speed.

Glowing, starlike objects at high elevation travelled at high speed from the area generally above the 45th parallel from the west to the east in a cloudless sky appearing as bright as stars. The single line formation caught my attention due to the determined motion: after spotting the first 2 or 3 “stars” moving I realized they were materializing or entering the atmosphere and generally speaking travelled in groups of 3 with many groups having 2 slightly closer and 1 lagging a bit. They followed each other in a PRECISE line as I counted roughly 35 “stars”. The last few were slightly more distanced and not in 3s as tightly. But the line was a giant arc due to extreme elevation. There was NO deviation of their flight path. Event lasted less than 5 minutes over the Portland, OR location though I viewed it from the Aurora- Wilsonville area.
