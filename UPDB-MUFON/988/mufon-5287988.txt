Bright star like object appeared in the SE sky. It ascended at a 45* angle until it got to far away and I couldn’t see it any longer.

May 13, 2021 10:20 pm 
Weather good and sky was clear. 
Sighting duration 2 minutes possibly 3
I was at work and observed a low star like object in the SE sky. Thought maybe it was an airplane until I noticed it was traveling in a straight line upwards at about a 45* angle. The object did not flatten it’s flight path like a normal commercial flight would. It kept going up until it finally vanished from my sight. 
As close as it appeared to be when I first see it. I should have heard the roar of a jet and marker lights. There were no marker lights, sound or anything of that nature.
Notes:
When I first noticed the object. It was in the same general area of 2 sighting I had back in 2004 and 2 years ago. I still say people in that neighborhood are being visited and they have no clue about it. I find it rather strange that I’ve seen 3 unknown craft in the same general area.
Also, I will be checking the security cameras to see if it was captured on video.
