Flaming ball, thought meteor, but didn’t hit the ground

Out in the driveway listening to a podcast and I saw what looked like a flaming ball, thought it was a meteor, very bright (1st photo) but it didn’t hit the ground. Flew/drifted south. I say drifted because it seemed to still be with some of the “smoke” even in the last picture. Did not hear a sonic boom.. Switched iPhone to “no flash” and expected to try to snap a quick picture before it hit the ground, but it didn’t. It wasn’t streaming smoke and fire anymore and seemed to level off..  There is about 3 minutes between 1 and 6. I could still see it beyond where I could take a picture of it. Departed south towards Fort Myers. Maybe 20,000 feet or so and maybe 10 - 15 miles away.. My estimate.. I don’t know what it was, but it wasn’t normal.. My intent is to possibly support others who saw this at a closer distance but maybe didn’t happen to have their phones handy... So bright, I can’t be the only one who saw it.
