{cak} Pulsating Ball of light

This was the day after Christmas in 2018, I was at my parents home in Amazonia, Missouri. I went outside about 11pm to grab a few items out of my car. When I walked outside through their garage, I looked up in the sky because I could see an airplane going over and it had the typical blinking lights that are red and white, slowly moving across the sky. I would say the airplane had to be about 10,000 feet up. The sky was pretty clear with few clouds. I stayed outside just looking at the stars. Then about 3 to 4 minutes after the plane was completely out of view. I saw a Gigantic ball of white light! It was about 450-500 ft above me. It was moving pretty slow. Then it made an abrupt turn upwards (90 degrees) out of sight 
I have never seen this before with any craft! And I see a lot of craft because there is a military air field just across the Missouri River in Kansas. A lot of times when they are practicing their air path goes right over my parents property! 
Then all of a sudden the craft came right back down to where it left, then it continued on its path slowly again. It seemed to pick up more speed as it went South. I watched it disappear. I could see it was heading south towards St. Joseph, Missouri. But I could tell it was phasing out. It became translucent the farther it went. I could see it just dematerialize. I did not have my cell phone on me to video it. Or else I would have. I have pictures of another incident from over the summer, sometime in July of 2018. I have not reported what I saw yet with anyone!
