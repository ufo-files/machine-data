During eclipse of ful moon, Blood Moon. Zigzagged in sky, quite large compared to stars. Fuzzy globe as luminous as the moon.

During the culmination of the full moon eclipse Jan 21, 2019 I also followed the movements of a similar smaller object that moved erratically in the sky, its bright luminous shape turned off and on....possibly as it may have been turning. It was like a lighthouse, where the light disappeared and then seconds later returned.I compared it to the stars that were very small, clear, and bright. This object was very large in comparison, and very far away so I could not tell its true size. It changed directions frequently, and then totally disappeared. It was over the Sea of Cortez looking toward the East and moving SE, then above, then NE, etc. I was watching through binoculars and while the moon was very clear I could not make out any details on the round object, shrouded in a mist.
I wonder if others saw similar object that night.
