3 flying saucers August 1996 over highway close to Beer Sheva (Israel)

It happened late at night (midnight or 1 am) in August 1996. I remember that it was Thursday. My cousin and I were driving along the highway leading to Eilat. It is a road running through the desert. Suddenly, we saw three objects approching the ground. There were two objects above and one below in the shape of triangle. They stayed still in the sky very close to our car (about 100 metres) with flashing lights. We stopped our car and noticed that the only car driving after us turned back and pulled away at a very high speed. They got obviously as terrified as we did. After a few minutes the objects moved upwards extremely fast and disappeared.
