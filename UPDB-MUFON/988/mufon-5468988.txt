{rjl} Looked like a star; same brightness and altitude but moving at a pace slightly slower than a plane. Way too high to be a plane and no flashing lights

I was on my side porch smoking a cigarette looking up at the sky and I saw it. It looked just like a star. It had the same shape and brightness of a star but it was moving at a slow pace SW to NE straight line. I am very certain it was not a plane; too high up, no flashing plane lights, etc.
I immediately called my wife to come out and see it and she witnessed it too.
I know the normal flight paths that planes take in my area and this was not one of them.
Not sure if a satellite, rocket, etc but definitely not a plane.
Also given the altitude, it must have been very large.
