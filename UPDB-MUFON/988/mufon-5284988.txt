Bright, Glowing Amber Circle in sky with additional pulses/flashes of light in the sky

I was in my bedroom at 7:31 PM, looked through my window towards northern Momtan, and happened to see this beautiful, glowing, amber colored circular thing coming into view straight over the horizon and heading seemingly up and closer to me. It seemed to come to the south, and then change direction to the right, which is the east. I got a video of this, and caught other flashes going on as well. In the upper right quadrant area. 

It was so vibrant in the night sky, that I said what the heck and whipped out my phone and try to initially film it through the screen which did not work, so I changed position to through the window, set the phone down to turn off the red light I had on in my room, and then try to continue filming as best as possible.
