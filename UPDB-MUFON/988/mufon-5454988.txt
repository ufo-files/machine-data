{tjd} A group of UFOs flying in a row with two coming together

I viewed the objects beginning at 20:10 hours. They were flying in a row moving from SW to NE in the night sky. I got my wife because I wasn’t sure of what I was seeing. She was able to confirm that a group of objects were flying in a straight line in the previously stated direction. We continued to view the objects for several minutes before grabbing her father to also witness the objects. We live close to an international airport (CVG) so we know what airplanes in the sky look like at night as airplanes have blinking navigational lights. These objects did not have blinking lights and were traveling a set distance before disappearing and then more objects would appear at the beginning, before disappearing at the end of the point in the sky which was always the same. At one point, a pair of these lights flew directly at each other and then appeared to join together before disappearing in the sky. This sighting lasted 8-10 minutes beginning at 20:10.
