{rjl} 5 orbs, slow moving e to w, no sound, low altitude

Stationed at Camp Pendleton, my wife and I were watching a video on our back patio on a Friday night. I had dozed off, and my wife woke me up asking what something was. We both observed five slow moving circular lights moving in unison (could not distinguish the outline, but definitely seemed to be a single object) moving east to west (mainland towards ocean). Our appx 8x12 ft patio was surrounded by a five ft wall. By the time we were able to get out of our chairs and exit our patio the lights were gone. The lights had been at a low altitude (appx 500-1000 ft), moving steady but slowly, and noiseless.
