{cak} Neighbor and I saw hovering white light move quickly and abruptly. No noise or trails.

My neighbor and I were on my rural farm's front porch after sundown, when we noticed a hovering white light in the northern sky. At first I thought it was a helicopter's spotlight pointed at us while the helicoptered hovered in the distance.
When the light moved quickly and abruptly to the west and then hovered, we got off the porch and stood in the front yard of the farmhouse to get a "closer" look. Because the light's brightness did not vary, I realized a helicopter could not have moved that far and that quickly while keeping it's spotlight pointed us.
After hovering for close to 15 seconds in the NNW sky, it travelled SSE 4-5 seconds directly at us, and then directly over us, before turning sharply due East.
After traveling east for 4-5 seconds, the light got blurry in the Eastern sky, then re-appeared clearly for 2-3 seconds. Then it got blurry again for about 1 second before disappearing.

It made no sound, unlike the distant airplanes in the sky. nor did it leave any kind of trail.
The light never varied in brightness, nor did it flash or blink, unlike the distant planes in the sky. (There were 2 heading North, and at least 2 heading West at the time.)
I cannot understand what me and my neighbor witnessed, but we witnessed something traveling in an unexplainable manner.
