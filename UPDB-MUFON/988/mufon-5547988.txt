semi circular lazer flash

I was driving west on Portola drive and looked up into the cloud cover just at the instant a semicircular laser flash shone through the marine layer. I was the only observer. One month ago I saw a huge disk at about the same location doing a laser flash and disappearing. The day after this second event, Military and other helicopters were scouring that same patch of sky for hours. How do I feel about it? I am still recovering from the first sighting and just hope I am not being singled out for something. My job puts me in the right places and times for these sightings so I am thinking it must be that. I have been having some street lamp interference events lately so I have been looking up a lot.
