{nd} Three orange glowing UFO's over route 81

I got off of work at 5 o'clock and I work off of exist 77. I take route 81 south home towards Harrisburg. As usual on Friday traffic was going really slow about 15 mph. The sun was setting and starting to get dark outside. I noticed a bright orange glowing streak descending from the sky and then two more. At first I thought from an airplane and the sun reflecting on it but as they came down they all turned and started going straight. Then one went up behind a cloud. Then the other two were just hovering for about 5 minute then started to go west. I watch them in disbelief. I took out my cell phone and started taking pictures while driving. Then all of a sudden they were gone. It wasn't until I got home and zoom in and saw what appears to be windows on the craft. I'm still in disbelief and thought I would here something about it on the news giving all of the traffic on the highway, but nothing. I'm definitely a believer now. I blew some of the photos up and inverted a couple to show that they solid objects. I just blown away.
