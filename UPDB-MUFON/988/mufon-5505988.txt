UFO over Edmonton International Airport

Saw what looked like the landing lights of a passenger jet. But the light suddenly dropped straight down over the airport and hovered. It held its elevation and moved slowly to the west and hovered again. I tooked a night vision photo with my telescope (attached) and a short movie which did not turn out. Then as a passenger jet approached from the east it took off to the south and dissapeared. The UFO was about 3 times bigger than the plane.
