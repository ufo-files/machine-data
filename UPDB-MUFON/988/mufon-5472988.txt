{rjl} Distant light appeared to be hovering at or near new transmission lines. Could have been an arching of some type, just stopped.

Woke at about 4AM, 17 Oct. 2017. looked out the back door and noticed a strong light at some distance from the house, off to the NorthWest, at or near what I believe are some new transmission lines for SDG&E.
Light was intense, appeared to GLOW with the appearance of a small fire on the ground, but it never got larger or spread while I watched.
Stayed the same bright white color and just seemed to BE THERE!
Then it was gone. Just went out.
Watched for awhile and never saw another aircraft in the air which I found unusual for the area. Usually see Something flying on regular paths ever since I have been her.....35 years.
