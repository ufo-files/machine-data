{cak} 6 red-orange orbs traversed across the sky from east to west

I thought the lights were a squadron of military helicopters flying in a straight line however after a few seconds realized the objects were completely silent. There were 5 orbs in a straight line spaced equally apart. All were traveling at the same speed and after a minute the dark red color of each object changed to a lighter amber color. After 1.5 minutes each object faded and disappeared sequentially. One additional orb came after singly 30 seconds later along the same path and faded similarly. No one in the group could explain what the objects were. I was very excited as I've been researching the UFO phenomenon for at least 4 years. I am an Environmental Scientist with 15 years experience in government with 10 years experience in helicopter aerial monitoring. Based on my observation and experience, these objects were not conventional aircraft or Chinese Lanterns.
