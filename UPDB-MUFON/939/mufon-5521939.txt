Witnessed 4-5 lights in a line formation hovering above a treeline then appear to descend into/behind them.

My boyfriend and I were saying goodbye to a friend of ours at the front of his house, they were talking about his birthday, I was staring in a north westerly direction at the bottom of the avenue at the sky just above the chimney of the house directly in front of me, i suddenly noticed the lights, whether they appeared from nowhere or came into my view from another part of the sky I couldn't tell. I pointed to the lights/object excitedly and yelled to the guys to get their attention on it, they took a few seconds to locate it in the sky at which point they expressed great surprise at it's appearance, at this point it started to "hover" to the left. We jumped back into the car quickly in an attempt to follow it and rounded the corner at the bottom of the street when the object dipped below the tree line; disappointed at losing sight of it, we continued to turn left and cross the train tracks, then driving into the streets behind the group of trees that the object had "hovered" below in a smooth motion and at quite a fast speed. Our searching from the car uncovered nothing and we drove back to our friends place to drop him off, all still very excited by the event.
