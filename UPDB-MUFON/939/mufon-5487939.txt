came down from sky and started hovering around our car in circles about 30 ft up

was sat in car down lane with 3 friends having a couple of beers.we were under age 17.i was laid in back with boot up looking at the stars.to my supprise one started getting bigger and bobbing from side to side.thinking I was seeing things I told my pal to look.we all saw it coming closer and closer. a brilliant white/blue light in the shape of a sphere.it was like it new I was watching it coming down.it got to about 30ft above car and started circaling around us as though it was bobing on water.it made no sound but must have been 30ft in diameter.by this time me and my friends were starting to panick I shut boot and driver driv off.the light followed us for about 5 miles down lanes until we came to a built up town then it went back up where it looked just like a star. to this day I have never seen anything like it. all our watches and the car clock were out by 35 minutes.
