{jpg} It was a flying saucer ship hovering above me when I was leaving cockrans Firestone.

I was leaving work on Friday night and stepped out of the front door. I noticed something coming towards me flying low and not making any noise. It stopped moving directly over my head close enough to hit with a rock. I could see the whole bottom of the ship. I was screaming for my boss man,s name to get his attention but my voice was not projecting loud enough. It had lights around the outside and a dome underneath. After hovering above me it slowly moved towards iron Hill and hovering over top. After a minute or two it took off into space at a high rate of speed that was shocking to me. I was young and ran home and told my family. My sister ran down stairs hollaring that she had seen it to. She was sitting at her window that was facing iron Hill. From were i was working to iron Hill was only about a mile. I was so excited and happy that I had seen a actual space ship.
