{cak} These circular like rotating, flashing, moving, color changing things appear fairly regularly in the sky. have been seen by multiple people. not planes or choppers

On multiple occasions my sister, her family, and I have viewed these lights in the sky close to her home. These lights are not aircraft such as airplanes or helicopters. They appear to be high in the sky. There is never any sound that any one of us can hear coming from them. There are generally 3 seperate lights that appear circular that appear to rotate and change (as the color seems to wobble a little bit) colors such as green, red, blue, gold. They also move up and down and across the sky zig zagging at quick speed. Then all of a sudden just disappear. Almost like they're putting on a show. One sometimes seems to be the leader of them. It's hard to explain. I've never seen anything like this. None of us have. My sister told me about it and I didn't believe her exactly until I visited her and saw it for myself and heard others say they've seen these lights too. I thought if I saw the lights I'd be able to give a logical explanation for them but I haven't been able to do that. Also when I tried to get a picture of it, the picture wouldn't show up on my cellular and the camera's wouldn't flash to take the picture. That happens every time I've tried to video or take a picture. It is definitely odd.
