Red orb {floating at the back of the field just below the tree line} - {djs}

I was letting my dogs out last year around 5 am sometime last July, it was before the sun was up. I let them out the back and I have a field behind my house with a tree line on the right side of me and woods really all around the field. We are out in the country, it was very dark. I had just woken up so i didn't turn the light on. I went out on the back deck to call for the dogs and that's when I saw a bright red light floating at the back of the field. It was hovering just below the tree line and seemed to be coming towards the house. I watched it for a few mins and then it banked right, into the woods and i lost the light after that. Also i remember it didn't make a sound when it went thru the trees,and the vegetation was overgrown. I was waiting for a crash but nothing. I was not quite sure what I had seen. I honestly thought someone was flying a drone or something in our yard. My husband was gone, so I told my dad about it that afternoon. He then informed me that it couldn't have been a drone, nothing could fly in the wind we had been having. We had been having storms the past two days, 30-50 mph wind gusts. It had been very warm and windy that morning... he then said " Hunny i think you might have seen a UFO." I laughed and didn't really think about it until I was watching the history channel and I saw a HANGER 1 episode on native American UFO encounters and they talked about a red, glowing orb. What they described its exactly what I saw that night. And the other thing that is weird is I have seen them twice now ( only for a few mins then they go into the woods) and we live near an old sacred native American site and burial ground.
