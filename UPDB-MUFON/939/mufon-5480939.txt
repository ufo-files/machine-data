Bright white light sitting stationary, then slowly disappeart.

I was in bed when I noticed a bright light thru the window. I went up, looked out the window and saw a very bright, white thing hovering high up in the air near the nuclear power station. First I thought it was a Star but I then realised that it was way brighter than all the other stars. It also wasn't an airplane, because the light wasn't flashing like an airplanelight would do. It started to lower a bit and then the light slowly disappeared. When I looked at it about 10 seconds before it disappeared tears came out of my eyes and I couldnt see it clearly anymore. The first reaction after that was to think clearly about what happened. I then took my Phone and wrote this Report.
