A large cloud displays unique lights that seem to come from a a very big unknown disk.

An unobstructed large stationary cloud had several internal lights whose wide beams projected up about 45 degrees. The lights seemed to be on continuously, fixed in direction and coming from the edge of an unseen object. The object, a ring or rings around its edge that held the lights was rotating in alternating directions. These rotations caused the sweeping motions of the beams. These illuminations gave a disk shape to the object or objects from which they emanate. The light beams stopped at the edge of the cloud. There no noise other than that coming from the normal light traffic and strong winds. The size and shape of the object, the size of its light beams, its stationary position, the angle of the lights' projections, and the lack of noise all make this object not possible to be any airplane, helicopter, balloon, or drone known to me.
