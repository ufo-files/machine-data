No UFO, alien walking on beach in broad daylight.

I was walking on the beach after paddling in on my surfboard. I noticed him standing relaxed and casual with eyes the size of pool balls and a cranial capacity twice that of earth-human. I thought he was a living star child, like the people who build the pyramids. He moved naturally and didn't say a word. I felt at an impasse with the universe, I wanted to bring him to the local tv station and tell the world but i couldnt muster that popularity was essential at the moment. I even let him pass out of my sight just like all the moments of my life go by.
