{cak} Sky lit up bright as day then returned to normal. Investigation revealed white orb traveling across the sky

I was watching television when I noticed the sky light up bright as day. Much like when a meteor crashes down to Earth. My curiosity having been peaked, got the better of me so I went to investigate. Given my previous experience with a meteor hitting Utah, I assumed it was just that. I sat outside maybe 5 minutes smoking a cigarette. Not even a whole minute after I lit it, I noticed the orb. It had been hovering a moment and blended right in with the stars, hence why I didn't notice initially. Iat first it was falling slowly, like a car idling. 'm no expert at judging speed but I'd say it took off at least twice as fast as I've ever seen a plane move. The orb was at least twice as bright as a star, solid and unwavering, and it took off in the direction of Vista (Northeast)
