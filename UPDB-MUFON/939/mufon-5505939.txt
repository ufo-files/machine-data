Orange Orb

my partner has reported to me that while on his travels back to his home town, he was passing through the small town of peterborough in south australia, sometime after this he reported to me that a strange orange shaped ball was following him, which he could see out of his window just above the tree top at the time he was doing atleast 140km/h and it was keeping up with his speed. he said that it followed him for some time running in a horizontel direction to his window and that at one stage it also effected his car eletrics. The radio and head lights on his car started to fade on and off and also the car engine started chugging and was almost about to die. this happened for about 20 seconds or so, then the strange orange orb disapeared into no where like it had just faded out and the car suddenly came back to normal. Around 10 - 15 mins later, driving through a hilly area, in the middle of no where. still doing at least 140km/h he came to a windy road, where he almost crashed his car because there was something standing in the middle of the road which looked human/alien like, but he reported it was altho it was almost covered in a white bedsheet. he doesnt recall going through peterborough.
