{rjl} Photographed from window in Amtrak train headed North to Vancouver, BC

Photos are taken from Cascades Amtrak train departed 7:45 AM from King Street Station taken about 2 hrs, 10 minutes into the trip, train was (approximately) in Bellingham, Washington. I was taking ramdom photos from the train window. It was a cloudy dull day, but I wanted memories of the scenery. When we arrived home, I went through the photos and saw a tiny light progressing through 4 different photos in the sky headed East toward the mountains. When I enlarged them to see what they were the results left me speechless. 

A friend finally convinced me to share them on this site.
