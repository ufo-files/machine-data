Black/ carbon fiber triangle with no sound

On the way there I notice 2bright lights on the ground to my right over behind some trees/ wooded area. I right away tell my girlfriend “wow the truck has nice bright high beams(lights)”... at that time I thought there was a back road there and this truck was driving there with its high beams on so I can see.
I grab my phone text my cousin- Yo were like 10-15mins away...When I look back towards the lights; they are now floating above the trees and I just got sooo confused and tell girl”ummm babe the lights are floating on top of the trees”...Right away she says whoa! That’s weird record it...
