Las Vegas

Moving from east to northwest of the las vegas valley, a bright object moved slowed and stopped a few times over my head. I ran inside and grabbed my wife so she could also see it with me so i didn't think i was crazy. She saw it and was surprised that it did'nt have any blinking lights at all and it was just a solid light. it stopped for 10 seconds, then moved into a different direction towards the north of the valley. It slowly moved past the mountains and it disappeared. last 3 minutes almost. never seen anything like it ever. ive seen seen satalites before move in the sky but nothing like this. as i right this.. i can hear air force jets outside my house.
