{dr}Saw object above the hills while driving home in afternoon on a fall day.

I was on my way home from work, alone in my car, driving south. Noticed the object to my left as I was looking over the valley. Did not have an idea of what it was, my thought was whats that?. The object was cylinder shape, green in color and appeared to glow. It seemed to have a spiked surface. It moved silently in a straight line, heading south. I watched it (about 1 min.) while I drove along the road until I needed to make a right-hand turn onto the road that I lived on. Our home was on a hillside about 1/2 mile from where I was when I saw it. While I watched it, I thought it was pretty cool and was curious about what it was. When I arrived at home, my daughter (13) and her female friend who had been climbing a tree in our front yard, came running over to me, very excited and told me that they had seen a green light over the hills, moving across the sky. I told them that I had seen it too and we talked about it for a few minutes, that we had seen a UFO. We then shifted our attention to dinner and the plans for the evening. We did not talk about it much after that but mention it from time to time as a very neat experience.
