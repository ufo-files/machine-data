I was standing out on my second story deck at 12:30 am on May 16, 2021 star watching. I go out most clear nights because there isn't as much ambient light around me at that time of night and so I have a good view of the western sky. This night There was some wispy light gray cloud cover to the north, but many of the stars were still visible.  

Suddenly a cigar or rod shaped object with rounded ends flew out from behind a big deciduous tree in full leaf to the north west of my deck in a neighbor's yard. It flew slowly in a straight line toward the east until it went out of my line of sight. It's shape was absolutely uniform from end to end with no visible features, no lights, and absolutely no sound. Unfortunately I had left my phone in the house so I had no way to record what I saw.
