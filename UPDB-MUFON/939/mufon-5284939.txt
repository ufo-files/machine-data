Took a picture of what appeared to be a rocket trail. However, a craft and several white glowing objects appeared instead

My daughter and I noticed an odd white trail appearing in the sky - like a rocket was going up in the air. I had her take a picture of it with my phone. When we got home and looked at the photo, there was no trail. Instead we saw several white shapes and the cloaked appearance of a ship (very large, upper left portion of the frame). The ship seems oval in shape and has several circular areas underneath. When we zoom in on the white shapes, they look like separate glowing entities.
