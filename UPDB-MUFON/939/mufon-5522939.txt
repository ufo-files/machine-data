One orb light was seen then two then a third and fourth. One was far too bright to be a star. It stayed high above. While watching after about 15 minutes an airplane was seen coming toward the objects. Also a green orb lit a nearby residence. It was very bright than dimmed got bright again and almost went out before it brightened again than turned orange and all the strange light ceased. Three of the of four were orb like and seemed to glow. They went from south to North in what looked like the Linden area. The fourth one was more directly overhead and it was intensely bright, unlike any star ever seen.
