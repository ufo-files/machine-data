UFO seen in digital photos only, after the event and not by eye

It was a pleasant evening. I could see that there was a crescent moon and a setting sun in the sky at the same time and I tried to capture both on my mobile camera, set behind the foreground of my back yard. I was frustrated that they were not close enough together to both fit in one photo, so I compromised: taking a few pictures of the moon first, then moving on to the sun. It was not until a few days later that I checked my photos and saw what appeared to be a UFO in four of the pictures I took, moving from south to south west and maybe turning slightly. In one of the photos, there is a colored aura around what appears to be a craft with lights arranged underneath it. The craft appears to be planiform in shape in the larger image of it, but triangular in the others, including the first one, where it appears to pass in front of the moon before becoming visible to my mobile (or vice versa!). It is even possible there are two different craft, moving in different directions, but I have not been able to make sense of the data to know for sure.
