{nd} Donna witnessed two bright white lights with a dark section connecting them.

I was walking down west on Capital Avenue and an old man asked me 'can you see that?' I looked up and saw two white round lights about one mile in the sky. They were stationary at first then began to moved to the right at the same distance from each other. They seemed connected by a dark section between them. I was very surprised and puzzled at the image. It has been 4 hours and Ii can't stop thinking about it. The sighting lasted about 5 minutes until they were too far away to view. I never saw anything like it before. I am 56 years old.
