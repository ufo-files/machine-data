{rjl} one flying object which looked like an open triangle/boomerang shape yellow/orange lights (maybe 10 per side) fly above me on 10/10/14 at 10:15pm. Lights had bright light in the center and a ring light around it.

I took my dog outside at 10:15pm on October 16, 2014. I always look up in the sky to look at the stars. All of a sudden there were these yellow-orange lights, like an open triangle/boomerang (maybe 50 degree angle) flying above me, coming from NE going SW. The lights (which formed the open triangle shape) maybe 10 per side, looked so different nothing like I had ever seen before. It had a bright light in the center and a ring light around it. What struck me was it made no sound at all, absolutely silent. I tried to see a shape (besides the one the lights made) but I couldn't. The whole event lasted maybe 10 seconds because the object was flying above me from NE to SW and I lost sight of it due to houses and trees. I thought maybe that was a hang glider but then I thought they don't fly at night and those lights looked so different.
