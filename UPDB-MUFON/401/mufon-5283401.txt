Strange lights in the sky

My kids and I were out enjoying the snow before bed...our roommate was observing a ufo/strange lights in the sky above the treeline. There was no stars visible or air traffic. We grabbed binoculars and took turns observing it. When it was my turn observing it through the binoculars, I saw the lights spread out and then come back together to form a triangle formation. We've lived in this area at this property for a very long time we know all the landmarks and all the usual lights from houses at night and where these lights were at there's no houses up there there's not even a road up there....the lights were definitely above the treeline, at one point one of the lights zoomed off super fast and disappeared. We observe this phenomena for about an hour.
