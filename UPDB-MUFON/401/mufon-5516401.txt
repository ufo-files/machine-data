I attend a series of motorcycle meets at various pubs in the Worcester/Glocestershire area every Wednesday during the summer. I take many photographs at these meets, of the bikes and people that attend and also landscape shots.

On this occasion I took 222 pics. I download them to the facebook site of the group. I did not notice the "orbs" at the time of taking the photo. Nevertheless, I was not looking at the sky, but trying to snap a longer distance shot of the pub where the meet was being held.

When I downloaded and posted the pics, I noticed the 3 "orbs" in a triangular formation, with 2 further "orbs" to the left and right of the triangular formation.

I take many pictures of all sorts, with many hundreds of the bike meets. The camera I use is a Fujifilm S5700 and I have never seen any of my photos with such objects in the sky.

I have no information on the movement etc of the "orbs" as I only noticed them afterwards. I zoomed in on them using my own PC and a some powerful design software that can greatly magnify photographs with a colleague at work the next day. There did seem to be a darker object behind each of the "orbs" of light.

I was puzzled by the photos and could not find an explanation. I consider that it was not a reflection or anything on the lense of the camera, as I had cleaned this prior to attending the event.

As background information. I am fascinated by the UFO phenomenon and I'm firmiliar with the major cases. Notwithstanding this, I do not have any preconceptions on what they are.
