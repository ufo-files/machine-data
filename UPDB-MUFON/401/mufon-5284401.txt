10-12 objects video taped flying very slowly bright glowing orange fire color preceded by two large sonic booms all flying in unison

My wife and I heard two large sonic booms and I went in the back yard 1-3 minutes after the initial sound and saw four bright lights moving through the air no sound not airplane lights not drone lights. No sound no drone prop sounds all moving in unison. After assessing for a minute or two I could not explain this and asked my wife to come out quickly to view this. She did and at low altitude 20 degrees or so we saw another group flying in an ascending line of 7 or 9 and then a few trailing behind that. And all the lights would suddenly disappear about 4-5 miles out abruptly. No light trail just gone suddenly. We live approx 4 miles from Beal Air-force base. I called the sherif this morning and they had no reports of anything but on the local Facebook page several comments showed up asking if anyone else saw the objects. My wife has the video I will upload later tonight.
