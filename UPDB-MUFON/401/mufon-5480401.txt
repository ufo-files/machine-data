Me and my wife were riding four wheelers and we noticed something in the sky. It would disappear than reappear.so I got the idea to signal it and see what happened. As soon as I waved my white t shirt in the air the object disappeared once again. We looked everywhere and thought it was gone then I looked straight up and there was a long tube approx.25ft long,and about ten to fifteen feet above us in the air. My wife flipped out and jumped on the four wheeler and started to leave me there she was so scared. I jumped on and it followed us a little. So that makes me think they were there to pick someone up.
