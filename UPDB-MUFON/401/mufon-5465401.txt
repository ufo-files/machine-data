On 10/4, I was outside in my backyard with my dog before turning in for the night. It was 10:20pm, I looked up to observe the night sky. It was partly cloudy and could only see a handful of stars. Suddenly, I saw 5 white lights come from behind the low lying clouds. They were not very bright but were moving together in a triangular formation heading Southwest. They never broke from formation so it seemed as if they were all part of a single object. There was 1 light at each point of the triangle, and 1 more in the middle of each long side. The space in between the lights was dark, I couldn't see any clouds or stars between them. If it was one object, it would be the size of a jet airplane. They made no sound as they moved. They moved in a straight path, and very quickly. I couldn't figure out what the lights were, there was no other movement in the sky, no typical planes with blinking lights were in sight. These lights were flying much lower then jets do. After 7 or 8 seconds, the lights had moved behind more clouds and I lost sight of them.
