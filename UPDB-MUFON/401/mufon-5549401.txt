Three red spheres that appeared in a triangle shape and then went to line formation

The night of New Years eve in the border city of San Luis R.C. Sonora Mexico. I was sitting facing west looking at the fire works that were being fired up into the sky by my adult and adult nephews when I seen three red spheres in a triangle shape in the west. I first figured they were rockets but they retain their shape and so I ran inside the house and got my digital camera and started shooting digital still video. As I shot the video the spheres began to become a straight line while they passed over head of the group that was observeing the lights. There were five to ten persons in our group watching the lights and at least three videos from different cameras were shot. The objects continued going east until one sphere just went dim and it appeared that the last two became one at the end. I have been told by other's that were visiting ing Mexico that night that they also observed the lights. I have tried to contact the AZ MUFON section with out any luck! I can provide the video if request for a review. I have provide THREE small files of a video capture for your review in the interm.
