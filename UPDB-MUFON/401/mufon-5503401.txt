{cak} Triangular UFO observed in Grants NM during lunar eclipse.

On April 16 I went to my back yard to observe the blood moon eclipse event. While observing, I noticed a strange craft fly overhead moving slowly, blacked out with no sound at all, from south to north towards Mt Taylor. It seemed to be about the size of a house, triangular In shape - altitude approx. 500 feet. No lights except the moon reflecting somewhat off of it. Very strange! I'm still shaken! There's something weird going on out there!
