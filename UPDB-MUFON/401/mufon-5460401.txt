{jpg} Very bright orange orb and larger than a small plane. No wings, no strobes, and completely silent.

I was looking out a NE facing window and noticed a fairly large orange object heading towards my back yard. I ran to the back porch and watched the object cross at approximately 200 ft elevation and moving at about 60 miles an hour. It continued in a SW direction until it disappeared. It was very bright orange and may have had a small white light near the center. This object made no sound. There were no strobes, green or red lights, and it did not have wings.
