Disc-like object with lights arrived very quickly; stopped and went in different direction; disappeared in south.

Silvery disc had extremely bright blinking red light and white-blue lights on edge of object which were rotating around disc and flashing. Object came from north and at one point was directly above witnesses. Disc slanted itself as it lowered and approached witnesses. 
It then retraced its path going higher. Approached moon and continued southward and disappeared there.
