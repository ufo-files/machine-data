White orb observed and filmed over Morrison Colorado

On August 2, 2020 8:47 am, I was fishing on a lake in Jefferson County Colorado when noticed a white orb high in the sky over the mountains. It was stationary and didn’t seem to move for over an hour that it was observed. I witnessed planes flying over and under the object so it was really high up. I had two other witnesses that wish to remain anonymous. I used my phone to film the object but it was super bright out so I had to guess where to point the camera so the video is shaky. We continued fishing and I observed the object for the next hour until we paddled on a kayaks to a point where we couldn’t see it because of the hills around the lake. We we beached our kayaks I continued looking for the object but it had disappeared.
