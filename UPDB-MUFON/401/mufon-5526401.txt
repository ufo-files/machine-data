Triangle UFO slowly moving over Salem Mass

At around 10:15 pm my family and I were coming home from a very late dinner and were pulling into our driveway/parking area. The lot was full so I decided to drop everyone off and circle around the neighborhood (one way streets) and park in front of the house. While I was circling around the neighborhood I looked up in the sky and saw 3 lights in a perfect triangle with a red light in the middle. These lights were not super high but not too low and were coasting over the neighborhood. To make this even more amazing the craft made absolutely no sound. When I saw this I was very excited and in a way couldnt believe that I was finally lucky enough to see something like this. I started to follow the lights in my car but eventually the streets didn't match up with the path that the lights were traveling. When I finally got to a clearing where I had a better view of the sky the lights were gone.
