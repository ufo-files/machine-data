Was in bed when we saw object through window outside coming over the house. It was glowing orange with white jellyfish whitish surrounding. Snaking slowly like a living creature

My wife and I was in bed when out of the window at just after midnight we both spotted an object traveling slowly towards and over our house. It was glowing orange with whitish edges like a jellyfish or squid. It was snaking almost like it was swimming. It appeared quite low and was quite big. Much bigger than any type of bird. I ran down stairs and out the front door after it went over the house but there was no sign of it anywhere. It was a clear night with stars. I was fascinated by this and hoped to see it nearer outside but it was just gone.
