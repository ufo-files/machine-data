{cak} While driving on hwy, people ahead of me pointed to object. We got out of cars and observed.

While driving east on old Hwy 70 in Arkansas on April 15, 1950, people in a car ahead of me motioned toward an object hovering over the highway ahead of us about a mile and about 39 degrees above the horizon. We got out of our cars and watched it for about 5 minutes when it suddenly flew upward and away. I drove on to my destination of Baltimore. The next day, a photo and article appeared on the front page of the Baltimore Sun and the image was exactly like what we had seen on the highway. Years later I contacted the Sun to see if I could get a copy of that newspaper article and was told they would not have it in Baltimore but it would be available at the Maryland Archives. I contacted the Archives who, after a few days, told me the photo had been cut out of the front page of the paper from its position that I had said it was in. The object we saw did not move during our observation until it took off toward the east.
