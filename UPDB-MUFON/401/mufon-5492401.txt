My husband & I had just been to church in Oklahoma City. We headed back to Norman & had stopped at a convenience store. We drove a short distance & commented that it was just 12:15. We had gotten out earlier then most services we have attened. The next thing we knew we were both asking at the same time" What time is it & where are we?." We looked at the clock & it was now 2:15. We both could not figure out what had happened & where were we. We figured out we were on the opposite side of town from where we had been but it would not have taken us 2 hours to get there. Both of us just knew we were missing 2 hours. We felt strange like we had just woke up & at the same time but that was all. We still do not know what happened to the 2 hours. We told a close friend a few years ago & she told me that she & her husband had had the same thing happen to them in another state & they too lost 2 hours.
