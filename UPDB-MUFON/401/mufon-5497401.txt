second time I have seen this light here. hovering and lowering before ascending

On this occasion the bright light was glowing in the sky. It was only about 100 ft off the ground and stationery for a min then started rising and stopping. Then the light slowly went off and a black object remained for a second before a duller light came on and the object rose into the cloud cover. The weather was clear and sunset was behind me. I have seen this same object over the village before late at night again on a clear cold night about a month before and on this occasion it moved fast dropping into the fields and hovering about 50th off the ground then climbing again. I was about two miles away across the fields with my dog.
