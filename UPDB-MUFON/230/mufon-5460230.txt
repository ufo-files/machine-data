{cak} Bright Orange Light In Ashton Under Lyne - Greater Manchester

I was at the hospital visiting a family member and I needed to walk outside from one side of the hospital to another part.

As I looked up into the sky I seen this bright orange light, it was so unusually bright and it was moving across the sky at a steady pace.

It did have some unusual flashing to it so I thought that it could possibly be a plane but with how bright it was I decided to take my camera out and start filming the object anyway.

The object appeared to change directions in the sky at one point but I was unable to get the camera recording in time.

I have looked at flight data in the area and there were no commercial planes in the area at that time. 

There was no noise from this object.

The only logical explanation I can think is that this was a drone of some kind but it was just so bright, I’ve never seen a drone so bright before. It's the only thing I can't yet rule out though and would like the opinion of others.
