{nd} Bright light (star like) object hovering for the past month, and then disappears*

I thought it was a plane descending in the distance but after 10 minutes the light remain there, the sky was clear and it was about 8:30pm at aprox 8:50pm I noticed that the light had changed direction so I knew it was not a star. This event has then repeated for the past month, I am going to say since early August of 2013, and now the light has been in a SW position in what it seems the same direction. The event is always the same, a bright object in the distance (at first it looks like a plane, since we have heavy traffic in the Miami metropolitan area) but it isnt. I kept looking at this object and it looked as if the light disappear for a few seconds, then the light reappeared again only to disappear a few moments later. The best example I could describe is as if you were watching a bright object at a distance and in an instance you see it disappear before your eyes, like if something was sipping it.
