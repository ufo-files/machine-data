{rjl} Low fly over object

As my mother and I were in the shed looking for something we had a street light in our yard that lit up the shed when suddenly the light went out we stepped outside the shed and looked up as a round disc shaped object passed over us slowly as it went over it was very low and knocked out our electric from street light house and the street light out on the street... the object had windows that we could see inside as it passed over and had humans inside in uniforms. This happened in the late 80s when I was 13-14 years old.... I have a very good job so I did not provide my correct name, number or email. This did truly happen and figured I’d report it.
