{cak} 2 discs over Owego, New York, USA

On the 12th day of October 2013, at about 13:00hrs EST, I witnessed a disc like object flying from north east over the Village of Owego, New York. Object omitted no sound, there was no 1trail1 after it there was no visible wings of any kind. A short 22 second video taken on a camera phone was shot.  The disc moved from South West to the North East over the western horizon.   Shortly after another object same size and shape, no wings, no trail, no sound was observed flying from east (Binghamton, NY ) to the North West, however higher in the sky than the first object. Having observed thousands of flights going over this area in the last 30 years, I have never seen anything like this before.
