Amber orange pulsating sphere shaped object went right over my house.

On Oct. 10, 2010 I was driving up to my driveway and happened to look up and saw this orange/ yellow pulsating sphere shaped object. At first I thought it was something for Halloween, but it was not. I came right over my house and right over me. I was standing in the driveway as it passed over me, it made no sound whatsoever. It was not an airplane or helicopter. I don't know what it was, but I don't think it was from our world. The place this object was sighted is in West Texas.
