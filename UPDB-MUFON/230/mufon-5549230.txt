l didn't see a UFO but a light . Where a light shouldn't be!

At about 2:30am I went outside of my house to have a smoke. There were no cars on the street or people. I saw a red light glowing at the end of my rain gutter. We have rain helments on them so they are covered. I walked over and stood under the eve of my house and watched the red light move from under the rain gutter into it. As l watched the light traveled down the rain gutter and slowly went out. By the time it went out it was inside the rain gutter. It had traveled about 2 feet down the rain gutter. Please don't think l am crazy. I wrote it all down in my diary and promised myself I wouldn't tell anyone. Ofcourse I told my husband and to be honest I don't think he believes me. I look for it every time I go out there now but,I have never seen anything like it again. I know this sounds totaly insane but I swear to you as God is my witness I'm telling the truth!!!!!
