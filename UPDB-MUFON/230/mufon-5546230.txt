Bright, shape-shifting object stationary in night's sky.

During my evening walk, I spotted this object approximately five miles high in the western sky. I have seen this same object in the same location at the same times for approximately two weeks prior to this report. It did not move from its location, was brighter and seemingly lower than anything else in the entire sky. To the naked eye, it appears to be a flying saucer. When viewed in photographs, it takes many different shapes. I, personally, am not afraid of aliens and/or UFOs, but I got the distinct impression that "it" did not like being photographed. As of the submission of this report, it is still in the sky.
