{rjl} Large ship moving from the North, Northwest over Tucson to the South, Southeast, creating a dust storm as it went.

While walking home from grade school there was a dust storm. I heard humming so I looked up and saw what looked like a ship from one of the star wars movies moving across the sky. Smoothly it headed as though from the Phoenix area toward Benson. It looked square but not to thick. The corner was like the front based on the direction it was moving. When I first saw it, it was a ways back and I watched it until it smoothly moved into the distance until it was gone. What was even more strange was that out of the many kids walking home after scholl that were in the park/field/ playground, I was the only one that looked up. And I was to amazed, I guess to saw anything to anyone. Burt after it disappeared into the distance, why did I not say anything to anyone. I wonder to this day. Did anyone else ever report this. And as for the exact date, I am not sure. I think I was in 6th grade and that's a guess. 5th or 6th maybe. I think 6th.
