Witness (A) and (B) on many occasions, while traveling together or at several locations around Jackson and Eaton Counties, have observed many unusual objects in the sky. The following nine events were related from a 23May15 interview with the witnesses; EVENT 9 of 9: While they were traveling around their area, on several occasions during the month of May, during the evening twilight hours, observed objects with red and green lights in a rotating pattern.
