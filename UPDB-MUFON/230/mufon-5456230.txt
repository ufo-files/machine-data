Cone shaped bottom, same for top of object, body had a cylinder shaped.

I was at the River Thames in London, I must have been about 4 years old.

I was with my parent sat on a bench observing the river, out of nowhere everything went silent, time itself seemed to have frozen, the wind stopped, traffic stopped, my parent frozen....all except me and what was about to be the object I describe next.

I was compelled to look straight up into the sky, right above where I was sat, there appeared a very small looking dot in the sky, I remember it being a sunny day with a cool breeze.

As I looked at this object it began to get closer to me it soon became obvious this object was much larger as it descended, it has a dark red base to it, ever so slightly pointed at the bottom ( like a spinning top ).....I was trying my best to get my parent to look as I stood up on the bench in shock, still no movement from my parent, time still frozen as this thing hovered silently above me, it was withing touching distance, I remember reaching out to touch it when it ever so slowly ascended back into the sky until it became like a dot and then until I could no longer see it, once I could no longer see it, everything went back to normal, my parent continued moving, cars began to move and the trees began to move in the cool breeze.

I felt confused, I felt alone but I also felt left....

I told my story to only two other people until today, even up to this day those feelings still occur whenever I think of this object.

Previous to this encounter I had never seen anything like it before, but since that day it has been the first of many sightings after and odd experiences.
