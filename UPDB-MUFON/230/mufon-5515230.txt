I was leaving work around dusk and saw dozens of red star sized objects floating above me and slightly to the west. They danced slowly and floated amongst one another as the sailed up until they were out of sight. They occasionally moved between one another horizontally. My reaction was a daze and I got very shaky afterwards because it was so strange. It was still kind of light out and was to the north of a very busy shopping area. I havent heard any other witnesses.
