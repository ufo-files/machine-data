{cak} 3 Bright Red Lights looked like a triangle moving as one

I went outside with my binoculars to see if I can see any meteors from the Per. Meteor shower. I could not get a good view from the front courtyard so I went to the back courtyard which has a clearer view of the northeast. As I approached the backyard, I looked up an instantly recognized 3 bright red lights. Perfect angle with a perfect view. I looked at them with my binoculars and can tell those lights are no airplanes. I frantically tapped on the glass slider to get my girlfriends attention, twice. When she came to the window, I told her to get out here and see these red lights that are in the sky. When she came out, she saw the 3 red lights instantly and realized that those could not be from an airplane. She looked at the red lights through my binoculars and realized that they are all moving at the same speed, as if they were one unit. We passed the binoculars back and forth, and I too was beginning to realized that they were not separate lights but that they looked as if to be as one craft. A very large craft, possibly an enormous craft. We lost sight of it because of the neighbors house, so my girlfriend went to the front yard to see if she could see it crossing over but nothing. I went on to call my brother. That was amazing and scary to see something that was truly without a doubt...Humungous, Enormous, Gigantic, and O.O.T.W.(Out of this world)!!!
