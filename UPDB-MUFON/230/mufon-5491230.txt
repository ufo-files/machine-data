{rjl} Hovering above a field. I couldn't take my eyes off of it

Driving back from Huntsville Alabama to scottsboro. A car was stopped on the side of the road. I slowed down to see what was wrong. And looked up seeing the craft. I had no idea what I was seeing. But couldn't look away. I don't remember driving away till I got down the road ( U.S. Highway 72. I was 19 at the time.
