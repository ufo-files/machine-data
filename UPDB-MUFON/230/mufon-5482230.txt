Stationary UFO with some shorts zig zag and spiral motions at the same area, for 40 minutes.

Adjuntas Puerto Rico, July 14 of 2016, 12:19 am, near 518 road. 
UFO of no more than 251, was hovering stationary and sometimes, made some zig zags and spirals short moves. The event last like 40 minutes or more. The UFO was round like a plate with a circular light from left to right, showing red, blue-green and yellow-orange colors. During all that time, two commercial airplanes pass separatedly. In the two separated moments, the UFO turns off the multicolor circular light, did not move and just left a white stationary light. When they went far away, the UFO turn on again the multicolor circular lights. It was above a mountain, near of the top trees and a main line of electricity cable.
