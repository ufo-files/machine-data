After seeing the DNC convention on tv, I went to the porch to smoke.

Around 11:15 PM I WENT TO SIT IN PORCH. ABOUT 3 minutes later a bright light streaked across the sky, E to W. In a blink of an eye it disappeared. Then four round lights, light amber, appeared beneath the clouds. They moved around each other, one moved from left to right, then right to left. They would blink off and then appear again, moving around. This lasted about 10 minutes and then disappeared.
