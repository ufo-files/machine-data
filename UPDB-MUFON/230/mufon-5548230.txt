A friend and I watched a green colored orb move across the horizon at a varying speed

A friend and I were having a conversation in my back yard when I happened to look up and saw a green colored orb at the 9:00 position. I alerted my friend and we watched the object move clock-wise across the horizon. The object moved from a slow rate of speed to very fast as we lost sight of it. I have seen many shooting stars, satellites and comets, but my friend and I are quite certain it was neither of these. I have never before witnessed anything similar. The sighting left both of us speechless and a bit disturbed.
