{cak} I went outside when I noticed a blue light from the east, two white lights came out of the blue and flew around

On August 8th, 2015, around nine p.m. I went outside to smoke a cigarette and out of the corner of my eye I saw a bright blue light coming from the east. I thought originally it was a star, but it was too low in the sky. Then two white lights sort of came out of the blue light. By this point I could not take my eyes away, the three lights were moving very fast in strange ways, and I deduced that it was no star or plane. The odd movements made no sound the lights gave me an almost playful feeling and I even remember smiling. This light show lasted about ten minutes when all three suddenly disappeared. I waited a few minutes to see if they would reappear and while I was waiting a helicopter came quietly from the east. I am a military veteran and I have never seen a helicopter move that quietly. The quietly moving helicopter went right to where the lights were until I could see it no longer. My neighbor caught the very end of the lights and she said she witnessed something similar the previous evening. I later found out about MUFON and decided to share.
