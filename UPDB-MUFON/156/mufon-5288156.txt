Orbs in the sky

April 15, 2021
Approximately 2325 I was pulling on to my street coming from the store when I noticed 3-4 orange reddish glowing orbs in the sky. The orbs were bright and visible. These orbs were hovering in formation. I quickly parked my car at my house and rushed down the street to get a better look. As I was running down the street the orbs quickly vanished within a blink of an eye. All I could think of was wanting them (orbs) to come back. Within a matter of a split second the Orbs returned. I scrambled to get my phone out to film this phenomenon. As I was doing so one of the orbs had vanish then reappear. At one point it looked as if one of the orbs multiplied into another one. At this point I had my phone out filming everything that unfolded. The orbs appeared to be several miles away. I wasn’t able to hear any propulsion system. As I was observing them they were making straight lines, squares, and triangle shapes hovering in the sky. It was as if they were putting on a show and would compare it to a dance. This must’ve lasted anywhere from 3 to 5 minutes. The next day I made a police report. I asked the lady on the phone if anyone else had witnessed this phenomenon; she stated “no”. I live next to McClellan Air Force Base. And Mather Airport is roughly 15 miles away. You have to think that these orbs would have been picked up on their radars....
