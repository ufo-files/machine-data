Bright white light rotating with a red light.

I saw this object under a bank of clouds northeast of Blissfield/towards Monroe, MI no other stars or objects around it. I was curious because I it was so bright and under the low clouds. I watched the light as I drove home to Toledo and as I travelled through Ottawa Lake, I noticed the light was closer to Toledo than Monroe. As I turned onto Sterns rode I watched and the object seemed to gradually travel to Toledo. I watched it until I returned home and the last I saw it, it was east of Toledo near lake Erie.
