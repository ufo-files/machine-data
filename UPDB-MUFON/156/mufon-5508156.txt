Walking home from a seeing friends in a field (I was under no influence of any substances) I walked past a supermarket I worked at at the time, I looked to the gas works next to it and saw the triangle formation directly above the cylinders that rise up and down. Isosceles in shape I witnessed for over 5 minutes in amazement then lights turned off in quick succession one by one, at first sight I thought three Chinese lanterns but they were remained still and precise in formation and all at the same height, this could suggest a flat underside to a triangluar shaped craft or three light orbs, it was night time so only the lights were visible. I was in some shock as I had been researching phenomina at the time and called up two friends immediately with the news and went back to mine and saw many images and reports of triangle formations confirming what I had just witnessed.
