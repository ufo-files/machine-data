Star like but certainly not a star

I noticed a extremely bright star like object which caught my attention in my back yard facing north east towards Sacramento. 

Object was very bright white continuously, seemed like there was constant movement of the white light. Object also had flashes of blue and red/orange sporadically, however not like plane/helicopter lights, more like sporadic bursts. 

Went between giving in one spot and moving both vertically and horizontally. Almost reminiscent of pacman, left right up down. Lights were visible 80-90% of viewing time, but started to cut out and come back once I was recording on my phone. After 10 minutes or so it went behind some clouds for about 2 minutes then disappeared. 

Lots of planes and helicopters in the area, however this was unlike anything I've seen locally
