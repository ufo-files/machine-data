{cak} Nearly stationary lights thought to be stars

2 very bright (star-like) lights at about 45 degrees from visible horizon . Side-by-side with the one of the right being slightly higher than the one on the left. One witness (wife) noticed them first and reported some (but little) movement. The other witness (myself) independently noticed them a few seconds later. They seemed to be at a farther distance that the typical plane but much brighter. Much brighter than any stars.
After 30 seconds or so both lights brightened somewhat and then rapidly faded out of sight.
