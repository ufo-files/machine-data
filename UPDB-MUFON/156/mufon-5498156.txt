{dr}low flying silent triangle craft

As I was getting into my vehicle approximately 8 miles west from Whiteface, Tx and approximately 3-4 miles south of hwy 125 a low flying triangular shaped craft flew almost directly over head at a very low speed headed north-northeast. It was moving so slow i first thought it was about to land. Craft made no sound. Had bright white lights at each corner. Watched for about 2 minutes before craft made more of eastern path but maintained same attitude. Watched till I could no longer see the lights.
