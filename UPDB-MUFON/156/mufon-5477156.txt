{nd} The UFO was Infiniti shaped and emitted a bluish green light

I was watching tv in bed. I heard a very prominent and loud sound that sounded like a jet I had seen recently. I went outside to look at it and I saw a giant green craft going across the sky that was shaped like an infinity ring, or a horizontal 8. I didn't know what it was. It flew along the beach and left as quickly as it came. I lost sight as it got too far away from me to see. It appeared to be searching intently for someone or something. I may have peed a little when I saw it.
