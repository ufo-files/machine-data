V-Shaped UFO WITH 5 WHITE CIRCULAR LIGHTS UNDERNEATH WITH NO SOUND.

In July of 2006, I was returning to my Friends house at around 6am after collecting my friends from a night out in Liverpool. After coming off the motorway and heading for Cheadle towards Roscoes roundabout, I observed an object coming over the railway bridge which crosses over the roadabout, The object was V- Shaped, Black and slow moving as if scanning for something with 5 white circular lights underneath it. My first thoughts were "Why is that plane flying so low and I've never seen a plane that shape before", as I was driving I slowed down to around 10-15mph to longer observe what I was seeing and as I got closer it appeared larger over head and my first thought was to floor the car and get the hell out of there but I couldn't do that as my natural instinct was to stick around and see what this was. I pulled over to the left side of the road and stepped out the car. I looked up at a 35-45 degree angle and this Object was no plane as there was NO NOISE being emitted which really freaked me out but as I stood with this what I can only call a UFO coming straight at me I could see the circular lights clear as day and it was at just above the houses so very visable is just stopped then turned right heading over the houses to my left. At this point there was still NO NOISE so I got back in to the car and drove off very freaked out and completely shocked and baffled by what I had just witnessed, but still to this day I know what I saw and Know what I witnessed and that object was not from this planet. UFO's Do exist and I do believe what I saw was a UFO.
