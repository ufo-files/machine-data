Strange Ligts in Sky

I have noticed these lights in the sky for the past few hours. There appears to be at least four of them going in a cross shape. The go back so far, circle and meet in the middle. I tried taking a video to figure out what it is but its hard to see in the dark and with the clouds and my battery died so I got under a min. They are still there and I am almost certain it is not a plane because a plane would not go in several directions like that; especially so late at night and with the weather we had today. (Was raining, lost power, thunder and lightning). I did not lose sight of it, it is still there.
