Bright green light in sky directly overhead, moving fast

My husband (who has better tgan perfect vision) and I were having a cigarette on the sidewalk looking at the stars at 1:30 am. My husband saw it first. It pulsed a bright green color then disappeared. I checked the time right then and tried to be ready to take a video, but it wasn't picking anything up. My husband was pointing where it was when we both saw it again, further southwest than it had been 3 seconds prior. It disappeared and reappeared further SW. Then we didn't see it again. It lasted 3 minutes total and blinked/reappeared about 4 times. There was no sound, it was dead quiet in our neighborhood at that hour. To me it just looked like a bright green aircraft like light, but I am very nearsighted. My husband, with great vision, described it as a perfect diamond shape, with the green light so bright it was splintering but splintering in a perfect diamond shape. There were no planes. No drones. Nothing.
