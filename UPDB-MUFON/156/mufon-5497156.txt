Ok . im now 72 years old and never mentioned this to anyone other than my wife. I was a police officer in Takoma Park, Maryland and was on routine patrol sometime after midnight when i decided to stop and take a smoke break and relax a bit.I pulled over in Carl xanders garage which was closed and I had a good view od the area and i stepped out of my car and lit up a smoke. as i did i looked up as something cought my eye.An extremely btight object in the Eastern sky that at first i thought was a star. i had never seen one so bright. as i stared at it, it suddenly shot off to the north at an incredible speed. I got the hell scared out of me. i havent felt that frightened in my life and i was even shaking but i dont know why. I got back in my unit and drove to the police station where i knew there was someone to be around. Nothing ever scared me back then being young and a tough cop, but that did.I hung around the station for awhile and the sergeaqnt chased me back out on the patrol but i didnt go back by that area for the rest of my shift.I never mentioned it to anyone for fear of being ostracized.
