Object stationary for half hour

Sighting 6:32am. Object was stationary for 30 minutes, v-shaped, 3 lights visible. 1 light was centre front, other 2 were left and right of centre on end of wings. Initial thoughts were Venus, however very low sparse cloud or stars, it was exceptionally low, possibly a couple of hundred feet from the ground which can estimated from the hills on the horizon. This route is walked every day at the exact same time and the object has never been seen before. The footage does not do justice to what was seen, albeit totally open to opinions and thoughts.
