{nd} I was young, we were playing on a playground. UFO came over head and hovered with lights then turned and left. Four people witnessed it, I am the only one that remembers. There was a fox on the ground afterwards, they remember the fox

I was about six or seven years of age, playing on a playground at a campground with my sister and our two friends. Time sort of stopped and this object appeared coming closer with blinking lights... red orange yellow blue green white red orange yellow blue green white. It got very close overhead where we were on the playground and hovered for what I thought felt like forever. Then it turned and very fast but not light speed fast it zipped the other way and out of sight. Afterwards I remember being very frightened and we all sat around waiting for something to happen. I remember there being a fox on the ground (we were on top a slide) and we were then scared to get down because of the fox. eventually we all went back to our campers after the fox disappeared, it's been years now and im the only one that remembers. no noise, just the lights, a big black object and a very uneasy time stopped feeling.
