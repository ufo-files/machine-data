{jpg} Blue orb flew between the moon and saturn (from my earthly POV), growing smaller and dimmer as it moved east.

I went out for a cigarette. Looked up and noticed the Moon, flanked by Mars and Saturn, shining brightly, directly above our home. I set up my basic point-and-shoot digital camera and mini-tripod on the hood of our pick-up truck. While composing my shot, looking at the display, I thought I saw Saturn moving closer to the Moon, then thought, "Wait...What?!?" So I looked up. Clicked a picture. Looked up. It was definitely moving. There was Mars. There was the Moon. There was Saturn. Clicked another picture.

It was a blue orb and appeared to be moving up and to the East. It didn't twinkle like a star or a drone and it wasn't a fireball or missile. It took a straight path and moved somewhat swiftly across the sky. I watched it for nearly two minutes as it grew smaller and dimmer, until out of view.

It was beautiful and peaceful and clear.
