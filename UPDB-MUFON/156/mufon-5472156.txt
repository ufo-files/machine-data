{rjl} Coming home through De Smet at stop sign.-looked left and viewed over bldgs..

I was coming home from a band job in Watertown going through DeSmet, SD. Came to a stop sign junction in town - looked left. Over the bldg. tops in the sky I saw an orb-like ball of fire that remained stationary. I looked away, and then back ai the the ball. It was still there. I watched for a few seconds, turned onto Hwy 14 and went on home to Huron -- .
