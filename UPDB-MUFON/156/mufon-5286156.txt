Orange redish orb rising from the end of lake then stationary then left with great speed

Although this happened on Auh 10th, 2007 I have decided to place it on record. It has always stayed with me as clear as to when we saw it. It was a Friday evening. My wife, a friend, and I were sitting on our front deck at our cottage on Grand Lake, Annapolis County, Nova Scotia when I noticed a reddish-orange light rising towards the southeast end of the lake. I told the others who turned and watched this light rise slowly then hover. The object/light was not a solid color but semi-transparent. At the time I was not aware of "orbs' and could not understand what it was. After approx. 5 minutes I made a huge mistake that I have regretted ever since. I decided to get up to go get binoculars in the cottage. My friend had turned back around but my wife had a clear view all along. When I returned it was gone. When I asked where it went my wife said it just left towards the right at incredible speed. Oh, how I wish I had seen that!! The experience was written in our cottage diary where it still is today. A few days later I went on the internet where I learned about so-called "orbs". It should be noted that all three of us are trained inspectors/auditors and one managed a municipal airport so we are used to detail. Although it's been a few years since it happened I still hope that someone in the area that evening saw it as well and may post.
