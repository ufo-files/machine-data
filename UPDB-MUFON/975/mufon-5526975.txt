Triangular lights float across sky

I went out to my car to get my backpack and laptop, and looked up to the sky as I usually do. There were 3 lights in the shape of an equilateral triangle which seemed to be at a reasonable altitude, no noise, went flying north from the south at a fairly decent speed. It was not rotating. I lost sight of it because of light pollution toward the capitol building. (I am in Memory Grove a little bit southeast of the capitol building.) There were other planes flying near the flight path of the triangular lights, but were at lower altitudes. Also, I was not able to visually see any kind of ship, only the lights. I was not able to determine if it blacked out stars behind it because of the light pollution and also because I was in sheer awe of what I was seeing.
