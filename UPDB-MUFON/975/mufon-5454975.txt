{tjd} Lights moving across the sky

I spotted what I thought was a satellite, as I was looking at Venus in the Western night sky, (I think it was Venus) during my evening walk. I stopped to get my bearings and to make sure what I was looking at was, indeed, moving. After I stopped to watch, I saw a light moving across the sky, like a typical satellite would. It was not as bright as the Space Station, which is not visible from Frankfort, Ky tonight. As I watched, another light appeared from the same direction. As i continued to watch, yet another one appeared. As I stood and watched, at least 6 of these lights appeared, one after another, exactly the same distance apart. They continued on in a straight line from West to East. Finally, they stopped coming. But I don't know if there were more before I could see them. I think I saw at least 6.

So I continued on my walk and a few minutes later, I saw another light, from the same area, and then another followed it. This happened at least three times. It was like there were 3 pairs. There may have been more, but I was walking in my neighborhood and I could not view that spot in the sky the whole time. 

I did try to take a picture but they seemed really distant and the photo did not turn out very well.

Possibly, a military exercise? Only, what I saw did not have any blinking lights. Also, there was no noise. They looked like stars moving, or satellites. But, do groups of satellites move in unison?
