Colorado Springs hovering orb in daylight

I was looking SE outside of my window from the 3rd floor after working out.  I noticed a shimmering orb that stood out in the relatively clear sky. I thought it may have been a reflection of an interior light but I did not have any on at the time. I viewed the object for approximately 3 minutes before it disappeared. It moved relatively slowly but made frequent directional changes. Unable to determine the size but it was 45degs above the horizon. Few clouds in the sky. Nearest airport is KCOS ~10 miles directly east. Metar was 12013G23 10SM Few100 few 250. Unable to determine distance from my location. I am Space Ops Officer and have experience as an Air Force pilot.
