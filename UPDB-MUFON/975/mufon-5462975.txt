Sigar shaped object on the southern peak of a mountain.

I was on a highway going south, I peaked over the horizon (at a distance of approximately 5km) when I saw a very big object (black and slightly reflective, cigar\blimp shaped) hovering statically and vertically, on the side of "Monte Sant' Angelo".
I pulled my phone out just in time to take one picture, looked at the screen then looked back, and noticed it disappeared.
The sighting lasted approximately 10-15 seconds.
