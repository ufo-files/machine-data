Saw boomerang of red lights come from out of thin cloud heading NW. Silent, right above me. About three fingers width in size. Fast.

I was trying to watch the lunar eclipse last night here in the UK (July 27th 2018), but heavy cloud prevented it. I popped back and forth to see if the cloud had broken and it was starting to break around 10.15pm so I stayed on my balcony. The eclipse was almost over and was rising behind the trees in the garden so ironically I couldn't see anything even though the clouds were breaking. So I just looked straight up to see if I could see any stars and I noticed Cygnus (I think), but with broken thinning cloud over it in part. Then I noticed a sort of shadow in the cloud and then out into the clear sky came 7 (?) red lights all in a row, but bent like a boomerang. I was truly stunned. There was no noise at all and it just shot over my head. The whole thing lasted about 3 seconds. It must have been really big too because it looked about 2 or 3 inches wing span if I held my thumb and finger up. My feeling was one of subtle awe and really happy!
