Rocket/Missile shaped UFO flying low over my home.

I remember the sighting very clear. I was young around the age of 6/7. I was inside my bedroom overlooking the garden where my cousins and sisters were playing, it was a hot clear day. As I look out the window I saw a very strange looking aircraft similar to a shape of a missile. It was very large with a white surface and green 'wings/rudders', along with writing with what looked the be a flag/badge. The aircraft was very low, flying at a very slow speed in a straight path and also appeared to be slowly rotating without changing course. The sighting lasted roughly 30-40 seconds before loosing sight as it flew over the house. The next day I discussed the object with my classmates. 2 others witnessed the object that day but in different locations and colours ranging from white/red, white/blue and the object I witnessed being white/green. Oddly a few days later no one seemed to remember the objects but me. 10 years on I can still recall the sighting as clear as day, but I seem to be the only one. To this day I have not seen a similar aircraft, not even military. This is the only UFO I have witnessed and I strongly believe it was not made by man.
