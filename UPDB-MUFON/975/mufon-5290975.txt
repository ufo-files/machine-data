translucent flesh color, capsule shape, {no sound, traveling in a perfectly straight line}

My girlfriend and I were standing facing north, side by side, when we observed at the same time, to out right coming from a due easterly direction, to due west, a capsule shaped object, approx 1' dia. and close to 2-3' in length, translucent, flesh or lite pinkish in color, no sound, traveling around 30 mph, and length of travel approx. 400', about 20-30' from the ground, in a perfectly straight line, lasted only a minute from first glance to its disappearance past trees across the street from our property. We were both mesmerized as to what we had just seen, and trying to figure what it was that we saw. Definitely not a balloon, yet was similar in a pink color and being translucent.
