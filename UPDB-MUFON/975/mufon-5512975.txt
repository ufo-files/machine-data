(GS) hovered in the air, moved laterally and just disappeared

I was driving home from work and noticed a small object in the sky. I at first thought it was a bird but it was too metallic to be a bird and it was moving like a plane. It was moving laterally and seemed to be swaying to when it eventually just dropped out of the sky and disappeared. It honestly just did a turn about in the sky and vanished. I was just confused more than anything and was watching it the entire time as I was the passenger but no one else seen it but me.
