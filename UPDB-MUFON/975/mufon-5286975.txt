Synchronized flying never ending sparklers

Two friends and I were on our way to a casino and were driving up I-5 near the 172nd Arlington exit, when we saw two objects flying in tandem over Arlington Airfield. The objects looked like a pair of never ending sparklers. 2 split into 4, then two of that 4 broke off and flew in like a synchronized air show. Then they came together again and 4 went to 2 down to one. It seemed to rise in elevation as it started low enough to look like RC planes, but rose and could still be seen as we drove west on highway 20 to the Swinomish Casino. It completely disappeared when we were about 2 miles from the casino. I was driving so I couldn't record it myself. Neither of the other two witnesses recorded it either.
