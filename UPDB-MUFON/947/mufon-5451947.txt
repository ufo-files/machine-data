Experience meeting when I was 8 years old

We were traveling from Tehran to a village in Qazvin with some family members (including my parents and two older brothers) to attend a wedding party, but near the village on the side of the road there was an object in the shape of a half-upper egg from the middle. Divided into two parts, we saw a very beautiful orange color that had a fixed color and sat on the ground.
It should be noted that this object did not emit any sound and was completely silent, and we were completely stunned and just looked at it.
We wanted to get out of the car, but my older brother wouldn't let us.
After about a minute, we stared in astonishment at the object rising from the ground, rising to a height of about 30 feet, and then moving toward the stars at about 10 seconds, disappearing from the clutches.
It should be noted that according to all the families, I have a very strong memory and the event of that night is well recorded in my memory.
Also, once again, about 6 months before the above incident, I remember the history of observing 2 UFOs in the form of full eggs that had bright white and in the form of formations in the sky of Tehran.
