{dr}Bright Star like Object moving extremely fast and paused got brighter and took off out of sight

Jan. 20. 2013 at 12:41 PM I witnessed a bright Star like object moving super fast across the sky, it slowed down, paused for about 1 to 2 secs and got even brighter and bigger, then it took off at a rate of speed that I have never seen before, it seem to just disappear. It moved so fast toward the end of the sighting I am unsure where it went. 
There was no contrail, nor sound. It did not have wings, it was a good size orb/ball of light bigger than a star but not quite as big as a aspirin. I had a very good look at it thankfully, I was wearing sunglasses and I did not take my eyes off of it. 
The sky was clear, no clouds and sunny.

There were a few planes in the area just before and after this with contrails and sound. The planes moved slow in comparison to this object.
