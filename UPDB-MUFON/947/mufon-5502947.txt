{nd} Witnessed a sauser shaped peuter colored craft in the sky almost directly above me in the middle of the day hovering between clouds then after a few minutes flying off in a very fast rate of speed with no sound at all.

I was at a pool sitting back with sun glasses on when I noted a craft in the sky hovering between clouds. It appeared to have some sort of structure like windows but not exactly windows and was sauser shaped. It just hovered making no sounds between clouds moving very slowly at one point then flew off in a northern direction at a very high rate of speed also making no sound until no longer visible. I was shocked and profoundly affected...I never looked at the world the same again. Prior to that experience I had no belief in UFO's and still cannot understand what they may be. I am not certain what to think but I know what I saw was real and clearly a high tech flying vehicle.
