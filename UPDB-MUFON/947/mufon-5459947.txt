I saw a glowing orb just south of The Narrows Bridge? At first I thought it was a flare or some kind of fireworks because it was moving across the sky slowly and it looked like ia piece of it fell off of it, like an ember that was hot and then disappeared. It continued moving towards the South between Titlow Beach and the G Harbor airport, basically over the middle of the channel.

I began videotaping it (not a very stay hand) as I was repositioning myself from sitting on the tailgate of my truck to standing.

After maybe 30-40 seconds of videoing it and trying to figure out what it was, my phone rang.

I quickly answered it, chatted for just a short bit, and when I hung up a guy about 25 feet away said "did you see that?"

We started talking about what we saw, and he described it shooting off at a high rate of speed and disappearing.

I didn't see this as I was on the phone.

It had no aviation lights. It looked like it was on fire almost. Glowing. It definitely wasn't an airplane, but I don't know what it was.
