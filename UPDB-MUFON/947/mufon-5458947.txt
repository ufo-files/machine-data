{jpg} White Pulsating disc-shaped object mistaken for a plane until it stopped moving over Hampton Road's naval bases.

A blinking white object was flying across the sky and mimicked a plane. It was at a higher than normal altitude and didn't catch my full attention until it stopped in place. It looked like a star, but I could make out the sides of a disc every time that it pulsated. The UFO was moving around (in circles and back/forth) and seemed to be hovering over something. There was a lot of air traffic in the vicinity. The object would stop moving and pulsating every time a plane passed by, as if trying to go unnoticed. The UFO was at a noticeably higher altitude than any of the other air traffic. This happened over the Norfolk and Portsmouth area, seemingly perched over the naval bases. I witnessed it for about an hour before going inside. I did take a photograph but somehow I don't have the picture on my phone anymore.
