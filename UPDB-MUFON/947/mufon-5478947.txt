Hovering bright start light object objects over water then fly off and cycle repeated

My wife and myself have noticed strange star light objects hovering in the centre of port phillip bay on nearly every monday night for 6 months or so.We have observed these craft from the end of beacon point road clifton springs.This road ends at a cliff face at the waters edge of corio bay which is a bay of port phillip bay.The craft we have observed are always in the same spot and approx 200 mts above the water.The activity that we have observed is very strange and occurs in a cycle.Sometimes we see only 1 star like object then other times 2 or 3.The objects are seen north of the end of beacon point rd looking towards melbourne.Usually after about 5 minutes of the star like object hovering it then moves off in an easterly direction at approx 50 mph.When in motion the star like light then turns into red and green and white flashing around the craft.l have observed these lights through binoculars.All the sightings have happened of a night time so its difficult to give accurate details of size and shape.My guess is the craft l see are about 5 to 7 miles away.When a craft moves off another craft moves into the same posistion and again after 5 minutes or so it also flys off in an easterly direction and so forth.l drive down beacon point rd 4 nights a week usually after 9pm and have not noticed these lights on any other night always a monday night until this last week when l noticed them on a sunday night which was christmas day.This is what has prompted me to file this report if they are simply helicopters on training missions why would they be working on christmas day?If you follow this up lm very keen to hear of the outcome.
