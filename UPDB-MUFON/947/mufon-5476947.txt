{rjl} I saw a light in the sky with five small lights around it

I was in Northeast Ohio, I was walking around my backyard. 
It was bright, which called my attention to it. 
I just thought they were lights in the sky, it didn't look like a plane or a star or anything else I had seen in the sky before. 
There was one large, red-orange light, with five smaller lights of similar shape, and color surrounding it. The object appeared to have no apparent movement. 
The UFO did not affect how I felt, it did not affect the surroundings and I feel pretty much indifferent about it to this day. 
I went inside my house which is how I lost sight of the object.
