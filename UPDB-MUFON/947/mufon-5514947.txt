(GS) Large glowing oval with flames going straight up from bottom. No tail. Desending like plane landing. No sound

I was visiting a friend in Rancho Cordova during the week of July 10-13, sitting in his backyard drinking a cup of coffee & smoking a cigarette about 4 am, when I saw this large oval object glowing like the moon, with flames coming straight up from underneath it desending like a plane landing. It did not make any noise. It dissapeared behind some trees. I listened for an explosion but none came. This whole thing lasted about six (6) seconds or less. That thing has been burned in my brain every since. I have only told my wife of this before now.
