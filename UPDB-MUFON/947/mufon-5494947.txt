{nd} Saw two cigar shapped objects

At approximately 1130 am on April 7 , 2015, I saw a buzzard circling, hovering my parents house while I was on vacation visiting in MIllsboro, De. As I was watching the buzzard I noticed two cigar shaped objects fly over head well above the buzzard. Smaller cigar shapped object was in front of larger shaped cigar like object flying in a straight formation. I was trying to figure out what they were, because I never saw anything like them before. They were silvery white, with few hints of grey. They did not have wings and were silent ! Leaving no trail of any kind. They moved over head in about 15 seconds and they flew out of site. It was a clear bright day and no clouds near objects.
