Cylindrical white craft moving slowly. Stopping occasionally and continuing east to west towards lake MI

Walking ny daogs the started to get agitated.one seemed almost afraid for no reason. I watched as 2 seperate commercial airliners passed at around 8pm and 840pm both trailed a mist and I could hear the engine after they passed.. But at 8:20pm my dogs both acted freaked out. I happened to look up and a white cylinder was hovering motionless at about the height of the previous plane. After i stared for a second it began to drift or cruise slowly from east to west. I filmed it at this point. Zooming in as best I could. There was never any sound of an engine. It continued to move slowly(to slow for a plane) I watched for another minute after I stopped filming. I glanced down at my dogs who were calm by now. And when I looked back up only 2 seconds later the object was gone. It should not have been able to disappear from my view that quickly. It was Miles away from the horizon but disappeared all at once.
