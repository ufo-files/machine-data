during my 6:pm break at work I move my car from the back of our building to the southeast side parking lot.one of my work mates was looking skyward and told me to look at what he was seeing.there was an object rising in the sky that looked like a small red light.my first thought was that we were looking at an airplane with an engine fire.my friend said that we were seeing the third light in a row and that the light would rise and go out.which it did.right after the light went out we noticed that you could still see the object and that it was going to travel right past us.as it passed in front of our building it was about 1 mile away and aprox.5000 ft in altitude.There were no lights or sound.as the object passed around the corner of the building the object was siluetted against the clouds showing the full profile of a saucer shaped flying object.at this point we were discussing the reality of what we were seeing and we are all in agreement that we just saw a flying saucer.I don't know who this object belongs to or where it came from.but I know what I saw and need no further "proof".the light continued flying towards Portland and flew out of sight.This was a most profound experience!
