Ufo seen while cycling to work

I cycling to work daily, usually set of around 5.50am. One this day the 20th of December 2018 the moment I left my house I spotted this bright shit light in the distance. Although I thought it unusual I just carried on cycling, as I got to the road I work on around 6.11am I spotted this golf ball sized bright white light again, quite low in the sky. I snapped 3 photos of it.
