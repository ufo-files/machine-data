{jpg} Ufo spotted moving quick

While driving west on a county road in central Texas my brother and I spotted an object that fell from the sky it came across from left to right maybe 200 yards away, then in an instant took off in and almost straight upward descent, from the time it started upward to disappearing it took maybe a second and there was no noise it looked as if it was an afterburner or something close too it but was way to fast to be a normal jet. A couple months later or so my brother and his wife spotted the same light moving across the horizon at the same quick speed only a couple miles from where we first spotted the UFO.
