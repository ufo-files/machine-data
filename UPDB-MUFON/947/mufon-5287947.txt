UFO in ND

My daughter was letting the dogs out a little after midnight when she spotted an object in the sky that was much bigger than a star. She realized it was moving and grabbed her phone to take a picture. She was able to get three pictures before the object completely vanished. It was spotted in the NE sky and was moving in a SE direction. The object is clearly visible in 2 pictures, but just a faint light in the other. When I enhanced the faint light it shows a saucer-shaped object. I have included all pictures for reference.
