{cak} Appeared from nowhere chasing airplane thought it was a missle

My wife and I were outside smoking a cigarette. We watch the sky pretty much every night, a lot of air traffic where we live and clear skies. We were watching an airplane fly South West over our house. Luckily we were both watching the plane (we have had other sightings, but will not report them without both of us seeing and confirming a sighting) It was flying normal with what we call a normal flash pattern (its lights). Out of nowhere a bright white light appeared a medium distance behind / under the plane (can't really tell the distance from the ground). The object headed directly for the plane at super speed, I said to my wife, oh my god its a missile. The object didn't hit the plane but flew beside it and kept pace with the plane for 10ish seconds and sped far past it and just hovered in the sky. The plane turned super bright almost like it turned on every light it had and the object just vanished. Another odd note but most likely a coincidence, we saw a shooting star happen during this too, headed North East. I was genuinely afraid that a missile was going to take out that plane. We wanted to call the Burlington Vermont Airport and ask if they had seen something on radar, but we knew they would not tell us anything.
