{jpg} Woke up to very intense bright light outside my window at 2 am.

It started at 10 pm on the night of the 28gh of May 1991. My dog, a German shepherd kept growling and staring outside the deck and then would run down to the basement and kept making a low defensive, warning growl and then back upstairs again near the sliding door to the deck outside. I thought that maybe the raccoon family were back again but I didn't see them except the dark woodlands below. We loved on the edge of the forested Park that goes down to Puget Sound. I must have fallen asleep just after I checked out back but have no recollection of going to bed. I remembered waking up to a very intense bright light outside my window at 2 am. I remembered thinking why are the police out there and what are they after that they are shining their spotlight on my window. But I didn't hear sirens or radios or dogs barking or people talking. I peeked outside and next thing I knew I was laying down on a bed or table with 5 beings with very big dark eyes that reminded me of insect eyes and big head & slender bodies. Communicated thru my mind. No mouth. Tiny nose protrusion. 1 of them touched my forehead & the other 2 was checking my stomach while the other two observed. The one by my head seem to talk to me from my mind, told me they are just measuring me. A bluish beam above the bright light source scanned my body similar to a hospital scan. After that I woke up on my bed at dawn. I felt very lightheaded half the day. I'm including sketches of other incidents.
