ORANGE GLOWING STAR LIKE OBJECT HOVERING ABOVE TREE LINE, DIPPED DOWN, CAME BACK UP, THEN MOVED OFF

WE LIVE ON THE 8TH FLOOR OF A CONDO BUILDING.
MY BAY WINDOW FACES EAST/NORTH EAST.
AT SHORTLY AFTER 9PM LAST NIGHT I WAS SITTING WATCHING AN EPISODE OF PRISON BREAK WITH MY WIFE. I LOOKED OUT THE WINDOW AND THERE ABOVE A TREE A FEW BLOCKS AWAY WAS AN ORANGE GLOWING THING ABOVE THE TREE. IT DIDN'T MOVE AT FIRST. HOVERING AND PRETTY STATIONARY. ASKED MY WIFE TO LOOK AT IT AS IT SEEMED WEIRD. I ASKED HER, 'WELL, WHAT DO YOU SUPPOSE THAT IS'. IT WAS NOT A STAR. TO EARLY IN THE NIGHT AND IT MOVED UP AND DOWN. NOT A PLANET FOR THE SAME REASON. NOT AN AIRPLANE AS I WOULD KNOW ONE. NO BLINKING TAIL LIGHT AND IT COULD MOVE STRAIGHT UP AND DOWN. NOT A CHEMICAL EXPLOSION AS IT LASTED SOME TIME AND DIDN'T GET ANY MORE OR LESS BRIGHT. NOT A HOT AIR BALOON AS IT HAD NO SHAPE, AND I'VE SEEN A TON OF THEM AROUND LONDON. THOUGHT MAYBE IT WAS GOING UNDER THE TREE LINE DUE TO THE EARTH'S NATURAL ROTATION, BUT THEN IT CAME BACK UP OVER THE TREE AFTER IT HAD GONE DOWN.

I REALLY CAN NOT LOGICALLY EXPLAIN WHAT I SAW OTHER THAN THAT IS AN UNEXPLAINED FLYING OBJECT.
