Bright stationary object, also moving bright object. No airplane lights on either

I was looking West across the Hudson River and noticed a very bright stationary object. Sometimes pulsating but thought it was just my eyes causing it. I took pictures of it thinking it was Mars. Then North West of the stationary object another very bright object was moving rapidly up towards the clouds. I took two pictures of this moving object before it disappeared into the clouds. The stationary object was still there after the first object disappeared. I didn't pay attention to it for a few minutes and then clouds moved into its path obscuring it. After the clouds moved away maybe 7 mins later the bright object was gone. The stationary object was there for about 25 mins before it disappeared. I have pictures of both objects. Both very bright and unlike an aircraft. One picture I have after shows an aircraft but without the same brightness or intensity.
