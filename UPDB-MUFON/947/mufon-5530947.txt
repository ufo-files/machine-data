Pulsating orb moving in front of and behind clouds.

My wife and I were about to go to the store when my wife said "look how twinkly that star is!" I looked up and it was just a bright star but now we were watching the sky. My wife said, "look at that over there, that's a plane flying high right?" I said, "no planes have flashing lights." We then decided that it was still too bright outside to see a sattelite as we could only see Venus and one other star at the time. It was confirmed, in my mind, not to be a sattelite when it flew in an arcing type path silently pusating and moving in front of and behind clouds. We lost sight of it when below the tree line of the neighboring street.
