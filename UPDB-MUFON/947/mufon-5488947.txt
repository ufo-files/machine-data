{rjl} End of Pleides shower, two tiny glowing orbs glided one behind the other to NE position in 70 degrees approx. They stopped, paused, and one went upward at right angle. I went inside.

My husband and I were watching for meteors during last Perseid shower this year (August 12, 2015 around 1:30-2:30AM). We were reclining in lawn chairs on our deck looking up towards the north sky. A meteor had just zipped by and caught our eyes. I panned the sky waiting for another and noticed how bright the stars were in Arkansas compared to California where we moved from. I noticed two lighted orbs moving horizontally in the sky, like a satellite, I thought? I would compare the size of the object to the glowing on/off button on my computer. But they glowed deep orange, not yellow or white. Two separate orbs in a straight line heading East in the sky not changing altitude it seemed. Then they stopped under a cluster of stars, paused 3-5 seconds and the front one changed direction and moved up over the second orb. They paused, then the one on top began moving away from the other one in a straight up or northern direction. I went inside because I felt uncomfortable.
