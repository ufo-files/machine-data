Cigar paces jet

Looked out my office window, sunny clear blue sky, several jets flying overhead from a north to south trajectory. Very high in the sky, all leaving exhaust trail behind.

Notice a white dot by one jet, at first thought it was a spot on the window, but instantly realized it appeared to be moving. Then thought maybe it was a “floater” in my vision as I sometimes experience.

Kept watching and the dot was clearly a cylinder shape and appeared to me to be somewhat below the jet, and was keeping perfect pace with the jet. Again, both were very high in the sky. Higher than a small propeller plane would fly.

The object was leaving no exhaust trail whatsoever. I watch it for somewhere between about 30 seconds to 1 minute until the object and the jet fly overhead and out of my field of vision due to being indoors.

I took a picture of the object alongside the jet. I also attempted to video tape the object but apparently in my haste did not press the record button after I switched my camera to video.

I was able to expand the picture and get a decent shot of the object, however I still have an older model iPhone and the camera quality is probably not as good as it could have been with a more modern camera.
