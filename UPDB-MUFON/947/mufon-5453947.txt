I observed a very large Orb or Sphere in sky just above tree line,it was Red/Orange in colour

It was Saturday 11th April,Easter weekend.I was looking out of the kitchen window just before I was due to go to bed.I saw through the branches of a tree in my garden a bright red/orange sphere or Orb.I went upstairs to my bedroom to get a better view & could clearly see that this was a solid red/orange..but looking mostly red sphere.It looked pretty massive to me.I feel sure that this is linked to another sighting I had this week a couple of nights before during the Pink Supermoon.The object I saw that day was again a large orangey red sphere that came down from the sky very fast .(into a sweeping curve.I did submit a sighting report for this (report number 107584). I just think that I may have seen the same object on two different nights.On this occassion Saturday 11th April,the Red Sphere just hovered there in the sky,it didn`t appear to move at all.I watched it for 20 minutes,I wanted to try to get a photo of it but was afraid to stop watching incase it went away.In the end I decided to take my camera into the garden to get a better view.I ran downstairs with the camera into my back garden....but when I looked up it had gone.I could not see the sphere anywhere & I stood out there for about half an hour hoping it would return to get a photo.It did not return sadly & I have no idea how it disappeared or which direction it went in.This is a great shame,I am at a loss as to explain what it was & remain convinced that I saw the same object twice within a few days.
