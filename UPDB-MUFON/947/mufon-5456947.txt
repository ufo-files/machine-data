{rjl} I observed two silver morphing objects directly over the St Louis airport.

Friday, Nov. 22 at 13:15 local time. I was walking back to my hotel near the airport when I noticed a 747, leaving a large contrail and heading in an easterly direction. I used to fly the 747 as a Captain for a charter airline, so I know one when I see one. As I was watching, I observed two tiny ( to my eye ), silvery "dots" near the 747's contrail. It was impossible to gage the relative altitude of the objects to the contrail. The objects appeared stationary. I'd estimate about 1 - 2 miles apart. My orientation was south of the objects, looking north. Object 1 was west of object 2. It then became apparent the objects were moving very slowly towards the west. I initially thought I was seeing two silver "party balloons" drifting along. Not the case! Both objects appeared to be morphing in shape, reflecting the sun and never presenting a constant form. As I watched, object 2 began to move south and west of object 1 while object 1 remained stationary. This continued until object two was no longer visible. At this point in time, object 1 began "following" object 2! Both went out of sight to the west. Using my iPhone compass, I determined the winds aloft were from approximately 030 degrees, based on cloud upper level movement. The objects departed the area on a 290 degree course! They were NOT drifting with the wind!! Very exciting!
