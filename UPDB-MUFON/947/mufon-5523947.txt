Triangular shape, no sound, no lights, looked like stealth bomber

Last Night 9/19/2011 at around 9:50PM, I just happened to be sitting outside in a chair looking at the stars only for a moment, and then from the North (over my house) about 10 or so tannish/grayish colored triangular objects went from the North to south and out of my view. The objects had no lights but I could see them because they were lighter color wise than the background sky. I consider my an amateur astronomer, andI have a decent telescope, and I do look at the sky quite often, and I have never seen anything like this. After I calmed down I tried to make a mental note of what I saw. The objects looked quite similar to a Stealth bomber with triangular long wings, but had no lights but I could seem them very easily kind of like the ground clutter light made them to be seen???, also no sound at all before, during or after they passed and I waited and expected to hear something but heard nothing. They weren1t that high in the sky, so I was expecting some noise.

Anyway, I usually don't say or do anything when I may have seen something in the sky, but this time, I had an eery feeling about what I saw.

I just also saw that someone in Dallas saw similar objects last night posted on this website.
