{nd} an object hovering in Mount Dora Florida on Feb. 18th 2014

This is a video I shot (facing south) with a Galaxy S4 of an object hovering (probably over Lake Dora from my perspective). I was sitting on my front steps in Mount Dora Florida on Feb. 18th 2014 @ 10:22 p.m. E.S.T.. The object made no noise. It sat there for a few minutes then as I called my wife out to take a look slowly it moves off to the left of the screen (towards the end you will see it go behind a tree) still with no sound. I watched it continue southeast until it was out of sight.
