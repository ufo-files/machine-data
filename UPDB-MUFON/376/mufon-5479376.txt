On Tendal Air force base I [name removed/CMS/jpg] a PFC and my friend [name removed/CMS/jpg] PFC were sitting near an hut on the air mans beach having a beer while two air police were questioning why we were there, we saw the UFO zig zaging across the horizon coming towards us. The Air police radio called and said there was an alert that scrambled two delta f102 jets to intercept an reported sighting. the object flew toward us within 30 feet and 20 feet off the ground. All four of us remarked on the sighting. It hovered there for 3 minutes pulsating as the f102 came across the sky towards our location the craft then zig zaged at extreme speed back across the horizon as the jets slowly attempted to intercept the UFO, radio chatter confirmed the two pilots sighted it as well and was seen on radar. Once the event had ended the air police did give [name removed/CMS/jpg] and I article 15 tickets and we were on our way. Weeks after an air force investigator from washington came to the base and interviewed us and his conclusion was that it was swamp gas. I can not remember the exact date but it was between I think 1965 and 1966 and some time in the Fall season.
