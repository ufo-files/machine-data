Bright distant light streaking across sky then into space.

We were camping over on a friends property. We were sitting around a fire when my 10 year old son noticed a bright distant white light moving across the sky from our left to right. I don't know which way was north in that area. We all looked at it and then suddenly it turn upward at a bout a 60 degree angle and shot into space turning red just before disappearing.
