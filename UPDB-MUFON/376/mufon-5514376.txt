(GS) Flaming Balls

1.) I was outside my home, working on my car, getting ready to take it to a shop.

2.) Well first off, Im always looking up into the sky day or night to find strange objects. I walked out of my home and I saw something strange.

3.) a Fireball period. Ive sighted 3 objects total, 2 were flaming balls. The previous time I saw flaming balls, there were a pack of them. At first I was thinking it was a plane, but there was no sound (I live in a quiet neighborhood), no blinking landing lights, and no wingspan.

4.) Was flying low to the ground (trees were in my way), as I went into the street, I was able to view it clearly. It was going higher as it was moving. It was also hovering at times.

5.) Its a UFO of some sort, nothing much to it. You see it, take pictures, observe it, and move on until it disappears, & look for my next sighting. Its very interesting.

6.)It disappeared into the night sky. It went up and up and up.

I wish I had a camera to capture my first UFO sighting. just a quick little description of my first: (I followed it for a while) very low to ground and trees, oval shape, black windows, 4 interchanging round lights (from my view), exterior color was silver.
