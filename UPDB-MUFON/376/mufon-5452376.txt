Craft passing over head

When I was 15 I was talking to a shop across from my house. It was evening and getting dark I looked down the road to check for traffic it was clean and I seen nothing I crossed then then something caught my eye crossing over the road in the air. It was a craft a bit bigger than a car hovering / floating across the road I continued to walk closer but I got very scared and a car passed under it. It was about 15-20 feet from me when it passed in front of me over my head and into a path with trees. It disappeared and I never seen it again. It was grey and black with flashing lights around the sides and it had lots of curved surfaces. It made a humming sound kind of like the sound of electricity buzzing, but more windy kind of sound. It did not have any Emissions. It just floated across the road over in front of me and into a lane beside a graveyard and then it was gone I couldn’t see it anymore. I walked into the shop shaking and then ran home when I left as fast as I could. I did a drawing of it as soon as I got home, I’ve had dreams about it and other ufos since - almost my whole life and I’ve seen many of them.
