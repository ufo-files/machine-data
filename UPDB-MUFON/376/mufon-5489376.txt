Triangular object with 3 lit corners over Durham, Grey, Ont.

I was visiting my grandparents in Durham, Ontario and we were having a small campfire in the back yard with the neighbours. I live in Montreal and I mentioned how nice it was to see so many stars. Afterwards, I was arguing with my sister's boyfriend that if you looked long enough you could always find a satellite whizzing by in the sky. So I decided to scan the stars and find a satellite to prove it to him. After looking around for about 30 seconds, I saw what I thought at first was the fastest moving satellite I had ever seen coming from the East. I quickly noticed not one light but three white lights that appeared to be the tips of a dark triangular object. I say tips because it didin't look like the lights were coming from a beneath the craft, rather the tips themselves seemed to be lit up. It was travelling fairly fast and very high above me in a straight line from East to West. It moved it a very sleek way, I'm not sure how to describe it. Its surface was dark, and it might've been slightly transparent but I am not completely sure. Once I realized what I was seeing wasn't a satellite I said something like, "Oh my God it's a UFO! Look! Look!". Of course, none out of the 6 people that were there saw it. The whole thing only lasted about 5 seconds before it disappeared from sight behind nearby trees. I'm not sure what the object could be, but it sure gave me goosebumps!
