Saw big, black stationary object in sky. Watched, angle changed-object seemed paper thin so hard to see; vanish

Driving approx 40 mph in mid day I noticed a big, black, silent object in the sky . Too close to be a plane, too far to be a kite. Hard to watch as I was driving. Could see a full black surface, no lights, no windows. It did not move and made no noticeable sound. When first noticed it seemed to be rather a boomerang shape, then the angle changed and it seemed to be as thin as a piece of paper. After glancing at the street/traffic and looking back up it was gone. Could not see it close or far in any direction. No sound.

1. Driving south on Wadsworth Blvd toward Coal Mine Avenue.
 Littleton, CO

2. Glimpse of dark object in sky.

3. Didn't know, which prompted watching.

4. Black boomerang shape.

5. Intriqued & curious. Could not come to a conclusion as to what I'd seen.

6. It vanished. It did not "move", it vanished. (I did have to watch the road, when I looked back it was absolutely gone).
