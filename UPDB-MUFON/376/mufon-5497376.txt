{cak} While watching Sandpiper Beacon West Webcam I observed a ball of light dive straight down at a rapid speed and disappearing either into the water or over the horizon in the Gulf of Mexico

While watching the Sandpiper Beacon West Webcam from my home on 11-14-14 at Approximately 8:46pm CST I observed in approximately the middle of the (black/white) screen at a South West direction a white ball of light much larger than a star falling from the sky straight down at a rapid speed for only a few seconds out over the Gulf of Mexico and disappearing either into the water or over the horizon. I thought that it could be a meteor but did not really observe a trail.
