My family, mom, step-dad, cousin, my daughter, myself were coming home from having dinner around 8 pm in October 2002-2003. We live in the country, the driveway to our house is a mile long with woods on both sides of the road. It had been raining all afternoon. We were driving down the driveway,rain pouring down, poped over the little hill and seen over to the left right in the tree line a bright fireball bought the only way I can explain it. It was red,orage,yellow glowing,it looked to be about 1-2 foot of the ground hovering in one spot. We all saw it, we all were like what is that at first I said someone must have started a fire, but then who would do that considering it had been raining most the day heavy rain for a couple of hours and it was my property. I know I felt scared I had my cousin stay the night with me, there was no way I was staying home alone. The last thing of that night I remember is getting out of the truck running to my house locking the door. Everyone in the truck was confused at what we were seeing. The next day my step-dad and cousin went to check out the spot were we saw it, they said that they didn't see anything not even a burnt place if someone had started a fire . That's my story to this day I still am trying to figure out what we saw that night.
