Orange orb above Irish sea.

Driving west from Eskdale to Ravenglass,saw orange orb above sea,approx18.40hr.
Arrived at Ravenglass,went to beach,noted it was 18.53hr,saw same orange orb motionless in sky out above wind farm.
Noted clouds obscuring it occasionally.
Woman came to beach with her dog about 19.05hrs.She didn't notice it,I asked her if she had seen anything like it before,she looked and then saw it.She couldn't explain it,thought it maybe a planet.She left.
I noted it was19.07hrs and I was getting cold.
I thought there was occasional red tinge to it,it seemed to be fading?
I left beach at 19.25hrs approx,not waiting for it to go.
When I got into house,looked from bedroom window,it was not there.
