Orb of light interferes with security system

Last night at approximately 2:58am our security surveillance system captured on video what I can only describe as an orb of light outside my bedroom window. It set off our security alarm by somehow interfering with the magnetic window sensor on my locked window. The police showed up to investigate the alarm and the tampering trip on the alarm system was recorded with the company that monitors our home.
