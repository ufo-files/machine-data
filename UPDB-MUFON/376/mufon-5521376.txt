Cigar shaped craft

Me and my girlfriend were coming back from pigeon forge she was driving and I was lookin at the clouds then out of the corner of my eye I seen a reflection from the sun on what appeared to be a plane at first I told my girlfriend to look up and see what she thought it was she said she didn't know then she pointed out it had no wings and at the altitude it was at was much to small to be a plane I kept watching as it passed by us on interstate 26 it looked like a cigar shape it was moving at a higher speed than most planes I have seen when the sun shined off the object it looked silver it was almost to bright to focus on when the sun didn't hit the object it was a dull white color after looking at the object it got in behind a cloud we waited for it to exit from behind the cloud but we could not see it any longer
