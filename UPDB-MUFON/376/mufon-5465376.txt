{tjd} stationary over black mountain, very sharp edges, slightly domed, silver metal, no windows, after about 15 minutes, turned my head and it was gone.

I was walking home from school, around 10 years old, saw the saucer hovering above black mountain, east of salt lake city. It was about 3-3:30 p.m., It looked large for being several miles away, and I could see it quite clearly. It was a silver disc, slightly domed and had very sharp edges. There were no windows or lights, and just hovered with no movement or noise. When I got to the crossing guard, an older white haired man, I asked him what it was, and not noticing it yet he did like a triple take, as we walked across the street. He was quite agitated and said to me,"are you trying to tell me that's a flying saucer"! I kept an eye on it as I walked the rest of the way home, but after I walked by a tree that blocked me seeing it, it was gone. A neighbor kid told me later that he saw it too, but that four small saucers flew out of it. (which I didn't see). While I was watching it I got the impression that it was watching me too.
