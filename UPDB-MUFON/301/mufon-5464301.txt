{rjl} Saw five orange spheres (flickered like sky lantern) flying together due east below cloud ceiling for about three minutes until they disappeared

My 18 year old son and I were trying out a new fishing bait casting reel in backyard about 8:45 to 9:15 pm. As I was talking to him facing SE I saw a series of lights moving slightly to my right into my line of sight, I glanced for a moment and noticed five spherical objects flickering an orange glow like a flame would exactly like a sky lantern. I called it out and my son turned around and was as impressed and we talked about what they could be. We quickly discarded lanterns because of their size, direction of travel and speed. Lights flickered and would disappear and reappear but moving together in unison from our southern sky due east (SE sky). They traveled over a mile and maybe less than 3 miles away from us at about 25 degrees from our line of sight below cloud cover but high enough to lose them in the clouds. Three flickered off, two we lost in the cloud cover. Event lasted no more than 3 minutes. They moved independently in the same general direction not in any formation.
We continued what we where doing entertained by the event and concluded we saw something we could not explain.
Looked for and found other similar event in area.
