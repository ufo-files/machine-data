large sphere traveling very fast over head

I live in the casecade foot hills. I went outside to star gaze one clear night this last July 2011 and I noticed a huge translucent white sphere with white twisted shaped wings at the base that might be propulsion or maybe just friction......this was very high up, I could not tell how high.........I have seen the space station fly over my home before, but this was bigger and faster and held a steady course.........My first impression of this object was " this is no jet or plane " I was transfixed.........I tried to reason through what I seeing for the minute plus it took to leave my sight.................It is hard to judge things high up in the sky, and I am not one to jump to conclusions, but this was clearly something like I have never seen before.......size, speed and shape.............After it was out of view I got on the computer to find out where the space station was. It was on the other side of the world from my Pacific N.W. home.........I have tried to find any similar sightings on web pages...........no luck so far........What ever I saw, it was wonderful to witness.
