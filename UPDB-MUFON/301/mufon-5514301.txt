(GS) Saw 3 lights in the shape of a triangle hover very low around the tree line then disappear.

At 9:08pm on 11/14/12 I was at my back door letting my dog inside when I noticed something in the sky that appeared to be flying slowly from my right to left, just above the tree line. There were 3 lights that formed a triangle. It was dark so I couldnt tell if the lights were on an object or 3 independent objects. It stopped, hovered for about 2-3 minutes, then the lights just faded away. It appeared to be within a couple of blocks from my house, made no noise at all. There were 2 other people with me who also witnessed this. We attempted to record this event with our phone cameras but it can barely be seen.
