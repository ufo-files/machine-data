2 round neat cold white, blanking and synchronized, 30000 ft high, steady direction

I was watching the sky for meteor (plenty of them in mid August 2012). Suddenly I observed a moving object, quite far, more than 30000ft away. 2 white round lights, blinking regularly, 0.5 s on and 0.5 s off and synchronized. A cold white, strong, neat, with immediate on and off. The object was moving slowly, and slowing down I would say. It kept moving slowly in the same direction until becoming too far (very small) then disappeared behind the trees in my garden. 

During the observation, It took me few seconds to run and pick-up my binoculars, then I resumed the observation. The object seemed not obeying to usual dynamic behavior of flying objects. It is a non-trajectorial movement, even if it kept the same direction, as if it is not following the earth curvature.
It is not a plane, as there arent any green and red lights, with the strobe white flash. It is not a satellite, as they are not blinking, and the size of the object is much larger, closer to a plane. It is not the ISS, as I usually observe it, and the observation time did not match with ISS observation on my location.
