{jpg} morphing object that slowly hovered from north west to north then decended below tree lines

I was at work and it was raining, so I went to make sure my su roof was closed. I was talking to my co-worker and said hey check out the colors from the sunset. At that point he said whats that black dot. It took me about a minute to see what he was seeing. I said I had no clue, I pulled out my phone to see if I could zoom in on the object and took a video of this object. When I first seen the object zoomed in, I was confused because of the shape. It was stationary and didnt move then it began to hover while moving north and began to fall pretty fast. I questioned if it were balloons or a drone, but the movement of the object didnt seem possible.No sound was made from the object, it seemed to morph and change shape. It was exciting to see, it fell below the tree lines and I have no idea if it landed.
