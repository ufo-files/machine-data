what looks like a passenger plane flies normaly over the Saint John, haults mid-air, then disapears.

me and a friend were driving down Waasis road, approaching the Burton bridge when i noticed a plane flying over the Saint John river. there was nothing off about it until the plane seemed to stop mid-air above the river. just stationary as if frozen in time. it made no noise, emitted nothing. i'm not sure what kind of plane it was, but it had the markings of an Air Canada plane and its landing gear was deployed. after a few seconds stunned and trying to make sense of what was going on the plane vanished. the disappearance wasn't flashy, the thing was seemingly just erased from existence, it was there, then it wasn't. i didn't tell my friend about it until we stopped driving as not to distract him on an icy Canadian road. he said he did see a plane over the river but didn't notice it stop or vanish.
