{cak} Hubby and I were camping just outside of steamboat enjoying a fire. I looked up and saw a light moving across the sky from the South to the North without noise and blinking and I pointed it out to my hubby because I thought it was odd.

Steamboat campground camping and enjoying a fire. I looked up and noticed it because it was moving without any sound and was just a steady light the same size as a star. When I first saw it I thought it had to be a plane. The object traveled from south to north at the speed of a plane without changing direction and about the speed if a plane. I felt alarmed when I realized it wasn't blinking and had my husband look at it. I pointed out to my hubby that it wasn't blinking and he said that's weird. Then it disappeared into a cloud and was gone. We just said to each other how weird. The object went north until a cloud passed and blocked it and we didn't see it anymore.
