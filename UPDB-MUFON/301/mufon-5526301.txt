Looking at stars and one of them took off in an acending zig-zag pattern

I was just inducted into the Army. Was at Ft. Jackson It was about 2200 hr. We were standing outside waiting to be assigned our sleeping quarters for the night. I was looking up at the stars when all of a sudden one of them took off in what appeared to be an acending zig-zag pattern. First it went to the left then right and to the left and disapeared. The whole thing only lasted about 3 seconds. It has been 46 years and I can't forget the night. Have no idea what I saw that night. I would like to know if ther were any other reports from that time or of that nature. thank you Frank Sykes
