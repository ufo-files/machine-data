{nd} Glowing shape changing orb of light

Outside our house on 8/9/2015 about 6 am getting ready to leave for work. I noticed a bright light and thought it may have been Venus until it disappeared. We recorded it going from bright to dim and changing shapes from a bright circle to a diamond shape, from being far away to making itself bigger with a much clearer up closer view, it also was moving extremely fast all over the place but also with remaining in the same area. It also stayed in the sky until after sunrise. I recorded up until I had to start work. I haven't ever seen anything like it before.
