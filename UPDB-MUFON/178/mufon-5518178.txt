I was watching Fox with Greta Van Sustern when I decided to return outside and look around. I had been out earlier and didn't see anything unusual, about an hour before and stars werent out yet. I noticed what looked like a blinking star or possibly a plane as the lights were blinking red, green, yellow and over the course of an hour, became red, blue and yellow. No sound. It was in the southeast corner of the sky at first and hovered. We looked with binoculars, but no definitive outline and no distance change. We originally saw it at 10:35 p.m. and went back out at 11 p.m. and from our vantage point, it had moved west by two inches visually, then stopped and hovered again. It 11:30 p.m. it had moved barely to the west just behind a treetop, but the stars around it hadnt moved. While we were looking, 2 planes went by it. One above and one below. It couldnt be a new tower or planes wouldnt go below it and we wouldve seen it before as we have lived here for over 5 years. I had a "feeling" that it was watching ME although I had no evidence of that. At 11:35 p.m. as I was writing this, my husband said that while watching it, it moved suddenly "up" or straight north in our direction. I do not wish to describe here what I "saw" and "felt " in detail as I am too weirded out! Please let me know what you find out.
