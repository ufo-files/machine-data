{cak} Three round bright white lights grouped together moving slowly across the sky

As I was driving in to work the morning of the 12th of March 2014 at around 6am, I noticed three very bright round white lights grouped closely together moving very slowly across the sky from west to east. The light in the middle was a little higher that the other two lights. The sky was too dark to see the object itself. I noticed the object as if something called me to look in the direction of where it was. I lost sight of the object as I passed it at the same moment I drove under it.
