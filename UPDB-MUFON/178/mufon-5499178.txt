{rjl} Sprialing lights downward in Gilbert/Chandler

Driving South on Gilbert Rd, in Mesa. Around 7:50pm, saw 4 lights, 1 appearing at a time and flickering on/off. At one point they were all "on", looked like they were spiraling downwards, like a firework dud, but they would "turn off" and reappear eventually. They were are all falling, maybe following each other. Not planes. Between the time I first saw the lights while driving, I stopped by my parents and told them. Went outside and they were gone and didn't see them again (about 3 minutes). They seemed like fireworks that had gone up and kind of floated coming down but not exploding.
