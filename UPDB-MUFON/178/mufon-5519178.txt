I observed 3 or 4 orb like objects hover through the sky before taking off at blinding speed.

6.24.12

My girlfriend and I were in the drive thru at Burger King in Coon Rapids when we noticed some weird light in the sky. It looked like a plane that was on fire and flew across the sky and then stopped and then jetted off in a upward direction and when this happened 2 more orb like planes appeared one blueish and one golden and glowing. the 3 orbs then increased altitude at such a rapid pace that I knew they werent planes. Eventually they dissappeared.
