12/26/2014 8pm My husband, daughter and I where out side on the back patio star gazing toward the eastern sky. We noticed what looked like a very bright star or planet on the Eastern horizon. It was stationary and did not move. It was much larger, extremely larger than any other star. The bright pulsating light seemed to change shape as we watched it. First looked kind of star shaped and pulsating light, than looked like cigar shaped pulsating light, etc. We viewed it for about 30 minutes. Did not move. Attached are photos.
