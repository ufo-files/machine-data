Observed lights in the sky while walking dog

I was walking my dog and saw a line of 7 lights heading East/North East. I immediately tried to take out my phone to capture video but I dropped my dogs leash in the process. By the time I picked up the leash and pressed record on my phone, the first two lights faded and disappeared. I was able to record the remaining five lights until they disappeared in the same fashion. They were at a high altitude. No sound and it was a clear night with no clouds. I'm familiar with civilian and military aircraft. I'm a military brat and USAF veteran. My area is in the flight path of DIA and Centennial airport and Buckley AFB is directly to my east. I regularly see planes as well as police, medivac and military helicopters flying over my area day and night. I've never seen anything like this. Sighting lasted about 45 seconds.
