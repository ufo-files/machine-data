{nd} I observed 4 to 6 objects emitting a yellow-orange light traveling in a linear formation from the north to the south, the objects individually made a slight turn to the west and then their light faded and I could no longer observe them.

I was exiting my vehicle at the Publix supermarket when I noticed the objects. There were four to six yellow/orange lights traveling in a linear fashion from north to south. I assumed initially that they were some sort of military craft, but I did not hear any jet engines or helicopter rotors.  
I could not make out actual shapes of the objects. They emitted a yellow-orange light that appeared to encompass the entire object. after further observation, they did not appear to be conventional aircraft; they did not seem to have FAA red/green markers.
