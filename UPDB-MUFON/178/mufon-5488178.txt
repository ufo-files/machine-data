Took dog out and when I looked up there was a ship hovering over the house to the south east of the house it was there maybe a minute then it shot up into the sky and was gone.. with out a trace ... That was Easter Sunday morning at like 230 300 on April 12 Easter Sunday am I was out and came home at that time.. The next day Monday April 13 was watching the Show Extra at like 7 pm Monday Evening and There was the Craft I seen on Sunday Morning over my house .. Some one in Ft Meyers Fl. Had Filmed the shop and Extra had picked up the story on that ship with Footage of the Craft I witnessed on Sunday morning at 230 300 AM... I will never forget that Sight ..
