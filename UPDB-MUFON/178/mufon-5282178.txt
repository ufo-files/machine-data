Black Triangle, Cloaked, No lights, Low flying, fast object

Ufo sighting 
Monday March 14th 2022, 7:46-7:50ish pm

I was outside stargazing for about an hour in my front yard drinking tea with my dog, Jake, and I saw something triangular shaped with NO lights fly across the sky. No sound. It looked like a large aircraft but because it was in the sky, I’m not sure how big it was. It was going pretty fast like plane speed and about the elevation of a low flying plane. If I wasn’t already staring at the sky, it would have gone unnoticed. I’m pretty good at identifying planes and satellites because I’m an avid stargazer. Was it a drone? But without lights? An owl?? Almost like it had a cloaking device seen in sci fi movies. I’m a bit freaked out.
