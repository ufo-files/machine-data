{dr}Dripped, dripping

My brother-in-law,Frank Tobias, who is now deceased about 15 years, told me he saw a flying saucer. The only detail I remember is "it was dripping". I saw the Denise Murter story on TV again and her details of the sparkly stuff falling reminds me of what Frank told me. I have no other details, but I thought you should know about the "dripping".
