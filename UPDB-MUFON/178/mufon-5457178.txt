{cak} 1 Unidentified Flying Orb was observed over South Clermont, FL

The following events are from the night of 9 Nov to the morning of 10 Nov 2019. 1 unidentified flying orb was observed over South Clermont, coming from Lake Apopka to the direction of I-4 following a path similar to SR-429 South. Flight was NE to SE. First time I saw it on the 9 NOV 2018 at 1828 local EST. I immediately took a digital video and digital photos with my Samsung Galaxy S10+ in Night Mode ( Low Light). Follow the object until it went south. The height of it was to my view just about at low orbit and descending, I was also able to record it on my NEST video camera, at the same time. The following morning, I got up at 6 am and took at look in my back yard and I noticed it returning SE to NE. I was able to digitally filmed it, When the object appeared at 1828 9 Nov 2019, I notice a much as 10 different airplanes and possible drone, searching for what looks like this object. I believe it came from Lake Apopka and it returned the next morning, heading to Lake Apopka. I think its height will be at least 20,000 feet, since I notice a flight from LAS to MCO coming in after. It had a straight flight path with no change in height. I was able to get some closeup digital photo of it. I had made a FAA report recently, and have notice vehicle and airplane over this area.
