{cak} red, green, white, yellow flashing lights hovering over trees for 40 minutes or so

I was sitting outside of my house enjoying the evening around 8:30pm - I noticed something quickly flashing out of the side of my eye and I thought it was a planet. I thought it was strange that it was flashing red so quickly, especially on a clear night without any clouds in the area. The colors were flashing in a circle. 

I grabbed some binoculars to see it up close and there were red, green, white, and yellow lights flashing very quickly back and forth in a random sequence. It was small, which is why I thought it was a planet. I then thought it was a plane, but it stayed in one spot, hovering over the trees, for over 45 minutes not going anywhere at al nor getting any larger or smaller. I put my binoculars down thinking that maybe it was possibly a cell phone tower, but still skeptical. After 45 minutes, it dropped behind the trees. I couldn't find it. If it were a plane, I feel like it wouldn't have disappeared so quickly behind the trees, it would be a long process. I looked away for no longer than 10 seconds and it was gone.
