{dr}Over my nieghbors/Aunt/Uncles house Circling chimney stopping and starting several times

This was seen by me and my cousin as kids. We were playing in front of my Aunts/Uncless with my Grandmothers behind us. Our family lived next door to each other. We noticed a light ball, red orange clingfilm around their chimney & frightened us since never seeing anything like it. It appeared to be intelligent. It would go around the chimney one way, stop, go around the other direction. We knew it wasnt a fire spark since it was as big as a basketball or more, and it was summer time. I looked at my cousin and said my house is closer, do you want to come with me? He said no, Im running home, which was approximately 1000 yards away, while I was 50 yards away from my house. We said nothing about it again.
