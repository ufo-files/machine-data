{dr}Huge triangle craft seen hoovering about the tree line by S 435 & 45 hwy going towards the Kansas Speedway.

ON 10-02-2012 AT APPROXIMATELY 0010 HOURS MY GIRLFRIEND, MY FATHER AND I WERE DRIVING SOUTH ON INTERSTATE 435 APPROACHING 45 HIGHWAY HEADING TOWARDS THE KANSAS SPEEDWAY AREA IN KANSAS CITY KANSAS. WE NOTICED A HUGE OBJECT WITH VERY LARGE BRIGHT WHITE LIGHTS VERY LOW ABOVE THE TREELINE OFF TO THE EAST.

THE OBJECT WAS APPROXIMATLEY 300 FEET ABOVE THE TREELINE AND APROXIMATELY 1/4 MILE OFF THE HIGHWAY. WE FIRST THOUGHT IT WAS AND EXTREMELY LARGE PLANE FLYING VERY LOW. WE THEN NOTICED IT WAS NOT MOVING THE OBJECT WAS JUST HOOVERING IN PLACE NO MOVEMENT AT ALL.

I WAS DRIVING AND THERE WAS NO OTHER TRAFFIC ON THE SECTION OF HIGHWAY. I SLOWED THE VEHICLE DOWN TO ABOUT 40 MILES PER HOUR AND NOTICED IT WAS ABOUT TWO STORIES TALL, APPROXIMATELY 300-400 YARDS WIDE AND HAD 4 LARGE VERY BRIGHT VERY LARGE STATIONARY WHITE LIGHTS ON THE FRONT OF THE CRAFT.

WE COULD SEE THE OBJECT OUTLINE AGAINST THE LIGHTER NIGHT SKY. IT WAS A HUGE TRIANGLE SHAPE WITH SMALL RED AND WHITE LIGHTS BLINKING DOWN THE RIGHT SIDE OF THE OBJECT. THE OBJECT ALSO APPEARED TO HAVE A VERY LARGE WINDOW ON THE RIGHT SIDE AS HIGH AS THE CRAFT.

THE OBJECT WAS A GUN METAL GREY IN COLOR AND THE BOTTOM OF THE CRAFT REFLECTED LIGHT FROM A UNKNOWN GROUND SOURCE FOR A BREIF MOMENT SHOWING THE DETAIL OF THE TRIANGLE SHAPE AND THE HIGHT OF THE CRAFT. THE BOTTOM HAD A VERY SLIGHT DOME SHAPE TO IT, ALL THE EDGES WE COULD SEE WERE SMOOTH AND ROUNDED. THERE WERE NO SHARP EDGES TO THE OBJECT.

WE HAD TROUBLE BELIEVING WHAT WE WERE OBSERVING AT THIS TIME. WE WERE TRYING TO COME TO SOME RATIONAL EXPLANATION, THERE WAS NONE, IT WAS A UNKNOWN CRAFT THAT WE HAVE NEVER SEEN BEFORE.

WE DROVE PAST THE OBJECT OBSERVING IT FOR A FEW MINUTES AND LOST SIGHT OF IT DUE TO THE TREELINE AND THE CRUVE IN THE ROAD. I DROVE TO THE NEXT EXIT ON SOUTH 435 AND HEADED BACK NORTH ON 435 RETURNING TO THE LAST PLACE WE OBSERVED THE OBJECT. THERE WERE NO SIGNS OF THE CRAFT IT JUST DISAPPEARED. IT ONLY TOOK US A COUPLE MINUTES TO TURN AROUND TO RETURN TO THE LOCATION WE OBSERVED THE CRAFT.

WE THEN EXITED OFF 435 TO HEAD BACK SOUTH AND WE PARKED ON THE OVERPASS TRYING TO RELOCATE THE OBJECT. IT WAS GONE. WE DID OBSERVE A AIRCRAFT FLYING OVER THE AREA AND IT WAS PRETTY HIGH IN THE SKY AND TRAVELING EAST. 

WE ALL SAT IN AMAZEMENT TRYING TO COME TO A UNDERSTAND IN WHAT WE SAW. IT WAS NOTHING NONE OF US HAVE VERY SEEN. MY DAD SERVED 20 YEARS IN THE MILITARY AND I AM IN LAW ENFORCEMENT IN THE KANSAS CITY, MO AREA. WE DONT KNOW WHAT IT IS OR WHO MADE IT BUT IT WAS EXTREMELY LARGE AND JUST HOOVERING THERE LIKE IT COULD CARE LESS IF ANYONE OR ANYTHING COULD SEE IT.

IT IS SOMETHING WE WILL NEVER FORGET.
