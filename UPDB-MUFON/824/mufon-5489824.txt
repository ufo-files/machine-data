{jpg} Observed disc shaped object that was really large with 5 red lights across it. It hovered for a moment then tilted and moved across the sky. 7 jets then followed within 15 to 20 minutes of sighting.

My husband and I were outside looking up in the sky while we were sitting on the porch. We live in Cullman county, Alabama. We saw a huge disc like object with 5 red lights on it. I yelled for my husband to run get his dad so he could see it too. It hovered for just a moment then started going across the sky. The lights looked as if they circled around it. We watched it for 4 to 5 minutes until we couldn't see it anymore. We were amazed at what we just saw! It tilted in an instant as we watched. It went at what seemed to be a slow speed. Within the next 20 minutes, we watched and heard 7 jets fly in the same direction. I tried to video it but Im not sure if it showed on it. The video may have to be enhanced...I don't have good video on my phone at night. The object had no sound whatsoever.
