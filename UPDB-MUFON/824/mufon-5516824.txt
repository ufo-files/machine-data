{dr}Yellow-orange orb passed silently overhead

I was returning from Buffalo, NY on Rt 219 at Genessee road exit. I pulled over on the on ramp to check my tires. I noticed a fairly bright yellow-orange light passing almost directly over head. I took a few seconfs observing it and then reached for my cell phone to take a picture.
Immediately as I started to take the picture the light went out.
It was fairly bright in the direction the orb was heading as the lights of Springville, NY lit up the sky. I could still see the object as it sped away, I would guess at 80 mph, because it was moving some what faster than traffic. The object then appeared as a dark object with maybe some short wings. There are no airports in the immediate direction the orb was heading.
This is the eight UFO I have witnessed over my life time.
