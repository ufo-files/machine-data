Light visibly flew in space followed by tail

I was outside smoking a cigarette when a zig zagging light caught my eye. When I processed what I was seeing I yelled for my girlfriend to come outside fast. The light then flew in a straight line and looked like a plane. Until ultimately flying away. But when it flew away, it didn’t fly out of view, it flew deeper into space leaving a tail. My girlfriend is a scientist and I’m prior military and neither of us to this day can explain it.
