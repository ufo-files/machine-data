Bright light near Phila airport.

On the eve of Sat 07/17/10 I went outside to have a last cig before going to sleep. I went out approx 12:30 - 12:45 am (on Sun 07/18). As I was smoking, and listening to the ambient noise I looked up to notice that the night was pretty overcast. The stars in the night sky were very muted. Upon looking east, I saw 1 light that was very bright, unlike the rest of the sky. As I was looking at this light I realized that it seemed very low. It was very far away, but I would guess it's position to be very near PHL airport. In my novice opinion, I would say the altitude was approx 5000ft. I watched it for approx 45 minutes to 1 hour, and went to bed just past 1:30am. The light remained motionless the entire time (although occasionally seemed to move side to side, but I attribute that to my tiredness & 47yo eyes). There was no sound at all. Curiously I only saw one plane during the whole time, which was heading north at very high altitude. Even at high altitude, I could still faintly hear the plane, and even made out the blinking light a few times. I watched the light for 45 minutes or so, hoping I would see some movement but never did. I went to bed troubled because it's brightness in the sky seemed so out of place. I have been checking the sky all night tonight, and although very overcast, it is now 24 hrs later, and there is nothing in that position at all.
