Viewed an unidentified flying object that flashed once, then again, then disappeared

My friends and I were sitting out on their front porch, watching the stars. They live in the mountains so there is always a good view. Initially when I spotted the object, I thought of it as a satellite, and pointed it out to my friends. But we then realized that it was travelling much too fast to be anything of that nature. It was white in color and moving quickly. It emitted a first flash and picked up speed, then after a few seconds another flash, and had disappeared completely from sight.
