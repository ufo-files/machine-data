Extraordinary sitting

With my wife we live in the foothills of the Andes mountains in the North of Chile. WE have been here for 11 years. The place is considered a hot spot for UFO sightings. It is common to see lights in the sky. This sighting was extraordinary.
I have an employee who helps us with the land. About a month ago he told me that he had seen two strings of "stars" cross the sky at about 6:00 a.m. The fist string flying from NE to SE with about forty set of lights in a straight line. The second string was brighter and over three hundred sets of lights.
Today I received a call from him at 6:30 a.m. telling me that the "stars" were flying overhead, to go out and watch them. His home is around three hundred yards from mine as a crow fly.
My wife and I went out and saw a formation of lights, three abreast, in a perfect line separated by about 9" from the front and rear set of lights traveling from N to S.
Our total time of observation was 3 minutes. I asked him how long had he taken to notify us? He stated around 2 minutes.
The lights were dim, the size of a pin head. They were flying very high. The speed was steady, not very fast nor very slow.
The reason I'm writing to you is that we had never heard of witnesses of such a formation. Be well, M & P.
