{nd} 300to400ft domino rectangular stationary object. Illuminated a kind of light not man made. Beyond brilliant.

Driving north
Just about dusk cloudy misty day . Object was so obvious in the cloud deck so pronounced brilliant light very over whelming site

I thought wow what is that? Stared at it and was puzzled it didn't move and its shape was so strange like unnormal to be just sitting still in the clouds and shaped like a monolith or domino frame
