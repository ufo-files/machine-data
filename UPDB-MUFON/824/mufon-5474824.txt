Basically, I went out with my father to take night time star pictures. We got there and took a few pictures with me as the foreground and we attempted to get the stars and clouds in the background. We took two pictures, one after the other in a certain spot. They both seemed not too different to me so i never noticed it. Over a year later, i look back and notice that there's nothing occurring in the first picture, but in he second there's an obvious glowing object n the top right corner. I don't believe its a star trail because it does not exist in the photo taken seconds before. It's very confusing to me and i tried to think of reasons why it wouldn't be in the first picture. But i couldn't think of anything.
