Was the brightest thing in the sky, was pulsating continuously red to blue. Took a few short videos. Watched it for about a half hour.

I noticed the light as it came up over the neigbors house. If it wasnt so small I wouldve mistaken it for the moon. We watched it for a few minutes before getting our phones, took a couple of short videos. I took 2 photographs and was astounded that it was bright enough to be caught in the still frames and the video. We saw it pulsating from red to blue continuously almost like it was flickering, and it translates into the videos. I took screen shots of the video and zoomed in, and they look wild. Ive never seen anything like this, and it is definitly not a plane or anything else ive seen in the sky. I honestly thought it might be a supernova, but it kept moving. More than just spin drift from time passing, and it didnt have a consistant flight path. It would go up and sideways then stop. Then go up and sideways then stop. The colors that surround what ever this might be look reminiscent of the northern lights, like the green and turquoise you would get from magnetic feilds being interacted heavily with UV radiation. After watching it go on like this until shortly after 5, we stopped watching it not sure if what we were seeing was actually what we were seeing.
