Spartan trail camera video of unknown lights approx. 10 miles south of Robin's AFB, GA. The camera was in a lock box, bolted to a tree for hunting purposes. By looking at the information bar at the bottom of the video the letter E is observed indicating a manual triggering as opposed to a motion triggered event. The object appears to be behind a small patch of trees were the area had a deforestation last year. I thought at first the lights were caused by a reflection of the infared camera lights, but the tech who reviewed the video said "a reflection off what? And why didn't all of the rows of LEDs show up?". There was no fog reported that night or rain. Judging by the occasional leaf falling, there was no wind to shake the tree so violently. The tech explained that only manually triggering by holding the camera was possible, not remotely as this camera is equipped with a Verizon transmitter. As mentioned, it was in a locked, steel box. If it was just an electro-magneticlly triggered event, and the lights are just an apperirion, please let me know. Thanks for your help in this matter.
