{dr}thought I was looking at a star, object started to move then disappeared

I looked out my first floor bathroom window and was looking at the stars. I noticed this object (that I originally thought was a star) start moving west to east. Then in a matter of seconds, it was gone. This was a totally unabstructed view. I have seen similar events before, I see what looks like an airplane with its landing lights on, (The Buffalo airport is only about 60 miles away.) Usually flying south to north, these objects appear to get dimmer, but if observed carefully, they are getting higher. They get so high, they can't be seen. There is no flashing, just a solid white light.
