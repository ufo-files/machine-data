Object similar to satellite moved across the sky while changing direction, finally rapid acceleration into space.

While watching the sky towards the east of I noticed an object similar to a regular satellite moving across the sky over the western part of the Baltic Sea. The object appeared to be at a very high altitude. (+70000 ft) and travelled from the east to the west. After it moved for approx. 10 seconds ift abruptly changed direction towards the north. It continued for approx.10 more seconds and then again abruptly changed direction towards the west. After travelling for 10 or so more seconds it changed direction one more time towards the north west. After traveling 5-10 more seconds it abruptly stopped. It stayed stationary for 10-20 seconds and then accelerated rapidly away out into space and disappeared. When it disappeared it was almost right above me slightly towards the north-east. The object travelled at a constant speed during the entire time I observed it until it stopped .

This object moved as if it was under intelligent control and moved nothing like any satellite I have witnessed before. It also did not move like any aircraft that I have ever seen. The change in direction was instant and abrupt and the final acceleration into space was almost instant. I have never witnessed anything like it before or after.

I am a former commercial helicopter pilot and instructor, former staff sergeant in the Swedish Armed Forces and is currently working as a software programmer.
