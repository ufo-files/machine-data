{rjl} Fire ball in sky that morphed in front of us into a diamond shaped craft... flying towards New Orleans, but made a u-turn and crossed over our vehicel

We were riding on la-46 near bayou road going east in saint benard parish heading towards shell beach. We saw in the eastern sky what looked to be a bright fire ball... it wasn’t falling like a meteorite. It was flying a path. Heading west towards New Orleans. Immediately took my phone out and recorded the video attached. Once I zoomed in, I could clearly see the objected revolving, spinning, and morphing. It looked similar to a molecule or some time of cell or atom expanding. If you watch the video slowed down and zoomed in you can also clearly see the object spinning, expanding/contracting and morphing. Once we started getting excited, we pulled over. At this time the orbs light dimmed, and my friend tried taking a photo and her flash was on... seemingly drawing its attention... it flew passed us and made a turn in our direction and flew over our vehicle. At this point I could clearly see the craft and it had “turned into” a diamond shaped craft with four lights on each point. All were orange except one, a red blinking light on the left “point”... after it flew over us it continued west towards the city. We didn’t see it again.
