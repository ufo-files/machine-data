I only bring this to you, because no one else believes me,, 
On a summers night when I was 12yrs old, me and 3 of my friends that lived on the same side of the block that I did,, we were playing basketball one night, and we stopped because of a weird hum or buz sound, Jeff was the the first to look up and notice that the stars where disappearing, I looked up, and seen what looked like a cigar shaped black out right above us, it stopped and hovered above us for a about 3 minutes, and then multiple colored lights came on, and then it slowly moved on in a southerly direction, I don't know if we were frozen by fear, amazement, or by the what we were looking,, but we ran to to my buddy Al Sargent's home, and called the police,, when we went back outside,, it was hovering what looked to be a few blocks to the east of our block, at that time it appeared to be a couple football fields long, and 6 stories from top to bottom,, when we reported it's position to the local police,,, there was a flash,, not a bright blinding flash, but a flash, and then in minutes it appeared in the northern sky, it looked like it was hovering above Phillips Jr.High school,,,
I have tried to forget about it, but I can't
