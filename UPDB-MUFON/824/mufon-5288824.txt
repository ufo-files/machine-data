Supersonic UFOs with four green lights on bottom, traveling in a wavelength pattern

While traveling home from a friend’s house, on June 6 2019 @ around midnight, I witnessed very large white lights about 300ft. off of the ground, covering and shining down on a well-known freeway.

Suspecting these observed lights were too high off the ground to be construction lights/toad work. I got out my iPhone XS camera to begin taking video.

After witnessing the large aura of lights and beginning to film, I remember feeling really uneasy being I was, at this time, the only vehicle driving eastbound away from the observed lights over freeway and was thinking the whole I was being watched. During this 25 to 30 minute commute back to my home I observed very low-hovering, unusual and out of place lights in the sky.

FOR ALL READERS: You must watch the attached videos in real-time, as well as screenshots I took while playing back the videos. 

Upon reaching my exit off of the freeway, I regained some of my wits and courage to continue filming, as what I was witnessing in the sky was still ominously hanging around and not leaving the area. Furthermore, getting off at my exit I observed two large but relatively far, bright orbs off in the distance I was trying to fixate my video camera on. Although I was driving roughly 30 to 40 mph with the drivers window completely down, the next video I shot captured four very small, greenly illuminated UFOs, each with four perfectly symmetrical lights on the bottom, traveling in an up and down wavelength type motion, flying closely and in same direction of my position, just above the height of the seen power lines in the video.

No faster than I was able to capture these fast and elusive flying UFOs on my phone, they jetted off and in a blink of an eye seemed to vanish into the night. But not before I gathered some very compelling video evidence of my incident/event that night!
