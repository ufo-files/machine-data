Large Floating Green Orb

When I was like 11 years old, a friend and I saw a UFO in the woods behind his house. We were watching MTV in the dark when I spotted a moving light in the dark woods out the window to the left of the TV. Shocked I told my friend what I was seeing. He thought I was was trying to scare him. We had no clue what it could be because we had never seen anything like it. We kept yelling at each other "what is it!"

It was a large glowing green orb that came horizontally out from the wooded hill behind his house. We were watching it out his basement window from his couch at first. We were scared but went to the window for a closer look. It looked like it was coming directly toward us. Then it dropped down vertically just below the wood line and we could still see the glow make it's way back up the hill until we couldn't see it. 

The orb like floated/hovered it moved in strait lines but appeared to weave a bit once below the wood line. I believe it was around 10-15 feet in height and width. It glowed and gave off some light surrounding it, but it never had a tail of light but never moved super fast or rapidly.

Then we ran up the stairs to tell his parents but they thought we were over tired cause it was around 1am. We both struggled sleeping after that and kept our eyes out the window. 

We had a couple other strange occurrences out there too.

Whenever I think about it these days I always look things up online and constantly check out my windows. It is comforting to know that we weren't crazy and other people have seen the same thing. 

I saw a suggestion to contact you guys on a comment of one of the youtube videos I just saw. 

There were no houses or roads behind the house at the time... since it was 20+ years ago.
