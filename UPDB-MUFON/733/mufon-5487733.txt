{jpg} Was taking orb photos and camera caught this...7 photos of it as it moved away

I had decided to take photos every night for a year in order to record orbs. One night the camera caught this yellow light that remained here long enough for seven photos. I did not see it with my eyes, but only on the camera screen later that night. Since it seems to me to have edges to it and a ring around the middle, I felt it might be a disk. I have hundreds of orb and what I call spirit cloud photos, but this seems different.
