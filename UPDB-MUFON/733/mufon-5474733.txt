{dr}two star like objects emitted from a glowing orb

Two star like objects appeared near the dipper end of the big dipper on August 2, 2017 at approximately 11 p.m. Shortly after the two star like objects appeared, a hazy, white/grey orb illumination appeared in the sky, the nite sky was clear, the object was approximately as big as the moon in the night sky and about half as bright. The two star like objects appeared to come from this white/grey, glowing orb that lasted in the sky for approximately a half hour. The star like objects moved slowly at first, ascending and descending, then ascending again in a zig zag pattern, then picking up speed on their final ascent until they disappeared. Bright flashes of lighting appeared in the sky, white at first and then turning bright pink. The flashes of lightning started in the north sky/horizon, going on in the back ground below the star like objects........the lightning then moved more to the northwest and turned a bright shade of pink as soon as the objects disappeared, no known reported storms in the area, the sky was clear.
