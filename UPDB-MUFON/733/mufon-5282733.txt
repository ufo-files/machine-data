Gold Craft witnessed Close Encounter

The craft was matte-gold metallic color and roughly 30-40 feet in diameter (I thought about the size of a bus). It had a window about 8-12 feet across and maybe 3-4 feet wide in the front and it hovered silently just above the treetop in my neighbor's backyard while we were out in my backyard. The window was directly facing us when Mike pointed it out and we all just stood there and watched it as it watched us. It was about 40 yards away and maybe 10 feet above the oak trees. It then rose up in a zig zag until it reached about 100+ feet high, it then stopped and shot straight up and was gone in an instant. The craft was smooth except for something that looked like conduits beneath the front of the craft just below the window and mostly the port side. There were no markings, rivets, screws or anything. The window was reflecting the sun and I could not make out anyone inside.
We never spoke about his until recently either, so it is like I just found out this is real. It wasn't until my 20s that I remember saying anything about it to my then wife and many years later (2020) to get in touch with my childhood friend through FB. He recalls it vividly and says he thinks about it all the time.
