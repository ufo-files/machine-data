They were shiny and silvery. The skinny point was forward. They moved from North to South over the school. Then they turned East, formed a spiral, then a line where they all pointed East. Then I talked to my teacher and he said that I was right about how they couldn1t be birds. Then I said they couldn1t be planes either because they were too small.
