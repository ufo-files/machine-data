Bright star like object

I was just on the phone standing outside at approx 1930 Monday Aug 19, and I looked up into the night sky and noticed a bright star that I thought was Venus. Proceeded to scan the sky and then cam back to the Venus like star as it began to move. As it moved the object began to speed up and dim until it disappeared. This lasted approx 15 to 20 seconds.
