Disc flying over Japan's nuclear site during earthquake

While watching on tv Nuclear nightmare: Japan in Crisis on Direct tv,channel 286 at about 3 minutes into the program I thought I saw a flying disc. A white male was talking and video taping during the earthquake. He showed a crack in one of the reactors and then turned his camera to the sky when something flew across. The camera was not steady because of the quake and I just got a glimpse. I recorded this show and I could stop the tape but the object was blurred. It did appear to be disc shape and it did fly over a reactor during an earthquake.
