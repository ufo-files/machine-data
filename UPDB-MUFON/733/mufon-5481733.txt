{dr}Webcam picture of square object during a lightning storm

I have lived in the far North of the Phoenix area for the last year. I do live behind a wash so I have webcams in the backyard for security purposes. The webcams will go off especially in a lightning storm. The mountain behind me has a lot of lightning during storms. Lightning will seem to hit in the same place and silent at times. It's a good show. When there is a moving object the webcam will take pictures and send to my email a series of six pictures. This picture showed up on my email on July 31, 2016 at 8 pm. There seems to be a square object at the end of the lightning. I thought I would send in a report and submit the picture. I posted this on social media but it was suspiciously removed after awhile.
