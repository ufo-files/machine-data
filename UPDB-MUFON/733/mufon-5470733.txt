{jpg} Black Triangle moving slowly and silently just above treetops before dusk.

Driving home just before dusk in Enfield, CT. We approached a stop sign and noticed a black equilateral triangle just above the tree tops ahead of us moving in our direction. The craft moved silently (windows were down) and appeared to be moving far too slowly for a conventional aircraft to remain airborne. The object was viewed through the front windshield as it moved directly above our car in a South West to North Easterly direction. Recollecting this experience, I'm not sure why my partner or myself did not exit the vehicle for a better look. Noticing traffic stopped behind us, we continued on our drive home; losing sight of the object as it passed over our car. The observation lasted approximately 20 seconds. This is the amount of time the object was visible between the tree line ahead of us and the top of the front wind shield. Also I have a memory of a white light facing downwards from each of the 3 corners but this memory is very fuzzy and I can't be sure if the object had lights or not. There were at least 2 other cars stopped at behind ours, I would assume they also noticed the object as it was very low.
