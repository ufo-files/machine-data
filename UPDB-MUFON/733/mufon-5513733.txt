(GS) Hunting whitetail see cigar like object above tree line

Well, I was hunting whitetail and something bothered them pretty bad when I heard the sound it was about 500-1000 feet away and approaching quite slowly,(The sound was like a buzzing type but then again not a buzzing sound.. hard to describe) But the white tail I was hunting ran off into the woods when they heard it. I sat there watching it and it lasted about 4 minutes or so, It was flashing all kinds of different lights and when it was going over I seen the tail flames like what a jet would give off but they were pinkish purple. Really weird.. 

It made a 70 degree turn like following a track and then turned back on to the direction it was heading in the first place. It was not a jet. It was way to close to the ground like 500 feet or less and it wasnt making that much noise... 

I didn't have a camera or anything with me or I would have had some REALLY good footage of it.. I could see it really clearly and it was NOT a jet..
