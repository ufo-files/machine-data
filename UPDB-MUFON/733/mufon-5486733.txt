{nd} a short bright glowing stationary rod only observable in binoculars near venus

I was observing the planet Venus to search for comet catalina nearby with powerfull 25x100 binoculars on a tripod. Just to the left (North ) of Venus I thought was the green comet named catalina. But I was really seeing a short, bright motionless,hovering glowing rod or cylinder with a diameter 1/2 the diameter of Venus when looking through 25x100 binoculars. It stayed in the same place in the clear predawn sky . It never moved. It seemed to be in the same place that comet catalina would be-but I never saw the comet-just the glowing cylinder, through binoculars.I felt puzzled that : could this be comet Catalina? There was no cometary tail. All the took place in the backyard of my apartment building (the north side lawn of my apartment building). I quit observing the object around 5:55 AM EST to begin my daily chores of the day . I plan to go out on Monday Dec 7, 2015 at 5:00 AM EST to see if the cylinder or rod is still there next to the planet venus.
