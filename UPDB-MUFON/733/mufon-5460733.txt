{rjl} I think I saw UFO. It was unlike any aircraft I've ever seen.

I was standing outside with my friend. We were talking and I always look at the stars. But I noticed a lot of airplanes. One right after another. On what seemed like the same flight path. We saw 4 of them. And then another craft coming from the same direction as the airplanes came passing over us but this one looked different. It was circular, like a disc. It had several different colored lights. Red, blue, green and white lights. Which seemed to be rotating around it or blinking all the way around. Including the sides and rear. We began to panic. My knees started shaking and I felt like I could not breathe. I felt extreme excitement but I also felt very scared. Suddenly I had tears coming because I was feeling so many strong emotions at once. An hr later I am still very shaken. The planes were bearing left, or north. But this strange craft banked right, or south west very sharply. It made no noise and it was moving much faster than the airplanes before it. It was moving so fast that every picture I tried to take was blurry.
