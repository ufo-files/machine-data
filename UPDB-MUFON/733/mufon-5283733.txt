Observed solid light traveling from 312 degrees heading in a se direction , no sound.

At first glance I looked up and saw a solid light pulsating and at first was traveling in a northern direction. I ran inside my house to get my phone and when I came back outside I observed it change direction and began to head from 312 degrees to my direction which was southeast of the object . I continued to observed and record it as at some point it appeared to hover in place then continued in a se direction. I heard no sound and only then observed just a solid bright light. It continued se as it went out of site. Note: I pulled up flight tracker radar and saw no airplane in my area at the time of the sighting. Other note: At 7:34 pm about a hour after observing the object I heard a helicopter come from the same direction as when I first observed the ball of light. The helicopter was traveling in the same path as the object I observed. I pulled up flight tracker and the helicopter did not show up on the radar which I then believed it might be military. The helicopter traveled in the same path as the object but returned a few minutes later heading back in a nw direction.
