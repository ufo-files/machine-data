Was stationed at Eglin AFB, 3201 Security Police Squadron. Was working midnight shift and about 4 of us were outside our vehicles bs'ing. We were where they had Air Force 1 parked, it was at Eglin for testing. We saw this massive green beam shoot across the sky, shortly after that we saw another. I recall someone saying it probably came from Underbrush.
