Look like a flying lobster claw

I was sitting in my living room watching tv with my kids and I looked out the window.
The object was shiny and shaped like a lobster claw with some square attachments. I thought it was a drone at first then I noticed the shape and it rotated one half flip and start to elevate and move South east toward Ancaster. I attempted to take some pictures and video, sadly I was focusing on a speck of dirt but you can see the object in the right side of foootage one third up from the bottom.
