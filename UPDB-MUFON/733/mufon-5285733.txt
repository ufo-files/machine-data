I think we saw a proto-type for the TR-3. flying very low across the sky in a rural area..

We were both active duty USAF, station at RAF Chicksands, England. While relaxing in my friend's backyard, at his off base house, we hear a humming noise. Then, the vehicle overhead blocked the sun and cast us into a shadow, causing us to look up. For the few minutes, we watched the flat black, triangular craft slowly cross the sky above us. Neither of us seemed too concerned since we both were aware of the SR-71 flights in the sky above us. We figured this was just another "secrete" US vehicle. Only, there was not sign of an engine or thruster. 
I have read in Dr. Steven M. Greer's book: "Unacknowledged" that ( page 50-53) Dr. B (possibly Dr. Fred Bell?) testified he was involved in the construction of an anti-gravity triangular craft at RAF Bentwaters, England around that time period. Which is Not far from RAF Chicksands.
