Looking at stars saw this one move too quick for anything man made and hover then change direction sharply.

I was having a cigarette with my wife and looking up at the stars when I saw one that was moving way to fast for anything man made. It moved quickly then stopped abruptly for 30 seconds or so, as though it was looking for something then sped of in a north easterly direction from stationary to stupidly fast instantly. When it left the area it was like a stripe in the sky. I mentioned it to my wife but she wasn't looking up at the time. The while thing took less than two minutes. I didn't have my phone so couldn't film it or take a picture.
