Bright Lights in Southern Sky

I was driving into Waupaca on CTH E traveling southbound from the Ogdensburg area when I noticed a bright light in the sky to South and a little West. The sky was clear with only bright stars visible to the south and more visible to the north. I pulled over to the shoulder and as I was watching the light, another formed next to it, only lasting for a few seconds. Both lights disappeared for approx 5 seconds, reappeared for approx 10 seconds and again disappeared.I did not see them return. The light was a bright orange/yellow color, steady and not moving. I,as well as others in the area, have see similar lights to the South in the past, with as many as a dozen lined up in formation just above the horizon.
