huge building like structure hovering above the river thames

i was returning home driving across waterloo bridge..2.so big you couldnt miss it...3 where are we if this can happen to mindblown to think really/mirage of skyscraper...4 it was a circular object emitting a blueish light with odd writing/symbols and large red swastika and lots of windows filled with humanoid figures watching us that seemed to be standing in fire. it came from outside our reality ie extradimensional...5 shock/ all laws of physics were broken before my eyes..the weeks after my mind trying to convince itself it could not have happened...this was rush hour in central london hundreds were stood with me watching yet in 33 yrs i have found only one person that was there and will talk about it but she is convinced of another date ..6 it slipped back into a direction that dont exist if that makes any sense
