{nd} Square shape with glowing underside traveling NE over Savage about 20 mph. No noise at all and about 200 feet off ground

We had just returned from dinner on the 4th of July and I was letting the dog out in the driveway when I noticed a square object flying NE toward Minneapolis. It was 100-200 feet up from me and traveling 20 mph or so. The underside appeared to be glowing as it moved above the very quiet neighborhood. I did not hear any noise at all as it moved. The sun had set, but it was still light out and you could see blue in the sky... The entire event lasted about 5 minutes. When our dog finished its business, I walked in and told my significant other about it. He ran, took the car and attempted to follow it but to no avail...
