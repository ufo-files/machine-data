{rjl} 7 Bright Red / Yellow lights moving slowly and then slowly dissappeared

My family and I were outside watching neighborhood fireworks on New Years. We noticed 6-7 bright red lights to the north. They were moving very slowly. The lights slowly disappeared as if moving away. More lights continued to appear as others disappeared (4-5 lights). This continued for 3-4 minutes.
