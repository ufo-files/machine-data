{dr}I thought it was a falling star, then it stopped, then shot east to west right over me ,so bright

I was out cycling at 830 pm on April 3, 2013. I was riding east when I saw a "falling star", so I stopped to look at it. When I stopped, the object was a couple hundred feet from the earths surface. It then shot west flying right over me. It was the brightest white light I have ever seen. It was silent. It had a trail behind it which resembled glitter. The shape I could barely see was round. I thought to myself, a "falling star" does not go East to West. I was extremely calm, the object disappeared in the West. I knew I had seen something special. Never have seen anything like this before. Very glad I saw it!!
