{rjl} apparent fireball leaving trail moving slower than meteor, abrupt change of direction

I was driving east on Cason Trail in Murfreesboro at 5:59 or 6 PM central time. As I approached the intersection of Cason Lane, a light in the sky to the east/northeast caught my attention. It appeared to be at least a few miles from me. At first I assumed it was a plane but quickly changed my assumption to a meteor, as it began to appear to be a white to yellow streak, but then the streak seemed to resolve to a trail with what appeared to be a orange fireball heading east at the head of it. Of course it would still appear to be a meteor, but it seemed to be moving too slow and burning too long, as I observed it in this state for nearly 20 seconds. I was further confounded when it took a sudden turn toward the south, still leaving a trail. I only saw it for another 3 or 4 seconds before I lost it behind some houses and trees. I hurriedly crossed the street and attempted to relocate it but did not. After dwelling on it for a few minutes, I began to have a horrible feeling that it may have been an airplane that had somehow caught fire. However, over the last few hours I have checked local news sites for news of a plane emergency or crash and have found nothing. I decided to report it here, hoping someone else may have seen it or even filmed it. I did not attempt to photograph it because I was in the car driving and figured my phone wouldn't get a good pic or video anyway.
