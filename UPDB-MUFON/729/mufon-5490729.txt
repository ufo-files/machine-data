{jpg} A very odd looking light appeared, made a swift and intentional movement, and disappeared.

I was sitting on my roof with some friends on a nice summer night and my eye for some reason was drawn to this patch of night sky almost completely behind me. I just turned my head and stared there. In a split second a beautiful light appeared and at first I thought it was a plane flying too low (but the lights were too bright so it couldn't be) appear suddenly fairly low, make a weird downward and intentional descent shape in the air, and disappear. It left a trail of light behind it as it moved. I couldn't breathe for a moment, had a delayed reaction and started yelling in panic. My friend Sean told me he has had the same experience, he feels randomly inclined to look somewhere as if someone is calling him there, and then he sees something. He told me about this website.
