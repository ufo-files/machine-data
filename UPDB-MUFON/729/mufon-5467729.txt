Observed triangular or chevron shaped object slowly traverse across the sky at high altitude.

Whilst in the back garden I was tracking a passenger plane moving across the sky. I noticed a strong light reflection from an object that appeared to be just below the planes jet stream and this immediately caught my attention. I called out to my son who was in the house to bring the binoculars so we could get a better view.

Through the binoculars a triangular or chevron shaped object could be seen which every 10 seconds or so reflected the sunlight. The object moved quite slowly (much slower than the observed passenger jets) across the sky in a southerly direction.

We watched the object for around 15 minutes in total and as it progressed further across the sky it appeared to have multiple orange/yellow lights which may have been pulsating. During this time two passenger jets were also observed in the area of the object.

After 15 minutes or so the object disappeared from view due to its
distance from us.
