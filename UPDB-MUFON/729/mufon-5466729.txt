{jpg} I saw this object while I was waiting for my wife and decided to get some pics of the old Methodist church.

I was waiting for my wife and I decided to get some pics of the old Methodist church. I did not see the object until I was using the video camera on my phone, which is an Lg K7. When I looked at the phone at the object it was hovering and appeared to be spinning at about the height of the churches roof. It then moved to the left a few feet to the left, and made a very fast circular motion. It dipped down below my line of sight. It then came back up still spinning I think thats when I moved to get in a better position to record what I was seeing, and that's when I lost sight of the object. The wind was blowing pretty hard that day and my first thought was that I was looking at a floating black plastic bag. And It may have been just that, I leave it up to you to decide.  I felt very excited and amazed at what I was seeing at the time of the sighting and after.
