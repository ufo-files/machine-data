{td} Glowing orange flat light moving towards me and became more circular and brighter as it dropped in elevation briefly stopping overhead before accelerating to south and disappearing extremely fast.

Had been out by barn sitting around a small fire watching the sky as I frequently do and had just gone back in house for a few minutes. When started walking back towards the barn noticed a bright orange light coming over treetops about 500 m to nw. Grew larger as it came closer until it was almost directly over me...hovered above a few seconds before shooting off to south and out of sight. While above, maybe 200 ft or so...appeared reddish-orange and circular but with 3 slightly brighter spots around edges...overall a round glowing object but with 3 brighter spots that would seem to have made it triangular, although too luminescent to distinguish a triangular shape.
