Bright light spinning counter clock wise

Noticed this bright light which seemed to be spinning counter clock wise slowly. When looking at it with the naked eye we were able to see white, red, green lights on it. It stayed in one spot over a mountain then disappears. No clouds were in sky at the time of disappearing. 
The full video I have seems to show little white lights going towards this large bright light.
When enlarging this video at the end when it disappears I noticed a white little light towards the right side along with shadows and a little flashing on the right side of the sky right after it disappears.
