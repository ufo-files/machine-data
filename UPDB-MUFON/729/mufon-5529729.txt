Ball of fire at the bottom of a cylinder shaped, slow moving and silent

A friend and I were at my house, watching TV. My friend noticed the bright object through my living room window, "what the heck is that?" he said. We ran outside to check it out! It was just to the west overhead, i got out my phone to video it after about 30 seconds of trying to figure out what it was. It seemed to be 500 ft above us, but could have been further. It had no sound, moved slowly. I thought it may have been a hot air balloon, but the red/orange ball of fire seemed to have no flickering flames. I noticed that it had a cylinder shape above the glowing ball, like a reflection of the ball of fire. the ball of fire looked like the surface of the sun to me. I do not have a clue to what it was. I took video till it was lost through the trees. It was traveling to the southeast, which is odd if it was a hot air balloon, cause usually our winds travel to the northeast?
