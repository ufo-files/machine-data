{cak} The object hovered over my barn for a few minutes it was super bright then slowly took off.

I was at home when this happened in Columbia Mo. I was on Facebook and seen some post that people were seeing some strange lights in the sky in Ste. Genevieve MO, a lot of people were saying the same thing in the comments. So I decided I'd go take a look myself to see if I could see anything. I live out in the country with no so close neighbors. When I first went outside I didn't see anything. I went out my back door so I decided I go to my front yard to see if there was anything still nothing. But my dog started barking and went running, I then turned around to go back to my backyard and then that's when I seen an object hovering over my barn, the barn from me was about 100 yards so the light the object was giving off was super bright. It hoovered enough for me to go run inside to get my Nikon coolpix camera. When I went back outside it was still hovering over my barn. The time was around 11pm. I was able to start recording the object as soon as it started to take off slowly. I live pretty close to the Missouri River so the direction it was heading was towards the river. Where I live I see all kinds of weird stuff.
