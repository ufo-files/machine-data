Very Bright White Light

I just saw the oddest thing, but it gets weirder. I was out back, I had just gotten home from work went outside to smoke when I saw this very very bright white light off in the distance, I thought maybe it was a plane coming right towards me to give it the bright light effect or maybe a helicopter but after watching it for 10 minutes it wasn't getting any closer. I opened the door to yell into my parents to come look at this. By the time I turned back around it was gone, nowhere to be seen. My mother then goes on to tell me what about 2 hours ago she was in the driveway and saw a bright white light. She went into the house to go to the porch area to get a better look at it and by the time she got out back the light was gone. She had not discussed this with me before I saw the light I saw. Very odd.
