I was on my way home as a police Officer. I walked along our street and saw a star in the sky that looked different than the rest. He was also brighter than the other and it was not Venus. The object was about 1 minute motionless in the sky, about 48 degrees above the horizon. It had the right and left two beams. The center looked around. Suddenly it was about 1/3 bigger and brighter and then disappeared. There was only a black background.
