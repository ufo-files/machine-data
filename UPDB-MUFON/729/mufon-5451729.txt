Blunt cylinder, silver in color, approximate size 300 feet circumfertsence

I was attending summer camp in Great Barrington, Massachusetts. On August 14, 1970, I glanced at the sky and saw a strange object. At first, I thought the blunted cylinder was a satellite or a rocket booster re-entering Earth's . However, it's erratic behavior was not similar to either. It sat silently at a 45 degree angle. It appeared to be about 1-2 miles away. Its surface was smooth without any rivets ir seams. As I observed the craft, it suddenly made a very sharp right angle turn, almost 90 degrees. This occurred rapidly, in 1-2 seconds. Then it hovered motionless again. After about 15 minutes, a bright emerald green flame shot from the rear portion of the cylinder and the craft moved off to the northeast at a high rate of speed, disappearing. It was entirely silent the entire time I observed it.
