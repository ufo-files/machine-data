{rjl} Witnessed unfamiliar craft all white, triangular in shape traveling in a straight line

It was a sunny day with large white clouds. They were moving from the southwest to northeast. I noticed what I thought was a small cloud heading in the opposite direction. It came from behind a cloud and followed a straight path to the southwest. As I looked at it I knew it wasn't a cloud because it was triangular in shape. It looked like a fat paper airplane. I waited this long to report it because I wanted to see if I could be something simple. But I have no explanation. It was not a plane because the sun was out and a plane even all white will be darker on the bottom or sides. This object had no shadowing effect almost if it was white and translucent . It also made no noise or left a vapor trail. It was like nothing I have ever seen. It was not traveling very fast and stayed in a straight line. I have tried to find something to compare it to but I haven't seen anything like it.
