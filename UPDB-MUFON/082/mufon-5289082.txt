One brighter orb with maybe 6-8 in front of it followed by possibly up to 100 behind it

Their flight path & formation was pretty much straight, but was not in an exactly perfect line, and each orb kind of did it's own thing, bobbing & weaving slightly but none of them overtook the others or strayed too close or too far away from each other in that same basically straight line formation as they went over. Was hard to estimate altitude and speed, but it did not appear like they were going really fast. Estimated time they were in my view was probably not over five minutes long. I can see the stars, planets, planes & choppers pretty clearly most of the time here, and this was nothing like that. Sometimes you can see planes leaving Buffalo with their landing lights still on, but that was not the case either, and coming from wrong direction. It was a really neat experience, and at first I wondered who would ever believe me, but then I thought that I don't really give a $hit who believes me or not... I saw what I saw and it wasn't a damned weather balloon or some other crap people come up with! Two years prior to that, my lady friend & I saw two single white orbs come down at an angle like shooting stars, but much slower and they left no trails, maybe 5 minutes apart, followed by a huge brilliant flash of light that I missed because I went back inside the house! Those looked to be only about one mile away, and there was no sound with the flash either. She said it lit everything up like daylight for just an instant.
