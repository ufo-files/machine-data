{jpg} Two flashing lights that hovered/bounced in the sky.

I have seen these lights twice, once about a week and a half ago, and once just tonight. 

Tonight, I was walking my dog and looking at the stars. I saw two flashing lights in the distance, and, upon first seeing them, thought that they were airplanes or helicopters. As I looked more closely, however, I saw that they were bouncing up and down and sort of shaking in a way that no helicopter or airplane could fly. The would move up, down, and side to side. They sort of drifted across the sky, moving very slowly, with one moving NE to SE, and one moving NE to W. I felt normal while viewing it, but I was confused about what they were. I called my family outside to see. My mom suggested that they were perhaps small drones in the distance? Small drones could move in that way, but I am not sure. I did not lose sight of the objects, I spent about 30 minutes looking at them and then went back inside because I was really cold.
