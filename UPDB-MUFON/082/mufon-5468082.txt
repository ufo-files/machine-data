A solid orange orb. Steady altitude against wind convinced me not a balloon.

1. I was at home, just browsing on the computer.

2. I noticed the object upon looking up through the skylight above my computer desk.

3. My first instinct was a balloon, given it's shape. I know/knew nothing really about UFOs generally. I certainly did not think they came in any other shape than 'saucer.'

4. It was quite large and colourful for a 'balloon' so I moved to my bedroom, with windows viewing SE/S/SW to continue watching. It seemed very odd. It had no gondola or suspended equipment. No netting or texture or ropes/strings. No irregularity in shape or extremities whatsoever. 

It did not waver at all in its direction the way helium and wind would lift and waver a balloon. It's movement was so linear and steady it may as well've been on railroad tracks.

5. I did not know what to make of this orb. It was vanishing from view quite quickly. The distance from my home to the ocean - this orb had to be travelling at ~80-90km/hr ground speed for it to reach the shore in the time it did. I went back to my computer and posted in the local subreddit for my town, asking if anyone was witnessing this thing. The response was essentially 'I'll have what he's smoking.' I can attest that I was sober. There would be far more doubt in my mind otherwise.

6. I lost sight of the object as I returned to my desk.

I chalked this up to... well just something weird. Quite by accident I came across some of the ufology stuff ~ 1 year later (present-day.) It was there that I read that 'orb/sphere' is a common ufo shape. I've now read that it is, apparently, the most common shape in this region as well.

And well, you know what they say. 'When you rule out the probable, and only the improbable remains...'
