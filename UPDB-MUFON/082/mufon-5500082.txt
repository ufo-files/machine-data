{rjl} 3 lights. Looked like stars but were in a triangle pattern and moving at the same spped and distance from one another.

I was star gazing and watching for meteors outside of Pocono Raceway. I regularly star gaze. I usually see what I believe are satellites moving through the sky. They look like slow moving stars. On this night, I had seen a few meteors and there were regular aircraft coming out of Avoca airport and flying over from West to East at probably around 3000-5000 feet. Around 10:30pm, I saw what I thought was another satellite. I then noticed two other objects of the same size (star size) and color. They were in a triangle formation. I couldn't tell if it was three seperate objects or if they were the lights on one single object. They moved at the same speed and direction and the distance between them never changed while I saw them. I looked at them through my binoculars but it didn't help. I did not notice whether or not I could see stars between the objects so I don't know if it was one solid object or three seperate ones. I only saw them for about two minutes. My video camera will be at my side from now on when I am star gazing in this area.
