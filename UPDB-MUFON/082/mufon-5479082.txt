{cak} Black Knight orbit

The evening of the event I was sitting outside on out patio reading on my iPad. I have always been sensitive to non-terrestrial events due to multiple direct contact events. I felt compelled to look up toward the sky and saw the object. It was completely black, was not an aircraft, there were no lights, no reflections, no sounds. I realized that the object was actually in orbit, as I tracked it I called my husband and son, they both watched it and we concluded it was almost "bird" shaped. It's speed was constant, and I tracked it until I could no longer see it. I then started researching similar type observations and was lead to the Black Knight satellite, the shape matched exactly what we saw.
