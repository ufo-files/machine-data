{cak} 6 of them 5 from directly over head one level flight coming from the NE

I was in the hot tub looking at the stars as I have been doing for years & noticed a point of light like a shooting star. It stayed white till I became afraid it was getting too low to the ground & I was scared it was going to crash . IT never di but it made a change of directions just above ground level & headed in the direction of the 1/4 moon that was low on the horizon. Then another point of light appeared in the same area the first did. It came in as white but changed to a bright Orange color & floated down. I ran to get my wife out of bed to look at it. it approximately 1 minute intervals 4 more showed up for a total of 5 sightings when my wife said oh my God look at that. Another one was coming from the NE I think going in line with the other 5 towards the direction of the low moon on the horizon. It appeared to have some kind of flames coming off the top of it. Not a tail or anything . The whole thing took about 10 minutes to take place. They all flew in the same direction until they went out of site.
