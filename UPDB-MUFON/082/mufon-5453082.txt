Row of lights heading north

While out looking for the comet atlas we saw 10 lights in a row below where we were looking for the comet. They all stayed fixed in a row and disappeared into the north. We used our app for the comet to see if they were satellites and they were not. The lights were all spaced out the same moving north, in a uniformed line. Never seen anything like it.
