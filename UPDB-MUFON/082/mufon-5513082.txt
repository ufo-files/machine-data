(GS) Transparent object/s in vicinity of Great Lakes Naval Training station.

My first child was born earlier in the day and I needed to take my mother in-law home, since I was staying at the hospital overnight. I let her in my house, grabbed my dog, and tied on his leash. I took him out to the front yard and started looking into the sky, thanking the lord for the birth of my child. For some reason my eyes caught a glimpse of what appeared to be a transparent object in the sky. However, when I focused in on the moving object/s I notices three orange lights or orbs in a triangular formation. I was unable to tell whether they were separate or one larger object. At first I thought it was the clouds moving, but when I saw the lights (orange objects) I realized it was not a normal aircraft nor was it a helicopter or meteor shower. It was too smooth and fluent of a motion and direction in flight for that. I was confused and stunned at the same time. I went to pull out my phone to take a picture and when I had it ready I lost the objects from pulling my eyes away. After the incident I went inside and told myself that I was not crazy. I waited until I calmed down and drove back out to the hospital.
