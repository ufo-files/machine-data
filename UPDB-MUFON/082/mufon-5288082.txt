20-50 lights appearing in line then ascending

The lights were seen at approximately 10:50 pm central time. The lights appeared at about 60 deg. up at the NNW. They would appear heading in a straight flight path staggers and appeared to ascend. The lights of the unidentifiable object would disappear from visual to our north. There were over 20 but less than fifty and were not able to be captured on film. The cameras would pick up the stars but not these objects. Shortly after they stopped a aircraft was seen taking off from Fargo Airport and Clearly different in size and had an identifiable strobing hazard/landing light.
