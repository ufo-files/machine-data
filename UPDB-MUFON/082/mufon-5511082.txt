{cak} Over flight of UFO scanning Cayuga Lake.

We were camping at my friends camp site on Cayuga Lake NY. It was turning to evening and we were sitting on the edge of a dock. The fog mist was coming on the lake for the evening and then we saw what looked like a blimp in the distance across the lake. We just sat and watched the object thought nothing of it. Slowly without a sound and not disturbing the lake mist it approached toward us. Nothing to to scarce or worry us until it was above out heads about 300 feet. It then stopped. It was a very large circular disk and had a dome feature underneath. It was directly above us while we were sitting on the dock. Three lights came from the bottom dome in red, green and blue. The light felt very hot so we panicd and ran. I ran into the woods and my friend ran under his familys camper. It seems like there was a time lose in that it was later than what it should be. My friend doesnt recall anything later when I questioned him about that event years later. 
The craft was silent until it left us, the sound became pulsacting when it left.
