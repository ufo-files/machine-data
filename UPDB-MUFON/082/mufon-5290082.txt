Me and three other people witnessed a dog-fight like event between 30 plus ships of various sizes

Me and 3 other persons witnessed a -fight like event between various sized ships in the sky on Jan. 18, 2021. They were all over the sky - some straight up as you look in the sky, some a little to the east, some a little to the western sky near and around Orion's . There were larger ships that would move more in a path, and smaller ships that looked like they were firing at the larger ships or at each other. The smaller ships would sometimes in formation, then split off and some would do circles around the larger ships, or zip in and around the sky. One ship was shot and began to fall like a fireball in the sky. My phone camera couldn't really capture anything -- it was happening so far away and so fast, and they just look like stars bouncing around and firing at each other. I looked everywhere this morning on the internet to try and find news about it and could not find a thing. I can't believe this hasn't become international news! It was incredible!
