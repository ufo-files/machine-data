Two bright slowly pulsating lights seemed to hover just below the clouds. we noticed this while star gazing, one star seemed odd, then a cloud passed over and the star turned out to be below the clouds! We kept track of the object for about half an hour, I tried to take photographs but couldn't capture anything that provided much detail unfortunately. The object grew continually dimmer over the next half hour or so, finally it faded into nothing.
