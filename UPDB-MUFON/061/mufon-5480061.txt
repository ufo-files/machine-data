I was a passenger traveling south of Riverside on Highway 218 before sunrise. What caught my eye was what I thought was a plane traveling low from west to east. It had a consistent white pulsating light that went from dim to bright. It then appeared to turn north and follow the road. As we got closer we could see it was right over the shoulder of the oncoming lane and was not moving. Fortunately the truck had a sunroof and I was able to look up at it. The center had a round white pulsating light and square lights that remained dim on what appeared to be the ends of the object. I could not see the square lights until we went underneath it. The sky was dark and I could not see an outline for a definite shape but the lights made it appear rectangular. Just the outline of the lights were quite large. After the shock of what I saw I looked back and the object had not moved. What stays with me is the size and how low it was.
