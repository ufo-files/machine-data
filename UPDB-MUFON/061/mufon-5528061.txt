DARK BOOMERANG SHAPED OBJECT

I'm just looking for advice because perhaps what we photographed was not an UFO. 
Last Sunday January 23, we were visiting a property in the suburbs of the city of Cali, Colombia where I live. We are planning to start a subdivision project there. I was with a professional photographer, taking photos for a brochure of our project. 
During the session (on top of a mountain), we didn1t see anything out of the ordinary.
Last Monday January 24, he sent me a CD with the 130 photos taken that Sunday. I took a look at all of them using my Macbook pro 17" and didn't noticed anything strange. Only yesterday morning, when I was showing the pics to my brother, but this time on a 42" LCD TV set, photo #80 showed a dark spot between two clouds against the blue sky. First, we thought that it was a bird, but something about its shape and size made me run to my PC and make a zoom over the dark spot. 
Well, it seems to be an object for sure, not a bird. Perhaps a small aircraft, one of those ultra-light ones? I don't know, because its "boomerang" shape is kind of weird. 
But what really called my attention last night was that I remembered that the photographer was shooting rapidly between pics. I don't know a thing about photography, but photo #79 and photo #81 are almost identical to photo #80 (you can tell by the clouds that changes only very slightly). The object only appears in photo #80 but it is not there in 79 and 81. 
I just called the photographer and ask him if he can get from his camera the timing between the 3 pics.
To my surprise, these are the times, taken from his Canon Camera ( no tripod):
Photo 79: 11:04:33 AM (Fnumber:9 ; Exposure Time: 1/400).
Photo 80: 11:04:45 AM (Fnumber:9 ; Exposure Time: 1/250).
Photo 81: 11: 04:50 AM (Fnumber:9 ; Exposure Time: 1/250).
The camera is a Canon EOS 40D. Sizes of the original photos are: 5MB, 7,2MB and 7,9MB.
The object is nowhere to be seen in Photo 79, appears 12 seconds later in Photo 80 and 5 seconds later disappears in Photo 81!!. Given that these photos were taken using Wide Angle Lenses, it seems to have vanished or hide behind some clouds perhaps? Thanks for your attention, sorry for my bad English.

I guess that we were lucky to take the photos at the precise time and with a great photo resolution that is allowing us to see the shape.
Thanks and sorry if all of this is wasting your time.
