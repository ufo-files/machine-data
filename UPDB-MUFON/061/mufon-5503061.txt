{kt}I was a 16 year old kid, huge triangle hovered over me and my friends.

I was just a teenage kid and we were outside, we planned to sleep on my friends trampoline. We were looking at the stars when this thing floated over us. Huge,it blacked out the stars as it passed. Triangle with a white light on each tip, and it was heading west. Very low hum, almost silent. I started to panic when I saw it. Was visible for a very long time, disappeared as it floated to the other side of town. It's been brought up in conversation over the years and I know several people from the area that have seen the same thing.
