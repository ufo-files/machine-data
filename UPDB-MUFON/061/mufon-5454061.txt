Flying machine ressembling Zepelin without gondola and whit small horizontal rear stabilizer

The noise heard was like a hiss of air, i saw it pass over a small white cloud which are around 10 km sea level. The estimed size of the flying machine was greater than anyting i know of our time.
I saw it pass directly over me. I was picking blueberries, a little north of Chute-Des-Passes in the province of Québec, Canada. It had no engine noise and no jet of gas ejecting.

Je l'ai vu passer directement au-dessus de moi. Je ramassais des myrtilles, un peu au nord de la Chute-Des-Passes dans la province de Québec, au Canada. Il n'y avait aucun bruit de moteur et aucun jet de gaz qui s'éjectait.
