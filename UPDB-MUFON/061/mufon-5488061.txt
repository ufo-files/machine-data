UFO hovering in southeastern side of WPG,

6:10 am 10,16,2015 my dad and I are heading out to work, we noticed a bright light in the sky, I looked up as well and seen a really bright white light south east of winnipeg, too bright to be a star. My dad took a picture with the camera on his phone. There was binoculares in my dads car so I took a look with them and it had a odd shape, kind of diamond, more square. I don't know it was definitely interesting but we were also running late. We went to the store to grab stuff before work, we forgot something at home so went back to get some things. The configuration, bright lights we witnessed in the sky before we went to the store were gone and only one dim star remained in it's place.
