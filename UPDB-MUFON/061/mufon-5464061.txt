Orange glowing disc with glowing red circumference

I work an hours travel away from home so I leave my house between 0500 - 0530hrs Mon to Fri. As it was Early and December it is dark.

I arrived at work, parked my car and walked from the company car park to the office building.

The walk from my car to the access point I use is around 100ft. I got with about 30ft of the building and what I can only describe as an orange street lightish glow clicked on directly above me. I looked up and for an orange disc with a glowing red circumference was there. The emitted street lightish style glow clicked off and the object sped off in a NW direction over the top of the trees on site. The object left a brief reddish/orange trail behind it then that also vanished.

As a British Army Veteran I have seen many forms of aircraft in the UK and on operational tour and I can say that whatever I saw is like no aircraft I have seen before.
