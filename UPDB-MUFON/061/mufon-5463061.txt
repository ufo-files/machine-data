{jpg} Myself and two others seen a trapezoid shape object that made no sound and its lights went off one after the other in air.

On July 6 2013 I just got off work at the fishing lake and went to be with family on the lake. Me and my two friends were talking and I just looked up into the sky. I noticed a large trapezoid shape with seven red solid lights. The structure looked like it blended in with the sky, it made no sound what so ever. I've seen aircraft before and this was very different. I turned to my friends and they were already looking at the same object and said, " that is strange". We were watching this object for a good 40 to 45 minutes it did not move. Then it done the stranges thing the red lights went out simultaneously one here then one there type of thing. I never seen anything like that before and neither did my friends. After seeing it I asked for a drink and said, " I think that was a UFO". My friends agreed.
