I heard the craft and followed its path

I was cutting my lawn with a strimmer the line broke so I stopped and started to change line it was a beautifully bright day with no clouds and no wind whatsoever I was throwing the grass to the cows in the field as I went on but when the object came down the cows ran off down the field ! It wasike a harrier jump jet descending I could hear it as clear as anything but I could not see ANYTHING it came down less than 20 feet from my position it had no physical shape ( cloaked ) I knew it was there and when it stopped descending it then turned I to a humming sound it started to move over next doors house I could still hear minutes later moving down towards three resovoires less than 1/2 mile away at this time I ran round to front of house where my wife was and she hadn't heard .me calling her to show her what was happening I then went back around to finish strumming and wondered what I had heard it didn't finish there about an hour later I was sweeping the grass up when the very same humming sound came back up over the next doors roof and carried on up the hill onto the Moor and then it disappeared (noise ) I am not a fake I can tell you that I have told many people and been ridiculed for it but I know what I heard and didn't see ! My theory is that it was a cloaked vehicle and I think it went to get fresh water from the resovoir I will swear on the bible this is true and happened it has been on my mind ever since
