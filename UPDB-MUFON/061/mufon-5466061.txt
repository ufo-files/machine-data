My wife and I were star gazing during August a couple a year's ago hoping to catch the Pleiades shower. What we observed instead changed our lives forever. We are Star Trek and Wars fans. We live in the country. People beleive we are nuts but what we say is beyond human explanation. There were 4 larger silent craft with 6 huge solid non blinking red lights on their belly. These larger craft would accelerate very quickly to smaller orb light craft. What blew my mind was these larger craft emitted a triangular shaped turquoise beam toward the smaller orbs. Just like in Star Trek, this beam functioned exactly like a Tractor Beam. Once the smaller orb was pulled verly close to the larger ship, the beam was disingaged. And the smaller orb accelerated at what seemed to be as fast as light. This happen a few times in the sky that night. The very next day, two blacked out helicopters came and circled the area where we witnessed the Arial phenomenon twice. Further in the woods there was indintions in the tall grass where something had landed. Something with a oval shaped had come to rest. Even some trees around the the intentions had bark scraped off. Now this is just one of our experiences.
