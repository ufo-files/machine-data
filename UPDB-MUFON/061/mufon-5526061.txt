Very large, greenish blue ball of light moving quickly through the sky in a straight line.

I was driving home from work, looking at the road, when I saw a moving object in the sky in my peripheral vision. I looked up very quickly because I thought it was a satellite or a shooting star, but to my surprise it was much, much too large to be either, much bigger than a plane reflecting light from something. It was incredibly eery and I didn't know what to think because I'd never seen anything like it or believed I ever would. To put it simply I was astounded. I gawked at it as it moved quickly from the center of my vision, where I first saw it descending, into the tree line and disappeared. It was also unlike any color I'd ever seen in the sky, like a very bright greenish blue, almost white. I felt very creeped out in a crazy way after this event.
