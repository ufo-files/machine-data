{cak} String of lighted objects fly over head.

I went outside to look at the stars and enjoy the weather. After my eyes adjusted, I noticed what I first thought was a large satellite traveling overhead. Then, I realized it was probably an object in our atmosphere. It was actually a trail of unidentifiable, illuminated objects flying over head in a straight line. The “convoy” of objects had already been traveling before I noticed them as I could see many ahead in the line and many after, or at the end of the line. They kept coming, one after another. I don’t know how many there were before I came out. I ran to the house to get my family. My wife lost count after counting 20 of these objects. They followed the same path and trajectory, moving at a rapid, steady pace in an apparent NW direction. Based on approximate altitude, they would have been very large. After the procession ended, we stayed outside longer, hoping to see something else. At least 5 more appeared and followed the same or similar path in the same direction. This was observed over a 20-30 minute period of time after the initial sighting ended. We got the video camera from the house, but, it was not charged and phone video would not have been descript enough, so we did not attempt. We looked at objects through binoculars.
