{cak} HUGE STRUCTURE WITH ABOUT 7 LIGHTS MOVING SILENT THRU THE SKY

On Thursday nights my girlfriend and I use a friends pool in Palm Springs Ca. Last night when I was drying off I happened to be looking up and noticed a large structure like craft with lights moving silently across the sky in a straight line. This craft was not flying in a traditional senses like a plane it was just moving. My girlfriend was still in the pool so I yelled to her and she was able to see it also. We both where excited to see this and stood outside for a few minutes hoping that it wood return.
