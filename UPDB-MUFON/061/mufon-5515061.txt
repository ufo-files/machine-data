{cak} Large and sleek V wing shapped object, back of wings jagged

I was taking my dogs outside with my wife in Highlands Ranch on October 8th around 745 PM. Upon looking above me while standing still I noticed a black, completely silent and dimly illuminated wing shaped object. The back edges had the appearance of being jagged. It was moving due west in an oddly strait and slow moving fashion unlike any aircraft I have ever seen, military or civilian. It was not a B-2 or F-117, it was a V shaped wing. It made a small course adjustment to southwest about 10 seconds or so after I originally saw it. The course adjustment was made when I looked away for a fraction of time. 

Roughly 15 seconds after it left eye sight my wife and I both saw a second one or the same craft, now coming from the north towards south east. We couldnt tell if it was one or two craft but both moved the same and looked the same. Gave us a good startle!
