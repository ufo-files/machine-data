UFO in panama, daytime photos and video

I was preparing the house for my son's birthday. I go out to the back of the house and see the object in the sky. I start filming and taking pictures with my cell phone (Samsung Galaxy S5).

The object was irregular in shape, rotated and oscillated. It did not emit light or sound and moved against the wind.

It was at a distance of 250 meters from me and at an altitude of approximately 300 meters. He followed it for several minutes then the object split in two. Then increase the altitude very high until reaching the clouds and before entering the clouds separated into 6 or 7 smaller objects. Unfortunately the photos I have of the 6 objects are very blurred by the altitude that I had
