On Nov. 25th, 2017 at 10:20 pm, in Longview WA in the middle part of the Columbia Height area I stepped outside facing East. It was very foggy and dark. At first I thought I was seeing the glow of an airplane, but then realized it was not flashing, there was no red & clear white, it was a solid glow. It was dome shaped moving NW, then abruptly E., then it was just gone. I did not see it travel any farther. It was such a fast observation, I didn't have the full thought of "oh, how pretty that plane looks in the fog." before it had changed direction & vanished. When it was gone I could still see a plane farther East still traveling North. I'm over 50, so i know a plane in the fog. This was NOT one.
