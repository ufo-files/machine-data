{dr}Several groups of 3 stars in very close triangle formation and in all directions of the night sky

The skies over Albuquerque were over populated by what looked like 3 stars in a triangle formation, the "stars" would brighten, change color slightly and dim, they also appeared to move suddenly and stop again without moving out of the space that they started in; I first noticed one group of three and then began to realize that just about everywhere that you could see stars there where groups of three in a triangle formation, a couple of them were much closer than others and they would brighten and dim, but not in any sort of pattern that I could see, just one "star" or disc at a time would change color slightly, It appeared that anyone in the Southwest that looked up would see them, but after checking the news nothing. We have an observatory 3 miles away at UNM, The ABQ Sunport and Kirtland AFB are within 6 miles of my sighting, so someone other than me and my wife had to of seen it; I don't drink or do drugs and I am not apt to seeing things or have an overactive imagination even though it started to make me think about the movie: War of the Worlds.
