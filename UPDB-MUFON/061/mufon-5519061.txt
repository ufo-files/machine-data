1 bright orange sphere/orb moving across sky South to North

I was standing on our back porch with my husband as we let the dogs out one last time before bed. I was looking up facing East when I noticed a bright orange ball/sphere looking object moving from South to North at about the same height as an airplane would fly, but traveling faster than an airplane. It had no blinking lights, and no other colored lights. It was just bright solid orange. It moved in a straight line with no sound coming from it. I pointed it out to my husband and we both watched it till it disappeared behind some trees. I ran through the house to the front porch hoping to see it again, but it was gone. Approximately 90 seconds long.
