{nd} The same bumming noise I have been hearing before came back and when the noise left I turned around and saw a light leaving into the clouds

I was smoking in my back yard when I suddenly heard a humming noise. I have heard this noise before it happens sometimes before I go to bed. We'll the noise came back and and I saw What appeared to be the outline of something invisible above the trees. When I noticed the humming got very loud and then took off and the noise stopped and when I turned around I could see the ball of light leaving. I enjoyed it's beauty then decided to film it while it left into the clouds. An airplane passed by right after and so did a helicopter. I have video of all 3.
