UFO (streaking through sky)

Around 0400 Mountain time, I saw an object streaking across the sky from my room. It looked like a thin cylinder or a comet tail. I stood up to see it, but it looked unfamiliar when getting a better look. It seemed to glow or give off a bright essence. My room faces north, and while observing it facing north/northeast, the object moved across the sky toward the east within seconds. It wasn't a plane, and it was moving too fast and was far too low to be a satellite. It had to have started from the west, for it was continuing east from the moment I saw it till it went out of sight.
