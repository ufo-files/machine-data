Large lights rotating in the sky with no noise and no light projecting from the ground .

I had to take our dog out because he was crying at midnight. I had just finished watching a movie when he started so I took him out. I put on his leash and we were across the street by the cemetery by 12:05 . We went less than 2 blocks and we turned around. On our way back we came about a half a block and while hes doing his business I was looking at the sky which I like to do and I noticed a large light in the sky followed by another then by another like the lights were circling but not moving . There were no lights coming from the ground and there was no noise . At first I thought it might be a helicopter but no noise. And I even waited to see if the noise of a helicopter might come a little later but it didn't . Anyway these lights continued to rotate as I walked closer toward home and then they started to move west ? toward Burmont Rd. I stood outside and watched while these rotating lights moved away. When I came in I told my wife who was up late making a cake for her bosses birthday but when we went out back the lights were no longer visible. And that is my UFO story. Very strange. I never thought I would write such a thing but it was too weird to just blow off.
