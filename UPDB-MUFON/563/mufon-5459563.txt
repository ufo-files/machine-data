Bright perfectly Circled Light in a constant High Speed

It was December 2010, when I was returning from Work and It was mid night 1:30 to 2 am. I was tired and my neck was paining. For a little exercise for my neck. I saw up in the sky and thought as if I was fainting. I sat down in the middle of the garden and saw up.
I saw a bright perfectly Circled Light in a constant high speed. 
Usually flights in the area which takes off at night has a red light below the Aircraft, but it was not a aircraft and nor a shooting star. It was a perfect bright white circle and just for taking a picture of the same. I tried to enter my password in the cell, But my cell was hanged for some time. And till the time I could call and tell my mom about it. It was gone and from then my interest in to UFO has increased a lot.
