Object in a straight line trajectory over the house and continued until out of sight.

On day and date stated in rear garden with 6 year old daughter looking through telescope for shooting stars- quantids meteor shower from memory- which were coming from the North East. Suddenly, object came into view from the South west approx 500 ft up. Skies clear and cold full observations of stars behind it. Travelling approx 200-300mph(rough estimate) no sound, no observable trail. Object dazzling bright, cone shaped, no wings seen. Object travelled in a straight line at the same speed in what I would describe as sliding a stone on ice. What I remarked on was that the line of travel was geometrically straight almost as if it was on a rail. Seen by my daughter who also observed the cone shape and is still asking about the incident 6 weeks later. No other objects seen apart from an passenger aircraft-twin prop- on its flight path to Glasgow Airport 10 miles away. If I could describe the speed and movement any clearer I would state that it was reminiscent of a projectile however apart from the fact that it was there nothing else seemed odd. The object appeared to be for lack of a better description minding it's own business. Although I had my mobile phone to hand I did not film any footage and was a little mesmerized just observing.
