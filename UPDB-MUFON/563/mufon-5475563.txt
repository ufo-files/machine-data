Beam of light, or water going up into faint outline of disc

Was on a Royal Caribbean cruise in January of 2015. We were in the Caribbean, headed towards Haiti. I was watching the water off my balcony room and saw something really weird looking, that looked to be pretty far off in the distance. I took a picture of it, and watched it until it was out of site (from our boat sailing past). The object didn't seem to be moving, from what I could see. I didn't hear anything either (again, it was very far off in the distance.
