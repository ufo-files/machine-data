april 24,2011-12 noon.i was at my kitchen sink, getting a drink when looking out of the window i noticed a silvery white object traveling in the sky slowly from right to left.i recognised this object as i had seen the same thing on the 12 feb,2011.it slowly pulsed a bright white as it moved.as it pulsed it seemed to increase in size and brightness.the pulsing had no discernable rhythm.i would call the shape more of an elipse than a disk.it was steady in flight and did not wobble.when first sighted its size was comparable to a 5p coin at arms length.after observing the object for several seconds i ran to the living room to get my camcorder and then proceeded to the back garden.the object was now hovering in the distance,a little further away than when first seen.i was in the process of trying to film the object when my 10 year old son came out and i pointed out the object to him.as i was trying to focus on the object my wife and daughter allso came outside.this is when the object came much closer and began making strange horizontal movements left and right.we were all in absolute awe as it began darting about in a fast,erratic manner.during this time the camcorder had been running for a couple of minutes but i was unable to focus on the object.several times the object seemed to totally disapear while we watched it and reapear in a differant part of the sky.i soon realised that trying to capture this moment with the camcorder was a bad idea so i asked my son to fetch my nikon d60 dslr with 200mm lens from the house.as i prepared the dslr i passed the camcorder to my son who allso carried on trying to film it.the object was begining to go back in the direction it first came from as i began taking pictures.to the human eye the object mostly looked like a white,flashing,pulsing elipse which sometimes would blink black for a split second.when i looked at the object through the zoom lens i could see a lot more detail.the object seemed to wobble and vibrate at a very fast rate.for a split second i could see a metallic disc shape then a sphere and allso lots of black flashes and a fuzziness to it.i would best describe it as it seemed to be every shape at the same time because it was flickering and changing so fast.allso i was shooting in burst mode with manual focus.several times it disapeared while i was photographing it and it would reapear in another part of the sky.during the time i was taking pics my son complained that he could see the object with his eyes but the camcorder could not pick it up.i eventually took 119 pics which show a lot of details that were not apparent when i was looking at the object.this i think is due to the fast shutter speed of the camera.the object eventually gained height and disapeared behind a neighbours house.whan i was watching this thing in the sky i was and still am convinced that this was a very unconventional object which showed impossible flight charachteristics and behaviour.i have worked outdoors all my life and planes ,normal aircraft,birds etc are a normal daily sight for me.this was far from normal and to be honest left me in shock for several days after it.
