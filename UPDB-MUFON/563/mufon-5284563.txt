Tic tacs and black orbs

So October 4th changed my life. 
Saw a flash in the sky was able to get one photo then 2 videos. I could clearly see a white objet all over the valley. It ended when the white tic tacs floated across the valley from slide lake to the Tetons and then disappeared.
So it wasn’t until I went back and went through my pics and videos did I see the black orb(s) flying around as well. 
My phone was having trouble focusing but I’ve come to realize that it’s a good way for me to see that they are around when my focus starts to have problems. 
The first pics and videos are from 12:28pm. 
The video from my car was shot 5 hrs later heading up to slide lake in the Gros Ventre range. I did not see anything that afternoon but my videos and pics speak for themselves.
