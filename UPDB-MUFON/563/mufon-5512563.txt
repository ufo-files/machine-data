(GS) Shiny Metallic ball in broad day light floating.

My dad and I were driving down Calico Rd, and we saw a shiny metallic ball in the sky just hovering, there were no clouds.It was a clearing in the woods were we saw it.When we saw it my dad hit the breaks and put the car in reverse. And when we got back to the clearing it was gone. no trace. This all happened within 30 seconds.
