{dr}Triangle Craft, 3 Large White Lights in a Triangle and 1 in Center Slowly Pulsating Red & White. Same Size.

10-22-15 9:30 pm EST. I was standing in my yard. I noticed something coming from the east. As it got more above me I could see it in more detail. Triangle Craft, Dark. 3 large white circular lights in a triangle pattern. 1 light in the center of the 3 (same size) slowly pulsating red to white and back. It was like the red was melding with the white, as it changed the 2 colors back and forth. Very Slowly, approx. 2 seconds. As I watch it pass me, I noticed something at the front of it. Grayish White Rectangular bent light in front of it. It looked to me as if it was manipulating the atmosphere directly in front of it, producing this grayish white flickering. It made no sound until it was traveling off to the west. Not jet engine sound. Like a low rumble of distant thunder. Which also led me to believe it was manipulating the atmosphere around it. It travelled in a straight line path east to west horizon to horizon. It was never abstructed from my view. Approx. 5 minutes. I am from Dayton, Ohio. I lived next to Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, for 23 years. I know what planes and jets look like. Someone told me to look up, TR3B on YouTube. It shows some similar type crafts. I don't know if it was Alien or of the Government. Either way, Some high-tech stuff. It was hard for me to judge the size of it, or the height in the sky. It was pretty big. Mid-level in the sky. All of the lights were on the bottom, not the sides. No FAA signal lights what so ever. That's about the best I can describe it.
