We all saw {a brighter then usual light pulsating light white red and blue} hovering lights over south bound 31 - {djs}

Myself and three of my friends were traveling south on us31 on the south end of grand haven. We were stopped at a light and I noticed a brighter then usual light pulsating light white red and blue hovering over what looked like in-between hollad and grand haven. I pointed it out to my friends. We all were taken back by what we were witnessing. We continued to watch this object as it started to move erratically. Then in the time it took to blink your eyes it shot acrossed the sky towards Grand rapids mi. I could not believe the speed was like nothing from this world. Almost like if u took a laser pointer and just went from o e point in the sky to the other.
