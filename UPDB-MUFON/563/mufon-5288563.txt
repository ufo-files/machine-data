Giant pulsating rainbow orb

Looked like bright rainbow star at firstt. Looking through high power binoculars looked like ring of pulsating shooting colors. With clearer focus looked like orb with every color. "Surface" looked like intricate geometric "panels", trapezoidal and almost like a circuit board constantly pulsing every color of the rainbow and more. Definitely an orb shape not light distortion. I use quotes earlier because the "surface" was not like a physical surface, was more like light or plasma. Almost see-through
