I was sitting in a car on my lunch break, eating a just-microwaved burrito obtained from the Circle K which lot I was still parked in. Being rural, it was a large, uneven lot and I had faced the front of the vehicle toward the hills and buttes surrounding the town of Prescott, AZ.
Absentmindedly gazing out the window as I ate, there it was: An approximately 20-25 foot torpedo-shaped craft a couple of miles away and about 150 ft above the ground, tilted roughly 15 degrees toward the East axis and making no sound, exhibiting no movement and seemingly stick to the sky, firmly lodged in position. It has no fins, no protrusions of any kind; it had no markings whatsoever on its dull, matted, pewter/silver exterior. It looked like a solid chunk of metal, shaped like a missile with exaggerated tapering at both ends that was somehow,--impossibly--completely stationary the entire time I observed it. 

It did not vibrate, emit exhaust that was discernible, it did not appear to be hovering in the sense normally associated with hovering objects. It was this large metal, out of place abomination to the senses. 

I blinked and rubbed my eyes and I stuck my head out the window for a view unobstructed by windshield contaminants. It remained as I have described. 

My immediate thought then was to sets an image in my mind so I could be as accurate as possible in the retelling I knew would soon be happening. I was excited, but I forced myself to contain it and be as informative as possible. 

I stared long enough at it so that today I can still bring to mind exactly what I saw and not just recall the story itself. While I continued to state and monitor the object, it vanished. I didn't get the sensation it had moved in a direction super fast, it just wasn't there anymore. I spent the remaining time of my lunch break staring in vain at the sky hoping for a reappearance.

This happened exactly as I have described and I am confident I will pass any polygraph or other test of veracity.
