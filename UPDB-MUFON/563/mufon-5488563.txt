6:50 am e/s time not sure how long it was there before but I watched it from then until 7:24 then it zoomed off very fast like a flash

I went outside this morning at 6:50 am to let the dogs do their morning business, something woke me up but not sure what but it was like I had to get up, looked up spotted the unusual object ran back in the house grabbed a cheap Nikon camera I had taken about 20 shots only a few turned out the rest showed only a black screen when I loaded them on my PC.I always look at the skies, it is beautiful and relaxing and sometimes I spot things then before I realize I am just standing there in awe I run to find a phone or a camera and usually I miss getting a picture. This time I got pictures.It was white and shinny, it hoovered, then made some very quick moves kinda zig zagging I kept snapping pics because I wanted to catch the movement in sequence but when I loaded them only about 3 turned out the rest were black images, My feelings were it was not of this Earth but I am not an expert in this, I just love to observe and learn, at first I thought it was just a very bright star until it moved around then I knew it wasn't , it did not look like any plane I have ever seen. and had 2 huge appendages out the top and a hook looking one to the side, I have never seen a plane like that, it reminded me of a cats ears except for the hook to the right.I lost sight when it zoomed quickly up ... straight up not on an angle like planes do. One things a few got this pink screen as well and I made no adjustments to my camera it really doesn't have many features just zoom in or out.
