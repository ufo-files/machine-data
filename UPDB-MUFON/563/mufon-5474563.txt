Pulsating light above the Sugarloaf Mountain.

I briefly looked out the window to check the weather when I see the full moon shining bright just above the Sugarloaf mountain. Below the moon but above the treeline is a star that I often see appear at this time of night. No big deal right? Just a faint star that rest above the mountain. This time something weird happens. The star begins flashing four to five rapid pulses. Like a strobe light. What the heck is going on? The first thing I do is grab my phone to record the event but the star disappears. The weather is nice and clear. So where did the star go? 

The pulses reminds me of the shiny object that I have seen before in 2007 and in the same exact location except for this time it was at night. What the heck is going on?

A few minutes later I sign into Facebook to tell my cousin. He explains that him and Jacob had a daylight sighting of the same bright object two years ago.
