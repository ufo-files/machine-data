Star like object first thought stationary. Item began to move after looking at a star visible through cloud cover.

I looked at a star facing east. It was the only star I could see. A second or two later I saw another dimmer star. 

After looking south in a standing position I noticed the dimmer object in the sky gain a second close light. They looked connected. It began to move south with a constant brightness and faster than a plane. It "looked" still and had accelerated rapidly.

If I pointed at the object, I followed it through the sky for 3 feet from a standing position. It was faster than an airplane. It turned east and travelled upwards fast enough to dim with in a second and be gone after 3 seconds.
