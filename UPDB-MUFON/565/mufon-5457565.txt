{cak} 5 in formation met 5 more stoped turned went over hill

This happend in mid 60's saw 5 in V formation moving slow followed them on bike about a mile then they met nose to nose with 5 more stopped in midair for several seconds turned 90 degrees (each 5 was like one solid peice when turning) then side by side still in two v formations headed north. There was no sound round silver disc stopping in midair (seemed unreal) then went out of sight over hill. This happened along the Ohio River between Athilia and Rome Ohio along RT 7 I was a young teenager at the time It went over the hill at Mc Call Creek. Round no wings no sound. I would find it hard to believe this if told to me but I know I saw this. There was an artical years later in the Herald Dispatch about this happening. I remember having fear while falling asleep for several months but I don't know if it was around this time I felt like I was going into a trance. I wish I knew what I saw My cell phone don't always pick up where I live my text [information removed] The date and time is estmated this happened in mid 60's durning Day
