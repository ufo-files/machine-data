{jpg} July 02, 2019 I seen a grey object flying across the sky

I was sitting on couch watching TV between 12:30 pm and 2:00pm on July 2nd. I looked out the patio window when I seen something gray flying across the sky it seemed to have a shape to the top of it before it made an abrupt turn up at an angle. I thought it was a jet (there is an Air Force base near here).  I got up and went to the window and seen this gray triangle object turning to its side and flew up into the clouds. I thought it was a jet, but the shape from the flat side was odd; it was a solid triangle and had two dark shadow areas on it. No sound was coming from it. It also looked like it had a piece missing or broken from the bottom.  I thought that was no Jet! It was solid, no wings, and moved fast like a jest would if it was normally flying.
When it was flying into the clouds it was more on its side going up.
