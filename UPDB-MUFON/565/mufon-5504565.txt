{kt}Seven objects in formation moving extremely fast from NNW to SSE.

At approximately 10:30pm on 2/26/14 I witnessed 7 objects in formation moving extremely fast through the sky. There were elevated amounts of Air traffic, and in comparison to the planes, I would estimate they were moving about ten times as quickly. I would estimate at an altitude of 1000ft, but I am not trained to estimate so this may be inaccurate. They were moving through the lower layer of the clouds, as I recall a "haze" around them. They were white, but not to bright. They were almost in V formation, and had a sort of slight horizontal movement as they streaked across the sky. They were egg/saucer/oval shaped. The sighting lasted about 6 seconds from beginning to end. I endured the bitter cold and wind, with camera in hand hoping they would return, but was unable to get a glimpse of anything unusual throughout the night and early morning.
