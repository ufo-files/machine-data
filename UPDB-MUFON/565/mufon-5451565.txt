We live in Los Angeles, and it looked to be hovering over LAX, about 7 miles away. It was mesmerizing, and I watched it for a minute or so while my neighbor continued speaking. Then the light went out, but there -as now a black speck in the sky in its place. I told my neighbor what I had just seen and asked him if he could see the black speck. As he began looking, the spotlight came back on, much smaller in size but undeniable, and then went out. There was no longer a black speck. The object just disappeared.
