{dr}I was just watching the stars with a friend when we saw a formation of ufos dart above us

Last night me and a friend were on my roof watching the sunset and right after it set we noticed big "flocks" of very fast lights zipping all across the sky, we watched them dart all across the sky for about thirty minutes then they just stopped, my friend went home and i went to bed but i couldnt sleep so i just kept watching the sky. Sure enough a big single light was slowly hovering above the city over from me and i saw hundreds if not thousands of these faint white lights fly in and out of the multicolored hovering light all night long, i went out to th e roof for a better view around five am. As things were starting to take on a morning glow i noticed the hovering craft start some sort of bright strobe light that lasted for a few minutes and all of the sudden a oval shape craft buzzed over me at about 100 feet. It was moving so fast that i barely saw it, the sound it gave of was like a deep rumble. It shot up into the sky and i lost it among all of the other ufos. Then right as the sun came up the hovering ship just vanished, i didnt even see if it flew off, just one second it was there, the next it was gone. I promply went to the bathroom and threw up from the shock of seeing all of this. I didnt believe in aliens one bit before last night..in fact i didnt believe my own eyes for the first few hours of watching. I tried to get video but the lights were faint and my cell phone couldnt pick them up, but after watching the ogden utah sighting video from May 2014 I can say that those were th e same ufos I saw last night 9/4/2014. I know that my lack of video hardly makes this evidence but I know what I saw and after comparing to the recent sightings in Houston Texas and Ogden Utah I know those are the same things me and my friend saw. Seems the aliens are here..so cool.
