{tjd} During Fireworks over Lake Tawakoni,Tx

We went down to the cove at the lake to watch Fireworks for Memorial Weekend on Saturday May 26, 2019 at Lake Tawakoni in Texas. It was just three of us but there were many people shooting fireworks around the whole lake . It was between 9:30-10pm and dad and I both saw something else moving besides Fireworks or a plane or Helicopter. It moved real fast and then stopped and hovered and then would move sparatic in different directions really fast and then stop . It also went away really far and then came back again. After a few minutes of watching my dad said we better record it so I pulled out my phone and started recording and that’s when I could see two lights on my phone but it was only one light seen by our eyes at first . They were pulsating vibrant colors and even changed from a bright red/orange to a whit/green color and then we saw it split into two with our own eyes like the phone camera was catching. We watched them move around like nothing we have ever seen and continued watching for a few minutes after the video stops and they eventually disappeared out of our site and the area had a strange energy or vibe to it . We all three saw it and watched for several minutes until they went away .
