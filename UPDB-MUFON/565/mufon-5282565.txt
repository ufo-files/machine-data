Flew directly overhead, bright glowing reddish/orange light that appeared to have angles

I was sitting having coffee by our pool which is in a screen enclosure. I'm sitting facing West. Noticed an orange glow up and behind me. It had come from the front our house which is facing East. When it moved overhead, the lights appeared to be like rods, and the rods seemed to form like an odd angular shape. I didn't know what to make of it. At first, I thought it was some kind of balloon or a drone but, it wasn't. No noise at all. It seemed to slow down above me, hover a little and appeared to wobble around, too. Seemed to be only 20-30ft above. I was confused at what I was seeing. then it started to move westward over the golf course. I then took off inside to grab my cell phone and was able to get some video of it moving away but, camera picks it up as an orange ball of light. It kept getting smaller and smaller very quickly. Not sure if that was because it was rapidly moving away or it was blinking out. It appeared to me that it was rapidly moving SW at very high rate of speed. I think this is one of the strangest things I've ever seen! i tried to contact our local tv news channel to see if they've heard of any other reports but, I haven't received any response.
