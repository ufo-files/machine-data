what looked like a satellite at about 25,000 feet traveled across the entire sky in only 8 seconds!

while standing outside on a clear night (may 27, 2012 at about 12:35am), my girlfriend and i both witnessed what appeared to be a satellite or commercial airliner at very high altitude - approximately 25,000 feet. what distinguished this single bright star-like light such that we knew it was not a conventional craft was that it went all the way from the western horizon to the eastern horizon in only about 8 seconds (flying in a straight line). we were astounded by the speed of the craft. once it passed out of view it did not return. so far (about 9 hours later), we have had no unusual experiences as a result of this sighting.
