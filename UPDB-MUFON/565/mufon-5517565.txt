{flg} Five spear shaped pulsating Amber and white in color objects moving northwest over portage Indiana on July 4, 2012

I was driving down the road in portage Indiana on the tollroad about to exit so I pulled over looked out my left hand side window saw two lights Amber in color pulsating moving northwest a bought a quarter of a mile away from me probably about a mile higth moving at about 30 miles an hour . So I film them for about 10 miles then they moved out of sight. Then I looked back in the same direction they came from and I saw the third one same speed same height moving the same direction they were all on a little different path though. Filmed that one briefly and took a digital photo. After that I did the same thing and saw the last two. I took some more video and one more digital still shot. As they got Outasight they either raise into a cloud which I didn't see any clouds or they just faded off. This all happened about 1030 on 4 July 2012.
