Round object travelling above house

I was in the garden. Dusk. Heard engine of a plane or helicopter approaching and it was loud and close. Looked above me and saw what I think was a military helicopter and it was flying close to this thing. It flew close for a while, then took off to the north, whilst the UFO continued on its path. At which point I had got my phone to start recording the video. I watched it after it became too dark to get more footage and it continued on a NE path and disappeared from view. It was definitely not a balloon and appeared to be under intelligent control. The following day, I became very ill in the evening and blacked out. I don't know if that was related, but it was unusual.
