Bright light flew toward other bright light and took it's place in the sky

My husband and I and four of our kids went to the country to watch the lunar eclipse. My 17 year old son, my husband and myself all watched a bright light move from the South to the North toward what we thought was a bright star above us. The bright light moved over the "star" and got brighter for about 3 seconds then the "star" moved to the side and disappeared. The bright light remained. It was like they were changing shifts or something.
