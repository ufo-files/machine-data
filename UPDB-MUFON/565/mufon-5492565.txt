One afternoon my sister and I observed a metallic disk like object with lights going around center and at very bottom that was hovering in tree line beside road on Lan Yair golf course. We pulled into median to observe it for a few minutes and observed a change in the light pattern that was flashing around the center. We then pulled into Star Lanes bowling alley parking lot to observe to get out of the road and as my sister was turning I practically stuck my head out the window to keep my eye on it and I observed it just zip away at an incredible speed over rd and over us and over the bowling alley in like one second flat it was just gone. If I had not been turned around in my seat I would've missed it just blast away in a second flat. It made no noise at all.
