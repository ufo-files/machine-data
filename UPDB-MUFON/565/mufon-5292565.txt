Dark object floating across the moon

I decided to take some photographs of the moon using my new camera and lens. The smaller pictures are the original zoomed in pictures. The close ups are crops of the originals.
There is a dark object that appears to the right of a white spot, and travels down the moon. It is easily seen in the photo's.
It could possibly be a satellite passing between me and the moon, but I don't know and thought it would be worth reporting.
