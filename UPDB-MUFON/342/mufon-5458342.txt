Flashing lights near moon.

As I zoomed into the moon on 15/11/18 with my Panasonic camera DMC-TZ40 max zoom 153, I could see many erratic flashing lights to the right of the moon.I took this video then decided to take photos. I took photos of objects, some coloured from approx 5.00pm to 9.00pm. Some are on this MUFON site search Hartlepool. Most I have recently uploaded to Youtube including this video under my user name Gen Uine. The lights appear to the bottom right of this video and are best watched in a dark environment,as they are quite small to see.
