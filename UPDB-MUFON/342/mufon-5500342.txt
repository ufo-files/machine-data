One bright light followed by dimmer one which camera didn't pick up.

Was just getting ready for bed when my wife told me to look out of window at what she thought was a meteor, I thought different as the objects where in tandem one bright light closely followed by a smaller dimmer one also the objects where cruising across the sky not falling. The objects then disappeared into the distance. I believe these objects were going to fast to be a lantern or plane plus the dimmer one following was too close to be two planes chasing each other.
