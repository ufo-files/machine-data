two glowing orbs

Noticed two very bright lights in the distance in the southwest region of the sky over I-195. These lights appeared brighter than any star or airplane. (this area has heavy commercial airline traffic) did not have any features to indicate it was a air plain (running lights/headlights/etc.)The objects seemed to pulsate and slowly become dimmer or brighter. Observed object through binoculars and noticed it was white to light green on top and had a purple hue on the bottom. At one point several small blinking lights discharged from the area around the brightest light, moved away from it, and then faded out. Witnessed a similar event in this very spot and orientation about two years ago after driving home and seeing what i thought was a comet streaking down from the sky.
