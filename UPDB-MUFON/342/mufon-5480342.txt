{cak} Occasional small lights on the western horizon, near Mt. Adams/Mt. Rainier

Back in the mid-to-late 80's, we used to see points of light on the western horizon near Mt. Adams that would hover and move horizontally at great, immediate speeds - usually from north to south. From our vantage point, we could see the "dot" travel in front of Mt. Adams, and sometimes move behind the mountain.

These were observed at two neighboring houses, and occurred occasionally (once or twice per summer). Witnessed by myself as a grade schooler, parents, and grandparents.
