Flash of white light apporximately 5 ft. off ground

I was with my friend driving down Mountain or Lickskillet Rd. (I'm not sure of which road)in Harris County at apporximately 5:30 AM EDT, When both of us saw a flash of white light, very close to the ground, that lasted 3-5 seconds.
