Light by Salton Sea

I lived in Mecca California and one night I was opening the gate so my wife could leave and drive to work. That was close to 9 pm every night, 5 nights a week. One night. UFOs have been following me since I lived in Kentucky. One night, opening the gate I looked to the mountain range by the Salton sea. Directly above the second tallest peak was an orange, bright diamond. As I looked at it, it got brighter and brighter and gave me tunnel vision directly with the object. It noticed that I was looking at it. My wife caught my attention behind me and as I looked back to the object it got dim and small rapidly and was gone, like it flew away with incredible speed. 2 weeks prior, my wife, son and myself saw another ufo that silently but with incredible speed flew straight at us and over us, scanning us with a blue light as it passed over.
