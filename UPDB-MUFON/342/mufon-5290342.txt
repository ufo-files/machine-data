Balls of orange light during fireworks on New Years morning

2 lights seen traveling on the same path during the time of the fireworks. My good camera’s battery died the minute the first one appeared. I don’t think they were planes because they were too low to the ground to be planes and air traffic control doesn’t allow planes to fly this close together. The video I took on my phone isn’t very good, but it’s better than nothing! Really cool way to ring in the new year!
