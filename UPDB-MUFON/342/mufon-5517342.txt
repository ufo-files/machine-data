(gs) Flew right over my apt. Was just above a very tall palm tree Two other saw this with me.

Just about dusk time, was called outside by friend, looked up and saw this disc just above the tree line, was flying slow enough for me to run under the disc. Saw the underside was jet black, with no exhaust, not a sound coming from vehicle. Moved away down the driveway and went over the freeway and hung a leftand proceeded up over the freeway. Was going at a slow speed. Was blinking a red dome light at top of disc. Proceeded about 4 miles to the east and returned back to my vicinity. About 2 miles away just flew around that neiborhood. Lasted about 2 hrs. I called the air force base, but was denied any info.  When it left, which was extremely fast just flew straight up and was gone.
