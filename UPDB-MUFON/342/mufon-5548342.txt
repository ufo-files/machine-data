Flying overhead

I was sitting at my desk which is right by a window that faces West, in Denver, Colorado. Something in the sky caught my eye because the sun reflected off of it. When I looked up, I initially thought it was a plane, but then I thought, "why would a plane be so low". At second glance, it didn't have any wings like a plane would. It was moving South bound, slowly across the sky. After about 10 seconds, it crossed over the top of the building I was in. I got up from my desk and ran to the other side of the building, opened the blinds and saw it again. This time it was going East. I passed a co-worker on my way to the other side of the building. She asked me what I was doing and I told her that I saw a UFO. She came to the window and saw the same object as me. The shape was like a cylinder/cigar laying on it's side. Then, the tip/front of it was like a cone. It continued South East for a short period of time, then it climbed and continued to climb, then it shot off, out of site. The both of us saw it. I couldn't believe what I was seeing. It definately wasn't a plane. I was very surprised to see something like this. It was great.
