Then, within a few minutes she mentions that she sees an orange ball of light moving over the lake (southwest to northeast direction) to my right (the lake home faces south east). I immediately focus my attention to a glowing orange and yellow orb in the sky, moving about 30 mph to the Northeast, directly over the center of the lake and at an altitude of ~ 200 ft. above the surface of the water. The orange, glowing orb was completely silent and moved parallel with the surface of the water. After the orb moved about 400 or 500 yds. to the northeast, the orb instantly 1blinked1 out!

However, there seemed to be a distinct design to the glowing nature of the orb that struck me the most!
