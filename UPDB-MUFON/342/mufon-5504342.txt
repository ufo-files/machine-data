{cak} I saw an odd light moving erractic, I proceeded to shine a flashlight at it. It reacted and danced in the sky while changing colors.

I came out of my back yard and looked west about 9:00p.m. 3/9/2014. I noticed an odd looking star, then it started flashing different colors and darting around like no man-made craft could do. I came in, got my flashlight, got my wife to verify I wasn't crazy. She saw it too. A few minutes later another object came from west to east and stopped in the sky a little west of my front yard, We thought at first it was a plane until it stopped dead still. I started blinking my flashlight at it, it started blinking different colors at me. I moved my light in circular and back and forth motions and it started moving around very quickly, up and down, left and right, no plane or helicopter could do that. We played light tag for almost 3 hrs. An airplane came over from west to east and the object dimmed and moved to the west a pretty good bit. I also had the same result from 4 or 5 other star like lights. I could shine my light at them and they would dart back and forth and flash random colors, would get brighter then dimmer too. It was cool.I'll never doubt that we have company again.
