Very bright orange orbs

Having drinks on beach deck crown plaza hotel observed two bright orange orbs hovering on Easter horizon.thought at first mast on ship or stationary platform. Objects Climed about 5 deg of arc remained stationary and hovered on horizon for about five to ten mins.To my surprise the locals and waitress were very excieted . Single orb Appeared in same area aprox two hours later climbed about 20 deg of arc blinking in and out at about a six second duration for about five mins. no planets at that position way to bright for landing lights. I am a Mufon member,private pilot, amateur astronomer and had never observed any aerial phenonmena like this before just would like to know if there were any other sightings at this time in this area.
