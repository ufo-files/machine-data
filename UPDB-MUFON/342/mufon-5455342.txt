Starlike / Satellite like object, but not in the usual path of satellite.

Between 5:47 am to 6:10 am, there were about 12 star like objects moving in the sky to cross the "Big Dipper" constellation. The first object seemed to be a possible satellite, however, there were multiple such objects traversing the West-to-east direction across the constellation, some even spaced quite close to preclude the possibility that these were satellites - one would imagine the redundancy with regards to multiple satellites traversing the path so close to each other. About 12 such objects were observed in total. These 12 objects were without doubt existent as I confirmed these with my spouse. During the time frame, I also noted 1 object traversing in a direction perpendicular (North to South) to that of the above 12 objects. This object also changed direction and hovered before disappearing. This object was about 45 degrees west of Alcor star of the Big Dipper constellation.

I am curious about what these objects were. I am sure any other star gazer at the time would have noticed these. I am open to the possibility of them being satellites - but as mentioned, the behaviour and number precluded the possibility in my view.
