{nd} Flat round object flew over the highway well under cloud ceiling. Appeared metallic and flying very fast.

My husband and I were driving on I-69 going north in anderson, IN. This object was too low to be a plane and too fast to be a helicopter, it was low enough to catch our attention as it crossed our path. We first questioned whether it was a drone, moments later we saw the dark underside of the craft and could tell that it was a disk shape, very flat. We were excited to see our first UFO in the day time. The craft stayed on A straight path over a field and beyond the tree line, when I lost sight.
