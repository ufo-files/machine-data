{dr}One of my first memory1s, backed up by family 40 years later.

I was around 4 or 5 years old, I got up late at night to go to the bathroom while reliving myself I noticed a humming sound outside my second story house window. When I got done I stood up on the toilet to look if I could find where the sound was coming from. What I saw out the window on the ground in our backyard was what looked to me like a merry go round, different colored lights going around in a circle. I watched transfixed for a minute or two then went back to bed. In the morning I told my family, two brothers, two sisters, my Mom and Grand Mom that I saw a merry go round in the back yard. Being the youngest they just laughed it off but I wanted to know what happened to the merry go round. After breakfast I went out and looked around, I couldn't find anything. All the other homes on that block were empty, I found out later that the city was buying them up to build a freeway off ramp. I never forgot this event and fourth some odd years later I brought it up at a family reunion and one of my sisters remembered me telling them about it. Until she said "Ya when you saw the UFO" I never truly put it together but now I know what it was and it shouldn't have been there.
