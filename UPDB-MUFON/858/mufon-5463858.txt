{rjl} observed aircraft flying easterly at lower and slower than normal aircraft with no sound

I was at my home performing maintaince on my vehicle, looking up to check weather conditions I saw what I thought was an airliner, after observing a short time I realized the object was at a lower altitude and appeared to be longer and larger than an airliner after looking more closely I could see no wings, tail fin or rudders and made no sound, the object appeared to be silverish white, possibly reflecting the clouds that were in its vicinity, I could not believe what I was seeing, where I live we see very few aircraft and the ones we do see are at high altitude, but, can still be heard. after a minute or so the object made a slight turn to the northeast, I then went inside and brought my wife outside to see it, at that point it had disappeared. I grew-up in Pensacola, Fl. and with all the military bases in that area I have seen just about every type of aircraft including blimps, I have also been an OTR truck driver covering almost all the states and I do not recall seeing anything like this.
