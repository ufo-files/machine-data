{dr}Silver, metalic disk, looked like upside down frying pan without handle. Stayed in same spot for 4 Hrs.

Noticed 3 neighbors pointing at an object high in the sky. Stopped to ask what they were looking at and they pointed it out. Looked like white dot in the sky and they said it hadn't moved for about 45 minutes. Got my 10X binoculars out and looked at it and it looked like a silver metal frying pan upside down with out the handle (Disk shaped). When my neighbor looked at it with my binoculars he said he could see a bulge on top. I drove two miles to work and showed the guys at work. About 10 of us saw it and continually watched it for about 4 hours it didn't move. Stayed in same spot. About 3 hours in it got really shinny and reflective on the East side where the sun was. At about 10:15 A.M. I watched it and took my eyes off it for about 30 seconds while I was getting gas and it was gone. My friend called me and told me at about the same time that it was gone.
