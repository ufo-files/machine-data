{cak} Brief sighting as it flew overhead.

Relaxing on the deck and watching the sky. Around 8:28 p.m I noticed a light moving over my head, SW to NE. I first thought it was the ISS so I checked the Heavens Above web site. It was not the ISS. 

On Saturday evening 8/3/13 at around 21:56 me and some friends watched the ISS fly over at a magnitude -3.5. The sky was dark. Tonight the sky was still a bit light and Id guesstimate the magnitude to be around -1.5 to -2.0. I was able to observe the light for about 20 seconds until it disappeared into some light cloud cover. The object was star-like, no blinking and no nav lights and appeared to be traveling a little faster than the ISS normally travels.

My observation position is: Pioneer CA, 38.43001N, 120.57471W
