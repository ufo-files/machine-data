{tjd} White ball of light being chased by two (2) fighter jets with afterburners on.

I was walking to the garage at the company where I work when I kept hearing two (2) fighter jets. At first I couldn't see anything. Then I saw one fighter jet banking hard in an arc from South to North. I then looked for the other fighter jet out front and that is when I saw the ball of light. It was moving faster then the fighter jet was but somehow didn't look like it was any hurry. They both disappeared in low clouds just East of Lambert St. Louis International Airport. Sighting lasted a maximum of one (1) minute. my first ever UFO sighting!
