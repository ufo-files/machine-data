{dr}I walked outside to move my truck from the street to the driveway and I saw 2 hovering objects

As I mentioned above I seen two objects in the sky diagonal of one another. At first I figured it was a star or plane until I realized they were hovering once one object slightly moved. The oddest thing was that one they were completely silent and two they gave off an amber glow.i stared for about 10 minutes then I took pictures on my cell phone. One object started to slightly move again then took off so fast into the sky, while the other stayed still. The next several nights nothing was out of the ordinary, but after about 5 days I saw the same colored objects again but this time they were spread out further and when I came back outside they were gone.
