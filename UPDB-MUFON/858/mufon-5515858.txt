{dr}Two orange lights above Lake Baldwin

Driving home late at night after work. There was roadwork so had to detour around the lake. I was at the light on S.R50 and noticed orange lights in the sky.Was very noticeable, quite large! Made the left, the road twists and turns around the residential area. It was distracting, didn't want to take my eyes off it. They didn't move. I checked the road and turned my head back and a third orange light had appeared, a bit smaller than the others. They made a diagonal line. As I was making my right turn, a helicopter flew over the apartment building on my left.Flying directly at it! I drove home really fast and ran upstairs to tell my boyfriend. He looked out our bedroom window and said he couldnt see anything but he could hear the helicopter. He drove around to the lake and couldnt see anything. No orange lights and no helicpoter! I have tried to explain it away but cant,The lights were directly over the lake and there are no buildings large enough to consider someones apartment lights. Dont really know what I saw, but have seen similar things reported in England and out west.
