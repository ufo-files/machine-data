{cak} Weekend getaway and observed a very bright large object and smaller object.

I was out on the deck, overlooking the water looking northeast across the straight of Georgia when a bright light in the clouds flickered on. It stayed on for approximately 5 minutes, stopped for five minutes then flickered back on. While this was going on a smaller craft was flying across the straight of Georgia, east to west. As it approached the larger object the smaller crafts lights(2 or 3) would fade out and as it flew away from the larger object, the smaller craft would light up again. Attached is a blurry video which could possibly determine the size of the object, I could not make out the smaller craft in the video.
