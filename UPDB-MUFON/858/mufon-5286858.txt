Six Months of Nightmares

The following happened to me when I was 7 years old. (1952). I am now 76. Every night for approximately six months I would wake up screaming as I found myself 'floating in space'. It seemed like I was floating in an orbit and circling a few times only to find myself floating into a closed area. At this point I would be approached by what I understood to be orbs, at which time, and I would begin to scream. This drove my parents nuts. Once awake I'd fall back to sleep and all was fine.
