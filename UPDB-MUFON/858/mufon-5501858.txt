Large orb with 2-3 smaller orbs

On 06/19/2014, at approximately 12:42pm, I was driving home, I came to a red traffic light and observed a white object in the sky through the power lines. At first I thought it was an airplane, but I noticed that it wasn't moving like an airplane should. The object kept changing direction and shape unlike an airplane and moved very slow. I then pulled over and parked my car at a near gas station and pulled out my iPhone. As I was trying to record the object, I notice that while it was moving I could see 2-3 smaller orb like objects flying around the big object. The objects traveled from North to south. I don't think it was plastic bags or balloons.
