{rjl} Glowing Light Morphs Into Two Lights Then Back Again Multiple Times

I was driving from Kansas City to Tulsa. Around 8:30/dusk, I noticed an odd light off to the left and in front of me. I watched it then it split into two lights. That's when I pulled out my phone and started recording it. As I captured it in a 5 minute video, it morphs from a single light back to a double light 5 separate times. I eventually passed it and could not drive and film at the same time anymore. After reviewing the video, I noticed a very odd sound at the 4:12 mark and the 4:22 mark that did not occur anywhere else on the video. Here are the time stamps I can see the light morph into 2 lights: 0:09-0:25, 1:41, 2:03, 3:24, and 4:00
