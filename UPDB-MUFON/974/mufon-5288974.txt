Viewed Circular Disc shaped object being transported by 1 military vehicle and at least 6 CHP patrol vehicles travelling at a high speed.

At approx, 12:55pm on 3/25/2021 while traveling with my cousin leaving Ridgecrest, California going south on Hwy 395 about 10-20 miles south of Ridgecrest we observed a semi truck with a flatbed being escorted northbound by at least 6 and possible 7 California Highway Patrol vehicles with full lights and sirens and what appeared to be one tan military van. The convoy was traveling at a very high rate of speed and the CHP were forcing oncoming traffic off to the side of the road. The truck had a yellow (wide load) sign on it. As the escorted truck approached we observed a large disc shaped object strapped to the trailer estimated to be at least 20 feet wide and maybe 6 to 10 feet high. The object was completely covered in a black cloth tarp so it was impossible to see the actual object. The convoy appeared to be headed to the China Lake military installation. which lied in the direction it was headed. I was unable to get my phone out in time to get footage and we deemed it wise not to turn around and chase them for a better look. I can provide a sketch of the object if needed.
