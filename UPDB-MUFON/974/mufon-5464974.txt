{cak} Object appeard and remained in the sky for over an hour, abrupt disappearance. Moved slowly due West

At 6pm I was running 7 miles due west along Woodmen Rd from Powers Blvd to Rockrimmon. As the sun set I noticed a light appearing in the west at the same degree as the top of Pikes Peak, but it was north, between Pikes Peak and Blodgett Summit. At first I thought it was a planet as it appeared much larger than a star. I thought that the object was stationary, but later, because it was shrinking in size, determined that it was moving very slowly due west. It was pulsating blue/green, red and white lights. When I finished running and returned home, I took my Meade DSK-90 telescope outside and with my two adult sons viewed the object. At times the red lights appeard to have a distinct triangle pattern. We watched it for about 10 minutes until about 7:20pm and then the lights just vanished as if it turned off by a lightswitch
