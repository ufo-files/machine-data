Triangular shaped object the size of a KC-135 tanker with a large orange light in the middile with 3 smaller lights on the ends of the angles.

While I was walking my dog late at night, I heard several people talking about something floating in the sky. When I looked up there was this KC-135 sized triangular aircraft flying at a hovering pattern, no noise whatsoever, it had bright lights like strobe lights but constantly changed colors. We live in a district where South Korean F-4Es, KF-16s usually fly overhead. This object made no noise at all and flew in a speed a normal jet would stall at. When I first saw it I thought it was a low flying military jet but I noticed it to be flying at a really really slow speed, and this object did not make any noise like the jets we get here. The object hovered like a helicopter for a short moment then continued on a very slow flightpath southeast from Icheon City. I felt a lot of fear when I saw the object, my dog started behaving in erratic manners. I lost sight of it after it flew right over a mountain range. The next day, the skies were filled with more jet fighters than they would usually send up (typically, the jets here number a total of 2 in formation, the next day almost all the jets were in a V formation numbering a total of 5 aircraft, mostly F-16s and F-4 aircraft).
