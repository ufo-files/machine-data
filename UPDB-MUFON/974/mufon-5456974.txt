{rjl} Triangle craft, flew overhead with low hum, additional lights seen in the the flight path by other witness on other occassion

Late in the evening, early morning, my friend in the car talking with girlfriend, i left car to give them space. Walking along northern shore of lake ottowa Iron county michigan. Snow on the ground, lake partially frozen. Witnessed light at southern end of lake , initially seemed like a low flying aircraft. Craft traveled from south to north, following length of the length toward me. As craft approached me, I noticed very low hum, no loud noise associated with large aircraft. Sounded like a vacuum cleaner. At point that craft is directly overhead, appears there are three amber colored lights at each corner of triangle, also 5 white lights pulsing in the middle of craft. Craft appeared to be slightly smaller than a 737. Passed directly overhead with no change in altitude or speed. Continued north with no change in behavior. Currently deceased witness claims to have seen lights and craft around 3 am on numerous occasions in association with same flight path, farther north of location of initial sighting. Former witness claims to have been awoken around 3 am on many occasions prior to , and after date of sighting.
