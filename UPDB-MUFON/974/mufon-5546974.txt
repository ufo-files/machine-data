Object made Mist/Cloud, mist enveloped object, object disappeared

We were at an outside dinner party, the sky was clear, no clouds anywhere.at approximatley 7:30PM Several of us noticed a bright light in the Southern Sky, the object/light was located beween Saturn and the Constellation Sagitarius. its altitude was parallel to Saturn, and was a little brighter than Saturn. I did not notice when it arrived, but about 20 of us noticed that it was generating a cloud or mist just below itself, the mist then crept upwards and enveloped the object, you could still see the object glowing in the mist, then the object slowly got dimmer then vanished, the mist then dissipated. There was no noise. This happened in Pittstown New Jersey
