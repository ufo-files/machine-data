IT HAPPENED REALLY FAST.THEY JUST CAME ACROSS THE SKY.VERY BRITE.

I WAS AT A GINOS HAMBURGER PLACE.WASHINGTON BLVD AT MONROE STREET.I JUST GOT OUT OF MY FREINDS CAR TO WALK OVER TO ANOTHER FRIENDS CAR.FOR SOME UNKNOWN REASON I WAS TOLD TO LOOK UP,I WAS BY MYSELF,NO ONE CLOSE TO ME.WHAT EVER IT WAS,IT SAID,LOOK UP WE ARE REAL.WHEN I LOOKED UP,I SAW TWO BRIGHT DOTS,BRIGHTER THAN THE BACK GROUND STARS.ONE WAS HIGHER THAN THE OTHER AND THE OTHER WAS DOWN AND TO THE BACK OF THE OTHER.THEY WHERE GOING VERY FAST.THEY WENT STRAIGHT.I DID NOT HAVE TIME TO ASK MY FRIENDS DID YOU SEE THEM.IT SEEMS THEY CAME IN THE SAME LINE AS FROM THE AIRPORT,BWI NOW.I HAVE BEEN AT THE SAME SPOT FOR MANY YEARS.JUST TO SEE IF IT WAS A PLANE OR PLANES,WITH RUNNING LIGHTS.AFTER YEARS OF WATCHING,I HAVE NOT SEEN THE LIGHTS LIKE I SAW.AS A KID I LOVED WATCHING PLANES GO BY.THEY DID NOT TWINKEL.IT WAS A STEADY LIGHT.DID NOT SHIFT COLORS.I HAVE TOLD PEOPLE ABOUT THIS,THEY ALL SAID IT WAS A PLANE.AFTER SEEING THEM MY THOUGHTS ABOUT OTHER PEOPLES FROM OTHER PLANETS JUST MIGHT BE TRUE.I STILL GET A GOOD FEELING ABOUT WHAT HAPPENED TO ME.I BROKE MY BACK SOME YEARS AGO,HAD A LOT OF TIME TO THINK ABOUT THINGS.I HAVE SOME MEMORIES OF SOMETHING HAPPENED AS A KID OF ABOUT 6 YEARS OLD.I WAS SLEEPING WITH MY FATHER,THIS ID NOT HAPPEN OFFEN.I WOKE UP IN THE MIDDLE OF THE NIGHT,IT WAS WARM,LOOK OUT OF THE WINDOW,I SAW A BRIGHT LIGHT OF MANY COLORS GOING UP OUT OF OUR BACK YARD.I SWEAR IT LOOKED LIKE ACROSS.I WOKE MY FATHER UP AND TOLD HIM SOMETHING JUST FLEW UP OUT OF THE YARD.HE GOT UP ON ONE ARM AND LOOKED OUT THE WINDOW,BUT IT WAS GONE.I TOLD HIM IT WAS THERE.HE TOLD ME TO GO BACK TO SLEEP,IT WAS JUST THE GOODYEAR BLIMP.THIS WAS ABOUT 1951,1952.THIS WAS LATE AT NIGHT,AND I THINK IT WAS BEFORE THE BLIMP.I HAVE HEARD MANY STORYS FROM PEOPLE OVER THE YEARS,OF WHAT THEY HAVE SEEN.I FEEL BETTER TELLING YOU MY STORY.
