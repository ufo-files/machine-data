Low altitude oval craft. Steady white light on perimeter, center pulsating red.

I thought this would be the best place to report what my wife and I saw many years ago but never forgot.

My wife has always been more observant than me, thankfully, or I probably would have missed this. We were out for an evening walk with a friend and I was chatting with him when my future wife remarked "What's that?". She apparently heard a hum and looked up to see where it was coming from. I hadn't noticed the sound because I was talking, but when I stopped and looked I could hear a low frequency, steady hum, like an electrical transformer. But what we saw was inexplicable. There was a large craft slowly moving from east to west about 100-150 feet off the ground. It was almost directly overhead when we first saw it and watched it move off to the west for at least a minute maybe more. It was oblong shaped, about 200ft by 100ft with small white lights around its perimeter and a larger red pulsating light located in it middle on bit of a bump. When the red light lit up we could see the body of the craft was a reflective metallic surface.

I have always been a keen student of aeronautical studies and have no explanation for what we saw. Of course anyone we relate this story to regards it as crazy. Whatever!
