on my way back to college, early one spring morning while descending a large hill going toward the village of mohawk,new york from sharon springs new york I observed the following. A bright light just above the tree line, followed my vehicle all the way down the mt. It never got any higher or lower then the tree line and stayed right with my car to the bottom of the hill just as I was entering the village. It was just starting to turn light out. I was so scared I pulled over finally by a telephone pole near the bottom of the hill near a apartment complex. I exited my car and the light stopped dead in its tracks for a second and then shot straight up in the air and stopped dead in its tracks. At this time it was turning light out and I could see a bell shape object hovering in a set spot It was silver in color and was spinning like a top All of a sudden it shot straight up and turned a 90 degree angle and was gone. I never told anyone about this because I had applied for a job with the F B I and was having a back ground check being done I did not want them to think I was nuts. I got back in my car and continued to Utica New york to campus at mohawk valley community college and never told anyone I only thought of saying anything at this time is because I watched one of your programs about the bell shape craft that crashed in Pa.years back because it looked just like that bell shaped object that I saw.
