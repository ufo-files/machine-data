(GS) Blue white light got brighter as it got lower

1. Looking out my bedroom window
2. Was looking at plane going over and saw a light starting in the south going south east losing elevation and getting a lot brighter.
3. A meteor at first but I have seen thousands of meteors just saw over 60 in the last shower this was moving to fast for a plane and to slow to be a meteor no smoke trail just a ball of blue white light.
4.First sighted almost due south dim blue white light moving fast more than doubled in brightness and size as it got lower heading SE moved in a straight line descending.
5. Ok what the hell was that stood there a few more mins and went back to bed.
6.Its path and angle took it to where the barn blocked the view of it.
