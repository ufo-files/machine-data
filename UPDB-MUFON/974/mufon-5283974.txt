I observed a bright colored orb like object against a cloudless blue sky.

While testing a new 500 mm camera lens in my backyard in preparation for a upcoming cruise I elected to photograph a passenger plane flying in a S/E direction about 70 degrees above the horizon. Immediately behind the plane I noticed a shiny, bright colored orb-like object zigzagging very fast near the back end of the plane about 500 ft away.  I decided to see if I could photograph it and pressed the shutter button only once. A few minutes later I checked my photos and I realized that I successfully photographed it and the object fortunately was in focus. In the many years I’ve been an avid student of UFOs it didn’t occur to me that this object could be a UFO until a few minutes later. I originally thought it was a reflection off of the plane or someone pointing a light source at the plane. 
A closer look at the circular object of my completed photo revealed a shadowy line near the bottom half and a grey halo like, darker color around its circumference. The object completely disappeared in about 4-5 secs.
Weather /conditions: it was a completely cloudless, sunny sky with no other objects visible nearby. There was no wind nor any other objects (ie birds, balloons, other planes) nearby or even far away. The temp was about 60-62 F.
