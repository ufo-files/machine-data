A glowing (red orange/gold) oval looking craft moving North then west and performed a evasive zigzag maneuver, unlike anything seen, and disappeared like it took off into the sky/ space. Having served in the U.S. Air Force, I have experience with military aircraft and commercial aircraft. What we saw was not a know craft, and did not move traditional. The craft seemed like it was hoovering but still moving, almost surreal, like a cloud moving through the sky yet it was not a cloud. As the UFO was moving west an aircraft (medium sized propeller plane) was approaching from north- north west, when it got closer the UFO performed a zigzag maneuver and took off into sky toward space at an incredible rate of speed and disappeared/ dematerialized. The aircraft that was approaching should have been able to see the UFO clearly. My daughter, wife and I were in shock and complete disbelief. We discussed what we all witnessed to ensure we all saw the same thing. It was a very impactful experience from all of us and it was my second UFO sighting in Florida.
