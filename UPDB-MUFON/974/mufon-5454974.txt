{tjd} A long and seemingly unending series of glowing orbs for 8 1/2 minutes

I was checking some work e-mails when my mother in-law called my wife to tell her there was something weird happening in the sky and to head out to the driveway.

We rushed out to see a long line of steadily glowing white orbs just drifting off very quickly in one direction, starting a few degrees above the neighbors house and just below what I originally assumed was the North Star (until I realized I was facing West towards the mountains and now I'm unsure what I was looking at since it had the apparent luminosity of the Moon but was in a completely different direction than Polaris, and then would move silently up and to the right until they disappeared somewhere around the Big Dipper.

This process lasted for the 8 1/2 minutes that we were outside with the majority of the orbs almost equally spaced apart with only a few momentary breaks.
