Tubular Ufo Just above tree line.

I was taking pictures of the skyline and noticed this object in the sky. As it came over head it looked semi transparent. It had no noticable wings and was tubular in shape. These type of craft come through this area quite often. However this one was rather large. It was about 30 to 40 feet in length with a dark band in the center and on one end. I didn't know what to make of it. Just thought folks would be interested in seeing this and maybe someone else has seen this before.
