My experience occurred around 14 years ago and I just cant forget it so I thought it would be helpful to describe it. Around 2 in the morning I was awakened from sleep and there was a large brightly lit disc object anchored over my bedroom. There was hieroglyphic like writing on all sides of the disc. I felt paralyzed and was surrounded by alien beings who were doing some sort of operation on me and inserted some sort of object in the area of my right ovary. it was painful but I wasnt particularly scared. A few months later I was having pain in my abdomen and the colonoscopy detailed the lack of continual vascular markings in the same area where i felt something was placed. I never went further with this but have always wondered what happened to me that night.

Before I moved to this location in Cambria I had also experienced some telepathic contact with some sort of intelligence. I am less comfortable talking about this but feel it could be related.
