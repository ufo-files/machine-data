Large red sphere with very large light/comtrail arching to left and right.

I was out in my backyard (Carlsbad, CA) and a very interesting flying object caught my eye to the South (off coast heading inland). I estimate it was about 20 miles away, about 3 miles in altitude and flying fast (much faster than other aircraft in the sky). It had a very large red light or orb with two large arching spans of light/contrail in the shape of a fan trailing behind. The edges of the fan/arch had different colors and the interior of the fan was white. I observed it for about 7 seconds and then it completely disappeared; reappearing after a few seconds 1 for another 4-5 seconds before it was gone. The sky was clear - no clouds/fog. I could see stars and other aircraft. When I first saw I didn't think it was a meteor, it didn't look like a plane, maybe a rocket having problems? It was not a helicopter or typical plane.  



Just thought I1d pass it along to see if anyone else out there saw it!
