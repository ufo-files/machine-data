{jpg} Bright round light the size of a bean of held at arms length no flashing lights changed direction side to side

I was saying outside smoking a cigarette and taking advantage of the lack of light pollution and admiring satellites. Over my head crossed a three light triangular object. It was flashing red and white lights each in a triangular pattern. I ran inside to grab my friend to observe. She came out and quickly dismissed it as a plane. Albeit a silent plane flying much higher than 20000 feet. Suddenly she said what's that! I looked over only to see a bright, glowing light about the star of a pea or small bean held at arms length slowly creeping across the sky. It was headed NE but was changing direction from side to side while maintaining a path due NE. My friend asked if we should take a picture but told her it's like taking a picture of the moon and will just be a small white dot. Hardly verifiable evidence. I watched the object move slowly NE for about two minutes until it disappeared behind trees.
