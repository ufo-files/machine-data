flashing red (1) and white (2) lights observed traveling in unison.

At 5-13 am on jan 28th 2014 I stepped outside to start my car before going to work. I observed an object that had a bright red blinking light in the front a brilliant blinking tungesten white light in the middle and a smaller white light to the end. My first thought was that this maybe a plane coming in to land at the Calgary airport, but as it passed over it made no noise at all. I have seen many planes come into Calgary and from my position I would say this object was much lower than I have seen any plane aproach before. If this was a plane it would have been about 3 times as long as a comercial jet. Also I have never seen a plane with a red flasing light on the nose. It moved from the s.e. to the n.w., from my position It would have missed the airport by a good 15 miles. It maintained a steady altitude and speed and I watched it until it moved out of sight. I did have an uneasy feeling as it passed over me and felt some kind of pressure in my chest.
