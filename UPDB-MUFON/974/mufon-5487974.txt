{rjl} First observed object as sparkling lights that appeared to change shape.

My wife and I were traveling east on i-20 and at around the Bobby Jones(i-520) exit saw what appeared to be sparkling lights to the left(north). We observed the object as we drove and around the Washington road exit it appeared to become two objects, both with the same appearance of sparkling lights and changing shape. We pulled off the interstate at the River Watch Parkway exit for a closer observation and there were approx. 20 other vehicles also pulled over viewing these objects as they passed over i-20 moving towards the south east. We took one picture as the objects were farther off in the distance but both were still visible just above the treeline. Unfortunately we did not get video ourselves but there have been a couple of nice videos posted to youtube of the same event at the same approx time and location which have images and descriptions which match what we saw very well.
