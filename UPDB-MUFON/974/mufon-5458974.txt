{jpg} Bluish-green light spotted in Coalinga, CA parallel to 5 freeway south

Driving on the 5 freeway south my friend who was driving and I witnessed a bluish-green light in the sky parallel to the road. It was too slow to be a comet and didn’t have any trail. For about 4 seconds it was in the sky, disappeared for 1, then came back for 2 seconds.
