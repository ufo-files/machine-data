5 bright yellow stationary orbs in formation.

I was pulling out of the 4th parking lot south of the Blue Water Bridge when I saw straight ahead of me five bright yellow orbs in perfect formation, north to south. There were 3 then a space the size of a missing one, then two more, sort of like OO OOO. I was driving, but kept watching them. They didn't seem to move. As I looked at the road to turn, they just disappeared. No idea what they were or if anyone else saw them.
