{cak} helicopter chasing object

I was in my backyard taking the trash out. I heard a helicopter that sounded very loud and was traveling fast. It looked like a small object speeding away from the helicopter in the same path.

It first appeared as the helicopter was about halfway past my full line of sight. It looked like it was traveling at a steady speed for less than a second and then sped off very fast and left a trail behind it and then disappeared. I was scared because and I’m still bothered by this event because I know it couldn’t have been a meteor. I really want to know what it was and want to find out if anyone else is reporting this or have seen it. I know I can’t be the only persons attention that the low flying helicopter grabbed. It left a trail and then disappeared. I lost sight of the helicopter because of the treetops about 2 seconds after the object disappeared.
