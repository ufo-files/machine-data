{cak} Blinking lights Random movement and patterns.

It was dark out so I went for a walk as a usually do. I saw a few airplanes pass over head like normal. I could tell what they were by the way their lights blinked. I like looking at the stars when I walk but one seemed to stand out to me. As I walked I noticed something was off about it. I couldn't put my finger on it but this one star had me confused. As I watched it it went from a solid red standing still to flashing different colors. I first thought well it's just a plane until the colors went from the usual red and blinking to a random pattern of blinks and colors ranging from blue to red to black and white. All while still in the same spot. I stopped and tried to record it on my IPod but it was too dark. Then it started to move slowly. It moved so slow. I can't stress that enough. I've never seen anything move that slow. The lights would seem to flash in a pattern then the pattern would change to something else. As it crossed the treeline I started home to call my brother. Then I looked into the sky again and saw two more objects. This time no lights, no sound, nothing. Just two black planes flying the same direction fast. I went inside and called my brother baffled about what I had just saw.
