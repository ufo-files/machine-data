Orange sphere ufo

I was in my livin groom when I saw a light out of the corner of my eye. I had a look and thought it was a meteor, so grabbed my phone and went on to the balcony to see if I could record it. I soon realised that it was not a meteor as it had no trail and did not burn up. The object appeared to be moving at speed as it crossed the whole sky in about 3 minutes. in the video the object appears to flicker, but this is because it was filmed through a net on the balcony. It actually appeared solid. I'm certain it was not a Chinese lantern as it moved fast, looked large, and did not waver in any form. I filmed for a couple of minutes, then observed it fly over the nearby airport and then vanish. The day after I did a search for ufo sightings and found that there was a sighting of the same object from the iss on the same day.
