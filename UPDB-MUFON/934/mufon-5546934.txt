Unknown object appears in picture.

This past Saturday afternoon, it had rained most of the day, and shortly after 5:00pm (cdt), the clouds started to break. My wife noticed a rainbow as she looked out the front (east) of the house. I walked outside to see it closer, and was able to see the "end" of the rainbow in the field across the street.
I called for her to bring her camera (which we didn't have on us), so she took a couple of pictures with her Blackberry. When she started to look at the first picture she took, she noticed an unidentified image right above the treeline (to the left of the rainbow) that neither of us saw when she took the picture. The second picture she took (immediately after the first picture) did not show that image.
Ironically, no more than 15 minutes later, we heard a jet/jets in the distance. This is in a part of Kentucky where it's rare to see (or hear) any kind of aircraft in the sky.
We're having a hard time determining what this object is. Just kind of eerie.
