{jpg} Up to five white orbs drifting at an unknown distance for about 16 seconds.

I have several pictures over a very short period of 16 seconds. The orbs began drifting across the evening sky (around sunset). First two appeared on the left, then four, then five, all moving from left to right. I was taking pictures of the sunset, which is often spectacular, at the top of Winter Hill facing Arlington, MA. I didn't even realize they were there until my wife pointed them out to me in the photographs. (She said they must be "drones.") Their apparent size I would describe as lentil- or pea-size. I know the total time was only 16 seconds because my iPhone camera recorded the duration. Unfortunately, I was not able to upload them (there were five good ones) because of difficulties figuring out how to navigate my MacBook's file system.
