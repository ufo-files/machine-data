(GS) Fighting bucks outside my slider window woke me up, got my cell phone & went outside to follow the bucks where stopped in frt. yd. looking up then followed a bright lite wsw of us.

09Dec2010, Appx: 1am, Main St. Saratoga, WY. Woke up because bucks were fighting outside my slider & hit the glass. Got my cellphone, & quietly went out the frt. door & slowly followed them toward the street. The were focused on something to the wsw of my place & on the other side of the street. Followed to the street the town went black & I was able to get this shot basically across the street but still above us. The deer werent scared & I wasnt either. Then the light vanished & with in a minute or so the town lights came back on.       

When I took the photo all that was visiable was the bright lite, nothing else was visible. I emailed it to myself later that day to make sure that I could save it if something would happen to the phone. With not having a computer at the time I didn't open the file until last May 2012 & got a major surprise. Some how the camera had been able to stop time presay or the shutter speed was just slow enough to get thur the bright lite & get a clear shot of the craft with the lights behind it, in the shape of the craft.
