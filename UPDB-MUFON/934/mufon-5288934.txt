Glowing Blue Orb again!

I had filed a report some years ago. Possibly 4 at the max. At that time I lived at Another location It was across from a swampy inlet and at that time myself and my girlfriend saw a glowing blue orb. It appeared and faded. I thought maybe it was a one time thing. But the orb seems to have followed me to my new address. This time I had my phone and just happened to take pictures! I just happen to see it while taking pictures of a cool looking moon scene. The first image was taken with a flash. The others after had the flash off. The orb is seen clearly moving in the images in succession. I have an IPhone 11. The orb ends its flight with a slight distortion before shooting up into the sky rapidly and becoming invisible. 
The images will start from 1 and on as In order of being taken. Strung together you see it’s flight path and intentional direction of movement.
