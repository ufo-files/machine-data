{cak} I saw a light in the distance heading towards my location.

Standing in the parking lot of my apartment complex on a clear night at 5:00am (EST) after work. I was looking at all the stars I could see when I noticed a light moving across the sky, I thought the object was a small airplane or helicopter but noticed no blinking light pattern just one solid light that was headed in my direction like a north eastern direction. I remember thinking get out your phone and take some video of what I am looking at, so that is what I did. I lost sight of the object when the light went out, even with my naked eye I could not see this object against the night sky. btw the audio in the video you can hear is that of the interstate highway.
