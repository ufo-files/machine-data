{nd} came above cloud line below and left of moon then disappeared

Decided to take a picture of the moon and the way the cloud line looked under the moon. At the time of snapping the photo, the object flew up from behind the cloud line flew towards the moon and curved abruptly and shot back behind the cloud line where it came from. The whole thing took about 3 seconds and I captured the photo. It was very very fast and appeared to be at least 30,000-60,000 ft. up (maybe higher).

The speed of the object (craft like object) and the speed in which it changed directions allows me to believe that it was neither a jet / airplane nor a meteorite.
