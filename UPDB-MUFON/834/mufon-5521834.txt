Three lights with one blinking behind the two closer. The two bright lights got closer and emerged into one

Went outside to my truck and saw three bright lights. One light was blinking like a plane would. I heard no noise. They seemed to be quite low for planes/jets and most likely wouldve heard them. It seemed that the one blinking light was tailing the two closer lights. As the two brighter lights flew further they became closer until it emerged into one. The one blinking that was following seemed to stay behind or distant until the two emerged, At one point, before the two emerged, it seemed as the two brighter lights hovered and then took off into the distance. I watched until the one blinking and two/one (which emerged) went horizontal and off into the distance where we could no longer see them.
