{cak} Multiple Fireballs

I was in my backyard approximately 7:30-8:00 pm, when I noticed a glowing red ball in the distance, coming from the horizon, heading in my direction. I stared at it in disbelief as it came closer, appearing larger and brighter as it approached. There was no sound from this object, no blinking lights, just a large, pulsating ball of glowing red-orange light. This object flew directly over my home, I went inside to get my wife and children, who then witnessed this too. As we were watching, another identical object approached from the same location as the 1st (just miles from a nuclear plant), and was on the exact course as the first, flying directly over our home. We watched as it flew in a northeasttward direction, toward the river. Then, one after another, they came, 5 in total, all on the exact same course, disappearing over the treetops and out of view. A neighbor had come out by this time, and was able to witness the 5th "fireball". My wife had recorded 2 of the objects flying over our house, as with no streetlights in our neighborhood, they were very bright and showed well on camera.
