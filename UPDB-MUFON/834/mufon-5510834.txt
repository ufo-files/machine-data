A few friends & i were on a train bridge looking across the horizon of a local airfield when the lights rose from the horizon

A few friends & i were on a train bridge looking across the horizon of white waltham aerodrome (maidenhead) when a dozen or more lights appeared on the horizon & slowly rose up into the sky then stopped at varied heights and just sat there, being that we were all quite young at the time we were obviously afraid, so we all started running home both excited and scared at what we had seen, our parents blamed low flying planes as been as we saw it over a small local airfield, but i know aeroplanes don't rise up and stop in mid flight, ive seen another sighting since then, but never seen the same one as that summer night since.
