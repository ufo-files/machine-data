{rjl} Large Bright Orb of Blue-White Light

I was watching a movie in my room, on the second floor of my house at night. My attention was taken away from the movie when I saw a huge glowing light glide above the street from my bedroom window. I was completely silent, too. The object moved from right to left in my field of vision at a slower pace. It seemed to be headed up into the mountains. I knew it wasn't a car because there was no noise and it was a singular orb of light that was too bright for headlights. Also, I wouldn't have been able to see the street from where I was sitting. I immediately got up and tried to see where it went and then asked my boyfriend and our friend if they had seen it as well. They in fact had seen a shorter glimpse of it than I had. I live in the hills of Lake Elsinore near the Ortega Hwy where there are a lot of mountains. We do not have street lights in my area so the object was very easy to see. Also, our roommate pulled into our driveway shortly after saying he saw a blue light as he was driving up the hill to our house. We stood outside, a little shaken up, to see if we would catch any other activity. It was a bizarre experience. I have never seen anything quite like it in my life. Didn't see anything else yet. Freaky.
