{cak} Silver sphere, 3-4 ft. no sound, right angle movement, hovering, extreme acceleration.

Sunshiny mid Oct afternoon in 1974. I was having my house roof painted and the painter came to the crest of the roof. Looking up at him, I noted to his left a silver object ascending slightly above the tree tops, but silouhetted against the trees. At first I thought it was a balloon, but it stopped and hovered for 15-20 seconds and moved slowly and silently at a right angle across an open field toward me at a height of approximately 200-300 feet until it reached the power line. There it hovered for approximately 30 seconds. It then shot straight up about 400 feet in the blink of an eye. It hovered for a moment, then it accelerated to the east so suddenly and rapidly that I could not follow it with my eyes. The painter, who was on the roof, saw it as it crossed the field and exclaimed 'what is that!'. My son, twelve years of age at the time, also saw it from another angle, across the street.
At no time did the object make any sounds, emit any lights or exhaust. 

I am an Air Force veteran of the Korean War and am familiar with high performance air craft of the time. The objects performance and acceleration far exceeded anything I had ever observed while in the Air Force. 

For years I have wondered what I saw and am at a loss to explain it. 

I have watched your programs on TV and thought it may be of interest to you.
