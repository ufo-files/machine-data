{cak} Appeared randomly and flew with glowing white light then lowered

I was taking a picture of the star Zaniah when I saw something appear to the right of the star. I put my star map app over the object and it was not recognize. It began traveling and that is when I began filming it. It went from southwest to south east slowly moving with a white bright light that did not flicker or change shape. It was unlike a meteor movement. It also did not have a tail. It did not make any sound. I do live near the coast guard base however the object was not in that direction and helicopters or planes from that distance have always made sound and/or had flashing lights or wavering lights. The object began lowering itself and then disappeared behind the trees. I was excited to have a phone on me but I was skeptical as to what the object was. I posted online and none of my friends had an option to offer. I was hoping to see if there was another person spotting the object at the same time.
