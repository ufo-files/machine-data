During a smoke break, I was trying out the slow motion video on my camera. I decided to film the rain mist falling down from the sky and when I went back to look at the slow motion video, there was an object in it. I didn't see anything with the naked eye, only after looking at the video later, did I see it. I would have wrote it off as a fluke, but the rain falls in front of it, and smoke drifts in front of it as well. In addition, I took 2 slow motion videos in succession and the second video does not contain the object.
