UFO moved from south to north. Two separate incidents.

We were in a car driving west on 750 Southland st at about 11:20pm. Saw a object move from left to right about 60% from the horizon and disappear, it was moving south to north. Went home to the corner of Poxson and MLK parked north, got out of the car and saw another object traveling south to north about 80% from horizon this one lasted longer about 5 minutes before disappearing behind a cloud. Both objects were moving faster than a satellite.
