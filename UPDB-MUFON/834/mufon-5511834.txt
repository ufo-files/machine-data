{dr}Two brightly lit orange-yellowish lights hovering diagonally off in the distance

I was walking over towards the front door and as I looked up out of the screen door I saw two bright orange-yellowish bright lights hovering at a distance; one diagonally above the other; just seemed to float in midair and remained stationary for about 2min. then the lower light slowly descended and the intensity of the light began to decrease and eventually went out as it descended; then the higher light descended and slowly flickered out. There was a police helipcopter flying around in the area at the same time shining its searchlight down onto the streets below but it did not seem to notice the bright lights off in the distance or at least it did not react to them at all.
