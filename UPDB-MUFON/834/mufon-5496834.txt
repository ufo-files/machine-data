Upon one evening in early 1973 my sister and I observed a giant X shaped object hover over woods behind our house and move slowly over and behind to the right and down over the trees. The giant X had large glowing circles covering it. We didn't think much about that evening until I remembered it several years later I questions we both suppressed it to this day I remember it like yesterday but my sister has no recollection. There were no beams just glowing halo around the X and the circles on the surface.
