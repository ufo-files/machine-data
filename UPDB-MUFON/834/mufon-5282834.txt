Reminded me of a boat in the sky i could see structure and a bright window looking like

I was having a coffee before work at my favorite spot to take day break and sunrise photos . When i could see day break was approaching i aimed my camera phone towards the sunrise horizon and waited for the sun to illuminate the edge of the horizon. As soon as it did i snapped a pic . When it got a little better glow i aimed again but as i snapped the pic i saw something else on the screen and when i looked out to the water i saw it. No sound , it didnt fly into place it was just there . The water below it was lighter and smooth .... it was gone seconds later . It didnt fly away or blink away , it just vanished . I have no idea what it was i just know it was there , it was silent and it was solid . The only weird thing i remember was the birds werent singing like the normally did at day break but within minutes of it vanishing they started singing again
