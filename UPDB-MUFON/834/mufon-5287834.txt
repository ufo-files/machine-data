It was slow and steady, Hummed close to a Drop D sound on guitar

Years ago when I was staying at my dads in oklahoma, I was watching tv late at night at like 2:30 am. Dad was at work and i had the house to myself as usual. I was watching an old black and white movie, night of the living dead, or one of the variants. Then i hear it. At the time I didnt know the tune, but years later after learning to play guitar, i closely associate it with Drop D. A powerful low resonating sound. 
 I pause the tv, Thinking at first maybe it was on tv. But no. It was so powerful i could almost feel it in my bones. I was so damn curious what it was i went outside in the front yard, my ears pinpointed it towards the west. And in that same moment i pinpoint the sound coming from the sky towards the west. I look up and i see it.. or.. rather, I don't see it. It was so damn dark, all i saw were the stars and then a great slow blackness creeping closer to covering them. Dim lights. Such dim lights my eyes could barely see them. I dart to eyes between the 3 lights to try and focus on them, but they were so blurry, so dim. middle of this The blackest black in even the night. Covering the stars as it took its time passing directly over me. It was hard to tell how big it was, because i couldn't judge how high up it was. My eyes couldn't judge, but my body belt it was so close to me, the hum constant. It took it maybe 2 minutes -Maybe less to pass from the west to the east as its hum faded into the distance.
