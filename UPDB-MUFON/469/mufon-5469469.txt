{td} Two orbs sighted flying over Tucson

I stepped outside one evening and saw an orb moving across the sky. It was not an airplane and was moving quite fast. I had my phone in hand and recorded the event. 
The orb flew silently from west to southeast across Tucson in less than two minutes. I calculate it was moving at about 400 mph or more, considering the distance and time I was able to see the object. It made no noise.
An airplane flew behind me and you can hear it on the first video as it comes overhead of me about halfway through the video. 
There was a second orb, about ten or fifteen minutes later. I recorded it also. IF you watch the entirety of both videos you can get an idea of the scale of the object and the distance it flew. I have used a map to determine the airspeed and both objects appear to easily be cruising over 400 mph. 
The second object flew in a similar path as the first object and looked identical to the first object. It was also completely silent.
