{nd} Bright flamming red light ,comming from eastern skys and moving towards NE

On April 19,2013 ,I was out side in my front yard enjoying the night sky with my dogs.It was about 8:50pm it was time for us to go inside, as i was about to put the dogs back in the house. I noticed a bright flaming light bigger than the stars. it appeared in the east side of the sky, i then quickly ran inside to get my iPhone to video tape it. when i got back the object was going NE direction, i videoed the object till it was just out of sight. This was not a falling star this object had direction.
