FOR LEAST 8 HOURS

[Abduction Case]
Liala McInnes said that she was taking her bins out at 5:30pm, whereby she saw a huge bright translucent light in the sky above her. It made no noise. It didn't look like any man-made craft. She took a photo of it from below. Picture is attached. It is blurry but one can see three distinct shapes/lights, almost joined. Photo not reliable as no landmark in photo to see size etc. 

She said that she sensed it was communicating with her, as when she moved to the right, it moved to the right, and when she moved to the left, it moved to the left. Her neighbour was also there and also witnessed it. She too tried to take a photo of it by lying on the floor. 
She soon returned inside whereby she claims she had 3 hour missing time.
She went to bed, woke up and was very exhausted and weak the next day. She also noticed markings on her body and bruises, which she says she still has to this day. They look like bruises but are not. Little spots by her ankles is the skin a different colour as well as the skin colour is greyish.
She went shopping, and felt like she "was not with it". She then collapsed in centre, and was taken to hospital. She claims that she made the mistake of telling the doctors of her experience and they placed her in a pshyche unit.

(However, it did come out in another conversation that there is a reason for her admittance to unit, not just because of her describing her experience).
