I was awakened by my alarm clock. I get up and start to get my things ready to go to court. i jump in the shower and i looked outside the window to see if it was raining since it was cloudy. Thats when i noticed a black oval looking object. I thought it might have been a trash bag, but it seemed to big to be a bag. I kept on looking at it trying to figure out what it could be. It moved from left to right then it started going towards the mountains. I was very excited to witness this since i've witnessed other things in the sky in the past. I kept on looking at the object and i started to loose sight of it as it kept going farther. I got out the shower and told my girlfriend about it. Then i drew it on a dry erase board. i turned on the news and started watching them. I saw a story about a police helicopter that had to make an emergency stop because the emergency lights went off. i thought it might have been coincidental since i've heard that whenever theres UFOS near they mess up their equipment. Hopefully there's someone else out there that saw it too,(in pomona or around pomona).
