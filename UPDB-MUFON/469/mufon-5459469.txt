{tjd} Two lights vertically aligned

Two star like lights vertically aligned in the south southwest sky that appeared to odd when we first noticed because of their brightness. The top light was bright white while the lower light was smaller and had orangish tint.  Once our eyes fixed on them it was obvious that those lights were ascending much quicker than the rest of stars in sky. After moving up about 10-15degrees in sky the lights simply disappeared. These star like objects were traveling in the opposite direction of the rest of the stars.  One note is that these two lights had some distant between them, probably at least 5 degrees difference.
