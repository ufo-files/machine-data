It was May of 1994 what day exactly I do not remember. My father and I were in our back yard watching the moon eclipse. We decided to go out front, we were looking up when we saw a very bright blue violet ball like objects move into our line of sight, over the 7-foot wall, just right about it! First there was one object, then two in a straight line, then six objects. The objects then move into a triangle shape, then they flatten out (straight line) then an oval shape then disappeared.

On the way home my phone rang and the girl that I just dropped off, called me and told me she was looking at a UFO! she told me It was yellow and the shape of a diamond, and it was in between the clouds. It then moved toward East Fork Lake. A beacon (from the Lake) sent the craft in the lake in a different direction.
