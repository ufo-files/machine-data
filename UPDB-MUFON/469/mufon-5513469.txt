{nd} Noticed 3 vertically linear lights in the sky while driving. Stopped to observe.

We were driving home from a Christmas Party when I noticed 3 vertically linear lights in the sky. I first thought they were planes but realized that they were clustered too close together and were stationary.

We pulled into a parking lot to observe them. Within a few minutes they winked out one at a time.
