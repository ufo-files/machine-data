'star' moved one way, then suddenly faded and shot away

I was taking my dog out into the garden before bed, around 9pm. I looked up, as ok usually do, to see what stars were visible. In the northern sky were two visible stars. A dim one and a brighter one below it. The bright star looked very bright. Then I noticed it was moving towards me, away from the other star. My initial thought was it must be a plane, with its front night light / flood light on. But then literally a second later it went very dim and I saw it shoot straight up in the air and vanish. I kept watching for a few minutes but nothing appeared after that. The sky was very clear. A bit later I took our dog out again and I saw a plane go over with its lights visible and I could see it looked nothing like the single light I saw earlier.
