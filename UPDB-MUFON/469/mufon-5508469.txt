Moved slowly, highly across sky like a star, curved then slowly disappeared

Standing on my back porch I saw what looked like a star moving slowly across the sky but quicker than usual while at a steady pace. It started in the West and moved to the East (over the course of 1-3 minutes) at what looked like a higher altitude than a plane but maybe a satellite but what convinced me it wasnt a satellite was when it went to the East and slowly disappeared, losing its glow and turning into a pinpoint of a light before disappearing. I witnessed this myself while my girlfriend and one of my best friends walked to the store together. When they got back they, without me asking or prompting them, asked me if I had seen the same thing.
