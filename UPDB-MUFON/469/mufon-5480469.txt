Some kind of foo fighter, first we tought was a drone, but wasn't

It was about 8:00 PM, we were near the Régis Bittencourt roadway, then we saw a really bright red light, first we tought it was a drone, something like that, but the intensity of the light was so strong that it couldn't be powered by small batteries or be LEDs, I would rate the speed as 30MPH, travelled about 0.8MILE(1.2 KM) going behind our motorcycle and disappeared under a small hill, it lasted under our eyes for about a minute, unfortunatelly we decided not to follow that, since we would have to invade some property, and that place is known to be a violent neighborhood, not exactly a favela, but a poor neighborhood.
I would describe the light being something like a foo fighter, but had a constant bright, no bluring, no pulsing, kept the same red vivid color, like a red traffic light with a really powerful light. It's size was about 1 feet, appeared suddenly behind some trees, more or less 60ft(20M) of altitude and kept this altitude until dissaperaing from our eyes. It wasn't either a navigation light of an aircraft since we would've seen the aircraft.
