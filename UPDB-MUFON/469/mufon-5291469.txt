Three moving round objects in sky

In NW sky about 11:15 pm I saw a round object. It moved to the Western sky. Looks like it is rotating, white with red lights. Then in the West another comes up from behind the hill. It was white with a lot of red flashing. Lost sight of first one. Then in the Northern sky I saw one that was white & green, some red & looks like was rotating. Then they disappeared.
