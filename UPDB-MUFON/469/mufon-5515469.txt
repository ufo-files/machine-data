{cak} Helicopter chasing blue light

Okay so I was trying to fall asleep and heard a helicopter. I normally look to see what theyre up to anyways, and this helicopter sounded really close to my house like it was flying over my roof. So when I look out the window I see the helicopter chasing a light blue sphere across the sky and they were bookin it. It was amazing. I tried to wake up my boyfriend but he was fast asleep and by the time he woke up angrily all he could hear was a faint helicopter propellers. They were both right above tree tops and this light in comparison to a helicopter was the same size as it. I have seen a couple unidentifiable objects since living in the Motherlode area, and it is really interesting to me. I am glad I got to see it. Hopefully next time I can get some footage.
