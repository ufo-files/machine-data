{rjl} A tictac looking light appeared out of nowhere. Then it cruised around in the sky above us until it was gone

My husband & I were in our backyard trying to see the meteor shower that was reportedly going to happen. We never saw meteors, but one tictac looking light appeared out of no where above us, really high up. We couldn't see an actual shape of the craft itself, but it went NW in the sky & it appeared to be lightly bouncing back & forth. It was going way faster than a plane & didn't move like one. The light faded & it was gone. This was the brightest one we saw. 
There was at least one more that we both saw. It was higher up in the sky & moved the same way. Then, we eventually couldn't see it anymore (too far)
