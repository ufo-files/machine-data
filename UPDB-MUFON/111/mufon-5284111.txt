Green glowing object riding just atop the water off Longboat Key.

This is an object that I have seen twice in my lifetime. Once was in the fall of 2004 which made such an impact upon me that I have visited the area 4 additional times since in hopes of possibly seeing it again. The first time I had someone with me and she recorded a detailed description of what she saw as well as I doing the same. 

That second time I was with another person and it occurred on 9-26-21 on a Sunday evening at approx 10:30 PM. We had purposely been on the beach looking for this object that I had told my friend about many times as I had originally seen it in 2004. It was clearly the same object as I had told her exactly what I had seen the first time and she is the one who pointed it out first.

Once again it was in a shape similar to the recent reports of tic tacs as observed by Naval Personel. It was traveling from North to South at approximately 25 mph and was about 75 feet from the shore. The original object that I saw in 2004 was closer to the shore. It was an intense glowing greenish shape just like the one in 2004 and also like another one on the internet that was in the internet publication IBtimes which looked exactly like this one. It was very bright, glowing and made no noise at all.

I have recently seen on the web another report of an object very similar in color and shape. It was seen from a boat and the greenish object had people commenting that they were really seeing something special and amazing. 

I have looked on the web for another example of what I saw and the only one similar was the one that can be found on the internet if you look for a green USO off the coast of Miami.
