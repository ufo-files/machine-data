(GS) Very bright glowing/flaming orange object moving slowly over house

My boyfriend and I just walked up the driveway after a trip to the store and he spotted this very bright, radiating orange object moving from the left side of his house to the right side of his house and then either faded into a red colored, tiny speck of light or zoomed in one direction extremely fast, making it seem like it was fading. Either way, the object was very close, did not make a sound and did not pulsate, but remained glowing.
