{jpg} multiple orbs scatter everywhere then I seen a triangular craft

I was driving west bound on the main highway that goes across the state and I witnessed a huge comet looking type thing burst across the sky then explode into a bunch of orbs or circular crafts that scattered across each other then disappeared. About 20-30 minutes later I felt like something was watching me so I looked out my window and saw an orb floating closer to my vehicle. I kept driving and started taking pictures, then before I knew it I was looking at some sort of triangular craft that had lights around the sides and a blue light underneath the carriage area. The craft hovered and followed me for about 3-4 hours until I got to the border of Nebraska then disappeared. During the experience I also noted that maybe 20-30 minutes after it was dark there was helicopter lights circling or following the craft all the way until I got to the border, not too sure if it was military or not? 

Pictures are listed in the order I took them. The first three shows the orb as it got closer to my vehicle. The last three pictures shows the craft (unidentifiable flying object).
