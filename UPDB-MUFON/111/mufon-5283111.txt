5 individual flashing lights green and orange hovering then moving straight path

Coming home from work traveling down Starling Airport Rd. the first 2 lights were seen hovering from car, then two more when arrived home were seen from house, then a fifth. Lights were hovering, flashing, and seen through binoculars seemed to have an energy field surrounding them. The hovering lights seemed to radiate light, were green/orange, no sound, no EMF. The first two moved south, the single light eventually moved NE, and the final two lights moved SE towards Arnold but near the Meramac River. Movement was approx. the speed of an airplane in a steady path. Two neighbors also witnessed the unknown light. Video and photos were taken and will be submitted.
