{cak} Over 10 lights flying in straight formation going SW

At about 6pm 11/12/19 l was outside in the front of my house when l noticed over 10 small lights travelling in straight formation for a period of about 10-15 seconds at an angle of of about 90 over the horizon. At first l thought it was a flock of geess but discarted after seen then going straight and not changing pace or distance. I ran to the back of the house calling my wife to see rhem but by the time l came to the back they were gone!. The moon was rising and there were stars shinning so no confusion with that. There is an airport near by but also discarted that as l seen them every night. These lights were travelling in unison and at a fast speed.
Never seen anything lime these before. Besides back in NY where me and my son and my neighbor's son saw two sliver bullets one over the the other ( bigger over smaller) travelling at an altitude of about 500 feet above ground without any sound emitting.
