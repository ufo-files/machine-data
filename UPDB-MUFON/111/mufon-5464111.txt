{jpg} Bright star like object hovering in sky. Under clouds.

Driving south on I79 from grove city pa. Around the slippery rock exit I seen a bright object in the sky in the distance. Clouds were covering the whole sky so it was below that elevation. I started videoing but couldn1t get a long clear shot due to intermittent obstruction from trees. I noticed an airplane flying west towards it and abruptly turn north, another flying west. I could see an odd single headlight type light in the nose of the plane. The airplane flew in loops as I drove under and past it. I took the slippery rock exit to park and watch but saw nothing from that vantage point. I drove on south to the exit for route 488 and stopped again witnessing only an airplane doing loops north of my location. When returning back to 79 south I once again seen the star like object hovering directly south of my location close to the horizon. Videoed again for a few seconds and never seen it again. It was so far away you may have to zoom to see it. Wish I would have turned phone sideways.
