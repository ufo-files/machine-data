{rjl} Sighting at hulman field, Terre haute, in.

I was a DOD police officer at Hulman field in Terre Haute, in. 
While on shift I was watching a satellite with night vision out of boredom.
I seen an object come from an angle, very fast and then stop just beside the satellite and ran with it at the same speed parallel. After approximately 10 or 15 seconds.....shot off at an angle in opposite direction.....from sw....to se..
I've witnessed this twice.
A few years earlier on my way home from work .....I did see what I thought was the moon..
I was on 150 just outside of New Goshen.
It didn't look right. I remembered when I left work there was a full moon. 
So when I seen this ....it was .... orangish red .... weird like NO harvest Moon I had ever soon....
But then I realized...wait a minute ....the moon is over here...I had to look down in my windshield to see it in an opposite direction....and looking normal.
So what was this? .
I seen the lights of Clinton as well as the McDonald's sign. ....and from the lights glowing over the trees.... I could tell this sphere was not on a pole or had anything suspended from it....so not a balloon or a sign.
It glowed so oddly.
As I came closer to new Goshen....the House's and trees obstructed the view. ...so as soon as I reached range line road I turned in the direction of the object. Hoping to get closer....I was fully intrigued at this time...
But unfortunately....after loosing sight.... never seen it again.
These are the only sightings I've witnessed. .  That were unexplained.
Thank you.
