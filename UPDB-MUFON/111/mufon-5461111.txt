{jpg} One object broke into three, blinked out

A single orb was heading in a south westerly direction, irregularly flickering white and orange light. After approximately 90 seconds of observation, it turned west and broke into three separate lights of the same color. These smaller lights made a triangle as they expanded, and flickered out after approximately ten seconds.

This was observed above the treeline, and also above two fixed radio towers. The lights were about as high from the top of the tower as the tower was from the ground.
