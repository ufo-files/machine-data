It flew very fast overhead no noise

I was stood having a cigarette at my front door, I thought I saw a wisp of smoke from my cigarette but I realised it was a vey dark shape moving very fast and difficult to see until it passed bellow a cloud, it was at around 12 30 to 12 50 on the 1st January 2015, I shouted others in my house to come see and my sons girlfriend just caught a glimpse of it as it went out of sight over a neighbours roof line, the object was very dark, made no noise, it must have been going at some speed, it was shaped like a triangle with the back 2 corners cut of, similar to a diamond shape
