{nd} Star like, triangle, 3 points of light traveling in same direction.

I had just came home and looked up at the constellation Orion. I noticed a triangle formation of lights and thought, thats weird. At second glance I noticed that the lights were all moving in the same SE direction. They were there for only a few moments, then one by one they faded out. I noticed that there was also a search light in the same general direction after the object had disappeared.
