Huge Mother Ship released smaller crafts

Myself and a friend were involved in probably one of the best ufo sightings ever, involving a massive mother ship, just above us, which released a group of smaller crafts and then disappeared vertically. We saw the craft in close detail. Looking back, it was also probably around the time of the Roswell incident, which occurred in the next county to us, as we are in Norfolk in the UK. We never reported it to anyone, as didn't know who to tell. If you are interested in much more detailed information as to what occurred, please let me know.
