I was on a commercial flight from Phoenix airport to Portland Or.  We left around noon. Headed NorthWest. Sun was overhead or more on the west side of the plane. I was seated by the window on the east side of the plane. We had just pasted the Grand Canyon. I have taken this flight to Portland a number of times and always seat on that side of the plane so I can see the Grand Canyon. I love looking out the plane window and seeing the clouds, the sky, the ground and really love looking at the Grand Canyon. Just after take off, pilot has stated we where flying at 33,000 feet and about 650 miles per hour (not sure about the speed). As I was looking out the window, A large plane shaped object, I thought it was another plane, go by use going in a southwest direction. I wondered at the time what airline that plane was with because of the color of it. Also thought it was awful close and going awful fast. It was very dark grey/black with brown mixed in. After it passed, I thought, what a weird plan. That was when it occurred to me that it did not have any wings or tail. I thought it must have been a rocket but why was it flying level with us and where was it going.
