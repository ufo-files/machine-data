Saw bright light outside of window then saw what looked like a shooting star outside to the right of where the bright round object.

I was sitting in my living room watching a show early in the morning. I kept seeing the Tv reflection in the window but it was behind the curtain that was blocking the tv. Things that move outside the window always catch my attention even if it seems like nothing is there at all. The bright perfectly round sphere I figured was just the tv reflection again but this time it was higher than then normal spot the lights were reflecting in the window from and it disappeared suddenly. I kept watching the same spot and saw it shoot across the sky like a shooting star. However, it was cloudy that night and you could see no stars in the sky. When the object was the brightest and stationary in the sky it looked to be about 4mm. It was as bright as if someone were using an LED high powered flashlight. Then it disappeared to the left (NW). It reappeared going right (NE) but was much smaller in size about 2mm and it looked like a dim lit star shooting across the sky in cloud cover. My feelings towards it was well that was cool but then I also realized I wouldn't be able to see a shooting star while it was cloudy out. Lost sight of the object after it disappeared into the clouds.
(No physical evidence was recorded of the event.) Curious if anyone else saw anything that night.
