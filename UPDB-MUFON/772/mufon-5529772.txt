I went outside, saw a large yellow triangle above a tree, then blacked out.

I was 8 years old. My grandparents and my parents were playing cards in the dining room. My grandpa told me to take the trash out to the curb. I went outside to the curb in front of my house. Something compelled me to look up. When I did I saw a large, glowing yellow, triangular ship above the tree. It wasn't moving or making any sound. I could only see the back of it because the tree obstructed the rest of my view. Right away I knew it was a UFO, so I started examining it. The bottom of the craft was covered in some sort of hieroglyphics, but it was all just lines connecting to dots. All of a sudden I blacked out, and the next thing I remember was having my hand on the front doorknob, already in mid turn. I walked in and said nothing. I don't know what time it was when I got back, but it couldn't have been to long. After that I had a small circular object in my right ear lobe that wasn't there before.
