{rjl} Dark Orb appears above waterway.

My friend and I took a walk to a dock in our neighborhood. While taking pictures of the water and the vegetation, we noticed a small hum, which grew into a low rumble. We then looked up and saw a dark orb in the sky. The UFO startled us, looking like no other craft we have ever seen. Our stomachs instantly dropped. Pulsating lights blinded us, but I still managed to snap a quick shot. When the Polaroid printed, we looked at it, and when we looked back to the sky, the craft was gone. We both had to sit down for a minute because of our great disbelief. So happy to be able to share our story.
