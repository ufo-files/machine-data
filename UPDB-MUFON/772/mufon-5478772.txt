{dr}DRIVING WEST IN TX OR JUST IN NM SAW LIGHTS IN SKY PULLED OVER TOOK PICS

I WEST ON MY YEARLY DRIVE TO AZ TO HANG WITH MY BROTHER. ON THE WAY I WAS IN TX OR MAYBE JUST IN NEW MEXICO I STARTED SEEING BRIGHT STRANGELY COLORED LIGHTS IN THE SKY AS I GOT CLOSE ENOUGH I PULLED TO THE SIDE WITH MY FLASHERS ON GOT OUT AND TRIED TO TAKE A GOOD PICTURE OF THESE LIGHTS THE HIGHEST ONE WAS CHANGING FAST FROM RED TO BLUE. THE NEXT LOWER ONE WAS BLUE AND STAYED THAT WAY. THE NEXT BELOW WAS OF A BURNT ORANGE COLOR AND STAYED THAT COLOR. THEN FINALLY THE LOWEST WAS A NEON GREEN, WHICH SEEMED STUPID TO ME. ON THE NEXT PICTURE I WAS TRYING TO GET A BETTER PICTURE YET BY LEANING ON MY ROOF ON THE PASSENGER SIDE. THAT PICTURE SHOWED THE BLUE ONE WAS ALREADY FARTHER AWAY AND THE NEON COLOR AT THE SAME PLACE. I DON'T KNOW IF THEY WERE MILITARY OR OTHER. IT SEEMS AS THOUGH I LOST 2 HOUR'S OF DRIVING TIME AND COMPLETELY FORGOT ABOUT IT; TILL I RECENTLY LOOKED SOME REAL OLD PICTURES IN MY CAMERA. THEY WERE MOVING AND HOVERING AT TIMES BUT MOVING AWAY FAST EXCEPT THE DUMB NEON GREEN ONE. THAT'S ALL I CAN REMEMBER.
