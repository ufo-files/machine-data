Green Orb fly across southwestern sky

We were firing fireworks then I (oldest bother) and my sister, little brother, mother looked into the sky we saw a green orb (bright as car headlights in a distance) flying 2X faster then a plane. We looked around the sky and saw multiple airlines flying with blinking lights across the sky, while this event was happening and there was a noticeable difference between to two. This green orb flew across the sky southbound until it was gone. Again the green orb flew straight 2x the speed of a plane at airline altitude. We brought out binoculars to get a detailed look, I noticed it was a oval shaped with green in the middle surrounded with a red/orange light. Without binoculars it was the size of a pin point. The object wasn't directly over us but was 45 degrees off the horizon. Very strange...
