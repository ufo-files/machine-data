{tjd} Fast moving, high altitude, 90 degree instantaneous turn

I was walking through a field at about 3 in the morning with my 3 friends, we were all looking in separate directions when all of a sudden our focus was brought to the same point. We saw a bright greenish streak flying at extremely high altitudes at an alarming speed, initially we thought it to be a meteorite, until it stopped for a split second, and changed its trajectory by 90 degrees and accelerated so quickly it damn near disappeared in an instant. That's was just the beginning too, we continued walking in the direction that the craft flew in until we came to a barren patch of land almost bowl shaped, once we stepped into said bowl our hairs started to stand up almost like we were statically charged. We stayed there for about an hour because one of my friends and I had noticed a very strange looking cluster of lights. They appeared to be in an almost double helix shaped structure, spinning and undulating with lights fading from pinks to blues to greens. The strangest part about this whole situation is that our eyes could focus on any other star or constellation, however whenever we tried looking directly at the cluster it appeared blurry, or unfocused almost like looking through a pair of unadjusted binoculars. We felt as if we were looking at something we shouldn't have been looking at. And I've tried locating the cluster every night since, just to see if it was a constellation and my eyes were playing tricks, but it's not there anymore which is very peculiar. However anytime you walk into that field, you're overcome with a very peculiar feeling. Even with someone who hadn't witnessed the incident.
