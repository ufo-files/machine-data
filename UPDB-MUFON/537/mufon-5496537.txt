{cak} Four flying objects to the southwest over Memphis Tn.

I saw four bright orbs traveling southwest direction. I was looking at the chemtrails the morning of 1/20/15. I was at work, midtown Memphis and I was looking to the sky (westward) and spotted what I first thought were birds flying. I got excited when I determined that they were not birds, but instead orbs flying. Two orbs were flying ahead of the other two. The second two orbs were afew feet behind the first orbs but to the right abit. All four orbs were flying straight, no sound from them at all. I lost sight of them because they were too far away. I kept saying to myself get your phone out and snap a picture but I didn't want to lose site of them so I didn't bother reaching for my phone. I tried to get a coworker to witness but he was too late to see anything, since it only lasted afew seconds.
