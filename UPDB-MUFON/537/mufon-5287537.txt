Glowing orb followed by 2 possible military aircraft then disappears

While on the patio at work, I observed a white glowing orb traveling due East,directly to the South of my location. The orb was being followed by 2 aircraft (airplanes or helicopters, I couldn't tell). I couldn't hear or see anything from any of the craft, except for the glowing white orb and the blinking red and white lights on the 2 tailing aircraft. The lead aircraft was climbing in elevation to match the orb, and closing the distance. Just before the orb was about to transit the moon, it faded out in less than a second, about 5 degrees West of the moon. Once the orb had appeared to de-materialize, the 2 red blinking craft leveled their positions to the same altitude and continued Eastbound, until out of sight. I checked a flight tracker website after the sighting and could not see anything traveling E in my area. There was one flight headed in the same direction, but it was only about 100 mi. E of Portland Int'l Airport to Billings, MT. The video I took is worthless, but you can see some of the blinking lights(They are blurry and extremely unsteady)
