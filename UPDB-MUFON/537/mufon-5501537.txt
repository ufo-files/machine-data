I first saw it to the west just a dark object going very slow I was outside barbecuing I went inside to tell my friend and girlfriend they came out and we watched it go by almost right above us a little south of us I think it may have been military since at the time van nuys air force base was next to the airport and it was just west of the base on strathern st.it traveled very slow was silent was just dark and was only visible because of the lights from Northridge hospital that reflected off it. it took minutes to go by, made no sound. but that was in 93' and now 21 years later still no planes like that. so I think I saw something secret. maybe not extraterrestrial or de mensional .the object continued to travel west until we lost sight of it. because it was so low the trees and houses got in the way.
