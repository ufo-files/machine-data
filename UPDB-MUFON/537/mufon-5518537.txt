3 large asymmetrical pitch black objects flying oner chinatown ottawa early evening sunday

Ottawa, chinatown/centertown. I saw three (3) flat black flying objects flying at a very high altitude (in relation to low cloud cover). The objects remained quite large (hot air balloon sized) at the observed altitude so I would assume they were quite large. They made no noise and were of a strange random looking geometry from what was silhouetted against the overcast sky. They flew slowly in a triangular pattern first north/south then banked gradually east/west and appeared to gain altitude until they faded from sight. I'm wondering if anyone from ottawa saw what I saw. I called down to a few people on the street (from my second floor preston balcony) but the houses obscured their view. Did anyone see these objects maybe earlier in their flightpath when the sky was clearer and identification might have been easier given more light in the sky?
