Rectangular shaped object above the cloud level but within the storm front.

My wife and I were traveling southbound on Hwy 19 south and Tarpon springs Rd. when I spotted what to me appeared to be an object that had a rectangular shape hovering in the air in a storm front moving in from the coast (west) from our location. From our location the object was approx. the size of 2 x 5 centimeters southwest at about 30 degrees from our location. The object stayed in the same location until the clouds rolled in and obscured it. My wife also saw the object.
