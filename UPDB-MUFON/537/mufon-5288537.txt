Triangle with red light, white pulsing, and disappearing orange light.

About 2 am or shortly after, out my front window I noticed lights which caught my eye. It wasn't very high above the tree tops but could have been up to a mile away I believe. Lights made a triangle, front light was red, back light was white pulsating, and bottom light was orange-yellow. The orange yellow light was only on a few moments before it just went out. Then the red and white lights spread slightly further apart than they had been. At first all three lights were in almost a cluster but when the orange light went off the red and white then spread out. It was heading NW, then suddenly changed its course to be heading North. I watched it then another moment or so before it disappeared, probably into the overcast.
