{jpg} I observed a hovering light over my daughters school. The light flew straight in the air noticeable through clouds.

At around 8:16 pm, I noticed a light outside my window when I came into my room. I thought nothing of it. I walked out, poured a shot of scotch, entered the room, sat on the bed, ordered a movie on Google, because I was sitting at the edge of my bed the window was behind me, and exactly at 8:20 I noticed the light was still there, and I did a double take so to speak. I had my phone in my hand, and as I started snapping at 8:20, the object slowly ascended. The object was hard to notice through my phones camera, but it was still noticeable through the clouds till about 8:22 pm. The light seemed to pulsate as it ascended.
