{rjl} 5 glowing orbs, 3 in a triangle formation

We were driving south on I-71, 7 miles or so from the Polaris exit in Delaware County, about 20 miles from downtown Columbus. 

1 of the objects was in the east and was stationary. 

3 of the objects were in a triangle formation and were also stationary for most of the observed time, then one of the objects appeared to travel east for a short distance.

1 other object was east of the triangle formation and was stationary. 

We both observed the lights for more then 2 minutes, maybe even more than 3. And then all vanished as though they were hidden by clouds with the exception of the 1 in the east, which followed suit several seconds later.

I'm very interested to learn of others witnessing the event.
