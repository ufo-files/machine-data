Stargazing turns into ufo sighting

Clear night Sitting in the driveway with a friend we began star gazing. Then one star began to move. As I followed it with my eyes for about 6 minutes I began to believe it was a plane but then It began to zig zag. I jumped up to point it out to my friend so they could see It too and as soon as I began to show them it disappeared. This is the second time we’ve seen one in that same location.
