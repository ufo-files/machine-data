(GS) Like a very bright slow moving satellite, but much slower and much brighter.

Wife and I were sitting on the deck watching night sky as we do a lot of nights. We think its fun to be the first of us to spot a satellite, bat, owl, shooting star, etc. This night a bright object passed directly over us and the slight cloud cover and I got to be the first to spot it. I thought it was a shooting star, it was so bright. It was moving west to east. Were used to seeing satellites get brighter as they catch the sun and I quickly changed my opinion from shooting star to satellite because it was bright and moving slower than a shooting star. Pretty soon my wife asked "Shouldnt that have dimmed by now?" I had to agree with her. She stated she thought it was lower than a satellite and I agreed. I speculated that it might be the ISS, not knowing its location. We watched as the object continued east until it went out of sight, approximately 5-6 minutes. It never lost its brilliance. We lost it because it went beyond the horizon.
