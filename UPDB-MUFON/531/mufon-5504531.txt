{kt}Visiting in the front yard, electricity/lights went out. Moments later we saw this UFO pass over. Moments later the electricity/lights came back on.

We had just returned from out Summer vacation. It was a warm, but comfortable evening to be outside. Around 8:30 PM, we invited our neighbor & her daughter over to visit & give them a thank-you-gifts for dog sitting. Me, my x-wife & neighbor were catching-up & chatting at the front walkway/driveway area. Our children were in the front yard playing fetch/catch the whirly-bird with our dog. (one of the gifts we bought was a toy that looked very similar to this one: http://www.amazon.com/Deluxe-Special-Purchase-Exclusive-FlashingBlinkyLights/dp/B005SIMR9G).

Without warning, the electricity shut down & the houses on the street went dark. There were no passing thunder storms, nor the sound of a car accident crashing into a power-pole that could have caused this. We (adults)were surprised, looked at each other & wondered what just happened??? I was looking around, up & down the street. A few moments later, I suddenly spotted a multicolored (white-Blue-Red-Green?)UFO coming diagonally across the sky out of the S/SE at about 45-55 degrees above the horizon. I pointed at it & shouted LOOK, WHAT'S THAT??? FYI: it was silent & partially concealed by intermittent, light stratus clouds. Regarding speed: it didn't streak as fast across the sky like a meteorite, nor was it slow like the steady climb of an ascending plane. It seemed to me, it's speed was more like a plane coming in for a landing; however it didn't appear to be landing when it disappeared out of sight about 6 seconds later, at about the same degrees into the N/NW horizon. All 3 of us adults saw it(I don't remember whether the children even noticed the power-outage, let alone saw the UFO). A few moments after it was gone, the electricity came back on-line. We (adults)were in agreement: we just witnesses an unexplained flying object. I felt perplexed & excited by this bizarre experience. We talked about it reassuringly for a couple minutes. I was relieved to know I wasn't the only one who saw it. This encouraged me to follow up by calling the electric company to report this power outage & ask if they had an explanation. I called & informed them we had just experienced a brief power outage lasting a minute or two. They had no reports of there being any power outage in my area, or on my street.

I left it at that & sometimes I still wonder what it was. Recently a close friend asked me if I believe in UFO's and seemed surprised when I said "Yes, because I've seen one". I also told her I don't believe UFO's have to be alien to be real. In my experience, my best guess is: maybe it was some kind of advanced military aircraft being tested/used for the Iraq Gulf War. FYI: there are two significant aircraft/military facilities in the area: 1. Pratt & Whitney Aircraft is less than 3 miles away & 2. Westover Air Force base is about 45 miles away.

Bottom line: Sharing this here has given me a sense of closure. I'm glad I came across your organization & this submission form after watching "UFO & Aliens; The Physical Evidence".
