{jpg} Glowing Orb Sighting Silent & Fast

I walked out of my house and was walking down to my car, facing the eastern sky just before sunrise. I observed what I though to be a bright star. It seemed REALLY bright, like brighter than Jupiter when it's a good position. So I stopped and watched as the star-like object flew silently in straight line from the NE to the SE where the sun was rising at a high rate of speed. I could see it pushing air out of the way. It left no vapor trail and was not an airplane or helicopter as it flew too fast. 

It turned and remained in the sky for another 30 seconds and then vanished out of sight. Unfortunately, I did not have a chance to take a photo or video of it because it happened so fast.
