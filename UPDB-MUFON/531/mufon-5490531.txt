I took a picture of the sunset west of Spanish Banks beach. When I looked at the picture, it had a large orb south west of the sunset, visible above the peninsula.

I was at the beach with family and friends and took a couple pictures of the sunset with my phone. In the last picture I caught a large sun-like orb just south of the sunset and above the peninsula, which was at a higher elevation than our sea-level location. I did not see the orb with my eyes. When I checked the photo and saw the orb in the picture, I looked up but didn't see anything in the same location. I've seen UFOs (to me) all my life, so catching one on camera without actually seeing it was a little disappointing.
