extremely bright light directly overhead, changed direction and rapidly faded.

My wife and I were in our spa at approximately 9:45 PM when I noticed a bright light almost directly overhead. When I looked up, it was probably magnitude -6 or -7, and was just west of a line between the star Alkaid in Ursa Major and Arcturus in Bootes. It seemed to move slightly east and then turned to the south, it hadnt traveled much more than 20 degrees before it had faded so much that I couldnt see it anymore. Limiting magnitude that night was about +4.5, due to light pollution from Topeka. My wife was facing the wrong direction to see it after it had faded, and didn't look up in time when it was bright.
