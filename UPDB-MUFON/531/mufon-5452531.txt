Venus like in size and brightness

The light was Venus like and brightness. It was at first moving like a normal airplane. But had no flashing lights that airplanes have. After a couple of seconds it appeared to speed up and slow and move side to side. The closer to the horizon it got the weirder it moved. It then speed up but continued to move side to side till it disappeared in the sky. But no the horizon it appeared to go up instead of disappearing over the horizon as a normal object would do.
