{tjd} LIGHTS IN TRIANGLE FORMATION HOVERING OVER 303 HWY IN AZ

While my wife and I were driving around night time along the 303 Hwy in Litchfield AZ en route to Las Vegas for the weekend, I came into a sudden stop in the middle of highway behind 3 other vehicles. At first I got so furious why these vehicles stop in the middle of the highway, but only to find out as I look up in the dark sky, we witness this triangle shape flying object (about 100-200 ft) outlined by white lights along the perimeter coming down from the sky hovering quietly close above us before it went to our right in the NE direction. The next thing i remember is we were driving back to highway. I have no recollection of what so ever happened after that. I also noticed that incident happened near Luke Air Force Base. I wasn't sure if it could have been a Military drone they were flying. I work in Aerospace Defense and holds a secret clearance but I am ware of no Military aircraft with that maneuvering capability.
