Appeared as 3 squares together in the shape of a boomerang.

Standing on my balcony facing north, saw object(s) in sky moving rapidly south to north at a fairly high rate of speed. Object(s) traveled straight-line paralleling Rt. 159 through St. Clair County. Object was dark and non-reflective. No lights or sound. I witnessed it only because the sky toward the horizon was lighter due to street lights and object was darker. Object appeared as three squares of unknown thickness configured as in the shape of a boomerang. Unable to determine elevation, but object was big enough or close enough that I could make out the three distinct sections. Object continued on until out of sight over a line of trees.
