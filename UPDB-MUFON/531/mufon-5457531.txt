{cak} A friend and I were kayaking in Cloquet on the St Louis River and noticed a triangular shape in the sky.

At 2038 on 05/27/2018 a friend and I were kayaking on the St Louis River in Cloquet, MN. When we looked up at the sky, we saw a triangular shape that kind of resembled a mountain, but knew that’s not what we were seeing because there are no hills or mountains anywhere in Cloquet or any structure that would resemble one. After looking at it stand still in the sky for a half an hour we decided to start paddling in the opposite direction. We paddled for another 20 minutes and by the time we got back to land- it was gone.
