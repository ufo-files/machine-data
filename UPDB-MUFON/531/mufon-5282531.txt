Two shiny objects over I-40 at Cedar Bluff area in daylight

Was driving N on Peters Rd, approaching Kingston Pike from Northshore. Noticed above and directly in front of me something at first glance I took for a plane, but something seemed off so I took a second look. I observed it was it was two objects, traveling approximately in line with I-40 eastbound at a slow rate of speed. This was on Saturday, 19 Feb 2022 at approximately 13:15 ET. The objects were flying tightly in formation, appeared from my point of observation to be oval, and chrome colored. Couldn’t make out any details because the sun was brightly reflecting off of them. I could not observe them steadily because I was driving. At first, I didn’t realize how unusual this was. I looked back up and they seemed to have changed course to a more westerly direction. They seemed to be just kind of gliding around in tight formation, at a fairly slow rate of speed. Even though I couldn’t make a definite determination, they seemed to be on the N side of the interstate, but not far away, and not that high. I kept looking up as much as I could as I crossed Kingston Pike onto N Peters and approached Best Buy. The last I saw of them, they seemed to be headed in a Northwesterly direction. I looked down for a moment and then looked back up and they were gone. I was on Peters Rd, Kingston Pike, and if I had to take an educated guess they were probably over the Cedar Bluff area. Once again, in the afternoon on a Saturday in bright clear weather. I can’t believe no one else saw this.
