{dr}UFO OBSERVED HOVERING AND THEN A SMALLER STARLIKE UFO EXITED AND FLOATED UPWARD

On Thursday night South of Marshall, TX around 7:05 pm turning off hwy 59 South onto FMRD 1186 we observed a glowing white large oval object in the sky hovering below cloud level..then it ejected a smaller star sized object that flashed different colors then that object moved upward into the sky.. We were in shock after seeing this as we have seen the star like objects in the sky for the past couple of weeks in the night sky.  After the star like object descended straight down and then up it continued to flash in different colors at cloud level. The large white glowing object began to move south southeast from it at a slower pace at first.. and then suddenly took off at a fast speed traveling southeast at a low altitude and disappeared out of sight.
