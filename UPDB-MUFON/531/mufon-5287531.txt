Blue beams

At 1.20am on 28th May I went for a cigarette & noticed multiple blue beams in the sky. The sky was quiet with no planes or helicopters. I couldn't see anything but the blue beams which were in a pattern. It looked to me like the beams were going around an object cos they curved & took up most of the sky. I woke my boyfriend and he said the same as me, UFO Whatever it was it was huge!!!! It lasted about 5mins & I recorded the sky but it was to dark to see anything.
