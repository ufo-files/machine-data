{rjl} small oragish object traveling 60 degrees north east direction very high altitude straigh line maybe satellite

I was at my house looking at the constellation Cygnus facing north when I observed a small orange/red colored object traveling in a straight line in a 60 degree magnetic northeast direction. It may have been a satellite? It was traveling at a very high altitude at a very high speed. I lost sight when the object disappeared on the horizon. I'm about 90% sure it was not an airplane. It made no noise.
