bright orange sphere

went outside to smoke at 9:20 pm looked up and saw a bright red/orange sphere hovering over the hills southwest of medford oregon. it was a cloudy sky, the object was below the clouds. i watched the object for a while to see if it had blinking lights like a plane but the object didn't appear to be moving. suddenly the object moved and appeared in a different location (approx a half mile)and after about 10 seconds the object darted back to its original location. this happened a couple of times. i ran inside to alert some friends, we tried to get a picture but the object suddenly dissapeared, just blinked out of existence. at about 9:45 pm the sky started to clear, there were absolutly no bright stars in the area of the object.
