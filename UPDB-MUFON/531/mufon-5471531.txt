Blast of light in my flat

I had just finished watching tv and laid on my sofa in my PJs with a quilt. I closed my eyes but heard a sound I thought was a lorry. It came closer and closer till I could hear it outside. I knew by then it was not a lorry as it produced a loud humming sound at three different pitches. This sounded odd and I couldn1t see what it was as I had polythene over the windows to keep the warmth in. Just then the whole room flooded with white light. Just as I thought to go outside and investigate - I was suddenly overcome by intense pain in my gut and fear crippled me so much I had to climb back on the sofa and laid down to ease the pain. I was then really tired, fell asleep and woke an hour or so later.
