Saw Light Move across the sky

I was letting our cat in and observed a white or silver light shoot across the sky from the North West heading South East. It was about 9:03 pm in Webster NY. It was extremely fast with no trail or tail.
It was dark out so all I could see was a white light almost like a star. It was so fast so I'm not sure if it was completely round or oval. We have a flight path for planes to the South of our house and it takes about 5 to 7 minutes for a plane to go the same distance as this object took in 3 to 4 seconds. No doubt this was not a meteor as I have seen them before. It was extremely fast but not as fast a meteor. I woke my husband because I couldn't believe what I just saw to tell him about it. As for how high it was in the sky I would say it was about 500ft or less approx. above the tree tops.
