Red and white flying object suddenly glitched to higher elevation, continuing same path direction

About 4 am or a little after, I saw something flying which caught my eye because of a ufo I had seen just two hours before. The object which I saw this time had a red light in front and white pulsating in back. It was quite a ways up this time, heading E/SE. It was moving quickly, more quickly than a plane that far away could. It was at one elevation, then within moment was flying at a higher elevation but still heading the same direction. Almost as if it glitched.
