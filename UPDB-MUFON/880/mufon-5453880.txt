{cak} Starlight objects moving in a straight line

On 4/15/2020 at 5:30am i observed multiple (about 20) starlike objects move across the sky, using the same trajectory. when i first noticed the object, it appeared to move south and thought it was a satélite. So I Got my phone opened the sky guide app. But it showed no satélites/rockets/comets. The starlike Object was moving in a steady direction heading south. A second starlight object followed behind and another and another, all moving in the same steady direction. As if they were parading disappearing on the horizon one after the other. I must of seen about 20 of them.
