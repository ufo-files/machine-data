Orbs, 2 maybe 3 observed from 2nd floor hotel balcony facing Lake lake Michigan

While on balcony with my boating binoculars I noticed 2-3 bright orange orbs positioned in a possible triangular formation. There was air traffic coming out of Chicago but the colors of the plane lights were much whiter and strobes on aircraft made it easy to identify as a plane. Also a few planes were moving through the sky, these orbs seemed stationary over the lake. At first I did not notice the 3rd orb it was far from the one I was observing and the second one was to the left at a distance as seen in photo. If you zoom in you can see the faint color of a possible 3rd orb. We were located on the second floor balcony in a Lake View room in St. Joseph, MI. To make sure it was not a plane I lined up the closest orb between two tree branches to gauge any movement but was little if any change in position. It did move ever so slightly from my left to right then held its position for about 7 minutes. It was so cold I had to return inside the room to warm up and when I returned they had gone. About 10 minutes later I went back out and it was there again. I did not see all 3 go out but when it did it flickered like it was moving close then far then disappeared.
