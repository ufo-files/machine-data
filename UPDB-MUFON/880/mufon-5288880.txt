Cube/Tic Tac/ Oval/ Irregular floating object - video and photo

Approximately 6:15 AM on October 1, I exited my home almost immediately noticing an cube shaped object floating above my neighbor's roof no more than 300' - the object appeared to be dull metallic with no sounds or emissions of any type. 
It was not a helicopter, drone, balloon or airplane. 

Disbelieving what I was observing, I immediately went inside to retrieve my I-Phone to take a picture of it. When enabling the camera function, I observed the viewer was showing a tic tac while visually I observed a cube. The resulting photo looked like a tic-tac
Understanding this may be some type of optical distortion, i quickly changed to video, as the object quickly moved to the southeast, as it somehow knew it was being observed by me. The resulting video showed the object transforming into an irregular object and then spun away through what appeared to be cloud cover.
