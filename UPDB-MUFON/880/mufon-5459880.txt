{tjd} Orange, round lights in the sky, no visible structure; smaller orange balls-of-light coming and going from it.

Lovely summer night; sitting out on my back porch in Colorado Springs. Many very large trees in the area. I then see, above the tree-line, bright, glowing orange balls, coming-and-going from what had to be a stationary object, with the same orange lights. The moving balls of light were shooting out, and then returning. I was mesmerized! So was my husband.
Decided to call the police; I did, and they sent a young officer out. He brushed it off--said it was a street light!  (Altitude was WAY too high; I was insulted!) And left.
It was the strangest aerial phenomenon I had ever seen. It last for hours, but I finally went to bed after 11:00 pm. 
I have done a great deal of microscopic work as a Laboratory Technologist, and am aware of tiny details that would be lost on others.
