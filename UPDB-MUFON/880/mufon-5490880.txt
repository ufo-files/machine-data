I am posting video of my brother's first-hand account of a flame in the sky that just stayed there for a few minutes. The flame "tail" produced no smoke or trail, there was no visible object or craft it belonged to. See the video for the actual flame in the sky. 
What made the sighting even more unusual was that as soon the flame disappeared a long, round black object appeared below where the flame was. The round black object had a blinking small light on it. Then it disappeared.
