{rjl} Star-like light cruised across sky, made a sudden a turn, increased speed, disappeared into nothing.

On Sunday, June 17th, 2018, at approximately 12:20, I had returned home from work. The sky was clear, with little-to-no light pollution. A habit of mine, on clear nights, is to admire the night sky for a few minutes, before heading into the house to retire for the night. After about 10-15 seconds of observing the night sky, I noticed a star-sized, unwavering, bright light cruising at steady, cruising pace, moving across the sky, at a very high altitude, in an eastward direction. The light the object emitted was neither blinking or flickering. After watching the object for roughly a minute, it made a sudden, 90 to100 degree, turn, South-westward. It increased in speed and, a few seconds later, quickly vanished into nothing.
