Below Orions Belt, 2 star-like lights; right one faded then left faded

1. Riding in card with 3 other people, heading south on Eastside Highway.
2. Looking at Orions Belt.
3. I commented to the other 3 in the car, "Look, Ive never seen those 2 stars before; do any of you know what they are?"
4. Star-like, very bright; from our viewpoint to the left and below Orions Belt about 2 feet, the objects appeared about 2 inches apart. No motion or movement, no sound.
5. We all kept looking at the objects and all agreed that none of us had ever seen those 2 "stars."
6. The objects faded: The one on the right faded first, then the one on the left faded. No clouds in the sky and no change in being able to view near by objects such as Orions Belt.
