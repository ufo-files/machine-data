{cak} Fireball type object above tree line

Saw a fireball type object moving slowly above the tree line. I moved away from visibility thinking I would see whatever it was clear the top of my neighbors house. I never did and instead saw what appeared to be a very bright star all of a sudden. The star went from a blinking orangish color to a white color. It had a beam of light shining down in a sideways pattern almost like the beam from a search helicopter. It didnt move at all and then after a minute or so it completely disappeared. Didnt fly away or move, just went dark.
