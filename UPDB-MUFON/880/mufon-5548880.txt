Cylinder sighted

I had been observing the moon's occultation of the Pleiades when I decided to take a look at Mars which was positioned just a few degrees west of the moon. I had heard that Mars was experiencing a planet-wide dust storm and just wanted to see if its relative brightness had changed very much. I had just gotten the planet focused when a gun-metal grey cylinderical object passed from west to east directly below the image of Mars. Probably 1/2" below the planet in relative terms. It took the object about 3 seconds to cross my field of view and unfortunately I couldn't track it any further. The object had no lights so I'm certain it wasn't an aircraft. Of course, it could have been a satellite but I don't know if they orbit in the part of the sky I was looking at. This took place around 4:00am.
