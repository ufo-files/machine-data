Black Crossed Shaped UFO with protrusions seen in

Mrs. Bolton reported that, on June 2, 1997, after dinner, she was sitting at herkitchen table at the rear of her home (facing north) watching her two minaturehorses frolicking in the back, when a cross-shaped UFO flew over her barnabout 100 ft. from the witness. She stated the object was about the size of aschool bus. It came from the southeast and traveled toward the northwest atabout 300-500 mph. The sighting lasted about 3 seconds.
