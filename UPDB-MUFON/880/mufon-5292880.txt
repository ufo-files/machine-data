PTERODACTIL SIGHTING

I want to tell you what I saw on Sunday april 18th 2004: I live at the highest place in Atizapan de Zaragoza Edo. de Mex. México. That nigth I was in my backyard looking at the sky, it was very clear without clouds. Suddenly, I saw a great bird ("pterodactilo") flying in the sky with the following characteristics: 1.- Its size was about 50 meters 2.- The shape of the wings appeared to be as the palm of the hand (oval) 3.- It was transparent but its outline had a very clear shape 4.- It had rounded lights around its wings 5.- It looked plain and as it went by I could hardly see it. It really shocked me because I couldn`t believe what I was seeing, but I still continued looking at the sky and after 15 minutes a second pterodactilo pased through in the same direction from the Presa Madin towards North, but this pterodactilo flapped. I contacted some people from the UFO organization and obviously nobody believed me. I know this is very hard to believe but in 2006 a website posted my sighting. From that date on it scares me to go out my backyard at night. I have seen much more things but I will let you know afterwards. I watched a program in the History Channel (2009) about pterodactyls in Nueva Guinea and I would like to know if you have heard something similar because it has been a great experience for me to see this and I don´t believe I´m the only one that has seen them. Thank you so much for reading my message. I`m willing to hearing from you soon. Regards, Juan Carlos Vazquez Mora
