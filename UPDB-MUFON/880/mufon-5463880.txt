{rjl} 10 orange orbs flying in straight line equidistant west to east flying into cloud cover over Atlantic Ocean

I was on a balcony smoking a cigar around 8pm.I noticed an orange orb apparently ascending from west from behind building . 3 came up and I called out my wife and father in law who also witnessed this. A total of at least 10,flying in straight line in formation, apparently one foot apart visually, no sound,proceeding slowly across sky. No change in color or intensity. None extinguished, just flew into cloud cover. Size and shape remained constant. It came from the direction of Patrick Air force base, they deny any aircraft being launched. This happened over Cocoa Beach, Florida. No time to get phone for video. Didn't want to leave balcony. Sorry. Curious if anybody else reported this. Channel 2 news reported lights from night before, from coral springs above Fort Meyers and sequential reports all the way to California.
