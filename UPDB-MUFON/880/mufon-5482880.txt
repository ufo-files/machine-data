{cak} driving w270 at 3:37 on Mon. 6/13/16. Seen disc in sky, reflected light, then disappeared.

I was on the way to a dr appointment and driving west on 270 at the interchange of N23 at 3:37 on Monday, June 13th 2016. I saw a light reflected/or come from? something up in the sky out of my front windshield. It was just below the clouds and appeared to be disc shaped. It also appeared to be darker gray in color. I don't know about the sound because I was in my car. It hovered for about 5 seconds then began to move north toward Delaware Ohio. I kept an eye on it while reaching for my phone to take a picture when it disappeared. I posted my siting on Facebook Monday shortly after seeing the object asking is anyone else seen it. I am reporting this because I then saw there was a massive sighting Monday over Cleveland earlier that same day. When I viewed The picture it looked exactly what I had witnessed over Worthington Ohio later on the same day. I left my post on my Facebook as proof I seen a ufo before knowing anyone else in Ohio had seen the same thing on the same day.
