{cak} Three glowing orbs

On July 4th between 7 and 8 o'clock my wife, son and myself went out on the back porch to watch fire works in the neighborhood. After about 2 mind I noticed 3 glowing orbs to the north just sitting still in the sky. As I fixated on them they started to drop slowly , or even at a time then they went back up and stayed still again. They were pulsating as well .after about 10 MI s or so they faded out completely.
