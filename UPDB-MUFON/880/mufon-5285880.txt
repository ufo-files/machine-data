An unknown craft came into view, hovered for several minutes.

Around 6:20 PM I was walking home. It was dark and the area was lit by a streetlight. I noticed a light moving above me and to my right. I was young and curious so I stopped to look. To my hind mind, it looked as of it was four headlights facing straight down, just far brighter. I stood there mesmerized for a little while before realizing I could see the snow swirling around an oval type craft. I could sort of see reflection, but it was more like light was bending around it. It hovered just above the light pole for several minutes. I would say at least five because I was five minutes late getting home, about two minutes from my door when I saw it, started at a friend’s home seven minutes away, and had left ten minutes early. 

I remember feeling like I shouldn’t move, not that I couldn’t, that I shouldn’t. I was curious and I stared right at it just thinking how quiet it was, wondering what it was, and just generally fascinated by and connected to it. After what felt like a very long time, I felt it was time to move on, and so I did. I went home, had dinner, told my grandma why I was late, and carried on with my life. We discussed it once more about three years ago when she told me it scared her. I was so serious and explained what happened in detail. I was a very animated kid and that calm focus wasn’t normal for me, she said. I don’t talk about this because I’m unsure what to make of it, but I decided it was finally time to do so. That moment lives etched in my memory like a lithograph.
