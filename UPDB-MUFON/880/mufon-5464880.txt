Bright sphere with attached lights of various colors that pulsated and brightened

I am reporting this for my mother who does not wish to be contacted due to health issues. Please contact me for any follow up details.

Around the evening of September 18th, 2018 around 8:30 pm my mother observed a bright white sphere shaped object with several multi-colored lights that were attached to it out. She viewed the object from her living room window. This sighting lasted about one half hour. The object was about 45 degrees up and fairly low in the sky. It was hovering in the western section of the sky. My mother estimated it was about 500 feet or less from her location at the time of sighting. The object appeared to be a very bright white star-like sphere with red, orange, blue, green, and yellow lights attached to the main sphere. She described it as a "Wheel With Spokes" such as a wagon wheel or bicycle wheel. The lights would randomly brighten and pulsate. The object did not land or emit any sound. Object disappeared about one hour after sighting.

Please note, this sighting coincides with the evening of the Jewish high day known as Yom Kippur, or also called "The Day Of Atonement" and resembled the "Wheel in the Sky" mentioned in the Book of Ezekiel.
