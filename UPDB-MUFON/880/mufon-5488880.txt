{rjl} Two star-like lights that moved off in opposite directions from one another, two minutes apart.

4:26am observing stars and sky from my backyard. Facing north and looking at two stars slightly to the NE, I notice a strobe of light to the left of these two stars. I am wondering about that strobe when one of those two stars starts moving straight up away from the other one, then makes a gradual turn to the west and continues all the way, at the same speed, to the southwest sky where it finally dimmed and I could no longer see it. No blinking, no strobes, still at around 45-55 degrees above the horizon.  I turn back around to where I saw it begin (NE sky) to get my bearings on it1s starting point.  As I am looking at the one star remaining where I saw two just a minute before, then IT starts to move also.  It begins by moving north, then it veers off on a gradual turn to the east and continues all the way to the south, past Orion1s belt and disappears between Orion's belt 3 stars and the bottom star of Orion (Rigel). I spin back around facing north to where those two stars began their movement and stare at that spot.  Again I see the strobe of light.  Two seconds later the strobe of light appears just to the right (NE) of that original strobe.  Again, about 5 seconds later I see a dimmer strobe of light even more to the right (NE) than the first one.  Do not see any more strobes.  Time back in house: 4:38am.
