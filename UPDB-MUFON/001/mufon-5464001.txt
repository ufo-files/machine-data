{cak} V-shaped Object

Approximately 10pm at night I took my dog outside. The sky was exceptionally clear, without clouds. I live on a hill above city lights. I looked up to see if there were any shooting stars, and from the corner of my eye I saw a large black V shape fly over. I watched as it flew over between my house and my neighbor's for no more than 5 seconds. It was like nothing I'd seen before. It was black with 5 or 6 large brown circles underneath and what looked like dark stripes or shading between the circles. Also, it looked distorted or wavy around its edges; like when an asphalt road or a bbq is really hot. It was silent and had no lights. It was flying from the south to the north east.
