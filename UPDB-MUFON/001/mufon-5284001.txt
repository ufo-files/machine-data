I saw a parking lot size object and when it turned on it's side showing me it's belly, it looked like a building.

I was travelling down Kildaire Farm Road towards Tryon road in Cary, NC. I saw a huge array of lights ahead of me in the sky moving right to left across my line of sight. It then appeared to be moving away but then started to move closer to me. When it was at it's closest to me while I was stopped at the red light on Kildaire RD & Tryon Rd, it turned on it's side and I saw it's belly. There was a solid object between the lights. This object was huge, like a parking lot in the sky and when it turned on it's edge it looked like a building. When moving on it's side, there were 4 or 5 rows of blue-white light and when I saw it flying level, 2 rows of lights. I thought I was recording great video of it but when I checked after, I had only the last 2 seconds of the recording. I was so excited, I must have screwed up somehow. I'm sure lots of people must have seen this, it was huge and there is NO WAY it could have been a plane or a blimp. Left my flabbergasted and in true awe.
