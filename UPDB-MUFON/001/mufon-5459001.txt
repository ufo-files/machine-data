{jpg} We saw a bright light in the darkening sky that looked like a star but was moving in a wavering manner.

Almost straight above us we saw a bright star-like object that went north in a wobbly manner. As we watched, it became less clear as if it had gone behind clouds. We believe it ascended at that point, but at the same rate of speed. I have no idea how fast that would be, but at least as fast as a jet.
The wobbly path it took led me to conclude it was not a jet or a satellite, as those go in a much straighter line. As we watched it for about three minutes it began to grow much more faint until we couldn't see it any more.
