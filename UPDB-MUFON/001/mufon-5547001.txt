Metallic sphere surrounded by unusual cloud emitting bright white flashes

I was on my morning run when I noticed a strange looking cloud not far from the sun, rapidly breaking and changing shape. It was odd because it wasn't a windy day and other clouds in the vicinity were much bigger and stationary. Then I noticed a metallic sphere appear in the middle of the cloud pulsating with bright white light. It wasn1t moving, just hovering there, silently. It looked like it was changing shape but the flashes were so bright that it was hard to see the exact form. Seconds later it was gone and the small cloud surrounding it also disappeared. The object disappeared so suddenly as if a switch turned off and it became invisible instantly. 

A few minutes later as I1m running along my route a saw an object again in another location, this time without the bright flashes and the strange cloud. It was a round metallic orb. I was trying to find a better vintage point to see it unobstructed by trees but it was gone by the time I could get a better view.

The location of the sighting is around St. George Street and Bernard Ave. in Toronto, Canada.
