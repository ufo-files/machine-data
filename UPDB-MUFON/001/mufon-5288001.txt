12 years old boy , Dad had a private plane so I was familiar with planes and helicopters this was neither

As a 12 year old boy heading home on my bike I observed at the tree tops one night a flying disc hovering over head. My dad had a twin engine plane so I was very familiar with planes and helicopters. This was neither of them , no sound at all , slight spin motion and lights under the disc . I don't recall any colors in the lights. I stopped my bike and watched at a corner near a street light until I got scared and rode home. We lived approx 5 miles from a large military base at that time. Never saw any thing since. Robert
