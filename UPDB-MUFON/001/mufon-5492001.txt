{jpg} Starlight object

My girlfriend and I were sitting outside of my house eating and enjoying the cool breeze looking out in the distance over the house in front of us when I noticed a star like object begin to move. I quickly motioned over to her telling her to check this out pointing out in the distance we both noticed it starting to move and get brighter, at one point it look like it flashed a beam of light straight out in our direction, then the light started to them and it look like it was moving slightly to the right before it dimmed and started to fade away. The entire incident took about 2 minute and 10 seconds. Notable features of the object we're that after the light moving to the right it had a very dim read glow until we couldn't see it anymore.
