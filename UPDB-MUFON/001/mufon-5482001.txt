{jpg} Observed a glowing sphere making erratic turns at treetop level

As reported to ASD by phone: "I am a deputy sheriff and was on patrol when I noticed a glowing white object moving just above the tree line approximately 250 yards from my position. I stopped the vehicle near the junction of 42 and 28 Hwy and observed this object moving in a general direction from SW to NE, however, it made several erratic and quick movements to the left, right, and backwards, then forward once again. These movements are not something normally observed with typical aircraft such as a helicopter of plane. It appeared to be about the size of a small engine aircraft or slightly smaller, but was just a glowing ball of white light with no structure and no sound. If it had been an aircraft I would have heard a sound from the distance it was from me. I was alone and did not think to get a photo but wish I had now because it was very bright and would have shown up in the photograph. I heard about the sightings in this area and wanted to tell someone that there is something going on in this area. I did see a UFO in the fall of 2014 which was a disc shaped dark metallic gray color with small white lights on it, but have not seen anything before or since except for this recent one. I've also heard about a number of sightings people are seeing in this area from many years back to today."
