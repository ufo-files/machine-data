Saw one moving light, thought it was a helicopter, it was silent, too high and too fast though.

Was sitting on the porch with my adopted mom and we saw a single light moving fairly fast coming out over a tree from due west. At first I heard like the hum of a plane but it stopped before it even got close, and it passed almost directly over us, then slowed and sort of hovered/wiggled around, then went in a path for a moment again, then slowed and wiggled again. It passed behind the house and we followed it, we watched it wiggle one last time, and it went quickly away from us and disappeared slowly! Super weird.
