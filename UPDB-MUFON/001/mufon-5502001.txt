{cak} Bright red objects moving from north to south and going dark when 90 degrees above me

Outside walking my dog and noticed a bright red round object moving slowly straight across the sky and then faded out when directly above me. Then more objects appeared and followed the same basic flight path. They were all going straight but wavered slightly side to side in their flight path. Some were almost side by side but all appeared to be moving at the same speed and in the same direction. Saw a total of about 10 of these lights. No sound and appeared to be very high but not 100% sure. They were bright red orange and fuzzy around the edges and varied slightly in brightness. A pulsation of sorts. Then they stopped coming and that was it. I knew these were not airplanes and just not sure what they were. Nothing I have ever seen before. Once the objects were directly overhead they faded out but one faded out while it was coming at us and before it was at 90 degrees.
