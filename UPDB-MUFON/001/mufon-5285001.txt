I had been awake about an hour and walked outside to take in the early morning. I looked up at the stars and noticed this bright light moving toward me overhead. It moved in a straight path for about 5 seconds and then just disappeared. I have seen the space station many times passing over and this object was brighter. It was larger than the space station or closer. It made no sound. The conditions were dead calm and completely silent. After it disappeared, I remained outdoors for a short time, to see if it would become visible again, and it did not. At this point it should have been 110 degrees overhead with a completely open line of sight. No flashing lights or anything similar to an aircraft.
