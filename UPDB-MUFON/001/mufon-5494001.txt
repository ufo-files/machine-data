{cak} noticed something through windshield above me. Got out of car and stood in road and looked up at it. Length of time unknown. It slowly crossed the road and went to my left.

In the Spring of 1974 a silver disc caught my eye through the windshield just as it moved ahead of me over the road in Piedmont, Missouri. Didn't know if it had been following me. I stopped car, got out and looked up at which time it stopped moving. It was silver with rings on the bottom similar to the rings on a vinyl record or to explain better, rings in an aluminum pan. There were no lights except that the street lights seemed to be reflecting on it. There was no sound. No windows. It was at least as wide as the two-lane road. I looked around and there was no one around, not one car or person that I could see. There was a tavern on the right and it was closed. I was literally stopped in the road and standing just outside my car door. I slowly started moving away from me after crossing the road and it slowly disappeared from my view. I could then see that the top was the same as the bottom. I was at the school in 1973 when the basketball team told of what they saw. As for my sighting I wasn't a stranger to UFOs or embarrassed by the stories. I just don't remember much and never have regarding my personal incident.  I am now almost 59 years old. Hardly a day goes by that I don't wonder where I was going, where I had been that night, and did I tell anyone about it. I just have no memory of before or after the event. I have wondered if I could be hypnotized to see if anything else occurred that I have blocked out.
