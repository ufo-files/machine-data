{td}I saw a moving glint in the sky and took a picture

I was on my front porch photographing Red tail Hawks to the North of me. I visually scanned the treetops to possibly find any perched in the trees when I noticed a rapidly moving glint way off in the distance between the treetops and got my DSLR to focus on it and took a photograph. By the time I got my camera to refocus, It was nowhere to be seen. Probably disappeared behind a cloud. At first I thought it was just another jetliner off in the distance but it left no contrail and it was traveling faster than I was used to seeing such aircraft fly. Especially one that far away. I figured I would see what type of aircraft it is after I downloaded the photos I had taken to my desktop. Since the photos were taken in RAW format I knew I could enhance the photos to bring out more detail and then convert them to JPEG.
