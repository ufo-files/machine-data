{rjl} Red-Orange round object was stationary until it descended.

Standing on my front porch looking E. when I noticed a 'Mars' colored object hovering at a point NE of me approx. 351 NE of true north. The Red-Orange object was stationary with a dark gray cloud bank behind it and a light gray fog bank about a mile away from me between the object and myself. I watched it for about 15-20 seconds before it descended. It took about 3-4 seconds to go in a straight line down behind the fog bank. I have not seen any object do this before. It left no trace or trail behind it and was not seen again. Ned
