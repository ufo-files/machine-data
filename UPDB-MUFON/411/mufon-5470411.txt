{jpg} Light covered in misty cloud changed shape then dispersed into hazy mist

I was inside my house watching tv with my husband. My sister who lives 45 minutes away from me asked me to look outside and see if I noticed anything strange in the sky. Me and my husband ran outside to look. I thought it was a cloud at first. It was a streak of light behind a foggy veil, I noticed it moving slowly and almost hover before it changed shape and then started moving slowly away and dispersed leaving a hazy fog behind before disappearing. I was a little baffled,and curious because it looked unnatural. I took a few pictures of it. After it disappeared I was stunned at how quickly it vanished. I lost sight after it basically disappeared.
