Two lights going to our left one in front then both stopped and adjusted to be in line with each other

My friend and myself were on holiday in hay on Wye nor Wales. We stood outside to have a smoke and looked up to the sky because it was so clear and lots of stars. We saw a plane go by quite high up then we noticed a light moving from our right. It was dim and we had to really look hard to follow it.....then my friend said " look it's just stopped" I said "no it hasn't look it's over to the left" it was then we realised ther was two of them....the one furthest left then also stopped. The one on the left adjusted its path lower to be on line with the first one and they both just stopped there for a few minutes and I am certain beams were coming out of them both towards the other but no explosion or anything it was as if they were communicating with each other we watched them for about 4 more minutes and then both lights just shot off upwards at extreme speed and we couldn't see them anymore. We were convince these were not planes or shooting stars because they manoeuvred in a way stars don't and were so high , too high to be planes....we are convinced that these were some sort of ufos. We were excited and happy. I am 60 years old and have had an interest in the subject since my teens but this is the first time I've seen anything like this....
