{rjl} Saw smaller sun coming off of sun while riding in airplane

I was in the plane riding from Los Angeles to Georgia around 6 AM on December 21, 2015. This was shortly after take off, and I noticed the right-hand window facing east the sun had a smaller sun like object coming off of it and sometimes slightly moving. At first it looked like potentially a glare but it was not. I managed to catch it on video. If you watch through the whole thing you will see that it is not a Camera glare. I also saw this with my own eyes before I took the camera out to record. Eventually it disappeared.
