{nd} Silent silver metallic disc that hovered several minutes with no sound. Daylight observation.

On August 2 1965, me and my friend David were wrestling in my back yard. He had me pinned down and it was then that I spotted a silver mettalic disc hovering above the trees behind us. I do not know how long it was there. We got up and walked closer to observe it. We stood there trying to figure out what this was by naming off anything we knew. I remembered my mother was in the house and I went in the house to get her. She was on the phone. I ran back out and the object had moved off in the direction of the river. My mother came out several minutes later but it was gone by then. It made no sound. It reflected the sun so I know it was real. It was viewed for several minutes by both David and I. We were afraid of being made fun of so we did not report it to anybody. Through the years I have told this to friends and family.
