{rjl} 7 Elongated M

I was driving with my father, having picked him up from work. As we headed East on Lincoln Avenue and were a few blocks from lake Michigan, we saw what looked like elongated "M" shapes over the like traveling South. We thought they were clouds or something else when they went in front of the moon and they were solid, moonlight not penetrating them. He stopped the car, we got out to look, and the 7 objects went up and out of sight.
