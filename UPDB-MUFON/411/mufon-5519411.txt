Lights morphing from two triangles to trapezoid and back. Moved erratically then disappeared

Dr Cleve Ziegler saw strange lights over the old Cavendish Mall in the early morning of Sept 30 2010. Lights were overhead, morphing from two triangles merging into a trapezoid and then back. Moved erratically at high speed in all directions then just disappeared. Total time of sighting was 3-4 minutes. Slightly overcast. Incident reported to police and apparently seen by other witnesses but exact number is unknown. Eventually covered by local news station. Dr Ziegler was uncertain of what he saw, but experienced both fear and awe.
