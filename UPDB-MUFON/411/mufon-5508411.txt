Just happen to look up at the sky, which was overcasted, and witnessed a triangle shaped dark/black ufo overhead

I was parked on a street in Long Island City which is located in Queens New York. The street I was parked on at the time is between the Mid-Town tunnel(which leads into Manhattan)and the Long Island Expressway. I adjusted my seat to lean back giving me a clear view of the sky which was overcast at the time. No soon as I looked at the sky a dark/black triangle ufo flew overhead and was out of sight in seconds. I knew right away that it was one of the triangle ufos that have been reported in the mid-west and western states. It wasnt the large mother ship reported, this was the smaller version. Like I mention it was triangle in shape and seemed to glide effortlessly through the air. The had a dull dark/black color to it, hand not lights emitting from it, it seemed to be smooth. It was so low that if it were a conventional plane I would have seen the passengers in the windows; the ufo flew towards the direction of Manhattan. When I notice the ufo fly over the car, I immediately got out to get a view of it but was no were to be seen. When I got back into my car I thought "well, you saw a triangle".
