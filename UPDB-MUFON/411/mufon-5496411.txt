{cak} circle blue green then turned red

I was coming out the front door. There was a greenish blue circle in the lower sky. Heading due north about the speed of a plane. It stopped quickly and turned red. Then greenish and then red. Turned greenish blue again and disappeared going south, There was no turning, just north and then south the speed of a plane. It was not airplane lights,red or green. This object had large lights. This is my first ufo sighting in 70 years. I have seen many things in my life, but this was the strangest . From my home in Southwest Austin, it looked like it was over Circle-C ranch subdivision in south austin. I am a couple miles north of them. These were full plane size lights not running lights.
