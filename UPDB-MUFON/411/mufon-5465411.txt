{jpg} First saw shooting star, then multiple lightning like explosions, then a beam of white light which traveled fast and wild over the mountain.

I was making deliveries at 9:45 p.m. on October 5th 2018. In Northeast Albuquerque New Mexico. Driving east toward the Sandia Mountains. I was in the foothills, east of Tramway. When I saw what I thought was a shooting star fall. as it passed the Ridgeline of the mountain in front of me. I saw a bright flash of light directly beneath where it fell. I thought I had seen a flash from it's impact. Within seconds, I saw five or six more flashes of what looked like little explosions of lightning in small clouds of smoke all along the top of the mountain. Immediately after those stopped. A bright white laser like beam of light began shooting up and down and all around the mountain terrain in front of me. This lasted for maybe 10 to 15 seconds. I have no idea what this light was. I could see the tram car for the Sandia Peak operating with his lights on. So perhaps if there was anyone on board the tram cars that night they may have seen it too.
