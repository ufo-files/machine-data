Lights in Boise foothills.

My son and niece and i were going to go to the gym. About a week before I saw a triangular shape of lights at the same time. This particular morning we saw two bright lights, along with others that appeared to come into the sky and go away, the two hill lights appeared for about 2 minutes then were gone. We all saw them. My son and neice went inside then I saw two sattelite looking lights, one approached the other and stopped then the other moved to the south and dissappered, when i looked for ther other one it was gone also. Very strange, both events, I know there is a dump located in the vacinity, but these were not gas lights, and the locales of each was definently in the sky.
