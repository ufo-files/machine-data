{cak} I was stargazing and a huge ball flew over my house, it looked like a soccer ball with segments

I was outside my house getting ready to go to work and stargazing when a huge ball that had segments like a soccer ball went over me. It reflected the light from a billboard on the highway and looked to be made from latex because it was opaque. It flew towards the west towards Camp Douglas. It tumbled as it flew, rolling in the sky, not flying in a straight line like a plane. It was gone in a few seconds over the trees.
