{cak} witnessed 2 Glowing orange red balls followed by multible blinking lights in staggered line

Out on upstairs balcony smoking and observed orange-red glowing ball of light NE in the sky. As I was observing the light another one appeared
a short distance to the left of the first object. After the second one appeared the first one blinked out. After several seconds the second one blinked out. These objects appeared to be in the same general location as an earlier report I sent in to Mufon. However these objects appeared to be closer and orange-red and globe-like in appearance.

A few minutes later still looking to see if they would reappear, I noticed
what seemed to be 3-pulsating lights in a upward odd triangle shape heading north away from me. These lights were blinking back and forth like lightning-bugs, lasting for several seconds then disappearing.

30 seconds later the pulsating lights reappeared, this time there were
around 5 of them in a staggered line running east to west with different
distances between each object. These were randomly-blinking back and forth and the line of these objects were heading north away from me. I watched until they went out of sight for around 2-minutes. If this keeps up I need to buy a good camera so I can get pictures of these things before people think Im some kind of nut.
