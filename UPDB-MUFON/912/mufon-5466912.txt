{rjl} Caught a cigar-shape in a photo during an special event at Thunderhill.

I was at Thunderhill Raceway in Willows CA, taking photos of a special event for autonomous vehicles for a client. I had taken a photo of an interview being conducted with a member of one of the AV teams while facing the mountain range. Later, after developing the photos I noticed a shape in the sky to the upper right above the mountain range. After making corrections in Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop the object became more visible. I don't know what the object is, but it is possibly cigar-shaped according to the photo.

I did not see the object at the time when the photo was taken, and I remember the sky being virtually clear. The object is not visible in the photos preceding and directly after the original photo.
