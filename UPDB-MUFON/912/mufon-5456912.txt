{tjd} At least 25 star like crafts.

Observed early this morning what I thought might be a meteor, but it didn't act like one, then I thought it was a plane but didn't make a sound and had no red flashing lights like most aircraft at night. Then the first one blinked out. Soon I saw lots of them following each other, circling each other, flying above each other closely, blinking out, hovering, and disappearing and reappearing. There were at least 25 of them and they kept this up for at least an hour until dawn. 
We also witnessed a cigar shaped object during the day. Others have also witnessed this object in this area. In fact there have been a lot of sightings around Lake of the Ozarks. [ Item moved to FI Report {tjd} ]
