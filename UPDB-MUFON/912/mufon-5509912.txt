One evening as I was one the cell phone outside speaking with a friend. I saw what appeared to be a group of stars, looking towards the nw from my driveway. From where they were positioned they would possibly be over the gulf of mexico. Then all of a sudden two stars that were close to eachother started moving in opposite directions.
