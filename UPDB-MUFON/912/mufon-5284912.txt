Observed 5 unknowns with 1 red and 4 white ones following and then all disappeared at same point.

Someone across the lake from us was having a party and the music was loud. I went outside to see where it was coming from. After I did, I noticed that there was this red line with a hook in the front above the tree line moving in a straight line with no sound. It was a solid line, and did not look normal to me. I called my husband out who also observed this but he saw it as a red orb or circle. We went down to the dock and watched as this red object went by. With this 2 white orbs one on top of each other came by following the exact same path and then 2 more singularly followed. In total there were 5. I thought when they reached a certain point over the tree line that they turned a bit but my husband said they did not. However at this certain point when each object reached this certain point. they slowly went down to where the orbs got smaller, came back to size and then disappeared. None of the objects made any noise. After they disappeared, we did observe 2 planes that flew over us and these we heard with no problem and they were higher in the sky than what we actually saw earlier.
