Saw an object in the sky that had lights moving around it and was silent

On 11-08-2008 as I was surfing the web in my home for music when I had a strong urge to look out the window and as I did I noticed a UFO moving north east at a very fast pace I could not actually see the whole body of the craft but I saw lights that appeared to be connected to something they moved around the lights which seemed to orbit something never blinked and whatever they were attached to was silent I know this because I ran to my balcony and it was close enough to where I would hear sound if it were emanating from it.

When I first noticed the object I thought it was a plane but then I realized that it was no earth plane or jet or even a helicopter as it was moving very fast, was silent and had lights moving in a circle around something which to me showed that the craft itself was circular/disc shaped.

As I watched the object I noticed it was next to a star so even though it was cloudy tonight in Maryland there was patches of sky that were cloudless and this is where the object just disappeared...it seemed to just vanish not like a usual plane that slowly ascends and vanishes above clouds it looked as though it got sucked in to a portal because as I kept watch of it there was no clouds in front, below or above it where it was and it simply vanished like it turned it's lights off that is the best I can describe it's behavior.

I have had many encounters thought my life but never as many encounters has I have had in 2008 alone in this past year I have seen over 5 UFOs which I strongly believe are not craft from this plane of existence especially a craft that I saw was made of pure white light and had the same exact behavior as what I saw tonight so my emotions are dwindling down but I as always feel excited and anxious and I feel very emotional but in a good way however I do not think I can describe exactly what I felt...it just cant be put in to words.
