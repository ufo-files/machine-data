{jpg} UFO spotted hovering with orange flame/trail. Began to move after a few minutes and the flame disappeared revealing a dark object

We were driving to the grocery store. It was a typical summer evening. First noticed the object because there were no other clouds or planes in the sky. It was very bright like an oval flat flame and not moving. We pulled to the side of the road to observe it. There was no sound. After about 5 minutes the "flame" just went out. There appeared to be a black cylinder/cigar shaped object and out of where the flame had been another smaller black object had emerged. From the time we saw it, we had no idea what it was. A plane had even flown a distance over it at normal speed and the object clearly was not a plane as it had no wings or aviation lights. During the sighting and thereafter confusion and disbelief. Still not sure what it was. We lost sight of the object because it eventually was behind some trees out of view.
