Deep red ball visible, at times there are 2,in left side of frame, 16,000 ISO on very dark March night

I shot the night sky on March 9, 2021 over (Location moved to Investigators Report Section/cms/LLL) and I was editing the photos which were terrible due to the super high ISO and noticed a bright red object in the left part of the frame. This was not visible to me at the time, only during editing. My eyes were on the stars and this appeared very close. Most of the frames in Photo Mechanic (editing software) were black, but randomly I started adjusting the exposure and discovered the red ball in a few more of the frames. Sometimes it was two red balls, then when they seemed to merge together they became dark red and more vibrant. I only adjusted for exposure, lightening the images. The quality sucks due to the high iso. I shot a total of 56 frames of about 5 slightly varied views. 12 frames have the red ball. 4 have something that looks like red lens flare. Nikon D850 ISO 16K, 14MM lens, 2.8, Exposure varied from 1/2.5 to 1/4th to 1/6th. Clock time is wrong. Last image, DSC_9362 looks like lens flare ? and was shot before the red ball light appeared.
