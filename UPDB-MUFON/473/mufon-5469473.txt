Pulsating sphere

I have seen these objects a lot of times at distance. This particular evening I was looking towards the ocean from my house when I noticed the object approaching from the West. I knew it was not ordinary as I definitively know the difference between Satellites, Planes, Helicopters, Comets, Shooting stars etc etc. So I saw it approaching and I heard my neighbor outside talking on his deck. I called out to him to come take a look. At this stage the object was directly over our properties. His partner had come outside at this stage and she also witnessed it. It appeared to be a dull grey metallic sphere that pulsated a brilliant white light at random intervals. I have seen these appear out of nothing in the night sky many times but this time it was really close and it was seen by 2 of my good friends who up to this point were quite skeptical about these kinds of things. We just watched this thing hover/fly over our houses in amazement to which i said to my friends "I told you I seen em" It just carried on a straight path on its East path until it was no longer visible to us. I call em 'sparklers' because thats what they do. Like I said, I have seen em at great distance before at night, but this time it was just getting a lil dark at dusk when it came the closest I have ever seen for the 1st time. On the 26/03/2018 which was last night I saw another object appear out of nothing in a clear night sky also. This one was an orange colored half circle that appeared and disappeared within a 3 second time. Directly above my house once again...but thats a different story...Its like I get a feeling that I know im gna see them, then I see them.
