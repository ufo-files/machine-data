{cak} Me & my wife viewed these crafts at the same time

It or they were triangular in shape , it was a burnt metallic dark grey in color , with some small white lights , moving slowly.across the sky , then it changed direction & moved up & away slowly , it was nighttime on a semi clear night , with stars in the sky , the craft moved to an altitude that made it's lights blend in with the stars it seemed , then it stopped moving & when it started moving again there was alot of lights moving in unison in the same direction , then they all stopped moving ,& suddenly they all at once shot off at once , like a bullet , at the speed of a bullet .... me & my wife whatched this at the same time , not saying a word during the event , as soon as they were gone out of sight ( seconds ) we both broke & said to each other , DID YOU SEE THAT !!
