{rjl} We saw a diamond shaped flying object that appeared to be made of fire.

My girlfriend and I were staying several nights in Cape May,New Jersey. While walking back to our room one night,we both saw an object fly towards us from over the ocean.the object was diamond shaped,about thirty feet tall and seemed to be comprised entirely of flames. Although there was likely a solid object beneath the flames, we could not see it.it flew a few hundred feet off the ground at about sixty miles per hour until it had passed over our heads when it suddenly shot straight up at a very high rate of speed and was out of sight in a second. I now wonder if the flames were due to some type of plasma effect.
