(GS) Between five and six red/orange glowing orbs or lights flew across skies in the northern suburbs of Chicago

Location: 4th floor balcony facing west off an apartment, sitting out talking/looking at the stars/sky
I first noticed the five red/orange lights because I have experience with aviation and flying and immediately noticed it was not a plane. I pointed it out to my friend and she watched as well. The lights move from north to west and spun around each other horizontally sort of flickering. At this point we began to notice that the stars were moving in a vibrating motion, while this was happening the lights disappeared and I decided we should go inside at which point we told my sister and continued to watch. We went outside at 10:20 PM, about 25 minutes after the sighting and the stars all appeared to have moved from where they were to directly above us.
