{nd} triangle shape with one light on each corner

we were in south Jackson TN around 12 midnight in fall of 2001. observed a triangle shaped object come in from the northeast . it made no sound of any type . was slow moving. it stopped over the top of us momentarily. and then moved off towards the southwest. It was at least 100 ft. from corner to corner. and hovered 100 too 200 ft. off of the ground.
