{jpg} Flickering lights appeared in sky at some distance

Went outside at 10:55pm for a smoke and noticed a flickering light to the east. My first thought was the moon, but when I checked for the moon location using time and date website, the moon was 11 degrees above the horizon which would place it behind the houses in my neighborhood.
I took three pictures and one video of the event. Started as two objects, then a third lit up and formed a triangle.
