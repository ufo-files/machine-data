{cak} Witnessed ball when smoking on patio.

At approx 720 this morning I went out to smoke and saw what I first thought was a hot air balloon.  I then determined it was not as it was round and metallic like. It did not move at all. I asked my daughter to bring me my cell phone and took several pictures.  I then went in to use the bathroom for around a minute and when I returned it was gone. It went from stationary to gone.
