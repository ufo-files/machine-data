4 glowing red orang balls in eastern sky

My husband and his best friend were putting away materials after building stairs on our back door when they yelled into the house Get out here NOW and went back out into the driveway. I observed two of these orbs, the top one just looked like a planet or star and didn't move other than kind of flicker or twinkle. The bottom one moved to the left, hovered for a minute then just disappeared, like someone turned out the light. The other one remained without moving and the three of us decided it must be a planet/star, but then it disappeared the same way the other one had. Before I got out there, my husband and his friend observed three of them turn in sync in an arc before two disappeared. These were not meteors, planes, Chinese lanterns, etc. I have no idea what they could have been.
