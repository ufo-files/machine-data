Walking at night and saw a bright deep red beam (Crystal shaped) on side of low flying craft.

Went for a walk tonight (August 31, 2011) and saw a bright red light moving slowly through the sky in a straight line no more than 500 feet altitude and no more than a half mile away. It was traveling North initially. It moved approximately a quarter mile after my initial sighting and as it approach me I could see there was a disk-shaped side to it. It then turned 90 degrees in a disciplined fashion and continued on for a short distance and changed another 90 degrees and then it wasn't visible since the light beam was now behind the craft and it wasn't seen again. During the same evening I observed approximately 6 - 7 star-like color shifting objects in the sky. These were all distributed fairly evenly in a circle about the size of 100 square miles. These are stationary and flicker Blue, red, green, white purple etc. These do not move and are not geosynchronous. They are easily discernible from surrounding stars and a visible planet this night.
