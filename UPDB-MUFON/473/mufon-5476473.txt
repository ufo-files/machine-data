Traveling across the mountains from Bergen to Oslo Norway. Saw white cigar shape fly across the sky

I was travelling in a car across the mountains from Bergen to Oslo Norway it was a bright sunny day and I noticed what I thought was a white plane fly across the sky but thought it was strange because I could not see any wings or details on the plane because it was just white. So I decided to take a photo with my mobile phone. I then lost sight of the object when it disappeared behind a mountain. It all seem to happen very quickly and then it was gone. Once I got home and looked at the photo I noticed more anomolys as I zoomed in on the picture.
