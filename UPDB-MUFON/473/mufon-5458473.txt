{rjl} Brightly glowing orb

I went out on my deck one evening in the dark to look over the San Pasqual Valley.
As I looked to the east I suddenly saw a bright glowing orb shaped object that appeared behind the light fog in the saddle between two hill tops to the east of me . The glow started out like a bright light and gained intensity as I continued staring at it in wonder.  Very rapidly, it started to glow and get hotter and hotter turning yellow to orangish red and appeared to me that something was ready to shoot or emanate out from the center of the bright orb. At this point I would say this is the brightest thing I have ever seen in the sky by five times, it was starting to alarm me as I had not ever seen anything like this ever before.  Tears appeared in my eyes as I could not believe what I was witnessing and I was sure something was about to shoot out from the center of this when suddenly everything stopped and it disappeared instantly ...
