{cak} a friend an I saw a very bright star on horizon. in 10 min's 2 pink orbs appeared, then moved to the star, they moved back and bright star was hovering right above us, above a tree about 70 feet tall, bright triangle, humming

A friend and I were camping at his 20 acre property overlooking the "Omega Diggins" in Nevada County CA. with a third friend, old buddies from the 1970's in school. Aug 17, 2007 around 9:30pm the first friend leaves and goes to sleep. Mike and I are star gazing, warm and clear night, sitting behind his cabin looking to the east toward "Grouse Ridge and north over the Yuba River Canyon. First we saw an abnormally bright star above the east horizon. it caught our attention, and it didn't move for at least 10 min's when 2 pinkish, fuzzy orbs appeared above it at 10:00 and 2:00. We were both glued to the sight when both orbs started descending toward the star both stopping below it. after 15 seconds the orbs started back up to their original spots and a little beyond, the star suddenly started rapidly moving towards us. It looked to be many miles away, possibly 50 or more when within seconds it was hovering right above us. We were in a small clearing next to some 50 to 75 foot cedar trees, the perfect triangle craft was hovering about 5 to 10 feet above a tree 20 feet to our left. We were both glued to our chairs stunned. It made a very prominent humming sound, no propellers. The perfect triangle underbelly was three bright frosty circular lights that covered all I could see. for only a minute or two, we looked up at asking the obvious, what was it. We knew what it was, when the last I remember happened, I remember suddenly being really scared I shielded the left side of my face with my hand as I cowered and whispered to my friend "they know we're here" repeatedly 3 or 4 times. I was too scared to look. Then, suddenly, not afraid I sat up straight and declared f**k it, it's just an airplane. We agreed, and without even looking back up a single time, we left to the front of the cabin to gaze at the southern stars. After about 15 to 20 minutes, both really tired, saw the time at 12:15pm and both went to our places and went to sleep. Several hours had passed, yet I only remember the first 20 and last 20 minutes. My friend has never spoken about it. I developer a boil type welt the following week 1 inch into hair on center of back of neck.
