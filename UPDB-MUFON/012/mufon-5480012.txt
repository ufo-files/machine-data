{jpg} Legends park explosion

Legends park explosion, a small explosion was seen at legends park niagara falls on tuesday. Hundreds of witnesses heard and saw a bright blue flash from miles away, all the way to canada! People reported rattling windows and ground shaking. After the explosion which was heard no crater was found at the sight of the flash which was captured on camera. After this happened witnesses are claiming that a commercial plane was seen being escorted by a fighter jet leaving the air force base. Others as far away as the fredonia foothills 90 min south saw multiple flashes from the city. After the event others have said that a similar explosion happened on October 2nd but was not published in the news or on tv. All of these stories were called in to local radio stations.
