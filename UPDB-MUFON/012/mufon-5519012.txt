Orange object heading west, changed direction south, hovered 10 minutes

The family was outside when we noticed a bright orange light heading west in the sky. We watched it move slowly across the sky, stop, then change direction and head south. It moved extremely slow, like nothing we had ever seen in the sky before. The object sat in the sky for about 10 minutes, changing color from orange, to red, to white. After not moving for a period of time, it disappeared in the blink of an eye.
