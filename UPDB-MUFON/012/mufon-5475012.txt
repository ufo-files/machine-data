I was looking at the weather on Sat24 and noticed this object, not a plane but big and high speed.

These pictures are taken from sat24 Africa satellite and daytime map on 23
July 2017. They show a satellite image looking at the sub Saharan Africa region, specifically the boarder between Sudan and chad. At 09.45 there is nothing but cloud but at 10.00 you can clearly see something moving at high speed coming out of the boarder area between north Sudan and Chad. At 10.15 the object is bigger and by the distance covered travelling at a very high speed, then at 10.30 it is gone. It seems to be coming out of north Sudan and curving into Chad in a SW direction.
