{jpg} An orb got blindingly bright and shot straight up leaving a trail of smoke and glow. No sound.

2:30am I just let the dogs out. And was standing outside smoking a cig with my light. And to the south a dim light caught my eye. It got brighter and brighter so fast, seconds even. It was like lightning it was so bright then it shot straight up in the clear night sky. It had no noise at all and it left a trail of smoke, orange glow and it stayed a few seconds then nothing. I was so scared my cig dropped right out of my mouth, I was frozen in fear. So close to our house where my 16 month old son is sleeping. I was shaking so bad checking the sky to make sure it was gone. To close for comfort. Scary.
