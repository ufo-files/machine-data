{cak} 26 Orange lights flying from North and turning West before disappearing

From my driveway, I saw a formation of two sets of 4 orange lights followed by another formation of 3 orange lights, then multiple sets of 2, followed by singles, half a minute to a minute apart. I counted a total of 26 objects. I could not hear any sounds coming from them. When I took a picture of them on my cellphone and zoomed the picture to see better, the lights had a disc-like appearance. I called two of my neighbors and none of us was able to make sense of it.
