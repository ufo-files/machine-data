My wife and I were moving from New Mexico to California on March 13 1997. We had come through Tucson and came up I-10 to the intersection of I-10 and I-8.As we drove north on I-10 we had noticed several lights in the sky towords Phoenix and watched them until after we had gone a few miles on I-8. 


Between the beginning of the I-8 and Gila Bend there is a small rise in the tarrian. The highway goes up this rige and goes between two hills of rocks. As we approached the top of this rige,all of a sudden the ground lit up like daylight. It was only for a second or two but whatever it was it was so bright that we could see for miles. My wife looked at me and I looked at her and I pulled the truck over and we got out. We looked around and never saw nor heard anything. After a while we got back In the truck and continued on our way to California.

On our arrival in California everything was ok except I found we were missing about 2 hours. I can not explain what happened and it has bothered me since it happend. Were we obducted? I don't know. My wife and I are no longer together and I am getting old so I thought it would be time to get this on record befor I die. I also no longer live in the United States, I now live in Thailand. The best way to get in touch with me is by my email address. I don't know if this is the kind of thing you are interested in but if you are please don't hesitate to ask me any questions you want to. I would like to know what happened too
