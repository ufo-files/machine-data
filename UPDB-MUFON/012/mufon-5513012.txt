(GS) Timelapse video of stars revealed glowing star-like stationary object that turned on then off then on then off again

[Link moved to FI section/cms/tg]

The above link is to a time-lapse movie my friend posted on YouTube. 

It was taken the night of July 21, 2012 in Twin Mountain, NH. The view is pointing due south with the lowest point showing a peak (Twin Mountain North?) behind the roof of a cottage. About a 1/4 of the way through the video there is a light source that does not move with the stars. His guess is that its a Geo-synchronous satellite reflecting the light from either the Sun or Moon. The location of the light is about 5/8 of the frame up from the bottom on the left side of the frame near 2 branches that form a sideways "V". The light source goes bright then disappears for a total time of 10 min. then 25 min goes by without it showing then it reappears for 11.5 min. The light source is obvious since it doesnt move with the motion of the stars in the background. View it full screen so you can see the myriad of stars zipping by. The digital video camera was set up to capture a new frame every 15 seconds. He noticed the stationary light after reviewing the time lapse file at the end of the evening. The camera was set up again the following evening but nothing showed itself in the sky.
