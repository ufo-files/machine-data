Redish-orange fireball traveling from NW to SE.

I was in Saint Paul, Minnesota getting into my car when I glanced up and saw a redish-orange light/fireball flying in my direction. At first I dismissed it as a UFO because I live with a few miles of an international airport, but as the light/fireball got closer and closer, there was no noise. It flew in a perfectly straight line and after a few minutes, the light/fireball was so far away, I just couldnt see it anymore.
