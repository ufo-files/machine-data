{cak} Looked to sky, saw what I assumed was a plane with lights trailing behind, lights scattered, disappeared...one light in opposite direction went into a cloud, did not see it reappear.

I stepped outside my home, facing south, looked at the beautiful sky, saw what I thought at the time was a military jet, heading east. Noticed that there appeared to be bright smaller orbs behind the craft, star-like. I realized, what I was looking at did NOT look like a jet, but a triangular shape, hazy in nature, so in the very short time I had to observe it, I was focused more on the lights. The orbs scattered, but I managed to see one heading west and went into a small cloud and I never saw it reappear. With that distance, I could see 6 or 7 orbs. I was not shocked at what I believe I was seeing, I yelled to my husband that I was seeing UFOS. it had moved so far east that he was unable to see it and it was a very bright day with very light cloud cover.
