Triangle shaped craft

Triangle shaped craft was spotted by my youngest son. He described a large triangle with lights on the underside. He said it was dark and traveled in a straight line and showed me exactly where he saw it traveling in a northwesterly direction, visible from the rear of our house. It would have had to have been very close to see it, as we have woods behind us that limit how far you can see. I asked him to immediately draw a picture of what he saw for me. What he drew astounded me.
