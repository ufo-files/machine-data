Shooting bright light over city

Was driving when I noticed a flash of light above the city... bright light moved from east to west, almost like a comet or meteor but it was a much bigger and brighter object than I'd ever seen, and it did't move as quickly as other meteors ("shooting stars") I've seen before. It went off over the horizon... it was quite something, one of those things where I hoped someone else saw it but couldn't believe my own eyes.
