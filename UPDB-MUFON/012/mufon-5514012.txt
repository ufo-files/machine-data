{nd} Very slow moving triangle shape craft with no sound flying south.

Something made me look out my window around 5:50 pm. and I looked directly at a triangle
shaped object moving very slowly across the sky. it had 3 blinking pulsating
red lights with a yellow light in the middle of the craft.
I could see the outline of the craft even though it was dark outside.
I got my camera & keys & headed to the car to follow it & take a picture but in the few seconds it took to get outside, it was gone.
Shortly after that I heard the sound of jet engines , like military planes use.
