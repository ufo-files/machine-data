Yellow orange lights hovering over treetop

My boyfriend and I were sitting outside enjoying the evening. We were both facing East. My boyfriend turned to me to say something that's when he seen the lights hovering just over the tree top, he immediately got my attention, I turned and looked and seen the lights hovering in the same spot, the lights flashed in a Marquee pattern. My boyfriend just stared at it, I ran to the house to get more witnesses but it was gone by the time I got back. There was absolutely no sound or wind. Within minutes of us seeing it it was seen 20 miles to the east.The next day we walked in the field and found nothing.
The photo attached shows where it was above the trees and the video shows how the lights were moving. Please do research on this.
