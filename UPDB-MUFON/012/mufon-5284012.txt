Massive circular disc ejects orb

Approximately 6:45 in Bethlehem Georgia. Driving East on highway 81, my fiancé and I spotted an extremely bright light at some distance away. Upon further viewing, it was a massive disc like craft with green lights blinking on either side. The ship proceeded to eject an orb like object downwards. The orb accelerated downward rapidly, then stopped on a dime immediately about 5 seconds later. The ship mentioned first, proceeded to travel northwest at a slower speed and out of view. The orb hovered in the same position it had been in since stopping for approximately 15 seconds, then proceeded to travel West at a constant slower speed then it had ejected from the craft.
