bright light flying with jet

it was just before 10 PM Germany time, sclear blue sky, sunny as the planes looked like being on fire. we were on the balcony with my husband when I spotted a bright light flying with a jet on the right side of it. It looked to have been above the plane when it surpassed it and turned left. The plane turned slowly right and the light followed for a bit then changed direction again to the left and kept straight line before we couldn't see it anymore. We thought it is another jet but another one passed and seemed to be the cruise altitude. Then we thought it could be a satelite but those don't change direction and move way faster than the planes.
