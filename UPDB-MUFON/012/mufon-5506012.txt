{kt}Short sighting, 3 helicopters or planes with two or more UFOs

I was standing outside the hotel Im at looking towards the Franklin Mountains (Past which is El Paso Airport as well as a military base)I like to look up at the stars and the flashing red of a plane or chopper caught my eye, oddly I saw another set of flashing lights behind it some distance then my jaw dropped, Ive seen UFOs before but never a triangle. In between both of the other aircraft was a triangle with a green light at each point also forming a triangle. I called my wife who was inside at the time and told her to run outside and fast (all objects and aircraft were going quite slow.) After I hung up, two of the 3 green lights dimmed out followed by the third. She missed them, but after she came out I pointed them out and she noticed a third aircraft with the flashing red lights behind the first two where the triangle was in between, though in front of this last one was a dull not lit object. They all kept flying out of my view but never altered course or sped/slowed down except the triangles lights went out. It was crazy!
