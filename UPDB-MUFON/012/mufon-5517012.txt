{nd} what i thought was a star and a possible satelite now has me wondering

it was a little before midnight, i was alone on my sisters back patio smoking a cigarette and looking up at the stars. i noticed what appeared to be a star traveling across the sky at a steady pace (seen this before but always thought it was satelites orbitting the earth). As the "traveling star/satelite" made its way closer to a "brighter star" (at arms length, measuring with my fingers, approximately an inch away). the "brighter star" casted a light towards the "traveling star". at first, the light looked spread out but quickly narrowed to more of a beam. like it "focused" onto the traveling star/satelite. the "focus" took less than a second, almost instantly but slow enough to see it happen. the light being casted lasted for about 3-5 seconds then the light went out and less than a second later, the "bright star" dissapeard as the traveling star/satelite kept traveling through the sky. the entire experience was only a matter of seconds and i know the "bright star" was not an airplane as there were no other lights(green/red flashing). before it casted its light, it just looked like a star.
