{cak} My stepson and I witnessed a cigar shaped object hovering in plain view very close to a road

We saw a cigar shaped object hovering early one morning on the way to work. It appeared to be silver mostly, with a touch of pinkish to the right, the bottom appeared to me to be light. Can,t say I saw light, but, rather, it appeared the bottom was maybe emitting light. It was hovering. Over a hilltop, at tree level, and appeared to be about half as tall as the trees, Douglas firs, so, am am quest imaging them to be 100 foot tall, thus making the object 50feet tall approximately. The object appeared to be in the 200-300 foot long ballpark. I saw it first, clear view, no clouds in the sky. I asked stepson what it was, he said he didn't know. I knew a road that lead to the exact area, so I raced to it, checked my watch, was there in one minute and a few seconds. The object was nowhere to be seen. I knew what I was seeing wasn,t normal, so I felt the need to go to it, I had no fear, just major curiosity. We got out of the vehicle, and had a view that We could easily see 30 miles or so in every direction at least. Nothing to be seen, anywhere in the sky, no sound, no nothing. The surface area appeared to be untouched. We walked around awhile looking for the object, listening, and examine the ground for any signs . Nothing. The object had no wings, no rotor, no conventional shape of any type known flying object. My only regret was not putting my passenger out, to observe the object as I raced to it. I can only assume, as far as I could see, it traveled from a hovering stop to being totally out of a 30 mile view in any direction, so, do the math, it disappeared very quickly.
