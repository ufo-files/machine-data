{rjl} Observed 5 Bright Lights heading toward us.

My Father is a Retired US Marine who Observed Ballistic missile launches from Nuclear Subs, Guarded SR71 in Japan worked Embassy Duty and Guard Marine 1. I was a UH-60 Black Hawk flight crew member working in several joint military operations. We are both Extremely well trained Observers. At first it looked like a Medivac chopper coming into land. Then about 15 Seconds later a Second Extremely bright light appeared to be trailing 1st. Eventually there were 5 Objects moving towards us and about the same time my Father also noticed there was no sound coming from the Objects. I grabbed my cellphone and was able to Record the last objects. Shortly before flying over us they all made a course direction change then Suddenly VANISHED. Sky was clear with no clouds. Once returning home I called the Parkersburg,Wv Airport ATC and asked if he had any Aircraft on Radar at that time or Flight Plan. Then I called WTAP television to see if anyone else reported this Sighting. They had heard nothing. Our local Airport only has 2 Commercial flights a day. Please call me.
