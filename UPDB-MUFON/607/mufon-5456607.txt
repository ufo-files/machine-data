{jpg} 49 craft witnessed flying near my home.

I went out in the backyard at about 5:45am to drink coffee and smoke. I was surprised to see some smallish flat on the bottom and a dome on the top, no lights no sound and they either glowed from within or were reflecting the light available around them craft, moving to the NE. I started counting and as I did I walked over a few steps to see where they were coming from. This is what I saw: the object would appear from out of nowhere, move forward slightly then drop down to the level of the other craft flying. There was no object or other larger craft visible that they were coming out of, I can only speculate that the object was invisible or it was coming from an opening of another dimension. I have seen other items disappear into another dimension in the same area so it was just very odd. The objects were flying in a straight line approximately 2 seconds apart and as it got closer to the end there were a couple groups of 3 or 4 looking like they were jockeying for a place in line or playing around. I did not have my phone handy to try and get a video of this, I doubt it would have been bright enough for the camera on my phone to detect it. I am not a crack pot, I do not have any mental issues that would make me think this up.  My next sighting is even more objects, in different shapes going South. I have also been having a form of communication with one of the groups out there and would like to speak to a Mufon representative. Thank you.
