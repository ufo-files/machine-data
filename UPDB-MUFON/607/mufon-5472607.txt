While flying my drone I noticed two flashes of light go directly under my drone and one off to the left

I was taking video of the fall foliage around our local lake.
I noticed this bright flash of light fly under my drone after I got home and reviewed the video I shot in 2.4K. You can see the first flash of light at the 11:35 time frame then the second at the 11:40 mark. The flashes go so fast that if you blink your eye you will miss it. The first flash is on the left side of the video and the second is on the right bottom going under my drone. Both were perpendicular to each other about 5 seconds apart going east to west. My altitude was approximately 800-850 feet.
I have seen a similar video of another drone flight a retired Air Force pilot took of the same flash of light passing under his drone.
I really do not believe that I captured this video, WOW!
