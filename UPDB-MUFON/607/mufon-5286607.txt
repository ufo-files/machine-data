Observed with naked eye and in 10x50 binoculars orange flames ascending and descending in sky in remote area of Mojave Desert.

While driving east on Highway 62, just west of Rice, California, I observed from a distance (greater than a mile) low in the night sky (about 10:00 PM) a ball of orange flames hovering in a remote area of the Mojave Desert. The flames then slowly descended behind a small mountain, then reappeared as it ascended at the same rate. This pattern repeated over a period of 5-10 minutes, enough time for me to stop my vehicle and retrieve a pair of 10x50 binoculars I had in my trunk. I continued to observe the object in the binoculars and saw that it consisted of flames in considerable detail. I had a map, and from my location it was apparent that no military base or airport was anywhere near the remote, unpopulated area where this object appeared.
