{jpg} square shaped object descended from what appeared to be about 5 miles from ground level, to below visible treeline, then shot directly upwards

Driving on US Hwy 74 Westbound between Shelby NC, and Kings Mountain NC. With the mountain illuminated by by glow of the full moon behind some thin clouds. The craft was first noticeable above the clouds and appeared as just a blue-ish hue above the clouds. Craft then came flying at rapid speed through the clouds, which were disturbed by this, and approached the forest between US 74 and the mountain itself. Craft at this time appeared to be about 5 miles away and about 150ft across I would judge. Craft was blue-lit, vert softly but with brightness that could be seen from a massive distance. Craft was cube or square in nature. Craft then reached the treeline, before which I would estimate speed at roughly before the sound barrier due to the clouds being blasted apart around the craft as it descended. Upon reaching treeline, craft reversed course and shot upward in the same trajectory and disappeared from sight. I was driving alongside another vehicle and by this time we have both slowed down to a near crawl. Other witness said they saw it too, and didn't hear anything, nor I. At this point entity seemed to be gone and I continued to drive but did not see any other lights.
