{kt}On the beach looking for meteors when observed a motionless flashing object in sky

Around 9:30 we were looking for meteors over the ocean at Belmar NJ when noticed a flashing light coming from a motionless object to the north. It did not move for 30 minutes. Object flashed from white to green to red. We watched till finally it decended. Thought maybe it was a baloon or kite with some sort of lights attached. Have found online others have seen the same object.
