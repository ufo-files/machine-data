{ck} My sister and I witnessed three orangish orbs in the sky (3) within a two minute period. They disappeared into thin air. Clear night.

My sister and I witnessed three orbs of orange/red color fly through the night sky as we were watching fireworks. The first orb like object flew over our house and stopped for seconds and then continued on until we could no longer see it. We then noticed a second one with a similar flight pattern and then it dispappeared into the night sky. During the second sighting, an small aircraft (airplane) was flying in the opposite direction. The third orb we sighted was coming from the south, but veered off into the opposite flight pattern as the other two orbs. The third one also disappeared into the night sky. It is a very clear night in Brier.
