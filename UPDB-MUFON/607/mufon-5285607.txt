Craft descended turned of its lights and hovered above us.

A very bright light that initially looked like a start started approaching us and getting larger lighting up the darkness as if it was day. When it got closer it turned of the bright white light and I could see red and blue vent looking areas going in a circle on the bottom of the craft. It stopped right above us maybe 500ft in the air and sat there I assumed watching us while we stared at it. I honestly don’t remember details on how it left but after it was gone I was scared and then saw a plane and decided to leave out of fear that’s it would come back.
