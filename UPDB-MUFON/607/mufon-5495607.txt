{nd} just above treetops, ascended, hovered, and descended, then flew along a path away into the distance

I was outside with my boyfriend who noticed what he thought was a star. After looking at the light I realized it was changing colors from orange, red, and then green. It would hover a while then ascend in a bobble-like motion and descend in the same manner. I was stunned because I have never seen anything in the sky with that kind of movement. We watched it for about five minutes before I called my children outside to look at it. They observed the same thing that my boyfriend and I had observed. We watched about another five minutes then it started traveling farther away from us, still just above the tree tops until we couldn't see it anymore.
