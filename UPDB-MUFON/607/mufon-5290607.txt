Two sightings about 20 years apart

I just wanted to know if any pilots ever reported this event. It was near Schlemeyer Field in Odessa, TX. I was only about 10 years old at the time so I never really told anyone because I knew they wouldn’t believe me.  There were two jets flying SW I believe. The 3rd “jet” coming from the same direction but much further behind the other two jets. Then all of a sudden the #3 speeds past the two jets and it doesn’t seem to have lights or wings like the jets did. It flys past heading SW, then back East, and rapidly speeds off heading East out of sight. 

I witnessed another one in Odessa again in about 2015 and I remember it being even more clearly an out of this world craft because of the speed and the way it was able to maneuver, however for some reason I can’t remember a thing about the event. I don’t remember directions or even where I was standing when I witnessed it. 

Has anyone reported these events before?
