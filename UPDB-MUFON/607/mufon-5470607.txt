{dr}Saw a star-like light far away, circle/ringed shaped, slowly went up and seemed to disappear

I was driving home and coming down a slight hill and looking diagonally upward was a star-like object that seemed to be circle/ringed shaped. The object hovered for approximately three seconds then it slowly went up and seemed to vanish into thin air. I knew exactly what this was when I saw it. I ruled out it being a star or aircraft when it had an odd ring/circle shape and seemed to disappear. I'm very interested in these things and knew what to do when I saw it and that was to submit the sighting here. The object slowly ascended then suddenly vanished/disappeared.
