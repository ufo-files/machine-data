{jpg} DOZEN TIC TAC WHITE OBJECTS MATERIALIZE THEN DISAPPEAR

WHILE WALKING BACK INTO THE BUILDING TO WORK I WAS STANDING UNDER A SHADE TREE AT 12:45 PM LOOKING AT THE CLOUDS TO THE NW OF ME WHEN ABOUT A DOZEN WHITE SMALL TIC TAC OBJECTS MATERIALIZED ON THE WEST SIDE OF THE CLOUD.  THEY FLOATED THROUGH THE CLOUD AND FORMED A WEIRD SHAPE. REMAINED IN THIS SHAPE FOLLOWING A SOUTH WESTERLY DIRECTION. FOR ABOUT A MINUTE OR SO.  ONCE THEY WERE OVERHEAD THEY SPLIT INTO TWO GROUPS OF 6/7 EACH AND FORMED A V. NO NOISE NO GLARE ANYTHING.  I WATCHED AND REALIZED I NEED TO GRAB MY PHONE IN MY PURSE AND REACHED FOR IT WHEN I COULDN'T FIND IT RIGHT AWAY I JUST WENT BACK TO WATCHING THE OBJECTS AND A FEW SECONDS LATER THEY APPEARED TO BE TURNED ON THEIR ENDS AND THE VERY CENTER WAS A WHITE GLOWING PERFECT CIRCLE.  THIS WAS VISIBLE FOR A FEW SECONDS AND THEN THEY VANISHED JUST IN THE PLAIN SKY. NO CLOUD NEAR WHERE THEY WERE. COMPLETELY DISAPPEARED IT WAS BEYOND BELIEF.
