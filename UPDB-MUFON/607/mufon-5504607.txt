{kt}multiple light objects observed, bright lights emitting multi-colors, 2-3 at a time, random and controlled manuevers

I was layying in bed watching TV when I saw lights near my animal pasture & barn. Being I have heard of problems in the neighborhood with the local chapter of Future Felons of America I keep a few cameras out with monitors in the house. Via the monitor it appeared there was someone in the pasture so I went out to deal with them. I saw multiple objects ranging in size from perhaps a basketball to a small car! They objects emited lights that changed apparently randomly to every conceivable color in the spectrum! They appeared 2-3 at a time. Manuevered in apparently random yet controlled (if that makes sense) manners. As they varied wildly in speed & direction yet hit no obstructions ie trees powerlines, my barn etc! It was the strangest thing! I took MANY pictures & shot a lot of video and upon reviewwing the video I had considerable static in the audio!! I have never had this static on other ocassions & could NOT replicate it! The objects would come and move about individually or pairs and I saw trees and powerlines on both sides so I know what size & range they were. My animals were in the barn silently staring at eachother! The objects would vary speed, direction, color, brightness and altitude! I am a fairly observant individual so I noted everything I could & I had heard a helecopter an hour earlier and while I was watching this show an aircraft passed at altitude leaving a visable contrail. I could see its lights & knew it to be a passenger/cargo jet. When I saw these lights higher up! They appeared similar to the objects seen before me but were higher up. At times they would pass above the jet contrail being dimmed by it, other times apparently below it. Their speed was almost as amazing as everything else about them. I saw one traverse 3/4 of the sky in about 2 seconds, LITERALLY! Then at speed make a 90 deg turn! And yet another & head back to its original point of origin! I watched these for id guesss about two hrs until the last one appeared to lift vertically then pause for a second or two & upon emitting a flash of light depart at an incredible speed!!!!! These were NOT lantern kites, balloons, reflections off swamp gas NOR a comet!! I'm not claiming them to BE anything.. I would like to know what they were.. its rather upsetting to not be able to discuss this with anyone!
