{nd} over dozen bright dome/jellyfish shaped objects at different altitudes and distances flying at same speed and direction.

My wife and I were driving from Norristown heading to Collegeville on Ridge Pike when I noticed three of these bright objects flying in a cross trajectory in front of us.I saw them as they were in direct path of my vision. My first instinct was these were UFOs of some sort.We were both amazed as we have not seen objects of their shape glowing as intense as they were glowing before in the sky. We pulled over to observe when more objects became visible. All of the objects were flying at the same speed at different altitudes and with no apparent flight pattern. The best description we could give is they looked like glowing mushrooms or jellyfish. It was a strange and odd situation. We finally lost sight of the objects as they continued their flight path until local homes, treas and buildings obstructed our veiw. As we drove away on our continued route we saw two more flying on the same path from the same direction as the others in the sky. I lost sight of the last two as I had to focus my attention back on the road to drive in traffic.
