{dr}Planet like object overhead, Los Angeles

Observed what I thought was Jupiter overhead. It was odd given the time and brightness. It was bright enough that I felt compelled to show it to my daughter; as we headed out for a walk. Roughly 8 to 12 minutes later I looked up again and the star-like object was no where in sight. While observing the object it remained stationary. Other objects were in the air, two airplanes-- inbound to LAX, and the moon SE sky. I never took a photograph thinking it was a planet.
