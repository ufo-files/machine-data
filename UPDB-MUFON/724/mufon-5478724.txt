Creepy photo that I took shows a ufo that I didn't see whilst taking the photo.

I was on holiday with my family in Salcombe, England. We were on a big walk on the hills near the dock going into the sea. I wanted to take a photo of the scenery because it looked really nice. When I took the photo, a strange object appeared. I didn't see it before or whilst it was being taken. I didn't feel anything, nothing around us happened. It was just normal. But I felt really weird, like I'd just seen something I shouldn't. It had just... appeared. I didn't see it in real life. When I got the photo, I lost it. It had deleted itself for some reason out of my camera roll. However I re found it as I saved it to my memories on SnapChat. It looked like a disc and it doesn't fit into any animal shape I know. Again at the end of my holiday (a few days later) my phone got smashed. I don't know if these are just coincidences but I did again get my phone fixed and all my photos restored. I did post it on my SnapChat story which gets about 30 views, and I showed it to my friends and family on different occasions.
