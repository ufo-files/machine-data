It was saucer light with strange blinking lights.

We were playing touch football with other teenagers in front of 1444 Parkham Crescent, just North of the Pickering Nuclear Power Plant in July, 1968, when our parents noticed weird lights hovering near the Power Plant. We watched for a good 15 minutes wondering what they were. Then a bunch of army trucks went speeding down Sandy Beach Road toward the Power Plant when the craft just zoomed off and was gone in a second. Later on CFTO news, Dave Devall reported that it was a weather balloon, which did not make sense to any of us. First of all, it was hovering for quite a long time and then it took off, faster than anything that I had ever seen before or since. It was not a weather balloon or any type of airplane or helicopter. I have written about it on Facebook and there are others that were not in our group that night who also witnessed it.
