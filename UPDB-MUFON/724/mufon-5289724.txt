I was what appeared to be a short oblong comet, approaching the earth, but not coming any closer

My daughter and I went downstairs in the morning, opened the window, and sat on the couch to read a book. My daughter looked out the window and asked “is that a star”? I got up to look and said that it looked like a comet or an asteroid. Then I became confused because the object didn’t appear to be moving at all, or if so it was barely perceptible. I grabbed my phone to record a video, but my phone was out of memory. I went to grab another camera, but it had difficulty capturing the object because it was so far away. I have a second or two of this footage. As I was fiddling with the camera to take more video with a better lens, I looked away and my daughter said “it’s gone”. Before my phone was full, I got two 1 second long clips. In the first clip, the object is closer and through the entire 1 second it does not appear to move. In the second clip, it appears more dim and farther away, and lower on the horizon.
