We were at a friends house. There were a total of 7 adults present. We were sitting at a chiminea and watching the stars, waiting for expected meteor shower. The homeowner pointed upward to an area of the sky behind where I was sitting. He said, "what is that?". We looked and there was an orange/red glowing orb shaped light in the sky. It traveled rather slowly from the western sky into the east. It didn't slow, stop or anything else. It kind of twinkled. We watched in awe and wondered if we had just seen a real UFO. It traveled out of sight behind some trees.
