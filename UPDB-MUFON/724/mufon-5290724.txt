A basketball sized sphere appeared above my wife's head (she was sitting in living room) moved around the room and exited through the fireplace brick. It was clear (see through) but had a band of multiple colors moving inside it in a circular motion.
   It hovered above her head for a couple of seconds, then moved around the room followed the contour of the corner and disappeared through the fireplace brick.
  My reasoning mind told me someone was behind me with a flashlight or large laser, when I turned after it was gone no one was behind me...I was standing in the kitchen.
