{rjl} Appeared at 1st to follow road then hovered over my house.

I was about ready to go to sleep when I saw what I thought was an ambulance coming down the road. Then the colored lights began to circle my home. I could see the reflection on the trees surrounding my home. As I lay there there was a buzzing or roaring sound in my head. I didn't hear it with my ears. It felt like my head was vibrating. Then it was just gone.
