Bright Disappearing lights in the sky over 422

Driving on 422 East Bound @7:55 AM on 8/22/11 I spotted in the sky a very bright reflection from what I thought was an airplane. It was round an bright and then it simply vanished. I kept looking but there was no airplane in the area. Then it appeared again slightly to the left of where it originally appeared. I was looking right at it and it disappeared again like it just all of the sudden became invisible. Then briefly, there was a static sound emitted from my car speakers. I thought I was just seeing things but as I was coming around the bed from Oaks heading into Trooper I saw it again this time over the Valley Forge area. Same thing...bright light about the size of a penny from my view on the road, and then gone. Again I looked for a plane but there was no plane. I am not a big believer in UFOs but what I experienced today has really baffled me. When I got to work I Googled UFO and 422 and to my surprise I happened upon a recent article about a sighting off of 422 in the same area a few weeks ago. So...I figured this would be worth reporting as well.
