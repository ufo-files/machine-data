{cak} Football game in brockton Ma. Eye witness of UFO about a mile from field above tree line.

There was a school football game at approximately 7 AM eastern time. As the ball was kicked off I watched it fly through the air which drew my eye to the unidentified craft. A few others and I joked around saying it was a UFO thinking it was a plane. It seemed to be almost like an oval craft that shined back light towards me. While others continued the game I looked away and back in the matter of 5 to 10 seconds only to see that it had disappeared. When I noticed that it was gone I was completely speechless. And in the years of believing and searching, I believe today I just saw my first unidentified spacecraft. I kept this to myself and proceeded to the game.
