{rjl} 2 jet trails with object in between them as sun was low on horizon

I let the dogs out of our back door just alittle prior to sunset. As is my custom, I scan the area and the sky. I was admiring how the setting sun was reflecting off jet trails in the sky, making them appear orange/yellow/pink, etc. There was one jet trail headed West in the front, and one jet trail headed West further in the rear..........both "trails" illuminated clearly by the rapidly setting sun. Then I noticed a very bright, solid white light appear between the 2 jet trails.........but it had NO "trail" at all. In addition, it became brighter and dimmer, but also appeared to be headed in the general direction of the 2 jets which DID have illuminated "trails". I observed this strange, exceedingly bright white "light" grow brighter and dimmer for about 2 minutes or so, and then IT WAS GONE. What was VERY interesting about it was that immediately following it's sudden disappearance, a commercial airliner began it's approach to the local airport (about 8 miles from here), and 3 ADDITIONAL jets came VERY FAST from the East, appearing to follow the exact same course...and with their "trails" illuminated, as well. Whatever that exceedingly bright light was, it was CLEARLY not a jet, a plane, a star, or a planet. I observed the last 3 jets going very quickly to the West, and did not see anything else. I watched until dark, and did not observe any other unusual things.
