{dr}Triangular shaped through binoculars, round red/orange ball/multi colored at times - saw several objects that disappeared

This video was taken on an iPhone 6 in Avon, Ohio looking south towards North Ridgeville on July 4th. 2015. We first noticed 2 objects hanging low right over the tree line. They ascended quickly higher in the sky, only to disappear. It was taken right after the fireworks were over at Crushers Stadium. This was the only one we could catch on video, but we saw a total of 6. They would fade out, then seem to reappear elsewhere. These were certainly not stars as we could easily discern the difference. The blinking of the object is ruled out as clouds passing due to the lack of clouds. We saw 6 over the period of an hour. It almost seemed like they knew we were filming them. We tried to get a good look through a telescope but were unable to do so. We also saw a triangular shape through binoculars that had several colors including blue, green, orange, red, yellow and white. It was shaped like a triangle when viewed through the binoculars. It seemed like they may have been upset with the fireworks, I just had this strange feeling like that was why they were there. It also seemed like at times they were mimicking the lights of the fireworks in order to camouflage themselves.n In the video, the object moves slowly, then flickers and fades out.
