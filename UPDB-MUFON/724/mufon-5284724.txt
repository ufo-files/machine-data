Strange objects

On the evening of 10/17/2021 I took the dogs outside & noticed a triangle shaped object straight west from me. It was hovering for a long period of time with red, white & green lights flashing. It was approximately 7:25 pm and it was dark. It was clear, many stars out. At first I thought maybe it’s a helicopter but it was too stationary for that & sat in one spot entirely too long. As I was recording several more objects appeared. There were a couple identifiable planes in my video so I know for a fact what I was watching was no plane. I 3 objects at one time, one moving in from the north appears. Then another but much higher up comes in from the north. The one ascends higher towards the other & then they were both hovering. Almost on top of each other but a good distance apart. I have no idea for sure what this was but it’s nothing I’ve seen before.
