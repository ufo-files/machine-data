An unknown object that I believed to be an odd shaped star in the form of 2 lines

I took my dog out to potty and saw what I thought was a star in the shape of 2 lines. I took my phone camera and took pics of it and expanded the pics to them to see better. I was shocked at what I saw and begin to video & take pictures. This object would move fast, zigzagging as I held my phone completely steady, it would move in and out fast, sometimes appearing like it was glowing and pulsating. Some of the pics and video show color.
