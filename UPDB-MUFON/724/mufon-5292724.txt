Something strange in the night sky

I was walking my dogs around 9 pm when I observed a bright light in the sky that appeared to be moving erratically. I pulled out my phone and started recording. It appears to be 20- 30 000 ft up and no idea how far east but it was moving around inside a circle of clouds. The video is not great because it's hard to hold a phone perfectly still pointed up at full zoom but I braced myself as best as I could. You can clearly see it dart from 1 point to the next instantly and it goes behind the clouds at times and disappears then its back. I watched this for half an hour and my neighbor came out to leave for work and when I asked her to look at what I was she said, the damn thing is moving around. I took over 3 mins of video and took some stills that actually showed much more than I'd expected.
