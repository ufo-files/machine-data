bright light, sphere, zig zagging but straight line,no sound,no flashing lights, faster then a plane

Time 11:10 pm, outside with dog, I was looking to the West and saw what I thought was a bright star, then noticed it was moving. I watched it for a few seconds and it was almost vibrating/zig zagging but still going in a straight line if that makes sense... it was then I called my husband and he came out and watched it also.
It continued to move West to East, no trail after it and absolutely no sound as I waited to hear any but none came. There was no flashing light as an airplane, it was a bright white light.
I watched as it for approximately 1-2 minutes as it went to the East staying at the same elevation the whole way until it disappeared from sight.
