glowing multi-colored orb seen in distance then followed moving car next to passenger window

November 2009. approx 1-2 a.m. I was traveling with a friend to Seattle and mistakenly took I-90 East toward Kittitas. My friend in the passenger seat noticed a flashing light in the distance and said "do you see that?"  I looked over to where he was pointing and saw a glowing/pulsating light, maybe 500 feet away. I looked back at the road for a few seconds and looked again and it was right next to the passenger's side window, maybe 5 feet away and it was matching our speed (70-80 mph). It was an orb with colors similar to a very hot torch, the edges were fuzzy as if it was pulsating at a very high frequency, I felt intense fear. The hair on my neck and arms were standing up. We felt like we were being watched and then in an instant it was gone.
