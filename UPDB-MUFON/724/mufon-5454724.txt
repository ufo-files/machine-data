Witnessed in northern Lancaster county, PA from 9:00-11:00 am. Object was a small white orb that barely moved in the sky for two hours that I observed it. It was a cloudless day. I had no objects in it's proximity to determine it's height. Two of the same objects were sighted in dauphin county later that day. They were reported on by one of our local TV stations, Fox 43. I have no idea what I saw. I got a good look at it thru my binoculars. It looked like a white translucent balloon, but balloons move with the air currents. Unfortunately I didn't take any pictures.
