Large flaming object falling straight down and not as fast as a shooting star

[Item moved/CMS/nd] I always left work at approximently 7:30 PM to 7:40 PM. I would leave work on a narrow county road, through the hills. Rarely met another vehicle. This evening, it was dark, as usual and I just rounded a curve when I seen what I first thought was a shooting star. But this object was on fire and it was huge and descending slower than a star. I had always seen shooting star fall at an angle and so very fast, you barely got to observe it before it disappeared. This object was falling slower than a shooting star and the fire was bellowing from it. It looked like the flames were comeing from a cylinder shape, but I didn't actually see the cylinder. It was falling straight down, not at an angle. It fell behind a hill, so I lost sight of it. I heard no noise. I continued to drive, still wondering if this was a meteorite or what? I was telling my brother about it yesterday and he says we should go to the area and try to find the objest.
I cannot climb the hills anymore, due to a bad knee. I can take someone to the area and point out where it was falling. This could have been a meteorite, but unlike anything Ive ever seen. I didn't have a camera and there were no other vehicles around.
