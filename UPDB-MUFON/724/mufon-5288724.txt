Glowing light underwater near naval base comes out of water

Near naval base in Victoria saw glowing light under the water. Thought maybe a submarine because they come in sometimes. Then glowing light came out of water could not tell if it was part of submarine appeared to hover over water super bright and pulsating then began to move side to side very robotically then stopped. Then split into 3 and formed like a triangle and each light would move and replace the one beside it. Eventually they rejoined. Pulsating stopped. And then without warning shot straight up into the sky faster then anything I've ever seen. Honestly didn't know what it was but didn't even think ufo until that part.
