{dr}object that looked starlike silently and smoothly traveling across early morning sky.

At 5:26 AM August 3, 2012 my fiance where outside facing north when we saw an object that reminded you of a star, but we realized it was moving very silently and smoothly across the sky. The light color was like a star but you could look closely and make out a circular domed object with a ring around it. The light never wavered,flickered or anything it was solid. We first saw it coming from the northwest, it traveled a straight line passing in front of us and heading southeast we watched about four minutes until you couldnt see it anymore.
