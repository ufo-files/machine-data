LIGHTS IN SKY WHICH TURNED ON AND OFF

AS I REMEMBERED I HAPPENED 3 TIMES WITHIN 2-3 MINUTES. 

LATER ON AT 10:30 PM, I WENT DOWNSTAIRS TO PICK UP A DEAR FRIEND OF OURS INTO THE APARTMENT AND 2-3 MORE TIMES THE LIGHTS IN THE SKY TURNED ON AND OFF AND THIS TIME I BELIEVE MY FRIEND COULD SEE IT AS WELL.
