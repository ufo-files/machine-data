{jd} Amber light descended then hovered

I had just pulled into my home and when my fiance and I got out of the car we saw an amber light descend rapidly and then abruptly stop and hover. This object was East of us and a few miles away. I ran to the edge of the property and tried to film the object with my cell phone camera. Because it was dark my camera had a hard time focusing on the light and I caught the tail end of the incident. It stayed there for almost two minutes but I only got a few seconds of it before it blinked out. I have seen these types of lights in that same place a few times now. A few months back my fiance and I saw a few pf these objects fly in formation, slowly moving and then blink out in unison.
