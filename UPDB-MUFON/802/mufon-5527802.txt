Not a shooting star!

I went outside to smoke and was facing the Eastern sky. I saw what I though to be a shooting star, yellowish in color. It seemed faster than a normal shooting star. As it streaked down at an angle...suddenly as it hit an approximate altitude...instead of the normal burnout disappearance, the speed slowed to half and it suddenly moved horizonally until I could no longer see it due to structures in my view. NO WAY that was a shooting star. NO WAY it was any known aircraft! The speed and change in direction were extremely apparent. I was excited and my heart was racing the instant it's speed and direction were noted. I ran inside to tell others what I had just seen.
