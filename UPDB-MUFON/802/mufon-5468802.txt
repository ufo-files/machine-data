{jpg} Photographing moon and jupiter with stills and short vid-- review of materials revealed orbs/objects curved beams of light, direct beams of light, atmospheric disturbance

I went to two different parking lots to photograph the full moon and jupiter. At the time I was holding up my phone (iPhone 7 plus) and didn't see these objects until I reviewed the photos at home. I couldn't believe what I saw. There were two orange tinted orbs in the first photo, which moved toward the moon in one of the photos I took in the second location 20 minutes later. There were also 3 objects which initially looked boomerang shaped with a forked center but could be due to rapidly moving light. 1 was matte/whitish off to the left and two were brightly lit aqua blue. The objects also seemed to change shape to simple discs of light. In the video I saw them ascend when a plane went by, and saw light being aimed either at the plane or at the building with the lit window. There was also significant bending light almost like giant lens refraction or pulses visible in the video and the photos, with one specific photo in which the phone went "haywire", was out of focus and just light everywhere. I think I didn't see them because they move a lot faster than what would be considered normal and there seems to be some type of cloaking/electromagnetic pulses. I only shot a few seconds of video and if I had seen this happening live I would have kept the camera on longer. I've been feeling ill since then but I don't know if it's a coincidence or if it has to do with the event.
