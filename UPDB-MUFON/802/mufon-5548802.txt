Boomerang multi light formation moving north to south Beltway 8 and Westheimer to Hwy 59. 300+ yards across at around 8,000 ft. alt. 20 seconds of viewing

Stepped outside in back yard to take dogs out for the night. Neighbor dogs barking erratically. Looked over toward house (east) and noticed formation of probably 20 lights boomerang shape but independent most likely, moving north to south along Beltway corridor from Westheimer to 59 Hwy before out of view. Lasted about 20 seconds, moving rather slow but large and low, probably 300+ yards across at altitude of 8,000 ft. at most. Appeared to be 20+ independent lights but holding tight formation in the shape of boomerang. No sound. Stars where breifly obscured as objects moved under.
