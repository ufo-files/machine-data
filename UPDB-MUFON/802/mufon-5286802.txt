Large white Tic-Tac/ Bow Tie reflecting silver.

I witnessed this large white TicTac object that was bow shaped in the air about 10° above the skyline while driving NE. I followed the object As a happenstance for the next 10 miles on the way to a job interview. I pulled over and record a video. However I couldn’t stop and waist time! A few moments before the object went over the skyline it glinted at me shiny silver in the sunlight. This really excited me because it was traveling really slow and now I knew it was metallic as well.
