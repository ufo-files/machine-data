White Orb flying above residential area

Around 10am on a morning with clear blue skies, I noticed a white orb above my garden moving with variable speed and periodically changing direction.

I couldn't ascertain the size, speed or altitude of this spherical object because there were no real features to gauge off of. My instincts told me it was around the size of a football or beach ball and at an altitude of between 500-1000ft, although it did appear to go up and down - but I really cannot say with any certainty if that was the case.

It slowly made its way north over the space of a few minutes until it faded from view. I did not get any photographic evidence as I did not have my phone, and I'm confident that the footage would have produced nothing of value anyway.
