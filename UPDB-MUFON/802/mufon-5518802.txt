red-orange colored globes of light seen in sky around 845 p,m. while walking dog

We were walking our dog when we noticed two red-orange globes of light flying in the sky in what looked like a circling path of a single area, yet they would disappear for a number of minutes then reappear. We checked back in 15 minutes, and they had disappeared. I have never seen anything like this in my life. These were definitely not airplanes, helicopters, or any type of drones. Very shocked and at a loss of words at what i just saw.
