Squadron of diamond shaped crafts

A CLEAR DAY.. home work and just had a feeling to look up, seen moving in control approximately 15 silver sparkling lights two of them solid red, two of them changed color from Silver to red. Heading towards Lakeland Airport. Five of them stopped North of the airport south of my view. two of them turned into black diamonds and hoovered for approximately 15 to 20 minutes, along with about five silver diamonds. They all slowly faded away one by one. Was hard to get on video, look close near end. You will see a white orbe pop into view. What a sight!
