Dark needle-like object in the sky

ON 11/10/2021 at aprox 4:10PM Upon looking up into sky while entering parking lot, saw dark needle-like object in western sky appearing to be headed in a northward direction. Object appears to be large and at a considerable distance from my vantage point. Object emitting a light contrail extending southward. Observed for aprox 5 minutes. Observed to determine if shaped stayed the same or if angle was just off. Did not appear to significantly move from location where first observed. No sound heard. No light seen. Took two photos timestamped at 4:19PM Observed for two more minutes then took zoomed in photo at 4:21. At this time had been observing for longer than 10 but less than 15 minutes and needed to put heavy belongings in my vehicle and start the engine as the temperature was cold. View obscured by neighboring vehicle in parking for 2-3 minutes. When returned to original vantage point behind my vehicle I couldn't find object anywhere in the sky. Vantage points to all directions except south are virtually unobstructed. Observed for 3-5 more minutes but object did not reappear. Family member forwarded screenshot from social media today (12/6/2021) of same object I saw but this appears to be from different vantage point from either a different location or on different time or day as clouds are seen in this photo and there were no clouds visible at my location.
