UFO flew by my house 14.06.2019

Ufo Sighted on 14th June 2019, HIG 2, Chennai

I was sitting in the terrace floor filming the moon at around 11.30PM when i notices a odd shape coming towards me . My first impression of it was a flock of birds flying in a close group . As it came a little closer i it looked nothing like a flock of birds so i thought maybe it was a group of balloons floating in the wind. This was a tightly packed mass of something . Moved like paper floating in the wind . Then i realised it was not windy at all , at least not as windy as this thing was flying along and also this was at least 3 meters in diameter. 

My approximation of its distance from me was about 50m tops and the speed it was flying maybe 200 km. My best description of this object was that of something changing shaped like a close group of bees but in the thousands, its colour was that of a light pink/brown and did not emit any noise or light. It was opaque. It moved right above my house . All this happened in a matter of few seconds so was not able to take a video although i was filming the moon. It took me by complete surprise and i have not seen anything like this before. I wonder what it was.
