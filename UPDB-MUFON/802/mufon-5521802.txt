There was no UFO sighting. A missing time event occured

My friend & I left Flagstaff, AZ heading south towards Phoenix on I-17 @ about 1:20pm. At 2pm I was in the far left (fast) lane & joked with my friend about the Sedona wanna-be "Hollywood" sign on the mini-mountain to my right. My friend said "thats the landmark just prior to the exit we needed to get off of. He told me to move over to the far right lane. At that moment my car radio went to static. Since I needed to pay attention to the traffic & move to the far right lane I asked him to tune the radio while looking at it. I looked up in to my rear view mirror only to see 5 white compact cars behind me. I mentioned that it was odd asking him "where did all of the traffic go?" All 5 cars passed me on the left. I noticed all 5 cars had Nevada plates. A few seconds later I saw a freeway sign that said Kingston - 50 miles. I asked my friend "isnt Kingston near Laughlin, NV?" I came to a crest of freeway only to view Laughlin which bordered Bullhead City, CA. At that point I realized I was now heading West on I-40. Freaked out, I pulled over & we both paced the side of the road trying to figure out how we got there. We got back in the car & turned around heading East on I-40 & came upon a van. I honked & my friend asked the driver what time it was. It was 5:30pm, 3 hours & 20 minutes had passed. To this day, Ive heard 3 other stories of missing time on this stretch of road, one of which was from a trucker. Truckers had to log there time & miles. This confirmed we were not crazy. Its been nearly 18 years since this incident. Neither of us experienced any physical or psychological trauma. I would like to know if anyone else cant relate to what happened to us or explain it. Im still curious. Thank you.
