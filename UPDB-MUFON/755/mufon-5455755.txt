Sphere hovering appeared on the sky 500 meters high, making erratic moves very fast & hide behind the mountains.

Night of September 24, 2018 at 2:30 in the morning. I am on my terrace, when I saw a very bright red object, brighter than Mars, to which I am used to taking pictures. Then I begin to observe that the object begins to make erratic movements. From top to bottom and vice versa in different directions. It's when I decided to take my camera and my phone to take a photo and check if it was mars or not through a star map. I put my camera on the tripod, I took the picture and 20 seconds later, the object dissapear behind the mountain at full speed in a dive. I opened the star map and Mars for 25 minutes that was not visible trough the clouds . So enlarge the image and all the stars appreciate as points, that is, the photograph was not over exposed; I already have the hyperfocal calculation clear with my objective. If you enlarge the photograph you can check it. I am very addicted to The star photography I love, I watch the sky every night and I have to say that I have never observed anything similar and look that I have seen meteor showers and I have done many timelapse. I catalog it as an Unidentified Flying Object. The Unidentified Flying Object its in the middle of the photography just at the top of the mountain.
