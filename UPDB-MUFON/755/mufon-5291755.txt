The object I observed at first sight looked like an orange ball to the left side of the setting sun in Oct of 2019.

The object was bright orange at first sight round and just to the left of the setting sun, the sky was very orange and typical of a fall harvest time sunset in corn country. I took several pics. I think these are amazing, but what shocked me most was when I blew the pics up simply with my phone settings like brightness hue color and the pictures are really incredible. The object never moved, it changed from a orange bright light to the strange propeller shape, i had pulled my car over to take the pics, about 15 min the sun went down and it faded from my sight.
