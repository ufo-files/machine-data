Orange ball of light

First off, I'd like to state that I am an A&P mechanic so I know what aircraft lights look like at night. What I saw was NOT an aircraft of an Earthly form. Whatever I saw seemed to look like a plane at first but there was an orange light that didn't blink or pulse, it was a light that stayed on. It moved like a plane until it stopped for a few brief seconds then shot straight up into the sky, it was gone within seconds. There is no aircraft that I know of that can go from flying in a straight line to shooting straight up into the sky in a blink of an eye.
