2 big spheres moving perfectly horizontally for 40 minutes

At 11,25am on Jan 31th 2913, thursday, from the bathroom windiw I can clearly see two big spheres in the sky. They look still, but the will show moving in a parallel and perfectly horizontal line along 30/40 minutes. 
I took 2 photos and 2 videos with a Samsung Galaxy note cam, I was with my girlfriend. 

At the beginning I thought it was two air baloons, but they shone silver and didn't lose a single degree along their moving. Afterwards I knew that no baloons can be white in Italy and anyway they showed like having no basket.

The 2 spheres looked like silver balls, from 10 to 30 meters approx. They left no trail and produced no noise, moving in a perfect line from NE to E, keeping a perfect distance in between.

The seighting was so real that gave me no strange feeling. It was a clear day with a clear sky, in full light.

The object disappeared behinf the eastern horizon after 40 minutes.
