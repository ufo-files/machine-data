{rjl} Orange Pulsating Orb

Me and a friend were walking to a parked car behind the Big Y parking lot. The parking lot has some lighting. I look to the sky and see a slightly larger than a star orange glowing orb. I ask my friend if she sees it. She does see it. She describes it like this: "You know those fireworks that have the embers that slowly fall to earth? It looked like one of those without the falling" The orb pulsated and flicked. It also moved a bit around the sky in a random pattern and didn't seem to be covering much distance. The it just went out. The event lasted about a minute. Neither of us had been drinking.
