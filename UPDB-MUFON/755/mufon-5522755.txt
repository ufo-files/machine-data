I was standing in my backyard smoking as I do several times a day. Noticed orb in the sky almost immediately after I sat down. Called my wife outside but she was uninterested. I was excited because I was sure it wasnt a plane or helicopter. Took four 30 sec - 1 min vids on my cell phone as it quickly became dark outside.
