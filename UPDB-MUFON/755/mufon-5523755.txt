triangle formation of three bright green orbs floating slowly towards the North of Tortolita Mountain then shooting up and disappearing

I was walking outside in Tucson with my mother when, while glancing up at the sky, i noticed three bright green orb-like objects flying slowly in a triangle formation. At first I thought it was paper lanterns or an airplane, but upon further inspection they were way too perfectly spherical to be lanterns, and too big to be plane lights. They hovered slowly in a Northward path for about 15 minutes, before stopping briefly and shooting up into the air, their light disintegrating as they shot up. When my mom and I first saw the objects, she had grabbed my arm and told me that she felt her stomach drop, and felt very uneasy. For about 10 minutes after the sighting, I had a dull headache near the back of my skull and my eyes felt dry and ached.
