{jd} Two very bright orbs of light

Ok the only reason I decided to speak of this is I am watching ufo's over earth on Destination America, and the description of the orbs one man seen is very similar and also I think about the sighting all the time. Also I dont remember the exact date but I was a junior in high school and we were on our summer break and my best friend and I were coming home from a weekend of camping at Rock Dam campground. And the exact spot we seen it is if you were to leave Rock Dam due west you will come to highway H, we turned right onto highway H which would put us heading north on H. That is all county forest area. And after we headed north onto county highway H there is a series of curves in the road, and right after the 3rd curve whammmm these two orbs of light came from the left hand side of the road, and my buddy hit the brakes and turned white as a sheet and yelled "what the ****" and I was speechless as the two orbs hovered in the middle of the road (we were stopped) and these things were very, very bright hovering 10 feet above the road and I bet they were no more than 15-20 feet in front of the car. They did not hover long, and zinggg they shot off into the night sky heading north east above the tree tops very fast...it was intense and I think about it all the time, and my friend and I couldnt even do anything but we looked each other in the eye and I asked him "did that really just happen?" And he was still pale white (Im sure I was too) and I bring it up to him or he brings it up to me from time to time to this day and we both remember it like it was yesterday. Changed us forever because we have without any doubt seen something from another world, thats about it I just thought I would share our experience so you can document the location, I have nothing to gain from this but figured it could in some way help you guys out....this was before the days of cell phones so unfortunately no photo but it would of been one hell of a photo because they were so damn close...I feel very lucky to have actually seen this, and will take that night to my grave with me as it is still very vivid in my mind.
