{nd} 3 pulsating lights that all came together and made one

I walked outside like I always do before bed and looked around just observing the sky when I noticed 3 pulsating lights. 2 were off to the right of the third. They were pulsating random colors very quickly. After an hour they all joined together. I had an overwhelming sense of peace. I went inside to get some tea and when I came back out they were gone. I have never seen anything like that in my 45 years.
