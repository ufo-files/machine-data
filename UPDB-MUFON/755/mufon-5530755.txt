Four "stars" near the moon that got slightly closer and brighter then faded away

I was coming home from a friends house and the sky looked particularly cool tonight with the cloud formations present. I stopped at home, and normally I would just get out of the car and head inside but something stopped me and I just looked at the moon, adoring it, when I noticed what seemed to be a constellation of 4 or 5 "stars" directly under the moon, which I had never noticed before. I focused on them and as I was looking they seemed to get "closer", or "bigger", and moved around a little bit. I had the feeling inside that I was witnessing something amazing, almost to the point of crying. They eventually faded away to the point where I couldn't see them anymore, just the moon. What were they and where did they go? They were not really bright, in comparison to say... jet lights or plane lights, but they were putting off some form of light.
