Sighting in NEPA

approx 6 pm, I saw UFO hovering in the sky 
It was not an airplane, as it was at a very high altitude.
I could make out that it was a circle shape from the distance observed.
It was too early in the day to be a start, although my husband thinks it could have been a satellite.
It would shine silver as it reflected sunlight.
