{rjl} Exited car, saw a helicopter chasing small orb. About 1min later losing sight. military jet from west heading in same direction of orb and helicopter.

we were exiting our car, having just returned from baseball game. As I exited drivers door I heard a helicopter flying low. I looked towards the south/west towards Anaheim. I noted a helicopter initially, then I saw a small orb (continuous glow) no blinking lights or sound. I quickly to my wife and kids to look up. They all watched the helicopter chasing(following) at closely behind orb. We observed the helicopter chasing orb for about 1-2 minutes before they flew over the hills of chino hills state park. Approximately 1 minute after losing sight of the helicopter and orb, a single military jet came from the west heading on the same flight path as both orb and helicopter. It was very interesting, it almost appeared as if the helicopter was escorting whatever the unknown craft was.
