{nd} orange orb like light in sky saw twice or two different

In bed approximately 9:30 pm. Saw through skylight window above bed a very bright orange light travel across sky from NW to SE direction. It was bright enough to distract from the television light in the room. No sound and did not look like a shooting star, traveled straight path. About 5 mins later saw same travel close to same path. Looked like a flare almost except was traveling in straight direction with now residual tail. No planes in site at time and there was only the single orb shape.
