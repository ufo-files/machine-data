{cak} witnesses a military plane chasing a glowing orange/red perfectly round craft, all w/no sound

My family was camping on Johns mountain. Sometime around 10:30pm. It was late and the boys wanted to drive to the top of a long forestry road. My husband and myself were in the front of truck, our dog and two teenage sons were in the back. Upon arriving to the top, we were amazed at the beautiful view. The boys were excited to have cell phone service,so they called their girlfriends. It was around 11pm and My husband and I were looking at the moon. I wanted to take a picture of the view. it was at that moment my brand new phone started acting crazy. I looked up and tree top level I observe a perfectly round brightly glowing object coming over us,no sound,moving at a good speed and just as we were all witnessing this,under radar comes a military plane seemed from nowhere, up the side of the mountain. Upon reaching the top, the plane leveled just feet from my head, as I was standing on a flat rock. The military plane made a wing tip turn and was clearly chasing this flying orange object. I was terrified of what we just seen. We wanted to get back to the camp site. Upon going back we were followed by a black 4 door car tinted windows, and my phone looked to have been zapped at some point, because I had a code instead of letters and I was missing pictures. This same activity was also reported by 100s of witnesses at 6a.m.the same morning. News channel 9 covered a short story because of all whiteness.
