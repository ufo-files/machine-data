Small white object with the naked eye

A small white object was seen moving quickly in a straight line across the sky with no sound. It was photographed and video recorded with a cell phone. Upon enhancing the images it appear somewhat like a Star Trek shuttlecraft. It had no visible wings, tail, or rotors, and left no contrail. The object was seen in daylight, sunny-partly cloudy conditions. It moved through it behind the clouds. We are located close to Offut Air Force Base, and have had other object sightings in the past, from orbs being followed by fighter jets, a triangle with circular lights, and what appeared to be a larger craft surrounded by smaller ones. All have been daylight sightings. The object is seen as a tiny white dot in the first photo. Subsequent photos are zoomed, and the black and white photo was an enhanced image to try to draw out details.
