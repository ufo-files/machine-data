{cak} 25 miles OFF SHORE while sailing south to San Diego we observed this UFO

We were sailing a 65 ft sailboat from San Francisco to San Diego for the America's Cup 1995. We were approximately 25 to 35 miles off shore somewhere around Big Surprise area. It was a clear cloudless night approximately 8- 9 PM. The Captain suggested we keep a watch out for a weather buoy before he went below deck to sleep. There were 3 of us in the cockpit when we observed on the horizon (hard to know size & distance because we had no reference points) a round object pulsating deep red to orange, appearing to be sitting on the water. We thought at first glance weather buoy??? for a number of minutes we observed it just hovering and or sitting in the water pulsating knew then it wasn't a buoy. The object then ascended at a speed I had never before witnessed. It was as if one second it was in front of us and the next second it was up in the stars impossible to follow it happened in a nano second. It made no noise that I could hear. We discussed what we had seen and we all agreed we had seen something which was extraordinary, and most likely not of planet earth.
