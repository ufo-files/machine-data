{cak} Caught while photographing contrails.

While living in Araguari, MG Brazil I liked to photograph contrails and in Araguari there was no shortage of interesting contrail patterns daily.
Anyway, while looking at my photos on my laptop I noticed a strange object. At first I thought it might have been caused by some sort of malfunction of the curtain or dust on the sensor. As I zoomed in to the object it looked more like a door, a pure black door with what looked to be 2 emissions coming out of two of the corners of this door/ portal. I have the photo and it is easy to see. I also think i can see some sort of disturbance in the air close by as if an invisible craft is hiding in the cloud/ contrail clouds created.
