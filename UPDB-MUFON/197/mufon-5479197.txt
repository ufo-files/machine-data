{nd} Transparent brown ovals decending in coordination with each other. Friend spotted it decending and my brother and I also witnessed them.

Driving down the 101 North of HEARLDSBURG when the driver spots two brown, transparent ovals decending from what appeared to be 1,500 feet. He immediately freaks out, points to the objects and they decend down into the tree lines of a mountain, appearing to move south-east. They were oval in shape, 11 -15 feet in diameter and brown but transparent. They startled three grown men. This, of course happened in broad day light over the highway so it leads us to believe there were other witnesses. The duration was very short, as the objects dipped between the mountain range and the tree line.
