{td} Silver object moving slowly from west to east, the turned north.

On 5/31/18 myself and 7 other firefighters observed a silver object moving from the west to the east over a 20 minute period. We observed this while on Buckley AFB. At first we thought it was an aircraft, but the object moved too slow to be one. The object made no noise, and it flickered in the sunlight. The object turned 90 degrees to the north and began to speed up rapidly. At the same time, F-16 jets were beginning to take off when the object made the turn. The object continued north until it was out of sight. The sky was clear with no clouds. When we saw this and couldn11t explain it, all of us just continued to rationalize what we saw.
