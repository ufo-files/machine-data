We saw shooting star, went in direction, saw thing following biker

Last night I and my younger cousin were on the swings at a park around 9 pm. We were in a playground in the bay area of California, in a suburban area. Suddenly we both saw a shooting star really close, like really close it lit up the whole sky and was fiery and went out in like a second. We were both fascinated and followed the general direction of where it landed. (By the way, while we were walking we saw a lot of planes flying over us as if they were scanning the area, around 2 per 5 mins). While we were walking the power lines above us were cracking and making weird noises. Suddenly my cousin started whimpering and pointed Into a hedge a block away next to the intersection. At first, I was confused but when I looked close, I saw in the dark, some large creature. It was massive, being 6 or 7 feet, and I'm not sure whether it was dark or not, but we saw it had some sort of veil or metallic helmet like the old-fashioned scuba diving helmet. We then realized it was emerging to chase a biker around a corner (it was right behind the biker) and that was the last of it, we heard no screams or anything. I wanted to follow but my cousin wanted to go home and I didn't want to leave him alone so we darted home.
