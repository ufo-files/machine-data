3 Star like objects traveling at high speed.

My husband and I were sitting in out back yard looking at the stars when suddenly out of no where we both notcied what we thought at first were 3 birds flying above us. They appeared suddenly from the south traveling north. After we realized that we were both seeing the same objects we concluded that these objects were not birds. This happened around 12:30pm. They could not have been were traveling at a very high speed. They were not bright but also not dim as it is completely dark out but we still noticed them.There was absoulutly no sound what so ever. Literally appeared and disappeared within seconds. My husband and I were both a little shocked at what we had just witnessed and surprised we were both seeing what we were seeing.
