Multiple non stationary lights

Multiple lights bouncing around in the sky were spotted over several evenings beginning on 20 September 2021 through the 25th. The first one spotted flashed what looked red to white to transparent as it bobbed like a cork on the surface of a lake. 

On following nights I spotted more of the type described and several other types with some moving along a path and stopping before changing direction or arcing around other moving lights. 

Last night and tonight there were many more of a type that could become very bright or dim. Those didn’t seem any color except white and glowed steady without flashing. They appeared to show 1, 2 or 3 lights arranged in triangular pattern. Several of these brightest lights had dimmer red lights behind them in pairs which moved in unison with the brighter white light. Their relative distance remained fixed with each motion.
