I live in Grand Haven, Michigan Township and I'm about 1/8th of a mile from Lake Michigan. It was the early morning hours of Wednesday, August 27, 2014 at approximately 1:30 AM and I was sitting in my car smoking a cigarette and observing the clear night sky. I usually come outside 4 or 5 times a night to have my cigarette and look into space usually seeing one or more meteorites streaking across the night sky and keep a set of Binoculars and my SLR-Digital camera on hand in case I see anything interesting. Well, this night was different than all the rest because as I viewed the space landscape I noticed an unusual craft about a mile or more away moving in a South-South Westerly direction over the Lake Hills heading out over Lake Michigan.r What made this craft stand out from all of the rest I've ever seen in the night sky was this craft had 3-Round Amber lights that formed a Triangle shape with a small round Red light in the center pulsing on and off. I estimate this craft to be rather large because when I see other aircraft (Passenger Jets, Civilian Aircraft, and Military Jets) at that approximate distance away they would be much much smaller than the one I was looking at. I didn't hear any sound nor see any com-trail, smoke, or any kind of exhaust coming from the craft. I watched this craft through my Binoculars until the craft was out of sight.
