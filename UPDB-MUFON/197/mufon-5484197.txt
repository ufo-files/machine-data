{nd} Myself & two friends saw a light in the sky over a two year period which made eratic movements. We taunted this light with a new 3 cell mag light. The light then turned out and a triangle was present. The triangle flew over us at 3 miles per hour

Sometime in the late summer of 1999 myself and three friends stopped in the middle of the country south of Trilla Illinois we began to mess with a light that we had seen for a couple of years this light was erratic and move in strange ways across the sky I had a new mag light and we decided to use it to mess with the light in the sky when we shine the flashlight at the light in the sky the light in the sky came much closer we could see the light between us and the Treeline behind it. as we noticed it was in the field in front of us the light became very dim and the craft moved toward us at a very slow rate of speed the craft made absolutely no noise and flew directly over our heads the craft was close enough I could have hit it throwing a rock. the craft flew about the same distance away from us to the south then absolutely and completely disappeared my friends and I were so scared that we jumped into our vehicle and took off as fast as we could toward home.
