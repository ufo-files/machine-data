Bright Green Light

I stepped outside and witnessed a bright green light flying directly over me pretty fast around 1:30am. It was a similar color to a green traffic light and did not blink. When it passed thru a patch of clouds in the sky it illuminated the clouds quite vividly. It was absolutely silent and had no tail.. and seemed to be too fast to be a plane to me.
