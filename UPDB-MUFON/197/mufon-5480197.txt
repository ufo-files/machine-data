{rjl} White orb like light, daylight.

I was sitting outside enjoying the evening. I was watching for jets in the sky. This white orb like light appears, but is high up above the clouds. My first thought is that it is a satellite. But it is still really bright out. I had my cellphone with me and video tape it. As I did my cellphone freaks out, and it looks like radio waves. I could not focus on anything so I focused on the tree. Thinking that it would have faded I look up and still see it in the sky. I video taped it for almost 3 mins. I can't upload the video but took still shots of the video.
