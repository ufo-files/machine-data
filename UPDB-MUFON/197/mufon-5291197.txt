Star like object noted above the trees in our back yard. Seemed stationary at first then as time passed it slowly moved from a NE to an E direction.

Star like object noted above trees in our back yard. I came in the house to retrieve my binoculars and take a closer look. Looked spherical or slightly oval. Bright white color appeared reflective. There was a darker center area that looked x shaped. Almost translucent in some areas. It stayed pretty stationary with slight movements to the north and south of its position. I asked a friend to photo the object and she did. Light clouds obscured it at times but it shone thru at times. There were 12 of my family and friends in the yard observing this oddity. After a couple of hours it moved from its NE position to the E and out of my line of sight. The next day on Nov1,2020, I again observed the object around 3 pm moving in the northerly direction toward the west very quickly. It moved faster than the jet that was going from north to south.
