White blob- Infared

At 2:00 on the sixth of June our surveillance camera with infared detected motion which initiated recording function. 
A massive blob of white matter slowly makes its way down our unpaved driveway heading from north to south.
Watching the blob on camera from my windowless bedroom ten meters to the south, I observed it's progress, dumbfounded.
After four minutes, I'd had enough. I armed myself and went out to confront the beast, but it was nowhere to be seen.
If I had infared vision like my camera I may have found the blob. But my eyes only detect light emitted in the visible portion of the electromagnetic spectrum and it got away.
After the encounter, I went back to my video.
The blob must have known I was coming outside because it disappears forty-five seconds before I got to it's last known position.
All this can be seen on my tapes from the camera.
