golden oval object flew in strait line towards johannesburg int. airport

Golden-copper oval solid object with black "markings" on its surface flew very very slowly towards Johannesburg International Airport on 20th June 2012 at clear, cloudless, daytime 16:15p.m.( Left to right from my house garden )Did not make any sound or noise, had no wings, no lights, nothing to indicate what type of machinery that was, did not change direction or altitude at all. As it approached the airport where usually planes are landing...at this particular moment an airplane took off and I realize the actual size of this object was as large as the cock-pit of the plane. The property is based 1km or less from the strip of the airport. The object flew " under " the commercial airplane without changing speed, direction, anything. It just continued on its course till it faded on the horizon. Next day in the afternoon hours again, a huge " russian army " looking like pure white helicopter with ( only marking ) UN, flew too close above my property towards the direction of the airport as well. 
After research on the net, only two other people who stay in close proximity to me saw the golden object, but unfortunately not the " UN " helicopter. The markings on the object looked like strokes( lines ) & triangles, almost like writings. So sorry did not have a camera at the time, so no proof unfortunately, although just a hope that someone, somewhere saw or will see the same object again and record it. Thank you
