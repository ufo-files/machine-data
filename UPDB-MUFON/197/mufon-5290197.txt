about 15-20 oval shaped silver objects flying in formation

Heading north on Sandario rd. just south of Emigh Rd. about 9am on 1/1/21 we observed a flash of light above Marana Airport which caught our eyes. Looking closer we saw about 15-20 oval shaped objects flying in very tight formation towards the west (Silverbell Mountains). We turned west on Emigh rd. then north on Sanders to see if we could get a better look at them. We were sporadically able to see them between the trees and buildings as they headed west. Their surface reflected the morning sun, which was behind us. We seemed to lose sight of them without the reflection of the sun. It didn't take long for them to disappear in the distance towards the west, They seemed to be going fast, maybe like a jet (which we see often here) but did not make any noise. Especially if there were 15-20 of them. There formation was like a cluster, with out any patter and did not vary in shape. Their size seemed to be smaller than a small private plane, which is the predominant airplane at this small regional airport.
