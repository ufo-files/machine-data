UFO Flying Over Bagram Airfield

I was sitting at the smoke pit outside the huts. When I decided to look up, I saw an object that camouflaged with the early morning dark sky; however, I was able to track it by seeing its outline. What I thought at the moment made me remember the scene from "Close Encounters of the Third Kind" where Richard Dreyfus (in his truck) is following the UFO's outline after he had its freaky light show experience. In full detail, the object flew across my position in a slow manner acting if it was conducting reconnaissance of the airfield. I lost sight when my attention was diverted to people in the vicinity. I looked up again and it was gone.
