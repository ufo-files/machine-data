{dr}Very bright stars hovering, one begins moving to left.

As I was taking my dogs into the backyard for the final time of the night, I noticed two incredibly bright color changing stars in the distance. There were no other stars in the sky, and because it was summer it was still dusk out. Originally I brushed them off as nothing more than the first stars of the night, as they stood perfectly still in the sky. I watched them closely for a few minutes, intrigued by how exuberant they were. At that time, one started to move to the left, about the speed of a helicopter. I was surprised but not panicked or shocked. The moving object disappeared behind the treeline of a nearby forest. I rushed upstairs to tell my housemates about the sight, but decided to hold my tongue.
