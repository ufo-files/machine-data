I was watching the Detroit/ Jets game, when the network switched over to broadcast the beginning of the Raiders/Rams game.
The transition begins with an overhead panoramic view of the stadium and surrounding area.
Within about 5-10 seconds I noticed some movement flying infront of the gold colored building on the right hand side of my tv screen. It was moving very fast, I thought maybe it was a fighter jet. I rewound the scene a few times and viewed it in slow motion, foward and reverse.
