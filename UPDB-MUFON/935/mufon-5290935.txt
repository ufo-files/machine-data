Five objects spotted at different times but similair paths

Daughter and son-in-law observed the first three. They came inside and got me and I observed the last two. Objects started in the south/southwest and traveled to the North/Northwest. The first of the two that I observed reached a point over our house and just disappeared. Within a matter of a minute or less the next one started at about the same location that the first one I saw had started in the south. Path was mainly straight with a few variations of circular manuvers. The objects were orange in color and appeared to be orb shaped and the objects did not emit any sound. There were times it appeared that the objects pulsated. We are near the flight path of a local airport but these five objects were not in any of the known flight paths. These were not aircraft. Being former military I would know formation lights and planes do not just disappear. This was on a clear night. In the videos they appear to blink at times but this is due to tree branches getting in the way.
