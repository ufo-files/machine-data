I was aged 9 going on ten years old me a some friend were playing in a tree house we built up a Big Confrence pear tree it most of been well over 100 foot high well my freind left for there homes its was getting dark around 10pm long days summer time late july I stayed and then fell a sleep for some time and woke up only to see this blue light coming from some where so a shinded up a branch of the tree to get a better look high up in the tree you can see for miles and what I was seen I did not believe it was a disc shape craft with glowing lights runing around it, but then it all went black I passed out some how and came to a mile and half away from where I was the funny thing is I was inbetween the railway wagons there siding there 4 rows of coal wagons 100 wagons long , i was afraid and ran like the wind home but later in was having horrible dreams from a long time even now at age 61 I still get the night mares its was some 50 years later when I seen two more star like light moving very fast in the sky some 500 feet up the changed direction from going north to going north west and it was not like a aircraft would do they changed at a direct angle this was 2009 summer
