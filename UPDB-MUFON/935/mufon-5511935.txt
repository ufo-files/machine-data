{nd} Three Silver Orbs (ufos) Mainly in Triangle Formation

Observed 3 silver reflective orbs in a close revolving triangle formation passing overhead in a Northwest direction at about 50mph, then stopped, hovered for about 5 mins, and then continued course. While hovering, ufos switched between triangle formations to vertical and horizontal straight line formations.
