{cak} observed initially with naked eye, then viewed thru cell camera zoomed

Initially one looked liked a very large star that got closer and lower. Flickering, pulsing, changing shape to the naked eye. White in color. The second object was south of the first. But still in the western sky. This one was smaller and appeared to be a bluish color. It also was pulsing. When zooming in with my cell camera it intermittently looked like a donut with a tipical flying saucer turning fan like device in the center. After 30 minutes the 2nd object had moved from site. At the same time the larger first object moved slightly north and appeared lower in the sky and brighter.
