Greeb-blue light came up from behind trees, flew over me (I was in a boat) and was gone

I was in a boat being towed by another boat as my engine had died. It was dark, likely around 9pm at night. As I was crossing the lake, a green-blue light came up over the trees to my left. It went up, then started to move in my direction. It flew almost right over my head, and as it crossed above me, there were three red-orange lights on the back side of the object. I shone my flashlight at it, and at my father who was driving the tow boat, but couldn't get his attention, or the objects attention. None of the lights blinked and none of them projected down or anything like that. I couldn't hear anything, but I was being towed in an aluminum boat so there was engine noise and wave noise that could have drowned out any sound.

I looked away trying to get my father and my friends attention in the front boat, and when I looked back it was gone.

Side note: My father and friend ask me if I had seen the weird light when we got to shore, but they had seen a different orange coloured light going up and down behind trees about a kilometer from where i'd seen my lights
