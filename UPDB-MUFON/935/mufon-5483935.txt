{rjl} An entity came into my room. A strange deep voice spoke in my mind. It spoke a language I've never heard. I felt my body being lifted. I remember screaming and trying to fight it off.

It was the summer of 2013. It was just after 3am. I was at a residence in NE Portland, OR. I was laying on my stomach on a couch watching TV. My daughter lay asleep on a matress behind the couch. I felt a pressure. It felt as if something laid on top of me. It was pushing into me. Weighing me down. I couldn't move. I couldn't breathe. I felt a sensation at the base of my skull. I heard a voice. It was deep, gargly. I was screaming, fighting yelling at myself as I felt myself being lifted. But I was paralyzed. I told myself to breathe. I screamed at my body to move. Next thing I know I woke on the couch. Everything else including my daughter seemed untouched. I have spent a lot of time since that night researching extraterrestrial beings, ufos, etc. I have now come to believe my first abduction happened when I was 11 years old. And it's now just coming to the surface for me.
