{rjl} Sighting on St. Clair

On August 18, 2014. 11:58 p.m. Mary rae baird was driving on st. Clair from the Ravensburg bridge towards the golden gate restaurant with her three children Alex,Morgan and kyle. She spotted a pinkish orange glowing object slightly to the right in the sky at this time it appeared stationary at the same time alex who was in the front seat also saw the object he says it appears more redish shortly after they pulled over in the Golden Gate parking lot Alex quickly snapped photos of the object starting at 11:59 p.m. as it was traveling south by southwest until I blinked out of sight through clouds in the distance at about 12:05 p.m. the object was to bright to be a plane and Alex could not see any blinking lights which are required by law at night it was also silent.
