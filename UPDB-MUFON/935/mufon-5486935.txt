{rjl} Circular orb that remained motionless. Could see fixed winged aircraft pass behind orb after approx. 30 minutes many miles behind orb.

Starlike orb that randomly flashed blue green and red lights. Thought possible helicopter but at a distance of 15 miles it was just to large to be a helo. Remains in same position for 2hrs then disappears. During the sighting I observed an fixed wing aircraft pass behind the object at approx 3-5 miles. Then the object disappeared. The object was hovering over Trinity or Houston Co. North East of my position possibly San Jacinto or Polk Co. Being retired military, I'm very familiar with the shapes, lights and flight characteristics of helicopters. At the distance observed it was just way to large to be any type of helo. Approx. altitude was 15-20,000 ft.
