Randomly flashing orb with speed and directional changes

Outside on patio overlooking lake, saw what at first looked like a plane moving from the South east towards me over the lake. The light it gave off grew to an intensity that rivalled the moon (also in the sky) making the apparently size grow double before diminishing. I could see the ufo the entire time while it moved from South east to North west over my residence, which I then lost sight of it as it when over the building. Everytime I mentally suggested to flash it did. Very awe inspiring.
