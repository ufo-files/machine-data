Large donut shaped UFO with at least 13 little UFOS

After putting my groceries in the car I went to get in my vehicle and leave. Something caught my eye directly above the Taco Bell. There was what appeared to be a military cargo like plane flying pretty low. I took my phone out to take a picture. Before I could a large donut shaped UFO that was grey and black appeared. There was no noise. It seemed to have an aura completely encircling it. As I turned the camera on (after wiping it off to make sure I wasn't seeing things) several smaller black UFOs appeared to come out of the donut shaped craft and follow a snake like pattern. I managed to take a picture, but before I could switch it to video mode the craft disappeared behind a tree and then was gone all together. I waited several minutes to see if it would reappear but it did not.
