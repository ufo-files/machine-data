Bright blue/teal color orb

I was sitting outside on my porch looking up at the sky as i usually do every night, and out of no where a bright white light appeared (kind of similar to someone turning on the flashlight on a phone) in the sky and inside the bright white light was an orb looking bright blueish greenish color light (definitely a color ive never seen before) it traveled down then right, then left, and then straight up into the sky and vanished above a neighbors tree out of nowhere just boom gone. It all happened so fast i would not have even had time to get my camera out i could only keep my eyes on it. Something i definitely will NEVER forget! It was pretty cool!
