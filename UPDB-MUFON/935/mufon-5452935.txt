We lived on a farm and heard this sound we took as a UFO.

Me and my husband were making a snack. It was pitch black dark out. All of our shades were open and suddenly heard a sound that we both agreed later, was as large as a telephone pole, powerful like a train engine yet soft in sound like the wind blowing thru trees. We both froze for as we remember, just a minute. My husband grabbed his sandwich and rushed to the bedroom. He said, " Lock the door." I remember thinking, "why? We were standing in the kitchen at nite with all shades open and we felt "exposed" as we discussed it later. We didn't talk about it until the next day and went out to see any evidence of anything and we did not. Not long after, I was riding my horse on the back of the ranch and saw a dead cow with a missing leg that was cut and missing as if someone took a razor blade and cut the leg off without marks that show sawing or hacking thru the bone and muscle. 
Later, when we moved to Fairborn nearby, we had another but different experience. I will file another report on that one. It was the last one that ever occurred to me or him as far as I know.
