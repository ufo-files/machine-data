Two bright pulsating red lights moving slowly and disappearing to the west beyond the hills.

I had let the dog out for the last run at about 11:20 pm when I noticed two bright, slowly pulsating, intense red lights to the west. I couldn't tell the elevation and they did not at first appear to be moving. They kept a constant distance from each other and were moving slowly south against the prevailing wind. They eventually turned to the west and disappeared behind some hills. I could hear no noise coming from their direction but could hear a commercial jet passing over at a high altitude to the south of my position (out of Las Vegas). I could not make out any shape of what the lights might be attached to. There were no other lights noted as you might see on an aircraft at night.
