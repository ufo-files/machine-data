{jpg} 8 ORANGE OBJECTS MOVING E TO W AT INCREDIBLE SPEEDS

FILLED OUT AND FILED AT MUFON HQ BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18809
ON JANUARY 24TH, 2019 FROM A WRITTEN REPORT SENT IN TO HQ.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

THE EVENING OF JULY 3, WE OBSERVED 8 ORANGE ORBS MOVING FROM EAST TO WEST IN BROKEN FILE FORMATION. AT 1500 TO 1800 FT. THE THIRD ORB DROPPED BACK TO 5TH IN FORMATION AND INSTANTLY BURST FORWARD TO IT'S ORIGINAL POSITION. HEY MADE NO SOUND, HAD NO CONTROL SURFACES, AND ONE BY ONE BURST FORWARD AND WINKED OUT. AS THEY DISAPPEARED A RAINBOW AURA SURROUNDED THEM.
