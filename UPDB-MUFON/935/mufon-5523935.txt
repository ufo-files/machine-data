ORANGE FIREBALL SPOTTED OVER STATEN ISLAND

Approximately 8:30pm on Staten Island, I was told by a friend to come outside because there is something weird flying over. I went outside and saw what looked to be a fireball (orb shaped) floating over and it appeared to be ascending and then at times it appear to descend. It was moving rather slow from our vantage point, but we really couldnt tell its direction or speed. Additionally, seconds before the fireball disappeared or went out- it began to flicker on and off. Perhaps the cloud cover masked the UFO as it began to rise?
