Series of lights moving erratically in rural NY.

A man in his driveway observed a fuzzy bright red light moving in the distance.The light descended and resolved into a 60-75-foot grid of lights -- four redlights on each side with a square of four blue lights within. A white light wasflashing below the grid. The grid descended below a treeline one or two milesaway. The man soon noticed a steady orange light, accompanied by a flashing whitelight, approaching from the same direction. The flight and speed were somewhaterratic. This anomaly was lost from view on the horizon after about 5 minutes.After a failed attempt to find camera film, the man returned outside to see thesame or another orange light moving erratically in the distance, including avertical "S" maneuver. Soon a white beam, resembling an auto headlight pointing down, along with asmaller flashing white light were seen within 100 yards. These began to riseabove the field. Then the grid of red and blue was again observed, this timeabove the white lights. The grid flashed once in a strobe-like fashion. All ofthe lights then proceeded laterally and slowly from the scene until lost in thedistance. No sound was heard at any time and no object shape could be discerned.Total duration was one hour.
