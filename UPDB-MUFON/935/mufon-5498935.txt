{cak} Looked out our sliding glass doors and saw bright object hovering over a security light.

This was my first sighting. I was 9 or 10 years old. I was alone in the living room when I noticed a bright light above the neighbor's security light. I stepped outside and realized that it wasn't making any sound and was staying in one place. I had, at first,thought it was a helicopter because it was maintaining the same position. I was very excited and not scared at all. Looking back it seems strange that I wasn't frightened at all. I went to get my parents but it was as if they didn't hear me. I got binoculars, stepped back outside and looked towards the object. Just as I started to focus the binoculars the object shot off to the South at an extremely fast rate then simply vanished. As far back as I can remember, I've heard unusual sounds at night and was terrified of one corner in my room. I had this feeling of terror and believed that some creature came out of that corner at night. This was the beginning of sightings and visitations that first I, and then my daughter experienced. We both came to believe, separately, that these visits have to do with my grandson, who is also her son. He is extremely intelligent. I have had several sightings. The last one occurred outside my grandson's bedroom window when he was 8 or 9. My daughter has had recurring visits from what is usually called the greys. We feel as if they are watching over us. We feel that they are protecting us and monitoring our family. We don't want a lot of people to know about this because we don't want my grandson's life to be disrupted. He is a very special young man.
