{jpg} 6 wedge shaped air craft

Two nights ago about 8:30pm 6 total objects flew south of our house. First 3 objects flew by with one object turned directly south the other two flew from the southeast to the northwest. 15 sec later a single objected followed the direction of the two that flew to the north west. 10 or 15 seconds later 2 more objects followed the path of the first 2 which was to the north west. The problem was these were NOT aircraft of any known type. There appeared to be NO sound. The object that turned south appeared to turn at a quicker rate than aircraft I have seen. It was NOT 90degrees but damn close. What was the most disconcerting to me is they ALL had est.5 ORANGE lights displayed in wedge shape. These were NO aircraft. There was NO red , green or strobe lights on any of the 6 object.
