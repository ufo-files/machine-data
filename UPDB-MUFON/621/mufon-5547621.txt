It fell like a shooting star and then swung back up in a pendulum motion (or like a yo-yo). It was bright neon green and as big as my thumbnail.

I was by myself at the bus stop near Garden Grove waiting for my bus ride home. I was staring in the general direction and I believed that I was witnessing a shooting star. I was looking Northwest towards Bolsa Chica and Huntington Beach. It was leaving a trail when it was coming down. Suddenly, it swung back upwards after almost hitting the ground! It happened at 7:23 pm exactly and lasted for about a minute, then trajected it's way back up to the top of the sky from where it began. I felt like it was peeking at me through some trees and it made me feel a tad awkward because it kept swaying back and forth. I tried not to stare at it for too long because I felt uncomfortable when I realized it wasn't a shooting star. It was beautiful and magnificent, nonetheless, bright-neon green-and an awesome sight of light to see!! I just didn't want to stare at it for too long. God I wish I would have had a video camera to have taped it for everyone who did not see it! Just 5 minutes later, a helicopter was flying in that same area. This did not affect my cellphone communications or my cellphone clock.
