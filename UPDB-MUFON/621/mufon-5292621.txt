I found newspaper articles about the 1975-76 cattle mutilation in Teller County Colorado

While investigating a case of an encounter, I came across a few newspaper articles about cattle mutilations in Teller county Colorado. I believe I was able to get the entire string from June 1975 to June 1976. These mutilations centered around a small area called Gillett, Colorado. The interesting part is that on September 18, 1975 there was a mention of helicopters being photographed in the area that matched witness accounts. Also on the Gillett Wikipedia page there is a mention of unmarked helicopters. I contacted the Teller county Sheriff's department, unfortunately their records don't go back that far.
