{cak} A round glowing object of significant size moved across the sky across New Mexico viewed from Taos on 9/17/2016.

We were in a Taos New Mexico Campground with our RV. I went outside to check the grill at approximately 7:25 PM and noticed a very large glowing object southeast of our location moving across the sky away from us. I called the other three people out to observe the object and grabbed my camera to shoot pictures. The object at times appeared to be standing still and other times floated across the sky. It appeared to through off an light object that circled around and came back into itself. The object moved away from us in a steady manner until it was a tiny dot and then out of sight.

After the sighting we watched the local News in the morning. The station based in Albuquerque mentioned that they had received many reports of a UFO but had checked with a Military base in Texas. The base confirmed that they had launched a weather balloon. My photo attachments I believe show otherwise.
