(GS) Very bright red light, passed at moderate pace overhead, dimmed suddenly, proceded over horizon

At 9:05 pm I was outside having a smoke when I saw something over the ocean (my fire escape has a view of the sea). It was BRIGHT red when I first noticed it (and I mean super freaking bright, almost exactly like an emergency flare, which is what I thought it was at first), and glowing with such intensity that it appeared to have a misty sheen around it, as if it was pointing a bright light directly at me (the sky was clear enough to see stars, but theres always some moisture in the air over the ocean). I was absolutely transfixed. It deviated slightly on its path as it appeared to be slowly progressing towards me, so at first I thought it must be a small airplane (as the big ones, big enough to have a light that bright, never visibly deviate from a smooth arc, I thought). But it was soon moving about as fast as a quick LEAR jet (heading toward the Southeast), and (heres the spooky part) was absolutely silent as it passed overhead. I see (and hear!) tons of air traffic over SF (my view faces south, toward the airport), and this was not like anything I have ever seen. It remained very bright until it had passed overhead, and then suddenly became a dim red glow which I watched until it receded over the East horizon. I am absolutely sure that this was not a satellite, as I have seen many. Sighting lasted about two minutes, or less.
