(GS) Very bright light, about a helicopters altitude, moved very slowly and smoothly in a straight line until out of sight.

I was at my girlfriends grandparents house observing the stars when her grandfather pointed out the bright light moving across the sky. Myself, my girlfriend, and both her grandparents observed the light until it was out of sight. I was unsure of what it was from the start, I just knew it was too large and close to be a star, it moved too slowly to be a plane and it did not blink.
