I casually drove up to an nighttime observation overlook, observed a bright light approx 1K ft altitude over my home neighborhood. I got out of my car to remove possibilities of reflections. After about 10 minutes, the bright light changed from hovering to instant flight with two sharp 90 turns, then disappeared upwards. I knew what it was as a reliable acquaintance was publicly mocked for reporting similar events one week prior which was reported in the Eugene Register Guard newspaper. Therefor I kept quiet, totally. Frankly, I was ecstatic.
