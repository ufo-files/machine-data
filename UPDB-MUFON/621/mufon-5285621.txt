Was dark starry night. Saw single red light moving at 30 MPH. Turned, went directly overhead. No sound.Blocked stars with triangle shape.

Cool crisp night. I went out into pasture next to house [exact location details have been moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] to look at stars. I could hear football game and lights from Kirk Academy school a mile away to the NNW [exact location details have been moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] I then noticed a single dim red light moving over, then south of the school. It did not seem to be very high above tree level and was moving about 30 MPH. I could not hear a motor or engine sound, nor could I see anything except the single light. When its position was due-west of me, it turned and came directly toward me. Still no noise heard. It then passed directly overhead and allowed me to see that the light was in the bottom center of a triangular body. I could tell its shape because it blocked the sky and I could not see the stars through it. It looked totally black, except for the single red light. I would estimate the craft was ~ 50 yd/side and was moving in the direction of one of its points. The speed was still approx ~30 MPH and it never slowed or sped-up. I made it a point to listen for any sound, but I could only hear the faintest sound of air moving over it! It continued east, then turned nort toward the Grenada Lake and Airport (GNF) area, and I lost sight of it.
