Alright so this takes place in Long Beach, CA at around 7:50pm. The clearest day its been in months. Im watching the Broncos Saints game and my roommate asks me if Id like to join him in a cigarette. I accepted and we walk out onto the balcony.

After about a minute of being out there I see a red-orange light just rising up in the sky, my initial thought was an emergency flare but it wasnt coming down. I interrupted my roommates story and said "Im sorry but WTF is that?" He first thought it was a police helicopter cause of the light and its altitude. But then I mentioned it was too quiet and the light was too dull and wasnt directed anywhere. It kept coming closer and closer to us, the light became much bigger. Within moments it was going to directly fly over my apartment.

I pull out my phone to record a video but the patio light was on and couldnt pick it up on my phone. I opened the door behind me and flipped the switch to turn off the light. I look back and the light makes a left turn heading south then fades out into a little dot.

And that was it, it disappeared. We stuck around for another 15 minutes in hope of a return. Ive been debating if I should call the police because we are SPOOKED, yet excited. Looking for other reports or sightings but havent come up with anything.
