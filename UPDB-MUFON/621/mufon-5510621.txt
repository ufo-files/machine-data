At about 12:45a.m. on June 1st 2013 I was in my bed in an upstairs master that faces due NE. I got up to look out of the window, and as I approached the window, I heard a sound that can only be described as pure energy. I immediately drew back the curtains. As I did so, I saw a large black disk flying over my house headed for the adjacent tree line at about 75 miles mph... It slowed down and with a loud crack flew off toward the NE at an indescrible speed. My girlfriend was downstairs and heard a loud sound approaching the house, she looked up and saw a red light in the stained glass in our second story window over the family room. The sound was piercing and extremely loud and destinctive. The craft made a sound like a ball ping hammer hitting sheet metal as it took off. There was no warning of approaching aircraft in the distance before we witnessed this thing. What I saw was about 50 feet in diameter, black, and definitely disk shaped. I did not see a red light as my wife reported, but i could make out an almost black light styled energy around the craft. This was not a jet or a helicopter. It only moved in one direction and it flew so fast that i don't know if it actually flew off or de-materialized. I will say that it definitly started out headed toward the Northeast. I don't mind saying that this thing scared the s**t out me! I have been waiting for a lng time to catch a glimpse of something, now Im not so sure I want to know. My girlfriend is very scared tonight and I cant sleep. It may something terestrial because it was loud as hell. Usually from the reports I have often read, these things are usually silent.
