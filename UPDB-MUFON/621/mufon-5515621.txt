Two other people noticed at the GoStation

I was at the GoStation, and I noticed the sky was very red that day. The sun was extremely orange and hot looking as the sun came up: it was very early in the morning.
I looked to the SE and noticed three bright orbs hovering in a vertical line. They hovered for 3 minutes and then started moving back and forth slowly. 

As I was riding on the train, I was shocked at how bright the sun was, I could hardly look into the sky. But When I did, I noticed the orbs moved closer to the sun and kept appearing and disappearing. I saw about four of them all together within a half hour. I know other people on the train noticed the strange activity as well. 

Weird thing is, I could not get a photo. It didn't show up in the camera...
