Three bright lights - didn't blink - moved NW slightly then sped off NE

I was leaving work when I saw three bright lights arranged in a triangle, it was still light enough that I could a faint black triangle behind the three lights. The object then slowly moved left (NW direction) then stopped, and sped off in the NE direction. When it sped off it was high enough that it was above the buildings downtown. Unfortunately I lost sight of it when it flew over downtown and I didn't have enough time to take a photo/video.
