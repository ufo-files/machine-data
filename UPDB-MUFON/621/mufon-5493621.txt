{dr}Orb. apx. 3 ft. long. flew right at the windsheild of the car I was Driving. When about to hit car flew right over the hood and vanished. Like a mirage. Paper thin. Like a jack that kids play with without the spike going thru the middle

I was heading North on Sconticut Neck Road with a friend of mine when I observed something fluttering about three feet off the ground and flying right towards us. My first thought was is my friend seeing the same thing I am and my mind immediately returned to the first time I had witnessed a UFO back in the 60's.I continued forward as the orb flew right at us. Just as it approached the front of the car it flew right over the hood! It was amazing however it scared the crap out of my friend. He told me he couldn't sleep at all that night and was scared to death. The orb was 2 to three feet in length and was flying two to three feet off the ground. It pulsated or waivered maybe like a cloaking device. It kind of looked like a jack that kids play with without the spike going thru the middle. As it was flying directly at us it moved back and forth like a needle in a compass going from east to west back to east again back to west back to east etc. until it flew over the hood of the car and vanished! Unlike my friend I had or experienced no fear. God Bless MUFON and all others who have had encounters of any kind!
