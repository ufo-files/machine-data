Highly reflective surface with dark areas flying straight and over a small cloud bank where it disappeared.

I am a Navy Vet having been trained in anti air warfare and have been a bridge look out. My house has many commercial, private and a few military aircraft fly overhead.What I saw was a an object at about 6,000-10,000 ft high, about 50 feet long, flying straight and level until it flew over a small cumulus cloud. I was able to observe all sides of the cloud but the object did not emerge.The object was very reflective in the bright sunlight with some dark areas. I could not see any wings or proper aircraft structure and it did not make any sound.Over the next couple hours, several commercial aircraft had flown overhead from low altitude seaplanes and high altitude jets, but none resembled the object and I could hear all of their engines without issue.Weather was high visibility, a couple scattered cumulus clouds but was otherwise clear.
