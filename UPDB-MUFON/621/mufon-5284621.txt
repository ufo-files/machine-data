Road sighting, the witnesses observe light going along their way during a rural trip. Strong lights observed but no sound... this seemed extrange to them so they decided to stop at the next free spot in the road. There were several other people taking a glimpse at the object but at the point they were looking was nothing else parked. They saw a big obscure object but with strong lights and this began to descend over the trees up above a hill. The witnesses felt panic and decided to get away in their car.
