{tjd} Triangular shaped metallic object observed flying over & across I-205

While driving southbound on I-205 south, I observed a triangular shaped, metallic silver object in motion in the air, approximately 2 miles to the south,  It appeared solid and the sunlight reflected off it. The object appeared smooth, devoid of features that could be seen from a distance. Its triangular shape was similar to the shape of a basic paper airplane - a slender, equilateral triangle with a base ~1/4 the length of a side. Object appeared to be moving west/SW, and slightly upwards. It crossed above I-205 and continued on same trajectory, then abruptly changed direction & moved downwards and more SW, then up again. View of object was then obscured by vehicles & signs. Unknown if it landed. Did not sight it again.
