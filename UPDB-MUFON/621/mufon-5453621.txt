Star-like white and red UFO

My fiancé and I usually go for walks at night to get some air during the Coronavirus quarantine. Last night as we were walking, I saw an object, slightly brighter than a star, down west a little over the horizon. The object shined bright white and red, not a combination of both but like the left half was white and the right half was red. The object appeared first approximately 30 degrees over the horizon and descended quickly in a curved path to 20 degrees over the horizon approx. It did not leave a trail of any kind nor did it make a sound. This was within the city, usually the city light opaques nearly all the stars in the night sky, that's how I know this wasn't a shooting star, as this star-like object was brighter than one.
