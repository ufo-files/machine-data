(GS) I was driving back to my base at NAS Oceana travelling Hwy 58 East towards Virginia Beach...

I was an Airman stationed at NAS Oceana. I worked days and nights in the Line Division on 2 carriers between 86-89. I was headed back to base one Sunday night to make muster in the morning from a weekend in NC. Driving through Southhampton County on Hwy. 58 East I noticed different flashing lights in the sky.At first I thought Helo exercises until I then noticed the blink of an eye speed with no BANG in the sound barrier. I pulled over.Yes.I was Awestruck.Hell, I worked on F-14s at the time with a NATO and World Criuse behind me. I seen it all....until this. It was triangular in shape.The size of 2 Aircraft carriers with flashing lights mostly around the 3 points..there were also dim white lights under the belly but too far off to tell.This thing moved like a yellow jacket..seriously.Thing is..Ive seen Harriers hover...nothing this damn big though.It made no sound.I could see no emissions nor could tell at what point the propulsion was coming from.I watched this thing about 6 minutes in a gaze. My headlights were still on..The sky was clear that night.Plenty of stars.After darting to anf fro a bit...It ascended into the heavens...this was in 1988...I rode back to base but dared not tell the watch commander lest I go to the looney bin.I knew what the worlds air superiority was back then...what I witnessed..Was not of this world.
