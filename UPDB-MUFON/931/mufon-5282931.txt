Seen in the Appalachian mountains

My dad was visiting friends in Pennsylvania that lives in the mountains. He drove up to the top of a hill where he has reception to make a call. Before making the call, he took 2 photos back to back (approximately 10 seconds between photos). In the first image, off in the distance (an exact distance is hard to say) on the right, there is a black disc shaped object that sits starkly in front of the white clouds. The object is clearly not a part of the mountain line peaking faintly through the clouds. In the next image, the black disc is nowhere to be seen.
