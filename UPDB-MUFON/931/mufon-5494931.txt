{nd} Saw a glowing orb that was brighter than any star and was hovering in one general spot. About 10 minutes later it had changed positions and later it was gone.

I looked out of my bathroom window which around 9:30 on March 26th 2015 when I noticed a large glowing sphere hovering over in the sky which I at first sight I thought might be a star but then I saw it move a bit so I went and got my girlfriend to take a look. She saw the same slight hovering that I did. I then went to my front porch and took a video and pictures of the sphere with my cell phone. The pictures were interesting in that there was some intereference in many of the shots which showed up in a pattern. I was able to zoom in and take a picture without the intereference if I didn't focus the camera on it but if I zoomed out the interference was in the same basic pattern. The object never moved much while I watched it but when I checked on it 15 minutes later and it had moved positions in the sky. It was much farther west and looked as if it had moved north as well. The next time I checked on it at least 45 minutes after the initial sighting I could no longer see it.
