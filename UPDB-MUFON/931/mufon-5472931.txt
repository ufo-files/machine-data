{rjl} My friend and I were saw a red light in the sky pulse and disappear.

On October 18th, 2017 at 7:25 in the morning my friend and I were travelling by taxi and coming off the Hepburnville exit off of Rt. 15 when I looked up in the sky and saw a bright red light in the morning sky. At first I thought it was maybe Mars, a plane or satellite but then it slowly pulsed brighter and disappeared. I then brought it to the attention of my buddy and he saw it when it reappeared. It did the same thing again twice more but then completely disappeared. I would normally think it was an airplane but the speed of the pulsation was far slower than any airplane I've seen before. Also, it was stationary and the light coming from it was more like a lighthouse speed than a commuter beacon from the local airport. It was more like a gradual dimming. I thought it wise to report even though it's still likely an aircraft possibly reflecting reddish dawn light.
