{cak} Traveling north to south

It was July 4 and we standing on my friend stairs. My wife my son and my self. I got a plate of food on the stairs and I was sweeping when my son was 8 years at the time. He start pointing to the ski and crying that the world is over. When I reach to him that thing was a ball in the middle was orange and yellow around. When I look I dodged my self how low its was. Its was traveling in a good speed but when I look that thing was pulling the clouds with it. Like small one to a real big one. Its was the same when they draw cartoons and beside the mouth they draw like a little bubble and the the big one where they wrote what happening. So people notice that character is talking. That thing was traveling in a straight line until I can see it anymore. What a experience. Its wasn't a fire work. No sound coming out of it. Its was real silent. That's why I didn't see it because I was busy cleaning. My son was looking to the ski and he was crying telling me the world is over.
