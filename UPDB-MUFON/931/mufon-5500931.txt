{cak} 16 pulsating orbs flying in a a formation spanning a mile. All went to the exact same spot in the sky and vanished.

Driving home in my neighborhood when girlfriend in passenger seat asked what those objects are. I could not see them due to the roof/angle and she made me turn around. We witnessed approx 16 reddish orange glowing orbs flying in formation (like a loosely organized group of geese). You could tell they were solid and under intelligent control. They maintained a fairly straight path, but when you singled one out, you could tell it was wobbling and a bit jerky, not like a plane/bird, etc. Formation bumbled along for a good 3 minutes before the lead craft got to a point in the sky, dimmed to half brightness, then just a pinpoint and then vanished. Over the next 2 minutes the rest of the craft in formation went to the exact spot in the sky and repeated the dimming cycle before vanishing. We started talking about it, think it was over when 2 more popped up from behind homes on the horizon and then slowly bumbled their way over the next 1-2 minutes to the same spot and vanished in the same fashion.
