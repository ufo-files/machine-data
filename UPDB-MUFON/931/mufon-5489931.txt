{rjl} I took 3 pictures within 10 seconds while driving truck on rte 13 bristol pa.

I was driving on rte 13 in bristol pa heading south to Burlington Bristol Bridge on 7-07-15 at 8:02 pm. I observed a cool looking sky/cloud/ sunset were the clouds were arranged in strange way which caught my eye. I grabbed my I Phone and quickly snapped 3 pictures in a time frame of about 10 to 15 seconds. (I've taken pics of sunsets like this many times before.)I proceeded on my journey over to new jersey to see a friend. A couple of days later i remembered that i took some pics with phone of a cool looking sky so i pulled up pics to see what they looked like. I looked at each of the three pics and i could see there was a dark formation in 2 of the 3 frames. I quickly uploaded photos to lap top to get a clearer picture when i saw the dark formation in 2 of 3 pics. I looked even closer and in the first pic u can see a truck coming towards me and then in second frame the truck is right in front of me. This shows the time elapse between each picture. The dark formation moves to right. In pic 2 object moves to a different part of sky, and then in pic 3 it completely disappears.
