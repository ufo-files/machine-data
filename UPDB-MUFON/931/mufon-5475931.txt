{cak} Stationary orange lights in shape of triangle

At home my wife and I were on the porch around 11:15-11:20 when she pointed out a single orange light moving west very slowly. We walked out into the yard and I noticed it was actually 2 orange lights. She thought it was lanterns until she saw they were moving toward us rather than up. It stopped and hovered for about 20 seconds and a bright white light "popped out". Immediately after this light appeared the orange lights began moving southwest at a much faster speed. The white light looked like a star, but within 10 seconds it dissipated. The object came over our house and I noticed a 3rd orange light behind the front "leading" 2. It made no noise, not a single sound. It gained speed until it was past the treeline. The event was approximately 3-5 minutes total. I made an attempt to get a picture as it went over my house but the lights WOULD NOT appear on my screen as if invisible to my camera but not my eyes.
