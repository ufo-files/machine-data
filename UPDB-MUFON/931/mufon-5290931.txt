Tapped In

I believe it was December 2016. I don't remember the exact date. My cousin John Braxton came to LA after seeing my profile on Facebook and thinking I was rich because he read I had my own company. Begged be to go to the dispensary to get marijuana. I didn’t know he was a drug head that had went to prison. I had only did marijuana but once in Texas with a girl named Ammie. I gave in, we went, he bought some grass. I bought a candy bar. Back at the house at the kitchen table, I broke the candy in half and ate it. 

Later to find out that it was 75 milligrams, after consuming, it took a while. Then I heard a click sound on the right lower back side of my head. I started laughing and couldn’t stop for hours. Worn out and exhausted, I lay on my bed and had an experience. I was alive without a body. I was aware of me traveling through black space. I saw a quick vision of thousands of white or light grey heads looking like they were all seated at their computers. There were other visions but I can’t seem to remember them. After the visions, I felt the need to communicate. I asked questions with my thoughts. Something answered them and IT was very clear and decisive. Consciousness was ALIVE and SPEAKING from within me?
