Spotted in odd colored objects larger than other stars in the sky as I was driving

Spotted in odd colored objects larger than other stars in the sky as I was driving Leaving Klamath Falls Oregon towards Chiloquin I noticed a star in the sky directly in front of me that looked like I could reach out and touch it. Just me on the highway pitch black with an open sky it’s stood out so I pulled over to watch it and try to capture it in a photo which did not turn out. As I tried to refocus the camera the object began moving slowly at first then rapidly change directions and disappeared in a streak of blue light. I thought I was crazy for years or maybe just tired. That is until I saw a Netflix special called hangar one in which I saw multiple video footage or photographs of almost an identical incident I rewound and watched again just to make sure I wasn’t trying to assimilate my experience with what I was seeing and it is absolutely the sameColor shape and movement pattern that I saw so I thought I would give you what information I could remember
