Two starlike fireballs, 1 larger wobbling then straight up following a smaller one

My wife and I were on our way home from shopping in Menomonie Wi. We were just driving towards Downing Wi and noticed 2 yellow orange starlike objects that were moving very slowly in a northeast direction.they appeared to be between Downing and Glenwood City Wi and quite high in altitude. The first ne on the right was either smaller or higher than the left one. It seemed to just move higher in altitude until it disappeared. The larger or lower one wobbled from left to right and then also appeared to move higher and disappeared from sight. Within the 1-2 minute time we observed this I did manage to get one photo of the larger object.
