Three objects in flight only viewable with night vision equipment followed by single aircraft.

On April 6, 2021 at 9:34pm on Philadelphia Road near Springboro, PA property owner was checking land for wildlife using military grade night vision equipment. During the check three objects were clearly visible in the sky with a bright infrared signature only viewable with night vision.  The objects were in a tight formation and on steady path across the northern sky heading east at a slow rate. Objects were checked without NV equipment and found to be invisible with naked eye. A single aircraft was trailing the objects and appeared to be at same altitude. The aircraft had beacon light as normal civilian aircraft usually does. No sound was detected. Altitude and speed were unknown for the objects and aircraft. Weather conditions were clear, and stars visible with naked eye.  Objects were viewed for approx. 2 minutes during the traveled path until no-longer visible due to tree line.
