{cak} Red orb moving west to north. lasted 15-20 MINS.

On the 21st of july 2014. I saw an Red Orb floating over Petosky Mich. I was standing on back porch looking at the stars when i seen this ball of light just floating in the sky in the direction of town. It just stayed there for 5-10 mins. It then started to flot towards the north over the bay. Im about.5 mls from the bay with trees and roads between. The orb was tree top level as it headed out over the bay still no sound. There was no winds nor any animals sound. I felt nothing at the time i was watching. No feelings at all. The orb kept its course heading out over the bay when i lost it. This is the third time something like this happened to my. The others were while i was in the navy. Once on the easy costs and once while I was on the west coast. Think you for ypu time.
