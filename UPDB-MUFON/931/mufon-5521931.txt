in the middle of the night on a camping trip i was awakened by the sound of wind in the trees and saw a glowing blue light landing nearby

in the summer of 74 or 75 i was at an y.m.c.a. summer youth camp in the san bernadino mountains. one of the activities was a blindfolded nature hike up into the mountain to a high meadow. our sleeping bags had already been delivered to the spot when we got there. the counselors chaperones and us kids( about age ten) maybe a hundred of us total were sleeping late into the night. i was awakened by the loud sound of wind in the trees a loud rushing sound. i managed to open my eyes and lift my head off my pillow to look around. i could see a brightly glowing object or light. something was giving off light but didn't light up the surrounding area it was a bright white/blue-flame blue-electric blue. and was flashing in an erratic strobe type flash. it seemed to be landing descending to the ground the last thing i remember is that it almost was on the ground. i was soo sleepy i felt drugged and was all i could do to keep my eyes open and head up for that long. i did not remember the incident until some time later when i was back home.
