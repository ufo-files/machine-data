Beach ball size slowly revolving, spherical object about 300 feet from ground

A dry, cold, bright sunlit February day at about 2pm I saw a beach ball sized revolving object approximately hovering or almost 'hanging' 300 feet from ground ~ was over woodland perhaps 150 -200 yards from my (then) back door. I saw this because my attention was drawn by the most terrible noise being made by more crows than I've ever seen in one place. These creatures were going frantic, attracted by something in their midst. There were 100s of them, I have witnessed crows mobbing a buzzard, an owl, falcons many times in past there'd be at most 10 of them ~ as said there were literally 100s.
I watched this spectacle for may be 3 - 4 minutes, suddenly crows swirled away from the centre to reveal this object hanging, slowly revolving in space ~ as it turned I could see the sun reflecting from its brilliantly polished silver looking surface ~ I couldn't believe what I was seeing as it defied physics as I have always understood them. I screamed for my son to come and see this phenomena ~ to no avail ~ I wanted him to bring my camera. I must have watched it for perhaps a minute or 1 & 1/2 minutes when it suddenly took off in a directly easterly direction at a speed I can only imagine ~ FAST.
By the way in which the crows were behaving I believe whatever it was must have been a living entity of some kind ~ they take little notice of remote control planes or hot air balloons ~ clearly they saw it as a threat, invading their space. What I do know is that the area in which I lived had a large iron stone content in the ground and it was hovering over the path of a fast flowing underground river, behind the house where I used to live there was a large fault in the hill behind the house.
When told my son what I'd seen ~ he must have been around 16 at the time he said it must have been a weather balloon ~ it certainly wasn't one of those. Also it isn't far from a large military base ~ perhaps 4 miles "as crow flies."
I didn't report it 'officially' to police or anyone as I didn't think they'd believe me or be remotely interested ~ I shared my experience with several friends over time ~ no idea whether they believed me or not ~ what I've described is 100% true. Later that day I took a walk up into the wood and there were a lot of broken, damaged laurel bushes, which was odd, I did wonder if the entity had landed there and caused the bushes to be crushed, I'd never seen such a thing happen before nor since and I don't think children would have carried out such damage., but I cannot be sure about that aspect of my experience. I realise this isn't what would rate very high on your list of encounters. It happened and I want to put it on record as I'm now pretty old and shall not be here for much longer.
