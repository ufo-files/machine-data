I awoke in the night and saw a disc object on back lawn

I was asleep in bed in an upstairs bedroom. I was awoke by something unknown and went to the bedroom window and saw a disc shaped object on the back lawn. Object was approx 6 - 10 feet in dia and had lights and a dome, a classic saucer shape in fact. I stood and watched it for some time and returned to bed as I thought I was dreaming. It was only in the morning when I went outside that I realised it was not a dream as there on the ground was a perfect circle of discoloured grass. My Father also witnessed the evidence in the morning.
