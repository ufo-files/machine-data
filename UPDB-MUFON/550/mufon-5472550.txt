{jpg} Saw a flash from the corner of my eye as I snapped these two images from my patio.

11/07/2017, 4:20pm

From my patio, while photographing a palm tree, I noticed an object corkscrew through space. The object reflected the sun as it spun and moved North. I took two photos within seconds of each other that show the object's movement.
I though this object might have been space debris or a satellite revealed by the sun's reflection. The object stopped glimmering and was no longer visible.
