Very white brute light, with dark grey triangle shadow.

At approximately 11 pm in pluckley village, heard a plane going over head, which draw my attention to the skies, then from there, a few minutes later..we was star gazing because it was a lovely clear night, I first noticed a very bright white light, going in very slowly, and silently in a straight line, which at that point both if us couldn't take our eyes off of the object. We watched it go right over head, when Tina noticed it had a triangle form.
We continued watching it silently going across the sky, until it went by a huge cloud..then at that point it wasn't visible. So we continued to watch to see when it would come out from the lowest part of the cloud, but after waiing for 20 minutes, we no longer saw it.
