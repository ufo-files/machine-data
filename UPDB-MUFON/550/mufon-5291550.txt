Orb UFO with a something inside moving as if searching filmed October 11th at 8:34 pm Hiking trail in Glendora Mystic trail/Punk out trail S

At 8:34 I came outside to stargaze as usual thinking it was a clear night. I began to see a light on the foothills of Glendora by a hiking trail named Mystic Canyon trail by Punk out trail. This object moved around several times as I was trying to capture it on my scope using a Samsung Note 10 and my landoe telescope. I screen record by having the feature on Photo because it is the best quality I can get versus using the video function. This thing eventually stopped long enough to where I was able to zoom in 60x on my scope and also with my phone. I was able to focus on the object and you can see this thing inside of it moving around as if it was searching for something. Eventually around the 9:47 mark I grabbed my other phone and left the scope still because the object just stayed there while the thing inside moved around. I recorded my setup so you can see from another perspective live while recording the object. Eventually the object disintegrates or disappears and reappears in another location on the same foothill and was able to capture it again. Eventually the object moved behind some tree lines and power lines close to me so it became harder to focus on it. If you watch the video on here skip to 9:00 minute mark. This was facing North at the time.
