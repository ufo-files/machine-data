{rjl} SAW IN BROD DAYLIGHT 800 FEET FROM ME FOR 8 TO TEN MIN.TILL TWO FIGHTER JETS CAME DOWN THE VALLEY MY SISTER AND MY FRIEND [Name removed/cms/tg] WERE ALSO WITNESSES

I WAS ON THE RANCH ALONE THAT DAY MY PARENTS WERE IN BAKERSFIELD MY OLDER SISTER AND MY FRIEND WHO WAS VISITING ME FROM BASS LAKE FOR THE SUMMER WENT TO A LITTLE DINNER WHERE MY SISTER HAD A SUMMER JOB SO I WAS WATCHING TV WHEN I NOTICED SOMTHING OUT THE WINDOW AND AT FIRST THOUGHT IT WAS A HELACOPTOR CHECKING THE TRANSFORMERS ON THE POWER LINES CUZ WE HAD 88 ACERS OF ALFALFA AND LIVE WAY OUT IN NO MANS LAND AND THE POWER COMPANY WOULD DO THAT FROM TIME TO TIME SO I SEE THIS OUT THE CORNER OF MY EYE AND THINK NOTHING OF IT TILL ALL OF A SUDDEN I REALIZE I DON'T HEAR ANY SOUND AT ALL SO I GET UP FROM THE COUCH AND INSTED OF LOOKING OUT THE WINDOW I STEP OUT ON THE FRONT DECK AND LOOK TO THE NORTH EAST AND OVER THE RIVER BEAD SITTING JUST ABOVE POWER LINES MAYBE 5 FEET I SEE SOMTHING THAT CHANGED MY LIFE FOREVER IM LOOKING AT A MASSIVE TRIANGLE SOME IN MY GUESSTOMATION 2 TO 3 FOOTBALL FIELDS IN LENGTH HOVERING RIGHT OVER THE RIVER BEAD FULL ON METALIC SILVER OR CHROME COLLOR THE TOP SEEM TO BE REFLECTING THE BLUE SKY FROM ABOVE AND THE BOTTOM REFLECTING THE DIRT FROM THE RIVER BEAD SO IT SEEMED THIN THO ME BUT MASSIVE!! A BLUE LIGHT AT THE TIP A YELLOW LIGHT AT ONE CORNER AND A GREEN LIGHT AT THE OTHER CORNER WITH A BIG RED PULSATING TYPE LIGHT UNDERNEATH ALL THOUGHT THEY WERE NOT REALLY BRIGHT CUZ IT WAS LIKE 2 OR 3 PM ON A SUMMER DAY SO I AM LOOKING AT THIS OBJECT IN COMPLETE AMAZMENT WHEN ALL OF A SUDDEN MY SISTER IS DRIVING UP THE DIRT ROAD WITH MY FRIEND IN THE CAR SHE IS DRIVING VERY FAST AND ALMOST LOOSES CONTROL WHEN SHE MAKES THE TURN TO COME UP THE HILL TO THE HOUSE IM STILL WATCHING THIS OBJECT WHEN ALL OF A SUDDEN I HEAR SOMTHING COMING DOWN THE VALELY I TURN TO LOOK AND FLYING DOWN THE VALLEY TOWARD THE CRAFT IS TWO FIGHTER JETS FLYING BELOW RADER AND AS QUICK AS I TURNED TO SEE THE PLANES COMING ITURN BACK AND THE CRAFT WAS GONE WITHOUT A SOUND OR A FLASH I CAN TELL YOU THIS THE PLANS AND THE CRAFT WERE SO CLOSE TO ME I COULD SEE THE OXEGAN TUBE HANGING OFF THE PILOTS HELMET ASS THEY FLEW BY SO AFTER ALL THAT I RUN TO THE FRONT OF THE HOUSE WHERE MY SISTER AND MY FRIEND ARE JUMPING OUT OF THE CAR AND MY SISTER SAYS TO ME..DID YOU F'N SEE THAT I JUST DROVE RIGHT UNDER IT!!!!! THEN MY FRIEND DAVE SAID WHAT THE HELL WAS THAT AND I SAID THAT WAS A UFO SPACECRAFT I CANT F'N BELIEVE IT!!!!! BUT MORE ODD THEN THAT IS NOW I RECALL I WAS WATCHING THE JEFFERSONS ON TV THAT DAY AND I RECALL IT TO BE LIKE 3PM WHEN THEY WERE ON IN 1981 DONT QUOTE ME ON THAT BUT AFTER MY SISTER SHOWED UP AND WE ALL SPOKE ABOUT IT LIKE I SAID I RECALL IT TO BE ALMOST DUSK SO THE POINT IM TRYING TO SAY I NOW THINK I HAVE A HUGE GAP OF MISSING TIME TAT WAS A SUMMER DAY AND NEAR DUSK WOULD NOT COME TILL AT LEAST 6;30 OR 7PM ON A SUMMER DAY STRANGLY ENOUGH I FELT NO FEAR AT ALL WHILE THIS ENCOUNTER TOOK PLACE BUT I CAN SAY THIS IT CHANGE MY LIFE FOR EVER I READ AND WATCH EVERYTHING I CAN NOW ON THE SUBJECT AND STILL HAVE CONTACT WITH MY FRIEND DAVE WHO SAW IT ALSO IDONT KNOW I NEEDED TO GET IT OFF MY CHEST MOST PEOPLE JUST THINK IM CRAZY WHEN I TELL THEM TAT STORY BUT I GET IT UNLESS YOU HAVE SEEN WHAT I'VE SEEN I GUSS I WOULD THINK THE SAME THING MAYBE YOU GUYS CAN HELP ME WITH THE MISSING TIME THING I WIIL EVEN TA A LIE DETCTOR TEST TO PROVE WHAT I SAW WAS REAL AND MINE WAS RIGHT THERE IN FRONT OF ME IN BROD DAYLIGHT PLEASE HELP I CAN DRAY WHAT I SAW AND I ALSO HAVE A PHOTO OF THE AREA AT THE RANCH WHERE IT WAS.
