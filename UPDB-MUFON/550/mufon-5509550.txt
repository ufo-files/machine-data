[flg] Object on fire in the sky, slowly moving took 3 minutes

My friend and I were coming out of my house. At first I thought I saw some orange fireworks going off in the sky so I thought nothing of it. We were talking outside for a minute then all the sudden this thing popped out of the trees. 

Both my friend and I were starring of course, wondering what it was. It took about 3 minutes for this object to go across the sky, it moved slow. My first thought of course, UFO, then I thought it was a meteor because this thing on fire. I could not see any shape or definition of any object, just a flaming thing moving across the sky. 

The flames were shooting upward. We could not tell it was on fire until it was at its closest distance from us. I thought it was a meteor until thing object started going upwards, slowly and also curving. 

This video I recorded is nothing compared to what I saw, I wish I would of caught better footage!
