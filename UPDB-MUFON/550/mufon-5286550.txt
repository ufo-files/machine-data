Oval / round multicolored changing lights in a semitransparent fog . Remainder of sky completely clear with stars visible

For some reason I woke up at 12:30 am and decided to take my new puppy out urinate. Before I could open his crate, I had the idea to go outside and look up. 
The sky was finally clear as it had been raining quite a lot lately. At about 70° South, I saw flashing colored object. It was stationary, and silent. I reviewed in my mind...not a plane, not a helicopter, ...The decision was made to wake up the wife, regardless of the penalty. I went to the bedroom and shook her until she got up and I quickly, pulled her towards the front door and grabbed my smart phone. I was quite concerned that the object would be gone. But, it wasn't. I asked her "do you see it?" She said yes, different colored lights?, yes. Do you hear anything? No, is it an airplane or helicopter? Definitely not. Here's the best part. After looking at it for several minutes my wife turned to me and said ""There's another one straight above us" She was right. Same exact object, same lights at 12:00 position. My wife completely underwhelmed, went back in the house to bed as I stared at the back of her head in disbelief.  I was able to take approx. 10 poor quality pics with my smart phone but all were blurry. I was able to verify the color changes. But it seems the object was changing shape while obscured in a white cloud or mist.
