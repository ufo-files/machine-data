{cak} Looked like another sun but smaller

I live in Irving tx. My husband wanted to go to the wind-star casino so we were driving down interstate 35 towards Oklahoma. It was a very bright and clear day. When we got to or around Gainesville I notice the sky was blue and the sun was bright but then noticed a bright light to the left of the sun and thought it was another sun or maybe a planet that was just visible that day. I even thought it was an airplane but it didn't do anything it didn't even move. I took a picture of it and went on about our business. A week or two later I was looking at my pictures that I have on my phone and saw that one picture and examined it. When I enlarged my picture I clearly saw that it was something else. It looks like a bright light but when you enlarge it you can notice at least four or five little lights inside it and last night when I turned on my I pad a UFO sighting page was already on my iPad so I clicked on it and something similar to What I saw and decided to report it.
