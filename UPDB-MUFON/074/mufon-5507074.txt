two star-like bright lights on an non-visible craft moving much faster than airplane zipping over mountain top

I came outside shortly after 9pm, think there was a weird sound, quickly scanned around. Bright lights immediately caught my eye. Two very bright lights in line with each other, with just a little space between them. They were somewhat low in the sky & close to the mountain ridge, about the height of an airplane flying quite low but moving very fast. I would say the lights were bigger than an airplane's would be & there weren't any accompanying red or green lights visible. I did not see any outline of the object itself, but feel as though the lights may've been more rectangular in shape rather than as star's appearance & my guess of the craft's shape would have to be saucer or disk-like. There was no sound as a helicopter or airplane would have, and it darted over the mountain southward very fast. I felt startled, excited, and a little silly. Also wondered if others had seen it.
