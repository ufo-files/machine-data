Solid Orange Color Triangle

Solid Orange colored Triangle that was lit up Orange was at about 5,000 feet elevation (guessing to be twice the size of a helicopter, hard to tell the size) was traveling about the speed a copter would fly maybe much slower, it was heading South South East and then about the time it got over my location it make a sharp turn to the left and headed into a North East Direction and then vanished in a blink of an eye. The direction it headed would of taken it over the Gulf of Mexico. Note also this object was silent.
