{cak} Saw orb hover ascend rush towards us stop change direction

My friend and I were sitting out on her front porch one summer night I believe it was 1981ish. It was maybe 10 or 10:30ish pm. I looked up to the end of the street and saw a bright round object coming across the front yard of the corner house across the street (two houses up from us). It went right up to the front of the house close to the bottom then raised up to the second floor. I nudged my friend to look up there. As soon as she looked up there this ball of light zoomed down the street (to the right) and stopped right in front of us (about 25 or less feet from us) and hovered a second or two then shot to the left and around to the backside of her house. My friend ran after it around her house but quickly came back after it disappeared. Never saw it again. It definitely had direction and speed changes and purpose in it's movements. Very cool!
