Light like objet moving in sky

One night in July 1995; I was sitting with my sister and 03 cousins on the roof of my apartment. The time was past midnight when I observed a light moving from right to left a some distance (approx. 2 miles) north west of my location. At first I thought it must be some plane or a helicopter. but then it repeated after 5 minutes; this time from left to right. then the object started to do the same without any turns. It was just a light and I could not hear any sound the usually aircraft has. After observing for around 45 minutes I told my sister to look at the object that. I said "what kind of aircraft is this; it just moves in zig zag pattern and no turns". it was at this time when my cousins and sister started observing the object and within 02 of that the object suddenly got frozen in the sky (from zig zag pattern) and started moving towards us. It was bright light moving towards us. I we could not see what is behind the light as it is coming towards us. There was no sound either. It was getting brighter and brighter as it was approaching us. when it reached about 100 feet above us my sister pulled us to go inside. we started to run away and about to go down the stairs when one of my cousins asked to take a look at the object again so we came back to our original position. What we saw; the object is returning back in the same path as it came i.e. with out turning and then disappeared at far distance. The approx. time when this all ended was about 3:00am.
