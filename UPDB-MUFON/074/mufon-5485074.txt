I went outside to have a smoke around 10:45 pm july 14 th 2014. Right away two lights high up in the night sky came into view. The two lights were flying side by side at like satellight hieght. They were going in NW direction and went to the darkest spot in sky and just totally blinked out from my sight just like that. Then another light came out from over our roof and went right to the exact same spot over our house and disappeared in the same spot. I saw a person on the news about five years ago who had a video of what I saw. I truely believe I did not see satellights. They do not come right over you and all three just disappear like this. I watch the skies closley and have seen thousands of satellights in the night sky. This is one strange event I saw.
