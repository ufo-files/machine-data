It happened really fast

August 23,at about 11:30 in the evening I was sitting outside and watching stars. I was looking to the North star,and suddenly,about South West from that star,something flashed REALLY HARD and it flashed my eyes. First,I thought its just a something flashing in my eyes,but then I looked to that star,and it flashed again like a camera flash. Any idea on you think it is? Maybe there was 2 meteors crashing to each other? And then,I looked above me,to the Milky Way core,and I saw something like a bullet or so.
