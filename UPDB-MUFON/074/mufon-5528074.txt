Orange flame looking thing flys slowly and silently about 4-5 miles north of clearwater beach then flies into the sky and disappears rapidly.

I was outside with my girlfriend having a cigarette and i noticed a strange dimmish orange light in the sky. it didn't strobe like an airplane, it looked more like a flame flickering, the color is what caught my eye. you normally don't see aircraft with lights that dim and when you see airplanes the lights are horizontal, but this thing was vertical. i kept my eye on it because it did not look like your usual aircraft that flies around here. the object was north of us traveling at medium speed west. then it stopped and then it started to rise up into the sky at about a 35-40 degree angle and disappear rapidly into the night sky. It didn't make any noise, totally silent. I've never seen anything do that before, it totally looked like it flew up into outer space.
