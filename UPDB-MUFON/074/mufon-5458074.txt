In 1968, my family was traveling to Ensenada Baja Mexico. We were traveling to Ensenada for the New Years celebration. By Las Pkayas we observed a UFO

We were traveling in two cars with a total of 12 people. My father, who has a PhD in aeronautical engineering pulled over and pointed out the UFO. We pulled over. He refused to put eyes in our head, but we quickly Leinster possibilities.

All 12 of us saw the same thing. We
Saw oval like a cigar shaped aircraft that was metallic with panels and lights. The object hovered and then zipped around over the ocean and zipped out of sight. 

The technology to do this was not possible at the time.
