Metallic Shiny Object in Nitro WV

My wife and I were setting at the Great Escape movie lot in Nitro, WV. We were watching the sun set and watching planes lay chemtrails in the sky. Then, a metallic oval/sphere shaped object appeared out of no where about as high as the planes were flying. The sun was extremely reflecting off the object. The planes around the object were reflecting as well but, no where near the intensity as the object. The object started fading in and out rapidly. (Note: the planes were leaving exhaust trails and chemtrails. The object did NOT.) Then the object disappeared into thin air.
