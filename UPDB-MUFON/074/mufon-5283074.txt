Daytime sighting of UFO/UAP five times of same object

I have observed a UFO/UAP five times during the day between October 2021 and January 2022 (26 October 2021 @12:35pm, 7 November 2021 @11:35am, 27 November 2021 @12:03pm, 30 November 2021 @4:37pm, and 10 January 2022 @12:15pm). The object was traveling east to west (estimated 100 degrees East to 290 Degrees West) on the first, second, third, and fifth sightings. The object was traveling west to east on the same trajectory during the fourth sighting. The object appears to disc like, silver/dull grey center, bright glowing light with transparent edges. The elevation/altitude varied each sighting and is estimated 2000-10,000 feet. The speed was similar each time and is estimated at 200-300 mph. There was no contrail, no sound, no wings, no paint/markings, and no FAA type lights for standard aircraft. I am not exactly certain of the size, but estimate that it is about the size of fighter jet. I was able to take two images during the third and fourth sightings with my cell phone (you will have to zoom and scroll to find it).
