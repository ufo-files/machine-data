We were at the beach waiting for the fireworks to begin when we saw this thing floating across the sky over Lake Erie. In the video footage it appears red but it was orange. We so many strange things out in that area that after a life time you get use to it. However, this time I had a camera with video on it.

It would actually disappear and then reappear and it just floated in a path. Then it disappeared and another one appeared.

I was excited because I was able to record it.  I did post on youtube and received many responses from it.
