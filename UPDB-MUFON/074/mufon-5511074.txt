{cak} observed 2 ufos in sky zig zagging like they were playing tag with each other

My mom was driving me to my baseball game, I was in the backseat. She pointed up to the sky and said "o my god, look up there! do you see that? I couldnt see what she was pointing to from the back seat so i immediatly jumped to the front to see. When i got to the front seat she stopped the car in the middle of the road. I observed 2 ufos a couple miles up in the sky and at first they were stationary. maybe a couple hundred feet apart from each other. Then they started to abruptly change direction like zig-zagging or playing tag with each other. The ufos moved faster than any helicopter, plane, jet, ext. Imagine playing a pin ball machine, with 2 balls at once, and the balls are the ufos, but on a much larger scale. Thats how these objects moved and zig-zagged back and forth with each other!!! I was young but im not dumb or blind. I have 20/20 vision and am very familiar with all the planes and jets ext in the sky. Ive been to about a dozen air shows and know what planes look like and how they manuver in the sky. I know what I saw and I know I seen 2 ufos at the same time. Also my mom feels the same way. I felt excited and nervous, at first, then a little fear, confusion, and wonder during the sighting. After the event happend I felt content cbecause I was always a skeptic but now Im a believer. After the objects zig-zagged with each other for approximatly 10 seconds, they simultainiously took off like a flash of light.
