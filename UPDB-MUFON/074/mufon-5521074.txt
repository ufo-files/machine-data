Light hovered in the same spot then just shut off

I came outside for a smoke break and noticed a light hovering in the same spot in the distance.(About Plane Height) After about 5 minutes the object hadnt moved at all and then it almost looked like it turned itself off or dimmed itself for about a second and disappeared. Ive experienced a few sightings in my life but the fact this was in such a largely populated area surprises me that nobody else had seen or reported this incident.
