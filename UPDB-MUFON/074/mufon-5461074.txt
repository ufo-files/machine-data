{jpg} Leapfrogging lights over beach

I was metal detecting on the beach and was leaving from one location to another down the beach to continue metal detecting. as I got in my car and drove I notice to my right hand side over the water there were three very bright Amber orange type lights. I pulled off at the next parking area and began to shoot video hoping it would show itself again. The cloud cover was partly cloudy but behind one of the clouds I could see an outline of a triangular object. It was blocking the stars behind it as it moved. I got my video camera out and started taping and it only showed itself one more time but briefly. The third time it did this is when I caught it on tape and it was much larger of a distance away than the first sighting while I was driving. My Moto G6 is not known for taking quality videos at night but if you slow the film down you can actually see that there are two lights and then one dissipated. Only to have it show up in front of the other two. These lights only stayed illuminated for just a couple seconds. I don't think they make flares that do this. I did not see any helicopters or hear any jets in the area until about 3 minutes after my sighting. Being almost 10:00 at night it was very dark I could not see jets but I could hear them. I did also see three military helicopters headed in the direction of the last sighting that I recorded.
