{cak} observed triangle shaped UFO hovering in field 15-20 ft off the ground-no sound-no light movement -non threatening

In the late 80's I was driving to a friends house east on state road 42. I saw an object off in the distance to the southeast-noted that it was unusual and it seemed to be watching me. It stayed with me at a distance until I turned north into the Reelsville area than I lost sight of it.(I am not good with directions but trying-sorry)Anyway, I left my friends house and started driving back west on st rd 42. I turned right -southwest towards Cataract Rd where we lived at the time. The road went downhill and then into an 'S' shaped bend in the road with fields on each side. To my right I could see the triangle shape vehicle just hovering above ground maybe 15-20 off the ground. It could've been been a little higher but not much. I could have easily driven right underneath it. It was a black triangle shape with three light running down each side on the bottom. The top may have been slightly illuminated but I am not for certain. It made no sound. I was absolutely not afraid at all. I made a mental note that I saw it and moved on. I never discussed it with my family at that time. There was always something odd happening in that area and that was just one more thing. Years later I mentioned it to my sister. She confessed that she had seen it hovering over our house that night. She told me there were others in the area that had seen it as well. I haven't seen anything like it since that time.
