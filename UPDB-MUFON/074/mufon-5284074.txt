Large object with three lights in triangle configuration.

I with my wife and kids saw this object and all three said it was way bigger than any plane that would have landed at our local airport, because the biggest plane to land there is a Cessna. I am a skeptic and also a prior law enforcement officer and still have no idea what it was I saw that night. We were traveling on south marquis hwy to south fifth street we approached the stoplight from south marquis and above the tree line we saw three lights in a triangular shape. The object we saw traveled from our right to our left we face a western direction at the stoplight. We then made a left hand turn and the object should have been on our right or crossed the highway ahead of us but it never did and then it was on our left traveling in a eastern direction. Then it went out of sight. We were unable to get photos due to glares on our windows and the light pollution. I wish to remain anonymous. Also spoke to a lady from here a few days ago.
