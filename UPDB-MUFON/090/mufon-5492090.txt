I was 16 years old. My boyfriend and I were were at the park. About 100 feet away I saw a person. But it wasnt a person exacly. It was made of what looked like static. All different colors. It was standing in one place but it's body was moving around abnormaly fast. It's arms and it's legs. Nobody can move that fast. I didn't say anything I just watched it, I was petrified. And I though my eyes were playing tricks on me. My boyfriend said, do you see that? it looks like a static man and it looks like it's dancing.I said omg YES. He confermed what I saw, I knew it was real. He said, do you wanna leave? I said yes. I think we left. I thought I saw a ghost and forgot about it. It was years later that I came across The book (The communion). At my mother's garage sale. And what I read in that book was exacly what I saw. There is a lot more to tell.
