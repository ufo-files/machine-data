Triangular UFO, 2 white/1 green light

I'm eastern time so it was just about the end of dusk, I was outside smoking a cigarette looking up at the stars/moon as I usually do whilst smoking.I saw this object flying really low in the sky (750-1250 feet) and making no noise whatsoever. No jet engines and I think I would have even been able to hear a propeller if it was a small aircraft. Anyway, it looked sort of triangular in shape with three lights on it, two where white and one was green. Hard to judge its size but I would estimate it was about 60x80 yards. I saw it fly behind two symmetrical buildings, so my view was obscured for a few seconds, then it passed by the two buildings. I looked away for a few seconds, then saw it fly by the buildings in the same area and direction I had seen it fly not 2 seconds ago. I don't know of any human made craft that could change direction like that. I got this sense like "that's not human" when I saw it, this gut reaction. I know it won't mean much here but my intuition is usually quite sharp. After I had this feeling like I saw something unusual and somewhat exciting.
