Staircase-like ufo seen followed by two more instances of shining objects

We were on the beach and my partner was fishing. He told us he saw a fireball in the sky and continued to fish. About ten minutes later I saw a sequence of red orange lights directly in front of me that looked like a staircase. One bright red orange light would show up and then another would immediately glow a bit above it and to the left. In total we all saw about six lights. About ten minutes later we saw an even more astounding sight. It was the same red glowing lights that appeared to be the bottom part of a circle. Directly to the left of these we saw the same red glowing light appear in a horizontal line one after the other. We were screaming at each other and could not believe what we saw. We called our friends to come down but we never saw it again. We stayed on the beach for about two hours after this looking up at the sky.
