Strange Lights In Tupelo

While we were walking our dog outside the apartments I noticed a sphere like object to the north of Tupelo traveling at a high rate of speed followed by some sort of aircraft with blinking lights trying to follow the object. I followed it across the sky for about 10-15 sec. and then it was gone. Also noticed two weeks before a strange object in the sky to the west of Tupelo.
