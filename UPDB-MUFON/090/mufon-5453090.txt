I seen a square shaped craft with light yellow lights glowing on each corner but they were glitching as if a cloak was malfunctioning.

I was driving, headed South about 15mph. I heard, what sounded like an airplane above me. I have heard this a million times before as the SLC Airport is very close to where I was & I see planes coming in every day & night. They fly North to South & vice versa. 
So, I looked up expecting to see an airplane but, instead I seen a square shape craft flying slowly, South West. The craft it's self was not visable. However, I know it was square due to the placement of the lights. There were 4 dimly lit lights on each corner. The lights looked as if they were glitching, maybe it's cloak was malfunctioning. That's the best way I can describe what I seen. The craft stayed on it's flight path & continued flying West. I had to make a right hand turn (East) & after I made the turn, I looked back up & did not see the object.
