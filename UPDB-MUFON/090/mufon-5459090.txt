{rjl} Warm summer night, window was open. I woke up and saw two ufos following the ct river just above tree level. Blinking lights with no sound.

I lived on a small farm in Northfield farms, in Northfield MA. I could see the CT river from my bedroom window it was only a field away. My family farm was one of a few in the area. It was a warm summer night with windows opened, I woke up looking out my bedroom window and watched two round saucer shaped objects dark in color with blinking lights that went around it. There was no sound and that really confused me. They were following the CT river, just above the tree line. I was 14, I told my mother at the time of this sighting.
