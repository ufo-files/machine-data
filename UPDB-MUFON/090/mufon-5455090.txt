{tjd} A train of lights in the sky

Now there is a chance what I happened to see was the new Star-link satellites from Space-x but I'm not for sure cause I have seen footage of those satellites and even though it looked similar there were some big differences, such as they were way more spread out, and I only saw about 38 of em and 2 of these lights were moving in the opposite direction. Now let me first state that this was exactly at 5:45 on the morning of the 1st of March, and it was perfectly clear night to spot the international space station which is what I was originally looking for. 
While I was honestly disappointed for missing the space station I spotted an meteor which drew my eye sight to the southern part of the sky, this is when I first spotted the first light moving faster than any plane in the sky and glowing or reflecting a white light in the south eastern part of the sky. Then two more on the same path as the first then another and another, then three in a triangle eventually totaling to 38 lights all in a row along the same path. Then after the last light I saw one more in the opposite direction almost on the same path but a little more higher in the sky and then another even seemed to change direction all the sudden, it was flying on the same path as same train of lights but then changed direction which I know of no satellites that can do that, this all left me speechless.
After looking online to see if anyone else had seen the same thing I saw, I found out about the Star Link Satellites, another guy in New Jersey saw the same thing at the exact time and he had no idea what he saw either.
I am a huge star gazer and I'm always looking up to the sky at night, and have never seen what I saw last night before, it was amazing, I feel there should be a record of what I saw just in case it wasn't the Star Link Satellites, that's why I'm writing you now, and I have previously one other sighting turned in to you guys at Mufon which I was contacted and we discussed the details of that sighting but this sighting was much different. Oh and one last detail some of these lights where right on top of each other and others were 2x2 and a few were in a triangle formation and others where all alone in the trail of lights just to clarify. 
Thank you and I will always be looking up at night from here hopefully next time I can get something on film.
