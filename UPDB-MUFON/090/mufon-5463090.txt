{rjl} Reviewing drone video when fast moving object was noticed in video

10:45am, October 15, 2018 I was flying a drone in the Lake Rim Park, Fayetteville NC taking aerial photos and video. I did not notice the fast moving object in the video until 3 months later while reviewing some of the video. 

I have attached 8 seconds of video, the fast moving object shows up in the video at the 6 second mark traveling from left to right from the 2 O clock positing of the frame and exiting at about the 8 O clock position. The object is small and hard to see. The video was shoot at 1080p 30fps. I can not tell how far the object was away form the drone but the drone was at a higher elevation about 170ft agl.

I feel a bit excited because I don't think this was a bird or some type of insect.
