I was on the balcony of my flat talking to my father when I turned my head to look over the balcony and I saw at 100 meters a black flying object emitting 3 lights. It was red, blue and white.This object had not propellers or thrusters.
I got up from the chair and started moving my arms so it could see me and then It turned off the lights. I took out the phone to record it but I didn't get to hit the record button because the object didn't look. It looked all dark. It started to approach and before it passed over the building I took my head over the railing of the balcony to look at the base of the object. A yellow X was illuminated at the base. A week later I started to see blurring in my right eye and I went to the doctor and he diagnosed cataracts in both eyes.
