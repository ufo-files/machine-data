{cak} Blinding light hit face & a buzzing sound.

It was in the 70's,I'm guessing of the exact date and time, I was bush hogging on the farm at [location removed] It was after midnight we mowed after dark in the cool part of the day, I was riding an M Farm-all which is very loud when all a once I heard a buzzing sound. A craft came out of the woods from behind a ridge and a bright light hit me square in the face.

It surprised me, I mashed the clutch and brake because I could not see, it took several minutes for me to regain my composure and be able to once again see to operate the tractor.

By the time I was able to see the object was out of sight.

Could it have been a plane? I can not say, the only light I recall is the light that hit me in the face.

I have been wondering about this incident since it occurred.
