Light beam

Traveling to Colorado from California for an annual hunting trip. Observed horizontal light beam in the sky shooting a beam of light horizontal. The beam stopped in mid-air (no aircraft of any kind in the air). Light curtain from beam descended to ground-disturbing dirt, dust. After observing for several minutes light beam retracted to beam and abruptly disappeared as the same as it was first observed.
