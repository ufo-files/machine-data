Was at a graveyard with some family and was filming the beautiful sky. I don't remember seeing anything unusual until I reviewed the video about a month later and was starting and stopping the video and captured the pictures of what appears to be something odd. Thought it was a bird in the video at first until I zoomed in. Also, a bird could not have traveled as fast as this thing did. In the video you can hear a clicking sort of sound when it goes by me and I have heard this same clicking sound at other times in other videos.
