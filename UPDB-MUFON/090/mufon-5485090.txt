A SLOW MOVING LARGE OBJECT, DARK MOVING SLOWLY AND SILENTLY

I was working as a night watchman in central scotland, near the Ochil hills,and i looked up and seen a massive ship, rectangular in appearance, it was pure black or dark if you hadnt looked at it more than once you would have thought it was a cloud until you looked at it more closely, you could see windows, docking stations. it was massive but moved very slowly without any sound. as i said it could have passed as a cloud if you never looked at it more closely. I watched it fly right over me towards the Ochil Hills n/e and went right over them very slowly. it did not change direction or speed and drifted silently over the hills. it had windows and bay doors but they were kind of blurry hence saying it could have been mistaken for being a funny looking cloud.
