Extreme bright green light that changed to red as an aeroplane approached

I saw a hovering green light from the balcony of my house. I often see aeroplanes from here but thought that this light was unusual. I looked around for stars and could see none. So I strained my eye to get a better look and it was actually a row of green lights. I ran inside got my phone camera and called my nephew. I have the thing on video although it looks more like a dot in the video! 
The object hovered there for several minutes until a commercial jet approached it when it seemed to change its light to red.
As soon as the commercial airliner passed it, it turned its light back to green and after a few seconds started to descend
