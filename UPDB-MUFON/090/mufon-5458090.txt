{cak} Bright white light dimmed to small red light then dissapeared

I was in the park with my dogs and looking up at the stars. I noticed what seemed to be a satellite to the West up about 70-80 degrees. It was too bright and large to be a satellite. I thought maybe it was the space station. As I stared at it moving from west to east it grew larger and closer and somehow was soon at about 30 degrees off the horizon. Flying past me from west to east the light remained solid and bright.    Then the light dimmed slowly and turned into a small red light for about 4 seconds then dissapeared all together. The light was silent and moved at a constant speed slightly slower than an airplane.
