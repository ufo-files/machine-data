Flickering, erratic, "star-like" object.

Me and a group of about 5 friends witnessed a flickering star like object in the southern sky. It moved to the west and passed out of our view. It returned about thirty seconds later still flickering and flying in a very erratic pattern. We tried to pass it off as a loose kite, or a high flying bird. It began flying towards us then and we realized that it was very high up in the sky as it flew behind a cloud never to be seen again.
