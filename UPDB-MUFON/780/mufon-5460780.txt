{rjl} Witnessed several orange orbs

I was driving to my bosses house to drop off a set of keys when a orange orb flew over my car. I immediately pulled over to the side got out of the car and looked up to see several orange orbs the size of cantaloupes. They were 5-10 feet above me. They seemed to have “heart beats”and would control their brightness. I was trying to see if they were solid but they weren’t which was odd because they were definitely intelligent .They were completely silent and seemed to have their own “personalities.” Some stood still while others wizzed by playfully . When I would stare at one it would “blink” , I guess letting me know it sees me. I was not scared I was actually euphoric and very excited to be witness to this. They seemed friendly to me. I watched them for about 3-5 minutes until they slowly flew away and each one disappeared. I was amazed and even stopped at a church that was close by to ask if anyone had seen these things. They said no.
