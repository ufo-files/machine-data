{cak} Spotted ascending from trees, SE from house 125 degrees.

Glowing object appeared as ball of fire at first, thought it was a firework dud on horizon, but came from an area where fireworks werent present. Then the object instead of falling like a dud firecracker, started to elevate. I used a pair of Barska binoculars (10x50WA) used for aircraft spotting, and have a description of a glowing pulsating craft. It has a sweptback boomerang wing, a stubby triangular top section, but then the fuselage is like the bottom keel on a sail boat; it streches down and is a right triangle in appearance. It glows yellow -orange, and it did a bank right toward the east above the protected wetlands area, heading near US-2. 

This was around 9:57pm PST, South east in direction, heading east toward US-2. It hovered and course corrected 2 mins into the 4 min flight, until I could no longer see it past trees.
