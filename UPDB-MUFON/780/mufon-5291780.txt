Non Moving Blinking light in night sky

I stepped outside just after 0600 to relieve some pressure. I look up during this time. Always. I didn't see anything. I looked up a minute later after I had finished, and saw a light appear in the form of a blink. It then blinked again and again, I think 4, maybe 5 times. The blink was very quick. Illuminated just for a very short moment. Then went dark for maybe a second, but probably not two seconds. Repeated this pattern 4, maybe 5 times (I think). Kind of an interesting looking flash of light. Round in shape. Very, very far overhead. Far enough away it appeared just larger than a star in comparison and flashed bright in the dark night. No movement. Entire sighting duration was less than 10 seconds. Interesting observation.
