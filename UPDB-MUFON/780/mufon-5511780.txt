{dr}My friend and I were standing by his house when a brilliant round object came over at a very high rate of speed. it never changed altitude

Me and my friend were coming home from slot car racing about 9pm on our bikes. We stopped at his house and all of a sudden a bright round obect flew over our heads towards San Fransisco. It went from horizon to horizon in a few seconds. it looked like about the size of a full moon. made no sound, and never lost altitude. It was faster than anything made then or since. We didn't know what it was. Never seen anything like it. It just went straight across the sky.We were dumb founded. Both just said what the the heck was that?!It just kept going until it was out of sight..This was in 1964
