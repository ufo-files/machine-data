Six or seven purple lights

I was on my way to a church sponsered seminary group in the early morning. Iwas driving in my car to pick up my friend, and in the sky I saw multiple purplelights. They were there briefly and then left. I was listening to the radio,and somebody called into the station and stated that they too had seen thestrange purple lights in the sky. Me and a guy in our car pool that I hadalready picked up, thought the incident very strange.
