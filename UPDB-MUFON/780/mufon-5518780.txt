Star like object that vanished instantly

Saw something strange in the sky on night of May 16th around 10:30pm in Preble County Ohio. Noticed sky was really clear & lots of stars out, then saw that one was moving. Thought at first it was probably a high plane, figured just high enough that I couldnt see any blinking lights. Then I noticed this white light was getting brighter as if coming my direction. Suddenly the speed increased, then instantly it vanished. There were no clouds at all for it to hide behind, it just seemed to disappear instantly.
