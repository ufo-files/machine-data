Witnessed a

Returned to friend's home from dinner at a local Mexican eatery, and was excited to light off fireworks for the upcoming holiday. Friend's mother noticed the stationary lights that were hovering over the 40 acre field adjacent to their house as we were cresting the hill that nears their driveway. Four bright lights would start on the corners of a large theoretical square approximately 500ft apart from each other, they would then simultaneously move to the center of this square, get brighter, then disappear, only to reappear at the corners of the "object" several seconds later. Absolutely no sound or engine noise , and there was nothing visible that these lights were connected to. There were four people not including myself that witnessed this event as well. All of us pretty much came to the understanding in a few minutes that these lights did not belong to any sort of aircraft or other craft. The sheer size and lack of noise made it clear that this wasn't anything we had witnessed before.
