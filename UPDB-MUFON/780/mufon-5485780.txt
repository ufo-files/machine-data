6 Red pulsating lights in Sky moving in formation, then changing to single file over city of Edmonton, Alberta.

I left my place of business with my 2 daughters walking towards our house at approximately 8:10pm. It was dark out, no clouds in the sky, I looked up and seen 5 glowing red circular lights moving in a rectangular formation. Each object was pulsating red. They were moving from the Southwest to the Northwest of my location. Suddenly a 6th craft came up behind the other five to join them. I then noticed a Jet airliner coming up behind them, which had left the Edmonton International Airport, and they suddenly went out of formation into a single file, and streaked of to the North, out of sight. They made no noise, whereas the Jet airliner could be heard.
