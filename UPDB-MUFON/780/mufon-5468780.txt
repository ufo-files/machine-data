Star morphed into diamond shaped object

I had my camera (Lumix fz80) on the tripod set up for me to try out the app that would enable me to remotely control the camera. Camera was focused on the night sky through the window, while I was in another room viewing what the camera could see on my ipad. Tonight I finally got around to actually seeing the complete video because I remembered that I didn1t view the whole time video was recording. 
I took a few screenshots of the video and it was then that I noticed the object changing from star like, to morphing into a diamond shaped, glowing object.
