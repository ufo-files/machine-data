I watched this for over an hour while taking out my dog. It seemed to slowly move closer to me than peel off to my right. I thought it was a star/planet at first, but it seemed too bright. After a while it seemed to move closer and change to a bright white flickering, so then I thought it was a plane. After 20 minutes or so It seemed to change its position in the sky and than sat again. The lights were flickering green, red and a bright white pulse, and it sat there for well over 30 minutes. I heard no noise, it was very quiet and clear in the sky. I took numerous pics as it moved across and tried to get a decent video. I only regret I didn’t run in and wake my wife but she would’ve probably killed me.
