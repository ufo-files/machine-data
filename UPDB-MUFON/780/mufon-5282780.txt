Small orbs of light flying over apartment building

Last night around 7:00pm I saw a single white orb flying much faster than a plane would (living near CVG I see a lot of planes arriving and departing). It was heading north with slight deviations in flight path. It was followed by another with a similar altitude and direction. I watching the sky and a few more would fly by with a similar altitude and direction. All captured on video. I went inside for a few minutes and went back out to check if there were more. That's when I saw over 50 lights flying low (50ft-500ft). They were silent and maintained the same white light. This is what is shown in the video. I have 5 or 6 clips that show the individual objects as well.
