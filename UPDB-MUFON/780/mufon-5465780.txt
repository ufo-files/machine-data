{rjl} Strange hovering ball of light with a tail below it

I was walking home with my husband after doing laundry and at 11:04 AM I looked up and saw a weird round glowing ball with a tail on it. My husband figured it was a weather balloon, but I figured that was a weird area for it, and zooming in on the object seemed to be made of light. I watched it for a few minutes, high in the sky, and it did not seem to move, at all. A man with a stroller saw me look up, and he looked as well. I asked him what he thinks it is, and he shrugged and walked away.

i went inside, and came back out about a half hour later and it was still there, but had shifted to the south just a small bit. Again, the object seemed very steady, as did the tail beneath it.

I felt very curious looking at it, and my phone camera did not seem to help me figure it out.
When we came back later in the afternoon, it was gone.
