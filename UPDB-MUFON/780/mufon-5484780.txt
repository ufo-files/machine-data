{nd} BRIGHT LITES AN MEMORY AND TIME LOST

BACK IN 1974 I WAS ON MY WAY HOME FROM WORK AROUND 6:20 A.M. I WAS DRIVING FROM LAGUNA HILLS TO MY HOME AND I WAS ABOUT 5 MILES FROM MY OFFRAMP TO HIGHWAY 39, BEACH BLVD. WHEN ALL OF A SUDDEN THERE WAS BRIGHT LIGHT AND MY CAR DIED. I COASTED TO THE RIGHT SIDE OF THE ROAD. I DON`T WHAT HAPPENED NEXT BECAUSE I BLACKED OUT. THE NEXT THING I REMEMBERED WAS I WAS A WHOLE 7 MILES BEYOND HIGHWAY 39 EXIT FACING THE OPPOSITE WAY. MY CAR STARTED WITH NO PROBLEM. WHEN I GOT BACK HOME I FOUND OUT I THAT HAD LOST A LITTLE OVER 30 MINUTES OF TIME. ALL I CAN SAY IS THAT FOR SOME REASON I HAVE HAD A PICTURE IN MY MIND OF A MOTOR THAT NEEDS NO FOSSIL FUELS. TO THIS VERY DAY, THERE ISN`T A DAY GOES BY THAN I STILL REMEMBER THIS MOTOR FOR SOME REASON. I AM NOW 69 YEARS OLD NOW AND I FEEL THE NEEDED TO GET THIS OFF MY CHEST.
