We were driving down Ansburogh Ave. toward university Ave. When I saw bright white lights low altitude. It was floating/ flying to the right for a second. Then stopped for a second and started going to the left. As it went almost overhead I could see it was triangle shaped. Tip was pointed left and the back side may have been wider than the sides. Also I saw 3 white lights on the back side/ bottom one at tip/bottom there was a red light in the middle. As it headed left over houses we tried to follow on the roads it seemed to speed up because after maybe 10 mins I could only see blinking white lights.
