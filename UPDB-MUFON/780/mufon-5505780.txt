It was just amazing. I was so excited that I just kept starring at it for almost 2 minutes.

Last night I.e. 30/12/2013 at 10:35 p.m. before going to sleep I opened the west side window of my bedroom and noticed a bright red light. It was at a distance of over 2 Kms from my house.

Initially i thought it was a lantern kite, but noticed that it was moving towards me at a considerable speed. It was hovering for a while and then speeding and again hovering.
I immediately called my wife and nephew to watch this spectacle.

Almost after a minute, we noticed that it was neither a lantern kite nor an helicopter, cause there was no sound. When it was almost at a distance of 500 meters from my house the light was getting brighter. As it was night time we couldnt see the object nor any emission as the light was very powerful. It appeared as if someone was focusing a torch right into my eyes.

As it was approaching our apartment we immediately rushed to the terrace to have a better view but were disheartened, as it had disappeared. We kept watching the sky for almost 5 minutes but couldnt find it.

Immediately I searched the net for similar sighting and saw a video on you tube similar to the one we experienced.
It was a wonderful experience...
