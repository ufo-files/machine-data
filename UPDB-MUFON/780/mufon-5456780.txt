{rjl} Blinding flash of light lit up the ground, then UFO was spotted

My friend and I were walking into the house from my car when a brilliant flash of light lit up the entire yard and my neighbor's yards from above. I looked up to see what it was and there was a disc-shaped silver colored UFO hovering in the sky above. It was very large and there was a single white light on the bottom. There was no sound. My friend and I both saw it - the object hovered there for approximately two minutes then shot straight up into the sky and it was out of sight in less than a second. I'd like to know if anyone else saw this. We didn't think to get our cameras out at the time.
