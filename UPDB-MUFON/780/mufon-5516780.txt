{dr}THERE WERE MULTIPLE RED AND ORANGE LIGHTS IN THE SKY.

I WAS WORKING AT SMITHHAVEN CHRYSLER JEEP DODGE LAST NIGHT JULY 31, 2012 AND AS I WAS WALKING OUTSIDE I HERD 5 OF MY COWORKERS SAYING LOOK IN THE SKY SO I LOOKED UP AND THATS WHEN I SAW THESE STRANGE LIGHTS. IT ALMOST LOOKED LIKE A HOT AIR BALLOON ON FIRE THAT IS THE ONLY WAY I CAN THINK TO DESCRIBE IT. 3 WEEKS BEFORE THIS ONE OF MY FRIENDS CLAIMS TO HAVE SEEN THE SAME EXACT THING OVER HIS HOUSE IN EAST ISLIP. IT WAS BY FAR AND AWAY ONE OF THE WIERDEST THINGS I HAVE EVER SEE. AS IT WAS SLOWLY MOVING ACROSS THE SKY IT JUST DISSAPEARED. IT WAS SO COOL!!!
