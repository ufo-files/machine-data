{jpg} Saw UFO at time of Vandenberg launch in sky near launch site. 2nd sighting in 2 years of same event.

I was watching for the scheduled Vandenberg missle launch, and my eye was distracted toward the west about 60 degrees above the horizon. I saw an orange-red bright orb. It then emitted a smaller orange orb from it, and the smaller orb went toward the west and disappeared. After the launch of the Vandenberg missle was complete, the bright orange orb suddenly grew much brighter, then moved toward the west and quickly disappeared before reaching any horizon. This is the second time I have encountered these orange orbs at Vandenberg launches. The first one I saw was about 2 years ago, but I didn't report it, because I didn't trust what I had seen. No planes are supposed to fly around the time of scheduled launches, so I know it wasn't a plane each time. Both times, the larger orb suddenly blinked out and disappeared right before my eyes as I was watching it on perfectly clear nights. Both times the larger orb launched a smaller orb from it and sent it away from it. I haven't even told my family for fear of being ridiculed.
