Matte black chevron shape 5 dull lights on bottom

Huge Matte black chevron shaped craft. Appeared slightly Bigger than a 737. Had 5 circular “lights” on the bottom that were dimly illuminated with yellowish/orange light. Two lights on the bottom of each “wing” and one on the bottom of the “point” of the chevron. Craft traveled from s/w to n/w at a slow speed over populated are below fog level. No sound, no obvious propulsion system. Flying way too slow to create any lift like conventional aircraft. Watched it for around 20-30 seconds until it was out of view.. Observed by another subject who was with me. Attached artist rendering is pic from internet that is closest representation of what I saw.
