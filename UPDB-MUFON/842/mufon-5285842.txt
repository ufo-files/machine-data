Small black triangular craft low altitude moving slowly south

This single craft about the size of a small fighter jet. It was a triangular wedge in shape. Non reflective black in color. It made no sound and had no lights. It was moving to slow to be a wing based flight craft in a southern direction approximately 500 feet to the west of the road. I was headed North from Roswell, NM. It was approximately 100ft above the ground. Time was local around 0430, before any light from sunrise. The date im reporting is estimated but there is a method to better guess at the date. I did make a sketch in a sketch book but it seems to have been misplace as of this reporting. I will continue to look for it. I looked for a potential craft that matched this but found none based in the capabilities I witnessed. It wasn't until I saw a YouTube video randomly about the TR3B Black Manta that a craft could have possibly match the capabilities. The video was not published until Aug 23 2021. I do have several other incidents I have witnessed and could further enter those as well. One of them in here in Roswell NM.
