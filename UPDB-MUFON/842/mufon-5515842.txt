{dr}Glowing fireball moving horizontally south - southwest leaving no trail

On saturday I ame out of the house to enjoy the night air. As it was almost 9 PM the sky was predominantely dark with the night sky lit up with stars and identifiable known planets.

As I looked east I noticed an excessively large star (brighter than the ISS when it flies over head) moving approxiamtely S/SW horizontally with no deviation in altitude. I use the term fireball as the colors eminating from it were of a redish orange fluctuations, almost as if it were on fire, but not trail of any kind to indicate pieces falling off. As time went on its speed, which wasnt fast, began to decrease, changing direct to almost due west, then ceasing movement all together. At one point it almost appeared to drop, not fast, but movement didn't last long.

As it slowed down I went into the house looking for a camera and when I came out the object was gone. I walked out into the middle of my court which would give me a commanding view of the sky and there was nothing there. No glowing object, no planes, no satellites. Just stars.
