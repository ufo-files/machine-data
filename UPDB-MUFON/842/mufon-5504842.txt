{cak} Dark object caught my eye moving in and out of low thunder storm cloud. Miami beach area 1999.

Hello every one. I have two wear sightings that I can not describe. This first sighting I was working at lagouerce country club golf course in Miami beach. In the summer months as a golf cart maintenance man. One day a big summer dark storm cloud caught my eyes over the ocean. East of were I was standing. This storm cloud that was approaching seem like it was so low that it look like it was sucking the ocean dried. Very little lighting notice in the dark cloud. Looking at the cloud a cigar shape dark thing or object was moving very rapidly in and out of the cloud. It will go in and in about 20 seconds will come out. No noise or lights noticed or heard. At first I thought it was a big bird like a voulcher or bigger.. But it was moving so strange in and out of the cloud in a horizontal motion for over ten minutes that I cut not pin point what it was. I showed to one of the guys that was working there and he could not believed his eyes. We tried to discover what it was but we have never seen nothing like it. Eventually the cloud arrived with the rain and that was the last I saw of it. Today I still don't know what that thing or object cut it been. But it was no a bird or airplane that I have ever seen. I figure I share with others. And ad at a little grain of sand to the idea that we are not along in the universe.
