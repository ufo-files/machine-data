recorded 3 objects with my VHS camera while in my backyard

In backyard getting ready to cook on the grill. Objects apeared out of nowhere above the rooftops/treetops of the homes east of my backyard. So as not to scare my children I said they might be helicopters although none of the objects had tails or rotating blades like helicopters and also made no noise. they looked like flying cars. they flew east to north and back again independently of each other several times and at one time one crossed over just below the other. after approximately 3 mins. they were out of my sight. all three looked to be the exact same type/shape of object. I submitted this report once before shortly after I recorded it in 1992 or 1993 and 2 of your people came to my house to further investigate. I heard no followup for a very long time then sometime between 2012 and 2014 you reached out to me by US mail but I had moved twice since I took this video and I could not locate the original tape so I did not respond. I recently found the original tape so I decided to record it on my phone so I could send it to you.
