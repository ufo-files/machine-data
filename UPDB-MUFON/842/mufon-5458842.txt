{cak} Two moving starts after pointing at them with laser..then military jet flew over us

My neighbor just bought a new side by side (a ATV with two seats) and he stopped over to show me, and give me a ride. We live next to a state forest with some ATV trails. I figured since the sky was so clear I’d grab my laser and point out some stars later. We took off around 10 or 11 pm and ended up down a logging road in CC Andrews state forest. At the end of the road there is a clearing loggers use to load trucks. It hasn’t been logged for a few years so now it just serves its purpose as a spot to stop and have a beer. So as we stopped, I began talking about how nice his machine was, I pulled out the green laser I brought. I shined it up toward the sky and pointed it at a star. My friend was blown away that you can see the “beam” from the laser so well. As I was pointing it around we noticed what we thought was a satellite, I pointed the laser at it...and it changed positions. We both started freaking out. After a few seconds of it moving around a second light appeared just to the right of it. My friend as this point is like “ give me that thing! What the hell that”! So I hand him the laser and as he points it up at one of the satellite looking lights a military jet flies right over our heads. Way lower then I have ever seen one fly. So we put the laser down, got spooked and headed home. Now I can’t stop looking at the sky. We still talk about what the moving lights could have been and why the military jet flew over us so fast.
