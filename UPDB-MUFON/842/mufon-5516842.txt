I was sitting in my yard talking to my step mom and looked up in the sky and saw 2 cigar shaped crafts,no wings at all,no sound ,I would say they were flying in a straight line about 800 to 1000 feet high headed in a south southwestern direction. I told my step mom what I was looking at,within 2-3 minutes later another one flying in the same direction but it was farther from me than the first 2 were. and a plane was also flying in the same direction came over about the time the last one flew over,I could see the wings on the plane and I could hear it. There was no sound from any of the three cigar shaped objects. I was sitting facing the south the time was about 7:12-7:15 pm. I looked on my phone when I started talking to my step mom and it was 7:09,we talked for 12 minutes and 30 seconds. As low as they were flying the sound of aircraft engines would have been very loud. I have no doubt they were ufos as i watch them every night but I cannot get anyone to come out and let me show them what I see. Every time I talk to someone from MUFON they don't have time to come out or some other excuse.
If I had thought I would have told my step mom to let me call her back and used my cell phone video to record them but I didn't think about it till after I finished talking to her.
