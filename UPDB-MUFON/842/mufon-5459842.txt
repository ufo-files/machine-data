{tjd} I saw small faint objects high in the sky go from left to right and then shoot off to the right really fast over and over.

I saw small star like faint objects flying around. Most of them would appear in a particular area of the sky and then fly to the right and then shoot off further right really fast. Occasionally I would see a small flash of light come from them. I watched at least 10 objects all traveling roughly the same path before coming inside. I can't be exactly sure because they were faint, but it seemed to me that they were gathering together at the point when the flying fast to the right stopped. No noise, I did see an airplane when I first started watching, but it wasn't there long.
