{jpg} Huge ball of light planet like or star like?

Large ball of bright white light passed just below of not in front of the bottom part of the moon at 5:30ish AM on March 4,2016 on my way home from delivering news papers and from Michigan looking south at the moon it passed from southeast to very low to southwest then vanished. At first I thought it was a planet since they have been close to the moon recently but then I noticed it kept moving so I told my husband to look and we both watched it descend at an angle and then disappear. We both agree it was not a plane or helicopter as it was much to close to the moon and how it just "vanished". It did not leave a visible trail to the naked it. We are both stumped as to what it could have been. At first I thought it might be the asteroid 2013 TX68 but that is not supposed to happen til tomorrow. March 5, 2016. I was thinking maybe small fragments before the bigger asteroid but whatever this was it was very large and very bright.... Unfortunately it happened to fast to get out my phone to get a picture of while I was driving.
