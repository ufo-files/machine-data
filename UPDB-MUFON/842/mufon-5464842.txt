Bright light in sky

I was in the kitchen when I looked out window to see a bright light in the sky at around 12.30am. From my kitchen window it looks stationary because of the distance but when I videoed it on my phone and looked through a telescope it is moving around. I don't believe that it is a plane or helicopter as it stayed in the sky for a few hours and was totally white. I also videoed and took photo of the object.
