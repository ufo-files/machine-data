{rjl} Orb. Morphing. Flashing colors

Been seeing this/these objects every night for about a week and a half. Looks like the brightest star in the sky at first, but quickly realized that it is in our atmosphere. Intrigued I zoomed in with my cell phone and was amazed. The objects have a spherical shape but it morphs like it’s not solid (coagulation). Colors changing rapidly,on the interior and exterior as well as the entire object. It also dominantly has a dark spot (hole) in the center,but not always. Never seen anything like this.
