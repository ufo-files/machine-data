Two star like lights were Hovering close together in the sky and quickly faded away.

I was standing in my neighbor's driveway and I looked up at the sky and saw two star like object in the south, just above Saturn. They were bright and close together and not moving. I thought they were stars and didn't recognize them because they were so close together. After looking at them for about 10 seconds, they just faded away into the darkness of the sky. I didn't hear a sound and they looked very unnatural. Like they were lights that someone just turned them off. My neighbor didn't see them because it happened so fast. But we stood there and watched they sky for about another 30-40 minutes to see if they would come back. Then suddenly he noticed another single star like object appear in the sky in about the same place. We both watched it hover and move very slightly up in the sky and then it too suddenly faded away. Very strange. It didn't look like anything I've ever seen before.
