Square Shape with Seperate Section at Front (like where you would expect a pilot) with Orange Glow at Back... Went Overhead and Disappeared

I was standing outside waiting on a bus. It was the 12th of September 2010, at exactly 9:48pm. I looked up above the building accross the road from me - and I seen an orange light ascending, as if it just hovered up from the ground. When it got to a certain height, it stopped for a second, then hovered slowly towards me (travelling east) and I managed to get my phone camera set up after 20 seconds or so. I could see the outline of this ship. Honestly, it looked exactly like the landing craft from the original series of V. or like the Imperial Landing Craft - or Imperial Shuttle from Star Wars. With the square main part, and another part attached to the front - with the 'boost' at the back.. It didn't look like it was a thruster.. was a perfect circle, orange, glowing not exactly like a fire.
(there was also nothing such as smoke emitted from the back)

I recorded this UFO flying but my phone camera just shows the orange light hovering until it disappears. No outline can be noticed in the video.
