I had been fishing and after loading gear I was sitting on tailgate looking at sunset.I kept sitting while I had a cigar,when I noticed what I thought might be a reddish planet.It was just above the horizon in the north-northwest just to the observer's right of a very bright star.Assuming as I said it was a planet,I saw perhaps an aircraft,but the light "joined" the reddish one which got brighter for a moment then returned to its former intensity.I do not know the exact degree in the sky,but if straight above my head is 180 degrees,it was about 45 degrees above north horizon.I do not know if it was anything,but it was the darndest thing i have seen in the bayou.Also the sky is very clear and there was little if any light where I was at.It was still there when I left,but Skeeter,my dog,was getting jittery for some reason so we left.I heard about you folks from my kid,so she said I should tell you.You can ask me more if you need to.Bonjour.
