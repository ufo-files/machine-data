{rjl} when i was four playing outside saw entity in craft and after abduction woke up far away. many witnesses

As I was at the back of our house, I heard a humming noise. I was four at the time. As I turned around, there was a very large ship hovering next to a tree and a door opened. The craft is of a very dark color bearing strange patterns that didn't seem to be writing but construction patterns. As the door opened, an entity came out and stood on it as a robotic arm picked me up. This entity was dressed with a shiny black cloth and had what I believe to be a mask that made it look like it had a beak. After that I remember sort of waking up in a table and something being put in my back. They told me it was a star and being a kid thats what I believed. I remember seeing smaller beings walking by but my vision was blurred. I remember waking up in a grass field about 200 meters from where I was and the grass had been pushed down like a crop circle type. I ran home and explained my parents what had happened. The neighbors at the time saw the craft and reported the house shaking as it went by and the lights flickering. The same happened at my house. We were poor and didn't have a camera so no photos were taken. I am now a Sargent in the Army with aviation background. If more information is needed please contact me asap.
