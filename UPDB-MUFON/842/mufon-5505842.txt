{nd} Nine star shaped objects in three

I am a cook supervisor at the Enterprise Health and Rehabilitation clinic, at 2045, I was taking out the trash at the rear of the facility when I noticed three red stars in a "v" formation moving from my right to my left, then I witnessed two more formations followed by a single star. They moved very casually, like a boat on a calm water, there was no noise, not even barking. When they came to the area above the ESCC baseball field, they moved into a circle formation and just as the tenth star approached, they went dark and were gone .
