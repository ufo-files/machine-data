From several miles away during daylight hours I observed a very large bright white square/rectangle (appeared pillow- or sheet-like) moving left to right in a slow straight path, then hover, close to the horizon.

On Sunday May 13, 2012, a bright sunny and clear afternoon, while driving down a busy street, I noticed what appeared to be a very large object moving along the horizon several miles in front of me. The object was square/rectangular, bright white, and appeared almost pillow-like, or like a large sheet. It moved left to right in a straight path along the horizon at an unknown speed (as it was probably 5 miles away), but it appeared to move slowly. It also hovered several times, and possibly reversed its direction. At the time I felt this was extremely strange and unlike anything I had ever seen before. My wife was with me and was puzzled by what we were seeing. I was forced to go with the flow of traffic and lost sight of the object after about 3-4 minutes.
