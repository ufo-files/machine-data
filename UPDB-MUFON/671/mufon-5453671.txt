{rjl} Invisibility Cloaked moving object

Every evening I try to go outside and stargaze as a way to stretch my neck from all the looking down into devices i do during the daytime. 
On this particular night, around 9:20pm i was looking at the starry sky and i saw what looked like a migration of birds or something. I could see a faint line in a triangular shape on the front end and then noticed the sky and stars were slightly warping as it slowly moved East across the neighborhood here in San Francisco just North of Golden Gate Park. As I continued to observe, I noticed the rest of the shape, (quite large), maybe the size of a city block, and realized this was something mechanical and moving slowly and quietly as if on a direct path and at a steady pace. The surrounding clear skies and stars being reflected as it moved across the city slightly warping the atmosphere along with it.
It disappeared finally behind a neighboring rooftop and I immediately texted a group of friends to keep an eye out for any news report I might miss regarding this event.
In retrospect, it's like I witnessed something that was scanning the city or taking a read of some sort. 
My reason for posting this 7 months later is because I just found MUFON this morning after a similar thing was witnessed again last night (April 18th 2020), which i'll submit here in a moment as a new report.
