{jpg} Pill shaped white object flying in straight line on clear day with no viable wings or jets. Two black helicopters flying around at time of sighting.

1) leaving union square subway at 14th and Broadway 
2) looked up because of two black helicopters in sky
3) a passenger plane
4) it was a clear day, no clouds, and this object was very high in the sky (a bit unusual in this area as most planes would be nearing take off or landing) I stared at it for 30 seconds at least and saw no wings, jets, markings, or lights. It glided in a straight line and took about 30-45 seconds to pass through my line of sight in the skyline (west to east) from about 14th and Broadway. There was a moment when it was about halfway across Union Square where it appeared to glow bright white.
5) I was confused. It was behaving very much like a passenger plane and I normally wouldn't give it a second look but baffled to why I could not see its wings, and why it would be flying that high over Manhattan, and why it glowed for an instant. After, still confused, skeptical, and not convinced either way.
6) I lost sight of the object when it glided into the west skyline of union square. I noticed an object that looked similar (small, high, white, with no other markings or discernible wings or jets) a couple minutes later, northwest of union square, heading north, but I'm sure if it was the object I'd lost track of.
