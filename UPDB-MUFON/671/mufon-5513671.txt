Blinking red lights

My wife and I observed 2 large red balls of light. The balls were in a vertical line of one another with the one on top blinking, and the one on the bottom staying lit the whole time. After watching these for about 5 minutes, we went back inside the house. 5 minutes after coming inside, we went back out to see if they were still there but they were gone.
