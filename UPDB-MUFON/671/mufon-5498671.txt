{nd} hovering red sphere broke into pieces then came back together

A Mexican youth who was taken to Sav-On Drugs in Norwalk Cali by his dad came out to find various people in the parking lot all standing up with their mouths open looking up at what his dad claimed had been a hovering red sphere which broke into pieces then came back together. My guess is the pieces descended then came back together, not sure if he said the pieces fell then merged together again. Apparently the object ascended up out of sight or perhaps faded out of sight if by the time he'd gone out these people were still looking up rather than off into the distance straining to see where it went for example. I got the impression more than four people had witnessed this event.
