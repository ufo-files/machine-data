{rjl} looked like a mans hat from underneath

Looked like a man's Fedora hat from the bottom at a slight angle. All slightly glowing white/gray colored. A friend and I were walking up a hill thru the snow to a football game and I happened to look back and saw it in the sky heading south. At first I didnt think much about it but when I looked back again it was going North at a fairly fast speed. It scared us so much we ran about 4 blocks to where the game was.
