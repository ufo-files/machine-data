(GS) cylindrical shaped object shot across sky then descended, and ascended again

I was outside my trailer, just watching the stars, thinking about how bad I was failing my Cultual Anthropology class, when I noticed a star that seemed much brighter and larger than any of the others. At first, I thought it was a meteroite or satellite or something, but it eventually stopped getting larger and instead changed direction and seemed to glow even brighter. It stopped moving and hovered for about 4 minutes before making a slow descent towards the ground. I was pretty far away so I couldnt see if it actually landed, but I could feel a great gust of almost uncomfortably warm air coming from the objects direction. It was so low that it was out of sight for a few minutes before it shot up into the sky like a bullett, bringing with it another gust of abnormally hot air. I actually felt as though I got wind burn. It moved so fast after that, that I could barely trace its movements before I lost it for good among the stars. Never seen anything like it in the Blue Ash area, it almost looked like it was near the college I go to.
