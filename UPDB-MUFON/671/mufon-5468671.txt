{td} 2 lights moving slow coming from the north turned to the west and dimmed over a landing airplane

On the evening of Dec 7th 2017 I was leaving Drysdale Western Wear while walking to my car I noticed 2 white objects in the sky coming out of the North moving slowly there was no sound. It was around 8pm there were no clouds in the sky. I have seen UFOS on 3 other occasions so I was pretty sure of what I was looking at. They slowly turned to the west and upon doing so the bright lights dimmed there was a commercial airplane coming in from the west landing heading north which I found it interesting that something would fly that close to a plane. After that they headed back north with no lights then they started to blink back and forth to each other in one video there's only 2 but after they turn north and start blinking then you see 3. I watched them for almost 15mins then went into another store and returning to my car they were gone.
