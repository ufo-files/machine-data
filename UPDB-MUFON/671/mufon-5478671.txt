OK I was walking next door to my mothers house, I saw something bright orange in the sky. first glance I thought it was just a bright star but then realized the sky had thick cloud cover. I watched the object for maybe 5 seconds, long enough to see it was traveling toward my location. I then turned ran to my house and got my best friends attention. He and his girlfriend both came out and saw the object, at this time was almost over my mothers house, all of us noticed 1 eerie thing no loud jet engine sounds only a whirring or humming sound, another weird thing is that none of us could see a fuselage of a normal jet it was blacked out and in a v or chevron shape, the object never stopped and it continued over the mountain until we lost sight. now to give you an idea of elevation my yard is about 875ft above sea level and the mountain tops are around 1600 to 1800ft above sea level, this object was way too low to be flying that route at night in the mountainous region of eastern Kentucky. This sighting makes the 4th time in 5 years that I personally with witnesses have seen a UFO in this general area.
