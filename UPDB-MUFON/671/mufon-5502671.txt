{cak} Bright shooting star looking light bright white with tail like a comet

My Husband and I were outside at 9:40pm smoking a cigarette when suddenly we watched what at first we thought was a shooting star. It flew over with a bright white light and a comet looking tail traveling faster than any shooting star or plain we have ever seen! If we had not seen it with our own two eyes we would not have believed it ourselves!!! It went down wards over the Fort Gordon base never slowing down! I then though oh no is that a missile! Called the cops to report what I had seen and the news to see if they had heard anything! I waited for a sound nothing. Waited for a large boom and nothing! I have no idea what it was or what it could be but to see it come out of the East flying just above the trees maybe 20 feet above them and to watch it go down but never hearing a crash or anything! This is not the first time my husband and I have witnessed odd happenings in the sky here! But this is the first time that I was ever scared that was a missile or something that was dangerous! We have made several reports of sightings that happen around this time of year starting in February and ending around August! Someone needs to find out what this was!!!
