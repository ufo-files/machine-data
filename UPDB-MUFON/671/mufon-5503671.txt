{nd} LAYED DOWN & WATCHED ON TOP OF HAY STAK , IT ZIPED BACK AND FOURTH ALL DIRECTIONS 20 MIN THEN ZIPED AWAY

WAS WALKING ON OLD DAIRY RD, SHORT CUT TO STORE, MY FRIEND NOTICED SOMETHING STREIGHT ABOVE US ( STAR LIKE ) MOVEING ABOUT, HE POINTED AND SHOWED ME, WE THOUGHT THAT IT WAS WEARD, STARTED GUESSING WHAT IT WASEN'T, GUESSED OF WHAT IT COULD BE (UFO)WE CLIMBED UP ON HAY STACK, LAID DOWN AND WATCHED IN WONDER OF ALL THE THINGS IT WAS DOING--ZIPED BACK IN FOURTH LONG DISTANCES, MADE 90DEGREE AND MORE AND LESS TURNS, WOULD STOP THEN GO AGAIN, THEN ABOUT AFTER 20 MIN'S IT JUST TOOK A STREIGHT PATH AND JUST DISSAPERD. WE CLIMBED DOWN AND FINISHED WALKING TO DAIRY STORE-- iT LOOKED SHINNY JUST LIKE A STAR, NEVER CHANGED APPEARANCE (TO HIGH UP) IT GAVE US A GOOD SHOW, NOTHING ELSE, AND JUST LEFT. WE BOTH NEW THAT NO AIRPLAIN COULD DO WHAT WE HAD SAW,SOMETHING YOU WILL NEVER FORGET.
