THE CRAFT [UNKNOWN] SEEMED TO HAVE A ROTATING SERIES OF COLORED LIGHTS WHICH DIFFUSED THE SHAPE AND COLOR OF THE OBJECT. THE UFO DEMATERIALIZED AND SKIPPED BRIEFLY WHEN IT REMATERIALIZED BACK IN OUR VIEW.

THREE OF US WATCHED FROM 100 YARDS AWAY A CRAFTOF [UNKNOWN] ORIGIN IT SEEMED TO HAVE A ROTATING SERIES OF COLORED LIGHTS WHICH DIFFUSED THE SHAPE AND COLOR OF THE OBJECT. MY BEST SIZE AND SHAPE DISCRIPTION WOULD BE A LITTLE LONGER THAN A VW BEATLE. THE LIGHTS ROTATED LIKE A HELIOCOPTER AND SWITCHED BACK AND FORTH DEPENDING ON THE DIRECTION IT TOOK. THE UFO DEMATERIALIZED AND SKIPPED BRIEFLY WHEN IT REMATERIALIZED BACK IN OUR VIEW, THEN THE OBJECT STOPPED AT THE SIDE OF A HOUSE, IT THEN EMITTED A RED LASER BEAM OF LIGHT SHOT AT AN ELECTRICAL POWER BOX BELONGING TO THE HOUSE, AND THEN IT BEAMED THE RED LIGHT INTO THE WINDOW OF THE HOUSE. ONCE FINISHED THE UFO THEN BACKED SLOWLY AND WHILE BACKING AND MAKING ITS TURN THIS CAUSED THE LIGHTS TO ROTATE OPPOSITE DIRECTION THEN THE LIGHTS RETURNED ROTATING IN THE SAME DIRECTION AS BEFORE AS IT CONTINUED ITS JOURNEY NOW TRAVELLING TOWARD A SMALL VALLEY AND UP OVER A SMALL MOUNTAIN DISSAPPEARING AS IT WENT OVER THE OTHER SIDE OF THE SMALL MOUNTAIN. 
  VERY PRETTY COLORS ESPECIALLY THE GREENS, ALL THREE OF US WERE LUCKY TO SEE THIS BEAUTIFUL HIGHLY ADVANCED BRILLIANT PIECE OF AIRCRAFT MACHINERY! PERHAPS THE UNITED STATES MILITARY HAS YET TO REVEAL SOME OF THESE KINDS OF SECRETS!! NOTE WHEN IT WAS LOW TO THE GROUND I NOTICED A BUSH MOVING FROM THE WIND OF THE VEHICLE, ALSO ON THIS PARTICULAR OCCASION THE CRAFT NEVER REACHED MORE THAN 10 MILES PER HOUR.
