Viewed orbital object come to dead stop twice and make sharp course corrections

While star watching and satellite spotting, I viewed an orbital object passing over Blount County Alabama, from a heading of 324 degrees NW towards the SE at 144 degrees. The object had a luminosity about half as bright at the ISS and was moving at typical satellite speed. Twice the object came to complete dead stop in its orbital path, made a large arc course correction, then continued on its SE trajectory until out of view over horizon. After each stop in-flight, the object required no speeding-up after each stop, as soon as it moved again, it was already going full orbital speed.
