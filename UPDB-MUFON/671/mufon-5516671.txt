(GS) 3 lights moving near solar panel field-AZ

8/3/12:
I was driving west on highway 8, passing Yuma, AZ. At approx. 4am, I noticed 3lights approx. 3-4 miles away, moving around over the highway and over to the right side of the highway in almost a figure 8 shape. Its like the lights were dancing and moving in unison. My first initial thought was a crazy pilot in a small plane, crop-dusting because the 3 lights always moved together - I thought the center light was a nose light and the outer lights were wing lights. As I quickly approached the lights, I noticed no distinctive shape, just brightness of the lights w/ a shade of red/blue-ish glow around them. The lights eventually zipped by on the passengers side w/o no distinctive sound or vibration. It literally whizzed by in an-almost silent mode. When I looked back and in the right mirror, approx. 1/4 mile after it passed me, one light went completely vertical and the other two lights maneuvered in a almost hairpin turn, and headed approx. north-west... disappearing, or as if the lights were turned off.
