2 bright points of light in a transparent sky over Poland

My daughter and I saw exactly the same two light spots in the southwest sky in Poland, Masovia ...on July 4 at around 10:25 p.m. local time (CET). The sky was 100% transparent. The form and specification of the phenomenon excludes in my opinion 100% that they could have been drones, airplanes, meteorological balloons, satellites, fireworks, lightnings, Chinese lanterns or other objects known to us. It didn't fly, didn't make sounds, didn't change color. The phenomenon lasted about a 30 seconds, first one point disappeared, then another. Their light was white and very intense, much more than the brightest stars of the evening. I've never seen anything like it. Before observation, there were no other objects or events in the sky that evening.
