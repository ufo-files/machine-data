{rjl} I saw amber colored solid object glowing, a plane flew by it, it faded out, app 8 minutes later a air force jet & millitary ufo zoomed by the NE sky.

I saw a plane, (commercial jet) flying in the northeast sky on January 30, 2020 at app. 8:35 pm. Near the plane was a stationary object much much larger than the plane, maybe as large as 5 or more jet airliners, it did not move. It had no sound. It had the typical saucer kind of shape except it had 2 extending arms on both sides, it was glowing an amber orange/reddish color. I watched the plane fly somwehat near the object, within 8-10 seconds the glowing amber light faded & discentagrated, it did not fly off or zoom away or move, it just became invisible as if someone turned the lights off. I continued to watch the sky, watch the commercial planes flying one after the other. App. 7,8,9 minutes later I heard that roaring loud mach sound in the sky along with very loud sounds of a helicopter. It was a millitary helicopter & an air force jet. They appeared to be together flying north east toward the direction of the object that I had seen. They continued flying very fast in the north east direction of the sky in formation.
