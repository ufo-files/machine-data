Big formation of UFOs

This morning I went with my dog for a walk next to the river. On completely clear sky with lots of stars I saw incredible line of straight-headed of shining dots (there were 12 to 15 shining dots) as same brightness level as stars have which had equal space between each other. I was surprised with that sight and I stopped only to realize that they were moving. I observed for two or three minutes while watching towards south, but they were unstoppably coming in as I looked on my watch to see it was 5:24. I compered the space between flying objects with two bottom stars of constallation of Ursa Major. I turned to north and saw that those objects were still flying in. The velocity, compered to speed of regular planes, was two, three times faster. After two, or three minutes there were fewer of them. I tried to count the amount of UFOs, but it was not possible, however I think that from time I first saw them till then there were from 120 to 150 flying objects. There were completelly soundless and something that really surprised me was that my puppy, two and half years old female beagle, was unrecognisably shook and disturbed. She has not separated from me while barking ever since, and running towards car which was clear sign to leave. Personally, it looked like some kind of invasion.
