{cak} Was golfing and looked up over the pine trees and a gray disc was hovering about 50-100 ft above the treetops

I was playing golf at Timber Trace in Pinckney Michigan. I hit a shot from the right side of the fairway on hole number four and was standing around looking at the sky which was still sunny for being well past 8pm but several darks clouds were moving across the sky as well. I noticed a solid gray disc-shaped object hovering about 100 feet above the tree tops across the street from the road aligning the left side of the fairway facing north east. At first glance I thought it was a big balloon of some sort. As I got closer to it I noticed it wasn't moving at all and it was too low to be a plane or anything. It looked to be maybe 10-20 feet in diameter. I kept staring at it and just couldn't figure out what heck it was. I was totally mesmerized and didn't wanna stop looking at it but I had to move to the green because people were waiting to tee off behind me. Unfortunately the tall pines surrounding it blocked my view of the object but I assumed it would still be sitting there when I finished putting and crossed the street to the next hole. I finished putting quickly and went out to the street and it was gone. I was really disappointed as I thought I'd be able to get my phone out of my bag and snap a few pictures. I never imagined I would see something like that especially in broad day light and it totally caught me off guard. I always watch the stars at night and have seen every kind of thing you'd expect to see in the sky but I always thought if I were to ever see a UFO it would it be some sort of strange lights in the night.
