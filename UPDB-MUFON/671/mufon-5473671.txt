{cak} I woke up to see a gray alien, then a strange dream of a UFO.

I woke up at 2:34 AM from a terrible headache, then I noticed a figure by the side of my bed. I could not tell what it was at first, but it appeared to be a gray alien. Though it was dark in my room, I could make out some features (gray skin, one of the almond black eyes, and four and a half feet in height). I tried going back to sleep, but the headache wouldn't go away. Before I got out of bed to get medicine, I noticed the gray alien was not there anymore. So I got some medicine to relieve my headache and went back to sleep. After that, I had a strange dream of seeing a UFO at dawn / dusk. While I was looking up in the sky, I noticed two bright stars rotating clockwise and very quickly. Then it turned into an orange spacecraft. From what I remember, it looked similar to a jet, but it had an odd shape. Suddenly, the spacecraft accelerated at a great speed (to my left) and disappeared.
