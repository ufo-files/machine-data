Hovering just above powerlines with random Squiggly lights going around ii

I believe that it was summertime in 1974 in Haltom City, TX. My brother and I walked to the corner store when a crowd was gathered in front of the store looking at the object which was across the street just above the powerline pole. It was oval cylinder shape and displayed random squiggly lights that rotated around the object. I was on 2nd grade and my brother was in 3rd grade but still remember the incident.
