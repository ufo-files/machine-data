Diamond shaped yellow incandescent was intercepted

At 5:45 a.m. est on November 8, I witnessed an object appear looking SW. When I say appear I mean it didn't fly into view, it just appeared and began moving slower than a plane from SW to SE. It was a diamond shape glowing like an incandescent yellow, with a back light or aura the same color. I could see the outline of the shape. I watched as it slowly moved for about 5 seconds to the SE in a straight path. Object then stopped and was stationary. Then a very small yellow light speck appeared, it looked as though it was traveling from outerspace, in a South to North trajectory. It was going extremely fast and intercepted the diamond object. It looked as though it touched the diamond object. The diamond object was stationary when intercepted, and immediately went to the SE very fast and was gone. The diamond object went from stationary to blasting away amazingly fast in an instant. The object that intercepted the diamond made a hooking pattern and was heading back in the same direction from which it came. It was fairly fast departure and i watched it until it was out of sight. It appeared to come and return to outerspace. I had to do a night shift later on the same day and had stayed up said night/morning of the Nov. 7/8. I had been outside for hours with a campfire. I was looking at Orion and conditions were clear and crisp. Observed some short lived and fleeting meteors before this occurrence. Meteors were not in the same direction as the objects I witnessed. I am positively sure of what I saw. It was amazing. I did see an unknown craft intercepted by another unknown object from outerspace.
