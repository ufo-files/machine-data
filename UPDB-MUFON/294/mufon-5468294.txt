{td} Friend and I saw UFO less than a quarter mile from my parents' house.

In the summer of 1966, in July, I think, after dark, a friend was at my house (6800 S. Poplar, Casper, Wyoming) at night, and we were sitting on the patio, on the NE side of the house and noticed a glowing very bright light NNE of the house. We didn't know what it was, so walked over to see what it was. There was a natural depression on the earth, with hills all around. When we got to the site of the bright glow, we saw a huge, to us, saucer-shaped object circular in shape, higher in the middle than on the very outside, which seemed flat, emitting a lot of light. We did not observe any actions or motions; it was just sitting there. We were both terrifically scared and ran back to my house, and did not see the object exit the depression in the hills, but did note that the light was gone about 30 minutes after we walked up and saw it. We revisited the area where we had seen the object the next day, but there was nothing there.
