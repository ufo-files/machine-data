{tjd} This was no plane

I sit in my hot tub and witness so many things. We live about 5 minutes from the airport and National guard. Close to hospitals too. I see all kinds of man made flying objects. Yesterday though, I thought I was watching a plane until it tilted and the sun cleamed off it's shinny top surface. It went by my line of view way too fast, with no noise or smoke, at about 4-5 thousand feet up, and just sorta disappeared instead of watching it go slowly out of my site.
