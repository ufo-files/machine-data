I rose slowly as to approach the being hoping to get a better look. I was about three feet away when it turned to look at me. It then beamed through the doors in a bright flash and disappeared. It stood about three feet tall had large eyes slits for a nose and mouth and no visible ears. It was muddy grey in color with shiny smooth skin When I saw its profile about half way down the back of its head it had a large bulge which blended down to its neck. The only light that was on was a night light in the kitchen so I could not see the rest of its body.
