I had just pulled into my driveway around 7:30pm and as I got out of my car I noticed 3 bright white lights in a straight line hovering just above the treetops across the farm field adjacent to my home. I watched them for a about 30 seconds and they began to move toward me as they appeared to rise. They flew silently over top of where I was standing and I noticed that they were in a triangular formation. They passed very slowly and quietly above me but when they were beyond my location I heard a sound which resembled a jet engine. I went inside and told my wife "I think I just saw a UFO."
