Bright light in the sky

I was with my wife on our loggia (we live in an apartment complex close to the community center), and we had dinner. First, I noticed a flashing light in the sky, which I identified as a sports rotorcraft (there's a small airport not more than 3 miles away, suited for small airplanes). All of a sudden, I noticed another bright light in the sky, next to the airplane (distance and size unknown, since it was dusk), it hovered stationary for a few moments, then, flew some distance, until eventually, the light ceased. If it was a helicopter with a seach light, then it flew too high to observe it with our naked eyes. Unfortunately, the event didn't last long enough to take any pictures or videos. (Plus we were busy wondering what the **** that was)
