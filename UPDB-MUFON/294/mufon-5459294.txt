{cak} Bright red and while lights, very large, low in the sky, silent, made a right angle turn.

I opened my bedroom window at about 9:30 PM, I saw a bright red flashing light moving fast straight toward the sky above my apartment, moving north, it came withing about a half mile, turned left, or East, and headed toward the neighboring mountains where the 134 freeway runs. When it turned, I could see that it had a bright red light in front, a white light behind it, another red light, white light and final red light, all flashing. It appeared quite large, bigger than a plane, but nothing visible except for the lights. It was totally silent.
