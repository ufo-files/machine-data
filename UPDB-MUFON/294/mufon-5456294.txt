{tjd} Observed 2 objects hovering 2,000ft above in vicinity of Navy air base. Both departed in different directions with extreme speed.

At the time of the incident, I was an active duty Naval Officer (now retired), locally stationed, and very familiar with all types of military and civilian aircraft, drones, various flares and airborne ordnance. 

While relaxing in my backyard on a clear afternoon, my attention was drawn to the sky by a dust devil lifting some trash aloft. It was then I noted a white oval object stationary approximately 2,000 feet up and to the south. Living in the immediate vicinity of NAS Fallon, a premier Air Combat training facility, it raised my curiosity. After about 10 minutes, I retrieved a pair of 20X binoculars from my home and regained sight of the object. It did not move, shine or do anything but remain stationary for another 5 or 6 minutes until an identical object approached it at a high rate of speed (but not extraordinarily fast). Upon the 2nd object joining close proximity to the 1st, both objects departed (about 1 second apart, with object 1 leaving first) at an incredible speed. Object 1 shot of east, while object 2 shot off north/northwest. I recognize now that these objects are identical to reports of "Tic Tac" UFOs, as I had estimated it to be a bit smaller than the size of an SH-60B helicopter.

I have withheld reporting to MUFON until now, since now the Navy has also publicly recognized the existence of UFOs. These are all the details of this event that I am willing to release for public consumption.
