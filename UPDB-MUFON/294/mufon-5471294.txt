30 years ago I was taken to a ship in outer space. I was shown where they came from and our position compared to them. And much much more. I was told they had to bring me back before to much time passed, I asked why and they said it had to do with C time. Didn't find out what they were talking about till now. Our time here on earth is different from the amount of time that passes in space, I looked it all up. They knew what they were talking about. They didn't want for me to be to far out of my own time. They cared about that.
