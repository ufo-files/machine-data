{nd} On block wide block letter V shape; shimmering, hard to see; moving slow.

While star gazing a large V shaped object flew over us; it moved very slowly north west. The object was hard to see as if it was translucent; it made no noise and about the size of a city block. We were stunned and couldn't believe what we were seeing. We lost sight of the object when it continued to move north west and out of sight. The object shape was a large letter V; we could see the block edges and it moved in the direction of the "point" of the V.
