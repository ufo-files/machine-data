As I was leaving the [location removed] I noticed approximately 5 bright orange/orange-red light that appeared to be hovering out over the ocean. I’m 45 years old and am from here and have lived here almost all of my life. I have never seen anything like this before. I’ve never even seen this color of lights on a plane or helicopter. There were sold in color and did not flash or blink. They were grouped together in a random pattern. I was driving into a party of the parking lot where the sky line in that direction was about to be obscured by trees so I doubled back and was planning on video recording them and then, if they remained, go back into the station to gather witnesses. However there was only one left once I doubled back and then it was completely gone. There was no trace of any of them any where in the Sky. I did not detect any movement in any direction while I saw them. The lights appeared to be electric and not at all like flares I’ve seen fired over the ocean.
