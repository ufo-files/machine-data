{nd} Bright Ball of light near mountain

My husband and I were on River road approaching the Grayson intersection when we saw this bright white oval object sitting in the sky. It could not have been a planet or star because it looked like it was hovering in front of the mountains. I turned left onto Grayson and looked out my rear view mirror and it was no longer there. Nothing was obstructing my view. The whole incident lasted close to a minute.
