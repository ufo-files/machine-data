Multiple plane-like craft flying low overhead with no sound

I took the dog out at approximately 11:00 pm on the 29th and noticed a plane-shaped object flying low overhead. It looked similar to a DC-10. It had a white flashing light in the center and a red light on both wings. It was flying very low - I'd guess about 1,200 feet or less at about the altitude that helicopters fly in this area. The body of the craft was a dark color and I could not see details. There was no sound whatsoever. At that altitude there should have been the sound of engines. Then a second craft came along at the same exact altitude and trajectory, then a third, and so on until I lost count of them. They all went from SE (the direction of Whiteman AFB) to NW right over Kansas City. Either Whiteman has silent planes now or these were UFOs.
