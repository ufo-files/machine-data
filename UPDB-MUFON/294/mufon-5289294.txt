Hovering object over head, could not make out, not aircraft.....

On March 9th, 2021, I was out in the front yard with my dogs for a potty break. The time was 11:00am. After some time of playing with the dogs, I managed my way around the yard and was heading back to the front door. I looked up and noticed the sky was clear except for some jet exhaust. I wanted to see how far it had expanded and cleared up. I looked over to the tree tops and very high in the sky, I noticed a reflective light. Like a little tiny mirror in the sky. I thought at first someone let go of a balloon. I was waiting for it to just go out of sight into the atmosphere but it hovered in one space for a few minutes. So I went back inside and grabbed my cellphone to take pictures and record. I couldn't believe it was still there. It started moving south and into a pretty straight path in the sky. I got a better look at it. Silver. Red. Silver Red. Reflecting light but I couldn't swear it wasn't making it's own light. Around 11:40, I had to go back inside to work. I texted the video to some friends to get some feedback on what they they it was. As I live across the mountain from the only airport in Charleston, I heard there was a no fly zone for drones or anything unauthorized by the airport. I went back out about an hour later and it was gone. During the 30 min I was watching it. It moved around but very slowly. Sometimes staying on silver light and briefly red. I have wracked my brain on what this could be. It seemed to have a round body and tails floating below it.
