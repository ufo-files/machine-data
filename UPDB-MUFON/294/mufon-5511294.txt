My wife and I were sitting outside in the front porch of our house Watching a thunderstorm roll in. We heard a low flying plane. Got up from the porch to look at the plane, as the plane was flying by we were following it. I noticed a light appear out of the clouds in front of the plane. The plane veered left,it seemed to turn away from the object. We started looking at the light trying to figure out what it was. As it passed over our house, the object didn't make any type of noise. It was moving at a slow rate of speed. My wife was able to take 3 pictures of the object before it disappeared in the clouds. I have never seen anything like it before.
