Silver Light!

At 7:16 am, I saw a commercial airliner heading a normal trajectory towards our local international airport, I spotted above and beyound it a silver light, round shaped object on a straight trajectory, which suddenly vanished. Sun was up, no clouds in the sky. I observed this for about twenty seconds and knew there was something odd about the silver light before it vanished.
