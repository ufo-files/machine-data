{cak} V-shaped UFO

On or about the 15th of January 2018 we were traveling east on Highway 542 in Washington State just east of Bellingham, Washington. It was a clear sunny day, cloudless, and visibility was excellent. We could see the sky unobstructed for miles around us, the only obstructions being the local terrain. I noticed what I first thought was a cloud just overhead and then realize that it was V-shaped. There were no other clouds. No structure other than the V-shaped cloud was visible. I pulled over to get a better look at it and in the minute or so it took to find a place to pull over the object had traversed from directly overhead to the eastern horizon and disappeared behind Mt. Baker. There was no contrail or other physical signs of propulsion. The width of the object was about the size of a quarter at arm1s length. With no reference points, altitude is unknown.
