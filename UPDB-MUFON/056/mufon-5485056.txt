4 lights one moved in a trajectory crossing others. Others started doing the same then flew off in different directions quickly!

Having competed on a night motor sport rally, retired, stopped to watch event. A small congregation were present waiting fellow competitors. Unaware to me others were watching the same skyward event I was. Four star light objects, not twinkling which suggests in our atmosphere, were in alignment where no stars would normally be located in the night sky. These remained motionless for what seemed ages. The rally cars had been expected but nothing came. I am NOT suggesting that time stood still but, it was odd. All four lights were evenly spaced out in a line. The one to the far right started to move very slowly. Its path, passed each of the other three lights in turn and settled a similar distance away from the original light on the left side (hope that makes sense). In turn, they all started to do the same, however, being joined by more lights. These lights moved faster and faster across each other until they suddenly all flew off in different directions at a speed no earth craft can achieve. Although witnessed by many, one lady scared locking herself in her car, no-one to my knowledge has ever spoken about this event since. Another incident happened a number of years later and I will report this separately.
