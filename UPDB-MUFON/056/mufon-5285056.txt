Metallic shiny disc seen flying low on a clear sunny day over suburban neighborhood.

Was walking a block from mother’s home in middle of the day. Clear sunny day. A metallic shiny disc swooped down toward me to an elevation of 100-300 feet and then stayed motionless. There was no sound. I stopped and just starred. Trying to see if it was a drone or something. I guess that is possible but i couldn’t see any propellors. It just looked round, metallic, and shiny. Then I shrugged it off and walked twenty feet do the street corner. Then I turned and looked up again and there was no sign of it. Just disappeared. Again, no sound.
