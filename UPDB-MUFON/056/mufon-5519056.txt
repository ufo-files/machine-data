I saw three different lights as I was feeding the dogs and taking out the trash

I was outside feeding the dogs and walking back toward the house to take out the trash when I looked behind me and in the northwestern sky was this extremely yellow bright light unlike any of the stars out. I then went around to the front of the house with the trash and my mother was standing in the driveway looking at a light in the eastern sky that was pulsating different colors. First white then red then white. I told her about the one behind the house in the northwestern sky so we watched both. Both objects seemed to have airplanes or some flying craft in the vicinity either beside or above them. This isnt the first time we have seen stuff like this. The light in the western sky seemed to expand a bit in other words whereas at first it was just a big yellow light it was as if you could see that the light was widening. I am going to keep looking for this object to see if it went anywhere but I just had to file this first report because some strange lights are showing up in this area of Ohio from time to time and I know I am not the only one seeing them.
