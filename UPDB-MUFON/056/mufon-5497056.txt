{dr}Saw 11 different fireballs cruising through the sky, no sound very bright

It was New Year's Eve 2014 when some of the neighbors were shooting off fireworks at midnight. My son and I went outside to see what was going on and noticed two very strange fireballs cruising through the sky from the SW heading ENE. They were about a mile apart and I honestly believed they were some type of cruise missiles and had gotten very nervous. Not more than five minutes later we saw two more coming out of the SW again passing in almost the same direction. I called my wife and my son's girlfriend to see this and they were also very nervous to see these fireballs cruising by as well. My wife wanted to call police about the sightings. Then we saw three more coming in a group flying, about a mile or two apart, the same route and then we saw a larger group of four of these fireballs flying lower and coming from a different area all following each other in a line out of the West heading North East. A total of 11 was seen that night by us. I can't believe I am not hearing from others about these objects, or the local news. I did take a video with my iPhone6 plus but not great. What did I see?????
