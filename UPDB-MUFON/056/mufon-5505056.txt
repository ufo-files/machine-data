{kt}observed stationary in the north- vehicle next seen coming toward me heading east

first observed gold light to the north of where I was standing.The object began to move to the west and light went out. I next observed the vehicle just west of me. The object was coming toward me . the craft was no longer emitting a gold exterior light. As the craft approached I noticed that there was an observation deck. The deck was lit with a gold light. The deck also had a platform with a gold rail resting on 3 posts. On the platform there was a figure . this individual was wearing a gold jumpsuit. The craft passed directly over me. There was no sound and no light. The stars were blacked out by the vehicles passing. After it passed the stars were again visible but no sign of the craft. I have only offered this info to my family and few friends. I have often wondered if this craft was seen by any one else. The incident occurred between 1967 and 1969 . I cannot be more specific. I would love to know if anyone else experienced this event
