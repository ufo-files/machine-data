pulsating light going west to east very fast

Outside smoking cigarette on Friday evening I was watching two planes/jets flying South to North, when a bright white pulsating light suddenly appeared and shot from West to East. At first I thought it was a shooting star but it didn't burn out. The light continued East leaving no contrail and disaapeared in about 45 seconds. I feel this could not have been a plane or jet because there were a few planes/jets in the area and I could clearly see the differnce between those and this object. This object was moving much faster than anything I have ever observed in the sky short of a shooting star or shuttle/rocket launch and made NO sound.
