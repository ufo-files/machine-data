{nd} Friend and I both saw a glowing, white disk travel 3/4s of a mile north instantly, stop on a dime, and after a few moments, decsend and fly toward the east.

A friend and I were walking down Manitou Rd. from Rte. 9D at about midnight when the sky lit up enough for us both to turn around, about as bright as if lightning was somewhere. There, about 3/4 of a mile south of us, was a glowing, white disk in the sky. A moment later, the disk traveled toward us so fast we didn't see it move. It stopped in place closer to where we are without making a sound, and after a few moments, it descended and we saw it fly away, just above the trees toward the east near South Mountain Pass. The whole event was so fast, there was no time to think about what the object was until it had gone. We were both frightened by it, because of its apparent mastery of time and space and we considered an extraterrestrial origin likely because of the same.
