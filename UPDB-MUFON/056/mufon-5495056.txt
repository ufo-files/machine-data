I got up around 7am, went into the bathroom and sat down to do my business. I happened to look out the bathroom window at a approx. 45 degree angle, and saw an object going slow, in daylight, about 10 mph, traveling straight east, just above the tree line. The tree had no leafs on it cause it was still cold outside. By the time I could get up and open the window and look out, it was gone. The object looked like an upside down pie pan. It was a solid shape and dull silver in color. I could'nt believe what I had just seen, but I know I saw it. Everybody I told did not believe me. I have'nt mentioned it till now.
