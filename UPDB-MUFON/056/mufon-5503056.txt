{kt}cloud-like structure

(Reporting citizen Mr. B:) This witness prefers to not be identified. It was a bizarre cloud-like structure. As a meteorologist in Atlanta for more than 30 years, I have never seen anything like it.
  (The GA State Director provides this narrative, a synopsis from the investigated report: On the morning of April 30, 2014, at 9:55 AM, a crew of workers for an Atlanta food-truck company was about to load their truck with supplies. From their location close to Interstate 85 and Monroe Drive, they observed a highly unusual cloud to the northeast, at a low elevation angle, in the direction of a local brewing company building and seemingly above Armour Yard (a MARTA rail yard). At 9:53 AM the crew leader snapped the photograph attached to this report. The crew had the cloud in sight for about 10 minutes. All 5 in the crew saw the cloud. The crew leader then sent the photograph via email to a local meteorologist who, that afternoon, forwarded the report to MUFON via CMS.
