While using Sky View to see the ISS, I was watching the sky above us. I watched a “star” moving, as I tried to determine if it was an airplane I realized the behavior was in no way a plane. It didn’t follow any flight path, nor did it have any flashing lights. My BF then looked and said, “that’s no plane.” He has his pilots license. About 30 seconds after it descended beyond my view, a blue light originated at the same spot directly above me, and flashed in the exact same path as the orb.
