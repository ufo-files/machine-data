{dr}10 flying objects coming from the SE

I was outside smoking a cigeratte at about 8:30pm on March 17, 2013. I looked down the street towards the main road which is south from the house. I noticed 3 red-orange lights without blinking just flying towards the north, then 3 more appeared right behind the first three, then finally as the first six flew by about 30 miles (approx) 4 or 5 more lights appeared behind those. I first thought that they were planes, but noticed that lights were not blinging, to see like 10 lights in the sky just flying is strange, never seen that before.
