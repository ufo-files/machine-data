{cak} white blurry oval object flew across Manhattan skyline

Wednesday, 2/19/2020: The whole sighting was under 10 seconds. I work on the penthouse floor of our building in Midtown Manhattan. I see the Manhattan skyline out my office window. I had just arrived at work around 8:50am. While speaking to a coworker, I noticed a bright white oval shape appear next to a building in the distance through the window behind her. I thought it was a cloud at first. I was facing SW in the general direction of NJ. I expected to see more of the cloud appear from behind the building but it didn't. The object was fuzzy like a cloud but a perfect oval tilted forward. It was bright white. As fast as I could say what the heck is that, it flew straight across behind the buildings-perfectly parallel with the ground. It flew SE towards the East river. The empire state building and other buildings are taller than where I was so I lost sight of it. It traveled too fast to get to a better vantage point to see where it went. I often see planes and helicopters outside. They move much slower and generally at an angle. Planes and helicopters are easily identifiable. This object was MUCH larger and faster and moved perfectly horizontal. It did not appear to have hard edges or a reflective surface. I was shocked and jokingly said I think I just saw a UFO (although i believe I really did). I digitally added what I saw to a photo I took a while back to illustrate what I saw. I am hoping someone else saw this and maybe got an actual photo/video...
