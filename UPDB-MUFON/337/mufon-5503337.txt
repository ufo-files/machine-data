{rjl} Saw a funny cloud-like thing in the sky and took a picture of it.

I was driving south on hwy 385 from Odessa tx toward Crane tx. when I noticed a funny looking cloud or vapor trail so I took a picture with my phone. Months later (for whatever reason) I was looking at pictures on my phone, saw that picture and decided to zoom in on it. Well, at the very top of the photo, above the vapor trail were two shiney, metallic objects with what appears to be one black dot on each craft. I'm not sure what this is. I did not see the crafts at the time I took the photo, so I didn't feel anything one way or the other.
