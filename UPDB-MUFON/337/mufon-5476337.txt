{rjl} 4 or 5 huge,disc shaped objects hovering low over power lines for approx 30 minutes, witnessed by several people, including police

One night, in about 1992, I was sitting inside watching TV when my father came in from taking the trash down to the curb. He was very excited, saying I had to come out side and see something, but when I asked him what it was, he just said "I don't know". So, I went outside, and found several of our neighbors standing in the street, pointing to the sky. I looked up and saw 4 or 5 huge,oval/disc shaped objects silently hovering in the sky, above the power lines, behind the houses. You could see the object's outline, it's coloring (dark grey) and rectangular shaped lights spaced equally around the perimeter of all of the objects, blink sequentially. One of our neighbors called the police, who came out. They called local airports who said nothing was flying over our area at that time. They said they would file a police report, and they called more police officers out to see the objects. Several people stood there watching these objects, staying totally still and silently hovering. Then, after 30 to 40 minutes, they all just disappeared. They didn't take off, or fly away, they just disappeared. We all stood there for a while after they left, to see if they returned, and several people said that even though we could no longer see the object, they "felt" like they were still there, maybe just hidden somehow. During all of this, my neighbor was recording with an old VHS camera. The next day she played the tape, only to find the screen was snow, but you could still hear what was going on. This was an incredible, close encounter sighting witnessed by multiple people, including police officers, and I've never understood how it didn't make the news.
