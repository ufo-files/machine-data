{cak} One dark object and two white orbs

I have no pictures or video but thought about security cameras in the area might have something. I was standing in my back yard facing north, I saw a large dark object slowing fly east when I observed two white orbs descending down out of the sky. As they descended one orb turned and it looked like aluminum foil. The descended and approached the on the north and south side of the dark object and fly along side of it for a few seconds when the one on the north side flew off north (lost sight immediately of it) and the one one the south side flew a little south and then went straight up and disappeared into the blue sky above me. I looked back for the dark object and it was gone.
