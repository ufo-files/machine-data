{rjl} WITNESSES UFO AT CONVENIENCE STORE ABOVE HIGH LINES ALONG HWY, 64

IT WAS TUESDAY EVENING OF MARCH 15 1987, IN A SMALL TOWN CALLED ROLAND, OK.,JUST 7 MILES WEST OF FORT SMITH , AR. MY HUSBAND, 2 CHILDREN AND MYSELF HAD BEEN LIVING IN ROLAND FOR 3 YEARS. AT .APPROXIMATELY 9:30 PM, AFTER HAVING PUT THE CHILDREN TO BED, I DECIDED TO DRIVE TO A CONVENIENCE STORE . IT WAS LOCATED ON HWY,64 WEST ABOUT 1 MILE FROM OUR HOME, TO BUY SOME SNACKS TO WATCH A MOVIE ON TV . ON THE WAY THER, WITH THE WINDOWS ROLLED DOWN, I NOTICED THAT IT WAS UN USALLY QUIET WITH LITTLE TO NO TRAFFIC IN SIGHT . I TRUNED INTO THE PARKING LOT AND PARKED THE CAR AND STEPPED OUT TO GO INTO THE STORE. WHEN I WENT IN I NOTICED THERE WAS NO ONE EXCEPT THE STORE CLERK AND MYSELF. SHE WAS ON THE PHONE TALKING TO SOMEONE SO I DECIDED, TO GO OUT SIDE AND WAIT . AS I STEPPED OUT SIDE I LOOKED AROUND, AT WHICH TIME I LOOKED UP AND NOTICED A HUGE OBJECT WITH ROTATING MULTI-COLORED LIGHTS THAT APPEARED TO BE AS LARGE AS A FOOTBALL FIELD . THE ELEVATION OF THE OBJECT SEEMED TO BE BETWEEN 100 TO 500 FEET UP .THE LIGHTS FLASHED SEQUENTIALLY.I STOOD THERE LOOKING AT IT IN AMAZEMENT REALIZING I WAS LOOKING AT A UFO. THE OBJECT, I FELT,WAS OBSERVING ME I STOOD THERE MOTIONLESS AND CALM FOR A MINUTE OR TWO . I TURN AND WENT BACK INSIDE TO TELL THE CLERK TO STEP OUT SIDE WITH ME TO SEE IF SHE COULD SEE WHAT I WAS SEEING IN THE SKY . SHE SAID YES I WILL .WE WALKED OUT TOGETHER AND I TOULD HER TO LOOK IN THE SAME DIRECTION THAT I WAS LOOKING . I ASK HER DO YOU SEE THAT OBJECT ? .SHE SAID YES I DO ,SHE PAUSED FOR A BRIEF MOMENT AT WHICH SHE TURN AND WENT BACK IN.AT WHICH TIME I TOOK MY EYES OFF THE OBJECT FOR A SECOND AND LOOKED UP AGAIN ,AND IT HAD VANISHED WITH OUT A TRACE. I WALKED BACK IN THE STORE AND PURCHASED THE SNACKS I CAME FOR AND LEFT TO GO BACK HOME. WHEN I ARRIVED HOME I GOT OUT OF THE CAR ,WENT IN, AT WHICH TIME I TOULD MY HUSBAND WHAT I HAD SEEN. HIS COMMENT WAS "YOU DID ? HE LOOKED AT ME PUZZLED LIKE HE DIDN'T BELIVE ME. A COUPLE OF NIGHTS LATER ,       AROUND 10:30PM WE WERE GETTING READY TO GO TO BED. THE LIGHTS WERE OFF , AT WHICH TIME , I DECIDED TO LOOK OUT THE BEDROOM WINDOW WHICH WAS FACING NORTH AND SAW AT A DISTANCE THE SAME OBJECT I HAD SEEN TWO NIGHTS BEFORE. I TOULD MY HUSBAND TO COME AND LOOK , HE DID . BY THE TIME HE CAME TO SEE THE OBJECT, IT HAD DISAPPEARED FROM SIGHT. ABOUT THREE WEEKS LATER , WE WATCHED ON TV A DOCUMENTARY ON UFO"S ON THE PROGRAM IT SHOWED DIFFERENT SHAPES OF UFO"S . WHILE WATCHING THE PROGRAM I WAS ABLE TO IDENTIFY THE SAME UFO OBJECT THAT I HAD SEEN RECENTLY BEFOR. I POINTED MY FINGER TO IT AND SAID, "THAT'S IT! THAT IS WHAT I SAW!"
