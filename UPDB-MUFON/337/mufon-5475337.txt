{nd} Very bright light in sky that faded away rapidly

We were sitting on our front porch facing SW between 12-12:30am. Jupiter was visible in front of us. I was facing my companion and talking when I glanced back at the sky and saw a bright, fairly large (in comparison to Jupiter) at about the 11 o'clock position in relation to Jupiter. I said "what's that?" and my companion looked and saw it as well. It moved slightly to the right and pulsed quite a bit brighter and then very rapidly left to the W/SW. I watched it until it was a pinpoint in the sky. My companion thought it disappeared but I could track it for a couple of seconds. It looked white to me but my companion said it looked orange-ish. I saw a plane go over heading north 10-15 minutes before seeing the "object". We were excited and both felt that it was definitely not an airplane.
