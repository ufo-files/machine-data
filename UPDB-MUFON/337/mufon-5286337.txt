I observed it rise up then stop in the sky it was a ball of light at first I thought it was a falling star then I noticed it was going up and not down

This happens when I was very young and I don’t remember the exact year but I know it was in the summer time and I know it was on a Friday night just after dark. Me and my brother seen it at the same time and we both remember what happened. I watched it for about 35 minutes then I went inside to call my aunt and when I came back outside it wasn’t there anymore but ther wasn’t any light behind it like a beam from a spotlight or anything like that just a quiet ball of light in the sky
