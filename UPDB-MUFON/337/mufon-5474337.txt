{cak} UFO traveling in a straight line covered 70-80% of visible field of view (horiz to horiz) in less than 10 seconds.

I was in my backyard, looking skyward attempting to locate and identify what sounded like a WWII era aircraft flying over. While looking in a south west direction, almost directly overhead I picked up a small white object moving from west to east. To me it looked like a typical commercial aircraft at very high altitude with a normal reflection of the sun making it visible, however within a couple of seconds I realized the object was moving at an incredible rate of speed. The object covered at least 70% of the visible air space in less than 10seconds (horizon to horizon being 100%). A commercial airliner was also observed flying west at an altitude that would be appropriate for an inbound approach to LAX or ONT international airport, the "object" appeared to be above the airliner (at a higher degree above the horizon) from my perspective. The object traveled in a straight line and was moving incredibly fast and I actually was expecting a sonic boom. I do not believe this was any type of conventional aircraft (private/commercial), I ran to the front yard (for an unobstructed view to the east) as soon as the object went behind the roof line of my house, but was unable to re-establish a visual of the object.
