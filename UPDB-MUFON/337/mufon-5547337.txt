Very bright star-like object

Land O' Lakes, FL. 10:35pm. Looking at moon. A very bright light streaked below the moon. No trail. About the size of a star or a little bigger. Brighter than stars, as bright as the moon. Not blinking. Very high. About five seconds. Then the object sort of streaked upward and completely disappeared.
