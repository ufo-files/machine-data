{rjl} Silent Craft Over Walmart in St. Mary's, Georgia

I'm an avid sky watcher and have seen and reported other incidents. I was at work at Walmart on break around 5:40 am when I noticed a ball of light traveling in the sky. It was silent, bright with no aviation light. I pulled out my phone and recorded it for several seconds. Unfortunately I had to go back inside otherwise I would have watched and recorded much more. Lately I've been seeing a lot of interesting things in the sky.
