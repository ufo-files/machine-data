a ball metallic with lights around equator section

Object was first observed acending and descending above and below treetop level in Brighton, approximately a kilometer inland before coming toward us.

Object came slowly and directly toward where we were, southeast
across the bay, turning eastbound, approximately 500 off shore, and 
heading toward the lighthouse at walking speed. 

We followed the object on foot, while the object travelled the shoreline at walking speed to the lighthouse where it then came closest to shore, approximately 15 feet offshore and within 300 feet above the ground, and it hovered there for about a minute above us while we sat down, with our backs up against the lighthouse and looking up at it. I asked my friend if he thought it was watching us. Then I waved at it, at which point it shot off toward Wellers Bay, about 8 kilometeres to the east. It reached Weller1s Bay shore within a couple seconds. It seemed instant, and was without any sound the whole time.  

At Weller1s Bay, it slowed again and changed direction heading south along the shoreline. We watched it slowly float along the shorline south east bound until we could not see it anymore.
