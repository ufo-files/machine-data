{nd} Orange glowing spheres

Driving north on my motorcycle at 2000 hrs. Noticed a orange light 30-35 deg. from horizon from the NW. I pulled over noticing the strange light was coming towards my position this was for about 45-60 sec. in time. Watched it pass over my point of direction North. Had no idea what it was. First thought might be an EMS helicopter. Absolutely no sound on a calm and clear night. The orange sphere had fuzzy border looked like it might be on fire, hard to describe maybe slightly flickering in intensity. As it past me from left to right it was heading in a straight line to the SE. I noticed the retreating side had what looked like a flickering spider web appearance in combination with the orange glowing sphere. Its retreat was 10-15 sec. before it disappeared into the sky. Turned to carry on my way, caught second sphere at my 11 oclock and watched it retreat in similar fashion, only it seemed to be faster. I felt exhilarated, yet I was wishing for a pair of binoculars because of the fuzziness and the spider web appearance. I went home told my wife, sat outside for 5 min. Then went to bed.
