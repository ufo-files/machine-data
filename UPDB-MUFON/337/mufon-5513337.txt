Lights in the sky

I was at home and my mother called me and told me that there was something strange in the sky. I thought they were fireworks but still no one was celebrating the New Year. The three lights stood still, as if frozen on the buildings and glow of orange light.
The sight gave me a little 'anxiety. The lights disappeared after a few minutes as if drifted away on the horizon.
