{nd} I was driving with my cousin going north on the i17 loop and we spotted maybe 5 UFOs over downtown phoenix.

I was with my cousin heading east on the i10 on the 2nd of January, 2016. We were on our way to a street located in northeast Scottsdale called Rio Verde. I have been wanting to take a drive on that particular road because of it's lack of light posts. As we approached the i17 loop going north to Flagstaff, I saw several oddly shaped lights hovering above downtown. Initially, I dismissed then as being commercial aircraft but the longer I stared, the more I noticed the weird shape and color of the flying objects. They had the color of a burning flame: bright yellow,orange, and red. It seemed as if someone had painted several small streaks of colors onto the night sky. I caught a very short glimpse as I was driving away from the lights so I was unable to record them, I asked my cousin to take photo or a video of them but it was too late as we were facing north and the lights were out of our view/reach. The lights just seemed to hover and maybe move slightly but for the most part were stationary, the brief moment I saw them. The moment I realized what I was witnessing, I began to feel excited for the phenomena I had encountered but I am saddened that I was unable to capture evidence of the objects. I attempted to find other testimonies such as myself via Facebook but I didn't get as many as I had hoped. Only another person besides myself and my cousin came forth validating what we had seen and she claimed they appeared and disappeared rather quickly.
