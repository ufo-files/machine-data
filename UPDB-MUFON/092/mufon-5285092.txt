One big red and one big green light moving fast, then stopped- UFO?

I saw an object flying fast by with one big red and one big green light. Lights were close together and were not blinking, flashing, fading etc. It was going fast (heading south) and I thought it was a jet, but the lights were weird (and I don't think airplanes have green lights on them) and, as I was watching it, it came to a complete and sudden stop. Zero movement. Then it moved towards me (east) and stopped. Then it moved forward (south) and stopped. Then it moved back (north) and stopped. I got freaked out because it felt like it 'knew' I was watching it, so I 'played it cool' and looked away like I was doing something else, but I looked back suddenly and it was gone.

This happened by the Millwoods Golf Course.
