strange lights

At the hwy 16-62 junction at the west bound stop sign on 16, to the NW approximately 1 mile away and at the height of a cell tower was a bright white light, not moving, no navigation lights. As we proceeded through the intersection continuing west, the light turns to 4 window like lights that are rectangular in shape and light blue in color that appeared to pulse almost as if it was breathing at times showing 6 lights. The craft outline appeared to be triangular with rounded edges, I would estimate to be the size of maybe a Cessna. My wife said it reminded her of a blimp. We turned off hwy 16 down 184th heading south putting the craft in my rear view where the light turned to yellow, became very bright and then disappeared. I might add, during college I worked at an airport for two years and can identify different craft and this was nothing I ever saw land at the airport before.
