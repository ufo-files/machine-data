Lost memory upon investigating orbs

It was my first night in town for work, and I was staying in a mobile trailer home.

Having been chased out of bed by some friendly wasps, I was trying to sleep on the living room floor when I started seeing a blue-white orb in my peripheral, outside the mosaic front door.

It evaded my line of sight for a while, but slowly grew more comfortable staying in sight. 

More identical orbs join the first one in a swarming pattern, until it could be believed that either a swarm of insects oddly reflecting light or a disco ball could be the source.

After putting on my shoes and jacket to investigate, I open the door only to find myself in a white void without the feeling of a body. I remained in this state for the rest of the encounter.
