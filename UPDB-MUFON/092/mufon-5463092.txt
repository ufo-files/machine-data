{rjl} round pulsating or spinning yellowish disc

Earlier today I posted still photos of the disc, and here I will post the video I did of the same disc. I have been photographing up to five different similar discs in the night sky for over two years, and to the naked eye they just appear as stationary larger stars. I have observed them in many different parts of the night sky. One early morning I observed one high above my back yard. When I attempted to photograph it, it took off and went toward the northeast. The discs all appear as a circular disc with bumpy edges, and a long blade appears to be spinning in the center of the disc.
