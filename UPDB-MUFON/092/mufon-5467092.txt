Orb hovering over Harrison Lake, June 17th between 2 am and 5/6 am. Viewed by 2-300 people, all agreeing it was not a helicopter, drone, or known human craft.

At Camp Cove, Harrison Lake the weekend of June 17th. A small music festival of 3-400 people. Some under the influence of drugs and alcohol, but many sober individuals (performers, medics, security, and other staff) all agree they saw the same thing.

Light/Orb hovering over the lake, moved towards us and away. The intensity of the light remained the same regardless of distance from us. At first it appeared to be a drone or helicopter, but there were many small erratic movements and a certain quality of movement that everyone agreed is not man made.

I watched it closely for at least 20 minutes, and returned multiple times to observe it. There was a moment when I was with 1 other person and we agreed it was moving closer to us, we felt a bit scared but still too curious to move. It moved back further away and slowly hovered up higher over a period of about 3 hours. At sun up, it was the only light left in the sky (no stars visible anymore). 

At about 5 or 5.30 it faded away and reappeared multiple times, before fading a last time and disappearing for good
