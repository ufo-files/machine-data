{cak} Sighting at FL340

While flying at an Altitude of 34,000 feet over he SW US in a Westbound direction. We saw a series of about 8- 12 lights in a horizontal line about 10-15 degrees above our level attitude. The lights faded and returned in a shallow V position only to fade again. The experience lasted about 20- 30 seconds for me. This sequence of lights caused a lot of chatter on the radio and was confirmed by 4 other aircraft in the area at different altitudes and headings.
