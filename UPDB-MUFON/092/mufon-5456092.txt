I had my east-facing window open, looked out eastward, & saw a fast moving baseball or basketball sized green glowing light
without sound or any afterburner exhaust travel ~200 mph from north to south, 
which appeared to be traveling above Williamsbridge Road in Bronx New York 10469, USA,and it was at about a 30-degree declining angle, then went behind a 6 floor apartment building at 2705 Colden Avenue at Allerton Ave.,at about 12;40Am on Jan 16th 2019.
I saw this from an open window at 2765 Matthews Ave, 4th floor, facing east.I called it into NORAD as a possible fast moving private drone or possibly a meteor.There was no sound or crash.
I figured it was about the size of a basketball or softball, but the exact location & size could not really be determined just from one point of view.I thought it was either someone's drone or a green glowing meteor.It was not optical illusion, because there was no intervening glass window or anything else between my eyes or the object I observed. It all happened in a flash, and the thing that made it odd was the lack of any sound or "tail" exhaust.But after reading NY Post's Jan 4 2020 it described another witness who saw a green glowing objects in the eastern skies. The only similar "green light" type UFO reported that I read about was Dec 20 1948 New Mexico on page 35 of "American Media Specials", Vol. 18, No.27, 11/16/18, "Are We Alone? The Search For Alien Life." {ISSN 1537-663X, bar code #009128480480.} Note I only wear glasses for reading, and had no glasses on for distance or any intervening glass or other clear object between my view and object in question. My guess is it was some sort of drone or alien probe.
