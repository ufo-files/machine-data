Satellite like object sudden directional change opposite direction

Vancouver celebrated tonight "Celebration of Lights" fireworks.During the fireworks noticed one strange hovering object in the background , moving up and down, changing colors red, green and white, then disappeared below horizon. Even some came very close to the downtown core of Vancouver and what seemed to be a plane, hovered, red light went to bright white light, then just red dot and travelled very slow entire city,no sound and seemed to be about 1000 Ft.
