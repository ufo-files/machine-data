{rjl} Viewed while traveling on the 15 near Ontario airport mountains were behind sighting

We were traveling on the 15 freeway approaching the 60 and 10 freeways. We were looking for approaching jet airliners about to land at Ontario airport as it was noticeably void of air traffic which directed my eyes further out in search of air traffic. I noticed in the northeast sky an odd form in the sky. My husband who was driving thought it was a blimp. I was not driving and thought it might be an advertising aerial display as continued closer to the object it appeared as solid and not a blimp or aerial display. It appeared to be stationary in the sky with maybe a slight rotation as we were able to view the object at a different angle about this time an approaching airliner was observed about to land at Ontario airport. The object was rectangular in form with no sharp edges and in our estimation it was at least as large/long as a large airliner. We were definitely amazed and curious at what we were seeing as we had never seen such a thing before. We left the 15 freeway and headed west on the 10 freeway when we lost our line of sight to the object. We are very sure that what we saw was solid and metallic and not shiny. It was not attached to anything it was stationary/hovering independently. Attempts to take a photo were unsuccessful. Wished we could have viewed longer.
