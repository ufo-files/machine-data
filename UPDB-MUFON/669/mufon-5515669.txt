Black ufo with no sound flew by and helicopter with green light and sound after it.

My friend told me that around 9am they were out when they saw black object with no lights or sound slowly moving above them. They are sure that it was no cloud because it moved faster than other clouds. They tried to take a picture but it was dark.

Few minutes later, a helicopter with green light flew by in direction of the ufo. After that, they saw few red and green lights flying in area. 

I heard helicopter few miles from that event in that time.
