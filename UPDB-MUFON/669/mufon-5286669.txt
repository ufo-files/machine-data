Saw a flashing red large upright triangle with intermittent white lights flying south to north west. The red light changed to white when north.

I first saw the red flashing triangle approximately a quarter mile from the intersection of Relief route and Sunset in Roswell NM. The triangle was perfectly upright and flying to the north west at a speed faster that airliners that fly out of the local airport. The flashing was not steady but it had the rhythm like that of a heart beat...flash, flash, pause. When the triangle got to my left heading to the north west the light became white with the same flashing sequence. I saw a video on the news this morning showing a triangle flashing in the same fashion. the only difference was the colors were not displayed due to night vision use to capture the image.
