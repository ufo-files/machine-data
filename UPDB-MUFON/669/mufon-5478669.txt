{cak} Stationary object that traversed sky from south to nortwest over a period of 45 min. There was a second object spotted. I have drone video

I witnessed the light as I was traveling to store in the south sky. at first is seemed like the light from an aircraft, specifically a helicopter due to is altitude. When I arrived at store, I observed the object for a minute or so and heard no sounds. it hovered and seemed to not change altitude or direction. I went into store for 30 min or so. When I came out of store object was in the west. It was just as bright but seemed further away. As I got home, I put my drone in the sky to see it better. I took video of it and still shots. In one of the videos a second object is seen moving in the direction of the first object from northerly direction at a much lower altitude. This is the second time I have seen these lights in the area. First time I saw them was 6 months ago. I dismissed them as recon drones, given my proximity to the border. I filmed them this time.
