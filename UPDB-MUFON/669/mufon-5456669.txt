{jpg} 2 sightings of same object , 20 yrs apart, in 2 different states. 2 sighting witnessed by some of my employees as well

1981 at age 15, in Richmond VA, I was by myself in an open area on a bright afternoon. I walked from the apartment to a power line field approx. 300 yrds away. Something compelled me to look up and gaze at the whispy clowds. I was in a very peaceful state. Directly over me, and very very high up, was a round, shiny metallic object that did not move as the clouds passed over and under it. I say it was very very high up because it was so small like how an airliner looks at cruise altitude. It wasn't orbiting or in motion. I watched it for maybe 10 minutes and then it was gone. The exact month and day I can't remember. 20 yrs later, again I can't remember exact day or month but time of day approx the same and conditions were the same. It was bright and sunny, white whispy clouds, in Fayetteville NC. I was working at my business, an automated car wash, with lots of customers and cars on the lot. Again while in a peaceful state of mind, something compelled me to look up. The exact same object was directly over me at approximately the same altitude, assuming do to it's size, and was stationary as the clouds drifted over and under it. This time I pointed it out to my employees and a few of them witnessed it as well. Again I watched for up to 10 minutes. This time I noticed bright flashes as the Sun ,I think reflected off of it. I do believe it wasn't a coincidence that twice, 20 yrs apart, the exact same object was directly over me. When I was really young, until I was around 10 or 11, I have had unexplainable events that were traumatic to me and that I will never forget. I can elaborate further via interview but don't feel like typing that much to explain.
