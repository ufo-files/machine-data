{jpg} Bright incandescent like ball steadily falling, hovered then twirled/rotated rapidly in horizontal elliptical motion.

Noticed a bright light that first appeared to be a plane descending over the middlefield hills while looking out my front window atop a Durham ct hill. I was located south of it, facing north. It moved slowly in a downward 45deg direction towards the west, paused, and descended again slowly straight down. It paused again then spun as if it was a centrifuge. Lights dimmed and disappeared. My guess at location would be, near wadsworth state park or further north. It was unusually bright.
