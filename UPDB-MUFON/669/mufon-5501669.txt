{rjl} 3glowing orbs disappeared and 30 took their place

We were watering the lawn and happened to see 3 orangy red orbs flashing off and on then the one on the right disappeared then the 2 others. One half a minute later 30 showed up in their place same color but in an ovally round shape with 30 or more evenly spaced in that shape.Ine drifted diwn to the left and within 3 minutes all disappeared. One showed up after that and we went inside. My sisters family witnessed it also at their house.
