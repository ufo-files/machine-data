Four to five orbs moving in formation then disappearing

As teenagers a friend and I walked down to our local elementary school after hours to play on the swings etc. We were on top of a hill overlooking a large field when I saw what I initially thought was a balloon. Upon further inspection it was way too far away to be a balloon, nor was it moving like one. It was moving through the sky in an unnaturally smooth line. I thought it was shining a light, but realized it was reflecting the bright sunlight as it was getting closer to sunset. Another of the same object appeared and was moving in the exact same direction, parallel to the first. As they went further across the sky, we saw two more coming from the opposite direction in the exact same movements. They appeared to make some sort of formation until they met closer together in the sky. I looked away for a moment out of shock to see if my friend was seeing this too, and she was looking up at them also. In the short second I looked away my friend said there was a bright light in the middle of their formation. When I had looked back all of the objects were gone.
