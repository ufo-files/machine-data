{cak} Driving and noticed boomerang object. Stopped to record and found orb formation on the video.

At approximately 1330 I was driving down SPID in Corpus Christi, TX, I noticed an object which I initially thought to be an airplane reflecting light. The object was moving towards me , and as it came closer I noted that it was spinning and shaped like a boomerang. The object would intermittently reflect light as it spun. It appeared to have a reflective side and a dull side. The dull side appeared to have an off white color. There were no clouds in the sky and the sun was shining. I pulled over at the next available area, and began filming the object as best I could. By this time, no more than 2 - 3 minutes after my initial sighting, the object had moved much farther away, but was still visible with the naked eye. It was still reflecting light, which made it easier to locate by eye. As it disappeared from my field of vision, I stopped recording. 

Upon returning home, I began to review the footage I had captured. In the video, I zoomed in to get a better look at the object. It is difficult to make out the shape and motion of the object, but at maximum zoom, you can make out the boomerang shape, the circular motion, and the intermittent flashing. I then proceeded to adjust the filter of the video in an attempt to get a better image of the object , and was astonished to find a circular formation of approximately 10-14 white orbs in the direction that the boomerang object was headed. I can only approximate the number of orbs due to the varying luminosity on video. I did not notice the formation of orbs with my naked eye, and they were only an incedental finding upon review of the footage I had captured.
