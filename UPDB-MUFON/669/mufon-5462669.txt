{jpg} Was outside by hot tub with friends, Saw an initial light that was a different color than stars and then a secondary light appeared underneath and jumped back up to the initial light after a few seconds.

I was outside by hot tub the evening of 11/6/18 with friends taking vacation photos. A friend noticed a larger low light that was a different color than the stars around. We watched it for about 15 minutes. In that time another smaller light appeared from sight about 2 inches below the initial light. It hovered then for at most 5 seconds and then shot straight up to larger light. We watched for a few more minutes and then went on with our night, it was still in the sky at that time. We originally thought that it was a helicopter, until we saw the secondary light.
