I was reclining on a lawn chair on my patio at aproximatley 2120 hrs on 30SEP2011. I was relaxing, having a cigarette, (I had NOT been drinking, and I DO NOT USE OR ABUSE ANY legal or illegal drugs or prescriptions), looking up at the stars as I usually do on a cool autumn evening. I saw, traveling directly North to South, either 7 or 9 lights, each one somewhat cross shaped, flying in a PERFECT "V" formation. This light group appeared from the North and were traveling perfectly south, then just before becoming obscured by trees, the light group/"V" formation changed direction by approximately 25 to 30 degrees towards the west. They were traveling at least 150 mph. They made absolutely no noise whatsoever. I live on the flight path of KC135s that have had their landing gears replaced and or rebuilt on Hill Air Force Base, Utah. These lights were flying as fast as any boing jet that I have seen in the area. These lights were flying much faster than any crop duster airplane that has EVER flown over my home. I have lived in my home for 10 years, and I am retired Navy. These lights glowed, unlike any bird or anything that I have ever seen before. These lights maintained a perfect "V" formation, even when performing a directional adjustment. These lights were silent, and the lights never wavered, meaning that since they were somewhat crossed shaped, the "wings", if they had been birds, never moved. I have never seen a flock of birds that could travel at 150 mph+, not flap its wings, have a flock that never broke perfect formation, AND THEN TURN, STAYING IN PERFECT FORMATION, 75 feet from tip of the "V" to tip of the "V". This was not birds, and I have never, or ever heard of a silent aircraft before, especially being as close as close to the ground as it was. I cannot believe that even the U.S. Govt, even if they have developed a silent aircraft, could be seen from the ground, at 400-500 feet, silent, moving that fast, and I saw NO OUTLINE OF A CRAFT BETWEEN THE LIGHTS. 
What more can I say.
