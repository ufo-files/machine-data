{rjl} Red Orange lights coming from the the coast moving west

So i walked out for a smoke, and of course to check the stars for activity, when i look up toward the east, ( which from my house) is about 5 miles to the ocean i notice 2 red-orange objects moving in formation west, then one burns out then the other. THEN here comes two more over the horizon from the same direction-( the coast ), one burns out rather quick, the other continues on a westward path then it burns out. None of these objects changed elevation or speed. Then a final one coming from the same direction moving to the west again and it burns out. All these burn out in different spots, no marker lights, its dark out, no clouds, high flying airliners thousands of feet above. There were no flashes or noise, the brightness and color remained the same through the duration of sighting.
