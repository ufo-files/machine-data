White egg shaped craft at treetop level.

The witness was feeding her cows when she saw a fast moving glowing opaque white egg shaped craft. The object was approximately 20-30 feet long and fat like an egg. She saw no external features. The craft was silent, but she had heard low rumbles, like distant thunder, shortly before and then again after the craft flew by.

It was at tree top height, and approximately 150 feet away. It flew very fast. She initially saw it west of her and it flew out of sight to the north in 3-5 seconds. Near the end of her sighting, it descended.
