{dr}Fast moving low flying craft disappears in a bright orange flash

On Sept 1 at 0455 I was walking north on hwy 441 in the city of Okeechobee, FL and looking northwest into a clear cloudless night sky, I saw a fast moving lighted craft that seemed too bright and moving too fast to be an airplane. The craft appeared to be about at about 1000 ft altitude and moving from west to east. The color of the lights were a very bright white light with two or three dimmer lights of red and blue much like an airplanes navigational lights appear. I watched it for about 6 seconds, then saw a bright flash of orange light where the other lights had been and then all the lights disappeared. I looked into the sky where the craft should have been before the flash and there were no lights, shadows, or smoke in that area of the sky. This event happened about one quarter of a mile from me, the craft disappeared about  1000 ft above the Okeechobee county library.
