{Two 7 foot diameter, 30 feet apart, 2 years apart}, Crop Circles left in my back yard. {djs}

On two separate occasions, 2 yrs apart, circles left in the grass/yard, both exactly same size 7' in dia., and approximately 30' apart, lined up in exactly a N-S latitude, one NE the other SE 8' away from the back of my house, or my bedroom. Noticed only weeds and thick crabgrass vegetation would grow in the circles for years, where only healthy well cared for grass/yard grew before, ''very noticeable''! This is about the time when Crop Circles were being more noticed/reported on around the world. At that time we weren't thinking of the connection until years later when the circles still remained. Slowly over the years the grass has finally all evened gown back out, evenly with the rest of the yard. We did nothing different with that part of the yard as far as fertilizing, watering etc. The circle were very noticeable from the first day they appeared, since this is a part of the yard that is visited/observed almost daily!
