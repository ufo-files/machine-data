We were lighting fireworks off on the 4th of July 2015 in Waycross, GA. My brother, friend and daughter ere present when my daughter asked me what was in the sky. We noticed several orbs flying I believe in a NE direction and then they appeared to slow down. They changed direction and appeared to head NB. We thought they had left then another appeared and in the second video you can see what appears to be something jumping all around the orb.

I did not know what t was but as I watched the orbs move in unison I thought that they were probably UFO's and at that point I began to video the orbs and their movements. The orbs appeared to be circular and pulsing orange light. My daughter seems to think that the orbs changed colors to a light blue I believe.

There were no emotions, I have seen what I think was a UFO a few years back in early October on my way to Waycross so it didn't bother me. It was kind of exciting. The orbs appeared to just exit off into the sunset at a good rate but not so fast that you couldn't see them.
