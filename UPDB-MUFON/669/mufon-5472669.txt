{dr}Orb hovers, begins to glow brighter until drops super fast behind hill

I was putting my kids to bed, sitting quietly while they were falling asleep. I noticed a light over Coxcomb Hill. At the time, I lived across the river in Springdale. The light grew brighter and brighter. I thought, Spotlight? But it wasn1t shining ON anything. Grew so bright and large, then dropped behind hill super fast. I never saw a helicopter or plane go that fast. It didn1t FALL behind the hill. It darted downward, so deliberately, so suddenly. Not a natural falling motion.
