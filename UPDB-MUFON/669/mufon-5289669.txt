It was approximately 8 pm and I was lying on my bed fully clothed looking at the sky. To this day I don't know why I would be doing that at age 17. This is 40 years ago now. Regardless, as I looked out my window the night sky appeared normal when three lights that looked just like stars appeared. One was traveling from the lower left of the window, one from the lower right, and one from the upper left. They then quickly spun in a circle once, and as each one reached a specific point in the sky, heading towards the upper left (or almost straight up) of the window, they each vanished. The entire incident lasted 2 seconds or less but I remember it clearly. My interpretation has always been that these things were traveling so fast they couldn't be seen until they slowed down just enough to spin in a circle one time at their rendezvous point. They then took off for wherever, and as they each reached their "cruising speed", they disappeared at the exact same spot in the night sky. As I said, this all took less then 2 seconds, and I'm certain that nothing from this earth could do that - certainly not in 1981 - or even today.
