Orange Glowing Triangles east of Dean Lake

Around 200 yards East of the NE tip of Dean Lake there is a small lake in a private wooded lot. My wife and I walked the trail around that lake and saw an orange glowing/flickering triangles moving reasonably fast across the sky two days in a row at about the same time. They were moving in a straight line at a speed which seemed faster than airplanes, but not near as fast as a shooting star. They were visible as they moved for ~20 seconds. They were flickering like fire, orange in color and seemed to have an aura around them giving off the impression they were on fire. Each one seemed to be about an inch across from our perspective, but appeared to be pretty high in the sky. So I imagine they were fairly large aircraft. The the atmosphere was calm and eerie and experience caused my wife to burst into tears and beg me to take her out of there as fast as possible.
