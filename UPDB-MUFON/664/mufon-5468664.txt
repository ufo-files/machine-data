Saw a plane go overhead and noticed five dots in the sky. Assumed they were skydivers so I stopped to watch them. Realized quickly they were not as they were moving around in erratic patterns and making various shapes. They didn't get any closer or further, just kept making different shapes in the same area. I thought they may have been birds, but they would occasionally turn and let off a metallic reflection of the sun, and their interactions and movements didn't match those of any birds I've ever seen. After about five minutes they got into a tight static pentagon formation and took off westward and were very quickly out of sight.
