Airplane like aircraft making strange noise an bright light bigger than a star and brighter

I was in the country right outside fortworth near Benbrook Lake and I was at my friend's "horse-back riding instructor getting ready to have a lesson and I went out of her house and went to go get her pony and I was just staring of into space looking for shooting stars like I had been the past few nights and I saw what I thought was just a regular airplane flying to Chigago or wherever and as I looked closer I didn't see the usual blinking red light I saw what looked to be like a an aircraft that was really high but seemed to be huge with a really big really bright light it seemed at times it went really fast and at other times it didn't seem to hardly move. I took the pony in the barn and walked to my friends house I was standing on the porch to take another look and as I concentrated on the aircraft I could hear a noise. I went inside and told them what i had seen my mom and her came outside and looked they told me it was probably military menuvers. As I stared at the aircrafts again the noise didn't sound like a jet and it didn't sound silent like an airplane that i would usually see going over. It sounded more like the ships from Independence Day and War of the Worlds. As I saw them go further in the distance I dismissed the whole convesation.
