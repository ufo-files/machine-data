{jpg} Triangle flew silently over my yard, bathing the area in yellow light from the bright lights.

I was stepping out my back door to take my leashed dog out. I immediately noticed my steps and yard were bathed in a yellow light. I looked up, expecting an incredible moon. Instead, a triangle was moving slowly and diagonally across my yard, from east to west. There was no noise whatsoever. The craft was much lower than the small planes I see but above treetop level. My dog, a Maltese, did not react. The lights were yellow/gold and extremely bright...two at the back and one at the tip. They seemed very large and round. If I held my arm out I would say it was as big as my thumb. It continued toward my front yard and trees blocked my view. I tried to pull my dog and catch sight of it on the other side of the trees. It was gone. As it had left my view, the yard was back to normal light wise. Just the small light at my door gave illumination and I was in mostly darkness. The event lasted about 2 minutes. I practically ran back inside and told my husband and drew what I saw.
