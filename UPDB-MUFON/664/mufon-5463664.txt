{tjd} Multiple craft, at varying height, appeared first like stationary stars

This occurred sometime in mid-July, 2017. I don't know exact date. This was my first experience witnessing UFOs. They were NOT airplanes or helicopters, or any kind of jet craft. They were way too high up. I like to look up and enjoy the constellations; and these objects appeared to be stars, but were not. That night, I took my dog out into the front yard so she could do her business, sometime between 2:00-2:30am (she woke me up). It was a clear night. I looked up and was just taking in the beauty of the stars, while my dog was on the lawn. I fixed my gaze to the East, and as I was looking up, trying to make out the constellations, I began to notice that some of the "stars" I was looking at were actually moving at a fairly slow but steady speed, across the horizon, from East to West. To my eyes, they looked just like the other stars in the sky. If I had not been focusing on them, and really paying attention, I would never have noticed their movement, because it was so slow and steady. The objects emitted a bright star-like shine, and were either of different sizes, or at different altitudes extremely high up in the sky. I first counted three, then 7, then 12, then many more. There may have been as many as 25+. Again, to the naked eye, they appeared to be stars (all intensely sold white and bright, but they were all moving. They were on a straight path heading East to West. I stood outside and watched for at least 10-15 minutes, maybe more. There was no sound. Eventually, they passed across the sky and disappeared out of sight.
