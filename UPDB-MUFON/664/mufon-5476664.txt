glowing aspirin shaped ufo, ascending to right NW

I was observing the sky (because it was very clear, without any clouds and pretty sunny), when i notice this white glowing aspirin shaped ufo, hovering for a couple of seconds then going up in the sky, making a right curve up. 
After that, more and less 10 seconds, there is another object, similar to a drone with wings, near the same area in the sky than the previous ufo. This one didn't left any kind of trace or mark in the sky, like a ''normal'' plane would. None of them, actually.

The event, between on and another ufo sighting, had the duration of +/- 20 second and it was witnessed by 3 people.
