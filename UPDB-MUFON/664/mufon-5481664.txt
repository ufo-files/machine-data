{dr}I saw one white cigar craft headed straight across the sky, then noticed a second one from the opposite direction headed toward the first.

I was standing in a parking lot at a local school where I take my dogs to go play when I was looking up in the sky and I noticed a solid white cigar shaped craft headed in a straight line across the sky. I scanned the sky and then I saw an identical craft headed from the opposite direction directly towards the first object I spotted. They continuded to head directly toward one another, and when they got close to each other, the first craft emitted a brilliant bright light, which caused me to have to close my eyes for a second and when I reopened them, the craft were passing by each other.  After they had passed, they each contined in the opposite directions of each other, until they passed out of sight.
