Stationary bright white light flashing with no patterns stays in the same spot for over 10 minutes

At 10:52 PM on Thursday September 2nd, 2021, sitting on my Bed-Stuy rooftop, I observed multiple stationary bright white lights well above the NYC skyline on an almost perfectly clear night. The video I took is slightly obstructed so be aware that the shaky greenish dots are not the object, they are my phone’s camera. However, I record this object’s bright flashing right above the only clouds in the night sky, and after a few minutes I lose it only to refind it in the video in a new stationary spot near the Freedom Tower from my perspective, towards my West. Object appeared to be a sizable distance away, but I was able to capture it to the best of my ability on camera. Another thing to note is that I see planes consistently as I’m in LaGuardia’s landing zone, this was not like any plane I’ve seen. In the video there is also a ton of activity with blinking red lights, which are presumably helicopters, but there had not been this level of helicopter activity over NYC until after I had seen these unknown objects. I also slowed my video down to 1/10 speed around the 4:15 mark (not what I am sending you, but I did this on a separate backup recording) because there is movement of bright objects flying in and flashing and disappearing in less than a second around this time. To be clear, I have never seen anything like this in my life- I presumed drones until I reviewed my footage which made the ordeal entirely more confusing. Please watch the full video and take this report seriously, it is genuinely the strangest thing I have ever seen in the night sky over NYC.
