{nd} saw what we thought was a star burning out , then saw a helicopter hit it and watched it fall from sky

At approx 8:15 pm on 9/23/13, we were headed south on joslyn road when my daughter noticed a bright light in the sky. We thought it may be a shooting star when we seen another plane oe helicopter with what looked like blinking red lights headed directly in the path of this object. I jokingly said look there going to hit each other! It looked as if they collided and the glowing bright object began to fall. It looked like it was falling in slow motion. We watched it continue to fall until it was beyond the tree line..needless to say the other 3 people in the car were beyond freaked out!!!!!
