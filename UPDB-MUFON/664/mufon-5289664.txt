Pulsating beam of light

February 14, 2021 Bloomington Illinois. I live in east Bloomington. The back of my house faces North. Around 10:30 pm My family and I witnessed a vertical beam of light from our back deck (to the northeast) for about two hours. The beam was very bright and had a vertical dark line going down through the center which you could see pulses of a bright light running up and down it. There were other less bright vertical beams but they were only visible for a very short time. The actual beam never moved locations but would sometimes disappear and then reappear but always in the exact same spot. The weather for the the day was very cold, wind chills in the negatives. The cloud cover was low and thick due to an impending snowstorm forecast for today (15th). Our regional airport is located just east of this beam a couple of miles. I know the location of the runways and the lights they use in bad weather and this was not that. Honestly I don't know if this is paranormal or something natural occurring. It certainly didn't look natural with the pulsating light inside it going up and down. I just don't know. I am including a picture taken from a google phone and maybe someone can explain it. I have about a two minute video I took from my Samsung A71 phone but it didn't turn out very detailed but you can see the pulsing.
