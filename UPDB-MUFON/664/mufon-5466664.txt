Large, slow moving fireball flew accross The sky silently. Too big to be star. To fast to Be plane

I was walking home from the train station about 10.40pm when I noticed what looked like a star moving only it was way to big to be a star and it was also red. Was way to fast to be a plane but lasted too long to be a meteor. I watched it for about a minute, it was also red! 

It was so silent but I was not scared at all. Considering it's one of my biggest fears it was almost calming. 

Eventually it faded into the distance but the further away it got the one big red light seemed to turn into a few smaller lights in a row.
