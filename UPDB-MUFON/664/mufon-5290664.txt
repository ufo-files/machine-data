6 hugecrafts exactly triangular going from north to south towards Indianapolis slightly before and after midnight Dec 8th to 9th. Below cloud cover.

I was taking my trash out before going to bed a few minutes before midnight. I heard a slight hum and looked up to see a huge triangular airplane with a light at the front, one in the middle, and three at the back. It was going North to South, towards Indianapolis. It was a dark night, but the craft was below the cloud cover and nearly directly overhead, going creepily slow, so I could make out that it was a triangle. The closest to it I had seen before was the stealth fighter jet. The craft had five lights. The front, middle, and three in the back. The mid one was red, the others white. There was some pattern blinking, but not like any plane I have seen. When it got out of sight, another showed up, Six in all, maybe others had passed before I came out? They were about a minute apart, maybe a minute and a half. I wondered what the military was up to at midnight, but I didn't know there would be so many, so I didn't go and get my phone camera, darn! Some had 6 white lights, front, two on the sides, and three in back making a perfect triangle. They barely made a sound. After they left, I saw an airplane going more east to west. It was a red blinking dot in the sky.
