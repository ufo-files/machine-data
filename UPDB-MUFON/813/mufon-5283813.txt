Vertical cylinder/humanoid shape in sky hovered & moved forward

While standing in [exact location details have been moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc], my husband and I observed what appeared to be a two segment (vertically stacked) object hover in the sky, to the east of us. It appeared to then move forward, hover a bit, then continue moving. This went on for about ten minutes; I took a photo at 3:34pm, looked away for a minute and we could no longer locate it. We had previously looked away and then back to see if it was still there. We considered it being a drone based on the ability to hover and then proceed forward, but the shape does not seem like a drone. 

I took two photos. They are iPhone XS live photos, one of the video attachments is a screen recording of the live photo. The second video is a screen recording of me adjusting the setting on the photo various ways to try to better understand the shape of the object.
