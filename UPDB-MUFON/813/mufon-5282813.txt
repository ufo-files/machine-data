Spiral Cloud with bright light in the middle.

After dark hauling in a load of wood driving west in yard towards our house. Noticed what I first thought was a bright star in a cloud. Light reflecting off cloud shinning like a light would in fog, causing bright V on each side dark top and bottom. Realized it was moving towards the west, got to the garage and stopped the mower by this time it was now moving southeast and had a spiral Cloud around it. There was no noise or any clouds in the sky other than the one moving with the light. I have spent a ton of time outside seen some strange stuff including a V shaped craft with green lights off the sides which couldn't be seen without binoculars. I spent time in Iraq, Kuwait, Sandi saw every shape and size of aircraft in the night sky nothing like the V shape. But it was even close to as strange as this light with the cloud spiraling around it. I attempted to take pictures and video with my phone but could not get the object to show up. My wife and son were also outside and witnessed the event. This may be just something I'm not familiar with like the first time I saw the star links in a row. Again this may have a simple explanation, but none of us have ever seen whatever this was before.
