Green light, very bright.

This light was observed right before Christmas. Was outside, very clear night. I know what satellites are and how they move. Same as meteors and meteorites. Light was moving slow, then covered a very large area of sky within a second. Stopped and moved at a right angle from original flight path. Again moving rapidly covering a large distance within a sec. Stopped. Blinked off. I was watching with 16x50 binoculars. Was just a clearly defined green light with binoculars. Made no sound. Object appeared to be over Naval Base Kitsap/Puget Sound Naval Shipyard. Homeport to 2 Nuclear Carriers and not far from Keyport/Bangor Nuclear Submarine base. Curious if anyone else reported this green light.
