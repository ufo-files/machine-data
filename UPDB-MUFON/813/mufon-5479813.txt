{nd} Bright white light moving at an incredible velocity, first hovering, then started zooming at a 45 degree angle toward ground

Driving north on highway 69 near Petersburg, IN at night. After driving under a overpass, saw a white light hovering in the east. It then starting moving at an incredible velocity across the sky, toward the ground, at a 45 degree angle. The white light was constant. It didn't flash. The object had no tail. It sped across the sky until the light went out above the tree tops. It was quite a distance away from me when it vanished. I immediately tried to call someone to tell them about what I had seen, but my phone wouldn't call for several minutes. When I hit call, it wouldn't ring. Overall, very unsettling experience.
