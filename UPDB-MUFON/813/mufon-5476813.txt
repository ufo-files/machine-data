Sitting on my patio at night. Noticed object in sky that was out of place. No stars or planets in that direction. It appeared to hover over a large mountain (I know there is a mountain over that way). Was visible for over an hour and simply disappeared. It reappeared on subsequent nights. But not since January. It was circular and at one point I asked for it to come closer, and it did. When it did it was only close enough for me to see the multitude of colored lights around the base. It was big. I was excited. I was abducted in 1989 so I wondered if these were the same aliens. My friend spotted the same craft in March 2017 over same mountain. We live in different areas of the city, but where she indicated her sight was the same area I saw the craft.
