{nd} 2 WHITE ORB SHAPES GOING NORTH

I saw two white orbs small in size because they were so high in the sky. They seemed to follow each other precisely but the second one appeared to slow but followed in the same path. It was flying much higher than a normal plane would. They were going north and the first one seemed to really pick up speed as we observed it as the second one slowed but continued to follow, they were out of our view within 3 to 5 minutes.
