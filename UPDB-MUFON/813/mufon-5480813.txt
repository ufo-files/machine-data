{rjl} There was a stationary large bright light for over an hour between Taos and Santa fe that was much larger than a star.

I was driving from Taos to Santa Fe on 9/17/16 and to my right in the sky was a very large bright light that did not move for over an hour much larger than a star and very bright. At sunset it turned redish and dissapeared. It did not look like a weather balloon. Some drivers pulled over and took pictures.
