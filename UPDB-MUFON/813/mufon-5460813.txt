{rjl} Triangle shaped craft flying over Brownsville International Airport

I was sitting out on the porch of my girlfriend’s apartment, on the second floor, when I noticed lights in the sky in the direction of the airport. I like watching the planes even if it’s just their lights going back and forth. I noticed a small plane possibly a Cessna traveling north to south. At the same time I noticed another set of lights which at the time only looked like three lights. I assumed it was a helicopter with a front light, center red light, and tail light. It was traveling perpendicular to the Cessna but seemed to be at a higher altitude. I looked down at my phone to check the time it was about 12:35pm. When I looked up the second craft was gone and only the Cessna remained. A few seconds later the craft reappeared only this time was parallel to the Cessna. This time I noticed there were four lights on the craft. A red center light, with a white light at the front and two white lights on the rear. It surprised me because now the lights moving in sequence seemed to form a triangular shape. It continued to fly at a relatively slow speed but it would disappear and reappear taking different flight patterns but always around the area directly above the Brownsville International Airport.
