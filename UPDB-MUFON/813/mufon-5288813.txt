Saw four UFOs - not sure what they were

the following paraphrases the account of the witness:

"Driving North on the 55 freeway from the Irvine area toward the 5 freeway, I noticed what appeared to be an elongated object with a raised top, something like a disc with a dome on top, perhaps 20 to 30 feet in length due north of my over the Tusin area. It was stationary and could only be seen with my polarized sunglasses on. I could not see it without the polarized lenses. 

After merging onto the I-5 freeway, in Santa Ana, I observed a second object like the first northeast of my position over the city of Orange and a third object of similar shape and size over the Garden Grove/Anaheim area northwest of Santa Ana. None were visible without polarized lenses on. Finally, after turning north onto the 57 freeway, then west onto the 91 freeway, I saw a fourth object like the other three but this one was over Fullerton. All were low in the air, slightly above the horizon. All were motionless and appeared silver/gray and white and reflective. On may have disappeared from view before I could turn away."
