what looked like a star,accellerated away very quickly

On friday evening,November 29 2919, i was walking home along Evergreen road in Campbell River.AS I approached the crest of the road I came to a spot where the street lights were not working, so it was quite dark, giving me an excellent veiw of the stars.
It was at that time that i noticed a single star that stood out to me because of it's colour. I had that orange-reddish glow that looked to me to be very similar to the glow given off by a high pressure sodium grow light I remember thinking to myself that maybe it was this colour to me because it was closer...i appeasred to be larger than the other stars.
It was at this time, I noticed that the "star" appeared to be tracking to the south.I had just enough time to think the word satillite,when it accelerated away to the south at incredible speed, of which I could only guess to be in the many thousands of knots,as it was gone from view in about a second.I should add that being on the crest of the hill, I had an excellent view of the gulf of georgia to my south.
