{cak} Two triangular ufos silently floated past not 110 ft above my family and I, closely followed by a bright orange glowing disc and then two fighter jets.

While stepping out of the bowling ally with my girlfriend for some fresh air I looked up at the late afternoon sky as I like to do, I enjoy seeing the summer stars, I saw two triangular objects floating 110 ft over my head that were so close that I should have heard them coming but they made no noise whatsoever. They both had two strobing BRIGHT white lights on the bottom rear tips, and five lights that looked like windows out the back, they moved more smoothly than any aircraft I have witnessed in an eight year military career and they were completely silent. Seconds after they had passed a third ufo, a bright orange ball of fire, for lack better discription, much larger and further away followed the same trajectory. None of the fore mentioned aircraft had the required FAA red strobe. Immediately afrterwards two jets roared past. The experience has changed my life.
