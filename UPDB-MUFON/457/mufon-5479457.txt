My cousin and I were on our way to get dinner at ~710 and we see this thing in the sky. We were driving south on Rosa L Parks Blvd and we saw this thing in the air just right of the road. When we first saw it we thought maybe it was a blimp or some sign because it wasn't moving. Well, I looked up pictures of blimps in the night and none of them look like that. Once we had our dinner we drove back to my apartment and we saw the thing in the sky from the parking lot. We took a picture (time stamped 7:33 pm). It was still stationary. We went inside. At the time, I honestly thought it was some sort of sign or light stuck to the ground because it was so stationary, but the next day I went and looked and there was nothing there. The thing we saw had to have been in the sky.
