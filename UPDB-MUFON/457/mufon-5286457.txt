object going in counter clockwise circle, stopped and headed east

I was working a night shift at a tire recycling plant on Sept 11, 2005. At approx 9:30pm that evening :I had to go outside to move the steel bin. The corner of my eye I caught something in the sky west of me. I turned to look and noticed an object turning counter clockwise in the sky. It continued doing it for 2 full revolutions and came to a dead stop. It remained motionless for 5 seconds and then proceeded to travel east heading for town. I called out one of my co workers to come out and look. We both noticed it travelling in the same direction. Although it was going in a straight line, its motion appeared erratic like it was having trouble flying in a smooth line. The closer it got to the lights in town the duller it appearance was until it completely disappeared when it flew directly over us. My co worker appeared unfazed, however he didn't see it rotate. The objects speed is approx to a prop aircraft and approx 2000ft in the air
