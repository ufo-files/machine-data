I was with my uncle in Glacier, Washington exploring up on an old coal mining road. Having never been up there before, I took my cell phone with me and was snapping photos of the scenery around me. I was taking pictures of what I believe is Skyline Ridge, and I took several photos in a row. Only one contained what I believe is to be a space ship of some sort over the mountain to the right. I did not see this ship with the naked eye--my camera caught the only image of it.
