{nd} large boomerang or wing shaped craft

I was on my deck having a cigarette when I saw a large boomerang or triangle delta shaped craft made up of 10-12 yellowish lights. I could see it block out the sky as it passed because it fly 'under" the constellation Orion. I saw the same craft approximately 3 nights ago in the same general area. it passed over my house quickly and headed in a roughly south direction where it faded from view. It moved silently and quickly in a straight line before passing from my view. I noticed it just by looking up at the stars and towards the moon like I usually do when I am outside at night. I did not have any reactions that I could tie directly into the object.
