{rjl} V shaped craft flew over town, with strange dim lights under wing.

At 11:00 pm both my wife and I observed a large v shaped craft silently move from the south to a north direction near downtown Superior. As it got closer we could see self contained glowing dim lights underneath. We were within a half mile of the object and it made no noise like a jet or plane. There was no FAA lights on the craft. It did not bank when it turned direction to the East and over Lake Superior. It was a fast moving object that left us both in awe. By the time I realized this was something abnormal it was too late to grab my phone to take any images.
