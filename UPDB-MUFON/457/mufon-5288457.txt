Bright lights at high altitude

The sky was clear with no cloud cover and there was no wind. The moon was clearly visible at an elevation of approx 90 degrees due south of my home. Slightly to the east of the moon was a fairly bright star/planet (sorry I don't know which) While looking up towards the moon I saw a bright light suddenly appear to the east of the star and it then moved towards the star. Before it reached the star it suddenly disappeared. I continued looking and saw a second light appear in almost the same position and it, too, moved towards the star and it also disappeared before reaching the star. This was repeated a further 4 times making a total of 6 sightings. Because of the altitude of these lights I heard no sound.
