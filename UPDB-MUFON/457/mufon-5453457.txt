{rjl} Looked like tiny really bright stars shooting strait up into the sky.

I was parked at the end of a dead end road, on the phone. Then I saw this beam of tiny lights shooting strait up into the sky. Repeatedly and very close to each other. The trail of lights continued for 2-3 minutes. Must have been 500 of them, kind of slow and looked far away. I couldn’t tell where it started but it ended really high in the sky and they all disappeared into the same spot
