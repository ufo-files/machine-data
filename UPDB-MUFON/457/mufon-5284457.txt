Two triangular objects pulsating white then red

My wife and I were out walking the dog while on vacation in villas NJ (cape may). We noticed an object that was blinking white. As it got closer, we noticed that it was pulsating white, then behind the pulsating white it would start pulsating red. The white portion looked like a string of pearls, and the red portion looked like a string of rubies. 
At first I thought it was a plane coming towards me, but as it came closer, I noticed that it looked almost triangular in nature, and the lights were pulsating. As it passed by us, that is when I noticed that the lights were white going on in sequence from bottom to top (There were 6 lights). When the top most light went off The red lights would start blinking. These would not turn off. However, after a few seconds the white lights would start their sequence again. 
We noticed two objects. The second object, was noticed after the first one, turned in a northerly direction. 
The first object traveled in a west to east direction, and the second object traveled in a south to north direction.
The wind was about 10 MPH, and it was partly cloudy out. 
The total time of the sightings was approximately 10 minutes. 
I have a drawing of what we saw as well.
