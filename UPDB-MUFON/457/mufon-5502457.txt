{rjl} Thought it was a star but colors were different from neighboring stars.

Watching for meteors from tonight's meteor shower. The very prominent colors were brighter than surrounding stars and airplanes in the area. I thought it was a star but colors were too unusual. It seemed as if the object was slowing going away, like flying more North and not "setting" as surrounding stars were. Over a period of minutes, I noticed the lights were much smaller and dimmer. Again, it was like it was moving North on a parallel plane, not as a star moves. But the movement was gradual. The object eventually got very small and I returned to the house to make this report.
