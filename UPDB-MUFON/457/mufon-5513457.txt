{nd} 4 red-orange glowing lights

we live in the city, and went out to walk the dog. Looking North, we seen 2 sets of 2 red orange lights glowing. They seemed to almost rotate around each other. After a few minutes some of the lights seemed to "turn off", then the last one just flew off to the east. We were very surprised, my daughter ran in the house to get the camera, she took pictures of them but nothing came out in the pictures.
