{rjl} 75-100 Flying Objects

On December 21, 2019, North of Tucson, AZ., I witnessed at least 75-100 flying objects very high up and perfectly spaced apart like a train of satellites. There was only 1 at a time and they continued across the sky for about 30 mins. They were all traveling at the same speed and were perfectly spaced apart like a convoy.
