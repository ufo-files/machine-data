{rjl} column with no wings had strip of green lights

Seen at night. Columnar flying object with a strip of green lights that extended along the entire length of the column, save for the front point. The body of the object was lighter in color than the point but the colors of the surface were not identifiable. The object did not have any wings or wing lights. No flashing lights or lights in the air around it. Very strange looking. 6 witnesses but only 2 detailed on the submission form. None of the 6 witnesses saw wings or wing lights. Elevation degrees unknown.
