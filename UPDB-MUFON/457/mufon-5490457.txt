{cak} blue light fell from sky- looked like airplane falling out of sky but no crashes reported

My 13 year old daughter and I were driving home from my sister's house around 10:20 p.m. when we both let out gasps. It seemed that a plane fell out of the sky. We were both quite upset. I was a little perplexed as to why I didn't hear a crash or see smoke but thought maybe it was farther away than it appeared. I have been checking the news and internet up until now for an explanation of what we saw but NOTHING. No plane crashes, (their are meteor showers occurring right now but it was not that- no trail of light/debris, it was not a naturally occurring type light either), no falling satellites reported. My daughter and I were CERTAIN we just witnessed a plane falling from the sky but again, no plane crashes reported in the area. It happened VERY quickly and it fell from the sky in an ark like angle and then disappeared behind the tree/house line. It looked as if it was less than a mile away, possibly within a few blocks where we were from but hard to tell because all we could see was essentially a blue falling light. My daughter said that in addition to the blue light (which remained solid in color and was a bright vibrant blue color) she saw flashing white and red lights which made her believe it was a plane that was falling. I believed it was a plane because the light I was seeing was not a natural light (it wasn't the type of light you see with a shooting star, a falling star, or a meteor and the way and speed it was falling was also different) it was more like a light you would on a plane. I was very, very surprised when I found no reports of a plane crash or anything else that could explain what we saw. It looks like it will remain a mystery!
