The portels were 10'ft wide. Flashing lights below. No noise.

I was coming home from work. Exited the freeway and stopped at the light for Wattles. Lightly snowing, no wind, large flakes. I looked to my left and saw a Giant size object. If I had to guess 1/2 mi to 1 mile in length. I noticed the tree limbs were bent under the object. You could very easily look into the rooms. They were pure white inside. Lights flashing below windows. No noise at all. The light changed, I couldn't wait to get home to tell my wife. When I got there she said don't tell anyone about this, they'll think you're nuts. I didn't for a long time. I feel people should know these things are real.
