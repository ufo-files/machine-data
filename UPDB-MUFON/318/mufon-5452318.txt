Observed a random moving star. A review via Ring door bell cam shows odd satilite behavior.

As I left for work early Friday morning, unbeknownst to my wife or I our door bell cam was recording the local yard and the sky beyond. My wife thought she had spotted a shooting star and later thought to check the door bell cam. With review she found the star she glimpsed was much more visible than she had seen at first.
The star/satilite made quick curving movements and zipped from place to place in an erratic path much faster than it seems it should be possible to do. It would stop suddenly, then move again in an opposite or 90 deg angle.
The object is just visible in the upper left field of view above the car port roof and adjacent the fir tree in the distant sky from the very start of the video to about half way through. I think there were high whispy clouds it was going through, back and forth periodically blocking it's view aside from the fluctuating camera brightness.
