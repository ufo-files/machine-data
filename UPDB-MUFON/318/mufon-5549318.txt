UFO landing in field with lights

My mother actually observed the object. We were driving through Kansas at an unknown location. As we were driving, she looked over and saw a bright object hovering over the ground. She thought it may have been a water tower or oil drill. As we got closer, she was able to see that the object had no legs and was floating approximately 100 ft above the ground. We continued to drive and the object eventually vanished.
