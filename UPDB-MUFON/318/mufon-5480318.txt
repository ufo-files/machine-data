2 orbs flying in wiggly lines before they vanished into thin air

I was walking the dog when I noticed two orb in the sky flying form NE to SE. Both orbs were flying in wiggly lines and there was no sound. I could only watch them for a few seconds before they just vanished into thin air, one after the other. I kept walking with my dog and searched the sky for them but I could not see them anymore. What I could see was that a few seconds after the orbs disappeared there were two flashes within the cloud cover. I am used to air planes flying over my head due to the cologne airport but these two objects did not behave like airplanes or helicopters.
