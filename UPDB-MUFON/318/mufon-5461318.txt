{cak} Lookout over Beach meadows beach (ocean)

Myself and two other friends were driving along side of the ocean near Beach meadows beach when we had decided to pull off at one of the lookout spots for tourists. Approx 10 minutes in we were just talking about every day life things when I had observed a blinking light coming from the NE part of the sky, at first glance I just assumed it was a plane which is not abnormal, upon second glance I had noticed that it was stationary, I then pointed it out to my two friends who were immediately intrigued. The object began darting across the sky at incredible speeds, we all knew it wasn’t anything that we have observed or heard of before. This object acted as if it was putting a show on for the three of us, for about 30 minutes we all watched in amazement. My friend recorded it on his Lg rumor phone, I recently got in contact with him but the phone is unfortunately long gone. This object was traveling at insane speeds, across the clear night sky as far as your eyes could see, hundreds if not thousands of miles in a blink of an eye. As the 30 minute show was almost up; the object darted to the SW and blinked out of range. I’ve been fascinated with other worldly beings and UFO’s ever since, I’m just realizing this is probably why. My Gramp also seen something with a peircing white light dive into the ocean near Sable Island, NS, roughly 30 years ago. No plane crashes were ever reported. I’ve also never spoken to my Gramp about aliens so he’s the least bit of a fibber. He just didn’t know what it was.
