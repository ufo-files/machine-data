My first Entity contact.

A couple months ago I was still living at my fiances parents house. One evening I was sitting on our bed and had the TV on just listening to music... I was in our room alone.. and suddenly I noticed a feeling on the right side of my neck as if my fiance was home. There was someone sitting to my right. Right beside me on my bed and his left hand was gently touching/ holding my neck.. and I was frozen. I couldn't stand, I couldn't couldn't my head but with my peripheral vision I could see this entity plain as day. His head was big, and the top of his head was level to my eyes.. his skin was forest pine green but his eyes were what caught and kept my gaze. He had eyes that were blue and green and his pupils were not round like ours they were slits that a cats eyes.. he was looking towards my closet and trying to communicate verbally with someone or something but I couldn't take my eyes off him. And the audible noises coming from him I couldn't make out. He had a sense of urgency in his expression and voice.. and then he was gone..
