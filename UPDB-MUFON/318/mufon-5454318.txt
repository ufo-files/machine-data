Series of about 30 lights fiying in a straight line in equal distances, some in a triangle formation

Observations on 29 March 2020

We observed ISS at 20.42. from W to E

Then we noticed another object coming over our heads at 20.45. It was travelling from SW to NE. After that we observed further 30 objects, most of which were travelling in the same direction. They were observable first in the Orion constellation, below the Orion belt. They were very similar in brightness and the speed of movement to the ISS station and they seemed to appear at equal intervals. 

Some of them (3-4 ) were travelling in different directions and were travelling simultaneously with the ones that followed the same trajectory. 

The observation of regular objects stopped at 21.03 with two more objects observable within the next 10 minutes. 

The brightness of the objects was too small to take a photo or a video. 

Account of observations by the second witness:

Direction: from the constellation of Orion 20.46 on 29 March 2020.
Spacing between the objects was roughly the relative distance between the Orion belt and Betelgeuse. This also indicates the direction the objects were moving in. 
Initially individual objects with the spacing as above. Later three of them appeared and seemed in the formation like a triangle, moving in the same direction. There were at least two such formations observable. 
The viewing finished with individual objects flying. Total observed objects; at least 30.
