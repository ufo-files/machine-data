{rjl} Circular ship with lights

I was on my way home from work in Frankline. And I was at the intersection of Rawson and Forest home. And I looked over to my left and saw a circular ship with about 6 bright lights shining down in a circle form and I was stationary at the red light. And it was also at a stand still. Then it began to move slowly and then took off going faster and went out of my sight towards the south east. As Soon as I saw this I immediately called my brother and told him about it and he told me to contact you guys.
