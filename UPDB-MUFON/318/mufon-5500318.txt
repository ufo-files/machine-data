{cak} 4 Different Reddish Orange balls of light moving east to west

The objects moved east to west at tree top level, Appeared to slow than stop then descend past point of view (First two) I drove west on a side road for about a mile, and witnessed the last two come at me directly from the east and curve to the northwest and than vanish into a cloud bank. They appeared to be 200 to 500ft. There were no aircraft visible or audible in the sky at the time. The first sighting occurred at approximately 9.10pm, The second maybe 5 minutes latter. the third at9.28pm. And the fourth at 9.33pm. Objects observed from two different vantage points, By three different people. I don't know what they were, But I am sure they were not Aircraft or Helicopters. NO SOUND1This took place 2 miles to the North of Oxford.
