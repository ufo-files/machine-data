{jpg} A row of lights

This happened in the mid 1990's around the same time of Phoenix lights. My coach, 2 other basketball manager's, & myself were heading north on Severance street in Hutchinson. We witnessed a row of amber lights (5-10) right in line of each other heading north. They were solid in color and higher than any street lights and not blinking. I lost sight of them but other team member's saw the same lights and they followed them to atleast the Reno & Rice county line where they stopped following them but the lights were still heading north. This is also about the same time a farmer by Inman, Kansas which is about 15 miles away found a round crop circle in his field.
