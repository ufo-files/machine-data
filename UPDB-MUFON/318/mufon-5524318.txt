Three lights down the center, two on the side. Upside down T shape

I was out swimming with my friends when I heard a noise. It was very loud, jet like. I looked up and saw a large craft above us. It had three lights going down the center, and two lights even with the bottom one. The sky was still a little grey, but you could definitely see that this craft was almost pitch black. You could tell it was triangle shaped. At first I thought it was a bomber or something similar, so I just started to get back to what I was doing. I could still hear the noise, but then it got louder. I looked overhead, and there it was again, this time seeming larger. It went off again, but then it came back to fly the direction it was originally headed. I didn't see it again, but I could easily hear it five minutes after it had left. I was pretty excited because I have always wanted to see a UFO, even though my sister doubted it. My dad was into that kind of stuff so I told him about, he thought it was cool, and later that day he found an article in the paper about all of the UFO sightings. I read it, and hopefully what I saw is not just an airplane, I want it to be known as a spacecraft.
