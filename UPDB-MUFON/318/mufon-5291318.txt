Spinning orb like object

I noticed a bright light out my bathroom window. I watched it move quickly to the right then back left where it was, but not very far. Enough to make me RUN and grab my phone so I could zoom in and wake my daughter up to make sure I wasn’t hallucinating. It would get smaller then bigger, spun all over the place in a fixed position. At one point, it looked like there was a much brighter one off to the left. I have no idea if it’s even a ufo, but I certainly don’t know what it is! I have an iPhone 11 Pro Max; I have videos zoomed at about 4x, then maxed out at 6x, and a photo zoomed in at 10x.
