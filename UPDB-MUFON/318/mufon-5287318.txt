I saw a Purple sphere coming my way from the horizon, it slowed twice, then blinked out

At about 11:30 pm, I saw a purple ball coming East from the West horizon. It peaked my interest because it looked like a purple ball and the FCC only allows red, green, white, etc. It reached my vicinity in about 7 seconds. Before it got close to my location it was clear the outline was purple and gradually turned to lavender toward the middle, and the interior was sort of flickering. It was a full-blown sphere now. The object slowed two times in an instant (not gradually) while moving forward in a straight line at an elevation of about 500-800 feet. On a very light night (semi-full moon), the object then continued almost directly over my location (my back yard), and at 90 degree straight up it blinked out like a lightbulb leaving the outline of the object visible. The object I saw was round with an indentation making it possible to notice the object was slowly rotating counter clockwise. The outline was similar to the Star Wars Millennium Falcon except the nose protruding from the craft was much shorter. It then kept flying to the East slowly (60 mph) until it was out of sight. I figure it was traveling 600-700 mph in the beginning, maybe faster because it traveled from the horizon to me quickly, then slowed to 200 mph, then to 50-60 mph. I live near Cable Airport, Upland, CA and have observed many aircraft in my 64 years on this planet so I am not new to airborne craft. I was able to see the outline of several other airplanes that night around the same time, the night was that luminated.

Two weeks later: I saw the same craft coming from the same direction about 400 feet further South, coming towards me. I ran to my back door to get my cell phone (camera) to record the incident and as my hand touched my door handle the object make an 80 degree southward turn, went over trees, and out of sight.
