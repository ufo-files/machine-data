{dr}Two massive stars way up in space silently moved in a circular pattern over our heads as we observed them with binoculars

My husband and I actively watch the stars at night. Last night we saw two massive stars (the size of Venus) way up in space silently moving as if connected, (one giant craft) in slow motion and complete silence. They came from a southwestern direction flying right over our heads and then turning above us in a huge loop and returning to a northwester direction, flying slowly away. We observed them through binoculars. They looked similar to Fast Walkers but much bigger lights and moved in SLOW motion, the two massive lights seemingly connected. We could not see the object(if there was one) in between the two lights, only the lights on each end which were identical to each other, appearing as two massive stars the brightest in the sky moving so slowly you would have to be watching them closely to notice that they were actually moving at all. A wonderful sighting!
