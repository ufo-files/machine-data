{nd} Flew over head late Sunday night.

While sitting on my patio enjoying a bonfire last Sunday night I was looking up at the stars and watching for anything that was moving. Several planes flew far above us and a few satellites were observed. Then as I was scan back toward the North I saw a movement that looked transparent and it neither reflected nor gave off and light. Almost seemed to magnify the stars that were visible and a little distorted through it. No sound was emitted and it was hard to tel at what altitude it was at. It was gone before any of the others could react to my WTF was that statement. It seemed to be in a level flight without any course changes while I observed it. It was gone in about 10 to 15 seconds from start to when it disappeared from view.
