Hovering cube

I went out to empty the trash around 11pm and coming back looking towards the house I saw a dark cube object hovering to the SSW. It had blue and red lights on the edges blinking. I could not determine size or distance. I watched it for at least 2 minutes and it might have been rotating ever so slowly and made no sound. The it zipped away with no acceleration but a visible deceleration like it was looking for something. Visually the cube was about half the former size. I ran into the house to get my camera but it was gone by the time it came out.
