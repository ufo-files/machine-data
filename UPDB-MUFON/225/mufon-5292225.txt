Black, round, solid

This took place at night. The UFO was completely black. It was hovering overhead. I could see the stars around the shape of the UFO but there was nothing seen where the UFO was blocking the surrounding stars. There were about 75 people at this location but I think (only guessing) that only a few people witnessed this. There was a very strong feeling of apathy in the crowd. I spoke with another witness after this happened and he only vaguely remembered the incident. I felt like "I didn't care" when I saw it. It was weird to "not care". It doesn't make sense that people didn't react, but it seemed like everyone just become 'robots'.
