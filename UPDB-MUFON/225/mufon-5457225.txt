Triangulate no sound huge size

I was on a small dredger that I was delivering from Southampton uk to Greece.
Due to a heavy swell running in the Mediterranean we anchored up along with other vessels in Annaba Bay off the coast of Algeria. On was on anchor watch on my own when I noticed this object in the sky off to the left side as it was so huge and unusual I hit the alarm button to wake up the rest of the crew to see this object in the sky. It passed over the other ships at anchor off our port side with no sound at all, it was a huge triangular shape with 2 beams of light going back in the clear dark blue sky and some of it was like looking into a light bulb so bright. We watched this for about three minutes maybe longer then it turned upwards into the sky and just disappeared before our eyes leaving behind in the sky a black huge wide ring which slowly dissipated. We got on the radio to the other ships that were anchored up and got the same reply what the hell was that. I can only say this object was bigger than any aircraft that I have ever seen and even been on. I know what I experienced and I know that it was a UFO and not of this world. 14-9-78

This is the second time I have experienced an unknown aircraft whilst at sea 30-7-1978 very long bright cylinder (cigar ) shaped object that circled our ship about 100ft above sea level. 

I hope all I have said helps.
