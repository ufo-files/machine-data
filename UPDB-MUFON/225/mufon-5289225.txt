Bright ball of light

Driving on a country road I traveled often, I saw a bright ball of light in the sky, at first thought it could be a helicopter or small plane. There were not any of the standard blinking lights on this object. It moved right to left very quickly.. I pulled over to try and get it on video, my hands couldn't hold onto the phone... the bright object which was at about the height of a water tower, then emited a beam of light toward the west (in my direction) it was a cone shape beam. The object hoverd for a cpl minutes pointed the beam toward space and vanished in a second.. no streaking up ward no sound (my window was down) 
As I continued home, I saw weird light formation to north of me further away. This was an incredible experience. 

As a teen walking to the mall with a friend..and we "woke up" walking in an area my friend and I have no idea how we ended up there. It was 2 hours later and dark. No memory except leaving my parents home...I have seen Greys in a person's home but myself have no recollection of any thing.
