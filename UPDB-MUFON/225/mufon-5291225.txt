Flying Saucer Speeds Across West Jakarta, Indonesia

Observed a dark saucer shaped object around sunset in the south west moving at a high speed heading 356 degrees toward the north. It took me 15 seconds to get my camera and the object was almost gone. Could only take 1 fast picture. Object then turned approximately 90 degrees to the right and sped off through the clouds. At its closest point it appeared saucer shaped but very thick in the middle... Its altitude remained at the lowest cloud level all the time.. Conditions was sunset with thunderstorms around most of the area...
