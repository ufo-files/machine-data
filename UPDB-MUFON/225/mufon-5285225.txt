Very bright shimmering, zig zagging in sky

Object was very bright showing many different colors. I thought that it could have been a plane patrolling The Susquehanna River since we have several Nuclear Power Plants near bye, (Three Mile Island, and Peach bottom). We usually see a squadron of A-10 Warthogs that patrol the river in groups of four or six, but here of late when I take my dog out at night I see jets with no sound whatsoever in a triangular formation. This object seemed to stay in one spot then all of the sudden it started moving speraticly then stopped and became brighter. I watched it as it dropped three objects at first they were bright but then slowly dissipated like a shooting star. Soon after that I took my dog inside.
