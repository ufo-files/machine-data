{rjl} Blinding blue light all of a sudden followed by an almost quiet boom, then all power and electricity goes out.

Sitting in my apartment on my bed, surfing the internet. Suddenly through my window I see a blinding (almost neon) white-blue light appear, so bright it seemed like it was daylight outside. Had no idea what it could be. It was only there for a very short amount of time. Maybe 2 seconds before it disappeared, I hear a boom, but it wasn't a very significant one. As soon as this happens, all electricity goes out in my apartment, my watch and clocks reset themselves to random times (mind you I was wearing an analog watch), etc. About 10 seconds later it comes back on, with no trace of the object outside. There is no source of electricity within 500 yards of my balcony, and no transformer was blown. Pretty puzzling.
