8 red spheres. Five in formation 3 by 2 followed by 1/3 larger bright yellow sphere followed by two more another followed a few minutes later by itself.

my wife saw them first, she brought them to my attention. five red spheres 3 by 2 in formation followed by a 1/3 larger sphere bright yellow, followed by two more red ones. a few minutes later a lone red sphere followed by itself. they came from the northeast and turned more than 90 degrees back to the east. no sound. could not tell how high or how far away. not airplanes, helicopters, fireworks or flares. brightness was constant. saw them from my back yard. after turning east they disappeared out of sight.
