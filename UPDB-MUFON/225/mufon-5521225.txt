Three lights in a triangle formation appeared and then one by one the lights disappeared.

I was driving making a left hand turn on Piner Rd in Santa Rosa when my boyfriend said "what is that" and pointed to the sky. I looked up and wax immediately intrigued by a larger triangle formation approximately 300 feet above ground. The lights (three of them) were bluish-white and had a strange glow to them. I knew immediately they were not stars ANC they were definitely not an air plane. There was no sound coming from the object at all. After staring in awe for approximately 2 minutes one by one the lights disappeared.
