{dr}lights over miamisburg ohio

One afternoon in late August of 2004, i was with a friend in Miamisburg, Ohio. I was sitting in a parking lot waiting for her to come out of a vets office, when i noticed a faint flash above the tree tops. I watched for a few seconds, thinking it was just a signal tower due to the color, I went back to listening to the radio. A few moments later i was looking into the trees when i saw the flash again, i looked up and realized the flash was a little to the left of where i saw the first. I starred up over the trees and noticed yet another flash to the right and down. Thats when I realized there where no tower connecting the lights vertically. While waiting I witnessed flashing coming from six different positions, roughly 15-20 seconds apart, this continued for a good 10-15 minutes. It eventually just stopped and I continued watching until she came out and waited a few moments after just to show her, but no lights. Ive tried contacting her a couple of times to get the exact location of the office, and the date, but she stopped talking to me shortly after the event.
