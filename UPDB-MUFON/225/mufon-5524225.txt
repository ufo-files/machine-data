large, bright orb,blinked on and off

Went out in backyard to sat in lounge chair and watch the stars. Looked up and saw large, large yellowish, white orb of light just sitting there. Looked to be half the size of full moon. Watched for a few seconds and it blinked off. In about 3 or 4 seconds it blinked on again. It kept doing this for around a minute.All of a sudden, it shot straight up in the sky and headed toward Columbia at a very high speed and was out of sight in what seemed like a second or two. I am a retired police officer and am still a Constable in Adair County, and I have no idea what this thing was.
