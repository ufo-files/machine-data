{td} A UFO craft traveling through the outer atmosphere like a snake travels through a desert landscape

This was many years ago, yet my mind always comes back to this. I was with my roommate and two other friends and we were smoking cigarettes outside as the sun set. I was the first one to notice it. The Sun had set and Dusk was creeping in. It was comical almost, I saw a snake traveling through the dusk sky. It had four points of light that were transiting the sky just like a snake would slither across the desert ground. Or,how a sea snake would swim across the surface of the ocean. Basically, it was blatantly apparent that this object was traveling through space in a way that I had never ever observed before, and it was doing it slowly. I alerted everyone of this strange sight and only my roommate was able to confirm this sighting. I can only surmise that the other two friends were nearsighted to a certain degree and didn't know it. My other friends lost interest yet I continued to track it for several minutes more. The amazing thing is,I did not lose track of it on the horizon, like you would normally see a commercial airliner do. It disappeared from view far above the horizon, like about 4 inches above. This leads me to believe,that this unidentified craft, while I observed it, was never in our atmosphere. It was transiting space and its direction of travel was outside Earth's orbit. Side Notes: Its movements appeared Organic; Not Mechanical. It appeared alive as though It Itself was a Creature and not a creation; Not a Craft that held Life or Technology. That's my take.
