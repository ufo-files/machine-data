{rjl} White silver mushroom cloud appearing and a white round fireball coming out of it than cloud disappearing with object heading South 2-3 sec than disappearing.

It was around 0300 and I was taking a break from Netflix, I stepped out in my backyard to get some fresh air. The skis were clear and as I got outside I looked up and noticed a bright round object at 90* up traveling from West to East I figured this could be the ISS. I have seen the ISS fly over many times and this object looked very familiar. After it faded into the Eastern sky I was looking back up in the sky at around 80 degrees when I saw a white-silver mushroom cloud open up and an white fireball object materialize from it and heading South. The Mushroom cloud than seem to fade away quickly. The fireball was a steady white color. After 3-4 seconds it dissappeared while heading in the south direction. There was no trail or noise coming from this object. There were no airplanes or aircraft flying in the area either. I am retired navy and have never observed anything like the 2nd object in my life.
