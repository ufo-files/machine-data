{cak} Black triangle shape pulsating white lights at front, blue and red blinking lights on opposite corners

I was leaving the parking lot of Publix Super Market at Abbotts Village Shopping Center at about 9:50 PM on June 12, 2019, when I drove onto Addison Way I noticed an unusually big bright light in the sky. I continued driving until I turned right on Jones Bridge Rd where I saw it again but got a closer look at it and could see that it had a red blinking light on the left side corner and a blue blinking light on the right side corner and then I saw as it continued to approach me closer and closer that the big bright light was actually a series of small white lights in the front of the triangle that were pulsating from right to left and left to right. I pulled over to get a better look at it as it flew right over me and I was expecting to see wings but could only see this triangle shape, that was when I thought that maybe this is a UFO.
