It had three main lights and floated silently, a bright beam came out the bottom.

I was walking to a friends house about 8pm on Feburary of '86. It was snowing and very heavily overcast, with the lights of the town reflecting on the clouds. It was so quiet you could hear the snow falling on the ground and on the branches of the trees.
I was looking down at my feet, watching the ground that I was walking on when for some reason I looked up and saw a light in the clouds above me. I figured it was a helicopter but then I realized that it didn't make any sound because I could hear the snow fall. I stopped to watch it and the lights changed color to a blueish red/white color. I started to move side to side very slightly then a bright light came out of the bottom of the craft. 
I watched it for at least five minutes then it disapeared, I don't know what happened to it. I stood there trying to figure it out, and then I started to walk faster to my friends house. I know it wasn't a helicopter because I grew up on Air Force bases, and then I joined the Army. I have been around military aircraft for most of my life.
It was an interesting experience and it is something that will stay with me forever. From what I could figure the clouds were at least 100 to 150 feet over my head. I know this wasn't any military or civilian aircraft, because it made no noise.
