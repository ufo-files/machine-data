Lights over Prescott.
I am in my spa every morning before dawn.
12/12 at 6:05 There was a stream of 36 points of light moving across the sky equidistant and at the same rate of speed. They came in from the north northwest. They came into view about 5 seconds apart. There may have been a few more that I did not see before the first one.

The wind was calm and there was no sound. They were slightly west of directly overhead by about 2 degrees. They were at high altitude. No vapor trails and no flashing lights. Light was from all directions not just headlights so a reflection from the sun and moon.
