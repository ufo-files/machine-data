Intensely Bright Shere That Fades Quickly and Then Is Gone

I live in a traffic pattern to and from OHare airport, where all planes have flashing lights when traveling above. I saw a faint object moving Northeast towards OHare, no blinking lights, The object got Real bright like a star, I ran and got binoculars and observed what looked like a intensely bright sphere or star and I could see a definate outline(round edge)on the left side of the object. It changed direction moving East, then Southeast, and with a quick fade out, was gone. There was no cloud cover, no sound and stars were visible in it's path. I looked and there is an earleir report today in Texas seeing the same type of bright star like object.This did not appear to be a satelight either.
