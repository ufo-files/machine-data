Two huge orange glowing objects, round in shape. One moving west, other stationary, the moving object "went" into the stationary craft and both instantly disappeared.

South of De Leon, Texas, I was driving my car on Highway 16 South, to go to my home at Lake Proctor. When suddenly, I saw one large orange colored round craft appear to the west of me, and then a smaller orange colored round craft appeared east of the larger craft. Then suddenly, the smaller craft merged with the larger craft and left an orange comet trail when it entered the other craft. When I first saw this happening, I knew it was U.F.O., and not flares, or any type of aircraft. I also looked to see if there were any aircraft around the crafts and there wasn't. I was stunned, because it happened so quickly.
