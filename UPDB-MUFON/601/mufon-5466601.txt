{cak} Four stationary lights in a row above horizon flashing random colors

Just got home and was turning off the lights to go to bed when I noticed out the window in the distance a blinking light. Didn't think much of it until a few minutes later when I let the dog out that it was still there. I watched it for a good 10 minutes. It was randomly flashing red, green, blue, and white. I went inside and got the binoculars and could see 4 lights in a row. The center 2 were randomly flashing colors while the outer lights stayed a dimmer red. I recorded a little of it on my phone and live streamed a lot more on Facebook. After watching it for 30 minutes or so, the flashing got slower and slower, but it did not move. Below the object are the lights from the ski resort on the other side of Little Traverse Bay, so I know it was above that. There was also 2 or 3 times a light shot out from the left side and faded.
