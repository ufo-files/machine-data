Bright blue light surronded house,childrens toys began to move by themselves.Contact was made but brief

Was about 5-7 years old,Bad thunderstorm outside house,Everyone was either asleep or downstairs watching thunder,power was lost.A strange and deep blue light came in from every window.Was upstairs,and noticed some old toy figurines moving by themselves,I was a child,so I thought it was amusing,went into bathroom to urinate,small blue headed being with rod in hand standing by door watching me urinate.I remember his face was sharp looking like his nose was very long or his face was curved in an angle from the nose.Eyes were slanted but not bug eyed.Ball on rod began to glow orange then yellow.And I remember no more.Have had dreams about the incident and this includes seeing a naked blond women on a metal bed,with a tall being standing over her.Head of being was elongated and the being floated,did not walk.Did not want me there.Moved me to another room.memory lapse and I have no more recollection.My experience is very mixed up,have had many other sightings.
