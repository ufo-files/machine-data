surreal, life-changing, dream-like

Coming from my parents house back to my house in waterloo. Headed west. Saw two lights down the road in front of us on 68 township rd. Saw 3 bright lights in the sky. I said to my girlfriend, "they look like ufos, but must be airplanes coming directly at us." Then I said, "it makes no sense for all three to be headed directly at us." Two got closer. We stopped and stared. Then I realized one disappeared, instantly. We drove a few hundred metres until the one was directly overtop/left of us. Maybe a hundred feet in the air. It was hardly moving. I got out to take a picture. I told her to get out; she was balling her eyes out begging me to get in the car. I prayed out loud. I stayed outside until she yelled to look over to the right. Another one was in the tree line maybe 30ft off the ground and 300 metres away. We watched it hover. I got in and drove to cut it off down another road. We lost all three and decided to turn around. On the way back we saw where the one in the trees landed. There was bright lights pulsating in the forest. We then left. I felt quite calm the whole time. My girlfriend was crying, she was overwhelmed. She still doesnt know what to believe, but we both know what we saw. (No drugs or alcohol involved.)
