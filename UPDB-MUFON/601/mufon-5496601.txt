{rjl} ~8 lights traveling across the NM sky

Just a handful of unidentifiable lights moving through the New Mexico sky. Captured 8:23pm Saturday January 17 2015 via an iPhone 6 unedited nor enhanced. I saw them in the sky while I was on my way home. They were orange or maybe reddish at some moments, there were about 8-10 of them moving through the sky North to South but they did not seem to all disappear out of view towards the same point as planes do toward a runway. They were bright lights seemingly traveling together faster than I've seen helicopters. After the initial group disappeared out of view another 3 moved followed.
