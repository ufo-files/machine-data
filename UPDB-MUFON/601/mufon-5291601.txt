On Monday night, October 5th, I was camping at Balnced Rock Camp Ground, near Buhl Idaho. The camp ground is in a canyon with shear 30 to 50 foot rock walls. At 9 pm I observed a pulsating green and reddish/gold light above the north end of the canyon. I got out my binoculars and saw that there were three pulsating lights all moving independently but in the same area. I watched for about 45 minutes as the brightest light moved slowly from the center of the canyon to above East wall of the canyon. The object (or light) appeared to be several miles away but distance was difficult to determine as there was little frame of reference other than the canyon wall. At around 9:50 I grew tired and went to bed.
