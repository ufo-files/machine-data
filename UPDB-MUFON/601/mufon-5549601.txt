High altitude circular object floating across sky

It was around 430am. I had been awake for quite a while. I was getting hungry soI went upstairs to cook some lasagna in the microwave. During the 6 minutes ittook to cook, I looked outside my balcony window as I often do, in hopes of aUFO sighting. It was a clear nightmorning, many stars were visible and therewasnt a cloud in sight. Frustrated with never seeing any objects, I mentallyexpressed something to the nature of if any aliens exist, please show yourselvesnow and I will be very grateful. Lo and behold this circular object veryreminiscent of a star in shape and size, flying much much higher than any planeI had ever seen, begun to slowly dance across the sky. It was glowingwhiteish-yellow, brighter than a star and begun flying in 4 or 5 S formationsfor about 30-45 seconds before it disappeared out of the range of my windowview.During the encounter I was very calm and remained skeptical. However, I wasalmost instantly convinced by the UFOs movements that it could not be a plane ora satelitte.This was my first encounter. Rather uneventful but Ill take what I can get.
