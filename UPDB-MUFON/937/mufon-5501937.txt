{dr}hovering then gone; F15 fighter spoted after

Hiking at Diamond Valley Lake on trails. I thought the object was a helicopter, but it didn't make a sound other than a hum and or swish sound. It was circular in shape and it made a straight almost perfect path down about a 100 feet then up exactly where it started, then side to side. I took several speed frame pictures and the two that came out shows exactly how fast this object disappeared. This happened for about 30 seconds and then it was gone. About ten seconds later a F15 fighter jet came into the area passing over the lake about 10 times; as if the pilot was looking for something. Very crazy set of events for sure. I could not wrap my head around what happened until I saw the photo; I am a serious skeptic when it comes to UFO sightings, but after this I'm starting to believe. When the object took off you can only see a light streak behind it and then it was gone what seemed to be straight up. I would really like to see if you can debunk this, because I am still skeptical and would like someone to explain this logically to me. Thank you for your time.
