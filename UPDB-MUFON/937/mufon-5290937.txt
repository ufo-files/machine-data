It looked as though it was setting on the water of my pond.

I was mowing with a bush hog. I topped the hill bringing my pond into view . And there it was. I stopped the tractor and watched it for about ten minutes. It was just setting there on the water. And then as if somebody flipped a switch it was gone. In the blink of an eye, gone.
