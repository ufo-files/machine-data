{rjl} bright orange light(s) ascending, separating, falling and fadeing.

At 4:40am I was pushing my motorcycle out of my garage when I noticed a orange light in the southern sky that resembled a bright planet / star. It was at least as bright as Venus in the clear predawn sky (brightest object in the sky that morning). Curious, I tried to identify it with Google sky maps on my phone to no avail. That's when I realized it was slowly ascending. I took a short video with my phone then grabbed my monocular out of my truck and when i got the object into focus it was apparent there were actually two identical cone shaped objects. After a few moments they stopped ascending and seemed to move horizontally to my left then one of the objects flickered and faded and abruptly fell/descended to the horizon. Shortly after that the remaining object flickered and faded out of sight.
