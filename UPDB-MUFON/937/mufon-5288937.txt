On the night before the spacex debris was spotted. I was working the night shift and was standing on the loading dock with a coworker, his back was facing a tall building, I was in front of him and could see over the top of the building, I saw something burning falling from the sky, I first said theres a shooting star, he said cool, I didnt say anything as it got closer because I was trying to figure out what it was, it was a big sheet of metal, flames were coming off it, it got close enough that I was looking for lettering on it, I thought thats man/woman made, it looked like it was coming from space, I though that it was part of a satellite that got shot down, the top was all burned and looked like like an explosion hit the top so it was hard to tell what it was, I thought it looked too thick and heavy to be part of an plane, it looked like shiny metat that a rocket or satellite would be made of, it was shaped like a sled, it looked like it was surfing across the sky as it got lower, like the front of it was pulling up, I thought I would hear a crash, it just keeped going until I couldnt see it, I though I would hear news about, it was so dramatic, the next night spacex debris was spotted, I thought it was done on purpose to try and cover this up.
