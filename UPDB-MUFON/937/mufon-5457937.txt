{rjl} Bright Diamond with end trail

I was walking my dog and it was still dark out. I had headphones on so I could only hear my music. I saw a bright diamond shaped object in the sky with a glowing end trail. It was large and was flying the same height as crop dusting planes. The whole object was lit and the end trail matched the light. The diamond was elongated, traveling from east to west, with a dark shadowy oval at the upper west corner. It appeared suddenly, and disappeared before traveling over the horizon. It was a quick encounter and wasn’t traveling too fast. It reminded me of watching a shooting star but large, close to the neighborhood and the shape was like nothing I have seen while looking through UFO images online.
