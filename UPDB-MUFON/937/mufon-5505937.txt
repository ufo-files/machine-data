fire ball like object traveling slowly

whilst traveling home from work I was approaching a junction so I was beginning to slow down when to the left of my view about 20ft in the air was a slow moving object that resembled a ball of fire with lots of different colours omitting from it, my first reaction was what the hell is that and why has it not falling out of the sky because it was traveling so slow, at that point it travelled straight over the top of my car, I observed it through my sun roof at that point I stopped it then disappeared over a hedge, I could not understand how it did not hit the trees that were only about 50 yards away, I got out of the car but could not see where it gone to over the next few weeks I watched the news and the papers to see if there had been any damage to houses or property or if anyone else had seen anything but nothing to this day it has always been on my mind and there has never been able to explain what it was or how it did not hit a building or a tree I don't talk much about it now because no one believes you
