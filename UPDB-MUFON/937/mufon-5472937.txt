{rjl} multi colored object, stationary

We were in the back yard to observe Uranus using binoculars. In the north east sky, which was clear, we noticed a shimmering light. We looked through the binoculars and saw a white, bright light with red and green orbs dancing around it. To the left of the object was what I can only describe as a "jelly fish" like mass that had multicolored lights inside. We observed it for over an hour. We did see airplanes flying below object, but object was stationary.
