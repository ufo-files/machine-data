(GS) dark appeared black boomerang object with round edges

Driving toward Apple Valley on Hesperia Rd as a passenger. noticed a dark boomerang like object in the north/east sky, above Green Tree Liqueur. Struck me really strange, because it was coming toward Hesperia Rd, before it could turn around and go east I took 1 bad cell phone picture of the object. The sun was in the way for the picture but you can make it out if you look. I actually took 3 pictures only 1 shows anything. As we rounded the mountain which is Highway 18 it was gone, but there was a helicopter buzzing around where it had been.
