{jpg} Multiple shots of Eclipse and this showed up in one of them. Not a reflection or camera mistake

This is pretty simple. I was taking shots of the eclipse with an Iphone 7 and not having much luck. I took multiple shots on different settings. At one point I took a photo and noticed a contrail running across the eclipse that appeared to be just be from an airplane. I decided to turn down the "exposure" setting (I think thats what it is) and took a photo with the screen being mostly black except the sun itself. I immediately noticed a very clear rectangular object. I looked up with my own eyes and couldnt see anything in the glare of the sun but the object was clearly there. I was in an open area with nothing above me. Theres obviously something there but I couldnt tell you what it was.
