2 or 3 Jumbo jets connected without wings or (long thin cigarette without the butt)

I was returning home with my 15 year old daughter from her softball practice. We were coming down the incline of HWY 74 east into Lake Elsinore, CA. The view spans the whole valley. Over the Murrieta / Temecula area we spotted an overly large object that was white, was stationary, and long like a cigarette without the butt. I instantly said "look, a ufo". It looked like it was at least 10 miles away. We both stared at the object and giggled uncomfortably. We watched as much as we could given the winding road, however we came around a bend that blinded us to the sighting. When we can came back around the bend it was gone. We both were miffed. I felt flush, kind of faint, because only a few seconds had passed and it was so big and was gone. It would have been somewhere but we couldn't find it. My heart was beating so fast that I wanted to pull over but didn't. I also did not want to alarm my daughter with weird actions. My daughter and I have not discussed it since but I think of it daily.
