{rjl} Appearance of line of statues leading to Mons La Hire

On Google Earth-Moon I looked at a supposed statue on the moon. I then looked and found one evenly spaced far apart leading in a straight line to Mons La Hire, which is a mountain in the region. Each object has the appearance of a large human statue's shadow.
