Tic Tac like UFO caught on camera day time sighting

Between 9:45am - 10:00am I was outside in my backyard on the 3rd of August filming clouds high up in the Northern direction with my Nikon Coolpix P1000. Suddenly an unknown craft was caught in my line of site ascending pretty high and fast towards the North East direction. The craft did not look or move like any man made crafts that I know of and presented no sound or exhaust trail. The object appeared white in appearance and presented no wings either. I found it hard to track and after tracking it for 17 seconds I lost concentration whilst filming and lost it on its ascension.
