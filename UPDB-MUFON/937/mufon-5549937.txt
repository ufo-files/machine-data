Black triangle with rumbling sound over NC.

Two young men on a rural road heard a rumble and saw a black isosceles trianglewith a slightly convex rear side moving overhead. The object was estimated to beabout 10,000 feet in the air and 3-4 football fields in front-to-rear length.Red and white lights were strobing at the rear. A detached blue rod or beam witha white lightat either end was seen at a lower altitude. They observed the triangular objectuntil it was lost from view on the horizon. Duration estimated at 3-4 minutes.
