{rjl} Black isosceles triangle with white lights on both bottom corners. A blue light on one side and red on the other next to the white lights but on different sides.

I was on the bus at around 6:28 A.M. I was on my way to school. I was looking out the window and looked up at the sky. I was viewing the sunrise. Then I saw it. It was black, almost a very dark grey. It was a perfect isosceles triangle. It had two lights on each side of the bottom corners. They were flashing like a plane and had one blue light on one side and one red light on the other.It also had one big white light in the middle of it. It was round, like a circle. When I saw it, it was moving in a straight path and then when I froze it just stopped and started to rotate very slowly. I started to feel very warm, like I had a fever. I tried to get my phone out so I could record it but it started to glitch. Then when I looked back at it, that's exactly when the bus started to move again because we were waiting at a stop light. I couldn't believe what I had seen. Now I can't get my mind off of it. Every time I look into the sky, I see some kind of thing. But these things might just be planes or helicopters, but they always remind me of what I saw that day.ti
