{nd}Traveling south on il rt 1 saw diamond shape craft hovering in sky over cornfield in north rossville il

I was traveling south on IL Rt 1in the north part of rossville il. I was a passenger. It was a a diamond shaped craft hovering over a cornfield with many solid white lights. It reminded me of a Christmas ornament. The exact dates and times are approximate. I saw it hovering motionless for about 5 minutes then we lost sight of it due to moving forward down the road. I did not feel anything different nor did we experience anything out of the ordinary. It could have been something government related, but I don't know as there is no military facilities in the area. It looked to be about 200 ft in the air and about 30 ft wide and 50 ft tall. Again this is only an approximation as there was nothing really else in the sky to provide perspective. I did not see it land nor did I see anything come out or in the craft. It just hovered motionless. Please contact me with further questions.
