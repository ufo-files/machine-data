{cak} I observed an unknown silver object in the sky that did not appear to be a plane or helicopter. it was hovering and I quickly grabbed my camera and took two pictures, then when I looked up, it was gone.

On or about 7/26/17 at about 2:30 pm I happened to look out the window from our sunroom and observed an unusual object in the western sky. It appeared stationary, chrome - like and was shaped like a rounded pancake. It had two trails descending downward from the craft.  I quickly grabbed my camera, took 2 shots, and when I looked up again, the object was gone. I showed various people and my daughter's significant other enlarged one of the photos for me. I felt excited to see something unusual. I didn't know who to contact, therefore until now I have only told/showed family and friends.
