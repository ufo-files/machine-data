{jpg} Two gently moving lights across the sky

After leaving the fireworks on July 4th,2013 at approximately 10:30pm, My uncle and I started to pack up our setup and started walking towards the direction of our car. As we turn to leave, W notice something a bright orange light floating in the sky. I pointed it out to my uncle, who said it was an ash from a firework. as we kept observing it, it turned out to be a gently controlled floating object. Once we got into the car, my uncle noticed another floating orange light going in the same direction as the first. We observed it for about three minutes, because it was moving slightly faster than the first one we observed. According to the information that my uncle collected form the last convention, we discovered that we witnessed a sighting of an orange orb.
