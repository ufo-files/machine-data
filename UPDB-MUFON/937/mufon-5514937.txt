(GS) Hovering lights at CAL vs. WAZU football game

Watching a football game between CAL VS. Washington State on 10/13/2012 and on the 4:08 mark on the 4th Quarter of the game when the "Pac-12 News and Notes" is shown during an injury time out - you will see on both sides of the screens that "we are not alone" - not unless fireworks or lights go sideways and up & down. Quite fascinating!

On the 4:10 Mark - the right side of the screen, you will see the first visual....secondary visual will be seen on the left side of the screen around the 4:15 mark, you will see 2 orbs hovering and see one orb shooting in a high rate of speed.

The video feed can be accessed on the PAC12 Network (via COMCAST Cable).
