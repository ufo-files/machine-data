{rjl} five highly reflective or brightly glowing orbs moving and stopping

I had just stepped out of the backdoor to get something when i heard an airplane so looked up and saw a small passenger jet go over . That is when i noticed what i thought was maybe a chrome aircraft because it was shining very bright, so bright that i could not make out what kind of aircraft. Then i noticed that it was heading toward four very similar objects , one was moving slowly south away from the rest so i focused on the ones that were closer together trying to figure out what they were. As one of them was moving east going behind the trees i went to the front yard to get a better angle. When i got to the front of the house the one moving east continued to disappear behind trees and the first one i saw stopped near the other two then slowly began to go east . So i looked around to see if anyone else was seeing this too , seeing nobody around i thought i need to get a picture of this. So i ran inside and grabbed my wife's phone and when i got back outside there were only two that i could see and when i tried to take a picture it was so bright outside that i couldn't see anything on the phone screen . That is when i heard talking behind me , hoping that someone else was seeing this i turned to look . I saw my neighbors looking the other way so i turned back to keep trying to get a picture but and in the two to three seconds it took me to look behind me and look back they were gone. I then spent the next half hour walking around looking at nothing but clear blue sky. I asked a friend i saw as i walking around and they saw nothing.
