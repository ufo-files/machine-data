{td} Saw craft 2 miles flying at aprox 150 knots at 1200-1500 feet. Flew right past us and on out to the west till it disappeared behind the hills.

In Nov 2014 my wife & I saw a grey metallic sphere about 35-40ft in diameter fly past us on a cloudless afternoon. We had our eyes on it for approximately two minutes, and at its closest it was no more than 1/8 mile from us. At first we thought it was a helicopter, but then realized it was just s sphere, with no visible means of propulsion. It was flying at approximately 1000 feet at around 150 knots. 

It1s flight path was parallel to the Santa Monica Mountains and it crossed the Los Angeles basin. We searched for reports of the it for days afterwards, but it appears nobody else in a city of 8 million had seen it. It was in the radar range of three major airports too, yet we could find no reference to it on the FAA or aviation websites.

We were just remembering it today, and our search led us here, so I thought I1d post to see if we can find out anything more about it.
