White Orb moving west and then blinking red light

I was watching a white orb in the sky yet again. Around the point it would disappear, it did something different. It began to display a red light that would brighten then fade, then brighten again. It had never done this before. It was odd. It might have been a kind of "goodbye" response because I would not see the object again for several nights.
