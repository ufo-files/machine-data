{cak} Yellow orange cylindrical shaped orb Glowing while it moved across the sky

I was taking out the trash one evening on the east side of Austin when I stopped in my tracks. Just overhead a bit higher than the trees was this yellow orange glowing cylindrical shaped object moving steadily across the sky.  It was almost as though it absorbed sounds which is how I noticed it I “heard“ it because of how quiet it was? I know that makes no sense. I was frozen while I watched this unfamiliar object move slowly but quickly and extremely smoothly across the sky. There were no visible propellants and no trail that I could see.  There was a silver or black metal like looking thing connected to the orb similar to how a lightbulb is configured But the ratio of glowing cylinder to the end piece was much greater than the ratio of a lightbulb to its end piece. It moved from east to west and was gone before I could record it.  I have never seen anything like it before or  since .
