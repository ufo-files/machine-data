large craft observed travelling above trees

This was an approximate date. But on this afternoon we were travelling home. In the vehicle was myself along with my mom and little brother and sister. We were on Highway 44 east when we observed a large triangle object just above the trees. It made no sound and was moving very slowly. We pulled over and watched the object as it moved slowly going east. Several other vehicles also pulled over and watched the event. What made this even more interesting is shortly after the craft was almost out of distance several military style helicopters came over chasing the object. We never saw it again or heard anything from local media. After this we began having several odd things happen in the community. We personally had 3 goats that were dissected. Heads were missing, some organ were removed but no blood anywhere. There were surgical incisions made on the goats that were cauterized. People in the community reported to each other of seeing some type of what can only be described as a small person with large head. My little brother even claims to this day he saw one in our house. He stated he got up to go to the bathroom when he observed an alien standing in our living room at the end of the hall. He swears to this.
