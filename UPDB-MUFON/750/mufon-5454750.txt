{jpg} 32 Lights observed (traveling on straight line path, next night 6 lights, next night 22 lights {djs})

On 3/6/2020 I had taken my dog out to go the bathroom. It was crystal clear sky. I looked up and there were lights moving East to West. I think there were 14 at that point. single white lights spaced almost evenly. Now they were higher than planes but I dont think in orbit and moving fast. Around the 15th or 16th came a double light and I swear I could see a very large shape of that one. I ended up counting 32 total. It was 8:15 when I went out and 8:36 when I came in..... Now the next night there were 6 going the same direction. Then on the 7th I went out an hour later due to the time change and I counted 22.... None since.
these were solid light nothing blinking. they all would get to the same point in the sky and vanish.
