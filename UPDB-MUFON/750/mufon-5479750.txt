{nd} Looked like plane with intense orange fire losing altitude heading NE changed to Easterly descended as glow went out

We stepped outside to have a smoke and happened to look up to the Northeast and at fist glance thought we were seeing a fireball meteor. Looking closer it resembled a plane blazing on fire as it descended in the East very slowly like a parachute. Then it leveled out at maybe 500 to 1,000 feet and changed to a more direct Eastern direction. As its descent slowed the light went out. By then we estimate it was 4-5 miles away. We often see military helicopters going in that general direction, but do not know if this is related. We are worried that something may have crashed after it caught on fire.
