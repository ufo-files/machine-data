I noticed a bright colored twinkling light above the mountains behind our house so I decided to get my video camera to see what it was. I was able to video over an hour of the object changing colors and shapes before fading away.

While on my back porch in Jaco, Costa Rica on the night of Feb. 14th 2015 around 7:30-8:00pm I noticed a color changing light above the mountain behind our house and decided to get my video camera to check it out. This object was very different than other stars etc I have observed in the past. This object pulsated and changed a variety of vibrants colors and in some instances a crisp outline of a geometric shape is visible. At around the 40 minute mark something flies in front of the object and either enters it or flies around it etc. Soon after that the object starts to change colors again as well as a wide variety of shapes ranging from an hourglass to the object separating into 2-5 different pieces. The object seemed to descend or dissolve on the other side of the mountain and then was no longer visible to me.
