{cak} Silver (or reflective) sphere

It was a clear day with a few white clouds overhead. I was on the roof of the business I worked at and as I looked at the clouds I saw a silver (or reflective) sphere come (horizontally) from behind a cloud. It stopped and did not move for about five seconds then it went backward (still horizontally) and slipped behind the same cloud that it appeared from. This happened in South Charleston WV around 2001.
