Busy skies above Golden CO

My kids and I witnessed something emerge from the clouds above our house. My first thought was a paraglider, which we have many in the area or even a parachute but no. It was something otherworldly. I have pictures and videos which show something was there but it's not very clear. However, I had a powerful monocular and was able to zoom in with excellent clarity. It certainly wasn't a paraglider, balloons, or anything familiar looking. What I saw blew my mind. It was so bizarre and foreign that I couldn't tell what I was looking at. Two objects revolved around each other while a third smaller one remained stationary below it. When I focused the monocular I saw them in great detail. Like right up close in my face detail. The objects looked alive. They looked biological. The surface was fluid and seemed to morph into different shades and textures. They moved East slowly and then stopped for 5 minutes within a few hundred feet of us. It then move back west and drifted out of sight. After reviewing my footage I noticed two more objects!
