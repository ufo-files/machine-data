{dr}Bright green orb with sides going very fast

I was sitting outside around 10pm on May 1, 2017. I saw to the SE and noticed a bright green orb with sides. It darted to the right and reappeared below then darted to the left and in an instant it was gone. Took around 10 seconds. I immediately thought it was a UFO because of how fast it darted around. I thought to myself that I have never seen anything like that and that it wasn't normal. When the object darted left it was gone after that. No trails, no light, just gone.
