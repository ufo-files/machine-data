Bright planet like light blinking light in the sky. looks like its in Earths atmosphere.

For the last few months from August to now I have been seeing a bright venus sized light, sometimes there are two, in the west and east at night. At first I thought it was helicopter, but it didn't move an inch for over four hours. I was in the military and am familiar with most military aircraft and what can usually be seen over the county of Los Angeles so I ruled out the idea of it being man made. I think it could be a planet but I know a little bit about astronomy and know what planets are in view usually. Ive google star mapped it and nothing shows up, plus it blinks different colors and it is never in the same place. Also the north star is the only star that I know of that doesnt move as the Earth moves. This thing just stays stationary in a different place almost every night. A friend of mine saw it too. He said he saw a smaller light connect with the larger light. I don't have a camera plus it would just look like a planet on video, but I was curious if anyone else has reported this. I think its a either a planet or maybe its the space station, its just sometimes there are more than one and they are not that close to each other. Sorry for bothering you people. Any info on this would be appreciated.
