(GS) 2nd sighting in year of same black triangle object. No noise and no lights. Flies very low and both times saw very well.

I have seen a black triangle craft twice in the past year. First time around Thanksgiving of 2011. But tonight, 12-13-12 @ 0110 I saw the same black triangle fly overhead towards the south east. I was outside taking my dog out before I went to bed and I was looking up like I do most nights, looking at stars (I look at night sky often on clear nights). As I was looking towards "big dipper", I saw something block out a star. I immediately recognized it as the black triangle craft I saw a year ago. The craft is black with no lights. It does however have some grey (maybe white) markings on the bottom in a "V" formation. There is no or little moonlight but plenty of starlight to see craft. Plus its only about 500ft away. Its moving at a good speed... Maybe 100mph?? It stayed in sight for about 15-20 seconds. It made no noise what so ever!! This is the exact same thing I saw a year ago in this exact same location. Only last time it was heading north and it was about 930pm. There is no doubt of what I saw. Both times I watched it infront of me and fly right over me and off in distance. This craft both times flew straight at a steady speed the entire time on both occasions.
