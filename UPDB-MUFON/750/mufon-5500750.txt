{rjl} large disk shape came over the drive-in movies

I took a gal to the movies and into the show management said we were being flown over by a ufo and it was not a hoax. there was a large military and police presence. we then were told to leave in an single file. as we left the military told everyone not to speak about this to anyone.The object was large and disk shape it moved slowly over the place it turned around very slowly as it went from east to west then it went over the aiport tower and hovered then it turned into a white ball of light and a small red light came out of it and flew around very fast. the object then flew strait up and out of sight. I told the gal I was with to look but she was so afaid that she put her head into her lap and cried. I said this was a chance of a lifetime and we might never get another one. It was like to plates on top of each other with atop that looked like a receses turned upside down,it had windows which were lite up and there was beings looking out.the craft was about 50-60 feet in the air.
