{dr}We could not see the actual ship in the sky because it was so dark. But we were able to see the lights on its surface moving in pattern like motions together.

I was at Walmart in Brownwood, Tx around 8:00 earlier today when I noticed bright lights moving around in the distance of the sky. I was looking up at the stars when I first noticed them. When I first noticed them my first thought was, "Holy crap!" There were four lights in a row to begin with and then one at a time they turned off U Too there was only one left at the end. It then shut off to disappear from its spot in another. After repeating this multiple times it shot in a zig-zag way up and other lights appeared once again along with it going in their own patterns. Quickly they faded after that and did not reappear. I was with my family when I saw this. I pointed it out and we all looked. It shocked us all. During the event though we all just stood there quietly and watched.
