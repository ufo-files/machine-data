UFO over farm lands

Just got home from a late dinner around 10:15pm. We live 30 minutes from the nearest restaurants. We are located in a very rural area with gravel roads and no winter maintenance. I was in my room when my husband said "hurry come here, there is something weird outside" my husband is not one to ever suggest UFO'S for lights in the sky. He said look straight ahead, these bright lights caught my eye. I'm not sure what that is but it's rotating or at least the lights are in a circle. After about 20 seconds the lights completely shut off and it was pitch black outside again. You could barely see stars because it was raining shortly before this and cloudy. Then boom, they turned back on and he started filming from his Android note 10+ fully zoomed in. It lasted a bit longer this time and then it turned into one small light quickly and then blackness again. It didn't return. I need to mention that the whole sky near it lit up and glowed an orange/peach color around it. Almost like the sun setting but only in that area and once the lights went out. It was pitch black outside again after a minute or two. It was like the light from whatever it was lingered longer than the object. There was one plane off to the far left of it and then once the object was gone, another plane was flying off to the right of where the object was. The objects lights were one solid white color. I checked the Space-X website and no satellites have been visible in our area and do no react the way this object did.
