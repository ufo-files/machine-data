{cak} 5 silver shiny objects flying in the sky.

I was standing by my car at about 10:30am today. When my 9 year old son and I noticed what looked like a flick of geese flying in the sky in between our neighbors homes high up yet low enough to be seen. We stood there for about 5 minutes watching because we started to notice that they weren't doing usual things like a flock of birds would and they seemed to be hovering around the same spot and moved very gracefully. I noticed when the sun hit one that it shined silver and had what looked like darker sides. They stayed in the same area at first point west in a v formation then would turned to point south and as they turned in the sky one would what looked like glide up to the front from one of the sides of the formation. They did not turn like planes. They did not fly like birds. My son stay fixed on the objects and I bent my head and sneezed when I looked back up they were gone disappeared. I asked my son where they went he said mom they just disappeared. I noticed what looked like kind of static on a television or the little white lights tracing across your eyes when you sneeze hard or cough hard in the sky when I was looking for them. I thought it was because I sneezed so I turned to look at the sky behind me to see if I still saw the static like stuff and I did not, however when I turned back to where the objects were I saw it in the sky again there and so did my son. I can't explain it and I'm not a believer in UFOs but this was by far the strangest thing I have ever seen and I cannot explain it.
