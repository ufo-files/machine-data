2 round objects with green and red lights

I was camping on my families property with cousins just looking out over the lake at the stars I often do this when an object appeared over the lake not directly over it but not as high as an airplane or crop duster it had lights green and red blinking on and off like its going around in a circle then another one appeared not to far from it on the left same shape could have been its twin it began to "shoot" what appeared to be a light beam red deep red in color after that i told my cousins to get in the van and we hid the rest of the night my first thought was a helicopter but there was no noise I believe it was on the front page of the news paper the Sturgis journal if I recall others saw it
