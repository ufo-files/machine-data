A bright light a little after noon followed plane tail hovered for about 2 minutes and then shot to the west

I was in the lake with my girlfriend's family on an innertube and a bright reflective light ( like metal glimmer) followed the tail of a family friend's plane and just hovered there and pulsated almost like it was a spinning glare. It hovered in the south for about two minutes after the plane flew over my head, then it seemed to gain altitude and shoot off to the west in the blink of an eye.
