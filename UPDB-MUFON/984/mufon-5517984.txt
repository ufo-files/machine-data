{dr}walking home an object move across the sky from the western horizon traveling north-east and was lost in the trees

I was observing how thin the moon was and a jet that was flying east, with obvious trails behind it and blinking lights, when I lost sight of the jet an object appeared on the western horizon and moved north-east. for approximately 5 minutes the object moved across the sky before it was lost from view and obscured by trees. it had no blinking lights, made no sound and had no obvious wings, unlike the jet. I was not scared, but fascinated. I spent the afternoon telling my brother about previous experiences and then joked that they were listening to me from my implant from a childhood abduction, which he remembers as well. my niece claimed to see a ufo while we were discussing it. I don't like to sound paranoid, but I feel I am being observed.
