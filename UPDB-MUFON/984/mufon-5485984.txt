I went outside and looked up trying to find the big dipper then I seen the largest aircraft I have ever seen and it had no wings and no blades like helicopter and it made no noise at all and did not leave a trail. The headlights were white with red tail lights and it looked modern. I was not the only person who seen it I was accompanied by two others none of us had any alcohol and none of us were on prescription medication. I initially thought it was a secret military aircraft but upon hearing no sound and realizing it didnt have wings and we dont have any aircraft that large i realized this has to be other wordly. It was rectangular in shape and had a rounded front end. The object flew a slow straight line heading west.
