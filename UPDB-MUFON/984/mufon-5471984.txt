Driving North on Hwy. 51 in DeForest, WI around 5:10 a.m. 11/28/2017. Saw object I thought was landing plane since I was about 5 miles directly north of airport. The "plane" appeared in a "fore-shortened" context, i.e., squarish. It was no more than 1/4 mile West from me. I thought it was quite low in descent for the distance from the airport. It did not appear to be moving, which I thought was due to my angle of sight and the fact that I was driving in the opposite direction. After a few seconds, there still was no movement. I lowered my window to get a better view, trying to see if it was a tower. The "object" I saw mirrors the 2/11/2016 sighting in Verona, WI, Mufon Case 74442, including the time of day and location in the Madison, Wisconsin periphery. The one exception is that I saw no blinking white light. What I did see was a squarish dark object about 60 ft. diameter with red lights on corners. It was difficult to see against the black sky. It was hovering about 100-200 feet up, and continued to hover after I drove past. The location was easy to identify since it hovered directly south of a large distribution-center type facility. I also saw a couple bar-like extensions on the ground side that reminded me of antenna. Driving home that afternoon, and on my way to work the following morning, I scoped out the area to see if I had viewed anything in the landscape. It was empty.
