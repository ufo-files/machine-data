Five minute earthquake with shaking house, loudest noise we've ever heard and white lights.

At 3:26 am we were all woken with an extremely loud sort of grinding and earth opening up as you would see in a movie sound. The entire house was shaking and this lasted for approximately five minutes. One of our daughters who is 11 came running to our room scared and sat on the bed waiting until it was over. We could see bright lights outside. The two dogs kept barking and crying. This morning my two neighbors whom I spoke to individually said the following:

The first one stated that to him it sounded like huge tanks driving through and his house shook. He said the sound was unusually long as well.

The second neighbor says she was woken as well with lights and shaking house and noise but that her daughter who is 12 saw the lights traveling in the sky from east to west. But that she had heard on the radio that this was some kind of lighting.

For us (myself, my wife, and kids) it felt like an earthquake as we have experienced it before. We are seeing many people on Facebook that reside in the area that are reporting the same and convinced of an earthquake.

There is no way lighting cause five minutes of lights traveling with shaking houses and grinding earth-opening noises.
