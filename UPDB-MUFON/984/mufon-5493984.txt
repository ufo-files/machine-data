{cak} abduction by greys

When my mom was 7-8 years old,she was living in the Mountain Park/High Rolls area of New Mexico, she had a dream that these little grey beings took her and were doing things to her. She woke up screaming and my grandmother came running into the room. My mom told her that the little men took her. My grandma told her it was just a dream and to go back to sleep. My mom had dreams about the little men for years until they moved to Arizona when she was about 10. The crazy part is that she was haunted by their eyes because the were silverish and their body's were smaller than their heads. My mom was born in 1930. It wasn't until the book Contact came out with the Grey on the cover that her childhood dreams came rushing back to her. The area that she lived not very far from Roswell NM.
