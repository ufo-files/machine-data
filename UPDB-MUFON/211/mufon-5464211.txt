{tjd} Bright blue object observed moving quickly while taking photos of sunset

While taking my dog for a walk in the park across the street from my home in Black Rock, CT, I decided to take continuous shots with my iPhone camera of the beautiful sunset. Upon review of my photos, I saw a bright blue object darting around the sunset in a different spot in each photo, all of which took place over a few seconds time. Magnifying the object shocked me. It is clearly a disc type shape.
