12 big orange lights moving in the sky.

My husband, myself and two friends were leaving to go to the fireworks around 8:15 July 4. We saw 12 orange lights low in the sky, looked like they were shooting into orbit one at a time, and then slow down hanging out together. We left...knowing we were seeing ufo's but went on over to the fireworks area in the neighborhood. We had just arrived and walked down for the best view and one orange light came over the trees from the South and went straight over us, and then another and another. They would get a ways over but still up over us and would go out. Then we noticed that their was a large black shadow floating and we thought at first it was soot from the fireworks but it was a flat object that floated to the north like the balls. We then realized that the orange lights would go out and would turn into the flat black undiscernable shape object and had lights blinking on and off around in a circle around the outside of it. Every light would go out and float away the same way. We left the fireworks to try and get the binoculars and see it closer but they were gone.
