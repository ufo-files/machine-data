A line of 6-7 tiny lights flying south east

There was a like of 6-7 tiny lights flying southeast. They were equal distance apart. I observed them while looking straight up at the moon then realized there was a light. I followed the lights in both directions and realized there were more. They were not flying very fast. I saw them for a couple of minutes before they were all completely out of sight.
