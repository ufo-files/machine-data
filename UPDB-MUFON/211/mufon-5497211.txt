{rjl} Bright pulsating white and changing to blue green rose above treeline then rose upward stopped then quickly accelerated leaving a trail of light

While camping alone on a hilltop in the allegany mountains near salamanca N.Y not far from the P.A. border, my campfire had died down and I was about to retire for the evening. I noticed a bright white light downhill from my position aprox.100 to 200 yards away, I at first thought it was a bright spotlight the kind people use to spot deer at night, but then it began to rise slowly until it was above the tree tops it paused above the trees and changed from white to a blue green then back to white. It then accelerated at a tremendous speed and dissappered leaving a trail of light behind it it was difficult to judge the size not knowing its true distance from me and being so dark but holding my thumb up at arms lenght it covered the object I could not make out its form but it appered to be round that was the last I saw of it.
