Circular or square object, silent, orange/red light on center bottom, frisbee type flight path.

Watching fireworks in NE Minneapolis, and can say, without a doubt that myself and 3 others witnessed an unidentified flying object on July 4th at around midnight. It was a single object dark grey in color, round, silent, with a red/orange light on the bottom center flying in a frisbee like path through the sky. I know it was not a helicopter or airplane as both were in the sky at the same time and had no resemblance to this object. This object was silent and smooth moving. Unexplainable. We watched the object as it flew from the Northeast to the Southwest, directly over our heads, almost as if flying toward the moon then veered to the East and descended to the horizon out of sight. Its flight path can best be described as like that of a frisbee, arching through the sky. The object flew at around 500' or less.
