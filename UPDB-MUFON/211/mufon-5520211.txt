We saw a large jet leaving a double condensation trail. A brownish metallic sphere hovered near the jet, then descended downward at an acute angle. I lost track of it after following it for about 30 seconds.

The sky was clear. It was about 5:30pm. A large jet with a dual condensation trail caught my attention above. I immediately noticed a brown reflective sphere near the jet. It hovered for a few seconds - enough time for me to shout to my roommate and say "What is that thing near the jet!?". My roommate also looked up and saw it. The sphere then began to descend at a sharp angle, almost like a projectile. We followed it with our eyes for about 30 seconds. We then lost track of it and could not find it again.
