{cak} Three lights in a triangular formation remained motionless and then disappeared

Before school, I was on the bus and as we were picking someone up, i glanced out the window and noticed the odd formation of lights. At first, I assumed they were stars. On closer inspection, i abruptly changed my mind. Stars do not usually form such a pattern or appear that close to earth. The three lights were very bright and emitted an orange-yellowish light. Another reason why is dismissed my star theory was that stars are not typically so low in the sky. The three lights remained there for maybe 5 minutes and then I pulled my phone out to snap a picture and when I looked up, they were no longer there. It was still dark outside and I thought maybe I was being silly and perhaps they were just odd stars. I pulled out my phone's compass and noted that the lights were coming from the west. I tried to find the lights again for another 30 minutes but i could not find them. This led to my conclusion that I saw some kind of UFO.
