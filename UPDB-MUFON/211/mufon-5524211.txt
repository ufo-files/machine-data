Sphere was noticed during High School Football game hovering just above trees

Was at my sons football game, seated on the home side of our football field facing south, for some reason I noticed this sphere just to the west end of the field to the left of the goal posts hovering in the tree line acorss the road that runs along the stadium to the south (FM 1644). It was if it was watching the game too. I know it was there for a good time because I would watch the game for a bit and look back and it would still be there, then some good play would happen and I would go back to watching the game. The next time I looked it was gone. So it was there for about 10 min. I know others were aware of it but didn't make much to do about it and egnored the object. This is not my first incouter and was in the Air Force for a few years and know the capabilities of our aircraft and this was not our aircraft.
