{cak} Fireball clouds that dissapeared revealing fast moving object that changed directions

Driving home during lunch break and I seen a small and short plume of smoke way up in the sky. High elevation. Looked like a meteor. The time was 12:30PM, clear and sunny. No other clouds in the sky except the falling plume of smoke. I then seen an object falling which reflected in the sun. Immediately the object stopped and flew straight across in another direction. The smoke instantly disappeared. Not like a jets emission but more like atmospheric smoke from a fireball! It was gone! The object that was flying left a small ice? trail behind and in a blink the entire object was gone. 
A few minutes later there were a couple Jets (High altitude) flying in the general area. 

I have not heard any news reports as of yet. Anyone else see this?
