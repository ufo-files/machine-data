two cone flight over chita city

1. I was in my apartment and sometimes looked out the window.
2. I saw two slowly moving object. A few weeks ago I've seen such objects, but I had no camera with me.
3.4. The objects were like inverted cone without a top. Bottom was glowing red or orange. Objects moving one behind the other on a small distance between them with a constant speed.
5. I freaked out, took the camera and took a few shots (canon 40d\tamron 70-300mm).
6. The objects disappeared behind a neighboring house.
