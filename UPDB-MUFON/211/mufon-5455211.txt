My husband and I left Cody’s Restaurant on Hwy 19 to return home. I was traveling down Powell Rd. near to Chocachatti Elementery School. Noticed three lights in the sky but thought it was a plane since there is an airport nearby. I continued to watch them because something was different, they did not seem to be moving. As we continued to go up Powell Rd we came near a housing area on the left with power poles and houses. I looked out my window and up to see a massive black triangle shaped object hovering over this area near the road. Three lights appeared to blink at the front of object. Two were white or bluish white and a yellow one. My husband who is on the passenger side of the backseat asked what I was looking at. I asked him, “you don’t see that in the sky?” He said, “all I can see is the top of the car inside.” He laughed at me when I said it was a UFO. We were still traveling on Powell Rd, so I turned my car around to prove to him what I had seen, but it was gone. I have no corroborating evidence, only my word.
