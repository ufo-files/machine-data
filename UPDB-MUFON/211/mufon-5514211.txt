First looked like a shooting star then stopped then three other objects circled around it. Then they all formed a triangle pattern and continued to circle. Twenty minutes later they disappeared and a brighter object was seen at the center of the triangle, this object began to move back and forth then proceeded to move in a triangle pattern. Later on an airplane passed above releasing a smoke like substance and clouds appeared to be blocking the night sky.
