A large fleet of unknowncraft traveling in a line (over 150)

Had just arrived home and stopped at my barn to tend livestock. Looked up to sky and saw a line of lights traveling from southwest to northeast. The lights were steady and brighter than planets in the night sky. It took about 4-5 minutes for each craft to traverse the sky horizon to horizon. There were so many that they disappeared in the distance to the Southwest all the way to horizon to the Northeast. Tried to capture video on phone but don't know if it captured sight under low light. I jumped back into vehicle to head to house to tell wife. It took about 3 minutes to do so, and we went out back of house to look into the eastern sky but saw nothing. After a minute, we thought to go to the front (facing the northwestern sky and and saw the line of craft. Most were evenly spaced or nearly so, but a few were closer together. No sound was evident, unlike jets, which would have have some sound. My wife counted 42 craft before the line ended. By our estimates, the number of craft would certainly have been at least 150 from just the view we had of the line of craft we saw, but didn't see the beginning of the line of aircraft.
