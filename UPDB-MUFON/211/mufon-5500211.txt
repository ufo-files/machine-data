{cak} Cone-shaped object in NW night sky with colored lights around bottom, white lights flashing on top.

Standing out in my backyard waiting for the dogs to have their last potty break before bedtime, looking northwest. Something catches my eye and I look up, thinking it might be a shooting star. It is not. It is not moving through the sky, but it is not still. It looks to be cone- or triangular-shaped and there are colored lights flashing around the bottom of it. The upper part just appears to be flashing bright then dim. I watched it for a few minutes, trying to determine if it was a case of double vision, but it was not. It was not moving like an aircraft of any kind, and did not look like the death throes of some light-years-distant star due to the colored lights at the bottom. I watched for several minutes. Did not go in and get my camera or phone as I was pretty sure with the distance and being dark, it would not show up. This is not the first time I have seen something similar in this same general direction. I finally just went in and went to bed.
