Slow moving glowing disc.

Object appeared in the upper right corner of two different photos that I was taking of clouds and sunset. My analysis would say that the object moved on a line from northeast to southwest at a rather slow speed, parallel to a line of clouds, and did drop slightly in altitude. A third shot take approximately 30-40 seconds later showed that the object had disappeared, perhaps into the cloud it was moving next to? 
Important note - I did not see the object until after I took the three shots and noticed it when I viewed them later.
It appears to be a glowing disc shape. Possible dome. Very difficult for me to access its size. I detected no unusual sounds at the time I was taking the photos.
