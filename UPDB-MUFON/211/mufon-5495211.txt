I was staying with my uncle in northeast Ohio it was the early morning around 2am of September 13th. I had awakened to the sound of one of my uncles dogs growling and slowly walking across the room towards the kitchen. As soon as I had gotten adjusted to the dark and my sight had fully come to me I realized that there were 3 figures between 7-9 feet tall standing by the window in-between the living room where I was sleeping and the kitchen. They were solid bright white with a feminine presence long white hair with long white gowns going to their feet. They just stood and looked at me and I was completely paralyzed by fear. This went on for about 10 minutes before I closed my eyes tight and when I had opened them up the figures were gone so I went back to sleep. I believe fully that I saw extra terrestrials that night. Why they were watching me I do not know but I feel that they will be back.
