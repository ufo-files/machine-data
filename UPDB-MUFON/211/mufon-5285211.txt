I noticed a bright flash, it dimmed and became bright again, then another appeared. They both disappeared together.

I am writing this approx 30 minutes after the event. 
I was working on silkscreens. I put them in the window to use the sun as a backlight. (Sitting inside, facing sunset)
I noticed a bright flash in the sky so I looked. It was very bright white and at first I assumed it was light reflecting from a plane, but as it dimmed slowly and then became very bright again I thought it was odd- it was stationary and barely visible when it wasn’t super bright. Perhaps too bright to have been reflecting from the sun..
Then another light appeared from nowhere. To the northeast of the first one, not too far away- maybe 200 yards. Appeared to be at the same altitude. 
They both became bright in unison and then faded rather quickly into nothing. 
I watched the sky for a few minutes after that and saw nothing. 
Side note; this is not my first sighting- but the silkscreens I was working on were replicated crop circles. I’ve personally noticed an improbable amount of serendipitous events while focusing on crop circles. I’ve been working on this project for two days and other, less exciting, but unusual things have happened. And also at other times.
