Observed 3 orange lights above my work at 8pm.

On November 4th 2020 at around 0800 pm I saw 2 orange lights in the sky. They were moving in direct sync with each other and one was following the lead craft. At one point one of the craft faded out of view almost like a candle phasing out slowly. Then shortly after the other craft faded out in the same way and the same spot of the first craft. I radioed for my mom on my 2 way communicator and she came to where I was. I told her what I had just seen and she called my dad about it. While she was on the phone with him a third craft appeared from above the tree line and moved in a slightly variate flight path from the original 2 craft. It flew very slowly in a straight line and then like the first 2 craft, it also phased out of view. Before the third craft went away I did manage to get a video, further documenting my sighting.
