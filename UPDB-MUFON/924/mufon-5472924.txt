2 triangle shaped orange lights flying in a coordinated manner, first quickly, then paused

I was in my back yard in Oakville, Ontario at approximately 8:20 p.m. on October 20, 2017. I was out trying to spot meteorites from the Orionids, so I was already looking skyward. A meteor had just transited the sky from west to east. Suddenly in the southern sky, two triangle or diamond-shaped orange (very orange) lights transited toward the east, moving faster than an airplane, slower than a meteor. They appeared to be low in altitude, approximately 30 degrees above the horizon. Initially I thought it might be an illuminated banner being towed, but it stopped suddenly and did a couple of direction changes very slowly, then disappeared from my view behind trees and neighboring houses. I held up my hands to measure the distance between the two lights. With my arms extended fully, and the tips of my thumbs touching, they appeared about as far apart as my extended little fingers. That measures about one foot apart at full arm1s length. The entire 1flight1 I observed was less than 30 seconds.
