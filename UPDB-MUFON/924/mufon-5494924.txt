we were spending a week at a hunting camp i saw movement out the window well after dark so i went outside & saw a ufo hovering about 40 ft over me. we had previously seen several before in the sky. this ship defied gravity as there was no noise & no air movement. I have flown in the us air force. Blue &white lights flashed accross the front of the craft. They turned on a dark blue light on me which froze me in place and all i could move was my eyes to follow what they were doing. A door evidently opened on the other side of the craft and they turned on a light blue light from the craft to the camp and two human figures man & woman dressed in full tights with only there eyes showing drifted down this light to the second floor of the camp and went right thru the solid wall. they came out carrying my son and took him back to their craft for one half hour i guess before taking him back to his bed. the craft took off with no noise no heat but like the speed of light.   I went inside to check my son and could not wake him up but he had a small surgury under his penis My sons personallity changed a great deal after this to where i even wondered if we got our own son back. I saw a landed ufo a year later and saw occupants outside the craft and i know how & where they hide to land rest and relax & even caught one sleeping. For the above reasons i have never told anyone before not even my ex wife or my son. There is a lot more to this story that cannot be told here and my wife believes i could face danger for trying to get the truth out. The woman that lives in the home where my son was abducted was also abducted years later and has told me she is willing to talk to someone investigating it.
