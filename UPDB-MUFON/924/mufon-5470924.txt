Stepping out of my house one afternoon, I was headed to my shop which is across the driveway about 75 feet away.. I do woodworking so for me to be home is fairly common. I remember it was a cloudy day a little rainy or very damp. I love living where I do and often when I step outside I look around. That day I looked up and I saw a black triangle flying over my house fom south west to north east. No sound, no lights, no markings , no exhaust trail. Given the cloud ceiling I guess at about 3000 feet I would say it was smaller than a single engine plane and larger than a drone. I saw it and said to myself "that's odd I wonder what it is" it disappeared into the clouds and that was it. Only in the last couple of years have I been following , for lack of a better way to explain the ancient alien stories. I,ve always been interested but when I put two and two together I now look up everyday.
