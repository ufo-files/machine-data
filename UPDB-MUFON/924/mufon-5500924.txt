I was waiting for coffee to brew on my porch around 5 am when it was still very dark. I saw what I thought was a firework at first, but it glided slowly up for a few seconds going south and then arced lazily down behind a treetop and winked out like a switch had been turned off. It was a very bright green orb that seemed to glow. I didn't hear it make any noise, but there was a garbage truck emptying dumpsters nearby so I couldn't really hear. I tried to match something in my head up with what I saw but I have never seen anything like it before. I was and remain a little freaked out and very curious.
