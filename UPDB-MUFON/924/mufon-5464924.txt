{cak} Bright white orb like crafts, with trails behind them. One changed directions

We dropping my youngest kid off at cheerleader practice and my oldest said she saw "little rockets" moving through the sky. At 1st I thought what is this child talking about, it's probably a jet or maybe a comet or something. Once we dropped my daughter off and I hit a open stretch off of Pennsylvania Ave (Maryland side) I saw what she meant, and it definitely wasn't a jet or comet as far as I know. There were about 6 of these bright white orbs for lack of a better word, with "vapor" trails in toe. They moved at a steady pace and some seemed to be going faster then others. I stared as long as I could while driving and it seemed like there were more. Once we got home we stood in the yard and just watched to see if any would pop up, and sure enough 4 more did (when I started recording) this is when I noticed the pulsing and disappearing completely light of this object. One we watched was a lot lower than the rest, and I swear it completely dissappeared, while we saw one change directions! We have zero clue what this could have been, also where my kid cheers at is a mile or so from Andrew's AFB. But I live in DC & we spotted them there as well. My oldest got scared & I don't blame her.
