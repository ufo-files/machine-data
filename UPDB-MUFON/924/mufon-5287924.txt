Formation of 10-12 silent low flying glowing tic-tack shaped orbs

My sister and I spotted a Formation of 10-12 silent low flying glowing TicTac shaped orbs flying toward us as I was driving west on Covington St. I pulled over and had my sister take a picture. I got out of the car to get a better shot but once out of the car they had already flown overhead veered north beyond the trees out of sight.
