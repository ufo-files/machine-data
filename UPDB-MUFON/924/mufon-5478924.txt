A round object with bright white light passed over our car.

We were in the family car on our way back home in Khouzestan region of Iran. My father was the first one to notice the round object with a bright white light beaming over the car. I was about three years old, so I do not remember the event, but my mother has described the scene to me. The object flew overhead in a straight path fairly fast. My father called out that it was a ufo, which flew over head.
