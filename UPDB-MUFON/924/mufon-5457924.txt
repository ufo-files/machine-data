Very slow track S to N with bright light and incredibly unusual noise!

We were playing football on our local playing field where some of us noticed a very bright light in the far distance at dusk. About an hour passed and we noticed this light was slowly getting closer to us and was perfectly in line with us on the field. After about an hour the light had travelled about 5 miles approx to our location where we really began to pay attention to it as we noticed that is completely silent, had a very bright light and was clearly of a fair size. 

As the light approached us on the field it became stationary above us; the object then turned on a very very bright spot light which, no word of a lie, was completely focussed on us! The light shone on us for maybe 1 minute, nothing else happened but we simply stood there, frozen in awe but also completely dumbfounded. Then the object made the most unusual noise (was completely silent up to this point!) before switching off its light and then began moving off on a perfectly straight track, the one it was on prior and then took probably another hour to disappear from view. 

A genuinely incredible experience and one we’ll never ever forget!
