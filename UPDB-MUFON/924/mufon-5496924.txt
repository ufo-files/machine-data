{nd} Glowing orb/ufo hovered in sky for 40 min

I was driving my mother back to Vegas from Southern California, about half way/ near barstow CA, my mom started looking out the window and complaining about something really bright flashing in her eyes. A few minutes went by and she kept shading her eyes with her hand so I asked her what she was doing. She told me that there was a bright ball in the sky and the reflection was so bright it was bothering her eyes. I was driving and tried to look but the roof of the SUV we were in made it impossible to see the object. I told her to take out her camera and take a few pictures and I would look at it when we got back to her house. She snapped 4 pictures then put her phone away. For the next 10 - 20 minutes my mom still kept saying that this thing was just hovering in the air and not moving at all. She finally stopped mentioning it and by the time we got to her house I had completely forgotten... I was in the process of moving and had so much to do. Now I wish I would have looked at her camera right away. A few days later she brought it up and reminded me of the photos on her phone. I took a look and was dumbfounded.
