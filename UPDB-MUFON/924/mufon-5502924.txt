{nd} Saw a triangular UFO with three lights that blinked, fizzled, and faded at alternating paces. Flew in a circular pattern before disappearing in northeast direction.

Was out in my yard around 8:30 PM on January 26th, 2014 when I saw a large triangular shaped UFO flying in a circular pattern around a general area of what was probably a few miles. When I first saw it, it was in the northeastern area of the sky around Atlanta, but it flew around in such a large circular pattern that I'd eventually lose sight of it before it would appear again. I first assumed it was a stealth bomber making a test flight, as Georgia is home to many military bases, but that thought disappeared one it broke from its previous flight path and started rapidly descending, ascending, and turning in such a way that I have never seen a human pilot be capable of doing. Soon enough it had what appeared to be a fighter jet trailing it around, but this only lasted a few minutes before the jet flew away from the object. The object flew around in its circular pattern for around forty minutes, with the jet trailing it only once it broke from its flight path. It kept flying erratically for another 15-20 minutes before it disappeared back into the general area I originally saw it from. My grandmother also came out to see the object, but only through a window, and she has finally asked me to send a report in to someone we hope may know about this strange occurence. Any help is greatly appreciated.
