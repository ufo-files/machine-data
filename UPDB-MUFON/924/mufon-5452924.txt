Dark grey orb that flew over me and my young son and headed North East.

Me and my 9 year old son had two sightings from two different times. About two weeks apart. I believe this sighting was of the same craft each time. I was outside looking over my fence which was facing west. I noticed it was the same craft as our sighting before. It was dark gray and about 500 ft up. It headed towards my house and as I ran inside to get my son I grabbed my cell phone to try and take pictures. We ran back over to get a picture of it and by that time it was almost over our heads. It didn’t change altitude and kept going steady in a straight line and headed east. I was able to snap a few pictures of it with my iPhone X. I then decided to record it and by that time it was over the woods behind our house and was headed NE and seemed to be a bit higher up. I was successful in recording it for about 40 seconds. In my video you can see the craft from the top right of the screen and then I reposition it, and at about 15 seconds into the video you can see it going in and out of the clouds from the middle of the shot.
