{cak} Heard humming. Saw craft through window. Craft hovered in straight-line path through yard.

I was about 15 years old, sleeping in my first-floor bedroom in my family home. I was awakened by a loud hum, getting louder. It was about 11 p.m. I sat up in bed and looked out the window, which was open, toward the road. I saw blinking lights, yellow/orange, red, and green. I climbed out of bed and stood briefly by the window. The object was moving slowly from east to west along a point parallel to the road, but, it seemed to me, closer to my house than the road -- about 25 feet from the house and about four feet above the ground. The object was humming loudly and blinking yellow/orange, red, and green lights, and traveling very slowly (about 10-15 mph). I at first thought it was farm equipment or an emergency vehicle, but then I realized it was very late, the lights were something I had never seen, and the object seemed to be hovering and was moving slowly parallel to the road but not on the road. I became frightened and crouched down behind the window sill, still looking out. The object continued at a steady pace on an east to west course, blinking and humming, and it appeared to be hovering about four feet above the ground between the trees in my front yard but not on the road. It didn't diminish pace or stop flashing, but slowly receded into the distance. The hum receded, too. The object was between 7 and 15 feet in length, but didn't have a clear shape.
