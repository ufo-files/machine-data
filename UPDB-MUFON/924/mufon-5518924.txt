I was outside having a smoke staring at the sky as usual. It was a clear day and I could actually see more stars than usual. All of the sudden an object appeared in the sky. It had a light blue glow and flew at an incredible speed due west. Then it made a sudden dip and disappeared. It was amazing and it was my first sighting of something I could not explain.
