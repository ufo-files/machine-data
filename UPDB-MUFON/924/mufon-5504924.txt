{nd} Very bright star like object below cloud cover

I was driving to work with my father when we both witnessed a pure white light in the sky. My first thought from a distance was it was a star or helicopter. As we approached closer, it was moving quite fast and below the cloud cover. Clearly it was not a star. It was also flying and very erratic speeds. A few times it would disappear behind hills then reappear. Changed direction from a north east location to a due north location. It moved way too fast to be a helicopter, and had no directional lights. It was just a pure white ball of light.  Unfortunately, it was too dark for a photo and we were on Rt. 28, so stopping was not an option.
