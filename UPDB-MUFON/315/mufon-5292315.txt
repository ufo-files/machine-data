Bright light that wasn't a plane.

It started as a stationary bright white light. I asked my girlfriend if she saw it and she agreed, so I ran inside and got my binoculars to see if I could see it better. When I got outside it moved from the north to south, took a 90 degree turn west then vanished. I was able to get a glimpse with my binoculars and I could clearly see that it was one solid light emitting from a dark metallic craft. I wish it would have not vanished so i could make out more. I know where the airport is and the path a plane would take to get to and from there. And there was no way this craft came from the airport or going to it. I don't know of a plane that can take a 90 mid flight nor a helicopter that can be seen from so far away with such a bright light, not to mention disappear.
