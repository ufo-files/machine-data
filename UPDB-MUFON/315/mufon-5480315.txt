{cak} Squiggly white lights that at times left long trails

My friend Shane and I we sitting on his tailgate. The sky was clear. I was just looking up at the stars when I noticed some strange lights way above us. I brought this to Shane's attention and so we watched. It looked like a couple white lights squiggly get in all different directions. I was mesmerized, but frightened. I immediately thought UFO. So did Shane. We were both pretty shaken by the sight. I'm in my 30s, he's in his 40s and neither of us have ever seen anything like this before. At some points while these lights were moving erratically, the trails on them were bigger than some constellations! I'm still freaked out! Shane's relative in Virginia or Maryland (can't remember) said he say it too. We've been talking about it since then, so I went on Google to see if there was any other sightings. Nothing yet. I just want to know if anything has been documented or any type of event occurred on that day.
