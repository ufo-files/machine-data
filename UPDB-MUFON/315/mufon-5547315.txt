Black cigar shaped without wings or tail.

At approx: 12:30 PM I was standing by the drivers side door of my 2000 ford Explorer and was looking up into the sky when I observed a Long Black object in the break of the clouds. It resembled a cigar shape but I didn't reconize it as anything I had seen before. The object was traveling North between Philadelphia, PA and the Blackhorse Pike in Turnersville, New Jersey at approx: 10 miles per hour and at a 15 degree angle. I observed the object for approx: one (1) minute. Then the object disapered suddenly. The were no planes in the air at this time. This event happen so suddenly that I didn't have time to get my camera or binoculars.
