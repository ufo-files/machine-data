{rjl} Bright reddish lights, two together one a distance behind silently moving across the sky before disappearing one by one

Today is Easter 2014 me, my wife and two of our kids age 12 and 13 were in our backyard. I was smoking talking to my wife on one side and my kids were about 40 feet away talking. My son says "Dad look at the red lights", so I walked over and observed a pair of red lights flying together maybe 1/8 mile apart but in formation. There was another identical reddish light lower and maybe a mile behind. The front pair crossed the sky at the speed of a fast plane before one just disappeared. Then the one following was gone and finally 15 seconds later the last one disappeared. There was no sound.    We thought they might be airplanes because we live near a small airport but we have seen all lights and know the sounds all the different planes, jets, and helicopters make around here. While talking about what we had saw another two just appeared from nowhere and seemed to be coming in our direction. I saw another one a second later way behind and to the left. The main one in the center got bigger as if it was getting closer still with no sound. I thought to myself we're gonna see it up close and it'll be an airplane, but soon after that it disappeared . We were all like "what the hell did we just see?" We talked about it for a while ,maybe five minutes later I was pointing and talking to my son when we both saw a bright white blink of light in the same direction of the first pair we had seen. Just a quick blink but very bright kind of like a LED light. We were like what the f...?!!! So a total of 6 reddish orange objects.. my wife says more orange... and one weird flash of light where there were no stars or planes.
