(GS) 2 pars of 3 RED glowing objects in a line with eachother, blinking and moving as if they were turning upside down. Saw the first set of 3 they pulsated on, 1 at a time followed by the next 3.

My girlfriend and I were traveling West on Tangerine Rd. Towards Twin Peaks Rd. in Marana Arizona. A suburb of Tucson. When the 6 redish-orange lights caught our attention right away. The first 3 appeared in a line which makes me think of a possible V shape. Than the Next 3 appeared next to eachother. In a matter of 5 seconds they seemed to be spinning upside down, after a half rotation they begun to turn off one by one in the same order they appeared. I have much interest in UFOs as ive come to find they are very common in Tucson. I have seen things before that have made me wonder what they could be. This sighting left me with no doubt it was NOT a man made aircraft of any citizens knowledge. This is my first report and i can only hope someone else has or will see something of this description. It gave me chills like ive never had.
