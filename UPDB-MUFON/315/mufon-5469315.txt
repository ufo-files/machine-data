I was walking my dogs down the street after four in the morning on a clear night here in SW Florida. I looked around the sky and stars and noticed red lights similar to an airplanes. Watching it for 3-4 minutes, it never moved its position. I thought to myself to try to take a picture of it. My camera started recording in movie mode. I uploaded it to my computer a few days later. I saw nothing in the 37 second recording. But when I slowed it to frame by frame, an object cylinder in shape with an open center appeared and then shot northwest over my head and stopped, and I stopped recording. The video show white stream of the movement. It amazed me to see it, but believable. I suspected it was monitoring Mar-largo as the newly elected Trump was meeting the Chinese PM at that time.
