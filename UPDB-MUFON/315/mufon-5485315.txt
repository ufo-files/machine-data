{nd} Strange light, breaking up and coming together

Just happened to look out the window last night, we watched a light in the SE sky from around 10 Pm till after 12 Am. Went to bed then. It looked like a large star, but had colored lights on it, it would separate into 2 or orbs circle each other, move away from each other then come back together. This was Feb 10th,2016 in North Branch, Michigan First thought was that it was a large star or planet, until it separated into 2 and sometimes 3 parts, would kind of dance around each other, dart away from each other, then come back as one.
