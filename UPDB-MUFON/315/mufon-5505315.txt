{cak} 2 objects going towards each other in space and red flash near both objects

while on hilltop near Pinebush NY 4 of us were there looking for UFO's . we saw an object come from southeast looked like a rocket weren't sure. the object seemed to follow the curvature of earth ,as it rose up to almost directly east we saw another object moving very fast towards the rocket object. suddenly there was a flash of red light maybe an inch wide to us, towards the rocket object.the other object suddenly stopped and changed direction, like a 90 degree right. we were so amazed when this happened that we lost sight of both objects. after watching NASA unexplained files i realized that's what we saw from the ground an apparent star wars test by the U.S. after leaving the hilltop my friend turned back and said he saw white light shine on the hilltop for only a second or two. i did not see it myself
