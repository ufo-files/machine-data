{tjd} light zipping up and down

The electric was off so I looked out my bathroom window to see if the wind was
blowing. I put my flashlight up to the screen and could see a light about five feet
off the ground out by the dirt road. All of a sudden it dropped to the ground then it was
two small lights. Then it zipped up to about 9 or 10 feet and was one light again
it started going up and down and side to side. Like it was trying to get out of my light.
Then it became two lights again and went down the road behind some pine trees.
Then it flew back to it's spot very fast and went up and down and side to side again.
Then it went down the road slow and didn't come back.
I live at 3000 feet elev. There were no storms or bad weather no wind and no electric.. I heard no noise.
