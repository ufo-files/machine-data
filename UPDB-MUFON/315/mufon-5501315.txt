{nd} Went fishing with family and noticed disc-like, reflective object with small dome flying slowly from right to left between two clouds. Tried to take picture, but object had disappeared even though clouds were far apart.

My family and I had driven to a family-owned lake to go fishing until nightfall. I had just stepped out of the car to unload our supplies when I looked at the sky on my left. I saw a white (or grey) disc-like object making a slow path across the sky from right to left between two clouds. It seemed to have a small dome on top. The sun was high in the sky and was causing a bright reflection on the back end of the object leading me to think it was metallic. I pulled out my phone to try to take a picture, but couldn't get the object to show up on camera. When I gave up and tried to observe the object some more, it was gone. It couldn't have gone into the clouds since both clouds were a large distance apart and when I tried to take a picture it was almost perfectly in-between both of them.
