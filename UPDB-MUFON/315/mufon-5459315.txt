{cak} Incredibly fast object moving from the right side of the video.

When taking video at a race called Pistons and Props at Sebring International racetrack I noticed a incredibly fast object moving across the sky. It comes into frame from the right side after a moped goes past in front of me. I am not sure if that is a comet or not. I looked at it frame by frame and it looks like it changes altitude just slightly midway point. I figured let experts look at it and they can tell better than me on what it could have been. I never saw the object until watching the video just recently this year.
