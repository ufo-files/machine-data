{cak} stationary lighted object 3 white lights, 2 pulsating color changing lights on bottom

I stepped out on the second story balcony facing west and noticed an object very high in the sky with three bright white lights on top. On the bottom of the object were two lights that pulsated and changed color from green to white to red and back to green in a very sequential manner. I watched it for about 20 minutes when another white light moving very fast approached the larger object from the north. As the smaller moving object approached the larger object, a bright flash of light appeared from the smaller object and then the smaller object disappeared. No other aircraft was in the sky, but the main object stayed stationary for as long as I watched it. I watched for total of four hours. I awoke in disbelief and still feel very unsure and uneasy.
