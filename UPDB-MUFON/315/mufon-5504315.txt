{cak} bright solid blue light flying erratically then hovering, then diving

Initially saw a descending blue light, like a meteor low on the horizon. Light then rose back up and moved erratically at a high rate of speed, changing color from solid bright blue to solid pale red. Hovered at times, diving, then back up and moving erratically. General area appeared to be over San Diego Naval base. Object was in sky for about 15 minutes. During the event our satellite TV lost reception. Object then disappeared below the horizon. Distance seemed to be at least 10 miles out. No blinking light such as seen on normal aircraft. Attempted to videotape but could not get a fix on the object, even though other lights recorded clearly.
