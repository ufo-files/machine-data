bright light moving E, stopping, going dark, lighting up much brighter, proceding

I got up to use bathroom at 1:26AM and when getting back in bed, looked out the open window and saw a strange object. It was a fairly bright white light travelling in a line from West to East about 30deg above the Southern horizon. No sound, totally quiet.

It was moving fairly fast and not blinking and then suddenly stopped very quickly, and went dark. After about 2-3 seconds, it got much brighter than before, stayed in place for a 1-2 seconds then quickly started up again. It repeated this again, sped off quickly to what looked like a change to the NE and went out of view.
