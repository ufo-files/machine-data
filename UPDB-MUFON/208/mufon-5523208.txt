Hovering Triangular Ship over my Vehicle

I was in Phoenix AZ in the Glendale suburb. I was called early into work (security guard) I was driving to work and stopped at a light with a white truck next to me and a car next to him putting me in the left hand lane. I found myself looking up at a city sized triangular ship hovering above the stop lights. I couldnt take my eyes off it. There were three lights precisely placed at each corner. It moved very quickly where at a blink of an eye it moved further upward to the distance. I felt as if I needed to call the police but in 1995 cellphones were not readily available and accesable. I felt very scared and humbled, I have never felt this way, it terrified me. I didn't hear any music which my car radio was playing, I remember vividly,Panama by VanHalen was playing right before it appeared then there was no music.
