Transparent Triangle Type Object

I took out my dog at 11 pm. I let her in then I came out to check out the stars. I looked west first then I turn south east. All of sudden a object past flying very fast no sound transparent with 2 static lights. One on tip and one on left point. I didn't notice the other light. It looked triangular. It was less than a mintue. It gave me the chills. Thats what I saw. I hope other did too.
