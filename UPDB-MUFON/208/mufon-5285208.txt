Something entered the atmosphere coming from one direction but the Bright trail stayed stationary, changed direction, and then was an object.

On my drive home I saw an extremely bright trail of light coming straight down from the sky, reminiscent of a meteor shooting across the sky. But the trail and light didn’t visibly seem to be moving or making headway at all. It was like it was frozen, just sitting in the sky. After about five minutes I lost it. About ten to fifteen minutes later I saw it again in the same direction I was heading but the trail of light was now horizontal, but still stationary. I stopped on a rural back road so I could take the time to watch it in the sky to see if I could truly see any movement or it my eyes were playing tricks on me. Again, I lost it completely, which was pretty shocking this time around. Within less than a minute I realized there was something exactly where I had seen the trail of light, but it was completely different. It was a large whitish / grey / blue hued object that was not very opaque at all. But it was completely still, and never moved even once. There was no noise, no movement, nothing. Once I got to my house, no more than a quarter of a mile away, I went to grab my binoculars because I knew I would be able to see the object from my porch considering where it just was on my way. And there was nothing, no trail, still no sound you would expect for it to be gone so quickly, and nothing in the either direction in the distance either.
