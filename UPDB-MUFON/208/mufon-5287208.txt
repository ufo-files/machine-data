At first I thought I was seeing car headlights coming down mountain from kingman, than the lights seem to join and become remarkably bright and big .

The lights seemed to dance together and dissapear, than would come back and join into one bright ball of light. At certain moments the object seemed circular with lights on the edges, than it would disappear and come back looking triangular with the surrounding lights. Most of the time it was 2 big bright lights than 4 a bit smaller then one huge glowing aura of white silver and a tinge of orange. A plane flew by, I believe to inspect the object but it did not get close. The object stayed near to a mountain side. I kept trying to tell myself it was headlights, but there is no way. I thought it might be nasa and their "spot the station", but it did not behave as such. I had my first sighting may 30th of this year. Now I've had my second. Interestingly the "spot the station" thing nasa does always seems to take the blame/credit for these sightings. I've spotted the stations and there is no similarity between the bright glowing light/lights near the mountain side and the fireworks from nasa.
