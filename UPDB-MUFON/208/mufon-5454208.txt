{tjd} Cigar shaped object in vertical position, first observed hovering, then rapidly accelerated upward.

The object was noticed because I was looking that direction at the sunset, and observed the object hovering not far off the ground over a soccer field off of highway 169 in Tulsa, Oklahoma. After noticing the object I quickly pulled to the shoulder and got out my phone to take a picture. Just as I started to snap some photos the object suddenly and silently shot upwards and out of view. I actually did not see the object in the few photos I took, but noticed that, as on some cell phones, I could view the object in the few seconds of the "live" photo that play before the still shot was taken. That is where the image of the object comes from. The object can be seen near the top of the light pole on the left side.
