two points of light stretched quickly to two oblongs of light and then disappeared without changing direction

I was sitting in my hot tub on the decking at 9.30 pm. It was dark. The sky was fairly clear with strips of low cloud with clear sky showing stars in between. I saw two points of light in the distance directly south which looked like an aeroplane coming directly towards us, but we see planes on the flight path to London Gatwick from the East and sometimes from West if they circle but not from the South usually. I moved to the edge of the hot tub to get clear of the steam to get a better look and in a couple of seconds the two points of light changed to two strips of light. Only about half a centimetre or less each but with a small gap in between and then as abruptly disappeared. I thought that they would reappear from behind the cloud as there was only light cloud cover but saw nothing more in any direction. I wondered if I had seen a plan side on with the window lights showing but it made no sense to have turned from head on to side on and be close enough to see window lights that quickly and there was no sound of a plane at all. This only lasted a couple of seconds in total and made no sense at all to me. Never seen anything like it before.
