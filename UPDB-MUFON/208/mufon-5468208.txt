{td} heard military jets, went to look saw 2 f18s flying aggressively low alt, looked straight up saw v shaped black ufo

Memorial day 2018 around noon we heard loud military sounding jets, I went outside to look and saw what I think were f 18s, two of them, flying side by side low-ish altitude making a sweeping wide turn. My attention was then drawn up, basically straight up and I saw what at first looked like a flock of black birds but this "flock" was not moving like a bunch of birds. It was a V-shaped object I realized and it was hovering at first, maybe 4 seconds, then bounced side to side instantaneously. It then headed in a northwest direction but was not moving like a plane would, I don't know how to describe it but it would move forward in small jerky spurts and what confuses me is that it seemed to disappear for a brief moment as it was moving then reappear before spurting forward again. I don't know how high up it was but the sky was completely clear, it was definitely a CAVU day as the pilots say so for all I know this thing could've been at an elevation anywhere from 5,000 to 20,000 feet. I went inside a short time later and we continued to hear the military jets flying around for another 5 minutes.
