5 Orange stationary lights over the Desert

I was on my way to my dark sky spot to use my Telescope 15 miles west of Gila Bend, Arizona when I noticed a bright orange light off to my south while I was traveling on Interstate 8. It appeared to be 10 miles off to the south and never changed in perspective from where I was. I was traveling at 80 mph and I never passed the light. It seemed to track with me for 20 miles. As I arrived at my exit, the light disappeared. After a 10 minute drive north of the Interstate, I arrived at my spot and noticed that there were now 5 of these lights to my south. They did not move but two of them did fade in and out of intensity. I put my telescope on the object to my direct south and discovered that it was an round intense orange object that had some sort of smoke falling below it. This object brightened so much that it made it difficult to make out Sagittarius and the Milky Way which were my intended targets that night. This object would disappear completely and then increase in intensity many times over the course of the two hours it was visible. These objects were hovering over the Barry Goldwater Military Range and also near the Gila Bend AAF auxiliary field.
