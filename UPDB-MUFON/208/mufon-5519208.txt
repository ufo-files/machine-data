VERY BRIGHT LIGHT NON BLINKING BRIGHT THEM DIM TO OFF

Good day.

I live on the Alabama Gulf Coast, I often cannot sleep nights so I spend much of my time looking up. I would like to inform you of a sighting I just had, approximately 4:30-4:45a.m central time. I was looking towards the east north east as I was sitting on my front porch as I often do. I saw an extremely bright object that was like a light dimmer. It got bright then faded back out. I kept looking in the same direction and to my surprise the object moved what seemed like 4 to 6" in the sky. This object did not seem like it was in our atmosphere or perhaps it was. It was far brighter than any star in the sky yet seemed closer. I am an ex Air Force enlistee and know very well what beacon lights or navigation lights look like on aircraft and it did not blink anything like them. We get all sorts of aircraft in our airspace often just over treetop level due to our proximety of both NAS Pensacola, and Brookley Field in Mobile, Al. This was like I said one long blink lasting nearly a second each time. The odd thing was if this was in our atmosphere and was an aircraft it would have covered the distance far sooner than this event lasted. 

PLEASE advise me if anyone else in the southern U.S. also saw this event. It was unlike anything I have ever seen to date. I often look at NASA.com to see if any satellites will be flying over along with the International Space Station. I have seen those also many times they have a completely different look and track most of the time. This truly was an unidentified flying object........
