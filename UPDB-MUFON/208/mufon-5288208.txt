W type pattern of stars rotating

We were sitting outside on the back porch. When we looked at the stars we noticed a strange pattern of stars. Upon further inspection we realised they were lights that were in a w type pattern rotating around a object over and over again. For about 10 minutes the object appeared to be hovering in the same spot then it moved to the right and disappeared. We were baffled by this I grabbed my phone to take a video but you realy can not see the lights just the stars around it.
