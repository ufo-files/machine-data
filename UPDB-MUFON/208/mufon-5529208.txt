Bright red fireball/light passes over residential area

We had gotten done with trick-or-treating around 8:30 pm EST in Kennesaw, GA, and had left our neighbor's house (they were still standing outside talking). I went inside and started to close some curtains and noticed a red fireball/star-like ball of light that was crossing the sky (from southwest to northeast) over the neighborhood (I'm guessing about a quarter-mile to a half-mile up). It was bright, and looked rather weird, so I went outside to see it better (thinking at that time it was likely just a plane). The neighbors were all looking up at it too (we exchanged a few "what is that?" type phrases). It only took about 2-3 minutes to cross overhead. It moved about as fast as I've seen helicopters move, but the fact it was just one bright light made it look odd. I didn't have time to grab a camera. I actually wasn't going to report it at all due to the lack of a photo and the fact it's path/speed resembled a helicopter or plane; however, I saw another report in GA of a very similarly-described red light in the sky that same night (titled "Star Like Redish Light Tranformed to a Flarelike Fire Ball" submitted on 11-1-2010), and perhaps these "cases" are linked. I can provide a map of it's approximate path, if needed.
