{nd} 2 Small Rectangles Flashing Lights with One Larger Star Craft Joining In

On June 5th beginning at 12:16 AM, the third siting of the "trilogy" occurred. At that time two crafts appeared from the west, the portal of entry and proceeded to go northward slowly at first. These two craft were perpendicular to one another, angled at approximately 35 to 55 degrees from one another. They appeared to be small rectangles because their lights, which were flashing white and red, were not coming from the same spot but rather slightly apart. In the dark it appeared to be small rectangles but I cannot be 100 percent sure. As they moved to the north these two crafts made a maneuver where by the ship that was higher than the other went lower and the lower craft went higher; much higher. Now the lower craft continued further north and the higher craft remained closer but they were still in contact with each other, flashing their lights seemingly communicating to one another. They then seem to have stopped moving, hovering in their positions when at 12:26AM the "star like" came from the north positioning itself close to me and eyewitness about 4 degrees west of true north and completely stopped as if looking right at me. It hovered in that position for about a minute before reversing its flight pattern returning back to the north past the Ghost Ranch and the two other craft following her and disappearing into the night. The whole event took about 15 minutes in duration.
