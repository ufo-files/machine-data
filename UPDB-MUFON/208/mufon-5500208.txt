{cak} Ufo went into warpspeed and disappeared

I was driving on the freeway 60 eastbound towards riverside from Los Angeles, I was looking at the moon and also the airplanes on the sky and I started to see a light that wasn't blinking like the other airplanes, at this time I was near Ontario airport, and I kept looking at the light and all of the sudden the light was moving slowly in one direction and it started going super fast like warpspeed and it disappeared.
