Filmed when landing at Heathrow

This is the unedited slow-motion video taken with an iPhone 5s from the vantage point of rear facing Seat 64K on the British Airways Flight BA0242 while landing at Heathrow Airport on Sunday 29 November 2015, around 1pm.

Unidentified Flying Objects (UFOs) can be seen at different stages of the video. Initially in black against the sky and towards the end of the video in brown/beige, flying close to the runway at great speed.
