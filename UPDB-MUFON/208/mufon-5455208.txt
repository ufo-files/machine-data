{rjl} 1 UFO Floated overhead to the north towards Paine Field Boeing

Unforgettable- looked like an upside down tin spinning toy- maybe 10 to 20 ft. In diameter had a grey bottom and maybe 3 bands of light covering the upper 2/3rds. It was over the parking lot just outside the store next to Highway 99. 
Perfectly clear sunny morning. We were on our bikes at the time. No sound- my friend John kept saying. We watched it float north for a minute in a straight line directly over Lake Serene towards the airport and out of view of just over the trees. We still talk about our experience together!
