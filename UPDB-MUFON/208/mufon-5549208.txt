Single object appeared low and went behind a cloud. It reappeared on the other side split into 4 equal size balls and sped off in an upward manner.

I was diving on I-25 south. A fireball appeared in the sky. I thought it was a flare. It assened behind a cloud and shortly appeared on the other side. At this point it seemed to split in four objects the same size. From my point of view these evenly spaced objects sped up at an upwards angle(using the mountains as a reference) and dissappeared into the upper atomosphere. I thought about this for a lond time and tried to explain it away. It was moving to slowly for a metorite and much to quicky for planes in formation.
