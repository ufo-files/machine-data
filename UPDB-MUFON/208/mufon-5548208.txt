CIGAR-SHAPED OBJECT AT TOP OF STORM CLOUD

AT APPROX. 6:00 SAT. AFTERNOON, i NOTICED AN UNUSUAL JET-ENGINE SOUND FROM ALBANY AIRPORT.I THOUGHT THAT A COMMERCIAL PLANE MIGHT BE TAKING OFF IN A DIFFERENT DIRECTION BECAUSE OF THE STORM. AT 6:30, AS THE SOUND CONTINUED, I LOOKED OUT MY SOUTH FACING BATHROOM WINDOW, AND SAW 2 F-15 EAGLES FLYING IN FORMATION DIRECTLY OVER MY HOUSE. THEY MADE TWO PASSES, AND AFTER THEY FLEW BY THE SECOND TIME, I HAPPENED TO SEE AT THE TOP OF THE CLOUD, THE CIGAR SHAPED OBJECT. IT WAS THE SAME COLOR AS THE CLOUD TOP- AN IVORY WHITE COLOR,AND WAS DEVOID OF ANY WINGS OR TAIL CONTROL SURFACES. I COULDNT SEE ANY PORTHOLES, BUT IT CLEARLY WAS NOT A COMMERCIAL AIRLIINER. IT WAS MOVING DUE SOUTH, AND I LOST SIGHT OF IT AS IT WENT BACK INTO THE CLOUDS. I CALLED SEVERAL TV NEWS STATIONS, AND CHANNEL 10 SAID THAT THEY HAD RECEIVED NUMEROUS CALLS ABOUT THE FIGHTERS, BUT I WAS THE ONLY ONE WHO SAW THE OBJECT. CHANNEL 10 WAS ALSO TOLD THE THE JETS WERE CONDUCTING "TRAINING EXCERCISES". I ASKED THE PERSON IF HE REALLY THOUGHT THAT THE AIR FORCE WOULD BE CONDUCTING TRAINING EXCERCISES IN COMMERCIAL AIRSPACE LIKE THAT. THE OBVIOUS ANSWER IS NO- THE USAF WAS SCRAMBLED UP THERE!! ALSO, I GUESS IT JUST A MATTER OF TIME UNTIL I SAW THE "MOTHER SHIP", AS THERE HAVE BEEN NUMEROUS PERSONAL SIGHTINGS OF VARIOUS OBJECTS IN THE LATHAM AREA. WILTON MAKES LATHAM LOOK LIKE OHARE BY COMPARISON!!
