Round sphere with 4 smaller round spheres equally spaced around larger sphere.

I was in our pool about 4:30 PM on March 2, watching eagles flying.. Eagles were very high as I could just make out the white heads and tails. Clouds were broken scattered; a passenger jet had left vapor trail headed east. I spotted a silver globe floating much higher than the eagles, near the vapor trail of the jet. I called to my wife to bring my 16 X 50 binoculars. Naked eye viewing, object looked about the size of Venus at closest approach to earth. Through the binoculars, the object was about the size of a BB held at arms length and there were 4 much smaller objects at 90 Degrees separation, that appeared to be spaced around the main object. I moved to get a better view and the thing was just gone. No sound, vapor trail, just gone. I have 20 years USAF experience and have never seen any thing like this object. I have observed high altitude balloons from military experience and this was nothing like anything I have seen. The sun was at my back, lowering in the west providing ample sunlight for observation and no background glare.
