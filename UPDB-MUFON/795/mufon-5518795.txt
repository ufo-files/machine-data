Approx. seven low flying orange glowing orbs over So. Cal on 13 MAY 2012

Driving - at an intersection, stopped.
Saw several (approximately SEVEN) bright ORANGE glowing orbs flying slowly over Oxnard, California on the night of 13MAY2012 at near 9pm PST.
At first glance, (given the number of objects) I thought they might be experimental aircraft being flown by enthusiasts. 
The objects seemed to be flying in a low altitude flight path. Their bearing was south-east. 
Puzzled. The thought of extraterrestrial UFOs did cross my mind, but being a skeptic I reasoned against.
The incredulous thought lingered, and a couple of days thereafter I decided to investigate any similar reports within the local news media and Internet. Upon researching a little, I quickly found that many others (throughout the country and within the same date period, had experienced and reported almost the exact same sightings!!!)
