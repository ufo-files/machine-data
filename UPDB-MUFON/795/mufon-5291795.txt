2 F-16's at low altitude pursuing 2 shape-shifting blobs through Hunter Army Air field air space while C-130's were practicing touch and go's.

2 fighter planes in a tight turn appeared behind us (from the North) . They were at a low altitude. Maybe 1,200 ft. They also happened to be flying through Hunter Army Air Field air space at the time. Just before they arrived C-130's had been doing touch and go's. I was watching the jets when my friend started to emphatically yell. 2:47pm. Clear skies with a few cumulous and cirrus clouds. All I can say is that I saw a VERY white almost amorphous thing moving across my field of view. At first I thought it must be a Snowy Egret (we were on the marsh) but then it got weird. The (I'm 95% sure) F-16's came around for another pass and this thing just kind of changed shape. The ?nose? part of it temporarily protruded and changed into a gold color that I can only describe as having the textural appearance of a gold disco ball. Very reflective and diffracting. Sparkling in the sun. Then it looped around and my buddy and I kept back-peddling to keep from losing sight of it as it moved beyond the tree line. I have never seen anything like this. If those two jets hadn't come around we would have never looked up. When we did we saw something that we cannot explain. Just the most unbelievable thing I've ever seen. I can't wrap my brain around it. Utterly reality defying.
