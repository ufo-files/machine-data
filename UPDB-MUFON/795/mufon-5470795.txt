{rjl} witnessed a red tennis ball size object flashing predominately red with silver flashes coming from the center hovering at about 4 feet off the ground. The object

Witnessed with my grandson of 9 a red and silver tennis ball size object hovering near our driveway marker around 8 pm. We opened the front door and watched it for approximately one minute when the object instantaneously moved across the driveway and appeared by our mailbox. I asked my grandson to watch while I put a coat and some boots on to go investigate closer. I wasn't gone 30 seconds and my grandson indicated it disappeared but he thought it may have gone west down the street. I exited the house and walked to the end of the driveway and could see the objects two houses down by the neighbors mailbox. I approached and when I got to our next door neighbors driveway I yelled "What are you doing?". The object flashed rapidly and disappeared only to see it close to a tree about 100 feet away at a driveway that leads into a wooded lot. Then it was gone. The object never got more than 3-4 feet off the ground. I retrieved a strong flashlight and tried to find evidence in the snow but was unsuccessful. My regret is I wasn't able to get any video or pictures.
