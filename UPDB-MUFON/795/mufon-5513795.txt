(GS) Step outside looked up seen blue light

I live in the country.
On December 11,2012 at 10:27 pm I walked outside of my house, The temperature was 36 deg F no wind no rain, slight overcast, The stars can be seen but there was a over cast. 
The lights were off on outside of the house. I looked up at the sky just over the trees, no leaves on trees, About 80 deg looking up from NE to SE, Very bright Blue light. There was no sound at al It was in a straight to curved path, It did not go straight it was going very fast. (Not sure what it was, as I never seen anything like it, no sound and fast)
I was not scared or nervous, I wanted to see it again. 
The object went so fast it went behind the trees.
