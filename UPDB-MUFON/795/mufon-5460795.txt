2 triangle markings made in local park grounds

I took the dog for a walk to the local park on the morning of 20th May 2019 the same walk I’ve been doing ever since having the dog. Upon entering the park my attention turned towards a shape in the grass that I’ve not seen before. I stopped for a moment and looked at the triangular shape thinking what was it. Then I turned around to see if there was any other markings and I saw a 2nd identical marking on the other section of grass. These markings are perfect shaped triangles elongated to the nose end with chamfered edges (not pointed).
It looks like the grass of the triangle has been starved of water and is withering away. The size of these triangles seem to be between 10-15 ft long. And they are pointing Northwards.
