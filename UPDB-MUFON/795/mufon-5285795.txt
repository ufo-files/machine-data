Silver-White Stationary Sphere-ABQ 08282021

My neighbor first observed and pointed the object out to me at approximately 1400 August 28, 2021. I personally observed a silvery-white sphere that appeared to be hovering in a stationary position approximately due north of our location at (Moved location to Investigators Report Section/cms/LLL). Both myself and a third neighbor took photos of the object and observed that it remained hovering from approximately 1400 and is still there at the time I am submitting this report, which is currently 1505. My photos are not very clear, my hand was definitely shaking when I took the photos, but they are all the same, and yes, the object is still there. First attached photo was taken after the second photo, which also shows some type of drone or something that flew past the object. Whatever elevation the cloud cover had, this thing is hovering under that for sure.
