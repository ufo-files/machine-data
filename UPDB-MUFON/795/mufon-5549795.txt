I Spotted a Cigar Shaped object and made contact

I was driving down a semirural road when my carstalled. I saw three cigar shaped objects in the distance. One of the objectsbroke off from the group and decended to Earth. At this point my companionsmemory went blank. She just sat in the car. An earthlike being that glowedcame up to my car and opened the door. I suffered a memory lapse and when Iawoke I was in a room on a table. I lapsed out again and when I awoke I was onthe ground in front of my car when the object again became airborne. It was aterrifying experience that I cant believe I am sharing it with anyone.
