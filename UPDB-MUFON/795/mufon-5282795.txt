A light gained brightness and disappeared

I walked outside to take a phone call at 7:10 p.m. . I looked up as I typically do when outside at night and immediately spotted a bright light directly above me approximately from Andromeda going in the direction of Cassiopeia. It appeared bright like the space station does except it was traveling in the opposite direction the ISS flies. I saw it for a couple of seconds watching its path go to the Northwest which was odd and then it grew exponentially in brightness, maybe four times brighter, so I thought it must have been an aircraft to change its lighting but there were no colors and it became apparent that it was just a flash or pulse...continuing in its direction it faded back to normal and then blipped out. I kept staring at the spot and in the direction to see if it had faded under cloud cover or something interfered with my vision but I could not find it in the sky after that. It was a clear night sky. It was traveling toward the Sun so it could not have gone into the shadow of the Earth like the space station does. My first anomalous sighting.
