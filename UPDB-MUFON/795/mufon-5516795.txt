{dr}HUGE white light right over top of me.

We (my family adn I) were relaxing late one summer night enjoying a fire in our new firepit when my grandfather (who was looking at the stars that night) said "what the hell is that". I looked up at that time and a HUGE (I cant sress enough how big it was) was hovering directly overtop of us all. It was the brightest white I had ever seen. It, whatever it was, only stayed within view for about 3-4 seconds. I don't know how long it was there before we noticed it. After we had all noticed it was there, it disapered. The light turned off and was gone. I bust into tears of fear afterwards and we all went inside for the night. We had seen multiple other crafts that summer and that sighting certainly wasnt the last. We have had multiple sightings following the one described above.
