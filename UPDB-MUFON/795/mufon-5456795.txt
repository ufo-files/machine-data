I was on a personal flight in my airplane on a clear cold Jan 3, 1988 around 3PM over LAKE GALENA, New Britain, Twp, PA 18901 when I observed an object front on high and to my upper right descending at a high rate of speed. It was gold in color, no sound, no disturbance of aircraft functionality what-so-ever. It stopped in front of me about 300 +/- Ft and stayed still. I tried to catch up to it but it took off to my S/W and accelerated faster than I could catch up to it. I swung my airplane around to try to get close but my plane was not that fast. I called the Willow Grove Naval Air Station on their control frequency and described the craft. They/he said there was no radar hit and he did ID my aircraft by way of my transponder Identification code. The craft then flow back to me and toward the ENE course, paced along side of my about 300 Ft and then tilted backwards and ascended back up into th4e sky so fast I could not see it leave. The tower operator asked if I wanted to report a UFO and I replied in the affirmative and that night a man from the U.S. Gov't UFO research center called me and asked me to describe, which I did.  I heard that the New Britain Twp, PA Police Dept. received calls from persons on the ground that witnessed this but I didn't look into it..
