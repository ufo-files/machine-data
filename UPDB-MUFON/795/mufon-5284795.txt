Red Orb Fireball like

In the town of Hamburg, NJ in Sussex County around 11:55 pm on October 10, 2021 and 12:00 am on October 11, 2021 the below sighting took place:

The night of the sighting it was very foggy. As soon as night had fallen there was a white glow in the distance left to my backyard. At around 11:55 pm et I was getting my aunt ready for bed, I turned out the lights and as I entered the living my inner voice told me to head back into the kitchen and look out the glass door. As I got to the door a red orb the size of a basketball was flying quite low in a diagonal pattern very quickly (much faster than a plane) in the middle of our backyards. My first instinct was to call it a "fireball" and at that it flew from mid-tree level to high above the trees making the red orb much smaller in view. About a minute or so later a second identical flying object appeared on the street behind ours, flying in the same exact flight pattern from the street behind ours. I couldn't believe my eyes so I ran back into the living pulled up my blinds to the sliding glass doors, woke everyone up and peered outside. The UFO hovered over our white birch tree as we looked up at, which felt like we were looking into each other's eyes. It spooked my mother a bit and she asked me to pull the blinds down, so I did. I was so shocked and everything happened so quickly I didn't have time to access to my phone, so unfortunately I was unable to record my sighting, which I am now kicking myself for not doing so. I checked flight radar 24 and there were no known flights recorded overhead during that time. Both flying objects were completely silent.

After the sighting I text messaged my neighbor to see if she had seen what I encountered, but she was already asleep. I didn't realize she had text messaged me around 11:20 pm on Oct. 10th asking if I was having issues with my internet connection as well as our fire alarm, for hers was tripping.
