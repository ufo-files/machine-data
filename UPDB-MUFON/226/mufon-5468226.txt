My son and I were at our home owners association community pool at dusk. We were commenting on 2 stars or planets and trying to figure out which one they were so we were looking up into the sky and seeing if there were any other stars. My son told me to look at a star that was moving and pointed it out. It was as small as a star would normally look but it did not have a twinkle to it as a star would. It also had no lights blinking on it as a plane would. It was moving very fast from the southwest to the northeast. From the moment we saw it to when it disappeared was less than a minute. Even when it was above us moving it never changes its size or shape and just disappeared into the distance. We don't know if was a experimental military aircraft or the international space station or a satellite.
