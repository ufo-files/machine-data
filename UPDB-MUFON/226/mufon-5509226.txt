{nd} I saw a bright orange-red object that I could not identify. Never have seen anything like that in the sky.

I was coming home from a ballgame in Indianapolis driving due west. I turned south and noticed a bright orange light in the sky to the east that Id never seen in that spot. I live in Speedway. I first noticed the object because it was so bright. I first thought it might be a helicopter or a firework. I saw a helicopter to the south and could readily see the difference. I had a CD playing and my windows were closed so I didn't hear anything. It had been sprinkling a little too. I thought okay if this is a firework it is going to descend or make a sound or something, but nothing happened. It just stayed there and glowed very bright orange-red. I thought about phoning my sister who was in the house but I didn't want to take my eyes off of the light so I didn't. I walked up the driveway and the light just diminished and finally vanished without a sound from where I was. I couldnt tell if it ascended and disappeared or disappeared where it was hovering. It disappeared into a tiny dot to nothing. The whole thing lasted no more than 3 minutes. I came in the house and told my sister I had just seen a UFO. It was a UFO to me. Ive never seen a helicopter shine orange-red and without flashing lights like the helicopter I saw in the sky at the same time to the south, nor have I seen one be silent. From what I could tell it seemed to pulsate some but stayed bright orange-red. Thank you for being here so I could report this! It didn't upset me, but I will remain puzzled by what I saw.
