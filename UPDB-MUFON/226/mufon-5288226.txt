Friday, April 30th, we seen lights on the creek, stationary. Disappeared right in front of us. The next day my wife seen what may have been an entity.

My fiance and I witnessed lights on the creek that's about 200 feet from our back deck. They were in a triangular formation and roughly 12 feet long. Two gold lights in the back and a light at the tip which was red and would change to white. It was a very windy night. We observed the lights which were on the water. No one was down there. We went in for a minute or two and I told her I wanted to go down there. We went back to the balcony and the lights turned off. Nothing was there. The next day which was May 1st, my fiance and her daughter went into the woods for a walk. My fiance said she seen something weird.
