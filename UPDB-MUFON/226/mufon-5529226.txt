I saw a small white sphere heading north at a high speed, with a commercial jet seeming to dodge it.

I was standing in my front yard in upper Yucaipa, California watching a commercial jet heading south overhead (which was odd because they generally go west), then it suddenly turned to head west. Immediately after that I saw a white sphere heading north across the plane's path. I watched the sphere as it continued north and then it suddenly vanished. I noticed the object because I was curious about why the jet was flying south. When I first noticed the sphere I thought it might be a fighter jet or some small fast aircraft because of the speed. I have never seen even a small jet move that fast from such a distance. My next thought was that I saw a UFO, especially because of the plane's reaction to it, and how it just disappeared. It might be something very normal, but I'd really like to know just what that would be.
