{cak} Human looking object floating above highway.

These photos were taken by a young engineer on his way to work. He was just coming up on the Patrick Exit across from the Frank H.Tracy Power plant. He prefers to remain anonymous at this time, and asked me if I would contact you. He (the witness) was driving east on I 80. He saw what he thought might be a trash bag hovering/floating above the highway near the power plant . Lots of traffic on the road, he took 4 photos through the windshield. He said that there was something strange about the object as it was a 5:30 AM and cool, and whatever the object was showed no thermal from a heat source, and it was "just sort of floating above the area". He also said it made no noise. When he arrived at his work site shortly after, he realized upon opening, and expanding his photos that it was a human like object. He immediately called the house to share his excitement, and to share his photos. he called several times during the day as he was very distracted, and upset by what he had seen. he submitted the pics to local news but did not leave his name. he does not think it was a jet pack type event, as no heat signature, and hovering in place at altitude. In any case he remains excited about the sighting. The 5th photo is a compilation of the object in the 4 photos expanded so the object can be more clearly identified. simply expand each pic, and the human form begins taking shape.
