Similar to a satellite but much faster and brighter.

At about 8:32 this evening I went outside and looked up as I do a lot and I immediately saw a sharp, bright light like a star moving south-southeast about 2-3 times the speed of a satellite and much brighter. The sun set here at 8:13 tonight and it was still light enough that I could not see any stars yet but this object was easily visible. As it went behind some small, thin clouds it faded in and out so I know it was higher than the clouds. The movement was very straight and steady. I lost track of it as it got further away and went behind trees in my yard. It would have been very, very bright if it had been dark but Venus wasn't even visible yet..
