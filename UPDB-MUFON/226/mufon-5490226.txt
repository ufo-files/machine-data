{cak} What started as two bright orbs in the sky Northeast of San Antonio ended up flying over us at my house north San Antonio.

We saw two bright orbs in the sky adjacent to each other while we were driving down 1604 going east about 10pm. They caught our attention, because they were bright in the sky. When we got into my neighborhood, I got out to track it with my star tracker app thinking they were planets or something, because I didn't know what it was. It went from being still to moving sideways away from each other and I jumped in my car and drove home down the road. When we got out of the car, it flew right over us. It was humming and turning off it's lights that were under it. They were green, blue and yellow and the surface was metal. It disappeared into the sky and my sister and I were freaked out. We tried to justify what we saw calling it a possible drone, but we don't think so. Surreal. We still have a hard time believing it, but we know what we saw. Just too nervous to capture with our camera phones.
