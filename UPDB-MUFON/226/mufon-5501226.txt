{nd} Extremely brighter then any star the ufo moved without care. Police helicopter observed then the ufo simply vanished

I was arriving home late when i took out my garbage. In the n. East sky appeared a bright star like object below commercial planes but high enough to dismiss any features. Moved to the S. East slow. Too slow to produce lift on a winged plane. No noise was heard and the light seamed to be non conventional like it was almost made of light. While the ufo was directly over head i noticed and heard a police helicopter south heading in the line of the ufo as to intercept. When the object reached near intercepting helocopter it simple faded out. Gone!
