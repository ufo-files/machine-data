{rjl} Spherical orange light moving in a glide-like motion on a straight path below the clouds with no sound

This is my second sighting in my neighborhood in the nine months. I live close to the everglades. i came out to walk my dog between 9:20 - 9:30 pm on Christmas night 2014. i looked in the sky and saw an orange spherical light moving across the sky in a gliding motion. it was about the size of the moon when it's in the far sky or i guess like the size of a globe. i thought that it was strange that it did not make a sound although it was so low. coming from the eastern to western direction, was a plane at about the same height that was clearly different in shape, the color of the light was different, and i could clearly see the wings of the plane and lights at the end of them, i could also clearly hear the sound of the plane.. this reaffirmed to me that the first light i saw was clearly not a plane. i was standing in the street of my cul de sac and watched the light as it came from my left side (north), until it went over me and toward the right of me (south), and as far as i could see it until it disappeared behind the clouds...what was even more strange, and i thought my eyes were playing tricks...but as it went into the clouds, i could no longer see the light but i saw a dark figure of the object as it moved through the clouds and went past the roof of a two story house...which was as far as i could see it in the sky.
