Flying Saucer hovered over house

It was back in 1992.

My Eldest brother firs call me and my brother outside and said there was a ufo following him. Then we went outside to see a bright orange-yellow starlike object. As it got closer the glow disappeared and as it came we first thought it was a comet but with no tail just a bright dot. As it came closer it reveal itself to be a flying saucer.

It became low at around a hundred feet or lesser. Then it slowly moved at one point and hovered on the left side of our house and stopped there for a few minutes and then slowly moved towards the top of our house. Below The Saucer there were big circles arrange around what appears to be around a circular exhaust and in between that arrange in a circular pattern are what to appear to be heiroglyphics (some kind of language) 

As it stayed on top of our house it remained there for a minute and slowly ascended and turned uspide down revealing its top to be a dome of some kind. Then it moved and increased to moderate speed and made a U-turn and disappeared but still in the same speed as it did before.

It appears to me like the craft was searching for something or maybe had problems for being slow. The Saucer was glowing orange and yellow and made no sound at all.
