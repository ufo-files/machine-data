{nd} saw fireball outside my porch, watched it as it moved higher in the night sky

on 2/6/2015 at approximently 1:15am - 1:30am I went outside my residence to smoke a cigarette. I stepped off my porch and happened to look up and there it was a object on fire in the sky, as I blinked and said what the heck is that I looked up again and it was a bright white light it hovered like it knew I was staring at it, I got scared but curious so I ran inside to get my phone to take pics and some binoculars. As I came back out it had moved slowly into the night sky, it was glowing orange/yellow it was on fire. My brother once told me about Chinese Candles so that was my first instinct on what it was. But it kept on moving farther and farther into the sky changing colors like fire does. I then started taking video but my hands were so shaky (its not the best video, but you can see the colors change.)When I couldn't find it thru my video camera on my phone i got the binoculars out and I spotted it again. By this time you could not see it with the naked eye only with binoculars. As you can see in pics there is the moon,the FIREBALL and a planet. My reactions were what the heck is this I am scared, but curious. I wanted to call someone but it was too late. I lost sight after I couldn't find the object thru my binoculars. I then went to sleep and the next day told everyone I knew about what I saw and showed them pictures & video. I already reported this but couldn't get my pics to this website, I am now using my pc and can add pics and video.
