Incredibly bright light hovered and flashed about every 5 secs then faded up

My husband was walking the dog about 10:20 and called me to come outside and see something. There was a huge (bigger than any star, smaller than the moon)very bright flashing light, like a flash bulb, at about 60 degrees above horizon and about due east. It didn't change position. I watched it flash about 5 times and then called 2 of my sons to come out. It was beginning to flash with lower intensity. They both saw it, but were not very impressed, because they thought it must be a satellite and they are teenage cynics. It was much brighter than any airplane light, and not moving. My husband and I stayed outside watching and then the flashes grew farther apart and dimmer, and the object appeared to be moving up and to Northeast as it faded away completely.
