Hovering ufo entity in plain sight on breakfast television stuns home viewers, yet no mention on any other media platform.

My family and I was watching breakfast television and was waiting for the weather update, however on this occasion they decided to do an outside broadcast from the top of a church tower. This is when we noticed something in the skies behind the weather girl. Luckily we had a feature on our tv that allowed up to rewind and replay live tv. We thought our eyes were deceiving us and even to this day we have not found anyone else who even noticed this phenomenon. We instantly knew it was an out of the ordinary object that was just there in full view. We took time to check the Lunarcycle website for June 22nd (2018) and it was nowhere near.
