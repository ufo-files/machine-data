{cak} Saw 3 stationary lights 1/5 the size of the moon from our view

On our way to Ruidoso we saw 3 enormous stationary lights. Every so often one light would go off, but then would turn on again, and another would go off, and so on so forth. There would always be 2 lights on at once. The lights were perfectly round, and perfectly in line with one another, giving me the assumption that the ufo was cigar shaped. There was no movement, but this thing had to have been 2-3 football fields long. We were that close to it. It may have been 1,000 ft above us, if not less. When we got out of our car, there was no lights, no sound, not a soul nearby. You couldn’t hear anything but the wind in the sky. Above 5 miles away you could see a plane in the distance flashing it’s little light. But there was no sign of anything. We saw something we can not explain.
