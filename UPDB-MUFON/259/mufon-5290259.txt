Light Orb pulsing, rolling, changing it's shape before disappearing

My daughter and I were driving this morning on her way to daycare. We were driving south on Bear Tavern Rd. in Pennington, NJ next to [name moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc]. As I looked out over the horizon of the farm I observed what I thought at first to be a reflection off a plane, but as I looked at it, there was no structure that revealed itself. And it became apparent that it was not structured at all, but a rolling, pulsing, light that varied in size and shape. I would guess it was about a mile into the farm. I am not a trained observer so distance and elevation is difficult to tell. It was at a 15-20 degree angle off the horizon and looked low to the ground, within a 1,000 ft. If I had to guess at the relative size it was moving from a 20-50ft diameter as it pulsed/rolled/changed. All the while it was hovering/bobbing in place though as we drove down the road at 50mph. 

After a few seconds of observing it, I realized I was looking at something unusual. At that moment, my 4 y/o from the backseat on the driver's side said, "Hey Dad, do you see that weird light over the farm bouncing up and down." This kind of spooked me, because she had a slightly different angle of perception and she was seeing the same thing. And, being a kid, she recognized it was weird enough of a event to say something to me about it.

The whole event lasted 15-20 secs. Basically as soon as we both acknowledged that what we were observing was abnormal, the light orb completely disappeared. It didn't just vanish though, it went down to a single point, like an old TV set turning off. It was then that we realized there was nothing there to cause a reflection. I slowed the car down to 5mph with no one behind me and we crawled down the road continuing to look. We made a left turn on Pennington-Titusville Rd. around the ranger station and kept our eyes looking left over the farm. We saw nothing. No planes, no clouds, just blue sky.

Or if you want to go older than that, similar to the 'foo fighters' of WWII in their presentation. Unfortunately, I was driving my child and it happened fast and I didn't have any time to try and get a video.
