{dr}Lighter flame like

I had just gotten back from dinner in town we drive back to my friends house we get out of the car.while they're smoking a cigarette out side a few feet away from me I'm on the porch stairs looking at the stars. After a few minutes I realize something is off it looked like a star at first but as it got a little bit closer it took the form of a lighter flame. It's flight pattern was sporadic and would dim all the way out, appear in a different place brightly lit then move around and dim back out. this pattern I would have to say happened atleast 4-5 times before diming out and didn't reappear.
