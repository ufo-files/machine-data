{dr}Boomerang shaped craft, totally silent

Had just gotten home and noticed a bright yellow/amber light in the South Western sky. The light was far brighter than those of airplanes that frequently fly over or I would have not paid it any attention. Upon standing there and observing the object, I immediately noticed the entire object was glowing. I observed no blinking lights of any kind on the object. Once it got almost directly overhead, I knew I was looking at something out of the ordinary. No sound, absolutely silent. The entire object was indeed glowing as you can see in the photo's. The object continued to fly overhead and eventually out of site. I dont know what it was but I know what it wasn't...It wasn't from anywhere around here.
