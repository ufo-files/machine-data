{cak} Bright light, directly overhead, guessing about 15 to 20,000 feet altitude.

My wife and I were in the backyard about 9pm. We were watching for meteors during the Persides meteor shower. While looking in the dark sky I noticed a light directly above us and it was a very bright light that seemed to be at a substantial altitude. At first I thought it was an aircraft because it was moving NNW. It would be like seeing a spotlight shined from a high altitude. It moved to the NNW and as the "light" moved it dimmed to nothing. There were no attitude lights, no strobes or anything like a conventional aircraft. As the light dimmed I followed what would be the projected flight path for a few minutes, nothing, no lights. The same exact event happened about five days before at the same time of the evening. I am an experienced aircraft photographer and I have spent a great deal of my life looking up in the sky observing aircraft. This object was not an airliner or like anything I have observed before.
