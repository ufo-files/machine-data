lights in the sky

We were getting out of my car around 8-9 pm. I looked up at the stars like I always do and was looking at Orion.....I noticed that it had 2 extra stars in it and started to think it was not Orion.....I told my kids to go inside and I showed my husband (who is a non believer in anything like this). I pointed to the 2 "stars" that started getting brighter and a little larger than the star that were in Orion. I started getting scared I thought maybe they would get closer since they were getting so bright, then it was like they stopped then went so fast in the opposite direction. In a matter of 2 seconds they were completly gone.
