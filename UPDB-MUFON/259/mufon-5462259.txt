Erratic thick laser light above tree tops one side red then other side green when light stopped the trail it left CAUGHT UP with the FRONT. Then SCINTILLATING SILVER ORB ARCED UP and out of sight did not see surface of anomaly.

Time      One - One Thirty am
When      Late in 1979 
Where     East End of London Clear Night 
Duration    SEVEN SECONDS or less

I was standing looking South at a second floor window, listening to music on head phones, my friend sitting on a sofa behind me at back of room also on head phones.

I saw an Erratic THICK LASER LIGHT above tree tops, one side red then other side green when light stopped the trail it left CAUGHT UP with the FRONT.

Then A SCINTILLATING SILVER ORB appeared at the centre of the LIGHTS it then WENT UP IN THE CLEAR SKY towards the SOUTH EAST and ARCED ITS TRAJECTORY, OUT OF OUR ATMOSPHERE out of sight.
