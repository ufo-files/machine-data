{rjl} I saw a falling star, then stop in midair! It takes off at a 45 degree angle at mega speed.

It was about 9:30pm and I was driving home on highway 82 going west. I noticed up in the sky over in the northwest direction. I look up and see what I thought was a falling star but it stopped in mid air. I was in shock! I blinked several times to makes sure what I was seeing. It fell out of the sky just like a meteor, it was bright white and shiny and it just stopped. It held its position for a few seconds and then takes off at a higher rate of speed than when it descended. It flew off to the north in a flash and was gone. I was shaken to my core, fear just coursed through me. I drove home very fast and never looked back. I didn't want to see that because I couldn't explain it. This happened in 1992 and still to this day I've never seen anything like it.
