Saw a fast light traviling from the North East to the South East.

My husband and I were sitting in our backyard smoking a cigarette when we saw a fast moving redish orange light traviling from the North East to the South East. The light stopped and hovered for about 10 seconds and then started to move again quickly further to the South East. Seven minutes later the same light reappeared from the North East and continued to travel much slower to the South East and then disappeared. At first look we thought that the light we saw was a ball of some kind. When the light got into better view I jumped up and took a picture both times. When looking at the pictures after they were taken we saw that the lights were not actually a ball but a round shape and a yellowish white color.
