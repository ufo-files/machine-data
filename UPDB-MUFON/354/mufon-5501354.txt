{cak} Flame ball too high to be a firework slowly disappeared while observation.

Sitting in my car and resting my eyes. Fireworks start going off and I notice a Flame ball in the sky first thought was a firework of course, but did not disappear. Stunned by the sighting I get out of my car and call my dad, but he didn't answer. Called the home phone and he picks up I tell him come out quick. I was just outside so he came out and caught the last seconds of the glimpse. It appeared to be two fireballs on big one smaller. Not symmetrical. Just like the color of a camp fire. But very high in the sky.
