{cak} Driving east on Route 50, ship comes into view from above, glows golden color, then disappears

I was on my way driving to Salisbury to see my friends. I was going east on Route 50 when I noticed this glowing, golden object fly overhead my car. It steadied its speed once it was ahead of me. There was a car next to me that noticed it as well. It seemed like the ship glowed a golden amber color from inside itself, and it only showed itself for about 10 seconds. (At first I thought the glowing object was a reflective balloon floating, but then I realized there was nothing for the object to reflect off of. There were no street lights anywhere and it was pitch black outside.) I also realized that the ship was only partially illuminated. I couldn't see the front or sides of it, only part of the back side. The only thing I could gather was that it was an oval/cigar shape, and it was weirdly glowing. (I think the glowing gold color was how the ship got its power.) There were no flashing lights or anything to signify it was man-made. There were no other airplanes/helicopters nearby. It literally disappeared in the blink of an eye, it was gone. I didn't hear any noises emitted from it, and my car and electronics were not affected during this time. I did not lose track of time, it was the most bizarre experience.
