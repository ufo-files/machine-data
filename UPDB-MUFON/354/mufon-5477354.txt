Was looking at the sky at some contrails and the patterns they were making. I did not see any planes in the area when I looked to the left and saw what looked like a white egg with another orb of light to the right of it. I stayed looking for another five minutes looking to see if it had moved on to another location but couldn't find it. We live in the country so we have open sky around us.
