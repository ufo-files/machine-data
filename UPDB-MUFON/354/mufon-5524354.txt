Bright amber starlike orb hovering & descending very slowly almost floating

I was outside on my driveway looking at the stars when this bright amber orb appeared out of nowhere. It was low, descending very slowly and looked like a bright star coming right for me even though it wasn't that close. There was no sound, I couldn't make out any structure to it and as soon as it appeared the light disappeared but I felt like it was still there cloaked.
