A bright light on horizon 2 others at greater distance.

MY friend and I were on the barracks roof of Charleston Air Force Base. in the 
summer of 1967. We had a 20-60-power spotting scope with us . I was on the Large Bore Rifle Team. We thought we would do some star/moon gazing. 
We spotted low on the horizon a very bright light. The scope was useless the light was too bright too much speed. 

We then noticed two other bright lights much higher then the first .They traveled to the ESE 4-6 times faster then any F-4 jet at full throttle.
The one on the horizon had hovered then moved slowly away.

My friend and I talked about what we saw up on the roof. When went down to the ground we did not report the even ,because we were not supposed to be up on the roof. We never mentioned the incident again. I have since lost touch with the friend I was on the roof with that night.
