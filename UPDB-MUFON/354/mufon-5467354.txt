My experience was around 1996 with my 16 year old daughter at the time on our way to seminary (early morning religious class for LDS youth).

I looked up into the early morning sky and saw three very bright lights moving quickly around. I joked with my daughter and said hey look UFO's and she laughed with me. Then the three lights merged into one bright light, then in an instant we witness it not more than 50 feet away from us hovering above the ground about 20 feet with no sound what so ever. It was triangle in shape with Egyptian like markings. We witness this for about two to three min then it hovered away. I turned my car around to follow and in a instant it was so far away I guess about a mile or two just like that! 

The whole experience was about 15 to 20 min. We both witness the event. It was amazing! It is now 2018 and 22 years later have not seen any government planes to resemble it.
