Taking a picture of supposed stars.

I was in the yard out the back looking at the stars, the moon had pretty much gone down already so Venus was pretty prominent. I went to take a picture but couldn't seem to find the star on my low quality phone. I turned on HDR mode and tried again still to little success. I went back through my images and saw a purple glowing shape in the sky. It wasn't very detailed and had bad graphics but it was something. I took more pictures (about 20 more) to see if it was a trick of light and if I could recreate it. No other images I took resulted in the light. I did not see the object with my bare eyes, only in the split second I took on my phone. I started to ponder whether it could be extraterrestrial but was too Afraid to really believe it. After some thought though, I do believe that this could be alien or some unusual object in the sky as creating the 'trick' again seemed impossible.
