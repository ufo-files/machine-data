{nd} Woke up in the middle of the night to a strange sound I've never heard and saw a blue/white orb hovering outside of my bedroom window.

I was sleeping in my bed and awoke to a strange sound. It was very high pitched and seemed like a chirping, purring, or singing of sorts...some sound I have never heard before. I own a set of crystal singing bowls and it slightly resembled the sound of two being played together. I rolled over to look at my window where the sound had been coming from. I saw a blue/white light, and at first I thought it was a cell phone or a laptop monitor or something. Initially I thought maybe someone was trying to break into my room, but then I realized that sound could not be coming from a human. The object didn't appear to move, and the sounds/toneswere changing as if it were communicating or singing. It didn't sound angry or violent, but I was terrified...I remember looking at my cat who was at the end of my bed and realized that my cat was observing this object too. I don't recall exactly how long it went on for but I can remember at least a few minutes of sitting, staring and listening, just petrified. I wanted to move the blinds from my window to see what was there but I just couldn't do it. I don't recall the exact moment that things ended, but I do remember checking my ipad next to my bed and it was 2:57 a.m. After the event I was terrified and almost wanted to cry because I had no idea what had just happened. I eventually cuddled up with my cat and fell back asleep...I remember a few times in the night that nugget (the cat) jumped in and out of bed, and at one point I remember seeing him sitting on the windowsill as if he were looking for something...He doesn't commonly sit there, so I thought that was particularly strange....
