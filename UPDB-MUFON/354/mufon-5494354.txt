{dr}Large shimmering molten morphing light ascending descending to naked eye but vidcam captured it as a sphere

At about 11pm on Thurs. nite, April 30, I went to close my bedroom window facing south and I noticed a very large and bright elongated light shimmering and morphing in a stationary position, seeming only a mile or two away. I thought maybe it was a tall light setup they use during the night on freeway construction projects. But this was not acting like a stationary construction light. It was just way too bright. So I went outside and had to pull out an 8ft ladder to see it above the treeline.It stayed in that first configuration until helicopters arrived then it seemed to get agitated and changed from a sphered to a raging vortex. The vidcam lenses only portrayed it as a sphere or vortex, yet to the naked eye it appeared elongated and morphing into all kinds of elongated shapes. The moon, later that morning, also exhibited the same vidcam look. And it moved in the sky , bathed in an eerie brown to ochre colored cirrus . It was not the moon and made me feel nauseated,.confused, panicked and tired. After 5 hrs of filming it, I went to bed.
