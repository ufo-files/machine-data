{jpg} I was outside looking at the stars when I observed a distant light moving at a very high rate of speed

I smoke outside numerous times a day and at night. I have seen meteors before but never have seen anything moving at this height at this rate of speed. The object appeared significantly higher than a commercial jet and traveled further and faster than any meteor that I have ever seen in my life. It almost appeared to be moving in an arc around the circumference of the earth and travel a great distance in just a second or two before I lost site of it.
