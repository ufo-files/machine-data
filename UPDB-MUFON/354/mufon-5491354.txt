{cak} black triangle white on corners one in middle and a lead red light

I was with friends on the driveway of my parents. My friend said what's that and point to the NW. I was looking in the distance but could not see it. He than said it right there and it was and 100ft away. It was a black triangle no sound and seemed to hover. there were white light in the corner a white red in the middle and a lead red light. It then seemed to just fly away and blended with the stars. This was in 1997 and since I was in the USAF for 7 years. I deployed 2x to Iraq and have seen every aircraft from Stealth Bomber CH-53 Osprey F-22 C-17 and many more. I have never seen anything else like it since and I truly believe what we saw was not of this world. Again I have seen aircraft from the US and Russia and never have seen anything like it. This was one reason I joined the USAF.
