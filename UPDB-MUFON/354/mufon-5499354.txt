{nd} Flotilla of 13 or more craft, acting like a flock of brids

While looking for the northern lights, I observed a flotilla of at least thirteen craft, they were very dim. I watched them for about 10 minutes, they were like a flock of birds playing in the sky. They would change the direction and there formation(s). They just kept getting dimer and dimer into the night sky, and then just faded away. I hope others have seen this and reported it also..
