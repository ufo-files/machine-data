Large lighted round objects

At approximately 900 pm went outside to see what sister in law was witnessing I figured just another aircraft as we are close to military base (fort lewis, Mcchord) but after looking with binoculars realized was not any type of aircraft I had ever seen. The two objects were well lit and one of the two went north and was no longer visible and the second object seemed to hover , and turn in position. A couple of minutes later the second object started moving north changing colors as it went, to a red and then a green, again I figured it was probably a plane but again with the binoculars I saw that the complete structure was changing color not just a stationary position such as a light on a wing.
