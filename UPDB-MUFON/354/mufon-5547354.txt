Three shiny pearl-like spheres at high altitude.

My father came in from outside to tell me that he saw an object in the sky. I walked outside and barely made out a small white speck high in the sky. After several seconds I was able to make out another object next to the first, then a third. The object looked only like a small white speck so I decided to get out my reflecting telescope to get a better look. Once I found the objects again I focused my telescope on the objects. They appeared to be perfect spheres with a pearl-like quality but were very reflective as I could see the reflection of the sun perfectly on the object. They did not appear to be balloons as they were a perfect sphere with no tip and no shape similar to that of a balloon. Also they were stationary above the small town in which we live. As for size, I witnessed a passenger airliner pass between me and the objects, the diameter was the length of the wings but the object could be larger as it was behind the airliner and it could of been much further behind and above. After thirty minutes or so they began to slowly break from their loose formation and began to very slowly drift apart until I simply couldn't look through the telescope due to the bright sky and my eyes. They are still there as far as I know.
