oblong, rotating, rotating color

I was walking south looking up watching the barn swallows flying for their dinner.I was at Volunteer park. Past the birds i thought I saw a bald eagle flying. I love to watch them and so I did. I thought I saw the white of the tail. The movement was strange for a bird , they usually fly up high on updrafts ,circling. This did not circle. It rotated,and sped up and slowed down. It looked like it became two, so i thought oh thats why it weird there are two. I sat and watched, and as it flew I knew that it was no bird, so I started to think it was a mylar balloon. As it rotated tail over end, it would go black , bright flash, then sometimes red , and then disappear.This was a constant the changing of color. Sometimes black to disappear, or black to red to a flash, then it would disappear.It was going in the direction of the wind,but it was moving faster than the clouds. I watched until i could barley see it. and at that point it would go black to a quick flash to clear. It became consistent, perhaps due to the distance.I was like wow that was cool. I felt giddy. I looked around and no one was looking. This lasted for about two to three minutes. It also appeared to have some sort of emissions, but i was not so sure because it was against cloud cover.
