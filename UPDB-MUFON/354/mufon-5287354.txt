Object seen on US Rt. 33 between Buckhannon and Weston WV

Traveling east on Route 33 at roughly 1:45am between Buckhannon and Weston West Virginia, my Wife and I observed what can only be described as three large spheres inside of a rectangle. It hovered about 30-50 feet above one of the hilltops on the left(South) side of the highway. The hilltops through there are about 100-400 feet higher than the highway. We got an excellent view of it for roughly 1-2 minutes. I was driving, so I was only seeing it for a few seconds at a time. I had just looked back at the road and that’s when my wife said “It just disappeared!” When I looked back in the sky where it was, it was ,in fact, gone. I had only taken my eyes off of it for a few seconds to look at the road. When we arrived back at our house in Harrison county at around 2:15am my brother texted me to ask if I had made it home ok. To which I replied yes, but that we had basically seen a UFO. My brother was absolutely floored because he and his wife had just seen the same object but they witnessed it roughly 2 miles to the west on Rt. 33 from where we witnessed it. They passed through that same area 30-45 minutes before we passed through. The object did not appear to move at all when we observed it. If it was moving it was moving at about 2-4 miles per hour.
