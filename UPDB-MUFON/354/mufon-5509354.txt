(GS) A bright orange glow a couple of miles away in the sky about the size of a large aircraft.

Southeast from my house and a bright orange glow (brighter than a plane) was a couple of miles away in the sky about the size of a large aircraft. It was below clouds so it was not a star or a satellite. It was also not stationary. It glided slowly across the horizon for a good 20 minutes then just faded out of sight.
