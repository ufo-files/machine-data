{cak} Stopped counting after 13. All were equally spaced & following a straight path. All appeared from 1 point in sky.

February 18, 2020. It was 5:50 a.m. It was still dark & cold, about 20 degrees.

I was on my way to work. I walked out of the house & looked up at the sky to see if it was clear or cloudy so I knew what the day was going to start out like.

I could see stars all around. As I was starting to look away, something caught my eye. I looked again & saw a red round object moving across the sky.

At first, I thought it was a satellite, but I have seen satellites before. This was much closer.

After I starred at the red light for a few seconds, I noticed another red light behind it and another and another. They were equally spaced following one another. I don't know how many I had missed that were in front of the one that caught my eye, but I counted 13 before I quit counting and they were still coming.

I followed them back to where they were coming from. They were coming from what appeared to be a faintly lit star. They just appeared out of nowhere & followed the one in front of it. This was not over the horizon. It was directly in front of me, overhead.

They were all equally spaced & following each other in a straight line. No other movement. They started right in front of me & traveled to the northeast.

I didn't see any flashing lights like on a plane. There was no sound or noise.

I have seen meteors before. This was not a meteor. There were no flashes of light or tails on them like when a meteor enters the atmosphere & burns up.
