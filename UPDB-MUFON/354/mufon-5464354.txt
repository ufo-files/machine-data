{rjl} Two vapor trails very apparent even though it was nighttime.

As we were driving home to Slaton from Lubbock my wife pointed something out to me as we were coming around the curve at the feedlots and prison. There were two low vapor trails that seemed very close together and very low. Even though it was already dark outside for the night both of these trails were very easily noticeable to the eye but not able to have a clear picture taken with a phones camera. We were most intrigued by their location as the direction they moved in was not from any direction of an airport or landing strip and nowhere an aircraft that could leave a vapor trail could take off or land at. We wish we were on the road earlier to see what could have made such a trail so obvious at night but unfortunately we never saw a craft of any kind. We hope our report will be a part of a story told by others so we can help collaborate an event and put our curiosity to rest over what it was we witnessed. Our minds are left to imagine and being two people who have seen unexplained things before we do hope to find an explanation. Thank you for your time in looking at this and I hope we can help with anything else you may need.

Sincerely,
