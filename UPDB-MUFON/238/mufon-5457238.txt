Small orange light that moved too fast for any craft across the sky

Orb of glowing orange light, from where I looked it was the size of an aspirin. It flew about 1 mile above a field close by and flickred and gone when it was above me. The distance it traveled in 10 seconds would have taken at least thrice as much for any kind of aircraft.
