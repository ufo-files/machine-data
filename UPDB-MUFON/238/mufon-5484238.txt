{nd} 3 large orbs observed, one by one faded followed by fast moving glow

While talking to a friend on his driveway around 9 pm, we looked up to the southwest skies to see 3 large reddish-orange orbs slowly moving, changing orientation. Being familiar with these I told my friend that they would start fading one by one. Right on cue they started to do so. After about two minute they had completely faded out. Seconds after the last point faded out a much larger greyish glow raced across the sky, from the area last seen and towards the west sky. The greyish glow seemed to be behind the low cloud deck but the orbs appeard to be below the cloud deck. The cloud deck was very low and thin this evening. A small portion of cell phone video was taken but its not very good and difficult to see.
