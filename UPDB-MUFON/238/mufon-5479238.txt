{nd} Unknown flying object flew over property

I was out checking my cattle at night and heard a strange noise to the east. I saw a glowing flying object that did not move like a plane or a helicopter. It flew over my field and hovered for a short period of time and instantly soared to the west. I have never seen anything fly like this before. My neighbor has described similar sightings to me in the past.
