{rjl} multiple orange lights flying overhead

I was taking out the trash while talking on my cellphone when I looked up and saw two strange orange glowing objects flying pretty close together coming from the southwest pretty much straight over my house. There is regular flight traffic over the area being right outside of PHL but I've never seen objects like these. The objects were flying in a direction that I don't usually see planes flying in either. I yelled for my roommate to come outside and he saw them too. First was a pair, then a minute or two later another pair flew over head. The second pair was almost crossing a commercial airline which appeared be unaffected. Both pairs flew in the same direction. I used the compass app and determined they were flying in the direction of 30degrees NE. A few minutes later another single object of the same brightness flew overhead in the same direction and then another one a few minutes after that. Making it 6 total. They certainly were not commercial airlines. They were dead silent and moved smoothly across the sky right before cloud cover appeared over the area. We only lost sight when they flew too far NE for us to see.
