Lights in sky and craft/object above me.

On the night in question I left my house into my car out front started the car and pulled out ,saw straight away out my windscreen the lights in the sky almost like stars but brighter and in formation moving in the same rotation pattern ,5 of them in formation then a sudden change they started to leave the atmosphere one by one on the same tragectory ,leaving at a fast pace into space , then as I looked down from the sky I saw a object/craft hovering above the house silently rotating ,it was dark see through almost and cylindrical about the size of a large car and I could tell it was observing me ,then it started moving east across the roof tops silently quiet slow and as it went I lost sight of it behind the light of the street lamp.
