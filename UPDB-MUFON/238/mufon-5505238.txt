{rjl} Witnessed a cigar like object in the sky.

We were driving from DIA to Colorado Springs. Enjoying the lovely scenery I noticed a cigar like object in the SW sky. It was light blue/grey in color. It hovered as clouds went over it.I pointed it out to my friend. He said keep watching it as did he, best he could while driving. A cloud went over the cigar like object and it was gone. It did not appear before or after the cloud stream. I was very excited with my siting. I fully believe we witnessed a I DO.
