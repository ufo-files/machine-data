{rjl} I WAS OUTSIDE LOOKING AT AIRCRAFT WHEN I NOTICED A WHITE FLUORESCENT LUMINOUS BOOMERANG FLY ACROSS THE SKY

FILLED OUT AT MUFON HEADQUARTERS WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18809 ON NOVEMBER 21, 2017
---------------------------------------------------------------
IT WAS 6:14. A CLEAR NIGHT AND I WAS OUTSIDE RELAXING AND LOOKING AT AIRCRAFT WHEN I NOTICED SOME SMALL RED LIGHTS, THEY APPEARED TO BELONG TO AIRCRAFT LANDING NEARBY. WHILE I WAS WATCHING THE PLANES I SAW A WHITE FLUORESCENT LUMINOUS BOOMERANG FLY ACROSS THE SKY.
IT WAS AS IF A SEARCHLIGHT HAD ILLUMINATED THAT PART OF THE SKY IN A BOOMERANG SHAPE. IT WAS FAST, ABOUT 10000 MPH. I SAW THE UNDERBELLY OF THE CRAFT WHICH WAS FLUORESCENT GLOWING WHITE WITH THREE LITTLE BRIGHT LIGHTS ON THE WING TIPS AND OTHER LIGHTS ON THE WINGS AND OTHER PARTS OF THE BODY OF THE CRAFT. IT APPEARED TO BE A LOT LARGER THAN THE COMMERCIAL AIRCRAFT AND FLYING A LOT LOWER. THE SIGHTING LASTED ABOUT 2 SECONDS. 
OTIS AIR FORCE BASE IS NEAR BY, BUT I KNOW THAT WASN'T A MILITARY CRAFT.
I CAN SKETCH A DRAWING OF WHAT I SAW.

ANOTHER SIGHTING I HAD WAS AT THE SAME LOCATION IN THE LAST TWO WEEKS OF DECEMBER OF 2013. ONCE AGAIN WAS OUTSIDE RELAXING, IT WAS ABOUT 8 OR 9 PM. I SAW A BRIGHT LIGHT IN THE SKY. IT WENT FROM WHITE TO ALL THE COLORS OF THE RAINBOW AS IT STREAKED ACROSS THE SKY. THIS LASTED FOR 10 TO 15 SECONDS. IT HAS A STRAIGHT LINE PATH. IT JUST TAPERED OFF AND DISAPPEARED.
 
A WEEK LATER I WAS OUTSIDE IN 6" OF SNOW ABOUT 50' FROM THE HOUSE AT ABOUT 11:45 PM. I WAS ADJUSTING A THERMOSTAT IN THE BACK YARD, AS I TURNED TO GO IN THE HOUSE I SAW A SMOKEY BLACK TRIANGLE IN THE SKY. IT WAS A CLEAR NIGHT SO I COULD SEE THE OUTLINE OF THE CRAFT VERY CLEARLY. IT WAS IN THE EAST AT ABOUT 5 MILES AWAY. IT SEEMED TO HAVE A FIELD OF ENERGY AROUND IT, I FELT THE HAIR ON MY ARMS MOVE.
