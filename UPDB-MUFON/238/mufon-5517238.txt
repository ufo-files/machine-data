(gs) Strange glowing light that rapidly flew up words

I live in the Hayward hills, I have a view overlooking the city. I often leave my curtain open at night to see the lights of the city. I was about to go to bed when I noticed a strange glowing light hoover in one place far above the city. I originally thought it was a helicopter but I noticed it stayed perfectly still as the light coming from it began to brighten. From the elevation above the city it was pretty clearly not a helicopter to me because there would be no way the light would reach the area below. It stayed in spot for about a couple minutes and the light appeared to slowly dissipate to where I almost couldnt see it anymore, and then it in a split second appeared far brighter than it had been originally and what ever it was shot upwards at an amazing high rate of speed I have seen any helicopter ever be able to move any where close to the speed it reached as it shot up.
