Orange orb, large orb, boomerang 3-4 aircraft carriers long

Two sightings reported; Sept 28, 2011 and Feb 2 2012. Observed orange orb moving across field toward home. Stopped and hovered then moved up and became very brilliant. After it moved away observed a large triangluar structure of an object to light. Went to a different location to get better view as object was moving away but it was gone. The second sighting was again at night and of a same orange type orb but this time at a distance. It shortly moved out of sight.
