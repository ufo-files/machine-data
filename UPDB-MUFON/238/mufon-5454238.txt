UFO was length of 2 football fields observed by myself and two children

We left Seattle towards Spokane on our way to Jackson Hole Wy for a vacation. As soon as we arrived at a motel in down town Spokane I called a relative to say we had arrive. Strangely, she said there was no way we could be there that fast unless we drive 100 mph!  I do not speed. I shrugged her comment off and we finished our vacation. Either one or possibly 2 years later my children and I were watching a “special” on local tv (I guess channel 7, 11 or 13) when the show began showing picture after picture of ufos. One photo came on and at EXACTLY the same time we all three said “that the one we saw!” At that time we all remembered everything about our sighting. Standing on a street in Spokane, looking up at this spaceship. I remembered thinking I had to relate the size to something and thought “a football field”. And then corrected myself to “2” football fields. We looked for a helicopter that must be flying this advertisement overhead. There was zero noise. We could see movement in the “windows” and multiple lights inside  I floated slowly away over the city and we went to our hotel.  Never to mention it or remember having seen it until that tv show years later.  We wish we could find that tv show again. The minors were 11 & 13 In 1987. We have to wonder if that is how we arrived in Spokane so fast and why wouldn’t we have taken photos (we all had cameras).  Also, there is no way I would have stopped on some side street in a strange town and had my children standing in the street.
