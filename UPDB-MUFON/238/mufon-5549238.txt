08-31-06

We went to our hot tub in Schertz at 5:00 Am. We observed a bright flashing light low in the south east sky it looked like it was over Seguin (North East). It had Red, Green and White lights and seemed to be revolving fast and all lights were seen at the same time some twinkling brighter than others trading brightness. We observed it for 30 min and went back inside. Could this be a planet magnified in the horizon? I say lights but these seem to envelope the object if in fact it was an object they seemed like holographic in nature it is hard to describe, it did seem to move a bit but was last seen in the same position. While we were looking at the object we failed to immediately turn off our 300 watt flood light (the only one on at that time) shortly after turning it down to its dimmest setting, we heard a harmonic hum for about 20 seconds a weird hum almost like a howl but mechanical. Two years ago we saw similar objects stationary in the north south and east and west when we live in San Antonio.
After viewing photos close up on my computer un-retouched just original photo zoomed in to 150% I don1t think that is a planet!
