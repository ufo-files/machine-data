{nd} Saw three UFO's above US 96 heading towards Grand Rapids. Drove directly under the first one. All three had their own unique characteristics.

A buddy and I were heading to Big Rapids from Lapeer for the weekend. We left around 9-9:30. 10-15 after taking the US 96 exit I noticed two extremely bright lights low in the sky above the road in front of us. The only reason it caught my attention was because it was extremely cloudy out. As we drove closer we realized that we were getting closer to the brighter of the two lights and that it was almost directly over the road(100-150ft high). It had blinking red and white lights on the outside and bottom of the craft. We eventually drove under the craft and that's when I noticed that it had boomerang shaped wings and and was hovering motionless above us not making a sound. After passing the first craft we started getting nearer to the second light(unknowing that it too would be an unexplainable craft). It wasn't as close to us as the first one and was above the trees to our left but as we came closer to it I realized that it was dome shaped and had yellowish lights spinning around it. Once we passed it it changed direction and proceeded to head in the opposite direction. The third one was also on our left about three minutes after seeing the second craft. This one was a lot darker but it had three red lights on all points of the triangle shaped craft. After several moments went by it disappeared out of the blue.
