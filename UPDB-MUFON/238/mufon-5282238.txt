Saw a light in the sky above remote mountain ridge with a beam pulling something along ground.

While going up to a remote gold mining claim near Cave Junction, Oregon, myself and four others witnessed a mysterious object hovering over a mountain top, approximately a half-mile away (google earth referenced). It looked like a bright white light. As we watched, fascinated, a white beam formed below the object, between the ground and the craft, and slowly stretched each direction until it seemed to contact the ground and the craft. It looked like multi-colored particalse were being carried up from the ground to the craft through the beam. The craft began to move slowly towards the north, along the mountain ridge. Then the beam shut off, and the craft shot up and out of sight. All five of us observed it and were at a loss trying to explain what it possibly was. We have also, since, seen several mysterious objects in the night sky while out at the claim, but nothing as strange as this. I tried to capture a moment with my phone camera, but was not prepared, and the video does not really give much detail, nor even look like what I actually observed at the time. I could not see the 'beam' in the video, but could in real life, which was odd. I am including it anyway, in case it has some value.
