UFO sighting when I was a child with multiple friend witnesses

This is a story related in 3rd person since I'm the boyfriend of the witness and friend of the others:
Santa eulalia, vizela, Portugal 
8-10 years old
She was playing hide and seek with friends at night and then she and her cousin saw what they thought to be a plane showing a lot of lights, they both starting looking at the “plane” until it got closer and closer to about 15m. The numerous lights, constant, red, blue, green. Looked metallic grey, no body panels, one solid piece, at the top looked like it had a dome that could be opened, it wasn’t rotating, slowly coming down , they had no fear, 2-3 minutes of contact, no sound at all, no heat, wind...
