{rjl} short creatures came into bedroom from the window and stuck a needle into my hand

I was a young child, still in a crib, suspect about 3 years old, my twin brother was also in his crib in the same room. there was just a hint of light from beyond the hill, maybe just the first bit of morning, but it was still dark. two creatures came into the window (strange because it was on the second floor) and one came to my crib, it was in silouette and had a large head in relation to its body and was about head high to me in the crib lying down (maybe 3 feet tall) for some reason i did not move or scream, it stuck a needle of sorts into my hand near the thumb but i felt no pain. one of the creatures went to my brothers crib. they left out the window. the next morning there was a red mark, like a red mole, where i was stuck. it remained for many years until a burn scarred it over. i told my parents first thing in the morning, they said i was dreaming? iwas only about 3, had never heard anything in 1955 about little creatures or even aliens. i still vividly remember it as if it were yesterday and still wonder what really happened.
