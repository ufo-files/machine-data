Photographed moon and afterwards noticed objects to the left of moon

On a sunset cruise in Plymouth Massachusetts I photographed with my Olympus E-M10 mirrorless camera the moon rising ESE at approx. 6:44pm Sept 19, 2021. I zoomed in on the image and noticed two objects to the left of the moon. I made a joke to some people that I may have captured and image of UFO's. I then said it's probably military aircraft, because there is a nuclear power site in Plymouth. A young man from the military said to me, wait I can check for you I have an app that shows the location of all aircraft commercial and military at any given moment. He checked and said no aircraft were in the area at that time.
