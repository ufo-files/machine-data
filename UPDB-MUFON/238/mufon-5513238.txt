{dr}It was a large, whitish boomerang shaped object in the southern sky, that descended quickly in an arc shape into the east.

I was skiing along the road side shortly after 5 p.m. The sun was not yet down, there were lines of clouds that were pinkish and dark grey, suddenly, I saw a whitish boomerang shaped object--kind of a cross between a U and a V, which caught my eye because it was a sharp contrast with the gray sky and the clouds. It quickly descended into the treeline or cloud in an arc shape. It lasted just a few seconds, but the object was very large and in front of the clouds--not like an airplane or jet at all. Hard to say if it was a meteorite--but if it was, it was very large...large enough that I waited to listen to hear if it hit--but there was no sound, and it didn't have a trail, and didn't streak through the sky--it definitely curved through.
