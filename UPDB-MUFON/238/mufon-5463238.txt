{cak} Multi Lights, Hovering, just over tree tops to East & South East

This item appears to be a "star" but has multi lights in the shape of a "Xmas Tree" that hovers and move very slowly. It has been seen on a number of days in the early AM (4-6 AM +/-) from my house deck. The first sighting was due east and moved slightly to the south. Recently it has been seen more to the south east. When I first saw it, I thought it was a star, then possibly a helicopter or maybe a drone, but after closer watching it was a cluster of "star lights". I would guess about 5 lights. No Red or Green lights like an airplane or helicopter. Aylett County is north east of Richmond, VA. I do not have binoculars or telescope and cell phone camera shots I tried do not show it very well.
