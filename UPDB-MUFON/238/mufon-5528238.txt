Small circle, changed to 2 teardrops connected then to a rectangle.

On January 8th, 2011 while working a security post in OK I was observing work being done on a crane. While I was watching a small silver dot appeared. The object was over the weight side of the crane about 10 degrees. The object shape started as a small circle, then while moving it appeard to change to 2 teardrop connected at the small end. When It was about half way across the horizon it seemed to change to a more rectangle shape. When it reached the futherest part behind my vehicle It sped up and then disappeared. I believe it to be a great distance from my location, the speed was a constant high rate of speed, absolutly no noise, no contrail. I see jet airliners, small planes and helicopters all the time. I was first surprised by seeing the object mainly because I had been watching the work on the crane for most of the day. The whole event was a total of less than 30 seconds. My training is observe and report, and to be detailed in all accounts. I did not have a camera with me at the time. I am looking for a explaination for what I saw and if anyone else has seen it.
