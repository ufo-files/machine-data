{rjl} bright object in sky then get dull get bright again moved slowly north

Saturday afternoon. relaxing on hammock and noticed bright object in sky, bright light like airplane then got dull, disappeared then appeared bright again. moved very slowly going north bound. Left to get binoculars and found object again. Through binoculars it appeared like a small cone shape or round, the reflection/brightness of light would not let me get a real good luck. When it would get dull (no longer bright) it appeared the object was long like a short pencil in a vertical position. I also spotted a white orb moving by it. I decided to keep focused on the shinny object and the orb left my view from the binoculars. I called my daughter who also saw a white object slowly move and appear/disappear. This event lasted a full 20 min. at least. That is my report.
