{tjd} Zig zag motion with 5 directional changes, slowed and hovered high above hiuse

I was standing outside looking at the sky. I first noticed an object moving which was flashing white and changing direction. I thought at first it was a plane but there were no other lights or sound. Then another light showed up which was white and steady. It zig zagged to about five different points very quickly and then slowly moved directly over the house. It must have been at least 1 mile up directly over house. I became overcome with total fear. I went back in the house and then returned outside a few minutes later and it was gone. I have looked for it again in the same area several nights and have never seen it again. I did not have my phone with me so I do not have a video which is very disappointing.
