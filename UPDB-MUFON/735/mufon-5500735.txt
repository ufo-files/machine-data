{rjl} I just saw it hovering through the trees. It was slowly pulsating then dimmed out. It was never there before.

I was sitting in my wheelchair on the ramp that's on the east side of my house, in the morning of the 23rd waiting on a raccoon that had been killing my ducks and chickens. At about 2.50 am I looked north into the woods and I noticed a fairly bright orange ball glowing through the trees. It resembled a full moon in size. It never moved at all. It just sat in the sky lightly pulsating. I was fixed on it, even though it was behind the trees. I could see it quite clearly, then it just faded out. I really didn't think too much about it until I seen an article from a woman in Altoona, Pa. She had a video and It looked so much like what I saw. I was really struck with aw at it. It only stayed there for about 5 minutes. You couldn't help but stare. I just wish it would have been there long enough to go grab a camera. But mine doesn't do so good at night. If anyone else has seen this glowing ball in my area , I would really like to know. Thank you.
