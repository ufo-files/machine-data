Unidentified Flying Object, March 15th, 2019.  Disc Shape stop and move fast at different directions , no sound.

March 15, 2019 at 22:24. I went to the terrace (because my neighbor told me the day before, what she saw a light making erratic movements in the mountains.) So I took out my camera, the tripod, I configured the settings, I focused and I was observing the sky. At 5 minutes, I began to observe a light that was flying at great speed and left a beam or a wake. The first thing I thought of a comet. But he started making circles in the air, that's when I decided to zoom in x10 digitally and I was surprised. It had a silver disc shape and lights. I focused and began to follow it, I was able to make 4 photos of which one went wrong and the other 3 went well focused and without traces in the stars. I have converted this photograph to black and white in order to better appreciate the detail. If you look at the top left of the photograph, there is a wake or beam. Expand the photograph to be able to observe it better and draw your own conclusions. What is certain, that on that Mount lights have been seen flying since 1960 and there are reports and reports of events.
