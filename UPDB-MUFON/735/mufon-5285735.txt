My husband and I witnessed 2 different crafts in 30min

My husband went out to smoke and saw a long dark object blocking out most of the big dipper in the sky. He said his eyes had time to focus and as soon as they did the craft shot off at unbelievable speed. I came out about 30min later to watch the sky and witnessed what looked liked part of the sky, but moving, shaped like a v, dart across the sky going south. Neither made a sound.
