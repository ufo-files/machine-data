A friend and I were way out in the country on a lake, clear night at the end of May, 2009. We were star gazing, and my friend had his tripod set up. We suddenly saw a moving large star like ufo. Extremely high altitude, and it moved at a semi fast rate, then turned and went away from view. It was not a satellite, as I've seen them slowly move through the sky before. It was very startling to both of us and my friend took a great picture, leaving his exposure open to see the path of the ufo.
