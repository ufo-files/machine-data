Bright light ejected a smaller dimmer light

The night of September 26, 2020 at around 9:45 pm. From my back yard in N/E Austin Tx as I looked at the night sky, I do this every night. S/W from my home towards Manor Tx. I saw an object light up very bright in the sky at about a 30 deg above horizon. Immediately a less bright object split from it and moved away then dimmed out. Next the very bright object moved a short distance and also dimmed out. There were no sounds or contrails . The objects seemed to be several miles away. Some activity is taking place here in Central Tx. Last night there was a bright pinpoint flash of light almost overhead, again no sound or evidence it was a meteor or meteorite. I keep looking.
