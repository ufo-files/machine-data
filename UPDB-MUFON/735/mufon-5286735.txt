Two Tremendous orbs by moon

At 5:39 PM on November 21, 2020 I was taking my dog out and noticed a strange light in the sky that was moving. I noticed that it was not blinking it was kind of pulsating between orange and white it appeared to be heading toward the moon, it past the moon and I was able to still see the bright object in front of the moon. At this time I didn’t notice the second object behind the moon until I was reviewing the video later. My neighbor and her husband, my wife and I all watched this object slowly move off at 60° Until gone.
