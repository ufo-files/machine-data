No sound Disc type with bright lights above big tree very slowly moved north at our home in village in India.

I was about 11 year old, it was evening time getting dark,and all my friends in village was together to was TV at our neighbor, remember that it was in village in india, and it was in early 1980s. in whole village there was only few TVs, not in every home so as all my friends and was wathching TV suddenly power went out very common in villages in India, next i very much remember to this day like all kid we start playing and making noises all the sudden i and my friends saw a disc type about with bright lights all around no sound at all above the big tree at our home moved very very slow from south to north I think at the most 40 feet above us, i remember us talking to each other that it not Helicopter or plane, some my friends at the time said it is Black magic but i do not think it was Black magic. the object did not reflect toward us anything or harm us.
