{jpg} I was traveling by car at night when an orb came directly behind my truck and then would disappear. When I stopped and got out of my truck in wells idaho I looked back and saw five 'greys' dancing under the street light where I just came from.

I was traveling from Jackpot Idaho to Wells Nevada around 2AM by myself in my truck with my dog. I noticed a small light coming up on the road behind me wondering what a motorcycle was doing out at night when the light got bigger and bigger and then was directly behind my truck, with no noise. My dog was growling. Then I looked back and it had totally disappeared. It did this several times and the more I looked at it when it was far behind the truck the faster it came right behind the truck. I started to get very scared because I knew this was something unnatural. This happened for several hours and I hurried to stay right behind a trucker. As I pulled into Wells Nevada I stopped at a small casino/restaurant to use the restroom and looked back at the road. In the light of the streetlamp were 4-5 skinny creatures with big heads dancing in the light of the street lamp. I rubbed my eyes and thought I had lost my mind but then looked again and they were still there. I went into the restaurant and stayed and had coffee. I told my kids about the light but never anyone about the little people until 2015, eleven years later. I saw the orbs on Ancient Aliens and those little people that other people have seen and the orb and little people/creatures were exactly what I saw eleven years ago.I am grateful that other people are telling what they have seen so I could open up about it too, because some people might think that you are loosing your mind.
