Well lit V shaped craft flying from West banking north

At 9:15 PM a well lit large V shaped craft with no sound entered my view from southwest made a smooth silent bank to the north at 20 to 40 thousand feet. Ten minute after I saw star like craft remain for a few seconds then accelerated into space. During this time there was a airplane making large low passes.
