{nd} Bright greenish light observed during daylight in the late 90's

My sighting occurred sometime in the Fall between 1997 and 1999. I cannot remember the exact date of my sighting; however I know that it was Fall because it was football season. I was sitting in the bleachers before the start of a football game. There was one set of bleachers that faced the football field, and behind the football field was a forest. It was early evening, and there was still plenty of sunlight, not a cloud in the sky. Out of nowhere a greenish ball of bright light appeared just above the tree line and was perfectly still. It was a solid light, not blinking or anything. After approx. 10 1 15 seconds, the ball of light shot off to the right faster than I have ever seen anything move. It moved faster than anything man made could even come close to matching. From time to time I still talk about it with other former classmates who also witnessed it. It is my one and only encounter, and I have never formally reported it. It is something I will never forget and I can still picture it in vivid detail.
