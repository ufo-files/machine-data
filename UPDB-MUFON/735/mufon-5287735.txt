Multiple solid white round objects moving left to right. Appearing in the Northwest and disappearing in the North part of the sky.

5-22-21. 21:45. Looking North, I saw multiple, round solid white, non blinking lights, approximately the size of baby aspirins. Most were evenly spaced moving left to right from the Northwest part of to sky, to the North part of the sky. Compass reference. Approximately at 45 degrees above the horizon. I observed them with my Bushenll 10x25 field glasses. No airplane marker lights were apparent. They just appeared in the NW sky and disappeared when the were in the North. I was Southwest of Miramar MCAS.
