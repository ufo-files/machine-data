{rjl} Stationery in Sky, geostationary

Stationary in sky at altitude viewed with binoculars for 1 hour. Perfect clear day. Appeared to be stationary with movement simulated by rotation of earth making it seem like it was moving from magnetic north to south toward rising sun position East at 2 hours prior to sunset. Inital location was 50 degrees at NE with apparent movement south. The reference altitude from several 40,000 feet jets put it at gt 100,000 feet. Clear observation with high powered binoculars with distinct shape like donut. Ruled out weather balloon, ISS, gas, star, moon, several witnesses
