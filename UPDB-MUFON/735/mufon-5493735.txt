{rjl} Saw a bright ball of light hanging in the air. About 8 or 9 of my neighbors in my condo complex also saw it. It was there all night. Nothing in daylight the next day.

On Saturday evening around 9pm, October 8, 2011, I was walking to the garbage bin outside my condo building and saw what looked like a bright ball of light hanging from the trees ahead. When I looked closer, it wasn't in the trees but in the sky. I moved to a clearing in our parking lot and saw a bright round object in the sky that didn't seem to move...it looked like it was just hanging there...hovering. I was facing South and am located at I-355 and the Maple Street exit (in Illinois). I waved over a neighbor of mine in the parking lot, and asked him if he saw it too...he did. We then scanned the sky and saw three lights in the distance behind our building and one more to the East of the object that had a red light flashing. None of these moved either. The one we were watching was the closest to us. It was East of the moon on a clear night. It's altitude was higher than a small aircraft (lots of small aircraft fly) but it was much lower than a commercial airplane. I took lots of pictures. During this time frame, about 8 or 9 other people in the condo complex watched it also. Since then, this object has been in the sky near my condo on a regular basis. It even has followed me to work in nearby Hinsdale, and followed me back home. The pictures attached were taken when I spent a weekend in Madison, Wisconsin March 27, 2015. The other set was taken at my condo building April 23,2015. It continues to appear in different locations nearby usually S, SW & North. Other balls of light are seen at a farther distance and usually are located in a triangle....S, SW & N.
