{cak} Multi white and one red flashing lights moving erratically.

For the past two years every night at approximately 8-9pm I see two sets of lights that seem to be too low and move too erratically for stars or satellites. There is one large white light in center with several smaller white lights from center and occasional red light that dim, brighten then disappear and reappear. These lights move in a very erratic pattern. while at the same time moving in a southeastern direction. I see these two lights every night at the same time as long as there is not heavy cloud cover. On nights with light cloud cover I can see the lights' reflection above the clouds. I am not familiar with satellites, but I think the movement is too erratic. These lights remain visible until they are out of vision from moving in a southeastern path-but they have an erratic movement while moving in a SE direction. I'm contacting your organization to hopefully determine what these lights are. I go outside on my back patio to smoke, and that is how I came to notice these lights. I have not taken any pictures because these lights are visible almost every night.
