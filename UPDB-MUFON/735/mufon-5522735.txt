I was traveling north on the 31 bypass near berrien springs on October 29 2011 sometime between 9-10pm. Somewhere between Snow Road and East Shawnee Road on the west side of the highway I saw a VERY large triangle of orangish-red lights taking up the sky in my drivers side window. At first I thought it was several (5) small airplanes waiting to land at the airport in berrien springs, but the lights were constant and moved together changing pitch in a V formation. The formation was huge, and i witnessed one light of the five fade out then another seemed to take it's place. I tried to monitor it's movements further but I was driving on a highway with my son sleeping in the back seat, I then got off at the Berrien Springs exit on Old 31 to get a safer look. Once I found a spot to safely pull over (at Berrien Dental off the highway) The formation was still by the area I first saw it and seemed to be moving slowly. Unfortunately I wasn't able to hang out long enough to see what happened. I believe what I saw was nothing "normal", but being a person of science I can't say what it was for sure.
