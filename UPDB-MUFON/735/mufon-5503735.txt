{dr}Noticed metal in a odd shape in Ocala National Forest

During a drive in the Ocala National Forest, we noticed an area of forest that had recently been cut and burned. As we drove by on the dirt road, something glinting caught our eye. We parked on the edge of the dirt road, walked to it and discovered a large area that had metal strips around it. As we looked at the area we then heard in the distance a noise that sounded like a MRI machine.
