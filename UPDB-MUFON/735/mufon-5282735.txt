Pulsating Orbs After Thunderstorm

I was taking video and photos of a thunderstorm that had passed by my home in Clinton Township, MI. As I scanned to the south to get some lightning photos, I noticed a pulsating orb just above the tree line. It was pretty bright and as I focused on the orb, I then noticed that there were 2 smaller orbs moving around near the larger orb. They all were consistently moving back and forth and hovering, however the larger, pulsating orb would hover more often. While I was taking the video, lightning was in the area, but it didn't seem to effect the orbs and they continued moving around or hovering. I have both photos and videos of this event. This went on from 9 pm to about 9:20 pm. I quit photographing/videoing when I couldn't see them anymore. The smaller ones just disappeared and the larger one moved off and then disappeared in the clouds.  I have seen these same type of orbs a few other times in the area, but this is the most recent sighting that I am reporting.
