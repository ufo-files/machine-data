(GS) It hovered over Dollar Tree and then started to move to the right. It turned into an orb of light and went flat and dissapeared.

My mother was in Dollar Tree in Rockdale County next to CrossPoint Cristian Church and I was in the car with my little sisters. I was looking out the window (with it rolled down) at the store to see if my mother was coming and I saw something out of the corner of my eye. I looked up and there was a triangular shaped... thing... I had no idea what it was!!!  It had circular lights that were very, very, bright in each corner of it. It hovered for a moment and then started to move to my right. As it moved, It began to get smaller until it turned into a small orb of bright light. It kept moving to my right and started to flatten. It kept flattening until it was so flat you could barley see it! Then, with a quick move diagonally, it vanished because into thin air. I didn't know what to feel! I kept staring at the sky until my mother came back, turned on the car, and drove away. I told her the whole story but I needed someone else to tell. I thought of you guys because I did a report for elementary school on you guys a long time ago.
