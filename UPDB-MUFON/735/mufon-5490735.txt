{jpg} Solid white cylinder slowly flying and appearing in and out of visibility

Somewhere before or around 7 pm Wednesday I was standing in my drive way before a storm rolled through. I looked up and saw what I thought may be a white piece of trash blowing in the wind of the storm and suddenly realized it was much larger and a solid shape. No flashing lights, no sound, no emission. Just a long cylinder in a slow trajectory heading south. What made it more bizarre is the longer I watched it, I saw vibrations seeming to imminate from it in a circular motion. Even more bizarre is that it was going in and out of visibility yet keeping the same exact path. Almost like it was cloaking and uncloaking its own visibility. Clouds never got in front of it. It finally headed over the tree line toward taylorsville.
