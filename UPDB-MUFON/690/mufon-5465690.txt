{rjl} Glowing object low in the sky, followed by a flash and a car losing all power

At a little before 9:00pm, I was driving eastbound on highway 12 in Wisconsin, between Sauk City and Middleton. The night was stormy and windy.
Right before I reached the base of a tall hill along the route, not too long after Breunig Rd, the highway seemed to empty out, and I found myself the only vehicle on the road. Suddenly, a bright white light flashed from the trees to my left, and my car simply seemed to turn off. I steered it to the shoulder..
Moments passed, and the car didn't turn back on. I heard the insects outside and everything was still. I slowly began to feel the uncanny feeling of being watched, and I smelled ozone.
The car turned back on after some minutes. Headlights and dashboard, and a truck drove past suddenly. 

Later, after calling a friend about this, the fact that it was nearly a full moon tonight was brought up.
