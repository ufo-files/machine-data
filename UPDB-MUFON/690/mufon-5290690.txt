Observed lights for hours before filming.

I observed several bright lights on a live beach cam for hours before taking a screen recordings unfortunately the first video was too long for my phones storage and did not save. One light remained almost stationary only fading in and out another single light appeared in the center of the screen slowly fading in and moving from left to right before disappearing when the fishing boat got to the center of the screen, which was filmed but did not save. There is a fishing boat with a light which I thought might be reflecting on the clouds or fog. The boat began on the right of the screen and ended on the left and in one video the light appears to drop for a closer look at the boat. One video shows the main light on left and then a small light fades in at top of screen, which I didn’t get starting the video to late, you can then see a third light at the top upper right of the screen clearly come into to view and fly out of view. Both videos are being taken at 4am, it’s raining , windy and bug flys in to view towards the end, I think.
