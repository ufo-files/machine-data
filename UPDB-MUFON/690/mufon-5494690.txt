{cak} Standing outside home, looked up and saw objects

I was outside my home talking with a friend. I tend to always look up at the sky and this time when I did, I saw 10 fireball type objects moving slowly over my house. The 10 fireballs were flying together in the shape of a V, which they held perfectly. We took pics with a cell phone. We stood and watched them move slowly over the area until they were no longer in sight. From what I had found out, there were many reports of these objects from my town and surrounding ones. The next day, there were helicopters in the area, flying very low as if looking for something. They were military and were around most of the day. 

We did take a couple of pics. One of the pictures I did clean up and found, amazingly, what was under the exterior "fireball" looked very much like a music symbol. It appears to have windows but you can see for yourself.
