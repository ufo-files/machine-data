IR filming of pulsing light slowly moving left near several airlines after takeoff

I was testing a full spectrum camera conversion in daylight on 13 April 2017 at about 07:22 hrs.
I first noticed the light when I was tracking airlines that had launched from East Midlands airport. I spotted the light above an airline and reset the camera to film the object which I was able to do for about 8 minutes until the battery eventually died.
At first I thought I may have seen Venus but it was completely the wrong direction, I ruled out a star as it was moving to the left and to fast for any stars. It appeared to be too slow for a satellite which I am used to seeing and filming with my night vision equipment.
I have seen daylight Iridium flares which last a few seconds.
It didn't appear to be a helicopter, party or weather balloon which I have filmed passing overhead several times before.
The light was moving from right to left and appeared to be in the flight path area of the aircraft that takeoff from east midlands airport. From my location the aircraft seen would be about 10-15 miles away.
The light appeared to be moving about twice the speed of the small clouds seen about and in the same direction. The wind speed was 7mph and gusting at 17mph.
I think the wind was blowing from the NW.
The light appeared to pulse or flash randomly, there were moments when it appeared very bright and other time I could hardly see it.
I had uploaded a longer video to my Youtube channel dejavuNightVision soon after the sighting. The link for that is included as other media.
