Very bright light that hovered over my house, then slowly moved across the sky

This object appeared out of the day sky. It was so bright. It couldnt have been a star because the sun was still out and I couldnt even see Venus. The object would dim every 30 seconds (estimated). It would go in and out of brightness. A commercial plan flew right by it but the object would loss its brightness as the plan flew by. When the plan was out of few the object would re-illuminate. There is no way anyone could have missed this. The brightness was just to much to be unnoticed. It moved slower than any craft that I have seen in my life. My whole family was there to see this thing.
