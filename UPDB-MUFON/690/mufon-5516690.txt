dr}Observed object flying across sky which took over 10 minutes

Im usually up and down all night long so I bring out my binoculars and look up at the stars. I noticed what to be a satelite going from between Arcturus the star and Vega. This was going much much slower than any satelite Ive ever seen. It took over 10 minutes to go 40 degrees across the sky until it slowly vanished. There is a constellation called the coat hanger that it went by and I counted the seconds at 35 just to go by this little star cluster.
I find that the theres a bewitching hour as one would say. Most mornings between the am hours of 3 - 4 am, lights go across the sky. Sometimes they go in groups of 2 or more and sometimes they change directions. Sometimes I see 1, then another 1 will follow it 2 - 3 minutes later.
Once I saw what seemed like a very bright meteor. It was way brighter than Vega, looking as bright as Venus. It went from the sw to the ne.
I know this isnt a satelite because I looked up where and when 1 would be near me and there should not have been 1 there at that time in the location.
