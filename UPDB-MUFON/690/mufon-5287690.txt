Huge Ball of Light Comes Down to See Me

This craft was curious of my activity. I saw the craft break the atmosphere. 30 mins later or less I see this huge light coming over the treeline beside me. I said there it is!! I was astonished and so stuck on what I was seeing...I couldn't think to grab a camera and record. Silent, white bright ball coming in a straight line at about 5 mph right over my head above the treeline, just above.. as if it was looking at me going ohh that was you. Let's just say I was reaching out. I stood grinning ear to ear waving at it And off it continued until out of sight ...a minute later a cop pulls in...to chase me out. I've seen this ball of light 2 other occasions in the area I live in. I know they are watching. Not all of them are bad you know, but some are. Just like us. The flash at me in the night sky as to say hello. We are not alone.
