{dr}starburst hovering over coachella valley

On February 21, 2017 I went to Coachella to visit family, I arrived around 7 pm. I was outside and seen how clear the night was and all the stars were so beautiful. I noticed a very bright star that was glowing, pink, purple, like it was on fire, then it faded off, then it came back brighter than ever. i grabbed my phone and video taped but i wasnt sure if i got the object because i have no flash on the phone, then my phone battery died. I only saw 1 glowing object but the video shows orbs and more than 1 object. i went to backyard to get a better look and it was gone. I was in Coachella for 6 days and never seen that object again. 5 days later i finally got my phone to power up and seen what the video captured. I have never video taped anything like this ever. weird stuff in our skies.
