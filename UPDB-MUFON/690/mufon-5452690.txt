First 3-light triangle, then 10 lights long shape, then 2 parallel lights all moving along the same path.

My dog woke me up to go for a walk before dawn. I checked my cell phone before going outside, it said 5:36 a.m. A couple of houses down from mine, while my dog was sniffing grass, I looked up and saw what looked like a triangle of stars moving in a northward direction. I thought maybe they were three airplanes on the same flight path, but the lights looked too perfectly triangle, and there was no noise. My dog and I walked a little more and I continued looking in the same part of the sky I had seen the triangle. At first I thought I was looking at a constellation, but the constellation moved and was traveling in the same path and at the same speed as the triangle. I counted 10 lights this time. We walked a little more, and following the same path as the other lights, were two parallel lights moving at the same speed and in the same direction. We walked back in the house and my oven clock said 5:43 a.m.
