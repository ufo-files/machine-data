{rjl} CIGAR SHAPE MOVING AT A HIGH ALTITUDE

WALKED OUT SIDE TO MY WORK TRUCK LOOKED UP AT SOME BIRDS ON A ELECTRIC POLE WHEN IT CAUGHT MY EYE IN THE SKY FAR OF IN THE DISTANCE . IT WAS VERY HIGH ALTITUDE ABOUT THE SIZE OF MY THUMB NAIL WIDE CIGAR SHAPE MOVING ACROSS THE SKY ON A CLEAR MORNING. WHAT MADE ME TAKE ANOUGH LOOK WAS THERE WAS NOT ANY VAPOR TRAIL ON IT LIKE THE JET THAT HAD BEEN BY EARLER AND LATER THAT DAY. IT WAS TO HIGH IN THE SKY TO BE A PROP AIRPLANE IT WAS ALSO A COLD DAY MOST JETS LEAVE A VAPOR TRAIL ON COLD DAYS I WATCHED IT ALONG WITH A CO WORKED TRYING TO FIGURE OUT WHAT IT MIGHT BE.
