{rjl} Cloud Holes And Blue Orb

While waiting for a traffic light to change I noticed a strange looking hole which appeared in the clouds. I took a picture of the hole with my camera phone. I moved forward one car length nearer the traffic light as cars went through the intersection. I snapped another picture of the hole in the cloud as it grew bigger and changed shape slightly. I noticed a second hole to the right of the first hole. That is when I noticed a blue colored orb as it passed the edge of the hole. I moved forward one more car length and snapped a couple more pictures of the holes and the orb. It moved downward from the holes and to the right of the second one. The last picture I took showed the orb in the tree line.
