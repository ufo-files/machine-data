Was walking home from my mother's and was looking at the location mars. Detected movement and observed large craft with a dull black exterior move north easterly and descend as possibly lining up to land. Craft was triangular with rounded tail and was emitting whay looked like thrust exhaust from three rectangular ports in the rounded tail. Also observed a semi dome shape in what appeared to be a cabin area and three windows down the side. Vehicle made a slight left hand maneuver and observed what appeared to be some type of maneuvering thrusters on the right trailing edge.

Vehicle resembled a stealth aircraft, but on a much larger scale with distinct design differences. Vehicle was moving way too slow to be a traditional aircraft, and was only emitting a whooshing sound.
