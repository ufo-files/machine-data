Horizontal string of lights hovering then faded away

Driving north on Hwy 22 approximately 1 mile north of Military Rd @ 8:05pm I saw 3 very bright orange lights in a perfectly horizontal line to the Northwest. Lights were stationary. The far right light slowly faded away, followed by the center light, then the left one. The next day I spoke with at least 9 other people who witnessed similar lights ranging in numbers from 3 to 6 lights.
