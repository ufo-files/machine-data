Blinking object with white and red light changes into large white orb and meets up with smaller orb while hovering in the sky.

I witnessed what at first I thought was a plane but suddenly it stopped hovered and turned into a bright ball of light. I ran and got my camera. When I came back it was back to blinking white and red lights just like a plane but more more lights blinking. I filmed it for awhile going back and forth until it transformed again into a bright white ball of light. This time I was able to catch it meeting up with a smaller orb that can be seen in the video. After a few moments it changed back to the blinking white and red lights and flew back and forth in the sky from East to South changing many times to a large white orb. 

Very weird and scary not sure what to think or say or do. I have a third video that I captured later in the evening when it was very dark of the same UFO that I will not be releasing.
