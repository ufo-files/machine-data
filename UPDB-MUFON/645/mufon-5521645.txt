I officially saw my first UFO!.... I was walking my dogs in the back yard, and I thought someone shot off a roman candle from across the water from the other neighborhood. It was a brilliant orange/amber orb with color flickering. It made no sound at all and was very beautiful. it was about 300-400 feet above me and I followed it till it disappeared to the East. A few seconds later another orb followed the same exact path rising from out of the tree line area across the water like the first one did. I couldnt get over how close it was and how quiet it was. I kept thinking it might be a helicopter with a spotlight, but the object was flickering/glowing and totally silent as it passed overhead and then faded into the East.

I was very excited and thrilled when I was watching this event, I was not scared but amazed how brilliant and bright in color the object was. I kept squinting my eyes trying to focus on the object to make out what it really was, but the object was to bright.
