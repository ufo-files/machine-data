{jpg} On the night of September 27, 2015 I decided to take photographs of the Full moon prior to the next days Blood Moon eclipse.

I observed a gray object in the photographs as I started to look at each shot. The object seamed to be rough or bumpy in surface and apeared to almost have a mushroom shape as it rotated showing all sides over the course of an hour. The shape apeared to move to different positions in a circle pattern. I took roughly ten photographs of this object. In several of the photographs I noticed holes or designs in the object and in one of the shots I saw what looks like a badge shape or definate shape on what apeared to be the back side of the object. The object seamed to have a face like shape at times then would change it's rotation and then look like a craft or rock type object with a bumpy surface yet it had very pronounced surface eliments as if it was shape shifting. I waited until a day or two to download the photographs. I did shoot the Blood Moon the fallowing day and did not capture and object in those pictures. I have included Five of the images. In these photographs you can see a small circle near the moon images, this is the object at hand. If enlarged, you can see the changes in shape as discussed in these Five images. The object did seam to emit an ora around it even though the moon was in clear view and the moon did not emit a ring or ora itself. I conclude that what I saw was an actual solid object in the area.
