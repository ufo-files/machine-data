{cak} Pulsating orange light made no sound was not an aircraft moved about the speed of an aircraft or a bit faster

I was travelling north on Murray Ridge Road in Lorain County at 12:48 am when I noticed a very bright orange pulsating light low to the horizon. The road was empty so I stopped on the side of the road about maybe 1/4 mile from Lowell Street. I had a digital SLR camera with me with a 35-70mm lens and I started filming the event. You can hear my vehicle idling in the background and a talk show on my radio. I tried zoom out to get perspective of the surroundings with the object however it was very dark, I did manage to get a tree with the object in view. I knew the object was not an plane I worked at an airport in the past and I know the colors of the lights and patterns. This was definitely something other than a jet or a plane. I felt it was close enough that I should of heard an engine but far enough away that I would not be able to get much detail with my little lens. I thought I should have brought my 300mm with me that night then it would have been real exciting. I have always wanted to see a UFO and I was disappointed that I was not completely prepared. I had just left a family party when the event happened and there were some people still there when I left. When I mentioned I saw something the next day my older sister said she saw the same thing at about the same time I saw it. She was about 5 miles away to the south from my location at the time which is the same direction the object was headed. I filmed until the light was just too far away to continue. No cars came from in front of me or behind me while I was filming. The metadata of the event on my camera had the date and time. My sister and I were the only ones I know of that saw the object and we were not together at the time.
