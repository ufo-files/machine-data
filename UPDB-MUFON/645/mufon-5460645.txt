Extremely long. Cigar / fat Pen like object within a pointed vapour like cloud and bright lights..

Whilst viewing Jupiter and its moons in a south/easterly direction at 1.30 am on 25th May 2019 thro my refractor telescope in North East Fife  I looked away to observe any other objects of possible interest when a strangely shaped cloud appeared high up approaching from the West .This particularly stuck out as there was hardly any breeze/wind and any cloud cover at that juncture was fairly static sparse .Having now turned the scope towards the oncoming cloud I noted a strange pointed shape within it and to my surprise.lightsand what I could only describe as a bow wave of cloud/vapour at the front of the object .Initially I thought my eyes were playing a trick on me but upon a second take, ,no this object was real, at which point I called upon a friend to come and take a look and they confirmed my sighting.We continued to watch this travelling in an easterly direction, by now it was directly in front of us to the south and the length of it seemed to have increased and rows of equally spaced round type lights appeared to increase from a dozen or so to possibly 30 but difficult to take an accurate account as it just seemed to be growing in length  [.like a bullet train carraige at night ]  We viewed this for aprox 10 to 15 ..minutes watching it thro the scope and binoculars and without visual aids until it went out of sight towards the east.Certainly do not think meteorites /falling stars! as direction of travel unwavering and precise.Regrettably both my camera and video were not charged and thus unable to submit proof of sighting [ Hope that will not happen again]however ,wonder if anyone in the UK or anywhere caught site of this strange object
