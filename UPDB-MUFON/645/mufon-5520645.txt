A light that was red and had smaller blue lights to its left side.

I was next to my window on my Laptop. I have just watched an documentary about aliens in Earth past. My window faces a major airport which I am use to seeing lights in the sky. Though this one caught my eye due to its size and color. It was red and the size was about star-like. I assumed it was a newly born star but as I looked closer I noticed it had blue lights to the left side of it. My younger brothers and cousins also saw the strange light. The object also seemed to wobble a little as it hovered. After about five minutes we stopped worrying about it. Though about 3 minutes later I look back and it was gone. I instantly knew it was a star nor a plane. What I saw matched the description of a UFO!
