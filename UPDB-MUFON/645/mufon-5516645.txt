(GS) I just looked up, thought I saw something, snapped a picture

I was sitting in my back yard just relaxing. I was watching the clouds and I thought I saw something zip into view. It just seemed to appear and looked like a shiny, metallic ball floating under the clouds. I took out my phone and snapped several pictures. It only appeared in one picture and was gone. It only appeared for a few seconds and seemed to appear and then disappear very quickly.

When I first saw it I was surprised because we see airplanes from the airport all the time. I know what they all look like and what their general flight path and speeds are. I knew right off that this wasnt an airplane. Although it was in the general flight path it was higher than I have ever seen a plane taking off and way faster. In fact, I snapped the pictures because I wasnt sure I saw anything at all. The picture is actually clearer than what I saw because it came and went very quickly. I actually felt more surprised when I looked at the pictures I took and saw that in one frame there was, in fact, something there. 

I was busy taking pictures and didn't see the object leave or which way it went. I did notice that the image appears to have a energy field around it in the photo. I didn't see that. All I saw was a metallic object with no wings, completely silent, and very fast.     

I will include a couple more pictures I took when I took this one. In an attempt to find the thing I took pictures at different angles but, it only appears in the one picture.
