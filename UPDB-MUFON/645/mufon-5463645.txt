My son, daughter in law and I went out to the balcony on 12/12/2018 around 8:30pm they live in Reno Nevada. I saw lights flashing all of a sudden and I grabbed my phone and started recording and I could clearly see it was a UFO. The airport is close by and a military base but when the craft was flying and hovering it seemed all other aircraft that usually fly by all came to a silent stand still. Nothing else flew by or around or landed nor took off while the aircraft was in the air. Everything seemed to stand still, which was very odd because there is always activity around there. After about 4 minutes or so the UFO just shot up and to the south east and was gone. Then planes began to land again at the airport.
