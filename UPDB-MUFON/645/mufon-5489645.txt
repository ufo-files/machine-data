i was about 6-7 years old.was in a truck with dad mom and 3 sisters.we were going to virginia from tennessee traveling at night.as we drove there was all kinds of shooting stars.we stopped in a small town to rest.there was a man in black suit at drivers door talking to dad..next thing i remember is being in a room on the craft looking out a huge window looking down at the top of the mountains as we flew over them.i was in this room alone, i knew my mom dad and sisters were back in another room on the craft.next thing i remembered we were all back in the truck,with 2 men in black suits one at each open door.one of those men told us..u can not remember what u just saw.in my mind i thought u can not make me not remember..after this my dad became very afraid of thunder storms.he was so scared he would sit at night with a loaded gun and watch out the window of our house.
