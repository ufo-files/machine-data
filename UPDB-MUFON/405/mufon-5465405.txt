{jpg} Red light ufo in the sky

I had decided to walk home after work, it was 12 midnight and I was almost home when I noticed a red light in the sky near buildings, my first thought was maybe a light from a radio tower, but then it slowly starts to move, my next thought was a helicopter, but it suddenly moved very fast and stopped suddenly. It went further behind the buildings out of my sight so I continued walking wondering what I just saw when a few minutes later I saw it again. It was flying fast and stopping now and then. It was starting to come towards my direction, it gave me chills. It went started to head away from me and start to slowly disappear into the sky. That was the last time I saw it, when I arrived home I couldn't stop thinking about it. The whole experience lasted about a minute or two.
