{cak} Green light traveling NW makes very rapid, abrupt turn and goes sw

My husband and I were driving NE at night, on a rural road. There were high ceiling clouds and a near full moon, but the clouds made it fairly dark and few stars were observable. As we approached, I observed a green flashing light perpendicular to our path. Thinking it was an airplane, it got my attention as it seemed to be very close to the ground but was horizontal in its path. Suddenly it changed directions very rapidly and went away from us in a SW direction, parallel to our path. When it changed directions it also changed colors, going from green to aqua to blue. I watched it until it disappeared. I then wondered it it were some sort of remote controlled device. I was perplexed as to what I saw and only then asked my husband if he just saw what I saw. He recanted the same story but because he was driving he did not see it change colors. He said nothing from this planet could structurally withstand the forces of the abrupt directional change and no remote controlled device could travel and turn at such. speed. With that all we knew to do was to report it as a UFO sightings.
