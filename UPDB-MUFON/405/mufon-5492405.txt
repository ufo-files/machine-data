{rjl} Small blue light growing into big blue light,with a weird foglike haze behind it.

I was living out in L.A. at the time and it was nice and warm out, kind of overcast overhead. I was outside walking around just enjoying the night. Sunset had occurred an hour or so earlier and happened to look up at the sky. That's when I noticed a weird small blue light off in the distance, at about a 45 degree elevation from the horizon. As I stood there and looked at it, I noticed that it just didn't look right and the longer I watched it, the weirder and bigger it got. I couldn't believe my eyes and started getting a little scared. So I figured I had better go and get some of the other guys to come outside to look at this thing, because I wanted verification that I was actually seeing this and that I wasn't crazy! So I did just that. After a few people came outside and looked up and saw what I was pointing at, several of them ran inside to tell other people to come out.The longer we stood there, the bigger it got until it was about the size of a quarter, if you held it at between two fingers at arms length. Then it just disappeared. This whole thing took about 45 minutes or so. It scared everybody there., some ran back inside and the rest of us stood there in misbelief and could not figure out what it was, that just happened. I have never been that scared in my life. I actually thought it was the end.
