{tjd} Matte finish gold, upright triangle, spotted about a block and a half from the Des Moines airport.

I was in the teller window of the drive-up at Financial Plus Credit Union one afternoon when I spotted a shiny object out of the corner of my left eye. It was an upright triangle with rounded edges on all three corners and it was staying on an even plane as it passed behind a big, gray building just to the south of the credit union. I started counting as it went behind the other side of the building and it took 12 seconds for it to appear again. I made a point to not blink while all this was going on so I wouldn't miss anything. As it gets over Fleur Dr., out of the corner of my right eye I see the Air Guard has sent a fighter jet up to see what this thing is. It was just stopped and waiting and as soon as the jet got a little too close the UFO disappeared into thin air. It didn't shoot off at a super high rate of speed, it just disappeared. Later I learned about the term 'cloaking' which is evidently what it did. It looked only big enough to carry three, maybe four little aliens. I got the feeling it was a probe just checking out the airport. I'm sure they had it on their radar and alerted the Guard so they could send the jet up to see what it was. I also can't believe I didn't write down the date and time and other pertinent information, since it's something you don't see everyday. All I know is I worked at the Fleur branch from 2000 through 2002 and it wasn't winter when I saw it. I'm thinking it was spring or fall. I could find out the exact years, down to the month if I need to. I'll bet the air traffic controllers have the exact info and the Guard also. Sorry I can't be more help.
