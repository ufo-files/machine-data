{rjl} As craft was hovering it appeared to have 6 translucent windows. After it hovered it reversed its directions. Clear view of rectangular shape as it moved away.

I was standing within a few feet of a barn with a horse. I noticed the object because of the illumination from the windows. I knew that it was, without a doubt extra-terrestial. I estimated that it was approximately 30 feet long and and 20 feet wide. It had three windows down each of the 30 foot sides. It came from the East then hovered for about 2 minutes. I did nothing but stood and watched. It left in an Easterly direction. While in motion its process was unhurried. The view of the craft, as it disappeared was clearly rectangular.
