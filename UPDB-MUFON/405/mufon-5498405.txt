{rjl} triangular with very bright central light hovering over tree tops with and abrupt and unnatural movement to the east

My husband and I were on our way home one night and we noticed an extremely bright and low light above the tree tops adjacent to the street we were presently on. Being so close we were nosy thinking it was police and decided to rubberneck. The closer we got we realized it was hovering stationary and without any detectable sound being emitted. There was a very large and very bright central light much brighter than a typical search light. We detected a triangular shape while approaching the craft by a street perpendicular to the one it appeared to be above. As we got closer it made an abrupt sideways movement to the east, but it did not make a turn it seemed to just move to the side at a significant amount of speed. We were already extremely intrigued and attempted to follow it. It was very hard to keep up with but we were able to maintain sight of the light being emitted. It had moved roughly 2 or so miles to the east and appeared to be hovering stationary again but this time over a field near some railroad tracks. It again starting moving abruptly and with great speed toward the direction of the regional airport. Because of its speed and the way the roads are laid out we were unable to keep up with it.
