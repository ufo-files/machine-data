{rjl} A flame like glowing orb flys over Staten Island, New York

Almost to 1am I was walking home from my car, which was parked down the street on my block. I immediately noticed a fire-y glowing orb that kept getting bigger and seems to be descending diagonally down. My initial thought was that it was an aircraft that had caught fire and was going down. I run towards my house which is the direction I saw the craftmoving towards and got a lot closer to it. It must've been only 100 ft away from me. It was too bright to make out any shape but it was big. I stopped right outside my house, took out my phone to video it then I lost sight of it. I ran inside, woke up my mother and we both looked out the back window and saw it flying away for a few minutes getting further and further away. So freaky!!
