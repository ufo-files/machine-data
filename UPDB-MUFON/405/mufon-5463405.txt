{cak} Circular disc with red lights flew overhead slowly

I was in the front yard at night, waiting for someone else to get home because the lights were off and I was afraid of skunks. The sky was overcast, with an orange glow due to light pollution. I noticed something in the sky approaching, which caught my attention. At first I thought it was a helicopter, but it was absolutely silent. At the very low altitude, I certainly would have heard a helicopter. It was circular, approx. 8ft. diameter with approx. 6 or 8 red lights around the edge. It flew almost directly over me at approx. 20mph, continuing on a straight path northeast and maintaining altitude of approx. 50 to 100 ft. I watched it until it was out of view, behind my house (the roof line was the horizon). I think I saw a circle on the bottom, like a hatch. I was not afraid, though I knew it was unusual. At the time, I thought it could not be a UFO, because I believed that UFOs are fake.
