Orange light in the sky

On New Years eve of 2020-2021 at 12.02 am I went outside with my 4 year old son to see some fireworks that where going off .. we got into our driveway and and he was looking the opposite direction of the fireworks. When I looked up we noticed a huge reddish orange light stationary in the sky . I yelled repeatedly for my wife that was around the back of the house . We saw the light through the cloud cover for about 2 minutes . Then it seemed to go straight up in an instant . I have no reasonable explanation for what we saw that night . I can say that it didn't belong , I've never seen anything like this . When I told my wife what we saw and asked her why she didn't come around to the front of the house when I was yelling for her she said she never heard me yell for her . I haven't really spoke to much about it in the past year . Now that's it's coming up on the 1 year anniversary of seeing it I just wanted to tell someone about it .. and maybe give me some answers on what we witnessed that night .
