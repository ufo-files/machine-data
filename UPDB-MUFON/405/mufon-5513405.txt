{dr}Looked to the sky and saw what looked like a bright light moving from east to south west

I walked out my front door at 8:25pm an looked to the east where I saw what looked to me like the flame of a rocket moving from east to the south west very slowly. The flame was not like a liftoff, but instead more like a hovering and slow even force for lateral movement. The object moved across the sky at a very slow pace for about 45 seconds before I noticed that it was climbing in altitude. After about 2 to 3 minutes the light was gone as it went straight up out of sight.
