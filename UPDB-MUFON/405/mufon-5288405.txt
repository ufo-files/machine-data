Robert & myself were out on the deck and saw a red light come up from horizon to the west then go down,

We saw a red glowing light come up above the horizon to our west, sense I live 4 miles from town it is very dark and easy to see any plane or lights in the sky. We saw this light come up then go back down the horizon then within a minute this light shows up to our southwest many miles from our first sitting spot it come up just above the horizon and started to move east. At that time I ran to get my camera and started to record video of the object we were seeing. We watched as this light turned into 3 lights two red lights toward the rear and another white light making a triangle then shifting back to a round glowing red light. I filmed the light as it moved from west to east all the time wondering what it was we were seeing. As I filmed I could see the tree tops go by when this light would be behind them. I have many more strange light videos I have captured from my deck and would like to share and get information on.
