Red glowing object seemed to skip across the sky and accelerated and decelerated erratically. no sound and no FAA lights

At around 11pm in Dabouq, a suburb of Amman in Jordan, I was browsing the web in my bedroom that has a large glass wiindow facing the East. I first saw a plane, which is usual, but it still looked suspicious, I continued to browse the web, then I saw a red glowing object that looked like it took off vertically. It then started to glide and "skip" SW bound while glowing red. I went outside and saw that my neighbors have done the same. I watched the object go past the houses still glowing into the distance. No FAA lights to be seen and no sound to be heard. It looked like it was skipping across water, and accelerating quickly then decelerating. Strangest thing I have ever seen. Never thought that I would see it in Jordan. Neighbors and I stood outside for another 3 minutes after it was gone and we all went inside. I was shaking after the incident and I am not easily shaken.
