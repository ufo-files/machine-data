My neighbor and his wife were returning to our townhouse development on 08/29/2015 around 10 pm when that saw about a dozen or so lights in the northern sky glowing yellow and hovering some distance away. He stated that some of the objects remained stationary while one moved around in and out of the formation. He and his spouse explained that they had no idea what these lights were or what their origin might be. He stated he heard no noises from the objects but he knew that they did not seem like man-made devices. He stated that the objects finally moved off after about 10-15 minutes. My neighbor is a former police officer, federal investigator, and EMT so he is not prone to fantasy! He stated that he took two(2)cell phone photos of these objects to document the sighting! After some discussion with me of the sighting I told him about MUFON! He was interested but not enough to report this incident to MUFON! He indicated, however, that if MUFON wanted to talk with him about this sighting that he would cooperate. Again I did not observe this sighting! I canvassed all of the local news media to see if any reports of this event were document! Absolutely no mention of this event was found in any television news or newspaper reports. Very strange!
