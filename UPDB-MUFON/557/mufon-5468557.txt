{cak} Glowing ball, transucent yellowy

My friends called me to play midnight basketball in the neighborhood. 1/3 mile walk. I observed to my left a ball of glowing light floating about powerline level 15 feet off the ground. You could see into it, but not through it. It floated with me as I walked. It seemed to be aware of me. As soon as I was hoping that my friends could see it, it ascended over the tree lines northwest and out of sight. My friends didnt see it.
