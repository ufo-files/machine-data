(GS) High altitude Air plane or white Cigar shaped UFO photographed

Driving US 219 from Washington DC. to Ontario, just north of Bradford PA, I notice a clear white high altitude air plane looking north. From afar the object didn't seem to have any wings nor tail. That caught my attention so I park the car, grab my Canon Rebel XT camera and exit the vehicle. I was able to get a few pictures of the distant eastbound object, thinking that I could zoom in later and get a better look.

As I made it south of Buffalo NY, I see a similar white object in the distance and snap couple of pictures. However, wings seemed to be visible and I included them for comparison. Taken with the same camera, same settings but merely aprox. 2 later that same day.

Now having examined all the photographs, it is clear that the object south of Buffalo is simply a passenger jet and the attached picture will demonstrate that clearly. The photos taken near north of Bradford, PA show a different object. No visible wings and the camera does not zoom as easily on it as it did with the passenger jet. 

Strangely, one of the photographs also captured a black-ish jet along with the white object in distance. Now, I did not hear nor see the black jet when I took the pictures of the white object. It sure looks like a fighter jet (hardly a black private jet?) but the screaming noise alone should have alerted me but did not. 

Now this wont prove anything in the absence of a zoom lens but perhaps somebody will find this interesting to examine.
