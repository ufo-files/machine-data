{cak} objects came over horizon, lasted 20 minutes and dissapeared.

It was the Summer of 1963. The back porch to our house overlooked a shallow valley, the far end of which was probably 5 miles away. I was seated, looking through binoculars I noticed very bright objects coming over the horizon at the end of the valley. They did not look like airplanes. I yelled out that we were being invaded. About 10 or so people came running to the porch.

Altogether there were about a dozen objects moving together in the same direction but not in a line. Some of the objects were lower than others and a little to the side. The last one was shaped like a pyramid and after a few minutes started to rotate counter clockwise. 

We reported the sight to the Litchfield Police. They said they had received numerous reports from others in the area. My mother was interviewed on a radio station in Boston after the incident. After that a gentleman in an Air Force uniform came and interviewed our family. He said he was on official government business. (I'm thinking operation blue book.) He was tall with a butch cut. I remember him trying to get us to say it was a one of the Air Force's planes. But, the objects didn't have wings and planes don't rotate counter clockwise. I checked the Operation Blue Book files and was unable to locate our sighting.
