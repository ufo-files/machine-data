{cak} hovered over lake,drop down,flew SE.40 seconds Later came back H.NW. tree top high 300yrds away.

I have been watching skies for months now every clear night just for stars I saw delta shaped white lights very high alt. blinding fast going north about 3-14-16. began looking every night regardless of weather.then on 4-23-16 10:30 ish pm I saw red,blue,white lights over lake Huron,quarter mile away,i used lighthouse as reference.hovering maybe 300 ft. over water,then drop down and move SE. towards Sardinia Canada and disappear.at first the lights were vertical,then became horizontal when moving away.i thought it was gone,45 seconds later I turn around facing north there it was again,this time 200 to 300 yrs away moving west very slow tipping side to side and all black with only white lights on both sides.I got somewhat of a better view between the trees.cant describe these white lights ,just that they didn't shine,and this time it seemed to be winged.it may not even have been the same one,but I think it was.so I was thinking some kind of drone,but I still had clear view of it when it blinked out,when It came past house it made no noise,when I thought I saw it skip or shudder or tip I knew it was different. I had a feeling of amazement to it,I know someone else had to see it so close to blue water bridge to CAN.Homeland security,coastguard station all in close proximity.
