{rjl} Over 20 starlike shapes flying in straight line

Observed over 20 starlike lights flying about 10-15 seconds apart heading south east from north west around 8:54 - 9:02 pm. First thing I did was check flight radar to check for planes, none close to area, then checked the star link satellite tracker, not even close to this side of the earth. Put on Facebook and others confirmed they saw also. They seemed to be all moving at same speed but one did slow down and wait for another one to come closer, then returned to original position. They all seemed to disappear at same spot like they were entering into something where they left. Very weird and never seen anything like it.
