{tjd} 6 brights lights over Disneyland

On January 6th 2020 at 9:30pm at Disneyland, my family and I were waiting in a group of about 20 people outside of the lightsaber build shop. While waiting for our group to go in for the saber build, my daughter pointed and said “look at that.” I thought she was talking about the decor of the place until the other people in the group who we don’t know, said “whoa what is that?” Looking up, there were six bright lights flying overhead in random directions but all in a north west direction. We watched them move around for about a minute then the six lights formed up into two lines parallel to each other then staying in formation, drifted off slowly continuing to the north west until they were out of sight. I’d say they were probably between 300-500 feet in altitude. I’ve been searching the web to see if anybody else reported it but since I haven’t found anything, I figured I would. I don’t know how many people saw them but I know at least 10 of is did. After watching it, I couldn’t believe I didn’t think to grab my phone and record it.
