Saw disc-shaped UFO while driving.

I was driving that night to watch a softball game. I was driving along a straight road and i noticed that there were three to four white lights in the sky right above the horizon.At first i thought it was a helicopter but then i noticed the lights looked as they were in a disc shape. The lights moved to the center of the road and i was getting closer(about 100 yards), as i got closer the object went into a field to the right of the roadvery quickly and swiftly. As i was passing by the field i looked for the lights and caught a glimpse of them and also a flashing red light at the top. I never stopped to get a closer look because there was no way to get off the road.
