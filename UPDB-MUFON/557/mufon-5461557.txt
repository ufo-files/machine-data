{jpg} high altitude oddly flashing light

Object resembled let's say, one of the stars that make up the bucket of the big dipper. It was moving from west to east or there abouts. I initially thought it was a commercial airliner but there was only one color, white. Upon closer inspection with binoculars it appeared to be one solid light. It flashed oddly, meaning there would be a dim flash, then about 4 seconds later a substantially brighter flash, followed by the dim flash maybe 5 seconds later. It reminded me of a lighthouse, only straight above me and moving in a straight line. It seemed to be moving slower than an airliner would move. After watching it for possibly 3 minutes it simply vanished.
