(GS) Saw 4 to five huge triangular objects fly from north to south directly over me while sitting in hot tub took video and pictures stored on hard drive

I was in a hot tub on my deck in the back yard with my wife when the first one flew over we didn't know what it was but noticed it blocked out most of the stars in the sky it was a clear night. We watched it move off into the distance moving south. Shortly after there was another and my wife was scared and went into the house I followed but only to grab my sony handycam with night shot. I sat in the tub waiting and it was about five minutes there was another and then five minutes and another. I shot video of it not able to see anything except the lights. Later I took the video and loaded it to my computer and saw little then I used software to put it into ultr violet light that is when I saw how huge this thing was and the outline and details of the craft. I printed a few hard copy pictures and then saved everything to the hard drive.
