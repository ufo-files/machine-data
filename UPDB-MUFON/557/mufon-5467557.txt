{dr}Looking at Venus and observed stationary red light to the NW above the tree tops. After a few seconds the light flashed red and green as it moved rapidly north and out of sight.

My friend and I stepped outside to see fire works in the neighborhood as they sounded close by. As we were looking at Venus I asked her what it was. She said a star. I said no it is Lucifer. As I started to explain what Lucifer was I looked slightly north and saw a bright red stationary light. I asked her what that red light was. She said a tower. I said no, there is no tower there. Just then it flashed red and green as it move off north at a rapid speed. I estimated it to be Mach II. As it left our view I said we just saw a UFO. I am a fighter pilot with about 3500 hours mostly in single seat fighters. This light did not make any noise. No sound associated with the light. It moved much too rapidly from a stationary position to be a normal aircraft. I am convinced I saw a UFO.
