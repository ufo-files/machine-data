I witnessed a UFO hovering left - right over Radcliffe town.

Between the hours 2 - 3 am, one morning in 1976, I witnessed a bright blue glittering UFO, hovering about 400ft,moving slowly from east to west, over Radcliffe town , Manchester, U.K. The morning / week after , I saw this object again , hovering in the same place ( 2 - 3 a.m. )This time I noticed the UFO made no noise & completely silent. It was definitely not an aircraft , weather balloon , satellite , helicopter , meteorite or airship , ( to my knowledge. )It was under some sort of intelligent control. I only remembered these two sightins just recently. Before then , I completerly forgot that this happened all those years ago.
