the object appeared far away and it was stationary, in the sky and high above the closest buildings, it reflected the sunlight

i was on a bus and it stopped at a traffic light, and i looked out the opposite window when a dull grey object in the middle of the sky caught my attention, i was very confused and i waited for it to do something so that i could identify it (like a bird or a plane) but it simply remained in the same place. It had the shape of a regular trapezium, it had no wings or lights or a trail of any sort, it was a plain grey shape. the bus moved a little forward and with this slight movement i saw another angle of the object, which reflected the sunlight on its bottom left corner and seemed to fade at the sides, which then gave me the impression that it had a disc-like shape. Also because the bus moved very little, i thought that it must have been very far away because its angle changed a lot. the weather was clear that day too so i didn't know what it could have been. when i tried to reach for my phone to take a photo, the bus had started moving again.
