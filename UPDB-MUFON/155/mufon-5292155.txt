Cereal box object on its side traveling down the river at about 200 ft

I saw the strangest object between Christmas and Titusville over the St John's River. It looked like a cereal box on its side slowly going down the river at about 200 feet high. I have tried to make it into a blimp in my mind but just can't seem to do it. Can someone tell me if a blimp was traveling south along the Brevard and Orange County line in the early afternoon. The day would have been July 29 - 31 and I quite sure it was the 30th. It was tin gray and the clouds were reflecting off of it. If there were no blimps in the area then I don't know what I saw.
