Black wobbling saucer flying high in the sky

I was sitting in my cousin's room upstairs. He Has a window on his ceiling so you can see the sky. I was just happening to be looking out the window when I saw a black saucer like craft high up in the sky. It was still very bright out so I could see it clearly. It was flying slowly overhead and was wobbling as it flew. I have never seen anything like this in my entire life. I wish I had a camera to take a picture or even a telescope to see it up close. I just watched it in awe as it flew by, wobbling the entire time.
