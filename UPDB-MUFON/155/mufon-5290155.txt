At about 6: 15 PM my girlfriend called me outside to witness what she was seeking. I saw a series of reddish orange circular objects rising from the Ocean in groups of 4 or five . The ascended to a height and disappeared . Approximately 20 objects rose over a five minute time frame. The objects seemed to rise about a third of the distance between us and Anacapa Island but close to shore. Two bike riders were near and we called them to watch which they did. We all were stunned and had no idea what the objects were. Since we are well South of Vandenburg Airforce base I doubt that what we saw was connected to any of their activity.
