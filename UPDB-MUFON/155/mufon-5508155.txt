{cak} Object Acends Around sun down Visible from North Of Venice Beach, South of the Santa Monica Peer

On August 31st 2013 just after the sunset Behind the Santa Monica Mountains a white contrail could be seen Ascending under high velocity at an extremely steep angle. Over 10 minutes or so Friends and i watched as the object continued to ascend under power and be came more and more over our points of view until the object leaving the contrail became a visible spec about a pencil leads width from the distance in which we were viewing the object. Then as the object reached the Terminator of Night and day in the sky, the object was no longer visible to the naked eye in the night sky, though I could still see the contrail visibly for a few more seconds. The object the disappeared. The Los Angeles Area has huge amounts of Air Traffic and so seeing air craft is as common as seeing a bird in most instances, what struck me as being odd about the object is that unlike every other aircraft in the field of view, both fixed wing and helicopters, this object had no Navigational lights or any other kind of standard aircraft outboard lighting. The objects appeared to me as if it had hit exit velocity and escape the Earths gravity, I did not here any tell tail sonic booms to indicate that this was the case, i may have been just to far away to here. The is a naval air station north OF LOS ANGELES county where it is possible the craft was lAUNCHED. If it is one of Uncle Sams rides then hats off cause that thing was BADASS!
