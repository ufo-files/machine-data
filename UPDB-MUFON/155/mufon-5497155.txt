{dr}Multiple glowing objects seen by 3 people hovering and zig zagging across southeastern sky

From 8:35 P.M. until 8:55 P.M. I witnessed a cluster of multi-colored objects hovering at treetop level and making definite geometric shapes at times interacting with each other. I saw several objects making a huge double triangular shape and others hovering in groups of 3 side by side pulsating and shimmering. These objects were yellowish gold orange red all blended together. I went over and talked to two people standing by their car parked above me and asked them if they were noticing all these multiple lights and if they were enjoying the view as we were way up in the foothills of Mt. Tamalpais. They said yes they have been seeing a lot of these objects and wondered if they were some kind of drones or something.I told them I just counted 10 objects all at once. Then I walked back to where I was standing and a few minutes later counted 14 of these orb like objects. A few minutes later I counted 21 objects- 12 to the left of me and 9 to my right. This was at 8:44 P.M. I could'nt believe it so I counted one more time before I went in and came up with 17. There may have been one more car witness to this as I saw another car leave with the 2 people I talked to.I also saw a cluster of 14 objects on the night of 12-23 as well as a group of 14 objects on the night of 10-29-14. I had binoculars with me during these sightings. Virtually all of these objects look identical and make no sound and light up really bright while they are close to the top of hills and at treetop level.
