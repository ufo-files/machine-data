Orange-red round object saw in Sao Bernardo Brazil at low altitude

A round-shape orange-red UFO was seen at low altitude flying over Sao Bernardo do Campo Brazil. The strange object appeared to be near the observer. The object was first observed stationary. After 2 minutes, it moved almost instantaneously in a "z" trajectory going up. After some minutes of stationary position, it slowly went to the NW and disapeared in the horizon.
