{jpg} Long straight line light in sky

At 5:50 am (local time) on Tuesday 1-7-20 I observed a very long straight solid line of a slightly yellowish white light in the eastern sky slowly moving toward the south south east.....moving so slowly that I, at first, wasn't sure it was moving. Sighting continued for approx. 3-4- minutes until tree tops blocked my view of the object as it slowly drifted away. At the time of the sighting skies were clear and the object was only slightly brighter than the moon & stars and, because of it's size and shape, easily stood out in the morning sky.
