{dr}Observed in daylight under a tree. Red/orange, dome shaped, approximately 8 ft. in diameter. Suddenly disappears.

I sited a UFO in the mid-1960s, perhaps 1966 or 1967. I may have been about 6 or 7 years old. It landed on our property located in Long Island. It was perhaps 8 feet in diameter, color red, solid, and perhaps glowing. To the best of my recollection, it was a domed shape. It landed under a large tree. I watched it for about 3 minutes (approximately, because it is difficult to specifically judge time, especially when one is young and not very conscious about time. I observed it in daylight from our living room, which was off limits to us children. Yet, for some unknown reason, I decided to go into this room, which had a locked glass sliding door. I was dressed in my pajamas and robe. As I said earlier, I watched it for several minutes. I then tried to unlock the sliding glass door to go to it, but it wouldn't open no matter how I tried. I then ran into the kitchen beside the living room, also with a similar view to our backyard. It also had a sliding glass door with access to the place where I spotted the UFP. This time, I was able to unlock the door, but when I got to the kitchen, the object was already gone. I ran down to the tree to look for clues, but found none. It is a memory that has stayed vivid, even after all of these years.
