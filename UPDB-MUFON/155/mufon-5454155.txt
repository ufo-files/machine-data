{tjd} Blue/green orb seen above major street

During the evening of April 1st, my wife was coming home from the grocery store when she stopped on Queens Blvd facing West to take a few pictures of some clouds that she found interesting. After the fact when we were looking at the picture, we saw the orb. It seems to be floating in mid-air and emitting a blue/green light. It is just NW of the setting sun and at a distance, it appears to be hovering just under or through a break in some clouds. The orb must have continued on its northerly path because it is gone from subsequent pictures.
