In 1972 my wife and my two year old were moving to Florida to stay with my parents in Fort lauderdale prior to getting settled there. On our way down doing no more than 45 mph because were were pulling a small U-Haul that would start to sway if we went any faster. We traveled the length of Florida during the night arriving in Fort lauderdale right after day break. We traveled from Jacksonville to Fort Lauderdale using US 1. While driving I noticed an extremely bright light that lit up the inside of our car and almost too bright to look at. It was out my left window which faced due east over the ocean. It was about 60 degrees off of the horizon. It was there always and never changed location. Not up, down forward or backward. Just there. My wife was sleeping in the back seat with our son. I woke her up and said, do you see anything. Here first words were. What is that bright light. I told here I had no idea and that it has been there ever since we got on US 1. I pulled into a gas station in Titusville and asked the attendant to take a look at what we have been seeing. All he said was .probably a UFO, we see them here all the time. He never took a look at it. Started driving and it was still there. In the same spot. It took us about 10 hours to get to Fort Lauderdale, all the time that light was there. Never changed at all. Same spot and same brightness. It was now dawn and even while it was getting light it was still there. Turned off of US 1 in Fort lauderdale. Got to my parents home and tried to see it. It either disappeared or I just could no longer see it from that location.
