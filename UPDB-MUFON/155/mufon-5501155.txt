{nd} Red lights above Fort Wayne

From around the 69-Illinois roads exit looking south there were multiple red/orange lights, looking to be as far south as the Yoder area. The lights were in formation and headed east towards the Ohio border. There was a lot of cloud cover so they eventually moved out of visibility. I decided to drive after them and later found that the airport had out aircraft bright blinking red lights around where the lights were initially seen, these were DEFINITELY distinctly different than the original UFO's.
