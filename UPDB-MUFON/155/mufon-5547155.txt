While driving down the road to our left we saw a bright white light hovering very low near the trees, When we got up on it it flew so quick, we seen roundness and red, green, and blue lights.

My daughter and I was going to pick up my youngest daughter at a relative's house. We looked over to left where their house is and saw a bright light hovering near their tree line, thinking it was a helicopter. We made a left onto their road and saw a car with their brake lights on in the middle of the road. Made another left into their drive way and this object flew over my vehicle and proceeded in front of the house so quick then disappeared. It was very low, we saw roundness with a red, green, and a blue light. It flew straight across their yard and there was no sound. We did not see any wings or tail so I know it was not an airplane or helicopter.  Something flying that low and we saw no neighbors outside.  It actually gave us chills.
