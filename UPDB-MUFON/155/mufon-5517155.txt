{flg} Bright hovering light

My sister and I were driving home around 1:30 a.m. on Friday/Saturday, 7/13/12 on Hwy 151 North, just passing Hunt Lane when we both noticed a bright light, like a yellowish star hovering about 100 ft up and to the right of us. Suddenly, the light shot down in a diagonal path and disappeared. Aside from the fact that it was hovering before it shot off, we thought maybe it was a shooting star.
