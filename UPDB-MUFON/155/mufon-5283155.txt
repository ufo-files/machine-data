I was playing basketball with my mother and little brother when I saw a white/silver sphere

I was playing basketball with my mom and little brother at the elementary school basketball court. Right before that UAP/UFO report came out. It was very bright outside with clear skies. I had just made a three pointer and turned around when something in the sky caught my eye. I saw a white/silver sphere flying by itself. Moving very slowly and was completely silent. And the only color on it was the white/silver bright sphere itself. No other lights so I knew it wasn't a plane. I checked for jet stream but none of that either. Even If they do make sphere shaped drones I took a drones class in high school and knew it was flying to low for it to be a drone or any other aerial vehicle. I asked my brother and mom if they saw it too. My brother thought it looked off but my mom just shrugged it off. So I followed it to see what it was. I idiotically walked behind a big tree next to the court and lost sight, since I was looking up at the sky. I walked around it and in seconds it was gone. Vanished. Looked up the street and nothing. No offense but I don't believe in aliens or any of that crap. But I had no other way to describe what I had just seen. But "other worldly". Coolest thing I've ever seen even to this day. It was about the size of a baseball. And bright and silver or white. And completely silent with no evidence left behind.
