What i remember from a long way ago.

Ok, i was young like seven, six or seven. Night had just fallen. My father was outside and me and mom come out.Theres this low slow , like a ball of fire orange light. I think when i first saw it it was a small distance away, 300 m or so, NE. Then it s glides over to the north anddoes this, gets nosey and goes almost over our heads N to S then SE, pausing first, and also before the end. Dont remember heat or not. At closest, it appeared like 50m or so away, but looking up isnt great for distance. If there were other features to it, they didnt much register. Noise... certainly no chopper. I don't know if it was silent, but may well have been. Also, have memory of like looking down on the folks and myself while looking up at the light. No odour i recall. And then just gone. Before mom passed i asked her, but she nor dad recall. I think thats it.
