Small sphere not perceptible unless in slow motion video at seg 10 moving W-E then turning N and disappearing

I was filming the full moon at the beach (Playa Coson in the Dominican Republic)around 644PM AST. I did not see the object untill I saw the video in slow motion. At the beginning I thougt it to ve a falling star but when I reviewed the object trajectory noticed that it never destroyed as falling stars do. Its shape never changed. It made a sharp turn to the north. I was flying West to East then the sharp curve to the North. It wss the same night the lunar eclipse was announced on January 20 2019 @1030PMAST.
