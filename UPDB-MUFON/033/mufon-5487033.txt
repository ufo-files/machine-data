{cak} Driving north on I-75 started to take exit 81 watched triangle with lights move and hover

We were driving home around 1am on 11/21/15. Taking North I75 as we approached exit 81 I pointed it out to my partner, they shrugged it off initially but started to watch it as I drove, it came from the North West going East crossed over the free way and turned around to head back west. It was silent. I could not make out exact shape until we were in the off ramp I looked up and saw that it was a large triangle with white lights on each corner. It stood still for a moment and then sped off. It seemed very low for a plane/helicopter and did not look or sound like anything I've ever seen.

There were maybe twenty other vehicles I do not know if anyone else saw. How could they not?
