Two spherical uap/UFO in sky over sea

Was photographing sunset. Noticed bright dash in sky to right of sun. Which moved and also became more obviously spherical. Another appeared. Other flying objects in sky, leaving trails - planes? appeared in sky. 

Also I noticed there were dimples in their middles with my eyes. When they became clearer. 

My husband briefly came to look and saw the lights but thought maybe planets. I had seen them move though. I recorded images for 35 minutes. We were away, so I left it for a week, then looked through the photos and videos and realised they couldn't possibly be planets as they moved.
