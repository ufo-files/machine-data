{cak} Coming home from work...saw strange lights pulsating in the sky through car windshield.

I was coming home from work and saw through my windshield what I thought at first was a plane. But there was something unusual about the pulsating light. The light rays came from the center in a very strange way...it's hard to explain. At first I thought I was one object...but as it passed over my car I think it was two objects very close to each other. The smaller one had a steady blue light and the larger one had a very intense bright white light that seemed almost more like lightning than a normal light. I saw a UFO a month ago but by the time I pulled over it was gone. I never saw anything move so fast! This one I was lucky enough to get a brief clip of.It was around 9 at night just like the previous one ( or 2...I think they were also 2 very close to each other). I got a very strange feeling when observing these objects. Very hard to explain. I wish the video wasn't so shaky...but I knew if I pulled over to get a steady shot this one would also be gone. It's not like the previous one. It had 2 steady white lights...nothing was flashing at all...this one was flashing like crazy. Incredible amount of energy came off that bright light.
