Just happened to look up and saw two u.f.o.'s flying next to each other.

I was having a cigarette on my back porch. When I was done, I went to dump the butts from the tray in to the trashcan. For no reason I looked up and saw two blurry greenish objects moving VERY rapidly, AND I MEAN RAPIDLY!, from the east south east. I was awe struck. At first I thought it to be a meteorite.Then I realized there is NO WAY this is a meteor! The object's looked to be about one foot apart. They did not appear to be at a very high altitude, such as a passenger jet. Probably about half as much. Both of the objects made a tight turn like a 'C' and headed out to the Northwest. In PERFECT formation with the other. That is when they went behind the trees and I lost sight of them. I know what jet's, fighter jets, and everything we know about looks like. Day or night. These things were moving WAY to fast, silentley and in such a manner that I cannot relate in words. This was absolutly the most incredible sight I have ever seen. I'm alway's looking up to see something like this, but I just happened to look up and got blown away. I hope someone else in the area saw it too.
