While photographing the northern lights this object appeared in the shot.

My parents were on vacation in Iceland to witness the northern lights in person. They shot several pictures of the lights on a crystal clear night when you could see millions of stars. Later, while looking over their pictures there was this crazy object in one shot that no one saw. Half the picture is the norther lights(which do not photograph well with a standard digital camera)and on the other side of the picture is this crazy object that when we blew up looks like a creature with teeth and a tongue. It looked to me as if some extra terrestrial giant insect was peering at us through a portal in space......It's just crazy!
