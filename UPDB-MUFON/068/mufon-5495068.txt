{dr}Saw a craft with the brightest beam of light moving about 30mph and could hear only a whisp of air.

I walked outside and saw a craft moving very slowly about 1-2 miles away and it was shining a huge beam of light that appeared to shine another 1-2 miles ahead of it. It traveled north for ten minutes or so,then made a 90 degree turn west,right towards me. The diameter of the beam looked like it was 12 feet tall at that point,taking into account it was still 1-2 miles away. Ten to fifteen minutes later it slowly moved across the five acre lot beside ours,approximately 500 ft.above the ground and from me. It had red,green and white lights,and I believe it was the white lights that were flashing. I could see a dark colored body to the craft but couldn't make out a shape because it was so dark outside. It did not appear to have wings. It was very still and quiet out being in a rural area and the only sound it made was like a whisp of air movement or the sound of a breeze. It was moving approximately 30 mph.
