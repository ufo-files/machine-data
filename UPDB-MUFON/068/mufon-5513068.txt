(GS) 5 GREY AND REFLECTING OBJECTS CLOSE TOGETHER, HOVERING, AND DISSAPEARING

MY OFFICE LOOKS OVER SUNSET BLVD, AND I NOTICED 3 HOVERING GREY OBJECTS VERY CLOSE TOGETHER, MOVING AROUND SLOWLY OVER BUILDINGS IN WEST HOLLYWOOD. (NEAR DOHENY AND SUNSET) THEY SEEMED TO BE TURNING AND REFLECTING A LOT OF LIGHT EVERY ONCE IN AWHILE, AND WOULD BECOME ALMOST INVISIBLE FOR A FEW SECONDS WHILE IT WAS TURNING, BECAUSE OF THE GLARE. 2 MORE APPEARED, SOME DISAPPEARED, SOME FLEW STRAIGHT UP SLOWLY AND ALSO DISAPPEARED. THERE WAS 2 AIRPLANES FLYING THROUGH THE HORIZON AT THE TIME AND THE OBJECTS STOOD STILL. I FOLLOWED THE OBJECTS UNTIL THEY WENT UP TOO HIGH TO SEE.
