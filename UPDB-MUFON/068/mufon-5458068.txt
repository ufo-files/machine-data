{cak} UAP Bright White Triangle

Hanging out in the backyard and enjoying the hot tub with the family on a crystal clear Friday night. Temperature was mid 70's with no clouds whatsoever. My daughter noticed a bright light above the neighbors garage and said immediately "What is that dad." Instantly we all started comparing it to a plane that was flying by at the same time, but the plane had running lights that flashed in a pattern which this object didn't have any characteristics like a plane would have. Flight path was in a Di-angle line West to East above 500+ feet or higher. UAP was definitely the brightest light in the sky and disappeared right before our eyes. Entire sighting lasted about 1 minute. Thanks for your time.
