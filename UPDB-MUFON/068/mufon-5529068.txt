Saw silver disc shape high in sky over Fifth Third Ballpark

I was sitting at the traffic light at West River Drive and Lamoreaux waiting to make a left turn. I saw the disc shape silver object high in the sky over the ballpark. It just stayed in the same spot, not moving. I had to turn the corner and after turning I caught sight that it was still there. After I parked in the parking lot on the corner I walked out to the street but it was gone.
