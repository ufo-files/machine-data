Reddish orange glow, aero shaped, traveled slowly in a straight line.

There was five of us sitting on my front porch stairs.. We were just talking enjoying the beautiful night, and the next thing you know , a bright reddish orange glow lit up the sky.It caught attention right away! Our first reaction was " what is that?" it looked like a triangular shape of orange glow.So my friend took a picture of it with his camera phone, and when he zoomed in on it, it had an aero shape, and looked like fire.It slowly traveled across the sky in a straight line for about 2-3 minutes.. Then we could no longer see it once it went through the clouds...
