My wife and I were going into our back yard, she was looking up at the sky towards the North, I just happened to look to the South East and I see a green light flying towards us, Seems about 4 or 5 miles out maybe and 2 or 3 hundred feet up.. I was joking to her and say look a U.F.O. and then think to myself, Look no flashing lights. As she looks it starts making these sudden jerking movements and then just stops. I tell her to go get my phone and she is watching runs in the house, I watch as it starts moving side to side, My wife gives me my phone and I film it going across the sky with jerking motions, North bound.. Then it stops again, looks like it turns red, It has what seems like a port on the side with a light solid green then red then back to green.. We watch as it starts to go down very slow, then disappears behind some tree's of our neighbors. We ran out front and looked for it. We did not see it again. 

I have never seen anything make such sudden stops and turns or go up and down in this manner. I was pretty amazed and it made all the hair on my body stand up, I am glad my wife was with me, Because no one would believe this..
