witnessed 3 bright starlights move and dim out, one after the other.

on the evening of Sun May 3, 2020 at approx. 10pm pacific time, i was approaching my vehicle after just dropping off my children at their home in the San Francisco bay area city of El Cerrito. I was facing northwest and looking up into the sky at what i thought was a bright star.
The light then began to move easterly and faded out of sight. I thought that was interesting, and about 2 sec later i witnessed it again, a second point of bright light in the northwestern sky begin to move easterly and fade out. I was excited and kept staring in that direction. Within 10-15 seconds another point of light seemed to appear and do the same thing, moved easterly fading out as it went.
The fade outs only took about 5 secs but were very noticeable and distinct.
