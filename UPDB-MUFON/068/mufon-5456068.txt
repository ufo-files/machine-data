{cak} Groups of objects with lights flying in formation not on radar

The observed groups of objects with lights that were seen by many people in area and reported in newspaper were actually test flights of the F117 stealth fighter jet. They were flying in formation just at treetop level at about 300-400 miles per hour with very little sound and no observation on local radar. At that time the war on drugs was full on and there were lots of radar assets aimed at the border. They left NV and flew into Mexico and came back over Ajo AZ and Casa Grande by way of Tucson as a test. It worked very well and frightened a lot of people. The flight was even reported by a F117 pilot in a discussion of the development of the aircraft. I did see them and thought they could be planes but didn't know of any that could fly that low and slow.
