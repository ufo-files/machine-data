{kt}me and daughter saw hovering over our house then moved in front and turned over us

my daughter who is 5 and myself were coming home in my semi truck. We got to the intersection just before our house and saw a beautiful orange glowing ball over the top of our house. When I told my daughter look at that it slowly started moving north until it got in front of us. When in front of us it made a u-turn and flew over us. As it flew over us it disappeared. We didn't feel scared but amazed. It's almost as if it wanted us to see it.
