Orangish ball traveling at a high rate of speed. No trail.

I walked out on to my porch to smoke. I sensed something that made me look up and to my west. I saw a light orange small circle traveling at a very high rate of speed from what looked to be not to far away. I tracked this orange circle with my eyes until it was pretty much directly above me. It looked as if it went into a low and pretty small cloud but it didn't come out of the other side. It literally made the hair on my arms stand up. I have been studying aviation since childhood, I know my planets, Ive witnessed countless shooting stars, etc etc. I have never seen anything like this. When it was nearly overhead it seemed as if it had a light orange glow but it didn't have a glare. It looked to me as if it had a straight line flight path. My gut feeling is that it may have been stationary until I looked up and it traveled into the cloud. I cant explain what made me look precisely into the direction it came from. I have been able to stop thinking about it.
