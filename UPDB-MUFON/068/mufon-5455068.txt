{tjd} Flashing & Fading Blue Sphere

Saw a blue light sphere up in the dark night sky off by itself. I was heading west on I-64. No clouds. It flashed, enlarged then shrunk back to its original size, flashed again, changed direction, then vanished. It was very much bigger than a star or plane lights.
