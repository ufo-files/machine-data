large silent rectangular ufo dull in color without running lights

My husband, myself, and my eleven year old son were stargazing Tuesday the 25th of August 2009 at approx. 10:15 PM. We were looking at Jupiter in our south-east sky. As my husband was repostioning his telescope after our viewing of the planet and I notice a large rectangulr object block out the starfield of the night sky. As I focused my attention, I notice it made no sound, nor did it have any running lights on. After several seconds, I asked my husband to look up from his telescope and acknowledge what I was seeing. He did, and we watched until it left our field of vision. The object was moving in a south east direction. Compared to an airplane the object was large, and it didn't look to be high in our atmosphere. The reason why I hadn't reported the incident sooner was because I wanted to see if we had any satellite trajectories in our area that night.
