Flying 10 feet above my head. Ring shaped object. No sound.

I was outside my old house, half way down the street, I was with my friend. I noticed something above my head. It was the strangest flying object you could possibly imagine. It was only 10 feet above me. It must have hovered above us for at least 10-20 seconds. It was a dull, light grey ring shaped object about 2 feet wide, and the width of the ring was about 10-15cm thick, it had 3 small lights on this ring - Red, orange and green. It had an additional rotating ring around the main ring. The rotating ring was the same colour, thinner width, and it was attached to the main ring.
This object had absolutely no sound coming from it. We started shouting because we were over excited about this most unusual object hovering 10 feel above us. The object started flying diagonally up into the clouds very slowly which took about 2 minutes and it was about a quarter of a mile away as it disappeared into the clouds
Of course I considered it to be a drone or some kind of secret military device. But why was it so close to us making no sound at all? No engine, no propellers. Why would it fly off into the clouds?
This happened about 11 years ago. I was about 12 years old. This memory always comes back to my mind vividly. I didn1t think anyone would believe this. But I thought now would be a good time to finally share my story.
