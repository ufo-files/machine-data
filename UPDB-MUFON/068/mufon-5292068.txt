Sparkling lights in the sky

While standing on my back porch I noticed sparkling lights in the sky by a tree about 2 streets over. The lights would blink in different directions. I watch for a while and them saw them go up. So my eyes followed it. This is when I saw an object in the sky. It seemed to be in the shape of a circle. Then I could see the round shape. It looked like a circle made out of clouds. It had at least 2 layers of clouds with empty space in between each cloud circle. I could see rotating lights flashing within the bottom cloud ring. There were also round shading circles being expelled from the bottom cloud ring. However they would then shut out, then go upwards. Leaving a trails like a fireworks. After Which some other lights came from both directions. They entered the bottom cloud ring. Then the full thing made a slow northeastern turn. When I first saw the lights I didn't really think anything. After asking my daughter and daughter to look towards the tree, my exact statement to them was " look over the Butler house at the 2 trees that looks like it has a U between them. What Do you see? I asked at different times when the other one was not present. They each saw the lights. I would go in the house then come back out at different times to see if it was still there. Each time I was getting more and more nervous. After 10:00 I decided to stay in the house. I had to get a grip an try to go to sleep. I tried drawing it but that didn't work.
