{rjl} Mutiple fire like lights

I was outside looking to the south and saw really dim fire like and color lights. There had to be at least 15-20. They were in a pattern like how geese would fly. Very, very distance and really dim. Appeared for 7-10 sec. Looked like it was on a direct southern tack descending in attitude then ascended and disappeared. With as high as it seemed in altitude it had to have been extremely large. Maybe a mile or more in length.
