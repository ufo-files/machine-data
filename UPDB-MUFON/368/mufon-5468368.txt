{rjl} 2 solid white objects with straight flight paths

I went outside while taking a break from gaming, was looking at the moon, commenting to my son how bright it was for half-full moon, not many stars to see. After a few minutes, we saw more stars become visible and I noticed one moving across the sky, from W to ENE, at a fairly slow, steady speed and course, observed until light no longer visible. While observing, ran to get camera, "no storage space on memory card". Approx. 5-10 minutes later, observed a SECOND white solid light which looked like a star, track across the sky from W to ESE, also at constant speed and course making no sound. Approx 5 min later a small airplane traveled from SE to W at roughly same altitude, relatively low. Distinctive sounds and lights could not be confused as anything otherwise, a solid comparison.
