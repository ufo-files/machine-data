{jpg} going to friend house saw object hovering over trees in backyard only remember getting to my friend house and was told i was two hours late

I left my house by the back door to go to my friend house he lived on the next row house from mine. as I cut though the back yard there was some light I looked to my right and at tree top high I saw a cigar shaped craft hovering over the top of the trees where we (kids) played football. it was a red orange in color and made no sound. I remember standing there looking at it and then there was noting. the next thing I remember was beating on my friend door. my friend answer the door and we went up stair to his room some of my other friends were there. they all stated that I was a few hours late. I do not know how that happen.
