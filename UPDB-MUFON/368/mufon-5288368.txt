Multiple bright orb objects that seemed to be engulfed in some sort of bright energy substance, each big orb had a smaller less bright orb following.

On Sunday 25th April at 1AM, whilst camping up in the Cranham Woods, Gloucester with a few friends, Me, my friend D and my friend J Decided to go for a little walk, it was a very clear night, with a huge bright full moon, we went down to the bottom of a quarry that is located on Painswick Road (A46), Gloucester, as we got to the bottom we decided to take a seat on a big rock, unintentionally we all began star gazing, around 5 minutes into star gazing my friend J says to us “Hey can you guys see that or what?!” Me and D looked up but could not see it at first but after around 5 minutes of looking I finally spot it, the first sighting we had was of a very bright orb, illuminating orange (it looked like it was also surrounded by some sort of energy that warped around the object in a very frantic and electrified manor, that was also illuminated orange, like what the surface of the sun looks like when it wraps around), this object also had another, much smaller orb following it but this one looked just like a star, except it was mirroring the bigger orbs movements, following it every movement and staying the exact same distance apart the entire time.

These object would be zig zagging at very fast speeds, they would ascend then descend very fast also, and would make ‘O’ shaped manoeuvres in the sky very fast also, at some points they would go stationary then do an erratic movement into another part of the sky, these things seemed to be following a certain path around in circles as we were watching them for hours and they seemed to stay on the same path whilst also performing different, strange manoeuvres, when I got my phone out, I managed to get a photo (not great but it’s there) and then I started to record it, obviously on a phone it is hard to do, I was trying to keep my hands as still as possible as I wanted to authentically capture its movements, I managed to get a 33 second clip, and then my phone just switched off, at that time my phone still had 33% battery. As we observed these initial objects for a few hours we actually noticed there was around 6 more of these objects also dotted around the sky, each with their own smaller object following it and mirroring its movements, each of these separate things were miles of distance apart from each other in the sky. (Only the one, brightest orb can be observed in the picture or the video)
