{cak} Austell, GA - 12/12/2014 - 10:15pm (appx)

There were a lot of airplanes flying that evening before I saw the UFO. I was on Mableton Pkwy around 10:15pm driving home and there was an aircraft that seemed brighter and more different than the airplanes. I didn't really think much of it beyond that. I turned onto South Gordon and got to the corner of S Gordon & Factory Shoals and stopped at the light. Something told me to look up - so I looked out my window and I saw the brightly lit aircraft hovering motionless and soundless just a few feet above me. The aircraft was in the shape of a triangle - it had 6 to 10 triangle-shaped lights on the bottom surface as I looked up(can't really remember the exact count). The lights were lined up the same way you would rack the balls when playing billiards/pool and took on the shape of the aircraft. I did receive a telepathic message stating they were the Galactic Federation of Light but I did not see any ET beings - only the ship. The ship flew off as fast as it came - it did not make any sound and it seemed like I looked down for half a second and they were gone... I didn't see any trace of them when I looked back up.
