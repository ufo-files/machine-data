{dr}silver object hovered then left view over trees with erratic path

i was driving nw along hwy 20 away from the town of sisters before reaching black butte ranch. I dipped my head down slightly to get a better look at the butte and then i noticed a silver spherical object hovering somewhere above the height of the tree line ahead of me and before the butte. it only remained there a brief moment before shooting eastward over the treeline with a slightly erratic path. the entire sighting only lasted a two or three seconds; but it was during daylight, the object was well lit by sunlight and close enough that i could see any details that would identify it as a regular craft such as a helicopter. instead it looked like a silvery spherical object that appeared fuzzy as though it had heat ripples around it. it was far enough away that if it had been a loosed balloon it should have been much smaller and not hovered/accelerated like it did.
