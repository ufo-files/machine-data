{rjl} Object observed with phone outside at night

On 12/25/19 in Madison, Alabama, while at work, I went outside to call my wife on my cell phone and at approx. 7:40pm, noticed 2 rectangular shaped lights( almost window like) (dark amber) flying at approx 500-1000 ft altitude. These lights were steadily ascending, with a 3rd amber light trailing the first two, approx 500 feet back and a 4th amber light followed as well, another 750+ feet or more behind, towards the end. These lights ascended steadily in unison formation, as if attached, heading in a Northern vector direction and turned sharply towards a NW direction towards the city of Athens. Consequently, this location where I was, is close to an airport, as I often see commercial and sometimes military transports descending towards the airport. It is easy to hear the flights as they are close over the facility where I am employed. These lights in question, made no sound, and were traveling in opposite direction, of the way the planes land/descend typically. . Interesting in the video, the 4th amber light falls back, almost to a crawl, and the initial 2 are seen trailing off., as seen in the video. I called the Madison Airport regarding any info regarding this event and was told "nothing was noted out of the ordinary." When I initially saw these lights, I actually told my wife,"I'll call you back".
