Ok, so I have told this story to many people. Like 1% of people believe me. 

My friend and I were hanging out one night at my house after work watching tv. There was suddenly a awkward vibration. It was almost sound , but different. Hard to explain unless you were there . We went outside and saw some white light to the south of the house emitting from behind a tree . We started to walk that direction and the white light started to move . It then banked around the tree exposing its underbelly that has 8 round white lights . The craft then accelerated at a rate that I have never seen before . I was having issues tracking it with my eyes. It made this crazy vibration as it was leaving . I could feel it all over my body . 

You would think the story ends there . Nope.
It gets even more crazy. 

Almost immediately after this sighting , we saw low flying fighter jets scream through like they were chasing the object . The object was long gone though. There is no way they caught up. The sky for the rest of the night were full of activity . Jets and helicopters were all over for the next 3- 5 hours . We stayed up almost all night watching it . 

I will never forget this. It was as real as anything I have ever seen . I have never seen anything unusual like this before .
