(gs) A bright white object shot out from behind the stars and startled me, hovered for a second, then at a high speed headed East

On 6-20-2012 at 10pm Mountain Time, my 4 dogs ran outside, stood in the middle of my backyard, and barked in a high pitch, looking up at the sky. I ran outside to see what was wrong. It was a clear night, the stars were bright,the dogs all stopped barking at the same time. Suddenly a bright white object shot out from behind the stars and startled me. I felt a little fearful. It stopped and hovered for a second, then suddenly at a fast speed it headed East within a split second and was out of sight. I did not hear any noise or see any lights or vapors. The dogs and I went back inside the house.I felt a little shaken from the experience,sounds silly, but I think I was afraid of being abducted.
