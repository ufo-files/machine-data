This was the largest object I have ever seen in the night sky other than the moon. I would estimate this at 1/5 to 1/3 the size of a full moon. This object came from behind us and just flew overhead. The object appeared to be as high as the satellites orbit, but a hundred times, or more, larger. This was several times bigger than the ISS which I1ve also seen pass overhead many times.
