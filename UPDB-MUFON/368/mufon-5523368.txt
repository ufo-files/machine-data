Two bright lights in sky

I was outside walking my dog when I looked up at the sky and noticed two bright stars. After watching these stars I noticed that they began to fade, one faster than the other. I went to put my dog away and when I came back they were gone. After looking for them for the next 10 minutes, a light shot across the sky from where the two lights originally were. The light then stopped and faded away.
