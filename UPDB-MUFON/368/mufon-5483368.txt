{jpg} Large illuminated object observed hovering at close proximity, lost due to driving, once reaquired had traveled long distance rapidly

UFO was first seen hovering as I drove south on Wilmot Rd. Object was at an estimated height of 200 ft above ground. As I drove closer, I was able to get almost underneath it (~75-80 degrees from horizon). I continued driving past in order to find location to turn around, losing visual behind trees. Turning around at the nearest road and driving back to location took less than one minute. In that time, object traveled northwest a good distance, and was seen moving slowly and slightly angled (banking?). Object became obscured behind trees on the side of the road and visual was lost. Entire encounter took less than 2 minutes.
