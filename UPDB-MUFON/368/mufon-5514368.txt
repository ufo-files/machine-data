(GS) 2 very bright white stars together -- jumping back and forth side to side

I let my dogs out in the back yard for the last time before going to bed. Standing on deck I looked up and saw a big bright star. It started to move, but more like hoping or jumping. You could not miss this, it was too close to be a star. I went to the front yard to get a better look. No sound of a motor, as it got closer it looked like 2 bright stars together. A small plane , I could hear that engine flew right near the bright lobject. I live near AC airport and FAA. Center. I got the dogs back into the house and yes I was afraid. Iam 61 year of female, widowed, only lived in this town 1 year 8 months. I really don't want to go see if its still there. But I would like to know of anyone else who saw this. I know pilot of small plane saw it. I would like to call police but --- they might think I crazy or something.
