{tjd} Observed white object very high in altitude in the sky - over 40k feet moving west north west, turned and headed south

Was notified by my Grand Daughter that there was a possible comet outside, my Grand Daughter, Grand Son, Daughter and I went outside, we could see it high in the sky it was not moving that fast, that's when I realized it was not a comet, comets don't move like this did, it was moving slowly from left to right going at a west north west angle and then slightly turned to the west, after a several minutes it start to slowly turn towards the south where it kept going at the same speed. From where we were standing to me it appeared to be gaining in height and still moving south it eventually disappeared. This whole event transpired over about 15 minutes. I do NOT believe it was a rocket as has been claimed by news casts. My Grand Daughter mentioned that it was also being seen down south in the Los Angeles area as well and photo's were taken of it.
