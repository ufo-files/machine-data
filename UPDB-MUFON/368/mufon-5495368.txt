My husband and I were heading South to go to a store because we were going to his sister's house for a get together. He was driving, I was in the passenger's seat when my peripheral vision noticed a light & movement out the passenger window. I turned to look and saw an opague white glowing ball of light, about the size of a soft ball, right beside the window. It was staying at same speed as us, approx. 35 miles an hour. I looked at it then turned to my husband to say,"What is that?, and when I looked at my husband he was looking towards my way, and he said,"What the hell is that? I then looked back toward the window, and it had moved forward and was at my front corner of the windshield. It was like it had looked at me then moved forward to look at my husband. Then it took off at the same height but very fast speed. We were in awe as we watched it vanish into thin air about a half mile ahead of us. We both felt like it had been looking at us to see who or what we are.We kept talking about it as we couldn't believe we had seen a UFO. We still talk about it and believe in UFO's.
