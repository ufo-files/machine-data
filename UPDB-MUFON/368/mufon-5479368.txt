{jpg} Looked like a satellite but changed direction. Never seen that before

This happened to me about 6:30am before time changed, so it was still very dark outside. I was at my house taking the trash can up to the road. The sky was clear (no clouds). I was walking back down my driveway looking at the stars and I noticed this object coming from the north. I thought it was a satellite because it was that high in the sky and had no flashing light just like a satellite and was moving about the speed of a satellite. The first thing I noticed that made me pay closer attention to this was that the light seemed to get brighter and then the object made a very sharp turn to the east and seemed to accelerate and accend very rapidly and disappeared. Now where this object disappeared there was 3 stars around it and my vision was never obscured from clouds or anything else so the object just disappeared. I have seen several satellites and I have never seen one change course and then just disappear. I have never been a person that believes in UFO's but what I observed that morning I truely believe was something that was not part of our world,and I also feel very strongly that whatever it was knew that I saw it. This is my story and I would swear that the information I have provided is true and as far as I am concerned this was something that was not man made.
