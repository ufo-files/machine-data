saw a UFO-like object moving from the Marina to just over Hayes Valley. It stopped suddenly, hovered in place for a few seconds the quickly began heading West towards the ocean. The object was very bright, multi-colored (red, green and purple) and was flying very low (less than three hundred feet). It made no detectable noise-- an object of this size, moving at these speeds and this low to the ground should have made a serious rumble.Unfortunately I lost sight of it as it moved West. After hovering another second or two it moved off quickly to the West towards the Pacific Ocean (although not as fast as when it was moving in a direction from North to South). I was on the phone with when this occurred so I could not get a picture. It was in my field of vision for less than 15 seconds so getting a decent shot would have been impossible anyway.
What I saw was not a conventional aircraft (plane, helicopter, hot air balloon etc). I do not know what it was other than it was something I have never seen before. I have been on Google to see if anyone has reported anything. So far that has been a bust.
