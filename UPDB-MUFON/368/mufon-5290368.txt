Looked like a marble in the sky

I'm in bed, sitting up. Just started my state mandated sexual harrassment training... I looked out the window and saw what appeared to be a shiny metallic marble go by in the sky between the trees outside my window and the trees across the street which are 50' at least. It really appeared to be higher due to angle of view. I sat forward to see if it would be visable past/through the branches and it was moving too fast. I registered it with my eyes and brain and then it was gone....
