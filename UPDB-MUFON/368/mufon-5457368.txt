Bright light in the sky in the early hours of the morning.

On October 14, 2019, at about 3:20 AM, I was in the backyard of my house, which is located in the City of São Paulo, Brazil, observing the Orion Constellation in order to identify the three huge stars in the Orion's Belt as well as Betelgeuse (I've always loved Astronomy and Cosmology!) when I noticed a very bright, stationary light in a region of the sky where there'd been nothing when I got to the backyard. I stared at the light for about one minute. I was speechless and and couldn't move a muscle! The light grew brighter as if it'd ''noticed'' that I'd ''spotted'' it somehow and suddenly began to dwindle away and eventually came off, so to speak. I stood there motionless in the backyard for about ten minutes. Maybe I was waiting for the light to reappear, which, of course, didn't happen. It was 3:20 AM, so I obviously ruled out all ''explainable'' possibilities such as airplanes, choppers, satellites etc...Therefore, I could only consider it to be a type of U.F.O. Whenever I'm in my backyard at night I still look toward that same spot in the sky hoping to see it again. I'll have to learn to live with something that just cannot be explained...:(
