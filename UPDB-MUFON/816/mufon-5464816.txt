{tjd} bright white light flickering at different speeds red light at times jolting in diff directions in diff directions diff directions small trail behind it

At 9:45 pm on October 29th 2018 myself and four others spotted a huge flickering white light in the sky that jolted in different directions and then hovering in one place for a few minutes. Once this star like craft moved into a completely opposite direction at an unusually fast speed it was evident the trail of light behind it before it moved it would flicker a bright red light along with the nonstop glowing white flickering light and then again would take off at a high speed in differentiating directions. Once it was out of sight another craft was spotted farther away with the pulsating white light, hovering, then only moving a few degrees up and down left and right but again extremely high speed then disappeared. What seem to be the first craft again reappeared in a completely different area moving very erratically before stopping, having a red light flicker a few time, then moving around all over For a little while and then jetting off to about north to north east direction to then somewhat disappear. Minutes later a huge military aircraft was seen flying towards the same area. About 10 minutes later another military plane was seen flying in that same area. This was in Springfield Massachusetts witnessed by five people.
