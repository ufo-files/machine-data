(GS) Bright Star Like Object Darts into Sky

I was on my way home from work on Woodend Road going Northbound I 435 when I looked up and saw an object that I thought was a bright star then all of a sudden it changed direction and at a very high rate of speed disappeared into the sky. The speed of the object was extraordinary and a helicopter in area was searching for something. The incident lasted about 10 seconds total.
