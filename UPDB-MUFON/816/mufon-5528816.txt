Luminous white dots (not bright) in a triangle shape

6:45pm, clear skies, no wind, 39 degrees outside, no planes visible. I stepped outside to have a cigarette in my back yard, looked up toward the south and at about 45 degrees up I saw what at first looked to be the white wings of birds in a V shape moving from east to west but as I looked it was moving too fast to be any birds and was apparent it was an object with a body with luminous dots (don't know quite how to explain it, it wasn't lights but could make them out faintly, like they were almost in a mist). Object emitted no sound. I stepped back to see around the shop we have that was blocking my view to see if I could still see it and I could. I watched still going west until it was out of sight. I have seen many flocks of geese (I lived in Jackson, WY. for 25 years) and these were not geese or birds of any kind. This had a body that was barely there (hard to explain). It had to be rather large object but move fast making no sound. When I realized it wasn't birds, I knew it was a UFO, I was in awe, I wasn't scare, I just wanted to watch it. I felt like getting in the car to chase it but knew it was way gone. I hope this makes sense to you. Sorry, no photos, there wouldn't have been time to get the camera. *(Wit's first name deleted--CMS/sg).
