{rjl} Red-orange sphere hovering over house

I was near my house and I hurried to tell my neighbors from the night before about an object I had seen a minute before. I see the door is open and the lights are on. I lay my bike down in the driveway and when I look up there is a large red-orange sphere 5-10 feet above their house. This is 6-8 feet in diameter. Larger than the one I saw just a minute before. I rush inside to get them. They come outside and the three of us look at it for a few minutes. It ascends and speeds to the South, flashes a few times as it goes, and is gone.
