{cak} It was in the sky moving slow with a circular rotation

In 1976 me and a few friends were in the street playing jump rope. It was9 pm at night so you could not have missed the bright well light object in the sky.instantly I knew what it was although I was 7 I knew what a blimp looked like, we seem them all of the time. I looked up I and i said look and I started pointing. It was a saucer shape disk it was white it had lights around the outside,it had a small dome like on the top. The lights that was on the outside was spinning counter clock wise and the saucer itself was spinning in the other direction as it moved slowly through the sky. One of my friends which was a bit started talking like Martians. The saucer kindly glided away. I told my mom in fact I told so many people but no one believed me. I stop talking about this incident around 25yrs ago but till Today I have forgotten about it and I'm most positive that I'll never forget it.
