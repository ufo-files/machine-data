{cak} Reddish orange orb dropped smaller orbs that fell straight down

At approximately 10:40pm, I went outside to walk my dog. I looked up at the sky and noticed a star-like object in the low northeastern sky. It was dark amber or red in color. I thought it was Mars at first because it's been very noticeable in the sky lately. But I realized it wasn't quite in the right spot to be Mars, and this object seemed to be close. Then this orb started dropping multiple slightly smaller orbs. It was dropping them continuously for what seemed like a minute or two? They were the same color. They were dropping straight down as if falling to the ground, but I can't tell if they actually landed because they were too far in the distance. As they fell lower, they got dimmer. One car was driving down my street and I started pointing up at the sky, but I don't think the person noticed me. The main orb stopped dropping the smaller orbs, and it slowly got dimmer and smaller till I could barely see it at all. It moved upwards. Then it's light came back and dropped a few more smaller orbs that did exactly the same as before. Then the main orb got dim again, and it appeared to be moving away in distance slowly getting more dim until I could no longer see it.
