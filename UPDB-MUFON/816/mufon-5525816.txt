Orange ball moving across the sky from West to East

There was four of us out by the firepit in the back yard watching for satelites when out of the west came this orange ball of light. This object was brighter than the ISS and moving a little quicker across the sky than the ISS. There were no flashing lights, just a steady bright orange glow and no sound. The object kept a steady pace across the sky directly over head from West to East at 11:52 pm. Sunday 07/03/11... Three of the four of us who witness this event, are Pilots. We know it wasn't a plane. The object moved to quick and was to bright for a satelite. We pretty much guessed it might be a metor skimming across the atmosphere. It's not like anything we have ever witness before. We watched the object fly beyond the tree line where we lost sight of it. Truly amazing to see.
