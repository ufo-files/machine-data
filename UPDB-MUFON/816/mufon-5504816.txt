{cak} Triangular shaped UFO flying 1 mile away. Lights flashing, dissapeered from view after 10 seconds.

My father, brother, and I were returning from a restaurant around 9PM. We were driving down the road to my house. I noticed an aircraft. When I looked closely, I realized it was not an F18's lights, or any other USN aircraft from the area. I know the Navy aircraft well by sight. It was a dark, triangular object. There was 1 or 2 lights in the center of the craft. These lights did not flash. The outline of the craft flashed every few seconds. It was flying a straight path. When I told my father ( former USN aviation officer), the crafts lights went out right away. My father did not look up in time to see the craft. No noise was heard, we were in the car.
