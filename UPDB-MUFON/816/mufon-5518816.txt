My husband and I were sitting around our fire pit enjoying then nice evening. When I went to look for a very bright star to the west. When I looked into the sky I saw these two pulsating orange color orbs came from the south west heading north east no sound traveling quite fast. pass behind trees and only one came out and then that one just fizzled out... I couldnt take my eyes off of them. I have never seen anything like this. It was not an air plane or a helicopter it didn't make a sound. The two objects stayed the same length apart. It was the weirdest thing I ever saw.
