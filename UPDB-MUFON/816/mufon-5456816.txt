Hovering craft giving off white and green pulsating lights

I opened my back door and I immediately saw something in the sky. I’ve never seen an aircraft give off a green light, it’s usually red blinking lights. This was different. The object was pulsating green and white lights. I watched the object for about 10 minutes and I realised I should be recording it. I videoed it for about 3 and a half minutes and then stopped. I went upstairs to try and get a better look but the object must have been behind a neighbouring house. I came back downstairs and viewed the object for a further 10 minutes (approximately). The object was slowly moving around the night sky and moved diagonally upwards to my right. I went back upstairs where I got a better view of the object. I went to get my phone as it’s got a better camera on it and as I sighted the object again it seemed to be considerably dimmer. And then it just disappeared. I could see stars with the naked eye but not in the video. All that can be seen in the video is what I’ve been talking about.
