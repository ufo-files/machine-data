Saw 1 object that was circular in shape. It had a silver or gray ring around it and a very big bright white light  in the center.Hovered .

I saw what I first thought was a plane because we live near the airport & see planes all the time. It flashed across the sky very fast & my first thought was that Ive never seen a plane move that fast before. Then I realized it wasnt a plane. I saw an extremely bright circle of light. It stopped in the sky & I could see a silver or gray ring around it but no lights were on that part of it. The whole center was a bright white light. It moved quickly, hovered, moved around in a circle & then looped around & hovered again. After it hovered at the last spot, its light intensified & then it just disappeared. - It did not accelerate out of sight or drop down - It was just gone. There was no noise. I was very excited to be witnessing it. Afterwards I felt tense & kind of aggitated & my stomach muscles are still tight. I KNOW what I saw but today I questioned in my mind how I could have witnessed something like that. I also have a sense of loss & have cried for no reason that I can think of....
