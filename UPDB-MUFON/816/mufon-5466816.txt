{rjl} White orb appeared out of strange cloud formation and hovered about 49 feet then disappeared.

On August 3rd at exactly 5:25 p.m. I was in my backyard bird watching when I noticed a strange cloud formation that did not seem to have been there minutes prior. The cloud resembled a short contrail however there was no jet that could have created it as I had been birdwatching for roughly an hour already and would have noticed. At the time I was using my Bushnell 7 x 35 binoculars so I directed my gaze at the cloud. Less then a minute after directing my gaze at the cloud a strange white orb appeared out of the middle of the cloud slowly ascending about 40 feet in approximately ten seconds. At first the object resembled a white balloon as it was ascending but it soon came to a complete halt in mid-air without wavering. Understanding that the mechanics of wind would not have allowed for this stillness I began to panic internally and gazed intently into the binoculars. The object now hovering appeared to have an underlying shape that extended below what I once thought was the white balloon. There appeared to be a gray stripe that outlined a curve along he bottom of the object. The underlying shape of the vehicle appeared to resemble that of an oval that gets wider at the bottom and has a flat bottom. I am under the impression that the white orb I was seing was a light that was being cast from the vehicle which at the time was being camouflaged. The object hovered for about 30 seconds before vanishing into thin air.
