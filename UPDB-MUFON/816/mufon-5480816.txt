Video taken in slow mode. Disk flew swiftly across sky

Grand forks Alberta. Husband took a video of my sister climbing a tree. He recorded using a slow mode feature for comic reasons. After watching the video afterward, a black disk could be clearly seen moving swiftly across the sky. It's reasonable to conclude that in 'real time' this disk must have been moving extremely fast! Hence, the reason we did not see it while we were recording my sister.
