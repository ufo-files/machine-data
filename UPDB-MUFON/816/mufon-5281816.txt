Ball of Light

Riding my motorcycle to work early this morning before sun up. I take a back road called 218 where it's just two lanes through woods and farmland. I notice something moving to my left. I look and see a bright orb that looked like a shooting star falling towards the ground, right before it reached the tree line it shot back up and vanished. This is the second time I've seen weird lights like this in this area and have pictures from the first time. Another co worker also saw the light this morning. 

The photos I'll attach are from my first sighting. I posted the pictures on Facebook a year ago.
