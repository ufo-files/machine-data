Long, rectangular bright/white object appearing in video of sunset

I was recording video on iPhone 12 of rain clouds over Sandia Mts. Began filming North direction and slowly panned to West and sundown. Dense, dark clouds over mts then moving to clearing skies towards the West. Sun is seen just above horizon. In reviewing the video I noticed an “object” appearing on the upper right of the screen at 55 seconds into video. As I continued pan the “object” descends, hovers, descends, moves up and down. The video terminates with shot due West at the sinking sun with bright orange sunset clouds. The “object” is now just above the sun. At times the “object” appears shaky - that's me, I think.  I had not noticed the “object” while filming but only on reviewing video. I went back to spot on patio where I shot video. Repeated my motions while making the video, looking for anything that may have caused a reflection. Nothing. My house was behind me, no lights on that could account for a reflection. I have attached video file below - I hope. Perhaps this is a video “artifact” and perhaps you can make that determination. Unusual enough I wanted to report it to you.
