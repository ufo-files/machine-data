{rjl} Black object high rate across sky & exploded but no noise

Around 1990 give or take a year. 3 of us were going deer hunting in Ross County, Ohio. We exited our car and began walking down a dirt road when I noticed something in the dawn sky, it was a dark / black object that perception suggested it was in outer space. I watche the object for about 3 - 5 seconds (at high rate of speed) it appeared to explode without any noise. The "explosion" looked like what gasoline / oil looks like when it's in water. Kinda of a rainbow of colors. Anyway, there were 3 of us, after the "explosion" I looked over at my 2 friends and thank goodness 1 of them saw it also. He looked and me and asked "did you see that? Of course, yes. Then he asked "were you waiting on the sound of an explosion. I replied, yes. The other friend did not see it.
Anyway, it is probably explanable, but it's the strangest thing I've ever seen in the sky.
