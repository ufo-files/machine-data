{nd} a red light blinking but would go off at times and then blink white

We were out on our porch and saw this red light blinking. We're not sure what it was but it was circling south of us going from n/w to s/e. It would be stationary at times with a white light and then continue moving with a red blinking light. It lasted about 20 minutes and then it was gone.
