We seen something almost like an acorn outside above the telephone pole ,at that time I thought it was just me and my mother there, but after years went by I told my sister , she said she was there and so was my brother , we never spoke about this till we were in our 40s ,and our story's are just a little different , but overall the same. Well after watching it there , my mother called the Pittsburgh air force base and reported it, they told her that a piper cub would come check it out then if it was something the jets would come , next thing you no a small plane came flew around it then went back to the airport ,not even 10 mins went by and here came two jets , that's when the object was gone, my sister said the object went straight up , I didn't see it disappear , I was watching the jets , then my mom called the air force base to see what it was , and they said we never called and they didn't no anything about it !! I never talked about it cause I thought it was a dream till one day we were talking about dreams we had and I told my dream that was not a dream I guess , I remember it as it was yesterday in the air ,our stories differ , like my sister said the piper cub came second the jets were first , that don't seem rt , but who knows the details are foggy all these years later I was about seven and I'm 47 now my sister is 3 years older then me, my brother would not talk about it he's 6 years older then me , but he said he was there, love to no if there was other people there that seen it in the seventies , haven't seen anything since , well that's my story love to hear from somebody! Oh ya this happened by the Pittsburgh pa airport .
