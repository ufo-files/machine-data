{tjd} My mother and I witnessed a disc like object hovering above the tree top. Stopped the car and watched it.

My mother and I were on our way home. We observed a large disc like object hovering in the air to our right. It was in a clearing on the edge of town near a rail road. It was just above the tree top. At that time there were very few trees there. We stopped the car. Got out beside the car and stood and watched it. It was a large disc like object. There were no significant markings or writing in it. It was seamless. About a five foot high ring around the bottom of it had an endless stream of multicolored lights. It looked like oil on clear water in the sunshine with a light behind it to accentuate the colors. It was dark on the bottom. I don't know how long we watched it. I know at one point I got back into the car and got in the floor trying to hide from it. And I could still see it. I remember getting back out of the car. And we stood and talked about it. I was very scared. It was an overwhelming experience. I do not remember us leaving. I just remember about half way home in the car and it was dark. I was not afraid anymore. We did not discuss it again until about ten years ago. And we still both remembered the same thing. It was the most beautiful and terrifying thing that I have ever witnessed in my life.
