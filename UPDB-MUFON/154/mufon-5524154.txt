Orange/Red Light observed hovering, no noise, and then ascended until disappeared

While walking our dogs on 5th street in Hampton, VA., at approximately 8:15 pm on September 10, 2011, my mom and I observed what at first appeared to be a falling star. My mom even commented, "Look! Is that a falling star?" As it came closer into our immediate field of vision, probably not more than a mile from us overhead, it just stopped moving and hovered above the trees. It appeared as a glowing red/orange ball of light, I can best describe it as resembling a "fireball." It remained stationary for just a few minutes and then began to move west at a very slow pace. It slowly ascended higher until it was completely out of sight and just disappeared.
