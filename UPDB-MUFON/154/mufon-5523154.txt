White then red blinking light

We were out star-gazing when I noticed a light that seemed to be blinking red and white light. It was a light right above the tree tops that was blinking white than red. We thought it was a star at first, but we kept watching it and s when we saw it moving. It was far away, but wecould see it moving slowly. It moved in a straight line, stop, than rise. It did that several times, than it became motionless. We watched it for a while, maybe an hour. We tried to take pics and video, but I guess it was to far away because nothing showed up. This was at night. Eventually we left because it was getting late. When we got home, my brother called and said that shot straight up and lined up with other stars. We went back te following night but we saw nothing. There was no star where we first saw it.
