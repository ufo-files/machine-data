Star child

While working in the desert on solar fields late at night my crew and i would see a big glowing object fly around almost like a test flight or something.... It would happen all the time then flight straight into the mountain.. There is a UNUSED MILITARY ARMORY that is guarded very close by black suvs and trucks. They almost look bullet proof. Well anyway one night on the way to work a child in a suit was in the middle of the solar field and all he would say is he was dirt biking with his family and got lost. It was like he was trainned to say it... Almost like he was captured or something.... So we call the police and before the police get there a convo of trucks and suvs pull up. The men all jump out and surround us. They grab the boy and put him in the middle suv. They one really tall odd looking man said" tell the police WE pick him up" then they just left.... A few weeks later our substation that we were going to work on EXPLODED... It was almost like a warning to stop talking about it.
