The time was around 8:20pm when I noticed a large object with flashing lights treetop level (object to dark too see)S. Then i saw a green orb/light SW:

I looked out of the window (South-viewing) and I noticed a possible UFO with three multicoloured flashing lights at treetop level. Distance was around 0.5 miles from me and the UFO lights. I observed and filmed it with my Lumix camera for about a couple of minutes and I actually captured the UFO take-off on camera, it just seemed to disappear out of view. Then a couple of seconds later, I looked up 55° SW and I saw a stationary green pulsating light which changed to a bluer colour after around a minute. This stationary light could have been the same object as the one that took-off. After around 5 minutes of hovering 55° Southwest, it slowly and eventually disappeared.
