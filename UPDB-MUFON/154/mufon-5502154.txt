{dr}Black object hovering in sky in Livonia Michigan. Fighter jet also directly overhead, but much higher. Observed by three people walking on gill rd. I stopped to look and saw it as well. Looked large and high but just hung in the air.

Diriving north on Gill Rd. At about 9:15 PM June 5th between Seven and Eight Mile Rd. Observed people looking up in the sky. I looked up about 45 degrees to the south west and saw this object just sitting in the sky, not moving. It was large and dark and almost like the side of a can. A fighter jet was directly overhead as I could see the contrail. But, the jet was much higher. Another jet was off to the north. Felt surprise as it was big based on the height of it, but seemed suspended in air. I'm sure others saw it as wellas they were staring up at it. I took my eyes off of it, drove home and lost it a block away.erhaps a small helicopter?
