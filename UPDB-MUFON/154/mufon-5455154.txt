{rjl} Saucer shape, moved right and left occasionally, but remained mostly in one spot.

For the past week, every time I take my dogs out, about 8:30 pm, I have seen what appears to be a very bright star, but its brightness and the fact that it changed from red to green occasionally, made me realize it couldn't be a star. The object appears in the same spot each night, best guess about 2 degrees south of west from my front door. I asked my (adult) son to take pictures of it, which he did, both here, and from the Walmart (17 miles east of our home) before sunset, the object was very clearly a spaceship. It was saucer shape, with windows around the middle. I have suggested strongly that he make a report to MUFON. Since the ship has appeared regularly, I hope someone will take some pictures to share.
