{cak} Strange lights and Object sighting

On 9/11/15 Friday night, I was at home with my two children, my mother was visiting..at approx. 9:50pm My daughter ask me to come outside and see what an object in the sky was, I was prepared to tell her it was a star and leave it at that, I noticed to southwest of my home a pulsating light changing colors red,green and white, east of it was another light solid green..out of nowhere it was joined by two other green lights which shaped to a triangle, my kids exclaimed wow as they saw a object take off at a great rate of speed..I looked up and then saw another object take off at a great rate of speed...me and my mother let out a gasp and started yelled ''did you see that?''..a few seconds later we noticed lights coming in above my home...it got as low as we could see the light changing colors from red, green to white..my son remarked ''that's circle shaped'' and i noticed it was as well..it made no noise as it passed over my house..and then we noticed a light come back over and descend south east and settle stationary in the sky..we noticed the triangle shaped lights were still there and another triangle shapes lights to our west...we all went inside explaining what we saw and as we went back outside 10min later, every light was gone..just like that..it was the most insane experience I have ever had..this happened of the course of 30min ...
