What I saw was a strange cloud like shape moving quickly from South to north just a few hundred feet above the houses. It seriously looked "angelic". It had a bit of a glow as if a cloud illuminated from the inside. Yet it was an extremely clear night. No clouds anywhere. The object quickly disappeared. I love to watch the night sky and spend a lot of time doing so and the fear I felt had never happened before this.
