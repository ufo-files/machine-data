{jpg} Saw a stationary silvery orb while flying from Sacramento to Las Vegas.

While flying from Sacramento to Las Vegas with a friend, she observed a bright silvery orb hovering in the sky. It was just before dusk. We flew past it and it seemed to be stationary. She took three pictures of the object which are included. You will see in the photos that it is clearly hovering above the ground. Could have been in the area of Nellis Air Force base or Area51.
