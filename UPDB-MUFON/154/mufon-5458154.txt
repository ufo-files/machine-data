{cak} Was heading North by the moon and made a left turn then teleported leaving a ring with a big clap sound

I was outside on the 4th of July and I was watching the fireworks with my dog.
When I saw 2 cigar shape both red and brilliant in color. Heading north by the moon. They were in line and following each other.
They made a left heading west for a few seconds then stopped and with a big explosion sound and leaving a ring around them they just disappeared. Both at the same time.
No more than 20 seconds later A jet plane heading South was curving towards the west at a very low altitude about 1000 feet with no noise and it was bigger than any commercial jet we have. And it just disappeared too.
After that I continued watching the fireworks with my dog.
