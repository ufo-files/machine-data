Objects were not moving fast - more like helicopters slowly following each other. I heard normal railroad and traffic noise for the area, but I could not discern any noise attributable to these objects. Best visual description I can come up with for initial sighting of each object is that of a Chinese Lantern - but large, and at a distance. Closest known object from personal experience would be drones with bright lights.
