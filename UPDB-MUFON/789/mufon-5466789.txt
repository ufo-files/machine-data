{jpg} Seen beams of light over mountain top moving in a pattern

The event happened around 12:30am on August 3rd. I live around Horsetooth Mountain. Around 12:15am 1 of my dogs started going nuts downstairs barking and growling, I went to look what was wrong, I didn't see anything in the yard or side of the house, while I was looking out the side window which faces south west I noticed what appeared to be like a search light in the sky. The beam of white light was rising up from behind a mountain top about 1000 feet higher then we are. Our altitude is around 6000 ft. The lights appeared to be coming from a small town of Masonville or in that general area. I've seen many search lights in my life but what made these different is that the light ended in a squared off feature like it was hitting a wall and stopping after about 1/2 of a mile. I could see that the light was more horizontal then vertical which to me meant whatever was emitting this light was elevated off of the ground. I've added a drawn picture of what I seen, the light started in position 1 took about 10 seconds to move to position 2, once it moved to position 2 it turned off and reappeared on position 3 I could see it continued to move but it went behind the mountain top after about 30 seconds it reappeared in position 1. this cycled continued for about 6 cycles. The whole time I was observing this happening my dog was continuing barking, I have 3 other dogs and none of them even woke up during this time. When the lights did stop shining in the sky I did not see anything or hear anything rising in the sky from where I witnessed this event. The other thing that occurred is once the lights stopped my dog didn't make another sound the rest of the night.
