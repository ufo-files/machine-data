unknown large object hovered over us and followed us

cousin and i were hanging out at froggys pizza and we saw a large almost round object move over us right over the building. it had a humming sound and flashing lights on it. don't remember getting home but remember standing in back yard watching it with my dad ,cousin and her parents while it hovered over us then shot straight up very fast and hovered for awhile then shot at an angle straight out of the sky and disappeared. the next day they reported it as a weather balloon. I know it was not the way it moved and followed us for at least 4 blocks.
