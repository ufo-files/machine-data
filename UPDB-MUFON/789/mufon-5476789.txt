{jpg} round, white orb

It was a cloudless, blue sky. I have a view of the Hood Canal and Olympic Mountain Range, and I was looking with my binoculars zoomed to maximum magnification, searching for birds in the direction of the mountains. Using only my naked eye, I noticed an airplane, traveling in a northwest to southeast direction, leaving a thick contrail, which persisted. Then it began to dissipate. I looked through my binoculars to see how quickly it was evaporating and noticed a white orb hovering next to it. Then it began moving erratically before hovering again. I put my binoculars down and discovered I could not see it with my unaided eye. I raised my binoculars again and located it and saw that it was still hovering. Seconds later, it accelerated at a very high rate of speed in a westerly direction and disappeared. I then spent fifteen minutes looking searching for it but could not locate it again. I didn't feel threatened or afraid, but at first disbelief then awe. I went to the house, which is up a hill from the observation deck to tell my husband. I then contacted a friend who I attend MUFON meetings with and she told me to fill out this form.
