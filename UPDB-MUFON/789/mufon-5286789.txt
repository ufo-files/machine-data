unknown metallic craft appeared, glitched and then disappeared

I was in front of my house and looked to the NE of my residence and observed a metallic object at 55 degrees NE of my location. The object appeared to be the size of a jetliner but had no wings and no con trail. The craft appeared to be traveling east from my location and appeared to be glitching, I reached for my phone and it appeared to begin to tumble and disappeared. I looked at my phone and it was 1131 and at this time and also saw a jetliner traveling west bound at approximately the same altitude east of the objects last location. At 1133 the jetliner was in the same approximately location where I last saw the object. The plane appeared smaller then the object I saw and I could clearly see the wings of the plane and it's con trail. I have no idea what the object I saw was.
