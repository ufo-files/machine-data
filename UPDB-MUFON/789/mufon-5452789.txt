Saucer shaped light flying in unison

Between 11.30pm and 2.30am from 15.05.2020 through to 16.05.2020.

Two saucer shaped lights zipped from WE to EW across the Arbroath sky together, as if attached. This happened numerous times until around 1am when a military jet flew passed. The objects later returned doing the same erratic moves until around 3am.

Saucer shaped bright white lights, movement looked like a shooting star zipping around. Phenomenal speed, unlike any normal aircraft.
