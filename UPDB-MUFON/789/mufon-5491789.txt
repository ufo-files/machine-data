{dr}very large double pyramid ufo and many sphere ufos over Breckenridge CO in fall 1994

My rock band Random Art Festival on tour in Colorado in '94 played an outdoor show at an abandoned copper mine in Breckenridge CO. We had a professional lighting setup with us and because the gig was outside at night and so high up in the mountains I predicted for two weeks before the gig that UFO's would show up. That night at around 3am - 4am multiple UFO's in formation were seen flying just overhead of the gig location. At least a hundred audience members and my 6 band members all witnessed this event. After finishing a song after playing for a few hours all who were in attendance started to point at the sky as a very very large double pyramidal UFO followed closely by a large spherical UFO both with much much smaller spherical UFO's which encircled both. 6-10 of the small sphere UFO's appeared to land upon the hillsides above the location where we were and across the valley. Tall alien figures like apparitions seemed to appear near to where we were.
