{jpg} Exceptionally bright orb floating south then light rolled upwards showing metallic silver orb

I was travelling south on 91st Street when I observed the object. It was exceptionally bright light travelling at a slow speed and low to the ground. The light rolled upwards and the object turned into a silver orb. Then it flew in the same direction and then vanished. 
UI was in my car at the time. I noticed it because it was bright and low to the ground. I was in awe of it and watched it for almost 3 minutes.
