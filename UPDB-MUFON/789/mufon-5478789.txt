{dr}AT GATE 6 MCGUIRE AF BASE, ENTITY CLIMBED OVER FENCE.AND THEN OVER SECOND FENCE

WHILE STANDING AT GATE 6 IN MCGUIRE AF BASE LOOING INTO THE WOODS AND AT A FENCE, A SMALL 4 FT HAIRY ENITIY WITH BIG EYES AND POSSIBLY NAKED CLIMBED SWIFTLY OVER A FENCE STOPPED LOOKED AT ME THEN PROCEEDED TO JUMP ANOTHER FENCE AND DIAAPPEAR. IT WAS HUMANOID BUT NOT HUMAN. LATER WHEN TLLING THE STORY TO ANOTHER MILITARY SERVICEMAN HE TOLD ME THAT THE BASE'S WATER TREATMENT PLANT OPERATOR SAW AN IDENTICAL CREATURE RECENTLY.
