Flashing light to the left of jupiter

Last night while preparing for a fishing trip, I noticed a light flash a single time, multiple times, just to jupiters left. About once every 5 minutes a single flash was seen for 2 hours until cloud cover obscured jupiter. The flash was half a pinky to jupiters left, with you're hand out stretched. It never moved and appeared to be very far away.
