Cigar shaped object appeared to observe Falcon 9 rocket in flight.

On 1/31/22, I was watching a Spacex Falcon 9 launch from my home. The launch time was 6:11 pm. The sky was clear and not cloudy The sun was setting to the west. The rocket trajectory was south, so the rocket was visible from my vantage point for about 7 minutes. I could actually see the stage separation. At approximately 6:15pm, seconds before the stage separation, a large cigar shaped object appeared to the south. It appeared to be just sitting there and not moving. the stage separation occurred and you could see the two staged in separate flight for about 20 seconds. Then the cigar shaped object vanished. The rocket was still in view for another 3 minutes after the separation. The object that seamed to be observing was present for about 2 minutes. I am sure it was stationary as I had a stationary object (tree) on my right that I affixed the object with. It never moved.
