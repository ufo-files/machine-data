Fireball like object slowly heading north over Orlando Fl.

At 7pm on Dec. 30th. 2011 we saw a large round red-orange fireball moving north at about 200mph at around 2,000 ft up heading north, at first we thought it was a plane on fire but after a while we realized it was not a plane. there was no sound or trail coming from the object and even with two of us using binocs. we could not make out any detail. we watched it for 4-5 mins. slowly head north and go into a cloudbank. It appeard to be around -20 magnatude with a appearant dia. of 1/2 to 1/3 dia. of the moon. looked like a red-orange fireball with flame like edges, the brightness stayed the same never dimming or brightining. we even saw several jet pass within a few miles of the object
