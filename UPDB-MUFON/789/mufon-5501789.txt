while visiting my brother at his home we were sitting in his driveway and i was facing NE when i happened to look up at the sky and noticed a object glittering in the sky next to some clouds. the object appeared to shimmer or pulsated while in a stationary position. It appeared to be round or circular in shape although it being high in the sky, the shape may have been a disc shape. the object stood stationary and shimmering then moved slightly to the right, returned to its original position when first observed. it then moved up then down and again stood still in its first observed position, it continued to shimmer or pulse the entire time. once again it moved to the right of the cloud then when an airplane i saw approaching at a much lower altitude come into my view the object abruptly moved to its left into the cloud and was lost to my view. the object was at a much higher altitude than the aircraft which was a commercial type and may have been coming from LaGuardia airport. the object appeared to be silver in color with no visible lighting or vapor trails. it was not obstructed in any way nor was my view of it. the event lasted approx. 1-2 minutes. the sky was clear except for a few clouds near to the object.
