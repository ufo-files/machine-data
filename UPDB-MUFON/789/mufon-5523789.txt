Three ufo's appeared over the city, for over an hour with movement and direction change

I left class at approximately 855pm and noticed 3 dots previously unseen in the sky, large groups of people were gathering to see them, they were star like in appearance, only larger. The continued to hover in place for some time almost right over the city, after 30 minutes or so I went inside, coming back out several minutes later. The ufos had moved across the sky, and up to 10 aircraft could be observed circling the sky. After a period of time they disappeared and the aircraft left.
