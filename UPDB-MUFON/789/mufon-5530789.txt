Large cigar shaped in sky about one third was lit up glowing orange, moving very slowly then disappeared.

We were walking to my stepsons condo on outskirts of Boulder, my friend and I both looked up at what we thought was a shooting star,but was barely moving when we saw it. you could make out shape of cigar by the orange ( harvest moon color) light that glowed from right 1/3 of UFO. we were looking east. It appeared to move very slowly then darted upward and disappeared. I have been very obsessed with the night sky at home in Washington after seeing this. I go over it in my mind and can find no rational answer to what we saw. I just wish I had had a camera.
