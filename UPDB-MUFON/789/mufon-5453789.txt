{cak} Dozens bright lights very high in sky

Many very bright lights flying across sky at extreme altitude. They were all in a straight trajectory like they were following each other. Dozens of them going higher and higher until they were out of sight. Very bright and many times faster than any jet or satellite could fly.
They were too close together and going up out of sight to be satellites which stay at a fixed altitude.
Could not possibly be jets because they moved across the sky going up WAY too fast to be any jet we have ever seen. We live near an airport and see all manner of military, commercial and private aircraft all the time. These lights in the sky were none of those.
I definitely would consider these bright glowing objects to be "UFOs".
There is zero chance that they were anything remotely like jets because of the speed and altitude they were traveling. They went from easily seen in the west to completely out of sight overhead in a matter of seconds!
