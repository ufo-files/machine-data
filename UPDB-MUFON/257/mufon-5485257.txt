{cak} 3 separate crafts.1 alone, then 2 together. Traveling fast, then stopped and circled each other. Then disappeared.

My husband was in the yard and saw the first ufo. It was large and fuzzy and glowing orange moving at a high rate of speed. He ran in to get me. I ran outside and saw a second UFO followed closely by a third. They glowed orange and were much larger than the stars. They were traveling very fast East to West. The third UFO pasted the second one half way across the sky. They seemed to stop and then they circled each other and then both vanished. My husband is a pilot and we are sure it was not a plane.
