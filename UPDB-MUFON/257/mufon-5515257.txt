Red/orange globes traveling at about the speed of a jet

Sitting in my backyard in Wayne Michigan, looking at a full moon. Suddenly cloud cover came over the moon. I observed several red/ orange orbs ~ 30 , exit the clouds flying in a straight line at about the speed of a jet air plane.
