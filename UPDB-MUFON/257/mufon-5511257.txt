{dr}Rectangle object fluttering by very fast.

On Wednesday, May 1st, I was sitting outside on my deck drinking tea and enjoying the nice weather. Sometimes if its a clear sky I like to sit outside and watch the satellites go by and stargaze. 

When I first saw the object I thought it was a bird. Only for a split second did I think that because this object was far too large to be a bird. It was rectangle in shape and it wobbled/fluttered almost like a flag does when its windy. There was a white glow to it and this sighting lasted about ten seconds. 

My feeling when I saw this was complete fear. I couldnt even watch to see how long I could see this object until it was out of view, I grabbed my tea and ran inside and tried to catch my breath. I became sick. I was shaky and my head felt weird.
