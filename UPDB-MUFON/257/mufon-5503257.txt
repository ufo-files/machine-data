{rjl} Bright light

I was outside with my dogs and was looking at the stars, I Heard an airplane and looked towards the East, where they normally fly when on approach to Cincinnati International Airport. After seeing that I noticed a bright light moving NE to SE. No strobes, nothing, just a bright white light. It traveled overhead of my house, as it did, it seemed to slow down a bit, speed up, slow, speed up, slow and continue on it's path, about 1000 feet up and no sound what so ever. I observed this for about. 3 min and then I lost it in the trees and I couldn't see it anymore.
