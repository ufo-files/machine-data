Dancing lights seen from Dewey, AZ.

At 9:07p.m. I got a call from my daughter in Dewey, AZ that they were witnessing lights similar to the Phoenix Lights incident. They said from their point they appeared to be over Prescott Valley, where I live. Looking towards their direction from my house I could see flashes from distant lightnening, but they said that the lightening was behind them and that it wasn't flashes, but sustained globes of light that were at first appearing and disappearing, then clustering, then began to "dance" around. At one point they said it became a triangle, then a straight line. Eventually, they moved off in different directions. After that she said it looked like "sparkles" and that she saw an airplane and could tell the difference. I stayed on the phone with my daughter for about a half an hour. She was with several other people and I spoke to them, too. What each said was pretty consistant among them. My daughter said she was so freaked out that she was shaking and she sounded legitimately upset.
