Flying silent triangle with blinking lights in middle

About 9:00 p.m. I was watching TV in my living room, directly in front of my French doors that face South. I had my blinds open at the time.
Out of nowhere a light caught my attention I turn to look and there's a flying triangle with blinking lights in the middle going south east to South along a flight path towards HIA I went outside to observe it, and it was absolutely silent. Normally drones and airplanes make a dull hum, and I see drones and airplanes all the time because I live by Indiantown Gap.
The shape was different from anything I've seen. I've seen reaper drones, assorted helicopters, c130s, warthogs, and its shape was completely different from anything I've ever witnessed.
