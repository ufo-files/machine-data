so we were on a house boat on lake shasta up the squaw creek arm ,3 couples sitting out talking and whatching the stars when i noticed a bright light,it looked like a helicopter hovering across the river up on the hill,i pointed it out and we thought they were doing night rescue then it seemed to move toward us and the light was slightly pulsing and when we all seemed to focus on it it shot to the left about 1 to 2 miles and stopped then it shot back to the right at 45 degree angle up about 1 mile then stopped again and then took off on the same angle out to space and left a trail about 300 yards long.we all turned to each other and agreed it was a ufo since no plane or helicopter can move that fast stopp and change direction like it did,this xmas we some some of the people again and we talked about it again and i then saw a show about mufon and thought i would report it even though it has been 10 years we all remember it like yesterday
