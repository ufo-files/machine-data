It was 1971 my father was called out because of a blow out of one of the oil wells. My mom told my father to take my brother and i. This was about 1 am. We were there about 2 hours once my fathers work was done we began to leave. Now this is about 15 miles off the main road on texaco,s property. All gravel roads so we come to a t in the road and before my father takes a left right in front of us over the tree line was thie enormous circular craft it had white lights blinking all around yet you could see the whole craft, it was moving so slow my father starting turning his brights on and off as my brother and i were screaming and jumping up and down, we just watched this craft as it slowly went over us. My dad then tore out speeding as fast as he could without wrecking the craft then hovered over us no matter how fast we went then disappeared. Three days later my grandpa took me to a lsu football game in death valley they were playing rice, when they went to punt everyone looked up and saw 4 or 5 craft the game was postponed as the whole stadium went crazy. Now the weirdest thing and why im now coming forward is because last year allmy siblings and my father got together, the first time in 17 years, and when I brought this up, neither my dad or brother remembered nothing, i no it was real.
