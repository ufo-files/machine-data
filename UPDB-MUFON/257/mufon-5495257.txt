MY BROTHER AND OUR BROTHER IN-LAW WERE FISHING AND NOTICED THAT THE SUN HAD A DARK CIRCLE AROUND IT. MY BROTHER TOOK SEVERAL PICTURES WITH HIS SAMSUNG GALAXY NOTE 4. THE NEXT DAY WHILE SHOWING ME I NOTICED A BLUE DOT SURROUNDED BY A MILKY WAY LIKE LAVENDER HAZE UNDER THE SUN. MY BROTHER ZOOMED IN ON DOT AND WE WERE BOTH AMAZED AT WHAT WE SAW. IT WAS A LIGHT BLUE CYLINDER WITH 7 WHITE MOONS SURROUNDING IT. SO THIS PAST SATURDAY I ACCOMPANIED THEM TO THE LOCATION AND WITH MY APPLE 6 I TOOK SEVERAL SHOTS AND PRODUCED THE SAME RESULTS. BUT THE LOCATION OF THE OBJECT WAS DIFFERENT TO THE SUN AND NOW ONLY 4 WHITE MOONS WERE PRESENT.
