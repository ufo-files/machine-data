Ball of light in night sky which quickly diminished into nothing

Came out of a Mall as we usually do at that time of night, looked to the SE as a large ball of light was "sitting" in the sky. If it was moving it would mean it was coming towards us because it did not appear to be traveling. We watched and commented how big and bright (gold/white)the light was. I could vaguely make out what looked like a "V-shaped" trail (whether it was vapor or actually part of the object, Im not sure). The other person with me thought they could see something "V" shaped as well.

After about 4 minutes (and it may have been there much longer before we actually saw it) the object all of a sudden diminished in size and disappeared. It did not move away, it just vanished (went from large to small to nothing). There were no clouds in the sky (night sky)so it did not become covered by a cloud. Once it disappeared it did not reappear.
