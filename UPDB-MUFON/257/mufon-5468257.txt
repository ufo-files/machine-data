White orb, no identifying lights, no flights tracked in area above within roughly 20 to 30 miles

I had been sitting inside. I went to my back door to get a little fresh air and observed a clear, bright night with plenty of moonlight. This was approx 23.15 on 28th May 2018. I looked up and to the west to see what I first thought was a bright star but quickly realised it was slowly moving west to east. Having seen shooting stars, comets and similar I observed it was not any of these and got binoculars to observe closer as I could see no lights as usually observed flashing on aircraft. I was unable to see any during any of the next few minutes before losing sight. I had a very excitable feeling as I have never observed this in a lot of nights doing similar and looking out during the night. I immediately checked UK flight paths and observed that nearest flight was way off headed South East, too far to be this craft and for me to have observed. I am very sure this was not a satellite but have not been able to check satellite paths that would match my observation times.
