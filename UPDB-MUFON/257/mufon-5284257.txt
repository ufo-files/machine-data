About 25 objects flew over reservoir in Newark, Delaware

On November 1st 2021 between the hours of 11:30am and 2:00pm multiple people witnessed tic tac shaped objects fly over the Newark reservoir located behind Timothy's restaurant and bar in Newark, Delaware. Local authorities were contacted by another witness to report the activity.
