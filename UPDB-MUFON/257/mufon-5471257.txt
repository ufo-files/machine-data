Bright red ball that seemed to be on fire as it skipped along the atmosphere

I was woken up by a noise and I felt compelled to look outside. I watched a glowing ball come from the West over my household and it looked to be skimming the atmosphere as it approached the eastern horizon it moved up and up into the sky. This has caused me to wake in the middle of night for a couple of weeks looking for it to happen again.
