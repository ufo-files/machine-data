{nd} bluish then red light, abrupt course change and fast speed, no sound

my stepdad was taking his dog out for the evening and called us out to see something "strange" we went outside and saw a bright blue light heading east, but it stopped dead, did a short 90 degree turn north, stopped dead again, changed color to red for a few moments, and continued on its path east, extremely fast speed changes, almost a jerky movement, no airplane can stop on a dime like that or start moving so quickly, no sound whatsoever!  very different color than aircraft landing or navigation lights. very beautiful bluish color, then red!
