{tjd} Bright light orb traveling at low altitude in and around mountain range could only be seen with starlight scope

The UFO was observed by members of the US Marine Scout Sniper Class during night shooting training on Camp Pendleton, CA. The rifles and other observation devices were equipped with Starlight technology that allows the user to observe objects and terrain at night. At some point during the training myself and other personnel using Starlight devices observed a bright glowing orb moving in and around the terrain in the far distance of a mountain range. It immediately caught the attention of everyone and interrupted the training. The strange thing was two fold. One, the object could only be seen with the Starlight device, with the naked eye the horizon was dark. Second, the object made no sound. The object could be seen moving slowly as it went into valleys or around hills where it would disappear then reappear. The object then stopped and started to rise up above the mountain horizon then in a flash shot up and out of site as if it was a shooting star going away from earth. The Gunnery Sgt. In charge of the training was extremely agitated and yelled this event did not happen and he was not going to submit paperwork about it. It was obvious we were not to discuss it with anyone.
