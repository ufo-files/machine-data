I was laying in my bed trying to sleep when I heard a low pitched hum coming from the north. I looked and I saw a disk like object with lights all over flashing at random intervals hovering in my direction. At first I thought it was a helicopter but as I took a closer look I noticed that this was no ordinary aircraft, I panicked and I looked up what I should do in these circumstances and then I reported it.
