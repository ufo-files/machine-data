Large triangle

I was sitting in my garden at the time the photos were taken which was 19:56 on 29-06-2019 uk time when i noticed a very prominent triangular shadow in the sky beyond the roof of my house and quite high. The sun was fairly high in the sky and set 1 hour and 37 minutes after the photo was taken. I live on the edge of a village and it is open countryside between my house and where the sun was. 
The shadow seems to have a serrated edge to the rear of the triangle. if you look over to the left away from the shadow there are a couple of orbs (it was to early and bright for stars to be visible) I don't remember how the shadow dispersed but I'm sure there was no missing time either.
The photos was taken on my Samsung J6 mobile phone to which I still use with the original photos on it.
