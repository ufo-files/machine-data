{cak} Saw pink meteor falling from sky. Shortly thereafter had an encounter with black delta wing with pulsating lights on the bottom of object.

I was working at a local tennis club and pool around 21:00. I had finished my work and was lounging around the pool waiting for the sprinklers to finish watering the clay tennis courts. I was facing East and observed, what appeared to be, a pink meteor falling from the Northeast to the East at about a 25 degree angle of descent. It was unusual because the color was the same pink with no variations in brightness. It fell below the horizon. Shortly thereafter I observed what I originally thought was a plane rising from the last spot I saw the meteor, the area is close to a regional airport. The object was coming directly towards the pool. The object appeared to be triangular. Since I wanted to get a closer look I scaled the large diving board. I got to the top of the board and noticed the black object was hovering. I also observed three lights pulsing on the bottom close to the corners of the object. At that point a rush of fear fell upon me and I ran down the ladder to the swimming pool and proceeded towards the pump room. As I looked back I observed the object vertically leave at a high rate of speed without any sound.
