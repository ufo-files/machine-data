{rjl} Columns and rows of lights where lights changed color

When I was in my early teens I had stayed up late and was trying to fall asleep in a recliner in the living room. I had the lights out as I was trying to fall asleep. I was looking out the window and I had seen what appeared to be one object with about 12 lights in like a 4x4 of columns and rows. The lights were changing color from green, yellow, orange, red. The lights were in unison. There was no sound as it flew over. The event didn’t last a long time and I wasn’t able to see it after stepping outside.

I was young at the time and didn’t know who to tell except my parents who brushed it off.
