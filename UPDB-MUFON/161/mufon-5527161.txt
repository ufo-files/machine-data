Stationary black disc sitting atop fluffy cloud.

I was in seat 4D, a window seat, on a flight from Salt Lake City, Utah to Redmond, Oregon. As we ascended to the skies I took advantage of my window seat and pressed my forehead, snug against the plastic triple pane windows of the small passenger jet. As we climbed the skies and rose above the clouds my hearts begins to race a bit. Nothing too weird. I write it off as elevation sickness. We move along, parralell to the clouds and I see a black dot out of the corner of my vision. I focus on the shadowy object and notice its disc-like. My goodness! Am I actually seeing a UFO. I stare at the mass, mouth open like a little kid. I say to myself its a plane. We just took off, it makes perfect sense. The disc doesnt change its position. I say to myself, if its a plane, it should be gone when I open my eyes. I close my eyes for three second and open them again. It is still there. Sitting atop the clouds. If my next-seat neighbor hadnt given me the aura of introverted recluse, I would have turned to her for confirmation. After the incident, I felt nauseous the whole flight.
