{cak} Observed flying disc while driving

I was 17 years old in the summer of 1985. I was riding my motorcycle with a friend of mine on Rt 18 north through the little Pennsylvania town of Wampum. We were each on our own bikes. He was traveling in front of me approx. 25-50 yards. It was a sunny clear sky day. It was in the afternoon, approx. 1500. We were starting to descend a small grade and a silver/grey, round, disc shaped object caught my attention and glided at tree top level over the road from east to west, as if slowing to land. I could not believe what I was actually seeing! It was not a quick movement or flight. The duration was long enough for me to get a good enough look at it to know that it was not an airplane or any other craft that I am familiar with.

The flight path was between me and my friend in front of me. After the craft was out of sight, I sped up and pulled my buddy over. I asked him if he just saw what I did, and he said no. I stated that I was disappointed because nobody would ever believe me. He did believe me but didn't see it. I did not notice any windows. There was no sound or any lights to be seen. 

The craft appeared to be approximately 20-30 feet in diameter (It was almost as wide as the two lane road that it was crossing). It had a tapered dome on top approximately 4-5 feet tall. The whole craft appeared to be smooth but not reflective like chrome. There is an old limestone mushroom mine to the west of Rt 18 in Wampum, PA. This is the same location where I had my sighting. The mine is currently used for public storage. It also houses a government/military facility. Supposedly to store documents. There is a sign stating there is no trespassing at one of the portals and that you can be arrested for being there. It is currently called [location removed] . I do not know if that is where the craft was going, but have always believed that it was even to this day. 

There have been stories over the years of a policeman in the 50's or 60's chasing a UFO on Rt 18 in the same area and reporting on it. The mine was built in the late 1800's for the cement industry. It was not open during the 1970's.
