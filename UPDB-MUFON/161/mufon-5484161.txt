{nd} FIRST OBSERVED WEST MOVING EAST, AT LOW ALT, NO SOUND. BECAME STATIONARY FOR SEVERAL MINUTES THEN BAGAIN MOVING NORTH

I WAS OUTSIDE ON BREAK, I WAS A SECURITY OFFICER. I NOTICED A OBJECT TO THE WEST OF MY LOCATION MOVING TOWARD ME. THERE WAS A BEAM OF LIGHT ORIGINATING FROM UNDERNEATH AND ON THE RIGHT SIDE IF THE OBJECT, AIMED 45% DOWN TOWARD THE NEIGHBORHOOD. I THEN NOTICED THE BEAM DID NOT REACH THE GROUND, IT STOPED APPROX HALF WAY TO THE HOUSE AND TREE TOPS. THE CLOSER IT GOT I NOTICED THERE WAS NO SOUND. IT CAME TO A COMPLETE STOP AND REMAINED MOTIONLESS (THE BEAM WAS NO LONGER SEEN), APPROX 300 FEET FROM ME AND APPROX 250 TO 350FEET IN THE AIR. A ER NURSE (RN) WALKED OUT FOR HIS BREAK, HE NOTICED IT AND EXCLAMED, "WHAT THE HELL IS THAT." AT WHICH TIME THREE OTHER RN'S (FLOOR NURSES) GOT UP OFF A BENCH SEAT AND BEGAIN TO OBSERVE IT ALSO. IT REMAINED MOTIONLESS FOR APPROX 6 MINUTES THEN WITHOUT PHYSICALY PIVIOTING IT BEGAIN MOVING NORTHWARD. I WOULD ESTIMATE THE SPEED AY 3 TO 5 MPH AT ALL TIMES IT WAS OBSERVED IN MOTION AND AGAIN SOUNDLESS. THE OBJECT WAS FLAT AT ITS BASE, SEEMED TO HAVE THREE DIFFERENT LEVELS TO THE BASE. FROM THERE, TRERE WERE THREE ARCHED STRUCTURES, THE OUTTER TWO SAME HEIGHTH WITH THE CENTER STRUCTURE TALLER. ABOVE THEM THERE APPEARED TO BE A SMALL BANK OF ATENNAS. THE OBJECT WAS A FLAT TERRICOTA COLOR. I MADE A SKETCH AND WROTE DOWN TIME. DATE, DISCRIPTION, ETC. MY GRANDSON HOWEVER COLORED ON AND CUT UP THAT PAGE YEARS AGO. I STILL REMEMBER WHAT IT LOOKED LIKE, AND THE NAME OF THE ER NURSE.
