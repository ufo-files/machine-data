{jpg} Light in the sky thought was a satellite

I was outside walking my dog when I saw a light in the sky which I thought was a satellite traveling from West to East. It was traveling the same speed as satellites I’ve seen in the past, but it was followed by more objects equal distance apart traveling in the same direction at the same speed. I tried to video but didn’t show on my phone. My neighbor Lonnie walked out of his house, he was on the phone, I asked him if he was seeing what I was seeing and he said what the hell are those. We watched them for approximately 5 mins and observed approximately 70 object traveling from West to East. No sound, no flashing and moving faster then normal airliners, same speed as satellites I’ve seen in the past. I have no idea what they were.
