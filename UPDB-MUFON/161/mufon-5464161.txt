{jpg} Weird rectangular object flies across the and disappeared

My friends and I were playing with a volleyball when it got knocked into a bush. I went to go grab it but then something compelled me to look up. Now it was late in the summer afternoon sometime around 3:00 and 4:00pm, when I saw this silver rectangular object with an orange almost flamelike streak, I quickly told my two friends to look up and they saw it too. One quickly ran inside to get a camera but when he did this, the craft started speeding up substantially to the point when my friend came back outside it was gone.
