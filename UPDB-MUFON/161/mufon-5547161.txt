Orange glistening ball of light

I was on the shore of Long Lake at the Steinke Resort fishing when I noticed a bright orange light come over the tree line. It made no noise and gave the effect like it was on fire, very slow moving as well. To me it seemed like a floating orange disco ball that traveled across the sky. I watched it for approximately 3 minutes as it got smaller and smaller and disappeared on the other side of the horizon. At first I thought it was somekind of firework since it was so close to the fourth of July, but its trajectory was to straight, and it was moving too slow and also gaining altitude the further it got away. I also thought helicopter but I was too close to it when it first appeared, and like I stated before it made no sound. My reaction was one of fear and awe mixed. I wanted it to come back so I could witness it again, but I was also scared that whatever it was might have known I seen it so well. I stayed glued to the sky the remainder of the night.
