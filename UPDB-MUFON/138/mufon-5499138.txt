monday night in avon, ohio- usually pretty boring. Went outside around 11 pm to smoke a cigarette, I always gaze up to the sky, when I did, I saw a bright light to the west. it was moving slowly upwards or I guess that would be eastward At first I thought it was the intn'l space station so I went in to grab binoculars for a better look. Was gone only a minute or less- when I came back out, it was gone so I figure it either blinked out or sped way up. At any rate it seemed really far away. Too bad I couldn't get a closer look. But one thing about it- it did appear in the same general area that I saw a group of u.f.o.'s one night with my son in the spring of this year.
