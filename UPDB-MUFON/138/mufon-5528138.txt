Bright White Stationary Light present around 45 minutes then it disappeared.

I was Looking out a east window at home last evening and saw a bright white light above the tree tops its position was around 4 oclock. This object did not belong there, at first I thought it was a plane but it did not move. No stars in sky that I saw at that time. Between 9pm to 9:45pm this light was stationary and did not move, I checked out the window every 10 minutes to see if it was still there and at 9:45 it had disappeared. No video or picture taken. Who knows what it was.
