Glowing Ball

We have been seeing these at least 100 times for over 10 years in the Central Oregon Coast where we live This was the first time we were able to get good footage of the event. This was shot with an iPhone 8+ and enlarged in Final Cut. No colorization, filtering or adulterations. Stabilization was done.

We have observed orange and red ones in the past. 

We have observed these in the day about 25% of the sightings. They look the same during the day.
