(GS) a friend and i were on our way to his house and cutting across the Northwest High School field.

I was going to a friends house by cutting through the high school when we saw a series of 6-7 randomly blinking lights. We were on or near the football field when we first saw the objects in the sky. We did not know what they were, surely not an airplane or helicopter. After a second three lights followed one another to make a line until they were out of sight. I was very intrigued and felt no fear.
