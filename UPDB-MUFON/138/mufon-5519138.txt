Ball of bright shimmering light flew slowly over head very low to ground from SE to NW.

I was sitting in the back yard at a table when I noticed the table top light up like the moon had suddenly gotten very bright. I looked up & it was directly over head flying very low. There were clouds in the sky & it was flying below the clouds. It looked like a ball of light about the size of a softball, that was shimmering with light. The light was yellowish orange to begin with but as it flew past very slowly, the light changed to red & then back to yellowish orange & off white. 
I grabbed my cell phone & took a video but I was so excited I couldnt get to the camcorder part of my phone quick enough & by the time I got the video started the UFO had moved on past me a bit. Being that I don't have a very high tech phone, the video is not the greatest but you can see what I saw on it. You can see it better by viewing it full screen. 
I have never seen anything like this before & have no reference for it was other than it had to have been a UFO!
I read on your website that someone from the OU campus had seen this on the same night. That is interesting because I am straight North & a little bit West of OU about 15 miles. Im sure they saw what I saw because it was coming to me from that direction & kept traveling North West. What they described was pretty much the same thing that I saw.
In the video it looks like it was bobbing around but it was not. The bobbing is from me trying to keep it on the thing as it left the area. I was too excited & a bit freaked out by it.
