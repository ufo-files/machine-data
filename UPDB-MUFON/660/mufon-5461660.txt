{tjd} 2 craft in chevron formation in front of mountain coming toward me and my uncle

The incident:.After attending an airshow in Cambridge NY, My uncle and I had gotten out of his car to go into my parents house. We were both in the driveway when we both saw 2 UFO's materialize out of blue sky. They moved toward us from the mountain gap between Woodford and Harmon Hill, They approached the house at approx. 35-50 miles/hr. and passed overhead at an approx. altitude of 600-700 feet AGL, then they de-materialized one behind the other.at the exact same spot in the sky, one behind the other directly overhead . This all took place in a matter of 10-20 seconds. Skies were blue with cumulus clouds at an approx altitude of 4500 feet MSL an altitude much higher then the craft. Both craft were cigar shaped and grey/white in color. I heard no sound.
