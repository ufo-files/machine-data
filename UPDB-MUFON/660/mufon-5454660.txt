{cak} Star-like orb object projecting a beam below it.

In northwest AZ, east of Holbrook, traveling west on interstate-40 the object remained at our 2 o’clock position at 35 degree elevation for most of our journey. We exited the freeway the object seemed to follow us changing direction keeping its distance. We arrived at our home shortly after and the star like object remained in the sky to north east near the freeway for hours. There seems to be projecting a beam emitting from below the object and becomes more visible it as passes through cloud cover. It pulses randomly at times but mostly remains steadily lit and the object sometime dims in brightness increases brightness at random. The object has been appearing in this part of the sky for the last two weeks since Feb 29 almost every night. I just happen to be driving home from town after hours.
