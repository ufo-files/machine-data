3 very high altitude ( star like ) objects

I went out to the balcony to get the cat in for the night, the cat was being awkward and would not come in, so i waited until it did.
As i waited it looked up at the stars, it was a very clear night, and many stars could be seen.
I noticed what looked like a star moving, the altitude was very high and the object was moving pretty fast, not as fast as a shooting star or a comet/meteor/satellite , but faster than the other various aircraft whose flight paths are over my flat.
it was bright like a distant star, and had no other navigation lights that I could see.
It passed south to north over my flat in a straight line.
A few minutes late another similar object passed over in exactly the same manner but more to the east, still traveling south to north.
I ran in and grabbed my laptop to check it was not a plane on flightradar24.com
it did not show up on the screen.
about 10 mins later another similar object flew ever in exactly the same manner.
I feel it worth mentioning that I noticed what looked like a powerful laser pointer coming from about a mile south of me pointing at each object as it went over, so I guess I am not the only one that noticed the objects.
This is not the 1st time I have seen these lights pass over from this location, which is why I thought I would make a report
