Large amount of orbs [30-50), from cloud level and higher

What I saw: we noticed that across the street there were a group of about five or six people all looking up in the sky pointing at first we didn’t pay any attention. Then we went back out side and they were still there still looking still pointing so we started looking. As we looked we finally saw what they were saying there were multiple and I would say probably 30 to 50 different orbs they were not in a straight line like star link. They were randomly spaced. They were in the East south east sky moving south east, as a whole. Most of The orbs were white or reflecting the sunlight and looked white but there were several that were also black. Most of them moved in a reasonably steady fashion. But others will change direction stop completely or stay in one spot for a while. They would also change speeds. They did have a sense of interacting with each other though. Once the sun was completely set they were not visible anymore.

What others noticed: Others (2people) noticed the same as me, with the addition of rods.

After we talked about it, it reminded me of the 15th century wood relief depicting rods and different colored rods fighting over Germany.

Plenty of time to take photos but with the size and distance, it was pointless.
