{rjl} Observed two separate objects moving in different directions

On this evening I was standing in my backyard preparing to view the Perseid meteor shower. While waiting and playing with my telescope I was able to see several aircraft flying in the sky. The aircraft all had the standard blinking lights that identify it as just that of an airplane. Several minutes later I happened to look into the sky facing east when I observed what looked like a white dot. The dot appeared to moving in a straight line at a constant speed from east towards the southeast. After several seconds the dot was out of site. A few seconds later I observed another dot roughly about the same height of where I saw the last one. This time the dot moved east to northeast and lasted a few seconds before I was unable to see it anymore. Each time I observed the dot it seemed to stay on a straight line and both moved about the same similar speed. 

Neither object had lights or blinking lights as that of an aircraft. Neither object had a tail, or flickered, or moved at the speed of meteors that I have observed in the past. The objects were moving slower than meteors, but faster than a satellite. Both of these objects looked very similar to the object I observed at my parents house back on July 9th 2019 (Ref: MUFON Report #101925, I was the second witness in this report).
