{rjl} A black, dull hovering object was seen circling around a certain place in San Bernardino county

I was working on my work inside my home when I wanted to check if it was overcast outside (it was common March to rain in Fontana). I look to the left and I see a hovering object shaped similarly to a blimp. Yet, there was something odd about this object. It had no reflection of light whatsoever, and was black and dull in color. It was located southward, and appeared to be circling very slowly around a place in San Bernardino county. It was very visible, so I decided to grab my phone and take pictures of it. I first I doubted it was a UFO since it occurred during the daytime, but as I watched it i began to suspect. It lasted for approximately 10 minutes, yet after seven I went to look for my telescope to take a closer look. After an unsuccessful attempt to locate it. I went back to check if the UFO was still there, but it was gone. Several hours later, I decided to report the incident
