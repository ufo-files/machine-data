{cak} saw an object appear to fly off the moon

To whom it may concern, On May 13, I was out in my backyard observing the sky, as I do everynight. I was looking at a full moon and witnessed an object which appeared to come off the moon at about three o'clock and went south towards the bottom of the moon and disappear at about 7. I saw the object fully lit up by the moon. I am not some crackpot or heavy drug user, I was not drinking that night either. I know that there has always been rumors of an alein base on the dark side of the moon, and based on what I saw the other night, it is possible. It was not a meteor or comet, because there was no tail to be seen.
