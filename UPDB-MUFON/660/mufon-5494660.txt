{rjl} 10 foot tall alien seen at night with headlights on it.

My brother, cousin and a friend of mine where driving in a back woods road. We had stopped and parked for an unknown amount of time. Suddenly I heard something moving outside of the car. Where we parked the woods were dense. You could hear something crunching sticks and leaves. I told my cousin, who was driving to turn down the stereo because I heard something outside. My brother, being a joker, said that he seen a hand reaching thru the window. I freaked out and closed the window a fast as I could. I instisted that there was something out there, but they just laughed. Afew minutes later we decided to leave and when we crested a hill in front of us, there in front of us was a creature approximately 10 feet tall. It's arms were at it's sides, could not see eyes, and it just stood there. It was about 50 feet in front of us. My cousin turned on his high beams, as we continued to watch it. We all got scared, and my cousin put the car in reverse and drove what seemed like 50 mph backwards, for about 1/4 of a mile. We got out there and went home. We dicussed what we had seen and agreed we seen the same thing. And yes, I said I told you so.
