{kt}Glowing lights

Was outside around a fire watching the sky for possible meteors. Saw a couple. I noticed two glowing lights flying across the sky, slower than a meteor and faster than a plane / satellite. They were the same size and moving at exactly the same speed flying perpendicular, one slightly in front of the other. Lost sight of it over the horizon. They made no sound, no blinking lights, left no trail. They came from the north and moved directly south. About 5 minutes later one light came back moving at the exact same speed, following the exact same path, except starting in the south headed north.
