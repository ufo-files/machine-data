{cak} Extremely bright objects in the sky near LAX

During the afternoon I was driving with my cousin on I-405 South, through West Los Angeles, and saw an extremely bright light in the sky, which i first thought was the reflection of a plane going by. it didn't appear to be moving, but sometimes planes don't either, depending on the angle form which you're viewing. It suddenly disappeared, which I thought was strange, given the clear conditions outside. About 10 seconds later it reappeared at the same spot, with the same intensity of light that it had before. It disappeared less than a minute later, and, I thought it reappeared in a slightly different spot (a little higher and to the left), but it was a separate one. the original became visible again, so now there were 2. Again, less than a minute later, they vanished (I am not sure at the same time or at different times, I looked away), and reappeared about 10 seconds later, but the one that was at the top left was now at the bottom right of the original. They remained in that position for no more than 30 more seconds before disappearing for the last time. The biggest reason I believe they are not air planes is that the airplanes that were in the sky before, and very soon after, were completely dark. there was no sun shining extremely brought off of the metal of the plane, and there was no plane that even resembled a giant ball of light, especially at this time of the day. also, they were hovering.
