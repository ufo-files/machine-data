fly over of chevron shaped ufo

My girlfriend and i had just come in and was sitting on the balcony having a cigarette, we were both looking up at the stars as it was a really clear night, we noticed a chevron shaped craft fly over really fast in a westerly direction. There was absolutely no sound (as i could tell) then seemed to disappear, there was what seemed like 8 dim orange like lights throughout the shape of the craft. Unfortunately it happened so fast i didn't have time even to reach for my camera which was in my pocket.
