UFO Sighting, as reported to [Link moved to FI section/cms/tg]

My wife and I saw an object hovering over a snow covered hayfield alongside Hwy 97 southbound in Prince George British Columbia. 
It was approximately 40 feet above the ground. 

It had very bright lights on the front, my wife described it as two panels of lights. It was v shaped like a roof. My wife and I were looking for a pole wondering who erected it, thinking it was actually a roof. There was no sound as we drove past it, so we knew it was not a helicopter or an airplane. 

We drove down to the next intersection and turned around. As we turned back toward the object, it took off to the north at a very high rate of speed and disappeared from our sight. To the northeast of the direction it was headed is the Prince George airport. It flew to the northwest, away from the airport. We drove around looking for it, but to no avail. 

We were both convinced it was an airplane until we were upon it so neither one of us were able to get a picture. When we turned around, we both grabbed our phones to take pics. That was when it took off. It hovered in the same spot for at least 5 minutes. 

That was how long I watched it not moving for. We are going to check the field out tomorrow, during daylight hours, to see if there is any sign of disturbance. Will post pictures of anything that looks out of place.
