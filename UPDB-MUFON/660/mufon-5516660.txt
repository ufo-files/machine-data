(GS) Glowing red-orange orb

I saw a red/orange glowing object - unlike Ive ever seen before. It definitely did not look like a place or helicopter. Those always fly over my apartment and I am fairly familiar what they look like in the dark sky. This object was over the lake, and I could see it from lollapalooza around 9:40 pm and 10:00 pm. I had a friend look at it, and she agreed she had never seen anything like it before. I watched it for about 10 mins and then it disappeared quickly into the distance. I had taken a picture of it in my phone (sorry cannot upload pic). Although I only saw one object, my phone shows 3 other dimmer objects in the distance around the orange orb. The dimmer object are of yellowish/green.
