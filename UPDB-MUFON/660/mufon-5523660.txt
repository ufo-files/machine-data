the bright light seem to move very fast

We left from my mother n laws house drove up the driveway. it was about 8:25pm oct 1 2011. my father n law was on a 4 wheeler right behind us. and I shut off the vehicle. we got out and i looked toward the Northwest, saw a bright light moving at a high rate of speed. the light seem to be moving in a linear motion across the sky. it made no noise. i got excited told my wife and father n law look up there in the sky. they saw the same thing. wife said it was a plane, and i said no its not a plane. i see planes often and was no plane. we continue to watch as the light seem to dissipate, (faded out). then i turned and asked what was that? I looked again the light appeared again. the light seem to decend down, kinda like a slow fall. the light again faded out and it was gone. I think that my wife and father n law was stunned. they had no explaination, but after the event i notice a aircraft, or maybe a chopper. not for sure i know that what ever the aircraft was it had a propeller sound, flew in the same direction as the object. it may have been maybe about 5-7 mins after the object was observed. but the experience was like shock and awe. but this is what we saw. and I know for a fact it was no airplane or helicopter. I witness lots of commercial airtraffic in my yard daily and also nightly.
