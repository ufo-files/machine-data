Flying green Orb

My friends and I were sitting around a fire in the back of my house. Around 12:15 am, me and two friends sitting by me caught a lime green object flying through the sky. The object was heading east in a flat path. It never turned or changed directions. We watched it go till we could not see it over the tree line. This object was moving faster then any aircraft I have seen. There was something weird about the way it was flying to, the only word we could use to describe it was "smooth."
