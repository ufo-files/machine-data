Star like objects flying over my house

Looking at the stars from my back door, saw a star like object (same colour size etc) move from above my house to the horizon, then when that was out of site another from the same point moved in the same direction but slightly zig zagging to the same point in the horizon as the other one. Sky was dark with many stars and light cloud. Earlier my husband and I saw a very bright light in the sky at dusk 09:05 it was larger than a star but smaller than the moon. It was stationary. 30mins later it was not there.
