{ck} Firey Orb, traveled at extreme rate from horizon to horizon

Just finished watching country club fireworks and walked with friends back to my nearby house. I saw a very bright orb appear in the southern horizon and observed it fly directly overhead before disapearing into the northern horizon. It was traveling very quickly and appeared to be on fire or giving off a firey trail. The object almost had a triangle or diamond shape, but was very distant. I know it was not a firework because of the distance traveled and I know it wasnt a plane because of the speed and color. It emmitted an almost flame like appearance that flickered a little but was a contant color. I know others had to see it, and my friends and I couldnt explain it.
