{rjl} Red and white paneled disc shaped object with white dome about a mile away

It was some time in the middle of school break as a child when i was 8 yrs old i remember looking out of the trailer window at a light i noticed that seemed to be fairly bright in the eastern sky just above the tree line ,upon closer inspection out the window i could make out a detailed discription of an object that looked like a ufo, it was disc shaped with a white dome on top the lower section was alternating in red and white panels my mom was watching M.A.S.H. on tv in the front of the trailer and it was about 7 pm. As i kept staring out the window time seemed to change all of a sudden.It was now 10 pm and my mom called from the back of the trailer to me asking to go to bed, the object was still there in the eastern sky then as i looked back to tell my mom about the object I glanced out the window but it was gone.I told her what i had seen but she said go to bed. 3 hrs had past in that time it was almost as if time stoped for me and I don't know what happend during those 3 hrs other than I was looking out the window.
