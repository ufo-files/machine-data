Multiple UFO

While driving home from 7-11 with my daughter and son in law we all noticed the air became absolutely still. We looked ahead and at the level of the power line less than 1/2 a block in front of us was a hovering dark copper/brown cigar shaped object hovering. I stopped and placed my vehicle in reverse and backed up a full block as we watched this object “float” soundlessly to the right of us as it floated away my son in-law looked slightly behind us and said there is something “firing out of something else” I took out my phone and attempted to begin video recording and accidentally took a photo before I began video tapping the remainder of this event. This photo which I looked at Weeks later shows an oddly round light blue lighter object that can only be seen in the video coming rapidly in and out of the screen once the video is slowed gram by frame and goes in out and across the screen in microseconds along with multiple other red flashing orbs.
