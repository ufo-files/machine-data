Small white object hovered over our house without moving for over an hour.

While watching F35 jets do test flights over our house near Hill Air Force base, we noticed a small white object hovering in place directly overhead. We continued to watch it for over an hour before we had to leave. The object did not move at all with the wind or for the approaching jets. The jets did about 3 passes of the object over that hour. We did not see the object come or go, it was just there. It was hard to judge the size or elevation of it. It was round and white in appearance. It made no sound and had no movement or trail.
