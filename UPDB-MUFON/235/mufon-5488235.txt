{cak} Star-like objects moving at various speeds and paths

At a park near Reeds Lake a little after 8 pm. Noticed a triangle of three "stars" about 70 degrees up in the South East sky. The "stars" suddenly changed brightness and the lower two moved quickly toward the tree line. I continued to see the objects moving about the sky for around ten minutes. I also observed two planes in the distance. The "star" objects moved much faster in the sky than the planes.
