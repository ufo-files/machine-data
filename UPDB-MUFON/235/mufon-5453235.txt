Red and green flashing light

I was having a cigarette out my window just before bed at 21:29 and out the corner of my eye i saw what started as a green light move very smoothly and accurately to the right and stop dead still and it started to flash red and green. At first i thought it could have been a police helicopter but there was no sound and this thing then moved left a bit and stoped dead again i watched it for a few minutes then it moved of left again and thats when it disappeared behind the house roof opposite mine. It was very strange and definitely somthing ive not seen before in my 41 years.
