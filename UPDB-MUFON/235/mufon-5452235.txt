WHILE OBSERVING VENUS & MOON SNOWBALL OBJECT APPEARED FOLLOWED BY 3 TRIANGLE SHAPED OBJECTS WITH LIGHTS AT EACH POINT

FILLED OUT AND FILED AT MUFON HQ BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR ON JUNE 11, 2020 FROM A PAPER REPORT SENT IN BY WITNESS TO MUFON HQ
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
As a lifelong amateur astronomer with over $100,000 invested over the years in telescopes and related equipment I this night decided to observe the matching crescents-the moon and planet Venus. It was dusk so viewing was excellent as I aimed my 3" apochromatic telescope at Venus 9about 30 power wide field) when I observed a snow ball-shaped object come across the field of view. It darted in and out of my field of view, so I grabbed y 10k 50 binoculars....found "dancing snowball" easily, it quickly darted out of view to me and about the same time a silvery triangle object drifted into view, it moved around in quick jerky motions, then instantly shot off to the NE, almost immediately it was replaced by 2 more "triangles" with lights at each point; they drifted around and were suddenly accompanied by an original triangle; after a minute or so drifting around, then "zipped off" in unison toward the NE....watched for a while, but no return...Venus & the moon awesome!!!
