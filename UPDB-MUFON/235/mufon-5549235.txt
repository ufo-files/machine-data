4 lights in the sky

I had just finished praying. And was about to check all the locks in the house which is a daily routine. It was 1:18 am. In brampton, Ontario < Canada. 4 fast lights in the sky in my backyard over the neighbors roof. very fast clashing lights that would circle each other and collide and then do it over and over again. It would form a circle and clash and then form a cirlce again. I called someone in my house to witness the event and we both watched for 20 minutes and then left the room and then 10 mintues later we called other family members and they were gone.
