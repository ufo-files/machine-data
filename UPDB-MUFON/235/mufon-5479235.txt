{nd} Hovering in sky, glowing then flickering lights, then no lights.

Driving eastbound on 364 between Bryan Rd and K. Looking out front windshield saw object flickering in distance. Size of object from my POV was smaller than my fingernail if held up against it for perspective. Light around object was yellowish white. Lights would pulsate/flicker than would be steady on. As I got past the object the light was off and it was black, by this time I was looking outside my driver's side window. It was very small (due to height in the sky). Something I noticed was a small white exhaust trail behind it, but again it didn't appear to be moving. Commercial planes were flying near it but in a different flight path, they were lower and did not appear to be approaching or noticing the object. By the time I got to Mid Rivers Mall Drive it was behind me and I could no longer locate. I don't know what this was, but it was something I have never seen before and cannot explain. I couldn't take a picture due to driving.
