{dr}10-13 orbs of light stationary in the sky.

10-13 Orbs of light all sat still in the sky. The majority vanished in thin air and 3 remained in a (sideways acute) triangular shape 1 red and 2 white. completely stationary until a jet-plane passed below them and they all vanished. These lights were far above the clouds. We stood there looking at them for about 15 minutes, the duration of the time they seemed to come in and out of visibility, yet remained in the same place. Pictures were taken but they didn't show up due to them being too far away.
