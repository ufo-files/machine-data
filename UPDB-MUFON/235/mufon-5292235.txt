Round white object zigzagging through night sky from east to west at satellite alt.

Was on a TDY to Incerlik, Turkey from Torrejon, Spain in Spring or Summer of 1981.
Was walking to the chow hall after completing a swing shift at approx. 2300 hrs when I saw a round white object zigzagging through the night sky from east to west at satellite height. Never told a soul.
