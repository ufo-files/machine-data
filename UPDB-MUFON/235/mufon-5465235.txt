{tjd} HOVERING UNIDENTIFIED CRAFT WITH BRIGHT ORB LIGHTS THAT FLEW IN AND OUT OF FORMATION

MYSELF AND TWO OF MY FRIENDS WERE WALKING TO A RESTAURANT WHEN WE SAW THE UNIDENTIFIED CRAFT IN THE AIR TO OUR RIGHT. THE OBJECTS APPEARED DIM AT FIRST AND WERE IN FORMATION THE DESCENDED AND FOUR BRIGHT LIGHTS APPEARED, THE BRIGHT ORB LIGHTS REMAIN IN A 45 DEGREE FORMATION FOR APPROXIMATELY 30 SECONDS BEFORE DETACHING THEMSELVES FROM THE MAIN CRAFT AND BEGAN FLYING IN FORMATION BOTH SIDE TO SIDE AND UP AND DOWN, THE WHITE LIGHTS APPEARED AND DISAPPEARED TO RED SEVERAL TIMES AND CHANGED ALTITUDE NUMEROUS TIMES INCLUDING MAKING COMPLETE 180 DEGREE ROTATIONS AND TURNS BEFORE REATTACHING BACK TOGETHER. THE LIGHTS THEN DETACHED A SECOND TIME AND WERE STACKED ONE ON TOP OF EACH OTHER AND BEGAN DESCENDING BEFORE EMANATING A BRIGHT FIREWORK TYPE OF LIGHT AND THEN VANISHING WITHOUT A TRACE INTO THE SANDIA MOUNTAINS.THE ENTIRE EVENT LASTED ABOUT 2 MINUTES AND 30 SECONDS. DURING THE EVENT, MYSELF AND THE WITNESSES WERE OVERWASHED WITH FEAR AND MENTAL DRAIN AND FELT AS IF WE WERE BEING WATCHED. THEIR WAS ALSO A HELICOPTER THAT WAS CIRCLING THE UNIDENTIFIED OBJECTS AND THE HELICOPTER ALSO VANISHED AND FLEW OUT OF SIGHT WHEN THE UNIDENTIFIED CRAFT DISAPPEARED FROM SIGHT.
