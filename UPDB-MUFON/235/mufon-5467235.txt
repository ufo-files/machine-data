{td} Witnessed a cylinder shape object w/ no propulsion or sound, no wings/no tail

Shortly after 7pm on Saturday, July 14th, 2018, my wife and I were sitting out back having coffee when I looked up and spotted a cylinder shaped object, very white and non-reflective. It did not make any noise and no trails. It was certainly low enough for if it were a plane it would have made sound. I observed there to be no tail wings and did not notice any markings on the belly of the craft or signs of landing gear. I thought maybe I saw two little wings on the side of each of the craft but after zooming in, they appear to be some sort of clear emissions like steam or something. Could obviously tell that they were not wings. To the best of my knowledge and experience watching planes in the sky, this craft did not appear to be flying to DIA (Denver International Airport), rather, its course was taking it northwest, away from the airport as it had come from the southwest. The object appeared to be floating more so than using propulsion. It seemed to me to be under intelligent control.
