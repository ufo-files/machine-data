Witnessed bright light move slowly over Flatts inlet.

My children and I were in the aquarium parking lot speaking to a family friend. We were standing facing the water. I happened to notice a bright light moving low and slowly overhead. I immediately stopped talking and looked up in amazement. Everyone noticed my reaction and looked too. It had a solid color similar to that of the sun but it wasn't hurtful to the eyes. It made no noise and continued on a straight path out towards the Sound.
