{rjl} bright object in the sky, stayed still for about 30 seconds and then disappeared.

On Tuesday April 12th 2014,in shepherd tx, I was driving my kids home from baseball practice. Just as I was turning into my neighborhood I noticed a kind of silver object in the sky, it was bright and kind of round in shape.. I thought at first that maybe it was the sun reflecting off of an airplane. I stopped on the side of the road of my neighborhood to get a better look because it just looked odd. I noticed that what ever the object was, was not moving. It was just staying in the same spot. I was watching dumbfounded for about 10 seconds and then it just disappeared. I have never seen anything like this before, I know it was not a plane. I was never much of a believer of ufo's, but I can't explain what I saw, but now I will take a little more time to look upward.
