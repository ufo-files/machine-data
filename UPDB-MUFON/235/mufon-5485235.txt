{cak} While doing a paper route at 4:30am Jan 17th 2013. Flash of light UFO spotted. In news next day

I was delivering news papers at 4:30-5am in Vacaville, CA. I was driving my car when a flash of light happened. I looked to my left and saw an object in the sky. I got out and tried to memorize every detail. After the event i drew it on a napkin and then painted it later. SO it was big. I mean skyscraper big. It was far away but because it was so big it looked quite close. It was missile like with four wings on each end. It was simply a giant object in the sky. The lights came after i watched for about ten seconds. It was a huge flash of light. It suddenly had huge streak of light pointing toward the sky and a smaller light at the tip. The light was jagged. it was sharp. going outward. You can see in the painting i made. And this is where it gets weird. It then fades away. I figure the thing mustve been going so fast its image stayed when it was already gone. It faded like an old lightbulb when you turn off the switch. That's my guess anyway. I was excited to hear on the radio later that morning others saw the light too. And i cut out a newpaper clipping. They said in the paper it was a meteor. But what i saw had a shape. The whole thing lasted about a minute.
