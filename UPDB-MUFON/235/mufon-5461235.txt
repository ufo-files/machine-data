{rjl} Round, shiny, like a star.

A little past sunset, just starting to get dark, brighter than twilight, I was looking out of my bathroom window to see if the stars had started to come out yet. Directly above my house, a bright light, it looked like a star. I made the remark out loud, "oh, What a pretty star, it's out a little early", Immediately after I said those words, it darted away like a bat -out-of -hell, headed E, jogged a turn to the SE and flew off into a cloud bank and was gone. It was no airplane, jet, or any other type of regular aircraft. It was moving way too fast. I can't say how long it hovered above my house, but that's what it was doing when I saw it. Funny thing is, when I saw it, I didn't have a feeling of dread or fear, I thought it was really neat. Over the course of my life I had seen other phenomena, only briefly, this was the longest and closest sighting I witnessed.
