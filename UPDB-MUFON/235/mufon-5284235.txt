several mysterious lights hovering and blinking hovering over the San Jacinto Mountains

This morning I was out walking my dog and noticed several strange lights blinking over the Mountains. At one point there were at least ten. I see them almost every day now, but usually its just one or two, and they usually are on top of the mountain ridge. This morning there were at least ten, and it lasted at least a half hour. I finally went back inside and grabbed my camera and took s couple short videos. I live near the hospital, so you can hear helicopters in the video, but the lights flashing on the mountain are much further away. This phenomena happens all the time and other residents I've talked to have seen them as well.
