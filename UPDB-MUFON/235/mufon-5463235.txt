{cak} boomerang shape heading toward my location then stationary

At around 7:25pm I took my dog out for a walk. I noticed what appeared to be heading NW toward my location at approximately 5000 ft. The wings had white lights along the front edges. It stopped moving and hovered. I also saw a small red light just above a mt. to my south moving in the direction of the object. I took the dog in and got binoculars for a better view. Through the binoculars it appeared to be worm like strings of multi colored lights, red,green,white and yellow. I called my wife and she saw the same thing. I then got my small telescope to get a better view. It then appeared to be a blurry round object with the same colors flashing. The object without binoculars or telescope appeared to be wing shaped with lights on the leading edges. The weather conditions were clear with temperatures around 10 degrees.
We went in to warm up and kept checking to see if was still visible cloud cover eventually obscured the object. Total sighting lasted over a half hour.
