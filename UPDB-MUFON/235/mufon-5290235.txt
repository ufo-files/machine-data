Lights in the sky over Lebanon Mo on 1-6-21 at around 6pm. Unknown

I live in Lebanon, I was so excited. It is the 3rd time I've seen strange things in the sky here, within the last 2yrs. One clear summer day last year I saw what appeared to be a single cloud shaped like a flying wing. As it moved across the sky it did not shift it's shape, like clouds do. It was moving at a study pace, I did not take a picture of it, I now wish I had. I looked away for a split second and then back up at it and it was gone. Then about 10:00pm about a month ago. I was coming home and saw what looked to be a triangle shape in the sky. It was as big as a house, it had different colored lights on the front and sides of it. Then they went off, and there where 3 lights on what appeared to be the corners. A bright white light and a red light and a cream colored light. It was moving slow, then just stopped in mid flight, just for a second.
Then it started moving again. I followed it for about 3 blocks. Then it come to a clearing, and I was going to try and get a picture of it, but it also disappeared. Now this sighting. All of the objects where moving from North to south, at pretty low altitude, except the cloud looking one. 

Also no sound with the cloud sighting. And no sound with the triangle one. But the picture one had a slight muffled sound that seemed to come on and then off again. No electrical problems from street lights, or buildings. And no animals around. The pictures I sent you do not show the lights I saw in the front of the object. I couldn't get out of the car fast enough. The lights in front where red, blue, green.
I used a LG. Stylo 5 phone with a 13MP/5 MP Wide Angle to take those pictures-
