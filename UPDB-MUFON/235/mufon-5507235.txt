{cak} Glowing ball of light that varied in color.

Got home from work about 7:45 and brought the dog out to walk it. I noticed a light that resembled a planet (as it was brighter than a star) and looked away. I then saw what appeared to be a shooting star that emitted a bright light and I looked up again and saw that the object was moving in a circular pattern. That when I ran inside and grabbed my phone to film it. I followed the object with my camera for about 16 seconds before it completely disappeared in a blink. We are in an area with heavy air traffic all hour of the day so its not unusual for see several planes in the air at once. This was no plane.
