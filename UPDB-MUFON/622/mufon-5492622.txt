My digital camera died while watching. I was shaking so bad that the video isn't very good, and it looks like a plane, but I can promise you THAT IT WAS NOT A PLANE. But you can hear me clicking zooming in and out but yet you can not hear a thing from this object. The reason why I started recording this was there were so many objects, I believe were helicopters coming from every direction. This object was too close for comfort and it was not a plane. Something strange is happening in the Dallas Sky at Night and just want to know what it is.
I have so many pictures and videos; I swear I am not crazy or delusional, something strange is happening, not sure if it is military or drones... but I have always been fascinated by meteorology and astronomy; and this is by far the most interesting, addicting and frightening Sky I have ever seen.
