{jpg} Black football with spotlights pointing down

I was standing up in the hills above the San Fernando Valley, looking down into the vally to the North, at about 8:00 pm. The area I was in was were all the fronts of the houses rested on the side of the street and the back of the houses were all supported by stilts. I saw a black football shape, smaller than the Goodyear blimp, slowly moving away from me, closer than 100-feet from the ground. The black football had multiple spotlights pointing down to the ground from its bottom, had no other lights, had no markings, and made no sound. This sighting happened during the spring or summer of 1982.
