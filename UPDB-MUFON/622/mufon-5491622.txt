{dr}I just saw the being and I Coulden't scream or move. It looked like a really short old man.

I WAS PREGNANT WITH MY SON IN MY FIRST TRIMESTER AND I HAD COMPLICATIONS SO THE DOCTOR TOLD ME I HAD TO HAVE BED REST FOR THE DURATION OF MY PREGNANCY. I WAS ACTUALLY BLEEDING. EVERY THING ELSE WAS NORMAL EXCEPT FOR I KEPT WAKING UP AND NOT BEING ABLE TO MOVE OR TALK. I WAS VERY TERRIFIED. IT HAPPENED FOR A FEW WEEKS BUT THE LAST TIME IT HAPPENED I SAW WHAT LOOKED A LITTLE OLD MAN ABOUT THREE FEET IN HIGHT SCOOTING ACROSS THE FLOOR. I COULDEN'T MOVE NOT A MUSCLE EXCEPT FOR MY EYES. ANOTHER WEIRD THING IS MY ROOM STAYS TOTALLY DARK AT ALL TIMES BUT FOR SOME RESON I COULD SEE HIM PERFECTLY IN THE LIGHT. HE LEFT AND THEN I WAS ABLE TO MOVE AGAIN. EVER SINCE I STOPPED BLEEDING AND CARRIED TO FULL TERM. I ALSO STARTED GETTING REALLY CURIOUS ABOUT ASTRO PHYSICS AND THAT WHOLE YEAR STARTED WATCHING ALOT OF THE SCIENCE CHANEL. MY SON IS TEN NOW BUT HE HAS HAD SIMULAR EXPERIENCES.
