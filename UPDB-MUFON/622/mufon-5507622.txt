Bright Light in the SW sky appeared to be towards Channahon IL. from my house in Mokena.. I watched it for 15 minutes as I sat by my front door. As I sat by my front door watching the object I gauged it with a roof line for movement. Its movement (if any) was extremly slow maybe drifting towards the right from where I was at. They came out dark but I lightened them up as much as I could. After 3 photos. The object started to desend below the roof tops to where I couldn1t see it anymore. The estimated size of the object I cant determine because of the distance between my observation point and the object itself. I can say with certainty that this was NOT a candle kite by no means.
