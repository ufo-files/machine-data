{jpg} Hovering disc

I was riding in the backseat of my parents car we were headed west on Utah avenue then turned north on highway 22 looked to the east and observed a hovering disc at the top of the grain elevator it was stationary rotating with looked like windows and lights illuminating from it all grain elevators leave lights on the very top of the elevator .it looked like it was staring at the lighted portion of elevator and just hovering.. Never said anything to my folks and continued north on the drive home.
