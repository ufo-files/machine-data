Golden Orb above Palm Coast, Florida ascended out of sight

Saturday Feb. 18th. 8:22 PM Directly above Palm Coast, Florida. 85 Degrees from the horizon, I witnessed a stationary, bright golden(or yellow)orb slightly larger than the largest visible star via the naked eye, above a nearby passing passenger airliner, ascend straight up until it was no longer visible, towards what seemed to be just a few degrees(2-5) North of what I believe to be the Orions Belt constellation from my viewpoint. The sighting lasted about 10 seconds.
