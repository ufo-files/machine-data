{nd} Lights appeared at tree-level then ascended in north direction gaining altitude then vanished shortly after

On Saturday July 25, 2015 at 10:30 PM local time. I and my wife were sitting in the front yard faceing the north talking when I noticed a red light above the trees to the north east of us. At first I thought it could be a very low flying airplane until I stood to get a better view. That's when a second orb appeared from the tree line. From where we were I could not make out a distance from these lights, but to guess I would say at least 2-5 miles away. Over the next 10 minutes we counted 18 red orb lights appear then rise toward the north. Several people on our street started noticing the lights as well and were gathering and commenting. Several people started saying "UFO's, oh my God we are seeing UFO's." I continued to watch as these objects rose slowly and seemed to "drift" toward the north as they gained altitude. Some would move a bit more quickly then slow down. They rose for several minutes then began to fade slightly then vanish. One by one they did this until all had risen well above the tree line then vanished. At no time did we hear any sound from the area. Earlier that day we saw 3 military drones fly over the same area. I first noticed them about 7-8PM local time as they flew from the north east to the south. I thought it odd that drones would fly here.
