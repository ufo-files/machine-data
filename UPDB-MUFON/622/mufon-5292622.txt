Very high in clear blue sky object first was noticed as a bright light - grandson who noticed it asked if it was Venus.

OBJECT HELD POSITION OR HOVERED AT AN ESTIMATED HEIGHT OF 1,500' AND THEN SLOWLY DRIFTED IN A NW DIRECTION.
THRU 50 X 100 BINOCULARS OBJECT LOOKED METALLIC IN COLOR AND EITHER HAD FLAT PANELS THAT REFLECTED THE SUN OR BRIGHT WHITE SQUARE LIGHTS THAT TURNED ON/OFF RANDOMLY.
BASIC SHAPE WAS CYLINDRICAL BUT IT APPEARED SQUARE WHERE THE LIGHTS APPEARED ON BOTTOM HALF OF CYLINDER WHICH ALSO HAD AN A MAST OR ANTENNA-LIKE APPENDAGE STICKING OUT OF ITS BOTTOM.

OBJECT APPEARED AGAIN ON AUG 3 AFTERNOON AT APPROX 5:15 AND BEHAVED IN A SIMILAR WAY AND I DID GET ANOTHER PHOTO THAT SHOWS SHAPE AND LIGHTS QUITE WELL WHEN PHOTO EDITING FILTERS APPLIED.
