(GS) 3 witnesses observed several objects moving South to North. Video included.

On Feb 10, 2013 @ approx. 7:30 pm, my mother called me to the bathroom window to look at something. I observed a glowing red-orange object moving from south to north. We were both sure this wasnt an airplane, and went out on the back porch for a better look. At this point I started recording with my smartphone. She went back in to get a friend of ours, and I moved into the back yard and kept filming. Six minutes of unedited footage are included, converted down from the original HD to SD due to file size.
