{dr}Like nothing I have ever witnessed!!

Camping at Lake Pomona Sept.21 at 9:36 pm when we saw a huge white moon like object in sky. It would Hoover then zip to left or right then it would zip straight up and went above us. It was not a drone or a plane. I am 100 percent sure of this.
