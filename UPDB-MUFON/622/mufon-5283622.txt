Red stationary light above skyline. Thought was a construction light. Accelerated into the horizon, disappeared.

It was a late evening, the sun was still barely up producing a orange-blue sky. I was in a car as the front passenger, coming up to some traffic lights to turn. Driver, witness 2, notices a red light in the sky extremely close to a building (perceived). I believed it was a light attached to a structure and told them this. We waited at the traffic lights and discussed as the light sat there. As the traffic light went green, this spherical orb moved slowly in a straight line path horizontally to my right. Unexpected, but I supposed that it was in fact attached to a crane or similar, and that this structure carrying this light was turning. Then however, as we began to turn at the traffic lights, this red sphere in the sky accelerated along this same path and moved at a speed greater than any aircraft I've seen, and disappeared from the sky.
