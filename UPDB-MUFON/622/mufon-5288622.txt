Three red/orange silent orbs passed overhead from NE to SW, roughly 2 minutes apart.

I was in the backyard after dark, listening to the Eastern Screech Owls that had taken up residence in our owl box. They fly in and out of the trees regularly after the sun goes down. I noticed a red/orange light coming from the NE. We live roughly 10 miles due west of AIBA and it's not unusual for aircraft to head inbound/outbound over South Austin. What was unusual was this object had none of the normal aircraft lighting, no blinking lights at all. There was no sound. When commercial or private aircraft fly over our house, we can hear it very clearly. The object seemed orange at the center and red around the edges, emitting a solid unwavering light. Elevation unknown. I watched it pass over the house heading SW until it faded out and disappeared. Two minutes later I saw another object, same coloring and lighting, coming from the same direction. I set my phone on a table to steady it and framed the video with our neighbors back porch light for size/reference. The moon was visible through the trees. I filmed the object as it passed through the frame, and watched it disappear the same as the first one. A third object appeared from the same direction and I ran inside to get my wife. She also witnessed the object move silently overhead and vanish after passing over our neighborhood. I checked a live flight tracker online to see if there were any aircraft on that trajectory and found none. Three flights had apparently been diverted north from their westbound departure from AIBA over downtown/South Austin at this time, away from where these objects had seemed to originate. I did not see any other objects that night, or since.
