Viewing experience for more than two hours and seen by nine people from the same family in various areas, without any doubt

While we were all walking towards a site, a dog fixed his gaze towards these lights and the owner of the dog reported the sighting. At first, we ignored it but basically because we hadn't even looked. As we watched, we all attended a display of maneuvers, movements, dances, impossible accelerations, disappearances of the lights and reappeared. We had time to notify relatives who were inside the house about 100 meters away. The little ones had anxiety attacks out of fear, but the adults enjoyed the experience to the fullest.
These lights seemed as if they were clouds and varied their intensity from more to less and from less to more. They went from being clouds to taking the increasing intensity of light. For a long time, we enjoyed sequential accelerations of the lights until they disappeared at a distant point to return at the same speed and stop at the same place where they were.
We even got to change places to improve visibility, which happened. In total, we were more than two hours. I could talk about these two hours for as many ... as my colleagues. For more than two hours, so many things happened. I just can't figure out why nobody else in the village saw it too (maybe somebody else saw the same but we didn't talk about this with anyone )
