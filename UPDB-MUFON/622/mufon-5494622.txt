{rjl} Not Your Daddy's UFOs

Here's a few photos I took on September 03, 2014, of some strange and unnatural looking clouds. It seems we all need to get over our infatuation with "flying metallic discs" in our thinking of what contemporary UFOs should look like. Over 65 years have passed since the Roswell and Aztec UFO crashes in the late 40s, and the "I Love Lucy" show is no longer a prime time staple on TV. I think it's safe to assume that our otherworldly visitors have not been sitting on their hands over this 65 year time span in regards to their own technological advances.
