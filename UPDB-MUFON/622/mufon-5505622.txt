{rjl} Observed many fireballs in the sky that appeared to be to me Chinese lanterns but learned of other sightings around the state. There appeared to be many!

I was at Universal Studios in LA when I looked up and saw one orange orb. I asked my friends what they thought it was because it had an unusual flight pattern, it hovered, ascended, and appeared unusual. I then stopped filming as I thought they were Chinese laterns as more and more started to appear. I would say 5 to 7 actually appeared and seemed to be coming from the nearby hills. I didn't understand what I was seeing at the time. The balls appeared to be spinning in the air or something. Finally someone drunk started jumping up and putting their hands in my video line which also motivated me to stop filming.
