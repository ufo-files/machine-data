{cak} 4 bright orange lights

My wife returned home from the grocery around 2045pm and I was walking out the front door to smoke. I noticed in the distance above the trees 4 bright, big fiery orange lights that seemed to either be rotating or lighting up in sequence. After all 4 were lit, it disappeared. Then it repeated the sequence a second time. I yelled to my wife to come to the door as I grabbed my video camera. By the time she got there (after the 2nd sequence) it was over and from the original position of the object I noticed there were dozens of star-like twinkling lights shooting off in all directions.
