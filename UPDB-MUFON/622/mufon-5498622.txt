TELEPATHIC COMMMUNICATION & VISUAL CONTACT 20 MTS AWAY

BEING DRIVEN HOME AT 4 AM BY A TAXI, JUST IN FRONT OF CUICUILCO RUINS, I SAW A CAR DRIVEN BY 2 OR 3 PALE GREEN 1.5 MTS HIGH BEINGS WITH MAO SWEATERS, 2 OF WHICH WHERE TURNING BACK TO US (20 MTS AWAY). THEY SEEMED TO HAVE TRANCED THE DRIVER TO SLOW THE SPEED & CHANGE LANES, WHILE PLANTING IN MY MIND AN ACCIDENT SCENE, WHICH INDEED HAPPENED 30 SECONDS LATER. THIS ENABLED ME TO REACT & COVER MYSELF TO RECEIVE AN 100 KM/HR REAR IMPACT & SURVIVE.
