Light traveling very fast across the sky then shot up at an angle and was out of sight in an instant.

I was traveling westbound on Montgomery Ave at about 9:30 pm in the evening at about 10500 E, between Oberlin and Dartmouth. Approximately 40 degrees up, about a quarter of the way from the top of my 2009 Subaru windshield, I noticed a very fast light moving from the N to the S in a horizontal path. I have never seen anything move that fast across the sky except a meteorite. I was assuming it was a meteorite and was watching for heat flash but as it got towards the left of my windshield, 15-20 degrees south of West it changed direction and instantly shot back towards the N but about 20 degree angle above the horizontal path it had been on and was out of my sight. I was coming back from delivering pizza and told my workmates who just looked at me and didn't say anything when I described it.
