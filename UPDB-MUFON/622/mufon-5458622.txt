Heading south about 10 mi outside of The Dalles, Or. I was a passenger in the sky and we saw an object that was stationary and it didn’t look like a plane. The driver of the car is a scientist and he said he didn’t know what it was but he doesn’t believe in ufos. He wouldn’t stop so I could take a video but I did take a photo from a moving car.
The road was winding so it was hard to tell which direction the object was moving but it appeared to be moving east to west. We watched it for a few minutes then I could only see it from the passenger side. Then it suddenly disappeared. The sky was pure blue and there were no clouds.
The attached photo is enlarged taken on an iPhone 7.
