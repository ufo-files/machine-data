Noticed a strange large hovering light in the sky at approx 12.30pm. Thought it was a hovering police helicopter as it was slightly above the altitude they would travel but could clearly see separation in distance between blue lights and red light and green lights all pulsating. 

After 30mins thought it strange that the same object had not moved and by the distance of the lights I should have been able to hear the chopper. 

Decided to walk outside and noticed no noise appart from a humming sound. Then noticed 3 other objects all hovering at equal distances from the first I observed. Roughly 1km apart from each other. 

Started recording intermittently at this point. Recording device was a Samsung Galaxy S20 Ultra.

When I eventually couldn't stay up any longer at 2,30am I went back outside to see if the other lights were still visible. There were then 7 observable objects instead of the original 4.

Attempted photo but was not so successful. Also attempted video. 

Upon going to sleep the lights were still hovering in place.
