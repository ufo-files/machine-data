SR71-esque shape traveling between earth and moon, gigantic, visible as passing in line of sight of full moon

Circa 2000 I was fishing on Cardinal Lake. By chance, I glanced at the full moon. A gigantic object, sleek and comparable to the SR71, was visible as it passed between my line of sight and the full moon. It must have been enormous as the object was large and had to pass between earth and the moon. I see large passenger jets all the time flying into ATL; this was on the scale of "super star destroyer" for you star wars fans. It was traveling swiftly in a linear path, hence the moment I caught a glance. No noise, no light, no clouds, nothing- just the full moon and a sleek gigantic SR71-esque UFO.
