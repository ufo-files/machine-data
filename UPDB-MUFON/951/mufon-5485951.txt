{cak} The disc shape was moving over a hill at sunrise. It would have been invisible if the sunlight hadn't been reflecting off of it.

It was the day before Christmas eve. I was spending the holiday with my sister in [Location Removed] Early that morning I got up. My sister came out to talk before her kids woke up. We were sitting on the sofa at sunrise and out over a hill maybe 500 feet high, I saw the sunlight reflecting off of something curved. I pointed it out to my sister and she saw it too. It moved slowly over the hill eastward until it disappeared behind the hill. It was a large disc shape. It reflected the sunlight pretty sharply. Otherwise it would have been invisible. The sunlight reflecting was revealing the shape of the object. Crescent around the exterior. I live near an airport and see jets and military jets all the time. This was moving slow which made me think it was a very large object. At least as big as a passenger airliner.
