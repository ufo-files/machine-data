{cak} 3-4 white lights appeared to be connected to an object moving very fast in the shape of a triangle or rhombus

It was a clear night around 23:00 and Saturn and mars were out so I thought I would take my telescope and look at them after about 15 minutes I got tired of looking so I thought I would look for shooting stars and satellites, then around 23:30 I saw 3-4 white lights, they all looked like satellites but they were all moving together, really fast, too fast to be satellites, and they were all flying in a triangle or a rhombus shape, not as if they were flying in a formation, but as if they were connected to an object in the shape of a triangle or rhombus, and it made no sound. Now I live right next to an Air Force Base and I get how people would think I just saw a military aircraft, but I'm an airplane type guy and I stay on top of what's coming in and going out and the weirdest thing we have is the f-35 and even it would make sound and not move so fast, and airplanes have red, green, and white lights, not all white ones. I want to say I also saw the silhouette of the object but I can't be sure, it moved across the sky so fast, by the time I saw it it had already made it's way 90 degrees above my head and I lost sight of it around 40-35 degrees I saw it for about 3-5 seconds, if it were flying at 4000 feet it would appear to be moving at 900 nm but like I said it was totally quiet so it had to be much higher than that, or some unknown technology that can break the sound barrier without making so much as a clap. Later after I saw it I saw sort of a streak of light that may have been a meteor but it was a really bright orange and seemed to curve as it went down around 30-25 degrees above the horizon I saw that around 23:45. So yeah that is what I saw I'll stay up tonight and see if I can get a photo or something if it comes back
