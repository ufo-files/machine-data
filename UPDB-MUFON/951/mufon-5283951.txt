Green orb morphing into a spaceship out of Independence Day

Was taking photos of this amazing sunset with my wife on Nov 22 at around 4pm and noticed this bright green orb hovering low in the sky towards west and above power lines. Orb was ascending higher in the sky and when it reached ~45 degrees altitude we saw to our amazement that the orb has morphed into what appears to be a spaceship, something out of the Independence Day, the one Will Smith piloted towards mothership, "the big tamale". High elongated dome in the middle with lower rounded wings profile all around, with a slit at the back between them, like a horseshoe. It's also resembling a little bit to Millennium Falcon, but upside down. No noise and no stream jet. Couldn't find this shape anywhere in the UFO shape database online. Grey/greenish in colour and estimating it to be about 250-300 ft across.
