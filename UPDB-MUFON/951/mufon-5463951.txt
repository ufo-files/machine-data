{cak} A cross - each end was a ball of light. All parts of the cross were the same size. It began to disintegrate as I took a photo of it. The photo showed no lights. Then it totally disintegrated in the sky.

I was washing dishes and looked out the kitchen window. I saw a cross in the northern sky - each end of the cross was a ball of light (so there were 4 balls of light). It wasn't a Christian cross - all parts of the cross were the same size. I told my husband to look at it and he did. I asked him, "Do you see a cross that has each point lit up?" He said he did. My thoughts were that someone upstairs (God? Aliens?) was telling me that my life was on the right track.
I took a photo of it with my cellphone. I looked back out the window and watched it disintegrate in the sky. The photo showed that it was starting to disintegrate when I took the photo. In the photo, the cross was faded and the lights were gone. 
The synchronicity of this was that this cross looked like the one I saw on Mysteries of the Museum on TV the day before. The program showed someone viewing a similar cross in the sky during the Civil War, but I don't think the cross on this show had lights on each point like the one I saw in person.
