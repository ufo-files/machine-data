4 to 5 yellowish/orange balls of light spinning over the trees across the street from our hotel in the distance.

I was on a float trip with my friend who is 15yrs old and her mom. We went outside about 9:00pm to look at the stars and my friends mom yelled "what is that!" We saw these bright balls of light spinning over the trees. There were 4 or five of them that we could see, the tree line was in the way. The lights were yellowish/orange, hovering, and spinning very fast.
