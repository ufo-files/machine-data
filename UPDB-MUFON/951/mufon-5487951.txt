4 bright white lights flying in formation in the dark of night

I was outside late one evening having a smoke and looking at the stars when from over to my right I could see 4 bright ball shaped lights flying in formation. My first thoughts were why the RAF would be flying this late so close to each other. They seemed quite a distance away and had passed over/near to a top US military observation base that's a couple of miles away. As they passed in front of me they changed positions. They were flying 2 at the front and 2 at the rear but then 1 at a time the ones at the front went to the rear in a diagonal move as if it went backwards even though they were still all flying forwards (this is when I knew they were UFOs). They all changed positions as they continued across the sky then all accelerated upwards and vanished towards space, not over the horizon. I was really shocked after I saw them and felt amazed at what I had witnessed almost it felt unbelievable. I ran in the house and told my gf (ex) what I had seen but she didn't believe it. A couple of days later I looked on the Internet and saw that other people had witnessed similar things that weekend. Even to this day I can not stand outside my house without feeling what I felt that night and often wonder if I will see them again and since that night I have felt different in my mood and how I look at things in our lives.
