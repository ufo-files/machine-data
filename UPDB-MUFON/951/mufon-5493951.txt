{cak} Orb like 3 balls of light pretty bright

On around Friday September 14,2007 I was at my friends house in Ocean Springs, Mississippi with 3 other friends and we were sitting outside on his back porch and around 2am we saw 3 orb like balls of glowing white lights all in a straight line and it moved in weird patterns for about 7mins and then it took off quite quickly heading west towards Biloxi, Mississippi which is where Keesler air force base is located but about 2mins after it took off 2 military jets flew by heading the way the orbs went. I wanted to report what we saw but I didn't think we would be taken serious or just written off as crazy.
