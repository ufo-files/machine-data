Lights triangle hovering over Saint-Laurent quarter

I was returning back home from my soccer practice on Friday night, Aug. 22, driving Hwy 13 south and about to turn on Hwy 40 west. At 10.45 pm I've noticed these three white lights disposed in an equilateral triangle shape towards my left, about north-west, with a blinking green light right in the middle. Place is close to the Dorval airport and also close but not exactly on the main flight path, which I'm very familiar with, and that made me think it could be an airplane. I couldn't see the object itself because of the dark, but only the display of lights, which seemed very unusual for an airplane. Plane navigation lights are green and red pulsating on the wings and only put the headlights on when landing. This object had white lights for corners, green pulsating light in the middle, and appeared to be in a static position. I was able to see the object for about 15 seconds, as I was alone in the car and had to keep my eyes on the road...couldn't stop either on the highway.
