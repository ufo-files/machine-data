My family was enjoying an evening dessert run at a coffee shop overlooking the city. A flying object slowly appeared in the sky over the city. Initially, I thought it was an airplane, but noticed the object was not flashing like a commercial airplane. All of a sudden, the object, which had been emitting a blue color, turned green. The object hovered in place, then moved horizontally left, hovered again, then moved horizontally right out of sight past the palm trees.
