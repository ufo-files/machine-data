UFO glowing orb.

My cousin and I were standing outside watching the stars as we usually do and I looked up towards the moon and there it was. It was moving really fast across the sky and went behind a tree and then disappeared. It made me and my cousin feel great because of what we just witnessed. Him and I never see too much other than satellites and about six to ten shooting stars a night.
