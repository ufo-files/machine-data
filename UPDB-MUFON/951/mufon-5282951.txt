Moving green orb as sky parts

My mother and I were driving home from my step fathers calling hours. Strange things were happening in the sky within a mile of my moms house. I pulled over three separate times within a matter of minutes because of what I was seeing. The gray skies parted in the middle the sun popped out where it hadn’t been before. At one point there was a large sphere beside the sun and I asked my mom how is it possible that the moon is right beside the sun like that. Within five minutes or less the clouds closed back up and everything went away. Once we got home I turned the live pictures into short videos. We saw green glowing orbs moving in each short video that we were not able to see with the naked eye.
