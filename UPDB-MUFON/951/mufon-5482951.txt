{cak} Orb moving from Mt. Adams area into Oregon or Idaho. Viewed from Yakima Valley around 10pm Pacific time.

I live in the Yakima Valley and saw this bright as the moon orb from the West. West of where I'm at is Mt. Adams. It swooped into the valley and got really bright, then swooped out and got dimmer. As it was leaving a plane from the Prosser Airport could be seen ascending and moving in a northwest direction. Approximate time as 10pm, or 1015pm, as I was taking my dog out. The orb continued into the southeast, towards Oregon or Idaho. It was BIG, then got dim and disappeared. My mother, my sister, and I saw it. I saw the whole thing, they saw it depart and disappear.
