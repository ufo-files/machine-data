Bright white erratically moving light at twilight.

A group of friends were sitting in our yard for a Bible study. The meeting ran late into twilight. 

I looked up and saw a bright white light high in the sky. It was about as bright as Jupiter (visible to the south). Initially it was moving south. I had my iPad open so I called up the GoSatWatch app. It showed no visible satellites in that vicinity. I watch the light some more and it came to a stop. It then started moving to the east, then stopped again. Over the next 5-6 minutes it moved in an erratic pattern of moving then stopping, then moving in a different direction. Usually with 90 degree changes in directions. The light was about as big and bright as Jupiter or the ISS. It was steady. It appeared to be at a very high altitude, at least as high as airliners. Possibly at orbital altitude. The object was off now off to the SW. I has sent my wife to bring out the binoculars and my NVGs. In the 7x50 binoculars it still looked like a point light source. I took the NVGs to turn them on and get them set up. When I looked back up, the object was gone. It had either moved off to the west, obscured by the roof, of turned off.
