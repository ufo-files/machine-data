{cak} While driving west on Gulf to Bay Blvd saw very bright light in sky

While driving with wife, west on Gulf to Bay Blvd, observed a very fast bright white object, about 1/2 the size of a full moon, with green halo and small tail fly in a straight path from east to west. The green glow increased in intensity as we watched it fly out of sight. It was faster than a jet plane and made no sound.
