Woke by bright light and spinning star in sky!

I was awaken by a beam of light and looked at my phone it was 12:10 am August 21, 2020. I looked out and saw what look like a bright star but I thought thats weird I’ve never seen a star that bright and the colors kept changing. I thought maybe it’s a drone looking at Ariel coverage of Cal fires but it did not move, so I went outside thinking maybe it’s a helicopter but when I got outside it was dead quiet and the entity was just hovering somewhat high in sky and yet visible with the lights changing color and not moving outside of twisting left and right. I sat and just watched... it felt peaceful and a tad benevolent. It did not occur to me take a photo although now I could shoot myself for not. Then all of a sudden it just disappeared, vanished!
