{dr}Ball of fire in the sky

While driving I noticed something burning in the night sky. I pointed it to my wife who jumped out and took a picture with her phone. Upon examining the photo we enlarged it and what we saw in the picture was not a human craft. As we watched the object took off straight up into the night sky until it disappeared from sight.
