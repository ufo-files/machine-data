Spherical highly reflective stationary object in a clear blue sky

My girl friend and I were in my garden playing chess when I heard the unmistakable sound of a hot air balloon burner, I jumped up to get a better view, and saw a yellow hot air balloon with advertising logos on its flanks, travelling NNW to SSE (I didn't have a camera with me). What surprised me was that despite two long bursts of the burner, the balloon seemed to be losing altitude fairly rapidly towards the M61 motorway, which is about 1 mile from my property. The pilot had two make further long bursts to regain altitude, and it continued its trajectory to the SSE towards Chorley, at an altitude of approximately 500ft. and disappeared from my view.

With the excitement over, (or so I thought) we resumed our game of chess, but with my attention already drawn to the sky, I looked back to the heavens between moves, to see if any more balloons would pass by. Then I saw a highly reflective stationary spherical object to the N. I exclaimed to my friend that there was something in the sky, and continued to observe it for another 5 seconds, then it just disappeared before my eyes. I didn't perceive any movement - it just disappeared.

I do not believe in coincidences, and I'm wondering if the balloons' rapid loss of altitude had something to do with the presence of the object? The pilot would certainly not intentionally descend near a busy motorway in a highly built up area.
