{rjl} Red Orange Light in Sky

first noticed the object through a sky light from inside our family room as we were watching TV. being near an airport we frequently see planes and helicopters in the sky but this light behaved quite different. the object moved in a straight line path to the north north east but wavered and varied its speed ever so slightly. after moving out onto the deck there was no noticable engine sound or flashing lights. my wife was able to snap a few camera shots with a point and shoot. The object seemed to have a "glow" or slight aura to it not a crisp light like an aircraft would have. i ran back in to try and get a video of it and lost sight of it by the time i got back on the deck. we viewed the object for about 5 mins, maybe less. there is obviously some camera shake in the photos due to the low light and cold temperatures but compared to the stars in the back ground the object seems to be moving in an odd pattern.
