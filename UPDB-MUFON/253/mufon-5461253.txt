{rjl} Silver object flying.

Sitting on my front porch for over an hour around 5:57pm CDT. Sunny conditions and clear. Other airliners in the area at time of sighting. Did not have to look hard to see this silver object heading away from my location to the southwest. Did not leave exhaust or contrail. I estimate the object to be about 10,000 - 12,000 AGL. No noise and did not see any fixed wings to object. I saw it pulse silver color then vanish and then turn silver again. Flight path was slow and straight. Was not as high as other airliners in the same area. I estimate the length of craft to be around 30 - 50 feet even though I was several miles away from it. I have several photos taken with my phone. I could see it with the naked eye however.
