{rjl} saw object similar to a partial moon flying towards the east then turned and went south and upward.

Just got home from work and went out o patio when I saw an object flying in an eastward direction. Often I see planes taking off from or preparing to land at Ontario Airport but this object looked a little odd. At first I thought that a planes landing lights were reflecting off clouds but realized there wasn't a cloud in the sky. So I watched it. The object was shaped like a partial moon or a circle which was mostly covered. There was absolutely no noise either. It appeared to change direction and turned south and upward then suddenly vanished. This took place all within 20 - 30 seconds. From my patio jet liners seem approximately the size of a dime but this thing appeared to be farther away and the size of a golf ball. This thing didn't look like anything I have ever seen before. Curious if anyone else saw what I saw.
