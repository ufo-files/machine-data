Hello,

I'm from Boston MA. Since I was sixteen I have been searching for my peers 
reports of a strange happening on August 15th 1976. There were at least 60 
mostly kids who saw a similar object to many of your stories. My neighbor 
knocked on our door and said come quick, look at this!

We all saw a typical flying disc much like that of the 60's show with Will 
Robinson. Many colors seeming to spin around the edges of the craft. RGB red, 
green, blue rotating around the edges.
I am the 1st to believe UFO'S are just misunderstood military excersizes, an 
explanation for everything! After this night unless our government is up to some nasty business I was a convert,
It Was Real!

It also behaved as many have reported, it hovered over the old Boston Edison 
Plant on 1st street and O street. A trucking route for "White Fuel" corp in the late 70's I was so intrigued I took my instamatic 26 cube flash camera to take 
pictures, I could not get close enough. It darted within seconds without a sound 
behind the shipping channel where the container ships dropped off their 
Loads behind " Castle Island" out to sea.

I had the presence of mind to call Logan Airport to ask if the tower had seen 
anything? I also called pease AFB all to no avail, they acted like who the hell 
are you kid?

I wrote about it in my English class in September of that year, my teacher gave 
me an "A" and said I have a fantastic imagination. I took my lumps, but I often wondered why my peers never spoke about it, we all saw it over 60 people including 2 Boston City police officers. They looked at each other got back in their cruiser and drove off, I know they did not say a word about it to their commander.

Have you heard anything about it? I still have my English graded paper from 
September 1976.

What say you? Was it a test plane for the stealth bomber? We would be quick to 
laugh at our Midwestern counterparts saying they are so dumb anything could be a UFO to them, but maybe they were not so dumb after all!
