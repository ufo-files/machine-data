{td} Hovering craft that lit up with a sequence of lights.

Age 8. Was awoken from sleep while staying with grand parents to my uncle (age 14) waving me to the bay window. Outside about 25 feet above the ground was a craft of some sort hovering. No sound except that of a series of lights that sounded like pops when they lit up.The lights seemed to light up in sequence and repeat. I hosestly dont remember much after observing the sequence and acknowledging to my uncle it seemed like a pattern. I asked him what it was. He said "aliens"? Years went by and I kinda convinced myself it was a dream. Have spoken with my uncle at reunions and such nowadays and to my surprise he confirmed that happened. And the way he acts about it tells me my memories may be pretty accurate.
