{dr}flickering redish/orange and white light, stationary then move upward very fast then drop back to original position

On 1/4/13 between 0400-0430 hours, I let one of my dogs out of the rear door so it could go to the bathroom. While standing on the back steps I noticed an object just above the horizon off to the southwest. The object described as an orb or round bright light caught my attention due to the colors it was emitting, orange/red and white, constantly changing. The object appeared to be over the Potomac River (south ) as far as I could estimate and appeared to be wobbling slightly or it was just the effects of the light it was giving off. The object was stationary except on several occasions it dropped slightly then ascending slightly however, at a high rate of speed. The object was just above some lights in the distance and one of the lights being a red beacon on the top of a tower that I am familiar with. The object was higher than the beacon and continued the above-described actions with no sound, at 0400 it is very quiet. 

I have never witnessed anything like this before and woke my wife up. I had her look in the direction of the object and tell me what she observed, she described the same thing I had been watching for 15-20 minutes. I was very curious and checked the internet to see if anyone else reported any such activity in the area and found nothing. I can’t explain or confirm what I observed however, I have never seen anything like that before. I am currently researching the internet for any phenomenon’s relating to stars or the atmosphere that will explain what I observed and have not found anything to this point. The object slowly lost its color and brightness and could no longer be seen. The sky was very clear, and no clouds or obstructions were present that would have obscured my view.
