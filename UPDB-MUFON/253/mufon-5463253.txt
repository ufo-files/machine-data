{cak} Sky became pitch dark at noon. No weather causing events. Lasted until late in afternoon

I was a young child approx 10 years of age in the early 50,s. Living in a small rural town in Pennsylvania. Suddenly the sky became dark well before noon. No thunder storms or other weather events. My mother and people living in the village became frightened. It was really dark like 10pm at night. Some people ran and hid in the Indian caves. The darkness latested until 4pm in the afternoon. It was explained away the next day as something the
