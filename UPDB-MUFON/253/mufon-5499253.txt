{cak} Driving on I-95 S in Fort Lauderdale, took picture of double rainbow, then spotted UFO

I was driving on I-95 S on Monday, September 15th at around 10:30 AM, when I noticed a double rainbow in the sky. I got out my smartphone to photograph the rainbow, and took four pictures in succession. On the fourth photograph I captured what appears to be a UFO, flying really fast next to the rainbow. As quickly as it appeared, it was gone.
