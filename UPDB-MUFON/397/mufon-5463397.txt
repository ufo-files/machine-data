{cak} The disc shaped metallic craft was seen by my entire family and neighbors when I was around 9 years old while standing on our back porch

When I was about nine years old (summer 1974), myself and my four other siblings had just come inside for the evening, around 9:30 or so. My father was out on the back second story deck having a beer when he told all of us and my Mother to come and look at this. We all hurried out onto the back deck and looked up in amazement to see a silver metallic disk shaped craft with a glowing white dome on the bottom and differently colored lights sequentially blinking and hovering just above our small neighborhood. Quickly one of my parents start calling all of the neighbors to tell them to look up in the sky. The UFO was large in diameter (approx. 100 feet across) and I recall the the craft was making a low whirring noise as it slightly tilted from side to side while hovering. The white glowing dome at the bottom was very bright and the exterior colored lights outlined and pronounced the disk shape. My older brother ran inside to grab a flashlight to try and signal the craft to no avail. As my family and many neighbors watched the craft for fifteen minutes or more, the craft sped away in a zig-zap pattern and was gone in an instant. Since this sighting I have never forgotten what my neighborhood had seen. My family and I talk about our sighting often.
