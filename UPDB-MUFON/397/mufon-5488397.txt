REPORTED AND FILLED OUT REPORT WHILE ON THE PHONE, AT MUFON HQ, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR, L. FLECHTNER 18809. 10/07/2015
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
WITNESS WAS OUTSIDE, LOOKING UP, WORKING ON A SHED. WHEN, IN HIS LINE OF VISION, HE SAW A SILVERY DISC AND A BRIGHT, FLAT APPEARING REDDISH/ORANGE ORB COME DOWN FROM THE EASTERN SKY. THEY DESCENDED TOGETHER AND THEN HOVERED AND THEN MOVED TOGETHER TOWARD WITNESSES RIGHT, WEST. WITNESS CALLED HIS NEIGHBOR, WHO SAW THE CRAFT AND GOT SCARED AND RAN INSIDE. WITNESS ALSO CALLED HIS DAUGHTER AND THEY BOTH WATCHED THE CRAFT. THEN TWO MORE SILVERY DISCS, LIKE THE FIRST ONE, CANE DOWN AND HOVERED AND ALSO MOVED TO WITNESS RIGHT, TOWARD THE WEST. WITNESS SAYS THE SILVERY CRAFT HAD A DISTORSION AROUND THEM, MAYBE ENERGY. ALL THE CRAFT WERE VERY HIGH IN THE SKY.
WITNESS LIVES UNDER THE FLIGHT PATH OF AN AIRPORT AND SEES AIRCRAFT ALL THE TIME. THIS WAS NOT A REGULAR AIRCRAFT.
