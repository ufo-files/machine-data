Flying craft observed for many miles. Bright green lights.

I observed a craft with bright green lights flying from the west over lake Champlain. At first it looked like possibly an airplane. Flying straight toward my area. It was miles away at first but it’s flight pattern was strange. (Almost angular). It’s speed about that of a modern aircraft. When it approached closer over the western part of town I could clearly see the bright green windows on top of a circular ish craft. Cold black almost like it eat the light. It was blacker then the night sky. At this point I ran to the back door and Hollered to my wife to grab my phone! I had taken my eye off for a moment when I turned back to it. It was slightly nw and not more then 500 feet from me. I now was almost underneath it. I saw a bright green circle of rings underneath its black shape. It seem to stop momentarily. The green circle turned red with a strange hue. And it shot straight up. Almost seaming to skip in time. It went from 500’ to in the outer stars in less then a second. With the red hue clearly visible. Then it was deep in space and gone.
