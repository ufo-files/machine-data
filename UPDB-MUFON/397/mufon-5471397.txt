I STEPPED OUTSIDE AND I LOOKED UP AT THE DOME ON TOP OF CITY HALL AS I ALWAYS DO AND LOOKING DOWN AT CITY HALL WAS A HUGE FACE WITH PERFECT SYMMETRY. ON CLOUDY DAYS THESE CRAFT CAN BE RIGHT ABOVE A BUILDING AND HIDE! I FELT A CONNECTION WITH SOMETHING, THE EYES LOOKED LIKE TWO SMALLER DISKS STICKING OUT. FOR SOME REASON AFTER MY COMA I CAN SEE THEM. THEY ARE HERE A LOT. I HAVE ALSO DISCOVERED SOME THINGS THAT WOULD ABSOLUTELY AMAZE YOU BUT THIS BOX IS NOT FOR MY PERSONAL STORY SO ANYHOW I CAN LOOK UP AT THE SKY AND IF THEIR IS WATER VAPOR UP ABOVE I CAN SEE THE IMAGERY. I KNOW THERE IS A LOT OF STIGMA CONCERNING THESE DISK SHAPE CRAFT WITH THE TOP OF THE CRAFT HAS TUBES STICKING OUT IN CERTAIN DIRECTIONS, ONE TUBE IS LONG ENOUGH TO REACH THE EDGE OF THE CRAFT AND THE OTHER THREE TUBES ARE SHORT. THESE PUMP OUT CLOUDY VAPOR AND THEY CAN AND DO MAKE ART IN OUR SKIES! OH AND I HAVE COLOR PHOTOS OF THERE HANDIWORK ON OTHER BODIES IN THE SOLAR SYSTEM.
