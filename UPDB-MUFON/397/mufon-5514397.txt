My brother and I were coming down from an overlook near Casar, NC. When we got the the end of the dirt road which connected to the road we both saw about tree top level (pretty close, id say 2-4 water towers high) HUGE blinking orange/yellow/red lights. four in a straight row. as we both said "oh my god do you see that" it disappeared. We hurridly got onto the road and around a curve so we could see the skyline better and it was over another county (you can see all the way from Cleveland, to Lincolton, to Hickory, and Charlotte from this overlook) looked to be over Charlotte, this was only within a few seconds. Enough time to get around a curve and was much smaller (obviously because it was further away) We both picked a focal point of where it was (there was a flashing tower near it) and when we came around another curve and spotted the tower, the object was gone. Whatever it was was HUGE.
