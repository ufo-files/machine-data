{cak} Dull triangle. Dull with lights. Fairly low

On April 3, 2013 at approximately 5:55AM I was loading my pick up truck at a motel in Susanville, CA. when I noticed an object that had two lights on the rear end. It was just starting to get light. I could see that it was triangular in shape and travelling in a Northeast to Southwest direction. It was about 40 feet in diameter. It was going quite slow and appeared to be 1-2000 feet in elevation. It made no sound and I observed no omissions. I motioned to a neighbor who was also loading his truck. We watched it together for several minutes until it disappeared over the horizon.
