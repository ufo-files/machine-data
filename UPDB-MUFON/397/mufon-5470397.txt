I was a passenger looking out the side window. I watched a star that I knew was too big for a star

I wanted to mention a sighting I had of a UFO around 1987. It looked like a star at first but I noticed it was too big to be a star. I was a passenger watching the oversized star in the Southeast night sky. I watched the star uninterrupted for at least 10 minutes. Then I seen it take off in a flash, like one second and it was gone. But it left an imprint. Left in the sky for a few seconds was this flash of greenish-blue light in the shape of a perfect triangle. This was awesome to witness.
