{jpg} Object was extremely bright in the sky and vanished behind clouds

On the night of September 25th 2017, I happened to walk outside to grab something from my car. Every time I go out, I look up in the sky at night to see the stars. This time I looked up and something was extremely bright in the sky. At first I thought it was a really bright star until I realized it was actually moving. No flashing lights like a plane, no sound, just a ball of light. It went behind some clouds for a few seconds then was visible. It went back behind some clouds and vanished. It never came back out. I posted to social media and it was a frenzy. There were planes out but you knew they were planes. This was something else.
