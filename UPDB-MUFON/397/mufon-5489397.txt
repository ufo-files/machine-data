{nd} Bright, glowing,bright as the sun, hovered, left straight up in a instant velocity , very sudden.

i was 11 years old , playing pee-wee league base ball , south of Eugene at a baseball diamond, we were playing ball, when all players stopped ,then I wondered what is happening , why did everyone stop playing ball, I looked , everyone in the bleachers was looking up, and all in the field were looking up, I looked up also, there was a large glowing ball of light hovering over the field, no sound at all, it hovered for several minutes, then moved straight straight upward quickly , then straight up quickly out of sight, no noise at all.... I told my mother when I got home, but then was forgotten, I've told people all of my life when the subject arose, and still tell people ....it is the truth....many adults witnessed it that day also......it was the most increddable thing I ever wittnessed......the only thing that I have seen.....it moveed at such a speed , that I know now, that no man, or pilot could not survive or fly such a craft.
