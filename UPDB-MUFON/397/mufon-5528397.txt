5 lts. in line,then v shape,dissapeared.

I walked out back to watch the random fireworks going off at mid nite on New Years 2011. I was facing n/w at the time and noticed 5 faint lights coming toward me.I called to my sister to come and look at the planes flying in. But I soon realized that it was moving too slow to be planes it seemed to be hovering. It continued to head toward us as we stood in amazement watching it change from a v shape into an s shape it then seemed to stop as the lights slowly faded until only one faint light was visible. I immediately went inside to check the internet to see if anyone else saw it too. My internet was slow so I looked back outside and I watched 3 lights reappear in the same area that it had faded to. The 3 lights faded into one faint light. WOW!!!
