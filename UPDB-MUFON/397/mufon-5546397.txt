Saw a star moving rapidly from right to left in the night sky and disappear

I took my dog out and sat down in my chair which was facing Northerly and I saw a star move out from (behind?) another star and shoot quickly from right to left in the night sky. This lasted only a short period of time, perhaps 6 seconds maximum. The arc was perhaps 20 (less than 30). The azimuth was approx. 75. It simply disappeared. There was no tail or trail of light. Just what appeared to be a relatively small star moving very, very fast. I was just lucky I looked up when I did and saw it and followed it. Amazing, really!
