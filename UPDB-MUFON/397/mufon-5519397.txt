My family and I were driving North on 59 around 10am, Sunday May 8, 2011. I noticed a dark object hovering near the tree line over the area of New Caney High School. It looked very dense and was about the size of a Suburban, but had an oval-like shape. Its surface was not smooth, but had many bumps and ridges. I asked my family if they could see what I was seeing. At least four of us saw it. It never moved and the whole sighting did not last very long as we were driving past it and traveling away from it. As my curiosity peaked, I insisted we turn the vehicle around at FM 1485 to get a better look, but one family member became very upset, so we did not go back.
