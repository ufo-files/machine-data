{rjl} Sphere like bright object desencding rapidly towards grown

I was traveling home from a friends house late Friday night March 25, 2016 on highway US-40 from Plainfield, Indiana to Indianapolis, about a 12 minute drive. Halfway through the drive I noticed high in the sky a bright redish-orange object freefalling towards the ground at a rapid rate of speed. It fell into an empty field just to my left. It almost looked like a flare but with a brigther intensity. I know the Indianapolis International Airport is in this area but it was no airplane...the only thing it could be described similar to is a flare. It got my attention at first because of how bright it was and how fast it was freefalling almost in a tail spin to the ground..I would say it almost started out at airplane level. I was startled at first and wish I would have had my phone out in time to take a picture or video but everything happened so fast. I decided to keep driving home instead of checking what was in the field but I regretted not stopping and looking the whole way home.
