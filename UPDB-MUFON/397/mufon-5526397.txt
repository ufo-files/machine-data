Saw a large disc in the clouds just right above me , noticed it while I was standing out side a grocery parking lot while I was talking to some friends .

I had just dropped of my mother and my aunt at the front of this grocery store and went to park my rental car at an area in the parking lot that was slightly higher so that way I could get a better view of when my relatives come out of the grocery store and are ready to be picked up , since I was on an emergency visit and did'nt know how much time I would be staying for I called an old friend to come meet me for a bit while I waited , when my good friend arrived he and his cousin greeted me outside between our cars , they were talking and suddenly I noticed a shape up in the clouds I could not believe what I was seeing ... It was a large disc , I could not stop from watching this disc ,I was trying in my head to come up with an excuse to what it was'nt but I knew it was not a ballon , because it would be floting away , This disc just stood very still , part of it I would say less than half was in the cloud , I wanted to reach out to my friends and have them look up but as I was about to do so The disc began to move perfectly straight up until It was so small it disapeared ! I remember it was a flat silver or grey disc, as soon as it was gone I asked my friends if they saw it , then we stood there talking about it while looking up in case the disk returned . I will never forget !
