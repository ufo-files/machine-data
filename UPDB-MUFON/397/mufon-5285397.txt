Not visible to my eyes until I looked at photos later.

I was out for a quiet morning walk, by myself, on a beautiful summer morning. I took a few sunrise photos. I could not see or hear anything unusual until I looked at the photos on my phone later. 

Even then, I did not immediately see anything on the first photo. I looked at it more closely after I saw a small hollowed-out sphere that appeared white, like the moon can appear during daylight hours, on the second photo. When I zoomed in on it, I saw that it did not appear to be natural. 

That's when I looked at the first photo. Only upon zooming in the sun's brilliance between the tree branches did I find a dark cigar shape that appeared far away from me. If the object is the same in both photos, then it appears to have changed shape. It's possible though that this (and the other apparent changes) were merely changes of perspective. Also, the object in the second through fifth photos appear to be closer to me than in the first photo; the object in them appears to be reflecting light in the wrong direction.
