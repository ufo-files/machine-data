Vertical formation of 5-6 craft w/ orangy lights.

I am on the phone with my brother,rounding the bend in my street when I see in the northern sky-about 9:30 position ( directly in my line of vision) what appears to be 5-6 what I assume to be small planes- each w/ what appears to be 2 headlights each,of a red/orangish color...a shimmery almost twinkly quality to the light,and they're in a vertical almost V formation... I say aloud " what the hell is that?!" . I stopped my car in the middle of the street to watch these things.. they appear to be stationary. I think of chinese lanterns ( I dunno why) and cell phone towers..( again,I dunno why) -as I watch these the lights shimmer/twinkle and disappear-as if they went into a cloud bank. I pulled into my driveway-parked and rapidly got out-looking for these things-or the clouds....there were no clouds-or cloudbanks. I was excited-as I had just seen something unusual,but calm ...I did not experience the hairs on the back of my neck standing on end. I was/am more curious as to what it was that I saw. I called Fox Lake Police,Lake County Sherrif and the FAA.The police and sheriff suggested it was meteor showers...what I saw was nothing like a metoer shower.
