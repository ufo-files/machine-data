{cak} Large bright green-colored orb moving very fast.

While watching television, I glanced out the patio door and saw this very bright green-colored orb streaking across the night sky. I was unable to follow it's path due to rooftops in this area. My wife, sitting beside me, did not see it. No noise accompanied the sighting (the patio door was open). It appeared low and extremely fast. I have never seen anything like it.
