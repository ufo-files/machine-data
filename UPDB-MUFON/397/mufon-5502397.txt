{nd} Small glowing red object flying low odd speed

Traveling south, spotted glowing object in front of me. Realized it was to low to be aircraft, but as I got closer the object now could be viewed from my drivers side window. Object had red glow and a thin black circle could be seen inside of light. Appeared to not be solid object but almost a "see through" feeling. Object was moving, but at a speed different from a helicopter or aircraft. I pulled over and took a picture with my phone. Object had moved further away by time I could capture it on camera. No sound, no trail. No other objects in sky. It was still light outside but approaching dark. I wish I could have viewed object longer.
