{td} Red star like light going across the sky with 3 white tiny star like lights moving around the red star.

I was getting ready to go into work for the day standing in the parking lot I was looking up at the stars, Orion to be exact. When I noticed what I thought was just a satellite going through the sky. I thought it was strange because it was red not the normal white you usually see from a satellite. As I watched it, I realized this was way further away then a man made satellite, also it looked like it was towing a satellite (white) behind it. As my focus became clearer I could see 2 other White dots (satellite like dots) that could move freely near it and around it, and fast. I watched this for approx. 3 minutes started in the west/southwest sky moved in a straight line to the east/southeast sky, traveling just below Orion's belt. Never sped up or slowed down kept a constant speed until it was out of my sight.
