UFO sighting. 6-2-19
10:45 AM Sunday, June 2, 2019
Sighting not unlike a sighting Jan 5, 2019. Location of view, Remington and South Lewis Way.
Backing out of my garage and heading WNW down our lane, contrasting against a huge white cumulus thunderhead, is a “twitching” object. I saw a black spinning top-shaped craft with flinging blades rapidly opening and closing. 
I stopped the SUV and tried to get my wife to see it. She could not. The sighting lasted about eight seconds. 
A jet and contrail is flying over the cloud, so this jet was above the object. The jet was moving the same direction as the one in January. 
We did not have the cloud overhead for five miles traveling north on C-470. The object was thimble size in my field of vision moving SE-NW and disappearing into the cloud. 
This is not unlike the Jan sighting which lasted three seconds before disappearing in a cirrus cloud. The object traveling opposite the jet contrail following along the Hog Back, NW-SE. (Another contrail was noticeable in the same direction of the first jet, but at a steeper easterly angle.) 
Observations: 
I think it’s likely there are more objects being sighted. The Navy was forced to admit these exist.
Since the January sighting, I’ve been more observant of the skies, increasing my chances of seeing another one. 
Along the distance of the Hog Back formation exist a series of abandoned uranium mines.

In both sightings the objects were within a mile of a Lockheed Martin facility.
