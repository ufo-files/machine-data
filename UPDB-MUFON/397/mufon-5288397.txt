Photographed a UFO

I went to the Wichita Mountains last weekend on Saturday (4-24-21) for hiking and photos. I went up to the summit of Mt Scott and walked down to an overlook area on the west side. There is a tree that I was wanting to photograph, so i took a series of test shots for proper angle and composition. I found a composition that I liked and decided to return at dusk to shoot a good photo of the tree. I never did make it back up that day, and didn't even review the images until today (4-28-21). When i went through my RAW files, I noticed an object in the sky. It appears to be at a down-angle of about 40 degrees, with motion blur at edges. There is a clear circular structure to the object. While I shot this, it was completely silent except for a little wind and other visitors on the summit. Nobody was throwing anything off the mountain, and especially at the distance at which I've estimated to be 500+ feet away. I shot the test photo at 640th of a second; too fast for a drone to get motion blur. I am leaving a RAW image file; all EXIF data is embedded with it. I had one friend with me and she didn't see or hear it either.
