{dr}Orange lights that accelerated up.

This is from my wife who would like me to report it. She was watching tv when she noticed three orange lights to the north west of our home. They werent blinking but seemed to be moving both south and towards the house. She got up and went to the window to get a better look. Thinking they might be a helocopter that would soon be moving over the house, she went outside to the drive way to get a better look. When she got outside, the lights had moved up and away at a fast rate of speed and went from orange balls to white lights. They continued to move up and away travelling east and south from our home. No sound was ever heard from the objects.
