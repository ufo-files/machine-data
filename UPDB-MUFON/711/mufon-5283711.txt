Several ufo's sighted on the 11:00 news.

While watching the 11:00 news on WILX in Lansing Michigan 
on Oct. 27th 2021. The time was approx. 11:03 pm. I witnessed several ufo's flying across the tv screen as they were doing a shot of the leaves changing color. The ufo's were white in color, varying sizes, as in distance differences, and flying in straight lines going quickly from left to right. I immediately hit the record button, so have the film clip on my DVR and have taken a still shot of the video on my cell phone camera, which I would be happy to share via text. I called the tv station, but was nicely rebuffed. The aired footage should be in their archives.
