THE EVENT IS SAID TO OCCURRED IN NACOGDOCHES COUNTY ONE NIGHT IN THE SUMMER OF 1957 WHEN CHARLES SHEPHERD AND COLEY BASS WERE OUT IN THE COUNTRY ON SOME PROPERTY THAT COLEY'S DAD HAD LEASED TO RAISE CATTLE. THE BOTH CLAIMED TO HAVE SEEN SOME TYPE OF NICKEL-SHAPED SPACE CRAFT WITH DIM, BLINKING LIGHT WHICH HOVERED NEAR THEM FOR A FEW MINUTES. CHARLIE SAYS THAT HE ACTUALLY BOARDED THE CRAFT. THE SPACE CRAFT WAS ABOUT 120-140 FEET ACROSS. WE WERE IN A SMALL FACILITY IN THE SPACE CRAFT, APPROXIMATELY 15 X 18 FEET. THE CEILING WAS ONLY ABOUT SIX-FEET HIGH. I WAS SITTING IN A CHAIR LIKE OBJECT. MY HANDS, LEGS, AND HEAD WERE SECURED IN THE UNIT WHERE I WAS SITTING. THERE WAS A HAND ON MY FOREHEAD PREVENTING ME FROM MOVING MY HEAD IN ANY DIRECTION. THE UNIT WAS NOT A RECTANGLE SHAPE, BUT RATHER AN OVAL. THERE WERE FIVE OR SIX DIM SMALL LIGHTS PLACED AROUND THE OVAL UNIT. WITH OUT NOTICE CUBES ABOUT SIX TO EIGHT INCHES BEGAN TO APPEAR IN FRONT OF MY EYES. THE CUBES LINED UP SIDE-BY-SIDE. ABOUT TEN UNITS ON THE TOP LINE. THEN SIX OR EIGHT OTHER LINES WERE CONNECTED DIRECTLY BELOW THE FIRST LINE. IT WAS LIKE VIEWING THE UNITS ON A CHESS BOARD, EXCEPT LARGER. THE COLORS WEE BRILLIANT, BUT I AM NOT ABLE TO RECALL THE EXACT COLORS BECAUSE THE SYMBOLS ON THE CUBES WERE MANY DIFFERENT COLORS. THERE WAS NOT A SCREEN LIKE A TV AND THERE WERE NO WIRES. THEY JUST MOVED INTO PLACE AND BECAME VIEW-ABLE. WHILE WATCHING THE CUBES,, THE ONE ON THE UPPER LEFT BECAME
BRIGHTER AND HELP THAT VIEW FOR ABOUT 4-6 SECONDS. THEN THE NEXT CUBE TO THE RIGHT LIT UP BRIGHTER FOR 4-6 SECONDS. THIS TOOK PLACE ONE MOVE AT A TIME ACROSS THEN DOWN AND ACROSS UNTIL IT HAD RUN THE BOARD. ai BELIEVE THAT THE UNIT IN WHICH I WAS SITTING HAD MODULES TO MAKE MY BRAIN RESPOND TO THE CHARACTERS IN WHICH I RECOGNIZED. THE UNITS THAT I WAS VIEWING WERE HIEROGLYPHICS CREATED IN ANCIENT EGYPT. EGYPTIAN HIEROGLYPHS WERE THE FORMAL WRITING SYSTEM USED IN ANCIENT EGYPT. HIEROGLYPHICS MEANS "SACRED CARVINGS." INSCRIPTIONS CARVED OR PAINTED ON TEMPLE WALLS. HIEROGLYPHICS ALSO HAS A BIBLICAL REFERENCE TO ANCIENT ALIENS. THEY WERE TRYING TO DETERMINE WHERE I WAS FROM AND FROM WHAT GENERATION OF THE PAST DID I EXIST. FOLLOWING THEIR TEST AND MY RETURN THE NEXT THING I REMEMBERED WAS US GOING FOR THE GUN IN THE PICKUP AT THE SHAWNEE RIVER. COLEY BASS AND OUR MEMORIES OF THESE EVENTS WERE ERASED FOR OVER FIFTY YEARS.ALL THE MEMORIES OF THESE EVENTS FINALLY RETURNED TO US A BOUT THE SAME TIME. THE YEAR WAS 2009, A FEW MONTHS PRIOR TO COLEY'S DEATH. MY MEMORY OF BEING TAKEN UP TO THE SPACECRAFT, I NEVER SAW COLEY. THIS WAS AN ADVENTURE THAT VERY FEW, IF ANY, HAVE EVER EXPERIENCED.
