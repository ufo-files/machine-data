{rjl} Saw bright red/orange flying object appear quickly and was gone quickly.

I was in the backyard with my dogs and saw this bright red/orange light appear in the sky and was quickly moving. It was moving in a straight line pattern. I thought it was a plane at first but it was moving too fast and flying too low. It made no noise. It was round and almost looked like it was on fire. The house flood lights were on so I ran the length of the house, which is not that long a distance to see it on the darker side of the yard and it had disappeared. I felt a little un-nerved.
