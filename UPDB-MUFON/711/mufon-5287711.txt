Flying from DFW to Santa Barbara.  Three massive bright lights on desert floor. Almost too bright to stare at without sunglasses. At first I thought it might be a solar farm, but as you can see in the pictures, there are no roads, or evidence of substations, landing strips... Absolutely no evidence of any human development within 500 miles in every direction.  I wasn't able to see any form to the lights, and I travel nearly monthly on planes (pre-covid) and I've never seen anything like this. Possibly a secret nuclear installation? But again, no roads, structures or landing strips anywhere within view from any direction. I stared at this for about 8 minutes and didn't mention it to anyone until I got home. And nobody can tell me what my pictures are. It looks to me like 3 bright lights landed in some sort of formation, maybe they were hovering but hard to tell from 35K feet. I'm sure there's an explanation that is Terrestrial, but I can't figure out what that explanation might be. **Note that I put in Clark County, Las Vegas... Only to get to submit this. I do have digital photos, which I am happy to email. I literally have no idea where we were, guessing somewhere over Nevada, Arizona, or California desert.
