On May1st,2016 approximately 8:00 PM I was sitting on the front porch with my wife. I then noticed a bright object streaking across the sky at a downward sloped trajectory towards the east. I walked out into the yard to get a better view of the object.I only ended up getting 1 picture of the object on my phone. Approximately 20 to 30 Minutes later 2 planes fly over my house heading in the same direction the object was heading. I didn't manage to get a picture of the planes though. I couldn't tell how big the 1st plane was, but the 2nd plane was really big with 3 flashing lights on the back with the red light located in the middle of the other 2 lights. It looked like a plane that could carry a big cargo. (Note: I'm not an expert on Planes, UFO's, Asteroids, Meteorites, or Comets.)
