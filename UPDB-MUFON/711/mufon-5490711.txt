mysterious yellow/orange lighted object moving/hoovering slowly before taking off and disappearing in a blink of an eye

My wife to be and myself left the house to take our dog for a walk and immediately stepping outside there was this circular yellow/orange light sort of moving slowly and hoovering almost above us. It seemed to be quite big and not too high but difficult to tell because there was no point of reference and the event only lasted aprox. 15 seconds so it wasnt enough time to observe distance and size. Nevertheless, 15 seconds is long enough to know what it is and what it isnt. The event ended by the object taking off in a flash and disappearing. I know it was an object and not just a light because once it took off its light went out and i had a partial glimpse of an object before disappearing upwards. I didnt recall hearing any sound though my fiancee says she heard something. It was a clear warm evening. The dynamics of this object NOT a natural phenomena and NOT of this world. 3 days later (the time it took me to process this in my head) i decided to do a Google search for OVNI St. J1r1me and it took me to a french website similar to this one and someone actually reported the same event only with more d1tails and even a video. This witness was a little farther away than me and more west therefore his viewing angle was different. the video shows a perfect circle whereas we viewed a distorted circle - though this person saw 3 separate appirations and only filmed one of them. We only saw 1 appiration - probably the final one.
