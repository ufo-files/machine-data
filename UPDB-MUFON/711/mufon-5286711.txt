Time loss

After work I bought a six pack of beer which was in a paper bag, I stopped at the park to have a beer at a bench, I popped open the first one and took a drink, the very next thing I knew I was coming back from somewhere and telling myself, “don’t forget, don’t forget!
Then I was sitting at the bench again and forgot where I was, then I noticed my ice cold beer was not only warm but dry as well. In fact all the beer was warm and the bag was bone dry. How long does that take?!? Then I began having those super clear dreams about UFOs and a little silver box starting that night!
Every time I wanted to tell someone, I would suddenly forget all about it. 
It wasn’t till years later that I was able to speak of it to others... what happened to me?????
