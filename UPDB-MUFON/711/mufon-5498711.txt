{nd} Obsd streak of light in or above overcast moving faster than any known aircraft.

I had gotten up to use the bathroom and upon returning to bed at approx. 0200 hrs, I stopped to look out my bedroom window. I noticed that there was overcast when suddenly, a cylinder shape of light travelling NE streaked across the sky. There was absolutely NO sound and went a fast as you can drop your pointed finger from a bent at the elbow straight up to a downward position to a straight head pointed position! We live not too far from an Airport and passenger jets constantly fly over my house! They are noisy and slow moving across the sky! This was maybe 1000 to 2000 ft up and either in or above the overcast. At first I thought it was a high beam search light (like to attract people to a carnival or sale of some kind) and waited for it to come around again, but after several minutes, I realized that that's what it wasn't! I worked the graveyard shift outdoors for many years and was always looking for UFO's but never did see anything unusual. This was approx. 1/4 inch wide by 3 inches long looking up at it from my position on the ground. I'm a former US Paratrooper and retired Police Officer!
