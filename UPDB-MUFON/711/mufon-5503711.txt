{dr}Triangle craft seen hovering with no sound above car

I am reporting this sighting for my sister and dad, both of which witnessed the event. They were driving a friend home and saw a triangle shaped craft with three large lights on one side of the triangle hovering above their car. The craft was approximately 150 feet above the car and made no sound whatsoever. They did not stop the car and kept driving, the craft continued to hover and slowly dissapeared behind a nearby treeline. My sister was shook up by the event and call the cops to report it.
