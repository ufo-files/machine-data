{dr}2 pairs of orbs slowly floated along a path from NW to NE

At approximately 21:30 on Friday, 21 September 2013 my wife and I witnessed 2 pairs of red orbs in the northern sky slowly traveling east & then northeast. Each set of orbs, one pair slightly following another, moved silently, and maintained a precise formation insomuch as keeping the distance between each individual orb and its respective partner. The second pair was in a formation that had one orb slightly southwest in relation to the other, while the first pair was oriented in an east to west fashion. I have video of this event.
