{kt}2 occurances red/orange glowing slient lights. 1 passed over head, then next time 2 on same trajectory

Sitting enjoying the night sky meditating. Opened eyes, saw very bright lights elongated shape moving low in sky. No noise. Always with the planes there is noise. We are on a flight path. But these were close and coming out the south. Very unusual. Then it came around again. Either the same one or another on the same route. Could not figure out what it was. Then shortly after, another came and right behind it another on its heel, like a baby light following its mother. All the same color on the on the same path. All at the same altitude. Came in to discover more about them and they seem to be everywhere. Came into house to get binoculors but they didn't return.
Must be something to it since I would never take this time to write it down here. Very, very unusual.
