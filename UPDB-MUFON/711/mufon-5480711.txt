{rjl} Just before dawn, saw light to the north. Then it shot to the NW.

Sitting on back porch having cigarette. Saw light not far above the horizon and noted it didn't appear to be moving. Considered it might be a plane moving in straight line towards me. Watched for 20-30 seconds and it never seemed to actually get closer. Suddenly it darted in a diagonal direction either up and left or towards the NW. It disappeared within a tenth of a second.
