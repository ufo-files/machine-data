I noticed stars moving in a fixed formation from West to East overhead.
I grabbed my camera from my room and pressed the record button.

I kept recording the formation of stars for another 5 minutes.
It was a huge formation of UFOs in constant motion the size of the July 11th, 1991 Mexico City sighting.
