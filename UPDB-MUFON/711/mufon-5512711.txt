{cak} Saw a UFO fly overhead

My wife called me to the back door and pointed to the West Southwestern sky. Following her indication I saw 2 bright lights, like headlights on high-beam, approaching in a low altitude strait pattern headed toward me. The noise level was lower than any aircraft I have ever heard. It flew directly over my house. I observed three lights in equilateral triangle pattern on the undercarriage of the UFO. Unfortunately, I didn't have the wherewithal to record it.
