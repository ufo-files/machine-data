2 bright white lights

At approx. 7:30 on September 26, Witness 1 observed what appeared to be two bright starlike objects maneuvering in an odd fashioning North East of our location at approx. 60 degrees with an estimated in atmosphere altitude of 300'. Subsequently W2 and W3 also were able to view the starlike objects. All parties observed what appeared to be 2 bright white lights moving simultaneously in an offset fashion for multiple seconds, until fading out. No identifiable surface, no sound projected and relative speed appeared somewhat low. Based on movement one might suggest that this may have been one object with lights at either end. -
