Flashing light in response to laser pointer.

We were doing star watching with a park ranger at Grand Canyon NP. He used a green laser pointer to point out constellations. When he did this a flashing light appeared overhead in the area that he had directed the Pointer. This happened a few times and seemed to catch the attention of some other viewers there. I have no explanation for this.
