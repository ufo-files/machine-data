Hovering Disc Glides Over Water Toward Road

I was walking back from the Joes Pond Beach, one early afternoon...arriving atthe jct of routes 215, I looked over toward the boat landing and saw what I madeout to be a disc like two pie plates, open sides together, hovering around 6feet from the surface of the water, maybe 20 feet from the rt2 road bed, movingin that direction. Whether there were cars on the road at that time, I cannotrecall. I just looked at it for about 10-15 seconds, remarked to myself I cantbelieve what I think I just saw and walked away in amazement.
