{cak} 3 reddish orange orbs/lights moving through the sky

I saw three reddish orange lights/orbs floating in the sky. From my vantage point, they were approximately due west. They were a fairly bright, not overly bright, reddish orange lights that moved slowly. They appeared to be a fairly large diameter, maybe the size of an aspirin.  They appeared to be close based on their size. The three lights moved closer together then one light faded out.  The two lights that stayed bright began to separate them move together in a southerly direction. I couldn't believe what I was seeing and I pulled out my phone and was able to get a picture of the three lights close together and then was able to record the rest of the sighting of the two lights. The lights eventually faded out. The video and pictures show the planet Venus to the lower left of the lights/orbs. The lights appeared much larger than points of light as seen in the picture/video.

 There was no noise. The weather was calm, no wind.
