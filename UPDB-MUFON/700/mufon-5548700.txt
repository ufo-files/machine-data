two hard hat shaped side by side flying slow under cloud covered sky heading north to south no sound same craft as seen in crashed russian military footage

a friend and I were walking back from a 24hr conveince store on dec 12 1993 at 4:15 am we just entered into the 1st eastern driveway of the lord tennison apartment complex and she said do you see that I looked up to see two ufo's side by side the size of an average single prop " piper cub type" aircraft they made no sound and looked like two backward hard hats in shape "very simalar to the shape of a horseshoe crab" each glowing dim blue and the texture of rough fiberglass flying north to south slowly like an aircraft coming in for a landing on the botton I saw a raised square like a hatch door or engine compartment on the top of them on each side was a raised area that if they were made of clay would look as though someone pushed up the sides with thier thumbs making what looked like lips or a wave at the ocean in the middle of the lips the shape of a hard hat was consistant I ran after them wanting to see as much as I could untill they flew out of sight 45 minutes later another ufo flying slow like the others flew in the exsact same flight path as if fallowing a lazer beam this one was much larger about the size of a 737 passanger jet it was glowing a dim yellow same rough fiberglass texture this one was shaped like three triangles stuck toghether at the rear and stretching very long to the front with the middle one sticking out the farthest no sound I was at first very excited "like riding a roller coaster " but soon after was filled with fear I don't think they are friendly thats just my opinion but then ask yourself when was the last time you heard of a friendly abduction ufo or otherwise ?
