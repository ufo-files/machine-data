Odd blue self propelled orb

I was watching the full moon and an odd cloud formation, the only clouds in sky, next to moon.  The clouds first looked normal and within a few seconds formed what looked to be a perfect circle and very large. I swear it looked like a portal. 
I was taking pictures of the odd cloud next to the moon and noticed an odd orb drop from bottom of cloud circle. 
I thought maybe somehow my flash had done this until I looked with my naked eye and saw a bright blue orb of unknown size hovering just below the circle in cloud and then it seemed to be going back and forth in a grid search type of movement. It did this for about 5 mins and just blinked out and disappeared. It was not a jet. No sound and no blinking lights. No obvious lights on object rather the entire object was glowing blue. Idk what it was but it was weird.
