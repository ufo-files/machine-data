Orb Apears to zoom into video of plane follows plane then moves up down and then away

Was outside video taping a plane coming into Ottawa airport with a new iPad. A few weeks later my son was looking at the pictures and videos I had taken with th iPad and found the orb in the video I had taken weeks earlier. After watching the video and examining it over and over, it does not appear to be due to camera movement or reflective lights. The orb seems to zoom in from the horizon follow above the plane , then move up then down the move quickly forward ahead of the plane and fade out or into possible cloud cover. Was really interesting to see video and thought I would forward it on to mufon.
