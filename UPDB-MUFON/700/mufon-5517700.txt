{ck} Going up hill on rt 33 north. Saw green fireball disappearing over hill

Wife and I driving north on rt 33 just north of wind gap. Saw large silent green fireball. Size of a house from about one thousand feet away. Caught or eye at about 30 degrees above the horizon from above right. It was heading north west at about two hundred miles an hour and disappeared over the hill we were driving up just about directly in front of us. Never saw any mention in the media
