{nd} Big white orb with small reddish tail moved smoothly and quickly across the sky and disappeared into the clouds. Weird feeling after occurrence, others in town also witnessed.

I was in New Jersey for my fathers wedding. My sister left the rehearsal dinner for a while and my father was concerned. I left the dinner to go back to the house on the same street. I started running. it was a breezy and cool night, with clouds but they were moving very fast. Almost as soon as I stepped out of the restaurant this big orb appeared in the sky. It continued moving from north to south at an oddly smooth pace. Too big to be a star, unlike any shooting star i've ever seen. Too smooth to be a meteor or satellite or anything. It continued across the sky and half way across disappeared behind the thin cloud cover. At first I was flabbergasted at what I saw. I got this weird feeling after it happened and the weather seemed to synchronize with the event (wind). A few days later I was describing the event to a friend in the same town and him and his father saw the same exact thing! The father swears on it being a UFO. I want to believe, but I still have my doubts.
