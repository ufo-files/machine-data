Two metallic orb looking things stationary in the sky (for a good 15mins and then moving away slowly into the distance

It was bank holiday in the UK and incredibly nice weather. My partner was working on doing some roofing on a garden building and I was sitting in the hot tub. I looked to the sky and saw one bright object at the distance you'd see a high up plane. Then I noticed another to the left of it... Both appeared metalic and shiny reflecting the sunshine. The strange thing was that they stayed in that position for a good 15mins... now and again either flashing or reflecting sunshine but not moving.

I called my partner to look and he also saw them - he didn't know what to make of it but returned to working... I stayed where I was and watched for quite some time.

They slowly moved upwards - not across the sky but away from earth!

I felt confused and slightly mind blown.

I couldn't think what these could be, planes don't stay still in the sky - birds don't... I'm very confused but felt these are not natural objects nor planes etc.

The objects started to move slowly away and I watched until I could no longer see them.
