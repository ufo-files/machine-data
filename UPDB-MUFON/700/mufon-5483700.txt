{nd} Creatures with big orange heads falling into the road in Eastern Kentucky

I was driving back from my friends house in Isom, Kentucky. It was roughly 1 am, and I had the music on just driving. While going through Dry Fork (a small community, not quite a town, outside of Whitesburg), I noticed what appeared to be a human like creature. They were staggering & falling into the road.. At first I thought this was a very intoxicated human, but when I took a second glance, I discovered otherwise.. I realized that not only was there one of these creatures, but there were 3 of them. They were orange creatures who had HUGE circle heads.. They were staggering, like I said, so I assume that they may be a crossover between a creature that runs/walks, and crawls.. While driving and seeing them stagger, my initial thought was that they were coming towards my car, so I swerved into the passing lane. After seeing this, I drove as fast as I could to one of my friends and picked them up... When we got back, the creatures were gone. This sighting really messed with my mind... Since then, I've had thoughts of this creature, and I can't help but wonder what these things are. I have not told anyone besides the friend I picked up that night, due to the fact that I know some people would not believe.. It was the most real and true experience I have ever encountered in my life.
