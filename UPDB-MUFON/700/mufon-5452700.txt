Unknown hovering object spotted and video taped ..32 minutes of footage shows more than one in formation

I was testing my new Sony digital 8 ca,era .it was the first sunset in 29 days so I wanted to film it..I stood on the deck overlooking the ocean and started. I saw birds flying,  And basically nothing.. as I stood there, a orange balloon like thing flew from my left, following the chetco river ..the camera locked onto it .i was able to zoom into it and it looked like it was changing shapes. Kinda warbling, spinning, like it was ..able to move...I lost it since I estimate it size to be 5 feet or less. I had a wide angle lense adapter,on too. Over the next 32 minutes, I captured this object many times as it hovered around in front of me to my right, then centered.. as the sun was setting, these were hovering out over the water, looked like they were spinning and sparking...and throwing off more black balls over the water..they were in a triangle formation and never move..they look like probes maybe..and were ..apparently centered around the clear bubble which looked like a invisible shield, cloaking a saucer inside.. as shown in the darker pictures three black dots are over it in a triangle. It appears and for few seconds,  All are visible...they then disappear together back up the river. Very fast. 3 frames they zoom off...these are stop frames I capture from my video. 2003 brookings Oregon
