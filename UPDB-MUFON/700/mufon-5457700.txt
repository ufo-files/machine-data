{rjl} UFO hovers over Lake Superior as freighter passes directly beneath

Witness 3 and 4 wish to remain anonymous 

Sighting date: June 12, 2019
Time: 1:00am-2:38am
Place Lake Superior, White Fish point
Weather: overcast, some rain, temperatures lower 50’s

Sighting description:
At approx. 1:00am, Witness 1 and witness 2, and 2 other witnesses were looking for Yooperlite
Rocks at Lake Superior off White Fish Point Bird Sanctuary. At approx. 1:00am witness 2 told witness 1 to look over his shoulder at the orange lights in the sky. Witness 1 thought they were drones at first but then the lights started to maneuver around the sky in an odd fashion. 

Description of lights movement:
3 lights in sky approx. 200-300 feet in altitude above the water
Distance from witness: 1-2 miles
Color: bright orange
Movement: lights began to quickly change positions in a circular pattern, changing eventually to two lights, then one light, back to two lights and finally back to one large single light in the sky hovering.
Hovering light reflected off the water
No sound
No EMF
Only thing between light and witnesses was sand beach and water

View thru binoculars showed light as not one large single light but several tight grouped tiny lights in a quivering motion. Thru binoculars Erik watched the light separate into two then back to one. His description was “like looking at plasma in the sky”

At approx. 1:15am a freighter (739 feet Spruce Glen, a 1983 iron ore freighter) came into the harbor by way of the channel which pasts White Fish Point and traveled directly under the hovering light. The light reflected on the deck of the ship as it passed underneath. The ship barely cleared underneath the hovering light as it passed the captains deck section. 

Witness 1 contacted the ship lines trying to recover the deck footage, but was told the ship lines had nothing to report. 
Witness 1 also has several minutes of footage including footage of the freighter passing directly under the hovering light.
