{cak} UFO sighted over Olympia air field

In 1965 I was on the Washington Wing Civil Air Patrol search and rescue team. I was on a search and rescue mission to locate a downed air craft.
 The time was 2 AM. I suddenly awoke. Sat up and looked at the run way.
I spotted a dome shaped ufo. The lights seemed to rotate round and round. Seemed I could see the lights going all the way around as if you could see
them through the back of the craft. Me and two friends were sleeping inside the office. I woke up my friends and said I just saw a UFO. They replied , you are dreaming, go back to sleep. In the time I woke my friends and told them, 10 minutes had passed. It was 2:10 AM. 
 On the way back to Seattle, We were listening to I believe it was KJR a
local radio station reported a UFO over the Olympia Air Field. We all looked at one another in total amazement. Also I have a question? I heard a rumor that a UFO was Spotted in front of Mount Saint Helens just before it erupted.
