{cak} Two vertical lights moved slowly parallel to the ground, then changed direction, and shot away.

I saw it in the sky towards the SE. I had never seen anything like it before tonight. Two lights, one directly above the other about a car's length apart moved very slowly directly across the sky. It moved parallel to the ground and seemed to be hovering about 200ft off the ground. Then it slowed down, turned on a dime, and the lights either faded out, or it shot off into the darkness. It was difficult to tell which, though i think it flew away as there was nothing there after the lights went out.
The entire experience lasted about 15 seconds, from my noticing the lights, to a sense that they were something different, to watching it move across the night sky, to starting to see what seemed like an object between the two lights, through it suddenly and noticeably stopping, changing directions, and then shooting offto the dark.
