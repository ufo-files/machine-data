From the SE towards ENE slowly. Accelerated and deccelerated, Subtle zig-zags and one almost full stop. As bright and large as Venus.

I was on my back deck grilling dinner for my girlfriend and me. I first noticed the object when I was casually observing Venus towards the southwest. Next to Venus was what I thought to be a star at first. When I noticed it was moving I became interested. I didn't know what it was at first. As it got closer to me, at the closest point almost right over head, I noticed that it didn't move like anything Id ever seen in the sky before. It was slow, and seemed like it was light as air. It bobbed and weaved (subtly) and at one point came to a brief stand still before resuming course. It was yellowish gold/orange. It made no sound whatsoever. It passed overhead all the way to the ENE horizon and then vanished from my view. My girlfriend is the second witness. I was extremely excited during this experience. Ive wanted to see a UFO ever since I was a little kid. When I realized what I was seeing wasnt a plane, helicopter, meteor, or a satellite, I became as elated as if Id won the lottery. After 4 to 5 minutes of watching this thing, it just disappeared from my sight like a plane does. It simply got too far away for me to see it any longer.
