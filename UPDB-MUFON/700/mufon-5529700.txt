approx 20 silver spheres

Walking on the beach with my son when we looked up at a plane that was flying overhead. I noticed way above the plane about 20 glimmering( it was actually strange, kind of like they were wavering/fluttering) spheres in a "V" formation. At first thought they were geese heading south for the winter but then realized they were way to high for that. As I was watching i noticed that they also stayed in place without moving. I thought this was strange so I kept watching. after about 10 seconds they suddenly went from a " V" formation to a circle and started moving slowly south. They then changed from a circle to a straight line while still moving south. After about 10-15 seconds in a straight line they simply vanished.
