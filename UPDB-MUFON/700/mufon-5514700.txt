{nd} unknow Objects in Sky

[Item Moved/CMS/nd] time: 11;20 Am

I was standing outside of the office and happen to look up at the sky for a moment I thought I was watching a flock of geese and some type of birds fly high above, only then did I realize it was to high for birds, I started to look closer and realized it was to high up for it to be a jet or to be able to see with the naked eye, they were in 3 arrow formations and with about 10 in each formation. From what I could make out was that they were metallic and round considering they were so high up it almost looked like a small dots, I could see them fairly well. They were moving at a very fast speed and I could only observe them for about 20 to 30 sec. then they blended in with the blue sky. It was very strange indeed
