2 star-like lights, one following the other

2 star-like lights, one following the other in a straight line across the sky. Lasted about 20 seconds. The light in front after about 15 seconds all of sudden glowed brighter in intensity, then faded back to baseline. Both lights eventually disappeared. If you held your thumb and index finger up to the sky, the lights were about an inch apart.
