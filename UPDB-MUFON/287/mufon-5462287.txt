{rjl} Black triangle hovering above open field emitting a bright white light lighting up ENTIRE field

My parents and I were heading back home from a road trip. I was 13/14 at the time. It was really late at night/early in the morning. We were driving down the highway and passed Fairmount Park horse tracks when about a mile further down the road my step dad noticed something in the sky lighting up an open field and abruptly pulls onto the shoulder of the road and comes to a dead stop. I look over to the field and above the field was a giant black triangular shaped object hovering roughly about 100 ft above the field. It emitted a very bright white light illuminating the entire field. It was a bit longer than a football field and about 2 stories tall. There were red lights that randomly flashed under the object. My parents and I were close enough to the object for me to be able to see reflective rectangles along the sides that resembled windows, but there was no light shining through from the inside. The object shut the light off and continued to hover for another short period of time before lifting about another 15 ft and slowly start moving forward following the path of the highway. My step father got back onto the highway keeping speed withe the object for around 5-10 mins when all of the sudden it shot straight out of sight at the flick of a finger! The entire time my parents and I witnessed the phenomina, the object didn't emit any sound except for a hum that was just barely audible, even when moving. And all the windows in my parents vehicle, were rolled down. My parents and I were very awestruck. It wasn't a frightening experience but a very surreal 'is this actually happening 'experience. It has affected me and my life even 19 yrs later. I still remember that night very well.
