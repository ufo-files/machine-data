redesh rock like object,

At about age 12, living on a farm in North Attleboro,Mass.returning back to the house after evening feeding of chickens, about 7p.m. large reddish rock like object appeared above the house, heading east at a 15 Degree, angle moving downward, at tree top level, object banked right, very slowly, (maybe 5mph), a bright light beam appeared from a pond, and object slowed even more and disappeared into the beam, and beam went out.
