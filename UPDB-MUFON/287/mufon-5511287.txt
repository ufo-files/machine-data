this has never been reported to MUFON. a note written by my maternal grandmother [Name removed/cms/tg]: 

Memo - April 28th 1968 
P.S. So I don't forget exactly how I saw it. 

Sunday evening between 10:30 & 11 p.m. 3414 West 77th St. Los Angeles 

I went out on our Front Porch to put out the milk bottles & the night seemed nice & clear so I stepped over to the Porch railing to look at the sky to see if I could see any stars as we don't get to see the stars very often anymore in Los Angeles - & yes I could see Stars - when suddenly this round Ball of white Light streaked across the sky going North towards the Hollywood Hills & then a smaller Orange colored Streak came out from it pointing North to the Hills - but the Ball of Light was still there even when the Orange streak was evidently coming out of it in the direction it was going [which was faster than any airplane] & as I was watching it to see if it went over the hills or disintergrate - Poof it was gone & not a trace of it showed in the Sky - as far as I could see it never went over the hills or even up higher it just vanished like a hand just erased it from the sky it did not come to earth like you can see a falling Star as its direction was not down straight & going North from our house - it was something to see & I will never forget it. 
[Name removed/cms/tg]"
