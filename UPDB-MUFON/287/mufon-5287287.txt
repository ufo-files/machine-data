Hovering stationary object between treetops,

Driving from Greenwood to Abbeville with a 9 yr old and a 12 yr old late afternoon. We turned a curve around where the 2 churches are and looked ahead and noticed to the left side of road what looked to be a silver circle shaped object with a small dome on top and small dome on bottom hovering just between treetops beside a house. All 3 saw it. Figured we would get a closer look when we come up on the location. When we got to location, it was gone. Disappeared. We tried debunking this and rode up and down the highway several times making sure of the location we saw it at. We were right and know what we saw. The 12 year old tries to say it was just a weather balloon. But there is no explanation for what we saw that day.
