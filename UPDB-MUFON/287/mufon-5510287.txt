(GS) Caught my eye while walking, chrome color, seemed large and intelegently controlled

I was walking to my friends house on a summer afternoon in the seventies when a chrome colored sphere very high in sky caught my eye by moving stragely for a baloon which is what I thought it was at first. I stopped and observed for about ten minutes until it ascended so high I could no longer see it.
