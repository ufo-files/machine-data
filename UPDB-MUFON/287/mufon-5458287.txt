{jpg} Dark object seen in first photo, but not in next two photos

We were on the main street of Virginia City NV, and I was taking pictures of church steeples with the mountains as a backdrop. I took three photos in succession, at 1:52pm, zooming in on the steeples with each photo, and the object only appeared in the first photo. I remember seeing something in the distant sky, but did not think much about it until reviewing the photos later in the evening.
Object is seen in photo 0368, just above the left cross. I zoomed on crosses for photos 369 and 370, which were both taken within seconds of first photo.
