Seen a golden light twice in one day

September 17 at exactly 2:15 pm I was driving home to Ukiah from working in Santa Rose which is about an hour south of Ukiah. While driving home on the 101 I seen a large gold light moving just about the trees on the west side of 101. This was during the daylight with a lot of people on the road. The long golden light traveled south west about five feet above the trees which was shocking to see. After driving home which took me about 20 minutes I asked myself what did I actually see. Once I returned home with my family I just brushed it off and told myself it must of been just a long day. That evening after putting my children down to sleep I step out my back door to smoke my last cigarette before heading to bed this is exactly 9:27 pm the same day. After lightning my cigarette the same wonderful golden bright light was just about my house and moving from west to east only about five feet above my house traveling down my street. It didn’t make any noise and continued to move slowly down my street and continued on that path until I couldn’t see it any more. I was very shaken by this experience and ran inside after it left. I haven’t talked to anyone about this experience nor have I spoken about my other two encounters I have had but I’m hoping to talk with someone who might have a few answers about this as I feel that it was really Close to me on three different occasions.
