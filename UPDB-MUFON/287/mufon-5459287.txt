{cak} Multiple Unidentifiable Craft Maneuvering through thunderstorm.

Monday 07/22/2019
19:52 to 20:35 observed multiple unidentifiable craft operating in various directions in various locations in the northern sky through a thunderstorm.
19:52HRS: Observed single cigar/torpedo shape hovering stationary directly North.
19:58HRS: Observed second cigar/toroedo shape craft hovering NNW direction while still observing initial craft to the North. This craft appeared to get struck directly by lightning with no effect.
20:04HRS: Observed same 2nd craft in NNW position pulsate light for approximately 6-7 seconds before turning off like a light bulb. Initial craft still hovering stationary.
20:09HRS: Observed same 2nd craft reappear in a different location approximately 500-1000ft from its original location at a lower altitude. 
20:16HRS: Observed 2nd craft ascend straight up at high rate of speed and out of sight.
20:18HRS: Observed 1st craft fly West approximately 5 seconds and then turned off like a light. As a lightning bolt lit up the clouds behind it, it cast a shadow. That was the funal sighting of this craft.
20:31HRS: Observed approx 7 craft appearing as tubular orange/yellow lights flying in a flexible formation heading due West to my Northwest. 
20:38HRS: Same 7 craft broke formation and gained altitude at different rates until out of sight.
