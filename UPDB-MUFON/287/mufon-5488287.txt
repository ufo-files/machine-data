{cak} A formation of Orange Orbs travelling together from West to East in the clear, starry sky.

I was with my 13-yr old granddaughter, heading home after dropping grandson at a friend's house. As we came to the intersection, I noticed several bright orange orbs in the sky, approaching from the West. I asked her if she saw what I saw, and asked her to describe what she was seeing. She saw the same things I saw. I stopped the car at the stop sign & got out. There was no sound from the objects. They were travelling in a loose formation, all at the same rather slow speed. There was sky and stars visible between the objects, so they did not appear to be connected. As they came right over us, one by one, they disappeared.
