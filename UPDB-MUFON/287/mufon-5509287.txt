{nd} After a storm, saw 2 big balls of orange/red light above treetops moving in a straight line towards the north

July 24th at around 1am, i went outside to pick up the tree debris that had landed in the street infront of our house when i saw 2 bright orbs of orange-red light coming from the south and heading north, about 2 blocks over from us. moving kindof slow then speeding up, but not severely fast, i could still see them for quite a while since there was no light from the streets.. The electricity had been knocked out and about an hour before, and all the transformers in our area blew due to the crazy storm that had blown thru about an hour an a half earlier.
