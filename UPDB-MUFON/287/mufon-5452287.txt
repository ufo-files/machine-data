Orbs Hovering in the Castro Valley/Hayward direction

Last night I was hanging out in my room on the second story of my house when I looked out the window. I have been watching the skies outside my window for at least 10 years, so I have seen helicopters, planes, jets passing through the area a bit. There were also 2 telephone towers in the distance behind it with red flashing lights that were not apart of this craft. This was very different, there were 3 orbs in an obtuse triangle formation to the southeast. I got the goosebumps and wanted to record them but was frozen with anxiety. So I ran downstairs to see if anyone was up? No one was so I ran back upstairs, at this point I looked back out the window and only one orb was there and was traveling north. I texted my sister to see if she was up so I ran back downstairs to tell her, then came back up and the orb was gone. This whole story took about 20 minutes from the time I saw the three orbs to the time I saw no orbs.
