{jpg} A gyroscope shaped craft floating

There were multiple sightings within a three month period as they seemed to appear closer and closer each time and in some cases “follow” me. This first sighting was a gyroscope-like object slowly moving across the sky near Cove beach in Stamford CT on February 16, 2011. I managed to grab my DSLR camera and get a few pictures of it changing shape before it swoops off the coast and disappeared. Then around 100 geese followed its path. What’s even more strange was there was barely any clouds in the sky, but hail started falling briefly just after it disappeared. A friend of mine recalls the day I went to his house to show him these pictures and the hail that came down. Very strange. 
As the next sightings occurred a month later, and then two months after that which I am writing my book about the whole experience where they finally came to me in my sleep and “connected” to me as if to observe the world and collect data for them while they allowed me to learn and absorb so much information in a short period of time and function like a machine as if being pushed by them. But for now this is the first physical sighting of a string of events but I have to search for the photos on my laptop.
