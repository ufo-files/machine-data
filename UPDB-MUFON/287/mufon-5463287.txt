{cak} Large bright blue orb, smoke ring, beam of light to the ground.

It was November 2015. I was in the Army and was doing training in the deserts of Ft Irwin California. About 10 at night at my squad was on the side of a mountain overlooking a valley. We saw a small orange ball moving across the sky. It stopped above the valley and then expanded to a large very light blue bright orb. It shot out a perfect smoke ring. A few moments later a beam of light went down to the ground that lasted a few mins. After about 20 mins it went back down to the original small orange orb and started moving towards to mountain to our right. We got out our night vision and watched it take a hard left turn and then a right turn to go around the mountain. Even the guys stationed there had never seen anything like it. It was totally silent the whole time. The next day they told us it was a missile but there is no missile in existence that can do what it did.
