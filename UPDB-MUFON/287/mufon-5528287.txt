Strange Star-Like Craft Hovering Over Boeing Field In Redmond, WA

In July of 2007, I was with my co worker Justin cleaning up the grounds at the Mercer Island Beach Club. We were outside and I happened to look towards Boeing Field in the east. There was a strange light that seemed to be hovering over Boeing Field. It was a Star-like shape. I thought it seemed odd and pointed it out to my friend Justin. He agreed with me. Then, about 15 minutes later, I saw the light, but it was getting smaller and smaller. About a half hour later, I pointed towards the light, and told Justin to come take a look at this. The light, that was once the size of a star, was now extremely small. Justin and I watched it until it dimmed out. The light just disappeared from sight. Justin and I looked at each other dumbfounded. We were speechless the rest of the night.
