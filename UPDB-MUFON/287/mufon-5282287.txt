I seen a 4 story objects the width of a football field travelling slowing across the lake.

I was in the boreal forest in my remote cottage at Batwing Lake. I woke up to stock up the fireplace and get the fire going. While waiting for the fire to catch, I went to the patio door which faces North to have a look at the stars. When i looked out the door I saw a large object over the lake. I yelled for my boyfriend who was sleeping and he got dressed and came out. We both watched the object slowly moving away in a Eastern direction. We both went out the door and stood on the deck watching it until it disappeared. The object didn't appear to be making any noise but we could hear a pulp log truck coming down the road. The object was approximately 4 stories tall and as wide as a football field. It was rectangular in appearance and had what appeared to be 4 rows of windows from side to side. There was light emitting from every window and it had a greenish glow.
