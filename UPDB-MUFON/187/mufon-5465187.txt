Very bright orange light hovering over germany, dissapears in less than 10 seconds after sighting

The object was a distinct orange colour in the northern sky, 20-301E of the big dipper. When I first saw the light it appeared to have an apparent magnotude of around -1.0 (could've been brighter) and around 5-10 seconds after the initial sighting it was too dim to see. The light did not move in any way, it stayed stationary over the northern sky at a significant distance and the light was far brighter than any neighboring stars.
