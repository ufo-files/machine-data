I was on an afternoon flight from Dallas to LAX. I think we were somewhere near the Arizona, California border. We were flying above and in the clouds, and couldn’t see the ground. It was beautiful so I was enjoying the view from the right side of the plane. I noticed a distinct solid looking white triangle, directly to the north, and level with us. It was the same white as the clouds, but without shadow. I say triangle but it’s base was hidden by clouds. For a size reference, another airliner was lower than our plane, and less than halfway between us and the ufo. From my perspective the ufo was at least twice as big as the plane; so it was actually much larger. I was so interested in watching it I didn’t think to mention it to anyone else. As I watched, it slowly pivoted from the diagonal edge facing west to a straight line up and down. It continued to pivot until the diagonal line was facing east.
