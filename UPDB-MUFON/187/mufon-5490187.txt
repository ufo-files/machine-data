{cak} Light in the night sky - ahead - right - left - then appeared close

While driving along an unpopulated segment of a turnpike, myself and my passenger's eyesight both were drawn to a bright light in the sky directly up in the sky in front of us; suddenly it disappeared and suddenly reappeared to our front right at an alarming speed for the distance it covered; it disappeared and reappeared again to our front left; then it disappeared and reappeared directly to our left at treetop level. The light emitted from it did not light up the ground coverage below it but was self contained. It was a soft glowing beautiful disk that seemed to float in its own cloud. It remained as I pulled over to "behold" it.  My passenger on the other hand cried out from fear ordering me to close my car window which I had opened to gain a better view. She yelled "Hurry, I don't want to be beckoned!" which startled me. She insisted I drive on and she started to cry because she had wet her beautiful dress pants and was terribly embarrassed and fearful. As I drove on, I immediately glanced several times in my rear view mirrors and twisted to turn around for another look and it had again disappeared as I saw nothing. I felt it necessary to stop at the next turnpike rest area and report the sighting. I attempted to do so but was told by the cashier before I had a chance to explain ... "Oh, you saw a UFO... people see them all the time around here." So, we felt we were discarded and left. We drove on to our destination and together verbally verified all we had seen and it was identical. We spoke of it only to one another over the years and our experience remained consistent and our feelings remained certain.
