{cak} Small, white orb observed

On Sunday, September 15, 2019, at 4:00PM from my backyard I observed an object that I cannot explain. The day at that time was sunny, very light clouds, and light winds from west to east. I was relaxing a bit after doing yard work, sitting in the shade under my oak tree, facing south. When I happened to look up that is when I saw the object, first seeing it at 11:00 to my position. It appeared to be the size of a softball, white, traveling west to east about 40 to 50 feet altitude. It was traveling at a constant speed, the light wind was not affecting its speed or course, and it made no noise whatsoever. It took approximately 5 seconds for it to cross my field of vision, until I lost it in the blue sky. It did not change course or altitude during the time I was observing it.
