While taking digital pics of the town square for the holidays on Dec. 02,2011 I saw a quick flash that appeared like a shooting star only moving a bit slower.It had a puplish blue light and was moving fast.I only caught it for a few quick seconds,but noticed it's color and that it did have a tail of sorts as it moved.It was out of sight on a 4 count.It was very bright and left a bright trail behind it and that quickly dissipated as fast as the object disappeared.When we examined our pics there were two images of the object with the buildings in the Gettysburg Square as persepective and the object as it moved in two different pics.It was gone as quickly as it came.Moved so quickly we could not follow it.
