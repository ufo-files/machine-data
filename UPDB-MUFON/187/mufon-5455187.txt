Triangle formation of lights followed by a string of 20 lights in a line.

I saw a large triangle formation of lights followed by about 20 or so of them in a line. Like a giant arrow moving thru the morning sky in the early morning. Stars were still visible as this formation moved from the NW to SE and vanished at 20 degrees. The lights were a crisp golden white light a little brighter than stars.
