{nd} Several objects observed in SE sky firing on each other over a period of two hours

I took my dog out to do his job at 6:30 PM, they appeared to be in battle between two against one and then one of the two split apart into two objects and continued firing at each other as I retuned into the house. When I took a bag of trash out at about 8{30 PM there was only two objects fighting, I am not sure what happened between the third object and the other two. They may have merged back together again while I was in my house. The objects were darting about the sky and firing beams of lights at each other. They also lowered themselves down when a regular airplane passed over The airplane paid no attention to the other objects in the sky They appeared to be shooting upwards as well as each other.
