{nd} UFO'S UNDER THE VEGAS MOON

ON JULY 14, 2014 AT ABOUT 3:00AM IN LASVEGAS, NEVADA, I WAS WALKING TO MY CAR FROM A NIGHTCLUB. I WAS STANDING IN FRONT OF THE TROPICANA HOTEL AND LOOKED UP FOR SOME REASON TOWARDS THE MOON WHICH WAS PEAKING OUT FROM A SKY FULL OF CLOUDS. I SAW FOUR CIRCULAR GREEN LIGHTS FLYING IN AND OUT OF THE CLOUDS ABOVE. THREE SEEMED TO BE IN A TRIANGULAR PATTERN WHILE ONE TRAILED BEHIND. I WATCHED FOR ABOUT A MINUTE OR TWO BEFORE I PULLED OUT MY IPHONE 5 AND STARTED TO RECORD WHAT I WAS SEEING. THE LIGHTS CHANGED COLOR TO MORE OF A ORANGE COLOR AND KEPT FLYING WITH THE TRIANGLE PATTERN SEEMING TO GET BIGGER AND SMALLER. I REMEMBER MY EYES SEEING SO MUCH MORE THAN WHAT THE VIDEO ON MY PHONE WAS GETTING. I WISH I HAD A BETTER VIDEO CAMERA BUT ASSUME YOU GUYS HAVE BETTER TECHNOLOGY TO TAKE MY VIDEO AND PULL IN MORE FOCUS OF WHAT I GOT ON MY PHONE. AS I UPLOADED THIS VIDEO ON MY COMPUTER IT'S VERY HARD TO MAKE OUT WHAT ON MY PHONE IS MORE CLEARER. THE LIGHTS SEEM TO DISAPPEAR IN THE CLOUDS AND SO I GOT IN MY CAR AND CALLED MY GIRLFRIEND TO LET HER KNOW WHAT I HAD JUST SEEN. TYPICALLY AIRPLANES AND HELICOPTERS HAVE TWO OR THREE DIFFERENT LIGHTS AND I SEE THEM EVERYDAY FROM THE BUSY LAS VEGAS AIRPORT. THESE LIGHTS WHERE COMPLETELY DIFFERENT AND WENT FROM ONE SOLID COLOR TO ANOTHER WITH A FLYING PATTERN LIKE A FEW FLIES.
