{jpg} 3 starlike objects descend down then move right

I live in a rural area 30 minutes east of Austin. I walked outside with my dog and looked up at the moon at exactly 7:26pm on Jan 6 2020. Then I look to the eastern sky then back towards the moon and notice a bright object starlike an inch to the right of Venus and 4 inches below the moon if you hold your hand out at arms length and it was moving to my right. I thought it was a satellite, so I watched it as I always do when I notice a satellite, and it moved to the right about 4 inches in 4 seconds roughly and it’s light went out. Then another starlike object appeared in the same area as the first one but this one I saw it actually appear and it was moving down about a total of two inches as if it was entering our atmosphere from space and it made a 90 degree turn to the right in the same direction and general area as the first one and after 3 seconds it’s light went off, and I’m freaked out now and a third object dropped down and mimicked the second object and It turned to the right and it’s light went off after 3 seconds, at this point I realize I’m witnessing something bazaar and run in the house and grabbed my phone to try and video this but no further objects appeared and the three couldn’t be seen since they went dark. The time is exact since I have a time stamp from making a call to family immediately after I witnessed these crafts. All 3 were star like and brighter than all other stars in the sky. No commercial aircraft were in the sky in my viewing range and these objects appeared to be entering our atmosphere descending straight down then turned to the right at a 90 degree.
