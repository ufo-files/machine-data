A very Dim Triangular collection of circular light (orange/redish). No sound.

I was in my backyard with my wife, she was smoking I was talking about our day. I was facing West looking at the stars when I saw some dim lights come into my field of view. It was very dim, so I was not sure if I was just "seeing things" but after a second or two it was clear that there was something in the sky moving southish, slowly and making no sound. I exclaimed to my wife "What is that! Look!". She turned around and she was it too, thank goodness! We watched it move southish and I lost sight of it, my wife saw it a bit longer and lost sight of it. A few seconds later she picked it up again but it flashed(dimly) and she lost it again.
