Object seen in sky

On Ryanair flight FR 3812 from ACE to LPL on 16/11/21 approx 1pm. 10yr son witnessed an object through plane window from seat no. 20A. No window smudges etc.. he spotted another airplane in distance and watched it + filmed it. Then he noticed the object in the sky which he took a photo of. We showed this to the flight crew who had no idea what it could be.
