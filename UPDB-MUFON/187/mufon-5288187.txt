Bright flash outside my window made me look, it was hovering right above my apartment, then wobbled to the side, and descended in a curved line

I was on my couch thinking about UFOs in my head, and there was a bright flash outside my window. I looked, and there was a bright object floating above my apartment. I went to my balcony to get a closer look. I brushed it off to be a plane, but then it quickly moved to the side and back. It blinked again, moved forward slowly. Then quickly descended into the neighborhood below me (I'm on the 9th floor). It vanished behind some buildings that are 3 stories tall. The buildings it went behind are 2 streets down from where I was. It looked like a tiny star, or glowing orb. Hard to tell the size but I think it was about the size of an exercise ball when it went behind the buildings. I started believing that UFOs exist 1 year ago, and have been mentally thinking in my head that I wanted to see one. I believe it specifically came to me and blinked outside my apartment to show themselves to me. I'm in a pretty populated area, but I didn't see anyone else outside at the time who could've noticed. The whole encounter lasted maybe 8 seconds. I was so excited and thankful to see it at the time, and still am, but I now am having anxiety and a hard time sleeping at night because I can't stop thinking about it.
