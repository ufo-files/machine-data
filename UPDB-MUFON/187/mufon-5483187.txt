{dr}Silver wavering object seen just west of Antwerp on U.S. 24.

I was driving home from Fort Wayne, Indiana on U.S. 24. I was heading east and the object I witnessed was to the northeast. It was a silver object that appeared long, vertically. It appeared to fade in and out as though it were passing through clouds, which there were clouds in the sky. The silver was very bright due to sunlight hitting it. It was visible for a very short period of time. It appeared to be moving northward along a straight path.
