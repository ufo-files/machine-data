This sighting took place over two nights. First night 5 UFO's were seen, two different types. The second night 1 seen night before and a different type. September 5th and 6th, 2016
Monday, September 5th, 2016 at approx 10:15 PM-EST
Walking just out front of my home, late in the evening on September 5th, 2016, I witnessed a huge cylinder craft come flying in from the north. Firth one, then another, with an egg shaped craft tailing behind, as if it were an escort. After calling my sister and telling her of the event, another cylinder craft flew in, followed by another egg shaped craft. 
The cylinders were multi-colored, with light blues, greens, yellows and orange but they were unlike the colors we see coming from an airplane, in that they were softer, glowing lightly around the edges while bright in the center. The colors looked like plasma rolling all around the craft as they flew in from the north. Just above the tree-lines and buildings along I19 headed South. From my view the cylinder crafts were approximately 80-100 ft in length. 
The egg shaped craft lights were glowing in the same way but were all white and much smaller. Approximately 20-30 ft round.
The first craft flew in, then the second craft just seconds behind it. Then an egg shaped craft followed behind them.
The whole experience lasted about 8-10 minutes.
With the call to my sister taking maybe 2-3 minutes, then another cylinder craft came in and then another egg shaped craft.
Tuesday, September 6th, 2016 approx 10:15 PM-EST
Moving in from the north, this craft first came in like a huge comet. All white, looked to be a comet that could of wiped out the earth if it had hit! It had to be more then 1000 ft, came in like a ball of energy while trailing the light behind it.
Right before my eye, this huge comet transitioned into a very large craft, it could of been miles in length, that had points of lights that looked like stars, seven that I could see and they formed a craft that had multiple triangles that resembled a photon. It moved in the same flight pattern as the crafts the night before, moving north to south along I19.
Within seconds a smaller egg shaped craft followed behind in a pattern just west of the craft but moving south as well.
Over the two night period I witnessed, seven crafts in total, with 3 different UFO's. The cylinder, egg shaped and pointed star, all three of which I have never seen before.
The lights did not burn the same as an airplanes, they were dim yet brilliantly glowing. I tried to capture a video the second night, to which the screen is black, no reflection of the massive UFO.
