{cak} Witnessed 5 pulses of lights then disappeared.

Me and three of my friends were outside on my driveway when we noticed an orb or sphere glowing. I was the first to see it then it glowed around four more times before disappearing. When it would glow it seemed as thought it was powering up because it would start off faint then it would get bright then fall off again. Each episode lasted around 15 seconds. On the last and brightest episode I did notice a small red/orange light color located inside the orb. The object didn't seem to move and if it did it was slight but it way up in the sky so it was hard to tell if it moved.
