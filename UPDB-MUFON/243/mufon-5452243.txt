Grey Dome shaped, just hovered there for at least 20 mins. Didn't appear to have lights, but it was day time so I may not have been able to see them.

My mother had been killed in a car crash a few weeks before this happened and I was cleaning out her house. At first i thought what is that, a drone, but no it would have been to large had it been closer to me and it didn't look like any drone I'd ever seen before as it was all grey, saucer round with a dome. Why I didn't take a picture I'm not sure, for some reason it didn't seem to surprise me at all that it was there. Nor did it seem to care that I saw it. It was afternoon in the fall time so it was daylight. I would go in and out of the house taking care of things and it had to have hoovered there in the same spot at least 20 to 30 minutes. I then realized it made no sound at all. Then it was gone after a while. This was not my first encounter, my mother worked 50 miles away in Montgomery, AL and we often drove home late at night. Twice we were follow in our car by some kind of craft and once in shined a spotlight down on top of the car. I was very small , but I remember being very scared and getting down in the floor board. I think my mother had more encounters when I wasn't with her and she thought it was awesome. Next time I will take a picture, but for some reason I didn't feel compelled to do so this time. It was strange all the way around. By the way we live in the country so I'm not sure anyone else would have noticed it at that time.
