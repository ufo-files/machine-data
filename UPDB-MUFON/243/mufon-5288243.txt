Observed 6 evenly spaced lights in a row at what appeared high altitude

I was getting mail from my mailbox at approximately 9:03 p.m. I dont know why I looked up just when the object was passing. I look at the sky a lot since Im very interested in astronomy. When I first saw the object I noticed 6 evenly spaced lights moving at a uniform speed from southwest to northeast. My impression was that it was a single object becasue of the light spacing and uniform speed of the lights. The object(s) were silent and seem to be at high altitude. A commercial aircraft flew lower than the object and in the opposite direction(west). I cant say what it was but I can say what it was not. It was dark outside with very good seeing.To use the star Arcturus as a comparison I would say the lights appeared roughly twice the angular diameter of Arcturus with roughly twice the magnitude of Arcturus. The lights were solid white, no flashing or blinking. There were no flashing strobes or green/red lights as seen on typical aircraft. I estimate the length of the lights from the first to the last at about 20 degrees. Two fist widths held at arms length. I ran into the house to get my wife but when we got back outside the lights were still heading northeast and fading out of view quickly.
