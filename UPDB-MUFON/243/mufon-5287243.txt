We saw a very bright disc that was spinning very fast that would flicker out then show up miles away

I let my dog outside at around 11;20 p.m. and saw what I thought was a fire so I went to get my husband to see what he thought it was and when I got back it had moved and was high in the sky. The object moved irrationally and very fast and was spinning like a disc at an incredible speed. The object would get very bright almost like it exploded then would zip away. It would flicker out then reappear miles away. There was no sound at all, I have never seen anything like it in my life. I’ve thought of every explanation possible but there really is none.
