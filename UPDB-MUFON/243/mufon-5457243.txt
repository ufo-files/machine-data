Driving home and see some strange lights in the sky

I was driving home from grays essex on the a127 london bound. My fiancee noticed some lights in the sky that was extremely low. We immediately pulled the car over and saw what looked like a rectangular flying object hovering above the old hotel on the opposite side of the road. It's light where changing from blue to red to green as it stayed stationery for approximately 10 seconds. I reached for my phone and as I put the camera on the flying object turned away from us. I started snapping pictures on my phone as it slowly disappeared into the clouds and then vanished
