UFO came from west and headed east then changed to north

I sat down at my couch at 1am. I looked out the window facing south when I saw a trianle shaped object in the sky. It had white lights on the bottom of it - maybe 6 white lights and one red blinking light in the rear. I followed it out my dining room window - facing out to the east and watched it turn and go north.
