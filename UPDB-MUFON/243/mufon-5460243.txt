{cak} Silver star light

Just got off the 5BX bus on Country CLub road and began my walk home over a small walk bridge onto Jarvis Ave, it was a clear blue sky. At about 1:14 pm to 1:16 pm I noticed a Sliver Point of Light going very slow high up, strangely, it made a stop and hovered, it felt like it noticed I was observing it and stood fixed hovering a few seconds with no movement. It appeared like a sliver star like object, at times I could see a potential out line type structured object but the sun was too bight and at times I saw an outlining of dark lines from the object. Yet the light emanating from the object appeared itself as if to cover its self from being fully seen. It then shifted as if headed to Manhattan. It just felt as it wanted me to know it was there. Weird feeling.
