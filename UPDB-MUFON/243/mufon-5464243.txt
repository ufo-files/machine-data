HUGE SLOW MOVING AIRCRAFT OVER 1000 FEET WIDE PASSED OVER THE ROAD I WAS DRIVING ON LIGHTS UNDERSIDE NO NOISE

I WAS DRIVING EAST ON THE A45 DUAL CARRIAGEWAY FROM NORTHAMPTON TOWARDS EARLS BARTON I SAW LIGHTS IN THE SKY WAS NOT SURE WHAT IT WAS BUT IT WAS HUGE LOTS OF SMALL LIGHTS UNDERSIDE MOVING SLOWLY AROUND 50/70 MPH I GOT MY FRIENDS ATTENTION WHO WAS IN ANOTHER CAR TO LOOK UP HE SAW IT, THE CRAFT PASSED OVER US HEADING TOWARDS EARLS BARTON VILLAGE, I HAD TO TURN OF TO THE VILLAGE AND WAS HEADING RIGHT TOWARDS THE CRAFT THAT HAD STOPPED AND HOVERED OVER A GRASS FIELD A WIDE BRILLIANT WHITE PIERCING BEAM SHONE DOWN, I RE ROUTED TURNED AND WENT AWAY FROM IT FRIGHTENED.
