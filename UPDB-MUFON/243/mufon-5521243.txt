On my way to work sometime in the middle of October I observed a ufo orb ascend from a tree line about 2 miles from my house. It hovered for 30sec then continue to rise it reflected off the clouds and as it entered the clouds it was bright enough the clouds lit up then it was gone. It made no sound but then again i was atleast 2 1/2 miles away from it. I would never have noticed it if I hadnt seen a deer that was running toward the road in the field I was passing.
