Objects falling near the moon.

While filming the moon during daylight hours, at approximately 75 degree angle from my position. using an iPhone 11 Pro Max, utilizing Celestron 25x100 SkyMaster Binoculars for magnification. Several videos where shot for approximately two minutes each at normal speed, I then decided to use the slow motion video capture setting. This video was approximately 3 minutes in length, I did not observe anything on the iPhone screen during initial filming. Later while looking at the video I observed two objects falling in the vertical plane perpendicular to the moon. These objects where visible without zooming in on the video. The objects fell at 20 second intervals. The speed of the objects was not congruent to the speed at which the video was being shot. They moved at a tremendous velocity and only lasted on screen for a few seconds. This should have been impossible due to the slow motion capture speed. I believe this rules out a balloon, birds, planes or bugs in front of the binocular lens. The object appears to change shape during the event. The time stamps for the objects is 1:30-1:37 and 1:50-1:56. There Is also a flight path change from the top of the frames to the bottom frames.
