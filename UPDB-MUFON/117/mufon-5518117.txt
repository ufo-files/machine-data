Ball of light heading towards north west buffalo.

I was at work cleaning the store parking lot when I noticed a red pulsating ball of light above the buildings in front of me. It was not directly above them, they just happened to be blocking the view for the most part. after a few moments, it was in full view and heading at a relatively low speed to the north west. I thought it may be a helicopter rotor light, there where no other lights on the object/sphere however. The light did seem to be a fairly dark red but was lighter near the edges, with a gentle blending of said shades via the pulsating. The outermost edges where rather blurry but it was 1am in the morning and there where florescent lights behind me, this may have played a trick on my eyesight. I am sure that there where no strobe lights. Perhaps it was a flare of some sort, I do not know.
