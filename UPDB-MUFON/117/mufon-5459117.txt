Not visible to naked eye or audible

Mid afternoon while visiting Machu Picchu we were at the "sun gate "overlooking the archeological site. It was broad daylight and clear skys. I decided to take a couple selfies. I took the two photos only 1 or 2 seconds apart. There were about 10 people all looking out over the city and no one heard or seen a thing. Later that evening while reviewing the photos I saw what appeared to be a condor which amazed me because we had not seen anything the when day even though great visibility. When I blew up the photo it was very evident this was no bird. As yet no persons from Peru or other have been able to give certain identification to the object.
