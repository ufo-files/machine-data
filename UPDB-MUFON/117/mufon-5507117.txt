Craft flies into cloud then crashes

Driving down Christian De Wet towards Fourways my kids and I saw a craft flying so fast that it left 2 x trails of a substance that looked like clouds but went in a straight line. Craft shot up into a cloud at about 85 degrees. My kids took photos and kept me updated because I could not stop the car. They said that it looked like the diamond shinny craft was knocked out of the could by something. It crashed to the ground. Later I took photos of a spiral cloud touching the ground in the exact spot where the craft would have crashed. So this spiral cloud came out of a cloud and touched the earth.
