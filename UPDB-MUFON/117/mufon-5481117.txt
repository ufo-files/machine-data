{nd} Comet like fireball with yellowish blue tail and white head.

I was driving a bus westward on Powell and SE 72nd at 11:50pm, saw what looked like a firework streaming across the sky low from south to north but it was way too big and slow. No sound, tail was huge and long with a bluefish yellow color like the flame of a gas stove. Object flew low. Felt panic and surprise looked like the fire and chemtrail of a rocket. Object went north over tree line.
