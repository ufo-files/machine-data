{nd} The craft seemed to have a bright white propulsion system along with changing light colors

The following event took place at night over Disney world. I noticed some red glowing lights that were able to change in number, orientation and even appeared to merge back together. The pattern of lights consisted of 1 to 4 separate but equal sized lights. There were definitely no sounds associated with that of a blimp, plane or helicopter. The lights were able to hover and were obviously under intelligent control. I photographed the light objects with a mediocre H5 Sony Camera. I believed that all I would see were some blurry lights on the computer monitor. Much to my surprise, there was also a clear outline of what appeared to be a craft. The apparent craft also seemed to have a propulsion system. The propulsion system seemed to be emitting a white energy or light. There were other strange objects in the digital image that I could not detect in the field. I can only explain the phenomenon as some sort of electrical disturbance perhaps a little like connected, irregular loops of dancing energy. These energy loops were irregular in shape and appeared in different colors. These energy loops were visible on both sides of the craft and the red light objects. I thought these light loops were the result of camera movement. Then I rationalized that if movement had produced the colored loops of energy, then why were they different colors? Why did the craft and red colored lights remain in sharp focus?
