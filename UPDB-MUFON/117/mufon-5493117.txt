{nd} 7 or 8 lights hovering and changing positions. Then taking off to the North over Mountains.

I had just got into my car and drove to my work vehicle where my paper work was located. I usually do this when I work after hours. In the sky I noticed something. Instantly I thought it was some  helicopter activity. But after a second or so I realized it was something else I was witnessing. I was watching 7 to 8 lights in the North West direction over Mt. Wilson just North of Sierra Madre.The lights were a yellow gold color. Similar to the color of fog lights that usually light beach cities. The lights changed position and seemingly switch places but collectively within a specific air space. After a few seconds of this the lights begin accelerating at a high speed in the Northern direction disappearing over the mountains. I was shocked and couldn't believe my eyes.
