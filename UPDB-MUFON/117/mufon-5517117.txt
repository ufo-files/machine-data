{dr}5 high 3 wide floating dull black object with single red light on bottom seemed to be aware of me watching it

On 7/5/12 at approximately 8 PM, I stopped my car in front of my house to shift into reverse so I could back into my driveway and garage. Floating over a nieghbors house across the street about 50 away and 50 off the ground was an oval shaped dull black object approximately 5 high and 3 wide with a single solid red light on the bottom. I got out of my car to look at it and it seemed to hesitate as if it was aware of me watching it. After about 30 seconds I got back in my car to retrieve my cell phone to try to take a video of the object. By the time I had got my phone out and ready to record (a few seconds) the object had moved Northeast and was now approximately 1,000 away and was moving fast. I backed my car into my garage and then saw a black helicopter speeding by toward the direction of the object.
