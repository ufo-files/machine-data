Something weird happened

One night at my house I was sleeping I was awakened with this bright red light coming through my window through my blinds. I did not thing much of it, so I went back to bed. The next morning one of my teeth was missing! At my dentist office they said that they extracted one of my teeth when I had no recollection of them doing that. The night that happened I remember that there was this light in my face and someone says, open your fu**** mouth! I do not believe they were extraterrestrials; it could be military craft doing some type of experiment or some other type of significance. I have no other explanation.
