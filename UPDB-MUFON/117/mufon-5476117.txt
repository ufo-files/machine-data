{jpg} Strange object sitting in sky-went straight up and disappeared-got photo

On Wednesday, May 31, 2017, about 3:00 PM, I was in my car going through the Braham's drive through waiting to pick up my food. I noticed a very bright object in the sky across the street directly in front of me. It was very high in the cloudy sky and was quite a way away. I am very bad with judging distance. It appeared to me to be a lighted cylinder standing on end and appeared to be still. It looked very large or at least that was my impression.It also appeared that the light from the object was casting a reflection on the clouds around it. I grabbed my phone and took a photo through my front car window. My food came and there was someone behind me who was tapping his car horn for me to go. I pulled around into the parking lot to get a better photo with the car window down and, while I was pulling around, the object went straight up and disappeared in the blink of an eye. I have no guess as to what it could have been. The photo I got isn't great but there is a building in the photo that gives some scale.
