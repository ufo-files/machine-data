{cak} 2 Bright lights traveling very fast with one speeding up to the other, disappeared & the other continued straight

Outside with 2 friends loading up a truck at approximately 9pm Central time. It was still dusk. We were all facing SE. I saw two bright white lights approaching swiftly, heading NW in what appeared to be a formation,in tandem. At first I thought they were helos (helicopters) as they appeared that low to the ground but were moving at a speed unattainable by helos. I asked "What are those lights?" One friend looked up and remarked "I don't know!". At that moment the light on our left appeared to change velocity and meet the light on the right either parallel or directly to the aft at which point it simply disappeared. The light on the right continued straight and also seemed to abruptly dissappear. Both friends commented on the remarkable speed. There was no downward movement one would expect from a meteorite, no arching and no fading. In addition no sound whatsoever and no universal aircraft lighting (red & green). I am still in awe of the "formation", height from the ground, change in velocity and the fact they appeared to travel directly over us.
