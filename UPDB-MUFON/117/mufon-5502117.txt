{dr}Intense red object looked like a mushroom or umbrella on top

I was just getting my second cup of coffee, as I turned around I saw this very intense light out my front window. It was a red object hovering over the lake. It was very VERY RED! It was not moving, but was very bright. It looked like a mushroom or umbrella shaped object. The "Stem" seemed to be touching the water, and was very bright red, but not as bright as the object. In those few seconds I was trying to make sense of what it could be. I was trying to see if it could be a reflection or light from someone elses house. There is no way it could be. It was the size of about 2 SUV's. This was amazing! I stood there for about 10 seconds, stunned. Then I called out to my boyfriend to come look, never taking my eyes off of it. He was sleeping, it took him a few seconds to come out to look. By the time he got there it was gone. It didn't fly, it just disappeared. The whole thing lasted about 20-30 seconds. We both stood there for a while to see if it might return. It didn't. I was not scared, I was very curious. I drew a picture right away, as to not miss any detail. I'm 51 years old, and absolutely positive of what I saw. I have never seen anything like this before in my life!! I have never seen the color red so intense. Wow.
