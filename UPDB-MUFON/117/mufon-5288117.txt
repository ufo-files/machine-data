While camping a bright light seemed to descend on us, with loud pulsating noise. I got out of the tent and saw 3 "people", blond hair, ~100 feet from me, dressed in what looked like Roman clothing. As soon as I noticed them, the 3 turned to me. I froze, and felt like I'd seen something that I should not have. They looked very startled. I popped back in tent to wake up boyfriend, and that's when we heard the pulsating noise and the bright lights ascend, straight up. When I stuck my head out of the tent, the "people" and light was gone. We were camped off a narrow forest service road, no other people around.
