Boomerang-like craft Close Up Encounter

It was approx mid Dec 2014 My son (then 18) and myself were on our way home to Long Beach - I pulled off the freeway at the Orange exit onto the access road, 32nd st that is parallel with an Elementary school on the right. Over the school playground was a 200-300ft across - boomerang-like craft, silent, with 3 lights on the underside of each wing that alternated in blue white blue and where the boomerang should have bent for the nose of the craft, it was grey mist/energy looking. It hovered at approximately tree top level less than 100 feet from my son and I. 
The craft rolled side to side slowly, almost moving in slow motion, rolling toward us so we could see the underneath then rolling the other way so we could see the top and back again - almost as if it was stabilizing? After settling flat, it slowly moved away, lights then appeared in the back on each side at the end of each wing- light flying low over houses, alongside the freeway. We watched it until it was out of sight. 
A news van was on the same street with us, seemingly oblivious., the rest of the street was empty I noticed as we drove slowly away. It was half a mile up Orange before the world came rushing back in. I felt I had been in some kind of bubble until then.
