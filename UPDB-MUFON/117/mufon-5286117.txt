I was closing my barn about 10pm and saw a bright blink of light and observed this blinking 6 times in 6 seconds traveling horizon to horizon

At approx 10:10 pm I was closing my barn and saw a bright white blink just above the eastern horizon I watched that spot to see if it would happen again. It did but it had traveled a good distance before blinking again. It did this 4 more times before going past the western horizon. The blinks were the same timing between each blink. The object traveled in a straight line, no discernible arc or wobble in the travel. The object appeared approx 55 degrees in the sky directly in front of me.
