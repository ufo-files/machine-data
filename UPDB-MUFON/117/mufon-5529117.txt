Several very distant lights interacting with one another forming differnt shapes

My husband and I were getting in the car and I looked up and saw five small lights blinking in and out. My attention was drawn right away because I love to look at the stars and this was strange. They were very far away, it was a strain to watch them but it was neat in a scary sort of way. They would make circles, zig zag, blink out, and then blink back on. At one point it appeared other lights came, but they then disappeared. Five lights throughout and three seemed to join in and then disappear. We watched them for some time and then checked and found them again when we reached our destination. They were very coordinated in their actions.
