{jpg} 15-20 Bright orbs over Avila Beach

After a night at work on Avila Beach. I was walking to my car when a family of 3, two parents and a young child, stopped me and said "hey do you know what those lights are?". I stopped looked up and noticed 15-20 bright white lights all moving in various directions around each other. I watched them intelligently move around each other for about 5-10 min. Then two of the brightest orbs gathered all of the other orbs into a straight line and took them east disappearing into space.
