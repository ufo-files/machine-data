{cak} Two black triangle craft observed in West Texas.

10:30 pm west of Marfa, TX I observed two craft fly from the north to south. Each craft had 3 white steady lights that formed points of a triangle. Each craft also had a red blinking light in the middle. Once both craft were to the south of me they began to follow each other at a very low altitude and a high rate of speed heading northeast. I have video and screenshots of one of the observed craft. 

I had to make the video black and white and zoom in to see all the lights of craft. The original color video is also included.
