{cak} viewed large object 30 miles across san luis valley in same spot 2 nights in a row just above the mountains

I happened to look out of a window in my home and saw an unusual large light sitting above the mountains, approximately 30 miles across the san luis valley from my living space in Crestone , Co. on July 29, 2013. I grabbed a 10x50 bushnell binocular and saw pulsating red / orange colors for approx. 25 minutes after which it faded away in a matter of seconds.
The above happened between 9 and 10 pm
Same thing happened on July 30th, at 9:30pm
I thought the light was a planet until it disappeared in a clear sky.
The object stayed in the same place the entire time I was able to observe it, being so far away I could only see the colors and the pulsating of.
