Glowing green orb

While walking my dogs at the Arkansas Riverwalk on Wednesday January 13th, 2021 at 4 pm, my one dog sat down and refused to walk. She does this when we cross paths with mountain lions or bears. When I surveyed the immediate area I saw a large green orb in the direction of the Bluff trail ( not by the river). It was close to ground level and started to rise, then changed course going to its left and towards us. As it approached my other dog started howling and barking, and my dog that had sat down was shaking in fear. I didn’t hear anything, or smell anything either. When my dog started howling it paused, then changed course again back towards the trees, dropping to ground level. It seemed to grow in size again, then shrank, seeming to wink out of existence. My dogs refused to walk any further on that trail, so we doubled back so I could walk them on the actual Riverwalk trail. There are no signs or structures in the vicinity to reflect from the setting sun. So no idea what it was, but it sure scared my dogs, and that’s pretty hard to do.
