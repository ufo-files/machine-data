{kt}White orb high in the sky near a lightning storm

This was back in 2008, it was the summer and my friend and I had just come back to my house after seeing a movie, I forget which movie it was. It was late July and we were standing out on my front lawn. There was a heat lightning storm going on in the sky over the north-east. I looked up and saw a craft moving across the horizon from the east to north. It was nothing more than a white orb-light, like a high flying jet. I initially thought nothing of it. But it vanished suddenly just above the treeline. It vanished a bit above the treeline. I thought that was strange and I asked my friend if he saw that. He shrugged his shoulders, he hadn't been looking at the sky. We went back to talking. About five minutes later, the heat lightning storm got bigger. I saw a white orb-light again. It was above the treeline. (The orb was kilometers off in the distance from me.) Suddenly it shout straight up in the air, vanished (or teleported?, reappeared next to the lightning storm and started bopping about in sharp angles that no aircraft could make. After bopping around in the night sky for about twenty seconds, it vanished into the storm as the lightning got stronger. I bid my friend good night as we parted ways before the storm approached. Afterwards I felt that it was pretty cool to have seen a UFO, as for my actions and feelings during the event, I was rather focused on my conversation with my friend, not that I can remember what that conversation was about.
