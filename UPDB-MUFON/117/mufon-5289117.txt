Unusual flight path for meteor

3 of us are working away from home. We got to the hotel and walked to get food from services on M62 in Blackburn UK. Walking back I thought I spotted a meteor, traveling west to east and coloured bright orange. It was travelling faster than any airliner but was visible for about 30 seconds to 1 minute. We thought it a meteorite, then it faded, abruptly turned 90 degrees to the right, then again turned 90 degrees to the left on it original direction. It then did the same to the left only a couple of moments later before fading into the distance on its original flight path. None of us have ever witnessed a ufo before, and the preceding conversation left us questioning what it could have been. We are all researchers, me in electrical/electronic systems, and my colleagues in polymers and physics. All of us have witnessed meteors before but never any with this unusual flight path. I’m afraid we have no evidence other than this statement to present.
