{cak} Two extremely large aircraft with blinking lights, Definitely NOT airplanes.

Witnessed first string of red and white lights at 8:25 pm Followed by second much larger streak of lights at 8:30 pm. The event lasted about 10 mins total, Lights were red in the front of the craft and solid and white in the back and blinking. Lights appeared to be connected as one very large aircraft definitely not airplanes or other natural phenomena. craft hovered slowly over my house into the horizon. craft was completely silent and appeared to be extremely large. A feeling of fear arose when i realized that the craft was not airplanes flying in formation as i had thought when i first saw the craft. Extremely unusual thing to see in the sky, Craft was witnessed by multiple family members and no one could identify the craft.
