ews report of sighting from an airplane carrying South American soccer team. No follow up

While driving to work one morning from College Park MD to Silver Spring MD. A story came on the morning news about a UFO sighting by passengers and crew of a plane carrying a soccer team (to or from?) a soccer match in South America (maybe Columbia). There were professional photographers traveling with the team who took photos of the craft. 

I tried to find out more on my way home from work but there was no follow up and nothing appeared in the newspapers. This happened around September of 1984, and I still have been unable to find additional information.

All dates and times I have submitted are approximate (other than the year was 1984 and the month was in late summer or early fall. I remember this because I was training for the New York City Marathon and this was shortly before the marathon took place. Any information you can provide would be appreciated.
