Light effect caught on Ring camera

At 1:45am on Christmas Day morning, a relative’s Ring camera recorded the following:

1. Two beams of light coming down the two houses, the fence and across the garden paving toward the camera (the left hand side is more apparent initially). Two audible whips / cracks heard as beams appear (audible again towards the end of the recording). 

2. Moments later a light appears to form in the sky above the house and shine directly in to the camera. 

This may simply be an effect of the rain, or an error with the camera, but I wanted to report it in case it may be of any interest. 

Additionally, and perhaps a pure coincidence, approximately two hours (around 4am) a white van arrived in the street outside and a man in high-vis lifted the manhole cover (located between the two houses) and inspected inside it for several minutes, before leaving. This may be completely unrelated, but we thought it odd for such an early time of day, especially Christmas Day.
