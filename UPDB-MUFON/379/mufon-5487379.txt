{nd} a stationary bright orb casting a downward dimmer light with mist

My daughters and I were western bound on the US 60 in Mesa AZ toward the 101 south. We noticed a bright stationary orb in the night sky about 6:30pm. It appeared to have a conelike illumination below it containing a white mist. Over the course of about a minute, the orb faded leaving the conelike illuminated mist which dimmed and faded away gradually over about 1 min till it disappeared. Other drivers seemed to slow down and observe. We kept saying, how strange that was. We had never seen anything like it.
