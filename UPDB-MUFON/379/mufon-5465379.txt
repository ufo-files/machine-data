Very fast aircraft, seemingly triangular judging by exterior lights on the underside. Lights were coral red.

I had gone out for a stroll around 19:00 (sat down to meditate along the way because I'm a kook who has dreams of successful CE-5's). On my way home, around 19:55, I stopped by the side of a grassfield where the lights from the road didn't block out the view of the stars, and I stood for a while looking up at the night sky, watching shooting stars and satellites pass. Suddenly, something came charging across the sky from my right, and it happened so fast I can't remember having a single thought in my head other than "oh my god oh my god oh my god". It was something with red'ish lights, moving at an unbelievable speed. It went in a straight, steady path and was moving so fast that by the time I was able to get a good look at it, it was almost already out of sight. I managed to make out three perfect round coral red lights, very dim, that appeared to be positioned like the corners of a triangle, and the contours of the lights were brighter than the inside, kind of made it look like circle-shaped neon lights actually. My jaw was literally on the ground. I have never in my life seen anything move that fast. The entirety of the encounter lasted only 4 seconds or so I imagine. I don't know what I saw, but whatever it was, it left me speechless and in awe. Made a ridiculously gorgeous illustration in MS Paint. Don't mind satellite A and B - they're only there because I was telling my friend of how I was watching two satellites seemingly headed for collision (and no, they didn't collide, very anti-climactic that was).
