(GS) Hovering cigar shaped at a slight angle above/behind a white large helicopter white or reflecting sun

On July 18, 2013 I was rushing home late from running errands to meet family members at home at approx 1;30-2pm in South Carlsbad, Ca just near La Costa Canyon High School. A large white helicopter was circling a nearby shopping center (Life Flight helicopter?) which got my attention and I noticed that above it and towards the East there was a white or brightly reflective metallic cigar/cylindrical/saucer shaped object 45+ degrees and hovering completely motionless and at a slight angle. The Helicopter made a lot of noise so not sure about any noises from the object but it def. was NOT a plane, helicopter, balloon, bird, etc. It was very defined in shape, highly reflective, not sure on exact height but higher than the circling helicopter. I was driving and looking, pulled over quickly to just stare at it to see if it was actually stationary, and it was motionless. I made the mistake of not taking a cell phone camera picture and was hoping to see it at my house just a few blocks away where I could yell for relatives to come see, get binoculars, take pics but I could not see it from my house or it had vanished(?) Very disappointed that I did not take a picture when I had the chance. It was against a perfectly blue sky with bright sunlight just behind/above me and it would have had the helicopter in the forefront of the picture as well for scale and comparison. I have always believed in UFOs strictly based on mathematics/probabilities but had never seen one until yesterday. I have looked at thousands of pictures and videos and would not have reported this unless I thought it to be genuine. It looked similar to the one reported a few months ago 30 miles south in Santee, Ca.
Because of the helicopter circling so low and loudly in this crowded residential area THERE MUST HAVE BEEN OTHER PEOPLE WHO SAW THIS AS IT WAS PERFECTLY SUNNY/BLUE SKY AND OBVIOUS/EASY TO SEE. I have not checked with Carlsbad Police or News to see if anyone else reported anything but cannot imagine that I would be the only one to see it especially with all of the activity of the white helicopter.
