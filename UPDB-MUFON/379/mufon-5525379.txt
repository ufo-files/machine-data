White star that moved quicker than airplane had no tail 3 witnesses

We were sitting outside watching the kids play and talking with a neighbor, I like stars and was looking up, and I saw a star moving quickly through the sky (I pointed it out to my husband and neighbor) except that it was at about the average height for an airplane, brighter and larger than any star I usually see, and was moving. I felt kind of excited about it thinking that I was seeing a meteor at first, but as I watched it I realized that it wasn't like the meteors we were watching last night during the meteor shower. It maintained a steady light for the distance over one of the town-homes, then began to blink steadily as it kept moving from our left to right (Southwest to northeast) it continued blinking until it went out of sight over the tree line. I don't have a video camera and didn't want to hunt for my phone, but now I really wish that I had since I only just found this website while trying to research and figure out what it could have been.
