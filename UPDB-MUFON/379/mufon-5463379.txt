{cak} Saw a landed UFO and had missing time!

The witness went out to his deck to have a cigarette. He heard dogs barking
and looked up toward his parent's property which was 2-3 houses away. There
was a brightly lit up UFO on the ground with blue sparks coming out of the bottom. Suddenly the object was about 10 feet from his roofline. The witness
saw a probe come out of the craft. The probe looked like it was made of glass. There was a neon blue rotating electrical pulse inside the probe.

The next thing he remembers seeing was his cigarette lying on the deck and the time was 2:35 in the morning which represented 3.5 hours of missing time. He realized that he had been abducted. He was very tired and went to bed.

In the morning, his sister touched him and he realized that his body was vi-brating. He could feel his body vibrate. The vibrating in his body continued
for about a month and then disappeared. He did not seek medical attention
because he was an EMT and his wife was an RN.

The witness has end stage COPD and wants to know what happened to him, before
he dies.

Two days before this event, in the same town, his aunt saw a landed UFO on
her property. It lit up her whole house. The shape of the object was a
disc with windows on it.
