Silver light emitting or reflecting spheres

Photos were taken of the Sept. 15th SpaceX launch of its new Spacecraft.
Reviewing the photos it became very apparent that there was something else in the skies at that time. 
There are numerous silver spheres in triangular formations scattered about the sky at various distances. Some close by in foreground and some at great distance.
One could conclude they were in their positions fir the purpose of observing this historic launch.
