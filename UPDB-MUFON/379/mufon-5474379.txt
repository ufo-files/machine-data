{cak} Teal-ish type color orb probably about 15-20 feet in diameter shot down the street just above the treeline

I'm not sure why I never reported this. Maybe because of the overwhelming amount of people that tried to debunk my sighting. I thought I was crazy. This was before I had my license. But, my buddy and I were walking back from my gfs house when we probably made it halfway back to meet our friends. We were walking eastbound down the sidewalk on the right side of the street. I was paying attention to my friend speak (he was looking forward, I was looking at him to my left) when I saw an orb maybe about 15-20 feet in diameter pop out of thin air and shoot down the street from my left to my right. It was just above the houses and trees. It made it about 250-300 yards in what seemed like 2 or 3 seconds, made a hard curved right hand turn and disappeared. We thought we were insane, so we kinda let it slide after we gave each other the "that really just happen?" question. Until we made it down to the park and it flashed in front of us again. This time it was only about 70-80 feet away, as before it was across the street and above the trees. The second time we spotted it was just a flash. Honestly thought my head was messing with me because it was so fast and so close. The orb was maybe only 60-80 feet way from us, and about 10-15 feet off the ground. Both were quick and close. Not to sound crazy, but almost like it was checking me and my friend out. Still, to this day, don't really know what I seen.
