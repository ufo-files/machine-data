{rjl} Object moved from east to northwest in under 15 seconds

While outside observing the stars facing North around 2130 Tuesday 9, June 2015,I noticed an object come from my right side at 66 degrees from the horizon (East)and move at good speed to the North by Northwest. The object was bright white, brighter than the background stars and was made no noise as it passed in front of me in less than 15 seconds. There were no clouds to my right from which is the direction the object came from. This object then passed above the clouds which were around 15,000ft-20,000ft (used the Sandia Mountains a reference for altitude) to the Northwest my (left) and still reaming bright till I could no longer view it when it passed over more cloud cover to the Northwest. It's altitude I would say was 22,000ft to 28,000ft and traveling faster than previous objects I have seen. There were no Navigational lights or flashing lights on this object and the speed was faster than a passenger plane a cruising speed. At the time of the incident I did try to record this but the cameras from my phone and sport camera couldn't pick anything. This is do to poor night viewing quality of the cameras, not from the object. There were no other aircraft in the area and no other witnesses at the time of the sighting to my knowledge. My cat was unaffected by the sighting. What surprised me about this sighting is the speed at which the object traveled. Other objects I had seen moved slowly across the sky this object cleared the same areas as the others in less than 15 seconds as compared to up to 2 minutes from other sightings. Hoping to get a better camera with better night quality viewing to capture these on tape.
