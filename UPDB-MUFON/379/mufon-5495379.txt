{cak} Observed white blimp like structure above 40,000 ft while flying to Hawaii

While I was looking out the window on the left side of my plane I observed a blimp like structure several miles away to the south. The object appear a few thousand feet higher than our plane was, which was 39,000 ft. It struck me as quite odd and out of place. Within seconds it seemed like it just disintegrated and then disappear. I kept looking and looking for it to reappear, but it never did. At first it appeared white, but as it disintegrated I could see brown. I thought about asking the pilots if they had seen it as well as I was leaving the plane, but the opportunity did not present itself. I did not experience any unusual feeling. I had for quite a while been scouring the ocean below in hopes of spotting a ship. I observed 3 commercial jets flying the opposite direction several thousand feet below us during the course of my time looking for ships. There were clouds in the area, but the blimp object was way above any.
