Orange light on the ceiling - indoor - hotel room

I was sleeping with my wife in the hotel room, got sleep around 00:30AM local time.

I opened my eyes during the night and there were on orange light on the ceiling, which faded. This recurred along all the night.

I thought it was something from the hotel such as fire detector. Then I also reminded to watch the documentary called extraordinary a few days before and got some flashes of the grey creatures in my mind.

I did not see anything beyond the orange light. So only in the morning when I got to the garage saw the marks over the wheelcover and also on the back door handle. One big circle on the dust as cleaned. and several concentric circles on the wheelcover and back door handle.

I was scared and associated to the documentary.

I did not see the object or whatever it was that make the marks on the car, I just saw an orange light such as a cigar flame on the ceiling.

As extra information, I sent the car to car washer and after getting more dust over the marks reapeared as it have some repelling electrical charge, so I took pictures of the circles.
