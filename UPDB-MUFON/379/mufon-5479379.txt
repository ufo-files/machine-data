{jpg} 2 sitings 1 week apart same place same day of the week my wife and me both witness

It was 1993 or 92. My wife (then girlfriend) and I commuted back and forth to college on tuesdays. We were coming back from college home to "tawas city". 930 at night there was a strange light in front of us. it was a perfect cold crisp night out with no clouds. YOu could see the stars. We were probable about 60 miles from wurtsmith airforce base which was closing at the time and i remember thinking "i thought all the B52 were gone already" My first thought was this thing was crashing because it was so low to the ground and i thought it may land on the road in front of us. The light was huge. when we came up over the hill maybe 50 to 100 feet in the air. the lights flashed and disappeared...I pulled over right away and listened and heard know noise or saw that the stars were not darkened out. This happened 2 x. exactly 1 week apart. I am a very busy person and have always wanted to tell someone about this. Both my wife and i witnessed this. the 2nd time the lights acted differently and spun and flashed. I would rather explain more over the phone. I could be more detailed.  no time have to rush to work.
