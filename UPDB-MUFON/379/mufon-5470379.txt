Four out of formation glowing lights.

I was about to do the washing up and looked out the window to see a glowing light appear form behind an apartment across our carpark. It looked unusual so I went to our balcony to witness it and spotted three glowing objects out of formation moving low across the horizon. They were also very subtly moving up and down. 

I then ran to get my girlfriend who followed me to the balcony and we then saw a fourth trailing behind the other three. The first three then changed angle one at a time to about 45 degrees. 

I was crying and shaking throughout the experience, I have always been interested in watching the sky with things like helicopters, aircraft, military aircraft, stars and satellites. I new as soon as I saw these they were extraordinary and possibly not from this planet.

They disappeared one at a time by diminishing into a fraction of the light they were for about 3 seconds then completely vanishing from sight.

They were not baloons, aircraft, drones, rockets, missiles, helicopters so unless the UK MoD has a top secret multi billion pound experiment going on (which is almost impossible in this day and age with transparency in large government projects) then I am convinced someone is visiting us from some other planet.
