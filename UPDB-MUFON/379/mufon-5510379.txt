I was waiting for my bus ride home on North & Western Ave from a night of fest-hopping. It was an unusually quiet. Everyone was probably watching the Chicago Blackhawks game that was going on, or too busy having fun at the festivals that were taking place a few miles away.

As I was waiting for the bus, I looked up (8:45pm) and I noticed the UFOs floating around like somewhat aimless bacteria in a petri dish. The first thing I did was whip out my phone cam and started recording. Off camera, the UFOs looked like glowing, orange, energetic jelly beans, with a steady white light on its surface. I figured that the orange glow was some kind of force field around a steady object that was too far to see. On-camera, they looked like stars (catching only the more steady white light). My camera didn't catch the full effect, but its great footage nonetheless.

Their orange, orb-like fields were pretty big, given how far away they were. Like the size of a jelly bean from my perspective.

Anyway, all was calm, they were very nonthreatening and peacefully floating. They started to fade when I notified FB on my phone lol...

Later on, some people have said that they could have been sky lanterns, and in retrospect, I feel that its possible, because they did kind of look like that, but I don't know. Because of the formations they took and the orangey glow that the camera didn't catch.
