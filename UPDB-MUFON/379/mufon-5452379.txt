Around 8:27 pm on Wednesday 6-3-2020. I was outside smoking a cigarette. When I picked up a object that was white in color moving from the West to the SE. But why this object really caught my attention. Was there were no other lights flashing or blinking like normal aircraft. So imagine a very large white egg moving across the sky. I can see the white object with my own eyes. And never once did the light flash or blink. Just stayed in view until the light just disappeared. Not from being so far away it went out of site. Its all most as if someone turned off a light switch. I took pictures of the object as well as video. And the photos taken one right after each other. Show the object almost changing in the photos from one to the next.
