I was working as a flight attendant on a flight from Denver to Pittsburg and when we were over Ohio a large craft was identified by other airplanes asking ATC to pick it up on their radar. ATC could not see it, the captain announced over the PA to look out the right side of the airplane to see a UFO. It was huge and above us with red blue lights. The exact date and year is vague but I do remember it was in the mid 80's and there was a Pittsburg journalist on board who took notes from the event and interviewed the captain. I also went to the cockpit to listen to the radio traffic and all the airlines were seeing it and commenting. It was keeping a straight line and was bigger than a modern airliner, much much bigger and the from was unknown, it was just very big. The event happened at night with no moon light and other than the light from our own airplane and the lights off the UFO was the only source of illumination at the time. I was not the only one who saw it, everyone on board saw it and people on the ground saw it. It was reported in the paper the next day as space junk entering the atmosphere. I do not believe this, it was keeping a straight line without any glowing matter of any kind except the blueish lights. It appeared to be a craft of some kind.
