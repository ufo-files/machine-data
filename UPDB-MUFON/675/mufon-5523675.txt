Starlike objest

I went to shut my back door when i saw the stars were particularily bright, so i stepped out to take a good look, directly above i saw somthing moving in an odd pattern, at forst i thought it was a hazy kind of star but it took some shap when my eyes focused, sort of triangular. At first it was moving back and forth from two points, i thought it looked like headlamps reflecting off cloud but there is no cloud and it was an odd path for a plane to take. I have been watching for over an hour and it has since started maving in a rough figure of eight, sometimes speeding up and it seems to move slightyl across the sky as it does this. I'm the only one here and i'm too frightened to knock on any neighbours doors as it's late.
