Unknown aircraft passed over my house

Monday night, July 27, 2020, for about 20 minutes I observed an aircraft pass over my house before traveling out to sea. I've lived here for 5 years and I am able to decipher the difference between commercial and military airplanes, drones and helicopters which appear far less frequently since COVID. There were no clouds in the sky on this night. The aircraft would appear to spin and the exterior lights would pulsate into view, then would fade for about a minute. The pattern seemed to be steady, although i was only able to clearly film it pulsating once because the street light was interfering with my iphone camera. 

I'm uploading two videos:
1. The first of which is about :13 seconds and zoomed out. The craft spins and pulses into view at the :02 second mark in the middle of the screen and slightly to the right. Above it, to the left you can clearly see a planet that shines brightly. The brightness of the lights on the aircraft become as bright as the planet. 
2. The second is zoomed in a slowed down so you can more clearly see that the craft is in fact spinning at the :06 second mark.
