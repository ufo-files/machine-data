Large triangle shaped craft with lights

5/28/2020 around 2230hrs just outside of Pittsburgh PA. I was approaching a stop sign and saw a very large dark colored triangle in the sky with several lights making a V formation. It was enough for me to slam my car in park and jump out of the vehicle.. my first thought was that it was a military aircraft of some type; as it was rather low, only a few hundred yards above the trees and houses in the area. It looked like it could a plane making a very hard bank. However, when I got out it was dead quiet. No sound whatsoever. I have never experienced fear like I did in that moment. The object proceeded to move away and out of sight towards the South.
