Orange lights in the sky

I saw a bright orange light coming from the east in the sky that was bigger and a lot brighter than a star. It didn't move like an airplane so I watched it for a few seconds. Then someone lit a firework and after it fizzled out another light just like the first one appeared and they both just hovered in the sky. I filmed this for a few seconds then stopped recording and a 3rd light appeared to make a triangle. I started filming it again as they hovered. When I stopped recording they started moving south east and must have shot off fast because I didn't see them after that. So they made an L shape, coming from the east and shooting off to the southeast.
