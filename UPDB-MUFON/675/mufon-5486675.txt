saw UFO hovering over river area

I was on my way to work and got off the number 5 bus at the stop across from the safeway. I was waiting for the light to change when something caught my eye in the sky as it was reflecting light off what i assumed was the wing as it turned. As it is a high traffic area for both small and large aircrafts i didnt focus to much on it while i walked to the sunnyside train station. I walked to the station and noticed that the object was infact not a plane or helicopter (i am an avid aircraft watcher) When i first saw it, it was just hovering and it stayed like that for about 10 or so minutes tell a helicopter flew close to it. The helicopter seemed to disturb the craft as it darted upward and moved away from the helicopter(NW direction after upwards). After the helicopter got close (i am unsure of what kind of helicopter it was) and the craft moved away the helicopter than did a 180 and went back the way it came. Once the helicopter was gone the craft went back to its orignal spot. It once again hovered there for about 5 minutes. Once again the same helicopter came back and this time the craft took off south. I watched it tell it disapeared behind buildings. All of this happened in like a 20 minute span or so. I seemed to be the person interested in it as i was not alone at the train station but no one was paying attention to the sky. The craft also made no noice.
