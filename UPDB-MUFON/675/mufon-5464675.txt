After listening to Dr. Greer talk about communicating with EBEs using your mind, I thought that I would try it while waiting on my wife and son to skate in a parking lot. I opened my mind, as he suggested, stating that I welcomed anything to this planet, etc. While looking in to the high sky, at least 75-80 degrees above the horizon a flash/light appeared out of nowhere.

This yellowish flash/light appeared out of nowhere and grew to be a larger yellowish flash/light. It grew somewhat large and appeared stable for a few seconds, until it almost became orb or halo-like. Once becoming stable, it's yellowish color turned to also a whitish color, and began to fade quickly as if it was travelling away from me. As it was growing smaller it almost reminded me of the cliche you'll see in movies of a "warp drive", but not quite.

After growing smaller and appeared to be leaving, the sky became just as black as it was before with normal twinkling starlight, etc. It seemed to me as if I was open and asking to be in contact, they/it appeared to say they were here, and then the event was over. Neither my wife nor my son witnessed the event and I didn't tell them because I knew they wouldn't believe me.

I am a Vet and have been schooled in recognizing aircraft, plus I am a Geologist and know what common stars, planets, and galaxies look like in our night sky--this was none of those. It was an uncanny experience, yet made me feel very calm and at ease, at the same time.
