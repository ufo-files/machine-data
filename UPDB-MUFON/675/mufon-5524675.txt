Dancing lights in front of lightning/thunder storm.

I went outside to roll up the windows to my truck because i noticed lightning to the east.As i was looking to the east i noticed dancing lights north of the thundercloud.I called my family to confirm what i was seeing and sure enough they all saw the dancing lights.These lights for a fact are not planes because ive never seen planes move like that, considering we live by an airforce base.The objects were moving all over the place....down, up, stopping, u-turning, diagonally, swirling, darting back and forth.They even seem to chase each other. I noticed around 10:30 and we still havent lost sight of them. I also called my cousin and friend who live in a different area of town and sure enough they saw them too! My cousin was surprised so he took off to a nearby lake, to see if he can see them better without city lights. Also a neighbor caught a suspicious triangle on his cell phone.Still trying to figure out how to download video to computer. Thanks.
