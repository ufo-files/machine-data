{rjl} Going out to feed my animals in the morning. Saw a cigar shaped light. Made no noise and was at 45 degree angle.

I was going up to feed my goats when I noticed a very large light in the sky. It almost looked like that car on Harry Potter when they were flying in sky. This thing looked like it had to head lights.   I went in my barn to feed and when I came back out it was still there. So I took a picture of it and as soon as I did fog suddenly came in and covered it up. My neighbor saw It also,she said it was there for three days.
