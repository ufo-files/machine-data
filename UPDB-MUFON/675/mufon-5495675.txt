{dr}Hovering light in the sky

My niece was in the kitchen when she noticed it. She yelled to everyone that there was something in the sky. Sure enough there was. It was 10:15 and dark outside. The object was just hovering above the trees across the street in the farmers field. It was flashing red and yellow lights and then started to fab off like it was going dark and shot to the right . It immediately shot back to the left and remained in the spot originally seen . It would go back and forth and did this pattern for over an hour . Then it moved to the left and disappeared . We still are creeped out about it . The dogs were barking on and off during the event and everyone felt very uncomfortable .
