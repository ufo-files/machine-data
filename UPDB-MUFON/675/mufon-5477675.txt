{dr}A very quiet spacecraft...

This all started with me laying down on the floor of my new tiny house I am in the process of building... I was laying down trying to visualize how the skylight would look at night... The stars looked really neat and then al of a sudden I see this thing floating up in the sky at first I thought it was a plastic bag floating around but as I stood up and followed it through the trees it was actually way higher than it looked... It was a disc shaped sort of but really didn't have any defined edges to it it made no sound at all.. it was almost like it was see through some kind of translucent matter... it did have lights but they weren't like manmade lights like we have here like leds or lightbulbs it looked almost like torch light or some kind of natural light source. It went straight for awhile and then turned off to the left and then just disappeared... this all happened in like 12-15 seconds. I had my phone in my hand but I was too intrigued to take my eyes off of it... it almost felt like whoever was in there was calling me somehow. I'll never forget this night.
