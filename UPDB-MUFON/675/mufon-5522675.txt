Multiple shapes, colors and objects

On Friday, November 11th, 2011 I was driving south on I-55 from St. Louis on my way to Texas. Around 11:15 PM and about 30 1 45 miles north of Memphis something out of my driver side window caught my eye. The sky was clear and it was a perfect night to watch stars. I noticed three large, solid, bright lights in the shape of a V. At second glance, there was more going on than just the three lights. There was a cluster of different size and color lights. My first thought was that I must be near a Memphis airport. However, none of the objects appeared to be landing or taking off. There was also no sign of an airport below them. 

The three original lights kept their formation while some of the smaller ones were just moving back and forth. Their movement was smooth, but the way they changed directions was odd. I didn1t feel like I was in any danger, they appeared to be busy doing whatever it was they were doing. 

I rolled my window down, steered with my knee, and switched my phone to camera. I checked the view finder quickly and snapped one shot. I checked to see if the camera caught any of the lights and surprisingly saw three lights were in the frame. I looked for a couple of more seconds and kept driving. As I was rolling the window up I noticed that there was a helicopter flying low to the ground and headed in the direction. 

The entire thing happened in less than a minute and I really didn1t expect much from the picture. When I put it on a computer and blew it up I was in complete shock. I would love to speak with anyone else who may have witnessed something similar near the TN, MO and AR border.
