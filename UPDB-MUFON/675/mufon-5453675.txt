{rjl} I witnessed objects as bright as stars moving overhead for roughly ten minutes before dawn.

After my daily predawn run on the east side of El Paso, Texas, I witnessed several objects as bright as stars moving in different patterns overhead between 5:43 a.m. until 5:58 a.m. on Tuesday, April 14, 2020. I ran into the house, grabbed my iPhone to try to capture the event and woke my sister up to show her what was happening. We both saw no blinking lights and heard no sounds which ruled out airplanes. We assumed that the bright lights had to be satellites. But that theory also seemed to be ruled out because the bright objects changed directions-one even made a quick right turn to seemingly avoid hitting another. We aren’t certain of what we saw over the Southwest desert that morning, but we both agree that neither of us can explain it with certainty.
