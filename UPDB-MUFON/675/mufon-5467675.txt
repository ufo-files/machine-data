{td} Hovering / Slow moving orange orb

On or about the date listed above I was making an exterior check of the buildings on the property when I noticed this floating orange light. At no time did the light make any sound or make any sudden movements. The light hovered for approx 2 min or 3 min then started to slowly drift (left of my position) before disappearing. I would compare the way the light disappeared to a light switch being cut off. I have been watching the sky the last few weeks to try to catch the object again but have not been able to locate any other light in the sky resembling what I saw on that night.
