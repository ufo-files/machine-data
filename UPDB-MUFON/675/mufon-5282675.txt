Silver UAP sighted while traveling on I-526 toward West Ashley over Westmoreland Bridge

At 0830 I was traveling over the West Moreland Bridge (I-526) toward West Ashley from North Charleston. I looked to the right off the Bridge and over the marsh. I saw what i thought was a plane reflecting off the morning sun in the sky. It didn't appear to move and seemed to be hovering over Ashleytowne Landing neighborhood in West Ashley. I continued to observe it while driving before trees obscured the object and I was no longer high enough to see it. 

I could see it shimmering in the sunlight and it seemed like just below it a silver triangle started to form.
