{dr}It called to me to come out and look up at the sky

Talking on the phone with my father on Memorial Day around 930 pm on my front porch; got this notion and urge to step out and look up at the sky and I walked out doing so and I was facing North then west and I could hear this inner voice tell to to turn around and look up and that's when I seen the amber colored object appear and hover for a moment then across the sky it traveled as I called to my wife, and son, all the while talking to my father then my phone went dead as it passed over . Then we all watched it travel Northward and not once did it make a sound nor did the light dull . No trail or tail preceded it.
