Diamond/Elliptical glowing orange light streaked across sky

I was walking West on the country road. It was a dark,cloudy night. Between 9:25pm to 9:45pm. 
I was just enjoying the calm, cool night when just in front of me at about 30 degrees up I noticed something moving quickly.It only lasted a second or two. I saw a elliptical/diamond shaped orage glow streak across the sky from SW to W. It's true shape was blurred by the clouds cover. It seemed too low to be a meteor. It also didn't change illumination like meteors do as a break up. It was a steady glow. It eventually disappeared behind some trees/clouds. It covered the span of half my view in a second or two. 
I thought I saw it dip and the go back to it's original latitude forming a quick v in it's path, but that could've been the clouds masking it. 

My coworker also saw it that night and around the same time. He was down in Torrance CA. There was less cloud over and saw it shoot up into the sky.
