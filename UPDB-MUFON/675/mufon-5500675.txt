{rjl} hovering object side to side up and down and moved quickly at times

We were admiring the stars because the sky was clear when we were in the backyard of friends when we noticed a moving red light. But it was different. It hovered and moved slightly right and left. Hovered and moved up and down. We watched it for about 5 minutes and it was staying in the same position but hovered and moved to the right and left. The first thought I had was I was a little afraid but thought it was amazing. But when it continued to just hover I began to wonder what it was. Our friends we staring at it as well, and we all were a little where it was going or looking at. After about 5 - 7 minutes it started to move towards the ground however, trees were in our way to witness it further. We lost it moving towards the ground in the distance.
