3 craft, 1st noticed travelling silently east to west within the atmosphere lower than the height of a plane travelling very fast and no emissions

Noticed a triangular white/grey object flying silently within the atmosphere lower than a plane would fly travelling east to west across the sky between a large break in the clouds. Then spotted two identical ibjects tailing 45 degrees below to the left at similar speed. These two objects broke formation, appeared to turn in on themselves to come together and the disappeared quickly. The first object continued on its path and then disappeared out of sight or behind cloud cover. No sound at all during the sighting - unsure if this is classified tech as it was close to Birmingham airport so surely should have been picked up on radar?? Definitely not an aircraft or anything I have ever seen or witnessed before and the speed was incredible. Never seen any evidence of this nature before in my life!No time to capture on the phone, I was in awe and didn’t react quickly enough. Just wanted to document it for the record in the hope that someone else spotted them or radar abnormalities were reported at the airport?
