{cak} oval shape, dark grey flying past WTC in September

I just recently played back a video of 9/11 (someone had filmed) in slow motion (for another reason) and noticed something in slow motion that wasnt there in real time. It is a huge ship, maybe the size of a football field or a little smaller, flying insanely fast (at least a 1000 mph) past the World Trade Center after the second plane crashed. It is pretty clear in the video on slow speed. I am a pilot, this is not a plane. There is no doubt what it is. It is oval or circular in shape and dark grey. Like I said, it is not visible in real time because the speed it too fast for the human eye, just visible in slow motion, but the image is pretty good for caught on film.
