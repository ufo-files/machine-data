{kt}fireball object hovering in Fox Chase/ Rockledge

I was walking down Lonely St around 8:40 P.M on Monday August 12 2013 with my husband. We were headed from Barnes St down towards Burholme St. On my left, there were 4 people staring up to the sky. I overheard the man mention a few things about aliens/ ufos. When I looked up I noticed a object (around the size of a helicopter or plane in the distance) it was a pulsating fireball of multiple colors. I made the mistake of continuing to walk and look up at it.. before I knew it, it would disappear in the distance (ascending? but not straight up). It is hard to say how far it was from me but it gave off a very large glowing "flame." It didn't make a sound. Im curious if this was an UFO or maybe some flaming space treasures?
