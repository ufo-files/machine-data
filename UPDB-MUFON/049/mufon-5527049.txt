Small star like blob pulsing between bright white and indigo

9:55am Standing on my NW facing balcony I heard what sounded like a LOUD military craft directly over head. Then the sound just stopped. I looked up north and saw what looked like a very bright star but then it turned indigo, then back to white. I watched it move east in a slow but steady manner in a straight line slightly going upwards. I ran inside to grab my camera and it seems it appeared in one of my picture. Upon blowing it up and tinkering with the pic using embossing and black and white reverse I indeed confirmed a disc or round shaped object there in the sky. I lost sight of it as it passed over my roof and out of my view. No other feelings accompanied this siting other that frenzy to get a pic before it went over the roof. Cannot determine altitude but I've never seen a plane EVER fly that high and it made no noise that I could detect after the cessation of the other jet I heard in the beginning.
