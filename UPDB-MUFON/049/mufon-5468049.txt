{jpg} Small, fast object at apparent high altitude reflected light at times while turning.

I am a retired USAF fighter pilot who has been trained to closely watch the sky for enemy aircraft, real or simulated, which are called out as "bogeys." On a very clear evening about 730PM I was sitting in a chair watching chimney swifts fly by but looking almost overhead I observed a very small unidentified object in what appeared to me to be at a VERY high altitude making turns and accelerating and decelerating, sometimes almost hovering over the city of Bastrop Texas where I live. At times it appeared dark but at other times reflected the light from the sinking sun to the west. It stayed overhead for about ten minutes flying in a  circular pattern I would estimate about within ten miles. It finally disappeared heading in a easterly direction but emitting some light as it did so. 
At first I thought my eyes were playing tricks on me but I have excellent eyesight and feel it was real, not imaginary. It left me with a profound impression I cannot describe, so I thought it worth reporting. I have seen or heard no mention of this from any other source so this surprises me somewhat although I realize very people look up at the sky. No mention in the media--I would have thought. Bergstrom Air Base radar would have picked it up, but no word.
