Hovering discliek object above 22 Fwy in Westminster/ Garden Grove CA

Was driving home from work abour 8 pm on 01/28/2012. Usually get off on Beach exit but saw strange disc shaped object with unusual lights and got off on Knot exit. At Garden Grove street, object was high to the right side so I ulled in parking lot to try to get picture. Had one bright red light and then white lights that were flashing in a pattered way around the disc. Tried to get a closer look by taking another street but object was gone. I arrived close to home about 25 minutes later and saw object reappear in area above 22 Freeway again. It was hovering and heading West. the white lights would go off and then reappear in a pattern again, turn brighter and then off and then object left very quickly.
