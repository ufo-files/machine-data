{cak} Circular Lights Moving Through Sky

I want to make sure that there are 2 witness' to this, unfortunately the 2nd' witness is under 18 and I do not have permission to share this witness' name. 

I was letting my dog out and I had just lit my cigarette. Something in the sky caught my attention of the way the movement was. I first noticed 3 circle (dot like) objects in a line formation going from North to North/East.

I wanted to know where these things were coming from and followed what I thought was their direction, I noticed that there were 5 more objects, the same way they looked like the ones before.

They were also in a line formation, no off centered formations, like as if they were in the military. 

I asked witness #2 to come see if they can see what I saw, just to make sure I wasn't losing my mind Witness #2 said that they did see what I saw and became scared after this.

I have detailed information from this event from a screenshot of the compass I used to a visual presentation of what they looked like.
