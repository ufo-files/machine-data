{nd} Bright solid light, small zigs in path, disappeared behind small cloud

I was outside taking out the trash. I noticed a light that looked different. It wasnt moving insanely fast, which is an easy way to discern UFOs. But the quality of the light is unlike anything Ive ever seen before. It most definitely wasnt the front lights of a plane. As a comparison there were a few other planes in the area. Also, I stopped walking when I saw it and saw it zig once or twice, in ways that are impossible. It wasnt a big movement, but just enough to notice and not be an eye glitch. 

I watched it go, and then it went behind a very thin cloud, never to return. The cloud wasnt large, and I kept watching. Based on the velocity, it had to have come out the otherside, but instead it just disappeared. Also, when it went behind the cloud, the light still didn't light up like headlights. Still no directionality, the ball of light just got more focused to a pinprick of light. 

The coolest part is that as I watched, it was the most natural thing in the world. I always imagined I would be scared, but I wasnt. I felt like, "Oh, I think thats a UFO. I should watch it. Hmmm...yep, pretty sure that was a UFO. Ill have to tell my wife." It was as natural as the planes. :-) Just different.
