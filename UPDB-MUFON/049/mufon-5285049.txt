Colored orb of sorts....slowly ascending then stops and lights flashing.

was outside early morning as always, when I was looking up, as lately skies been cloudy was looking to see if I could in fact catch anything, when i happened to come across a bright white light ascending in the sky in the direction of the Scarborough Bluffs which is Lake Ontario, and then stopped. When I decided to get out my phone and try out the camera on it was amazed to see several different colors of flashing lights. As the sun became brighter, it disappeared. As a Canadian Member and now International member, I felt this was worthy of recording and reporting. I have seen this several times before but different direction, more eastern towards the direction of the Pickering Nuclear Power Station in Ontario. I honestly have always thought it might be a star or maybe a planet....video will be attached.
