{jpg} SOUTHERN

I WAS SMIMMING WITH MY YOUNGER SISTER AROUND NOON AT MY COUSINS HOUSE WHO WAS BABYSITTING US WHILE MY MOM WAS AT WORK. SHE HAD ON HER BLACK BATHING SUIT TANNING FACING THE POOL LAYING DOWN ON CHASE WITH SUNGLASSES MAYBE NAPPING.

IT FLEW IN FROM SOUTHERN "ENTRY" DAYTIME UFO METALIC 6'-8' DISC W/LIGHTS SLOW ENTRY, HOVERING ABOVE HOUSE 1-4 FEET VERY VERY FAST (ALWAYS SILENT) EASTERN "EXIT" WHILE IT HOVERED ABOVE HOUSE FOR ABOUT MAYBE TWENTY-FORTY SECONDS. I STARED FOR MAYBE 3 SECONDS BEFORE YELLING FOR COUSIN ANGELA TO LOOK. AFTER HER SAYING WHAT? AND A BACK AND FORRTH WITH ME POINTING. MAYBE 20 SECONDS OF MY PANICKING YELLS FOR HER TO LOOK. SHE CAME TOWARDS US AND THE POOL. WHILE WALKING TOWARDS US(CRAFT SHOOTS AWAY EASTWARD OVER 100MPH INSTANTLY AND SILENTLY) SHE HAD HER BACK TO THE HOUSE(THE CRAFT WAS 1'-4' ABOVE THE RIDGE OF THE RANCH HOME). BY THE TIME I GOT HER TO TURN AROUND THE "UFO" HAD MADE ITSELF SO SMALL WITH DISTANCE IT WAS A DOT IF IN SIGHT. SHE THOUGHT I WAS BEING A "LITTLE BOY" I ASSUME. NO ONE BELIEVED ME SO I HAVENT TOLD VERY MANY PEOPLE SINCE THEN. BUT I HAVE ALWAYS HAD A FEAR OF BEING FOLLOWED BY "UFO" SINCE THAT TIME, AND THE MOON AT NIGHT MY DAD USED TO TELL ME.
