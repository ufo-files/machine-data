boomarang object descends and emits light beam, changes shape.

I am an amateur astronomer and was looking up at night sky 15 September 2015 at 12.30 am noticed patch of grey light moving south to north. craft appeared boomarang shaped outline was dull grey and appeared to have static electric type outline. at zenith it sharply turned and moved to west continued then moved in north direction and appeared to have descended as it was larger. As it moved a funnel of light appeared, then a bright beam emerged towards my direction it appeared to have been from a triangular opening on the side of the outline of the craft the beam and funnel were visible only for a second or so the craft continued north over a lake, seemed to shimmer in the atmosphere, then appeared to be two grey disks moving north, one following the other then moved out of sight. The whole episode lasted for only 12 seconds or so.There was absolutely no sound made during sighting. Object initially was high in altitude then descended, I was unable to judge distance but perhaps a couple of kilometers away.
