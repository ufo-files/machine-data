Object appeared as if seeing a shooting star, then appeared closer than a star, and with a rolling, straight trajectory

Wife and I were walking through the neighborhood heading west when we saw an object traveling in a straight route across the sky south to northwest. It appeared to be rolling with shimmers cast as light reflected off edges. It was visible for about 15 seconds until it came into the light of the two well lit planet/star in the western sky. First thought it was a shooting star, but due to the rolling/tumbling effect as we watched, we thought otherwise. I was excited as I pointed and then I was baffled as to what we had seen.
