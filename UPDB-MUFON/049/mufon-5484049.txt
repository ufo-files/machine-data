{cak} Looking out to the West, I saw the 2 bright lights, blinking back & forth (mimicking each other). One light stretched out like lightning, then returned to the original shape a few times.

It was around 9:35 p.m., Wednesday, April 12, 2016. It was a really clear night, and I just happened to look out my dining room window. I noticed two flashing lights in the lower West. They were brighter than airplanes. I kept looking at them, and one would flash/blink off & on (blink blink blink), then the other one (on the right) would do the same thing back (blink blink blink)! They did this a few times, then all of a sudden the one on the left changed its shape, like a long light beam that stretched out! It changed back to the star shape, but then it stretched out again . It reminded me of lightning going horizontally! Weird! The light on the right stayed the same, but blinked! Then, I saw a smaller light fly by quickly heading to the southeast. It looked like the light flew between them? It all happened under 3 minutes. The two larger lights got smaller then headed west and disappeared quickly.
