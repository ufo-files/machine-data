{cak} Metallic sphere enveloped in a very bright white light moving extremely fast the light trailed like a tail, may have crashed/landed, no sound

I was driving home from work heading north on I-95 just about to turn onto the John Turner Butler exit when I looked up and saw a metallic sphere with an extremely bright white light, almost glowing around the object zoom by at a very incredible, non-earthly speed. The object was moving so fast and almost looked as if it was descending or curving downwards behind trees near the highway without slowing, however I do not think it could have landed at that speed and survived. The object was about the size of a golf ball from my view, much larger than a star and the light was so bright it seemed like an ''eyeball'' with the metallic part being the pupil and the light the exterior. It moved so fast it had an inch long tail of light ( from my view out of the windshield )and as it passed I heard a very faint high pitched whistle. The object headed north parallel to I-95 but I could not tell if it descended or just disappeared out of sight, from the very high rate of speed. It lasted only about 10 seconds so by the time I had my phone in hand it was long gone. I am curious to know if anyone else witnessed this.
