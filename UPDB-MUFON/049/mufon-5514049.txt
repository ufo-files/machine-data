[flg] Sphere object flying high speed pierced the cloud leaving a trail

I was driving home from work and I was admiring the clouds.They looked like they had a wave patterns. When I pulled up to my driveway this glowing orb just appeared stationary then took off in a blink of an eye. I could not film or take a picture of the orb because it moved so fast,but it left a trail in the cloud with an entrance hole and an exit hole. The exit hole was still swirling after the orb had left.
