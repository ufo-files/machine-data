{rjl} orange light hovered in the west for at least 30 minutes 3 times this week

I observed a single glowing orange to red and orange to gold/white, very bright light in the western sky just before and after sunset. I live about an hour from Indianapolis Airport and see planes frequently but they have small blinking lights and are generally accompanied by a variety of noises. This light did not have blinking lights nor did it follow the usual paths associated with airport traffic. I have watched it three times in one week (July). I would step away to ask someone else to look and it would be gone by time I got back. It's too low to be a star and it is not high enough for commercial flights. Everyone disbelieves me and I tried to observe for the duration but something always happens and I miss it leaving. I do not feel uncomfortable or uneasy but it is probably farther away than it appears because it is near the horizon. Maybe 30-45 degrees. I'm not saying it's aliens, or the government,or anything,just that it is unusual and not the normal evening scenery.
