Spherical & hazy lights of various shapes/sizes from very far away to very close, capable of moving from point A to point B almost instantaneously.

At first we thought we were looking at Venus in the sky. I took a picture. I noticed the light was now relatively close but appeared about the same size, as if space/distance made no difference in my perception of it's size. It started moving around, almost exclusively staying in the yard we were in. I was taking more pictures. I pointed out to Brad that it looked the same size in the grass near us as it did further away. I was following it with my camera until I ended up looking at my face in the camera with the light on my forehead. I was very disturbed bc it reminded me of lazer sights for a gun. We immediately went inside. Later upon inspection of the photos, we discovered some ...characteristics that we either couldn't see or didn't notice earlier.
