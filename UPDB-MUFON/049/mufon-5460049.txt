{rjl} two stationary starlike objects the NE part of KC

My friend and I were walking on the Riverfront Park walking path when I saw a distant faint starlike object in front of us over the area of the river north and east of downtown KC. Looked like it would have been several miles ahead of us, and quite faint, and stationary. After watching it a few minutes I looked up and there was a much closer starlike object, also stationary; my best guess is it was a bit less than halfway from horizon to zenith, and according to my friend's phone, from our location the closer object was located at 40 degree NE.  We watched this object for a good 20 minutes and did not notice any movement, it appeared completely stationary. I have photos and video of our sighting. In the video are other objects that appear to pass by the UFO when viewed frame by frame but I have no idea if they are insects or cotton or something more inexplicable.  I saw the report today of the UFOs and the supposed connection with DARPA but have a hard time thinking these are connected, for a number of reasons. Wind direction and speed need to be carefully considered as we saw and photographed these UFOs east of downtown, and later in the evening they were seen closer together and they supposedly had moved northwest and were huddled together over the airport area north of the city? And we are supposed to believe that these were released together 2 days prior to them being spotted in KC, again, like they travelled around the world holding hands and ended up being spotted in KC, after eluding other witnesses for days?  - OR, were the ones my friend and I saw entirely different UFOs than the ones reported later in the day over the airport area?
