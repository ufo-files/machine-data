{cak] a white/silvery orb/oval bright ball slightly bouncing/hovering in air then gone

I was driving eastbound highway 24 towards Caldecott tunnel. I was a passenger in the front seat of our car. the orb/oval shaped object was seemingly hovering/bobbing above Orinda, just on the other side of the tunnel. it was very vibrant white, shimmery silver light. I immediately realized that it was not a plane or helicopter. I seemed to instantly think it was a ufo. I was happy to see it, excited. it was hovering and slightly bobbing up and down and then it was just gone, no trace, no pathway of departure. it was about 9:30 to 9:35 am. it could have been at about 10,000 feet or even way up to 40,000 ft. very hard to tell the altitude or distance from where I was. it was just so odd that it was there and then gone. I get excited when I see these things. It gives me hope that there is more to our existence than we might realize.
