{jpg} Looked like a fidget spinner with flashing lights

I was sitting outside with a friend making jokes when some blinking lights caught my attention and I just sat there staring at the sky and pointed to the sky asking my friend "what is that?" it didn't have any color lights like on a plane so I asked if it could be a satellite he replied with no so we just watched it, trying to figure out what it was and we noticed it was slowly changing direction it wasn't going straight. after we got up to follow it I noticed from the reflection of its own lights the shape reminded me of my kids fidget spinner and asked my friend if he see that too which he agreed it did look like a fidget spinner with lights floating across the night sky. sadly none of our camera phones would pick it up.
