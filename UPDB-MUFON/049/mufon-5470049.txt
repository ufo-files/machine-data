{td}Looked like a boomerang, you would imagine a plane but stopped in mid air. had no tail just like a full wing.

I was coming home from afternoon tech class, driving down I-75 when traffic stopped. I noticed a tiny black object above my car so high up it was hard to look at it for a certain amount of time. At first I thought possibly it was a traffic drone because the small object stopped and changed directions a few times. It took a course over my car slowly looking at it through my sunroof, I got a better look at it without the sun in my face. It looked almost like a long wing. boomerang shaped. I noticed some colors on it with a red and black surface that looked metallic. A couple red lines hard to tell. The object did not seem to have any shake to it like a kite would. It seemed to keep stopping and moving again. It looked like the green goblin's wing in spiderman 2002. But red and black. The ends of wings were rounded off and there were no tails or heads to it. Like a flying wing. Possible to be a lost kite or balloon but unlikely due to the maneuvers it took. I tried to video it but I didn't seem to get the object on camera. I will analzye the footage and send it in if i can find the object.
