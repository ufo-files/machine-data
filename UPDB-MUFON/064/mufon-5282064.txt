Observed UFO on the evening stated 8/21/1983 , parker car in driveway got out of car turned around something caught my eye and heard the craft

On the evening of August 21st, 1983 . I parked the car in the driveway at my home. It was approximately 10:30pm at night . As I got out of my vehicle . An object in the night sky caught my attention . I and my girlfriend at the time witnessed what we believe to be a disk shaped object. The object was relatively close to us . It was hovering in sky . The craft had three round lights , each light would great brighter and fainter . They were not in sync with each other. The craft emitted what I would consider a hum, like a power transformer. It felt like what it feels like if you were to stand in front of a loud speaker. You could feel the vibrations. I told my girlfriend to take note of the event. What we were witnessing was a one-in- a lifetime event. As we watched , I thought to go inside the house to grab my camera to see if I could get a photo. I found my camera and the craft was still there .. I took some pictures but when the film was developed nothing was present , probably because I did not have night film . I told my sister and her friends to quickly come outside to see what we were watch in the sky. As I watched the craft , it moved from one location across the sky , in a moment and stopped . I was amazed that anything could move that fast across the sky and stop on a dime in the air. At that time it must have been positioned over New Haven somewhere in the Yale Bowl area .
