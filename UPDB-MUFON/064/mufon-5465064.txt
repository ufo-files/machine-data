White orb, three beams of light from each point like a triangle, appeared spinning on video footage close up. No noise. Plane passed by it. Unlike a star as no light was emanating from it as a star does or the moon.

I walked outside my back door because I was shouted by my mother who could see a strange orb in the sky. I initially thought it was a helicopter but no noise and after zooming in it appeared to be spinning and moving up and down at odd angles. A plane passed by and looked completely different to this object. Three beams of light emitted from unseen points like an equilateral triangle. I decided to photograph and film this but lights aren1t apparent on film. Plus it was a smart phone camera so not the best quality. Object just disappeared from sight. Lasted about 30 mins.
