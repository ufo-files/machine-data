{cak} Very Bright white light flying in a straight line then dimmed out completely. Then a higher dimmer light flying in opposite direction

I was standing on my front porch smoking a cigarette. I looked to my left which would have been towards the NW. The light was flying towards me. I kept watching because of the brightness of the light. I expected to eventually see flashing lights that woul normally be on an airplane. As the light got closer over head no flashing lights ever appeared. At this time I opened the front door and had my mother look at the light with me. At this time it was directly over her. It continued on the same path until it dimmed out slowly. Then it was just gone. Then there was another light flying in the opposite direction over head but this light was much higher and no where near as bright. There were also 3-4 airplanes in the area.
