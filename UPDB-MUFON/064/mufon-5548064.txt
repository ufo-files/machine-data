looked like a mans shoe heel

I was laying down on a lawn chair and looking at the stars fully awake, this thing flew along above me at something like 1000 feet or more. It circled above me 3 or 4 times, gaining altitude as it did until it disappeared out of site. It looked like a shoe heel from a man1s hard sole shoe. It had a rounded end and a squared end and it had 4 round dimples or impressions evenly spaced on the bottom in a square pattern. I could not tell how thick it was, I could only see the bottom and the leading round edge. It didn't look very thick. It wasn't really easy to see. It had no lights and a dull dark color The only thing making it visible was the ground lighting reflecting off of it. From my vantage point it was about 6 inches long from rounded end to the square end and 5 inches across the other way. It was oriented with the round end going first. Speed, it covered about 1/4 of the sky in about 15 seconds. It was travelling west from Lake Ontario.
