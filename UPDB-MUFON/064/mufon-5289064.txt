A huge, bright light.

A friend and I were sitting on my deck when to our left a big, bright light (almost like a football stadium light) rose up from behind the trees and hovered for a few seconds just shining on us. It felt like a spotlight on us actually. Then it zipped to its right really fast and poof! Disintegrated into a falling shower of sparks.
