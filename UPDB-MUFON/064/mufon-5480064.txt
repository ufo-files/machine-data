{jpg} 3 Lights Appeared Out Of Thin Air

I work at target in Mt Kisco. I went outside for a few minutes while on break, and there was an odd young man standing outside. At first he started to talk to me, but then I almost assumed he was on drugs because of what he was saying. He was talking about disclosure, and then he asked me if I wanted to see something. He reached into his pocket and pulled out a flash light and shined it in the sky. All of a sudden an orb appeared. He did it again and a second orb appeared. And then a third time and a third orb appeared. They made a triangle formation and disappeared shortly thereafter. I asked him what it was and he said "I'm not going to lie, those things you are witnessing are extraterrestrial. He said "they only allowed me to show them to you because you are a stranger". Then he went on telling me that they are here, and talked about a citizen disclosure project that will imminently make their existence known to everyone. I was in no way prepared to be amazed by this guy, but it left me wondering what it was he was showing me, and who he was. I am writing this minutes after witnessing, and if he is still there when I get out of work, I plan on trying to get it on video. There is a lot of light pollution in this area so hopefully I can get something on film. In any event I figured it was definitely strange enough that I felt the need to make a report. I don't follow UFOs and conspiracy theories closely, but I am thinking whatever I witnessed made me believe.
