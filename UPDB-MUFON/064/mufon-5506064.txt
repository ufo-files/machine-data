{rjl} Triangle of lights steady

On the 11th of Dec. 2013 I was traveling home from work on Junction 214 toward Sumerco toward WV RT. 3. It was approximately 7:45 pm I was looking up out my driver side window to see if the sky was clearing, because it was suppose to be very cold that night, when I noticed three lights very close together in a triangle shape. I slowed down to see if it was blinking like a plane, but it was not. It traveled like a plane across the sky but I have never seen a plane without blinking lights and in that shape or formation before. I observed it for about 2 min. flying across the sky until it went out of sight over the mountain. Im not saying its a traditional UFO but, not a plane either.
