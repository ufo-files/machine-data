{jpg} sphere slowly acended then abruptly changed direction

At approx. 2:30 a.m. local time, I was taking my dogs out to pee. We have a neighbor who lives a little over 100 yards behind us. I noticed a bright glowing sphere just above and behind the house. The night was dark, so the sphere was easy to notice. It was orange, but bright like a spotlight. If I had to guess, I would estimate that the object was 15 - 20 feet in diameter. At first it just hovered. After several seconds it slowly ascended, grew slightly dimmer, then abruptly changed direction, moving off to the west, rapidly increasing in velocity as it went. It made no noise that I was able to hear. My dogs didn't react to it.
