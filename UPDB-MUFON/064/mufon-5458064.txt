Green Blue spherical object travelling straight a couple of metres above rooftops.

We were walking through the historical plantagenate part of Le Mans before going back to our hotel. It was a warm night, no precipitation with a little high cloud. Both of us looked up at above the rooftop in front of us. We saw a very bright blue green spherical object with what looked like a short flame exhaust. It travelled slowly from left to right, what looked like a few feet above the roof tops. There was no sound and we both felt very calm and a bit fascinated. It continued in a straight line for 1 to 2 minutes and then just vanished. There was no vapour trail or anything throughout and nothing when it vanished.
