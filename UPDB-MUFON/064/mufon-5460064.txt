{rjl} Lights seen

When I was driving home around 1:33am I was in a remote area when I noticed two lights just above the tree line. At first I thought it might be just a plane. The odd thing was there was no runways near that area and it seemed to be really low and moving very very slow for that height. I pulled over and rolled down my window expecting to hear an engine of a plane but the air was silent even though the lights were almost directly over head. I was also confused because they were bright white lights unlike anything I've seen on plane wings. After a few minutes I began to drive again but kept looking over my shoulder out the window towards the lights. once they were almost directly behind me over the road I noticed that the two lights had suddenly become four lights in the shape of a square but there was still no sound of any engines.
