Flashing Sphere/Circle Directly Overhead

I was sitting outside, looking away from the sky when it suddenly hit me that it was clear out today- finally- and that I could see the blue sky...so I turned around to face North, towards the open sky, and the way that I normally look when skygazing from where I live. I looked North, and then immediately looked overhead, and was literally greeted with a circular-esque/spherical object very high up in the sky, 90 degrees overhead. It was luminescent, bright white and almost silvery, faintly glowing while hovering.

I recorded it above me for a few minutes, from around 10:58 to 11:02/11:03. While I was recording the sky it was coming in and out of appearance in the sky- it was VERY high up, and even with my great sight I eventually lost track of it as it seemingly began a slow descent up, becoming more and more a pinprick of light in the sky, and less like an object that was closer to me physically, reflecting light from a surface as well. Which is how it felt/appeared/looked at first.  

At 11:20 I CAUGHT IT FLASHING AGAIN, in the relatively same area, about 85 up from the horizon, to the NNE, as I sat facing looking due North. This time whatever "came back" was definitely pulsing light, and it did the most incredible thing! 
It was emitting a very discernible almost silvery white flash, and then it would alternate and roll from the silver/white into a luminescent spring GREEN/green almost *pulse/beam* of light that seemed to emanate outward gently in the the sky, and then it would change back to the white/silver glow/pulse/light emanation, and I was literally too breathless to even *want* to grab my camera, and break eye-contact in order to try to record. It kept pulsing between the white and green for about half a minute, visibly coming in and out of view, and would fade out for a few seconds and then begin to fade/appear, and blinked about for the next four minutes or so.
