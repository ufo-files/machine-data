White light hovering over crop silos, then disapearing into the clouds

I was in Zábřeh town train station standing on a platform, waiting for my train to Brno where i study. I noticed stationary white light over the silos near the train station about the size of basketball, maybe bigger. It looked like a hovering drone with its lights blasting on at first. But suddenly it moved about 200ft to the left. The motion was sudden and linear, with no acceleration or braking visible in the movement. I fly FPV drones in my free time so i know how quadcopters move and this was not anything like it. Travel path was perfectly straight horizontal line. Then it jumped up into the clouds in the same fashion of movement and i lost visual contact. It was too far for my smartphone to record it.
