Witnessed a boomerang like object in the sky

My Wife saw a silhouette of either a square or boomerang shaped object. I did not see the shape but we both saw a blue point of light and an amber point of light. The amber point of light was fartherst away. The object was just above or a little below the tree line. The object banked in front of us as it was just above the road. I assumed this because we lost the amber light. We watched the blue point of light continue on a north northeasterly path over a field till we lost sight of it. The object was completely silent. We were on our way to Church at the time on Regan Church Road. I noticed the time after we lost sight of the object.
