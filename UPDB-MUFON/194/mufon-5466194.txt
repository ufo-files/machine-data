{td} Observed a tiny white light, traveling very fast. It suddenly turned into a large bullet shape, with the front appearing to be on fire,, then disappeared e it was on fire

On Tues, August 28th,at approximately 9:15 pm,I was sitting on my front porch and noticed a tiny white light, traveling low and very fast. As I was watching, it suddenly appeared, as if it just went through an invisible wall, as a very large bullet shaped object. The rear was a dull metallic color, and from the middle to the front, it appeared to be a very bright yellow/orange. The color pattern reminded me of something that had been dipped in wax, with no particular lines or pattern. The front was rounded just like a bullet and this front section was the only part that appeared to be on fire. As I was watching, it suddenly disappeared, like it went back through an invisible wall. During this entire event, I never noticed any kind of sound. As I watched it completely disappear I waited thinking I would hear a crash or see smoke, but nothing more was observed. I have observed 100's of meteors and this object did not fall from the sky, with a blaze that gradually faded. This object was traveling a very intentional east to west pattern, with a slightly noticeable decline, but not like a meteor. I had my binoculars and cell phone right beside me, but it happened to quickly to capture. I used my binoculars after it disappeared, in search of any remaining visibility. I was hopeful that I would at least see the original tiny white light that I had observed in the beginning, however, I did not observe anything after it disappeared.
