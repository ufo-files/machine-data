Aircraft in the distance moving in a seemingly random pattern

At about 3am, I woke up and looked outside my bedroom window. Off in the distance (North West from my location) I could see something that blended in with the stars, but was significantly brighter and was flashing green and white lights. As I watched the object, I noticed that it was moving towards the north slowly. Suddenly the craft stopped and hovered. And the it would abruptly change direction to the south. The directional changes seemed very abrupt and random. I also observed sudden/rapid elevation changes.

Approximately 10-15minutes after my initial observation, my wife woke up. I pointed out the craft to her, and she confirmed that the object was moving. After letting the dog out to go to the bathroom, she went back to bed.. At 325am I went to the bathroom. When I returned, the craft was gone.

Neither my wife nor I have ever seen aircraft move with the speed and sudden trajectory changes that we observed this morning.
