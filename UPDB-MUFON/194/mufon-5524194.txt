Two Star like objects appeared faded out and reappeared numerous times.

A very bright star like object caught my eye. It was much brighter then any other star in the sky and after looking at it for a few seconds, it faded out. Another star like object appear a few feet (objectively) above it. It then faded out and the first would reappear. By the time I had a camera, only the one object remained, changed color to blue then red then white again. This was seen by five adults. We eventually got tired and went to bed leaving our star like object still hovering.
