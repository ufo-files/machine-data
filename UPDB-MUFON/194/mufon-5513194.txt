{cak} 3 red lights in a triangle pattern, pulsed then turned to a bright yellow and disappeared

I first thought the light was a satellite but after i saw it flash in a triangular pattern I realized it was not. I tried to see if a could take a photo with my phone but no lights were visible from the phone. I called my fiance to come outside to check it out but she did not answer. I was excited and I didn't want to take my eyes off it in fear i would miss something.
