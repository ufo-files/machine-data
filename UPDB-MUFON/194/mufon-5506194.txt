{cak} Metal fragments found at site need further lab testing to determine whether isotope ratios indicate extraterrestrial origin.

Researchers Chuck Wade and Art Campbell have recovered metal fragments from the Plains of San Agustin site long thought to possibly have been the locale of a UFO crash around 1947. Preliminary analysis of these fragments suggests a possible anomalous nature of the metal, but multiple testing is needed to corroborate/clarify this.
