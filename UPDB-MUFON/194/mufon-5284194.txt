Observed a perfect triangle formed from three points of light at each tip to the right of the moon

At approximately 5:30 PM, 11/15/2021, I was looking at the moon while walking my dog. To the right of the moon, I observed a perfect triangle of three pinpoints of white light. 

At first, the three points of light appeared to be stars. However, as I was looking at this perfectly formed triangle, the two points of light at each bottom corner of the triangle slowly faded away leaving the point of light at the top the only remaining point of light. The remaining point of light then disappeared immediately with no fading effect. The points of light at the corners of the triangle were not as bright as the point of light at the top of the triangle. The top point of light was very bright. The area within the triangle was black without a trace of light.

These were just points of light that were the size of what stars would appear to be. They were not moving at all. There were no distinguishing shapes or what appeared to be aircraft. The triangle these points of light formed was perfectly formed and was not a triangle of stars. 

There were a few clouds around, being blown around by the wind. The moon was quite bright. Bright enough to obscure the stars I normally see this time of night. The points of light I saw were not affected by the moonlight at all. Which leads me to believe these lights were close to earth.
