An extremely bright looking triangular glowing craft which took an abrupt turn towards our building, slowed down and starting ascending at an angle.

At about 7:10 PM on February 17th 2016 in my apartment faced towards the North side, right in front of Taylor Creek Ravine. My mom witnessed what seemed to be an extremely bright helicopter. It looked very much like a white star, but was pretty far away, and at a high altitude. I ignored it for a second, but then it somehow looked like it changed directions toward my building; it was at an angle downwards. I have an extremely clear window in my 2nd bedroom, so I ran to the bedroom and was absolutely mesmerised. I saw what appeared to be a glowing triangle with three distinct lights - blue, red, and white, flashing periodically, while at an angle ascending upwards very, very slowly, but at the same time very, very fast. I saw it up close, maybe within 400-500 feet. It was so close that I was asking myself: is this real? My mother was right beside me as this event happened to confirm that my eyes were not playing tricks on me. It looked somewhat large, maybe 30 ft. My heart was beating extremely fast - it looked so cool, yet I can say for sure that it was NOT an airplane, or helicopter. I saw it with perfect visual acuity that it was a defined triangle with lights. I lost sight of this object when it moved rightwards relative to my window, moving towards the south direction (upwards).
