object hovering below severe storm cloud top then lifted into cloud as jet approached

A storm had just passed through the area an I was standing outside watching the lightning to the south. I then noticed a round object hovering below a cloud top to the thunderstorm. What caught my attention was the black dot standing out against the cloud and lightning. I was able to watch the object for over 4 minutes.I first thought the object was a balloon but with the wind in the area from the storm it was stopped. Object gave the appearance it was spinning.It was black and a second color that was nearly the color of a flame or copper color. As I watched it it began to lift into the clouds and the flame color disappeared. This happened about 30 seconds before a jetliner headed to Hobby airport flew just below the area the object was in. I then noticed it just to the side of a cloud and it lifted extremely fast into the clouds as a second jet flew into the area. I did not see the object after that. THe two jets were Southwest Airline 737s.
