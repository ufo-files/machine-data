{cak} five of us riding in a Ford convertible observed a spherical silver object traveling very slow between two mountain peaks.

Five of us traveling in a Ford convertible Headed back to Kelly-Dahl campground located above the town of Nederland Colorado. There were mountain peaks to our left. At one point I happened to look to my left and observed a spherical silver object traveling very slow between two mountain peaks. I asked the driver to pull over and told everyone to look in the direction of the two mountain peaks. There was no doubt that this object was a UFO. We were awestruck and just stood there staring at this silver object. There was a vehicle behind us that I motioned to pull over. I ran up to the vehicle and asked everyone if they were seeing the same spherical silver object that we were witnessing.  Each person in the vehicle said that in fact they were observing the same object. After several seconds to about a minute the object disappeared behind a mountain and we did not see it again.
