{rjl} Hovering over a manmade body of water.

I returned to my boyhood home as I often do; which is now 200 plus feet below the surface of an isolated lake. It was July 26, 2013. I began snapping photos of the lake with my camera aimed in the direction of the sun; one photo after another. I heard no airplane or other motorized air vehicle. I was focused on the landscape and not the details of the landscape. When I returned home, I began looking at the photos; probably 20 to 25 photos of the lake. I noticed that in one photo (just one) there was an object in the sky which I could not identify. I had heard that UFOs often hover over bodies of water. Tonight I was watching a documentary on UFOs and I remembered the photo from July of 2013 and decided to submit it for the observation of experts. I do believe in UFOs but I am not an expert to render an opinion.
