{cak} glowing red light with small flame trail became star size after coming close to landing

My girlfriend and I were wanting to watch a comet shower that the news said was supposed to happen that night. We moved to the window and observed a red/orange pulsating/flashing orb coming in our direction. Thought it was maybe a astri coming to the atmosphere and then it kept growing in size and abruptly changed direction to the right (west) and started slowing down in speed extremely. It hovered for about a 30 second period then disappeared into the tree line. After a couple minutes the trees began to glow. We started to walk out across the yard toward the light when the orb/light took off toward the sky in a 25 maybe 30 degree angle south and at a high rate of speed. After about 30 seconds the light became the size of star and stayed there all night.
