I was driving S on the turnpike, right near the consulate drive exit. I saw what looked like a helicopter in the distance, as I drove closer I noticed it was hovering, and then when it was in eyesight, I'd never seen something like it. It was pod shaped craft, with struts that stuck out from the bottom of the pod. There was no obvious propeller, and there was no sound to the machine.
