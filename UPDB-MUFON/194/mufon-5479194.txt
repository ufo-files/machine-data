Like a shooting star which changed direction on a couple occasions, very high in the sky

So I was walking outside my college in Grimsby, England. As I was walking walking out I looked at the moon and thought how cool the moon looked. Then I saw this very star like object in the sky which was very very high up and was moving quite slowly. It looked exactly like a big star.I realised it wasn't a star or any kind of aircraft by how it moved. It looked exactly like a star but moved in a weird way. It'd often go up and down changing heights and was heading west but it still remained quite slow. Unfortunately as I tried to look at it more the light just disappeared. I moved myself away from street lights but I looked up and it was gone.
