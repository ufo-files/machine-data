Fishing on lake winnipeg, weather turned bad two tornados spotted 1 hour after event.

My family and I were fishing off shore of Lake Winnipeg, during light rain, off to the southeast, I noticed what I thought might be a big ship or a water tower in the distance, on the lake. it could not be a water tower as the there was no shore to be seen, I asked my son what he thought it could be and he was unsure. He pulled out his cannon camera and telephoto lens and said " What the hell is that? he showed me and we showed my wife and his brother and his fiance, no one had a clue as to what we were seeing. It looked like a black oblong pill in the middle of the lake 30 to 50 feet above the water with no shore behind it and it was stationary. It seemed to change its shape or perhaps was turning and sometimes appeared taller or thinner. Once when the clouds broke and direct sunlight came out it disappeared altogether, only to reappear in the exact spot when the skies again were covered in dark cloud, the weather soon turned horribly bad and as we were getting pelted with hard rain, it started lightning and thundering badly, we started to pack up when we noticed the tornado coming from hyw 8, at our backs. We left some gear there and drove to Gimli ( Town). Thats basically it except that a water spout or water tornado came to Winnipeg beach, where we had been. from the same direction that the craft was after we had left, (multiple accounts.)
