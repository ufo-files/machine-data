Light stationary, moved, stopped, moved, shot off like a shooting star in opposite direction.

I was standing outside looking at the stary night, beautifully clear and you can see all the stars light up the sky, completely silent, not an aircraft in sight, when i noticed something odd, i thought it was a plane but it made no sound, it was a satellite because of the light and movement, i looked at this star like object, suddenly appear in the sky, just kind of, hovering in the sky, it appeared with a sort of flash, solid white in colour, it stayed still and flashed like (flash.flash.....flash.flash) which is odd as no aircraft has that pattern, especially in solid white, so i continued watching, no audio coming from it (you can hear sound from hundreds of miles away due to the ocean being so flat but no noise) it then suddenly moved forwards a bit and did the same patterns, then all of a sudden, it literally shot off in the opposite direction (backwards) like a shooting star with such force, you could hear a little rumble and also feel it, not that loud, and it left a trail just like a regular shooting star would, only then for the outside pool heater to kick in when it never normally does at this time or temperature, so either it's all connected or that was one hell of a set of coincidences, but either way, that moving light at the beginning, was certainly strange with the colour, patterns, movement and no audio.
