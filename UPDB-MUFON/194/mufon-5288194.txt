unidentified flying humanoid

On August 10 2020 i was standing in the court yard between my house and garage (they are detatched)when i noticed a weird ziggly looking cloud that i thought odd. We had been having evening thunder clouds with light drizzling and I thought the shape of the further ziggly cloud was interesting enough to take a photo of. I snapped the picture on my LG phone and honestly forgot about it until last month when I was going through pictures in my phone and deleting ones that were extra. So in deciding what i was going to keep, I happened across the cloud photo. I thought there was a fleclk of dirt on my screen and upon realizing that it wasnt dirt or debris I zoomed in and discovered what I initially thought was a bird.....but is obviously NOT!
