Bright spot, ejecting lots of debris

Looking straight up, I saw a huge nebula shaped cloud on a clear night. Through binoculars, I saw a bright central spot. I quickly set up my telescope, and could see this moving in relation to the stars, but not fast like a meteor. As it moved, lots of debris was coming off two sides of the object, similar to a neutron star. Then everything dimmed. I suspect the sun was illuminating the event. Maybe space junk.
