Took a photo of the sunset from SWA fly 2522, on descent into Nashville. Zoomed in to see three disk shaped objects. Maybe lens flares?

I took a photo of the sunset from SWA Flt 2522 at 4:58 pm central time, 6 minutes before landing, on 1-12-21. When I zoomed into the photo there were three objects surrounding the sun, one slightly west (left) and above the plane of the sun and also above the horizon. The second was above the sun and slightly to the left. The third was above the sun and slightly to the right. The upper objects appeared closer together. My son related that these objects could be lens flares. I only took one photo and did not see the objects clearly until I zoomed in.
