egg/oval in sky, still, then disappered before my eyes

After me and my husband went to lunch, We headed down to my inlaws house for a visit, driving towards the sun, my husband pointed out this metal looking egg/oval type object in the sky , just under the sun , it was still not moving, It was quite far away but still could see the shape clearly. So I assume It was very big. We drive down the road for about another 5 secs, and it was like it faded away. We couldnt believe our eyes, We get to the red light down the road, turn left, drive a little more and we see it again hovering. and it faded once more. Clear blue skys no clouds what so ever.
