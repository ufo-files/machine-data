{rjl} Looked like a star moving in a heavilylight polluted area

I waited to post this because I thought maybe there was a reasonable explanation for this, but the more i thought about it, the weirder it gets.

I was outside smoking a cigarette (as i always do). I always star gaze and have seen some pretty cool things in the sky (including the Int'l Space station) but nothing out of the ordinary. However, this one particular object was able to get my attention. 

I first noticed it south as it appeared to pulsate a very bright orange light. It kind of looked like a fire ball in the sky. This is how i was even able to notice it. It then also appeared to go straight up into the sky. I's kind of hard to tell size and direction when you are on the ground and at a distance. But i was convinced this object was heading straight up. Then it appeared to change direction and go due North right over my house. It literally looked like a star was moving. Just barely bright enough to see. No noise, or flashing lights either. I've never seen anything like it before. My initial thought was a satellite, but I live right outside of Philadelphia, the light pollution is normally too strong to make satellites visible. And if they are visible, i would have seen something like this more often. None of it makes sense. Thought i should share.
