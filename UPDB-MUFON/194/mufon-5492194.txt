{jpg} Round disk/saucer shaped UFO with blinking lights around the edge -stationary no sound

I was at the end of my driveway around 9-10 pm talking to my neighborhood friend who lived directly across the street from me. He was a few years older than me. I believe the year 1990, or 1991. 

We were standing by the street talking like we had many times before, and he looked up and said what is that 1 pointing into the sky. I looked where he was pointing and we saw a disk/saucer shaped UFO hovering above the next door neighbor1s house. This UFO was quietly hovering about 300 feet off the ground about the tree tops. It had changing lights blinking around the outside shape of it 1 giving it the outline of a saucer shape. 

We said to one another what1s that? And stared at it for a short time, and then we got scared and decided to run home. I ran home and my parents said I had eyes the size of grapes from what I saw. My dad decided to call the fire department and ask if anybody else had reported seeing a UFO around that same time. The dispatch reported nobody had seen a UFO that they were aware of.
