Bright white cigar-shaped object streaming across dark sky leaving extensive trail. Moving extremely fast and silently.

About 0600 am I saw a bright white cigar-shaped object streaming across dark sky leaving an extensive multi-part trail that remained visible for several minutes after the object flew out of my sight. The object was moving in a general south to north direction at a very high rate of speed and made no sound. There was also no odor associated with the sighting. I was unable to determine an approximate altitude for the object due to lack of viewable reference points, but it was approximately 45 degrees above the horizon.
