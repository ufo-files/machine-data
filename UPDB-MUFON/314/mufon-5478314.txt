{jpg} Two objects appeared in my pictures  I did not realize they were there until later showing off my new phone

On my way home from work I stopped at the grocery at about 530 or so.  It was a gloomy afternoon and I noticed behind me a rainbow.  My husband gave me a brand-new phone so I took three photographs of the rainbow. I only just realized there was something in two of the photographs.  After enlarging the photos on my phone, both objects appear to be disc shapes with lights that go around the objects. I did not notice them at the time I had taken the photographs with my new I phone 6S.  Only the first two photos show the two objects. I included the third photo because it shows the rest of the rainbow. The last two photos are only close-ups of the first two photos that show the objects within.
