Bright Venus type light moving s.e directly overhead. Large view area. Contiued same path till no longer visable.

I was at work & stepped out to take a smoke break. Ive been interested in planet location so I looked directly up to locate Mars & Saturn when I immediately noticed a very bright light. Im familiar a bit with the position of bright stars & this one wasnt right. I was alone but still out loud I said "what in the world is that"? I focused on a nearby star to make sure my eyes werent playing tricks on me, to verify that the "object" was moving. I could see that it was moving just like a satellite would in orbit but it was far brighter. I watched it until it went out of view due to a neighboring single story building. Ive seen satellites move & Ive never seen one stay as bright for so long as this did. I was so curious about the object that when I left work at 11:pm I came home & went to the tracker site for the ISS to see if thats what it was but based on its path over the east coast it didn't seem to fit. Ive never seen anything unexplainable in the sky but was strange.
