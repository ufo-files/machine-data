Object slowly crossed the sky

I was at church camp, Kirmont, near Bellfountaine, Ohio in about August of 1968. On our last night, a clear starry night, about 120 youth and counsellors were having a worship service on a hilltop at around 9 p.m. We all saw this triangular craft with purple and green lights glide across the sky, not directly overhead, but at about a 45 degree angle. No one discussed what we saw other than to say, "Oh wow, did you see that?" It has never left me. Bellfountaine is about an hour by car from Wright Patterson AFB.
