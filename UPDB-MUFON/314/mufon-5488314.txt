ISS Live 21/09/2015 UFO capture

Was watching the ISS Live feed from my laptop. It was crossing the area over New-Zealand where I saw a bright orange/red light on the screen tracking the ISS or possibly harvesting energy from the storm at the time. It had 4 bright lights and they were all linked together (part of the same structure). It stays in shot for A good minute and a half meanwhile its pulsing brighter and dimmer. Zooming in on particular areas near the rings and the UFO there can be seen smaller crafts (4-6) which appear to be solid also. The rings disappear along with all crafts and UFOs some time after. Video was taken on a Samsung S6 of the laptop screen with the live feed.
