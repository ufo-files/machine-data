I know what I saw and it lasted 2 seconds

I was smoking a cigarette late in my back yard one night when it happened. It’s dead silent out, no crickets, no cars, nothing. If a plane flies over, you hear it. If a car drives by, you hear it. It’s a quiet neighborhood and most people are in bed. The only lights on are the street lamps out front (to my south) There’s medium cloud cover. It’s 50-60 degrees (no big sounds). While looking at the clouds and stars a giant black triangle flies over my head from the northeast heading southwest. It’s dead silent, no lights with perfect sharp edges, a perfect geometric object. It lasted 1-2 seconds. It blocked out clouds and stars. The most striking and memorable aspect was the extreme silence because before and after the sighting, any car that drove in my general vicinity could be heard from either the engine or the tires. Domestic flights lights are likely grounded given the time of night. It’s possible, given the proximity of NASA, that it might have been a stealth bomber belonging to the military, but it would still give off some sound; it blocked out cloud cover so that made it relatively close
