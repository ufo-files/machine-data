Object moving North changed direction

I saw an Object heading North approx 5000ft. There was like a meteor to the right of the object, the object, in turn, descended and changed direction to the south East. It did this manoeuvre at the same speed it was travelling. Then the object descended giving off green flashing lights. 2 lights on each side of the craft. that blinked in unison. 
The object then changed direction back to the South still giving off the two green flashing lights. Flashed every 2 or 3 seconds. The object then descended to the ground and out of view.
