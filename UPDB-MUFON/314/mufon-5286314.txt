Brief bright light moving through the sky at low altitude at unusually fast speed

Sitting on the roof deck of an eight story building in the South End neighborhood of Boston, I noticed a bright light, seemingly circular in shape, moving really fast through the sky. I was able to bring it to the attention of two other adults who witnessed the light speeding across the sky before the light vanished. It did not make any sound, and was in the sky below the clouds. It did not appear to be traveling downward towards the ground. The quality and intensity of the light did not appear to be variable; rather, it remained constant for the few seconds it remained visible.
