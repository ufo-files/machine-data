Sherwood Park - Chevron or Triangle

It was Mar 03, 2012 at 5:03 in the morning. I looked out the back window to see an overcast sky with a few snow flakes falling. Just then I noticed a faint hue of light moving in the clouds. My camera is always ready to go and was sitting on the kitchen table. I was looking south and noticed that it was now on a determined path to head north. I kept snapping pictures as it went over my house. When I ran to the front window I could not pick it up again. The attached was shot into the clouds using a 16 megapixel camera with night vision capabilitied and zoom. Looks like a Chevron or Triangle.
