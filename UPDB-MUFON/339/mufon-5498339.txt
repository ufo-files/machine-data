{rjl} Orange Sphere hovering over beach/Ocean disapearing and reapearing

I was walking my dog about 1:30 am. I always look in the sky when walking my dog. On this night I noticed an Orange Sphere light over Siesta Key Beach that was floating over the ocean. The beach is 1 mile away from my house.It was quite large. Looking at it from 1 mile away, it was just a bit smaller than a dime, so I would imagine it would be quite large if I was standing underneath it. It was very bright and it moved from right to left and stopped. Then it moved back right and stopped, always staying at the same height. Then it started to shrink until it disappeared. In the 15 years living in this house I always have looked up at night, but I have NEVER seen anything like this.
