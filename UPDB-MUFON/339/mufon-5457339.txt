{tjd} It came down over the trees I was at moved off south bound.

At approx 9:30 pm on Oct 23, 2019 I observed a circle craft approx 100 ft in diameter come straight down to the tree tops in front of me. It looked almost translucent but the outer part of the craft was glowing a beautiful red and faded to a grey to metal look in the center with what looked like a door angled at the bottom between for glowing green globes under it. As the craft began to accelerate to whole craft started glowing red where as before as it was stationary was only glowing on the outer edges of the craft. I have to admit it was the most beautiful colors I have ever seen. My property has Indian Mounds directly under where the craft hovered, not that it has anything to do with the sighting I'm guessing. My neighbors said at the same time I seen the craft they were a few miles away coming out of their barn at observed the sky glowing a bright red in my area and thought it looked really odd at that same time frame.
