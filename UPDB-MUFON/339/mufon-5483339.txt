{jpg} Gray/white dull sphere same color as the hazy sky in one spot about 300- 500 feet above me. Slowly faded away.

My wife and I were canoeing on the Susquehanna river between Columbia and Washington Boro. On a Sunday morning in August 16, 2015. The sky was slightly hazy and the air was fairly still. For no apparent reason I looked up and looked directly at a white/light gray sphere I estimated to be 3-5 feet diameter. This distance could be wrong I am not totally sure if I was looking at a small object close by or a large object far away. I concluded it must be small I could see the shading on the bottom as you would expect a solid round object to have. It looked like a volley ball just sitting there. It was not moving I did not want to take my eyes off of it or I would have trouble finding it against the same color sky. I pointed for my wife to look and she could not find it. After about 30 seconds it slowly faded away and I did not see it again.
