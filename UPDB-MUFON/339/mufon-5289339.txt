strange light moving in different directions

Just after I went to bed, around 1 AM on 3-7-2021, I could see a tiny light in the sky through the french doors. It was flashing like an airplane but didn't look like it was moving anywhere. I got up and went to the door to take a closer look and I saw red and green lights flashing while it was hovering. It stated to move strangely, a little to the right and a little to the left as it rose, but it was way too high to be a helicopter. I couldn't really tell if it was moving laterally or kind of in a spiral motion as it went up. It looked like it traveled farther north, but then seemed to come south again. It dropped down a little and continued the right and left movements, but all staying in the same general area.

I pulled up SkyMap on my phone and, if it was calibrated correctly, it was located between the constellations of Draco and Hercules. There's no guarantee that's accurate since I was just doing a quick check to see if anything was showing up in that area of the sky.

I couldn't see it on my cellphone camera.

I watched until about 1:30 then went back to bed. I woke up again around 3:30 AM and it was still in the sky, with the same blinking lights and motions, but a little farther to the Northeast.

I have no idea what I was seeing, but an airplane would have followed it's flightpath and disappeared (yes, I live near an airport), a satellite would have followed a path and disappeared.... it moved like a helicopter but seemed way too high and was there for a very long time. So who knows?
