{jpg} Elevation of a plane. Bright. Held steady elevation then went straight up and into the atmosphere the light dimming then an orange color as it left.

Flew From the Honda plant in OH/North Lewisburg area over Marysville, holding a somewhat straight path and was moving how a fish swims back and forth. It looked just like a star or plane lights. No flashing just a starburst. As close as a plane losing altitude to land in Columbus. In fact two commercial planes were flying West past it at a higher altitude. I watched it fly over 33 towards Ostrander/Dublin OH and it took a turn upward and the star like appearance grew dull then all of a sudden an orange red color as it exited the atmosphere...growing smaller and smaller and disappeared. Same entity appearance as what I witnessed a few times last year above Marysville and Honda area. No clouds. I saw planes flying toward it heading west at the end of the even from the East as well. Stars all clearly visible at 10:30-10:40 pm local time. No video. Was too afraid I would miss something by going and grabbing my phone.
