While in the backyard I saw a white cylindrical object slowly moving in SE direction.

I went outside to smoke as I do everymorning with my dog and while outside normally sit and look around. This morning it was a clear blue sky over head not a cloud in sight. I saw what appeared as an object moving in the sky out of the corner of my eye. Looked over and what I saw for about a full 4 minutes if not longer was a white cylinderical object moveing in an oddly way. Seemed to look as if it was gliding threw the air. No sounds and No this was not a plane or blimp. I am sure of this as I could clearly see itin very good detail it had no back tail like a plane or wings. basically what it look like was a tube shaped object. It was solid white same as a ciggeratte if you were to cut off the filter. Exactly the same. Expect this was I would say estimating the distances much larger then a 747. It also had no nose shape in the front and there was no noise with the object it passed behind a tree at the same time it hit the roof section I saw it turn and head north. I ran back inside the house and headed to the front were I was sure to get a much better look at the object at that point but by the time I got there it was gone. When i first noticed it I thought it was a plane but the longer I look at it the more I noticed the detail of it and the movement was more like a balloon then a plane. But the ends on both sides where flat like a cut tube it would not make since for a balloon to have such a sharp cut on both ends.
