Reported by state police officer from 10 miles away.

I did not witness the event but it was reported by a state police officer that a glowing green object the size of a whale appeared to descend in the town of Morris or Bantam at 2 am on the 11th of April, 2012 and another witness from Bantam claimed to see the object land in Bantam lake. Fire and rescue teams were sent to investigate but found no debris or oil on the small lake and did no further investigation. The siting was reported on the local news network and in the newspapper. A meteor the size of a whale should have made enough noise to wake those who live on the lake. It would be nice to have someone perform an underwater search. The object was not described as on fire but just glowing.
