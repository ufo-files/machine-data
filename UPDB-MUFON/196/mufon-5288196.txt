Bright ball of light

Doing some night fishing at a local lake and I see what I thought was a helicopter search light coming over the rise just above the tree tops. After a few minutes I realized it was almost over the lake and I couldn't hear the rotors. So I picked up my six D cell MAG light I keep for a tool / protection and I turned it on and shined it at the ball of light, nothing happened until I singled with a Dot Dot Dash, Dot Dot Dash. I singled twice with my flashlight it was then that the object slowly turned by making a slow sweeping 90 degree turn, took off out of sight in less than a second. 
If that's one of ours then why are we still using fossil fuels that are destroying our habitat, or are we terraforming our home for someone more advance.
