{dr}Object was 10o clock from planet Mercury then left

I walked onto my porch for a cigarette and looked up at planet mercury or Venus whichever one is bright in the morning in the eastern sky before sunrise just like I always do because it looks pretty cool, And I noticed there was another bright light about ten O clock feom the planet. This was strange. There is usually no other bright light in the sky like except the lone planet. The other bright object stayed there for about three seconds then to my absolute surprise the light, which I thought was just another star, suddenly moved to the left for about three seconds at a constant rate ( not too fast) and then just vanished. It looked like it sort of imploded and it was gone. I know what I saw. I'm out here every morning and there is only one orb of light in the eastern sky before the sunrise, and that is a planet. This other light did not belong there. And suddenly it left. This took place around 4:45 AM on June 4th,2014. I don't know what to make of it. But I do know that it was not a geese, or swamp gas or a weather balloon. Trust me. It was spectacular in the least. I felt moved by it. As if it wanted me to just see it and then vanish. The whole event lasted about seven seconds but that's more than enough time for me to know what I saw.
