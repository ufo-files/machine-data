{cak} Dog alerted me to something. Saw a blueish white super bright (white/blue) light stay stationary and engage in odd behavior before vanishing. 2 min

I was sitting with my dog in my golf cart looking at the pond, when he started to growl and made a couple barks and brought my attention to the sky. I look to the southeast and noticed an extremely bright light that appeared to be very close and very large. I started taking video of the object as it moved nowhere. It essentially set stationary as far as I could tell from my vantage point. I can't explain what it was. It made no sound. It was extremely bright. It made my dog very uneasy. The object began making strange maneuvers so to speak. I don't want to call it phase-shifting, but it was very interesting. Almost like it wasn't shifting through dimensions to move....bear in mind it very well may be the camera trying to focus on an object however I did have my camera supported. This is a phone Galaxy note 9, which does take great pictures, and I did have the camera supported on the front windshield the golf cart with a steady hand. The object out of nowhere decided that it was just going to disappear. I took a picture, the last picture I saw of it looks as though I used my s pen to erase a squiggly line in the middle of the sky. I really don't think this is extraterrestrial in nature, however I cannot explain it. It vanished. I look forward to any info into what this may be. I have more media, but don't want to spend the time uploading it. I hope there is a quick and easy explanation and I can't move on.
