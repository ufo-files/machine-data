Green disc shape hovered across sky under moon then slowed and sped off in extreme speed and vanished

Me my partner and my Son (age 10) were driving toward Derry City (Northern Ireland) via the old Letterkenny Road at around 5pm New Years eve and were admiring a waxing moon above the river foyle. We collectively observed a large green disc shape, dull behind the clouds then emerge out and move slowly across the sky for about 1-2mins, then it came to stop and sped off at an unusually fast pace and dissapeared. Whilst this was happening we were asking eachother could it be a plane, or helicopter but it did not follow any of the usual patterns. We are used to lots of helicopter/drone activity here in Derry due to the troubles but this one has baffled us. It was unlike anything we have ever witnessed.
