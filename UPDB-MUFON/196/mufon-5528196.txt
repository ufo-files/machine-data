driving south on I-95 when my wife and i saw a u.f.o.

my wife and i were driving home from plymouth maine as we had come up to visit,between clinton almost to farmington i saw a green light out of the corner of my eye when i looked up we saw an object at least 16 feet across ,i pointed it out to my wife at which time it went completely black then like a dimmer switch on a light the green color came back,we were right under it it was at the top of an enormous pine tree and it seemed to dwarfe it i went to stop and my wife punched me she later told me she was very scared of what would happen if we stopped.we observed it do this color change three times before it was out of our mirror then she told me i could go back,one other thing to mention a state trooper had someone pulled over about 3 miles down the road,anyone who knows this stretch of interstate know how flat and long it is.it appeared the object was observing the state trooper.i personally was more curious than scared.if anyone else has seen anything loke this please post it.one things for sure if i see it again or anything like it i will stop and we always carry a camera with us.
