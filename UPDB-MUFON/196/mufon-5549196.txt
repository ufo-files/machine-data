Peculiar lights over small Georgia town!

It was approximately 10pm on Saturday, October 14th, 2006. I walked out onto my back deck to enjoy the crisp, cool night. The heavens sparkled above me, even the Milky Way was clearly visible. After about 10 minutes of gazing north, I turned around so I could gaze at the stars behind me, the southern sky. When I turned, a flickering light caught my attention. I watched the light for a moment, believing it to be merely a fading or twinkling star. However, the longer I stood and stared, the more it became clear it was not a star. I can only describe the flashing as if someone were cutting a flashlight on and then off again. After approximately 1 minute of flashing every 5-7 seconds, a second object(on the first object's left)flashed once. Then the initial object began to move in a northerly direction. For whatever reason, it turned back to where it began. This went on for a few minutes, back and forth. I ran in and retrieved my fiance, who came out in time to see the object moving and flashing, one last bright flash and then one final fading flash. The object ceased all activity and was not seen again, even though we stayed up for hours searching the night sky for it. This all occurred roughly within a 5 minute time span.
