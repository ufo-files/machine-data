{rjl} A huge 'half moon' clearly visible just over the horizon

I had left a friend's home in E. Oceanside a few minutes after 10pm (I noticed the time exactly on my car radio because I wanted to hear a program that started at 10:07). I drove west on Oceanside Blvd. and about a half mile west of the College Blvd. intersection, the road slightly curved and I saw what looked exactly like a giant half moon, as big as the Super Moons we had seen over the late summer, but it was a half moon, bright orangey-yellow. All aspects were exactly like the moon, but it was too close to the horizon, and a little too big, and since I look up in the sky most nights when I take my dog out, I knew that everything about this was wrong! It scared me to death, I made a quick u-turn and called my friend and told him I was coming back, I needed him to see something and I quickly drove back to his house. We returned to the spot no more than 7-8 minutes later, and to the southwest a large glow could be seen between the trees, but there were obstructions (houses, hills, trees) and nothing could be seen clearly. He saw the glow, but no finite shape. As we drove back to his house, he kept looking out the back window, and couldn't see anything else. When I drove the same route again 10 minutes later, I could see no remains of the earlier light. I looked at the 11:00 news and searched the internet this morning, but haven't seen any news of this. It was incredible, and really scared me, I thought it was going to be some sort of End Times thing, I'm still shaking to think about it. I started crying on the way home because I felt like no one would believe me. Someone else HAD to see this!!!
