{nd} Observed 3 lights in the south east.

I was traveling south, On hwy 17-92 as I approached the Davenport sand mine I observed 3 yellow Orange lights in the south eastern sky. I tried to find a location so I could get a better view. As i proceeded another Group of 3 lights appeared. I proceeded to the Davenport baseball field. I saw only one group of lights I was able to video this group as it moved farther south and winked out.
