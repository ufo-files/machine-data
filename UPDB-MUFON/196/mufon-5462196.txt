On my way to work when I spotted ufo.

I was on my way to work this morning when I noticed a red light to the right of me which is north. It was traveling at a fast pace. I realized it is what we have been seeing. I stopped and it also stopped then it came my way. I was travelling west and it came east over my car. I was scared. The object was huge as it came my way the red light went out. There was a white light with a smaller red light. As it was over my car the lights disappeared and all I could see were the clouds. I was trying to phone my husband and finally got through to him. He stayed on bluetooth till I was in town.
