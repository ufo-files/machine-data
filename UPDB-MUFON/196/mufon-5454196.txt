High Altitude Series of Star like objects Moving North

05:20am looking North/ East at about 80' for the Conjunction of Jupiter and Mars when a star / Satellite came into view the sun was in progression but still dark. The first object did what satellites do and faded out when another object appeared in procession about 5" behind where the previous object had disappeared, no sooner had this object vanished then another appeared. In total, I had counted eight objects on the same trajectory. They were not all equally spaced when they appeared and the vanished out of the line of sight. Once the last object was seen I quickly logged into flight tracker, there was no aircraft movement up the coast on the Bayside only inland aircraft activity, a quick review of satellite tracker for Starlink showed they were in the top arc of the Northern Hemisphere. Amberley RAAF Base is 50klm's away to the West and William town to the South 1,000klm. I see aircraft departing Brisbane Airport regularly and easily identify air traffic with navigation lights. UFO's or a flight of High Altitude F-35's transiting up the coast at high altitude in stealth / military mode caught by rising sun?
