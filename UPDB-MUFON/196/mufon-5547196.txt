At DIA along rental car row at thrifty car rental watching planes take off dropping off my sister to get a rental. Facing north sitting in my truck caught something out of the corner of my eye toward the northwest looked up it was hard to describe something between a square and a rectangle with unknown apparatus (wings, antenna?) dull brown or rust in color with what appeared to be a hole offset in the corner. Thought it was cardboard in wind but it stayed stationary and rotated slow. A 737 flew under it it did not take evasive manuevers from that view it appeared the same size as the plane but hard to judge scale. Might have been possibly very high in atmosphere if so it would be huge. watched for maybe 2 minutes then it faded from site.
