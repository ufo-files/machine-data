{rjl} Police escort covered object being hauled with a low boy trailer.

We were traveling to a funeral & there was a flat bed low boy trailer coming down the road with a maroon tarp covered object that was ufo shaped, circular, flat, dome in the middle, flated circular shape on the bottom, being towed on the trailer. Took up two lanes of the road & we had to get over on the far side of the shoulder of the road & went right by barely squeezed by. Being escorted by the police. Our cousin's were about 5mins behind us & seen the exact same thing. This was just a few mins from Detroit metro airport. We all thought the same thing of a covered ufo being escoted down the road by the police being hauled on the trailer. Once at the dinner after the funeral it was the topic of conversation. None of us have ever seen a police escort with a oversized load by a semi truck. My husband (49) and I (41). Until we were watching the history channel about the bemudia triangle of Michigan we looked at each other & this is how we found this site to report what we seen.
