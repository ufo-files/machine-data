We were outside in the backyard, while me and my Girlfriend were stargazing and my boys were roasting marshmallows. I saw a bright object in the sky, at first we thought it was a space station because it had a constant flight path, then it stopped in the sky and some clouds started to move in. At this point to the naked eye we could see that it was under a cloud now which removed the idea that it was a space station because we could see that it was under a cloud.
