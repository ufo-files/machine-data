{cak} Sighting of multiple illuminated objects flying, hovering over Boise, Idaho.

I was exiting a parking lot at a SW Boise shopping center and saw a illuminated object hover and ascend directly above my location as several other illuminated objects were flying and hovering above the city. Within minutes of observing the objects and taking pictures with my cell phone the objects ascended and disappeared from sight.
