{jpg} Saw sphere flying through the sky.

I witnessed a light in the sky above the trees. It started out what I thought was a Y shaped object. By the time I got my wife’s phone out and started to film it had a spherical shape. It went behind the trees and I caught it on film just before going behind the trees and then for a few seconds after coming into the opening. It flew in a straight line to the north east and then I lost sight.
