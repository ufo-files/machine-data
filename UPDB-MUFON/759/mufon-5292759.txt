Star-like Object Descending Slowly Downward

On July 30, at approx 10:30 p.m. I was on my deck facing south when I noticed in the sky what appeared to be a bright star, I'm familiar with the location of the bright stars and this one looked new to me. I was taken back when I noticed the bright light was actually descending straight downward, slow and steady. The light started off very bright then dimmed down to what you see in the video. It appeared to be fairly far away. I watched it slowly descend straight downward for about two minutes, before the trees in my backyard blocked the view. When watching the video, you can use the trees as an outline to see how it's moving slow and steady downward.
