{rjl} 5 red lights moving in formation, disappeared 1 by 1. then a sixth moving the same. then gone.

my husband and i were walking downtown gainesville at 10pm on feb 15 2014. saw 5 red starlike lights moving in formation to the east, from north to south. they started to disappear one by one at a specific point in the sky, then another appeared to the north and followed the rest until all disappeared. there were no trees or buildings blocking the view and no clouds at all. they just blinked out one by one. we were completely awestruck and confused. not fearful but definitely convinced it was not anything mundane... we thought they were planes until the formation eas held perfectly and they started to disappear.
