SAW UFO AT THE EAGLES GAME

I WAS AT LINCOLN FINANCIAL FIELD WATCHING THE EAGLES GAME IN PHILADELPHIA.IT WAS THE 2ND QUARTER AND WE WERE SITTING IN SECTION 210 IN THE END ZONE UPPER LEVEL.MY FATHER LOOKED UP IN THE DIRECTION OF THE FIELD AND SAID EDDIE (THATS ME)LOOK THERE IS A UFO.SURE ENOUGH THERE WAS A SHINY SILVER OBJECT IN THE SKY.IT LOOKED OVAL IN SHAPE AND IT SLOWED DOWN FOR A SECOND THEN SPED UP AT A VERY HIGH RATE OF SPEED.I COULD SEE THE OBJECT MOVING ACROSS THE SKY NICE AND STEADY AND SMOOTH.IT DID NOT GO UP OR DOWN.BEFORE YOU KNEW IT WAS GONE.THE EVENT LASTED FOR LESS THEN 30 SECONDS.I HAVE TO ADMIT.THIS IS THE 1ST TIME I AND MY FATHER EVER SAW ANYTHING LIKE THIS.NO ONE CAN CHANGE MY MIND ABOUT WHAT I SAW.I KNEW THIS WAS SPECIAL.YES THERE WERE OTHER PEOPLE THAT SAW THE SAME THING.EVERYONE WAS TALKING ABOUT IT. IT WAS TRAVELING AGAINST THE DIRECTION OF THE WIND SO IN KNEW IT WAS NOT A BALLOON.I NEVER SAW A OBJECT MOVE THROUGH THE SKY THE WAS THIS DID.WELL THATS MY STORY. I HOPE MORE PEOPLE WILL REPORT WHAT THEY SAW.
