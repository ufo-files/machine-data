Strange, white-blue, regular flashing lights on a large scale, lit up section of sky.

Was watching a movie at home, window is right next to where I was sitting and all of a sudden I saw three quick flashes of light (like a camera) out the corner of me eye. I looked out and saw this multiple times over the next hour or more; I recorded it on my DSLR and have stuck it on YouTube. Will attach link to that.
When I first started watching I didn't know what to make of it. I have seen other weird things on the skies over Edinburgh to feel honest so my first thought, given that it was so immensely bright, was a possible UFO. Now I tried to rule out 'sensible' possibilities first including weather but there was no thunder or lightning forecast and we don't get a lot of that here usually anyway.
I don't know if I could say it had behaviour, so to speak, although I do think it had a regular pattern to it, regular but short bursts, like a two or three note arpeggio on a synth or a multi-shot burst on a camera. It's hard to put into words really, I tried several times whilst recording it, which, watching back, is almost unintentionally funny.
It amazed me. If you watch my footage I'm pretty obviously amazed, apologies for the occasional swearing that's in the video in advance.
I eventually just stopped watching after a few hours of the same thing, it seemed to be moving away and getting less intense plus I was getting tired with it being almost two hours after I paused the movie I'd been watching to begin with.
