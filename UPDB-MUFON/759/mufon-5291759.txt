Cylinder object on the highway

I was driving home with my son from Austin to San Antonio. While driving in New Braunfels traveling south past the Bucee's is when I saw it. As I came to the top of the hill on the highway, I saw an object hovering silver in color, and cylinder in shape. At first. I thought maybe it was a bird, but it just hovered. As I got closer I thought maybe it's a helicopter or some other plane. However, this object was completely motionless. I grabbed my son's arm and pointed up to where it was, and it was already gone. I have never seen anything like this in my life, and honestly, I wish I never did. I will now spend the rest of my life knowing that I saw a UFO. I'm a military veteran, who knows the difference between a plane, a bird, etc. This had no heat signature, or plume, it was as if it was suspended in gravity stuck. And then BAMMM!! It's gone. Craziest thing I've ever seen in my life.
