{rjl} Dark Acorn Shaped Object with Vertical Light and second green

While reviewing several sunset pictures taken o in Sargent, Texas with my Iphone, I noticed one that had a white light spot in the sky the others didn't have. After zooming in the picture, a dark acorn shaped object with bright vertical tubular lights appears high in the sky. Underneath it, near the clouds on the horizon appears a strange transparent green "bubble" object with a black internal "cube like" structure. Another photo that was part of a burst, shows the same object passing in front of the sun that looks like a pair of black headphones. I did not see these while taking the photos as it was bright sunlight. None of the other pictures taken before and after showed the objects.
