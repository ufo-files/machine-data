Driving home from day out in Blythe coastal town.partner sighted low flying aircraft ahead which stopped motionless in air then moved off slowly as we followed it.

My partner and I had just been to the coastal town of Blythe near Newcastle upon Tyne.we were on our way home heading south along the coastal route on Blythe road which runs through seaton sluice and takes you toward Whitley bay links.it was a beautiful bright and clear sunny day the time was around 1.30 in the afternoon. As we drove toward Whitley bay links my partner suddenly said ,have you seen how low that aeroplane is coming over?as I looked I noticed that the aeroplane was not moving.she said its bloody low isn't it but didnt seem to notice that it wasn't moving which I couldn't understand. I said I can't believe it but it is the same aeroplane I had seen a month earlier with my father at exactly the same location.that event left me totally bewildered and wandering what had I witnessed. I mentioned to my partner again it is not moving and this time I am going to pull over and stop for a better look. I slowed down to practically a stand still when suddenly behind me out of nowhere a car was right behind us flashing it's headlights to be past us. I can't remember the car actually overtaking us or where it went from that moment.this is strange as the road is straight but bends to the left at the bottom taking you around toward Whitley bay light house past the caravan park. The aeroplane came from our right which is west and followed a straight path alternately stopping and moving on as we approached.the aeroplane moved at roughly walking pace passing over Whitley bay cemetery and heading toward the light house.we came toward the bend and the aeroplane was right there in front of us bright blue and white lights flashing in sequence along the full length of both wings.as the aeroplane headed toward the light house it had practically came to a stand still in front of us.the plane looked like it was going to land in a near by field and came right down to practically ground level with lights still flashing out at sea toward the light house.i was in complete awe of what I was seeing as I was saying to my partner this is impossible for any kind of passenger aeroplane to do as we watched it hover at ground level.the aeroplane seemed to be signalling to something but I could not see anything around and then I thought to myself this is strange as there isn't anything around.i couldn't believe that no one else had seen this happening and that we were the only two people witnessing this event. As we neared the plane toward the light house it seemed as though it new it was being observed and moved slowly off and stopping keeping ahead of us as we tried to get closer. I remember looking and seeing what appeared to be a heat haze from the rear of the plane as it started to rise from ground level also I recall the plane seemed to have become a smaller version of what we had first witnessed a full sized Thompson air craft with all the same colours. We reached the right hand bend running along whitley bay links and had to continue driving as the craft looked to be ready to move off toward the light house.there was no sound or roar of any engines nor any sign of any landing gear and any increase in speed.my partner does not recall the event as the way I seen it for myself and has a totally different recollection to me.we had our differences of what we had just seen for the rest of our journey home.from the beginning of the sighting to the end seemed to last for 10 to 15mins all in slow motion I continue to question what we both saw on that day and have not yet got any answers for this account.
