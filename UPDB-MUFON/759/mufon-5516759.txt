{dr}golden fireball breaks up over rolla, mo

Last night, August 1, 2012 my family and i were driving some relatives home. they live about 3/4 of a mile to the west of the rolla hospital, so initially I thought I were seeing yet another chopper coming in for a landing, but this thing made no sound, and must have been further away. It sort of came into veiw as though it were a meteor, then it began to slow and it appeared to be a plane or chopper for 10 or 12 seconds, but then I could see it begin to breakup and dissappear. golden fire at 1 to 3 points all fallowing in a line, and then the breakup. very strange.
