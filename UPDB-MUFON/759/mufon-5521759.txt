appearing and disappearing orange/red dots in the sky. Made triangles and lines.

Went outside to look at New Years fireworks. They were not by any other fireworks, isolated near the horizon. orange fire in the sky, because there were about 3-4 of them. they would slowly appear and disappear and sort of float in the same area. At one time, they made a line of three, and the two on the sides swung down to make a triangle. Then, they went back to a random formation and zipped up into the sky leaving a trail, almost as if a shooting star. They were there for about 8 minutes until they would start disappearing with none to fill-in. In awe, starting calling friends. They disappeared or faded into the sky.
