My husband and I observed 4 balls of light rotating clockwise..meet in middle back out again repeating.

We took a break from watching a movie on tv. My husband looked out our window and saw some lights which were strange. We went out our front porch and watched these 4 balls of lights spin around clockwise then meet into the middle as one then back out again repeating the cycle.  We watched for 5 minutes then suddenly poof..they were gone. They were over the river and quite large. I had phoned my aunt.She was about 5 minutes away from us. She looked out and could see the circle of lights. I found it weird,errie ,strange but very beautiful at the same time. I initially thought of northern lights but soon realized that it was not. They were stationary rotating in the one spot into the middle and out again.
