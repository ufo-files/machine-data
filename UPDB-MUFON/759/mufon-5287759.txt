My wife and I were camping at Cape Lookout NC. While star gazing, I noticed an orange/red light that was quickly changing directions. I was in the USAF and Army. It was definitely not an aircraft, flares or anything man made. Nor was it a star or planet.  

I took ok a super crappy video with my iPhone but it does show the quick movements of the craft. It was hard to see what I was taking video of through the phone. 

After I took the video, I continued to watch it for a few more minutes. It was still there wen I went to bed. We stayed 3 more nights and I didn’t see anything else.
