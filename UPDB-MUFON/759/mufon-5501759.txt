{rjl} COMMUNICATED WITH CLOUD

posted my sighting on my Facebook page in order to record the event without risk of loss. I have unfortunate luck with tech gear. Good thing I did, as it turns out - both phone and computer died since. On February 13, 2013, I was traveling from Beltsville to Silver Spring to the grocery store. At various stop lights along the way I noticed cloud formations off to the left-front. Weird cloud formations. Since my camera on my phone died, I didn't get photos, but when I got home, I drew them so I wouldn't forget orientation, etc. When I researched angles, I found the right angle with corner in upper left relates to "west" in navigation. Straight vertical line usually means "one". I took the two unequal/uneven diagonal line to then mean "two". My destination, Silver Spring, is exactly 12 minutes West of Beltsville using longitude/latitude. Freaky!" I first noticed the object due to the square shape of the cloud "window" ... clouds don't come in squares. When the 270-degree angle appeared in this cloud "window" in a darker shade of cloud, I was intrigued. Then what I took to be a number 1 appeared within the angle, faded, then the two diagonal lines I took to mean 2 appeared withing the angle, then the whole "window" faded. I was bemused, continued to the grocery store, parked, and drew what I had seen so as to be able to research angles and glyphs to see if my interpretations were actually correct. When I got home, I redrew with my computer what I had seen, with cloud color and shading and lines and directions. These are the attached pictures. I haven't been able to locate any glyphic which match these. Do any of you know of any which match? Not at all frightened, just really intrigued. Have you any other cloud-cloaked vehicle reports?
