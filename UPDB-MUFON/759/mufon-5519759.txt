Red Orb slowly crossed the sky.

I was looking out my living room window where I had my last sighting of a different form of UFO back in July of 2011. When I first spotted it, I thought it couldve been the planet mars - due to its bright red color. Thats when I noticed it was moving very slow from left to right across the sky. And this is over the brightly lit city [item moved/CMS/nd] It was a starlike orb that twinkled and pulsed. It was the slowest thing Ive ever seen, and Ive seen many possible ufos.
