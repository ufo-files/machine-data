Witnessed 3 football sized ojects hover then accellerate a a fantastic rate into space with no sound.

In 1969 while on night manuvers at Fort Gordon Ga. I and three others looked up in the shy to see three football shaped objects hovering in the sky over us. Being dark, it was difficult to determine their altitude. They had lights around their perimeters and were soundless. We were staring and wondering what they were when in formation they began to move away again soundless and as we watched they accellerated within seconds into pin sized spots of light again without any sound. All 4 of us saw this and were obviously amazed by what we saw. It happened for sure just as described.
