Bright light jumping/warping

on January 25, 2022 my wife and I were outside standing on our driveway in Tuscola, TX looking at stars. As we looked at the sky, I saw a orange light that shined and then had flares around it on two separate times in a span of a minute. I could not get my phone fast enough to video what was going on before the light disappeared. We thought maybe it had gone away, but about 3 to 4 minutes later the light appeared again, and this time I was able to catch about 14 seconds of video. The light in the sky would be on and then it would go off jumping or warping in the process. I don't know if this was a flying object or not, but it was very strange especially since there was a report in 2012 about an orange light also in Tuscola.
