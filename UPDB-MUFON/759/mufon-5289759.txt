San clemente Island sighting

This video was recorded from a phone of a fellow sailor on the H60 Romeo's FLIR. The helicopter was grounded off of San Clememte Island, CA on a 2 week training event. They were doing routine maintenance after shutsown for all flights when they saw the lights. There seems to be 2 balls of light in the distance, and when the FLIR locks on it speeds away so fast the FLIR can't keep up with it. Can you explain what it is?
