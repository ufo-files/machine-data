I woke up and was standing in hall window staring outside as it was so bright-like day. Saw about three aliens looking at me.don't remember after that.

This event occurred when I was approximately 12 years old. It happened at home on the farm. The house and barn were close to each other (barn has since burned), within 50 feet. I do not even remember walking to the window in the hall. But I was standing at the window looking out and wondering why it was so bright out there. It was night time. It was so bright it looked like when you turn on the lights at a ball field. On the ground was 3 alien looking figures looking at me and pointing. I lost time after that. I do not know how long I was standing at the window or what happened after. But ever since then I lose track of time so easily. It scared me and my heart was racing. I was also fascinated-seemed to be drawn to watch them. No one else in my family saw this. I have thought of this incident many times since it happened and even now as to why I was picked for this and then to see the UFO so close later (12 years after saw the figures).
