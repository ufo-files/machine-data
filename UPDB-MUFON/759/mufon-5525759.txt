Glowing red and white ball 2 of them

I was driving home from Marysville Indian reservation after lighting off fireworks. Travelling southbound I-5 and getting close to the 164th st Exit i noticed a ball of red and white light hovering on the east side of I-5. As I drove closer i knew it was not a cell tower and there were fireworks going off underneath it. It hovered and didn't move and i took the 164th street exit to get some water at the well. Then I looked up i was not in direct line of sight. Then filled up my water bottle and proceed back on I-5 south and I pulled over to the shoulder and see I that object again and I located it. This time it was moving and heading east in fast rate of speed as it was climbing. The light was getting dimmer as it was travelling away. Then I noticed a second object trailing it and departed in the same manner. I filmed it on my cellphone but it could not pick it up. This was not a plane or a helicopter and I know when see those types of aircraft.
