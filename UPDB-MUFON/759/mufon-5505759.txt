{rjl} Orange object appeared like a plane on fire but no trail. The became dull and faded away.

Just got done barbecuing for New Years Eve (I don't drink). Looked up in sky and saw many stars for a Los Angeles night. Something caught my eye and it was a large orange light, larger and brighter than any star. It was moving very slowly about 35 degrees on the horizon from the east by southeast towards the west by northwest. It looked like an airplane on fire but it trailed no flames and seemed to stay the same orange color. It stayed the same color, speed and direction. After watching it move very slowly for 3 to 4 minutes, it seemed to slow down, bright orange color became dull, appeared to hover for a few seconds and disappeared. There seems to be a very tiny white light left but I think it was a distant star. I looked away and could not refind the tiny white light. My roommate saw the same thing and had no idea what he saw. I have always loved airplanes, know them very well, and have seen many meteorites. I have been in aerospace quality for 44 years and been to many west coast airshows. My first thought was that it was a 737 on fire but no particles, fire trail, no anything trailing. With all the years I have watched the skies, I have never seen anything like this. If it was a meteor, it was headed right at me as it had no tail, no sparks, and appeared to be going very slow.
