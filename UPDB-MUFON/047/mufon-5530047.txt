An orange orb with rainbow patterns on the bottom crossed Las Vegas.

I watched an orange colored orb thorugh a telescope cross Las Vegas at about 3:45 this afternoon. The event lasted about an hour. It was at about the same height that planes fly but it moved slowly then with quick jerking movements. It moved from the middle of las vegas and moved north towards area 51.Through the telscope it seemed to have a rainbow colored bottom to it with a flashing light that went off about every 5 minutes. Once it hit north las vegas it took off with amazing speed and was gone.
