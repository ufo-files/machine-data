{cak} I spotted a large outer rim of a massive disc partially poking out of dark storm clouds.

I was watching a nasty looking storm moving into the Lawrence County area. Was in my father's car on the way to the hospital for an x-ray. I know ufos seem to use naturally occuring storms to possibly power their craft and as cover. So, I always keep my eyes to the sky. That is when I noticed the saturn-like ring shape sticking out of a dark area of the clouds. I thought it was just a formation and would change with the wind but the large shape became more defined. Needless to say I was in awe and dumbfounded by this massive shape. If this was a craft within these clouds it was over 3-5 hundred feet long, since the area I witnessed was only partially observable. I lost sight of it as the dark clouds seemed to cover it. Stranger still, after seeing it, we all noticed a very large white cloud that stood out among the darkness and it was the only one of its kind. It would be easy for me to sketch what I saw.
