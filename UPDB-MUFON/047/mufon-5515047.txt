{cak} Travelling south on 86th & Urbandale, my wife and I looked up and saw a metallic cigar shaped object with sun reflecting off of it

Driving south down 86th St. at 6:00PM going toward Douglas, we looked up and saw a cigar shaped silver object with the sun reflecting on its side.
  We were driving south on 86th and I saw the object, then pointed it out to my wife. I stated "whats that", then I said a "UFO", (the sun was above the horizon and to our right) she looked up at it, and also noticed the sun reflecting off of it.
  It was headed north east, and we lost sight of it due to trees.
 We don't have any feelings, or reactions to seeing this, But my main action is to make a report to MUFON.
  Again, we lost sight of the object due to trees.
