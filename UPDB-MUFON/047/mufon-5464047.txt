i saw a dark shape pass my bedroom and open my fridge

I was sleeping and suddenly awoke for some reason but was paralyzed and watched a dark shadow pass my bedroom and then heard the refrigerator door shut, you know that sound like jars make and the suction sound, but i never heard it open, and it was an impossibly short amount of time to see something that is physically 10-15 feet away from making contact with something then that something being handled in an unconventional way, you know as if the fridge door was already open and that entity was going back to shut it. The whole thing was like crossing wires in my brain. There wasnt anyone out there, and I was the only one in the entire suite. I was awake and screaming but nothing was coming out when i was paralyzed. That isnt something normal for me, I looked up sleep paralysis and it seems to be the same, but has only happened that time, and i think one other that I cant even recall now. I remember asking my girlfriend who didnt live with me at the time if she had come over last night and opened my fridge, i wouldnt be mad if she let herself in and went through my stuff, just tell me the truth, all that sort of stuff.
