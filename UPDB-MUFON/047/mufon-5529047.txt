Saw a bright white star travelling South East. Its motion was not smooth and was also in an arc.

I'd just picked my little boy up from nursery when i saw a bright white star travelling South East. Its motion was not smooth, it seemed to slow down and speed up as it travelled. It also seemed to be travelling i nan arc, bending more East as it went. I watched it and remember thinking that it was below the scattered mid level cloud although in the end it seemed to dissapear behind the clur - or just fade... It was dark with only a slight blue tinge left in the west. I tried to finm it on my mobile phone but couldn't see it through the view finder so not sure if it came out yet.
