5 of us were out riding in car. summer, radio on, drinking. we went around a curve and saw this hugh light. i don't know why, it was like a light i'd never seen before. as i looked at it a voice in my head was saying "this isn't real, u don't really see it. i didn't say a word. nor did anyone else. everyone was quite. after maybe 1/2 to 1 mile on down the road one of the girls in back says "what was that". that's when everybody all at once proclaimed, u saw it to. we turned around and went back. it was total dark. 2 of us have died, 1 moved and hasn't been seen, one lives close. all i know is whenever we ran into each other the question always came up. what did we see that night. i didn't know then and i don't know now. it haunts me,i know i saw it but i cain't describe it. it was like the light was coming down but everything in it was somehow moving up. i would like to be hypnotised to find out what we saw. this happen on a saturday night. the next week our small town paper ran a paragraph saying a ufo was reported on sunday 10 miles from where we were.
