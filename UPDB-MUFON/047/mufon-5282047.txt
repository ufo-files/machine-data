Woke up at 5:50 am because my neighbors Cows came into our property and we’re going absolutely crazy. When I went outside to investigate, I gazed across the river, due north- saw what can only be described as a “moon dog” phenomenon, except it changed altitude VERY quickly. Seemed to have an intelligence, because the second I saw it, I began yelling for my wife to come outside and look. When it “heard me” it immediately descended below the mountain on the opposite side of the river. It appeared to be close, in this atmosphere.
