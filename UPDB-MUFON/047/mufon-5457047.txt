{cak} Unusual light patterns in night time sky over rural small Indiana town

At 10:40 I went outside on our front porch and observed an particularly twinkly star-like object in the sky. I observed that the object seemed to be exhibiting vivid colors. I went to get my husband. He observed the same and went back in to get binoculars. We both observed the object with binoculars and it seemed to have a blue, red, and white lights that would pulse. The object was stationary in the western sky. At 11:10 PM my husband noted two more objects near the first. These were at the 11 o'clock and 9 o'clock position to the first object. They did not have the same color but rather appeared to be white lights. They were not as bright.  During the event, an airplane flew by. The object seems to be below the normal flight path. There is an airport to the west of the town of Ferdinand. It is a rural airport.  We considered it might be a drone. But drone activity at this time of night over a rural town would be unusual. This is also a long time for a drone to be in flight.
