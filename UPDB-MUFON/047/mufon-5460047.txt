{rjl} Green Luminous Sphere Seen Following Darlington Polo Game, 8-25-2017, at approximately 9:15pm, Darlington, PA.

During this luminous green sphere sighting there were perhaps 50 spectators or more positioned along the southern side of the polo field. The game had just ended and so all of the team players were still gathered together by the east-end goal-post. I would suspect that most of the spectators were then also looking toward the team players, as I was, when the luminous green sphere suddenly appeared from over the southern ridge and to my right. This UFO's illumination was greater then that of a green traffic light. The object's size was much larger than any aircraft's navigation lights against the night sky though much smaller than the moon. I watched it travel northward and over the team players on their polo ponies. 

Just moments later, a man within the crowd shouted, "Hey, did you see that?" And yet no one from the nearby crowd seemed to respond. Their lack of response baffled me. How could they have not seen this object. And so, I yelled back to him, "You mean that green thing?" He replied, "Yeah, must have been a meteor." And yet it didn't appear to be a meteor. 

The object's flight path was straight and horizontal with no noticeable descend. It moved too slowly to be a meteor. It was too consistent in its circular shape and illumination and wasn't breaking-up. It had no tail nor flickering halo. It made no sound and had no navigation lights. Its flight path was somewhat low in the sky. I first sighted it over my right shoulder, having then observed it for about 4 seconds before it passed over the next northern ridge. The object's size was about 1/4 inch to 5/8 inch in diameter at arms length.
