It was 1977. We were 16 years old & I remember it as if it were yesterday. I was driving my car with a friend to meet another friend at Walnut Lake neighborhood beach access. We pulled into the parking space looking right onto the lake. Perhaps just 20 feet from the waters edge. My friend & I were there for about 5 minutes. Suddenly, we looked up about 20 to 50 yards ahead of us and saw a glowing, pulsating light hovering over the lake. We looked at each other in disbelief & said "do you see what I see". Yes. We both saw it. We could hear it too. It was not a helicopter sound. It was a humming, pulsating sound. At first we thought it was a helicopter,but it was not. It was a pod like craft with a low glowing pulsating light. The shape revealed a small rectangle pod like craft. Then we saw that water was being transported into the belly of the craft. The water glowed like a waterfall. No doubt it was going into the craft. We started to freak out. We shrunk down in our seats and were very frightened. We were afraid to talk. We didn't want them to see us. Then, suddenly after a few minutes, it shot straight up to a Mother ship waiting 100s of feet high, a glowing disk pulsating. It then zig zagged in a star like formation leaving tracer lights then zipped up out of sight. We took off immediatly driving straight to our friends house rathet than waiting for them. We told them what happened. We still talk about it today & our story has not changed. So glad to finally be able to disclose this as it is an unusual sighting.
