Traveling east on Highway 50, bright blue light looked like it was a falling star.

My cousin and I were traveling east on Highway 50, it was late at night and dark. We were going to Rocky Ford, Colorado to visit a girl that my cousin knew. We were drinking some beer, I remember that we were not drunk but we had about a six pack that we consumed. Suddenly a bright blue light was falling from the sky, and then ducked behind a cloud, and illuminated the cloud in a bright blue color. It was beautiful blue color. Then just as sudden as it fell from the sky it went dark, and we didn't see it anymore.
