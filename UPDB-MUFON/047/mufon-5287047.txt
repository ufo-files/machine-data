Mostly cloaked craft passed overhead

I was outside around 3:15am observing various stars, planets, and the moon. While looking westward, I noticed a crescent or possibly chevron shaped very light mist or transparent white object moving rapidly over my head heading east. I notice it distorting the sky as it passed very rapidly with no sound. I would estimate an altitude of around 300-400 ft. with a width of approximately 200 ft. (My best guess).

Mentioned it to a friend who saw a bright light going across their back yard around the same time. The event was captured by their security cameras.
