{rjl} Fast moving very bright lights that stopped and descended below tree ilne.

I was outside on my front porch talking on my cell phone. I looked to the west and saw bright lights, and at first, I thought I saw fireworks because the lights were so bright. I realized it was raining, so it couldn't be fireworks. The lights were below the clouds. I realized the lights ( which appeared to be red, white, and green side by side) were moving toward me to the east very quickly. I yelled for my husband to come outside. He witnessed this also. The moving lights came to a dead stop. It hovered for a few moments, flared bright white/blue colors and started descending. It descended below the tree line rather quickly. There was no sound at all. When I called for my husband, I was very excited and felt when the object stopped, it was almost like it saw us. We tried to get a picture with an I-Pad, but was unsuccessful.
