15-20 lights travelling in groups of 3 like backwards triangle.

Standing outside with husband having a smoke looking at the stars and noticed a light travelling from West to East. It was very far away, tiny, at first I thought it was a satellite, then I noticed another following behind it and then another above the second light travelling in like a large triangle shape. Then we just kept noticing more and more coming, each travelling with 2 at the front and one at the back (backward triangle). One of the lights suddenly changed direction and went North, coming over our house. There were at least another 5 or 6 groups of 3. I found it more strange that they were first in a straight line and looked like they were coming from nowhere beside one of the stars just to the right of Sirius and they were catching up with each other to form the triangles. One in particular appeared to keep speeding up as if it was going to pull out of formation, but kept falling back in. We stood for about 10 minutes but then there didn't seem to be anymore so we came inside.
