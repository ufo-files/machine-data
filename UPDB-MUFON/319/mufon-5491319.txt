3 Bright lights appeared in a triangle shape as the I.S.S was passing over then faded out.

It was 12.15 AM 12th August 2014, I was taking a long exposure photograph of the International Space Station as it was passing over, My location was Gort, Co Galway, Ireland.
As my exposure was processing on my lumix camera i kept an eye on the I.S.S as it continued to move across the sky, as it passed west of my location i noticed 3 very bright lights appear in the shape of a triangle and remained stationary just where the I.S.S had passed, the area that this shape covered seemed quite large in the sky, they remained there at a constant brightness for about 2.5 mins then faded out to nothing. As i witnessed these lights i was starting to doubt my knowledge of the stars and their locations but i quickly realised that this was not any constellation or shape of stars that i know of as i have been a keen amateur astronomer for many years now and quickly dismissed this shape and brightness as usual in the night sky, if i had of waited 1 min later before taking the shot at a direction more to the west, i would of captured these lights in the long exposure. Hence no image. Note to self and others, get a second camera. My Brother immediately phoned me as he was also watching the I.S.S. pass over with my Dad, they both saw the same thing and were located about 150 Miles away to the North.
