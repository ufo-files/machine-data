(gs) Witnessed a glowing orange sphere-like craft above lightening/thunderstorm.

It was May 4th and I was on a commercial airline heading to OHare to make a connecting flight to Kentucky. For reference, I was sitting at the window on the left side (facing the cockpit) of the plane. While in flight, I was admiring the storm off in the distance when I noticed a peculiar orange light/object moving slowly over the storm. i would estimate that it was at 10:00 and down from where I was at about a 30-35 degree angle. At first I just wrote it off as a plane, even though I could not see the form of it. I reasoned that the distance must have made it look more like a sphere. However, as I observed the object more I became aware that there were no blinking lights. I was further intrigued when the glowing orange object/sphere was at about 7:00-8:00 as it was still bright orange; if what I was seeing were the front lights of an aircraft, then they would be pointed away from the plane I was on and therefore should have changed in luminosity. I also found it strange that this object was very close, almost tracking the lightening/thunderstorm as opposed to avoiding it. It seemed to move at times while at others it seemed to hover. I thought that these movements may just be a matter of misinterpretation of moving around in the plane, but the plane was flying a relatively steady course to OHare. There may have been other witnesses, but I didn't really care to discuss it with anyone on the plane since I was tired and the object could have been anything.

I want to put a disclaimer that Ive grown up having an interest in fringe-science and paranormal experiences, so I have a clear bias of wanting to believe. However, because I rely in the scientific method to establish credibility for such findings, I don't jump to conclusions.
