We were sitting on my patio, my grandson, my friend and i when my grandson asked me what the light in the sky was. I looked up along with my friend and saw a yellowish white light with a small blinking red light on one end. It went over the house and flew for a short distance and then made a very abrupt drop in altitude and then disappeared. The whole time we heard no noise coming from it.
