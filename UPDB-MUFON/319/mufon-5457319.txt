{tjd} 4-5 round independent lights that moved independently of each other.

Earlier that morning my wife called to tell me that her and our kids had witnessed a bright light in the sky. When she went to take a picture of it, it vanished. It freaked them out so she called to tell me about it. Later that night around 830 I went to pick my youngest son up from the local high school football game. We where discussing what they say that morning. I asked him to point out where they say it. So we started scanning the skies and immediately saw 4-5 lights in the sky in the area where they saw the light that morning. I stopped in the road and snapped a couple pictures. They were very visible with my eyes bit not so much in the pictures. They were in a line at first and then started to move independently. I thought they where helicopters at first until they started moving up and down quickly and turning very fast, curving and banking sharply. We watched them for around 5-7 mins before they disappeared. It's not our first experience but my first one I've reported.
