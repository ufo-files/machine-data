{nd} Bright light in the sky that caught our eye

I was inside working on the computer when my mother came into the room and said I had to come outside to see this strange light. I noticed it was the only bright object in the sky. The skies were clear at the time. The time was about 6:55 pm mnt 
I grabbed my phone and turned on Google sky map. there was nothing there but a constellation. At first I thought it might be a plane. My naked eye saw one big white bright light. But it never moved. It seemed as if it was hovering or staying in place. So I thought maybe it might have been moving away from me to make it appear it was staying still. I grabbed my camera and started to take still pictures. Then I made a video and zoomed in. The weird thing was to my naked eye it looked like a white light. Im guessing it was 5-10 miles way from me. but Im really not sure. when looking through the video, there were all kinds of colors coming from it. but I didn't see the colors with just my eyes. On the video it was changing colors. Blue, red, orange, purple, white.

I remembered where I was standing and waited to see if it moved. For maybe 5 mins it was in place then it either moved further away or was descending. eventually it went below my viewing eyesight. 

When I first saw the object. it looked like one white light. 15 mins before I lost sight. there were at least 3 objects. the middle one was on top. I closed one eye ad looked. then another eye and looked. The other witness said they also saw 3 lights and not just one.
