Orange colored constellation zooms across sky silently at ultra, ultra fast speed

Looked up into the sky above Ventura on approx. March 9th at 8pm, and saw 5-6 orange constellation shaped craft flying silently and faster than any plane I've ever seen. It flew from the hills to over the ocean in 5 seconds flat and was very high in the sky. The formation was asymmetrical, and if it was outlined it would look like a triangle flag on a pole, or roughly like the constellation of Cancer. I watch planes over my house all the time and this went too fast to be one. It was absolutely silent and flew towards the Channel Islands.
