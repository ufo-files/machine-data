{rjl} Bell shape /w cylinder connected below-lights in triangle-green at top-white at mid right and red at bottom

Stood in alley by my home observed object at NW in sky about 1-2 miles west of my home. Hovered for over an hour and seemed to pulsate over ocean most probably El Segundo. Husband noted object upon leaving for work at 8:30 pm and asked me to watch object. I saw it at 8:30 pm and again at 9:00 pm still in same position; did not move, not even slightly. Helicopter in area, probably police (common at night). Object some distance from helicopter. By 9:30 pm, the object was gone though I did not observe it leave the area. The object was bell shaped with an attached cylinder projection below fullest portion of object. Lights were as follows: green at left top and then white at cylinder portion left side and red light at bottom of cylinder. The object did not move but pulsated and seemed to change shape slightly while pulsating. I was interested in object and intrigued but not frightened. I went back inside after intial sighting at 8:30 pm. Checked again at 9:00 pm; object still in same position and did not move. I re-checked at 9:30 pm and object was gone.
