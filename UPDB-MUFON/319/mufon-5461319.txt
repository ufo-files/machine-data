{cak} looked up and saw a raindrop shaped craft with pyramid shapes all over it. The craft seemed to be made of crystal and had a roiling fire inside.

Craft was approximately 200 feet above me coming from the southwest,it was leaving a trail of oily black smoke. The craft was shaped like a teardrop on its side going point first, it had crystal looking 4 sided pyramids mounted all over it.The length of the pyramids seemed to be equal to the diameter of the fuselage where they were attached. The craft looked like it was made of some clear crystal material and the inside was filled with a roiling firestorm. The object flew towards the northeast towards a break in the mountains and out of sight.
