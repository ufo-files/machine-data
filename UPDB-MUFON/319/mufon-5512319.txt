{cak} 3 lights moving in the sky

I saw 2 lights in a straight line in the sky thought they were stars and continued to watch (as i was the passenger of the car) we turned north on Kipling and I continued to watch the 2 lights ascend and descend in parallel motions then a third light appear to the south of the other 2 and it stayed quite dim the first 2 lights moved in a clock wise and counter clock wise motions usually opposite (one going up the other going down) objects continued to dance in the sky until one enlarged (got really bright) then seemed to just disappear the other 2 lights (one original and the other dim one) seemed like they were teleporting back and forth( from my perspective left to right)for about 10 mins until they finally enlarged (got brighter) then disappeared. Happened for about 15 mins. The whole time I was watching planes fly by them and was attempting to judge their distance,unfortunately I couldnt judge their exact distance.
