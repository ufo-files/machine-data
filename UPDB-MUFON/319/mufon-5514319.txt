(GS) WENT OUTSIDE TO SMOKE.SAW LIGHTS IN A PERFECT V-SHAPE MOVING STRAIGHT AHEAD.NO SOUND.DISAPPEARED OVER HOUSES

BETWEEN 7 & 9 P.M.,NOV.13TH 2012, I WENT OUTSIDE TO SMOKE.IN THE SKY ABOVE I SAW A ROW OF MANY LIGHTS IN A PERFECT V-SHAPE MOVING ALONG IN THE SKY. THE LIGHTS WHERE LIKE A DULL YELLOWISH WHITE, ALMOST TRANSPARENT-LIKE. THE STARS WERE BRIGHTER THAN THE LIGHTS. @ 1ST I TRIED TO ADJUST MY EYES. I THOUGHT MAYBE BIRDS @ 1ST, BUT THEY WERE WHITE & IT WAS TOO DARK FOR THAT TO BE. THEN I THOUGHT MAYBE A PLANE, BECAUSE THERE IS AN AIRPORT CLOSE BY & PLANES GO BY ALL DAY, BUT IT WAS WAY 2 MASSIVE TO BE A PLANE. I WATCHED AS IT KEPT MOVING, IN A STRAIGHT SLOW PACE, FURTHER & FURTHER AWAY. I AGAIN TRIED TO ADJUST MY EYES & THOUGHT I LOST IT WHEN I SEEN IT AGAIN,STILL MOVING FORWARD & AWAY FROM ME. IT WAS AS IF IT WAS TRANSPARENT @ TIMES. AS IT GOT FURTHER AWAY & STARTED TO DISAPPEAR OVER THE HOUSES I JUMPED ON A FENCE TO TRY TO CONTINUE TO WATCH IT & IT WAS GONE. MY FRIENDS THEN CAME HOME & SEEN ME ON THE FENCE,WONDERING WHAT I WAS DOING. I WAS IN SHOCK OF WHAT I HAD JUST SEEN & COULDNT EXPLAIN TO THEM. IT FREAKED ME OUT & I WAS TRYING TO PROCESS & MAKE SENSE OF IT ALL. I IMMEDIATELY GOT ONLINE TO SEE IF ANYONE ELSE HAD REPORTED ANYTHING. TODAY I WAS DIRECTED TO THIS NETWORK . THIS IS MY REPORT
