{nd} walking on golf course pre dawn, I saw an object race across the sky then hover then disappear

Walking on golf course in my community I reside in and I came around a corner into a utility easement that runs east and west towards Shearon Harris Nuclear power plant. I believe that is about 5-7 miles away but you can see the top of the cooling tower in the distance. As I came around the corner, I looked up and maybe a mile?? in the sky there was a hovering light. I had headphones on so I couldn't hear anything. I stared at it thinking it was a helicopter. I finally took my phones off and realized it was making no sound - very strange. Thinking this was an "Osprey" or helicopter still, I then watched it completely disappear. I became very cold like some sort of entity had been in that area1.the air became much cooler like a draft blew in and I got chills 1maybe from fear but it was in the low 70's this AM.
