{nd} My fianc1 and I notices a large orange silent orb, it changed direction and dimmed. Still visible after dimming

My fianc1 and I were returning from store when we pulled up to my house I noticed a very bright orange orb. With the 4th of July just passing I thought it was fireworks at first. When the orb was still hovering and actually moving towards our direction I knew it was not fireworks. It got almost right overhead of us making no noise at all, then abrubtly changed directions and started to dim. After it dimmed you could still see the orb as if it was giving a faint red glow then it disappeared as it got farther away. I shot 24 seconds of video with my iphone, it's not the best video but you can clearly see the orb hovering away.
