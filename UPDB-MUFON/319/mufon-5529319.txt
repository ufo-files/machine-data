something orbiting the moon

This is something that always haunted me for an answer.
I was only about eight years old. I don't remember the exact date. I know it would have had to been before 78 because I moved from Lansdale to Harleysville Pa. Anyhow, this is what happened. 
I was with some friends hanging out at the Oak Park school in Lansdale Pa. I grew up on Pine street which intersects the school property. I was on the school Property with some friends on what I believe was later in the day. It was summer and it was one of those days where you could see a large full moon. The moon was lined up towards my house on Pine street so we were looking towards my house from the school. We saw a very large object orbit the moon starting from the top and working its way to the bottom. As it got to the backside it disapered. I could have not been the only one on this day to see this. I think that the object was too big to be man made. It looked like a meteor but I don't think the moon has much gravity to make a big object orbit. Any ideas? Please drop me an email.
