{tjd} Strange Cloud Formation over Laguna Seca during race

I was shooting photos behind the fences on Turn 5 at WeatherTech Raceway Laguna Seca for the final day of races for the Porsche Rennsport Reunion VI on Sept. 30th 2018, when I looked up and noticed a strange gap in the clouds just above the racetrack while I was facing down the straightaway from Turn 4 to Turn 5 (North). The gaps in the clouds seemed to stay in place for a few minutes during a race, so I snapped a couple of photos with both a Canon Rebel T5i with a Canon EF 75-300mm f/4-5.6 III Telephoto Zoom Lens and my cell phone, an LG V30. 

There were four "holes" visible in the clouds in a perfect straight line going from a Northwest to Southeast direction maybe 85-90 degrees directly overhead, and each hole was equidistant from the next in the line. In addition to the "holes", there was also a smaller gap that looked like a line connecting all the holes. The effect looked like a string of pearls being stretched out. 

The gaps soon disappeared when the clouds moved in a Northeasterly direction. I kept tracking it as the race when on for maybe five more minutes before the cloud formation dissolved and the gaps were no longer visible. After the race I looked up again and saw nothing, so I moved from that location to find another shooting angle for the next race. I didn't see anything else during the rest of the day pertaining any other cloud formations.
