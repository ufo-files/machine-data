I was riding my bike through Hounslow heath in the UK when i observed a round/circular object slowly descend and hover just above ground level. I watched as the yellow / orange object slowly descended and hover nervously over the ground. It paused and gittered for a second and moved towards me, silently flying in an arc like trajectory. The object is now 10 feet away from me. and slightly above my eye level. I had my phone with me and took the pictures which are attached. I looked up at this now hovering small round ball that was gittering slightly. I stood there for about 2 seconds staring at the ball and it was staring back at me. At this point, i smiled, and the object slowly moved upwards and appeared to disenintegrate, like small particles into nothing. I have sent these same two pictures to an english Ufo society, who have anaylsed them and believe the object to be a lens flare , despite the fact that i witnessed this object away from my phone, i.e, i saw the object move with my own eyes
