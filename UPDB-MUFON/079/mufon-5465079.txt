Circular Metalliic Object

I had just bought a Panasonic HD V180 with Full Spectrum Conversion in order to do exactly this sort of thing (Sky/UFO Watching) and today was only my third day!! 

I was outside my front door and was looking South along the edges of the clouds with the camcorder and came across this object which was obviously not natural. I was already recording. I thought at first it may have been a kids helium ballon or similar but the spherical object remained tight on it's X/Y Axis in comparison to the clouds round about it.

It appeared to my eye to be rotating and /or changing shape. On closer inspection there appeared to be small lights present. (These can be seen more clearly on still and paused film. There appeared to be a metallic colouring to the object. 

I am Ex-Forces (Army) We get trained to observe and report in detail.
I know it wasn't a plane or Venus etc etc or any of the usual excuses we get fed. I know what it was and I know what it wasn't.

I was feeling rather chuffed at finding it only 3 days into my searching the skies! No feelings of dread or scared. Just inquisitive and pragmatic.

I tried to fasten the camera onto the tripod and ended up losing the object. My camera work is rather shaky and all over the place as I am still learning how to work it with the many options of specifc manual/auto or intelligent focus etc! That was when I lost sight of the object. I believe I was using the "1KIR Infra-Red Filter" on that particluar occasion. However I imagine that this object may well have been visible without this filter looikng at the footage.
