{cak} Not a clear image, did not look like a satellite.

I was out in the mid summer, looking at the stars, and I saw something that all the sudden, I thought maybe, it was a satellite. But now, I realize, if a satellite was to be going that fast, it would probably burn up. It was clearly up around 36,000 feet, and I saw lights... It wouldnt have been an aircraft, because of all the colors, and the sort of aurora that it seemed to trail. I would watch it, and then think, I had no idea satellites could go that fast. The object started to move to the right, and then it moved back into its pattern. Its colors were listed above, and I don't even know at this point if it should be counted as a sighting. I was 8 then, and I don't think I knew.
