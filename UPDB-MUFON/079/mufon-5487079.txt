{cak} witnessed two ufo's last night

Coming home from work last night, I witnessed some really strange activity in the sky.Traveling from Westminster Maryland to Sykesville/Eldersburg on Route 32. I saw a lot of lights in the sky, it was cloudy but none of what I saw was up that far. As I started on 32, due to what I noticed I watched as driving (30-40 mph) lights and two huge , thought they were planes, one on either side of 32 traveling with traffic. The one on my left sorta traveled along side of me and was not traveling very fast.Strange to see a plane going that slow. As I got closer to Mechanicsville (between Westminster & Sykesville) I noticed that the two would hover and go up a bit and switched sides of the road. As I traveled it almost seemed as though the one on my left was the same rate of speed as I, 30 mph. I was behind a line of cars & I know they saw the same activity as I. We were all looking. The one on the left seemed to stop in mid air, go backwards about 10 feet then move far left and came back again to follow traffic then sped up a bit towards Baltimore. There was no sound from them what so ever. Now, I also noticed in the far distance there were two helicopters and possibly a third but I was more interested in the one closest to me. I'm no aviation expert but I have never seen any type of aircraft do what these two did. They were at least as big as a football field. Boomerang in shape on the outside and triangular in the middle. By the time I got home. I just saw helicopters patrolling the areas I described above. Its better to explain in person but if you have questions feel free to call. but this is not my only experience seeing ufos.
