I was in Berlin New Hampshire walking out of the building I work in looking up at the moon and this big gold color 4 pointed star just below left of the moon about the position of Venus. As I leave the area the headed north to the town of Milan I noticed a gold light moving at a high rate of speed headed in the same direction that I seen the other lights.
