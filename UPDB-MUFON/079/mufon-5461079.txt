I was at work that night. At this time I delivered pizzas. As a result of course, I spent a lot of time driving. On this particular night I noticed this object hovering in the sky a mile or two down the highway from the pizza place I worked at. I at first thought it could have been a helicopter or jet, but as I got closer I saw it was far larger than a helicopter and was hovering almost stationary. As I watched it as I drove, it turned in one direction while it was hovering and began to fly away. I then kept seeing it around town for the next 30 mins to an hour, ending up all the way across town in a rather small amount of time. I saw it when I was driving right next to my house and tried to get a good quality video but I was driving so that was a bit hard to do, but I at least got something. This video was the last time I saw it that night. It sped up towards the northeast and was gone. Shortly after that, I see like military helicopters flying around the area. I continue to see this object every now and then. I saw it a couple months ago over the area where I live. I have no idea what it is.
