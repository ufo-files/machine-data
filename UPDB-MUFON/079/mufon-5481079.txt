{nd} saw 3 lights in sky in a formation that changed driving south on rt. 222

At 8:53 pm on September 5th, 2016 my wife and I observed 3 lights in the sky to the east of us, driving southbound on Rt.222, near the Ephrata exit. We quickly passed them by but as we watched them they seemed to rearrange their formation but consistently kept a triangular formation between them. I've seen things before in the sky that I thought might be unidentified but these lights were impactful enough to make me report.
