{cak} Out with a small powered telescope we were chasing moving lights in the sky. When Eyes got tired backed away and looked strait up and there it was

One late fall night 1965 or 66 I was out with my low powered on my back lawn.After chasing a few needing eye relief I leaned back and right over my head was a triangle or boomerang strait over head going slow. The back of the craft looked molten and was giving off sparks and the thing that brought it back to mind was seeing the English Air Bases that were American where 100 servicemen witness it. The drawings could have been twins Was going west all the while and lost sight after 4 minutes. Astonishment and awe This was the time that a wave of unexplained lights and sightings took place.
