[MISSING DATA] Report was submitted by cell phone per witness.  I contacted the witness by phone and he provided a verbal description of the event.  It follows.  

The witness was approximately 7 years old at the time of the sighiting. He was on the porch of his house shooting a BB gun. The house sits above and not far from the road in front of the house. The witness claims he turned and saw a craft that looked like a giant propane tank a few feet above the ground slowly following the path of the road. By slowly he said about 4-5 MPH. The tank was metallic, chalky grey in color, about 4 to 5 feet wide and 25 feet long. He remembers a small blinking red light on it that was beeping. The craft emitted a hummng sound.  The witness claims he was no farther than 30 feet from the craft and he could make out the grain in its metal. He watched it go around a bend in the road until it was out of sight. He said the craft was so low to the ground that a vehicle could have run into it. He ran into the house to tell his parents but no one believed him. About a year later, his mother showed him an aritlcle in the newspaper about a woman who had seen the same thing he told his parents. He does not have the article but would be wiling to take a polygraph test to help confirm his sighting experience. He said he has remembered this his entire life and wanted to speak to someone about it but did not due to potential ridicule
