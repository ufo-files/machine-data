My home in Detroit was haunted. We had cubby holes that locked in each upstairs closets. Tall, scary, red eyed, creature came out of my brothers closet. During day and night. It had high pitched squel. If I ignored it it would chase me downstairs but not go past stairs to front room. It would try to communicate. The smaller light colored beings came out of my closet and they were no so scary. Door handle high. I would watch the door open and hear them inside. My brother knew of this but don't want to remember nut does say it was haunted. My mom remembers someone telling her that something was on the stairs
