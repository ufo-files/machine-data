{rjl} My eldest son was pulling up in front of my home when I noticed something in the sky.

One sunday morning, I woke up and decided to go to church. My sons (ages 15, and 17) attended the service and returned home. My eldest son was driving, and the youngest was in the back seat behind the driver. I looked out above my neighbors house (who lives on the opposite side of the street) and saw a craft. It was just hovering there. The best descrition I can give is, it was shaped similarly to a bicycle seat. It was metallic in color. I asked out loud "What IS that?" And pointed towards the craft. My oldest son was putting the car in park as we had just pulled in front of the house. My youngest looked in the direction and said "OH MY GOD!" Within a second or two (before my oldest son could look in the general direction), my youngest son and I watched it just vanish. It wasnt as if I had blinked and then it was gone. It literally disintegrated right before our eyes. When my oldest looked in the direction the craft was in, he said I dont see anything. I looked at my youngest, and asked him "did you just see that?" He said "yes....it.just disappeared!!"
