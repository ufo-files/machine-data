Large black sphere 0

Exceedingly large, silent, stationary black sphere hovering in distance over tree tops, darker than the night sky. I saw this while riding as a passenger one evening as a kid of about 10 years old. It was just outside city limits so pretty dark with only one nearby street lamp. I have never been able to sensibly fathom what I saw.
