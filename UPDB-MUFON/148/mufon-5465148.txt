Bright light in the sky

I was driving home in the early evening when I noticed a bright light above the clouds. Occasionally I see a bright moon, this object was smaller than the moon. Other times I see Venus, which is a bright dot. The light I saw was about half the size of the Moon, but a lot brighter. It was the brightness that caught my attention. 

I was trying to work out what it was, when it appeared below the clouds. it was even brighter. As it was dark and the light was very bright. it was as bright as a spot light but there was no beam. 

It was also then that I noticed that there was no nav. lights and there were no anti col lights. 

I am an ex-RAF aircraft engineer. I know what aircraft are supposed to look like. Passing though the clouds there were no other lights bouncing of the clouds. The clouds were probable less than 100 feet thick. Thick enough to be observable but not thick enough to obscure.

The light was moving North. Unfortunately I was driving on the motorway and was only able to observe it half a dozen times. 

If it was an aircraft it would have been a bright light to descend through the clouds with. Blackpool Airport is less than 10 miles east.

It was the brightness of the light and the lack of other lights than made me take note of this light.
