{rjl} Two Spheres in formation sliding very slowly eastward for 1-2 mins and faded

I took my 2 dogs out after 7pm. Very cold, clear, dark skies, new moon. Stars were very bright. I could only see 1/3 of skies between the tall trees outside. I glanced at all-familiar Orion and Canis Major region and noticed two yellow bright spheres brighter than Sirius B, in the M93 Puppis region. The 2 spheres glided slowly eastward, in formation. I have seen this formation and behavior dozen times while in Arizona in previously, half moved North (toward Sedona), other half moved East. 
As in AZ, these spheres seem to communicate. I was in trance while holding my dog on the leash. After a minute, these spheres started to fade. Suddenly, my dog went into unusually extreme aggression and charged to attack something in the bushes, I barely managed to drag my large dog inside. 5 min later my neighbors said there was a coyote there. My 2nd dog was running loose, so perhaps he was protecting. 
I have listened the book Ask and It is Given by Ester and Jerry Hicks before and was in a meditative mode. I was not amazed at the objects, I've seen them before, but it's been 6 months since the last sighting. I was trying to communicate and ask for identification. The feeling was familiar, as in 'family'. Objects disappeared. Previously, many times I'd ask the objects to 'twinkle again' and most of the time they did. This time, I didn't have a chance to ask - had to drag my dog inside. I was happy to see them again.
