Two white spheres hovering. Very close to each other but clearly two separate structures.

In a flight from Denver to Reno. As we were making our descent into Reno (about 20k Ft in elevation) I saw out my window two white spheres that looked to be just hovering there in the air maybe 50 to 100ft from our airplane. At first I thought they were two birds and then when I took a closer look it was actually two solid looking objects fully white in color. No lights were blinking and I couldn't see that anything was even on these spheres, just plain white spheres very close to each other as we passed by. I don't have any guesses as to what these are but we were probably 20 miles southeast of Reno when I saw them and it was only about 30 seconds of view before I couldn't see them out the window anymore because of the plane's movement.
