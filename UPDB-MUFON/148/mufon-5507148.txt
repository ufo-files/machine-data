bright light came down from sky, then turned at right angle behind island 1 mile from shore

I was sitting in my hot tub with a friend at 3:00pm daytime, when I saw a bright light come straight down, then take a sharp right angle & disappear behind an island less than one mile from shore. Lights don't normally travel like that. There was no sound. It traveled fast. I researched the internet to see where I could report it, but found nothing. I knew it was the strangest light I've ever seen in my life. I rubbed my eyes to make sure I wasn't seeing things, but I really saw it.
