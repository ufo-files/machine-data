{dr}Observed slow-moving white light moving SE toward me in Newberg.

Chehalem Mountain lies North of Newberg; Parrett Mountain lies East of Newberg. I was in my apartment in NE Newberg, talking to my 16-yr old daughter who was housesitting on Chehalem Mountain. As we were talking, I watched a slow-moving white light advancing south toward me. I had begun to wonder when I would see the flashing red and green wingtip lights as it seemed to be getting close enough when the light suddenly changed course by 90 degrees and zipped extremely fast toward Parrett Mountain. When my daughter suddenly took a sudden intake of breath, I asked her if she had seen the same light and she responded that she had. I told her that she had probably just seen her first UFO.
