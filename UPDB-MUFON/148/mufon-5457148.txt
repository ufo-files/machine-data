{cak} It was needle shaped and had three lights.

Kaelis and Talyn were outside in dark, gathering a hen out of the bush to put her in the coop. It flew at a steady and continuous speed across the sky. It was long like a needle and had no wings or protrusions on it. It had three lights across it, one near the front with a second one closer to the middle and the third was close to the middle while still being near the back of it.
It flew across the sky towards the horizon and vanished behind the tree line. We made a facebook post immediately and a family of three across town said they saw the same thing at the same moment.
