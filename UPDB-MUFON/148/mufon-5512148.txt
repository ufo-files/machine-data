(GS) something was in my room

I was in the top bunk ,(bunk beds) my brother had his own room , i could not sleep that night . i was on my back looking up , all of a sudden out of the corner of my eye to my left, there was something standing there , but the first thing i thought was somehow i new he was short, i could not understand how he was my level,this really botherd me. i was still looking up at ceiling on my back , As i started to turn my head to the left , at the same time i felt 3 fingers across my left arm, between my elbow and hand .my hands were crossed over my belly . when i fully turned it vanished. A couple days later i found a scoop mark on my ankle (inside). Now i still have this mark and it looks allmost exactely like it did then .(40 years later) I never told my parents or anyone , who would believe me . Then in the 80`s when i saw pic`s of the greys . i was floored ,that, was in my room. When i use to think of this event, i called it pumpkin head . because it had a big head i would really like to have someone come over to show exactly what happend , and show this mark. i can send a pic threw my phone .
