{jpg} At first I thought it was a meteor

Saw a ball of light arching across the canyon, I was driving through. At first I thought it was a meteor crashing but about 10 min later while driving on a very dark, very rural part of the road my car started acting strange! Radio & lights went out for some time, at least several minutes. I just sat there with my 2 small kids, afraid. Thought I'd see something about a "meteor" in the newspaper but nothing. Side note- I was was 50-70 miles from Malstom Air Force base! More stories about that another time! Didn't think too much about then but all these years later, I feel strangely compelled to report it.
