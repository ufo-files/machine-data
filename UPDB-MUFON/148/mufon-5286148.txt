Circling

At approximately 22:30 on August 10th in Squamish, BC, Canada, I observed three star-like lights in the sky that were rotating in a circle for less than 20 seconds. Then the three "stars" stopped rotating and one of the "stars" travelled in the opposite direction (from South to North) at a perceived accelerated speed (faster than a satellite or commercial plane) and altitude for approximately 30 seconds until it disappeared from sight. There were no visible trails following the "star" as it crossed sky, and the light emitting from the "star" remained static the whole time. No other observations after the travelling "star" disappeared.
