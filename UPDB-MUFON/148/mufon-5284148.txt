blimp sized sun on the nose

I saw a soft orange light out over the inlet where I live. It was small and I thought it was an aircraft. It seemed to be coming my way and getting larger the closer it got to me. It was moving very slowly and I could only see the light which looked the color of the sun but more of a glow and not intense light. I got to the point where it looked to be less than a few hundered feet from me. You could just barely make out what looked like a fuselage with patterned sections. So I'm seeing this huge ball of light about 50 feet diameter and then extended beyond the light is what looks like sections with doors or windows. All moving very slowly. I see 2 or 3 other people at the dock looking at the same thing. I went in to grab my phone and try to get a shot of it. When I came back outside the object had turned east and almost disappeared as I couldn't see the light anymore. Just a dark mass as I was seeing the rear of it
