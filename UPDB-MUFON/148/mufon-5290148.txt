Orbs traveling fast

I was standing in my balcony looking at Orions belt when I see 6 or 7 orbs moving at an unusually fast speed. They were in a wavy formation going straight toward the Mexican border. I have seen space craft before but not this small. Very organized and very fast. I am not joking when I say they were approaching 2,000 miles per hour at least.
