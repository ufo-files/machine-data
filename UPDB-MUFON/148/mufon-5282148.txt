I saw a shape move very slowly across the sky.

It was a clear night. I noticed that the Orion constellation was clearly visible. I was not able to make out the stars that form Taurus however I could faintly see the Pleiades star cluster. I then noticed a dark shape moving slowly across the sky. It started in Orion, heading towards Polaris. It was shaped something like a bow, but it was black against a black sky so I could not see it clearly. It was approximately the size of Orion's belt. It was moving in a northern direction, I followed it for about a minute until I lost it in a streetlight.
