{kt}Saw disc like object hovering NW of my location. It remained stationary, looked dark with row of lights on edge. Lasted about 10 min until I went back in house.

The sighting occurred during a late night dog walk at 1 AM along my road. It is customary for me to look at the stars; I try and pick out the constellations that I know and watch for the aircraft going over. (I live under the flightpath of airplanes between Chicago O'Hare and Detroit Metro Airport.). This object was not in that flightpath, it was Northwest and probably about thousand feet up (it wasn't low but it wasn't real high either) and I would estimate about 10 to 15 miles to my Northwest. it was noticeable because there was a row of lights on the edge of it in that a row of lights is not a typical night sky appearances. I kept looking at it trying to figure out what it was but I have never seen anything like it before. It was unusual because it was circular, had a role of lights (or windows) on the edge and it wasn't moving. There was no noise. Once I figured out it might be a UFO I hurried the dogs along to get back to the house. As far as I know the object never moved; a row of pine trees blocked my view the closer to the house we got. Nothing else unusual happened. I was a little bit scared of it and it may sound strange but I kept telling it to just stay over there, don't come near me. I don't care if you are there but I want no part of you. I could make a sketch of what I saw if you're interested.
