{nd} Looking out on an overcast night I noticed three stars in the sky when I looked closer I realize they were not Stars it was clearly in a triangle pattern

I woke up out of a dead sleep at 4 a.m. and went straight to the bathroom. looking out of my bathroom window I saw what I thought were stars. I looked around to see other stars and there were no other stars to be seen in the sky because it was an overcast night. I didn't realize that the stars that I thought I saw were actually three lights in a triangle pattern hovering over the tree line. Earlier that day we had crazy weather lightning thundering hail snow and very windy all in a matter of a few hours or less. I watch the three lights hover and I tried to notice if they moved it all judging by the branches of the trees that were in front of the lights. It did seem to very slowly move what seemed to me a few inches judging by how far away it was although the lights seem to be less then 200 feet away. The light's still remained even when I went to a different window and they were still no stars in the sky due to the overcast night. The lights remained and I went off to bed I have no idea how long they stay there.
