According to my father and mother in law ,
They saw an object lighting up the sky , south of theyre property . Not an aircraft but an object . This object shot out beams of light , they said the beam of light came from the bottom of it . They also said that they reported it to the local police . The episode lasted about 45 minutes , or at least that is what they thought , they lost hours somewhere , but the whole episode lasted from around midnight until about 4 a.m. . The object shot away like lightning and was gone . The next day my father in law went to where the beams of light hit the earth and found nothing .
