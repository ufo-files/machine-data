{nd} Eight UFO's over South Las Vegas Blvd

I was driving down W Richmar traveling West around 9:35PM on Dec. 7, 2016. Within a half mile of reaching the stop sign of Las Vegas Blvd I saw two Orb like objects in the west sky flickering reddish light traveling east. I noticed them because they didn't look like a plane or helicoptor or anything I've seen before. When I first saw them I thought they looked odd and a it made me feel curious so I drove across the street to see if I can get closer. One of them changed direction to the south and the other one went north then all faintly disappeared in mid sky. Three more appeared in the lower part of the western sky and did the same immediately after the first two but formed a very wide triangle pattern across the sky then faintly disappeared. Then three more traveled out of the lower western sky again and like the others did, two of them changed direction to the south and the last one traveled north but then quickly traveled towards me coming east and came within about a quarter mile where it started to descend, it was close enough to where I could see it was red in color but no longer emitting any light and appeared to look like two bubbles connected to each other. It descended out of view and appeared to be landing near the highway or in nearby neighborhoods.
