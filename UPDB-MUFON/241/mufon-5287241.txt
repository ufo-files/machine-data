Mushroom shaped object in the northern sky

It was mushroom shaped. Could see it clearly with binoculars. It began as a very white point - thought it was a star/planet. As the sun set, it turned yellow, then orange. It very slowly went away from us in a northern direction. It was too far to get a clear picture or video with my phone. My husband and my niece all saw it through the binoculars.
