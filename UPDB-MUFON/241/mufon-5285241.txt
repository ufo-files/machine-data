Bright Planet/Star like white glowing object in night sky

White glowing object in night sky. I decided to show my dad a green laser I use to point at stars in the night sky. I showed him by picking a bright planet out in the night sky to point at. I pointed the laser at the planet so he can see how bright the beam is. When we did so we discovered the planet was something else. The object went dark in the night sky. Realizing this wasn't a planet or star we became alarmed. The object emitted light again brightly then would dim down and disappear. Sometimes it would slowly begin to glow again and other times it would just suddenly appear. The object never changed its position or moved. We also realized that the object was in full glow and stationary in the sky all evening UNTIL we aimed the laser at it. About 15 minutes later a 2nd object appeared in the sky more to the north and functioned in that exact same way as the first object. This lasted 35 minutes or so until some patchy cloud cover came in.
