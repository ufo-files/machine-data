On Saturday night April 21, 2012 I observed an extremely bright star in the west and on Sunday evening April 22, 2012 I wanted my husband to see the star, so I went into the back yard to see if the star was visible yet. It was, so I stepped back to the back porch to have my husband come and see and as I approched the door there was a loud "boom" (like an explosion sound) and my husband was coming out as he had heard the noise also---I had him walk with me out into the yard to see the star and as I was pointing it out to him we saw an extremely bright green flashing light, then a extremely bright red flashing light a little distance west of the green one. They were moving very fast toward the south and thru a clearing between the trees we could see a red light farther south from the other 2 lights. The whole event took about 30 seconds. We stayed out a few minutes looking for more but we did not see anything else. The event took place at 8:50 pm. I have never seen anything like that ever. It was exciting but at the same time disturbing.
