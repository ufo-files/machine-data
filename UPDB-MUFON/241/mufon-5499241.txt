{cak} BETWEEN 15 - 30 GREEN LIGHTS ALL HOVERING FROM A NE DIRESTION & DISAPPEARING AS THEY TRAVELLED TO A SE DIRECTION BY BEAR MOUNTAIN

IT WAS APRIL 12 2014 AROUND 8:40 P.M. I WENT TO PAY MY SISTER SOME MONEY I OWED HER, WE WERE IN HER FRONT YARD TALKING WHEN OUT OF THE CORNER OF MY EYE ON THE NE DIRECTION, I SEEN WHAT SEEMED LIKE A GREENISH CLOUD MOVING BUT THEN I SAID TO MYSELF NO, THEN I LOOKED AGAIN, ASKED MY SISTER IS THAT A GREEN CLOUD MOVING OR AM I SEEING THINGS, SHE LOOKED AND SAID ITS PROBABLY FALLING/SHOOTING STARS, THEN WE SEEN SOME PAUSE IN MID AIR FOR ABOUT 3-4 SECONDS AND CONTINUE HOVERING IN A SE DIRECTION. SHE WAS CONFUSED AND ASKED IF FALLING/SHOOTING STARS CAN DO THAT. I TOLD HER THOSE AIN'T STARS, THAT'S SOMETHING ELSE. WE OBSERVED THEM FOR ABOUT 5-10 MINUTES BEFORE THEY JUST DISAPPEARED INTO THE NIGHT SKY 1 BY 1, THEY ALL SEEMED TO BE TRAVELING IN LIKE TRIANGULAR FORMATION WHETHER IT WAS 3 OR 4 OR 5 IN A GROUP, THEY ALL KEPT A TRIANGULAR FORMATION. AFTER THEY DISAPPEARED I WENT TO MY GIRLFRIENDS HOUSE, TOLD HER WHAT I OBSERVED AND I GOT BACK IN MY CAR, DROVE OUT TO THE MOUNTAIN AREA TO SEE IF I CAN SEE OR OBSERVE MORE, STAYED OUT FOR ABOUT 2 HOURS BUT NOTHING SHOWED UP AFTER THAT.
