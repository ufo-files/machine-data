{tjd} Orange Flashing Light - SE Lawrence

At approx. 12:10am I looked out the window to see a light orange flashing light near the top of the tree-line across the street. It appeared to be much further out, over a mile. It was fixed in place for about thirty minutes, then started quickly darting up and down, side to side before completely disappearing around 12:45am.
