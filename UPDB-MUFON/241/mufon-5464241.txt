Observed UFO Over Little Rock Arkansas

I was at downtown Little Rock, Arkansas near the Marriot Hotel when I saw this bright white object in the sky. The object was slowly moving from North to North East. The light did not blink once, nor did the object make any noise. What was interesting as well, was that there appear to be two aircraft following the object.
