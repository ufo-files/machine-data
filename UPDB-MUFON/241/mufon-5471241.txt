{dr}Flying then split into two.

I was locking up my truck and looked into the sky and noticed an orange light almost like a plane heading north to south. It stood out to me with the object being hazy, moving faster than a plane, and no flashing lights. It seemed like the objects had some lights on the front of it and was moving over twice the speed of an airplane at that altitude. I stood there watching it to see if it was a plane for about three minutes then it all of a sudden split into two and rocketed away from each other going east and west at a high rate of speed. After it split, it was no longer visible. I remained looking in the general vicinity for another minute to see if it would appear, but the objects never reappeared.
