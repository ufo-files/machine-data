{nd} I saw a bright orange light moving from left to right, then hovering, then exiting our atmosphere

I was exiting a 7-11 right near my house when I noticed what looked like a massive explosion in the sky, however the light was steady. The object emitting the light was obviously solid but I couldn't determine the exact shape. It was traveling from my left to right in a straight line at about 3-5'000 ft then stopped and hovered for about 10-15 seconds. I put my bag down and grabbed my phone as the object started to fly straight up. I was able to get 3 pictures before the object appeared to exit our atmosphere and it's light faded out. The object never made a sound but I did see a ripple in the sky around it. It was basketball sized (WAY BIGGER than anything I've ever seen in the sky) when I first saw it and got smaller as it left, finally leaving my sight.
