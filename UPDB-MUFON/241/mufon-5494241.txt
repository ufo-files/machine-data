{nd} Twenty plus Orange lights almost in a huge flock moving slowly towards downtown Charlotte, NC.

I just pulled out of my driveway and turned down Shamrock Drive heading east when I noticed twenty or more Orange lights straight ahead in a giant flock. They were about 20 meters apart moving very slowly west towards uptown Charlotte. I continued heading towards Eastway drive and as I came closer to the stop light I tried pulling over but due to other cars and witnesses looking up as well I was unable to pull over and was forced to continue east on Shamrock Drive and eventually lost sight. I was almost stunned in shock and wondered if anyone felt the same way. I arrived to dinner and could not even eat.
