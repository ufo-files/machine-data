{jpg} Solid ball of integrated moving light. Closest I can come up with is plazma like. Light was integrated.

My wife goes out each night around 10 PM to view the stars before bed. She came back in and said come look at this. " I first thought it was a star but it is twinkling too much and too much color." I went outside and it appeared about the size of Venus but twinkling light. We got the binoculars and it was definitely not a planet. I went back in immediately and got my telescope. After locating the object in the telescope it appeared to be a mix of blue, red, green and some white on the edges at times. The light was not flashing, pulsating or solid. It appeared to be a mixture. Like the light was a solid and was being mixed around like a lava lamp. I called it plasma like. I could then tell it was slowly moving. It was about to go below our horizon. Appeared to be in a very slow orbit.

The next night we went out and saw it again. It appeared to be further out when viewing in the telescope. Also, I found one more a little to the south of the one visible by the unaided eye.  It was very small as seen through the telescope. Found it by trying to locate the unaided visible one in the telescope.
