An unusually bright object moved from NW to SE with no strobe lights

At about 0730 this morning my wife was getting ready to head to work when I looked up out my front door and saw a bright steady light moving toward the west moving from a NW to SE direction. At first I thought it was just another aircraft passing by but quickly noticed how bright it was, as though a light was pointed in our direction from it, and its brightness never changed regardless of its position in the sky. I also noticed that though it was still dark out, there was not any flashing strobe lights coming from this object. I asked the wife if she saw the strobes and she did not. 

It was a partly cloudy sky with stars and the moon visible with clouds ranging from 4 thousand to 6500 feet which at one point the craft disappeared behind and was seen again on the other side of the cloud. It was clearly higher than the clouds but seemed lower than aircraft normally flying that general path. 

Being in a remote location in SW Alaska we routinely see commercial aircraft flying at cruising altitude from places like Russia, China, Japan etc. This object was flying at a lower cruising altitude from most aircraft we see here. What stood out was the unusual brightness that did not change regardless of the angle we saw it, and the fact we never saw any anti-collision strobe lights.

My wife took some pictures with her phone and I will see if I can share those later. I have lived here for going on 8 years and have never seen anything like that before.
