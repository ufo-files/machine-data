{dr}Fifteen white, glowing, boomerang shape with no sound moving quickly acrossthe sky

I was lying on the porch looking at the sky when i saw two white, glowing boomerang shapes moving together across the sky east to west. A few minutes later, a third one moved towards a fourth one going east to west. Another went southwest to northeast, one was seen moving north to south, another moving northeast to southwest. This all occurred between 1045 - 1145 pm. The objects moved quickly at a steady speed with no noise. I could see them through the clouds and in between clouds. At first I thought it was a light beam moving across the sky but then saw the shape and the fact they were coming from different directions. All together, I saw fifteen objects.
