UFO glided past parked car, then took off into the air, lit up and shot off into space

When I was a High School Senior in May of 1986, I picked up a friend of mine from his Girl friends house one night at around 11 PM, and was giving him a ride home. We stopped a little ways from his house, on East Road in East Windsor CT, and were talking about the days events before I dropped him off. I was in the drivers seat, he was in the passenger seat. I suddenly saw an object silently gliding through the air approximately 8' off of the ground and about 20'' - 30' feet from the car. It was triangular shaped, no markings on it, no windows. It had a very dull orangish/copper glow, and, although I don't remember a sound, I felt like there was a "buzz" or "charge" in the air. In fact it was a lack of sound that made it seem even more eerie. It was on the passenger side, just over my friends shoulder, and was travelling parallel to the car. He saw the look of shock on my face and turned to see it. We watched as it effortlessly glided past us, and my friend [name moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] yelled to follow it. I started my car and we went in the direction it was moving. We travelled about 1/4 of a mile and saw it suddenly light up and streak off into the sky at incredible speeds. I tried watching it through the windshield as I was driving. It left 3 light streaks, or "trails" as it traveled in the sky, and changed direction like a rubber ball ricocheting off of a wall. It made three turns like that and just shot off out of sight at an incredible speed (the whole incident was < 1 minute in total). After that I dropped him off and went home to bed.

My friend did not ever want to discuss the topic after that, and was furious when I told a few of our other friends and my Shop teacher [name moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] the next day, as they started to laugh at him and tease us about the "weather balloon" we saw. We graduated a few weeks later. I have not seen him since then, nor have he and I ever discussed it after that. I only recently started talking to people about this, but it has haunted me my entire life. I feel that with the number of sightings, and the government admitting to the phenomenon, I wanted to finally report this. I am wondering if other people in the area saw anything that night.
