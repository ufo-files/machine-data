Observed object hover above car shine beam of light down onto car and then move to a tree line where it hovered above the trees and then quickly left

This is a long story to type out into my phone but it is true and there was another witness who I’ve lost touch with that can verify my story as actually having happened. The gist of it is this oblong smooth cigar shaped object about the size of a small sedan and only maybe 30-40 feet in the air hovered silently over my car with me and my friend inside. We were stopped and parked in a graveyard up by our local high school after work one night, just shooting the shit and smoking some cigarettes listening to music, we weren’t under the influence of any drugs or alcohol, we had only just got done work a restaurant about 25-30 minutes prior. We look up and both notice this thing because we had my sunroof open and we were checking out the stars and only noticed it because it blocked out the stars as it moved. We looked at each other I assume to ask the other if they were seeing it too but we both looked pale and scared shitless so I knew he did I think he could tell I saw it too. We turned right back to it and now it had stopped above the car, and I distinctly remember paying attention to see if I heard or felt rotor wash or the heat from some sort of thruster but the temperature did not change and neither did the wind. It flashed a bright white beam down on the car for no longer than one second and then moved on to our west about 20 yards and stopped again right above the nearby tree line, wheee I also observed that the trees just 5 or so feet beneath this thing did not sway or move out of the way like they would if it was applying a downward force to stay in the air, it’s also important to note it was completely silent the whole time. It then moved up in altitude abruptly about 20 feet then moved to the south another 20 feet and then shot off to the west like a bullet and I never saw anything like it before or since.
