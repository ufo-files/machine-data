{cak} black triangle with lights at corners hovered above highway stationary

It was between 10:30 and 11:30 at night. I was westbound on I40 west of Raleigh, NC. I initially saw what I thought could be an airliner with white lights in the sky much like you might see approaching for a landing. As I watched the aircraft in the night sky, it didn't go left or right but stayed in line with the highway. I assumed it might be approaching directly towards me or traveling directly away from me. Eventually, after maybe 5 minutes I noticed it was directly above my car still high in the sky. Being a clear night and my cars sunroof cover being open (not the sunroof itself, just the sliding interior cover) I was able to watch it up in the sky above my car. It had a definite triangle shape and I could see the actual edges and exact shape of the craft. I wondered what type of plane this was as NC has many military bases. I knew it wasnt a commercial plane/passenger airliner.
It was getting late and I still had 6 hours of driving left to do so I didn't let it bother me. I was curious at best. Today 12/2/13 I thought I would surf the net for pictures/info about what aircraft I had seen and I have not seen anything like it except some UFO stuff. I also figured there would be other reports of people who had seen it because this was Sunday after Thanksgiving-a major travel day. Please let me know if there were other drivers seeing this and what it was that I might have seen. The object was motionless. I have a noisy car so not able to hear anything. No feelings on my part-I was more concerned about getting to Ohio. The object being stationary was behind me after that or so I assume. I did not see it fly away. It was high enough I wasnt frightened but yet low enough to see the shape without any difficulty.
