Appeared like a bright star with naked eye. Appeared as a disc with cone at the centre viewed with a camera.

There are no lights directly to the rear of my property and views of the sky are clear. I recognise the path of Venus and other stars but this star only appeared for one night. It was brighter than Venus and I decided to take photos. It appeared to dim then brighten again. When I used a zoom function on my camera it was granite grey with a darker centre which appeared raised. It appeared like an egg with a light grey disc and dark grey centre.
