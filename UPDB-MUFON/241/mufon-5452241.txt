I spotted what I thought to be a plane.

My wife & I were sitting outside having a fire in our pit. Our home is located on the flight path to land & depart from Spokane Airport, also Fairchild airforce base is here, so we see planes all day & helicopters. At about 01:15 I looked up to the sky and saw what I assumed was a plane coming our direction from the SE direction. It was higher than a plane coming in to land in Spokane. It had white bright lights & was moving at a good pace and I pointed it out to my wife & said look at the plane not coming to land in Spokane. My wife is a pilot. She looked up & said that is no plane. We observed it flying more towards us which is North of when I first spotted it. As it moved more towards our direction from my view the light's started to dim & then totally disappear. My wife was able to keep track of it and she was pointing to me the direction it was going but I still couldn't find it once I had lost the light's. She said it was ascending at an angle & kept ascending until it disappeared. When first spotted my wife estimated it was about 20,000 feet above us. I probably saw the object for about 20 seconds before I lost track of it. Since I first thought it was a plane I didn't think to grab my phone & record it. It happened so quickly & I just watched it move across the sky with fascination that it didn't occur to me to grab my phone.
