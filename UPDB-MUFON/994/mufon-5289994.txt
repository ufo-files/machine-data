Silver, reflected the sun, shaped like a large blimp with no rotors or undercarriage.

I was driving home from work and stopped at a series of traffic lights for approximately 10 minutes. I noticed a metalic blimp-like structure hovering in the sky, but it had no rotor or undercarriage. It was a symetrical blimp-like shape, with a smooth surface. The sun reflected off of it. As I sat at the first light for a few minutes, I noticed the object was not moving, not getting larger or smaller, just hovering. I turned on the camera on my phone to take a video but the object would not appear on the screen. I changed my phone from video to camera, but I still could not see the object. I moved to the next light, which was a very long light. I tried to film it again at the next light, but I could not see it on my screen. The object remained hovering. Then all of a sudden the object flew east at a speed I have never seen before or since. It was not an airplane, helicopter or blimp. I do not know what it was.
