{tjd} 3 star like objects flying in and out of eachother

I was looking up as I do in the park by my house. Zero clouds. I saw three star like objects flying in and out of each other. i really thought at first they were birds. The way they were flying but then noticed planes at least two or three flying below them which told me, way too high for birds. They kind of looked like stars flying in and out of each other. I can compare to when you get up to fast and you get light headed and see stars. (visually) Although I did not and these appeared out of nowhere extremely high and lasted a lot longer. Also noticed their bizarre flight pattern. I remember wanting to dismiss as birds but definitely could not. Could see them way up high flying almost together, in and out. I watched to see where they would go and vanished. IMO definitely not a plane of any kind. Not a star or a satellite because of movement. Way too high to be a bird. Was not my imagination or seeing things because I still have the image and movement of the unknown objects in my head. They were extremely high up, obviously above the stratosphere. No feelings or anything from them. Just thought was the most bizarre thing I have ever witnessed. Should of tried to film but was caught up watching. I would say it lasted over a minute or so realistically. Felt longer. They stayed in same area flying in and out. Then poof, gone.
