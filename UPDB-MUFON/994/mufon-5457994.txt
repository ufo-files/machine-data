{rjl} Hovering dull white sphere accompanied by f35 jet

I was driving northbound on 71 highway at 9:30am on Wednesday Sept 18th when I noticed a low flying jet traveling west to east over the highway. I was trying to identify the jet and thinking wow he is really low and flying slow. As I watched it fly over the highway a small white orb was directly in my line of sight behind the jet. My eyes stopped on the orb and I lost track of the jet as it headed to the east. I kept my eyes on the orb but had to try and understand what I was seeing. I put on my brakes to back off the cars in front of me and then refocused on the orb. There was a dull white orb hovering just to the east of the skyline. I couldn't tell its exact size or altitude. But it stayed perfectly still for about 20 seconds. I continued to slow down and tried to see if it was a helicopter or if I could see any marker lights. But it didn't have any lights or moving parts. I kept my eyes on it and I noticed it starting to get smaller and smaller before it quickly disappeared. I am pretty positive that it went from hovering to taking an extremely fast path to the north at a fixed altitude before it was too far away from me for me to see. I did not see the jet again after I was fixed on the orb but I am fairly positive that it was an f35. I have seen many aircraft moving to and from the downtown airport as well as to and from Whiteman. However I have not seen an f35 around kansas city. Both objects seemed out of place, let alone seeing what seemed like the jet circling around the hovering orb at a distance.
