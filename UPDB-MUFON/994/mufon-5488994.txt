{cak} looked up to see a passenger jet and spotted a ufo

on the morning of Saturday 9/19/2015 I was working on a cabin on the lake shore of diamond lake . at around 830 am . I looked up at see a large passenger jet pass over , thinking it looked cool at this high altitude , the lake is around 5000 feet elevation. as the jet passed over head it appeared to pass what looked like at first glance was a large bright star , only this is day time and its not a star . a co worker and I observed the object for five or six hours , until I had to leave the jobsite to go to my other job as a security guard. during those hours we looked at object through binoculars and could see the object much clearer. the object seemed to be hovering but was tumbling and spinning in one spot , you could clearlie see the sun reflect off of the shinny metallic surface.and lights red , blue white , yellow and a few different shades between . we kept watching the object as we worked , not getting much done because of what we where seeing . I eventually had to leave for my other job. I stopped a few times as I was leaving so I could spot it agine , and did . took a few pictures with my phone but it is cheap and I don't think I got any that would show up , because I could not see it through my viewfinder. I did take a few pictures and a short video that I have not looked at yet but is on my phone . I have not had a chance to download the stuff yet because I just got home and began to do this report while it is still fresh as possible in my memorie.
