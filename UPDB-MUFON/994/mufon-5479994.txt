I was outside walking my dog at 1:00 PM EST when I heard a small propeller plane and looked up to find it. At that point I spotted something else in the sky, at first I thought it looked like a satellite, and then thought perhaps it was a plane. But the shape of the object was plane-like, however the wing-span was much shorter and they sat further towards the back of the craft, it was mostly white, with silver or gray joints. The UFO was moving at a much faster pace than the jets that usually fly over, it left no trail. It was headed northeast towards Bristol, VA from Johnson City, TN seemingly following a normal path for everyday air-traffic in the area. As I gazed at the object it appeared to be blurry, or my eyes were blurry because I was looking at the object, I don't know which. I didn't take my eyes off the object for at least 3 minutes, after-which I glanced down at the dog and when I looked back up, the object had completely disappeared. Not a cloud in the sky, mind you, I imagine the object was also far too high to land that quickly, as it was at a reasonable elevation.
