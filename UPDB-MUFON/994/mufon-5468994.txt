As I was leaving the subdivision which is Northgate and Linden Road, I looked over at Carman-Ainsworth High School and there was a diamond shape orange and yellow glowing object. This object was just hovering there. I stopped at the corner and told my wife who was riding with me, my God look at that, she said what is that? We just sat there in our car looking. My wife then stated that our daughter is at Speedway waiting for us, a you better hurry up because she's got to get back on the highway to go to school. She was returning to college at the University of Michigan Ann Arbor . I slowly exited our subdivision onto Linden Road going south, my eye on the object that was floating in the air over the school. Suddenly it started moving slowly South in the air. I went on and as I'm driving I could still see the glowing object, it was dark because it was pretty late she usually left to go back to school at 8:30ish at night because traffic was light . as I'm going south I still kept it in sight. I can see it moving as I drove south. as I got to the gas station Speedway I looked in the sky and suddenly it was gone.
