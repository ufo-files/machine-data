{cak} An object that initially looked like the sun, grew to enormous size, became orange, and became an elongated oval.

In approx. 1965, I was a student at a university in Richmond Virginia. A close friend and I decided to take a ride on Rt. 460 on a sunny and warm day, leaving Richmond and traveling west. After an hour or so, we began to return to Richmond on the same highway, now traveling east. I was in passenger seat and we chatted as my friend drove. I noticed the sun through the windshield - it was up fairly high, almost touching/being framed by the top edge of the windshield on my side of the car. As we chatted, I noticed the sun seemed to be growing larger - gradually. We continued chatting while I took a glance at the sun every once in a while. It was definitely growing larger. My friend noticed also and we stopped chatting as our attention was now riveted on the sun. Very gradually - maybe as long as 15 - 30 minutes it grew in size until it almost filled the top portion of our view through the windshield. The color changed from yellow (like the sun) to orange. The shape changed from round to oval - a very elongated oval. There was no sound and the object hovered in the sky. It was bright and gently glowing. There were no details of any kind on the shape - it was uniformly orange and softly glowing throughout. I said to my friend, "What is that?" and she responded "I don't know" We were mesmerized. I felt apprehension but not fear. The strangeness of it was compelling. There is missing time and missing memories because the next thing that I can recall is that the orb was gone and we were still in the car, driving, but almost back in Richmond.
