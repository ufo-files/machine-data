A bright yellow orange light

My husband and I were driving home on the freeway. I looked out the front window and saw a bright orb. It was the same color as the street lights that line the freeway, so at first I thought that it was those. But I realized it was separate and above the freeway lights. It was bigger too, about thumb sized? It was just hovering, maybe spinning. Suddenly the lights winked out, but there was a tiny light that just fell to the ground. When we got home we were looking at the stars and then the same colored light appeared in the sky. This time it was more star sizes, but same color. It appeared , hovered, then fell again, the light getting dimmer as it fell. My husband said he saw it turn to the west. It reminded me of a flare in the way it was bright then fell. But we live in a city, so why would someone use a flare?
