I was taking pictures of the John Hancock Building downtown Chicago and accidentally caught an unknown object in the shot. I did not realize it was there until I downloaded the pictures to my computer and took a closer look at the cloud like trail from the object in the upper right hand corner of the picture. It appears to look like something coming in at a high rate of speed. I do not think it is a meteor but not sure. The picture has fairly good resolution and can be blown up to get a better look.
