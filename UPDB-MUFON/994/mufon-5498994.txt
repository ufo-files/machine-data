{cak} multiple red lights blinking on and off

My wife called me to come outside, she said she never saw anything like this before. I observed about six red lights off in the distance remaining mostly stationary but some would blink out only to be replaced by others at a different altitude. There was no trail like you would see with fireworks. The lights remained stationary as far as I could tell from that distance. The video is jumpy because I was at max zoom. I had a hard time keeping them in frame so I didn't shoot video the entire time they were visiable. Some lights appeared just above the horizon. Eventually, they all dimmed and disappeared.
