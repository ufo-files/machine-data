Fireball that ascended and descended, slowly hoverered and shot off out of the atmosphere

I walked into my parents living room and looked out of the patio window and there was a bright fireball hovered above the fur tree in the garden. I walked outside and stood close to it and the light got much brighter. It moved closer to the ground for a few seconds and then hovered again above the tree. It then slowly moved across open ground (parkway) and shot off out of the atmosphere. 

When I went outside it started coming closer and the light was almost blinding. There was no specific shape or any form of life that I could see. Strangely I did not feel scared.

I am not looking for any publicity, but simply wonder if anyone can explain what I witnessed that evening.
