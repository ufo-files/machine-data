Four glowing balls in the sky, some would fade then reappear in the same and different spots, movement appeared to quick for an airplane or helicopter

Fishing on the beach, no lights visible when I arrived just a clear night with stars and some planes, the moon was out but in a crecent shape, I happened to fish for awhile until I looked out towards the main city of surface paradise, and noticed four very large yellow glowing lights in the sky, at first the looked almost to be arranged in a cross shaped formation. I immediately realised that this was something unusual that I had never seen before and was quick to race up the beach to my bag and get my phone to film the event, by the time I got my phone the lights became a bit more spaced out and two lights had gotten smaller looking while the other two were still large and bright, one would fade out for a few seconds then reappear in the same spot then sometimes would fade and appear next to one of the larger lights. I believe there is no way a plane could cover the distance that these lights did and there was no red or green flashing navigation lights that aircrafts have on of a night, these were solid yellow glowing lights and appeared to be closer to the ocean then the planes at the time which I could tell were slot higher in the sky then these. After another minute and I stopped filming the all appeared to travel in the same direction heading further north then they all just faded away, I never saw them again after that. I've seen plenty of helicopters and planes and to me this experience was completely out of the ordinary and can not explain exactly what it was I saw tonight.
