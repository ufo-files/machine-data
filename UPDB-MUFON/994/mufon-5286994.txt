Triangle with one main light source at front

I am a musician and was performing outside at a gig. At aprox 9:45pm I became aware of an odd light in the sky, yellow/gold in the center, becoming more red/orange towards the edges. It was solid and unwavering. It was moving slowly from my left to right then stopped and turned so it seemed to be facing me. This is when the edges became visible, coming back from the light in a V pattern. I could see light reflection on the underside, forming a triangle. There where no other lights on the object. It then stopped, began heading down, stopped, moved up, stopped, and then turned and began moving to my right again behind some trees. I could see it move behind the trees for a few seconds, then it rose above the tree line and disappeared. I have no point of reference for size, but I get the sense it was some distance away. My band mates and I where the only ones facing that direction, but they didn't notice it. It was in my view for approx 90 seconds, half a song.
