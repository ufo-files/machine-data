{nd} Driving home and saw lights

We were driving home from eating dinner and the moon was rising in the east and due to high winds there were clouds in the sky but the moon was visible and interesting. We so a bright set of lights that seemed to be in an oval ribbon form on the lower right of the moon then it vanished a minute later it appeared again to the upper left of the moon with the same types of light. The last time we saw the lights and object it was below the left side of the moon. My wife saw the lights as well.
