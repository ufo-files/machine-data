{cak} Large group of lights over windsor/greeley

I walked out the front of my house and looked west towards windsor colorado, from roughly bittersweet park in greeley colorado, and saw a large grouping of red/yellow lights they were strategically maneuvering northwest. Although, they did not seem to follow a pattern, they were certainly following a single light at the head of the grouping. Almost instinctively, they would disappear and reappear suddenly above, below, and around each other. But hey never passed the brightest reddish/yellow light. By the end of it, the entire event lasting roughly 6 and a half minutes, all the lights simply disappeared.. I'll never be able to understand what I saw...
