Random pulses of light

I was attempting CE-5 for the 9th time between 8:45pm and 9:45pm CST in St. Charles, MO. I was observing from the Northeast to the Northwest. Around 9:45pm, a star-like object in the appearance of a satellite or debris flew in from the East to the Northwest, flashed twice, then disappeared. Seconds later, over the course of two and a half minutes, random pulses of light appeared to the Northeast and North of my position. There were no planes or helicopters or any navigation lights to indicate such things. The weather was also clear. After the last light, the sky fell dark again with no oddity.
