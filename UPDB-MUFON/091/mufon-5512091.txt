(GS) Extra-Terrestrials and UFO

I was taking a walk after school, like I usually do, and my curious mind thought to see two people walking, but as I looked through my binoculars, they turned out to be two ETs walking on the other side of the Lake in my backyard. I looked through my binoculars again and they noticed I was watching. As I was walking back home, the same ETs showed up as I was halfway to home. One stared me in the eyes. I held my breathe for 15 seconds. Then they "turned to ash" and I looked above and noticed 3 lights in triangular formation and flying away very fast.
