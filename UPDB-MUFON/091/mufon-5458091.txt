{cak} Two Low flying Objects Moving through Yakima Valley.

I Had walked out my front door to notice these two red blinking lights, as well as one solid white one. They were two the north west, I was looking at roughly bearing 30 degrees, as they moved east. They were way to low to be a plane, though I brushed it off as a Helicopter at first. Though I got curious and tried to drive out to them. They flew outside of Sunnyside when I gave up my chase, flying on the North side of town along the hills towards Handford. Though flying towards Yakima, I went about my evening when I got a call 20 minutes later from my father, asking how to check for overhead aircraft with flight radar 24, an app I had shown him a month ago, - when I asked why he had mentioned the two craft flying over his home, parallel to each other, also finding it really odd. I didn't get to hear where they had flown from after that.

I am making this report in hopes that if anyone else had seen these two objects. That they could also make there own report. Also - the tracker had shown nothing but a cessna 175 flying North from Yakima towards Ellensburg and a Boeing 737 heading to Seattle that was not along the same rout.
