{jpg} Very bright Reddish glowing object.

This was possibly a meteorite or space junk falling to earth. I was in my front yard looking towards the east at the very bright moon. I turned to look over my house and I saw an amazing bright Reddish light cruising across the sky going South West to North West. I wanted to run into my house to grab my camcorder but I knew I would not have time to get it. So I stayed and watched this light for about 20 more seconds as it finally faded away. The whole event lasted about 30 seconds. I've seen satellites and the space station from my house and this was very different. The intensity of the light was Brilliant..... Nothing like I have seen before.
