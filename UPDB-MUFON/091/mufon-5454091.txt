So it was a clear night out and I decided to get my telescope out because it was quite warm tonight. I like looking at the moon every night I get a chance. So I focused my telescope at the moon. I believe it was a waxing gibbous tonight if I'm not mistaken. I was not expecting to see what I saw tonight. Now I've seen satellites pass the moon and they have a clear define shape and same with most asteroids. This was weird. I witness for about 45ish seconds a black almost glowing object traverse the lower half of the moon. unlike a asteroid is gave off light I think but it was a weird light if that makes sense. Also it moved very slow compared to satellites. As I said it lasted about 45 seconds.
