{rjl} Observed coming from the SW turned over front range, hovered, changed direction to North disappeard

Approximately 7:35 P.M. went out my patio door that faces West toward the mountains. I always look to the sky when I am out there because I enjoy looking at the stars. I noticed the object coming from the South West moving North East and the light around the object was different from that of an airliner. Generally, Planes come from this direction on approach to DIA. This light was different and I noticed no blinking or exterior light as in a taxi light. Object appeared "Different" in size and shape. As it approached what I believed the Hogback area of the foothills the object slowed, then stopped, moved in a descending manner maybe a couple hundred feet then changed direction to North. I watched the object and when it was what appeared to be several miles away the light around the object simply went out. Duration of time observed was about 6 minutes. I was amazed at first that I might be witnessing something that was definitely not an airplane or Helicopter. I then realized that I might just be witnessing something not man made. When it hovered and then changed direction the move was erratic and very fast. I took photo's on my cell phone camera but do not have the ability to transfer them to my computer to include them.
