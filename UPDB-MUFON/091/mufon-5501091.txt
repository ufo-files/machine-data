In back garden looking for hedgehog with torch. Saw something out of corner of eye, looked up and saw bright green orb.

Around 10.30 pm.on the 12/07/2014 i went into my back garden with a torch to look for our resident hedgehog. I saw something out of the corner of my eye, looked up and saw a luminescent green orb-like object. I have never witnessed anything like it, i would describe myself as a sceptical person and a realist.I did not know what it was but i knew it wasn't an aeroplane as it was too close and was silent. It was at treetop height and hovered for a few seconds. I kept watching and it moved behind, then above the trees and shone a beam of bright green light over the surrounding houses. It was first seen at SSE and ended up at SW. It then just disappeared.
