Orange and quickly shot down with orange tail and sparks at end of tail.

The object was orange. I thought that it was a fireball, but I haven’t gotten confirmation of that from the American Meteor Society, so I’m asking Mufon to investigate it. The object was east, shooting down with rapid speed that lasted about a second and a half. The part at the bottom of it was dark and the tail was orange. The end of the tail had sparks that formed as the object descended. The object appeared and then perhaps landed upon descent? I saw it from inside my home, looking out the closed window so I don’t know if it made a noise. It was bright and quick, like it just appeared out of nowhere and crashed downwards quickly. I was in awe. I felt like this one and a half second viewing made time stand still for me. I would really like to know exactly what it was.
