{cak} 2 balls moving swiftly traveling north, one split accelerated and disappeared

They suddenly stopped and hovered for several seconds before starting again. At that point they accelerated and one of the balls did a 90 degree turn on the dime and accelerated again at very high speed. The speed was insanely fast and it just disappeared completely. The other ball accelerated as well and was moving at an insane speed and disappeared as well.
