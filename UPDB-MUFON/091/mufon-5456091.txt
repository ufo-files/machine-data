I and my father were in the car when we arrived in Pinoso, a town in the province of Alicante, and we saw in the sky several kilometers away an orange light that remained still. I looked at it and saw how it started to move and followed us all the way. We lost sight of It when it passed through some mountains. Later we saw it again but this time on the opposite side of the car. It passed over the car at an altitude of 200 meters and I could see that it was triangular in shape with a light on every corner.
