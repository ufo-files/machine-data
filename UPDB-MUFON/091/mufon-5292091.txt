Looking towards the sunset small bright as much as the sun round pea size ufo hovered for about a minute and went up and broke into 2 pieces then 3

I was walking my dog 9pm 25th June and it was a mega hot night, the sky was clear totally clear, I had a strange feeling there was kind of an anxiety feeling around me like I felt something was going to happen it was a strange atmosphere. I looked up at the sky and noticed how clear it was.
I turned around 180 degrees and was sighted by a beautiful sunset and straight away I noticed a bright orb glowing as bright as the sun was shining at around 2 o’clock if the top of the sun was 12 o’clock, so I took out my phone from my pocket and took the pictures provided, I wish now I’d filmed the anomaly. It took a few minutes for it to go from an orb to just drifting away in the sky’s atmosphere, as if all its energy had been depleted, it’s progression was slow, roughly 3-4 minutes. I’ve seen a few ufo type objects in the last 3 years in this area. But I have no photo or film footage to prove this and those sightings are a completely different story and scenario.
