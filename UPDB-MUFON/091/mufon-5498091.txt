{nd} Walking dogs, observed shiny object with trail, thought it was an asteroid but then it dissappeared instantly.

I was walking my two dogs in the neighborhood in broad daylight (about 9:30 AM). The sky was clear, with not a single cloud. This is probably why the sudden appearance of a dark, black trail in the sky immediately drew my attention. At first I thought it was an asteroid, as the tail had the characteristic arc and misty appearance of an asteroid tail- but as soon as I traced the trail of the object with my eyes, I observed the object at the front to be incredibly shiny and reflective, like it was covered in chrome. As soon as I had seen the reflection off the shiny object, it seemed to blink out of existence, with no trail left in the sky. I searched the sky for probably 30 seconds looking for where it had gone, and whether it could have been an airplane, and I couldn't see a single other thing in the sky. I was particularly confused at how quickly and completely it disappeared. The entire event was probably less than a second in duration.
