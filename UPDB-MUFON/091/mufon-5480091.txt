{rjl} Hovering green and red blinking lights wir red very fast pulse

While having a campfire in my yard I looked up into the sky and noticed a green blinking light with a very fast pulsing red light.The red light stopped and I only saw the green light blinking at a rate of about 2 per second. After about ten seconds the red light began pulsing again for about ten seconds. This happened 3 times while hovering at what seemed a high altitude, at least 1000 feet above but could have been higher. After hovering for about 2 minutes after I noticed it it began moving NE, the red light was not seen any more. I watched it as I headed towards the hills at a constant rate of speed but then it deceneed rapidly out of sight.
I thought it strange as a person that has worked on aircraft for over 30 years that this did not act like a normal aircraft. Anti collision lights never pulse at that high rate or as brightly and never turn on and off like that.
