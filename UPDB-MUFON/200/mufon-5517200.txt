{flg} Sailing on the Chesapeake. I said

We were sailing on the Chesapeake Bay and saw pipe-like large object with no wings going left to right. The object had no wings, and I took a picture of it while husband was sailing. The object made no sound as we were about 1 mile away, then it took off through the clouds. This is the second time we had an encounter. The first time was in the evening in Tiffin, Ohio. I have a picture, but you will show my husband’s face, so I will not send the photo I have. Would be happy to send it to you with a different venue without public viewing
