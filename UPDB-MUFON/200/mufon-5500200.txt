{cak} Star like appearance slightly blue tinge. Much like a bright LED

I was using an ISS app on my smart phone to watch an Iridium Flare that would occur at about 9:10 PM. I was in my front yard looking in the general direction of where the Iridium Flare was to take place. I thought I was seeing things when I saw what appeared to be a star that was brighter than most stars, much like a planet. I thought I saw the star move several times. The Iridium Flare occurred right on time. I went in the house, told my wife that I thought I saw a UFO. We both went out in the back yard and began to watch the object. Shortly after we began to watch the object it moved in abrupt right, left movements and several times it moved in a small circle. It defied what I consider the laws of Physics. My wife then saw another object to the left of the bright object. The object was not as bright,however, it was also moving like the first object. We watched the two objects for about 50 minutes. I tried to view the brightest object through binoculars, it was difficult to focus, however, there was a bluish tinge to the whiteness of the object. I tried to take a video with my point and shoot camera, on a tripod, however, there was not enough light emission from the object to take the video. The brightness of the primary object is much like the intensity of the ISS as it passes overhead.
