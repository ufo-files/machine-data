Witnessed from my balcony at a hotel in Phuket

This occurred shortly before complete darkness. I went out on my hotel balcony and witnessed 6 objects that were moving across the sky in a deliberate manner. You can see from the photos that in a relatively short time, 7:28 PM - 7:30 PM, the pattern changed as did the direction for some of the objects. They almost appeared to pose as constellations. The actual event lasted longer than 2 minutes, more like 10 minutes and eventually went out of site.
