I was looking at the stars from our camp site. No city lights. Stars were brilliant. I noticed an unusual light that was moving forward then back. It would move around in a sporadic patter. The center light was a white/red with a pattern of additional lights positioned at 2 6 and 10. From the center the pattern was blue light and then a white light. I turned and asked my wife to come out of the camper to see it and it was gone. Here I was looking at it for nearly a half hour and in 15 seconds it was gone. This this a first for me.
