Red Blinking Light, Low Over Talbot Trails

Driving home from work, Harper/14 mile area. Red blinking light just SSE, very low, almost over Talbot trails, know there is no tower in that area. As approaching I turn down Talbot for better view, drive down maybe 4 houses and start to make out a defined structure, very small, silent, light color, hovering maybe 100-150 feet above ground level. Then object begins to move north 2-3 streets, suddenly white light appears with red. I turn car around back towards Harper, as I arrive at Harper/Talbot object has moved very quickly and is now NW of Quinn/Harper still red blinking with constant white and has maintained ridiculously low altitude. This was under the curtain of the ANG base's airspace, and I checked aviation charts for new tower notams in that area, there were none.
