Very bright round object seen in the sky in the Sorrento Valley area of S.D.

At about 6:40 pm last night I was laying down outside, listening to my ipod and looking up at the night sky. As I laid there I noticed a VERY bright round light, almost like a planet, moving VERY slowly in the sky. As I watched the object coming closer to me I took out my earphones and noticed that the object made NO sound. As the object flew over me I could see that it was round and was so bright that it looked like it was on fire, white fire. I watched the object for about 4-5 minutes moving slowly coming from the south and going north. I then watched the object shoot across the sky and was gone in less then a second. There is a military airbase, MCAS Miramar, just south of me, so I am used to seeing and hearing planes and helicopters flying over. I also attended the MCAS airshow about a month ago and know that all the aircraft I saw there made a lot of noise when it flew over. This object never made a sound, even though the speed at which it took off should have broken the sound barrier.
