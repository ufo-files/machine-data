Glowing red circular object like hot metal or burning amber.

This object did not have a smoke trail. It was silent and flew NW from Austin right over Butler Park as we were watching fireworks. Other people saw this object and yelled out a UFO. We looked up and were freaked out by what we saw. This object was traveling slow across the sky. About the speed of a 2 seater plane or slower. It was low enough that i could see the bottom of this circular craft changing colors like burning amber. I watched it disappear on the horizon but it reappear for a split second then was gone again, almost as if it had gone through a cloud and out again. But this object did not have any blinking or running lights on it. It definitely was not a plane, hot air balloon, firework, crashing satellite, or a star. This craft seemed to be under some kind of control and it just kept the same speed and elevation and flew in a straight line NW on the 4 of July at 9:35pm central time.
