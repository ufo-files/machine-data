{tjCaught sight of several bright lights from my west facing family room window, bright white lights in a circle. Lost sight for a half second as I made my way around a table to the window, saw object comprised of six or seven light arranged in a circle

Saw a bright group of light from my west facing family room window. I was in my kitchen and facing northwest. I lost sight of it for a second as I moved around the table to the window. I saw it moving southwest above the trees. moved like a copter but the lights flashed on and off in unison in one second intervals. No red running lights or any typical aircraft lighting. Just six or seven very bright, white lights arranged in a circle, flying flat and flashing in one second intervals. In seven seconds it had flown over the trees and disappeared behind them and over the low hill and probably crossed Rt. 57. The trees are bare and I could see it through them. When I first saw it, I estimate the size about half my index finger at arms length. Speed perhaps 50 to 79 MPH. No copter noise, just a rumbling. We have many life flights around here but this object was unlike any of them.
