{rjl} upwards, orange comet like tail in NW disappeared reappeared horizontal dir, no tail few bright lights eastward

A friend called me an hour after the event. In Virginia Beach, VA, approx 755pm they and their son saw a bright object in the NW sky. It was dark but no clouds. The object looked like an orange comet going up. Had a tail like a comet. No sound was ever heard. The whole sighting lasted 4 to 5 minutes. A couple minutes in it disappeared, then reappeared without the tail. There were a few bright lights, maybe a cylinder shaped object. The round lights were very bright. When it reappeared it was going in an Eastward horizontal direction toward the ocean. It was not fast. Moving slowly then it just disappeared and did not reappear. Did anyone else see?
