Cylinder, brown / black, with triangular edges. Silent.

Two large brown/black silent craft flew 20-40 feet above me. Reasonably quickly, a little bigger than cars. It was quite dark but not night time but it was after sunset. They looked like a losenge shape with a triangle on each side (a bit like wings). They were moving perfectly in sync with each other, almost side by side but one was diagonally behind the other. No lights, no sound, very low. Wider than a car overall but the lozenge section was a little thinner than a car and slightly longer than a car.
