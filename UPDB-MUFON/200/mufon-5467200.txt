{td} Triangular with 3 lights

I was walking down the street and I decided to look up at the stars as I always do when I saw a triangular object flying past. It had 3 circular lights that would pulsate. 2 of the lights were a greenish color and the other was white. I was very nervous when seeing it because when I would walk and then look back it would seem to stop midair. I decided to walk faster until it was out of my sight and upon returning home it was nowhere to be found.
