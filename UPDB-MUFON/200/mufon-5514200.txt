{cal} glowing yellowish orange teardrop object headed from east to west

At 5:25 am Nov 15th 2012 I was driving north on 71 hwy. from Harrisonville,, as I passed Peculiar Missouri, I noticed to the east, a bright object, at about 10:00 oclock position ,,,,, no blinking lights, not red or green lights around it,it stayed in a straight line going east to west, I observed it for 25 seconds as it trailed out of sight,,,, I called 94.9 radio station to report, and 9 people also saw it also one was a pilot driving in from Harrisonville 
  The object had no flame as in no debris falling from it, no flickering of flames , but it looked like it was on fire,, a perfect tear drop shape cone head ,,,pointed west as it traveled ,, I watched it as it just flew out of sight in a straight line,, it never gained or dropped from it;s traveling postion,,,,
