Oval Bright yellow glowing orb and with numerous small black flying objects

The skies were clear and sun was just starting to set. I was trying to take a picture of my family in the backyard, but there was a very bright glow in the background which caused difficulty in getting a good picture. Then we noticed a bright glowing yellow oval shape orb/fireball in the sky. It appeared stationary for about 30 seconds but very slowly descended out of our view. But before we lost sight of it, we witnessed there were several smaller dark objects erratically flying around the glowing orb. Most of these we didn’t see with our naked eye due to their speed, but noticed them on the video afterwards. Some of the small dark objects appear to possibly be ejected from from the yellow orb. Near the end of my video, one of the small dark objects appears to directly strike (or maybe enter) the glowing orb which causes the orb to briefly change shape into a more rounded orange sphere that appears blurry. The moment this occurs, it also appears to make my iPhone camera blur briefly. The glowing orb then goes back to its original yellow glowing oval-ish shape and then descends below my view.
