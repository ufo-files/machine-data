{rjl} Saw directly overhead then down the valley(7 mi.) then up valley(7m.)Blink of eye

Out for morning walk at school track. Saw overhead at high altitude.Hovered for several minutes. Glowing cylinder with light beam radiating from sides. While watching we turned our backs to walk 15 feet under shade tree and it was gone. We scanned the sky and saw it down valley about 7mi. It hovered there for couple minutes then came back over us and then down to the other end of the valley where it hovered for several minutes. Other people saw it and said it was nothing ever seen before We took pictures on phone and went home to see if it was on the news. Could not believe no one reported it...the pictures we took need to be expanded to see object in sky.
