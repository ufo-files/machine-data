{rjl} Glowing white and orange orbs traveling slowly then disappearing into nothing

I recently had torn ACL knee surgery on my left knee and have been resting it for the last week. I've been walking a little lately to get my stamina back and to help with muscle atrophy. Tonight I walked out front of my house listed at the address provided.
I noticed a strange orange and white orb East of my location and stopped to look at it. I've seen strange objects flying at night that do not appear to be air craft because there are no flashing strobe lights which air craft typically have. 
So this incident caught my attention as there were no strobe lights. I then noticed the orb fade to black. At that moment I noticed another orb NE of that one that was bright. It followed the same flight path from NE to E over the Superstition mountains and it to disappeared. Then I saw several more orbs NE of that ones location. I went inside and got my phone and started recording. (attached to this report).
I saw at least a total of 8 to 12 of these orbs all following the same flight pattern and disappearing.
I know it wasn't jets as the after burner would not be visible from a side view.
