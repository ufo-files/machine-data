Lights appeared got bright then slowly went out

Standing outside work with two others I notice lights in the sky not a helicopter or airplane, they got brighter then went out, they them appeared to the left maybe 8 miles over then brightened and went out twice, there were at least five of them 3 were in a line and the other two to the left , either the object was huge or they were all separate lights but came on and went off all together, they then disappeared and did not return.
