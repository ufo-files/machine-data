Hovering transparent-like triangle with red-orange flashing lights on each corner.

I was returning from a day-long trip by car with a friend of mine and it was just 20-30 minutes after the sun had set and as i was sitting on the co driver's seat i noticed a red light not far from (judging by the relative distance as i was seeing the lights of a house behind it). After telling my friend to slow down the car so we could see better, he pointed out that he can't cause other people had already parked on that part of the highway (and there is nothing there like a food stop or toilet). After about 100 meters we stopped and we were the closest witness to the lights about 50-100 meters from us. We were still in the car just before a bridge which was 50-70 meters high and this red light was actually 3, which we noticed after getting closer. We could clearly see them hovering at a very close distance from us and we knew it wasn't anything man-made because the closest power line was ON the bridge that we were on and below the "3 lights" was nothing but trees.after about 4-5 minutes one of these 3 lights stared flickering with a standard pace until 1 second before it vanished the second one started flickering too and one second before that one vanishing too, the last one started flickering until it disappeared completely, never to be seen again that night. The time between the starting of the flickering until it vanished completely was about a minute. After returning home and telling my brother he told me that two (2) days ago the exact same sighting took place about 20 kilometers from the place i witnessed it and he showed me a video footage of the previous sighting. After watching it i realized it was EXACTLY what i saw ! Note: the day me and my friend saw it, it was the Christian Easter. Sunday 5/5/2013. The previous and recorded sighting was at 3/5/2013
