{rjl} Bright orange star went out

I was sitting in the car, waiting for husband, and saw a remarkable bright orange star near Jupiter, a little up and to the left. I watched, thinking it was a plane, but it didn't mine, and the color was pumpkin orange, not red. It did seem to shimmer pink for a second. Also, it seemed to move a little, especially when I looked at Jupiter for a point of reference. And then, it quickly faded to black, like it was on a dimmer switch. It had been brighter than Jupiter, and went dark.
