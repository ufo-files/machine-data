Hi I was about 14 years old. (1996) This is an estimated date. I was living in a house that sat back in the woods Lumber Bridge, N.C.
I spent time reading the bible and going outside under the night sky to pray. I remember sitting on tree stump looking up in the sky praying. To the right of the sky I saw a really bright light. At first I thought it to be a star but it was moving in a peculiar way. All of a sudden the light turned in my direction and was coming really fast. I ran as quickly as I could down the dirt driveway in through the front door and to my bedroom. I remember getting under the covers in fear. I looked at my window and there stood an alien (short) I hid under my covers. Somehow I passed out and no recollection of anything else. 
I have kept this to myself for a long time only telling people that I felt close to or felt compelled to share. I am now 37 very spiritual but not religious. I'm a mother and wife. I am here today to share my story.
