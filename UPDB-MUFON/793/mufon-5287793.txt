2 triangular white objects travelling diagonally across the sky, one behind the other. Far away, moving fast. Abruptly turned to the east.

I took the dog out at midnight and saw two perfectly triangular objects in the southern sky, travelling diagonally toward the west, one directly behind the other. They were bright white, and I think far away based on their small size (much larger than a star, though), and since I couldn't see any detail other than their shape and that they were white. They stayed the same distance apart as they sped through the sky. They appeared to be travelling MUCH faster than an aircraft. Then, they abruptly turned to the west. I couldn't see them after that because of trees and terrain. I live half-way up the side of a small mountain.
