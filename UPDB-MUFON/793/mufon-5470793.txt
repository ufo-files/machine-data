White disc observed below aircraft traveling in apposite direction over northern Germany.

I was going on holiday to Rhodes flying from Newcastle UK on Jet2 flight LS 581 we had just been informed by the captain we were at 35000 feet,we had crossed the English channel/north sea about 10-15 minutes previously so we must of been over northern Germany or southern Holland. I was observing another aircraft travelling in the opposite direction to us, I was looking at the ground below and almost directly below our aircraft I observed a white disc with a dark circle in the middle travel in the opposite direction to us. I knew it was not a bird or aircraft as it had no wings or form of propulsion ,it was traveling in a NNW direction it may have been a large balloon not sure which way of wind direction. I lost sight of object when it was obscured by the left-hand side wing,I was sat 2-3 rows in front of wing.
