It's back... with escort

On Tuesday evening, approx. 8:30, usual time: sitting inside the house watching TV. Heard a deep, low frequency rumble. I thought, "that's the UFO again." I've heard this sound before and it's unique. I jumped up and went out on the back patio. The flight path on every previous sighting (six) was West to East directly over my house. Pull up Google map and find Martin airfield West of College Place WA. Draw a line West to East just South of the Airfield. That's the flight path every time. First time the wife and I saw it, reported previously, was at a different house. We moved and within months saw it again. Our new house is on the same East / West line. This time it was being followed, back and to the right by an aircraft. Couldn't tell what kind. Airplane was keeping same relative position to UFO as it proceeded out of sight. Escort?
