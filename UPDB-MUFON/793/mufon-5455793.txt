{rjl} I was going to yard sales and seen cigar craft

At around 3:00pm 7-15-2017. I was driving on panda drive in Fayetteville Tennessee when my daughter said look at that plane. My husband said that is not a plane so I stopped the car, pulled to the side of the road and Watched as it moved across the sky. My husband got out of car watching in awe while getting out phone to take photos still focused on object when all of the sudden it changed direction and elevation then disappeared at light speed. It was a clear blue sky with no clouds except a small one to the west. Object was white/silver cigar shaped approximately 80 ft long. No sound, fumes, seams or engine.
