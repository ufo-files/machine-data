{cak} 45 to 50 high altitude, very fast and evenly spaced objects

a high altitude line of white round objects brighter than stars, appearing one at a time yet then followed by another. At any time during the event approx. 9 objects were visible evenly spaced in line from SW horizon to NE horizon, I counted between 45 and 50 objects in this line before it ended, but there were other objects of same description in other more erratic paths. This was observed by my brother 7 miles from my location and by friends in Kansas 80 miles north of my location, observations confirmed by phone during event. This was also observed yesterday, same location in sky and same path, but it was limited to single or double objects with no strict pacing as observed tonight. Speed appeared to be much faster that of the ISS which I observed a couple of weeks ago.
