Bright light appeared out of nowhere while sun was going down

I was relaxing in my house. I have a large sliding glass door that I look out at the sunset through. I was observing the sunset and how pretty it was then this bright light appeared out of nowhere while the sun was slowly slipping down behind a mountain top. I grabbed the binoculars and a family member grabbed the telescope. The light became brighter and brighter as if coming closer towards our home. We thought perhaps it was just an airplane but it stopped and we got to observe it through the lens of both telescope and binoculars. It was pulsating and moving slowly towards the south. It was not an airplane. It was saucer shaped. Then it descended down and disappeared as if it landed. I'd say it was a few miles out near New Melones lake. It did not look like it dropped behind the mountain in the cattle fields out that way. We felt scared but excited.
