{cak} A silent object moving across the sky from the northwest to the East. Then, it moved back to the northwest then west. Then disappeared.

At 3:30 PM Eastern Daylight Savings time, I was outside taking a photograph of the clear blue sky overhead in Croton, MI, and after I took the photo, I noticed a very small object high in the sky. I took one photograph and I captured the UFO. It was moving East and its coordinates were 43 degrees 2711 00 N. 85 degrees 381h 55 W. It was moving silently to the East, at a very high rate of speed, and to my right. I went into the house to get my binoculars to get a better look at it. When I returned outside less than one minute later, I began looking East to find it again, but it was now in the Northwest. Nothing that I know of, can turn and move that swiftly in a completely different direction. Thinking it still might be a bird, a hawk was also in the sky very high up but certainly NOT at the height of the UFO. It then made a sharp turn North. Then, a jet flying very high in the air flying Southwest from the Northeast had a condensation trail behind it and NOT a chemtrail. I could hear the Jet engines, but ever so slightly. This UFO was still moving North without a sound and then just disappeared. This was the first time I had ever seen such a UFO.After my sighting, I was somewhat upset with myself that I didn't take more pictures, but I was simply curious of what I was witnessing. Then, I went into my house and looked at the one photograph I took and I had captured the image. The image is very fuzzy but I still captured it.
