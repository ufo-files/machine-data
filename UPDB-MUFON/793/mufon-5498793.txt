{nd} I thought was a blue bottle rocket, or shooting star

At 10:40p on10/03/14 I was looking south in the sky, then from the west what look to be a blue bottle rocket appeared.But it was slowly traveling across the sky at an incline. I then thought it was a shooting star because of the trail left behind, but this was inside the earth atmosphere and moving too slow. Once it got to the treeline descent it seemed to explode into 3 separate parts, which looped up and fell towards the earth.
