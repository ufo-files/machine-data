We noticed that it flew in all kinds of crazy directions- something an airplane wouldn't do, and of course, it looked nothing like a plane. And if flew so fast. We couldn't keep up with it. We tailed it the whole ride home, until it eventually it flew into the distance. After all that, I was very amazed and overjoyed at what I had just seen.
