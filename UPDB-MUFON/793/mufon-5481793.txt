{dr}The strangest, scariest, coolest moment of my entire life.

I was riding in the passenger seat of my grandmothers car. My grandmother was driving. My aunt and two cousins were in the back seat. We had driven into town to go to walmart. On our way out of town, my cousin yelled "LOOK IT'S A FLYING CAR!" Everyone in the car started laughing, we told her "it is probably just an airplane" but when we looked out the window our opinion quickly changed. Our jaws dropped. We had no idea what we were witnessing. But it was just hovering over our car. I can't explain how it was hovering to give it justice. I have never seen any plane that moved the way this did. It didn't have wings, had an oval shape, and was much much larger than our small Kia car. It moved so fast and then would suddenly stop and hover. The last glimpse I had of it, was hovering over several houses in a neighborhood, shining a bright red light. The lights were large and round attached to the outside of the craft. It did not have windows. We watched it for about 10 minutes before we had driven far enough that we could no longer see it.
