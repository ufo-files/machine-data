Stationary silver shiny object hovering low in sky, Then It vanished

At 3:40pm 1.5 hrs before sunset) 40ty June, A Shiny silver object viewed low in the south/south west sky between Millthorpe and Blayney NSW. The late afternoon western sun was reflecting off it, which caught my attention. . It was Close enough to see it was not a plane or anything I recognise but far enough not to see any detail. It had NO obvious wings like a plane, it was roundish/ dome shape I think. The sun was reflecting bright which made outline detail harder to see if it had any. It was the size of a small plane or small bus. Maybe larger ? It Hovered in a stationary position in the sky, no sound. As I watched it more intently for more detail, it became apparent to me it was very odd , it then became dull or dimmed slightly (as if the suns reflection suddenly stopped being so bright) - and then instantly vanished/disappeared from sight. Didn’t zoom off it was just gone. 
I don’t understand it, it was there shining bright reflecting the sun then gone.
