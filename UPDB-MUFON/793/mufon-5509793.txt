{dr}Five silent glowing orange orbs moving from SE to NE looked like fireballs

We were watching fireworks over a lake when we saw five silent glowing orange orbs moving slowly from SE to NE over a period of 15-30 minutes. Most were solitary but two were near each other. At first we thought it was an ember from a firework, but it didn't die off. Then we thought it might be a helicopter but it looked like it was on fire and there was no noise. Each orb moved in a straight line; with the exception of the two that were together they were well-spaced out. We lost sight of each orb as it passed the treeline to the NE, then a new one would appear in the SE. We took multiple videos. What are these orbs?
