standing on back porch and a aircraft came over heading northeast

At 1:36Am 30 April 2011 my brother and i walked out on the back porch of our house to smoke a cigarette, when lighting my cigarette i noticed that the birds were not chirping and there was no noise at all, when i turned my head to the right i noticed a sudden change in color on the ground and trees, immediately after seeing this i see movement in my peripheral vision and looked up and saw a aircraft in the sky moving northeast moving over the corner of the house. the air craft is very hard to describe in words, it had 5 circular lights that emitted the orange reddish glow that was in a v shaped pattern but the craft itself was not exactly v shaped. it had a long body that seemed to narrow at the front and expand to the rear and it was a blackish metal that had a slight shine to it . the way it moved it was gliding at a slow pace than slowed a bit more to a near stop than started moving in at a angle but the lights or craft did not turn at all and continued moving. upon seeing it i thought i was seeing things and pulled my brother out from away the house for him to see it. when we both acknowledged it, it was close to being over head by then, i felt i sudden fear Ive never felt in my life come upon me and forced myself into the house. coming in we woke our father up and took him outside but by that time it was gone, minutes after the birds started chirping again. afterwords we both felt very tired and shocked, it was very hard to sleep after that for me.
