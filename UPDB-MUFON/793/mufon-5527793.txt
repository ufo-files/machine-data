Hello again. The object observed this evening was a large bright orb off in the distance. My friend( who will remain annonymous at this time) and I decided to drive toward it. The whole while driving toward this round gold colored object it remained stationary as if observing something until we got up to it. Once we reached the object it began to slowly move away. At this time we noticed that there was a small amount of flashing behind the object. I have a small amount of video footage to put toward the sighting. Once the object went across the road it was no longer an orb. It was a very large multicolored craft with absolutely no sound what so ever. It flashed in a strobe like fashion. The colors seen were red, white, blue, and green. L.e.d like. Very beautiful. I knew exactly what the object was when I went toward it. Considering it was absent of sound. As low to the ground as it was I would expect to hear an awful lot of engine noise or helicopter noise but with this object there was nothing except a wonderful sight to see.
