Large circular object passed over Sunshine Villa parking lot at 11:00 p.m.

[Item moved/CMS/nd] At 11:00 p.m., I was waiting with a co-worker for my husband to pick me up after my shift. I was facing north when my co-worker exclaimed, "What is that?" I turned to look south, toward the ocean, and a huge object was passing overhead, approximately 50 feet above us, moving north. It passed quickly, much faster than any small plane Id ever seen. It had a dull metallic bottom, like aluminum or titanium, and there were lights around the edge. It was silent.

The object disappeared toward the mountains north of Santa Cruz. I was astonished to see the thing and cant figure out what it could be. I told my husband that night and since then, he saw something on TV about USOs. Santa Cruz is on the edge of a deep trench and my husband thinks it may have come from there. The ones on TV matched the description of the thing I saw and he thinks I should tell someone. So, Im telling you. 

My son-in-law apparently saw something very similar when he was only six years old that he said hovered over redwood trees when he and my husband lived on an isolated parcel [item moved/CMS/nd] but he doesnt want to talk about it.
