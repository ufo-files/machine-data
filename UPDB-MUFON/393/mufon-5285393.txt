Witnessed a strange object on the sky while taking photos of the Moon, Jupiter and Saturn.

I was in the backyard with my daughter taking photos of the Moon, Saturn and Jupiter for her astronomy class, when I saw this object in my view. It was moving all over the place very quickly almost bouncing up and down. I was actually getting quite annoyed that it was photo bombing my picture. It wasn't untill I looked at all my photos that I took and zoomed in on them that I realized it was a spherical object. It appears in four of the photos that I took and one of my daughters from her camera. I finally got a photo without it in the picture once it disappeared and attached it for comparison.
