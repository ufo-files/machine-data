Cigar-shaped object reflecting the sun. Crossed path with 2 chemtrails from plane.

1. As me and my son was walking to his school, we both studied the clear blue sky, looking at 2 chemtrails. That we saw was put there, side by side(from west to east and vice versa), by a plane about 20 min earlier. 

2. The chemtrails had started to descend a bit as we continued walking, and as we where looking up due south, we both saw a shiny cigar-shaped object pass by in a southward direction, about 150 ft above the chemtrails.

3. First it looked like another plane, shiny but without wings. Then I realized that it left no contrail, and was totally silent.

4. It looked like a shiny silvery cigarr with no windows, or any other protruding objects. It headed in a straight steady southward direction, about 150 ft above the chemtrails, at about double the speed of the plane, we had seen earlier.

5. Firstly, I asked my son if he could see it too, and he could. I tried to explain to him what it was that I found so strange about it, and if he experienced that too(no wings, no sound, no lights etc.)We just can1t get it out of our minds, and it passed by us almost above our heads.

6. We lost sight of it as it slowly drifted away in the distance. In all we saw it about 45 seconds, before we lost track of it.
