Observed rectangular object in air heading ne

At about 4pm, 7 October, 2018 my wife and I were traveling southbound on I75 near Mesquite, Tx. I looked south east and observed a large rectangle in the sky below the clouds. The object was larger than a piper cub airplane. There were no aircraft in the area. It had a dark spot near the leading edge. It stayed at the same height and orientation. It traveled at a fast airplane speed in a north easterly direction along the axis of the flat plane of the object. I took pictures of the object as we traveled. The sighting lasted for about ten minutes. I am a 31 year Army veteran and have worked security for the past 10 years.
