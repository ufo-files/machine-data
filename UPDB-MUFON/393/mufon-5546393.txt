6 or 7 white objects, moving in controlled manner

I was at the intersection of Flamingo and Hualapai coming out of a store and happened to be looking west. I saw 6 or 7 white objects that I first thought were balloons but then realized they weren't moving like balloons that had been released into the sky. These white objects were not planes, or helicopters as there was no motor/engine noise. They moved in all directions but not as if the wind was blowing them. And it did not look like a formation, yet all the objects stayed within a close distance to each other. It didn't last long before they moved off into the distance and I could not see them any longer. I've never seen anything like this before so I felt the need to report it. Did anyone else see this? I know the time is accurate within a few minutes because I have a receipt that has the time on it from the store I was in a few minutes before.
