Exact shape of object undetermined due to night sky with only lights to be seen.

I was standing outside having a cigarette, looking into the sky as I always do, when I saw an object moving pretty rapidly in the night sky. I was attracted at first due to the 4 solid "sodium coloured" lights, not blinking shaped in a triangle, moving away from me, as I could see the shape that the lights made from the craft become narrower due to the distnace it was creating between myself and it. It was a clear night, cold, and I heard no sounds being emitted from it! I watched it pass through the branches of a tree and decided to change vantage points. Once I cleared the tree from my view, I could not see the craft anymore. The lights were bright enough to follow it through the tree branches, but suddenly turned off, or the craft disappeared altogether. It did not posses any marker lights like in aricraft, it was travelling at a faster rate of speed then a commercial jet liner, and I estimated its altitude at a small craft at 3000, or a large craft (boeing 747 size) at 10,000 feet. Never seen anything like it before, in all my years of aviation enthusiasm!
