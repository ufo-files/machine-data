2 neon green “being” sitting on my neighbors roof

I stepped out onto my balcony off my bedroom which faces my neighbors house and I was taking a picture at night of something I thought looked odd by their house (I don’t believe related) when I looked at the picture after I took it there were green lights coming down from the sky and on the roof there were 2 neon green “unknowns” I guess I should say sitting on the roof. Two of them just sitting there bright neon green. This was late so no lights on streets or windows etc. After that night I didn’t see those 2 things again but when I step out to my balcony the green rays of light shoot down from the sky. Months later in the middle of the night I woke up bc I heard something, so I looked on my ring app which has a video camera at my front door and there were all of these bright lights shooting absolutely everywhere, they would leave a trail that looked like a chain link. So I videoed it, and it has not stopped since every single night. Then last night my daughter and I were looking at them through the app and all of the sudden the whole porch, everything lit up, or glowed brightly a yellowish color and I swear I saw a circular shape to it. When it went away everything went back to how it was with these comet looking shooting things all over my porch.
