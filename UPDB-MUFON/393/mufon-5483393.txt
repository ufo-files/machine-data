{rjl} A Formation Of Small, Bright Spheres

There was a group of about half a dozen small, bright white orbs high above. One seemed a little closer and brighter than the others. They seemed to be either stationary or moving very slowly. The orbs were visible for about 15 to 20 minutes before disappearing completely. I attempted to take a picture with a cell phone, but there was nothing clearly visible in the image. Using my 35 mm film camera, I took several pictures but I will have to wait until I get them developed.
