Going northbound on I-5 object appeared in NW sky.

I was going northbound on I-5 on my way home from my boyfriend's house when at approximately 11:15pm(PST) the object appeared in the sky NW of where I was at the time. I thought it was a shooting star at first, but it had no tail. I've seen shooting stars before during meteor showers and this looked nothing like one. It stayed consistantly bright the whole 2 seconds that I saw it. It was just a single spherical white ball of light that was too fast to be a plane or helicopter, too bright and too big to be a satilite. It appeared out of no where. The sky was clear. It traveled in a straight path down towards the horizon then vanished. I had a strange mixture of fear and excitement overwhelm me as I continued driving. I was curious if anyone else might have reported it so I checked the MUFON live map. The closest sighting was the one in Redding,CA the same night(10-26-10)... 4 spherical white shapes only 45 min before my sighting. I thought this was an interesting coincidence.
