Clearly watched Flying Cigar absorb heat lightning

I went outside on a late summer afternoon to smoke a cigarette, I saw that there was heat lightning so I grabbed a lawn chair and set it up to watch.
I was watching the heat lightning for about 3 minutes. To my east there is a clearing that can see clearly to the clouds, without any way to block the view.
As I'm watching something comes out of the clouds from south to east from inside the cloud to below it where the heat lightning is occurring.
The object, silver and cigar shaped moves from east to west under the clouds and stops. Now the lightning is concentrating on the object. No sounds, as it seems like the shape absorbs lightning for 10-20 seconds.
Now the object moves in a J shape into the cloud and disappears 
There's more lightning happening in a different area of the clouds. 
The shape now descended out of the cloud in the same sort of J pattern to under the lightning aand now the object absorbs more lightning for about 30 seconds. It acends into the clouds and that is the last I saw it .

This happened to me over 10 years ago and I remember the moment clear as day . It made me believe without a doubt there is something else besides us out there
