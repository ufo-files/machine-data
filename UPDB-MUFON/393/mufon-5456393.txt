High speed light trail (two)

When I direct my camera toward the zenith (almost the same direction where I videotaped UFO flash in September), I recorded two light trails though it might have been actually one due to its high speed that my camera could not catch up with. Slow-motion replay is a half speed.
The seacoast is pitch dark with no light. Of course I did not record it through the window.
