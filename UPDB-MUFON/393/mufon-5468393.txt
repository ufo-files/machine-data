{rjl} bright light over long island sound with two smaller lights. casted light appeared to be searching then completely disappeared.

My friend and I just arrived down to the beach in East Lyme, CT. We were about to go fishing for sunrise. At 4:49am we arrived to the waterfront and the first thing we saw was a bright light in the sky over the water(long island sound). It appeared to be slowly moving from west to east with two smaller lights accompanying it. The bright light seemed to be searching as if it were a spotlight from a helicopter in the fog, but there was no fog and definitely no sound. The light anomaly lasted for one minute and twelve seconds and then appeared to get brighter then changed directions towards long island(south) and disappeared leaving a faint trail. My friend and I videotaped the whole event on our phones.
