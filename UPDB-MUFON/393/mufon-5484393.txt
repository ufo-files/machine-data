2 pale orange lights moving fairly slow one following other

Coming back from the store on my bicycle about 11:20pm, noticed a pale orangeish light almost directly above which looked out of place. First thought it was a helicopter but no flashing lights so it wasn't an aircraft of known origin, it was followed by another which looked the same but was moving a little faster, which is when I knew it wasn't normal. Watched them for an estimated 2-3 minutes moving west, then the first one turned north/northwest followed by the second one about 30 seconds later. The lead one just faded out on a seemingly ascending trajectory moving away from me the same for the second. It was very cool... my reaction was I wish I had a camera or smartphone to record what I saw.
