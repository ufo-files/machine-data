{rjl} I saw a bright red light and I ended up waking up in a parking lot near my house hours later.

I was driving down the road around 10-10:30 at night on August 22, 2016. I saw this bright red light in the sky and I just figured it was a plane or something along those lines. The light then began to blink and move around. I started paying much more attention to the light rather than the road. Next thing I know I wake up in my car parked in a parking lot near my house and it's after 12 am. I have no idea what had happened or even how I got there but I felt scared at first. I came home and realized I felt achy and my back really hurt but nothing severe. I don't feel unsafe or harmed but I do feel a little bit off. I know I don't feel off as in I'm not myself, I feel off as in I feel more like myself again. I'm hoping that overtime I will start to remember bits and pieces but as of right not I'm not really sure what happened.
