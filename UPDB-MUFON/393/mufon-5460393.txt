{jpg} Abducted by a bug like creature. Almost praying mantis like except with an oval head.

I was at my mother's house on a Sunday morning. She was getting ready for church and kept asking me if I wanted to go. I declined but I was overwhelmed with feelings that I wanted her out of the house. I just felt she need to leave for some reason. So, I'm laying in bed in the middle bedroom of the house. Then I hear kind of a small boom and a buzzing noise. Now the heating and air unit is right by my window and i just figured the noise was from it. All of a sudden the buzzing sound fills my body. Like an electricity , a very uncomfortable one. I was not paralyzed though. Then I began to se these orbs of light coming through the closed window to the right side of the bed. Where I had heard the boom and buzzing sound. So see these orbs floating to my right and in front of me. They're larger than a softball and transparent. Just like the movie The Predator when he camouflages himself in the jungle. I'm thinking is this real? So I start yelling at the orbs. By that time there are at least 5 or so in room. I'm yelling, "I can fucking see you" over and over. Finally one shows itself at the foot of my bed. It was tall, bug like with an oval head with large black eyes on the side of its head. It also had a long, thin squiggly neck. I think it was wearing a white suit. Almost like a lab coat. That's all I remember except waking up a couple of hours later in a different bedroom with a small burn on my right hand below my middle knuckle.
