It was travelling west to east southeast of Edmonton

While driving south I saw a cone shaped object travelling from west to east. It was silver on top and highly reflective on the bottom. Could have had lights but it was so bright I couldn’t tell. I watched it travel slowly for 2 minutes and then it just disappeared. I estimate it was between 3000 to 5000 feet above the ground. I pulled over thinking it was just a small plane turning and the bottom of the cone was a wing. It never reappeared. I was too close not to have seen it if it had been a aircraft from a different angle. By the time I started to question what I had seen it was gone so no evidence.
