{jpg} Fluid moving light orb

I was taking the trash out in North Tampa and looked up and saw a very fluid moving pulsing light orb. What made it stand out was that a plane was also flying in the area and made it even more noticeable due to it not flying at all like a plane or helicopter. Helicopters are very frequent in the area and this was nothing like it. 

It's motion was very smooth and fluid like and changed from gold/yellowish light to a bright red and then moved straight down in a perfect line almost like it was gliding down a pole, then it went out of my range of sight.
