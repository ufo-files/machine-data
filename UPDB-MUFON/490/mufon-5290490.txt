Large hovering Triangle

12/22/20 3:30 am
 I was headed South on I-65, just outside of Louisville, Kentucky, when I noticed something in the distance. It looked like a plane, I assumed, since I was near an airport. We were going in the same direction so I thought cool maybe it was attempting to land, but I was getting closer to it faster than I should have. That's when I noticed it was stationary, as I passed by, I saw it out of my driver's side window a perfect right triangle with red lights at each corner and a pulsing yellow/orange light in the center and it just stayed there motionless, after I passed by I looked in my mirror to confirm what I saw yet it had disappeared.
