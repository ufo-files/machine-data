Woke for unknown reason, looked out window in front of me...there it was!

At 5:30am today ,I woke for some unknown reason,I was feeling kindof tingly.I didn't hear anything going on ,no dogs,airplanes,etc. all was quiet,so I decided to get up and go to the bathroom ans as I slid out of my bed I looked out the window in front of me and that's when I saw it! A red flickering V shape,the whole V was lit w/red flickering lights,I rubbed my eyes to see more clearly and it was the same thing .It was so close in the neighbors yard that from it's distance was the size of a good 5 inches across,and it was hovering stationary. I went to get my binoculars and glasses,when I got back ,I couldn't find it,then it flickered again a little further over in the neighbors yard,I tried to see it in binocs. but it was too late,or the buildings outside were blocking my view...I couldn't see it anymore.So I stood there a min. or two longer and went back to bed. Maybe next time....
