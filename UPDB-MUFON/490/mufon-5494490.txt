{dr}3 cylinder shape objects dark copper color flying in formation to NW

Leaving my mother's house after dinner, around 7:54 pm on August 8, 2014, while walking to my car parked on the street in Miami Beach, FL, I noticed 3 objects shining with the sunlight coming from the East ( beach). They were right in my sight, and the shining effect got my attention. I didn't know what they were, so I kept looking at them while I got my cell phone and started to record. The objects were coming from the east (Atlantic Ocean) with a constant NW direction; they were shaped like cylinders, like oil barrels or soda cans. With the sun in front they shone no lights or anything; it was just reflection off of the body of the objects. They had no wings or any other part. They made no noise, and they keep the flight formation until I lost sight of them in the distance. Unfortunately I turned my phone sideways while recording and lost sight for a moment when they were right above me (or in the closer part of the sight). They kept flying in the NW direction. I never noticed speed changes, but one of the objects was getting a little behind the others, and they kept going straight until my phone couldn't capture them anymore in the distance.
