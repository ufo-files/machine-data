A star-like object on a clear night which after 20 minutes moved away.

I was just at home watching TV. The object was out of place for two reasons. The first being it was really bright and the second being it was really close. I just thought it was a star at first even though there were no other stars.
It was a little under a mile away and no more than 200ft in the air. After hovering for 20 minutes it changed which was what made me think it was something else. It began to pulsate slightly and moved away slowly. Now as I am writing this watching it it is beginning to change course and head West (though I'm not sure if the direction really is West people around here tend to change things if they don't like them the way they are) the pulsating has slowed. It isn't showing up on my camera. I can see it but all the camera sees is pitch black.
It is similar to the object which followed me and my brother along the road a when I was younger. I've seen the same kinda thing around here a few times, usually hovering out West but that area is empty and this thing is now heading in that general direction.
Quite frankly the thing is terrifying to see. It makes me want to run and hide but where can you hide from a thing hundreds of years more advanced than you. But now it is gone and like last time the relief is unbelievable.
The object has vanished into the (assuming West means West around here) North West.
Like last time is just ascended out of vision.
