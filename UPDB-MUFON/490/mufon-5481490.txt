{cak} There was supposed to be meteors that night; I took some pics when I noticed 3 lights in the sky general area of moon.

It was a night when meteors might be sighted, so I took my phone outside my back door. The moon was just to the south west. It seemed clear out, the moon was very bright. I noticed 3 lights spaced far apart in the sky. I took 5 or 6 photos, because I have seen things in my photos that were not visible to the eye when I took them (previous times). The objects or lights didn't move when I was outside. The reflection from the moon changed in each picture. In one photo there is a very bright orb close to the ground, below the moon. It wasn't visible when I took the photo. This is one of several times I have witnessed something very unusual. It is very puzzling to me. I am open to these kind of experiences. I took the pictures and returned indoors. I only looked at the photos the next morning; that is when I enlarged the small lights and found them to be chevron shaped "craft" in the sky (3). The orb appears only in 1 photo; a complete mystery to me. Interesting to see the order the pictures were taken.
