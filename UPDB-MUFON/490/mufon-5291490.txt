Stationary object, wings but no fuselage, red light blinked once

10/16/2020 6:45 PM, just after sunset but still lots of daylight.
Looking east from north Melissa TX, at the 10:00 position in the sky saw a stationary object with winglike structures but no visible fuselage. Watched for several minutes, a red light came on, blinked once, went off. Object remained stationary at approx 5,000 ft altitude judging by nearby aircraft in an approach lane to DFW. This is a busy aircraft lane, should have been spotted by aircraft incoming NE to SW approach to DFW.
Observed for approx 5 minutes, went in to get others and a camera, but it was gone when I came back outside. Maybe two minutes later - it had vanished...we searched the sky in all directions. Could not have been drone or conventional aircraft, no way it could have gotten out of sight that quick.
