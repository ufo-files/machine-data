Slow pulsating light then occasionally bright white flash, blink of eye changed direction and zig zagged. Too high for a plane.

I was at work in a dark yard in Errol, between Perth and Dundee in Scotland. It was dark outside and noticed the stars. All of a sudden there was a bright white flash in the sky towards the south above the river Tay. I noticed that this was from a small pin of light in the sky which was moving slowly to the south east. I noticed that it would slowly pulsate yellow/white light and then flash a bright blue/white light. Immediately after one of the flashes the object had moved in the sky a considerable distance parallel to its previous line of travel. This happened immediately as there was the flash, then it was pulsating in a different direction. The pulsating and flashing happened at random. The object carried on its course then all of a sudden streaked in a 1Z1 like shape to another area of the sky and carried on in the same direction whilst pulsating and flashing until it was out of sight and obscured by cloud cover. I would estimate that the object was too high to be a conventional aircraft. There was no noise from the object. It1s speed wasn1t terribly fast until it moved its position in the sky and then zig zagged.
