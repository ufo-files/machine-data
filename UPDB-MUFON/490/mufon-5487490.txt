{dr}Blue white orb about 4 to 5 feet off the ground

My wife and I were driving to town traveling west on keck road about 30mph. 
We seen a blue white ball of light streak in front of our car .It appeared on the right side of the road and streaked from north to south about 20 to 25 feet in front of our car. It was about 4 to 5 feet off the ground and vanished on the other side of the road. This is a small two lane country road there were no other cars on the road at the time we only seen this for about 5 seconds or so and it disappeared as quick as it appeared.Not sure what it was maybe ball lighting or an orb.
