Orbs recurrently seen around my house.

Every night for the past several months I have seen 1 to 7 orbs (as I can best describe them) hovering in the sky around my house and exhibiting very bizarre behavior such as emitting "threads" of light and splitting into pieces. They tend to hover in the same place but occasionally move around. They have about the same color as stars and pulsate in brightness. There have been several witnesses to this phenomenon. I made an attempt at filming one of them, although the result was poor because it was taken in the evening and my cellphone camera is of low quality. The "orb" in the video can be seen as the small bright dot which is above the cloud which is above my head. Link here:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n2EBmiLRxlQ&feature=youtu.be
I invite MUFON researchers to my house to witness this amazing phenomenon.
