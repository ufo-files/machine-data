disc ove Lansdowne Merrick subdivision

I am unsure of the exact date/year but i am pretty sure 1974 is correct. i was sitting on the porch with my dad and brother when we spotted the disc over our neighborhood. it was right over our heads as close as 100 ft up. silent with a band of lights around the center flashing red green red almost in segments of the craft. instantly, it zipped to the back of our neighborhood then zipped again towards the south and then in a flash, zipped out of sight. No noise. it would move instantly and stop on a dime. the strange part was when it was over there, there were several cars that had stopped to view the event and people were out of their cars looking. no said anything to each other. They just got in their cars and left. it was like we couldnt speak about it. the next day in the local paper there were many reported sightings. we were on Montavista Dr. I would love for more people to come out and talk about the event.
