Green orb hovering above and within trees

I felt such a strong attraction to going hiking nearby that day that I left work early to get out before the sun set. On the way back from the hike I noticed the way the trees looked against the sunset and wanted to take a picture. I pulled my car over and aimed into the sun and took a few pictures, one after another, hoping one of them would look alright as I couldn’t really see from the brightness of shooting into the sun. I had forgotten all about the pictures I took until I was scrolling through to delete pictures that I no longer needed/wanted, when I came to the photos I almost began deleting them as it looked a lot like lens flare, but after looking closer it looks as though in a few frames the orb is obstructed by the trees. Also I took all the photos within the same one minute time span but the orb changed positions in the last frame and then showed up 3 minutes later when I was taking more panoramic type pictures in a different location.
