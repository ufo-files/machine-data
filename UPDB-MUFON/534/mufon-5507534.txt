{dr}Looked Like a very large piece of the night sky

Me and three of my friends were driving to the mall as we were arriving we saw what we could only describe as a large misshapen piece of the sky moving in a straight path there were about 65 star like lights glowing and twinkling and the majority were very close together then there were about ten in various locations following the same path almost as would be the flight pattern of migrating birds trying to catch up to the flock we observed this for 5-6 min around 8 pm 9/28/2013 we were all very shaken up and dumbfounded as we had never seen anything like that and any guess as to what it could be was illogical we lost sight of the object as it got too far away it continued at the same speed the whole time and remained on a straight course.
