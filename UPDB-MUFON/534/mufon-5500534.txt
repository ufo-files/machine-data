{cak} I was outside at night and saw a very bright light and got my binoculars and watched it all night. It has pulsating red lights on the outside and blue and white lights in the inside or I should say, in the middle.

I have been seeing lights moving the sky for about 3 weeks now, mostly at night, starting around nine oclock. But July 27th, 2014, I noticed a bright light in the distance. I got my binoculars and watched it. It was very bright. Sometimes flashed across the sky. I watched all night until 6:00 AM. I have been watching every night now. Now there are many of them and they are getting closer. The sky is very busy with them. The outside of the object is red and the inside is pulsating blue and white. I noticed tonight they make a humming noise. My feelings at first was that I was excited, but now I feel worried, because they are getting so close and there are so many of them. I have not lost contact with them. They are there every night.
