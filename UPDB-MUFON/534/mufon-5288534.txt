Cluster of reflective or glowing objects that moved across the sky as it appeared and disappeared.

As I was traveling West on Highway 26 near the Cedar Hills exit (as a passenger), I observed a cluster of white objects to the north of me (I couldn’t tell if they were glowing or reflecting light) in a triangle-like formation. 

At first, I thought it was a flock of white birds, but I realized I was wrong when the cluster slowly disappeared and then reappeared slightly to the west of where I originally saw it while staying in the same formation.

I observed the cluster disappear and reappear several times in a span of about one minute. It remained in the same formation and continuously moved from the east to the west until it disappeared and failed to reappear in my line of vision.

I tried to take a video, but by the time I got my phone out, it was already gone.
