I was out for a hike climbed up a trail to 5,500 feet on the mountain. I stopped at the top and proceeded to take a picture of the view. I took the picture and continued hiking for a few more hours. When I took the picture I did not see or hear anything no sound other than the normal wind. I went home had dinner and started looking at the pictures I had taken on the hike. I looked at the one from the top of the mountain and noticed dust on the screen of my phone so I wiped it off. To my disappointment the speck was still there. I then enlarged the picture and boy was i surprised at what I was seeing. I did not shoot video but to my surprise my phone automatically records video right before I took the picture 3 seconds to be exact my phone prompted a video selection so I proceeded to watch this 3 second clip and I could watch it fram by fram and tack screen shots. That when I realized I had captured a remarkable event. The largest object i could see fly through the video at a speed faster than anything i have ever witnessed faster than any military plane in the sky that we see day to day. But I also was able to see 4 to 5 more objects at a higher altitude in a straight line 4 for sure but I believe one more is in the pictures.
