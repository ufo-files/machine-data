Ten days before the Phoenix lights happened, I stepped out of my front door to look at the stars like I always did. As I looked up, I started to notice the stars disappearing in a triangle shape. I heard no noise or saw any lights. I had no depth perception at all! I could not deduct the altitude at all! It was moving in a south/southeasterly direction slowly. As it passed by, I could see the stars re-appear in a straight line behind it. It was huge! When it was directly over me, I could not see any stars to either the east or west! I have not told many people about this. I just wanted to let someone know.
