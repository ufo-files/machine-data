Orb moving in and out of clouds observing our vessel at sea

I am a charter boat captain and was moving a boat from Mexico to Long Beach, CA with 2 additional crew onboard. We all witnessed a glowing orb that followed us on our starboard quarter, partially hidden by a low cloud that moved with it, in the opposite direction of the wind. It moved independently of us so we were certain it was not some kind of anomaly being generated by our vessel or lights. Most of the time it was hidden and the cloud would be glowing, but occasionally we could see the orb through the cloud and after approximately 10-15 minutes it passed us then vanished. We felt like we were being observed or it was curious why we were 10 miles offshore in the middle of the night traveling where vessels are not very common. We agreed never to speak about it again, but now that so much information is coming out in the area off San Diego we feel it's safer to report it without issues.
