Thirteen sphere like hovering objects standing still in a low altitute on Northeast of Patraikos Golf over the sea.

I was in the kitchen having breakfast at that time, getting ready to leave home for work, when my mother noticed some strange lights over the sea and then she told me to get out to the kitchen balcony, where she was, to see it myself.
I tried to find out if there were helicopters in the area but it was clear that we were witnessing something else. Thirteen sphere like objects hovering over the sea in an unusual way. These objects were shining in a white/silver color and made our eyes full of tears as long as we looked at them because we had to focus on them for half an hour during a shiny morning in Greece and this factor made our eyes suffer.
As for my feelings well... this thing is not ordinary here but I felt great because it was a new experience for me... during and after sighting these objects!
