{rjl} was taking a picture of the moon and didnt realize i caught it on camera

On the night/morning of May 19-20 I was outside of my house and was taking a picture of the half moon. I snapped my picture and as soon as I took my phone down I saw the object ascending and disappear. When I looked back at the photo I took I saw that I actually caught the object on camera. When I first saw it with my eyes it was ascending for a few seconds then reached an unknown speed before it completely disappeared. I was astounded at what I witnessed that I posted the photo on Facebook.
