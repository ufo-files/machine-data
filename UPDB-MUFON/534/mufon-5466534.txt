At 5:30pm on Aug 15th, while travelling west on washmill lake drive, the occupant of my car (my son) observed what he initially described as moon-like in the northern sky just above the treeline. The object was spherical and white and was initially hovering, however immediately began spinning/wobbling erratically until it dissolved out of sight without making a sound or leaving a trail. 

The event lasted just over 20 seconds.
