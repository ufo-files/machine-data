(GS) 2 bright orange orbs

My friends and I were sitting outside on July 4th watching the fireworks which were about 350 yards away. Off to the right of the fireworks I saw a bright orange orb or fireball moving steadily from left to right. Directly after that, another one, exactly the same, appeared and followed the first ones exact path so that both were briefly visable at the same time. They looked like two huge basketballs on fire. I would say they were about 70 feet above us. They disappeared over and behind a building in less than 30 seconds. My friend sitting next to me saw the exact same thing. The other two people with us had a somewhat obstructed view and did not see anything.
