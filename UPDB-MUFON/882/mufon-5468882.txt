{jpg} Looked like a dragon kite then morphed into a dark spherical object that hovered stationary then ascended

I work for the Corpus Christi Texas main United States postal service & went outside parking lot for a 6pm break along with two colleagues. Immediately we noticed a long black line that resembled something to the affect of a dragon tail or a long worm like object crossing slowly across the daytime sky. At first we thought it was debris flying in the sky but the object curled up & took on a dark spherical shape that stopped suddenly & remained stationary. This is when I took out my iPhone and began recording. I have seen many objects throughout my life & even daytime ufos so I felt happy to have witnesses to share my experience with. We never lost sight of the object as it just sat there until our half hour break ended and we had to return to work. When I last saw the object it remained high up in the sky stationary just motionless & silent. Not a balloon, not a helicopter, not a drone, or trash flying in the sky. What we saw truly defied the laws of gravity as it morphed shape, slowly silently moved to a part of the daytime sky then sat stationary against the gulf winds we have here. I believe it was a rare daytime ufo sighting. Thank you.
