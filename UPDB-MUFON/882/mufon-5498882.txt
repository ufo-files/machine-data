{cak} Small objects, spinning around and into a larger orb.

Looking to the S.W., with binoculars, about 9 in the eve. I observed several small lights, moving in a counter clock wise direction around a larger round, white object. They appeared to enter this central object, and then spin outwards from it. It appeared to be very far away, and not easily seen without the use of the binoculars. It remained stationary, and I watched it for about 15 min. It was not there when I went out later that night.
