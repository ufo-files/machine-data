sgt of the airfoce observed an unknown flying sacer while on guard duty in 1967

in 1966 i was ordered to go to thailand to support the vietnam war. 0n one ocassion it was my duty to be placed on guard duty for a empty warehouse near our flightline. i was placed on duty without weapons but was given a walkie-talkie for comunication. the guard duty was necessary  due to the arrival of president johnson during sometime of my duty. the guard duty began at dark and ended at day light. the shy was clear and the weather was about 100 degrees and steamy. i began my duty and i was distracted by a long-ciger shaped unidenify flying object with yellow lights coming out of the port holes along side of the craft. the oject made no noise and it hovered about 300 or so obove the ground. as i observed the craft i noticed several gray figures darting arould the interior annd one gray figure had long hair about shoulder length. the craft would adjust there height above the ground every hour, they done this until daylight when it was totally disappeared. my feelings and actions were normal without excietment.....
