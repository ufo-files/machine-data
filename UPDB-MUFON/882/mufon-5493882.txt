{rjl} Unbelievable and an Unforgettable Experience to Witness

My dogs were going crazy barking at back door so I went outside and thats when I noticed what I thought a very bright Star. It started to move closer and then away and then turned red and then back to bright. I was also hearing a very strange sound, between a hum and maybe wind. (I have never heard this sound before) I first grabbed my IPAD to start recording; it was not even close to what I was witnessing so I ran inside and got my digital camera and started recording so I could the best view of this very odd object. After 2 min of recording; I stopped; to be honest because it scared me. About 10 min Later it went slowly north from its location.
