It was about 3am. I was in my bedroom on the second floor looking out the window stargazing. There was a fool moon out and observed the stars for a short time . then walked away for bout 10 minutes or so when i came back to the window i observed a star in the lower sky that i did not see previously it was a little higher the tree top height. It was a luminous white that pulsated red and blue or green i watched this through 4 power binos for a better part of a hour. I went to lay down in bed for bout 10 minutes and i got back up and went to my window and seen this object was moving lower in the treeline and away south west towards philly.
