{jpg} It wasn’t built by humans

At 7 in the morning in winter in Alaska, it’s still totally dark outside. The government doesn’t test secret aircrafts about cities. They do it in isolated areas. This triangle has a light so bright that if it was closer to the ground, it would have been like looking at the sun. It was going 2-3 thousand MPH and no sonic boom. At first I thought it was a asteroid because it wasn’t in focus to my eyes and was a streak of light. I thought I was going to be killed by a asteroid. Then it became in focus and I saw it for what it was. A perfect triangle glowing like the sun on the belly of it. It flew right over me around 10,000 feet high. It was so powerful I think it could fly in space if it wanted to. It was going towards the Anchorage civilian airport. It didn’t seem like it was built by humans. It had the power of a Star Wars/Star Trek ship
