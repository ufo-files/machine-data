Dayton UFO sighting, 1971 or 72

We werecoming out of the library on Germantown Pike by the back door. Looking south Icalled to Penny, do you see that. It was dusk and the sky was clear. About fiftyfeet in the air and about a 1/4 mile away was a hugh cigar shaped object justhovering without making a sound. In fact there were no sounds from the entireneighborhood. I noticed red, blue and green lights which flashed in a pattern. We were very scared. The two girls started walking slowly toward the object asPenny was very curious. Demi warned Penny not to go toward it. The object thenbegan a slow slant type of ascent and then it took off real fast and zoomedaway. Direction of departure was from the south to southwest. We observed italtogether about four minutes. It was a large object about two semi-trucks insize. The two girls sat in their automobile for quite a while and thentelephoned WHIO-TV Channel 7. The television station advised Demi that manysightings were reported to the station that day. Some sightings were mentionedon the television news show that night.
