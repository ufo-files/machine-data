Observed an object following a satellite from W to E abruptly change course 90 degrees to N.

I was star gazing at a family outing West of Marquette, Mi. While looking for satellites, I found one being closely followed by a similar looking object. I have seen satellites in the sky before, but never one that looked like it had another immediately behind it. After watching for about a minute, the tailing object changed directions 90 degrees and took off away from the satellite it was following, roughly N in direction.
