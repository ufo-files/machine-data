{rjl} 4-6 rapidly moving white circular white lights rapidly changing direction

During a thunderstorm to the east of our home in the foothills west of Denver, we observed 4-6 circular lights moving at a rapid speed and randomly changing direction continually over a 20-30 minute period. These lights were well above the lightning and clouds associated with the thunderstorm and light movements did not seem to be associated in any way with the lighting strikes below.
