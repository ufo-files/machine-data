Bear in mind, that at this distance, the object would have to have been very large, possibly a mile or more, since the lights were the distance apart of a golfball held at arms length. 

Far to close for any airplanes to be together, which was what made me realize it was something very abnormal, and not any airplane. 

At their speed, they would have passed over us while we were running. They did not, however, they just seemed to appear where they were over the mountains as I turned around. Then the lights slowly started accelerating away, mainating a perfect "golfballs width at arms length" for about 2 minutes and 45 seconds. Then, all of a sudden, the lights broke their distance, increased speed, and started rapidly descending for about 1 minute over the mounains until they disappeared, as if they had been found and did not want to be seen.

These craft were also flying significatly faster than any of the commercial or passenger planes that ply overhead.
