They sort of look like a tulip flower on its side. They were heading north in a fast path. One of the objects dropped down and behind the other, then flew back up and once again they were flying side by side. They were very bright, but made no sound at all. They proceeded north for approx. 10 seconds (?) Then one disappeared, while the other continued on its forward path. Approx. 2 seconds later, that too disappeared in the northern sky. We were in shock, my husband more so than me, as I had seen this type of object in the past and had reported it. I had told him of what I had seen in the past, but for him to see if for himself, he could not deny what he saw, especially him being a small airplane pilot.
