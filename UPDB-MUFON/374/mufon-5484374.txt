{dr}4 Bright orange red disc shaped lights in v formation

My girlfriend and I were sitting on patio in back yard when we saw 2 then 3 then 4 orange red lights cross the sky. The first 2 were larger then the last 2, They crossed the sky in triangle formation moving together at the same time from the sw heading ne. We have traffic copters fly by all the time these object moved faster across the sky at about the same height with no sound at all. We were able to see them until the vanished towards the northeast, they just faded out. I saw objects before and believe, my girlfriend on the other hand doesn't. She yelled "what are they" several times. This took at least 30 seconds or more and was able to snap 3 pictures with my phone. I was unable to get more then 3 of them at the same time as they spread across the sky to far apart. it was one large object or 4 but they moved together. About 10 to 15 minutes later you could hear several jets going by then 2 military (large and loud) copters going by one heading north one heading south.
