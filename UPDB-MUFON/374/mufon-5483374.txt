Large, red orb seen over residential area

Standing outside when a large red orb was spotted overhead by my daughter. I thought at first it might be a plane. The object then hovered and changed direction, flying in a zig-zag type pattern. It was much too large to have been a light from a plane. It headed south and out of sight.
