{jpg} 1st saw light like star but keepes getting bigger till it stoped next to me!

I was walking from my car toward my house(north to south). I looked toward the west evening sky to see what constellations or stars or planets might be seen since it was a clear night. I saw what first looked like a bright star about 10 degrees above horizon. as i keeped walking the object then looked like a planet! I stopped as it didn't seem right. It keeped coming and getting brighter at a speed faster than the Blackbird! Since it was coming so fast and curving toward the front of my house i ran to the front and in that short time (seconds) it was coming down toward the street. I saw a neighbor walking toward me(going home with groceries) and asked did she see it , it stopped right in front of me. She said she did and commented on how fast it was coming and then stopped in front of us. It was all a shimmering white about 30 ft long and about 15 to 16 feet tall. Then in an instant it seemed to turn around with like a small snout in front i had not seen before and then headed west so fast it was out of sight in about 3-5 seconds!! I couldn't believe it and my neighbor couldn't either! I thought i reported it before but not in such detail. I had seen one previously but it was long ago and only consisted of a light coming from south to north very fast and high in the southern sky , i first thought it was a meteor but then it stopped for a couple of seconds made a left turn and headed east again very fast. In both cases there was no sound and no flashing lights.
