A line of lights 50++

Seen several lights in the sky coming from the northwest heading southeast. It looked like if you seen cars going down the highway at a distance. They all followed eachother. Some changing positions but not direction. At first I thought it was a shooting star. But, I seen no bright start to the event and they didn't trickle off or change brightness as they went across the sky. Like all shooting stars I've seen in my life. As I got parked I called my cousin to get outside and look at them. They got to a certain point in the sky and disappeared. They all disappeared at the exact same point in the sky. I looked hard to see if they were flying into something bit didn't observe anything othernthan the lights. And my cousin got outside too late to see them. No blinking lights and flying way to close to be aircraft in my opinion and what I understand about flight. That's it.
