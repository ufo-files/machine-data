{dr}UFO with Flashing Lights and Erratic Directional Change Spotted in Richboro, PA

Driving with my boyfriend to the Richboro Pub in Richboro, PA, I looked up and saw what I originally thought were two airplanes flying incredibly close to each other. I pointed to them and asked him, "isn't it weird that they'd fly so close to each other?" Upon further observation, we realized that the brighter of the two was stationary. A star maybe? We pulled into the pub's parking lot and watched it. It started to glide to the right, towards the plane, it stopped. It glided to the left, away from the plane, it stopped. A red light appeared on it. At this point it ascended upward. Many lights appeared and began blinking. There were two main lights, with smaller red and white lights flickering around the perimeter of the disc shaped object. It began to glide diagonally across the sky, stopping to hover occasionally. It was clearly oscillating. I took a short video on my iPhone. I never felt a sense of panic, just awe. Shortly after my boyfriend felt as if he was being watched and started to worry that they knew who he was.
