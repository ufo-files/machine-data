Observed this brigh flying object across the Monterrey sky

I was about 12 or 13 years old and was headed to visit my sister in another area of the city. It was around 7 pm and I was standing at the bus stop and it was getting dark when a bright light made me look up. What I saw was a real bright object flying across the sky and illuminating everything around. I followed the path of the object thinking it would crash against the mountains ahead but it went past over the Sierra and I lost sight of it. A couple of guys standing at the bus stop were as astonished as I was and we asked each other If we had seen that. One of them said we would for sure hear about it the day after on the local media. Then I went back home and forgot about seeing my sister. The day after I checked the news and there was only a brief mention of the sighting on tv.
