light moved in one direction for a bit and then shot upwards towards the stars

Saw something unusual near New Germany, Nova Scotia on the 20th of August, 2013. 3 of us were sitting and chatting outside in a garden on a clear, quiet night, shortly after dusk. The area is very rural, lots of empty wilderness. I looked up and saw a very visible light pass overhead and joked "anybody believe in ufos? there's one". It struck me as being a bit strange, due to its fiery colour and its full shape. We all watched it. The light was bright , warm like fire and round, although slightly pointed on the front and back like a short olive pit. Moving slow like a satellite, but lower, similar to the height of a 2 person plane. Suddenly the craft took off, straight up but a bit irregular and faded like a spark into the stars. I think we were in line with the trajectory that it bolted upwards in, so it bounced around slightly as it went up (would have likely looked like a straight line from the side)- reminds me of what sparks look like as they bounce around and dissappear if you look above a campfire (note we weren't having a fire). It wasn't until after it took off that we realized it was in fact some kind of ufo. My friend who is in his mid 40's said he was a skeptic until now. There is no denying what we saw was out of this world!
