Butterfly shape object beige color

On May 29,21 @ approx midnight my wife and I saw fast flying object in the shape of a butterfly heading North to South over our home in Ottawa,ON. No sound could be heard and it was approximately 1000ft TO 1500ft; speed was approximately 500-900km/hr and was in our sights for about 5-7 seconds. The object had a beige color underbelly and we could see a slight wobble as it moved.
