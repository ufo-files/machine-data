{nd} Two strange objects sighted over Boston

I was riding the bus home like I do everyday, staring out the window into the sky. Then, I sighted two objects moving very fast (probably around 750 mph). They both appeared very small, but were probably around 10 feet across. I could only tell that they were there because the sunlight (it was about 3PM) was reflecting off them. After watching them for about two minutes, the both stopped abruptly, then hovered for another minute, until one whizzed off incredibly fast (warping away?). The second one remained for a few seconds longer, before gradually shrinking into nothingness, perhaps it was ascending into space.
