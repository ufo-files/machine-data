{jpg} At a family picnic we saw a object flying back and forth.

It was Summer and we were having a family picnic at my parents.house. All the adults were in the backyard behind the house and we ,the kids, were playing in the front. I remember we heard a humming and we looked up and saw a object that looked like aUFO from a movie. We all got excited and watched it and one of us ran down to get our parents. They came up and we all watched it of a couple minutes till it zigged away. My Mom called the police (Whipane Twp) and they said they had received a number of calls and would get back to us. They called back the next day to say it was a air force testing out of a base in Virginia. It was so clear that even over 50 years I can still see it and have never forgotten it. I don1t know why but I searched the area across the street where it flew all Sunner looking for a piece of it.
