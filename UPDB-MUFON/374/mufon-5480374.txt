{cak} Three white orbs in a triangular configuration flew across the sky at an incredible rate of speed.

I was walking to get in my vehicle at 4:11 AM on 10/9/16. The sky was very clear. I looked up at the constellation Orion. I hesitated a moment because it was so bright. Then three white orbs in a triangle configuration flew overhead. They came from the southeast to northwest in downtown Northville MI It only took about 2 seconds to fly from horizon to horizon and out of sight......No noise. I felt very lucky to see it. Wow!
