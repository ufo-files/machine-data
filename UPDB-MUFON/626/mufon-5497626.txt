ISS Live viewing Experiment

Hi Mufon
I was watching the ISS live feed at 1858 GMT and seen this "Entity" pass right by the ISS. The event lasted approx 45 seconds. I was able to hit "Print screen" and got a picture of the object. The live feed then went down. Its a bit hard to judge the size but best estimates it was Oval shaped 100 meters across, translucent, white,grey in colour, pink/purple/yellow gentle glowing lights. Definitely seemed like a biological entity. On the right side of the oval there was a tentacle facing downwards triple the size of the length of the object (300metres). again very hard to judge size. In the centre of the object there was another tentacle facing downwards, approx 150 meters. 
It was like a jellyfish if that makes any sense. I hope others have seen this as it was live feed from the Earth Viewing Experiment. Please contact me.
