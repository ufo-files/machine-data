8-10 glowing discs, in a v formation

My family and I were at the Sunset Jamaica resort, on our balcony, when my daughter asked what was coming toward our hotel, in the sky. I noticed 8-10 red/orange/purple discs in a v formation, coming over land from over the ocean. There were no other lights in the sky, no antennas nearby, or power lines. The discs slowly moved toward the hotel, the two by two, they went upwards. We saw pairs flying over "Mystic Mountain" a few minutes later, then nothing else.
