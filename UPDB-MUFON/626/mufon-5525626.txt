Orange shpere of light slowely moving south above tree line.

Walking towards the dog's kennel from the greenhouse, crossing our property from west to east 9pm. The object I observed, was heading due west from my northeast turned almost immediately to the south as soon as I saw it. If east was 12:o'clock it made its turn south at the 11:o'clock position, continued south for 2 minutes, disappearing over the hill treeline at the 2:30o'clock position. It's heading was towards Bloomington's west side it was about the height as the crop-dusters cruise by heading to their next job, and twice as large at the same distance. It made no sound, winds were calm. The object was a solid orange light, shereical, the intensity of the light didn't change as it moved across the horizon, and it maintained its altitude.
