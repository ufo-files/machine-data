{rjl} A fleet of seven little M&M shaped ships, white in color with fuzzy outlines.

My family was on the way home from buying graduation clothes in Parkersburg, WV which is about 50 miles from home. We first drove by thinking there was a forest fire. My parents talked about it for a minute and decided to go back for another look. No more than ten minutes passed and when we got back to the spot, many cars had pulled over to look. There were as I said, seven little, white, furry M&M shaped things hovering there and when they realized we were watching them began to perform. Unusual aerodynamic feats unheard of at that time. After about an hour of performing, they got into a stairstep formation and one by one went to a certain place upward and just disappeared as if behind an unseen door. This was my first encounter and from that day on I felt I knew stuff but didn't know exactly what "stuff" was....just the feeling. I kept this feeling from 1958 until around 1989 when I lost 20 minutes of time on a trip home from Orlando to Ormond Beach, where I then lived. During this period of "unknown knowledge', I seemed to have some deep connection to Macchu Picchu and also Red Rock Colorado, neither place have I been in real life, yet I knew about them. I had another experience in Alexandria, VA in 1964 during rush hour in broad daylight. Some entity had stopped traffic and people got out of their cars to watch this thing, which was transparent, but multi-colored, about 3 feet off the ground, around a football field long, zipping in and out of cars and people, went right through both. It was talked about for awhile, but never heard if anything came of it. I have also had the experience of the little colored orbs floating in a room. I feel blessed to have seen these things, especially the first encounter, but people tend to call you crazy rather than try to believe it did happen. Anyway, I know and will always know. Thanks for letting me share. I read Project Blue Book of course and neither of my experiences were listed therein.
