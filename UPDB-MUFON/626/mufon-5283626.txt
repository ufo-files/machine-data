Radio Broadcast on Armed Forces Radio regarding UFO's with officer assigned to Project Blue Book

In early 1967 I was a U. S. Marine in I Corps in Viet Nam and I was listening to an interview on Armed Forces Radio. 
The interviewee claimed to be an officer assigned to Project Blue Book. I do not recall the officer's name, nor the exact date of the broadcast after all these years but this I do clearly recall: The officer stated that UFO's were in fact REAL, and that the government would reveal this sometime in the "next 12 years." 
Obviously 1979, twelve years from the broadcast, has long since come and gone. And no word from the government. 
I have no idea what the significance of "twelve years" would have been.
Some of these details have been lost to memory over the years, but I do recall the gist of the broadcast, the fact that the interviewee claimed to be assigned (or to have been assigned) to Project Blue Book, and that the truth would be revealed in the next twelve years.
Perhaps MUFON has heard of this broadcast or that there are other Viet Nam vets out there who remember hearing this.
