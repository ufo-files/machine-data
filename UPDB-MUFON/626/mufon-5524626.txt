This is a multi-night event- 2 nights in a row, rainbow colored, pulsating lights.

I went outside around 11 PM & noticed what at first I thought was a plane, it had red lights and was definitely noticeable. However, as I continued to look at it, I realized it wasn't moving at all & it also had blue, green & yellow lights, pulsating. The lights were going around in a circle. You could see it with the naked eye, but especially well with binoculars. It stayed in the same spot for at least 45 minutes, then gradually went further west & below the tree tops from my vantage point. Then, I noticed another, with the same pulsating lights north of me. This one was also stationary, but did sometimes move, very gradually west, then back east. It was still there when I went to bed at 3 AM. I also observed a very large triangle formation southwest of this new one- one at the top and two at the bottom making up the bottom of the triangle shape. That all took place on Thursday, 8.25.11. Tonight, I specifically looked to see if they were there & they were, several. There was one that was especially bright, the same pulsating lights. Tonight, however, this object appeared to zip around when viewed through binoculars, almost creating a "ribbon" of rainbow lights, like if you whipped a ribbon around in the air, all kinds of loops, etc. It was there for hours. I also asked my neighbors to look & they had never seen anything like this & agreed it was NOT a plane, a star, a satellite. It didn't move, but it must have, as it seemed to once in a while be farther away. If you didn't know what you were looking for & stare at it w/o binoculars, you could easily dismiss it as a plane or a star. You definitely won't think that when you look through binoculars. I never lost sight of the object. My arm got tired from holding the binoculars. I'm hoping someone can investigate this with far better equipment than I have. I just have a feeling they will be back tomorrow. I'd love someone to bring a high-powered camera to capture evidence of this thing to show experts. This is truly unexplainable and since they did move, cannot be stars. Not to mention stars don't look like this, nor do they change shape & zip around in the same spot (if that makes any sense) forming rainbow light ribbons.
