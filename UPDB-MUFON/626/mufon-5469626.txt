{rjl} Reddish orange orb hovering and pulsating then took off with unbelievable speed

I was going outside to put something in my car around 10:30pm. I looked up at the sky and saw a reddish-orange orb. It was silent and pulsating and hovering and I watched it for about five minutes. It was brighter than the brightest stars in the sky. I was in awe, it was beautiful. Then it took off with incredible, almost impossible, speed. In a split second, it was gone! Moments later, there were 3 jets in the air moving in the same direction, they seemed to be in pursuit. These were not normal airplanes! Within a minute of the jets, there were 2 black helicopters circling low in the area where the orb had been hovering. It was an amazing experience, made more exciting due to the fact that it seemed I wasn't the only one who saw it! I went to get my mom and by the time she came out, there was only one jet still visible. And then she witnessed the helicopters with me.
