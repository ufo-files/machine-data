July 4 just as fireworks started

Myself, (a commercial pilot), my father, (a private pilot), and my 30 yr old (non pilot) son, were watching beginning of fireworks show. Noticed a light near a fixed star. (Could have been venus). The light went abruptly up, then down, then waaaaaay forward at a rate of speed that jet airplanes cannot do and stopped “on a dime”. It changed altitude again, then it flashed. It flashed a second time much brighter, then changed direction and took up a steady pace, southwestbound. As it was travelling away, it appeared to have a red tinge that looked like a glow but not anything like an afterburner. My son and dad and I never looked at the fireworks during any of this, as it had our complete attention.
