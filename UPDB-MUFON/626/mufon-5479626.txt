{cak} red/orange orb

It happened in Haunted Canyon near Miaimi Az during a hiking club camping trip.

It was a hiking club overnight trip to haunted canyon. I woke up which i believe to be between 1-3am i awoke to go to the bathroom. With in 5 steps i saw a orange/red orb looked to be a size of a basketball floating in a pattern of up, down, up down, in a floating, hovering fashion slow moving, then it disappeared. I saw this through branches of a tree that was to my left. Watching this object was hypnotizing like. I tried to make my way towards the object as soon as i did it was gone.
