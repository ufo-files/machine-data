{cak} UFO sitting between two craters on the moon

This was a top or dreidel shaped object positioned between two craters. Photos taken later show that it's no longer there. One crater appears unique among all visible moon craters as it appears to have a black concrete berm around it and it's triangulat shaped. The othe crater appears to have a tower in it. The object in the photos appears to be shaped like a top or dreidel. Observations and photos taken through a Celestron 11 inch telescope using a 12mm Tele Vue eyepiece and a Nikon 1 camera.
