{nd} Craft moving very fast (Mach II) South in a straight line toward the open waters of the Gulf of Mexico.

I was sitting in my back yard on a nice clear evening at 7:00PM when I saw something I've never seen before, i.e. a strange shaped craft moving very fast, fairly high, silently, leaving no vapor trail. At first I was just curious because it was moving out of the normal flight paths for this area, i.e in a direction towards the open waters of the Gulf Of Mexico. Nothing ever goes that way. It passed directly overhead, and the bottom of the craft was colored sort of a dull blue-grey and the bottom shape was kind of like a short fat hourglass shape made by putting two delta wings together at the apex of each. (Sorry for the lack of a better description) As I watched it move away, I saw the outline of the backside of the craft, and it was an oval shape on the bottom part with a more rounded protrusion on top like some sort of a fusalage. I calculated its speed at circa Mach II, but there was no sonic boom or noise of any sort that was apparent. I've never seen a UFO before, but I finally reasoned that this had to have been one. Nothing dramatic happened, it just continued on a straight line until it could not be seen. I had no camera or phone at the time so I couldn't take a picture, and I'm certainly not artisticly inclined but I made a rough (very) sketch of it.
