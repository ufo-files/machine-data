MULTIPLE UNKNOWN OBJECTS

From my position roughly halfway between Chepstow and Gloucester on a hillside overlooking the River Severn, I observed at least 50 lights that followed an ESE to WNW trajectory above the River Severn heading towards Gloucester. It was a clear night with no aircraft in the sky at the time. They made no sound and had no flashing lights. They looked similar to a satellite but much lower, possibly around 2000 feet (our house/observation point is on a aviation crossroads and I often track aircraft direction and height). They were spaced out fairly equidistantly apart from one which caught another up and edged slowly ahead of it. The speed of all other objects was the same and the sighting continued for 12 minutes from my first observation. I stopped counting after 25 but the stream kept on for another 7 minutes. I had a similar sighting at 6.00 a.m. one morning in November 2019 when I counted 17 objects similarly spaced out and travelling in the same direction.
