{cak} Hovering object with red lights over RLDS Temple

I was waiting in the parking lot at the RLDS Temple when I observed an object moving in slowly from the NE, then it stopped and hovered in the same position just NE of the Temple for approximately 20 minutes. It had a reflective silver and black surface and lights in a triangular shape, but when it moved I could see that it had some sort of appendages like wings on the sides. It was difficult to tell if it was a triangle shape or something else, because it was so dark outside. The object appeared to be watching something, them it took off slowly, then sped up quickly after that and moved out of sight behind the tree line and buildings. It was at about 80 - 100' from the ground and appeared to be the size of a volkswagon. There was absolutely no sound, and that is what perplexed me. If it had been a helicopter or even a drone, I would have expected to hear a sound as close as it was. I think I was only 100' or less away from it. There were no other people around at the time.
