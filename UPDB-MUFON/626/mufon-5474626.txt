{dr}Orange glowing object with a circle of cloud around it.

August 1st 2017 clare michigan. Was watching iss pass over frome west to east. When i noticed above me a circular object glowing a pale orange light with a dark center and a perfect cloud surounding it diffusing its appearance. I ran and got my brother to also witness it. It was silent and it hovered and took path to the north till i could no longer see it. I have never seen anything like this in my lifetime. I live in a rural area in michigan and always have a black sky and it was not cloudy that night as i was watching iss and earlier irridium flares. This was an unbelievable sighting off something that my older brother and myself will never forget and are still trying to figure out what we saw. The only events at this time were antares star which does not move across the sky.
