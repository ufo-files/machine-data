Around 9:30 p.m. CDT tonight we witnessed an incredible UFO over Slidell, Louisiana. (We chased it for about 45 minutes) From the light patterns, the UFO appeared to be of a triangle shape. The unknown craft moved at incredible speeds, while flashing many different bright white light patterns. Some of these light patterns appeared to be in the formation of triangles. At times the craft appeared to tilt from side to side, while the bright white lights flickered outside of the craft.

We were able to get quite close to the craft and it was completely silent. (No sounds from the craft) This UFO moved much faster than any plane or helicopter could move! It was too large and too fast to be a drone. When driving back over Lake Pontchartrain towards New Orleans, I observed a couple of white lights headed quickly down towards the water over the lake. USO? We know that some UFO's have been witnessed going down into lakes, oceans, etc. These are called Unidentified submerged objects. Is there possibly an underwater alien base deep in Lake Pontchartrain? Not sure, but what we witnessed last night was some type of highly sophisticated craft, possibly of alien origin.
