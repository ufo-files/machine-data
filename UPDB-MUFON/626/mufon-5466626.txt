Short sighting, at first i thought it was shooting star, but four sphere like objects did not disappear, and they quickly moved in the same direction.

I was watching sky in the middle of commercial break. It is august so i was hoping to see some Perseid shower. suddenly out of nowhere i saw 4 (four) sphere like objects, i really thought i got lucky it was some quadruple shooting stars, but they did not disappear, they were flying at the same speed in the same direction. no sound, no cold/hot wind and very quick. i was looking at it until it just was too far, and simply not visible anymore. i think they we 4 not connected with each other objects. from my perspective they were in a vertical line, with one sphere slightly, just slightly in front.
no photos/videos, too quick and i was just not prepared.
