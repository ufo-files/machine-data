It was almost 4am, I felt like I was having a panic attack so I decided to sit on my back steps facing south. It was quiet then I looked up and saw what appeared to be a large all black triangle. No lights. It hovered at a unknown height (seemed to be tree top) I heard a low hum and the air felt like it got warmer. Then I felt a pressure, like being on a elevator. I was too scared to move, after a few moments it floated upward and shot in a southern direction. The air got cold and the pressure feeling was gone.
