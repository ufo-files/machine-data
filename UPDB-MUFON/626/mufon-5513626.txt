{dr}Was looking at millky way with daughter when we both saw a square shape with lights, then triangle and took off quickly.

We were in our yard in PA. Around 8:45-9:00 PM. My daughter and I were talking about how clear the sky was. She asked me if I could see the Milky Way. I told her no. At the same time we both noticed this square, gray shape coming across the sky. It had dull bluish white lights on the bottom. It was heading from the west toward the east very slowly. We both said "What is that?" Then it turned sideways and appeared trangular with three orange/reddish lights. Then it went quickly and appeared to dissapear entirely.
