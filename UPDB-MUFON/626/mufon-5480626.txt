{rjl} Bright, steady white orb travelling high altitude from treetop to treetop S to N w/no trail

Step out to porch for late night view toward west (about 3 am) and saw a small, bright, steady, white orb travelling high rate of speed and at altitude of approximately 30 -35 thousand feet with viewing from treetop to treetop, S to N, w/no tail. Trajectory was shallow entry toward earth. Did see falling meteorite (from SE to NW) a minute or so before which made me notice as it was clearly different due to direction, dim to bright to dim/dark light pattern. Is not Denver traffic as it traveled at a high rate of speed and traffic usually travels south.
