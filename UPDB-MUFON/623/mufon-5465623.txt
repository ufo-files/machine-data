{dr}Flashing, blinking light. Seemed stationary at first. Then noticed was moving up and down and over very gradually. Plane lights flying in front, above, and around it.

Camping near Lake Superior. Girlfriend noticed a light and asked if it was a planet because it was flickering. Realized it was flickering between white, red, and bluish green. Then realized there were several planes flying in front, above, and near it. Didn1t know if it was just a flight pattern but it seemed odd. In a matter of 20-30 minutes there were at least 7 plane lights that had flown by the light. The light seemed to change location very slowly but it moved up, over (left/east), and down. It also kept getting dim and brighter. We were looking north at the lake we were camping at. Which is right off the coast of Lake Superior.
