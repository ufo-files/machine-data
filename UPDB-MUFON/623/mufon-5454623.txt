{cak} Visual UFO sighting while flying 1963 - 1965

Hello MUFON, 

I'm a retired FAA TRACON/Tower air traffic controller (KJFK & KPIT). I'm also a Commercial Pilot, 50 years Master Pilot Award Recipient, May 2019 - Certificated (current) Flight Instructor for 44 years. 
My story - 1963 - 1965, my father and I were flying in our 1947 11AC Aeronca Scout, N3870E, Southbound from New Castle, PA to Beaver Co. airport, PA (KBVI) flying at appx 2500 MSL when a silver metallic Disk Saucer (hump in the middle) passed approx 300 ft below us - Northwest bound towards Ohio. 

I was sitting in the right front seat (age 11 - 13) born in 1952, and I was the first one to see the craft at our 
1 - 2 o'clock position... appearing to be flying right at us!
I literally yelled Dad, a flying saucer; as we watched it pass under our plane! I have a vivid memory/image, me looking down at its metallic surface as it passed below my side of the plane. I also remember my Dad abruptly raising the left-wing, as we watched it...fly out of sight from our 8 - 9 o'clock position, NW bound towards Ohio. Being young, I was fearful it was going to return and harm us. 
Knowing Aircraft - my Father was a WWII Army Air Corps Veteran, P-47 Thunderbolt Crew Chief and I grew up with airplanes, served 20 yrs combined Army, Air Force Reserves, Rank: Chief E-9 and continued on with my 39-year DoD and FAA ATC/ Supv career. 
What we saw that day - changed our lives and always remained a reassuring conversation between ourselves, my Son's and family members...up to my father's death in 1996. 

This craft was real and to my knowledge, nothing that advanced - ever came into production or was shown to the American public. To this day...I vividly remember the emotional relief, when we landed and taxied back to our tie-down location at Beaver Co. airport, KBVI, airport ID in the 1960's KG01. 
After watching the History Channel and seeing your MUFON Website - I don't feel it's feasible, that my father and I were the only two people that saw this craft on that particular day? 
It was too large and too low, and I'm wondering if your database may have captured a reported correlating incident?
The Aeronca Scout we were flying had a 36 ft wingspan and we estimated this craft to be 50 - 75ft in diameter...we were cruising at 100 mph and we saw it for appx 7 seconds.

Dad's logbook pages, (source showing we flew during this time period) - see attachments below - unfortunately, he did not annotate the day or month it happened. However, I can narrow this research down to Mar through Aug 1963 - 1965 and it happened on a weekend, most likely a Sunday, possibly Sat between the hours of 0900 - 1400, our routine flying days/hours back then. I'm certain my years are very accurate...leaning more toward 1963 & 1964 because we brought the plane home to our basement in the Fall of 1965 for Re-covering its fabric etc.  

I realize this may be a shot in the dark...however living with this for so many years - I felt it necessary to finally make it a documented incident and I'm hoping it was seen by others and possibly documented in your database?
