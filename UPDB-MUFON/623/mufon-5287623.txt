We saw this outside for almost 20 minutes. My friend and I got the chills, obviously we were scared and there are some swear words in the videos. We tried the best we could to get video. My phone had a better camera than hers, so I used mine. It was different colors. White, yellow, orange, and red. We saw it pause shortly, it moved vertically, then continued. It made a path like that and also changed speeds. We do see planes sometimes, but we know planes have red and green flashing lights and they flash at evenly spaced time intervals. They also have sound, this had absolutely no sound. This was not a plane. It was below the clouds, it looked circular or disc shaped. It was just so different than anything we have ever seen. Maybe some video enhancing will help see this better.
