UFO hovering between 2 Mountains in the middle of the desert highway

Driving down the AZ highway late at night there was a UFO between 2 peak mountains down a dark highway between them. As we drove closer it flowed towards us and out from between the mountains. It then flowed w/ no sound and diff colored lights slowly moving side to side and heading sideways towards the dark desert to the right of us. It stopped then the lights looked to have swooped towards the ground and it was gone.
