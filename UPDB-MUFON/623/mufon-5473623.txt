{rjl} Saw a glowing, pulsating orb that hovered, ascended, and rapidly changed direction

Driving in a parking lot with my wife and son. Just left my daughter's and was pulling away in the parking lot. I turned a corner and I first saw a very low flying helicopter. The helicopter was flying in a Southeasterly direction. I then saw a glowing light or orb hovering over the parking lot in front of us, just to the west of the helicopter. I stopped the car and the orb starting flying in a Northwesterly direction. It would stop, hover, pulsate, and change direction abruptly. We observed it for close to 5 minutes and it disappeared in the sky.

At first, I thought the helicopter shot off a flare. The only thing is, the orb was hovering when I first observed it, and I did not physically act like a flare. We lost track of the helicopter right away.
