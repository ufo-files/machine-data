Very bright star like object

During work this evening we had a power outage.On going outside to see if the rest of the street was affected I noticed at first what I thought to be a very bright star. I continued to observe and noticed it was moving very fast, I called my college and he too witnessed the event. It came from the SE and traveled to the SW, No noise was heard,no navigation lights seen. What impressed us the most was the intensity of the light, It was too low and too bright to be a satellite it baffled us both. the light continued to the SW and seemed to turn slowly towards the south. It was seen clearly through the rain clouds we have this evening before disappearing from view. To add I tracked the ISS ,it was low in the azimuth tonight and not visible, I also tracked other satellites with my phone app and observed their hardly visible glow, nothing compared to this thing. A bright pure white. Amazing!
