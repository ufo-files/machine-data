{rjl} GUARDING PARKING STRUCTURE WHILE POSTED ON TOP FLOOR

I WAS GUARDING THE TOP FLOOR OF A PARKING STRUCTURE AS A NIGHT SECURITY OFFICER AT ABOUT PASS 0300 AND I WAS INSIDE MY PATROL CAR WITH THE ENGINE IDLING LISTENING TO THE RADIO. THE RADIO SIGNAL SUDDENLY BECAME DISRUPTED FOR NO APPARENT REASON AND I CAUGHT IN THE CORNER OF MY EYE A SLOW MOVING GLOWING OBJECT THAT APPEARED AS A DISC SHAPE. IT APPEARED OUT OF NOWHERE AND WAS SLOWING GLIDING TOWARDS N.E. AND I WAS KIND OF FIXATED TO IT, STARING AT IT FOR A GOOD APPROXIMATELY 5 MINUETS BEFORE IT DISAPPEARED IN THIN AIR, IT DID NOT SHOT OFF THE SKY, IT JUST SLOWLY DISAPPEARED IN THIN AIR. AFTER IT WAS GONE THE RADIO SIGNAL CAME BACK TO NORMAL AND THE TIME IN THE CAR CHANGED TO 0400, I CHECKED THE TIME ON MY WRISTWATCH AND MY MOBILE PHONE AND THEY ARE ALL SHOWING THE SAME TIME. I KNOW AND I FELT THAT I WAS ONLY WATCHING THE OBJECT FOR LESS THAN 10 MINUETS, BUT SOMEHOW AN HOUR HAD PASSED.
