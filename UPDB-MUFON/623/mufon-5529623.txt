Falling object which appeared as a plane and then more of a fireball as it neared earth.

I saw it out my dining room window from my house and noticed it by chance.I then got in my truck and drove down river road to see if I could get a better look. At first it appeared like it was cylindrical and had chem trails that a jet would have.As it moved closer to the earth the object turned into more of a ball of fire.When I was further down River Road towards Knowles Atomic Power Lab a jet took off from Schenectady Air Force base and headed towards it. After that I lost sight of it behind the tree line.I was surprised to know hear or see anything in the news or paper because this thing was big.
