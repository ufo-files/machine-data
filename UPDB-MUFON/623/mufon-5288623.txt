Dark triangle ufo with 3 white circle lights at each point of triangle

Driving up sport hill road around 7:45 on Friday night (4/9). Sky was a perfectly lit blue/gray evening shade where I could clearly see an airplane and it’s blinking green/red lights. I saw underneath what at first appeared to be a star. But realized it couldn’t be bc it was below clouds. As I got closer this hovering object began to move very slowly as I followed it down my street. During this time the object became CRYSTAL CLEAR. It was a charcoal colored triangle shaped craft with 3 large circular white lights at each point of the triangle. The craft went from hovering beneath an airplane to slowly moving in a snake-like pattern and as I began to pull out my phone to try to capture it, it increased at an unbelievable speed and vanished in what I would describe as a gray streak. I have never been so mind blown in my entire life..
