white cigar shape object that was higher than airplanes in the vicinity.

was going 35w just outside of denton and before the texas motor speedway on my way to weatherford. I work out near dfw airport and see how high the airplanes are normally. This object was flying much higher than any airplane i had seen and was not going near as fast as they go. The object was a white, cigar shape object that appeared to hover, move slightly and then i blinked it was gone. looked up again a few seconds later and it was there again and then gone for good. It was a bright clear day, hardly any clouds. I was getting near alliance airport but this thing was flying way too high to be one of the airplanes i have previously seen either near alliance or dfw or love field. definitely strange enough i noticed it. 

I just remeber being observant enough to call my mother and tell her what i was seeing. I didn't have any of my field equipment with me and my phone camera was not any good to capture it. I lost sight of the object while driving. I literally blinked and it was gone. 

I noticed the object when i happend to look up for a split second. I thought at first it was a plane and then realized it was too high up to be a plane.
