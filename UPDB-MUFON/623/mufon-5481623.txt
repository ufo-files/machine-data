3 stars started glowing brighter and then moved in formation until too dim to see anymore.

I was sitting on my back deck stargazing after work. I was looking around trying to see shooting stars and when i looked back around, one of the stars was glowing really bright. It was very noticeably brighter than any other star around. I thought WHAT the f**k is that? It then started moving slowly upwards to the left at a 45 degree angle. It was then that I noticed 2 more stars that were normal star brightness moving off with it in a loose and wide triangular formation. It was not a tight grouping. They travelled together for what seemed like up to a minute until they got to to be so dim I could not see them anymore. I thought maybe my vision was playing tricks on me but it couldn't have been that. My vision is perfect and it had been long enough that my eyes had fully adjsuted to the dark. Also, I grew up in the countryside and so I've seen my fair share of satellites, meteor showers and planes but this tripped me the f**k out. It reminded me of the time my best friend Matthew had told me about one time him and a couple buddies had seen strange lights in the sky but in their case the lights had made abrupt movements. Still, I messaged him right away as I was in awe at what I had just seen. My sighting took place less than one hour ago. I would have attempted to take a pic with my S7 but I know the resolution wasn't good enough and looking at the home screen would ruin my nigh vision so I decided not to attempt a capture.
