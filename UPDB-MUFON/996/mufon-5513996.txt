{nd} Multi lighted craft came from the south and headed west toward Monroeville from Murrysville

I was star gazing/moon gazing/ufo hunting when at approximately 9:20 PM an unknown craft type came from the south (across rt 22) banked left over my property and proceeded west toward monroeville (from murrysville) rapidly until out of sight.
The object had none of the normal faa lights but had many other lights round about it pulsating and blinking randomly.
The craft was completely silent.
I have video of the object.
