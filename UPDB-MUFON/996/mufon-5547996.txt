Red light on power line, 24 hrs later, alien encounter at air port

Labor Day weekend 1976, as I was walking up Coast Highway in Ventura County. early a.m. I saw a red light several times on a power line between two poles directly opposite of me. The light was exactly like Stephan Spielberg first movie (Firelight). Also close encounters. The next night I was taken by police to Venture county airport, were I was taken to a Quonset Hut, where I may have seen an alien like in close encounters. I leaned to my right on a gurney and said "I love you" to the alien, but I was unable to make any sound. 
  I was then taken to a State hospital for six weeks, and was told I couldn1t work for two years. Two months later I got a job as a Special Service Police Officer in San Francisco. In the spring of 1978 as I was going to sleep I had a strong shock in my forehead. I quit the Police Force because I had flash backs. There is much more to this story, if you are interested please contact me by phone or letter, I am a former State Section Director for Del Norte County California.

Thank You,
Frank E Johnstone
