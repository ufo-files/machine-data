{rjl} Ball park type multi lighted, 4 or 5 white, greenish lights and a burgundy colored one

At night around 9 pm about 3 miles west of jonesville la as I was was driving west on hyw 84 it was as if a ball park multi lighted object appeared to me from the south about 50 ft over the tree tops then immediately moved medium speed toward the ground behind the trees and disappeared. I stopped on right side of road and listened thinking maybe a plane crash but heard nothing. The lights were brilliant but shed no light at all. Night was very dark. I then proceeded west for about 1/2 mi. And saw another light at a 45 degree angle to the south. It was a brilliant burgundy like color with a perfect stream of smoke shooting out its top. It. Was moving slowly eastward and about 45 degrees downward. It continued this flight for about 300 ft then split in to two parts. One part continued slowly and methodically in the same direction as before breaking up and the other headed straight down in a slow methodical fashion. Slower than gravity would have caused it to fall. The two objects continued in these two directional fashions until they disappeared never seeming to have hit the ground. Next day I returned to the area in daylight but could see no signs of any thing crashing in either place. Two police officers saw something in the same direction that night but from 5 or so miles from me and at a different angle. They described it as being cigar shaped. They radioed around inquiring about a plane crash but none were found.  Thanks.  JSF
