It was very early 3 am, I for no reason woke up and went out side
into the driveway of my house. It was a still morning , there was
what is known as coastal eddy at the time. A thick layer of fog or
cloud cover with a ceiling of about 500 feet. there was another fog
that could be seen off in the distance with a visibility of about a
mile and a half. It was approximately 7 min. after 3am, digital
alarm clock read 3:03 went I got up and went outside.

I was standing in my driveway when I noticed a red glow above and to the
right, I then saw the craft emerge from the fog layer above. as it emerged
I could clearly see a spheroid object, essentially a cube with the
corners shaved off, I have sent drawing of it to the directors email.

Out from each side, top, bottom, right left front back, of course I
couldnt the far side, but I could make out that there was a flame on
that side too.

The nature of the flames(plasma jets) were unlike that of rockets, they
were thicker as in more dense it whipped around slowly but was not 
exausting as our jets do, pushing out thrust, these jets as I say whipped
around slowly like a cats tail except for the front jet that was
being pushed by the head wind as it moved through the air.

The local news reported several sightings during that time, it was reported
to be a natural phenomenon and not a ufo
