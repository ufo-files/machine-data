Delta lights

The first the light appeared together in formation while remaining still. After a minute or so the fourth light seen above faded in and went off on its own. The other three followed while remaining in triangular formation. Based on the field of view you can tell the grouping was either three separate entities locked in formation or one massive object. All headed north towards Niagara Falls until my line of site was blocked. In the photo we are at beaver island state park viewing the river at night. We couldn’t believe our eyes
