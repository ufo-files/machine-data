I saw two lights that changed colors and broke the laws of physics.

I saw what I thought was a really bright large satellite, but then it started to make really strange movements. Moving in ways that nothing should, then it darted off into the night sky and away. Then a second one showed up and danced around a satellite that was flying by, then darted off same as the first. The lights were never the same changing in an indistinguishable hue. Really threw me off. I’d like to say I’m a rational person but that went beyond what I understood.
