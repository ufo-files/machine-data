On January 21, 2019 at 1747 hours I was at home seated watching television along with my wife. Looking out a window to the south I noticed a very bright stationary light motionless in the evening sky. The light wasn't moving so I grabbed my cellphone and placed it against the window and recorded what I thought was only one object. At first during the 1 minute 38 second video two objects can be seen then a small white orb darts around in circles and zips to the ground and back upward. The two brighter round lights begin moving slowly in a northern direction. The smaller light of the two moves closer and farther away or back and forth from the brighter larger light. The smaller light moves along with the larger light and the eve of the roof blocked them from view. I went to the other side of the house and both lights were no longer visible.
  I made some additional observations which were as follows, the weather had high clouds and I had clear visibility of the objects. The area is wooded heavy to light. The objects didn't have any flashing or colored lights like an aircraft would have. I never heard any sound of an engine the objects were silent no sound. I'll provide a normal photo looking out the window during the day in an email to Tom Bowden.
  On January 19, 2019 at approximately the same time around 1800 hours I had observed a bright light out the window which hovered motionless in the sky. I looked away toward my wife telling her to look out of the window and in an instant I looked back at the light and it was gone. After that I kept my cellphone ready in case the light came back which it did.
