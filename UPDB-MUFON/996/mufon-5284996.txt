This was a huge reddish orange orb that glowed bright red and lit up the whole nucleus site that I was guarding

I was performing guard duty on the nucleus site in Germany in 1983 when I came outside to go on guard duty I noticed it the bunkers were lit up bright red when myself and another soldier that I choose not to name walk down to the end of the building and made a left turn it was sitting right there in the tree line on the ground when it noticed us it lifted up about a hundred feet and it took off out into space in seconds at an incredible rate of speed
