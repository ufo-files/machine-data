She took a picture of sheeps with her cell phone, on the picture we can see UFO.

Madeleine was driving on the Dunham Road (No 213) closed to Frelighsburg (QC) June 13th 2015. On the left side of the road, she saw sheeps in a field and decided to take a picture of them. She stopped the car, get out and crossed the road to get closed of the animals. She took a picture with her cell phone. When she zoomed at the picture, she saw on the right side an object in the air she couldn't identified. A farm was closed to the field, no electrical pole or communication tower around the place.
