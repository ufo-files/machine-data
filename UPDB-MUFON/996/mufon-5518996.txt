2 pulsating lights treetop level

On the night of May 5th between 10:00pm and 10:30 pm. I was in my back yard looking to the south west sky. I was looking for a strange light I saw on may 3 and I had my cam corder on me.I was looking outside for about 10 to 15 minutes when something from the south caught my eye.2 bright redish orange lights about treetop level started dissapearing and reappearing.I started recording the objects as they hovered and decended very slowly and dissapeared. I turned my camera off and figured that was it. All of the sudden one reappeared.I turned my camera back on and the object moved a bit west then vanished. The area that I recorded these objects was the same area as the ones I recorded in 6-20-11 and 6-16-08. I have seen these things over here quite a few times. The video I am attaching to this is in two parts because its from when I started recording and they dissapeared and I thought they were done, then they reappeared and I turned my camera on and started recording again.
