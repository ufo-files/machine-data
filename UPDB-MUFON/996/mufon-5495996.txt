{cak} Disc shaped object with green lights around edge

I witnessed the object on the way home from the supermarket at exactly 8:13. I first saw it from the north-west and it was headed toward the south-east. I pulled into my driveway a minute later and got out of my car to get a better look at it. It moved slowly and in a straight line while slowly ascending. It was shaped like a disc and had approximately 8 green lights around the edge of it. It was dark in color and metallic. There were about 10-12 square holes on the underside of the object. I called my boyfriend to tell him to run outside to see it, but by the time he came outside the object was gone. I was able to see the object for a little over a minute before it went over the horizon. The object left no trail and made no noise.
