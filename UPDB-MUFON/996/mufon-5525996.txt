Saw a noiseless white light fly over Mount Vernon

I work onsite all weekend as I am the weekend manager at a retirment community. I went out to our courtyard to have a cigarette, around 12:30am,and I noticed a large white light that was round and intense. At first I thought it was a helicopter with a spot light, as it appeared to drift a little side to side and seemed to slow down or speed up on its trajectory. As the light was overhead, I could hear no noise and I expected the light to dim or go out as it passed over, but it did not. I could not make out any fuselage or such, only a bright round white light. It was just as bright going as it was coming. There were no strobe or colored lights blinking. The intensity of the light was, as I said before, was great. I watch it as long as I could, before the treeline obscured my view.

It has left me stumped with what I saw, so I decided to report it to your site to see if anyone else may have seen the same thing that I did.

This is not the first time I have seen strange objects at night in the Skagit valley. I have had three prior wierd sightings, with corroborating witnesses, however this sighting I was alone.
