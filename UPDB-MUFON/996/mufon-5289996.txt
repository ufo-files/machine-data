UFO metallic in Don Mills Ontario

On January 9th, 2021, in Don Mills, Ontario, around 1:22 am, an unidentified flying object was moving back and forth for about half an hour. This object had a blinking red light, but the phone camera converted the colour to white, and when looking at the video in slow motion or by frame, you will see a metallic disc shape that flipped from flat to sideways then back to flat. There are two videos, one going south and one going north, but since my phone memory was running low, I could not continue filming. The video has a blinking red light near the bottom, but that light remains constant to this day. At 1:22 am, there would be no reason why an object would be flying in that area.
