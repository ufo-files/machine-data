Blinking diamond

Was a strange light, it would randomly start to blink, then stop. It would then turn a deep red that was almost barely visible and it didn’t move. I attempted to see it with a telescope but it appeared that I could see right through it. All I could see was that it was a tall diamond shaped object that was hazy.
