Saucer or Bell shaped with lights on bottom rotating in a circular motion - had bright light facing ground until it seen us.

Sitting in backyard around dusk this object with a bright light came toward us, once we saw it we stood up perplexed as to what it was. It seemed to be about 500 feet or so in the air. I believe it noticed us and as soon as we stood up an took notice the bright light went off. The object made no sound and had pulsating lights on the bottom in a circular fashion. It made absolutely no sound at all, it was not a balloon or plane or anything like the such. It flew in a fairly direct line heading North over our heads and the farm we were at. We moved toward it as it was moving and it flew right over our heads , we walked heading north and followed it. It headed out over the highway and then we lost site of it. It looked as though it was heading out over the lake towards Michigan. There were three of us there and each one of us saw it. I would have never believed in UFO's until I saw this thing.
