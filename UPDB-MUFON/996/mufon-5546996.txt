Dark Triangle with round lights

The witness description of the UFO event:

After I went to bed, I looked out the window and saw a dark triangle shaped object which was almost stationary. It had round lights at each corner which were alternating between red and white. I did not get a good look as I was viewing it through some trees. After about four minutes it left.
