{rjl} UFO with purple lights over Salem airport

About 9:30pm late September of 2017 myself and another person witnessed a UFO move in a constant straight line from South to North overtop the horizon from our driveway facing East. The UFO had a similar shape to the Millenium Falcon without the hyperdrive on the back, instead it had a constant purple beam like from an engine coming from it. It also had 4 purple lights on the bottom that reminded me of the electronic Simon Says game.
