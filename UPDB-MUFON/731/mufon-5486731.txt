{nd} round object with domed top, silver or chrome color, flames coming out

I was driving on a gravel road when up ahead of me I saw what looked like 2 bright streaks in the sky. The trail didn't continue to lengthen, like a jet's trail. The size stayed the same. It was all moving upward, but almost imperceptibly. As I drove closer, I got out a pair of binoculars. I saw a round object, with a domed top. It has what looked like flames coming out of it. As it neared the sun, I saw a glint on the top of it. It seemed to be a silver or chrome sort of color. It traveled upward the whole time I watched it. I stopped tracking it as it got too close to the sun for me to observe it. I traveled on, and when I looked backed around a minute and half later, I didn't see it anymore.
