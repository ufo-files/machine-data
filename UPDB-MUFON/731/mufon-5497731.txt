{rjl} looked exactly like a star but moved in verticle spurts then vertically plus horisonally toward the North

Woke up at 5:15 am saw what appeared to be a star. It was directly below Orion in the sky. It began making short vertical movements toward the ground and on occasion would go back up a bit. It paused between these spurts. It was very high. It would have been mistaken for a planet if not for the movement. As it approached the treeline while still dropping, it began to take a more horizontal path and drifted towards the north. This is were it was when starting to get upstructed by the trees. It began heading north (still moving quite slowly) only after it had dropped half way below Orion and the tree line. I knew it was a ufo. I had seen a much more impressive one before. I got tired of watching it and would have had to go outdoors to watch it further so I went back to sleep.
