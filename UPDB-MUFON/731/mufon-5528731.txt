saw multi-colored object w/3 white lights in triangle & 1 red ctr. light blinking

on Saturday evening, october 23rd, 2010 I, *(Wit's name deleted--CMS/sg), was heading west in a vehicle on Center St. just east of 69th St. when I noticed a multi-colored object approximately a half of a mile south of Center St. at an altitude of approximately 500 feet. The object was heading east/southeastly towards Offutt Air Force Base in Bellevue, Ne which is a suburb of Omaha, Ne. The object was only multi-colored until I noticed the underside of the object had three(3) bright white lights in a triangular formation with a center red blinking or strobing light. That's when I recognized the object as a typical "flying triangle." Then I noticed the "front leading white light" dimmed out. I wasn't able to continue to observe the object, because I had to keep my eyes on the road. The object didn't appear to be like anything I had seen before due to the array of colored lights. I liken it to a Christmas tree on its side or a Christmas reef "floating through the air." It did not make a sound. I made sure I did not have a problem hearing it, because I turned off the radio and rolled down the window in the vehicle. It appeared to be just "gliding" along. The object didn't produce any sound that I could detect even though it was just approximately a half mile away at approximately 500 feet in altitude. I have seen multi-colored flying objects before hand and since then, but that was the only occasion that I was able to identify the object as a known/classified U.A.P. due to the fact of the "flying triangle" signature.
