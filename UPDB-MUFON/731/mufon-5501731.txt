{rjl} Blue light changing direction and speed.

Light observed overhead moving very fast heading west. Starts making zig zag maneuvers. Suddenly changes altitude of 20,000 feet or so. Hovers above building going up and down and moving back and forth at extremely fast speeds. Disappears below building and reappears with a red light on the left side of the craft. Goes above and below building about 3 times appearing to become invisible and performing extremely maneuverable maneuvers. Another aircraft comes into view and then changes course toward other object and disappears. Blue object continues to make extreme maneuvers then disappears toward the building for about 5 minutes. Then, the object shoots toward the sky at unbelievable speeds, faster than I could blink, it was out of sight.
