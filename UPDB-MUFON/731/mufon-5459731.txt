{rjl} Giant UFO witnessed

When I was 4 or 5 years old I was setting in my house suddenly my father who was setting outside started yelling for my mom to come outside. When my mom stepped outside she started freaking out and yelling. I walked outside to see what was happening and noticed my parents and neighbors staring at the sky and pointing. I looked up and saw a giant flying saucer. It was so big and looked so close to the roof of our house. It was silver and had lights all over it. It was so close I remember being able to see rivets in the metal. My mom started yelling at me to get in the house and rushed us in. The UFO was moving very slowly and didn't make any noise. I remember being terrified by its size. It made a shadow over a large area. We were in Bono Arkansas and I remember my mom saying people all the way in Corning Arkansas could see it. I also remember my mom being very upset when the local news came on and dismissed it as being a weather balloon. I have researched this as I have gotten older and can't find anything about it. I know there was numerous witnesses. Every since I have been obsessed with Ufo's and haven't seen any since.
