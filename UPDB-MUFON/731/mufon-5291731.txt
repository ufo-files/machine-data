We were visiting Mariposa park, Yosemite and were looking up at the Giant Sequoias and noticed a silver sphere in the sky way above the trees, it wasn't moving and it was silver and shiny. 
We watched it for about 10 minutes, I took several photos but didn't have a zoom lens at the time. Wish I had! These photos are zoomed in from my computer and couldn't believe the shapes it was making. I've sent them to a couple of friends in the past by email and my emails have weirdly disappeared. No one has come up with an answer yet as to what it was.  Here's hoping you can help. If you need the originals (not zoomed in) I'll try and find them. Thanks
