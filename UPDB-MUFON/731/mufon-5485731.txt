Round sphere where other little spheres was

Me and my Boyfriend was standing on Ernst Reuter Platz watching the many fireworks on new years eve. When I saw the round objects which was glowing in no color like all the other fireworks, it was also much to big and higher then all the other fireworks. It was hovering with no movement, I thought first of a flare, which is not common to sold in germany as fireworks, but some yacht equipment shops sold them, and sometimes are seen at NYE, but the color and the size was much bigger as I said. Then little orbs came out of the object on a line moving downwards like falling leaves, when they disapeared the next little sphere came out of the big Sphere, again and again the same until the big sphere disapeared fast upwards in the clouds. We were both puzzled, also other people standing around saw the same, and couldnt explain what they saw. I know many will think we saw fireworks, but it was DEFENITELY no firework.
