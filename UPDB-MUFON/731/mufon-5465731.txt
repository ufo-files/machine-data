Rotating cylinder hovered for over 35 min with one direction change.

While swimming in our pool in the backyard at 4:30 pm on Sept 15 - a rotating cylindrical object that appeared to be high in the sky (as planes flew under it) hovered for a period of time close to the Toronto International Airport then started to move very slowly south toward Lake Ontario. Once that it seemed to be very close to the lake it seemed to move along the shoreline moving towards Toronto until it was to far to observe. It reappeared the next day at 11 am and started over the airport and moved north until the trees blocked our view.. The first day we observed it for 35 min and the second day for less than 10 min. 
The object was observed by myself, my son and my neighbor on the first day.
The second day only my son and I, saw it. It was much closer on Sept 16 but it was less shiny. and was easier to see that it was cylindrical.
I am always looking up in the sky and I thought it was a plane at first until I saw it was not moving like a plane should. I was excited to have filmed it , my son was not so excited and my neighbor was a bit scared after watching it. She could not understand what it could be and didn't like the fact that it was there acting like no other flying object she had seen before.
I posted two 8 min videos of the object on www.earthmysterynews.com
