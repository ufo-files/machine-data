90 km winds and my power went out total darkness outdoors. Many beautiful stars took iPhone to take a movie of particular bright star.  Playback stumped me!  Looks like a hand in the sky

May 4 2018 precisely 9:40 pm...very windy!!!  meteomedia announcing chance of tornado for many parts of the region;  with winds obtaining 90 to 100km/hr.  My power went out earlier during hockey match ( 8ish I guess) and sitting in total darkness, decided to enjoy the beauty of the many many stars in our sky! Wondering was the bright SE shining star I could see Sirius? It twinkled so much and was so big I decided to see what I could capture with my iPhone. I went on my balcony in the wind and took a minute video of that star! While filming I could see on my screen weird movement of the said star...it no longer looked like a star,  it looked like a mouth opening and shutting.  After finishing I entered the house and proceeded to view the footage on my phone!  What on Earth was that?  I have not been able to think of anything else since. At times it looked like a huge hand holding something up in the sky!  The activity around it were baffling to say the least.  Please check out my footage and tell me what that was.

Thank you
