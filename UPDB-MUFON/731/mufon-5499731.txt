Several lights over Aylsham

One bright light hovered and made several patterned coloured light signals.. Bright white,gold/yellow,red blue.. I watched it for 5 mins and it didn't move at all, then two more appeared from either side of my window frame so im not sure where they came from but they only had red and blue lights, these moved very quickly and after thinking they were going to collide one then seemed to disappear and the other turned 90 degrees towards the first bright one.. I then went to get my other half and when we got back only the first one was there.. I fell asleep so im unsure of how long it was there for.
