Three high altitude stationary white lights with fast moving red light

Observed a white light at 6:50 AM on January 27th high in the sky that appeared at first to be a satellite. It moved slightly SW to NE and then disappeared and the white light faded out. A second white light formed slightly further SW in the sky from the first and followed the same trajectory. It too faded out. Then a third white light formed slightly further SW of the second, followed the same path and vanished as well. The first light lit up again at the same spot in the sky and followed its short path, vanished again. And the pattern continued for 15 to 20 times. I noticed a red light at the same very high altitude, in the same general area of the sky move in a wobbly, non-linear fashion toward the south. Then that light zipped at tremendous speed and was gone in an instant. I ran inside my home to try to get a second witness to this but no one else was up at the time. I ran outside again, but the three semi-stationary lights were gone. The three white lights moved in a pattern less than an inch in the sky as the eye sees. They held at the same point in the sky for several minutes.
