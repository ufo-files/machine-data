at least 3 large irregular coiled objects accompanied by appr.12 spheres over dallas

At approx. 645pm I observed a bright light stationary in the clouds at a 45 degree angle from me about twice the size of Jupiter just disappear before my eyes. I became alert to the possibility of something more happening so I got my camera and tried to photograph the area it was in to look at it better in case something was still their I could not see.
About 2 minutes later I noticed suddenly what appeared to be only 3 irregular, almost triangular objects flying overhead. I took over 60 pictures and videotaped app 1.30 minutes. Upon examining the photos it was apparent that it was numerous large irregular objects surrounded by a formation of spheres or discs, some of which appear to interact with the objects during their flight overhead. I observed the objects change direction from the south going west and then turning to the north before I lost sight behind my building. The irregular objects appeared to be reddish or purplish coiled tube like structures that were attended by smaller spheres that ranged from gold to grey, with some actually changing color from gold to grey. Some objects appeared rectangular and red. There were 3 different sizes of objects. It seemed to me to be at about the 2000 foot level, under the cloud layers but over the Southwest Airlines flight path with jets leaving to the east. I stuck my head out of my 18th floor apartment to film them and could not detect sound from them. My partner [removed/CMS/fgc] was present and also observed them go overhead. I have submitted a few of the pictures and two video clips. If you want the rest I have them to send you. Thank you cordially, Capt. [removed/CMS/fgc]
