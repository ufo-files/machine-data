A Bright Orange light with Blue Green Craft

Standing outside and I was looking up to the south. A bright orange object was coming right at me. When it got closer to me I could see different color lights below the bright light, which looked blue and green in color. It looked like it was the outline of the craft. I watched it till it got out of sight heading north.
