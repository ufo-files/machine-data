Ufo descended from sky downwards and seemed to head towards me then reappears in sky above me

I was playing with my Sony CX220 modified to full spectrum camera with Infra red around 22:30 PM at night on 1st July 2015, while looking threw the viewfinder while videoing I seen this white light shoot downwards and felt it was heading towards me. I first though it was something unusual as there was no sound at all from it and though it moved too fast for it to be a plane. It first dived down towards me then vanished then a bit later on it re-appears in the sky and flickered then disappeared again and then re-appeared elsewhere in the sky. I felt a little nervous and excited at same time as seeing ufos here is very rare. I lost sight of the object as it just vanished into thin air.
