{cak} They were just lights in the sky that kept coming and disappearing.

It was March 14, 2020 about 9;30 at night. My parents were on there way home from dinner that night and I was at home, my mom in the truck told my dad to look, and they looked liked planes. So when they got home my dad had me come outside to see them. We were all in amazement. They just kept coming from the same spot in the sky and disappearing in the same spot in the sky. Which they started from the brightest star in the sky, and ended at the same spot. They were equally apart from each other. From the time that we noticed them end, there had to be at least 50 of them. But there may have been more from the time that we noticed them. Then we went and did some research. It turns out that in 1969, a farmer was out plowing when a bright light came down from the sky, and lit up everything around him. In fear, the farmer got back in his tractor and drove back to the farm. All this happened 4 miles north, and 1 mile west of Algona Iowa, and the funny thing is that, that happened exactly where we live today, and witness this event..
