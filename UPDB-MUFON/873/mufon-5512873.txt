{nd} Very white bright light over south of Manhattan. I took serie of photos with iPhone4S HDR Mode. Here is what i got.

I was with my cousin in his Manhattan apartment. Standing on balcony we spotted very, very, bright light over south, between 2 Antennas of one building. I started taking pictures with my iPhone4S with HDR mode (2 photos in one shot) with different zoom. Event took place January 26 2013, 5:19PM, New York Time. The Bright Object was moving about 4 minutes very slowly to the left and slowly disappeared. It was Very Bright for human eyes and I could not capture that brightness on the iPhone! Not on photos and video I took. I cant tell how big it was and how far away from me. There is also a helicopter on photos. After zooming pictures on computer You can see better what it was. It looks like "Saturn Like" spaceship. Sighting lasted about 4 minutes. I have positive feelings about it, because we already know, that we have never been alone in the Universe Ha Ha!!! :)
