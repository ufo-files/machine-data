My family (wife and 3 boys - 8, 7 and 23 month old) and I were swimming in our pool and I looked up and noticed a bright light (slightly larger than a star) moving slowly across the sky from the west heading eastward. It looked different than anything that we might usually see like and airplane or helicopter, or anything like a shooting star. I told my family to look at it and everyone agreed that it was different. It didn't blink or pulse and it didn't make a noise of any kind. Close to 2 minutes it seemed to slow down and then it started moving upward (ascending) fairly quickly and the bright white light changed to a reddish-orange color right before it vanished.
