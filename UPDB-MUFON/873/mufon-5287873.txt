String of lights move across the clear night sky

The video I recorded has a time stamp of 10:34 PM 5/15/21. We noticed the craft moving in the sky a minute or two before and contemplated what it could be. The string of more than a dozen lights had hardly no separation and stayed in a line throughout the duration of the event, until we lost sight of it beyond the tree line. That was too close to the ground to be in orbit like a satellite. This craft was flying with a purpose. It took maybe about 5-7 minutes to move across the sky in our area. The altitude was similar to what a commercial plane would be at if an airport was in your area. It was moving slow, and there was some kind of energy distortion around the string of lights. The rest of the craft was not visible, likely due to the time of night. We did notice other planes in the area and satellites before this encounter, and this did not move in the same respective fashions. I will attach a video. In it you will see it come into focus once, but it was difficult to maintain.
