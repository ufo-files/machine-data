large cylinder with no sound

I was in our side yard and happened to look up and saw a large silver cylinder with box shapes sticking out of the craft. It did not make a sound. It was hovering steady just above the tree line. I yelled to my Father who was in the garage to come out and see this thing. I kept my eyes on the craft while I was trying to get my Father to come out. My Father wasn't coming out so I ran into the garage, which was about 25 feet from where I was standing, and dragged my Father back outside. When we got outside, it was gone. From start to finish was about 1 minute.
