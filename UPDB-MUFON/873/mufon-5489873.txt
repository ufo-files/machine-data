Mom texted pics she took w iPhone from river cruise in Russia. She didnt see objects when she took pics. I Noticed them immediately

My mother was on a river cruise vacation in Russia. She would text pics using an app that allows one to send pics and text for free internationally. On August 5th I received a text with a picture that was taken on her balcony, outside of her cabin, without any glass or window in front of her. I know this because I scrutinized the picture very closely and also asked her many questions about her environment as there is an odd reflection of what looks like a table in the left side of the picture. Okay so getting back to the firs pic I immediately noticed the silver/metallic disk in the middle of the sky clear as day.

I wondered if it also was a reflection and again after scrutinizing the pic and speaking with Mom re confirming that while she was on her balcony and there while was a glass table on the balcony there was not a window. It looks very much like a classic silver/spinning disk: 

When my mom returned home I asked her to go through all her pics and see if she snapped any other pics of that time/moment. The next day she texted me a blurry pic and told me to zoom in. In the same position as the silvery disk is a purplish circle surrounded by several metallic/light reflecting objects all over the frame of the pic including the top right corner.

It was then that I realized these werent reflections but rather Unidentified solid objects in the sky....the KICKER is my Mom didnt see ANY of these Objects when she took the pics.
I believe these pics should be looked at by a professional photo analyst.
