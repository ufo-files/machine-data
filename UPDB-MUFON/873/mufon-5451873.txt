Round silver object darting in blue sky very high above car

I was driving west on I-69 from Belsay Rd. towards Flint. The sky was a gorgeous blue with beautiful fluffy white clouds and I was noticing how pretty it was while the warm sun was shining in on me through the windshield. All of a sudden while I was looking at the sky I saw a shiny silver roundish object that looked like it was very high up darting around and it startled me because I had never seen anything like it. It moved very quickly and sort of zig zagged around briefly and then seemed to flip up and completely disappear. The whole thing only lasted a few seconds. I yelled at my 2 daughters who were riding in the van with me. I said something like “Oh my God! What was that?! Girls look at the sky I just saw a UFO or something over there!” We all looked at the sky as we drove on down the highway but didn’t see it again.
