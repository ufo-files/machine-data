{dr}UFO before fireworks

We were at Amanda's Field waiting for the fireworks to start. We thought it was the beginning of the fireworks but this large oval like object that was a reddish orange hovered in the sky then proceeded to change path and slowly crossed the sky. Right before our eyes it just disappeared! We were all in amazement and couldn't believe our eyes. It moved very slow at first then quickly left.
