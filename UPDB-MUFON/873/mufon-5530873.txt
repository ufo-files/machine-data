Pill-Shaped UFO over 90W (Cleveland, OH).

I was traveling on 90W at around 5pm between the Hilliard and Clague Rd. exits. The sky was perfectly clear except for some clouds very low on the horizon. I saw out of the corner of my eye what I first assumed was a helicopter hovering in place. I looked up and noticed immediately that I was mistaken, as this craft had no wings, blades, or anything. It was simply a smooth, metallic silver pill-shaped object. I only got to look at it for a couple of seconds before (I assume) the car in front of me noticed it, too, and hit their brakes suddenly. I looked away for only a second to gain control of my car and looked back less than two seconds later to see that the object had disappeared without a trace.
