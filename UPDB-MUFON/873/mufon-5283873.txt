Shimmering Lights observed

Tonight around 2040 hrs I went outside to water my plants as I looked to the SW I noticed a long what looked like a giant sparkler in the sky moving very slow and no sound, I immediately ran back in to grab my SiOnyx Aurora Pro night vision camera to film it in a rush I forgot to turn the sound back on. This object was observed very clearly both with my eyes and filmed with the camera. In the video I have attached near the end I believe is an aircraft, coming in the opposite direction from E to W (probably landing at the Bilox/Gulfport airport). Then the shimmering light seem to disappear never saw them no more after that. I am sure it was not any type of fixed wing aircraft or the International Space Station. This was all observed for about 2 minutes or so before the lights vanishing/disappearing. Not sure definitely weird looking

Could possibility be Satellites none that I have seen before
