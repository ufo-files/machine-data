metallic orb

I was out for a walk around the neighbourhood at sunset.

Something that seemed to be rising to my right drew my attention.

It was Canada Day 2015 and I thought it must be some fireworks (the date for the pictures shows as June 30, but later on I checked the date on the camera and it was indeed off by a day).

I didn't hear anything, so I looked up again in that direction and I saw the object approaching.

I figured it must be some drone.
It seemed metallic (silvery) and it reflected the sunset. There was a bit of roll (the reflection seemed intermittent).

As I kept looking at it I realized something was off... it had no wings, no propellers. How was that thing floating up there?

That was when I pulled out the phone (a Sony Ericsson W810i) and started taking the pictures.
It was moving quite slowly, I had time to take 3 pictures. I aimed the phone in the general direction of the object (the object was too small to see it on the display).

The object kept moving in straight line. 
When I couldn't see it any longer I resumed my walk.

*** 
I perceived the object as larger and silver in color. I was rather surprised to see it so small and black in the pictures.
