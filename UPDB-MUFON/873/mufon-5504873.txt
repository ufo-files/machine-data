On approximately, September 14, 2013 (I know it was a weekend, my husband age 33, my 7 year old child, and I age 37 were walking through the field back to the house after checking on the chickens in the chicken coup. It was approximately 2100 hours. I noticed a light in the south west area above the tree line and below the clouds. It was a clear night, I originally thought it was shooting star. It had a ball like head with a tail just like a meteor coming into the atmosphere. I quickly got my husband and daughters attention to look at the shooting star when the tail burned out. I thought it had burned up in the atmosphere and was done. Then the white/yellow ball traveling towards the west changed direction 180 degrees and started to the east at that time the tail reappeared like a jet propulsion. I then realized it was definitely not a shooting star. The object did this several times turning right and left with the tail disappearing and reappearing. Then as it was heading in a southwest direction the light turned off and was no longer viable. We did not hear anything. It was an unusually quite night.There was a tall radio tower that was in the background. I also noticed a plane the was flying by which turned direction and flew by the object we were looking at. It appeared to be watching the object as I do not usually see a plane change its flight path in the sky as it did. My husband and I were both amazed at what we saw. We both knew it was a UFO.
