{tjd} My incident is extremely unusual (fighter jets flying over the neighborhood and out over Lake St Clair, accompanied by police chatter over scanner, 3 bright lights zig zagging over Lake St Clair, repeated 5-7 nights {djs})

If you wish to contact me I be glad to share but not on internet.
(Witness was awakened @ 2:01 AM by the sound of fighter jets flying over the neighborhood and out over Lake St. Clair, and the sound of his dad's police scanner radios going off in the basement in the room next to his bedroom. (He described the radios as "going beserk".)
This was unusual because fighter jets normally do not fly over residential areas in the early AM hours.
He can remember voices on the radios very excitedly asking what should they do, contact the FBI or what, and reporting three large bright lights "zig-zagging" around over Lake St. Clair.
He said his father was a Detroit policeman, and that their house was near Selfridge Air Base.
