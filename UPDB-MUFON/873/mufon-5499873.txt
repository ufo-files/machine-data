{jd} Spherical orb floating through the sky.

Saturday night August 2nd, 2014 my brother and I were out hog hunting in Glen Rose, Texas at the ranch of his wifes uncles. My brother had forgotten the flashlights that we were going to use to hunt some hogs, well he went back home to retrieve said flashlights and I went to go check our game camera that we have in the North East corner field of the property. While walking to the camera nothing happened, but when I returned to my original position in the central most field of the ranch, that's when I saw a small light in the distance that was slowly approaching my position. It didn't really seem to get any bigger, and was doing slow horizontal circles and just floated on by.
