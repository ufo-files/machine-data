Streak of white light, flashed lighting up the sky then disappeared.

Was deployed on board Navy ship, standing aft lookout early in the morning when a streak of white light similar to a shooting star, but appearing much thicker and more pronounced, appeared flying overhead before creating a bright white flash brightened up the sky almost like sun rise for several seconds.
Reported contact to ship's CIC, no air contacts reported on radar.
Have not seen anything like it in following 3 years at sea.
