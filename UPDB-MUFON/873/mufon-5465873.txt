Looked like a falling star then stopped suddenly and shot straight up.

Caught my eye while driving home at night. Very clear night sky with lots of stars. Was directly in front of my line of sight of front window. It looked and moved fast exactly like a falling star moving from right to left in angled (45 degree??) decline but then stopped suddenly in mid air and shot straight up (90 degrees from horizon)I stopped the car and got out as couldnt believe what i just saw, staring at where it had been, but the star/object was gone.

I have never seen anything else like this since.(25years)
