{cak} Green orb that hovered for an unknown amount of time. I then looked up to see it and it sped off in one direction, then soon after in the opposite way

One night, I was shooting basketball. Practicing my jumpshots, to be exact. I was around 15-16 at the time. When I happened to look up as I was following the ball after a shot, I saw a bright green luminescent ball in the sky. I immediately got chills and my heart dropped. Almost as soon as I spotted the orb, it flew off to the right. It didn’t make a sound as it sped off and if I’d have blinked I’d have missed it. Then I looked back up, and saw it bolt the opposite direction. 

It was way too fast to be an aircraft from the local airport, and even then, our airport only has single propellor passenger places and a single private jet owned by the local millionaire family. (We live in a pretty small town. Maybe 3,000-4,000 people?)

I looked up meteorites or comets that could be this color as they burned up in the atmosphere, but the erratic movement and hovering in place didn’t seem to be normal for something entering Earth’s atmosphere. 

I need closure. It’s bothered me ever since that night.
