Kuala Lumpur Malaysia, what appear's to the naked eye to be an unidentified flying object shaped like a humanoid with 2 antenna like probes on the sides and the bottom, streaming silently at slow speed across the sky and disappears behind the trees!

It was evening around 1930hrs approximately and I was in the garden bird watching when all of a sudden i noticed in the background this black thing flying across the sky "thanks to the bird..lol" which looked strange because it had a shape of a "R2D2" (that's the perfect shape to describe it) but it also had 2 probes sticking out the sides and the bottom like hands and legs. It was just flying across the sky silently at a steady slow pace and disappeared behind some trees in a distance. I waited for it to reappear but failed to locate it after losing it behind the trees. I could see clearly it wasn't a garbage bag or a balloon for sure. Immediately I took out my camera phone to record it but I was a bit shaky from excitement nevertheless still managed to capture it on my Nokia C6-01 8mp.
