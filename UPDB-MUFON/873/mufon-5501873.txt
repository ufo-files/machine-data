{dr}Two orange/red lights in sky above ocean appeared out of no where and hovered for a few minutes, then vanished.

Two orange/red lights appeared in the sky above Atlantic ocean, they appeared out of no where and hovered for a few minutes, then vanished. I was sitting on my oceanfront balcony around 9pm on 6/11/14 in North Myrtle Beach, SC near the Sea Cabin fishing pier and noticed this event right in front of my face, with no explanation. The next night, my husband and I saw another similar event, only one light that time. I thought it was a plane's lights about to fly over, but it never went anywhere, it just dissipated into thin air, with no sound. I couldn't believe my own eyes and I want to know what it was!
