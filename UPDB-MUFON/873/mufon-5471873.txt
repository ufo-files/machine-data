{cak } Like a very bright falling star

I was driving south on myrtle Ave approaching Marshall st. Bright light caught my attention in my field of view as it descended from about 50degrees above horizon to the south of my location. Lost sight of it when trees blocked view. No variation of speed, brightness or path as if falling. It was not a vertical path, but close to vertical. I thought it might have been a bolide. I was excited to have been in the right place at the right time and got to see it. I could not ascertain distance from me.
