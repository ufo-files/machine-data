{tjd} This is just a sky sighting.

My 2 friends were setting up to deer hunt 20 miles into the countryside. One called me  very excited & told me to go look outside in the western sky moving northeast. I thought it was a meteor shower, however, they say up to 50 lights following each other in a strait path across the dark sky. They would just appear, go a few miles, then disappear.. I am only two miles from town and I observed the exact same thing. There were no other lights in the sky except the normal stars, no other movement. No one light was brighter than the other, none blinked, and none varied the one-after-another straight path. I have not been able to find anyone else that observed this. We called a planetarium at Pittsburg State college and they had not seen or had anything reported to them. I have no idea what this event was...just unidentified flying lights in the night sky. I would love an explanation.
