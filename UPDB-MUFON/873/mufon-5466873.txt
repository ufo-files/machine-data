{rjl} I was 10 years-old, mid evening before sunset, 3 disks passed directly over me in my backyard

Columbus, Ohio  It was the summer of 1957, August, near sunset, and I was playing in my backyard with friends. We were laying in the grass and looking up into the clear blue sky. Suddenly, three disks in a triangular formation, silently came over my house, going from West to East. They were silent and moved smoothly. I could only see their undersides which appeared circular. In the center was a red-orange glowing circle that gave me the impression that it was very hot. Surrounding that was a circle of pale blue, which might be the blur of a spinning propeller. 

My friends and I just laid there and watched the objects glide over us. I was amazed and just stared at them. No fear, just curiosity. Then, suddenly, I thought I needed to get my folks to see the objects. I jumped up and ran yelling into our apartment. My dad came out with me, but by then they were gone. My playmates had disappeared. Later, we never talked about what we saw.

The reason I decided to report this sighting, even though it was 61 years ago is that recently I was watching a science documentary about light. At one point they were talking about how color can indicate temperature. The example given was a blob of molten metal that had the very same color as the objects I saw in 1957. The temperature was 875 F. 

I figured that at finally, I had something substantive to report.
