I was sitting in my hottub on a summer.evening My whole family had just gone inside.
I see a massive object flying in the sky coming towards me. It stopped right about me, hovered for a few seconds about 10 feet above the treetops. It was made of thousands of blinking lights and I could hear the engines. It turned around went back the way it came and then went up into the clouds. I remember hearing on the radio the next day that there were sightings on the West coast. I may have the year incorrect. What I couldn't understand was how could I be the only person to see it.
