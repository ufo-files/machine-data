Observed in photo taken after the event

The object was evident in a photograph taken of my brother on Gabriola Island on October 6th 2012. I had taken two photos of my brother standing on a rock with my iPhone 4. The photos were taken in quick succession, no more than 5 seconds apart. In the second photo an object is apparent over the water to the right and just above my brother's head. It appears to reflect the sun and to be about a mile away or more. The object is not apparent in the first photo taken. I was not aware of the object at the time the was being taken but have wondered about it for the last 6 years.
