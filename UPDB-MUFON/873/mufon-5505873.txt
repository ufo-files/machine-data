I was going to work this morning and was the only car on the road. traveling nw on Carl T. Jones road just past the Large church of christ the left. You can see the area Im talking about on Google Earth), Im always very observant on this particular stretch of road because I ride a motorcycle and its an area frequented by deer. Ahead of me the road curves slightly to the left and goes over the hill. Once over the mountain you are in Huntsville al. I observed a HUGE, (it looked to be the size of a football stadium), glowing incredibly brightly a green color. Imagine the color of a lightning bug, (firefly, depending on where you are from). It was perfectly round and glowing very intensely. It was a cold morning, 26 degree, and perfectly clear sky. The object appeared and came straight down. I cant say I heard anything because I had my earbuds on inside my helmet. It descended down into the trees, but may have dropped below the other side of the hill, however as I was on the road going over the hill, I got over the top less that 3 minutes later, there was NO SIGN of anything. It descended at a fast pace, but not as fast as if it was falling from the sky. Jut a rapid steady descent.
There was NO time to pull my motorcycle over and get my gloves off and then take my phone out of my pocket to snap a picture because the entire event lasted approximately 5 -7 seconds.
My initial reaction was shock and disbelief, I remember I yelled "What the F*** is THAT THING?!", and was a little afraid to get to the other side of the hill, not knowing if it would be hovering there or not. I found the entire event very disturbing.
