{cak} High altitude single light moving at a fast rate.

Not sure what I saw but I saw a light that was moving very fast at a very high altitude moving from South to northeast.

I was picking up the mail this morning at 6:30 am when I glanced up at the sky and saw what I thought was a shooting star (moving very fast) but with no trail or shine to it. Was waiting for it to disintegrate but it didn't. My next thought was that it couldn't be a passenger plane because it was moving at a very fast rate of speed, very high altitude to have the landing light on, no flashing lights, no sound. The light wasn't very shiny but definitely noticeable. I didn't have my phone with me to try to capture a picture. The memorable things that stuck to me was it's very high speed, very high altitude, single non shining light.
