Strange, blue lights over Las Vegas night sky

I did not view these lights with my own eyes, as I was not paying attention to the sky, however, I snapped a series of photos over a period of several minutes which were able to capture these floating orbs, being led by a glowing rectangular shaped orb. I cannot explain these lights as reflections, as we were standing outside and there was no barrier between me and the night sky, and do not believe them to be satellites, due to the fact that the lights appear to break formation at a certain point and even appear to be at completely different places, and different shapes before re-forming in the last photo. From the photos you can plainly see movement happening as you view the photos in sequence. I do not know how to explain what I captured, also I do not recall seeing them at all, so these lights may of even been invisible to me but were able to be captured by the camera.
