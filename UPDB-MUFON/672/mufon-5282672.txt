Two big star like objects hovering

She went to balcony and called me to see this. At first I wasn't sure if that was a UFO but thought it could be reflector emittng light. But it seems odd. They were very near each other. I didn't take them seriously (I also had to study for exam so I didn't pay attention to it that much. After a minute we went inside). Half hour went by or approximately 45 minutes. I went on balcony again and they were still there. I have never seen this lights there before. Neither after this day. So I don't know. For me it is an UFO because it is unidentified. In the morning, when daylight, I went to balcony to spot something fishy there but it was just forest, the objects have gone. Oh, and location of this objects was on some local hill. The density of forest is weak. 

I don't know but is only our area full of that stuff (happening almost every year, sometimes more times in the year) or are UFOs everywhere and people are just not paying attention?
