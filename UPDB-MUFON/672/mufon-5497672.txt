I was driving home from work with my wife when we crossed the Mohawk river bridge and noticed a blue green ball of light go across the evening sky leaving a small contrails behind which would disappear immediately. I heard no noise and it took about 9 seconds for that ball of light to cross in front of us and I lost it at the tree lines. My wife and I could not believe what we just saw.  I looked to the west from where the object had come from and saw a commercial jet flying towards us but nothing else. It appeared to be flying level and low in the sky. What a sight..
