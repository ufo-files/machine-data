{dr}Spinning 3 bright orbs.

My friend and I just sat down at a bench in awe, looking at the fine arts plaza building after taking a lap around. I looked at the stars above hoping for a shooting star, then I saw 3 orbs that slowly spun in a triangle formation. I didn't think of a ufo at first. I asked my friend, "Hey, what is that?" We just starred at it for a few seconds appearing to go north, then it slowly disappeared, silently. She claimed she saw it too. Then it hit me, we just saw a ufo.
