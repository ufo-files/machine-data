{dr}Taking pictures of the sunset, I have captured over 50 pictures of a green circle, some with an aura around it. They appear to be over Lake Erie.

Since moving into my 11th floor apartment, I have been taking pictures of the sunset over the past 10 months. One night, I was looking through the pictures and noticed there was this object in many of the pictures. None of the daytime photos showed any objects, only those taken during the sunset. They were not visible to the naked eye. I realized that some of the pictures had a green circle, and others had an aura around it. The green circles are clearly seen in pictures on both cloudy and clear days. I have taken over 50 pictures with the object, but never noticed until a few weeks ago. The object is very tiny and thought it was just the reflection of the sunlight in the camera. The object can be seen in locations around Lake Erie and sunsets in the pictures. I can see the object moving around from one picture to the next. The object is consistent with color and shape, as well as the aura around the green circle.The aura is a dome shape, creamy color. It is prominent on the right and left sides, and is consistent in all pictures. Only one picture captured the object as red, which was out of focus. I did not ever see this object with my own eyes, but have captured many pictures. I thought I was seeing things, and it was a reflection of light somehow.
