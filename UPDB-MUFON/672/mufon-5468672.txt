About a 1/4 mile after turning onto Fr 525 I spotted a round ball of light moving in an unusually manner. There were no roads nearby in that direction so I knew I was not seeing car lights. My wife saw it too and videoed it. She uses the bright star/ planet above as a reference-point to refer to. It moves a bit with her hand movement, but you can clearly see that the object at the bottom of the screen moves independently of the star above. The ORB slightly illuminated the brush below so we are certain it was close by vs something far off in the distance. 

We returned the next morning to find no roads or anything to explain the orb we saw the night before. Will attach photo of daytime landscape as well.
