Out of a clear dark sky a bright light just suddenly appeared for a split second, then seconds later did it one more time.

On the morning of 12-12-2021 at approx. 05:25 Hrs. I was having my morning coffee when I received a text message on my cellphone of an anomaly detected. I have a MADAR (Multiple Anomaly Detection and Recording) unit installed at my location. I went outside to my back porch to look at the sky. As I scanned to the North, West and East I didn't notice anything then all of a sudden, a bright light appeared for a split second to the North. This obviously got my attention and I continued to watch this area of the sky and a few short seconds later, probably less than 10, another light appeared to the North but gave me the impression that it was now further away from me. The light was a bright white and did not blink. I live just a few miles from an Air Force base, and I am familiar with aircraft lights with the typical green and red and white lights. This did not display anything I've seen before...very curious? No sound was heard during this sighting.
