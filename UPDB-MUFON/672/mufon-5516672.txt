(GS) 4 hours of encounter witnessed and have lot of video of object

My wife and I are staying at our condo in Marathon FL. I awoke are 2:30 am and couldnt sleep. I decided to get some fresh air on the patio. At which time I saw the object in the night sky. I awoke my wife and she grabbed the binoculars. We both saw the object and a companion object we could not identify. 

The encounter lasted from 2:30am to approximately 6:30 am. I continued to film all morning until 6:30 am - my wife went back to sleep around 2:45 am.

I was scared. I shot snippets of video throughout the entire evening. Some longer than others. At one time the object departed soon after I heard a jet noise - the main object began blinking bright red lights as it with its companion moved off north westerly and disappeared (roughly 4:45am), then the object came back a few minutes later, hung around until the sun started coming up only a few minutes later. 

The companion (at least one, at one time appeared there may be multiple) at one point must have moved off and over to the south eastern portion of the sky and was moving in sort of a sweep which at one point swung around and appeared to me at least to be coming towards my location - at which point I dove inside my condo, but later went back out to film again.

I have lots of video. The stills Im attaching are some of the best images I could grab from the video on my laptop. They are not retouched.
