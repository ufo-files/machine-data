{nd} bright reddish ball traveled straight line down street 15 ft above ground

I don't know the date and/or year of the event since I was a young kid. Throughout the years I have often thought of this event and I continue to often think about this event. In the early 1960's at night time my parents called my brother and me from bed, to come to the front window to see something unusual. We both got to the picture window and observed a large bright red flaming object slowing coming from the direction from the left of the house directly down the street, approx. 15 feet above the street, toward the right side of the house at the same elevation going down the road to the right which is a dead end street of trees and past the trees is a lake and girl scout camp. shortly afterwards we saw people in the street and then we saw police cars and fire trucks going down the road to the dead end. they returned shortly there after without any sign of a fire or crash, etc. the ball was extremely bright and lite up the road and everything around it as it slowly went down the road. in the morning my brother and cousins and I walked down the road looking for any signs of fire, etc., to the dead end. we saw no signs of fire. we walked into the trees/woods at the dead end and then to the lake/water walking what seemed endlessly in search for any signs of the bright fire ball, but we turned up nothing. In the morning think I remember seeing a fire man looking for signs of anything along the road and I believe he said he did not find anything. I think of this sighting very often, but I have never looked into the matter to determine if there were any reports from police and fire department ever dispatched along with other types of reports from people and I wonder if you have any information concerning this event.
