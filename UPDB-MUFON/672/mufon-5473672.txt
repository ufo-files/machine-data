{rjl} Looked like a rocket or meteor breaking up but had direction changes

Observed falling comet like objects at least four large enough to see that appeared to be enveloped in flame. Close together at first, snd spreading as they fell. 

When they had spread over what looked like a mile from each other, a couple of them changed direction a couple of times.

They appeared to be obscured by clouds a couple of times, and they kept shrinking as they got closer to the horizon. Lost site of the lowest one about 5 minutes before the others, which continued to decrease in size and brightness. 

When they finally winked out it was one by one. Saw the last one change directions after it got behind the trees.

Over all the decent was pretty steady, and the general direction appeared to be toward the U of U from my house.
