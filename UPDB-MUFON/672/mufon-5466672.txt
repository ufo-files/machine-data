It was a lazy hot summer afternoon in the month of early June in a valley nestled in the lap of Shivalik hills of Uttrakhand in India. It was the year 1975. I was all of six years, playing all alone outside in an open balcony of our home. The spotless tranquility of the azure sky was suddenly woken from a deep slumber by a bright silvery white saucer cutting though it in a zapping speed just a few odd meters over my little head. The lush green flora that added a sparkle to the surroundings like glittering gems in our garden were forced to bend down under the command of the mighty gushing gale th object triggered with its lightening speed. The wind almost blew me away as I struggled to gain composure holding on to the wall nearby. In a flash the amazing object was out of sight but it did manage to engrave the ever lasting effect of the astonishing unexplained event that always questions me.. Was it really an UFO?
