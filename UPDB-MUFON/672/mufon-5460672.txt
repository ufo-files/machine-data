{rjl} Witnessed a straight line of objects illuminated coming from upper atmosphere

I was outside admiring the stars on Friday May 24th, 2019 and at 11:35pm I noticed in the northern sky several (seemed like over 100 lit) objects that descended from the upper atmosphere, appeared and traveled in a straight line formation across the sky to the east. They did not appear as a shooting star as they looked like in a controlled path of flight. I have observed the sky many times at night and I have never seen anything like it before.
