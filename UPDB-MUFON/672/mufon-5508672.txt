On 2:30 am on August 13th (East coast of Australia, Port Macquarie, I decided to wake and see if i could view the Perseids. I walked around to the road to get a better view of the sky and check it was clear, and it was crystal. I started to walk back to my lawn to make myself comfortable when a large bright firey object caught my attention. I looked up at what apparered to be a huge meteor/fireball travelling from north/northeast the north west. I watched as this giant ball of flames flew accross the sky for about 3-4 seconds before seemingly disintergrating. left in awe at this siting, The perseids ive loved and viewed many times before just couldnt compare after id seen that. I was left sitting there all morning with adrenaline pumping through my veins. I feel like it was a moment specially for me. still cant find any other sitings. hopefully i do.
