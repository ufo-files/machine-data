{rjl} Bright white light in the Eastern sky to ther South/below the star Arcturus, moved rapidly to the SE about 15 degrees, becoming smaller as it moved, and turned to a small red speck before disappearing.

On this relatively clear evening I was standing on the back deck of my home in Talent, OR at 9:30 PM (PDT) looking up at the evening sky, looking to the East, when I viewed a steady bright light, like the evening 'star' Venus, about the size of Venus, white in color, in the Eastern sky to the right and below the star Arcturus, It was noticibly brighter than any other stars in the sky, and appeared to be at a great distance from me. It was moving rapidly to the East and South. I used the star Arcturs to judge the object's movement 'up and away' from me. As it moved the light grew smaller and smaller and just before disappearing appeared to change to a red color. THere was no sound associated with it. I felt no fear or emotions, before, during, after this experiene, only excitement when I realized that the bright object was moving and becoming smaller at the same time. It turned into a small red speck for a split second before disappearing.
