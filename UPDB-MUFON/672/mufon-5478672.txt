{cak} While driving on the 10 highway between Arizona and New Mexico a very bright light buzzed our car.

FILLED OUT WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18809, AT MUFON HEADQUARTERS ON JANUARY 10, 2017.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
IT WAS 1946 AND ME AND MY MOM AND DAD WERE DRIVING THE 1936 FORD AT ABOUT MIDNIGHT TRAVELING EAST ON THE 10 HIGHWAY BETWEEN ARIZONA AND NEW MEXICO.
I WAS 12 YEARS OLD AT THE TIME AND SITTING UP IN THE BACK SEAT WITH MY ARMS RESTING ON THE BACK OF THE FRONT SEAT BETWEEN MY PARENTS. DAD HAD JUST GOTTEN OUT OF THE SERVICE AND WE WERE ON OUR WAY TO TEXAS.
A VERY BRIGHT LIGHT CAME DOWN FROM THE SOUTH AND BUZZED/STRAFED OUT CAR. IT IMMEDIATELY ROSE BACK UP IN THE AIR AND WAS GONE. WE ALL SAW IT, IT HAPPENED VERY FAST. IT MADE NO SOUND. THERE WAS NOTING IN OUR MILITARY AT THAT TIME THAT COULD TRAVEL THAT FAST AND THAT SILENTLY.

I JUST THOUGHT I SHOULD REPORT IT AND SEE IF ANYBODY ELSE HAD SEEN IT. THIS HAPPENED BEFORE THE ROSWELL INCIDENT.

(IN 1946 THE ROADS WERE DIFFERENT, HE WAS MAYBE ON HIGHWAY 70/80 GOING FROM
CALIFORNIA TO TEXAS. THIS IS ROUGHLY THE 10 FREEWAY AND ALSO RUNS NEAR THE WHITE SANDS NEW MEXICO TESTING GROUNDS AND NEAR THE TRINITY SITE WHERE THE FIRST ATOMIC BOMB WAS DETONATED ON 
JULY 16, 1945. L. FLECHTNER)
