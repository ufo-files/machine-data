Sighted high hovering Disc

I was a teen-ager in the 1950s going to mail a letter at a local mail box it was a very clear night with the stars out I looked up to see a domed disc very high up hovering for about 5 or so minutes with a green stripe down the center it then departed very very fast sideways.
