{cak} I was looking at the night sky and stars. In an instant, I viewed a group of disc shaped objects flying in formation.

At about 11:00 pm on 7/21/18, I was looking at the night sky and stars. It was a clear night. Right in my view, within a 2-second interval of time, there was an instantaneous appearance of at least 15 disc-shaped objects all flying from the north to a southwest direction. 

All of the discs flew at the same speed however they were not in an-evenly spaced formation. They all flew in the same direction,very fast, and then disappeared.

The discs were uniformly ovoid-shaped, and white with distinct edges, however no shadow or outline. There was no trace of light or any other object that appeared afterwards.
