{rjl} At 1 a.m. on both Sunday and Monday mornings I observed something flashing 3-4 colors hovering over 45 minutes at approx 1,000 ft up

On both Sunday and Monday mornings from 1 a.m. for 45-60 minutes I observed flashing lights of blue/green, white and red while hovering about 1,000 ft up in the Northeast sky over Springfield/Eugene Or. I checked with local police to ascertain if a helicopter was in that area. I was told "no" by dispatch unless it was private or military. It didnt behave like police helicopters I have witnessed in large cities. Also it didnt shine a searchlight. After 60 minutes, it is still hovering in place. Thinking back, I have seen it before this summer, but being tired, I dismissed it as a planet even though I realize that no planets flash multi colored lights even if they seem to twinkle (such as Venus). This is my 4th sighting of UFOs, the 3rd this year. I viewed this from a high rise building looking northeast and using a simple pair of binoculars.
