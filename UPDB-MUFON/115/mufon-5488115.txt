{cak} triangular ufo with three amber lights (one at each point)

My fianc1e and I were walking beside my house and commenting on the stars/constellations in the sky. She noticed a dark triangular object approximately the size of one to two moons with three amber round lights and pointed it out to me. We both watched as it quickly flew silently in a straight line at an unknown altitude in a southeasterly. The sky was rather dark but the triangular shape was visible particularly as it blacked out the stars that it passed in front of. The object became more distant appeared to turn on its side from the arrangement of the lights and then vanished in the night. Within seconds/minutes we could hear helicopters converging in the area. My first thought was that it was either a drone or secret military aircraft.  We were both in awe and realized we had seen something inexplicable.
