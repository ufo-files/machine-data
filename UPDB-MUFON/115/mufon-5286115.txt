Me and my friend see a large craft desend from the sky hover over tree top then came towards us and it didn't have any sound

The object was probably size of grayhound bus as it came closer to us it was so bright that the ground around us was lit up and we jumped back in the car and the inside of the car was lit up also..We were terrified because we knew something that large should have a sound and as we drove away to escape we seen to more hoovering in the sky at a further distance and they took off upwards leaving a trail kinda like a shooting star does as we passed by the one we were running from it was only 30 yards away to our right but we didn't see if it flew away once we rounded a curve..We stopped about a 1/4 mile away still watching the sky and within a few minutes we seen at least 5 or 6 jet aircraft flying very high back and forth in the area we seen the other two UFO take off..We could see the flashing lights on the aircraft.. I will never forget that night because that's the scared I've ever been in my life..
