{jpg} UFO sighting summer 1979. Solid bright white craft, shaped like a manta ray.

Driving home from work one morning I saw a UFO. It was bright solid white UFO, shaped like a manta ray. It had no lights, no markings, no windows, no wings, no tail rudder, no visible rotors or engine, and no visible exhaust. It appeared to be flying low and slow in and out of low white cumulus clouds on a bright sunny blue sky day. I saw it for about 15 to 30 seconds and then it went into another cloud and did not come out the other side as I expected. Until now I have only told a few people, I just wanted there to be a record. This was in the summer of 1979. On or about July 3rd. Best regards.
