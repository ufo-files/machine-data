{kt}Red light hovers above Mora 1981

While deer hunting in Mora,MN in the winter of 1981 when I was 15 years old I went outside to go the bathroom (We were staying in a travel trailor)and I looked up at the stars and there it was. I noticed a red light hovering in the sky swaying back and forth. It made no noise and I watched it just hover and glow for about 3 minutes or so, I was excited not knowing what it was and not knowing anything about UFO's at this time. I went in and got my Dad and my Uncle and we watched it for another 7 minutes or so and then I got the owners of the house, a couple, to come out and watch it for another 10 minutes or so.At the end it just took off going away from us at such a speed it was out of site in a matter of seconds.We just stared at where it went and talked about it and went back in the trailor. Today, only my Dad and I are still alive that witnessed it. He denies seeing anything now and I'm sure would like to remain anonymous. I never thought of reporting it till now, I want there to be a record of it.
