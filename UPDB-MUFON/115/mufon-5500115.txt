{rjl} UFO spotted heading SE over Colorado Springs

I was sitting on the patio of a bar with a friend of mine when someone else on the patio pointed out an object in the sky. It was hard to tell, but it looked like there were two small red lights next to one another. My hunch is that it was only one aircraft with two lights. The object had to have been at a very high altitude. My initial thought was that it was a satellite and I expressed that to everyone, but then it abruptly stopped, changed direction for a moment (moving directly south) then began moving southeast again. It was moderately alarming, but Colorado Springs does have a lot of military activity around it. I wasn't sure if something like that could be a drone or another military aircraft. I don't know if drones can change flightpath that easily. Eventually the object flew out of sight heading southeast.
