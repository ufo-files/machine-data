I was coming home when I saw the strangest clouds. I've never seen anything like them so I pulled over to get some pictures. After taking the first photo A big bright white/shiny round object came down out of the cloud. It just seemed fall and stop, then hovered for only a few seconds... and then continued to descend in a diagonal fashion into another cloud and disappeare. It wasn't until I was looking at my pictures that it seems as if there may have been 2. The object that got my attention seem to be reflecting the Sun because it seemed to Sparkle or flicker a bright white light. A very short lived but unexplained experience.
