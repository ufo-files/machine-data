Pulsating object that was stationary in my southern sky that was observable for at least 10 minutes

Around 11:10pm on November 14th 2021 I was in my bedroom and heard a car annoyingly honking it's horn. I looked out my bedroom window viewing southward and noticed a bright object through my window that looked bigger and brighter than the usual stars that you see clearly in the night sky. The object was mostly stationary but seemed to wobble also on occasion while I observed it. I began recording it on my cell phone after a couple of minutes as it seemed to pulsate and then it appeared to slightly change shape. I recorded it for at least 10 minutes and sorry if moments of the footage is a bit shaky but once I stopped recording it was still visible for at least another 30 minutes.
