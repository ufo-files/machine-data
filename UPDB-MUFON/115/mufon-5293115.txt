3 'stars' in triangle formation headed by 1 other star

Sitting out in the garden my mother noticed a group of very bright stars in the sky. As I looked up I saw a triangle formation headed by one star, all moving slowly and steadily NE until disappearing below the horizon. 5 years ago, I saw a similar formation but during clear daylight in Geneva, Switzerland where dark disks made the same kind of movements and where metalic when catching the sunlight. 

I only have a smartphone which wouldn't have worked to film anything.
