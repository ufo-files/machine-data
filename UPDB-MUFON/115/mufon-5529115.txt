Comet like bright white light

Carve Pro Skate shop was having a grand opening event. I was taking photos and looked up at a beautiful blue sky with zero clouds. To the southwest a bright white object was visible at extreme altitude. The object had no winds or marks that could be identified as any conventional craft. I snapped two photos a couple of minutes apart. The object was very high and appeared to be moving faster than an airliner that usually has visible wings, windows, and markings. This had none. Once it was out of sight about two minutes after the second photograph, two ARMED blue F18s flew right over our heads, low, less than 1000 feet above the ground in the general direction the object was last seen.
