2 evenly spaced diagonal blinking & solid white lights moving N to S.

On 11/02/20 @ 8:30pm CST, I was in my front yard letting my dog pee. I always look at the sky at night to pick out constellations or anything else. When I looked west, I noticed 2 objects (est. @ 45 degrees). At first, I thought it might be a satellite but wondered how could there be two sets of identical lights that weren't very close together. They traveled north to south at a steady pace; evenly spaced apart and diagonal to each other. Each had 1 blinking white light (in unison) and another steady white light. While I would love to see a UFO, I feel these objects are man-made and just wondered what they could be. I don't know enough about astronomy to figure out how to research man-made objects in the sky. I stopped watching them when they were about to be obscured by the neighbor's house. I then sent an email to [link moved to Investigators report section/CMS/fgc]. Their response said they didn't research UFOs, but they did direct me to a website that eventually led me to MUFON.
