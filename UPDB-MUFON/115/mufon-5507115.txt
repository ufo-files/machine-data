{nd} FOUR BRIGHT RED ORANGE SPHERES...THOUGHT METEOR, BUT NO TRAIL....FIVE MINUTES APART, SAME TRAJECTORY

WHILE ON THE BEACH BETWEEN 11PM AND 11:20PM, I AND TWO OTHER WITNESSED 4 FIREBALLS WHICH WE INITIALLY THOUGHT WERE METEORS. HOWEVER, NONE OF THE FOUR HAD TRAILS OR SOUND. THEY CAME AT ALMOST EXACT 5 MINUTE INTERVALS. THEY STARTED IN THE NORTHWEST SKY ABOUT 60 DEGREES ABOVE HORIZON AND TRAVELED IN A FAIRLY STRAIGHT LINE. I HAVE OBSERVED MANY METORS IN THE SKY, BUT NONE LIKE THIS. DID NOT SEEM TO HAVE ANY RANDON QUALITY. AFTERWORD, WE THOUGHT PERHAPS IT WAS A MILITARY EXERCISE AS THERE IS AN AIR FORCE BASE 30 MILES TO THE NORTHEAST....BUT AGAIN, NO SOUND.
