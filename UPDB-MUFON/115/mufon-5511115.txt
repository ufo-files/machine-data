{cak} I saw a boomerang shaped object go from east to west directly over my head, it made no sound.

I was in my backyard, looking for the family cat. I looked up and saw a boomerang shaped object less than 1000 feet overhead. It had white lights in a V-shaped pattern on the wings. It moved slowly from east of where I was standing to west. It made no sound at all. At first I thought it was an airplane, then I realized it was way too low and wasnt making any noise. It continued west until I could no longer see it because of the treeline. After I could no longer see it, I realized what I just saw and ran into my house to tell my wife. She was up with our baby grandson, and came out and looked, but did not see anything unusual.
