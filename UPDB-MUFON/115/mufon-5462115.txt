Myself and a colleague were driving home when 3 objects in the sky hovered over us, one disappeared and the other two circled in the sky for a while. Another disappeared when the one left in the sky hovered approx 50ft in the air above us. It was square and approx 20x20ft with a blue, green, red and white light on each corner of the square. It continued to follow us very slowly until it abruptly made a fast assent and disappeared.
