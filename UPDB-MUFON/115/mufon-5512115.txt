A Long Rectangular Object With Brightened Windows Hovered and Passed.

March 14th 6:50 p.m.

I was browsing internet in my cell phone while in the other room my wife was watching TV.After some minutes,my cell-phones signal dropped and I was unable to open the sites,wondering why my both the cell-phone's signal dropped,I moved to our terrace (at the back of the house)to check whether in any direction that I can detect a signal,but it was useless.After few seconds when I turned,I saw an object hovering in the the eastern sky,it was coming from the North-eastern direction and passing to the South-eastern direction.For a few seconds I observed this object to verify whether it was a plane or any other aerial man-made objects,but I couldn't find any aerodynamic features attached to it.It was a long rectangular object with golden bright windows.The moment I realized it was something,unusual I called my wife out loud to show it,but the time when she arrived it just disappeared into unknown.I ran to the other terrace (which at the front of our house)to follow its trail,but nothing.It just disappeared.
