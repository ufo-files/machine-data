Collection of lights lower than commercial aircrafts, consisted of triangle lights and individual lights

Initially saw three singular lights one after the other moving at spread from SW direction to NE direction, followed by a larger set of three lights in triangle, followed by further singular lights, I then phoned Air traffic control to report around about 20 lights spotted not in a straight line but spaced apart heading from SW direction to NE direction, after phone call I later counted a further 6 lights all going in same direction but spaced apart more from previous lights, all lights seemed to be of similar size, colour and speed, no noise from lights, we are used to different aircraft passing across and around and never seen anything like this, I also know where/what satellites we have/tracked, both myself and my son looked through the binoculars but moving to fast to get clear image other than fuzzy roundish lights, no sound, no trail especially as it was a colder night.
