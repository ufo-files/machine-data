Outside calling in my dog when I spotted 2-3 glowing orbs circling each other moving in a NW to SW direction

I was out on my back step calling my dog inside when I noticed 2-3 glowing orbs that looked like glow in the dark stars that you place on your roof. They were moving in a slow paced North-West to South-West direction circling each other in an erratic pattern. Due to the dark it made it hard to see if there was two close together or just the one. When I first spotted the orbs I thought they were Parachutes but they never dropped any lower from their height and didn't move in the way that a parachutist would. I followed the objects across the nights sky until they went behind a large tree in my neighbours back yard where I lost sight of them. I had tried to film the objects but my iPod and Phone do not have a camera that would pick up objects that far away and at night. After the sighting I was in a bit of shock, came inside and told a couple of friends about it online.
