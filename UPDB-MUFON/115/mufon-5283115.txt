Saw three glowing dots in the sky getting followed by planes

Me and my boyfriend were driving back home to
see family in North Carolina. On our way down we noticed three glowing dots and shortly after a lot of planes/helicopters started to surround them. One of the glowing dots disappeared for a while when this happened and then it came back. They all started to spread out and they moved to the left and got a little closer to the ground. They just began moving around some but generally sticking with each other making a triangle. The planes continued to follow. Until they just disappeared. I think they may have landed because it seemed as if they kept getting closer and closer to the ground
