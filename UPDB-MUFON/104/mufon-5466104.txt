{jpg} Triangle shaped craft hovered above the car and observed us for an extended duration

Me and two friends where out in the country back in the early 20001s near lemoore naval air station less than a mile from the air station. When we stopped the car and I reached out to knock the ash off my cigarette, I saw a light from above the car and looked up and noticed an object the size of a school bus above us. I yelled for my friends dude dude look up ! They both looked up and we watched the object observing us it floated above the car moving forward very slowly for about 24 minutes we got some very detailed descriptions of what it looked like and how it hovered including the tail was the point of the triangle. A friend of mine lived in the area and requested no one tell the story while he was alive, he recently passed away from cancer. I no longer live in California and would be happy to talk about it. This was before cameras where common on cell phones so no pictures where taken but I have details on it that could help we where less than two car lengths from the craft for nearly a half hour. Shapes,designs and moldings colors and speed. It farted off in the direction of Fresno California within seconds I could hear the engine powering up over the course of seconds to where all you could see was a blur and a trail in the shape of the craft that lasted about 5 seconds. Please contact me we guessed for years it was an test craft the Air Force was working on. But then we would go back and forth that the shape and design was too complex with the propulsion non existent as in not observable but we could hear it powering on and off as it hovered. There was three witnesses but I am the only still around.
