Object in sky bursts info flames and moves at high speed.

Occurred either 1993,1994, or 1995, it was a spring, summer, or fall day. It was evening and it was dark. We were driving on the trans canada highway just east of the gander airport when we saw the object/orb to the north side of the road and pulled over to the side of the highway for a better view.

Its been almost 30 years so details are difficult to remember but the thing I remember most is how this object, some 1-2 km away from us and maybe 300 meters off the ground, it would burst into huge flames then go back to its original appearance. What it looked like when not burning is difficult to remember, i'm going to say it was an orb of light or an object emitting light(s). It just hovered there exploding in flames and then back to an orb 2 or 3 times. 
Suddenly it shot across the sky to another location perhaps 10-15 km away from us. Actually I remember the object disappearing completely and then reappearing in the distance, while the 2 witnesses with me claimed to see it zoom at high speed to arrive at its new location in milliseconds. For me it just vanished, and i was like where did it go? And then i picked it up again in the distance towards gambo. We continued to watch it for some time but lost it in the trees as it appeared to be moving away from us.
We did not hear any sound. 

An article appeared in the Gander Beacon Newspaper which described our encounter almost exactly. The witness was a fisherman in Gambo in his boat at night. We did note that the object travelled toward gambo, and that was the general direction we were looking when we lost sight of it. I saved the article but lost it when we moved to a new house in 1995. I am awaiting a digitized archive of the Gander Beacon newspaper to appear online hopefully someday, so i that can find the article.
