{rjl} Watched it fly over the tree line then over us.

I was sitting on my deck with my wife looking at the stars at around 10:30 pm when I noticed a bright orange light flying. I thought it was an airplane with an orange light but never seen one before. There was an airplane in the sky to the far left of it going in the same direction, the unknown object passed the airplane quickly then as it flew directly over us we saw it was a disc shape that had a orange aura circling a solid circle object. We were shocked and could not understand what that could have been. We couldn't see it anymore when it flew out of sight. We have never seen a round aircraft. We live near an airport and have never seen an airplane that looked like this.
