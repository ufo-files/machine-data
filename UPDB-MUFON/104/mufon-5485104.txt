{rjl} 4 blinking lights over Phoenix

I was leaving Wal-Mart in Mesa, as I had stopped there to pick up medicine for my sick child. I noticed 4 very bright kind of blinking round lights in the sky to the west, over the Phoenix area. They would have been several miles away from where I was. There are always a lot of planes in that area, and sometimes they come into a formation to wait for landing. However, I realized almost immediately that they were too bright for airplanes, and that they were not behaving like airplanes. Once in awhile one would move and kind of circle around the other 3. They did not move or behave like airplanes or stars, and the lights kind of twinkled and were very bright. It is very hard to say how big they actually were since they were so far away from where I stood. My mother and child were in the car and they also witnessed the lights, and we could see them on and off all the way home (sometimes the view was obscured by trees or buildings.) When I got home I had my fiance go outside to look and we could still see one of the lights from the front of our house. From there we could see that sometimes the light blinked red, and he agreed with me that it was really unusual and was not an airplane. Because I have a sick child, I did not get any video or pictures. We did not stay outside the house looking for long, so I do not know the duration or how it "ended." I would say that from the time I left Wa-Mart and first saw them to when my fiance was outside with me and saw them was probably no more than a 30 min. span of time. I would really love to know if anyone else reported anything from last night over Phoenix.
