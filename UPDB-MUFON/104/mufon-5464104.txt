{jpg} Observed a glowing red dot above Salem Oregon 9-26-89

Salem Oregon, 9-26-89, around 9:20 pm. Was sitting in my living room watching
TV when I felt the need to go outside, once outside wondered why I came out.
Wondered if I came out to check the laundry room, Dryer was empty and washer was dry, so was a little confused and walked back my house. Soon as I closed the door behind me I thought "No, go back outside". Was a little perturbed so I went outside again took a step turned right, took one step then thought "Ok, I'm outside now what?" That's when I looked up and saw a glowing burgundy-red dot (sphere). My immediate thought was Wow!. What is that!. Have been a fan of aircraft, astronomy since I was a child and this matched nothing had ever seen before or since.
