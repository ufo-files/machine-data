Falling lights from the sky

I didn't know if I had saw what I thought I was seeing, but a girl in front of me saw the same thing as well. At that point I know I wasnt crazy. We had no idea what it was, or why the event occurred, Im sure the has to be an explanation, possibly and probably something natural.
