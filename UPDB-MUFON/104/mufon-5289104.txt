Rotating, box like object

Rotating, box like object at approximately 500-1000 feet altitude.Maintained direction and altitude as we observed it over a field. We were driving down Cypress lake road, heading toward Statesboro. My wife Beth first spotted the object over one field then it was obscured by trees. At the next field I too observed the object. It was moving to the NE, maintaining altitude and direction. I only saw it for about 5 seconds as I was driving and having to lean fwd and look up through the windshield. It looked metallic and rotating a a low spin rate. As a FAA licensed pilot and A&P mechanic I can say that I have never seen an aircraft that would fit the description of what we saw.
