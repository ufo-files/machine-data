(GS) Star like object moving unusually fast across the sky

I was walking to a friends house at around 7:59 PM. I saw a star like object moving unusually fast across the northern sky. Way to fast to be a plane. It was too bright to be a satellite. It was in the north sky, moving from west to east. It took a sharp turn, and then began to head north. I first thought the object was a plane, but it was moving way too fast. I couldnt believe how fast it was moving. Then, I saw it take a very rapid turn and went north. I lost sight of the object as it was behind the tree line and buildings.
