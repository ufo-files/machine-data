{nd} Black triangle during Lunar Eclipse

I was outdoors watching the Lunar Eclipse take place. Once the eclipse was full, I noticed a light moving in the sky at a 45 degree angle from myself. The light at further examination was the shape of a slit and it was followed by two more lights on either side of the first light and to the rear. The position of the lights formed a triangle. Once the object sailed directly over my head, I was able to confirm the shape of the object against the backdrop of the stars. It was a triangle just as described by many. It made no noise and just moved smoothly in one direction. Nothing else was trailing it or around it. I am convinced that I was able to spot this object only because of the lunar eclipse and the fact that the moon was blocking most of the light pollution from the sun which cleared the night sky. The object moved south to north and I watched for about 2 minutes until I couldn't see it anymore. There was a slight overcast.
