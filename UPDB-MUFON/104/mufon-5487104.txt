{cak} observed brighter star like light thaat changes color of flahing lights and moves in different direction sometimes small and other times larger movement

My daughter informed me she had seen these lights so I checked it out. I have watched them on and off for about two weeks in the night sky (westerly and high up) and I have seen the light flash blue and red and white which I can see without aid of binoculars. Last night the light was moving much more than usual when I observe it. It can move in all direction and at one time it made an ascent in a small spiral motions. Last night it descended straight downward in the biggest movement I have seen. I saw if above my neighbor's roof at first and suddenly it moved downward until I could not see it (in was below the rooftop). I went upstairs and it was there, went downstairs again and could not see it.

This light definitely moves in all directions and some nights seems to not be as active but last night it was moving more than I had seen before.

When I first was shown the light my daughter pointed out a not as bright star/light in a more southern direction and I saw it move too. She now tells me she sees 5 of them. I have only watched the single one on a regular basis.
