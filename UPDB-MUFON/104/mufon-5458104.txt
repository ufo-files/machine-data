{cak} Amber Sphere and Triangle Craft

On July 5th, 2019 I was vacationing with some friends. Shortly after midnight I went down to the dock and noticed what looked like an amber sphere flying from the south, it looked strange to me because there were no repeating light patterns, the sphere was itself was a light meaning it didn't have external lights, the speed wasn't consistent and as it came closer it's flight patterns changed in that the sphere would fall, and reversed directions a few times as you can see in the attached video. I've attached pictures and you can see multiple objects including a triangle craft. If you slow down the video, the sphere changes shape every frame even at 60FPS. This isn't the first craft I've seen, it isn't a drone, an airplane, a helicopter etc. There's nothing man-made that I'm aware of that flies like this object did.
