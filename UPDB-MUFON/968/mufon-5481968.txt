wall,that surrounds the back steps to the house,and kitchen door.I was talking to my friend Tony,When i looked up and over his shoulder,i could see in the night sky a complete circular,swirling mass circular mass,i was aware that their was no clouds in the sky,apart from this ring,yet to the left of me stars were visible,but nothing could be seen in front of me apart from this ring.The mass was
