My assistant and I were on 64 headed towards Williamsburg from Richmond approximaty 1 mile from the RIC airport. A flash of light and my assistant said she saw lightning. I asked if it was heat lightning? And she said I think so. No cloud coverage then a second flash and I looked up in the sky directly in front of me we both watched this UFO Maniifest right before our eyes! I remember thinking I was a little worried about it staying near that part of town. As I drove to the other side of town near Busch Gardens it reappeared lower and following my car. I made it home and. There was another flash of light as I got out of the car at my home and i noticed it had traveled up cloud level.
