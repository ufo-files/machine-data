(GS) 7 th objeect in the last month single craft

walking up street toward the north of my house. Looked up and saw same pattern of lights as the last 6 craft with a plus or cross of lights of various colors and patterns. I caught sight of it as it passed out of sight going toward the NE wich is a change of direction for this bunch. They seem to picking up the pace on the pass overs of our house.Time of night was a little earlier than usual.
