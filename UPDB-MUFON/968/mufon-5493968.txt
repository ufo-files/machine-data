{cak} My sister saw a light on her way home. Shortly before turning into our driveway, she saw a bright beam of light.

The date is a guess since it happened some time ago. My sister had gone to the store at around 9 pm in the evening on or about April 1996. After a short time she returned home, yelling for me to come outside. When I did, I saw a green beam of light shining around my neighbors yard. The beam was an intense cone shaped light. It was approximately 100 yards from where I stood in my driveway. It lite up my neighbors front and side yard. The beam was intense more like a laser light than normal light. The beam was about 30 feet in diameter. I could not see a source of the light. I could not see a craft. There was no sound. Two cars had stopped on the paved road, staying out of the light, as if they were watching it. After about 10 minutes, the light disappeared. I was so excited and shocked that I called the local Airforce base, and asked to speak with the duty officer. I asked if there were any maneuvers in progress with the military. I was told there was none. I asked if there were any helicopters in the area. I was told no. I thanked them and hung up the phone.
