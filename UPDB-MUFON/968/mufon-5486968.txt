{rjl} While in a pool late at night and friend and I observed a gold/bright yellow ball of light fly over his house.

At approximately 2 a.m. in August 2011 I was at a friends house, it was very hot so we had decided to get in his outdoor in ground pool but, due to the late time of night we had to be very quiet so we wouldn't wake neighbors. We were each in our own inter tube just floating around in the dark looking up at the sky and not talking. I noticed a gold or bright yellow ball of light coming out of the west up in the sky heading our direction. It was so incredibly bright that I couldn't make out the outline or a shape other than a ball, it was moving pretty fast, faster than airplane you see up in the sky. It just had the one glowing color all over it with no pulsing. It also seemed rather large, but I could be mistaken, it was hard to judge how high up it was so the size would depend on that information. It reminded me of a full moon but was about half that size. Both my friend and I had the impression that it had a definite destination, it also seemed to have a path it was following, it wasn't falling or on fire or changing direction, just flying a particular path. It passed over our heads and headed roughly towards Boise, Idaho. I watched it until I couldn't see it anymore then it was gone. I asked my friend, "what was that?" and he replied, "I have no idea".
