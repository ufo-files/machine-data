{cak} 2 red-orange bright star-like lights

I was driving down 309 South when I saw two red-orange lights out the right-side window. At first, I thought the objects were aircraft, but they were not blinking and didn't seem to be moving. I thought maybe they were stars too, but they seemed way too bright and way to red-orange to be stars. I also thought they might be lights on a tower of some sort, but I think they were too high to be that either. The sighting didn't last long, maybe a few minutes or so, because I drove past where I could no longer see it because it was obscurred by the trees. I really don't know what it was, again, best guess it might have been a tower or something, but it just doesn't add up.
