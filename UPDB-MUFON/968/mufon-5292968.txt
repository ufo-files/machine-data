Group of 10-20 “tumbling beanbags”

At a friend’s birthday party stood outside his garage which is off an alleyway on Clough St in highland, Indiana. I watched a group of “tumbling beanbags” that were metallic dull orange morphing into metallic dull grey in color. They weren’t in any type of formation, just sort of mobbing through the sky in a south by Southwest direction. They seemed to change shape and color but remained the same size and one of them towards the front, between three and five from the front... This one fell out of formation and seemed to check me out as I watched them. There were about 30 people in the garage behind me but I couldn’t get anyone’s attention to show them. This one fell back as the group passed then caught back up with them and kept on going. I watched them for another minute or two then they were gone. I asked other people in the yard if they saw anything but nobody did. I didn’t feel scared or like I was in danger. In fact, I felt pretty good! That one seemed as curious about me as I was of it.
