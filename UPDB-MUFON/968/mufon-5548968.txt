Spotted Bright Object with trail of light from the bottom

I was in Wareham with family and friends sitting on the porch facing Northeast. My wife spotted a bright object in the sky that was different than the stars. It was oblong in appearance and I am pretty sure it was off in the distance quite a bit. It appeared to move quickly left and then back right. It was not blinking like a plane, but it was obviously not a star. It "pulsed" brighter a few times and then a beam of light projected from under the bright spot three times. The object became brighter and then it just disappeared.
