First viewed moving with a small white blinking light and a big solid green and red light

I was walking to the Mac's convenience store just entering a schools field to cut across to get to the store. I looked up and behind me and noticed the object moving slow with no sound. I thought is was a plane but the green and red light were too big and solid(not flashing) and I could see in the night sky the shape of the craft was triangular in nature. I could see the silhouette of the craft. I pointed at it and almost instantly the green light turned off; it was just the small white light blinking and the big solid red light. As soon as that happened I said in a whisper voice "I see you !!" and pointed at it adamantly(the hairs on the back of my neck stood up and on my whole body) Almost immediately it gave of a white transparent force field ripple effect that did not go far from the craft itself. I felt sick at that moment and looked away walking towards to store. I looked back and it was gone.
