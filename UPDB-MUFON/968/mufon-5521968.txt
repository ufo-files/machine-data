Large orange light moving across night sky, curved slightly and then came to a stop and hovered, then slowly dimmed and disappeared.

I was just out getting my mail last night (Tues 12/20/11)at 7:15pm and saw this weird orange thing flying up in the sky - it was strange because the light was larger than a plane, and it flickered like a flame. I was moving across the sky, make a slight curve and then it stopped - which really got me interested, because planes don't do that! It stayed stationary in the sky for a few seconds and then it slowly started to dim, until it finally disappeared completely. I stood waiting for a while to see if it would reappear but it didn't - it was just the dark night sky with no trace of it. BTW - it was a completely clear night (no clouds at all) and stars were visible. I had never seen this kind of movement in the sky and thought it must be an alien spacecraft. I was not afraid or anything, and even tried to talk to it when it had stopped - asking - can you see me?
