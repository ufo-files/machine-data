{jpg} Appeared triangular and black/invisable with lower moving lights and static upper 3 lights.

I was sent the footage via 'whatsapp' on my mobile, approximately 1 hour after the event had been recorded. I was sent the footage by the mother of the man (approximately 21 years old) who recorded it on his mobile phone. Therefore, I do not own this footage but know the person who filmed it. With the owner's mother's permission, I uploaded this and an edited (slightly brightened) version to YouTube. Therefore, this footage is now in the public domain. [Link moved to FI Section/cms/tg]

I was told by his mother (I never spoke to him) that his phone's battery was only at 1%, hence the shortness of the footage. However, I was informed that a few minutes after shooting the footage, the stationary object shot up into the black night sky and vanished at high-speed. Sadly, that part was not filmed due to the mobile phone battery dying.

No sound was heard at all from the object and the park where the young man was, was extremely quiet also. So, if the object had made a sound, the man suspects he would have been able to hear it.
