Glowing V-shaped Objects

On 10/20/11 Myself, a family member and two neighbors were standing outside talking in our driveway when we all witnessed a object flying Southwest from the south Sarasota area to Miami in direction that was shaped as a flock of birds. However, birds do not migrate south in the dark. The object also had a green/yellow/white glow almost like a kids glow in the dark stick. We looked around for a local spotlight since Halloween is approaching and maybe a store or business was advertising. Nothing! Then a second object approached in the same direction, same glowing color, traveling at the same speed, seemed to be at the same altitude. They were just one minute appart and were out of sight within a minute. They were moving fast and steady. Too fast for a flock of radioactive birds, glowing in the dark. The best way to discribe the shape is a flock of migrating ducks in a perfect v-shape formation. There was no sound coming from the flying objects.
