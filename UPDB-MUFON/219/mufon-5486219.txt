{dr}2 Hovering and interacting over train crossing

I was on my way to pick my girlfriend up from work teusday the 29th of december 2015 6:00pm. As i approached her work i was stopped due to an amtrack train passing by, but as i approached the crossing (1 block away) i noticed an object moving very quickly with a rapidly flashing light that caught my attention. my first thought was maybe it was a drone, but As I got closer and came to a stop i could see that it was rather large(4ft or so) and much too fast and erratic in its flight to be any drone easily available for public. Then as the train started to pass another object, similar in flight characteristics but different in design (this one had appendges). They seemed to interact almost playfully hovering and almost touching while the train passed, during this time i got several pictures. As the train was gone (a few min.) they each shot off in different directions rather quickly and abruptly. a block away i pulled in to the lot where i waited for my girlfriend i noticed one of the objects hovering about 100+ft in the air near me with a solid light . The past month have made me a serious believer in ufo phenomena as there has beein increasing intensity of sightings so I wasnt totally freaked out but This was the closest I have gotten and documented.Later That night i got up to use the bathroom and noticed A large very bright apparition through my window about 75ft in the air, it hovered for around 5 minutes and slowly moved out of sight in a linear path. i took photos of this as well.
