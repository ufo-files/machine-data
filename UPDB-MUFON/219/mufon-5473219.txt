{dr}Two lights moved in opposite directions at high speed

October 6 2017. Los Angeles CA. Burbank. Object(s) observed sometime between 20:49-20:50.  I was driving my vehicle on Hollywood Way in Burbank heading southeast. At or near the intersection of Hollywood Way and Chandler Bike path. Looked into the sky at the exact moment that, either two objects were crossing each other at a very close distance OR one object had split into two and the two objects moved away from each other at a incredibly high rate of speed. I witnessed the objects(looking straight at them for 3-8 seconds before they were completely out of my range of vision and not within view of the horizon. This street has lots of trees on the sides but as I drove down the street I could see in-between them and was never able to see the objects again on either side. I have never seen a recreational drone move at speeds like that and I estimate whatever it was was 2000-5000 feet up in the air but I really don't know for sure. This area has BOB hope airport near by and I see planes fly by all the time. I have never seen anything in the sky move this fast. The objects were too low to be considered shooting stars was my first initial thought due to the high speed but odd appearance..  The objects had a solid white star appearance and I THINK I saw some kind of flash but NEVER saw the tell-tale green/red lights.
