At 7pm I was visiting with family when suddenly my mother noticed 8 what looked like white balls floating perfectly still. The sun was setting and the objects were in yellowish sunset, on the western horizon. I immediate began to take pictures and film the objects. There were a total of 5 of us who saw these objects.
