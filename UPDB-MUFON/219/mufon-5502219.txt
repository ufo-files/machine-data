Starlike pulsating bright light

As I stepped unto my doorstep I noticed an unusual bright light at 25 degrees NE. It was moving across the sky about the same speed as a satelite however it continually pulsated from nearly blinking out to a very bright light and moved towards the NW. As it moved straight about me I lost sight of it. I mentioned the sighting to Stan Friedman who said it didn't appear to be a satelite and he had no idea what it was. It was not the ISS as it was not in the same orbit.
