{td} A white oval shaped object that without sound traveled quietly moved in the orning sky toward an existing rainbow in the west. It disappeared behind clouds.

On May 30, 2018, I was admiring a partial rainbow in the west as the morning sun was rising up. The rainbow was not complete so I went to see if I could see the other section that would be on the ground but did not see one. Instead, my attention was caught by a illumines white orb in the sky, leaving nothing behind it and no sound. It quietly moved ssw to sw toward the rainbow. I wanted to call my roommate outside to witness this but I did not have my phone with me and it would have taken him too long to come outside to see it before it was over. It lasted about 30 seconds and slowly disappeared behind the clouds.
