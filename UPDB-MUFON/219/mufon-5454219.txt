{tjd} Sitting in car looking at night sky and saw three lights in a triangle.

I was sitting in my car at the now empty lot which was the old Raley's Supermarket. I was with my girlfriend at the time, who was inside the store picking up her friend. I sat there looking out the drivers side window of my car when I noticed lights in the sky. I had to look away a bit to and then look closer at them to focus my eyes, but, there were three lights in a perfect triangle. The craft . . . or whatever it was . . . flew from the North East to the South West. That's as must detail as I can remember about the experience.
I remember about Ten years later on the show 'Sightings' they should what some people saw in Belgium. They showed the picture of the craft they saw. It was the same one I saw.
