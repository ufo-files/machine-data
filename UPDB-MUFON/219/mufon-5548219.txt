square, white luminescent, divided like window

Standing under a tree at a car show, I noticed a white light filling most of the spaces in a tree above me(1). as it passed the tree into view it was a large square appearing 2 dimensional above the lit parking lot. It was divided into 4 sections by a thin partition seeming non luminescent like a window frame(2). The object glowed like an x-ray viewing board. Because it appeared 2 dimensional its size was not immediately apparent, however it was on a steady heading at a steady speed(3). Its course was heading from Oxford to Trumbull along route 111. At this time it was apparently 4 to 5 times the tree level and the area of a two football fields traveling between 50 and 100mph. I was still able to see it as it diminished to a dot on the horizon(4). Until now I have had no place worth reporting this incident too, aside from a few classified add looking for others who saw it. I do believe in Mufon's purpose after having viewed the sight and I hope this report can fill in a blank somewhere.
