Several blinks of red and white then I turned and took a 1 sec photo with my Canon camera on a tripod, later I saw the RAW CR2 file and it had 13 or so stationary ships

I was taking star photos with my camera then a flash of white light caught my left eye but I ignored it as this has heavy aerial traffic from the airport several miles away. Then about a few seconds later a bright flash of white light along with a flash red light that was very intense caught my eye so I turned and saw a vehicle standing still with bright orange red light like an led strip on its side. So I then turned immediately and took a 1 sec photo with my Canon EOS REBEL T3i camera on a weighted down tripod with a remote trigger.

The next day I saw the Canon CR2 RAW file and it had 13 or so stationary ships within it. I lightened it in Photoshop to see more detail of the other ships and saved several images in PNG format to share with people. After sending it to several my subscribed YouTube sites I got zero response. That was strange so I made my own YouTube video to show people all that I had seen that evening.
