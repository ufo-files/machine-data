Object slowly moves across sky as if it has nowhere to go

I was in my front yard and I was getting ready to go back inside after playing catch with my young brother. I'm not sure what made me look up onto the horizon. Immediately after I saw the object, I ran inside and grabbed my binoculars to get a closer look, but I still could not tell what the object was. From the angle I was seeing it, I can only describe it as a long cigar-shaped object with ends that curve downwards , or a saucer-shaped object rotating on a plane parallel to my angle of vision. It slowly lumbered across the starless night sky. I just kept thinking to my self "that is something interesting." it wasn't until afterwards did I start to think that maybe that was a UFO. I finally lost sight after about five minutes when the object move too low in the sky for my to see. This happened five-six years ago so my details are little fuzzy.
