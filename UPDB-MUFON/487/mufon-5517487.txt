{ck} spherical object, glowing a bright orange/red

My girlfriend and our daughter and me all went out into the street in front of our house to see the fireworks numerous people were setting off. We noticed a bright red/orange glow in the sky moving in a line, not up or down. We though maybe it had something to do with the fireworks but the object after about 5 minutes was still there and still moving. it was very low, below the cloud cover. At one point it vanished while we were watching it and we assumed it was something else. Then about 2 minutes later we watched it reappear further in the distance of the direction it was heading. It then turned and started going in the opposite direction. Then it again disappeared and we did not see it again.
