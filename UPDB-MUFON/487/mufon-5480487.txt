{nd} Reflective object in sky that disappeared out of thin air

I was just finishing up my morning walk and looked up to see what I thought was a plane. What I noticed different about this "plane" was that as far away as it was, it looked like it had "tilted" and it was a very bright reflective metal. I have never seen a plane do this. Then it went back to its original position when I first looked. I then looked down and less than 5 seconds later I looked back up and it was nowhere. There is a beautiful, clear blue sky today. No clouds. No other planes. That sky is way too big for a "plane" to just fly out of there that quick. Where did it go to?
