{cak} Large ball of light appeared suddenly, descended quickly and disappeared in sky

Shortly after 9 p.m. I was walking westward on the northwest side of Chicago. In the southwestern sky, at about 45 degrees, I suddenly saw a large oval ball of blue-white light in the distance many, many miles away. It appeared suddenly, quickly descended angling slightly northward but falling almost directly downward. At about 30 degrees it completely disappeared leaving no trace. I searched the same general area of the sky for several minutes afterward and saw nothing more.
