(GS) glowing star bottom corner of big dipper

im not sure i would call this a ufo sighting. it was as the stars were coming out, maybe 8:20/30. 7-31-12. i was looking at what i would in a few minutes identify as the star at the bottom of the big dipper. i would describe it as the intense glowing of this particular star, just as it was beginning to appear. It was as if the light of a super-nova finally arrived, and was seen for a few seconds, briefly glowing brighter than anything in the sky, except for the rising full moon to the east. the dipper was to the northwest. 

i admit to being a bit of a ufo skeptic, but the frind i was visiting told me someone else witnessed this and to check your site. it was a beautiful thing to see. i didn't see any reports to confirm what i claim to have seen. not that it matters.
