{cak} After taking a picture of a passing plane on way to near base, observed an orb in photo above the plane.

I was working outside and looked up to see a passing plane on its way to Lockheed Martin in Lancaster, Ca. This is a usual sighting as planes frequently pass overhead due to the bases. For some reason I wanted to take a photo of the plane passing and I did so. It was not until I went back and checked the photo I snapped with my phone, that I could clearly see a Large Bright Glowing Orb which appeared to be above the passing plane. The Sun can be seen in the Bottom Middle section of the photo with the plane to the top of the photo and the UFO right above it almost like it was passing horizontal to the plane. I did not see this object with my eyes when focusing on the plane for the photo. I did not have a chance to search for it in the sky after since I unfortunately waited to check the photo after work at home. Regardless I am excited and Amazed at this event and hope to see more sightings soon.
