Double light observed

I was looking up admiring how clear the stars were that night (it is rarely clear where I live as it's an industrial area and with plenty of light polution) then as my eyes adjusted to the stars I observed two parallel lights, fainter than the stars and slightly hazey as if being seen through cloud even though there was none. They travelled west at an immense speed (I could tell it was quick as there were planes further away which seemed lower but were going incredibly slower).They snaked through the sky rather than zig-zagged, left, then right, then back onto it's original path with incredibe propulsion and manueverability, this thing was like a fly wizzing over and the whole thing only lasted 2 to 3 seconds, then as it sped towards the horizon it vanished.
