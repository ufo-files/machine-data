Brush Fire UFO Observation

On 9 April 2021 at approximately 8:00pm EST, I went into my back yard to take a picture of the smoke filled air surrounding our neighborhood that was caused by a brush fire that was burning about a mile or so from our home. I took the picture to send to my sisters who were concerned about the proximity of the fire to us.

When I took the picture I just sent it from my iPhone without paying much attention to what else was revealed. I didn't notice the object until I was showing the picture to a friend of mine a couple of weeks later.

There are actually 4 objects in the picture, but 3 of them are obscured by the smoke and are hovering at a higher altitude. The one closest in the frame clearly shows a row of tail lights on what appears to be a triangular shaped craft.

There are stadium field lights in foreground as our home sits at the edge of county ball field. The object is hovering a good distance above those lights. The tail lights of the object are bright enough to cut through the smoke in the air.

Take a look at the picture and let me know what you think.
