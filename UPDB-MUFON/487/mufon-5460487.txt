{tjd} Witness to moving sphere traveling over water and make turn just before reaching our campsite...

My wife and I were camping on the Northwest side of Cooke Pond for the weekend with a friend. The camp site is located right on the water with a clear view (approx. 2 miles) up stream (Southwest). Approximately 9:30 pm. just after dusk while sitting at the camp fire I notice a light coming straight at us approximately 3/4 a mile away. I pointed out said light to my wife and friend. Object appeared to be a sphere about the size of a basketball with a soft white/yellowish light equivalent to a 60 watt light bulb. Object's speed was estimated at approximately 15-20 mph at approximately 150 feet above the water.  Once object came withing approximately 1000 feet of our site it made a turn of about 35 degrees to continue down stream and maintain its position over the water. Object made no noise. Conditions at the time were clear with very little wind. About an hour later just before going to bed we noticed over beyond the tree line to the north, north-west we seen very bright flashes in the sky lighting intermittently for approximately 5 minutes.
