{rjl} Took cell phone photos and a video of a bright white feature in the night sky. I did not think that it was a star.

I have noticed a bright feature in the sky in Columbia, MD for several years. I did not think that it was a star because it is in the same general vicinity.

On the night of August 31, 2019, I decided to take some photos with my cell phone. I set the camera to 4X. When I held the phone up to take the photo, the bright white image in the sky was quickly moving around and I thought that it was due to the action my. Since my hand was holding the phone stable, I started taking photos. I took 7 images. One was a video.

In viewing the images, I expanded them to get a closer view. The configuration of the objects were different in each of the 7 images. The  images were taken seconds apart.
