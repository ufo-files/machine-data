{tjd} Ascending light.

At approx 0800 26th October 18. I exited Seward hwy @ Tudor. Facing north White circular light caught my eye. The light was at approx 500ft altitude and traveling straight up. At first I thought it was a drone. My brain said drone..as I was thinking drone. I noticed the white light and blue aura and thought "flare or tracer" I was expecting to see missile shape attached to light but did not..I tracked the object visually for about 2-3 seconds until it shot through cloud cover. It was moving at a high rate. Faster than a missile , it was at max speed instantly. I sat at green light with cars honking behind me, thinking WTF was that. 
In hope someone else saw it or knows what it was. 

I watch F-22s, F-16s daily operating out of JBER. Speed of object was much much greater than anything I've seen in our skies
