two small craft flying in tandem, third craft flew in different direction.

On 2/6/2012 at approxamitely 1843 hrs, I went outside to catch a breath of fresh air. As soon as I opened the front door two (2) small craft were flying slowly in tandem head in a north westerly direction at a slow rate of speed. As I watched them move away a third circlular vehicle much larger then the first two was also flying from the same position but headed in a southerly direction. As my attention was directed toward this craft its brightly illuminated shape began to dim until its light was completely out. This only took about 2-5 seconds. It should also be pointed out that the third craft appeared to stationary when first noticed.

The first two crafts seem to have multible lights and there was a blinking strobe on them, but none could be seen on the third craft.
