{cak} Bright orange light in the sky in odd flight pattern. Then split into multiple lights and shot downward

We were standing outside my friend's house late at night in Peoria, AZ. We saw this bright orange light in the sky that had a strange flight pattern. It seemed to move east, then west, then north and would repeat very slowly. Then 2 other orange lights slowly emerged out of the main one we were observing on the left and right side of it. It seemed to just hover in place for a couple minutes and then all 3 lights show down 3 more orange lights towards the ground. They were very fast and made no noise. My friends and I got scared, so we got in the car and started driving away. I dropped my friend off at his house 1 or 2 miles east of where we saw the lights. When we got out the car and looked in the sky we could see the 3 orange lights slowly moving east and then they disappeared.
