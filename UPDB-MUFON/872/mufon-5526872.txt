two events same night. Stationary star like object starts moving north & two intersecting star like objects.

stationary star like object - starts moving north, very faint light coming from object not blinking just steady light looked to be very high altitude craft. Once it started moving we noticed a very bright beam of light came out of craft like a spotlight for the duration of about five sec. then back to low light and came to a stop far off. Then after about 2 min. the craft got brighter & looked as if it was going straight up into space until we couldnt see it anymore or the light could have just got bright and then demed out. about ten min. later we saw two star like objects steady light not blinking intersec, they looked like they were actually going to hit eachother. these were also at very high altitude. they crossed pathes at same time like they were going to hit and we watched them until they faded out of view. we always seem to see this activity close to or right after the eight oclock hour pm.
