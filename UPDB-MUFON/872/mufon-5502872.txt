{nd} Heard jets ran to window 2 hovering between towers N. severn naval towers 3rd met at site hovered rocketed away

My girl & I were in separate rooms after an argument. We both heard fighter jets and ran to our balcony; we both saw 2 UFO's hovering between the submarine communication towers at N. Severn naval base. A third light arrived to meet the two and hovered a few seconds and the 3 shot off at a staggering speed. The towers are clearly visible from our balcony. We both agreed that there was no doubt that the jets were attempting to approach crafts. It was a no brainer.
