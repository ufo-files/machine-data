Witnessed unusual lights in the sky started filming then they disappeared.

I walked out onto my back porch at about 10:30pm to a skyline I see every night. Immediately I noticed these 2 lights that were strange and out of place they weren't moving and their shapes were odd. Kind of like a 2 huge diamond shapes. I grabbed my phone and started filming. I pan around to show the lights, then to Cheyenne Mountain which are the Red blinking lights. The objects never moved. After a couple minutes one faded away then the other.
