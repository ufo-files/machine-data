{cak} Orange Disc about 2000 feet in altitude.

At exactly 9:45 PM, I was walking my dog in the open field on a moon less night when I noticed an orange disc approaching from the North at a slow rate of speed. The disc would have been the size of a dime at arms length. The disc disappeared for a second and then re-appeared a second later. And then it changed directions 90 degrees due west ... moved about a 1000 feet in that due west direction and shot out of sight over the Massanutten Mountain range. I would guess it to be approximately 45 degrees above the horizon.
