A long bright white object

My girl friend and I was taking our RV in to be worked on she was driving my truck and I was driving the RV. She had to drop of the garbage and was going to meet me at the store at the end of our RV park. I was sitting there waiting for her in front of the store at the entrance of I-60 when I looked up (at about 10 O’clock when I noticed what looked like a vaper trail from an airplane. At that time all the other trails were going North to South but this one was going East to West and much higher than all the others. But what got my attention was when I looked in my side mirror and looked back it was gone then I looked in my mirror again to see if my girlfriend was behind me yet and I looked back it was there again, now it had my attention. I kept watching it and after about a minute it disappeared again but I kept watching and sure enough it reappeared as I watched a second one stared to appear but before it came all the way into view it disappeared. It disappeared again I was going to tell my girlfriend to look if it came back but after a couple minutes it didn’t so I pulled out.
A couple things I noticed was the shape, the best way to describe it is it was shaped like a aluminum foil box, the ends were blunt and not tapering of like a vaper trail also it was a bright white I mean really white much whiter than a vaper trail.
