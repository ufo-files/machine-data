{nd} Group of a orange, star-like lights in the sky, made different formations

Six of us (4 adults & 2 kids) were sitting in the living room talking. The living room faces an outdoor, screened patio. My husband saw something outside and brought it to our attention. All 6 of us went onto the patio and observed a group of orange/reddish lights in the sky. We all knew it wasnt normal, but we spent a few minutes debating what they could be, but we knew, and I was excited as hell! There were more than 10 lights, they changed formation a few times and then slowly disappeared like matches being put out.
