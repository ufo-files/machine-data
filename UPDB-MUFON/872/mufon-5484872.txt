So I'm in upstate bfe Indiana. Obviously with the nearest city I listed. Anyway, I am on a hill overlooking a big field with a main roadway for that area passing through the back quadrant of this field. There was a tree line another 50-100 meters beyond the road. The ground also inclined from the road to the tree line 30-40 degrees. So my view has got to be from left to right a good 600 meters.It's a clear early morning and I am just looking out over this field when an enormous shadow engulfed me and subsequently across the field. It made me look up. I thought, that's a low flying craft. There was nothing there. The shadow it cast covered the entire ground. As the shadow moved I kept looking up and down. I still cannot get over the size of the shadow but then I caught a glimpse of the outline of a craft. But it looks transparent. Then it vanished over the tree line. It was slow moving and silent. I still to this day cannot get over the size. It's taken a while for me to really believe in my own mind what I seen, I seen. It somehow bent the light, sky, or projected the image above to the bottom of the craft. But at the right angle it was like heat mirage. I'm no crazy nor am I a [removed/CMS/jpg] looking for some kind of notoriety. The thought of a craft of such enormity that in not only silent but cloaked? It opens a doors to many eye opening possibilities. Makes me think of how humans take a cruise or a drive to the zoo. They must look down on us as we do chimps digging worms out of the dirt. No foil helmets here just wanted to tell my story.
