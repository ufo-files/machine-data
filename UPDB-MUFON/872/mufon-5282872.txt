Lights appear suddenly as white, then immediately begin to dim to yellow, float horizontally for 3-4 seconds, then fade and disappear.

After seeing multiple lights appear, dim, then dissappear in the night sky east of Quartzite, Az, I watched yellowish lights rise slowly into the sky and fade away. The yellowish lights slowly lifted into the sky, some drifting to the south, others going straight up, rose to about the level of the mountain Ridgeline seen in the distance, before dimming down to nothing. They rose up from multiple locations, spread out over several miles mostly single lights, occasionally a pair would rise together. This event began a little before 8 pm and continued till I went to bed around midnight. I saw around 30 lights appear and dissappear over the course of the event, hovering, floating, drifting and rising, lasting for between 5 seconds to maybe 3 minutes. Around 11 pm I watched 1 light rise up and hover for well over an hour, it slowly drifted right to left, back and forth, up and down, brighter and dimmer. 
This was the 3rd consecutive night of seeing these lights east of Quartzite, the first 2 nights I only saw 2-3 lights and they were gone within seconds.
