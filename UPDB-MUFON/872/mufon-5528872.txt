Unidentified object observed in upper left portion of photo of Circumhorizontal Arc after photo was downloaded and blown up on computer screen.

I was standing on my back porch with other members of my family observing a beautiful circumhorizontal arc to the South of our house when I decided to take a picture to e'mail it to friends. I took a few pictures, deleted the ones I didn't like, and downloaded the one I did to my computer days later. When I blew up the photo on my computer screen I notice what looked like a small ball in the sky to the upper left; I thought at first it was a glitch in my camera's pixels since my camera is old (Sony DSC-P31, 2.0 Mega Pixel); but the object cast an undershadow, and also seamed to be reflecting sunlight. I guessed from what I saw on screen that if object was real it must have been about 300 ft. in the air, and about a 1 mile South. Hope you enjoy looking over this photo (and the circumhorizontal arch), and my two zooms - the second was done by color heightening: interesting how the object takes on an orange color like in the arc.
