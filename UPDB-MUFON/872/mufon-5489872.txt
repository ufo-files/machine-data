I was working in the oilfield the graveyard shift. I was looking up at the sky when I saw this big single light coming towards my direction. I did not have a camera. I only had my I Pad. By the time I went to retrieve it there were two lights then three. It started to circle the gas well. Then it just hovered for a short while. Then all of a sudden between the front and the middle of this object it started shooting this brown stuff. I don't know what it was but I don't know how to explain it just take a look.
