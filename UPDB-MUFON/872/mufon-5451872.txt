Pulsating light traveling from North to South

Observed one pulsating star like light traveling from north to south in a straight line at the approximate speed of an airliner. Light would become extremely bright, and then fade completely out. This repeated several times over the course of approximately a span of 2 minutes until the object was out of range of sight.
