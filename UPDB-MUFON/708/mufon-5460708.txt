{rjl} several bright flashes followed by brief satellite-like appearance then disappear

I watched that area closely, few seconds later another flash, and then a satellite-dim light for a half an inch headed north. I've seen this before, but only one light each sighting, most recently a month ago, and last fall, late at night, north and northwest. I've seen a couple flashes years ago, followed by very fast half inch light quickly disappearing. Separate single light sightings.
