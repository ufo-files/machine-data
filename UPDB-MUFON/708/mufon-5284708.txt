Before airshow hovered 20+ minutes above airfield above commercial traffic

At 3 pm blue angles were to preform , delayed , on lounge chair with wife we noticed a bald eagle and a few eagles in area , looking higher up we noticed an orb, looked thru binoculars it reflected sun light , looked metal like.
  It left ( dident notice it leave ) the airshow started I looked for it , it was gone . I filmed using my iPhone on slow motion and regular and took pictures. After going home I watched what I took and it showed up, it was buzzing past the planes in formation twice on film with sun to back. Once on slow motion so obvious and once on regular filming . It was not sun glare , I know the films I took , can tell the difference, the object was dark then light as if it was propulsion of some type in bursts , not like a jet .
  I know it’s nothing like our technology, I worked on helicopters 17 years in the national guard , this is not our technology.
