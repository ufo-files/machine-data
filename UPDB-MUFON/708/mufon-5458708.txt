{rjl} UFO With Bright lights hovering low in the sky

Me and my mother spotted this UFO hovering over a state park in Milford, CT at about 8:35pm on August 18th, 2019. The object was a flat diamond shape with green and red blinking lights, 1 light in each corner of the diamond shaped ship, which means there were 4 bright red and green colored lights, as well as what looked to be some kind of flame that a rocket would have in the back to power the UFO. We were driving past it in the car and I looked back and it took off in a heartbeat.
