{cak} Was a security guard with krypto clearance

I worked for a private security company. I was 18 with high level krypto security armed at an electronic company in Miami. My job was to report anything that was unusual.. back then security guards carried a clock and whenever you did you rounds in the building and the other buildings to put the key in the check station. I finished the building I was stationed in. I had to walk across the parking lot to the next building. Lots of trees. I worked the overnight shift. I came out of my assigned building to tut the key at other points in the next building. I was half way across the lot when I had this chill that something was watching me. I looked up and there was this huge saucer shaped object with lights all around it hovering over the tree tops. I was under those trees but I knew I wasn’t seeing things. I stood there looking up at it and couldn’t move. Then it took off. No noise, nothing. I then ran back to my building. When my shift was over I washed my uniform and gave it back to my boss. He said it’s not the first time this has happened. I looked into things once we had the internet and looked up the company I was guarding. They did a lot of government work. I think this ship was there to collect government information. I kinda feel foolish writing this but fell better that I’ve finally shared. Only my ex-husband, my son and my best friend know. People think you’re loony. I know exactly what I saw that night like it was yesterday.
