{tjd} It was a glowing orb, moving pretty fast.

Went out on the back stoop at my work (Auburn Correctional Facility) to smoke a cigarette. I was star gazing as I usually do, when I noticed a bright light traveling east to west. I tried to make sense of it, thinking it may be a bug flying around in the light pollution or a plane flying over. After I thought about it for a for awhile. I came to the conclusion it could not have been a bug because they don't move in straight lines get that bright or move that fast. The reason I don't believe it was a plane it made no noise and the light was not blinking.
