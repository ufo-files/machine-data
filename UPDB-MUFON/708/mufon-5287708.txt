Multiple glowing lights appearing multiple times from left to right and restarted sequence over and over - possible drones?

Was on out boat docked at the marina late at night when I noticed a flash of light appear in my line of sight. I alerted my wife who turned around to look and we saw the same multiple objects appear over and over. Sometimes we would see 3-4 lights while other times we would only see 1-2 lights. When the lights appeared they traveled from left to right horizontally. Seemed the objects were shining a beam of light outwards in the sky and when the object turned away from us the light disappeared. Unfortunately it was a cloudy night and the beams of lights were shining through the cloud cover. The event went on for 6 minutes. It took us a minute after the first sighting to get the camera going. We suspect these may have been drones but flying multiple drones over the lake on a very cloudy night seems risky as one may lose track of a drone flying in the clouds. If they were drones they would have to circle back quickly to reappear at the same location in the sky after each pass.
