{tjd} Disc shaped object pulsating and traveling very fast in different directions.

Arrived at work at about 0515 and was the only person in the parking lot. I got out of my car at 05:20 to head into work. When I got out of my car for some reason something in the sky caught my attention. I looked up and witnessed a disc shaped object traveling south very quickly then came to an abrupt stop and hovered. It then quickly shot West then stopped and hovered for roughly 15 seconds, then shot east and hovered for roughly 15 seconds, then shot North towards my direction and stopped and hovered for 10 seconds, then shot east and disappeared in the blink of an eye. The object was relatively bright and pulsated with a red/orange yellowish color. The object seemed to be a grayish black color. I do work at a facility that has tons of acres of land and is generally a no fly zone. We do see some aircraft but usually really high. When I first saw the object I felt a strong attachment to it as in I was drawn to it and felt as if I almost had to stare at it. My first thought was something extraterrestrial because of its movements. This was my experience.
