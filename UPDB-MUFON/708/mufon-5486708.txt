{nd} Traveling West on Interstate 20/59 near the Mercedes Manufacturing plant outside Tuscaloosa we oberserved a bright, white light at 45 degrees above the horizon. It broke as one smaller piece climbed and the larger stayed near level

Tonight. in a well lighted industrial area traveling on I 20/59 near the Mercedes Manufacturing Plant near Tuscaloosa, Alabama, my wife and I observed a bright, white light across the Western Horizon. It moved from South to North, breaking into a smaller ascending diamond, with the larger piece staying near level. The event lasted 1-2 seconds. Personally, I've seen shooting stars or meteors all my life ( I can remember seeing Sputnik in 1957), but this was different from anything I have ever observed. As it broke across the horizon the light was so very bright (nothing could be observed normally because of all the light pollution and heavy Interstate traffic in the area), but this light seemed strangely white- like phosphorous. I shall never forget it. My wife reported feeling strangely afraid after the incident.
