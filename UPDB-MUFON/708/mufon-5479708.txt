{nd} Observed object with multiple flashing lights hovering and zigzagging

My mom called me outside at 6pm to look at an object in the sky. I came out to see an object with flashing lights hovering in the sky behind our house. The lights appear to be red, white, blue, and green. After a few months of observation, the object began to dip and rise, then hover, then zigzag, then hover again. So far the object has been hovering in the same location for 1.5 hours and is still in the sky. It is too dark to record a proper video, unfortunately.
