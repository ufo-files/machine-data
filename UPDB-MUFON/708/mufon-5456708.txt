I saw a straight line of lights travelling slowly across the sky from my bedroom window.

I saw a line of over 10 star like lights travelling slowly across the sky in a perfectly straight line apart from the final one which was slightly askew. They were travelling towards a line of 112 degrees approximately. I see this view from my bedroom window every morning and I have never seen anything like this before. The lights were very high in the sky because I also saw a plane and this was much closer. I tried to film this but they were just too far away.
