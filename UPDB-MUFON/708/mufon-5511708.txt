{dr}Starlike object twinkled moved stopped then took off unbelievable speed

I was waiting on a ride to go to work at exactly 5 30 Friday morning when I was looking up at the Big Dipper when a moving star caught my eye. At first I was just thinking it might be a sattalite but then it stopped. It twinkled like a star then stopped moving,it twinkled again then took off at amazing speed toward the same direction it was heading before it stopped. There was no sound no vapor trail . Nothing I have ever seen has moved like that. I know I saw it it had to be in space because it was so little like the surrounding stars
