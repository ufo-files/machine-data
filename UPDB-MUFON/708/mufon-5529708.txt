Star like light which grew brighter and larger.

On september 11, 2010 at aproximately 11 am my wife and I were driving home from the hardware store heady southward on Amirault st in dieppe when we noticed a tiny star like light far away in the sky to the south. Within a few seconds it became very bright and much larger, about 1/4 to 3/8 inch in diameter. The object looked to be over Nova Scotia. It didn't appear to be moving at all then the object started to dim and appeared to be turning on the spot as we could see a shadow on the right side which made it appear to be long or cigar shaped it dimmed completely and turned into a tiny black dot and moved away what seemed like an inch , but from the distance we observed it ,that would be quite a distance. And moved very quickly
