{rjl} 3:45-4:30am approximately 15 ascending orbs one by one west of Marina Del Rey, Ca.

From 3:45-4:30am I witnessed approximately 15 ascending orbs a few miles west of Marina Del Rey, Ca heading due East and.spaced equally 1 every 3 minutes from the exact same location, only becoming visible at 10 degrees of the horizon as a red orb until finally disappearing at 40 degrees off the horizon. Orbs did not fly a straight line path but slowly meandered upward then they disappeared one by one in roughly the same location of the sky. The orbs were following each other and were obviously under intelligent control. I estimate their maximum speed at 200-300 MPH while visible. Orbs appeared to rise out of the ocean within a few miles of Marina Del Rey, Ca based on their near vertical direction of travel when illuminated as orbs at about 2,000 feet up. Skies were clear and winds were calm at that altitude with unlimited visibility. Orbs made no sound at all and the orb cloud or mist appeared to slightly change shape as the objects made slight directional changes like a candle in the wind. Orbs distance from me was approximately 10 miles minimum.
