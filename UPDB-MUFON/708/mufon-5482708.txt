{rjl} Orb above weapons qualification course. Set stationary then moved to the west and then disapeared. Dull crome.

On June 7th at approx. 11:00am on the weapons zero and qualification range I spotted a dull chrome round object in the North sky. The object hovered above our location for approximately 20 minutes. During that time at approx. 15 minutes it moved 6 miles to the west. After about 5 more minutes in its new location it simply disappeared. I an 7 to 8 other soldiers witnessed this object.
