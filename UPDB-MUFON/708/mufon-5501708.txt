{rjl} While on my patio to smoke a cigar, I witnessed this bright light

I stepped out on my patio to smoke a cigar as I usally do. The lights outside were not on because I like to look at the stars. As I tilted my head back to relax my neck I saw a very bright light traveling from the North to the Southern direction, toward Searchlight area.
The object made no noise, no humming or buzzing sound, or any noise at all. The light which was the only thing that I could clearly see appeared to be large and it made me think of a police helicopter light with No Beam emmitting from it. I tried hetting pixtires of the object with my smart phone made it hard to aim directly at the object. I never made a big deal of this because I figured I was probably the only one to see it, it turns out other got video of it. Good!
