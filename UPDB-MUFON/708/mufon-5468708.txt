{td} I saw a light zig zagging and maneuvering at incredible speed

I noticed an object with blinking lights at an incredibly high rate of speed and zig zagging through the sky. It would stop abruptly and then accelerate covering large distances. It also went straight up. Please see video.

I was standing in my driveway facing west when I first saw it. When I last saw it, it was in the eastern sky. It basically travelled north from me in an arc around my position.

I first noticed the object due to the pulsating lights and the erratic movements. It also was traveling incredibly fast.

I honestly did not know what the object was, and I still have no idea. I am confident that it was not an airplane or helicopter or blimp or any other flying object that I've ever seen. 

While witnessing the object, I was very calm. I just watched it. It was very high in the sky appearing to be at an altitude like an airplane. I was not fearful or anxious. Afterward, I became generally more excited as it started to dawn on me what I had just witnessed. 

I lost sight of the object after it traveled out of sight in the eastern sky beyond my line of sight.
