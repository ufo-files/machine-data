White rotating and glowing unknown sphere into room

This event happened 6 years ago and I am absolutely not sure of what really happened that day and if even related to UFO. But after all these years of silence I thought it might be helpful to share and eventually discard the UFO possibility. I apologize for my English level which is not my mother tongue.
I was sleeping at my parent's in their country house in Saint-Armand (Québec) border with Vermont. I was alone in the second floor. My sleep is usually very deep. I then woke up and turned around my head on the right (heading West) and saw a rotating sphere illuminated very close to me, in the room and flying a little bit above the height of the bed. It was silent and beautiful. I didn't feel afraid. I received information from a way I didn't know before (sort of telepathic way...) I turned my head away from it (toward South) and then I saw something that looked like a stingray, very vaporous. The sphere was gone and then the ray was gone. Window was not opened as far as I remember. The whole event might have felt like a minute or so in usual time but for some reason when the ray was gone I felt that this event had happened in another time frame. I felt the clock had not move since happening. But I could never verify this feeling as I could never verifiy or understand what just happened. This never happened to me again.
