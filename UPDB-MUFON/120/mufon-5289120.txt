UFO in Sebastian, White Capsule ejecting orbs

White capsule shaped cloud color cloaked ufo hovering for about 15 seconds. Then 4 to 5 round sphere objects of same color (but not shape) were ejected out of the side of the main craft 1 @ a time in approximately 1-2 second intervals. After all the spheres were ejected the capsule shape craft pulled away banking somewhat left then right and was gone in an instant. The 4-5 orbs remained there stationary for a few more seconds but by the time I got to my phone to try and photograph and look up again, the objects had disappeared. I did not witness them pull away they were just gone when I looked up again and was ready for photo.
