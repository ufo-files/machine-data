red lights moving across the sky

In early May 1976, I was 16 and sitting on the grass around 10pm enjoying the night sky. Looking Se I saw a bright red light slowly moving North. After a minute the light split to 2 red lights, flying at the same altitude, same direction. Then the second light split and became 3 red lights, again same direction, same altitude. I could hear no noise. There was no flashing, just a steady red light. As it moved above the horizon, the last red light merged in the middle light and only 2 lights now. After a minute or so, the second light merged with the first and became a single light again. Still moving at the same speed, same altitude. Then suddenly the last red light sped up and disappeared in the NE sky. All the while, no sound. the whole event lasted around 5 minutes.
