Strang light/line appears in the sky above the fire

Last week I saw 2 very bright orbs above the Serra de Monchique, moving very slowly. I made a report also on MUFON because it was very weird.

At the exact place were last week the orbs were. I don't know how long it was there because I was constanly zooming in with my camera on the fire and could not see anything else.
