Two objects flying in formation

We were star gazing in our back yard when we observed two objects flying in formation. When sighted, they were above western horizon at 83° heading south in a straight line. With in 3 -5 seconds they were out of sight. Objects appeared to have lines running E-W as it flew south Gray-green in color they appeared to flutter or rotate in a forward manner as though rolling through the sky. Time was approximately 11:15 pm. No sound was heard. No lights observed. Due to it being a clear night , with arm extended, each were the size of a dime, one flying just ahead of the other, in formation.
