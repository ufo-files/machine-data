Seen while driving home from exercise class.

Coming home from an exercise class traveling south on 12th avenue. Turned west onto Greenhurst. A shiny bird caught my eye above the school to my left approx. 100 or so yards away. Upon closer look it wasn't a bird, but, a boomerang shaped metallic object several feet long and wide. Appeared to be only about a hundred feet or so above the ground. It was traveling just slightly faster than my car and going nearly the same direction. It crossed the road in front of me at an angle approximately 3/4 mile from first sighting location. At that time my view was obstructed by houses and other objects and lost sight. It made no sound of any kind. There was no other traffic on the road at the time.
