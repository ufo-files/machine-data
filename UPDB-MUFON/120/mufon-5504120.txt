3 of us were asleep two were fishing out at the fairbairn dam near emerald queensland australia. I got woken up to look at what was coming. During this time we were swearing our heads off in amazement then the other 2 woke up too. It came as if you were driving a car at 50kmh in reverse, blinking red and blue lights like a star. Hovered above the water without making a sound. Then after about 5 minutes it slowly cruised out of sight south the direction it came from then while we were talking amongst ourselves it came back the same way again and repeated the same process only this time it was gone in the time it takes to click your fingers and it left a light trail behind it and the light trail followed the earths curviature and was about 100 to 200 metres up also which would mean to me that it actually was faster then the speed of light . It was amazing.
