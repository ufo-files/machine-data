{nd} Observed grayish, triangular silent vehicle floating over our house. No emissions.

I got up from the couch to get a drink from the kitchen. Looking out of the kitchen window, I saw this triangle-shaped "thing" then thought to myself that I was sleepy, but I stubbed my toe trying to get out of the house to get a better look. When I got out the door, I looked up & saw the "thing" rise and shot off east at a rate of speed that was impossible to guess, but it disappeared almost immediately, and that's when I saw the black helicopter going in the same direction. The thing looked almost like the "Belgium wave" craft(s), except that it didn't have a red globular center or bright white lights on the 3 corners.
