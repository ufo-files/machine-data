Very slow moving meteor-like object that appeared to be breaking apart however slowed to a hover.

I was driving home around 7:40 at night when I saw what I thought was a meteor falling in the SE sky. It looked as if it was breaking apart as it was starting to leave a trail of light behind it. The object then slowed down to a hover and seemed to disappear. About 4-5 seconds later the object reappeared looking like a cluster of lights. It disappeared and reappeared randomly every few seconds five or six time. Each time the object seemed to be moving and changing direction until it did not reappear.
