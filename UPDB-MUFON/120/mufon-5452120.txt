For the last 4-5 night my friend and I have witnessed various phenomenon traveling the sky

For nearly two hours my friend and I witnessed flashing lights and orbs travel the sky at incredible speeds while making instantaneous directional changes while making no sound. These lights and orbs appeared from many different points and directions in the sky. None seemed to operate with the others nor did they fly in formations or in concert with one another. Since the night of the first sighting we have spent an additional 4 nights, all between 11pm and 1am, witnessing the same or varying phenomenon. For example, last night, June 15, 2020, we witnessed a large (large considering it appeared to be a significant distance away) white light traveling north to south. As we watched, it became anywhere from 5-10 times brighter/larger and then just as suddenly became dimmer and dimmer as it swiftly flew away. We have seen many other rather spectacular phenomenon along with the stated sightings.
