(GS) While looking for Pan-STARRS comet I witnessed and photograpghed this object

While looking for the Pan STARRS comet, I photographed this object thinking it was the comet and a meteorologist stated it wasnt the comet that I photographed, he said to present it to you. I shot this at 24 megapixels with my camera and a 300mm lens. The object appeared circular but oblong with a trail and moved faster than a plane and disappeared after approx 10 seconds .
