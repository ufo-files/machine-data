Myself and my family were driving from Annathill to Cumbernauld when we saw a bright light in the horizon. We stopped and proceeded to film it.

Driving from Annathill to Cumbernauld (Scotland) on Routan road. Noticed bright light, brighter than that from Helicopter Search Light. Stopped & proceeded to film/ light diminished leaving a circle wave of red & white light. Object initially was a orb then went cylindrical with lights moving in a wave vertically. 

Checked sky scanner app/ no helicopter or flights in the area (evidenced in footage). Filmed for over 5 mins/ object did not turn nor change in altitude. Drove further into area to try and obtain better look, still at same altitude/ not circling like a helicopter nor changing elevation. Filmed again for 5 mins then moved closer. The object must have been a significant size as the closer we got/ the light did not fade. 

Drove to a closer location & again displaying same light pattern, filmed for 5/10min then the object at same elevation moved covering significant distance. The object emitted no noise & we were close enough to hear a if it was a helicopter.

Left baffled with video footage!
