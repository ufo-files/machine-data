{dr}Glowing, green orb NE to SW. Central Florida

It was around 5:20am. I am in Weeki Wachee, Fl. This is north Tampa by an hour or so. I got up and went out for the paper. Pretty black outside, still could see stars. Out of nowhere, low on horizon I saw a green orb traveling from northeast to the southwest. It arced over the roof of our home and disappeared. It was slow moving on the downside of the arc, and I did not see a tail. Amazed and in awe!
