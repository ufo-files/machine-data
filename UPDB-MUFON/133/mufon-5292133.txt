Flashing lights with a fixed pattern inside cloud near Byllesby Dam in Ivanhoe, VA

MY husband and I were fishing on the New River 8-29-20. We were located at the 1st public fishing access between Byllesby Dam and Buck Dam on Buck Dam rd. The fish was not biting after being on the river for hours. At 2:10 am we noticed lights flashing on the mtn in front of us. Then we noticed a long oval shaped cloud crossing over the mtn in a straight path. The lights was in the cloud. They lights had a set pattern and they were NOT random at all. The lights were strobing. There was NO sound what so ever. I ran to get my phone as it was crossing the river. My husband yelled and told me to hurry that it sped up. It had been moving very slow until I ran for my phone. It dissapeared out of sight across the river! Our LED fishing lights went out about the time the craft/cloud dissappeared. We decided it was time to pack up an leave! My husband said had he had a sense of dread 

The remnants of a hurricane was supposed to hit our area. The sky was cloudy and at some points earlier there was distinguishable lightening in the distance to the right of us. However, that thing in the cloud was NOT lightening! The lights pulsated with a set rhythm! The dark cloud cover moved with purpose in a straight line. It was almost at tree level. There was NO noise at all. I keep playing it over and over on my mind!
