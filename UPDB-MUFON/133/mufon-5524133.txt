A triangle of about 8 yellow lights

Walking down road in the evening, looked to the northern horizon and saw a blip of light. I stopped and watched it grew bigger. I could make out the details of the light pattern. I thought it was planes in formation but there was only a low constant hum. it whent straight from north to south. I looked around and saw another man staring at it as i was. He later turned out to be a drifted down from ontario, says he sees stuff like that when he's out in the wilderness but never in cities or populated areas. We parted and I went home sand told my room mate he told me about this site and here i am!
