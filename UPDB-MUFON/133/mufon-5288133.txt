Bright light in sky during thunderstorm

We are having a severe thunderstorm in Kansas City, MO. My husband, brother and I went out on the front porch to look at the storm, I thought it was beautiful so I started videoing it. Upon looking back at my video when we went back inside, I noticed a very fast light moving around the sky. The light comes in at about six seconds, to the left of the beam but at the top right of the beam. It then goes in different directions while videoing. I would have kept videoing had I noticed it at that time. We went to look at the video my brother took and you can kind of see it in his as well. The attached video is my video from 12:00am.
