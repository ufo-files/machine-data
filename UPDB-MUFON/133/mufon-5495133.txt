{dr}traveling uphill from late night walk, looked up and noticed ufo. no sound besides woodsy wildlife.

I, Mike, and my friend were walking up the hill towards my friends house after a late night walk down by the lake. As we were coming upon a spot in the road with a clear sky view we happened to look up and we clearly saw a bright multicolored object traveling across the sky. It had a sort of fiery comet trail, as if it could have been a meteorite. But those fall quickley, with this object took as near approximate 5-7 seconds to cross the clear patch of sky. It also should be noted that the object could have traveled quite a ways once it left our view. There was no sound from or caused by the object. From what I and my friend observed the object looked to be on a slight downward-arching descent to its flight pattern. We have not seen any similar sightings.
