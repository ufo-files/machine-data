{dr}Saw a black tube moving N under the clouds that eventually turned E. Was not turning like a balloon would in wind.

I was outside looking at showers off in the distance when I noticed a black tube-shaped object moving north under the clouds. Base of clouds was about 2000 feet. The object could not have been a tube-shaped balloon as it did not twist and turn in the wind. It was not moving as a missle as it was moving lengthwise through the air and not endwise. It gradually turned eastward and was out of sight behind the trees after about 2 minutes. Object kept the same height as long as it was witnessed.
