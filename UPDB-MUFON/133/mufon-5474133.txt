{rjl} Bright large Orb in far distance NE towards Ft.Collins

I was out smoking a ciggerette at 3am on the dot. I always glaze and stare at the star's when out smoking looking at the constalations. As I was looking NE towards Ft. Collins this large orb shot across the Sky and then it just stopped while hoovering in one spot. Still glowing and changing from a blueish to green/white. It stayed in this same spot for a good minute and blinked out.. I was so shocked by what I was seeing I jumped up out of my seat and stood up looking till it disappeared. Like nothing I've ever seen before.
