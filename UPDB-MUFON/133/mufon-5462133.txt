{tjd} A white oval crossing the sky in a straight line from Southeast to Southwest from Corpus Christi, TX

I am not reporting a new sighting, but I want to add what I saw as a child to the database. I believe I and my classmates witnessed what is called "The Mexican Roswell". I grew up in Corpus Christi and when I was in grade school, about 5th or 6th grade I saw a white oval crossing the sky in a straight line from southeast to southwest. It was odd in that it was white like a cloud, but had a definite oval shape. Also, there were no clouds in the sky. It was a beautiful summer day. 
Our classroom opened directly onto an open air courtyard. My friends and I were returning from music class across the courtyard when we saw it. It was odd enough that we stopped and looked. Other classmates saw us and came out and looked as well. Even our teacher came out and looked for a while. We stood there for a long time trying to figure out what it was until the teacher finally gave up and took us back inside. 
Corpus Christi has a military flying training base, so all of us were and are familiar with typical aircraft and noone thought it was that. It also didn't seem to be a cloud. It was odd enough to stay with me through the years since. About 10 years ago I started looking for matching events and have become convinced that it was the event referred to as "The Mexican Roswell". While I am not sure my dates are exactly right, We do begin school that early. I also thought that if my dates are off it is possible what we witnessed may have been a rescue ship? Anyway, I just wanted to throw it out there as one bit of information in the larger mystery.
