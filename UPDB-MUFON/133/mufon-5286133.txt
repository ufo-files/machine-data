I was walking my dog and the string of lights appeared in front of me

I was walking my dog. Heading northeast in the RV park towards a wall and power lines. There was a light in the sky I was thinking about taking a picture of as I was walking. I looked down for a moment when we were about 20 feet from the small street to cross. When I looked up again there were suddenly lights moving in aline Infront of me. I stopped and took a few pictures. I thought I was the starlink but as I was taking the pictures I realized it wasn't moving just rotating lights. I crossed the street and took more pictures. It was like 20 minutes or at least it seemed like that. Then my dog moved and I looked away to check on her and when I looked back the lights were gone.
