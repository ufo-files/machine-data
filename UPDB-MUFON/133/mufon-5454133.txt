Black Triangle hovering overhead above clearing in Trees

I was standing on our driveway at roughly 2/3am with a friend. I lived in an old farmhouse in rural Wales at the time and the house is surrounded by a thick (pine?) forest. For about 5 minutes we coukd hear what can only be described as a humming/vibrating noise but even this doesnt quite describe the sound. From the North direction over the trees by about 4/5ft emerged a very large black triangle, it was moving at a miraculously slow speed for an aircraft, maybe 5mph at most.

The only way to describe it was approx 50ft in either angle, it was immensly black, so much so that it stood out against the nigtht sky it was that black, at the centre of the craft was a small glowing red orb. The feeling as it passed overhead was indescribable, there are no words to explain it. After it passed over the clearing and dissapeared over the treetops we heard the humming sound for another 20/30 seconds before it completely stopped, no fading away just an abrupt stop of the sound.
