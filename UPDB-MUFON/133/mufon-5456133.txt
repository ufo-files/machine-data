{jpg} UFO SIGHTING ON JULY 4 2019

On July 4, 2019, at 10:44 p.m., i was outside of my home, looking at they sky. When I scanned the southern skyline, I noted two yellowish orbs that were suspended above the horizon at about 45 degrees. They initially were side by side, with a fixed distance between them. They then began to slowly rotate counterclockwise to a vertical position, and then clockwise, returning to a horizontal position. The distance between the two orbs remained the same throughout. At the end of this sighting, they virtually just flickered out of sight, without a specific direction (although you could say that they drifted slightly to the west during the rotation, but not much).
