I live in Culver Indiana and it was around 11 pm march 13th 2012.i was outside smoking watching the sky because it was such a clear nite out.i saw a shooting star or two and then saw two star like objects above me moving super fast across the sky from west to east completely across the sky as far as i could see.they were white like a star and evenly spaced apart the entire time next to each other,they moved as fast as a shooting star would but was clearly not a shooting star.it was as high up as the stars.i heard no noise but did notice all the birds and dogs in town were going nuts during my sighting.not even a minute later i saw what looked like a glowing like bird squiggle across the sky just above me and i have never seen a shooting star squiggle like that but it appeared and disappeared like a shooting star though. immediately after that i saw the same two lights moving at a high rate of speed still evenly spaced apart side by side moving from south to north as far as i could see again.it was then that i figured this was not a coincidence of two shooting stars at the same time.my girlfriends son came out to watch with me for awhile. we saw a few shooting stars and then we saw the same two lights a third time moving from east to west just north of us except this time they were following each other at a high rate of speed.we sat outside for another 15 or 20 minutes but saw nothing else.i also noticed all the birds and dogs got quiet again.i am very curious to know what we saw in the sky that nite.it fascinated me and gave me goosebumps at the same time.
