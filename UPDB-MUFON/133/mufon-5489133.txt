hovering, pulsating, ball of light

Woke up at 3am. Looked out my window and saw this massive bright light. At first I thought it may have been a planet, but it soon became apparent it was much too large.
The objects light was pulsating and hovering above the city. I watched it for half an hour, and it didn't move. I went back to sleep, woke up 2 hours later, and it was gone.
