{rjl} Filming video when I saw a triangular shape of red orbs. It then split and went back together quickly.

I was at the end of my friends street filming a trailer for a movie that we were making and thats when I noticed it. I was doing an around the corner pan and I then say a straight line of red glowing orbs with an orange-yellow illumination. At first I thought it was just a standard passenger plane but my thoughts changed when it quickly split into a triangle shape and went faster. It consisted of twelve dots in total. They moved in an odd pattern almost like molecules in a solid, moving around in close quarters, and then finally they morphed into a triangular shape. The dots stayed like that for a while until they moved into a diamond shape, then a straight line, and zoomed off. I felt like I was the only one seeing it and when I looked at my friend, he had a clod stare, an unbelieving stare, him being a tough guy I knew that for something to scare him and me that it was not fake. I also felt a little chill and twitched a little bit after seeing it. The following days I was sleepless and couldnt get the image out of my head. I lost sight of the object as it flew over the South East horizon towards the woods, and it also went faster than anything Id seen in my life.
