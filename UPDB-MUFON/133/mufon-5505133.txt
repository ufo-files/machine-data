{rjl} Noticed 3 orb lights above treeline in distance moving upward and across sky, then 4 more appeared behind those, then more until about 15 orbs were in sky, moving in same direction.

I was driving home and noticed 3 yellow-orange orb-like lights above the furthest visible treeline, moving upwards and across the sky. I thought at first they might be a few helicopters or military craft moving in formation. Then 4 more appeared behind those, in the same fashion as the first and moving in same direction. More orbs kept appearing in groups of 2 to 5, each group following behind the previous group. At one point there were a total of 10-15 objects in the sky at once. I noticed the woman driving next to me was on her phone, slowing down and pointing at the objects. I parked in my driveway and noticed the first orbs passed over my neighborhood and slowly got dimmer until they were no longer visible. More orbs kept following this path in the same manner, dimming in the same spot as the previous ones. I couldn't tell if they were dimming out or simply moving so quickly that they were soon too far to see. My phone was dead but i was able to charge it at home and got a single orb on video with my i-phone. It was the last one i saw, before it dimmed out and was no longer visible. I honestly thought there was going to be some kind of UFO invasion or something. I have never seen anything like this before. I had a sighting about 7 years ago but it was only a single orb. However, the orb i saw 7 years ago and the ones i saw tonight were of the same color, size, and behaved very similarly.
