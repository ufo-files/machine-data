{dr}It was a triangular light formation in the sky.

On this night I was watching television with my mother, when I went to go to the restroom, I looked out the bathroom window like I always do and there in the sky I seen three clustered lights that later arranged into a triangular formation. I immediately ran outside to my backyard and watched it as it hovered about a mile away. I was maybe thinking that it was a plane, but then I remembered that never in my life have I ever seen anything like this before and I didn't here anything, no sound whatsoever. As it went over I only seen two lights, one was leading and the other was following, the third light just disappeared mysteriously. Lately Ive been reading in my local newspaper about recent UFO sightings in my area but I didn't believe it, after what I saw I have to say that I now believe. Witnessing this made me feel somewhat nervous, extremely startled and after I went inside, I began to feel a weird energy inside my body that I couldnt explain.
