This happened on Wednesday, February 1,2017 at 6:25pm. My daughter and I were driving on 79N, going to my mother's house. All of a sudden, we saw 2 lights that were strobing across the sky, from East to West, right above the trees with a Jet plane directly behind it in pursuit.It was so low that we were able to see the doors of the landing gear on the jet. This happened within seconds, my daughter started freaking out, because it scared her and she looked at me and said," Please tell we just didn't just see what I thought we just did!" All I could do was confirm with her what we had both saw. I was at my mother's house within 2 minutes and called my husband immediately because my daughter and I have Always been sceptics but my husband had always been a believer. He told me and our daughter to draw what we saw immediately, so we sat in the car and we drew it out. We went into my mother's house and told her what we saw and my mother said she heard a roaring, rumble noise that vibrated the house. Like an engine was accelerated to the max! We are still in shock, not really believing what we saw. My husband told me about this website and wanted me to report this.
