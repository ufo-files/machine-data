Multicolored ship

I took my telescope out for viewing the moon and planets and came across this “star”. It caught my attention because it was shining red, blue, green, orange, purple. I managed to record it and it looked like it was shape shifting. On one of the videos I took it seemed like it burst into a sphere. I can’t explain entirely what I saw, but it certainly looked interesting.
