Tic-Tac shaped craft stationary very high in sky

My wife and I happened to notice a "star" shining brightly in the broad daylight in an almost perfectly clear blue sky. Although difficult to judge, it appeared to me to be about the height that the highest jets fly. At first I assumed it was a satellite or the ISS, or even a weather balloon, until I realized it was not moving at all; it was perfectly stationary. I could tell because I was sitting in a chair looking up through the frame for our canopy, so I could see, relative to the frame, it was perfectly stationary. I could not make much detail so I retrieved my binoculars (not sure of magnification) and sat back in the same chair. Once I focused on the object, it appeared as though it was shaped like a tic-tac, although it was at an angle from my viewpoint. I was facing NE and the Sun reflected very brightly on the left side of the object and I could see the right side was a darker shadow, with a slight crescent shape to the dark/light edge facing me. I decided to get my telescope to get a better look, but by the time I got it in place and had the object in the view finder, I began looking through the telescope and could not see it. I looked back to the sky to see it, and it was gone. No trace of it.
