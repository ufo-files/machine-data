(GS) Star like blue white orb

I was outside smoking when I noticed a bright star like object enter from the north west. It made a zig zag pattern south to west several times before heading in a southern direction increasing in brightness and reflecting blue white light off the intermittent clouds. After traveling fast rate from north to south it very quickly brightened then faded out to a grayish black oval shape, I could still see the object for about 5 more seconds before it was not visible anymore. I quickly Checked the time on my watch which was 12:35:20 Am.
