There were 33 objects we witnessed. Orbs, some dimly lit, others had a sort of.. Flare.

At approximately 7:44 PM, my mother, father and I witnessed 33 orbs cross the sky from the west. One after another, in a straight line. Except these 3 specific ones. They formed a sort of triangle. We couldn't find a logical explanation for it. We've never seen satellites like that. They most certainly weren't planes.
They appeared a ways below Venus, above the treeline. Each had a bit of space between them. It wouldn't be the first time we've seen unusual objects out here before.
My father, being ex-military... Vietnam Era Veteran, he knows what military crafts, etc, look like and he couldn't find any logical explanation.
