{dr}Flat black, boomerang, chevron shaped object flew over sighting sight slowly, silently. From North East East to South West West in a straight line under and through ionized clouds.

I went to visit my mother, [address removed/dr/cms]. It was March 25th 2011 Good Friday. About 7:00p. There had been these strange mamatized clouds in the sky all day long proceeding the sighting. I've never seen clouds like this above Norman before or since then. And I had lived here 25 years before this. It was warm, and mom wanted to walk around the yard and talk as she often enjoyed doing. There was strange silent cloud to cloud lightning going on by now. It was just about to be dark,twilight. But still plenty of light to see the object. When there was a flash of lightning directly over head, it made no sound but something made me look up in the direction of it. This is when I first saw it, it was a flat black, boomerang shaped object. It was completely featureless, no lights, and completely silent. In fact when it flew over, things suddenly became extra quiet, no birds, no wind, nothing. And I new from the vary first moment I saw it that I was having an authentic sighting. This area is in the middle of a residential area. What was it doing here? Is what I remember thinking. I saw it for about 45 seconds as it flew over moms house going from N.E.E. to S.W.W. It was shaped like a boomerang but it was pointing to the S.E.E.in opposition to the direction of flight. It was about 45 seconds before I could get mom to see it. But when she did she exclaimed, what is that. She does not like to talk about it, but she saw the object for about 45 seconds before it flew out of sight. It was about 125 ft. High or skirting the bottom of the clouds. I have no pictures but could draw the object. The total time of the sighting took about 1min. 15 seconds. I saw the object the whole time, mom only saw the object for about 45 seconds.
