Lights on the evening sky.

I was just taking some long exposure photos of the night sky from my room when 13 strange lights looking somewhat like orbs and aligned in a strange pattern appeared on one of my photos just over the forest. Whatever it was I did not see with my own eyes and did appear only in one of the several photos I took that evening. The exposure time was set at 30 seconds.
