{nd} Glowing, slow hovering path orange in color, from NE across to NW then disappeared

Friends and neighbors were outside popping fireworks and about 10 of us adults and children witnessed this glowing orange sphere slowly traverse across the sky. Could not tell the size or distance as it was at night but if it was a mile away then it was about ten feet in diameter (guessing) if it was closer than that, then it would obviously be smaller in diameter. It did not seem threatening but it was unusual. It was not a firework of any type I assure you. It hovered then took off or disappeared- last seen from our vantage point was at an angle to the horizon of about 15 degrees and took off to the NW or disappeared. Not the first time it did though. Two of the witnesses claimed it had done an earlier pass-by.
