{dr}Bright Starlike object over Hampton, NH

At approximately 2200 hrs a bright spherical object appeared in the Eastern sky just above the tree line. The object stopped in its flight path and "hovered" over the tree line for approximately 1 minute, then changed direction and headed north west until it was out of sight. Viewed through binoculars the object appeared as a bright sphere that emitted/glowed light in all directions. No visible strobes and no engine noise. The size and elevation of the object was unknown. The wx conditions were clear and there were no visible clouds. 

Object was first sighted through a kitchen window, by my father who yelled to me to come view the object. We stood outside with a pair of binoculars and viewed the object as it moved westward and then north west. The object defied explanation as it was not acting like a common airplane, helicopter, satellite or common celestial phenomena (shooting star or fireball) and thus falls into an unexplained category.
