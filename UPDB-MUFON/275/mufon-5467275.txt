{td} LARGE BLACK TRIANGLE SHAPE HOVERING OVER FWY 210 AZUSA, CA MOUNTAINS

This happened around 2003 in Azusa, CA near the i210 and i605 fwys, next/near to Santa Fe Dam, to a very close family of friends that we have... 
While driving on the freeway, 3 adults and 2 children, approx. age; 1-2 year olds, spotted an object far away, they thought it was a kite at first. The object started getting closer and they realized it wasn't a kite, and it was getting bigger/showing it's true size as it got closer. The object was hovering towards the direction they were on-the freeway. When the UFO got closer, it was apparent it wasn't anything they had seen before. It was impressive. Both directions of the freeway came to a stop as the UFO hovered over them. The UFO's size covered both sides of the fwy, so it was really big. 
Everyone got out of their cars and you could hear people crying and panicking. There were even police officers in that same freeway crowd that couldn't explain what was going on. They didn't know what to do, they were also in shock. 
They were looking at this thing up as it hovered over them.
My friends describe the object as black in color, metallic like material, and they could see that it kind of had "wavy" component below. It was triangular shaped and it had lights somewhere on the corners (maybe) 
While the UFO flew over them, it kind of made an angled turn, at this point they could see that the huge object was very thin. 

People were crying and panicked, including the kids. My friend took out her cell phone and took some pictures and a little bit of video. The cell phone was a flip phone. Eventually she misplaced the cell phone and could never recover the footage. I'm sure other people from the crowd got to photograph or film something with their phones. 

There were many witnesses to this event. I would like to hear other witnesses' testimony on this.
