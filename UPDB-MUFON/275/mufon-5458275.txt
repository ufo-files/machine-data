{jpg} A bright glow in the sky daylight hours

My wife and me were traveling from Whidbey Island Wa. to Greenfield In. in late September 1978 for a funeral of a relative. Traveling across South Dakota on interstate 90 we noticed a few vehicles pulled over and the occupants outside their vehicles looking north into the sky.to our surprise there was a bright glow in the sky between 30 to 45 degrees of the horizon. I got out of my car and went to the passenger side and retrieved my 110 camera and snapped a picture.It was the last pic in the roll of film. The picture was misplaced for all these years and was found when me and my wife recently moved and looked thru old pics. The picture is real and untouched in any way its an old pic but still shows the whatever it is. when zoomed on there is a glowing something right below the object and thats what caught our attention.I have no reason to lie or deceive my wife and me saw it and others on the interstate saw it hope you enjoy the pic its a trophy to me as i have only seen two unidentified in my 62 years and this is one of them.
