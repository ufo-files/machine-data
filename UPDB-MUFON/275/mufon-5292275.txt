Lights

I was talking on the phone and happened to look up into the sky and saw a super bright light coming on really really slowly toward me. I got really excited because I thought it was the space station and stood up to walk to the end off of my porch so I could see better up into the sky. There is not just one light but four blinking lights in a trapezoid shape- spaced out pretty far from me another- blinking/ then one by they vanished. Planes? The formation was weird. The video is rough as I have tremors in my hands.
