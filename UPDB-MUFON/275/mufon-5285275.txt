It began in a fire ball inclining upward then fire dissipated and turned into a silvery glittery trail

My sister and I were sitting on the front porch talking when we heard a loud pop and looked up to see a fire ball inclining upward in the sky. I caught some of it on my Snapchat, it then burst and turned shiny with a glittery silvery trail. We couldn't stop watching and wondering if anyone else has seen what we just saw. After about 5min or so it zoomed straight up disappearing in the night sky. It was amazing.
