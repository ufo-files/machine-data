{tjd} Eight bright orange/red/yellow orb lights floating E to SW just after dark

On the evening of 10/8/2018 while coming down my street I looked up and witnessed a single bright orange/red/yellow light. I thought it was a helicopter at first, then there were two more,then another with two more with three more after that. Eight total, wow I thought a whole squadron. living near Ft Bragg nothing new there, but as I drove into my yard and exited my vehicle as large and close as these were, they made no sound. No strobes to be seen. I take out my cell phone to take a video and it shuts down. As I watch, these objects just float through the sky and start to just disappear one by one as they each reach the same spot in the sky. the best I can describe the objects I observed is round or maybe oval in shape, If held up at arms length about the size of a large asprin, yellowish, orangish, redish in color almost like a flame in a fireplace. After it was over I walked into the house and looked at my phone to my astonishment I do have a streak of lights but its undeterminable and is just some lines although you can tell they are in the night sky.
