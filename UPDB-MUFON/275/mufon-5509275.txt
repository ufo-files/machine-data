{nd} Driving tractor trailer from San Diego CA.to Casa Grande AZ truck stop to pick up in Chandler AZ.

I had got back from getting married in Hawaii and was enroute to Chandler AZ. Interstate 8 I was running west to east when out of the south appeared a missile shaped craft traveling a couple hundred miles per hour. I watched it pass overhead maybe 500 feet and there wasnt any noise. One of the first thoughts I had was Mexico firing missiles into the US?? I had heard of the drug cartel employing a similar strategy to deliver drugs but this craft continued to the small mountains directly noth and disappeared. The small town just north is visible and so as a visual aid to distance the craft passed over that town and would then need to have stopped immediately before entering Chandler AZ. When I arrived to the truck stop in Casa Grande another driver asked me why I wasnt going to report what I had just saw? That caught me off guard and I realized others obviously must have seen it although no one was as close as I was. The nearest person would have been a rancher a half mile away, if he had happened to see it. There were no other vehicles on I8 for at leat 2-3 miles west or east of me.
