A massive object flying towards Frankfurt. So close and no sound. Police stopped behind me to witness the event.

IT was X-mas Eve in Germany and I went to pick-up my Grandmother in a village close to the event. Light Snowfall and almost no traffic on the road, we went to join the rest of the family in a Castle Style restaurant in Kronberg Taunus. Between the villages Koenigstein and Kronberg is a elongated road with a scenic view onto Frankfurt. Very spectacular and especially with all its lights, it creates its own glow from that viewpoint. With the goal to not worry my Grandmother with driving to fast, we took it slow... very slow. And as we are reaching the view point with about 30 km/h, there is this massive object above our head in a very slow speed gliding towards Frankfurt. 
This was no disk or boomerang; it was a massive bulky shape, dark, no lights and very clear the edges with the backlight from Frankfurt City.
We stopped right there on the road and I noticed a Police Car about 200 ft behind us and them too leaving their car to follow the object.
Not the minor sound or blimp, just gliding. And before it would fly over the city, it turned right and disappeared behind a mountain line.
