{cak} Have selfie photograph of object in background

I was in my boat at sunset in Lake Pueblo Reservoir when I took a selfie of myself with the sunset in the background. I did use a flash. I did not hear or see anything unusual until I looked at the photo and saw a starange object appeared in the background. I zoomed in on it and noticed that there was a trail following it, that it was moving at an incredible speed and was blurry like it was moving very fast. It appears to be a very long distance from me in the background and you can notice that there is a trail behind it. I do not know if it was a astroid or a UFO but was caught in the short time of when the shutter on my camera opened and closed.
