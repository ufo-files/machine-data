On watch on USS Berkeley ddg15 observed 3-4 lights behind us,making turn and speeds never seen before COB would not let be logged.

While on watch aboard naval ship DDG15 between midnight and 4am near Midway island as port watch I observed several lights far off above the horizon 3to4 of them flying. They were making turns at 90 degrees or less at speeds faster than any jets we were doing ops with the carrier we were escorting. These lights would hoover then dart off at high rates of speed make turns in a triangle formation. When I first saw them I thought they were from our Carrier Ranger, that was in front of us.The lights were small but moving at such high rates of speed I Knew they were not any thing we had.when I reported the objects the officer of the deck came out to verify my sighting I was told it would not be logged in the ships official log book that everything is logged.He (ood) told me it was not ufos but Venus. The fantail and starboard watch confirmed my sighting. I lost view of the lights when I had to take up my position at the helm.
