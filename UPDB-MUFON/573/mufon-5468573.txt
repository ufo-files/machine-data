Pulsing star every 3 seconds to dim, 3 seconds to brightest.

Witnessed a starlike object with my Fiancee that pulsed as it moved westerly across the clear night's sky. It would take 3-4 seconds to completely dim to no light and again 3-4 seconds to complete brightness. I thought perhaps it was a tumbling satellite however it slowly changed course to a gradual north-west direction before we lost sight of it. It was easily above 35,000 feet. It looked like a star in terms of size and brightness. I have seen satellites before that are either constant in colour (white or red) or flash but having never seen one gradually dim and then consistently return to brightness. It could've been tumbling with a light refraction but this wouldn't account for the change of course. We just saw it. I'm not sure if it appeared or we just noticed it when we looked up. We lost sight as it moved across the horizon but probably saw it for about minute to 2 minutes.
