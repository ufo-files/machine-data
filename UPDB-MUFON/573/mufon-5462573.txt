{jpg} Witness large object hovering and then moving along the horizon above a mountain/hill

While returning from the beach on Vandenberg AFB I rounded a corner and saw a large object hovering. I pulled off the road to catch a video and it slowly started moving! It was slightly behind a large hill , small mountain headed directly towards the tracking stations responsible for tracking all objects in orbit! I could not see any markings, tail fins or cockpit as you would expect to see from a blimp, however I was at a considerable distance but this was over an air force base and isn1t typical in this area!
