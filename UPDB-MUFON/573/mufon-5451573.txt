Extremely fast, glowing, disk-shaped object zips over Interstate 15 - observed by 3 of 4 people in car

It must have been Summer of 2002, and my brother and I had spent the holidays with our father in St. George, UT. We were driving down Interstate 15 from there towards Las Vegas, NV, from where we were taking a late flight back home to Frankfurt, Germany. It probably was somewhere around Ute, NV (Clark County) that my step mother - who was driving - suddenly said "what the hell is that??". At that time, I had my head tilted back on the back seat and was looking out of the back window, and suddenly I saw an extremely fast, glowing, disk-shaped object fly without any sound right over our car. The object was faster than anything I have ever seen, but we heard no engine or sonic boom. I recall it crossing the highway at about a 90-degree angle and fly towards (judging by the map) the 94 and Crystal Springs, NV. My brother also saw it, and we have confirmed the occurence multiple times with each other as well as my step mother since.
