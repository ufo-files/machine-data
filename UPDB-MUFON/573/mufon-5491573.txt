{dr}3 plus fighter jets and one oval shaped UFO.

The date of this incident was October 8, 2013 at 7:30 AM EST, I was sitting on my front porch drinking my morning coffee waiting for my wife to get home from work. I'm not really sure what made me look up but I did, and I saw the 3 fighter jets flying really high, I'm not sure what they were but they looked kinda like F-16"s. The thing that really got my attention was an oval shape right below the center aircraft, you could see the contrail coming off of the jets but the oval object had none. Then it just disappeared and about 2 or 3 seconds later reappeared in front of the lead jet which totally blew my mind. Approximately one minute after they were out of my field of vision another fighter jet comes along on the same flight path and elevation. I was very excited as being 54 years old and this is the first sighting I had ever had of what to me had to be a UFO. I told my wife about it when she got home and she said I should sketch out what I saw and see if I could find someone to talk to about it and the first people I thought of was MUFON but apparently I did not get the e-mail address right as I never heard from anyone. Just for the record I am ex-military and a former police officer and I know what I saw.
