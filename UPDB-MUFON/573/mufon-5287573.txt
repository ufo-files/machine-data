Large Black Triangle ufo with orange lights on bottom

Was leaving work from my job at Activision at about 3am. As I was heading south over the highway bridge there was a super bright light in the sky in the distance that was really low, the object looked almost like a mini sun. I thought it was way too bright to be a helicopter, the light was blinding and intense. I started to drive towards it until I got to a side rode near the eden prairie mall, stopped my car in the middle of the road and got out of my car. I looked right above me to see a giant black triangle with a bunch of orange lights on the under side, the object was hovering right above the tree line. I got such a good view of it that I started freaking out. The object made no sound and eventually started moving away after a few minutes until I lost site of it past the tree line. I did call the police right after this incident but the phone operator said no one else had called in about it. At this time of night the roads were dead.
