Large object seen moving across the sky the same way that the ISS does.

I was walking my son around the neighborhood tonight and since the sky was so clear, I couldn't help but look up from time to time. At around 8:15PM something caught my eye near the center of the sky. At first, I thought it might be a spot-light. However, it didn't move like a spotlight; in fact, it was very difficult to see as the light was so faint. It was in the shape of a V but I had to use my peripheral vision in order to see it. What caught my eye was the movement. It moved south across the sky, but the way it moved reminded me of the ISS. It seemed as if it was outside of the atmosphere. If you were to compare an airplane or the ISS to what I saw, the airplane and ISS look like bright dots in the sky. This object was much larger, almost like looking at a small constellation moving across the sky with a speed >= the ISS. There as no noise and it did not put out much light at all. No flashing or anything like that, my eye just caught the movement with peripheral vision. 

When I got back home, I told my wife I thought I just saw a UFO. I've seen things in the past but I've always been able to find an explanation for them. I can't think of any atmospheric condition that would cause this. I was a little hesitant to report it but I figured it might be worth reporting. At the very least, it someone can identify a type of atmospheric condition that would match up then I could research it a bit. Unfortunately, I didn't have enough time to get my phone out and record it. Within about 15 seconds it was gone.
