IT LOOKED LIKE A FLAME GOING ACROSS THE SKY. THEN IT SEEMED AS IF IT BURNED OUT.

I WAS STANDING OUTSIDE SMOKING A CIGARETTE, WHEN I SAW SOMETHING GLOWING ORANGE IN THE SKY, IT LOOKED DIFFERENT THAN THE STARS OR AIRPLANES. IT WENT ACROSS THE SKY THEN LOOKED AS IF LIKE A FLAME IT BURNED OUT. I LOOKED FOR ANOTHER 5 MINS TO SEE IF I COULD SPOT IT AGAIN AND I DIDNT. IT LOOKED LIKE IT WAS MOVING ABOUT THE SPEED OF THE AIRPLANES IN THE SKY.
