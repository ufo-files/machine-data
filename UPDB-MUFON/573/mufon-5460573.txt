{tjd} 3 star like objects moving in unison

My wife and I were outside last night standing next to the fire pit observing the stars.My wife first thought she saw Orion's belt and pointed it out to me and we both thought we were having vision problems as we realized it couldn't be Orion's belt it was moving across the sky fast. It looked like Orion's belt 3 star like shapes but moving in perfect unison but if you looked close they each had a kind of slight wobble effect from each other showing that it was not in fact one solid object with 3 lights on it but actually 3 separate objects moving in formation together
