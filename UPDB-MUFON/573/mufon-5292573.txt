BLACK DISC SHAPED WITH VERY BRIGHT LIGHT MOVING BACK AND FOURTH AT THE TOP. THE OBJECT ALSO HAD A GREEN LIGHT AT THE BOTTOM WHICH WAS BLINKING. IT WAS HOVERING OVER THE POWER LINE ABOVE TREES. LASTED ABOUT 1 MIN AND THE OBJECT WAS GONE.

I WAS DRIVING AND AT THE LIGHT ON THOMPSON RD GOING TOWARDS COUNTY RD T.
I NOTICED THERE WAS A VERY BRIGHT LIGHT WHICH I THOUGHT WAS THE SUN. 
IT BLOCKED MY VIEW SO I PUT MY VIZOR DOWN AND THE LIGHT WAS STILL INTENSE. FIRST THING I THOUGHT WAS BOY THAT SUN IS BRIGHT TODAY.
WHEN I DROVE DOWN COUNTY RD T I HAPPENED TO LOOK TO MY RIGHT THE OBJECT WAS STATIONARY HOVERING BY THE POWERLINE AND ABOVE THE TREES 
AND IT MADE NO NOISE AND IT WAS IF THE LIGHT WAS MOVING FROM SIDE TO SIDE. WHICH CAUSED THE BRIGHTNESS.
THE OBJECT HAD A GREEN LIGHT AT THE BOTTOM THAT WAS BLINKING. UPON LOOKING AT THE OBJECT I WAS IN SHOCK AND A LITTLE NERVOUS. I COOULD NOT BELIEVE WHAT I WAS SEEING. ALL I KEPT SAYING WAS PLEASE GO AWAY. THE OBJECT JUST VANISHED AND DID NOT GO UP.
(WITNESS STATES HER EYES WERE RED AND IRRATATED AFTER LOOKING AT OBJECT)
