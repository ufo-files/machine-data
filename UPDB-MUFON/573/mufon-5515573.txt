(GS) 10 orange/ pinkish lights slowly drifting s/sw

We were driving West on the PA turnpike between the norristown and valley forge exits when my wife asked me if I saw "those lights"? I did not and then I looked over my left shoulder and there they were, 10 orange/pinkish lights in an erratic formation slowly moving in a s by sw direction. I pulled off the right hand lane of the turnpike and watched these 10 lights slowly disappearing into what looked like cloud banks. All but one eventually remained until it disappeared over the horizon moving s by sw.
