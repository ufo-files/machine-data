flew past my feild of view at high speed, 5 seconds or less

I went outside at about 10:30 for a smoke. I sat back in a "zero gravity" lawn chair and looked up at the sky. ive been seeing high flying solid lights all week so I looked to the direction that I normally see them and nothing was there. I looked up and noticed three bright stars and said jokingly aloud "I see you." Fewer than 10 seconds after saying that out loud I boomerang shaped craft flew across the sky from the north east to the south west. it crossed the sky in less than 5 seconds. it looked less than 100 ft in the sky and had dim lit lights in a v formation on the front of the craft. I could hardly believe what I had seen. immediately I texted and called my sister to tell her what I had seen. craziest event of my life.
