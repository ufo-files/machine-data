Heard the sound of a ufo and captured on outdoor camera

In the early morning hours of 4-15-2021 I heard a loud whirring sound coming from the sky directly over my home. My bedroom window was open so I could hear it clearly. It sounded like energy whirring in a circular motion and lasted for about 5 min before I heard the sound of jets (2) go by. The whirring stopped for about a minute then returned and stayed another 2 min before leaving. Quite frankly I was too scared to go outside and look. The bizaar part is, I was telling my son about it the next day ( he lives 28 miles south of me) and the same thing happened to him and his wife the same night! And....their outdoor camera captured the sound! I am attaching the camera footage. You can hear it upset the dog who also started barking. Their encounter was about 40 min to an hour before mine.
