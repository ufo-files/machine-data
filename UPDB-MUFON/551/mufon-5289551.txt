Live WAPO webcam from Washington Monument in DC

I was watching the WAPO Cam LIVE (from top of Washington Monument in D.C) about 1:45 am on February 24th. While simultaneously in a zoom chat I saw an object enter the Live feed from the left (think that is SW?). It looked kind of almond shaped with almost a glow about it with an occasional white flashing light or two.. There were strange trails behind it like it was dropping something or sputtering something out the back. It moved slowly and the front of shape appeared to slightly raise up. As it began to hover in place I realized that this could not be a plane as it is a no-fly zone and a plane would not be able to hover...nor did it look like a helicopter. It slowly began moving again and when it got almost to the NW (opposite side) of the webcam screen I could see it begin to spin/rotate and a red flashing light was now added to the only white lights I'd seen previously. It seems to have a steady spin now instead of a wobbly flight, but was still traveling slowly. It turned slightly and went in the same direction as E-Street. The WAPO camera is fogged up on the sides, but it did not zoom off, and eventually I could not see it on the screen any longer. It appeared to be below the WAPO camera, about halfway between camera and ground...maybe 300ish feet above ground? I was so shocked and dumbfounded didn't think to take a pic from webcam app.
