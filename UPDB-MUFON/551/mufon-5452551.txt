Found on my trail cam

I just bought a new trail camera and I was going through "Set Up" in order to set the trail cam time, date, and year. Once done I continued to read the directions (Two pics doing so). Now the thing is taking pictures without me knowing as I'm looking for a red blinking light that the instructions tell me to do. Now I have taken 8 pictures without knowing it of the sky, my face and the sky, and 2 reading the friggin directions. The 9th pic was of the ground where I found the camera the next morning. Maybe just a fluke but the location I found the camera in was suspect.
I loaded the data card into my computer this morning hoping to see the hogs that are tearing up my lawn. I had just glanced at the pics before work and was getting ready to shut down my laptop when something caught my eye. An object just above me was caught in two pictures that I didn't see or hear. Hopefully you guys can take a closer look at the pics and let me know of your opinions.
