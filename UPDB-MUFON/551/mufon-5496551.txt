Taking night shot of pool and aimed camera up at night sky and saw the bright object

1. I was at the above address, outside in my backyard, taking night photos of our pool. I looked up to see if there was a full moon and I saw the U.F.O. 

2. It caught my attention because it was very bright and unusual.

3. I thought it may have been a shooting star.

4. It was very bright and gold/yellow in colour, it was oblong shaped. And when I had first noticed it, it appeared to be stationary, and then when I was taking a photo of it, it started moving. It ascended a little bit, and descended in a diagonal line to the north, and then it disappeared out of sight completely within seconds. As if it had just vanished. 

5. When I first saw it I thought "Wow, that's pretty.", and while I was taking the photo, when it started moving strangely, it felt very weird. I felt amazed, and so I kept trying to take photos of it. 

6. It vanished completely.
