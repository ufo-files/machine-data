Silver/white tic tac shape

My wife saw the tic tac shape in the East sky, while we out on the rear deck of our home. The sun was still bright at 5PM. I could not view it until the bright red lights blinked on and off. With a pair of binoculars, I could see the craft, especially when the red lights blinked on. When that happened you could see the craft was a triangle shaped. The craft had to be extremely high, because the commercial airliners were flying at least 10-20 thousand feet and this object was much higher. The object stayed there for at least 20 minutes. We viewed it as it very slowly drifted more East. Then at one point it just disappeared. We found when the red lights blinked on, the craft tended to cloak itself and only the tic tac shape was visible. I was surprised my wife spotted the object. Unless you happened to be looking and have excellent distance vision, I'm not sure you could have seen this object. Unfortunately, we don't have video equipment that I could have recorded it. I have still camera equipment with long range lenses, but it wasn't available quick enough to assemble and take pictures.
On another note the object was in the direction of the Bedford Air Force base.
