Woke up feeling odd, realized I was hearing a loud noise and went outside to see what was happening

I woke up at about 3:30 and wasn't really sure why I woke up, so walked into the kitchen for some water and realized I was hearing a weird noise, like a plane overhead or something. I went out on my deck to check it out, and it was a lot louder outside but I couldn't figure out what it was coming from. It sounded like it was coming from all around my house. I stood outside for a while, confused and then I noticed that the sky above me looked odd because I didn't see any stars or clouds. I went back inside and went to bed and didn't remember what happened until this morning, days later and it still feels kind of fuzzy, but very vivid. I know it wasn't a dream, because I had dirty feet the next morning and stepped out in the snow so my sheets were a little wet, but I didn't remember why they were like that. All in all, very strange. Whatever it was it was huge, big enough to block out the sky above my street and the sound it made was fairly loud, but felt more physical than noisy, the kind of sound you can feel in your chest.
