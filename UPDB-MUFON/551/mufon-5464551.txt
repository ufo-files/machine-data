{cak} Was Cigar shaped, reflected sky/clouds, but could see faint outline as it moved forward, parallel to our car.

My best friend and I were driving down a back road in Waycross, Georgia on our way to take our husbands their lunch where they worked. We were having a conversation and suddenly we noticed movement to our left and as we turned to look, we saw a faint cigar shaped outline, but the center seemed transparent (rather it seemed slightly shiny at times), but reflective of the sky and clouds around it. We first noticed it as we passed by some old electrical towers (the kind that resemble giant robots) that you see scattered along country roads. It only seemed present for approximately 10 minutes, it seemed to move slowly parallel to our car, then it just faded. We joked that it might be a UFO. From that point we continued on to deliver our husband's lunch and didn't talk about it anymore.
