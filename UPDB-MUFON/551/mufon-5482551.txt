{nd} Multiple objects hovering and flying across the horizon at night.

Driving home to our residence. Made a right hand turn off Baltimore Annapolis Blvd. and onto Kempton Road. Before making a left onto Barbara Court my wife noticed a configuration in the night sky she described as the "big dipper", I looked up recognized it wasn't a star configuration, had her stop the car and turn out the vehicle's lights. I was then able to snap 2 pictures. The first picture showed four lights in a square configuration with two other objects catching up to the first four. The second picture shows the objects lining up into angle shaped crafts with multiple lights. We witnessed these objects for several minutes before they took off across the horizon at high speed.
