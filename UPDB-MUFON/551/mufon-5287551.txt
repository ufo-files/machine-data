3 stationary lights in the clear sky night, glowing and morphing

One bright light caught my attention in the peripheral part of my eye vision through the window of my living room as I was watching a movie on the couch.

I decided to check it out, and exit through the garden door to check it our over the roof. There, to my surprise when I looked up as I was exiting another bright stationary light was hovering at 60 degrees in the night sky. It was a very cold night without a could in sight.
I looked over the roof as I was setting in the garden and the other light I initially saw was there. Same light, stationary, kind of glowing, if stars are pins, these were marble sized.
I looked at it for a few minutes, and then went up to fetch my son who was gaming in his bedroom. He came down, looked at the lights and thought the matrix had a bug :-). I kept on coming in and out to check on the lights. and then at some point when I came back in I realized there were a third one, that one was in Oxford's center/downtown direction. 
I quit the observation as 2 of these lights were getting dimmer like fading away rather than moving closer, shape had somewhat morphed, was like a hazy kind of concentration of lights initially, I was thinking it might be a triangular solid shape behind these lights because of what I could make out from their arrangement but as it seems to fade away I thought it was kind of starting to look more like a hollow orb. The third light formation towards Oxford center was still in the original arrangement from what I could tell.
