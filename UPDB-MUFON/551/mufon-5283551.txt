Sighting out of window of front of home

This was told to me by my Mother. Her best friend/neighbor had a sighting of an entity in the early eighties . Her name is Mary- I changed her last name in the report . She told my mother that she would be ok with an interview. 
When she was goin to take out the garbage one night - as she went to the front door , she happened to look out her large front window facing the street. She then saw an entity (as described below) floating back and forth between bushes of the neighbors house and her front yard. She immediately crouched down, peaked slightly over the bottom edge of the window and watched the entity go across her front yard to end of the driveway and disappear.
