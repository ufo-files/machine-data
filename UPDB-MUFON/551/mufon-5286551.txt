Three lights over RAF Bentwaters

I was a munitions transporter working overnight in the NWSA. I had driven a forklift out to our next location and was waiting on the crew to show up. It was a moonless night and many stars were visible and I was passing the time trying to remember the names of constellations. I noticed 3 brighter stars that formed a perfect triangle directly overhead. I tried to place them as they were brighter than all the other stars. They were not planets, I was ruling out stars when they suddenly started rotating around the center of the triangle. Seeing them move paralyzed me. They made almost a full rotation then each one made a right corner turn and shot out to the horizon, in three different directions. I watched one until it faded into the lights of the flightline and I just caught a glimpse of another as it disappeared from view behind some nearby trees. Later, the crew arrive and said I looked like I saw a ghost. I asked if they had seen anything but they had not. I have never told them or anyone about this and having been at a place as notorious as RAF Bentwaters I didn't think anyone would believe me anyway.  This was 37 years ago and I still have nightmares about this. I have had another encounter in Nebraska but I will file another report for that one.
