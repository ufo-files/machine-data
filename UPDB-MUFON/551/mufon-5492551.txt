The evening of Monday June 8, 2015 at 9:41 p.m., I took two videos of another unusual flashing ship that travelled from the direction of the lakes in a westerly direction. The craft was visible at an angle of about 201 altitude from the horizon. The first video lasted 20 seconds and the second, which was more stable, lasted 16 seconds.
The red energy field reappeared in both videos. This time however instead several new shapes in the red field occurred. At one point in frame 9:24 it seems as if a hole was appearing in part of the spherical red energy field. In frame 16:03, the craft even seems to be absorbing the red field.
It now appears that the red energy fields are not restricted to simple spherical shapes as several finger-like projections appear alongside the spherical field. There also seems to be some method or purpose with the ship darting into the red energy, possibly 1mining1 it. That seems to be the case at least in frame 9:24.

From the second video, the image of frame 7:11 shows the craft in different forms of its morphological changes. The first one has a curious almost 1L1 shaped appendage that occasionally is visible. 

Most often however, the triangular shaped craft is seen with two of its lights, then one goes out and the third light is shown. I took two frames depicting that and photomerged them to get a better view of the craft. That is what is seen in the merge of frames 7:35 and 7:39. It is definitely a triangular shaped craft.
