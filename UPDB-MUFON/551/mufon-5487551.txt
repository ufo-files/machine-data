Bright light during day time - UFO?

Arriving home my 2 year old daughter pointed to the sky, above us a street light but as I moved I noticed a bright fixed object on the sky at very high altitude. In a outer orbit probably.
No movement was noticed. It allowed me to get to the 7th floor and from my varanda take several photos that I compiled in a video.
for more than an hour it stood there Even approaching mid day and the sun getting close to it's zenith it kept shining bright, very bright.
At the firs glance I thought it was a planet or a star but it was almost noon. I Immediately started to reason and thought of satellites, stars, planets, but no this wasn't it!
So I searched on the web for possible visible plantes or man made objects and none was listed.
There was zero movement on the object.
I mounted my 75-300 mm lens on my Nikon D90 And had a hard time to pull focus on the object as I had no tripod. Even so I made several shots, hover a hundred, to try and document.
