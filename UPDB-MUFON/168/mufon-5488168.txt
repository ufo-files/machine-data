{cak} BROOKLYN 1967

I WAS 18 AT THE TIME, LIVING IN BROOKLYN NY. I WAS LEANING ON THE STOP SIGN POLE ON THE CORNER OF 12AVE, & 43RD. STREET IT WAS BETWEEN 3AM & 3:30AM I WAS TALKING TO A GIRL THAT COULDN'T BE SEEN BECAUSE SHE WAS SITTING ON A STEP IN THE DOORWAY OF THE BUILDING. I NEVER LOOKED HER WAY AT THE TIME BECAUSE I COULDN'T BELIEVE WHAT I WAS SEEING. THERE WAS A VERY LARGE UFO, THE KIND THAT PEOPLE NORMALLY SEE, THE ROUND DISH WITH A DOME IN THE CENTER WITH LIGHTS GOING AROUND THE LOWER PART OF IT, THERE WAS THREE COLORS BUT I COULD ONLY REMEMBER THE TWO THAT I MENTIONED. IT WAS A BIT INTO THE BLOCK ON 43RD. STREET NOT IN THE CENTER OF THE TWO STREETS. IT WAS ALMOST AS WIDE AS THE TWO BUILDINGS WERE ON EITHER SIDE OF THE STREET. IT MADE NO SOUND IT WAS JUST HOVERING AND I SURE AS HECK KNEW SOMETHING WAS LOOKING AT ME I FELT IT BIG TIME AND I COULDN'T STOP LOOKING AT IT. AS I SAID I JUST KEPT ON TALKING TO THE GIRL AND WHEN I FELT I COULD STOP LOOKING AT IT I WAVED FOR THE GIRL TO COME LOOK AT IT BUT BEFORE SHE TOOK A STEP IT TOOK OFF IN A DOWNWARD DIRECTION HEADED TOWARDS 42ND. STREET AND IT WAS GONE. I HAVE NO IDEA WHERE IT COULD OF WENT BUT IT WAS GONE. I TRULY FEEL IT WOULD OF TRIED TO TAKE ME AWAY IF I HADN'T WAVED FOR THE GIRL TO LOOK. BACK THEN IN THE 60'S I WAS A WEIGHT LIFTER AND A TOUGH GUY BUT IT SCARED THE CRAP OUT OF ME, I TOLD THE GIRL TO GO IN AND I WENT UP TO MY APARTMENT. I CAN NOT REMEMBER ANYTHING AFTER I TOLD HER TO GO IN, ITS ALL A BLANK.
