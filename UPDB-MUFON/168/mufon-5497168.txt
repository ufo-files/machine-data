Object filmed from International Space Station (ISS)

Hi,

We were looking at the live stream of the ISS on the 28/12/14 at around 9.40PM (Australia/Sydney Time).When the ISS was about over France, we saw the camera move and focus towards the object (pic attached). Someone (from the ground or from the ISS I don't know) was moving the cam to look at this object. My wife, my daughter and myself were surprised and did not understand what we were seeing, the object had small lights in the middle flashing (blue and my daughter remember the light turning red as well).
I was joking and said that was an alien ship... my wife had the quick idea to do a print screen.
The camera, after maybe 1 minute of observation seemed to focus more on the object then zoomed, but did not zoomed on the object as we were hopping, it zoomed into the clouds, then the live stream stopped.
We stayed there for a while for the live stream to reappear, when the live stream restarted we heard a voice with a russian accent that said: "Computer rebooted as requested".
That is all.
The object we saw did not seem to move... well it was certainly moving at the exact same speed the ISS was...When it appeared to the screen, it is when the camera moved and looked for it, this is the impression we had. Clearly, there must be other people who saw that... is that a satellite? Is that something attached to the ISS? We would like to know!
Thank you...
Fred
