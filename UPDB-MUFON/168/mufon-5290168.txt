The three of us were fishing near the boat launch area at the Wawawai campground on the snake river above the Lower Granite dam. My friends wife was steering the boat and my friend and I were facing the back of the boat. We saw this silent motorhome sized UFO traveling and hovering over us and the lake. It had red, yellow and white lights on it. We watched it for about 15 minutes and never told the wife who had her back to us to turn around. I still go to the campground to see if it will come back.
