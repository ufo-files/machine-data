{dr}two glowing lights/objects descended to eye level

Driving down a back road, my girlfriend and I saw two glowing disc-shaped objects/lights about 7-10 inches in diameter (not too bright) come into view from far upper right and descend from at least tree top level, to about four feet off the ground. during the descent, they closed from what seemed like ten feet apart to headlight distance apart, then remained at this height and distance apart for about two seconds. they seemed to be spinning, and during the entire experience were moving rapidly directly toward the car at what seemed like at least automobile speed, then right when i thought i was about to "hit a ghost car head on" and went for the brakes, these lights/objects made it RIGHT up to the lowest part of the windshield and then seemed to go through the car, leaving a physical and mental feeling that i really hit something (or more like it went through us). it felt like a wave went through our bodies and almost felt like we died for a second. This was a back road in the pine barrens, with no objects around to cause reflection or anything of the sort. there were no lights on or cell phone on inside the car either, i know because ive been trying to find any sort of logical explanation, but I have nothing. there was no water around, no fog,just a dark road, and these...things.
