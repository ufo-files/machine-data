Light with radar beam at Ocean Shores

Stayed at the Quinault beach and resort casino on June 6-7th It was 1:15 am. I saw two people walking on the beach with flashlights so I decided to record them through my hotel window. There was also a boat out on the water. I recorded them walking around for about 9 mins. Around the 6:45 time the camera picks up some light in the left hand corner of the frame. I didn't realize the light was there right away because I couldn't see the light with my naked eye. Only the camera. My camera then shuts off and I have to start recording again. The next video is about 4:51. This video has some weird flashing in it. You also hear me kinda quietly freaking out because I can't understand what is going on and my fiance is sleeping. The camera shuts off again. I next get about 27 secs of video and it shuts off and then I start again and I get about 14 seconds. After that my phone said no more storage. So, I had to take pictures. This happened for about an hour before I got frustrated with my phone and the light and went to bed. I did try my fiances
phone with not the same thing happening.
I am not sure what I saw and recorded but if someone could please logically explain this I would love to know what it was.
