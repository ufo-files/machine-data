Not sure what it was, I was driving my truck down a dirt road at the La Salle Rail yard for work related reasons. I first pulled onto a dirt part that lead to a silo. Straight ahead of me I seen a weed white light hovering in the sky near some radio towers. At first I thought maybey it was a h ed helicopter but as I moved towards it you could see its outline a little as and it was more pill shaped and I couldnt hear any sounds coming from it. ( note that I was in a semi truck which is a little loud so it may have drowned out any noise from something at that distance) it had no read and green light light aircrafts and drones usually have. It began to go in a circular pattern, around what looked like the radio towers but I cannot be sure if it was circling the towers or if it was further back from the towers because distance is hard to judge in the sky. The road I was on wound to the right and it put the light outside my driver side window and that's when I stopped the truck to record the video. It was at that point I realized that it was changing from one light to 2 lights very close together than back to one light. It did this several times and circled the towers several times. When I finally got the camera going it seemed to slow a bit in another lap around the tower area before fading into thin air. I'm not sure what it was. I'm not saying its aliens or anything I'm just simply saying I couldnt identify it, it gave me chills. I thought I would send it in and see what you think. It took place in the La Salle Rail yard in Encinial Texas.
