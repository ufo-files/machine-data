{cak} Bright light in the sky, helicopter approached and passed, light got brighter and disappeared

Turning on to Fenton rd headed south from Baldwin rd coming from the east. We observed a bright light that was just hovering and the noticed a helicopter approaching(MSP). Upon the helicopter closely approaching and passing the light began to glow intensely and disappeared instantly leaving no visible trail. The glow of the light was in a crescent moon shape, possibly from a saucer shape which became more prevalent as the light intensified. After the object or light disappeared the helicopter passed and then hovered maybe 500 yards from the light disappearance.
