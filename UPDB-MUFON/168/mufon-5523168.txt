Sitting in back yard at 2 am looked left and saw the cratft at about 1,000 feet for 5 seconds between trees

I was laying in my lawn chair in my back yard this past Friday in West Hills California 10-22-11 sky watching and at approximately 1:30 am as I looked west I saw a black triangle shape craft glide through the air very fast in about a 500 foot window from tree to tree before it went out of view,the craft was at an altitude of about 500-1000 feet(it was very close),the craft was traveling North to South,at about the middle of the 5 second window of view I had, the craft banked sharply 45" and I saw its underside completely,it had 3 very dim lights shining at all 3 triangle points,there were no other lights that I could see.The craft made no noise and I CLEARLY saw the shape and form of it as it was very starry out,there was a sliver moon and the sky was completely cloudless also it was eerily quiet and you could have heard a pin drop..at the close range sighting of this craft I should have been able to have heard some type of engine if it indeed was military.. The appearance of the craft seemed almost as if it was holographic,the lights were very dim on this craft almost as if being seen through cloud cover which there was not..I did not see this craft again or any other for the remainder of the early morning into next day.The size of the craft from as much as I can judge in the time I saw it was between 30- 50 feet wide....
