About 1:20 AM Jan. 24 2014 I was in bed and heard a faint humming sound. I thought it was my generator in the garage but the sound started to get louder...I started wondering if it was my furnace. I didn't want to get up so I continued to lay there....no more than a min later the bedroom and great room filled up with a bright white light...I could see tree branches being casted on my patio doors in my bedroom... so whatever it was had to have been very low and right above the tree line at an angle. I jumped out of bed thinking that something was going to crash into the house the light was so bright...it should have brighten up the other two houses on either side of me...( At this time I have not checked with the neighbors)...I ran through the house looking out windows and open the front door...I did not see anything or hear anything. The noise and the light was gone that quick. I don't know what it was but I know it was not a helicopter.
