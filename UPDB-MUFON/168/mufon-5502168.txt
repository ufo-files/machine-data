{dr}glowing orange light right under cloud level

Driving up pleasant street I saw a bright orange sphere towards high street. There were people on High Street looking up at it. I watched it as I drove past Spruce Street and crossed the bridge as best I could. When I got on Brockway Ave. I pulled into the Daily Kneads parking lot to get a better stationary look. It appeared to be hovering just blow the clouds. It was stationary though. It had a slight wobble. Imagine a ping pong ball sitting on top of water. It slowing started heading north at first horizontal but then ascending up into the cloud cover. I could see the orange light reflect onto the cloud right before it entered. The period when it started moving it still maintaining a slight up and down motion, as if it was riding very shallow waves. Maybe it was the very damp air last night, but I couldn't make out a very clear shape, even though it appeared to be very close to the ground. It just seemed like a ball of bright red/orange light with very hazy edges.
