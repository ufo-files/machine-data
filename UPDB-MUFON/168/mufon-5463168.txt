{rjl} Zig-zag pattern & hovering star-like light

I was surf-fishing at New Smyrna Beach. I looked up at the stars to admire them. I noticed the constellation Orion & looked at it several times before i saw the ufo. 
When i first spotted the light i thought it was a star because it was hovering in the sky. Then i thought it was a plane because it started moving. When it zig-zagged slowly back & forth i knew it was neither a plane or star. The light was below Orion. 
I had an eerie feeling while & after observing this light in the sky. I am also excited that i have seen a ufo. I didn't stay to see it fly away because I wanted to get home to my fiancee.
