Light travelling at very high velocity below the moon.

I am a professional videographer and I was working with my new camera, a Sony NES-FX100, shooting a time lapse of the moon at my brothers farm. I didn't notice anything until I was working with the video in post and I saw this thing come in frame and move through at an incredibly high speed. I don't know if its a satellite or not, but I didn't realize satellites moved this fast. 

One interesting note is that there is a weird flash in the sky right before the object is seen in the frame. You have to look closely but you will see it.
