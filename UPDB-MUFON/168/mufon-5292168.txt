Brilliant light in the distance approx. 90 miles as crow flies

I was driving S. on 101 from Brinnon down Hood Canal. A brilliant light caught my eye to the ESE of our position.I t was WSW of Mt. Rainier,sitting still between Carbonado & Eattonville, I would say 10,000 feet up. It was still there when we emerged from the trees on the shoreline. The whole sighting was less than 3 minutes. It got brighter ,then the circular light turned counter- clockwise, & it was gone.
My husband was asleep during the whole thing.
