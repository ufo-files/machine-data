Blinking blue light that zipped away leaving a brief streak

At 10pm on Tuesday July 19th 2016 I was leaving my grandmother in laws underground parade here in South Surrey. As I drove up I looked strait at the twilight eastern sky and above the houses I saw a smooth blinking blue-white light making a sweeping turn from south east to east. Within a few seconds of watching it, the object appeared to disappear in a short streak of light. I could see the light had a dark ball behind it. I grew up on a flight path and am familiar with aircraft and I've seen plenty of drones with led lights, this was none of these. It's movement was so smooth that if I were watching it on a video I would have sworn it was cgi. I'd say it was 1000 to 1500 feet off the ground and a block and a half away. The blinking light was a crisp blue white in colour but when it shot away the brief light streak had warmer faint yellow orange in the blue. Anyway, it made my skin crawl when I saw it.
