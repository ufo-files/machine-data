Same as Belgian triangle with T shaped buddy

On or around 28 Sept 2012 I witnessed the classic [Belgian] triangle UFO but still cant fathom what I saw.
The event started with 2 lights in the distance as I was walking down the Chesterfield canal toepath, back 
home to staveley 5 miles away at around midnight. I was about 200 yards from the start of the canal, adjacent
the disused Arnold Lavers car park when I first saw the lights, headed directly my way. I jumped into the undergrowth
and ducked down to shield my eyes from 'stray' light and to open up my pupils for a better view. All the time through
this sighting I could hear helecopter(s) in the background, posibly over the horizon, but even when the UFOs passed 
overhead, there was no downdraught and the sound still appeared to be distant. So I'm squating in some brambles
when the light on the left started to change shape - from a round yellow,orange light, looking very much like a fire
balloon / chineese lantern, to a greek thorn or an upside down T, and became more yellow and started to rise to about
twice its previous height whereupon it appeared more dark red. After this, the right hand light morphed into the classic 
triangle shape. At the time I thought the centre yellow light on the 'Belgian triangle photo' was larger, but after seeing 
the picture again, realise it is smaller - just like whatever I saw here. I could see the dark triangular shape against the dark 
background as the sky wasn't as dark as the triangle. No photo - low rate DLA (for hemiplegic stroke) means zero fancy 
gadgets. And of coarse, no one else saw it! But the 'Belgium' photo is identical to what I saw. Anyway, it flew/floated on by
and am still baffled - drone?, elaborate fire balloon?, blimp? little green guy from beta ridiculeazoid?
