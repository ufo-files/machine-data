{nd} UFO skimming across the sky

On May 25 2015 I was in my bath room. I looked out the window I saw this bright gold light round shaped object. It was moving above the tree tops and then it was above some gray and black clouds. I could see it through the clouds. I stood in amazement and it kept going in a straight line towards my neighbors trees. It looked like it was going fast just skimming across the sky it had no sound.I could not believe what I was seeing and said what is that? I was so excited and ran to the front door saying I seen one. I was so excited. By the time my husband and I looked for it was gone or there was to many trees to see it. This has made me a believer. The object was shaped like a round cake pan with detail at the bottom and with the most beautiful gold color I have ever seen. This all happened at 5:23 A.M.
