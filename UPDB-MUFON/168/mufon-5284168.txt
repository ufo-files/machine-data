Strobe type light

I observed a strobe type light within the Orion constellation near the star Bellatrix around 0407 on 9/24/21 with the naked eye, thinking it might be a flickering star as the sky was clear, I grabbed my Binoculars (Tesco 16x50) to take a closer look. While observing through my binoculars I saw a strobe type light flicker on then off very fast there seemed to be a mist of some type with in the light, counting in between strobes it was approximately 19 seconds between strobes this was observed twice again
near and to the right of the star Saiph within the Orion constellation.These strobes where a quick on/off no type of FAA required light where observed i.e. red/green strobe The longest in between strobe that I observed with my binoculars was 43 seconds I no of no military/commercial type aircraft with that long of a strobe most that I have observed and counted is usually 2 seconds red on right green on left this object/lights show no signs of either This all took place from 0407-0443 no more strobes where observed with either the naked eye or with binoculars. The Orion constellation was approximately SE of my observing location my front yard the object/light seem to be moving from Southeast to Northeast. Not sure what I saw but I am with absolute certain that this was no type of aircraft that I have been around in my 25 year military career or my civilian life. This object was very high and seemed to be within the Orion constellation it's self. I got up several mornings around the same time, but I have not observed anymore strange lights in this constellation.
