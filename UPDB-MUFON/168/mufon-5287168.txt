Camouflaged UFO or Invisible Jet

I had a very strange experience on May 7, 2021 in the afternoon. Sitting on our deck with nothing but blue sky I hear and see planes all day. Sometimes I hear them but don’t see them because the roof blocks them or they fly behind us. We don't get many Lear Jets fly over at lower elevations intending to land at our local or nearby airports but I heard a Lear jet go over lower than usually and I didn’t see it because I thought it went behind us. About 1/2 later I heard another one which seemed to be coming from the NE and should be in view.

Well if you look at a blue sky you’ll see different shades of blue. Some darker, some lighter. Also, many times I’ll try and spot the helicopters as they also approach from the NE and after a few minutes I’m usually able to see them.

So I’m looking for this Lear jet which is getting louder when I start to see it. This jet is coming towards me from the NE and I see the tip of the right wing (on my left) first and then the rest of it appears as if a dark shade of the blue sky is covering it up and blocked me from seeing through it! It wasn’t a quick transition either, there was something that blocked me from seeing through that area of the sky. And, the plane couldn't have been more than 300-500 feet away from me!
