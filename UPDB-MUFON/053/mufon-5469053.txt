We are camping in the Canary Islands Spain. In the town of Tejina. The past two nights a UFO has been very close to us. It seems as though it is hovering over the shore line. The first night we saw it we thought it was a bright star but then it started moving and getting close to me. The object had bright lights and had absolutely no sound. It seemed to be aware of me and started getting closer and closer as I walked to my tent. I felt like it was a evil entity and prayed for it to stop in Jesus name and it did stop but it didn't go away. Shortly after praying it started to back up very slowly and hover to the left of my tent near the water. The lights were very bright and after about a half an hour started to fade and change. 
The second night the object was right in front of our tent!!! It was like the size of a small air plane with Windows and it hovered motionless without sound for about an hour. Then it went backward very slowly.
