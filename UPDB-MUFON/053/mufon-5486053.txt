In my neighborhood evening sky with large fluffy clouds looking south down Interstate 77 I saw a hovering object. The object appeared stationary with a wide bank of red lights located east of the interstate at an altitude of at least 1,000 feet. Not in traditional flight path of airports. It was stationary for at least 1 to 2 minutes appearing to change directions twice. While observing I did not see any other running lights but 3 to 4 large red rectangular running lights. No white or green lights observed, so it struck me odd because of no other colored lights. My location is located near both Charlotte and Concord airports with frequent Helicopters flight paths seen in the general location of observed UFO. Observed object did not appear to be a helicopter it seemed too large...I have attached a digital photo taken with camera phone.
