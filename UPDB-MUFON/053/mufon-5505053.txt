round, 6ft up, six little entities inside with a pig on the table

I finished work at six a clock then I went home then I put water in the tub. Took of my clothes then something pulled me out of the water. After that happened I went to the front porch and just looked out and there was a grey, round saucer about 6 feet off the ground over the corn field. In all at once the windows on the UFO opened and there was grey green children like entity. After that I was looking at them and they were looking at me with great big eyes,maybe a little nose, and a little small mouth with a big head. As I was looking a saw a animal but it looked like a pig and it was split open{we had pigs on the farm were I lived and work}. as it was starting to leave the windows closed and it moved across the road And it started to go up gradually then accreted and it looked like a ball of fire. Then it changed to a bright light{it seemed like the heaven opened up and it went in}. Later my family came home I tried to tell them what I saw but I couldn't. So now 20 years later I had a nightmare I woke up screaming and my voice sounded like a animal that was inside of me. so now 2014 I'm thinking something else happened to me but I don't know what.
