BIG, Metallic,about 100 feet long,silent,two long antennae on the rear

Myself and three other co-workers who worked the night cleaning/lavs crew for United Airlines airplanes, were on lunch at O'hare Airport.....this was around 02:30 am on a damp, foggy night, it had rained earlier in the night....we were right across the taxiway and hangers for United planes, behind a warehouse called Ozark ( the warehouse that provides everything from cleaning to provisioning United airplanes)...we were cooking some steaks on a small weber grill......WHEN WE SEEN IT!!!!....about 60-70 feet away and about the same distance in height (right over the taxiway) was this UFO.....I can't recall if it was I, Moe,Lydia, Bill or just all of us but.......here it came..as we watched it pass by from the right of us, I noticed the Odd looking, matallic color ( it had no seams..it shinned, and as it was in front of us, I looked into one of its many lights/windows.....they were real bright, the color was real light yellow....I was hypnotized by the light....and the craziest thing was that it made NO SOUND!!!!!...( if you had your back turned you wouldn't even know that this UFO was there) from what I remember there were about 25- 30 of these lights/windows on the side I could see....it was going approx 12-15 m.p.h if that...as We looked at the UFO pass by we heard aloud rumble in the distance (since we hear so many planes taking off and landing we could id that sound) an airplane taking off down a runway we looked to see the plane coming , quickly it was out of view due to the warehouse and other stuff blocking our sights from the otherside of where we were standing) we looked back (this is a matter of 2 seconds if that) at the UFO (which was almost in the path of the live runway) (about to crisscross) we all thought this UFO and plane were going to collide....it was nuts this UFO on the back had these two long looking antennae they were approx 15 feet long ( Bill the other witness recalled that they looked as if they were ALIVE!!!), the UFO was about 25-30 feet in width. in that same span the plane got louder, (in two seconds) this UFO had angled upwards and accellerated at such a quick speed it was gone into the clouds and fog.....and then 2 seconds later a United 747 airplane takes off!!!!.....
