Approximently 2 min after witnessing the comet

On December 15, 2011 I went out to observe the comet Lovejoy and its sister flying over our home this evening, it was so beautiful, I had my binoculars and hung out on the front walk way to observe the clouds and sunset. I was curious to see if anything else was coming! And I couldnt believe what I saw! It was a long skinney shaped object, there were 4 lights in a row, glowing the same. Nothing blinking...just a steady glow and the object made no noise. 
It was heading west, I observed it through my binoculars several minutes until it left my site. The airport is southwest from here, it was not quite heading that direction. I have never seen this shape in person but have seen similar looking objects on UFO footage. I cant wait for my next encounter! Next time...I will have the video camera charged and ready to go !!!
