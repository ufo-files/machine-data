I was on guard duty in 1969, about 3:30 am. I looked up, and saw a delta shaped object with about nine yellow lights, around 4,000 -5,000 feet. Moving very slowly across the sky. No sound, no emmissions. Saw one again, this time in Johsntown, PA, in 1976, same thing, only it was about 300 feet above my head, moving very slowly with no sound. Freaked me out.
