Multiple bright star-like objects flying over the city... A few appeared to of stopped moving

Between 11 PM & 1 AM, multiple star-like objects flew over the city. Two bright objects in particular flew one after the other heading East until they appeared to of stopped moving. Several other dim star-like objects sighted. One sighting of likely a military aircraft with a 10 second interval flash followed a dim star-like object heading North.
