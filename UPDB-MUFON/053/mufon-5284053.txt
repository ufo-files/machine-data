Yellow, glowing orb

Bright yellow light in eastern sky around 1015 pm, evening of November 22nd. Confirmed by Facebook, Port Orchard community page. I thought it was the moon shining through my bedroom. I thought, "oh finally the aliens are here." Went to the bathroom, convinced it was the moon. Looked again after about 2 minutes and it was gone. I have been abducted prior to this, but couldn't confirm it until speaking with my adult son. He remembers seeing the 9 yellow rectangular bars, under a triangular ship. He didn't have the memory until recently. We were literally sharing our experiences the day before this occurred.
