Initially thought it was a star until I noticed it was moving.Then thought satellite, until it turned suddenly and sharply.

Abbotsford BC Canada, July 10, 2014, approximately 2:30 AM, away from city lights. An email I sent to a friend minutes later. After thinking more about it, I believe the angle of direction change was at least 45 degrees:

They fly among us!
I just saw a UFO !!!! 2:30 AM

Having another smoke, I looked up as usual. I spotted a light way up in the sky, figured it was a star. But it was moving quickly. This was no star! Then I figured maybe a satellite - I see them once in a while. Then the object took a sharp turn! maybe 30 degrees or more. Not an arc but a sudden turn. A few more seconds and it was gone.

This was way the f*ck up there - I'm thinking the upper end of what a commercial airline would do. But normal planes can't turn like this did.

Perhaps a military plane? Maybe, but this thing was fast, much faster than a satellite, much faster than jets I see up high during the day. And that turn at that speed would likely kill the pilot and destroy the plane. 

The light source could easily have been reflected sunlight as this was so high and in the west north west sky, yet laterally not far.  I'm not a subscriber to the assertion that ufos have loads of flashing lights on them.

Overall my viewing lasted maybe 10 - 15 seconds.

Very similar to the videos on Youtube purporting to be ufos videoed by Apollo/Shuttles. Only I just saw this in person with my own two eyes.
