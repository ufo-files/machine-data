Shooting star type object, did not burn out, changed course twice

I was outside walking the dogs at about 1:26am, looking up at Orion and I was lucky enough to see a shooting star, duller ang orange/copper color, except it wasn't. It didn't burn out.
It kept on going, at very high speed. It banked to the right and then banked to the left on it's original course to the south. No emission trail.
My conclusion is that this object is a piece of classified advanced terrestrial technology given that it banked like a conventional aircraft, except at very high speed, and it did appear to be above the atmosphere because it had no streaking behind it.
