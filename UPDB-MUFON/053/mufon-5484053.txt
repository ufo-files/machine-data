{cak} 2 lights fixed 100 feet apart hovering above school then moving at extreme speed

So my friend and I were out flying his drone. Well as soon as we flew the drone up into the air I saw these two lights, about 500 feet up directly above us just shoot off heading NE. The lights appeared to be a fixed distance apart of about 100 ft. The oddest thing besides their incredible speed and silence was that they were semi transparent, as if they were from a cloaked ship.
