{rjl} Bright strobe light, then another a short distance away.

I was outside having a cigarette as usual, and also as usual I like to look at the sky to see where the planets and stars are at the moment. It was about 5:30 am and I was watching Jupiter rise when directly under Jupiter a light that started dim and became brilliantly bright and then gone. A second later the same thing a little further north. Then I don't know if it was just coincidence but about 1 minute later I saw what looked like a fairly bright star track quickly across the entire sky from north to south. I am assuming this was a satellite or something manmade but just a little brighter than normal because I usually cant see satellites from my house because of light pollution.
