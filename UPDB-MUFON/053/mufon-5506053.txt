{rjl} Strobing white lights varied in size and direction but stayed mostly in one spot

I observed this object on and off for the next three hours. I went out about 9:30 and the object had left or had gone up into the clouds. Although I have obseverved him react to other UFOs this is the first time I have ever heard him growl at one. It was a contrast to the other lights that are a stationary tower light which in our area are generally red and blink on and off at regular intervels. What ever this was it was not a normal human air craft. No piolet in his right mind would be flying in this weather. The altitude was very low and I don't think even a helicopter would have hung around that long in one place.
