Saw a hovering fire in the sky that didnt move

I was driving home from a New Years party. I was designated driver. I had just passed the 315/270 interchange driving on 315 north. I looked up in the sky to look at the stars while sitting at a light. I looked out the window to my left and noticed a fire in the sky. I could clearly see the flames. The object didn't move in any direction just hovered in the sky. At first I thought it was a plane on fire or a meteor, but the fact that it didn't move in any direction made me wonder why it wasnt falling. I showed the fire to my passenger who also describe it as a fire just kind of hovering there. I started driving when the light change to green and kept obvserving it until I was too far away and some trees obscured my view. The object just hung there for at least 5 - 6 minutes that I could track. 

For some reason I cannot get this image out of my mind. I would like to know if it was a plane on fire, or a meteor or something else. Do you know of anyone else that might have witnessed this? Do you know of where I can find out if this was a plane? 

This is not the first time I have seen strange lights, just the first time I have reported it. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact me.
