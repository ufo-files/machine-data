Varying number of stationary black spheres (up to 5) appearing and disappearing over Ocean City, New Jersey’s boardwalk

There was a black sphere in the sky above the boardwalk of Ocean City New Jersey. The sphere was stationary and eventually more appeared. Some of spheres would randomly disappear and appear again a minute or two later. The spheres were way too high up to be helicopters or birds and they were spherical and seemingly stationary so it’s unlikely they were planes. They were seen by one other person I was with.
