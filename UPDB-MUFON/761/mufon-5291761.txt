Large craft captured on camera while taking picture of a star

I was camping in the Selway-Bitterroot Wilderness on July 16th, 2020 and decided to try and figure out the Night mode on my phone. It was completely dark with and there was no light pollution. I don't believe that the moon was out that night and if it was it was hidden behind the mountains or trees. I held my phone over my head with the screen pointing directly down and tried to take a picture of a fairly bright star hoping that I would at least get a faint picture of a constellation. Again, I was just trying to figure out if the Night mode would make any sort of difference. After taking the picture I looked at it and saw an odd blue cloudy area in the top right so I zoomed in on the photo and saw a weird triangle/shark mouth type of thing. After that I started examining the rest of the image and saw a long rectangular type shape to the left and noticed unexplainable structure and some lights. The top and bottom of this part of image seemed blurred. Not sure if that's because it was in motion? I took another picture in the same spot and in the same exact manner but got nothing.
