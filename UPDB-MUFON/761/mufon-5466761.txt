Driving south-west, daylight, and it stopped right beside me, setting at an angle, where I could observe it out my driver window. 
All that week I felt terrorized. 
Another account. Heading to the store late before they could close. I observed 6 or more beings of different sizes. Dark figures, but could see their shape.
Hit my breaks, then gas, brakes, gas until Fear won out and I fled the area. But I don't remember really making the store or getting home. 
All this week these things happened. Ships over my house. I called Rickenbaker air bas3 3 or 4 times that week and per night. They sent choppers out. Told me there were not just 3 but 100s in the area!
I half remember them interviewing me. I had taken pictures. The camera was diapered even. I only remember bits and pieces.
Tormented all week, so much so my family thought they would have to come and check on me from out of town. I also thought at one time I carried a hybrid.
