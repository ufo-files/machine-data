It was like a meteor but suddenly it went up! Brightness kept the same

I was going to the mail box at around 6:30pm and I looked up and saw a red trail like a meteor for 5 sec, however unlikely a meteor that keep getting brighter and brighter, this one kept always its red brightness constant and out of the blue it went up! At a very high speed and disappeared. It was really strange. Red trail, red light and bam, up it went very fast.
