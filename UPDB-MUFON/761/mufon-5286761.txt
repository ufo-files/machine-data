Massive triangular craft

We witnessed a huge triangular craft with rounded edges and three lights. This thing was BIG. it was silent. The lights were constant and pulsated every so often. It flew just under clouds slowly and the light reflected of clouds illuminating a solid triangular steucture with three lights on the corners. Animals in the neighborhood were going crazy. A highway patrolman and other community members also saw this.
