Saw two ufos the size of golf balls

I heard a banging at my door and opened the door quickly expecting kids up to no good and saw two two inch orbs gyrating three feet in front of my face. I remember thinking that I needed to get one or both of them into my house. But before I could reach for them they went into matching manouvers and shot away to the south at the speed of light. They were gone. No pictures. Just a memory of the smallest ufos I’ve ever heard of. [ Item Moved to F I Report {tjd}
