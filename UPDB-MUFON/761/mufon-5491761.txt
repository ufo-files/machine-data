{dr}Bright orange glow hovering close by with triangle shape in middle, headed N, stopped and then disappeared

I walked out my front door at 11:40 p.m. on 7-4-15 with my adult son. I saw a big orange-red glow above my line of vision. I knew it was not fireworks or an airplane because it was not moving and was too low. It appeared to have a glowing aura around it, and I could see a triangle shape within the glowing aura. My son and I watched it for a couple of minutes, then it began to move toward the north. I went inside the house to get the binoculars. I was not able to see much other than a ball of orange light because it had moved further away, among the treetops. At one point, it stopped for about a minute or less and then moved on and disappeared. I felt I had actually seen a UFO because I had never seen anything like this before. I told my son to film it with his phone, but all he got, by that time, was an orange ball in the sky, moving north.
