Orange orb being followed by four aircraft.

I noticed an orange orb hoing across the sky to the north of town while i was in my backyard. As I watched it i noticed two planes of some sort were following it and they had flashing white lights on em. Then I noticed two more red flashing lights behind them. Each set of flashing lights was far enough apart to look like seperate aircraft. The four aircraft followed the orb across he sky for a couple of minutes until the orb disappeared. The aircraft seemed to survey the area because they reappeared after disappearing behind the treeline.
