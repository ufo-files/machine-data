{cak} Orange reddish glow moving across the sky, 2 times.

I was out on my lanai watching fireworks with my husband when we noticed a red-orange object flying. It out shined the fire works. At first we thought it was a space station, but Ive seen them before and they were never that big or that color. The way it flew or floated resembled the flight path of one. It flew over us and continued on till we could no longer see it. We commented that it was going to fast for a plane, ha ha must be a UFO. Meanwhile we were mesmerized by it and found ourselves ignoring the fireworks and staring at this orange thing flying. Five minutes later, it did the exact same thing. Now we know somethings "off", as nothing I know of can go around the earth that fast. No it did not just circle around like a helicopter, it flew horizon to horizon. It flew SE-NW. Didnt sleep well.
