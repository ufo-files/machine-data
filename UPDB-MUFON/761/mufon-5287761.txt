I was driving my cousin home on Schafer road about to head under the bridge when I seen 4 orbs. 2 by 2 moving in a circle like pattern.

There were four orbs of light. Two of them moved simultaneously together while the other two did the same. The distance between them didn’t change at all and seemed to be very well controlled. They were moving synchronous without any obstruction in their shapes or distance between. I tried my best to record a video in which you can see the orbs of you play it frame by frame. This is the first time something caught my eye if this type and left me in awe. I tried circling back after dropping my cousin off to find them again but they were nowhere to be seen!
