{nd} Witnessed a diamond shaped object moving very fast with white circles at the bottom of the air craft

I was out on my deck, it was clear and quite night, I was starring up in the sky when movement caught my eye in the south, I saw an object moving very fast with several white circles barley visible, at first I thought it was birds until it got directly above me, I could see the white circles that were barely visible and perfectly symmetrical, also I could see the edges of the object, the object was camouflaged or transparent as it took on the color of the back ground it was against. It flew to the north and disappeared. It was moving at a very high rate of speed, about the same speed as a jet that does a fly over at a stadium. The object made no sound at all.
