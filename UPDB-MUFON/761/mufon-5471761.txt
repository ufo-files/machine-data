{cak} Like a dream

I was asleep, and then I could feel something around me and I slowly opened my eyes everything was very blurry and I could see three figures one standing directly over me to looking at a big wall of different colored lights in front of me. As I open my eyes and saw the figure standing above me I felt thrown back and then I was back in my bed. I could not move my body and I looked around the room with my eyes and I tried to open my mouth and call my girlfriend who is laying next to me and the only thing I could make was a small sound and then my eyes shut and I was back asleep and she can confirm that I made a weird noise that night. The next day we saw on the news that there were several unidentified flying objects over Lexington County.
