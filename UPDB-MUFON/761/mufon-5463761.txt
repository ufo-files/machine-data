{rjl} Nocturnal lights that changes in brightness and color

My grandfather and I were star gazing when all of a sudden, what I thought was an air plane ' quickly' flew into view, it then stopped grew in brightness, which I had thought now to be a bolite, then it sort of bobbed up and down several times before casting an intense beam of light! Thinking it was a helicopter, it began to move unlike any aircraft I've ever seen, and I grew up next to an Air force base so I know what aircraft( planes- balloons) look like. As it moved erratically overhead it flew south of us before becoming as bright as the moon, and dimmed to a pin point of light ( a green flash) which started pulsing! It stopped, stayed steady green went to flashing again and flew so fast as to clear the sky in a matter of seconds, as it now moved to the north, north/east. I can say now with out a doubt I am a believer now! Mind you several other people were with us, but were terrified!!
