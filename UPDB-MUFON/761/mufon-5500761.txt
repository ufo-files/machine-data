There was no sound made from this object and by now the Lake was very quite and peaceful! WE continued to watch it hover way above us, then suddenly it disappeared,I mean completely Vanished before our eyes. We were all astonished! This was my 1st UFO sighting and it was for my friends as well. I am a firm believer in UFO'S and I know what we saw July 4th was not of this world.
