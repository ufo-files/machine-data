hovering over a train bridge by water

it was a nice summer night, and three of my friends and me were riding around in a car. when we came to what we call the pond, we noticed something just hovering over the train bridge. we stopped and stared, the thing made no noise, but suddenly lit up with bright white lights, and slowly proceded over the tree line and out of sight! at first we thought it was a helicopter, but there was absolutely no noise. we all just kind of sat there laughing about what we just seen. then we just rode away. we were teens then and no we were not drinking. this was at a time when there were lots of sightings around hermansville. we never said this to anyone before. thank you mike petrick
