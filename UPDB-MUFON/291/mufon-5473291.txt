{dr}Thought there were 2 star together. Rapid and brightly twinkling. Cool colors. Hovered. Obscured by cirrus clouds later ~ 20k+ ft cloud ceiling.

Stepped outside and noticed what looked like two bright stars or planets touching each other to my WNW from Seguin, Texas. Lights seemed more intense for a star or stars. Pulsating and twinkling. Looked through 8x45 binoculars and noticed colors like multi-colored Christmas lights all in one. Got my camera and took a few photos. They were all blurry except one photo which can be zoomed into. The others show a disk shape I presume due to blurriness. The object stayed in one place flashing. A cluster of high cirrus clouds came over and obscured my view or it left. I checked back an hour later, but the high clouds were still there.
