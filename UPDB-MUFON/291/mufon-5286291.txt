Daytime tracking by 3 glowing objects of a boat in a Norwegian Achipelago

Found on Google Maps.

Three objects that are glowing orange appear to be tracking a boat in the Nordvest-Spitsbergen National Park, Norway. I will try to imbed or attach links. 

This boat travels several miles and runs a Google Maps Street view.

When you look directly overhead, or to the right between 10 and 20 degrees from the nose, you will see what look like one or two burned out pixels. 

If you zoom in on these pixels, you will see 3 objects. The two that are directly overhead look (unfortunately, perhaps) like a tic tac or a propane tank standing on end. The 3rd, which is always to the right, looks like a three dimensional, elongated diamond. The zoom on my computer monitor makes it look as though there is transparent structure around this diamond, but it may only be camera artefacts. 
If you look at other ship tracks in this area on google maps streetview, you will see the same objects on almost all of them.

I have posted this info to s/UAP on Reddit, and have reached out to a high profile UFO person in hopes of having this visual documentation preserved before google decides to wipe it, so someone else at least sees it.

These are small lights with definite shapes that appear to be following this boat. There must be 100s of pictures in this google street view. Please, please look and see what you think, so this finding does not get wiped.
