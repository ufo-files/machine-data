Convoy of 20+ objects

Looked up in night sky and saw a series of 20+ objects moving fast, single-file, evenly spaced. No contrails, no sound, brightly lit. The light they emitted was a steady white. They lacked any lights normal aircraft use to reveal position (red or white flashing lights). I went inside to get my girlfriend and ran back out. They traveled SE and one-by-one just blipped out of existence all at the same point overhead... blip-blip-blip-blip. I know there’s strange stuff going on around here with Bell Helicopter and Pantex here, but this was strange. Too fast for ‘copters
