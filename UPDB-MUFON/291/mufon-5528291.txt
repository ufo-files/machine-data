A flaming meteorite that stopped suddenly & spread into 3 balls of light.

I was sitting on my couch watching tv. My sister was just walking out the door to hang out with her friends when all of a sudden she called for me frantically. I ran outside just in time to see a ball of light split into 3 balls. They hovered for about 15 seconds before going out one by one. Then everyone told me about how it had been a giant fireball. My sisters friend got at least one good photo and I got a video. Unfortunately, the video cuts out after 2 seconds. This morning my friend said the news was saying it was a skydiver with flares. I do not believe that one bit.
