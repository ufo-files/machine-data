Over 40 constant lights moving same speed

I witnessed between 40-50 small lights high in the atmosphere around 8.40pm positioned at around 9 o'clock from the moon's position, placed between 3 stars that align next to eachother. These small lights would suddenly enter our atmosphere, move along at the same speed, sometimes slightly monourveing left to right in sharp quick movements, all at the same distance apart, following the same path between those 3 stars. There was no sound, no flashing and no trails, on one occasion between 25-30 lights there was two brighter lights moving together maybe 200ft apart but using the same formation and line as the others. My dad also seen these as I called him to go outside and see what I was watching. To me it seemed to perfect and precise to be anything else like a meteor shower, shooting stars or planes. They travelled the same speed, same distance apart, and same line of journey, apart from those two I mentioned that were abreast of eachother and were brighter. I'm not sure if they were lights on the end of the object that may have been black or just not visible, or two separate lights moving in coordination. Unfortunately my phone camera wasn't strong enough to pick up any pictures or videos, but this is my very first massive experience of something like this and never ever had a thought of ever reporting anything like this, but last night with what we seen, it's impossible not to say something, I'm more than convinced it was extraterrestrial.
