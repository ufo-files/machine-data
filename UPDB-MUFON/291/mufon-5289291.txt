I saw 2 glowing/pulsating orbs flying in a serpentine path one following the other.

I saw 2 glowing/pulsating orbs move across the sky from west to southeast. This was about 8:30 pm. About an hour earlier, I heard 3 very loud explosion type noises. I have no idea it the sounds were related or not. This area is rural and at that time of night you can hear a pin drop. The explosive noises were NOT fireworks or guns. The sighting happened when I let my dog out and noticed the first orb flying in a serpentine type flight path. It made no sound and was glowing orange/red moving at a relatively low speed. No typical aircraft lighting was seen. The second identical object followed the first one as it weaved a path in the sky until it went out of my field of view.
