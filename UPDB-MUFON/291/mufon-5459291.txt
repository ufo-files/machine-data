{cak} Orbs of light on game camera.

We have multiple pictures taken on our game camera. Orbs of light look like headlights in the sky, then there will be a third light or they separate and go in different directions. Sometimes they look like they are shining down, other times they don’t. Their are deer in the photos and they are looking at the lights as well.
