Two green lights in still photo of night sky

While taking still photos of Sirius and Orion with night mode camera on cell phone, we took several photos of the same scene. In one photo we noticed two green dots so we zoomed in and noticed one disk shaped object with what appear to be windows and a green glowing light from inside. There was another green object to the east of it that was smaller, glowed green and appeared to have a dome on top and small windows. Objects appeared in only one of the two photos taken. Second photo is zoomed in.
