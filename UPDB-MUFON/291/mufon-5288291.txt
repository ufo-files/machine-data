Round Sphere

Sphere viewing took place on Aug 1967 as I was was driving back to Ellensburg wa . It hovered near the Bonneville power lines then floated over new constructed highway 10 which was not yet open. I drove down the new highway to get closer. It floated down to the Columbia River, crossed and then rapidly climbed the hills and disappeared. I could never hear anything. 
I drove on to Vantage and called in my report to State police. No response. 
The Orb seem to be coming from Titan Missile base near Royal City Washington.. I used to work on that base while I was going to college.
I drove that same road every day for two years later and never saw the orb again.
I worked in Moses Lake as a teacher and went to School in Ellensburg.
