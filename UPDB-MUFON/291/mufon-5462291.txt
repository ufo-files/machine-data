Driving home from work I noticed a light in the sky. I didn't think it was a star because it was larger than anything in the sky. This was looking out of my drivers side window. That would be to the ESE. I would continue down the highway until I took a bend to the south on another highway. It followed my car at that point, which I thought was strange that it changed directions as if following me. It followed me maybe a mile or so to my road. As I made my right hand turn it abruptly shot off to the east.
