It was a bright green ball with a white halo around it. The ball was huge not like a shooting star. The tail was green and yellow and probably 2 feet long from my perspective so it was freaking huge. It first started falling vertically toward earth and then paralleled the earth. It was behind trees for a little bit and was so bright I could see the light through the gaps in the trees. It then continued for a few seconds and then the light just turned off.
