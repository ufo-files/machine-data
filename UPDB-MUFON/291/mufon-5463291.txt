I TRAVELED WESTERN OK. AS A SALES MAN IN THE 60S AND 70S. IN JULY OF 1973 I WAS DRIVING NORTH OUT OF THOMAS, OK. CROSSING A LONG NARROW BRIDGE OVER THE CANADIAN RIVER. AS I WAS CROSSING THE BRIDGE I OBSERVED TWO DISCS AT TWELVE O'CLOCK HIGH. THEY APPEARED THE SAME COLOR AS THE MOON IN DAYLIGHT AFTER CROSSING THE BRIDGE AND STOPPING I OBSERVED A DISC FLYING A CIRCULAR ORBIT TO THE WEST NORTH WEST. IT APPEARED TO BE AT A MUCH LOWER ALTITUDE. IT SEEMED TO VARY IN COLOR FROM WHITE TO BRONZE. I GOT A FULL FRONTAL VIEW OF THE OBJECT. IT WAS APPARENTLY TRAVELING AT HIGH SPEED. I WOULD COME TO A COMPLETE STOP THEN MADE A VERY SLOW TURN. AT THE APEX OF THE TURN IT APPEARED VERY THIN. IT MAD 3 OF THESE ORBITS THEN FLEW SOUTHWEST OUT OF SIGHT. I KEPT THINKING THIS ISN'T RIGHT. I NEVER REPORTED THE INCIDENT THINKING NO ONE WOULD BELIEVE ME. I HAVE NEVER OBSERVED ANOTHER UFO SINCE
PS INCIDENT WAS AT AROUND 300 PM.
