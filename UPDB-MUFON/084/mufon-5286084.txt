{large explosion of color crashing out of the sky} followed by ufo over house

In 2010 early in the morning my sister and I were waiting for the bus while viewing the stars over the airport, we had seen a large explosion of color crashing out of the sky. The colors that exploded were pink, blue, green and purple. It was loud when it exploded. Later that night after we had gotten home and laid down for bed I had looked up and a UFO was hovering over the sky light over my bed with lights on the bottom that was an oscillating pattern in the color of the rainbow, starting with red and ending with purple. It was a grey saucer with a cut in-half orange or pizza-like pattern for the colors to light up in. Once my sister and I noticed it and started screaming it zoomed off southeast and made no sound.
