{cak} Two disk shaped objects hovering then shooting into the sky

On 01/06/2018, at approximately 04:06 ET, myself and four other people witnessed two disk or oval shaped objects hovering about 500 ft, above Bear Ridge, just North East of the Capon Springs and Farms Hotel and resort. These objects were not drones or terrestrial aircraft (unless there has been gravity defying technology created). The objects did not appear to make any noise and was a glowing white / blueish with yellow. There did not appear to be any sort of navigation lights on the objects as would be typically observed on man made aircraft. The objects were observable for approximately 2 minutes before they shot rapidly toward the sky into a low cloud cover. There was no smoke trail or anything that would be associated with a typical propulsion system. The incident took place above Bear Ridge, in Capon Springs, West Virginia. Bear Ridge is situated North East of the Capon Springs and Farms Hotel & Resort. The coordinates according to Google Earth Are: 39.1347721,-78.4720227.139.
