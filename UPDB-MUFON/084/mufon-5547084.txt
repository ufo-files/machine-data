First a large round orange(not fireball) size of full moon,a few minutes later another but this stayed level in sky and moved level at speed into the sitance of the sea.

I was outside looking for my cat, and out of the corner of my eye I saw bright orange colour, as I focused It came from above the hill behind me and came down to earth not changing in size,it must have fallen somewhere before Limassol.It was so orange it just looked like the moon had fallen out of the sky.
I ran to my neighbour but it had gone out of sight, and just as I started to go back home, another appeared and he saw this,it was the same size and colour and it was about 4 minutes after the first one.
But this one stayed level,difficult to judge how high, but clearly visible on a humid night,It just moved about as fast as a light aircraft over Pafos town and out to sea until it disappeared because of distance, it didn't just disapear.
I have seen comets here, amazing ones with big blue flames behing them, coming down,but this is a first and amazing,so orange and big.
I have worked all my life as an airhostess and retired now.I have seen meteor showers, but this was so close to earth.
I am sure many must have seen this in Cyprus.
The first one frightened me as it was so big and coming down pretty fast,I thought there might be an explosion or house damage,and when I saw another my mind thought it must be fragments of a planet that exploded,I really didn't know,but I will never forget this amazing feeling of being vulnerable and how we forget how little our planet is.
