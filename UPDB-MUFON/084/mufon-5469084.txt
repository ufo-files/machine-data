{cak} I was thinking I would really like to see a UFO while reading about CSETI. Something in my mind said

I was outside of Wal-Mart, reading about the CSETI program on my mobile phone. As I was reading the website, I thought to myself that I would like to have some manner of extraterrestrial experience, to validate the phenomenon in my mind. It was right after I thought this, that a thought was interjected into my mind, which simply said "Well, look up!" 
When I looked up, I did not immediately see the craft, but I did notice that the air was "sparkly," or filled with small luminous motes which seemed to almost behave like gnats or mosquitoes. There was also a misty spot of cloud on a very clear day at roughly 30 degrees high from the horizon. 
Very shortly, where the spot of cloud had been, I saw a dark object hovering in the sky. It resembled an elongated trapezoid, broader on the bottom than the top, and in the top center there was a triangular point. It seemed hazy gray in color, but that may have been a distortion created by it's apparent distance. It was very small, quite distant, it seemed.
I began thinking "Is that it? Is that you?" As I was very mystified, given that it continued to hover. I thought then "Can you come closer?" Not reckoning that I was standing outside a busy Wal-Mart, and it was then that the craft flew off, extremely swiftly to my right, due south, behind the building where I couldn't see it further.
