Cluster of bright white lights moved very quickly then hovered

I saw weird flashes of lights out of my window when I woke up to use the bathroom. When I looked out the window there were a few flashes of lights all across the sky that did not seem to be like lightning. Then a bright white cluster of lights caught the corner of my eye. The object(s) was at about 215-220 degrees southwest about 25 degrees above the ground, and then quickly descended about 5 degrees and slightly to the west, and then hovered for about 3 minutes. When I was looking at the object(s) the sky seemed to glitch a bit like a computer or TV screen. I took a note of what I saw on my phone and then went back to sleep.
