{kt}Bright green disc with green/white trail

I had gotten off work shortly after 2am, two of my friends were waiting for me. I got into the front passenger seat of my friends car while the other sat in the back. We went to the gas station and then grabbed a quick bite to eat. Upon returning to my place of employment where my car was parked, we parked facing west across 3 spots rather than pulling into one spot, parallel to the curb. My friend and I in the front seat were looking ahead at the horizon while my other buddy in the back seat was on his phone. Suddenly, a giant green disc with a green/white tail appeared in view flying from northeast to southwest came into view. It looked like it was on fire, but instead of orange it was green. I'd say it was about 70ft in diameter flying about 400 feet above the ground, with the tail trailing another hundred feet or so. The object was travelling at a high speed, must have been hundreds of miles an hour since it disappeared from our view in no more than 4 seconds. My friend and I in the front seat let out a few "holy s***s" "WTFs" "Oh my gods" and "What is that" during and after. Our friend in the back unfortunately missed the short-lived event. We then went driving in the direction it went for about 20 minutes looking for evidence of a crash or landing but found nothing.
