Fireball UFO seen cruising over Winnipeg

It was 11:21 PM and there were 9 of us sitting in the backyard at a friends house and one of us yelled to look to the south side of the yard in the sky. There was a bright orange glowing/luminescent orb/fireball traveling west very fast then turned north around the treeline. Once past the treeline it seemed to hover and descend a large distance very quickly. Then seemed to almost recover and continue on its path northbound until out of sight past the horizon towards the northwest, which is about 10 km from our location, towards headingley. It looked quite large and was travelling very fast. It was viewed by all of us for about three straight minutes. It almsot looked like it was on fire, I caught some of the event on cell phone video, although the video doesnt quite due the sighting justice. Also the object made no noise at all.
