{cak} 3 unidentified craft flew over head while ascending and dissappear from view

3 craft which I first thought were birds or aircraft due to their shape. They were reflective in the center and appeared to have a reddish/darker color where "wings" would be. They made no sound and as I observed them they changed formation and direction once or twice before appearing to increase elevation until they were no longer visible. I live right next to Buckley air force base and I am familiar with normal aircraft. these objects made no sound and either cloaked or ascended put of the atmosphere. Due to their behavior and odd flight I felt this needed to be reported.
