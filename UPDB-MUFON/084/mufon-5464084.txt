{jpg} A clear, see- through disc was hovering above us. You could see the stars through it. It rotated 180 degrees and shot off, no noise.

Halloween 1999 some friends and I are"camping" out at Sacajawea park and we are laying on the ground faced up. Clear night and many stars were visible and as we lay there talking and starting up at the sky I saw what looked like a clear, see-through disc hovering above us about 20 feet. You could see the stars kind of distort as the clear object very slightly moved as it hovered. Then, it rotated 180 degrees and shot away very quickly. There was never any noise, any lights, or any disturbances to the trees around us. As soon as the disc shot off, all three of us go really quiet. I then asked my friends if they had just seen what I had seen and both confirmed it simultaneously.
