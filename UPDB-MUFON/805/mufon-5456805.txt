Light observed going back and forward over 1 mile area

I was lying in bed looking out the window when I noticed a reddish light going about the sky in a random direction back and forward. I got up to the window and could tell by its sudden change of direction it was not an aircraft or flair I went downstairs and took my phone off charge and went back up stair and filmed the observation. My wife refused to get up out of bed to see this. I am a retired detective and a former member of the Royal Observer Corps.
