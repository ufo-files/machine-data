We were fishing at mepal when I heard a low hum to my right a single light came along the top of the bank a second or so later another light appeared beside it the object came towards us the object then turned towards ely the silhouette appeared to have a tube on top which cast the forward light though there was no beam the oblong shape beneath this when going away from us appeared round with numerous pink lights flickering there was no more sound
