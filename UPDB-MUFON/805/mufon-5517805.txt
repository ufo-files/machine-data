My wife and I were outside watching the kids when I started to notice strange objects flying around... I have seen these before for as long as I can remember... But this night was different. The sun was down and we were going inside when i saw a moving light moving north west over mingus mountain. I pointed it out to my wife and as soon as we were both watching it rapidly started to grow bigger and closer. It got close enough to know it was not a plane but could tell it was a craft.. the object grew in intensity on approach and stopped and hovered for a few seconds then surged with light and rapidly went back the way it came in..... After that I went and got my camera on a tripod and took picks of anything strange... The pics that acDrrriptually caught something were astonishing to say the least....
