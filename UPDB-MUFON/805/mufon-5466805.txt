UFO following the Dipper

When I was 15-16, I was at my best friends cottage. We had just finished playing Yahtzee with her mother, and my friend asked me to join her outside to check out some stars.
She was showing me the constellations we didn1t get to see back at home due to the city lights.
It was then that I noticed, what I thought was a shooting star, or a satellite, until the zooming light just stopped, at the tip of the dipper (I am not sure which one is was at this point, maybe the Big Dipper).  But then we noticed the light moving, from each star of the Dipper, to the next.  My friend was an Uber Christian, she use to tell me I was crazy for thinking there was life out in space, until this moment.
I felt excited, my entire life I believed there were others out there.  up to this point, I was just called crazy for believing like Fox Mulder did in the X-Files.
I started to rapidly slap my friend screaming out, 1DUDE! IF THAT GOES DOWN, OVER, AND BACK UP.... IT1S A FRICKING ALIEN!!!1
And that1s what happened next. The object had gone on a straight path to each star of the Dipper, down, over, then up, and just flew off. The only way I can describe it1s departure from the last star as 1light speed/Warp Speed1s

We were young teens, there was no alcohol, or drugs involved. We just went star gazing.
From that moment both of our lives were changed. My theories were finally validated, and she no longer believed in god. Still doesn1t. 

I have told this story for years, but now I am wondering if others say this that night too.
