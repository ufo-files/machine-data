(GS) Red/Orange Lights in the Sky on 12/07/2012

I walked outside of my house to say goodbye to my bf when I looked to me left and up at the sky there were a series of red lights. I thought it could have been a meteor shower but the way they moved proved otherwise. I couldnt tell whether they were coming from the sky or from the ground because 1-2 floated up from the ground and 1 from the sky. They seemed to hover and glow almost like a burning ball/star. The smallest about the size of the North star, each different sizes. One appeared to get larger, almost as if it were getting closer. Then suddenly it got smaller and returned to the same size as the rest. They seemed to disappear into the clouds after a few minutes. All were concentrated in a certain area of the sky. One moved from left to right at one point (I thought they might be lanterns but the way they moved seemed rather odd).
