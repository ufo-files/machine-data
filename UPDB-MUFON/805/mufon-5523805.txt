Flat object with about 6 to 7 red and white lights visible descending behind trees about half a mile from my house.e.

I was standing on my front porch around 9:30 p.m. and noticed an object that seemed flat with red and white lights in the sky and thought at first it was a plane, but there was no noise and it was floating slowly. Then it stopped above the tree tops around half a mile away and it slowly descended down behind the trees. I know this was not an airplane and it was not a helicopter. I watched for a few minutes but it never reappeared.
