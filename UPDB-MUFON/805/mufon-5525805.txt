Looked like two large bright stars *     *  one in front of the other moving slowly getting brighter ..then sort of spiraling upward and dimming with dull connection between then dimming to disappearing.

At a friends house in Oregon City, Oregon. sitting around a fire talking. I spotted the Big Dipper and while helping one of my friends locate it ...I saw these two very bright starlike lights ...sorta looking like one was in front of the other moving very slowly. My friend, who had had trouble locating the dipper then said 'yeah they are moving' . We, the five of us, watched as the lights sort of spiraled upward into the sky and dimmed as they appeared to go up.They seemed at one point to have a dull light connection between them. They continued to slowly rise and spiral and then dimmed to the point where we could no longer see them. The whole event was approximately 3 to 5 min. in duration. I attempted to video it on my cell phone camera but, it didn't show up.
