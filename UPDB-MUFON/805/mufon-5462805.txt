{tjd} Was on my way to school

I was on my way to school and saw the lights. It seemed to be spinning horizontally. It was to my 11 o'clock when I first saw it. It was partly cloudy, and I lost sight of it. I then either saw another one or the same one between my 8 o'clock and my 9 o'clock for only a second. Before and after I saw it, there was a plane no farther than a mile. I was somewhat close to LAX airport.
