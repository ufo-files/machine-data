Saw from airplane window

During a flight from Detroit, Michigan to Fort Myers, Florida I observed a stationary object in the clouds to the left of the plane. I am not exactly sure of my location, as I was in flight, but I was approximately a half hour from landing in Fort Myers. It did not move, but the clouds moved around it. I was only able to see it for a minute or so because the clouds covered it.
