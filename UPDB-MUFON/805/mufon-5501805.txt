{dr}I watched a small, starlike light travel across the sky before flickering and going out

I was sitting in my room doing basically nothing when I looked out of the window to my room at what I thought was a plane or helicopter. The light wasn't blinking like an aircraft's light would. It almost looked like it was on fire, which led me to think I may have been looking at a meteor of some sort. I went outside to get a closer look. It travelled from southwest to northwest at a constant velocity before dimming, flickering and going out completely. The event didn't have any profound effects on me emotionally or physically before or after the event, other than the fact that I had thought it was very peculiar.
