clear siting in front of white cloud

For past reference. I was 13 and my mom an dad were teaching me to Drive on back desert roads near Edwards AFB. Heading west we saw a red square box stationery in front of white clouds over mountains east of Palmdale. We saw it clearly for apx 5 minutes. As I got to paved cross road it went straight up at extreme speed. I turned right and road went by end of a runway at Edwards AFB. A F-84 took off in front of us and went straight to the cloud white cloud bank the red box had been in front off. Thinking back the red box streaked up about the time the F 84 would have been positioning to take off. Like it saw or read tower to plane?? That was 66 years ago and is still very clear in my memory. I saw a disk 2 yrs later thru a monocular. Nothing else.
