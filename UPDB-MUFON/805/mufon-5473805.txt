High altitude light orb. Daylight sighting.

I was sat in my garden meditating, when i opened my eyes and looked up. Directly above me was a high altitude silver light orb. It was a very bright sunny day. The object moved slowly South over a period of half an hour.i couldn't see the object on my phone but I filmed in the direction of a passing plane. When I looked at the video the object is only just visible. It was moving too slow to be a satellite and I took a few photo stills but it looked more impressive to the naked eye due to the altitude.
