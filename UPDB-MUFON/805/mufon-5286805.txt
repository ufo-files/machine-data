LOSS OF TIME AND CHANGED DIRECTION WHILE DRIVING

WAS FOLLOWING RIGHT BEHIND HUSBAND'S CAR ON WAY TO TIRE REPAIR STORE, PULLED UP TO THE EXIT OF OUR SUBDIVISION AND LOST SIGHT OF HIM, THEN I COMPLETELY FORGOT WAY TO STORE, BEEN MANY TIMES. DID A MAP SEARCH AND MAPS WOULDNT CONNECT. HUSBAND SAYS HE SAW ME BEHIND HIM WHOLE TIME, BUT LOOKING LOST AS I APPROACHED INTERSECTION WHERE I WAS SUPPOSED TO TURN RIGHT, ABOUT A MILE AHEAD. I SUDDENLY CAME AWARE THAT I WAS ON THE ROAD AT THE INTERSECTION, BUT THE GROCERY STORE SHOULD HAVE BEEN ON MY LEFT, IT WAS NOW ON MY RIGHT. I COULDN'T PLACE WHERE I WAS. I SUDDENLY SAW MY HUSBAND'S CAR APPEAR AHEAD OF ME, BUT IT WOULD HAVE TAKEN ME AN EXTRA TEN MINUTES TO DRIVE TO WHERE I ENDED UP FACING. AFTER SOME WEEKS OF REFLECTION I REALIZED THAT WHEN I CAME OUT OF MY FOG THERE WERE NO CARS ON A VERY BUSY ROAD, I DONT REMEMBER THINKING OF THE TRAFFIC LIGHTS OR CARING. I WAS JUST SUDDENLY BEHIND HIS CAR AGAIN.
