Star like object moving across sky

Was out in the back yard with my dog around 730 mountain time. And as I always do look up at the sky looking to the west. And I see a star like object moving across the sky (north)in a straight line . At first I thought it was a satellite. But when it never showed up on the other side of the tree I knew something was up. I kept looking for it, and then saw the same type of object come out of no where going south opposite direction. it appeared to be lower in the sky. And then that object just vanished. I went out the next night at the same time and was looking to see if indeed it was a satellite and saw nothing.
