{rjl} Observed lights in morning Sky, very High maybe 7 or 8 miles High, moving very Slow assumed lights belong to some type of object.

Around 9am Pacific Time on 6/21/18 while getting in a morning workout, I was laying on my bench press when I noticed lights very high in the sky. I stopped my workout & began to watch the lights. I assumed that the lights were part of an object. Object moved very slowly from the west going north east. I could make out 4 white lights I assumed that I was seeing the bottom of some object, thinking to myself the object & lights must be massive for me to see them from so far away & that the lights must be very bright to be seen in sunlight. 4 white lights in a square shape like 4 corners of a box. So, I began to wave in the direction of the lights/object & They began to flash different colors. Red, yellow, orange, green & then the lights changed to a single strip of white lights, best I can describe is right down the middle of object. Then object moved to the left, so effortlessly I was left in awe! Going by distance alone it must have moved hundreds of meters to the left then back to right as easily as I could side step a puddle! It Actually slowed down even more when it was directly over head! & Gave me what can best be described as a light show! I thought I need to report this thinking maybe MUFON Had someone In the I.E. Who could get eye's or cameras on this object. Was very disappointed with response. This experience was absolutely amazing! & fit every definition of an unidentified flying object! Amazing! In the time I spent grabbing my phone I lost sight of it. Second time I've seen an unexplainable object during daylight hrs. Amazing! Didn't know of MUFON when 1st & most spectacular sighting occurred. It was yes ago now, but in the future I'll document it. I remember it like it was yesterday.
