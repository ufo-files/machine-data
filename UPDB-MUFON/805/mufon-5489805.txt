I had sent the congressman an email about another subject and my dad sugested I should send him some pictures of the chemtrails. We stopped at the Dollar General store in downtown Blanchard La. When we went out to get in the truck I thought and took 3 different pictures in different directions of the chemtrails. When I got into the truck, I was showing my dad the pictures and realized I had photographed a bright white light in the first pic toward the sun. When we backed out, we both looked in that direction and no lights could be seen with the naked eyes. So I ask him to stop at the red light so I could get out and take another picture. I did. I got out of the truck and pointed the camera in the direction of the sun and took the 4th photo. The light is in it also. Still cannot be seen with the eyes. It has to be in the infared light range which means it has to be advanced technology and it did seem to be sitting stationary. When I zoom in on the object in the picture it looks perfectly round and the same color as the invisible light on a remote control.
