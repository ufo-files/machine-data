{dr}Nightly UAP sightings from a craft that glows all colors and flashes red lights. Does tricks when I watch it.

I was outside and only saw one bright object by the moon when I looked. I thought it was odd how bright it was. I kept looking at it to see if it was a planet or something that I wasn't aware of showing on that night. I grabbed a pair of binoculars and looked at it. It wasn't apparent in shape as it glows very bright. I went and grabbed a laser and shined it at the object as I knew it wasn't a plane/helicopter since it was hovering with no noise. It flashed back a red light a few times. It has done tricks in the air that man-made aircraft cannot accomplish on a few occasions. It is still outside in the sky, in roughly the same spot. It comes back nightly. It has disabled my compass on my phone and the emissions from it make my eyes blurry and then sharp. The first night or two it was making my head hurt a little but it doesn't do it anymore. I have around 20 minutes of video and some pictures from different nights to prove the craft is coming back every night.
