Bright orb-like object feel fast, then stopped, changed direction, stopped, then got very bright and disappeared.

Witness was driving south on I55 approaching route 30 when object was witnessed out the front windshield towards the passenger side. Direction estimated to be predominantly south, maybe 20 degrees to the west. Witness observed a bright sphere/orb descending (falling) very fast at a steep angle (almost straight down) over a period of about a second. Witness thought in that instant that object was a meteor or debris but became surprised at next movement. Object's descent then came top abrupt halt at about 50 degrees above horizon, moved to the right (west) appearing to hop twice then stopped again. Object then got very bright and almost appeared to give an aura and then disappeared. Entire event only lasted about 4 or 5 seconds. Commercial airliners were noticed afterwards, although it is common to see those in my area. This is my first time seeing a possible UFO, although I am wide open to other explanations.
