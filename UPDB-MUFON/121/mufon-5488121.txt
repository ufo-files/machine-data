{cak} A slow straight line flight

I was taken a break from a meeting.I went outside by my truck to have a smoke.I was looking up watching some Turkey buzzards gliding around .when I noticed what I thought at the time was a plane . it was kind of odd because it wasn't moving that fast and was a very bright white. I kept watching it as it slowly came in my direction. Then I noticed it had no wings. But I started doubting what I was looking at.Then it went into or above a cloud. Well I waited for it to emerge from the cloud and it did. Now its right over my head and with out any doubt it had no wings.then I noticed there was like a ring or a separate part about 1\4 from the end. Well it kept going until I lost site of it. When it all ended my hair was standing on end. I went back into my meeting and told the story and drew a picture of what I saw. I have to say , I know what I saw but the feeling of maybe i should of kept it to my self.
