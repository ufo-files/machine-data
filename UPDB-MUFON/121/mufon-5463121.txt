{rjl} Photograph taken from commercial jet above Roswell approaching ABQ

On November 26, 2018, I took this photograph of the sunset with my iphone from the window seat of a commercial jet above New Mexico. We were flying northwest from Dallas, about 30 minutes from ABQ. Still cruising altitude. I took about a dozen shots or so within a few minute period. A few days later I was looking at them and noticed this feature below the wingtip. It looks like a disc. I did not notice this feature when I was taking the photos. And there is nothing like it that appears in the photos I took before and after this particular photo. I1m a skeptic, but it amused the hell out of me when I saw that the geo stamp on the photo says Roswell, NM.
