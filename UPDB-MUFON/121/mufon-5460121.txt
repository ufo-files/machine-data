{rjl} Very large bright light that suddenly appeared and then vanished

I was on 380 south near tobyhanna headed home when i noticed that up above me a very bright light suddenly appeared. At first i thought it was a helicopter but when i rolled down the windows i could not hear any noise that would usually be associated with any type of helicopter or plane. I continued on down the highway but i had slowed down once i noticed it. I watched for about 40-45 seconds and it sat stationary and then in the blind of an eye it just shut off and was gone. It was as if someone flicked a switch and turned off the light. I continued to watch thinking again it was a helicopter but i saw nothing in the surrounding area. No planes no helicopter not even a balloon. This is NOT the first time i have seen something unexplainable in the skies near tobyhanna. My husband has also seen things in the sky around that area.
