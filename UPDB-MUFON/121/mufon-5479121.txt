{cak} Noticed nexplained object in 2 pictures taken last year; object moved far within 1 minute

I discovered the object in two pictures I took on August 20, 2015 at the location of an accident in which my son was involved over a year ago Both pictures were taken at 03:41 PM according to the data on my phone. In the first photo the picture is tiny - barely noticeable - and flying or hovering to the south and maybe southwest. In the second picture the same object is visible much lower and larger, looking pretty much due east. Whatever it is it moved a great distance within one minute. These are two of several pictures I took to document the accident scene some time after the accident, trying to understand/explain to the insurance company how my son didn't see the car that hit his car. At the time I was taking the pictures neither I nor my son or my then-girlfriend noticed the object. I was just reviewing some old pictures on my phone today (December 14, 2016) and noticed the object. I am reporting only one witness because neither of the others saw it at the time and they have not seen these photos.
