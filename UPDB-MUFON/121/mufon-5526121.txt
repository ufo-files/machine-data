Slow moving disc changed speed and direction twice on being observed

Out at night sledding with younger brother and neighbor in Eddington,Me.,we obseved dark disc slowly flying over us,as we all almost spotted it immediately it stopped dead in the air turned 90 sped quickly to short distance stopped then turned again 90 back parallel to original course and flew over horizon at incredibal speed a short time later I see something fly across whole sky on same flight path ,this also flew incredibly fast.We notified local paper,but was only ridiculed in article to make it sound like we had seen airplane with blinking green and red lights.This was not an airplane and I have since known that whomever first called them flying saucers saw what we had seen and they are believed by me.
