Saw a bright glowing object while taking pictures of the moon.

I was on my way home from a friend that lives nearby in the small town Hallsberg, Sweden. When I left the apartment building, I first noticed that the moon was closer and brighter than usually, I admired it and was gonna take some pictures. Before I took the pictures I stood for a while and just watched the beautiful night sky and the the bright moon, suddenly a big moving object appeared. It was definitely not an airplane or something common, and it was bigger than a star, it had a different shape and wasn’t moving normal. When the object moved, it was really fast but it moved in a strange way and it kinda stopped, then it moved a little bit, and stopped again and kept doing it during one minute until it disappeared from like nowhere. My first thought was like “shit, did I really saw this, I hope it’s visible in the pictures I took!” And it was. But I didn’t thought so much of this when I got home 5 minutes later, because it didn’t frighten me in that way. I have witnessed strange phenomenas in the sky before this time, just 5 days before this event with 2 friends. So it will report the previous event to you as soon as I can.
