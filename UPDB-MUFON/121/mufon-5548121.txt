A short encounter

Early in the morning, I was standing outside with my sister talking to my mother through the window and we were talking about UFOs and then my sister says about how there just might be one in the sky, first I thought it was a joke, but then I looked and saw it. It was flying high in the sky at a fast speed. I originally thought that it was a plane, as I live near an airport, but then I realized that is wasn't making any sound. It was a long oval, like a cartoon version of a pill. It was white with a black line going down the middle. It was surrounded by a faint cloud. I wasn't frightened when I saw it, I was actually happy. The sighting was lost after it flew behind trees behind the house.
