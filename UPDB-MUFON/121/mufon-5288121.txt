Series of lights in a horizontal line sighted to the West in CA.

I was in my car in a parking lot facing west. I saw a series of lights, in a horizontal line, evenly spaced. The lights were moving from south to north. They were equally spaced and moving at a slow pace. As they moved left to right (south to north) the ones to the north would disappear and more would just appear from the south. After about 5 minutes the light in front just disappeared. This was definitely NOT an airplane. It was also NOT part of Spacex, since their satellites fly west to east to utilize the earth's rotation, thereby using less power and energy. Also, a launch from Edwards AFB would not be visible from Colorado and other places in the U.S. as was reported on this site. THIS WAS DEFINITELY A U.F.O.
