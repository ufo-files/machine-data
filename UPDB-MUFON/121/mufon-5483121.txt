{nd} dim, fast moving, ball of light

So me and my friends Ray and Tyler were all at a local park called Harrison Park in Riverside CA, at about 8:30 on 6/4/16. Me and Tyler were sitting on the swings on our phones and Ray was sitting on a bench about 10 ft behind us. All of a sudden Ray gets up and runs over where there isn't any trees covering the sky and he yelled at us to come over. So me and Tyler both ran over there and he was trying to get us to see a little dim ball of light. After about 10 seconds I saw it, then another 5 seconds Tyler saw it and we were all 3 watching this dim ball of light move in a diagonal pattern moving NW very fast. Then out of nowhere there was a big ball of light that flashes really bright and really fast. And it was gone. What we all three were watching was gone. We started freaking out like little girls. Nobody knew what we saw. It was nothing we could logically explain. We all did research the next morning and couldn't find anything. We all saw a UFO.
