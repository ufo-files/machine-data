{rjl} It was dark outside. I had a flashlight while walking the dog on the lawn. I looked above the tree line in the distance and saw a string of lights. The lights stayed still for a few minutes then slowly ascended.

It was about 6:25 pm at night in a rural area in Vermont Houses are distant but house lights are visible from a far. My husband and I took the dogs out and I carried a powerful flashlight with me that lights up the field and treeline which are over 100 yards away from us. I saw that beyond 130 yards and beyond my next field over and above the tree lines in my woods that there was a string of 4 lights. All were red except one that appeared pinkish. I remarked to my husband that I had never seen house lights in that direction and that it didn't make sense that there even was a house over there to see. The land would have to rise abruptly in that area for lights to be seen over the treetops. I asked him to take a look as I shone the flashlight on the string of lights just so he could see the general direction. His glasses were off and he could not see them at all. I watched the lights trying to figure out why they were there. Then the lights started to move upwards as if they had been hovering in place all the time I was watching it. I watched the lights ascend slowly and then it disappeared into darkness. I ran into the house frightened.
