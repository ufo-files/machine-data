Bright, flashing object - bright enough to be seen when other stars can't be seen.

I woke up at approximately 3:30 am and couldn't go back to sleep (not an unusual occurrence for me). I looked at my window, and through a crack in one of the blinds, I noticed what appeared to be a very bright star. This was interesting to me, because I live in a highly populated city and I never see any stars at night (and I have looked). I looked at my window and there was a very bright, small object that looked quite far up in the sky. When I saw it through my window, I thought it was a star, but when I looked more closely, I thought it might have been a plane because it was flickering and changing colors. I mean, BRIGHTLY changing colors. Red, blue, white, yellow...I've never seen a plane do that before. It also appeared to move a little,in small circles, but I couldn't be sure. It also appears to glow brightly and then fade within the space of a few minutes. If this is a star, I have never seen a star behave or appear that way before. My city is so well-lit that I can never see stars at night - I am skeptical that this is star. Perhaps it's a satellite or something, but I felt scared and nervous and disbelief at the sight of this object. I just looked at my window and the object is still there, flickering away.
