{jpg} White orb flying very high altitude with fast speed abrupt change in direction

I was outside on my back porch with my 2 children. I looked up and saw a plane and to the left of the plane coming from the east was a white orb traveling at a very fast rate. It must have been extremely high in altitude. There were no trails which immediately caught my attention. At first I thought it was a bird, but it was flying very quickly in a very straight line. After following it for about 10 seconds it immediately took a 90 degree turn north and after about 2 to 4 seconds it zig zagged very quickly before I lost my view of it. The object was way too high to be a drone and looked like an orb or white sphere. When I pointed to it my 6 year old daughter she claimed she saw it too. My 9 year old son did not see it though. I wanted to video it with my phone, but I knew if I took my eye off of it I would lose it because of the altitude. Also I think if I were to video it then it probably would have been hard to see on the video.
