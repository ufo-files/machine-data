Bright light in sky turned on and off and traveled large distances in the blink of an eye.

My wife and I went outside for a cigarette around 10 pm. We were talking when a bight light in the sky caught my attention. I assumed it was a plane at first. It appeared to have white lights and a very light shade amber light that seemed to pulsate around the object. I asked my wife to turn around and look up. We agreed it was not an airplane, helicopter, blimp, balloon, satellite, or meteor. We watched it for a few seconds when the light(s) turned off. You could still see a dark objection the sky. Then the lights came on again very bright where we could clearly see the location of the object. This happened over and over. The object moved in a way neither of us have ever seen. It appeared to travel huge distances in the blink of an eye. We reacted in amazement and wonder. Its very difficult when you are seeing something that youve never seen before, theres nothing to compare it to in your mind. I couldnt believe my eyes when the object appeared to leave the atmosphere and travel toward space. I kept asking my wife over and over, " did you see that? It went into space!" after the event we continued to look at the sky and trade and confirm details of what we just witnessed. There were no clouds in the sky at the time of the sighting. I am replaying the event over and over in my mind. I just read a post on your sight from a woman in MI and it sounds like we witnessed the same thing. Her post encouraged me to write to you about our sighting.
