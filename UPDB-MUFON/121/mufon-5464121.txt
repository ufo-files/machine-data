A horizontal explosion of twinkling lights which then ended with 3 sets of 2 red lights

As i was walking my dogs at 4.55pm on a cold winter evening I had been looking up at the stars as it was such a clear night. I saw 4 Iridium Flares go by over head and they were very bright due to the exceptionally clear skies that night.

After I witnessed an Iridium Flare go over head in an Easterly direction, I suddenly saw something incredible in the sky!!

It was a horizontal explosion of about 20 different lighs that had a twinkeling effect to them. Instantly, A bright amber spotlight like kind of light appeared in the middle and then vanished. 
The sparkling lights then seemed to merge into 3 sets of 2 red lights.

These red lights were offset from each other in their sets of pairs. The red lights then started to rapidly blink in random patterns with each other as If communicating

The red lights seemed to be completely stationary at the time and then just seemed to vanish over the North Sea.

It was without a doubt the most strange thing I have ever seen. My wife was with me at the time and also saw these lights
