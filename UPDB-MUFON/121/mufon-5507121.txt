{nd} Shape Shifting Object

The sighting was in the Bayfield County Forest. The witness was on an ATV
with her husband who was the driver. Saw light in the sky and started taking pictures because of previous UFO sightings. Then phone/camera jammed
Went back out about 11:00pm and saw the same object floating over their
house. Witness heard strange noises in the woods around her home and the dog began to bark and act aggressively. The witness became very fearful and her husband had to go outside several times to check the yard that night. When she reviewed the photos by zooming in she saw the actual craft that was changing shapes. The witness and her husband have seen many UFOs previously, but never had a camera with them. The next day, the witness had severe cramps and felt sore. But she chose not to seek medical attention.
