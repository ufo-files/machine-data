Star winks out

I was driving home from work (state route 20 mile post 196, looking northwest), it was dark and clear, various stars were out with a full moon. I drove around a bend in the highway and saw all of these stars in the sky, one was brighter then the rest almost like a planet, then it winked out like someone turned off a switch. I asked my wife, "did you see that?" and she said "what?", and I told her a star had just winked out, she said "really?". I told her it was just there now it's gone. I tried looking at the stars to see more but with the slick roads and deer I just drove on. I thought to myself "did I really see that?", but I had, I knew I did.
