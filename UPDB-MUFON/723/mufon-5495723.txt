After dinner 1/14/15 at 6:22 PM, I went outside with my camera to take photos of the sun. Looking South at the sun, at around 20 degree elevation I took the photo. When I looked at the photo image I noticed what looked like objects that appeared to resemble Morse code centered across the face of the sun. I did not see them at the time the photo was taken as the sun was very bright.
