I was outside trying to spot some meteors from a meteor shower and was looking directly above me when I noticed an object moving in the sky.. it was hard to see but I could clearly tell it was a V- like or triangle like object that was clearly moving slowly from north to south. I kept telling myself I could not be seeing this but sure enough I followed it in a straightline until I could no longer see it. It was black or grey very dark, had no light or sound. And from what I could see it appeared to have some sort of circle pattern on the outside of the object. It was a very odd experience that I could not rationalize in my head.
