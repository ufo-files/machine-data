{td}silver/gray disc hovered then darted straight up leaving flourescent green streak

(I have no idea exact time frame)When I was an early teen, and lived in Greene Maine I was in my 2nd story bedroom just prior to dusk. I noticed our family dog looking out my window staring straight up into the sky. She did it long enough that I thought it was 'odd' and decided to investigate. I peered out the window and and was surprised to noticed a silver/gray disc- smooth, no lights just hovering there motionless over our field. There were no houses, or people, no clouds, no birds, no other objects. I blinked once or twice and tried to focus on the object to see if I could tell what it was- a few seconds later it zig-zagged and zoomed straight up and disappeared as fast as lightening, leaving a fluorescent green streak behind it that disappeared as quickly as it appeared. I had had never seen anything like it. I was young and didn't know to or even how to report what I saw. No one I told believed me and would tease me about seeing 'little green men' so I have mostly kept it to myself all these years. Every now and then I come across a UFO sighting documentary on television, which got me wondering if anyone else had ever reported seeing what I saw that night and decided to search which is how I came across this sight. Thanks for reading my story.
