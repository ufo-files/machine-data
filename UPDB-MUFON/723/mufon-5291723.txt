Two lights then one light. Randomly zig zag and change elevations.

I was sitting by my fire in the front yard. I saw something in the sky flashing at me. I saw the first one and thought it was a satellite so I didn't catch it. The second one came by and acended upwards. It seemed to stop and hovered up in the clouds observing me. It had 2 or 3 lights then would merge together only to separate again. After awhile it began to move in a zig zag pattern and would stop whenever it got closer to me. After about 20 minutes, it flew over me, my house, acended to the treelines of our neighbors home and disappeared.
