BLUE GLOWING SPHERE OVER MY HOME.

AT APROXIMATELY 10:40 I WENT OUT MY HOME TO WALK THE 3 SMALL DOGS I HAVE AND UPON LOOKING UP I NOTICED A BLUE SPHERE HOVERING OVER MY HOME IN HOMESTEAD. I GRABED MY I-PHONE AND SNAPED COUPLE OF PICTURES AND A VIDEO CLIP. IT MOVED FROM A 90 DEGREE ANGLE IN THE SKY TO A 80 DEGREE ANGLE HEADING WEST BOUND. NO SOUND, NO PULSATING LIGHTS, NO NEV RED LIGHTS OR OTHER SHAPE OBSERVED BUT THE SHAPE OF A GLOWING BLUE SPHERE. THE SPHERE THEN WENT UP AND DISAPEARED.
