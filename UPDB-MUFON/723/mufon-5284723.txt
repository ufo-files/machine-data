Two Glowing orange amber lights circling the area

Each night for the last week there has been UFO activity that consistently occurs between 11pm and 2-3am. 

For the most part an orange amber coloured glowing light will appear in the south sky and make sweeping transits and fade away. A few minutes later it will reappear and make another transit, as though it were circling. 

Once in while a second light will appear close by though not as luminous as the main one, and fly along with it. 
Within half an hour they have disappeared completely. 

Like clockwork they will reappear seemingly closer to my vantage point but an hour or two later. 
It’s very puzzling, and even more so that it’s happening every night. I managed to get what video I could from my iPhone which does have decent low light photo mode, for a phone. 

If this is anomalous in nature, I would have to say we are experiencing a UFO wave.
