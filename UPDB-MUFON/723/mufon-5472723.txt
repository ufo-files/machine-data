One silver orb seen then other craft captured unknowingly on SLR camera

I was filming/scouting for footage for my documentary on my interactions with New Zealand's bigfoot. I had been driving along. Port Jackson road searching for nice panoramas to film with the Mt. Moehau Range in the background. I pulled over a location by farmland next to Port Jackson Road. I had some of my cameras out. I was looking at the mountainous scenery when I saw what looked like a silver orb like object near the mountain range but this only remained visually for about 3 secs or so before fading away. I thought this was very strange but wondered if it had been some kind of optical illusion on the mountain reflecting something. However after looking around that part of the mountain I could not see what I saw beforehand. I felt a bit puzzled by what I saw. I decided to film around the mountain area and around the valley below. I took some photos as well. It was not until months later after finally going over my footage in detail on computer that I saw some fast moving anomalies. I viewed the footage frame by frame and noticed silver 'saucer' shaped craft. One anomaly was slow moving and it was hovering near the ground. Another flew straight into the hillside without crashing. 

I have only sent some of the video footage to MUFON as this is being used in the documentary.
