{rjl} San Gabriel mountains

I was a young man who family always went gold mining in San Gabriel mountains.we had a camp site just beyond a 2 lane bridge about 1 mile .it was a long day as for a child we had dinner and went to sleep .we did not sleep in tents we slept in camping bags .I remember weakening up and looking at the sky and there was a ufo .it was at least 2 football fields long and maybe 100 feet up in the air.it had lights around it. It's shape was circle ,all it was doing was hovering above us . I did not think about it to much like I said I was a young child and besides who is going to believe a kid. But now as an adult I know what I saw.
