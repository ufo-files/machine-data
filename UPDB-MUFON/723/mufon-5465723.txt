Star like Object attracted to high volt Laser

It was just another sky gazing night coming to an end with a few meteors across the sky.

We were using our Green Laser to point stars out to each other. While we lay gazing with laser pointed right at 90 Degrees we noticed a small star object. 

We first thought it was a satellite but then. It stopped. Dead before our laser light.

We killed our laser light & kept viewing. Object stayed there. We pointed our laser at about 1 inch distance in sky from this object in opposite direction. The object moved towards our light. We were not sure what was happening and hence didn't kill the laser light this time but rather moved it in another direction opposite to its axis. It moved again towards our laser light.

This followed for about 15 mins & object kept appearing closer to us every min.

Once what we thought was a small star or satellite was now getting bigger and closer to us. About 30 mins into this we got a bit terrified as the object seemed coming really closer down and we killed our laser light.

Now we were really worried and a bit scared too as this was not normal.

We watched for about 3 -4 mins & the object suddenly vanished right in front of us without a trail or any light.

This was first time we spotted a orb attracted to laser light.
We have seen orbs before but this was different.
