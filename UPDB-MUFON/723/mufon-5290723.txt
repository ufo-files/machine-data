I was standing on my friends balcony at night and they were in the room

I went out to the balcony and stayed looking at the sky at the same place. First I say one object race across like 50 mph from my perspective going west to east then. The second raised by at the same speed going South to North. both objects were way up there probably stratus sphere or something. Far enough up there that you can see them but not close enough for detail. Then the second object came down about a mile above me and started doing figure eights. I thought it was maybe my eyes because I was staring to long so I yelled to my friends to come to the balcony of my friends house. All four of us witnessed the craft still preforming figure eights for me and then it just shot out east from a perfect vertical line into space at about 150 miles per hours from a ground perspective.
