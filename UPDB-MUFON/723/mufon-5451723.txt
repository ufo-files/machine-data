Two blinding flashing yellow lights

I woke up at 4am to go to the bathroom and laid back down but the headset in my office was flashing so I couldn’t fall back asleep. I went back to the bathroom about 15 minutes later and I saw bright flashing lights in my backyard. I thought “Is that my headset?” I looked through my mini blinds and traveling low over my neighbors tree were two extremely bright flashing yellow lights pulsating with the same frequency of my headset. It was so bright I couldn’t look at it. I called for my daughter but she was sleeping. I got kind of scared. Didn’t want it to see me looking at it. It floated over my backyard and continued south. I ran to the front to see if I could see it but the flashing was gone.
