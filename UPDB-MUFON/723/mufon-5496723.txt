{cak} I took my dog out and saw the brightest object I have ever seen in the sky besides the moon at night. It was moving towards me above the trees, stopped, reversed and hovered there.

I took my dog out to do her business when I saw a very bright light in the southwest sky at around 7:30pm. I called my wife out and told her to bring the camera. We watched it to make sure it wasn't a plane coming towards us because it was moving slowing towards our direction. It seemed to pulse light. Fade in and out bright then dark. Suddenly it stopped and started going in the opposite direction so we decided to get in the car and follow it. Then it just stopped and was hovering right there in the sky for everyone to see. It was amazing. We have a video of it, but unfortunately it is a camera phone so it doesn't zoom very well, but you can see it for sure. We knew it wasn't Venus because Venus is a planet and they don't move like that and don't pulsate color. You could see a red color every now and then because it looked like it was spinning at times. It was crazy because it was so big and bright and would fade out and almost disappear and suddenly it would brighten up again. I only wish we could have gotten a closer look at it. When we turned the car around to go home it was behind us and by that time the object had disappeared. We plan to look for it again tomorrow night. We are thinking of uploading the video to YouTube. I found this sight by searching to see if anyone else in North Carolina had seen the same thing we saw tonight and sure enough someone did.
