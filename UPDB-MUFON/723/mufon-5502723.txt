{cak} The object was round, large, going at a medium speed, no tail, going straight over Blue Ridge Parkway, medium orange yellow, perfectly round.

We had no bathroom or running water. To use the "bathroom" at night, we had a "pot" on our back porch. I went there. No lights on, no lights in the country. I am 61 now, 14 at the time I saw this. I thought it was a meteor. It had no tail and was traveling at a medium/slow speed, the color was yellow/orange. It traveled in a straight line over the trees by the Blue Ridge Parkway. I knew little of meteors and nothing of UFO's. There was no sound. It never wavered, just kept going straight until it was out of my sight. I told my parents the next morning, they thought it was funny, that I had really not seen it. Over the years I have thought about this incident. I wonder what I really saw. The object was very large, and round. My i.q. is 143. I use no words that might perhaps confuse anyone. My feelings at that age was wonder. The only other person I have told about this is my husband. For a long time now, I have not believed this was a meteor.
