{tjd} Bright vibrant orange lights hovering and flying like planes then disappearing.

6 orange lights came up from the west over near Occidental or Bodega. Saw the first one that seemed to have a little tail. It got brighter and then disappeared. If drones, they didn't make any drone type movements.
