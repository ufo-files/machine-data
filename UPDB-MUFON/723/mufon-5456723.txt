A row of 8 vertically descending, equally spaced lights.

Observed be 2 police officers while on duty witnessed the following. They wish to remain anonymous!!!
Seen in the night sky approx. 5am. A line of vertical lights descended towards ground, moving in unison. As the lowest light of the group got to its lowest altitude it faded out and then immediately a new light would appear at the top of the formation. This lasted for about 15 mins.
