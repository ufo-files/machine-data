Capsule shape, 3 main lights, 8 rim lights

1:4 proportion on main fuselage. With a brim around entire perimeter. It was 250 feet above me. 10 feet wide x 40 feet long. Blue/silver/gray in color. 3 main lights on underside. 8 smaller lights around brim. Completely silent. Felt like it was floating, not flying. Just glid along in perfect straight line. Slow. I would say about 20 to 25 miles per hour ground speed. Lights were constant and very dim. Sorta of had stealth like visual quality. The surface of the whole was fodgged out of focus. Fuzzy. Like seeing something in the fog. Brim was a flange about 30 to 36 inches in projection off of main fuselage. It was capsule/pill shaped, like tylenol.
