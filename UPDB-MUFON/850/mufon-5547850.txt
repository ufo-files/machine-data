I saw the ufo when I was about 14, I am now 45

I was baby sitting at my mother's house, she used to run a day care. There were several children in the living room watching tv. For no reason the tv started to turn on then off a few times, I think the channels changed as well. I figured one of the kids had the remote and was just goofing on the rest of us. No one had the remote , I found it on top of the fridge where my mother would keep it so no little hands would get it. I remember my mother had a huge picture window in the living room, for some reason I went to it as well as one of the little boys. That is when I saw a greyish cylinder just hovering over the junk yard across the street from my mom's, it wasn't much higher than the telephone pole. It might have been there a minute maybe two then all of a sudden it took off and was gone in like a half a second. I can remember not even hearing all of the little kids that were in the living room, it was really weird. I just looked at Petey,he was just as shocked as I was.I think we tried to tell my parents we saw a ufo, of course they never believed us. Years later when I was about 21 I told my boyfriend who is now my husband. Every once in a while I tell the story, Some believe me and some do not. I can still see it as if it were yesterday. I didn't hear any sounds coming from it either. Unfortunately I do not have photos,just my memory.Whenever there is a show on about UFO'S I am always very interested, I always watch just to see if there are any similarities.
