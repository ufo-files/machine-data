Many star-like objects during the 7.1 earthquake

On 14th January, 2018, this happened during the 7.1 magnitude earthquake that rocked north of Arequipa, in the sea, near Nasca. 
People woke up and rushed outside their homes. 
From several places of Nasca (specially those not near the city) people could witness dozens(nobody could count) star-like objects going down from above, looking south-east to the sea. 
In other places near the sea they could not see, because the sky is cloudy. But Nasca has clear skies. It was funny to hear some religious people saying that "was written in the Bible, the stars will fall...". 
I can1t say how many people saw this, but in the city people is still talking about it.
