{tjd} light, over powerlines, some movement, rose up, brightened, shot off too fast for manmade.

I was night fishing at San Luis Reservoir, Calif., mid Summer, 1977, sitting in a lounge chair on the bank. The night was very clear and warm. I was about a mile from the lakes large transmission towers. I started watching a light moving somewhat around or above where I knew were power lines for the dam. I took it to be a helicopter, but couldn't hear any sound at all. After about 10 to 15 minutes, the light rose quickly, maybe 500 feet or more, brightened several orders of magnitude, and shot off to the North, gone in a jaw dropping instant. If I had blinked, I would have missed it. After all this time, I don't remember the date, but I will never forget the incident.
