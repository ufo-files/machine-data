hovering light in the sky

On the morning of 10/30/2020, I pulled into my driveway about 7:45am. It was a very clear, bright morning. I noticed a bight light in the sky to the SE. At first I thought it was a planet still in view but it seemed too large and there were no other stars visible. The glowing light hovered stationary in my view above the treeline. I watched for about 15 minutes, even calling my husband to see if he saw it but he didn't. While 2 jets passed by it, one to the east of it at a higher altitude and then a few minutes later one to the southwest of the object. This object was glowing or reflecting the morning sun tremendously. Once the 2nd jet passed to south of it, it disappeared.
