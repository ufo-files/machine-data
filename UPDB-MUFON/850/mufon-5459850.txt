{tjd} Woke up to a loud but very slow whirring sound and bright red glow that surrounded the entire building.

I can't say that what happened was an actual UFO experience. I can only say what my experience was. It was late at night. I woke up to what sounded like the whirring/swishing blades of an enormous helicopter. Normal enough right? Wrong. I was in a one room stand alone bungalow on about half an acre of land. The sound was directly overhead (above the roof) and it sounded like the helicopter blades were slowing down to land (on the roof), but it never did land. It also sounded like it was at least as big as the property it hovered over. I was too scared to look out the window, but I could see the entire area was flooded with a bright red glowing light (even the room was filled with the glowing red light). The sound of the whirring/swishing helicopter blades was not normal because it sounded like the blades were spinning much too slow to keep a helicopter in the air. It also sounded like each blade was bigger than the building I was in. While it seemed like the craft was hovering above the building for a long time, it was probably no more than a couple of minutes. The extremely slow whirring/swishing sound of the blades never changed, but all of a sudden the sound and the glowing lights vanished. They didn't fly away, they just vanished. To this day when I tell anyone about it (if I tell anyone about it) I say it was probably just a helicopter, and my mind was playing tricks on me.
