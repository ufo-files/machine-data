Bright, reflective cigar/orb(s) in the sky, changes color and direction sporadically. They seem to disappear/reappear at random

About 6 months ago (September 19, 2021), I noticed a bright light outside of my window (roughly 2am EST). At first I thought it was a star, but it started moving and blinking various colors and I went to get my dad who saw it moving as well. It has returned on a regular basis since then, occasionally up to 3 of them at a time. I believe they're orb shaped, but it seems to shapeshift into a cigar shape before shifting back into an orb. 
They move sporadically, sometimes disappearing for a moment before popping back up again. The lights are mostly green but I have seen them be orange, blue, purple, white, etc. (the surface appears to reflect light at times). They appear relatively in the same spot every night, however not always at the same time and stay around anywhere between 30 minutes and several hours. I have not seen them land but disappear over the tree line.
