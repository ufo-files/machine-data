Saw this dull grey like colored object flying over West Allis last weekend.

So me and my nephew took a walk for some snacks last weekend. It's a completely clear night for star gazing. My nephew noticed this odd object in the sky high above the tree line and due north a short distance of where we were headed. So I looked up at it and it seems to be hovering or on a path similar to ours. We keep walking and watching as the craft then speeds up on the same path, turns northwest on a dime and at a much faster speed than any jet I've ever seen. It's quickly out of sight or disappears in less than five seconds.

This being a clear sky and add in that we witnessed at least two planes with flashing lights and a helicopter in the area.

We did not hear any of the objects flying in the air that night. We did hear some light traffic on the freeway though. 

The UFO we witnessed had no lights, was boomerang shaped, no sound and appeared greyish/dull in the moonlight sky. It had speeds greater than a jet with the ability to change direction in a snap. It either got beyond our eyesight or disappeared?
