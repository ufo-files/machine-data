{rjl} Object Over Baseball Field

In the spring of 2005 my oldest son was in little league baseball and all of us were at a game. I had just had our second son back in January and we brought him with us to the game. It was warm so I put my son in the stroller and strolled him under a near by tree for shade. As I was standing there, I saw a very large, black, oval rise slowly above the trees. The object was close, very close. It wasn't off in the distance, There was another baseball field right next to the tree line so one of the kids could have easily hit it with a baseball. As I was standing there in amazement, I looked around to see how others were reacting to this "thing" rising above the tree line, no one was reacting, NO ONE! I couldn't believe it. It seemed as if I was the only one who could see this thing. I watched for a while and walked back over to where my husband was sitting and asked him if he had seen the object and he said yes, I did, but it was like I was the only one who was seeing it. 

My husband and I to this day still feel like we were the only ones who saw this object even though we were surrounded by lots of children and adults. I am 44 years old and I have never in my years seen anything like this before. Since then though, I have seen many other UFOs. I don't know if I have just become more observant or if there is something else going on, but, the sighting I just told you about is my most memorable.
