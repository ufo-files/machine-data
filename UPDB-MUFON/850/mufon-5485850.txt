{cak} Unknown craft made 90-degree vertical turn

I was driving west on Cheyenne Avenue, before going over the US-95 overpass I noticed some interesting cloud formations. Laughing to myself I figured they were clouds seeded from Chem-trail operations, so I took out my phone to capture the skyline with the clouds. I was about to take the picture when my stoplight turned green, so I put down my phone and proceeded over the overpass. Once on the other side I looked back up at the sky to see an object move out of the clouds, and make a sudden 90 degree turn straight up. I took out my phone again and was able to capture the trail of the object and possibly the object itself ascending, though that is not clear from the footage. But the craft clearly did a 90 degree turn upwards. I have worked with aircraft for the USAF and witnessed some interesting flight capabilities, but never have I seen an aircraft fly through cloud cover such as this and perform such a dramatic change in direction, in atmosphere. Within 2 hours of viewing the object there was another sighting over SoCal/NV skies, which was said to have been "Russian Missile Debris". Are the 2 related? After the sighting I felt a little on edge, especially after another event happened that same night.
