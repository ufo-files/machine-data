As I watched it began to move more to the right of where I was wathing from. (it was in the SW horizon and moving in a northernly direction)Although in general it remained in the South Western horizon because it only moved briefly, but enough to actually see movement. I did'nt know what to think. What the heck was this?! Then the object became very bright. Bright-bright! Like an arc welders arc glow. It began to move straight up and very fast. I watched it until it was gone, it disappeared into the sky. There were no clouds, the sky was clear and it was early enough to still be dark. All this happened within the span of 1.5-2.0 mins.
