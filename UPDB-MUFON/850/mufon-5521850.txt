We were driving from our home into the local town on Christmas Eve. It was approximatly 9:50 p.m. We saw high in the sky approximatly 6 or 7 round reddish - orange blobs. They were not flying slow and they were not flying fast, sort of a medium pace. They did not make any sound and were evenly spaced apart. We watched them at they flew out of our site. Many people saw these and we joked that it was Santa and his raindeer.
