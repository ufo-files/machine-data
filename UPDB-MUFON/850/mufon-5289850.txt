I was sitting outside when I notice high above me just to the east were 2 identical objects circling each other. I have an extensive aviation background and estimated their altitude at 5,000 ft. I went inside, grabbed the monocular I use for bird watching. With a closer and clearer view, both objects were white flying wing shaped with a single black protrusion coming from the rear that was slightly offset to one side (not like an inline tail of a plane. The 2 objects kept circling each other, getting slowly closer to each other with each rotation. When both were quite close to each other ( I would estimate 40 to 50 feet, a single engine plane flew below them probably 1000 feet or so. It was a small plane, similar to a 4 seat Cessna. When the 2 objects got close enough to each other, they joined together at the black protrusion, hesitated for a second then took off heading northwest and a very high rate of speed (1000mph+). There was no cloud cover, clear day, and were obviously not birds.
