{rjl} Bright neon green circle with a tail, decended in an angle at jet like speed

Picked up my daughter and we were driving east on Suisin parkway. On my right across the highway, about 151 degrees in the sky, maybe higher, appeard a circle with a tail. It was neon green with some blue, not like a fireball, more composed.

It flew in an angle down, super fast and vanished

For a minute I thought I was imagining but then I asked my daughter and she said she saw it too. She tried to convince herself it might have been a firework, but then we discussed the direction, altitude and speed, as well as coloring, and we agreed this was very unusual.

We looked back to see if there was some explosion, fire, anything and there was none. My daughter thought it might have turned and flew behind the hill.
