{rjl} Red flashing lights huge craft no sound over Port St. Lucie

I went out to watch the rocket launch tonight and about 4 mins before the launch I looked to the west north west and there in the sky were three flashing red lights on this massive silent wing shaped craft. It flipped over like a pan cake and kept moving slowly. I was with my folks and we all saw it with our jaws on the floor. We all tried to get a picture but none of them turned out. Just grainy black photos which totally SUCKS!!!!

The red flashing lights definitely did not look like normal lights you would see on any aircraft. They had a strange hue to them. Very pin point accuracy. The stunner was watching it tilt on its side and then roll over to the other side while it silently drifted to the south west direction. Someone else HAD to have seen this thing. You can11t miss it on a night like tonight when there are very few clouds and could clearly see the stars as well as other planes.

We all thought the lights were very very strange. No sound, huge and triangle wing shaped. Not that high up in the sky. Flew over the homes that back up to a golf course. It was on the golf course side of the homes.

Time: 7:43pm est.
Location: Port St Lucy, FL

Hope someone else saw it too.
