Photo of UFO at Monte Alban Mexico

I did not see this with my naked eye. My girlfriend and I were at Monte Alban Archaeological site in Oaxaca Mexico. Saturday August 28th, 2021 at 12.23 pm. I framed up the shot and took the photo. I looked at the photo and spotted a strange object over one of the Pyramids. I immediately looked up, there was nothing there and so I immediately raised my camera and took another 9 photos of the exact same spot, it did not show up in any of the following shots. The photos have times associated with them (i-phone) so they were literally one after the other. There was nothing in that space or anywhere else in the sky. It was not a plane, helicopter, or balloon. If it had been a balloon it would have shown up in one of my follow up shots. And it was not a drone as we would have seen it, and they are also banned from being used at this site. There was nobody underneath throwing objects in the air. As I said I did not see this with my own eyes, only what you can see in this photo. I do not know what it is but it looks circular on the bottom and dome like silver on the top, like it is reflecting sunlight.
