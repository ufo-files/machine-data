Rectangular black with 4-5 round multicolored

My wife and I were driving back from Mission, on #1 Hwy at Langley and approx 232 ave, west bound and we both saw a large black rectangular object with 4 to 5 round exhaust ports that slowly changed colors. It was laying flat like a monolith. It paced the traffic going West and accelerated at a 45 degree angle and took off into the sky. Going SSW. The sun was setting off to the right and was about 10 degrees off our starboard side of the car. People saw it while driving and we were ALL pointing at it until it flew away. Pretty darn cool stuff!!
