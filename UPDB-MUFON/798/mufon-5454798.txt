{jpg} Cube by the Moon

We photographed a cube by the Moon on the Worm Moon night. We saw it on our 65 inch monitor. Camera was a Pentax K1 with Pentax 300mm pro telephoto lens. I cropped the picture then used it as a background in a 3D program and rendered it as big as I could. 10,000 pixels. I will share both crops with you.
