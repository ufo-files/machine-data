While parked at the beach witnessed a bright light hover and change directions/altitudes

A friend and I were parked facing the ocean at Ocean Beach in San Francisco CA at around 11:30 p.m. We both noticed a glowing craft that appeared to be an extremely bright star at first hovering in front and slightly to the right of us.Suddenly it changed its position completely going to the left and lower so that it was more directly in front of us. It hovered again in the exact place for 5-10 min then quickly changed its altitude and positioning in relation to where we could see it from inside the car. It moved extremely quickly and was able to stay in the exact spot. Then suddenly it dissappeared. It was an extremely clear night with no clouds. From what I could tell it remained over the ocean the whole time. It was bigger than what a star appears to be and had it had blinking lights I would've mistook it for a plane while it was stationary.
