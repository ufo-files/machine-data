(GS) Lying on hill, stargazing, craft lazily hovered over, light show

Stargazing on a site previously occupied by an elementary school, called "the plateau" by my group of friends, where we first reached enlightenment.A craft flew over us, low in the sky, but higher than the tree tops and buildings. Town has low skyline, big skies are a main feature. Sounded like nothing ever heard before, low rumbling engine with a slow turning turbine. At first, we thought a helicopter, thats a fairly logical assumption. But it seemed to be sloping to land, while never doing so, and then the sound wasnt consisted like a helicopter. The craft hovered for a couple minutes, turning off the brighter lights, and turning on rainbow-colored ones that flashed in a certain sequence manifested as triangles. As this was happening, the dome shape once perceived was translucent. Then, the brighter outside lights turn on, and the craft moved to the NE from its first location due N. Hovered once again, repeated the light sequence, and proceeded to move higher in the sky, and to the W. At this point, my group started traveling in hopes of getting a better look, however, our feet couldnt carry us fast enough and visual was lost. One other and I decided to walk to the general NW to see if another view was possible, and upon reaching the outskirts of town, walked through and incredibly powerful "wall" of what seemed to be sulphur, or gunpowder, but there was no smoke or residue to see. Only the sensation. Right before this occurance, I perceived a very strange energy in the area around me.
