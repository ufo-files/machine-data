small fleet flying in V formation.

About 2 minutes before 1 am on march 8th 2021 I went outside for a cigarette, and I was looking at the stars. when i notice flying over my house about 100 feet in the air were fifteen chevron shaped craft flying over my house in a V formation. they were a glowing reddish color. They made no noise at all, and the sighting lasted about 30 seconds before they dimmed and vanished. Wish I had more to report, but that was the extent of it.
