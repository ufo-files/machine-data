I KNOW that I saw a UFO!

I was driving north on highway 495 in Massachusetts back in 1997. I was on my way to work and it was 2pm. There were no clouds in the sky and the sun was out. What I saw was triangular in shape and had lights at each corner. The main body of the object was invisible and only the 3 lights at each corner were visible. The lights were so bright that they compared to the brightness of the mid-day sun. I watched the object rotate and hover for several minutes while driving. At first I thought I must be seeing things, so I slowed down and stopped in the breakdown lane. I watched the UFO slowly rotate and hover for serveral minutes. Then it accelerated at a speed that NOTHING MAN MADE CAN DO! It took off straight out of the earths atmophsere in a fraction of a second. I have seen just about every flying vehicle man has to offer...I have been to space shuttle launches...and after that day it was clear to me that...someone/something out there can move around the galaxy/universe as easily as we drive to the corner store! This experience scared the hell out of me and I did not tell anyone for years for fear of ridicule. My best guess is that this UFO was just watching us.
