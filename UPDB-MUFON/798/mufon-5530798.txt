Silent triangular/boomerang shaped aircraft.

I was sitting outside my patio with my girlfriend when I looked up and saw an object flying across the night sky. It was silent and moving the same speed as a jet. I live nearby Edwards afb and Plant 42 in Palmdale. I'm used to seeing military craft flying all the time but this object did not look like anything I've seen before. It made no noise and the entire body was illuminated. At first I thought it might have been a bird gliding but I cant think of any birds that glow at night. It flew northeat towards Edwards afb. It faded off in the distance. It looked the size of a fighter jet. It was too small and WAY too quiet to be the B-2. I saw it as a triangular object but my girlfriend said it looked like a boomerang.
