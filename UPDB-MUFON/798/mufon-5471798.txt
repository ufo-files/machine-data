{dr}The sighting was unusual, multiple lights in a long zig zag patter, very large, not a plane, light blinking.

I left my home at 9:08 to pick up my son the next town over, as I exited my home I noticed a large object in the sky.  It had 6-10 blinking red lights in a long zig zag pattern that appeared to be hovering over the mtn area near my home. I ran inside to ask my boyfriend to come witness this phenomena, he looked out and agreed it was unusual, not a plane or helicopter the blinking lights were hovering and to spaced apart. We didn't make out a shape but ran to the end of the driveway and notice it became smaller as though it was moving away, it was hard to tell but definitely not any craft we have ever seen in our beautiful skies.
