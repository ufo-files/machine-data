Never seen anything like it

It was a clear day. Mid summer before noon. Blue sky...no clouds...sunshine was Behind me...I was looking west from Surrey towards Richmond. I was standing on my sundeck looking southwest when I saw this glinting object in the sky. It would probably been out over Boundary Bay area. It was very slowly heading my way. I continued to watch it moving very slowly in a straight line, pausing off and on. It finally stopped moving and was probably hovering over South Delta/Richmond area. I was watching it when my neighbour came out so I pointed it out to her. She asked what in the heck is that? There were two orbs at least that is what it looked like. A big one and a much smaller one. It also seemed to be slowly rotating because it would shine very brightly as if reflecting the sun then it would rotate back until it was hard to see. Between the two orbs there was this undulating something. Think of a cuttlefish and how it swims. We observed it for 5 to 10 minutes. It just stayed in the same spot. I turned to talk to her for about 30 seconds. We looked back and it was gone. Vanished. Couldn't see it anywhere and I had a lot of good sky.
