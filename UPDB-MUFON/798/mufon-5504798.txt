{cak} Glowing light in our woods and big headed entity photographed

Huge glowing light at the end of our backyard. Appears to have landed. Powerful red-orange light. We were watching TV inside our home when we saw a reddish glow over the trees. We went outside, it was snowy but surprisingly warm. We approached the light because we wanted to know what it was. Normally, there is no lights here and we don't have any street lamps on our street. I put my hand in my pocket and reached for my mobile phone. We (me, wife) were all nervous. Our dog was barking at the light. We then all saw something moving beside the light, where it was at its brightest. I tried to take a video with the mobile phone but it got incredibly warm to touch. I pressed the button to snap a picture and the phone shut off. In a matter of seconds, the light ascended towards the sky and in about 5 seconds, it was totally dark outside. We don't know what it was but we are all terrified. We got home wondering what this could have been. When we got back inside, nobody talked, we were all wondering what this was. We looked at the phone and saw the picture of what seems to be a 4 or 5 foot tall entity directly in front of us. It was standing next to the red-orange light. Please get back to us because this event scared us and we don't know what are their intentions. We heard about events occurring in another town in the area (Sainte-Brigitte de Laval) and we don't want anything this weird happen to our family. Thank you. D.C
