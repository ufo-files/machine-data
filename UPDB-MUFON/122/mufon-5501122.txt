{nd} Two very bright lights flying super fast with a comet like tail chasing them then just disappeared. lasted approx. 15 seconds

Between 8:30 and 9:00 pm my mother, my little cousin and I walked to the end of our court to observe the beautiful full moon. We were staring in Awe, when we noticed two very fast white lights flying real low streaking from just the left side of our view with absoulty no sound. And disappeared in about 15 seconds or so.All three of us seen this. We know it was not any kind of air craft we ever saw around here. Strange indeed.I have witnessed quit a bit over the years, never reported one before. But for a split second I thought we were under attack..lol maybe missile's.. But then they just disappeared as fast as we saw them.
