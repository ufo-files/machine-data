{nd} 3 orbs in formation, then 2 followed; then 2 more within a couple of minutes, then single orbs several minutes apart, all at various distance between them

Husband and I driving to Miramont Health Club about 6:30 pm, I noticed 5 orange orbs in the sky coming from the southwest, we turned off Harmony Rd and parked on Oakridge Dr. and got out of the car. The first three orbs were in formation, 1 in front and 2 in back, and 2 more followed them. No noise whatsoever, they were silent and glided. Also at lower elevation, not high altitude like a plane. Unfortunately I didn't have binoculars, camera or phone. The 5 orbs ascended as they glided by and faded into light cloud cover in the northeast part of the city. One car stopped and the young man asked if we saw the orbs and said "what was that?", he also had video of the first 5 orbs. We were parked by a restaurant parking lot and a group of people stopped to watch as well. Another car stopped, and the man also talked about the 5 orbs. A woman walked by and asked if we saw the orange orbs, asking again, what was that? As we were talking, 2 more orbs glided by together. The man from the first car called a friend and was on the phone the whole time as we stood there. A few minutes later, another orb showed up, and then a few minutes after that, the last orb glided by. All headed in the same direction. We said we hoped they were ufo's because whatever it was, it would be difficult to explain.
