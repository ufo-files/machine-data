Saw an entity near my bed touching my shoulder

I was half sleep in my bed when I felt a presence in my room . I turned very quickly to my right when I saw a small humanoid beside me for 2 or 3 seconds. I saw a definite shape and a hand with no digits on my shoulder. I just saw a feature-less humanoid figure . The height was about 4 feet but was not solid but sort of translucent and went out of my sight like a star trek transporter would do. It seem phased or not in my space-time or inter-dimensional. I have had enough strange happenings( missing time, lights being turned off when I wake) to know this was just part of it and real. I immediately got up and sat on my bed for 15 minutes wondering what I saw.
