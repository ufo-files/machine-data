{flg} Entity observed in my home.

One night last fall, I had fallen asleep on the couch in my living room. Around 3:30 or 4 in the morning, something startled me awake. I looked up and an entity was standing over me. He/She/It appeared to be between 5 to 6 feet tall, with white skin and the usual large, black eyes. The eyes were shiny, deep, and about 3 inches across and angular. The entity was thin, but not excruciatingly so, with a larger head. The entity appeared to be wearing some sort of stretchy suit, but i was too focused on the face to really notice much else. I did notice that the hands were long (as if they had an extra joint in each finger) with dark colored nails. I had never had an experience of that kind before, but I wasnt even remotely afraid. Actually, and I feel like an idiot thinking back on it, I had this uncontrollable urge to laugh. I felt inexplicably happy and just laughed. The entity bent down as if to observe me (the movement was unusual and fluid in a way). I had the impression of curiosity. Then the entity was just gone. It wasnt so much as if the entity had disappeared, but more like I had rolled onto a different plane. My mother, who had just begun to get ready for work, came into the room and asked me what was so funny and why I was awake. I was still laughing and talked gibberish, so she assumed I was sleep talking. My life hasnt been the same since that night, and I joined the air force that same week.
