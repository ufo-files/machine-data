{cak} It was on an angle as it floated from the West and slowly faded away into the South

During an average night walk, I usually look up into the sky and not see much with the exception of gentle glowing stars. So knowing the stars, and looking up at random intervals, I see these strange lights, slowly moving across the sky. What made them strange is how they angled up a bit while moving and how bright they were. So I immediately knew it wasn't stars as I'm sure stars don't just move across the sky and fade quickly. Or military flares that move vertically from the ground. I tried to take a picture of the thing with as it was quite dark and the camera doesn't have that good of a darkness filter. So it is a bit sloppy.
