{jpg} I was just driving home and saw a glowing orb

On December 26, 2016 I was driving home from springfield when i saw something strange in the sky. It was a crystal clear night with no clouds or wind, just a beautiful december night. I was just driving around the backroads with no other cars on the road, i went around a curve that had a tree overhead and when i looked up i saw it. It was a glowing white orb that was sitting in the sky motionless, under the moon and above a small building. I looked at it for about 10 seconds when it slowly but steadily arched up and then back down as if it were moving in the shape of a candy cane, and then suddenly it sped off northwest faster than anything i had ever seen in my life. I proceeded to drive down the road it had taken for a few miles before i gave up my search. I was in complete shock to where i was shaking. Later when i started telling a few family members and friends about it they assumed i was on drugs, mind you that this was the night before i was leaving for meps to test for the navy so there was nothing of the sort in my system. The orb was completely silent for the duration of the time i saw it. To this day i can recall what exactly it did in perfect detail
