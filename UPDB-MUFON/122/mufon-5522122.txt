At first i thought it was a shooting star,m but then it stopped and flew up and then to the right and its direction changed.

Mind you its winter here in NY so 5pm comes around and its already very dark here. So I was taking my dog for a walk at about 5pm, on my way in I saw a plane in the sky that drew my attention, then coming down is what i thought a shooting star that crossed paths with the plane. it keeps slowly flying across the sky downwards as the plane flies into the distance, but then the thing just stops and pulsates a light and starts going up suddenly! Then it flew to the right and i watched it for about 10 minutes just staring in suspicion. And then after i had been watching it slowly move in a straight line, it stopped moving and just started bouncing slightly, or what appeared as it "shaking". I just stopped staring and came in, quite freaked out.
