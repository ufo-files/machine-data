Two bright flashing lights moving quickly , from opposite directions, maybe a mile or two away. When they met, the one moving east quickly changed direction.

We were out on our nightly walk in the tiny Gulf town of Cedar Key, Florida. We were both looking out over the Gulf of Mexico when we saw two very bright flashing lights appear. They seemed to be a couple of miles away, over the water, and moving fast toward each other. I should note that these lights were low over the water, maybe 100 to 200 feet, and much brighter than any airplane lights Ive seen. They seemed to be moving at a steady speed, and when they approached each other, the one moving West didn't seem to slow or change coarse. The one moving East however, immediately reversed its direction without slowing down, and joined the other, moving West right beside it until we could no longer see them (because of trees and a building between us and the water). After taking a few minutes of excited talk about what in the world they could have been, and wondering how anything could move that fast and just change direction, we had no choice but to accept the possibility that they may have not been from "around here". We now look for them every time we walk, with anticipation of another encounter!
