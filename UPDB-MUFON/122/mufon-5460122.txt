{rjl} Bright orange light

I was standing outside my home smoking and observed a bright orange light streak downward at about a 45 degree angle. What was strange was it only lasted about 3 or 4 seconds and came out of nowhere. It was not a plane or meteor. The orange light appeared at about an altitude of 1000' and disappeared at about 500'. The sky was completely clear. Approx. in the area of I-17 freeway and Carefree Hwy just north of Phoenix.
