I was travelling west on highway 1 with my grandkids and took a series of photos of the area not at anything in particular and I didn`t see anything at the time. two days later I was going over my photos when I noticed I had captured an unexplained craft at very high elevation . the area of sky where I captured the craft was clear with only some wispy clouds which did not interfere with line of sight. I sent the photo to the McMillan space observatory in Vancouver BC to see if it was the space station but they said it was not visible during the day and they had no idea what I had photographed. I will leave it up to you to hazard a guess what it is but I know it is not an airplane or a balloon.
