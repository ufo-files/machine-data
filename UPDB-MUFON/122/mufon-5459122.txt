{rjl} 4 Balls of Light with no sound flew by my 13th floor window

On Monday, July 29th, 2019. Four Bright objects flew by my 13th story patio door. The entire duration was less than five seconds. The curtains were open because we were expecting a thunderstorm soon. Just as I was looking out these four objects that looked like balls of lights instantly passed by my retirement tower apartment. I choose to report this after a friend recommend I report this incident.
By the time I reached my patio door the objects were completely out of sight in the direction they were headed.
