{nd} Extremely bright blue and green light rapidly descending in sky

Went out on back deck of my house to let out the dog at approximately 3:15am. Sky was clear, stars completely visible. I noticed out on the horizon an extremely bright blue object with a bright green back end appear out of no where and rapid move forward and then almost in an arc descending downwards then disappear. It was too large to be a shooting star and it was not up in the sky it was more out in front of me on the horizon. Next thing I knew it was gone.
