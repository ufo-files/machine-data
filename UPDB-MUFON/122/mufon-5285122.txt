I saw a reflective round object hovering well above flight path of S.L. Intern. Airport. It made no noise and very slowly traveled North

I was walking my at dog approx 8:45 am on Sept. 17, 2021 and saw what I first thought was a clump of Mylar balloons floating in the western sky. Then I saw an airplane go by in the same area and realized the object was much higher in the sky than I thought and it couldn't be balloons. It was a very reflective, shiny metallic color and made no noise. It seemed to just float slowly across the sky, moving to the north. When I zoomed in on the video I recorded, you could see what looked like lights on the bottom of the object.
