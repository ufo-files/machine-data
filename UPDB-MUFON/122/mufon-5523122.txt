Observed bright red

Approx. 8:45 p.m. on Oct. 22, my husband and I saw a very bright red "glowing" red object in the sky outside of our home. It was moving north to south laterally but making no sound whatsoever. It resembled a chinese lantern but was much larger (like an aircraft) and higher in the sky. Unlike a glowing lantern or balloon, this object did not ascend. It moved laterally (south to north). My husband usually has an answer for everything, but not for this. Unless it was a blimp flying late at night, we are very perplexed!!! Ironically, upon doing a search, there were other similar sightings (the 10/18 Munster, IN, sighting on your website) and a 10/8 Crown Point, IN sighting found on UFO's Northwest.com. We would love to know more about this!
