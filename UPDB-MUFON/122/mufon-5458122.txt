{cak} sound of a fighter jet

Woke up after midnight. Not sure of the exact time. Kent had a fly over on 9/11 and I heard like the sound of a fighter jet. Looked out of bedroom window. I thought I was going to see a fighter jet like the fly over on Wednesday. From my window I could see the Kent school across the Housatonic River, though it is impossible to see it from here, but I was able to see it. It also looked like daylight. Saw ufo coming directly at the house I live in and took a sharp turn over the house when it reached my window. Looked up and saw bottom which was like a bright light. I was amazed that I was seeing a UFO so close. The UFO then hovered over the back yard about 4-5 ft. and changed shape. It became cubed as it hovered and had a square window. From my window I felt like my head was being held by this framed window in the UFO. Felt vibrations around my head. All of a sudden it aimed at me and zapped me. Woke up at 1;30 am and found myself on my side No memory of incident after. Just remembered being zapped.
