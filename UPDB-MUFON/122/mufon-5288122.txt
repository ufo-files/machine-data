Multiple in-line unidentified objects traveling one-after-the-other

On date/time listed, I was in the 3200 block of Canyon Road taking my dog out for her nightly break. I looked to the North and saw multiple star-sized objects traveling in a straight line from the NW to the NE just North of Los Alamos. There appeared to be a starting point to the NW of Los Alamos from where the objects first appeared, but nothing visible, not even a cloud. The object would just appear in the sky and then they traveled one-after-another in a straight line to the NE. The objects would then disappear after being visible for approximately 30 seconds each. The distance they traveled in the time they were visible took them from NW of Los Alamos to a position NE of Los Alamos in the night sky (they appeared west of the N Star and traveled to a position E of the N Star).  All total, I must have witnessed about 3 dozen of these objects.
