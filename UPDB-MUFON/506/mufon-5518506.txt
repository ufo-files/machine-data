Bright white light moving like jet then suddenly briefly disappeared, changed direction and was out of sight in 2 seconds

Last night May 28th, 2012, (Memorial Day) a friend and I were sitting on a dock on Logan Martin Lake near Vincent, AL. It was a very clear night and we saw several jets flying over as you normally do on any night. 

We noticed a much brighter light that appeared square shaped that was moving about as fast as most jets might. We thought it was strange that it didn1t have any flashing lights like other planes typically do. All of a sudden the light seemed to disappear for a second from its southern flight path, then it sped off in an easterly direction at an oblique angel, upward at an incredible speed when the white light shifted to orange/red and was out of sight in about two seconds. 

I have seen comets, meteors, satellites, even Skylab, but I have never seen anything that moved like this. If anyone else saw it or has any idea what it could have been, I would certainly like to hear from them.
