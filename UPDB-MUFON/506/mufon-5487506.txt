{dr}Standing looking at the stars, appeared to look like a moving constellation.

I walked outside and stood there watching the stars for about 10 minutes, as they seemed very bright because there were no clouds. while talking on the phone, once my eyes adjusted, I saw a black (or transparent because it was dark) object moving at a relaxed rate in what seemed like a fixed path. It appeared 50 feet away from me and about 50 feet above me as well. The object became still for 3 seconds once it was above me/once it reached my brightly lit home, and then continued on at the same rate. I was shocked and awed. I felt stuck where I was, riveted. I noticed it was there when I saw what appeared to be a moving constellation. Upon further inspection it blocked out other stars as it moved above me. The front or center (it was difficult to tell where the formation exactly started) had a light-colored part that reflected the street lights which are orange. The whole thing moved silently and smoothly. 3 minutes into the sighting it made the sound similar to the sound of something mechanical moving into place, and it eventually dissipated into the sky. Or maybe it just moved too far for me to see. The exterior lights looked like stars in a triangular but jointed formation. I counted 4 "stars" on the object.
