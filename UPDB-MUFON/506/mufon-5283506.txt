Indoor home security camera captures paranormal activity

My friend has suspected for some time that his house is haunted. He set up motion detection activated security cameras. He was at his girlfriend's house when the event(s) occurred. He got three alerts on his phone which captured what appears to be a dark image that moves in a manner inconsistent with shadows cast by insects, dust, and other normal household occurrences. For comparison, the third video captured a spider. The contents of the video are as follows:
Video 1:
        • 10 seconds in duration. 
        • anomaly appears 2 seconds in, and disappears at about the 6 second mark.
        • shadowy/smoke like object moves from left to right and then appears to "fall" into a small vortex and disappears from view.
        • there is a slightly audible vibration sound in the video. It sounds as though the vibration sound occurs when the anomaly is visible.

Video 2:
       • the entity appears to move starting on the upper right (back of the TV), then down, and back to the bottom left. Duration is approximately the same (from the 2 second to the 6 second mark)
       • at approximately 2 seconds, door can be heard in the background closing in one of the distant rooms.
       • after the anomaly disappears from view, a distinct "rattlesnake" sound is audible. 

Video 3:
       • a spider activates the motion detector.
       • the "rattlesnake" sound can be heard again at approximately 5 seconds in. 

In the first two videos where the entity is visible, the entity seems to move as if it's floating, and seems to turn into a vortex that points in the direction of intended travel. Then the anomaly appears to accelerate in that direction. Additionally, it seems as though there is a mildly audible vibration if you listen closely in conjunction with this entity.
