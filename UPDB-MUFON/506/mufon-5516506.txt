(GS) It was rectangular in size, it just hovered up and down then flew away with a loud hum.

So like usual I go outback at my house and the time was around 8:00 P.M. so I started looking up at the sky since I kept seeing different shapes in the stars then all of the sudden I could see this bright red and yellow light glowing and hovering above the trees in my neighbors backyard. My neighbors moved so no one was home but the object just hovered above the trees. After watching this for about five minutes I got out my IPhone and tried taking a photo of it but my camera wouldnt work, on top of my phone it said it had no service but I do have a drawing of what it looked like. Anyway after about ten minutes I heard some weird sound like a door opening in space, I looked at the object and it projected a white beam of light at the trees then turned off. What was weird was when the beam went off so did the yellow and red lights. I honestly don't know what it did but after about twenty minutes of hovering there the red and yellow lights came back on and it just started making a humming noise then took off into the sky.
