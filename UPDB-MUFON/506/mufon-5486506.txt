Hovering above for a few seconds then disappeared

Firstly all the above dates and times are approximate but close. I was sitting out at the end of the drive were it meets the back garden so I have the wall of the house on the right of me and a six foot garden fence on the left. wit trees and bushes in front of the fence, I had just finished looking trough a small telescope and decided to sit down for a while and just look around at the small amount of night sky visible between the house and fence. It was a few years ago but remember it well, sudden and fast the object appeared, and just hovered, oblong and 4 or 5 small windows brightly lit, I was mesmerised and didn't and couldn't take my eyes off of it and as fast as it appeared it disappeared, but while it was visible it seemed to me that every thing around me froze, it was like time stood still. No movement at all, bushes motionless and it was just me and the object and all in a couple of seconds, I think !! Funny thing is that after it had gone I could not explain to friends how large or small it was, or how far or near it was, it just was.
