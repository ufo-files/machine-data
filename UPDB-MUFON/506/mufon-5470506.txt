I went out to lock up my driveway gate, and saw a red star-ish like spot in the sky at 45 degrees approx. It was stationary. I thought it was a Police helicopter. We see them a lot around here. It turned light green and then red again. I still thought it might be a SAPD helicopter, but then it moved Northerly in the sky abruptly, a short distance and stopped. It was too fast to be a helicopter, jet or a satellite. Then still red, it continued North, and just seemed to drop out of the sky quickly. It was too high up and far away to be heard. It disappeared in the horizon behind my neighbors house across the street. The sky was exceptionally clear at the time. I never took my eyes off it. It descended faster than anything I ever seen.
