{cak} Slowly flashing light going in a zig zag pattern

I was doing my morning workout at Griffith Park when I saw a light slowly flash in the night sky. I looked up and noticed that it came on again but not in line to where it appeared to be going. It slowly flashed again and this time it was at another location. This flashing and zig zagging went on until it was hidden behind trees. It was going in a direction (SW) but kept flashing and zig zagging along. It appeared to be rather high (maybe several thousand feet) and made no sound. I wondered if it may have been the Astra jumping?
