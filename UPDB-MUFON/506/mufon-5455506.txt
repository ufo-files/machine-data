The object was long and thin, cylindrical in shape. It was super bright green in the front 2/3rds or so, grayer at the back end. It didn't flare at any point, including when it disappeared, well before hitting the horizon or passing any other thing to block my clear view of its path. It traveled parallel to the ground, with no arc to its path and never changing elevation while I watched, which was for probably 5 ti 8 seconds. Then, it just disappeared. Wild guess as to metrics would be perhaps 10 to 20 feet long and maybe 2 to 4 feet wide, if it was as it appeared to be, about 100-200 feet up. I wondered maybe fireworks, because of the color and how low it was, but it didn't travel in an arc and it didn't flare or fall when it went out. It moved like it was under steady propulsion. It made no sound I could hear.
