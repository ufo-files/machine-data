{rjl} Two red orbs with white on edges, pulsating, changed shape.

Husband came home from work about midnight. Something made him look up in the sky and he saw two red orbs with white edges hovering in the southeast, right above the treeline.There was no sound. There were no planes or helicopters that he could see. The orbs pulsated, hovered, and moved erratically, changing directions in a way a plane or helicopter could not. He took photos with his cell phone. The first photo turned up just static, but the other two show the orbs clearly and how they changed shape. My husband came into the house to tell me about it and show. When we walked out together, the orbs were gone.
