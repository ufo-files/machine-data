{rjl} Object traveled in a circular wobbling motion.

I was seven years old at the time and was indoors watching television. My older sister returned home from a date with her boyfriend (he was present)and announced that there was something strange in the sky out back. My mother and I joined them on the patio and all of us observed a bright orange disk moving in a circular path while wobbling allowing us to see the edge of it showing that it was a disk not a sphere. Someone said it was a "flying saucer" which terrified me and I ran and hid under the kitchen table. I did not see how it arrived or departed. This sparked a life long interest in the subject. I later became MUFON State Director for Virginia but never formally reported this event.
