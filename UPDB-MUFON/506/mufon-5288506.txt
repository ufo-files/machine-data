UFO appeared out of nowhere

Me my sister and my mom were traveling on South River Rd when out of nowhere a disk shaped object appeared out of nowhere. My mom pulled off to the side of the road because she didn’t want to drive underneath the object. The disk hovered just above the tree line observing us as we were observing them. After a minute or so the object shot straight up and disappeared within seconds. I could see windows around the middle of the disk shaped object. Lights pulsated around randomly. I can still picture the object in my mind. The next day the newspaper reported a downed Russian satellite in the area in which we saw the UFO. This was no satellite! I did not remember this incident for years! Then suddenly a couple of years ago I was watching a show about UFOs and abruptly remembered my experience.
