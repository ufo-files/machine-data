{nd} silver,stationary,cylindrical object, grew super bright, dimmed and disappeared.

Was driving east bound on Shadow Lane on my way to my storage unit. Had a strong desire to look up. Saw a silver cylindrical craft with a circular pattern of energy emanating from it. It was stationary. I pulled over to the side of the road, put the car in park, got out and looked up. I was at the crossroads of Michaelson right before Rosebud streets. The craft grew super bright, almost blinding, then dimmed and disappeared. I had my phone but I was too afraid to get it off the passenger seat for fear I would lose seeing whatever this thing was going to do. I so regret getting a pic of this thing.
When I got to the storage unit I found Rich Hoffman on the Facebook feed and posted a comment while he was watching football. He is Alabama Mufon.
