I saw the light flickering on the sky so I followed it. It descended close to me and stay for seconds the ascent until it got lost on the sky.

I was walking/jogging when I notice in the sky the light flickering. I thoughtwas a plane but keep watching because plane do not flick that way. Then the thing move vertical so I got I was not a plane. It started getting close and closer. I stop walking to see it. When it was view distance like 200 meters I thought it could be a piece of paper or 
Kite but.... paper do not move as smooth, I didn't see a cord. When closer it was like paper texture folding into irregular shape, like an origami. I would say like a origami bird or complex plane. Around 50 cm square but not square at all, to many folding lines. Cause I saw from a side when close to me, it had a folding like wind, and it moves gently. This wind or it was silver or it reflected the light on silver, I am not sure. That is the Flick has. It stay just for a few seconds and started ascending, I keep watching. That is when I took the video with my phone. I couldn't see it on the phone. The movement on the video is me looking for it, I thought I didn't got it untill I saw the video.
