{rjl} Light green sphere, with dimpled surface, photographed among thin cloud layers during sunset off Naples beach

Seven (7) high resolution still digital photos I took during the 01/20/2020 sunset off a Naples, FL beach captured a glowing, light green sphere, with a dimpled surface, moving through layers of thin, wispy clouds. During a roughly four minute span, the object moves from its initial position in a high clear patch of sky to the West North West, to several lower positions directly West, to a final position West South West, tucked up into and partially obscured by a cloud. Several of the photos captured the object moving through or partially behind wisps of clouds, ruling out lens flares or camera-related artifacts.

I did not see or notice the object at the time I took the photos, discovering it two days later when reviewing the photos (attached).
