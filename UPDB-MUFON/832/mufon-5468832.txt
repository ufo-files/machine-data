On or about June 2017 at approx. 2 am. Albuquerque, NM. Forrester Ave. NW/Kinley Ave NW. Restless, I went outside of casita and sat on my chair, looking into the night sky, finding myself star-gazing. A bright light caught my attention. I do not recall how or when it first appeared, meaning, I do not recall how it came to be that I became fixated on this. The object "danced" for some time (maybe some minutes, not sure--). Being so unusual in nature & behavior (like it was swaying or "dancing") Coming closer then backing away. The object stayed long enough that I thought to go inside and get my Iphone. I set it to film, and went back outside. To my surprise, the object was still there. I set the phone on top of fence post to hold the camera steady. (So I wouldn't be tempted to follow the light while filming, and to give the most accurate depiction. I filmed this object, until my hands and arms grew tired from holding phone up on the post. Shortly, the object began slowly moving to the South/Southeast, disappearing behind the low treeline, across the street. I felt intrigued to have captured a video of the object. Excited to have some "evidence" of an unusual craft, as I have seen unexplainable objects for years. 
The film shows the object going "dark" and then light, again. However, to my naked eye view, the object never faded, but remained illuminated.
