{cak} 150 feet above next house on street corner

ON SEPTEMBER 15, 2018 @ 535 AM MY WIFE AND I WERE PREPARING BREAKFAST BEFORE LEAVING FOR WORK AT HOSPITAL. THROUGH OUR KITCHEN SINK WINDOW WAS A HUGH WHITE FLYING SAUCER THE SIZE OF A MANSION IN STATIONARY POSITION AT CORNER OF NEIGHBORS HOUSE ABOUT 200 TO 250 FEET ABOVE. IT WAS GLOWING WHITE WITH FLICKERING LIGHTS REPEATING BACK AND FORTH. AFTER THREE MINUTES SLOWLY WENT DOWN STREET WHERE WE OBSERVED IT REPEATING SAME MANNER IN FRONT OF OUR HOUSE FOR ABOUT ONE MINUTE. IT MOVED LIKE THE SPEED OF LIGHT NE AND OUT OF SIGHT IN UNDER FIVE SECONDS.
