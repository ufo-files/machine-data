Triangle shaped object, three blue lights, one on each edge, bright red light in the middle

I went out to take the trashcan to the street, and as I went up the driveway, I looked out into the wilderness and suddenly, a solid bright red light appeared in front of me. As I stood in shock, it started moving, very rapidly, in a zig-zag almost Z like pattern. It was moving at a very high rate of speed, probably between 900-1200 knots, almost instantaneous movement. This craft had no visible green or strobe lights. It made no sound, other than a low whirring, almost like a quiet vacuum cleaner. It approached me as it lowered its altitude, I got a good look at it: it was triangular in shape with the large red light in the middle, surrounded by 3 blue lights on each corner of the triangle. The edges were round, and it looked like the rear of the craft was almost like a door. It after hovering over me, zig zagged back, then stopped and hovered, it was probably about 40,000 feet above the ground, judging by the angle of where the light was from the horizon (probably about 75 degrees) and the noise, which had quieted. It stood there then all of a sudden: emoted 5 streaks of beaming red light towards the ground: these beams were probably about 100 feet long each, and were not as bright as the craft. Then the whirring noise came back, this time going from quiet to loud, like it was accelerating. Then, as soon as the craft appeared, it shot into the sky and disappeared. I have never seen it again.
