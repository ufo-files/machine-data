{tjd} I could see it out my bedroom window. It was too big to be a star. When I stared at it I could see a ring but inside it didn1t look solid. It looked like beautiful dancing green and red lines.

I was laying in bed which is next to a window. I opened it and when I did I noticed what at first I thought was a star. But it was too big to be a star. I watched it for at least 40 minutes before falling asleep. When I stared or squinted at it I could clearly see a ring that looked as if it was almost hollow. But on the inside you could see lines of green and red that vibrated and pulsated together. It felt hypnotic to watch. Almost as if I could feel my actual heartbeat in sync with it. I felt trancelike. They were straight lines of bright green that were also bent at over 90 degree angles and bright red straight lines that were bent at lower than 90 degrees. It was the strangest thing I've ever seen. They were literally all shaking so fast. It stayed in about the same spot but I think it moved slightly to the right which would put it heading south west but I never saw it cross even half way south west from south. My fiance saw it and so did our roommate.
