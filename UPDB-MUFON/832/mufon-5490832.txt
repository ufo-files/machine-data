{jpg} Girlfriend and I coming home from late night in-n-out trip saw a huge streak across the sky just as we arrived at homel like it was meant for us to see

As my girlfriend and I pull up in my car we both at the same time saw a huge streak across the sky like we have never seen before. Immediately we gasped and turned toward each other to see if we had both seen it. The sighting only lasted 1-3 seconds but still left an impression strong enough that we wanted to report it. In eagle rock California many people light fireworks but this made no sound and didn't appear like any firework we had ever witnessed. The object made no change in direction. Only a fast but long streak as if it flew away at a very fast speed leaving a thick trail of bright flashing light.
