{rjl} A blue-green round light seen stationary in the sky for 20 minutes when observed

It was storming to the north of my house. A lot of lighting. My wife looked out our front room window and saw the blue-green light in the sky. It was about the size of a large star but there was too many clouds out to see any stars.  It didn't move at all in about 20 minutes. I called a neighbor and she saw it too. I called a friend in the northern part of the county to see if he could see it but it vanished before he got outside. It was amazing how bright it was. It looked like it might have been far away. I had no reference to size it by for distance.
