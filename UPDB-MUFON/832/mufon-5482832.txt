{cak} Sound of like 100 motorcycles woke up then saw red flashing light travel along road path and turn sharply

At exactly 1:50am, the sound of like 100 motorcycles with the loud tailpipes awoke me from my sleep. The noise was directly over the house. The noise then moved east and began to dissipate, and I looked out the window to see a flashing red light, right at the top of the trees at the end of the road, take a sharp left turn then it was gone. The noise also ended as soon as the light was out-of-sight; which doesn't happen by any man-made object, as you would normally hear the sound travel further along until well out of range. At first, I thought it was indeed just a lot of motorcycles traveling along the road, as this is a loop during the day for joy-riders. However, given the hour, and the fact that none were seen ON the road, my sighting was confirmed by the visualization of the red light.
