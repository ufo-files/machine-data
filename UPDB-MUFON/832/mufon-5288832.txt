2 V shaped craft with a third bright red light floowing

My wife and I observed two V shaped craft, each with three, bright, white lights in a V shape, flying in echelon formation. The craft to the right rear suddenly gained altitude and immediately after that a bright, red light light, very large, appeared and joined the formation before flying out of sight. These craft were large. If they were military they had to be transport planes or bombers. The lights were like nothing we have ever seen before. Especially the bright, red light. It was large. Nothing like any marking light or running light.
