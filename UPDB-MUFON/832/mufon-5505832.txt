{nd} Unusual lights.

Took out my dogs about 6ish pm. I looked to the east sky and saw lights that look like a pirate ship. Green lights in the middle with white lights on the side. My first thought was a rocket launch. I am outside quite a bit with the dogs and always looking at the sky. I have never seen anything like this. I grabbed my phone from the house and recorded something. Green lights were off then. The object was going up in a diagonal direction toward the sky. Waited for some rocket boosters to fall off thinking it was a rocket but I never saw that. Some lights stayed on and some went on and off. Even saw a triangle figure. I tried to keep the object in the center of phone screen. I zoomed all the way in the get this. Had to stop recording when my dog went after a cat....
