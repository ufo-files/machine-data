{rjl} I observed a huge, noisless mass with lights hovering above the neighborhood.

It was pretty late at night when I decided to drive to my mother's house. When I was in my car and nearly reached the intersection down the street, I noticed a string of lights that were slowly moving north slightly above the neighborhood. I could tell the lights were attached to a pitch black, sphere-like object that appeared to be huge. The main feature about the craft was that it was COMPLETELY silent. No hum, no vibration, no anything. It puzzled me greatly and my head followed the slowly moving, but noiseless, craft even when I slowly steered my car in the opposoite direction. Although I was greatly confused, I was deeply curious as to what it was. I dismissed it as some kind of new naval aircraft since I live near the " cradle of naval aviation ". But how could something so huge and flying so close to a residential area produce no sound whatsoever? I lost track of it when I proceeded to my mother's. When I returned home, it was gone.
