Two redish orange orbs floating across sky heading South East Over Lake Ontario

After watching hockey game went out side to have a smoke. looking NE I saw two redish orange orbs one higher than the other and one leading ahead there was no sound and moving very slow but both were moving at same speed, as the distance beetween them never changed. Then an air plane came into view from the north going south plane was moving much faster than the orbs. and was out of my sight in couple minutes as i continued to watch orbs they went behind large walnut tree and i could see them going past the large limbs then al of a sudden they were no longer visable i was waiting to see them apear on the open sky past the tree but never seen them again.They were heading SE over Lake Ontario.
