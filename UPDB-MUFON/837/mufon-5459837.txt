It all started 25 years ago, in 1995 I was 14 then. It was the time of December it was almost my 15th birthday. I was born in the year of 1979. December 18. It was that, like at 8 o'clock at night my older brother and I went up to the roof of the house, as we did almost every day, to see the stars, we had always liked to know if there was life outside our Planet, and it was as a hobby for us, because we also counted how many stars were in the night sky. After about an hour my older brother got off the roof, I was left alone, I just looked for a while where the constellation Orion is, I saw a small light come out from one of the stars and get into another . Since then it has been marked, because and how it did that light to leave one star and get into another.
