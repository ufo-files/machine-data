A friend and I were having a chat on the hood of her car, overlooking Chautauqua Lake in Lakewood New York around 9 pm or possibly a bit later during the summer of 1994 and we noticed two glowing blue orbs flying off, changing course at will in the western sky beside the lake. I remember saying, "Do you see what I see?" and she said "Yeah, those two blue lights." And I asked, "UFOs?" And she replied, "Yeah." We both kind of chuckled in amazement as we watched them fly off until they disappeared.

Strangely, we didn't talk about it until months after when I called her to ask her about the event. When I asked her if she remembered that night on Grandview, she answered, "Yeah, the night with the two blue lights." We talked about how we both felt as if we were hypnotized and thought it was strange we hadn't discussed it further until months later when I called her. We both kind of wondered what exactly happened because we remembered these orbs leaving from being closer to us to flying off and disappearing yet we have no recollection how they appeared. 

After this event, I had strange circle bald spots on the back of my head that were hidden underneath my longer hair at the time. I also noticed missing hair/bald spots where my beard grows and around my knees too. The whole experience left us wondering if perhaps something more had happened.
