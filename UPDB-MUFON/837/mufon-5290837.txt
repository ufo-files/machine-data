Very bright light flying overhead in Chelsea, AL

I was outside playing with my dog around dusk in the back yard, catching a few more games of fetch before it got too dark. I pulled my phone out to try to get some pictures of the moon, cause it looked kinda cool that night. I looked up and saw this bright white sphere of light in the sky moving pretty quick going NE, not absurdly fast, but it was moving, and looked like it was a lot lower in the sky than the other airplanes. 

It was the brightest thing I’ve ever seen flying away from me. There were no blinking lights to indicate it was an airplane or helicopter, and you can see what airplanes look like in the video, as there are at least 3 or 4 that fly above it during the video. I couldn’t tell how high it was, just that it looked substantially lower and larger than the other airplanes overhead, which we see a lot of as there is an airport on either side of the city we live in and we get a lot of traffic overhead. 
After the video ends, I watched it for another 10 seconds or so, then it went behind my neighbor’s roof and when I jumped up on a little patio table to see if it was still there, I wasn’t able to find it again. I also checked the airplane tracker website and didn’t see anything on there that was heading NE at that time. So I’m not sure what it was.
