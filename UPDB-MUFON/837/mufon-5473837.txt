{dr}Hovering Violet Orb while moving in a vehicle

On my way to LGA airport in a Lyft, me and the driver noticed the orb hovering over us, at first I thought it was a beam of light being shot up from the earth, but it wasn't moving even though we were travelling around 60 miles p/h. We travelled under bridges and some distance. I saw it hovering for about 6 minutes and 1 it felt nice. There was communication in an energetic sense. It was an open, inquisitive and friendly feeling. Then as soon as I started filming it, it moved abruptly to the driver and things felt grumpy, then for weeks following I had many many mishaps and unfortunate events.
