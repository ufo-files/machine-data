i was on my way to work as a us air force aircraft control & warning operator stationed at duluth air force base. i was going in to work the midnight shift from downtown duluth. i was riding down a back road to the east side of the main hwy. i was driving north to work when i saw brightness in my rear view mirror. i was driving on the road alone. i was driving with the car windows open there was no sound. the light around me got brighter and i looked out of the front passenger window i saw it flying over me about 200 to 300ft above me it appeared round and completely the color of the brightest moonlight and as large as football field. it was moving about 5 to 10 mph faster than i was driving. i had full view riding down the road for what seemed about 5 to 7 minutes. i was of course shocked because i knew what i was seeing. i was not nervous or afraid just not believing what i was seeing. it went straight ahead rapidly. it just disappeared. when i got inside the building where i worked a young Lt. was hysterically talking about seeing a ufo on the way to work. he was being escorted away from the weapons section back on to elevator i had just gotten off of talking about a ufo. i decided not to say anything to anyone. later in life i told my brothers about it and my son. i have been watching your program for years and decided to see if you are just television or real. well that is basically all that occurred for me on that day. i later heard around the job that a ufo had crashed in lake superior.                    thank you [witness name removed/cms/dr]
