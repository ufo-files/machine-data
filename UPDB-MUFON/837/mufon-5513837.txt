blimp of light

I was driving home on dec. 8 at around 10:30 at night when I saw 4-5 bright lights in a straight line in line with grouse mountain. As I drove closer to home(nearer grouse mountain) I noticed the bright objects had changed position. They were slowly moving as I stood in my driveway I observed them moving around the sky slowly in different directions. The objects looked almost like really bright stars and I watched them move around for around ten minutes. It was a cloudy night with no other stars in the sky. Really weird s**t.. hope others saw as well.
