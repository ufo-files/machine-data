OVAL SHAPE OBJECT SEEN IN CLEVELAND COMING AS IF FROM VENUS

AT ABOUT 8:30PM LOOKIN TO VENUS BECAUSE IT IS SO BRIGHT RIGHT NOW I NOTICED 2 PLANES THEN LIGHT FROM VENUS. I THOUGHT IT WAS A SATILITE UNTIL THE LIGHT CAME UNDER CLOUDS REVEALING AN OVAL SHAPE SOLID OBJECT. IT LOOKED AS IT WERE FOLLOWING THE PLANES. I THEN SAW ANOTHER PLANE THAT LOOKED TO BE BEHIND THE OBJECT. AT THIS POINT I THOUGHT IT MAY BE A MILLITARY EXPERIMENT. I CALLED TO SOME KIDS PLAYING BALL TO LOOK. THEY DIDNT SEE IT AT FIRST BUT SAW THE OBJECT FLY STRAIGHT FOR A LITTLE WHILE THEN ZIGZAGGED AND WENT UPWARD LEAVIN A SPACE IN THE CLOUDS. ONE MORE PLANE FLEW THRU THE FLIGHT PATH AND THE SKY CLEARED
