We saw two orbs hovering at high altittudes to the north of our location on June 20, 2019

We were enjoying my son's baseball game on June 20, 2019, when we saw two identical orbs in the sky appear at 8:20 PM (CT). We hadn't noticed them before that time. They were reflecting the evening sunlight and looked as bright as stars in the night sky. They hovered in place till cloud cover obstructed their view and we could no longer see them around 8:30 PM (CT). They did not move and kept within equal distance of each other during the 10 min. period.
