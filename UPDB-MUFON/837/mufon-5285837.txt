Silver orbs over North East Missouri seen from Western Illinois

7:49 PM , 8/24/2021 - We (two of us) watched a stationary silver orb appear like a star, blaze forth to at least twice as bright, dim and blaze out again, then disappear, only to reappear
in a more distant location and repeat, sometimes moving slightly to the N before fading out. It did this 4 times in 3 different locations. No noise, no jet trail (though jets were making trails in the west). I am guessing the initial light was above Palmyra, Mo, give or take, and by 8:03 it was over Rutledge, MO.
I also noticed brief silver flashes in the sky (at 45 degrees above horizon due west and NW) as if it were zipping about, and occasionally giving a single flash bulb burst.
Venus appeared at 8:02 PM 10 degrees lower in the sky than the initial sighting and W SW of initial sighting. Sightings ended at 8:03 - give or take a minute - when the Orb moved out of sight behind the tail end of a storm-cloud bank. iPhone radar app showed a line of clouds along Missouri/Iowa line to our NW. I do not know if the clouds I saw it move into were the same ones showing on radar.
MADAR unit 97, at my location, did not alert during this time, but baseline mGa bumped up to 8.25 at 7:48PM as sighting started. We had an alert at 4:30 AM on 8/25 and another at 5:45 AM 8/26/2021. Also, MADAR unit 97 had roughly 6 alerts in the preceding ten days.
This is very similar to the sightings reported to MUFON from this same location one year ago. That activity began about 8/20 and continued to about 9/15/2020. Tried to film this event but iPhone did not pick up the object against the sunset light.
