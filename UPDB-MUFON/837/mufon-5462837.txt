{tjd} Armada appeared in open between clouds traveling south at 400mph

I was at Chabad on campus smoking a cigarette. I looked up and notices two unidentifiable objects come out of clouds. I was with 7 friends and we thought they looked like red balloons, but they were clearly traveling at a high rate of speed then a fleet of them followed. They were traveling south in and out of clouds and the pulsing could be seen inside cloud cover. They were a blood red plasma color the front two were in right eschelon and there were about ten or so following in random formation. I knew immediately what I was looking at and my friends all agreed except for one person who simply couldnt believe what he saw. It stirred something in my heart that leaves me unsettled. We watched them fly into or above thick clouds until no longer visible.
