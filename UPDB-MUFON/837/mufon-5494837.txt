{cak} Solid red light hovering in the sky. Then moving west it was followed approximately 500 ft by a white object.

I was driving to the grocery store approximately 10:15 in the evening when I noticed a solid red light that appeared stationary in the sky. As a traveled down the road closer, I noticed another white light seemingly appear from nowhere. The white light blinked at the red light a few times and then both lights started to travel west while still maintaining their exact distance. Suddenly the lights just disappeared. Now, I lived for over 13 years in the flight path is detroits metro airport and I've seen hundreds of planes. These were not like anything I have ever seen before. I won't say that the lights were UFO's but, I can tell you with 100% certainty that these lights were no aircraft that I've ever seen. I was very curious but confused as to what these lights were.
