{nd} Sudden appearance of green light then quick descent, a flash of red light, then disappeared.

I was driving along Kings Highway in Minneapolis, Minnesota and as I was looking up a bright green light appeared. I got very excited as soon as I saw it because, be it a plane or not, I had never seen such a bright green color in the sky. It quickly shot down or forward towards the horizon, I am unsure of which direction because of my perspective on the ground. It did not descend softly or slowly; its movement was quick and precise. At the end of its movement, the light changed from a green to a red and then disappeared. In total, the sighting probably lasted two seconds, one and a half of which the light was green and the other half a second being red. The light disappeared before it went below the tree line. There was another passenger in the car and, although she did not see it, was frightened by my enthusiasm. I drove further towards the light, but found nothing and didn't see anything else.
