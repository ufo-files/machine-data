muffin shape-like ufo hovering and fly really fast to other sky

It was after dinner with family on typical friday afternoon, that we decided to borrow a video tape, White Fang(1991), so I assume the year was 1992, before summer. We were passing a street and I just looked up and saw this object, a muffin shape like disc, emitting orange lights, just hovering there. For a second, I thought I was seeing illusion, but then, our car past some trees and the object was out of sight. But after passing those trees and looking up, the object was still there, motionless. Then, it just zoomed across the sky, and out of sight. But speed of the object, sudden acceleration from hovering was unbelievable. I presume it took less then 3 seconds.

The other day, I saw this Hanger 1,(mufon) documentary on History Channel, and thought that, although is is 2016, and the event took place some 24 years ago, maybe it would be best if my story was shared by others who take interest.
