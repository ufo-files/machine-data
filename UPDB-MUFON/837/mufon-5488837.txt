Triangle with three lights zigzagged over house top then disapeared.

I was standing on my deck, looking at the moon through my telescope. I went inside to get a few more lenses for my telescope and when I came back out, I saw the UFO. It appeared to be three lights in triangular formation. It was moving up and down just over the neighbors roof for about 5 seconds it then vanished. I thought nothing of it until the next day when two pitch black military jets at high altitude flew overhead in the same direction the UFO was in the sky. It could be nothing, but I had to share it.
