A black cigar shaped ufo changing the shape of a small cloud.

While driving north on I-35, 3 miles south of I-20, I noticed a lone cotton ball shaped white puffy cloud. It was the only cloud in the sky except for a cloud front at least 30-40 miles due east. I new that it was not normal at all. The cloud was changing shapes at a rapid rate.It started out as a cotton ball, then a triangular shape, and then to a spiral shape, and then went from white to a dark gray color. I then noticed a black or very dark gray cigar shaped object that appeared to hovering motionless to the lower left of the cloud. I looked at the object several times to make sure that it was not an airplane or a helicopter. I then drove by a large building trying to get closer to the object to try and identify the object better and it was gone. I scanned the entire sky looking for any aircraft and none were found.I have a background in U.S. military aircraft and experimental versions also. I have only observed aircraft such as this on ufo web sites. I was not scared because I know that they exist. I don't know the origin of these aircraft, and I don't need to know. I like my career and don't want to jeopardize it in any way. I just thought you would like to know what I saw.
