{cak} Orange Orb

Around 10:55 took the dog out for a walk. Saw a bright orange light appear from over a row of pine trees that were about 150 to 200ft to the east of me, the light was going northwest I believe. There was no sound that I could hear it giving off, some times see helicopters around here and see plenty of planes both light and jumbo jets but I can always hear them. The light it was giving off was like nothing I'd seen on other aircraft, there was no blinking or multi colored lights, it was just big and orange and glowing with a bit of a halo effect around it. After I saw it for about 5 to 10 seconds I ran inside to get someone else to come and look, got back outside and was able to still see it for at least another 10 to 15 seconds before the light started to fade and then just blinked out. Not really sure how high up it was, was much higher than the pine trees, but coulda been anywhere between 500 to like 2000ft maybe, the speed was about the same as a small plane. Didn't really have any "feelings" from the seeing the object, just thought it was strange and not something I had seen before. I'm not an some amateur expert on the night sky or anything like that but I do keep an eye out whenever I'm outside and am always watching for planes, meteors, sometimes can see what I assume are satellites passing over since they are so high up and don't change direction.
