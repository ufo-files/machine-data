Flashing lights

I was driving on the golf cart when I saw lights flashing that went all through the sky. This event happened in the city of Destin in Florida. After standing outside and looking at it flash for about 20 minutes, it just stopped. At first, I thought it was the powerline by the hospital, but we asked a worker the next morning and she said that no light had been turned on that night.
