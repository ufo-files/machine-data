{cak} High altitude craft extremely fast very short contrail,

I was sitting on porch looking west about noon. I have seen many jets in my 64 years at high
altitude and I am familiar what they look like traveling at high speed at high altitude, familiar with how long it takes them to cross the sky in front of me. I could not tell if what I saw was s plane but could see contrail forming across the sky at extremely high speed, faster than I had ever seen before. The contrail was very short. I thought this could not be a plane because of the speed. I thought maybe meteorite but it was not descending. Max 60 seconds in my view.
