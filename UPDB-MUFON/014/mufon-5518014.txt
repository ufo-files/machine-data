{dr}Bright glowing disc appeared & then flew West over Dresden nuclear power plant & continued West past military plane which was heading East

I was sitting on my patio at about 8 PM facing West when a quiet, brightly glowing disc shaped craft suddenly appeared at approximately 5,000 - 10,000 feet 10 miles East of Dresden Nuclear power plant. It flew West over Dresden and passed a military jet which was about 2 miles South of the object and several thousand feet higher. The disc was moving about twice as fast as the military jet and continued West towards the horizon. It would have been impossible for anyone looking at the sky in that direction to have missed it as the object was VERY bright, very fast, made no sound and left no contrail.
