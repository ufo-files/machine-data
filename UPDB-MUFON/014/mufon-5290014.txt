Large (very bright) light appeared over mountain across the lake (2miles) from our window. fell slowly stright down then on beyond mountain

Jerry and I were sitting in front room watching T.V. when we saw a very bright white light fly straight down from above the mountain, (Gold Mountain) across the lake from us. (see photo). It was so bright we thought as first that it was a meteor.
However it fell straight down. Meteors we have seen in the past fell in a horizontal path.  It was also falling too slowly.
Not sure what it was but it was very large. The photo shows our view out our window facing the lake and I put a white dot on the photo to indicate the size and possession of the object we saw.
The lake here is 2 miles across and the top of the mountain is aprox. another mile. This was 7:00p.m. tonight, 1-21-2021

Did not get a photo of the object. It all happened too fast to get a camera. Dale
