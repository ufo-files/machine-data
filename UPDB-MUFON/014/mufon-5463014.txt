I'm an Air Traffic Controller. On January 15, 2019 at approximately 0400Z I received an inquiry from a commercial airline pilot asking if i had or knew of any aircraft that were maneuvering at his 1-2 o'clock and above his current altitude of 36000 feet. He stated there appeared to be two aircraft preforming "dog fight" maneuvers. I expanded the range of my radar display and did not observe any primary or secondary radar targets matching the location and altitude of the pilots information. 

There were 2 other aircraft in the area and when question, neither flight crew would/could confirm the initial report. 

I asked the pilot who made the initial inquiry if there was any way the light could be reflections of interior/exterior lights on the cockpit windows and he replied "No". 

The information was passed to the next controller and to the watch supervisor.
