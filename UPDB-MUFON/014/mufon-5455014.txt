{tjd} Observed what appeared to be four lights in a westerly direction. I then looked at them in binoculars and saw nine lights.

At approximately one thirty five am on 3/4/2020 I went outside my residence to smoke a cigarette. I noticed in a western direction a series of lights above the tree line where there shouldn’t be any lights! I called my girlfriend out and she claimed to see one. I took a few pictures and then ran inside to grab my binoculars. Once I adjusted them to view the lights more clearly I noticed seven lights in a mismatched pattern. After a few minutes two more lights came into focus making the total of nine different lights. It was approximately fifteen minutes later the lights seemed to “shoot off” is a way to best describe it, in different directions but none went downward. I reviewed the pictures I took from my iPhone 6(quality not the best) and after zooming in on what appears to be one light you can in fact see four then five and even a faint six different lights all in the series of pictures I took one after another! I don’t know what I truly saw and I would like some kind of answer please! As I said in the area these lights were located was above the tree line with no houses behind for a distance and none high enough to have their lights shine that high up! My girlfriend and I lived here since December and we go out multiple times a night to smoke. We’re also very observant and never seen these lights where they were at! And never any lights in a cluster like these were! Also if you zoom in on the photos you’ll see the multiple lights even though it looks like one bigger light if left not zoomed.
