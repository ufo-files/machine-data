{rjl} Pulsating multi colored sphere/orb slowly moving.

It was about 9:30/10:30pm. I drove over to my girlfriends house. She lives in an apartment complex with a lot that has great view of the sky. Little to no visual obstructions (trees/buildings...) I parked my car got out and started to walk to her building. I looked up as it was a very clear night and I love watching the stars anyways. I noticed a "star" in the sky that was way way brighter than the others and had some unusual characteristics. It was slowly moving and rapidly and randomly changing color. I stood there for a few minutes in awe observing the heavenly mystical. I then walked to my girlfriends building knocked on her door and when she answered I said "call me crazy but there is a very unusual star in the sky. Come check it out." We then both walked out to the lot where we could see it clearly. We both sat there and stared at it for about 10 minutes throwing ideas out there as to what it could be. We were both stumped because of the rapid color Chang pretty much omitted any rational guess as to what this could possibly be. It looked like a star in the sky but the longer we looked the more we thought it to be much much smaller and close to earth. My personal guess is it was no more than 2 miles away and possibly only several hundred feet in the air. We both went inside her house to try to research the stars in the sky after about 10 minutes of observation. It never landed nor wavered from its slow path.
