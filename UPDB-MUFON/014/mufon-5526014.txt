14 TO 15 OBJECTS IN SKY

ON JUNE 11 2011 I WAS OUTSIDE WITH MY COUSIN AND MY NEFEWS. THEY WERE THROWING THE FOOT BALL BACK AND FORTH AND MY NEFEW WENT INSIDE TO GET THE PUMP FOR THE BALL. MY COUSIN TIED HIS SHOE LOOKED UP AND SAID (HEY WHAT IS THAT.)I LOOKED UP AND THERE WERE 14 TO 15 OBJECTS IN THE SKY MY NEFEW RAN INSIDE AND GOT MY CAMERA. SOON AFTER THAT I TOOK A FEW PICTURES. THEY SLOWLY MOVED ALL TOGETHER BUT ONE THAT STAYED RIGHT OVER ABOVE FOR ABOUT 15 MIN. SOME SAY BALLONS SOME SAY UFO YOU BE THE JUDGE.
