Orb with long tail. Bright white, not as bright as sun.

1:38pm 28 August 2017 central Christchurch, New Zealand. 
Looking east above 4 story building noticed a bright white orb (perfect circle) with a tail about 5 times the length of the diameter of the orb. Tail tapered to a point with a small break in the middle. Tail was the same bright white as the orb. Reminded me of the brightness of burning magnesium. Was moving fairly quickly, but not as fast as a meteorite and no sonic boom. Suddenly disappeared as I watched, making a sound like the crack of static electricity as it did so. The outline of this thing was clearly defined (not fuzzy or transparent). There were a few clouds in the sky but it was well below these. Not sure how close it was to me but it must have been at least 50 metres long including the tail. The tail did not appear to be a vapour trail,but rather a part of the orb. I watched it for only a couple of seconds before it disappeared.
