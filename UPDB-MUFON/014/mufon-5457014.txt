{rjl} At first I thought it was the best shooting star I'd ever seen, then it became evident that it was something else.

I was driving home from working in Plano, TX. West on Hwy 70. East, Southeast of Ardmore OK. When I saw a shooting star and called attention to it. One guy looked and the other one had his face buried in his phone and never looked up. The object did not leave a long trail, and the trail dissipated quickly. It was bright green and the trail was more gaseous looking than sparkly. As it descended it became more oblong, larger and pale green in color. For a moment, as it first slowed, it looked more spherical and more defined in its outline, revealing a dark ring towards the center of the object. As it slowed further it appeared even larger, more "white-green", more oblong, and for an instant, it looked like the light silhouetted an outline of a more oblong-shaped saucer with a darker band around the bottom portion of the object (like it had turned downward). The object hovered for a quick moment Then it appeared to blip away, leaving no trace. A few minutes later, as we approached the area where the object appeared to have disappeared from, two military jets flew in at vary low altitude from the North to the South.
