constellation shape of about 16 star lookinglights flew very fast stayed in formation

My husband and I were sitting in our driveway looking at the super moon. Then we looked straight up above us. At first I thought I was looking at a constellation. The strange formation of about 16 lights moved extremely fast and never changed its formation. There was no sound. My husband had run into the house to grab the video camera. In about the 10 seconds it took for him to come back out they traveled too far out of range for him to capture it. They were traveling from west to east and I continued staring until I just couldnt see them anymore.
