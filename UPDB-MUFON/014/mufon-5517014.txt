Orange orbs ascending in the southern sky

While watering the lawn at night three objects in the sky caught my attention, as soon as I saw them I knew this was something out of the ordinary, and immediately turned off the lights to get a better view, the sky was very clear: three red-orange orbs ascending in the sky to the south southeast in a rather straight path, when they reached a certain height each one faded out and began moving in an arch pattern until you couldn1t tell the difference between the orb and a star.
A few minutes later another three objects ascended one by one, again fading out after and mixing with the stars; after witnessing the first six orbs I went inside to call my dad, when we returned we witnessed the same pattern repeat with about another six to nine orbs with the same characteristics, after a little over 20 minutes it stopped and we were left scratching our heads.
