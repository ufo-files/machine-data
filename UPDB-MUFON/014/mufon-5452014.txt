Observed a black helicopter and black sphere in direct contact with each other.

On September 15th, 1999, I was driving home from work. I was heading west on Stevens Creek blvd. As I approached the hill in the road, I looked up and witnessed a black helicopter hovering in front of me about 400" away. My first inclination was that it was from the nearby Moffett Field airbase. located 8 miles to the north. On this base is also NASA Ames Research Center. Once my eyes realized I was seeing a black helicopter, that was when I saw the solid black sphere hovering in front of the helicopter which was facing east (towards me). I slowed down my car so I could get a better look. The object in question was between 4'-10' in diameter with no visible markings, lights or propulsion. 

It hovered for approximately 15 seconds before it "accelerated" abruptly to the North, towards Moffett Field. The helicopter swung its tail around and immediately proceeded to "chase" it. By the time I reached the end of the street, both the helicopter and object were gone. 

When I spoke to a aeronautical engineer that worked for NASA at the time, he confirmed that what I saw was real but he couldn't divulge any more information than that.
