it was a glowing light of red yellow and green color

That evening i saw a glowing light from my house windows. The base color of the object was red light and it was also flashing yellow and green light after same time interval. There is a river and mountains behind my building site and i saw the object probably on the upper sky of the river. The object was not only changing its path but it was also hovering for few minutes. I started filming the object and also showed it to my wife. I guess the object immediately noticed it and disappeared behind the buildings.
