{rjl} 3 orange balls of light traveling right above tree tops

Was down at barn about 1030 11pm. About 25 ft away an orange ball of light comes travelling almost right above me right over the tree tops. A few moments later a 2nd ball of light comes over the tree tops following the first. A few moments later a 3rd ball of light comes over the tree tops following the first two. They were going the same speed, same height, same distance between and all were totally silent. They passed almost right above me, passed over another tree, and went behind a 3rd tree and then just disappeared.
