Orb, leaving a streak, then re-appearing over neighbors home.

Sunday 7-25-21 In the meantime. Nothing happened Saturday but last night, about to step inside when a streak flew by like a shooting star. A flash caught my eye and I turned to look in its vicinity over my neighbor’s house across the street. A glowing streak from low too high for about 30 yards did I see this "light" streak/trail. So, my mind processes, shooting star, super close, but shooting stars don’t travel from low to high. It appeared at 4 feet above the roofline, but burned out at 30 feet, a certain degree of angle, no burning debris trail, just a streak, like you wave a glowing light stick through the air super-fast. So, I watched where the streak of light had burned out at its apex because instinct told me to and...out of nowhere, I can see depth, neighbor's houses, stars and everything behind it when a bright white light like last Wednesday flashed, then it just closed to black, gone. Happened just before midnight.
