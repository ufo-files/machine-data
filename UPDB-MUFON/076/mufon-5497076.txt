I was working at a restaurant, shift was almost over and I was taking out the trash. I noticed a large light as if there were 2 moons in the sky that night. Before taking a good look I pinched myself, slapped myself and spun around in a circle just to make sure it was actually there. This thing was huge, small city size possibly, it hung in the sky as quiet as a cloud. The shape was odd. Looked like 2 almonds pointy side facing away from each other and the large ends were connected by a sphere. Not sure if it was emitting light or reflecting moon light, but that was about the color. It looked as if it had metal scales almost as a texture, however they were not as uniform as a fish or lizard. After watching for a few seconds I had a feeling like it knew I was watching and it went dark like someone turned off a light switch. Has anyone ever seen anything else like this?
