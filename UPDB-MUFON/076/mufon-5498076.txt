{nd} A spotted hovering UFO over Bayonne, New Jersey

I was just leaving work when I noticed a glowing orange orb appear out of the middle of the sky at around 10:30 P.M. and 11:00 P.M. It streaked across the sky at a hovering speed no faster than a plane, slowed down and than picked up its original speed. It was very reflective but you could easily tell it was NOT A PLANE. It was an orange orb glowing and flashing across the sky and eventually disappeared into the clouds. I have video proof of this event that had happened on my phone as well. The resolution came out quite well and I was even talking to a witness next to me who had also seen the UFO too.
