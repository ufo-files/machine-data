I was leaving the University of Farmers which is directly south approximately 2 miles south of the Gerald R Ford Airport. Where I was at is one of the main N/S runways for the airport. As I was leaving I looked up and noticed a very large jet moving slowly across the airport airspace not landing and it caught my attention since it was in their airport airspace, not landing, and not suppose to be there. It was flying e/w right over top of the aiport itself. 
A light caught my peripheral vision right over head to the east of me. It was a triangle of light and there was one at the tip and three across the back. The light on the back right corner was moving in an odd pattern but did not break the triangular shape. It was silent.  It was moving extremely fast. It was awe inspiring and I am going to be sincerely disappointed if this was some sort of military craft. It was in GRF airspace it cut right across the their southern runways right over top of the University of Farmers. It happened so quickly and I was thunderstuck. I never once thought of grabbing my phone for a picture.
