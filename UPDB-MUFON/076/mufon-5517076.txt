{dr}Three lights that initially formed a triangular shape, then moved independently.

I looked into the northern sky from Granada Hills, CA to see three lights forming a triangular shape that appeared to be rotating slowly. Two of the lights faded out while one continued. Two more lights rose up and faded out rather quickly while the third light remained bright and began to move Westerly (toward me) then drift down and to the South followed by a slow change in direction to the North. Finally it descended and became so dim you couldnt see it anymore.
