Unexplained Object Found On Digital Photo

I was sitting at the front door at 10.10 am with my camera on the 1st July 2018 ( Canon SX600 HS ) , it was a beautiful , clear summer's day and the sky was absolutely cloudless , i remember it being very quiet , i took a digital photo of the view and at that time saw nothing unusual , on uploading to my pc i discovered what appeared to be a disc shaped craft hovering in the otherwise clear sky , i examined the object with zoom and it didn't solve the mystery in any way and it remains unexplained.
