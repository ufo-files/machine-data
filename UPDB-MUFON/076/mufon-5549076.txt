Glowing red ball with smaller white balls circling

I was honeymooning at a place called Secret Harbor on St. Thomas. My wife and I were on the beach around midnight and three strange clouds came floating in from our right. All of a sudden this big bright ball came floating out of the first cloud. My wife became extremely frightened. The ball started getting brighter at the same time 6 to 8 smaller white balls started circling around the big red ball and gave the appearance of an atom. Suddenly the little white balls began to circle much faster and shoot what looked like light rays down. This lasted about 5 minutes then it went back into the clouds. Then the clouds floated away.
