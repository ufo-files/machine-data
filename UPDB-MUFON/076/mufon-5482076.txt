{jpg} 2 bright white star-like lights seen in the ENE sky; one hovered and then moved off to the south. 2nd light followed the first one.

The witness was on the back porch of his home in Dixon, IL having a cigarette when two very bright lights appeared in the east northeastern sky. The lights were considerably brighter than the planet Jupiter and were silent. No FAA mandated lighting was noticed. One light was stationary for about 3 seconds, dimmed somewhat and then started moving in a straight path off to the south.
