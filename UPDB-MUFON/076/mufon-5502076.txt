3 lights closely clustered over lake Ontario. Hovered together for about 45 seconds then went up slowly and disappeared

On June 7th 2014 at approximately 10 pm I was walking back to my house from the neighbor's around 10:15pm. I was walking south when I observed 3 lights in the sky quite distant (likely over lake Ontario). The lights looked like 3 balls of fire in the sky. They shifted slowly and slightly for about 45 seconds and then started moving upwards and slowly disappeared. Initially I though it was an airplane coming towards me but the red and blue lights were not visible. I am a pilot and this certainly did not move like any aircraft I have seen. I thought is was really neat but immediately got on the internet to see if anyone else had reported these lights. I noticed one other similar report on this website but I think the person got the date wrong. My wife also witnessed the event
