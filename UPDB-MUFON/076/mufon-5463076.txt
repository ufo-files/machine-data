{rjl} I seen it in the review Mirrow. It was traveling behind us in the air above the road

My husband and I were heading West on a backroad at 12:57am on Jan. 19,2019. We were under a 1/4 mile to our turnoff to be home,so I glanced in the rear view mirror out of habit and it looked like police car lights in the sky above the road. Then just as I turned onto my street-my husband and I looked to the west, and the sky was lit up, and looked to be on fire. I got kind of freaked out and drove the remaining 2 minutes home. We stopped in the driveway-looked at each other and said: "Let's go look". We went back down the road,and there was nothing crashed in the field or the road. Nothing was on fire. Since it was so dark-we did not get out and look. We just went home.
