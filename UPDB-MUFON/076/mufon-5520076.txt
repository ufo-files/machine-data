TO THE LADY THAT REPORTED:

When I read your story my hair stood up, we seen the very same thing off in the woods. It looked like someone was up in a tree swinging light bulbs around (they were that big),they also had a insect type movement. We seen 2 and sometimes 3. They would light up and then go out, they were huge!

My husband said it looked like a lightning bug on steroids. We had a total of 3 people in the car, and we back your story 110%! 
Please forgive me, but I have to admit had I not witnessed this with my own eyes and just randomly read your story I would have thought you were crazy.(sorry) : )

I have never seen anything like it. We went back tonight and sure enough they were there, they lite up and then went out, we waited about 5 mins but they didn't light up again so we left plus it was very late. I had a camera ready to make a video too! Tomorrow night I am going back in hopes of capturing this! Its pretty incredible to see. 

*I used the date March 14 on my report because that is the first night we seen them and they stayed around for the full 10-15 minutes that we were there.
