Witnessed a bright blinking light with a trail descend very fast then move in erratic motions.

I was sitting in my living room when I caught a bright light out of the corner of my eye through the windows in the front of my house. I immediately got up and went to the window where I witness the object at the end of its descent and I lost it behind the trees in my neighborhood. I opened my front door and stood out on my front porch for a better look. I witnessed the object move from the east to the west then back to the east again. During these movements the objects was partially obstructed by trees. The last I saw it it was moving from the west to the east at tree top level.

The object itself was very bright. Brighter than any star, I would compare it to the brightness of a military or SAR flare. During the sighting the object had a very fast strobe/pulse to it. This object left a noticeable trail/tail behind it, the most apparent thing was how the tail looked like 16 or 32 bit frames. Each frame/pixel was a different color ranging from bright white to blue. The object maintained the trail/tail throughout the entire event.
