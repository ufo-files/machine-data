{jpg} Brilliant white light flashed 3 times, blinding me then disappearing.

Was observing some planets through my binoculars when a brilliant white light flashes three times in succession moving from east to west. It was so bright my friend and I were blinded and couldn't make out any shape. It was well above any nearby buildings and well below the height most aircraft fly at in that area. Yes I know this is NYC, and there are a lot of bright lights here already, but I've lived in this city 15 years and NEVER have I experienced something that blindingly bright, so much to the point I couldn't look at it. By the last flash it was gone. Because we weren't aware of it before its flashing caught our attention, total sighting time was about 4-6 seconds.
