The details of the email sent by my Father to share details of the Sighting in India to :
askngs@nationalgeographic.com

"During my travel on sixth of October from Delhi to Baroda by Paschim Express train ,when the train was around Faridabad at approx. 1802 hrs IST. I happened to see a strange spectacle in the sky. I saw an elliptical ring quite large in size and was in the direction NW-WEST, at an elevation estimated to be 60degrees above horizon ,it was a sharp well defined ellipse in a grey tone and as if moving parallel to the train, and the same was like a flattened circle, and I could track it for about seven minutes or so but Ttime was lost in taking out the camera, aqnd due to fading light and moving train and intervening trees etc. I could only take two snaps of the same and also shoot a multiburst image with my Sony DSCP100 digital camera ,in my excitement i failed to shoot a movie. Besides me the same thing was shown to my co-passengers about 5 or six of them"
