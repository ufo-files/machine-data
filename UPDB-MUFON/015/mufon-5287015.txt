Hovering large bright object in sky.

On 17 Jun 2021 while driving West on Hwy 421 in Deep Gap NC very near the Continental Divide and in the Blue Ridge Mountains there was an extremely large bright shiny object stationary in the sky. I pulled off the road twice in different locations trying to capture photos on my cell phone. When enlarged the object looked almost box like with 8 sides. At times the object looked like a tic tac. It also appeared to have some sort of dark emission around it but this was only visible in the enlarged photos. There were no stars in the sky. The sun was still up. The moon could be seen but it was no where near this object. I have never seen anything like this before in my 57 years of life. It had to be an incredibly large object as it was far in the sky but appeared with a light so bright and strong.
