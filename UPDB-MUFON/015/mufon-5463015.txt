{jpg} Boomerang Flying Object on Super Blood Wolf Moon Eclipse

Date/Time: 1/20/2019 11:43pm Central Time
Location: Arlington, TX
Weather: Very clear, not a cloud in the sky. Stars and moon highly visible.
Duration: 3-5 seconds

I went out to see the Super Blood Wolf Moon and take some bad pictures with my iphone. I sent pictures to friends and family encouraging them to go out and see the eclipse.
I looked back up and from the Southwest there was a Boomerang shaped object gliding through the air. The object was as dark or darker than the surrounding sky lit by the red moon. At one point it seemed like it could be a partially transparent triangle, but I believe it was more of a V-shape.
The front of the craft glimmered. It was not clear if this was a reflection or if the light was coming from the object. From the initial direction, it did not appear as individual light sources but more of a shimmering.
It flew in a straight line overhead in a Northeast trajectory. After it passed overhead it was clear the front outline had a series of evenly spaced individual white lights. 9 to 11 total lights making a V to my best estimation. It crossed the sky a couple of inches to the west of the moon from my perspective. As it headed away to the Northeast I unlocked my phone and fumbled for the photo app. I looked up and it was gone.
I estimate I watched it glide across the sky in 3-5 seconds.
It appeared to have a steady velocity and straight path.
It was completely silent and did not appear to leave any contrail.
