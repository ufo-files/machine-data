{rjl} VIEWED BRIGHT STAR LIKE OBJECT WHICH BECAME SOLID

My wife and I were standing on our deck Monday evening 08/18/14. She said what is that bright light? When I looked it was gone. Then she said there it is again! This time I got to view it for about 10 seconds before it faded behind a cloud. It was bright as a star but moving slowly across the sky.

She then exclaimed about a minute later there it is again but it isn1t shining bright like a star. This time we both viewed the object for at least 5 to 7 minutes. There was no lights and no sound. The object had a dull grey color to it and wobbled a little as it glided across the sky. 

The object had edges to it and had for lack of a better description a hump in the fuselage if you will kind of like a B-2 Bomber. I ran into the house to get my binoculars after about 6 minutes. When I came out my wife was off of the deck and looking behind an 80 to 90 foot tree which stands in my neighbor1s yard which was now blocking her view. When she looked behind the tree the object was gone and I waited on the deck to see if it would come out the other side but it never did. 

I am retired Air Force. I have seen many types of aircrafts in my 20 years of service. I have trained to turn off planes that have crashed and safety their guns and ejection seats and rescue the pilots in my duties as a fireman! This was no platform I have ever seen and am truly intrigued by what we saw. 

We just moved into our home late April of this year! On almost any given night we can go outside and see strange lights and movements in the sky! Don1t know why the activity is so prevalent at our new location but I am keeping my head to the skies.
