Very bright white light

Hello, I saw a very strange light today on the sky, way brighter compared to a helicopter search light, etc. (and I saw one of these in action, so I can relate).

It's color was mostly white with a very little bit of red in it.

So, I opened my window to smoke a cigarette, looked up at the sky (because I love a clear sky with some stars) and at some point there was this very bright light (it almost looked like the light of a little comet or an asteroid entering Earth's atmosphere) appearing out of nowhere, it flew straight from S to N with its lights on for around 5 seconds, turned it off and I saw that it stopped (there was a little dot up there in the sky, that's why I saw it), after that the little light dissapeared as well. There was no sound either.
