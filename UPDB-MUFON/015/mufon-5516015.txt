{nd} Lights above clouds

I was home in my backyard at night, I can only see them at night and only when there are clouds. 

I always look up whenever Im outside so when I saw these lights, on top of a giant cloud, it caught my eye. I had no idea what it was when I saw this light show, but I thought it was very strange.

I see groups of lights, hard to tell how many, but at least 4. They move around quickly and seem to each have their owna pattern, as if each one goes a different route but all traveling the same pattern.

I live in Las Vegas so at night I can always see the beaming light from the Luxor, however I cannot see a source for these cloud lights. I have gone looking in my car and tried to find a source for these lights, and since Vegas is relatively small, I could not find any source on the ground that would explain why there are lights above the clouds.

I first noticed these lights maybe a year or so ago. I saw them many nights on top of the clouds, then they just stopped for a few months and they have started up again.

I lost sight of them when the clouds move.

Also, Im sure youll think Im crazy, but the clouds have all kinds of faces on them. I had my daughter look at them sometimes to see what she thought it looked like and she saw the same thing as I did
