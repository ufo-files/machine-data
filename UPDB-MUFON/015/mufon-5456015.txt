MY OLDER BROTHER (15 YEARS OLD) AND I (10 YEARS OLD) WERE MOVING DIRT FROM THE YARD TO NEXT TO THE FOUNDATION OF THE HOUSE WHEN WE FELT A WARM WIND BLOW DOWN ON US AND SWIRL THE DIRT AROUND US GETTING THE DIRT IN OUR EYES. THE WIND INSTANTLY DRIED OUT THE DAMP DIRT. WE FELT A TINGLING SENSATION LIKE A STRONG STATIC CHARGE. WE HEARD A NOISE THAT SOUNDED LIKE A WRENCH HITTING METAL. WE LOOKED UP AND SAW A WHITE STATIC DISGUISE DISINTEGRATING REVEALING THE FLAT BOTTOM OF A SAUCER. IT WAS ABOUT 35' OFF THE GROUND AND ABOUT 100 FEET ACROSS. WE MOVED AWAY FROM IT AND THEN WHILE IT WAS HOVERING OVER THE GROUND SAW A TALL BLACK HUMAN LIKE ALIEN EXIT THE SHIP. HE LOOKED SO SKINNY, HE LOOKED LIKE HE WAS STARVING. HIS EYE SOCKETS WERE SUNK IN. HE WAS ABOUT 8 FEET TALL. HE WENT TO THE SIDE OF THE SHIP AND OPENED A PANEL TO REVEAL THE INNER WORKINGS OF THE CRAFT.
THERE WERE TUBES AND IT APPEARED THAT GAS WAS BEING RELEASED, LIKE REFRIGERATE GAS
(THE CRAFT MUST HAVE BEEN LOWER TO THE GROUND, THE WITNESS SAID IT DID NOT TOUCH DOWN BUT HOVERED AND NEVER MOVING MORE THAN 35 FEET IN ANY DIRECTION. THE ALIEN SAW US AND HISSED AT US. WE FELT A HUGE SENSE OF EVIL COMING FROM HIM. .THEN THE WINDOW SHIELDS POPPED OPEN ( THEY MADE A POPPING SOUND) WE NOTICED SYMBOLS ON THE CRAFT.
THEN THEY HEARD A NOISE LIKE A TRAIN ON A TRACK AND THEY SAW ABOUT 12 HUMANS EXIT THE CRAFT. THEY WERE WEARING UTILITY UNIFORMS (PANTS AND SHIRTS) WITH EMBLEMS ON BOTH SHOULDERS. ON THE LEFT SHOULDER WAS A FLAG, SOME USA, GERMAN, RUSSIAN, POLISH, BRITISH AND CANADIAN. THE RIGHT SHOULDER PATCH WAS A CIRCLE WITH AN HOUR GLASS INSIDE OF IT.
3 MORE ALIENS CAME OUT, A DOG FACED BIG FOOT LIKE CREATURE ABOUT 7 FEET TALL AND TWO SMALLER SOLID BLACK ALIENS ABOUT 3 FEET TALL WITH BIG EYES AND NO HAIR AND AN OBLONG HEAD. THEY HAD TEETH YOU COULD SEE WHEN THEY TALKED. MEANWHILE THE TALL ALIEN HAD A WRAPPED CRYSTAL THAT MAY HAVE HAD SOMETHING TO DO WITH THE REPAIRS OF THE CRAFT.
THE HUMANS JUST MILLED AROUND AND AT ONE POINT ONE OF THE HUMANS HIT ONE OF THE SMALL ALIENS WITH A BATON. THE CRAFT HAD A RED LIGHT ON ONE SIDE AND A BLUE LIGHT ON THE OTHER SIDE. WE HAD FELT A PRESSURE COMING FROM THE CRAFT, LIKE MAGNETS PUSHING AGAINST EACH OTHER. WHEN THE CRAFT LEFT IT MADE A QUARTER TURN AND ROSE UP ANOTHER 35' TO CLEAR SOME TREES WE FELT THE PRESSURE INCREASE. (WITNESS DID NOT MENTION ANYTHING ABOUT THE CREW GETTING BACK ON THE CRAFT) IT WENT EAST BLOWING THE LEAVES OF THE TREES AS IT PASSED AND TO THE WEST AND THEN EAST. A FEW MINUTES LATER IT FLEW BACK GOING NORTH TO SOUTH.
TWO WEEKS LATER THE DOG FACED BIG FOOT CREATURE AND SOME OF THE SOLDIERS BROKE INTO OUR HOME AND ABDUCTED MY BROTHER AND ME. WE WERE PARALYZED AND COULD ONLY SEE, THE COVERED OUR MOUTHS AND WE COULDN'T HEAR. THE REST OF THE FAMILY WAS NOT DISTURBED THE SAME CRAFT WAS IN THE BACKYARD AND WE WERE TAKEN INSIDE OF IT. OUR CLOTHES WERE REMOVED AND WE WERE PUT ON TABLES. I WAS PROBED, TUBES WERE PUT IN MY MOUTH AND NOSE AND I FELT LIKE I WAS BEING SMOTHERED. I SAW A BRIGHT WHITE LIGHT AND THEN THEY PUT A NEEDLE INTO MY EYE TWICE..THIS MEMORY IS VERY HARD TO TALK ABOUT. THE BIG ALIENS WERE TRYING TO BRIEF ME ABOUT OUR TWIN EARTH AND GIVE ME COORDINATES. IT SEEMED LIKE THE HUMANS WERE IN CHARGE. I GUESS THEY ERASED OUR MEMORIES AND WE WOKE UP IN OUR OWN BEDS. I DID NOT REMEMBER THIS FOR MANY YEARS AND THEN THE MEMORIES CAME BACK.
