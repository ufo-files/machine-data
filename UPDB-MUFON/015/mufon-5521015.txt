Solid, bright pale lights. No blinks, no sound

I was driving home from school listening to the radio when I noticed an unusually bright and slow, low flying object coming toward my direction with a rough altitude approx. 500 ft AGL. The closer the object got to my car, I noticed a disruption in my radio station in an area with no past radio interference occurring. I pulled over to see it for as long as possible. When I exited my vehicle I noticed that the object has no sounds reverberating from it. The path of the object laid just NW of a prominent airspace traffic area of which I witnessed a commercial airliner flying in during the observation. After watching the object for several minutes, it slowly disappeared behind trees going toward (what I saw) was Beale AFB.
