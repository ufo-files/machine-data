Hi...

I took these pics before the New year. I don't remember if it was the 28,29 or 30 of December 2012. It was a clear night. Approx. 9:30-10:30pm. No wind or cloud cover.

I noticed a very obvious light in the southwest sky. It was incredibly apparent, yet I'd never noticed / seen it before. 

It seemed to have 3 different colours, red/blue/white which seemed to intermittently blink on and off. I did not immediately think it was a UFO but found it intriguing so I hopped in my car and drove to the beach where there is less light interference to have a closer look.

It started off as a weird bright light abot 45-501 in the sky... It would get very bright, then dim to nothing more than a pin-prick in the sky and then get bright again.... It wasn't moving from side to side... or up and down. Then it split into 3 lights . I kept taking pics and then a shape emerged on my camera...didn't really notice it until I looked at the pic at home and enlarged it a bunch of times....

I was showing a colleague this morning and he asked "What happens if you turn the pic upside down?" Well, I'm not sure about the 3 lights, but the "metallic" object when turned upside down looks like a "craft" of some sort to me! It actually almost has a "stealth jet" like appearance..I don't believe that it IS a stealth jet...but maybe something other worldly that resembles that shape.
