{nd} Red object with white orb appeared in photograph

I was walking through the parking lot at work going to my car for break. The moon was full and there was a large chem trail that was lit up by the moon. I thought it would make a good picture so i took out my cell phone and snapped a shot. The pic came out really dark so I thought nothing more of it. Last week i was going through my phone and saw the pic again. I figured i would brighten it up hoping that i would be able to see the pic better. When i brightened it a red object with some sort of glowing orb underneath it showed up. At the time i took the pic I didnt see anything. I cant figure out what it is. But when i go back to the dark pic and zoom in i can see the orb faintly. It appears to have been really large because it seems to be partially behind the chem trail left by a jet. There is an electrical pole in the pic. I thought maybe it might be a light of some sort extended up from the pole, but when i looked again during the day there is nothing there so whatever i picked up in the pic was up in the sky. I've never seen anything like it before. If you have an idea of what it could be please share. I'm very curious about it. I have seen unidentified objects twice before years ago and it didnt look like this. I used an app called PicsArt to brighten the photo. I thought that maybe the brighten and contrast part of the app may have left something in the pic but it doesnt do that with any other pics i have taken. Thank you for your time.
