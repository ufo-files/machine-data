orange lumious ball

The witness was out on the front steps of his residence at 16 Beaver Street in San Francisco, California at 6:55 PM on February 23, 2007. He was out specifically to see if he could observe a UFO. He spotted a glowing orange ball travelling from south to north. Just above the ball was a 747 airliner that was travelling in the opposite direction. The ball appeared to be moving faster than the 747. The sighting lasted approximately 5 seconds, ending with the object entering or traveling behind a cloud.
