Triangular craft, silent, heading south through valley, as if atemting test maneuvers

I saw the craft from my balcony, was confirmed by my cousin who wishes to remain anonymous. We said that's a ufo, It was at the altitude you would see a helicopter. It appeared to have 5 or 7 lights. It appeared to be performing a roll maneuver, maybe 20 degrees clockwise, counter and back. Completely silent. It was black, but I could detect a slight outline against the night sky. Could not say it's flight speed. Was coasting, came from central vernon (my view) and head south through the okanagan valley. From down town vernon to lake kalamalka was about 10 seconds. After it flew out of sight.
