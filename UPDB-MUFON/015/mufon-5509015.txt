{cak} bright star-like object stop then jumped,stopped,jumped and changing directions many times

Aug. 3, 2013-5:10 a.m. Dundee, Michigan a light as bright as Venus moving in an erratic manner (stationary then appeared to jump). the direction changed from south east to south west then back to south east. it looked as to be making a U turn then made a 180 degree turn back to south east. this went on for approx. 2 minutes. we first thought it was a satellite until the direction changed and jumped. it was approx. 10 oclock in the sky when we first saw it and 2 oclock when it went over the building and out of sight. there was no blinking lights. i have no idea what it was or the altitude of this object. we are not far from Detroit metro and we see many planes in the sky, this was not a plane. this thing was a lot higher in the sky than most of the planes we see. i do not understand the jumping or changing directions. any ideas what this might have been?
