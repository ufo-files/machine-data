{dr}encountered UFO sighting while talking with a friend that was on her back porch (2nd story bldg)m

it was on 19 Jun 91 around 10:50 when I was asked by a friend of mine to borrow a stick of butter. I left my house immediately afterwards to meet her at her back porch, which was only a house away from hers, to deliver the stick of butter. Once there, I tossed the stick of butter up to her and began a conversation. As I stood below her porch on the sidewalk talking to her, a neighbors dog (Bentley) began to bark....I tried to hush the dog, but he just kept barking.......I couldn't tell if he was barking at me for attention, or if it was something else. All of a sudden, it got eerily quiet, and I noticed that the dog was no longer barking....and had gone under the neighbor's porch....but I could see he was peering out from the darkness under the porch....and that's when BAM....the brightest light I've ever seen just suddenly appeared directly above us! I asked my girlfriend if she could tell what it was, because she was so much closer to it than I, but she stated the light was too bright to look at....but we both continued to try and figure out what it was. But then I noticed that the leaves on the tree, directly under the light, weren't moving.....and there was no sound coming from the object...it was a stillness I've never witnessed before......not in "THAT" area which I lived at the time, the street was usually littered with crackheads and dope boys, but not on this night.....no one, I mean absolutely NO ONE was around....and it spooked me so I ran.........and didn't stop until I got in my house!! My friend and I never spoke of this again because we didn't think anyone would believe our story. But I did tell my husband 10 years later....his response was...."I see them all the time out on SR 40 going to work (towards Columbus, OH), their always over hovering above the cornfields and empty fields!
