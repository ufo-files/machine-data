{rjl} 10 satellites intersecting single point.

Native Americans from Bay Mills Reservation in Upoer Peninsula went night fishing in Lake Houron off Cheboygon county Michigan. I went along for the ride.

While they set the nets, I stretched of on the hood of the truck and watched satellites go by. I observed 10 satellites over approximately a 20 minute period cross at at a single intersecting point directly above me. They were in orbit. All were travelling a different path, as though they began their orbit from that intersecting point on separate orbits, in different directions. 

The odd thing is. The Nation Enquirer had a story a week or two later (my mother liked the newspaper) about a UFO exploding in orbit and leaving 10 pieces of the spacecraft orbiting the earth.

Strange, but true.
