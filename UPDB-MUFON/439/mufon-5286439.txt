Orb in sky for minutes then flashed bright light

My sister and I took a blanket to the park by my house and laid on a basketball court to watch the sky and talk. As we were talking and laughing we saw a circular white orb far up in the sky moving in an odd manner. Not a satellite Bc direction changed in weird angles. It kept getting dimmer. It blinked out and my sister yelled come back and reached her hand out to the sky. At the exact moment, exactly where it disappeared a bright white flash appeared with a straight white line going up. It seemed like it heard her and was saying goodbye! We saw multiple orbs but this was the strangest due to the flash of light.
