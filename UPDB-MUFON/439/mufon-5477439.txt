{rjl} It appeared to rise above the treeline...then moved horizontally...then up rapidly

I was taking a photo of the brilliant blue sky...it was so warm outside. I caught movement that was bright out of the corner of my eye. It was round with what appeared to be a blueish aura. Its movement was unusual...up from the trees, then it moved quickly south and horizontal. It stopped for a minute then rapidly ascended out of sight. I didn't hear anything. It was really beautiful to watch, but I have no idea what I saw. I took several photos though they don't really capture what I saw...it is only a cell phone camera.
