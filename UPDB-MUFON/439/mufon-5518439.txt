came out of t-storm in south and flew overhead looked like fireball but never came down

I was at home in my driveway when I noticed a glowing orange object come out of a thunderstorm cloud that was south of my position. Object kept flying toward me, went over my head and continued heading off to the north. I took out my phone and snapped off pictures of it as it was coming toward me, overhead and moving away. It made no noise and left no smoking trail even though it looked like a fireball. I watched the object till it was no longer visible to me. The object NEVER changed altitude and continued in a straight path to the north. I was fascinated when I looked at the pics though. When I enlarge the pictures on my phone the fireball isnt a fireball it is a glowing orange line that changes shape. If I tilt the phone a little I can make out the outline of the cylinder from the heat source. The glowing line is inside of the cylinder. But that ONLY shows up on my phone, it wont transfer to the computer. My opinion is it was a cloaked object that was struck by lightning. I still have the original pics on my phone if needed.
