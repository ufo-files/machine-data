{cak} Saw a ufo hovering above the powerlines next to my house.

My husband, daughter and I drove up to our house, saw nothing. After we got out of the car we both looked up at a light that was above the power lines. It seemed like a helicopter light shining on us but there was no sound. After a couple seconds of us staring in silence, the light took off away from the ground at lightning fast speed, moving up and out of the atmosphere at a slight angled trajectory till the light seemed so small that we couldn't see it anymore. After talking about it my husband and I both found that after getting out of the car we both heard a voice in our heads that told us to look up. 

Also notable- the day after this event we saw *2* black military helicopters fly over us, low to the ground, while driving home. My dad had been working in the lot across the street and told us he saw *3* helicopters fly low to the ground over our house, minutes prior to us seeing it.
