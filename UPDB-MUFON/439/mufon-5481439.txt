I was standing outside waiting for my dog when I noticed the first object. It appeared out of nowhere to the NE, below 2 faint Stars. It appeared very bright then appeared to travel away from me drifting back and forth as it went. After about 15 seconds it faded away. Almost immediately after that another one appeared in the exact same spot and did the same thing. Then the third one appeared just below and north of the first two. I think this was a satellite as it traveled directly south across the sky. No turns or drifting back and forth. Much more faint than the 1st two. Although it didn't fade in and out as satellites often do. I watched it for about 4 mins until I could nolonger see it. When I saw the 1st one I called to two people inside to view this. They did not come out in time to see the 1st two but did see the third.
