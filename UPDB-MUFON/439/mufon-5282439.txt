A fixed circular object like a star

I saw a light that was not moving, and it looked like it could be a star, but it seemed to me in the Earths atmosphere. It flickered, I had seen various colors as it flickered, but mostly bright white. There was no sound, and the object was not moving…I have no clue what it could have been. It was about 0540 in the morning, I had just came home from work.
