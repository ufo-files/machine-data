My daughter and I were out on a pleasure drive. We had set out to see if we could find any paranormal areas near where we live. When we arrived in Antigo, we decided it was time to turn back after a rest stop. On our way home, I spotted three areas in the clouds that did not appear to be clouds. I pulled over to the side of the road to observe. When more of these areas started appearing, I told my daughter to get out the camera. We sat by the roadside for several minutes watching. During this duration, we also observed several of these objects float back up into the clouds. We had to move on before the event was over as we were becoming chilled and needed to run the car for heat. It is interesting to note that I had requested a picture of an alien and his craft prior to leaving on this trip.
