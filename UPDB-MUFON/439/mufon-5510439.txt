{cak} 5 lights hovered over lake

We were driving home from dinner when we noticed 5 reddish lights over Rice Lake that seemed to be in a formation. They were hovering in a stationary position. We tried to get off on the next exit in time to get a picture of the lights but the went from 5 to 3 as we were exiting. By the time we got off the exit and were able to pull over there was one light remaining which proceeded to disappear before we could get a picture. My wife and two children were in the car and saw the lights as well. As the last light went out the street lights turned off. Then as we drove up a block and turned down our street we noticed the power had gone out in a 4-5 block radius. Not sure if the two are related but it was strange. (4 witnesses)
