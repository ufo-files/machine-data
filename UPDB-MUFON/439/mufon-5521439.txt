Black triangle shape with two red and 1 blue light, non-reflective surface

Approximately 9:30p.m. traveling East bound Hwy 40. I noticed a pulsating red light that I thought was an airplane. Moving south to north. I did no more than blink. The object had moved straight backward along what seemed to be a straight line. This object was directly forward of my line of sight while looking out the windshield first. After it moved, I had to turn my head where my chin was over my left clavicle. The object hovered for a split second. It then moved toward me covering what would seem a great distance in a matter of seconds. It banked toward the hwy crossing the river. Approaching me, I hunched over the steering wheel and slowed down because it was moving fast. I saw a black triangular shaped craft. It was this shape that the red and blue lights made. It was non-reflective to any external lighting from the highway lights. The lights did not shine but were of a softy glow. I got such a adrenalin rush to what I was seeing that tears came to my eyes. I had to tell someone. I looked around and there were no other cars or trucks around me or coming from the other direction. I almost was as if this thing knew it. And was not afraid to show itself to me. I lost track of it because it flew directly over my vehicle.
