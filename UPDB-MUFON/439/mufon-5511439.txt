{nd} three bright orbs or sheres in distant sky above mtn. ridge all flying eradically with abrupt changes of directions and extremly fast speeds . the observation lasted for approximately one hour maybe a little longer.

My girlfriend and I were at home. It was late evening. My girlfriend called to me to come outside and look up at three object flying erratically above the mountain ridge about one-half mile away.It was a very clear night and we could see the objects moving in what seemed to be erratic movements that was between a standstill to quick back and forth motions all at the same time without colliding with each other.At times it looked as if were playing with one another or practicing maneuvers.But it went on for a long time, so long we eat hed then returned back inside. Went back out moments later to witness the same actions. This happened a long time back but I still remember the aware experienced, experienced the feeling of witnessing something truly spectacular and a event that I would never be able to explain or forget. They eventually left our sight one at a time and never returned.
