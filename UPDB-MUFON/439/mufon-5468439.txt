{rjl} Star-like, then briefly lit up in expanded orngish glow when accelerated.

Approx. 3:30'ish AM. Couldn't sleep and went outside to stargaze. Noted a whitish star-like object slowly moving directly above me moving NE 130 degrees. Thought at first it was a satellite. Suddenly lit up as if I was looking at the brightness of Mars and took off at phenomenal speed in same direction NE 130 degrees. No sooner had it accelerated it dimmed. It went from zero degrees above me to about 90 degrees in front of me. There, it stopped briefly and hovered, when suddenly it briefly lit up again and took off SW 200 degrees. Followed it until it disappeared from eyesight.

About 30 minutes later saw a satellite moving W to E. Nothing unusual. Then about 10 minutes later, saw another W to E about 10-15 degrees to right from first satellite. 

My experience? Well, that was odd. I'm confident this was no fighter jet. It moved too fast, then briefly hovered and changed directions pinpoint about 70 degrees from original direction.
