{rjl} 2 Bright Lights

I'm not sure how my mom, sister and I knew to go to the dam (spillway @ Coro Lake) but there were several people from our lake and several people from Robco Lake, the lake below. Everyone was pointing up to the 2 bright lights hovering for quite a while apparently. We all talked freely about them being UFOs. Someone said they called police. A few minutes later I looked over my left shoulder because I heard helicopters. 2 police helicopter with search lights pointing toward bright lights were coming across lake. I followed their beams back toward the bright lights and as I made visual with the lights again they shot off. Everyone let out a loud groan because the helicopters scared them off. 
I spoke to a friend a couple of years ago who said his brother was there and saw it as well.
I have intended to follow up with him to verify and have him report here as well, but never have.
Its amazing I'm reporting here right now because I have not been inclined to to, why is that?? I dont remember people thinking it was a big deal back then or talking about it. I have told the story a few times over the years and have really wanted to report here, but never inclined to do it! 
I have a another sighting in area I am also reporting today, and a reporting shared to me by mothers husband that lived in this area. I also found out that the police helicopter pilot reported it and there was an article about it in the Commercial Appeal.
My mother has passed so I cannot verify with her but she and I did discuss all of these occurances several times over the years.
I dont know how to complete Elevation, it was high in the sky as if you were looking at stars.
