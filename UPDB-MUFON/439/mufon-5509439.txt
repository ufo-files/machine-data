(GS) A object was falling down to earth

A friend and myself were lying down in the grass talking. We both were looking up at the sky when all of a sudden a bright object was comeing down very very fast. We thought it was a falling star but all of a sudden it stopped. It stayed stationary for awhile and then it went straight up very fast and disappeared. All we said to each other was "did you see what i saw?"
