{nd} I seen it take from lake direction(south)! Lake tillery

Watching fire works over the lake! Looking in that direction! Did not know, still don't!                 Orange orb Rose from the lake (south) and proceeded north out of site! This was on the 3rd of July! On July 4th two orange orbs repeated the same as night before!
