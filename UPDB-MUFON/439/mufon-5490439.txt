{cak} More than one event. Happened 4 times over 3 years.

First time was a light first seen by my friend. We where looking through the scope of his rifle at the city from a mountain top using it like binoculars. He noticed a light come up out of the trees and hover just above them on a neighboring mountain top. He told me to look at it and pointed it out. About that time a airliner flew over and the light went back down into the trees. After the plane left the light went back up and started moving towards us. We where looking at it through the rifle scope taking turns and I can only say it looked like a bright light with a tail. We got in my truck and started to speed down the logging road away from it but it just followed us at tree top level keeping pace with us. We stopped and my idiot friend shot at it and at that time it just took off at an angle fading out looking like a star and eventually disappearing. It only took it about 2 seconds to fade out like a star and disappear. To much to say about the other times but they where just as strange. I have never been more scared in my life. My friend and I don't talk anymore because of it. It bothered him that much. Please contact me by phone. As there is more of this kind of thing on t.v. I am finally wanting to talk about the 4 times this happened to me over 3 years.
