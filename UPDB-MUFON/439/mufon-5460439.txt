{tjd} On a very breezy afternoon, a single STATIONARY dark grey cloud hanging low over the water emitting a bright light at regular intervals.

From Long Island N.Y. we were approaching the Whitestone Bridge following a highway with a large stretch of open water on our right. My husband pulled into a waterside park with a long bicycle/walking path running next to the water. He went for a walk while I sat on a bench and watched our children at play. There was quite a breeze and a clear blue sky, and innumerable small fluffy white clouds, one after another in a steady stream, moving quickly S/W low over the water....all bright white in color...except one which was a dark dense grey color and which suddenly stopped directly across the water from where I was sitting. All the other clouds kept moving at a steady pace, but this particular grey cloud remained in place, which was very odd - so I kept my eyes on it- I watched it for over 20 minutes - and at regular intervals I saw a bright light flash from inside the cloud. I think - whatever it was, it was communicating with something deep beneath the surface of the water, and because it was broad daylight it chose to remain concealed within that cloud rather than expose itself. My husband returned, dismissed my story as nonsense and we all returned to our car and continued to our destination, my in-law's home in the Bronx. I don't actually know what it was that I observed, but I know that I will never forget it.
