A white orbish light turned blue then broke into multible orange lights with tails and traversed the sky, about 9 or maybe ten

I was having a drink in my local GA club, I was on my second pint of beer and went outside to smoke a cigarette. I looked to the NE and saw what I percieved to be an airplane, but then thought to myself it was in a weird flight path. I generally know how planes come and go here. It looked like a bright white orb. It gathered my curiosty somewhat when just before it entered into the clouds the centre of it turned blue. Then within that cloud I saw about nine white orbs. As they left the clouds and went into clear sky they suddenly turned orange and seemed to speed up a little. As they flew onward they did leave trails, kind of like sparks. At times I also seen other colours, blues, red and yellow. They never changed direction but did gather speed. They certainly flew faster than any aircraft I know and yet when I had first spotted the white orb it seemed to be rising slowly into the sky. I did get another witness but he only seen the last part of this episode and yet was amazed at the little he saw. This is the most incredible thing I have ever seen and yet it left me feeling numb instead of excited. I would appreciate other witnesses comming forth.
