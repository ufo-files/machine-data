Dark triangle travelling from North to South. Low noise steady path at about twice the speed of normal plane.3 dim lights at corners.

Awpke from sleeping about 1:30am. went downstairs to get a drink of water. sometimes i will look out the window at the stars. Around 2:10am I noticed a dim set of unblinking/unchanging lights coming from the North and heading due south. As the craft approached I noticed it was triangular in shape and heard a low humming noise. It was travelling about twice the speed of an airplane at a low altitude.  Definitely not a plane or shooting star. Noticeable shape and dark grey/black in colour. The craft passed out of view from the window and was gone.
