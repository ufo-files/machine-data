{dr}Orange globes moving fast across night sky. No sounds heard.

My wife was letting a neighbors dog out because they were on vacation. She came in our house at 22:00 and told me she had seen 2 different pairs of globes flying around the sky. I immediately went in my front yard and observed 2 orange globes traveling north to south across the sky. They made no noise and appeared to me to be at a very high altitude. I have lived in my home over 12 years and I'm always looking up at the night sky and I have never seen anything airplane or otherwise that looked like this. I did not see the other pair of globes my wife had mentioned seeing at all. I took a video with my iphone 5 and it is attached. I realize also it is the 4th of July and there are many fireworks going on in the area but these were absolutely no firework.
