{tjd} Looked like a close star.

The "Star" did not move for a few minutes when I first saw it, then it was moving in a straight line for a short distance, north, then back to center, then south in a straight line for a short distance, then back to center, then it did the same for east and west. At first I couldn't believe what I was seeing so I called my daughter to step out of the house. She saw it move and ran back in the house. I called my neighbor and his wife and they saw the same thing. I didn't list them because I don't know all their info. It moved across the sky very slowly, and kept moving up down side by side. I don't know what I saw, it was too far away. I tried to record it with my phone, but, nothing appeared on the video. After about an hour I went back inside my house. I emailed NASA, but, all they sent by email was about the NASA website and and joining as a member to get access to info on various topics of planets and black holes. They never addressed my questions.
