INCIDENT #2
As described by Art Stallings

The one I saw personally was also low altitude and large, roughly 200 feet in length, in the fall of 1968 or 1969. This craft was cigar shaped, rounded at each end, and silver-grey metallic. No markings or windows

Clear daylight sighting. Wasn't a weather balloon or swamp gas. 

Some friends, my brother and I were playing football when I happened to look up and saw this thing moving through the sky very quickly, 150 to 200 mph. I tried to get my brother and friends to look but they were scrambling for the football. By the time I got their attention it had gone.

There must have been other people along its path that must have seen it, but I never tried to track anyone down and ask.

How fast was this craft moving and what sound did it make, if any?

It was moving maybe somewhere around 150-200 mph. It was silent.
Only saw it a few seconds, before it disappeared over the trees
