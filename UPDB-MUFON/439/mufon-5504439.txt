{nd} Wife observed craft hovering behind the local Wendyy's, had dark thick body with triangular wings. Had bright lights shinning. Craft hovered in same place.

On 4 March 2014,at 18:15, my wife observed, to the east from Wendy's, a dark craft with triangular wings hovering in the distance. The craft was cigar shaped with triangular wings. The color was dark with metalic or dull silver wings. The craft had two lights, like landing lights, shinning, but was not a airplane. It hovered the whole time she observed it. She also saw other people in their cars pointing and looking at the craft. Do to the icy conditions, she did not pull over and take pictures.
