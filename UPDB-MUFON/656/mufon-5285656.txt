Multiple hovering lights

Looking due west from our location, there was 1 fairly large craft with bright red and white flashing lights. The lights dimmed then we saw 2 more crafts with flashing red and white lights. All were hovering and moving slowly toward the south. One of the crafts flew due east and we didn't see it again. Another craft flew due north for about a mile and proceeded to turn going straight up. I followed it until it disappeared out of the atmosphere. By that time, the 3rd craft was gone. No video or pictures.
