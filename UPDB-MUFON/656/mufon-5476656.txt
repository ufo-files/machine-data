{nd} 7 small red orbs of light moved from NNE to SSW at a moderate rate of speed.

I was standing in the parking lot outside where I work on break. Me another fellow employee observed a total of 7 red orbs cross the sky from NNE to SSW moving at a moderate rate of speed. Altitude unknown, however, as the objects moved further south they disappeared into the clouds. First there were 2 red orbs observed, as they disappeared into the clouds, 3 more orbs appeared coming from and going to the same direction, and as they disappeared, 2 final orbs appeared coming from and going to the same direction. No noise, NOT helicopters or planes.... These were singular red orbs... ALL of them exactly the same color. I cannot imagine that 7 drones were being controlled by someone and that they all look alike.
