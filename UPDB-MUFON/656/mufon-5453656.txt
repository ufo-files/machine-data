Approx 30 in a line in the sky

At approximately 9.40pm we looked up into the sky and saw what we thought were aeroplanes until we counted 30, all travelling at an equal distance, in a straight line then arching off to the left. No noise was heard. They appeared from the South West of Huddersfield and travelled to the East. This went on for approximately 10 minutes.
