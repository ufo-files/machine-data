{dr}Unidentified object hovering above the freeway

I was driving home after hanging out with some friends at about 10:30pm. I had just turned onto 315 N from Bethal Rd in Columbus Oh, when I noticed what appeared to be a bar of lights in the sky above the road. At first I thought it was some kind of airplane. I kept looking at it and it did not move like an airplane would. It just kind of hovered in the sky above the road. It was quite large and I tried to notice if there was more to it then the lights. I grabbed my phone and thought about trying to take a picture but then remembered that I was driving and decided against a photo attempt. Then I thought about pulling off the road to get a better look. Then I thought, what if I get abducted? As I passed under the object I continued to look in my mirrors to get a second look but could see nothing in my rear view or side view mirrors. I got home and felt excited at what I saw and dissappointed that I did not investigate further. I told my wife about my experience and she kind of laughed at me. What I saw was strange, unlike any aircraft that I have seen before. It did not move. I have traveled that same road at night hundreds of times and I have never seen anything like what I saw tonight.
