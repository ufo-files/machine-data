We were driving from north of Marseille towards the city center, on the highway A55, at ~18 or 20 (6 or 8 pm). 
I was looking at the lights, first thinking : "how wierd, usually we can't see any stars at this hour of the day" but I realised they were much too big / bright / close to be stars. Two moons ?? nope, the moon is on the other side. Planes maybe ? much to bright. And why aren't they moving ? planes are supposed to move...! I showed them to my husband, making fun "hey look there are spaceships over the sea !" But after laughing, we noticed that they really showed no mouvement, and they kept so constantly extremly bright. 
Two very bright white "lights" / round vessels were standing on stationnary flight over the sea (1km away from Marseille harbour - 200 m from each other - maybe 1 km high up the sky). They could be seen also from the 8th arrondissement (a friend in south Marseille told us on the phone),
Extremely bright, completely stationnary. Not at all like the many planes passing on this part of the sea shore. Just two very very bright "lights", huge "light-vessels". We were glad to see them, even if they did nothing else than just standing there and being there. We went trough a tunnel, and in the center of the city with all the high buildings we lost the sight of them.
