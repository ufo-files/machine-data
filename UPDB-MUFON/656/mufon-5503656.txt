{nd} Reflective type of material craft. Strange shape.

We were having breakfast in our backyard & we saw a craft shoot down at an angle from the north. It was making turns in the air. The craft had reflective type of material. I grabbed my camera & took pictures. The craft had turned & showed an octagon side with big circle lights & they flashed 3 times. It was in the air traveling really slow in the same spot for 15 mins then traveled north.
