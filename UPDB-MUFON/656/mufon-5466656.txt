I took my dog outside in my front yard around 9:10 pm August 10th and looked up above me to see an Unidentified Object with muted red and blue large rectangle lights. It made no sound, flew slowly over me headed east, then made a series of circles,then flew west before ascending straight up until I could no longer see the rectangle lights. I could not tell the shape of this object but the lights were very visible even though the colors were muted from my perspective.
