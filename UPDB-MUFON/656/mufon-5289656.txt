Boomerang 2 football fields sized

While doing my exercise in my backyard I paused to look at the sky for Orion's belt and other stars. I then look North East and see a boomerang shaped craft about the size of two football fields side by side. I thought it was too wide to be a military plane(and those make a ton of noise). I could not see a fuselage nor red and/or blue lights at the 'wing' tips. The white lights it had remained constant. The outline towards the back was dim even before going off in the distance. The craft's speed was consistant (did not speed up nor slow down) . I will attach an artist's rendition of what I saw.
