Multiple of something seen in the middle of a completely clear day.

I don’t know what to even call this. UFO? Maybe a weird weather anomaly? All I know is it was a lot of something that appeared to be clouds at first. Except there were zero clouds in the sky on this day. My son and I were laying on the hammock doing some homeschooling and watching the planes fly by. Something caught my eye so I started recording. It looked like little orbs of energy floating along but once I zoomed in I could clearly see different directions and speeds of them. They would also just disappear from view. I’ve asked a lot of really smart people what they think (even some pilots) with no good answers. Everyone says the same thing “weird”. We have exhausted all other ideas, bugs, birds, ball lightening, geomagnetic storm...Im still at a loss and more curious than ever.
