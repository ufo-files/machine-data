It was a miday hike in the trinity alps on bake oven ridge.I was on the south side of the ridge looking to the valley.we were taking a short break.all of a sudden I herd a strong rumble,what sounded like an enormous aircraft in a distance.I instantly wanted to see what we were hearing, then I ran as fast as I could up hill to get a full view of the valley, getting to a good rock perch,it was then we saw"why it is I am righting " an unidentified flying object.In clear view my brother and I both starred at what I thought was a nuclear missle. When we saw it appear I instantly sized it up.the ojects shape and collor was like a silver bullet.This objects size was enormous, in estament it was three to four school busses round, and estament three school busses long.From my view,in distance it was 2 to three hundred yards away.we had a great view of it. The object had the loudest barrel rumble,as if it had g-force behind it,but to our amazement it was going no faster than 20 miles an hour, so from our view we witnessed the flight of the object for about 20-30 seconds.this obect was flying no more than 100 feet obove the valley trees,so it was flying in the valley in which mountain ridges hid it from any other view.The entire ufo looked like pallised steel,held together in sections by pallished rivots.the ufo had no windows for view, on any sides.The ufo had no propellers, no jets, and was not being hoisted by another craft.also to our amazement as the ufo flew over the trees, it pulled them as if it had g-force.we bothed stared in amazement until it dissapeared past another ridge, and out of our view.I turned to my brother a little worried and talked about getting out of the alps. Both my brother and I finished our hike. When I got home I told, and talked about it to a few people, no one seemed to take it for real so I have kept it to myself for the past several years.
