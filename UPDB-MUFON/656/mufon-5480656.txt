{rjl} A solid white craft flew silently over the tree tops and kept on one course

I woke up in the living room on the couch to see three entities at about 6ft 6 in tall standing and looking at me I was frozen with fear so much so I couldn't even scream for help all I could muster was a slight breathy whisper. I closed my eyes as tightly as I could and eventually could go back to sleep. The next morning I was outside feeding my uncle's cats and saw a solid perfectly white teardrop shaped object fly slowly over my house proceeding over our power lines and over the wood line finally disappearing into the distance. I gave chase on foot but had no further sightings.
