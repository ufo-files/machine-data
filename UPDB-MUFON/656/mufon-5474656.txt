5 or more rectangles of light transforming forward into a different pattern and then changed to a pulsating orb of light

It was 3:55 AM when I noticed light in the sky which was reflecting onto my blinds. There were 5 rectangles of light with other smaller rectangular lights standing upright in a formation. These lights then moved in a programmed fashion together collapsing forward into a shape that resembled the outline of half an arrows tip and reforming upright in repetition. It then became a pulsing orb of light in the sky. By the time i started recording it was changing into an orb. When i zoomed in on the video ( although the quality wasn't great )i could see it was changing from its multiple rectangular state at the start to the orb like state. It had a mist around it when it was changing. If you zoom in on the video you can see this.
