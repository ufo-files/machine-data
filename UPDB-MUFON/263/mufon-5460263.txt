{cak} Fast low flying orb

I heard my horse snorting and moving around (his pen is about 100 feet from my bedroom) at 2:00 am. I thought maybe it could be a cougar or maybe he didn’t have enough water, so I got up, put my glasses on, grabbed a flashlight and walked to the window in the dining room facing my horse’s pens. I turned my flashlight on through the window an immediately saw a white light zoom off right over the tree line. My first thought was there was no way that was a plane, and it did not look like a shooting star. Could it be a reflection off my glasses or window? I tried to recreate it but couldn’t get anything at all. My horse is very chill so if someching makes him snort it’s usually because he's seeing something he hasn’t before.
