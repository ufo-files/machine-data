Faint star moving in spline-like patterns

I was out walking my dog and while she was doing her business I was looking up at the stars as I always do. What I saw looked exactly like the other surrounding faint stars. My first thought was that it was another airplane, but there was no flashing at all, it was just like a solid star -- except that it moved in spline-like patterns (quicker than any helicopter or plane could do), no trail, and occasionally stopping/hovering or moving in a straight line. I ran inside and grabbed my DSLR (Canon 60D) to try to get pictures and video, but sadly the light was too faint to be picked up by the sensor. Eventually it went behind some clouds and I didn't see it again after that.
