{nd} Saw silver/gray angular object (asymmetrical) move behind a mountain between 2 peaks.

I was at the Java Love coffee shop this past Saturday morning around 10am. I walked outside and sat in one of the seats. Across the street you can see a red rock mountain. The mountain has 2 peaks. I looked up and saw an object that is difficult to describe. It was silver/light gray and angular. It was very large but asymmetrical. It seemed to have a triangular shape but oddly placed angular components to it. The object was partially behind the mountain so I could not see the entire shape, only the top of it. I can best describe it as a sort of triangle with another triangular crosspiece and panels or rectangular shapes attached to it. It appeared solid. The object was spotted between the two peaks, and moved left to right and disappeared behind the second peak. I did not see it initially fly behind the mountain, or exit on the other side. It just appeared in the center of the two peaks, and moved behind the second peak where it was no longer visible. The sighting lasted a matter of seconds but I saw it dead on, on a clear sunny day.
