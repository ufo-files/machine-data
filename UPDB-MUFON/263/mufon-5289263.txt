My grandmother & I observed as group of 5 gray/silver spheres in the pattern of a 5 side of a dice.

It was approximately May of 1982 because it was warmer like spring when my grandmother,57 at the time,& I,6 at the time were walking out of her back door,east in direction,into her backyard. She & then I observed towards north & our left were 5 spherical grayish/silver that were coming from the north & heading south over the backyards of all the houses towards us the only people out at the time. They weren't traveling at any extraordinary speed & if anything is say they were rather slow. They were oriented exactly like a 5 on a dice never deviating from their pattern & although they could have reflected sunlight they didn't which was likely due to the time of day & the elevation of the objects. When they appeared to notice us they slowed down & appeared 2 slow as if they were going to hover over us but at that moment my grandmother became scared for my safety as we never saw anything like the objects with their capabilities & being that of orb spheres. I should note that they made absolutely no apparent sound. After going inside we looked out if the kitchen window & could no longer see the objects so we then went back outside & they had gone. Another thing you should know is that they came from the direction of Lambert airport in st.louis, Missouri but in no way were they anything like the aircraft technology of that day or even today. I have never observed anything personally like it after that day. I was a believer prior to that but this cemented my belief in ufo's. Thank you for your time
