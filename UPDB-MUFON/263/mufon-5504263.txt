{cak} Bright Flash of white light. Star like white dot of light moved slowly then faster till it moved very fast and was gone.

My friend and I were talking in his driveway when we both seen a bright flash of white light in the sky. It was like a flash bulb going off. It was a very high flash about the size of a penny held at arms length. After the flash a light what looked like a bright star was visible. The light started moving to the south slowly and then moved a little faster. It moved about the distance of 2-3 inches at arms length in 4-5 seconds. The light then looked like it jumped to warp speed and was gone. The total time of the event was around 10 seconds. We herd no sound. Me and my friend both said did you see that! We both had seen the same thing. I have no clue what we had seen.
