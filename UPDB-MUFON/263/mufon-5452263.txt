I saw a large black triangle with three white lights in each corner and small red light in center.

I had to report this and should have a long time ago. I did not have my phone with me so I can only give you my word what I saw was very real. It only lasted a few minutes and I was afraid to move. It was a large black triangle flat black in color with three big white lights near each corner and one small red light in the center. It did not zip off in great speed it just vanished in a blink of a eye. It did cause the air (space) it was just in to seem to be out of focus for about 3-5 seconds after it was gone. This happened in July of 2015 around 1 pm a perfect clear blue sky. At the time I was living in Altamonte Springs in Country Creek Estates.
