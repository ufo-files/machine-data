Fleet of crafts in perfect line moving and disappearing

My husband and I were sitting on a fishing dock and looked south and saw approximately 30 crafts (small, resembling white stars, round) coming out of nothing (cloaked) and moving in a perfect line south. The best way I could describe it is to imagine a row of Christmas lights being pulled across the sky. As they reached a certain part of the sky (south) they would disappear one after another. It was amazing to see! My husband and I stood there watching every single one disappear. When they all seemed to be gone, we noticed approximately 2-3 others coming out of the cloaked area of the sky strangling behind. They moved in the same trajectory and also disappeared at the same exact point in the sky that the others disappeared at.
