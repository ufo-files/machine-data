{jpg} Two different amber colored lights

I was out on my deck, star gazing and observing the near full moon. I noticed, what I thought was, a satellite heading N.E., amber in color, moving in straight line. I continued to watch, waiting to see the light diminish as it flew away from the westerly sunset direction. It continued to stay bright. I thought I would lose my visual track on it as it approached a layers of clouds, but it did not; it was below clouds. I then realized that it was not a satellite. There was no running lights, just one amber light. There was no sound that I could detect. 15 minutes later, I noticed another satellite flying over head, amber in color, moving in a E.S.E. direction. No sound, and no running lights just the amber colored light. It continued on a straight path. I was sure it was a satellite, but then it turned and headed south. I am familiar with things in the sky, satellites, planes, military jets, sky lanterns etc. and both of these sightings were curious.
