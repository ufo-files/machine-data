2 small very bright lights

driving along trehannick road in st.teath, bodmin, cornwall at 6.53 pm ( i checked the time as soon as the event happened ) heading west, i saw to my right two very bright pure white balls of light moving across the fields to my right. they were almost touching each other at a height of no more than 50 feet and would have been roughly 100 feet away from me. they were fast and were silent. they passed directly in front/above of me, i thought that they were about to hit the side of the valley to my left when they vanished. the sighting was very brief, has anyone else in the area seen them? someone must have. i live about 20 miles from a military airbase  (st.mawgan )and am very used to jets practising low flying exercises. these were not aircraft. i realise that this is not the best sighting reported by a long way, but i would like to say that i have never seen anything that gave such an intense pure white colour, i can only compare it to the kind of light that you would see from a firework, which it clearly was not.
