{dr}Red orange fireball like object in the sky with no sound

I was at funland riding a Ferris wheel and all of a sudden my mom and I saw a red and orange glowing fireball type object in the sky. At first I thought that it was a Chinese lantern and it was going straight inland. I just saw it for a second so I was not worried about it. The next day at our campground which was about 10 miles away, I told my friend what I saw and he immediately said that he saw the same object that same night with about 10 other people. Immediately I said to myself, "I just saw a ufo".
