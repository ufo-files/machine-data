Two white lights traveling at same speed but at different elevations. One above the other but spaced apart.  Viewed at 8:45 pm on Aug 27, 2018 in an eastern direction in Denver. No cloud noted. I watched another few minutes to see if the lights would reappear but they did not. What interested me was that I don1t usually notice lights like that as it appeared to be two separate vehicles not a single light or tight light pattern of an airplane.
