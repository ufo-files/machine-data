{dr}Lying in bed facing the window. Star like object flys into view from the left,stops,descends quickly, stops on a dime and shoots quickly out of sight

Sometime in 2002 I was lying in bed, facing the window, trying to fall asleep. Something that looked like a star entered into view quite quickly from the left side of the window. It stopped instantly, descended just as quickly, stopped instantly for a couple of seconds then shot out of sight to the right side of the window.

I didn't know what to think. I was having a hard time processing what I just saw. The next morning, I told my husband about it and of course he thought I was crazy, but I know what I saw. Also a few nights later I was awakened by a deep, low, pulsating humming sound. Kind of like a very strong electrical current. Because I was half asleep, I didn't get out of bed. The room wasn't lit up or anything like that. I ended up falling back to sleep and recalled what I had heard the next morning.
