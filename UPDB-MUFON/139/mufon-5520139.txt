Bright orange lights. Up to five seen at once. Glowed brightly, dimmed out. Then relit.

We saw a steady orange light, similar to sodium vapor lamps. The light remained very bright for a few minutes then dimmed to darkness. After a few minutes it relit. Two more pairs of lights were observed. Once, we saw all 5 lit simultaneously. We were traveling about 80 mph on Interstate 8 and the lights appeared to keep pace with us for about 40 miles. It was also visible as we passed the border patrol check station on Interstate 8 and they should have seen something too.
