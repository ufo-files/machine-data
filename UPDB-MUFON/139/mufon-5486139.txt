Moving orb of light, solid greenish/Blue colouring and when it moved quickly, ornage/Redish on opposite side of movement

I went out on my balcony to have a cigarette while talking to a friend on the phone, I observed this glowing orb moving in a way a plane or airplane could travel. It went behind ridges and in front of them. It hovered and moved, when it moved quickly you would see the colour change on the opposing side. I tried to use my phone to capture pictures but was unable to focus on the object, I did get a short video of the object before it winked out of existence. I was unable to focus on this object and attempted several videos, but my 41MP camera could not focus on it.
