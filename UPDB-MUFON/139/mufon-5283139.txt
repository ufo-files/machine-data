I observed a large triangular object hover near me.

As I was driving on my road to my home one evening, I witnessed a large triangle object hovering in the sky near my home. What caught my attention was the huge afterburners across the back end, or what looked like, the back of the craft. It turned slightly as it was hovering. The 3 afterburners, all connected, had a glowing orange color coming out of a vent like system. I watched the craft for about 7 minutes, as it just hovered and turned slightly. I then tried to drive closer to the craft to get a better look/ But it then vanished.
