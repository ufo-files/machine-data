{jpg} Looked like a satellite then grew brighter and larger in size and changed to red in color. Decreased in brightness disappearing. Reappearing again.

On December 27th around 8:15 pm. I saw a star size shaped white light moving across the night sky from the southwest to the northeast. I thought it was a satellite until it started increasing in brightness and changing to a reddish color and about the size of Venus in light intensity. Then it began to decrease in size and change back to white in color until it completely disappeared. I continued to track where I thought it would be for about 10 seconds and it then reappeared again as a small white light continuing to move in a northeast direction. This is the third time in the past two years that I have seen this same phenomenon in the night sky. I've seen many satellites, small planes and airliners flying in the night sky but nothing ever like this.
