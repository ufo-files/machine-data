{jpg} Glowing Orange Orb

I was on a Delta flight departing Atlanta, northbound to VA. We had just broken through scattered clouds and were somewhere above north Georgia. To the west, the sun was setting. I was sitting starboard side (facing east) in a window seat in the last row of first class, just ahead of the wings. I was gazing at the farms below when a brilliant orange orb passed by, flying parallel to my flight in the opposite direction at what appeared to be a very high rate of speed. It seemed to pass under the starboard wing, but it was very quick and I could not really judge how far below us it was or its actual size. It appeared to be the size of a basketball from my view. It was definitely not another airplane. (I have flown a great deal in my life and have seen aircraft flying in the opposite direction. The object was not a plane.) All passengers seated in front of me had their shades down, so I knew they'd not seen it, nor would have anyone on the opposite side of the plane. I did not ask the crew about it and regret that I didn't. If the pilots were looking forward, it seems they would have seen it.
