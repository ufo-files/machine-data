{tjd} Dusk 3 V-shaped Ships in V-formation-Very Close to each other-Disappeared

Traveling home from grocery store, still light out. Looked up and saw 3 huge ships, moving/gliding, no noise. Stopped car and jumped out as they were directly over my car. Wanted to watch where they were going. Turned around to see, all had disappeared. 

Didn't know what they were or where they went. Did not pay attention to lights, or other features as I was stunned and just taken back by what I was seeing, but the fact that they disappeared, totally, within seconds of me jumping out of the car and turning around.

This was a semi rural area, no other cars on the road at the time. Did not even consider at the time that they were UFO's. Wasn't until years later that I began wondering more so and started my own searching online about triangular ships.
