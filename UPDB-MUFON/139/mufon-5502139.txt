{dr}Triangle with white lights at corners, red light bottom center

Glanced out my South bedroom window about 9:45 PM and noticed brighter than normal, slightly low lights. We live near an airport so we see the airplane lights all the time. These struck me so I got up to look. I was shocked to see the typical triangle, dark form with 3 white lights on the corners, red flashing in the middle of the bottom. First thing I thought was look for the colored lights on the end of a planes wings. There were no colors or blinking except for the center red light. I just watched it thinking 'I can't believe I'm really seeing this'. I do believe the triangle is government something, but still. It was moving from East to West. When it was out of view at that window, I went out front to watch. There was a hum/rumbling noise fairly loud, but it was fairly low compared to planes that we see in the area. Still moving West but now seen from the back. The back 2 white lights that I could see from this angle were steady lit but flashing brighter in unison. I was wishing I hadn't already turned my phone of and could have gotten a picture. I watched a few minutes as it carried on steady West, no fast or random movements, just above treetop level (from my view point) as it got further away. Total time was several minutes.
