{cak} A line of lights moving from the nnw to the sse.

Approximately 18:55 I stepped outside and looked up and to the west. I saw a line of lights, 5 to 6, travelling in a straight line from the NNW to the SSE. There was no apparent sound. They were traveling faster than any other air traffic visible. I called my roommate out to see them as well. He was unable to see them. I continued to watch as they on in their straight path until they passed out of sight.
