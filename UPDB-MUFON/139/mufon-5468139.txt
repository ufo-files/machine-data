{jpg} Bar-shaped object noticed over moon not seen when pic was taken

Me, my boyfriend, and a friend of ours who was staying over at our place that evening decided to walk to a nearby grocery store to get some snacks. As we came out if the store, I thought the moon looked really pretty and decided to take a picture of it. I took about three pictures with my cell phine, then opened the photos I just took to see how they turned out. I then noticed in the first picture I took this bar object above the moon. I hadn't seen it before I took the picture, and it wasn't there physically when I tried to look for it, it was just there in that photo. I showed my boyfriend and friend and neither of them had witnessed it physically either, and I thought maybe it wasn't visible to the naked eye and tried to see if I could catch it again by taking some more photos and video, but it didn't show up again.
We proceeded to walk home and that was pretty much the extent of the situation. 
I don't know if this is relevant to this particular photo, but two nights later (last night) my boyfriend took some more pictures of the moon in this general area showing orbs (these were also visible to the naked eye, not just on camera), which I will submit next.
