{dr}Orange moving Object in Arvada 7/16/12

At band practice came outside after practice, we all witnessed the yellowish orange globe moving accross the sky. then it came back about 10 mins later.

When I turned on my camera and the flash came on, the object went black. You could still see it but it was black now.

It was a little above tree top level.

It faded into the sky as it moved further away.

I worked for a mojor airline and this was very strange indeed.
