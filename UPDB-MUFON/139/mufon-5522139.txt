After an attempt to Communicate with UFOs, they responded.

I was picking up my girlfriends sister from her house and I had previously read that you can contact aliens through telepathy, so I thought as hard as I could the words I want to meet you. I exited my vehicle and looked to the stars, but what I saw was much closer. Just over one of the houses, I saw a shape start to move. It was about 15-20 feet wide and 10 feet tall. It was a disc and had panels on the bottom that looked a lot like lights. I started to panic and I think they had picked up on it. The ufo was stealthed though, the outline was distinctly moving and it was close enough that I could make out very clearly what it was. The ufo disappeared completely after about 5 visible seconds. It vanished into thin air, I cant believe I blew my one shot at meeting aliens.
