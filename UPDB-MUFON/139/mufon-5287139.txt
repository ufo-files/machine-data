Ball of fire/Light

My wife and I went onto our deck around midnight, after being there a few minutes we noticed what looked like a very big ball of flame. It was quiet outside and it did not have any sound. It was very big and bright initially and faded out for a couple of seconds before moving Northeast and had what looked like blinking lights which made me think it was an airplane. It continued Ne out of view.
