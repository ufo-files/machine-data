{rjl} Saw an object on the way home with family.

I was leaving the laundry Matt with my brother and grandmother. Traveling on a golf cart on the main road in my town, landis avenue towards home. When I noticed to the north eat sky a low flying huge object flying over the wetlands. We stayed at the light on JFK boulevard and watched the object travel extremely slow.the object was dark and smooth shaped like a boomerang almost. The object we could see was traveling at a southern trajectory when it reached the boulevard then turned around heading to the north east. Again the speed of the craft was to slow to be a plane, it took the craft at least one min to completely turn around to head. It's new trajectory. The object then appeared to pick up speed as it went off and as it travelled away we spotted two helicopters following the craft. We determined the helicopters were following the craft due to the flood lights on and they travelled in formation at the same elevation of the object heading off in the same direction. That evening as we sat in the living room the local news station 40 out of Atlantic city reported the event also listing more witnesses and their trajectory of the craft.
