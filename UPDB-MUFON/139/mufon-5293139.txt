I observed the landing of an object.

I was sitting on the back deck overlooking a large valley where the town lays. We saw a square object appear approximately 500 feet over the town of Cedar Hill. It was slowly sniping on it's axis as it descended. The object appeared to be approximately 15 to 20 feet in width with a sphere in the middle of it. It was matte black and looked like it was shooting fire out of it as is spun. It traveled approximately half a mile before it slowly landed in a field across the valley. As it descended it passed it front of a group of grain silos but behind a tree line. There was no smoke or any noise as it passed and landed. We went to investigate but there was no trace of the object in the field where we saw it land. I called the owners of the farm the next day and they confirmed that there was nothing in that field as well.
