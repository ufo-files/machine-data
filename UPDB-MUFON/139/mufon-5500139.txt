{cak} Observed 2 small lights and a larger one in the middle hovering over the mountains in front of my house.

It was around 9:30 pm and my husband was late from getting home from work so I stepped out onto the deck to see if I could see him coming down the road when I noticed to the right hand side of the roads on top of the range of mountains 2 small lights and a large light in the center just hovering over the mountain. It didn't move and I kept trying to focus on it and finally decided to get my binoculars to try and make it out, just as I got back outside and raised the glasses to my eyes the lights merged together into one big ball of swirling lights of red,blue and white mist with small pin sized lines shooting through the middle. Once they had merged they suddenly disappeared leaving no trace.
