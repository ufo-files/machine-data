{rjl} UFO Cylinder

On June 20th, 1964, I observed a large black cylinder appear in the afternoon sky over the NE quadrant of our neighborhood in Grand Island, NE. It was slightly overcast that day with cloud layers at approximately FL080 to FL110. The entire neighborhood came out to view the object.

Insofar as size goes, I think it was at least 300 ft to upwards of 500 ft long. It came in from the NW, transitioning NW, then slightly southerly, before moving to the east and simply disappearing. It was very dark, black if you will, definitely a cylinder shape. There was a red line that ran length-wise around the craft. It oscillated almost like an electrical field of some sort. Initially there was no sound coming from it, then there was some weird hum that would come and go, but it was always very faint. 

There were multiple adults, as well as kids, in the street watching it. I asked my dad what it was, if it was a spaceship, he never answered. He just stood there watching it. The truly odd thing is that none of the adults who witnessed this remember it. However, all of the kids do remember it. There is also and unexplained time gap that none of us can account for.
