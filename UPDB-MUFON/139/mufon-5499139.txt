{rjl} Eight glowing lights over the intersection of 40 and 270

Coming back from dinner, my fianc1 and I observed a line of eight glowing yellow - orange - red circular objects in the sky. They were lined up in a linear manner and seemed to appear out of dark and then moved east, following 40 east. They moved at a speed faster than a hot air balloon (this weekend was the big hot air balloon race in St. Louis) and different than a air plane, it was like a fast hover. We pulled over and attempted to capture video on our iPhones and neither of our videos turned out (strange because I was certain I had the craft in my line of sight).

We watched for about ten minutes and we were fascinated at the fact that the craft made absolutely no sound and the craft seemed to glow and hover in a strange way.
