{dr}Bright orb in low orbit. Abruptly vanished.

In the western sky a bright orb was observed. It appeared to be in a low orbit and did not change trajectory. After 1 minute of observation the orb abruptly vanished. The colors of the orb were blue and white with a hint of red. These colors were part of a hazy aura. The object was first thought to be a satellite until it disappeared.
