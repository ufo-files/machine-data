{rjl} dim shevron shape moving rapidly

I happened to be outside playing with my dog and would occasionally pause to look at the clear night sky when I suddenly noticed a chevron shaped craft moving at a high rate of speed from South to North pretty much directly overhead.
I could barley make it out, the movement is what caught my eye. It was just a dim gray/white glowing line moving rapidly.
At first I thought it might be geese or sandhill cranes as I do see them flying in formation on occasion. However, I did not hear a sound from overhead as one would hear when cranes or geese are flying overhead. Also, I have never seen them fly in a perfectly symmetrical formation.
The craft took at most 3 to 5 seconds to go from directly overhead to being obscured by trees.
