{cak} Multiple orange lights appear after power outage

On the night of 10-04-19 I was noticing strange transparent lights following each other and rotating around each other near San Anselmo-San Rafael area slightly sou h of Red Hill. A powerful cannon ball blast sound was heard to the east which immediately caused the power to go out all over this area.The power remained out for the next 4 to 5 hours.Multiple glowing orange yellow blueish lights were seen during this 4 to 5 hour time frame.I witnessed these glowing objects at 3 different times in the same power out area at approximately 7:30, 10 P.M. and after 11P.M. Objects appeared in groups of up to 6 to 8 objects at treetop and hillside levels at various locations near Mt. Tamalpais and out near the Bay.Lights appear and disappear while being observed. A very similar scenario was also observed during a PG@E power shutdown at approximately 12 midnight the night of October 27.
