A pulsating light like a star but not a star confirmed with Sky View Free App

At about 11 pm from my 10th Floor Solarium me & my Family saw this star like object pulsating with bright white lights and pulsating red lights. This was observed by me & family (4 inlc. me), so we double checked with an app called Sky View Free(Mobile App) to check if it is any Star, Planet, Hubble or any other Artificial Satellite but to our surprise it was none then we checked with our 10 X 50 mm Binoculars we clearly saw a Circular Object with flashing Red lights.
