Witnessed large pyramid with glowing orbs leaving and entering.

My girlfriend and I watched a pyramid shaped ufo about the size of a walmart hovering above the fog line blanketing a strip mall. I lived on the second story of a house located above the strip mall on a hill. It was about 3am or so and we looked out the window and watched glowing gold orbes flying in one side of the pyramid and out the other. There was a platform at the base with 2 sets of search lights on opposite sides moving in circular crossing patterns. We watched it from bed long enough that we both fell asleep.
