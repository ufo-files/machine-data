I left for work at approximately 10:16 p.m. and witnessed the lights on Guernsey Lake Rd (Delton) to Middleville, Mi.

I left for my 3rd shift job at 10:16 p.m. and immediately noticed the objects/lights, while driving, for a period of approximately 22 minutes. They were below the clouds and stationary and brighter than any star. I initially thought the lights were planes that were coming straight for me but soon realized that they were hovering in place. I was in awe but didn't tell anyone other than my wife because I didn't want anyone else to think I was crazy. I lost sight of the lights when I had to report for work.
