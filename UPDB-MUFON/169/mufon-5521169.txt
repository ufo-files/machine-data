Fast moving cigar shaped object

At 7:35 am, my son saw what appeared to be a BRIGHT shining star to the east. As I slowed down to look at it, it began to move in a western direction. In what time it took for him to get the camera up on my phone it had moved a LONG way VERY. So far that I could not find it in the view finder. As it passed in front of my van, it was actually flying quite low. Low enough that we could tell that it was actually cigar shaped and very shiney white. It was low enough that we could tell that there were no wings and it DEFINITELY WAS NOT an air plane.
