{jpg} Green orb passes airplane

I was a passenger on a 19 seater beachcraft (at the time America West airlines). We we're flying from Prescott to Phoenix. At cruising altitudes a green orb slightly bigger then a soft ball slowly passed outside my window, from the front of the aircraft to the back. The orb passed by very slowly. About .5 MPH. It was a dull green color, like a tennis ball. It did not look metalic or shiney. Kind of blury. Oddly enough I was very calm. Almost passified. I will never forget.
