Went from about 1000 ft to tree top across the Parks hwy then flew south

We were traveling north on a dark 2-lane highway, and we stopped at an unlit rest stop. It was a clear, dark night. We saw a dark ship that glowed blue on the bottom on our left side. It looked like the ship in "Flight of the Navigator." The craft seemed to teleport to our left and moved closer to us, then again right and closer again, moving almost directly over us. It moved past us and we tried to follow it to get a picture, but we could not catch it. It made no noise and did not seem to affect the car or us directly. We were both very excited and fully awake.
