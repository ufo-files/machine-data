{dr}1 bright white light turned into a visible object(oval shaped) w/ 6 white lights & 1 red ascended into the sky

I was smoking a cigarette, sitting in a friends sunroom near 6:45am when I suddenly saw a bright star and another to the left of it appear, alarmed and experienced with UFO sightings, myself and my two friends ran outside to get a glimpse of the bright white light perhaps 100 feet above the ground. We watched the object slowly move to east and increase elevation when the one light turned to two, then four smaller white lights were also visible, at the end of the oval craft was a small red light. My friend videotaped the sighting (although it did not capture well) and watched it until it disappeared into the horizon. I knew immediately it was a UFO and all three of us were shocked that such an obvious craft was in the daylight. It moved in a straight elevating line west to east.
