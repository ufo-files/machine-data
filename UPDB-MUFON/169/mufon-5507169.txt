{nd} While driving North on interstate 5 noticed objects out of passenger window

We had just finished lunch and it was 1:45pm and we were on our way heading North on interstate 5 when I observed a funny looking bright object in the sky out of the passengers side of window.I asked the person riding with me to keep an eye on the object and when it went from 1 to 2 objects and the person made the comment that one of the objects changed shape and that a picture was being taken of the objects so as soon as the picture was taken the objects separated a little more and then vanished. The interesting part is that when we looked at the picture that was taken with an I phone we noticed tiny little orbs around each object that we couldnt see with the naked eye and when enlarged you could make out the bigger objects as Bright saucer shape.
There were no other aircraft visible in the sky at the time of these sightings.The weather was clear with no clouds.
