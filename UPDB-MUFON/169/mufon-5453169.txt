7 Spheres

I see star-like ufo's often and I have a theory that they are all around us all of the time and that the best place for them to manifest during the day would be around the sun because A, we can't look at it directly, and B, it's a great place to hide since it is bright. This idea popped into my head so I walked outside to take some pictures of the sun expecting to see nothing, but to my surprise, I found these metallic orbs in the photos.
