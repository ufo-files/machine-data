{dr}single white strobe @ 6 sec. for > two hrs., moved only 8-10 min. of angle in 2 hrs.

Was observing night sky in Prunedale, CA, Monterey County, Calif. with gen. 2 night vision from ridge at 620 ft. elevation above sea level @ 8 PM, clear sky, no wind, no visual obstructions. Observed single strobing white light (@ 6 sec./strobe)at 45 deg. up from horizontal in SSW direction from observation point, roughly in a vertical line above Pacific Grove, Calif., as seen from observation point. I observed off and on for 2 hrs.; I do not know how long the object remained after I stopped observing. 

Strobing was barely visible with the naked eye, i.e., every third or fourth strobe was not able to be seen. Object moved extremely slowly in a southerly direction in a straight line - moved only 8-10 minutes of angle, total. Objects very slight movement was discernible between flashes by comparison with adjacent stars.  There was no sound heard. During observation period, commercial aircraft were observed every few minutes traveling in north-south directions at much lower angles - those aircraft could be heard and their colored nav. lights seen.
