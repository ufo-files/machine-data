Holiday

My girlfriend and I were on the front porch, smoking a cigarette and watching lightning hit two communications towers that we can easily see from the front porch. As I looked toward the one on the left, I saw a light that was moving rapidly from left to right...there was no blinking lights on it at all, and living somewhat close to an airport and seeing a lot of air traffic, none that I have seen have moved like that. It showed no erratic movement, just steady and fast. We at first thought it was an airplane until we noticed no blinking lights, and there was absolutely no noise associated with it. (again, very strange, because you can hear every plane that travels overhead here.) I asked *(Name deleted--CMS/sg) if she knew what that was, and she said no...I was overly excited, because having seen things before, I have never seen one this close...we lost sight of it as it very quickly disappeared out of our view. I could not tell what kind of craft it was, and I tried to see it better with the lightning, but to no avail. It just appeared to be a very large spherical white light.
