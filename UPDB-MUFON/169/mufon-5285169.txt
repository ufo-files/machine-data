What I thought was a star in the sky but at 4 p.m. daytime

Well I was driving home from work and what I thought was a star in the sky at 4 in the afternoon I carry binoculars with me so I pulled over to look at the store but it was a perfect sphere which was black as night on the bottom and bright as a star on the top of the sphere and I looked at it for at least 5 to 10 minutes I felt that it knew that I was looking at it then in the blink of an eye it shot straight up into the sky and I never saw it again this was in 2001 I never reported it till now of course this was before video on cell phones and good quality pictures with a cell phone
