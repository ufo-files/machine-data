{td} Driving with kids. Strange objects in sky.

Driving with my kids when we were first moving to a house we just rented out. I looked up in the sky and saw strange objects flying and after a few seconds they changed formation and hovered over my vehicle while I was driving down my main road. I tried to look above from the drivers window for a split second and I believe I saw at least 3 of them all in a tight formation, appearing to be a type of aircraft but I am still unsure. I was surprised to see a bird that big, and soon realized it was most likely not a bird. I pulled over to tried to capture a video, but the orbs of light were seemingly miles away. Three formed a triangle, another three formed a triangle and one was left out. They soon formed into a straight line. Confusion and curiosity. I tried asking my son what was outside the window and he wasn1t sure. I asked if it was an airplane and he said yes. It slowly vanished into the distance. Potentially became transparent.
