{cak} Purple, dome-shaped UFO with a rotating pink ring hovering 50-100 feet above our townhouse

On May 29, at 9:05 PM, as I took our dog outside our townhouse, a purple, dome-shaped UFO with a rotating pink ring 1/3 of the way up the object, was hovering 50 to 100 feet above our townhouse. I ran inside to get my cellphone and told my wife to come outside. The appliance alarms were going off and I smelled electrical discharge from a surge protector. We both were standing in our courtyard watching the object move in an up-and-down fashion above our townhome. It then slowly moved to the west. I had my Droid Razr phone up and ready to photograph the object, but the screen stayed black until the instant it crossed behind the treeline to the west. The sighting lasted about 4 to 5 minutes. As a classically-trained guitarist, I regularly perform (play guitar) on our deck, as well as project my positive thoughts to the universe. I feel the sighting may be related to this fact.
