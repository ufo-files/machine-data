Gray Object Near Newark, NJ

I was driving [item moved/CMS/nd] down the NJ turnpike to 78. This route takes me right passed Newark Airport. [item moved/CMS/nd] I drive under scads of planes coming in and out of Newark, so I rarely pay attention. Route 78 runs East-West and as I was merging onto 78 West I notice a large gray object West of Newark airport. At first, I didn't pay much attention because of the constant plane traffic. However, I quickly realized that this was strange because plane traffic at Newark typically flows North-South, so I looked at the object more closely. It was about 1-2 miles (estimate) from the airport to West. I was actually talking to a friend of mine on the phone so I looked at my cell phone log when I entered the date & time above. I noticed it for about 2 minutes, it really didn't seem to move, I didn't see any lights. Then my view became blocked by an underpass and then by some trees, after all of the objects in my view cleared I no longer saw this object in the sky. The next day I did a google search for UFO & Newark but didn't see any recent sightings.
