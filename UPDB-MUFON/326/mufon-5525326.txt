Bright star-like object that was traveling eastward. Had a steady emission of light and then suddenly the object got very bright and disappeared.

I was standing outside with my wife,daughter and a couple of friends looking up at the stars when we both observed what we thought to be a star with the exception that the object was moving. As we watched it, it continued to travel in a straight and level path. After about three minutes the light got very bright and the object disappeared. About five minutes later we observed a second object do the exact same thing. These objects were on a consistent path with a previous reported sighting around the first of July.
