{cak} Triangle craft paced with my family in my truck

Triangle lighted craft space my truck from Wilmington Ohio to my home in Butlerville Ohio. When I parked in my driveway the craft turned slowly and headed toward Wilmington Ohio. I have been around aircraft for many years and have never seen this happen. Craft was completely silent. I could not see any shape except for the triangle shape of the lights. Craft was approximately 300 yards to my right and around 500 feet in altitude. Absolutely no sound !!
