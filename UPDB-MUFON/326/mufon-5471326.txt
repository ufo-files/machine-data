10 to 15 orangish lights flying past at speed in a type of curve

it was night, i went into our spare room to have a smoke out the window, i had just started my cigarette when i saw 10 to 15 orangish, oval shaped objects in the sky, they were moving very fast,travelling in a wide arc but the objects themselves kept perfect formation, it only lasted about 5 seconds and they were gone out of view. it was a clear cloudless night, im not entirely sure of the exact time but it would have been after 11pm on the 26th december. as soon as i seen the objects i called to my husband to come look but they were gone by the time he came to the room. i felt a bit frightened after seeing this, i have never seen anything like this before. i know what it wasnt, it was not a plane or fireworks or chinese lanterns or anything i have seen in the sky before.i have no idea what it is i saw.
