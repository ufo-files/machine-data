Bright White Sphere Seen Flying Over Chilhowee and Parksville Lake

I was flying a Cessna 172 over Polk County at approximately 5000ft MSL maintaining a heading of 140 degrees. The sun was getting low on the horizon and it was a beautiful evening. I looked down to my left and looked at the top of Chilhowee Mountain near Oswald Dome. I see a bright white sphere flying along the top of the ridge. Estimated altitude above the ridge is approximately 500ft AGL. It flew from my left to in front and below the aircraft out of my view at a high rate of speed. I perform a right banked turn to bring the object into view. It is hovering over Parksville lake near the dam after I complete the maneuver. I am flying towards the sphere on my 11 o’clock low, still about 5 miles out when it shoots straight up out of view in a blink of an eye. Perplexed by the event, I fly back to 1A0 and land the aircraft.
