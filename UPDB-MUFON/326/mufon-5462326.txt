Alien sounds smell like sxxt

It was dark and my heart was just remembering the old days of poetry and harmony... I was thinking about art and the meaning of life... and suddenly... an alien noise... I attached some files.

Another alien noise... Wow. 

One alien noise can happen. But two alien noises one after the other? This is no coincidence. 

What does this mean? What does this all mean? Maybe the end of the world is near? 
Maybe I should just stop eating broccoli?

I should totally open the window now. This made me think that maybe Shakespeare was right about the human nature, about the real meaning of life and our spirituality. If we can witness such incredible features of nature then there must be something out there and we are not just here to survive.
