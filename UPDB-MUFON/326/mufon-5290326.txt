Slowly gliding, solid black and boxy

Was out on my front deck. Looked up in the sky. Slightly south. Slightly east. Noticed a solid black object slowly floating across the sky. Eerily similar to those "floating witch" videos. From the rear, it was boxy but not 4 straight lines to form a square. Maybe 40ftx40ft. Could not determine length or side shape. No lights or other colors. Too far to tell if there was sound. Approximately 2 miles south of the 8 freeway. First noticed when It was about 1 mile east of the 805 freeway. 1-2 miles high. Ran inside to grab phone. Have some video, but unsure if I captured it. Random clouds and not sure if I was recording the right area. Unable to transfer it to my computer to view on a larger screen. Lost view of it as it passed over the Rancho San Diego area. Went behind a cloud once, reappeared and behind another cloud.
