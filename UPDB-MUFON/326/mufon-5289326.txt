A bright white circle about the size of a nickle held out about arms length. Traveled across the northern sky from west to east in about 1 1/2 seconds

Approximately the first week in April 2019 at about 5:30 am I was going out on my deck in the back yard to have a cup of coffee. The back of my house faces north. The sun wasn't quite over the eastern horizon yet. The sky was pretty well lit but the ground was still in shadows. The sky was clear with no clouds. Ass I walked on to my deck I saw a bright white circle streaking across the northern sky from the west to the eastern horizon. From the point I saw it traveled across about 3/4 of the northern sky in about 1 1/2 seconds and disappeared over the eastern horizon. I was truly expecting a sonic boom, but no boom nor did the object make any sound. If you held a nickel out at arms length that would be how big the object appeared to me. It was very bright, not like the sun but brighter than a full moon. It had a well defined unobscured perimeter from the light. There was no trailing debri, fire or anything. Just the bright circle. 
  I have no idea of the actual size or distance of the object. there was nothing to compare it to.
  After it passed over the horizon I just stood there dumb founded. I was trying to make sense of what I just saw but I had never seen anything like it in my entire life. If you can tell me what I saw I would love to hear it.
