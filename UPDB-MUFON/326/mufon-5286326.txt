It is 3rd of 4th reported sightings within 6 weeks: Flashed many colors, tumbled and bounced around and was spinning, later joined by 2 others.

July 10, 2021 – Antioch, IL
Saw another “unidentified object” again, over the lake tonight. This is my 3rd of 4 sightings since June 6th. I have not been looking for these… but have seen them at random. 1:18 AM: The Object appears to be at a high altitude. It flashed and sparkled in the distance, but from the naked eyes does not have much color. It looked very star-like, similar to when planet that is close to the earth. When zoomed-in on the phone, it was moving erratically, up-down and all directions. Sometimes, it seemed to hover in place, while moving erratically, but it eventually moved across the sky from NW to SE to South. The last observation was at at 2:47 AM
“On Video”, (zoomed in)… it is flashing white and many colors, sparking, spinning, tumbling and flying erratically all over the place and was quite difficult to track. Sometimes, it appeared to tumble in geometric shapes with glowing white and flashes of Pastel Colors...sometimes it looked round-ish and wheel-like with slight, star-point projections… or that “effect”.  
It had many rapid color flashes and sometimes turned totally red or totally green or blue. It faded in and out a few times and I lost track, temporarily. Sometimes, when zooming in on my iphone, I could see the structure of the flying object. It was, at times round and geometric in appearance with 8 sides like a stop sign. There were darker grayish metallic structures within this this roundish spinning object, with the center being dark or perhaps indented, like an “Iris of the eye" in an eyeball. A previous iphone photo that I had taken on June 6th, had shown a roundish, white glowing object in an 8 sided geometric shape, out-lined in gray with three dimensional ‘Nodules’ around the perimeter.
See My Other Lake County, IL Sightings reported to Mufon: Case# 116110- June 6, 2021, 116872-June 20, 2021, and 116986 - July 20th, 2021
There are 2 major Differences with this July 10th sighting:
1-There were eventually 3 flashing objects in the sky! I could only see ONE with the naked eye. 
2-Two of the Objects were “joined together” at one point. I could not see this until reviewing video. The color flashes seemed much more intense during or after this time.
