{rjl} A strange stationary and bright light observed on the early morning sky for about 15 minutes.

It happened in Bellevue, WA, Nov.22, 2018 at about 7:30 AM (Pacific Time) during routine morning activities at home . Me & my wife saw a strange looking light, much stronger than any star or planet or satellite. Light was solid stationary and not blinking. It was a sun-rise time, sky was almost cloudless and blue with orange glow from East. Stars were already not visible (except this light). My wife did noticed one passing aircraft (much weaker and blinking light) moving high overhead from South to North at that time, but before me joining her. That strange light was about 30 degree above horizon in the direction SE (about 135 degree). Looking through the binoculars it was still the light, seemed as triple points (maybe some aberration from the optics). In one moment it was visible through binoculars a faint (& also stationary) light close to the main one. When looking for my photo-camera, some low clouds arrived and light was not visible any more. Altogether the duration of the observation can be estimated as about 15 minutes.
I am an aerospace engineer, know aircraft, like to observe ISS on the night sky. This light was not resembling anything known to me and my personal conclusion would be that it was "UFO", but after confirmation from somebody better informed about position planets on the sky in this particular time that Venus and Mars were somewhere else.
