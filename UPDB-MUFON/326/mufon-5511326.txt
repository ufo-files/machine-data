{dr}3 UFOs Observed Over Queens, NY

I was watching the moon last night from my window, which had attracted me because of its reddish color. I was watching towards south east direction for no more than 2 minutes at about 10:26 is when I saw them. There were 3 heading NW, in unison and not breaking formation. The 3 were a good distance apart from each other and seemed to form a triangular shape. The most prominent thing about these crafts were their shape. I cant really describe what shape they were, but it wasnt a classic disc or cigar shape. Almost boomerang-like, and they were spinning within its own orbit with some pulsating lights emanating from within. All this occurred in the time frame of approximately 10-15 seconds as they then went over my neighborhood, over buildings and I lost view of it. They made no sound whatsoever, that I remember. This was my first actual sighting where I can truly say I saw in great detail the object and its properties as well as its movements. I quickly ran outside afterward trying to see if any more were coming, but nothing else was seen. I have to point out that its a strange coincidence that just earlier Id been watching a 911 seminar on Youtube by Dr. Judy Wood on evidence of breakthrough energy, and I remember thinking that some type of out of world energy must have played part in the disintegration of the World Trade Center. Again, strange coincidence. I will never forget what I witnessed last night. Gives me a great feeling finally knowing we are not alone in the universe.
