Travelling west bound on 401. Three or 4 km1s West of Tilbury notice off the distance verticle object floating just above the tree line. Object appeared to move faster than expected but seamingly floating. With no sound or propulsion

Travelling west bound with several cars in the immediate area both west and east bound. The object appear to be hanging verticle in the sky. At first seamingly floating in the sky above the trees off to the NW a mile or two away just north of the hiway. I was not able to grab my phone quickly enough as I reached down it seamed to instantly appear much closer us. Approaching to within 2oo to 300 feet of our car. Approx 200 ft off the ground it was vividly coloured in Red, yellows , greens and golden. Long or vertically tall it was 5x times as Tall or longer vertically as wide. Almost pillowy looking the colours were very distinct reds, yellows greens and a golden hue but yet hard to describe. It continued on traveling over the hiway past the car in a southeasterly direction until out of site a minute or so later. Both of us Experienced the effect of sore upper gums on the left rear upper jaw area. Wife sought dental help, dentist found nothing.
