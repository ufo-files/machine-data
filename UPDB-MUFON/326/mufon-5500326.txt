{nd} Bright moving lights and then hovering and took path East Naples, Florida

Aug. 9th , 2014 at 10:45 pm. in East Naples, Florida .it was a clear night. husband got home and parking the car as he walked out of his car he saw in the sky 2 very bright lights moving as if in a line but very separately from each other. There was no noise what so ever. He ran to our condo appartment on the second floor and told me to come out. The condo building only has two floors, It was hovering above our condo building looked like a big star as big as a basketball with orange and yellow lights in the middle it seemed to get a little bigger and then it disappeared, the other other one also disapeared. We had never seen anything like it before We just looked at each other but that was it.
