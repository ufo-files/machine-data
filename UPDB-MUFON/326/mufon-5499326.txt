{cak} Meteor-like light that hived off into four objects

Friday night September 12, 2014 at 11:52 p.m a bright object appeared in the northwestern sky. It appeared at first to be a meteor, except that it travelled more slowly and lingered longer in its descent than normal meteors do in just a fraction of a second. The bright light appeared next to 1hive off1 until 4 objects were now continuing the downward path and then disappeared below the tree line. The total time that elapsed was about 3 seconds.

The light was in the NW part of the sky and travelled downward to the right. It looked to be approximately 1 mile or so distant. I judge the size of the objects to be somewhere between 10 and 25 feet, although that is very difficult to know for certain.

Each of the objects was as bright as the original and the downward flight path did not deviate from the slope it originally had begun, roughly 451.  What is especially interesting, is that these lights descended to the same exact place that the bright sphere emerged from on August 31. (See reported case # 59513).

As previously reported, this area to which these lights descended is uninhabited, and forms the western shore of Blue Springs Lake, and the Lake itself.  It is not known if the lights descended into the Lake, or if the bright sphere from 13 days earlier had emerged from the Lake.
