{nd} Blue & Red lights, looping circles & zig zag patterns, then disappeared

UFO spotted flying & hovering over San Elijo Hills, CA 02.28.2013 Suburb of San Diego, CA. UFO had a bright blue light, then switched to red, then back to blue again. Was doing loops and circles and zig zags - definitely NOT an airplane pattern. The very small twinkly white lights far above the UFO are airplanes. This was taken from a cell phone video camera, so the quality is a little poor, but in real life this blue & red ball UFO was huge! There were no white lights on the UFO at all, the ball of light switches from bright blue to bright red to bright blue again, then all lights off, then appears again. Way too far up in the sky from the valley below to be a toy of any kind, and this was up in the sky - nothing for this image/object to be projected onto etc. Was not a helicopter either. Movement from the UFO was quite rapid.
February 28, 2013 at approximately 7:30PM. Lasted about 7 minutes long.
