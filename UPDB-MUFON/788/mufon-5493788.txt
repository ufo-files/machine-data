{rjl} Light that went from above rooftop to suddenly directly ahead

One night in Lubbock,TX, my girlfriend and I went to our favorite spot to park. Just a quiet neighborhood location in the southern part of Lubbock. We usually would park for about 30-45 minutes and return to her house. This particular night, when we began to return to her house, as I drove down the street adjacent to a field, we noticed a bright light in the air just above rooftop level. To my left, I was driving, there were some homes under construction that had the reflective insulation panels nailed to the outside of them. I saw a flash of light suddenly that reflected from the panels and when I looked forward the light we had seen was directly ahead and level with us. As I turned the corner to continue on, I spotted this white rat come running across the road and then the car died at that moment. It took a couple of attempts to start the car again whereupon I looked in the rear view mirror and saw a dark figure in the back seat. I told my girlfriend not to look in the back seat and accelerated to her house. Fortunately, her house was just a few blocks away. As we turned into the driveway, I continued to see this figure in the car. Once we stopped, we both jumped out of the car and there was nothing in the car any longer. I can only surmise that this may have been an alien form. I still get chills when recalling this event.
