{nd} lights in daytime sky

My wife and I went to a family gathering on Phoenix, cave creek Rd and the 101. I was in the house when I saw a crew people looking at the sky and pointing so I went out to see myself. What I saw was two star like objects, one stationary and another moving in a easterly direction. I took a picture of the moving object with my cell phone. The objects were well above the clouds. The hostess of the gathering had a high end camera and she took serial pictures of the stationary object. We saw the stationary object for about a hour and a half. I can not attach the pictures onto the report.
