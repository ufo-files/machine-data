Blinking Object

I filmed this while walking my dog on April 17 2021 at 8:30pm. I was exhausted as I had just arrived home after driving for 14hrs. I was filming a blinking light which you can see in the video and photos in the background. Look to the upper middle area and you’ll see it.
I took 4 pics, then zoomed in on that light/object and took more photos. Then I videoed it for 16 seconds. It wasn’t until later when I was looking at them w my son did I see the other object flying in a bizarre way. And then I looked at the photos and the object is in all four.
When watching the video, look above the sign/tree to the center left on the screen.
