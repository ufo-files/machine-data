Unexplained shapes seen in my photos

I was taking pictures from our hotel room moments after arriving in Wembley Park hotel, we were 9th floor with amazing views.
I began taking photos of the stadium and then my daughter sitting on the window ledge looking out dreaming. 
I remember seeing 2 white lines moving in background, assuming they were planes. 
Once we arrived home I began looking at my pictures and decided to see if the lines were planes, I then looked at all window shots and could see one picture looked clear but disk shaped. Would like to know was it unexplained or something else.
