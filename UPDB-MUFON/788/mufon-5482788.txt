{rjl} Balls of Fire with smoke trails. Traingular formations.

Was heading north on Minnesota highway 371 about 10 miles south of 210. 

I heard a "piercing" / "popping" kind of like boom sound or something of that sort. I could see "balls of fire" appearing in the sky. I'm thinking the sound I was hearing was of the object entering the atmosphere but I'm not expert. That was just the assumption I made based on the sound I heard.

I immediately knew what I was seeing was out of the ordinary. I pulled over, put the flashers on and attempted to record what I was seeing with the camera on my phone. The quality isn't as good as what I was seeing in person, but I was able to catch it on camera with video and audio included.

These things looked like balls of fire, leaving smoke trails. These trails were in a wavey/spiral trail. 

They were descending in a controlled pattern. There were times where it appeared they were holding a "formation" that of a triangle before fully descending. 

This went on for about 10 minutes. I got about 5 minutes of footage when my phone wouldn't let me record anymore. I also drove away and could still see the event happening in my rear view mirror. So I'm not entirely sure how long this was going on for officially. 

I lost sight of the objects behind tree lines and combined with leaving the distant observable scene.

I put the video on YouTube.
