I contacted the Air Port in Eugene and there were no flares launched/dropped. There is no one up @ Oakridge air port to do that. Eugene Air port told me they launched NO FLARES on that date. I would classify this sighting as significant due to the number of craft in the sighting. There is no way the other people on the Highway did not see what I was seeing!
