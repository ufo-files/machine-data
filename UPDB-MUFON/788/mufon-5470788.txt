{rjl} hovering triangle shape, 3 white lights, 1 red flashing light

I left home at approximately 5:15 a.m. At a red light, looked to the left. Saw something up high. 3 white lights in triangle shape. 1 red light in the center that flashed on and off. I thought "Well, that's not a UFO because UFOs don't have flashing red lights." It was maybe as high up as a hill top and I thought, "It's probably from the military and it will go south towards the base, over the hill top." I turned the car to the right. I got a coffee. I had to drive in the direction of the object to get on the freeway. I looked up and thought, "Hmm, it's still there." And I drove on to my destination. 

I feel I need to report it. I'd like to know if other people saw it.
