{dr}Very large about 2 football fields long. Had a top and bottom section steel gray in color

Driving to work.Almost to Sheridan Or. Looked to my right saw something in my peripheral vision. It was a cloudy day.Clouds were low in the sky. And I saw a huge ship like as big as 2 football fields long. It had two levels,a top and a bottom with a section in the middle to connect the two. It was gray in color. Had my I phone with me but did't have time to get a picture. was driving 55 miles an hour hard to stop. about that time I came to a small hill with trees could not see it at that time I got to the other side of the hill and could only see the bottom of it as it was moving into the clouds and disappeared. I was so upset when I got to work I kept asking myself did I really see what I know I saw. Later that day I told my husband he was very surprised and found it difficult to understand. Anyway I wanted to keep it quiet. But it's been on my mind for quit sometime and would like to know if anyone else saw what I saw.
