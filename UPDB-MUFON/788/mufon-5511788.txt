My granddaughter who took photos was in the car with her Mother, Uncle, and cousin. It was spring break they were coming from Mo. to my home in central Illinois. March 27, 2013.. When they were just south of Springfield my granddaughter thought the moon looked strange so she got her camera out to take photos. Then she notice a very large arrangements of lights. She took photos. When she showed them to me. I told her you got a perfect clear photo of a triangle.. She said Grandma . It keep changing shape of lights. Then she show me more photos. It was a triangle with a tail of five lights. She said it had windows between the main body and the straight long line of tale lights. . One small blue light was inside the triangle .. Her Mother could not see it. It was to the East and my daughter was driving due North. Her Mother kept telling her it had to be a plane. She told me Grandma it was no plane. It was huge.. She said she got scared a little when she seen it much closer..... My Son was in the back seat behind driver. his son was on the east side window but he is autistic so he would not be a witness.
