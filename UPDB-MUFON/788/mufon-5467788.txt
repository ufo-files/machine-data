{rjl} Saw twice within 2 hrs, thin flat oval disk, white, cloud-like, hover then high speed flight

In mid-September to early October 2017 I was in Unity Hospital in the Town of Greece, Rochester, NY. I was being treated for pancreatitis, but was not on any medication that would have affected my perception or cognitive skills. 

On or about September 18, 2017 at approximately 11:00 AM, I was sitting up in bed looking out my north-facing window when I observed a cloud-white, flat, thin, oval disk zip across the top left corner of my window. I believe the direction was NE to SW. It was moving very quickly, and the sighting was so brief that I could not estimate speed nor even be sure of exactly what I had seen.

A couple of hours later, at approximately 1:00 PM, still puzzled by what I had seen, I again looked out the window and there, right in the center of the top third of my window, hovering in a cloudless, clear blue sky was the same disk.

It was flat, thin, oval and cloud-white. Its outer appearance was stable and unchanging, with absolutely no viable structural anomalies. It just hung there. I stared at it for a few seconds and remember thinking, "What the hell is that?" Suddenly, it zipped off to the East at an extremely high rate of speed. I remember thinking, "Damn, it 'heard' me."

To my knowledge, I have never had a sighting before and haven't had one since.
