strange object hovering above hill top

Was looking across at steep hills near my apartment when I saw what I thought was a helicopter or aircraft moving very slowly. Then I realized it was too big to be helicopter and too slow to be aircraft. It moved slowly across the ridge maybe 3/4 mile from me. I watched it for maybe 20 seconds then thought I would film it. As I reached for my phone it suddenly changed direction and headed away from at incredible speed. It was dark with moon but could be seen clearly as grey in color. It was oval in shape but with a dome on top and bottom and lights around the middle. I quickly checked flight radar 24 to see if any helicopters or planes in the area but nothing visible and airport approach pattern was several miles away. Spooked me quite a lot.
