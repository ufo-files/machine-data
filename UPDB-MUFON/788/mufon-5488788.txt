{rjl} Vivid pulsating light

Noticed immediately when I stepped outside my door. It was moving in a study horizontal line. The wobbling is due for me trying to get my iPhone video camera ready. I am very bad about judging distance. There was no sound at all. It was large compared to other items in the sky. Larger than a jet would be by far. I tried to follow it before it went behind the trees but I lost it so only the first few frames of the video have the actual object. I am not very good with enlarging or slowing things down but I did manage to isolate and zoom in on a couple of frames. One looks like a detailed disc object with something perhaps protruding from each end and the faint outline of an inverted V in the middle.  The other photo looks like a blob, I don't know if my wobbling phone caused that, but it is very different color from the first photo.  These were both from the same object
