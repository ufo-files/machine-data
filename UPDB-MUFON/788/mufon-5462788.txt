grey disc appeared in break in the cloud cover slowly moving south west

I was sitting at the dining table facing the garden where I like to observe the birds. It was a day of sunshine with broken cloud.A grey disc appeared in a break in the clouds exactly in my eye line. It was moving quite slowly in a south westerly direction and although my view lasted only seconds I have a clear recall of the object.
My daughter was sitting to my right and got a glimpse of the object before it moved behind the cloud again.
Neither of us had seen anything like it before. We were amazed. It was a solid object moving horizontally across the sky.
