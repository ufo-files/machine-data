{tjd} Flying V shaped craft

My brother, best friend, and I were walking across a field. We looked up and saw a large V shaped craft silently flying overhead. It was above the clouds, but we could see 3 lights along the bottom of each wing and a light at the tip of the V. It was very large and didn’t make a sound. It flew north until out of our sight. We thought it must have been a flying wing from Lockbourne AFB. We later learned flying wings were more boomerang shaped and made a lot of noise. This was very angular shaped.
