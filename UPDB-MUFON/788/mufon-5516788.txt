bright blue orb

I saw an enormouse blue orb shoot down over the horizon/my field of view ....my initial thought was to try and explain/rationalise it...thinking ...mayby a shooting star...but i've seen plenty of them befor, and this was definately not one.....this sucker was BIG...it appeared to me from my line of site as the size of the end of a pen if you held it out at arms length....and it was bright blue....it's trajectory appeared to go straight down....looked nothing like a shooting star...sucker was insanely fast, but it didn't leave a trail behind it like a shooting star...the light looked like it was emitting from the object...I could see its detail from initial observation, all the way till it went out of my field of view...hard to explain...the only sort of trail it left was when i caught it out of the corner of my eye, and turned my head, it left kind of a Zig Zag effect in my brain, like a solid lazer, from when i moved my head....than that dissapeared as i held my head still, and watched it shoot down....f**king crazy......hard to guess a size, but judging by the fact i estimate it being at least 20 miles away...and i saw it to be the size of the end of a pen as you held it at arms length...thick humid korean air made it seem a little bigger than it probably was...but regaurdless it would have be pretty huge...
