Object observed in two of my neowise pictures

I was taking pictures of the comet Neowise on July 20, 2020 in the middle of Crane, Tx and Penwell, Tx. I chose this area because I wanted complete darkness to observe the comet with the naked eye and to also take photos. I bring the light situatuon up to explain just how dark it was and what I saw in the photos (in my opinion) had no relation to any nearby lights. I have multiple photos before and after the 2 in question that clearly shows there was something in the 2. I had changed the pro settings on my phone to brighten the photos taken but I promise these images were not placed in the photos. I really have no clue how to do that. In order to get the photos I took I had to have my phone placed extremely sturdy so the photo would not come out blurry. I did not see the object with my naked eye as I had adjusted the setting on my phone and held it while it took the photos and my eye had not adjusted to the dark sky in time. I went back to see how the photos turned out and then I seen the object. I swiped to see the previous photo and was shocked to see the same object elsewhere in location. I then began to take more photos and looked hard with the eye and could not capture it again or see it in person. I then went back home because I got a little freaked out as I was alone.
