{jpg} Changed directions 180 degrees with incredible acceleration

Myself and a friend observed what appeared initially to be a shooting star or a satellite. It traveled from west to east, from the horizon, until it was overhead. At that point, the object made a sharp 90 degree turn to the north, and then made another sharp turn back to the west. At that point, the object accelerated at probably 5 times the speed it was initially traveling. The whole event lasted maybe 10 seconds. I1m certain it wasn1t a plane, helicopter, or a drone.
