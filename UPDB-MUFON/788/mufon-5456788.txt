{rjl} About 14 lights, moving slowing from the north of spokane towards the sw and then they would dissappear one by one

Saturday, Nov 30th 2019 at 9 PM, I stepped out on my front porch that faces north, I looked up and noticed a light up to my left towards the north, thinking it was a plane, I went to step off my porch when I noticed a second light right behind it, I thought that that was odd, and then I saw another behind them, then another, all heading from the north towards the SW in a straight line, then I saw another and others followedin the same path. Some right on top of each other, others lagging a little behind but all following the same path. They were moving slowly, and there was no sound that I could hear. As they got to my extreme left, they would dissapear. All disappeared in the same fashion. After seeing about the first 5 or 6, I ran in the house and grabbed my phone and recorded a few videoos the best I could. The recordings are not great but they show enough to see what I was seeing. I knew I was not seeing something a person sees everyday. Thje whole thing took maybe 5 to 10 minutes, but I wasnt counting, I was in amazement
