{dr}Looked like a flare but didnt burn out. Then I was able to see grayish disk type craft.

We were sitting out on the deck. The area is quite dark and not much ambient light. At first when it appeared we thought it was a firework since it was the 4th. When the object didn't burn out we realized that it wasnt a firework at all. It started as a "fireball" glowing like a flare. Then as I followed it behind some trees and the trees blocked the glowing part I could see the actual craft that was much larger then the glowing part. The glowing part was near the front of the craft. The craft was a dull grayish in color with no other lights and emitted no noise. I then lost the object behind the trees as it continued its northerly path. Then at 10pm it was back. Again coming from the south heading due north but a little higher in the sky this time. Were not sure is this was the same one we saw at 9 or a second one. The "flare like" glow was identical but the night sky was much darker so this time we couldnt see the actual craft. If I didn't see the actual craft the first time I would have thought that the glow was the craft whe in fact the craft itself was much, much larger. I was able to capture 2 photos and a minute of video before we lost sight of it in the distance.
