(GS) Brilliant flash then reduced to small light and turned west and disappeared

I was outside on my deck that is on a cliff. I saw a bright star like object appear out of nowhere. It first appeared as a round golf ball size and then it grew in size quickly and spread out a bit to a stretched out oval. I thought at first it was a shooting star then thought it might be an asteroid and then it got so big and bright I thought it would land on top of me. At its largest you could compare it to the size of a fully risen full moon yet its shape was more oval. Little scary I must admit. And then it started diminishing in brightness slowly back down to a golfball size and then it popped to a dimly lit pinpoint light and changed direction. At first it went slightly upward and then to the west. As it was moving in the sky I could see a very large helicopter travelling very fast coming from the Superstition Mountains. It hovered in the area but the object was gone. Then the helicopter went southeast very fast and I think I may have seen a shooting star cross its path. I have seen UFOs before because I spend a lot of time on that deck at night however I have NEVER seen anything like this. First time I ever became scared.
