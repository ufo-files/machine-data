{jpg} Nightly event, second year. Very bright, multi-colored, centered over one location for hours, sharp directional changes.

First noticed September 2014 as a bright, multi-colored object in the distance that first appeared to be an airplane but mainly stationary with quick, various directional changes. Object would come closer, though still several miles away, and remain in view for several hours. Through binoculars appeared to be a 4 pointed diamond shape, like 2 triangles connected at the bottom, with a long line coming up out of top point, all lines were brightly lit, multi-colored. Object appeared again late September 2015 and observed at time of this writing of September 27th. Object has red, green, blue and white moving, rotating lights, they do not flash. Object again makes sharp, sudden directional changes, going up, down, left right and angled movements but always seeming to return to one location in the sky. through binoculars (25x) object appears to be round with lights on outer edge this time but changes shape. This is a near nightly observation always between the hours of 9 pm and midnight. Aircraft can be viewed flying by it confirming what is being viewed is not a typical aircraft and much too high, big and too bright to be an RC vehicle. MUFON PLEASE send someone over with powerful enough lens to get a close look at this object so it can be documented and photographed!!
