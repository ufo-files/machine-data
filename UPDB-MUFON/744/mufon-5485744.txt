Slowly pulsating white light, star-sized

I was in my backyard with my dog. It was pretty much dark with the moon overhead and several stars visible. I was looking at the stars and noticed one of them moving across the sky at a slow pace. I don't know if it was stationary before I noticed it or not. It was a white light that would dim until it was almost invisible, then brighten to an average star-like luminosity. The time between its lightest and darkest states was about 4 seconds. It was also moving north at a plane-like pace, and after a total of about 20 seconds of watching it, it disappeared behind the treeline.

As I watched, I felt a sense of curiosity. I knew my camera wouldn't be able to focus on it in time, since I've experimented with taking pictures of stars only for them to barely show up.

We have planes flying overhead all the time, with their flashing lights and relatively low altitude, so I'm positive it wasn't that. I then thought it might be a shooting star, but I've never seen one pulsate like that.
