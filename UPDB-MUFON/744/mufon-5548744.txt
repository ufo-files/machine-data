Cork Screw affect

I saw what looked like a Cork Screw type object passing overhead, it was light green in color, traveling very fast and appeared to be rotating while passing overhead. No sound. I was on a fishing pier at Corpus Christi NAS at the time. I just happened to look upward as the object passed overhead. Other people were there on the pier but not near me. I didn't report this at the time because I thought perhaps someone used a lazer beam to project this object in the night sky, but reviewing recent UFO sightings on your website tonight, I saw a photograph which was reported on 2007-11-25 (South mt AZ)which shows the exact same thing I saw in the night sky at Corpus Christi NAS.
