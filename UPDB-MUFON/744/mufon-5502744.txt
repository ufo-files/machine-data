{cak} Three Orange orbs over Oak Island numerous times

My sister was visiting so we took her out on the boat for an evening boat ride down the intracoastal waterway. We discussed the orange orbs I had reported to MUFON a few weeks ago, so naturally our eyes were on the sky. The first sighting was about 9pm..I,.my sister, brother in law, wife and daughter all witnessed seeing them. There were the three orange orbs appearing higher in the sky than the last time we saw them...about 60 degrees. This time they were only there for a few seconds...not long enough for me to get the camera and grab a shot of them. The next time we saw them it was at 10:03 pm. Again we all saw them, three orange orbs...very bright and just a little lower in the sky. This time they stayed for around 5 seconds. Again not long enough to snap a photo. We were amazed by seeing them twice. On the way back to the marina I had my daughter keep the camera on the sky. We were all busy talking when she screamed and pointed....we did not get tonsee what she saw. She described it as the same type of orange orb, just one this time. In approximately the same position as the last sighting but with one big difference. She said it appeared as all of the others did but instead of just disappearing as the others did, this one flew straight down very fast but leaving no trail like a tail you would see from a shooting star. No trail as it disappeared behind the trees on the horizon. She was freaking out just as bad as she did two weeks ago when we first saw the orbs. No further incidents on the way back to the marina. I do plan on taking the boat out more often to look for them, as you can see most hall of the island from where we were at tonight. Camera will be in hand next time.
