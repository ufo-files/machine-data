Noiseless, black triangle, 5 prominent lights

I was driving south on Highbury Ave. in London, ON in 2007. It was about 10:30 PM. As I began my exit onto Commissioners Ave., I noticed a flying, triangular shaped craft with the apex pointing forward in the path, and the lights beginning at the underside apex along each side. I rolled down my window, perplexed as to what I was seeing, and hoping to identify it by sound. It made NO sound, and appeared to be following me along Highbury, until I exited. I understand that Stealth Bomber tests were well underway in these years, and so I am unsure as to what I saw, but it was impressive.
