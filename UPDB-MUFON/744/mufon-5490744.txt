{jpg} Thanksgiving Night Right after Halftime of the Seahawks game. Bright Red-Orange Orb hovering outside my Window.

I was just getting off the phone with family Thanksgiving Night During the Seahawks football game approx. 7-7:30 pm. I was hanging up the phone when I noticed a Orange-Red light illuminating my living room threw partially closed blinds through my Sliding glass door to my porch approx. 100' - 150' high 75'- 150' away to the West. I ran over because I thought it was awfully close for a helicopter to be to the apartment complex. When I got to the window I moved the Blinds so I had a clear view of a bright Orange-Red Orb (Size like a large Basketball Sun-Orange glowing in color) floating upward and to the north very slowly like it was scanning as soon as I got a good look it dropped down and sailed to the north along the mountain side above the tree line about 30-40 mph. until it went out of site. The whole event lasted no longer than a minute. It seemed like it was some type of probe or Drone not of this world!
