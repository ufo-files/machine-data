{nd} Saw a group of ufo's flying in formation then merging.

Two other people and myself were driving on route 3 south going into Massachusetts from NH. Suddenly, my passenger points out this strange group of objects in the sky. They began zipping around in the sky, so I pulled over. I was in shock. They had merged from nine different objects, into three. I didnt know what to do, so I started driving again and got off the next exit which was maybe three hundred feet or so. I pulled over again and my friends were searching the sky's and eventually spotted them again. When I went and looked in my rear view mirror at them, the three merged into one, and with the brightest flash of light I've ever seen, they were gone. My friend in the back seat began to cry, and my other friend used thumbtacks on his wall to dictate the positions of the original group.
