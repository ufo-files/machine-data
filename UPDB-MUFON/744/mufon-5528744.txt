IT WAS COLD AND I HAD NO SHOES ON SO I CAME BACK IN AND CHALED IT UP TO A NUMBER OF SIGHTINGS OF THE SAME CRAFT.IT WAS ABOUT 400 FEET OFF THE GROUND AND ABOUT 50 YRDS AWAY.THE FLASHES WENT ON 5 TIMES AND STOPED.
