Bright light in sky changing colors over DeSoto Ks.

While driving in Overland Park Ks, I decided to shop at one of the local department stores. after exiting the store I noticed a really bright light in the sky to the west. I then got out my camera and attempted to take a photograph of the object. After taking a picture, we got in the car and drove in the direction of the object. We headed west to Desoto Kansas where we lost the object but we did manage to get a picture of it.
