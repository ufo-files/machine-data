Object traveling Eastbound following Pacific Coast Highway

I was out side riding the Yamaha 70 when I observed the silent traveling craft, proceeded to climb the roof to get a better view. The craft was gone as fast as I could climb the chimney. At least a footmball field long, Three circular lights at the rear of the craft, totaly silent. Second sighting was about 6 years later traveling Northbound over the ocean going towards Point Magu and I witnessed this from Hermosa beach. Interstingto say the least.
