3 lights, could be separate could be one, flew in from south hovering over tree tops, then disappeared east into the sky

In summer 2011, 3 of my friends and I were witness to Either One or 3 separate UFO, I’ll get to why I cant possibly be certain at the end of my events details. we were out In the yard of my friends house hanging out talking before one of my friends were leaving. When the event occurred, 3 lights flying south at break neck speeds it was faster than any plane and then it STOPPED (incredible) around 150 feet away, 3 very bright lights stopped all movement at the same time and just hovered down the road atop the trees, from the moment they flew into our line of vision we all stopped talking no one said a word I felt like I was being watched by someone And a very uncomfortable feeling washed over me as all I could do was just stare into the lights that had stopped, i genuinely felt a fear of the unknown, my gut churned, the hair stood up Over my entire Body as it tingled, I remember a buzz and hum noise in the air, no engines, no combustion, no air being chopped by propeller blades. No flames, it wasn’t loud but it was discernible from Anything that was audible before they had arrived. As we watched in silence and after what felt to be an eternity which must have actually only been seconds they zoomed east and into the horizon at what I can only say is the fastest moving object I’ve ever seen. When these lights stopped to hover they did not slow down, they stopped immediately there were no brakes no easing into a stop. I could not tell a shape of any craft. I saw only light, I’ve had 9 years to replay this in my head over and over again and Have also open to the idea that the three lights may have all been apart of the same craft they all moved precisely in sync and time with one another but again, I saw no craft behind the bright lights so my story Must remain 3 ufo because all I physically could see were 3 separate lights.
