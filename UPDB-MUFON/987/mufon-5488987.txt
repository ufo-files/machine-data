{rjl} It was a glowing orange orb looking bigger than the moon. Slowly descending until the observer couldn't see it past the treeline.

My neighbor described the sighting, she woke up around 5:30 to use the restroom and noticed the time then she saw out her bedroom window facing southwest, a glowing orange orb. She said it looked bigger than the moon and was descending slowly. She watched it in wonderment not knowing what it was. She expected to hear a crash but didn't witness a landing because it went behind trees. She didn't hear anything to suggest it landed or crashed.
