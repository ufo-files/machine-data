Observed hovering Triangular craft at Aerojet that followed me, then hovered north away from me.

I cannot remember the exact date of my sighting, but I believe it was in the summer of 2005. I was traveling eastbound from Sunrise Blvd on White Rock Rd late at night heading to Folsom when I noticed a triangular set of white lights following me. I decided to stop and when I did, it stopped too. It was maybe 500-800 ft high and maybe an eighth of mile to possibly a quarter of a mile behind me. It was dark, but I could still see that it was a black triangular craft. I would guess its size to be 30-60ft. or so, corner to corner. It was silent; I heard no noise from it. I got back into my vehicle and started driving slowly with my head out my window watching it. It began to follow me as I started driving, so I stopped again and it again stopped. I got a bit scared at that point and took off at normal speed limit and I watched it as it followed me for about another mile or so before it changed its path and I lost sight of it. White Rock Rd borders Aerojet and Lockheed Martin has a sight in Aerojet, so I assumed maybe Lockheed Martin had some experimental aircraft going on there or something.
