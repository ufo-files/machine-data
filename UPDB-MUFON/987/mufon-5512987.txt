(GS) Amber glow moving side to side smoothly.

*Outside at 4:30ish in the morning smoking a cig.
*I noticed a bright light on the horizon. It got brighter and my attention went directly back to the area and it was much closer now.
*I wondered if it was Mars but I also knew that Mars wasnt on the area in the sky at that hour nor was it on the horizon that low being that bright.
*It was so quiet there was literally no noise. It gave off a amber color below and the capsule top was a amber/white color that reflected off the outer layer of the disc. It moved up closer to me but in a zig-zag pattern that was so smooth that I knew this wasnt the behavior of any of our vehicles.
*I thought it was very beautiful and just stared at it as I could see something inside just looking at me. I wasnt sure what I could do and didn't really care because I had this sense that whatever this was, it wanted me to know that I was visible and that I could see it. I didn't feel threatened and I always wish I could go back to that day, since I always think about it.
*I lost sight of the object as it very smoothly moved over the hill and I ran after it but there was no sight when I got over that hill.
