A streak of light

I saw what looked like the moon and the sun rising on the eastern horizon. Thought it looked pretty cool. So I grabbed my mobile phone, pointed it in the general direction and quickly snapped 4 to 5 photos. It wasn’t upon review of the photos did the object appear. I thought, perhaps it was a lens flare at first. But even the photos where the sun was obscured by objects, i.g. trees, and billboards. This object appeared. Zoom in between the sun rising and the moon on the photo attached in this report to see what I captured.
