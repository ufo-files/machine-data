Upon passing, when I could look to my left to see it directly, it was about the size of a car, hovering in one place, and putting on a light show youd expect at a rave, not on a flying vehicle. I felt to communicate with it telepathically. My thought was "I cant believe what Im seeing" and I felt that an impulse came from the object to change my thought around it to "I know what Im seeing". I was elated and wishing I could stop and look at it without having to drive. I was on a section of freeway where I couldnt pull over safely, so I couldnt stop and film it. I sent the feeling and message of love and peace, thumbs up. My gut feeling is whatever the intelligent source of this vehicle was, is very benevolent.
