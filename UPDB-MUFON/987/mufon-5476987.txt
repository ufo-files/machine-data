Multiple lit up crafts hovering at different altitudes then traveled slowly in the same direction.

My girlfriend and I noticed a colorful light hovering on the horizon from our building. We grabbed binoculars and witnessed a triangle shapped object of pulsating light motionless in the air. Colors ranged from white, blue, green, red, orange, and yellow. The light was always one solid color and would sequence different colors in a fraction of a second. The light appeared around the edged of what looked like the triangular shape. The object then started to slowly move north through the skies at a steady and constant pace. Three more identical crafts were then spotted when they all started to move. All 4 were within visual contact with eachother and were flying at different altitudes and different distances from us. The event started around 2am and I can still spot two of the crafts as I write this at 3:30am! Still following the others north/northwest.
