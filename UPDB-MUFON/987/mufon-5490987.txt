{rjl} Two glowing lights at high speed.

I was heading north bound on route 35 with me my wife and children. My wife and I noticed two glowing lights at a very low altitude. When we got closer to the objects we realized that it was not a plane. There was one glowing white ball heading east and a red glowing ball right behind it just above the tree line. It appeared that the red ball of light was chasing the white ball of light. The white ball of light was much faster and proceeded to gain speed on the red ball of light. Both lights were moving at a very fast speed. I turned to a side road to follow but lost visual contact. Later that night me and my wife were home and at approx. 11:30 11:45PM we herd a loud helicopter passing over our house. We both ran outside thinking there might be a correlation and upon observing the helicopter we observed two shooting star like objects above the helicopter. There might have been no correlation but it was just weird to see two shooting star like objects above the helicopter after we just had the ball of lights experience. Thank you
