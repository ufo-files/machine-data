{cak} Hovering UFO seen clearly from upstairs window at 5 AM

I got up at 5 AM on Wednesday March 6, 2019, and went into my bathroom. I looked out of the bathroom window which overlooks a large lawn and horse pasture at the back (south side) of the house. We have 3 acres and there are woods beyond the back edge of the acre horse pasture. An object was sitting perfectly still over the woods. It was very clearly outlined but since I did not know how far away it was, I could not tell how large it was. It appeared quite large and fairly close.
It was the shape of a rectangle that was standing vertically on one short end, with each of it's corners cut off. The side that was faceing me was covered with many small lights that were sparkling on and off randomly. I watched it for a couple of minutes then went away from the window for a minute and then went back to see if it was still there. It was, but had moved slightly down to the right a bit. I then ran in and grabbed my robe and went downstairs to get my husband up. He has been sleeping in the living room because our little 15 year old dog who is blind and deaf, needs to go out a couple of times a night and it's easier for both of them not to have to climb up and down the stairs at night. 
We both went quickly into the breezeway to look out the sliding glass door, but the object was gone. It only took us a couple of minutes at most to get to the door, so the thing must have moved pretty fast.
