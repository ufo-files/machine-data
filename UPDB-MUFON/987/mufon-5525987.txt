Blinking light moving fast, changes directions a numbr of times, loops, disappears

I was outside having a smoke and looking east/southeast or so, 35-50 degrees (guessing here) up and see what I think is a plane in the distance. Just a flashing white light - appearing to be fairly high up - I don't see the other blinking lights like you see on a jet. It is moving Northest to southwest (left to right) and it is moving fast - the blinks are farther apart than the jets I normally see. It then abruptly changes direction almost back on itself, but not quite, just like a pinball bouncing off a bumper - now going west/southwest to east/northeast (right to left). It abruptly changes directions a few more times then actually follows a circular path which looks like a small loop, makes a few more direction changes, then heads either east or down and vanishes - no more blinking light. I see nothing in that part of the sky but the stars. I've never seen anything move like that. Like I said, at first I thouht it was a jet but it was moving too. It was at least a few mile east of me - maybe more. Just a blinking white light in the sky. My feelings at the time were - wow that's a fast plane then what the f?! What am I seeing? What did I just see? I looked at my watch as soon as it vanished - stopped flasing - not there anymore. I really have no idea what I saw - just sort of dumbfounded.
