{nd} White star like object with a white ring around it hovering and moving slightly in the night sky

My roommates and I were hanging out on a Saturday night watching TV. When I looked into the sky, I saw something that seemed like a star but was showing signs of movement and blinking in a sky with not much cloud cover. I thought it was a star, but as I mentioned it showed signs of movement as well as blinking in a pulsating manner. It exhibited a ring around it which seemed weird for a star as well as it seemed to low in the night sky. The white star like object with a ring around it pulsated as well as it moved slightly up and down as well as it moved left to right as it was hovering. It could be a satellite, but it did not seem like it had a path or trajectory. It felt weird, but I am interested in this subject; so, I was not emotionally affected besides a feeling of befuddlement. I decided to report it to the experts, so I went inside to write this report even if it was a low orbit satellite even though it did not seem to have a trajectory as I have mentioned. Well, it was interesting to say the least.
