Spherical, Black, glittering, Huge

- Saw it back in 1992
- I was on my way back to Dhaka (Bangladesh) from Jeddah (Saudi Arabia) on an aircraft of Kuwait Airways.
- The aircraft was flying 30000+ above the Persian sea.
- It was night, moon was in the sky.
- Can't exactly remember the time on the clock when I looked outside of the window and saw the object.
- It was probably a mile or more than a mile away from the aircraft, almost with the same altitude (as it seemed).
- Spherical in shape, black in color, it's outer body was glittering against the moonlight.
- The object seemed to be rotating from left to right.
- As I wasnt carrying a camera on the plane, couldnt capture the shot. BUT I am attaching the image of it made on MS paint.
