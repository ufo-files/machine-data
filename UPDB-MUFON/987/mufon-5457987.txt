{rjl} I witnessed a silent huge vertical triangle slowly rotate on both axis until it was flat like a plane would fly. It took off at incredible speed.

On September 23 2019 I observed 2 lights one over the top off the other. They were rotating from vertical to horizontal slowly. It wasnt until the third light came into view that I realized I was looking at a triangle. As soon as it rotated to a horizontal position it took off to the southeast at incredible speed. Minutes after it left several silent aircraft circled my property and the area the ufo was in. They appeared to have lights that would be associated with modern aircraft. At 10pm after not taking my eyes off the sky for 2 hours the ufo appeared again. This time it was horizontal in the sky traveling slowly directly over my property from the southwest. It appeared to be escorted by some of the response aircraft I'd been watching. After it was over the second witness and myself noticed an intense metalic taste in our mouths. I suddenly felt disinterested in the event and tired. The tired feeling quickly shifted to minor body aches and pains. I also felt as though I pulled a muscle in my back. I tried to stay awake because honestly I wanted to make sure my family stayed safe but it was near impossible. I've never felt so drained and exhausted.
