{jpg} Brightly illuminated cloud/mist with blinking strobe

I was hunting feral hogs at night on a large hunting lease on the Oconee river. I stopped on a stretch of dirt road to cut back some vegetation as a part of road maintenance. It was around 1:30 am. And at one point I stopped working and turned around to relieve myself. I immediately noticed an area of pine trees in close proximity with the tops of the trees brightly lit. 
My initial thought was someone on the nearby county road was shining a light bar up into the trees. I listened for the sound of a truck and realized the lighted area was very misty, fog like. I moved a short distance and saw a single flashing
White light flashing in a strobe light fashion.Very curious at this point, I got in my truck and made my way to the county dirt road. As I did this the bright area began to appear to move southwest( downhill) towards the river. The light was bright and constant, not jumping as if it came from a vehicle. At this point the blinking strobe was not visible.
I drove down the road towards the river and passed three gates to one side. I checked to see if the locks were secure and they were.
I reached the river and could see the "illuminated cloud" cloud across the river perhaps a 1/4 mile away. It remained stationary but the brightness of the cloud very slowly dimmed and brightened. I did not see the blinking light. I watched this for slightly over 30 minutes.
I then returned up the county road to where it met the highway. It was the highest vantage point and was recently clear cut. I scanned the area but saw no evidence at that point.
