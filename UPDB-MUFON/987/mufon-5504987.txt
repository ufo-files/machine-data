{nd} Me and about 12 to 15 people saw 3 UFO's w/beautifully bright colored lights dimming and brightening

I got out to allow my chihuahua "Chocolate" to urinate because he had been in the vehicle for awhile. There were 2 girls parked in the aisle ahead of us and the girl on the passenger side opened her door and asked me if I knew what that was and she pointed to the sky. There were several people in the parking lot now and they had gotten out of their vehicles and were all staring and pointing asking what is that ?

I have been watching and reading almost everything there is about UFOs, the Paranormal, etc.
