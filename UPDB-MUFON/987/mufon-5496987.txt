{dr}saw 3 ufos hovering over my mother & I for seconds

My mother & I were living out in the country around 1962 in the summer, while working on her small cactus garden in front of our house at the time around noonish I was like 14 years old good eye sight. I had a hoe in my hand looked up to take a breath and saw 3 ufos above us in a triangle setup 1 in front 2 in back about maybe a mile to 1 1/2 miles up from our location almost directly above us. It appeared that it was about 3 to 5 seconds and they went straight up and gone. After they left I looked down & saw my mother had fallen on a cactus and had cactus needles all on her neck. I also noticed I had on both of my shins a small like marble on both legs. I have told this story to other people and they say it probably did not happen. I am now 66 years old and in bad health & for some reason I have to tell somebody.
