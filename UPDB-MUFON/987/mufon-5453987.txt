{jpg} Unidentified object in my wife's photo of the Full Moon.

On Apr. 8, 2020 while taking pictures of the Full Moon at 5:39 A.M. My wife was going thru the photos to find the best shot. She handed me her phone and ask what is this in my photo. We both had been taking photos of the moon and we never saw anything unusual, until she saw some kind of object in her photo. We were not using any flash, so the object has lights or reflecting light from the moon. 
We have many photos before and after but only one photo that clearly shows the object. Some of the photos do indicate a tiny reflective spot far away. Strange that some of these pictures also have a blue orb that can be seen in different locations. We have kept all photos for more study or evaluation.
The 2nd photo is a magnification of the object and it is very strange looking and almost seems to have an exhaust or field of some kind. I am sure with the proper equipment the focus could enhance
these pictures.
