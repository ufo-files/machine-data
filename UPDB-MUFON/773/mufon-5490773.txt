I was 16 and listening to a talk radio station. In the summer I slept on a screened in porch. I noticed a bright light in the east sky. I thought plane or satellite. I was just looking straight where it appeared. Then in a flash (1 second )It flew to the southern sky and stopped then totally disappeared straight up. My room was approximately 12 feet by 16 feet.The entire sighting was less than 1 minute. as fast as this thing traveled it was basically impossible for our technology then and still not possible today.This is just a theory but it was lie some anti gravity propulsion. I am a qualified aircraft electronic mechanic. I have thought about this for years At the time I wasn't an a/c electronic mechanic. I was only 16. But my training came later I know what I saw.....The impossible....
