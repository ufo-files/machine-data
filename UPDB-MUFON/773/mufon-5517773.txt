Three turquoise colored lights at each corner of a triangle caught my eye as I was riding my bike on Gillham St. near 43rd St. in the midtown area of Kansas City MO. The lights came into view as I turned right onto Gillham Rd at 43rd St. The lights were only visible for a few moments as the object was travelling east south east and was quickly obscured by the tree-lined horizon. The very short time I could see it, it appeared to have just changed direction toward the east south east. I could see the lights for only a few seconds after it was obscured by the trees, but for a second or two, I could still see the lights through the trees - but then it was gone.
