{rjl} Six of us were at the Rock Tub hot springs.

We were watching the clear stars, airplanes, satellites, etc. I noticed a small white orb traveling in a nonlinear path right above us. After about 5 seconds it emitted a very bright white light flash/beam that seemed to be directed right at us (or over a very large area that we were part of). This light made everyone look at it immediately. After the one flash, it meandered around and faded away into blackness. A few people became very afraid after, I felt excited. It was impossible to tell how far away it was, couldn't get a scale as it changed size and intensity so fast. It seemed very high up though.
