{tjd} Very large gray disk object moving very fast toward ground appeared and disappeared five times in straight line slowing each time it reappeared.

I was traveling South on route 2 in WV when I noticed a fast moving large featureless disk streaking above Dulles Bottom Ohio. It was distinctly gray against a blue sky. All clouds were white only. This was no cloud.It disappeared and reappeared 5 times in a span of less than 10 seconds. It slowed each time it reappeared. It became stationary at an altitude of a few hundred feet above a hill. It then disappeared completely. The sky had broken clouds but the disk appeared in the clear blue sky. It was about 300 feet in diameter. No lights were observed. As it descended the bottom of the disk appeared to create some kind of thin discharge all over the visible bottom of the disk. This discharge like smoke disappeared over the edges of the disk.
