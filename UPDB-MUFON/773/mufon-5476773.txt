Huge craft illuminated bright white light uniform massive too big to be from earth

This was almost 20 years ago now - remember it like yesterday, I was in trosley (trottiscliffe) woods at the time standing talking to my friend on the top of the north downs at the edge of the tree line at the top of the escarpment. She had her back to the trees and we were chatting when very slowly and silently from over the trees a huge too big to be here massive circular disk craft came over the top of us it was emitting a brilliant white light similar to that of the moon when full and it's a clear night although it was perfect not a flaw anywhere if I held my arms up to the sky I had to hold them up like I was doing a jumping jack to fit the width of this thing in my arm span we were both totally silent as I had grabbed my mate turned her round so we were both just watching it in silence - it moved slowly over our heads and got further away moving fairly slowly --then it chucked a sort of fast without warning left and shot away over the horizon which we watched as it raced with the shape of the earth before disappearing altogether 
I can just remember knowing it wasn't from here and it was intelligent (controlled by something intelligent) 
Can't really stress enough how big this thing was
