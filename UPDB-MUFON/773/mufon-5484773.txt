Unexpected sighting in Kanata North

My husband took these pictures in the parking lot at 840 March Rd, Kanata, ON K2W 0C9. He was taking pictures of the sun dog as it was unusually bright. Later, he told me that he didn't notice the disk when he took the pictures.

When I looked at them later I found the anomaly. You be the judge! It looks to me like a saucer at varying angles and location. I circled them on the pictures to show were it was and labelled the images for ease of reference. I also included the original pictures unedited for confirmation that the pictures are not edited.
Thank you for your time.
