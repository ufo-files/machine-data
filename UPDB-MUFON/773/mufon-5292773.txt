Was subjected to scanning process

Approximately between the hours of 0200 and 0300 in July-August of 2014 I was subjected to what I believe was a scanning process.
This ocured in our home while I was sleeping out in the front room.
My wife had also sensed the back door opening, but paid no attention.
During this process I was partially awake (in sort of a suspended animation state).
I could see a silver colored object (approximately the size of a small bread box) hovering above me going from my head to my feet.
It was during this time I could not move or speak.

After this encounter, I tried calling out to my wife. All she heard were groans coming from me.
When she came out from the bedroom she found me on the couch in what looked like my body was "deflated".
She touched me and noticed that I was very cold. She thought I was dead.

I did regain consciousness and my deflated body had regained normal proportions.
