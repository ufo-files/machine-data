3 star like Lights straight line - form triangle then shoot off in different directions Nano second - gone!

I was walking dogs facing west around 7 PM on December 2, 2020 . Crystal clear sky. Between the trees and branches, I saw a light that looked like a star among other stars but it began to move south south west to south east. As it was about to come Into full view from behind the trees I noticed two identical Starlake lights following. Bluish white, now slightly larger and brighter than stars. The star like lights formed a triangle for just a few seconds and then in a split nanosecond they were gone.
