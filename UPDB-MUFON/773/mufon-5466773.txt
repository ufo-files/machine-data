Moving starlike light changed direction and intensity. Appeared to be diving, then went up fast and disapeared.

I was in my backyard looking at the sky when I noticed a moving light that appeared to be an airplane. It did not flash and loomed like a star. Then it slightly changed direction and speed, and seemed to be diving, with a brighter light. I was afraid it could be an aircarft falling, or a helicoptet, but it was too fast and distant, and there was no noise. Then, after a few seconds it seemed to have moved away, dimmed and disapeared.
Everything took about 10-15 seconds.
