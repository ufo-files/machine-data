{tjd} Three objects appeared in my living room.

I am watching television when three objects appear before my face blocking the screen. One object has wings and resembles a T. It is metallic. The second object is a Delta, an isosceles triangle and black. A third shape is disc-shaped or an oval and metallic. All three objects line up side to side in front of me, just floating, then accelerating toward me and disappearing into my chest. Seeing a Jungian analyst at the time, she is astonished, but she's also running a UFO abduction group, she suggests I join. I argue that UFOs are projections (per Carl Jung). I think my view has changed. I am slightly panicked when the objects start toward me. I decide to make this report watching the Hangar 1 second to last video of Season 2, where a woman witnesses a dome-shaped object move through her house.
