watched blink for 10 min. then straight up until gone in a flash

i was out hunting deer in Mexico just south of the Rio Grande river. i was sighting in a Turkey, when i look up about 45 to 50 degrees and see a super bright object changing colors. it waa silent, and just hovered up like it was a star.  i would estimate it was hovering at around 15 to 20 thousand feet. it was brighter than any star and changed colors. i watched this for 5 to 10 minutes. there was a mexican rancher with me, and i looked at him in amazement.  he looked back and told me "we see that all the time senor".

i looked back up and just a few seconds later, it shot straight up with no sound and disappeared.
