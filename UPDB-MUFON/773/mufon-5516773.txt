{dr}Object flickered like a flame, hovered, did 90 degree turns starting and stopping.

I was stepping outside of my house for a cigarette, when I looked up in the sky as I usually do to observe the stars, when I noticed a bright red light 70 degrees above the western horizon. I initially thought the object was Mars due to the reddish/orange color, but soon realized it was too bright to be a planetary object. The object hovered for about 30 seconds, then made a smooth repeating 90 degree motion back and forth from the point of original visual contact. It began to randomly flicker like a flame, and eventually came to a full stop, and finally dimming completely dark until no longer visible. I was awestruck at the sight, being as I frequently stargaze when weather conditions permit, but I was not shocked due to this being my second sighting since I was a young adult (15 years ago).
