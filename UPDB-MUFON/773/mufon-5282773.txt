Black triange with white light on each corner. Straight line flight. Low engine hum.

Object Sighted on right side of Brisbane Valley Highway, 5 mins south of Esk, QLD, in late 2021, about 10pm. Night sky was dark, blue-grey and I observed a black triangle with 3 stationery lights, one in each corner, flying in a south westerly direction. Initially I thought it might be one of the triangle UFO from tv, rather than a slow moving military plane, as was triangle shape, no distinguishable wings and no blinking lights, I stopped car, turned off engine, as fast as possible on hwy shoulder of to observe. Craft emitted low engine hum. After hearing the hum, figured it was a plane of some variety, so continued driving. After telling the story to a few people, I was recommended to make a report to MUFON.
