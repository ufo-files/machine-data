{rjl} A large object loud and with flashing lights awakened household and departed loudly. In like a circle. With a flash. I thought it was a garbage truck

Awakened by loud noise. Sounded like a loud garbage truck. Saw rectangular object with many flashing lights and lots of noise. Aware of a circular motion. Big flash of light. Metal blinds in room blew in and then back in place with a very loud bang. Everyone in household woke up. Two of 3 saw object in road and flash. Felt like it ascended but after the flash it was just gone and did not see it leave.
