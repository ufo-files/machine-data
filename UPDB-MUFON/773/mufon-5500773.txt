{rjl} swarm of lights entered through a window, hovered awhile near ceiling, descended in a ball and stung us

this is different than other ufo stories but i don't know what else the entities/experience could be labeled other than ufos...this took place in the third week of june in 1976 in my home, i was 5 years old...4 of my cousins were spending the night,we were in bed, 3 of us laying down vertically at head of bed and 2 horizontally at the bottom...we 3 at head of bed noticed a small (1-2 feet in diameter) cluster of lights in the bedroom window, eventually the lights came thru the window and hovered over the head of the bed...we were curious but not frightened...but the lights descended and enveloped us 3 and began to bite and sting...we pulled the blankets/sheet over us but the lights/entities behaved as if no blanket was there, they passed right through and continued stinging us...the crying brought my mother in, she didn't see anything and neither did my 2 cousins at the bottom of the bed...my mother moved the 3 of us from head of bed into the living room to camp out on the floor because we were afraid the lights would come back, i don't remember where they went when my mom came in...after settling us she went back to bed, we were then inside a sphere made of silvery lines of longitude and latitude maybe 10 feet or so in diameter ( its hard to judge size, everything seems big when ur little)the globe/sphere rotated slowly, it was peaceful and soothing...here's the part i debated about leaving out,the next day my mother was murdered by my stepdad,my 2 cousins who didn't see the lights were gone back home when this happened...i know about mass hysteria and realize a very long time has passed since this happened, but i still seek answers from time to time...my cousins remember this too ( the 2 at foot of bed were older, 9 and 11 i think)but the older says she remembers all the fuss because she was trying to sleep and we were 'flipping out'...maybe there are other stories out there like mine but i've never found one ...
