{rjl} Lights changing Shape

I was driving home on the freeway from Ludington, MI to Pentwater, MI. As I came around a curve I saw seven bright lights in the distance above the overpass. In that region there are many windmills used for energy, however the lights on those windmills are red and flashing. The lights I saw were in a slightly curved, but mostly straight line. They were bright, white, and did not flash. The three lights on my left side slowly rotated upwards while the three lights on my right rotated downwards. The light in the center did not move. I slowed down as I came up on my exit, it dipped slightly then flashed once and completely disappeared.
