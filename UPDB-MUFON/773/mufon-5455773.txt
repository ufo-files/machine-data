Orb

Hovering pulsating orb shape color similar to a large beach ball hovering about 30-50’ above the water Moving at approximately 16 to 18 knots could see reflection on water could see clearly underneath no wake. This sighting was in the South China Sea off the port beam. Our ship was an ammunition ship carrying nuclear weapons. Felt as if we were being probed. It followed for approximately 1 hr there wee other witnesses but I can only remember one others name.
