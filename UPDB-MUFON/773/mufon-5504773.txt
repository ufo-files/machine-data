{rjl} bright light appears and disappears 2/13/2014 lewisville tx

i was driving home around 3:15 am in lewisville going west on valley ridge. I had just crossed over Gardern Ridge and was about to turn on my street to go home and I saw an object in the sky and then disappear. the object was black and round, it had a green glow around it and a white light trailing it. it was moving very quickly south west and dissapeared. was only visible for a short amount of time. at first i thought it was a shooting star. but it was way to big to be one of those, and the green glow around it was kind of strange
