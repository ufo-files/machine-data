HOVERING ORANGE ORBS

I stopped my car to view these hovering orange orbs. At first there were 3 and then a final floating orange orb appeared after the others had faded away. I even pulled out my iPhone and filmed this event. Unfortuatnely, the iPhone did not record anything despite that it was definitely flashing the red "recording" button. Next, I called a friend in the area and he confirmed the sighting. These were very bright orange orbs moved very casually southward about a mile above. After floating for about 3 minutes the first 3 disappeared and another appeared. The final orb continued floating for about 2 minutes before it stopped glowing orange. I could distinguish a faint object where the glowing orange orb had been. This tiny dot of a thing floated for a little longer before becoming difficult to see.
