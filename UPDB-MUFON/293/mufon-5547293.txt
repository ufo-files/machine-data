Lights moving very quick and back and forth.

We were having a camp fire around 11:00 pm on sat night June 13Th out in the country so sky was full of stars and mostly clear when I caught something out of the the corner of my eye as I was looking at the stars. It was a set of three lights that moved very fast back and fourth in one area. I pointed it out to the other people there ( about 7 of us )and for almost 2 minutes it ( for a lack of better words ) danced in the sky. Then just disappeared. We were all stunned and in disbelief at what we saw and spent the next hour or so trying to come to some conclusion as to what it was. I live in the country were sky viewing is very good and me and my boys love to look at the stars. We see satellites almost nightly and planes both high and low altitude constantly. THIS WAS NEITHER.
