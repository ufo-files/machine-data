{dr}Pulsating circular craft in sky vanishes when military jets pursue

My step son and I were outside on the driveway, out by our vehicles when I looked up to see high above us what looked like a large star. It was moving north south east west very quickly, getting brighter then dimmer. I asked my step son to go get my binoculars to get a better look at it. Before he came back the object completely disappeared like it was never there. Suddenly 5 jets rocketed into sight. I was too busy searching for the object to have noticed the jets approaching so fast. They were gone across the sky faster then I've ever seen a plane fly. I didn't get a picture of the object but I did get a picture of the jets that I assume were military trying to intercept the ufo.
