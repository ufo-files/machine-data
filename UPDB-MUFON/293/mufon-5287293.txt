Tic tac kinda floating in a straight line

I was sitting on my deck which is on the second floor. I have a fantastic view of Carson City Valley skyline and sky.
I heard a helicopter going over my apartment complex. (Going from East to West)
I looked up in the sky directly above my head and yelled to my husband to “look! What is that!?! At first I thought it may have been the ISS but it was very white in color and seemed to have a glow around it. I picked up my phone to immediately video it. I also took a couple of pics. It moved kinda fast and seemed to float while moving in a kinda straight path. It went out of my view after the second pic . I started to video, stopped then was able to take two pics before I lost it. I am not sure if it went straight or up in sky further after it left my sight.
