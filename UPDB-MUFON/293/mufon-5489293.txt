{nd} Directly above downtown Columbus...shown a large purple light but only for a moment. Other witnesses, but not many people around looked up ya know

Very briefly, I was in the Columbus Commons when I saw what appeared to be a falling meteor SE of the city, when the object then appeared to fly in a straight line very high up. Assuming it to be a satellite or some such other space traffic, I pointed it out to my companions. Suddenly, it lit up brightly and shone a purple-ish beam down towards us. The lights then went off and it sped on Northward.
