{cak} Bright blue/teal light hovering and shooting across the sky

I was in my driveway about to unload my groceries when I noticed a very bright blue/real light in the sky. I also noticed a smaller red light next to it, so I figured it was a plane but noticed that it wasn't moving and the lights weren't blinking like a planes lights usually do. Then suddenly the red light dissappeared. The light remained still for about 2 minutes, I kept telling my husband to come look at it. Suddenly the light seemed to shoot straight up in the sky a few feet and then stop and sit still. At that point I knew it wasn't a plane and yelled to my husband to get over there and see this! A few seconds after my husband got over there the light seemed to jump to the left, we thought it was gone. Then all of a sudden it dropped or fell or dove down in a diagonal line towards the ground and I could hear a buzzing or zip type sound as it dove down and then it disappeared!
