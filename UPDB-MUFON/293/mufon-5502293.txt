{nd} Looked like a star but moving at a high rate of speed. Resembled the iss when it passes over.

Walked outside to get in my truck to go to work. Mars was visible in the sky and I was looking at it. Noticed a airliner flying over after taking off from Hobby airport. Further above the airplane I noticed what looked like a star but moving towards the NE at a very fast pace. It was out of my range of sight very quickly. I thought it might be the ISS but that is usually brighter when it passes over.
