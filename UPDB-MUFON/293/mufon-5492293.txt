{rjl} Triangle shape with white circle attached on top. Triangle red but turning orange pulsing light.

Approx. midnight. Went to let dogs out and they wouldn't go out. I turned on back yard light and noticed a light coming from above trees in back yard. High up on top of trees. Felt sick to my stomach. Turned off all lights in kitchen to see better and saw an orange triangle with a white circle light attached to it, on top of it. It made no noise. The triangle shape would pulsate from Orange to red. My first thought was to run and hide as I was terrified but I thought this is a UFO and I really need to look at it so I can remember it and try and document it. Right after that I saw a white ball of light shoot out of it and roll down my lawn go under my fence into my neighbors yard. I got my cell phone, put it on video but it would not work. The same happened with the picture option. I have a camera but for whatever reason, fear I guess it did not occur to get it. I came in the house, locked the doors, went to bed with all lights and TV on. I will have my camera ready tonight if it comes back.
