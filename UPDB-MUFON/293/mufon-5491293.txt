{cak} A seemingly transparent orb was floating or flying in a straight line above me

I was working at the time, and it was particularly hot that day. I looked up for some reason, can't remember why. I saw this orb, seemingly transparent, but I could see it. It was floating through the clouds and going in a straight line. It looked small but it was very high above me. I ended up writing it off as it just being too hot and I saw something, but I've never seen anything quite like that before in my life.
