{cak} 2 Planes chasing craft

I was in the garden to watch my dog before going to bed. I often look up at the sky, tonight like every other night I looked up. This time upon gazing at the stars I saw one begin to move, rapidly heading north. As it approached another ‘Star’ the second one moved out of the way. They then circled around each other as the first craft continued its path north. At this point 2 planes were heading in the same direction, then the second craft starting heading north too. I watched as I saw these 2 planes chase the craft. I tried to send positive thoughts towards the sky as I then got 3 very bright flashes of light blinked back at me from the starting location of the first craft. Again I thought the words “hello” I then got 2 flashes of light, Then 4 flashes. I followed my dog outside as something caused him to give chase. Could there be more to this?
