{cak} lights that I have never before encountered

Lying in bed about midnight, I could not sleep. Looking out my window facing near the northeast, it was there. A solid bank of red lights, the object moving slowly north. Stopping in mid flight, it did a slow "back-flip" (inversion?) and suddenly turned yellow; still in its back-flip, it suddenly turns green and slowly spread two "wings" and reverses its flight, retracing its course towards the Hot Springs S.D. hospital. 

It appeared to be less than two miles away when I saw it that evening. I had no recollection of this even until the afternoon the next day when I saw an AHC program on TV discussing UFO's; I then immediately told my wife, brother, and brother-in-law that day (3-11-2018), and chronicled the event.
