Observed 4 lights that were not stars.

Driving towards the jersey coast over the ocean I observed 4 separate round to sphere like lights all in different shapes and colors that moved irrationally and iradictly from North to South. I observed them driving east heading into the town of Seaside Heights, NJ and managed to snap a few photos of these objects. Now I'm somewhat of an astronomy hobbiest and cosmology and what I do know is that these were not stars, helicopters, planes or drones of any kind due to the size and the way they moved. They were pulsating and seemed to change various colors and 3 of the 4 objects were in a triangular pattern with the 4th object located south of the other 3. Was definitely something I feel you should know about. Thank you
