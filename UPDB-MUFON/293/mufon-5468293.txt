{td} Bright yellow-orange pulsating light

My girlfriend and I were driving home from getting food due west. We looked in the sky and saw a bright yellow-orange light in the sky. At first we thought it was a planet or a star but it was too large. We got home and went to the back door to see the light. It hovered at a 35 degree from the horizon due west. The object pulsated, getting brighter and dimmer. Afterwards it slowly moved below the treeline and got dimmer and we could no longer see it so we went inside. There is a video but it it hard to see. I was filming on a potato phone but I tried.
