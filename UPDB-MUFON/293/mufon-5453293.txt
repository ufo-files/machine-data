Lights in the sky

We were looking at the moon when we saw a flash of light. Then what appeared to be a satellite but then in the same exact spot in the sky another flash and what looked like a satellite. Then we proceeded to watch at least 24 objects appear. They were spaced the same distance apart and traveled in the same line and same direction. They did not fade out they just traveled to far to see with the naked eye. Tried to take video, however they did not show up.
