A fleet of lights going in the same direction two fly around in the sky

Was on the phone to my friend Emma at 21.00 my girlfriend run into the garden and said her friend K who lives in the area said look at the sky so I did and saw a fleet of lights travelling in the same direction it looked like the stars were moving at first. My girl shouted at me to get off the phone and film it with my phone. So I told my friend Emma what was going on hang up the phone and tried to film the sky but the phone went to photo then I changed it to video but it wouldn’t work I couldn’t see anything but black so I left the phone to witness it with my eyes. As me and my girl run from the back garden to the front the 3 children came running out and was also witnessed what was going on. My girl started calling other people in the area to tell them to look at the sky this went on for a while the baby woke up so I was in and out then when I was in she said a plane came all I saw was what looked like smoke blocking the sky where the activity had been. My girl was still on the phone to her sister who said she still had a visual on something when we did not. I made a video to record the event made a few phone calls then went for a walk down the road to see if I could see anything else.
