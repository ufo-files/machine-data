I took a sequence of photographs with a zoom and the object appeared on one of them. I had not realized it until now. three years later.

The event happened three years ago. I was taking pictures of a full moon from my house on the south valley in Caracas, Venezuela. I took a series of 12 pictures with my zoom. I did not realize the appearance of a disc-like shape on the upper right part of one of them until now. I started looking at those pictures a couple of days ago and saw that strange object.
