Flash of light in sky far off, then ufo moved in straight line approx. 20x faster than a satellite

I was in the hot tub looking up at the stars at around 4:15 am. It was a very clear night and full moon with only light clouds. I observed a satellite fly by (looked like a SpaceX satellite in size/distance). All of the sudden I saw a flash of light, higher in the sky to the Northeast outside of my view. I quickly looked up to see where it came from, then I saw an object, the same size, color and distance as the satellite, move in a similar but more horizontal path but ~20x faster. It traveled in a straight line, and then had completely vanished out of sight within a few seconds. There was no trail on the object the way a meteorite or shooting star would appear. It moved slightly slower than a shooting star, and did not change size or shape.
