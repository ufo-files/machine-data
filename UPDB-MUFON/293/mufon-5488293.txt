{cak} was checking eclipse, had camera, saw unusual light and snapped picture

It was the night of the lunar eclipse. I was going out side about every 15 to 20 minutes with my camera to hopefully get a few good pictures of the eclipse. I went out at 9:00pm and could barely see the moon, it was still rising to where I could see it well. I saw a light move quickly from the south to the north following the horizon arc like the space station does when it passes. I thought maybe it was a satellite. Then about 9:30pm I went out again and looked up and to the east, saw the moon had risen more, I snapped a picture, looked up and snapped another picture. I went back inside and went to bed. Last evening, my husband and I viewed the pictures and there was an unusual glowing object in the first picture. It's not in t he second picture taken moments later. We zoomed in on it and we're not sure what it is. I don't know if the camera flashed or not when I took the pictures, I thought I had the flash turned off. Thanks.
