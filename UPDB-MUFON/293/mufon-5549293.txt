bright white, looked to be solid, stayed in one place entire time

My husband and I were up early this morning sitting on the couch. My husband looked out the window and saw a bright white "orb" in the sky. I was convinced that it was just a star, but to him, it looked too close to the earth to be a star. It was also the only thing in the sky other than the moon. This orb, or sphere, did not move. It stayed in one exact spot the entire time we watched it. We videotaped it for over 30 minutes and it never moved. Eventually, it got too light to see it. After viewing the video, it seems even less likely that it is a star. The UFO seems very "solid" in its state--when we zoomed in on it, there was a black outline to it, as if it was a solid object. If it were a star, we would have expected to see a more blurred shape, due to the "gaseous" quality of stars. It is very exciting to witness something like this because it is definitely out of the ordinary. We are very interested to know if there are others who may have witnessed the same thing we did.
