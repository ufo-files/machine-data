Orb of Light that had a visible structure

I went outside to smoke a cigarette and texting a friend of mine when I seen a bright light out of the corner of my eye, I looked up and there was this bright object hovering above a mountain. It was very cloudy out that night and there wasn’t a plane or helicopter in site. I continued to watch it and it would brighten up and vanished on and off. I took some pictures of if and after I did 2 other ones were accompanying it. I heard a ringing in my right ear which usually happens when a UFO is around. I eventually went in my house when it permanently vanished and cleared up the photo, zoomed in on the photo and in the light orb I discovered the structure that was in it. In these photos you can see the Greyson silver dome with green trim and green circles which I assume are windows.
