Observed dark triangle - extremely large and hovering

I reported this when it happened and then did not follow up as my mother went into hospice immediately afterward and I was focused on that. I always thought it was too late but last night heard [name moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] on a podcast who mentioned a man reporting an incident that happened in 1989 so I wanted to bring this up again.
I was pet sitting at nearby home and put dogs out on last visit for the night - one dog stood up from laying and stared in direction I didn't see anything and forgot about it until I was leaving and noticed very bright lights that were not usual as I did this trip every night - when I entered the main road I saw light source and the extremely large black triangle just hovering with no sound. I drove slowly and wondered why no one else noticed it then realized there weren't any people around - it was sort of late on a Monday night. I dont' know why I didn't take a pic but I never thought of it and went home just around the corner then decided to go back and take a picture. I immediately went back and it was gone. I drove all around the area and never saw it again. I know what I saw and and am 100% positive that I saw it and it was not a plane or anything like that (it was humungous in size and not moving that I could tell).
