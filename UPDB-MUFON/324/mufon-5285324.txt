Orbs. Looked white or silver. Possibly reflective

Stopped to take a break on a bike ride on the south east side of town, under a small structure for shade. Something initially caught my eye as I stepped from under the shelter, almost directly overhead. What I first saw was what I thought was a star but it was just after noon and it was fairly close to the sun. Anyway , I grabbed my cellphone off my bike and tried to locate the orb again and couldn’t see anything for a few seconds. I did spot again a tried to take a video. It was so hard to see against the blue sky and I was shielding my phone with the roof of the shelter. Anyway, wasn’t sure I caught anything on video, but when I got home I reviewed the footage and got more than I thought, with there being four orbs in one frame with one moving north while two others move to the west. One of these was the first one I saw and it moves off much slower than the second one. When I airplayed it to my TV, I really noticed the others after the first one . Most of the action seems to occur in the first six or seven seconds
