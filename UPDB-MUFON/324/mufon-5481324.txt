I filmed the Moon with 400mm tele lense, after reviewing the material I found this unexpected event.

as a hobby photo and videographer use my equipment frequently for different kind of takes. My interests are focussed on timelapse videos, macro, nature and astronomy.

this time I set the camera to make some takes from the Moon in Third Quarter descending the blue sky with 400mm lense.

After reviewing the take (10minutes) I found this unexpected event recorded.

The object reached the camera field of view from SW direction.
The Timecode 04:58:47
Then Timecode 04:59:37 start the turn approx. 101 northwards. Cross the Moon and disapear at Timecode 05:03:34 from the camera field of view.

The Object left no kon-trail and even in 400% magnification no wings or other features are visible.
