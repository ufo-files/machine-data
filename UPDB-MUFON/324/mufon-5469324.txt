{jpg} Shinny silver disc shape moving NE over one mile up.

My wife and I took my grand kids and daughter and son in law to Knott's Berry farm on 4/3/18. A little before 6PM my daughter wanted to go on supreme scream. I sat on the ground next to my youngest grandson falling asleep in his stroller. I kept looking up waiting to see my daughter go up on that insane ride. As I was waiting I noticed a silver disc very high up moving slowly north east direction. I seemed to lose sight of it for a second and then it would come back into sight but still going in the same direction. When every one met up I told them what I saw and everyone just looked at me. lol. I know what I saw and it wasn't an airplane.
