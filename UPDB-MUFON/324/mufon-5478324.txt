{jpg} I was laying in bed, saw small star like things moving....took iphone to try record.

It was about 12:30 a.m. - - I was awake trying to get a raccoon to leave the property as it was rooting around the property in an attempt to get into the chicken cages. So, since I was awake in bed with the sliding glass door open, I had an excellent view of the stars on a very clear night. There were many big stars and small stars. Upon looking at the stars, I noticed that a couple of the smaller ones appeared to be moving, and in vey strange ways. Like looping and stopping and hovering and then glowing and zooming off again. After clearing my eyes a couple times, I decided to take my iphone out on the deck and hold it up in an attempt to record the moving star like objects. I held the iphone up with two hands above my head after pushing record. I waited about a minute and stopped recording as the screen was black, I could not see what I was recording. When I played the video back, I could see that I had captured something.
