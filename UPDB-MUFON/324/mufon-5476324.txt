{rjl} Dull yet obvious light hovered and then moved with a loop and disapeared up.

I was camping at Racliff Lake in campsite 12 and it was about 8 or 9 at night. I was observing the stars when a light blue strobing light appeared behind the trees. It appeared east and my body, laying down with my head looking north, the object moved left made a loop and then disappeared up. In complete awe and with my mouth open I tugged on my girlfriend to look up but it was too late. At first i thought it was a beam of light from a flashlight on the trees but the light shined down not from under or from another side of the lake.
