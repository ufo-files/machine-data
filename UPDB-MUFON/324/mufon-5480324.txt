i witnessed a et in a school when it was closed on a sunday afternoon in 1981 i was actually face to face with a winged et with only a small window between our faces i have never been so terrified in my life

I was riding my bike on a sunday afternoon and was driving in a parking lot at nova scotia community collage in Dartmouth I know how this is going to sound and I cant believe I'm writing this but anyways I leaned my bike up against a rear entrance door so I could stand on the crossbar and look in a very small window with wire mesh. that is when I saw a humanoid with what looked like a cape or wings on. at that time it looked directly at me and in a fraction of a second we were face to face looking at each other threw that small window I can remember trying to scream in terror but nothing would come out of my mouth. I fell off the crossbar of my bike and ran. to this day I have no idea how it moved so fast and why I could not scream but the worst part about this experience is I can recall exactly every detail of its face and I'm sure its not from this planet. thanks for taking the time to read this I would like to say this is not a hoax or a lie I do feal a lot better getting this off my chest finally telling somone really helps thankyou
