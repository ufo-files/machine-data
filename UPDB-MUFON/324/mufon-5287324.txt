Bright unknown object in both day and night sky

Bright object sighted in afternoon sky that appeared north of the Sun. Object appeared solid, stationary and reflective. We watched the object until going to bed around 10:00 PM. By 10:00 it had moved more into the true north sky. It appeared very bright both day and night. It did not look like any satellite or celestial body I know. I called all three of our local news stations and they had no information, stating they had received no prior calls. I checked the sky again this morning upon rising and could not find it.
