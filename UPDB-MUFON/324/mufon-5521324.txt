Beam of light about 20ft wide

Walking down britainna road coming back from main street with my teenage daughter we could see a strong beam of light about 20ft wide looked to be beyond the green hill caravan site in the fields or the river beyond that it was at low tide so the river would have been very low and no boats would have been on there. Could here a faint bleeping a bit like a reversing warning from a lorry but slower this and the beam of light stopped after about 2 minuets with the beam of light fading upwards. There was only light cloud cover and the sun had set but could not see anything in the sky.
