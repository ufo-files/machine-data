{cak} My wife and I had seen it.

My wife and I were driving from Belfast where we went to do work on her Grams home for the day. We were in my Chevy Silverado going Route #3 West towards Augusta when I noticed two square shaped White halogen like lights above the tree top on the left side of the road within an 1/8th mile from us South West over Saint George Lake My wife said it must be a tower I said no it's not a tower like light pattern or red lights. I said it's not a plane or a helicopter because I know what those light patterns are I spent 8 years in the Army with the Calvary in Texas and the 152 F/A from Maine. The lights hovered just above the tree tops and switched directions North EAST to South West and kept elevating and descending. We watched it for a good 15 Minutes as it pulsated two lights that were large on the ends and had smaller lights in between it looked like 5 or 6 of them but it also looked like the craft was spinning Two gig lights on the end to one on the end with 5 or 6 smaller tailing. I could sort of make out the shape of the craft with the design of the lights it sort of looked like a whoopee pie. I asked my wife if she had a camera as she always carries one but on this occasion she did not. I tried following the craft but the speed was unreal I have never seen anything like it as it changed directions then I almost rear-ended someone while I was doing 80mph trying to watch the craft then I got caught up in traffic and totally lost it. We tried paying attention for it when we came into clearings but it was gone.
