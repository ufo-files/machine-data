{cak} Large orb that could be taken for a weather balloon. Bright white in daylight sky before sunset. Color changed to orange as sun set. Stationary.

Around 6:30 in the evening I went into my backyard to move a lawn sprinkler. Observing the still daylight evening sky and horizon as usual, I saw a bright orb like light. It was bright white and large, too large and in the wrong position to be a planet I thought. I wondered if it might be a balloon of some sort. I watched it with some curiosity for awhile noticing that it didn't move at all. I went inside and brought my mom out into the yard to have a look at it. She agreed that it was an unusual sight and oddly bright. We joked about it being aliens. As the daylight faded a bit I noticed that the still motionless orb turned a sort of orange color. I asked my mom if she saw the color change. She said she did. We stopped the observation to go get something to eat. As we drove down the street, still being curious, I kept glancing up to see if the orb was still visible. It was, but seemed dimmed and it also began to look bluish. The sky was rapidly progressing to darkness and as I drove I had to fight the urge to look up to see if it was still there, while driving through traffic. When we reached our destination, I looked up to the place where the orb had been and still saw a dim light that was very much like a star in the darkened evening sky. At this point I let the observation go as many stars were in the sky and nothing more of the unusual light I'd seen was visible. I'd really like to know what it was.
