Witnessed two initially then one changed direction. When filming one another showed briefly in my camera view.

[time & location moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] 

The objects were first on my left line sight of significantly above the tree line. They were very bright and were moving slowly. They were high up as a silhouette of an airplane with flashing lights went in front of one of them and it appeared small. They were both oval, but so bright that it was difficult to make out the edges. Then the lead one began to fly to the right of the other one and go East while the other stayed on path to North. It then appeared to stop. 

The one that turned to the East appeared to flip? It went from a sphere to more of a bell shape. It then rolled until a flat underside appeared, at first, I thought it must be a plane and was mistaken. The bright white glowing lights lessened, and 3 green circular lights lit up solid on the flat side. They did not flash like airplanes, but did flash. It continued to move East. I was on the phone with my husband and was telling him what I was seeing. 

I was travelling at 60 miles an hour and could not pull over quickly. I continued to travel North parallel with the other that was moving farther away and higher. By the time I pulled over on the bridge over West Point Lake, my video captured it much smaller than it was when I first saw the two. I was able to video and zoom in. It had stopped moving for a while. The light, even though it was far away, was very bright. It was when I was filming that I spotted a 3rd one that glowed more reddish than the one I was filming. It shows briefly in my video to the right edge of the shot.
