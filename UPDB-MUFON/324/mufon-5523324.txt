Brightest light in sky traveling straight path at steady rate. Went parabolic while simultaneously dimming before completely disappearing into thin air. Brightest light went out. Weird!

My husband and I were sitting on our deck outside while talking about planes and their crusing altitude when we noticed the brightest light in the night sky overpowering all other extraterrestrial bodies. It was moving parallel to the milky way in a easterly direction. It travelled quickly but at a steady rate. We thought it was strange it was so bright and not blinking nor had a red light like all planes do. There was no sound emitted. We watched it for almost a minute before it dimmed then rapidly acended in an exponential curve outward as it completely disappeared into nothing, and we could no longer see it. We both just stood in amazement immediately confirming the details of the event to each other. We were excited that we had both witnessed together our second ufo event.
