(GS) Over 10 Years of Commercial Airline Survalance Video

Video has been used in commercial airline security and maintenance since 9/11 both inside aircraft and outside the aircraft as well. Airlines found it pointless often counterproductive to turn this video footage over to government(s). They are usually destroyed however the most compelling encounters are archived by the airlines for future documentations. Airline pilots, maintainer crews and ground crew know the cameras are there but cant tell you what happens to the video. This video isnt even used in court cases as evidence to prove the airlines case against unruly passengers.
