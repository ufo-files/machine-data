Bright light in the sky

I was heading to work, going north I noticed the light in the sky I thought maybe it was a plane at first but it was really bright and big. I made a right turn into my jobs parking lot, my car was facing east I stop the car and continued to look North. After watching it for about 30 seconds I found it very odd because it was so bright and it seemed to be standing still. At this point I took out my phone and started recording it, shortly after I started recording it seemed to start moving. At one point I thought it was a plane and the light just looked bright because I was facing it but then I noticed when it started to head east that the entire unknown object was still lit up and that I found very odd. At no point did I see any kind of structure it just seem to be bright all over. I ended the video because it was going behind trees and I figured I would drive up further in the parking lot and wait for it to come out from behind the trees. It just disappeared. Vero Beach airport is not far from me and I see planes all the time none of them look like this.
