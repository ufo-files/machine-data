patterned disc with symmetrical properties seen coming down out of no where

As I was at work one morning I had a minute to take a picture of a nice sunrise. As I was taking my phone out I saw movement in the sky. I looked up as I slid my phone into camera mode and this green disc descended out of no where. I took the picture and it was showing up there too. As I was looking at the picture it had ascended and just vanished out of sight. I was stunned. I zoomed in on my picture and noticed very strange qualities I couldn't describe.
