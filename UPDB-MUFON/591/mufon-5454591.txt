{cak} Counted 31 spaced apart entering night sky in a line from west to East in southern sky. Husband saw one after waking him up.

Sat down on porch at 5:30 am. Look to south and saw 2 lights one following the other. I kept watching and there were more following each other spaced somewhat evenly apart. I counted 25 and went in and woke Husband, there were three more that passed but he only saw one of them. They would just appear west high in the night sky and descend in an arc to the east. And fade out. They traveled quite a ways before fading out. These objects all followed each other, with the exception of three that were much higher and a bit more to the north. They were all the pretty much the same brightness and size. They did not flash or make any sound. Their speed was all about the same not really fast, like a glide. I saw a total of 31 lights, the last few were farther apart or late coming into view all from the same entry point. This is not the first time I have witnessed lights in the night sky. This is the first time I have reported it.
