8 orbs appearing and acending

Was looking out my mothers tenth floor balcoiny window when I noticed over a period of about 5 mins. low in the nothern sky 8 bright white orbs just appear out of nowhere and begin to move horizontally in the sky at a slow speed and then rise rapidly straight up above the cloud cover. They lit up the clouds as they moved through them.
