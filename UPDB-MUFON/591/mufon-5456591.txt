{jpg} Round Yellowish white object appeared in sky then descended toward lake Grapevine and disappeared behind tree line.

I was on my back porch around Midnight or so. when I noticed a round yellow-white, glowing ball appear in eastern sky. It seemed to be over lake Grapevine -- about 2 miles away. It then descended behind tree line and disappeared. it took about 5 seconds My first thought was that it was a shooting star -- but I've seen shooting stars -- that's not what they look like. This thing remained constant in its size and shape and appearance as it traveled. And its descent was too slow to be shooting star.
