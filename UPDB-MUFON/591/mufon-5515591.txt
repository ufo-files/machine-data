(GS) extremely bright light in the sky

I was sitting on my back deck with a north to south view just glancing up at the stars since the clouds had cleared out. Something catches my eye off to the side, so I immediately looked almost dead north where there is a very very bright white light in the sky. It just appeared and then it appeared falling towards the ground, while fading to nothing. I immediately ran in, noting the time, to grab my tablet. Turned it on and loaded Google sky so I can figure out the exact direction I saw this, and see what is in that part of the sky. I also checked spaceweather.com to see what satellites might be visible to see if that was a possibility, but it indicated no visible satellites in the area, and with it almost being dead north of here going down towatds the ground, I don't know of any satellites in a North-South orbit right now.
