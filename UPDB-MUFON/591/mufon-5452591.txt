Round black object flying to the east

I was standing in my garage with the door open watching an incoming storm when I saw a round black object flying quickly in a straight line to the east until it went behind some taller buildings on mall road. I was alone when I saw it. It had no lights on it and it was flying lower than planes when they come in to land but I have no idea how high. It was going faster than the planes I see on the flight path but not by too much.
