(GS) Circle of lights appeared then disappeared.

On a boat for a fireworks cruise in the Merrimack River near the Route 1 bridge, in Newburyport, MA. While looking east toward the Plum Island/Atlantic Ocean area, a single red light was observed. Several more lights appeared in a perfect circle around the center light. The center light then disappeared followed by the circle of lights one at a time. Lights like this were never observed in this area before as verified by the experienced captain of the boat. The area was busy with other boats and thousands of people waiting for the fireworks to start, however the fireworks were being set off in the opposite direction. So I would assume most people were not looking in the direction of the apparent UFOs. The attached pictures were taken of the lights but are not of good quality since the boat we were on was moving. The town below the lights is the waterfront section of downtown Newburyport, MA.
