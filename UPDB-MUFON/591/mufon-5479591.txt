{cak} I heard a large rumbling i looked outside and saw 7 black unmarled helicopters surrounding a disc like ufo

I was eating dinner with my fiance in saint paul when i heard this super loud rumbling above the house. It sounded like a helicopter had just landed on top of the house. I ran outside to see what it was when i got outside in the distance about a mile or so away i saw this black or dark gray like circular disc that had about 6 lights on it blinking and 7 black unmarked helicopters surrounding in a line. All 8 objects were moving a steady distance in a straight path when all of a sudden the disc took a sharp turn and just vanished. I kept watching the helicopters that then retracted in a straight line and continued off into the distance. About a month before that i was driving down 94 east with my fiance and saw the same 7 black unmarked helicopters traveling in a straight line one after the other. I am still just in awe. In the picture i cropped it on Instagram to see if there was any markings on the closet helicopter.
