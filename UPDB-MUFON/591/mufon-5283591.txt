Large portion of sky lit up orange

I was driving home from work on a cloudy night. I was driving east down Etowah and noticed a big part of the sky looking to my south east was lit up orange that has never been lit up before in that direction. There is no town or anything that way close enough to light up the sky like that. I had absolutely no idea what it could be. My phone was in the phone holder on my dash so I took a quick picture. I think I noticed it in the sky about 1.5 miles before I took the picture. Once I got to the next intersection that was .7 miles away from when I took the first picture; I pulled over in a parking lot on the north west corner of the intersection(156th and Etowah) and turned to face west so that the light was on my side (drivers side) of the car to get a video of it. I did not get out of my car. I rolled the window down, took a picture and then the video. The pictures and video look farther away than it was, and smaller than it was in person. The light seemed to have gotten bigger or closer to me quicker than I expected it to. When I was stopped looking at it there was no sound and it was not moving. I headed on home, east, after I got the video. I pulled over and stopped when I got to the next hill (about 3 miles east), which is a lot higher elevation than where I was pulled over to take the video. I should've been able to see that light, but it was gone. My pictures and video all have details of exact time and location of where they were taken. I will upload the pictures and video and a screen shot of the details of each.
