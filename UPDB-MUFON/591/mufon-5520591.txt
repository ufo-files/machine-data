Photo of 7 lights by Eiffle Tower seen from Palais de Tokyo 21.12.10@23h02

It was near Christmas last year, my last night in Paris before going home for the holidays. I was invited by a girlfriend to an artists party the Palais de Tokyo (overlooks the Eiffle Tower on the other side of the river). At the begininng of the party we got some drinks and headed towards the balcony. There were lots of people, movement and noise at the party. I saw through the balcony window the eiffle tower starting to flash (it flashes every hour on the hour for ten minutes). My girlfriend and I stood by the balcony and I wanted to take a picture of the flashing lights as they seemed very bright. It was also raining so we couldnt go onto the balcony and there was a security guard facing us blocking our entrance onto the balcony. I put down all my coats and bag and I took a photo of the lights with my blackberry. I was very happy at the time for seeing the lights. 

I did not notice these 7 lights to the right of my photo until my Greek friend contacted me the day after saying what on earth had I uploaded onto my facebook page (it had appeared on his newsfeed). He called and called trying to contact me and finally explained to me saying how significant this was (the 7 lights, the fact it was 21st december (mayan calander), he says they were coming to look and check up on us. and they came to a prominent place - eiffle tower, and came at a time when they knew all eyes would be on the flashing eiffle tower lights. thus distracting our attention).
