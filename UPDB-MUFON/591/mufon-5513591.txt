{dr}saw an object in carefree, arizona tonight. it changed shape, no noise, and saw green lights coming from the neighbors backyard

I was in the backyard at my house in carefree. I was looking up at the stars when I noticed something moving around that wasnt normal.it was a strange looking light darting back and forth across the sky, not making any noise. It was close enough to hear any noise the craft was making. Then all of a sudden the shaped changed to a central yellowish light with multiple rays coming out of it on all sides. At this moment I began to see strange glowing lights coming from my neighbors backyard I couldnt get a direct line of sight, but this green glowing light came and went a few times and only lasted a few seconds each time. After this the object/light changed back to the original shape and slowly started moving across the sky, heading south and out of eyesight. During the times it was moving across the sky it would disappear one second and show up in a different area of the sky. I knew when I saw object that it wasnt a normal aircraft, something felt different, a strange feeling came over my whole body. I was instantly attracted to the object, something I have never experienced before! I did try to take pictures and video with my tablet I had with me, but they didn't seem to capture exactly what my naked eye was seeing. Im not sure if you can really see much in the pictures and videos, but Ill upload them anyways. It also scared both my dogs half to death!
