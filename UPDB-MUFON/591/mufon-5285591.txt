A dark colored sphere moving at a constant speed and trajectory

The spherical object I observed was traveling from NW to SE in a seemingly straight trajectory. I observed it’s approach for several seconds, perhaps half a minute, before reaching for my camera phone to shoot video of it as it passed in front of me.

At first sight, from a distance, I thought it might be a helicopter, I see them around here often, as I’m near West Point. I soon realized it was no helicopter, though it’s size seemed comparable to one. 

It was dark in color, but also reflected the sunlight, revealing it’s spherical shape. It seemed to be flying near or just below the altitude of the clouds. The object was featureless, but somehow didn’t look like it had a smooth surface, it seemed textured in some way. There was no visible evidence of a means of propulsion, but it’s movement was constant in speed and direction. It was clearly not “floating” like a balloon.
