{jd} Two star like lights doing 90 degree turns then one sends a pulse of likght to the onther one then it exploded

When I was 11 me and my best friend where in my back yard in Folsom California. At about 9-10 pm we seen 2 lights in the sky that looked like a star when not moving but when they did move they moved in a zig zag  pattern doing over 90 degree turns. and going from left to right as far as we could see in a blink of an eye. then they both stopped and from the one that was higher in the sky shot a light at the other.When it struck the other light it blew up like huge fire ball then the other light went strait up and was gone in a flash.it was the most incredible thing i have ever seen in my life
