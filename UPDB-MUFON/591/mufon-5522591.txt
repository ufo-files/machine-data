stationary reflective object that disappeared and reappeared for a short period

while driving south on wilma rudolph blvd, a shiny object caught my eye during a traffic delay. i thought the object might be an aircraft reflecting light so i waited for it to turn at an angle where it would stop reflecting and move in front of the cloud where I noticed it. however it just stopped reflecting and no apparent aircraft was visible or seen moving in any direction. then after less than a minute it reappeared in the same spot. It appeared to be in the southwest part of our county near hwy 48/13. I texted a couple of my freinds to see if they could see the object but they were hunting and had not noticed the object. Im former military with aviation background and a MUFON member. Its my first sighting of an object that I couldnt explain. I remain objective in my sighting and hope someone else had gotten a better veiw of this object.
