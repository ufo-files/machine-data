{nd} 2 rows of bright light hovering and go to south and descending behind hill

I live on a lake. Couldnt sleep. Went out on balcony and saw flash of white light come from behind and over the house and across the lake and then hover and then went over the hill beyond the lake and descended behind the hill. When it hovered, I noticed two bands of white lights moving sequentially and a band of amber (copper) and green alternating light inbetween the two white bands that was pulsating. The colored lights were pulsating at a slower speed than the two bands of white light.  On other occasions, my husband and I noticed an orange glow from behind the hill (there are no homes there). It was clearly not an airplane or helicopter.
