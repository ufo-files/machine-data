A black oval with flashing lights

I was in the park when I saw a weird looking black oval in the sky. It looked like a drone but was obviously bigger. When I got a closer look it had a curved rectangle on the front changing from blue to green. It also had two cuboids dangling down vertically from the oval. I was very scared at first then it vanished completely. But around 15-20 mins later it was back going in multiple directions. I got on photo of it this time it was much further away.
