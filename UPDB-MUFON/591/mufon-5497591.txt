I was sitting outside of the holiday inn having a cigar in a somewhat open but covered area while waiting for my wife who was teaching a class at the convention center (kccc). It was evening with a bluebird sky and occasional cloud floating by. I was watching the light/shadows on the buildings (I am an artist and sailing enthusiast who in the 70's could identify every extant military aircraft...I had the redeyes!). The ground winds were 10mph from the north. There was no alarm upon sighting the UFO. I noted the time at 7:02 (I just do stuff like that!). First instinct was aircraft but quickly reconsidered by lack of movement. I was sitting on a park bench with a back and moved myself over about a foot so the UFO was against a textured concrete column. No movement at all...no wind drift, nothing! Since I hadn't a scale on the craft, there was no guessing altitude or distance. If it was the size of a cola can, it would have been about 300 yards away and 150' in altitude. Were it the size of a silo it could have been 12-15 miles from me. It was above the kccc which is approx 60' tall. At first when the sun was higher I had no idea of the shape, but as the sun lowered the object reflected the yellow orange light and I could see that is was the shape of a Tylonol caplet. I had a quartering view and it was perpendicular the sun. The craft had no perceivable details, but again could have been very far away. There was a man sitting on bus bench between myself and the object about 35 yards away and I, not being one that shys away from engaging strangers, started walking toward him to ask if he saw it, but his bus came and I went back to the bench. I kept it in sight going but was walking away from it returning. It was gone at 7:25.
