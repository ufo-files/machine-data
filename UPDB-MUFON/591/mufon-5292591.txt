Three steady lights rotating in night sky in triangular shape then took off and lights seperated

I was standing outside with my roommate who was showing me his new car when I just looked up at the sky and saw three bright lights in the shape of a triangle. As soon as I started to record they began rotating where you could sort of see these lights were connected. The object then took off to my left where it went behind some clouds then only one light was visible but when it appeared from behind the cloud it was no longer glowing and was disc shaped and dark. It moved very fast I couldn’t run fast enough to follow it. It made no sound.
