Light zigzagging in the early morning sky

I was going to my car to wait for my next run as I was a truck driver for Ryder Trucking in Pickering Ontario when a movement in the night sky caught my attention. It was a solid white light that was moving in a zigzag direction going it seemed from one star to another. Now that I think about it it the object was high above the Pickering Nuclear plant just five minutes west of where I was. It never came down close to the earth the whole time I observed it. I watched it continue to climb and zigzag till it was out of sight.
