Robotic, slightly domed machine with head-like projection at front of it.

I woke up just before 10am on the morning of May 13, 2021 and did very routine chores, feeding pets, making coffee, checking the national and local news. My cellphone had been charging all night. Later in the day, I opened the phone, and found never-before seen pictures and some short videos taken just outside my front door. I live alone, and no-one had access to my phone. I tried to enhance some of the stills to make the lit-up object in my driveway clearer, but there was very little enhancement. The videos are much clearer, as the motion-sensor spotlight over my front door had gone off, and I could see that there was a machine suspended about ten feet in the air, with blinking red and white lights over the slightly domed "body" of it, and a head-like projection at the front of it that had two extremely bright steady lights, with a bright white streak running down between them, and the projection moved very steadily from side-to-side as if it were searching or scanning the road in front of my driveway, or perhaps the buildings across the street from my property. I have zero memory of anything from about 9pm May 12, 2021, until waking up late morning on May 13, 2021.
