Observed a star like being in sky flash red light and move in a triangle in and out.

While driving in car along road I saw a star falling fast down. I thought it was a shootinf star but quickly realized there was three in a triangle shape. I noticed that it was moving in and out of a triangle shape and was very weird. Anxiety filled my body as I felt like the word was ending. A red light started flashing from the far left light. I have never seen anything like this in my life. Soon two of the lights randomly turned off and the final light glowed very bright as it soon sped off as a plane but way to fast to really be a plane. Very scary it must have been an alien in this ufo. It seemed to be out of our reality and advanced. Tried to take a picture but it was way too small to see.
