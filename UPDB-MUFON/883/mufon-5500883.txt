{rjl} Two pulsating lights sitting in sky. Dull gold color (not amber or yellow), actually gold color, with red in the center

At approximately 10:15 pm, I was driving west on 78th Street. It was dusk, and I looked at the sky, because it was an unusual deep dark blue, color, not too often seen. Sitting in the sky were two dull gold colored lights, with red centers, They were pulsating. They were not amber or yellow in color, they were actually a dull gold color, something I have never seen. They were not as high as the airplanes fly. They were low, but much higher than treetop level. I realized they were ufos because they were pulsating, and because of the dull gold color. There was no place to pull off the road, find my cell phone, and get a picture. When I did pull off the road into a parking lot, got my phone, and looked up to where they were, I was upset to see that they had vanished. I immediately got the idea to take a pic of where they had been in the sky. Sadly, I took more pictures of the beautiful night sky, in different directions, and went home. This is the picture of the area in the sky where I had seen them. I had put my cell phone out the window, and snapped a shot of where they had been.
