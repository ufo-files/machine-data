Pulsating gold star like object

I was walking the dogs and up in clear dark sky I spotted what looked like a pulsating, golden yellow stationary star. It was hard to tell how far off this object was, but, the scale was similar to that of typical stars. It did not move at all, I had a tree in foreground for reference to determine that. It simply pulsed, seemed to get a tiny bit larger, then smaller, then simply disappeared. There was some military jet activity that night (not at all unusual given our proximity to Eglin Air Force Base) but this was not consistent with an engine glow, flare or other object I have seen before that I associate with the military. I am also quite familiar with the Chinese lanterns, this was not what I was looking at.
