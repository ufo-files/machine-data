Observed aircraft with a pattern of lights

I briefly looked outside as I often do only to see what could be an airliner heading northeast. There is a row of wing lights flickering in a pattern from left to right with a larger red light in the middle. Airliners have a light in front, on the tail and a blinking white light on each wingtip. 

It is difficult to determine the exact shape since it's dark out but to me it seemed to be a cigar or maybe triangular. I rush to my computer to verify with FlightTracker24 and to my surprise there was NO air traffic in the area at the time of the sighting. With my phone in hand I race outdoors hoping to capture this thing on video but sadly it was too far away. The sighting took place at night at roughly 11:33 pm and lasted for perhaps three to four minutes. There is absolutely NO SOUND, no distance rumbling sound that I expect to hear from an airliner.
