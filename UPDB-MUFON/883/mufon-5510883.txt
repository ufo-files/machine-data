{nd} bright orange orb flew across the sky at a slower pace but in a straight line

I was sitting outside talking on the phone when I noticed the bright orange-red ball of light coming from across the treetops toward where I live. I noticed it because it was very bright. At first I thought it mightve been a shooting star, but then I noticed it was too big to be one and it was flying toward my home. It flew at a slower but steady pace over my house and continued to fly NW. I tried to focus on it, but it was so bright that even with squinting my eyes I still couldnt focus on it. I was scared of it. I didn't want to move, I actually wanted to hide so that it wouldnt "see" me. I lost sight of it when it flew farther away.
