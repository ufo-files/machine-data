{tjd} Large spherical (cigar shaped) object

The object was large cigar shaped, white/silver with bright white lights emanating from the object. One large door on the side was open but no one was observed. The object was moving at a very low altitude (barely over power lines) east to west over a field on my left as I was headed south on the highway. It was moving so slowly, I watched it for several minutes. I eventually stopped in the highway so I didn't have to drive under it. There was no other traffic that late at night (approx 11 pm). I sat for approximately a minute waiting for it to cross in front of me. I was very close to object, well within a 100 feet. There was no noise or vibration that I noticed. The object had no wings and I couldn't see any kind of engine or propulsion device.
