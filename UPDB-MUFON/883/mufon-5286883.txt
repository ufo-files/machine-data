multipule orb like objects seen in the sky over my house about 1 hour before sunset.

roughly about 8:45pm on june 22, 2021 i was outside with my kids watching them play and just happened to look up in the sky to the north and see something that i absolutly can not explain. all of us were aldready looking up at the moon because it was unusually large and clear to see in the sky. i yelled to the kids to look up at what i was seeing and all of us seen the orb like objects. i immediately pulled out my cell phone and started recording. they were flying in formation and interacting with each other, doing 90degree turns at an instant. we watched them for over 10 min till the they were out of eye sight. they formed circle patterns and flew for a few seconds then would break apart. they flashed multipule colors but the majority of the orbs were a silverish bright pulsating light and just a couple of them were red, gold color. it was probably one of the most amazing things i have ever seen, nothing in my rational mind could find an explanation for what me and my kids seen in the sky. the video wasnt the best but i did my best in the exciting moments that we experienced.
