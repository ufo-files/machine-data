Bright Sphere

I was coming out of a shopping center and looked up into the night sky over Reno about 10:30 at night and saw a bright round light traveling from the south to the north. It was not blinking or flashing as a plane would...and I saw a plane just minutes later as a comparison. The pace was fast and constant and I heard no sound. Could not tell how high it was but it did go into a small cloud for a few seconds. Then it disappeared into the glare of the city lights.
