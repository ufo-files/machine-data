While filming the sun set in the Pacific Ocean, this object appears on film

I was watching and taking time lapsed photos and video of the sun setting into the Pacific Ocean.  Two days later I reviewed my photos and videos and discovered a disc like object on film that I did not see while taking the pictures. It appears to move quickly in erratic patterns both vertically and horizontally in the videos. When the video stops and on the still pictures, the disk shaped object appears white and blurry > Then, when the pictures are enlarged into a close up view , the object shows clearly windows.  The pictures show the object having a large aura in a perfect circle surrounding the craft.
