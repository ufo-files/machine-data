{cak} Triangle Shaped Object w/ Three Bright White Lights

I was driving east along a major roadway in Portland. I saw three bright lights in a triangle shape at about 65 degrees above the horizon traveling west. Initially I thought the object might be a plane. However, I realized that if it were a plane it was very very low, flying below the low cloud cover, and that it was operating directly over a major road. It seemed like a very unusual place for a plane to be flying under normal circumstances. My wife thought it was a drone, but to me it seemed too large to be a remote controlled craft. I watched the object travel over the car. It had three white lights that did not blink and appeared to be large and round. The object appeared to have a triangular metallic structure but I was unable to make it out distinctly because the bright white lights on the bottom of the object and the rain on the windshield of my car. After the object passed over the car I felt it was strange enough that it deserved a second look. I pulled onto a side street and turned around. When I got back on the street where I saw the object it could no longer be seen. I have no idea what it was, but it was strange enough that I thought it warranted a report here to see if anyone else saw it and can confirm what it was.
