{cak} Red light with red sparks and tail turns and appears to leave atmosphere

After arriving home from the store in my truck, I went to the outside garbage can to throw out some trash when I noticed what I thought was red fireworks. It appeared as a bright red ball with a long red tail and red sparks coming out of the tail. When the red ball did not fade but continued forward I yelled to my wife to come look at the object (she was in our vehicle still). The sparks and tail faded but the bright red object continued moving towards my house from the north moving south. The object then made a turn towards the east and proceeded to increase in altitude until it appeared to either leave the atmosphere or at least high enough to be out of sight. I took two videos, the first was very short because my LED light was on when trying to record the object in the dark. That video should give a relative angle since you can see the top of my roof and you can still make out the object. The second video shows the object moving towards the house and then turning. Unfortunately I did not capture video of the object as it had a tail and sparks.
