{cak} Large black triangle spaceship with res lights

Last Tuesday the 7th of May as I was driving home, I was driving alone in the freeway and all of a sudden I look up and see a huge floating object. It took me about 5 seconds to realize what I was seeing was not of this planet. It was large, black, with red lights. It had a very cool sleek design. Shiny material. I was terrified and started crying and drove faster to get away from it because i felt like It was after me. I immediately called my husband just in case I would never see him again. I have attached a video that shows red lights. I can’t guarantee the red lights in the video is the UFO I saw. It does show my initial reaction upon witnessing the ship. I didn’t ever see the ship in advance from afar, I only saw it until it was almost directly hovering over me. It seemed to almost have just magically appear out of thin air. One reason the lights in the video may not be the UFO I described is that the lights in the video seem to be way further away than what I saw the spaceship being. It was pretty close to me for me to see each individual light. I drew a rough picture of it. I am no artist so it may look silly.
