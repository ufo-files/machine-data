Weird pulsating red lights in clouds

Was on my back deck just after 9 PM and noticed really strange red pulsations eminating from some clouds. It was dark but you could see with naked eye and through the camera on my phone (S9 has a nice night mode) but would not record properly on video mode. So, I grabbed another camera and recorded my cellphone screen to show that this light was there and that is was pulsating. Never seen it before and I am always looking for weird stuff in the sky so I'm sure I would have seen it before in the 4+ years living here. No sound and it lasted about an hour before fading away. Very odd
