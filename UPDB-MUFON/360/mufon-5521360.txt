Saucer Or Orb with short wings, accompanied by 2 smaller orbs.

time of sighting 11.45 PM January 15 2012" I witnessed an object descend rapidly from above 5000 feet roughly and brighten. It suddenly leveled off and slowed to almost a hover. Then 2 smaller orbs began to move around and come into view. The 2 smaller orbs maneuvered around the larger object. The larger object maintained a fairly straight course moving slowly at roughly a mile away. It was between 1 and 2 thousand feet in altitude. All three objects suddenly and rapidly ascended and vanished. The larger one dimmed out as it ascended fading momentarily into a dim blue before completely vanishing.The entire event lasted for a few minutes.
