Large orb travelling across sky like it was floating

My daughter saw in the sky, near dusk, a large orb in the sky not far away from her. It was 'floating' and travelling in a straight line at the same altitude just for a minute or so. 

She told her friends who were with her, but they looked up and said there was nothing there. Only my daughter could see it. It was star colour with a clear halo round it. But was big in comparison to how stars appear in size in the sky. Also it was relatively near - above the trees.

She told me about it the following day, because her friends had said it was nothing but she knew I had also seen something identical a week or so prior, also reported to mufon. 

Both of us have seen orbs before in other situations. So she could trust me to understand. I am reporting on her behalf. 

It travelled for a short distance and disappeared. Exactly the same as the craft/orb I witnessed over our home. She had looked away to speak to her friends and it wasn't there when she looked up again.
