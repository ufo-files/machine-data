{rjl} Driving during the day, made this trip numerous times. As I was driving, I briefly saw an object in the sky.

I was driving north on I-17, 10 miles south of camp verde, AZ. It was mid afternoon (2pm). As I was driving, obviously I had to keep my eye on the road, a football shaped object appeared and disappeared within a second. The object had to be about 750 feet in length. I admit, if I saw this and didn't get confirmation of someone else seeing it, I would've blew it off. What confirmed my sighting, was another vehicle to the left of me (passing lane on I-17). As soon as I saw it, it disappeared. I said to my passenger, "did you see that?". Passenger response, "what?". I responded, "there was something right there". Same time I was pointing to where I saw the object. Passenger, was like yeah right and around that same time I observed the passing car with two people in it and the driver in that car was pointing in the same direction as I was. I assume he was telling his passenger the same thing. I then told my passenger, "look, he saw what I saw". I believed proof of the other car driver, pointing to where I saw the object would be enough to prove to my passenger that I did see a 750 feet silver object in the sky.
