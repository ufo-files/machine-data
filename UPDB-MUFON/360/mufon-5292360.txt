I saw an extremely bright, star-like light appear and disappear in the blink of a second.

It was around 8:30 on Friday, August 14. My girlfriend and I had just gone to the store and were walking back to her apartment. Lost in thought, I looked to the clear and still bright sky; there were no stars out yet. When I was about to look back down, a star seemed to blink into existence and before I could process what I had just seen, disappeared. I jumped up and loudly exclaimed to my girlfriend: "It was a UFO!! I Just saw a f***ing UFO!" She was extremely hesitant because she did not see it and to this day does not believe me. I stopped talking about what I saw then to avoid any conflict but I couldn't get the image out of my head. I kept seeing the scene of an empty blue sky interrupted by an extremely bright light over and over. My mind was racing and my heart was beating faster than I remember it beating ever before. It took over an hour for me to calm down and I bringing it up to my girlfriend throughout the night because I was so unnerved.I was excitedly terrified of that unknown. I wanted to wait a couple days before reporting so I could fully process what kept I saw; unfortunately I'm just as lost now as I was then.
