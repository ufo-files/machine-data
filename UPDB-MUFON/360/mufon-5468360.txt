{rjl} Traveling south bound down interstate, I caught sight of something out of the corner of my eye. At my first instinct it may have been a plane but kept glancingly in the direction and saw that it was not a plane or helicopter.

I was traveling down interstate 555 south bound going back towards home, when I caught a glimpse of a object out of the corner of my eye. I noticed that the object was descending and at first thought it was a plane or a helicopter. But after continued glances noticed it was neither of those. I slowed down my vehicle and noticed the vehicle in front of me must have saw it too because they pulled over to the side of the interstate and I could tell they were looking in the same direction as I had been. The object was black in color and was descending across a field. It kept descending and I kept looking back for signs of smoke, fire, or some inclination that would indicate a crash of some sort. None was observed. I texted my husband to let him know what I had just saw. There were no reports that we could find of any aircraft crashes in that area. I could not determine what the black object was. Husband suggested I put a report on here. Maybe someone else also saw this.
