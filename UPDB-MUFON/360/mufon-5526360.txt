i saw a large, bright star-like object move slowly northeast at 11:35pm then it just faded away to nothing.

I was fishing with my brother-in-law late Saturday night. I looked up and saw a very large, bright, white, star-like object moving very slowly going northeast across the night sky. I stared at this object with extreme scrutiny and saw no blinking lights of any kind, just a steady bright white light. I observed it moving silently for about 30 seconds then it slowly, slowly faded away until my brother-in-law or myself could no longer see it, total time, around 2 minutes. it is hard for me to say what the altitude of this object could have been. There were planes flying around that night and this object didn't seem to be flying as high as those aircraft (commercial and local). The size of this star-like object was about the size of a penny (and just-a-bit smaller) if you held it up at arm’s length. I know this was no aircraft or meteor and I am certain it wasn't the space station. I would like to know what it was because it was very interesting to observe.
