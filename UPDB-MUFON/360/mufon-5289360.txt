Bright like stars, as they left slowly disipated

I was letting the dog out when I saw them. I thought it strange because I have never seen stars that bright in the northern sky at that time of the morning. One was brighter than the other both did not seem to be moving. As I observed them they took off at a high rate of speed. The larger brighter one just slowly disappeared while the other one went northwest or west at a high rate of speed and disappeared until gone.
