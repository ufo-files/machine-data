{jpg} Star like

Noticed a star like object zig zagging in the sky directly straight up , I called my 2 friends over. As I pointed to the object, it began to ascend into deep space as if it knew I or we was watching it. Then a beam of light came out of the sky in semi slow motion when all 3 of US turned our heads to the right. The light had a sound or hum to it. Then the event was over. We were at a location in the wind river national forest. We was at a campsite called photographers point ar 11,000 feet near pinedale Wyoming. I have also had 3 different sightings. Go figure
