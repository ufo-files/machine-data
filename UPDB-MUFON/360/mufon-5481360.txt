Exactly at 4.30pm on 24th August 3 I was on my way to my clinic which is located in the second floor of a building. As I reached my floor I saw a vertical emission tail which was very peculiar because it was very short and then I looked around to see if there is any plane or any such thing that's when I noticed that a shiny cylindrical rounded object moving in a straight line perpendicular to the emission tail which left me guessing as it was moving very slowly exactly in straight view from my clinic. So I took out my mobile cam and started shooting it. I do see a lot of planes going over our location but never in this direction and it didn't make even a slightest of noise though it was flying so low. That's Wat made me take the video. After ten mins or so a private plane went atleast 3 times in a perpendicular direction of the ufos path. Planes are usually seen bigger but this was very small and soundless I initially thought it may b a drone. It went above the cloud cover after which I lost sight. It was very shiny in appearance.
