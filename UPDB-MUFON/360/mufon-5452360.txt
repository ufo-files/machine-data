A star that seemed to explode and then a slow light went the heart of the flash and seem to speed away

walking home from a scouting event, I was walking thought a school field. Looking up and the clear night sky, I noted a star that seemed out of place. I sat down to watch it.. over the span of about 5 mins .. it grew brighter and then seemed to explode giving off a glowing cloud that rolled way from the center. When the "cloud" looked something like a doughnut held at arms length, I noted a dull light come in from the west and seemed to fly into the center of "doughnut" and touch the fading center light... the dull light seem to pick up speed and zip off to the east and about 3 time it rate of speed. I watch the "cloud" till it started to fade away.. this lasted for about 30 mins from start to end.

I have seen this kind of event three time over the span of 40 years
