I saw this object stationary in the sky!

I am a preacher, professor, and writer. I was praying in our chapel before our Wednesday night prayer time and Bible study. I walk around the room while I pray. I looked out the window to the west of the church and saw this object with was gray just siting in the sky! It wasn't moving. It was just there under the clouds. I walk around the room and came back to the widow. It was still there. I went and got my wife she saw it as well. As we were watching it, it went straight up through the clouds. Crazy! I've never seen anything like it before. I grew up by Little Rock Air Force Base. I now live five miles from Grissom Air Force Base. I know planes, all kinds of planes. I watch the sky all the time for them. I never saw anything like this.
