Glowing orb seemed to have some sort of propulsion coming from it.

Traveling west looking SW sitting at a stop light the object was noticed. Appeared to be hovering but had propulsion coming from the bottom of it. I say propulsion because you could see it coming out of the object. I was able to get a photo of it but by the time I could turn my vehicle to a spot to pull over and monitor it more the object had accelerated at a seemingly impossible rate and was gone. I can assure you that the attached photo is not any sort of reflection or glare as my window was down and my phone was outside of my vehicle when this was taken. I am not a pilot but I am an aviation buff and having lived my entire life near military bases in both Bellevue,NE and San Antonio,TX. In my opinion what was observed tonight was not a conventional aircraft that I’ve ever seen.
