I saw a Large orange ball coming at me very fast @ 30 deg. to the horizon

I was at work in a rock quarry in Corona Calif. on the control tower. It was between 9pm & 12am. I looked up and saw a orangish redish extremely large ball of light coming from the north straight at me. It got within 2 or 3 miles of me made a 90deg. turn at high speed and go east. It broke into two parts . The main part continued east ; the other broke of at a 30deg. angle to the north east. Did any one else report a sighting like this one ,around the same time ?
