{jpg} Fireball Moving quickly, south to north. Completely silent.

I was siding my garage, my girlfriend pointed it out to me. It flew directly overhead and was observed by myself, my girlfriend and our neighbors. It flew from the south to the north and maintained a steady altitude and speed. It moved too quickly for me to get my phone to document it. It was very clear to make out that it was orb shaped and literally on fire but there was no smoke or any sort of contrails. It was maybe 2000 ft at altitude. It also moved quickly and completely silently. It was clearly flying and not falling or crashing as one would assume seeing an aircraft on fire.
