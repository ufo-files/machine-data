{dr}2 bean shaped objects sighted to the NW sky Los angeles

Observed: 10:32am 2/21/15. I work at home and went out to my back yard to bring in the cat. I could see the light clouds overhead to the North. I noticed a small white object along a line of clouds. I dismissed at first as a balloon but it seemed not to be moving with the air flow. There is a very light wind at approx. 1-5 mph from the South to the North. I got a pair of binoculars and fount the object still in the same position. Through the lens it appeared jelly bean shaped, white and holding position. It was slowly moving South as I began to track it. It did not change shape. It moved from roughly 75 degrees above the horizon to directly overhead. in the pan of 30 mins. I then noticed a fainter rose colored similar shaped object 10 degrees lower and 20 degrees to the right of the first object. Both were moving to the South. I observed this till both object had slowly risen in altitude till they were no longer visible roughly 35 mins later
