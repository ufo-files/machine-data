{jpg} Unknown object observed one night in September. Four bright white lights and one red light at a low altitude.

During the last week of September, I went to let the dog out in the backyard at about 1:30am. After being out there for a minute or two I heard a noise. When I looked up, I saw an object in the sky. It had four bright, white lights, and one red light. The lights were all steady. The noise it made was like a loud roaring noise. It came from over my neighbor's house and glided over my house at a very low altitude. As it went away from me, the noise it made began pulsating as it went out of sight. It never changed altitude the duration I observed. It lasted just over a minute or so. There are a lot of military and commercial aircraft in this area, but I have never seen or heard anything that compares with this event.
