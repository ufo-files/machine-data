{nd} It looked like it was over the radio tower but it wasnt then after like 10 mins it just blinked out.

I was in the car with my mom and dad and sister coming back from my grandmas house and I noticed a bright light over the radio tower way off in the distance, we were going north on route 22 heading home. I caught sight of it out of the corner of my eye. At first I thought it was a star or a plane but it didn't move. I told my family and they all saw it. And about 30 minutes before when we were at Fudgies for icecream , my sister saw it far off above the trees west off of route 22. Then coming home we turned on to chestnut ridge road by the farm and looked and it just blinked and disappeared. This is the 4th time weve seen it in Dutchess County.
