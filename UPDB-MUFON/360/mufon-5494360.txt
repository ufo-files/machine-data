I was at my computer, suddenly out of the corner lf my eye, I saw a bright light. I looked through the window and saw this light (similar to a star) zoom down and back up. When it was coming back up, it left a light grey shadow. The shadow stayed there for a few seconds after the "star" remained still. Soon after (10:16 PM) I saw the star zoom farther down behind trees. Still very bright because I can see it. One of my friends texted me saying he saw it too. I'm not sure if it let out a noise, because my windows were closed. First photo is after it zoomed up and down. Second, is when it zoomed down behind trees, still in the sky.
