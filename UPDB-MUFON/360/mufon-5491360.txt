{cak} A light that came from the sky and landed on the ground and opened up. It revealed a light bulb hanging from the ceiling and a wooden chair below it.

I was a kid playing in the neighborhood when a light that came from the sky and landed on the ground and opened up to show a room inside. There was a light bulb hanging from the ceiling and a white wooden chair below it. It then closed and went back up into the sky. This all happened a few feet in front of me. I was frightened and I froze in my tracks as it happened. I didn't see anyone in the phenomenon. I only saw the light bulb hanging from the ceiling, the white wooden chair and white walls behind them.
