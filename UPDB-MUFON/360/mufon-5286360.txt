Strange walk home

My daughter and I were walking home around 10pm at night with our dog. My daughter asked me what was in the sky. I looked up and there was 3 very large glowing balls sitting in a straight horizontal line. We stopped to look at them. As we watch the line of balls started tilting until it was a vertical line. At the point the top ball broke into 3 balls. Those 3 balls start flying at high speed. Zipping around the sky above us. They changed directions without even turning. They zoomed around for about 10 minutes. 2 of the zooming balls went back to the original line and disappeared. The 1 that stayed out hovered above the trees for a minute or 2 and then it zoomed up high and then back down quickly and then looked like it landed behind the trees about a half a mile or so away. The light from the glowing ball could be seen through the tree trunks for a couple of minutes before just disappearing. The line of 3 balls slowly got smaller and dimmer after it disappeared and then disappeared themselves.
