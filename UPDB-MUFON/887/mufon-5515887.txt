{dr}15- 20 red glowing orbs

Around 11pm we walked outside and saw 15-20 red glowing, flashing orbs moving west across the sky. They appeared to be changing speeds from fast to slow and slow to fast. They seemed to be in 2 lines or configurations to one another. As the got farther west across the sky they all began to speed up very fast at different times and then disappeared completely.
