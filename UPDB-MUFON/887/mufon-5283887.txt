Very strange rope like segment of white lights. Appeared to be a singular object

At about 7:05pm EST on December 3 2021. I noticed what appeared to be a extremely long segment of lights moving in a perfectly straight line across the sky, There was absolutely no sound. The lights were spaced in an odd fashion. Each were separated by about the width of one of the individual lights, but they were in a pattern with what looked to be like visual Morse code sequence .
At this point we have seen this posted to Ashe in the know on Facebook continuing towards Virginia. More keeps appearing in Facebook for the beach up through the state of Virginia.
