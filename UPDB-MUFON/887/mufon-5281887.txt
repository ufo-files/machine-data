UFO flies over Snowflake

My friend and I were driving into Snowflake southbound on highway 77 just a little after sundown when to the left of us a ways away from the road, we saw what we thought was a plane coming right for us, looking like it was about to crash right into the road. 

But then, it gets to the road, which has all these tall streetlights, and it turns direction slightly and slowly hovers over the street, from one side to the other, and then turns again and heads southbound itself. It took about five seconds for it to get across the street, and it didn't make a sound. Illuminated underneath by the streetlights, I could see the craft was a V-shaped, boomerang-like craft. There were circular lights on the corners of it, and in the middle was a red light. There were other circular lights next to the ones that were on, but they looked like they were shuttered closed. It reminded me a lot of the "Phoenix Lights" craft. 

I have never seen anything like this in my life, and there were several other witnesses who posted about seeing it on Facebook.
