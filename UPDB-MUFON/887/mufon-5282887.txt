Co worker asked what was in the sky, we obvserved to Tic Tac like objects.

On a cigarette break during work at the Leander post office at the intersection of 183 and Sonny road in Leander Texas. There are four of us including myself. A co worker (Sarah) seems surprised and says "what the hell is that in the sky?" We all turn back and witness two dull smooth tic tac shaped objects no more than 100 feet over the top of the power lines gliding smoothly on a straight path. There was no noise and the objects may have been white because the weather was overcast. Only one object changed its velocity because when we first saw it it was slightly behind the other, but after getting above the intersection it formed alongside the other object and they looked to be separated by only a few feet. The objects were low enough that our workplace obstructed the view so I got up and ran to the other side of the building expecting them to show up but they had vanished. The incident lasted no more than 30 seconds and I personally only saw them for about 5 seconds. They had no lights, no wings, no exhaust. They just looked like 30 foot large tic tacs that appeared to reflect the overcast sky. The way the objects moved was incredibly smooth and they didn't appear to move any faster than a car driving down the road rated at 55mph.
