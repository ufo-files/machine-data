{tjd} 30 star like objects traveling in straight light

At about 5:40 this morning my dad came in and woke me up telling me to come check this out. I go outside and look up to see star-like objects being ejected from another pulsating star-like object. There was no formation, but the lights had no sound and didnt leave a trail. The lights were traveling from SW to NE. When the star-like object that was ejecting the others was done it pulsated and disappeared.
