{dr}A silver cigar shaped craft vanished in broad daylight.

I was driving home after visiting the Air Force museum at around 3:30 PM. I noticed something in the sky in front of me. It looked like sunlight reflecting off of something. At first it appeared as a white orb around the size of a dime held at arms' length, I assume that it was light reflecting off of whatever it was but I am not certain. What I am assuming to be the reflection stopped and there was a silver cigar shaped craft moving north. The craft seemed to elongate while moving and then vanished. Had it not disappeared I would have thought it was just a plane seen from an odd angle, but the disappearance has me perplexed.
