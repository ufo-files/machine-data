{tjd} Witnessed 6 or 7 glowing orbs that appeared out of nowhere while driving St.Rt 188 into Circleville from Lancaster.

My family and I were driving from Lancaster to Circleville on St. Rt 188 at 1912 hours on March 5th when we noticed an orange orb of light appear and disappear on the Southside of St Rt 188 between St Rt 674 and Amanda Northern Road. Confused and intrigued by what we had just seen we stopped at my parents’ house on Ringgold Northern Road to see if we could observe anything else from their driveway. After looking for a few minutes we decided to continue back on St Rt 188 to home. Then at 1935 hours on March 5th we noticed what appeared to be a military helicopter hovering at low altitude on the North side of St Rt 188 where Winchester intersects. A quarter mile passed the helicopter proceeding towards Circleville 6 to 7 Orbs of orange light appeared in the sky out instantaneously. The orbs would appear and disappear, almost as if the light was collapsing inward upon itself and did so for about 10-12 seconds before disappearing completely from existence. The orbs maintained a low altitude, probably just a few hundred feet, and appeared to be hovering, yet slightly moving to the south east. This was not the starlink satellites recently launched. I know what those look like, this was unlike anything we had seen before.
