Object spotted at sunset

I was sitting on my porch watching the swallows that fly around this time of year and noticed one that was not moving. It was a distant speck of black. I watched it for a while thinking it was either coming toward my location or moving away. I finally went and got my binoculars and was shocked to see a square/diamond shaped object hovering at a great distance. It never moved despite the clouds moving.
The more bizarre thing was that the object had "streamers" or long protrusions sticking out about twice the length of the square part of the object and pointing to the right horizontally.There looked to be several of these, maybe 4 or 5. The object and protrusions were dark in color, nearly black. I watched for about 1 minute through the binoculars and decided to go in and get my video camera. I was gone no more than 30 seconds. When I got back outside, the object was gone. I even went up on the highest part of my roof, hoping to get the object on tape but it was nowhere to be seen.
