Two round white lights that appeared to chase each other in a circle, would get close then spread apart again moving in a circular pattern right under cloud cover

I stepped out onto my back deck, and immediately noticed two white lights, orb like, moving in a circular pattern right above the edge of our backyard. They appeared to be chasing one another, would be a distance apart, then would catch each other, disappear for a second then would start again. Sometimes they would move in the opposite direction. My first instinct was to see if there was some type of light coming from a neighbouring backyard but all was dark in the neighbours yards. I watched for about 15 minutes and something about it just seemed weird. I felt uneasy as I watched. When my partner called that night I told him about it and it was still going on above the backyard. He suggested I take video or a photo so I tried, not thinking it would show up because it was dark, and quickly gave up trying. The lights danced, under the low cloud cover for about 2 hours. There was no sound and I wound up trying to convince myself maybe someone had a mobile light for their child and the light was just coming out a window. It wasn't until the next night when going through photos on my phone watched the few seconds of what I thought was useless video. I could see the lights dancing on the video and decided to Google it. When I saw photos and videos others had posted it was exactly what I had seen the night of Sept 2,2017. And that's why I'm sharing what I saw...
