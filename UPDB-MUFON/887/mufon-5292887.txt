Five black doughnut-shaped objects were spotted flying over northeast Pennsylvania.

I witnessed five doughnut-shaped objects flying in unison over Childs, Pennsylvania at 8:35 p.m. on Saturday, July 25, 2020. The 5 objects flew in a V pattern like a flock of birds, before changing to wide single line formation. It appeared as if the 5 objects combined into one. When I first pulled over to witness the strange occurrence, I tried to photograph/record but nothing was showing on the screen. I drove a couple hundred yards before pulling over again. I still wasn't seeing the objects on screen, but I ried to record anyway. The objefts in the recorded video are far away at that point, but can still be made out. I'm not claiming the objects to be alien spacecrafts. In fact, I have no idea what they were. I'm a very healthy, very sane, 38-year-old newspaper reporter, who is aware of the catastrophic effects this would have on his reputation. I don't want any publicity or attention in the slightest way. My only reason for contacting MUFON is to find out what the objects were.
