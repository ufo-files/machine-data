{rjl} Two bright lights that hovered, then dimmed and joined three more dim lights and took off Northward.

I was walking home with my partner from the store when I saw two very bright star like lights in the sky. Upon looking further I noticed they were out of place compared to the constellations and planets in the sky at the time. We continued to watch the objects, assuming at first that it was an aircraft of some kind, but it did not move or have any body mass, as I could see other stars around the two bright points. After about 2 minutes we witnessed both bright lights fade over the course of about 10-15 seconds. Once they faded to around the brightness of a satellite, we witnessed three more lights of the same brightness join them our of nowhere and swirl around, changing positions and then very quickly (faster than any satellite we've ever seen) take off Northward. We watched them until they were too far away for us to see.
