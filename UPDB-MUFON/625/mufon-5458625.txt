Sighting in the Skagerrak

I was serving as a sailor in the German Navy aboard Destroyer D-182. The ship was in the water strait "Skagerrak", which links North Sea and Baltic Sea. At that time, I was an experienced lookout and had served in several NATO exercises involving all kinds of ships, aircraft, missiles exercises, sonar buoys etc. At that day, I was on a shift as a lookout, covering the port side of the ship. Towards the end of the shift I see a shining bright object flying out of the water, making a half circle and submerging in the water again. This was several Miles off the ship. And it was fast. About 10 Minutes later my shift was over, and I reportetd the situation to my successor, HG Westphal. I said and pointed: "There is a freighter heading xyz, there is another ship, heading xyz. And I have seen an object I cannot identify." Westphal: "What do you mean?" I answered: "I saw something strange. An object that came right out of the sea, flew in a half circle and then went back into the water". We were exchanging some jokes and I left towards the commando central. My next shift was "Coffee Bringer" for the lookouts. So, after 10 minutes, I return to the port outlook to bring Westphal a coffee. He is pale white and says "I have seen it, too." We watch the sea together, sun sets, some fog comes up. All of a sudden, a light ball surfaces close to the ship. It moves, stops abruptly, moves again. Stops moves again, up, down, left, right. We get deeply scared, my legs almost give in. A petty officer joins us. He sees the object, too. He is also scared to death. Then the thing flies away. We all go into the commando central, ask the officer in the radar, whether he has observed anything. He says no. This was perhaps the most frightening experience, I have had in my life to date.
