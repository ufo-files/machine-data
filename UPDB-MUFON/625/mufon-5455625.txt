{rjl} two glowing red very large ball like objects directly in front of me at treetop level one following the other to the left .

While stopped at the stop light at Taylor rd about to make a right, directly across is "Steam-O-Rama" grounds. At treetop level I see two very large orange/red ball like objects. They had the appearance of what iron may look like when removed from a fire. They seemed to have almost what looked like a liquid fire inside of them. They moved slowly from right to left and were very close to me. Earlier that day there had been a steam show on the grounds, however when I saw the objects there were only a few vendors sitting outside an older model camper. The objects traveled through the sky, across the field. One just 'blinked' out, and appeared to have disappeared. About 30 seconds later, the other one also 'blinked' out and seemed to have disappeared. I know I witnessed something unusual and feel that this siting is particularly unusual because of how close it was and how low it was to the ground. I apologize I do not have an exact date, I always have wondered if the vendors that were sitting near the camper saw them as well.
