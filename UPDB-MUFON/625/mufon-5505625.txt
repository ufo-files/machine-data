{rjl} Up to 15 aircraft, very high, moved in various formations from south of Pismo to the north.

Three of us, all witnesses, had just finished hot tubing after sneaking into the Cliffs Resort. We walked down unto the beach and were discussing constellations when we noticed a tightly packed group of lights coming from the south. They flew in various formations and had different colored flashing lights. When we first noticed them they were moving very quickly and changing direction in ways that seemed unnatural for aircraft, and then they flew north, the formations becoming increasingly elongated from the north to the south as they flew. The group consisted of steadily pulsating lights, with the occasional bright white flash.
