Seen them before. Tonight was different.

First there was a jetliner moving from the south west to the north east visible lights and strobe with engine sounds. When it was no longer in sight there was what looks like a star moving from the south east to the northwest and appeared to be much higher in elevation and moving faster than the jetliner. A few minutes later. Another star like light moved from the northeast to a southern direction going fast and zig zagging as it traveled. Immediately and while the zig zaging light was still visible another star like light crossed paths from the west to the east and disappeared just before reaching the trees that would block my sight.
 They were first noticed on July 5th. That night there were 8 sighted within 20 minutes witnessed by myself, daughter, and two grand daughters.
