Blue orb in the sky

I was cycling home from work in the late afternoon. The sun was already down but it was not completely dark yet. I was riding along a country lane at a speed of 20 km a hour to the east. I do't know what made me notice it, suddenly it was there at my right side. It was a glowing electric blue orb, it admitted no sound and it went overhead with a enormous speed and it dissapeard behind a tree. It must have came from behind me from a great altitude (there were clouds in the sky and there was some normal air traffic in the form of passenger planes) and it desended very fast to earth in a straight line until it dissapeard behind the tree. I never have seen anything go so fast except meteors. At first, i thought it was a meteor becouse I've seen a blue meteor crashing in a field a couple of years before, but i realized it didnot had a tale of light. The tree were the orb had dissapeared behind was about 20 meters in front of me. I went as fast as i could to see if i could find it behind there, but nothing was to be seen. At this point I hoped to see it again, and it left me a bit puzzled about what it was. I decided it was not a normal sighting of a meteor(no tail) or a human aircraft (to slow). And somthing about that intense blue light made me think it could have been a UFO.
