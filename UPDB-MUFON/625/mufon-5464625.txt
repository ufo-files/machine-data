Changing lights, speed, path

I was in club pool with the best friend at the time.
We decided to go to the spa, as it was getting colder. We were just talking and looking the stars. The pool was on the outskirts of the city, facing the south, where there was not other lights. Everything on the south of the city was just sugar cane plantations.
Suddenly, an object have from southwest. We though to be an airplane. But the object was changing quickly the lights which were emitting.
It started to make patters on the sky, with sudden changes on the speed and direction of the flight. It would cover couple hundred of yard in one direction and just change the direction about 120 degrees to the other, make start shaped patterns.
It last for few minutes, then left to the south, almost as a meteor, with a trail.
At the time, my friend and I were about 13 years old, so we agreed to say nothing to anyone, as the people would think we were making the story.
Few years later, speaking to a friend of the family, he did confirmed was travelling from another city, about the same time of the events, and show similar event.
