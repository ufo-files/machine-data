White sphere

Walking on a trail near Tamarack Lodge in Cascade, ID. Towards the right of the lodge, above the mountain line, near the clouds (see video) I saw a VERY distinctive bright light. I thought it was an asteroid/meteor of some kind at first. It had two thin white tails (like asteroids do) right above it. Watched it for a good 30 seconds wondering what it was, before I pulled out my phone to video. Of course right when I went to video it, it descended slowly into the clouds. I took some snap shots of my video, zoomed in on what I saw. Super weird! No distinct lines. Very pearly white and sphere shaped. Maybe a weather balloon? Not sure??
