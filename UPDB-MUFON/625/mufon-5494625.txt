{rjl} I say this ufo over hanauma bay in Hawaii. It hovered in three places and moved in straight lines and could stop on a dime. I watched it for 5-10 minutes

I saw a ufo while snorkeling in Hanamua Bay Hawaii. It rose from behind the hills on my right, then moved to the middle part of the bay over the ocean and made some unhurried moves in straightlines and was stopping and starting on a dime. It then moved to my left and lowered itself from view under the hills. I just happened to notice this thing and actually filmed it although no evidence remains of that because I deleted the video. It was too far away in the sky to see very well on cam. The thing looked like a ty fighter from the stars wars movies but more antenna-like. I pointed it out to my dad who was there and he said it was a drone. My feeling was bewilderment in that no one else was looking at it.
