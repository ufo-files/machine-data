My daughter felt compelled to take a picture of a field on her way home from work. The sky was bright and the field was very green with nice vegetation. When she was lining up her camera for the shot she noticed a round almost pearl coloured object in the middle of the field just hovering slightly off the ground. Confused about the object she took a few pictures and shortly after her first picture the object began to ascend. I am telling this story on her behalf. I am not an eye witness
