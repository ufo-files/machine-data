{rjl} Observed with night vision binocular

At a friends house in north hollywood. He had just got some new night vision binoculars. Told me to check them out. I started mesiing with them. Adjusting the zoom and the blur. I looked around with them and saw nothing. When i sat down and looked in the direction i was facing i noticed something unusual. At first i couldnt believe it. I looked in the direction without the binoculars and saw nothing. With them i still saw it there. So it was invisible to the naked eye. This is when i was in awe. I was saying "wow, amazing". My friend thought i was just messing with him. Then i told him to look and pointed in the direction he should try to see. He couldnt believe it himself. I would say it was in the same place for about 15 minutes until it was not there any more. When using a telescope to look at the moon, you have to adjust it because it moves. This didnt. It stayed in its place. So what we saw was three lights, orbs or round objects. One was stationary in the center. The other two went around the stationary one in sync. One from the left, the other from the right. It was perfect. Not even touching each other, but passing close by each other. When it got closer to the one in the center they moved fast, and slowing down as it got to its far side. They both had an oval orbit. The one from the left, would go around the center one on its right side. The one on the right would go around the center one's left side. This was only visible with the night vision binoculars. Only reason im reporting this is because i heard one of the mufon guys on coast to coast. And it sounds like it can be possible someone else has seen this as well.
