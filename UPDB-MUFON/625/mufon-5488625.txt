{jpg} Bright like an orb and on fire, lit up the sky as it fell.

I was driving east down Anderson Mill towards Austell rd when I saw something that looked like it was on fire, falling out of the sky. It exploded and lit up the sky as it fell and left a visible trail. It seemed to be pretty close to the ground, and disappeared quickly into the tree line, but I did not hear a crash like I expected to. I am unsure if this was a ufo or simply an extraordinary sighting of a meteor burning up in the atmosphere or a satellite falling out of the sky. I hope that maybe others in the area got a better look at it and might know what it was better than I.
