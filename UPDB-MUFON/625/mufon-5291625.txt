Craft in cloud traveled west from boston and nearly collides with airplane

Sitting on the roof I heard a low hum and noticed an odd spot of light in the eastern sky (coming from the atlantic?). As it moved closer i noticed that the whole thing seemed to be moving at the center of a cloud but there were definite lights coming from within. It moved west until it was out of sight but not before nearly colliding with an airplane which took off from logan airport, i dont know if the passengers or pilot realized how close they came to disaster.
