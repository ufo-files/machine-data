Materializiing and De Materiaizing Metallic orb emitting gold

I was planting some cactus in my front yard, when my phone rang, I had the brightness all the way down so I couldnt see who was calling, so I held the phone up to the sky to try and see the number calling, when I did, I saw a fast zip of a metallic ball go across the sky and it disappeared,like a comet almost. I then hung up my phone and looked around, I thought it was the glare off my phone screen maybe, as I took my hat off to wipe the sweat from my head I watched it reappear out of nothing, then dissapear and I lost it. I then put my phone on record as I see lots of weird stuff here, I then saw a flash next to the house across the street, and it appeared again, but much much closer,I watched it materialize,change from a cube to a sphere/egg then it began emitting almost like golden sparklers fireworks all around it like a "static". I filmed it just as it went behind the houses and it sped over the mountain hills in the distance. No more than 20 seconds later a twin rotor helicopter or Chanook showed up, no markings, circled for about 5 minutes and flew away.
