I was swimming at a friends apt, looked up in the southern sky. That's when I saw cigar shaped that also looked like a disc when it changed directions. I watched it move southeast for approximately 60 seconds. The object suddenly took off at an incredible speed. It was gone in an instant. I had no time to get out of the pool and photograph it. After I came home, I reported it to MUFON. Now, I do see a lot of planes in that area,but it was silent, no con trails,and did not look anything like a plane. I have also experienced visitation, but have difficulty remembering certain details.
