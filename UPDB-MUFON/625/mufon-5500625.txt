{cak} white cylindrical object going from NW to SE near McCarran airport with planes landing nearby

At approximately 2:00pm PST from the window of my office building myself and 4 other co-workers witnessed what we believe to be a UFO. The object was white in color with no apparent windows or wings. It was a solid cylindrical object. It was traveling at a fast rate of speed as compared to the airplanes that were landing at McCarran International Airport. The planes were going West and the UFO was traveling in a SE direction from the NW. We observed the UFO travel for a good 15-20 minutes.
