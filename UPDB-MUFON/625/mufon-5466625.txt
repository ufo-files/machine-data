Glowing object obscured by smoke/ clouds? Much larger than brightest largest stars but smaller than moon which was only fingernail waxing crecent and not visible at that time.

As children and I got out of the car to go to dinner at club near my house we noticed a bright glowing object shrouded in clouds or smoke in the SE sky. It looked anamalous- too small for moon and much too big for even the largest stars which were also out. It looked like something entering the atmosphere but it did not look to be moving or if so only slowly. I thought a rocket? Satelite? We watched it for 10 mins. I took photos and videos. Object visible in photos but invisible in videos. High strangeness as I made 3 attempts to video. I eventually took children inside and thought if it was the moon we would see it as we left.sky was mostly clear some clouds may have been low on SE horizon-unclear. Very dark sky because we live on an island in middle of a large bay.As we returned to car an hour later, object completely gone and sky clear.video sound and photos recorded event. We were not afraid- fascinating and I felt a sense of elation and confusion when video screen went black accept for trees when I tried to film.
