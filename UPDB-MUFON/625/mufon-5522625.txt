The Iron chicken's nest from the Clangers TV series

My parents took me to the fair at Banbury, it was an annual event around the end of October, I was 5 or 6. I was in the back seat of my dad's car, retuning home and as kids do, I was bored so, I tipped my head back to look up out the rear window. And there several hundred feet up was the Iron Chicken's nest. So I told my mother who informed me I hadn't seen it as it couldn't exist, because it was only on a TV show (69-70 UK Release Series 1). I didn't see it depart because I had to sensibley sit back down in my seat incase of a car crash. It wasn't rain drops on the glass as it was a clear dry night, it wasn't a star. The object was a big as a fingernail when your arm is outstretched. Thats all really, I've no history of mental illness and went on to Serve 22 years in the UK armed forces, I saw plenty of aeroplanes and helicopters in my time, and I'm at a loss to what it was, I can only think it was a big space station, but they wern't up there were they then, and it do look like a jumble of silver scrap metal. No one else saw it and I don't recollect the local paper, the Banbury Cake reporting it in the next few days, I never had any visits from officals and neither did I get abducted.
