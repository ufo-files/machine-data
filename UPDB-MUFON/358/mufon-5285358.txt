Stationary orb in the sky for 15 minutes before disappearing.

I was laying in the hammock of my backyard watching the clouds roll by a d noticed a bright sphere in the sky behind the clouds. I didn’t think much of it at first, but it didn’t move, and the clouds were moving passed it. I watched for some time to see if I could figure out what it was. It was a bright sphere from what I could tell. I pulled up my star maps just to make sure it wasn’t a planet or stars that was suppose to be particularly visible, but nothing show up. I was facing west towards the setting sun so I’m sure the suns reflection from exaggerating it in some form but for an object to sit perfectly still, as everything moves around it seemed very odd. I took a couple photos of it but it doesn’t reveal much. 

Two days later I checked for sightings reported by other people and there were a handful of people who seem to have seen the same thing. So figured I’d report my sighting as well. I’ve had many experiences over the years but none where I was able to get a photo, even if it is totally inconclusive. 

Anyway, after about 15 minutes, it just disappeared. Gone.
