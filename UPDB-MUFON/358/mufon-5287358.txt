Sphere shape UFO

This one was an spheroid in front of my house when I was 8 to 10 years old. It wasn't making any sound. It was moving very slow. When I saw it i thought it was a balloon, but then I understood it wasn't because it was moving horizontally from south to west. Then I ran to grab the object, but it started moving fast and I climb a fence to see it and I saw it when it become invisible before a tree. Before I saw one bigger in the sky orange and spherical moving from north to south with my friends. On Iraq I saw other one a white spheroid moving from north to south between year 2010 and 2011. The combats jets came but before the UFO went into a cloud and become a light and it was gone. Combat jets came before disappear and I count around 60 after the UFO was gone. In Fort Hood I saw one with triangular shape in 2012. After that I haven't see it again. Maybe because I am most part of the time inside a building or at home. Then one Captain told me that in a debrief he was told the extraterrestrials are working with the U.S. military.
