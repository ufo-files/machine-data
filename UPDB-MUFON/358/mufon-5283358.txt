Saw triangle craft shadow at dusk approaching yard i was in. No lights no sound. Went directly over us (500‐600 ft judging angle over trees it went over coming from the west with setted sun light behind it) slowly and 3 of my friends witnessed it too.
Summer of 96 or 97.
We assumed we saw a spy jet or some sort of that.
Thought what a cool thing to see.
Then I lived close to airbase 1 year later in Marrietta Georgia, realized jets are very loud and very fast.
