Took dogs outside, dogs began barking, looked up and noticed slow moving light

At approximately 9pm, Sept 29th 2017, I took the 3 dogs ouside to do their buisness, I was having a cigarette on the back deck. At approximately 9:15pm, the 2 older bogs began to bark upwards, towards the bushline(NW), as I live in a forest area, I thought nothing of it at first, until I looked above the tall maple. I seen a very bright white light slowly moving North across the sky, then it made an instant right turn(NNW), traveled that way for a bit, then it made a nother instant turn, but 1801 from where it was traveling. Then it made one more movement, it continued along the path that it traveling when I first noticed it. It continued to than move in a straight-ish line heading North. It then started getting smaller, turned an orange/red colour and vanished. It wasint a long sighting, but it didnt look or move like a satellite, man made and space debris. Ive been noticing more strang night time activity in the sky here, but tonights was the most stunning. I will also note that once the object was almost overhead, the 3 dogs ran onto the deck and began clawing at the patio door to get in the house. I was also on the phone with my boyfriend during the first few minutes of the sighting, but hung up to focus on the object better. I unfortunately wasint able to get a picture or film.
