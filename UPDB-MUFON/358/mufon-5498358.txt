{rjl} seven lights changing configurations from v to straight to v and others

oct. 22, 2014 between 7:15 & 7:20 pm 
grover beach ca.

visible sky about 140 degrees vertical from south to north
total transit time, less than twenty seconds.

sitting in my spa looking at stars, I saw coming out of the haze,
south from the direction of vandenberg air force base,
seven dull light gray circular lights that formed a V
with gaps between them.

at first I thought it was one large shape, but as they came toward me, 
they moved into different configurations until they were almost above me,
then they stopped momentarily and swarmed into a line from east to west.

while they were overhead, on the bottom of each grey object, 
I saw a white light, a circle with a tapered wing shape on either side 
about the same width as the circle.

then they moved north out of sight over my house.
I got out of the spa and hurried to the side of the house
and saw them in a formation again disappearing into haze into the north.

my feelings while this was happening was that this couldn't be what I was
thinking it was, to satisfaction that I was at the right place at the 
right time to see this.

I have no belief that there will ever be proof or a confirmed explanation during my lifetime of what I observed.
