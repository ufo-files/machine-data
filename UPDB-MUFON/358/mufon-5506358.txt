{nd} something falling through our atmosphere

I was walking my dog when I looked at the sky and saw a bright fireball with a tail falling to earth. I assume. It was pink, yellow, and orange. My dog and I ran all the way home, and I told my husband, and I called a friend, and even emailed a sort of expert. I did not realize I could report it on here till now. It was falling towards New Hampshire. I live in Maine. My husband and I watched it fade out of sight. Likely a meteor, but it was pretty big looking. I never found any reports on it.
