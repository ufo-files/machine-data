Observed orbs around highway, one entered car

I was driving through Nebraska on I-80 somewhere west of Kearny. It was about 3am and I thought my daughter was sleeping with her back to the door facing me. For nearly 30 minutes I was watching lights that appeared to be dancing in the sky, but near the car. I was trying to figure out if they were airplanes or what, my mind would not accept that they were just balls of light. Then one came down and paced the car on the passenger side (we were going about 70mph), it was about the size of a baseball, glowed white but faded into red and some orange. Then it changed direction and came right through the car, near the rear window, over the rear dash, behind the back seat. It lit up the interior of the car like a 100 watt bulb and slowly floated just inside the rear window then through the wall again and out of the car. In a few moments all the lights I could see in the sky were gone. I felt strangely calm but utterly amazed and I just sort of stared down the road. Just then my daughter said, "Did that just happen?" I wondered if she had seen the same thing I saw so I asked, "what do you think just happened?" She described exactly what I saw, and had not been asleep. She saw the dancing lights, and the orb that came through the car. Then I told her that's exactly what I saw and we were quiet the rest of the trip. She told me she felt strangely calm as well, like this would normally be something to totally freak out over but it's like it was just something that happened. Neither of us told anyone for years.
