One object moved toward the other then moved backed, then the other one moved toward the other.

My father was stationed in California in the sixties,his mother had a heart attack and we were on the way home from Oklahoma.I remember we were in the desert late at night,and I was in the back seat looking out at stars when one star  started to move over to a another star,back,and forth it went then stopped and the other star started doing the same thing.I told my parents but they ignored me, I watched this for what seemed forever and all of a sudden they took of in opposite directions.
