{nd} Two very bright gold/amber orbs

Friend was outside and called us to come out and take a look at something. As I looked up I saw 2 very bright orbs of light traveling slowly South and continued for over a minute. Lead orb continued at a steady speed as the trailing orb slowed and blinked out then came back on a couple seconds later, continued for another 15 seconds then dimmed and went out. Lead orb traveled for about 10 more seconds and also dimmed out and neither were seen again. This incident was witnessed by several people who were with me at the time and friend who called us out said she noticed two people across the street looking up and taking pic/video with cell phone. The picture I have attached was taken during incident. I have seen these orbs two streets over from this sighting location in the past 4 months at my apartment on 2 other occasions and have filed a report 54346 with mufon concerning those 3 sightings. I felt these orbs were very similar to the previous lights I saw except thought at first these were slightly brighter. I received picture from another person at sighting as my video was washed out by the parking lot lights across street.
