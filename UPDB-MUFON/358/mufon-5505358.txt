I was walking my dog on sunday afternoon at the school across the street from my home. As I turned to head back in the direction of my home I just happened to look up and saw what appeared to be a sphere gliding silently across the sky. I couldnt tell what it was and took my eyes off it for a few seconds to get my cell phone out of my pocket to record it. I managed to film a brief video of the object and I even managed to zoom in a bit for the final few seconds but im still unable to recognize what the object is. I kept walking my dog and as I walked beneath a part where trees overhang I lost sight of where it went.
