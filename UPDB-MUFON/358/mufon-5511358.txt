{nd} White dot heading Sw 2 Ne 1st strange, 4 sets of 3 orange lights SW to NE

Went out front facing SW. Observed a white dot, not blinking, heading NE, speeds caught my attention, slowed down, changed direction, towards the north, briefly, headed back towards the NE, slowed again, no abrupt turns, very slight, not too noticable, headed towards the east. 
20 seconds later....
In the SW sky, three orange lights, in a triangular pattern, headed towards the NE. Another set showed up underneath them, another set, and another set. 4 sets of 3 triangular lights, orange, approx. 8:30-9 pm on April 26th, 2013. They seemed to absorb into each other, after a period of time,disappearing into the first set, then disappearing altogether. Wow! Ive seen a lot of stuff, but nothing like this. My family witnessed this. I keep asking them, just to be sure that they saw the same phenomenon. They are as dumbfounded as I am....
