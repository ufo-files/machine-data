flickering bright star-like object hovering for 45 minutes observed

While going out for a smoke at 4:45 am Jan 16, 2012 I spotted a very bright, flickering star-like object approximately 15-20 miles to my north hovering est 3000 ft, depending on size. Due to its brightness and changing intensity I knew it wasnt a star. There are no other visible stars in that section of sky at that time. However, I waited until now just to make sure it wasnt there again today before filing this report.

I stayed out for about 20 minutes keeping an eye on it. It varied in intensity the whole time. At its brightest it almost took on a shape similar to (only thing I can think of) the Capitol bldg in D.C. That was very vague though due to its distance. Because it was so cold I went back inside, peeking out the front door occasionally to make sure it was still there. Went back out around 5:20 and watched it for another 10 minutes. I had noted a location my 1st time out, above a tree across the street. The 2nd time out I saw that it had drifted slightly north above the next tree in the same front yard. About an inch worth if holding a ruler up. It had gone in a perpendicular direction to the rotation of the rest of the night sky.

Rather than twinkling like a star, it flickered quickly and constantly changed its intensity. There were no other aircraft visible the whole time and it was a cloudless sky. Since it was so cold and I was getting sleepy (and wanted to see the end of The Longest Day on tv) and it didn't seem to be in a hurry to go anywhere, I gave up watching and eventually went to sleep.
