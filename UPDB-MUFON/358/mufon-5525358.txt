It appeared out of a portal above the neighborhood nextdoor. My daughter was afraid. I was standing on the sidewalk. As it approached it had the most beautiful white light. I tried to debunk it. I looked at my house, the street, My neighbor's house and the light did not luminate anything but itself. It was oval white 30ft. by 60 ft. and no sound it just passed over a tree that was atleast 100ft. tall and I was underneith the object and next to the tree. It was so close to the treetop, I thought it was going to scrape it. It didn't touch it.
As it passed over me, I was directly under the center of it. I had to turn around underneith it to watch go away. It crossed the street, then I went to get my wife and son to also witness it.
