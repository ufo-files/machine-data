{dr}Glowing orange red light appeared and slowly moved then faded out

I was outside enjoying the evening when I looked up and saw a very bright orange red light on a starlike object. It slowly moved west and looked like a plane on fire. It got brighter and then dimmed out and disappeared. It was brighter than any other star. About 15 minutes later I saw another one that appeared in the same place in the sky and this one moved faster in a southerly direction. It faded out the same way as the first one.
