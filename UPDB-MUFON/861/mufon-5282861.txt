A giant rectangle of blinking lights.

First saw what looked like all the stars above the truck were blinking, then realized I could see stars and a couple airplanes in the same area above me, and that the 20 or 30 blinking lights were all moving in perfect unison like they were attached to a giant rectangular shaped thing that was invisible.
There was no undulation or movement when it came to the relation of the lights themselves, they seemed to be rigidly in formation, or attached to something invisible.
It moved pretty slow, almost like a fast moving cloud in the wind.
It was HUGE, as in perhaps the size of three or four football fields arranged in a rectangle.
The lights were sort of a typical blue/white/green like a star, but smaller, and blinking seemingly at random, never in unison.
Parked the truck and got out to watch this thing sail overhead. It made no noise whatsoever. 
If it had had a visible body between all the lights I think I would have been terrified, it was so big.
Took maybe 15 minutes for all of it to fly overhead and proceed to the south.
When it got way out over the field to my right (south) it seemed to tip upward on its edge, like if it was a rectangle, it turned up tall ways with its edge toward me. Last I saw before it was out of sight, it looked very tall and thin instead of wide and flat.

I kept thinking the way it moved so slow and was so big, it could perhaps be some sort of flare net or something, but it was so fixed and rigid, there was no drifting between the lights. Looked very much like all the lights were attached to something invisible.

Populated area with lots of clear sky, and several cars drive by as I observed on the side of the road. Surely someone else saw the thing too.
