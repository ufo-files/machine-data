FLASHING LIGHT IN THE DISTANCE IN THE SKY

I was looking at the night sky catching a breeze and noticed a bright but small flash to the west of me at about 60 deg. About 30 seconds later there was an other flash slightly to the south and 1/2 Deg. or so lower from the last position. About 30 sec. later an other flash a little more slightly to the south an about 1/2 Deg lower from the last position. I waited an other min. or so and didnt see any more flashes.
