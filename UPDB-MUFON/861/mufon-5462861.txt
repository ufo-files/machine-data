{tjd} Me, my sister and my mother were on our way home. We were driving down South River Rd in Elmore Ohio when we saw a ufo hovering in the sky. My mom pulled over because the ufo was in our path

Me, my mom and sister were on our way to Elmore, Ohio and saw a huge disc shaped object hovering above the trees. My mother pulled the car over because she would've drove directly underneath the object. The object was so big that we had to tilt our heads and look underneath the top of the front windshield. I'm not sure how long we were pulled over as that part seems fuzzy to all of us. It was disc shaped but in the middle were separate windows that were visible but dark. Bright multi colored lights pulsed around the whole objects. No sound what so ever. Then suddenly it went straight up and in a matter of seconds it disappeared into the sky. It was probably around 9 or 10 PM. When we finally got home we were all shaking and we explained to our father what happened. The next day we heard on the news that a Russian satellite fell from the sky in the area that we had our encounter. For years I forgot about this experience until one day a few years ago I suddenly remembered our experience in vivid detail.
