6 objects, all white, all as big as a airplane, 3 on one side and 3 on the other forming a arrow.

I was driving west on I40 in Albuquerque NM. The sky was clear, no clouds. It was about 9:50am on 8/22/07. I was just getting to the Coors exit when I looked up in the sky and saw 6 huge objects, all white, all as big as a airplane, 3 on one side and 3 on the other forming a arrow. One broke away from the pack then went back. I looked down for 2 seconds to get my cell phone to take a picture and looked back and they were gone. I have never seen anything like that. It was like 6 white passenger airplanes flying in an arrow. They looked like passenger airplanes without the wings.
