we saw a huge rectangular object

In the late summer of 1978 mid September at around6:45,a friend and I saw a huge rectangular object about 30 feet above the powerlines in north Bastrop LA.My friend pointed it out to me as we were leavinganother freinds house7Bwho was not home7D.It was sitting stationary to the north,we were about 50 yds from it.We couldsee a light in the upper left corner ,I could see the left side, front,and partof the bottom.We watched it in awe for it had no wings or propellers and made nosound,it was about 50ft tall and 100ft long.I tried to see the right hand sideand walked over about 15 feet to get a better view,as I did this it began tomove slowly and spiraled up .Upon the 3rd spiral it shot straight up anddissapeared in a matter of seconds.The entire sighting lasted for about 15minutes and it was just starting to get dark.I am certain someone else must haveseen this object due to the size and being close to a main road.I looked in thepaper the next day,but there was no reports.We didn't report this and I amreluctant to talk about it because of the riducule we recieved from our freindsand family.
