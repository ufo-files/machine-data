{cak} Four triangular shaped objects, glowing with pulsating or blinking colored lights consisting of orange,red and yellow sighted at approximatley 10:06 PM above or just west of the village of Central Lake, MI.

I was at the time of the sighting lying in bed reading a book at the time of the occurrence.
My brother walked outside approximately 10:06PM and noticed four triangular shaped objects coming in from the west. I was called down to observe and at that time, which was 10:14PM, I could make out three of those objects. My brother described seeing a flash of light and hearing a bang and one of the objects disappeared apparently before I had arrived to witness the event.
There were three objects which were triangular shaped , appeared to be just west of the village, suddenly one of the three vanished . The remaining two glowed very brightly, appearing to be of three distinct colored lights sources from the objects. The colors were of an orange/yellow/ red pulsating or blinking nature.
Movement was slow and after a minute or so they changed direction to the south west and faded from view.
I continued to scan the area of the last contact sighting and also to an area of sky behind me for any other objects but observed nothing.
I felt a great elation to having viewed the objects and tried to keep calm and make some mental notes of the type, distance, speed and direction of travel.
