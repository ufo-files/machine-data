{rjl} Witnessed bright orb like objects hoovering the the sky on 6-7 occasions in a 4.5 hour period.

My wife and I saw very bright object in the north/north western sky over Chandler PHX) AZ at approx. 7:30pm on 4/21/16. We found a good vantage point and park the car. We observed 5-6 very bright orbs like objects in the northern sky. They would glow very bright and then, over to the north, stop and fade in and out. Then move back on the same horizontal path. Stop and glow. We went home and I saw 2-3 objects in the same areas of the sky. I got video and still with my cell camera. One 3 occasions the same lord appeared in seemingly the same spot in the sky. It would glow white and light blue. It would be solid white at times, and then have a center with a halo and the light seemed to swirl and change shapes. It always moved North West to north horizontally and faded in and out. I last observed this around 11:30pm. At that time I went to bed.
