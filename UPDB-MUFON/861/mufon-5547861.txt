Witnessed fastwalker with 2 other people.

While on a smoke break in the rear parking lot of Penske Corp. myself and two other witnesses watched a Fastwalker very high and bright in the sky go from W to NE at a very high rate of speed. As it neared the NE it started to climb out of site. Very bright and very fast. A commercial aircraft flying in opposite direction at altitude made the object easier to document how high in the atmosphere it really was. I will give a more detailed report to MUFON Investigator as I am at work.
