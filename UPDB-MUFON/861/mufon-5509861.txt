Spherical like craft hovered in the middle of the sky watching the fireworks display with colourful blinking lights.

We were watching the light show fireworks display out on our west facing balcony for Canada Day Celebrations. Off in the distance we spotted this spherical looking craft just hovering as if it was a balloon. It had twinkling lights--green, red and white. We watched it hover in the middle of the sky for about 5-10 minutes..then, it started flying off in a descending fashion over the horizon.
