{jpg} Observed

I must have been around 7 or 8 (around 1974)? I was walking to a friends house when all of a sudden I had the sensation that someone was looking at me? I turned around a saw some shiny lights hovering above the treeline that was to the right and rear of my house. At first, I was confused thinking it was a helicopter? Then I realized that in fact it was several orbs. They were several different colors, all shiny like Christmas tree decorations. A sense of calm came over me, then I heard a voice that was neither masculine nor feminine. Telepathically it spoke to me and said,"WE WILL ALWAYS BE WATCHING OVER YOU." I nodded my head, as to say "Yes I understand." And then the orbs slowly moved to my left, which would be to the East. At this point, I turned around and continued on to my friends house. I don't remember this "encounter" affecting that day? Later I would be able to remember that day. This memory is around 40 years old and yet, I remember it as it was yesterday. Thank you for allowing me to share this with you.
