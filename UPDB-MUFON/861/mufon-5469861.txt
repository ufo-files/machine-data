{jpg} Zig-Zagging at high speeds, impossible for known aircraft, light-trail

Was outside with other co-workers, shoveling snow around parking lot. Sky was crystal clear, hi visibility, zero cloud cover and no ground lighting due to power outage in the area. (from winter storm the day before) Saw the sky light up in my peripheral vision, heard a low-frequency boom, sounded very far away. Looked up and saw an object moving very fast in one direction, leaving a light-trail behind it, covered a large area of sky very fast, then without changing speed changed direction about 170 degrees. It then appeared to slow a bit, make a few more abrupt changes in direction then exited sight very quickly, again a low-frequency boom was heard after. This all happened in what I would guess to be no more than 3-4 seconds.
