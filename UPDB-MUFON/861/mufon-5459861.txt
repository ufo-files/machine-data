{tjd} Bright white cylinder with black band moving slowly across sky north to south

I was walking up Hilltop Drive and observed a bright white cigar/cylinder shaped objected slowly moving North to South...No Sound..No contrail..No wings or tail...there were several commercial jets at various altitudes criss crossing the sky ..and the comparisons were strikingly different...Photos show no object present although it was plainly visible in viewfinder...Video showed very blurry object, and I was able to capture 1 single frame from video that was clear enough to upload. I zoomed in on this picture and clearly see what appears to be two objects with a dark space in the middle. The various planes observed in the sky at the same time this object slowly moved by made for a stark contrast. The jets glinted and reflected in the setting sun. This object remained a super white color with zero reflectivity. This was our second sighting of this object in the past 2 weeks.
