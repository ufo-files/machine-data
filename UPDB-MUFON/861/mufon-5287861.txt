Glowing orb captured by my Ring camera

On April 8, 2021 I was reviewing the footage captured by the motion detection on my Ring security camera while I was asleep. I usually only have videos of critters coming onto the deck to eat some of my cat’s food. Between 3am and 5:30am, six videos of a bright, glowing orb hovering above a chair on my back deck were recorded. For the entire duration of the orb being there, my cat was asleep underneath the chair it was hovering over, seemingly unaffected by it. At one point a raccoon comes onto the deck and also appears as if she cannot even see it.
