Small red-orange orb visible just prior to sunrise in the woods.

I went out to my tree stand before sunrise for a day of hunting. Up in my tree stand I noticed a small red-orange ball of light hovering about 5 feet off of the ground and approx 15 foot from my tree. It then moved up and past the tree and up again and then disappeared. Approx 10 minutes later I heard a stick crack behind me. When I looked back to see what was there was nothing there but he heard what sounded like a loud breath in my right ear. No sensation was noticible as I was already hot and sweaty. Only the sound. I then left the woods and have not been back to that tree stand again.
