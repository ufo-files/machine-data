Metallic object

Driving to work down the M1 in Nottinghamshire between junctions 26-25 Southbound around 10.10 am.
The sun was very bright and to my left, I spotted a metallic glint to my right and presumed it was an aircraft or helicopter as we are close to East Midlands airport. As I travel the route on a regular basis I thought it to be odd and continued to watch it for around 30 seconds whereas it completely disappeared from sight.
Thinking I may have caught a glimpse of Venus in the daylight I made a point of doing the same journey in darkened hours this morning and nothing appeared.
Weather details were exactly the same for both days.
