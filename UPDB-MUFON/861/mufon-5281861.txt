Thought it was geese

I walked out of my shop and looked up. I could see the moon on this clear day, next to it I saw maybe 9 white objects. I thought they were snow geese, but they weren’t moving, so I tried to get a video, but once I thought my camera was on video and I looked up again they were in a circular formation with one in the middle. Once I figured out my phone wasn’t in video mode I looked up again and they were gone.
