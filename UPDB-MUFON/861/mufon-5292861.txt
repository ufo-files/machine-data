6 to 8 bright lights over the Camp Pendleton Marine Base.

6 to 8 orange (star shaped) bright lights appeared to shoot up over what could have been the Camp Pendleton Marine Base area. The lights were solid and not blinking or flashing. They appeared to look like stars, only larger and more of an orange color. They moved East in the sky and then disappeared. Two of them turned a bluish green color before they dissapeared.
