Orange ball of light, less then 500 feet traveling silently from south to north then ascending .

Sitting around a fire pit in the yard on new years, about 15 min after the ball drop. Lots of people in the neighborhood were setting off those mortar style fireworks, like an excessive amount of them for at least 10-15 min. As the fireworks died down I was looking up, there was fairly low cloud cover, not raining but solid clouds. I then saw an orange, for lack of a better description, ball of light come over the trees on the south side of our yard. For a second I thought it was a firework but it continued at a constant speed, not fast, just a steady straight path, across the yard, over the house and toward the northern horizon. Then instead of disappearing over the horizon, it ascended slowly into the low clouds. Slow moving, silent, no sound. I did have my phone in my pocket but by the time I finished repeating what the f%$^ is that and staring in amazement it was disappearing into the clouds. Rookie move.
