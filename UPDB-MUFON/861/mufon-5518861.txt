Strobing lights flew at very high speed across Bristol and took a right turn towards the south

I was on my deck looking East towards hartford and saw a very bright white flashing light flying across Bristol, the fastest object Ive ever seen. No sound or shape what so ever, just the strobing light. got to about the Hartford area in 5 seconds and took an abrupt right turn towards the South and it was out of range in less than 8 seconds total. It was not the flashing towers in Plainville and it wasnt a plane. Im planning on making a full report tomorrow.
