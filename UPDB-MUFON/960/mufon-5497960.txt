Red orbs hovering

I was visiting my father, who lives around the corner and was going to the grocery store to pick up items for dinner. I grew up around the corner from the area so i am very familiar to what is normally in the sky in that area. I spotted the lights immediately as i turned from Pottruff Rd to Ede street ( east bound ). I then stopped the car on the SW corner of Ede and Nugent Dr to take the picture and watch the lights. I originally thought that it was helicopters. I noticed that there was no flash of white light, that i normally see from airplanes and that the red was a different colour from what is normal. I observed them hovering for about 2 minutes and decided to move to different vantage point. I headed south on Nugent to Kentley Drive and turned east on Kentley. Objects were then on my right, or south, they had to have moved, otherwise the orientation in the sky would not work. I observed for about another minute and lost sight of them after that.
