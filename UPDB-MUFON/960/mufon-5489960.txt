{rjl} Observed Orange orb during fireworks, then turned blue. After hovering for a few moments another orb joined the blue orb

On the evening of July 4, 2015 approximately 9:06 pm, I was standing at the mobile station with my son located at 4801 Laurel Canyon Blvd., Valley Village, CA to watch the fireworks. To the west of the fireworks about 50 to 100 yards I observed an orange orb which was ascending, once it reached about 500 feet it started to head east at a 45 degree angle towards the fireworks. Moments before the object went through the fireworks the orb turned blue, as it was passing through the fireworks the orb became slightly obscured from the smoke. As this was taking place a second orange orb looked like it jetted up from what appeared to be the Los Angeles River.  Once in the general vicinity, the orb that had previously turned blue began hovering giving the appearance that it was waiting for the orange orb from the Los Angeles River to join it, moments later it did just that.  Once the two orbs joined they went on a north by northeast trajectory which went directly over my son and I, then disappeared. As I was observing this objects I felt there was a childlike curiosity, as if they were had wanted to observe the fireworks.
