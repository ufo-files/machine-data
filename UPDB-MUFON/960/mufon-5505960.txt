{kt}Pulsating light high just below cloud line aircraft were visible during observation last pulse bright then vanished. Out of place.

Dec. 20 2013 5pm heading home from work. I just passed WG exit on the turnpike heading eastbound. The annoying usual as cars race to get around each other to get nowhere. I come around bend to long straight section of the turnpike heading toward Rt. 1 and I observe 2 aircraft lights or so I thought. One was higher and further out above the clouds. It was bright for the distance but not as bright as object 2 and clearly was an aircraft of the usual kind seen every day. Object two was much brighter,high but I would say just below the clouds. I thought it may have been another closer plane at first but it was a pulsating light which I thought was peculiar. As I watched I noticed it was not moving so I thought maybe a helicopter hanging around from the accident in the area earlier in the morning. As I closed in the object stayed in the same place I was getting closer to being under it and thought I might see something that would help identify it. The pulsing was about 1 second maybe slightly longer per pulse smooth from dim to bright no flicker or flutter. Just as I was thinking I would get a better glimpse from under the object it got bright then vanished. Just gone. No further observation of the object. Ill accept any answers I can be offered and would like to know if anyone else reported seeing it. I do know that not everybody looks at the sky but my eyes don't miss when something is out of place up there.
