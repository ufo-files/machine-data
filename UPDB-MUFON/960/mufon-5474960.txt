On Channel 2 News weather report A white light entered from screen left, just above the city's skyline, and rapidly crossed to exit screen right.

Monday morning during the local Channel 2 News broadcast, the station was showing a live shot of Charleston Harbor from a web cam when a white light entered from screen left, just above the city's skyline, and rapidly crossed to exit screen right. 

Definitely was not a plane and was too low to be a meteor 1 it was just above the city skyline. Additionally, we had cloud cover at the time and were it a meteor, it probably would not have been visible due to the low cloud deck. 

No one at the station commented or seemed to have seen it. My wife and I re-watched it on our TiVo a number of times trying to determine what the object may have been.

I have emailed the station, but received no response. The speed with which the light traversed the peninsula that was within view was quite astounding.
