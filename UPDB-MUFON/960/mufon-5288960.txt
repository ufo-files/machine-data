UFO over Boulder city

Just happened. Looking north. Just south of Boulder City. It was square and long and black and was shiny. Went up between the mountains and hovered for a few minutes. Went above the cloud and then hovered. When I looked down to put my phone on video it was gone. Luckily got one picture.
