{jpg} 2am Looked out saw a Star, then it moved left and back then in circle, flashing lights

woke up at 2 am to get water. Saw a bright star, south, then all of a sudden it moved to the left and back. I thought it was a star. It flashed, like a light moved around it. It darted back and forth, then would sit still, then went in a circular motion, then sit. It was exciting when I realized it had more motion than a star has. Fascinating, I watched it for 15 minutes before it moved farther south and out of sight. I love to watch stars, this was not a star
