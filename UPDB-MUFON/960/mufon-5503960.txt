{kt}A craft with Christmas lights spinning around it hovered over the tree tops for three minutes then took off fast

Around 1994 myself and four other friends of mine were standing outside of an appartment building. Someone said look at that so we all looked up and I saw a craft hovering over the trees. I couldn't make out the shape but I could see a row of lights spinning. It reminded me of a row of Christmas lights... It stayed hovering for about three minutes. It quickly took off staying right above the tree line till we saw it no more.
