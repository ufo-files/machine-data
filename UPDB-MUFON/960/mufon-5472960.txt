{rjl} I saw a bright star in the southern sky below the Big Dipper and then it disappeared and trailed off quickly.

On Saturday October 14, 2017 at approx. 5:17am I was on my driveway leaving for work and I looked up in the clear sky at the moon and stars. As I looked to the south I saw the Big Dipper and below were some scattered stars and a bit further down was what looked like a really bright star. I thought maybe it was a planet that was in a closer orbit to the earth. After looking at it for maybe ten seconds it quickly disappeared in a short trail of light in a southern direction, away from me. I was shocked by what I just witnessed, thinking that I might have just witnessed a UFO.
