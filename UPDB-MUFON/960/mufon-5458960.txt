{jpg} Bright amber colored object moving slowly north/west in a straight line, then abruptly stop - change color to bright blue/white then disappears.

I was coming home from work and about to board a northbound CTA Redline. I was looking at Jupiter in the western sky when I noticed what I thought was an airplane moving north/west. The object grabbed my attention because of how bright the initial amber color was. It continued to move north/west at a conventional pace for about 45 seconds. The object then makes a noticeable stop for about 30 seconds, changes to a blue/white pulsating light. The object then quickly increases in brightness until it completely disappeared.
