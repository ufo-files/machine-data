Twinkling light slow moving

I was outside the store when I noticed a strange fluttering light to my right in the sky.. it was pretty bright and fluttering.. I then noticed it slowly started to move across the sky. It stopped for a second and started moving up and down then to stop and move again across the sky.. I finally started recording and that is when it seemed to pick up speed and go behind the clouds. 
I didn't hear anything coming from it at all and it was pretty high up. It's small so it may be hard to see but look above the traffic lights where the sky is clear heading towards the clouds on the left I also added another video I recorded a couple months after the first sighting..of small white lights circling each other in and out of the clouds right above the house... now there is no club, or advertising lights in that area 
I have seen these lights before and if different areas of my neighborhood.
