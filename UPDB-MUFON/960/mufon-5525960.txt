Three red orange lights in triangle formation.

While sitting at the beach at night, we witnessed three red orange lights in a triangle formation above the ocean. They made no noise and hovered. After a few seconds, they disappeared. It was over the Atlantic Ocean and appeared to be nothing manmade.
