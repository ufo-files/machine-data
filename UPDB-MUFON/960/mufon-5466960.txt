I was lying on my back in my backyard in the Greenlake neighborhood of Seattle, Washington. The object flew directly over me, right in my line of sight. I immediately thought it was a U.F.O. It didn't look like anything I have seen with my own eyes before. It was shaped like a disk, however the top and bottom were slightly convex, not flat. It flew from the West to the East, until it was out of sight because of the trees in my neighborhood. It was flying faster than the airplanes that fly over my yard and a bit lower (I live 15 miles from an airport). The airplanes that fly over my yard are quite loud, but this U.F.O. didn't make a noise at all. I didn't feel anything in particular, except excited to see a U.F.O in broad day light! I immediately started texting people, to tell them what I saw.
