Large V/triangle shaped craft that moved silently. Disappeared at incredible rate of speed.

I was driving home when something very large in the eye caught my eye. I was stopped at a light near my home and only got 15-20 secs of view. The craft was very large v/triangle shape. 9-10 lights lining the V pattern. I heard no sound from the craft. The craft was heading NE at an estimated 15-20k feet. Dark in color either black or dark gray. It made 2 movements. It seemed to descend rapidly and then abruptly shot out of sight along the same path it was traveling. I am not aware of any other witnesses to this phenomenon. Life changing.
