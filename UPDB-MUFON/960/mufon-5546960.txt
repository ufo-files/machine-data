A Large object made up of 7 or 8 lights

I went outside for an evening cigarette an i looked out to the town we live outside of to see a mass of 7 or 8 lights very slowly moving from west to east. By the time i had fully grasped what i was seeing My girlfriends mother had came outside and seen the same object i called down for my girlfriend to come see said lights and by the time she had gotten upstairs the lights faded out one by one. I made note that the object was very very low and move silently
