Two moving lights in sky

Every morning for years I walk down my driveway around 6AM to get my newspaper. I always stop and search the sky for anomalies. This is the first time I saw something I couldn't explain. In the western sky I saw what appeared to be a strange configuration of four stars. All the same size and brightness. I noticed this because I had never seen this configuration before, it was so unusual. I watched intently to see if there was any movement and I noticed that the two stars in the center were moving slowly down and away, perhaps more westerly. I was careful to watch for a few minutes and noticed that they were definitely moving away from the other two "stars" They were way too high to be planes and then, as I was watching, they just "blinked" out! They weren't shooting stars, there wasn't any truly fast movement and they didn't fade, they just disappeared.
