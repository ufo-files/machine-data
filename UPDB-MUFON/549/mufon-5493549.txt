{dr}Bright blue ball of flames that looked like it landed near needles ca.

On may 14 2008 at about 4AM me and a couple of co workers was heading to work towards twenty nine palms on route 62 north from the 10 east. we were on the 62 for about 10 minutes, by the wind mills still. as we drove north 2 out of four of us saw a big bright blue ball of fire that look as if it got brighter the closer it got to the ground. we watched it untill it desiperd behind the hills ahead of us but it looked to be at least 200 miles away when it crashed. after we all freaked out and talked about it the rest of the way to work. 6 mounths later i was surfing the web and came across an event that took place near needles ca about an object that crashed out there near the river and for about a week after they had the black vans out there. it may have been a metour or something but i believe it was something else because i didnt tell anyone else but my wife about that morning and when i found that out on youtube about the black vans in needles it came to me that that would be the direction of travel and crash when we seen it coming down. i know now theres more than what were being told.
