In my office typing a letter, saw light from corner of my eye

On Dec/3/2020, I was in my office, typing a letter on my computer, I suddenly became aware of a light from the left corner of my eye, I turned my head slightly to the left then moved my upper body to my right, as I sat in my chair, with my left hand, I grabbed my cell phone, which sits in a small nylon phone case, attached with screws to my old wooden desk. I did this as nonchalantly as I could, I pushed the camera to start with my Motorola Moto e5 cruise Android, photo size 4:3 8 MB camera and was able to get 4 photos, before it dematerialized. I quickly looked behind my oil painting, which was behind the weird light, as well as up my ceiling, though I knew with was futile since the only light source in my office, is a single 40 watt LED, inside a glass globe.
