{tjd} Several groups of lights following same path.

Over a period of approximately 20 minutes we saw three sets of satellite like objects following each other. In the first group there were 8 lights traveling in a straight line following each other towards the east. They were mostly spaced evenly but a couple of the lights would overtake the others. The second group, within 10 minutes time had 9 similar lights that behaved the same way and were following a similar track and heading. Within 10 more minutes, we saw a third set of lights , this time only 4 lights, acting the same way but flying on a slightly different heading to the east southeast. They looked just like satellites but seemed to fly substantially faster. There was no sound at all. There were as many as 7 lights visible in the sky at one time so it was hard to say for sure but, it seemed as though the lights that were able to move faster would overtake and then slow down to allow the others to catch up again.
