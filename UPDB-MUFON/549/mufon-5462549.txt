{cak} loud swoosh then lights going back and forth for a few minutes then gone.

Standing at my bonfire pit with my sons, very loud wooshing/jet engine slowing, only lower octave. We look at each other and both looked up saw a set of light that were either running back and forth across a very large craft, or a very small craft running a tight zig zag formation, it went quiet a distance and while i was watching, i thought i lost sight, soi stepped away from the fire and nothing it was gone. about an hour later a light was moving directly out of the north and a small light dropped out of it falling to earth miles north of me, maybe in the Missouri river?, Any way I watched to other light not flashing get about half way across the sky behind my house it it disappeared, or shut it's lights off. Regardless it was gone. I wouldn't say that these things happen all the time but I am this was the fourth and fifth sighting from here. I have seen the one with the lights running back and forth 2 times in 8 years. I believe that if I were outside more at night this would be observed much more often. Thank you for your time.
