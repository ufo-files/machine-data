Strange triangular object with extremely bright light.

This summer, around the middle of September me and my cousin were on the rooftop of my garage, observing the stars when suddenly from North-East appeared a strange super-bright light. 
At first we thought it was a plane as we saw a lot, but they were way higher in the sky, had two lights(red and green) on both wings. 
The light appeared as if there was some sort of gate that opened from the bottom of the object (slowly). It was so bright that it lit up the whole sky and you could clearly see the clouds. Suddenly it was brighter than when there is full moon. I tried to take a photo of it but all i captured was a bright dot (so i deleted it). As we were taking photos, the light started to come towards us. We then started to worry and become a little scared. The light was coming closer and closer, until it was right above us, but it didn't stop, continued. As it was above us i saw the triangular shadowy-black shape. But the strangest part is that there was absolutely no noise. 
It then passed until it hid far in the horizon (South-West).
I am sending this now, because i just watched a show on Discovery where i got the name of the site (mufon).
