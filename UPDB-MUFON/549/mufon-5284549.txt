ufo shining in the night sky

I woke up at 5.30 am. It was still dark.
I went to the window and suddenly I saw a luminous object the size of a basketball. The object was flying over the mountains of Farsan. It was going from west to south.
I quickly turned on the camera, opened the window, and filmed it for about 30 seconds.
Working on the film, I was able to choose the best frame. I zoomed in on it with the software.I also attached the photo.
