{kt}A bright white light caught my eye and within a second faded to a tiny white dot and was gone.

I was driving on 65 N towards Nashville at around 4:30pm, glanced out the window and saw a bright white shining light. It looked at least twice the size that Venus or Jupiter appear in our night sky but this was in the afternoon in clear blue skies. Within a second it faded to a tiny white dot I could barely see then was gone. At first glance I thought it was Venus but had never seen anything so bright or that big. I was shocked when it disappeared, especially so fast. I was pretty excited after seeing it because I've never seen anything like it before. It definitely blew me away. I saw several planes and jets right before and after the sighting and this was neither.
