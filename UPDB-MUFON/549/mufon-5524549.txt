Strange blinking object - Satellite? Space Station?

I was out back smoking a cigarette before bed, watching the night sky. Beyond the tree tops, a gold object came into view. Immediately I thought it look similar to a satellite, just moving a bit faster. The object had a direct path across the sky. It was much higher than a plane can go and much much larger also. As it passed over head, I could make out an elongated triagular head piece with a large blinking light on the top. There appeared to be separation and a cube coming off the back of the triangle. It looked like some type of space station, but I found that the space station doesn't have lights. This object absolutely had a large blinking light on the front. The best description I can give to the overall experience is a "space station missile". I believe this was a man made object just because of the flight path and steady blinking light. Whatever it was, it was large.
