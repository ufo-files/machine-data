{nd} Large Grey angular over beach area proceeded south along coast toward Oxnard. appeared to shift from horizontal to vertical. Disappeared from my view.

My son and I were in Ventura California 7/3/16 at approx 1:00 PM 1300 Hrs. As we were turning from Seaward Ave. onto US101 freeway North, I noticed that there was what I thought was a parasail near the beach. Since my son was driving, I was able to look at it for a few seconds. I then realized that it was way too high and hence way too big to be a parasail. It was large and grey with a very angular shape that is hard to describe. We were in the turning lane onto the freeway and traffic was heavy so we were commited to our turn so we took the next exit (Sanjon) and came back south on Harbor Blvd toward Seaward Ave. While on Harbor Blvd we could catch glimpses of it but it seemed to be farther away and had shifted to a vertical position and was heading south along the coast. By the time we got to the beach at Seaward Ave. the object was gone. I was very excited to see this as I've never had a sighting as an adult. I had my smart phone in my hand the whole time but was too excited to think to snap some pics or video. I'm pretty disappointed in this fact but at least my son saw it too.
