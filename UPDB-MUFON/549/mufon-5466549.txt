A few minutes after nightfall, around 8 p.m., I went outside for a walk (as is my habit in the summer, to avoid the heat of the day). I walked on the sidewalk in front of my residence. The sky was clear. At 8:26 p.m. (CST), I noticed an unusual light approaching from the east. Since I live relatively close to a municipal airport, I am well acquainted with seeing aircraft in the night sky. But I quickly realized this object was different. It flashed and pulsated with a white light. Quickly I grabbed my digital camera (which I keep on hand for situations such as this, since I have experienced multiple UFO sightings at night in my lifetime). It wasn't until afterward reviewing the video that I realized the object appeared to be rotating. Also, there was one big light in the middle with two smaller lights - one on top and the other on bottom. It appeared to fly in an upright, vertical position. I have included the video, along with still shots, with this report for documentation.
