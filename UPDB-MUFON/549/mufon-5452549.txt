Orange Lights Ascending from Mountain Base to Mountain Top

I came up to our rooftop deck on Deluge Drive at about 8:20 pm. I immediately noticed at least 4 or 5 dozen red-orange lights ascending from the base of the Sheep Mountain Range immediately north of our house. There was a steady stream of them that covered the mountain in the twilight. I ran downstairs to get my partner and he came up. We both watched the lights, which had now dwindled to about a dozen lights. After about 20 minutes, the last light ascended. I am attaching a video of the last couple of minutes of the sighting.
