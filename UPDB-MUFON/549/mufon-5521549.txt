saw round reflective ball near tail of smoke from jet, when jet moved East ball moved quickly west, blinked like a star, disappeared from sight

On January 6th 2012 between 5:30 - 6pm I was driving in a northerly direction. I looked up to see a jet and its usual line of smoke that follows, but a round, silver circular ball was hovering just under the trail of smoke. I kept watching it trying to figure out what it was, but had to look at the road to turn in a westerly direction. I went to grab my phone, but when I looked back up, the ball was gone. I told myself I mustve seen a reflection of some sort, but then caught sight of a blinking round ball moving quickly in a westerly direction ahead of me. It looked like a star does when twinkling at night. I tried to keep my eyes on it but it was moving very fast and I couldnt see it anymore. I counted 6 jets in the sky at that time, all with the smoke trailing behind them, but no sight of the object I saw earlier.
