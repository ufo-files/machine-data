7 second Wonder

Chilling outside on roof looking up at sky when out of nowhere I see a shape with two or three Very bright and distinct lights, the shape and lights moving slowly and horizontally across sky towards my left. I start freaking out and tell my friend to look up at it. He looks, sees it for a moment just as it up and vanishes. It vanished in a blur and was not seen again. Very Bizzare. I was disturbed the rest of the night.
