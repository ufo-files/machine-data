Object flying betwen buildings in La Defense Paris

I personally did not notice or observe the object directly. I was able to locate it after developing the 35mm negatives as the photos were taken with a Pentax analog camera. The object appears between the buildings, not above them. Some people seen in the photo can be used as a reference to know the time between the two photos. The photos I show are scanned from the printed photos, I currently keep the negatives.
