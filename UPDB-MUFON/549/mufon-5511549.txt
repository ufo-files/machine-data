(GS) glowing gold golfball in size hovered from backyard over the house, paused then traveled slowly over trees on the next property observed it was only 20ft up in sky, blinked 2 or 3 times then disappeared

I walked out my front door around 10pm. Walked down driveway and turned around at the house and witnessed a golfball shape in size very bright gold glowing like orb over my house. Slowly rose up about 25 ft from the ground. It paused for about 2 minutes and took a path west very slowly. I thought it was somekind of bug but I knew it wasnt. I was about 30ft from the orb. It traveled about 40 feet on a northwestern pattern, it blinked about 3 times then it dissapeared. I seen the same situation happen when I was a child. But the orb was only 10ft above the ground. It sat there for a few minutes then shot off upwards into the sky really fast.
