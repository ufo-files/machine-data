Shocking.

I went outside to my Balcony to call a friend. It was 12 Midnight Exactly. I was feeling sleepy. I yawned and looked upwards to the sky. There was this strange looking Perfect circular object in the Sky. At first I thought I was dreaming. There was a little moisture in the air. It was raining a little. It was not noticeable. But suddenly the Rain increased and at the exact same moment, the Circular object started to flash Green Lights around its circumference. The lights flashed after every 2 seconds. The object started to hover around the sky. I turned on my Cell Phone's camera and just as I was about to click a picture, My Phone mysteriously switched off, without displaying a message. The battery level was around 80%. I tried to turn it on but it didn't help. The phone did not start. I noticed the object's trail. It was circling around. The Circular shaped object was Circling around in the sky. And suddenly, it disappeared. Left no sign. Nothing. At the exact same moment, My Phone turned on, and the strange part, it was right where it switched off. The camera app was open and it did not capture any image. I saw the time on my phone, It was 12:5:10. I couldn't sleep the whole night.
