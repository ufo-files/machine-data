{nd} four to five ufos

just before sunset in the past month we have seen four to five seperate UFOS in about the same area each time. We live in Sibley, MO. me, my mom, and my little brother were pointing out planes after dinner so we were looking to the sky. all of them appeared to be extremely silver and starlike very bright. one of the sightings (around 9pm at the beginning of August) i had just walked outside to get some air right as i opened the front door i notice a bright starlike sphere.after the object passed the open field just past the tree line a bright green light fell from the sky to the ground about 15 minutes later all the dogs in the area all started freaking out all at the same time. but they all seem to appear out of nowhere and fade in and out until they disappear for the night.
