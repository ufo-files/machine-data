{tjd} Obeserved a solid glowing sperical light of white and yellow color that appeared in night sky. I followed the light across the mountain tops .

On a pretty clear night I was seated on the 3rd floor of our home across from my husband. We live about 5 miles north of Summersville WV. It was 9:21pm when a glowing ball/object appeared in the N window out of the top room of the house. The 3rd floor room of our home sits at about 2200 feet in elevation and has 18 windows giving us a 360 degree of the sky. The object moved fast and was gone in under 8 seconds. It changed direction and almost appeared to swerve or bounce as it went across the sky. It was a zigzag pattern of flight but not with sharp directional changes. Pretty clear night with some wispy clouds in western sky, the moon was a quarter moon and seen clearly in the night sky. My husband did not see the object as he was seated with his back to the north. He saw the look on my face as I followed the object but thought I was looking at a bug flying around inside. :-) I did not get the chance to bring it to his attention as it happened so quickly and I was busy trying to analyze what I had seen.
