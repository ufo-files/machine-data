Blue light was blinking in the east sky from Scottsdale, Arizona. Thought it was something on a mountain, until I realized there was no mountain where I was looking out to. The light blinked randomly for awhile, and then descended quickly. Reascended, blinked a bit more. Seemed to hover and shift left and right a bit, until it took another quick dive towards the horizon and I lost sight of the blinking blue lights below the treetops.
