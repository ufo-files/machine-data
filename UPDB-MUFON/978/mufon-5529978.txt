6 orange balls in formation

driving east on allyn st. 8 ORANGE balls of light traveling north .one ball in front two sets of three in a triangle pattern and a sixth following dissapeared one at a time in the same spot dimming out (maybe behind a cloud) .when I saw a formation of bright orange balls i stopped driving and watched for a few minutes .when they one by one dissapeared i then turned around and got on the highway there is an overpass on I95 n. overlooking the mystic river unfortunately they never came out of the clouds in any direction.they looked to me like flares but i never see orange bright balls for flares moving in a straight path and staying at the same height there are no training grounds for the military here airplanes here are green red and white these were bright orange. there was also no noise
