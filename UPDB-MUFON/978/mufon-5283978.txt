Light thought to be aircraft abruptly accelerated toward ground

I was driving west on I-80 just east of Gary, IN headed back to the Chicago area. There were a lot of aircraft headed toward Midway airport as this was the Sunday after Thanksgiving. I had my eye on what I thought was an aircraft (it was just a solid light like landing lights on commercial aircraft) that seemed to traveling lower than the other air traffic. The sun was going down and the light was clear; this was a solid light moving at the same speed of the other aircraft above it. After about 20 seconds of watching the object, the object flashed suddenly and shot at incredible speed toward that the ground. The light faded and suddenly disappeared when it got near the ground. My wife was the other wife witness and gasped at the sudden speed of the object.
