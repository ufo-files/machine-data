2 friends and I saw a large black triangle with 4 underside lights fly overhead on our 10th floor balcony during the evening

On April 22 around midnight my friends And I were having some cigars on my 10th floor balcony and enjoying the first warm evening of the spring. It was a nice clear night and we could see a few stars in the distance. Our conversation was interrupted by a pulsing sound and we all looked up to see a large black triangle flying overhead at a pretty fast rate. It had 4 large silvery silvery lights on the bottom of the triangle, with one light in the center and the other 3 at the corners. There was also a strange dark green aura around the front of the craft, which outlined the front 2 edges and seemed to glow. It took about 5 or 6 seconds for the craft to fly overhead and out of sight from our balcony. It seemed to be fairly close to us, or just very large. All 3 of us heard and saw the craft.
