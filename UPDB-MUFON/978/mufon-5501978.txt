{cak} Strange objects spotted near the JB Bridge crossing the Mississippi River. No lights or sound.

When just getting off of Interstate 255 via Exit 2 I noticed a metallic object in the sky. It was very brightly reflecting the sunlight. At first I thought it was a plane but the reflection of sunlight from it appeared very bright. The object appeared small but it did appear larger later on as it got closer. It appeared grey at a distance but then it looked black later on. It was very close to the JB Bridge. The shape is hard to tell with how bright it was but it looked like it was the shape of an oval. The object was first far away from my location. There also appeared to be another object in the photos although I did not see it unlike the main object. They were nearly above the JB Bridge. My son rapidly took 13 photos within a about 7 or 8 seconds. The objects traveled very quickly in a very short amount of time. It is hard to say how far they traveled because they appeared too far away to tell where they were precisely. They traveled way too fast to be planes or helicopters. They both moved north west toward me. They both traveled together too. They then started traveling south and they both left "streaks" in the sky. These streaks looked as if they were both at lower elevations than the objects were at. They both moved fairly quickly then but definitely not as fast as they did when traveling north west. I lost track of the object because I was just getting off of Interstate 255. During the sighting I felt very excited but a little frightened to see such an unusual object. After the event I felt as if this had to be shared.
