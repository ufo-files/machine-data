Two bright orbs high in sky. Changed direction and seemed to circle each other before flying north at high speed..

Two crafts flying over large man made lakes. (Gravel pit) Appeared to be searching or observing entire lake area. 

Objects flew independently. One covering south end of property while one covered north end. Crafts were able to fly at high speed and quickly stop and hover. Changed directions over 20 times. Crafts then circled each other before shooting north/ascending at extremely high speed. Lasted 5 minutes. 
Sighting was at 6a.m. in mid October of 2019. Sun had not come up yet. So I was unable to make out any shape or color of craft. They were only visible from the light emitted. Very bright green/white.
