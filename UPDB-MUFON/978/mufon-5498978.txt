{cak} 12+ silent craft moving very fast and another that was different from the others

I was going out to my work truck to get something. It was a bright star filled sky and I looked up and saw 2 separate set of blinking lights , flying next to each other, moving very fast from the west to the east, faster than any plane that I have ever witnessed. It was totally calm out, not a cloud in the sky, the strange thing was that there was no sound. By the time I got through the house and grabbed my wife to see this they were almost gone on the east. We live in a field in the country, we can see a great portion of the sky. My son and I went back to the west side of the house where we observed upwards of 15 more pop up on the horizon heading in the same direction as the first 2. The kicker that made this very unusual was as one was directly above us both of us saw what looked like any ordinary star that was stationary all of a sudden start moving very fast towards one of the craft and when it got close to it make a right angle turn like it bounced off of it, making a check mark in the sky then either disappeared or became stationary again blending into the stars. There was no sound the entire time of any engines. This is truly the strangest thing we have ever seen.
