Event started about 9:45 and viewed it moving from S to N front our lake front lot on Hess Lake, passed overhead a bit W yet over head, I viewed it with others and even viewed it through my zoom lens camera, B7W film showed no images filmed seemed ruined by the event. I worked in photography and film developing in at least 4 jobs lasting 20 plus years. At first I thought this light was just a early bright star in the S looking S.over the lake and was showing my nephew it and it seemed to be moving very slowly to the N. in our direction, As this was early Spring it was getting dark at 9:30, I went and grabbed my Camera and told at least 2 other adults of this object moving towarns our area. It took at least 20 minutes to where it was much brighter by then it was completely dark and everyone said it was or most be a Blimp, I recall telling them that a Blimp at that distance would be able to be heard as I have been to many evens where blimps have come over, This Craft was Long ciga like and in my camera appeared very dark color and I could make out faint lighted area's like windows, but never seen any images of people or aliens. This craft just seemed to float S. to N. without any sound , except a very low pitch hmmmm, None of the other adults believed it was anything and kept drinking beer and the next day seem as if it never took place, because I had wanted to report it the night before as it took place. I can't give a exact date, but I left there within a week and have Telescope set up just in case and my friends nick-name for me is The Night Stalker as I'm up nearly all night every night to just check out the Universe and make sure all the stars are still shining.
