{rjl} Triangle 2 red light 2 white silent no emmision hovering

Nj parkway going twrd Galloway noticed 2 red lights over the road & 2white lights as we got closer appeared to be triangle.. 2 miles up as I got closed I was high beam-in it off and on and cutting lights to get attention then we pulled off the side road less mile before we jumped in the back of my truck and watched for sometime. it move up in down slightly like air passing under hovering, silent, no emissions, no propellers hard to tell how long we watched. Came a lil closer then went over tree line till we lost sight. I can still remember my friend slapping his face saying I can’t believe what I’m seeing!!! As I did too!! 

I thought could of been diamond shape back was like hard to see until passed in front of moon. But def triangle💯
