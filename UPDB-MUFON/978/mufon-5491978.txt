{jpg} Looked up upon going out of my house to car. One larger white light accompanied by small red light accelerated straight and disappeared.

A couple of my friends, my girlfriend and I were at my house after hanging out and having a couple beers. We arrived at my house afterwards and I forgot my phone charger in the car. When I was walking out to my car about 45-50 feet above me at about a 48 degree angle I saw a larger white orb of light accompanied by a smaller red orb of light directly underneath it. Traveling west it zipped about 50 feet and then disappeared right in front of my eyes. During the event the air was electric. As if it was filled with static electricity. I know for a fact what I saw. I am not sure what to do next.
