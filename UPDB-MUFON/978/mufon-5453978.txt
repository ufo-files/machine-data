{jpg} appeared like helecopter with no rotors, no sound, much smaller than a HUEY

Small tear drop, front looked like a navy A6 with a dome like window, entity turned head to observe me when passed. no noise, no visible means for propulsion. entity appeared to have helmet and O2 mask.  Don't know he exact number of craft as I was focused only on the one closest to me, could have been 2 -5 craft. NO NOISE they all were flying nape of the earth, 10-15 feet in the air, less than 15 miles/hour if that fast. There was no prop wash observed on the vegetation. That was a Friday night 28 Feb 75 just during sunset. I was hooking up a turning plow for the garden so I could plow the next day.. The Tuesday prior, late after dark, while feeding cattle, I noticed a light around the cattle. My tractor was equipped with a roll bar and sun shade. I continued to feed the cattle and noticed a white light shining on the area around the cows, The spotlight had distinct edges on the ground around the cattle. and the source appeared about the size of a volleyball I shut the tractor off and the only noise I heard was the cows eating hay. neither was there a prop wash on the hay or vegetation I threw the hay as normal until I finished and drove the tractor down the hill side. I then stopped ~50yds and the craft appeared to have small red lights above the beam. I watched it and (must have blinked my eyes as it was on the far side of the pasture. I still believe , but not as convinced that all this was a black ops project. Hard for me to believe in little green men. A long time ago but vivid in my mind.
