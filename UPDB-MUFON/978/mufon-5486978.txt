{rjl} 3-6 orbs changing color from white to red. Pulsating.Multiple witnesses.

I was at work in mid-city Los Angeles when a fellow co-worker told me to go up to the roof to see some unidentified objects high up in the bright sky. It was approximately 4 pm and I joined the rest on the building's roof and saw 3-4 bright orbs in a near row traveling from east to north, then change direction from east to west. These lights were very high up, changing from white to bright red, seemingly pulsating. Even though it was so far away it was very bright in the clear blue sky. The sky was clear and some of the objects seem to appear and then disappear. The objects were moving very slowly, it was defiantly not airplanes or helicopters. The original employee said he saw 6 of these orbs but when I got to the roof I only saw 3. Fifteen minutes later they disappeared. I took some video but my video does no justice to what we saw because I had no tripod nearby and it looks like a tiny white dot in the blue sky. The object was also pulsating bright red (crimson) and that did not come out in the video. Interestingly my co-worker's cell phone was malfunctioning, but my sony HD video camera was working fine.
