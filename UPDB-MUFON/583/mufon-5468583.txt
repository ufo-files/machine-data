{cak} Attached video as viewed on my infrared security camera for 7 plus minutes.

I live in a rural environment and it was a Late winter afternoon when my property caretaker/ neighbor came to my home to tell me we had a U.F.O. visible on a security monitor from one of our infrared security cameras. I asked him to go to the camera location to see if he could visually observe the U.F.O. and to check the camera lens for any debris. He could not see the U.F.O. with his eyes and the camera lens was clean and free of debris. While there, he waved his hand in front of the camera lens as observed in the video. 
We capture the video from the wireless security cameras on memory San Discs in the monitors, then transfer the memory San Disks to the computers for viewing and saving.  

Later that afternoon my caretaker/neighbor brought over the memory San Disk of the U.F.O and my first reaction was to laugh, as it reminded me of the old Corvair car, or the Jetsons car from the long ago T.V. series and certainly not an interstellar craft capable of traversing the cosmos. Be that as it may, it does show some resemblance to the typical saucer-like U.F.O. and hence my reason for posting it here.

As observed on this misty video, which I shortened, you can see the misty environment cleared up when the object and colored orbs left and the late afternoon became clear and mist free.
