{kt}my entire family witnessed this event..

WE HAD GONE TO OUR LAKE HOUSE ON LAKE LIVINGSTON TEXAS, WE WOULD LAY IN THE STREET, AN WATCH THE SKY, LOOKING FOR SHOOTING STARS, SATALITES, THE MILKY WAY.......... IT WAS ALWAYS REALLY DARK SO WE COULD SEE REAL WELL, MY WIFE NOTICED SOME LIGHTS COMING FROM THE NORTH JUST ABOVE THE TREE LINE.. THEY COVERED THE ENTIRE HORIZON FROM LEFT TO RIGHT, ALL WE COULD MAKE OUT WERE THE LIGHTS, BUT THEY MOVED AS ONE UNIT, NO SOUND. WE THOUGHT AT IST IT WAS A SQUADRON OF HELICOPTERS.. WE TRIED TO WAKE UP OUR NEIGHBORS BUT NO ONE WAS HOME.. IT WAS MOVING RIGHT TO LEFT TOWARDS OUR SON ON OUR DOCK WE YELLED AT HIM BUT HE NEVER SAW IT.......WE CAME IN THE HOUSE AN WE SAT DOWN TO DRAW WHAT WE SAW........
