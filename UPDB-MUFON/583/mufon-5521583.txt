On November 30th, my son was alarmed at the sight of a very large, star-like object moving erradically at amazing speed

This video goes with my original November 30th report. It is a large, bright, multi-colored, star-like object, moving erradically, changing direction, yet staying within the same general vicinity. My son had already been observing this thing for over an hour before he came and got me. After only a few seconds of gazing at it, I grabbed my video camera and shot 2 videos and several pictures.  My son and I went inside to view the pictures and were astounded that after 25 minutes of viewing pictures inside, that it was still out there. So I took more pictures. The object was visible for over 2 hours. I never saw it leave and eventually, I just went to bed. I still continued to look for it and it became visible again on December 10, 2011. I have been documenting this object ever since.
