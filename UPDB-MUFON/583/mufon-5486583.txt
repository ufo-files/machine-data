It was huge, massive, tin can shaped with gold round middle could be small wings. two trails of white smoke behind it. Ascended flew up across and down .

Picking up the kids from school, talking to a mom and her daughter asks if the flying object was a rocket. Looking at it, it wasn't a rocket, it was a very large tin can shaped object with gold round middle could've been small wings with two trails of white smoke behind it. It ascended up from the north west about a mile away and it came over slowly at a moderate speed and continued east and descended down. 

Other people noticed it but no one thought it was unusual but me. A friend said it was a weather craft but on investigation none of the crafts look anything like what I saw.
