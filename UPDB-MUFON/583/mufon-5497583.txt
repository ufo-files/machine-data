Observed a Bright object at high altitude.was not moving,Initially looked like Contrail,but never changed

I was at work in Northern BC,and noticed how nice the Sunrise was,so decided to take a few pics of it with my cellphone. I noticed an object above and to the left (North) of the Sun that I initially thought to be a High altitude Jet Contrail. I'm a spotter,and so watched it for a minute or two. It wasn't acting like a contrail. No motion to speak of...just sitting there not changing shape like a cloud or contrail would. i observed the object and took a few photos over a period of 10-15 minutes....and then it simply vanished.
