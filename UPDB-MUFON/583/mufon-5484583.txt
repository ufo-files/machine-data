{cak} Dull lights 3. Wedge shape nozzles in front of lights to obscure brightness. Hard to judge distance appeared to be 4-500 ft up, 80-100 mph

Approx. year 1995. I returned from a trip (truck driver ) At approx 8:30 pm I was standing behind my truck looking west, waiting for my wife to come around the corner to pick me up. Something in the air caught my eye. It was hard to judge the distance but it was near to and above a building that was maybe 150 yards away. It had three lights that were kind of oval in shape and in a v formation . At first I couldn't tell if they were all separate or on 1 craft. Then I noticed a kind of fog coming out of nozzles near each light. As if to obscure them from sight. Indeed if I wasn't looking in that direction I wouldn't have even noticed them. It wasn't until later that I recalled not hearing any sound. I have heard another person that sighted a UFO and recall him saying " I saw the nozzles" would very much like to talk with this person. I was awed by what I was seeing. If my wife would have got there sooner I would have chased after it. I lost sight of it as it flew behind a restaurant and some trees at the end of the block. Had one other sighting on east coast while traveling from NC to Pennsylvania, with three other witnesses. Maybe another time. I normally would not mind giving my name, but I must consider my family and their aspirations.
