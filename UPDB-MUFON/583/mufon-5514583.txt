light hovering, flashing different colours, flashing lights on horizon

I was hanging out with 2 friends on saturday night when i noticed a light in the sky whilst having a cigarette. at first i thought it was a star but it caught my eye and almost 'pulled' me in, I felt a euphoric feeling like i had seen something incredible. It wasn't until a few seconds after that i realised that what i had thought was a star was actually moving and flashing different colours whilst remaining mainly white. The UFO started moving erratically, this made me question what it was, i knew it wasn't a chinese lantern because it was too high up, not a plane as it had no path and not a helicopter as it had no sound. The UFO stayed there for around an hour, we would check it regularly, it gradually moved to the right, then when we checked again it had completely disappeared. Whilst the UFO was floating i could see flashes along the horizon as it the thing was documenting the earth, this was weird though as there was no flash coming from the UFO, only flashes 'landing' on the ground. I have looked for similar sighting and found one nearly exactly the same that happened locally a yea ago, the video showed the same thing i saw! Are we under alien attack? maybe they are just keeping an eye on us?

We did make attempts at videoing it but our cameras weren't good enough

Has anyone seen similar?
