Two pulsating red lights moved across the sky then dropped down at the Missouri River

I noticed a red pulsing light moving from east to west at approximately 30 degrees off the horizon and north of my position by approximately two miles. At first I thought it was a plane, but it was flying lower than normal flight paths. Then I realized that it was pulsing red and that there was no green light as would be expected with a plane or helicopter. When it reached a certain point it simply dropped down and disappeared. Approximately one minute later a second light that looked exactly the same as the first did the same thing and also dropped down at the same location. I estimate that this was over the Missouri River at Sugar Creek, MO.
