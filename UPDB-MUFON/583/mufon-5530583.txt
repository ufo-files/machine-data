Reflective egg shaped

Driving home from work at approx. 830 am. I was driving North on interstate 75 on the exit ramp to 275W. I saw the egg shaped aircraft in the north eastern sky. It had a reflective exterior and was very odd shaped compared to an airplane. It had an irregular body with a wider bottom. It as only hovering in the sky. No movement was seen. It stayed in the sky for a couple of minutes than vanished.
