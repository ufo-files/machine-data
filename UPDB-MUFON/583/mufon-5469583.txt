{rjl} black football shaped craft doing field maneuvers

I was watching late night TV at around 1am on my front porch when I heard a loud boom, like a jet breaking the sound barrier. I expected to see jets from Whitman AFB or fort Leavenworth doing practice maneuvers, but instead I saw four or five black, elongated football shaped craft with no discernible engines but swept back wing configuration bolt across the sky. I saw a mix of standard military moves such as the tactical egg and aileron rolls, and other apparent counter measures that are not possible with current technology One of the craft broke formation and hovered, at which point the craft began to emit purple search lights. Upon spotting the craft I picked up the nearest thing to watch them with which happened to be my rifle that has a 10x power scope. Upon shouldering the rifle, the aforementioned craft began hovering as described. The crafts had no afterburners that I could see or rotors for that matter. At this point I felt I could not move any part of myself except my head and eyes. Then the crafts shot off towards the southwest and I was released from psychological stasis and immediately wrote this.
