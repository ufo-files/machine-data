4 Bright Orbs in Sky Over Ocean

While taking a walk last night on the beach around the Family Kingdom in Myrtle Beach, SC we witnessed 4 bright red/orange objects in the sky in a diamond formation. 
We were walking to the beach by the Swash walkway, checking out the full moon. We heard fireworks to the right of us (south) so we both glanced that way. My wife looked back to the sky, to the NE, and said "maybe that's what we heard". I looked over and seen 4 bright orange diamonds in a diamond shape. They appeared to be about dime size in the sky, they were there for a few seconds then one by one quickly went out. It wasn't like the lights faded out or were cut off, it was a mixture, in between. It was really weird.
I asked my wife what exactly did she see, she said she seen 4 huge, almost basketball size, bright red/orange orbs in the sky in diamond formation. She watched them slowly fade to a smaller star size then fade away or cut off. 
We watched the sky for a good hour or two afterwards and never again seen anything else except the regular airplanes or tour helicopters. 
We've lived here almost ten years, love the beach, visit it regularly day and night. We've never witnessed anything UN-explainable until now. We know there was a rocket launch scheduled, we checked the time and path, it couldn't have been that. We witnessed these orbs before the launch occurred and that launch wasn't viable here.
