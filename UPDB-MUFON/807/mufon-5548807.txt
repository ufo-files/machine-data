Mothership and smaller lights over Denver

My husband and I (and other witnesses) have seen nightly sightings in NH in the Western sky. We are visiting my daughter in Denver and decided to look and see if we could see anything here. We went out to their deck to look. Well, we, my daughter, her boyfriend, and their roommate all saw, with us, a very similar sighting. A large star-like mother ship and smaller lights that move around it. The mother rose from 15 degrees on the horizon to 60 degrees over 3 hours iwth a scout in tow. They rose to the northeast. It made rapid movements to the west and east and went up and stayed stationary several times.
