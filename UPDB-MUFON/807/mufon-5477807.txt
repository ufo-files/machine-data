My husband and I were outside at 8:45 PM watching a lightning storm off in the North sky and talking about our day when I noticed a bright light off to my left. At first I thought is was a falling star and immediately pointed the object out to my husband since he had his back towards it. The object was bright silver and shaped like a bullet or a cigar flying horizontal with the ground. A bright silver light trail was behind it and seemed to be headed toward the lighting storm. Right after I pointed it out to my husband it seemed to slow down and its brightness dimmed for a second or two then became bright again as it speed up and fly strait towards the lightning then just vanished. I was excited that my husband was able to see the object and he is the one that urged my to come report it.
