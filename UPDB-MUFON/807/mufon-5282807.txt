An object at sunset moving slowly that had two lines lit up by setting sun reflection

At sunset, facing west at a set of stop lights this object at first looked like 2 chem trails from a plane… at closer look the object didn’t have chem trails but was reflecting sunlight from the setting sun , two lines close together.. a plane would have shown wings reflecting and would have been moving faster… 
I took 3 photos with my cell phone.  The 4th photo is a close up of one of the shots..
When I turned at the lights about 30 seconds later I was down another street but couldn’t see it any longer…the angle would have been different so it makes sense that there wasn’t a reflection, but no sign of any craft of any kind..
