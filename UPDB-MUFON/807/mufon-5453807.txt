{cak} Several uniformed distant objects

Neighbor came and told me to come outside to look at “this.” Very high up he pointed out a constant glowing object traveling west to East. Here is the thing though, another object just like the first one was in a straight line path behind it traveling at the same speed, then another proceeding behind the second one and continued on for another 12 objects just alike, all equal distance, traveling same speed (speed was very fast given how high the objects were flying.) it was a very uniformed flight pattern. Then at the end there were two flying in tandem. 

There wasn’t any flashing lights, they were a constant dull color. No sound coming from objects either.
