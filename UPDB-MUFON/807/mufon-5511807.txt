{dr}Very large flyng craft

It was about 7pm on a week day earlier this year, late January or early February. I was making dinner when I received a call from my parents who were driving on their way home from work. They told me to come outside because the object they had witnessed flying a few weeks prior was back. I grabbed my camera phone and went outside in my front yard. As I got outside my parents arrived home and pointed out the object to me. It initially looked like normal plane or helicopter lights (red and white/yellowish). However I noticed the lights were not blinking and it was flying at a very low altitude and @ a high rate of speed with no sound emitted. The object was flying south, it then banked to the east, this is when the size of the object became apparent. At first I could only see the center portion of the object, but when it banked I could see the sides of it. Two massive rows of white lights were visible, one row coming of each side of the center of the object making a large V or boomerang shape. If I had to estimate the size of the object it was no smaller than one football field across. After the object changed flight to the east we lost sight of it. The whole sighting lasted only about 20 seconds, and as I stated earlier I thought it was an everyday craft at first so I had put my camera phone away, so unfortunately I didn't get a picture or video. I don't know what we saw that night but I am pretty sure it was not an ordinary commercial or private aircraft. My house is situated in the flight pattern of at least 5 airports, so I see a lot of air traffic.
