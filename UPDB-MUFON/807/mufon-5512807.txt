{nd} bright lights shot straight down from just above treetops and hovered over ground

My boyfriend and I were on our way to the grocery store when this "thing" with bright red, blue, white and purple lights that was flying just above the treetops near a park descended in a straight line down to hover over a football field. For a second it stayed there and then in a straight path, still hovering, it flew over the length of the field, made a right turn, still hovering, to fly the width of the field, made another right turn to go back the length of the field, ascended about 15 feet, and started back down the field. We looked around the park for people to see if it was some kind of toy, but no one was there! The object seemed to have wings, but the body of the object was translucent. All we could see were the bright lights, but couldnt really make out a shape. We have witnessed many occurrences before, so we were once again amazed by this ufo! We lost sight as we drove by the park, but every time I drive by it now I look to see if it will come back!
