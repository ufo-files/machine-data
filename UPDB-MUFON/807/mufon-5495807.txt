Looked like satellite but travelled E to NW and changed direction (smooth transition, not sudden); wobbled slightly.

18:40 CET / 17:40 UTC: I was standing in front of my house ready to get o my car, when I noticed a satellite at about 75deg above the horizon. Did not change speed but travelled from East to West, which rules out satellites. Had a star-like appearance to it (yellow / orange) and was either extremely small or very high. Also it "wobbled" a bit from side to side but I'm not 100% sure; could have been because I kept staring at it. First it moved along a course of appr. 270 - 2801, then it changed direction to about 3401 or more. I'm pretty sure about these numbers because I'm a pilot and I even double-checked my observation on google maps just to be sure.
After the direction change, the pseudo-satellite disappeared in a straight line behind the horizon.
Sighting lasted for appr. five minutes, maybe a little less.
