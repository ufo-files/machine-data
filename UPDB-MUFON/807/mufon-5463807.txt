At approximately 5:30 AM on 12/19/2018, I let my Dog outside. As I stepped onto my porch I Noticed a VERY Bright light to the SE of my Home. It was Cube shaped, and it was hovering in place. The brightness was intense, and as bright as the 3/4 Moon. The Color was a Silver/White.

I brought my Dog in and called my Wife to see what I was seeing. Together we observed the object for another 10-15 seconds. Given that the temperature was very cold, we didn't stay outside long. However, I did go back indoors again and got my Cell Phone. I took a short video of the object. At no time did it move or change position or color intensity.

In retrospect, I wish I had gotten dressed and taken my Camcorder out and got a better video.
