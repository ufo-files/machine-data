{cak} saw 2 hovering objects with identical lighting configuration in the sky

Sorry I do not have any photos or video as the objects were much too far and all I had was my cell phone. I parked my car in the street, and as I walked home on the side walk I first saw one object that at first seemed to be a star, but after observing it a few minutes I realized it had multiple lights that were changing colors and seemed to be flashing. The only reason I felt this was not a plane, helicopter, or blimp is because it was absolutely stationary, and they never moved left to right nor got closer or further away. I stopped at the best vantage point I could find and kept a few stationary objects in view next to the UFO so that I could tell if the object was moving or not. After I set my view up, I glanced around and saw that there was an identical object just north of the first one, perhaps 3 degrees higher off the horizon and farther away. I was not able to tell exactly how far the objects were or how high as I had no point of reference and I could make out no actual shape. I would guess they had 4 or more visible lights there were flashing in my view. They seemed to be slightly circular in shape but from that distance they could have been flat on the sides I could see. I watched these objects for approximately 20 minutes before I decided to go in my home and get my cell phone to try and take a picture. They objects never moved from their positions the whole time. This is my first sighting of an object(s) that I 100% believe to be a UFO. Unfortunately I have no visual evidence but I am making this report in hopes that I am not the only one to make this sighting. Please let me know if there were others that reported this since these objects were plainly insight of all of Southern California. Thank you!
