{dr}Bright flashing red lights going off and on in synchronicity spanning about 3/4 mile across. Red lights were about 50 feet apart from one another. All were in uniform line across the sky flying west about 500-1000 ft above ground.

My son and I were heading south on Metcalf Ave coming back from picking up a pizza from Dominos Pizza. I noticed a strings of red flashing lights across they sky straight in front of us. I pointed to it and my son finally noticed them as well. We were confused and didn't know what they could be. There were 10-14 red lights in a uniform line about 50-100 feet apart blinking on and off in synchronicity. They looked as if they were hovering but then we could tell they were heading west. We turned west onto 183rd St and stopped on the bridge over 69 hwy. I got out of the truck and recorder with my phone. My son gave me his phone to record as well. We were in shock and didn't know what to think of them. The red flashing lights would not show-up on the videos due to focusing issues etc. Went home and told wife about encounter. Turned on news to see if there was any other sightings. Nothing. Very exciting moment and certainly won't forget.
