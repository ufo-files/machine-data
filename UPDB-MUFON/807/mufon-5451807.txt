Silver multi-directional balls of light

At least 3, possibly more, shiny silver balls zooming across the sky, spaced away from each other.. assumed they were balloons at first, being carried on the wind, but then they completely changed direction.. from flying right to left, they came back the other way, then disappeared in a split second... Managed to get a few video shots, which I've done stills from... amazing how fast they're going! There was hardly any wind, almost clear blue sky. You'll need to stop/start the videos to see them, as they were so fast.
