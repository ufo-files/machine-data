Sitting on front porch on the ground level of my home. This is my normal summer habit. I enjoy the stars and spotting aircraft while listening to music. Suddenly I see appear from the porch roof line something that looks like an aircraft on fire. The overall color was a deep red flashing to orange and yellow in various places on the sphere. I turn my cell phone camera on but it was too dim to register. I continue to watch it move silently in a straight line until it goes behind trees in the distance. I imediatly made notes observing the 9 58 pm July 13 2018 time. It definitly was not Mars as it is much more to the East and appears stationary. This thing was much brighter than any lights on any aircraft and appeared to be at a lower altitude.
