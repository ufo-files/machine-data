While visiting a local pet store several cars off the side of the street 3 cars with occupants out looking in the sky, so I pulled off to see what they were looking at and there was a silver sphere hovering. As an army veteran that has been deployed to a combat Zone I can safely say that this was no publicly aknowledged military aircraft. I moved to take a picture of it with my phone but my phone was glitching like it had a virus or something. The other witnesses said the same was happening with their phones. After joking with them about no believing us without proof some of them started getting in their cars and one of them called out to us saying the radio stations weren't working. The rest of us tried our car stereos a range into nothing but white noise static. We then returned our attention to the object, which appeared to be moving closer, moving smoother that any aircraft I have seen or heard of, and it was moving into the wind. Then, if it wasn't strange enough, it just vanished. No flash of light or streaking off somewhere, it just wasn't there anymore. Our phones and radios suddenly went back to normal. This sighting has bothered me for 3 years now. I wanted to at least tell someone.
