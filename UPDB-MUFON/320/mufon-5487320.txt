{nd} pulsating light at great distance

On 11/9/2015 at approx. 505 am I was on front porch in Golden Co. having morning cigarette and looked sw at the southern cross and saw a pulsating light at extreme altitude thought it had to be military jet the light was different then I have ever seen on jets.. watched for 10 plus seconds it then took a abrubt 90 degree turn to the south for 5 degrees and was gone no possible way our technology. The way it went out of sight is like it left earths orbit and at a unbelievable speed
