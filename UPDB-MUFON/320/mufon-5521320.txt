Object Reflecting Light From Sun - Stationary for 5 Minutes - Faded Away

I was out with my camera smoking a cigarette while trying to get shots of the weird clouds that day. Above one cloud - diagonally to the right of the sun - a spot of bright light appeared. It seemed to shine brighter than an airplane and it did not move for the five minutes I observed it. My camera could not focus on it particularly well and I was also so astonished that I forgot, for the most part, to turn on auto focus. The object eventually blinked out and it started to snow.
