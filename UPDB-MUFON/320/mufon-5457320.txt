I was scanned in my car.

Driving alongside a main road near a nuclear submarine station. Driving then suddenly engine and radio turnoff really bright light and a whooshing noise. I was frozen and had a beam of light run from front to back of car. I felt it go through my body and could not move. All objects in the car were lit up - no shadows. Everything in car was alight. As soon as beam moved through me I could move and saw it pass through the car. The engine then starts up it goes black in car and radio works. I looked for witnesses and a car came past shortly after I slowed thinking they would stop to check everything was ok but the car just passed as if it saw nothing. I could not understand how the car behind did not see it. I went home but didn’t speak to anyone for about 3 hours as I was in shock.
