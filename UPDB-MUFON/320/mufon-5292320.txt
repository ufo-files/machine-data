In parking lot Convertible top down noticed glimmering in sky stopped to record

Convertible top down exiting parking lot, noticed something glimmering in the sky. Facing northeast, saw white dot randomly flashing. Started to record before I lost it behind some trees. I had an iPhone SE, captured 1 photo & a short clip. Only other weird thing was a woman rolling slowly downhill on the sidewalk to my left in a wheelchair singing loudly, can be heard on the recording. I posted video on reddit, someone mentioned it could have been an escaped metallic party ballon since it was flashing randomly and going in a relatively straight line. I didn’t take note of wind direction that day
