Driving west bright white light passed overhead in westerly direction. Out of sight in 2 seconds.

I was driving home from Milton Ontario in a westerly direction on 15 side road at approx 6:30 pm. I was looking out the front windshield of my vehicle. Suddenly a bright white light passed by in a westerly direction directly over my vehicle. The light was a sizable dot clearly visible travelling in a straigth line. The elevation was difficult to estimate as it was dark with no significant points of reference. I estimate 1,000 to several thousand feet. It was travelling at a very high rate of speed and was out of sight within 2 seconds. The event happened so quickly and left me wondering what I had just witnessed. I attempted to come up with a logical explanation but I have never experienced a phenomenon like that before. The 15 side road parallels highway 401. I wonder if any others travelling westward witnessed the same phenomenon.
