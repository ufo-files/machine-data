Photos of what appears to be a black, triangle UFO

I took these photos during a flight from Miami to Quito, Ecuador. I would say these photos were taken just as we were entering Ecuadorian airspace. To be honest, I did not see anything when I took these photos, but upon reviewing them, I can see what appears to be a black triangle in the sky. Its very hard to see, but is clear if you zoom in in the photos. In the first photo the object appears near the engine of the plane and in the second photo which was taken almost immediatley after the first, it looks like the object has moved a considerable distance. Also, it appears to me that this object was fairly large given its size in the photo / distance from our plane.
