{cak} Triangle Shaped Craft

On Monday October 7th approximatly 8:30 P.M. My brother in law, Niece and myself were sitting on lawn chairs in the front yard watching the meteor shower when we spotted a fast moving triangle shaped craft with very dim amber lights heading east of us at a high rate of speed and it made a sharp turn heading south east. The craft seemed to be fairly low in altitude and made no noise at all. At about 8:45 P.M. my wife, brother in law and myself witnessed a smaller craft going the same exact path as the first sighting. This craft had no lights and made no noise. (4 witnesses)
