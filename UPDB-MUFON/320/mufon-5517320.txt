(gs) Spotted a flame like object moving slowly, it turned slightly and vanished behind some trees

My husband, brother and I were standing in the road helping my mom move. We happened to notice something flame like flying (it was daylight) in the sky. We were rather stunned and we were trying to figure out what we saw. It flew in a line and then turned slightly behind a tree line. We ran to the back of the house to get a better view and it disappeared. That same night it was seen again by my mother and niece. They saw it again last night along w/neighbors on their street. Everyone describes the same type of object "flying flames". No sound at all.
