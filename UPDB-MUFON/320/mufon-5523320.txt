Craft sighted with multiple lights blinking sequentially, hovering or moving slowly

Driving home from Kansas City to Lawrence, Kansas on highway K-10, heading west near De Soto. I spotted a craft with a series of lights blinking in sequence. The craft appeared to be hovering. I pointed it out to the other passengers in the car and noted that it appeared to be similar to the reports we've read of sightings over Lee's Summit, Missouri. As we neared the De Soto exit, the craft seemed to be moving slowly overhead, moving in a northwest to southeastern direction. If I had to guess, I would say it was several hundred feet up, but it was difficult to gauge size and distance. As we crossed the path of the craft, my wife looked up at it and described it as "triangular." I noted the time to be 8:13.
