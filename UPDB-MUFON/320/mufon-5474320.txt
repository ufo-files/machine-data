Myself and a friend were just going out the back door of a friends home and I saw first light to the southeast, it got brighter and I said to my friend, Wow look at that bright light, pointing. As we both watched two more less bright lights to the left and behind appeared giving the impression that all three were on one object. We hopped in the vehicle and headed down the road keeping lights in sight. A little way on the dirt road we stopped and pulled over and watched and suddenly another light appeared to the right, west at about the same elevation appearing to be maybe 100 or so yards away. As we watched all of a sudden the first 3 blinked out and in a couple seconds the other blinked and was gone too. I said "what is out there that direction ? My friend said nothing much but one mountain range to the east and Alamo Lake to the south and a bunch of desert. The lights appeared to be at least 20 miles southeast of our location.
