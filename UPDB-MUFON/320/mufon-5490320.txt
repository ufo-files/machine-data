My other encounter is still ongoing and in a remote location. I've seen 3 to 16 small craft and a gigantic mother craft. All l could see were masses of flashing and pulsating lights underneath it as it loomed over me incredibly close and l fled. I admit l shook violently for several minutes. The small craft have been following me every night for 5 months and continue to do so. They have not harmed me. I wish l had someone brave enough to share this sighting with me. Only one other person has witnessed the small craft with me but was too frightened to stay.
