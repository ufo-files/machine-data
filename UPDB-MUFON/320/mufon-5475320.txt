{cak} Object hovering over woods then disappeared with no trace

We were driving along a road near Unionville, il heading east...when we saw this object hovering over the woods just ahead. My husband stopped the truck and ask me to take a picture. Couldn't tell what it was...first appeared some smoke aroung it in the ring shape. Appears to have smoke below and around the object. It appeared as a figure 8 on its side. It remained in one spot then simply disappeared. No motion just gone.

If you zoom in on the attached image appears ring mass floating in the air.

We though it a group of birds, or a smoke ring from a ground explosion but hearing non or seeing nothing In the area further eliminated any known source.

After talking with friends a same object was seen in South East mo on the same dayear about 70 miles from our sighting location.
