triangle/wedge shape, reflected city lights from bottom of craft, no sound, fast low flying

Sitting on my parents front lawn with my brother, my wife had a baby shower going on inside and we didn't want to take part, I saw somthing out of the corner of my eye, I instantly said to my brother, "look! A shooting star!" But then I realized it wasn't, a star, but something flying close enough to the ground to reflect the city lights from it's belly. It was low enough that I would have expected to hear engine noise, but there was nothing. It was flying about as fast as a jet going over a stadium before a big game. It was wedge shaped, very pointy nose. My brother didn't see it, as it was very faint in the dark sky. He kept trying to see it but it had dissappeared very quickly, just going in a southern direction. I thought it was a jet, but then there was no sound before and after, I was amaized and curious and no one but my brother belived me, my wife still laughs when I tell the story. About a 9-10 months later, I saw the same thing, while I was driving home late one night after work, but was about 13 miles north of where I saw the first sighting. My parents house is right next to Interstate 15, and I was driving on I-15 when I saw the 2nd sighting. The craft did the same exact thing the 2nd time, heading in the same direction.
