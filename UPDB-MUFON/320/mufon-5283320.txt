Disk over Broadway Bridge

At the stroke of midnight, New Years 2015, I was on the porch of my 8 story condo. When it turned midnight on the dot, a disk illuminated a blue light that circled the outer edge of the object twice. After the light stopped the disk immediately went from a hovering idle to a steady 10mph going SW directly over downtown Portland. If I was not high up on a building I doubt I would have even seen it as it reflected absolutely no light at all and blended with the background of the night almost perfectly. The disk moved slowly into downtown Portland first going over Union Station then slowly over the tops of buildings in the Pearl District. I had two friends with me at the time, although they saw the disk in motion, they did not see the blue light that drew my attention to it in the first place. Along with being in awe of the surface not reflecting any light, I also have never seen a object in my life go from a dead stop to a instant steady propulsion like this object did. It was an incredible sighting that lasted under a minute due to once the object got a certain distance from us it was impossible to see. After a few years I started thinking that there had to be dozens of cameras that captured the object on video with the hospital on the hill most likely having the best chance if they had a camera pointed toward the city.
