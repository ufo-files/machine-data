Red and green and yellow pulsating light hovering at different altitudes for 20 minutes

A light in the sky caught my eye, it was blinking red and green so I checked the flight radar and there was nothing and I had just seen the air ambulance heading southwest not long before that and this light was north. In the binoculars I could just see the lights but no shape to it at all.
When I started recording it disappeared for a minute the reappeared at a higher altitude, became very bright then faded out and reappeared again at a lower altitude. This went on for about 20 minutes until it finally just vanished.
I have no idea what this is.
