{nd} On Nauset Beach saw orange light Hovering above the water going North Direction

It was the end of summer beach party. It had just gotten dark. I noticed what was an orange light, at first I thought it was a plane, but then I notice it had no blinking lights. So I grabbed my camera to take a few photos. The object moved down the beach over the water, in a slow hovering type way. I thought for sure it was going to float on by the people I was with. Then as the light got to me,  as if it knew I had a camera the light faded and the object moved in an upward direction, faded out of sight. A few minutes later I went back to the people I was with and my daughter said to me hey dad do you see that moving star, why is that star moving. So the object was now over head and looked like a star slowly traversing the night sky. This is now at least the 5th-10th one I have witnessed over the past 6 years. I have taken photos of a few, and they mostly come out blurry. I have no reaction to the sighting, people around me don't notice until I point it out, so I don't point them out to people any more.
