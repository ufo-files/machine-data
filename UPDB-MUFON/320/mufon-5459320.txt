{cak} Large object, round, very bright

As usual, every night I was waiting for my wife to arrive from work in public around 9 pm, when I realized that the sky had brightened with a very strong light above me, I looked up and through the clouds I saw a large white light, or the size of a foot ball, so white it seemed to be half a bluish, very strong, apparently round, that was moving very slowly, almost stopping, coming from the direction of the sea, I was looking amazed and at the same time paralyzed , without thinking what that would be, because to the right side of this light was the moon, that night was a half moon, then disappeared after 15 to 20 seconds.
I talked to my wife when she gave the bus, she was terrified, I also talked to my relatives, but I have it in my head, I did not know who to inform so far, I did a research in the internet, where I found this site of yours.
