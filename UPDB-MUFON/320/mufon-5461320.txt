{cak} UFO was swinging, while approaching, without light. Soon it disappeared behind the cloud.

Event happened 8 years ago: UFO was swinging, while approaching fastly towards the land. still, it was very high, just below the clouds. It didn't emit any light. Therefore, I reckoned afterwards it could be some damage in the engine, since it looked like that. I saw the object, while standing at the balcony, the 4th floor in the area near the city centre. The event happened during the summer holiday and not too many citizens would have been present those days in the city. After I called my husband, who was inside the flat, to see the object approaching, the moment I turned back my head to see where the UFO was, it had already disappeared behind the cloud. Therefore, I didn't have any witnesses for that sightseeing, except myself. I had explained then my husband what have happened. He was suspicious. Numerous events occured that decreased my life standard, eversince that occasion. Now, I am divorsing my husband - by his initiative, who was and still is, by the way, very eager to explore topics related to astrophysics, incl. unidentified space objects. Today, I live normally, after that event.
