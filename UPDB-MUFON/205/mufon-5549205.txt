Six bright objects witnessed by four people,fly into cloud,but don't fly out.Only one has memory of event.

We were in Batavia Illinois lying on our backs on my friends pool deck staring up at the clear star lit sky noticeing how weird and unusual the only cloud in the sky looked.I have never seen a cloud like that before or since,and we all commented on how weird it looked.Then from out of the west first then the east,a total of six,three from each direction very bright balls of light entered the cloud but never exited.The four of us stayed up for about an hour,I think,watching and waiting for the objects that we thought for obvious reasons were UFO'S, to come out of the clouds,but they never did.I was 12 years old then and it is a memory that is burned into my brain,and something I will never forget.The thing I can't understand is,when I asked my brother,cousin,and best friend about their account of that night,all of them said they had no memory of that knight.It was one of the highlites of my life,and I guess thats why I'm writing this.If someone can look back at the air traffic control records for the summer of 1973 over Batavia Illinois,that would be great.If you find something,let me know.If not,have a great day. Sincerly,Mark S
