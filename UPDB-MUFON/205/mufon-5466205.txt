triangle craft with red lights on points, silent fly over rendlesham

On a sixers and seconders scout camping trip to Hallowtrees Campsite in Rendlesham forest, UK. During a night hike my friend and I had moved to the back of the group to pick up conkers.
The group ahead had turned corner in the woodland walk when we both looked up to the sky because we'd seen movement.
A dark, perfectly triangular craft passed over our heads, we were aware there are aribases in the area so at first thought it must be an RAF or USAF jet. We both immediately ruled this out as none were that shape or colour in the area. What struck us as most odd was the fact there was no engine noise emitted from the craft even though it had passed within 100 ft of us. If it was an aircraft that had cut its engines to glide in it would have crashed as it was only about 50ft above tree level and we were not near any runways or suitable landing sites on its trajectory.
The corners of the triangle craft had dull red constant lights and it was flying point first. There was a very gentle sounds of air over a low resistance surface as it passed over.
Overall we must have only seen it for about 10-15 seconds but the proximity was alarming as was the notion that it should have crashed not far away if it was un-powered.
We were scared and excite, we lost sight of the UFO as it passed over the forest ride we were stood in and carried on a linear route behind more trees.
