{rjl} Three UFO Seen In Photograph Taken During Total Solar Eclipse Not Visible With Naked Eye

Outside at 1148AM during total solar eclipse. Took this picture facing south toward Scottsbluff National Monument while moon was completely covering the sun. Nothing was seen in sky with naked eye or through camera viewfinder. Photo shows three dark unidentified objects in formation above the city. My first thought was that these were probably birds, but the objects seem to be at a distance that would be too far to be birds, and no birds were seen in the sky during photo. Did not notice objects until looking through photos later in the day. None of the objects seems to look exactly the same. One is long and bullet shaped and the other two have hazy edges.
