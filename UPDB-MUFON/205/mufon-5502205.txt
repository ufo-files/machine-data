{dr}cylinder shaped craft appear over long island sound

I was working as a security guard at the beach park and observed a bright light lift from the Connecticut side of the long island sound and make its way toward the Long Island side. Finally when the craft was close enough to the shore i got out of my car and observed a reflective cylinder raft made no sound and the bright light on the tip of the craft seem to scan the land, the craft was close enough that i would have been able to throw a baseball at it. It slowly flew over head and disappeared over the tree tops. Seeing the craft i was frozen with wonder of what i had just witnessed.
