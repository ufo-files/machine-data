red-orange fireball in Westfield IN. on July 5th. Now have attached photo

Already reported once, but now have photo to attach. I was in the Target parking lot in Westfield, and saw a red-orange fireball go from the Target store almost right above me to Clay Terrace. Travling north to south. Then I lost sight, but got four shots with a Nikon D90. This shot is zoomed in, but untouched. This was definately not an after-burner on a jet. It was going too slow, and no noise at all. My brother saw a red-orange orb in Kokomo just a few days before I saw this one. He said it looked like the same thing, and was surprised I got this good of a shot.
