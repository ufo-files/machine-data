{rjl} Several pinkish purple starlike objects or orbs seen at treetop level

Several pink purple or magenta star like objects were seen at treetop level near San Anselmo-Ross Mt. Tamalpais area. Orbs literally coming from within or behind the treeline and hovering and moving very slowly through this foresty area and over hillside homes. No structure could be seen and these 4 or 5 objects were glowing from within.These objects were stunningly beautiful and floated up and down as they moved.
