Seeing Craft, Enity on the ground, tryed to enter cabin,went back to craft and left.

In1971 my brother Ed, was staying in a small rental cottage in Ashburnham He was watching TV late at night, when he saw colored flashing lights in a field in back. He turned the TV off and the lights as well. The only light coming in was from a security light, from the cottage next to his. He saw a shadow on the shade moving past, with a big oval head, long skinny neck, skinny shoulders and chest. Then the noise of scratching and walking on the the roof. He thought it was the shadow of a tree branch on the shade, but there were no tree’s around. It was a long skinny arm, with long skinny fingers, scratching the window. He was so terrified, he grabbed his 357 magnum and shot at the ceiling. Whatever it was, quickly jumped down and started to run back to the light’s in the field, Ed lifted the shade and got a better look at it. He grabbed what he could, of his belongings, leaving some thing’s behind. Jumped in his truck and arrived at my sister’s house in South Ashburnham at 2 in the morning. Shaking and barely, able to talk. He never went back there. He sketched a picture of it for me, which I still Have. He didn’t know what It was then. But when I saw the sketch, it was a Grey.
