Black silent object, rotating as it passed and disappeared out of sight

I was standing on the roof of my flat in the Regents Park area of London on a bright and sunny evening. I watched a black UFO slowly floating from the distance and pass me nearly directly overhead and continue until completely out of site towards the city district of London. The object was appearing to constantly rotate and was not spherical. 
It was completely silent and didn’t change speed or altitude. There was no wind that day.
