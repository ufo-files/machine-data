{rjl} Triangle UFO sighted by Lake Mathews, CA

My dad and i were headed home on Cajalco1 Rd. from Corona around 6pm when we noticed several lights from aircraft ahead to the east, we both noticed one aircraft was still as we got closer & closer we were speculating what it was. I rolled down my window as we got near, 80ft up & 75 yards right of the road the road, there was no sound and I could clearly see that it wasn't a helicopter. It was a triangle aircraft with white lights on the tips also a red light in the center. I know my aircraft very well & live near March AFB, I have no idea what the heck it was. A few cars pulled over to see but my dad kept going. I looked back and watched it ascend up then dart south. Craziest thing I've ever witnessed.
