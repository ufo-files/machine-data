Green disc or orb near public beach

I was at an outdoor bar/grill at Point Pleasant Beach, NJ on 7/22/2021. There were fire works on the beach and I heard someone behind me say "What's that?" "Is that a firework?" People were looking up and that's when I saw a green circular object moving from south to north about 75-80 degrees to the west of where I was. As I thought to myself "Let's see if it hovers and changes direction", it stopped immediately and then hovered a few seconds and then moved back toward the south more quickly than it was originally moving. Then it rapidly moved back to the north and out of my line of sight. I could not get to my cell phone quickly enough to get a video or picture. Two other people with me saw it. One of the people was my friend that I was visiting and the other person a stranger that we were sharing the table with. Not much more to say about it. Some people were saying "definitely UFO", other people speculated that it had to be a drone.
