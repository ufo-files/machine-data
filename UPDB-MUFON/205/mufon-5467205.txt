{td} Bright object vanishes

I was looking at the sunset when all of a sudden a bright shining object appeared above the horizon a few miles away. I observed it for a few minutes thinking at first it was a star but noticed it emitted a blue light on one side and a green light on Either side of the bright object. All of a sudden it vanished in thin air. I kept looking for a few more minutes in case it was being covered by a thin cloud but it never returned. I love near an airport and it looked like it was directly above it. From the distance it was from me it had to be a couple hundred feet.
