{cak} flying toaster?

I am a graphic artist working at home (I work at a window a window that faces towards the Stead Airport). The view over the top of my monitor looks out on a patch of sky between trees with nothing but the top of the house behind us. I looked up from my monitor and a siver box/toaster looking object flew by. I could not tell how far away it was (If I had to guess I would think the size of a moving truck), so could not tell how fast it was moving; we have hot air balloons up in this area at this time of year, but it was moving too fast to be one. It was slower than a helicopter. Maybe it was a drone? Drones I've heard around here make a lot of noise. I had my window open, and it made no noise. I bolted out to the backyard (a few steps from my desk) and could not see or hear anything (there are a lot of tress in that direction). 

Funny side note: when I saw it I distinctly remember thinking "there it is again", but I have never seen anything like that before (I've seen lights (who hasn't), but never anything that solid in broad daylight)... Anyway, thought I should report it somewhere. Sorry no pics...
