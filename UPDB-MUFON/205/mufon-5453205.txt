Orb-like UFO

There’s this object in the sky with no sound but slightly moving up and down and in circular motions very slowly. It has a bright white light and two smaller lights reddish and bluish. Very faint. And quite a ways away was another smaller object that was flashing a light. I took my dogs back inside and went back outside to investigate with my camera. The object kept flashing three lights and kept hovering closer and then further away. Then all of a sudden about five objects around it that I originally thought were stars started blinking and orbiting the larger object. I took my camera out to go film it and the battery life dropped from 50% to 1% in an instant.
