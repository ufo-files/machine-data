UAP above Franklin Mountains

My partner and I came home from the gym, and as we got to the top of our stairs, we both noticed a bright light hovering over the mountains. It would dim for about 30 seconds, and then it reappeared. It ascended and hovered around 15 seconds before going NE in a horizontal path. In the video, a brief beam of light can be seen. I would also like to note that there is no blinking in the video before it begins to move.
