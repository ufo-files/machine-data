Very bright light moving south to east in Ohio on September 17 2021 at 8:15pm

Looking up in the sky I noticed what looked to be a star but too bright and moving from the south to the east. I’ve lived by an airport most of my life and this was not one of them. I also have a star tracker app and the ISS an Hubble were no where close in the sky. I have seen two others this month one at my house and another at a fair but have no proof so I’m only reporting this sighting.
