Later that week, other witnesses, reported their sightings to our local news station, WJAR NBC10. An unknown object, was seen around the Cranston area. There is a photo of this UFO, posted in their website. I am not crazy, and this event is something that just happened to us, that I cant explain. I felt so strongly about this ordeal, that I decided to reach out to MUFON.
