Me and my son saw 3 lights moving above Mars.

Me and my son were on our back deck looking at Mars where we noticed lights moving really fast above and below mars in different directions. The craft must of been huge because of the brightness and how far they were. We were both shocked at what we saw. This went on for 30 minutes then the clouds came and block our view.
