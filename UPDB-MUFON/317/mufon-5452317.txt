A large stationary bright object began moving, flared up, then dissappeared

I was sky watching last night when I looked at a stationary oval-shaped object that looked too big and too bright to be a planet begin moving from south to north in a straight-line path. the color was a brilliant white. I thought "I wonder if that is a UFO?" when it flared up to 10 times its original size and disappeared completely. I thought it might have been a satellite, but after it flared up or exploded it was no longer there. I watched it for about two minutes. I kept watching for another five minutes to see if it would reappear and continue on its trajectory but it did not. I have seen several UFOs in this same area over west Independence in the last few years.
