{jpg} Explosions and lights

I stepped out on my porch for a smoke. I saw what I first thought was heat lightning, because there was no storm or thunder. I love to watch lightning, so I looked around the rest of the sky to see if any was happening elsewhere. Then what seemed like right above me, but way way above the Earth, I saw two little balls of light. Each of them blinked twice before zipping off into complete nothingness. I ran in the house to grab my phone to take video. I started recording in the area where the light show was happening. The lighting sucked because of surrounding porch and street lights.
