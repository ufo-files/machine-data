This object seemed to be flying in-tandem directly above a low flying airplane and at about 35 degrees E/SE it made an abrupt turn off the flight path and disappeared.

The object came in to sight from about 30 W/NW directly above a low flying airplane. It flew the direct path with the plane across the sky and at about 35 E/SE it broke off the path and dropped off to the south and disappeared. There were no clouds in the sky but it seemed hazey. The object seemed to be rectangular or diamond shaped and wobbled from left to right as it traveled. There were 4 noticable white lights on it, one on the nose of the craft and the other 3 looked to be in the corners. That's what made it hard to tell the actual shape. It was traveling fairly fast and the whole event only lasted about a minute.
