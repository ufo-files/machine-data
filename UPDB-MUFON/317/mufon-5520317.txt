I first noticed a blue light looked like a star but it was a cloudy morning, no stars visible.

As I was driving westbound on Rt 6 near Utica IL I looked to the southwest of the sky because a blue light appeared out of nowhere. At first I was like cool, the only star in the sky! But then I realized it was cloudy and had been raining so there were no stars visible at this time. All of the sudden the object got brighter and was moving northeast at an incredible speed. No flashing lights, no marker lights or anything blinking. It was a solid light source and controlled flight. As the object got closer the color changed to a yellowish orange color. Its kind of hard to describe the shape because it almost looked cylinder but positioned vertical rather than horizontal. The speed was so fast. I guessed that from where I spotted the object and to where I last seen it had to cover at least a 100 miles and the object covered that in less than a 10 second time frame. I kept turning my head to look out the back window and right before I lost visual the color had gone back to the blue color looking like a star again. It was so far away yet I could still see it. The feeling it gave me was that I knew right away it was some kind of craft not from this planet but I remained calm and felt as if they knew I was watching it fly across the sky.
