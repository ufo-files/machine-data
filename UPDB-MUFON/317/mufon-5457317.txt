OVNI IN THE MORNING

We were on the rooftop my father and I, when we saw an object that was floating on the mountain, when we saw it we began to notice that the object was going to the right, then it was on us, made a circular turn on the city passing through the mountains around to reach us, it was going to be 12 noon, when it was set next to the sun and could not even be filmed, it is where it seemed that the object was watering its energies because after that the colors to vibrate with more force and more splendor, I lost sight of him at about 1 pm when he crossed the back of the mountain where he could not see more. I managed to record the object and take a picture, when I saw it on my computer and zoomed in, I noticed that it had well-defined lights, seeing that, it was very scary.
