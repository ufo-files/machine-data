Took photo of ghost in “ The Mountain of Beverly Hills” year - 2011

Got lost hiking at night. Accidentally snapped some photos trying to turn on the flashlight of my phone, saw this the next day. In 2011 I was hiking with some friends in the area that was known was the vineyard of Beverly Hills, which later was renamed “the mountain of Beverly Hills” and has now been seized by the federal government. It one point it was the most expensive piece of real-estate in the country but it has been plagued by unfortunate events with it’s often rich and famous owners succumbing to a crude death. Apparently in the 80’s a crane on the land mysteriously flipped over and killed a contractor. Just a couple streets, south on Chevy Chase dr there is a memorial to a battle between the Spanish and Tongva inhabitants. I firmly believe this area was sacred to the indigenous people and this point in particular is where the collection of energy settles.
