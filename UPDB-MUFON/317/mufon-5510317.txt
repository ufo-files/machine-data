(GS) LOOKED LIKE A FIRE-BALL IN THE SKY

Approximately 10:50pm Tuesday June 11, 2013 I witnessed a glowing fire-ball like object in the sky near Gratiot and 696. Object was traveling East to west then changed direction and went North. I Watched it for about a minute then it shot straight up into the sky, I watched it until I could no longer see it. What ever this thing was it made no sound and left no trail that I could see. When I first saw it I thought it was a meteor until it changed direction.
