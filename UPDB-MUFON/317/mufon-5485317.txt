{nd} Standing in formation with my company and bright yellow light appeared then left.

I was standing in formation with my company getting ready to go do PT (Physical Training) in the morning about 0500 hrs and a yellow circular bright lighted object show up in the sky, fairly close to my unit, it stayed there for a couple minutes then just separated into 3 different yellow circular objects and they all just sped off very quickly in the same general directions but slightly moving away from each other. This was observed by my whole unit out on the basketball court that morning for PT formation, pretty much what we were doing in the formation stopped and we all watched this happen.
