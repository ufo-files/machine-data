{cak} Triangle bright orange silent

The triangle ufo was immediately visible by my friend who was driving car. We pulled over and I got out of car. My friend stayed in car. The triangle object flowed bright orange and was directly over me for what seemed like 1 min. I sensed it was intelligent. I felt it reading my mind. Shortly after I became worried and fearful and quickly got into car. I told my friend to hurry up and leave. We needed to get out of there! We drove off and the triangle craft followed us at high speed until we reached a gas station and well lit area it then swerved a fantastic speed to the right and shot off into the night sky. I suspect missing time but cant confirm.
