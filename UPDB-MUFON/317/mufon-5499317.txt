{cak} Bright white light with cone of fog

Driving the 210 freeway in San Bernardino headed west, approximately 3 miles from the 15 freeway interchange. Dead ahead at 45 degrees vertical was this bright white light, emitting a gas cloud that was reflecting the objects light. It reminded me of a rocket engines firing in low earth orbit where there would be some atmosphere to interact with. The light and its cone of gas seemed to rotate up and become occluded by an unseen edge over a 2 minute period. There was no noise.
