4 Glowing Orbs Travel North Over Central Florida

I was outside having a smoke when I thought I saw a constellation in my peripheal vision float across the sky. As I focused, I realized there were 3 orbs in triangle formation traveling north. They descended slightly bending east. The front orb split into two and then all 4 abrubtly turned northwest traveling at an unimaginable speed over the horizon. They were yellow glowing orbs, very bright. There was also not a sound from the objects and the night sky was almost completely clear that night. I immediately called my friend that I had just been talking to 15 minutes prior and asked him if he had "just saw that!" His response, "you saw it too?!?" I asked him to explain when and what he saw before I told him what I saw. He said it was about 5 minutes ago (3-4 mins before mine, He lived just southwest of me a few miles away) and it was a "tear drop" that "dripped." He said he turned around to get another friend to look up, but by the time they looked back up it had disappeared.
