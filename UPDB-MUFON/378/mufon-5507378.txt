My husband and I were looking at the stars and seen a fireball flying though the sky. Another one right behind it. It came right over our house. We have never seen anything like this before and we see things all the time. It was reddish/Orange (Like a fireball),egg shaped,pulsated and was moving slow. Looked like the cherry of a cig when someone takes a drag. We were shocked.I had enough time to take out my phone take 3 pictures and call my mom twice, so she could go outside and look. (since it was going in her direction).I was using my phone and the pictures didn't come out good. We lost sight when it went behind trees.
