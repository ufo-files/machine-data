Photograph of clouds revealed odd formations and an expanding beam of light emitted from the ground surface.

Driving west on Carson Rd. in Lowndes Co. Mississippi in February 2020, I was photographing cloud formations on the north side of the road. When I reviewed the photos at home later, there was one photo with a beam of light expanding from the ground level into a cloud on the left side of the photo and a truncated oval white formation on the right side of the photo. I saw nothing at the time I took the photo.
