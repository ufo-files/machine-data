Object moving left and right directions appeared on the same time regularly

This object was observed in Turkish town Bodrum-K111kb1k. In August 2014 I was there and this observation was done in the first days of August. K111kb1k is small town near Bodrum city. Bodrum is a city where has a high UFO activity. 
When it appeared in the sky I was at the balcony of a house and locals there showed it to me at 8:08 PM. They told me that these kind of objects were appearing there regularly. I there for holiday thus I had no idea about that. Firstly I thought it was a plane but it kept on appearing at the next days and I took my camera and took some photos. It really appered regularly between 8 PM and 8:30 PM. 
The object was a starlike object. It was looking like an orange star. It was suddenly appering -like somebody turn it on- it was moving on a line to left and right. It was appearing from west and flying over a hill where telecommunication tower was standing. It was small but had a bright orange light with pulsations. After a minute passes approximately it was vanishing.-just like somebody was turning it off-
When I firstly saw it I was suprised since this was my first time to see a UFO with my own eyes. After a few days of observation it did not appeared again.
