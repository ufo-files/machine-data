2 orbs witnessed or possible the same one on Thursday September 16th

On Thursday 9/16/21 I witnessed what appeared to be two glowing orb objects that were moving across the sky over my Townhouse in West Nashville. The first one was to the left of my back patio area headed north east. I did not capture this one on video the second object headed in the same direction and spotted west of my location. I captured this one with my iPhone video in a clip lasting slightly over two minutes. The object appeared to be slightly pulsing or spinning traveling at a slow speed and flying directly over my roof as seen in the video recording.
