Disk shape. 20ft large. with violet glow.

I was in the living room when i saw the disk shaped UFO 100ft from our apartment building. UFO descended on the edge of the adjacent building. In the background there were other buildings. So it was easy to judge distance of 100ft. UFO descended on the edge of the building and pulsating in purple color. Then suddenly disappeared. After ~2min appeared suddenly. Ascend fast and with great speed speed up away to the horizon.
