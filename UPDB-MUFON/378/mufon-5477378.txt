{rjl} Orb came in like comet, travelling east, then took off at warp speed with a high pitch hum.

Travelling home from school with my kids one evening we were heading west in the car when my eldest son pointed out what looked like a comet in the sky. When we pulled into the gas station nearby I got out and began recording a golden copper orb travelling through the north skies heading east. I watched and followed it until it disappeared at warp speed. It wasn't until watching the video later that I noticed I had captured the high pitch hum off this object taking off, which was pretty exciting considering most tend to be silenced by some force field or what not. My feelings well, I feel we all need to know and stop being so willfully ignorant, we all need to look up more. We've never been alone.
