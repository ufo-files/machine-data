{tjd} On 10/5/2018 at 550 am I looked up to the South and witnessed a going object transverse from South to North then turn 90 deg to the east and eject two secondary objects

I witnessed a glowing , pulsating object that traveled from South to North then made a 90 degree turn to the east. The sighting lasted approximately 3 to 5 mins. After the object which appeared yellowish gold in color turned to the east I witnessed two smaller objects white in color come from the main object and travel away from eachother in two different directions . The main object continued east and vanished when it was almost due east of my vantage point. The main object crossed in front of the constellation Orion at that time. This event was witnessed by my wife as well.
