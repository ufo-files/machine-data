{nd} Red Star to White Star to Explosion or Supernova White Mist

I was walking my dog in a westbound direction when I first noticed a small reddish "star" low in the Southern California south western horizon. I thought it might be Mars or Jupiter but as I continued to look at it the "star" appeared to be moving in my direction. Very imperceptible at first but then faster as it got closer to my location. Then the reddish "star" became bright white and at that point grew huge as though it exploded very much as they depict a supernova for illustrative purposes on television. As it "exploded" or "disintegrated" it was like a white mist and then it disappeared. I thought what I saw looked absolutely amazing.
