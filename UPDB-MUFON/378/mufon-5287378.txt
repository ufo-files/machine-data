It was a Sunday in November about 9pm at night. Clear skies with no cloud cover. My girlfriend and I were out for a drive in my 1958 Corvette with the top down. There was a long line of traffic and many people fishing on the side of the road. We were coming home going back to the main land from Lido Key. All of a sudden out of nowhere appear a huge Saucer shaped aircraft. All things stop like time stood still. No one could move. All traffic stopped too. I looked at the clock on my dash. I then saw a bright light coming from the center of the bottom of the craft. The top of the craft spun in one direction and the bottom another with lights on the side. It hovered over the Bay. It was huge the size of a large building. The beam went down in the water and was sucking up sea life. Then it seemed as everyone was put to sleep with time frozen. We woke 2 hours later. We got to see the craft take off. It was gone in seconds. Leaving at an amazing speed. Then everything went back to normal. The traffic started to move. i never saw or heard anything about this event on news, radio, newspaper or TV.
