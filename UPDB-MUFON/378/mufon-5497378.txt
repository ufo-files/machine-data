{cak} large helicopter following non blinking bright orb

We live in the flight path of the Van Nuys Airport which is subject to all manner of aircraft - Small prop - Fire planes/Helicopters - Private jets - classic military/historic aircraft & occasional Military Jets which make a lot more noise, so we are familiar with the sound of normal air traffic.On the evening of 12/13/2014 the uniquely loud and reverberating sound of rotor blades was heard within our house. Over several hours & after multiple occasions hearing the approaching aircraft rotor sound increase and then decrease as it flew over and past the house I looked out side but could see nothing. The fourth and final time I heard the sound of the helicopter / aircraft approaching I went outside immediately to see what aircraft was making all the noise. I exited my home which faces West, looking towards the approaching sound I observed a Large helicopter with it's lights blinking on my left moving from South to the North West, within a few seconds the glowing object appeared from the tall trees that line the street on both sides emerged into the open sky to our NW. the helicopter was right behind it. The helicopter had the standard yellow/white running lights & a red blinking light the object it was following/pursuing did not. The object was 100-150 yards ahead of the helicopter. Both moved very fast to the NW and as it did the orb/glowing object became less visible & seemed to fade out of sight or was obscured by trees ect. I did not wait to see if it would reappear I wanted to tell my wife what I just saw. The helicopter was about the length of a smashed flat 6 inch subway sandwich at arms length in the sky to give you an idea of scale and distance. The glowing object/orb perhaps the size of a penny-nickle.Im making this statement because as a rational religious and moral person this event struck me as very odd & unique - After digging through many Google searches with multiple terminology I was able to find almost identical accounts from others within this last year on the website UFOGRID
