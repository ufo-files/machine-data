Strange lights above the forest

UFO wasn't seen by me, but by my grandma. It all started about 10 to 5 minutes before midnight on Monday 1.6.20. Duration of event is about 30 minutes. Object did not move, it was stationary, it didn't blink, pulse or anything, it was all time the same. UFO was huge, it was far away from her, but still seen and photographed well. It was seen little in the air above the forest. UFO was shining brightly. UFO has shape of scythe. Visibility of UFO was good. When looking at it, it was everywhere equal. It made no sound. We don't know if anyone else saw it. Object had no seen surface, only lights. It was night. Than clouds covered up it, but this lights were still seen through. it was really shining with white-yellow light. By revealing a photo we saw that Ufo was not made only with one light, in fact there are more lights which are connected to each other. When aplifying a photo we saw another detail: around lights are poorly visible glowing lines.
