Bright starlike object rose from behind the treeline and travelled sideways above a lake. There were smaller objects around.

I was at my home when i noticed from my window what i first thought was a big bright star stationary just above the treeline. Until it began to move sideways and i saw how big and sparkling bright this thing was. It was travelling sideways along the treeline just above it, and dipped down few times, and even then i could see it shining through the trees. When it came to sight again there were few smaller objects around it. The object was unbeliveably bright and twinkling, about 5 times the size of the biggest star. I couldnt believe it when i saw it moving sideways, up and down, and even staying stationary sometimes. I grapped my cellphone and got a short crappy footage of it when it travelled between the trees. It was like a star had came down to earth and was now travelling above a lake. Just unbeliveable. I then ran out to the lake, so i could see it without the trees being infront of me. By the time i got there, there was nothing. I cant believe if no one else saw this. The sky was still blue and the stars werent visible yet. only the moon was up in the sky and bright.
I have never seen anything like this before. I was literaly shaking.
