Blinking lights in long horizonal line moving across the sky

First a vertical sighting at sunset in South. Over the next hour, 2 sightings within 15 minutes of the other, of horizontal lights, in Southern sky, over moon and Venus, moving from West to East. Attempted to document. Lights disappeared shortly after filming. Was on the phone with a friend, who can attest to my reaction. It was my first sighting. The lights were at least 40 or more, blinking, and several football fields in width. The moved in a straight line across the sky before disappearing. The lights appeared in the same spot, horizontally again.
