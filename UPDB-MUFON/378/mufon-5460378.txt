{jpg} Giant orange ball

About 2 am, 3 of 4 of us were tripping on acid, we went for a walk down a very dead, open road, about a mile long. We get about halfway and ALL of us (even the sober babysitter) see a glowing orange orb. It was huge. And literally just above the tree lines. Ive heard of 'fireballs' and thought maybe that was what was seen, except that after we stood and watched it silently fly above and across the road, it made an exact right angle turn and FLEW (extremely quickly) back behind us and disappeared. Not a single sound the entire time. About a minute later. A helicopter goes through. Very low, compared to normal. It was also easily 6x the size of the orb we saw. 

We live in an area where its semi common to see military planes practicing maneuvers exc. This was not a plane. At all. (Ft. McCoy is only 30min away)
