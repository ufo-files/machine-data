A con trail like form changed into 10 dots travelling in pairs travelled in line from NNW to NNE.

Was in middle of Bushwolf Lake to view the Perseids meteor shower on August 13, 2020. At 03:50 hrs, saw what looked like a jet condensation trail in the NNW section of the sky at about a 40 degree angle. The long thin (maybe 12 inches long at arm extension and 2 inch thick) cloud like object slowly moved in an easterly direction keep the shape and look of a regular jet trail. As I was looking in a NNE direction watching for meteors, the 'jet trail' seemed to take on a granular texture. It was like the jet trail was evaporating and converting to a bumpy texture. In about 15 seconds the cloud changed to bumpy to 5 pairs of dots traveling in line one pair follow in line in a easterly direction. The common looking jet trail converted to 10 dots following in line caught my complete attention at this point. I blinked a couple times to make sure I was seeing what I was seeing. I called out to friends who were getting into their paddle boat to join me meteor watching to look to their left which they did but the dots I was looking at were behind their view due to trees on their shoreline.The pairs of dots proceeded to continue over a hills to the front right. Total viewing of the 'jet trail' that converted to the line of dots was about 10 minutes. 7 minutes as a cloud and 3 minutes I observed the dots move across the sky above the northern horizon.
