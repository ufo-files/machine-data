Cube like UFO that appeared to be spinning and changing colors

Saw a UFO, bright white orb, flying overhead and disappeared. I looked in the sky again and saw another one. I pulled my phone out and recorded it. I adjusted brightness and exposure of video and saw that the object appeared to be a cube shape, spinning and changing shapes. It also changed from blue, red, and green colors. It was not going very fast, but would sometimes move swiftly in one direction. It did not stay in a straight line, but rather moved in all directions it seemed. Towards the end, it started to descend and started blinking, like a light turning on and off, and then disappeared.
