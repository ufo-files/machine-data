{rjl} Pulsing light moved, then fixed

I brought my dog outside to do his business. Straight ahead and up in the west, I noticed a light in the sky that I thought was a very bright star. I stared at it for a while and it seemed to be getting brighter and closer. It seemed to be moving gradually northwest so I figured it must be a plane, but then it fixed in one position and stayed there pulsing. I went inside to get my phone to record it because it just seemed so odd but cool. I recorded for about a half hour and it stayed in the same position, just pulsing. 
I noticed another object that I thought was (and most likely was) a plane far away to the lower left of the pulsing light. Farther to the left in my view (towards the end of the video) I saw a few more pulsing lights (white and faint red and blue) come into view. I moved my phone to scan that area better and then moved it back to the pulsing light. I had to stop recording, though, because I didn't want the neighbors to think I was taking video of their houses.
