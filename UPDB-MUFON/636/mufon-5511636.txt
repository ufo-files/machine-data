{cak} Starlike craft flew over and disappeared

Sitting outside star gazing I saw what resembled a star in size and color. The light emanating from the craft did not blink or resemble an airplane or helicopter. It flew at an extremely high altitude and appeared to fly at a high rate of speed. It made no noise at all and disappeared right before my eyes. This is the 4th one Ive seen in 3 days.
