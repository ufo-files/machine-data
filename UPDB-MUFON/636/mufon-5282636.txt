Line of orange lights, sequentially disappearing and appearing

Witness traveling west on 62/180 5 miles east of Carlsbad, NM. Girlfriend and step-daughter in car with witness. Witness first noticed the line of lights, drew attention to passengers who also observed them. Multiple orange/ fiery orbs that blink out and appear in somewhat sequential order. Shortly after, multiple other witnesses reported observing the same. There are videos available.
