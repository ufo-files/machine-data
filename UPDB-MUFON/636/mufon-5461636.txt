{tjd} Two very bright amber lights

Very very bright lights. There were two very close together but still with enough space in between them. They weren't lanterns or airplane lights because they never moved in that way. They ever so slowly descended in a way that looked like they were setting, but remained still. They never reached the ground and we lost sight of them even we drove around to see them better.
