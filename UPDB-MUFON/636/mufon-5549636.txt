i was sitting on the tailgate of my truck, as i do a lot i was looking into the sky. it was a clear night, not 1 cloud..and i was looking at stars and i saw something moving around rapidly, i focused on it and more came. there were about 6..they made formations and then went back to a single file line. i watched for 1 hour or so. they appeard to be stars at first, but they were closer and brighter. but they looked as if they were in the atmosphere. they circled around each other. they got brighter and dimmer one at a time. they went away totaly..and about 5 minutes later they came back and did the same thing..after watching for a a hour or so..i got real tingly down my spine.. so i left. thi shappend close to decatur, alabama.
