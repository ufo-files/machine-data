Sudden appearance of green & white orbs/lights that descended quickly and disappeared with no trails.

While driving and on the phone with the the driver behind me (a friend following), I observed a green light with a white light very close to it suddenly appear, grow brighter and travel very quickly N/NE while descending. It did not leave a trail.

When I asked the driver behind me if they just saw it, they stated they did and we were both awestruck.
