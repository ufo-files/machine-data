Man made like craft witnessed by 2,as large as a skyscraper,moving at walking speed

My brother and I were talking in our mothers driveway right by her car at the end of the driveway when we both noticed a light shoot up some 5 to ten miles away to the south east.then the light dropped some and then seemed to hover,this caught our attention and really intrigued me and it also strikes me as odd.it was about 1:00 am and we just started to talk some more.the light just seemed like it was staying in one place so we werent concerned till about 20 minutes went by and we noticed the light was getting closer moving really slow and really low. The light was faint but we could see it the whole time and we waited till we could see the craft or whatever with the naked eye,when we could see it ( an hr later)it was as over the freeway down the street,it was then we saw a dark craft approaching the size of a skyscraper(a close estimation)completely silent the craft was and so were we,we couldnt believe our eyes this thing was heading directly over us and it did.i/ we saw close detail.this thing was shaped sort of like a giant space shuttle from the front,bottom and back,but imagine the space shuttle with a lot smaller wings(my brother calls it the missile as a joke)kinda tucked in.you could even see the thing looked plated or tiled like the space shuttle,the thing was mostly a unique green color with some red outline (or accents) there were no windows that we could see and I looked good,there was only one light under the craft which was located on a protrusion on the ship that resembled the passenger area from a blimp but there were no windows( the light blinked like a slow strobe.this craft made no noise ans even blocked out all noise and the sky when it was overhead,only when we we directly under the center of this long craft that it made a faint rumble or thrust noise,the noise came and went quick,this thing was as long or longer than my mothers street( not kidding)it was moving a a fast walking speed and and took a couple minutes to completely pass over,when it did we could see a very large propulsion or thrust area that took up the whole back of the ship,it resembled a large rocket thrust that was quite dimmer and completely silent. Most importantly is the fact that this thing should have just fell down,there is nothing that could hold something like that in the air at that speed, on a strange note the red and green colors resembled Russian or soviet colors and it did look like a giant man made missile/shuttle. Then the thing just continued on at slow pace like it was headed toward Canada. We hadnt a camera or we would either be famous or assaniated,we did call the local police station to make a report assuming others must have seen it and also called but we were only young teens and they didn't take us serious,or treat us nice,any way Ive been looking for another like sighting ever since,I believe in ancient aliens and modern day aliens,
