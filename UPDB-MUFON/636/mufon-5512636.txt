(GS) Large red pulsating object flying NW on a clear night.

Coyotes were going crazy howling in the back field, so I opened my back door to see how close they were, to my amaze they were closer than normal. I sat out on the back deck to listen for a moment when I noticed a larger than normal object in the clear night sky! The object was a red glowing sphere traviling NW as a somewhat steady speed. I grabbed my lenses to get a closer look, and to my surprise it was defiantly not a plane!! I called my wife out to take a look, and she was instantly stunned, she took a look through the binoculars and was shaken up with what she saw. I didn't have to say anything to her, she new what she saw and went back into the house startled . I remained outside until the object was out of sight steadily flying over the back mountain side into the night sky. This is the first time I am reporting anything I have seen out on the country side, this is not the only occurance of strange objects I have seen out here. There has been more and more occurances in the past 6 months that I cant explain! Luckily I know Im not the only one seeing these things, we have had multiple witnesses, and I have photos of a landing with time, date, and GPS coordinates that happened back in July. I just thought its time to quit keeping all this to myself and share what has been occurring in the skys over Tennessee!
