{rjl} Giant flying V that seemed to split into multiple objects

This is going to sound completely nuts but thankfully I have a witness and there’s no way we’re both bonkers. Stopped at my buddy’s place at the front of my neighborhood on my way home just before midnight. I was standing in his driveway about to leave when something caught my eye and I glanced up to see a giant black V flying silently from west to east in the night sky. I couldn’t believe it and I said “Holy F&%#”. My buddy heard me and came out from his garage asking what happened. I said “Look up! Look at that!” He looked up and we both watched as this giant V continued flying silently from right to left, kind of diagonal to our position. It looked like you could see through it but it still had a perfect dark outline, almost as if it was a mirror image of the clouds except for the edges which had several points of faint light tracing along the V. A cloak, if you will. It was big like a wide-body commercial airliner (747 or 777) and between 2000-4000 ft in altitude. It was very fast and looked like it was making the air shimmer around it. Then it morphed into six or seven individual objects that each had their own eerie glow. My buddy describes them as the swirling tentacles of an octopus, a tail. I don’t know how to describe them. I’ve never seen anything like it. They spread out but stayed in what I’d call a rough formation, then accelerated and vanished. It was a fleet of little “things” that morphed from one giant “thing”. It looked like magic. Like CGI in a movie. The entire episode took about fifteen to twenty seconds from start to finish.

We stood in his driveway in absolute disbelief over what we had just seen and we talked about it for almost half an hour noting the fact that no airplanes had flown through since we saw it. Not one and we’re in the approach path to both KAUS and Executive. Then (I swear to sweet Jesus) a giant spotlight came on somewhere off in the distance and started sweeping the sky as if it was looking for what we just saw. We don’t know if that was a coincidence or not but all of it happened. /mind blown
