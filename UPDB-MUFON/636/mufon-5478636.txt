{cak) Object floating over field in a photo that I took went back to same area object is not in distance

I was taking pictures of scenery at a park Hill near the Kern county Aqueducts. Later when I viewed the pictures I could see that a white object was in the photo over a field about a half mile out. This was in June 20,16 I later returned to the same spot in November 2016 and took another picture in the same exact area. And the object is not in the distance. I have the pictures on my phone. Time was around 4 pm . The object was white and was a kind of a flat bottom but had a dome shape or egg shaped . It casted a shadow under its bottom surface.
