I was driving home from work,traveling south on I 55 , I exited on route 126,as I came to the end of the exit ramp I saw and extremely large triangular shape hovering over a small retention pond on my left. I observed this aircraft for several minutes and during that time I was able to get a good sense of the altitude,size and height and shape of this aircraft. This craft hovered about 75 feet above the pond the total length was about 600 feet and was 40 to 60 feet tall. The back of the aircraft had 4 rows of lights which looked like windows and they were emitting white and bluish lights, all of these windows were not lit brightly but did emit light.
