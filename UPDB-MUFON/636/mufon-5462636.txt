{jpg} craft similar to Rex Heflin UFO but had round portals that changed color glided an hummed as we watched it on a clear night maybe 300 yards or less up in the sky

Myself and 4 friends were camping on a hillside in Cherokee Al. We had a fire and were sitting around it talking. It was a clear summer night.The craft had just crested the hill when we noticed it. It looked like a squashed top hat or very similar to the Rex Heflin UFO but with round light portals. We watched till it went out of sight which was probably less than a minute. Several things stand out to me to this day-The shape-it glided slowly-and it hummed. We talked about it that night but never spoke of it again. We were in awe because we had never seen anything like that. I know it wasnt a plane or jet or helicopter. No rotor sound- just the hum. An the way it glided slowly from the time of duration. And it was not high up in the sky-maybe 300 yards. Like a low nap of the earth flight.
