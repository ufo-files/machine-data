35 starlike lights evenly spaced, travelling in straight line, North to South to vanish overhead

Clear sky with no clouds, saw 35 star like lights travelling very high evenly spaced in straight line from North to South, however directly overhead they all seem to vanish at the same exact point, their lights faded out quickly when each reached the exact same point only to vanish into thin air......
Another light, little bit brighter traveled from West to East crossing over the line of lights, this light was blinking slowly, 2 sec on and 4 sec off. After it crossed the line it came to a halt and remained in one place blinking slowly for about 2 minutes and when it blinked the light just never came on again, thereby also vanishing into thin air........ 
No sound was heard during the whole sighting.........something strange going on over Europe....
