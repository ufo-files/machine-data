{cak} about 2 hr long observation of white light, two other appeared and the first left with them

I was taking a walk, it was nighttime around 11, I was at a park and sat down to smoke a cigarette, after about a minute or two I looked up into the sky to my right and saw what I just assumed was a meteorite or whatever they're called, I've seen them before not the big flashes across the sky but the little dim lights you can hardly see but if you look close enough you can see them sometimes, so I thought it was that cause it was moving just like them, except it was a little brighter than the ones I've seen before, I still assumed it was just that though, then it stopped moving and I kinda got the sense it might be something else, so I watched it for about a half our and it did different things, there were times it moves quick around in the sky, not far distances though, it was partly cloudy so I kept going back and forth between is this something or does it just look like it's moving because of the clouds, sometimes it moved slowly just for a bit, there were times when the clouds were gone and the sky was clear though and then whenever it moved I still kinda was doubting what I was seeing and in the back of my mind I was pretty much telling myself it just looks like its moving, then I went home about a 4 minute walk and could see it from my house too, I got my binoculars thinking maybe I could see it better if it was something, I went to the darkest part of my yard and found it in my binoculars and there was a very dim light beside it that was blinking, I couldn't see that blinking light without the binoculars though, I went back to the park with my binoculars and found it again and it pretty much just sat there in the sky without moving,I was trying to find a place to have my binoculars so they could be steady while I looked but I was keeping an eye on it when all of a sudden started moving across the sky, this time I knew it was moving there was just know doubt, as i was watching it i noticed a faint light ahead of it and it appeared to be moving, finally i looked in the binoculars and could see the original light with the blinking light right beside it and they were both behind and a little to the right of that last fainter light and they were moving together, i kept my eyes on them as long as i could but they disappeared into the clouds. I walked the neighborhood the direction they went but didn't see anything. On my way home i heard what sounded like a jet but i don't know if that's relevant. I tried to get video but I only had my phone and they were all too high in the sky to pick up
