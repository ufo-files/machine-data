Saw steady red light in one location that moved steadily but quickly from east to west, pulsated red and white then took off to north.

I was driving home from work and turned onto Miccosukkee road from Hillcrest street at 10:45 pm and saw a steady red light fairly large sized above a church yard across the street, I thought this was odd because Lifeflight usually flies in from this direction towards the hospital but this was a steady red light and there was no helicopter sound. When I turned onto Terrace street I discovered that it was moving away from the hospital and was moving away from me (toward the west) at a quick but steady pace. It was hard to keep an eye on it with the trees and old, tall houses in my neighborhood. I didn't know that it had gotten so far away so quickly until I pulled into my driveway and stopped moving to try and see what it was between a break in two trees. It was stationary and red, then started to pulsate white and red all over alternately then headed north and at a slight upward angle towards the cloud cover we had tonight. It then took off at that angle (looking like a small meteorite streak). It had to be 10:47 when it left because that is when I called my husband to tell him what I saw.
