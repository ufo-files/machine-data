It was early, before 6am and still dark. I stopped along the road in Daniel's Park near Highlands Ranch and observed what I thought was a satellite traveling north to south over the mountains, not an unusual thing. Then I observed another, perhaps a hand width at arms length behind the first observed object, traveling at the same speed, along the same flight path, then a third, then a fourth, this got my attention. I stood watching and counting as 27 lights, satellite looking objects, very high, moved from north to south.
