{rjl} A large red/orange fireball passed behind and above a jet airplane going east. Ufo heading southeast

I had just stepped out onto my porch with the light off at about 1am and saw 3 ufo's and suddenly a red orange fireball appeared and shot southeast across the sky. It appeared and disappeared like it had gone through a hole in the sky. When it disappeared I noticed the jet airaplane just underneath and to the east of it. It took about 2 seconds to shoot across about 6 or 8 inches of sky. The other 3 ufo's that I originally saw remained much higher up and continued with the flashing red and white lights. A few minutes after that the big one began drifting down and northward then parked low on the horizon. After about 10 or 15 minutes as I was filming the big one directly to the west another one appeared just above and to the right of it. This whole thing had my nervous system pinging like I'd been shot with adrenaline for several hours afterward. I'm attaching only 5 documents but I have dozens more that I'd be happy to share with you.I don't know how long this continued because for the first time I was quite shaken by what I saw and I felt like they were going to try to take me and I didn't want to go so I hurried back inside. I've had a headache ever since.
