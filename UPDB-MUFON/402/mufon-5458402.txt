{rjl} Rectangular shaped cube copper colored with gold edging object about size of thre story office building 6:00 PM mid October 2015

Rectangular shaped cube copper colored with gold edging object about size of three story office building 6:00 PM early to mid October 2015. Hovered over trees while numerous fighter jets were flying over area. I first thought I was transported into the future as the cube like object looked like an office building. The cube hovered over the trees for 5 minutes then came directly over me and shot vertically and disappeared in one second. The jets continued to fly over the area for another 15 minutes. I had the exact date and time but lost in when my computer crashed.
