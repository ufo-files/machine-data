Large craft with a forward facing white light and a side facing series of red lights in an odd shape

I was on my back deck when I noticed a red light in the sky to the northeast. I saw a grey metallic looking craft in the shape of a tic tac with a single bright white light at the front and a series of multiple red lights on the side that flashed off and on. The shape of the red lights was a rounded section at the base and a pointed section at the top, and it was very large, larger than the height of the craft itself as if it were some type of appendage. There were at least 20 red lights in this shape. There was no sound and I saw no wings or tail. It looked like it was about half a mile from me and very low, perhaps 500-800 feet in altitude. It would have taken a quarter held at arm's length to cover it up. I watched it move slowly from east to west, then out of my field of view. About 30 seconds later a plane flew by the exact same location from west to east. That plane must have seen the object. It was also flying very low. It looked like a private jet and was very loud. I should have been able to hear engines on the unidentified craft at that distance.
