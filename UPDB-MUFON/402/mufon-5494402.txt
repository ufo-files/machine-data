{dr}Looked like a gigantic fish net pulsating, changing shape glowing and getting brighter and alternately less bright. Planes from air base were definitely set out to look at it. Saw them around it at a long distance. NO news reports as of yet.

Saw a large array of lights twic heading home from my office and when I got home I took out binoculars and saw what looked like a large pulsating bright light in the shape of a fish net like the ones they use to cast over a large area to catch small fish. It kept changing shape and appeared to have large holes in it but they kept reconnecting and a large white light was circling around the outside and sometimes into the object itself. Almost looked like a large parachute that was brilliantly lit up, but was not floating down at all. Watched it for about an hour and it remained stationary in the sky, but apparently some planes from the nearby airbase were sent to look at it. Heard them launch and the object remained where it was but started to blink more and change shape more rapidly. Got cold outside, went in to see if there were any news reports and went back out but it's no longer there.
