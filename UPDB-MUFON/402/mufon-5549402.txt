Three UFO's appeared first as bright stars and became visible as solid objects.

On the above listed day in Dallas, TX, I was walking home facing North on a partly cloudy morning when suddenly in the sky in front of me a bright star like object became visible and moved to my left. As it moved it changed from a bright light to clearly a solid object that looked like an upside down saucer and it hovered. As I looked at it, out of the corner of my eye, another bright star like object again appeared and quickly moved to my left and upward stopping parallel to the first object. It looked exactly the same as the first object. As I looked at the two objects, a third star like object appeared at the same spot as the other two. It also moved to my left, but remained in apearance like a very bright star. As I continued to look at the three objects, I decided to call 911 on my cell. While talking on the phone to 911, all three objects simultaneously moved very slowly behind clouds.I waited several minutes after they disappeared and never say them again.
