{nd} WITNESS WAS OUTSIDE TRYING TO SEE A SATELLITE AND SAW A VERY,VERY BRIGHT, BLUEISH RECTANGLE HOVERING ON HIS PROPERTY

REPORTED AND FILLED OUT AT MUFON HQ WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18809 ON 10/07/2015
--------------------------------------------------------------------
WITNESS LIVES ON 32 ACRES, AND HAS NO CLOSE NEIGHBORS. 
WITNESS WAS OUTSIDE TRYING TO SEE A SATELLITE AND SAW A VERY,VERY BRIGHT, BLUEISH FLASH. THIS WAS THE BRIGHTEST FLASH HE HAS EVER SEEM. THE FLASH BECAME A VERY,VERY BRIGHT, BLUEISH RECTANGLE HOVERING NEAR THE GROUND, ON HIS PROPERTY.THE RECTANGLE WAS LARGER ON THE TOP THAN ON THE BOTTOM. IT SEEMED TO HAVE A FLAP ON THE TOP THAT ROLLED BACK AND FORTH, VERY SLOWLY. IT WAS ABOUT 6' TALL, 5' WIDE ON THE TOP AND ABOUT 3 1/2' FEET WIDE,TOWARDS THE BOTTOM. IT SEEMED TO BE ABOUT 300' AT THE MOST FROM THE WITNESS. THE WITNESS WATCHED THE RECTANGLE AS IT MOVED NORTH OVER HIS TOOL SHED AND THEN STARTED RISING UP AND SHOT UP IN THE SKY AND DISAPPEARED. WITNESS WAS NOT SCARED, MORE AMAZED AT WHAT HE SAW. HE FEELS LIKE "THEY" WERE ALMOST PLAYING WITH HIM. WITNESS THINKS THE CRAFT MAY HAVE BEEN BIGGER THAN THE BRIGHT RECTANGLE HE SAW, HE SEEMED BLINDED BY THE BRIGHTNESS OF THE RECTANGLE, LIKE AN OPEN WINDOW IN THE DARK, SO HE COULDN'T SEE THE FEATURES OR SHAPE OF THE CRAFT.
THE NEXT DAY THE WITNESS CHECKED FOR TRACKS OR ANY DISTURBANCE ON HIS PROPERTY AND FOUND NONE. NO ANIMALS HAVE BEEN HARMED OR INJURED.
WITNESS HAS TRAVELED ALL OVER THE WORLD AND NEVER SEEN ANYTHING LIKE THIS BEFORE.
