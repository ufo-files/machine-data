{cak} small round bright blue light followed by gigantic black boomerang shaped object

At approximately 11:30pm I was outside smoking a cigarette when I saw a bright bluish white light fly over my house at a phenomenal speed and turn sharply to the left until it went out of view. As I walked out further into my yard to see if I could get a better view I was completely amazed to see a giant black object moving at a very slow rate of speed. At first I stood mesmerized for about 30 seconds then I became frightened and ran under my porch looking out from different angles to see if I could understand what I was looking at. Completely silent. After approximately one minute after initially see the black object I ran inside to get my phone. When I returned the object was gone. Around 30 mins passed when I heard a helicopter pass over my home and when I went back outside I could hear the helicopter behind the woods beside my home. The helicopter remained about 20 to 30 minutes then I didn't hear it anymore.
