White sphere

During the suspension of flights due to the eruption of Eyjafjallajökull in April 2010 I was waiting for crew members of our lifeboat to arrive on station in order to launch at 6.30am. Dawn was breaking, and there had been no flights for a few days. A bright solid white light suddenly appeared some distance away and headed towards me not getting any bigger in size. There was no noise and no flashing lights. It then turned 180 degrees and headed away from me still displaying a solid white light and disappeared in the same direction it had come from. As I was watching it one of the crew members arrived and I pointed it out to him asking what it could be to which he replied "it's definitely not a plane" as all European flights had been grounded for a few days by then.
