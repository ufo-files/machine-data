Witnessed 2 objects in Easten sky at dark (9pm) Ascending and decending opposite ways parralel to one another.

My two boys and I were just finishing up a game of frisbee and were walking up our driveway, when my youngest saw what I thought at first was Venus or a large star. Another appeared to the right of it and both simultaneously moved parallel but opposite of each other, higher in the sky while the other dropped and one vanished, the other hovered in the sky for a min or two and vanished as well. Assumed they may have been Starlink satellites? There is a small airport to our South but was not a plane or copter. Being two objects and moving almost together really stood out. Only filmed for about a min two videos, the street light reflected a bunch and caused issues on the second, so only attached the one of the more eastern object.
