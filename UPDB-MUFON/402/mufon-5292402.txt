No lights, hovering on top of me

That day my husband picked me up at my work, I used to work in a Supermarket back then, and I had to clean the entire area after the store closed at 11 pm. When he picked me up he was with 2 other family friends that had just arrived from our country and were visiting us for a few days, it was around 11:30 pm and we drove home, only 10 minutes away. When we parked outside the building complex in Virginia gardens, we came out of the car and we were all talking but I suddenly felt the need to look up, no idea why, but while we were walking to the apartment, I looked up and noticed something hovering on top of us, not too far away, it had like a hexagon shape, material seemed like metal and the color I can say it was a very dark green color, it had like lines in the same hexagon shape. I had to adjust my vision to see more clearly and was when I was able to see more in detail. there was no noise and it was moving super slow in South direction. I told the people that were with me, but for some reason they didn't pay attention as they were talking and I just kept looking up, we continued walking and the object just keep slowly moving away until I couldn't see it any longer. It was fascinating because it was just hovering there, no lights or sound whatsoever, no humming sound, nothing, you really had to force your eyes to see it because there is a lot of light pollution in Miami, but I was fortunate enough to see it, every time I think of it I wonder what could this be and there is no logical explanation, since then I have been much more enthusiast about the UFO phenomena and I keep looking up to the sky everytime I am outdoors.
