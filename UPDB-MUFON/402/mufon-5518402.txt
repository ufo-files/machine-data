A Star like stationary object that began to glow brightly then turn in the sky leaving no trace or sound.

Looking toward the southern US Sky, observed a dim light that quickly glowed bright the turned abruptly toward the west and continued west until out of sight. At first both my wife and I thought the light to be a star, until it began to glow bright. Just after making the sharp turn west, it looked similar to a satellite orbiting earth.
