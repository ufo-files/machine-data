{rjl} Large light sphere splits into 3 smaller spheres and takes off in multiple directions.

I was moving into a new home. I had just finished dropping off a car load of boxes at the new place and was in the process of heading back to my old apartment to grab another load. I backed out of the driveway onto the 1200 block of west Jackson St. and headed west towards Western Ave. About halfway down the block I saw a bright ball of light rising above the tree line. I'm not sure how far away it was. Maybe a mile or so. It seemed to be spherical in shape. The color of the light seemed to change from various shades of white, orange, yellow, and red. It's hard to tell if it was flashing/pulsing each color individually and rapidly or if multiple colors were present at the same time. The sphere paused for a few seconds and the size of the sphere started to grow larger. It expanded and then it split into three smaller, but identical (both to each other and to the original) spheres. They moved in a spiral sort of "dance" around one another. As the lights "danced" around each other they were also slowly gaining altitude. Then they shot off in different directions and disappeared from my sight. The thing about this sighting that stands out the most for me, personally, is that I have a pretty severe phobia of aliens/abduction. My whole life, ever since childhood, it's just something I've always been afraid of. But for some reason I wasn't scared at all while watching these UFOs. They didn't seem threatening to me and actually came off as being playful, which is why I describe their movements and interaction with one another as a kind of dance.
