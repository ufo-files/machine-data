Red/Orange/Yellow Lights in Sky

My mother, sister, and myself were returning home from grocery shopping when we stopped at the top of a hill and noticed lights in the sky. They were orangy red in color, but seemed to shift or pulsate to more red or more yellow as we watched them. We spotted 4 originally, and then a fifth light further away. We thought maybe they were airplanes, but they did not blink like planes do and they did not seem to be moving in any obvious direction. They seemed to "wander" around the portion of sky they were in, never movie very far from each other but definitely changing places and moving around. My sister and I tried to keep an eye on them as we drove home, but they seemed to disappear one by one. We finally lost the last one behind the trees. It was a very odd experience as none of us could seem to identify the lights. They didn't even really look like helicopters. It was really weird. Having seen The Darkest Hour earlier didn't help.
