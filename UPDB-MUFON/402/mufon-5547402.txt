Very large flashing object.

A couple friends and I were at another friend's house watching a basketball game on tv. We left at 10:50 p.m. and one friend drove three of us home. As we were driving on the freeway, I jokingly pointed to an object in the sky and said that it was a UFO. This was at about 11 p.m. That got everyone's attention and we had no idea what it actually was. One friend dismissed it as a radio tower because of how low it seemed. It also seemed to be hovering over a certain area. He then realized there were no radio towers in the town we live in. Once I saw that it was moving, my heart began to race. We were all in shock at what we were seeing. It had three visible flashing lights, all three of them different colors. It resembled a jet, but was moving to slow and completely silent. We quickly pulled into a parking lot to get a better look but it vanished behind some trees. Thinking we had seen all there was to see, we dropped off two of the friends. Minutes later, as I was being taken home, we saw it again. This time it was moving toward us. We quickly drove down a sparse yet usually busy road for a place to park. We parked the car and turned the lights off. At this time, we could see the bottom of the object. It was massive; about the size of an apartment building. It seemed to be so close to us yet it didn't make even the slightest noise. We watched it fly slowly into the distance until we lost site of it. I know all the flight patterns of commercial airlines around here and this object was on a course of its own. It was also ten times bigger than any jet I've ever seen. I was stunned by this experience and also very happy that I got to witness something so rare. After I got home, I did a bit of research and found a video on youtube of the same thing that I saw. http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N99DMRmRc-8&feature=channel_page. I had a much better view than what is seen in this person's video. I am kicking myself for not recording it on my phone but I was way too caught up in the moment to even think about it.
