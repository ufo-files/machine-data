{cak} Crazy moving orbs in the night sky

I was out back around 9pm on 7/14/15 playing with my Yukon Night ranger monocular looking in the sky. Once they were focused in I was looking at the stars then noticed two bright orbs slowly moving to right direction. The top one stopped and then the second one kept moving slowly. After 30 minutes of watching these things move back and forth and then up and down a third one pops up out of the tree line. It started slowly moving towards the other two orbs. I watched these things for a good 2 hours through my night vision setup and never saw anything like this. They were not plans since one passed by and defiently not lanterns or drones of anytype. I wish I would of recorded this since it was a interesting show. I called my gf to watch and then the two top orbs moved closed together like dual headlights then slowly separated. Not sure what they were upto but having night vision lets you see everything that the human eye can't in the sky. All I can say is that was something ET. Will be watching the sky tonight with a recording setup to capture these things.
