{tjd} Trails from Space while flying 30k feet

While coming home from Buffalo NY, flying at cruising altitude, say 34,000 feet. I was taking pictures of clouds and their formations. I was using my phone. My phone was a Galaxy S7, its settings were ISO 50, 1/4000 to 1/5000 with a F2 lens. Upon reviewing my images 3 out of 10 have these UFOs or window scratches. I am unsure as I really didn't look at the plastic windows for scratches. But I did like the images.. So I did not see these images while taking the pictures, which might lead to the fact that the high shutter speed stopped these images while traveling at a high rate. Or the sun glared off the wing just right to make lens flares. In either case,, there both compelling. The images were taken in same minute,, so I can say there was not much time in-between each image.
