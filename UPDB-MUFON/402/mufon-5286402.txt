Flashing Red/Pink/White Orb Observed in Sky during Evening Walk

My significant other and I were enjoying an evening stroll in our subdivision on Tuesday, July 6, 2021 shortly after 9pm when we noticed a bright star-looking object in the twilight/dusk sky. The sky was clear and without clouds at this particular time that evening. There were no other stars visible (as the sun had only recently set) - so this strange, bright celestial object caught our attention. 

I pulled out my phone and started snapping photos and video. Upon zooming in, I noticed that the object was NOT a star - instead, it was orb shaped, glowing, and kept blinking/changing colors. It seemed to hover in a relatively stationary spot. The colors we observed the orb pulsating ranged from a pure white glow, through to light pink/rose-colored, and then to a darker pink-reddish color glow. I took as many photos and video clips as I could - both zoomed outward and zoomed in for close-ups - so as to capture the flickering of the object exactly as we observed it in real time. 

I continued to film and photograph this phenomenon for approx. 15 minutes before we left and returned to our house. I have not checked to see whether any one else has reported this same sighting or not. 

Note: The video clips best show the blinking/color changes that we observed.
