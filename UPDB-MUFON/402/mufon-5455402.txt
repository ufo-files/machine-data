Bright light

Driving home along M62 motorway heading East at 16.30 pm GMT the sun behind me in the West.
Clear sunny day very few clouds to the North and South.
I spotted directly in front of me an object/orb that appeared to be a bright white light around a mile in front of my position and approximately 3" from the top of my windscreen ( VW T5 Transporter) which I observed for approx 5 - 6 seconds when it vanished.
I looked to both North and South directions and nothing was visible in either direction.
