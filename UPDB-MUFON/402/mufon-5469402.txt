{td} Ultra slow craft crosses northern nevada sky

A craft that had a blinking white light, a red colored light and an ultra bright white strobe, slowy crossed the northern sky. It would blink and pulse these lights in no real pattern. The strobe seemed to come after the white slow pulse. It took 5 minutes to cross only about 20 degrees of sky. No sound. Far far away. It moved like a satellite. But had very very bright lights. We recorded it for five minutes on an iphone. I am a pilot, never seen anything like this.
