First object was a light in SW going NE changed to barbell shape, second object was triangle in the W going E possibly football field in size

We were on Clearwater Beach when we first noticed a ball of yellow/gold light with spokes coming from it and it began to move closer. The next thing I remember ios a craft flying over my head approximately 800 feet off the ground, maybe. What I perceived was an object that was flat long object with two bullet shaped objects at each end and a light on the side of each of these two ends. I said out loud, "Did anyone see that?" No one answered. 
Fifteen minutes later, over the western horizon a yellow/gold sphere with spokes of diffused light eminating from it, approached. As it passed over head the leading edge had luminous light which curled over and under it remaining on the edge. The undercarriage was dark, a grey/silver color with no details visible. It appeared to be the size of a football field. It traveled due east. It was the most beautiful sight I have seen in my whole entire life.
