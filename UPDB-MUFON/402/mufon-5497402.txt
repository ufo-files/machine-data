3 white orbs in triangular formation

Last October 2014, I was staring at the full moon when suddenly 3 white orbs in an isosceles-type triangular formation flew in a swift gliding motion and moved towards/passed by me taking a westerly path. The lights are close to around 100ft or so but no sound was heard as if there was no engine like that of a jet or commercial plane. It was fast, took only a few seconds to fly across the sky. It is not like a meteor that leaves a streaking white line in the night sky when it enters the atmosphere.  These 3 bright orbs remained circular in its shape as it traveled and maintained its distance between one another as it moved about.
