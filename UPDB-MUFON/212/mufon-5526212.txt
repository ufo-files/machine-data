Disc shaped object hovering over tree line

It was 1AM I was testing out a thermal camera. About 1:20AM I noticed a heat signature above the tree line that was pulsing low heat to high heat. About that time a plane coming into Tampa airport was going by to the right of the object when it completly went cold. Once the plane flew by it starting pulsing agian. I switched the camera to black is hot setting and you could see that stereo typical disc shape. It then started getting smaller like it was flying away then went straight up very fast.
