Oval white object with no tail moving ssw, changed direction to west then zig zagged behind the tree line, super fast.

In Buford Georgia. On April 24th, 2012. I saw my first ufo. The time was about 10pm EST. I was sitting on the front deck of the house with two of my friends. The house faces north. I had my back to the house, facing my two friends and they were facing me. My view of the sky is clear with no tree obstructions except the one story houses across the street. I noticed a light in the sky, from my peripheral vision.
It was at about my 11 oclock direction. at about 45 degrees off the horizon. I immediately thought it was A meteor. I looked up, It was white, as if it was glowing, It did not pulsate. It was solid looking, oval shaped, and it had no tail. It was traveling south, southwest. 
It passed to my 9 oclock still at 45 degrees, then it changed direction.
It looked to go in A more downward fall, like it was going toward the ground but since it was high off the ground, I thought it might just be turning in A more west direction. At this time, Its location off the horizon was dropping and it now was heading toward the tree line next to our house, on my left. I wasnt going to say anything to my friends but it did not disappear right away like a typical meteor. I stood up and shouted 
"Its still going!" As my two friends looked up, the ufo zig zagged many 
times like /\/\/\/\/\/\ as it was going, from my view, into the tree line, but not crashing into the trees, it was still high in the sky. I could see through the first of three trees that made the tree line.
Then it disappeared behind the thicker trees and the neighbors house. My friends never saw it. It all took place within a matter of three seconds.
I don't claim it to be any aliens from another planet. For all I know, it could have been the latest US Air Force, top secret, air craft or space craft. I know what I saw and IT WAS COOL. It was silent, white in color, oval shaped, it had no tail like the meteors do when burning up in the atmosphere, and it changed direction many times.

     I have heard of many peoples stories of ufos all throughout my life and its cool to finally see one of my very own sightings. Remember, I don't claim it to be any E.T.s. I don't know what it was, But I do know it was no light show, it was no bird, no jet, no airplane, no air balloon, no kite, no glider. At this time, it is unknown to my knowledge.
