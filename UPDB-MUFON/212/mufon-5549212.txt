Light brighter than meteor or plane descended at high speed behind Mount Baldy, CA

Whilst travelling eastbound on 91 freeway at intersection of toll road in Yorba Linda saw very bright light descend vertically being mountains to North. Light was brighter than any flying object I have seen . Descended faster than a falling object and faster than any known aircraft. Took 2 seconds to descend from what looked like a height well above mountain level to behind mountain. Witnessed by co-worker.Descended behind either Yorba Linda Hills or Mount Baldy . It was 4.30 am and dark out . This was not a plane or any reflection from car headlights .
