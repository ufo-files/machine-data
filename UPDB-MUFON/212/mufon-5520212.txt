I went out to have a smoke and saw an unfamiliar object in the sky. My girlfriend also witnessed it and said "that's a star" . Then another bright light appeared about an inch away in the sky. They started to change colours from an orange to white to red and seemed to pulsate and at times disappeared and returned glowing bright. I called my friend and described to him what I was seeing. He came to my house and when he saw the objects in the sky immediately said that they didn't belong there. We watched the two objects glow and pulsate for about an hour. Then within 5 minutes one seemed to drop out of sight and the other remained stationary. It then also went out of sight. The two objects appeared in the sky in a elongated \ pattern with the objects being at each end of the trajectory.
