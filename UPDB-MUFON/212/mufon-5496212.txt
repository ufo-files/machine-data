{nd} Taking photo of border blimp discovered object in photo

I am a truck driver I was westbound on interstate 10 just east of Deming New Mexico. I saw what I believe to be the border patrol blimp in the distance over some small mountains I rolled down my window and took a picture to see if I could get it in on film. After I took the first picture I looked at it and saw that I had captured the the image of the object I am describing now. I immediately within 20 seconds took a second photo of the same area and the object did not appear in the second photograph. Approximately 20 minutes later as I was still traveling westbound and on the west side of Deming New Mexico I observed the object again however this time it was much closer and not a light but an actual vehicle. I took two pictures of it at that time and it did not appear in either one of the pictures.
