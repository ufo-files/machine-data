Gigantic ship completely silent with unbelievable flying capabilities

A friend who is now deceased and myself we’re outside of town in my car add at an old hang out. sitting in the car I thought I saw usomething in the corner of my eye. When I looked out the window I noticed an enormous ship the size of a football stadium coming up behind us very low to the ground. When the craft got over the car it stopped. The underside didn’t look at all like it was aerodynamic. It shined a light down on us but not what I would call necessarily a beam. It hovered over us for at least I minute maybe a minute and a half. it seems like forever. My friend was trying to hide under the dash I was hanging out The window yelling and screaming. Slowly and with no noise it started to move off to the northwest. The amazing thing was that it could cover such a distance and almost 0 time it bounced from one point to the other a few times and then shot straight out of sight. I’ve spent my whole life trying to forget it it was exciting and terrifying my friend and I never talked to anybody about it because we were scared and trying to forget what we saw.
