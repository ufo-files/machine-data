{cak} Light stationery in sky for 3 1/2 hours, so bright it was hard to look at it, 1/10 size of moon

Husband woke at 2:00 am in night and saw light out bedroom window. He thought it must be a jet with lights aimed at house. I woke at 3 a.m. and saw the light out the window. I awoke him and he told me he saw it at 2:00 a.m. We watched it for over an hour. It remained unmoved and was stationery all that time. We looked with the binoculars, but as with the eye, the light was so bright it hurt to look at it. We went back to bed around 4 a.m. and watched it occasionally out the window till the dawn. Once the sun came up in the east you could not see the light for having to look into the sun. We live in a very remote area. No airports or air travel in this area except at a great elevation. I have seen weather balloons in this area before in the daytime, but they drift over. This was stationery and highly illuminated with a terribly bright light. It was way too large and bright to be a mistaken star. I look out that window every night and never saw anything like it before. There are no towers or buildings in this area. Very remote. Please contact me if you get other reports. I tried to takea picture but couldn't get enough light inside the house. I was afraid to go outside. It really felt like a giant headlight beam that was huge and nver moved.
