Similar to ISS, BUR APPROX 2 to 3 times faster. Internet ISS tracker showed ISS was just about to pass over Australia.

Observation date 18/7/18.
Time 0155hrs. +/- 3 mins
Viewed looking South.
Inclination approx 30 deg.
Tracking West to East.
White light similar to that of the ISS (which I have seen many times before and which I thought it initially was!).
Very smooth path possibly slightly brighter than I've seen the ISS and no iridium flare seen.
Travelling 2 to 3 times faster than the ISS (in my humble opinion, providing that it was on a similar orbit to that of the ISS).
Duration not more than 20 sec closer to 15 sec and also approx 15 deg of arc.
There's was also an aircraft flying SE to NW during the observation .
Also a satellite moving Sth to Nth just before I lost sight of the light due to low level cloud to the SE of my location.
If it wasn't for the speed aspect, I would have guaranteed that the light was the ISS!!.
