Golden/copper orb hovering on the horizon line.

First observed what I thought was a planet on the horizon. It was brilliant, gold, yellow and copper in color. I observed it for the first five minutes with the naked eye. I went inside to get my telescope and binoculars and focused my telescope on the object first. The light appeared to dim when I focused on the object. I checked my telescope to make sure it was functioning properly by aiming it at the moon to check the brightness, no issues. I returned to the object and it was still dim. I then switched it out to binoculars and it brightened again. I decided to do some lunar observation for five minutes and the object just disappeared. There was no cloud cover, and it disappeared in a matter of minutes. There was also an aircraft in the vicinity and it appeared to be very close to the object. There was another commercial airliner in the airspace, but it did not intersect with the object. Initially I thought it was a planet, but this was more brilliant than any planet I have seen while observing the night sky.
