Sighting of several lights moving in a perfect formation

Several lights, at least 25 in the sky were moving in a straight perfect formation almost "morse code" like but not. Each object had either 1 or 2 lights, the ones with 2 lights were right next to each other front to back (like 2 periods next to each other).   One object towards the end of the formation had lights that were positioned at 3 o'clock and 7 o'clock instead of right next to each other. and the area around the lights looked semi transparent as the stars over the light looked less bright as the object passed under it.
