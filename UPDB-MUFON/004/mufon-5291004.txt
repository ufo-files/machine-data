Hovered then accelerated and disappeared

Bright white(Snow White), slightly elongated object spotted hovering for a few seconds. It then appeared to accelerate incredibly fast leaving a short and brief bright white tail before completely disappearing. The entire sighting was only seconds long, maybe about 10. It was spotted a very early in the morning in the dark. The night sky was the only backdrop which made judging distance and size extremely difficult. I captured no photos of videos because of how quick the sighting was. I did however find a photo of a sighting in Canmore, Ab from 2016 that is exactly what I saw. Only difference being that sighting was during daylight. The size from my perspective was roughly pea - aspirin size.
