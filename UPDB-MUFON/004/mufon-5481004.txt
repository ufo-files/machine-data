I was parked out near Marshall Junction, at an old Air Force building no longer used. I began to hear loud humming coming from off the telephone lines. I looked up to the West and saw an orange-red spherev slowly coming just above the telephone poles. I was shocked and surprised. As I stood there frozen, I watched the object slowly cruise right above me in my clear vision. THE inside lit UP AS ORANGE-RED. It seemed to have no pilot inside. The glow lit up outside the object. It looked like the shape of a large bullet. After I watched it go by, I got into my car and drove away. It made no sound and I felt like it was observing me like a giant eye.
