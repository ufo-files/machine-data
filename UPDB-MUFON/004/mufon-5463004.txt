Glowing star like object at high altitude moving at non variable constant very high speed.

One day after the last moon eclipse I got out to the street (Buenos Aires, Capital Federal) to do some grocery shopping and then I felt like something told me to look up. the skies were clear and the starts could be seen bright up there.

Then I immediately saw how a star-like object, very similar in light emission intensity to the brightest star in the sky (at that moment Sirius) started moving from South to North in a non variable constant speed and straight trajectory until it disappeared from sight sinking in the dark horizon.

I stood there in complete awe the whole four minutes it lasted, hoping for someone else to notice it, but everybody seemed to be minding their own businesses as usual. I consider myself to be a pragmatic individual, mostly regarding UFO activity, but this got me surprised since the last time I had the chance to presence such an event was a long long time ago.
