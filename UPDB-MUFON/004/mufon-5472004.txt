{jpg} Seen flying over a field early in the morning

I was driving to work at about 5:30 a.m. so it was still dark. I noticed the object when i looked up to see strangely patterned lights flying over the road by the church i was passing, but it suddenly moved over a field to my left drivers side of my car. At first, i thought the object might have been an airplane but i realized that it was really low (just over the tree tops) and i saw a distinct outline to it that suggested it couldn't be an airplane (to describe lamely, one of the appendages looked like the protruding end of a fidget spinner). It continued to fly past me to my left over the field and i felt very confused as to what it was because i was unable to identify what it was exactly. I lost sight of it when i had to continue driving forward and lost sight of it that way.
