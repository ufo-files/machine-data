{dr}This event just changed my life.

I went to bed at 2:35 am on Wednesday morning (March 20th) 2013. No drugs, no alcohol. Just got out of a hot shower, mind cleared- ready to snug up with the pooch. I looked out the window at the sky (as I always do before bed) and admired the moderately clear sky. To the NW I notice an extremely weird patch of clouds that have light illuminating from the inside up towards miles into the sky. I am having goose bumps re-visualizing it. With in a few minutes, the clouds broke away & an object CLEAR AS DAY sits hoovering, stationary in the sky with the lights alternating in brightness & color left to right. I assume the object was about 1-5 miles away (directly over Chesapeake City MD) & it was HUGE. Just like what you expect to see in the movies. It was visible for over a minute then the lights began to shut off (almost as if powering down) then went completely black. The brightness of the object was a type of light I have never seen. It is hard to explain the light but it was as vibrant as fire (like the sun) but not blinding. The light was very clear & crisp. Once the lights shut down to just one moderately white light.. it then completely disappeared. I took video with my iPhone 4s but after trying to view, the screen looks black- think Id need video editing software. If someone would like to plug at it & try to work something with it- they are welcomed to contact me & I will email the 3 videos I took ([Email removed/cms/tg]). I have waited my entire life to see something like this & am behind baffled. Kind of scared to be honest. I saw that ship and I damn hope it didn't see me. I literally climbed out on the roof to take the videos- this was a life changing moment.
