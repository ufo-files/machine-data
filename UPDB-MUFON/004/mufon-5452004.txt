Green butterfly like ship chased by two other planes at extremely fast speed

My siblings and I were sitting out by a bonfire when a loud rumbling roar came from the sky. We felt the rumble in our bodies and looked up to see a green butterfly like structure being chased by two planes across the sky. We watched them until they disappeared into the mountains and we were no longer able to see them. It left one of us feeling slightly nauseated, but It could just be they are scared. overall no major symptoms.
