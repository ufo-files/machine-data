{jpg} Triangular outline of red lights - appeared star-like

While lying on our backs on the beach star gazing, a triangular outline of red lights appeared gradually above us. (Roughly 9? - one each at each of the 3 point of triangle with lights of the same size spaced in between to complete the shape. Rear of triangle was slightly wider than 2 sides. Lights that appeared distant & star-like (hard to focus on) though object appeared much closer.) Object moved quietly towards the west as lights became more defined. After a short distance, it quickly faded - as if it went behind a cloud. Clouds in the sky were at a much higher altitude than the object. (i.e. Not between us and the object)
