{cak} Sunny day, no clouds, object slowly descended at angle from the west heading east with no sound slight smoke was noticed in rear of craft then dissipated as it leveled, hovering briefly before flying east

1969, in Maricopa, California I was only 10 years old I was standing in the front yard of our home, I noticed movement in the sky and looked up to see a huge white cylindrical object that appeared to be descending. At first it was angled with the rear of the object pointing down and there was a hint of fire and smoke. The object then came to a stop, leveled off with the front of the craft pointing east rear west. It hovered briefly, the small amount of smoke completely disappeared. It then began to travel at a steady speed westward until it disappeared in the horizon. The next day the local newspaper (taft Driller)said several people had witnessed Russian space trash enter the atmosphere. Even at only ten years old I knew what I saw was not an uncontrolled object. This sighting had a profound effect on me and for the rest if my life. As I grew older and we moved to Oklahoma as a teenager I and a number of friends in a rural area where I lived witnessed an orb ascend slowly from some trees slowly then descend back into the trees making no noise and frightened us so much that all of my friends left me alone in my driveway in the middle of the night. No one talked about it.
