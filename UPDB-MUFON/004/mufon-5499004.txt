{cak} witnessed 3 fire ball like orbs traveling from the direction of Philadelphia

around 8:45 PM I went outside on my deck facing South and saw 3 red glowing balls of what looked like fire heading from Philadelphia north towards Bucks county. They looked to be under airline cruising altitude as I am fairly close to Phila and most planes are not at cruising altitude when they pass my house. They made no sound that i could detect. They were spaced apart by a second or two and continued so until they got to a point northeast of me where they just seemed to flutter out. At first i thought they were airplanes but i never saw an airplane glowing like this both coming towards me and away from me and never with that intensity. It was dark so i could not make out any form other than a round glowing orb. After i dismissed it as being airplanes I thought it might be a few meteors but no contrails were visible. I ran into my house to the front door so i could follow it going past my house and watched it for about 10 seconds until they disappeared in a flutter well before an airplane would have faded out. I saw two airplanes in the area north of me who must have also seen it to the east of them.
