{nd} Chemtrails going across sky. Saw several few minutes apart. Shot still photos. Same day for last three months and some on alternate days. All at sunset.

On Oct.19-2015 I looked out of my front plate glass window and saw a bright gold object in the sky at sundown after the sun had passed the horizon.I took a picture and viewed the object with binoculars. I then walked approx 15ft to door, walked out my front door, and the object was gone. I have Photographed several different objects afterwards. The picture that is displayed appeared as a chemtrail,but after enlargement a saucer shaped object with a small dome atop is seen. Object continued on, and more trails appeared going different directions.
