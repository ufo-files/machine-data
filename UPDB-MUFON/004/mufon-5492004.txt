{jpg} While out in front of my house taking pictures of lightning, I noticed this object in one picture

On 10-21-2014, a big storm passed through our area and I went out into the street to take pictures of the lightning. I took a series of pictures and this object showed up in one picture. I have pictures just before and after, not showing it. I never noticed it, until I downloaded my pictures onto my computer. At first I thought it might be a big bird or possibly a balloon, not sure. This is why I decided to send it to you. It's definitely round or either a sphere, with two black spots across from each other and you can determine what it might be.
