Glowing rotating UFO seen in SF night sky

While star gazing on a warm SF night - I decided to take a picture of a bright glowing star I couldn’t quite identify. Next thing you know - I’m viewing this ‘star’ via my iphone and then it starts zipping around, disappearing, changing shapes etc. This goes on for almost 4 hours and I’m pretty sure it’s not a drone..The ‘star’ like UFO was only visible through my iphone camera and not visible to the human eye. I saw this again the next night from my same vantage point.
