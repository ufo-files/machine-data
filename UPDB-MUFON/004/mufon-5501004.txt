While golfing , I was standing on a plateau , and saw a large silver globe hovering above a local subdivision , across the valley

This incident took place about 15 years ago , as I was on a golf course in the Kelowna area of British Columbia. It was during the middle of the week during the summer, and as I can recall, it was very warm . I was all alone at the time , as it was a slow time of the week , so I was the only one on the golf course to see the object (to the best of my knowledge)... I was on a plateau where there were two tee-offs to two separate fairways , so I had a pretty good view of the surrounding area. As I was taking my practice swings , I happened to look Northwards , across the valley, and noticed what appeared to be a hot air balloon,(but quite a bit larger than a typical hot air balloon), about half a mile away..Upon further inspection, I noticed that this object had no basket or undercarriage that a typical hot air balloon would have...It was silver , and was kind of shimmering , much like a metal ball bearing would if exposed to the sun... It didn't move or make any sound at all and just sat there, shimmering in the sun , about 250-300 feet above some houses in the subdivision across the valley...I had a very strange feeling as I was observing this object , kind of like someone was watching me....I watched this object for about 3-4 minutes , then decided to hit my ball... After I hit my ball , I looked up to the same area , where the houses were , and realized that the object had vanished, just disappeared without a trace...I just stood there in disbelief , with the realization that I certainly hadn't witnessed a hot air balloon ! Unfortunately , I didn't have a camera or cell phone with me to take a photo , so was unable to have evidence to show, and I think this is why I've been reluctant to share this story publicly , although , over the years , it has made for some interesting story telling around the campfire !
