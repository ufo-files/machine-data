{dr}Dark or gray disc shaped approx 50ft dia.

While looking up at clouds about 5pm saw what appeared to be a disc shaped craft approx. 2 miles high, dark in color, on the side of a large cloud...craft was traveling very slowly.....est. about 50 mph....it gradually slowed and came to a stop and hovered. I watched for a few seconds then I ran indoors to get my camera...came back and managed to get a few pics....then ran to knock on the door of a neighbor to get a witness...he wasnt home. I returned right away to watch the craft but it had disappeared so I didn't get to see in which manner it departed. Im attaching photos. Im estimating the craft was about 50ft diameter so at approx. 2 miles altitude(difficult to judge) and if it was 2 miles high, thats how I judge the object/craft to be approx. 50ft diameter. pics don't show much after theyre enlarged...too pixelated.(also an indication craft was only 50ft dia.)
