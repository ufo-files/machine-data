White Tumbling UFO Milton WV

It was about to storm and there was an opening in the clouds above my house. I was laying in a lazy chair in the yard and was looking up at the sky around 3:30pm. The object moved from west to east just a little above cloud height. The object was white in color and was tumbling through the air at a high rate of speed. There was no sound and object was moving very fast. The object disappeared in the clouds.
