{cak} Got very bright dimmed out, four separate times

I was sitting on my friends porch talking about college, we had been watching the sky for shooting stars, planes, etc...I had been facing the west with my feet on the railings leaning back in the chair staring at the sky. We saw several shooting stars pass by, planes, then I saw what looked like a star get brighter than the other ones around it. This immediately caught both our attention, I stood up so I wasnt on a chair that wobbles to see its direction and movement. It wasnt moving more than a few hundred miles an hour, based on planes that had been passing by. The object became extremely bright, then dimmed out to approximately the light a plane would give off, then repeated this three more times over approximately a minute and a half to two minutes. I couldnt tell whether the it changed its direction by the distance from us, but we both counted four separate extremely bright pulsating moments. Only one light was observed. I didn't have my phone or a camera on me to record the event, but there were two observers. My other is a friend and business partner of mine who is 58, and has owned a highly reputable business here in town for 30 years. I am going to college for Biotechnology, and have a great passion for astrophysics. Hope this helps. I can absolutely draws a sketch for you since I am a artist.
