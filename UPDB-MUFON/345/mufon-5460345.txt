I arrived to my home in Avon from work around 1:50 AM 6/4/2019, as I was walking into my house I saw a bright looking star in the sky. it looked out of place and was not the north star. I took out my phone and filmed it with full zoom with my iPhone. This object was "spherical" white, "star like" orb with pulsating movement. it looked like the edges were fuzzy and almost liquid like. This object was not a star! it looked like the brightest star in the sky though. With the iPhone camera focus I could see this thing had a circle like structure emanating light from its center, this could only be seen when the camera focus was spot on. I'm not sure if the focus of the camera was making the object change shape (trick of the camera) or the object itself was changing shape. I have caught this object in the sky before on other occasions and have video proof with dates on my YouTube page. You can see my raw footage on my YouTube page, search for [link moved to FI Section/CMS/jpg] you will find some of my videos on there. MUFON can use them how ever they see fit. If you contact me I have more videos and I could possibly send you some that are not published on YouTube yet. Please note: I do not edit any of my videos. I will say that this object likes to stay in the same general area of sky. It does have slight movement but that movement can really only be seen with a camera not the naked eye.
