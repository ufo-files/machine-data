{rjl} I saw what I think was a UFO while I was on a plane

I was on an airplane flying from North Carolina to LAX and during my flight, I noticed out my window that there was a huge thunder storm a couple of miles away. I took out my phone because I wanted to take a video of the lightning, and while I was filming, I noticed an object flying. The object was blinking red and white lights. At first it was flying in a straight line, and then it flew downwards, then flew up again and started going backwards. It then stopped, and continued flying forward. I was so confused because we were only mid flight and still super high in the air. I thought it was a drone, but because we were so high up, there was no way that was the case. It was too close to be another plane, and because it flew backwards for a second, I knew that it couldn't be. It also moved super quickly. It was flying away from the airplane I was on, which is why I lost sight of it. At first, I couldn't believe what I had saw. I was in shock that I even got it on video!! I was so confused and all I wanted to do was get off the plane so I could show my friends the video and confirm that I wasn't going crazy. In the video I shared, you will first notice a few white/yellow flashes. That was the thunder I was originally trying to film. Half way through the video you will see the flashing lights. Watch how it goes down and then goes back up and then goes down again.
