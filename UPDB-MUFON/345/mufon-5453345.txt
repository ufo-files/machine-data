I was on our landline, when my husband, Daniel yelled for me to get outside now. I stepped out on our back steps and he pointed upwards. I personally saw about eight crafts flying in a straight south, south-east direction evenly separated apart and when the front craft got to a certain point, it disappeared and each consecutive craft thereafter disappeared at the same point the first one did. 

My husband saw at least twelve crafts do the same.

There was no noise that we could tell.

It was too quick and sudden to get pictures.
