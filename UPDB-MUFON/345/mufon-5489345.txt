{nd} At first the ship was clouded in a blue mist. Later the mist went away and the ship was perfectly visible.

We were going to the Elton John concert that night which was at Auburn. I was driving and saw a blue cloud in the sky. I thought it was radioactive material from a test at Auburn or something like that. I was with one other person that is now deceased. We pulled over when we could see a perfect line to the object. We got out of the car and set on the hood. The blue cloud went away from the ship and its outline was perfectly visible. It had windows made from lights about three I think. Never a sound. We watched for a long time and the ship began to cloud up again. We were now getting late to the concert and left. After we got to the concert it was nearly over. I am unsure about the times. If I had the camera and tripod I have now we would have gotten some terrific pictures.
