{tjd} Bright like that when viewed thru binoculars was a cluster of lights that moved

Originally looked like a bright star amongst other stars, planes, and helicopters. However, it stayed brighter than the stars after the helicopters and planes left. We looked at it through camera zoom and binoculars because we were curious and found that it was a cluster of bright lights, not a single light. It moved up and down, back and forth, wiggled, split into two orb shapes then back to one. It moved slightly higher and westward over the course of an hour and eventually disappeared behind cloud cover after a little over an hour.
