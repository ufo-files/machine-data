{rjl} It appeared as twenty lights and some disappear while another went to a triangular shape.

My husband and I was coming from listening to live music from a bar inside of a complex. As we were leaving the complex we needed to make a left turn to get out. When my husband looked to the right he realized that there were strange lights in the sky. He wanted to find out what the strange lights were so we turned right and pulled over to see what was going on and that's when I started filming. I automatically thought that they were ufo's because they started off at somewhere around 15 or 20 lights and than some began to fade away into thin air while three objects made a triangular shape. Than it seemed like all the lights disappeared but when I looked at the video again I realized that even though the lights could no longer be seen with the naked eye they were still showing up in the video. It appeared at if the lights were just hovering before disappearing and changing shape. I always knew that we weren't alone I was just shocked because I never thought that I would see an unknown object. After it seems like the objects disappeared a helicopter came immediately afterward and began to encircle the area of where the objects were seen. We left the area but as we were driving home we could still see the helicopter searching the area, and the helicopter didn't make any noise. If you weren't looking up you would have missed the whole thing.
