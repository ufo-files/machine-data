Looking south on central and saw one large orb of light and four more showed up.

It was 8/18/10 at 10 PM exactly, I saw a large orb of light, yellow orange in color. It was joined by four more lights two minutes later. When all five lights were out, I went in to call my girlfriend. I went back out and some of the lights were shutting down. I called 911 and asked for Luke AFB and was told I could not have that number (?). She explained she would contact the right authorities. I was still outside and within five minutes all the lights shut off within seconds of each other. Twenty minutes later the lights (7) went on at south mountain. All kinds of aircraft was in the vicinity. I was facing south. These lights looked like the phoenix lights in 1997. I had five friends over and asked them to look and when they did the lights started to disappear. These lights lasted for at least 10 minutes and they came back on in about twenty minutes. The neighborhood dogs barked for no reason all night.
