{jpg} SHAPE CHANGEING

I WAS HELPING MY UNCLE WITH HIS COWS AT HIS FARM AND I JUST HAPPEND TO LOOK IN THE SKY JUST LIKE I ALWAYS DO THEN I SEEN SOMTHING COME OUT OF NO WHERE. I NEW IT WAS NOT ANY THING MAN MADE BECAUSE I SEEN IT FROM OVER 10 MILES AWAY FROM ME BUT STILL TO LARGE TO BE A KNOWN AIRPLANE.IT KEPT CHANGEING RECTANGLE SHAPE'S LONG,SHORT,THIN,AND FAT IT WAS MORE THEN FOOTBALL FEILD LONG.IT RAPITLY CHANGED SHAPE'S IN THE SKY THE HOLE TIME IT MOVED BY I FELT LUCKY TO SEE IT AND KEPT WATCHING IT BECAUSE THIS WAS NOT THE ONLY TIME I EVER SEEN A UFO I HAVE SEEN THEM WHEN I WAS YOUNGER. I WAS WATCHING THIS ONE TILL I LOST SIGHT OF THE OBJECT WHEN IT WAS LAST SEEN OVER BY THE NUCLEAR POWER PLANET ON HWY 94.ALSO I DON'T HAVE ANY PICTURES OF IT BUT I CAN DESCRIBE IT TO SOMEBODY THAT CAN MAKE A SKETCH OR A DRAWING OF IT I WILL TRY TO MAKE A PICTURE OF WHAT HAVE SEEN THE BEST I CAN.
