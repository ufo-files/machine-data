Black object with oddly rotating exterior features seen moving north to south along the western side of the Chisos Mountains

Due to the location, I suspect this may be some form of border monitoring drone technology but thought it was worthy of reporting.

While seated outside at the Startlight Theatre Restaurant I noticed a large black object moving north to south roughly between my location and the Chisos Mountains. It appeared to be closer to the Chisos. It was black and thin horizontally from my perspective, not unlike a drone might look. However there were odd movements along the edges of the object. Almost as if large blades were rotating slowly, vertically (like a helicopter turned on it's side but the blades moving slow enought to see). The "blades" created movement around the entire exterior of the object. The blades were considerably smaller than the object itself. Another analogy for the blades would be a freestyle swimmer and watching the arms move outide/above of the water.

It moved horizontally, perhaps decending every slightly over its course from just north of the Chisos, in front of the Chisos, then behind a small mountain that was between the Starlight and the object. I pointed it out to my wife, but she could not locate it (her eyes are not as good as mine and she didn't have her glasses). Other people on the deck said they saw it breifly.

By the time I discrned that this may not be a drone or helicopter, I attempted to pull out my phone camera but it dissapeared behind a mountain and did not return to sight.
