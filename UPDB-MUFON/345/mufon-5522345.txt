I was on my way home from work that day and it was suddenly very cloudy. There were many different color clouds in the sky. I started taking photos while driving and when I got to the streetlights along the way. I love looking at rainbows especially since I don't see them that often and when I saw these, I noticed that there were two (one right behind the other). After I showed the photographs to a few people and not only thought they were odd, but that they had never seen anything like that before.

What I saw were two rainbows, they were not moving or anything, but they were in the clouds. They had what appears to be two headlights in front of one of the rainbows. By the time I reached home, which was less than 30mins, they were nowhere in the sky, but there were still a lot of clouds.
