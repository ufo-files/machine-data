{jpg} Watched what I thought was comm satellite change direction 90 degrees at full speed

I was sleeping outdoors next to our house watching the sky, when I noticed one of the "stars" moving SE to NW. I assumed it was a communications satellite (a not unheard of thing by 1966), and as I watched it travel almost directly overhead, but just slightly to my right, which would be slightly east of overhead, the "satellite" turned 90 degrees to the right (east) without slowing down! It didn't curve to the east, and it didn't slow for even a moment. It simply changed its direction. It continued to the east until I could no longer see it (our house blocked my view), and when I crawled out of my sleeping bag to a spot where it might again be visible, I couldn't pick it out any more.
