{cak} 3 red Lights in formation with a single red light a minute later.

I had came in from watching the fireworks display at Bellevue park.I was watching T.V when my cousin called me outside to look at something. when I got outside and looked southwest I saw three red lights slightly pulsating in a triangular formation traveling northeast. Then a single red light followed probably 30 seconds later in the same flight path. the objects did not have any blinking aircraft lights. whats most interesting to me Is that They moved without any "engine" noise and the triangular formation.
