(gs) 2 Glowing lights, slow descent

My family and I live in a home that sits very high on a ridge. Its largely fields all around,so we know well the usual lights we see every night. The night of July 9th, 2012, my wife went to get something out of her car around 10:45, and noticed 2 hovering lights a few miles due South in the distance. The lights were definitely out of place and interesting, and seemed to hover for 3 or 4 minutes in the same place (one a little higher and to the right of the other), and then both slowly descended below the distant horizon at what seemed to be a synchronized descent. If there were any sound, I wouldve thought it was 2 helicopters searching for something, but there was no sound, and two helicopters wouldnt be a common occurence either (and we didn't see any news stories referencing any searches).

We tried to break out my sons telescope to get a closer look, but were not very telescope savvy, and the descent below the horizon came a little too quick.

Nothing crazy or scary about the incident, we were just curious if anyone else had seen anything similar, as we were left saying, "Huh?, that was interesting" :).
