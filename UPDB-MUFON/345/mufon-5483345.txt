{jpg} VIDEO White Circular UFO - Gulf Of Mexico In Biloxi Mississippi - May 23 2016

I was staying at a hotel on Biloxi Beach in Mississippi on May 23, 2016. My son and I were on the back balcony area of this hotel looking at the gulf of mexico water and beautiful sunset around 7:30 p.m. We both noticed a large circular white UFO in the sky above us. It had NO wings and appeared to be at a high altitude above us in the sky. The UFO seemed to be moving at a very high rate of speed but it had no sound. This UFO glowed a bright white color and at times it appeared to be cigar shaped but mostly circular. It was difficult to tell the exact shape of the UFO since it was at such a high altitude. It was amazing to see this object in person and I was excited to capture video footage of this UFO.
