{dr}Quick glimpse of 3 bright lights in circular motion.

This was back when I was in middle school and my parents were driving me back from a school concert. I was in the back seat looking out of the window because the sky showed more stars than usual that night. There were 3 white lights among the stars and they formed a triangle shape and were moving in sync in a circular motion and when I watched it I felt a sense of wonder and amazement. I looked at the front seat to my parents to tell them that there was a UFO right outside circling our neighborhood, but they didn't believe me and when I looked back outside it disappeared. The car radio turned to white noise and our cable was out the rest of the night. That was the first and last time I saw a UFO but I still feel a bit euphoric whenever I think about it.
