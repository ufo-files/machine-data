{tjd} looking at planes with my oberwerk BT-100-45 binoculars with ff23.9 mm lens passed over a light that should not have been visible at sunset if a star or planet

I was watching planes at sunset when I passed over a stationary light. It would not have been visible if it were a star or planet. I watched the object for 30 minutes. It was primarily stationary but its apparent movement was west to east. I looked at my astronomy apps and it would have been at where Neptune was, but of course there was no way that I could have seen Neptune. I was using Oberwerk B-100-45 binoculars with a 23.9 mm eyepiece. I was able to see the object in the spotting scope also, but I could not see it unaided. As I watched the object divided into two objects. One dropping from the original. The dropping object had what looked like a smoke trail. The new object had blinking lights. After they separated they were visible for a minute or so, then both went out. I kept scanning the sky, but saw nothing. When it became dark, there were no stars or objects anywhere in the sector were the objects were sigthed. When I originally passed over the object I thought that it may be a bright star or planet that was just bright enough to be seen under magnafication. When I looked at my astronomy APPS there was nothing in the area where I observed the object. At the end of the sighting the objects just disappeared. The smoke trail was visible until they disappeared...I did not notice if the smoke trail lingered.
