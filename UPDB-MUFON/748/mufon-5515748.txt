(GS) I saw a gold, luminous, vertical oval orb above mountains in the western sky.

I took a trip to Sedona, Az from Chicago & stayed at the Kings Ransom Inn. On Thurs.,
Aug. 30, 2012, at about 7:30 p.m., I walked out of my hotel to snap some photos from my cell phone, while waiting for a friend to pick me up for the evening. At approximately 7:45 p.m., as I
was gazing at the western skyline of dark cliffs silhouetted against a fading sunset, I saw, to my
utter amazement, a brilliant, golden, oval orb in vertical position, hover over the mountain peaks.
At the same time, a small jet appeared and flew in front of the orb, then disappeared.
 I stared at the orb in disbelief, yet awestruck. I watched it hover over the dark peaks, then, 
slowly descend behind them. I took several photos of what I had seen, but, unfortunately, my
cell phone camera is very basic. It could not even capture the full moon way up in the mountains,
where there existed no other light.
   My friend, whom I shared the evening with, has lived in Sedona for 1 1/2 years. He had talked 
about seeing plenty of ufos there. I described what I had seen to him, and he said that he, himself,
had witness that type of orb. I was actually quite excited and happy that I witnessed an orb. I have
believed in their existence for a long time, but had never seen one. 
  As far as being affected psychologically, it is al very positive. I thank you for your highly
informative website, where those like me, can report ufo sightings. Documenting this sighting
to your non-profit organization has allowed me to express the wonder and amazement of what
I saw, what I know is not of our earth.
