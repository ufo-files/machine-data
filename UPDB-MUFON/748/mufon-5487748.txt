2 ufos- first disc like not very above and second star like which is going slowly.

Me and my friend was taking walk near her house and suddenly I saw a disk like object hovering nearby not very high in the sky but is not making any type of noise. It was flashing white , red and blue lights and it appeared like a disc. Two aeroplanes also crossed the ufo. And after 5 minutes it was disappeared. One minute later me and my friend saw another star like object which first appeared like a start having no movement but suddenly it started moving. It was of white color and first it was moving very slowly and after some secs it increased its speed and then again it stopped for a sec and then again start moving. It was visible for 5 minutes and in that time it was moving in the manner described above.
