A jet engine sound went over head but saw nothing.

I was working outside and around 11am on 9-23-2020 we heard a very loud jet engine come from the south to north with no object ever spotted. It was a cloudless sky and I looked to the north to look ahead of the noise and saw nothing. The person I was with saw nothing either. After talking to several guys I was working with, none of them saw anything. I spoke to my wife who was out at the time, heard and saw nothing either. It sounded like a jet that was flying very low and the oddest thing was that about the time the sound got to overhead or a little past, the sound went silent for 3 seconds then came back like it was paused.
