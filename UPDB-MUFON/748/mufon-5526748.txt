Silver or white cigar shaped ufo

Around 6:30pm my kids came running in asking for the camera because they saw a ufo. (Usually this is just a plane that they are seeing) I went out without my camera to see it. When I got out there it was just flying over the house. It seemed to have a black band around the middle.I am certain that it didn't have any wings. By the time I had gotten my camera it was pretty far away. It was hard to make it out against the hazy cloud cover. I did get two pictures. The first two are unedited and the last one has only been sharpened and colors enhanced. I would like to know if this is just a type of weather baloon drone or what. Last summer we saw exactly the same thing flying in the same direction SW to NE but there were 3 of them flying close together in a straight line.
