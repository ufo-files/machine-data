Saw what looked like a falling star, stop as it was falling then change direction and move somewhat slowly

At approximately 8:40 PM on Wednesday 8/25 I was sitting on my couch watching tv. Behind my TV is my dining room and a large window leading out to the back yard. Through this window I saw what looking like a falling star/meteor. It was orange and falling fast with a tail, it seemed orange and the tail was the same color. All of a sudden as I'm watching it, just as it's about to be out of my line of sight, it stops on a dime and starts moving east, slowly compared to the speed it was falling. I jumped up and ran into the back yard and watched it head South then East over the hills. I went back inside to get my phone and that's when I filmed my attached video, as it was heading East over the hills until it was out of view. Now maybe I saw a falling star then noticed an airplane next to it as it burned out but it really seemed like it stopped and turned. It made no sound and it seemed like the shape was either a delta (triangle) or a square (four fixed lights). My video doesn't do it justice but that's as good as my phone gets at night.
