I saw 10-15 red white circler lights flying horizontally in single file south to north over te ocean. At a certain point the lights did a 90 degree up turn and became smaller and smaller untill they disappeared.

I was vacationing with my girlfriend in Mazatl1n, Mexico Feb 20-27 2015. Every night after the entertainment was finished I would go up our room and sit on the 7th floor balcony and look to see how many fishing boats were out and star gaze; specifically the moon which crescents are located differently as the moon would appear in Canada. I could only see 35 degrees to my left (south) as the hotel blocked my view. On Feb 21 or slight possibility the 22/2015 at approximately 1130 PM red-white circler lights started to appear from the south at 50-55 degrees. They appeared about 10x the size of the stars flying horizontally in single file south to north. All appeared to be at the same altitude except 2-3 were 3-5 degrees lower. After seeing about 5 I hollered at my sleeping girlfriend to come outside now. She was fortunate to witness the final four before disappearing. I would say there were 12-14 in total. My guesstimate elevation for the UFO's is at 40000 feet plus. At straight west from our view point the lights would turn 90 degrees and go up towards space. As they went up the lights would appear smaller and smaller until they vanished. After the 3rd or 4th light appeared I new I was witnessing an uncommon sight and was fortunate to share it with my girlfriend. The only time I saw anything as bewildering as this was in the summer of 1974 or 1975. Three white lights flying like nothing made by man and then darting off into space at lightning speed.
