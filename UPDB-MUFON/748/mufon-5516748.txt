{dr}2 red lights 1 white cluster of lights at a distance of arms straight out pointing fingers 10 inches apart

my wife and myself witnessed 3 lights, 2 red, front and middle, and 1 white cluster of lights, trailing. looking at a 45 degree angle from the horizon with arms straight out and fingers pointing, the distance from front light to the back light was approx. 10 inches, could not see the form of the object but the lights moved as if it were one object. it moved from east to west the turned and moved north. there was no sound and moved at the speed of a blimp. it moved out of sight over the tree line.
