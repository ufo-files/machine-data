{nd} a wedge looked like 4-5 white geese just gliding no noise moving fast

Was in my hot tub looking for shooting stars on a clear night, spotted what i thought was a flock of 4-5 white geese with wings extended in wedge formation but moving way too fast and disappeared beyond neighbors trees. No sound at all. Travelled N to S. Very bright. Stayed at same elevation throughout flight. Hard to tell how high but not super high elevation because I thought they were geese. A spotlight from a corn maze was going at the same time but this was unrelated as i saw the spotlight moments after and was not nearly as bright and could not account for the brightness of the wedge or the direction.
