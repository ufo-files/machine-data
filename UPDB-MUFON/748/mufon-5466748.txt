{jpg} Third time witnessing orb. Usually flies wobbiling straight line, once turning unnaturally fast. Once imitated formation lights when commercial flight neared.

On three separate occasions, was out walking dog at about 8:30pm (EST) and looked up. On first and third occasion (3rd occasion happened today 8/6/18) witnessed a bright white, Star-like, orb moving from west to east in a straight, but wobbling path, without sound. On third occasion, a commercial flight was seen south of the object. (flight's formation lights clearly defined) Orb vanished without sight obstruction.

On second occasion (on, or around 7/31/18) witnessed a bright white (no other lights seen), star-like orb, to my south. It flew faster than any military jet I've seen, and was flying a pattern that appeared to erratic for a manned flight. A commercial airline was seen getting close (from my point of view) and the orb suddenly started mimicking the formation lights. Red and green lights suddenly appear, and did not match flashing pattern on commercial flight. (red and green would occasionally be paired like object did not understand the lights)

Only second occasion witnessed with another person
