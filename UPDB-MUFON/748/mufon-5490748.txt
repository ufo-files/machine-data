My dad was working about 20 miles south of Pacos Texas, in Reeves county. He had seen a deer and wanted to take pictures of it while it was running strait away from his position. He then proceeded to look at the pictures, and on the 17th or 18th photo he noticed a strange object in the sky in the picture. He couldn't see it with his naked eye.
