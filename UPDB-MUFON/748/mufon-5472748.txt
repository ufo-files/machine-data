{dr}On 10/27/17, my wife and I observed 2 walnut shaped craft with blinking lights like Christmas tree lights. Tonight, 10/28/17, we decided to see if they would return. Boy, did they. We observed 10+ walnut shaped craft.

On 10/27/17, at about 10:30pm, my wife looked out our kitchen window and said she saw some strange blinking lights. I grabbed our binoculars to get a better look. There were 2 walnut shaped craft with lights blinking quickly, (like Christmas tree lights). We observed them for about 30 minutes. They were separated, one was due east, the other was south east. They appeared about 35-40 degrees above the horizon. We went to bed.

On 10/28/17, at about 11:00pm, we decided to see if they would return. When we looked in the direction the objects had been the previous night, there was over 10 objects....same size, same walnut shape. Some had the same blinking light pattern, some had constant yellowish lights. The thing that caught my attention was that they were arranged in a pattern kind of mimicking the Orion star formation. I again observed the objects with binoculars. But they were bright enough to see with the naked eye. There red, blue, yellow, green blinking pattern is what due our attention.

Two of the objects had smaller, brighter craft that seemed to come out of them. The smaller craft stayed close to the larger one. The smaller craft at first appeared like an orb. But then it elongated to a thinner, pencil like shape.

I can't believe that there were so many of the bright blinking objects in the sky and no one else saw them.
