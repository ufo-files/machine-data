tic-tac shaped object viewed over Lake Catherine near Brighton, Utah

A tic-tac shaped UFO was sighted at approximately 5 PM (MST) on 8/17/2019 over Lake Catherine near Brighton, Utah. The object remained stationary despite high winds and fast-moving clouds. The object appeared translucent and had black equipment attached to each end. The object was observed with high-quality optics for approx. 7 minutes until it vanished.
