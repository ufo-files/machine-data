{nd} Very bright red light over Wayne County Michigan.

10/8/14 approximately 8:30 pm I observed a very bright red light hovering in the sky to the south east of my home. The light pulsed and blinked at random, no pattern. The light also changed in intensity from very bright to barely visible then back to very bright. At least 3 times brighter than any marker lights on an airplane. The aircraft the started moving east to west, a few miles at least, then hovered for about 15 minutes. It then began to move back and forth in a north/south direction for about 5 minutes. Maybe a half mile distance back and forth. Then hovered. Then it moved back south, hovered then did the north/south movement again. It then moved very slowly in a west direction, stopped after about a mile or two then started heading north. It traveled about 5 miles very quickly before it came to a stop and hovered. It sat there for about 20 minutes then seemed to split into two bright red lights. Short after the split a small dull white light designed quickly from the clouds on a direct path for the red lights. The red lights seemed to adjust it position to avoid a collision with the smaller white light. Shortly after that the red lights seemed to have turned off. Nothing after that. During the incident the crafts speed varied widely from very slow to much faster than any aircraft in the sky. I couldn't hear any sound coming from it, but that could of been due to distance. The weather was fair with only a few light clouds in the sky. I live near Detroit metro airport so I am accustomed so seeing all sort of craft in the sky at all times of the day and night. This was very unlike anything I have ever seen.
