Looked like a satellite

My wife and I were sitting in the Hot Tub enjoying the night and watching for satellites when we observed what we thought to be a satellite coming form the SE. As it passed overhead, the light was wobbling which we thought odd. As it passed and was going away from us, the light got bright for a brief second and disappeared.
