{dr}two orange orbs being chased or followed by two military helicopters

I was doing laundry around 10:30pm on 8/28/2015, when I heard very loud helicopters close to the house. I stepped out in time to see two orange orbs followed closely by two helicopters flying over my house.The orbs had a bright orange red glow and were very small, like the size of a golf ball. They were flying in sync with each other and they were wobbling while flying in unison. The helicopters were right behind each one and they quickly flew out of sight.
