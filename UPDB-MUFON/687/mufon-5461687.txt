03/26/19 Approximately 06:15 AM I was traveling south on 3rd Ave. Driving up the viaduct I noticed to the SE a bright white light that almost matched the brightness of the streetlights and almost appeared in line with the street lights. Upon reaching the top of the viaduct I could clearly see it was not the street lights. There appeared to be an object above the field. Driving off the viaduct and beginning on the flat road as I slowed, I could see clearly a dimly lit dome, an apparent cluster of bright white lights and what appeared to be a red tail light. The lights were solid and there was no flashing associated. The object appeared as though it may have been moving slowly in a NW direction however I was driving south on third ave. Shortly after regaining focus on my driving and still trying to maintain a visual the object it appeared to vanish. I never thought to use phone video.
