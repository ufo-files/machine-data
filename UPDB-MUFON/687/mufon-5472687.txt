{dr}looked like a very bright star, but it moves.

I gaze at the stars several times each night. I was looking out the glass door, and noticed a very bright star, low in the sky. The only one that low, just above the tree line. As I'm looking, I wondered, 'did that just move?'. I watched, and, yes, it was moving. Sorta hovering, but also moving. In any direction it wanted. It went up, down, did arcs and circles. I went to get an adult to see, but he was asleep. This was just after midnight. My kids were up, ages 11,12,and 13, so I called them. Independently, I asked them, 'Do you see anything in the sky?' They each remarked on the brightest star, just above the trees. One at a time, they asked me, did that move? Lasted about 10 minutes, I was glued to the window. My eyes ere crying. Then star ducked below tree line momentarily, then path straight out SW direction, until we lost sight of it, which took about 4 seconds.
