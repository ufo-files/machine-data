White oval shaped object

Was outside around 11:00pm when I saw white oval object in sky. At first thought it was bird but when it cleared top of nearby tree I noticed it was not a bird. There were no lights, no sound, just an white, oval shaped object heading in a southerly direction moving faster than a bird, lower than a plane would fly. Really awe struck, couldn't believe what I was seeing. Tried to report it via email to the Kansas Chapter of Mufon the night of incident. However was checking my email tonight and had notice that email was not sent, saying email address sent to was not a valid email address!
