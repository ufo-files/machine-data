{jpg} Object captured during timelapse leaving trail from moon.

I was taking a timelapse with my gopro hero4 black, I was testing settings while I aimed it at the blood moon event on september 27th. I captured several frames @ 10sec exposures with a constant interval. On only 1 of my frames I captured a glowing object which appears to traverse from the moon towards the camera exits the cameras frame and re-enters the frame, changes direction several times and appears to get larger or closer while doing so. We did not see the even Live in Action. It was upon review of the timelapse that we discovered the unusual light show. The time lapse was @ I believe 10sec intervals (possibly 15 secs) but I was pretty sure it was 10secs shutterspeeds. The sheer distance this object travels is amazing. Please review the image I attached, I've been asking photgraphy experts if I may have accidently created this oddity, but I was reffered to submit this report because it appears I captured a "flight path"
