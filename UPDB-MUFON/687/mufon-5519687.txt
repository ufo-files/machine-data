low and unusually large off-white yellowish light slowly moving in clear sky, then disappeared

Me and another person were standing outside of Jefferson College in Arnold, Missouri. I pointed out the satellite that I see most nights from my backyard and mentioned how you can see a faint blinking of red and blue lights. To our right we noticed a very large yellowish/off-white lighted object. It was rectangular in shape only it didn't have sharp corners. It wasnt too high and it wasnt too far. It was a clear night and there was plenty of distance around it in every direction of empty sky. It was very slowly moving in a straight line. We looked at each other and tried to figure out if it could be a comet or meteor but we concluded that the object had no tail like a meteor would have. Maybe 10-15 seconds went by, I looked back up and it was GONE!!!! It was just gone. We freaked!!! The only reference I can give to explain the size and shape of it is the size of a cigarette butt if you are holding in in front of you--that big only in the freakin sky!!! It was amazing. I hope others seen it and perhaps got some footage cuz I would really love to see it. I don't know how long it was there before we noticed it. I have waited forever to see evidence for myself and this was real. I have always believed, but i just needed to see. Thanks
