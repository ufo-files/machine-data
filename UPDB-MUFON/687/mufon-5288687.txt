A very large cyndrical dark grey object hovered without any movement before lifting straight up very quickly

I was walking out to my car when I noticed a dull light close to the main road maybe 120-150 yards away.
Walking maybe 12 feet closer toward it, I saw a dark grey metallic object that seemed to have a light source underneath. It was at least 40 feet off the ground and made absolutely no sound.
It appeared to have some dark bands of a brownish color that went around the hull of the craft.
The way the lighting was, I couldn't see the top of it but judging just of the viewable bottom it was quite large.
It was incredibly eerie, and a part of me just wanted to get away from it.
I stared at it for what seemed like a few minutes trying to process what it could be. It took off in an instant with again no sound or bright light.
