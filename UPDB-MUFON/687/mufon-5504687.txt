{rjl} Reflective objet that I thought could be the space station, seemed to be the size of a millitary jet.

I was in my back yard and saw a reflective object moving across the southern sky heading east. Clear sky and very high altitude, thought it was the space station because there was no trail from the engine and moving fast across the sky. After watching it moving across the sky I noticed it was followed by two military jets leaving exhaust trails and the object was leaving no trail, this got me thinking that this was not normal. I could tell by the military jet trails that one jet had two trails and the other had four trails (engines). I could see the object was moving faster than the military jets because the distance was getting greater between them as I watched. I watched as they headed east then moved to the front yard to watch again but they were too far across the horizon. I went to the back yard and saw another military jet lagging behind on the same course.
