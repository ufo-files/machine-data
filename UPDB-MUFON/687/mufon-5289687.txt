Ovate, Oval or Cigar shape hovered, then lifted and disappeared.

I was playing in Bronx Park, I looked up and a large Ovalish machine was over my head. It had flashing lights and no sound that I can recollect. After a few minutes it lifted straight up then disappeared. My next memory is strange. It seemed like I was in a Dust Devil and landed on the ground about an Eighth of a Mile away near a local School Yard.
  This memory manifested itself years later when I felt a buzzing feeling on my back. I kibitzed with friends that it was an Alien tracking device implanted when I was a Child. In that moment the memory recurred.
