Cigar shape

Extremely low, allowing me to view it. At first glance I saw windows and people looking at me. As I tried to make out faces the windows disappeared. I thought it was a plan about to crash. But it kept traveling very low towards to ocean. Fire was coming out from the back. This took place in October 1968. I had another sitting in 1967 in Vietnam along with 50 other Marines. It was at night a bowling ball size object white in color moved slowly towards us. Then shot quickly into the sky. This ball was like glowing white. We all saw it. I have always wondered why I was allowed to witness these events and what am I supposed to do with this information.
