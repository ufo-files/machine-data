{dr}Saw a large dark boomerang shaped object with orange lights.

I was driving west up a hill on Ohio State Route 125 near a water tower in West Union, Ohio. It was several years ago in November around 7:00 PM after dark. I looked over the top of the water tower and saw a very large dark object with the shape of a boomergang. Along the back edge were 12 to 16 orange lights about a foot in diameter. Between 5 to 10 seconds later the lights went out or the object disapeared. I checked with the sheriffs office and no one had reported anything. During the next several years I did talk to severel people who had seen the object but didn't report it in fear of calling in a hoax. I know them all very well with one being a wildlife officer. I was in the Army National Guard for 21 years and retired as a Major. I was also on the local volunteer fire depatment for 34 years and was chief for 16 of those years.
