Three Luminous Objects

The witness was fishing in the middle of the Raccoon River at approximately 10:30 p.m. Three luminous lights caught my eye that were unlike anything that I had ever seen. They were traveling from the south to the north east of my location. They were moving in a snake-like path engaging in a hovering/rising speed-boosting flight path. I tracked the first object and then yelled as loud as possible to get their attention. The object in front immediately started to disappear. As it disappeared the witness could see some of the stars through it. Then, the other two followed suit. The witness took a mental picture of the objects half-way through their flight path.  Two rotating fan-like movements were observed on the bottom of the object, one going clockwise and one going counter-clockwise. In the fan-like area the colors yellow, blue and pink were there but there were many other hues. These objects disappeared at exactly the spot that someone two years earlier had told the witness that there was a portal at this location.
