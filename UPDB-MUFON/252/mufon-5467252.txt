{td} held camera to sky to take pics of Aug 22, 2018 total Eclipse with phone camera, then tablet. Saw pics later when reviewing.

August 22, 2017, prepared to watch total solar eclipse from my front porch, but was a better view to sit on corner of bottom porch step. I laid my cell phone close, and tablet to use cameras to take photos of eclipse since this was an amazing event to witness. It was shortly after 9am, about 9:10am, the sky darkened to a hazy grey, a cool breeze was felt, and the moon what seemed snail like, but yet also quickly covered the space in the sky of the sun. A bright fire like ring, was in the sky very Large in size where I was looking north east. I quickly held my phone to the sky to take photos of this ring of what looked like a circus ring of fire. I put down my phone, then picked up tablet and took a few more photos. The eclipse ended and I was happily going to look at my photos, expecting to see the amazing ring of brightness. I looked at my photos, over and over, and saw what was a bright light above a UFO shape, like I have seen in photos over the past years of my life. I looked at all of my photos, then I could see the bright light, the sun I think, and in front, a tipped glowing disk shape, each photo, the shape slightly moved a little to the left of where I had been looking when photos were taken. At the time that I took photos I did not see with my eyes what is shown in these photos. I have not known how to enjoy the amazement of these photos. I have shared with a few trusted people. I do not want to take away from the amazement of my own secret event of taking these photos.
