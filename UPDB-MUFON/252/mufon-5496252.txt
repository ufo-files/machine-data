{nd} Bright blue-green orb of light with white trail fell vertically at high speed.

While sitting at a stoplight in my vehicle facing southwest, I witnessed a bright blue-green orb of light with white trail fall vertically at high speed. I took notice due it to being so bright and large in comparison to other lights in the sky. At first I thought it might have been a firework, but no other display of lights were emitted. It was not a "shooting star". It was not an airplane. No sound emitted that I could hear. I lost sight of it as it dropped below tree line. I estimate that it was several miles away from my location. I was not impacted physically or emotionally. Have never witnessed an object like this before.
