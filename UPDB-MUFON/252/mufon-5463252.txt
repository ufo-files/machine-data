{cak} Round and segmented....solid brown excepted where shadowed.

I was traveling nw on hwy 44 in down town Inverness when I saw a large brown segmented round thing with a lumpy pole sticking out of the side of it. I slowed and watch it for about 5 to 10 seconds. It did not move. I looked in the rear view mirror and saw that some one was behind me, so I put the window down and put my arm out and pointed up. I watch him for a reaction and there was none. I then proceeded home. I just could not believe it was a UFO. I first I thought that is was some sort of promotional balloon. In that part of town, no way. It was just to big also. It was almost over the old County Court House. I figured if it was a UFO I would here about it; but there was nothing. I'm 71 now and it bugs me everyday and I guess I need to make a record of it. I've also made a drawing of it and I could fax it to you. 
I lost sight of it when I drove to the point I couldn't see it through the windshield. 

I can send a pic of my drawing later when my wife gets home. I'm not good at sending pics over the net. She does all that stuff for me.

This thing was about 45 to 50 feet in diameter and was 10 to 12 foot thick and was segmented with a small diameter pole sticking at an angle from the side of it. It was covered in rectangular protrusions like gear boxes. The pole looked like it was about 4' in diameter and 15' long and lumpy.

You don't have brown as a color choice.
