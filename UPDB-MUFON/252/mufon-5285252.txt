Saw this unidentifed object at an afternoon baseball game

We were out of town for a friends wedding. I saw this unidentifiable flying object at an afternoon baseball game. I got a strange feeling looked up and saw it hovering. I had my camera in my hand and quickly took a picture. I feel that once it realized I noticed it, it shot straight up fast and took off. I don’t know if anyone else saw it because everyone was paying attention to the game. It was super fast and gone before I knew it.
