{cak} Bright light

While outside I observed a very high bright light traveling from the SW heading NE.At 1st I believed it to be a satellite or the space station which I have observed in the past until the craft made a hard turn to the right followed by a hard U turn to the left heading at last view heading NW.It was much higher then the broken clouds in the area as well as an airplane which was also in the sky at the time.I've have never seen a craft move so fast or make the turns it did in all my life! Much to high to be a drone or a normal air craft.
