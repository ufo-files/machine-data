SITTING ON FRONT PORCH. SPOTTED A STRANGE CLOUD THAT LOOKED DIFFERENT FROM ONE AROUND IT. TOOK OUT BINOCULARS AND IT WAS NOT WHITE BUT LIGHT BLUE. VERY LARGE TYPE GLOWING TYPE BALL, VERY LARGE! A RED BALL WITH A LARGE CLOUD MOVED TOWARDS THE VERY LARGE BLUE OBJECT. THE RED CLOUD FORMED UNDER THE BLUE OBJECT AND LOOKED TO AS IT WAS COMING TOGETHER. THEN THE 2 CLOUDS EXPLODED AND I COULD SEE ALL KINDS OF COLOR OF LIGHT. THE TALLEST LOOKING LIKE IT WENT THROUGH THE TOP OF THE BLUE OBJECT. THEN IT LOOKED LIKE MANY SMALL VERY WHITE BALLS, OBJECTS. THEN IT LOOKED JUST LIKE A SPACE SHIP WITH WINDOWS AND IT WAS TILTING LEFT DOWN. VERY LARGE, NOW THIS IS THE BLUE OBJECT I WAS TALKING ABOUT AT THE START OF ALL THIS. THE IT WAS GONE AND THE SMALL RED OBJECT IN THE CLOUD FELL SLOW BEHIND THE MOUNTAIN. RIGHT WHERE I NOTICED IT CAME FROM.
