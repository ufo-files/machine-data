{jpg} White pill-shaped craft seen from commercial flight above Utah.

My family flew across the country from a family member’s wedding yesterday. While we were flying above Utah, around 40,000 to 50,000 ft in altitude, we saw a white oval-shaped craft flying in the opposing direction of the plane. It was traveling at the same altitude and was moving rather fast. It was also emitting red/purplish fumes from its rear, something I have never seen before. It was very surprising. I took my phone out to take a video but did not do this in time. This occurred around 4:05 NDT. We also saw two jets traveling in the same direction of the craft later on at high speeds. We saw the first jet around 4:12 NDT and the second around 4:54 NDT. This specifically occurred as the commercial flight was above central/southwest Utah. In regards to the craft, it was extremely close to the plane, which is surprising as known flying vehicles would never be allowed to be that close to a commercial airplane. The shape and appearance of the craft differed entirely from anything I have seen before. It was a white pill-shaped craft with no noticeable wings, exhausts, or external features that I could see. As far as size, I am uncertain. Obviously it looked pretty small from the window of the airplane as my eyes perceived it. I think it was a pretty big craft though because the jets that followed were of similar size from the perspective of the plane. This was a very shocking event for both my mother and I, who observed the craft and the subsequent jets. I will be reporting this event to as many organizations and entities as possible. Thank You.
