{cak} cross like shaped UFO flight in direction west, east.

At about 3.30 a.m. at 24th July, 2009, I have gone to the balcony and noticed cross like shaped black object appeared above trees and flew above the house in direction from west side to the east. It has been black, darker then the night sky and it had straight orange lights along and across. Both sections were the same shape. Lights did not make any glow, they have been straight as a LED, it produced no sound. It flew at about 500 meters height, and reminded on Mustang P51D, as it had front moved cross part in flight direction and it had no tail. The speed has been maybe twice the speed of an airplane in approach to the airport. It lasted several seconds and it was clear as a day. 
I have called military air traffic control to ask if there were any flights at the time in the area or noticed by radar, but they replied no. 

It had the same black color of the body, the same orange lights and the same speed and path as it had black triangle noted before, but being smaller, triangle is say the size of jumbo jet, this is the size of MD80. Called the civil air traffic control, they confirmed no flights.
