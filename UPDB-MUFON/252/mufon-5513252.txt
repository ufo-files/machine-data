Driving home saw 1 orange light traveling from the SE. Pulled into the drive and watched as the light traveled to the east and started to fade. Then looked to the South and saw 3 more traveling in the same direction as the 1st in a triangular formation. They pretty much traveled the same path and started to fade. Saw a 5th light from the same location as previous 4, traveled the same path then faded away. All lights were traveling the same path, speed and altitude about the speed of a helicopter. They were blinking pretty fast similar to anti-collision but were constant. Dont believe Ive ever seem anything like that.
