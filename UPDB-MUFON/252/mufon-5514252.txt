(GS) BRIGHT LIGHT IN THE SKY OF UNKNOWN CAUSE

WAS DRIVING HOMEON PEARL ROAD AND NOTICED A BRIGHT LIGHT IN THE SKY. IT BRIGHT SO I TOOK NOTICE, I AT FIRST THOUGHT THAT IS A PLANE, THERE WAS 2 AIR CRAFT IN THE SKY IN THE DISTANCE, YOU COULD SEE THEM AND THERE TRAILS, IT SEEMED NOT TO BE NEAR THEM....THE SUN WAS LOW BUT NOT YET SET,THE MOON WAS OUT.... I THOUGHT IT WAS THE SUN REFLECTING OFF THE SDE OF A PLANE... I THOUGHT IT WAS ONE......I THOUGHT FOR SURE I WAS GOING TO SEE ONE ....... I SAID TO MY SELF THATS A PLANE ISNT IT?......... AND THEN IT WAS GONE.......... THERE WAS NOTHING THERE , BUT THE 2 PLANES THE MOON AND SUN........ I WOULD THINK NOTHING OF IT BUT THE FACT THAT IT WAS A PERFECTLY CLEAR SKY THERE WERE NO CLOUDS.... IT WAS THERE THEN THERE WAS NOTHING.... I WAS SO SURE I WAS GOING TO SEE AN AIR CRAFT...... OK MAYBE IT WAS BALL LIGHTING BUT IT SURE WAS STRANGE......ARE THERE ANY OTHER REPORTS? PARMA HTS OHIO NOV 16 2012.
