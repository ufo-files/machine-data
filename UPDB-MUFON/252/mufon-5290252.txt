single light in sky during sunset period

I was driving home from work, west bound on highway 20, approaching the Saint-Anne's bridge, when a single white light low the sky to the west, grabbed my attention in the sky. There were no clouds and the sun was starting to set.  For the next 15 minutes or so, I continued to observe it. It appeared to remain in the same spot in the sky. I pulled off the road and parked behind the Pharma Prix drug store and took two photos with my cell phone. As I watched the object appeared to slowly possibly change in shape, but it was too far away from me to tell.  I then decided to leave for home, so cannot say how long it remained in the sky.
