I believe it came from the R.A.F Northolt that is a mile away on flight path

I had not long retied from the local council, so I decided to buy a telescope and take up astronomy as a pastime. And on said date, I was outside viewing North while smoking. After which I turned round and could see a light like a jar of honey glistening in sunlight.To my amazement a second light came into view over my bungalow.Still not able to see the third light. The shape of a triangle came apparent. I moved toward the back door for protection and the looked back to the sky to now see the object in its full. It was moving a at rate that is consistence with other known planes taking off from R.A.F. Northolt. After calming down, and putting what I see in to perspective. 

The weather was a still moonless night, no road noise,or animal noises, And I do not have much light pollution. I did notice that there was no emergency sirens in pursuit after noticing that it was on an exact flight path as other plains that take off from their and head over my bungalow. I would put it as the same size as an R.A.F Herules, after one came over, on the same flight path, the next day. I would say both objects were of the same size, same height from ground, and both going about the same speed. If you take the noise from the Hercules out off the equation, and adjust for the lake of noise from the triangle. They had to come out off the same place, or it flew right over it.

I must add that without the three light, I would not of seen it, as it was a black as the night sky behind it.
