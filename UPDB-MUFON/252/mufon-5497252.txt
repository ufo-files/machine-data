u-shaped / horseshoe-shaped light in the sky

On 12th December 2014 I was walking my son to school at 8.55am (Belfast Time). I looked up to the sky (North/North-East) and seen a U-Shaped / Horseshoe-Shaped light in the sky, it stayed there for about 60 - 90 seconds, I didn't have my phone on me to take a picture and when I turned round to say to another parent it was gone... 
I came straight home and contact Northern Ireland UFO Society and reported what I seen, Shonie Owens the person i was dealing with, checked to see if anything was in the sky at the time and she came back with nothing
