Very bright and quiet

I had taken the dogs outside to do their business, and my Olde English Bulldogge, who has only barked twice in a year and is a VERY quiet dog, started to whimper and try to hide behind me, staring off to the north. I look and there, at about approximately 2 Oclock position in the northern sky, is a VERY bright white light, with about 4 red lights coming off from it. I thought it was a plane but after watching it for about 7 minutes, I realized it was just hanging there. I took 3 steps forward towards it, and it started to move towards me. Roughly 11 minutes after I initially spotted it, it flashed REALLY brightly, all the lights, and took off straight towards me, heading south.
