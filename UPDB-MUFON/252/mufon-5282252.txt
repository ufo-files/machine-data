Moving pretty fast

I live about 10 miles from South Bend International airport, I heard a commercial plane flying over as they often do but it seemed a little lower than usual, I was standing at my dining room window so I bent down so I could look up higher. I did not see the plane, so I stood up and was looking north, my property in the back is a little over 1 acre and the north end of my land has quite a few large trees. I caught something in the corner of my left eye then I saw it and it was moving fast and I heard nothing, it was behind all the trees (no leaves on them now) and I'm judging it was about a quarter mi to a half mi away and guessing about 800 to 1000 ft off the ground. I was kind of shocked and stepped back and asked myself, what the hell did I just see and at that instant, I thought this is what the Navy pilots saw, I ran outside and looked to the northeast, and there it was, but it was at least 2 mi away then shortly it was out of sight.
