{jpg} 4:30 am, saw bright yellow crescent moon in NE - but it wasn't moon?

I saw in PA what was seen in Port Orchard WA 12/5. I was up early Mon or Tues morning and glanced out living room window saw bright yellow orange SKINNY crescent bordering a dark round object against black sky. I thought it was the moon but yellow crescent was going around the bottom and the left side of the dark circle, not the sides of the moon. It was moon sized and visible through bare tree branches and obviously some distance behind. On second look it had moved to the right (but more than the moon would have in 15 minutes). I looked for it again and it was no longer in the sky around 5:00 am EST. I could see grey clouds where it used to be but the object was gone. Where did it go if it WAS the moon? I forgot it until the Port Orchard account and photo. What they and I saw this week reminds me of an object I saw last spring further away in the sky more to the East. It appeared at first to be a yellow star against black sky which caught my eye. Through binoculars it was a very small flat, round craft with windows around the side. It was at least a mile off in the distance, about 45 degrees from the horizon. With binoculars I could see three yellow windows: a floor-to-ceiling window in the center and a partial on either side as they went around the craft, the top was dark grey. Could not see all because of the deck over my deck blocking view. It was Mar 2018 and too cold to go out on deck in pjs. I submitted #90888 that night and believe there is a connection between what is reported in #90888 and the windowed craft. The photo from Port Orchard appears to be a flat craft with yellow sides. The object I saw in March was slightly tilted to right and did not resemble the moon.
