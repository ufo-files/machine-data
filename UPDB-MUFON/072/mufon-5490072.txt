While driving home a turquoise ball passed above and to the left we where travelling approx 30mph the ball slightly faster at 40-50mph

After visiting relatives and heading home at 8.10pm we turned right out of the minor street onto the main road as I looked in the review mirror to check no oncoming faster vehicles where approaching I saw a blue/green/turquoise ball of light behind and slightly to the left approx 30-50 feet away and 50-100 feet above , when we got up to speed we was travelling at 30mph the object passed us at approx 40-50mph, I had to slow down to 20mph for a roundabout junction, once the junction was cleared I accelerated up to 40mph the object was now in front and to the left at the same distance left of the road and at same height, I could see the object clearly in the top left of my windscreen it had traveled in a straight line at same height and speed the whole time we could see it , after approx 1/2 mile the object passed over a tree line and we lost sight of object it took approx 5-10 seconds for us to pass the tree line in the car and when we did the object was gone , when I first saw the object I didn't know what it could be , during the sighting I experienced wonder and excitement, after we got home 5. Mins later I just felt I'd seen something strange and put it down yo ball lightening although it was a dry partly cloudy evening with no sign of thunder or lightening
