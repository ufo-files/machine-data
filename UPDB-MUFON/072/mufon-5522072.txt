Looked like a disc with orangish red square lights (4) . Each light went out then it seemed to just disappear

We were on top of our 5 story sand hill watching the meteor shower when someone with said whats that bright light? as I turned I saw 4 Orange-red square lights over what seemed to South Augusta GA. The craft seemed huge and from left to right the lights went out one by one. Never saw a "craft" just lights lasting for 30 sec. or so. It seemed to be a couple of miles away, as we live close to the SC GA border, right over the tree tops. I have never seen anything like this and I know it is explainable. We all were stunned and scared and did not finish our relaxing watching the meteor shower as we had planned....
