{jpg} Orb appeared dim and brightness grew quickly then it traveled very fast and changed direction several times then phased out (recurring event)

Our house is in an area of Massachusetts close to what is known as the Bridgwater Triangle. My kids mother and I are very dedicated to our research of UFOS and E.T. life. Approximately twice a week, outside our house, we can see on the horizon about a mile away, a glowing ball of light, it appears as a star at first. It hovers then begins traveling in a linear path. It will stop and it will become extremely bright before changing direction and traveling very fast in another direction. After doing this for several minutes it will fade away. As I stated earlier, this happens in the same area (over a power plant in a neighboring town), several times a week. It is not an aircraft or any type of drone that I know of.
