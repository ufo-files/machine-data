{nd} Twe equal lights flying slow in a straight line and stopped fast ascension straight up. Two lights dimmed due to increased distance till gone.

Two equal lights flying slow for 1 minute from the northwest in a straight line one after the other about 500 feet apart. Stopped hovered for 5 seconds and then two lights ascended apart. After that the second second light (bottom) formed into the first one (top) into one light and then shoot straight up. The light dimmed due to increased distance till gone.
