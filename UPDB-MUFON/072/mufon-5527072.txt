intense bright light

i woke up around 4 am with a glimpse of an intense glimmering light a few miles away. i got up and stared at it to see if it was a plane or other aircraft. but it had no flashing lights and it hovered in one position. thinking it might have been nothing, i went back to bed. i woke up around 45 minutes later to the same thing in the same spot. i've woken up at 4 am multiple nights since, and have yet seen it again. highly unlikely that it was a planet, or star. most definitely an unidentified flying object.
