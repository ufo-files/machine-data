Saw UFO hoovering in northeast Gainesville

Around 10pm on Jan 10, 2021 me and my wife witnessed a craft emerge just above the the treeline south of the [exact location details have been moved to Investigators report section/cms/LLL]. The airport was nearby so we initially thought maybe something getting ready to land. We were heading Southeast on 53rd towards hwy 441. As we got closer to the craft we noticed that is was hovering very low just above the treeline and it was too far away from the airport to be landing. It was moving extremely slow as it crossed over 53rd. I looked for indication that it might be a helicopter but there was no sound and didn't appear to be a helicopter or anything I have ever witnessed. We didn't observe any blinking lights on this object at all. It had at least 4 sets of lights beaming. I couldn't make out the pattern of the lights. As it crossed over 53rd at main street intersection light it began to decend down into the trees. As it did this we noticed a line of lights that were on the craft multiple colors red, green, orange, yellowish, more but it was too quick and we couldn't see everything. We then made a left on main street and headed south to see if we could get a glimpse of it but it was gone.
