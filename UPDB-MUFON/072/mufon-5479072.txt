{rjl} Bright light that had 5 smaller orbs within it.

I noticed a bright light. Thought it was a star or planet, but then it started flashing and then started moving more to my right. Thought it was a satellite, but thought it was too large to be one. I got my iphone out and zoomed in on it. I saw a big orb with 5 little orbs inside. I tried to take a video but it was too far out there to zoom in on for a video. I kept looking at it through a zoomed picture and it kept breaking up into 5 smaller orbs/3/2 and then reassembled itself into one big orb. I then noticed an orb by it detach itself and then sporadically went left of the orb and then vanished. The object was in my sight for about 2 hours and then slowly vanished behind the treeline of my house.
