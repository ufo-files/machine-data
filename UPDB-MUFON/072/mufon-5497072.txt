{dr}5 UFO's traveling South to North

Our family room and couch faces South, On Saturday Nov. 27, 2014 at 8:30PM while sitting on our couch watching TV I see a big solid round red light over the house behind us. I often see planes on approach to Cuyahoga Country Airport but those are heading East to West and the red aircraft lights are flashing red and MUCH smaller than what I was seeing. I had no idea what these were as I'd never seen anything in the sky lighted this large. I got up and walked to our sliding patio door for closer look, I then see another one coming approximately 200 yards following the exact same South to North route, I immediately go outside to watch them, as they're almost overhead I look to the South and see THREE MORE following the exact same route and spacing, I yell for my wife to come out and she see's the last 3 as they silently went overhead and to the North following the exact same route. I run back inside, grab the phone then go back out and call 911, I explain what I saw and they said they had no reports or explanation. They were always in level flight and I'd guess between 500-2,000 feet in elevation. To approximate the size I'd say if an aircraft marker light looks the size of a BB these would be the size of a quarter. We watched them all until they went out of sight over the house's and tree's across the street to our North. I'm not saying these were aliens but would love to know what they were, definitely NOT a plane or helicopter! I'm only sorry that in the excitement of seeing this I never thought to grab my iPod from it's charger to take a video of this.
