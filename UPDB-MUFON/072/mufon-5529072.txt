Multiple UFOs and man-made aircrafts

I was in the back seat of our car, coming from Merrillville to Michigan City Indiana. It was about 6:45 PM and the stars were out. I was looking outside of the window and saw a couple of star-like lights that were moving and flashing. I saw three airplanes in the same air space in parallel lines. Then I saw a very bright thing that looked like a star, at first I thought it was the north star, but it was three times the size of the other stars so I thought that it was a planet, then I realized the it was moving real slowly upwards. It seemed like it was getting closer. I looked at the other lights around it and saw three other starlike moving objects around it as well. Then as the large starlike object came closer, I saw other lights on the object. The lights were red, green, blue, white, and yellow/orange in a triagular shape. As it got closer, I think I saw a triangular shape but I can't be sure. It definitely was not any aircraft that I know of, not an airplane or a helicopter or a jet. It also moved as if it were floating/hovering. After about 10 mins from seeing it, I lost sight of it. The drive from Merrillville to Michigan City took about 30 minutes and during that time I saw a lot of weird moving lights that looked like stars all of them changed and flashed colors atleast one point or another and they also moved in a way that planes cannot possibly move. All in all I believe I saw 8 unidentified flying objects and 6 planes.
