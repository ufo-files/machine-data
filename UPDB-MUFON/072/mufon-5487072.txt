{cak} Two orange bright lights, low, fast, no sound

My husband and I were outside laying in our lounge chairs watching the meteor shower. My husband said look - there is the ISS. I said it can't be because it is too low and too bright. We then notice there were two lights with one following the other. Both were the same bright orange solid color. The lights were extremely bright and no flashing. They moved quickly and passed right by us. I could not see any structure other than the lights and absolutely no sound. We first noticed the lights coming from the southwest and passed by going due north in a straight line with no turns. We probably observed the lights about 45 seconds.
