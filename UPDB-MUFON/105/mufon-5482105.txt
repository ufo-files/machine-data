{rjl} I took a series of photos on the beach looking south.

At a cookout on the beach, Assateague Island. Facing South hour or so after sunset. Mars, Saturn, and Antares form a loose triangle near the middle of the photo, lots of light pollution from the moon and it was still early. Ocean, beach and some cars and campers’ lights in the distance at the bottom of the photo. In the upper right of the photo is the anomaly. I am submitting 2 photos with a blowup of one. These photos were taken ten minutes showing the anomaly in different parts of the sky. I have additional photos as well.
