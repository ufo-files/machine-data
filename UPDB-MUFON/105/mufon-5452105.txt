What did I just see..

Was outback with my wife last night in Jacksonville Fl. Had just set a bunch of blankets on the patio chairs to do some stargazing. The temperature in Jax was beautiful high 60s so it was cooler than normal for this trimester of year. Anyway we got settled in and within 15 minutes my wife was out cold. So I stayed up watched a couple of planes fly by. A couple of Satellites we’re visible which in June in Florida is rare due to the humidity at night but again it was a gorgeous cool night. Then what I thought was the international space station came over the house.i got so excited! I was able to track it from my 2:00 position to about 10:00. It was brighter and bigger then the ISS which I thought was weird. I tired waking my wife up to no avail. The size of the bright object would be comparable to 3-5x bigger than the ISS flying overhead. I would have estimated the object to be 25k to 30k in elevation. So as it crossed the sky at about my 10:00 position it abruptly did a candy cane like maneuver and shot straight up into space. I was in awe! Stayed up a awhile longer saw some more satellites and headed to bed.
