A work colleague of mine gave me these photos. He set up a WIFI camera in his backyard in a district of Brentwood called Hutton in the south east regions of England. The camera was set up for his daughter's school project, which was set to take a photo every 10 minutes for the entire duration of the year - which would be later compiled as a JPEG slideshow.

I've attached photos from before the light shows up, to after it disappears.
