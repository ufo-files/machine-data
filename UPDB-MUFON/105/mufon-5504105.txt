{rjl} Glowing orb pulsed, changed direction then disappeared.

Went outside because my littlest dog was very upset. Bigger dog was in yard staring at the sky. Looked up because I thought she might be looking at a rat in the chicken coop rafters and saw a large glowing orb in the sky. Thought "is that a plane?" I thought "no way is that a plane" then it went crazy fast & disappeared. I thought "Did that just happen? What did I just see?" Stared at empty sky trying to figure out some explanation for it. Went in and told my kid / texted my spouse. Went back out around 15 min.saw a plane/helecopter(?) at SE area. Bigger dog ran into house and hid under bed & would not come out/shaking. Smaller dog ( more aggressive) was guarding back door afterwards for about 10 min. Would not budge - very not like her. Dogs relaxed after about 30 min. but human witness still freaking about it.
