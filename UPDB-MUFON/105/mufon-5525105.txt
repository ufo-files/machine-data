Red orange glowing balls. Changed speeds and directions

Shortly before 9p.m. got out of my truck and saw two clusters of three with six individual red orange glowing orbs in the sky. They were coming from the north and turned west and elevated past the cloud cover. I ran into the house, got my mom and two nephews to look at what was going on in the sky also. Lasted a good 4-5 mins. They moved at a slow pace but would speed up and slow back down to the same slow pace. Made no sound. Even had time to get binoculars, but couldn't see any more detail. They all took the same path in the sky, coming from the north turning west. I did have time to make a short video, but it is not very good. I didn't think it was even picking up the light, but I will upload it.
