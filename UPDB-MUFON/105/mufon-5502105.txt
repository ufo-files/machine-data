{dr}On back porch looking up at clouds. Saw a perfectly black object moving steadily and in a straight path going north.

Okay. I we were under a tornado warning for our area. So the curious person that i am decided to go outside and observe the sky. There was low cloud cover and the clouds were very dark but in spots some were white. The wind was steady. I was on the back porch looking up towards the north because thats were an apparent tornado was forming. 20 to 30 miles north of me. So as im in awe of the low cloud cover and weird clouds i saw a pitch black spherical object moving pretty steady and moving in a straight line going north. I watched the object for a minute or two then it disappeared into the dark clouds. These clouds were dark and this object was pitch black. No sound. Didnt waver at all even thou the winds were steady to strong. Also i observed a large black bird lower in altitude but the bird was having a hard time flying in the wind. I was in awe. Like time stopped for a minute. Then i saw the bird and realized what i had seen. Time was around 4:10 pm on june 7 2014. What was weird was it was heading straight towards the storm.(tornado) I've heard of ufo's being seen near volcano eruptions. So maybe this is some what of the same thing. Observing the tornado? Whatever it was ive seen nothing like it ever before.
