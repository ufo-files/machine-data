Humming/Buzzing Sound

I live on the Space Coast of Fla, 'n I have seen a lot of strange objects in the sky, usually at night. One afternoon I was sitting in my back yard, taking pics of my Tropical Plants, when I heard a buzzing/humming sound to the North. The sky was overcast, I looked up in the direction and saw a kind of a barrel shape floating. I took some pics w' my digital cam but they didn't seem to register. I also had a 110mm cam which I also shot a pic to the North. I followed the sound toward the East 'n snapped a pic as it went behind some trees. it then was heading South 'n came out into the clearing 'n I snapped another pic. It then kinda leaned forward 'n took off really fast heading West. When I came into my house I tried to download my Digital Cam but it was blank except for the flower pics. My 110mm had a 24 film roll 'n at this time there were 10 snaps. I used the cam over 5 yrs 'n when the roll was used up, I had it developed. I had thought maybe the pics didn't come out like the digital. I was surprised when they looked so clear. As I was watching this thing, I suddenly knew how they move, how they beat gravity. The pics I enclosed were scaned, I have original films.
