UFO picture captured over Sedona

While visiting Sedona Arizona on August 15th, 2009. We were driving in a vehicle waiting to visit a local establishment that was opening at 12:00 PM on Saturday, August 15th. We were early, and because we were in the vicinity we drove up Coffee Pot Drive. Once we maneuvered through the neighborhood, we found ourselves at the base of Coffee Pot rock where I got out of our vehicle, walked down a small path and took a few pictures of the rock formation. I heard no sound, I saw no lights, and there was no breeze. I noted only one small cloud over the Coffee Pot rock formation when the picture was taken. Approximate time of picture 11:55 AM 8/15/09.
