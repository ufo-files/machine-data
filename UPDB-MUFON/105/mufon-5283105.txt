Lost 2 hrs going to work one morning

I was stationed at Camp Pieri in Weisbaden from 1985 to 1988. In October of 1986 I was going to work early one morning and lost 2 hrs. I was late and missed 2 formations. I can’t explain it but something happened to me that morning. I have a 2 hr gap in my mind and it’s really bothered me all these years. I want, need to know what happened to me that morning.
