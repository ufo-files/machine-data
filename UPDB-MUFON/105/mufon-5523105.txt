Bright saucer-shaped object moving south to north.

I was driving west on U.S. Expressway 83 within the city limits of Donna, Texas, when I observed a very bright saucer-shaped object off slightly to my left (southwest of my position). The object was moving on a steady course from south to north. As it moved, it tipped slightly, revealing that it was saucer-shaped. It gave off a steady, bright white light. I continued watching it for about a minute, but while fumbling around for my cell phone to take a photo, the object had vanished when I looked back up.
