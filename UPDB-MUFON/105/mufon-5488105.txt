{cak} Cape Coral FL two whitish orbs chasing each other from North to South direction

I take my dogs out every morning between 5:15 and 5:30 am and while they do their thing, I look up at the stars. I often see meteors and even the ISS and satellites a few times. This morning I looked up and coming from north I see two big glowing whitish orbs zooming through the sky heading south, then they even seemed to be racing each other, with one getting ahead of the other and so on, this went on as they passed over me, a hair east of the big dipper, and kept going south until they were out of my view. They were very, very high and going quite fast. I have no idea what it was, so I found this site in hopes that someone saw the same event. I was terrified by it, but it was amazing to see. I was very startled and just stood out there for a while, hoping it would happen again, but I didn't see them again. I did see a small meteor streak by a few minutes after, but that streak went east to west and those are a common sight to me. I also saw a small airplane flashing, also a common sight. What i saw was NOT common or like anything I have ever witnessed.
