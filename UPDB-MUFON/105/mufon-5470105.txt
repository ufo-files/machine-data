{rjl} orange sphere silent stationary for 18 min then moved towards east vanished

I was walking home around 9:30pm with my girlfriend at the time and as always im looking up at the stars. As we came around the corner i noticed the orange orb just hovering above a 2 story home. Their was no noise it just hovered above the house. At first i thought my eyes were playing tricks on me but thanfully i wasnt alone. I wasnt sure what it was exactly but it was about the size of a small helicopter. It didnt have any features such as other lights, windows, wings, ect. i got so excited due to the fact it was my first sighting that i didnt take my phone out. I wish i would have. Its something ill never forget. As i stood their watching this mysterious orange orb about 19 minutes in it started moving very slowly towards the east and at the 20 min mark it vanished. it made me think of harry potter and his invibility cloak. It was absolutely fascinating.
