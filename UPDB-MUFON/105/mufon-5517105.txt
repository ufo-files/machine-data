{dr}Bright light, mostly white with vibrating, pulsating green and red and yellow.

I went outside late at night, it would be very early morning July 19. I saw a strange light through the trees in the western sky. It was very bright, with strange light vibrations, difficult to make out exactly. It wasnt moving so I ruled out an oncoming airplane. I grabbed my binoculars to get a better view. I could see white light with vibrating, pulsating red green and yellow all over it. It was there for some time. I came out half an hour later and it seemed like it may have been further away. It was gone the next time I came out, or else I couldnt see it behind the trees, I live in a heavily wooded area.
I searched online and saw a news video from Blue Springs Missouri, pretty 
much the same thing that I saw.
