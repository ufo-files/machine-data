Red Blinkers, White blinkers, and high alt fast movers

Every clear night since noticing the first anomaly (3 weeks now) we see what we first thought were planes. However we are not in a flight pattern, have no planes during the day, and these are silent. Some blink just white, others are red/white, some are near, some a few miles away. We cannot make out any discernible shapes. We then thought maybe drones, but these travel over 10 miles. Last night we saw around 20 in an hour. Additionally we see what may be satellites, some of those blink on/off at a rate of .2Hz, others are steady. These could be out of atmosphere but difficult to tell. We have seen upward of 12 of these in an hour going in every direction.
