{jpg} Object moving clouds, changing directions

At around 11 PM on a partly cloudy night, I noticed the object moving from above one cloud to another, in a straight line. It was a steady white light, and did not appear to be a jet. However, I thought it had to be and continued to watch it while talking with a friend. When the object did not come out from behind the second cloud, I noticed. Then I saw the object move back from the second cloud to the first one in what appeared to be a 180 degree turn. It did not come out from behind the first cloud. I told my companion about it, and when we looked again, we saw it had moved to a totally separate area of the night sky, at about a right angle to the original direction. It then moved behind a third cloud. We watched for about 15-20 minutes. It did not re-emerge.
