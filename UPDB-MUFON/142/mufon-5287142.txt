Cluster of unknown objects

I was driving to work, westbound on highway 60 when I could see a cluster of objects swirling about 2-300ft above the ground and about 1 1/2 miles further west. I exited on Higley rd northbound and at the traffic light on southern ave I was able to take a video. My first thought was it was maybe a flock of birds but I didn’t notice any wing flapping which would be required for the maneuvers they were performing. They also were white and reflecting light. Id never seen a white bird species in the area in the 4 years I lived there. There’s definitely no gulls in the desert. My camera was having a hard time focusing on them because of the distance and my window being up. I also don’t think they were balloons because they were changing direction from moving north to south and then back to south. They remain unidentified to me
