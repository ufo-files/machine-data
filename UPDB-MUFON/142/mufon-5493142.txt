The even took place over a 2 month period during February and March of 2015. It appeared over many nights in a row. So many I did not even bother to photograph it every time. The object was in the southwest sky every time it appeared. It was a orb like object that changed colors from blue to green to yellow to orange. When we photographed the orb it appeared to have tracers especially when you zoom in on the picture. It made no sound and moved in all directions. I know for a fact that the object was moving because there are power lines in front of the apartment and the object would move above and below the power lines. There was no wind that was blowing that would have moved the power lines. The orb would just vanish out of thin air. The orb was witnessed by my fianc1'. We also witnessed the same orb like object in a town 60 miles south of Lubbock. It was also in the southwestern skies.
