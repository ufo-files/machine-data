Observed a slow moving round bright yellowish object

The object appeared above highly populated area in NW of Sydney. It was flying in a steady motion. No indication of red or green aircraft wing blinking lights, no noise, no erratic movements. I started to monitor from 30 degree angle, its was larger than stars and colour has caught my attention. It was not satellite or ISS. It was much larger and closer to earth, and it did not disappear over the horizon.

I initially thought it is an aircraft or drone with a front projector turned on. When it reached me in angle view of 80-90 degree, its lighting somewhat become flickering, intensity dropped and then completely disappeared in a split second. There was no object right after that moment to observe.

I'm an experienced observer. This was not usual object. The Sky was clear at the time of observation.
