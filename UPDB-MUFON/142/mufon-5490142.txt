530 am went out side to go to work

530 am went out side to go to work. as usual I went to the side walk and looked around to make sure every things ok with the house. I remember I lit a cigarette and looked up at the front porch and that's when I saw glowing red ball. about the size of a softball. I walked slowly towards it.I started up the fire escape at the side of the house. as I slowly went up I could see it plane as day. it was about the size of a basket ball but see through. the soft ball size ball was glowing bright red. I was maybe eight or nine feet away from it. as I was looking at it the red ball stopped glowing and the thing was gone at a tremendous rate of speed.that was the last time that I saw it.
