{cak} Very long, straight line of 12-15 white lights

I was taking a walk with my young son and he looks up and says, "Daddy, there's Santa! Or maybe a bunch of gnome planes?" I looked up and there was a very long row of white lights moving across the sky, west to east. Best estimate, it was several hundred feet long and as it moved away from us, it appeared more to be a single structure with lights attached. It looked whitish in color and made no noise whatsoever. Neither my son nor I had any guess as to what it could have been. It began to head on a more southern path and then we were unable to see it anymore. I attempted to video it on my cell phone but i wasn't able to get it fully in the frame and when I tried zooming in, I missed it completely. Maybe someone else saw the same thing?
