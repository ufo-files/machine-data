{rjl} UFO lights over NJ Turnpike Pennsylvania extension

Last night my 20 year old son and I were driving him back to his school from spring break. We were on the Pennsylvania Turnpike extension, the road between exit 6 of the New Jersey Turnpike and the Delaware River Turnpike toll bridge. About two miles before the bridge, my son noticed to our left, red and blue lights in the sky and asked if I saw them too. When I looked up about 20 to 30 orbs with several red or blue lights surrounding them were lined up in a triangle formation. They appeared to be following a plane that was descending, not sure which airport it was headed for. The triangle shape looked massive compared to the plane. At first I thought the lights were the outline of a large flying object but my son saw them fly into this formation so it appears these lights were individual crafts of some type. We were driving and I got nervous and told him to keep driving. Now I wished we had pulled over to the side of the road to get a better look. I called the Florence Township police but no one has reported seeing this last night.
