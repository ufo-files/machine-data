This happened in July 1960. I was 8 years old. This was about five miles south of Henderson. From my open window I saw a flaming ball that had a flowing trail. It was in middle of night. Came from East going West. It was going slow and remained at the same height. There was no sound at all. I followed it until it went past the trees and hills. I have never reported this because at that time there was no one to report to nor have did I talk to any adults at that time, including my parents.
