{td} Triangle UFO

I was asleep having a weird dream about something wanting my teeth so I awoke terrified. My husband was awake and before I could say anything I had this overwhelming feeling to go outside. It was between midnight and one. I live out in the country and it can get pretty dark. Last night was clear, many stars in the sky. I am a huge UFO watcher and I usually go outside and I see things from time to time. However, last night I looked up in the left hand side, over the thick tree line and I noticed a couple of stars had vanished. I was mesmerized but only saw it for about forty seconds til I was overcome with an intense fear and I ran inside. I was shaking and speechless.
