Three flying objects, 1 larger than the others.

I saw three UFOs: one larger (apparent size of a golf ball held at arm's length); two smaller ones (apparent size of a penny held at arm's length). The smaller ones had some protruding objects coming from what seemed to be the front. These protruding objects were glowing blue. A glowing blue dome was at the very top of both of the small ones. There was a straight horizontal line of black dots around the middle of the two smaller objects. 

The bigger one had some things on the bottom that was black, which looked like viewports. There appeared to be nothing on the surface really other than those. 

They seemed to be moving from the South to the South East through the sky, then stopped. View ports or what looked like viewing boxes, or maybe even turrets, turned towards me. The bigger object had glowing blue things with a lighter glowing blue ball which shot a glowing blue beam that hit the domes at the top of the smaller ones, and then all three objects vanished from sight, apparently without having moved.
