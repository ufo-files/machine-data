Object sighted in clouds when reviewing photographs taken after a hike.

Hiking in the Black Mountains on 25th September 2021 on the England/Wales border, 3 miles outside Hay-on-Wye near the foot of Hay Bluff. Stopped to take a few pictures on my Samsung A32 phone.
Reviewing the pics today on my PC, spotted a dark ovular object in a gap in the clouds at 10 o'clock. 
If you use the What3Words app for geolocations, my friend and I were standing at or close to eruptions.steamed.tenders.
The camera was aiming in a west to north west direction.
We didn't see the object at the time - just noticed it this morning.
