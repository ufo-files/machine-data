It was late summer 1997 incline village Nevada my girlfriend and I were sitting in my truck at the beach facing south watching the waves it was about 9PM it was a very clear sky that night and we had noticed unusually high aircraft traffic usually we see maybe 1 plane over lake tahoe but this night maybe 10 helicopters and All types of planes. They all seemed to be headed in the same direction "south east" we then noticed in the south eastern direction approximately 25 miles away at the south shore a bright almost lightning type flashing light in white then green turning yellow then orange/red all within 15 to 20 seconds then it glowed the red/orange color for about 1 minute it looked like it could be a wild fire but it just turned off like a light switch stunned at what we saw trying to make sense of it all we we watched even more helicopters heading in that direction we thought maybe they were fighting a wild fire but it was gone about2 minutes go my and we see it starting again just over the ridge but this time closer maybe 15 to 20 miles towards the south east. Same thing white lightning turning green and yellow and orange red. Looked like lightning and a forest fire but no clouds around anywhere bright clear stars in all directions. after 2 minutes of a burning look it turned off again. Now even more excited and confused trying to come up with a reason for this it couldn't be lightning or fireworks there is limited to no access to this area of the mountains that we are watching it seemed to be moving north along the mountain ridge about 4 or 5 minutes later we see it all over again but closer maybe 10 miles south east of us I was so intrigued by this I had to see what it was we then left the beach in my pickup truck and headed up hwy 431 my rose hwy. At the top there is a big meadow and on the other side of the mountain range you can see Reno NV lights and the valley to Carson city NV. I thought that would be a good vantage point when we got to the meadow there was alot of other vehicles there pulled off the side of the hwy looking for it to. I went past the meadow and to the turnout on the east slope to see if I could see anything but nothing so we went back to the meadow and pulled off to the south side of the hwy facing south we can see a pickup truck had drove way out into the meadow to see if he can see anything. No roads out there he was driving straight through bushes the two passengers stepped out of the truck and walked south in front of there headlights leaving both doors open and the dome light on at that moment just over the hill in front of them the lights started. Lightning like very intense changing colors and again looking like a massive forest fire maybe 200 to 300 feet across now only about 2,000 feet in front of us. My girlfriend sitting next to me started to cry and beg me to leave. leave now please get us out of here fast.at that moment I felt a huge rush of electricity I looked over and her long waist length hair was all up against the roof of the truck her still screaming it felt like something just entered my truck I felt a pressure like someone heavy sat on my lap and leaned back hard I panicked and started to drive off as fast as I could there was a buzzing sound in the truck as we drove off. looking in the mirror the fireball got closer but I went over the hill out of sight before I could make out what it was as i passed 10 to 15 other vehicles with people watching the same thing from the side of the road some standing outside there cars as soon as we got out of the meadow the presence left the truck instantly but our hair was still standing up I quickly drove to my mom and dad house only about 15 minutes from the meadow my aunt and uncle were there visiting we had told them what happened they thought we were crazy at first but we convinced them to come out to the truck and feel what was in it if you stick your arm in the truck the hair would stand up as soon as you pulled it out it would go down. That was enough to peak there interest we took there van back to the meadow where nothing was going on anymore it had past but still alot of cars on the side of the highway but no people were around. We went home the next day my girlfriend and I went up in the morning early to see if we could see anything on the light. The only thing we saw were 10 ot 15 tow trucks from all around towing off all the cars from the night before including the pickup truck in the meadow. all there at the same time. Tow trucks from Carson reno truckee and lake tahoe all different companies.as a kid I rode dirt bikes all over this mountain I knew all the trails very well there was nothing in this area very remote. Two weeks later about 8 miles west of the meadow I went camping with a group of friends in the morning we noticed twin prop helicopters logging all over the area between our campsite and the meadow I started telling my freinds what happened in the meadow and we went to investigate the helicopter action alot of the trails were closed down for no reason when we got close we saw a new graveled road 2 lanes wide ther was nothing like it in the area so we followed it toward the meadow we found 5 out of state forest service trucks and big rigs with dozers from a logging company from Oregon the helicopters were flying out logs all day we kept going east on the new road until it got to a wire fence with barb wire the gate said guarded by mp we drove our jeep along the fence to see if we could see anything but it went on for miles. This all went up in 2 weeks the road fence and logging a massive operation we never see logging in the lake tahoe basin we tried raming the gates and they opened about 1/4mile onto the gated area we were chased out by a little green jeep with 2 soldiers in it we were able to out run there jeep heading north on there new 2 lane road in a highly protected wildlife area very odd we followed it to truckee CA. a very long way to get there about half way down this road that wasn't there 2 weeks ago we saw 4 houses on our right some kind of pop up instant houses used by military they looked like the sea container houses that fold out all set up like a new neighborhood with more military jeeps parked in front soon after this day everything was gated and had security mp over the next year all of our trails we used in the area were closed not just gate closed but 6 foot around trees were cut down across all trails and boulders placed in the trails not just the entrances but the whole thing miles of trails and forest service roads gone
