Saw through window

I was probably around 7 or 8 years old (I am 25 now). I was visiting my Grandparents' house during the summer. It was at night and I was in the bed with my Grandmother. I remember not being about to sleep because of a loud thunderstorm that night. From the side of the bed I was on, you can see straight through her bathroom. The bathroom has a window that you can see from the bed. The thunderstorm was doing its thing and lightning flashed lighting up everything, and at that moment I saw what looked like an oddly shaped head trying to peer through the window. The blinds were shut on the window so I couldn't see exactly what it was but I could tell something was trying to look through the window. What you need to know is that this window is probably 7 to 9 feet off the ground so this thing had to either be extremely tall, been able to jump up and hang onto the ledge under the window, or it got on another one's back to look in. From others' description of their heads, Grays seem to be likely.
