{cak} Glowing copper color ufo on north side of fm185 then a triangular shape ufo on the south side of road. It hovers for 10 minutes then shot straight up

We were driving home headed east on fm 185 towards shins spring rd.
One ufo on north side of fm 185 it was a copper glowing oval shaped craft at first believed to be a tower as it was the color of a street light. It was just higher than the nearby water tower so I knew it was not a streetlight. My 10 yo old son and I were fixated on the ufo when I tell him to grab his phone and record it, as soon as I say that it took off at a high rate of speed straight up into the sky and vanished. As we looked in awe I then noticed a second craft of triangle shape on the south side of fm185 at the same height, we did not see it originally as we were fixated on the first ufo. My son I don’t believe saw it and I did not point it out as he was terrified when the first one shot off into the sky. He said it had to be a plane or star until he saw it zip off straight up. I knew it was something unlike I have ever seen but I was not afraid, I felt very calm and a peacefulness all throughout the experience.
