Bright white object

We were driving south on Interstate 75 just south of Valdosta, Georgia. It was around 7:25 at night, very dark but very clear skies. Suddenly, directly in front of the car, at about a 45 degree angle, appeared a bright white object shooting diagonally downward across the sky towards the west. At first, I thought it was obviously a meteor. I pointed my finger upward to notify my wife of the object. She was already looking at it. As the object moved, it suddenly made an abrupt "turn" upward and to the right. Just after that, it disappeared. There was no smoke trail to it (as usually seen with a meteor). The object appeared to be many miles away. Nevertheless, if you hold your fingers in front of you about a foot apart - that would describe the distance the object traversed across the sky.
