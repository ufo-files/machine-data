Sort of coffee bean/copper brown collor. Just sat in one place and then split into a formation. About six in a line about 10-20 feet apart. just stayed in one area and then clouds covered them. I have video footage.

I went out in the back yard and looked up (like I always do) and saw a small brownish copper object. I quickly noticed it wasn't moving, it was just sitting in one spot. After about one min. I ran inside the house and grabbed my camera. When I came back outside I began capture it on video. During the time I was grabbing the camera and got back outside there were two and then they just sat there and slowly formed a line and I didn't actually see any movement but they strangely increased in numbers approx. 6. Then I ran inside and looked and looked for my binoculars (no luck:( though) and when I returned the clouds were starting to cover them And I never saw them again :(
