{rjl} Last day of Harvest Moon/Picture shows what we saw

My husband and I are not telling our name as we don't want any repercussions from this report/sighting. We went outside to look at Harvest moon on the last day. We looked at this object with naked eye and with a telescope as it stayed stationary for 10-15 mins. It is and remained a orange type circle. I then watched as it moved from the East by 108 area til it disappeared over the Vinton area. My husband does not speak of these things but I felt a need to report it. I have included a photo(clearest one I had as I was nervous and shaky).
