I was on the front porch of the house, sitting on the stairs. It was a clear night, not a cloud in the sky. I was looking up at the stars, just gazing when I noticed an object moving from the south. It'd didn't have a sound, so I knew it wasn't a helicopter, nor did it have blinking lights like a plane. Also, from the height it was, it appeared to be adjusting speeds to much to be any plane I know of. It was a round object, and from the distance, appeared small in overall size. It didn't have a tail, no wings, no cloud like stream like you would see from a jet. Nothing I had ever seen before. I continued to just sit there, on the stairs, just watching it; wondering what it was. After a couple seconds or so, it just stopped in mid air, and hovered. The object then suddenly came west for about 3 seconds or so, moving slowly, the just stopped again. I was amazed bye what I was seeing. Here I am, a skeptic at the time, and seeing this; I was left speechless.  I hate to use this analogy, but it was like a car crash, I just couldn't look away. The object then moves north for a few seconds, and then just hovers again. After a few more seconds of hovering, it (well bye this time, I'm assuming they [referring to the pilot\s]) moves east very quickly this time. Then after a few more seconds of havering, it starts rising slowly, and abruptly shoots off into the sky. I have never seen anything like it in my life. The ext day, there were military helicopters flying over our property. This of course is nothing new to me, because I had seen them fly over our area my entire life. However, it would be 1 or 2 times every once in a while. A formation every now and then. but what struck me as odd was that they flew the majority of the day, and not one flight was a single air craft. Looked like smaller helicopters, plus a couple Chinooks, and an attack helicopter.
