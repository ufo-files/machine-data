{td} Bright light floated over ridge, stopping over house for several minutes before moving ! Watched moving up adjoining ridge for twenty minutes, then it crossed over ridge !!!

Saw the object in the fall of 1976 !!! My cousin and I had just come back from a drive in my new car, a 1976 Monte Carlo !!! We pulled up in the driveway of his parents home on a rural
road, called Dempsey Mt. Road, in Fayette Co., WV !!! The night was dark cloudy, no stars 
Could be seen !!! As we sat in the car, we suddenly noticed a light that came floating over the
ridge and tree line behind their house !!! We instantly got of the car to watch it !!! The bright light right floated over the house and stopped !!! We were overwhelmed and started yelling !!!
I remember that when it stopped I felt the hair on the back of my head stand up !!! My Aunt and and younger cousin came out of the house to find out what we were yelling about !!! I
Can still hear my aunt still screaming what the h-ll is that !!! As we watch in awe at the for
Several minutes it started to move across the tree tops, across the holler, then zinged and
up the adjacent ridge for about another ten minutes or so before topping the ridge and 
disappearing !!!
We were all four stunned !!!
The object had no sound !!!! Could not make out a shape !!! Just a bright eri light that put off a glow of sorts !!! 
I have only told a few people about this !!! A few gave me a hard time !!! But I know what I saw that evening !!!
My cousins and I have spoken about is a few times !!!
