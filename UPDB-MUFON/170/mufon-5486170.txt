I want to let you all know I saw an orb fly over Richardson, tx. On December 23rd at 6:25-6:30pm I observed a bright orb while out taking my dog for a walk in my neighborhood. It came from the NW traveling SE. I saw it about 10 days or so ago just after dusk. Wasn't a plane. Wasn't a satellite. Wasn't a helicopter. There were planes in the area on approach to the various Dallas airports. It was higher than any aircraft in the area but lower than any satellites that might be observed. In the 3-4 mins I observed the orb it traveled from north Dallas to the south east and completely out of sight. It traveled in a straight path at a constant speed with no sound. I took a picture of the orb over head and shot a brief video. It was hard to see the orb on the video as the iPhone is limited and it was moving at a relative high rate of speed.
