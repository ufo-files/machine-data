{dr}4-5 orbs flashing into view, travelling a straight path, then dissapear

My roommate and I were standing on our second floor balcony when I noticed a light to the south flash slight and becoming fully visible. Jokingly, I said to my roommate "hey, look a ufo" (we were watching Independence Day and stepped outside during a commercial to see if we could see any fireworks)as it began moving from directly south of us at a heading of about 020 about the same speed a low flying aircraft. After about 20 seconds another orb light appeared in the same spot and traveled the same flight path as the first object. When the third appeared in the same manner the first object appeared to gain altitude and disappear. When the second object reached the same point where the first object disappeared it did the same thing. We saw five objects total, equally spaced, moving at the same speeds, and all appearing and disappearing at the same points. these were not conventional aircraft (I work in the aviation field), they were not fireworks ( from our perspective the objects traveled several miles across the sky), not Chinese Lanterns as they were moving much too fast.
