Driving alone down Highway 1082 around 8:20 PM on 5 / 25 / 17, approaching Pat O'Brien Road intersection. Two bright amber colored "dots", one next to other ( estimated 20 - 30 feet between "dots" )in steady hover--dangerously near high voltage power line structure, caught my attention. I actually brought my car to a complete stop on the highway to get a better look. My first thought was possibly they were private owned drones--piloted by carelessly stupid people. I took a look around ground area, looking for drone pilots; saw nobody around. Had another brief look at the amber dots: Steady amber glow; no flashing colored lights. Both seemed,( using nearby power line structure to estimate size ), to be size comparable to a large motorcycle or sub compact car. If these objects were drones--they were expensive ones--the kind that had to make some sound. Rolled window down and heard nothing. Approaching traffic caused me to move on. I tried later to rationally speculate what I saw--and I can not say for sure what I saw.
