{rjl} Two shiny diamond and cylinder shaped objects captured during August 21st solar eclipse

I was going through some old photos and videos on my phone. I was looking through all the footage that I took during the solar eclipse on August 21st, 2017 and noticed 2 speeding objects in one of the videos. During that time, I was at a park in Arlington, Texas called B.C. Barnes Park. I was their with my girlfriend to witness the partial solar eclipse. I decided to take several photos and videos of the event and surrounding. In one video taken at 1:03 pm, I seemed to have captured two objects that both appear to be of a solid dark color and also appear to reflect light at certain occasions. I don1t recall seeing or noticing these objects with my own eyes. These objects appeared for less than a second shooting across the sky at high speeds in a straight line. The diamond shaped object appeared to be from the north when entering in video frame then last seen moving southeast when leaving the video frame. The cylinder shaped object first appeared in the east moving west. Both objects appeared at the same time. Around that time, a commercial airplane flew close by followed by a smaller aircraft. In the video, you can hear the airplanes and emergency sirens but the objects themselves didn1t appear to have made any noise. One of the objects appeared to be diamond shaped while the other object appeared to be cylinder shaped. The cylinder shaped object appears to the right of the diamond shaped object. On the video attached below, they appear at the 5 second mark. The video is the original that has been unedited and uncut. The photo is a snapshot of a frame.
