{rjl} Golden Ball Seen N/NE of Waco

I was driving home from getting a Dr. Pepper from the gas station. As I was driving, I saw multiple lights in the sky that seemed to follow a path, these objects were planes or small air crafts. 

I stopped in an area close to my house to record, in which I recorded it on my iPhone XR and the video is 2 minutes and 20 seconds. During which time the object started to fade out and then came back to full blast.

Object then started to move NE slowly and then picked up speed. Before the movement, it was stationary for about a minute and 15 seconds. 

I didn't have much of a feeling or emotional response other than "Holy Crap!" but I did get a little goosebump during the recording. 

I lost the object as it moved and I tried to keep up with it as I got home and then the object was gone
