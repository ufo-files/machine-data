{dr}woke up. saw figures looking down @ me. saw table thru transparent looking craft floor

I fell asleep around 11pm. That night on my living room couch. The next thing I remember was a sensation of falling flat in my back onto the couch. I was laying flat on my back and looking at where the ceiling was supposed to be; however, the ceiling was hazy and transparent. Through the ceiling I could also see through the floor of what I am confident is a craft. In this craft I could make out six or maybe more short thin beings with a huminoid appearance. Due to the strange hazyness produced by whatever they used to make make my ceiling and their floor see through I could not make out any details of the beings. All of these beings were lookind down upon me. This lasted roughly 10 seconds. I believe it noteworthy to mention that one of these beings was holding something in his hand shaped like an iceicle roughly 8 inches long but I could not make out details. There was a table or more accuratly what reminded my of a dentist chair in the craft as well. It was empty.
