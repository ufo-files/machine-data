Unknown craft, flew straight ahead, loud buzzing noise, noise dived into mud, left crater that filled with mud / water

I was in the fields at the time early morning tending to the lambs. I could hear a buzzing sound in the distance that became louder and saw an object in the sky that had a cigar shaped body and two wings but didn't look like any air plane that I had seen before.

The buzzing noise stopped and the unknown craft plummeted into the mud flats at the edge of the river. The impact left a crater several feet deep but was quickly filled with water. When the tide went out only a shallow depression remained.
