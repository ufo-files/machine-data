CRAFT WAS NESTLED IN A SMALL RAVINE THAT I WAS FAMILIAR WITH.

I WAS WITH MY EX- WIFE LOUISE , MY NEPHEW BRIAN AND HIS FRIEND SANDRA . WE HAD TAKEN A SMALL INTEREST IN STARGAZING AND BEHIND THE METRO ZOO WAS QUITE A GOOD LOCATION FOR IT WAS QUITE REMOVED FROM THE CITY LIGHT GLARE . WE HAD LEFT THE CAR AND STARTED TO WALK TOWARD A SMALL BRIDGE THAT CROSSED A RATHER DEEP RAVINE WHEN I NOTICED A PINPOINT OF LIGHT QUITE CLOSE TO THE WOODEN STRUCTURE . ALL FOUR OF US LEFT THE TRAIL AND TURNED TO OUR RIGHT UP A SLOPE THAT EVENTUALLY OVERLOOKED THIS RAVINE . JUST ABOUT TO THE CREST BRIAN AND SANDRA STOPPED AND DID NOT CONTINUE ANY FARTHER , BUT MY WIFE AND I CONTINUED ON. WE HAD THE HABIT OF HOLDING HANDS WHILE OUT WALKING AND THIS TIME WAS NO DIFFERENT . WHEN WE REACHED THE CREST WE WERE BOTH SAW THE OBJECT AT THE SAME TIME . IT LOOKED LIKE A DISK EDGE ON . DIRECTLY IN THE MIDDLE WAS A RATHER LARGE WINDOW WITH A BEING VISIBLE FROM THE WAIST UP . THIS MAN LIKE CREATURE WAS DARKISH BROWN , NO VISIBLE HAIR , LARGE EYES , NO NOSE , AND A SMALL MOUTH. AT THIS POINT IN TIME I HAVE TO STATE I AM COLOUR BLIND IN SOME SHADES BUT I AM SURE THIS WAS THE COLOUR OF THE CREATURE . HE OR IT WAS VERY BUSY WORKING BELOW THE WINDOW EDGE WITH SOMETHING OR OTHER WHILE LOOKING TO OUR RIGHT , NOT AT US . THIS IS WERE THIS REPORT GETS ALL TOGETHER STRANGE AND THIS IS WHY I HAVE NOT TOLD ANYONE EXCEPT MY CURENT GIRLFRIEND. AT SOME POINT IN TIME WHILE LOUISE AND I WERE GAZING AT THIS STRUCTURE , MAYBE TWO OR THREE MINUTES, I GOT A VERY , VERY STRONG FEELING TO PULL MY WIFE BACK FROM THE CREST BUT SHE SEEMED TO REFUSE . I STARTED TO SHOUT AT HER AND WARN HER THAT THE THING WAS GOING TO EXPLODE , WHERE I GOT THIS FROM I DONT KNOW. I FINALY STRUGLED HER BACK FROM THE CREST AND WHILE HEADING TOWARD MY NEPHEW AND SANDRA WE HEARD A RATHER LOUD BUT MUFFLED BANG . WE WERE NOW STADING BESIDE THE TRAIL LOOKING AT EACH OTHER AND TRYING TO TELL WHAT WE HAD SEEN WHEN A CAR DROVE SLOWLY PAST US WITH TWO PEOPLE INSIDE . IF YOU WANT MORE INFORMATION ON THIS REPORT PLEASE FEEL FREE TO CALL . I WILL GLADLEY FAX DRAWINGS AND EXACT LOCATION .
