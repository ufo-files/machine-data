Bright star-like object - varying speeds - stopped, turned direction

I try to look up at the sky as often as possible, in hopes of increasing my odds of making a sighting. Today while smoking a cigarette out my 3rd floor apt window - I saw in the clear blue sky a bright star-like object moving at a very subtle speed - then increasing in speed and slightly changing direction. I rushed outside to get a better view - and there it was stopped, or at least barely moving. Then it came to a stationary position - and slowly began to move to the right. After about it a minute it just disappeared. Although it was too far away to see it's exact shape - I don't know of any airplanes or helicopters that behave like this - I don't believe it fits into any category besides unidentified.
