Spent the night surrounded by UFOS. 4-5 orbs and a craft in the trees.

I was at an astronomy park to take photos of the moon-set around 10-11pm. Immediately I witnessed a bright orange glowing orb hovering above the tree line, where it changed height and direction before it disappeared. I was the only 1 around, so I sat in my parked car for some time. It reappeared over and over again all night. 

At 1:50 am I noticed a second orb blue/white in the opposite direction hovering like a bumble bee in the trees down the road. I looked around and noticed 2 more like it in the trees behind me, and again the orange 1, and then hovering lights in the trees right in front of me (I was surrounded in all directions).

At this point, I was afraid to leave. I wasn't sure if they knew I was there since my car had been off for so long. I recorded video and documented the ufos all night (trying to wait until they left, which they never did. I stayed until I felt comfortable leaving when the sun started coming up and there were a couple cars that drove by at 6-6:30am.

The ufos became more lively near sunrise - activity got higher and I saw some weird things.

The next night I went back and again saw the orange orbs hovering over the tree line (this time there were 2 at 1 point in this direction). A helicopter came & passed above me with lights around 10 pm. I wanted to check the trees in front of my parked car before sunset but made it after dark. I snapped a photo with my Nikon camera and captured the craft in the trees both days.

I went back the following day with a friend and there was no trace of any of the objects.

When I returned home late that night /morning, I noticed a star that appeared to be moving. I started recording and sure enough, it was. It was joined by another bright, small star-like object. I recorded them for nearly 20 minutes before I said okay i'm done, i'm going to bed. To which the objects then disappeared.
