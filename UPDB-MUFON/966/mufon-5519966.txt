Very Bright Orb

A friend and I were sitting in my backyard having a smoke while I let my dog out and looked up in the night sky on March 19, 2012 at roughly 10:00 P.M. EST and saw this bright object in the sky. We watched it for about 10-15 minutes and then I went in to get the binoculars as we were debating whether it was moving or not. I got the binoculars and we viewed it through them and saw roughly the same thing, a bright orb object with no visible features other than a bright unwavering light surrounding it. We saw no windows, no shape, no color; only a extremely bright white light in the shape of a ball. Size would be about 3 millimeters at the distance which would be around 5 or more miles away plus around 10K ft or so in the air, maybe more maybe less, Im not good with the altitude part, roughly 60-75 degrees above the horizon (Mountainous area so horizon is a little harder to judge as well). Object sat still for about those first 15 minutes and started moving very slowly like a balloon/blimp towards a nearby mountain/treeline. It took from around 10 PM to 10:44 PM for this object to disappear completely behind/in/through the treeline or behind the mountain. We watched it the whole time and even while it was behind/in the trees with the light blinking in and out from behind the trees as it descended or some such. It was very interesting and we are not entirely sure what we saw, whether it be a UFO or a comet or something similar to that. We heard no noise or anything, and there were even airplanes in the sky at the time, roughly 2 or 3 (a well traveled area for airlines though this is a rural area) and we can easily see the red and white strobes on the airplanes. This object had neither, just an astounding bright light. How someone can explain this or maybe they saw the same thing? Thanks.
