Approx 18 glowing, pulsating objects passed over our house separately during a half hour period

We live in Chicago on the northside. Looking out of our 2nd floor window we observed a golden pulsating light about 5 miles north of us that was getting closer. It kept approaching until it silently passed overhead. It continued south and we lost sight of it. A second light then appeared in the same place north of us and repeated the same flight path over our house. We saw about 18 objects during a 28 minute time period. One of the objects appeared far north and then took a sharp right turn and headed east over Lake Michigan. One of the objects appeared and then disappeared. Airplanes were landing at O'Hare, arriving from the east and heading west. They were crossing paths! I was too fascinated to take a picture thinking each object would be the last one. I finally went in to get my phone and took a video. After that no more lights appeared. When I checked my phone there was no video - just one still photo. And the photo does not look like the shapeless glowing objects that we saw pass by. It looks like a cigar! We thought there would be something in the news about the event but nothing has come up online.
