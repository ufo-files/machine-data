{jpg} Appeared after a 20 min meditation. Originally thought it was a drone. No sound or wings or propeller

I was meditating under my tree after yard work. It was a Saturday, clear skies around 1030a. After I completed my meditation, I began to walk inside and I noticed a metallic sphere the shape of a bowling ball/ basketball moving slowly east to west no more than 100 feet over my head. I originally thought it was a drone, but it made no noise and it had no wings or propeller. It moved across my backyard moving east and eventually disappeared into a low cloud formation. I didn1t take my eyes off it afraid I would lose sight.
