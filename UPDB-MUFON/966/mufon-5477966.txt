{cak} Witnessed a small starlike object that put out a beam and changed direction

I saw a bright object that I thought was a star at first, because it looked similar to the other star that I was looking at in size and glow. But looking at it for longer it was a slightly different shape, almost like a beaker, and it had a small beam pointing upwards coming from it. It moved very slowly horizontally away from the other star. Way too slow to be a plane, and the glowing white light was a solid constant light, never flashed it just remained glowing. Once it got a little ways away from the star, and very very slowly, it started to move upwards. It got a little ways up and then a cloud rolled over both it and the star. I couldn't see the star from the cloud coverage but I could still see the glow from the object through the cloud. The brightness faded slightly, and didn't move too far above the height of the other star before I had to go inside because I felt a bit scared.
