Bright silver object appeared from no where in the nite sky

Looking out my window from my 7th story apartment, on the nite of 5/5/12 around 10pm after the rain and a little lighting and the conditions were cloudy, appeared out of know where this bright silver vertical shape object just sitting in the north sky. Then it started to move very slow across the sky headed east with white lights going around it. I got my binoculars to get a up close view, but could not really focus in on it to well, it appeared to have windows with light shining thru and there was no sound at all. A few minutes later another one appeared in the same area, not moving at first and slowly it started to move southward towards me, I was feeling a little scared at this point. Also I got this metallic taste in my mouth while this was happening. I watched the sky until about 12:30 am and did not see anymore strange sites in the sky. I would also like to mention earlier that day I seen a dark rod like cloud in the sky in the same area, very strange.
