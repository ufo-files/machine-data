Bright circular light in the sky.

Me and my family were spending 2 days at Turi beach resort, Indonesia.
The resort has a long sea bridge that extends to the sea (as you can see from the pic) where tourists can walk and take beautiful pics of sunset and enjoy sea breeze. While we were on the bridge and observing some tourists doing para sailing; I happened to notice something circular and bright in the sky. It was stationary and just there. Had a bright white color. Did int seem like airplane as it wasn't moving and the glow as stationary and circular in shape. It wasn't the moon as the moon was visible. It stayed for about 10 minutes and then just disappeared. I don't think its some planet or star as those don't disappear. I had my dslr and snapped a pic at my maximum zoom. After reaching back home i still couldn't find the object in my pic. I could only see the object when i zoomed in considerably and it was clearly visible when I edited the pic using picasa and changed the pic to heat map setting. I'm submitting the original pic and 2 other edited ones along with this report. I'm not really sure if that's a UFO or something else. Maybe somebody can help checking the pic and decide.
