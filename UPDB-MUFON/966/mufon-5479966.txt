On the 20th of April 2014 at approximately 9 p.m. I witnessed a blue star-like object travel across the sky for approximately 1 minute, then vanish.

On the 20th of April 2014 at approximately 9 p.m. myself and several friends were camping in Vine Hill, California. We had been camping for the previous three days tanning deer hides. On this night we were all sitting around a campfire talking when I felt compelled to look at the sky. There was no cloud cover and the sky was a dark blue. 

I immediately noticed what appeared to be a blue star moving across the sky in a straight line for approximately 1 minute, then vanish.

I wondered if what I had seen could have been the ISS. However, having seen the ISS on previous occasions, the light reflected off of the ISS had always been white or yellowish white as it reflected the Sun. The object that I saw was an intense blue light that looked like a star.

I did not mention what I was seeing to the others around me until after it had vanished so no one else saw it.
