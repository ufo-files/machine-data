{nd} Saw two UFO's. No sound. No contrails. No tracking lights. Bright silver. Cigar shaped. took pictures

I deliver newspapers every morning. While traveling south on Grant Street in Shamokin, PA. A flash/twinkle/movement of light caught the corner of my eye as I had just tossed a paper onto the porch of an educational building. I looked up into the perfectly clear dawn sky and saw the two cigar-shaped items. My jaw dropped and then I fumbled for my cell phone and its camera. I got pictures but they aren't great. I strained to hear anything thinking it might be a jet or of something of that sort. Way too low for two jets. No wings. No discernible human air-flight apparatus features of any sort. They were headed two different directions away from each other. No contrails. Not beacon lights flashing. In two of my pictures the items appear white. In the other pictures they are black dots. I see jets taking off and flying all the time on this route. They are way much higher than these were. I like the quiet of the early morning. I know this route cold - I notice things when they aren't normally there. I said to my husband about it and showed him the pictures. I haven't touched the picks or nothing. I need somebody to see my pictures. They are from my phone and not of the best quality. I will attempt to upload them for you but it may be difficult to see. I apologize for the lousy quality of the pictures. Please contact me if you have any questions or wish to talk about it. Thank you for your time and attention to this sighting.
