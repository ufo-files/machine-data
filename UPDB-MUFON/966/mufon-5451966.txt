This sighting is in a time lapse video of a sunset at my house

This sighting is in the form of a time lapse video of a sunset that I made at my house on June 23, 2020, The time lapse covered about 1 1/2 hours with a photo interval of 2 seconds, for a total time lapse of under 2 minutes. Near the end I saw 2 unexplained objects moving. One changed directions twice, then zipped away fast. The other one just moved across the screen really fast. The second one can be seen passing beyond a television broadcast tower that is approximately 4 miles away from my house. I have captured many airplanes in my time lapse videos before, and even the commercial jet planes moved much slower than these. This time lapse video in on my YouTube channel. It is an edited version to show an arrow at the start of each one. I also have the unedited video as well.
