{nd} Spotted 4 glowing orange lights in night sky above Fort Bragg, California

Walked outside with girlfriend as she was going to work. Spotted a single light in the north sky and remarked on it - she said it was airplane. I said that glows different and there are no green and red lights marking lights - just a orange glow, unlike airplanes. We watched as 2 more joined it, and I said "getting camera", ran inside, came out and she said there is a 4th light, I started filming as we talked, but only seemed to capture street lights. The other 3 lights seemed to start to go up into the low clouds after 5 minutes or so, as the first light started flickering as it appeared to disappear into the low clouds as well about a minute after the other 3 lights vanished. No sound heard by either of us. (Would submit video but was only able to film house lights, not the lights themselves.)
