{cak} saucer shaped spotted hovering in sky, then vanished

I was driving south on county road 1 and it was 1:30 pm in the afternoon. It was a beautiful sunny day and there wasn't a cloud in the sky. The sun was pretty bright and it was shining on my left side. Straight ahead of me and a bit to my right, in the sky I saw "flying saucer" hovering. It didn't appear to be moving like an airplane. It was either hovering or flying very slowly in the sky. There was no jet stream left behind but it looked like a "flying saucer. It looked like the sun was reflecting off it and I was actually watching it to see if it would turn into a bird flying or just a stupid airplane. Sometimes, when the light reflect off a bird flying just right it looks like an orb or something so I was just watching it to see if it was a bird or a plane. Suddenly, this thing just vanished. It never sped away or took off but just disappeared from the sky. At this point, I was about to make a right hand turn onto highway 212 and head west. 

Where this was sighted, there is a municipal airport a mile down the road so its not unusual to see smaller airplanes take off and land there. However, this seemed to be really high up for a small engine plane. There is also an international airport about 50 miles east of the sighting so its not unusual to see jet planes or commercial planes coming in from the west. However, this craft was too high up to be a bird or a small plane and was not moving like a plane or a jet would.
