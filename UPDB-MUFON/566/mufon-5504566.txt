{kt}i awoke to a noise and brightness to find my daughter on stepstool in kitchen and son at patio door both mesmorized and being drawn towards an overpowering light and pull

We were all sound asleep. I heard a noise and saw a flash so bright it went into the house from the covered patio and kitchen window down the back hall and woke me. I thought a transformer blew. I got up to go check. My daughter was on the step stool reaching towards the window in her gown but now I think it was her in the front of the patio door and son in window. They did not respond as though they did not hear me. I literally shielded my eyes and had to grab them both from their spots. They as adults can verify this. The next day and many years later a circle in the back yard of great green grass there remained a ring of no growth maybe 4 x 4 circumference.
