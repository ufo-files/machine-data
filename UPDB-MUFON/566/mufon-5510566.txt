Blindingly bright light hovering/flying near A42 between Birmingham and Nottingham

This is an account from my mother as she is not great with english. She was travelling back to Yorkshire from Birmingham taking pictures initially of a car crash and also the sunseting when she noticed a bright light. Initially she had thought it was simply glare or a really bright star/planet but this was constantly bright. I had asked if my mother was simply taking pictures with the flash on but she had no flash on and the light was also seen by my father who was driving.

The light was round initially, but seemed to change shape at different angles. It was blindingly bright, and as the picture shows, was as bright, if not brighter than the sunset seen in the background. It was silent (though they were in a car driving on the A42).

She described the surface as "moon-like" which made her initially think it was a possible planet or just a really bright moon, but the way it moved closer to the camera and even in front of the trees made her realise this was something different.

My mother said looking at the object was like looking at the sun. She had her sunglasses on, but even after the event, she remained to have blind spots in her sight for a few hours after.

The object was visible throughout the large stretch of the A42 for 30-40 mins before disappearing.
