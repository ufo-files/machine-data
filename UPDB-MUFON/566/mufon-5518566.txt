Strange object with lights hovering, darting, glowing, pulsating

My boyfriend and I had stepped outside on our 3-season room to smoke a cigarette, when I noticed a light crossing the sky from left to right. When the light passed behind a group of trees, I waited for it to emerge from the other side, but it never did. I then noticed a very large glowing light that would flash atop a big tree in my back yard. It then fell like a leaf a few feet further down the tree and continued to glow, flash and change colors, including a red and a blue-green. It then went dark and flashed 50 feet over to the the right, went dark, reappeared and changed directions and then hovered by a tree in my neigbors yard for a few seconds. It then reappeared again several hundred feet over to the left and back in my yard before it disappeared. I felt so bewildered that when I finally went to bed, I couldnt fall asleep. I went back out to the patio around an hour later and after several minutes saw a larger(but still small) diamondish shaped object crossing the sky about 75-100 yards away, while pulsating/fluttering brilliant lights that were changing from red, to white to blue-green. I observered it in this solid form for at least 90 seconds before it disappeared into the night.
