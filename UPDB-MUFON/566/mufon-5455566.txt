{tjd} Massive light shining down from the night sky

I was driving home from work when I noticed what looked like a spot light shining from my neighborhood. I didn’t realize the light was actually shining down from the sky until I noticed a woman pulled over looking into the sky. I was so puzzled that I didn’t tell my family because I thought I was crazy. It wasn’t until later that I saw on social media that I wasn’t the only one who had witnessed this. People claimed it had to be a rocket that was sent into space from Southern California but I didn’t believe it because of how far away I lived from where the “rocket” was launched. What I saw was massive and didn’t move the whole time I watched it. The light was a unlike anything I have ever seen, it was a massive beam that was shining towards my town in the Sierra Nevada Mountains. What I saw was not moving, it was stationery.
