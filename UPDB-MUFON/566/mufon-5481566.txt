{cak} Triangle Shaped UFO

I had submitted this about a year ago and you attempted to contact me. For some reason I was so paranoid I did not reply, can't say why but just a feeling. Anyway I am ready to go forward. I observed a triangle shaped UFO when I was living in Tucson AZ in 1998 around August at 2 am in the morning. I just could not sleep and went outside to my patio to have a drink and just look at the night sky. I lived in an area outside of Tucson in an area called Rita Ranch. The sky is always crystal clear and the stars are just so bright! As I was looking out to the west I just had a feeling there was something above me. I looked up and all I saw was black, which was strange because I should have seen stars! I kept looking and I heard a very distinct humming/pulsing noise. I kept looking and then noticed that the black was moving. I saw that it was a triangle shape, it had large white circles on each point of the triangle and large red circle in the center that pulsed. I started looking round the neighborhood to see if any neighbors were up, and of course they were not. I just kept watching as it moved very slowly overhead and it took a good 5 minutes to where I could see stars where it used to be. Then all of a sudden it just took off, not going up but just in a straight line and was gone in the blink of the eye. I kept standing there thinking I must be really tired and I was just seeing things. Then 3 A10 Warthogs came screaming overhead. The air force base is close to my house and A10's are seen a lot so we are all accustomed to them. However I had never seen any of them late at night. The next day I heard that several people at work had seen the UFO as well. I was not able to take a picture but I am attaching a jpeg of the exact thing that I saw.
