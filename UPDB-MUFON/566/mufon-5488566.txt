{jpg} Heard an airplane, went outside, saw this amber/orange glowing orb moving very slow above the tree line

i was sitting in my house and i heard an airplane so i went outside to see if i could see it. as i was searching the sky i saw a light that i first thought was in the woods but as i got to looking it was right above the tree line moving way to slow to be any type of aircraft or helicopter and it didnt make any sound at all!! it was a roundish sphere/orb that i would say was maybe the size of a big beach-ball, it was a orangish/amber color moving at a slow steady pace above the tree line. it basically came directly over my head from the northeast traveling diagonally to the southwest. i couldnt believe what i was seeing and basically stood there and watched with my jaw dropped until it finally disappeared over the tree line.
