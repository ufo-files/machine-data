{jpg} I observed a light in the sky. At first it appeared stationary and then began to move about due North.

I was in my backyard just looking up at the stars. At approximately 10:20pm EDT, I noticed what appeared to be a star that suddenly started moving. It was off to the South moving in a Northerly direction. It was at least as bright as the brightest stars visible but as it progressed almost directly over me, it began to dim. After observing it for about a minute, It faded and I lost sight of it. I suspect it may have only been a satellite but, as I said, when I first spotted it, it appeared stationary. Then again, that could've just been my eyes playing a trick on me. All things considered, I figured I'd report it just in case. I'm sure NASA or someone could tell you if there was a satellite passing over Connecticut at the time. If it wasn't a satellite.....Oh, boy.
