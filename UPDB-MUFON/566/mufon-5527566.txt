Two red dots, moving simultaneously in an oblique angle at the same speed, in a northwesterly direction. Disappeared behind a cloud.

I had just finished jogging, and I was walking up to my house. I was looking at the constellation Orion when below and to the right I spotted two red dots traveling at a northwesterly direction. I would estimate that they were traveling at an altitude of about 10,000 ft. I am uncertain about the speed, but it was certainly too slow for meteors. They were then obscured by a small cloud, and did not reappear on the other side. My fist thought was that it was a plane, however the lights were solid, not blinking, and there was absolutely NO sound. In retrospect, I am confident that what I saw was not a plane, meteor, or space junk.
