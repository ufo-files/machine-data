{cak} multiple spheres San Fernando Valley

My family and I (3 of us) were in our backyard and we noticed a hawk and a crow that got our attention. As we were watching the birds we saw what we initially thought were metallic balloons but then suddenly there were multiple ones and they were appearing out of nowhere. Then a red helicopter was seen in the vicinity which sort of hovered then abruptly left. The spheres were changing direction and I believe at one point there were about 20 of them. They were very light reflective and pulsating. I don't believe they were balloons because of the sudden appearance of them and the constant path direction. I thought I was recording on my phone but in my excitement I didn't push the record button and ended up recording my conversation with my family when they had disappeared. This was in the San Fernando Valley.
