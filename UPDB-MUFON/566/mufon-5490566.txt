I saw a huge fireball falling very slowly, the sun was coming up this object was as big.

Late in 1979 I was doing a delivery job at the time , it was about 6 30am just leaving the town of Narborough in Norfolk England when out of the left side or passenger window I saw a huge fireball appear in the sky the sun was just coming up and this object seemed to be falling at about the same speed as the sun was rising.There were huge flames all around this massive ball it was the same size as the rising sun, I was sure there was going to be a huge explosion As it dissapeared out of sight below the horizon but that never happened,I watched it for about fifteen minutes the odd thing this is a very busy road but that morning there was no one else on the road,it was as if time had slowed down and I was the only person seeing this. I am not sure what happened next but I started to drive, the next thing I knew I was at a road junction about twenty miles down the road it was as if I just woke from a deep sleep. The other odd thing there was still no other traffic and I was not on my regular route. I have never told this to anyone only my wife and my Dad for the fear of being made a fool.I never heard of anyone else seeing the same thing, the only incident I have heard of in that time frame was Rendlesham Forest of which I used to pass RAF Woodbridge and Bentwaters every day.By no meens am I saying I was abducted I just dont know what went on that morning.
