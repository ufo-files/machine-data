Low level lights, No sound, moving relatively fast, made a sharp 90 degree turn - right angle immediate. No turning radius. Kept moving.

This happened a long time ago, but I never had any way to report it where it would be taken seriously. My mother and I were heading East down Neck Path Rd in East Hampton. It was night and was dark out. We were at the top of a small hill when I noticed these lights going faster than I would expect going just over the tree tops perpendicular to our position, left to right. At the time I had some flight training and it didn't seem like any aircraft I had seen before. We stopped the car, and I heard nothing. We turned off the engine and opened the windows and there was absolutely no sound. As we were watching, the craft made an abrupt 90 degree turn tracking the road we were on, going in the opposite direction as us. We sat there until it was out of sight, and there was no noise at all. We kind of sat there looking at each other for a minute and then started the car and drove home. Long Island was no stranger to military air traffic with Grumman located in Calverton. My father had been an engineer there, so I was very familiar with the regular aircraft. At the time, there was no ability to take photos/video. I included a sketch, however. I saw this when I was a teenager. I am now 48 and it still sticks with me like it was yesterday and I think about it often.

I am currently in California, but the address I listed was where we lived at that time.
