HUGE triangle moving very slowly.

I was driving from Vermilion Hills Levittown to Fairless Hills (right around corner) and saw lights in the sky through the trees. I turned the corner and had a clear view of the sky and saw a huge triangle with lights moving very slowly. I didn't hear any sound. I continued driving while looking up and couldn't believe that I was actually seeing this. The thing was very very big. I drove and turned around to go back with the intention of parking to watch it, but when I returned only moments later, it was gone. I drove around looking for it, but couldn't find it again.
