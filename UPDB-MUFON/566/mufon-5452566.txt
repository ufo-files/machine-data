Possible siting

My neighbor and I were outside smoking, when I observed a red blinking light object. The object hovering for several mins. The object began going from it location going north. The object stopped and began to hover for several minutes again. The object then began going south and then hover again. It continued to do this for a long period of time. The light pattern would go from a small single burst and other time to multiple burst. I check my local flight radar and there were no evidence of any indication of aircraft in the area. I also checked to see if there were and satellite indication and no evidence in the area.
