Looking over towards warton, my friend and I saw 3 brights balls of light, soon 2, then one and there gone. Bob thought there were helicopters, No no!

This has always stuck in my mind, so I’m trying to remember the timeline for the incident? 1994/95, we were out mountain biking and just before we get home, (carnforth. Robert sharples) we stop off on the benches on kellet road, dusting our selfs off, and having our last banana etc.. it’s still high there you can see all the Bay Area, and most off the lights are off the m6 motorway, that evening. Slow and you can here the traffic. Then we saw these bright lights over warton crag. I said there looked far further away, Bob thought there were helicopters, I said no way look how far away there are! They seemed to roll around each other, then one by one disappeared. The trajectory of our visions is in line with wind scale! Sellafield to you, but there’s heysham power station just 3 miles to my south, holds a world record now I think, longest running. But bright lights, orange tint, rolling around in the black sky. Never forget it.
