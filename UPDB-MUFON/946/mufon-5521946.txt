Long expanding blue colored trail that slowly then quickly expanded then disappeared after a quick flash.

I was driving up interstate 5 in southern California around the Northridge area around 6:30am today December 23rd, 2011. I could see in the distance what at first I thought was a meteor almost directly in my sight path. It reminded me of when someone locks up their brakes and leaves 2 distinct solid trail marks. But these trail marks were an unsual glowing blue parrallel to each other. Both trails started to expand slowly and suddenly moved rapidly in a straight line. Both lines were a solid color that did not fade at either end. Then there was a quick blink/flash and instantly the solid lined blue trails instantly disappeared. I have seen meteors before but this was nothing like it. There is no way I'm the only one to see it because it was in the sight path of a lot of commuters. The whole event lasted probably about 15 seconds. My jaw just dropped and was dumbfounded about what had just happened for the next few hours. This isn't the first time I've seen strange lights in this area.
