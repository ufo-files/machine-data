Red pulsating sphere that appeared out of nowhere.

On July 10, 2010 at approximately 11:55 p.m. I was looking out my bathroom window towards the Ohio River (west) when this red pulsating sphere appeared. It was moving up the river towards Pittsburgh (north to south) at about the height of 400 feet. I watched it for about 5 minutes until it disappeared out of view. I think it either came out of the river or it just appeared out of nowhere.
