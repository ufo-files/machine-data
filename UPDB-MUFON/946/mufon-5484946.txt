{jpg} Orb shown circling the moon on night of 9/15

On 9/27 & 28, 2015, my husband sat outside to watch the changes to the moon, until quite late. I took several pictures with my iPhone, marveling at the beauty of the red, blood moon. We went in about midnight because it got very cloudy. About 2 a.m. I woke abruptly (not unusual, I'm not a very sound sleeper) and decided to see what the moon looked like then/if the clouds disapated. When I saw there was a break in the clouds, at least around the moon, I took several more photos. Clouds quickly moved back in so went back inside and looked at the pictures. I was amazed to see a small orb circling the moon, clearly apparent in the photos. I immediately went back outside to try to take more photos, but the clouds moved in and completely covered the moon by then. I woke my husband to show him the photos. He told me then I should contact you. I waited only because I didn't want to waste your time with my poor quality photos, but then decided, you should be the ones to decide their value.
