I am not at UFO enthusiasts. Though I believe there may be alien life form in the universe I do not believe it would be capable of traveling to Earth unless it was in our solar system. I was outside looking up at the night sky around 10 p.m. . I noticed a white star-like object traveling Southwest from the West and then fading after traveling approximately 50 degrees across the sky. I didn't think much of this as I figured it was a shooting star. But as soon as it faded another object of the exact same star like appearance begin traveling the exact same flight path across the sky and then fading in the same spot. That definitely got my attention and after the second one faded a third one started again. I ran to the back door and hollered for my son to come and watch. This exact same thing happened 12 times that I saw before it stopped. My son witnessed several of these. It may have already been in progress when I noticed. Definitely very odd. I felt I should report this to someone. Please note, as one object faded the next object began each time in perfect synchronicity.
