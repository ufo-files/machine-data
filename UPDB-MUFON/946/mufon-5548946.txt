Bright light, Bouncing in the air, and changing colors from white to blue and back to white again, red beam sent down to the ground

I was at work when i got a call from my brother telling me to look at the sky. He lives close by my job. I looked up and saw an object moving back and forth, up and down while changing colors rapidly. It also shone a beam of red light which it appeared to be flashing to the ground. This lasted for almost two hours. I was able to take video with my cell phone video camera, I have an older model phone which cannot send video's out to e-mail but I have it saved in my phone if there is interest in viewing or any possibility of transferring the info to anyone. It was also viewed and photoed by my brother. I am including a picture but I reinterate that I do have video of it moving and changing color. It was still there when I went to bed so I have no idea how long it actually stayed there.
