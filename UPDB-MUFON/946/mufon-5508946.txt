Me and 3 friends were out at deer flat just looking at the stars when we realized that there was a UFO flying in the sky. We knew this because it was moving faster than the rest of the air planes that were in the air and it only emitted one bright light that pulsed a few times randomly as it was going across the sky. It would move in a straight line then slow down abruptly like it was hovering then it would go again, sometimes in a different direction than it was originally headed in. It pulsed like two or three times and each time it would only be 3-5 seconds before it would turn on again, until it eventually disappeared.
