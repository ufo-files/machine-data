{rjl} Seen football shaped object dropping fireballs straight down and fireballs stayed even with object as it flew by.

My wife and father and stepson were gathering nightcrawlers at around 10pm during a stormy lightning night. Suddenly my dad told me to look southward in the sky and asked me "what is that?" I didn't look at first then when my dad repeated to look again I did. I seen fireballs falling from the sky and then when I seen lightning that's when I realized there was a football shaped object dropping the fireballs. The object was silent. The fireballs that fell kept up with the object as the object slowly passed by. The fireballs didn't lag behind. I was fascinated as was all of us. We called the police and they came and took a report. They inturn notified Grissom AFB and they said there was nothing in the vicinity.
