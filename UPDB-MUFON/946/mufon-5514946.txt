(GS) Bright traveling star.

Mom was waiting for me at the front door of the barn while I locked the back door when I heard my brother from the driveway up by the house holler down to us, "Look, its a UFO!" I hurried to and out of the front door where Mom was looking up at the south sky. I directed my eyes in the same direction and saw a very bright white light slowly heading directly towards us. It wasnt much bigger than a star, but the clarity of it was unworldly. I was in awe watching it Pass Over us as it continued across the eastwardly sky. Its fluent travel and utter silence corresponded with its inexplicably bright, clear and unwavering glow. Just as I noticed what appeared to be an arched tail extending off the lower rear side of it, Mom told me to go get my camera. Not wanting to miss a single fascinating second, I bolted up the hill to the house while Mom desperately prodded my return yelling, "RUN, RUN! HURRY, RUN!!!" During my minusc absence the object had altered course and was heading away to the east and had put a lot of distance between us and continued until we could no longer see it.
