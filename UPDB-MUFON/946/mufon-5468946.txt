{rjl} Single light, flying north then turning northwest, low above treeline, no noise

In late March 2017, I was returning home from a visit with a friend about 11:30 at night. Upon turning down my driveway, I noticed a single, yellowish light flying in a northward direction from the south, and coming up the valley below my house. 

I remember thinking that if it were a plane, it was flying incredibly low, as it appeared to be only 100 - 150 feet above the treeline. I continued to drive down my driveway, watching as the light approached.  I then parked my truck in the garage, and exited the garage from the back entrance (south side of the garage).

At that point, the light continued to approach and was flying above my neighbors houses. Again, this was about 100 to 150 feet above the treeline. It continued northward and turned slightly west until it was obscured by my garage.

I then went to the front of the garage (north side), where I observed the light now traveling west-northwest, following the path of the nearest highway (US 22).

I thought it was odd because: 1.) it was only a single yellowish-orange light, and most planes have more than one light. 2.) there was no noise whatsoever, and it flew only a few hundred feet way from me. At the closest point to me, the light was a little larger than a softball. The time duration that I observed was about 3-5 minutes. I observed it for a while flying away from me. Then lost interest and went into the house.
