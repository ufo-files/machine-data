It was late one evening I believe on a Wednesday night, between the hours of 9 pm and 2 am. Summer night in august of 2018 if I am correct. I witnessed a glowing yellow light about 45 degrees just above the horizon, hovering in place. not moving. I thought it was a plane at first but the light was hovering in place and it was an amber color, not really the bright yellow or white lights you see on a typical plane. I was suspicious but thought nothing of it until a regular plane seemed to pass right by it, and it looked nothing like the plane did. I woke up my friend/passenger who was sleeping in the passenger seat, and told him to look at the glowing yellow dot in the sky that seemed odd.
