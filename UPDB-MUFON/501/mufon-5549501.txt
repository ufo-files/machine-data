Saw an object with three different colored light moving slowly and quietly above us.

I was driving home on Bryant Rd. going north. The two lights (red and green) first made me notice the object. First thought maybe it was just a small airplane but as it got closer and was above us, I noticed a third light(yellow)and it seemed to be triangular in shape. It was about 100 - 150 ft above us moving very slowly and making NO sound. I told my son to get the camera so he could take a picture of the object while I turned the car around to follow it. Once we turned around, the object had sped up to a great speed making it impossible to catch up.
