{dr}At roughly 9:53 PM on April 25th, 2015 I saw a red, point-like light in the sky, at first moving straight like an airplane, but strange enough for me to bother grabbing my camera and taking video footage.

On April 25, 2015,at about 7 minutes before 10PM, I went outside having coffee and cigarette and saw what appeared to be an oddly colored airplane at first, but curiously red and without blinking lights. For a moment I even considered perhaps the sun's reflection off a satellite. After enough consideration, perhaps 30 seconds, I decided to grab my camera, which took perhaps 30 additional seconds to retrieve and begin filming. I saw the light upon returning outside and pointed the camera and filmed over a 2 story apartment. I noticed something out of my peripheral vision, yet I was trying to see the camera screen, and really didn't see the motions filmed in the video until subsequent viewing, other than out of the corner of my eye. I wish I'd at least looked up and filmed the moon, which is waxing and just under half full this evening. At any rate i hope my video can be analyzed to show the horizon, as it was quite dark. I have no real way to determine how far away the light or object was; however I want to emphasize the object was tracing a straight trajectory, and in a quite ordinary way before I stared filming, and it's red color and lack of any flashing lights were what prompted me to film, and not any odd movements or behavior. An airplane had flown across the sky just prior to filming and a dog is barking in the video. My video is unedited and I also uploaded on YouTube as quickly as possible, within 15 minuts. Beyond that honest descriptions of events, the video speaks for itself.
