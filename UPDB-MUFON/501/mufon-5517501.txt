{ck} 4 bright red-orange lights taking same flight path one after the other.

On July 4th 2012 around 10:15-10:30, the time of nearby firework displays, Several of my family members saw a bright red-orange light in the sky slowly ascend over the mountain range and and then stop in one spot hovering for a few minutes. shortly after another object of the same description followed the exact same flight path, as the first one ascended in a shifty manner the second one stopped taking the place of the first one and hovered there until a 3rd one took the same flight path and did the same thing. A total of 4 objects took the same single file flight path, hovering for moments until they shiftily ascended and eventually disappeared one by one.
