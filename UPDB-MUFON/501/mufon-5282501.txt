Strange large craft over Connecticut

I was coming home from work tonight, Monday, February 21,2022. It was a little after 830 PM ET. I was driving my car on Interstate 91 Northbound. I first noticed the object in the sky around Exit 17. It was a large rectangular object with 3 or 4 red-orange lights that were blinking in an odd pattern. It looked way too big to be a plane or helicopter. Then it began moving strangely, it went in one direction, then stopped and changed directions. Then just hovered. I then noticed what appeared to be a small grey craft flying towards it. From my vantage point it had disappeared completely. I took the following photo I was near Exit 20 on I-91 in Middletown. and I’m a bit shocked at what it shows. The object in the sky almost looks like a giant forklift and the small grey craft on the left was NOT visible when I took the photo. I’m at a loss for words - zoom in on it!! Shortly after I snapped the photo I went around a large bend and lost sight of the objects in the sky.
