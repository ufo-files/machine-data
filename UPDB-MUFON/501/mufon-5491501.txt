Sunday evening at 21:38... bright red light stationnary.

Went out on my patio, looked up and saw a very bright red light above my head in the sky. A bit bigger than Saturne. Appeared to be somewhat far, stationary. Was not moving at all for a good minute. The edge what rather white/yellow. So, I observed until it disappeared... and it was like it was moving away in space at a very high speed, until it fainted away. No clouds cover that evening, blue sky, stars all over, but that thing was new in the sky, very bright, not a reflection of the sun on an object as it was not moving at all. I can1t imagine that I am the only one who saw it, it was too bright not to be seen!P.S: It was spotted NNE of my location at about 75% angle. To be frank, for the few first seconds, I though it was an airplane... but the brightness was too intense to be that. It was just hovering, no sound, no movement, and it got even brighter for a second or two. The edge was sort of white/yellow, oscillating. I though also for a moment that is was the reflection of the sun from a satellite, as the sun was going down at the same time. But it was so bright, fixed in the sky, for a good minute, then flew away until it disappeared. I took a picture with my cell phone, but can't see anything on the picture, probably too small for my phone.
