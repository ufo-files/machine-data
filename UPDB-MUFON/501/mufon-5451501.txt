Ref #110148 Light Returned With A Vengeance

Tonight my light returned, but this time I was Ready with my EOS 5D Mark IV w/ 200mm 2.8 lens. I noticed it hovering in the same quadrant as last night but a bit further East. It appeared to attempt "camoflage" of sorts by hovering near a star and behind a tree between my LOS. As I was smoking (on the same picnic table), it began to "sneak" toward me moving VERY slowly. I can only assume it was trying to not attract attention.
THIS time it got close enough that I could clearly make out a concave contour on the bottom with that green-tint orb sitting on top of it causing it to appear in the classic "flying saucer" shape. It was then that I retrieved my camera hoping for this exact shot.
Upon returing to the spot I noticed it had moved farther away. As I began shooting pics it IMMEDIATELY began moving farther away and took a higher altitude. It climbed into a low level cloud, which made the image appear "misty." This can be plainly seen with a star in the same shot as reference.
As I watched, the object continued to climb but seemed to stop and hover again when it became the same visual size as the reference star in the pictures. 
It was Obviously aware, somehow, that I was taking photographs and definitely did NOT want to be recorded.
