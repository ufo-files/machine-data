{tjd} We saw ufo flying

On the early morning of 7/8/2019 after a day of fishing in cooper landing Alaska I was headed back to anchorage when a friend and myself observed a UFO. We both saw it and described what we saw and went home and drew it separately and both we the same. 
About 30 miles from Girdwood where the mountains open up and the Cook Inlet become viewable we saw a slow moving - bright ufo heading in the direction of hope Alaska. There was construction at the time and pilot vehicle were escorting cars - however we were fishing and when we came out onto the road system we had by passed the pilot cars. I feel as if the construction crew was part of the ufo seen. They were stopping traffic far away enough so they could not see the ufo passing - we go out of the vehicle and tried to make sense of what we saw, where it flew, what could we have mistaken it for - it was a ufo and the construction crew was not a real construction crew. After we stopped and got out where we saw the ufo we could see a construction car coming towed us- I felt like I was somewhere I wasn’t supposed to be - I have fished and hunted these areas for 25 years - I never felt this way before. We got in car and drove towed anchorage.
