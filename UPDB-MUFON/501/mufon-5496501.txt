We were coming home from Wellington, ontario where we watched a ball hockey game at the local arena. It was a summer night and we didn't leave until 9-10pm. My husband at the time wasn't feeling well so we pulled over and i got into the drivers seat to drive. before i pulled back onto the road i noticed how quiet and dark it was....there were no stars or moon that i could see, no crickets or night sounds....I stayed on the side of the road and pointed out the above info to the husband and then we got out, i believe, of our vehicle and we looked up and realized we couldn't see the sky at all unless we looked to our extreme left or right of the thing in the sky. It seemed it was just above the tree line it moved so slow that i though it was stationary but i eventually realized it was moving and it was HUGE! Very black and no lights anywhere and absolutely silent. It was going in one direction with no variations. The trees in this area are pretty big and there are many so we lost sight of it as it passed over a couple of acres of trees. this must have went on for at least 10 to 15 mins until it dissap This was the second time i have seen an unexplained flying object. nothing that large could be that quiet....at least nothing that human kind has the intelligence for in my opinion. as for how i felt, to this day i cannot remember alot for some reason but i know i was pumped, exvited and then unbelieving.
