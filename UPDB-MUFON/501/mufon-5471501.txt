{rjl} Returning to home and objects were hovering over driveway.

Myself, my husband and two sons were returning home from a friend's house when we started to pull into our driveway and were met with two spheres, hovering over our driveway, approximately 200 ft. above. It was approximately 10 p.m. and one sphere was red and one was green. There was absolutely no sound and the spheres hovered for about 2 minutes. My husband was driving and we had stopped at the beginning of the driveway and just watched in disbelief. All of a sudden, the spheres went vertical into the sky and accelerated into the clouds, at about a 45 degree angle. My husband and I looked at each other and said,"did you see what I just saw?" My 11 year old son saw the balls of red and green light because we had the sun roof opened at the time and he was scared. I tried to reassure him that it wasn't anything but I wasn't quite sure what we had seen either. I have not been able to reconcile what we saw that night with anything that made sense. I told friends what I saw and they said it was landing lights from a plane. The problem with that theory is that you would have heard a plane but what I saw was absolutely silent and they hovered. They also moved in a way that a plane never could have moved. I just wanted to tell someone who may believe in UFOs because that is the only thing that fits the description of what I saw. I am a fairly logical person and what I saw that night defies logic, as we know it today. I was not scared when I had the sighting, more like mesmerized. And, even though it happed in 1994, I remember it like it was yesterday. Thank you for letting me share my experience.
