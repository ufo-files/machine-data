{cak} Trapezoid shaped UFO with dark red pulsing flickering outline appeared above me then hovered away after I unfroze and waved.

I stepped out into the driveway to have a cigarette at 1:30am. I had a black iPod nano and had the urge to pause it and wave my hand over the light to create a strobe pattern to see if anything would come in. I got a strange feeling and looked around to no cars, animals or people. I look up and directly above me was a trapezoid shaped craft with the thinner side pointing towards behind me. It had a dark red pulsing flickering outline that seemed to flicker more intensely randomly. The craft was a little over double the height of my garden home, so probably about 30-35 feet up. It pivoted side to side, back and forth in a fluid-like motion. It made no rough stops as it danced around above me, and after a few seconds I built the courage to slowly raise my right hand and wave. It then changed light pattern and hovered away behind the house towards highway 69S. As it slowly drifted, I saw the top of the craft and it had a small solid red light with a dimming white light that relit. It made its way out of view SE in probably 20-30 seconds. I have seen this same shape craft on two other occasions, and potentially a third.
