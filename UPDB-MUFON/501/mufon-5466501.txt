A orange/red planet like ball that moved and dropped something to the ground

I was watching a movie in my house. Suddenly my smoke alarm went off at 01:00 am.

It looked like how mars is standing in our skies right now but it appeared bigger and it was brighter. I grapped my cellphone and started recording. It came from the west and moved south without noise.  After a while it dropped a smaller ball of light to the ground. This second object went out of sight because of a big building. The bigger object dissapeared heading south.
