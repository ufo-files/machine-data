I was out having a beer with my neighbor on his patio. We were looking at the stars and looking for plains and satelites in the skies. I noticed a plain flying from the west to the east close to the Milky Way. Then i saw the 5 glowing lights! The 4lights were in a diamond shape and the 5th light was located just behind the 4th light at the nine oclock position. They were flying faster then the plain moving from the north to the south. I saw it for about 5 seconds and was not able to point the lights out to my neighbor. The lights were at a higher elevation then the plain. The lights moved over the home and i lost site of it.
