It was too slow to be a meteor,no blinking lights or tail and too high to be a lantern.

I am research scholar at IISER kolkata. I am a climatologist. I was returning back from my lab with a friend of mine. I looked up to see the full moon and this moving star like light caught my attention. I asked my friend to watch it. The object was star like with no distinct shape. It was bright. It moved in straight line and vanished after some distance. As the skies above my institute comes in the regular flight path, I am quite sure it was not a plane. And the speed was too low to be a meteor. It appeared to be obstructed by the clouds so can not be a lantern. And its sudden disappearance makes its more mysterious. I dont think it was a satellite, as it vanished all of a sudden.
