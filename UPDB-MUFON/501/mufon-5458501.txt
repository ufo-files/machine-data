{rjl} At first appeared to just be a bright star but then it moved in circular motion and various other movements

Decades ago I worked with MUFON in the San Francisco Bay Area and I have now lived at my current address in Yreka for the last 30 plus years in the Klamath National Forest. For the last two weeks I have observed what appeared to be a bright star in the Western sky at approximately 9:00 PM at about 45 degrees. While watching same it began to perform circles and various different movements. This has continued for over two weeks at this time and have also noticed a much less bright star to the South of my location that is also performing the same type of movements. This continues to occur every night and [name removed/cms/rjl]has been here on two occasions and observed same. All other stars in the night sky remain steady without any type of movements. On one night two objects appeared to have come out of the major object, much smaller from the major bright circle of different colors such as red and off white. In addition on one evening Mr. [name removed/cms/rjl] pointed a very bright laser at the object and it disappeared completely from view. We have attempted to photograph the object, however, the camera's both failed in the attempts.
