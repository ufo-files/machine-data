July 1, 2014, 11:30 PM, Ottawa, ON, Canada

July 1, 2014, 11:30 PM, Ottawa, ON, Canada: I looked out my window facing North. In the northern sky, traveling north-east at high speed (not a helicopter or balloon), towards an area where fireworks were been displayed but still considerably west of that area, I saw a lighted object (couldn't discern shape) pulsating (not blinking) at about 2 pulses per second and undulating up and down in it's flight path until disappearing. I couldn't hear if sound or not because of noise from air conditioner. Likely the idea was for the fireworks to provide clandestine cover so as to be undetected. I could not discern distance but it looked pretty low.
