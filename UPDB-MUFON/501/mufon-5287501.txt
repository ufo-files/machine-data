Slow moving, auto shaped craft, alum colored space frame, big wheel front and back, 2 figures front and rear, towed by big orange weather balloon.

Observation about mid-March 2021, date uncertain, during evening twilight, approximately 2100 hrs, partly cloudy to the north, event duration approximately 10 minutes, observation direction SW at about 30 degree inclination, short gap above tree-line. 2-3 days later observed same event, same time of day. 

Slow moving; jerky motion, would flash light off shovels figures were using, then gain some altitude and travel forward with the wind which was from the NW. 

Auto shaped; aluminum colored space frame, thicker in the middle, tapering to the ends, about 50 feet long, big wheel at each end, with a figure standing by the wheel just a little taller than the wheel operating a shovel, discarding something off the craft with a shovel, causing a reflective flash of light 2-3 times and a small gain in altitude with each of the shovel flashes series along with a short forward movement.

Towed by large orange weather balloon, connected by a cable about 50’ long, at the front of the craft.

Called neighbors to SW, in direction of observation, to verify his observations.

Called MI State Police, who treated him and his report politely. Advised him to call them anytime he may observe similar events.
