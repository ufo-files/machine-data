Was followed by a silent triangle that also hovered

I was riding my motorcycle to a friends house many years ago. I noticed what I originally thought was a plane. Then I thought it was either a small plane or a helicopter because of its apparent low altitude and speed. I turned onto my friends road and the craft also turned in this direction, traveling at my exact speed which was about 35-40 mph. I got to my friends house, which had a large tree in the front yard that blocked my view. At this point I assumed this craft went on its way. No one was home at my friends. I got back on my bike. When I was out from under the trees I saw the craft was still there. It followed me back down the road in the opposite direction we had just traveled. I stopped at the stop sign, cut my bike off, and removed my helmet. This was a dark triangle with a white light in each corner with two small red light on one edge. It was motionless and silent at this point. I watched it for a couple minutes then rode away. It slowly flew away in a northeast direction. After seeing some videos on the net, I’m 100 percent positive this is the astra. I’ve also seen two other craft on two different occasions. One in daytime that appeared to be a dull metallic ball that suddenly vanished. And a very large bright light just over a tree line that also followed me. Both silent. All of these sightings were in about a half mile radius. All within a couple years. I’ve never seen anything strange like these since
