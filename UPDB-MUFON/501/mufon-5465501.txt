2 Traingular craft overhead

Sitting in the hot-tub looking skyward, I observed overhead 2 triangular objects with round lights in in each tip. The 2 crafts appeared to hover momentarily and were gone in a flash. They were travelling from the northeast to the southwest. My guess is that they were at approximately 10,000 feet, in relation to other aircraft overhead. The triangular shape seemed to be a shadow of the crafts and the lights in each tip had approximately 8 0r 9 lights arranged in a circle.
