Triangle shaped , grey skin color, solid object with ridges both onto top and bottom

I was fishing with my nephew at a river called Houston river in s/w Louisiana. We started to hear a light wind and in one spot in the trees there was a light breeze that at moved the limbs. About 15 seconds after that wind started blowing three triangle objects shaped flying craft passes right over us and it was like they were close enough to touch . There no openings on the craft and colored like a cloudy sky . They flew one behind the other in a staggered formation . That was the second sighting by me and my mom also had a sight of a cigarette shaped object in the exact same area .
