{dr}Looked like plane yet all white lights and triangle shape and hovered. Moved slowly. Have pictures.

My daughter and I were driving down 71 in the Lake Travis/ Spicewood area of Texas. It was getting dark outside and I noticed an object hovering in the sky. I thought it was a plane, but it had only white lights and it was shaped like a triangle or boomerang. Planes have blinking red and green lights. The lights on this object didn't blink and they were only white. It was hovering or gliding - a plane moves faster in a given direction. My daughter was driving, so it was hard to tell if the object was moving up and down. I took pictures and videos. There have been other sightings of this thing, so I'm guessing it's not a mystery to someone. I think it's cool to have seen it. If it was a mystery, there would be more calls for information on the sightings.
