{tjd} Outer space star moving rapidly making 90 degree turns

Approximately year 2000, I was on my back porch having a smoke enjoying the night, approx 10:00 PM.  I was looking at the stars, clear night, and noticed something moving. I focused in on it to be astonished watching a star like object moving at extremely high speed.  It would move, stop, make a 90 degree angle, stop again, and repeat this for several minuets. I watched it make boxes, going over some of the same non-visible lines it already made.  I watched it make many 90 degree angels, knowing this was not anything from Earth, and that this object was traveling at extreme speeds, making adopter stops to make a turn, would sit sometimes in one spot for 10, 10 30 seconds at a time.  Looked to me like it was trying to get the best angle for a photo shot of area Earth. This object traveled at light speed to do what it was doing.  I know this was a UFO, but did not know who to tell at that time, 2000.   But I feel it should be shared now, thank you.
