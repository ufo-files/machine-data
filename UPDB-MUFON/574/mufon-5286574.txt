2 orbs and 1 very thin cigar shape that tapered down at edge.

At 10pm July 8, 2021 I observed 2 very bright white light orbs in the sky just west of Lake Havasu City, AZ through my bedroom window. The orbs were moving around each other slowly and smoothly. The lights then slowly disappeared...fading out, from the outside down to a single point of light before going black. I could not see them at all at this point. At this time I hurried out to the back yard. Then just as they had faded out, they became visible again, one at a time, but in a different location nearby. This pattern repeated itself 4 times. When the orbs faded out the final time.... a few moments later, a long, thin, cigar shaped light that was tapered down at the 2 ends appeared. It was thinner than the orbs, but much longer. It was visible for about 15 seconds and then also faded out. But faded from left to right. I continued to watch the sky for another 30 minutes, hoping to see more. But that was the end of the event.
