Surrounded by blue and white flashing lights.

Laying in bed looking out through a south facing bedroom window, a decorative half circle above the regular window, what appeared to be a large low flying square shape went by rather quickly. It had a slightly domed top with white and blue randomly flashing lights that completely surrounding the object. Holding out your arm a golf ball would have covered it up. I quickly ran outside and saw it disappear in the distance. It went in a straight line east to west. The complete event lasted less than two minutes. I see airplanes fly by in the distance regularly; but, never anything that comes close to this.
