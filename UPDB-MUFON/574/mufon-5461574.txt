{jpg} Cloaked Craft spotted near Ohare Airport

I was driving near Ohare airport near Chicago when out of the corner of my eye I was the outline of something small that was surrounded by an active camoflauge, bending light around it. I could only see it because it was twilight. The craft then accelerated past the sound barrier in less than half a second and was gone shortly after. If this was a man made plane, it would've been torn to bits by the sheer force of its acceleration. I've seen fighter jets fly before and this was faster by a magnitude of 10. It went from hovering to out of the atmosphere in around a second.
After it accelerated it left behind a glowing blue trail in the sky which lingered for a second or 2 then it vanished.
