Sphere of light that hovered over tree top but then darted back away when we started to run, and finally shot straight up til no longer visible

Light sphere that hovered in the tree beside our car while parked in our home driveway. When we got out to run back to the house, it zig zag darted further away still right above the tree tops. Then it shot straight up and was no longer visible in a fraction of a second. Never made a sound or caused any disturbance.
