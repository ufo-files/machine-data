Perceived as very large nebulous Boomerang shape reflecting city lights on leading edge

I was out in my garden enjoying looking at the stars on a still cloudless night and saw a large hazy shape crossing the sky. It appeared to be reflecting the street lights (there is a lot of light pollution over my town since it is just south of Glasgow. The craft (if it was was one) moved silently in a straight line. I observed it for around 15 seconds before it want behind the roof of my neighbours home. I went to the front of my home to see if I could continue observing it but lost sight of it.

I have NEVER seen anything like this. I am a 66 year old engineer with an interest in aircraft but know of no aircraft like this.

I regularly look at the stars and we have numerous aircraft that cross the skies so I know what the look like and there were a number of aircraft flying that night. This was no ordinary aircraft.
