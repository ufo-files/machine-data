My husband and I were driving down a back road in the Maryville,Walland area in South East Tennessee. I happened to look up at the sky due to an array of lights hovering not far from the ground. I quickly told my husband to look and he immediately recognized the craft due to a childhood run in with it not even 5 miles from where we were. We pulled over and watched as the craft hovered, slowly ascended and began to slowly move in sort of a square, making quick 90 degree turns that are simply not possible with civilian or commercial air craft. The craft was seemingly doing a square path over a large corn field. It moved so slow yet, it was so precise in its movements. My husband and other locals in this area have told so much about the same experience and I had always thought they were imagining it..... Not anymore.
