Red flashing rotating lights with a circular saucer like object

Feb 8 and 9, 2022 [exact location details have been moved to Investigators report section/cms/LLL]. My attention was drawn to the West of my car traveling North to Mason City to red lights. I first thought it was an airplane taking off from Hampton Airport. However, it was within seconds I noticed it to the west of my car and the lights were flashing in a circular motion. I first thought it could be a drone but noticed it was about the with of my Smaller SUV. I thought I was seeing things and looked at it. It was high enough to make out a circular saucer like shape but low enough to project some beams of light. I then was frightened and immediately looked forward but noticed out of the corner of my eyes that it quickly moved straight up and then darted off to the West of my location. However, I turned on my bright lights and it immediately darted back East in my direction. I then saw it dart North then West and East Again a few times then just disappeared. This was witnessed two days in a row. The second day it was lower to the ground and the lights emitted beams toward the ground to the West of me. This time it was as if it followed me from a distance North towards Hampton. But it seemed to hover over a cornfield miles away to the west of my location for a few seconds (this brought about bright white lights). Then it raised up and disappeared. I since then have been trying to see if there is anything that would remotely be an explanation and have not come up with one. I have traveled to work in this area for at least 3 years now. The only unusual thing I have ever witnessed was a comet a few months ago and shooting stars. I am hoping there is a rational explanation. I do not have any evidence as I was too frightened to stop and get a picture or video of it.
