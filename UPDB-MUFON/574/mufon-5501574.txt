{dr}LARGE lit boomerang flew overhead with large jet flying next to it.

The first thing that made me see it was the size of this thing. It was easily 1/2 mile wide from tip to tip. It was lit with colors that didn't seem to make sense in terms of clearance lights. The body was black or dark, but lit with beautiful colors. Alongside the right tip was a large multiple engine jet aircraft. I could hear the engines and see the clearance lights. The apparent travel route was from Northwest to Southeast. The object itself made no noise whatsoever. They moved fast and were gone before I could take a picture. I knew that what I was seeing was not anything in the known arsenal. Certainly not any of the "stealth" aircraft that had been revealed in recent times.
