{ck} Orange orbs moving slowly from the south to the north...no sound.

Second year that we have noticed the orange orbs. Both times on July 4th around 11:30pm.  Last year we noticed three orbs moving in a triangle shape across the sky in a south to north direction. Moving in formation. This year, we saw two lights again moving from the south to the north. However, they were in a straight line. They do not float on the wind. They have accurate direction - they move in a formation. They admit no sound and no white stream of smoke. They move over our home without distrubing anything. Very interesting and exciting to see.
