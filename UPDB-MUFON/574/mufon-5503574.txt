{nd} I took random photos of the brewing storm without knowing I took a photo of an object I know was not there when I took picture.

On or about March 27, 2014 I was sitting in my back patio observing the low storm clouds brewing, when I went inside to get my cell phone to take photos of the brewing storm. I took the photos and tried to post them on face book to share with my family back home, but couldn't get an internet connection. I continued to observe the growing storm then decided to go in and tried again to post the photos still no internet connection. Basically I forgot about the pictures, until monday night April 7, 2014. I was going through the photos my husband and I had taken on post at Fort Hood that afternoon to delete the ones that weren't too clear or just bad photos, when I came across the photos of the hail storm days before, as I was going through them I noticed something and zoomed to get a better look, when I saw a dark object in the clouds and said "what is that" there were no planes nor did I hear any when I took the pictures Then I realized "that can't be a plane" again I thought there were no planes nor helicopters nor did I hear any. I ran out of the room looking for my husband and showed him the photos.
