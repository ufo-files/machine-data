Cross/partial swastika like object with very bright lights to the SW shot to the NW very fast and disappeared.

At around 2130 hrs, while driving Westbound on the 101 Hwy from New Minas to Greenwood NS, my son (17) and I noticed an object in the air near Exit 16 (Berwick) high in the air. It looked like a partial swastika/cross shape with several bright lights on the bottom. It suddenly shot NW from its position SW of the Highway. It then disappeared. We live near the Air Base in Greenwood, as my husband is an air traffic controller, and are accustomed to seeing aircraft operating at night. This was not recognisable.
