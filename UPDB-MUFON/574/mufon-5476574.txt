{nd} Contact warning of nuclear future

I felt as though I was being watched, I investigate and see what appeared to be a shadow at my kitchen window. I then go out back door and see the craft in a field behind my home and in a flash I am somewhere else communicating with three unknown beings warning me that if humanity does not lay down nuclear arms that we would suffer as a whole. Then there was a flash and I was back at my back door and craft was gone.
