On a flight, viewed glowing worm like object above the horizon.

In December of 2011, I was on a flight from Malpensa Airport in Italy going to Birmingham Airport in the UK. I had looked out the window at sunset or evening and wanted to take a picture because the sky was nice. As I looked above the horizon, I noticed a glowing worm like object squiggling around and I knew it wasnt the sun so I tried to get a photo. As it turns out, this glowing worm object looks like three different orbs of light. Ive not edited this photo image in any way. I cant say exactly what it was but this wasnt a reflection from the window.
