Day 2: April 24th, 2019.I felt like I sensed something so I went out the south facing slider about 10:00pm, looked up and said again, "What is That!" this time I was in total awe and stunned to see this triangular craft, one bright white light in the middle and multicolored lights on each corner, it was as big as a football field. That's my impression anyway. I ran back in to get my phone to take a photo and couldn't find it and yelled for my son to come out and he didn't make it out in time to see it steadily move over the tree tops, coming from the west to the east towards Idaho.
