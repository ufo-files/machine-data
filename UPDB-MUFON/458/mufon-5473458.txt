{jpg} Loud humming-sound that was not drone-like

I got up to use the bathroom at about 1 AM in my house. When I was going to the bathroom, I heard what sounded like a loud humming coming from outside of the house. It sounded like it was coming from right above the house. This sound lasted for about five minutes, then it faded away. I didn't bother to look, because the noise really creeped me out. I was just happy that it stopped! We've heard some creepy noises around here, but this one was really terrifying. The first odd noise we heard was a few years ago during a meteor shower my daughter woke us up at about 2 or 3 AM in the fall to see. We were out there for awhile when we heard a loud screaming coming from the North West. Scared the hell out of us. My daughter wanted to go have a look, but I made the two of us go back inside. This humming, though, was the longest bizarre noise we've heard around here.
