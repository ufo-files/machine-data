OBSERVED ORBS OVER CHARLOTTE MOTOR SPEEDWAY AT APPROXIMATELY 9-10 PM ON MAY 26TH

THERE WERE ABOUT 8-10 ORANGE/YELLOW ORBS MOVING NORTHWEST. THEY WERE NOT IN ANY FORMATION, BUT CLEARLY MOVING TOGETHER. AFTE THEY PAST, ONE LARGE ORANGE/YELLO ORBD CAME OUT OF THE SOUTH EAST AND BECAN TO MOVE IN A NORTH NORTHWEST DIRECTION. WHILE MOVING IN THE GROUP, THEY BEGAN TO FADE AWAY AS THOUGH THEY WERE MOVING OUT FROM THE ATMOSPHERE, RATHER THAN WITHIN THE ATMOSPHERE.
