{jd} multiple blue green flashes of light in sky

My husband and I were traveling South Bound on Ih35 near Walzem, and noticed a first flash to the right looking West in the sky that was a bluish green. The sky was clear, and we thought maybe there was some lightening in the distance, even though we didn't see clouds. The second flash was a few seconds after and much closer, and definitely was in the sky, and above the ground. We were almost under the third flash, which was very bright and the sky lite up like it was daylight for a moment. There was no sound, and I have no explanation. We both felt scared, and watched the sky later as we drove home, but there wasn't any more activity. This happened about 9:10 to 9:15 pm.
