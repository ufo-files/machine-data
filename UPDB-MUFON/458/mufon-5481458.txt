{cak} 3 lights in a semi circle and it looked as if it was carrying something

I was on my way to work the grave shift and noticed lights that were never there the 5years I've taken the road to work. I freaked out and when I pulled my phone out to record, I couldn't see it because of the tree line. When I saw it again I began to record. When I got to the next town, I called the police station and asked if there were any reports on lights in the sky and he informed me that it wasn't and I was the first one to call. I was too embarrassed to give my name because I didn't want people to think I was crazy. I'm still lost for words.
