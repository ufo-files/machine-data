Very fast object triangolar lights no sound

I was relaxing around 8pm in my garden with my wife looking for some shooting star, when she pointed at something, asking me if i could see the same strange thing she was watching at.
Three steady lights forming a triangle were flying above us (not more than 2000ft)at a very high speed with no sound with a north/south straight direction.
I used to be a pilot with cpl licence, i know planes, and i have never seen anything like that before. I got very excited.
Unfortunately i had no time to film it.
