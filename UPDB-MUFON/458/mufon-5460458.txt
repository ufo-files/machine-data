{tjd} 3 bright lights on hovering object

I was walking on the boardwalk next to the ocean and the sand. I showed my friend an article that Navy Seals have sighted UFOs. When I looked up from my friend I saw in the sky above the water a hovering object with three huge circular bright lights. It was stationary and just hovering above the water. There was little to no sound coming from the object. It was above water and the water did not even ripple. I took my phone out to take a photo, and it seemed to have charged at me. It came close and lower to the ground towards me and when right above our heads maybe 100 to 200 feet above and then it traveled behind a hotel building and disappeared.
