On Tuesday 7/6/21 I saw these very bizarre lights in the sky. I saw two of what looked like large balls of fire moving rapidly from southeast to northwest.  They did not ascend or descend and seemed to travel nearly the exact same laminar trajectory while passing mostly over G-V then heading towards Orlando. They were both about the same altitude which was below the cloud level. There was an approaching hurricane at the time so cloud cover was dense and seemed low. I would think cloud cover would be too low for them to be low earth orbit satellites but after chinese lanterns that was my next guess.  Both were completely noiseless. They both moved about the same speed, which covered 30 or so miles of my visibility in about two minutes approximately- so moving very fast (speculating speed here around 700-900mph). They were a flying object that I can’t identify, up to you if you want to call that an Unidentified Flying Object. Both the Cape Canaveral (Nasa) and Harris (defense engineering firm) are North of my location so it wouldnt explain the flightpath. It looked much like a rockets hue but absolutely no contrail and those wouldve been northeast (nasa/ space x) of me, plus gaining altitude. Just thought I would see if anyone else saw them or has an explanation. The video does not do them justice, they were much closer. This was the first of two I lost the video on the second one when I called my wife out to take a look. Chinese lanterns are about the only thing I cant eliminate logically but I would expect them to change altitude and not travel so fast. I stayed outside another 30 minutes and could clearly hear and see other planes at lower and higher altitude than these confirming these did not make a sound that I could hear.
