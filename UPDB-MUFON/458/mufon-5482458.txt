{nd} bright light, bright flash, missing time

So, I was at a friends hanging out with a few people. It was late and we went to the living room to watch a movie. I remember my friend putting the movie in the VCR, (yes, i said vcr) And i remember sitting on the couch and seeing a light from the big window behind me. I turned around to look out the window to see who it was thinking it was a car pulling in. I didnt know this persons house well so it didnt strike me until after that the position on the driveway theres no way the lights could have been facing our direction. So i looked out the window and the light got really bright. Thats the last thing i remember before i remember sitting on a diffrent part of the couch (it was a sectional) and asking another friend aside of me what happend. He just said its okay your fine. Almost like he was trying to calm me down for being upset over nothing. Like you would talk to a comfort a child when their lollypop drops in the dirt and its the end of the world for the child but your cool and collected. I couldnt figure out why noone was feeling the anxiaty and confusion i was. I remember looking at the t.v. and seeing the credits down the screen. And just being very overwhelmed. Noone talked about these lights or what i felt just happend at all. Like nothing happend. Again adding to my confussion. I instantly felt isolated, and honestly a little crazy. This happend about 11 years ago. Ive told one person. Its not something i talk about with people but in my head i feel like ive kinda become obsessed trying to logically explain what happend that night. Ever since my attention has been drawn to the skies. I find myself always looking up, i just cant explain it.
