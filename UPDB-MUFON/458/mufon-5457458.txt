{rjl} Round Glowing Orange/Reddish Light.

I saw a Glowing Orange/Reddish Spere, Ball in sky that was just hovering. No sound, Definitely brighter than any plane or helicopter lights with a slight pulse. I watched it for about 3 minutes when it went to the left about 6 feet and came back to where it was hovering. Then it went straight up about 6 feet and came back to where it was hovering. That's when I knew what I was seeing! It then started traveling to the left which was East. It must have been traveling very fast because it kept getting smaller & smaller until it just disappeared right before our eyes. It couldn't have been a plane or helicopter bc there wasn't any sound what so ever and it stayed a spere. I think a plane or helicopter after changing directions you wouldn't be able to still see the round headlights. Most exciting thing. Ever!!!
