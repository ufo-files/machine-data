Saw this object when watching the ISS flyover

Was at home watching the ISS flyover , then all of a sudden we seen another object approach the ISS and seemed to get close to it, this object was dim at first then when it got in front of the ISS it became bright for about 2 seconds then went dim again. After this it left the ISS and went in a Easterly direction. Definitely Something I never seen before..
