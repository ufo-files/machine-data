Red-Orange object moving North at high speed, droppng obects as traveling

I am very familiar with the night sky. I have experience with aircraft and meteorological phenomenon. I know what I saw was neither I could rationally explain. I witnessed a red-orange object, traveling south to north to my west, Napa CA. It was visible for about 2-3 minutes and as I watched it moving fast, couldnt tell exactly but, several degrees per second, faster than a satellite and definitely not something burning up in the atmosphere. The altitude did not change not did it appear to change speed. I saw glowing objects (2 of them) appear to fall from the main object. They fell faster then what would be a free-fall and traveled at a forward speed that matched the main object, similar to the way a bomb leaves a bomber aircraft, but much faster. There are hills to my east and it was above the hills from my view at city level. I went inside to get my parent to show them what I was looking at. They came out and we watched the object head north and pulsed in brightness for 45-60 seconds until we could no longer see it, which from the best I could tell, it had traveled several miles away (maybe 20-30 miles). I could not tell an exact altitude, but if I had to guess, I would say 15,000-30,000 feet, and traveling at maybe as much as 1500 mph. this YouTube link is the most similar to what I saw, except what I saw was moving much faster and further away, and I did not see the objects that fell hit the ground. http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qFPlbnkyZS8
