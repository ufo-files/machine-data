{dr}object alternated between hovering and suddenly changing direction... row of non - flashing red lights above a row of green flashing / pulsating lights

I attended the Taste of Oak Brook in Oak Brook, IL with my family on Thursday July 3. We were there mainly to see the fireworks. We headed out to the parking lot before the fireworks show ended to beat the crowd. This was approximately 9:30pm. As we walked south I looked up to my right because there was a beautiful crescent moon (I'm a habitual sky watcher). It was then that I noticed a lit up object that looked stationary down to the left of the moon. At first I thought it must be an airplane or Chinese lantern (some had been released before the fireworks), but the longer I looked I knew this was something different. My gut instinct told me to keep watching, and I then realized this object was hovering. It then suddenly started moving to the right for a few seconds, stopped quickly to hover again, then suddenly started moving to the left. The object had a row of 3 to 5 non - flashing red lights. Below the red lights was a shorter row of 2 or 3 green lights that flashed rhythmically in a pulsating manner. I had seen this before at a greater distance a while back outside my home, and recognized the similarity immediately. The feeling it caused made my hair stand on end, it was quite surreal to see the lights that clearly. Unfortunately I could not make out the shape of the object, but it seems it would be an elongated shape of some sort. We were on our way out of the parking lot being directed by traffic police so we were unable to stop, and as it got smaller and farther away I lost sight of it. Very interesting experience, and I still can't shake the crazy feeling it left me with!
