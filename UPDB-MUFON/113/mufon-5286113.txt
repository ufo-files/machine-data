Unusual sighting in NASA ISS Streaming

At 10:30 p.m. (MDT) August 13, I was viewing the live video stream from the NASA Stream from the International Space Station when I observed a white pinpoint of light pass through the field of vision from left to right (same direction as that of the ISS) in a vector that appeared to be beyond the ISS from the perspective of the fixed video camera. I believe that the ISS was above the South Pacific, but I am unsure of the location. 

I am confident, however, that the strange white light that I observed was abnormal and unrelated to the operation of the ISS. The light crossed the field of view in approximately 3 seconds. It was in view at the left of the screen, passed behind and was obscured by ISS structures, and reappeared at the right of the screen. 

I would love to know an explanation of that light.  Thank you!
