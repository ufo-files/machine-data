2 starlike objects appeared in middle of star constellation and flew in a straight path then hovered again and vanished

On October 21st 2011 I was visiting family out in Mesa, Arizona. At that time I was actually almost done my 2 week vacation and doing what I did after dinner almost every night there, which was looking up at the star constellations and showing my young 5 year old nephew what stars were which constellations when I noticed there were 2 stars out of place, they shouldnt have been in the Little Dipper at all. These 2 out of place stars were not there a couple minutes before when I looked up the first time after dinner before my young nephew came out. Both Star-like objects flew in a straight path across the sky and then disappeared, there was no sound or blinking lights like an airplane or helicopter, there was no engine sounds. There was a small airport near my brother where small planes and helicopters flew in and out of but these were 2 shining round star-like objects too far up and too bright to be airplanes or helicopters.
