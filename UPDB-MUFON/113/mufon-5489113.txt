{cak} the object was enormous in size, dark triangular shaped 3D like unexplainable and unbelievable!

my boyfriend and I were driving down I.680 on September, 14th 2015 at about 11 pm, we had just passed the 84 exit to avoid going through Livermore. Sitting in the passenger seat I noticed bright lights, red, blue and lots of white. I leaned forward and started to observe it for a quick second noticing the craft was 3D like and triangular in shape. the white lights outlined the shape of the craft, Knowing ive never seen anything like that and it was nothing near similar to a plane in shape or size I 2nd guessed myself and started to lean back. I took a quick glance at my boyfriend and realized he was looking at the same thing, I quickly looked back and within seconds the object blinked about 3 times (pulsating) when I thought the lights were going to blink again it vanished! We both looked at each other in shock, it was unreal. He was scared to drive underneath not knowing whether it had just cloaked and was still there. The whole time it was still above the freeway in perfect view, no movement at all. (We came home and I drew a sketch I couldn't make out every detail of the object but I remember white lights outline the bottom of the craft, panel like shutters if you want to call them on the side. Very hard to describe, I've never seen anything like it. It's like paper over lapping paper.)
