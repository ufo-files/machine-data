UFO over Leduc

I just got home from work and turned on the radio. Now Radio Station (102.3 FM) announced that a fireball was observed flying north to south over Edmonton and it was still visible in the south sky. I looked outside and sure enough I could see it. It was getting dark, it was between 4:30 PM and 5:00PM, the sky was overcast and no stars were visible. But this object was clearly visible, beaming bright and appeared to be sitting over Leduc, a community to the south of Edmonton, Alberta. Being a former Mufon investigator, I grabbed my camcorder and took a few pictures and a short video. I did not have time to set up my telescope and night visio equipment. Definitely looked like an et vehicle. It was moving a bit when I first saw it but then stayed stationary.
