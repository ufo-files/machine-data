{kt}watched silent disk float by me

Was taking out the trash when the object caught my eye. The blanking out of the stars that caught me. Looked up and saw the large disk coming from the east over the carports behind the apartments. Watched it approach. When it was directly south of me, I dropped the trash bag and ran into the house to get my folks to come look. By the time dad came out, the disk was far to the west and still moving. He said he couldn't see it. It kept moving west till it was out of sight. Dad just looked at me and said to pick up the trash, put it in the bin and come inside.
