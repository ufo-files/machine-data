Making a right turn at a light and see UFO

I was driving to my moms house Saturday night for mothers day as I wasn't going to be seeing her on Sunday and as I am making a right turn at a light about 30 degrees vertically I see 4 fast moving lights that catch my eye and started watching what it was, at first I thought it was a plane so I didn't immediately grab my phone. I also was in the process of looking to see oncoming traffic so I could turn which is when it caught my eye. Because of how low flying and fast the object was I wanted to get a better look so I stopped and looked for about an extra 20 seconds and could see the craft tilting left/ and right as it headed north (with the tilts it would erratically move for example if it tiled to the right it would go to the right, and then tilt to the left. Like it was keeping momentum) The reason I can tell it was a disc shape is because when it was tilting on its axis i could see the light on the opposite side of the craft giving me a good idea of the depth/width of the object. On it's left and right side I am 100% sure they were red lights but am a little hazy on the front and back lights, I believe one was a green light but I could be wrong. The object was moving so fast it was very hard to get a good look at the exact shape of the object. I would say it was going easily over 175mph+ only 100-125 ft off the ground.
