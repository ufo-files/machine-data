Unidentified object caught on camera near Bodrum Castle

Having dinner with 2 others beside the sea at Bodrum Castle. I turned around to take a photo of the bay, the sun was close to setting and the view was pretty. As I took the photo I noticed the time was 19:19. I took a shot and observed the photo immediately - I noticed a distinct and almost shiny object in the sky that I hadn't seen with my bare eye. Immediately looked at the sky but saw nothing there. Myself and my acquaintances felt any bird or human aero would still be in eye shot. The speed of anything leaving eye shot would have to have been split second. The photo, upon zooming in, shows what appears to be a craft with a metallic shell. We heard nothing.
