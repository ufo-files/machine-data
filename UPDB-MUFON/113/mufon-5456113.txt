A v shaped object or objects in sky.

I was outside. I live in a walkout. I looked up and I saw what I thought might be fast moving smoke from a chimney but the smoke coming from other chimneys wasn’t moving the same way and wasn’t as thick. Then it “turned into” what I initially thought was a flock of “highlighted” geese due to the formation. It didn’t really make sense because “they looked lit up”. I decided to keep watching as it was very odd. I kept watching as a house roof was now obstructing my view and I waited until it passed back into view. Then it appeared as a series of round bright white lights. About 7 or so on both sides of a V. There was no sound and appeared to be either flying in precision or one object. It was moving in a southward direction and moving fast.
