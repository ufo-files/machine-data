{rjl} apparent craft with strobe light on top beaming a smaller light heading toward it and seemingly waiting for it to join up, which it did

On the patio looking east where nothing happens really, something going east in flight with pulsating light on top (planes don't have except the f-17). It drops a beam downward, then toward a smaller, very small light, white, moving toward it. I thought what is going on here? And the small lighted thing was being drawn toward the bigger object while both are moving I think. The little one got dimmer and joined to the other object, still only a couple or four miles out. Then the unified thing flew out of sight into the clouds. No small light emerged again on the other side of it. The most shocking aspect was to see the beam drop down and then swing upward to the small object, and draw it in, like pausing to bring it. I thought maybe an officer was getting escort by a fighter to Luke, but this was weird, tiny like an orb, and no fighter. No sound and no car noise. The small light just approached it from the north like hi daddy. This was no escort.
