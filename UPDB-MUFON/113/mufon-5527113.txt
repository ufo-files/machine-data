taking a walk at midnight with a friend

I was taking a walk at midnight with a friend, when i noticed a yellow object,moving in multiple directions, making manuevers that no present aircraft or satellite could do. After My friend went back inside,i decided to look further into this, it was then i discovered there was not just one, but about 20-25 of these.
