Two aura-like objects senn by naked-eyes, binoculars and camera througt binoculars.

I am an amateur astronomer (33 years old). 
Im an Engineer in Electronics and Telecommunication.

Sky was clean, no clouds. Ideal to stargazing.

I was watching Jupiter and its four moons by my Barska 25x100 Waterproof Binoculars with tripod mount.
Earlier I attached Logitech Webcam C600 to one eyepiece of this binocular to be able to record a view of Jupiter.

Suddenly I saw something I have never seen before. Strange looking light. No pulsating or reflector lights, just orange ambient-like light.
I took a rercord and what i saw was amazing. I was shocked.

By naked eye it was just an aura object (or flying cloud of enlighted mist) masking the inner part of the object.
By eye throught binocualrs i saw some moving gradients of ambient light.
By camera throught binoculars i saw shape of the object with circular windows or portholes. 

The best quality view of this object is presented in frame 335 of a video.
You could see very hot core of engine which is the source of these strange light.

It was hard to point binoculars on this fast moving object because my tripod was no functioning properly well.
Its speed was about 250mph.

There were two identical objects.
First showed up at 1.40a.m.
Second object showed up at 1.53 a.m.
They flew the same path.
