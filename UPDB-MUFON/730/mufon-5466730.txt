{jpg} UFO videoed at Ellicot Creek Park in Tonawanda NY on August 4, 2018

My family and I are on a summer vacation going to see Niagara Falls. At the time I was in a dark part of Ellicot park and there was a live concert going on near the park. I was trying out my new Canon Vixia 57x zoom camera videoing the planets (Jupiter and Mars are out and to the south) when I saw the UFO rise out of the east. 

The UFO (reddish light) was videoed at Ellicot Creek Park in Tonawanda (Amherst) NY on Saturday 8-4-2018 at 10:23 PM EST. The video is looking East and the object is flying toward the park (camera) location and slightly north. At :22 in the video the UFO drops something. The UFO is an unknown distance away but I would estimate at least a few miles way. A few times during the video some power lines go in front of the UFO as it's flying. After 2 mins 30s the object disappears after no apparent movement out of view. 

Weather conditions were mostly clear with some wispy clouds. 72 degrees out with no wind.
