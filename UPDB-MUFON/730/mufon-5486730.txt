{nd} starlike light moving slowly north with bright flashes of light

on September 29th.at around 7:50p.m. i walked out of our 5th.wheel trailer to the bottom of the steps and looked up to the sky as i am an avid sky watcher. I quickly noticed what appeared to be a very bright star at 140 degrees S.E.and 60 degrees off the horizon.I know a bright star should not be there at this time.I watched for about 30 seconds and it dimmed by about 75% and started moving slowly north.After 10 minutes the object was aprox.80 degrees west and 70 degrees off the horizon. I ran inside to get my binoculars and when i got back outside it took around 5 minutes for me to find the object again as it had dimmed again.At that moment the object was aporox.40 degrees and still 70 degrees off the horizon, a bright flash came from the object.It was as if a large camera flashed.I followed the object with my binoculars moving E.N.E. slowly.Every minute or so the object would flash brightly.A friend walked up at that time and witnessed the last two flashes.In all i witnessed 8 flashes as the object moved E.N.E. and out of my sight.The event lasted for about 30 minutes.I wrote the details of this sighting that evening as well as compass readings.My binoculars are 10x50 power.I researched to see if anyone else reported anything on 29th September in my area and found a report at another reporting site not unlike my sighting.
