(GS) electric green trail and toke a hard turn to East it was like a purple or with the green trail behind it

I was outside with my dog about 8 oclock and I saw some arking lights Green reflecting off the roof of houses and the trees pretty bright my dog even noticed it, at first I thought it was a firework but looked up noticed more lights Trail ,it was making turns and coming down and a hard left to go east and then I noticed it was round purple orb prob 15,30feet round with a trail green glowing trail following it and it was bright Trail I watched it shot of. I stand there and think about it .got closer to the ground about 80 100 ft disappeared with really tremendous speed no sound. I have no idea what it was.
