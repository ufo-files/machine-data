My girlfriend and I were on our way to get ice cream traveling north on main street in auburn. I noticed a black object in high altitude go across the road heading west then quickly back north out of sight behind the tall buildings in a blink of an eye and I said out loud woah a ufo, but jokingly, My girlfriend laughed and said yeah right, as we drove about 300 feet I pointed it out and it was probably a mile maybe two away and to the left of us, and flying south away from us, giving a side view, At first we both say oh it's a helicopter because that is exactly what it looked like. We both stared in amazement and silence as we watched it change course on a dime coming directly at us and in less then a second was right in front of us. It came to about 2 or 300 feet away and about 30 feet above the auburn regional hospital and it seemed to stop in place and broad sided as it turned in place giving a crystal clear shape and detail of the thing, and all in 100% silence, (windows down) we both were silent, seemed Like all sound was non existent when it happened. When it turned and faced north it flew off and out of sight behind the buildings and trees. From first sight to finish was only about 15 seconds. We both were freaking out and knew exactly what we saw. And it was without a doubt not a plane, hangglider, drone, helicopter. I've never seen something go those huge distances in such little time, (been to many blue angel shows) and I've definitely have never seen a triangle "A" shaped craft.
