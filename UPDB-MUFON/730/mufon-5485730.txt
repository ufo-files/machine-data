{rjl} Large, round silhouette seen in the clouds in daylight in Las Vegas.

I was driving to work and I noticed something in the corner of my eye towards Sunrise Mountain in LV, Nevada. In the clouds, there was an elliptical silhouette that moved in the same fashion as a spot light in the sky. The silhouette was very large and dwarfed the planes in the sky. There was considerable daylight since this was at 7:30 in the morning and the direction of the sunlight did not make sense with the silhouette. I felt kind of shocked and confused. The object wavered in place then moved to the left and then just dissipated. I tried to take a video, but the distance was far, I was behind my windshield, and I didn't bring my phone out in time.
