{nd} Sighted round white/silver? object stationary in clear sky

Went to the end of my driveway to talk to my daughter. Noticed a round white object stationary, high in the sky. It was too early for stars to be out. The object appeared larger than a star would be. Initially thought it could be a balloon or weather balloon. The object remained stationary for several minutes. Could not judge altitude, but appeared to be very high. Got my 35mm SLR and looked at the object through my 50-250mm lens. Object was round and appeared to have an indented bottom, but that could have been a trick of lighting. Sat in a chair on the front porch to observe and take pictures. The object moved very slowly in a straight line heading east over an hours time. During that time a thin cloud moved past the object. The object was above the cloud. At approximately 1925 the object took on an orange shade from the setting sun. I lost sight of the object as the sun went down. I initially was not going to turn in a report, but the recent sighting in Breckenridge Co. seemed to be a similar object. This made me feel that it was more important to report this sighting.
