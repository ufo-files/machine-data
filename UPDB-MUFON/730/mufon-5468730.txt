{td} Witnessed a light in sky much higher than the plane. Flight path was smooth then erratic and would interchange patterns then it shot straight up and disappeared

We were on our back porch around 10 pm and noticed a light in the sky that was much higher than the planes also in sky at the time. It would fly straight then sort of pulse up and down like a heartbeat. There was no rhythm it was very erratic. We kept comparing it to the other objects in the sky and there was nothing else like it. We saw it for about 5 minutes and then it shot straight up and disappeared. The next night we saw it again, but it was in a different position in the sky. We still can't explain it.
