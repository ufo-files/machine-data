{rjl} I saw what looked like a triangle with two longer sides and a short side.

I saw a large triangle with two long sides and one short. I saw the triangle appear to be hovering. I thought I was looking at a star formation but then I saw one flair shoot out the front point of the triangle. I then thought it was a shooting star, but quickly knew it wasn’t because it then dropped six or seven flairs directly out of the bottom. The front and lower flairs did not travel far before burning out. I observed the triangle coming down then going back up. At times the front point light went dark. The whole thing seemed to go dark and it would move in different directions. I feel I could still see the shape when moving even with the three light points not lit. Air traffic got heavy towards the end of the sighting. Fast moving jets. I would take a lie detector test to prove what I saw. It really freaked me out.
