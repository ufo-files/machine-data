{cak} Bright fast passing low light

My friend and I were sitting in the backyard on a Saturday night chatting, when around 1:20 am we both looked up into the sky at the exact same moment. That's when we both saw a fast bright tear shaped light ball (no tail) passing maybe about 18000 feet high going about 90 mph. It disappeared behind a cloud and never came out on the other side of the cloud. It also appeared that inside this bright light was another smaller, brighter and round light.
