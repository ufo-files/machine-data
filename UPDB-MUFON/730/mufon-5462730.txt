{tjd} on 15 northbound saw sphere above crane on stratophere motionless then lifted slowly and faded out of sight

I was traveling northbound on I15 close to Flamingo when I saw what looked like a reflection on the windshield the color of streetlights, that yelloeish color the city was changing to in the early 1990s. The reflection never moved so I knew it was not a reflection, the stratosphere was being built at that time, where the three legs were spreading back out with wooden scaffolding around the top with a crane on the top. I saw a sphere about the same size as the scaffolding that looked like it was resting on top of crane. As I got closer the sphere slowly lifted up then went out of sight. I believe the sphere was shiny silver reflecting the yellow streetlights and when it rose up it faded out of sight. During the 90s I was driving on the 15 every night, I have never seen anything like that before or since then.   Thank You for reading this. Sincerely [ Item moved to FI Report {tjd} ]
