Observed large hard edged rectangular object hovering

I was traveling north on I-35 from Austin and as I came up to Round Rock I observed a large rectangular box shaped object in the sky to the west of the interstate seemingly hovering and still. It resembled a shipping container, was mainly light colored, almost white with the bottom third phasing to medium blue. There was nothing around it. It stood still and alone in the sky. I was alone in my car and couldn’t stay fixed on it as I had to keep driving but looked up at it six or seven times in which it held its position the whole time I was able to observe it. The was nothing vague about it. It was a physical object in the sky just sitting there. No one else seemed to notice it .
