Two hovering craft over house at high altitude.

There were six people total who viewed the aircraft/objects. I am unsure who observed the objects initially but it was me, my wife and son a couple of neighbor boys and my neighbor directly accross the street. We live on a cul de sac and were outside being social on a warm September evening. Two unknown aircraft were hovering for close to 40 minutes at a high altitude without really moving, just sitting in place. I did not have any reference point for distance but they were almost directly over head and a little bit difficult to see, so I am assuming they were pretty high in the sky. One was easier to see than the other as if it were either a smaller craft accompanying the larger craft or at a higher altitude making it appear smaller. The distance made it impossible to make out details but it had a definite triangle shape. We watched until it became to dark to see anymore... it was not like anything any of us had seen before in regards to it's behavior... just sitting there, all other aircraft fly by where as this just seemed to hover.
