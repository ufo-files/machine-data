Rotating light.

We looked up to the act and saw a blue light shoot across the sky. Then after that light we saw rotating lights come into view one by one and after 5-6 you could see it would rotate to the first one vanishing and another light coming rotating in its place. All aircrafts stopped flying in the area and we were close to a major airport. Charlotte international.  After it left. A plane came into view and the the objects came back in the same spot and all airplane traffic stopped. Was so weird not seeing any planes in the sky when you are used to seeing them all the time.
