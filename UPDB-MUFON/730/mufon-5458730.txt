Brilliant Blue Glowing Object

I was driving him from dropping my son off at school, there was literally nothing in the sky, no clouds at all so seeing this cloud like trail really caught my eye, even while concentrating on driving. It was giving off a kind of metalic bright blue, almost aquaish colour and it was glowing brightly at the end of the trail. It was only a short trail i saw, mabye about 5cm long from my POV (would have been much longer up there) and the trail was pure white. No sound, no time lost, just very strange seeing a bright blue glowing object with a pure white cloud like trail behind it. Unfortunately because i was driving i wasn't able to get photos because there was no safe place to pull over.
