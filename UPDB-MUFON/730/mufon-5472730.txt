Was outside setting up my new telescope looking for a clear area to point it, I saw a plane coming in from the northwest when I noticed a silver object in the sky, it seemed to be twice as high as the passenger airplane passing at the time. I watched it in comparison with the east bound plane and it seemed to be stationary. I ran inside to get my phone to check the time(3:44pm pct) and grab my binoculars, I wasn't able to focus the telescope. I focused in on it with my binoculars and checked the time (3:45pm pct) the object appeared to float in a sweeping motion at what seemed to be varying heights, occasionally I could see what looked like blue lights, 2, on what seemed to be the front, it was shaped like an elongated diamond upside down. It took about 7 minutes to cross the field of view then hovered in the southwestern sky for a minute the vanished.
