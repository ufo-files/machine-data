Triangle flying low and slow

I was driving down Stanley Blv. and as I aproached a stop light I saw an unusual set of lights coming toward me. It is common to see helocopters here but this did not fit that image. So I kept looking as it approached slowly. When the light turned green I pulled out and pulled over to get out and get a better look.I got out and just watched as it slowley moved by. I knew when I could see it what it was as this is the sixth time I have seen one. I thought it to be a TR-3. I do sky watches every night with my night vision goggles and I do record them. I have not yet got one on film as they usually move accross the sky very quickly. I was on my way to a Boy Scout meeting when I saw it. It had three white lights that were pretty big on the under side. It also had blinking white lights under it. There were white lights around the outside of the craft as well as a red light in the front. White blinking lights that were placed where the strait side of the craft started to roll around to the other striat side. One on each side. This craft was flying east at about 10 MPH. If it were any type of airplane it would have fallen from the sky. I saw it at 7:36 PM and watched it for 3 or 4 minutes. I could not hear any sound but I did have freeway niose which may have made it hard to hear had it been present.I have been trying to get one of them on film but it just has not happened yet.
