Driving east toward the town of Belgrave in the Dandenong Ranges,a built up residential area in 2013.I noticed a thin moon like object in the sky above Belgrave town.It was moving along at a slow rate toward the mountain It was about 11.30 to 12.30 AM on a sunny late winter day, no clouds were visible. At first , i thought it was a thin moon outline but when i stopped my car and watched it ,this craft was obviously traveling on its own independently of the view from my vehicle. This craft appeared to be massive and was seemingly hanging low in the sky but, this was very hard to ascertain and i remember feeling like there was more than one way to see it. Either it was very high and very massive or it was low and very big but not as massive..? The exterior outline of this craft was metallic with a beautiful saturn finish to the metal..It was a disc shape, round but with a curious tapering half way around the circle which gave the appearance of an oval nose shaped front.My impression was of an engineering quality of incredible beauty and sophistication. I immediately knew or sensed that this was NOT from our planet. Normally i am very careful about walking across roads and traffic but on this day i walked into the middle of a two lane busy road with a fair amount of traffic , completely oblivious to the danger and only realized how dangerous this was after the craft had disappeared across the mountain and behind the foreground trees.
