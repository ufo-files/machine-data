lying on trampoline with friend as child. light in a diamond or square sharp came together ad apart several times and then shot off after hovering over the area for a short time.

I was probably about 10 years old. One night my friend Jack and I were laying on the trampoline in the backyard.after staring at the stars for a moment we noticed 4 "stars" becoming brighter.from what I remember they then began to come to what would have been the center of the diamond pattern. They shot out to the four corners and then back in several times.after hovering for a few minutes. At this point, I remember my little sister running into the house in fear. Jack and I were to excited to stop watching. They lights then came together as one again and shot of towards the east. It was an event I will never forget. We tried convincing our parents about the event, but they at the time refused to believe us, and shrugged it off as childhood imagination. We know what we saw. Jack and I still from time to time being it up in conversation years later.
