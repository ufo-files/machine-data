{rjl} like bright Venus where it never is. Hovered with slow descending movement

Last night I walked up to my kitchen sink at about 10:20 pm and thought it was odd to see this bright star in the low western sky outside the window where I don't believe I've seen a bright star or planet before in 17 years of living in my home. Looked like bright Venus but the light was fluttering like a star. I thought it might be slowly moving around. I opened the window expecting to hear a distant helicoptor sound, but could detect no sound. It just didn't seem that far away (10 miles or so perhaps.) I watched it for about 15-20 min. Color of light was not toward the orange or blue, just similar to what Venus might look like. Looking at it with not very strong binoculars didn't give much info, but it looked smaller and was not at all fluttering through the binoculars. My iphone compass showed it at 272 degrees west; then a minute or two later it was clearly at 284 degrees and then I realized that it was indeed drifting out of position. Over the next several minutes it slowly desended in an arc and I finally lost sight of it at 10:35 pm at 340 degrees west. I drew a diagram using the roof of the house across the street with its smoke stack, vent pipe and nearby tree to be able to determine just where it was when I cane upon it and exacting where it dissapeared behind the roof.
