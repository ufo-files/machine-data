I received a phone call around 6:38 in the evening on December 12, 2019. My friend {name moved toFI Section/CMS/jpg] called me and told me to look west over the white Tank Mountains at a very bright light in the sky. When I open my front door I immediately noticed the light. It sat there for quite some time roughly 15 minutes and slowly descended from a 45° angle to roughly a 33° angle. I noticed it was stationary because civilian aircraft were flying above it and I could see their anti-collision lights flashing. I know being an Air Force fighter aircraft mechanic It was not one of our planes out at Luke Air Force Base. I have seen previous settings but they were all to the south, this one was unique in the fact that it was coming from the west this time.
