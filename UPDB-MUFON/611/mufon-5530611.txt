Light orange disc NW to NE of Reno.

I was in my boyfriend's back yard smoking, after his dog woke me up to go outside. I noticed a slow moving, light orange light moving across the NW part of the sky. I knew it wasn't a satellite because it was light orange, and wasn't the right shape. I went inside and grabbed binoculars. It was still there when I came back out. I could clearly see the outline of the classic disc shape with the light orange lights the were glowing softly, and appeared to be from the top to the bottom of the ship. I watched it travel eastward. At one point, it "jumped", but otherwise, it did not blink or move sporadically. It just moved slowly across the sky. I watched for about 15 minutes until I lost it behind trees in the NE. At that time I drew a sketch of it. I was really excited because I was sure I was seeing something strange.
