2 star like lights below the star Rigel, very bright just disappeared together after 4 minutes

I was outside having a smoke, it was a clear night, about 6:35pm, EST, Ontario, Canada, when I scanned the sky...as I like to do, and I noticed two lights, very bright below the star Rigel, in Orion. I always scan the sky and these two lights I had never seen before, so I decided to watch them...just for curiosity. They didn't change position or move, just sat stationary. After about 4 minutes, they both disappeared at the same rate, in about 6 or 8 seconds, until they were just gone. I continued to look at the same spot for about another 10 minutes and they didn't reappear. If these lights were right in front of my face, I would estimate that they were about 6 inches directly below Rigel and about 1 and 1/2 inches apart. They were very high in the air, I'd say much, much higher than any aircraft can fly. I know it's not a lot to go on but I thought I would report it in case anyone else does and you can know that it was also spotted from my home in Keswick, Ontario, Canada...About a 45 minute drive north of Toronto, Ontario. At that time of night there is a satellite quite a distance west of this sighting and these two lights brilliance was in between the brightness of whichever satellite this is and the stars of Orion. Unfortunately, I never had my cell phone with me to record this event. Please inform me about any other sightings reported regarding this report via E-Mail . You may contact me at the phone number I provided also.
