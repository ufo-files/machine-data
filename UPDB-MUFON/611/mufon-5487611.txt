{dr}Observed large lighted wing like object overhead in ste shape of a boomerang in the evening after dark.

From the back porch of my house overhead in the sky I first thought I saw the lights of a very low flying large aircraft. It seemed to be too low and larger than a commercial aircraft. Next I was amazed that a lighted object that large was silent and moving relatively slow for its size. I jumped over the railing of the porch into the yard to get a better look. The object was a wing like in the shape of a V or boomerang with lights along both angles. It disappeared from my view in a short time do to trees. It flew south towards elevated railroad tracks about a block away. I thought for sure I would hear something about it in the news because it was such a strange sighting and someone else most likely would have spotted it also. It was after dark and its flight was silent, but I find it hard to believe I was the only person that seen it. I don't strongly believe one way or the other whether more UFOs exist. I do know I did see this UFO many years ago. At the time I was a young teenager. I was sober and not under the influence of any medications or drugs of any kind. I talk about this experience rarely because I know what I saw and don't need to convince anyone of it. Only now have I found an organization that may be interested in it and I always thought it should be reported, but didn't know how to go about it.
