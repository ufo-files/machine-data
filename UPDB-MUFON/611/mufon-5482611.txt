{jpg} What I saw was an actual space ship it appeared neon green and just hovered . no noise but vibrated and hovered but went back and forth

I was just a young kid I was 13 years old... my mother was taking me to see a movie later that night but before we left the house I decided to go outside and run and play. We live at the end of a deadend street, about 1/4 of a mile back off a main road. So as I am running I got to the top of the hill and look up and I notice what looked to me like a spaceship as big a 2 football fields. it was just hovering not making a sound. I watched it for a long time maybe 1/2 an hour but I soon noticed it was vibrating and kinda moving backwards and fowards.the property I lived on was where the plantation pipeline was in the process of building a new pipeline across country and it just happened to run right throuh out property. It was neon green, the shape of what I have always known to be a spaceship.. Looked like the one from the TV show Lost in Space except it was this different shaded neon green.. but different shaded , I mean the portholes and windows on it were neon green as well but just a slightly different shade of neon green.. At 13 years old I tried to rationalize it and figure out how did it appear as it did... I tried to tell myself that it was just a reflection but what would it reflect off of... I watched it for 30 minute but as a kid I got tired and showed my mother.. I left it sitting where it was and went on to the movie never watched it take off.. but it was gone when we came home..... I have always had weird dreams and visions of people comeing through the windos at me.. but I have never been afraid of what I saw ,, just courious I never got any kind of feeling that anything was going to hurt me
