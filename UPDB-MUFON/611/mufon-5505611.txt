{rjl} saw huge lighted craft hovering then move off to west

I went out onto back porch to have a smoke, and i saw a very bright light through the trees. I was sure it was not the moon, so i moved around a bit to get a better view. It was definitely moving, and the lights were white with an orange tint to them at times. As the light got brighter, i could see that the object was enormous and triangular in shape. The lights faded out within a minute or so, maybe one at a time. It appeared to be hovering over the vicinity of the Olympia airport, and it was moving in a westwardly direction. The skin had a orangeish rust color from what i could tell in the glow of its lights.
