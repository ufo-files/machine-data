It was Mid-March 2014. I was out having a smoke (live in a non-smoking apartment complex)and looked up (towards the eastern direction) as I always do to see the clouds (we have beautiful clouds) and spotted what I thought might be a plane. I noticed it was different. It was moving slowly and was to low to be a Jet-plane and had no sound. It was cigar shaped with a cigar type band of symbols, which may have been a reddish or orange color ad reflected a silver-ish color. I made notice of no wings, no tail, no front (like pointy Plane nose) but flat ended like a cigar. I proceeded to get out my phone from my pocket. It entered a cloud so I snapped a picture of the cloud and the area to the right that should be its exit place but nothing exited. I waited but after 5 minutes still nothing. It was gone. It was daylight, about four in the afternoon, the blue sky was all with big puff balls type clouds sparsely slowly floating. I see many planes and choppers. This was not something I have seen before. I am 65 and seen many types of aircraft. I have lived in many states and lived near bases. This was not a blimp or a jet. Not my first UFO but was ecstatic that I saw this type.
