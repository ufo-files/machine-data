9 big stars flying from north to south in very hight speed

In that night i was waiting for bloody moon. At night from 27th September to 28th September 2015 was annouced we will have in Slovakia ideal visibility for observe bloody moon. And because at that time i had new Nikon D610 i was shooted almost everything. 
Because i had short teleobjective Nikkor 180mm i had limited options to catch moon in full frame.
But i was waiting for best moment, camera setings was for moon, so pictures from anything others will be very dark. 
At aprox 3:44:22 group of 9 lights was uncover behind high block building on the right side and with unbelivable speed flying to the south. Because i had my camera a front of me everything what i can do was bring it to my hand and without any focus (focused to moon) shoot 5 pics in row. After this shots, objects are continue in flight but i cannot see more because on the left side of window is huge pine tree. Because this scared me a lot, i woke up my mother and tell it to her. On camera was nothing, just black screen (because settings for moon) I always shoot into RAW, at the morning i was add in software more exposition and on all 5 pictures i have 9 light objects in row. 
In attachment you have one picture from Camera RAW, you can see, you must add exposure in software. Others is RAW files, you can analyze them how you like it.
