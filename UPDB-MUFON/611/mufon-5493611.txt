{dr}ROUND LOOKED CLEAR OUTSIDE REFLECTED LIKE MIRROR YET COULD SEE SOMETHING IN MIDDLE HOVERED THEN FLEW INTO CLOUD DISAPEARED

I WAS SITTING ON MY PORCH SWING AND DECIDED TO LAY DOWN ON IT.I LOOKED UP AND NOTICED SOMETHING JUST BELOW AND TO THE LEFT OF A CLOUD THAT LOOKED LIKE A LARGE PERFECT ROUND BUBBLE I STOOD UP LOOKING AT IT WONDERING WHAT IT WAS, TO BIG AND HIGH TO BE A BUBBLE. MY HAIR STOOD ON END AS I REALIZED IT WAS REFLECTING THE SKY AND I SAW SOMETHING IN THE MIDDLE SMALL RECTANGULAR COULD HAVE BEEN A REFLECTION OF MY HOUSE. THEN IT FLEW STRAIGHT INTO THE CLOUD AND IT SEAMED TO DISAPPEAR.I LOOKED ALL AROUND COULDN'T SEE IT ANYMORE. IT VERY WELL COULD HAVE BLENDED WITH THE CLEAR BLUE SKY. AFTER TELLING MY HUSBAND ABOUT IT WHEN HE GOT HOME HE SUGGESTED I REPORT IT.I WISH I KNEW WHAT IT WAS.
