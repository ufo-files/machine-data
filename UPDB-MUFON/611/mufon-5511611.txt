{cak} Hovering silver disc in OK

Driving down SH 4 getting ready to get on Kilpatrick Turnpike Spur in OK between Blanchard and Tuttle. I was looking up at the signs making sure I was in the correct lane and saw a silver disc hovering toward my right. I looked back and forth at it 5-6 times, trying to figure out if it was a reflection or a highway lamp, etc. The last time I looked it was just gone. It was flat, silver, no lights. This was about 1 PM - nice day, I think it was sunny.
