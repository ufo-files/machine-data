{kt}Witnessed large triangular shaped craft with 3 lights at the tips.

We were driving south on I-5 from Burbank, CA to I-10. The driver and passenger in the front of the car saw the object first and then I witnessed it a few seconds later out of the back window. It was a triangular shaped craft with 3 lights on the tips of the craft. The craft seemed to flip or shift around effortlessly. It was on the East side of I-5 just above the highest treeline. The craft appeared to be about 90' in between lights.
