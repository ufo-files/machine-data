red-orange light over KSC

I live, and have lived all my life, less than 15 miles from the launch pads at Cape Canaveral. One night around 9pm i stepped out of my house to smoke a cigarette, and facing east i saw the strangest light in the sky.i thought it was a chinese lantern at first, but it did not flicker at all, and its elevation never changed . i have both seen and set off chinese fire lanterns and i am 100% certain this was not a lantern. the weather was not bad, but the sky was not clear. The object was south east of me, about the size of a aspirin at arms length, and there was just something strange about the way it gave off its light, and i couldnt stop looking at it. then it passed behind the tree in my neighbors yard and i realized it was moving north. it continued on its path, i wanted to run inside to get my phone to attempt a video, but i was afraid to take my eyes off of it and lose it. eventually i had to walk down to the road and move south down the street to be able to watch it continue north behind more trees blocking my view from it. then it stopped, faded out, then about 2-3 seconds later it pulsed back on for about 5 seconds, then back out again two more times. the next day i went back out side to re walk it and think about it and it realized that, the position at which this object stopped and hovered then eventually turned on and off 3 times and vanished, would have been somewhere around the northern border of Nasa's property. there was a rocket launch scheduled around this time, but they had been scrubbed a few times.
