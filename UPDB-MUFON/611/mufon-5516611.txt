(GS) round metalic disc with dome in center

I was standing on my second floor balcony taking still pictures of an awesome storm rolling in when I first saw the object decend from between two massive clouds. I couldnt believe what I was seeing so I searched to see if anyone around was witnessing the same thing, but there was nobody else around. I looked up to see the object again, only to catch a blur of it speeding away. I dismissed the whole event until I realized I had captured it on film later that evening as I was reviewing all the pictures I has taken that day.
