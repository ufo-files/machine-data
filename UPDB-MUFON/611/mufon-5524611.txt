Triangular UFO on a straight path with 4 orange lights. 3 at the ends and 1 in the center.

I was at my living room's window smoking a cigarette when 4 dull orange lights caught my attention, travelling on a straight line. I immediately thought that it was a UFO. There were no blinking lights. They were "steady" but "dimmed". The object was travelling at what seemed about 80 Km/h. The object was travelling through some clouds, but i never lost sight of it's 4 lights. I couldn't see the full shape of the object, nor it's real dimensions. I interpreted that 3 lights were at it's edges and 1 at it's center. Shivers ran through my body. I was surprised i was seeing what i was seeing. A real UFO. I called my girlfriend, who was preparing dinner in the kitchen, but she didn't arrive in time to see it. I lost contact with the object when it went through some clouds, while continuing on a straight path.
