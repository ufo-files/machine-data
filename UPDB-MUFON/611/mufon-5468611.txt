Big bright star like object, bigger than a star hovered then zoomed off at never before seen speed

The light I saw was too big to be an aeroplane light. It was definately bigger than a star which was the reason I focussed in on it straight away after looking up. It didnt flash. Id say it was about 3-4x as big as a star. Almost as soon as I clapped eyes on it, it literally took off over some houses that were about 3 seconds away from where I was standing.
I just know what I saw was something completely crazy. Ive never witnessed anything like this.
I think what got me was the speed it zoomed off although im adament it was not a comet. It was either hovering when I saw it or moving very slowly.... there was no engine noise. It was almost directly overhead.
