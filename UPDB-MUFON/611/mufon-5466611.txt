Two of them looked like Mars only brighter and the other was white

18138 Edgar Pl.
I was sitting on the back porch about 9:45 to 10:00PM when I first saw the two red star like orbs. They seemed to be very high in the sky like satilite height. They were about 35 and 50 degrees up in the western sky. They did nothing but sit there for like 10 to 15 minutes. Then the one higher in the sky started drifting off to the south. After the upper red UFO stopped the lower UFO burst then reformed just to the south. After a couple of more minutes I saw the third UFO come into view. It came into view like a satilite does in the night sky. The white UFO then fired a white pulse of light at the upper red UFO. The white UFO took what look like a attack run at the red UFO. That's when the red UFO repeatedly fired on the white UFO. The white UFO exploded. The two red UFO stayed in the sky for several more minutes before they phased out. They did not fly away, they faded.
