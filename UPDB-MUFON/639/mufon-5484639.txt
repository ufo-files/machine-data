It was 2010. I was 12 at the time. I finally decided to come out with my story after my dads friend told about his UFO experience. Me and my father just finished watching a movie he asked me to take out the trash. I walked outside grabbed the trash cans then made my way up the driveway. I reached the top of the driveway placed the trash cans down as I turned to walk back inside I noticed out of the corner of my eye 5 blues lights perfectly placed apart spinning in a circle manner. The lights then started to come towards me. Something came over me.. Something I will never forget. I couldn't move. My body was in a trance like state. It got to the point to where it was right over me. Then out of no were it vanished. I ran faster then I ever had in my entire life back inside. I couldn't even speak to my parents I was so scared. After awhile I started to calm down I told my parents what I saw. They didn't believe me. For the next 3 years I had trouble sleeping. It was truly an awful experience. I really want some answers. I have so many questions. Thank you for reading my story. This type of study interest me greatly.
