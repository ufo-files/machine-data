Orange lights, t-shaped objects appeared from the western sky

Orange lighted glowing, t-shaped objects appeared from the western sky and moved with no clear path to the northeast as they disappeared from sight. They did not move in a straight line rather slowly darting in an apparent path to the northeast. These nine objects caught both my eye and that of my wife, Esther. They did not appear all at once, more like one or two at a time. They had a weird orange glow. They were not planes, large or small. They could have been helicopters but I don't think so. Helicopters that I have observed in the past did not move or have the appearance that these objects had. This observance lasted no more than ten minutes. It was an odd experience which has left a question mark in my thoughts as well as that of my wife.
