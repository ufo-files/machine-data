HOVERING THEN FLYING AROUND THEN HOVERING THEN SPLIT AND HOVERED

OUR FAMILY WAS DRIVING INTO LONDON,ON TO THE HOSPITAL WITH FALSE LABOUR. AS WE GOT CLOSE TO COLONEL TALBOT ROAD, LONDON WE SAW WAS LOOKED LIKE A JACK-O-LATERN FACE IN THE SKY ON THE LEFT SIDE OF THE HIGHWAY. WE JUST STARED AT IT LIKE WHAT THE HECK IS THAT. I ASKED MY HUSBAND WHAT IT WAS AND HE WASNT SURE WHAT WE SAW. IT LOOKED LIKE FIRE SPHERES IN THE SKY. IT HOVERED TO THE RIGHT SIDE OF THE HIGH WAY LIKE NOTHING AND SPLIT INTO SEVERAL OTHER THINGS THAT NO LONGER LOOKED LIKE FIRE, IT LOOKED LIKE HOVERING LIGHTS. IN THIS MOMENT IT DAWNED ON ME TO TAKE A PHOTO. I WISH I HAD CAPTURED IT ON VIDEO BUT I WAS SO IN AWE OF THE EVENT IT DIDNT EVEN COME TO MIND. I AM SUBMITTING MY PHOTO OF THE OBJECTS AS THEY HAD PARTED INTO SEVERAL OBJECTS. WHEN I LOOKED AT THE IMAGE IT LOOKED TO BE A SHAPE BUT THE SHAPE COULD NOT BE SEEN WITH THE EYE. VERY COOL EXPERIENCE MY HUSBAND, TEENAGE SON, DAUGHTER AND I WONT EVER FORGET.
