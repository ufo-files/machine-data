I saw a cigar shaped ufo fly overhead

I was sitting on the swing in the backyard and I saw a big plane fly overhead flying from southwest to northeast and then about five to ten minutes later I saw a big cigar shaped ufo fly overhead at around 30,000 feet or so from north to south almost where the plane was that I saw earlier. It moved fast and I didn't have time to get my camera, I wish I would have had time to get it. It stayed at the same altitude and just flew by at a fast rate of speed, no lights and no sound.
