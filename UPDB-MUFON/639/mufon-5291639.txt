A white two tiered disc appeared on a cloudless day

I was videotaping the surfers at Surf Beach in Laguna Beach, Ca. from the balcony of the house we rent on Gaviota. When I viewed the video later, I noticed a bright, white, two tiered disc like object at the top right of my picture. It seemed to fade in and out. The sky was cloudless. It was very bright white. It came into my picture about three times, yet completely disappeared in between. It seemed to move slightly SW. Again, no clouds that day.
