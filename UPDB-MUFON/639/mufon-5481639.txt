Lights moving opposed to meteor shower. No strobes. High altitude. Fast moving.

I was watching the meteor shower and I noticed a light moving from south to north as I was looking east. The light had no strobes, nor did it have a trail. It appeared high in the sky, and was moving fast, it took maybe twenty seconds to disappear from view. This was repeated three more times. I went into the garden facing west. I saw two more lights, the same as the first. They travelled from east to west travelling under Cassiopeia. The clouds came over and I returned inside.
