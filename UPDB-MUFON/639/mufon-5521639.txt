Extremely bright. Pulsated white lights. Slowly moved to my right then vanished

My sister and I were driving down the highway going back home when I noticed it in the sky. It was beautiful, and flashed a white light, pulsated for a quick second, slowly hovered to my right and then disappeared. It was my first sighting, and even though it was not that crazy of one, it left me in awe.
