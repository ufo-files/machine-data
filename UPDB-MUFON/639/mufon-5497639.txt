Me and my husband were standing outside our balcony and all of the sudden a bright object in the sky just appeared and seemed to be moving rather quickly. It was going back and fourth, straight up into the sky, and then falling back down at unseemly high speeds. It had a red light and a green light rotating between colors and then the light became white! When we first saw it we thought it was just a plane but then it would suddenly stop and drop low and planes don't do that. The neighbors that also saw it said it didn't seem right. we felt confused at what it could be it seems to get closer and further within seconds apart. we finally lost sight of it when it dropped down really low behind trees and didn't see it again after that. While watching this happen we felt as if it was unreal and we definitely knew it wasn't just a plane, they can't move in those directions at a rapid speed! Can someone explain this phenomenon? And what is the significance?
