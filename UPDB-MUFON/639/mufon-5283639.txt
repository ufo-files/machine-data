Green light streaked across sky

At 17:50 GMT on Tuesday 14th December 2021, a bright white light of similar shape and velocity to a satellite streaked across the sky (only larger, similar to how the ISS would look) assumed to be a satellite/ISS. One minute later, a green light of the same shape, size and velocity to this, following the same flight path also streaked across the sky. Sparse cloud cover but object visible for duration of sighting. Covered my arc of vision (almost 180 degrees) from South to North in less than 3 seconds. Unable to get a photograph as it was that fast and had disappeared before I realised.
