30 or more tiny dots of light flying around each other

12:00am to 01:30am. Tiny dots of white light in the distance (similar to a satellite but smaller and not as bright) SW and SE, sometimes with both areas active at the same time, flying around each other in different directions changing direction and flying very fast. 

At one point there was a slightly larger blob of red and green coloured light that the tiny lights dropped out of, they seemed to merge and separate and kind of dance around each other. on three occasions the entire nights sky flashed bright for a split second and I also viewed a shooting star directly above the SW area that they were flying in. They were too dim to film unfortunately which is frustrating but it was an amazing experience and the first time I have seen this sort of activity.
