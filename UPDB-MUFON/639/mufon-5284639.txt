Trapezoid light formation appears heading SW and then disappears

I was aligning my telescope around 8-830pm on 10/22/21 and witnessed with my naked eyes a trapezoidal formation of lights briefly appear about 10 degrees to the left of Vega, move southwest about 1-2 degrees and then disappear. The duration was no greater than 2 seconds. The size of the formation appeared to be slightly larger than the moon, maybe .75 degree. The lights were very small, blue-white and star-like in appearance. No sound or anything else to note. The skies were perfectly clear and wind was calm.
