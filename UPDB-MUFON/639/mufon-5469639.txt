Con trail from right hand engine on passenger jet engine appeared to fall off plane con trail dropped downward with object . Object hovered no con trail then very slowly moved of to the west out of sight

I was out in talking to a neighbor we looked up at a passing passenger jet we lived on the flight path . Not a cloud in the sky perfect day.we both saw the passenger jet without any con trail. 
Suddenly a con trail came from the right hand side engine area we thought it strange we continued to watch then it looked like the engine fell off with the con trail dropping down with the object. The jet carried on its way the object slowed then stopped no con trail now, then it slowly moves to the west and out of sight. There were no other object in the sky previous to this event . What it was has been a total mystery to me and my friend . It was not a balloon a helicopter or another plane we are and have been baffled as to what we saw
