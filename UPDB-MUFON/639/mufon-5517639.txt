{ck} Fire in the sky

Liberty MO. July 02 2012, I was sitting on my front porch looking at the sky, being an amateur astronomer for 45 years; well that1s what I do. At 10:15 pm I noticed a bright light coming up from the east behind the trees, like a helicopter spot light. But it had a strange tint to it (amber with orange glow) like it was behind a fire, my first thought. Well it was getting brighter (closer) and when it cleared the trees for a lack of words it looked like a flying Flame! So I rubbed my eyes to get the best look I could. It was in a lazy left turn (my left) and the only clear sky I had was the width of the house across the street. It seemed about 2000 feet up and less than a mile away. It was very bright, hard to look at bright. The object itself was a sphere/orb it was emitting an aura that looked like a plasma flame. I don1t know what it is but for now I will call it plasma. Now very close to the orb the color was a very bright blue/white and as the plasma moved away from the orb it took on an amber color. The plasma seemed to go about 75% upward, 50% forward, 50% backward, and 25% downward in the shape of a flame. I could see what looked like interference lines in the plasma and seemed to be in motion (twinkle). This whole thing seemed to be surrounded by a containment field that glowed orange (like when you put your hand on a plasma ball-lamp). The speed it was going was about the same as a conventional aircraft and was silent. It left my view heading to the NNE direction, Mosby and Excelsior Springs area. That1s my 2 minutes with it. My opinion is this was a display 1Look at me1 kind of thing. Also as it left I could hear a jet in a turn west of me, like KCI was diverting traffic away from the area.
