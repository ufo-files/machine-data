{tjd} V Shaped Craft, 5 white lights underneath, glowing all around

I was driving into Medina on 224 from my home in West Salem. Just before getting into the main part of town I saw a craft flying low cutting across 224. It was V shaped, had 5 white lights underneath that were constantly on -- there was no blinking. It was pretty large -- plane sized or slightly larger. 

Surrounding the entire craft was a soft misty light, but I couldn't tell where the source was. It was a different kind of light than the ones underneath. It was more muted and hazy but surrounded the craft top to bottom, and all sides. 

When I drove underneath the craft, I could see all 5 lights in a V formation, and there was nothing in the middle. There were extra green lights that were above and slightly below the craft. 

I'm not sure if this was some kind of military drone. It was quite large, and I doubt that it was civilian owned.
