Unexplained light blue light in the sky hovering.

On the night of 5/21/2020 I observed a light blue light visible in the clouds. There were sharp edges of 90 degree. Only the light was visible and only during the cloud cover. When the clouds would clear for a minute or so, there was nothing there. No light or edges. The light was only visable with the cloud cover. I watched it for 20 mins. I called a friend who also went outside and seen it as well. I did see an aircraft in the sky at a much higher altitude than the light.I was unable to video.
