{nd} Light rising over Pike's Peak getting brighter, then less bright, and descending, second light appeared, acted similar

I was standing on my porch when I saw a white light above Pikes Peak. As I watched, it rose up, and started getting brighter (for about one second), then less bright. When it started to descend, I saw another one, lower than the first. The first light seemed to hover near a cloud, then I lost it, and the other one. Within a minute or two, I found it again, it did the same thing, rising and getting brighter then less bright. this time, it continued to ascend until I could not see it anymore. I have video, but did not catch the part where the lights got brighter. I can't attach video because my computer is down, and I am using someone else's computer to report.
