Light high in sky, flashing changing directions, cem trail plain flew past during sighting, object was way higher and vanishes when plane got near

Was laying in park w my pitbulls. Sky is clear sun is 🌞! There was a flashing light way up high. To make sure it was not a jet or satellite I watched its movement. It was still, then went towards south! Then it stopped moving and light was glowing brighter then softer, then went north. A cem trail jet was in the air near it and was so high could barely see jet but cem trail was pumping. The object was way higher then the jet and was all light vs jet you could see the aircraft. Pretty miraculous.
