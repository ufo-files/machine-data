very loud rumbling noise, white light.

It could have been a plane, but it was flying way too low to make such a rumbling noise. Plane fly around here all the time, but not that low to make that kind of sound. I was in my room, upstairs and it sounded like it was flying over the house. I went to the window and looked out, all i could see was this little white light in the distance. It blinked in a line, what looked to be three lights going across, all three white. It was really far in the distance by the time I got the picture, but the sound carried on for about 6 minutes.
