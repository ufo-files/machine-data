{cak} SAW RED ORB HOVERING OVER HOME FOR A MINUTE THEN MOVED WEST

On January 11, 2017 at approximately 2028 hours( 8:28 PM) I was watching channel 35-1 ion when the antenna signal went out. After couple of seconds of turning chanels I noticed that all Chanel's had no signal on their screen. I decided to exit my home and check the antenna. When I got out from the front door and looked up, I did not see anything wrong with the antenna, but when I glanced to the south west of my homes roof I observed a red orb like object that was approaching very steady and at a fast speed. Within seconds the red orb got to the center top of my home and hovered for about 30 seconds pulsating very fast different shades of red color light. at the end of the 30 seconds it started moving up and west with the same fast speed and no sound that it came... I didn't have enough time to pick up a camera or even run inside to tell my son and wife. This will be the 3rd time that I have seen at a very close distance a red orb type of flying object over my home...
