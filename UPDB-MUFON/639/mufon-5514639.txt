{dr}Two lights following same flight path, thought they were airplanes, but they ascended

I was on the patio with my friend talking. I am in a flight path for airplanes so I thought there was a plane flying towards the airport, but the light was orange and not white like the normal landing lights. I thought there was also red and green lights on either side of the orange light, but I am not sure. Then I asked my friend to take a look at this plane. At this point the first object began to ascend. Then my friend pointed out a second object that was doing the same thing as the first. It also appeared to be ascending because the light kept getting smaller and eventually faded out. I felt no nervousness and reacted very calmly. My friend expressed concern and thought it this was pretty crazy, but didn't have much anxiety from it.
