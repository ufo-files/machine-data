a friend and I were on skidoos about 9 or 10 miles out of 100 mile house b.c. in the woods. we ended up in the middle of a 10 acre field behind were my friend lived and decided to take a break lean back on the seats to look at the stars for the sky was so clear and could observe the millions of stars with no trees In our field of vision so there was a panoramic view. we noticed about a dozen asteroids burning through the atmosphere then I saw a different but almost the same shape and color but a little larger object that didn't burn out and was traveling at such a high speed shoot across the sky from one end of our view to the center right above us. without slowing down the object stopped instantly for about 10 seconds then circled counter clockwise slowly that took about 10 seconds and stopped in the exact position it started to circle from, stayed in that position for about another 10 seconds then instantly took off at a very high rate of speed in the same direction it was traveling. a straight line from south to north until it was out of site. my unprofessional view is that the object was traveling at a speed of between 50 thousand and 100 thousand mph.
