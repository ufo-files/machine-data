{rjl} falling streak of light that turned a brilliant green

At approximately 1:17 am Central Time, early Tuesday morning 1/20/15, I observed a green fireball while looking to the WSW from my vantage point in South Lincoln, NE. A streak of white light, then a brilliant green before it vanished. The downward angle was a little less than 45 degrees. I was looking over a neighbor's garage roof line and if the slant of the roof was 45 degrees, the "line" the falling object made was less than that. It appeared to be falling from a South to North direction (although that is a guess). Very fast; it was over before one could say "one thousand one."
