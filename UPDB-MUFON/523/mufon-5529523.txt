UFO over the night sky in Conyers, GA

On October 19, 2010 around 9:30 p.m. and 10:15 p.m. I was riding in the car with my best friend when I saw a circular, round, shaoed object in the sky. It had tons of lights at the base of the object in different colors. We live in an apartment complex and could see the object as clear as day! It was far up in the sky yet there was a relection of silver or metal coming from it. It was not an airplane, hellicopter or any other flying vehicle that I ever saw. I yelled to my best friend to "LOOK"! She did. She was astonished like I was. As we were leaving the complex, the object began to take off, like a jet almost, then we lost sight of it. Once we got to the intersection of Dogwood Drive and McDonough Dr. it appeared again from behind some business complexes near Cracker Barrel Resturant. I was petrified! We continued to watch it as it disappeared again behind the darkness/clouds. It just went away. I believe that I/we saw a real UFO. I can not explain it...
