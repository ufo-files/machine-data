{kt}Green light shot across sky then stopped. I turned toward it and it shot off to the west disappearing from view.

Driving west on Stanton street in West Olive Michigan a flash of light to the left of my vehicle caught my attention- I looked up just in time to see this green ball finish shooting to the west toward lake Michigan and behind some trees. I thought to myself that I just seen the biggest, brightest, greenest looking shooting star-I was amazed. The object was faster than any aircraft I had ever seen..like I said it shot like a shooting star. I then got to the end of Stanton St. and at lake Michigan I turned left(south) onto Lake Shore Dr. As I completed my turn I was shocked to notice the object was probably 100' above the trees and about 200' in front of me hovering over Lake Shore Dr. I drove toward it for about 2 seconds then it shot toward the lake and was gone. I just had a feeling of amazement, I had never seen an object glow like that or have the ability to move from a standstill to so fast I could not believe. It was a green glowing sphere/Oval I could see a solid outline of the object but the green glow was amazing, the only way I can describe it is as a mist of light around the object. The cloud/mist of light was being emitted about 10-15' out from the entire craft and was constantly moving outward but maintained a perfect round shape around the object.
