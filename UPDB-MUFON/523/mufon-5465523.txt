{jpg} Hovering, vibrational humming, abruptly took off out of sight at high speed

It was the spring of 1989, my mother, younger brother and I were returning home from my grandmother1s where I live now. I was 9 years old, we noticed something hovering and stopped where a building known as monadnock fruit was at the time where Swanzey Fact Road intersects Route 101. The triangular craft had 3 white lights at the tips of the triangle and 1 red in the center. We watched the craft hover for a few minutes while it emitted a powerful hum. The center red light grew brighter in intensity then the craft took off abruptly out of sight at an alarmingly high rate of speed. We sped home in Marlborough NH, at the bottom of our neighborhood hill on Canada street we observed two amber lights blinking back and forth over mount monadnock. We stopped and looked, noticed a neighbor who appeared to be watching as well, my mother rolled her window down and asked the man if he was seeing this too and he dismissed us and went inside. Recently I watched a documentary about Michael Schratt and after obsessing about this since childhood I have the clarity that this may have been a TR3B sighting. My brother and I also as children saw a B2 stealth bomber in the late 801s as well as a F-117, well before they were public. It is my personal belief that these craft could of originated from Pease Air Force base as throughout my life we have witnessed many military aircraft coming from that direction while at my parents house in Marlborough NH.
