{cak} I was watching a plane and observed a small orb following it from underneath and then it began climbing to above the airplane.

I was standing on my front porch and was watching to the East and saw the second of what would be five planes going to the North and below the second plane I saw a small ball of light following the plane. I estimate it to be about 200 feet below and about the same behind. The ball was following in a straight line but seemed to move slowly left to right.
After just a minute the ball started climbing in altitude as both craft proceeded North until the ball was above the plane.
At about this time, I lost sight behind the trees. It is kind of exciting to see things in the sky and it is becoming easier to tell the difference between planes, satellites and other craft. I seem to be seeing more in this area.
