{dr}Pulsating light active in southern sky

We observed a pulsating, multi-colored oval shaped object in the southern sky. First noticed at 1 am CT and continued for more than 2 hours. There were no stars near it. Object appeared to move very slightly from left to right during 10 min video (movement of the earth, perhaps?) There appeared to be thousands of lights inside the object and outside appeared to be glowing multi-colors, brightest being orange/red. A similar object has been observed by this writer on 7/7/07 in Trout Lake, Washington with resultant 'light' visitation. 

We have some photographs of this object, but our hands were shaky, so object appeared to 'jiggle'. We then used a tripod for photos and video and could see it more clearly. 

First thought was that it may have been a comet or meteorite ... but didn't seem to move quickly. We looked at Heavens Above to eliminate the ISS and satellites. ISS was nowhere near Arkansas last night. 

Object was not a plane, helicopter, bird or bug -- too high in the sky to be bugs in front of the lens. Planes have blinking lights, but they move across the sky quickly; this object appeared to barely move from left to right.

Our first reaction upon noticing the object was to grab binoculars, then a spotting scope, even a telescope. Although we could clearly see the object, there was no way for us to photograph with clarity.

It also appeared to be ejecting something from time to time, but can't be certain because it was pulsating constantly.
