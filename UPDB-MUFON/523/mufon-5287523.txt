small object coming towards us, flew behind 1 story building, then flew off..

Dark night at grandparents ranch in NE. With my 2 cousins. Saw an object out in pasture that I assumed was airplane (I was visiting family as a 11 year old, I lived in CA and live in a flight path so knew what airplanes looked like) So this appeared to be an airplane that was far off because it was small, but awfully low. Then it came right up towards a 1 story corral building & seemed to hide behind. It was I guess what you call cigar shaped. long & flat and lights on it, I'm not positive of the colors of lights but I don't think they blinked, just lights all along the object. That's when I realized it was actually a small object NOT a plane far away. We stood in amazement as it then came out from behind the building into a clear space before then darting behind a 2 story barn. At this point I was getting nervous because I just couldn't get over the size (Now we would say it looked like a drone but we sure didn't know about drones in 1977) I didn't see it fly away & at that point we ran. I am no longer close to the other 2 cousins because of this current political climate so I did not include their names. I remember after this I had a lot of night terrors there. But never did I attribute it to the sighting. Being on a 9000 acre ranch you are really in the middle of nowhere & that in itself is scary at night to an 11 year city girl. The thing that just kept popping up in my brain & was so hard to process is the only thing in my world at the time that had lights & flew was an airplane. so that's what I thought it was...but then when it came so close to where we were standing and was simply able to "duck" behind the 1st building..and it did seem to be playing with us...like peek-a-boo & I kept thinking "how can a plane become little & hid behind the corral building?" that just blew my mind.   (sorry I didn't know what elevation in degrees would be) but it was probably 12 feet off ground?  also the city I reported is just the biggest city nearby. the actual city (town) is so small I don't want to say because of my current relationship to the family.
