{cak} U.F.O.came from East,moving West turned North and passed over me.

I was on my way to Big Wheel resturant,traveling South on Shadeland Ave.I saw what looked like the window lights of an airplane,going left (East) to right (West)and desending,like it was comming in to land at airport.When it got above Shadeland Ave.it followed Shadeland Ave.,a 90 degree turn.When it got closer the square windows look like red flame but I got the impression of figures in some of the windows,but the lights from windows was so bright that I could not look at them for more than a nano second.When the object passed over me,I tried to look up,but my eyes burned and watered.The air was heavy and there was no sound.Ilost sight of the object when the traffic moved and so did I.It made my hair on my neck and arms stand up.
