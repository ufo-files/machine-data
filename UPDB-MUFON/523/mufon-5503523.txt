{nd} Standing outside house between 9-9:40 pm. Starless evening. Saw one object that looked unusual rise up from behind trees. firework like red lights appeared like fireworks ascended vertically then over 75 red lights ascended west.

Standing outside on landline phone with a family member. I first saw a unusual bright light ascending vertically. Then over 75 red lights ascended from this object. I thought that what I was seeing were fireworks. Until the lights started moving in formation westward. I yelled for my son to bring his cell phone and who took a video. It was the most frightening and unexplained thing I have ever seen. My hands are still shaking and I still cannot explain what I saw. My son and husband also came out to see and cannot explain.
