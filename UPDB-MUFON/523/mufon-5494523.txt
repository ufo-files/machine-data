{rjl} observed a sun like object following behind me.

it was around 1 am in june 1979, i was on my way home from work.usually there was very little traffic on my way home. i was riding my motorcycle when i noticed a funny looking light in my mirrors, i slowed down and looked back, the light was big and bright and in the air not on the road. i stopped in the middle of the road, when i looked back , it was hovering above me, it looked like the sun, i had shut my motorcycle off and i could not hear any noises, i was in a area with no houses or buildings around for a few miles. it just stayed in one place for a few seconds then it just vanished, it shook me up pretty good, i started my bike and flew home, i have only told a very few people about this, still do not know what it was.
