{kt}Observed slow moving red/pink pulsing light moving from south to north.

I stepped out to get some air and saw a light approach. Only observed about a minute of objects flight. A reddish pulsing light moved slowly from the south to the north. Not able to ascertain the altitude, but it seemed to be rather low. It looked like a paper lantern but speed was constant and there was no deviation in direction. The light seemed to flicker like a flame. I lost visual of light behind tall trees. Came online to see if anyone else has reported seeing it. The report from Orlando, FL is VERY similar to what I witnessed.
