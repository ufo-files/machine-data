Glowing yellow lights observed hovering for 20 to 30 seconds then disappeared.

On 11th June 2014 at 8:14pm me and some of my friends were at a roadside food corner eating some street food, the one of my friend shouted "What is that? and we all looked towards the northern sky. It was a clear day without clouds and we observed 8 to 10 glowing objects in the sky glowing in a sequence. It hovered there for few seconds and disappeared. I have clicked some pictures with my phone but the pictures were not clear enough. Lot of people witnessed the event and even cars and trucks in the highway stopped to see the object. After I reach home I heard fighter jets from the nearby air base circling the area for almost an hour.
