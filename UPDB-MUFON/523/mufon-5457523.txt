{cak} Flashing lights

I was out walking my dog on a chilly October night it was about 8:00-8:30. All of a sudden the sky lit up like lighting at first that's what it was. I looked up in to the West where it was the brightest and in the middle of it all I saw was a solid dark object. And it stopped and then it flashed again but this time it was just the north of me it took only a few seconds to get that far. This time i counted the seconds between the flashes I counted 22 seconds. I wanted to capture this on my camera so I took my dog in the house and grabed my phone but by the time I got back outside it was gone. I never mentioned that sighting to anyone but my family they think I'm making it up, so I never mentioned it again until now. I was wondering if you had anybody report a familiar situation. Thank you for listening.
