west of town, enjoying scenery. Very large reddish cigar shaped object emerged from behind butte. Knew immediately it was a u.f.o. First one hovered motionless for a few minutes, then exterior suddenly changed from solid color to silver fish like scales. Seconds after this, the object moved west and disappeared in less than a second. We were dumbfounded. As we were watching the westerly sky, another object exactly like the first, appeared from behind the butte in the exact same place, and repeated the same scenario as the first. We waited for a few minutes, and when nothing more happened we immediately drove into town and told our friends what we had seen. We found no one who saw the objects but us four.
