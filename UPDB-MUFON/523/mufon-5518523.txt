Two starlike blinking objects dancing in NW sky

Tonight I walked out onto my porch to see if I could see anymore of the blinking lights in the sky and I saw two!!! They were both parallel to each other about 35-40 degrees from the horizon in the North Western sky. They stayed stationary for about 10 minutes and then alternated on one rising and the other lowering below the rooftops. They danced like that for about 35 minutes before both lowering below the rooftops to where I could not see them. They both blinked red, white, green, and blue. Red and blue seeming to be the most used. The one on the left would blink through the colors quickly while the one on the left blinked a little bit slower. After I saw the objects the temperature around me got a lot warmer and I started to sweat. Not too happy about that part of it. I also couldnt get video or photos as I only have a camera phone and it wont pick them up at all... I invite anyone out here to see them on a clear night though!!!!
