{dr}First thought was a star but it was 10x larger than any other star in the sky.

6 am was walking dogs and like always I looked up in the sky and did a double take. At first I thought it was a star but this thing was huge. I would say aprox. 10x the size of any other star in the sky (maybe 10th the size of a full moon). The object didn't move at all. It just stayed stationary for good solid 5 minutes. I would say it was 4+ miles away from where I was standing. I then had to go inside and get ready for work but the object remained in the same location.
