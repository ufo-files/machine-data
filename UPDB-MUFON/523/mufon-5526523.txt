Saw what looked like a short alien being in neighbor's back yard.

At 3:00 AM I let my boxer Buster out. I was standing in the doorway waiting for him when I saw a little "ET" run across my neighbor's back yard. It was three and a half feet tall with green skin. It had big eyes with a prominent head. The street lights allowed me to see it clearly with few shadows.
