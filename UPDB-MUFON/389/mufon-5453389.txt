Triangular Shaped UFO

While backpacking, my husband, Adrian and I camped at a large, flat clearing near the Verde River about 20 miles outside of Paulden, AZ. The night sky was clear with no cloud cover and the stars were bright. We went to sleep and both woke up at around 3:00 AM. We watched the stars and talked. We noticed falling stars, airplanes and satellites, calling them out as they passed or were observed. At about 3:30 AM on 4/24/20 I noticed a particularly fast moving satellite and said, "Wow! There's one!" and then noticed another "satellite" moving next to it at the same speed and exclaimed, "there's another!" At the same moment, Adrian said, "What the hell? That's a UFO." I simultaneously realised what he saw. We both observed, in a triangular shape, maybe 100 miles above us, moving from the south to the south east, three brightly glowing orbs. They all moved at the same speed, in the same direction as if they were the lights on the outside of a triangular craft. It would have been an enormous space craft. We watched it for maybe 4-5 minutes before the orbs began to dim. It was as if the craft was moving farther and farther away but still right in front of us- just getting deeper in to space until the glow was completely gone. They did not move out of our sight distance. They simply faded into the night sky.
