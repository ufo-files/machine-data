{jpg} Unusual bright object w/appendages, hovering to the SW

I was sitting in my car in a parking lot waiting for other employees to arrive. I saw an unusually bright light. At first, I thought it was a star, but then it appeared to get closer, and then it just stopped and hovered in that spot. At that point, I noticed there were 2 appendages coming off of it, and one of those was forked. They were not straight but were curved. I looked at it for about 15 minutes, and then had to leave. It was too far away to get a decent picture with my phone. It was still there when I left. I was not affected by it, but was curious about it.
