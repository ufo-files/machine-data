Star like object moved overhead, disappeared and reappeared.

As we were standing in my driveway a starlight object began moving overhead from SE to NW. It changed direction and began moving NE. It disappeared. About ten minutes after it disappeared it appeared out of nowhere brighter than it was the first time, following the same flight path in reverse direction. As it got closer to the original sighting location it started to fade and then disappered completely.
