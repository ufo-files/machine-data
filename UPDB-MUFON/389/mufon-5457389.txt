{rjl} Football field length square to rectangular object about 2-3 stories tall

While traveling SW on 385 we circled around on an off ramp to Standing Springs Road and witnessed a football field size square object hovering between the clouds and tree line. We then decided to try and get closer and travelled down West Georgia Road for approximately 3-5 miles until we reached Greenville Technical College campus. We then witnesses the object change shape to two rectangles as it moved away quickly in the direction of Donaldson Center. It was not an illusion! We saw it and we followed it and took a photo and video.
