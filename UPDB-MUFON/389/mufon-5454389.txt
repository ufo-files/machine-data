{rjl} Flew in pairs and groups. 21 counted

3 of us went outside for a smoke at about 920pm and I happened to look up and see 2 lights a ways apart from eachother, flying side by side. Like military pilots do. Never seen normal planes do that before so I kept watching. Next came a belt of 3 in single file order. They looked like orions belt, but flying. A few single lights flew by and more pairs kept going by. We counted 21 total by the time we noticed and when we saw the last one just under 2 hours later. 19 of them were flying south to north and 2 were flying east to west. There were visible airplanes all around them. These lights stood out. No blinking lights, no flashes or anything. They all looked like solid stars. Stars on a march. Almost all going the same way. Same speed. The groups always stuck together.
It seemed like a never ending parade.
