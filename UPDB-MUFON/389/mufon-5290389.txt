I saw a Star-like ufo hovering above the clouds while simultaneously changing colors.

I was flying from Vegas to Chicago on Frontier Airlines which takes a little bit over 3 hours. I occasionally looked out the window the first 30 mins of the flight until I fell asleep. About an hour and a half in I wake and see the beautiful stars but noticed one was lower than the rest. I kept my eyes on the Star-like object and noticed it was moving almost back and forth, then to my surprise it began changing from white to other beautiful colors. (Reddish/orange,Bluish/green&purple) 
This went on for 2 hours which ended my flight and as we're landing in illinois I can still see it in the air.
