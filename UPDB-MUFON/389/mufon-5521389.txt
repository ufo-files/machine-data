Close to dark and was hovering at about 1,500 ft emitting red light and then sliding to the So before ascending

On Jan 14, 2012 at approximately 6:20 PM I went for a walk arounf the South half of the RV park where we are spending the winter on the Colorado River near Parker, AZ. I had covered about 2/3rds of the route when I noticed a bright, reddish light off to west over the river. I at first thought that it might be a planet that I had failed to notice earlier because there was some light cloud cover. But, the clouds slid behind it and a commercial jet was high over head above it. Its height is hard to estimate, maybe 1,200 - 1,500 ft. It was slowly sliding south and gave off no emissions. It did not have any appendiges or emit any sound. It began to ascend as I rounded the corner en route to our 5th wheel. It was now dark.
