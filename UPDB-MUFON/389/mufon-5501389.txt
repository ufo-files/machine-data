{dr}Glowing ball seen by many

In June of 2013 I saw a flaming or glowing large ball moving very slowly from east to west over my house then off to the horizon in the west in the late afternoon . many others saw it in the area that day and it made local news as well as being discussed on Coast to Coast and Ground Zero radio shows that night. I didn't think to report the sighting until last night when I saw another unusual object. The object. from last year I had actually seen before in 2000 when I was in the Army stationed in Bosnia.
