Came from righ side behind me, slowly, stopped above my head for some time, continued in front of me slowly away

I was walking my dog at 10pm , stopped to cross road, looked to my right to see if any traffic was coming then noticed it in the sky coming over a building very big and low. I just stood and watched as it came towards me, it stopped above my head it was completely silent. As was everything else around me, I was stunned it was like an egg shape on its side, I did notice what looked like 3 very dim lights in centre underneath, I remember red yellow but can't b sure of 3 rd colour . I did notice 2 loofed panels flush with body of ship. It began moving again very slow away from me in front of me. At this time I remember looking down at my dog as she hadn't pulled on her lead at all and she was looking up at it too. I looked quickly around for anyone just to shout look but no one was around at all. I walked across the rd as far as I could as train lines and marshes prevented me going further, I watched to it was completely out of sight. Then ran home and phoned everyone .
