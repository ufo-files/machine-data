{cak} Sitting on my deck, my eye caught a bright glowing fire red/orange large circle that was hovering for a while then quickly changed directions and then disappeared.

Sitting on. Deck in my back yard talking with my boyfriend as I looked out into the night sky. I watched the plans fly over for years and were used to them, but this was not a plane. This was a large circular object hovering displaying a bright orange/fire red glow. So bright and steady you could not look away. The object hovered for 2-3 minutes then made sudden changes in movement dropping down and then up and then slowly to the east and then towards us again until the light randomly disappeared after a total of 5 minutes. Completely shocked and speechless by what was seem by my boyfriend and I.
