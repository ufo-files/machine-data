4 flashing lights equally spaced flew from NW to SW in 2 minutes or less

My fiance and I were sitting on the front porch steps facing west at 9:14pm. While looking slightly Northwest From behind a big tree came a flashing white bright light. My first thought was airplane as there is a big airport not far southwest of our home. As I continue to watch it another flashing white bright light appeared from behind the tree, then another and finally a forth. They were equally spaced apart and flashing in the same pattern all moving nsync with one another at the same level roughly equal with the tree line. I was in shock and kept asking my fiance what I was looking at and if he was recording this. We continue to watch the flashing objects as they traveled in a straight line Southwest till they were out of sight all the while staying at the same speed, height and distance apart! I was very excited and confused at what we just witness and although my fiance phone randomly stopped recording in the middle, we were overjoyed to have at 3 of the lights caught on camera.
