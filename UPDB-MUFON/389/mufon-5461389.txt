{cak} Rectangular white light

I noticed a plane landing while I was at a stop light and what I thought was another plane beyond it. The object was longer and wider than the small plane that was landing. It was a rectangular shape and was glowing bright white. It maintained a slow level path and I lost sight of it. Five miles later I seen it again. The rectangle began to shrink rapidly and a gray spot formed in the middle and I watched as it disappeared right before my eyes.
