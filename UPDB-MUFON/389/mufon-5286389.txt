An Angel Being

When I was around 10 years old I woke up to what I thought was my mom calling my name. I remember wincing my eyes when I sat up in bed (like someone had just turned on the lights) I opened my eyes and there was an angel at the foot of my bed.
It was so bright I couldn’t look at it for long periods of time. It resembled angels depicted in art and religious text. It had neither feminine or male features. It was giant and seemed like it was coming from another dimension or open portal. Iit brought this calming, warmth, safe energy that filled the entire room. I’ve never felt anything like it before but if I had to compare it to something I would say it was like how a baby feels inside their mother’s womb.
So I’m trying to look at this angel but its too bright I cant look at it head on. The being starts speaking to me telepathically. I remember the vibrations being so loud and intense in my head when it spoke that I said grabbed my head and said ouch. After that It seemed to lower its vibrations and asked me “is this better.” I heard it clear as day in my head. I responded yes. The beings mouth never moved. It was from then on out a conversation telepathically. The second I thought of a question to ask it, It had already given me the answer. It told me something that night.
