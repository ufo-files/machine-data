{rjl} Star-like light changing direction

I was at a friend's house. I and a group of friends stepped out onto the deck to get some air. The sky was clear and I had looked up to see the stars. I thought the object was a star. It looked like a small point of light in the sky. It stared to move in a circular pattern. It then sped up and started moving in zig zag patterns, then shot in a straight line, and zig zagged again. After doing this a few times, it shot off in a straight line and disappeared. After it disappeared, my friend turned to me and said "did you see it too?" We were shocked and a bit frightened.
