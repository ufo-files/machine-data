{td} Red light on silent, multi-directional aircraft that hovers and descends straight down.

In Saint Albans, WV, my husband and I saw an UFO. It was a solid red light visible in the sky to the South with a sporadic blinking green and white lights. The craft was silent, was able to stop and hover in one spot, and change direction on a dime. The bright red lights were not blinking, but every once in a while the green or white light would blink. It ascended from a mountain behind our house in the direction of South Ridge, hovered over what looked like South Charleston a few seconds later, and with a wavy path flew North and within seconds and hovered over Cross Lanes. It stayed there for a few minutes, descended a bit, and flew South again. The aircraft hovered over the mountain it had come from and went straight back down behind the ridge. Straight down. About ten seconds later, a second craft with solid red lights went behind the same mountain as the first craft. I was a bit freaked out, even though it isn't my first time seeing what could be a UFO. My only thoughts were "what just happened? WHAT JUST HAPPENED?!?!?"
