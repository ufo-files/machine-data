White round very fast light

It was the end of November and it was getting on towards eight o'clock. I was sat on my bed with my windows open smoking a cigarette. I spotted what I first thought was a firework so I stood up and in a split second I realised it was not. It was a round white car sized object that had a glow to it. I watched it fly over the estate and disappear over the hills. I live on the side of a hill, it's not massive but I can see over a fair bit. It was fast and quiet . If I had blinked I would of missed it. I've been to a lot of air shows in my life and I was raised by a military man. It also came back over my house same time ish but at the end of March 2019 going the opposite way and on the over side of my house. It was coming at me this time but I only got a glimpse for 2 seconds maybe. I am very very keen to know what this is. Since March the tree in my garden has filled out and obscured my view.
