{cak} Orange UFO ejected two other objects

I went out to have my habitual smoke and looked up southwest over PCH and noticed the craft stationary. I ran inside to get my camera and alerted my 10 year daughter, 16 year old son and my girlfriend. the craft began to move slowly east and began to flicker. Immediately after flickering two craft dropped out of the craft and descended straight down until no longer visible. My attempts to film only ended with short unfocused videos because its a new camera and I was unable to focus quickly enough. The craft then blinked out and immediately teleported west a few miles, then blinked out again and was gone.
