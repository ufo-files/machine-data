{cak} I could clearly see inside the craft for 30 minutes

I was on the 2nd floor of my home at night with 3 family members. We saw something in the window and looked outside. We all saw a flat black disc shaped object with a slightly domed top within 20'to 30' of our window and at the same elevation of our window. The object had one long clear window located within a 3' to 4' high band that connected the domed top and more flat bottom of the craft. The interior of the craft was well lighted and we could see 4 people all dressed in uniform. Their shirts were white with black epaulets with a gold band on the epaulets. Because of the uniforms we guessed that they were possible our military personnel. Three people in the craft stood at the window looking at their controls while one more person was behind the three and appeared to be overseeing what the three people were doing. All people inside the craft seemed as though they were in a predicament and trying to find a way out. They did not look afraid but they the did look as though they were focusing on a problem. During this time one person from the craft looked directly at me, smiled and then turned his attention back to the controls. 

During the event I felt surprised but not afraid because my Dad thought we may be looking at our military personnel flying in a secret craft.
The craft hovered in one place directly in front of our window for 30 minutes and then slowly moved away from our house and up at the same time. As it moved away and up from our house it stalled for a few seconds and the shot up and out of sight in a matter of a few more seconds.
