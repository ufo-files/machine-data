Orange County NY 2011

I was driving down a road after leaving a meeting in Rock Tavern NY. At the end of the road at the stop sign 2 people who were also at the same meeting had stopped their cars and were standing in the road. They were both mesmerized by the thing in the sky. When I looked up, I was mesmerized too. Holy ****! what is that!!!!! We all started yelling at each other over what were looking at in disbelief. We were off of Rt 207 up the street from Toleman, the object was in the trees hovering and it appeared to be over the MTA refuge area. Stewart Airport gets a lot of aerial traffic, but this thing was just hovering, not on a schedule to get somewhere. It was shaped like a elongated egg next to a racquet ball next to a egg <0O0> where the ends slope down to a point with 3 interior compartments. It had a amber yellow glow to the outer aura, with a brighter yellow/whitish pulsating inner glow, each compartment appeared to have a black center to it. 3 of us watched this thing for a few minutes as it was above the trees but from our vantage point it appeared to be in the trees. It was in a north north west sky from us at 9:30ish at night. There were 2 aircraft both flying West and East of this object at much higher altitudes, their red and blue strobe lights identified them plain as day. This thing was pulsating YELLOW. Like dummies we changed our position to higher ground for a better look. By the time we got to the higher ground, mellow Yellow was gone. None of us thought about using our phones or cameras to take a picture of it but it was cool to watch...
