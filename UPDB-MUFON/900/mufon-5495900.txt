{rjl} I saw a boomerang shaped object with as many as 10 or more stationary light pink or white lights in straight line angular formation

I was standing on my porch smoking a cigarette. I as usual was looking up at the sky and was a little upset because it was very cloudy at the time and I could not see the stars. Out of the corner of my eye I noticed the moon was trying to break through the clouds in the north west sky. As I was watching the moon flicker through the clouds I saw a row of what I thought at first was a cluster of stars. Upon further investigation I realized that the "stars" were very evenly spaced away from each other. I then stepped down my stairs still looking at the north west sky and saw that these lights that I was seeing were below the clouds and they were very evenly spaced away from each other in straight line Boomerang Formation. This was observed between the hours of 6 to 7 P.M. Eastern standard time on feb. 22, 2015
