Strange white circular light followed us through a bend

We were looking for a place that had little light pollution to look at the stars better and I used an app to find a spot up near Mount St Helens. During our drive up to the spot we went up on Spirit Lake Highway and right before this small shop called North Fork Survivors I had this immense sense of dread overcome me to which I told my fiancé we need to turn around, we then began heading back now west bound on Spirit Lake highway we drove for about 5-7 minutes then approached a bend to which my fiancé noticed a single white circular light behind us approaching, she was relieved as she thought there finally was another person she was getting ready to pull off to let them pass, however she began worrying as it was getting closer really fast and we were traveling approximately 60mph so she then told me and I look out of the side mirror and I noticed the strange light which appeared to be right in the middle of the road maybe 200 feet behind us, the light a few seconds later appeared to fade off not fade away into the distance but slowly turn off. We then came back the next day to try and debunk what it may have been we could not find any light source that would have given the impression that it was following us along with actually turning the bend with us, the only light sources we found in the area was a road reflector on the railing before the bend and houses far beyond the river behind the tree line, the light was also circular and almost too perfectly white none of those potential light sources would have emitted a light like that and stayed in view long enough for us both to observe it.
