I have seen this object between 1974 and 1999

It all started when I was looking up at the stars, when I noticed the 3 stars in a line Orion's Belt, I looked away and then back again and the bottom of the 3 stars moved away Horizontal, then about 12mths later, me and a few friends where walking towards Hindley baths, when we looked up and this big dark red ball about 50 feet in diameter and about 75 feet up flew towards the swimming pool, I ran towards the baths and around the back but it had disappeared, then some years later in the early 90's me and my girlfriend moved to skelmersdale and we liked going for walks between 11pm and 3 am, where we observed this same object several times in the 9 years we lived in the area, I have checked on google earth and carfield runs in line with Hindley swimming pool 15 miles apart, I have never seen this object since.we don't drink and have never taken drugs, It did not make any noise but moved in an exact straight line.the reason I say that is there is a long path cutting through a field near where we lived and it followed the path.
