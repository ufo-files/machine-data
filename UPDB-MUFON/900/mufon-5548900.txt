Daylight Disk Sighted in Garfield, N.J.

I was 10 years old at the time and I was playing in the backyard. All of a sudden I got this creepy feeling that someone was watching me. I looked around and spotted a large disc-shaped UFO hovering about 6 feet above our one-car garage. Its width was about the same as the length of the garage. The object was black and silver, but I can't recall if the top or bottom was silver. Also, one of the halves was rotating slowly, but I can't recall which one. Although the object was hovering in place, it did so in a kind of unsteady manner. I was about 30-40 feet away from it as I watched it. I decided to run upstairs and tell my parents, but when they got to the window, the object was gone.
