{cak} Large red orb decends behind trees.

As I was driving home and nearing my house I saw a very large red orb in the sky. It was larger than the moon so it easily caught my attention. It hovered in place for a few seconds then descended a few hundred feet into the treeline. At first I thought the object was a plane but it was to round and bright. Right after I saw it I ran inside to get my friend in hopes to see it as only a few seconds had passed. We ran back outside to the same spot on the road where I saw it however it was not there. I have never seen anything like this in my life!
