First UFO sighting at my parents house.

This happened at my parents house two miles south of Taylor and I believe it was 1976.We still did not have power yet.I had a friend over Andy S. and I was getting tired of all the [cms/tg/pd] that goes on when you have friends over nite.I was looking up through the sliding glass door when I saw a bluish white light moving from spot to spot in the night sky.It appeared to me that it was going from star to star.I told my friend and he laughed at me and told me they were galaxies and there is no way a ship is going to be that big.He came to the window and by then it had stopped and he said "oh sure I see it !" and went back and laid down.I kept watching with my Brother and it started up again and I yelled at him to get up and look again.It stopped again and he laid down again.Some time had passed I guess a half hour and I was determined to prove that I was seeing something by now my brother was layong down too.It started up again and now started to descend (really it was like it dropped) as it approached it seemed to be blinking a soft red glow and appeared to be about twenty feet in diameter and was blinking on for about four seconds and then off for about the same( It seemed to keep a very dim red glow from within at all times as it moved ).It came to stop about a quater a mile away and about one hundred and fifty feet off the ground.We were all screaming at my Mother who was sleeping in the back bedroom to get up and come look but she was very tired and was yelling at us to go to sleep cause it was a quarter to midnight.It moved west toward Ralph H.SR. ranch at about ten to twelve miles per hour.It put out a single spot light as it passed over Lovelake rd.and then another and another till there was five light one in the middle and four around the center it started spot lighting the corn fields in a wild manner it reminded me of Van Halen when I saw them twice on the 1984 tour.The light all spot lighted a single place in front of the ship and the ship was now dark and it came to a stop when all the lights were directly under it.The four lights blinked off and the center light seemed to brightened for a second before it to blinked off.We waited for probably a minute before the red glow returned and now the red blinking quickened as the ship sped off and arching back up into the sky and disappeared, it took on a steady bluish white glow as it had before it's descent.My Mother would have been a good witness but she arrived in the living room to late.
