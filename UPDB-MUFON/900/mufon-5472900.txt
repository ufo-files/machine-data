I was driving at night in Columbus, Indiana. I felt the urge to look outside and above my windshield. (Not sure why). I had no idea what to think of this object, I was stunned, almost like I couldn't think while I was looking at it.

After about 90 seconds of hovering above my car, the object ascended straight into the air. Basically, the diamond spun in place at a high speed and hovered above my car at the same time. A rainbow of colors was displayed from the lights. This was NOT a Drone. An entity could not have been inside this object, it was TOO SMALL. 

When I first saw the diamond, I didnt know what to think. I just stopped and stared. It seemed almost personal to me. Almost as if there was an object (perhaps MULTIDIMENSIONAL) that was spiritual in nature, watching over me. I DID NOT TELL ANYONE ABOUT THIS EVENT FOR OVER A YEAR. For some odd reason, it was almost like I didnt feel the need to. I was so mesmorized by my encounter, I couldnt talk about it.
