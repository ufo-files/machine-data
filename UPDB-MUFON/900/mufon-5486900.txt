Clear object

I was smoking in my back garden, the object was extremely clear below the clouds, cloud cover was patchy with very good vision of stars/moon between cover. The object was extremely prominent, I wouldn't describe it as glowing but it certainly seemed to absorb light unnaturally which made it extremely prominent in the night sky.

Initially I thought the object could be a plane albeit the altitude was far too low (approximately 800-1000 feet) and the flight path was contradictory to other planes which land at a nearby airport.

It was perfectly symmetrical however it seemed to moved at a slight angle, the "wings" were approximately 10% shorter than the length of the object.

The object left no vapour trail, made no noise and moved at a consistent pace of approximately 60-80mph.

There were no noticeable lights on the object however its' form could be seen with absolute perfect clarity along with the colour of the object (a dirty white, not quite grey).

I lost sight of the object as it travelled over nearby buildings which obscured my view.
