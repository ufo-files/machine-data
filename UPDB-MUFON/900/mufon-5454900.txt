{tjd} 20 or more ships moving across the Arizona night sky.

Stepped outside RV to look at the stars. I notice two moving. Then realized there were more. I stood there and counted at least 20 moving across the sky, all in a perfect straight line, all equally spaced, all heading due south. They were not blinking lights, looked exactly like distant stars, and all were plain white in colour. They were silent (a jet plane I could hear at this time of day). If they were flying by at 30000 feet above, they would have been equally one mile apart, and they were flying more than ten times the speed of a commercial jet.
My phone camera could not pick them out in the dark sky.
