I saw bright lights gliding low across the sky

My children and I had been outside with a camp fire and I glanced over the trees and saw some bright lights. At first I thought it was the crescent moon but soon realized that was the wrong location for the moon. I got my kids to whitness this. We watched it just glide over the tree tops and over power lines, kinda low in the sky and as it crossed Hwy 101 ( we were looking North) it just disappeared.  It was like the lights just went out. Judging by the length of the lights the object would have to be approximatly 30 feet. I've tried researching if any satellite's were in the sky. The video's of Satellites do not match what we saw. I've never seen anything like what we saw. My daughter did video it. But it's a very mature video. We really would like to know if there is an explanation for what we saw.
