{nd} Observed a stationary white light over Gulf of Mexico,then 3 more appeared near it went off and on then all disappeared.

While on the beach at Orange Beach AL at about 8:05pm CDT,I observed a white light come on at about 45 degrees above the horizon to the south towards Mobile, AL and over the gulf. What caught my attention was when the first light went out, another appeared in the same basic area, then a third then a fourth in no particular formation. They did not blink like a plane's lights but stayed on then off. The lights appeared to remain stationary then all disappeared after less than 30 seconds after appearing. I watched for a few minutes to see if they would return but saw nothing more.
