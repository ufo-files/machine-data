UFO was moving NW above phone/power lines.

On December 1st, 2017 at 19h00. I had just finished work and was heading home and that's when I noticed a white object moving above the power lines. I had stopped my car to make sure that it was not light deflecting since the sky was overcast. That's when I saw the UFO move North West and moving around, between, the lines. The object was about maybe 10 to 15 feet in diameter. It's hard to explain because the best way I can describe the UFO, it was shaped like a fried egg, with the object misty white and the middle a brighter white. It also moved like a flying "magic" carpet. I followed it for about half a kilometer and then it headed upwards and I lost track of it. I was intrigued by what I was seeing and I guess in awe since I've never seen something like this and as low. I had seen UFOs before but only moving lights in the distance.
