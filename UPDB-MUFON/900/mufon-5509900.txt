{rjl} Orange yellow object Moving NE to SW

Three of us were sitting out at a fire pit and I noticed an orange object in the sky. I noticed it because it wasnt pulsating but coming closer. When I alerted the two other people with me, we all stood up and watched it move from a straight path to a zig zag pattern. It first came in straight from the north east and then moved to a south west pattern. While doing so, it made some zig zags while still moving. Its color was orange and solid. Nothing was pulsating like a plane.
