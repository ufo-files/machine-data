Solid light seen hovering in the sky over Plymouth dockyard

I was at home when I heard my son arrive on his motorcycle so I went and opened the front door for him. As I looked up the road for him, I noticed a bright stationary light which, to me, didn't look quite right. My son, walking towards me, turned to see what I was looking at and immediately said to me "I was looking at that star from home, bright isn't it? ". I told him that it wasn't a star and I'm getting my mobile to film it (video attached). My son was blown away when the light extinguished, although not completely and instantly but more like in two steps and then came on again, although not as brightly, then disappeared completely.
