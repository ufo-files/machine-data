My cellphone camera accidentally caught what appears to be UFOs

On my way back from Grand Island I stoped at York to pump gas around 6:30 am and noticed 3 flying objects that looked like drones flying around me. I felt as if they were following me home but had lost vision of them so I figured maybe they were above me where I couldn't see them. I grabbed my phone turned my camera on and set it on my dashboard and let it record for a while hoping I would capture the drones following me. I let I record for about 20 min and when I got home and reviewed the video and I knew there was no way whatever was following me were drones.
