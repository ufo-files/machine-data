Orbs over Ojai

It was a few years ago. I was sitting on the front porch with a friend. It was nearing dusk but still light out. My friend in a frantic voice told me to look up. High up in the sky the were two glowing lights moving in a very fast erratic pattern. Their speed was also variable. At one point they just hovered stationary apart from each other. Toward the end of the sighting they converged on each other and seemed to become one object. Then it ascended very fast and seemed to just vanish. 
My friend and I both witnessed this.
