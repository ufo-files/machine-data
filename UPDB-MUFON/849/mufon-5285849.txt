We saw what looked like a flying saucer on August 25th, 2021 while driving down Oakwood Ave.

Yesterday going to the dentist, Sue and I were driving down Oakwood getting ready to turn on Andrew Jackson, I saw a long light silver object in the sky. Looked like a flying saucer to me. Sue said it was a jet. There were no windows or wings on it or lights. Broad daylight. I saw it for a few seconds. I was driving. Sue was watching it. It totally disappeared out of the sky in a flash. I say it was a flying saucer. Anyone else sees this yesterday? Around 9:30 am. Totally silent, no sound. The sky was clear as a bell. This would be my first real sighting.
