Light over Army training site in Nebraska.

Myself and other members of National Guard witnessed a light traving from south to north at the Greenlief training site, just before the light got near the barracks it did a course change and moved away from us heading north east. This object was below 36,ooo feet as myself and several others watch a airliner fly over the object. If object was a airplane or helo we would have heard engine noise but there wasnt any sound from the object.
