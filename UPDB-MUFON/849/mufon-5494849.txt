{cak} Lights flickered on/off and a bright white light illuminated our whole living room, we went outside to investigate and saw a UFO

I have been skeptical to report what happened many years ago. But with soo many people coming forward I want to come forward with my experience too:
I remember being in middle school (about 15 years ago), writing my book report at night in the living room. When all of a sudden the lights in the living room flickered on/off and then a bright white light illuminated our whole living room as though it was day time. So we went outside to investigate what had caused this. We saw something that we will never forget. My brother went outside first, followed by my mom and as I was slowly walking towards them I remember the look on my brother and moms face as they looked up at the sky (awe and fear). When I went outside and looked up at what they were looking it, I saw a UFO, It was round and had different lights around it (red and green from what I remember). But that moment I saw it I froze and in a matter of seconds it zig zagged and disappeared into the night. When it was gone, we were speechless. Did we really just see what we think we saw? My block is usually always busy with people and kids riding their bikes. The weird thing is that for once, our block was completely quiet. No one around. I don1t know if anyone else saw this. It seemed like we were the only ones who had seen this. I forgot to mention that my little two year old nephew was left inside the house while we quickly went outside to investigate, and when we came back inside the house; my nephew was making weird sounds and gestures. We had no idea what he was trying to let us know. But it seemed as though he might have seen something. I remember sleeping in my parents room that whole week and then with the light on from that point on until I moved in with my boyfriend.
