{rjl} Five lights hovering over Augusta

Driving west on Calhoun expressway, noticed a stationary light fixture to the left of the highway ahead of me. As we approached in my car, the object did not seem to move and I recognized the lights from another suspicious sighting a few years ago so I took a picture. The fixture was about 200 ft above the tree line and I pointed it out to my passenger, trying to decide if it looked like a plane of a cell phone tower. I was convinced it was not and turned around at the next exit to find it had disappered. We had watched it for about 1 minute then in 30 seconds it was no where to be found. The shape itself was 5 white lights in a V shape with the tip of the V facing west.
