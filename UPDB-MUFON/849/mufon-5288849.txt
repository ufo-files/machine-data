Floating amorphous object changed shape

Driving east on Sunset Blvd on the Sunset Strip; stopped at red light and witnessed what appeared to be a trash bag floating gently straight up with the wind. Except it was a windless day (at least at ground level). Was going to pull out phone and take picture but kept telling myself it was just some sort of gold/brown bag carried up by the wind. Until it stopped completely, changed into an ovoid, rounded bullet shape and headed in a straight line due North at about 35mph. It's dancing-like leaf like movement upwards and then shape and direction change was so completely unnatural that I will never ever forget it. I actually drove up into the hills northward to try get high enough to see above roofline of the tall commercial buildings and locate it (I was already late for a meeting) for several minutes but never saw it again. All this in broad daylight.
