{dr}My wife and I saw lights in the sky not normal lights you see.

On July 4th 2015 My wife and I were coming home form a gill out. as we started pulling in our drive way. We saw these lights in the sky seven of them at one end 3 were in a triangle while the rest were in a line behind them. They were not like planes or helicopters they were solid lights. If you saw them you new they were something else. we watched they as they faded away in the night then more came from the east to were the other were at then they faded away too. This happened three or four times. We could not believe what we were seeing. We tried to get picture but they would not show up on the camera because it was so dark out side.
