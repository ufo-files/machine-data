It was about one AM, Sunday, September 9, 2018, Claremont, California when I went outside from a second story room and sat at the top of the stairs to smoke a cigarette. I was facing due East and in front of me was a bright light on the horizon. I filmed this airplane, but at some point a spot of light entered. It can't be a star apparently moving down due to the airplane moving up because it is descending nearly vertically, which would mean the airplane was ascending nearly vertically. I know the air plane was coming out of the East ascending at about ten two twenty degrees at the most. It is too bright to be a satellite for certain, thus it is unidentifiable, a UFO. Whether it is under extraterrestrial control is another matter.
