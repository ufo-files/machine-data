{rjl} wasnt sure what i was looking at, but something inside me knew the truth

ON September 28th 2013 at 9;30 A.M. in the morning I was installing a fence when I wiped the sweat from my brow I happened to look up in the morning sky appeared to be crystal clear day then I seen a bright object none movement thought to my self wow that is a bright star for the morning being that it was the only one around, so, I continued with my work and kept glancing at it, then! that bright object seemed to be moving very slowly seem to be bright like phosphorus? the sighting for me seems it was following the Raritan river south between N.J.& N.Y. close to the Outer bridge crossing. kept an eye on it till it dissipated because of distance in morning sky. I tried to take photo of it but too high and phone camera not good for that.
