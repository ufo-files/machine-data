{nd} Bullet shaped object with various colored emission flames, descended from west leveled off across field/wooded area, then ascended and flew rapidly away eastwerd

Arkansas MUFON Field Investigator #16648 submitting for two eastern Oklahoma witnesses. The young couple had stayed up to observe a rare planetary alignment with their children in the autumn of 1977. They went indoors about midnight and were preparing for bed when out the second story window they observed a large (about fifty feet long ten or more feet thick) bullet shaped object with emission flames in the rear, swoop silently down from the west, level off near the far end of an 18 acre field, about a quarter mile to the north, transit the field/border trees at just above treetop level at approximately 100 mph speed, then ascend at a steep angle and increased speed disappear eastward. They never made an official report but readily discussed the event with friends and neighbors. Some friends in Texas did make a secondhand report but the experiencers do not remember the name of the organization it was reported to.
