(GS) 4 very bright flare like lights seen from vehicle all in a straight row. Lights slowly went out one at a time .

At approximately 8:30 pm on 8/8/12 my wife and I were driving home toward the beach on highway 9 in Little River SC. Approching State Hwy 57N (4 miles from beach) we noticed 4 very bright reddish orange lights in a straight row, slightly angled in our direct view just below the vehicles sun visor. The lights were very bright like flares but not moving. They were unlike anything we had seen before. Did not appear to be a plane, lights were too far apart and were not moving. First we saw 4 Lights then each light on the left side went out slowly about every 10 seconds untill no lights were visable. Tried to get my wife to video the lights but by the time we got the I Phone in view they were gone.
