4 round hovering objects, 3 of them were hovering in formation, 1 had more distance

I was passenger in car at highway. We were driving to south (not straight to south, but sort of) to Helsinki from Mikkeli. When there was 35 kilometres to Lahti, I noticed the Moon was clearly visible, even when times was something bout 10:30 am. When I watched the moon again, suddenly there was 4 round object near it. I first thought they were birds or something like that, cause 3 of them was flying in formation. When I spectated them longer, I realized they werent flying at all, they were hovering. And they definitely were much higher than birds fly. Their bottom seemed spinning circle, which made it look sort of like blinking. Colors I saw, were white, silverish and black. I thought the white color was reflection from the Sun.

Like I said, 3 of them were in triangle shaped formation. One was a bit further, but still quite near them. While 3 of them were hovering still, the last one moved slowly towards them until they were square shaped formation. (Remember, I was sitting in moving car as passenger whole this time.) After what I saw, couple pine trees blocked my view for 2 seconds. After that, I never saw those flying objects again. They like disappeared in thin air.

When all this happened, weather was sunny, and there was no even single cloud. I never mentioned what I saw to driver. I really dont know why. I think I was sort of... confused. And I thought he might think I'm a lunatic.
