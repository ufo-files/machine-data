{cak} I saw many crafts passing over head. They would have appeared to be jets but I could see the outline against the light polluted sky. They looked like organic beings, ships moved like sea creatures swimming. Silent. One saw me and looked at me.

This is the second night this week I have seen strange craft. They appear to be jets with steadily flashing lights. But if you look directly at them you see the actual outline of them and they are not jets. They seemed to come in many different shapes. Some more like structures, one looked like some kind of sea creature, it felt like it turned and looked at me then swam away. They are silent, but they are much lower than the lights would make you believe. I lost count at 12 last night. The previous night was the same way. I got a feeling of peace and love from them, no fear. They are here to help us.
