USO just before noon then UFO late night both viewed from ship

I was in the USCG aboard the USCGC Gallatin, a 378 ft. cutter. We were drifting in the Bermuda triangle and just before noon, I was tossing reflective soda can lids and such off the fantail to watch them sink. The water was so clear you could see hundreds fo feet down. While I was watching, a very large, disc shaped object passed under the ship directly under our keel from stern to bow. I did not report this for fear of being put on report of being nuts! I could tell it was very deep and was huge, many times wider than the beam of the ship (35 ft).

Late that night I was messanger on duty and was on the bridge when the lookout reported a contact dead ahead. Everyone on the bridge saw the contact. It seemed to be about 2-3 miles distant. Suddenly it appeared to be heading in our direction at a good rate of speed. We checked both radar and sonar and saw nothing on either. The object seemed to be just above the water and as itmoved, it didn't cast a wake. We turned the 21" search light in that direction and turned it on and it instantly vanished.

I'm sorry I can't give the exact date or location but it's been quite a few years since it happened. I do believe we are being visited and because of what I saw, no one can convince me otherwise.

I don't know if the ship's log will show anything about the incident knowing how the GOV likes to keep all sightings for themselves.
