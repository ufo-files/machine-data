Slow moving Sphere directly over freeway

Noticed a small sphere shaped object while driving down the Eastern Freeway in Melbourne Victoria Australia. At first I assumed it was a balloon but as I continued nearer I noticed it was far too still to be a balloon and was was heading on a slow near hovering course west, towards the CBD at a height of no more than 50 metres. There was nothing special about it or anything in particular that caused me to see it, other than the fact it was very low. Just chance as my eyes wondered down the road and had a greater view of the horizon. Was a dull whitish-grey (like a low sheen brushed aluminum) in colour, no features other than colour, shape or size was I able to determine in the short amount of time. The reason I reported it is purely because I found it very odd that it appeared to be following the contours of the road and directly above the median. Lost sight of he object when I inevitably crossed paths and was no longer safe for me to look in my rearview mirrors. It was peak hour traffic so I cant say whether only I saw it but with the hundreds of cars that drove past at the same time as I did, surely others noticed it.

The exact location when I noticed the object was the Bulleen Rd overpass on the Eastern Fwy. Did the exact same drive again today, same time, same weather conditions and can most definitely say it was not a street light.
