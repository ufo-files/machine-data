Carson Wa experience

So six family members & I went for a drive on feb.25,2012 at 8:00p.m we were heading through Carson a town in Washington state.We continued up to snow park toward Hoodoo Recreation Area that is about 20 min north of Carson. My husband ,sister-in-law and i were sitting in the back of the SUV with the back door kept open .My husband had noticed a extremly bright light hovering above us. As my sister-in-law and i had looked up we had all three noticed that the object had been moving rather fast towards us as if it was a high speed chase.My sister-in-law had yelled to the driver to please stop,the driver had then slammed on her brakes at that time the object had also come to a complete and total stop so fast that it was clear to us the front of the craft dodged down. We all sat & looked at this thing for about 5 minutes before it slowly rose up & faded away.We continued up the road for a mile or so before it appeared again, not as aggressive as it had been but definately following us.
