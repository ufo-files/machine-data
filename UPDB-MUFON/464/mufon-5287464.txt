UFO phenomenon before Starlink event West of event

Wife and I were waiting for a Starlink event to pass over. Minutes before the event we looked to the West (around 9:40 PM)....(Starlink event would be behind us 5 minutes later )...An objective was spotted in the sky by both my wife and I. Appeared to be of oval shape with the naked eye, but when I looked through my binoculars the object was in the shape of a cats pupil tilted at a 45 degree angle . (Uppermost point facing south) exterior was flat white cloudy soft glow with the outer tips with defined pointed edges. I had the object in view with the binoculars for approximately 8 to 10 seconds when my wife noticed that there were now two objects of the same shape that separated from one object. I brought my binoculars down to get a fix on the second object, to which I notice that both objects had changed direction after separating from a single object. Direction of object (1) parallel to horizon come in from the north to the south separated into two objects. First object had stopped. During the time it took me to drop my binoculars and view both objects, my wife witnessed the first object do a tight 360 degree loop almost as if it had back flipped while the second object took off to the south approximately 30 degrees upwards from the original flight path. Both objects then faded out and disappeared.
