{rjl} The objects appeared to be Glowing Orbs

On Wednesday, June 18, 2014, I was alerted by a fellow employee of unusual stationary lights in the sky. I would describe these lights as Orbs, which would shine brightly, then dim, disappear, and reappear, sometimes in the same location. We observed a total of 5 lights during the encounter.My fellow employee has worked at this location for approx 10 years, and I have been there for 5 years. At no time have either of us seen anything similar in the night sky at this location.
