{jpg} A Star up by the Moon turns out being a craft and begins moving towards the northeast

I was inside the apartment when I felt I was to go outside and usually when I feel that there is always a celestial craft . . as if it calls me to let me know they are there/here. SO I walked out onto the balcony looking up at the moon which was high in the southern sky. There was a star up by the moon which I thought was an actual planet or star but there was too much light in the sky still. This starcraft begins to move in an arc like motion indicating it was not yet in the lower atmosphere. Still it moved in an arcing sweeping motion. It continued to the northeast as I observed for a couple minutes before the light faded or disappeared. There were many planes on approach to DFW and others being guided on their approach flight. This had no blinking lights and brightened before finally disappearing.
