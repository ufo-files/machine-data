Star like appearance, crossed the sky from west to east and got gradually dimmer and disintegrated

Around 6:10am I let my dog out to go to the bathroom and had a cigarette. We live in a very rural area with absolutely no light pollution. I looked up and noticed how clear the sky was, and suddenly became affixed on a single "star" which I assumed was a satellite, but I noticed it did not flash, or blink, it seemed to have an organic light quality that seemed to pulsate between white and white/pink randomly. I followed it across the sky until it finally dimmed to almost darkness and disintegrated. Perhaps it was a meteor or comet but had no visible trail.
