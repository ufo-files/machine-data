7 ships over two cities

In Fontana, CA A mothership ascended then shot out 6 smaller ships which flew in different directions while the mothership remained stationary. Several planes flew around the ship.
At the same time by a phone call with the Fontana witness. One of the smaller ships was photographed In Rialto, 10 miles away pulsating, with, baby blue, white/gray and red/orange glowing colors. It had 7 lights or ORBS around its perimeter.
It was videotaped and photographed. 3 airplanes surrounded the ship and several more airplanes 5-7 came to see the ship. At 9:40 the mothership on Fontana began to spin some white bright lights around its perimeter, and we began to drive toward Fontana. While on the phone call, the Fontana witness saw the mothership shoot up suddenly and disappear from sight. We drove back to Rialto and the ship we was gone, while many small airplanes seemed to be circling the area looking for it. The planes were looking for it for over an hour.
