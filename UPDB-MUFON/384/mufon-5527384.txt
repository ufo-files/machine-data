Unexplained phenomenom seen in the sky above New Tampa

I was driving along Bruce B Downs Blvd on my way to work and the day featured a clear, bright blue sky, with absolutely no clouds. I kinda saw something in my peripheral vision and I looked up. There was what looked like a triangle with a curved base (Teardrop shape) made up of a bunch of white dots. I initially thought it was a flock of birds flying together really high at first. After a second or two, all of the dots faded out simultaneously and disappeared completely from sight. I looked all around and couldn't see the formation anywhere. I assumed that maybe there were high fog-like clouds that I couldn't see that maybe obscured the formation. Then slowly, the dots appeared again slowly and simultaneously like a fade-in and I tried to squint and see it better. It definitely wasn't birds and wasn't really moving from it's position at all either, so it wasn't a plane. Then it faded away again until it was completely gone. After a second or two, it came back and I tried to look around and see if it was attached to something and couldn't find a source. It faded in and out about 3 more times. Then, I got a shock. I literally gasped and shouted "No freaking way!". I swear to God, a line just above the curved base blinked one dot at a time and it looked like that particular lower section moved in a counterclockwise motion with the blinking dots. Then the entire thing faded away and completely disappeared again. I never saw it again after that no matter how hard I looked.
