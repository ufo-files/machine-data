{jpg} I watch this object pass in front of the moon, it looked like a bad video

I had decided to let my dog outside, and as I stood there, there was a full moon, and I couldn't help but notice and look at it. As I observed the moon I saw an object pass in front of it heading north, except the object was not clear, it was like a bad video that all I could see was squares, that were not in order. It reminded me of the stealth tech monitor devices, that reroute what's behind the object to the front side, making the equipment invisible except this one it wasn't just one monitor, it was more like 30 on one side, that someone forgot to line up, and were all pointing slightly in different ways. I just laughed because I thought that whoever did that was stupid for flying it in a controlled zone for air traffic. But at the same time smart because other than flying in front of the moon, I couldn't see it. My impression on the matter was that, that one was ours, and someone trying to duplicate their tech.
