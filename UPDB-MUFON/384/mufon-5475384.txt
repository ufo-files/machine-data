Camping in Philipi, WV with some friends. While observing venus (4:30AM), a bright yellowish orb which was covered perfectly by a cloud appeared in a cloudless sky to the right of where we were looking, immediately followed by four reddish orbs in an angled square pattern which also then was covered by clouds, then another four more lights in a square pattern, each time new ones appeared the cloud grew to cover them. They began to fade after apprx 60 seconds but the cloud remained for hours until sunrise. I was frozen and terrified. Multiple people in Philipi, WV confirmed sighting, including a police officer.
