{nd} Pear/cigar shaped UFO hovered, pulsed light or reflections, then disappeared. Appeared right after a near-collision between two chemtrail planes

I was with a friend hanging out in a parking lot in St Johns. We were watching chemtrail drone planes flying, leaving trails in the sky. Then there was a near-mid-air collision between two chemtrail drones. As we watched in awe, I was compelled to keep looking up. All of a sudden, a silver/black object appeared out of nowhere, right in the spot of the near collision. It was sort of pear shaped and appeared to be reflecting the sunlight off of it's metallic surface. It appeared to be spinning around, constantly dazzling & reflecting the sunlight. It hovered just below the cloud line. When we first noticed it, I grabbed the attention of my friend to make sure he was seeing it. At first, he thought it was a drone, but then immediately I said no, that drones don't sparkle during the daytime & they certainly don't spin around. I was in total awe & amazement, as I've only ever seen ufo's and orbs at night. The object first hovered in one place, then started to drift to the left, towards the NE direction. It appeared to be hovering right over the Columbia River, between Portland and Vancouver and it's possible the people in Vancouver could have seen it. My friend grabbed his phone to record it and his phone immediately 'died' and rebooted. I kept watching it. It continued about 100 feet north and then stopped again and just continued to hover and spin around. At this point, I grabbed my ipad and opened it up, clicked on the camera app, and started taking photos very rapidly. I took a bunch in a row, because the object appeared to be fading in and out of 3rd dimensional reality, as it spun, it would disappear and then appear again, making it appear super sparkly! This was proven in my photos, as the object only appeared in 3 of my 7 photos. In the other photos there is no sign of it, but there are 100's of black & gray orbs around the area where the object was. The orbs appear in all photos. After a series of photos, I switched my camera to video mode & right then, the object just disappeared into thin air, and never came back. Looking like it morphed out of this dimension. My whole body felt very alive and amazed! I felt like the UFO gave me energy!
