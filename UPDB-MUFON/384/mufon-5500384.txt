A bright flash of brillant white light appeared for approx. 5 seconds then disappeared, A few seconds later it flashed brightly again, only this time it shot a red laser-beam over to a mountain to the east of it's position.The beam returned thenwent out

After a late dinner I went out to my front patio to have a smoke. As I sat there, facing north-west I see this bright flash appear in the sky. It lingered for 5 or 6 seconds then blinked out. A few more seconds go by and the bright light returns. This time it shoots out this red beam of light towards a mountain to the east. The beam lasts for maybe 3 seconds then returns back to the spherical bright white light just as fast as it went towards the mountain and then POOF!! the light disappears into thin air just the way it came. Ever since then I've been watching the skies, and bought a little 14 pixel Fuji camera to hopefully catch something. As it turns out I have caught some interesting shots that I would like to send to your organization. I have about 4 photos.
