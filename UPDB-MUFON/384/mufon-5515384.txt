{nd} Shaped object appeared at once, brightly lit, then faded out.

At 10:30 PM on September 20, 2012 while walking my dog, I happened to look up and saw something all of a sudden light up. It glowed bright yellow for several seconds and slowly faded. I could not determine the size, but it was large. This was not a flare or meteor, it had a definite outline.

The object was about 2801 relative to my location and 301 above the horizon, above the hills (or farther) that are 5 miles to the west. I did not see any movement, only the shape of the object. 

To give an idea of the shape, think of a B-2 "Stealth Bomber", at a 901 bank, cockpit to the right. The difference is that the rear portion was a crescent instead of the faceted trailing edge of the B-2, and had a dark spot in the center.  We are nowhere near an AFB or any AF practice area! My husband is a Multi-engine Pilot, (for almost 40 years) and I know what is, and is not an aircraft. 

I am reporting this in case others saw this and needed someone else to attest a sighting...

Thank you...
