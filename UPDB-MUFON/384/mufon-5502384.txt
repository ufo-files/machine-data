{nd} big bright red orb sailing across the night silently and quickly

On the deck of a house in Feasterville Pennsylvania saw a giant red orb coming across the night sky, called to two other people, to come and see. we all watched it trail across the sky towards the Northwest it was too quick to pull out our phones and get a picture before went behind the trees. It was pretty low in the sky it was sailing across in a straight line, no tail/contrail, and there was absolutely no sound.
