The main sighting that i experienced was when i was a young boy. My brother, whom claims he doesn't remember the sighting, and i were outside on the swing and noticed this triangular craft flying very slowly over where we were. It didn't make any noise and flew in a straight line over our property over the street over the neighbors and off into the distance headed south never changing speed or direction.
