{rjl} Cigar shaped object with many lights around the exterior

I was traveling on route 53 from South Fork toward Portage (sorry I'm not sure what direction that is) but I was heading to get on to route 219 South to go to Somerset, PA at around 8 p.m. on Sunday, 6th October, 2019. When I was in the general area of the Erenfeld bridge I noticed a bunch of lights almost directly overhead. At first I thought someone had strung a bunch of lights across the roadway pretty high up until I stopped my car and took a good look at it. There were many lights that encircled what appeared to be a large cigar shaped craft hovering directly above where I was sitting in my car. I turned my vehicle off and got out to look at the object in amazement as it sat perfectly still and completely silent. It felt like a very long time passed but in reality was only a couple of minutes had gone by when I looked toward the bridge and seen 2 other vehicles sitting on the bridge stopped and looking at the craft as well. The lights remained stationary for a few minutes then suddenly vanished as the craft sped off to the south without a sound. I have never witnessed anything like this before in my life!
