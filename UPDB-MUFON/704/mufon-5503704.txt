My father and I were driving to my grandmother's. Stopped at a red light he noticed a triangular object hovering a few hundred feet above the ground. He asked "do you see that? What is that?" He is a former U.S. Marine with knowledge of common aircraft. He had no idea what type of craft it could be. It ascended slowly while rotating to face a new course and slowly began to fly north. We followed it driving around 50mph. It quickly flew in a diagonal line to the left. Then flew extremely fast out of sight ascending as it did.
