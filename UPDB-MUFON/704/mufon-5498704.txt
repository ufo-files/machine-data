{nd} Object sitting in cloud .

I was on a trip to Tucson , AZ and about 6 miles South of Clovis , NM I pulled over to take a picture of the sunset and that's when I seen the object sitting at the bottom of the cloud . The only thing around is a chemical plant and it looked like it was hovering close to it . Not really sure the distance of the object but the chemical plant is about 2 miles from the HWY . When I seen the object , I was happy but shocked . I have seen strange things in the sky before but this is the only one I got a picture of . I watched the object for about 30 mins. and then it just vanished . Clovis , NM is about 100 miles North of Roswell , NM .
