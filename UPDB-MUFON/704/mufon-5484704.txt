{nd} Photograph of high altitude plane and sun revieal an orb off top the North of both

While at work walking around outside I saw a high altitude plane flying close towards the Suns location so I took out my camera and snapped a few photos as the sky was very clear and blue. 

When I returned inside I looked at my photographs as I was going to post them on social media. One of the photos I had zoomed in on. When I looked at it I noticed the orb. It was identical to one I took a photo of a year earlier. I still have that photograph as well.

At first glance it looks like a singular round cloud or debris but when you zoom in you can clearly see the shape and what appears to be lights. Last years photo, which was taken about this time of year and in the same general spot relative to the Sun. Lasts years however was when there were clouds and you can clearly see it above the clouds and then drop in as it appeared in multiple photos. It also appears to be generating a field just in front of it (in last years photo)as it passed through the clouds before diapering into the clouds. 

On both occasions I thought it was pretty awesome to see and to be the only one, that I know of, to have captured in so clearly in a photograph.

The photos attached are put together to show what I have in the photographs. I have the originals as well. only thing done to the photos was a cropping so i could zoom in on the object. 

The first photo was taken May of 2015 with the object in the clouds. The second photo was taken today, 10 March 2016 at 09:10 hrs.
