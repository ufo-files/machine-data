{cak} Was at sea aboard an Aircraft Carrier when witnessed was told to keep quit

Was at sea aboard a Naval Air Craft Carrier in the Bumuda Tringle during night opps. All of a sudden the hole ship lite up like day light. It about 01:00 hrs. All of a sudden this lite move in a second the light moved South ward the same direction we where headed but was gone in a second. This was 1973. The Navy has nothing that moves that fast in the air or in that matter under water I don't think. What really bothers me is that about 5 minutes after that the Skipper comes over the 1 MC and told all of us, gentlemen what you have witnessed did not happen you are to keep quit it did not happen. End of statement.
