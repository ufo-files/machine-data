Strange red aerial light.

uly 04, 2020 10:04PM, I was taking a video of fireworks from out my south-facing living room window when I noticed a red light to my upper-right in the sky traveling from NW to SE at a constant speed like a passenger jet flying in or out of DIA. There were no flashing beacon lights, no discernible aircraft of anykind, just an elongated red light. It suddenly stopped in midair like a helicopter for a few seconds, before making a sharp left angle turn toward the north and then a sharp right angle turn and suddenly shot south where it appeared to stop and hover in the distance. Because it's movements momentarily startled me, I didn't point my cellphone camera up at it fast enough before it zipped to the south where it hovered in the distance. This video is just a few seconds later as I find the light on my cell screen, when it suddenly reappears. My cellphone camera doesn't take the best night time videos, so it didn't capture the red color of the light or it's movements, but it did capture it shimmering/pulsating and disappearing. The camera also doesn't capture when the light disappears, it is actually zipping away and moments later it zips back and reappears. The light does this 3-4 times before finally disappearing for good.
