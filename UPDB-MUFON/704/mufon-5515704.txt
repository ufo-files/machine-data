(GS) 3 bright lights in triangle shape. Silent approach with a light humming after it passed. Low elevation.

As I was coming home and driving up my driveway on Tuesday evening, July 3, 2012, I noticed 3 very bright white lights coming straight in my direction. The object was so large and lights so bright, I was startled. I stopped the car midway my driveway and got out of the car to view. It had a triangle shape, and was in a low elevation, about 800 ft heading right towards me. It was like nothing I have ever seen. It passed directly over my head and I could not see any body or structure to it, just lights. There was absolutely no sound coming, but as it passed overhead I heard a light hum. It was traveling in a straight path, approaching from the northwest towards the southeast.

I live up on a hill about 3200ft elevation and have a clear view of our town lights. I observe many flying crafts from helicopters, private and commercial aircraft. I also have flown small airplanes so I am familiar with altitudes and craft lights. This flying UFO was just that. Unidentified flying object.

The following week I was reading our local paper and they were reporting UFO sighting in our town. There was also some photos, one was exact the same lights I saw, that someone else had captured.
