Brilliant, pulsating light observed from vehicle in southwestern pennsylvania.

Driving back from Pittsburgh to Morgantown, WV on April 1st, 2012 at roughly 1-1:20 am, a bright, pulsating light was observed in the clouds west of I-79 roughly around Mile Marker 36. It had been raining that evening, so when I first saw the lights, I assumed it was lightening and thought nothing else of it. My friend, who I was riding with mentioned it, and I looked again. This time I noticed that the light was consistently pulsating a brilliant reddish-orange, like you would see with a forest fire at night. Just the amount of light would indicate that had it been a fire, it would have been of massive proportion and would have been reported on the news by today, not to mention it had been raining most of the day. We watched the light as we continued travelling southbound, for at least 6-7 miles, discussing what it could have been. The light stayed stationary as we drove past, still glowing brightly behind us. Ive never seen anything like this before on a drive back from Pittsburgh at night, and if theres some kind of logical explanation, Id like to know, but I cant find a single thing.
