White/Red orb

I was facing north on 3700 Cottage Grove Avenue in Des Moines IA. The object appeared eastward bright in the sky. The object moved slowly but in a straight path. The object resembled an orb emitting a white/red glow. The object was at the same level of the stratus clouds and moved in-sequence with the pace of the stratus cloud. The Orbs light faded until it dissolved.
