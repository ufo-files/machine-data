{dr}Three different orange glowing spheres on same flight pattern, minutes apart

We were watching fireworks from my friends yard, 4 of us. And saw the first orange glowing sphere quickly moving from South to North. No sound, constant, steady, and fast. Appeared quite high...then 2 more followed, each a few minutes apart. My friend and I were both able to capture the last one on video from our phones. None of us could determine what it might be. It faded off into the horizon, beyond our sight.
