Sun like orb hovering/pulsating

The orb was stationary for 20 minutes seen through my canon telescopic lens. The Orb was observed over Niwot Ridge from my 4th floor tower room looking north at North and South Arapahoe Peaks. Niwot Ridge runs East below the peaks. I snapped a few shots till it disappeared. It diminished slightly before it vanished completely. I am still looking for a repeat performance. I have also observed a different sunlike orb that moved over caribou Ridge twice: May and June 2014. This orb moved in an L shape down a mountain east of caribou ridge very very slowly. In fact, in the beginning when it breached the mountain treeline one could see the orb silhouetted behind the trees. When it finished its course it moved again over the mountain and blasted out into space. The first sighting in May was witnessed by myself and another person. There was no sound from this sighting or the one mentioned today.  Today's sighting that I photographed would be about 25 miles from the glowing orb.
