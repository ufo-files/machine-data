My cousin and I saw a ufo one night it zig zaged across the sky then stopped. The craft then dropped from being star like to being 1000 feet or so. When it dropped it was a split second and the object became 3 different lights. When we saw this we went inside and grabbed guns. We sat up until 7 am. This was probably 3 am and lasted 45 minutes. Three years later my cousin came to my house to grill food and hang out. We had a few drinks on my deck and smoked cigars. We discussed the ufo we saw a couple years before and what it could have been. Then something caught my eye, i could see it moving around through the trees. We both got up to take a look and saw the craft emit a light that would be comparable to 2 full moons. There is nothing that we have that could make a light like this. It was unreal, then after about 15 seconds it disappeared. It was as if the speed took the light and it was gone toward space.
