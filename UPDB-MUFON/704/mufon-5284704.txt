Two parallel white objects or one two-part object

While driving home from shopping saw what looked like two parallel backslashes (same direction as keyboard backslash), white in color, about the width of a finger held at arms length, about 45 degrees above horizon in the WSW. They did not separate. They appeared to hover, then drift slowly toward the south. I pulled my car over to watch it for a minute. as it drifted south. I continued west on Cadbury in my car. When I got to a clear spot in the trees a few minutes later, it was gone. Since these were parallel objects I had to rule out jet trails, which are always in pairs. These were thicker than jet trails (of which we have many in this area with DCA and IAD in the area), and very discreetly "chopped off", no fuzzy edges, but that same white color. I couldn't tell how far away it was. It "seemed" maybe a mile up, but impossible for me to determine.
