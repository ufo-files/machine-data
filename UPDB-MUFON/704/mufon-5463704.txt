{tjd} Several objects making an arc shape traveling independently

I was in the house having a phone call when I heard my son yelling outside. I got up to see what happened and he addressed my attention to the Bright Orange circular objects in the SE sky. I noticed at once how bright they were and their respective positions. From very close to the horizon to the next step up in the sky with a gap between and 3 flying in triangle formation at the highest point. Slowly they began to fade one by one with the lower ones disappearing first. The lowest ones had been stationary but the higher ones were moving to the NE. After the lower ones faded away the higher ones began to disappear one after the other while losing brightness as they moved. Eventually they were completely gone. I didn't think they were any kind of aircraft or helicopter. The lights were steady glowing. My son said he saw 10. My wife said 8 . I know I saw 7 because I counted them. My phone was in my hand and I just didn't think to take a pic. I wish I had they were absolutely beautiful .
