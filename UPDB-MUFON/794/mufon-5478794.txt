At least 10 UFO light cruising over downtown Toronto, Canada.

At 1:30AM, Dec 30, 2016- it was below freezing- I spotted at least 10 UFO lights noiselessly cruising at a constant speed in random order in the same NE direction above and in-between the clouds. Some light were closer to each other then others. At least 2 of them stopped behind in mid air. They all looked like distant stars, but slightly bigger (1.2-1.4 times). No vessel was discernible except for the round very powerful twinkling lights. ( I did take a picture of one of the lights but it just confirmed the round shape). Shortly afterwards, I saw a noisy plane (military?) with a very intense nose head light that went combing through the underbelly of the clouds in the same direction. And after this, the sky was enveloped with complete cloud cover. This was my strange sighting in Toronto in the skies tonite!
