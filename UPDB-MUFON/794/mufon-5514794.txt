(GS) Large spherical object with 3 triangular lights whooshed over intersection at about 15 mph and over my suv.

At about 6:20 am, I was driving to work on rt. 202 and just passed the Duck Deli when I noted a brightness over to my front left above the 7-11. The large object appeared over the microwave cell tower. There was a whooshing sound with the object which first appeared as 3 large triangular white lights. I have a coleman compass which I had on my front seat. The compass whirled. As I looked up again, I noted the structure behind the lights and saw it was one object, not 3 separate triangles. It passed over my suv in all its brightness. Several cars were in the area by the intersection on this overcast rainy morning.

I was a former UFO investigator and this is my second sighting since 2008, the Bucks County flap.
