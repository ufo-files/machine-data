{cak} 2 helicopters following very bright orb or light

Walking down driveway to house, I heard loud millitary type hellicopters and looked up. I could not see the helicopters initially, but saw a very bright orb of light flying across the sky. Then, I saw the two helicopters flying single file behind it. I could hear the helicopters, but no sound from the light. The light was very bright and steady. The distance between the helicopters and orb would change, but I could not tell if the orb was speeding up or if the helicopters were slowing down. I ran through the house to the backyard and was able to see them fly off towards the southwest, until my view was blocked by the treeline. When I first noticed the object, I had no idea what it was, and still don't. I immediately noticed how incredibly bright it was and that it had no standard safety lights. It was just the most incredibly bright, round light I have ever seen. It was the weirdest thing I have ever seen, not to mention the helicopters following closely behind it
