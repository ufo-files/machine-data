{cak} 2 stars flying extremely fast making zig zag patterns and other impossible movements.

I woke up early one morning to go to the bathroom. While there I witnessed through the bathroom window two bright shining stars. The night sky was so beautiful and clear. Then suddenly two stars took off shooting across the sky at impossible speeds zig zagging, looping it looked as though the two stars were playing tag. They shot off to the left out of sight and in seconds shot
Back across the sky out of sight, then back again zig zagging then took off forward and they were gone. I was stunned. I remember it like it was yesterday.
