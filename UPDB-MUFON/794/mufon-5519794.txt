like two coffee cup saucers with a round metal rotating flashing object in between saucers

I was staying with a friend and around midnight I couldnt sleep so I got up made a cup of coffee and took it to the dock. I was sitting waiting for the coffee to cool a little when I noticed flashing lights on the lake. I looked up and saw a round object slowly moving over the tree tops then it came down and hovered over the lake. The nearest part of the saucer was no more than 70-75 feet from me. It looked as though it could house no more than two to four people my size.
The lights flashed then pulsed I didn't understand what it wanted. But I heard myself say aloud "okay"..then the next thing I remember is sitting back on the dock and my coffee was cold. I watched the object slowly rise to the tops of the trees and zip it was gone. I walked back into the house and looked at the clock it was about 1:35 a.m. I told noone back then if I had said anything about it I would have been placed in a mental hospital and called crazy.
Many years later around 1988-89 I quit smoking and gained quit a bit of weight at that time I noticed small grooves in both arms as if tissue had been removed. I checked and found two scar like patches one on each arm, resembling a polio vaccination, but much lower. Why hadnt I noticed them before I wondered. Well for one thing they are in an area not easy to look at, on the back of the arms. Was I affraid "NO" I curious, I was more interested in the object and what was inside than affraid.
