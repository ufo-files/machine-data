Hologram/Ghostly , of a traditional Grey Entity .

It is fairly simple. I woke this morning and looked to my left after a good sleep i had been already awake a few minutes laying there eyes closed. All was well no issues health is great my mindset was great. I rolled slightly left opened my eyes and standing by the drawers about 5 ft away was what looked like a hologram of a traditional Grey Alien looking at me. It wasn't like the beam me up Scotty from star trek thing it was more a faded ghostly figure fading. As i said a traditional gray as you see on TV although a little thinner than i thought they would be and thus the head looked bigger but i think it was as you would typically see in size. I had a sense everything was ok almost friendly and began not to think of it and it faded in an instant. It wasn't quite light yet outside but about 20 minutes before the dawn i guess. I've had no fear, pain or any issues. I sort of feel like it came for a look and was friendly. I don't make a habit of reporting these things. In fact i never have... I admit though i have had an after death white light experience years ago and a couple of strange things happen. But never anything like this. I'm ok with it i may think of it before bed i guess but it seemed fine as i said it was like a curious kid who came for a look and everything was ok.
