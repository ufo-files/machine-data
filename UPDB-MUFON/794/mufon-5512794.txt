Star-like object was still and suddenly speeded up in straight line towards west and vanished

I was sitting at home browsing the Internet (hockey games results) on laptop. It was a windy night out and a few seconds before event, I heard louder sounds outdoors (I tought some army aircraft/jets were circling the skies nearby) and decided to take a peak if I could see a plane.

I went to the patio door and looked up in the skies. There were a few clouds but I could see multiples stars. I then noticed one star that was shining a little bit more and focussed on it. After a very brief moment (maybe one second) the star that was still suddenly moved at very high speed towards west. Gave me the goosebumps... I also realized that the "noises" were not there anymore. Not sure at what moment they stopped. And I figured it could have been the high winds.

I went to other windows to try and see it again but it was gone. I looked back a few times during the rest of the evening but saw nothing else.

I wondered a bit about what had just happened and if I relly saw what I think I did. I also tought that I could have had an hallucination... 

Still, just thinking about it now, it gives me the goosebumps again.
