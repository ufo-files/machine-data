{nd} Silver ball in sky did not move for 1hour

My cousin and I were climbing lookout man up a power line. We stopped to rest, laid down on a rock and noticed the object. We observed for at least an hour and it never moved. Thats why I don't think it was a weather balloon. It was still there when we resumed our climb.
