{rjl} Object appeared while photographing five vintage planes flying in the area

I was on the top floor of the Peterson Museum parking structure waiting for the moon to rise over the downtown Los Angeles area. A few minutes prior to the moon rising, I observed five vintage warplanes flying in formation nearby. I thought I'd better get a few shots of them. When the formation passed a building that was close by, I fired off a burst of about 4-5 shots of the planes. It wasn't until the next morning, after viewing the photos I had taken, that I saw this object between two of the planes, that appeared in only one of the frames. So, it must have only appeared in sight for only perhaps a tenth of a second, since the photos subsequent to the photo with it in it didn't have the object in it. It took only a second or two for the camera to shoot the photo burst. It's disappointing that the object was blurred, and I wonder if any of the pilots in those planes saw anything. Image #4677 (Media File 4.) is the photo that shows the anomoly. The photos before and after it don't show the "ufo".
