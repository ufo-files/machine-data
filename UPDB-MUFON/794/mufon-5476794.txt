{jpg} Group of white dots moving fast followed shortly by a smaller group of the same. All disappeared at the same spot in the sky as if going behind a curtain

It was about 540 in the afternoon with clear skies. I happened to look up and saw what I at first thought was a flock of white birds very high up. As I watched I felt that it want birds due to the speed and altitude. There were between a dozen and twenty of these white dots moving together as a group but randomly, not in formation. The individual dots moved a bit in relation to one another within the group. Two or maybe three of the dots from kinda in the middle of the group did some sort of a flip or loop kinda move to the outside of the group and back into position at the rear of the group. At that point the objects disappeared as they crossed a certain point in the sky, as if going behind a curtain. I was a bit awestruck dumbfounded whatever....it didn't make sense. I kept looking up at where they were and a few seconds later another group of maybe five or seven of these objects came on the same path as the first and disappeared at the same spot.
