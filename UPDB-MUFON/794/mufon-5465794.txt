Witnessed objects in the southern sky slowly moving closer. Eventually stopped over my home. Made quick movements back forth up and down. Eventually went higher and higher until disappeared.

About 8:30 pm my mother, stepfather and I stepped out onto the back porch. We noticed an orange orb of light in the southern sky just above the tree line. It was moving towards us. I took out my telescope to view it and quickly realized it was not a drone or a plane or helicopter. After about an hour it stopped at what seemed right above the edge of our yard to the southwest. We then noticed a white orb of light in the western sky making movements all over the place and after about an hour disappeared high into the sky. In the meantime the orange orb was making the same type of movements basically staying in the same small area. It slowly moved west staying at the same altitude the whole time. We watched for hours in amazement. I knew it couldn't be a drone being up there for that long and It is 11:07 pm now and it is still out there. I took some pictures through my telescope before coming in for the night. Pictures are attached. All photos are of the orange orb.
