Saw 2 large triangles flying W to E along highway 50.

I was driving home from my sister-in,law's house. In the distance West, I thought I saw two planets near the horizon. As I kept driving, I realized they couldn't be planets, because they were getting bigger and brighter.Then I thought they might be planes. There is a flight path far to the south that is visible from the road. Eventually, they got close enough to see they weren't planes either. They flew very close to the road, almost tree-top level. I was crossing a bridge when they were closest. The first passed on my left, about. 100 ft away. The second passed a few seconds later on my right, about 300 ft away. They were equilateral triangles, glowing white. The corners were rounded, and the edges were bevelled. There was a red dome light in the center. I could see shadows in the dome. I slowed down to an idle,rolled down my window and listened for a sound: they were absolutely silent. My husband and son werte also in the car, but they were asleep. I couldn't wake them up. I don't know what they were, but I got a very good look at them. They were 50 ft on each side. I had a passing thought that they might be going to Wright Patterson AFB.
