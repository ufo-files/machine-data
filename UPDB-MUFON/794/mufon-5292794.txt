White cylinder floating above Golden Ears. Took two pictures about a minute appart but it vanished sometime in between

Saw a long white cylinder way off in the distance over Golden Ears. At first I though it was a plane but I noticed it wasn't moving and it didn't appear to have a tail. Took one photo, played with phone brightness and focus for about a minute, took another. When I looked at the sky again I didn't see the object. First photo revealed a small spec when zoomed in, second photo had nothing. All in all I watched the object for about 3-4 minutes.
