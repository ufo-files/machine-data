In the fall of 94 two friends and I saw a UFO.

Driving South on a highway outside Durant, Oklahoma. My two friend and I noticedit simultaneously moving slowly above the left treeline as if it were hovering.We all thought it was a UFO almost immediatly. We were scared at first becauseit seemed to be on an interception course with us. Then as it began to move awaywe became excited following it. It hovered, flew slowly at an angle over theforest and then flew quickly out over a field where it slowly dimmed its lightuntil we could no longer see it. Then we saw two strobes of light illuminate theentire field that seemed to eminate from its previous location just above theground. I do not know where one of my friends is these days but I am certainthat the other would be willing to tell his version of the experience as well.Please, contact me if there were any similar reports around this time andlocation, or if I can provide you with more specifics that cannot be containedin this format.
