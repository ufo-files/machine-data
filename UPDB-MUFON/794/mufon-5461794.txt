{rjl} Bright orange “flaming” circle

From the flightline at Holloman AFB NM at approx 4am I along with 2 others, saw a bright orange “flaming circle” in the middle of the sky. It moved horizontally for about 6seconds before completely disappearing. There was no cloud coverage and there were no aircraft flying at the time of this sighting.
