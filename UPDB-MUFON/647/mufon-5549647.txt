Saw red-orange blimplike object

I was going to Whataburger that night for a burger of course and I saw somethingup in the sky. Like...something on fire but it was pretty high up and it wasobviously moving. I passed one treeline and made sure that it wasn92t just a flag or a radio antenna or even a sign on a building...it was toohigh for that. So I passed a second treeline and it was more faint. Then Ipassed a building and when I could see that part of the sky again, it was gone.At first I was thinking it was a plane on fire or something along that nature,but it just disappeared. Very odd.It seemed to me that what I was seeing was the exhaust from a jet-engine.Instead of there being a shape of the actual object, I saw the shape of the fireor jet propulsion. It was red-orange and it was flickering, but the object wasdefinitely moving. The shape of the emmission, I suppose, was blimp-like.
