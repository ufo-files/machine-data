{nd} Within 50 feet of a silent hovering craft. Loss of time followed

A friend and I were headed home from basketball practice one evening. It was in the fall of 1991 and it was a cool day and very overcast. We had a bad practice, so we were not talking and were basically sulking. We did not have the radio on and were just riding in silence. We were approaching the local lake and was crossing the land dike and as we were crossing the bridge, we both found ourselves looking up and to the left of our vehicle. For some reason we were stopped, or at least driving at a walking pace. We saw the vessel and is was about 50 feet from us and about 50 feet up from us. It was shaped like a triangle with rounded corners. It appeared to be around 3-5 feet thick, it was dark gray with a pattern that appeared tile like. There was a circular light in each corner on the bottom of the craft. As I said earlier, we were riding in silence, this dude was within 50 feet of us and it was totally silent. It seemed soon after we really focused on it, it flew over us, up the lake, over the trees and made a bank to the right, turned up and was gone. So from the location of hovering to out of site was about 1/4-1/2 mile and it took less than 2 seconds. That was simply amazing. As it was going away from us, the back was covered with a green band of light which was rectangular and covered about 90% of the back end of the craft. Still, no sound. As the light disappeared over the horizon, the next memory I have is "snapping out of it" about 2 miles down the road. I suddenly realized that I was driving, but I had no recollection of how I got there from the sighting location. As for my buddy, he will not even discuss the event with me, to this day...
