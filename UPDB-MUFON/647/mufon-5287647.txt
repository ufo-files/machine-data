Noticed an unknown object in the sky while photographing clouds and sunset in my yard, different from usual air traffic in area

Around 8:45 pm I was photographing the clouds and sunset from my yard, I noticed an object in the sky approximately at a 60-65 degree angle above me. I could tell that the object was spherical and pinkish in color while zooming in to it, this was different from the usual air traffic in the area, it was moving in a straight line towards the east. I zoomed my lens to maximum and was able to get 5 photos in before it vanished. Approximately 3 minutes and 30 sec later it appeared at same place and took the same easterly track, I took 7 more photos until is vanished again.
That day and all that week I was experimenting with the CE5 protocols, I don't know if that had anything to do with the incident or maybe just coincidental. Three days ealier I photographed from my yard what appeared to be some type of rocket , I reported this to MUFON as well, case #109934. Please see attached photos, several have been cropped and edited for bright/contrast for better detail.
