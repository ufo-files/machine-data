{rjl} Lights in the clear sky at night

Four lights above our truck. Four lights that would go out one by one and then light up again. Then it shot straight to the right. We looked for it several more times to our destination. This thing was bigger than a 747 and low in the sky. Freaked us out. My dad, who doesn’t believe in anything, still can’t deny what we saw. It was weird!
