{dr}Looked like a satlelite. Stopped. Reversed. Forward. Zig Zag Flight.

Standing outside looking for meteor shower. Noticed object, looked like satellite in orbit. Passing through handle of Big Dipper. As I was watching, it was almost like it knew I was. It Stopped. Stood still for awhile then darted in reverse. Stood still again then darted in original direction. Continued this zig zag darting action and stopping. Seemed to be heading away from Earth. Kept going in this zig zag flight pattern until it was too faint to observe. Was heading towards the Planet Mars.
