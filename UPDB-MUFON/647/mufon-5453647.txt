100 - 200 objects flying in a straight line East over Haarlem

I was watching the stars when suddenly lights came from the east. Looked like a satellite at first, but it went quite fast and then more were coming. The third object was slowly passing the second object while the objects that followed flew in a straight line while pretty much following the same distance.

When I saw this I called a friend who lives a couple of kilometres away, he saw it too. A long line of objects, about ten in a row from horizon to horizon, while a new one came up every 20 seconds. A few exceptions, where 2 objects flew next to each other or have irregular distancing. I estimate that I have seen between 100 - 200 objects all together. I also saw a bigger object with flashing lights, similar to an airplane, but a bit different and flying the exact same line as the others. A similar big object flew back to the direction the others came from.

It was quite hypnotizing to watch, since I can't think of a common explanation. More than a 100 objects flying in a straight line, who does that? I noticed that almost all objects looked like a bright light in the sky, not like outboard lighting of an airplane. And why would a military operation be so visible? Unanswered questions.
