{cak} Extremely bright solid red & green light-could not tell what it was.

On Saturday, May 3, 2014, had a group of friends over boiling crawfish when saw bright red/green light in sky traveling in a straight line from Katy Tx direction toward Tomball Tx direction. 1st thought maybe a helicopter, but no noise and lights were unusual, due to brightness. As an Electrical Engineer I would say the lights were more like extra bright LED's. Lights were not flashing as lights on an aircraft would be doing.It traveled in our view for about 30 seconds from the time we saw it directly overhead as it disappeared over the tree lined horizon. This was also seen by my son, an engineer at an Austin, Tx computer company, and his wife. Don't know if any of the other 15+ persons at the party saw it. At first dismissed it, but after a while realized it was something uncommon.
