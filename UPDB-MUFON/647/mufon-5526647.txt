UFO While Driving on I-49

I was driving home from work one night, I am in restaurant management, and while on I-49, I saw a light appear from the sky. At first I thought it was a helicopter, but the light shined on my car and then it was just gone. It didn't 'fly away', just disappeared. Whatever it was, hovered over my car from about 40 - 50 feet above me and it lasted just for a few seconds.
