{cak} multiple flashing star like lights that changed direction rapidly and would blink and glow

Strange star like shapes and flashes occurred in Tulsa Oklahoma on 07/27/14. There were about six or more star shaped lights moving rapidly in different directions. The would pulse and flash rapidly. I used an LED light and flashed back at the lights which then appeared to flash back the same number of times. The appeared to be a mile or so in elevation and made no noise. Many would disappear and reappear at fast rates. I felt as though I was being watched while this occurred and felt fearful. I felt like it was a possible large fleet entering the atmosphere.
