At 1:30 AM, I observed a small plane going in circle, repeatedly. Naturally I was curious as to what the occupant (s) was/were observing. Shortly thereafter, I observed a huge green ball of light, which looked to be about 60 degrees in elevation from my viewpoint. It was difficult to estimate how far away it was, due to lack of known landmarks which could have been used for reference. The light remained stationary for about 45 minutes, while the small plane continued with the circular flight pattern. Then, the object turned color, from green to fiery red. Less than 5 minutes later, the object darted South at an amazing speed and suddenly stopped. Then it proceeded to descend, at a rate much faster than a helicopter would ( I’m used to flying in helicopters, having served in the U.S. Army).  I lost sight of the object behind nearby tree tops. The small plane continued to hold the same circular flight pattern for another 30 minutes, until 2:45 am.
