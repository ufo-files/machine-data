Drone HD video of UAP fly-by

I was practicing taking video with my new DJI Dravic 2 Drone and was taking video of my home and the surrounding area. I took about a minute of video and landed the drone and went inside to look at the quality of the video when something caught my eye at 21 seconds into the video. As I rewinds it and watched again in slow motion, you can clearly see a black disc flying at an insanely high rate of speed pass right by the drone. By 22 seconds, it had already flew out of sight. I have video evidence and screen shots of the event.
