I was in bed sleeping i was 7 years old in 1964,my bed was at window on the farm,i woke and was stareing at the stars when i seen this bright dot going fast then it stopped,and then just took off in different direction,after few mins two objects just appeared they were round shape with coloured lights,they would disappear then appear again this was going on for good 10mins,it was clear night no wind and all the stars were out no moon,mybe i was lucky to witness this because i woke, and my window was faceing the stars.been bugging me for years im 58 now and can picture that night night quiet well.it seened they were just sitting there playing with there lights testing disappearing and appearing.then they just did not appear again didn't see them fly away.
