{nd} flock of buzzards cought my attention but behind birds i saw round black object. it didnt move whats so ever but just hovered..

Was at state park looking at campsites for later trips. Was traveling by car and was nearing the farthest part of the park near the river. I saw a group of buzzards flying around as it was a very breezy day. I was just watching them as they were flying in the breeze when beyond them i noticed a blackish round object floating in the sky. I had my friend(driver at the time) drive up to top of hill to get better look. As i got to top of hill. The boject was still in same place. Birds had moved on to different part of sky. No noise or any movement was made. I could see the sunlight reflecting off the western side (left) of the object so i could see it was round ball like object. I viewed it to the north west direction for a period of 30 minutes without any movement from object. I know what a blimp looks like and balloons and hotair balloons look like and being a very breezy day..this could not have been any of those. I dont know what it was but it didnt behave as any know conventional aircraft. I took three pictures and two videos of it from different angles of it. When i went to leave the area to head home we left the ridge and when i got back to the original spot where i spotted the object i looked back and object was gone..
