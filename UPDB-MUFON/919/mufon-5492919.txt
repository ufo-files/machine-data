{cak} Unknown cylinder shape object being trailed by jet/airplane over military base

I was at work on the local air force base in my area. I work as a civilian housekeeper. At the time I was taking the trace out and I for some reason I looked up. I watched this cylinder shape object slowly cruise across the sky as if it was just gliding slowly. I've never seen anything like this. Right behind it was clearly a jet or a airplane I don't know which but it had a strean coming from behind it. It was following the object at a steady pace never getting to close.I caught most of it on video with my phone. I put my phone away cause we're not suppose to have them. As I watched for the next few mins the further the object got it got smaller till it looked like a bright star and then it just vanished leaving nothing there but the jet. Monents later as stood there looking in amazement a military patrol car passes by so I went on with my work. I had been working there since April of this year but that evening there was heavy air traffic after the event. I couldn't help but wonder did the two have something to do with each other because I had never seen that much air traffic over the base since I worked there. Shortly after I was fired and told they didn't have to give me a reason why they were letting me go.
