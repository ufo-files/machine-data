Thought it was 1-3 Orbs as bright as a star, camera caught that it was an actual craft!

We reported this yesterday. But it came back! And this time we have footage. My distance and size could be totally off and it may have been bigger if we were up close. I think the thing that amazes me most the the intelligence part. Not because I’m arrogant enough to think we are the smartest things out there (that would be pretty sad if we were the best this multiverse could do, LOL) but because it’s like it helped confirm we weren’t just looking at a drone or something. When we flashed cameras it went farther away for a minute. When we cut of the lights and stopped using the flash it seemed to “proceed” with whatever it was doing. 

Truthfully, I think this thing was a LOT bigger than just the part we could see with the naked eye. Why? Because there would these lights that would twinkle from time to time in direct spots and each spot was in a perimeter around those two orbs. The other reason is my iPhone camera every once in a while really caught a frame of the object and on of those still frames shows why appears to be a open hatch, as if a vehicle or entities could leave from it. 
A couple of pics are the same frame with different lighting to see if the camera could pick up something I couldn’t. This was exciting but in all honesty pretty scary.
