Saw green and red flashes in sky. Thought it was fireworks, was not. Upon closer inspection it looked like lightening flashes that lit up the whole sky for a second or two. The flashes that I observed were first green, spaced out about 2-4 minutes apart. Then red, the one I saw real well looked like a giant red flash encompassing the horizon with a lightening bolt sort of beam of white light in the center...Then saw three lights hovering in a triangular shape, pretty far distance from one another. I am up on a hill above the city so I can see the airport, at first I thought they were planes, but I saw other planes, these lights were not moving and too low to be stars. Then all of the sudden they all moved away in the same direction at the same distance from eachother, away from my line of sight.
