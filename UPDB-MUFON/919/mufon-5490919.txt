{jpg} Ufo came from the west at a high rate of speed then when it got over us it shot to the north then south and then came back to center point

My Mother and myself where outside on the deck just looking at and enjoying the night sky. We were out there for under a minute when this object came from the west. I grabbed my Mother by the shoulders and turned her around to witness what I was seeing coming toward our direction. She grabbed her binoculars which where just inside the door. I ran in the house and grabbed my Camera with my 100-400m lens. My Mother screamed when she saw what she saw through the binoculars. When I got outside and got the object in my viewfinder I had braced myself against the railing to get the shots I got. The images were taken at about 1.5 timed exposure. We watch the object shine lights into the surrounding clouds that were moving in. They very bright beams of light and moved very quickly. We watch the object after it stopped shooting all over the sky as it hovered for about 8 minutes. Then it quickly disappeared into the clouds. We waited for a while to see if we could view the object again but were unsuccessful and went inside to look at the pictures on the computer. Needless to say, we were shocked.
