{nd} a moving constellation.3 lights in triangular formation,then four lights in a square, and one trailing. lights remained in position, very slow eastward movement

June 23, 2012, Belleville, Mi,48111, 146 High St. While standing on a dock at a restaurant at 11:05-11:15 p.m. 30+ people witnessed a moving constellation of one green,red and yellow lights, three in a triangular formation, four in a square, and one light trailing, very slowly moving eastward over Lake Belleville and into cloud cover. It was lower than airplanes fly. It took up a huge portion of the night sky. I saw people recording this on a cell phone.
