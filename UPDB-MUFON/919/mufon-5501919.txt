Saw a hovering triangle, which zoomed away at high speed

Hi, i did not witness this myself, but my husband did, coming back from Marrakech a couple weeks ago. Had it been anyone else, i probably would't report it, but he is not one to tell tall tales and, unlike me, he is not interested in this subject.

He says it was mid afternoon, he was headed toward the freeway and the object caught his eye because it was hovering, or flying very slowly. That's when he noticed its triangular shape. Suddenly, it shot off super-fast in another direction. He pulled over and tried to take a photo with his phone, but the picture shows nothing - perhaps a faint dot.

He said it was probably about 1km away from him, but looked to be the size of a passenger airplane.
