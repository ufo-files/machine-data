Two starlike objects moving near each-other in close formation. First, they were slowly moving in a south-west direction, then they ascended away.

I went outside for a cigarette at about 2:20am. I noticed that the sky had two unfamiliar stars in it. Then, I noticed that those two stars were moving very slowly, in a south-west direction, while maintaining their close to each-other formation. Then they stopped moving south-west, and they begun to raise higher into the sky, get dimmer and smaller, and vanish. During the entire roughly two-minute process, they maintained a close formation with each-other.
