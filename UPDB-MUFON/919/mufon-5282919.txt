It was round, like a saucer, maybe 25/30 feet circumferance, green or green & white lights

I was attending to baby born 7/30/69 in crib below large window when I saw this saucer like thing sit down in parking area of church across the street from our home. It was late at night. I wanted to go investigate but I recall stories of people who said they were taken. I had 4 children to raise and decided to duck, in case they were able to detect if anyone saw them. I decided never to mention it, as we were fairly new in a small town where my husband was a high school principal--it would not do for them to think his wife was a nut case, but I told myself it was something I would never forget and tried to memorize what I saw, In case anyone else saw such a thing at that time. Its shape resembled a photo i saw in an article recently printed in a newspaper, which it why I decided to report the incident. I meant to look it over the next day to see if the ground was disturbed, but I didn't and really regret that.
