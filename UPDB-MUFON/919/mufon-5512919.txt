Man in black

First of all: if you saw an entity (humanoid, alien, and so on) you must fill fields like "Where did it land ?" "What colour were its lights ?": I found it pretty annoying, this thing should be fixed (like, if you select entity some question disappears).

Anyways. I don't remember exactly the date, but I think it was at september of 4 or 5 years ago (I remember the month because in Italy we start school the 12th of september, and I only had some days from the start). It was night (probably 3 or 4 AM), and when I "woke up" I saw three men standing in front of my bed. They were still, the wall behind them was grey (probably the light of the moon illuminated a little bit the wall) while they were of a darker color, so I suppose they worn black clothes. As I saw them I fell asleep, I did not think anything like "Oh my god the're some thief in my room" because I were half awakened and half asleep. I MUST say that I never saw the film "Man in black" before that day, and now I'll explain why I tell you this: the next day I was curious about what I saw, but I was not worried at all because I thought it was a dream...so I searched on the internet "Three man black clothes" (in italian: tre uomini vestiti di nero) and one of the first links talked about something I never heard about: Man In Black. As I started reading the article (I remember you that it was in italian and it is still online nowadays) everything matched: the article said that they wear black clothes, that they usually go around in groups of 3 men, and other things...from that moment I just pretend it was all a dream, but something happened some time ago (I'll write another ufo report)
