Reptilian like entities appeared inter-dimensionally and communicated telepathically and with a visual text in my vision.

I was sleeping and was awakened to a rustling noise in my ensuite. At which time I sat up and looked, only to find three reptilian entities holding instruments they were looking at. As I watched them I was afraid of why they were there and wondered to myself of how they got in my house. Instantly I knew they arrived inter-dimensionally and I wasn't as afraid. I then wondered to myself, "Are there really such things as aliens? Are they really here?" Then one main reptilian started walking towards my bed and the other two seemed fascinated with their surroundings and stayed back, looking more at my ensuite. At that time, in answer to my question, appearing in my vision were letters typing out in neon green that spelled out "They're definitely there". I layed back in my bed, and I could see up close the one reptilian who came to the side of my bed. I saw his legs and the detail of his scales on his legs. I stayed there until they left.
