An apparently metallic sphere crossed the space during an air exhibition in Bogotá Colombia

During the celebration of Colombia's independence day, on July 20, the Colombian air force always makes an air show over Bogotá. My husband made several videos of the planes. Some people reported on social media that they had seen an unidentified object, when I went through the videos in detail, I also found the sphere-shaped object that quickly crosses the sky at the same time that air force planes make their exhibition.
