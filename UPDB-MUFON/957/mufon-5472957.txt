{rjl} Straight trail of 5 circles perfect straight line in sky & back, plus 4 or 5 numerous ufo's around it & disappeared

Upon driving south on the interstate I got south of Plainfield & north of Hancock and I saw 1 dot, 2 dots, 3 dots 4 dots, 5 dots like up every second in a straight sideways line. Then they went back and unlit the opposite way, same way they lit up. Then there were numerous, 4-5,weird ufo style lights that zoomed around the area by the lights that lit up & then disappeared. 2 of them stayed right next to each other & flew to the east. They didn't look like jets or planes. Not the same lights or the same way they travel together.then it went away. I pulled over on the interstate immediately & waited for it to light up again & that was it. I was overcome by desire to be frozen to take it in and feel the connection. I've only had one other experience years ago, much more attached to that one that controlled me for hours, but it was just a few miles north of there.
