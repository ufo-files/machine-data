{rjl} Beam of vertical light projecting from space to ground

I am a retired F-16 pilot and current corporate jet pilot. During departure from KMIA airport, my copilot and I witnessed a column of white light approximately 50 miles north of Miami International airport. The column of light was approximately 40ft- 80ft in diameter spanning from the surface up to infinity. We were on climb-out passing 10,000 and passed approximately 200yards from the light column. We both were completely baffled at what we were looking at. In my 35 years of flying I have never witnessed anything like this. Unfortunately, when traveling at 300+ kts, we quickly passed this phenomenon and had no time to take a picture. The column of light was stationary and was not a ground based spot light.
