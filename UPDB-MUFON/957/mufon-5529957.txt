small binking light, orange and green in colour travelling in a straight line in a WNW direction

I was star gazing with my sister, looking at planets, the moon etc. Then my sister asked me what a spot of light was in the sky. I saw what she was looking at and it was a strange blinking light, orange and green alternating, visible colouring to the naked eye. The object was moving in a west-northwest direction. The rate of speed that it was travelling at made me think that it was a plane of some kind. I aimed my telescope (specs: 8" f/6 Dobsonian Skywatcher, 10mm Plossl lens) at the object. While I was expecting to see a plane, which I have seen through my scope, what I saw was more like the star Betelgeuse, except that it was travelling through my scope faster than any planets that I have observed. It lasted until I lost it after 26 mins of observation, after which I lost it behind some houses. Apparent altitude was less than 1000 feet. No sound was heard nor any electronic or magnetic interference.
