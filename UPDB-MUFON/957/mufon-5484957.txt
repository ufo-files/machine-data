{jpg} Three green glowing round UFOs appeared over a soccer field with hundreds of witnesses.

This was the final night and annual closing ceremony for Raquette Lake Boys & Girls Camp in the Adirondack Mountains. Several hundred people were gathered around a large campfire in the center of a soccer field. The sky was perfectly clear and all lights were out surrounding the field. While the camp director was giving his speech, three fluorescent green round glowing orbs appeared rapidly coming from three different directions. There was no sound, and the objects appeared to be between 5000' - 10,000' directly overhead. They formed an equilateral triangle pattern, and began spinning in unison retaining the triangular shape. This lasted for approximately 5 - 10 minutes, then as quickly as they appeared they each shot off, in the blink of an eye, as quickly as they had originally appeared. There was never any sound. I have never seen any man made object move that quickly. They were literally gone in a second or two. Everyone who had been listening to the director's speech had their attention on the sky. Even the director himself stopped speaking because of the commotion among the crowd.
