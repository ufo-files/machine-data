I was in my back garden watching my Grand daughter playing on the trampoline then noticed what looked like a star but this was daytime about 5 pm in June hot clear day  how i first noticed the object was because i was watching a plane on a certain flight path which they seem to all fly on that same path. i went in doors to get my binoculars and zoomed on the object then it seemed to look like a metallic ball with a red lower bottom and i watched it for nearly an hour with out it moving, then through the binoculars i told my son to have a quick look when he came home and he said it could be a balloon but then he said why is it not moving how could it just stay still i told him i have been watching it for nearly an hour. we was both stunned then about 10 mins later watching through the binoculars a little white object that looked like a ball came in from above and joined it but this looked smaller or maybe further behind the object very strange. then a few mins later the lights just blinked out vanished both at the same time.  very odd  the next day i looked in papers and on internet but never saw any strange reports. this happened above hull east Yorkshire June 10th 2018.  the objects were not planes or helicoptors or jets  they looked to be a lot higher than normal airliners and i could only see the 2nd object through the binoculars. still a bit shaken up after all this. but it was amazing and i feel different about things now
