{rjl} Orange/Red orbs traveling from West to North West, 10-15 of the moved in groups of 3

Saturday 9/7/2013 @ 9:10 PM
Kankakee, Illinois U.S.
Home walking our dog outside.

Spotted orange/red glowing balls (out of the corner of my left eye) moving from tree line by Aroma Parks Two Rivers Festival to the West of our house. They were moving in a straight line from North to South, then changed to North West. There was a single unit in front, then multiples followed in groups of (3). Lost sight of all of them as they moved into clouds. Lasted for 10 minutes. No sound or trails seem from objects. Called wife out to see and she had no clue what it could be. I enjoy looking at the stars and have seen satellites, space shuttle, space station, and shooting stars. This was different!!! I have no clue what this was...

I tried to get a picture, but was too late.
