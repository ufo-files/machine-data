Glowing light over London docks

The date is approximately as stated earlier. It happened at about 7.30 am,.
I had just finished getting ready to go to school, when I caught sight of a glowing light out of my bedroom window to the south of my home.
I stopped getting dressed and just knelt on the bed looking at the light, which was just hanging in the air and not moving. I did think at the time it was just a reflection from the power station but the light was above and beyond it.
I watched the light for what seemed no time at all. However next thing I knew my sister was telling me to hurt or we would miss the bus.
The time was now beyond 8am.
I tried to get my sister to look at the object, but she said dont be silly and hurry up.
I have no idea if any body else saw this object, but it was there for so long I cannot believe nobody else had.
When I mentioned it to my parents that evening I got the usual reply most if not all get, "Ok, nice story, go do homework.."
That was probably the last time I ever mentioned it to anybody else for nearly 20 years, before I told a friend of mine, who said hd hadng seen that but had seen others himself. His answer made me feel better that someone did finally believe me
