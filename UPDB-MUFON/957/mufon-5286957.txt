Saw it hovering over tree top as I drove by

This was in 2010 on New Year’s Eve about an hour before midnight. I was driving along a curved road when I saw bright lights hovering just over the tree tops. Looked to be in a boomerang or triangle shaped. I instantly knew what it was and hit the breaks to get a better look. I saw it for about 4 seconds before looking back at the road as I was driving. When I looked back up it was completely gone. I remember the feeling of such certainty and then suddenly the whole thing felt fuzzy and difficult to describe.
