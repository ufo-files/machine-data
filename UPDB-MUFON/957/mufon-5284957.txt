Outside playing around with a pair of binoculars we were spotting planes and noticed a glowing object in the sky.

My family and I were at my parents house helping my dad fix the cameras on the RV. My husband grabbed his binoculars and him and our son started looking for planes. My son spotted a plane passing by, and as the plane passed back over us that's when we noticed the object in the sky. When we first saw it, we weren't sure what it was, so we looked through the binoculars. It was obviously a different shape than a plane because when we looked through the binoculars we saw a silver cigar shape with no wings or tail. It moved at a constant speed and at times changed directions. It was headed north-east. Me and my husband were amazed at what we were seeing, My son couldn't believe his own eyes and my father was in disbelief but admitted he had never seen anything that moves like the object did. We were all still in shock trying to figure out what the object was. The object went out of view eventually but as soon as it went out of view two jets followed in pursuit (i assume) in other words they went in the same direction the object went.
