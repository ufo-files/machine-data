Cigar/Rod shaped UFO moving rapidly across sky with no sound and HUGE in NJ  !

Last night(Dec.20th)in Hillsborough, NJ, my husband and I were outside on the porch at approximately 1:25 AM, and something caught my eye. It was definitely rod/cigar shaped, moving quickly across the sky. It looked like a thin cloud layer, but it was a definite structure, and completely silent. It appeared to be huge from our observation..with arm's length out straight, it appeared about 2 inches tall from top to bottom. It was headed southwest-ish, and went between Jupiter and Orien. It was about as high as Jupiter was in the sky at the time. I have witnessed UFOs before around here, but never seen anything like this!! Unfortunately, it went by too fast, and we were not able to get any pictures or footage. I am thankful my husband had also seen it, so I know I wasn't imagining it! We lost sight of it after a few seconds due to some cloud coverage. My husband also claims afterward, he had a ringing sound (like crickets) in his ears. I did not experience that myself. We definitely felt it was NOT man made..there was no mistaking this for a plane!!
