{jpg} Looked like a meteor with bright green light trail behind it

I was driving on the way home from Wal-Mart when I noticed a meteor like object in the sky and that's what I thought it was but I've seen it before. It was just an object maybe a little larger than a basketball..at first it just looked like a shooting star until I saw green streaks trailing behind it before it broke into 2-3 pieces and disappeared into the tree line. My initial reaction was "oh a shooting star" until I saw the green and then I thought about another time in the past where I saw the same thing on highway 123 a couple months back...I just pointed and told my gf "look!". I lost sight of the object as the green trail went back to gold..then it split into 2 or 3 fragments before hitting tree line.(couldn't see the ground)
