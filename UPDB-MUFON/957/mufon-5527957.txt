Black triangle with bright huge spotlights

I was driving on the highway and could see huge spotlights in the sky, it seemed as if they were moving very slow. the lights were so big and bright I could see the beams shining down to the ground, I knew id never seen any lights this big on a plan or any thing we have. and then as I was just coming beside it on the highway I could see that it was a triangle craft, jet black the size of a gas station or so, with two spot light on the front and one bigger one undernethe at the pointy side of the triangle, I pulled over my car and I heard no sound coming from it. it had came to a stop and was floating in mid-air, I stupidly flashed my headlights, about two seconds later it started lowering down to the ground, which gave me a better look, I could see every detail, the color the flat sides it had. I got way to scared and turned my car around and took off. Then I could see in the rearview mirror that it started slowly moving again.
