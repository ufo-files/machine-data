Bright light moving erratically

My middle son, Aaron, and I were on the back patio looking up at the stars. We could see random jets flying overhead. The we saw a light I assumed was a helicopter due to the light emitted feeling like a spotlight was on us, like when police helicopters are searching the area and you look up as they pass the floodlight over you. It was south of us, unknown elevation. The spotlight feeling stayed on us as the light moved in multiple directions faster than a helicopter and like a drone. There was no noise. As we were discussing what we thought it might be, the light lost brightness like the spotlight had shifted slightly. The light got very small and flew towards the south past the horizon. As we were looking, the National Guard helicopters lifted off from the base a few miles to the southeast of us and flew south. That was it. Wish I had pics and didn't know it was the first of two sightings in one month
