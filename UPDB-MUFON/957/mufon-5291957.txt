Pulsing glowing red orb

On 9/4/2020, we were awakened by a bright red light illuminating our bedroom. Thinking emergency personnel were at our house I went to the front door and observed a huge red ball of light hovering over our house. It was very bright and left a red dot in my vision for approximately 30 minutes after I observed it. This glowing orb then went from hovering, to moving across the sky while abruptly changing direction. We were fortunate enough to capture a portion of this object moving on our video doorbell. I have never seen anything like this in my life and I can’t seem to find any reports from anyone else on this particular night in Kansas.
