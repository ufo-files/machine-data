Woke up and looked out of my bedroom window and saw event. There were numerous small lights flying around (much different from airplanes), which would spark in sequence...They flew north, then appeared to fly south east, then back towards my general area (west), then southeast. In each direction they floated around and hovered. There was also one large bright oval orb to the southeast, which was stationary but wobbled. The large orb was much larger and brighter than any stars, and as the other objects flew further southeast, the large orb became more and more distant. I stepped out of the room for a minute and came back to look out the window and all of the lights/orbs were completely gone. This happened between 11pm and 12am on 4/23/18 and lasted for around 20 minutes. In the video included, you can barely see one small light floating fairly close- it appeared like it could have been hovering a few blocks away. In the photo included, is the bright oval orb- if you zoom in you can see the shape.
