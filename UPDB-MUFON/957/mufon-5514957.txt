(GS) Starlike disappear reappear

While relaxing outside enjoying a beautiful early Friday morning on August 17, 2012, a really bright and rather large "star" captured my attention. I spotted the star again when I sat back down after fetching my camera. I looked down for a few seconds to adjust the camera settings but when I looked back up, the star was gone. As I studied the sky trying to establish the cause of this mystery, the star reappeared as a bright speck in the sky and gradually grew larger only to disappear again after lingering for a few moments increasing and decreasing in size.
Im not sure that what I was looking at was a star, but rather a UFO. Anyhow, whatever it was, I feel privileged for getting to witness the whimsical phenomenon!
