Bright star on the night sky zig-zagging, stopping, flying in half circles, insane speed changes

I was in Hungary, in the least light polluted part of the country (Zselic).
I was laying on the grass, looking at the night sky, relaxing. I was looking at airplanes flying, satellites rushing through the sky.

Then I noticed a light, a bright star, that is moving in a zig-zag path, sometimes stopping and then accelerating to unbelievable speeds. The most odd thing this object did, was that sometimes it changed its zigzag path to a half-circle path, and it did these half circle jumps very rapidly.

When I noticed the behavior of this light, my first thought was this can not be a human built craft, because accelerating from 0 to 5 times of a satellites speed in 1 second, and then zigzag.

I followed this object with my eyes for around 20 minutes, when eventually it disappeared, instantly.

I would really like to ask someone educated, what could that have been? No satellites, planes, helicopters, space junk flies like this.

I drew the picture, that shows the path of this unidentified flying object.
