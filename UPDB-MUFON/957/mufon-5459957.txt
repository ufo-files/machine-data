{tjd} Traveling on airplane, noticed light, 1 light became 2, 2 became 3

I was traveling from Eugene Oregon to Phoenix Arizona on Allegiant air flight 185 on 4/3/18, landing at the Phoenix Mesa airport. We left Eugene at 505 pm, and a little after 7 we started approaching the Mesa airport. I looked out the window to the east and noticed a light hovering off in the distance. It seemed odd to me, so I decided to take a picture. I had my phone in my hand because I was listening to music on the flight so I took a picture of the light and continued to watch it. The one light all of the sudden turned into two lights. I snapped another few pictures and watched in amazement. Then the two lights turned into three lights, I snapped another few pictures and as I’m watching / taking pictures the lights all took off east and were gone in the blink of an eye. 

When we landed I waited to be the last one off the plane to ask the pilots if they saw anything. I asked the pilot if he saw anything to the east when we were about a half hour out from landing, he said no, nothing, why? I said I saw a light hovering toward the east at about the same altitude as us, and that one light turned into two lights, then three, and I told him that I took photos. He asked to see them so I showed him. He just said wow, I have no idea, he said the military sometimes does tests in that area, and he thought there could be a military base out there but he couldn’t be sure. I left that night not knowing what to think, I showed it to my parents and some friends, they all thought it was interesting, but had no idea what it could be. 

What stood out the most to me is how quickly the lights separated from one another, they weren’t really moving, and they were basically gone within the blink of an eye. Plus, the photos I took are all time stamped and taken in sequence, so there’s no question about 1 light turning into 2, and 2 into 3. I looked for a place I could contact the next day but didn’t see anything that struck me as legitimate. This has always stayed with me and been in the back of my mind. Recently I searched online about ufo activity and came across this CNN article where a few commercial pilots saw something strange about a month earlier in the same general area on 2/24/18.
