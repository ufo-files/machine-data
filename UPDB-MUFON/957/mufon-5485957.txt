a bright light splitting into two bright lights and then going opposite directions

Me and a friend where sitting in a park at night when I noticed a light slowly going across the sky, I pointed it out and commented that I thought is was a satellite. We semed to lose sight of it and then I once again saw another star-like light moving a lot faster than the "satellite" we had seen earlier. This light was moving one direction but seemed to be curving in another direction while still moving very fast. It slowed down and then my friend and I saw it eject or split into two fast moving lights, and they were also going opposite directions at a high speed. The two objects seemed to be the same size as far as I could tell which just opens further questions. As they sped in opposite directions they also started to fade into the night sky and then they were gone. It was during a festival and there were a lot of other people sitting in the park but me and my friend seemed to be the only ones who saw it. Later that night I told another friend who wasn't with me during the sighting and he said he had also seen a bright light moving at high speed, but did not witness it split into two like the object I had seen.
