UFO sighted around North Star and more spotted hovering and moving over Yorkshire

Was walking round town on the night and then we saw this unusual star-like object, it passes across the sky at a reasonable pace and was disappearing and then coming back, we watched it for around 10 minutes until eventually it went completely out of sight, and then as we walked around for longer we was looking at stars or trying to find more ufo's, and we ended up looking at what we believed to be the North Star, but as we looked around we found out it wasn't the North Star that we was looking at, and was actually a bright star, it was flickering and was going smaller and bigger, we then saw a strobe of green light go from it, this lasted for around a second and didn't actually go very far, there were other eye witnesses and we all freaked out at the same time when we saw it, so it wasn't something that i thought i was seeing. There is also a video on youtube of the 'North Star' growing and shape shifting at the exact time we saw it.
