Took a photo of the clouds and noticed two objects in the photo

Was sitting in my car parked having a cigarette when I thought the clouds looked strange. Snapped a photo of the clouds and when I looked at the photo I noticed to unknown objects in the photo. I never heard any noise to affirm there were any aircraft in the area at the time. I sent the photo to a friend and he said it is probably something on the lens of my cell phone. So I took another photo while still sitting in the same place 5 minutes later and the objects were not in the photo. I can't tell what these objects are, but zooming in on the objects I can tell they aren't planes or helicopters, and I think if they were I would have hear some sort of noise coming from the general direction.
