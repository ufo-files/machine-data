(GS) Taking out Trash. Round, bright orange org stationary then moving north slowly.

It was 11/10/2012 approx 11:30pm. I was taking out trash and taking out some trash to the trash can. While walking towards the trash can looking west I noticed a stationary bright orange glow slightly to the S.W. just above the tree line across S.E. 11th St. I at first was thinking is this the moon? I looked again and thought that was not the moon which was out and more directly south. The bright orange Orb stated moving fairly slowly to the north with no noise at all, much slower than a plane. It then when in front of my house and I lost sight. I went back to get my video recorder but it was gone. Several sightings were recorded at the coconut festival in Cape Coral and other locations in Cape Coral. I was the only one up at the time. the next day I told my room mate what I saw. He told me today that Fox news did a story on another report for a person in cape coral who video taped a group of them at the Coconut Festival. The reporter was Jihn Rupolo Fox news S.W. Florida.
