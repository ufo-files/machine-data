{nd} Stationary object outside of Menomonie, WI

I was driving toward Chippewa Falls, WI from Menomonie, WI after work on HWY 29. At approximately around 8am I noticed an object in the sky to the southeast that was about 600 feet in the air and maybe 1/4-1/2 mile away from my vehicle. I immediately pulled over and got out to get a closer look. Another driver pulled over behind me. The object was shaped like a top that children play with, but not pointy at the bottom. It was a dull white color and had no visible features. It was slightly tilted downward. I knew this object did not belong in the sky, I drive this route every day. After looking at the object for what seemed maybe a minute or less outside of the vehicle, the object appeared to shrink down in size, then there was a tiny blue-white light and it was just gone at that point. The other driver and I looked at eachother and he said, "did that thing just [Item moved/CMS/nd] shrink?" I said, "yes". He then got on his phone and just walked back to his truck. I called my girlfriend to tell her about it as I got back into my vehicle. 

The sky was clear...not a cloud in the sky. At first I didn't really get too excited, I knew it was something I have never seen before, but could be something man made. When I got out of the vehicle though, I just remember being really excited and I never even thought to pull my phone out and take a picture. When the object seemed to shrink down in size and then a light emitted after it was gone I didn't know what to think other than this was a strange event and I cant believe I actually saw what I saw.
