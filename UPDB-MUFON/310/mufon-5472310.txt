{cak} Took a picture of unknown object

I saw an object in the sky. It looked like it was in our south cow pasture. I pulled up to the stop sign and observed it for about 5 minutes. It never moved! I exited my car and took a picture with my cell phone and kept observing it for about 10 more minutes. Kept trying to see it drift by using the tree line, but it stayed perfectly still. I thought if it was a plane, drone, or blimp it should move even if very slowly. It never did! I had an appointment in town and had to go and it wasn1t there when I returned. There was absolutely no sound and I kept watching other drivers pass on the highway to see if they were looking at the object, but nobody seemed to be looking at it!
