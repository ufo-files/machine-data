2 Fireballs in the Sky?

I went to check the mailbox which is down the street from our unit. After I got back to my parking spot, I got out and looked at the sky as usual to stargaze. Immediately upon looking up I was in direct clear vision with what seemed like a perfectly round fireball. It was emanating this white from the middle and then red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and then it turned black and stopped. It was a tiny red dot for a few moments. Then my partner walked outside and I was frozen in place from awe. I tried explaining to her what I had just experienced and mid conversation I noticed a second fireball was in the sky and I forced her to look for her own two eyes. We began noticing these 2 fireballs hovering around very freely in any direction they wanted. They merged at one point as they were both emanating these brilliant colors. Then after a few minutes, the fireballs completely stopped radiating these rainbow colors, turned back into red dots and very very slowly started hovering away into the sky. I tried taking video footage or pictures but it was too dark out for my phone to pick up any light. I had dreamt about this scenario about a few weeks beforehand and realized that in my dream journal I was referring to these orbs as "little suns".
