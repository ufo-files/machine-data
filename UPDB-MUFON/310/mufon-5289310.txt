Square-shaped craft with distinct under-belly, port 3rd solid black, middle 3rd solid white, starboard 3rd sold black, ~600 kts at ~9,000 ft

Early January, 2021 at 3:48PM ET. Sitting outside on terrace facing south, just looked up to see what commercial aircraft was flying by. I live very close to the Washington Dulles Intl Airport arrival/departure pattern and aircraft are frequently flying the pattern overhead. Then I noticed a square-shaped craft at approximately 8,000 ft overhead, traveling from the west at about 70 degrees and at approximately 600 kts. The underside of the craft was very distinct. The port 3rd of the underbelly was black, as was the starboard 3rd. The middle 3rd was solid white. There were no lights, no sound, no exhaust and no contrails. I could not see the top of the craft due to its altitude. The sighting lasted about 10 seconds when the craft, still at altitude, disappeared behind the townhomes across the street. I studied general aviation and aviation weather in college and am an avid airshow attendee and aviation enthusiast. I know all about navigational lights, red/green indicators, exhaust, contrails, prop sounds vs jet engines. And due to the proximity to Dulles Intl, I am able to gauge distances and altitudes fairly accurately. I've attached a representational graphic to this report to better visualize what it is that I saw.
Having had another sighting just the other night, I've decided to report both. This is the first sighting. The other was Saturday, March 6, 2021 of a completely different type of sighting.
