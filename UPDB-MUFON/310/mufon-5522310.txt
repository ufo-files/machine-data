UFO floated over the top of our truck, very bright lights, no noise at all, just floated by our car, and across the road.

My son and I was driving to my mothers house in blue springs on 7 Highway, I was at a stop light, near Winsteads and Jack in the Box. My son who is 18 looked off to his right and pointed and said all excited "What is that" I turned to looked and it was floating over our truck, it made no noise and there was so many bright lights, it just slowly floated over our truck, I looked straight up into it and seen a round disk like shape through all the lights, but the lights were blinding, it flew across 7 highway, and then on down the street and changed directions. It was so large and had so many lights, I have never seen anything like it before, it had to have been from another planet!
