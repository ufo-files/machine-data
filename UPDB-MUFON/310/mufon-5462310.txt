{rjl} Lights appeared to be larger ufo with multiple smaller objects/lights coming and going for hours

My stepfather got home from work at 11:30 pm then he went out on the front deck to enjoy the cool night while he drank some beer, after a few minutes he came in and insisted that I had to come see what he was seeing. I thought he was messing with me but I went anyway and there in the sky was a stationary set of 5 lights 3 white and 2 red in a diamond shaped pattern. In either side there were white lights coming and going from the larger object. They would come out slowly then accelerate till becoming a streak of light then they would disappear, at the same time streaks would suddenly appear then slowing down to a light that would then disappear into the larger object. There would be 4 or 5 leaving each side and the same number returning constantly. We watched till around 2:30 am and we were freaking out and couldn't take it anymore so we went inside but it was still happening an hour later when when I peeked out the window. I don't know how long it actually lasted. My original opinion was that it was a mother ship with smaller ships going out to do whatever, but it could have been a smaller ship with orbs coming and going, but, of course I don't really know. My stepfather died in 1993, so he can't corroborate this sighting.
