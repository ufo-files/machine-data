{rjl} Flying off left wing of commercial airliner.

I was standing in my driveway. I hear the sound of a airliner. I looked up and saw the airliner which was approximately 28,000 to 34.000 feet altitude. Which is normal and have observed airliners at this distance many times. What was strange, I seldom hear them at that altitude. The aircraft was white to silver. I looked a second time and that is when I observed a silver egg shape object off the airliners left wing. pacing at approximately 16th of an inch from the wing. Keep in mind, the plane appears to be an inch or less at the altitude. It was was next to the aircraft. It stayed with the aircraft, until it was out of sight. If I were to guess, a jet liner wing is about 30 feet at the fuselage, the object would than be approximately 50 to 60 feet in comparison. Both the aircraft and the object lighten and darken due to the slight clouds. I watched this for approximately 6 to eight minutes until they were out of sight.
