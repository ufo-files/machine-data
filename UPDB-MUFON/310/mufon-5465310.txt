{tjd} Hovering, emitting light like from moon behind hole in clouds but clear sky. Then seemed to drop with rays like a trail

We were traveling hwy 49 from camptonville to moonshine rd. We first noticed the light and thought it was a bright moon shining thru a hole in the clouds except there were no clouds and the moon wasn't up. It emitted light rays ( 7 or 8). The road was very curvy and we were going down hill. We repeatedly lost sight of it but it seemed stationary at first. Then it seemed to start dropping and the rays seemed to follow behind like a trail with 3 or 4 light rays. Finally it disappeared behind the mountain. As we are fairly close to Beale AFB we see many odd things. We did not believe it was an aircraft. It was not the moon or planet, seemed to be near enough for aircraft but it wasn't like one. There didn't seem to be any object in the light, just the light. Several cars were pulling off the road, we assumed to observe what we were seeing. By the time we thought to do so it had disappeared. It was around 7:30 pm.
