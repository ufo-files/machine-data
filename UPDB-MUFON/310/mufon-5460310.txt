{jpg} Walked out of Ross with my daughter and suddenly knew there was a ufo about to appear

I walked out of Ross Monday night and I don't know how or why, but suddenly I knew it was coming. I looked around and my daughter asked if I was looking for a ufo and it appeared just behind a tree. It was moving left to right and right to left with no pause. It seemed to be moving up at the same time. I began searching for my phone and began filming as the object was slowly coming in our direction. It looked like a star but was flying very slow and low. My daughter suddenly tells me there's a helicopter coming towards it. When the object passed over us it made no sound. But from the rear view of the craft it was now two white circular led like lights. From no angle could you make out any sort of shape. The helicopter was sort of unusual in that it was surprisingly quiet for how low it was. I lost sight of them as they traveled over the store. I immediately came to the Mufon site and used the local air traffic tracker to see if they were listed and took screenshots. Nothing showed up on the air traffic tracker. I will include the video, a picture of the local air traffic at 10:27 pm, and two screenshots I took from the video. This is my first report but we have recorded so many sightings since they first showed up on July 7, 2017. Since that time, on several occasions I just know one is about to show up.
