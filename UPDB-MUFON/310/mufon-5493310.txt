{jpg} Dual red light, changing to dual green lights

I was standing outside of Pure Eatery, preparing to go inside for Dj Gig. I looked above the building across the street and observed two stationary, dual red lights. About 30 seconds after noticing it, it moved across the sky and came to a stationary position nearly above me. I began filming the object, which made no sound. As I filmed the object, several other people on the crowded street took notice. I filmed the object for two minutes and then stopped filming. I looked down for a second and when looked back at it, the color had changed from red to green. I began filming again and it suddenly made its way swiftly across the sky, until it went out of view behind the buildings. I stopped filming and about 30 seconds later, a helicopter flew across the sky rapidly.
