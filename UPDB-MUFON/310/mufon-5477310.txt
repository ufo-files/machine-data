Octagon like shape

I was driving on the highway to Scoudouc and just before the industrial park I saw this bright light. I thiught it was a street light and then realized there is none on the highway so then I thought it was a star. As I got closer I noticed the green light, it was above the trees I slowed down to about 90km/hr and noticed it was hovering, and spinning around and that it was an octagon like shape. As I passed it on the highway in my vehicle (12:20am & pitch black out) the lights disappesred.
