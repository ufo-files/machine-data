Bright light with trail

In the sky over NJ, witnessed incredibly bright light that was either stationary or slowly moving across the sky. It was almost as bright as the sun which made it difficult to look at and actually caused us to initially and momentarily mistake it for sun. Recorded 25 seconds of video which shows a trail behind it. The object never made a sound and disappeared after roughly 5 minutes.
