{jpg} Pink Brain Headed Entity at local Burger King

I was approximately 10 or 11 years old. I was sent by my mother into the Burger King on Plymouth Road to get dinner for my immediate family. The line was packed with easily over 25 people in two lines. Ahead of me by about 10 people, I seen the weird looking back of a "man"'s head. What was so weird was that his head looked like a pink brain. It seems he must have felt me staring at him because he turned around to look at me. His face, while humanoid, was not human and he looked very scary with big evil eyes. He absolutely terrified me with his sinister demonic look. He got his order, I soon got mine and left. I still remember the total fear as I waited for my turn. I still find it incredible that this strange looking entity was getting burgers and no one but me could see him.
