Driving N on SR127, south of the city limit of Paulding. 
I saw a triangular object flying low and slow from my right to the left, flying west. 
I thought it was a military stealth jet at first so I turned off the heater blower and radio to roll down the window to hear it but it never made a sound. 
Felt slightly frightened but interested to what was before me and pretty much went back to getting to Defiance Ohio amased at what I witnessed.
