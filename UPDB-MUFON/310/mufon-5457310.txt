{tjd} Long wing span, hundreds of lights, rapid speed

Stargazing, I had just turned from Northern sky to focus on Southern sky and immediately saw a UAP/UFO flying south from above me. I was struck by the expansive wing span (each "wing" was several 100's of ft. long~300-500?) with 100's of lights. I will add that the wings were not a perfect solid straight line, more of a slight back dip halfway. The nose of the craft had a short forward notch. It now occurs to me that it could have been a couple of craft joined, flying together. I only had 3-4 second view before it faded away.
