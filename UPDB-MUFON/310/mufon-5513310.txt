{dr}Yellow Orange light same color as a sodium arc vapor nightlight

On January 1st 2013 at approximately 8:30 or 9:00 pm, Exited my daughters apartment with my two son-in-laws, when we noticed the yellow orange light that was the same color as a sodium arc vapor night light. There was light low cloud cover and noticed what at first we thought was an airplane landing light. Upon further observation the light moved in a northerly direction then it appeared to stop. It made a 90 degree turn to the west then slowly turned back south after watching for about 10 or 15 minutes it either disappeared or went into cloud cover because we could no longer see it. I have been a skeptic up until now and there was no drinking or drug use taking place by either me or my sons-in-law. Just saw the video about the sightings that was shared on www.wordtruth.org and decided to respond with my story. I am checking with my son in law that I think may have gotten a picture or short video of the incident.
