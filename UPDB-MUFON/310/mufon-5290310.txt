The spin

From NZ currently living in Australia. I was following the rugby world cup in 2015 UK. When I decided to visit Edinburgh Scotland. I played an open mic night 13/10/2015 in Edinburgh city around 2230. I then decided to take pics of the area before driving to Glasgow. After taking pics I decided to drive on to Glasgow. 5 minutes later I came to a roadblock and had to uturn. The only street I could drive up was mound street leading to the church of Scotland. When I arrived it was the 14/10/2015 130am I remember looking at the car clock. I also saw 3 people they were leaving. I started taking pics of the church when the place started changing like a lite mist blew in..the sky was overcast it had been drizzling. The first pic was taken at 135am of the 3 people. Then the air smellled stale 207am the last pic this light appears hovering over me. It was like a hover craft blowing out air from its sides.then 3 more lights appeared the 1st light went into a spin clockwise around the steeple the 2nd light followed then the 3rd and 4th light followed behind.. fastest things I had ever seen. The first light stops the 2nd light hits the first causing an explosion which sounded like a tree falling. The 3rd and 4th light go straight through the explosion and disappear. I had taken shots of the spin but not the explosion because I wanted to see that what I was looking at was real. After it was over I could smell fresh air hear cars in the distance. My throat was dry as if I had been through some kind of ordeal which I can't remember. It was like the machines had froze time in a radius of 400 meters fuelled up had their fun and games then left. After viewing the footage everything taken from that night only was carnage. The first pic taken at 135am was taken at 224am? The last pic at 209am there were no pics from 135am to 2am. The date I arrived was the 14/10/2015 all the pics at that time were taken on the 13/10/2015. The pics before and after that time had the right time and dates. So something happened i can't explain. I lost my son to suicide, I lost my marriage after 32 years I lost my job due to covid. I just want to have a good year and a happy life from now on in. Have a great 2021..
