3 Orange-ish Lights, very low and close, moving towards my direction of travel

I'm a truck driver that works nights. I made a delivery to Aldi in Tinley Park, Il. I left at 22:47. I was talking on the phone with another truck driver who works with me. I turned from the parking lot onto Harlem Ave, headed south towards I-80. I immediately noticed 3 orange-ish lights in the distance. At first I thought they were airplanes, but then realized they were too close too each other to be airplanes. They were moving north and just to my right as I was driving south. There were many other cars out, that must have seen them. They were very low, seemingly only a few hundred feet off the ground. They passed right over me, a bit to the right. They appeared to have a strange quality of light, not perfectly spherical, glowing, like a ball of energy.
