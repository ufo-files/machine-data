It was night, saw a big fireball

This was in Korssund, west coast of Norway 20 Feb 2016. It was 4 at night, i woke up because i was thirsty. Got out of bed, went to the kitchen, got a glas of water.. looked out of the window, just for a few moments then a huge fireball fell stright down towards the sea, then it just stopped like 100-150 meters over the water for a 2-3 seconds and went stright up agian into space. I was shocked. I have wanted to see something like this for a long time now! and just in matter of seconds there it was. That was not from this earth! Made not a sound, It was on fire, it was huge!
