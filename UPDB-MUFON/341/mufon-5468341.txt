When I left my job, I first noticed an opaque object that seemed to float and that went up and down on the same vertical.
I took my phone to zoom in and started recording when the object started moving horizontally. The object crossed an approximate an angle of about 150 degrees from the vertical from where the displacement began until the vertical where I stopped seeing it because the building where I work hid it.
About two miles away in the same direction as the object is the Rep1blica airport.
At first I thought about the possibility that it was a hot air balloon but the sky was still quite dark for these artifacts to fly and the proximity of the airport cancels the possibility of the flight of objects that does not have control of direction like a hot air balloon.
