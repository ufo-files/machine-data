Strange orbs that show up on a regular basis

At my house from my front porch you can see 2 cell phone towers off in the distance. Almost every night sometimes, you can see these bright orange orbs that fly around the lights of these towers. They seem to change shape from round to cylindrical and we have seen them split into 2 and sometimes 4 separate orbs. They also change color from orange to red then to white. They seem to be pulsating and they move independently, but ive also seen them fly in a triangle formation.. I wouldve dismissed this as drones but this has went on for around 9 years.
