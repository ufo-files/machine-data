hovered over vehicle before accelerating away at great speed

driving down the road at night and a bright somewhat transluscent green/white light appeared from over a rise in the farmers field approaching from our left side (east). it was just above the treetops. it went directly over the car and travelled above us as if following or observing us for several seconds and then sped off soutwest and was gone from sight in a couple of seconds . i remember thinking it was like looking at an extremely large street light as it didnt appear solid but like a light and couldnt see through it. when over top of the vehicle we had to lean over the dash and look up to see it . 3 of us in the front observed it. we had gone to drive 2 girls home who had fallen asleep in the back seat. i do not remember dropping them off or the drive back to town.we knew it was not from our planet. strangely we never mentioned it to each other after that night
