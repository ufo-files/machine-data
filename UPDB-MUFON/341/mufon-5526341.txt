Observed circular object with lights flying over Lowry AFB

CMS/mcm In 1979 witness was 17 yo airman on Lowry AFB (now defunct) in Denver area. He and two witnesses observed a circular object with lights around the edge, flying very slowly overhead at about 200 ft. 50 meters wide, no sound, quite large, approached diagonally while they were walking in the dark in a snowfield on the base. They followed object for about 20 mins until they reached a fenceline and could go no further.
