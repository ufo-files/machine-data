{rjl} i saw big black triangle.

I was driving down North Prairie Ave when I observed bright lights coming over the tree tops. I thought it was someone coming in late for Thanksgiving. I pulled over and rolled down my window to listen to it and there was no sound. I kept watching and here comes a great bit black trinagle with 3 big bright white lights and one smaller red light in the middle. It glidded right above my car. I turned the corner on 10th street to watch it and it had totally disappeared. It was gone..no sound or anything. There have been several sightings of these triangles in this area for sometime. Our town is right by I90 and people have seen them along there as well. People have seen them during the day..my sighting was at night.
