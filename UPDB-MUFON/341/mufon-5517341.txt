(gs) I saw 3 lights in a triangle formation

me and my husband was going to get our son from his mothers we pulled up and i told him i wasnt going in for him to go get our son it was dark around 9pm and i was setting there in the suv i looked straight ahead and i saw across the field in front of me above the trees 3 lights in a triangle formation 1 would blink red then another then another then one would blink white then another then another they were stationary setting above the trees they did this for about 4 minutes then one started coming towards me but to my left then another went in a straight line behind it then the last one followed and they flew off and i didn't see them anymore i told my husband when he came out but he didn't believe me he says if god made any other beings they wouldnt come here like we cant go there
