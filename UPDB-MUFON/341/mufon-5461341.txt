{cak} Fast moving object directly overhead.

At approximately 9:30 pm on Saturday night, October 3, 1964, I viewed a light quite a bit larger and brighter than a star directly overhead and slightly to the north. It was stationary when first sighted looking directly overhead and slightly to the north. It then moved extremely fast to the east and stopped abruptly for a second or two, then it moved just as fast to the west along the same straight path. It then stopped abruptly about exactly in the middle of where it was first sighted to the west and where it had first stopped to the east. It paused briefly in this middle position for a second or two and then very quickly shot straight up into the darkness of the night sky, getting smaller and smaller until it finally disappeared. Each time it stopped it was very abrupt and sudden, it did not slow down then stop, it just stopped. 
The man standing in front of me, in the line to the paddock area of the Watkins Glen race track, turned to me and said "Did you see that?" I said yes and we both agreed we had no idea what we had just seen. We were attending the 1964 U.S. Grand Prix at Watkins Glen. I was fifteen years old at the time and was there with my father who had taken me to the Grand Prix that weekend. I asked my Dad if he had seen it but he had not.
