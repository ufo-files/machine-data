Triangular shaped object seen at 90° and tracked northward

In my backyard looking for satellites with my binoculars and noticed a strange blur. I looked at it with my binoculars and it was a high flying black equilateral triangle with 3 dark greyish lights at each corner. I saw it at 90degrees in the constelation Leo and it then flew through Ursa major towards the North Star. Duration was about 2 mins
