{jpg} Three objects hovering then vanishing one at a time.

On the corner of Madison Avenue and Hillsdale, in Sacramento, CA at approx. 2100, three orbs caught my attention in the sky of an almost burgundy color. Two of the three objects had their own pattern that they seemed to be scanning the parameter. They both came back to the "point" orb. The first faded out in a manner that seemed as if it disappeared by "hollowing" out from the middle. The second orb jolted across the sky one more time, then returned and faded out in the same manner. The "point" orb then jolted right, and then downward, in the path of a "7" and faded out as its past two had done.
