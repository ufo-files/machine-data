{cak} 2 sets of four lights, one straight line and the other like an oval

Was driving home and noticed four lights in a straight line stationary in the sky. I pulled over to get a better look and when I got out it the line rotated and started to move east. Shortly after moving, all the lights were connected by a bright light, so it looked like a single long line of light in the sky. When I couldnt see them anymore I finished the drive home. When I got there I noticed another set of lights in the sky but this time was in an oval shape. I didnt see this set stationary like the first but was moving slowly in the same direction as the line.
