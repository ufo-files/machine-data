Black and orange shapeshifting angelfish

I was waiting to board an early morning flight near Christmas. The sun had risen and an ice fog had appeared covering the entire Albuquerque Sunport which is extremely rare. The flights were delayed by approximately an hour. The terminals were fairly empty which can be normal for Albuquerque. As I was sitting in a seat parallel to a large window, Something caught my eye and appeared to my peripheral vision like a large black trashag blowing in the wind. As I turned I saw a blackish orange object with the initial shape of, as best I could describe, angelfish. The object had triangular panels that were moving and the shape was slightly changing as if morphing. The object was approximately the size of a large car. The object was hovering around 2 feet from the concrete flightline. It slowly moved to from the east to west direction at approximately 2 MPH. It was visible for around 15 seconds and then entered the ice fog. After witnessing the object I looked around to see if anyone else had seen the object but no one seemed aware.
