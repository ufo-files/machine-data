Howard Road Glowing

While on an evening drive near a friend's house, we witnessed a round, glowing orb, maybe 4ft in diameter, hovering over a very young corn field. We pulled over to look more closely, thinking it might be a large light on a tractor. We realized it was much closer to the road than we thought, and much larger than we initially realized. There was no tractor attached or sound. We could hear the young corn rustle because there was a gentle breeze. We both had a very strong urge to stand there and watch, completely at a loss of words and unable to look away. I was about 17 years old at that time (I'm 47 now). It very slowly started to move across the top of the corn, causing a tiny bit more wind because I remember the corn rustling more loudly. It stayed at a consistent speed, then started to ascend until we watched it blend in with the stars in the sky. We lost track, freaked out, and drove back to my house. I couldn't sleep with my window open the rest of the summer. My family has had several sightings, throughout our lives, starting with my parents, before I was born.
