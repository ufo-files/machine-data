Glowing object in sky

I was talking to my mum and her friend and I saw a star that caught my eye. I watched it and could tell it was moving (the opposite way to the stars) I thought the object was a star 
The object moved slowly and started off really bright but gradually got dim until the point where I couldn't see it anymore 
I felt humbled and a little fearful as we were on the subject of these sort of things. I sat in my chair and watched in awe as it moved across the sky and faded, I didn't think to take a picture because I was so intrigued by it. After, I was amazed after and carried on as normal 
I lost sight of the object because it faded and got dark so I was unable to track it
