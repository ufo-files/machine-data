Slow moving, oval shaped, red and gold glow

While driving home highway 212, east of Enid, OK at appox. 2PM I spotted the craft hovering slowly from North to South across the highway. It was a couple miles ahead of me but I could clearly make out an oval shaped craft glowing brightly with red/golden light. I tracked the object across the sky, it kept a steady speed a few hundred feet above the ground, while it moved it emitted a pulsing light that seemed to be scanning the surrounding land. It turned in a more easterly direction and disappeared over the horizon. I sped up to try and see the object again however it was no where to be seen. There were other drivers who could have seen this object.
