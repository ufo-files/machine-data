Travelled behind Chemtrail after Airplane has passed.

Was waiting for my ride to arrive and I looked up to the slightly cloudy blue sky.
Near a large Chemtrail a commercial airplane was passing behind( it slightly disappeared as it past it), going in NW direction. This is common in my area due to MIA(Miami International Airport) being close by.
As I continue observing the sky, I notice a small black object go in an opposite direction of the commercial Airplane. I found that odd that they would fly so close to each other. As a continue to watch it speed up across the sky in a strait path going NE travelling a few miles from the Chemtrail in mere seconds to a complete stop and it just hovered, completely still. 
Comparing by the size of the commercial airplane and the UFO I would guesstimate it was about 20 meters in length and black in color. 

Commercial airplanes fly at 31,000 and 38,000 feet — about 5.9 to 7.2 miles, so I assume that is why it looked so small to the naked eye and got a better view once I took pics and video.
