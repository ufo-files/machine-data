Lights in the sky

For the past few days on clear nights I've been going outside to look at the stars. I live in Wisconsin and was looking towards MN. Every time I go out there has been this light. At first I thought it was a star until I realized that it was sort of blinking. Mostly it looks white but then you see some orange...Also, it moves. It kind of darts off in one direction then moves back to its original position. Then it's just gone. Whatever it is it looks really far away. Last night I went out to see if it was there. It was, and it had a friend with it. One that was much smaller. This time the big one didn't move much it was the little one doing all of the work. The little one shot a beam of light down. It was really fast, kind of like it was just taking a picture. My mother saw it as well. I told her about the light and she wanted to see it for herself. She didn't see the beam/flash of light though. She had to go home so she missed that part. I lost sight of the smaller light it moved out of my view. But the larger one was there when I went in the house.I tried to get a video but my camera wouldn't pick up the light because of how far away they were. I have no idea what they were but I know they aren't stars. and I've never seen anything like it before.
