Heading down Westminster Blvd. traveling East toward Golden West St. . I noticed unusual flashing lights in the sky to my left looking North. At first glance I thought it was a helicopter. The object appeared to go very low in the sky and then disappeared out of my view looking South East. This all occurred in day light facing away from the direction of the sun.
