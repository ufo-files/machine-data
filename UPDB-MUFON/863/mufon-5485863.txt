{cak} I was in my yard letting the dog out. As I looked in the sky I could see an object flashing various colors and seemed to be hovering above the treeline. I got my camera and took a picture.

It was a beautiful clear night on Tuesday, August 25th in Metamora, MI. I went out in the backyard with my dog around 9:00. I'm always looking at the skies as I love this time of night. The surrounding area was dark without any neighborhood lighting and there was still a hint of twilight in the sky. I saw the usual stars and a couple small planes. I looked to the SW and noticed a light going somewhat fast and the colors looked like a traffic light, reds, yellows, and white. I watched it and I knew it wasn't a plane because each of the planes were still in the sky in the SE and NW . Kind of seemed like they were watching it too!!
It seemed like it wasn't that far up in the sky. It could have been about 5 miles away and up! Then it suddenly stopped moving, I could still see the colored lights but they seemed to change to a white. They didn't blink, they "throbbed".. I watched for about 15 minutes and even had time to take a picture with my phone. I came back inside to check my pictures and when I went back out, the object had disappeared. The planes were still flying around.
