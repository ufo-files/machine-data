Bright Lights

My wife and I were vacationing in Lincoln, NH.

The view from our deck was of a lake and mountains. There were no visible structures visible anywhere in the distance. Just mountains and trees.

At about 10:00 PM on Sunday night, April 12, 2020, my wife noticed a bright light (much brighter than any star) off to the southeast.. The light was higher than the mountains and appeared to be further away. Upon closer examination what we thought was a bright single light, appeared to be a close cluster of what looked like 4 lights. 

At first we thought the lights were stationary. But after watching for a few minutes we noticed that the were moving very, very, slowly from southeast to southwest.

We watched the lights for about 20 minutes to a half hour before going to bed. Getting up around 4:30 in the morning I saw the lights were still in the sky. But, by then, they were in the northeast, meaning that at some point they must have changed directions.

I took pictures and a video on my cell phone, thinking I was getting only pinpoints as seen on my screen. When we got home and looked at the video on a 25" monitor. we saw distinctly that there four distinct lights and one or two seem to eject smaller lights.

What makes this truly bizarre, and the reason we are apprehensive to report this, is that the hotel is at the site of the alleged Betty and Barney Hill incident.
