{tjd} There was a bright light and then a lizard girl appeared. I was a kid. She appeared to be of a similar age or size. She glowed. Was very pretty and crouched upon a vanity stool and just watched me. I was stunned. She had a light bioluminescent glow.

I was visited as a child. It was the most surreal thing to ever happen to me. I was drifting off to sleep. The room was pitch black. There were some lights, but nothing alarming. They could have easily been a car traveling up the road, but were followed by the sound of the window opening and closing and a soft glow traveling from the window to my sister's vanity stool. I was alarmed and under the covers and soon gained the courage to investigate. Before upon the vanity stool was a female of a similar age and size glowing. She was nude and had skin like that of an iguana. She had a soft bioluminescent glow and big beautiful golden yellow eyes. She was crotched down we we stared into each others eyes for seemed like eternity. I was afraid, but at the same time mesmerized by how pretty she was. Eventually my fear got the best of me and darted beneath the covers until the glow left followed by the sound of the window rapidly opening and closing as I reached for the light switch with one hand.
