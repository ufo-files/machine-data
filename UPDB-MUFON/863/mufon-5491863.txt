{dr}Hovering light, changed colors position then shot off.

While driving down a very remote road to work around 2am I saw a red orb star like object, at first I thought it was the top of a radio antenna but then remembers there where none in the area, as I drove closer it changed to white and hovered to the other side of the road about 500', I had the window down and there was no sound. as I drove even closer it changed to a blue color and after a couple of seconds shot off into the sky at incredible speed. I was perty scared since at points in the drive you can see the road for miles and there where no other vehicles in the area so I felt if something happened id be completely alone. when I got to work I told me coworkers but they thought I was full of it.
