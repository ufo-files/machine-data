Strange lights in the Sky

When we arrived home last night, my eldest son pointed at the sky and asked me what it was. There were four lights moving across the sky. I thought at first that they were some kind of bird that was glowing, or fireflies that had been somehow magnified; however, I quickly noticed that they were repeating a pattern. They would meet in the middle and then spread out into four corners. As they spread out, their shape became more oval. They moved fluidly. I had never seen anything like them before and for a moment, I thought they may have been spirit orbs. We observed them for several minutes, then my daughter became frightened and we went inside.
