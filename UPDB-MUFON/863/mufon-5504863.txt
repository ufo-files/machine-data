{cak} Orange glowing ball, that wavered back and forth in air, while moving easterely, went away by getting higher in sky

Came out of friends garage, with a friend to leave. As I was driving away I noticed her looking at the sky. I looked the same direction it was a strange orange and white/yellow glowing object. Color was almost radiating in and out. It was too high up to be a helicopter, the lights were not like a plane nor was it moving straight across the sky like a plane would. There was no noise. I reversed my vehicle and got out to be by my friend and see what she thought about it. We were both flipping out, because this was not anything we had ever seen before. She said she saw it come up above the tree line. It moved in a rocking back and forth motion while moving across the sky, very smooth in its movements. It then continued straight for a bit, before it left our sight by rising higher in the sky, not by continuing straight out of sight.
