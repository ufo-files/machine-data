multiple objects/ships of different shapes swooped down then away plus straight line flight over my home!!

We were watching a comet way off in space when a V shaped object with triangle markings swooped down right in front of the binoculars! It had lights on outer shape at the corners and along sides, it went up and out of sight, then the largest ship slowly passed over house and overhead low enough to make hair stand up!! No sound and translucent  25 white lights on top edge of one side of wing area...lights totally surrounded outer edges of craft. Right behind was a smaller version of huge craft. these flew over at least four more times over a three hour period  A small aircraft flew over not even aware of these huge ships!!  We watched triangles dip and shoot away many times during this time period. The local airport had NO aircraft in area during this except the little plane with two lights that flew over!!

Associated with the Comet IRAS–Araki–Alcock (formal designation C/1983 H1, formerly 1983 VII) The comet passed from north to south between 9 May to 13 May, passing through Ursa Major towards Cancer. It crossed just inside the earth's orbit on 11 May.
