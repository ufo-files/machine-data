{cak} Saw firefly pulsing lights around 5:15 PM for 20 minutes hovering in the sky in Sugar Land Tx, took a few videos.

Around 5:15PM Oct 23 2017 in Sugar Land Tx I saw something I thought was an approaching plane, but as I watched it I noticed pulsing very bright white lights. It didn't come over head like most plane but it hovered off in the distance all the while with white pulsing lights, almost like a fireflies. A plane will reflect the sun back and stay consistently the same hue; these lights would pulse and sometimes split into 2 lights and then go back. I compared other planes and helicopters and all of those have red lights but these were very bright white. I have 4 or 5 videos of the sighting. It made no sound, there is some back ground noise in my videos that are not related to the lights. I am not sure it wasn't some kind of drone but those kind of pulsing lights would have to be very bright and not at all "street legal". I lost sight just over the top of a neighbors house of it as it hovered and pulsed away.
