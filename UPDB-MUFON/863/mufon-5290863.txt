Green pulsating object.

While outside on my back deck having a cigarette I happened to notice a quite bright green light which was visible in the distance. I first believed it to be an aircraft given our proximity to the airport in Mirabel. It appeared stationary at first but then proceeded to make several seemingly erratic directional changes while varying it's altitude. I raced into the house to get my wife and she also witnessed the object. It then briefly hovered once more and the light it was emitting began to pulse for a few seconds before blinking off. The light returned a couple of seconds later and the object began to once again change altitude and directions. This went on for about 2 minutes before the object raced off towards the North East and out of sight.
