My 10 year old boy and his friend, Chase, witnessed multiple bogies entering the atmosphere at super sonic speed (sonic boom) then hovering 1 landing

My 10 year old and his friend witnessed a series of objects yesterday around 3 pm. I heard screaming and ran outside. I was able to see the boat shaped object before it sped off to the southeast. The kids heard sonic booms and the objects had comet like tails of ‘flame’ on their descent. One of the objects had landing gear as can be seen in his drawing. This happened in broad daylight with a couple of very dark cumulus clouds just west. 

I observed the bell shaped object at perhaps one mile or less distance hovering at about 100 feet. It then zipped off horizontally to the east before I could get a photo of it on my smart phone.
