{tjd} A localized fireball resembling lighting was witnessed in the forest just south of Flagstaff, AZ

While travelling S/B on I-17, just south of Flagstaff, AZ, at 2315 hours on November 25, 2019, a fireball that appeared to be localized lightning coming from a mist was sighted approximately 500 yards to the east of I-17. Electrical emissions resembling lightning were observered exiting a mist in the area where the event was taking place. Total observation time was approximately three minutes. The witness was driving S/B on I-17 at the time of the sighting. No photos could be taken due to the hazardous driving conditions at the time. The temperature at the time was 40 degrees F. It had been raining and snowing earlier, but was not doing either at the time of the sighting. Traffic was light at the time, but since the object was very bright, other drivers may have witnessed the event. The event was immediately reported to me by the witness because I am a MUFON trained UFO field investigator.
