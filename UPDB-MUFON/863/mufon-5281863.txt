Hovering orange/yellow ball of light

I am at Torry Island Campground in Belle Glade Florida, right at the SE corner of Lake Okeechobee and by the Everglades. I saw an orangish-yellow light above the hill that surrounds the lake and watched it heading east. It then went the total opposite direction for a few seconds coming towards me, then went back in its original direction. It was moving too slow for it to be a plane because it would've fell out of the sky. Not only that, but it wasn't too high up either. It was also silent and the light just disappeared after it turned back in its original direction. I tried to get a good picture but got one right before it disappeared
