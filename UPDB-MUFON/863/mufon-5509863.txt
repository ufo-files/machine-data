{rjl} Very bright orange & red ball

UFO was in the S/E sky above Irvine. CA., heading N/W toward Tustin, CA. Its path of travel came down at a steep angle, then flatten out and continued N/W. Object came to a stop and remained motionless & hovered. I thought it was a helicopter but it made no noise. Object continued to emit an extremely bright orange & red light that reflected off the surrounding overcast (not clouds)like a halo. Light looked like the color of a burning match. It appeared that the entire object was illuminating not just typical on board light(s). Object remained motionless until a small airplane began to cross its area, as commercial aircraft typical fly this route into John Wayne Airport. As the aircraft got closer to the object it immediately did a slight down turn maneuver shot up at a high rate of speed and appeared to just blink out of sight. Total duration of the event was 30 to 40 seconds
