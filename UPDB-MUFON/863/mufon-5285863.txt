Ball of light was traveling south until I used my spotlight. Then it stopped

On a mostly clear night before 9pm, my friend and I was outside playing with a spotlight. We had just went for a walk and were cooling down. I was using the light to shine up into a few clouds. I also used it to hit the cliffs and some trees. I did shine it at some jets flying over but not until it was right over me. We both saw a ball of light moving north. I knew it was a satellite. Nest was a larger ball of light. Similar to Mars size. I used my spotlight to signal it in a joking manner. After a few seconds, it stopped. It hovered above us for four seconds and then changed direction to the east. It looked as if it was climbing in altitude and then slowly faded away. 
A few years ago we saw a similar bigger ball of light coming from the north. It was fast and at one point became 10 times brighter and then changed coarse towards the northeast. 3 of us saw that.
