{jd} Light was very bright gold it split into 2 and then came back while one stayed the whole time.

I was driving on the I-5 freeway and noticed two golden circular lights in the southern facing sky. One of the lights split into two causing there to be three lights now and less than 30secs the two lights faded away completely leaving one light. This light lasted for another five minutes (while I followed it on the freeway) and then it also disappeared. It did not fly away it faded out like a light switch turning out.
