Bright ball of light m oving from West to East southeast

I was on my deck, facing south, looking at the sky at 5AM. I noticed a white ball of light moving out of the west, heading East to southeast a 5:06AM. As the light grew closer, it had a white glow but no other lights on the object. There were no blinking lights as you would see on wings or a tail if it were a plane. When the object got close it disappeared behind some trees directly behind my house. I realized that the object made no noise at all. I could hear traffic from the highway picking up a bit, from about a mile away so I thought it was strange to not hear anything when the white object passed. I first noticed it because it was very bright and there no other objects moving in the sky. I assumed it was a plane.
