{rjl} transparent morphing ufo

6/25/2015 11:30 am pst..driving thru Carbon Canyon in Chino Hills California object appears in the sky..it's base the shape of an airplane. its wings are ridged.some pointing up..some down.its flying sideways..hovers..then starts moving..no windows..one circle shaped light. it starts changing colors its base sky/baby blue.its ridged edges were a transparent white.it starts to appear transparent in color. And it was changing shapes as well..more ridged. the way paper looks when its crumbled..thats the shape it started to take on. An actual aiplane flying in its direction. The airplane must have seen it because it changed it's course. The object disappeared. 11:55 am..In chino..and low and behold a military chopper flying towards Carbon Canyon. That was absolutely not a man made object!
