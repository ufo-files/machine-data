{nd} bright ball then a flying wedge

Im not sure of year but I was like 8 or 9 years of age at the time. I was sitting on my front porch drinking a soda about 8pm waiting for parents to come home. I noticed this really bright light north of town and it was quite large. It hung there for over an hour and other folks came out of there house and i yelled at there kids to look at the light. About an hour later it went out with out moving. Then I look up and going above the house about 500 feet up is a flying wedge type object with about 11 lights that were gray in color. I read the paper next day and it said it was pidgeons lit up by city lights. Thought that was funny do to the fact we lived about 6 miles outside the city. I watch your show hoping to see something on this. Never have. Was hoping you had something on this cause it was a sight to see and one you don't forget.  Thanks for your time
