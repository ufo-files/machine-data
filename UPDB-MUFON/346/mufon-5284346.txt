Blue Orb {moved at incredible velocity over my head} Over Dearborn, MI, USA - {djs}

I was leaving dinner with my younger brother and as we opened the door to exit the restaurant, I look up and immediately noticed how clear the sky was. No clouds in sight and commercial airplanes can be clearly seen off in the distance. The sun had just set, but there was still enough light to easily see the evening sky.

We live near DTW Airport and really enjoy plane spotting as they fly over our house / neighborhood so we know all the usual cues indicating a conventional aircraft.

What I saw moved at incredible velocity over my head and was gone before I could realize it. The whole event was around three seconds from seeing the orb to it practically disappearing due to how fast it was traveling.

The coloration was electric blue with a white light reflection. I couldn't make out the specific shape, but the color was so vibrant. From what I could recall as I remember the sighting, it was spherical in shape. I could not be certain of any hard edges indicating that it was a solid object though.

There was absolutely no sound whatsoever. No contrail. No indication of any conventional propulsion. No blinking lights presented on typical aircraft.

It was difficult to discern how high up the craft was. Definitely higher than the planes in sight, but since we are close to the airport, the planes usually fly pretty low around the area.

This was my first sighting and I am just blown away at what I witnessed. I'm a hobby aviation enthusiast and have attended quite a few military aircraft demonstrations. I have seen F-22s, F-35s, F/A-18s, F-15s, etc and appreciate the maneuverability and acceleration of those types of aircraft. This orb was on a whole other level of speed. It was truly incredible.
