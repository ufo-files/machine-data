{dr}Bright red, could be considered bright pink or orange

I went outside to walk my dog at 11:45 pm eastern time. While outside I saw two very bright objects as they came above the horizon at approximately 160 degrees from my home. The objects were moving very fast towards me yet at an altitude as to not appear threatening. The objects did not make any sound. I watched with fascination as I could not see any contrail although the night sky was poorly lit., There wasn1t any sound and no aircraft navigation lights. My first thoughts were they were military aircraft departing the south end of the Avon Park bombing range R-2901 and perhaps heading towards the Florida panhandle. Their heading appeared to be 340 - 350.
When I saw them I immediately knew something was not right. Fortunately there were multiple lights and many more continued to come from the same area traveling in the same direction. Over 15 minutes time, I took pictures and video with three different cameras. My former wife who lives nearby contacted me to ask about aircraft lighting. We discussed the events and both saw the same thing. Now, Im hearing reports from everywhere about this same event. Here is a link to a YouTube video from Puerto Rico [Link moved to FI section/cms/tg] . My video is identical from Lakeland Florida. According to the description the Puerto Rican video was captured 45 minutes before I saw them. That being the case along with other sightings I have read about, I no longer believe these craft were out of the Avon Park bombing range.
Although the red lights appear to flicker in the video, they did not flicker, they did not have aircraft rotating beacons, nav lights, strobe lights nor, tailing white lights. I have more pictures if youd like.
