Unnatural centralized lighting cloud making no noise

A relative called and told me to go outside and look north west. Massive slowly moving centralized lighting cloud formation going north over New Braunfels. No other clouds in the sky. Lighting can be seen radiating from a singular point inside cloud over and over. Absolutely zero noise, no thunder, wind, humidity associated with rain, even the wildlife seemed to be quiet or respond to the atmosphere. Small orb seen but may be insect. A few of the movements it makes are questionable.
