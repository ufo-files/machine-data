Curved line of light

I was at the top of my city, a little park-like compound in the center on a hill and observed the stars. I was about to leave, packed the blanket in my backpack and looked randomly in the sky.
The object that i saw was penny-sized in the sky, a curved line of light, brownish-orange colour tone, i don1t know what i saw, looked repeated times precisely at it. it moved from east to west in a almost straight line in a consistent speed, faster than any kind of plane but slower than any meteorid i ever saw. I watched it in awe but felt nothing special. Also no sound was heard. I lost sight of the object because of some trees and couldn1t change my position fast enough.
