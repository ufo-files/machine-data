{jpg} White unknown object in southeastern sky above St George, Utah, USA

Appeared to be two white balls on top of eachother. At first glance I thought it was a cloud, but it did not move or change shape for the hour that I watched. Witnessed it about 10:00 am on Saturday September 7th, 2019 while I was walking my dog. That evening after the sun went down, there appeared to be a light in the approximately same location as the unknown object. The light was like you would see from a star or a satellite. 
When I got to work on Monday morning my sister shared something she saw in the sky on Saturday morning before I shared my experience. She took a picture of what she saw. It is identical to what I saw. Her object was in the northern sky. She will be posting her experience on here along with the pictures she took.
