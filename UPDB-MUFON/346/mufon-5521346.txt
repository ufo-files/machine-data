Three lights that moved around up down left right faded in and out

It was about 6:10 pm Jan. 16th 2012 over Grass Valley Ca. Three lights were hovering over the town. They moved around slowly and faded in and out. Two of the lights were close together and one was further away from the others. The two that were close together would move around each other. I have scene lights like these before over Santa Rosa Ca.
