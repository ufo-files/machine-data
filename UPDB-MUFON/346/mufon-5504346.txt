{cak} Observed 5-6 objects flying in formation followed by lone object all orange color

Driving on Kaloli ave ocean bound with my friend in passenger seat, noticed objects flying in zig zag formation all orange in color, I pulled onto 12 th ave to the right or south east I stopped and turned off the engine and the lights of my jeep, me and my friend both jumped out of the jeep,and observed the objects fly in a changing formation in south west direction, I took 4 pictures of the objects with my I phone, my friend got alarmed and asked me not to take pictures of the objects he thought that they changed direction toward us, at this point the object flew behind the clouds , a home owner came out and asked us what was going on at that point another object flew from the direction of the ocean, it followed the other objects , the homeowner saw the last object, we drove home and that is when I looked at the pictures on my I phone , I was able to enlarge the pictures and to my amazement I saw a structured object of unknown origin like nothing I've ever seen ,I texted my wife the photos , she transferred the picture to her I pad , the pictures we're enlarged even more and we could see it was nothing that we could recognize as any known aircraft.
