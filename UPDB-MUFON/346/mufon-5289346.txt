Long flat object with no sound. Traveling at a high rate of speed.

I was outside with my son, and off in the distance to the east, I witnessed a long flat dark object about a mile or so away. The object had no sound whatsoever. It was flying north to south about 1000ft or higher at a very high rate of speed. The object flew at a level flight trajectory for a short distance from time of first sighting and made a left turn to the east. The object did not bank to the left like a normal aircraft. Instead it just changed its flight path to the east and disappeared into the clouds in a blink of an eye. 
Now you must note, that it was flying in the direction of Moody Air Force base in Valdosta Ga. I pondered the though if it possibly being a drone, however I have never seen a drone that large or travel that fast. I will leave the assumption up to whoever reads this description. Have a good day.
