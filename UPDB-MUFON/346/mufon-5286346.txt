While watching fireworks of our neighbors we noticed first one object followed immediately by another.

We were staying at the family’s lake front home on Kennebunk Lake in Lyman, ME, and watching a few fireworks going off across the lake to the east when our granddaughter noticed a flying object coming from the south east heading to the north east. It was steady and very bright. My husband is a former pilot and had never seen anything like it. A second one appeared and followed the first. There was no wind and they did not change direction yet were moving at a steady pace. They did not blink or wobble but were very steady traveling to the northeast. First I took a still image of the first light, then a video of the second. At first we thought it was a Chinese Lantern, but we’ve looked at videos of those and these did not behave or look the same.
