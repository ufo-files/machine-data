2 Lights in space same size as stars. One gold one white. Moving at hypersonic speed. Stopping dead, changing direction, stopping reversing direction. Faster than possible to be human craft. Incredible speed, no sound. With witness. After 20 minutes the lights took off seemingly into deeper space at incredible speed. Lights were very high in a clear sky with lightning (no clouds in the sky which was strange - fork lightning, which is the reason we were looking at the sky. No thunder. No noise from Star like lights. It was out of this world. I don't know what I saw even though I saw it. My companion saw the same, I kept asking him, "are you seeing this". Neither if us were drinking etc , just smoking a cigarette outside. Country area with no lights, clear view of sky. If I didn't see this I would not believe in UFO's. As hard as I try I can not explain what I saw, although not amazingly elaborate on these lights were moving at hypersonic speed. Stopping dead, resting and taking off in several directions before resting and eventually disappearing. I've never told anyone for fear that I would be considered crazy even though there was a witness. Gerry
