Grey Alien on Wildlife Camera Infrared Image

We have a Bushnell Natureview Wildlife camera that we have out at the back of our property which is secured to a fence post and about a foot off the ground. It faces North towards our property which is located just North of RAF Shawbury airbase in Shropshire UK. The camera is set up to take still images of anything that sets of the infrared trigger and has 32 no glow LED lights so not to spook any anaimls when it triggers.
This morning at 3:31am UK time it was triggered by a fox and a still image was taken by the wildlife camera. the image was stored onto a 32gb memory card and when we checked it today 10/09/16 to see any images we noticed that on this particular trigger at 3:31am in the left of the frame was a strange looking head or top half of the body of what looks like one of those grey alien type things you see on the TV in movies etc. In the image it is to the left of the frame and looks like it is approaching the camera and looking straight at it. Now when this type of wildlife camera triggers it uses no glow or black flash infrared technology which the human eye cannot see. So whatever this thing was to us it seems as though it noticed the camera triggering and taking the still image!
We have never ever seen anything like this before in our lifes and apart from the odd fox and rabbit certainly nothing on our camera like this. It does not look human to us at all and now we are actually scared to thing that we may have extraterrestrials running around our land! There have been some pretty weird things going on at night at the nearby airbase so is this connected? If anyone can help or advise please do let us know as this is freaking us out.
