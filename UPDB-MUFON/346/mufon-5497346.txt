{cak} Sighting at sea

While looking around your site, I decided to go ahead and report something that happened long ago. I was lead radar operator at the time aboard the USS Tolovana AO64. We were operating in the northern Pacfic southeast of Russia and east of Japan. I am not sure of the month or year but the time frame of autumn 1971 should be close. I was on watch at the time and was manning our SPA-4 radar. I reported to the bridge that I had a contact coming toward us out of the NE at the unbelievable speed of 750 knots. A couple of minutes later the bridge responded that they had a visual on blinking red lights. Watching the scope I calculated that the contact came to an abrupt stop at a range of 2000 yds. The bridge responded with confirmation that the craft was hovering. It was observed by myself on radar and by the bridge visually, to hover about 5 minutes. Then just as suddenly as it appeared, I tracked it move to the east at 750 knots. As I recall, I was sweeping at a range of less than 50 miles. The SPA-4 was a surface radar that would not detect at a very high altitude. The visual horizon from the bridge was about 15 miles. When I first saw the craft on radar it was approximately 30 miles away. Hope this can be of some help even though it was a long time ago.
