{tjd} Strange lights in the sky

Driving down a country road saw a light in the sky. It looked to low to be a plane and to high to be a tower. It wasn’t moving and as we got closer we could make out 3 lights. 2 white and 1 red. They were square shaped. As we went by it stayed still and we went to turn around. As we went back towards it it was all of a sudden way up in the sky and flying up. A plan could not get that far away that fast.
