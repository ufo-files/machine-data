10 large bright Orange/ Yellow Orbes traveling east to west

May 27th 2012 about 9:35pm, I was sitting outside with my brother, nephew and a neighbor (Nelson) we were just talking about general events when Nelson said, 1Look at that!1, my brother and I both turned to see three bright Amber Orange Balls rising in the night sky. My first inclination was that of fireworks, and then I realized fireworks don1t travel in a straight line formation, which is when I ran to the apt., and retrieve a camera and record what we were witnessing. The objects arose from the East to a height between 4,000 1 7,000 ft. traveled form the east to the west, the colors were as I said bright Amber Orange with a faint White center. 
We witnessed a total of 10 of these objects, there were three sets of three and a single one by it1s self. The three sets of three were timed about 10 minutes apart as well as the single one. The time of duration was approximately 38 minutes from the first sighting to the last single object. They all ascended to the same altitude and traveled east to west and just seemed to fade out. The lead one of each group always seem to be the last one to fade out, when the last one faded out it almost seemed to be cloaked in a haze, like a cloud cover but the ceiling was unlimited, no cloud cover at all, we were able to see what seemed to be a satellite pass by. After the first group of three, my nephew said, 1 Look, two jets seem to be following them1.



Long. And Lat. Is as follows, 40.62657, 82.24239



The weather was clear and the wind was calm no breeze where we were at.
