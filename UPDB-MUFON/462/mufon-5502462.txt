Orange/Yellow light. For sure not a plane or helicopter. Silent. Moved faster than a plane. But not to fast. Static light.

At home. Looking at the ocean at night. Orange/Yellow light in the sky caught my atention. Immediately noticed that it was not a plane/helicopter. I saw the light for 20 seconds and ran inside to call my parents to see it. When we got back outside it wasn't there anymore. It was moving in one direction, faster than an airplane, but it was still supposed to be visible when we got outside, considering it's speed. I see planes and helicopters everyday and I'm sure it was something else.
