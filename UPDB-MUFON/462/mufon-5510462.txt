{cak} Circle with swirling colors

After waiting a year I am going to report this because I cant get it out of
my mind. Between 10 and 10:30 pm on 5/11/12 I was headed to my mail box
before I left for work(I work overnights) and saw something in the sky. I always look at the sky at night because I love the stars. It appeared about the size the moon would look except it was low in the west sky and at that time the moon would have been rising in the east. It was a sphere that had multiple swirling colors and was stationary. At first I thought it was something on fire, but there were no flames or tail or smoke. It was perfectly round. After a few minutes, on the side of the circle facing south, a rectangle opened up and the thing started moving very slowly toward the north. I watched until it was out of sight as it went behind some low clouds to the north, maybe 10 minutes or so. I was so mesmerized by it that I didn't think to take a picture. All of a sudden I found myself standing in the middle of the street with my mouth open. I have tried to draw it, but I cant capture the feeling of the swirling motion of the colors. It was amazing to witness this. I have at times been watching something on UFOs and thought "Why didn't they take a picture?". I just couldnt believe what I was seeing. I have no idea how large or how far away this was. If someone had just glanced up and not really paid attention, they might have thought it was a harvest moon. But I stopped and looked at it closely and saw the swirling colors. I have never experienced anything like this before or since.
