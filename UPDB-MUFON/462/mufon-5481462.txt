{cak} It looked like three stars in the sky flying in Triangle formation far from each other in same speed. Only one had left a trail

My window has a view to the air space of Newark airport in NJ. I always what the airplanes landing and taking off when I'm in my living room. On August 17th at 7:20pm, I was surprised when I saw initially 2 (I thought was airplanes) objects flying close to each other. They were high enough in the sky not to affect the airport traffic, but I could see they were flying relatively close to each other, so I wonder what they were. I know airplanes don't fly together like that. I took my phone and zoomed to take a picture and what a surprise when I notice the 3rd object on my picture. They were in the triangle formation, but the third one was kind of far. I watch them crossing the sky for about 5 min. They were shining like a star and reflective. I stared all the time I could trying to see something that could indicate they were airplanes, but as they move to another angle, I still seeing three bright objects like a star. I didn't see any wings.
