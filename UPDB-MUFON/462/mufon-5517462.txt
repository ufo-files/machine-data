{ck} Appeared to be 3 orbs. On the same flight path.

I was outside sitting on our deck, which is on the south side of our house, watching the neighborhood kids in the apartment complex on the south side of 79th street shoot there fire works. It was approx 9:42pm. I saw what appeared to be a glowing object coming from the south. As I watched it it became bigger. First thinking it was some sort of a new firework. After a few minutes I realized that it couldnt be. Because no smoke trail was being left. Behind the first one was two more. Both were identical to the first. No sound was coming from these objects. The path that these objects were on was at first on a north northwest path and then made a change to a north north east path. These objects glowed red/orange/white. Changing randomly. I watched these objects for approximately 10 minutes until I lost site of them over the trees to the northwest of my residence.
