A large 'V' shape flat dark craft with a few twinkling lights.

I was star gazing with my telescope early in June 2004. i was looking at Andromeda galaxy when i suddenly lost sight of it when i pulled away from the telescope i saw about 40 to 60 twinkling lights moving slowly all together, my first thought was "Oh cool the ISS" but then as it moved further away i seen the silhouette in the glow of the city lights and i noticed a V shape with a line of the twinkling lights on the edges of the wings or side of the craft. i watched it as it blocked out stars and moved further away for about 6 to 8 minutes until it was no longer in sight. i looked at Andromeda galaxy again but was soon bored knowing what i just seen so i called my girlfriend and excited i told her what i saw but she said i was crazy and just must be tired so i never really talked about it after that. i went outside to get my telescope scanning the sky but no more ufo was there so i went inside and did not sleep much that night, very puzzled and excited.
