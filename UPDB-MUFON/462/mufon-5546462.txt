12 orange colored balls of light

My friend and I were walking his dog around his apt. complex when I noticed a orange glowing object coming up behind some trees. We both moved closer and out from behind the trees to get a clear view. We then watched as orange balls of light started to appear above hwy 60 from the east and fly west. All of the balls of light just disappeared at one general location in the west. There were never more than 3 of them in the sky that could be seen at one time. The majority of the event, the orange balls of light made their course alone except for one instance in which 3 of them flew together in what looked like a V shape or possibly a triangle. The objects all headed steadily towards the same location in the west but at different speeds. The balls of light also were flying through the air as if gravity was not an issue. There were no clouds in the sky during the event, the sky was clear. The balls of light made absolutely no sound whatsoever, and also left no vapor/chem trail. The event only lasted 20 minutes, we counted 12 balls of light in total. My friend was able to snap 4 pictures of the objects on his camera, however because of there being no light source other than the objects themselves, there is no telling how well the pictures will look. It is now one of the most powerful experiences of our lives and we are grateful to have experienced it.
