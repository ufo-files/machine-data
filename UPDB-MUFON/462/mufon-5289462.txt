Bright, white aura. Seemed to glow. Not too bright to view with naked eye.

I was 10 at the time and me and my bff just got out of school for the day and headed to our fishing hole. On the way, out of nowhere, a bright white orb comes whizzing down towards us and stops about 80 yards shy of us up in the air. It hovers in position for about 10 seconds and suddenly it darted up and away into the clear blue sky. 
As if that weren't crazy enough the way the UFO disappeared was, "out of this world". As the UFO pulled away from us and retreated back into the sky, it paused for a second and that is when a black hole appeared in the sky. As if someone had found a little hole in a canvas and ripped it apart. The hole was barely bigger than the UFO itself. As soon as the hole appeared the UFO disappeared into it, and as it was entering the hole I witnessed the blue sky reach out and pull the UFO in. And just like that the black hole was replaced with blue sky. As if nothing happened. The whole sighting took less than 15 seconds, but I can recall it as if it just happened. And this occurred almost 40 years ago!
