I green star sized light, that defied physics.

It was the mid 90’s and a friend and I were lying on the hood of her car near Timberline Ranch, Maple Ridge, BC, Canada. There was very little light pollution and a huge view of the sky (the mountains were to the North and we were facing South). It was late at night but not past midnight. I saw what I thought was a star that was glowing green. So I kept looking at it wondering what gave it a different hue than the other stars. Very abruptly it shot across the entire sky, making sharp & instant angular turns. It zig zagged back and forth, in a crazy almost star pattern, then shot out of sight. This flight path covered almost the entire sky, going from one end to the other and back, so fast I could barely follow it with my eyes. The whole event lasted maybe 5 seconds. Afterwards I looked at my friend and asked “Did you just see that?” She replied “Yes”. We sat in silent wonder and just continued to watch the sky.
