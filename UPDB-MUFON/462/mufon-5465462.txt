{jpg} Pulsating Orange Orb No Sound

Was at my community softball game when my girlfriend tells me that a bright orange light came from over the treetops from the West. I assumed it was the helicopter due to it heading towards the hospital. When we did not hear any sound I also assumed it was a drone as the high school football game was going on and I was aware they have a drone for videos. I soon realized it was way too large and bright to be a drone and way too quiet to be a helicopter. It was extremely bright and pulsated as a whole off and on. It came from the West heading towards us (East) and made its way South before disappearing into the clear sky. I turned my head thinking it was gone and my girlfriend reported that it grew bright one last time before finally disappearing. As this is not my first time witnessing strange lights in the sky in our area I immediately told her to grab her IPhone (Better camera then my Samsung) when we first sighted it and she recorded the object. The video does not do it justice on the brightness but it does show the object pulsating as a whole.
