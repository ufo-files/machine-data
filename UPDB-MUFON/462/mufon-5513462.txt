{nd} Gold disk in the air, white orbs, cylindrical object above

At about 4PM on December 21, 2012 I noticed a bright beam to the west and started taking pictures with my IPad, I came in and saw several pictures of a gold disk moving around, then went back outside and took more pictures. I have about 20 pictures. This is our second sighting, the first was on November 3, 2012 which we also reported and we have many pictures. Someone from MUFON contacted us, but we have not heard from him again.
