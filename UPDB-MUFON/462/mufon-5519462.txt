bright light north to south in eastern sky extremely fast. at 1 point looked like it went behind a bright star.

About 9:00 pm stepped out back porch for a smoke as always looked up at the sky to find my stars that I follow throughout the night. Looked to the east and saw what looked like a star moving across the sky, very high; higher than Ive ever seen an airplane or jet. Kept my eye on it & it looked almost like it went behind one of the big bright stars & kept going. I actually said out loud "Holy cow, am I the only one seeing this right now?" I followed it until I could no longer see it. It seemed to be traveling in a straight line, even though my eyes moved off the light a couple of times as it was moving farther away, I was able to pick back up on it. Almost exactly 2 hours later @ about 11:10 I went back out & saw a falling star, a sort of curved trailing yellowish light that lasted about a second or two. Was this a comet passing followed by a meteor passing through the atmosphere? I don't know, but Ive never witnessed anything like this before. I look up at the stars all the time & have seen the sky from many different states & this is a first. Looking for answers on internet & found this web site from someone who had posted from Pennsylvania.
