{jpg} White green bright lights traveling south

I was sitting on my back porch just before sunset and saw 3 bright green lights grouped together. It caught my eye thinking it was a Roman candle but then saw that it traveled horizontally along the horizon at fast rate of speed headed from north to south. This was no flare as I1ve seen many in the military. The bright green light disappeared. No sounds or any other air traffic for several minutes before and after.
