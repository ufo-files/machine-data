{nd} Triangle shapped object with winged bottom point flashed green and red lights randomly on all 4 points

At 9:05 I was driving North on Highway 93 at around exit 34 at aprox 65 mph. as I came up on the cell tower on the south bound side up on the hill i noticed a triangular shaped object with hooked points flashing (they changed every 2 seconds) randomly green and red lights from all 4 points. It hovered west to east from the cell tower and then went back east to west. Very methodical and smooth. This almost looked drone like but was definitely bigger then a car but smaller then a plane. I lost it after i drove under it and could not catch it in my review or side mirrors. The highway was definitely busy so I hope other saw this as well
