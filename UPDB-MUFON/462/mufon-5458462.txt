{rjl} Multiple Lights over Catalina Mountains

We were all celebrating 420 on the 3rd floor apartment of the Pantano Apartments in Tucson, the deck facing the NE towards Mica Mountain. Over the mountain were 5 lights, mostly a reddish-orange color. They changed in brightness, but stayed at the same altitude, some of them over the mountain, and some further out over NE Tucson. 
At one point, one of the lights moved to be next to another one, and it looked WEIRD, as if it fell into place next to the other ship, moving at an astonishing rate of speed, and stopping on a dime. 
At another point, three of them came together, circled for about 20 seconds and then spread apart again.
I saw one ship at the top of the mountain highlighting the tops of the pine trees.

Before clouds overtook the mountain, two of the objects disappeared within about 2 seconds of each other in a super bright white flash that reminds me of the Star of David that is shown at Christmas every year. 

Clouds covered the mountain and we saw nothing for about 15 minutes.

Then my friends pointed out towards the Rincon mountains to our east and there were 3 lights that seemed to be a slightly higher altitude than before flying in a V formation E/SE.

Wildest thing I've ever seen.

My friend caught some of it on camera, but it was a crappy phone and shooting through a mesh-like material, so poor quality. Witnessed by at least 5 people on our balcony.
