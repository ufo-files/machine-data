{nd} Lots of orbs. One hovered right above me and my wife while walking our dogs.

On October 18th at exactly 6pm my wife went out to walk our 2 dogs. Within 5 minutes of her leaving she screamed my name to go to her. I freaked out, grabbed her gun and ran to her in the field where we walk our dogs. She pointed up and we seen lots of orbs shooting up into the air then floating towards us. We stood there in shock as one came right above us and just hovered. Another one floated down and slowly flew by a motel structure that was being constructed and it's light reflected off the wood walls. I stared right at the one above us, gun in hand and the middle was round and shaped like a white bubble. There were 3 bars outside the orb connecting to an outer ring that was like a tube and in it were small balls of light going round and round on the outer tube. After standing there for what seemed like about 15 minutes. Then the orb by the motel flew up and so did the one that was above us. They were joined by 2 other orbs that came from nowhere and the 4 of them flew off without sound towards Denver. As they flew they gained more speed then all 4 went and kinda fanned out flying up then disappeared. Me and my wife went inside and drew what we saw for comparison and it was the same. BUT, we did notice that it was 8pm when we went back into our apartment. No reaction from the dogs except they both sat down and watched the orbs. At first we were both shaken up but we didn't feel afraid. Just never had that happen before.
