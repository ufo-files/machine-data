{rjl} Apparition appeared out of a light

At the Brazos River, on Lake Whitney, sky was black, had an unusual orange cloud, Husband saw and it looked like long socks. Took three pictures. Enlarged image looked like a child's head. The light was about 1000 feet high and maybe 500 feet away. Sky was clear and very dark. Golf ball size at arms length. No noise...hovered. Constant colors. Could not be a lantern, much larger.

Other sightings were not the same. Looked like a moon...but strange.
