{cak} three of us were alerted by our dogs .we went outside and wached several lights making strange movements for several min.

I was with family about to turn in for the evening. We noticed our dogs outside were barking like they really were scared .We all went out front of our house to see what was going on. In the distance, with no sound at all,we saw four then three lights moving slowly up then down. They were above and below each other. Then four combined to three and moved up about two or three hundred feet then back down to about one hundred feet above the ground. One of the lights moved to the right a few feet and became a bit brighter .The dogs then got very Quite, the lights started moving up slowly then in a fraction of a second they picked up speed and were been gone. At that point we all realized we had been outside several min. and were cold and had been standing there with our mouths open but not saying anything. we did not talk about this after that day .Never saw it again or anything like it. My mother and older brother are gone now so I think I should say something to someone befor I am gone as well. I remember that night from time to time very clearly. That area we lived in was very isolated at that time so I guess we just figured we lived in the Boonys.
