{kt}Gilroy UFO June 2013 morphing, hovering light.

I was sitting in my Moms room when I noticed this odd light hovering outside her window. At first I thought it was a planet, but it quickly moved and started to morph into different shapes. I then thought it must be a reflection from something in the room, but it went behind the trees. I ran and got my camera and sat there and filmed it. I have never seen anything like it. The window I was looking out of was only about 18" tall and the light seemed to stay within that area as if it wanted me to see it. I saw 3 orbs in the same direction one month prior to this on May 28 which I also reported. If you were to head toward the area where I saw them they would be directly over the lake area in Morgan Hill. I filmed this light for as long as the video is, until my camera died. The light stayed for a short while after and then just faded out. It came back for two more nights and I didn't have my camera so I just observed it. I really wanted to go outside and get a better look but I was afraid it would disappear before I got outside, so I stayed where I was. At one point it makes 3 quick moves like its here, then there, then there. I didn't see it move. At the distance it seemed to be, it would have moved a few miles in a blink of an eye. It seemed to me that it was about the distance of Venus except Venus should have been South West of me and this was North West. I have lived in the house for over 30 years and have never seen anything like this. I did see some lights around 1993 or so but that's another report.
