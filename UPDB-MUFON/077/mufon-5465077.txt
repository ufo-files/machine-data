Past thru line of sight in about 2 mins, no sound, no change in brightness, slight flickering maybe

I was in the back yard. Damn it was bright. Not a plane, too large, too fast, too high and no sound for the entire pass. No engine noise and no sonic boom. It passed over in a straight line from first sighting at elevation about 25 deg to loss of sight over the horizon in about 2 minutes. Apparent size didn't change to horizon so probably not a drone. At that height and altitude the horizon is well over 200 k from here. The were 2 commercial jets that must have seen it. I checked Flight radar 24 immeadiately - QFA497 at 30k ft and JST611 at 35k ft.
