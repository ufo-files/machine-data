Green sphere landing

I saw light green sphere landing in the near forest. It was raining hard that evening. It could not been rocket or any other manmade thing because of its flying route.

1. I was laying on the sofa and watching TV (facing window). 
2. I saw very light green sphere object flying in the sky.
3. I thought immedially that thing must be UFO. Green lights were flashing and its flying route was boomerang-like.
4. Boomerang-like flying route, very light green flashing lights, sphere shape. Object was flying in the near forest.
5. I jumped up from the sofa and went close to the window see. I was horified. It was very quickly over but could not sleep next night at all.
6. I lost sight of the object when the UFO landed into the forest (approximately 500 meters from our building).
