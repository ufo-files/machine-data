{nd} Flashing UFO quickly descended behind the clouds

I had just finished eating dinner and I stepped out on the back porch. I saw three lights in the sky which I assumed were airplanes. Suddenly, the middle light quickly descended and then vanished behind the cloud line. It was flashing just like the other two airplanes. Afterwards, I quickly looked at the other two lights and they seemed to be cruising just like any other airplane.
