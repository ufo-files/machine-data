Cigar puffing smoke

I just walked into the break room sat down and looked out the window. The whole wall is practically windows. From behind a cloud came a long,dark, brownish, black cigar looking object. I saw like a white mist come out of the front and at first I thought something was happening to a plane since there’s an airport close to work. But this object was too high to be coming in to land. As I watched it come closer it emitted something that looked like smoke in the shape of a cloud out of the back end of it. I called for the others that were in the lunch room to look out the windows. There were three others at the time. But they look the wrong way at first and until they looked towards the west it was already behind the trees out in the parking lot.
