A bright yellow light with glowing red bell shaped halo following it

At about 11:45 pm Saturday June 16 observed a bright star-like object in sky, similar in brightness and size to approaching aircraft landing light, only no blinking position lights, approaching from the east-northeast but then turn and continue at an apparent upward trajectory to the south-southwest with a glowing red bell-shaped halo or aura behind it larger than the star-like object, a very distinctive looking object. Thought it may be a small asteroid skipping off the atmosphere except for this directional change. It also seemed to accelerate as it headed south-southwest then flicker out as it was gaining altitude, the trailing red aura went out first then the yellow light dissolved into a thin line of intermittent light then vanished altogether. Before I could get my phone out it was over. It was certainly not an airplane hence this UFO report. My first thought was a missile test or something like it, only nothing was in the news anywhere I could find talking about any rocket launches visible from the midwest at this time.
