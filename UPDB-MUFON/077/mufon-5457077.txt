Clear Photos/Video of two separate black objects above Ivanhoe Park

Was on vacation in Manly and on our first night (Nov 3rd @ 7:15pm) myself and the group I was with witnessed two separate crafts. In the videos if you slow them down you can see lights blinking as well as a few other colors that show up. The first craft looked to be a Triangle shaped black craft and the second was very similar in color however it was a clear disc shape. At times in the video these almost seem to be helium filled, like there's a hole in the middle but if you check out the screen shots and/or zoom in on the video, you'll see that there isn't an actual hole in the middle and that the middle is raised. We didn't see how these crafts arrived, we just looked up saw the first one almost dangling there, after it headed south and after a minute or so, another saucer shaped black craft appeared.
