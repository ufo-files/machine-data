Bright light that moved was there and then wasn't

Myself and my Granddaughter walked outside to get the fire pit ready to have a nice cozy fire with the family outside as it was a clear, cool night with no wind. We both looked up at what I first thought was a bright star i.e Planet. I have a telescope and can tell when Jupiter or Saturn are in the sky. I mentioned to Chloe something along the line of, look that is a bright star I wonder what planet it is. As we both looked at it we noticed although bright white like a star or planet it wasn't stationary but was moving in a NNW direction. At first I thought it was just a plane and said it must be a plane but then noticed that there were no lights blinking on it. We both watched it for about 10 to 15 seconds as it moved and then it started too, the only why I can figure to describe it was that it simply vanished but slowly like it was for lack of a better word cloaking. It simply just kept moving and then wasn't there. I stated to my Granddaughter that was weird where did it go, She said it just disappeared. The odd thing about it was that it had no blinking lights like a plane and was bright like Jupiter or Saturn. This past weekend the Movie Star Trek the voyage home was on and as I watched it when the Klingon ship cloaked it hit me that what I saw did something similar when it slowly disappeared
