{dr}Without binoculars looks like a spherical orange object about silver dollar sized. With binoculars you could see all of the lights which were red and green

I went over to my families house. It was about 930pm. As soon as I got out of my car I saw something strange in the sky. It looked like a giant star in the sky. My mom came outside and witnessed it as well. We got binoculars to look closely to see what it was. Once we saw it through binoculars we noticed it was spherical in shape hovering in the sky. It had a couple red lights at the top and around the center of the ufo were green and red lights pulsating. It was hovering low at first. Then after a while you could see that it would turn to its side. And get further away. After about an hour of it hovering it started to move faster out of the atmosphere and we could not make it out.
