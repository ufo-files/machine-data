{dr}3 lights moving across sky together that were all red-orange, then disappeared.

It was about 10:00 PM on July 20, 2012. I was laying on my bed, talking on my cell phone to a coworker. I looked out the window to my left and saw an orangish-red light flying soundlessly through the sky. I kept watching it to see what it was as we often see helicopters and small airplanes, but I have no idea what this was. 

As I laid watching it another orange-red light appeared below and behind the first. I told my friend that I was seeing something weird in the sky. Then I saw a third light behind the second and at what appeared to be the same altitude. The 3 lights were flying behind some tall trees so I got up off the bed and walked over to my window seat and looked out a side window and they were flying free of the trees. As they came into clear view the first just kind of disappeared, then the second, until finally all 3 were gone.

My first thought was that these were obviously unidentified flying objects, no doubt. No sound, no normal aircraft lights, just 3 orangish-red glowing balls of light flying through the air.
