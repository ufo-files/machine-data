Clear skies, saw and heard airplanes. Looking Up at sky, in the same spot without moving I saw the area flash 7 times, each flash dimmer than the last, flash 7 was almost fully gone. Saw the same even happen again the following day

I was at Algonquin Park on the beach of Lake of Two Rivers with my fianc1, and when the night is clear I always look at the stars. Being so far away from any major cities there was minimal light pollution. As we were looking at the sky a bright singular flash caught my eye, I stared at that same spot and saw the flash a second time, I asked my fianc1, Clyde, if he saw it and he did as well. We never took our eyes off the spot and it flashed 5 more times until we no longer saw it after 20 minutes. A few days later we saw the same thing except the flash only happened 3 times.

We felt excited because we believe we may have seen our very first UFO sighting. I love space and can tell the difference between a plane, satellite, or even a shooting star. But this, this random blinking that I thought at first was just my eyes tricking me and it was just a star but it was in a blank space in the sky. 

Either way, whatever it was it was an amazing experience.
