I saw an orange orb flying lower than planes normally do on my way home from a friend's house. I followed it down highway 218 for close to 15 miles until I was almost out of gas. I stopped on a dirt road, and the object also stopped and hovered in the sky in a single spot. I waved, thought about why it stopped when I did, wondered if it could sense/detect that I had been following it, and then I turned around to go get some gas. It followed me to the gas station hovering between a 30-45 degree angle above the road, and then it followed me about 10 miles back to my house. Once I turned off my lights and got out of the car, I looked up and saw that the main orange light was off. I saw a tiny white light flash very faintly as the object passed silently overhead. Then I went in the house as the encounter was over, at least what I could observe of it.
