{nd} I saw a silver flashing light and object in the sky

Me and my aunt were leaving a Big Lots Parking lot, which required us to drive up an incline. When I reached the top of the hill I could see what appeared to be a bright light flashing in the sky. I thought maybe it was a plane or ballon, but it just stayed there and flashed. Also there was a North West wind blowing toward us and the object stayed where it was. I asked my aunt if she could see the object in the sky? She said that she did, and I pulled out to go home. We decided to see if the object was still there so we drove about a quarter of a mile down and parked in a church parking lot to try to observe the object. It was still there. We sat there for about 5 minutes watching it. During that time it would flash a bright reflective silver type of flash. Maybe a starlight type of silver blue. It just kind of hovered there, several thousand feet up I would say. After about 5 minutes it seemed to kind of move around some maybe back and forth or up and down. It just kind of moved around. Then It started to go off toward what I would say was a north east direction. So we thought that was an interesting sighting. My aunt wanted to call 911 but I thought she was nuts. What are you gonna say, "Im calling in a flashing light in the sky. Could you go chase it down and arest it? LOL. After that we just left. I started to think about it some during the week and wondered if anyone else Had seen something that day. I found a sight that reported sightings the next day in another part of kentucky.
