blue beam of light

I was waking home from my grandmothers place at or around 11:30 pm, it was cloudy where I can't even see the moon, and my house was only seven houses up the street, I was walking while looking down on the road I'm walking on, then all of a sudden, I saw my shadow in an enclosed circle of blue light, I looked up, and saw this light as big as a basket ball then slowly going upwards until it disappeared, this seem to last only a few seconds, but in fact it was actually two and a half hours, because by the time I reached home, everyone was sleeping, I was surprised to see everyone asleep already, I looked at the clock and was surprised to see the time is 2 am,....I have no recollection of the 2 1/2 hours that passed.
