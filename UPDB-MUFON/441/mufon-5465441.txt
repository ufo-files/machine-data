Three circular lights moving together and changing color. (red, green)

My landlady and I were on her sundeck having a smoke. There was overcast, and I noticed three lights that were moving together as if they were part of a single craft. They were a fair distance away, as at times they were visible behind some mist that I estimated to be a few miles away. As the lights moved, they changed color, fading from red to green and back again. My landlady's back was to them, and I pointed them out to her. She saw them too. I couldn't see the body of the craft, just the lights. If it was a single craft it must have been really large. If it was multiple crafts they were very synchronized in motion and color change. The lights were moving way too fast, and making turns that would be impossible for any craft that I am familiar with. The total sighting only took about 30 seconds, and the lights disappeared into the clouds.
