{rjl} (15) lights traveling in straight line path disappearing in same spot

Brother in-law and sister called me on the way home from fennville and said to run outside and look to the south. When I looked to the south there were 6 lights in a perfect row spaced at least a 1/2 a mile to a mile apart from each other and moving at a good speed. When they got just above allendale they would disappear one at a time in The same spot just as the other ones did and they kept appearing to the south in a line. I counted at least 12 to 15. As that was happening a light appeared just to the west of the line of lights. Under the light a second dimmer light appeared and they both hovered. After all the lights had disappeared in that one spot the two lights to the west dissapeared about 5 mins later.
My sister and brother in law had seen about 15 to 20 lights in total and a possible portal they were going into. There were to many city lights for me to see the possible portal.
