{cak} Was hovering 10' above road and took off across field

I was driving just before sunrise on way to work in rural area. I turned corner at rural intersection and it was hovering about 10-12' above highway. I turned on bright lights and it moved off highway over cornfield and disappeared. The best description I can give is a diamond shape with longest point on the horizontal plane. The movement wasn't exactly linear. It almost appeared to "swim" as it moved. It had a burnt orange glow and the surface almost appeared to be in a liquid state. I drive this road 6 days a week. There is nothing along this stretch except fields. No lights, no signs, nothing to reflect light, no other traffic. Also no lightning, or wind. I have decent eyesight, no medical conditions. I was maybe 150-175' from it when I saw it. It still makes my hair stand on end to talk about it.
