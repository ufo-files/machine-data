Woke from sleep and saw perfectly round object that was polished and reflected trees and moon.

I was about 13 years old and normally I slept with window open.I am guessing at the time and date when I had this happen. I woke up from sleep suddenly due to no noise as normally heard during the night. I think it was the fact I didn't hear the crickets anymore that woke me. I also notice no tree/leave sound in the breeze, no wind/ breeze. I pulled back the corner of the blind on my window and looked out over the pool in the backyard (southwest out my window to the backyard) and saw the object above the tree--large eucalyptus up the backyard bank up about 50 feet up ther hill. The object , a large polished ball, was just about 40-50 feet over and just southwest of the tree. I think it was approximately 100-120 feet in diameter and made no sound. I looked away and looked back again and it had not moved, I also noticed the reflection of the tree, the fence and a moon light. I wasn't uneasy, but I felt a little unsure as to if I was dreaming, or just seeing things. My father came to my room and looked out the window and said that he thought it was wierd that there wasn't any noise and told me to go back to sleep.He said he didn't see anything. I layed back down in my bed and in about 20 secs after I layed back down, I sat back up and looked out again, the thing that made me sit up and look again was the breeze was again blowing and the crickets were chirping. I never saw this object again and still wonder to this day why I saw this.
