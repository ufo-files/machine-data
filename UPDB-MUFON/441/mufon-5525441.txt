silent and very fast orb of light

While taking my children trick-or-treating in Lapeer Michigan, essentially downtown Lapeer, we were going from house to house when overhead a large orb of light appeared. It was completely silent. It was hard to tell a size or distance of the object, but I would estimate at 20 feet in diameter and 100-200 feet in the air. The orb just hung in the air while we and about 30 other people looked at it. It began to move with out purpose going to the left and to the right, back and forth, all in very quick jerky movements. It then shot off to the horizon faster than a blink of the eye. We did not know what the object was, but it was 100% not any form of man made aircraft.
