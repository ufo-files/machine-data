{cak} Bright red/orange light in the sky that moved oddly

I decided to go outside around midnight to get some fresh air. I was just hanging out on my back porch and just so happened to glance up at the stars, and saw a new one I've never notice before that was a strange reddish orange color. At first I just thought it was a star, but when it started slowly moving to the right of me and flashing so I figured it must be an airplane because i'm fairly close to the Portland airport and have seen some at night before.
The movement started out slow but quickly gained speed, and suddenly jerked upwards in an angle I've never seen an airplane move before. I watched it for a little bit longer, trying to figure out what it could be, before deciding to try getting a picture of it. But as I pulled out my phone, it suddenly moved quite a distance to the left really fast, then darted upwards before disappearing. Afterwards I was just shocked and in disbelief as to what I saw.
I'm still not totally sure what exactly it was that I saw.
