{rjl} I saw at least 50 ufo’s flying in straight line

I walked out of my garage at 5:45 a.m. to take my brother to an appointment he needed to get to. Still dark outside and very clear morning. I looked up in the sky to look at the stars like I always do when its clear. First thing I saw was a line of lights traveling from south to north. Almost looked like a satellite would look like at night, I’ve seen a satellite before. BUT this was a line of them , one straight line that literally went on for almost 5 minutes. They just kept coming!!! NO SOUND!!! I stood there for about 1 minute and they were still coming. Ran to call my brother who is elderly and moves very slow to come out and see this. I really did not expect him to make it in time. Takes him about 2 minutes to get outside and these lights are STILL COMING. !! I say we saw at least 50 but realistically I think we saw more like 100 ufo’s , straight line,no craft passing another craft,no sound,all going about the same speed,some catching up but not passing the one in front of it. A procession that went on for close to 5 minutes!!! I tried to film it on my phone but nothing really came out. All I managed to get was me running and yelling for my brother but every time I tried to get it on film , i just could not get them in my viewfinder. It’s 8;54 now and my heart is still pounding. I do wish someone would get back to me to let me know if anyone else saw this. I’m 65 yrs old and I’ve NEVER seen anything remotely close to this event. WOW is all i could say
