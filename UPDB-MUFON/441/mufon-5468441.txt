{rjl} Bright lights (3) pulsed and descended until under tree line, did not see anything else.

I was walking to my truck after hunting coyotes and hogs on a family friends small farm in the country.The darkness was at the point where I could no longer hunt, so I took a small logging road back to my truck. After maybe 10 minutes of walking, I was was jolted as I saw three pulsating objects in my peripheral vision. I watched, croutching in the logging path, as the lights moved across the path about one hundred yards in front of me, pulsating a cool white light as they hovered over twenty-year pines. It took maybe half a minute for the objects to leave my direct line of sight as they continued East , but I could see their pulsating lights flashing dimly through the pines. Eventually, they moved into a hardwood bottom a few hundred yards away, dipping lower in the horizon, evading my view. Almost as soon as they left my view, I noticed a loud noise approaching from the West. When I looked to my left, a low-flying aircraft flying what I would guess to be 500 ft. darted East, seeming to follow the light's path. I believe plane was going way to fast to be civilian, but I did not get a clear enough view to be able to claim it was a military craft. The plane did not reappear as it disappered to the East. During the rest of the walk to my truck, I saw nothing else, and could only faintly hear what I believed to be the military aircraft.
