I was in the car at 6:30am driving my kids to school. The sky was clear (no clouds) any airplanes in the sky were leaving trails. This object had no trails. It was such a profound and unusual shape (U Shape/Teardrop Shape) to be hovering in the sky. As soon as I saw it - I stopped my car in the middle of the road to take pictures and video. I kept squinting my eyes to make sure I knew what I was seeing. It was surreal. The object appeared to be moving away, but very slowly, no quick movements. I am submitting this within an hour of witnessing this to make sure I get all of the details clear.
