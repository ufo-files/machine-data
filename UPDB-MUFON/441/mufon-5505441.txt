{cak} Multiple lights flying in formation then flying in different paths

I was driving north on i35 in ft worth. I took the i20 exit towards dallas. Almost immediately after merging onto i20 I noticed a group if four lights in what appeared to he a perfect line. One of the lights blinked out within 30 seconds. I thought that was strange so I kept my eyes on the lights. Roughly 15 seconds later the light in between the 2 on the end started to break formation. It instantly jumped out in my mind that it was a large triangle turning. They looked very similar to some of the videos from the Phoenix lights. About 2 minutes later when I got them back into view, it was obvious they were different objects because they were moving in different directions. They were no longer in formation. One light was going north west, one was going west and one south. I noticed the air traffic got real heavy at that time. Normally you could do a 360 looking up and see maybe 12 planes in the air, it seemed that in the direction of the objects there were at least 15 things over there. I noticed that the light going south looked like it was going to go over i20 in the distance so I sped up to try to get a look. Before I got close enough it starting going back north and all of the lights seemed to meet back up then go north. I lost sight shortly after that. The highway was very busy so others had to see. I make this drive at this time, 6 days a week and never saw anything like this.
