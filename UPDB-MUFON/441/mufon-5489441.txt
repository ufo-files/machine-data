I thought I saw a pink helicopter above the stage.

I vaguely remember this experience - it was a long time ago - I was in Grade 2 or 3. Some of us were practicing for a program, me and my friends were playing basketball in the courtyard. Then from nowhere I thought I saw a helicopter - oddly - pink - pass over the stage roof, with a marginal error of only a few inches. It had a helicopter noise.IT was so low, I thought I saw the pilot through a transparent window/door. I even saw the controls! My friend said, "Wow, a helicopter!" It seemed to be a little lower than a single-story house as it passed over the school garden. It disappeared as mysteriously as it left.
