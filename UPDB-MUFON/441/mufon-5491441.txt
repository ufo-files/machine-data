{cak} Kids saw it first. Adults next. Hovered over water the shot off to the north.

We were visiting relatives nearby. We both lived on the same lake. Our two kids were playing outside and started yelling at us to come outside and see what they were seeing at the lake. We ignored them at first and waited several minutes but they kept yelling at us so we went out on the deck and saw a round craft about 20 feet in diameter with colored lights around the disc hovering over the lake. One adult said they could see people inside of it. It was almost dark outside. The object hovered for a few minutes then shot off to the north in a blink of an eye. The whole event took 15 minutes at least. The craft looked like it had a dome on it. They apparently let us watch them becuase they didn't leave immediately when we first saw the craft. We never saw it again.
