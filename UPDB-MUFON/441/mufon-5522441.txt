reddish/orangish light, about 1ft in diameter hovering about 200 feet over Rockingham Mall

We were traveling on rt. 93 northbound and around 10pm we were passing exit 1 (Rockinham Mall). Ahead of us my wife and I spotted a reddish/orangish light. We both watched it as we got closer, and she said to me "do you see that?". I rolled down my window and stuck my head out. It was a single light, about 200 feet above the ground, hovering. I tried to see if a structure obscured stars as we passed, but I didn't spot that, suggesting that the object was no bigger than the light itself. My wife said it could have been a floating lantern.
