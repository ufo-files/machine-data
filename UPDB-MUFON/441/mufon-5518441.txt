Flame like light with geometric red aura that resembled a snowflake

Saw light behind cloud cover thought it might be a plane. I live within ten miles of a major airport. Then I realized that it wasnt on any flight path I am used to seeing. I was expecting to see the usual signs of an aircraft as it cleared the cloud cover. But once it cleared it stayed the same. It appeared as a flame like light with a geometric red aura that resembled a snowflake. Once it cleared the cloud cover it appeared to stop mid air, paused then made 90 degree change of direction headed what I think was north then accelerated at a rate of speed that I have never seen before( extremely fast ). The skies were clear of air craft for about two minutes and then I heard what sounded like fast moving jets. Shortly after I heard what sounded like jets ( about two minutes ) the normal air traffic I am used to seeing over my house continued. Like I said the flight paths I am used to seeing are very consistent and this was completely out of the usual.
