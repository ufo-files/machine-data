{cak} Green comet light (like firework) observed descending

At approx 6:30-6:45 I was looking East 120 degrees on our deck in Milan, NY using a telescope to see a star just below Jupiter. With my other eye, I noticed bright green light in the South 186 degrees and first saw what appeared to be a firework descending at a 45 degree angle and going beneath the tree cover. I heard a faint buzzing/humming sound which I hear occasionally when I am outside at night - normally I assume this is an animal sound, not something in the sky!

I did not feel scared when I saw the object, and I thought about it all night. As I began thinking more about it I became concerned that something landed with beings in it that may be on the property, looking around. I woke up unexpectedly at 1:23AM and thought I heard a loud sound around 2AM but it could have been the wood burning stove. I checked the site I first posted my sighting and noticed there were a total of 11 similar accounts. This morning there are a total of 16 similar accounts. 

I lost sight of the object because it fell out of view through the tree cover.
