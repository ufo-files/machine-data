{cak} Multiple witnesses from different counties reported high levels of activity north, n/w of Columbus

WSYX (local channel 6) received numerous calls about this incident last night around 9 to 10pm. My personal observation was n/w of my house. A solid white light moving left to right rapidly. Back and forth for about 3 minutes. Interesting that the light kept the same distance from left to right each time. It never traveled further to either side. The light then descended behind a tree line. Reappeared briefly above the treeline then vanished. Some people reported the light changing colors. Mine was a solid white light. Please investigate this as it had many, many people startled across several different locations.
