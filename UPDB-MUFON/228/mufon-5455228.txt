{rjl} 4 hovering crafts sped off maintaining distance from each other

About 10 years ago i witnessed 4 hovering lights over eglin afb. they were grouped together but were separate crafts. After they hovered for about 5 minutes they all shot off extremely quickly in the same direction but they all stayed grouped and continued to hover somehow maintaining their distance from each other.  These 4 round crafts hovered and then sped off 3 other separate times before disappearing.
