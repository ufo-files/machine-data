Unidentified Flying Object

Between 7 p.m. - 7:30 p.m. I was driving along route 8 and saw small lights above the treetops on the drivers side or left hand side. As I was driving closer along the route, it became clear the lights were not from air plane or helicopter because they were way too low in the sky. I noticed the lights were moving and were clearly white clear round lights in three corners sort of making a triangle shape. Every so many seconds a red square light would flash in the middle like. As I continued along the route, the shape started moving back and forth as if it was waving at me and somehow noticed that I had noticed it. I continued driving and when I got to an intersection the object went across the way and out of my sight off to the right over the treetops. The object was dark and only could see the lights.
