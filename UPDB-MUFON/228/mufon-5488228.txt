{cak} Light blinked on and off and then would appear again a short distance away

I tried to let the dog out at just about 11:00, maybe a minute or two before that. She didn't go out so I stood on the porch and looked at the sky. I saw a bright flash like a meteor for just a millisecond. No trail, just like a star flared and then went out. I then saw another dimmer light blink on just about ten seconds later due south of where of I saw the first flash but still fairly close to the first location. It resembled a satellite light except it disappeared and blinked off. I looked a little south of where it blinked on and then off( blinked on once each time) and again I saw a light appear and then blink off and disappear. It seemed to go on in about ten second intervals. I saw it again blink on and then off. Just one light blinking on and then off each time, then an interval where there was nothing and I had to guess where it might be judging by its direction and the fairly slow speed it seemed to be. It had the light emission and speed of a satellite as you track it across the sky, it just blinked on and then off about every ten seconds or a little longer. That made it hard to follow. It was almost clear with clouds only in the southern sky and not overhead.
