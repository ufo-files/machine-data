Sky was bright blue and sun incredibly white.Too bright to look anywhere near it . we were walking near to our house and noticed more and more trails being left across the sky in a pattern unlike the usual flight paths. We stopped to chat to a friend and continued looking at the sky.The pattern became a full cross hatch over the whole area. There were 2 small jets swerving across the sky independent of one another and appearing to survey the area. Suddenly we noticed a glinting reflective motionless orb which was next to a cloud. It looked at times like a bright light and then a glinting reflective orb. It hung in the air with the clouds going infront and behind it, then it slowly moved upwards and slightly further away.
