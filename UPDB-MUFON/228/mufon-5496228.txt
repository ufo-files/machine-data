{nd} 3 UFO's flying over Topanga Canyon Ave

I was hiking at Topanga State Park heading my way to back toward my car. Took a moment to gaze at the bright full moon when suddenly something compelled me to turn my head and that's when I noticed of in the distance 3 glowing objects in a triangular formation. They were stationary for about couple minutes but eventually started floating west of the San Fernando Valley. I felt astonished that I was witnessing this event and dismayed when I tried recording the objects with my iPhone but the battery was dead. I should mention it had 40% battery last I checked. It was soon after that these UFO's disappeared from the sky.
