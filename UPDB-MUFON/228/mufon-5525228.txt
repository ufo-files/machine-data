Unknown lights moving through night sky.

On July 23rd a little past 10:00 pm I went outside to view the neighborhood fireworks.The weekend of the 24th is a state holiday in Utah and fireworks are as popular on this day as they are on the 4th of July.While I was outside in my backyard I noticed 4 faint objects that looked like stars very high in the sky.Three of the objects were in a straight line but at a slight angle moving across the sky.A fourth object was moving along just off to the side of the other three objects.They moved through the sky in formation from north to south.Although I could not see if it were one unit or four separate objects.They seemed to be moving at a fast motion through the sky as they began the get dimmer and dimmer until the vanished.My house is in the flight path of the Salt Lake City airport.Commercial aircraft are a normal occurrence flying over my house.This was NOT a plane or helicopter.
