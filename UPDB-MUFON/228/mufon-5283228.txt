What appeared to be 2 red circles of rectangular lights with a matching inner circle of lights as well.

On New Year’s Eve, I had lit a large burn pile on my property. It was drizzling rain enough to ensure the fire wouldn’t spread into the surrounding pasture. Almost directly to the East, I saw a red light. Thinking it was from a tower in the distance, I didn’t pay much attention to it. Realizing it was far to bright, I turn to face them. What I saw were 2 red circles of lights with matching inner circles hovering approximately 40’ in the air less than 30’ away from my position. In the amount of to it took for me to reach into my back pocket for a flashlight, they had disappeared. There were no sounds and when I swept the area with light, there was nothing to be found.
