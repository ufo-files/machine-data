[flg] Hovering reflective object ejecting (dropping) smaller reflective object.

Went outside to smoke a cigarette, back yard. Noticed a flash of light in the sky, started watching it, it hovered in same spot for about 4 minutes, then it ejected another smaller reflective object which seemed to ascend evenly with original object. Smaller object moved westward then disappeared. Original object ascended, then moved east. Lost sight of object at about 6 minutes of observation. Video camera on phone would not work, so I attempted to view it through binoculars. Viewing it through binoculars had a Yellow "prism" type effect and I could not make out any distinct shape. During observation a Southwest Airplane flew by below object and another unknown jet was flying in the south east area.
