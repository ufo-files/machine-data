{rjl} Threee extremely bright lights, traveling in a diagonal formation.

I walked outside and noticed three bright lights in the sky. They were arranged in a formation like this ( \ ). I watched as they traveled horizontally and then down vertically. They were traveling at medium speed. They disappeared below the horizon. It was raining and very cloudy. The objects were about a mile up and east of me. They traveled in a SE direction. The objects made no sound. They were bright, but did not pulsate or flicker. Each object seemed, from their distance away, seemed to be about as big as a hot air balloon. I only saw light, no physical characteristics.
