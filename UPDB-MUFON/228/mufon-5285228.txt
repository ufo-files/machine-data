Object hovering then ascends

I was driving into work and at around 8:15 am, I observed a stationary metallic object high in the sky. There were no clouds on this cool, clear morning. At first, I thought it was a plane and waited for it to fly in another direction, but it didn’t move. I stared at it for 3-5 seconds and then it disappeared upwards as if it were going behind a cloud, but there were no clouds to be seen.
