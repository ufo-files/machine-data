I walked outside & saw a cigar shape moving across sky

I walked out my front door and looked up above the treeline at the sky. I noticed a cigar shape moving and thought it was part of a cloud but there were no clouds, so I watched and was shocked at the movement and shape. I called for my mom to come outside and she made it just in time to see it moving before we lost sight of it over the mountain to our east. It was 9:25 PM when I went outside & we have very little air traffic here in the Pacific NW and we heard nothing. It also seemed to glow from the bottom.
