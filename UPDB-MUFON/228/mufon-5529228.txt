3 X SPHERES IN UPPER ATMOSPHERE: CENTER SPHERE RED IN COLOR; TWO OUTSIDE SPHERES WHITE IN COLOR.

11/01/2010 Approximately 2:00 p.m. My friend and I were outside talking on separate phone calls when my friend pointed out a "daytime star". Being 2:00 approximately I found it odd. We had seen a similar thing a few months ago and we got a very rudimentary telescope which, when focused on the stationary object in the sky, turned out to be 3 spheres. This time we brought out the big telescope. When we finally found the object and this time it was actually moving at a quick clip. Although moving we were able to track it and because of the better equipment were able to see that it was a red sphere with two white spheres going around it. Shortly thereafter it was out of our sight.
