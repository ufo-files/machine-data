As I walked from my normal night shift at the hotel, I glanced at the ocean as I always have. I have seen many things in the waves, including them glowing from planktons, etc, but what I saw this night made me stop, stare, and then go for a closer look. The waves seemed electrical, but in a way that was glowing for at least half a mile. The light was of whites and greens and reds, all water slushed from the looks. There was a moon out and I looked right at it and followed the reflection, which was not at the beach line I was watching. Also would not make sense for the moon to be red and green. Was a clear night and I continue to look for anything that could be around to cause maybe a reflection or something, but nothing I could see. I continued to watch it as it slowly drifted away and seemed to sink, or go, deeper into the water until it was gone. Was there just slowly, drifting, or looking or sumthin as it just seemed to dive deeper. Still can not be sure what I witnessed but was not normal to my eyes.
