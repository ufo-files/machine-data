On 8 June 2015 at approximately 940 pm my daughter and I witnessed what appeared to be 8 brightly lit orange lights in the shape of a long rectangle. There were 4 lights and then a separation with 4 more lights. The lights were approximately 2000 ft in the sky and stationary. They remained stationary for at least ten minutes then 4 of the lights went out and disappeared. The night sky was crystal clear. The lights were extremely bright and appeared to be the size of navel orange if you were holding it in front of your face. After about 1 minute the lights appeared again but this time there were only three and the object appeared to have moved lower to the ground. These lights were fixed and bright in the sky. They were not commercial or private aircraft. The Orlando airport is located to the southeast these objects were due south and remained stationary for more than 15 minutes. We will attempt to upload video which we have of the objects taken with an iphone 6. The object or object was located over lake toho and to the south of Poinciana Florida. I have never seen anything like this before.
