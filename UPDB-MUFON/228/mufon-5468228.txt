{td} Oblong object observed in straight path to ground

My boyfriend and I were driving along a back road when we both saw a very bright light between two clouds. After both acknowledging it we pulled over and observed it's rather rapid descent to the ground. The brightness was like the north star and with the help of binoculars we judged the overall shape to be oblong. After watching it fall for a few minutes it winked in and out of sight, even through binoculars then would reappear after several moments. It then went from moving straight down to off at an angle then sort shimmied lile a snake before continuing a straight downward trajectory. After approximately 15 minutes it finally disappeared behind the mountains to the west of our spot. Before we saw it there was heavy air activity overhead, several large helicopters heading west towards the mountains. During our observation of the object I had goosebumps as well as a bit of rush at seing something completely unexplainable to me.
