Cylindrical, grey object

The object was cylindrical in shape, the color was grey on top and black underneath the object, it admitted no sound and did not leave anything trailing behind it as it flew. It manoeuvred very abruptly, was there one moment and gone the next. I remember before and after there were no aircraft other the the helicopters in the area and or sky, not even on Mccarran International airport flight path as if this object had violated airspace and the controllers vacated all aircraft in the vicinity for safety reasons.
