It's around 1700hrs when my daughter called me and told me she saw two unidentified flying objects above the trees. She said she got afraid upon seeing these objects. She is familiar with stories about abduction and that's the reason why she got afraid. She hurriedly told me, at first I thought it's gone, then she hurriedly ask me to go upstairs and found out that the objects were still there. It was black with a shape I cannot identify from typical geometric figures. It was hovering and flying above each other. The smallest object hovered to other direction and was suddenly gone. The biggest object stayed to west direction until it suddenly disappear while I continue filming it. The problem is my camera won't stay focus. I got frustrated. I took two videos, one from my phone and the other one is from a digicam. You will hear the voices of my daughters and myself as I filmed it.
