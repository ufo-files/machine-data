{cak} I looked skyward while sitting in backyard at night, and saw a triangular craft with lights drop down to my yard at tree level.

While sitting in my backyard at 11:30 p.m. on approximately April 15, 2012, I noticed a triangular object in the nightsky. It circled around overhead and I saw lights on the bottom, including a large red light. It continued to come lower and my first thought that it had been a military craft changed to realizing that I was seeing a u.f.o. I wondered if I could go inside my house and find my camera, but then felt confused about where it was. The craft continued to flash the lights and dropped to tree level and fence level in my backyard. It stopped. It looked bronze colored metal and solid. My cat was sitting next to me and remained calm. I wondered if it was interested in my cat or me. Then panic overcame me and I fainted. When I came to, I was still sitting in my lawn chair and my cat was beside me and the craft was gone.
