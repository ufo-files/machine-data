{nd} I saw a strange long object flying across the sky, heading north, making a very odd sound.

Tonight I was walking my dog, and just before I walked inside my house I saw this strange elongated object flying north. I thought it was a plane, but then when I took my headphones out I heard this weird...pulsating swishy noise, like if someone was whispering and trying to imitate a police siren. I used to live by O'Hare, and I currently live close by a Naval base where we often hear and see jet fighters and commercial jets, and I can guarantee I've never ever heard a plane make that noise. It looked like a giant flashlight, and had no wings or airplane lights from what I saw. A beam of light was shooting out of the front of it, and a few times a light flashed from the top of it. I couldn't make out the shape exactly clearly, it was a very fuzzy looking object.
