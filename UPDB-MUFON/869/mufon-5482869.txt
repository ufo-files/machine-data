{cak} Object appears 2 times in a series of photos taken of a contrail prism

I was on my front porch when I noticed a prism forming at a particularly dirty plane contrail. I thought the prism effect was rare as I have never observed anything similar so I took out my phone and started taking photos of it. The weather was extremely calm with almost no wind so I was able to observe the effect as it slowly moved across the sky. I took a series of photos on the front porch at both standard and 4x zoom. When the effect went behind the tree line and out of my sight from the front porch, I proceeded to my 3rd floor bedroom window and snapped two more pictures of the now dissipating effect. When I later observed the photos I saw something that appeared in only one of the photos. After searching the other photos I saw what seemed to be the same object at a further distance on a later photo. Three minutes past between the first and second photo containing the object in question. The airspace is a busy traffic lane for Northeast Philadelphia and Philly International Airports.
