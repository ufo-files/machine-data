{cak} Strange Blue light

I was standing in my garage when I notice a blue light shining through some pecan trees in front of my house. It caught my attention because there's nothing over their that could be making a glow of light. At first I thought someone was playing a trick with a laser light but there's nothing behind those pecan trees and no houses for miles. As I stood there and watch this blue light for about five to seven mins, I still wasn't convince that someone was doing this until I started to walk toward this light from my garage and the blue light moved further back but as I backed up it came back to its original position. I then stood there for a few moments really wondering what is this so I move two steps to the right and the blue light did the exact same thing I then move to the left two steps again it moved. when I move back to where I first was, so did the light but then after that it move up to the top of the trees slowly and moved backed down level with me. So at this point I'm almost convince that this is not someone playing so I did a series of movement from forward to back and side to side and the light follow as I was making it move by this time my mind is racing and heart is beating a little faster. So I took my phone and started to video it as close as I could zoom in to the light and for the next 30 mins the light did movement from up and down to side to side and finally just disappeared I really didn't know what to make of it then and till this day I still don't but, I do have it all on video.
