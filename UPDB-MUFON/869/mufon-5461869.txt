{cak} Perpendicular to ground rectangle with light spinning

Driving north on Michigan road approaching traffic light to turn to 146th east.
Its about 9:50 pm. I notice an object cross my line of sight. At first I thought it was a large bird so I slowed not to hit it. but as it continues I notice its rectangular spinning like a propeller.
Then it pass to the left of me and continues spinning just above the hill which angles away from the road. Its here that I notice the outline of the object is rounded rectangle of purple, blue, green, white lights. The middle of the rectangle has two or three stripes of lights that divide it but dont touch the edges. It seemed like laser lights on a disco floor. as it spun. But to the right was an open farm field no lights. In front of me cars off in the distance. The hill where I last saw it was dark. When it flew across the line of sight it appear pale off white. But when it was to my left all I saw was lights. I had to head home it was late.
