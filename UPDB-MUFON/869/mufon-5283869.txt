Low flying Red Lights Flashed Rapidly In Sequence With Loud Low Jet Sound Very Small White Object Went Toward it

In the SW sky 45 degrees above the horizon there was a bright white light and I jokingly flashed my flash light at it several times while looking for something outside. I went to turn to go and I heard a loud low jet noise above my head. I looked up and at approx 500 feet up, a string of approx 10 red lights flashed on, one by one from north to the south. I could not make out a shape of a craft. The lights were spread out across 1/2 of the night sky. The northern most light was directly above my head and the rest were to the south. The one above my head was the size of an aspirin held at arms length and the others were a bit smaller and the center ones could not always be seen and the southern most were faded and the size of a pin head after 5 seconds . They flashed in a north to south direction, following Interstate 70 across town to the mountains. There were approximately 10 sequences of red lights flashing one by one until I could no longer see them or hear the loud jet like noise, It took them approx 10 to 15 seconds for them to go 10 miles. Just as the lights reached the Mountain tops and disappeared a small white light/object that was stationary in a small group of stars started moving from the group of stars moved toward the red lights just as they reached the mountains. The white object was very small and faint and moved from North to South descending at a 45 degree angle. It disappeared after one second.
