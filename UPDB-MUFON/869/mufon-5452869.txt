At 10:10pm I witnessed a triangular shaped craft travel at very high speed from the SE to the NW across the sky above my home/city block. The craft made no noise what so ever. It was very visible to the naked eye in the sky as it traversed above but had no lights rather it had what can be compared to a cloaking device or active camo that cause noticable displacement in the sky as it went by. I could almost feel it as it went by as well, almost like when a magnet pulls itself to a surface I felt drawn to it. Due to the low altitude it seemed to be at I predict the size of the craft to be similar to a B-52 in its "wing span" but with the shape being triangular actual wings may not be a thing let alone a side. I expected to hear anything while or after it passed overhead, considering the speed it moved a sonic boom was not unlikely to expect and yet i heard nothing. Not even the wind passing or a whoosh, hum, nothing at all.
