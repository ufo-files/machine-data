{tjd} bright light unmoving, took photo, and so saw item

We were at the Laguna Race track in Monterey, California. My wife pointed out the very bright pinpoint of light in the sky, unmoving. I was taking photos of race cars - 300mm lense. I aimed at the dot of light and took the attached photo. Then light disappeared. We took a print with us to Sutherland, the giant telescope center in the Cape in South Africa. They said they'd forward my orignal to the 'heavies' in Cape Town. The scientists/astronomers at the center in Cape Town said they circulated the picture, and no-one had a clue as to what it might be.
