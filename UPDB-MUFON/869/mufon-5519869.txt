More lights over Sherwood Park

I keep my night vision camcorder and night vision binoculars on the kitchen table just in case something shows up. It was about 4 AM and I was up for a glass of water when I looked out the south patio windows and saw a moving lights. I grabbed my night vision binoculars and it confirmed my eyes were not playing tricks on me. So I set the night vision camera to 16 Mega Pixles and zoomed to 4x and fired off about 6 shots. Two proved to be fuitfull. They are attached.
