fire ball very fast

In Dublin, I was walking back home. I was waiting the traffic light to cross the road (on feet). Looking at the sky I saw this fireball, I tried to check for an airplane but no shape of that around. Then I noticed it was to fast to be an airplane. I've seen doing the same trajectory 5 mins later going towards the city center of Dublin. I felt a bit scared as it was clear to me something strange was on going. Today I've seen an helicopter going around my place for a couple oh hours.
