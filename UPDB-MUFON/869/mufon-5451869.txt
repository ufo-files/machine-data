I captured some strange lights on a game camera at 3:00 am that I can't explain.

I live in Kansas City but earlier this year my wife and I bought an old farm house with 5 acres out near Cameron, MO. We've been going out there and working on the property almost every weekend. Shortly after we started working out there we had some things stolen from the property. In hopes of catching the thieves, we purchased and put out several game cameras around the property. We have them set to record 45 seconds of video each time they are triggered. We get a lot of videos of deer and turkeys, and even got what I believe is a mountain lion recently, but one video has me stumped. Something triggered the camera at 3:03 am and in the frame is a row of 7 lights. The lights look like they are near the ground in the beginning of the video, they hover for a few seconds then start to slowly ascend to around the top of the frame. They kind of bounce a little on their way up they also seem to turn to a slight angle. Their is also some kind of background noise but it's possible that it's coming from the highway a few miles away. This property is on a dead-end road and the nearest neighbor is about 1/4 mile away. The camera also has many trees around it and branches above it, so I can't really see how it could be some kind of reflection.  I've included a video of the lights as well as 2 others from that same camera/position for reference. I should also mention that the date on the camera is off by 1 month. It reads July instead of June.
