{rjl} OCCURRED DURING APRIL 14-15 2014 LUNAR ECLIPSE. TOOK A PICTURE OF RED MOON IN 3 FRAMES ON CELLPHONE, IT APPEARED ONLY ON THE MIDDLE FRAME PHOTO.

IT WAS THE NIGHT OF THE LUNAR ECLIPSE IN APRIL 14-15, 2014. I HAD JUST FINISHED PLAYING A SERIES OF COMPUTER GAMES WITH MY NEIGHBORS IN LOS ANGELES, CA NEAR THE LAX AIRPORT ON SOUTH LA CIENEGA BLVD. G-PA AND DANIEL BOTH STEPPED OUTSIDE OF THE COMPUTER ROOM AND I WAS ADMIRING THE LOVELY RED MOON WITH A NEW-FOUND FRIEND. NEXT THING YOU KNOW IT, WE ALL 3, WERE LOOKING UP AT THIS RED MOON IN AWE, SO WE ALL DECIDED TO CATCH A PHOTO OF THIS LUNAR ECLIPSE. I DIDN'T KNOW MY PHONE WAS ON THE SETTING TO TAKE 2 FRAMES INSTEAD OF JUST ONE PHOTO AT A TIME. WHEN I REVIEWED THE SERIES OF PHOTOS, THE 2ND FRAME CAUGHT A LIGHT OF SOME SORT, BUT IN MOTION OR SOMETHING LIKE THAT. MY NEIGHBOR TRIED TO SAY IT WAS A REFLECTION OR LIGHT TRICK BUT IT WAS JUST TOO VISIBLE TO BE ANYTHING LESS THAN A REAL PHENOMENA. I STILL HAVE THE PICTURES TO THIS DAY.
