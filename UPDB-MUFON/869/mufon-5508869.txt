{cak} Colorful ufo seen in West La, shooting up into sky, falling, disappearing again and again

At 830pm on Tuesday I passed by my window and noticed that a green orb-like object shot into the sky at a speed that I hadnt seen before. I had seen the same type of craft last week and weeks before that in the same direction. The craft shot up into the sky, hovered around, changed colors, turned the lights off, turned them on again, and then fell into treetops. Then a few minutes later it did the same thing. I recorded the scene the best that I could with my camera and iPad.
