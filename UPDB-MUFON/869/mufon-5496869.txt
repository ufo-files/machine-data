{nd} Was either very large or very close, it glowed bright white but did not shine like a star would.

I was traveling on I-20 east bound around 9pm through Stephenville TX when I observed a white boomerang shaped object demonstrating what looked a crash course toward the ground heading southeast to northwest over the highway. The object was very detailed and bright but did not give off a starry glow like something on fire or a star would. The observation was 2 seconds or less and the object seemed to disappear before a close impact rather than crash. The object was either very large or very close. My POV would have been that the object was 2 inches in width and an inch or so in length. Altitude was either very low or again, would had to have been enormous in size.
