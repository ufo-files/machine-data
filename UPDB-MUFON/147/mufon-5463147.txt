{rjl} Bright white orb/sphere traveling fast at 90-degree angle

I was driving home from work at approximately 2:30 in the morning on country rd 42 in Burnsville when I noticed a large object in the sky zip down at a 90-degree angle for a few seconds. I was located just west of Nicollet Ave in Burnsville and the object was oriented towards Eagan/Mendota Heights area. I have seen satellites, shooting stars, comets, and meteors in the skies before. This object was too large be confused with any of these. The object was within my sightline and not oriented way up in the sky. The path this object took was a straight up and down pattern at a 90 degree and angle and was clearly in our atmosphere. There was a vehicle( pick-up truck) in front of me that pumped his breaks in front of me that draws my curiosity and leads me to wonder if they had seen it too.
