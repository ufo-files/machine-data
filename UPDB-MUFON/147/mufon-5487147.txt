On August 27, 1964 around 9:30pm, my father,mother and me were watching the 1964 Democractic National Convention on tv when my father took the trash outside in the backyard to the incinator,shortly thereafter my mother & I heard my father call to us to come out to the backyard. We did and he told us to look-up,when we did We saw a levitating metallic craft slowly moving from north west to southeast at tree top. I can clearly remember the top dome portion had rectangle Windows that you could peer through(transparent )and lighted from the inside like a hallway. It also had white lights on the center seam. We continue to watch until it went behind some buildings! I was 5 years old when this happened I'm 58 now, besides the craft itself, the most mysterious thing was that nobody in the neighborhood saw anything. Nothing!, during that time that area was very populated , somebody should have seen something.....it was my thought that "they" have power to block thing!..... [Name removed/cms/tg]
