Star-like spheres

This is the second time I found them in the sky around the sun at a different time. They seem to be orbiting the sun or following its path. Each time they are equal distance from one another in every photo in pairs. When I took the pictures my phone immediately shut off. I had 82% battery and suddenly it went dead. When I pulled up the photos I got a sign saying some were truncated which must have happened when the power randomly went off. They are only seconds apart and the spheres disappear and reappear in different places.
