Huge Glowing Orb/Stars moving around great speeds

I’m Ronnie Tapp, me, my mom, and stepdad all witnessed this.

The first thing that happened was a very large pulsating orange orb that was to the Southeast. It pulsated with random roars for about 5 minutes then was gone. About 30 seconds later we look to the Southwest and there it is again doing the same thing for about 5 minutes then it was gone. I have a video of the first of it.

Then this is where things got crazy, about 30 minutes later we are out on the balcony looking to the sky. At this moment we see up in the stars what looked like 5 stars in formation going west across the sky to a certain point. They were moving at incredible speeds and when they got to the location the lights went off. Then another 3 going along the same pattern did the same thing. Then coming from the north headed south another 3-5 moving at the same rate to the same location. Then a cloud went by and we saw 3 go behind the cloud towards the same location but saw an incredible flash of light and only 1 came out of it got to the location that the others headed to and like all the others the lights shut off. I was so mesmerized by this I couldn’t look away and didn’t even think to get my phone to try and record. They were so high up I don’t know if it would have been possible anyway.
