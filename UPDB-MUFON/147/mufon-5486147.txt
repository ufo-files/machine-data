{dr}Saw a drone before them but it was different. First saw one then I believe 3 or 4 more. Witnessed by both me and my wife. Not sure if ufo or something rider

Wife and I were home in south Austin, tx. It was new years eve and we had recently seen what we were pretty sure was a drone. Watching the night sky for fireworks and hoping for another glimpse of the drone. I heard a humming. Thinking it was the return on the drone we started looking. Then from the north moving in a straight line we saw something not making a sound but moving across the night sky. At first I thought it was a burning piece of debris from a firework. But it kept rising and moving further and further away until it had moved either out of sight or into the clouds. Then when it had disappeared anther came into sight from the same direction as the last on had originated. Then I ran inside and my wife called out because another had started to go across the sky and then 2 more at shorter intervals. I'M still not sure it wasn't a weird flare orfirework. But it was definitely something I nor my my wife had ever seen.
