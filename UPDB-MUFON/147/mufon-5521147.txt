White orb moving east to west in skies over LAX

At approximately 13:00 I was unloading my car with supplies for the office. I happened to gaze north into the beautiful blue skies over LAX when a white spherical object caught my eye, about 30 degrees above the horizon, moving fast from east to west. I will liken the speed of the object to what you might expect of a fighter jet on mission. At first I considered it might be a balloon, but the speed was tremendous and the trajectory was straight line to the horizon and unwavering. The size of the object appeared to the eye as about the size of a very small spherical pill. The color was white, but had a metallic quality, as the sunlight seemed to reflect off the surface and I caught a glint or two. Because the object was so small I did not want to take my eyes off it for fear of losing it. Keeping my eye on the object, I backed to the door of my office and called for some of my colleagues to come out. They came but too late, as the object disappeared from view behind the roof line of a house. I tried to anticipate its emergence on the other side of the house, but it never showed. I ran a little further down the street to see if I could re-acquire the object, but alas, it was gone. Neat little mini-sighting.
