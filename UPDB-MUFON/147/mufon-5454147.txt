Silver reflective pill shape rotating unknown object

I was about to walk upstairs of my house and I noticed a reflective object that was in the blue sky. At first I thought it was an airplane but it stayed stationary in one place. I quickly grabbed my son, daughter and wife who all observed the object. It appeared very difficult to see and it was reflective spinning and moving up and down very very slowly, then all of a sudden it was gone.
