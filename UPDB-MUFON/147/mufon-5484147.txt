Bright light travelling upwards and strobes either side

Looking out of my bedroom window I noticed two strobe like lights swirling around the sky ... Then a bright light appeared between the two.. This light started travelling upwards and grew brighter... It went behind a cloud then it was as if there was an explosion and puff of smoke came from the bottom and formed a smoke like circle.. The light then veered to the side and grew brighter and started travelling upwards again ... This then disappeared and so did the two strobes
