It had the look of an airliner with running lights on.

I stepped out of my front door about 6:30 PM on a dark February evening, facing due North when I saw what appeared to be a plane ascending after take-off from Logan Airport in Boston, a not infrequent sight. I watched it for a few seconds as I sometimes do, wondering where it was headed.

There didn't seem to be anything unusual about the object. As I said above it appeared to be a passenger jet with running lights until it made a sharp right turn of more than 90 but less than 110 degrees to the best of my judgement while continuing to gain altitude. That surprised me, of course, as I have never seen a conventional jet make such a sharp turn. What surprised me is that a second or two after seeing the object make the right turn, I heard what sounded like jet engines at a much lower altitude. I should point out that I've seen many planes on ascent from Logan but never heard a sound before.

Then, I realized what I was hearing was a delayed sound that could only have been the result of the object flying at supersonic speeds. It's posssible that what I heard was a sonic boom. It's been many years since I've heard that sound so I'm not certain I could indentify a sonic boom if I heard one today.

Finally, just after the object made an acute turn back to the left, again while gaining altitude, I had to look away, finding the whole thing a bit confusing and a little frightening.

I have no idea what it was but I'd really like to know.
