{rjl} Glowing orb was white but then turned red and began moving. Eventually lost sight as it descended behind tree line.

As I loaded my bags into my car, I noticed a very large bright star-like object near the moon. I assumed it was a planet but thought I had never seen a planet so bright. I drove the 3 miles to my house and as I exited the car, the white light had become a bright glowing red orb that hovered in the sky and appeared to be moving in a slow circle. My boyfriend came out to help with the shopping bags and I pointed at the orb. He initially thought it was a helicopter in flames but then realized it wasn’t falling, but just sitting in the sky. And then it started to very slowly descend. We lost sight of it behind a tree line.
