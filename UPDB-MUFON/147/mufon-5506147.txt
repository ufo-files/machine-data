{rjl} 4-5 light objects flying slowly in a straight line then dissapearing

It was around 10 PM and I was in the front passenger seat on my way to the theater in Napa. I noticed the passengers in the car next to me had their windows rolled down and wer filming something with their phones. 

We made the turn to get on the street where the theater is on and I saw what looked like an airplane flying in the sky, a bright far away light moving slowly in a straight line. Then I noticed more lights moving behind the first one. There was a total of 4-5 lights moving slowly and close together in a straight line across the sky. I opened my window to see if they were making any noise and they werent. After about a minute, they all disappeared except the one in front leading. After about 30 seconds, that one disappeared as well.
