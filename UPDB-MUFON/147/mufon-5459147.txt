{rjl} Object/Light tumbling from sky extremely fast

We were at a vista point of the Kings Canyon National Park, and I took a picture. Nothing unusual was observed by me or my wife. This is a live photo on iphone XR. Later when I checked the photo, I saw a light streak in the middle of the live photo, almost like an intense halogen flash. My wife said it must be a reflection. 
Before I went to sleep, I was reviewing the photos taken during the day, and found this "flash" photo intriguing. By going frame to frame, I found the "light" was coming toward us from far away. In less than 1s, the photo captured 4 "flashes" and we can see the "flash" is in front of the trees/mountain. If that is all I could see, I would not have reported this incident.
Many weeks later, I was going frame by frame tracing the "flash" to the clear blue sky in the background, that is where I am stunned. It first appeared as a faint "bright" cigar (horizontal) shape high in the sky, and quickly drops in altitude into a "dot", then becomes vertical cigar "dark" object in front of the bright horizon, then it appeared as a "flash", followed by 3 more "flashes" . In the animated picture, you can not see its decent from the sky because it happened so fast and background sky is bright. You can eventually make out the faint decent by staring at the last visible flash for a long time. Clearly, this is not a reflection. It is an object from sky but seen as "flashes" in the end.
