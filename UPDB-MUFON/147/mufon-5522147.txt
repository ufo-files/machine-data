Odd large contrails...

On two occasions I have seen enormous contrails in the sky, typically with contrails, are so high that they appear to be extremely small, however, in this case they were very low (about the same thickness of the moon at the time)and was across the entire visible sky. I am not an expert but if an object that was this close to the ground made a contrail as big as these were, there should have been some kind of noise. On the first night I observed two contrails that were being sweep by the wind at home during the early evening hours around 6pm-6:30pm with my wife and the second was early in the morning around 4am-4:30am while on my way to work. I am not sure if these were created by man made vehicles or what, but I was hoping I could possibly get some kind of answer one way or another, the whole thing was very weird, I hope I can get some insight on this!
