{cak} fast moving reflective object making no sound

Around 9:30 in the morning I was walking back to work when something bright in the sky caught my attention. It was moving really fast and based on the height of the clouds, and the fact that it had no wings, it was bigger than a plane, therefore not a plane. It was moving towards Disney (our store is 4 miles north of disney) at a very fast speed with no sound. it was round, metallic, and reflected the sun like it was covered in chrome.
