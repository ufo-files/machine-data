{nd} 2 objects, 1 hovered then straight line shot away. the other left a descending trail that dissappeared in seconds.

Was on dinner break at Woodbury Commons in central valley ny at 530 pm. 100% cloud free sky. I looked up and ahead and noticed what looked to be a short chem trail/ plane cloud trail. It appeared to be descending but I couldnt see any object that was creating this trail. It was also very short in length compared to the trails you see that cross the sky regularly. I looked to my left over the mountains that sit next to the shopping complex and noticed a silver oval shaped object that I would guess was about 3000 feet in elevation hovering. It was too far away to make out any characteristics on its surface. This object I would guess was about 5 away from the other object creating the descending clous or smoke trail. I turned my head away for maybe 5 seconds at most to look at a person walking by me and then looked back up and ahead towards where the descending trail was and it had completely dissappeared. Usually plane trails linger and spread as they fade away. This one was completely gone! Just bright blue sky. I looked for the silver object to my left and it haf now moved miles past where the trail had been. Almost out of my view which I would guess could be about 15 miles or so. I cant imagine an object traveling at that speed. I couldnt explain anything I had just seen and about 5 later a low flowing green helicopter flew over the mountain tops and continued until out of my view due to the mountains. I went back into work and have been thinking about it ever since. I have never seen any cloud/chem trail dissappear in a matter of seconds or an object get from one point in the sky to another in seconds. I wish I had time to get my phone out to shoot video of this but I don't think I would have had time since the whole event lasted under 10 seconds.
