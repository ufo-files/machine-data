I was driving home going down 117 in Bridgton, just before the 302 junction, I was on the top of the hill, where the road faces south. The storm clouds were still heavy in the sky, and you could not see past them. This light just sat their, larger than an air tower light. It didn't blink, but it was sparkly. It was directly down the road but obviously below the cloud line. NO other light could be seen, at all last night. This thing sat there for a good 2-3 minutes, and began to fade as I turned down the road I needed. I could not spot it after that. It was really bizarre. A golden light with heavy clouds above and behind it, was definitely a UFO.
