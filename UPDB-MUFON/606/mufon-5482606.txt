{jpg} Bright light over south east corner of lummi island

On Haxton Way driving west to work at Intalco when I saw a bright light basket ball size about 3/4 up the highest point on Lummi island on the Southeast corner. I thought I saw just seeing things myself but when I got to work. 2 other people said they saw the same thing but that they saw it earlier and it was above Island and then dropped down to fog level so they couldn't see it anymore.
