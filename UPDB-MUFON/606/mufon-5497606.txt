{dr}Disc shaped objects appeared in hills 1-2 at a time, hovered for a bit then moved increasing in speed east.

I was watching television with my boyfriend, [name deleted; trb], when we noticed a large, red light swim around in the sky then quickly ascend up and disappear. Not long later, we noticed two golden colored star-like things hovering above the hills where the red light had come from. We noticed these lighting slowly floating up and down before one started slowly moving east and increased gradually in speed. We ran upstairs, where my dad kept a pair of binoculars near the window. We observed the objects, which were disk shaped with red and white lights on the bottom and moved in a rotating motion with the red light flashing every rotation.As soon as both objects flew out of sight, we noticed another faint light appear in the same location where the previous two appeared. And it did the same thing as the other two. We saw about 7 before seeing one that appeared fainter and almost as if it were struggling. Out of nowhere, one of the ones from before it turned around abruptly and sped over to the struggling one. It hovered above it for a little bit before slowly moving east again, which the other one that had struggled a little lower and behind it, both rotating in sync and maintaining a constant distance between each other before disappearing the same way the others had. We went downstairs onto the porch and observed the discs, until we heard a loud female scream that came from the SW and ran back inside. We saw around 10 before my dad and I had to drive [name deleted; trb] back to his house. After coming back home, I observed 4 more of these discs before forcing myself not to look anymore as I felt on edge, nervous, and scared.
