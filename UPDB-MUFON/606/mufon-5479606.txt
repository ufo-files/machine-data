Last night around 7:20pm to 7:25 pm, my family and I were on the way home from a school event. right before we turned into our subdivision, my daughter screamed" look! is it an UFO? " I dismissed her calm the first time because we were about 30-40 miles away from a very busy airport so it could be an airplane that she saw. But she insisted so I looked up and I saw a large very dark triangle flying object in the sky. It was almost right above our heads. Because it was very far up but below the cloud, I think this flying object would have been bigger than my estimation. It had blue and red lights all over two side of the dark triangle. It was not just a think strip of lights. They have a certain width to it. I thought it looked like a V sharp air craft but my daughter said the center part and the tail did not have lights so only V was easy to spot. It was about 1 to 2 minutes, the craft was stationary in the sky. I asked my husband to stop the car and park on the side so he could see. But he said there was a car right behind us so he couldn't stop. By the time, we turn into our subdivision, I asked my husband to look up because we were slowing driving by a corn field, he should be able to see. But the flying craft disappeared. I did not think it is possible to disappeared that quickly specially we had unobstructed view by that time. The whole event did not last more than 2-3 minutes. My daughter was worried about alien abduction so I checked the time and there was no " missing time" so she felt better.
