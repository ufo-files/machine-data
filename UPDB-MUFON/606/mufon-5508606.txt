08/14/2013 I was standing outside in the driveway on the phone with a friend. It was approximately around 10p.m.ish. I noticed a beam of light coming from behind the house up in the sky. It was moving somewhat slowly then was out of sight. It was a bright white/yellowish light beaming across the sky the it just remained still in a straight line. Didnt move or anything. I thought it was the strangest thing I had ever seen. I told my friend on the phone go outside check it out. So, she did and didn't see it. I said its directly above my head in a straight line up in the sky. I kept saying what is that and where is coming from? She couldnt see it and then she got off the phone with me. I kept staring at the light trying to figure out what it was and where it was coming from. I thought for a second maybe its a meteror shower still but then again they said it was on the 12th and 13th. I tried to take a picture from my cell phone and it was just blurred light. So, I deleted it and tried take it again. Still wouldnt turn out. So, I gave up. Then I went inside when I came back outside about 5mins later it was gone. Like it disappeared.
