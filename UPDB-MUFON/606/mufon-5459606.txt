2 round shadowy objects in the clouds.

Recording out of the window of a Norwegian airlines flight heading from Stavanger Norway to Oslo Norway when when I spotted with my Galaxy S8 camera 2 objects of an obscure light is spotted in the cumulonimbus clouds toward the top of the video. I thought they were reflections from the camera but as I moved the camera to get a better vantage point another round and kind of greyed out object joined in. You have to look closely but the wing of the plane shows that i am hold my phone stable.

The same day in Stavnger Norway driving to the airport in Sola suburb recorded what looks like a cloud but this cloud is moving behind other clouds way to fast and keeps it cigar like shape. These are our first videos of recordings of such kind.
