{dr}slow moving, low flying ufo

Went out for a ciggarette. Was looking at the news on my phone when my phone lost all service. I looked up and for about ten seconds saw a triangular aircraft probably of an altitude of 300 to 4oo feet. Made no noise. Had flashing white lights on what appeared to be wings. Red and bluish green lights were underneath. The thing that startled me was i heard nothing, no sound as it approached or it past. I thought it may have been a plane when i first saw it but it is very cloudy tonight and the craft was lower than any plane of that size i have seen at that altitude.I don't think i would have noticed it unless my phone got screwed up. Was moving in a straight path and constant speed as it was lost from sight behind trees. There was a strange blue flash that appeared to be like a camera flash a few seconds before i noticed my phone wasn't working and i happened to see the object. It startled me quite a bit and strangely my dogs began barking as I was outside, immediately as I saw the craft. The dogs were inside the house. One of my dogs is deaf and she barked as well (don't know why, she never does).
