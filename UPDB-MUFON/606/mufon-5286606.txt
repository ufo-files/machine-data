Round star like object flying in the stars making 4 45 degree turns in 1 minutes and 45 seconds.

This happened at my home in Gilbert SC around October 2015, while I was waiting for someone to pick up a Planner that I was selling. It was around 9PM and very dark I just started looking at the stars it was very clear outside. Looked at the big and small dipper noticed some air planes. Then after awhile I noticed something moving in the sky at 45 degrees high in the sky. Thought to my self the space station then I realized it was to high in the sky, all of a sudden it made 45 degree turn to the left. It moved past about four stars and made another 45 degree turn to the left, then same thing again after passing 4 to 5 stars. Next it made a right turn all this in about a 1 minute and 45 seconds. I was thinking to myself that this thing is moving very fast, then the same movements repeated all over again. Think I watched it for about 10 minutes all together, then my guy showed up to buy the planner. After he left I think about 20 minutes had gone by. I couldn't see it after that and was thinking this had to be a UFO. I have never seen anything like this in the sky before and will never forget it. I told a couple people what I saw they looked at me like I was crazy, so after that I kept to myself.
