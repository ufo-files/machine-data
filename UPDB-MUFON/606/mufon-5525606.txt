Slow flashing round object that flashes dark to very bright the goes dark.

3 Times over the last 3 weeks Witnessed all three times by me and once by my mother with me. I have seen a round object that flashes white very slowly from dark to very bright then completely shuts off, but you can still see a small dark object flying afterwards. The first incident lasted for about 5-10 minutes. The second time was only for about a minute or 2 with only 3 flashes. The 3rd was only seconds and was only 1 flash but was absolutely the same object. I'm used to seeing meteors and airplanes where I live and this is neither.
