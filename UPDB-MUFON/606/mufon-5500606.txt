{cak} Dark sphere flying at high speed with no sound or lights just above the treetops

My wife and I were driving southbound on Himalaya just before midnight on 7/22/2014. Just as we approached the intersection at Chenago, a dark shadow streaked across in front of us about 100 feet in front and ~100 feet above. There was no other traffic and as the object was about 3/4 the way across my field of vision, the reflection of the street light reflected off of the side of it. That is when I caught a glimpse of it, just before it disappeared into the darkness. It appeared to be a black sphere large enough to hold two people. The exterior was shiny and the speed it was going made the reflection look more like a camera flash. The whole thing lasted about 1 second. I saw out of the corner of my eye that my wife jumped from the flash. I asked her if she just saw that. She replied "I saw something fly by and then the flash"
