Pulsating orb emitted an orb

Upon returning from shopping and turning into our street in a southernly direction we did observe a pulsating glowing orb very far away appearing to possibly be over the foothills of the beginning of the Allegheny Mountains in Ashford Junction or possibly beyond. It may have been closer or farther impossible to tell. What we could tell was that the object was hovering and pulsating from white to red to orange. We didn't know what to think until it emitted a smaller white orb for several seconds and then the orb went back into the larger orb many many times it's size. This is when we began to take video footage of the orb lasting 58 seconds in duration. We got back in the car to head south to try to get a better look at the object. At the end of the street the object had moved from view or disappeared altogether.
