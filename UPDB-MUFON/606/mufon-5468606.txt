{td} Small white object flew across sky at fast rate of speed with absoluty no sound

June 7 2017 I used to live in northern NH along the Canadian border where it is extremely remote. I was out on the lake on my canoe where it is very peaceful and quiet. I was making a video of myself to send to my girlfriend who was in another country. When I got home I was preparing to send the video to her and upon watching the video I noticed that an object flew extremely fast from west to east with no tail at all. No sound no nothing. I have shown this video to many people and no one has any explanation. Even when I stop the video and when you can see object I pan in as close as possible it looks like something very small and round with some kind of energy around it almost like a mist, but that is only the still shot. Again no sound or tail at all.It was a crystal clear day. No clouds,no noise, nothing. I do know that back in the early 90's there where many unexplained occurrences in the sky in this area and I have a photograph I took of something unexplained that looks like it hovered over ice in a river. I cannot remember name of organization, but I remember reading in the paper back then that they had sent a team of people to the area to investigate all the reported activity.In the video around 17 seconds into video you will see over my right shoulder object streak across the sky.
