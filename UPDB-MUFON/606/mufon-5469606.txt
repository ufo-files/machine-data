Observed three lights traveling south in v formation at 9:40 pm in western sky

Went outside for a smoke, facing western sky. I was watching what I believe is a satellite traveling north. Before the satellite went out of view I saw three lights in v formation coming south. I first thought they were three satellites but they were too close together, then the middle one, which was closer to the east light, moved closer to the west light and then back again before I lost sight of them. I'm hoping someone else north or south of Toronto also saw this. I often see satellites but never seen three so close together.
