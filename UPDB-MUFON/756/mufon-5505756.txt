{rjl} Glowing Red/Orange Triangular

Walked outside the front door, looked up in the sky and seen what we thought was an airplane on fire. It was triangular in shape and had a reddish glow and at times the glow was Red/Orange. The path the craft made was straight but it would hover or bounce up and down along the path. As it got closer to the house there was no typical airplane sound when it went over the house, it was quiet and simply glowing and hovering. Then the craft started to go up in elevation. We continued to watch the craft and then it suddenly vanished from the sky. It was December 31st at 11:58pm. We watched it for almost 2 minutes until it vanished. (We went outside because it was almost time to yell Happy New Year and saw the weird triangular craft hovering in the sky). We were fascinated by the glow and shape and then it simply disappeared before our eyes. I just hope I do not get abducted by aliens to start my 2014 :)
