{nd} Over two dozen silently pulsating lights seen in troy sky heading east

I was driving north on john r rd in troy with my husband at approximately 9:52 PM on June 30th 2012, approaching the maple rd intersection when we both noticed a "train" of lights traveling slowly and quietly just ahead moving east. There were trees in the skyline but more kept entering our line of vision over the road, over 2 dozen softly pulsating lights coming from the west. We pulled over .25 miles up the road and I got out of the car. I tried to count the lights, there were maybe 27-30 of them but they were fading as they headed east. It was hard for me to tell how low they were but I could not make out any distinct characteristics of the objects except that they looked like low moving stars traveling in a clustered line, no sound at all. I took a quick video on my phone but the image quality is poor and most had already passed.
