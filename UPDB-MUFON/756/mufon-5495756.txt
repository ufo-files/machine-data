TV production team (Peru) saw a stationary object at a great distance, while recording a program.

On February 10 (2015) while the TV program called "Alto al crimen" its members (including the congressman Renzo Reggiardo and journalist Valia Barak Pastor) was filmed on the roof of a building suspended filming realizing the existence of a flying object unknown to them.
The television producer Eduardo Chavez Guerra went to youtube (same day) 3 videos with very high quality. One edited with a short introduction (2 minutes long) and two 26-second and 31 respectively.
On February 12 a new video taken by a young from the terrace of his work which showed the same object from a closer position was added. This video was captured using a lower quality than that used in the original sighting.
Three days later and seeing that the case began to circulate rapidly throughout Peru and Latin America producer Eduardo Chavez Guerra went back up two videos over 19 and 11 seconds.

The witnesses were unable to identify the object.
They described that was shaped saucer and a dark color.
The object remained static, according to the witness, for two hours.
