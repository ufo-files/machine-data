IT WAS JAN 1st 2014 NEAR SUNSET. (I ALWAYS TAKE MY FUJI FINPIX 1300 WITH ME AS WE GET SOME NICE SUNSETS)I NOTICED 3 VERY BRIGHT LIGHTS TO THE WEST OF THE LIMERICK NUCLEAR PLANT AND STOPPED TO WATCH THEM. THEY CAUGHT MY ATTENTION BECAUSE THEY WERE TOO BRIGHT TO BE AN AIRCRAFT.I THOUGHT IT MAY BE SOMETHING OUT OF THE ORDINARY. NEVER SAW LIGHTS THAT BRIGHT IN THE SKY BEFORE.THE OBJECTS SEEMED TO BE ROUND SPHERES THAT AT FIRST MOVE AT ALL AND THE CENTER ONE SEEMED SLIGHTLY RAISED ABOVE THE OUTER ONES. THEY WERE VERY INTENCE BRIGHT WHITE ALMOST SILVER. BY TIME I GOT MY CAMERA OUT OF MY JACKET POCKET THE TWO OUTER LIGHTS HAD VERY QUICKLY GONE FROM A DEAD STOP TO WHERE THE ONE ON THE RIGHT HAD GONE DIRECTLY RIGHT AND THE LEFT WENT DIRECTLY TOWARD THE NUCLEAR PLANT. AT THIS POINT AND TIME I WAS GETTING PRETTY EXCITED AT WHAT I WAS WITNESSING.. I LOST SIGHT OF THOSE TWO AND THE CENTER ONE REMAINED TRAVELING TOWARDS ME. I GOT A PICTURE OF THE REMAINING ORB. I WENT TO TAKE ANOTHER PICTURE AND IT DISAPPEARED. I STAYED AND WATCHED HOPEING I MIGHT BE ABLE TO SEE THEM AGAIN FOR ABOUT ANOTHER 20 MINS BUT DID'NT SEE THEM AGAIN. THE ONE PICTURE I DID GET SHOWS STRUCTURE.
