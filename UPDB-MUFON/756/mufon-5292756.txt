Watching night skies when I seen a yellow/orange lit object coming from the NW towards my direction. It veered to the E and disappeared over the horizon. As soon as I seen it, I began to take pictures of the object. Unfortunately, the shutter speed was slow and the tripod is a little shaky. So the pics have a slight blur to them. Then it disappeared towards the East. Note: the pictures I will post with this are zoomed in/cropped for easier viewing. (also- sorry for the camera settings being messed up)
