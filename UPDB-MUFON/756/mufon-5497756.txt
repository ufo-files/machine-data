{rjl} sited Triaglular outline object stationary in sky left side of Orion constellation' belt

On October 29,2014 at approximately 04:00 hrs. I stepped out onto my patio at my home residence and seeing a clear crisp sky, I went inside my apartment and retrieved my binoculars (Celestron 10X50 field) and started to observe the night sky that was very clear and calm. I was observing the Orion constellation for a few minutes in the S.E. section of the sky. After a slight strain on my neck I then put down the binoculars and continued to observe Orion without the binoculars when I noticed to the left side of Orion's belt a star that seemed to be in the middle of a triangle shape object. The triangle was narrow but I could see the star right though it. The lines of the triangle seemed solid but had a bluish tint to it. I observed no running lights on it nor any landing like lights. I could not detect any sound and figured this object was approx. 3-5 thousand feet or more in the sky . This object remained stationary in the sky over the star for over a minute. Thinking it could have been my eyes I quickly looked at other stars in the vicinity but did not see any shape around them. I then looked back at the star in question and still could see the triangle shape object in the same spot. It was then I remembered my binoculars, turned to pick them up from a small table on my patio and when I trained them on the star, the triangular shaped object was gone. I have no video or pictures of the object in question. Nor did I report this to any authorities thinking it would have been quickly dismissed anyway. I did discuss it with a co-member of my astronomy club who suggested I report it to MUFON.
