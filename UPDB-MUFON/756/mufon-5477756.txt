Pulsating really really bright light 1 day after storm. Never seen anything like it

Pulsating white blue light right over the rooftops of my neighborhood. Never seen anything like it. No chopper, no known aircraft or military aircraft. I can assure that. No star. Firstly way too close, unless stars move underneath our clouds now, secondly way too big! 

Tried to film the sky too, to show that it was a covered black night. Then there is an aircraft, most likely commercial, flying into the direction of the object. You can see the tremendous size and brightness difference.

The object was there for over 3 hours. Everytime I filmed it longer than 2 minutes my phone gave me a warning over an unknown error and deleted the video. As I turned off mobile data connection and WiFi, it had no issues anymore but only had battery left for 1.5 minutes of 4k video recording. 

With the naked eye it was way larger tHan the video shows and was so bright, it literally lit up our entire side of the Appartement block. The pulsation was so weird, it looked like rays from the stars but they kept extending and extending and suddenly it was juse ja Hugh sphere again. And then it repeated the whole thing. 

Never seven anything like this in my life and I have lived next to airports all my life. Even military! 

Third encounter in my life and it's just too clear to me now.
