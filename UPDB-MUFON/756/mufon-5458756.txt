{cak} Shiny Chrome like object

I was driving on highway 395 South just outside of Walker, California when I noticed a very shiny wobbly looking object in a canyon right in front of me. It had no wings and seemed to have an irregular surface as the sun was shining off of it in an irregular way. I have seen something similar when I lived in San Jose, California, but this was at a very low altitude. It was a really shiny looking object, like the very polished chrome I lost sight of it quickly as it was very low, I was driving and navigating through small canyons. I got a really good look at a it which prompted this report. Incidentally, the Marines have a winter training facility approximately 10 miles to the West.
