Glowing Orange Sphere Hovered over Field

As I was driving along the edge of a field east of Rockville's city center I spotted a glowing orange Orb. At first I thought it was an airplane. I stopped and watched it, saw no glowing lights and realized it was not moving but rather, hovering over the field. This continued for approximately 15 minutes before the orb shot straight up in the air and disappeared. The object left no vapor trail and moved faster than any aircraft I have witnessed.
