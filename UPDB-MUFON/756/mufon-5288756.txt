I don't know if I saw more than the eyes or that

I was on my way to work a 5am. It was a very dark night as I remember. As I approached an area higher than surrounding fields I saw a normal looking spotlight coming from a flying vehicle I thought at first was a helicopter so I stopped and watched. Immediately as I stopped the spotlight disappeared and a big round ball of light appeared. I got out of my truck and watched in awe as the ball of reddish light approached me and got bigger as it shot right up to my truck and hovered over the ground. I tried to get back in my truck but couldn't. As I was standing in front of my truck I can remember a feeling like extreme static and bright light. At first that was all I remembered but I have recalled in later life a very white light and black eyes looking down at me. Not scary but something like the eyes are talking to me saying things like I am well and don't panic we are helping you.i don't recall being hurt or poked with anything. The next thing I can recall is driving down the road. I was 45 minutes late for work and the only person I have told this to is my wife. I want to tell this now that I'm retired from my company and really want answers or anything. It has really weighed heavily on me through the years.
