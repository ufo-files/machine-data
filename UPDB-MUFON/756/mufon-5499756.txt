I was driving to work at the Narth Side Post office in Pittsburgh, on PA Route 51 North, at about 4:00 AM. It was in 1966, I believe in the month of July. I saw a light orange colored disk ahead of me, to the left, at about 10:30. At first I thought it was a light shinning up from one of the buildings below. As I got beside it, I knew it was a UFO. I had my window down and there was no sound. As a proceeded, I realized that the disk was traveling beside me, straight at me left,at 9;00.As I approched a gradual right bend, I looked to see the road, and when I looked back the disk was gone. It was down low, as the area is between two slopping hills. It was not very high above the buildings on the ground. Thats why at first I thought it was a light shinning on something. It was pretty large.
