Large bright flash of light, disappeard and reappeared as smaller light

My daughter was looking out the car window to the west at 3:30 p.m. when she saw a large bright flash of light above the low, clouds in the distance. She told me that it was about the size of a penny. So I looked and there was nothing until a small whitish-blue light appeared. This light was about the size of a bright planet. It disappeared and reappeared several times within the same small area of the sky, but in random places. It appeared to be staying close to the thin, dark edge of the cloud moving just below it. There were no clouds covering it. All of this took place during ten minutes. We both saw this happen and I recorded it on my cell phone. The light was too small to be seen on my phones video but I will include it as a frame of reference.
