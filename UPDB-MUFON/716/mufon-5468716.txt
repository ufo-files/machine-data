Sphere showing through very thick storm clouds followed stationary orbit

I observed this very bright sphere ongoing every night since the 29th May. It appears in the West then slowly sinks towards the North. I thought OK some planet like Venus but this is 10x bigger and brighter than any planet seen in the sky. What struck me is now for two nights there has been intermittent cloud cover and even storm clouds and although it may disspear for a few seconds it reappears with no other stars, not even the moon able to penetrate the thick cloud cover but this sphere shines through the thick cloud. So I then concluded it has to be a UFO in a lower orbit or so bright it shines through even thick black thunder clouds. This is ongoing!
