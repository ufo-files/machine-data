{rjl} Hovering clam elipse in clouds

I was at work and I looked to the SE as one of the workers was approaching my position and saw a object in the sky. At first I thought it was a blimp but it was not the correct shape and was not making any sounds. Just as I started to call out to the worker it disappeared behind the clouds.
The ship was an elongated shape but had no gondola as a blimp would have and it had a longitudual stripe with anteneas/appendages the entire length from stem to stern.
