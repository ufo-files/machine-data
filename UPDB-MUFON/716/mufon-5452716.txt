Triangle/Pyramid shaped UFO traveled from Northeast to East banking before disappearing.

At approximately 9:02 PM on 5/17/2020 my friend and I noticed a triangle/pyramidal formation of lights traveling from North to Northeast to finally East.

The lights were configured with a leading red light, a green/yellow light forming the the upper corner , a white/yellow light forming the bottom or third edge along with two red lights in what would be considered the center of a lower plane. See attachment drawing. The formation moved as a singular object and the lights never moved from their locations on the object. The lights did not flash nor fade. 

There did not appear to be any visible surface to the object. As the object moved through the sky it did pass over one star. The star was visible through what should be considered the body of object.

As the object moved from North-Northeast-East there were two bright flashes of white light that appeared to the left after the object had moved to the right.

The object took approximately 4 minutes to travel from N/NE > E and banked before traveling out of view.

I am attaching three photos captured on an iPhone 10, 1 video that may show the movement after processing by someone with the talent to do so, 1 screen capture from a Night Sky app that shows the area of the sky and 1 sketch of what the object looked like created in Photoshop.
