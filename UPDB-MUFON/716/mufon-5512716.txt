{cak} Black object in the sky during the day....

I was out walking my dog. I just so happened to look up and saw the strangest object in the sky. If i had to describe it, i would say it looked like a piece of garbage bag floating. There were birds in the sky but none near this object and i made sure to look at the birds and they were below the object. It almost looked like a bird too but without wings. It floated and just sat there. There was a wind so i could not understand why it wasnt going anywhere. It just rolled and floated pretty much in the same place. I kept walking my dog but would stop to see the progress. It just remained floating.... much like a leaf in the wind only it stayed in the same place. I just kept staring up at the sky and it seemed at this time ( after 5 minutes) to be traveling towards me. I was rather excited and waited to see if anyone was walking down the street so i could point it out to them but nobody was on the street. It was pretty much above me but only now , it looked exactly like a black star. Not completely black, more like dark grey. It was no longer moving. The clouds went over it and i lost sight of it:( I took 2 pictures with my cheap phone camera. I cannot see it tho:( I wonder if anyone could clean up the picture or if it happened to be behind a cloud. Around this time, a man in a pick up truck was pulling into his driveway and looking at me strangely as i stared up into the sky.
