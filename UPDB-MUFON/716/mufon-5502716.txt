{cak} UFO with three pulsating lights following an airplane.

My boyfriend took me to see where he started working. As we were pulling out of the parking lot, I seen an airplane with three white pulsating lights trailing behind it. We quickly got out of the car to get a better look. I knew whatever was behind the plane was not anything I've seen before. I tried to quickly get a picture or video but did not show up on my screen. It was too dark. The plane flew behind clouds with the UFO close behind it. I couldnt see the plane for about 30 seconds but re-appeared again after the clouds. The UFO seemed to take more time to go through the clouds. I thought it may have vanished. I was excited to see it still following the plane. The plane and UFO disappeared as they got to far away. Right when we couldn't see them anymore, cops came to see what we were doing. I did not tell them what we had just seen.
