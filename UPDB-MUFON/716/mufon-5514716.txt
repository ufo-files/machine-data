{nd} I saw the ufo and then i cried

I was at my home drinking hot tea when all the sudden my favorite indie rock station was interrupted with static. I looked out the window and saw a ufo. I told my mom and then I went to sleep. I slept that night with my limbs wrapped tight around my skateboard and my thrasher magazine incase the aliens came to get me.
