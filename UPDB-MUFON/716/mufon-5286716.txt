Bright fiery object flying east to west in a monsoon with low cloud level. No noise.

I took my dog for a walk after the monsoon storm. It was still drizzling. We walked out my front door which faces east. I am a plane enthusiast, and commercial flights to Sky Harbor go over my complex. Plus F35s out of Luke fly over all the time. 
It was super cloudy with low level clouds and rain drizzling. I walked my dog to the north and when she stopped to go, I looked up and east. A bright, red, fiery orb came over the trees make no noise. I grab my phone and recorded it as best I could while controlling my dog. This thing was bright, red and travelling east to west. Flew right over me and was ascending. I heard no noise. No flashing lights. This was not a plane, not a fighter jet, not a drone, no fireworks due to 4th of July, and nothing I can explain. As it flew west it ascended and disappeared. I have it on video on my file. The file is too big to share. I am 100% certain this was nothing I have seen before. I do not know what I saw.
