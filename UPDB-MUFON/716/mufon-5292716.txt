Hovering , then moving,hovered above corn field and my barn.

I just happened to go out on my porch at the right time about 9: 20 pm on July 31. It came over my barn and house very low. It went up the road hovered over the corn field and then I lost sight of it. Went it came over me it looked to be dark gray or black color and was triangular shape. I though it was going to land in the field when I lost sight of it. In the first pic you can see it above my barn with the moon at its right side. In the second pic its hovering with the moon on its right side.
