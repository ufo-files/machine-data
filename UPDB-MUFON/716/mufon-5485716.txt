A large beam or orb of bright yellow light as big as the moon.

I was outside on the balcony of my two storey home having a cigarette at exactly 9:35 pm 21/01/2016 when from my right hand side I saw a large yellowish ball of bright light that was bigger than the moon begin flying through the sky. At first sight it was just above the tree tops yet I still couldn't manage to get an estimate of how far it was nor could I see past it's light. It flew through the sky at a steady pace yet still much faster than a plane or satellite. I called for my mother who was also witness to it. It was completely silent and began ascending towards the moon on my far left hand side. It flew further away and became smaller until it was slightly bigger than a star and then completely vanished in a blink. I heard neighbors commenting on it and believe they were witness to it as well. The moment it disappeared I heard numerous car alarms go off from around the neighborhood. I was in complete shock yet excited about the encounter however I now feel incredibly uneasy. I have never seen anything like it before and am 100% certain on what I saw.
