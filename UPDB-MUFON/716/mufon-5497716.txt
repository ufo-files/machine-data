{dr}Not first time of sighting. Seen several times. Looks like a star at first but very bright. with binoculars it is a large round ball with multi-colored balls spinning around it.It turns in all directions.

Could not sleep so I went on deck. looking out over mountains and tree tops and noticed this large star. then noticed it seemed to be pulsating and changing color. Then I noticed another one. Went in house to get binoculars and looked closer. They seem to have a center light that is larger with smaller round lights spinning around the larger one. They rotate like a carnival ride. forward backward downward and upward.The prominent colors are blue green and red.Without binoculars they seem to stay in one place but with the binoculars you can see them move up and down and sideways. For the most part they stay in the same general location. I have seen these objects several times in the past two years.My family and friends see them also. They are beautiful to me but also make my imagination rage. Sometimes they seem to disappear but come right back to same place. I never lose sight of them. I just stop watching them and go back inside.
