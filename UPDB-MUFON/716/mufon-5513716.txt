(GS) Red Hovering Light

My boyfriend had went outside to smoke,and I had told him about the meteor shower that was supposed to take place. Just as I said that he saw a meteor. I was getting up to look also, and he said " Look at this Red Star". I thought maybe it was a planet or something,but it was really bright in the sky. Just far away like a star. It was a reddish, pinkish color and looked as if it was twinkling, But also like it was hovering. Because it was pretty much stationary but looked like it was kind of moving around in 1 spot. I had called my other friend to the door to look at it also. We were all trying to figure out what it was. After 5 minutes or so, it kind of "fizzled out" then appeared again just to the right of where it was originally. Just sitting there. After that I just went inside. I should have taken pic , but my cell phone takes crappy photos/videos. Needless to say,we are a little spooked.
