{cak} Seen hovering, then 2nd light appeared, and towards end a third light flashed.

I was heading home from work and noticed a low light in sky. I stopped on side of highway and snapped a few pictures. Proceeded home and seen a third smaller second light trailing behind it and flashing a lot faster than the bigger light was. Pulled onto my street and stopped into the church parking lot and took a video with my nikon camera 500 mm lens. In the beginning the camera was not stabilized while I was shooting it and then later I stabilized it the best I could with the truck door and mirror to shoot the rest of the video. while reviewing the video I noticed the smaller light flashed faster than the bigger light and its coloring was mainly red with orange and a greenish white color light and would fade in and out while the bigger light looked mostly white with some yellowish orange to it. Then towards the end of the video with 17 seconds left a third light flashed brightly and then was no more. I have seen these lights a lot since December of 2014 and am wondering what it is that is hanging around our city in the dark. To look in the sky it blends well enough to think its just a light cause it is on the low side just hovering. Planes can be seen higher in the sky and the lights of the planes and the sequence of the blinking is totally different from what I am observing.
