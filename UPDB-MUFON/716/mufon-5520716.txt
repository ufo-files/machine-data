Observed military planes flying in the area then witnessed craft shoot across sky and another descend.

My girlfriend and I where on our way home when she witnessed something in the sky. When we got home I looked to the sky to see three military jets flying in a circular motion around the city and surrounding area. To the south I witnessed a bright object I had originally believed to be a star or planet to the west start to descend slowly and eventually disappeared. As I continued to watch the jets fly around the sky I saw a bright object hovering slowly at an altitude higher than the planes to the East heading West. Then in a split second the object accelerated at an extreme speed Westward and disappeared into cloud coverage. The jets continued to monitor the skies for about another hour.
