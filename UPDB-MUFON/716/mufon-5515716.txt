(GS) Two objects flashed across the sky in the same direction on opposite sides of the sky.

One object was streaked across the sky while driving to my house with my boyfriend and two friends. I was driving down highway 7 going west when in the corner of my eye i noticed a bright light but disappeared right away leaving only with a ghost trail behind it. I quickly jumped a bit and kind of played it off at first, shortly after on the other side of my windshield, I recognized a bright orb a few seconds then it shot in the same direction heading quickly west. As soon as the second one passed I turned my head to my boyfriend, without saying anything to him he told me "Yeah, I seen it ." 
I asked if he seen them both but he only seen the first one that I did, and neither of the friends in back knew what we were talking about.
