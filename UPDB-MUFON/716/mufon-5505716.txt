{rjl} white orb

Took dog out after fireworks stopped and while waiting on dog noticed a white light passing above me several hundred feet up , orb like , traveling silently above me low enough to illuminate the misty clouds. Some times I could clearly see the round white light then it would illuminate the clouds as it passed through them. It was completely quiet. There was a light sprinkle coming down. I watched it until it dimmed from the clouds as it went out of view. Not a plane , happened just north of MacDill AFB.
