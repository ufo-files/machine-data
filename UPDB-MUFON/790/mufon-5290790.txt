Three bright lights flash on briefly then off, traveling inline following each other

The evening of 12-3-2020 at 6:15PM while looking for satellites i observed a bright white flash in the sky, then another flash shortly behind where the first flash was, Then another bright flash shortly followed, It appeared to be a third object. This all happened within about 6 seconds and did not happen again. My IPHONE compass showed them to be moving approximately 50 deg. NE.
