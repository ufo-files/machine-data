Multiple red/orange lights in a tight pattern over 90/1604, aprox 1500 ft. 2 jets in circle pattern around objects.

It was about 7pm CST on the 25th of March, 2012. My wife and I both saw several redish-orange orbs in the sky, aprox 1500 ft. They were moving away, up the I-90 corridor. What got my attention as a inactive officer for the US Air Force, was the two jets in a circle observer pattern flying around the objects, one of each side, about a half mile away from the objects. It was clear that whatever these were, they were of interest to our military. This was around the old Medina Air Force base, which used to be a missile silo before it was annexed by Lackland.
