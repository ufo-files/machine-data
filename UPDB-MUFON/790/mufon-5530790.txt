Disc shape seen on film not by naked eye.

Was taking pictures of the sunset after a rainfall. Was standing in my front yard. upon examining film, disc shape shows in one photo but not in the previous or next slide taken within the same 60 seconds. 5:30pm on 5/24/10. Attached in 2nd photo is a close up. Using the street lights as a frame of reference, the object appears quite large. The street lights are located approx 3/4 of a mile from me. It is not the moon or a star as those would have appeared in every photo.
