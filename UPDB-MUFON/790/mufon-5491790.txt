On July 5th at 12:45 am I opened my rear deck door and to my left was a bright red orb. As i looked at it I noticed over the treeline another one. It raced over and positioned itself below the first one.This is when I began to record. unfortunately video does no Justice as it was a bright large and solid red. It flickered to a greenish color as well. I had little juice on my phone so I ran into the house to get my charger and when i came back 20 seconds later they were both gone. I posted on FB and a friend seen the same orbs about 5 miles north around the same time. They made no noise.
