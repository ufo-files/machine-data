Two round white lights

We observed two perfectly round white lights in the Southern sky almost directly above. They remained stationary parallel to each other for approximately five minutes. Then, the light on the right moved to the right upward rapidly in an ascending Southwesterly direction then disappeared instantaneously. Then, the light on the left moved exactly the same as the first light except to the left ascending Southeasterly and disappeared instantaneously. The lights had no light being reflected. They were perfectly round and seemed to move so fast they disappeared intstantly. I was aware that there is no technology existing that would allow a human or a human craft to move at the speed these objects travelled.
