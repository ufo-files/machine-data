Round object moved across sky west to east, large as a passenger jet

I was driving home from work and in the sky ahead I saw a round, white object moving at a fairly good clip across the sky from west to east. I tried to grab my camera but it was already gone by the time I got it out of my purse. I saw a passenger jet pass by about a minute later, and the object was at the same altitude as the jet which had just taken off from a nearby airport. The object's diameter was the same length as that of the passenger jet. There is no chance of this being a jet, however, as it was perfectly round. It also moved quite fast across the sky. I remember thinking to myself "they're back'. I had had an experience in October of last year. I wasn't alarmed or scared - it just seemed like it was back and that it was perfectly normal for it to be in the sky near my house.
