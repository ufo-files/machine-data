{cak} Flashing light at high altitude

I was outside in the parking lot of the apartment building on Louisiana Blvd. in Albuquerque when I looked up and noticed a high altitude, stationary light SE of Albuquerque that was changing colors: red, green, blue, and white. Altitude was at or near ceiling of commercial airliner. I observed the light for about 5 minutes and took about 2 minutes of video footage before going back inside. There was no sound, and the light did not move during the time I observed it. I thought it was unusual because it was brighter than most things I have seen in the night sky as well as the fact that it was changing colors.
