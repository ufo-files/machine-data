{cak} Silent Triangle Object Observed

I was 10 years old at the time. I was outdoors with a friend jumping on the trampoline in the back yard of our house just as it was getting dark. Approximately 7:30pm. Clear skys. Only some stars would have been visible. Both sets of parents were indoors. Friend looked up and she saw something and said, "What's that?" I looked up and saw a triangular object moving very slowly and very low in a North Easterly direction. There was no sound (though, my memory seems to make me thing there may have been a slight whisper in the air coming from the object). You could clearly see the outline of the object as triangular, and it had lights around it. I can not remember the amount of lights. I believe they were all whitish in color. It seemed to be moving too slow and too quiet to be any known aircraft. After sitting there watching it for about 30 seconds, we ran in to tell our parents. When they all came back out, the object was gone. It was moving very slow, but it was moving at a rate and elevation where the time we spent indoors gave it enough time to get out of sight. I would say that it would have been around 500ft or lower altitude. Maybe slight more. Possibly the size of a 737. No major airports are in that area. Commuter prop planes would normally be in that area traveling in a South Easterly direction. Those are small, noisy, and at a higher altitude. This was large, low, and silent with a clear triangular shape.
