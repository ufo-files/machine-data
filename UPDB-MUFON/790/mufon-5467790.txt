At a park near home saw two red lights very low less than 200ft followed by a black triangle with white lights all around

I was at a park in Mississauga Ontario at jefton crescent at 1040pm smoking a cigarette on a bench as usual , then at 11pm while still on the bench I felt something gently touch my right shoulder. This startled me and I stood up and looked behind me and nothing/no one was there. Then I sat back down in the same spot on the bench still startled . I looked up at the sky and very low (way too low to be a stars , a drone , plane or helicopter I see two red lights flash a few times then a big black triangle appeared with white blinking lights all around the sides with one VERY bright light at the top right corner . The red lights were not present at the time the triangle appeared. I was fascinated and managed to get a pic and video
