Dundee UFO

It began in the west, at approximately 15 degrees. A set of bright lights as bright as the full moon. As it broke through the low cloud it was clear there were 8 lights, 4 pairs of 2, perfectly round, no glare and clean edges. All in a perfect straight line. The lights were bright but did not cast a beam.. As it moved from west to east it reached its highest elevation at around 40 - 45 degrees. It was due south at this point where the roof obscured my view temporarily. So l made my way to the east of the building with my phone and tried to video the object. The object made no sound at all and l guess it was no further than a mile from me. The last I saw it, it was about 15 degrees high in the east heading out over the North Sea. The videos l shot have to be enhanced as they are very dark.
