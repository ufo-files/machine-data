{cak} Vertical popcicle shape light in sky blinked then left. Two round orange lights appered just after.

While Walking my dogs at 5:40 am my dog stopped and looked back, I too looked back. About 15-20 degrees above the western horizon was a long vertical shape in the sky it was obviously not a plane drone or anything like that. I moved my head and it blinked. When I stayed stationary it would just shine at a constant rate. When I tilted my head again it would blink. If I put my head to the side it would blink or fade... EVERY time, to the point I was scared. This went on for about 3 minutes. I walked away in the opposite direction and kept looking back, I could see it slowly fading away. I looked back one last time after about 20 seconds and saw it fade away, when I turned back the direction I was walking I noticed what I thought was a plane. It flashed like a plane, but was an orange circle. Then another materialized or turned on it's lights or something. This one was right next to the first one. I watched for about a minute and neither object moved at all. They just sat there and pulsed. Then one disappeared and seconds later the other disappeared or maybe they turned off the lights.
