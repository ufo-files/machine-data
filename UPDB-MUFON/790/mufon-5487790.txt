My husband, a friend and myself were walking in a quiet desert area near Thomas and Recker Rd We were watching all the planes line up and come in towards Sky Harbor Airport. We started walking the opposite direction or East back to the truck when we noticed a red oval shaped orb in the sky that appeared to be just above or in front of the nearby mountain. It appeared to be moving towards us and phasing between an orange and red color. We just stood there in amazement discussing what it could be. My husband and our friend Dan are trained observers. My husband is former air force with above top secret clearance serving in Desert Storm and as an MP. He was also a police officer in Ohio prior to his military service. Our friend Dan has served for 12 years doing search and rescue to help locate missing persons in a variety of landscape and has been an author/editor for the past several decades. This object didn't make any sound that we could hear, nor do we believe it to be any form of recognizable aircraft or flare or lantern. It moved under intelligent control.
