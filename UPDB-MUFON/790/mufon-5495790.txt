{rjl} object detected in photo of the moon

Around 11pm was taking a picture of a thin cloud back lit by a bright moon. Took two pictures less than a second apart. Upon view the pictures I noted an object up and to the right of the moon. On the second picture the object appeared to have traveled in a straight line down and to the left. In the second picture the object appeared to be passing through the cloud between my vantage point and the moon. Picture 1 attached shows object up and right of the moon, picture 2 shows object just below and to the left of the moon.
