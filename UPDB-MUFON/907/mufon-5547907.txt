Pulsating light.

My neighbor had called me in reference to a light that he was seeing in the night sky. I went over along with my boyfriend and observed a light that was pulsating blue-red-yellow at times it almost looked like it was swirling.The light stayed there for approx an hour and reoccured for several nights, for about a week. On one particular night I tried recording the light and it drained my video camera. On one night a helicopter with a spot light came down to the top of the tree in my neighbors yard while we were out looking at it. I freaked and hid the video camera, the helicopter stayed for a minute then took off. I thought it was very odd because it came down right on top of the tree and had the spotlight focused on where we were.
