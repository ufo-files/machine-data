On a road trip, first saw above Griswold, Ia. stayed in that place for around 40 minutes as we approached, passed thru, & for about 5 miles North of town.  On the way back at end of trip, we teased that we would see our "friends" again. As we exited the Interstate, and passed through Atlantic, Ia. we saw the pulsating light in the sky south and west of the Hwy to the home place. When we left Atlantic, it came down to head level of the car, stayed just above the fence on the right hand-side of the car and followed us all the way to about 2 miles outside Shenandoah, Ia. where it just disappeared. But it kept pace with our car no matter what the terrain, up or down hills, round curves, etc.. The trips were done late at night between 10pm to 5/6pm. I thought it was a UFO and wanted to stop and say Hi, and find out who, what and where they were from. But my Mother was afraid and whenever I suggested it she would say "Drive, just Drive." Just before it disappeared, I ask my mother if these people followed us home did we have enough food to feed them, just teasing her. Then they were gone. My mother said she was sure it was from Offit Air Force Base in Omaha, Nebr. The strangest thing about this time was that all you could really see was the light that started as a blossom in the center and then shot out a line of bright white light, almost searing in intensity, in all 4 directions; longer to front, back and up then down. Did this every 5 seconds or so continuously for the duration of the visit.
