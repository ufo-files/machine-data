{rjl} white glowing orb. At my zenith. Maybe weather balloon

Near dusk, almost straight overhead, in my backyard, I noticed what looked like a very small moon, too large to be a star or planet. My attention was drawn to it because the sky was still too light for stars to be visible. Thru binoculars it looked like a small full moon. The outer most portion looked brightest with the interior mottled. The sun had just set but I suspected that this object was high enough in the sky that it might have still been illuminated by the sun. I was on the phone with my friend and he suggested it might be a weather balloon. I think that could be a reasonable explanation but I had never seen a weather balloon before. I watched it for 20 minutes or so and it ever so slowly "drifted" to the east. As it moved enough to become obscured by the forest I stopped watching it.
