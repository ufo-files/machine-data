{tjd} Black Triangle with pulsating lights

Q This unknown object flew very fast at a low altitude behind Mt Rubidoux, Riverside CA. It reappeared minutes later across from Fairmount Park, hovering directly above the vehicle I was in with a friend. We instantly brought our phones out to record this hovering craft with the bright lights. As soon as we went to record the object was almost instantaneously headed straight into the night sky at about 120 degrees. It went from about a couple hundred feet above us to miles away from us in seconds. While up close you could almost see through it. However, the few visible stars which were out that night, could be seen through the object. They were a little distorted however. No noise was made and it was hovering midair right above us. This event changed my life forever and is what ultimately lead me on my path into the field of chemistry.
