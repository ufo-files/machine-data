{dr}Huge, round, red light behind the tree line in my yard. At eye level.

I was swimming in my backyard around 10:15PM on June 17th,2014.I was not looking up at the sky. If I had been, I wouldn't have seen the object. I looked across the backyard and saw a red light, approximately 15-20 feet in diameter. Moving very slowly behind the tree line. There was absolutely no sound, and it was too dark outside to see anything but the light. It disappeared behind my house, and reappeared just beyond our property line, changed direction, and crossed the street to the right. And continued slowly, moving between a cemetery and the next street over. Before disappearing behind the trees. It reappeared one more time, and had again changed direction and moved across highway 33 out of my sight.
