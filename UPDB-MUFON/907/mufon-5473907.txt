Orb next to strange condense strip

Walking on a parking lot I recognized a strange condense strip in the sky. The plane flew NW from Vienna Airport and was ascending, so the condense strip changed by height. I took a picture with my smartphone (Nexus 5), because I loved the shape of the strip.
At home I uploaded the images taken that day to my server and had a closer look to the picture. There I saw the transparent ball next to the strip. It was not visible to me before when I looked up through the lens of my cam taking the snap. I am quite sure that I would have recognized it before taking the picture, if I could.
