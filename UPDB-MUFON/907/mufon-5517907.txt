{nd} We saw something bright and moving throught the sky. It eventually vanished.

We were just arrived at residence. The time was 2200hrs. We looked up to admire the clear sky. We observed a bright light moving at approximately 119 degrees. The initial impression was that it was a satellite. However, it was too bright and too low and moving too fast. We observed this for about 2-3 minutes. The light dimmed and then vanished. There was no trail observed either. A plane was seen shortly after traveling at 90 degrees to the trajectory of the observed object.
