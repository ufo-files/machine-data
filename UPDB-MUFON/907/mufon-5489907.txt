I seen lots of lights cris-crossing each other at ground level,like watching a bunch of dirt bike lights zig zagging each other with absolutely no noise

The year was 1982/83,I was young so I don't remember the exact year,at the time I lived in Moonbeam Ontario,Canada,a bunch of us were sitting in the back outside at a chipstand over looking a swampie wooden are it was dusk all most dark,my cousin his cousin aunt and boy friend and I seen some flashing lights out in the woods,now I don't know how exactly how we got to the place in the woods,we were standing in a line looking at these lights cris-crossing each other,for some reason I decided to walk towards the lights,i remember my cousin and his Aunt yelling at me to come back,I was about 100 feet away from them,these lights look like dirt bike lights,cris-crossing each other,I notice fog around me, the lights were really bright getting closer that's wen I realized that I couldn't here the engine noise from the dirt bikes coming at me,they were still yelling at me to come back,I got scared at that point so I turn around to go back to them???what I remember after that, is we were in a storm sewre all five of us,these storm sewers were above ground,before they put them in the ground,I guess it was a construction site not far from the chip stand,what I remember the most that morning is that I didn't know how I got there?and my cousin aunt was in a panic,I have no idea what happen that night,I never told any one for long time,it bothers me to this day ,
