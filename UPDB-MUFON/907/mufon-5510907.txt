(GS) Bright star like light that moved.

I was walking out to my shed at 9:30 pm when I noticed a bright red light in the sky. I watched it for a while wondering iChat star it was. Then I noticed it sort of blinking. Hovered then moved slowly, picked up speed then stopped. It grew brighter for a while then started flickering red and white. Then it dissipated . I called my daughter out to see what I was looking at as a second pair of eyes. She saw appx 1-2 minutes before it disappeared . There were 2 planes flying in the area at the same time.
