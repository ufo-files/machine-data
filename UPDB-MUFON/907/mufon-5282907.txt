The night we saw them

My friend and I were having a sleepover and we decided to look at the stars outside our window. When we looked there was only one bright huge star in the sky. The “star” was flashing over & over and in different patterns once. The “star” was also hovering in one spot and bouncing up and down in the same area that is also why we didn’t think it was a star. We kept watching it and it started to blink on and of After 20 minutes of watching this thing in the sky it started to flash different colors blue and green and the same white light it was already flashing. Shortly after we kept watching it & more & more started to appear in the sky & that’s when we got so freaked out. They were all blinking in different patterns. We noticed there was one in the tree line down below and many more above in a certain pattern or placement in the sky. We noticed on another street over this one street kept going off and flickering. Usually when a street light goes out it doesn’t flicker the way it completely on and off. It would either stay on or go completely off. It was matching the light patterns of the ufo’s that we saw above. Could have been a coincidence as well but it was weird to see a street light doing the same thing the huge light in the sky was doing. 
When I zoomed in on my iPhone camera (not the best quality at all) I it looked like it had a cloud around it. The object also looked like it was morphing into different shapes like a triangle & back to the dome & flipping around. At one point in one of my videos you can see 4 different coils on the side of it. It almost looked like an old stove top. When I zoomed in with my iPhone camera it looked like a slim dome or an a plate upside down I don’t know how to explain the shape because it had this glow around it like a mist almost. We were watching for an hour or two
