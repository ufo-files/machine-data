white circular lights in the sky

Around March of 98 i was watching tv in bed at my studio apt around 9pm26 midnight when a bright flash of light outside my window and my tv receptionflashing on26off a couple of times caught my attention.I went out on my balcony26 observed an oval white light streak across the sky at an incredible rate ofspeed followed by 2 more ovals in formation.All 3 objects seemed to cover anapproximate distance i would say of about 30 miles in less than 2 seconds.
