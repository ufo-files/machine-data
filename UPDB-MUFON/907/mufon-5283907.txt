Flashing lights moving in synchronization

Not the best quality video, but we've never seen anything like this and we have aircraft flying over us at almost every minute. They hovered and danced around (on and off) in the same area for at least 5 minutes, I was only able to capture a little over 2 minutes, but we were both clearly surprised by this sighting. It looked like it was either over the north part of Antelope Island, or across the Salt Lake directly West from where we live. I've tried to gather more information but I think we may have been some of the only people to actually observe it. There may have been more than 5 lights, as the blinking synchronizations were somewhat sporatic for a moment and it made it exactly unclear how many there were. Eventually they just blinked away one by one. I don't think these were F-35s based on the light pattern, and if they were drones those lights would have to be very bright to have been seen from miles away. I did end up cutting the middle of the video out because I needed to put pants on to go outside to try and get a better recording.
