It was a straight line of five lights, red, white red white red. They blinked one after another, in order until I stopped my car. When I stopped, the five lights got really bright and moved quickly to the top of a local bluff, stopped, and then the lights went out. No sound, and huge. Less than 1/10th of a mile away. This took place on US Hwy. 14 near Stockton, Minnesota at about 09:30pm - on 11/28/2017.﻿- I first noticed silver lights at tree top level and thought that they were farm lights. When the Hwy. changed direction, I spotted the lights to my left or NW and just hovering between two bluffs, say 350 feet off the ground - estimated. It was a clear, warm evening.
