{nd} I was cleaning my outside firepit when I saw a solid bright white light moving slowly from the NW to the SE.

It was dusk, at about 18:21 PM CST, and I was cleaning my outside firepit when I saw a solid bright white light moving slowly from the NW to the SE. I observed that it was moving in a straight line. At arms length it was the size of an aspirin. It appeared to be very high in the sky. I ran inside and grabbed my cell phone and went back outside and re-engaged the object visually. I tried to call a friend but he did not answer. I then ran out into the road and tried to wave down vehicles with the light of my cell phone. Some vehicles slowed down and I was pointing up so they might see what I saw. But no one stopped. I did see a red truck stop a short distance past my house and it looked like the driver got out and was watching the sky. I did not speak to this person. I then called my mom and spoke to her diring the rest of the sighting. The object continued to move slowly towards the SE. It never changed in size, nor did it make any sharp movements. It eventually just faded out of my sight.
