Hundreds illuminated flying extremely fast

I was sitting on my back porch late at night in summer, 2014. I often look at the night sky and I saw many, many yellowish objects flying very high overhead at a great speed. At first I thought it was a large flock of some type of bird. Then I realized that they were moving too fast to be birds or even airplanes. Also, there wasn’t enough city light to account for their light; I thought if they are birds why are they lit up? And they were all lit to the same degree. They weren’t in any specific formation, so I kept thinking they must be birds. But again, they were moving very, very fast from one end of my field of vision to the other. It took maybe five seconds or less to travel from one end of the sky behind me to the other end in front of me. Notably, they appeared again the next night. They seemed to be lit from within. They were high up, but it wasn’t like there were exterior lights, or at that distance I couldn’t tell in other words. There was no sound. There were no clouds.
