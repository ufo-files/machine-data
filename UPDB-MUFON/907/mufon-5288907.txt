Hand print left on skin

I woke with a handprint left on my wrist. I though maybe I'd slept odd and it would fade, it hasn't. 
Back story: My husband is a firefighter and is gone for days at a time. It was my first night alone as he'd started his 4 day shift. I put my baby to sleep and slept hard through the night. I "dreamed" I was abducted but not by aliens. In my 3 second dream, they looked like people everything was void, but a blinding light and white back drop. Standing face to face, I came to and turned to escape, slinging my arm as I turned. That is all I recall. 
When I woke, everything was normal aside from the prints, which I still wear on my wrist, and my 14 month old sleeping through the night for the first time since infancy.
The prints are not a bruise. Three fingers of what seem to be burns. I have not experienced any pain or singed arm hair. Now, they seem more like a birth mark.
I sincerely hope I am not wasting your time or coming across as a ridiculous claim.
