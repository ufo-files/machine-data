Multiple starlike objects flying in many directions. Regular jet airforce patrols.

This night was warmer than all the other cold winter nights so I went out with the intention to observe for any interesting aerospace activity. The sky was active with multiple starlike objects. I am however investing in a pair of night vision binoculars soon to record the night sky. It will surely come in handy...
