{nd} Observed redish orange light moving across the sky moving northwesterly

I went out on my deck at approximately 10:15 PM and noticed a reddish-orange light moving across the sky from the southeast to northwest. I watched it for approximately 15 or 20 seconds until the trees in my yard obscured my vision. Then I ran to the back of my house and waited for it to come out from the cover of the trees, but it never came out and I didn't see it again. I cant say for sure that it was anything extraordinary, but it was something I have never seen in the sky before.
