{cak} Three large glowing balls in triangle formation

I was driving West on South Blvd in Pontiac and noticed on my left that there were three large glowing balls in the sky. At first I thought it was a part of the St. Joseph Hospital renovation, but then I noticed that it was not over the hospital. The lights were in a triangle formation. There were a few helicopters flying towards it. The object was hovering and it didn't remind me of an airplane or helicopter.
