{rjl} Stationary red light directly over Mars. Then started flashing red and green. Then moved swiftly directly in front of moon and stopped. Then descended in a straight line down.

I went to close the front door for the evening when I saw a bright red light covering Mars. I knew it was way too red to be Mars so I went outside and then it began to flash red and then green. It then moved swiftly toward the moon and stopped dramatically in front of the moon and then desecended straight down toward the earth. I could see it through the trees and then it was gone. It made no sound but was large enough to block Mars from view.
