Over 40 star like objects travelling single file slowly in a northerly direction

At approximately 5:30 am on Nov. 24 2019 I was sitting on my back deck having a coffee and watching the stars, when I noticed the first object due east and about 45 degrees. I though it must be a satellite because it was similar in size to a star or satellite and was moving slowly through the sky in a straight but slowly descending trajectory. About 5-10 seconds later another one appeared and I though it was strange that another satellite was so close and also on the same flight path. When the third and then fourth appeared 5-10 seconds apart respectively, and the fifth and sixth were in tandem one on top of the other but moving at the same pace and direction I knew it was something else. There was a slow and steady stream of these objects over the next 5-10 minutes, mostly single file but every so many there would be a tandem pair. I counted approximately 40 but that number could be way higher as I may not have noticed the first ones. I recorded for about 5 minutes on my I phone but it appears dark and unable to see any of the objects.
