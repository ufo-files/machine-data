Then it turned and headed south in the direction I was going. I was not able to confirm that it was the Helo and I thought maybe its a drone? Then I see 4 or five more but smaller ones following each other and the first one all headed north along side the parkway. I continued south and I think they must have been drones but thought I would post this for anyone else who saw them. this is the area
