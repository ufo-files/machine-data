Round grey metallic looking round, looked at first as a weather balloon but it was totally still and then went at a increadable speed straight up and disappeared into blue sky. I have looked at pictures of u identified objects but never one like this .

Wife and I were heading out to grocery store and stopped at a stop sign on Lancaster St in Waterloo and I looked right to check traffic and noticed this object in the sky over Kids Ability building about 15 degrees and maybe 500 ft in the air that I thought to be a weather balloon but grey in colour. This object I pointed out to my wife in passenger seat and she also observed it. After a few seconds this hovering object took off straight up at a blinding speed and disappeared into the clear sky without any sound. I am sure others had to see this object! If needed I can draw a picture from memory.
