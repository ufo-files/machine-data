{jpg} Driving home from work about 1/2 mile from home, a green lighted triangular object moved quickly into my view and disappeared at a high speed into a different direction disappearing into dark sky.

Driving home from work on Nov. 6th in the outskirts of Hattiesburg, MS. about 1/2 mile from home, my granddaughter and I witnessed a Triangular object lit in green and white lights come from the left and stop briefly in front of us before it flew at an incredible speed forwards ahead of of us into the dark sky and disappeared in a couple of seconds. No sound was heard, but the radio was turned up so could not of heard if it did.
