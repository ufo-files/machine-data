UAP seeming to reflect the clear morning sky.

Was working my mornings grocery store job that Saturday morning when I noticed a "Bright Star” and just as I looked at it almost it started moving along a path before pausing and then doing a figure eight motion side to side, it then paused one last time and quickly exited the stratosphere without a sound. 

Key Notes of this craft:
-Seemingly reflected the natural light around it from the sun and sky as this was a completely clear sky without any clouds. 
-craft have the illusion that it was no more than 500ft but also the height that a passenger plane would fly at maximum altitude.
