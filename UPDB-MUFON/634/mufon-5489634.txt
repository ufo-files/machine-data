One day in 1992 I was walking to up Frankford Avenue, and I witnessed this green glowing light flying across the sky this is actually the first time I say it, so I told my friends, and they didn't believe me but one day we all were walking up Frankford Avenue and I saw it again and I pointed to it we got scared and ran back to our neighborhood. I remember it flying through the sky slowly then it stopped hovered a few seconds then it shook side to side then flew away. About 4 or 5 of us saw it. A friend of mine who was there has been in prison for 20 years I recently talked to him about it, and he remembers also nobody believe us but us.
