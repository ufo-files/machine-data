it was about 355am when i walked outside on my deck with my dog to let him use the bathroom as i always do around this time. while he does his business i take the time to skywatch. i looked up into the sky in a northeastern direction and witnessed what i can only describe as a large comet(within the atmosphere) traveling slowly in a north-east direction. this lasted about 8-12 seconds. it was extremely bright. white-ish yellow in color. leaving an enormous tail which lit up the sky for many seconds after the glowing "comet" had disappeared behind the hills. i was awestruck. i would love to know what i saw. ive seen nothing comparable other than a comet in my 35 years.
