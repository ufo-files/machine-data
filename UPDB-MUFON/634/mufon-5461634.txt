Bright, Star-like objec. Got brighter, then disappeared.

I was on my way to work 5:25AM driving along road 25 between Tammisaari and Karjaa heading east towards Karjaa. Clear sky not a single cloud and lots of stars visible. And here's where it gets weird. I was looking at the sky, reminiscing the earlier event, (Case 99434) and tought to myself how cool it would be to see such a thing once again. And within seconds of that thought i spotted an object glowing like a brighter star, moving across the dark sky. I could see it moving because of the stars "next to it". As it started to dim down and fade away, i thought "must have been the landing lights on an airplane" (even though i saw no blinking white or red lights as planes usually have) and immediately it started glowing much brighter once again, as if responding to my thought. Shortly after it disappeared completely. I know how this sounds, and i needed to tell someone. I explained it to some realtives but they think i'm crazy of course.
