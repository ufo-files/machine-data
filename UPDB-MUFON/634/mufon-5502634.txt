{cak} Some thing large flying by the moon

On May 15, 2014 at 0650 I felt compelled to take picture of the moon from my front porch. I took one with my tablet but that didn't work as well as I had planned so I used my Nikon D3000 with a Nikon DX AF-S Nikon 55-200mm 1:4-5.6G ED lens. I took 2 pictures about 30 seconds apart the first should the moon but when blown up to see the details you can see what looks like an almond shaped craft in a flight path heading behind the moon. The next picture taken just a few seconds later showed the object was gone.
