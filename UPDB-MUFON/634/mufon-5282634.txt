Saw a craft pulled over and approached and it flew off

I was driving down a fairly trafficked back road. There is a field beside a line of condominiums and just on the edge of the field i noticed an incredibly bright white light. I looked over and out my window to see a cigar shaped craft with a red light on each end on top of the craft and the white light was coming out of of the bottom towards one end straight down. I rolled down the window and didn't hear a sound so I did a u turn and went back. Parked my car got out and began to approach. About 100 ft from it the light shut off so I froze. Then the craft bolted off in an awkward zip zag pattern just over the rooftops at about 60 or 70 mph. I watched it until I couldn't see it anymore.
