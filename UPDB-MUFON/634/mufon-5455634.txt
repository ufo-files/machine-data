While driving south on U.S. 101 through south San Jose, I suddenly noticed bright shining object ascending. When object flew under a cloud it stopped shining and was a solid black circle.
I first thought it must be a small plane reflecting sunlight. There is a small airport, Reid-Hillview, nearby. When object flew under a cloud it was silhouetted as a solid black circle. It took me a few seconds to realize it wasn't a small plane like I first thought. It was ascending at about a 45 degree angle. When the circle got above the cloud back in the sunlight it started sparkling and shining to where I could not see any of the black circle.
