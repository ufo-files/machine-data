{jpg} UFO flew in formation with car then accelerated before hovering ahead of us. Pulsating light from bottom of UFO preceded us discovering car stopped on interstate with dead man at wheel.

Two friends and I were returning to New Orleans on I10 when UFO was observed flying in formation with us for some time. Maybe a minute or longer. After recognizing how strange this aircraft was acting we observed it closely before it quickly accelerated ahead of our auto - maybe a mile or so before hovering and shooting out a pulsating light. As we approached its position, the object shot off toward the gulf. Amazed at what we observed we followed a turn on the highway to discover a car stopped in the rt hand lane. I pulled my car behind the stopped Monte Carlo to protect my companions as they left the car to wake up the driver we had observed slumping over his steering wheel. They quickly returned to me announcing the driver was not asleep but dead. 
As we removed him from the car,the car started accelerating. I ran to the car and pulled it over to the shoulder. Shortly another passerby stopped to check and we stayed until a state trooper arrived.
