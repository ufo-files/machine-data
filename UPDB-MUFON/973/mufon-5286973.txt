Orbs / lights moving around strangely

I was in Mexico on vacation with my wife staying at our friends property completely off the grid just out side of Yellapa.Got up around midnight to use the bathroom Stoped to look out over the water and some lights caught my eye that were moving around strangely.it’s pitch dark out and we are in the middle of nowhere . No outside light influences what so ever . At first I thought is that a boat on the water or a helicopter ? But then the lights jetted around and moved in a fashion that did not seem natural . I tried to wake my wife to get a second opinion on what I was seeing but she dismissed me and told me to go back to bed . I then began recording video on my phone and tried to get some photos . At first glance of the video footage it just looks like a couple dots of light ,barely Evan able to see, not much to look at but then I zoomed in on video and am convinced that what I was witnessing was other worldly .
