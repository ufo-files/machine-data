3 objects in the space , one that looks like a big spacecraft an 2 smaller crafts

My wife and I were on vacation in Cuba from December 10 to 20

On December 13, we were heading towards the dining room of the hotel.
It was around 6:30 pm.
It was full moon.
I took my cell phone and took 2 pictures of the Moon.
I usually take two pictures in case the first one is not good.
I didn't look at the pictures right away.
It is only the next day when I reviewed all my photos that I saw 
the two pictures and noticed those strange things.
First I want to say that when I took the two photos of the full Moon, 
I din't see that with my eyes. But the lens of the camera caught it.
And this is what I see on the photos taken by my camera.

1. a very  bright form or object in a form of a banana
  in front of the Moon.No idea how close or far it is from the Moon.
  We can see the Moon in the background
  and two objects seeming to be leaving from the big banana
  object and that have left a very very bright tail

2. The second picture shows the two objects already distant from the Moon and the big banana form (that could be a space mother ship ! ). 
Then  I enlarged the two pictures on my computer and we can clearly see what I described. Especially the two objects in the second picture : you can see very precisely the form and the top of the objects

This is not a hoax . I hope there is some sophisticated equipment
somewhere to analyse the two photos
