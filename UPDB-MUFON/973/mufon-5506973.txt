Looked like a star, hovered for hours then sped off and vanished.

My buddy and I were cooking some steaks on the fire outside and we saw a very bright star. A few hours later it started moving suddenly and it disappeared, vanished completely. We were really freaked out but did not think anything of it. And then few moments later, a helicopter passed nearby and another aeroplane flew into the same direction as where the light was to be seen. I took a photograph of it but it is not recognisable and barely visible.
