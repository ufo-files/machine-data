{cak} Was already watching lots of strange lights in sky when saw three large, dim circular lights first in a line then triangle shape then move together then apart. No sound. gliding movement.

I was in my yard doing my nightly sky watching. Watching the regular white light orbs move across the sky, some flashing a bright light, others dim, moving across sky mostly south to north or north to south. Then, noticed something larger in my periphery, what I first thought might of been birds but when I looked directly at the object it was three, dim, circular lights lined up then moved into a triangle or V formation. Then observed these three dim lights move in closer together then again apart in a triangular pattern while what seemed like gliding across the sky, silently. It seemed to be within the atmosphere as it appeared rather large, moving quickly across the sky above the trees. I witnessed it glide heading southwest. I then called my sister, shaking, my hands and knees where shaking, I felt scared as well. She lives in the direction I saw it go towards and my sister has also witnessed strange lights in the sky. She did not see it but did see the other several smaller white orbs. I lost sight of it within a few seconds of seeing it. The further away it was the harder it was to see because of the speed and the dimness of the lights. I was so unprepared to see this that I was not able to record or take any pictures unfortunately.
