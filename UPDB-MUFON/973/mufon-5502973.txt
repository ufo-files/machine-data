Red pulsating star like ufo

I woke up at 4:10am this morning and went to bathroom. When I went back to bedroom, I noticed a red pulsating ball in the sky the size of a basketball. It was flashing it's lights. I thought it was a star maybe Mars, but no I went out to livingroom and went out on patio. It descended to the ground and within minutes it changed direction and went up on a 45* angle heading to the north side of the city. There was no clouds in the sky. I have seen similar things in the sky over a period of a year.
