Lights above us casting different colors to the ground

In the early summer of 1992 during the evening me and two friends were talking in a small grass field between the old BAMC hospital and Gorgas Circle Road. It was dark and clear skies. It was a typical evening with only the lights of the surrounding area illuminating around us. The area where we were standing had no lights and it was dark.

We were talking as friends do when around us we noticed the area lighting up around us. Reflected light was pulsating around us from the ground in different colors like fireworks. We noticed it and started to comment... "What's that?". We were all confused since it was dark and it was quiet. Our natural instinct was to look up because we new at that point it was coming from above. We looked up and I clearly saw what looked like an explosion in the sky above us, but it was after the event. It was not fireworks. There was no sound, it was eerily quiet. All I saw was what looked like the after effects of an explosion. Red and yellow colors (which is what was reflecting from the ground), a dark cloud, and sparks in all directions. It looked very high up and it dissipated very fast. 

I don't know if it was a meteor, UFO, UAP, but it was not fireworks for sure. It was too high up and had no sound.
