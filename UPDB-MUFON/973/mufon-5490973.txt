{rjl} Cigar Shaped UFO spotted with orange and red lights

I was getting into my family's car to go to a restaurant when I spotted the UFO. I was looking at the moon originally, it was the evening, and the daylight sky was just starting to darken. 
I didn't know what it was when I first saw it. It was too skinny to be a blimp, but too slow to be an airplane. Plus, it didn't have any wings.

I asked my dad to look at it since he was an ex-pilot, and he had no idea what it was either. I wanted to go and get my binoculars, but my family was waiting to go to the restaurant, so I decided just to hop into the car. I kept looking at it through the car, but I lost sight of it. The UFO did not make any noises, moved somewhat slowly.
It was cigar shaped with a grey body and orange and red lights all along the cigar in a line. It moved in a straight line. I have pictures, although in the pictures it just looks like a blurry white dot. When in reality it wasn't even white. You can see the moon next to the object. (No, it wasn't Venus. Venus doesn't move)
