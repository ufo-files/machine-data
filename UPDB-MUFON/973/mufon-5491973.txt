{jpg} I was walking home at about nine o' clock when I saw three lights hover over a mountain then simultaneously descended on the other side of the mountain. Landing unknown.

While walking home from a friend's house, I noticed three balls of light with a slight dome shape hovering over a mountain near my house. I stopped in my tracks as I felt nauseous. I watched as the lights slowly descended on the other side of the mountain. This only took seconds and a trail of light followed each ball. That night, I don't remember making it home. I vaguely remember a dream of tall, human like beings standing over me. I don't remember what they did or for how long. The "dream" only lasted a few minutes and I only saw shadows. The next thing I remember is waking up in my bed the next morning. At the time I lived in Arizona and was 14 years old. I have since moved back to my home state of Michigan and have had other incidents. This is simply the first one I remember.
