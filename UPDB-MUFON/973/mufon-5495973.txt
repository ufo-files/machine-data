{cak} Green fireball crossed in front of us while driving west on I-70 near 40 highway exit. Very fast with slight descending trail. No impact noticed.

On January 30th at approximately 3:30 am, we were driving west on I-70 near the 40 highway exit. We had just spent the evening at the hospital with my mother-in-law so there was no alcohol or drugs involved. I noticed it was a clear night and there wasn't anyone else on the interstate. I told my wife, in jest, to keep her eyes out as it was a good night to see a UFO. 

No sooner did I finish my sentence, a bright green glowing object with a tail crossed the highway on a slight decline toward earth. I'm guessing that it was about 40 degrees above the horizon. It was traveling at a very high rate of speed, in fact, so fast that I knew it wasn't a jet or anything like that. With the angle of decent, I expected to see an impact or explosion of light but there wasn't anything. We couldn't even hear anything. It just disappeared behind the trees. 

I was lucky that I had alerted my wife to watch as she normally reads in the car. She witnessed the event as well. I asked her not to tell me what she saw and that we would draw what we saw and then compare. The next morning we drew what we'd seen and they were near identical. We witnessed a bright green glowing ball with a bright green glowing tail. It had a slight curve in the middle and was angled toward earth (or perhaps around the earth). It was the first time either one of us had witnessed such an event. I've seen meteors and falling stars but this was quite unusual.
