On the evening of 5/7/12 at 2106 local while walking my dog.I looked to the East toward the Pierce County Airport. I always look at the sky being an old pilot and aircraft mechanic. The righthand traffic pattern landing to the north goes directly over my home. I observed first one ogange/red object within 2-3 minutes I was looking at four objects. The color being emmitted from the objects were not red collision lights. In fact the only lights I observed were orange/red pulsating, no bright white strobes. The objects appeared in a more or less climbing straight line. Living within 15 miles of Joint FT. Lewis and McCord AB my first thought was that I was watching four helicopters in trail returning to Ft.Lewis. However after watching the objects for 2-3 minutes they didnot fly over my home on the way back to the Fort. The first object continued to assend to my guess would be above 2500-3000 at which time its light blinked out. Within two minutes the second, third, and forth objects did exactly the same.
