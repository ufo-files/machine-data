{kt}Green orb lands

I was heading south on IN-5, was about 10 miles from Huntington . It was during the night, around 10 pm or so. Out of the corner of my eye I notice movement directly north of me in the sky; a green orb descending from the sky, traveling south and parallel with the road.

From the time I noticed it, it only took two or three seconds for the object to descend from the sky and make it to ground-level. The object fly over the house that was on the east side of the road that I happened to be driving by at the time, descend and land not even 10 ft from the back door. It made a distinct, intelligent path, not free-floating and couldnt have been bigger than a golf ball. The house in question was in the middle of a farm field, as if the homeowners rented/bought the property off of the farmer as is common practice around here, and also was only a good 30-40 ft from the road.

The field itself looked as if the farmer didn't use it during the winter because there was tall grass growing sporadically on it. The orb had apparently landed in the midst of a patch of the grass, because the light the orb gave off illuminated grass in close proximity, as if it wasnt reflecting some source of light but glowing in itself. Out of anything in the whole experience thats what stands out in my mind the most. After it landed, it only remained for half a second before it just seemed to disappear.

As a personal note, I found it extremely disturbing how close the object landed to the house (right next to the backdoor of all places), and still get goosebumps every time I recall the incident.
