{jpg} My daughter was trying to take pictures of the blood moon on Jan. 20th in Sutter County. These showed up in her pics.

These pictures came from my daughter who was taking pictures in Sutter County late in the afternoon of the Blood Moon on January 20, 2019. It had been raining and she started pointing her camera to the sky. She did not see these objects until afterward in the photos. They were not visible to the eye but only showed up in her pictures. I was fascinated by these when she sent them to me. I thought it was the Blood Moon starting and the reflections were showing in the sky and the ground. I showed my friend, Nancy, and Nancy directed me to you. She has known of your organization for a long time. I had no clue that these pictures could be something other than the Blood Moon. My daughter and son-in-law wish to remain anonymous and so do I. We would love to know if this was in fact the Blood Moon starting its eclipse or whether my daughter actually caught something with her camera that was not visible to the naked eye.
