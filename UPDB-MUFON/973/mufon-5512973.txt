(GS) Garden State Parkway South Bluish-Green object flew straight up, then stopped for second, then flew forward.

Me, my wife and kids were on our way home from visiting a family member sick in the Mainland Hospital. We were driving on the Garden State Parkway South, when at around exit 40 a bluish-green bright object shot straight up into the air from what seemed like the trees in between the north and south lanes. It hovered for about 1 second before zooming off straight forward what seemed like 1,000mph. I know somebody else out there witnessed this, as there were many cars driving near me.
