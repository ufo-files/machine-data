We had just seen a strange aircraft go by quickly heading west. About a minute later, we saw a bright illuminating light coming toward our direction from the west. It was lower than the stars but definitely much much higher than any plane. it did not travel in a straight line and kept going slower and slower. It would kinda make little zigs and zags here and there, as if a young child were to try to draw a straight line. It seemed like it was at it's brightest point above us, then it picked up speed and was heading south east. Still not going super fast. Then out of nowhere the light got weaker and weaker till gone. It's didn't fly out of sight, it just faded to nothing.
