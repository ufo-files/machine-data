Two large black triangles

At approximately 02.15 am, I was on my local park, Torkington Park in Hazel Grove with my two dogs. I had been taking them out late at night and in the early hours of the mornning to avoid contact with people due to COVID-19. Whilst I was watching them run aound in the snow, I noticed 4 or 5, very bright white lights through the branches of the trees. The lights were in the distance, approximately 3 to 5 miles away and at an altitude of about 3000 to 5000 feet.

What caught my attention was the lights seemed to move in an unconventional manner unlike a helicopter or aircraft and they were moving quite slowly. After gathering my dogs, I quickly walked back home from the park (which was across the road from my house) to get my binoculars. 

After finding my binoculars (Nikon 8 x 42 magnification), I walked to the top of my driveway and I could still see the lights. When I located the lights through the binocluars I saw the following:

1. Three, large, pulsating, flashing, spining, silvery white, very bright lights in the shape of an equalaterial triangle. Inside the main lights, there were also bright blue, bright red and golden copper coloured lights that would flash intermittently at the same time. 

2. The lights were moving very slowly and the lights kept changing shape in to various forms of differing sized triangles.

3. I could not see a physical shape between the lights but I do believe there was one.

4. In front of it was another set of lights that were exactly the same.

After observing the lights for about 3 minutes, I eventually lost them due to them entering a bank of cloud.

This account is accurate and truthful and would have been picked up on radar by Manchester Airport some 6 miles away.

Yours sincerely,

David Moore
