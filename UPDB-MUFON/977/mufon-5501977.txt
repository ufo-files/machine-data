{cak} Caught after thunderstorm. Object seemed to appear out of nowhere and hovered with a blue light emitting from it then took up abruptly.

I was videotaping some lightning during and after a thunder storm just before 2 AM. I had been shooting short videos with my cell phone when I noticed it on my screen and videotaped it. I had no idea what the object was. It seemed to be just hovering on top of a tree about two houses down. I was videotaping from upstairs. I noticed a blueish light emitting from and a few white lights before it took off quickly. I was quite intrigued and it scared me when it took off. I have no idea what it was. My husband and I were stationed in Tinker Air Force Base,Oklahoma when it happened just 6 days before we moved to another base.
