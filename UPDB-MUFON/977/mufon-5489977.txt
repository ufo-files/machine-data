{rjl} Dark camouflage-like winged boomarang shaped object moving north to south.

Object was very discrete like camouflage, but you could make out its winged boomerang shape. Looked like multiple circles (in a line that followed curvature of object) of either extremely dim light or reflection on lower surface across whole craft.

I was in the courtyard watching the sky to the northeast for possible Perseids remnant meteors when I saw the object overhead just to the east. It was traveling north to south. I lost sight of object when I went out front to see if I could still see it.
