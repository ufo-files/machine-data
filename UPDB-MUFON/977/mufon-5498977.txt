{cak} Bright Star like light moving in fast straight line considering extreme distance.

I walked out on my back deck to let the dogs out and was watching the stars like I usually do. It was a very clear night. I was trying to figure out the constellations and noticed a star (or I thought it was a star) that seemed to be moving. I watched it for a bit and it was definitely moving. It was a far, far distance like a star would be from earth. About the same size spec as maybe a star in Orions belt. But it was definitely moving in a straight line slightly SW to slightly NE. I had to move to the corner of my house to keep watching it go. I witnessed it basically from horizon to horizon which it covered in under 30 seconds. As it was beginning to get farther away I noticed it sorta wobbled side to side a little, kinda like a plane would hit turbulence and maybe veer off course slightly then regain the path. Now just FYI, im directly in the flight path of Philly Int airport and its common to see planes fly over every 45 seconds to a minute or so apart on various approaches or flying in other directions such as Newark. I KNOW this was NOT a plane. It was a SOLID bright light no blinking or anything. It was a spec in the sky so I know it was really far up and the distance it covered it had to be going quick. That's it, not much else to say. Maybe someone else noticed it.
