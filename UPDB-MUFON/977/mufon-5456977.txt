{rjl} Blimp/ oval-like lights. Almost completely white light with a hint of yellow. Red blinking light at the bottom center. Aprox. speed 60-120mph.

Just before 8:35pm Pacific Time, I'm driving away from the Facebook building in Menlo Park. I look North East towards the Bay and I spot a bright large blimp/oval like UFO. It was moving interestingly slow for a large object. I decided to park the car and record it. Interesting features include strong white with hint of yellow lights with a red blinking light at the bottom center of the craft. It almost had a bulge on the top center but could not tell very well. The front of the craft had a more rounded curve while the back had a sharper and smaller curve, almost egg-shaped. It took about 8 minutes to move NE to South. That's when I decided to drive towards the freeway and try to spot it again. Unfortunately, it got farther and farther away and after about 40min of driving, I lost it because the trees, buildings, and freeway direction obscured my sight. The initial approximate location under the craft was ~250-500 ft above the Dumbarton Bridge connecting Fremont and East Palo Alto, and last seen heading towards Cupertino which is about 5miles from San Jose.
