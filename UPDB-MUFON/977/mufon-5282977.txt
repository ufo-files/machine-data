Went to roof for meteor shower, turned corner and the UFO was right there above us.

1/4/19 approx. 1 am, my husband and I went to the roof of our building to watch the annual meteor shower. It was freezing out, so we had our coats, hats, gloves, and scarves on, plus blankets and our pillows with us. It was quite bulky and cumbersome. The door faces west, so we went around the side, to set up the gear. I looked towards East, as that is where the sky is open, but I was frozen, as there was a 2-story, 40-50 feet long, pill shaped UFO (Tic-Tac oval but with straight sides, not rounded), with 2 huge oval amber/yellow-orange lights on the bottom hovering RIGHT THERE across the street and slightly to the right, approx. 50-70 feet up. I froze for about 5-10 secs, then without taking my eyes off of it, I started to swing my right arm behind me, trying to swat my hubby while saying "U-fo, U-fo, NO REALLY A UFO LOOK!!!" He wasn't paying me attention at first, because we joke about saying we see a UFO when we see a jet way up. He could tell by my frantic, "NO REALLY..." that I was serious and turned. He dropped everything and took a couple steps to stand by me as we both just stared. As soon as I saw HE saw, it moved ever so slightly. *Just* as I asked if it was my imagination, or did it start to move? When 2 secs later, it was gone. It didn't turn, pivot, pitch, nothing, just sped away absolutely straight toward the East/slightly Southeast. I know this sounds strange, but it felt like it was there for me to see. When I told this to my husband, he said he got the same feeling. Weird. It was a partly cloudy night, fairly low clouds, and the UFO was almost the same altitude as the bottom of the clouds.
