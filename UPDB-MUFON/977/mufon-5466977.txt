Large triangle shaped strobed light formation travelling alongside iss

I was watching lunar eclipse. At 5.25 a.m I observed one white strobe briefly appear then reappear then disappear to the right of the moon. 10 minutes later, in the same spot, the iss appeared. Approximately 2 handwidths apart and slightly ahead and on the starboard side of the iss and travelling at the same speed was a triangle formation of several red strobe lights much larger than the iss. I couldn't tell if the lights were the perimeter of a large craft or individual objects in formation.
