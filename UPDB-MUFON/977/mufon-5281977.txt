I was travelling north on gravel road,a metallic cigar shape travelling west and turned sharply to travel towards me along Rr254

Sun setting in West,I was travelling north on RR254 - gravel road-off HWY 13 south of Wetaskiwin ,Alberta Canada.
I could see a metalic object travelling west following Township Road 470 gravel at about telephone pole height . I couldn't identify it .I thought because the setting sun was glinting so brightly off of it. thought it was a helicopter because it was slower then a plane and travelling so low and slow. About 500 feet from the intersection of 254 and 470,the object suddenly turned south and began travelling towards me. the turn told me it was nothing I could identify....nothing I know turns that sharp without having to correct- ie tail of helicopter would swing out as it turned.
By now I had gone from driving very slowly to stopping in middle of road. The object flew towards me and even as it got closer,I couldn't identify it. it was so bright. I could only guess it was shaped like a cigar about size of a car but it's edges weren't defined- too bright.
I opened my windows as it travelled closer to see if I could identify by sound.it MADE NO SOUND. It travelled over my car and I looked out back window to see it go past ....but it never emerged back into sight. I got very scared then!!! I wanted to open my sun roof but was scared. I felt for sure it was still there hovering over me! I quickly accelerated ,drove like a crazy person all the way to my acerage ,scared it was still hovering as I sped home .
got to my driveway - cracked the sun roof before I got out of car but it was gone . whew!
