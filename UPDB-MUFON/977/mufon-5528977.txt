Seen swirling object dancing around in the sky, had a rainbow color effect on it, then it was followed by round red glowing orb like objects.

Driving down the north east extension of the PA turnpike, going south, coming out of the tunnel, i was looking at the sky, I like star gazing, but it was kind of cloudy, on my left I look and see this swirling rainbow color object dancing around in the sky, going in crazy directions, just swirling around, , then it disappeared and then these small red round objects started darting around, coming together, then separating again, then seemed to form an outline of a triangle. I found the rainbow object to be quit beautiful, i wasn't afraid at all. I took a few pictures with my cell phone as i was driving, but couldn't keep taking my eyes off the road. They were still there as i drove past the area that they were in.
