{jpg} A streak of lights moving northeasterly, possibly a squadron (?) of ships??

I was sitting in my back yard, talking with a friend on the phone. I stood up and looked to the south to notice several strands of light streaking Northeasterly. The phenomenon indicated 10-15 strands of light at different levels. When I first noticed it, I thought it was some sort of firework, but fireworks travel vertical arc outward from the center, and fall back down to the ground. This phenomenon occurred horizontally/laterally. The apparent fleet of starships traveled quickly from southwest to northeast in my line of sight.
