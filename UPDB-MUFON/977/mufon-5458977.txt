Two lights one large one small (star like) moving towards each other and then fading out

A large light with fuzzy edges and a dark center was moving from N to S-SE. After 5 seconds, another starlike light started moving from SE towards the larger light. The smaller light flashed brightly for a short duration (shorter than a second) and as the larger light was approaching they both faded out and disappeared.

The ISS usually passes the region, however at that time, it was over the Pacific Ocean.
