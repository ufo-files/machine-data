(GS) Red/Orange glowing orbs

Out on balcony having a cigarette,looked to the left and saw a steady red ball of light. Looked like a golf ball on fire kind of. Mostly red, but changed to orangish and even lighter almost whitish color. Didnt blink, just was like a burning ember. Watched it fly in straight path over top of my apt. building. Went inside and put pics on computer. Went back out on balcony and saw a second one going in and from same direction. What the heck they are I do not know!Video and pics not great, but these were pretty fast and I was pretty excited.
