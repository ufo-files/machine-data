{cak} Three silver orbs flying together with orange flash discharge.

My fiance and I were sitting in a hammock in the back yard. What we saw were three orbs following a straight path from East to West. The altitude that it was at made it look hazy like the Jets that flew over earlier. I tried to snap a picture of it. I tried to get video of it and when it was directly overhead there was an orange flash like a 4th light came on that was much smaller than the other 3. Immediately the sound on my video cuts out. I got up from the hammock to regain site of it, but it disappeared.
