However, as the large bright orange object approached, fairly low in the sky, I noticed that it did not have the port and starboard flashing lights of a regular airplane flying at night. The UFO looked fairly large and was moving at a low altitude, probably approximately the same height as an aircraft that had just taken off from SFO airport and was now flying at a height of approx 1 mile. The other thing that I noticed was that the UFO was moving in straight line, but fairly slowly in the sky, much slower than a regular aircraft. As the UFO approached, I noticed that it then began to zig zag across a straight line the sky, at a controlled rate of speed, making abrupt turns.
