{rjl} A fireball streaked East to West in the Southern sky from my vantage point.

I was outside, taking a break from work at [address removed/cms/rjl] in Syosset, NY. There were clear skies, it was 3PM. I saw the object come into view over a building to my left (East) and it looked like a white object, leaving a trail of sparkling dust, moving East to West across the Southern sky from my vantage point. I immediately thought it was a meteor. It was approximately 10-20 degrees up from the horizon. It traveled slightly downward about 3 degrees over the length of its path.  As it traveled, it fluttered, then became sliver, then a very intense blue, then a bright green, then it fluttered again, turned white, then fizzled out. The trail it was leaving was illuminated approximately 3 seconds behind the fireball itself. The degrees of sky from 0-360 (relative bearing) were a total of about ten to twenty degrees across. From the time I first saw it, until the time it fizzled out and the trail disappeared, was about 5 seconds. There was no noise. No explosion. There was no way to determine how far away it was. It flew in a relatively straight line with a slight downward arc.

I noticed it because it was bright, it was moving and came into my direct field of vision. I immediately felt elated that I had seen something so beautiful. Then I had a dreadful thought that it could've been an aircraft. That thought quickly passed because that didn't fit the scene that I had just witnessed.

I tried to get one of my co-workers to turn around and see it, but he didn't get turned around in time.
