(GS) Triangular craft traveling north

Me and my girlfriend observed a triangular craft traveling north at a very high rate of speed. It disappeared from view in a matter of less than 10 seconds. It was a structural craft that was brightly lit. We were outside looking at the North Star when it flew by. It was the first that she had seen and the third for me.
