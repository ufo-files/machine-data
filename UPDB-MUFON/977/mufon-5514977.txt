{cak} White to Orange shaped triangle hovering and behaving strangely

While out doing astophotography we observed a slow silent golf-ball sized object, which appeared to be an orange-white triangle of lights (opaque surface) traveling on a E to SE trajectory. After passing us to the south the object headed more to the east and began to descend behind the horizon. Sighting lasted no more than 1-2 minutes before disappearing.
