{ck} flying object in the air straight in front of us.

My wife and son were driving home from vacation on Main Street in Eaton, OH when I noticed a flying object in the air straight in front of us. I got one of feelings that tells you that the object doesnt look familiar and you instantly start feeling like youre witnessing a UFO. Well it made it all more real when the object flew out of sight behind a small group of trees and when we passed the group of trees the object was gone. I thought I was crazy until my wife also started asking where it had gone. I slowed down enough to scan the whole sky and we found nothing. The skies were clear and there was no possible way a airplane could have vanished that quickly after leaving our vision behind a group of trees.
