{cak} cloud like orb. disappeared shortly after seeing.

Laying in the grass. Looking at stars, etc. Few clouds not many. I look to the tree line to my left. Towards an abandon warehouse/factory might be. Above the trees and to the right I see an orb like, cloud moving at rapid speeds in opposite direction of other clouds that might have been around. I was frozen. My friend said panicked "Do you see that?!". The UFO disappears in the middle of the sky (almost directly above the abandoned building). My other friend did not see it but got scared by our reactions. We were all shaken by the sighting.
