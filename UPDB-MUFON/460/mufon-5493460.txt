Whilst watching British TV series broadchurch episode 4 of series 1, which was filmed at west bay Dorset uk, we noticed in the blue sky with some clouds above David Tennant and Olivia Colman who were walking up the beach bank (about 5 min in)there was an object out at sea hovering, it was very bright white oval shape it changed from right to left very fast, hovered then shot off at incredible speed through the clouds and then up in an arc out off of the screen shot but had changed to a dark colour and longer shape before disappearing, if you watch this clip from the series and pause and slow frame it is very clear. I have looked up when this was filmed and started in 2013, so I checked if there were ant sightings around this year in Dorset and there were 2, so there is plenty for you to investigate please.
