{cak} Jets escort object over Saint Stephen, SC

Here is what I uploaded to You Tube. When I set the date upon putting the battery into my video equipment, I used March. I should have used April. The time, and day, though, are correct and true. The actual video was recorded on April, 9, 2014.

The real interesting point of the video happens from about one minute, ten seconds into the video till about the 4 minute mark. That is when extra curricular movement was noticed around the object, be it jets or orbs or whatever.

I have a free app called Sputnik that tells me when the ISS Space Station and Iridium Satellites will be visible in the skies above our home. My wife and I went out to see the ISS Space Station on the night of April 9, 2014. We were looking at the Space Station to our northwest when we noticed an orange light coming in from the northeast. My wife ran to get our Night Vision video equipment. It is a monocular called the Newton Hornet and it multiplies by 4. We recorded the event at that point. At no time did we ever video the ISS. We videoed the object that was coming in from the northeast. The object was visible to the naked eye. Whatever was escorting the object was not visible to the naked eye. We heard military jets so we don't know if any of the sound was being caused by the object. The object was basically circling in a large area while being escorted by, at times, one, two or three craft. One craft could be seen falling away right after the objects passed behind a telephone pole close to our proximity at the 2:35 mark of the video. The large object was orange. The jets or escorts were white and small. I filmed the object until my back and legs got tired and I went inside with the object still up in the sky. 

The sun was down. The sky was dark and clear. Light wind.
