{cak} Large object at least 3/4 of a mile long. Hovering over nuclear power plant.

We noticed a large object hovering over the nuclear power plant that Is adjacent to our home across the bay. It was station Ary for at least an hour. Completely surrounded by storm clouds that were emitting lighting with no sound. There were no other storm clouds in the sky at the time. You could clearly see what appeared to be movement in the form of a trail coming from the object down to the plant and vice versa. After about 15 minutes what appeared to be drone planes started canvassing the area. 30 minutes later the cloud like object appeared to fold into itself and dissipated. While observing this object you could clearly see the clouds rolling over the edges of the object. This whole event made zero noise and also did not move during the course of the hour.
