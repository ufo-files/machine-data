{jpg} Bright pulsating triangle to spherical brighter and bigger. Bright arms. shrink and disappear.

I was in my bedroom getting ready to turn in for the night, the curtains were open and I could see out the window. I noticed in the sky a bright triangular shape that glowed red, green and yellow. It was maybe 10:45 pm, I called my wife to come and see. We watched the object change into a spherical shape with red arms coming out from it, mainly yellow, with hints of red and green. It was even brighter as a sphere. It would pulse light intensity and got bigger and brighter. Then it dimmed and shrank until it disappeared. The object had been hovering in the sky. We watched it for about 1/2 hr.
