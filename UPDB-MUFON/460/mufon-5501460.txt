{dr}Extremely bright green light like stoplight green but brighter

Was on my way to my storage and saw a bright green light reflecting off the hood of my truck. Looked up in the sky and saw a bright green diamond/round pulsating light moving very rapidly from the SE heading NW. It made me miss a shift and i looked away for a couple of seconds. I came to a clearing where there is an elementary school and stopped. The light had changed direction to directly south best as i could tell. I did not see it change direction as i had to look where i was shifting, but it seemed to have increased speed and faded from sight rapidly. There was absolutely no sound and the sky was clear. At first i thought it may have been a meteorite but they don't change direction at such a sharp angle as i try to catch meteor shows when they are forecast to appear. I have never seen one change direction however, other than a very slight curve. I tried to catch it on my cell phone camera but strangely, the battery had died, and it had almost disappeared from sight anyways.
