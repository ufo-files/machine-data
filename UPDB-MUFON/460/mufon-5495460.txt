{cak} Saw a white orb above a skyscraper downtown.

My cousin and I left my apartment around 9:15 pm on 3-15-15 we were going out to eat. I looked up and noticed a bright white orb hovering above a small skyscraper and got my cousins attention. At first we thought helicopter but no aviation lights and it did not move. We then saw a high flying airplane going by it but at a much higher altitude and they were not even close to the same let alone no airplanes just hover. We then thought maybe it was a tower on the top of the skyscraper with the lights for low flying traffic. So I got out my digital camera and my cousin got out his Apple I 6 plus and we zoomed in on it. It was not a tower as nothing was attached to it. He took a pic of it and I got a nice zoomed in shot of it. We then moved across the alley to an open lot with a clearer view of the object and he took more photos and I shot a few more videos of it. After 15 minutes we noticed it was actually moving but very slowly across the sky. We then left. After we got home we uploaded our pictures and videos and looked at them closer. The weird thing was 2 of his shots had a different craft in it along with the orb that we did not see and did not show up on my video. It was different than the white orb. It was 2 yellow orbs together. Now the first sighting was NW of the parking lot outside of my building and it's slow path was going N.
