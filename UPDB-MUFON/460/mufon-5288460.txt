Bright white oval shaped light

Diving Southbound on the M1 motorway 5 am ish.
On approaching j26 I spotted to my left and forward a bright white oval light at around 1000 ft above the city of Nottingham that was stationary and as I live local know of nothing in Nottingham it could be.
After losing sight due to the tree line alongside the motorway it wasn't until I approached j25 that it reappeared and in the same position which I observed for the best part of a minute before losing sight again due to the surrounding area.
Having passed j25 to the right of me and in the distance I could see a similar object which I calculated to be over or near Donington Park and East Midlands Airport which I presumed to be a plane when a plane actually took off and crossed the motorway approx 2m from my position that could be clearly seen to be a plane as daybreak was starting.
We have a lot of air traffic over where I live and can recognise most especially the police helicopter which this wasn't !
