{rjl} 2 sets of orange orbs in triangles, one set in sqare

On way to store, saw first set of glowing orange orbs in shape of a triangle. Just to north of it was another set in a triangle , then to north of it was a square-they all 3 were in a row. I stopped in road and asked another person in car if they saw, and they said yes. They thought it was hot air balloons. but they were in perfect formation. I drove about 30 seconds to turn car around and they had disappeared. Just gone! I am pretty shook up by this. Have called Festus police and radio station and neither had reports of this sighting!
