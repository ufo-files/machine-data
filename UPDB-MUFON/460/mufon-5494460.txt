{dr}Saturn-like sphere hovered for several minutes with many little lights that came out of it and zipped all over the sky, then went back into sphere and it ascended rapidly and disappeared

In approx. 1975, maybe in the summertime, around 7:30 or 8:00 pm, traveling on Hwy 53 near Glen Ellyn in Illinois, I don't remember what direction we were heading, my husband at that time and I spotted something that looked like Saturn.  It was a large, darkish metallic colored sphere with a luminous ring around it. We saw others pulled over to the side of the road gazing at this. There must have been about 15 other people standing there. We pulled over as well and got out of the car and just watched it.  It stayed stationary for several minutes and then suddenly all of these little lights that looked like stars came out of the sphere and started darting all over the sky. We were all amazed at this.  It went on like this for about 10 minutes and suddenly they all returned to the large sphere and the sphere ascended and zipped away and it was gone.  A newspaper Article was written regarding the incident. It was apparently observed by hundreds of people. The Article mentioned that the military bases around the area had no activity that night.  I remember seeing the news Article in the Chicago Sun Times.  I had tracked down the Article at one time, but never purchased the archive. I have always been surprised that this incident has never been mentioned on any of the UFO programs on TV.
