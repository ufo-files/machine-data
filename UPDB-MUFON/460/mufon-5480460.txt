{nd} I am originally from Indianapolis. Sometime in the late 60s my brother & I, along w/half of Indianapolis, sited a UFO which subsequently buried itself in a water filled gravel pit on the (E) side of Indy. Soldiers from Ft. Benjamin Harrison cordone

Object seen by witnesses in the north east section of Marion County near Geist reservoir in the summer of 1965 (date unknown). The witnesses were a girl about sixteen and her brother who were driving in the area. A glowing triangular silver object larger than a car maneuvered over the witnesses and then plunged into a nearby pond. Shortly afterward soldiers believed to be from nearby Fort Harrison appeared and cordoned off the area. 

Description given to FI 10/4/2016
