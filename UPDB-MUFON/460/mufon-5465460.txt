{jpg} Stationary white circular object high in the sky

We had just finished an early supper at Outback Restaurant in North Myrtle Beach with our Little League baseball team and parents around 5:30pm. We were saying goodbye in the parking lot when one of the players asked me "What's that?" pointing to the sky. We all looked up and observed a white circular object sitting stationary high in the sky. I first thought it may have been a group of balloons but the object never moved. It was a bright white color and stood out against the clear blue sky. A few clouds went by but the object remained stationary. This was definitely not an early evening star (planet) as it did not "shine" like a light but was just a bright white color. I zoomed in the best I could with my cell phone and took a picture. I'm sure some clarity is lost in the zoom but it is clearly something unusual.
