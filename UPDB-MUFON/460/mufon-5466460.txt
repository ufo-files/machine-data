{rjl} Object was round, flat, had visible structures of alternating, pattered colors.

While trimming a tree in yard, object appeared directly overhead. I only observed the bottom of the object. It was a disc and had visible structures of concentric rings and spokes coming from the center. The rings and spokes formed segments that were colored gold, silver, and a luminous yellow-like quality that I find especially difficult to describe. It appeared below the clouds and appeared to be quite large, though I had no visual reference with which to compare it. It appeared suddenly, made no sound, made no movements, and disappeared just as suddenly after about 5 seconds.

For being so anomalous, my reaction to seeing the object was one of brief puzzlement. I felt no fear or shock. I simply returned to my chore. I thought about it as I worked and considered it a hallucination of some sort. However, I have never hallucinated before and the object had such a quality of being "present" that I found it hard to categorize it as a hallucination. I said nothing of it to anyone and simply thought about it for some time before deciding to make this report.
