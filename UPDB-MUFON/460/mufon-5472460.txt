ON CRUISE EARLY AM, BRIGHT SHOOTING STARLIKE BODY FLEW INTO ORION THEN COMPLETELY STOPPED AND THEN SHOT OFF AT 451 ANGLE NORTH

I WAS ON THE CARNIVAL MAGIC CRUISE SHIP ON THE MORNING OF 11/5 WE WERE HEADING TOWARD AMBER COVE DR, BUT WERE STILL OUT TO SEA HEADING SOUTHEAST. I WOKE EAARLY AND WAS SITTING ON THE BALCONY OF OUR ROOM. IT WAS VERY CLEAR. I FIRST SAW 2 SMALL SHOOTING STARTS COME FROM THE SOUTH AND BURNED OUT JUST PASSED ORION. SHORTLY AFTER THAT A BRIGHTER SHOOTING START CAME FROM OVERHEAD FLYING EASTERLY IN SAME DIRECTION OF THE OTHERS, BUT THEN IT STOPPED FOR 1 TO 2 SECONDS AND THEN IT SHOT OFF NORTHERLY. I SAW IT FOR SEVERAL MORE SECONDS UNTIL IT WAS GONE FROM VIEW. IT WAS TOO HIGH TO BE A HELICOPTER, IT WAS SOLID WHITE AND VERY HIGH. IT WAS NOT A SATELLITE AS IT WAS MOVING FAR TO FAST.
