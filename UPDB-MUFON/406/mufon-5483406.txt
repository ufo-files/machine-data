{rjl} It just floated over us at 5mph with all these multi-colored lights

We were driving from Bradford up to REW when we noticed this lighted object off to our right. We then drove up to the summit and made a right turn and proceeded approx. 500ft.We then exited the truck to observe the object. Myself and two companions watched object slowly pass overhead until it disappeared over the horizon. Another vehicle with two or three passengers also stopped to witness object. I believe we were all rather awestruck and bewildered. We didn't discuss it much to my recollection except to ponder what it could have been. I don't recall anything ever in the news. None of us ever reported it. I have thought about it very often since it occurred all those years ago. It was nothing like anything I have ever seen before or since. I just wish I knew what we observed that night. I sincerely believe it was extraterrestrial.
