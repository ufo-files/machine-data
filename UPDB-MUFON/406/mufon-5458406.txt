{rjl} Metallic Reflective Surface Sphere over Lake Tulloch

On my way to work in Jamestown CA I was driving around Lake Tulloch in Copperopolis CA on Obyrnes Ferry Rd headed to Hwy 108/120. as I was crossing the bridge, I looked to my right and was surprised to see a large, reflective, silver sphere up in the sky hovering over the lake. The glare off the surface was so bright that it could not have been a balloon or a plane. There were no wings on the craft and it made no noise. As I approached the “hair pin” curve it moved across the sky and disappeared.
