While crossing field object appeared overhead in next field. While still crossing field shown itself as silver disc and emitted white light beams .

While crossing a field on my way to work to catch a bus, I saw extremely white light over houses and trees. I first thought it was a piper cub, as they do cross these fields before landing. I was in field number one when the lights were overhead in field number two and the two fields were separated by a road. The light never moved and stayed in the same position overhead in field two. I then realized that it was not a plane. I kept crossing the field and the lights on both sides retracted. The lights were beaming the most intense white light ( this was in 1975 before led lights) it showed itself as a cigar shaped disc and then put out the lights again. When the lights were on they were so white that you could not see the disc. I crossed the first field and road, the object was still there. I had made a decision that I was going to approach it and it and then it retracted all the lights, showed itself as an intense silver disc and vanished. When I got to work I called my mother and told her what I had seen. ( this was before cell phones) She called the airport to see if anything was reported and they said no. To this day I will never forget this and have told a few people who might not think I was nuts. My curiosity got me, as I was determined to approach it.
