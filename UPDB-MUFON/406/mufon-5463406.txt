{cak} 5 red lights Big Dipper fixed formation for 2 minutes, I have video.

Was walking home from neighbors house last night (01/06/19) at 615PM-Arizona time.

5 very bright red lights (stars?) suddenly appeared in the cloudy sky in the shape of the Big Dipper. No other stars were in the night sky due to cloud cover. The lights pulsated, but were abundantly 1other1 than anything I had seen in the sky before. They stayed in place, and shined bright.

I recorded the event for about 30 seconds (iPhone XS Max), then ran to get my family (witnesses) to observe the surreal event. My wife came outside with me first, and the 5 red lights had reduced to 1 red light, and ten seconds later, there was nothing.
