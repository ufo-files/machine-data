No bird dives like that...

Looking south from residence toward the intersection of S. Simms and Belleview; small metallic disk approximately 1000 ft agl performed a counter-clockwise turn from east to west (180 degrees in my direction) approximately 5 seconds in duration then invisible; 15-30 seconds later in same spot, a black object flying like a bird appeared and performed roughly the same manuver but then dove, straight down until at maximum visibility I could see the object was not a bird but rather a sold, dark colored object in the shape of downward facing horse head; approximate size; 3-5; peace among the peoples of Earth in our age of ascension...
