Black rectangular object with blinking lights

I was sitting on my balcony relaxing, I normally view helicopters fly by every once in a while, from the tourist helicopters to police/military helicopters... This night as I was sitting on my balcony, I noticed a black rectangular object hovering above the horizon with blinking illuminating lights on each corner of the black rectangular object. The blinking lights seemed to be pulsating sequence, almost like a heart pulse. The object hovered for a moment, then began to from SW to W slowly, too slow to be an airplane. The object then stopped for a few seconds, go backwards slowly, stopped again, and then moved W until it went passed my line of sight. Throughout the entire time the white pulsating lights on each corner, continued to pulsate the entire time.I have attended many Air Shows, I have seen many military aircraft, my uncle served in the military, and I am a aviation and military aviation enthusiast. I have never seen anything like this in my life!
