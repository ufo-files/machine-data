{nd} Noticed a blueish white orb in photos only after they were taken.

I was working on construction site, and I snapped two pictures south across the Eisenhower Expressway at Laramie Avenue just as a storm was passing and the sun was coming out from behind the clouds. I looked at the pictures later, and saw a blueish white orb in both pictures in different positions in the sky. The time stamp on the photos says they were taken 7 seconds apart. This is the only time I have seen anything like this in many sky pictures I have taken.
