(GS) Orange glowing X like UFOs spotted off 71 Hwy in Belton MO

I was driving my son to his fathers house while he was looking out the window to watch some of the fireworks that people were shooting off. Most of them were only just above the tree line and he was disappointed that we couldnt go over and see them closer. All of a sudden he yells out that he sees a UFO. Of course, as a parent I count it to a childs over active imagination and tell him its probably just a cool firework. He stands his ground and contines telling me it wasnt fireworks. All of a sudden he yells out again that another one has joined the first one and they are flying in the sky. Because Im driving I cant look out the window to where he is pointing and still think its probably fireworks. The next day I check the reports of the last couple days and notice there is one from a man in Belton that sees exactly what my son was describing. Pretty fascinated that he actually did see a UFO I contact KS MUFON who urges me to make a report on it. I think its pretty cool that after all the sightings I have had my son has had one himself now.
