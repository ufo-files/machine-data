{dr}I saw a saucer with disco lights hovering, when I called my friend, it took off, like they heard me.

I was about 10 years old, it was summer time, just got dark and I was playing outside at with my friend. I stopped and looked up and saw a saucer shaped object floating in the sky, about 2 blocks away and about 50 feet above the tree line. The only easy way to describe the lights would be like disco lights flashing. I felt my body turn white with shock, and as soon as I called my friend Craig to come and see, it took off. Later that night when I was almost asleep, I woke up to a low rumble going over my house. It took almost 30 seconds for it to pass and then it was over.
