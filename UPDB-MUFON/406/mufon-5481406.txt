{rjl} Saucer hovering over small cloud. White some top and black bottom. Two witnesses involved.

On the way back home from an appointment, I spotted an object over a small dark cloud. Pointing to it, I asked my mom, "What is that?" She saw it in the sky as well, and we both freaked out a little bit. It was saucer shaped, with a silver body, white dome top, and black bottom. It was far away and up high so it seemed small from our point of view, but it was easily in our vision. It simply stayed above the cloud, not moving. It was a very quick event, no longer than 10 seconds before we lost sight of it. We were driving and trees blocked our view very soon after we saw it, and when the trees cleared, I pulled my phone out to take a picture, but it was absolutely nowhere to be seen. The object completely disappeared from the time we lost it behind the trees to when we could have seen it again- we couldn't find it at all- we even slowed to see if we were just going too fast go see it. It was just gone.
