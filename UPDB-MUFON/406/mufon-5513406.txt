{dr}Thought it was a star because it was hovering near a real star then it took off.

It is new years eve, I was standing on my front porch awaiting family coming over for a BBQ and I was looking up at the sky and noticed what looked like 2 stars directly above my porch. About 2 minutes went by and looked back up and the second "star" was moving slowly across the sky. I ran for my Android phone and recorded it as it moved from the west towards the east. The attached video is sideways and not the best of quality because it is on my phone, but if you turn it to the right, you may be able to view it better. You will see the actual star just sitting still and the "Unidentified flying object" moving slowly away. There are no flashing lights or sound. The sounds in the background are children in my neighborhood playing with fireworks as it is New years eve. It just slowly continued until no longer seen towards the South East past the tree line where I live. I live near an airport and this was no plane.
