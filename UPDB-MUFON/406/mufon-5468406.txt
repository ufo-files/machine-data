{rjl} Pulsated down River St.towards Lake Superior

When I was 13-14 2 friends & I were hangin out on the River street in Ontonagon,Mi the summer 0f 63-64 around 3-3:30 a.m.. Town was quiet no one around and we were sitting on the pool hall steps,when we heard a pulsating sound. We looked up & a rectangular shaped object was following the main street and heading towards Lake Superior. It appeared to be at least 50-75 yards in length and was about 100' or so above us. We ran down the street almost below it and it went right out of town over Lake Superior,we watched it till its lites faded out over the lake Like I said it made a pulsating/swish sound,it was great-wish I could see that again.
