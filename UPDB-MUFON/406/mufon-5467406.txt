{jpg} There were 4 of us present we observed the lights for what felt like 5 minutes but we lost 3 hours

My friend and I had gone for a walk with 2 other kids and as we were walking back to her house we saw a large round band of lights. At first I tried to rationalize it at some sort of reflection it was quickly pointed out there was nothing to reflect. We stood in the road staring for what felt like just a few minutes and then the lights were just gone and it was like coming out of a daze but when we returned into the house we had lost 3 hours with no recollection of the time. 13 years later I had another experience in the home I was living in at that time for approximately one month I would wake up every night convinced something was burning upon inspection nothing was burning.
