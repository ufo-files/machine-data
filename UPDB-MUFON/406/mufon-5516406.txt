{cak} Glowing orbs while watching the persid meteor shower

I was wondering if you guys have heard of or could look into the glowing orbs that were flying over head in Salem Oregon out of the north heading south last night around 10:30pm? We have five people that were out side watching the meteor shower and then one orb came up out of the north glowing orange and flew over over us heading south. It made no sounds at all and was flying faster than a jet. Then a little later four more came up over the northern horizon flying south overhead. One orb was red and the others were orange. And they faded out. as they were over head heading south. One looked like there was a clear bag like thing over it with a dim red light in it. I looked at them with binoculars and I couldnt tell what they were. They were too low for satellites. Ive never seen anything like that in all my life. Was wondering what they were? I looked around and other people saw them too. But no one knows what they were. Ive seen weather balloons before but these didn't look like them. And there was no wind at all and it was a clear night.
