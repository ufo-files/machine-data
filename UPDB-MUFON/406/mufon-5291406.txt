V shaped tightly packed cluster of 50 to 100 objects

Observed a tightly packed cluster of 50 to 100 dimly lit (possibly sunlit) objects flying at a steady course and speed in a V shaped formation to the southeast at 7:25pm cst. Cluster crossed the entire sky in about two minutes, was about two full moons wide, and appeared to be at a very high altitude. Cluster was too dim to catch on cellphone video.
