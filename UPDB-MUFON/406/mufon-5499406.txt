{nd} Three lights flying faster than jets through the sky very close together for a long distance.

Walking home from a friends place, not intoxicated. First noticed the object moving faster than anything man made in the sky. I knew the object could not have been a shooting star or meteor as there were three perfect lights aligned moving in synchronicity very fast across the sky, under Orion's Belt, north to south. Three lights looked like they could be space craft moving together at fast speed through the nights sky. I instantly had years in my eyes as I was by myself and with a dead phone, I was at a loss for words, knowing that this was something I had never seen before as someone who has spent hours looking at the sky. The three lights eventually got too far to the south I lost view.
