{cak} Bright light flashed from stationary object, satellite? The another flash with sudden movement lasted about one second

On the evening of May 31, 2013, around 2215hrs, was looking straight up in sky at the Big Dipper. Observed 2 satellites moving from s/w to n/e, several minutes apart in same flight path through handle of the Big Dipper. At 2234hrs noticed a bright light turn on then off. Noticed a faint pin point of light stationary from point of bright light. Shortly afterwards observed an aircraft moving from mostly west to east and it crossed between last to handle stars before ladle of dipper. After passing through the stars a sudden bright light came on and moved west a hi speeds, turned off then the light came on, still moving west, then disappeared. This lasted less than a second. Was this some type of laser experiment?
