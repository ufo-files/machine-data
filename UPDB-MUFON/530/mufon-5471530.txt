Red globe, pulsating at ~2Hz, no other lights

I was in my car, spotted the globe at my left, the object seemed to hover at an altitude of ~250m, I tried to get closed turning to a side road, leaving the object at my right. Getting closer the object appeared to move in NW direction, considering my speed (~90km/h) the object got easily farther, I can guess a speed greater than 500km/h. After 5 minutes following the object (no change in color, only red visible, pulsating frequency maintained at ~2hz during the whole time, no change in direction) the object got too distant to be clearly visible.
