{jd} The craziest thing happened while I was driving home, when I didnt have any choice but to stop and look at the pulsating lights infront of me.

At approxamitly 2:30 in the morning as I was leaving work and heading home, I was driving up 15th street towards conneticut from rangeline. I noticed a light pretty far away in the sky, and not thinking much of it kept driving home. I figured it must be an airplane or helicopter. The object got closer and closer in an almost unrealistic amount of time. Within three minutes the object was already only about 200 feet in front of me. It was tear drop shaped and has multicolored pulsating lights, with a gray/ or silver body type. They windows revealed lights on the inside but I saw nothing walking around. My car started acting odd and the speakers turned all the way up and killed the battery. As I went to grab my phone to take a picture it was dead. I was terrified. As the object hovered for another thirty second I finally saw some sort of apparition inside the object, but nothing well enough to actually determine what it was. Im not sure what the object was but I was relieved when It had finally gone past me. My car came back on by itself like nothing had happened. There was a brief moment inbetween my car starting and the object flying away for 2 minutes that I dont remember anything, and the time seemed to have just dissapeared. After the object started to dissapear I noticed a military style airplane flying in the same direction as if it was looking for the object. Im not sure what was going on, or what exactly I saw but It was the most terrifying moment in my life.
