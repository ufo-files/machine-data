I'm having a hard time believing this just happened... 

I just got woken up by a very bright light outside. For a moment I thought it was a perimeter light on the neighbor's house behind the treeline, but then realized it would have been much too high up and way too bright. The moon was still up and it was as bright; the moon was a bluish white, but the other light was a yellow/orangish.

I went to the window to see what it was; for a few moments I thought it was a reflection but then realized no, it was actually a light source of its own. It dimmed a bit, seemed to take on a more defined orb-like shape, and started to move south, towards the treeline. 

I had the sense to get my phone. I got this pic of it... It's the light in the center. While I was messing with the settings, trying to get it in a longer exposure mode, it went behind the treeline and kept moving southwest. I wish I had snapped it a moment sooner, because again it was so bright, it woke me up.
