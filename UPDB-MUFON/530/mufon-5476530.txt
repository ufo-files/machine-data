At the time which was between 12 and 2 I was at the selah little league fields with a girl which is located on mcgonagle rd off of gore in selah Washington. At first it looked like a really bright star but it kept getting bigger and brighter at first it was one light as it got closer it was 3. At first I figured it was an unknown vehicle as there is a military base in our town. It's like it knew I was there and could see it. I couldn't speak I was shocked although I felt joy afterwards and no fear. When it seemed the craft noticed me it moved diagonally and very smooth behind a nearby cloud. It was so quick smooth the way it moved I'm not sure how it was possible to be so powerful yet so quiet. No beam was emitted it really seemed like more of a test drive the way it avoided me. My spirituality durastically changed from Christian to agnostic soon afterward. I was so happy to feel like there was other life out there but I'm not ruling out our own technology. I lost sight of the object behind a cloud and did not see it shoot up or come out of the clouds. The undocumented flying object was triangular shaped with 3 lights inside each corner and descended right above me . Although at first the light was in the middle.
