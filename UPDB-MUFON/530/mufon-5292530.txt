Ketchum, ID UFO

I was riding in the passenger seat of a box truck with two co-workers when the driver and I saw what looked like a bluish green comet streak through the sky. It didn't disappear like a normal shooting star, it stopped and hovered for about 10 minutes. I took out my iphone and zoomed in to see if I could make out a shape and it seemed to be a circular halo with a smaller circle inside.
