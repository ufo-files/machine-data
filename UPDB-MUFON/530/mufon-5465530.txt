I was getting ready to leave my mother in-laws house when I looked up in the sky and noticed a star like object moving higher and faster than a plane from east to west. I observed the object for about one minute before the light faded out. There were no clouds at the time because there were stars out that night.I was excited to see the object because last October witnessed a simular object moving from east to west then south to north. Myself and 3 other people witnessed both events.
