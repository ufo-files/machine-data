{dr}Driving on a country road. Dark outside. Saw a V shaped object in sky with red blinking light. About 1 hour later saw same object over more populated highway.

I was driving on state route 895 near the Vraj Temple at about 6 am. The sky was still completely dark. I looked up and to my right through my front windshield and saw 2 blinking red lights. I assumed it was just an airplane at first, but as I got closer I realized it was lower than a plane should be and also that it was not moving - it was hovering. I could make out that the object was either V shaped or diamond shaped, but seemed to be more V shaped. My destination was only about a mile away from there. When I got out of my truck I looked in the sky and couldn't see anything other than planes. Roughly 1 hour later I was driving on Interstate 78 and saw the same object, but this time it was much higher in the sky, but again, it hovered and did not move in the sky. Other people had to have also seen this, as I-78 had a lot of cars on it. The second sighting was roughly 8 to 10 miles away from the first sighting.
