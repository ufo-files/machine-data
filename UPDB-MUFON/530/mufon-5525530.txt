5 bright lights hovering in pattern, all rise and blink out in distance

walked outside about dusk, headed across the yard to the swimming pool with family. 2 adults, 2 kids witnessed the sighting. we saw 3 bright orange forms in distance, close to ground above horizon. chevron shaped, no balloon silhouette above them, seen against sundown. 3 forms were in a triangle pattern. 2 more rose above the treeline, from the south. we walked closer and could only see 1-2 above treeline. i called neighbor on phone to come witness, he did - saw 2 of them. bright orange light in chevron shape after the sky darkened too. they seemed to be floating/gliding/moving away and pretty quickly. last one finally blinked out. all headed westward from our view southwest of little rock, ar. we went swimming, pondering possibilities. nothing reported on local news websites yet. hmm...
