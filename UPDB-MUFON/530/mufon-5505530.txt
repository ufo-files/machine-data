{nd} 3 round objects. One shot straight up, 2 shot west at high rate.

I was in my living room, reading. It was a sunny afternoon. Something caught my eye that made me look out the window. I saw 3 round objects traveling SW. They appeared to be moving slowly. I have no idea how high they were but they were low enough for me to see that they looked round, that they looked white and that the sun was reflecting off of them. They were not in any kind of formation. I was very excited to see this as I had no idea what they could be. Suddenly one of them broke off from the group and shot straight up and out of sight at a very high rate of speed. As soon as that happened the two remaining balls took off west, going in a straight line at an extremely high rate of speed. 
The next night, 12/30/14 My husband was in our back yard and saw 3 very large green lights (round). It was after midnight, maybe 12:30AM. He grabbed his camera took video of them as they just seemed to be hovering, not moving. The video however looks as if theyre going crazy with movement but that was from my husbands hand shaking. He said they were stationary and not moving. After about 3 or 4 minutes, one by one they just faded out until they were gone. My husband was telling our neighbor about it and she stated that she too had seen them and she was very excited about it. "What were they". The next night 1/1/14 they were back again only at a closer distance. I went out when my husband called for me. I cant determine the distance but my husband again got them on video and we noticed that there was absolutely no sound coming from them. As with the night before, one by one they eventually faded out until they were gone. So for 3 days in a row they same number of round objects were here.
