{cak} Bright orange ball wavering

Around 12:05am I was stopped at the light to turn onto Jefferson Hwy and the light turned red. I saw what looked sorta like a firefly in the sky. But it wasnt. It was dark orange and was at first moving straight across the sky (it was way too big considering how far away it was from me to ever be a bug, a mile at least from me and dozens of feet above the ground) and it was going straight until it starting zig zagging slowly. My heart fell into my stomach seeing what certainly wasnt a plane in the way it moved and the bright color it was emitting. I should have taken a photo but the light turned green. I couldn't stop looking back even though it was going toward Harahan in the opposite direction.
