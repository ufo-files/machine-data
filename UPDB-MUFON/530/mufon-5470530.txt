A bright light that hovered then disappeared into the distance

Me and 3 of my friends were crossing a road while walking home. From the corner of my eye I noticed a large bright light hovering in the distance. I thought nothing of it but then my friend said he saw something strange in the sky. I then described what I had saw and my friend agreed that he too had seen the same thing. When we told our other friends the object disappeared suddenly and was nowhere to be seen. The way the object moved was what caught my attention, it was hovering, then suddenly it zoomed into the distance and disappeared into clear sky.
