Royal blue crescent moves across desert sky, lost time

I was in meditation facing south, looking at the sky. A large (200 ft) glowing royal blue crescent appeared several thousand feet up. It appeared out of clear sky, the moon behind me . It panned to the west across the sky for just a few seconds. Tips of the crescent facing forward. It then disappeared completely. The glow left somewhat of a sunspot in my vision. And I lost track of an hour or so of time.
