{jpg} Cluster of lights traveling quickly across sky toward south south west. Lights weaved in and out of each other as they traveled then all light abruptly disappeared.

I was walking my dogs eastward down an alley after dark when I happened to look into the sky above the rooftops. I witnessed a tight group of blueish white light moving very quickly in a southwestern direction. At first I thought the objects were pieces of a shooting breaking up but then I noticed they had no tails and then most of them began to veer and curve in direction while remaining in a tight cluster. After about ten seconds all the lights abruptly disappeared and I continued to watch in the direction they had been traveling for about 5 minutes in hopes of catching another glimpse. I was amazed because I had never seen anything like this before and the light definitely did not move in any type of similar manner to any manmade craft that I1m aware of.
