{dr}Observed when local historian photographed the barn museum at Edgemont.

I was not the witness to the event . The Historian of the Barn Museum in Edgemont, a suburb of Santee, Ca. went to the Museum to photograph the barn in order to have the lettering redone. When she took the photograph she noticed the object in the upper left of her view finder. She snapped the picture. She said on the news report that her camera broke at that point but she did capture the image.
