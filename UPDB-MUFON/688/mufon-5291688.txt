Triangular object followed by two smaller orbs/spheres.

On the night of August 27, 2020 I was sitting in back yard when I looked up and noticed a strange object in the sky. I watched it for about a minute trying to figure out what it was. I realized the object was not moving in the sky and was not making any sound. I called my wife and family out of the house to have them look at it as well. As they were coming out of the house the object began to move in a straight path from South to North. The object appeared to be triangular in shape two smaller spheres following it. As the object continued to move North at a very very slow rate of speed the two spheres moved to the East side of the object. After about five minutes the two spheres moved back to the south side of the lead object. We continued watching the objects for approximately ten minutes as the continued in a Northerly direction and we eventually lost site of them due to trees.
