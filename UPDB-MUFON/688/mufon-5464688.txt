I was driving south on Pinal Ave from I-10 going into Casa Grande, when I noticed off to my left a bright orange-red light pulsating just over the SW horizon,when all of a sudden another one popped on just to the left of it. Just after that one two more came on in the same direction. After a minute three more came on just above these four. After a few minutes, they began to disappear one by one in the same order that they came on. It reminded me exactly of what the Phoenix lights looked like. I also notice aircraft in he same area before and after the sighting. There is an airport next to Pinal Ave. I did not think to stop to take a photo. Others had to have seen. No one else was stopping. I have never seen anything like this before.
