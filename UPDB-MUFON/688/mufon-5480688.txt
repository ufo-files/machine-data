{rjl} Sighting of non plane structural object in the clouds

While playing a typical round of golf, after finishing a hole, I along with 3 other players noticed this structure in the clouds. We all noticed and confirmed this sighting but we're not able to stay put to observe because of the circumstance of playing golf. We had to move on. None of us really took this seriously. We all just thought it was an anomalous cloud formation. Because of curiosity I took a picture with my iPhone. I recall perceiving the structure / object to be much larger than how it seems on the image. After the golf round I sent the same picture to all the players that witnessed this sighting.
