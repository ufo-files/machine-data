I was star gazing when i noticed a barrel like cylindrical object with dim red lights move fast towards north east direction

I was laying down and star gazing in my back yard at around 11 pm pacific time, looking straight up when i noticed a fast moving barrel like cylindrical object with faint red lights that seemed to outline the shape of the object. The object had a wobble to it and looked like a metal barrel on its side. There were a few small clouds present and this object was above those clouds but not by much, i am guessing 500-700 feet. The sighting was quick and took my breath away and made me say WTF is that ! i could not believe what i was seeing and have no idea what it was. initially i thought space junk but there was no trail and it appeared to have red lights on the perimeter of the object. there was no sound. I have seen orbs in the daytime and other unexplainable light/ events in the sky at night but never something so object like. wow. anyone else seen anything like this ?

ps:
the orbs that my wife and i saw during the day on a clear day in March 2013 were star sized, had an ominous and bright white glow and were descending in a spiralling fashion. when we noticed them and had the WTF moment, i ran to get my phone to start recording them. at this time the two orbs changed direction and slowly took off south east direction evenly spaced out in tandem. no noise. anyone seen such a thing ?
