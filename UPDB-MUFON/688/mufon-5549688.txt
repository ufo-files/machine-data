Observed triangle shaped craft travel north east

We were returning home from being at a friends house. We pulled into the driveway at home and looked into the sky. Above us was a triangular shaped craftmoving slowly in a north - east direction. It occurred at 1130p.m. I looked atmy watch to confirm the time. This is the first time I have observed such anevent. The craft had lights along its delta wing shape, they were white incolour. I would guess its speed at about 200 to 300 miles an hour.
