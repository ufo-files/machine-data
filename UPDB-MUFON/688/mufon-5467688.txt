{td} 4 light green rectangular light slowly drifted over my car

I was driving to work at about 6 AM on a morning in January around 1980, it was a particularly warm year and it was raining that morning. I was heading toward Baraboo on Hwy DL and just as I was nearing Hwy 123 I suddenly noticed 4 rectangular green lights, approximately 18 inches by 8 inches each and they were is a rectangular formation approximately 8 feet apart horizontally and 3 feet apart vertically. The lights were only about 20 feet off of the ground coming up the road toward me. I came to a complete stop and to make sure that it was not a reflection off of my dash I turned the dashboard lights off. The lights kept coming toward me. My drivers window was cracked open but I did not hear anything as the object flew over my car. It was very dark that morning but I do remember that there was an even darker area behind the lights but I was unable to discern a shape. When I got to work a coworker reported that he had been listening to reports of UFO sighting up and down the Wisconsin river on the police radio that night. I would be interested in knowing if anyone else has reported anything similar.
