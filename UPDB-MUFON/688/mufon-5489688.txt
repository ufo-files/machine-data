{dr}Very Bright Star like light moved right then left then appeared to exit atmosphere in 4-6 seconds

I was fertilizing hay fields with my son and it started getting dark so I parked the tractor got off and looked into the night sky. I said to my son did that light move. He said yes. We both stood there and looked in amazement. It first moved left just a little, then back to the right and stopped. Then it moved quickly away from us in an easterly direction, gaining altitude like it left the atmosphere. The entire event lasted less than 1 min. I looked at my son and said I think we just saw a UFO. I am a pilot; I own 2 small aircraft, and I have never seen anything like this in my life. The object was never close, my guess is it was 5-10 miles away. It looked at first like a huge star, but it was way too large compared to other stars in the sky. I know it was not a meteor, because the trajectory gained altitude as it exited the area and there was no trail behind it. I am not sure of the size, but it was about the size of a nickel held at arm's length as we were looking at it in the sky. When it left the area, it went due east and was out of sight in about 6 seconds gaining altitude.
