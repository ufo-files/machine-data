{cak} Saw a ball of light.

I walked outside of work and saw a light out of the corner of my eye. I thought it was a star for a second, then all of a sudden it illuminated brighter as it was slowly moving south, and then it just blinked out. I watched for several minutes and it never reappeared. I never heard any kind of sound. I would say it was circular to oval in shape, and I've never seen anything like that before.
