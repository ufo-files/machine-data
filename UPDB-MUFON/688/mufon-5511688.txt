{cak} very slow pulsating orange lights in formation that did not waiver extremely high in sky aligning near moon

I was taking the trash out,wednesday,december 27,2012 at 8:30p.m. In the eastern sky,I clearly saw for 10-15 minutes a triangle connected to a rectangle fixed formation of 10 orange pulsating lights traversing slowly from north to south aligned with the moon. Not near the moon, but in that section of the night sky higher than where jets fly.This is the same formation I witnessed in July,2012 in Belleville,Mi. I lost sight of the formation as it continued south.
