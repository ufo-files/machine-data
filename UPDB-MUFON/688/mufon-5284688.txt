Fixed star moving right to left, up and down

I work at a golf course, first one there. I have a led light strapped to my chest like Ironman, so I can see at night. I’m out mowing greens at 4am. For a year now I’ve been having communication with 3 star like lights. I will shine my light back and forth and turn it off and they will move in that direction, then I will move the light up and down and they will do that also. Same if I move my light in a circle. Every morning it happens if it not cloudy.
