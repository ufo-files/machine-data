{dr}Triangle flies commercial route over south St. Louis towards airport then veers away

I was outside having a smoke, walking around some, when I turned around towards the west and immediately noticed a "classic" triangle flying in a normal commercial route to the St. Louis airport over south St. Louis (when the flights are landing from the west to east). It was initially the relative size of a golf ball at a 45 degree angle, southwest, in the clear sky. It was clearly an isosceles triangle of white lights, but all looked perfectly round and none were blinking. There was a red blinking light in the middle of the triangle. It made no discernible sound, even when there as there little ambient traffic noise when I can normally can hear a commercial plane as well as see it taking that route. After observing the lights/object for about 5 seconds, the front light shut off! I see many planes whos front landing lights slowly become less visible as it passes by my house getting closer to the airport, but this front light definitely shut off. I could not see a structure, only lights, that appeared to be on a flying craft changing orientation as it was flying by. The normal commercial route is straight as they are flying as they pass my house until I cant see them through trees to the west anymore. Tonight the craft banked left as it neared the point I usually cant see the planes continue in their straight course. The "roundness" and size of the white lights, the front light shutting off (while approaching an airport for a landing!), then veering away from the airport all prompted me to make this report of an unidentified flying object, but yet I would "identify" it as a "triangle UFO". I did not notice any other commercial planes on that route this evening.
