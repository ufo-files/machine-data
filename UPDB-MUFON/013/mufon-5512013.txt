{dr}Fireball like thing, with flat top in silhouette. Hovering and going up and down.

My step dad, sister and I were in the living area. He says "Hey what is that?" after he goes to look out the window. I jokingly say "Is it a UFO?" and with a serious face he looks at me and says "I don't know what it is." So I look out the window and its moving um I would say south east then it starts going up and down. It looked like a fire ball at first but the light was pulsating then it would stop and you could see a silhouette of a flat top of something with it which I am guessing is the craft itself. I couldnt really see how big it was. I do want to say that right before ( i would say 3 mins) our lights flickered. My mother and I also ran outside after we lost sight of it over the houses and we saw it to the north east and then it hovered for a few seconds and just disappeared. My mother says she saw 3 plains in the sky after that. Which is the most we have ever seen. 

When I saw it I felt overwhelmed and excited. I was blown away. I am somewhat of a believer. I follow the stories and I read up on this stuff. Im not a crazed fanatic, I am a rational person when I look at these things and this is my first time seeing one. I have waited my whole life to see this for myself and I am honestly stoked! I will remember this forever. 

I am sorry I couldnt take a picture. We didn't have anything on hand and honestly we were just so amazed and in awe of it.
