triangle shaped and made no noise

I was coming through Columbus IN heading north to Indianapolis at around midnight on a motorcycle. Noticed an object to my left slightly above the tree line. Stopped under an overpass and watched it for several minutes. It would slowly move for with a white light scanning the ground. It would then stop and hover and a red substance would come through the white light and fall to the ground. The red substance reminded me of watching a lava lamp only going down not up. It did this several times the came across the highway and accelerated impossibly fast and was gone. There was no noise from the object at any time
