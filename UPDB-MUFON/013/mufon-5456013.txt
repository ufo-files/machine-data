IN MAY OF 1999 I WAS DRIVING HOME TOWARD MORRO BAY ON HIGHWAY ONE ABOUT 10:45 PM. I NOTICED AN OBJECT IN THE AIR WITH MANY BRIGHT LIGHTS COMING TOWARDS ME. THE OBJECT WAS ON THE NORTH SIDE OF THE HIGHWAY, ABOUT TWO MILES AWAY. I SLOWED DOWN AND WAS APPROACHING THE SAN BERNARDO CREEK ROAD ON MY RIGHT. I SAW ANOTHER DRIVER HAD PULLED OVER TO LOOK AT THE UFO. I TURNED ON SAN BERNARDO CREEK ROAD AND AFTER PASSING THE FARM-STAND I LOOKED TO MY LEFT AND COULD STILL SEE THE LARGE OBJECT HEADING TOWARDS ME. IT WAS STILL LOW ABOVE THE GROUND AND MOVING SLOWLY. I STOPPED MY CAR ON THE RIGHT SIDE OF THE ROAD AND LOWERED MY WINDOW AND TURNED OFF MY HEADLIGHTS AND ENGINE. THE OBJECT WAS NOW ABOUT A HALF MILE FROM ME. IT WAS JUST ABOUT TO PASS IN FRONT OF ME AND THEN IT SUDDENLY STOPPED. ALL I FELT IN THAT MOMENT WAS THE EMOTION OF BEING IN AWE AND FEELING NO FEAR. AS I STUDIED THE OBJECT, MY FIRST THOUGHT WAS THAT IT LOOKED LIKE A FLYING BUILDING. IT HAD MANY LARGE BRIGHT BLUE, WHITE AND YELLOW AND LIGHTS SHINING OFF OF IT. IT WAS ABOUT A QUARTER OF A MILE AWAY FROM ME. IT WAS HOVERING ABOVE THE GROUND AND I HEARD NO SOUNDS COMING FROM IT. THE DISTANCE FROM THE BOTTOM OF THE OBJECT TO THE GROUND WAS ABOUT THE HEIGHT OF A FIVE STORY BUILDING, ABOUT 60 FEET. BECAUSE OF THE BRIGHT LIGHTS, I COULDN'T SEE A SHARP OUTLINE OF THE OBJECT, BUT IT WAS LIKE AN OVAL, BOX LIKE SHAPE BUILDING. THE HEIGHT OF THE OBJECT APPEARED TO BE ABOUT 8 TO 10 STORIES TALL AND THE OBJECT WAS A LITTLE TALLER THAN IT WAS WIDE. THE OBJECT HOVERED IN FRONT OF ME FOR ONE TO TWO MINUTES AND I GOT OUT OF MY CAR TO GET A BETTER LOOK. I. STILL WASN'T AFRAID. WHEN I OPENED MY CAR DOOR THE CEILING LIGHT CAME ON AND ALL OF A SUDDEN THE OBJECT STARTED SLOWLY MOVING TO MY RIGHT AT ABOUT 25 MPH IN THE DIRECTION IT HAD BEEN TRAVELING. IT ACCELERATED TO A FASTER SPEED, TURNED SLIGHTLY TO THE LEFT HEADING UP INTO THE HILLS ABOVE THE CUESTA GRADE AREA. AS IT MOVED AWAY IT PICKED UP SPEED, TRAVELING 100 TO 200 MPH AND GAINING ELEVATION.AS IT FEW OFF IT STAYED FAIRLY LOW RATHER THAN SHOOTING UP HIGH INTO THE SKY. I LOST SIGHT OF THE OBJECT. 
I HAD NO CAMERA NO CELL PHONE, I KNEW WHAT I SAW WASN'T A HELICOPTER, AND IT WASN'T A PLANE , I WONDERED WHY IT HAD STOPPED IN FRONT OF ME. 
THE NEXT MORNING I WENT TO THE AREA WHERE THE OBJECT HAD STOPPED TO SEE ANY BURN MARKS, BUT I DIDN'T WANT TO CLIMB THE BARBED WIRE FENCE.
