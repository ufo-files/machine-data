appeared from no where, Shot through the sky like a bright missle/oval shape then expanded and radiated light then dissappeared

I was in Honokaa, Big Island of Hawaii at 10:40 PM. After dinner my uncle, his friend and myself were on the front porch when i see an object which my first thought was a shooting star. The area i am in when this event took place has many shooting stars every night and immediately realize it wasnt. i point and say oh S*** and the two people im with turn and look the whole time my focus remains on the object. I noticed it because i was staring right where it seems to appear out of thin air. It was a ball of light that resembled an egg/oval/missile leaving a pretty large trail that glowed just as bright behind it. we watched it cross the sky for about 3-5 seconds when it accelerated and the object itself expanded or glowed brighter and disappeared. The way i felt during the sighting was complete shock and awe because i thought i was witnessing an awesome shooting star and instantly realized it wasnt at the point i knew it wasnt a shooting star i knew immediately it was a UFO not a doubt in my mind. After the sighting my adrenaline was rushing and was also a little bit freaked out. To be honest i believe in ET, aliens, other intelligent life but when i witnessed this event it put to rest any doubt there may have been. And niether myself nor the two other persons i was with were under the influence of any type of drugs or alcohol which would be further proof that what we saw was really there. After the whole even i did notice a plane flying in the area the object was seen, but there is a flight path to Hilo international and may have been a commercial flight. To add to the previous sentance the object seen was flying across the flight path and did not resemble anything close to an airplane-at least not one that exists on this planet that i know of. Also there was a cruise ship clearly visible at sea with all its lights on-im assuming it was a cruise ship but am more then certain since i have seen them pass by before.
