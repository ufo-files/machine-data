{jpg} This event happend the night before the solar eclipse here in Kauai

Extreme flashes of light during several parts of the night which was isolated and directly overhead of the property which is situated at the mouth of the river entering the ocean. Several strange paradoxes accrued. There was a large rain, lightning and thunderstorm that night and the next day, the day of the eclipse, followed by the river flooding into the ocean. Electricity was off and on for days.
