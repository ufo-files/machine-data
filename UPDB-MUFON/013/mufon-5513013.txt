(GS) Noticed two orange light traveling East. They got close slowed down and then the lights faded out and disappeared.

I was coming home from an doctors appointment and pulled in my drive way at about 19:15 (1-17-13). I happened to look up after hearing an airplane pass over head, and I noticed two copper/orange lights off to the west.I thought maybe they where helicopters but I heard no noise. They seemed to be moving pretty slowly to the east from the west. The lights seemed to have a flicker to them. I watched them for about 2-3 minutes and the lights got closer together and then they slowly faded out. I thought maybe the clouds covered them up so I waited to see if they would come through the clouds but I saw nothing after waiting form another 2-3 minutes. I doing really recall feeling anything besides what the hell are they, and where did they go.
