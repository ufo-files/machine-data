Sighting in Milwaukee 9/17/2011

Wife came home from shopping and noticed 4 bright red/orange lights in sky in close formation. Pulled into driveway and watched for awhile. Called me to come outside. When I got there, there were 2 ufos. One was V shaped with 4 red/orange lights, other object was single red/orange light. The V shaped ones 4 lights then shifted and moved and became 1 red light. Before it did this it was stationary and hovered. Rather large, quite spectacular really. They eventually flew off beyond sight. I did grab a flashlight and beamed one but nothing became of it.
