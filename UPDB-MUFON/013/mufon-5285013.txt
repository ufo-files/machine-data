Dark, reflective triangular object hovering over Los Feliz

I saw this today over Los Feliz. I was about 1000 ft above sea level, since the Griffith Observatory is 1134 ft above sea level, and I was on a hilltop that was a little bit under that height. Looks similar to the one I saw exactly a week ago. Also kite shaped. Also reflected light. Irregular movement, went up slowly, then hovered; this time it didn’t fade in and out. It was a bit of a windy day, so it shouldn’t have been able to stay so still—then it started moving very slowly east, dropped altitude, at one point picked up speed a tad, then went up again. It passed over the I-5 traveling East. No propellers, no sound. No other air vehicles around. I watched it for a solid 10-15 minutes. Maybe it’s a government spy drone, maybe aliens 🤷‍♂️
