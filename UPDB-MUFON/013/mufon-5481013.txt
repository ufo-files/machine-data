{nd} bright light hovering then two small lights in a different location

We went for a morning walk. Shortly before completing the walk we observed a light to the north that was hovering. At first we thought that it was a drone, but figured that is was to high for that. I took the first picture (the one that looks like a star above the tree in the center of the picture),then looked again and then there were two smaller lights slightly to the west of the original light. I took another picture, (the one with two streaks), then looked again and the object had disappeared. After I took the pictures I zoomed in on them and discovered that the first object had a strange shape to it and the second picture was just really strange because that is not what I was looking at when I took the picture. I attempted to capture the two lights. Two of us were just amazed at what we saw and the other witness was scared. At this point we were less than a quarter mile from our destination.
