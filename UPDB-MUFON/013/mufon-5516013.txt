{nd} I saw an object shaped like a large wing hovering just above the tree line.

I was traveling on hwy 1 in Rome Ga.in the early part of 2011.I noticed four lights directly ahead of me over the hwy;they were very low and I remember thinking a plane was about to make a emergency landing on the hwy.As i continued traveling toward the object,I lost track of it for a moment but quickly relocated it in the top of the trees off the edge of the road,but could only see a couple of the lights through the trees.I was pretty confused at this point about what this was.As I continued on I got a really clear view of the craft at this point because I had stopped at a red light.The craft was almost hovering just above the tree line.Maybe at the speed of five miles per hour.This thing was huge and was shaped like a giant wing with four lights across the front.These were the only lights on the craft,it made no noise and definatly had structure to it.I could see some defanition on the bottom of the craft and could definatly see that it wasnt a airplane.it hovered right over me,slightly off the edge of the road.I was lucky to have had a coworker driving in a seperate vehical behind me and he saw the same thing.
