{cak} Just as the sun went below the horizon I saw an intense, bright, pinkish sphere following the sun down the horizon.

I was going into my house after filming the sunset so I had my camera in my hand. I felt I should turn around and just as I did there was this amazing sight in the sky. I could clearly see it with my eyes and it looked like a tiny marble following the sun down the horizon with a trail on top as it came down. I was able to take pictures for about three minutes and I put the pictures into a video as well as attaching all the original untouched images in the description in case anyone wants a closer look. In the original images you can see debris coming down in the trail. It was amazing and I have been feeling joy since seeing it.
