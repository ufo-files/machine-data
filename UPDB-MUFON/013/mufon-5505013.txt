{kt}red green UFO or drone sighting Brooklyn

I was watching Conan. To the left of our TV is a window that has a mostly unobstructed view of the sky. There are no buildings behind our apartment, and the trees are bare, which allowed me to see the sky clearly. Suddenly a circular red and green light caught my eye. I believe I either saw a UFO or (more likely) a drone. It was hovering in the sky. I got up and went to the window. I pointed it out to my girlfriend, who was sitting on the couch facing away from the window, but she didn't look fast enough. Once I got up, I saw the object for only a few moments, and then it flew out of my view. Again, it was circular, red and green (half red, half green, shaped a lot like that old Simon game), and it moved fluidly. We live not far from airports, but it was nothing like the usual air traffic (planes or helicopters) we see on a regular basis.
