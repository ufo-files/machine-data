{tjd} Something strange caught my eye, and I’ve never seen anything just hover with lights like this.

I took my dog outside to walk, and I always stare up at the sky/stars when I’m out. The sky was partly cloudy with not a lot of stars visible, but I did notice two stars rather close together. This looked odd to me, so as I looked up at it, one of them began to move down in a southeast direction. It didn’t go too far, then it just stopped, and began to just hover there. I had my phone, so I started filming. From the ground I could see a star that looked ‘blurry’. My phone kept going out of focus, so I could not see it very well while filming. On the actual video, you can see that it looks like a round ship, with lights flashing around it. Then a line of lights come off the bottom of it, like being part of a larger ship, or connected to another ship? I would have stayed out there longer if I knew then exactly what I was getting on video.
