I looked up from the tv to see two glowing lights out the window.

I live at the top of a hill on a dead end street. The traffic here is infrequent and at night my dogs tend to bark at any cars. Last night I was lying on my couch watching tv and happened to glance out the window. I saw two lights out the window a yellow light followed by a red light. I was confused because the lights were generally heading up the hill about thirty feet from the ground in generally the same direction that a car might. I didn’t hear a sound and my dogs were quiet so I wondered if it was somehow a reflection on the glass. I turned to search the room for a possible light source. There is nothing that could cause a reflection. I turn back to the lights which I now realize are much bigger visually than headlights or tail lights. Also they are glowing softly, no beams are projecting from them. It’s at this point that they pass between the oak tree in the north pasture and the top of the hill. At this point I start to become frightened, realizing that I don’t know what it is and it’s in my pasture. I run downstairs and wake my wife and grab my rifle. We go back upstairs and look out the window but we don’t see anything. I stayed up the rest of the night finally falling asleep about five a.m. the two lights visually looked about the same size as the moon does when it appears. Maybe a small amount larger. That’s everything I saw.
