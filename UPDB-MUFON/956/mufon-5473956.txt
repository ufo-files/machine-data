{jpg} Stationary object with dull lights or windows over Gig Harbor

I was commuting to work Eastbound on Hwy 16. The new Narrows Bridge was under construction so traffic was moving slowly at about 10-15 mph. The sun was barely illuminating the sky, just enough to outline My. Rainier and the the foothills. I was admitting this view as I scanned the horizon and I noticed a ring of lights over the Fred Meyer store. I had never noticed this and thought they were construction lights, but noticed there was no pole supporting them I could make out the out in of trees and light posts, but the object was airborne. It was still, I rolled my window down to insure I was seeing a reflection. I heard no.unusual sound, just slow traffic.the object reminded me of a giant anniversary ring, like a band with uniform sized diamonds encircling it. The lights may well have been windows, the light emitting from them was soft. I was intrigued but had no choice to continue my commute and soon passed the area from which I could see it. I very much wanted to stop and observe.
