{cak} observed what appeared to be 3 stars in an obtuse triangle right point flashed/twinkled white red blue

I was in a back yard waiting for two dogs to finish business and looked up and saw in the NW sky a triangle formation that was stationary. the right base point flashed/twinkled white red blue..they were some distance off. It seemed to be a perfect triangle...base was long, guessing over 5 miles apart...apex point was about 1/3  over center.. The base line of the triangle was parallel to the horizon. the apex was above the horizon. I watched the formation for about 20 minutes. The right 'point' Blinked/flashed white to red to blue to white. The flash happened three times.
