Giant sphere

I had left king kullen in bridgehampton ny at around 1-105pm on March 7th 2021 was heading west bound on montauk hyw and noticed a giant almost “death star” looking shiny silver object which I at first thought was the moon but then when I saw the same object in Southampton over the ocean heading south it would sometimes seem very bright and others almost seemed like it had fog or cloud coming out of it to almost cloak it. This is not the first time I’ve seen an object or objects like this . Last summer around June 5th-9th while commercial fishing about 35-45 miles sse of shinnecock inlet in Hampton bays Long Island ny. Myself any my cousin Tom witnessed several spheres glowing blue and sometimes white come out of the water and followed out boat for several hours but were always at least 1-2 miles away and every time one of us would try to take a pic something would happen like my phone wouldn’t unlock then when did they were gone again or get camera out and then all of a sudden the net get snagged an boat slows down abruptly or the deck lights would turn on n off the throttle of the boat would go up n down by it self. This happened 2 different times when I was on the boat and my cousin says it’s all the time at least once every other trip they see something strange .
