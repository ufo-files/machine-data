{tjd} Saw multiple lights moving in a straight line equal distances apart from each other.

I was driving home from work last night when a light in the sky caught my eye because it looked like it was moving. I stopped and got out of the car and watched for several minutes. There were at least 5-8 white lights in the sky and were extremely far away. They were an equal distance apart from each other and were moving in a straight line heading from the South West and heading North. I knew they were not planes/helicopters because they had no flashing lights and were moving in perfect formation/distance apart. I also have a app on my phone that tracks planes and where they are currently located in accordance to ones current position. The closest plane on the app was miles away and heading a completely different direction. After watching for several minutes, I got back in my car and continued towards home. I kept watching through my windshield and after driving a few more miles, I no longer saw them. I got out of the car and looked everywhere but they had disappeared.
