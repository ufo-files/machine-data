Glowing object that ascended at a 45 degree angle then appeared to jump and vanish

I was out on my nightly walk when I observed a white luminous object rapidly appear just over the tree line. It traveled ascending in a straight line, appeared to pick up speed, and then completely disappeared after appearing to “jump” forward after exhibiting extreme acceleration. I walk the same road every night, and I live near an airport (CHS), so I know what planes look like in the sky, and this was definitely not a plane. It also wasn’t a drone, nor anything I’m aware of given the extreme velocity of its acceleration. While the sighting only lasted < 5 seconds, it was time enough for me to look directly at it and accurately observe its movement. I’m 52 years old and I’ve never seen anything accelerate like this object did. It appeared much brighter + bigger and way lower than a shooting star. It also wasn’t a satellite given the rate of acceleration and then disappearance from sight on a clear night. If you’re interested, I’d contact CHS International Airport and/or the CHS Airforce Base, as I’m sure both were utilizing radar at the time. While I couldn’t make out a definitive shape because the encounter happened so quickly, I would guesstimate that it was round and it only emitted a bright white light, with no colors observed. It also did NOT leave a streak or contrail.
