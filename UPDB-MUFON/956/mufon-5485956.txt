Shortly after July 4 1987 i was washing dishes at a late 2 am not unusual because i worked second shift at the factory.i noticed a bright sphere shaped object flying low altitude from the southwest.it flew in a circular path like a spiral at a good rate of speed exhibiting a non ballistic flight characteristic.instantly excited i ran out front door to try to see object as it passed by.looked northeast but didn't see object pass by.looked around and saw object hovering over back yard.rural location no street lights and completely overcast.watched object for about 5 minutes then ran in house to retrieve rifle with scope to view object.looked through scope at object for several minutes.sphere shaped object extremely bright light.went to back yard to see better.object didn't move at this point.walked north to south under object to try and estimate how high it was from ground.got on the other side of it quickly estimate object was hovering under 200 feet.stood in yard staring at object with naked eye thinking i would like to see object more closely.object then moved downward and toward me stopping and hovering around 20 feet from ground and about 50 feet from me.i had feelings of extreme excitement.feeling i wanted to try and communicate with object i went to a wind chime hanging on the edge of my duplex about 30 feet from object.i tried to mimic tones from close encounter movie on my wind chimes.after a few times was able to mimics the tone pattern and repeated it multiple times.then i walked back toward object where i had been standing before.object descended to about 10 feet and came closer to me about 20 feet away.immediately felt emotions of joy and elation.object returned to previous position.no sound,no illumination of object on ground or surroundings yet bright enough to produce glare on my glasses lenses.thought in my mind come closer i want to see you better.again object immediately came closer. slow descent same path as before but got closer.once again i was filled with feelings of elation.object returned to previous position.i did a little dance and thought come closer i want to see you up close.object came within ten feet and more quickly this time.this time i felt overwhelmed and felt like i used to before i played a sporting event or went to the principals office,like butterfly stomach.object returned about 20 feet off ground about 50 feet away.this repeated several times.every time object came closer when about ten feet away from me i got overwhelmed with nervous anxiety.when i did object would return to farther away position .same flight path same position.mosquitoes were really bad and was without shoes or socks.was getting bit badly and retreated to duplex for shoes and socks.returned to back yard and i spotted object moving slowly away.was over road about an eighth of a mile away.thought in my mind for object to come back and immediately it came back to same position above back yard.thought in my mind come really close i want to know what you are.object came closer than before i am 6 feet and it was even with my head and 5 or six feet distance from me.extreme anxiety in my stomach to the point i could not take it.object reacted immediately and returned to distant position.went through this a couple more times.feeling wore out and overwhelmed i said goodbye did the chime tones again and went inside.watched object slowly take a path over near road and moved away to the south.looked at clock to check time about 2:30 am.duration about 30 minutes.
went to bed exhausted.woke up next day covered in mosquito bites but exhilarated from previous nights experience.told my neighbor about it laughed and seemed skeptical.the night after first incident went to bed around 10pm.trying to sleep i kept hearing my wind chimes dinging softly.had bedroom window open hot humid and no air conditioner.looked out at chimes no wind at all.went back to bed a short time later heard chimes ding again this time louder and with the same pattern i had used the night before.really scared me.looked out saw nobody there and no wind thought neighbor might be pranking me.laid back down and it happened again same pattern and loud.
felt scared and panicky.put pants on and grabbed my pistol then ran around to back yard.looked around and saw a figure standing near a friends dwelling illuminated in his back porch light.clothing seemed odd with a very high collar.to much clothes for the heat and humidity of July.could not see face or details of garments was to dark and shadowy.felt agitated and said who are you what do you want.figure just stayed there like it was staring at me.thought my neighbor might have put my friend up to spooking me as a joke so i approached the figure quickly.figure turned and moved away from me to the east as i approached from the west.as it moved away in a line heading east about10 or 15 feet from the back of the back of the duplexes i quickened my pace to a trot.as i was catching up to figure it went past a window with its kitchen light on.figure was similar height as me around 6 feet tall and looked like it was wearing a high collar coat or robe.figure moved out of window light and towards a large tree.i was agitated and said get out of here as i went past the lit window with my pistol extended in a firing position.i heard a gasp and turned to see my state trooper neighbor drop her dishrag and plate into a sink full of water,i was about 6 feet away from her and could see by her facial expression and vocalization she was startled.i turned to see figure go behind a large tree kitchen.bewildered i went to my friends house where the back porch light was on went around front and noticed through his window he and another of my friends visiting were asleep on sofa and a chair.another neighbor was working on his car in duplex next door and i asked if he had seen anyone walking around he said no.i walked on home expecting my state trooper neighbor to call the sheriff the first night of my experience was wonderful a life altering experience in a good way.the second night was very scary.wish i could of controlled my fears .always have wondered what i could have been privileged to experience.did my neighbor get the ride of a lifetime.wish it could have been me.after my experience i developed severe headaches and a itchy rash behind my ear lobe on right side.some small bumps developed behind right earlobe over time but never got bigger,still there today.this happened over28 years ago in July 1987 not long after 4th of July.but remember it like yesterday.never told about this event except to some family and friends still not sure i should be speaking of it.still have fears of who was involved and what it might invoke.
