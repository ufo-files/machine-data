{cak} Craft hovering, then takes off south, with short trail of white and blue light following then disappearing in seconds.

3/11/2019 8:30pm, clear sky's.
 I was leaving my buddies house in Willis, MI. As im driving down turtle hill coming up to a stop sign to willow rd I happen to look up..what I seen was amazing. Hovering about 600ft was a..its hard to explain but I drew a picture of it, but a arrow head looking craft facing south as i was. The outline was a bright white with a blue tint on the very outside edge. The back of the craft had a bright ball of white light and when it took off it left a trail of white and blue light for a split second then it proceeded to follow the craft for a split second and then just disappeared into the sky like it opened a portal and vanished.
