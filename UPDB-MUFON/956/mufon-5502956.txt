{nd} 2 lighted objects passing over head

My wife and I went out back to smoke. As always i look to the sky because ive witnessed strange sightings before. As we were sitting there I began to notice a star like object begin to move from north to south. Aprox. 201 to abt 701 then appeared to ascend and disappear. I was excited to witness another strange sighting. Abt 30 minutes later I went back outside to look out of curiosity and I saw the same lighted star like object moving this time from south to north aprox. 801-301 and also ascended and disappeared. Neither objects speed increased or became brighter. There wasnt any flashing lights. It litterally looked like a star slowly moving across the sky then just go up and disappear.
