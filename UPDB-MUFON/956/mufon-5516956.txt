{nd} silver ball way above class A airspace gliding toward me, slowed when I noticed it then hovered each time I stopped walking

I was walking across an almost empty parking lot from Charleston Blvd to Barnes & Noble and looked up specifically to see the strange clouds and the plane ascending from McCaran like I usually do. Just happened to notice a silver orb gliding toward me NNW/almost directly above me. I had to look almost straight up. The plane flew under it. At almost the same time a black helicopter came from the direction of Mt. Charleston. I cant be sure it was deliberate because I was watching the orb the whole time waiting for my eyes to distinguish wings or a tail to rule it out as a military plane flying above commercial airspace. However, it remained an orb without a trail the entire time. It was too high to hear any sound it may have been making. I noticed it slow as I stopped walking and just stood in the parking lot looking up. I know people driving by thought I was crazy but I couldnt take my eyes off of it. I took a few steps and it moved with me. As I stopped it stopped. As I moved it moved. As I sit here in Barnes & Noble writing this my eyes keep scanning the bright blue sky out the window. 

It had no visible lights or windows that could be seen at 3:30 on a clear day. It seemed to float but at an amazing speed with the ability to slow & stop at an amazing speed.

All I can think is, "What did I just witness?" AND "I hope its still out there when Im done here." This is the 2nd sighting Ive had in my life. The first time was from the top of our backyard swingset looking out over the fence to SF Bay. The SAME rush of adrenaline and goosebumps.
