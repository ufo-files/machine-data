Disk seen hovering then moving over I16 near Dublin, GA

at 8:15 est i was driving west on i 16 with passengers and observed a saucer like craft approaching at tree top height on the right shoulder . the object then hovered over my truck at tree top height for a couple of seconds and then continued to travel east above the west bound lane at approx 30 mph. it had 8 white lights around the underside perimeter and one light of equal size in the center. the lights did not appear to illuminate the ground only the object itself
