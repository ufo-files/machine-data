{rjl} Green ball of light that flashed to highlight the entire sky, no sound.

New Years Day, 12:30am roughly, a friend and I are walking in the front yard of a NYE house party heading to the front porch, facing Northwest. At about a 50 degree angle from the horizon, a green ball of light appeared and within one second the light eminated a flash of green that lit up the entire sky. Fireworks had been previously in the area, with the last explosion occurring a few minutes prior. There was no sound within 1 minute before nor 1 minute after the green light appeared and disappeared, respectively. 
The other party-goers on the porch witnessed the sky light up green but did not see the original green light. The witnesses in the back yard saw the flash but did not see the source.
