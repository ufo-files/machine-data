Reddish orange light flew north across sky

I took the dog outside and was sitting on the steps when an orange/red light flew in a strait, unwavering line, across the sky. I stood up and walked out to the street to follow it's path. It was obscured by trees but I was able to see it for a few more seconds. It was totally silent. I'm guessing it was lower than a jet flies. I'm not sure, but I think the light was rectangular in shape and orange with red edges. It brought to mind a Japanese lantern, the cylinder shaped type, but as if it were laying sideways-meaning that it was longer horizontally than vertically. My first thought was that I was seeing a firework, but because it continued on until it was far away, I discounted that notion, but have never seen anything like it before.
