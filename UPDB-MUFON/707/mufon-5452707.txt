Multiple sightings

Bright light rose from the south horizon. 
Normal air traffic is east/west. As it reached about a 45 degree angle, another rose from the south horizon. A smaller light traveled at increased speed from this light and caught up with the 1st light now directly overhead. Both of these lights made a high speed right (90 degree) turn side by side and the second large light continued on a northern path. No sound. Too high for landing lights. Too fast for known aircraft.
Many sightings are observed over the past 40 years.
I was on leave from the Air Force at the time. 
Other sightings included a bright light high in the sky traveling at very high speed traveling north in a zig zag or saw tooth pattern.
