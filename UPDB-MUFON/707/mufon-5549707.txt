Big object in sky

I was in my back yard at 9423 Nagil Ave in the San Fernando Valley North Los  Angeles when I felt somthing hot behind me. I turned around and saw an object  in the sky slowly rising at a steep angle towards the Sun, which was a little  to the left. The object left a trail of heat behind it as it moved. It rose  passed the Sun on the left side, almost tuching it, then it turned on end and  passed over the Sun, then turned down and fell at a steep angle towards the  right until it passed behind a telephone pole at the right corner of the yard  at a hight just slightly above the wall.When I told my mother that I saw something in the sky, she told me to shut up,everything in the sky is associated with religion.
