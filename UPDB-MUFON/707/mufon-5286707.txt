I was taking pictures of the moon on the morning of May 27th, 2021, until I saw reflective objects over the moon.

On the morning of May 27th, 2 am, in Huntington, Indiana, I was taking some shots of the full moon. It was a clear night. I started to notice fast streaks of light just like one would see seeing a shooting star burning up in the atmosphere. These objects started coming into view on my camera so i switched to record, and started filming. One reflective object came from above the moon and these other reflective object were shooting out from the bottom of said object and flying around the moon. They were very reflective, some would shoot down from the moon, some over the moon, and to the left and right sides of the moon. From my perspective, i couldn't tell if these were in or out of our atmosphere. The sporadic movements of these objects, has me questioning whether or not these are of human technology. They moved in packs. Never have i seen something like this.
