October 1973 the night object intercepted helicopter at Mansfield OH friends farm county road 1706 parl with 71 west (back Lawn)

FILLED OUT AT MUFON FROM A PAPER REPORT SENT IN TO THE OFFICE, BY THE FIELD INVESTIGATOR, ON 2/11/2021. 
WITNESS DID NOT HAVE A DAY FOR THE SIGHTING SO I PUT THE 6TH, REPORT DEMANDS A DATE. 
WITNESS DID NOT UNDERSTAND THE ALTITUDE AND ELEVATION QUESTIONS, WROTE IT WAS ABOVE THE 71 GOING WEST.
WITNESS WROTE IN LONG RECTANGLE INSTEAD OF CHECKING SQUARE/RETANGLE BOX.
UNDER SURFACE OF OBJECT WITNESS WROTE IN REFLECTIVE LIKE PLEXIGASS.
UNDER NUMBER OF WITNESSES', WITNESS SAID THREE BUT SAID NO ONE ELSE WOULD TALK, SO HE DIDN'T OFFER ANY CONTACT INFORMATION ON OTHER WITNESSES.
