UFO HOVERING TYCHO CRATER. THE OBJECT SEEM TO PRODUCT A SHADOW ON THE MOON.

This video footage was taken by T-FEB (Independent Research Group) from Italy, coordinate: N 45170'86,02", E 10166'58,85" at 00:02 on June 24, 2018 .

The video shows the passing of an U.F.O. over the moon surface between Tycho and Maginus craters. 

After further analysis, and contrast correction we can see clearly two unidentified objects moving toghether, where the biggest black one, seems to be the shadow of the smallest reflective one, on top of it. 

The dimension of the 1shadow1 is similar to the central Peak of Tyco crater. 

Help us to find what could it be!
