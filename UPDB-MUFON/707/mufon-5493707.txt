{dr}Stationary object sighted over Virginia beach over the ocean on busy holiday weekend

My girlfriend and I were having coffee on our second floor balcony.She observed the object in the east over the ocean.I then observed it myself..The object appeared to be quite far but I could see a spherical object that was somewhat reflective underneath high thin clouds.The object was stationary but was emitting at random times a bright reddish light.There was at the time a strong onshore easterly breeze yet there was no movement of the object for about a half hour.
