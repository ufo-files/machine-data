First one multicoloured light descending and joined by another one that partly circled it and then they both descended in the direction of Lake Illawarra. No noise, clear beautiful night, no wind, cold and two witnesses.

Witnessed unusual lights in the north of a clear, cold, night sky at 10.50pm Thursday 20th February 2014 by husband first who called wife outside to witness the event as well. While husband checking the garage is locked for the night he saw a very bright, soundless light first showing red and white and at first he thought it was our local emergency helicopter, but when he noticed there was no sound on a clear night with no other aircraft in sight and it began to change to green and blue colours, he noticed it was unusual. He noticed its flight path was a slow but jerky descent toward the tree line in the north, and as it slowed it was joined by another exactly like it but the second light circled precisely around the first before both lights descended slowly over the Lake Illawarra area. We both felt curiosity at the event and hoped we could see more as these particular UFOs are quite beautiful compared to others we have seen. Couldn't get the camera out quick enough and sighting was unusual as we don't make machinery that looks and behaves like this. Lost sight of objects behind tree line on the horizon.
