{dr}video taping airplane vapor trails and caught ufo in footage, noticed after looking at the video.

On May 20, 2013 I was out in my backyard with my dog, thought I saw something in the vapor trails, went in to get my camera. I then saw 2 airplanes crossing each other and decided to video tape them. First following to the right and then to the left. This was at 7:52pm Eastern Time. Within an hour later I uploaded the video to my computer and played it back and when I did I was very surprised to see 3 dark/black spheres in a triangle formation, to me it appeared they came out of the vapor trail from the North/NW and went to the East. The airplane was in view the entire time. The ufo came into view at 30 seconds into the video and lasted only 5 seconds, it was all very quick. However, as I watched the video the spheres seemed to move as one and it appeared to have almost a translucent center, with the 3 dark spheres forming the outer triangle.
