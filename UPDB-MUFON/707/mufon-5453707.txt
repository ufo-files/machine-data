{cak} Triangular craft seen near Brownsville Oregon

While sitting outside and looking South I noticed two lights beyond Eugene Airport at the point of the furthest horizon which would be about 60 miles away. It was at least 40 miles away when I first observed it.

The object was flying NNE and approximately directly towards my position. At first I thought it might be aircraft approaching EUG, when it didn't approach the airport I was thinking perhaps two Satellites on the same path. The lights never varied in their direction or apparent distance from each other. As it came close I got the impression it was one object and not two.

As the object approached still a bit to the west and above the trees from my perspective I could tell it was definitely in our atmosphere and not in space. It was less than half the altitude of a passenger jet at full altitude. I estimate it was at about 15,000 feet in altitude.
At it's closest proximity(at approximately 60 degrees sight line) I could then see there were scattered small or dim lights across it's bottom and, briefly, I could absolutely see a long, triangular shape! I would estimate the nose angle at about 25 degrees. I thought to myself, "this thing is massive in size!". I put up my fingers and from my perspective the distance between my fingers was 3 inches. Using size/distance ratios this could easily make it the size of an aircraft carrier if my assumptions are in the ballpark.

I moved away from the south side of the house ( I was in my hot tub) so that I could continue to watch it move to the north. We live on the south slope of a hill and I quickly lost sight of the object. I told my wife and called my brother with the news.

The entire time elapse I estimate to be about 3 minutes and I watched it travel perhaps 50 miles.
