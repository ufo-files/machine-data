{nd} white/silver flashing sphere object floating/hovered in sky

I was looking out back from my home and noticed this white/silver round object floating near cloud level. The object didn't make any sound and just seemed to be hovering there. It may have moved a few feet, but not very far. I was not sure what this object was so I grabbed my camera and video taped it for about 4 minutes. It was exciting to see, but I could not figure out what it was. I sent the video to MUFON Headquarters and talked with Dinah L. about it, she is the Florida MUFON State Section Director here in the Ocala area. She invited me to her MUFON meeting this past Sunday in Ocala and several of her Field Investigators looked at the video and helped me make this report.
