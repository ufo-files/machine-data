{jd} On January 7th , 2013 at 6:00 am , we witnessed an orange orb of light in our home...

On January 7th , 2013 at 6:00 am , I experienced something unreal... We had gotten up to let our canines out after they woke me up... After we let them in , I was standing in the living room door and happened to look down at my stereo speaker... The front of the speaker , just below the felt cover , was softly flickering a orangish glow... I thought I might have been seeing things , so I kept looking at it and it did it again... I was standing there in the dark , fully alert watching this happen in front of me(only about 4 feet away)... Then my wife walked into the room from a different entry way and witnessed it as well... It eventually just faded out... Before I described anything to her , I asked her to describe to me what she saw... She said she saw an actual 'orange orb of light' from where she was standing... Needless to say , I did not go back to sleep... It wasn't really a 'scary' experience... Just a 'strange' one...
