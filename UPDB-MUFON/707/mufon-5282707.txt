ufo craft below the crest of the falls

Woke up early on Monday morning 06/02/2008 about 6:00 am after spending a weekend at Niiagara Fall on the Canadian side. As I was getting ready to leave to go back to the States in Ohio ,I decided to take some picture of the falls since the morning was clear and the sun was rising with a nice view of the falls . I shot 10-12 pictures at the falls and around the area. .My room was about the 18 floor of the falls view hotel facing the falls .When i got home after uploading the pictures I found objects I could not explain. Never saw , never heard. But the one object appears to be feet from the crest of the falls with a right angle probe protruding from it .I cropped and enlarged , added contrasted and concluded it was no a helicopter or anything i ever saw .I contacted Transport Canada and sent the the photo and they told me that the AIR CRAFT should not have been there.....They sent a Enforcement team to investigate the camera's pointing at the falls from other hotels and could not find nothing of a object below the crest of the falls....I believe i caught a interdimensional craft at the right time.....
