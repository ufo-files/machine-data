{cak} Rotating lights in the shape of a triangle

My sister and I were stargazing(on our backs),looking for meteors in Woodsville, NH; at about 1030 at night.At first we thought we saw a small plane coming. As the object got close and noticed it didn't sound like an airplane and it was flying much lower then one too. It made a soft humming sound. It had three bright rotation white lights in a triangle shape. It traveled from the SW to NE in a direct line over us. It continued NE till be lost site of it. The whole thing happen in about 2 to 3 mins.I don't think we realized what we saw till after it had passed us.We got to see meteors and then some!
