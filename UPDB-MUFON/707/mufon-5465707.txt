{rjl} Slow moving retangle shape with blunt bullet nose moving across sky

At approximately 0720 on 9.20.18 I observed a slow moving object that appeared to not have wings, rotors, or basket. The body was a white/silver, rectangle in shape. The nose was greyish and blunt bullet in shape. There were 2 windows, 1 in front that wrapped to the side(s) and one at midsection. I took a pair of binoculars from my jeep to get a better view and observed, no wings, rotors or basket. There was also NO call letters or numbers or insignia and it made no sound. After verifying these issues I jogged up to an office window about 15 feet from my jeep to get a co-worker to come out, then jogged back to my jeep. This took about 20 seconds, but when I looked back up with co-worker, it was gone. The object had been moving NE to NW although I'm not good at judging height or distance, it took about 10 minutes to cross our work farm and office area (approximately 60 acres). A few minutes of talking about it, a jet flew over - which varified to me that what I saw was not a man operated aircraft. The jet was much higher and faster and yet we could see the wings and it had a vapor trail.
