Solid Ship-Like a Large Object gliding along within a giant cloud formation.

Under a silly notion this was “my” Angel in a cloud on my birthday, February 3, 2020, I snapped 20+ pictures of this unique Cloud from my iPhone 7. 
The pictures reveal a very large solid Ship-like object within the cloud moving along within it.
All pictures were taken consecutively, beginning at 9:21 a.m. into 9:24 a.m.. Some of the photos are taken with “live” turned on”.
I took photos for the approximate 4 1/2 minutes, and then watched it over the next 15 minutes or so as it glided gently away toward the north east. I quit watching it at this point.
