Like a white light

Me and my cousin we're driving on a gravel road I don't know the name of the road but I know where it's at. My cousin was driving I was passenger we were heading west. We stopped when we saw this light in the sky hanging over a tree line which which seem to be sitting still but it looked like it had a spotlight coming down and following the tree line along the field. It came right towards us in the car and the car lit up inside like a bright light. When the light hit the car and passed the car the scene like spotlight went into the ball off light. Then it took off to the north really fast and disappeared
