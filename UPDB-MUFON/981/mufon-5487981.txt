I was outside on Route 23 in Butler. Girlfriend pointed out an orange orb. Looked at it and it wobbled slightly and had a haze like halo around it. Nothing extremely remarkable, just odd. Kept watching as it flew from route 23 in Butler toward the area of Wanaque Reservoir. Soon as it got to that area. It started to hover. Then it started moving in one direction and making tight 180 degree turns back and forth. Think of a Orange orb on a formula one track. Thats how tight the turns were yet it made those turns in different directions. It made 3 of these sharp turns, hovered for a minute, then descended behind the tree line which I later found out is where the Wanaque reservoir was by checking google earth. It was too high to be a drone and traveled from Butler to Wanaque which is several miles in the pitch black night. A quick glance at first and it could have been mistaken for a plane. As soon as it hoovered and made those 180 degree turns, the feeling was totally surreal. 3 people witnessed this including myself. It was so crazy that I believe someone else had to have seen it. This is a populated area. What the hell is going on at Wanaque reservoir.
