(GS) Three Gold / Copper lights dancing in sky north of Branson Mo

Spotted lights moving up / down and around each other to the north of Branson MO. Incident was viewed from Hwy 248 near Kmart by both myself and my Wife. We pulled into Great Southern Bank parking lot to better observe. lights were moving up and down and around each other and would change direction of rotation periodically hovering. All three lights were clearly moving Independently of each other. May have been over Christian County. Movement had the pace and cadence of a ballroom waltz - very elegant.

Left parking lot to see if better view could be had from 248/Branson hills parkway area. Lost sight of one light at intersection of 248/Gretna as it went below tree tops. Lost sight of other two behind hill traveling north on 248. Never regained visual on them, even from up on top at 248/Branson Hills Pkwy.
