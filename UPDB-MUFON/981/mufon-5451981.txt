Regarding case #109658 of starlike lights traveling in straight line formation: they numbered 35 objects.

In my initial report (case #109658 of starlike lights) I omitted that I saw 35 of theses”stars” traveling in one straight line formation! The first half dozen or so ones that caught my attention were pretty consistently spaced apart, but as they kept coming there were spacing of 2 followed by a third slightly lagging. 

Then I observed their spacing pattern was not as consistent as they moved from NW to the NE and out of my field of vision (behind my roof line). Toward the middle of the line-up (by then around 15 had passed) I noticed the last ones were farther apart yet the line held completely straight above the 45th parallel. The last couple objects were tailing even farther apart but still same even high rate of speed and line formation.

Their point of origin did not start at the west horizon, but rather Emerged/appeared at a point I would guess was above the Oregon Coastal range of mountains but difficult to tell due to being so high in atmosphere. 

If the lights of Portland metro weren’t so bright they would have been easily spotted by many people, but the agricultural area where I live is less illuminated at night so they appeared equally bright as stars’ (not the planets) brightness and general size. 

The thing that caused me to first focus my attention was the precision, speed and determination of their movement. Their path literally traced a giant arc in the “dome” of the night sky.
