{dr}Second video of glowing orb over Chandler, Arizona

This is the third time I1ve seen this object, and this is the second video of it. The first video is also here from a sighting on June 5, 2012. Because I had seen this twice before (and in the same part of the sky), I set my camcorder on a tripod, placed it on the porch pointing in the same direction, and turned it on. On Friday, June 15, 2012, I captured the object again, and this time from the very beginning until it faded away, as usual. However, it does not first exhibit the line of lights as I had seen before, but just the orb itself. Near the end of this video, some lines are noticeable going into the object from the left and right side near the bottom. I will continue setting up the camcorder to capture another video.
