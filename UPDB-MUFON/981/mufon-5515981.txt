{nd} Two orange/red/yellow lights above parking lot next to our house then they left

My wife she was hearing a strange humming sound (my hearing is not so good) when I looked up to see what it was I saw two very bright orange lights out side. I stood up and studied them and realized this was not normal. Immediately ran out the door to get a better look and t my wife to get our granddaughter to come out with her I-Phone to video this. By this time the lights were moving away from us headed in a northerly dirction but my grand daughter did catch them on video before disappearing in the clouds. About 10 mins. after this we saw one more of the lights headed in the same direction until it also disappeared in the clouds that cover the mountains. I thought this was very strange because of the mountains in this same direction and no normal aircraft fly in that direction.

The lights seemed to a triangular shape but seemed to shift to a diamond shape. It was very hard to focus on these lights and determine their exact shape. My wife and I have different experiences of the objects/lights, she said the first pair of lights were on one larger craft but I thought they two different craft. Its very possible she is correct because the lights remained an equal distance apart from each other until they were out of sight. My granddaughter(15) just thought it was cool and posted the short video on YouTube.
