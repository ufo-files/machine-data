{rjl} Large oval shapped bright orance object with unsemetrical black patches on bottom

Object simply appeared in the sky NW of our location, it did not come up from the horizon, one second there was nothing and the next second it was there. It then moved on a slow steady path to the SE across the sky with no change in speed. The object was very large and bright orange with scattered black dots (windows?). In the direction the object was moving the oval was concave, think of a oval shaped chocolate chip cookie with a bite taken from it. The object moved across the sky until it vanished over the horizon. Best I can describe it is a dull red-orange glow but not really casting any light, like it was lit internally. The black dots were void of any color and were random, not symmetrical.
