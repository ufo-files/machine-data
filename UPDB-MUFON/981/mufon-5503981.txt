witnessed bright light hover over toronto then dissapear. no plane had head lights on

i was on my balcony watching the sky when i notice something unlike the others. every plane coming towards me or going away had two lights and the rest between them, and no stars were visible at this time because the sun was up. this light in the sky was quite low and it hovered for some time and pulsated quite a bit. it was a single light with no visible craft carrying it. i watched it for a few minutes until the light quickly turned as it dissipated. i think i saw something shoot off into the distance from where the light was, but i was too far and it was far too fast to know for sure.
