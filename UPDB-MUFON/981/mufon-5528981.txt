7- 10 objects that were emitting white light behind cloud cover.

I was in Lincoln Village in Austin. I noticed strings of webbing float in the wind. Then I noticed that trees and streetlights were covered in them. I got in my car to drive home. I then noticed 7- 10 objects that were emitting white light behind cloud cover. These lights in the clouds were moving very quickly in back and forth sweeping motions over long distances. I pulled over to observe them more. I do not think they were spotlights because the amount of light emitted from these objects were dimmed as it moved through thicker cloud coverage. I got in my car and returned home.
