{nd} White glowing saucer shape with two levels of lights. Larger disc rotating in one direction and second smaller disc in opposite.

During one evening in the fall of 1971, myself and the neighboring community in a busy residential Avenue with nearby small local shops, witnessed the descent of a glowing object descending very fast in the sky from the southeast of our location. The obvious looking saucer shaped object hovered directly over two buildings across the street from where I stood with others, and we could clearly see it was a round, saucer shaped, white glowing surface, with multi-colored lights appearing to rotate in sequence in one direction. Initially we were unable to see underneath, but the craft began to turn at a different angle to disclose its underbelly and then we could see the smaller shaped disc attached (as part of the same craft), also with multi-colored lights also rotating in sequence in the opposite direction. The object slowly began to ascend and then took off in a blink of an eye and was "gone" heading in approximately the same direction it descended. No noise whatsoever. Not a sound. The entire surrounding community of eyewitnesses fell silent after it's departure. A Metro bus approaching stopped right in the middle of the intersection when the craft was in view. It was an amazing sight.
