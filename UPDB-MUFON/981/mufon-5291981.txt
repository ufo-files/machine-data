I saw 3 oval objects, black in color.

I was on my deck and I looked up and saw three rice shaped objects moving across the sky. It was still daylight out. The objects seem to be flying in formation, and each one had an orange light at the end of it. The objects were black in color and I have never seen anything like it before. I live close to Camp David, so I've seen most everything. I knew they couldn't be any sort of American aircraft because they only had one light, the color was orange, and it stayed on all the time period. I also live very close to a military installation called SIte R. It is built into the side of a mountain, and all the tunnels entering it have armed guards at the entrances.
