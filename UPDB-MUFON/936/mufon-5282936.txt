video of an apparently globular, reflective object, possibly rotating

I have included the videos of the object but did not grant the republishing rights, please feel free contact me to discuss if you are interested in this sighting. A globe-like object that may have had extensions and a rotating action was seen and videotaped near the shoreline in Long Beach, California. The object appeared to be reflecting the setting sun. To my eyes it seemed to reflect orange-red evening sunlight, but in the video it appears whitish and metallic. The object floated in a direct path across the sky and and did not appear to change elevation like helium balloons would. It appeared to have something dangling from it, sort of like instruments attached to a weather balloon. However, it seems like parts of the object may be moving or rotating. In the video, there is a darkness and possibly a shadow that appears on the right hand side of the object, disappears and then reappears again, suggestion rotation or movement of parts. 

At first I thought this was a bunch of party balloons, but as it came across the sky, it did not look like a bunch of balloons. A close look at the video casts doubt on the idea this idea. It could be a weather balloon, which would explain its consistent path across the sky. But I don't know and it seemed strange to me, and similar to a stationary UAP I observed in the New Mexican desert some years back.
