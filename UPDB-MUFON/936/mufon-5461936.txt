Pulses of bright light in the sky observed from my vehicle

While out in my vehicle with my husband sitting in our van listening to music, we observed a bright white pulse of light in the night sky, followed by several other light pulses of varying intensity,
This last for approximately 75 seconds. The appearance of the light reminded me of representation of the Star of Bethlehem which I've seen before.
