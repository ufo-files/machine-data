Solid Light as Bright as a Star Does 90 Degree Turn, Stops, and Goes Other Direction

Saw a light size of a star moving rapidly across the sky and then change directions at almost a 90° angle and then stop for a brief moment and head in a different direction. I live by several Airports and there is no way this was a plane as I have videos of planes flying overhead including same exact air space. Object was almost 90° upright in the sky an altitude much higher than any plane that ever flies by, made no noise, and was traveling faster than any plane. The object’s course came from over the Atlantic Ocean which is 15 miles away along with NYC. Object was heading west, does a 90 degree turn to head south for a brief moment, stops, and proceeds to head northwest. Object had to be extremely bright because only major stars, planets, and Big Dipper are visible due to light pollution in area. This isn’t a drone, plane, or anything man made. Video quality is poor as is any video captured at night with any phone. But to be able to capture and see the movement on video says something as it was more impressive seeing with naked eye.
