I stepped out of our metal building and looked up towards the north west and seen 3 black triangle crafts flying in triangle formation heading south west. They were about the size of a Cessna plane about 1000 feet up. They made no noise at all. I live in the country so no background noise at all. Around 5 minutes later I seen another 1 heading in the same direction as the other 3. Straight flying no noise out of that one either. We have alot of planes fly over our house so I'm always looking up but most of the time I hear the noise and then look but these did not make any noise at all.
