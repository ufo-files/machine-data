Witnessed multiple bluish green (v) shaped objects in night sky

While visiting family I went outside to smoke. Looking around I looked up to sky and witnessed several bluish green lights in night sky that outlined what appeared to be a V shape. Some were behind clouds and some in open of clouds. Objects were not in uniform pattern but were close in relation to one another. I called my husband to come outside and he witnessed the lights, shape and how they just stayed in one spot. I used cell phone and tried to capture pictures. They came out blurry but I believe they do hold true to what I described as v shape and well lit. They were not night stars. My husband asked me to come in house and I went to call my son from the house looked out window and they were still in same spot. I walk back outside and that quickly they were all gone. Later that evening on drive home to NJ 10ish pm, my husband witnessed them again and told me to look out window into left of sky and there they were again. All stationary except one fluttered to left and right erratically.
