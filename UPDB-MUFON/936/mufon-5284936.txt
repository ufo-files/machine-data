I believe I may have been abducted when I was 17.

I woke up in the early hours of the morning, it wasn't anything unusual as I am a restless sleeper. I closed my eyes but then found I couldn't open them properly. I forced my eyes open as much as I could, which wasn't much, everything was bright white. Two Silhouettes came into view on either side of me, I couldn't make out any features, just the silhouette of heads. I was completely paralysed and couldn't even feel myself breathing. After a few seconds of the silhouettes coming into view, there was a bright blue light in the centre of my field of vision, at the same time there was a horrible sensation, almost like a vibration, but almost indescribable. After 10 seconds or so the light and sensation stopped. This was repeated several times. I closed my eyes because I couldn't cope with what was happening any more, when I opened them, I was sat upright in my bed.
