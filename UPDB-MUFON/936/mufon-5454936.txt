{tjd} Object appeared after leaving inside lobby of bank (ATM machine)

I first noticed the object as I was leaving the bank lobby. Upon exiting I saw three orange dots to the east. The dots moved in my direction and passed overhead.

The object appeared triangular in shape with three lights, one of which was extremely bright but then faded to match the other two lights as it passed overhead. There was no strobe light as is typical on airplanes.

An airplane to the north of my position made noise which was picked up on the video tape, but did not come from the object just described.
