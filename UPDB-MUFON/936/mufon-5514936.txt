(GS) grey saucer shaped

i was entering garfield park in chicago at the jackson blvd. side, when i noticed people looking up in the sky. i looked up and observed a metallic colored disk which appeared to be almost imbedded in a cloud it was a clear and sunny day and i could see this object clearly. other people in the park noticed it and were looking up at it. it seemed totally stationery. at the time i was looking to find my friends who were in the park, so i stared at it for a few minutes and moved on. i believe this happened in 1952 which would have made me 12 yrs. old. i told no one, however years later my older sister asked me if i ever saw anything like that and for some reason i answere no.

 the event date is approximate, as is the time. i got out of school at1500 hrs. and took about 20 mins. to get to the park. it was n.w. above the hamlin ave. entrance to the park.
