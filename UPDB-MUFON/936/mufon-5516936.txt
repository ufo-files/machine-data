{nd} On Sunday, July 22, 2012 at approximately 12:30 a.m. I witnessed a total of 6-8 objects flying low from Southeast to Northwest. Non-pulstating, no sound, no contrail. At least 6 yellow/gold and one bright red in between the six in a broken pattern.

On the morning of July 22, 2012 at approximatley 12:30 a.m. I was on my deck smoking a cigarette and just happened to glance over my left shoulder. At that instant I noticed 6 yellow/gold, non pulsating objects followed by one non pulsating red object and then at least 5-6 additional yellow/gold non pulsating objects flying in a very slow semi-broken pattern. They did not ascend or descend from their flight path.

They were not hovering but were moving from the Southeast to the Northwest. Oddly enough I had time to go in the house to attempt to wake my wife to no avail and return to the deck where they were still in the same pattern headed Northwest. No hurry whatsover, No noise, No contrail.

I was able to observe them until they were no longer visable to the naked eye. What a rush !!!
