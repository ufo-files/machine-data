Star like firework like slow moving

I saw something that looked like it was a meteor emiting a trail of blue and white that looked like a firework trail. It was completely parallel to the ground and did not make any noise. It moved very slowly from N to S. When I first saw it, it just appeared and moved for about 10 secs then I just disappeared. It is possible it could be a firework or something else but I couldn’t find any rational explanation that would explain that.
