{jpg} Coming through downtown Greensburg and saw an orange light in the sky

On July 4th 2105 I was riding in a car traveling west on Otterman Street in Greensburg Pennsylvania at approximately 9:15 pm. I came up the hill where the helicopter launch pad is and looked up into the southern sky and noticed a bright orange light floating toward the southern sky over Westmoreland hospital. It floated very gently until it disappeared into the south western sky.
