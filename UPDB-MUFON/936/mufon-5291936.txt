I observed 5 orbs moving east to west on a sunny afternoon.

While taking a walk on [location removed/cak], I looked up and noticed two reddish-white orbs moving silently across a clear blue sky. One orb looked significantly higher up than the other. The orb that was moving at a lower altitude seemed to be white, but it glowed a reddish color in the sunlight. Upon zooming in on the lower orb in the video that I shot, it also appeared to be rotating slowly on a diagonal angle.

A couple of minutes after those two orbs passed out of sight behind a treeline, I observed another white orb moving east to west in the sky above a school across from the walking trail. A minute after that, another white orb flew directly over me on the trail, moving east to west as well. Upon reviewing that video, I noticed that there appeared to be a second orb sitting stationary at a much higher altitude above the one that passed over me.

In summary, I observed 5 reddish white orbs, moving east to west, for a duration of 6 minutes on a clear and sunny afternoon. I have brief video recordings of all three separate sightings, which I have attached to this report. There were other people using the trail, but nobody else seemed to notice the orbs or pay much mind to me filming the sky.
