{jpg} Very bright light turned beam to the east, then vanished

I was driving home from work in Elko on 35N. Just as I was entering Lakeville I saw to the NW of the highway an extremely bright light. I thought maybe it was a helicopter. I saw the light turn towards my right casting a beam of light. Just as im staring to see what it would do next, it vanished. Just gone. I kept searching the sky, and there was nothing.
