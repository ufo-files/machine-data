{cak} 3 lights, triangular shaped craft in sky...bright lights!

It was approximately 1115-1120 and my friends and I were sitting outside by the pool discussing fireflies when suddenly we observed a strange flash in the sky. The flash was to large to explain and had no chracteristics of modern aviation. We noticed upon the second flash (about 10 seconds later) that there were 3 lights in a triangular shape. The object seemed to be moving at break neck speeds between the flashes which moved from low to high and east to west. In all we witnessed 5 flashes and constant light with quick movement. No doubt it was a UFO, this coming from an retired member of the aviation community.
