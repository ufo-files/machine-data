Domed oval approached deer hunter; possible CE-4.

A prison employee off duty and deer hunting observed lights approaching slowlyuntil nearly overhead. He discerned an oval-shaped object with a silvery blueperimeter and dark underside. Four white lights were spaced around theperimeter. Fin-like protrusions on the bottom appeared to rotate. The object waseither silent or emitted a faint low hum. It continued on slowly, changeddirection and was soon lost from view.
