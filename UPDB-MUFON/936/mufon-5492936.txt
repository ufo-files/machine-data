{cak} Silver ball which seemed to have a small airplane tailing it

Me and a friend went outside. We where talking and I heard a airplane flying. I looked at the small airplane flying east to west when at the same time I noticed a silver sphere flying in front of the airplane in the same direction at the same speed. The ball looked higher up than the airplane and quiet a bit bigger. It looked very shiny and reflective. We didn't know what to make of it. My wife was inside playing a video game and later told me the TV got unclear with waving lines and static. I wanted to get her outside to look but just couldn't take my eyes off the sphere. I few minutes later it had flown out of sight behind the tree line. I wish we would have asked the local airport about it and reported it before now.
