Craft as big as a football field over the trees emitting lights over the city with humming noise

In 1996 I was living with my family in Medellin, Colombia (South America) I don't have the exact date. At about 10 pm I saw from my bedroom window some type of craft the size of a football field hovering above the trees on the mountainside 200 - 300 yards away. There was a low vibration humming you could feel in your chest and the craft was emitting powerful lights through what appeared like big rectangular windows that illuminated the entire sky. I called to my brother who joined me on watching this thing for a couple of minutes until it first visually disappeared then the humming stopped and it was all quiet again. We were petrified, my mom and my stepfather could not see the craft from their window but said they saw the lights in the sky and could feel the humming. A nearby electrical plant named ISA reported technical problems from that night published on the papers the next day.
