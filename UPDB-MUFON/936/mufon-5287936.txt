Noticed a blue line in the clear sky traveling from the West moving in a strait direction South Easterly then it vanished Eastery

At approximately 1:58 am on May 16 I stepped outside. It was a very nice night with a complete bright clear sky. The visibility was incredible in the sky. As I was glazing at the night sky. I could see all the stars . As I was looking up at the sky I noticed what appeared to be a blue color strait thin line in the sky . This blue line appeared to be same color as the stars. It was coming from the West at about a 75-80 degree angle up in the sky and was moving and proceeding to travel in the South Easterly direction at a constant speed. It was high up in the sky I'd say about as high as a jet plane. As I observed it moving across the horizon I was thinking that there is no way that this can be a jet plane. At this time of night there is no way light from the sun can be showing the jets trail of evaporation from the exhaust. Also if it were a jet plane the trail line of evaporation wouldn't be blue as it always appears white and it would be getting longer if that was a jet This blue line which if you held up a ruler into the sky appeared to be about 2 " long. It didn't get larger or smaller. I observed it for 2 minutes as it was moving along the horizon. I ran into the house to grab my phone to record it taking not more then 15 seconds to retrieve it. When I came back outside and looked back to the sky to where I observed it, It had vanished.
