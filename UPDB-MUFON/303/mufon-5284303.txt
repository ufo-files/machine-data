Black Isosceles triangle

I was driving home in the late afternoon. I was 20km east of Armidale in Northern NSW, Australia when I first saw a bright light low in the sky. At first I thought it was an aircraft or helicopter. It was stationary. I kept driving for another 5km watching it the whole time (and the road obviously). As I got closer to it, I realised it was no aircraft I’d ever seen (I was an international flight attendant). It had 3 light running along 1 side of it, 2 blue and 1 red. The red was in the middle of the lights and very slowly pulsating. I continued driving and passed right underneath it. At that point I said to my 4yr old “hey bunny, can you see that thing in the sky” he said “yes mummy, it looks like a spaceship”. It turned out to be isosceles triangle shape with 3 smooth rounded corners and a light in each corner ( I think they could have been green or blue). But the biggest thing we noticed was a massive light in the middle of the bottom. It was amber coloured and pulsating slowly. I wish I hadn’t been so scared and stopped to get a picture of it. It was very close.
