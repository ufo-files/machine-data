2 many events. Have a cabin on a lake 40 miles in country. See them almost every night when not cloudy.

Will keep this short or be typing all day. First time was about 3 in afternoon middle of August, clear sky, no wind. Pulled up in front of cabin and saw huge yellow ball with red ring around middle, it was hoovering with no sound. When I got out of truck it started moving very quickly at 90 degree angles, then it darted into a cloud and turned its light off. I could see the shape then, it was like an Appollo capsule only silver and only a tenth of the size when lit. Then it came out of cloud, lighted up and took off faster than my eye could follow. It was like I caught someone with they're pants down. It was one of the worst experiences I've had in my life, it felt like a surreal nightmare. I wanted to get in the truck and go home but if I did I knew I would'nt come back. My father and I built the cabin and I can't leave it. Since then I've been seeing it and others on a regular baisis. There is a light at the end of the lake that glows likes a small town in the woods, this is where they go to. Friends and famaliy have seen this. GO to youtube and bring up "ufo near central newfoundland". This is one of the videos I've taken from my cabin. If you really want to see this you're wellcome to come up for a trip.
