Bright neon Egg looking thing moving very slowly

It was mid-November, 1997-about 8:00pm at night. I was watching a movie with my girlfriend; the movie was Don King, Only in America. I went outside to pee because I was living in a remote location and I enjoyed the freedom of peeing outside at night. 

When I was peeing outside, during a break I made for myself during the movie, I noticed to my right was a very brightly illuminated green neon egg shaped object about 50 yards away from me at treetop level. It was silent, very bright and shaped like an egg on its side, not standing up. 

The color was a green light like the Christmas tree lights that people used to decorate their trees with before more modern lights. It was moving very slowly, barely moving at all. I was still in mid urination when I looked down for a split second and then I looked back up, it was brightly neon red now but the same old school neon red that was used to décor Christmas trees with lights, and it moved to my left now but still about 50 yards away. Essentially, it jumped maybe 75 yards but continued to move very slowly as it did before. I went back inside, completely freaked out after this.
