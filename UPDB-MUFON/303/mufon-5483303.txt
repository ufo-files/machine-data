{jpg} My fiance and I were sitting on the front porch and heard a strange noise

My fiance booked a trip for us to go to Lazy Lane cabins and the first night we were there, we went outside to the front porch to sit down and talk. A few minutes went by and we heard a strange noise coming from the tree line. I looked up and didn't see any airplanes or anything that could be making the noise. The tree line was shaking like there was wind blowing even though there wasn't wind blowing. We heard the noise for about 15 seconds and then the noise vanished. I have never in my entire life heard a noise like this before. It was not an airplane, helicopter or any known aircraft that I have ever personally heard.
