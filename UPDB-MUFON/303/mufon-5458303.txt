It was around November 20th 1998. Me and my mom had been hunting in the Old Town district of Galax, VA, .Grayson county.
Mom was visiting a friend that was also family to the land owner. Mom was inside visiting while I stayed outside in their driveway. It was a full moon that night and the moon was very close to the Earth. There were no clouds in the sky anywhere. i had a strange feeling that something was either wrong or unbalanced. I'm intuitive in that way. I felt an urge to look to my right, so I did. I saw nothing at ground level, so I looked up. it was then that I saw the long, tangerine-yellow colored object moving slowly and silently across the night sky in my direction. It was also flying at a low altitude. Low enough that I barely raised my head to see it broadside. i raised my binoculars to look at it closer, with no success. I then raised my hunting rifle and looked at it through my rifle scope. Through my scope I made out a couple of figures inside a well lit area inside the craft. After seeing the figures I looked for any kind of exterior marker lights, like those used on aircraft, and was not able to locate any. I then focused my hearing to try to identify any sort of noises or other sounds. i didn't hear anything except the mild breeze blowing around me. I then lowered my rifle and watched the craft with my naked eyes until it vanished into thin air. By vanished I mean: The craft was obviously on a direct flight path but, as far as I could tell, didn't complete it's pass beyond the low handing moon. The craft few to a position between myself and the moon and didn't complete the pass. It vanished at the point between myself and the moon. I looked for the craft for several minutes with my eyes, binoculars, and rifle scope and was unable to locate it.. This was my first ever encounter with a UFO and I'll always remember it.
