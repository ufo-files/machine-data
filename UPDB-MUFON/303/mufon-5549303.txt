Large, silent black triangle

My son (10 at the time)and I were at a Boy Scout Jamboree/Campout. It was held at the Rutland State Fairgrounds. During a storm, high winds started to uproot tents and everyone was trying to hold them down. I happen to look up toward the storm front approaching and saw an object slowly decend through a low cloud cover and hover. It was unaffected by the high winds and did not move. At first I thought it was a balloon or kite, but quickly realized that was impossible. After a minute or two, it slowly, and silently rose up through the cloud cover and disappeared. My son and I saw this, but everyone else was busy trying to secure their campsites. I've always been curious if this was reported by anyone else and/or have others had similar experiences.
