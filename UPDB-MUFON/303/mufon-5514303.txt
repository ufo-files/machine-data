I been observing them now on multiple occasions, they are dancing disco balls that change lights from green to blue to red to very bright light and they are multiplying day by day I can observe more and more of them ...

Well, It started a month and half ago when I seen a bright flash light flashing at me from the stars... It was flashing at me in intervals of 10 seconds ... I called my wife and we both observed it for another 2 minutes than it disappeared... 3 weeks ago we started seeing dancing disco balls only two of them but today the sky is full of them ... They are everywhere, sometimes few of them flash than they stop turn of the light than other start flashing and turn of the light ... sometimes if we are observing one of them and cloud covers it another one starts flashing brighter its like hey keep looking we are here ... Also they sometimes build formations but not trough moving trough just turning on lights yellow or similar and all of them make a arrow and at the end of the arrow there is one flashing multiple colors ... Then they turn of the lights here and there and extend the arrow than another one is pointed at that again got its lights on brighter than the others :-) It a spectacular show that can be seeing here in Spain right now around the Cartagena / Mazzaron Area ...
