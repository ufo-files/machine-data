ISS live Ufo Captured on laptop recorded on a Samsung.

On September 21st 2015 my brother and I were watching the live feed from the ISS viewing site, when passing over New-Zealeand and into darkness we viewed a bright red object appear from nowhere and started recording it on my phone. The videos are in two parts; the first one is just the red object and the second shows both the red object and rings which appear. When viewing these videos closely I found that their were multiple objects surrounding these two phenomenons; some disc shape, round, triangular all harboring interesting formations. Hope someone could analysis these to get a clearer picture.
