{nd} One light at first with a trail, falling, then seperated into 5 lights.

At 9pm I saw one falling light with an orange trail, falling straight down (I was going west on the Wonderwood and looking south over the Intercoastal river) Then the one light stopped, and formed into 5 lights, each with its own orange trail. A couple seconds later the trails stopped and then it was only the 5 bright orbs in a Z formation. Then I was at the tree line, so I lost sight, I made a u-turn at Girvin to try to keep watching but they were already gone. I felt totally awe-struck.
