(GS) There were two dark objects, one larger one smaller, moving in unison. The larger object was somewhat triangular

I was walking to my car in the parking lot between Hampton Avenue and Main Street in Sumter, SC when I looked up to the North and saw two dark objects, one larger one smaller, moving in unison. The larger object was somewhat triangular. They moved very slowly in the same direction for about two minutes before fading into light cloud cover. I first noticed it because it was moving so slowly. I found that odd and continued to look assuming it was just a plane. When I realized it was two objects then I really got curious. I thought perhaps it was a glider, but that wouldnt make since for the area, and also wouldnt explain the second smaller object in front of the larger one. I managed to record just under a minute of video, but it was very hard to see it recorded. I lost sight of the objects when they faded into the light clouds in the distance.
