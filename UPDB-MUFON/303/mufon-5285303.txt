Living creature of some sort above power lines

I think there is a thread of truth here. What I saw about 10 years ago was not a metallic object but only what I could describe is a living thing hovering just above some power lines at the local village hall whilst walking my dog. (I live in the UK). It was about 10pm at night. The power lines were about 30 feet high and this entity was hovering just above. It was translucent and green and shimmering. Its hard to explain, almost pulsating but I felt definitely not a machine but was somehow a creature of some sort. It was quite large, I can't really say but at Least 20 feet in diameter. I wrote to the local newspaper and asked if anyone else saw anything. They wrote back asking to interview me for thr paper. But I didn't want the ridicule I would get so declined. Thinking back I should have taken a photo but I just wanted to get out of there. I haven't read too many incidences that were similar but I know what I saw. Others must have seen it as it was so clear and large. The only thing I can think of is that these things are always around but we can't see them. Why did I see it I do not know. Happy to discuss If anyone else have seen something similar
