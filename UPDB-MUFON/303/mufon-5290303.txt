I witnessed a total of 3 objects on new years , take off from the north of Laval. The first was alone, then 2 took off side by side, and ascended

On Jan 1st at approx 7PM I witnessed a bright object take off , north-north-east of the building that I was in. It looked almost like a flare at first, though it took off as a conventional aircraft would on a runway only it changed its ascent rate to well over 2500 ft up per minute and almost went vertical, then disappeared into the clouds.

I called a close friend of mine, to describe what I saw, then witnessed 2 more objects of similar type take off from the same spot and do a very similar ascent profile. These two objects started side by side, in formation, then changed as they ascended to one on top of the other.

I was able to grab about 25 seconds of footage of the last 2 before they disappeared into the clouds.

The objects produced no sounds nor did they create any pyrotechnic artifacts/byproducts associated with flares, or fire works.
Being a private pilot for about 10 years, I have never seen a conventional aircraft/ Drone fly like that . As there is a small aerodrome to the north of the island of Laval . however to my knowledge , aircrafts are not permitted to take off or land from that field at night. 

Object seems to have been approx 4-5 miles away. which would put it somewhere near " Duvernay Woods" or " Equerre Woods "
Id like to note that , though this was during new years day, and there were a few displays of fireworks from celebrations that day, this did not come close to any pyrotechnical display that I have seen, where pyrotecnics change direction , and fly in formation.

Note that the video is taken from the 9th floor of a building.
