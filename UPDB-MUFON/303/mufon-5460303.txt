We were traveling south on US 54 approximately 40 to 45 miles outside Alamogordo, New Mexico. Following behind, approximately 200 to 250 ft a semi truck. Noticed something glowing in the sky to my upper left. Had to lean forward into the windshield to get a better look. At this time the truck in front of us began to slow down, I believe they may have seen what we were seeing and slowed to look. Observed a large orange glowing oval in the sky, no markings could been seen, it was just hovering. After about 15 to 20 seconds a portion of it became flat (for lack of a better description) and the orb seemed to disappear into the flat area, almost as though as it was entering something.
