{nd} Observed red orange orbs true south.

I walked towards my house and a friend had just arrived and she pointed up at the sky while asking what are those things. I first observed 4 or 5 red orbs traveling south above us and then I looked to see more of them coming from the area of the lake, which was about 2 miles to the west. They were traveling diagonally in relation to the others.Then they all traveled in a straight line due south at accelerated speed.
