In three second intervals, straight lined dim star like lights x 15

At precisely 1.30am on June 30th, I was outside my front door that faces east, when I observed a dim star like light suddenly glow and dim down again, travelling north to south . It was followed by 15 others of the exact same light, in three second intervals, all were dim at first , then suddenly glowed as if entering the earths atmosphere, then dim down again but travelling from west to east.. This is the second time within a year that I have seen exactly the same lights, but with a slightly different flight path, altho in the same directions. 
I find it curious that each light was exactly three seconds apart and suddenly glowed at exactly the same place in the atmosphere, as I see it. The first time that I saw these star like lights crossing overhead, approximately ten months ago, I tried to contact my local RAF base, but got no answer .
