{cak} Moving light

At approximately 11:10 PM on 814 2019 I witnessed a strobe in a light that consisted of a single strobe that moved from west to east in the vicinity of Broomfield Colorado. This light flashed one time and then moved approximately 2° east and then flashed again and then moved another 2° east and flashed again for a number of five times. The light resembled a commercial aircraft beacon in intensity but the difference is it was a single white flash with no subsequent red or white flash after the initial one. I observed this flash  Happened five times going from west to east in the course of 30 seconds. To give you an idea of where in the sky this was located, if you looked at the big dipper the siding was at the two stars that make up the top of the handle facing east to west and the first siding was approximately 10°  West of the western star in the handle of the Big Dipper. The last sighting of the flash was in the approximate location of the third star in the handle of the big dipper going east. This happened approximately 2310 hrs. on 814 2019
