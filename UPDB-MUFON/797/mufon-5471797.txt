{dr}string of blinking red and white lights, hovered, eventually changed direction

Sometime before 8pm, a string of flashing red and white lights were observed by a friend sitting in the drivers seat of the car. She pointed it out to the rest of us (there were 5 of us total) and we all became immediately confused and began to freak out. We were at a stoplight when this occurred. The driver in the next car lane heard us and began to look out of the window as well. We all attempted to take videos of it but couldn't get anything solid where you could see them clearly. We also have an audio recording of us all describing what we had seen exactly, but we're not sure any of it is good evdidence. The strangest part was that they all seem to be connected. At first they were in a straight diagonal line, then they began to shift into a different shape. We observed them for maybe about 10-15 minutes. We could still see them in the sky, just further away. There were other much bigger lights in the sky as well, but we could clearly tell they were planes, where the other ones we had no idea of what they could possibly be. None of us have ever seen anything like it. I checked the most recent sightings on here as soon as I could and was shocked to see there were similar sightings in our area. It's comforting to know we weren't the only ones who saw it, however we are all still pretty spooked.
