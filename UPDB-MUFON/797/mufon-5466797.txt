Metallic object, stationary next to flight path of commercial air traffic, not a star as went infront of cloud, didn't move with the clouds so not a balloon - captured on video and photo (from mobile phone), 4 eyewitness, 2 separate locations

We were enjoying the sunshine in the garden (not drinking). As we live under the flight path of Heathrow airport I like to watch the large passenger planes on a clear day. I looked up to the east and noticed a flash of light. It looked out of place as it was clearly not moving. At first it looked clear as a cylindrical object reflecting the sun. I text my sister who lives a few roads away and she caught the object on her phone camera (picture & video), it very slowly moved to the north east (in a different direction to the clouds), the sighting lasted about 1 hour when the object disappeared. Me, my father, my sister and her husband saw the object. Multiple planes flew past the object (their port side, further to the north).
