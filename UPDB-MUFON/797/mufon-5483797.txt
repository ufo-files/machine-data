Then, others orbs, and maybe 20 min later, a "flying saucer" with different colored lights, pulsing like a garland from left to right, and the saucer went from north to south, above the same building, and disappearing behing too (couldn't witness more because of that frickin' building :) ).

The one I saw the closest was the 1st and the 2nd one. Not more than a mile I think. And very low in the sky. 
After all that, I also saw something looking like a really big shooting star but low in the sky, like maybe it even went between the building and mine, very quickly, going up through the whole visible sky. Maybe someone inside but too quick to really see what it was!

I was amazed by all this. I never witnessed something this close and this real-looking!
