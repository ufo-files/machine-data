{dr}White/silver reflective UFO

I was just coming home from being out on a drive with my husband and son, it was around 7:30pm, the sun was setting but the sky was still light and it was clear. As we parked the car I noticed this white streak in the sky just sitting stationary. Im a constant sky observer, so at first I thought it was a chem trail line. But then I realized the line wasnt getting any longer. The fact that this line was so bright it seemed as if it was a fresh chem trail, but when it didn't get any bigger I realized right away this was a craft. I ran inside to get my camera, which took all of 1 minute, by the time I ran back out it had moved considerably farther away. At first the "line" from my distance was like the length of a foot, when I came back out it was 3/4 less in size. I scrambled to take a picture, which isnt great because of its distance by that point. Right after I took the picture I saw it fade off to the point where it was no longer visible to me. The craft must have been a disc or a cigar shape, because of the fact that to me it just looked like a streak in the sky. Im not sure what the color was it looked white or silver-ish, it definitely looked like it was either illuminated or reflecting the light from the setting sun. When I was observing it from my point of view, I could see that it must have been clearly visibly from downtown Stamford. The picture that I have was taken right before it disappeared from my line of sight. Its not the greatest photo, and I am sure people will argue that its just a cloud or a random spot in my photo. But we all know it was a UFO. :)
