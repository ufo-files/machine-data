{jpg} multiple white orbs appearing, hovering and largely moving in a northerly direction

On Oct 9th 2017 I was house sitting for a friend at her home in NW Portland uptown hills right above Westover. I was locking up the house around 10 pm. The fron of the house has an unobstructed view of the entire west side and east side of Portland. Essentially looking directly at Mt. Hood. I noticed multiple bright lights hovering over the east side of Portland.  There was a stationary diamond which looked like it was hovering somewhere between Mt tabor and the river. Multiple white discs were in view, some stationary others moving right to left and appearing seemingly out of nowhere. I got my phone and started filming. I filmed them appearing, moving etc. I filmed one moving from just to the left of the diamond all the way to the airport. I filmed for about seven minutes before going to bed. I got a couple still too, one shows the outline of a classic triangle ship. I went to the MUFON meeting in Portland last night (3/10) and gave my videos and stills to the leader, so he already has them. Also, last year I was in a Broadway cab and somehow the subject came up and the driver said not only did he see them that night but several of his co-workers did too.
