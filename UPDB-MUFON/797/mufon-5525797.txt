white circle very high se sky above clouds moving in circles, behind clouds

in downtown howell for farmers market. standing on county courthouse grounds, partly cloudy day with cotton ball clouds at about 5-8000 ft. high stratus clouds most likely around 20,000 ft. at 11:55am (i checked cell phone for time after i realized what i saw) i looked up checking clouds out and saw white disc traveling in circles. i first thought that seagull was extremely fast and high, then i noticed that it was disc, and it went behind stratus "mares tails" and then back into blue sky at least three times, then flew away or i lost it. total time of sighting was 12-20 seconds. looked for another 10 mins but did not find it. reminded me of you-tube video of "mothership" from london, but higher. thought all night whether i should make report, but i did see what i report. it was not bird, i saw birds and they were noticeably lower and darker, this almost "glowed" white.
