I saw a head of what I believe was an alien

My husband and I sitting in a partially lit family room watching TV. I happened to turn my head to look at my sliding glass door. I immediately saw a head looking at me. Just a head. We made eye contact, then the head slowly turned sideways and disappeared. It was a deep blue, like indigo color. The eyes were slanted (not big eyes) in a dirty yellow color. I could barely distinguish a nose but no lips. It happened quickly so could not see a body. A human, even a very slim person could not stand there as our barbecue is pushed up against the door.
There was no noise. I quickly hit mute to hear for foot steps on our deck.
About 1 minute went by before I told my husband I was getting up to open the slider to see if I saw anything. I was a frightened but more curious.
I saw nothing. 
It was mid November so very dark outside. Next morning I went out to see if I saw footsteps.
I know what I saw. I’m not into scary movies, I’ve not ever experienced anything like that.
As the months went by, I have become more frightened but not enough where it affects my daily life. I can walk outside in the dark. 
Also, as the months have gone by I get the impression it wanted me to see it. I also believe it most likely won’t come back.
After, the much increased sightings of UFO’s I felt that I needed to tell someone other than my family. I actually find my experience fascinating!
