Two sets of three rectangular bright yellow-orange lights that blinked on in a sequence twice then disappeared.

I was walking on DeBordieu Beach near Georgetown, SC, at around 9 p.m. on July 19, 2010. I stopped to look at the ocean and to my right over the water at about the level a small plane would fly, I saw two sets of very bright, rectangular shaped yellow-orange lights which appeared, blinking on in a sequence of three two times. The first time they blinked on, the two sets were next to each other, separated by a space about equal to how big they appeared. Then there was a 1-2 second gap, and then the lights appeared again, blinking on in a sequence, but this time separated by a space about three times as big as before. Then they blinked off, leaving an empty sky. The whole sighting lasted about 5 or 6 seconds. I saw two people down the beach and walked over to them to see if they had seen the same thing. They had and were excited and mystified, as I was. I watched for another 30 minutes or so and walked back to the house. I told my brother and sister-in-law about the lights. They told me they had seen them too from our deck overlooking the ocean.
