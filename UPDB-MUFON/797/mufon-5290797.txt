7-9 Bright objects seen in the distance in a straight line, 2 objects glowed brighter, disappeared

driving west on east Elizabeth street and coming to a stop at the stop sign on Stover street, I saw 7-9 very bright lights in a line low in the sky to the west. after pulling over to the side of the street right after pulling through the intersection, I stopped and got our of my car, crossing the street to get a better view, two of the objects grew brighter and then in seconds, all of the lights dimmed and disappeared. The lights were initially brighter than any of the planets (Jupiter and Saturn were still visible to the left of the formation of the lights). the whole sighting from when I first saw the lights at the intersection, pulled through and parked, got out of the car and saw them glow and fade was probably between 20-30 seconds. I have included a drawing of the position I saw them in the sky and indicated which of the line of objects grew brighter before they all faded
