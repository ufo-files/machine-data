(GS) observing a planned ISS sighting from spot the station

I receive texts from NASAs program called spot the station. This was my second sighting. At 6;30 PM, this evening(12-11-12) my husband and I were in the front yard to watch for the ISS. This time it was expected at 6;40pm moving from the SW.
It was to be sighted at 48 degrees and disappear SW. This text confused me as I wasnt sure how it could appear SW and disappear SW. 
At exactly 6;39 we spotted it moving from the S to SW. but lower on the horizon than expected. There were several planes moving in different directions as well, and there were very faint clouds low on the SW horizon, otherwise it was very clear. Excited once again that we were watching the ISS...we were about to go back into the house when another white orb appeared from the area we were watching and began to trec North.
it was coming right towards us. Star like orb. white, moving slowly, very simular to the ISS. No blinking lights, just as expected. But this new direction confused us. Just as we were trying to figure out if the SW sighting was or wasnt the ISS...the second orb, now, right over our heads,
vanished before our eyes. No clouds to cover it.... just gone. I had asked my husband if the ISS can roll over so we couldnt see its light??? But it was gone. My husband and I returned to the house, thinking wed lost our marbles. I was so haunted by this, I decided to look on the net for any other news to see if this matched up with anyone elses experiences...and found your reporting web-site. I am not usually someone who doubts myself, but wondered if you receive any other reports about anything simular, I sure would like to know.
