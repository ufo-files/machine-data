{rjl} Unknown lights ascending, disapearing, reappearing in original location; repeated multiple times..

Multiple lights continued to originate above the tree light, ascend diagonally upward at approximately 45 degrees, then disappear only to have new lights reappear in the original location and follow the same procedure. At times, originating lights were extremely closed to one another before separating at 45 degrees and approximately 20 degrees, respectively. This occurred for approximately an hour with 10-15 light occurrences appearing during this time. The lights had no discernible flashing markers indicating aircraft and there was no sound.
