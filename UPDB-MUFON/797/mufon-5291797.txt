Craziest thing I’ve ever seen

I was with a friend at lake point state park in Eufaula Alabama we were down at the dock and were looking at the stars. It was a crystal clear night. I said oh look a shooting star then it stopped and went up down all around. I witnessed this for hours. There were too many to count it literally looked like they were doing battle up there. Lots of different colors they would appear and then disappear. The craziest thing I have ever seen!!! I had to take videos because people would not believe what I saw. My friend mike is willing to talk about it as well. I’m going back beginning of October and hope to god I get to see that again. No major airports are around and it was amazing.
