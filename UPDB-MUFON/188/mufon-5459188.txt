{cak} An strange shaped object in the night sky

We were driving Around dehsorkh road near Mashhad in kho rasa now province. It’s the way back from koohsorkh (red mountains). In the blank sky we saw some strong lights which were a bit unclear at first then started to rotate extremely fast while decreasing the height. After a second the lights went extremely bright with white color and then disappeared from sight instantly. It was triangular shape with it’s sharp edge facing us with lights at each point which had very bright light with a less bright golden light at the back. It was very huge and bright that car behind and in front of us stopped in order to observe it. The photos I could take with my tablet in those little seconds have low quality but can serve as proof to my sighting I believe.
