{cak} 3 orb like objects changing their flight paths to creat shapes while changing colors. Landed in my field about ten years ago. Other witness saw the same thing. Changed our lives.

My friend was taking me home after school in around this time 2005. We are about a mile from my house when I see an orb in the sky. I thought I was just seeing things when we went into a small patch of woods. When we came out I looked back and now there were 3 orbs in the sky in a triangle. I told my friend to stop the car. We were across the field from my house at the time. We are now both watching these 3 orbs changing colors and moving together changing shapes, moving in patterns with each other making very fast direction changes. Then they hovered in a triangle over my field and descended. I almost broke into tears. I did not want to go home at all costs. Then the orbs ascended and changed forms for about another minute or so and took off with unimaginable speed. I did not want to go home afraid of what I would find but my friend reassured me that we had too. When we got back everything was normal. A few days later I checked the field for a potential landing spot and found a few laid down spots of weeds possibly deer beds. What we saw that day will affect both of us for ever. I remember the sighting like it was yesterday. This is the first time I came public with this story because I hear similar stories and hope we can figure out what actually is happening. Thank you for listening.
