{cak} Cigar shaped object with row of red lights along the side moving slowly at low altitude

I went outside my home to place materials in the recycling bin and noticed a cigar shaped object moving slowly from right (south) to left (north). It was mainly noticeable because there was a straight row of evenly spaced red lights running horizontally the length of the craft. It moved slowly, disappeared, and reappeared a couple of times. I tried taking photos with my cell phone but it kept flipping into self portrait mode and I got no shots of the object. From the photos I know the time was 7:06 PM. The object appeared to be about a mile or so from my home, to the East, which is in the direction of the airport. My guess is that it was about 100 feet long. There were four or more lights, all the same color and size. The lights were not very bright. The surface of the object was dark and dull. It was visible for about one minute, half of which time I spent trying to get a photo, the other half watching it. It finally just disappeared from view. It was moving more slowly than an average airplane or helicopter, and disappeared or blinked out a couple of times. Because of the shape and multiple red lights, I didn't think it was a conventional aircraft and thought it might be a UFO. I was surprised to see it and my excitement may have played a role in not being able to get a photo. I didn't think much about the event until the next day when I looked at my photos and realized they were either black (no image) or self portraits of my face in the dark with lights reflected on my glasses. The total distance the object traveled was about the width of my fist when held at arm's length. It was just above tree top level from what I could tell.
