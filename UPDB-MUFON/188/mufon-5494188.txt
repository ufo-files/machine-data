Vortex in the clouds above where I was staying.

On Christmas Eve I was at my ex father in law's house in rural farmland of Ilmajoki watching lights over the direction of Sein1joki from the upstairs bedroom window. I decided to go outside to take a better look at what I was seeing. It was a cold night with snow on the ground. As I was viewing the lights I looked up at the clouds above the house and saw a vortex, a circle with the clouds churning inward with a hole in the middle up towards the heavens above. I have never seen an anomaly like this before or since. It continued for well over 90 minutes plus until I could not take the -20c any longer. I am reporting this only because of your recent program concerning sky people and it is something that has been bothering me for some time. What did I see and how does it contribute to your investigations?
