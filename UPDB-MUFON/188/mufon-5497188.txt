{rjl} Driving home. Spotted a line of orbs in a formation: 4 near eachother, one tailing further behind.

The 3 of us were returning home from a shopping and witnessed very bright and glowing orbs. There were 5, 4 in a formation of one behind the other, and another further behind trailing them but it was an identical type. We initially considered radio towers or planes, but planes flash and don't go that slow, and towers don't fly and hover. The orbs then spread out over the hills after passing over houses and literally dissolved into the air. Two remained 10 minutes later when we stopped at a cornfield to observe. I myself have had a long fascination with aliens and the UFO phenomena, so this was a real eye-catcher. We all saw it, and I plan on inquiring at the local air-force bases if they had 5 vehicles on routine drills flying that low and slow due East at this time of night which I highly doubt. I want to know what they were and I've had sightings before but I just had a feeling this was it. They hovered and moved very very slow. Too slow for planes. I have pictures and videos but they are difficult to distinguish. However, two other people saw them as well. We lost sight after 3 dissolved and 2 flew behind the treeline so we never saw sight of it again.
