(GS) Acted like an R/C plane/helicopter with multiple LEDs

I was out walking at around 10 pm Michigan time when I saw an unknown object to the the WestSouthWest. It acted like an R/C plane/helicopter with multiple LEDs of different colors, changing color every so oftenand moving horizontally in both directions. It made one dive and continued to move horizontally again. It then descended below the treetops so I couldt see it anymore.

About an hour after the event, a helicopter with, I think, one red light, flew over.
