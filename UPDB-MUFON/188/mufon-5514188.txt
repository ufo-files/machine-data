{cak} White, larger than a quarter coin-like size, straight angle-like path then disappeared within about 2.5 secs.

I was outside with my best friend taking my dog out for a quick bathroom break. While I was in the middle of talking to my friend, I happened to notice something at the corner of my eye. I thought at first it was a shooting star. But it wasnt. It was a quick 2.5 second angled straight-line path in the sky. I litterally gasped and point in an angle NW above my friends head. I happened to notice but my friend was exactly a second too late at that time. I got over excited about it and jumped in the air because I have never really got to see anything like it before in my life. I lost site of the object because it disappeared into thin air pretty quickly as soon as my friend turned around.
