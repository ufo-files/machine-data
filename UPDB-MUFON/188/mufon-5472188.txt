{rjl} 3 lights total or perhaps all the same one changing directions. Very high up.

I was standing outside besides the house. It was night time and I didn't observe any clouds. I looked up again and noticed a star like light moving a short distance from the North to the South. It had no blinking red lights so at first I thought it was a satellite. However, I have seen a satellite before and it was much smaller and moved at a pretty decent clip. This light was slower. I than thought it might be the International Space Station.

Within 10 seconds the light went out. I scanned the sky thinking it went behind high clouds but never saw it again. Next, near where the first light disappeared, another slightly smaller light reappeared traveling the opposite direction. It traveled slowly, pick up speed and shot out of visibility.

A third light appeared. It was traveling perpendicular to the first two.
It seemed to travel a short distance as if slightly descending; that light then faded out.
