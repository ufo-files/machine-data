{nd} metallic in color my wife got scared

it was sunday evening the Viking game had just ended my son was 6 years old and wanted to change channels and watch walt Disney,we had a picture window facing the lake just when he changed the channel something come from the north over the lake,my son said what is that dad I said I'm not sure.i called for my wife she was in the kitchen I told her what I saw looked out at the lake and did not see it.she said look up there I glanced up and told her I was going to get the camera she got scared and closed the drapes and did not want me to go outside.we called the police station they had not received any reports,we had a small airport they had saw nothing but they were 17 miles away.i was stationed at Miramar navy airbase 1962&1963 worked around all types of aircraft never have I saw anything like this I am getting older wanted to tell someone else my wife and I still talk about it ever once in awhile I know whati saw sometimes people laugh and sometimes they listen.
