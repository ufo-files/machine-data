{nd} Bright light fell into a trailing position behind a commercial aircraft

Noticed a bright light that looked to be as bright as aircraft landing lights coming from the south southwest heading north northeast. As I watched the light it dimmed slightly a couple of times as it reached 90 degrees to me it did not act like regular lights. As I continued watching the light move north northeast it changed direction toward east northeast. At that time I noticed an aircraft moving away moving from me in the northeast direction. The light moved into a trailing position behind the aircraft. As I watched the light and aircraft travel northeast the aircraft disappeared behind some trees at the same time the light went out.
