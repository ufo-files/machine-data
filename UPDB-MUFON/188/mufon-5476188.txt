Bright light triangular shapes from a distance hovering not far from the ground

I had woken from a dream whereby my 7 month old baby was being pulled by an unseen force. She was also unsettled at the time. I got up to go to the bathroom and noticed a very bright light pulsating, hovering and moving around. It was quite low in the sky and not in a normal place of a star or planet. I watched it for about 30 minutes trying to work out if it was a star or some such. It was quite scary considering the dream I had just woken from the light was so bright there was no way it was a star possibly a planet buy there isn't normally one in this position. It looked a possible triangle shape from where I was stood but it was probably a couple of miles away from me. I took a picture on my phone with the flash off through the glass I had to zoom in on the picture as much as the phone would allow to see the shape there. The night was very clear the light must have been very bright for my phone to pick it up with no flash and also not catch any of the stars. I went into another room to Google planets for approx one minute went back to the window and it had gone roughly the same time a house close by to it had switched on their lights
