UFO captured in digital photo over Carr Park in Glendale, CA

I was taking photos early evening in Carr Park in Glendale, CA. The park is right next to my apartment building. I was experimenting taking night photographs of trees, etc. I took one photo looking directly up in the sky. I saw nothing when I took the photo, but when I was looking through the photos later I saw what appears to be a UFO in the sky.
