{cak} Friend and I driving see object then come under large triangle ufo hovering, pass under slowly and then it rises turns and in a flash disappears all with no sound

I was in passenger seat of car with my friend driving. We were in a heavily wooded area, but frequently travelled, not in the middle of no where. The road was hilly and we saw an object about half mile or so away and thought it was a strange place for a plane. This was at night. As we drove we noticed it seemed to be getting bigger and not moving. We were pretty puzzled and actually joked that it was probably a UFO! As we cleared more hills it became apparent that it was indeed something VERY large. We slowed down as we came up to it. We both started saying Oh my god, it is a Ufo. It was hovering over the trees right above the road we were on. We came up on what I only assume was the back.  It was triangle shaped but not sharp angles, smooth. It had lights underneath, but it was so close to the ground we couldnt see how high or if there were lights in front. It took us 10 secs to pass under and when we came out from under it, it rose high up ,turned and was gone in 2 secs.
