{rjl} Witnessed slow moving bright lights above treeline moving towards house

Witnessed bright lights moving towards house just above the treeline. Saw it coming, thought it was an airplane. Went upstairs to get laundry. When I came down, noticed it was still coming so I decided to go outside. I didn't hear anything. Just as I walked behind my car and looked up to get a better view behind a magnolia tree, it was directly above me! I took a step back and took a deep breath. It was huge! Startled by how close it was and completely silent. Dwarfed my two story farmhouse. Easily over half the length of a football field. It had 4 white lights that didn't blink. Watched it move down the field to the trees and out of sight.
