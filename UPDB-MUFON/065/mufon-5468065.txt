Black, 15 meters long side. Rectangular, similar in aspect to a refrigerator.

It was June of 2010. Warm sunny day, early afternoon, blue skies, a few high cloud wisps. Was returning to Langford after a delivery and stuck in traffic on the trans Canada northbound, waiting in the lineup of traffic near the light at the Tillicum intersection. Apparently hovering above Tillicum mall was a black rectangular object that had the aspect of a refrigerator, 15 meters on the long side. Featureless otherwise. At first I thought it was a balloon being tethered at the mall, although at approximately 300-400 meters elevation it seemed a little high. At the time I realized there were no appliance stores at the mall, I noticed the object was moving and rotating, albeit very slowly, almost imperceptibly. It was only because I had been watching for several minutes that it's movement became apparent. The movement was deliberate and did not appear to be caused by wind. The driver, who wishes to remain anonymous, also viewed this object and was baffled. After about 5 minutes the object had moved closer to us and still just a black rectangle, smooth and featureless. Then it shot off towards the Olympic mountains of Washington state at an unbelievable speed. My window was down, no sound, no boom, it accelerated smoothly and easily, effortlessly. It was out of sight within 3 seconds, and I estimate it's velocity, before it went out of sight, at 3-5 Km/sec. I've spent 20 years in the military, taken courses in observation, and spent countless hours at lookout. I have never seen, nor are aware of any vehicle of this design or capability.
