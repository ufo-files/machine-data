{jpg} Silver gray sphere

`While bicycle riding on the Burke-Gilman Trail in Kenmore WA. with a friend my rear tire blew out. There just happened to be a repair shop a short distance away and we took my bike there. While waiting for the tech to do the repair we went outside and while talking we looked up and saw a somewhat large (20-30 ft.) silver gray sphere in the sky. It was at least 500 ft. up and just hovered, moving slightly. It wasn't perfectly round but wasn't an acorn or egg shape either. The sky was cloudless and there were no tall buildings and a few tall trees nearby. We commented on it to each other and pondered what it could be. We ruled out it being a balloon of any kind because there was no noticeable tether and the bottom was smooth with no attachment point. We continued talking and occasionally looked up at it for five to ten minutes and suddenly it was gone. We had a unobstructed view of the sky and it was no where. The object was near the flight path of Kenmore Air, a seaplane service, which gave us a size and altitude reference. Planes fly by regularly. 
I saw a similar object 6 or 7 years later in the University District in Seattle. I watched it hover for several minutes, looked away for a moment looked back and it was gone.
