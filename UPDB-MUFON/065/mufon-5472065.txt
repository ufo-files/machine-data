Dark square with orange orb in the center

My wife and I had just finished eating and were on our way back to our room when she noticed an odd looking orange light in the sky.We thought maybe that it was a satalite because it was moving in a straight line and moving about the same speed as a satilite. It was moving silently toward us and the light was pulsating. Once it was more or less over head we could see that it was a square shaped object with an orange light centered on the bottom.There were low clouds (approx. 500-1000 ft) in the area that evening and the object was below them. It was exciting to see something so unusual and when we got back to the room I made notes regarding the unusual sighting. We lost sight of the object due to buildings obstructing our view.
