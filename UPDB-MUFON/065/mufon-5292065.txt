EGG LIKE SHIP SEEN AND THEN CHANGED SHAPE

I was sitting on the back porch smoking. Then a ship floated down between my neighbor's trees. I was within maybe 30 feet from it. It was shaped like an egg. You could see that the main part was silver. It also had three rows of what looked like upside down diamonds. The top row was Yellow, Middle was Pink and the bottom was Green. I sat there and watched it go between our two houses. Then I ran thru the house to the front door to see where it went. I saw nothing. About 5 minutes later I had to go to the Mall to pick up my granddaughter. I kept looking in the sky to see if I could find it. As I approached Jennifer Road I noticed a yellow, pink and green thing in the medium strip by the hospital. It was three triangles rotating around themselves. Nothing touched the ground. It was at least 15-20 feet in height. I worked for the hospital at that time and knew that that should have not been there. Thinking that it would still be there, I continued to get her from work. But it was not there. I called a couple of co-workers to see if there was any talk around the hospital. All replies were NO. But it did happen. Now whenever I sit on the porch I just keep looking up.
