Another bright light high in the night sky...

My wife and I reported a sighting in the North Eastern sky over Yucaipa on June 24, 2009. Since that report I have seen the same thing happen twice again. I smoke a pipe in the evening, so I am out on my porch a few times each night between 8pm and 11pm. Three times now I have seen this small white speck that looks at first like a satellite high in the night sky that suddenly glows to a brilliant whitish blue like a helicopter spotlight then dims again and moves off away from us. I have considered that it may be some sort of energy flash from an actual satellite when it is repositioned or something...but I'm not sure that there is such a satellite out there. I have also considered it may be a very large solar panel that is reflecting the sun...but it is always at a different time and the last time I say it it was traveling in the opposite direction from the first time. We don't know what it is, but it is unusual. Anyone have an idea?
