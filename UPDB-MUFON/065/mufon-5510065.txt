My friend "C" and I were walking down the alley way between Garden and Mansion Streets. We were about a hundred feet from my house. I was walking him to his house. We joked with each other as we walked, then "C" said something that I didn't like, so I began chasing him. He hid under a box truck which was parked outside of the warehouse near by. It was night, so I wasnt going to follow him. Who knows what kind of animals were crawling around under the truck.

I waited for a minute and looked skyward and sighed in anger.

When I looked, I saw what I call a "Flying Carousel" I saw a pattern of multi-colored lights. The light moved around the object like Christmas chaser lights, only slower. 

I yelled for "C" and said.

""C", Im not [Item Moved/CMS/nd] around, come out here and look at this."

"C" emerged from under the truck and stood with me in the alley and we both watched the lights move northward until it was too far away we couldnt see it anymore. It didn't appear to have any structure only a circular pattern of lights.
