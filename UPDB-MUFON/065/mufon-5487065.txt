{cak} Larger then star, unidentified glowing white and blue unmoving object.

I was getting something out of the trunk of my car and happened to look up to observe the stars. Eyes were caught by a larger than star like object directly below the constellation Orion. Curiously observed for a few minutes - it was glowing unnaturally white and blue. Did not move at all. Went inside to tell spouse... went back outside and we both witnessed it in the same spot. Periodically checked on over a 30 minute period and it is still there, unmoving, but still glowing. Airplane passed by but did not seem to pay attention to object. Told friend in another city who was also able to spot it and agreed it seemed unnatural.
