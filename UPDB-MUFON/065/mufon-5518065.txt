Noticed object in the night sky after exiting my car...

Got out of my car... felt something over my left shoulder - looked then looked up. Object hovered, then it nosed up, went left, straightened out and silently moved from my left to right. Chevron shape, 2 lights on each wing(?) one light at tip of chevron shape. I watched it for a couple of minutes until it faded into the night sky.
