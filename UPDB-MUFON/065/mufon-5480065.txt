As the weather has begun to become enjoyable I decided to take my lunch break outside, as I often do. My partner was on his way so I was just finishing up when I looked above the two buildings i was sitting behind, the business next door and the building I work at. Reveling in the delicious meal I had just devoured I threw my head back and noticed it. I thought that Airplane looks strange. I stared a bit longer to notice it had no wings. I immediately stood up and took notice. From my vantage point it was traveling along the building top and was about to disappear. I could also see another plane way further in the distance that like normal had vapor trail behind it and the thing i was looking at didnt. the more I looked at it the more I could tell it just didnt fit. It also seemed Hazy or even blurry and though there wasnt any kind of wing there was the sun glinting along the entire body of the craft. On this particular day I did not have my smart phone and as I held up my burner phone to work out a way to get a photograph, the object without a sound Blasted into the sky. It was so quick, it was as if it were never there at all. Just a blink and it was gone.
