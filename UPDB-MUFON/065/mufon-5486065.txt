{nd} Witnessed a silent,low flying object that was distinguished by 5 points of light that made up triangle. Flew low and rapid.

Returned home from getting a late night meal around 1:30am and before entering my house I decided to look at the sky given it was a crisp clear night without any real cloud formation to obstruct my view. After about 5 minutes of gazing I was starting to head in and was stopped dead in my tracks to see what I thought at first was a shooting star. As i focused in i could very clearly see that this object was not a star but rather it was 5 stars that made up the silhouette of a rather large and low flying triangle. It came into view traveling from my NW and continued towards my SE. This object was traveling incredibly fast and to my astonishment, no sound!! I couldn't see any surface characteristics which made me feel as if this object was transparent. The lights that made up the triangle were exactly the same size and brightness with only 2 visible on the crafts left side and three on the right. This object seemed to be flying very low, although without any frame of references I can't really know definitively if it was either a huge craft flying high or smaller craft flying low. If my assumptions on size are correct this object was easily 100-200 feet in size. It was completely silent and I watched it travel across the sky for a total of 10/15 seconds before I lost it behind my rough. It was nothing like I've ever seen before and I've spent many hours gazin at the sky and feel I have a very healthy dose of skepticism and this event really astounded me and further validated my belief in UFOs from either us or extraterrestrials.
