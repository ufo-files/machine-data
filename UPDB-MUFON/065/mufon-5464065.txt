Cylindrical and vertical, bright white while changing between ruby red and orange

It was 7:06 pm when I got to the top of Haisla Hill and I saw a white light (as bright as a vehicles headlight) moving from east to west at a fast pace, it slowed down and came to a complete stop. I noticed that it was(a general) cylindrical shape and constantly vertical and it began to alternate from bright white to ruby red and orange while continuing to move east in a slow manor. While it was alternating between the 3 colours it descended to some houses behind a tree line and temporarily out of my sight. After it slowly got above the tree line I decided to get closer and walked to a parking lot to watch it more, it slowly ascended for approximately 2 minutes and began to move east while still alternating between the 3 colours. After 9 minutes of watching it, it went east over multiple streets and fading in brightness. At the end of watching it the object began to alternate between green and blue, at this time it was 7:15 pm and I went to tell family what I had seen. Since it went of multiple streets I do not know if there are any other witnesses. I do have a short video of this experience but my phone is old and the camera began to malfunction some time ago, about half of the recording can be easily seen but then it malfunctions. After the experience I saw (what I believe to be) a helicopter moving from south to north, perhaps the object continued to the north? I1m not sure. That was at 7:45 pm.
