{cak} it was like a ball of light hovering mere inches above ground then as i got closer it flew off at incredible speed

i was about 16 years old walking home from a friends house at aprox. 12-1 am i was only 5 mins from my house near an old cotton gin in my neighborhood i was walking down the street my uncle lived on and looking at the ground like i always do but i saw a bright light in ahead of me so i looked up when i did i saw a giant light sitting inside the fence of the cotton gin which has been closed since before i was born and had no trespassing signs posted out front so i knew it wasnt just some kids cause if it were the cops would be there in no time i myself have had them called on me when i went in there late at night and i didnt have a giant light with me so then i thought maybe it was a helicopter but dismissed that idea quickly because there was no noise and when i say none i mean absolutely none not even a cricket and where i live you cant escape the sound of crickets or dogs ever i wasnt afraid i was just curious so i kept walking when i was about 20 feet away the ball of light that i was staring at ascended aprox. 40-60 feet in the air slowly when this happened i stopped moving i was to in awe of what i was seeing it was just pure light that was all i could see no structure or materials just light and no sound i found that very odd even a bright outdoor work light gives off a humming sound but this huge light was giving off no sound at all this light had to be at least 10 feet in diameter with no visible structure and it wasnt like it was an object covered in lights the light seemed to be coming from one source at the center of the ball of light once it reached about 60 feet in the air it started to move in strange ways like it was bouncing off of nothing back and forth up and down side to side almost like if you took a bouncy ball and put it into a bigger clear ball and shook it and after a few secs it shot off in the direction of orions belt at incredible speed
