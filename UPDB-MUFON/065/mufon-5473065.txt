{cak} Thought it was a drone, but it didn't fly like one and dissappeard really fast.

Sitting on a couch looking out the door on a sunny day. I was facing Lockheed and had a perfect view of planes taking off. Planes usually don't take off during the day here and it was 1pm. I noticed what I thought was a drone hovering over the trees at the house next door. I said to my two friends Hey there is a drone. It hovered over the trees and I said again, do you see it? Just then my friend on the right of me stood up and said where? I said right there, over the trees. I too stood up and followed him outside. As I did this, the drone shot straight up and was gone by the time I got outside-about 10 steps. I realized the drone wasn't flying like a turtle, with four legs under it, it was like a turtle walking upright, like a cross, as it hovered. Maybe drones do this now, I'm not sure, but it disappeared in a second-faster than a plane.
