{cak} Driving home tonight I saw a hovering object that appeared to be too high for a drone (it disappeared behind the clouds) yet too stationary to be a plane.

At approximately 6:05 I was driving south down Stillwater avenue on my way home when I looked up and saw a cigar shaped object hovering high in the sky. I immediately pulled out my phone and started to record. There was no condensation trail, nor did there appear to be wings, though the object did appear cigar shaped like a fuselage of an airplane. It appeared to be hovering and traveling slowly N/NE. Bangor has height restrictions with drones, due to the international airport, which is why this struck me as odd when it disappeared behind a cloud. There was no rapid movements, just a slow drift above the clouds and it was not seen again. This was witnessed by myself, my fianc1 and my daughter.
