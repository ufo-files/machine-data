Orbs seen from delta flight

This is the second encounter I have seen this was on a delta flight from Atlanta to Fayetteville nc before my decent I was staring out the window and noticed two really bright orb light circles. One orb was a little higher than the other was before decenting towards the other. I was pretty much in a aww light state before I took the photo and video the objects got closer to each other and it looked like it came together right before our decent into Fayetteville airport… the picture I took I zoomed in and observed two perfect circles still glowing around the objects u can clearly see two different orb light balls coming together…After we landed I waited for the pilots to come out and I asked the pilot if he had seen the same thing I saw but I just got a grin like look before walking away the co pilot never stopped walking … very odd deal ..our flight was the last flight in Fayetteville that night we landed I got onto my flight tracker app just to see if there was any aircraft in the area at that time and I didn’t observe any aircraft flying at least for a few hundred miles from us.
