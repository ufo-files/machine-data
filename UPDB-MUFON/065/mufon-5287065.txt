I saw a bright white object, directly to my right about 50 feet off the road, through my side window, in side mirror, saw object enter road behind me

While driving to work in Flint early in the morning at 3:30, traveling NE at 45-50mph, along Linden Rd, at approximately ½ mile past the intersection fork of Old Linden Rd to the north. I saw a bright white object, directly to my right about 50 feet off the road, through my side window. Its shape was rectangular the size of the back of my hand with out-stretched fingers at arms length. It appeared as a rectangular glow with 4 bright white lights at the corners, observation time of about 1 second as I only noticed it off the road to my right suddenly.

As I continued on down the road, I saw the object in my right-hand side mirror, move to the center of the road immediately behind me but not following me. It was bright orange now, flame shaped, 20 feet tall. As it entered the road it immediately shot upward and disappeared. I was about a ¼ mile down the road when it disappeared as I did not stop. Total observation time 2 minutes.

Later on that day, after work, I returned to the same area along the road and found a knocked down circular swirl pattern of swamp reeds in a swamp area just off the road at about the distance away that the object was. But I felt this location was closer to last residents passed by (12106 Linden Rd) than I thought I observed the bright lighted object but the off the road distance was about right.
