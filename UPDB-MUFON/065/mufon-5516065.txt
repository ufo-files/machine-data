White light moving East to West. Suddenly the light expanded and contracted then disappeared. The light was totally contained within the circular shape.

My husband had called me out to our deck which is on the South side of our house because he was looking at a moving light. I saw the light moving North, went in the house for a few seconds and when I came out it appeared that it was now moving Southeast. He said it had changed direction suddenly but the speed was the same. Because of that, I continued to look at the sky and in the South I saw a light moving from West to East. It looked like a satellite to me. Within a few seconds, it expanded then contracted to a very tiny light, then disappeared. When it expanded, it did not look like an explosion. The light filled a perfect circle shape. I'm going to guess that it expanded to 5x the size it was originally. I remember thinking when I saw it that I've never seen nor heard of anyone seeing anything like this before and I can't imagine what natural or man-made thing would do that.
