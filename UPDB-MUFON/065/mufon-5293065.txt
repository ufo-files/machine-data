V-shaped craft headed from south to north. dark but could see distorted stars through it. Darker than sky.

We were sitting in the back yard looking up at the sky when a black or transparent v shaped object glided overhead traveling from south to north. The light pollution around the house was pretty bad. The craft was darker than the sky. There were a few visible stars out but as the craft traveled across the sky you could see the stars on the other side of the craft. Those stars were distorted and brighter than they should have been. It was like a moving section of dark sky that had no light pollution.  There was no sound.  Out of view to the north in 10 seconds.
