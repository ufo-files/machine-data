{cak} 3 fireball orbs playing a leapfrog game in the sky

I was sitting outside talking on the phone around 9pm. The bright light caught my eye then two others appeared, followed by what appeared to be them leap frogging around each other turning off and on and circling around. They did not appear to change altitude. They were bright yellowish gold fireball like spherical orbs. I ran in to grab my wife and she came out and saw them as well before they began to fade out and disappear. I kept looking and thought I saw a light white ball floating away not in the fireball form. this is the second time I have seen this in about a month. Honestly the thing it mostly makes me think about is the question, Is God real, what would the religious world do if we were told the truth. Would wide spread panic/chaos be a result? Proof? Faith? Why am I seeing this? Is it a privilege? Surprisingly not afraid but inquizical. My dumb phone was out of storage space to take a picture.
