{nd} Orange orbs following path slowl then stopping then moving up then continuing.

My husband and I were on our back patio watching some fireworks in the distance. We saw an orange ball floating up to the sky from the tree line and thought it was a firework and was going to explode but it didn't. It began an arc to the left and up. It was definitely not shaped like a plane and did not move like a plane. It was moving slowly and then would hover completely stopped then move up a little and then continue on the arc until it got farther away and we couldn't see it anymore. Then a few minutes later another orb appeared and did the same thing.
