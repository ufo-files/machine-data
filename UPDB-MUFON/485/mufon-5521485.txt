Attention grabbing flash and erratic flight

I was driving south from Oswego to Fulton on rt 481 with my wife and dog. A very bright shining aircraft appeared a mile away directly above the highway ahead of us. I slowed down and we watched it until my wife said What is that? It looks like a cigar.  I didn't have my glasses so I could not see in full detail, but I could see that it was a bright white object that moved more erratically than other aircraft I have seen (I build model planes so Im familiar with most types). The object started moving south and blending in with very distant clouds and eventually we could not see it. We continued driving and 10 seconds later my wife said Wait, its coming back at us. I pulled over and it was now moving north and was situated further east than when we first saw it. It was probably surveying the small airport in that area. We found a camera and took some vid and pics. There was not one sound despite its close proximity. The absence of engine noise was most alarming to me, coupled with its erratic flight and high steady speed. The 2 pics are high megapixels, but one is slightly zoomed. The object is in the approximate center, quite small. It can be zoomed quite nicely in photoshop. The video is alright but low res.
