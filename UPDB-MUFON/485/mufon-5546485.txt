Witnessed an object flying very fast at a low altitude.

Somewhere between 9:00 and 910 P.M. on Sunday the 21st, I know because Family Guy just came on and I was outside with my dog. I witnessed an object flying low and CRUISING,I mean this thing was FAST. I did not report it earlier because I wanted to see if there was something on the news about it. There wasn't. I was outside with my dog and from where I was facing, west, I witnessed an object come from the south heading north and flying very fast, faster then any plane I have seen. Yes there was planes in the sky that night also. I saw only one light, until it got to the north then it looked like a red and a few blue lights went on and it looked like the object was slowing down and or stopping.
