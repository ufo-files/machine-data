{rjl} v-shaped craft flew directly overhead heading west,clear night street lights reflected off ship-six round red rear tail lights, Top Gun-Miramar due east of sighting ghts at rears

I was taking my Wesstie for a walk when I saw a shooting star before I closed the door. A crystal clear night.I walked around the block when my dog stopped so I thought I will look up to see if I see another shooting star. I looked overhead and there it was...a football field in length and width it was silent,no exhaust and was visable due to the reflection of the yellowish street lights...this triangle shaped craft had 6 circular red lights at it's rear.As I looked up and the ship blotted out the whole sky I realized I was just left of center...as it headed west to the Pacific I looked to my right to see it's length which was visable because of the lights on Torrey Pines Road.This whole event lasted only ten or so seconds. I lost sight of it when it was blotted out by buildings. I felt great. I had been listening to Coast to coast -Art Bell since 1992 and was aware of V-shaped craft.Due east about 3 miles is Miramar Naval Air Station runway. The Pheonix lights episode took place six month earlier.Ia it one of ours?
