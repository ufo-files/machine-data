I first noticed what looked like a star in broad daylight on a clear cloudless sky

I was in the pool floating on my back when I noticed what looked like a shiny star on a clear blue sky, It was around 6-6:40pm on May 29. it was a shiny silver color reflecting the sun light getting brighter at times and then dimming.. To the naked eye it looked round like a star, a pea could cover it. it was extremely high up in the sky. The object came from the east then took a slight path to the south then stopped. it was then southeast of me. I was surprised to see a star like object in broad daylight. it made no sound. It finally disappeared after approx. 35mins.
