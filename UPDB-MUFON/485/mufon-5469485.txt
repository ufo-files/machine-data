Bright light moving right to left then up and down. Once it stood still, numerous smaller lights (maybe 50-100) came from West and were sucked in by the brighter light.

We were all by the campfire when someone noticed that this light in the sky was a different kind of light than the stars... when he pointed to it, we all stood up and watched it glowing. It was high in the sky to our West. It almost looked like a star. Then it moved to the left a bit, then downwards, then upwards, then in a straight line to the right. Then moved backwards to the left again and up, down, left right. Someone ran to the camp to get the binoculars. We all took turns watching it through them. I saw what was like a flying saucer shape with coloured lights around the middle. The lights seemed to be going in a circle and they were flashing different colours (red, blue, green)...as I passed the binoculars on to someone else, that's when I noticed a whole bunch of smaller lights (50-75?) come from the West (almost all at once) and seemed to be sucked into the brighter light which was staying still during this time. When there were no more smaller lights, the bigger brighter light seemed to move farther back very quickly and disappeared never to see it again.
