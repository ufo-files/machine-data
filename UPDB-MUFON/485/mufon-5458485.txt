{rjl} Saw two strange bright lighst that defied my logic.

I was sitting on my porch facing North and noticed a light appear out of no where. I looked at it trying to figure out why I was seeing only one star in a pitch black sky. As I was looking at it, another light appeared, also out of no where, above and to the West of the first. I quickly went in the house and grabbed my cell phone and began recording. The initial light remained steady as the second light descended diagonally Eastward of it, then back West toward the steady light; hovering briefly. The second light then went East and just disappeared. The initial light then shot straight up at an incredible speed and out of sight.
