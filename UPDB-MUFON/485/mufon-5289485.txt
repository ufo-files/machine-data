We observed a spinning, reflective silver, oval / circular object in the sky.

When walking east across Woolwich Common we observed a circular / oval object at about 500 - 1000 feet altitude rotating around it's horizontal axis. At first we thought that it may be a silver foil balloon but the object stayed fixed in it's location even though there was a wind across the heath from the North. It also did not appear to gain or lose altitude but remained at a fixed height. It was spinning around it's horizontal axis while it stayed in it's position. We took some photos with our mobile phones but the pictures do not really show the object clearly. After about 5 minutes we exited the common from the eastern side and left the location.
