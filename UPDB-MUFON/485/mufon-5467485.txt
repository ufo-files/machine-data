1. I am in Nisswa/Lake Shore, MN and I was walking back to the bunk house from the main cabin - coming back from using the bathroom.
2. I was walking and was looking at the sky because the sky was very clear and I know somewhat about astronomy so I was trying to find the Big and Little Dipper.
3. At first I thought it was a normal star because it was not moving at all. I knew for a fact it was not a shooting star (which I've seen many) and it was defiantly not an airplane because there was no way it could be up that high (it looked like it was a star).
4. I was looking for the dippers and then I saw a star that was momentarily still start flying smoothly across the sky. Straight across the horizon. It seemed to be in space because it was so far away.
5. I stood in shock for awhile then ran into the bunk house as fast as I could once I realized what it could very possibly be. I was very confused and freaked out. I didn't believe what I had just witnessed because I always believed in things like this but never thought I would experience anything.
6. It eventually flew past behind a tree covered area and I tried to find it again but it was already gone.
