2 objects hovering above the clouds near the coast line. One was disc shaped and the other cylindrical.

I was in an airplane at the time, and we were on descent into Nova Scotia. I was filming the coastline, as it was a clear day when I spotted two stationary objects above the clouds. They did not appear to be moving, but rather hovering. It's hard to say how far they were from my position, but they were quite small from my point of view, so I'd guess they were pretty far. I was amazed to see them, and glad that I'd been recording with my phone so that I could try and get a better look at them. When I looked at the footage later, and zoomed on the disc shaped object, it has a definite lenticular shape. I lost sight when my plane moved away from the location.
