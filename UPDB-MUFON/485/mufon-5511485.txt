{nd} I was watching 3 meteors and noticed the UFO.

I was getting in my car to leave on a Saturday morning and was just getting in when I noticed what looked like three meteors going in different directions. I then noticed what looked like a reflective object in the sky slowly following the flight path of the larger meteor. I ran in the house leaving the car door open and went to get my camera and wake my wife and son. They came out and saw the meteors, but the did not see the UFO. Luckily I had taken the picture before they got outside. I have a picture of the object and video of the meteors. I thought it was funny that I didn't hear anything about meteors on that day, because I am sure they were meteors. And at least three of them.
