{dr}see three cross in sky in south, two went south one stayed, behind from noth came a round object that I could see under, it had probes, and a skin like material, went pass me and the cross then took off and both went south

It was just starting to get dark when I look at the moon and saw three black objects, they were shaped like a cross but very large, each at least 80ft x 40ft. Two turned and went south one behine the other and one stay. Then I look up and there was a round object about 100ft wide, it had a slow spin, I could see up into the bottom which was was at least 6ft deep.
The object had a skin like surface, and had 8 probes, 4 large that reached out about 20ft 90deg apart and 4 smaller probes 10 ft each in between the larger which were also smaller in dia. No noise as it floated over me and I was able to get a pic, but it does not show any detail just a blob in the sky. You have to blow up the pic and see the object between the bottom two trees.
