{dr}watching the northetn light and saw a bright light in the sky

At 11pm on June 23 I drove my car to my field approach to watch the northern lights. As I watched the northern sky I saw jets and satellites flying. Then around 11:30 pm I saw a flash of red fill the sky then a bright large light appeared and hovered over a mile away from me. My immediate reaction was to laugh because I was not expecting to see anything but northern lights. The light then appeared to turn east and left quickly. I drove home because I'd seen enough.
