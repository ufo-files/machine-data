I saw an incredibly bright object hover till it was directly above me. It made no sound, had no wings and had no sign of propulsion.

I was in my backyard speaking with my girlfriend and she said, “What’s that?” I turned and saw a light brighter than any light I’ve ever seen during the day. It was at the altitude of a helicopter some distance away facing southeast and hovering in a straight line in our direction, making no sound. I immediately knew this was a craft and it was nothing I had ever seen before. 
As it got closer the lights became brighter and flashed and I cupped my hands around my eyes to block out the sun and fix my stare on it. I told my girlfriend to film because I wanted to get the best look I could.
When it got directly over me it stopped and floated for just a moment. I was staring up at the bottom of the craft and what looked like 5 or 6 diamond-white lights beneath it flashed rapidly and randomly and seemed to formed the shape of a trapezoid. It felt like it was either scanning me or perhaps communicating with me. 
My girlfriend had a different vantage point at about 60 to 70 degrees and she saw the lights change shape and become circular all around the craft, as seen in the video.
After its extraordinary light display it resumed its original path and hovered off into the distance till it either faded away or disappeared but it did seem to suddenly vanish at the end of my sighting.
