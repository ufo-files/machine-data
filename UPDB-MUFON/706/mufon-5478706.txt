{cak} Train of objects slowly drifted Eastward as each would fade and disappear.

After a friend had left my apartment ( after the end of a football game on TV, Seahawks vs Lions ) I walked to my second storie apartment, balcony glass door and opened the vertical shades. my wife and I were confronted with the sight of 6 glowing objects, in a even spacing in a train-like configuration. At first I thought, it was a series of flairs, dropped by an airplane, But the pulsating lights were not descending, but were slowly moving towards the East. My wife grabbed her phone and tried to get a photo, I suddenly realized my iPad was in reach so I began recording the exact same type of object I had reported seeing a few years ago just a block away from where I was filming. In the video you can hear me recalling the experience I had. There was a realization that, made me feel calm and vindicated, because I was now able to give proof of this phenomenon happening when you least expect it. I can't recall the witness report number that MUFON designated then, but I was contacted by an investigator a few weeks later. He told me that there have been several reports, in this region, of similar objects that were dubbed ( Fire Balls ) due to their orange color and scintillating pulsation. They all have identical color, intensity and each in turn slowly fades and disappear. I will submit the video I recorded, but demand, full rights of original content ownership. I wish I was able to help fund your noble cause, that of seeking the truth behind this mysterious experience that far more of us have had, but are unwilling to stand up and say, yes I also witnessed a mystery, that must be revealed.
