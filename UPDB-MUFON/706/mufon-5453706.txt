lights moving in onne direction

We were at a technical inspection station. We used to ride a bike, because I often look in the sky to see some kind of anomaly. And I often see anomalies in the form of moving lights, and today what I've seen has never happened to me before. More than 20 lights were heading north east. The lights were moving without any sound. Since the sky was clear, I could see the lights very well. Me and my friend felt surprised. Then we went a little out of town on one of the Polish roads leading to the surrounding villages so we could see the phenomenon better. I was thinking about recording this phenomenon, but since my camera is really bad, I couldn't shoot the whole show, but I recorded one of the lights. Quality is very bad because it was shot on the phone and zoomed to the maximum. When the last light went by, it went missing as if there were none.
