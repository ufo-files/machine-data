Clearly visible and identifiable flying saucer shape craft against a bright blue sky in the middle of the day

I was driving west along Thorburn Road , St. Johns NL when I saw something out of the corner of my eye on the left. It was clearly an icon saucer shape with a dome on top and slightly dome shaped on the lower half. It hovered completely sationary for approximately 30 sec before disappearing into thin air. It winked out rather than flying away. I felt weird. I was thinking ...why aren't other cars stopping to look at this and at the same time ...there's no reason to find this odd. I watched it for the whole time but it didn't waver and it was a windy day. If it had been small it would have been unstable in the air. This craft was not wobbling at all. It did not move in any direction just hovered in the air. I could clearly see that it was a saucer with windows and the sun was reflection off the right side of it. It was large enough in my field of view that I was sure of what I saw. It could not have been a drone shaped like a ufo saucer because it ws not affected by the wind and it disappear without flying away. I drove away and mymind was telling me that there was nothing strange abut seeing the craft. I didn't even tell anyone until much later that day. I feel that my mind was manipulated in some way. 

Another woman I know saw the same craft (from her description) in a different area of town on the same day.
