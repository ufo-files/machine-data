Glowing UAP over Ft Lauderdale Beach

On December 4, 2021  Time was been 10:30 - 11:30 PM EST  I was standing looking out at the ocean while talking to several of the other bartenders who worked Audacy Music Festival  I notice a stationary object hovering above the ocean  I said to the group I think I see a UFO which at first I didn't think it was one.  I didn't think it was one until it shot straight and I mean straight up so fast and it was gone.  Why I know it wasn't airplane is because from my location I could see airplanes coming into the Ft Lauderdale Airport  Those airplanes you could see the running lights and they were white red or green and they flashed  They stayed on direct paths and would get larger and smaller  This object stayed the same size and went straight up and had no flashing lights  It was solid color, glowing, no flashing light, stayed stationary until it shot straight up.
