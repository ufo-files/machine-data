Went right over our heads .

A few yrs. ago In our back yard at about 9:30 pm I saw a bright light, high in the sky coming towards my wife and I. It had a one big white light on the front and no other lights anywhere and there was no sound from it. As it went right over the top of us I could see the outline clearly. It was as long as a rocket ship and had 4 sets of wings. I kept saying look how long it is and noticed the extra set of wings. The 2nd set of wings were just a little shorter and straighter than the first set of wings which looked like regular plane wings. No lights on the wings at all or anywhere but a big one on the front .
