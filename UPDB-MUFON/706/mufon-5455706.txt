12-15 star-like moving lights in a loose line from west to east with some moving alongside or back behind others.

I was outside feeding the horses. It was a very dark but clear evening. -4C with 13km SW wind. I noticed an abundance of lights in the sky that looked like stars but they were so linear and out of place. They were slowly moving, west to east! They weren’t always in perfect alignment and seemed to move independently from each other, often moving beside another or turning around to another part of the line. A soundless display that mesmerized and awed. Gradually the farther they traveled they faded out one by one.
