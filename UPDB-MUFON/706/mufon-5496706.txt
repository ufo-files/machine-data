WAS FISHING AT FIVE TWENTY EIGHT SAW WHAT I THOUGHT WAS A METEOR BECAUSE IT LOOKED LIKE IT WAS TRAILING FIRE. THEN IT STOPPED AND HOVERED AND THEN IT CHANGED DIRECTION SEVERAL TIMES THEN WENT STRAIGHT UP AND DISAPPEARED

On the morning of Jan 13 at 5.30 in the morning I was setting up my 
fishing gear and talking with one of the workers at our condo when I 
noticed what at first I thought was a meteor traveling from west to east
but at a slow rate, which didn't make sense. Then it stopped and hovered
approximately for five minutes. Then it moved west a short distance,
then move back towards the east and hovered for several minutes.
It moved again to the west and shot straight up until I could no
longer follow it. IT was exciting to see what at 71 years of age I 
never expected to encounter.
