On Feb. 23, 2018 At 6 PM around dusk but still daylight my husband and I were on our back deck with binoculars looking at birds on our lake when I looked up at what I thought was an airplane. It was in the south west sky, the sun was setting, it was below the horizon. We live in hilly terrain. It was round like a moon and very shiny, like a mirror reflecting light. It was shiny like mercury, and the light wavered like a mirage. We looked at it through the binoculars and then it divided straight down the middle while in continued to hover in the same spot. After it split the two halves remained side by side and you could see blue sky between the halves. We viewed it for about a minute or two amazed at what we were seeing then it just vanished. A few seconds after if vanished it reappeared a little farther south and this time it was moving south at the same speed as a jet airliner would move. It was still divided in half as it moved, then it vanished again. We didn't see it anymore after that.
