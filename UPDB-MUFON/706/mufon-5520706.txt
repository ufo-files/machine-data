Massive Rectangular object with lights on each of the four corners appeared to be landing just south of my home

I was sitting outside with my dog, smoking when I heard what I thought was a loud jet, looked up and saw a massive rectangular craft descending much like a plane, however this almost appeared to be more like a giant flying tank or something; it was huge and had no wings or propellers; it was flying very low and appeared to be making a landing nearby, possibly at Hill Air Force Base in Layton, UT, just south of here. The craft disappeared my view behind some homes before I could take a photograph. This happened just after 7:00 PM, which seems quite early for an experimental craft to be out flying.
