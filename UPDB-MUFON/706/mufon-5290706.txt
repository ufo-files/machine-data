deep maroon colored high altitude fast mover seen after dark

I live in an RV in the Marin Headlands on top of a hill. I exited my vehicle after dark to "see a man about a horse" and was looking up at the stars. The night was clear and I could see jets circling in their landing queue (for SF Int'l) to the south and west. A movement in the heavens caught my attention. It was moving rapidly (faster it seemed than possible based on all the other aircraft I observe at this location but slower than a shooting star). I observed it for over one minute moving south to north (a flight path I don't witness normally). It seemed to be at very high altitude. The object glowed a deep maroon color but had no other lights. It was shaped like a .45 slug - rounded at the tip but flat at the rear. It resembled the recent photo released by the Navy pilot so that's what motivated me to report it. The lack of aircraft lights also set it apart from the aircraft I see every day in this area. The Blue Angels frequently practice nearby. I'm former Military (Army Tanker) so I've seen missiles launch and fly over head - I lived in SE Florida and have witnessed rocket launches. This object was not something I am familiar with.
