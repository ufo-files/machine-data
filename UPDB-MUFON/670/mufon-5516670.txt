(GS) One bright green light flying slowly by. Then another one came that was flashing red & green. They were flying back & forth.

I was sitting outside talking to my mom on the phone smoking a cigarette when I saw up by the trees a really bright green circle flying by. Pretty slow. So I said "oh my god. Hold on" & I got up & walked closer to the trees so I could try to get a picture. Then that was when I saw the second one behind the trees. The second was was bright green, and then would fade in & out of red & green. But the other one just stayed green. They sat there for a minute or two, then kept crossing each other back & forth. Then just vanished. The whole thing lasted about 5 minutes. I immediately thought UFO ! I was kind of scared & shocked for a while after it happened. I keep wondering what it was that couldve been that bright of a green ! It looked like the color of a green street light, but obviously it wasnt because it was moving & it was too high to be a street light !
