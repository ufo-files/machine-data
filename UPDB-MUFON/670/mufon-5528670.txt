Bright sphere traveling lighting fast stops instantly

The time was about 10:00 PM in mid August 1977. Two friends and myself were parked on the eastern slope of a mountain west of Yreka, CA. We were standing outside the car talking. The sky was clear and the moon was not up yet. We were watching a small airplane flying due east. I estimate the airplanes altitude to be about 10,000-12,000 feet. We were at roughly 4000 feet in elevation. That airplane had come over the mountain and had been in view for about 90 seconds. Following the same path as the plane there appeared a sphere of pure white extremely bright light. It came over the mountain and covered roughly 3 miles in about 1/2 a second. It stopped or more accurately slowed to match the speed of the plane instantly a short distance behind it. The sphere appeared to be roughly about the same size as the airplane or maybe a little larger. We all watched in pure amazement as the sphere followed the airplane for the next few minutes over the horizon and out of view. This thing went from a few thousand miles an hour to about a hundred miles an hour in an instant. All I could think about was that the pilot had no idea what was happening as there are no rear view mirrors in airplanes. Many years later I told this story to a friend who said he saw the same event from about 5 miles further to the east.
