Orange beam of light following car

My boyfriend and I were on a drive just after sunset when it had really gotten dark. We were driving through what seemed like a new residential neighborhood when the road went into the woods and suddenly turned to dirt with no streetlights. The gps said it was the way home. There were no other cars around us and we didn’t pass any other roads. We went around a bend in the dirt road next to what I think was a cranberry bog. All of a sudden out of the darkness there was a very bright amber light coming from behind the truck. Neither of us said anything at first. The light appeared to come from behind and almost above us. My boyfriend didn’t see the source of the light, but for a split second I was able to see in the mirror. It looked to be a singular light, maybe a foot in diameter, no more than ten feet off the back of the truck and following our movement three to four feet off the ground. There was absolutely no sound other than our own tires on the road. We went around another turn and it was gone. We didn’t pass any other roads or trails, and the encounter lasted maybe two minutes. We tried to justify what it was but we couldn’t come up with anything. A motorcycle would have been loud, if it was another car or a piece of machinery it would have had additional lights. Way too big to be a flashlight or headlamp. Gave us goosebumps and a pretty good spook.
