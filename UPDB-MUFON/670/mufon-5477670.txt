Bright white moving light in the sky for 2 seconds

It was around 5:48am in the morning. I was walking towards my scooty parked nearby the railway station when I looked up to the sky. I saw a bright white light in the sky travelling at supersonic speeds from north east to south west. At first I thought it might be a firework or something. But within 2 seconds it vanished far away into the west sky. I'm sure it wasn't an aircraft or whatever. That thing was surreal. I've checked comet videos online, but this thing didn't have a trail.
