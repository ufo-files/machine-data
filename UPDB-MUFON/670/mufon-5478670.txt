{cak} Saw Orb and Column south of a mountain pass drive

I was driving my mom from Silverton thru a mountain pass that split right at Ouray and Engineers Pass. It was rainy up there, as usual, and I saw the sun come out behind me in the rear-view, so I stopped the jeep and stepped out to take a picture of the rainy-sunny mix on the mountains. Took pic and drove over the pass. I didn't look back thru these pics until three weeks ago or so and noticed it in the background, so I thought I'd share it with you. I can't explain it! :)
