{nd} Boomerang with lights underneath seen against night sky

Around midnight to one in the morning as I was out for a walk I noticed that there were lights moving across the sky that were not blinking like the airplanes I had been seeing that night. I could make out a shape due to the position of the lights underneath the object and how stars behind the object were blocked out. I at first thought it was obviously a commercial aircraft as there were many flying around even as I viewed the object but the shape and non blinking nature of the lights strayed me from that conclusion. The object moved slowly across the sky with no visible changes in direction until it simply vanished against the black sky. I was awe struck in trying to figure out what the object was. I frantically looked back between the object and the commercial aircraft flying at the same time. This object seemed a lot closer than the commercial aircraft so I removed the earbuds I was using to listen to music to see if I could hear anything from the aircraft but there was no sound despite it seeming so close. After the object had vanished I looked to see if I could still see the object in other parts of the sky but to no avail.
