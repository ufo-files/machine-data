{nd} Black V across the face of nearly full moon

I went outside on my back deck to look at the moon with my binoculars and while looking at the moon saw a black triangle or v shaped object pass in front of the moon from top left to bottom right. I have no idea what is was but it had no lights and appeared to be very near the moon surface. A far as feelings go this was my first UFO sighting and it kind of creeped me out. I lost sight of the object when it was no longer in the face of the moon meaning I lost it in the blackness of the sky.
