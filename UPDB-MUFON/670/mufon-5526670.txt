Ball of light, low over the ocean

My husband called out for me and my children to hurry outside. He was downstairs in the driveway. Our driveway and home face a marshfront. On the other side of the marsh is the island of Tybee and then the Atlantic Ocean. The Savannah River Inlet is to our left. There was a Coast Guard helicopter with the spotlight pointing down into the river so my automatic reaction was to look there. My husband said, "no, over there."  To the right was this ball of light. It looked like it was very low in the sky and coming right toward our home. We sat there in silence, looking for blinking lights. We did not see any. It suddenly stopped, and looked to move in the opposite direction, due East, straight out to sea. It was out of sight by the time I could count one one thousand!
