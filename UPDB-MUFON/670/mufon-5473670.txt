{cak} midnight same azimuth as moon 1/3 dia. of moon moved slowly then off very fast to the NE into the sky and gone.

September 9 2017 approximately midnight Reno NV facing East at same azimuth as moon maybe 1/3 the diameter of the moon I saw a large floating red / orange orb. Could not ascertain altitude. Made no sound did not blink or flash, not collision lights or a balloon; it floated there maybe more like a cork on water for maybe 20-30 seconds..and slowly began to move away from the moon on a level path to the North then very quickly shot off ( got much smaller as it moved up and away) into the Northeastern sky at possibly a 20 degree trajectory. The entire event lasted a minute or so.
