{rjl} After noticing that the SpaceX satellite train was visible, two other objects flew into my attention.

This report is similar to one I filed earlier this year in mid January 2020. As I was gazing at the western sky around 8 pm April 17, 2020 i again sighted the SpaceX satellite train. Again as I was Viewing the sat train, two objects of different sizes flew in from the west and quickly changed their direction to parallel the sat train which is moving to the north east. Immediately the larger of the two objects appeared to descend and turn itself on it’s side toward my viewing position and change shape, getting very much larger and gaining brightness. The object continued gaining size and brightness until a black iris formed in the center and moved out rapidly to the objects rim exploding in a flash of light that was literally thrown off the disc shaped object into space. The object then returned to its previous size and luminosity flying again parallel to the satellite train to the NE. 
When these particular objects interact in this way, I get an overwhelming sense on being connected to the intelligence presenting there. I have experienced this interaction many times over what had become a regular thing in my life and is better than a handshake for sure. It speaks to me in indescribable ways. The amount of light the object produced was phenomenal. These are the only object I’ve seen capable of producing that kind and amount of light. It always delights me to no end, I find I really am attracted to the experience.
