My dog barked at car turning around in driveway. Went to window to see what the vehicle was doing then I noticed the object and went outside to try and capture it via video. My son, wife and daughters witnessed from window as I walked to the road with my cell phone. Across a field in the direction of the local airport (Kinston NC GTP) west and about 15 degrees to the horizon is where I observed the light. The light appeared to change colors rapidly, pulsating and stationary. After approximately 01:15 the objet or light orb disappeared. I have worked for a total of 17 yrs on or near military air stations as a contractor and have witnessed fixed and rotary wing aircraft in flight with lights for landing and take-off. This object looked nothing like I have seen before. I really can't say what it was and my iPhone struggled with the focus because of the darkness and with the neighbors outside lights that you can see in the bottom left of the video frame. I do believe however, the orb was at least 3-5 miles away.
