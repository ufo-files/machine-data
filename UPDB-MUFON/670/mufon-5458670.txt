{rjl} I observed a bright light/object near moon. While trying to capture on video, Is second or similar object appeared traveling at impossible speed.

On the above date and time, I was working for an airborne law enforcement agency in Los Angeles County. While walking out to my aircraft on the flight deck, I observed a bright flashing light or object on the left side of the moon. The object appeared to move laterally towards the left of the moon while flashing and reappearing in different positions. I attempted to film the light and upon review of the video, I observed an oval object with a dark belly and sunlight reflecting off the top traveling at a very high speed. The object traveled from the left of the screen to the right (east to west) between the revolution of the helicopters rotors in the start up phase. In a second video, I captured the initial light observed next to the moon as it flashed.
