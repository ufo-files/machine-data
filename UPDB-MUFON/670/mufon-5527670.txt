Approximately 7:00 pm on Tuesday February 22nd my husband, daughter and I saw what we can only describe as a UFO to us in the sky. We were standing in our back driveway and I looked up to see a bright "star" moving erradically in the sky. I watched it for a few seconds and realized that it was moving much too quickly for any object I had ever seen in the sky and was moving too jerkily and quickly to be real. We watched it for a period of perhaps 5 or 6 minutes and then it took off across the whole horizen of the sky in the matter of 2 or 3 seconds and disappeared. We have never in our lives seen anything like this, and never expected to in our lives. We don't say this is alien or manmade but we saw it with our own eyes and are as amazed by it as anyone could be. Our daughter only had her cell phone to get pictures of it and in the pictures it only looks like a series of dots in progression but there were plenty of stars in the sky that didn't show up in the pictures. It was not a plane, a helicopter or anything we are used to seeing. We reported it to Fox News and you. Please let us know if you had any other reports.
