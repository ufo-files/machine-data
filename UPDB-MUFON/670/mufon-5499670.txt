{dr}We saw pulsing white lights.

I was inside my house with north facing windows. I was watching TV. Two pulsing lights came into view traveling north. The lights were bright white and were regularly pulsing. This caught my eye since it was dark. I thought it was a plane, but noticed right away that there no red or green lights like a plane has. I pointed it out to my husband who went out to our porch. He said that there was no sound and he saw the same object. The object continued north until out of view. As it proceeded north, it appeared to hover at a couple of points. We lost sight of it after out view was blocked by trees. My husband felt it was a UFO because we couldn't identify what it was and it was silent.
