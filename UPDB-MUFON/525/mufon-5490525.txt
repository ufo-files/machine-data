I was with my family at frontier days and i was sitting with one of my grandparents, while one of my parents was looking around the things to buy at the Indian Village and i heard as always the big c-130s taking off nearby, because the air field in just across the park. i was looking up at the sky for awhile taking in the beautiful day and that's when i noticed a smooth surfaced, thin, slow moving, silver disc moving realitivley slow at a 45 degree angle upwards. at first i could see it clearly for ten seconds or so and then it ascended behind the clouds and i could see a glimpse of it and then before i knew it, it was gone. All the while while i was watching it move upwards i felt though as if i was locked into watching it and everyone and everything else around me was gone until it disappeared.
