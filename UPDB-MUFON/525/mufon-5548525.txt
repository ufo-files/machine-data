UFO FLYING ALONG THE CANADIAN BORDER

WHILE STATIONED AT AN ARMY NIKE-HERCULES MISSLE SITE IN THE UPPER PENISULA OF MAINE, OUR RADAR UNITS PICKED UP A FAST MOVING OBJECT APPROX 12 MILES INSIDE THE CANADIAN BORDER. NOT ONLY DID WE PICK UP THE OBJECT BUT ALSO THE THREE OTHER MISSLE SITES & LORING AFB (A SAC BASE) & ALSO NORAD. THE OBJECT WAS MOVING FROM WEST TO EAST AT APPROX 600 MPH. OUR RADAR VAN OFFICER & OTHER VAN OFFICERS AT THE OTHER MISSLE SITES TRIED TO CONTACT THE OBJECT TO IDENTIFY ITSELF. THIS WAS TO NO AVAIL. OUR MISSLES WERE UP & READY TO GO IF THE OBJECT CROSSED THE BORDER. LORING AFB ALSO HAD THEIR JETS IN THE AIR. SUDDENLY THE OBJECT IMMEDIATELY STOPPED, FROM 600 MPH TO "0" IN AN INSTANT THEN WENT STRAIGHT UP. THE RADAR HAS A GLITCH EXPLAINED THE VAN OFFICER, & THAT WAS THE END OF IT. OF COURSE 4 MISSLE SITES, A SAC BASE, & NORAD ALL HAD THE SAME GLITCH AT THE SAME TIME. I'M A FIRM BELIEVER & HAVE BEEN SINCE THEN THAT THEIR ARE UFO'S "SEEING IS BELIEVING"
