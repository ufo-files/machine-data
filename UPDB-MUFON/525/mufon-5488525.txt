Piqua, Ohio Friday October 2, 2015, had to be after 11pm but before I went to bed around midnight. I know this because I was waiting for notification of my aunts passing. I let the dogs out in backyard and noticed the sky was dark except a few lighter clouds and dismissed them as part of a weather system we were expecting in Ohio, it was windy and had been drizzling rain. The lights were behind the clouds passing at very high rates of speed from the northeast to the southwest. At first I suspected a spotlight, but I began to count as each one passed and lit up the clouds. There was no set pattern. I looked online today to see if there was perhaps a meteor shower. There was none. Dogs were wanting in and out, remaining out more than wanting in, which is odd as it was drizzling and they would normally want to remain indoors.
