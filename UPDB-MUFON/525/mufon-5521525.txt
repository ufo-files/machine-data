SLOW MOVING STAR.

I WAS WALKING MY DOG AND LOOKING AT THE SKY. I SAW A BRIGHT OBJECT IN THE SKY IT LOOKED LIKE A STAR BUT IT WAS MOVING. THERE WAS FIVE AIRPLANES IN THE SKY THIS OBJECT DID NOT LOOK LIKE THEM. THERE WAS NO FLASHING COLORED LIGHTS. I WATCHED THE OBJECT FOR ABOUT 10 MINUTES UNTIL I WALKED BEHIND A HOUSE AND AS I WALKED OUT FROM BEHIND THE HOUSE IT WAS GONE. I DONT KNOW IF WAS THE ISS OR A SATELITE. BUT I WOULD LIKE TO KNOW.
