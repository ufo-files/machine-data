{dr}Hexagonal objet with blue/red lights on perimeter. Fast directional changes, short hovers, high-pitched whir sound.

I was standing on the front lawn with my dog as we do every night, seven days a week for 11 years. I originally saw a large, slow moving aircraft moving eastward at about 60 degrees above horizon (N/NW). It was partly obscured by a large tree on the opposite side of our residential street. I am always curious and interested in planes as i had wanted to pursue a flying career nearly 40 years ago ... which I did not. While waiting for the plane to emerge from the canopy of the tree. I saw two "blue" streaks that were at approximately a 45 degree angle of attach to the trajectory of the original aircraft. The first streak came from below and west of the aircraft, directly towards the aircraft then from below and beneath of the aircraft towards the aircraft. Each streak lasted for 1 second. Then, with the aircraft in full unobstructed view, having come from behind the tree, the UFO moved in short bursts, in close proximity to the aircraft. The UFO moved in short straight lines, changing direction 7 times. The last movement of the UFO was in a NW direction, clearly moving away and, generally, in the opposite direction of the aircraft. We stayed on the front yard for another 15 minutes before coming inside to file this report. I have never filed a UFO report before but this event inspired me to file the report in case it correlates to other reported sightings in this area.
