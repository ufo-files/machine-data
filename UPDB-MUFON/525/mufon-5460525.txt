Fishing with dad. Seen greenish blue ball of light

Was out fishing with my dad just before sundown.  We were sitting side by side in the boat when I looked over at him and beyond and noticed a ball of light hovering over the hill top. I was flickering this beautiful greenish blue Hugh. I could not make out any distinguishable adamant objects, just light.,, almost like a gas but very large and slowly descending. I tell my father too quick look over there and by the time he turned his head to see what I was talking about, the ball of light quickly shot down toward the shoreline/water and then disappeared. Father said he seen some light but wasn’t sure what he seen. I personally stared at this light for 5-10 seconds before telling dad to look. We both were confused though as to where it went, no sound of a crash yet it fell from the sky so fast nothing should have been able to recover. No splash in the water as it was a calm day that night. Very perplexing.
