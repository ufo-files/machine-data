{tjd} Triangular craft following jet, rapid changes of direction and incredible acceleration

I saw a jet approaching from SE and black triangular craft following it by about ten lengths of the jet. I believe the jet was on approach to Portland International Airport (PDX). It took about 30 seconds to get my phone out and start videoing and during that time I lost track of the triangular craft. I videoed for about seven seconds. A few days ago I found the video on my phone again and thought I saw a flicker of motion that I couldn't account for, so I emailed it to myself. On my PC screen I can see the triangular craft dropping from above the jet and doing a loop down and back up before disappearing off to the right at a very high rate of speed.
