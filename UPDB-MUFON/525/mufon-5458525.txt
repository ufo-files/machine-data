{tjd} Didn't see a landing

Walked out our front door facing Mt. Rainier on the 4th of July 2014. Straight ahead hovered a red glowing ball. Soon after another one appeared and then one more making a perfect triangle. They sat stationary before disappearing and showing up in 3 other areas to the north. Each time fading in and then just gone!
