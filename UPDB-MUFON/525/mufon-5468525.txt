{cak} Saw white circular light flashing nonsequentially, changing size, and revealing a black object behind it

At 3:00 on May 14th I was driving down Main Street toward Park Ave. in West Orange and saw a white circular flashing light up in the sky. I took a photo of it while at the light at the intersection of Northfield Ave. The photo shows a white orb above the traffic light. You can zoom in to see it.

Then I continued to drive down Main Street and pulled over twice, once past Park Ave. and again just past Charles St. I took a video at both stops. I also took another photo of it. 

As you can see from the videos the white light flashes on and off, but not sequentially. In the 2nd video the circle of light enlarges before it disappears, then seems to shrink and appear like a line, then appears again. Also, at the end of the 2nd video it looks as if a black object is behind the bottom part of the white light. Also, I saw a commercial plane clearly pass behind it going North to South. The plane was easily recognizable and typical looking and confirmed for me that this was not a plane.
