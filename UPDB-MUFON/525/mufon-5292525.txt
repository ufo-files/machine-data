Disc above my house just meters away from me

Late last year I was up late cleaning my house, it was about 2am, I took a few tools out to my shed, when I came out I took a few steps towards the house, I looked up and seen a disc with a red light on it, the red light was EXACTLY like the light on K.I.T the car from the knight rider TV show, the craft had a gold or copper ring around it, the ring was about 2-2.5ft from top to bottom, the red light was on this ring and it was about 10-15 meters wide, I couldn't see the top side (above the gold ring) of the craft as I was almost directly underneath it, I cant really give any more details of it as I was fixed on that red light, it seemed as if the light was scanning because as soon as I seen it, it seen me and stopped and hovered above my house, I just knew it was looking at me, I wasn't scared of it, I wish I had of tried to communicate with it, but I was to busy picking my jaw up off the ground lol, it was moving at the speed of a quick walk (very slow) when it stopped and hovered it was only 2-5 meters above my house, I was with in 10-15 meters of it and it did not make a sound, there is a tree next to my house as well and the disc did not effect the tree either, I mean leaves and branches were totally still, I called out to my house mate and when I did the disc started moving again super slowly, I walked with it (right underneath it) until I got to my gate and I watched it until it was over my neighbours house which is about 400 meters away and it was to hard to see, from time of first seeing it until it was over my neighbours was about 5 minutes, I have taken so long to report it because I was sure someone else had to of seen it, if its going around so slow with a unmissable red light so low to the ground someone somewhere has to of seen it too, but after 8-10 months I have not found another report like this, if anyone knows of or has read of a sighting of a craft like this please let me know. the photo I am going to post is just to show what I mean when I say a ring around it, the craft in the pic is similar to what I seen
