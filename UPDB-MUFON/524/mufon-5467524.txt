{dr}Looking out passenger seat window. Saw object come out from behind a hill. Very clear, open skyline.

Travelling on I-10, heading east between Lordsburg, NM and Deming, NM.
I was in the passenger seat of the car I was riding in. I was watching the scenery out the window when we passed a small hill. I saw the craft come out from behind the top of the hill. I kept looking at it trying to determine what it was. I thought at first that it looked like a white blimp. I decided to start taking pictures. After I took the pictures I couldn't see it anymore with the naked eye. The object was travelling in an easterly direction along side the road. The sky was clear, I could see for miles. The craft was flying east, the same direction we were travelling. The sighting was maybe 2 minutes. My mother was driving the car and did not witness it. I didn't feel any different after the sighting.
