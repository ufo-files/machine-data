I went out to watch the night sky and look at stars, I looked off to the north and saw what looked like 2 stars as they were stationary and then a few seconds later they started to move together in sync flying parallel with each other going south. A couple of seconds later the one on the right split off and headed straight west. The other object,very bright and high up continued to head south but in a jerky spurts like motion. It continued on like this until it vanished on the southern horizon NOTin a straight path like a jet or plane. It did not vary its path much but it was not a straight line either. I was raised around jets and airports. I know what a plane looks like as we do not live far from O'Hare. This was not that. Also they had no marker lights and made no sound. The whole event lasted about 3 minutes.
