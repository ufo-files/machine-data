{cak} White orb traveling SW approx 100-200 ft in the air traveling approx 50 miles an hour.

I was traveling on RT 43 from Northwood to Deerfield around 8:15-8:30 at night and noticed what I thought was a very bright star moving ahead of me. I watched it for about a minute and when I was 100% sure it wasn’t a star, plane or helicopter I took out my phone to record the light. The light continued in a straight path, seemed to be traveling at the same speed I was which was about 50 miles an hour. I was able to record it for approx 1-2 minutes until the direction of the road and the tree line blocked the light from my view.
