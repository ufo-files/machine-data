{tjd} Found cigar shaped object with a trial while processing landscape photographs.

I was photographing landscapes in the Chichaqua Bottoms Natural Area west of Farrar, IA in Polk County. While processing the photos later a cigar shaped object with a trail was found In two adjacent frames. I didn't notice them until I had enlarged the image to eliminate dust spots. I was not aware of the object in the photos at the time I made them.

Frame one was taken at 5:52:22 a.m. April 20, 2019. The camera was a Pentax 645 D set at 1/15th of a second at f/16. The lens was a Pentax 45-85 FA lens set at the 85mm focal length. ISO was 200. The camera was on a tripod. 

Frame two was taken at 5:53:10 just a few seconds later. Camera set up was identical expect the shutter speed for frame two was 1/20th.

The object can be seen above the thick white line which I added to make finding the object easier. The included photos are straight out of camera without any additional editing -- except for the white lines -- done to them.

My guess is that it's just a passenger jet, but would be interesting in knowing what MUFON staff think. Thanks!
