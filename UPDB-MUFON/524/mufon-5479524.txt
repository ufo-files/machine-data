7.30am 4 small cloud like shapes, moving in unison and looking like they had something solid behind them.

It was 7.30am approx. On the morning of the 18th November 2016. I was standing outside having a cigarette, before getting ready for work. From my perspective in the back garden, I have a good view of tree tops and sky. I was looking up - it was a very frosty morning so lots of clear blue sky's.  I noticed 3 white - what appeared to be clouds in the distance, they were travelling one after the other and then noticed a 4th travelling a bit behind. The only reason I noticed them is that they appeared to be travelling in a uniform manor and have what I can only describe as some structure/mass behind them, I could vaguely make out a v shape behind the cloud (like clouds with a purpose). They did not leave any chentrails or anything to make me believe they were planes. They travelled along and then changed direction - they did not turn but just sort of went sideways! and up. They carried on going up until they disappeared into the distance. I did not have time to get my camera as I was mesmerised by what I was seeing. I do know they were not anything That I can explain: Planes, clouds or any human object. I was that shocked I almost called work to say I would not be coming in that day - I also half expected to hear about this on the radio. It felt like what I had seen had some massive magnitude.
