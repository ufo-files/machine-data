{bright white/bluish} orb of light silently flew over trees - {djs}

My step son and I have been watching the skies nightly, in hopes of seeing the Neowise comet, or the recent meteor shower it had created. My step son spotted it first with his binoculars in the sky to the East of us, and I recorded it with my phone as well a took a picture. It was a very bright white/bluish light that silently flew over the trees heading Northbound away from 3 nearby airports. The light moved steadily, but made zero sound and had no flashing lights, nor any port or starboard lights. Living near the airports, we see dozens of planes daily and this did not seem to be a plane. If it had been a plane, we should have been able to hear it and see other identifying lights (red/green) but this was just a steadily rapid moving light phenomena.
