Dull Aluminum square shape with slightly rounded corners. Could see the entire bottom of the ship as it was almost directly over us and low.

Bubble lights were round and light was white but dull. There was no rays of light coming from the lights. There were approximately 8 lights on the bottom of the ship. There was nothing else obvious such as landing gear. 
My friend and I were sitting outside talking and 15 minutes or s prior to the sighting I was joking and said "UFO aliens, if you can hear me send a UFO over in 15 minutes." We laughed and forgot about it. It was getting cold so we decided to sit in her father's car which was parked on the street which was narrow. As I was opening the door to get in the driver's side something made me feel like I was being watched so I turned around and saw it hovering 10 feet away and about 55 feet above a tree. I said to my friend who was opening the passenger door "Sue, Look!" She looked up, screamed and ran into the house. I watched her run, then turned back to look at the ship and it was gone - no sound, no sight of it. I ran into the house and asked my friend not to say a word but to get two pieces of paper and 2 pencils so that we could draw what we saw in separate room then we would meet and share. Our drawings were identical. To me that verified that what we saw was really there. We talked about this for years. It wasn't until the next day that I remembered what I had asked for....the appearance of a UFO in 15 minutes.
