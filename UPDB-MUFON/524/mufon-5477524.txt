{cak} White orb over Indio

My husband and I were sitting in our patio in Indio about 7:30pm on March 10th, when he saw something and asked me to look up in the sky. I looked up and we saw a white orb gliding along at a good clip. Initially, I thought owl, but it was way too big and round, too fast, and no wings. Next thought, weather balloon, but this thing was going fast with no wind and it was not wobbling one bit, so not a weather balloon. It was moving horizontally in a straight line headed southeast with no fluctuation in altitude whatsoever. I'd have to say it was 100-200 feet above us as it passed over silently with no contrail. We watched it travel until it was out of sight. It must have been 10 to 15 seconds in duration. The object didn't have lights so it wasn't illuminated, yet clearly visible. My husband and I looked at each other and he asked me what I thought it was. I told him It wasn't a balloon, bird, plane, helicopter, drone, satellite, or kite, so.................you tell me. He said he didn't know wtf that was and not to say anything ot anyone. We've talked about it several times since and wondered if anyone else saw it.
