{tjd} Ufo hovered in front of car

I was around the age of 10 or 11.my grandmother and me wad driving home in the afternoon around 5 in the afternoon.not sure of date it must have been in the summer because we where co ing from vacation bible school.well we where maybe a half a mile from home and this round disc shaped object came down I. Front of our car it was about 6 feet from the car.and my grandma she continued to drive it just hovered in front of car and moved right along with us for at least 30 sec.then I remember I just shot off really fast.when we got home she never talked about it around me.years later I asked her if that really happened and she said yes it did. To this day I remember It very well. It had windows that when around it like u would see from a ship the round port windows.and it was a copper in color.this was a completely true statement.
