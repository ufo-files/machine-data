{cak} Moving very slow, void blocking out stars, very big cigar shape, no sound or lights

Played horseshoes till after dark (lights) turned all lights off, was standing and talking. I looked up and saw very big cigar shape black void moving very slowly and above trees blocking out the stars. Treeline about 100 yards on top of hill, open veiw from me to trees. Others were in denial and kept saying it's a blimp. I've been close to blimps and it was not,no lights, no noise, no gondela,very stary out and it just blocked them out. Guys would verify seeing it. I've always looked for the lights and this was totally unexpected. I'd like to take someone there to explain the whole experience. I've always regretted not jumping in my car to try to follow. It went right over my house.
