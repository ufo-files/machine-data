Star like light emerges from big flash

The light emerged from a round disc shaped flash that lasted only a fraction of seconds, it then starts pulsating while moving and changing directions irregularly, then stops moving and changed into red a little bit less bright. A few moments later an other light appears to north west of the first one and move in different directions before stopping too and fading in red, then both fades out completely. We also say meany flashes that were in the same area. no trail like shooting star, no movement in straight line like a satellite, only repetitive quick flashes at three different spots near the first big sighting.
