{cak} Saw 4 red-orange lights crossing the sky from N to S then disappearing.

Sitting outside about 1:15AM I noticed 2 red-orange lights in the sky coming into view over the trees from the north. They appeared to be following each other. They moved across the sky in a matter of seconds going in a south direction. Those 2 disappeared shortly after the point when they would be "straight above". Then about a minute later another pair of lights came following the same path, and those disappeared around the same point. I thought I had been recording it on my phone but it just took 1 photo unfortunately.
