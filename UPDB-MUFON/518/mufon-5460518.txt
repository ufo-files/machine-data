{tjd} It flew like a rocket.

My wife and I were out in our screened-in pool area when I noticed a contrail going straight up, some distance away. At first I thought it was a jet, but it was a totally different flight pattern than I'm used to seeing. I mentioned it to my wife and mentioned how fast it was traveling, and straight up, as far as I could tell. I went outside the enclosed screen area and stood on the lawn to get a better look. It was going straight up, took a sharp right, traveled a short distance and went straight up again. As it came out of its right turn, I got a view of the top of the vehicle, and it looked like the shape of a space shuttle. It continued moving straight up and then it was like a hole, the shape of an inverted ice cream cone, opened up and it went into it and disappeared, and the cone thing just closed up, like POP! Gone. The cone shaped thing was white, like a cloud. I didn't take pictures because my camera was in the house and I figured it would be gone by the time I retrieved it. I don't have a cell phone.
