Triangle object with 3 dim white lights traveling at extraordinarily high rate of speed

My girlfriend and I was in the park looking for shooting stars from the Ursids meteor shower.we were orientated toward the west from where the meteors were emitting. From the south west a triangle shaped craft crossed directly over our heads traveling at a very high rate of speed making no sound and continued to the north east until out of our sight.It was dark with 3 dim white lights traveling at an estimated 20,000 mph. The object was at an estimated height of 10,000 ft. The duration of the event was less than 3 seconds.
