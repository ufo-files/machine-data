Glowing Airborne Object

Approximately 8:47 pm I observed a glowing object above the horizon to the east. When it first appeared I noticed it glowing bright orange then red then faded out. After a few moments it began glowing again and remained constant only shifting slightly to the right then left every few seconds. I told my wife about it and then it faded away and didn't come back. I took a screenshot of a sky map showing what celestial objects might be in that direction and nothing matched what I was observing. I did take a photo and video. The Object was visible for around 5 minutes before fading out and not returning. I asked my wife to wait with me around 8:53 to see if it would return and the object was not observed again. The Object appeared to fade from a yellowish color to a very bright amber red.
