{dr}Bright light hovering then moved away very slow changing colors. Looked very different with binoculars diamond shaped with ring

Me and my wife went to the front porch to look at the thunder storms to the west and noticed a very bright light beneath the cloud cover with faint colors pulsating around the edges. My wife got my high powered binoculars and you could see a diamond shaped frame inside the light with lines inside of it. I called my son's from next door and a neighbor on the opposite side of my house and they thought it looked the same to them.We were really excited because we had never got this good of a look at a ufo. I took a picture with my cell phone but it was getting further off. It did show up in the picture but it looked different than it did with binoculars. A couple of minutes after I took the picture it faded out completely then in about 4 or 5 seconds it came back. At that point it looked more like a triangle with pulsating lights around the edges that were red orange yellow purple. We watched it for about 20 min total until it went out of sight to the West.It seemed to fallow the storm ahead of it. I was raised near Edwards air base in Ca. And have seen lots of different airplanes,helicopters and weather balloons. This did not look man made. Very exciting. The object is to right of the street light has a halo around it.
