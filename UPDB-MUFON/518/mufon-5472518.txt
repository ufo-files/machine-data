I noticed what I assumed was a satellite,straight up in sky, then noticed 3 other satellites following in a square formation, I thought jets? military? (not a sound though, none)then they made a sharp right angle turn, then again, then again. objects were going spacestation speed across top of atmosphere, if not higher. I had about fifteen other people watching before they left sight, everyone was silent or just said "WOW"
