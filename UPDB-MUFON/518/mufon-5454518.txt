{rjl} Multiple lights flew across sky and disappeared

My boyfriend and I were at the top of a small mountain in Mesa, AZ off of Brown RD. At a 5:36am, while waiting for the sun rise I looked up at the sky and saw multiple white lights in a straight line in the sky. I can estimate I saw 20-30 lights in this line, the line of objects was straight but the objects themselves were not perfectly organized in the line. As the lights flew over our head they disappeared with out explanation into the sky. My boyfriend also saw the strange occurs and we were both a little confused and freaked out
