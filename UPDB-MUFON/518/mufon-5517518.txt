{ck} large dome shaped objuct over downtown area

On July 5th, I observed a large dome shaped craft over the Nashville downtown area. There were orange lights around the mid section of the craft. Although I was driving down the interstate, I could tell the object was hovering in place. It appeared to be emitting a purple and green mist or dust of some kind. It was huge. I know Im not the only person who witnessed it. It looked unlike any aircraft I have ever seen. I heard no noise since I was in my car. I couldnt keep the object in view because I was traveling too fast.
