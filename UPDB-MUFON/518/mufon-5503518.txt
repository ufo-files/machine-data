{cak} Spotted triangle shape object coming home from work

Was going home from work and spotted a UFO near U.S. 422. Object was triangular shaped and flying low, made an abrupt 90-degree turn. Blue and red lights, some pulsating, some fixed. Heard report from another that they spotted the same object about 10 miles west shortly before I had observed it. No other unusual occurrences.
