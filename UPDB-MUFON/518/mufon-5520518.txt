V- shaped object flew in a northeasterly direction overhead

I came outside to walk my dog and happened to look up and saw what at first appeared to be a flock of geese flying in formation. I looked a little closer and thought it was odd that I was not hearing anything at all. I watched the object move almost as if it were on a current of air in a northeasterly direction. One of the characteristics that seemed to set it apart from a flock of geese was that there were lights which made the outline visible. There was almost no moonlight and it was partly cloudy as well.
