Stationary green flashing lights disk just hover in place

Saturday night February 19, 2022 at 19:25 ( 7:25) PST in Fresno CA I was at my girlfriends house and we stepped outside on the front porch to enjoy the night air. I sat down on the love seat she has out on the front porch, and immediately my attention was drawn to some flashing lights in the SW sky. At first I thought it might be a helicopter but no sound was coming from the object. I pointed it out to my friend and we both saw it just flashing green lights and stationary. There was no sound or movement other than the disk was flashing green lights. Than a second it shot straight up several thousand feet in a second, than shot back down, stopped abruptly. It stayed there for a few minutes than dropped down below the horizon. We ran out in the road to try to get a better look at it. To the SW of Fresno is Lamoore Naval Air Base in that direction. My girlfriend asked me "Did I just witness my first UFO siting?" I said yes you did! But I say it was Unidentified Arial Phenomenon.
