2 pictures of same scene, taken a few seconds apart. One showed a strange hovering object, which was not observed by the naked eye.

On 28 June 2014, an 11 year old girl staycationing at the Hotel Sofitel Manila with her family chanced upon a married couple who used to coach at her school. The couple told her they were staying at the hotel too to celebrate their wedding anniversary. The girl took out her mobile phone and took several pictures of the couple as they lounged waiting for the sunset on a grassy area of the hotel facing Manila Bay. The girl immediately uploaded the first picture she took to her Instagram. A friend saw the post, didn't notice the coaches, but commented that the picture was that of a UFO. The girl looked at the picture she posted and said that she did not see the object when she took the photo. According to her, there was nothing in the area that resembled the object in size or shape at the time. She was simply taking pictures of Manila Bay with the man and woman. She posted a second photo of the same scene, taken immediately after the first one, and the object was no longer there. The girl's friend, who also happens to be a minor, showed me the pictures. I suggested that they send the pics to MUFON. They requested me to do that for them and emailed me the 2 pictures.
