(GS) Bright flashing light hovering about 10,000 feet above airplane flight path,

I observed an unidentified object two nights in a row hovering about 30 degrees above the horizon, directly west of Pahrump, Nevada. Object is stationary for a long time. Observed with 8 power binoculars, but couldnt get a clear image. Ill try to get a 50-60 power telescope tomorrow in case the object comes back. It is a little further East tonight than it was last night. I watched an airliner fly right under it around 11 to 11:15pm.Very beautiful flashing colors, definitely different from anything that I have ever seen before.
