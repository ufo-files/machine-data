{kt}I noticed a star like object moving in zig-zag motion in western sky moving eastward.

I was on my back porch looking at the sky when I noticed something that I thought might be an airplane, due to the airport being in that direction.
The object I watched had a red light below a blue green light and sort of a white search light and moved from about 65 degrees to 75. degrees slightly zig-zag motion until it stopped in the sky when the white light became predominant. I looked to the right of the object and noticed that in a slightly curved line was 4 other star like objects.. One had a rotating white light with more of the red light in view. They were stationery in the sky. I looked around at the sky and noticed the sky was clear and the stars were visible except in the area where the objects were.
I had to go in for a few minutes and when I returned, the objects were gone.
