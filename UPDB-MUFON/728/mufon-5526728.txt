6 orange orbs. single file 1 night / 2 side by side first night /4 in line

I was at Bald hEAD iSLAND NC FOR THE WEEKEND AND WITNESSED 6 ORANGE BALLS GOING SLOWLY THRU THE SKY AND THEN DISSAPPEAR AT A CERTAIN SPOT. tHERE HAd been a power outage where I was staying and I went out on the deck to observe the stars and saw the orange objects in the sky moving slowly with no sound. I couldn't tell how high they were. The first two were in single file. The second two were side by side and the other two were in single file. When each got to a certain point in the sky they dissappeared.  Several family members and friends witnessed the same thing. The next night , Sunday May 1 we went out at the same time and saw the same thing except all were in single file. They dissappeared in the same place as they did the previous night. I thought they might be helcopters but there was no sound and they vanished. We saw them April 30, and May 1, 2011 heading west.
