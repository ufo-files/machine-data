2 fireballs in the sky

We were driving home about 10 pm last night when I noticed people outside watching what I thought was the eclipse. My husband said he saw it too and described it as being two circles in the sky. All of a sudden I saw it and it looked like two fireballs in the sky. One was higher and one was lower. They were spaced apart from each other. Within a few minutes they appeared to fade away.
