{cak} 6,glowing, orange, oblong objects, with a 7th object joining them from behind before they disappeared. Objects appeared to be moving very fast.

On Jan.11, I saw at first 6 objects flying in an oblong formation similar to geese above our home. The objects were football or cat1s eye shaped, orange in color and they were solid instead of blinking. I observed the objects flying erratically for approximately a quarter mile. I saw the first 6 objects for about 15 seconds. After the 15 seconds, I witnessed another of the same object flying towards the first 6 and then joining them. The 7th object flew towards the center of the first 6 and then all 7 objects disappeared. In total what I witnessed last about 30 seconds.
