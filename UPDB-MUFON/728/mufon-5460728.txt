fast moving light in sky with a coupleof minor course changes

I had stepped out to smoke a cigar in my yard and also to enjoy the night sky (view is overhead to West) Big Dipper was well notedhigh and westerly pointing to polaris. As I looked at the Big Dipper I spotted a slow moving satellite moving North which I lost sight of soon as it lost reflected sunlight. As I was trying to spot it another rapidly moving light (fainter, slightly orange) sped across the sky in the opposite direction moving very fast and changing course a couple of times. If it hadn't been for the minor course changes I would have assumed it was a meteor. It was pretty high but I could not give you an altitude. I am an ex pilot and worked in aviation for many years so have a prettywell trained 'airborne' eye. Course changes were two adjustments to the West as it moved South. Night was clear and visibility was good.
