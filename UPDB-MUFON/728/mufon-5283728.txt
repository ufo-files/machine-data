It was 4am and we were fishing off an open party boat somewhere between San Clemente and Catalina Islands. Another guy told me to look up. It looked like a star but was “shimmering” or moving around rapidly. I thought it might be from the movement of the boat, but none of the other stars were behaving this way. 

Then a big a school of bluefin came under the boat and everyone got hooked up so I starting fishing. Object was gone when I looked up a few minutes later.

I was later told that what I observed may have been multiple military drones flying in some kind of formation. If each drone was emitting a flash at different times, that might explain the appearance of rapid movement or shimmering that I observed. And it was near San Clemente island which is a military area.
