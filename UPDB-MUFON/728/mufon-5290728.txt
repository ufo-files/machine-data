Similar to Russian meteor explosion.

I had taken the dogs out before bed time and was admiring how sharp the cut across the moon was. Even at half moon, it was very bright. The sky was clear and stars were bright. Straight East of me at 11:49 PM I saw an extremely bright light come from 90 degrees up and descend straight down. Even after it passed below the tree line in front of me at an angle of perhaps 30 degrees, I saw a huge portion of the sky light up as if it had exploded similar to the one in Russia, but I heard nothing. Considering the brightness of the moon, it must have been some blast, but no sound reached me.
Sure it was a meteor, but wanted to report it as it covered such a distance..
