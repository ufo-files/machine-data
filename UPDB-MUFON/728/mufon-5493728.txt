{rjl} Saw something changing colors. Ran to the road to see what it was then it changes to two big bright white lights with a smaller below it.

This happened along time ago. Not even sure if it helps in any kind of way. Just felt like getting off my chest. A few nights after my Dad died, one of my friends and I were sitting on my front porch talking. I noticed through the trees, in the sky, out by the road, something pulsing different colors. It was hovering in one spot. I pointed it out to my friend. Then we ran out to the road to see what it was. Right about the time we got to the road it stopped pulsing and changed to only having two bigger bright white lights with a smaller one below them. As we watched it, it moved slowly left and right making no noise. It was also getting closer so obviously we started to get nervous. Just as a car was coming down the road it moved out of sight because of the trees. It was the only time before or since that I seen it. One of my other friends in the neighborhood said he had seen the three white lights before when I told him the story. The one thing that makes it interesting to me is that we lived about 10 miles from a National guard army base. They have acres of land. More than enough room to test secret or extra terrestrial craft.
