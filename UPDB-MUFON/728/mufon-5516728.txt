{dr}Flare like solid Red light

Observed one bright flare like solid red light that appeared to be approaching my direction from the south-east starting at approximately 30 degrees above the horizon. The objects speed of approach varied appearing to pause-hover-continue. At near 45 degrees the object entered the cloud cover (near the full moon) dimmed to not visible and has not reappeared. 

Several commercial flights flew nearly the same path both before and after the unknown at approximately the same altitude. Those flights are easily identifiable by their lights as well as their sound.
