{rjl} Gray and silver, cigar-shaped object hovering and rotating slowly

At approximately 4:30 p.m. Central time on Friday, October 4, 2019, I was traveling south on U.S. Highway 67 South of Festus, MO.  I witnessed a gray and silver, cigar-shaped object, ahead (south) of me, hovering and rotating slowly. I said, "Look what is that?". My brother said, "maybe a weather balloon or something?". My very first thought was a distant helicopter was ahead of us. When I say that it was rotating slowly, I say that because it was sometimes a flatter gray color. Then, it would appear it was turning and sunlight would reflect from it or something. It was like I was seeing a more silvery part of it.  When I started visually tracking this object, it was ahead of me (south), but eventually, it was to my left (east) as I drove along the route of the highway. I descended a hill and followed a major curve in the route. I reached the point where the object was at about an 85 degree angle to the left (east). To keep visually tracking on it, I had to look carefully out of the top of the driver's side window, otherwise my view was obstructed by the top of the door.  I wasn't able to make out the shape of the object well, until I was passing the object on my left side. At that point I ascertained it was a cigar-shaped object, but perhaps it would be more accurately described as an oblong, pill capsule type shape. It was probably 500-100 feet above the ground. I am estimating this because there was a cellphone tower in the foreground, a little closer to the highway that I know from experience had to be around 150-200 feet in elevation.  I think the object was probably around 25 feet in length and less than 10 feet in height. I did not stop, but kept on traveling because of time constraints and traffic. As I thought about what I had observed, I realized there was not a simple explanation for it that was readily evident to me. Therefore, I decided after giving it further thought to report the object.
