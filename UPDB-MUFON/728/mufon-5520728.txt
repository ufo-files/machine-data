Light blue airplane, turned into black tube

I was delivering a pizza onto Saw Road from North Enochville Avenue, right before I turned onto Saw Road, I saw a light blue airplane between my left and right, as I turned onto the road, the exact spot where the plane was, it is now a black tube, alonside it were 2 smaller tubes(also black) I could not tell if the 2 smaller tubes were connected, the 2 tubes were that close, I was shaken up, and so distressed I almost ran off the road, yell out loud a few choice words, and since i was driving, I only saw it for about 13 seconds, when I returned, I looked all around and saw no plane, no strange tubes, no nothing. I now drive everywhere with a media device that can take photos as well as video.
