{jpg} An object appeared as a star but started moving and traveled south, occasionally disappearing and reappearing

At about 1736 CST, I was outside burning boxes just before sunset. I was looking at the constellation of Orion's Belt noticing that only two of the lower stars were getting bright. Shortly after the 3 stars in Orion's Belt were bright enough to see, another star began to shine to the right of the middle star in the Belt. That star then began to move south in a straight line and would occasionally disappear and reappear. I observed this event for approximately 5 minutes before it was no longer visible.
