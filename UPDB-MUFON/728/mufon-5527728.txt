Two Obects traveling at a very fast rate of speed.

At about 9:05 pm my friend and I were walking to my car in Saint Joseph, Missouri. He called my attention to two fast moving objects in the sky by the full moon. One was a very bright white leaving a contrail behind. The other was a red light that seemed dimmer but left no contrail. The red one seemed to change course at a high rate of speed and the two flew by each other very closley. I have never seeen things move so fast or glow so bright. This happened over the couse of about 45 seconds to a minute or so.
