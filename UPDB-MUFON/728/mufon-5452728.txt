2 flat sided discs rectangular lights strobing

0n Nov. 9th 1975 at approximately 9:30 p.m. myself and a friend were walking north thru a field in Beloit Wi, east of Krueger Haskell golf course between Ridgeland ave. (at Bluff st.) and Hazel st. to meet a 3rd friend when we noticed two flat sided disk shaped objects at about our 2 O'clock position approximately 1/4 mile away and 1000 feet in altitude. 

 One was leading the other with the rear one a little higher. They had vertical rectangular amber colored lights that appeared to strobe counter clockwise in sequence much like the rear turn signal of a 68 mustang. We stood transfixed as they moved silently & quickly in a south easterly direction across the rock river flying over the Fairbanks Morse engine div. foundry before disappearing low over the horizon. This took all of 20 or 30 seconds but seemed like slow motion. We debated on what to do next and then decided to push on.

 Our friend never showed & was skeptical when we saw him the following day saying "it was probably just an airplane" Sorry folks but this was no plane. I hadn't seen anything like it before or since. That is something you NEVER forget. We told very few people and kept and eye on local news reports to see if anyone reported seeing it, but apparently no one did. This left us both believing we had a true UFO experience as it defied all explanation.
