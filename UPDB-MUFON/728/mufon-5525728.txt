Three fire orange glowing oblects

My wife and I observed three red/orange glowing spherical objects directly above. They would fade in and out, move away and towards each other sometimes merging. The movement seemed to be very deliberate and was silent. They eventually faded out one by one. The last one took a little longer and seemed to flicker various colors before disappearing completely. Very strange experience. Pretty sure they weren't Chinese Lanterns. Too high, too bright and the movement seemed intentional and swift.
