{tjd} I was at a public school event and noticed a bright white orb in the sky

I was at a school event at night, the sky was very clear except for a few clouds, around 7 or so I looked up and saw a white glowing orb in the sky. I knew it couldn't have been a plane because it had no red,blue or green flashing lights.
I watched it for 13 minutes. In those 13 minutes a friend of mine witnessed the event with me. I felt slightly scared and I turned to look at my friend for a moment before looking back at the object to see it was gone.
