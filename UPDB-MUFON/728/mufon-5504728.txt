I SAW WHAT I THOUGHT TO BE A POLICE HELICOPTER. i SAID THE PIGS ARE ON THE PROWL AGAIN AND LATHED (ENGLISH SAYING FOR POLICE). i OPENED A WINDOW LIGHT TO GAIN A BETTER LOOK AT THE LIGHTS. I NOTICED THERE WAS NO LIGHT COMING FROM THE LIGHTS, WHICH WAS STRANGE AS YOU ALWAYS HEAR THE SOUND OF A CHOPPER. MY BRO WENT AND GOT HIS VIDEO CAMERA.
