Appeared to be a bright light

Wife and myself had just come out the house walking to the car to attend our 7 year old granddaughter’s basketball game. I looked up towards the northwest to check the weather.That’s when I noticed it slowly moving to the southeast. Got my phone out and started recording because I didn’t think it was a airliner because there was no sound for how high it was and it seemed to be to high up to be a small plane. I recorded it until it went behind a tree in my yard plus my wife was pressing me to get in the car so that we could leave(my granddaughter’s game was starting at 9:00 am) When I was able to look at the video, I paused it and enlarged it and noticed it didn’t resemble a plane in any way,shape or form. It was the classic tic tok shape. It appeared to be brighter on the ends than in the center.
