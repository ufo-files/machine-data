{nd} silver cylindrical object came into sight, ejected a black pod toward the ground which flew off in a horizontal path behind a building; left a black trail of smoke.

I was sitting outside Henry's coffee shop with several other people (all strangers who also witnessed the vent), when suddenly i noticed a distant object in the sky. AT first glance it could have been a low flying jet (it was large enough), but it was flying VERY low, lower than a commercial craft would have been able to sustain. Then the object made several turns, at a very slow speed, and when we thought it couldn't get stranger it ejected a small black pod (probably the size of a car), toward the ground. The pod proceeded to start up a separate engine (which made a thin black exhaust trail). We thought it could have been a pilot ejecting from a failing aircraft, but pilots generally don't have secondary engines in their parachutes. The black pod continued its path horizontally, above the buildings until it was out of sight. The first craft followed it at a lower speed.
