I HAD JUST GOTTEN OUT OF MY CAR AND SAW A BRIGHT LIGHT COMING TOWARDS ME, IT WAS FLASHING ON AND OFF EVERY 2 SECONDS. IT WAS A BRIGHT WHITE LIGHT LIKE A AIRPLANE LANDING LIGHT. I WAS FACING EAST AND SAW THE LIGHT COMING TOWARDS ME. THE LIGHT WENT OUT FOR 2 SECONDS AND THEN CAME BACK ON. IT DID THIS ABOUT 7 TIMES, COMING TOWARDS ME. THEN SUDDENLY THE LIGHT STOPPED AND I NEVER HEARD A SOUND AND SAW NOTHING AFTER THAT. I HAD STOPPED WHAT I WAS DOING AND JUST STOOD THERE WATCHING IT COMING TOWARDS ME. IT WAS HELICOPTER HEIGHT, BUT I'M PRETTY SURE IT WAS NOT A HELICOPTER. I WOULD HAVE HEARD SOMETHING. IT SEEMED TO BE COMING FROM THE OLD FORT DEVENS AIR FORCE BASE.
