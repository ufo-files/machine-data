{cak} saw semi circle of 6 lights moving very slow over field

While driving home from work about 12:15 am entering my street jackson mills road in freehold which runs north to south and was very rural at the time 30 years ago, i saw a semi-circle of six bright white lights hovering or moving very slowly. i stopped the car to watch and got scared and left. the object was low to the ground and very close to me. i couldn't make out the shape of the object itself but the lights were in a semi-circle.
