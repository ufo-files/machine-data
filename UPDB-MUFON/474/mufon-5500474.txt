Blue light filled tent, memory of a small hooded figure nearby

I was camping alone on the night of July 31- aug 1. I awoke in the night, I am guessing around 3 am. My tent was filled by a bright light that started off whitish but changed to bright blue. It was shining through the tent, from the outside. I do not remember hearing anything, but I remember feeling strange and disoriented. I did not have the urge to leave my tent, just to close my eyes and hope it went away. I woke up again, I am not sure how much later. I felt less disoriented and was wondering why I had woken earlier. I then remembered the blue light and I then had a memory of a small hooded figure at the edge of my campsite. When I noticed it, it flashed closer to me without walking. I have no memory of a craft or anything else, but I have had sightings in the past. This has been disturbing me for the past few days and I needed to share my story.
