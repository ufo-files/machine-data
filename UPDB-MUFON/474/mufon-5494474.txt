The Arizona UFOs I captured on Tuesday, April 7th, 2015, followed a visit by our new Secretary of Defense the day before, who was in Phoenix on Monday, April 6th. On Monday, when the Secretary of Defense was scheduled to give a speech at the University in Tempe, followed by a meeting with local "military officials", there was only one cloud in the sky that day. That lone singular cloud was parked directly above the University area of Tempe. I remember coming out on my lunch break and seeing what struck me as a suspicious looking cloud over on that side of town, much like the ones you'll see in the photos I've included here. As I've done before, with another cloud I suspected of concealing a UFO, I raised my cup of coffee in a gesture of salute and waved. It seems I received a return gesture when I came back out for my afternoon break. From that same area of sky, a white orb appeared from high in the southeast relative to my position. It appeared to be both traveling due northward, and descending at the same time. Eventually it bounced, leveling off and then ascending in an easterly trajectory away from me. This eliminated any possibility that this was a balloon, and also suggested that my earlier greeting was being acknowledged. The last time I waved and toasted them, I ended up in a UFO "in your face" stare down. As for our new Secretary of Defense, given his background, I suspect he has at least a passing interest in UFOs (or whatever it is they are called at NORAD). Heck, he may even be a big fan of the Phoenix Lights.
