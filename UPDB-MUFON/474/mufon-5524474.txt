I thought it might have been a satellite at first but then it changed directions

I live in a very dark area just outside of Joshua Tree National Park. I was having my evening cigarette in front of my house while gazing at the stars as usual. I've seen what I've assumed were satellites before, a solid light tracking across the sky from one end of the horizon to the other. Tonight I saw what at first seemed to be an airplane because of how low on the horizon it was, but I didn't see any blinking lights. It was in the North sky traveling East to West about 25 degrees over the horizon. Then I thought it may be a satellite, but it was moving really fast and smooth. It moved behind a tree so I figured I'd keep an eye on it until it came out the other side. I saw the lights twinkling through the tree as it was making its way past, but it never came out the other side, so I stepped out into the street and I saw it going the opposite direction back behind the tree. So I waited for it to come out the other side. It never did. Then I looked up to about 50 degrees and there was a stationary orangeish light. It then slowly rose a couple of degrees, blinked 3 times and then it was gone.
