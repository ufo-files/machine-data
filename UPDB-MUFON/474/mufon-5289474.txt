Round and red-orange with no beam or aura of light, such as a setting sun.

Driving home after work around midnight, very cold winter, low clouds, no stars, 10 degrees. Farmland area, saw an orange light ahead through some pines, thought it was a helicopter hovering with searchlight, go to the open field and saw to my left about 300 feet off the ground a basketball size bright orange-red ball sitting still. I turned off the motor and rolled the window down, no sound at all, no movement. You could hear a pin drop. I hoped for someone to come along, I got frightened and started up the vehicle, suddenly it shot straight up into the low clouds, no sound. Three nights later, again at mid nite and very cold, I rolled the trash out to the end of the driveway. My dead end street faces the farmland to the east. I saw the same ball shaped orange orb rising up in the valley and turn toward me to the west. Before it got to my location it was gone in a flash. I live a couple of miles east of a small town. On these cold clear nights one can hear a jet from high in the sky but those nights it was dead quiet.
