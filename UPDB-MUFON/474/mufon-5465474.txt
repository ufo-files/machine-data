{jpg} Super fast bright orb, with trail, westbound flight followed by full sky flash

Laying in bed i have a skylight window that faces west. I usually see planes, stars, the moon etc. I also live in the flight path for the local airport. This orb like object that was SUPER bright shot past my house. I heard nothing. But it was bright enough to light up my room for a split second. Another second after it passed by, i saw the light reflected off the clouds that are in the gulf, which the coast is about 12 miles directly west. I checked the radar for storms and lightning, but there is none. Thats when i filled out this report.At first notice, it seemed like a shooting star, but it was too close, too big and too bright. It looked like a welding arc. I lost sight of the object when it left the viewable area of my window.
