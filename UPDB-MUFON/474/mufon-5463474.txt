I looked up in the sky and saw a blue triangle that blinked in front of the evening star. I was a passinger in a car. It was right in front of the bright evening star. It was bigger then the star. It covered the view of the star.

At the end, it turned a foggy white round with red blinking lights before lifting up and it left. Turning into a round white light. Then dispersed.

I was coming from the Maplewood Mall area with a friend who was driving down HWY 36 East bound. By the time we pulled over from White Bear Ave to Century Ave exit the ship was gone. Time was about 12:06 AM no more then three minutes time of seeing the IFO.

By the time I pulled over the driver only saw the round IFO in white and blinking red light then it was gone. We could then see the bright white stars in the sky alone with other stars.
