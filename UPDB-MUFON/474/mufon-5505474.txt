{cak} When leaving I noticed strange lights in the sky. Orange red orbs glowing... 4-5 in a line. the line distorted as they moved and faded.

My boyfriend and I were going out for a few last minute shopping items when I noticed all these lights in the sky. When observing closer we noticed these orange red orbs floating and glowing beautifully. They were all lined up in a line but slowly moving as if apart of a flock. Upon observing their brilliance they started to fade out one by one, slowly and gracefully as if on a dimmer switch. When they all finally faded after a few moments we couldnt wrap our heads around what we had seen. I have noticed they are very similar to the PHX lights sighting in the 90s.
