{cak} Object Stationary, flashing Red, and Green with constant Blue/White Lights

While sitting on my front porch smoking a cig at 5 AM, I noticed a Blue/White light that appeared to be in orbit. It was flashing red & green constantly and did not move for an hour of observation. The only possibility other than a UFO would be the International Space Station? However I was unaware the lights from it could be seen from the ground. It was eerie that it was not moving. I did not lose sight of it at anytime. As dawn broke it was obscured by its brightness. I tried to record it with my phone, but could not see it on the screen. I am keeping the video none the less in case it could be enhanced somehow.
