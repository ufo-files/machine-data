{nd} Large camouflaged Ufo approached south-to-north silently with triangular light formations

I had recently watched the film "Sirius" and had begun to engage in intense meditations at night with the intention of initiating contact with extraterrestrial life. I was on my back porch meditating on the night in question. My concentration was broken however when I heard a rush of wind approaching in the distance. It made no mechanical noise, whatsoever, throughout the entire event. I tried to ignore the sound until it grew so loud it sounded as if it were right above me. My view being obstructed by the awning over the porch, I stepped down the stairs and into the grass. As I looked around, a craft slowly appeared from behind. It was incredibly large with no visible wings, or any other appendages. It was a simple cigar shape, but at least the length of a football field. It was perfectly camouflaged to blend in with the night sky. I was only able to see it due to the slight shift in depth perception around the craft, since it was only traveling at a height just above the treetops. As it appeared from the south, I observed 3 white lights on the craft in the shape of a large isosceles triangle. Once the original triangle was centered above me, a second set of 3 red lights appeared within the white triangle to form its own smaller isosceles triangle. It continued north in a straight line until out of sight. I attempted to follow in my car but quickly lost sight of the craft.
