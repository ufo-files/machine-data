saw 4 large glowing white objects in the sky

Our son, 9, came in from stargazing to tell us hed seen four large glowing white (golfball sized) objects traveling from south to north. They were in a group and moving very fast. His total sighting was less than 3 seconds. He came in to get us and ran back outside, with me following. He observed three of the same size and shaped objects traveling now north to south. This is not the first time hes observed these same objects in our vicinity. 

He was understandably excited about having seen these, but at the same time nervous about what hed seen and what they are, considering hes seen them before. We did observe a jet traveling about 20,000 feet above after the second set of lights were observed. 

The objects were fast-moving and glowing white, according to my son. 

On 7/13/2013 I observed about 4:30 AM a large, silent "plane" above our town. It was pulsating green and white on wing tips and a red light on the nose. This was an extremely large plane and yet it made no noise and moved slower than a jet would at that altitude. I would estimate it to have been about 15,000 feet up but again, no sound. It was headed in a southeast-northwest direction.
