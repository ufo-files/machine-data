Lights sight, wired relaxation before sleep, irregular heart beat recorded on Fitbit.

I saw a bright light from the kitchen window. It looked like 3 lights spinning and changing colors together. I recorded 2 short videos with my cell phone. I had other similar experiences in the past. This is the first time I recorded and have evidences.
