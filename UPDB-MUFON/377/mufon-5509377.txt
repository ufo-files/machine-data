(GS) 10-12; In a strange W pattern

My sister and I were about to leave to take photos for a friend--she was letting out the dog. I stood at the edge of the lawn looking at the local park and saw these bright lights. I thought maybe "huh, military?" and then when I saw more we ran across the street to the open lawn to watch them across the sky. There were about 10 and they just barely cleared the treeline. W formation until they just flickered and went out. Were unsure if someone set off paper lamps after the fireworks display--merely 20-30 minutes before--or if it was something unexplained.

We drove out in the direction of it and kept track of our times in case we had an encounter but we didn't see anything.

It was a bit unnerving but being an enthusiast Id like some answers.

Unfortunately, our camera and phones couldnt capture the objects--we just about lost our heads because of it.
