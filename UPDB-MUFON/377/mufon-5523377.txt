3 orange circle lights appear and fade, 4 at the most appear at one time and then fade away

As I was driving over Hwy 544 bridge in Myrtle Beach, SC 10/13/11 at approximately 8:26pm I noticed 3 orange circle lights appear (equal distant apart). As one faded away another would appear. I saw as many as 4 at one time. As the four faded away another cluster of 3 appear for a few seconds and then they faded away. The event lasted for about 20 seconds. I kept looking for them to reappear but no lights showed back up as I was driving.
