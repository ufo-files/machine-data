Craft hovering outside my 2nd floor bedroom window...asking me to come with them

I had a Alien visitation or close encounter in my twenties and i was the contactee. In the middle of the night, Aliens come to my second-floor bedroom window in a craft hovering outside my window. The three small aliens appeared standing in their craft in a large windshield and wanted me to come with them. This was communicated to me telepathically.

As they were sending me the message. "COME WITH US" my bedroom was highly illuminated and simultaneously a gas like substance filled my room causing a state of "FROZENNESS"....in my encounter I Prayed to God to have them leave and within seconds they were gone and I peacefully went back to sleep.

As anticipated, the next night they came back and telepathically sent me a message: "BE GOOD AND DO GOOD". And l lived this MANTRA for the rest of my life.

Within the next few years, I decided to live the Mantra concept: Be Good and Do Good and took a career in Law Enforcement.
