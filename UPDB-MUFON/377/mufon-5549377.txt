Lighted disk hovering and moving slowly

A lighted disk shape object was hovering west of Evergreen, moving slowly. I saw it four separate times within 45 minutes. Each time it hovered and then disappeared away from me or moved behind a mountain (Floyd Hill). I have seen this object three times/ days this week, each time in the late afternoon just before dark. The first 2 times I was driving and was not confident in what I saw. Today I was outside my house facing west and had my husband come out and look too. It looked like a very bright star but it was daylight. It faded away from us to the west as we watched. I saw it 3 more times while driving to Floyd Hill to pick up my child from daycare.
