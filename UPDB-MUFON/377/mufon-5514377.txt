(GS) Craft with sequential lights off I-10 West about 40 miles from quartzite

My roommate/friend and I were traveling back to Los Angeles on the I-10 in Arizona about 40 miles from Quartzite. His car got a flat and we had to pull over. He didn't have a jack nor a spare tire so he had to call AAA for a tow to the nearest town. It was roughly 10PM and while he was on the phone with AAA, I spotted some strange lights off in the distance (about 4 miles away) above a small mountain range. It was a craft of some sort that didn't have any blinking lights or resemble anything Id ever seen before. It was traveling too fast to be a helicopter, and too low to be a any kind of plane. It caught my eye because we were in the middle of nowhere and I saw it in my field of vision as I was looking out into a small mountain range to the north of the highway. After about 7 seconds, it just disappeared and I asked my friend if he saw it, and he said no while then proceeding to joke with the AAA operator saying, "My friend thinks he saw a UFO or something".

  I didn't think much of it so we left it at that. About 15 minutes later, were still waiting for a tow truck. Im leaning against the side of his car on the rear passenger side still facing north towards the mountains with my hands in my pockets as it was the first Wednesday night after Thanksgiving and it was a little chilly. Hes sitting in the passenger seat with the door open facing the same direction. All of a sudden right directly in front of us this craft appears. Its hovering still, making absolutely no noise, about a mile away and maybe 500 feet in the air. I cant remember if it had 4-5 lights. Its lights would turn on simultaneously in pairs. After one second, they would turn off and the next two closest lights would illuminate. When they illuminated it wasnt a "quick" illumination like a flashlight turning on. The lights kind of glowed brighter and brighter quickly like an amplifier tube turning on except a lot faster than an amplifier tube. I cant remember which colors the lights were but I do remember there being various colors. I do think orange and red were two of the colors for sure, with maybe one or two other color but I cant recall what they were. It was very dark out as we were in the middle of nowhere so the stars were very visible, the reason why I know I saw something there was because this object was blocking stars. That is to say I couldnt see any stars where this object was so I can only assume I did see a solid object. This lasted somewhere between 10-15 seconds. 

I can just remember an overwhelming feeling of excitement and my friend saying something to the likes of "whoa, NOW I see that s**t!" and me saying "Holy S**t! F**k! WHAT THE F**K! THAT S**TS REAL!"

Unfortunately we didn't grab our phones fast enough to catch anything interesting. I just remember reaching in my pocket, turning on my screen, hitting camera, switching to video mode and maybe 1-2 seconds right before I hit record, it was just gone. 

  It didn't "woosh" away, didn't make ANY noise. As these lights were flashing in appx 1 second increments in pairs, the final 2 lit up just like it had been doing the entire time it was there, but instead of the next 2 lighting up, the final 2 lights turned off and it was just gone.  I could see stars in its place again. It had just disappeared. In the video recording you can just hear our excitement immediately following the incident. 

  One thing that always stays with me though is the fact that as it was happening, I had this sense that it knew, or whatever or whoever was inside it knew that we were there.
