So when we were coming in from a night enjoying a fire in back yard. As we were putting chairs away. I and my next door neighbors son. We saw big Orange/yellow glowing slowly moving toward us very low. It kept coming no noise at all. I was standing on my deck and neighbors are now on their deck. This orange/yellow object came to a stop over our city. As we are watch it gets lower and lower. The neighbors and I almost said at same time. We said it looks like a flying bus. We watched this for 5 to 10 minutes. Than this object started going up straight up. Than it went faster up and disappeared into the stars we could see it anymore. We were shocked my wife would come outside. Yet my neighbor son who was 22 yrs and his mother about same age as me. I will never forget seeing this most amazing thing I have ever seen. Now I'm always looking up. Wondering if I'm going to see something like this again.
