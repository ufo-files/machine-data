Observed two translucent crescent shaped objects flying side by side in a northwesterly direction. Objects at a very high elevation->35000ft at a high rate of speed. Between golf ball and baseball size. Sighting lasted ~5seconds.

I was looking out of my seventh floor west facing apartment window on a clear day obseving the sky as I often do. It was approximately noon (Feb-March?) when I noticed high in the sky above me two translucent crescent shaped objects. They were travelling side by side (convex side forward) at a high rate of speed (500-1000mph?) in a northwesterly direction. They were travelling in a straight path at a constant elevation (35000 ft?) until they disappeared in the distance behind the mountains. I heard no sound but I was indoors. The sighting lasted approximately 5 seconds. I am a senior and have always had an interest in ufos and over the years have had 1 or 2 suspected sightings but this was the most unmistakable one. It left me feeling very positive and eager to share the experience with a few close family members and friends. I regret not reporting this to Mufon sooner when the exact timing would have been more accurate.
