{cak} Bright light moving west to east

Went outside to grab the babies diaper bag from the car and looked up to see two unusually bright lights, one of which I am pretty sure was a planet and the other moving next to it headed east overhead. I and my girlfriend have observed this light before numerous times and it always appears this time of year and is always moving in the same direction. It moved about 35 or 40 degrees through the sky then faded out as usual. Photos were snapped with an android Zeki tablet with a crap front facing camera... it was bright enough to register it as the only light in the sky, even next to planets and bright stars. Look closely though as it is hard to see.
