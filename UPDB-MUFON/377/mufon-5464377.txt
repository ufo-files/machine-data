{rjl} I just saw something weird then took a picture

I was taking my dog out to use the bathroom in my backyard which faces north east. I saw something flying in the distance above the tree line. I didnt know what it was so I took a picture of it (see attached). I didnt think anything of it until I looked at the picture. Then I thought, wow this is not something I had ever seen. It didnt do anything crazy just flew in a straight line across my backyard. When my dog was done using the bathroom we came back inside. I showed my wife the picture and she agreed that it was not normal. Its November now and I was just looking though some old pictures and remembered that night. So here I am, submitting my story. Not sure its anything special just wanted to share.
