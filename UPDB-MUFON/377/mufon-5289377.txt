Two circular orb type bright lights in my bedroom.

Two circular orb type lights 12 inches in diameter that hovered over me and my husband while we were in bed and then moved to the side of the room and out through the window. The light was very bright and they stared at me. I did not feel any fear just felt courteous about why they were there. After that night many times my husband and I wondered what they were and why they were in our bedroom.
