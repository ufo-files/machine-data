{tjd} Driving home late in winter, observed a strange, glowing orb in the sky that turned to follow me up my driveway as if it “saw” me.

On January 4, 2013, I was driving home from my friend’s house. It was a cold, clear winter evening; no cloud cover, just wide, empty sky. I was driving down White Rock Road in Davis Junction, Illinois when I observed an oval, brightly glowing object in the sky. It was quite bright and prominently oval-like. Similar to an unusual looking star. I stopped my vehicle in the middle of the road, unsure of what I was seeing. I observed it hover for a minute or so over a field, trying to understand what it was. I opened my windows and could hear no noise, despite being in the country and having no other sound source around me. As I continued watching, it grew larger, appearing to move closer to where I was parked. A strange wave of fear and disbelief came over me; I turned into our driveway, sped to the top, and tried calling my brother so he could see what I was seeing. He didn’t answer. That’s when the object began descending and moving rapidly towards my new location. It hovered directly in front of my line of sight overhead, I snapped a quick picture, and ran inside my house in terror. I have fantastic vision, was 100% sober, and have not experienced anything else since. I have seen what helicopters look like in the fields at night—this was not that. I honestly felt as though this thing was able to “watch” me and that I sensed I was “watching” it. Thus its descent.
