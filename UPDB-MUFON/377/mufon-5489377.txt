{nd} high alt traveling NNW to SSE after short time course change WSW

I was facing north in my yard watching a large bird flying overhead. Then a round white light caught my attention. At first I thought it was a jetliner but it didn't have the same reflection. The light seemed to be coming from the object itself. It was coming from NNW directly overhead. It passed over and I lost sight momentarily as it went by the sun. A second or two later I picked it up again and shortly it made a course correction. It turned tighter than a jetliner but not a 90 deg. turn. New course was WSW and I lost sight as it flew into haze and light clouds.
