{dr}During 4th of July firework shows

It was July 4, 2014 and I was watching crazy local fireworks show in a neighborhood in Gardena, Ca. I was looking out my kitchen window at the fireworks EVERYWHERE (aka like a war was going on). I noticed a round orange colored, fireball-like, glowing object in the sky. It looked to be as high as a helicopter or higher...object looked pretty far away. It hovered for approximately 3-5 minutes in the sky on the outskirts of the fireworks. I thought this was very strange.....then just disappeared. It was between 10pm-11pm. I first thought it was a firework but it was neither ascending nor descending. It just stayed in the same spot. I then thought, could this be a UFO. I could figure no other explanation for the light and have never seen anything like it before. The object just disappeared, no fading out, just gone!
