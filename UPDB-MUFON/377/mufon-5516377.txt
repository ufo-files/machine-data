{cak} 3 orbs forming triange

I work at Mt. Sinai Hospital on Miami Beach. Im also homeless. I sleep in my car in the employee parking lot, which overlooks the bay. This evening I decided to stand on the bay and throw rocks in the water. I picked up four rocks and skidded them on the water. As i turned to pick up more rocks something bright in the sky caught my eye. I looked up and there were THREE ORBS IN THE SKY FORMING A TRIANGLE. I looked for about 10 seconds in sheer amazement and then ran to my car to retrieve my camera, which was in my front seat in my computer bag. I came back and snapped 6 pictures and then my camera started acting funny... it went into a 5 second delay setting, which I had NEVER set and then went dead. I had just recharged the battery and had only taken about 35 pictures total. I found that odd.
