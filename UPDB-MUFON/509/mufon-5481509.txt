{cak} Triangular shaped craft observed at night

I was outside at around midnight smoking when i spotted what looked like an airplane headed in my direction but soon realized it was totally silent and had two white lights at the base of the craft and one red light on the nose. The object passed overhead and then reversed course. I lost sight of the object as it proceeded in the direction from whence it first came.
