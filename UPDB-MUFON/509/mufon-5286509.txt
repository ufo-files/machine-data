A large inverted pyramid object was travelling in a southerly direction, then stopped and hovered above me.

I was sitting outside having a cigarette after the days work. I spotted what I first thought to be a plane. Nothing unusual about this because I often see passenger aircraft in that particular spot. What was unusual was it didn't maintain a planes usual flight path. Instead it flew straight towards me. It then hovered above me for 4-5 minutes. There were many cumulus clouds in the sky which made viewing glary. Tears were rolling down my cheeks as I stared up at this thing. Not believing what I was seeing, I stayed to observe it. After a while I ran inside to grab my partner, but in the time it took me to convince her to come outside, the object had disappeared.
