{nd} We observed a yellow white very fast opaque sphere traveling from Tustin area and descending over Seal Beach area

While walking the dog tonight in Santa Ana, around 7:50 PM, my girlfriend and I observed a yellow-ish white sphere traveling from the Southwest, and gradually descending, then either hovering or moving away from us, before dropping straight down somewhere to the northwest of us. I'm guessing it could've been around 5 to 10 miles away, traveling about four times the speed of any aircraft also visible in the area, and appeared to be half as bright as the visible stars and possibly opaque. I'm guessing it may have been 30 to 50 feet in diameter. It was completely silent as well. My thought was it could've possibly been landing at the Naval weapons Station near seal Beach. It definitely was not a conventional aircraft as we didn't see any strobes, navigation lights or landing lights etc. i'm always looking at the sky as this is the fifth unexplainable thing I've seen in my lifetime and the second report that I've made to MUFON. Really looking forward to the next one, thanks....
