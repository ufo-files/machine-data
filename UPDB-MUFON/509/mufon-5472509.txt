{jpg} observes a narrow, vertical column of lights over ocean, blinging green and red, alternating colors

I had to go out to my car at 11pm. My car was parked in a lot just inside the sand dunes with the Atlantic ocean 200 or 300 ft away. I observed something in the sky over the ocean... I kept watching and realized it was not a plane. It was a narrow verticle column of lights, blinking green and red... alternating (what blinked green then blinked red and visa versa). It stayed still for the 20 minutes or so I watched and then I went back inside. I would say it was at least a mile away and 20degrees above the horizon... it was in the air, very clear cloudless night sky. If it was a mile away I'd guess it was 200 to 300 feet tall... if it was farther then it was bigger........ the best way to describe it would be if you had a double column of colored spotlights attached to a telephone pole and the lights were as close to each other as possible and each light would blink green and then red and ten green again. I took a photo with my cell phone but it was so far away you can only make out that there are lights in a short verticle column (not as tall, not as many lights as I saw)... the colors did not resolve so it just looks white/offwhite and does not represent what I was seeing.
