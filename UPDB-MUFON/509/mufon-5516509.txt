(GS) Series of orange colored lit units uniformly spaced and trailing one another over the lake....disappearing as they reached a certain altitude.

My 19 year old son and friends were out on our dock at smith mountain lake around 10:45 in the evening of August 9, 2012. They came in to tell us to come look a t these lights they were seeing over the water. In a fairly uniform manner, were a series of orange colored moving vessels, rising from the southwest and ascending in an arch-type pattern and disappearing into the clouds after reaching a certain altitude. We watched approximately 10 of these cross the sky. No noise, or visible emissions were present. We wondered if it could possibly be a military manuever of some type? We looked to the 11:00 local news but didn't see any reports. This sighting was right across the lake from the black water marina in union hall.
