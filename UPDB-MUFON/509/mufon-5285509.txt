A seemingly satellite that stopped and reversed direction

I don't think this is the wopper of all UFO sightings, but I was laying on my back looking up at the sky in the middle of the night (I suspect early morning, 2ish). I caught a satellite out of the corner of my eye from the north heading due south. I've always enjoyed when I catch a satellite and will track it visually as it makes its way across the sky. The satellite was moving along its course until it got right above me (visually I mean, I didn't feel that it was in any way close, this was undoubtedly an object in the heavens), then it suddenly stopped, paused a moment, and then departed exactly back in the direction and velocity with which it had come. Like someone forgot to unplug the iron or something. That's it. Don't know what it was, am not claiming to, just saying that it wasn't a typical satellite because I'm quite certain they don't do that.
