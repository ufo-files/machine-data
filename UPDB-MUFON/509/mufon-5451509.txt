Large box like craft with bright white light on front.

Noticed a very bright light while driving north. As I approached it and realized it was moving forward, when I looked out my windshield at it I saw a large craft moving thru the sky. It was box like, almost like a cross with an extremely wide crossbar. I would estimate it’s elevation was anywhere from 500 to 3000 ft and I estimate it to be at least 300 ft long and about 200 ft wide. The bottom looked perfectly flat but on both the left and right side there seemed to be 5 or 6 rows of small lights but they were not on. I pulled over and got out to watch it but by then I could only see it through the tree tops but it looked like more lights had came on and then I lost sight of it.
