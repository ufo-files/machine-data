{nd} A row of glowing red-orange lights, barely visible outline of larger craft.

My brother and I were returning home from visiting a mutual friend. We were in my car, driving down back roads, when my brother pointed out a light he'd never seen before. I told him that it was a cell phone tower, before I realized that there were no cell phone towers in that area -- we'd driven down that road our entire lives and had never seen a light in that area.

I slowed down a little bit so we could get a better look at it. There was a row of three lights hovering above the cornfield. The sky was blacked out around the lights in an area roughly equal to the size of the field and 20-30 feet above the treeline, though prior to this the sky had been clear. The lights were fuzzy around the edges but did not flicker or waver.

We didn't know what to make of it, we discussed it for a minute or two, not really scared but just wondering what it was, before the lights quickly rose straight up into the sky, much faster than anything I've ever seen flying before, and promptly disappeared, returning full visibility to the stars. It was completely silent.

We just sat there in shock before I resumed driving us back home. I actually don't remember stopping, but the car was in park. We did not experience missing time or anything of that nature, but our heads were both what we can only describe as "fuzzy" for the next day, I assume because of the shock of what we had seen.
