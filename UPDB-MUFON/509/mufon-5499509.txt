I was watching stars when I saw a light appear out of nowhere. It became very bright and was like a diamond shape. Then it became very small and vanished into space. It took a total of 3 sec. Then i saw it happen again about 15 sec. Later

It was late evening and I was enjoying a bonfire with my girlfriend. I was staring at the stars almost straight up in the northwestern sky. Suddenly a small light appeared in the sky a bit bigger than a star. The light grew about 3 times bigger kind of in a diamond shape then shot straight up into space. The whole event took about three seconds. I was amazed at the speed and brightness of the object. Then about 10 or 12 seconds later I saw the same thing happen again very close to where the first one appeared. The event reminded me very much like what a spaceship in "Star Trek" looks like when they go to warp speed. I continued watching the stars for awhile but nothing more happened. My girlfriend was not looking up at the sky and missed it. I got up and went to my truck to check the time and it was 11:33 local time.
