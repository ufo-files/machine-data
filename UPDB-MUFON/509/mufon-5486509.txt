{cak} Bright and dim star like lights third dim star like first 2 vanished 1 descended

I was standing at my backdoor looking east into the sky where I saw one bright star like object and a smaller dimmer one next to it which caught my attention and looked odd because of their proximity to each other, another small dim one was to the right of those 2. The 2 on the left did not have any movement but the third one to the right started to descend disappearing behind a tree top, the bright and dim one to the left of the one that was descending suddenly disappeared like they were shut off. My wife had just been at the doorway with me when this happened, I called to her and told her what I had just seen. Two commercial jets flew over a couple of minutes later one just a little ahead of the other heading east, these were easy to see with their flashing red lights nothing like the lights that disappeared.
