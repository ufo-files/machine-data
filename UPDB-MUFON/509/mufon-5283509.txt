Very fast moving lights

I was travelling 100 klm an hour when an orange light caught my attention in right side front windshield. Thought it was an orange meteor but when observed it was an orange bar of light with a blueish white light on top travelling very fast passing me alongside of car, it started to turn to the right slowly and I could see the rear of it if 2 dull orange circles and than it disappeared. Very fast no noise .
