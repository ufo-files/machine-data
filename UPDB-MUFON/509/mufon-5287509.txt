White dots flying in looping path, first 2, then 3, then 4

I was at the Cenex in Sauk City at 1:04 PM, looked up NNE and saw two white dots way off, felt like above the Prairie du Sac Dam, they were flying in a looping pattern, they faded and then I could see three doing the same unusual looping path, a jet was approaching from east to west on the same path throughout, then I couldn't see the three white dots and noticed four of them really faint fading slowly to the north...the speeds were constant and no right angles...maybe very high flying birds, but didn't seem like that at all...felt like I wouldn't be able to see birds at the perceived distance, but it could have been seasonal pelicans
