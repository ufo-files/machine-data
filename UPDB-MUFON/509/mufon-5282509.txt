Heightened Sensed being watched and sensed a craft was directly in front of me.

Walked out to waterline of beach. Was a very cold and windy day - had two children with me age 11 and 12. We took photos and returned to car and stayed in car facing ocean for about 2 hours. During that time I had a strong sense that we were being watched by a craft in the sky and in my sensing this I mentioned to the kids we were being watched and to keep a keen eye out at the ocean - that I thought we could see a craft go into the ocean. However we did not see it at that time. The following day after this experience I was exhausted and had a strong /intense headache.
