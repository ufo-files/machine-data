{cak} Missing time and flashing lights

I drive home from Monument, CO from work the same way 5 to 6 times a week at various times of day so I know my route very well. On the night of 02/05/19 I was driving home on southbound I-25 and hit the exit for East 24 (exit 139) at 10:34 pm. The next thing I remember is I'm about 30 to 40 feet up the exit ramp and I see a green light pulsate 2-3 times around my car and when I looked at the clock it was 10:48 pm. There were no other vehicles around me at the time. I did not observe any craft or entities at the time of the incident. I honestly wasn't even sure I should report the incident as I don't know that I was abducted as there were no odd markings or wounds that I found on my body when I got home. However I did notice that after I got home that night and into the next morning that at least 1 of my 3 cats insisted on being by my side and would not leave me alone which is not typical behavior for them. They almost acted like I would be taken from them.
