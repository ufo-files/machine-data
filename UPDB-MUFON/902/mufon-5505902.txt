{nd} slow moving ball of fire that zooms off in another direction

Sitting in my house working on laptop, glanced up and saw a yellow ball of fire slowly moving across the sky. Pointed it out to my 5 year old, we both ran outside to get a better look. At first I thought it was a low flying airplane with an engine on fire but there were not lights and it was completely silent. I ran back in the house to grab video camera and began filming the object. several seconds later the object changed course from SE to E and accelerated until it disappeared. I can not think of any legitimate object that would glow like a ball of fire, slowly across the sky then suddenly change course and accelerate at such a high rate of speed. Video uploaded to youtube.
