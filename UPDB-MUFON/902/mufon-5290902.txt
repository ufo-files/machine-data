A light floating a few feet above the trees

We were off second shift from a factory we took one car to work we were talking about 1130 after work a white light came floating down the street above the trees we both said ufo I waved at it then I was looking down at myself waving up at myself I looked up saw movement in the darkness then I was back waving up at the light we saw the light three nights in a row the last night I ran into the street under it ,it shot straight up a 40 feet or so and continued at the same speed as before last time we saw it
