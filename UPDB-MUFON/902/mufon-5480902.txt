{cak} Object above rocket during launch 8 Sep 2016

I video taped, using my Samsung Note 4 phone at full zoom, launch from my home Approximately 50 miles from launch site at 1810 hours on 8 Sep 2016. I did not notice object until I viewed the video. At first I thought it was a booster coming off rocket, but it is clearly well above the rocket when first appears so cant be a booster falling. When viewing video the object appears above rocket traveling from north to south until object is out of frame of video. I emailed some still shots to a NASA rep who told me he didn't kmow what object is either.
