We were driving home on A highway headed north. Husband was driving, I was in passenger seat, daughter was in backseat. I saw a light ahead flashing that didnt seem normal even from a distance(about quarter of a mile). My husband replied it might be a tower. I knew some how that it wasnt right. Couldnt think clearly. All I could think was "Oh my god. This is wrong." Then husband said maybe hellicopter. Within a few a seconds we were directly under it in the middle of road. We stopped for aproximitly 10 15 seconds, felt like a lot longer. It was about 25 feet above us. I say this because I could see things on the bottom of it and when we drove off it was about ten to fifteen feet above the trees. It was triangular,dull grey, darker long shapes on bottom. Brighter light in middle, two smaller lights on both sides of front point, two small lights on both sides of large light, then three lights across wider backend. The light was not normal. Dont know how to explain it. Bottom was flat and had some sort of bars or legs that the lights kind of reflected off of. My husband wanted to get out. I yelled for him to go. I was scared and wanted to get the hell out of there. He started to drive away and I grabbed for my phone to take pic but was to shook up and scared to work my phone. It sat in same spot after we drove away. We could see it untill trees started blocking it out. Three or four cars passed as we were driving and it still didnt move. Later Husband and daughter said they couldnt stop staring at strange lights. My husband acted happy and giggly. My daughter was scared also.
