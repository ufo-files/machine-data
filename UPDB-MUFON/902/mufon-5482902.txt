{cak} Orb like light floating in my neighbors field across the street. It hovered and changed directions at least 7 times. Would disappear and reappear at random.

I was sitting on my porch enjoying the breeze before bedtime. I noticed a bright light across the street too low to be a star. It's started floating back and forth over my neighbor's field and house. I watched it for almost 5 minutes before I started recording it. It flashed bright and seemed to disappear. The it came back again, floated over the farm again and flashed bright once more. Then it disappeared. I stayed outside for about another hour to try and see if it came back and it never did. Third time I've seen things like this. First time I was able to capture it on camera.
