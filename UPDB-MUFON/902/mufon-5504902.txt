I went and got my Samsung Tab 2 and began filming, honestly I was a little aggravated because it seemed so small looking at the recording. Then I noticed when I was watching the video the other one I had saw but it seemed to be coming off of the craft or whatever that was. And confusing to me is that in different viewing programs it was in a different angle, so I am trying to figure out if its upside down. I did not have a way to rotate it. So please feel free to figure that out. The copy I made with the enhanced brightness was WOW, all I know is had I known this was hanging around outside our neighborhood, I probably wouldn't have this footage. To know I was this close terrifies me because trust me the camera on a Samsung Tab 2 has limited capabilities, so I have this feeling it was pretty close.
