Chartreuse light. Descent perpendicular to horizon @ meteor-like speed. Lights of Santa Fe flashed on & off twice as it disappeared below horizon.

We were returning from an evening star watching stroll in the nearby arroyo. The light was brilliant and incredibly fast! I've seen hundreds of shooting stars in my life. None like this. It was "fatter" than other meteors and the chartreuse color was unique to me as a star gazer. Its descent was 90 degrees perpendicular to the horizon in a perfect straight drop. Most peculiar of all was the way the distant glow of the lights of Santa Fe flashed on and off twice as if someone had a master switch to turn off all the lights. Truly amazing to us. It was quite far away it seemed to me. Closer to Santa Fe than Galisteo. I knew the area very well as I lived in the area for 8 years.
