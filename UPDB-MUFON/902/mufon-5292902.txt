Seen {round, black, silhouette disc that had small, dim lights of greyish tone, inward slightly, around the perimeter} through opening in very low clouds. - {djs}

I was fishing for smelt in the drain at the singing bridge, between US-23 and Lake Huron in Arenac county, Michigan, with my family. I was 16 years old. It was around 11:00pm at night and the clouds were so low, I could shine my flashlight on them. As I was doing so, I noticed a small, opening in the clouds and could see the darkness of the rual, night sky, highlighted by a dense cluster of stars (possibly the Milky way).
As I looked at the opening, a round black, silhouette, darker than the dark sky between the stars, passed from west to east, across the opening. It took about 2 seconds. The disc (?) Had small, dim lights, of a greyish tone, inward slightly, around the perimeter. Possibly 8 or 10 total.
After it passed back behind the clouds, I told my stepmother about it and showed her what I was doing with the flashlight, and while we were looking at the light on the clouds, it crossed another opening in the clouds, just as before. My stepmother saw it as well, that time. My father and she got divorced a couple years later and I haven't seen or been in touch with her since. I don't know if she's still alive. She would be about 78 years old now.
