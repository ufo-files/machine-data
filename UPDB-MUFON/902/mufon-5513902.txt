{cak} Big Bright Multicolored UFO Manchester, TN

I was inside gaming and had all the windows closed. I noticed a loud roaring type sound over all the usual sounds in the house, so I walked outside to take a look around. I seen all the stars and then I seen a big bright object cycling through every color. I immediately compared its size to the north star and Venus, this object was bigger than any star in the sky and brighter mainly because of the brilliant colors. 
A small white star like object shot out of it a couple of times. In the video you can hear the sound pretty well (it was much louder in person), see it change shape, hover and move. 
I used a Nikon camera on a tripod set to manual focus. I did have to adjust the tripod a few times when the object moved.
It stayed around for about 30 to 40 minutes. I was actually videoing it for about 30 mins, but I heard it a few minutes before that so I am guessing 40 mins total. I went back out and checked and the object and the sound was gone. Just before this happened I heard a booming sound in the distance. Not sure if that had anything to do with what I witnessed. 
This was incredible what ever it was!!!
