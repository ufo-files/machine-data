{jpg} Orange lights appeared overhead and changed shape before disappearing

One small orange light appeared to the west. I thought it was a shooting star until I noticed the orange colour and no trail being left behind. All of a sudden, one by one, four to six more lights appeared. They were in a similar formation to birds flying. I told my girlfriend to look up and she saw this phenomenon just as the lights started to shift formation. They remained in a triangular formation but some of the lights became a little closer to each other in the line they were in. They then disappeared over the tree line. It was a quiet night and we heard no noise from them. We sat there for maybe another ten minutes sort of in shock just staring at the sky. A plane flew by and it was nothing like the lights witnessed beforehand
