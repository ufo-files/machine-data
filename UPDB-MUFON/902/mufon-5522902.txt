Observed lights over Lees Summit, MO

On Halloween night, a big group of us were taking our children trick-or-treating and we observed lights, fairly low in the sky, coming over my rooftop from west to east. As the lights got over our heads, we heard the distinct sound of jet airplanes in formation. We observed these lights circling Lees Summit for approximately 45 minutes. The reason I'm writing you is because we've been watching some of the flap over this particular sighting, and while we've seen some weird stuff in the sky over Lees Summit in the past few months, this was undeniably a group of jets flying perfectly in formation.
