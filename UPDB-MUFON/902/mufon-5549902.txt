Ufo Sighting in Northern Wisconsin

1. My family was in Northern Wisconsin camping. My father and I were fishingoff an old wooden train bridge(which we did quite often) at the Rat river, WI.2.My father noticed it first and pointed it out to me.3. I wasnt sure what it waswhen I saw it.4. I was surprised and interested and continued to observe itthrought the sighting.5. What I saw was a small red light like a pinpoint orstar that flew in random directions from behind one star to a point behindanother star. It continued to do this for the entire time of the sighting.6. Itstopped darting out from behind the stars.
