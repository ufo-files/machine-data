{dr}Star-like object that seemed to be rotating.

First noticed this object a few weeks ago. Ive noticed this light 5 times in the past 3 weeks. Definitely wasnt an airplane or a helicopter. I live not to far from the Willow Grove Air Base so I definitely know what a airplane & helicopter look like at night. Its was not a star, planet or satellite. Was able to watch this object for about 30 mins. Went back in the house & came out to check on it about an hr later it was gone. It was a clear night out. This object seemed to at times grow extremely bright & then dim. Looked like it was rotating. Hovered for 15 minutes & then very slowly started to move to the east. Several airplanes and a helicopter in the area. Way more then I normally see on a given night. And they were all in the area of this object. My husband was also puzzled by this object. Attaching a picture which does not do it justice it was several times brighter than the picture.
