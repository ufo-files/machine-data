My daughter & I were walking out of our house towards the car. As soon as we got outside, we look up to see this fireball hovering for a second then sped off fast(hundreds miles per hour) going in mostly a straight path then decending downwards slightly. This object had a fireball trail & at first thought it was a meteor but there was a blinking light on craft & the way it sped off wasn't meteorish. It was lower than plane height(little below clouds) and about 1/4 mile away from our house. As soon as I saw it I knew what I had just seen. My daughter & I were shocked. We ran back into the house to tell my mother what we had just seen. So cool.
