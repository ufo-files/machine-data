Large Shrouded Mothership over Ft. Myers

Photographed this very large craft near Fort.Myers FL Feb. 05, 2021 at 1:40pm. I recognized this anti-gravity capable vessel due to an earlier sighting and photo I was able to capture and documented in MUFON case # 103691 Sept.26,2019 at 10:40AM of a much smaller but similar vessel. The weather conditions on Feb.05, 2021 were nearly identical to the previous sighting with deep blue clear sky, no wind, and very few clouds present. This craft hung stationary for over 15 minutes and I was able to snap multiple shots in the hopes of getting at least one high quality photo. What was great about this chance encounter was that a commercial jet had just taken off and was flying directly at the huge shrouded ship that probably did not appear on the jet's radar. The jet liner is easily seen on the left hand side of the first photo and makes judging the size of this craft at 4000 feet long as a realistic estimate ( if this object is actually in our dimension of space/time). I have included several addtional photos that highlight some of the features that can be discerned on this huge ship. Of particular interest in the middle of the ship is a synthetic fog that can clearly be seen pumping out of a square exhaust port and the fog appears to be electrically charged so that it is curling back and drawn to the ship's surfaces. The left end of the craft appeared to be where the propulsion units are housed. On the right end of the ship are several protruding features that look like radar dishes or possibly particle beam or laser devices. There is also a large glowing sphere with large tubes protruding (perhaps a power plant). Finally note the smaller cloud at the upper right partially shrouding a smaller vessel.... Alien or man-made, some people know the answers.
