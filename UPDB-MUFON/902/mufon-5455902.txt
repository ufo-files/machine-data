Strange large light over Gorey

I got up to get a drink and looked out the window, I seen a bright pulsating light (brighter than stars) traveling quickly and then it changes its path with a curved shape and headed a different direction. I went to the front of the house to get a better view and it was very big and had no rythem to its flashing lights. I asked a family member to confirm what I'd seen. She seen it too and we watch it fly away. It was faster than a plane in the sky and looked solid.
