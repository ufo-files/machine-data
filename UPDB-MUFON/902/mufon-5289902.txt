I saw and digitally photographed three U.F.O.'s

July 25, 2016 just after arriving at work I had my digital camera, a Nikon p520, and seeing a nice sunrise took some pictures. When I pointed SW I saw 3 strange objects in the sky. They seemed to be very high up in the atmosphere for there was some blue haze between me and them. I'm enclosing a digital photo and all I did to enhance it was to increase contrast and decrease exposer a little.
