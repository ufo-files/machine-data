I took a few pictures of what I thought was a bright star, but it turned out to be something far less explainable.

I was at home at the time and had been out for a walk when I happened to glance up at the sky before going in for the night. That was when I saw the bright star-like dot which at that moment, that’s all I thought it was, but it seemed brighter than most stars that night. So, I decided to grab my camera ‘a Nikon D3200 with a 55 to 300mm lens’ and take a few pictures just to see how well it could capture the brightness of the ‘star’. Getting my camera, I left it on auto for moving objects and disabled the flash option, going back outside it didn’t look like the ‘star’ had moved so I took two quick pictures- the main one which is very clear and shows a lot of detail and the second which is far more blurry and less clear on what’s being pictured. When I saw the first one going back through the images I just took I was shocked to saw the least, I ran inside to show my parents and a few moments later when I went outside again the bright ‘star’ was gone either the clouds covered it, or it finally moved. I don’t know what I took a picture of that night, but I have tried several times to replicate it without success.
