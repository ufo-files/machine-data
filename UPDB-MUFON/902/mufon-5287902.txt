Ball of gas light object hovering in to lights formation

I was walking to Hainault Train station New North Road Essex England.
When i was walked past some allotments which were on my left hand side.
i heard a sound that sounded like burning kind of how a gas cooker or how A bunsen burner might sound when i saw in the corner of my eye what i thought be a Chinese lantern.
I remember thinking this object was hovering up and down to much to be a lantern i pulled out my phone to start filming but remember thinking it must just be high winds or must be another explanation.
By the time i went to continue walk it was now ascending upwards which i found super strange it was late at night and i was the only one around and felt uneasy about what i was seeing.
As the object ascended towards the clouds the object changed from a ball of gas looking object to 4 lights in a diamond like shape and lit up different colours and then faded towards the clouds.
I have not really spoke about this experience and people often try to palm it off as something else but i know what i saw and knew this was something well out of the ordinary
