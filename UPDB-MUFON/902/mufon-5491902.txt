{dr}I heard a loud wind in our trees, all else seemed calm, I saw a bright light that hovered above electric lines in a eucalyptus tree behind our house. I was mesmerized and stood there a short moment but when I awoke it much more time had passed.

I seemed to me in a trance. WHen I awoke I felt disturbed but couldn't figure out why. As I sat down on my bed I felt a sore spot on the back of my right calf. It was a hard bump as if a piece of metal was inside. But there was no wound on the skin. It is still there.
