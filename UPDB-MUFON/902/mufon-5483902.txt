{rjl} I was taking my dad who is deceased now, to Ramer Alabama & we saw it cross a field from a church. it took off and i dropped my dad off to gis house then on the way back, i saw it again.

I was taking my dad home back to the country. The bright lights made me first notice it.At first i thought the bright lights were a police car up ahead before we bent the curve. It had a dome shape like a saucer with 3 stacks of lights going around in different directions. It hovered off the ground and it was force that was blowing the trees like it wanted to blow it down but there was no noise. I was afraid to go outside at night after the event for over 6 months, scared and wondering. I would not go back to Ramer Alabama at night at all. I lost sight of it after the ship put me back down and i watched it until it went out of sight.
