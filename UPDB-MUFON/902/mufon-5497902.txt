{cak} orb appeared at top of ridge across lake, descended into trees, came half way down to lake, paused, reversed direction

Camping/fishing on Lewis Smith lake with two friends. The object appeared just above the top of the ridge across the lake, approx 1/4 mile line of sight. After pausing there for a few moments it descended, coming slowly down the hillside toward the lake in the trees, not above them. We heard no sound.                                 We were concerned because it did not appear like a lantern or anything we'd seen before. It's hard to accurately estimate the size but it wasn't large maybe 4-8 feet in dia. It had a defined ball shape. The night was dark and clear, very quiet.                       We called out and waited for a response which did not come. I fired a rifle into the air, still no response.                     After a few moments it went back up the hillside, appearing to float and was not impeded by the trees. Before it went over the ridge line, it went dark.                                          I have told friends about this but never filed any kind of official report other than this. I never felt I have anything to gain or lose by reporting it. I've read all kinds of bunk about atmospheric/weather phenomena, swamp gas, etc. I'm not sure what this was but I don't think swamp gas is the answer.                               After the sighting I spent 20 years in the USAF. I saw a LOT of aircraft. Including working in the SR-71/U-2 program for 6 years. This was not an aircraft, not the moon, not someone carrying a lantern.
