Unidentified light 'teleports' around night sky culminating in 8 flashes of object in two parallel lines.

Driving west of Silver City, New Mexico after dark on the 180, I noticed a strange orange-red light about 45 degrees out of the drivers side window of the vehicle. Assumed these were landing lights of low level aircraft arriving or departing airport nearby, but color was not conventional. Felt affirmed when light vanished. about 30 seconds later light seen again to the SSE of vehicle again seen from drivers window. This time I watched object 'blink' out in about .5 seconds and re appear in a reverse 'blink' on the complete opposite side of the sky from SSE of vehicle to NW of vehicle. Light was stationary for a few moments then vanished again, and was seen about 60 seconds later in different part of sky through low clouds directly over vehicle about 90 degrees from center of drivers window. I then lost sight of the object for a few moments. After about another 30 seconds i was still scanning the sky looking for any more sigh of this strange object or objects when directly in front of the vehicle about 30 degrees elevation and in the western sky the object then appeared and disappeared 8 times with in two parallel lines across the night sky. Like an equal to symbol tilted to about 20 degrees, constituting four orbs per line. The object flashed on then of and as it "blinked out" of one of the 8 positions described it simultaneously "blinked in" to the next space. This blink was like the opening and closing of an aperture, but in perfect timing and sequence as described above.  the best representation of this is:
                         4 *           8  *
                    3  *           7  *
                2  *           6  *
           1  *          5  *

The numbers here represent the order in which the 'object' "blinked in and out".  I do not know how else to describe this. It was bizarre, to say the least.
I made it to my mothers(presently deceased) house about 5 minutes later and the object was still visible in the northern sky for many minutes after i arrived, and my mother, Priscilla, did witness this, but it did not appear to her extraordinary. For the rest of the incident no other strange behavior was observed. The object hung high in the sky at about 75 degrees for maybe 10 minutes motionless and was not seen again after that.
