{dr}UFO with red-orange lights in Sequim Area

My daughter and I were driving down a rural county road in Sequim, WA around 9:40pm in late July 2015. No street lights in vicinity. To the left (west) we saw a flying object. What caught my attention was that it had red/orange pulsing glow to it and was flying in our direction. It then stopped and moved in the total opposite direction very quickly. I have never witnessed a UFO, but this was so unusual that we pulled over and turned off the car to watch/listen. It moved very fast and in several different directions. The movement and color were very unusual. It was definitely not an airplane or a helicopter. They wouldn't be able to move in different directions that quickly. Also, the color was odd. Not like the blinking lights on an airplane or helicopter. It was more of a pulsing red/orange glow...if that's how to describe it? The experience was very unsettling as I have never witnessed anything like this in my life. We talk of it often as it really made us aware that UFO's can very well be real after witnessing this first hand.
