It was a clear sky no clouds around 8:40 a.m. July 6th ,2020. I was at a stop sign looking straight ahead and looked up to see this metallic looking image in the sky that looked like a blimp traveling at a pretty good steady clip going from West to East. I thought it might be a plane but could not see wings or a tail on it. I turned left onto the road went about a block looked again to see if it was a plane going into our airport or leaving and heading south. I could not see it so drove another block and turned onto the main road again heading South looked out of my windows to see if it was a plane and it was no longer there. It made no sound and had no lights on it. It was just a shinny silver color in the sunlight.
