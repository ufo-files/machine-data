It looked like a shooting star, and separated into 5 separate orbs

I was headed home on 70 east, and I saw wat looked like a shooting star. As I continued driving the light never faded out, like a shooting star would do. I lost sight of it for a minute, because of a treeline, but after the clearing- the one light I first saw had separated into 5 separate lights(Orbs). When I first saw it I thought it was a shooting star, but it didn't burn out like one. Then I told my fiance that it was a UFO, she told me I was tripping and that it was so aircraft performing from the Aircraft Show that was here. I said not this late at night. Bottomline, I was surprised to witness a event like this in Kansas City. I saw a sighting in Arizona, that was similiar but it was five lights that were in a diagonal line and they just hovered and then disappeared.
