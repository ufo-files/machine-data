my wife and i were sitting in our hot tub in the back yard looking at the stars, when all of a sudden i saw three objects in a triangle pattern. I couldn't speak so i pointed at them to my wife and she said what the hell is that? It seemed as if they weren't glowing but reflecting light from the moon, when it got to a certain angle i could make out a dark triangle shape with a "glowing" triangle like shape on each corner. The objects at each corner appeared to be a triangle shape,but had a weird shape to them almost like a design. It was about the size of a football field, and it's really hard to judge how long we actually saw it for. It seemed like minutes but I would say it was only a couple of seconds maybe five to ten. It was moving quickly in a upward motion, then just vanished. It never made a sound, and it was a clear night with a full moon. It was almost like it "cloaked itself" and disappeared. My Wife and I were cold and had goosebumps and i could feel every hair on my body stand up. We were sitting in 100 degree water in the middle of summer. I jumped up and ran inside to see what time it was, and had intentions on reporting this last night but my computer was upgrading to windows 10.
