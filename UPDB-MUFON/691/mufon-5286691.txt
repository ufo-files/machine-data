5 red lights up high moving against the wind, no sound and each got closer and just winked out. There was no objejects or smoke after.

My wife and I were driving home on the 4th of July from the store. There were a ton of regular fireworks going on but I saw these solid red lights up much higher and they were moving unlike fireworks. We saw no smoke and they were moving at a good clip.This is on Price road heading south. I asked my wife for her phone to record this. For some reason, her phone kept going back to menu. I told her to pull over at the Dollar General store because they were getting out of view and the phone still won't let us record. As we pulled in, each object seemed to slow down behind the 1st and each object just winked out. There was nothing to be seen where they where, no smoke and or solid object. I hate the fact that it was the 4th of July to see something like this. They were not fireworks or aircraft...
