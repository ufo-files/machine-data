v shape moving erratically then up and away very quickly

I was camping at Elk Island National park some time in the summer, I'm guessing it was 1973 or1974 with a buddy of mine. I lived in Sherwood Park from 1972 to 1975. I was star gazing on a clear night with my head out of my pup tent when I saw this triangle of colored lights moving in a zig-zag erratic pattern that could not have been a fixed wing aircraft or a helicopter. I heard no noise while these lights moved back and forth, changing directions abruptly as well as elevation, then after a while moved up and off very quickly in one direction and was gone. I had no idea what I had witnessed, and my buddy in his tent next to me was in his tent, and did not see what happened. I don't know what I had seen, but knew what it was not, any type of aircraft that was around in 1974! I believe it was a pattern of 7 lights that were not flashing, and moved in unison. Someone else must have seen this, but it was a long time ago, so I can't recall the date, the month, or even the year, but this is something I will never forget that I have seen an unexplained craft of some type. At the time I would have been a high-school student, and I was not under any influence of alcohol or any drugs!
