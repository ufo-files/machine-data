{nd} Craft flew over my house and hovered over downtown Nashville

I was laying in bed and felt a rumble and over a 3 second period heard a whoosh. I went and looked out my window facing north towards downtown and saw the craft heading north. There was a bright white light on the left side, a dim white light on the right and a red light in the center. The lights pulsates randomly and moved along a singular plane randomly. The craft hovered over down town in one place for 2 minutes and then slowly began moving towards the east and then I lost line of sight behind a tree.
