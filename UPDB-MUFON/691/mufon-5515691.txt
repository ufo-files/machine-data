(GS) A dim gold/brown object moving quite quickly from west to east.

I must preface by saying that since I reported observing a couple of strange lights back in April, I have been spending a great deal of time looking up at the night sky ever since. After tonight I will admit that I am a certifiable nut-job, or I saw one of the most incredible things Ive ever seen. 

  I was out back on the porch staring up at he sky, and strumming on my guitar. I just happen to look over my righ shoulder and witnessed a dull gold/brown traversing the sky. I watched this object move from 2 oclock, to 9 oclock in roughly 6 or 7 seconds. This thing was rather high in altitude, as it appeared to pass above the clouds before coming back into view then fading out of view. I have never seen an object move this fast in my life. I suppose it could have been some sort of aircraft Ive never seen before, but travelling at that speed, I feel I should have seen a trail, heard some noise, or something. 

  Anyway, that about sums it up. I wish I had a better explaination for what I saw, or possibly a video, but I just wasnt prepared. That wont happen again.
