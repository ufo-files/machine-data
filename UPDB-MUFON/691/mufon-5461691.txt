Light emitting object moving in various directions

Light emitting object first seen tracking North to South near to the Plough star cluster. Light would dim and object would re-appear tracking in another direction, sometimes North, sometimes North North East with various other Southerly routes. Later when tracking North to South a very high speed burst was seen and then returned to a slower speed instantly. Aircraft locations and ISS location checked and confirmed as nothing in the area.
