Disk-like UFO seen near Howrah, India

Yesterday (05.11.2013) I was going to Dhanbad from Howrah by train (Coalfield Express). As I did not get a seat, I was standing at a right door facing the direction in which the train was going (North-West). At approx. 5:50 PM (IST) I saw a disk-like structure in the North-East direction. The disk had a belt of white lights around its periphery, which appeared to be spinning anticlockwise (if seen from top). The speed was not much. A red light was blinking at the bottom. I showed it to my friend, who confirmed that, that was not any kind of firework, but some kind of machine. As the visibility of the atmosphere was low the object could not be seen for a long time (only about 1.5 minutes). It appeared to move in a parabolic path. The lowest altitude was much lower than that of a plane, but it went away much far and within few seconds it was out of sight (couldnt see it disappear in horizon, because the weather was little foggy/smoky). As it was already dark, the material and the exact shape could not be seen clearly.
