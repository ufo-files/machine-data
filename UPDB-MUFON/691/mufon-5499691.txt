{dr}Triangular beam of light, narrowest from landing point. Blue beams and a buzzing noise.

I was sitting near the water looking off into the street. Behind the row of houses directly in front of me, a giant triangular beam of blue light opened into the sky. Usually in UFO cartoons the beam is coming from the spaceship in the sky and widening at the bottom, but this was reversed, making me believe the air craft had landed. The blue light came to its widest point, flashed a few white lights, became brighter and simultaneously made a buzzing noise. It sounded just like plugging a guitar into an amp.. Then the beam closed horizontally and shrunk back to the ground. I looked at my friend said 'that's some alien s**t' and we ran back to the car. I stared out the rear view window to see if maybe it would happen again, but nothing. On the street there were no cars until we reached my house.
