{rjl} 14 to 16 orange/white round objects traveling in equally spaced formation in a straight path NE

My son was taking the dog out to go to the bathroom. He called me outside to look at something. He pointed up to two orange, round objects in the sky that were traveling fast in a North East direction, seemingly in formation. At first he thought they were space junk, but then we noticed more orange, round objections following them - also in formation - same distance apart, traveling at the same speed, in the same direction. As we watched, we kept counting more and more up to 14-16 total. We then went inside and decided to report it to you. We think they were coming from the direction of Orion's belt - but they seemed to appear out of nowhere, so the exact origin was impossible to tell. Right now, in our backyard - Orion's belt is dipped just below and to the south of Venus or Mars, which is shining really bright tonight - and has been for some time.
