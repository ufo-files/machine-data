A very long line of lights

I was outside with my dogs in the garden, when I looked up, there was a very long line of individual lights, very close together moving from sw to se very rapidly. There was no noise, no 'trail' and was moving faster than a regular airplane. On seeing this, I rushed into the house to get my husband to come see, also grabbing my phone for a video. Unfortunately, it had then moved too far for my camera to pick it up at it was very high, but my husband still saw it clearly. The line of lights would have to be anywhere between one and two miles long, I've gauged this on the plane that was in the sky at the same time. The plane was much, much lower than the lights. The lights actually looked like a line of stars moving very quickly.
