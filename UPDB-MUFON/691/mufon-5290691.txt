Wobbling bright white light

I was on a 3 way zoom call walking my dog when I saw a bright starlike light coming from an unusual direction. It was about 10-15x larger than a star and wobbled as it moved. An airplane was also in the sky on a normal trajectory I see all the time. This is also why the object stood out by comparison- there were no other lights on the vehicle and it looked like it was shaking as it moved, skipping slightly or wobbling. It was going much slower than the nearby aircraft. I could hear the nearby aircraft but the object directly overhead made no noise. I told the people I was in the phone with and tried to show them on the video chat. But it was too dark for them to really see it.
