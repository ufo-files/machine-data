{dr}Red pulsating orb flying towards lightning

I was working at the public pool. There was a pool party going on, but we were taking a break due to lightning. I was sitting on a bench, facing south, when I looked up from my phone to see an orb with pulsating red light flying away from me. It looked to be flying lower than airplanes tend to do, and as I watched, I saw it move suddenly to the west, without banking or turning. It didn't move terribly fast, but that turn struck me as very unusual. I watched it until I lost sight of it behind the trees, then I filmed it on my phone as it reappeared from behind the trees and flew towards the lightning. It eventually flew behind a building, and I lost sight of it.
