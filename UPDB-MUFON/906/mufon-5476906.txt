{rjl} Black disc shaped object. Spun around or did

Me and my friend were sitting in my driveway. I saw a black object flying fairly close to the ground, about the height a bird would fly. I thought at first that the object was a bird. After watching it for a minute and realising it couldn't be a bird, I pointed it out to my friend. After watching it for a moment, he said "that is not a bird". We watched the object fly until it was too distant to see. Upon first spotting the object we were able to see it quite clearly. It was a black disc shaped object. It was spinning in circles vertically, as if it was doing "summersaults". It was moving in a straight path, unwavering. Quite slowly. Me and my friend both observed a sphere of "energy" around it. I was thinking my eyes could be playing tricks on me when my friend independently stated "do you see the globe around it?" The circumference of the circle around the object was static, even when the object was disapearing in the distance. The "energy" emitting from it was clear and had no color. There was a visible edge to the sphere. There was no other aircraft in the area before, during, or after the occurrence. Which is odd considering I live miles away from a government airbase called selfridge field. Me and my friend felt as anyone would feel who just witnessed a UFO. We sat in silence as we watched in utter curiosity and nervous excitement.
