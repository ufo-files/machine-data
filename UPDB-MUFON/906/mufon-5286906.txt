White blinking orb light, pulsating, disappearing and reappearing

My girlfriend and I were at a family picnic in Alum Rock Park July 20th 2019 and was talking with her cousins, then noticed a blinking light in the sky. I took my phone out and recorded 2 separate clips of it. Then on June 20th 2021, I saw the same blinking light and it shocked me because I know I had seen it before and got a video of it. I also included a collage of screenshots to better see this object. And also I included a picture of a blimp like object which I can not identify which was taken September 2020 Santa Clara CA.
