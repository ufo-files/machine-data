{tjd} Lights in sky (45 lights, from SW, formed a trail {djs})

March 6, 2020- Counted 45 lights in the sky from Alpena, MI. They came from the SW and seemed to go into the bright star in the Western Sky (Jupiter) and many lights were ejecting from it. Some formed a trail formation and headed North. Some acted erratic and went together and came apart. Some dropped quickly and then joined another and moved North.
