Was on the drop zone for my airborne unit and saw something odd

Was working on the drop zone for airborne operations. It was around 5:40-6:00am and I was sitting in one of the abandoned hangers waiting for the first plane of our guys to come since they were jumping starting at 7:30am. I’m sitting there just looking at the drop zone when I look up at the tree line and see what looked like a chevron shaped object with grayish/dark gold lighting on the exterior. Just looking at it you could tell that It was thick or almost like heavy. I thought it was one of our c130’s but no military aircraft has the colored exterior lights that I saw. Only green and red and they pulsate. The lights I saw didn’t pulsate but almost collapsed into each other and then just disappeared like nothing. Like the snap of a finger quick.
