flashing flying objects

This has occurred two Saturdays in a row. The first event there were onlythree, the next week seven small and one huge one. We were driving south on Hwy385 and saw orange flashes of light on the ground, directly above was one of theobjects. In town, 2 more came closer. The second week we looked for them everynight, and they came back at dark on Saturday. We then picked up 2 friends tocome watch them with us. They hover low, then go farther back, move left toright and up and down. It is frightening, but also exciting.
