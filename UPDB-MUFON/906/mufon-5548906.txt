went camping,half mile into forest,sent up tent,turned around star ship sitting 500 feet into woods

1957 was off for final exams,went camping at mount airy forest,used friends mopet bike to get there,after getting there rode down path about one half mile. saw big clearing stopped and began to set tent up,friend was suppose to meet me there. sit in camping chair listing to radio,went inside tent to git sandwhich,radio begin acting up and then stopped.radio was warm to the touch,but the radio was in shade. picked up radio to take battiers out thats when i saw it. five hundred feet back at least. in clearing i know was not there when i set tent up. walked back to five feet of starship, no sound. could see threw it like glass.went to walk away thats when ramp slide out from no where. heard voice in head asking me to come in.got scared went to turn to walk away that when something warm and cold touched the right side of head,just below ear lobe.everything went black.woke up leaning agains tree near street,but back far enought not to be seen. looked at watch stopped. walked back to tent. radio was playing good as new. star ship was gone,clearing was now filled with tall grass when i first got there.took everything down,placed on bike. friend did not show.got home by 1130.went inside took bath.went to lay down and for some reason i looked at date. it was not friday but saturday i had lost 24 hours. i was 14 at the time. there is more to story but would fill at least 2 more pages or more.you have my number and email if you have a 1-800 number i would be willing to call you and tell you the rest of story
