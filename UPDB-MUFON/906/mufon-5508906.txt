{cak} Saw star-like object at mid horizon flying in straight horizontal path, then hovered, then disappered and came back 4 times at hovering position

I went out into my front yard, couldnt sleep, about 3:00 a.m. Looked at the crescent moon to the east. Then saw an object that looked like a moving star just below and north of the moon. At first I thought it might be a plane. It moved steadily in a horizontal path. Then it stopped in mid-air and hovered. It was quite a distance up in the sky, mid-horizon. I watched because of the hovering, this could not be a plane. The object must have been very large, looked like a major star. Light was always white, looked like a diamond. Then the lights diminished and the object disappeared completely. Then it came back again. It did this four times. I became very tired and could not keep watching it. It remained there and I went back into my house. I believe this was a ufo. I have never believed in ufos, but I know what I saw, I have never seen a flying object do these things before. I knew I had seen a ufo, but didn't know what to do about it. Also, I had a sighting about seven days before this one, same type of object but in the western horizon. Will detail that in another report.
