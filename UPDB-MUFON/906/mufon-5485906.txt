Cigar shaped with rough edges, orange-red, above it appeared a large orange-red orb or sphere.

This took place on the Big Rideau Lake in Ontario, south side of Horseshoe Bay in late summer 1974. My friend and I were on the dock thinking of going swimming (it was about 10 p.m. or so). I looked out at the main lake to my right and saw the roughly edged large cigar shaped object in the sky. It was orange. I didn't think too much at first, but then asked my friend what she thought it was. She seemed alarmed, we kept watching until above the cigar shaped object a large red-orange sphere or circle suddenly appeared. Neither objects made any sound or moved after that. We were very frightened and went back inside the cottage where we were spending the night alone. We tried to rationalize the objects and felt someone else must have seen and would be able to tell us what it was. No one we talked to seemed to have seen anything. We were teenagers at the time so our story was dismissed as weather, or a plane, fireworks etc. I spent many years at the cottage since and have never seen anythink like it.
