Blue Star Like Object moving very fast 9/14/20

At 8:36 PM I was sitting on a a swing on a swing set (was not moving) smoking a cigarette and looking up at the sky. There was light over cast, but I could still see a few bright stars shining through, I saw something really bright at the corner of my right eye (SE Direction) turned my head slightly and saw a very bright, blue star-like object moving VERY fast toward NW and disappear within seconds. There is NO way it could have been an airplane, helicopter or drone. It was moving way too fast! Whatever was flying it must've been in quite a rush, because it did not stick around so that I could get a better look at it. It disappeared into thin air. As I stated above, there is overcast tonight but the clouds are thin and transparent. It was below the clouds. Maybe 500 feet away from me at the least, a mile at the most. It was almost like a shooting star, but I've seen those before and it was way too bright and low.
