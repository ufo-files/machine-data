{cak} Three skinny half moon shaped objects moving at meteor speed.

I was standing on my back porch, on the phone with my father, at approximately 10:40 PM, Sunday May 12th. As I glanced up between a gap in the trees in my yard, I saw something streak through the sky. My initial thought was a meteor because it moved that fast. But just as they disappeared behind a tree, I realized there was three objects, in a line abreast, perpendicular to their direction of travel. The objects had a skinny half moon to crescent moon shape, kind of like a thin fingernail clipping. All three were identical to each other. They had a definite shape, but were slightly indistinct or fuzzy at the edges. They were a faint pink color with a slight tinge of orange. I made an exclamation of shock and immediately described what I saw to my father. The description I have provided here is the same one I gave him mere seconds after the sighting. I have a degree in astronomy and am familiar with most natural sky phenomena. I am also a skeptic when it comes to UFOs, but what I saw was truly bizarre and resists my attempts to explain it. Thus, I felt compelled to report it.
