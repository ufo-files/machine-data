White object with twin flame tails. Blinked out and moved to new location.

Friend and I saw bright object to the north-west above the horizon. It had twin tails that looked like flame. The object would brighten then dim and disappear to reappear a short distance away. It moved away to the north-west until we lost it in the haze. This was our second or third sighting of an unknown object. All the object we've seen have had an orenge color. For this one I took pictures as my friend looked through bi-no's and told me when it was brightest. All three object that we have seen are in the direction of Handford.
