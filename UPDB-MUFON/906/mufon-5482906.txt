{cak} Dark disk appearing to be out of control flew out of the clouds, regained control and flew east.

Walked outside to look at the clouds. Immediately saw a dark disk shaped object in the north west come out of the clouds wobbling like it was out of control. I first thought that it was a frisbee that got caught in the wind until it kept flying east, gained control, sped up and flew over Mt. Lemon and back up into the clouds.
