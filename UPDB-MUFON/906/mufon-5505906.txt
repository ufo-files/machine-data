{nd} Bright red object hovering then moving E, dropped another red object which fell toward the ground and the original object disappeared.

I was coming outside to smoke a cigarette on December 24, 2013 at approx. 9:15 pm. I saw a really bright red object that was just hovering over my apartments. It was larger than all of the stars in the sky and didn't seem to be as high up as the stars. After approx. 3 minutes it began moving in an easterly direction. It then stopped, hovered for another minute and dropped another red object out of it. The second red object was about half the size and began slowly flying to the ground. It didn't leave a trail or anything just was slowly falling toward the ground. After about another minute the original red object disappeared. This really freaked me out! I watch the sky all of the time and have never seen anything like this before.
