Was taking the boat out to do some late night hornpout fishing on the Pontook reservoir with my buddy when we saw huge cigar shaped cluster of lights.

At approximately 2200hrs on 05/16/2021 at the Pontook reservoir. Me and my buddy went out to do a little bit of hornpout fishing. Upon our arrival and exiting the vehicle I noticed a huge cluster of lights in the sky. there was easily thousands if not millions clustered together in like a cigar shape. It was first noticed in the North and moved North East before turning and fading out of view as if it was getting further away. Me and my buddy stood there in the middle of the road for about ten minutes in the pitch dark watching whatever it was. No light pollution very clear sky with a crescent moon pointed west. If I had to guess the distance I'd say probably from space. Never seen nothing like it. I tried to take a picture and my buddy had an Iphone all came out crappy and hard to see but I'll post anyways.
