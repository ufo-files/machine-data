About a month ago, I was driving in the car with a close friend of mine who also saw the two unidentified flying objects that I witnessed. The first aircraft came into sight out of the trees that lined the road, it had three rotating blue lights that were shining down onto the ground and it was very close to the ground and the first thing that I noticed was how large and fast it was moving, it didn’t have wings, it was definitely not a plane. It was so clear the three lights on the bottom face of the aircraft were spinning and about 3 seconds later another aircraft that had the same exact exterior except the lights were red instead of blue. The aircrafts were out of view behind the trees by the time either of us thought to record what we witnessed, but we both couldn’t stop mentioning it because of how much disbelief was running through our bodies. 
The drawing below is an exact diagram that I made with google draw, I only put in the significant details that stuck out to me. It was triangular with straight edges, and there was a shiny metallic bar going from each big circular light towards the points. There was another circular light in the center of the aircraft that almost looked like an opening, but it was too far to tell for sure.
