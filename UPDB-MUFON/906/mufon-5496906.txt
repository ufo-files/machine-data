{nd} several objects diving in and out of a cloud reflecting the sun

While I was sitting in the shade I saw several objects that were diving in and out of a cloud... They made a stair step climb as they went up into the cloud.... At first I thought these were giant birds but birds don't do this type of thing..... All of the objects were joined in an elongated circle evenly spaced diving in and out of the cloud... I never would have seen them if it wasn't for the reflection each of them made being in contact with the sun's rays....
