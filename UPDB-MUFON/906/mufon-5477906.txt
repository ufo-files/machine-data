Eight lights in a row captured on trail cam

A trail cam (Full Light Tech 12MP 1080P with a Sandisc SDSDB 32GB SDHC Class Memory card)is set up in my garden to capture images of local wildlife. The video footage, which appears in the top centre of the screen was captured at 06:25:15 on the 21 February 2017 (not 20 February as the date on the camera shows and which was incorrectly set) shows a row of what appears to be eight lights travelling in a East to South Easterly direction. 

I was not actually present at the time of the event.

(I apologise for my cat in the foreground, nature called).
