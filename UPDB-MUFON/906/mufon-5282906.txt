Thought it was a bright Mars in the dawn light.

On weekdays, my alarm clock wakes me at 7am.
I remember that this bright orange celestial body was right in my line of vision from my bed, and I truly thought that I was looking at a bright Mars.

For roughly 5 minutes I kept looking back at fixed, bright orange point, right in line with my vision from my bed (I do not sleep with my curtains closed).

The fixed point of light truly looked EXACTLY like a bright Mars in the early dawn sky in terms of size, appearance and brightness.  I knew that the light was high up because a wispy, high level cloud passed between my line of vision and this point of light.

Just after this wispy cloud cleared my line of vision - this object, which up to now I believed to be the planet Mars - started moving vertically upwards (from my perspective) until it flew out of sight through my bedroom window.  (roughly another 60 seconds).  Given that I cannot estimate distance, I cannot accurately determine speed.  However, from the information I can factually witness to - it was fast. 

Sadly, as the entire first part of this sighting, I truly believed I was viewing the planet Mars, I did not bother to get any camera out.

Furthermore, after I got to my laptop, I checked the actual location of Mars on that morning and at that time, Mars was actually behind my head and behind a wall.
