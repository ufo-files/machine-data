(GS) Orange / Red Glowing Orbs hovered for a few minutes then moved a bit blinked a few times and then just dissappeared

Amount 8:45 pm (Mountain Time)on 1/18/2013 My husband and I went outside to go into our hot tub. My husband went straight out to the tub to remove the cover. As I steped out the door about a minute after him I looked straight out to the East and saw 4 orange/red orbs glowing brightly in the sky. I asked to him what are those lights? We both stopped and stared at the four glowing objects in the sky. As if they saw/heard us they started to retreat. One set at a time, they moved behind each other blinked a few times and then just disappeared into the night sky.
