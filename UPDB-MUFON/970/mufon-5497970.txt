{cak} orb of light

I witnessed a very blue with white edge sphere of light about midnight. It was about the size of a coffee table. I would say that the inner 90% was a very beautiful light blue and the outside 10% was white. It was a perfect sphere. It was about 75 yards from me. There was no light source in the area. There wasn't any aircraft in the area. It was completely quiet outside.I have never seen or read about anything like what I witnessed. It was going NW and dropped down over my roof towards the ground of a field. About 20 feet above the ground, it banked back up and then banked left. It lasted about 4 total seconds and then it completely disappeared. It was definitely not of this world and my best guess, due to it's size, is that it was some type of probe. This is the first time in my 50 years that I have ever seen anything I could not explain.
