{cak} a Blue pulsating light coming from the south, stopped, then a flash of red/orange, changed direction heading back south on the original path, then another flash of red/orange then blue pulsating

I was out facing south at 9:50 PM. Saw a blue pulsating light coming from the south heading north. Looked like airplane navigation lights though I thought I've never seen blue navigation lights before. Watched the light, until it was approximately 35 degrees above me, (Note was approximately the same degree as Jupiter along the ecliptic, Jupiter was approximately 15 degrees away to the left of the flightpath). At this time the light stopped in flight, then a flash of red/orange, at this time the object changed direction heading back south, on the same trajectory and flight path, as where it came from, still a blue pulsating light. Then another flash of re/orange appeared, then back to the blue pulsating. Watched it until it disappeared over the horizon.
Note: when the first flash of red/orange appeared there was an airplane, (with normal navigation lights) was coming from the southwest heading northwest, that's when it stopped and flashed the red/orange then changed direction heading back south.
Watched the airplane fly over as the blue light headed south.
