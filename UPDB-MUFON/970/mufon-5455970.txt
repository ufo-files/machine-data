{cak} Saw ball of fire with sparks coming off it. I sat and waited for the crash nothing happened.

I was driving home one night and i got to a stop sign and stopped. Then like something out of a movie this really bright ball or orb flies directly over me. It was really bright but did not light the area. There were sparks coming off it like a sparkler on the forth of July. It was going in a downward angle. I sat at the stop sign waiting for a crash. Nothing. Then all of a sudden out of nowhere these five black suburbans with just their parking lights on fly by me. They had to be going at least 80 to 100 mph. They went by so fast the wind rocked my car. I looked the following day in the news to see if anything was reported. Nothing. This is just one of four encounters I have had so far through out my life. They are REAL.
