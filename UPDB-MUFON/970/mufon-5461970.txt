{cak} Driving around and looked up to spot a weird hovering object float across the sky without emitting sound. Did not have normal plans lights.

We were driving along Taylorsville road in Louisville, Kentucky on the edge of Jefferson county. It was 3AM and we saw a bright orange orb across the sky. Initially thinking it was a planet, it appeared to get closer and we stopped on the side of the road to look at it. It got closer and closer and the lights changed from a orange orb to 6 regular lights (I think) with a green light. The object of the UFO was triangular and it didnt have normal plane lights of red and white blinking. As it got closer, it didnt emit sound and it went almost directly above us within 500 feet. It didnt make sound and it was very strange. It was either a stealth government plane or a UFO. It was too large to be a drone.
