Craft hovered stationary as we watched and at tremendous speed shot directly upwards out of sight

Myself and family members were on the porch at my grandparents house. The object was miles away yet seemed huge. My cousin who is years older than myself phoned the USAF and as the planes approached at high speed the craft shot straight up at incredible speed and disappeared. The airplanes flew in extremely wide patterns and then left the area. No calls were ever returned.
