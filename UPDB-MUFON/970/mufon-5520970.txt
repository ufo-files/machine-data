At approx. 8:17 p.m.(tonight - February 2, 2012 - I spotted 5 very bright white lights with a smaller red flashing light above each, heading in a South Westerly direction. At first sighting, they seemed to be in a diamond pattern with a tail, as they continued to move further away, the formation turned to a Y.  By this time it was 8:22 pm.  Then I glanced to my right (West) and spotted one brilliant white light. At that time I decided to grab my telescope. This brilliant white light remained stationery and constant; and was about 1,000 ft. above a mountain range approx. 9-10 miles from my residence. I continued to view it both with the telescope and with my naked eye. Through the scope it appeared to have red/blue and white light emitting. After approximately 18 minutes, it began to descend slowly and within 3 minutes it had dropped below the treeline.
