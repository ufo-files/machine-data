Matt black triangle, soundless, hovering treeline, car underneath observing, 2 flashing lights, extremely fast acceleration without sound or disturbance

In car heading south just near Broughton, Scotland. Saw matt black triangle, hovering just above trees. Stopped car to observe UFO. Opened window to look up. 2 flashing lights. Craft was totally soundless. Trees were not affected by the hovering of the craft. Observed it for approximately 4 minutes, then craft accelerated exceedingly fast north, but again soundless with no air disturbance. Never seen anything so close like this before. Very obvious wasn't recognisable aircraft. Trees weren't that high; so we had a good view of the craft. We felt very excited to have witnessed it. Found it incredible, especially as there was no disturbance by it and completely soundless. The rate it left the scene was unbelievable! Again no noise whatsoever and all around us was calm.
