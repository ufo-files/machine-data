{dr}Bright circular looking light that moves slowly, but moves out of sight in 20-25 min (See Photos)

I was driving from Farmington, Mo to my home in Perryville, Mo via Hwy 32 then IH 55, on Friday, Oct 28,2016 between about 6:45 pm to 7:45pm. As I approached New Oppenheimer on Hwy 32,I noticed a very bright and seemingly close light. I've noticed this light from various places in SE Mo, including my home, since Oct 1st. I have multiple photos beginning with Oct 1st, but this sighting was closer and brighter, and the pics are bizarre. The attached photos are shown in the original "Raw" format photos taken from my HTC 10 phone and then enhanced using the Raw Auto Enhance feature. The detail in the enhanced photos are astonishing. We have 3 cameras,incuding a new EOS Rebel, and none of my regular cameras photograph this. This object just seems to appear on random days and isn't there every night. It moves in the same pattern each time I see it, like it has a set flight path, that moves in the same linear path, but it's position in the horizon moves a few feet each time. Our home is in a crossroads of commercial airline flight routes and I can't understand why no one is reporting this. It's almost as if an airliner isn't able to observe this in the air? I've searched the internet and find photos of round, white, light that are similar to mine before enhancement, but nothing with detail. Does anyone have a natural phenomenon explanation?
