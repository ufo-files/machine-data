{cak} 8/4/13 ~ 9:30 pm est 3 Orange glowing orb lights in South East sky Lehigh valley Pa

August 4, 2013 approx 9:30 pm est. My self and a friend saw two Orange glowing orbs in the South East Sky Moving more south and upward the two orbs stayed on the same plane and moved in lateral line maintain same distance appeared to be approx half mile distance between the two the one on the left dispersed first and approx 30 seconds later the one to the right dispersed. about 5 min later another orange glowing orb took the same exact path moving from the south east to the upward to the south and appearing to disperser in the same location as the other two. each event lasted approx 3 mins.
