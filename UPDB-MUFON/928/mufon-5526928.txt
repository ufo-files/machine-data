Bright star like object moving west to east

I was out for a smoke and happened to look north. I spotted a bright star like object moving from west to east in a straight line. There was no noise and no blinking aircraft lights. It kept moving at a slow but steady pace until it disappeared behind some clouds. I noticed that it was lower than the stars but higher than the clouds. The size from my perspective was about a bb held from arms length. The color was like a bright white glowing color, like the headlight of a car.
