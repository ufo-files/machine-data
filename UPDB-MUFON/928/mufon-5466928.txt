{rjl} taking night pics with DSLR of the Monument near my home. These lights only appeared on camera.

I was taking pictures at around midnight of the High Point Monument from my parents backyard. At the time of this picture was taken there was no aircraft in the area and definitely not in my view through my camera lens. 2 pictures were taken within 10 seconds of each other and these three red dots only appear in one. They where not observable to me with the naked eye and i did not discover this until i looked through the pictures later that evening. I take many pictures this time of night here and have never gotten anything like this. I thought of this as weird because there was no trail of light that would normally appear like some of my other pics with airplanes, satellites etc. This was clearly just hovering for a few seconds and then it was gone in a flash. It was not cloudy either that night.
