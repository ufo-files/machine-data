IT WAS A CLEAR DAY ON HIGHWAY 95 GOING SOUTH, I WAS VERY NEAR SEARCHLIGHT NEVADA. I WAS DRIVING ABOUT 77 MPH WHEN I SAW A VERY LARGE DEFINED SHADOW COVERING THE ROAD AND SOME OF THE SURROUNDING AREA . WHEN I LOOKED UP NOTHING WAS THERE. 
THE SUN WAS HIGH IN THE SKY BUT I THOUGHT I SAW A BLAST FURNACE TYPE OF LIGHT COMING FROM THE SKY AND OVER THE SHADOW. 
I HAD HAD A SIMILAR SIGHTING BEFORE AND KNEW THIS WAS THE SAME TYPE OF SIGHTING, A CLOAKED SAUCER SHAPE IN THE SKY. 
SOMETHING HAD TO BE MAKING THE SHADOW, IT APPEARED TO BE CLOAKED AND ALL I COULD SEE WAS A "BLAST FURNACE LIGHT" WHERE A SHIP SHOULD BE.
IT WAS NOT MOVING IN THE SKY JUST STATIONARY. I DROVE UNDER IT AND KEPT GOING.
I ESTIMATE THE CRAFT TO BE 3 MILES IN DIAMETER AND THE SHADOW ABOUT 3 ACRES.
I WAS VERY EXCITED TO SEE THE SAME KIND OF SIGHTING AGAIN.
