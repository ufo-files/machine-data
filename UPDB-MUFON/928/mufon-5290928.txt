Happened 2 mornings: 11-19-20, 11-20-20 at 2:00am

I work evening shift and get home at 2:00am. I always look into sky to observe stars and a bright twinkle towards the south caught my eye. I watched and then took my Moto Z 3 phone and took a video, also took a still shot. Watched object for about 15 minutes, no sound and stayed stationary. The next morning (11-20-20), got home around 2:00am again and looked up to south and saw same twinkling light, but not as bright as morning before. Once again took video and still shot. In videos, hand was not steady so object appears to be moving. I zoomed away and you can see object is in one position. While recording, I did notice the object color changed with blinking red light faintly. If you look closely at video you can see faint red. Also, on still shot, I tried to filter object and zoomed in. On the 11-19-20 still shot, you can see some kind of vehicle. Don't think it's a helicopter because of no sound. Did check space station schedule and it did come around my area but was way earlier in evening and come from SW to NE. Object was stationed south. First filtered still shot shows best picture, something with a large window. Second filtered shows object with kind of a tail in center . My opinion, not our aircraft because there was no sound. Sending all videos and pics, sure you folks have a better filter than I do. Let me know what you think!
