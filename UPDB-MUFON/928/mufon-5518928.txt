Orange ball of light

I was talking on the phone on my patio when I looked up to observe what looked like a large ball of orange light hovering to my north. It began to move to the east at a fast pace until it seemed to disappear behind a cloud. The object never reappeared. It was MUCH to large to be a plane and was so bright it looked like a large ball of fire. I heard no sound until I lost sight of it at which time I heard a loud noise I have never heard before. It almost sounded like Godzilla.
