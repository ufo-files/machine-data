{cak} MASSIVE LOOKS LIKE CHINEESE LANTERN

ME AND TWO OTHERS WERE DRIVING DOWN SARATOGA ROAD ABOUT 11:30 P.M WHEN I TOLD MY SISTER WHEN DID THEY INSTAL A LIGHT IT THE MIDDLE OF THE WOODS .SHE SAID THEY DIDNT THIS THING WAS MASSIVE AS SOON AS SHE SAID THAT IT STARTED TO DECEND AT FIRST IT WAS JUST HOVERING THERE WE WERE THE ONLY CAR ON THE ROAD AS I APPROACHED THE STOP SIGHN MY SISTER WAS HANGING OUT OF THE WINDOW LOOKING AT IT I HAD A CLEAR VIEW IT HAD MULTIPLE LEVELS MASSIVE . I RAN THE STOP SIGHN AND TURNED ON OLDBROWNSVILL ROAD TO THE LEFT AND IT WAS DECENDING PARALLEL TO THE RIGHT OF ME ALL THREE OF US HAD A CLEAR VIEW OF IT. AS I WAS DRIVING IT WAS DECENDING GRADUALY UNTILL IT LANDED IN AN OPEN FIELD BUT AS IT LANDED IT BLENDED IN WITH ALL THE CITY LIGHTS IN THE BACKGROUNG HAD WE NOT SEEN IT LAND NOBODY WOULD HAVE NOTICED IT , I ASKED MY SISTER WHEAR DID IT GO AND SHE SAID ITS STILL THERE AND IT WAS . THIS THING WAS AT LEAST THREE TO FOUR STORIES TALL WITH BIG HUGE ROUND WINDOWS THAT GAVE OFF AN ORANGE LIGHT BUT DIDNT EMITT LIGHT AND IN BETWEEN THE WINDOW IT WAS AN OPAGUE BLACK THIS THING WAS ONLY ABOUT 60 -80 FEET AWAY BUT WITH ITS SIZE IT FELT LIKE IT WAS CLOSER . IT HAD THREE BIG ROUND CIRCLES IN THE CENTER PART OF IT AND ON THE TOP AND LOWER LEVELS IT HAD 30-40 SMALLER LIGHTS THAT WERE ORANGE AND WHITE . THIS THING LOOKED FAKE,LIKE IT SHOULDNT EVEN BE ABLE TO FLY. THIS SOUNDS DUMB BUT IT LOOKED ALMOST LIKE A CHINEESE LANTERN THE KIND THEY PUT CANDLES IN .WELL I CONTINUED TO DRIVE TO MY HOUSE WHICH WAS ABOUT 30 SEC AWAY AND TURNED IN MY DRIVEWAY GOING ABOUT 30 MPH AND THREW THE CAR IN PARK OPENED THE DOOR AND JUMPED OUT WHILE YHE CAR STILL SLID AND JUMPED ON MY DIRTBIKE AND HEADED FOR THE FIELD IN WHICH IT LANDED WHEN I GOT TO THE FENCE AND SHINED THE HEADLIGHT OF THE BIKE IN THAT DIRECTION IT WAS GONE. BUT THE CRAZY PART OF IT IS THAT FIELD IT LANDED IN BELONGS TO THE CORPUS CHRISTI AIRPORT THERE IS NOTHING BACK THERE BUT FLAT LAND AND A CLEAR VIEW OF THE AIRPORT TOWER WHICH WAS FACING THIS THINGS DIRECTION HOW COULD THEY NOT SEE THIS THING BY ITS SIZE IT COULD HAVE BEEN SEEN BY MILES AND WITH THOSE HUGE MASSIVE ORANGE LIGHTS .THEAR RADAR SHOULD HAVE PICKED THIS THING UP FLYING IN THEAR AIRSPACE.IVE LIVED BEHIND THIS AIRPORT MY WHOLE LIFE AND SEEN ALL I MEAN ALL TYPE OF AIRCRAFTS LEAVE AND LAND BUT NOTHING LIKE THIS. THE ONLY REASON I AM SUBMITTING THIS IS BECAUSE I HAVENT BEEN ABLE TO FIND A SINGLE PICTURE ON THE INTERNET THAT RESEMBLES WHAT I SAW THAT NIGHT AND BELIEVE ME IVE BEEN LOOKING OF SOMETHING SIMILAR BUT HAVENT SEEN ANYTHING .I CAN PROVIDE A DETAILED DRAWING I WILL NEVER FORGET THAT THING.
