They formed a triangle formation & flew off in same direction slowly

My mother & i and my nephew were in a vehicle driving to a hockey practice. My mom noticed lights and wondered why the street lights were a different color. Then i noticed that there were four or five bright red-orange orbs forming a triangle. Once we got to the top of our hill i got out to take a picture or video with my nikon camera but nothing worked. Then two of them turned their lights off after my flash on the camera tried to go off. We lost sight of the other object by the time we had parked by the road. We felt like we were witnessing something incredible & rare. Might have been a bit frightened. My nephew even said what if they were just hot air balloons? & i said i wonder if they r drones: i heard they were flying around now more than we know.
