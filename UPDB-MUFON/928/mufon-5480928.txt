Awakened by extraterrestrial who had a message for me

I was in bed asleep, when i suddenly became wide awake and alert, i heard a voice in my head keep saying "dont panic, everything is ok" then the voice told me to "check on your child" so i ran straight to my 2 year olds room and seen her awake in her cot smiling up at 3 extraterrestrials, and they were stood watching her, i was about to ask what do you want and before i could speak all 3 looked at me at the same time and said. "dont panic everything is ok" i couldnt move just stand there and watch them watching her, but i was otherwise fully aware, i remember the smell of them was disgusting like an old fish tank. i knew they could read my thoughts so without speaking and just by thinking i thought one simple word, why? and they all looked at me again and to my surprise they answered. i had a feeling come over me that put me at ease almost as if the appreciated this type of psychic communication, and they explained that they were Reticulans as we know them,they werent hostile but could be if a race didnt accept their plans and that they were a young race a few million years older than humans but still young in regards to other species they know of, and they had mastered technology and interstellar travel a long time ago, and that they had never seen their homeland they where created on board a ship, and part of their mission was to take DNA from species capable of creating hybrids as they believed that the only meaning of existence for a biological being is to pass on their cells. and this was the only way they could do it as they no longer had the need for sexual organs as they were took away genetically at the embryo stage of the cloning process and that science had gotten in the way of evolution and they didnt want to die out as a species. they informed me they had taken blood from my daughter and thanked us. i then woke in bed with my daughter on my chest, at around 7am although the whole encounter must have lasted 10 minutes at most.
