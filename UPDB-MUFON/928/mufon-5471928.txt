{jpg} Bright blue white spherical object with decent size hovers then leaves

Driving home on my way back from picking roommate from work. As I slowed car to intersection on dirt road I looked to my left (north) for clear traffic. There was no traffic but it the sky this massive bright blue sphere was just hovering there. After about 5 seconds which was about the time it took me to stop it started heading north north east below the clouds. It's cloudy to my north but no other direction.. lost sight, just vanished. As I turn south I see a low flying plane heading due north but the only airport for a plane of that size would be hays I believe and that s over an hour away so it wouldn't be that low yet. Very loud plane bright white flashing lights on each wing.
