{jpg} lights similer to a star or sattilite...but changing direction not on a straight course,like the space station

I parked my truck on the north end of Lake Leelanau on July 3, to watch the fire works in Leland.I saw the space station pass overhead and then I saw a light,smaller than the space station, moving through the sky....I watched it move around the sky in different directions..I saw 6 such lights moving around the night sky at the lake...then they were not so visible and I drove to my home about a mile away,and observed 2 more in the sky...then they were gone from sight.
