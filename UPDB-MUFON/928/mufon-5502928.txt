I was at a music fest in my town. I was admiring the moon and the huge halo around it. I noticed something come up from behind the mountains in the distance. It was the opposite direction of planes usual paths so i kept watching. It had an upward arched curve from the moment it started.. It continued the same upward motion until it was next to and past the moon..up up up til out of sight. It was round seem to glow or reflect gently. It had a steady fairly slow and even ascension. it appeared to be bluish. It left behind a very distinct 'chem trail' which never faded and never dissipated until after the object was gone. I had many people looking at the moon and when i saw the object first appear from behind the mountain i had everyone there watching it as well.
