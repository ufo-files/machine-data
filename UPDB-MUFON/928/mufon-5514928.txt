(GS) Two dozen+ glowing orange circular objects moving across the sky from SE to W in Columbia, MD

Observed 10 or more orange-colored objects moving across the sky while driving south on Rt. 29 at Rt. 108 in Columbia, MD. Pulled off at Rt. 108 and proceeded to Fairway Hills golf course on 5100 Columbia Rd. to get a better look since it is on a high hill. Observed two dozen or more orange objects moving across the sky, rising from the horizon in the southeast and moving west. Objects would rise two or three at a time and arc to the northwest moving at a moderate speed and a steady pace. No sound was apparent. Object was a steady orange glow, and there were no blinking lights. Two commercial airplanes took off from BWI during the sighting but were below the lights. Observation took place from approximately 10:00 to 10:19 EST. Lost sight of objects when they turned due west and faded away.
