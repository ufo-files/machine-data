{tjd} Gray disk seen in Willamette valley near silverton OR. Very clear view.

My son said he seen a UFO looking thing with a red light which we assumed was just a drone looking out our front window between some trees. He said it was flying odd like up and down not like a normal plane. A minute later I looked out the door just to take another look and I seen what I thought was a gray plane about the size of a fighter jet around a thousand feet elevation. The disk banked right while making a slight upward direction and that's when I seen ZERO wings no tail rutter no windows nothing . I've seen plenty of planes crop dusters helicopter you name it this was a very classic disk and to big to be a drone. The reason I'm making this report is because it was broad daylight with traffic near my home so someone else had to have seen it. I did see a commercial jet pass by above the cascade mt range at typical height I know my air traffic in this area well . This was a good size and moving very odd but it was the right banking move that told me this is not a plane.
