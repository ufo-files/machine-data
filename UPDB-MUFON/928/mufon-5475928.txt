{cak} Observed 4 or 5 star like spheres changing directions then stopping, starting,and changing direction at incredible speeds

I was in Gosport In.and was taking my dog out to do her business .I looked up toward the stars ....slightly east and almost straight above my head.I caught sight of what I first thought was a satellite.Moments later I observed 3 or 4 more orbs in the same area of sky. They seemed to have no certain path and changed directions at will. They did this for a matter of about 5 minutes . And as I was watching them they disappeared 1 by 1 until only 1 remained. This 1 was on a heading toward the south.It stopped then dimmed,changed direction and started heading North as toward Indianapolis .Then it looked as though it had landing lights and proceeded north. after about a minute it became extremely bright.Brighter and bigger than any star in the night sky stopped flickered a few times then totally disappeared.I have observed these same type orbs at least 5 times in my life.Dating back to New Mexico in 1975.This is my first encounter here in Indiana.I wonder why this so frequently happens to me.It is as if they are putting on a show for me.Maybe it is because of my love for the beauty of a night sky and I just look to the skies more often than most.....not sure.
