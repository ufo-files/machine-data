Observed multiple hovering/pathing craft - no noise or visible means of propulsion - pulsating/rotating lights of varying color

I was walking my dog as I do almost every night at around 7pm. As I was heading north on Winchester where it crosses highway 85, I noticed multiple unusual lights at various altitudes to the south - above Netflix HQ. Some lights were stationary and some were slowing pathing around, almost as if executing a search pattern. I heard no noise and saw no visible means of propulsion. The more I stood and observed, the more lights I saw. I took photos and video with my phone, some of them clear enough to see pulsating/spinning/rotating lights of different colors as well as odd movement and what appeared to be shape shifting.  At one point, a number of these craft lined up just above the treetops with one of them flying over top of the rest. As I was filming this, something moved behind me and scared my dog and I (probably just a wild animal but I was on edge!), and by the time I looked up again, the line of lights and the craft that had been flying above them were no longer visible. However, there were still many orbs/craft slowing flying around the area at altitudes/distances that were difficult to gauge ... I could see the with my naked eyes, but video revealed much more detail in terms of spinning/rotating/"popping in and out of existence" type of lights. I called my wife and she came out and met me and also observed the lights/crafts. We ultimately walked home over an hour later and could still see various lights slowing pathing above. These lights were not stars as they were moving and were not satellites as they were changing direction. There was no sound, so I don't believe they could be helicopters or drones. I'm outside at night fairly often and have never seen anything like this before ... I have lots of photos and video, but it was dark so the quality isn't great.
