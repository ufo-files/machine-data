{kt}Shooting Star then, 2 red orb appears and then accelerates away fast

In my back yard looking west/southwest approximately 7pm me and 2 others witness a very white shooting star appear to disintegrate while descending somewhere over hayward/san fransisco. I cannot make a determination on range. Shortly after, maybe 5 seconds a bright red orb appears at 30-40 degrees above the horizon stationary for 15 seconds and moves across the sky about 10 degrees to the left over about 5 seconds, and then rapidly accelerates away, not over the horizon but up and out, the light faded slowly over around 4-6 seconds as this happened. As soon as it was gone another one appears where the other had started out, but remains stationary for 20 seconds before disappearing in the same fashion as the first light. A friend arrived shortly after and he reported seeing the shooting star like object and red orb while driving but lost view of it when the road he was on curved.
