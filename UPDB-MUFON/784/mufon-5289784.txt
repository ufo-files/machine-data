Object brighter than anything in star field

While standing in the back yard of my home noticed a very bright object nearly over head of me. Moving slowly to the south. I was not aware of any passes of the ISS on this night. It suddenly move quickly to the south leaving a thin silver blue trail that quickly faded.. It stopped moving for several minutes (10-15). I walked into the house to let my wife what I had saw. She returned with me to the yard and easily identified the object due to it was much brighter than any other star in the sky. We continued to observe for the next 25 to 30 minutes. The was a front starting to enter our area, so there were broken clouds at approximately 1000 feet AGL. We remained in same spots as clouds passed. the location in the sky moved dramatically to the south west before returning to unmoving state. It was very bright and had a glow around it. My wife who has much better eye sight than mine stated she saw what appeared to be beams of light or a structure on the object. (My vision is 20/40 and hers is 20/20) We felt no threat from the object or suffer any ill effects. My wife retrieved a camera from the house but by the time she returned a large cloud back associated with the front had obscured the object. No photo was taken.
