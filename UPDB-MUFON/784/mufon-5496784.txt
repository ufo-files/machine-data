{cak} Large multiple story object hovering near highway

At around 7:00pm, I was returning to St. Louis from Effingham, IL, driving southbound on 255. I glanced down to check my speed and when I looked up I saw an extremely large multiple story object hovering near the highway in the distance. I saw flashing lights that seemed to create a rotation-like pattern and what appeared to be small windows. The object appeared to be metallic. I couldn't understand how an object so incredibly large could just hang in the sky like that. Once I processed what I was seeing, I immediately opened my car's window as I continued to drive to see if I could hear the object, but heard nothing other than the highway noise. I very specifically recall noticing that all of the hair on my arms was standing on end (I had removed my coat in the car for the long drive home). When I payed attention to the road, I noticed other cars on the road swerving and slowing down, so for safety's sake, I attempted to get off at the next exit ramp so I could get a better look at the object. After a few seconds of focusing on the road and before I was able to successfully get off the highway, I glanced up and saw that the object was gone.
