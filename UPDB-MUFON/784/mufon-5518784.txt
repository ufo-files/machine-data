Floating orb

Spotted mid afternoon, video taken from Buena Vista Park looking across Corona Heights, San Francisco, which would place the object above the Castro district / Market Street. The orb hovered behind Corona Heights and then slowly moved NE as you can see in the video where it syayed for several minutes before moving back behind Corona Heights (from my perspective). I then lost sight of the object and didn't see it again.

It was quite a windy day but the Orb was not swept away and did not appear erratic at all, movement was very steady.

I suspect this could be some kind of weather balloon, though obviously too low for a weather ballon, perhaps testing air quality? Or.... ?

The video is shaky as it was shot on a digital camera with a 200mm lens and no tripod, as you can see there are people on the hill who are interested in the object too.
