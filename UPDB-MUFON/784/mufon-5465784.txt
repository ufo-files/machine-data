{rjl} Looked like a soap bubble about 3-4 ft. diameter. traveling steady and unwavering with no apparent wind. 200 ft. elevation. Clear blue skies..

I was about 50 at the time and had never seen anything considered paranormal. Standing in my backyard I happened to look up and saw what appeared to be a soap bubble at about 80 degrees and 200 to 300 ft altitude. On the ground there was no wind, but at altitude...maybe. This thing traveled in a straight line; not at all in the erratic manner in which you might expect a soap bubble. I use the term 'soap bubble' because of the translucent oily looking sheen a soap bubble has which reminds me of the almost rainbow-ish sheen you would see on an oil slick in a pool of water. I hope you can understand my meaning. I don't want to over-emphasize this oily looking feature as if it were extremely prominent, but I thought I should mention it in the interest of giving every detail and this was about the only feature the object exhibited other than being a clear bubble. A totally transparent sphere is the way I would have described it otherwise except for this oily slick appearance. Also, the sphere never oblated back and forth such as a large bubble will. The bubble traveled in a straight line on a steady course for about 600 feet until disappearing behind some trees.
