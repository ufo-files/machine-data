Huge inverted Y of smoke like it was breaking in to 2 parts light was brilliant white & lit everything up for some time then faded

I was letting my dog in to the yard while I was getting ready for work as I went outside everywhere and everything was lit up brighter than day with an very intense white light that dazzled me the light lasted for some minutes 
I looked up and saw an inverted Y of greyish white smoke that looked bigger than anything I had ever been witness to before I can only compare it to the smoke and steam coming from the launch of a Saturn V rocket that came from 2 directions and joined to make the vertical shaft of the inverted Y
I have looked in all local newspapers watched the TV and radio news and nothing was mentioned about it I called the local TV station and the BBC and neither said that they had any reports of this incident 
The Y was about 10 degrees from the vertical as I was looking at it
