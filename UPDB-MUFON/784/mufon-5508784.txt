{dr}My mom saw a white-blue orb hovering in the sky and then it disappeared

My mom was on a walk through my neighborhood when she saw a blueish white orb in the sky. She said it was stationary and appeared to flicker slightly. The object was bigger than a star and was about a mile away from her. She took out her phone to take a picture, but when she looked up it had disappeared.
