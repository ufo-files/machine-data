{jpg} IN 1947 I SAW A HUGH SPACE SHIP SHAPED LIKE A BLIMP AND THEN SAW A SAUCER COME OUT OF IT

FILLED OUT AND FILED FROM A REPORT MAILED INTO MUFON HEADQUARTERS, FOR THE WITNESS, BY L. FLECHTNER 18809 ON 5/3/2018. STATEMENT BELOW WAS LETTER SENT WITH REPORT, NAMES OMITTED 
---------------------------------------------------
GENTLE PERSONS
THE STORY I AM GOING TO CONVEY TO YOU HAPPENED ABOUT 1947.
MY BROTHER (NAME OMITTED) WASW AN AMATUR ASTRONOMER AND BUILT TELESCOPES OF THE REFRACTING NATURE. HE MADE QUITE A STUDY OF THE SURFACE OF THE MOON AND AFTER OBSERVED JUPITER, SATURN, VENUS AND MARS. AT THIS TIME I WAS 10 YEARS OLD. (NAME OMITTED) HAD SEVERAL BOOK ON ASTRONOMY AND I WAS PRIVILEGED TO HAVE ACCESS TO THEM.I WOULD OFTEN GO INTO A NEARBY FIELD OF ABOUT 100 ACRES COVERED WITH BROOM SEDGE. HERE IN THE EVENING I WOULD LOOK AT THE MILKY WAY AND THE NOOM AND ALSO VARIOUS PLANETS, SUCH AS VENUS AND JUPITER. ON ONE SUCH OCCASION JUPITER WAS TO BE REALLY VISIBLE. THIS WAS PROBABLY IN THE AUTUMN SKY. I WAS STANDING IN THE FIELD WHEN I NOTICED A BRIGHT LIGHT IN THE EASTERN SKY. I WAS STANDING IN THE OHIO VALLEY  THE TOWN WAS PADEN CITY , WEST VIRGINIA. TO THE EAST AND WEST WERE HILLS APPROXIMATELY 350 TO 400 FEET HIGH. THE VALLEY WAS APPROXIMATELY 2 TO 3 MILES WIDE. THE OHIO RIVER RAN DOWN THE MIDDLE OF IT. I SAW THE LIGHT IN A LOWER PART OF THE HILL PROFILE, FOR EXAMPLE.
(WITNESS DRAWS A PICTURE IN THE LETTER) AS I LOOKED EAST I NOTICED A BRIGHT LIGHT, AND I THOUGHT IT WAS JUPITER. AS I WATCHED IT FOR SEVERAL MINUTES IT BEGAN TO MOVE PARALLEL TO THE HILL. I WATCHED IT FOR SEVERAL MINUTES AS IT MOVED NORTH VERY SLOWLY. I JUST HAPPENED TO LOOK TO THE NORTH OF THE LIGHT AND I THOUGHT WHAT I WAS WAS A CLOUD, ONLY THE CLOUD WAS LIKE A COUPLE OF SAUCERS INVERTED ON THEMSELVES EG (WITNESS DRAWS A PICTURE RESEMBLING A SAUCER)
THE LIGHT SLOWLY MOVED TO THE NORTH TOWARD THE CLOUD AND EVENTUALLY WENT INTO ITS EDGE. I FIGURED IF I WATCH LONG ENOUGH IT WILL COME OUT THE OTHER SIDE, BUT IT DIDN'T. I LOOKED AT THE REST OF THE LANDSCAPE ABOVE THE HILLS AND AS I LOOKED DIRECTLY NORTH HERE' WHAT I SAW. SEE ATTACHED
(WITNESS ATTACHED TWO PICTURES)
IT WAS AN AIR SHIP OR A SPACE SHIP MOVING NORTH WEST. AS IT MOVED WESTWARD IT WAS MOVING THROUGH EVENLY SPACED VERTICAL (WHITE) CLOUD FORMATIONS. IT WAS MOVING VERY SLOWLY. IT COULD NOT HAVE BEEN A DIRIGIBLE BECAUSE IT WOULD HAVE HAD TO BE SEVERAL MILES LONG. THE BACK WAS OPEN AND SOMETHING VERY INTERESTING HAPPENED.
A SAUCER SHAPED OBJECT FLEW INTO THE BACK OF THE SPACE SHIP AND CAME TO REST. THE BACK IN CLOSED AS THE SHIP PULLED AWAY. AS THE SHIP WENT INTO OHIO (WEST) I HAPPENED TO GLANCE BACK EAST TO SEE IF THE LIGHT HAD COME OUT FROM THE SAUCER SHAPED CLOUD. HERE WAS NOTHING THERE, SO I DECIDED TO WATCH THE SHIP GOING WEST DISAPPEAR INTO OHIO. THERE WAS NOTHING THEIR SHIP, CLOUDS EVERYTHING THAT I OBSERVED WAS GONE. ONLY BLUE SLY, HILLS, AND THE VALLEY WHERE THEIR.. WHAT DID I SEE? TO THIS DAY I HAVE RELAYED THIS STORY TO VERY FEW PEOPLE AND THEY HAVE A LOOK OF AMAZEMENT ON THEIR FACE UNLESS THEY WERE ATTACHED TO THE AIR FORCE OR SOME SUCH THING. BY THE WAY  WRIGHT PATTERSON AIR FORCE BASE WAS ONLY ABOUT 120 MILES AWAY FROM WHERE I LIVED. BY THE WAY . ABOUT THIS TIME I VISITED MY UNCLE AT JOHNSTOWN OHIO JUST ABOVE COLUMBUS OHIO. HE WAS IN SECURITY FOR NORTH AMERICA AVIATION. HE TOLD ME THAT HE KNEW MANY OF THE GUARDS AT WRIGHT PATTERSON AIR FORCE BASE. THE ASSURED HIM THAT THE WRECKAGE AND LITTLE GREY MEN WERE REAL AND FROM OUTER SPACE WHICH THEY BROUGHT IN FROM ROSWELL.
I HOPE THIS IS OF SOME USE.
YOURS TRULY,
(NAME WITHHELD)
PS FORGIVE ME ABOUT SOME OF THE GRAMMAR. I AM 81 YEARS OLD AND HAVE TROUBLE SOMETIMES WITH SPELLING AND GRAMMAR.
