Bright orange spheres rising

Second time we have seen this phenomenon in the past 12 months. We sit out back at night. From the NW we saw app. 4 bright orange glowing spheres rising. They were traveling at great spped up into the atmosphere. Over the course of about 2 hours they continued to rise in great numbers. Some took formations and some did not. We noticed the same things reported all over the country over the past few days.
