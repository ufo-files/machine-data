{cak} red lights in the sky

I was standing at the back door talking to my daughter on the phone when I saw about 7 to 9 bright red fireball looking lights in the sky. At first I thought maybe its military helicopters going to the Rifle, Colorado airport but there was no sound & they were flying too close together. A couple of the red light seemed to be to the left but, most were in a straight line traveling to the West over the mountains. I woke my husband up to come see & he caught sight of them towards the end before they went over the mountain tops & he thought they were strange as well.
