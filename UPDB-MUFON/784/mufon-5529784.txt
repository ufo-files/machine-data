Hovering Saucer over Mopac at 2222

The "IFO" had a doom top, flat bottom and bulbous sides. It was hovering at around 1500ft. I was driving southbound on 183 and at first I wrote it off as a plane, until I realized it wasn't moving. By time I exited and return to get a second look, it was gone.
