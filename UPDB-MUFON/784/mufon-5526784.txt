Light green oval shape with orange flame behind resembling the burn of an alcohol lamp

My fiancee and I were driving through Blackshear at aprox. 10 p.m. last night and saw two bright flashes similar in brightness to lightening. We then saw a light green ovaloid object with orange flames from the back of it moving through the sky west to east and making no sound. It seemed to be losing altitude but was not moving at an extreme rate of speed like a meteor. It was only visible for 3 to 5 seconds before being obscured by the tree line. Once on Hwy 84 driving toward Waycross, we caught sight of an extremely bright orangish light in the Eastern sky in the same approximate area of the previous burning object. It floated in the sky moving slighty from side to side before fading from view after a few minutes.
