{jpg} object hovering over river

I was still in high school and the school playground and soccer field was behind my grandparents' house and behind neighbors house which is no longer there. My mother,grandmother , and neighbor lady had watched an after school soccer match which had ended. The players had left the field and the three stood on their property side of the fence having a neighborly chat. The Tioghnioga River runs parallel to the field. Suddenly the three saw the object described above hovering at just above tree level above the river and they mentioned lights around the side of it. They told each other that they would tell no one due to the nature of the event. My mother and grandmother,however, did come into the house and told us all quite flabbergasted. They said it had disappeared suddenly. Only my mother survives of the three and we bring up the event in discussion not infrequently all these years later.
