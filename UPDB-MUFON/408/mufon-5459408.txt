{tjd} Several strange objects over west los Angeles

I was sitting on a bench on top of a sprouts farmers market on the corner of la brea Avenue and San Vincenta in west Los Angeles. I looked up and noticed a stationary metallic object sitting in the sky.  I watched it for about five minutes before it began to move. It moved to the east and then stopped once it got close to another similar looking object.  The two objects then moved together towards the west and then stooped and hovered in place. At that moment I then noticed two more objects appear to the east.  After I noticed the other two objects I reached for my phone as I was pulling it out of my pocket the two objects to the west began moving in circles as if dancing with each other and changing directions sig zagging.  I was able to catch two shirt videos. The objects were hard to catch with my phone but I did get one on film and would love to share it.
