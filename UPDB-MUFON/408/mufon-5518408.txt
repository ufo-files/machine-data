FAST MOVING GLOWING FIREBALL OBSERVED OVER STATEN ISLAND

On June 3, 2012 at approximately 12:20pm an orange/red fireball was observed in the western sky. The sighting lasted for about 30 seconds, there was no noise- completely silent. It traveled in a straight line (very fast- about the same altitude as an aircraft) and ascended very slightly while traveling. The UFO flew past the moon, no outline was observed. Before the object disappeared it changed color (blue/green star-like) and disappeared.
