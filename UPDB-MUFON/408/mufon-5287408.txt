Flashing light moving erratic pulsing brightly.

I don’t know how to explain what I was seeing. I just know I have a phone clip that lasted 57 seconds. I was vacationing at an oceanfront condo. As I was getting to go to bed a reflection of lights caught my attention so I looked out the window and I saw many lights to the left of my window in the water flashing one at a time as if signaling each other. I didn’t think much of it maybe they were boats in the water making sure they saw each other. But then I turned my attention at the sky and I saw a bright pulsing light moving erratically. I picked up my phone and began to film it. The movements were strange to me not movements that our known conventional aerial movements. I then took the phone video and expanded the pulsating light and I caught a still photo that showed a triangle in the light.
