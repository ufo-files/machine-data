stubby winged aircraft on my way home from work

After work from Minneapolis. I noticed lights in the sky and thought they were car dealer spotlights. As I got closer, I thought it was maybe a helicopter from Benson airport because of the red light flashing underneath it. As I pulled up to it, it was a bright white light (extremely white) from the front. I passed another vehicle that was pulled off on the side of the road and stopped almost directly beneath it and got out of my vehicle. Because of the wind and light snow that was blowing very strong, the white light illuminated its shape as a stubby winged aircraft. I watched it for about 10-15 minutes and then drove down the road a quarter mile, turned right and looked at it directly behind. At first, I saw 2 orange orbs on the back of it and after looking closer, I could actually see the shape of the rearend of the craft. I watched it for 5-10 minutes as it slowly pulled away. When the craft was stationary, it was pointed directly at White Bear Lake. In the high winds, it was about 100 yards up and it was so steady in the strong winds (completely still) and there was no sound at all. I have thought a lot about this over the years and have never seen a description of this type of aircraft. Now that I am older and retired, I just wanted to get it on record.
