{rjl} Green light leaving a white trail

I was standing on my back stoop having a cigarette. I saw a jet and was watching it. The jet was flying east to west. Out of the corner of my eye, I saw something. I looked at it thinking it was a shooting star, but it was green and left an appearent trail that lingered as a jet trail would. It had a north to south direction. It appeared, from my point of view, to be going straight toward the jet. I was immediately concerned about an impact with the jet. It, however, passed overhead of the jet. I lost sight of it when it went over the clouds. I went on the internet to look up shooting stars. The only reference to the green that I found was in the trail but not the meteor.
