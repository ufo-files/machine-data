Very shiny cylinder object seen in am then faded away, in clear skies, followed by low flying jet heading in same direction.

Husband and I driving south on 83 at about 12:15 a.m. We both saw a very shiny long cylinder shaped object flying south. I was very reflective almost radiating, or reflecting white light. We watched it move fast and then slow like it was banking or turning. There was not a cloud to be seen in the sky. We both stared at it then it faded and disappeared. Within 1 minute a low flying jet flew right into the same path the object was in and kept heading south. This plane had jet fume trail behind it so we assumed it was a jet, it was flying very low like the object. We would like to know if anyone else saw this.
