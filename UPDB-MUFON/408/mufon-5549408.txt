a blue light with tale similar to meteor

I was in bed lying on my left side facing a mountain less then 1 mile away.A blue light (not a flash) but a light moving steadily moved across my vision from left to right.The light was moving downward toward the base of the mountain.I have seen meterors before and it was not a meteor.It disappeared from my vison after about 30 seconds.I looked at my night stand clock (red LED) and it read exactly 3:15 (in the A.M.).I could determine no particular shape.Hard to tell the size because I was noticing the blue light (did not flash, just a steady color).It (the light) appeared round in front with a tale.Would have written it off as a meteor but moving just to slow. If it had crashed I would have heard and seen an explosion. It was that near.
