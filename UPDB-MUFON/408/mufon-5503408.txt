One summer night I was about 8 or 9 years old we were coming home to New Jersey from my grandmothers traveling south on Rt 94. As we passed by the stretch of highway that has a cattle farm on both sides we noticed traffic was going slow almost at a stand down. I was riding in the front set with my mom. As we got closer we noticed three lights in the sky. It was a green a red and a white light. The sky was clear. As we got just about under it we could see a dark triangler shape and each of those lights was in a corner. It hovered and just slowly rotated making no noise. Where we were on the road we were about 5 miles from a hospital and a heavily populated and active town. We just slowly moved along watching it as we passed under it and got further down the road. And looking out the back window you could see it in the distance as we kept driving home. My mother and I told my father and he looked at us funny. We always told people but the looked at us like we were crazy. So me and my mother kept it to ourselves. We saw your report on Hudson Valley and maybe thought it might have some relation. To this day I remember the exact spot where I saw it. It was almost like you reach out and touch it. My mother and I have always been told we are nuts. But we are just looking for some answers to this. It was that it scared us it just seemed really odd and to this day not something we can explain.
