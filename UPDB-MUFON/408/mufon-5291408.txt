Hovering Disk with rotating lights

Over a duration of 30mins, I observed a dark disc with a dome on top with lights around the disc pulsating in a left to the right pattern at a medium rate of speed. The object hovered over the downtown core of hamilton Ontario Canada for approximately 30 minutes then proceeded at a high rate of speed north over lake Ontario. I lost sight of the object when I walked out of view behind a tree for 1-2 minutes the next moment the object was gone.
