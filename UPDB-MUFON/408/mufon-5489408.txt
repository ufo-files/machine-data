{nd} A swarm of red-orange lights with dust trails moving in random patterns.

I was walking my dog at 8:26 pm on 9-06-2015 when something caught my eye. Close to 30 red orange balls of light were dancing in the sky as I looked to the east. At first I thought they were fireworks shooting up into the sky. The flickering light trail behind them said I was right. But I never heard the boom or saw the flash of fireworks. Then they began changing directions like a swarm of bees. Then some would disappear and as others would be there. The lights must have been a few miles away so I could not see what they were. I came back in after about 10 minutes and my cellphone and Internet were having connection issues.
