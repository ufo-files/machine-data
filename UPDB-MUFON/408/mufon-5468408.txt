Strange lights started to flash me, I've investigated and stayed vigilant and that's when I saw it

I was smoking a cigarette outside my house as usual before going to bed. All of the sudden right in for of me and above me I saw something emitting two very bright flashes, at first I thought it was a plane, then the flashes started to get more "random" and more powerful to the point that I started to get scared, there was no sound emitting from it except sounds made by airplanes nearby. I decided to stay outside a little longer since I wanted to know what did I just saw, that's when things started to get crazy, I had this laser pointer (green laser <1mw) in my pocket that I usually use to play around with my dog, I started to make circles with it around the area where I saw the object and that's when I saw it. It started with a low light slowly becoming brighter and brighter to the point where it almost looked like a falling star, it remained stationary for 2/3 seconds..it was beautiful to watch..then out of the blue it took off at great high speed leave behind it what I would describe as a white flame/line. I Will never forget that moment even because since that time, once in a while it seems to come back, with same circumstances and events, maybe that thing knows that I saw it.

I Have no reason to lie or make stuff up, I'm a random Italian guy that never believed in any of this stuff, but that day changed my mind..and in some ways my life.
