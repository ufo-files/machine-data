Strange-looking

At first it looked to me like a very bright star. I do a lot of photography so I pulled out my Samsung Android cell phone to take a few shots. As I focused in on this bright object, it seemed to throb or pulse, sort of from the inside out. It appeared to have its own halo and when I viewed the shots I took, it sure looked like a circle within a circle. Best way I could describe it would be like a BUTTON. It stayed stationary the entire time I observed and photographed it. The distance was anywhere from 5 miles to 10 miles up.
At times while watching, it appeared to have a protrusion on top of it, but I didn't see it on the photos I took. It was an intensely bright object that stood out from the handful of stars in the sky.
