{rjl} Huge disk flying over me 50ft above

I was across the street lying down on a trampoline looking up at the sky with my next door neighbor. We both saw a huge disk 50ft in diameter fly over us treetop altitude. No lights and no sound. It was so close to us it blocked out the moon light when it flew over. Only 3-4 seconds and it was gone. They only thing that happened afterwards is I looked over at my friend and we both acknowledged what we saw and we went home immediately. I felt strange.
