{rjl} Big sphere with small sphere orbiting around the big sphere. Shined very brightly.

I was on my back porch, which looks out to a huge field. It was 8:00 PM and very dark. I say a spherical object hovering 4-5 feet above the ground. The sphere was glowing extremely brightly, and I could not see any features. The color of the light was a white/yellow. There was a second sphere that was red and VERY slowly hovered in random flight patterns around the big sphere while it was about 2 feet away from the big sphere, but not touching it. The small red sphere was about 17 inches in diameter while the big sphere was 2 feet in diameter. My lights flickered on and off once and very fast. Then, my TV turned off and had an unusual amount of static emitting from it because it shocked me. It was there for about a half hour, when it became too cold and I was forced to come inside. I remember hearing an EXTREMELY faint whirring sound. (Same as a computer makes when it is running). It was strange how a big shining sphere had a small red sphere orbiting around it.
