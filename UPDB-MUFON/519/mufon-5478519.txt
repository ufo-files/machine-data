{rjl} Saw elongated green light traveling across the sky which suddenly changed directions and broke in two before blinking out completely.

I was driving north to work on hwy 32 about 2-3 miles south of Holt and observed something with a blinking green light on back and solid red light on front (the lights were about the size of a pea at arms length) and was traveling at about the same speed as an aircraft in a north direction. I paid it no mind at that point, thinking it was just an airplane, but about 2 minutes later I notice a green light (which looked a bit elongated almost like 2 pea size lights right next to each other) traveling in a northeast direction. I had to lean forward in my drivers seat to observe it fully. It traveled for only a couple seconds in this direction then dove down in what seemed like a northward direction. I thought I was witnessing a plane crash because it abruptly shot down, but then the lights just completely dissapeared even though the thing was still well above the tree line. Later when I got to work I heard other people had seen strange lights in the sky also, at about the same time as me, but I'm not sure if they saw the same thing or not.I did notice the radio acting funny before seeing the first light, as well.
