{dr}bright orange light hovered, and then went up and disappeared

I sat on my balcony and peered at the stars. Just relaxing and all of a sudden I saw a huge ball of glowing orange. I sat there a few seconds just taking it in and then trying to figure out what I was seeing. Best I can describe it was bigger than the stars around it. Like 10 times the circumference. I sat there looking at it like it was just a big orange ball of light. I assumed that was all it was until it seemed to shoot up straight into the sky in an instant and was gone. I never saw it again . It hovered for a matter of minutes and then snap, it was gone. What it did is not what I know our military or any type of flight is capable of. I was left with a sense of awe and a lot of questions. I had people with me who did not witness it with me, so I did not tell them what I saw for fear they would laugh at me. I know what I saw....
