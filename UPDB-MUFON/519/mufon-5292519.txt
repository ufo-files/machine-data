UFO OVER AJAX ONTARIO AUGUST 8 2020

This thing was gigantic... It was probably the size of 10 football fields. It was lined with 100+ windows and inside was very very bright. It did not make a single sound. I was in my backyard and my dog came running to the back door and started barking non stop. I was coming in to quiet him from waking up my household but decided to look up at the sky one last time and that's when I saw it. I grabbed my binoculars and couldn't believe what I was seeing.
