Blue/green sphere shaped luminous object,

In late August or early September 1998, my now ex-wife Veronica and myself were on our way to Illinois from Washington state, driving east on I-90 a mile or so after passing Rapid City SD and about a mile before Ellsworth Air Force Base. Veronica was in the right side passenger seat and looking up at the night sky through the side window. She said, "Wow, that's a bright looking star", I took a brief glance and saw the "star" and then looked back at the road as I was driving. Then she said, " It's moving", so I looked again to see this star like object descending down at about a 45 degree angle through some scattered clouds. As the object cleared the bottom of the scattered cloud layer I could make out a solid blue green translucent sphere that emitted a blue green glow. It seemed to be moving at around 1000 mph and when it reached about 500 feet altitude it suddenly leveled off with a sharp 45 degree change in direction. The object continued at around 500 feet and crossed I-90 in a North direction about a mile ahead of our position. Then after a few seconds the object did a similar maneuver, sharp 45 degree direction change toward the sky and accelerated out of sight within a split second. The acceleration seemed to be instant, like changing from 1000 mph to 10000 mph at once. In a split second the object became a tiny speck in the direction of space and vanished. Though the object did a high speed low pass very near Ellsworth, never mind thinking it was a jet, as there are no jets that can change direction or velocity as this object did, and jets do not have the dimensional layout of a sphere.
