{nd} Flying Object that changes colors

I was working a night shift outside checking buildings to make sure that the doors were secure when I happened to look up and notice an object that resembled a green star that was moving a rapid pace much faster then any helicopter, because while I was watching this object I could see a helicopter just behind it with blinking lights a little further in the distance. I observed this object moving for a couple of minutes then noticed it changed to a pure white color and changed direction. It started moving fairly quickly at this point. I decided at then to keep my eyes on the object for as long as possible before it was out of sight, but before that happened, I noticed it change completely red and turn direction again. This time it started coming back to the original location I first noticed it at. After a few short minutes I sent a transmission to my partner asking if he was seeing what I was and he said that he noticed the same thing. I was hoping to see the object for a longer time, but it eventually went behind a line of trees and out of sight.
