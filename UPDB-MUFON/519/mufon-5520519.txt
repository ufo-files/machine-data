walking home from girlfriends house

walking along road i walked every night home from girlfriends house while crossing wooden bridge I heard a low whoring sound, as I looked up I saw a very large object hovering about 50 feet above me and about 30 feet in front of me, the object had a square body more than 3/4 of its entire length then what I assumed was the front made up the rest,it was shaped like an arrow head the body was illuminated in white lights @ every 10 feet outlining its shape, in the back of the object there were three large red lights pulsating in unison with the whirring sound, I looked at the object in awe, it then slowly moved off to the west and was gone i arrived back at my sisters house where my good friend was waiting for me to return and he had asked where have I been, i was apparently looking at this thing for a good 3 hours but to me it was only 15 minutes, I looked every night for this thing and never saw again this happened in Caspian Michigan
