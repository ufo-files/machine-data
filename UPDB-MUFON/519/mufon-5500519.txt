{cak} round metallic object moving slowly to the n/w

On Tuesday July 29th at 4:30pm as I was driving N/B on Martin Luther King Way a reflection of light in the sky caught my attention. This was a beautiful sunny day with no clouds in the sky. I observed sunlight reflecting off of a round-smooth metallic (chrome) ball in the sky. It appeared to be hovering about 500 feet over Boeing field. As the object began moving very slowly to the N/W I could see it clearly. There were no markings on it and appeared to be perfectly smooth and chrome in color. It1s hard to judge the size because of nothing near it to compare to but I1m guessing it was about the size of a house.
  As I watched the object move slowly to the N/W it seemed to 1fade out1 or blend into the sky (I could still see the shape of it). I continued watching it until it went completely out of view. I kept looking in the area to hopefully see it again. The round metallic object reappeared and began to slowly ascend straight up- then it just disappeared!
