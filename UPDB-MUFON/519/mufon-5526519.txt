An triangle-shaped object with lights on it flew slowly through the sky

I was just chilling in my backyard when this think crossed the sky, not very fast. I randomly saw it, it did not make any sound or something that made me notice it. I could not determine what it was, so i knew it should be something like an UFO. As the triangle flew by it didn't make any sound, neither did it leave a gas trail, like a plain would do. As I was following the object I could see more people getting on the street to see it for themselves. After it flew behind some buildings I lost sight of it, and I talked a lot with the neighbors about it.
