Multiple star like lights in a single file

At approximately 05:00 (5:00a.m.) out side in the back yard looking up at the early morning stars/ moon. A star-like light appeared over the horizon starting from the south west traveling north east. Another followed and many more (maybe 10 or more) star like lights follow behind the first on a straight line one after the other. They were the highest objects is the sky. The sighting lasted from my visible horizon to visible horizon. The objects never wavered off course nor did the change following distance from each other.
