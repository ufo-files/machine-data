{nd} Stationary, then slow crawl, shot up, shot down, then horizontal super speed

I was out walking dog and all the stars were very illuminated in dark sky. I noticed what I thought was another star but saw it had a red light flashing and that caught my attention so watched it. It was just still very high in sky, not moving - just a flashing red light. Then, very slowly it barely moved across to right at a snail pace. Then it started moving downward in an almost vertical direction. Suddenly it stopped and climbed upward extremely fast, then again, downward just as fast. I couldnt believe what I was seeing. Then it shot to the left horizontally, came lower and moved back to the right and behind me so I had to turn in a circle. My dog was pulling on the leash so I finally moved on. Then after a block, I turned to see if it was still headed in that direction and spotted it moving back the way it came except now, there was a smaller white only light moving at exact speed following behind and lower to it. The two objects continued back direction it had made from the circle toward the spot where I first observed it. Ive never seen a plane able to make those types of moves. The night was black dark so all I can describe are the ligths and movements. (The time is uncertain, somewhere between 6PM & 7PM.)
