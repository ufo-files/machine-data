Saw a disc-like object at a birthday party/sleepover.

I can't remember the exact date and year, but i was about 7-10 years old at that time, and it was a summer month. I'm 16 now, but i still remember it. I was at a birthday party at a classmate's house. It was a sleepover, and her house was in the rural area of the city. It was about 1 o'clock at night, and we were all going out to stargaze. We all laid on the trampoline, and just looked up at the sky. A couple of minutes went by, and then a flat disc-like object flew over us. The object was close enough for us to see details, like it looked that it was made of metal. I think the object may have been about 50 metres up in the air, or maybe even less. The object was flying in a straight path. It had 3 white lights, arranged in a triangle on it's underside/belly. I don't know if it was the whole object that spun, or if it were only the lights. But it spun. It made a low buzz/humming sound. It didn't flew very fast, i think the sightning lasted for about 2-4 minutes. We lost sight of it, as it flew towards the horizon. We didn't panic, we just felt puzzled. But later that night we felt a little unsettled, but i think that's normal for kids in that age. We tried to search the sky for the object a little later that night, but we didn't see it again.
