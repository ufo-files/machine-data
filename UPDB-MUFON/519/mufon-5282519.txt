Black cylinder

SW of Vegas Strip, moving toward NE. Swinging like a pendulum. If on a clock face, moving from 10 to 2 in rhythmic pattern. Appeared to be quite large. Lots of military activity in that area, with craft taking off almost vertically several times a day, with two contrails. FAA told neighbor that those daily flights are to supply ISS, which is obviously bs. Not coming from direction of Nellis AFB.

This was freaky. I never doubted, or believed with certainty, that alien craft existed. But I know what I saw, and this was not under any circumstance a military or other vehicle from Earth.
