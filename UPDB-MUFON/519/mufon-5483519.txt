{cak} sighting

We were walking around our neighborhood with 7 people (including myself) and we heard a rumble approaching us and we hid in the grass. We thought it was a car, but as it approached, multiple lights came into focus and it became clear it was not a car. It had three lights in a triangle pattern with a large light in the center and another light above the top most light of the triangle. It seemed to descend and hover/land, but after seeing us quickly moved away on a straight path line. We tried to process was we saw but could not come up with a solid answer.
