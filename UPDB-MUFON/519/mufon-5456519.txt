{cak} bright lights over the Atlantic ocean near Cape Canaveral

On this evening, I saw about 10 bright lights in a direct row looking to be heading northwest from over the ocean. They had no sound at all and no blinking lights. Some of the lights disappeared others appeared to be going up and would disappear at different elevations. More and more appeared and some were in a V formation. They were there one second then gone immediately. The lights had no particular color like red or green or something a plane or helicopter would. Different lights kept coming for about 15 minutes never seeming to come closer or farther just kind of - up and then dissappearing.
