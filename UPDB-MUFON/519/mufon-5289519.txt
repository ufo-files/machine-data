Fast, hovered, chg directions, lights flash

Saw it moving from the NE moving very fast. At first thought it was an airplane but was moving too fast and appeared to be round in shape with flashing bright red and green lights flashing randomly. When it was directly in front of us I could see it was round in shape. The outer shell was dark and the lights seemed to go all around the shape. They were very bright red and green. It suddenly stopped and hovered for a few seconds, then moved quickly left, then moved back quickly right. It then moved upwards stopped and hovered for a few seconds. Suddenly it went completely black as though it had disappeared and we could not see it for a few seconds. It then reappeared in the exact same spot with all lights flashing. It moved abruptly to the right then down and back to the left, hovered for a few seconds. Then suddenly it moved so fast straight at us, and it was gone. It was so far up but it was about the size of a basketball. It was a clear night and a full moon so you could see far off and clearly. All this happened within about 30-35 seconds, but it seemed like it was longer. I tried to film it with my phone but I had been charging it and by the time I got it back on the incident was over.
