Bright object in the horizon

I was at home. Then I was called to go to the park to see. They told me it was an UFO that became greater then smaller. I went and saw a bright object in the horizon. The sky was almost clear in the night. I returned home to take my camera. Took some pics. Some minutes after the object seemed to be more to the south.
I checked skymap and there was not an object as bright as this so low in the sky. It disappeared minutes after, because it vanished or because there were low clouds.
