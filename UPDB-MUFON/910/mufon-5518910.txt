triangular shape

I was going on a darker road in the country, when I looked up in the sky {like I always do} and seen 4 small fast blinking lights in a triangular formation heading north. I knew they were not planes, and not going the normal east/west...west/east directions. The formation changed a bit, but kept mostly triangular. I had my camera/video and was trying to find a good place to pull over to get some video, but there was nothing. I kept watch as the formation seemed to be going pretty fast, and I was going rather fast also because I wanted to find a spot to get some video. They were blinking very fast white color...no reds or blues....like tiny stars, but they were not stars. I kept them in sight until they kinda disappeared, and I finally was able to pull into a parking lot of a church. I couldnt see the triangular formation anymore, but then I saw 2.more of the same things heading north on the same path I had seen the triangle formation...but I heard these as jets..one above..one below and slightly behind. They disappeared, and soon I saw 2 more in the same formation headed in the same direction as the other 2. By this time my brother had joined me, as we were meeting to exchange metal to be powder coated for our business. He saw them also...and couldnt believe it. Then we saw 2 of them come back north to south...going extremely fast! All the time we were seeing regular planes and jets going west to east...east to west, but these jets were flying much faster...like they were looking for something. It was very cool!
