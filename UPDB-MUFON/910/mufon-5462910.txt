Went into my back yard shortly after 6:00 p.m. on January 24, 2019 to let my dog potty and observed what I initially thought was a very large star. But very shortly after I first noticed it I realized it was blinking different colors. I saw red, blue, green, white. I went back in the house and grabbed my cell phone. Although the object seemed very far from me I could still see that it was changing colors and appeared to be rotating. I observed it without interruption for about five minutes. I took a brief video for my husband to see. I asked him to tell me what he thought it was and he said he couldn't tell me. I haven't been able to stop thinking about it since I saw it and I'm just really curious as to what this thing was/is.
Maybe it was nothing...but it sure looked like something.
