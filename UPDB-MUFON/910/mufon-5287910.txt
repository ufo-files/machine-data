Straight line of lights moving across the sky

My family was sitting around a camp fire as one of us noticed the lights moving across the sky, we all stood up and started trying to take pictures and film what we were seeing as we were unsure what we were seeing and in kind of a disbelief. The lights lasted nearly ten minutes as they moved from one side of the sky to the other. We all sat around wondering what we just seen and some of us were kind of scared.
