2 round bright lights side by side

I first spotted it towards the east coming from Myrtle Beach direction. Observed as it silently crossed the sky from the east to west -- I'm guessing it traveled 50-70 miles on video. Three unusual things I noticed 1) totally silent 2) as it passed, the lights never dimed or vanished like an airplane's should once it passes I did see a flashing light at times 3) towards the end of the video the object flips from horizontal to vertical. I did drop the camera (phone) 2 times. The second video shows it until I dropped the camera yet again and that was it.

I don't know what it was, but it was unlike anything I've ever seen in the night sky. I shot it the best I could--sorry for all the shakes!
