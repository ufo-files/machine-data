{nd} lights observed moving erracticaly

September 2007 My friend and i left my house to walk to the niehbors 2 miles away and as usual we stared at the sky walking along until we noticed a star blinking wierd we then noticed another. So at this point we started looking for things we knew were there like sattelites and planes. After spotting a couple of both we started watching these 'stars' which by this point had grown brighter than all other objects minus the moon. They then started moving erratically throughout the sky in all directions until they suddenly vanished. About a week later in Riverton just about the same thing happened. I was standing on the corner next to my friends setting up a grill. I noticed a star next to the moon which shouldn't be there. It did the same exact thing, zipping about the sky all super crazy for about 25 minutes and then bam it was gone off and away. I would not shut up for weeks and as a result I found i was not the only one to have seen these 'stars' on both nights. Also i only asked people i know so it was not a large pool. i did get three credible people who said they observed them on the north side of mille lacs lake in palisade and in a township called burrows outside of brainerd. A lot of people also see b2 bombers around our area and mistake them however they always fly with a triangle pattern 'forward or backward i have seen both'of white lights on the bottom and follow roads and air routes.
