Observed a large pink self illuminating cloud over State Police Academy and Wine Rd.

Driving home from dinner with family about 8:50PM I spotted an enormous billowing pink cloud above but also behind the State Police Academy in MA. At first I thought it was a fire lighting up the sky, I called people in the area and there was no fire so I stopped and took photos. Nikon D 850, ISO 4K, F2 at a 1/10th of a second. I assumed it was glowing from the moon but Moonrise was at 12:16AM photos taken about 8:50PM.
