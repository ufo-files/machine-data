{dr}Triangle shaped object

I was sitting in my backyard when I noticed an object from the distance behind houses going up as if it were taking off. It had two Bright white huge lights. it was flying toward a straight direction (unknown direction exactly) but then turned coming towards my direction and it looked huge. All of the sudden the object turned around smoothly and I noticed a third bright light. That is when I noticed it was a triangle shape craft. Where I live I constantly see planes, helicopters and private jets flying in my area and notice they have blinking lights on them and are very loud of course. Now this craft (ufo) I saw was huge, had no blinking lights like mentioned above and had NO SOUND. I was amazed for the size of the object how smooth it flew and the shape but the fact it had no sound amazed me. I really would like to know what I saw but of course we never get answers. I have never seen anything like this and it really will be on my mind forever. Amazing!
