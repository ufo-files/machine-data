{tjd} Wow this was the most incredibly orange light I have ever seen

Wow.....this was the most incredible thing I have ever seen in my life. I was taking my dog for his evening walk which usually occurs some where between 9:30-10:30pm daily. Tonight just happened to be at about 10:10 pm Eastern . I walked out of my house down my wall to the side walk and turned South,South East and in the sky emitting through the clouds was this intense Orange light. It was cloudy at the time but I could still see a huge light . I usually have my phone with me when I walk the dog,unfortunately this night I did not. The best description I could give to explain it was it looked like when lightning is building up inside of a cloud but orange. I couldn’t see any objects per se,only lights. It might have been there for a minute tops probably not even that long .it took my brain half of the time it was there to process what I was seeing. Maybe my eyes were adjusting to coming outside etc etc ....but then as soon as my brain comprehends that what I’m seeing is the most amazing thing I have ever seen in my 40 years on this planet....the light totally was gone and disappeared like it was never even there 😥. I ran to get my wife and to show her but by the time we got outside nothing else was happening. It was WAYYY to large of an event for me to have been the only person in Michigan or anywhere in the northern area near Canada to have seen. Just amazing.
