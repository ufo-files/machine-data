grey oval object

I was out by my back door just taking a picture of my garden which I intended to use as a screen saver on my laptop, the day had been very warm and at time of taking the picture there were no clouds in the sky at all, not until I put the picture on my laptop did I realize that there was something very strange in the background of the picture, high up in the sky there was an airplane with a vapour trail but below it was something else, oval in shape, and a light grey colour and was not a cloud, if someone out there can throw a bit of light on this object it would be great, there was also no sound at all at time of taking picture and the camera was new
