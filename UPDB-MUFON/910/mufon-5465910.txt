{cak} Random atmospheric flashing, slow moving

About 330 am I noticed a flashing light in the sky as I watched for meteors. I thought it was an airplane at first, but the lights were random and seemed to change color. Almost like something tumbling through the atmosphere. It was moving very slow, just creaping across the sky. I pulled up my radar ap and saw that there was a project loon balloon in the area of my sighting. However, why the heck and I seeing a project loon balloon, at 60 thousand feet, at 330 am? I saw one the other day as the sun was going down and was illuminated this way. I mean, it could very well just be the loon balloon, it1s just the illumination and random flashing seemed odd so I thought I would report it.
