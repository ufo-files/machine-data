Star speeds across night sky.

I was at home enjoying an evening with my wife.
I decided to have a cigarette and went to the window around 9:30 pm.
While I was having that cigarette I was observing the night sky.

While looking at stars and constellations, I noticed 
out of the corner of my eye a very very bright star that
appeared/seemed to be tracking very fast, through the upper
atmosphere, across the sky.

Being aware of what it couldn't be I quickly realized that I was
seeing something quite unusual, so I decided to use the camera on my iPhone 
(which I had been using for the constellation app a moment before) to record what I was seeing! 

You can watch the video here: [Link moved to FI Section/cms/tg]

you see the the video as a negative that it clearly reveals what i was seeing with my naked eye. The object honestly was close to the size of the star I clearly point out in the video that it passes by. An intense white light that I have honestly never seen on or used by man made air craft.

My thoughts at the time were, am I actually seeing this!?
What could this be, and I immediately began trying to rationalize
what it was we were actually seeing.

My best guess is moon or sun light reflectied off the
space station or a SAT. that was passing through low orbit?
I lost sight of the object as it appeared to suddenly change direction and dissapered.
I don't know, I'd like to find out. I'm hoping you can help me?
