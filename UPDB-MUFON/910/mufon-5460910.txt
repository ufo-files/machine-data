{cak} While driving north on I75 saw objects to the east that passed overhead

While driving north on I 75 north of Ft. Myers, FL witnesses observed up to 6 star-like objects fly toward them, overhead and change directions and come back overhead. One object was estimated to be a large triangle shaped, about the size of a 747, and had lights at each point. Some objects were star-like to aspirin size anf were white, red, and blue lights.
