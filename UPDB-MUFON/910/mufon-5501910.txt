{dr}3 Triangle shape craft spotted on I-20 in Ga.

I was driving from Atlanta to Columbia,SC on I-20. IT was approximately 2A.M. and my family was asleep in the car. About half way to Columbia, I looked up from the highway and saw 3 craft in formation. One lead craft and 2 behind and off to the side. They were triangle in shape with a large white light at each corner. The corners were not sharp, but rounded. As I drove under the crafts I could clearly see their shape because they blotted out the stars behind them, since it was a bright, starry night. The 3 craft made no apparent sound. I continued to drive under the 3 craft and on up I-20. By the time I had awoken my wife, we had passed the 3 craft. The most puzzling part is the fact that I did not stop the car to investigate. I had always told myself if I ever encountered anything similar that I would definitely investigate it to the best of my ability. I drove on to Columbia wondering what I had seen and still wonder what it was to this day. I would appreciate any input into solving this mystery. The crafts appeared to be at an altitude of 500 to 1000 feet. Their size appeared to be 500 feet from tip of their triangle to the next tip.
