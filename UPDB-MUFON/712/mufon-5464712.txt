My attention was drawn upward to a static object below cloud level

At 8:25 a.m. on Thursday 11th October 2018 I was travelling by Tram from home to work (towards Nottingham City Centre). The Tram had just left the tram stop at the Queens Medical Centre when my attention was drawn upward to a grey shape hovering below cloud level. The shape was static and had no exterior lights. It appeared oblong and box like in shape. Almost immediately upon noticing it, the object started to ascend. It appeared to fade out of existence well before it got close to the clouds above. I immediately had the very strong feeling that it had reacted to me seeing it. It felt like some kind of connection at been made and I knew deep down that what I had just seen was significant. The whole encounter lasted 2-3 seconds. It was very quick but I still remember it very clearly.
