{dr}Two day event with multiple objects

Me and my family were at home during the evening hours. My kids and wife were in the house and I stepped outside to have a cigarette. I was casually looking at the stars and I noticed a star like object moving through the night sky. My initial thought was it was maybe a satellite because I have observed one in the past. I quickly noticed that it was something different because it split into three different objects and they all went in different directions. One went north and another went east and the other object went south. They all moved in a straight line leaving no noticeable trail. I called my wife outside to see the objects so maybe she could come up with another explanation of what I was seeing. She also was baffled by the sighting. We proceeded to watch the objects until they were out of sight. Due to fear of being ridiculed we kept the sightings to ourselves. I work weird hours and had to work that night. Upon my return home the next day my wife called me into the garage to have another cigarette. She was excited to show me a picture she had taken earlier in the day. She was outside watching the thunderbirds practice for the upcoming air show. She noticed another object during the practice session. This object she explained was shiny and flying above the thunderbird aircraft. She happened to have her phone with her and snapped a picture. She said right after the picture was took the object disappeared. She stood outside for a few more minutes looking for the object but it never reappeard. I was not present during this sighting.
