2 glowing orbs moving together

Three of us witnessed two glowing orbs flying in the sky together staying pretty close to each other while sitting on our porch. Almost had a blueish glow to them. Definitely look like they are spinning when you zoom in. They were low then went higher. Up and then away and then back again. Then flew away staying almost same distance apart 
They were pretty low in sky. Staying pretty consistent distance from each other. Moving a little to the left then rose higher and then flew to the right towards the Susquehanna river together.
