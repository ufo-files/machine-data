It looked like a moving star, it made directional changes.

I was near my house at a forestroute, walking the dogs. I was looking up to the skies hoping to see something, and I got more than I had hoped for. At first it looked like a regular star, but then I saw it started moving. It was moving a lot faster than a satellite, to the left. Then it turned and flew down/left. I was in awe, almost paralyzed. It was so amazing. After flying there for about three minutes, the light faded away, and I didn't see it again.
