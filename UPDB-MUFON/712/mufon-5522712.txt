Bright White Light

Saw VERY bright white ball of light pass very fast in front of me while driving on 422 aroud the Shelocta area. Passed horizontally in front of me for maybe 4 seconds across the sky, then vanished as if the light had just dissapeared or shut off. Not a cloud in the sky to duck behind. NOT a shooting star, or a plane, helicopter, etc. Never have seen anything like this before.
