Undoubtedly a sighting of the TR-3B

What I saw was undoubtedly the TR-3B. The craft moved with absolutely no sound. It's speed was pretty standard like any commercial aircraft. When I first spotted it at 12am on May 19 2020, it was flying at a lower altitude possibly less than 1000 ft. The craft had light on the three corners of the triangle. The second time I spotted it, it was flying much higher at about 2000ft or so and moved silently at a consistent speed from East to West. The third time I spotted was a part of the second spotting except I ran from my balcony to the roof of my house.
