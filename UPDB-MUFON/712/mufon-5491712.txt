Late night I cracked my window to let in some fresh air, I suddenly felt a feeling of warmth in my stomach. I look up and immediately noticed one star in the sky seemed brighter than usual, I watched it carefully until I was certain it was moving in the sky doing sudden stops, small waves at an angle, then back to sharp turns. I watched it until the sun rose and the u.f.o hid behind the mountains. Every night since then I look outside and see the from 9 till 2 am, they return but never land and always seem to be in the same place. They could sense my fear the first night and froze still, I could feel that they ment no harm to me and continued to move strangely in the sky.
