A few disks of bright white light emanating from the bottom of the craft, with red exterior lighting outlining its form flew from the SE to the NW.

After hearing my neighbors lighting off fireworks, I went outside to sit on my porch to watch the firework display, but unfortunately I missed pretty much all of it as my neighbors lit the last one as I came outside. My neighbors returned back inside their home, and I continued enjoying the night’s sky. I may have missed the firework show, but I was definitely treated to another show in the sky. I witnessed two, possibly three disk-shaped UFOs; the closest one that I caught on video coming from the SE, flying in an erratic path going left & right and even up & down and made their way to the NW. The disks emanated white light from the bottom of the craft and smaller, red exterior lighting outlining its disk-like form and one of them also dawning an extremely bright green spotlight downwards for a short moment. All of the lighting from the crafts blinked rapidly as you will see in the video attachment. Although the video doesn’t have the best quality, I would like to point out that the outer red lights aren’t captured in video format which is odd considering how bright they were. To confirm that I was actually seeing UAPs and not some fireworks or drones, I called my fiancé outside to catch the last glimpses, and with his military experience he could determine that what we were seeing couldn’t be identified as a plane, helicopter, satellite, drone, or any other man-made flying object that he was aware of. I also compared videos of the exterior lighting to that of man-made crafts and couldn’t find any similarities or comparisons to what we witnessed.
