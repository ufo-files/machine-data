bright light caught my eye thought it was a plane coming in for landing but it never got closer or further

It was October 29 2014 @ approximately 2:10 am I was driving home from working afternoon shift and my eye caught a bright light off in the south east sky. I first thought it was a plane coming in for a landing I decided to keep an eye on it to see what kind of plane it would be but the light never got any closer or further away but continued to flicker and pulse from white to red to blue to yellow to green then repeat I wasn't sure what I was looking at so I decided to pull over just before my house and get out of my truck with my I phone 5s and video tape what I was seeing I kept the light in the middle of my phone screen while I was videoing the light its all I had to record with. After I got home I just sat there in my truck looking up into the sky wondering if what I saw was alien life form looking down on us. I looked back in the area it was in and it was gone . I,m still in awe over this and watch my video regularly
