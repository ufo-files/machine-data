A pair of lights, yellow on one end and red on the other. Cigar shaped. Very high

Lying out by my swimming pool in a pool lounge. Was holding a pair of binoculars 10x50 power with a zoom feature. Noticed the lights moving westerly to easterly. Red and yellow lights got my attention. I knew aircraft don't have yellow, only red, white & green. Object was flying very high and date was earlier, like about 1967, but only approx. due to time.
