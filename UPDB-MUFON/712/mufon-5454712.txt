{cak} Light Waves (over family of geese, then a blink, the family was gone {djs})

On July 14, 2018 field studies were being performed in Riverside Park, Grand Rapids, MI. It was a perfect sunny summer day. Heading south on the pedestrian walk a Canadian Geese family was spotted in the lagoon just shy of the covered picnic area. An initial count of 2 parents and approximately 8 goslings was done. Since they were closer to the northern island a second count using binoculars was considered. Looking down to pick up the binoculars then looking back up, a movement that took approximately 3 seconds, a large patch of yellow and white lights at a 45 degree angle had appeared over the family of geese. Watched in disbelief; then a blink. The family was gone. The lights lasted approximately 5 seconds. Looked around to see if anyone else was observing the phenomenon. No, everyone was going about their normal park activity.
