{kt}Orange objects then star like objects in the same night. Calabash N.C.

I and Three others at Calabash N.C.,saw two orange lights light up individually then go off. Then three orange lights that did the same. We then counted nine star like objects fly around like helicopters but the blinking was irregular. They would appear to change direction very quickly or stop blinking and reappear very far away from where they last blinked. Then form up to move SW toward ASH. Lost sight of them after they went over the tree line. I hope some one caught it on video.
