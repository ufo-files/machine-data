{jpg} 4-5 star like lights, circling and moving east

At approximately 9:45 pm while walking my dog I remembered that the space station was supposed to be visible in the coming days. I looked to the sky to see if I could observe it and saw a strange phenomenon instead. Just above the neighboring building at what appeared to be a similar height as passing planes I saw 4-5 star like lights. They were moving at differing speeds, and flashing at the same intervals, but at different times. They all circled around each other and remained in this grouping as the gradually moved east. I was able to take a brief video on Snapchat before my dog pulled me away, as I looked down the lights vanished all together in a blink.
