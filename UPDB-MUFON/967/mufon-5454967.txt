{tjd} Straight Line Orbs Flight Path

I saw a white glowing orb coming from the western front range heading east. The object had no blinking lights and made no sound whatsoever. It started off very bright over towards the mountains but as it got overhead it started to disappear because it was on an upward trajectory. Here's where it gets very interesting. I slowly tracked back towards the mountain with my binoculars and at intervals of what appeared to me to be about one foot apart from my perspective on the ground were more of the orbs in a single line following one another. I counted a total of 30 glowing orbs. I've never seen anything like this in my life before. It shook me a bit...
