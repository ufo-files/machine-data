{cak} Object flashing red, green , and blue lights seen in the Western sky at around 10:00 pm on April 16, 2016

Well, about 9:30 pm on April 16, 2016, I went out to get something from my car and I started crossing Rt 11 which intersects my property , and I saw a small flash in the sky that was red. I went to my house and got a pair of binoculars and went back outside and crossed the street to the driveway of my garage and faced to the West- I used my phone app and saw that the object was approximately 250 degrees West in the sky. Once I located it with my binoculars I could see that it was flashing red, blue, and green lights inside an area of white light. It basically hovered- I saw a red light from a plane pass nearby and it helped to show that the object must have been large but how large I don't know. Decided to go and get my son's telescope and after having a little difficulty locating it, I had a good view of a sphere shaped object that was flashing red , blue , and green on the inside. I watched it for about an hour and then it vanished from my sight about 11:00pm. I took a picture and a video with my iPhone, but they didn't show what I actually saw.
