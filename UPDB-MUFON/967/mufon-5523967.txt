Star-like object moved steadily, stopped, then it and 2 other star-like objects sped off in diffrent directions at a very high rate of speed.

Star-gazing at the hunting camp that night my girlfriend and I observed a bright white dot moving steadily from west to east from over Lake Superior. As we watched the object for a couple minutes it came to an abrupt halt, then a couple seconds later three bright white dots which blended in well with the overhead stars took off in different directions in the blink of an eye. I turned to her and asked, "Did we just see that?" She agreed we saw something unexplainable.
