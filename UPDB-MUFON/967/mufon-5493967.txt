{cak} three seperate lights coming together in triangle then leaving seperatly

On 9/11/2011 a friend and I were hunting on property that borders VAFB. We were in the field at 3am trying to pattern the hogs. As I was scanning around I saw lights glowing from behind a hill. I thought it was a vehicle coming my way but something didn't seem right. When the lights came into view there was one and then one slightly behind it to the left. A couple seconds later as they moved closer another came in from the right and they formed a triangle. As it moved closer a cone shaped beam moved back and forth coming from the middle one. suddenly the one in the middle came quickly towards me and stopped which really freaked me out. It began to change colors white red and greenish blue, and almost seemed like I could reach out and touch it. When I looked back for the other two lights, there was only one. Then it amazingly fast streaked out of sight to the right. The one that came towards me occasionally moved a little left or right or up or down until sunrise then I couldn't see it.
