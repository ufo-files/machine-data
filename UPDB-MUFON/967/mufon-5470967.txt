{cak} Strange Jack Frost configuration on windshield of car January 11, 2018

Leaving the house after a minimal, overnight snow dusting, to pick up the paper, the windshield, in early morning light revealed this most amazing Jack Frost event. We have seen ice formations before, but this appeared to be in Chinese! I have no idea what physics could have formed this pre-dawn pattern. Have you ever seen anything like this? My husband got his camera immediately before the sun touched it. Friends in Santa Fe encouraged me to send it to MUFON because we have also been witnessing some amazing ORB formations recently outside of town. I will post those to you on another entry. They also felt this might be some kind of "message" but I would love to know how this came to be.
