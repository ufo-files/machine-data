Fast “blip” of light with no arch headed south for a split second, then vanished

My partner and I both said “did you see that?” At the same time while driving. It was directly above us, so the top of the windshield almost cut it off. It was a fast ball of light like a shooting star, except I noted there was no downward curvature and it was very short. It appeared out of nowhere then projectiled/darted quickly south for say 3 seconds and vanished like a “blip”. That’s the only way I can describe it. It Didn’t dissolve like other falling stars. It was very fast and shotty like a UFO craft. I checked the time in the second because I wanted to send to Mufon and see if others saw it and it was exactly 3:33am.
