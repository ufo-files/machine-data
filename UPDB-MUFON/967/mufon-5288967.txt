Huge light hovering then dissipating with direction change

I was traveling with my family on the cascade wonderland hey, today , March 25,2021. It was around 8.58pm and while observing my surroundings, the Mountain around a mile from my passenger side, I saw what I thought a small flashlight moving amongst trees near a mountain summit. Within 9sec that light started pulsating and becoming huge, illuminating the base of 2 to 3 trees on the snow covered summit. What was surprising was that it had a tail of light coming out of it as it started changing shape from a orb to a medium sized oval bullet.. it rose up , hovering over the trees, no sound , went around the mountain top full circle.. and then started flying towards the clouds.. in the process it broke into 4 flame like orbs following each other and we all saw 2 huge flashing red dots.. abt 60 seconds into this, only one light was left and it slowing dimmed and went behind the cloud. All this time we were on the shoulder of the road and recording whatever we could. It seemed as if they were searching for something on that mountain top.please help us understand more.. thank you
