row of white lights

At 7:30pm eastern time my wife and I looked down street west in the sky a row of white lights evenly spaced almost looked liked stars but they were a dozen or so lights in a perfect straight line on an angle to the right in the night sky. Looked to be moving past a star so we could tell it was moving in a spinning motion silently. It was miles away but very large at least two football fields.Went to the neighbors and showed them ,they also were seeing something they have never seen before we went back out 10 mins later and it was gone.
