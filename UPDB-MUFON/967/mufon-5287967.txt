Slowly rotating object seen over Montreal

As our family was walking to the park near our apartment, we stopped on the sidewalk to have the kids look at the nice quarter moon which was highly visible in the clear lit sky tonight. We still had some daylight and the moon was out at 8pm.

As they were looking at the moon i noticed a far up object off the side. There was no noise, no trails, nothing to signify being a plane. I continued watching thinking it was a high bird but it was moving quick and high enough just to distinguish it's shape. It was neither triangle nor rectangle. Odd edges and angles seemed to be it's features. Only noticeable because it was rotating slowly while moving in a single direction. There was no lights. Nothing shiny. Just a dull rotating object and the only time it caught reflective light was when one of it's bizarre edges slightly reflected the setting sun.

I tried to get a picture but my phone was out of space. As i looked up to see if it would be there long enough to make room in my phone it had dissappeared completely. Neither my wife nor I saw how fast it got away, what direction or anything else. We looked away for one second and could not find it in the sky again. We cannot think of any explanation and thought it would be best to simply report and collaborate/confirm with other potential witnesses who may have more evidence.
