Fast Moving Star?

I witnessed what looked like a Star or a Satellite. It let of an intense light then started moving quickly along a flight path and eventually faded in the horizon as it moved further away from me.

I used Siri to find out what flights were overhead, but nothing came up, which was odd because I live under a flight path.

This was so high up it looked like a star but moved like a ship. It didn't flash and burn like a meteor.

I've never seen anything like it before.
