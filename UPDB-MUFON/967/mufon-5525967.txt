Disc shaped ufo passing at night

I went to my backyard for a smoke, I always look at the skys in the night.
For the last 5 months i have seen strange lights traveling really fast.
But this time it was fading it's luminocity, from mid brightness to full. That got my attention, First I thought it was an airplane (I live close to an airport, so i see a lot of airplanes), but when i stared at it it was doing that strange fading of light, then it just faded in the dark (not by distance... it was like it dissapeard).
I was really scared, I did yell to the rest of my family to come and see but they where inside the house and couldn't listen to my call.
The object was moving from south to north.
