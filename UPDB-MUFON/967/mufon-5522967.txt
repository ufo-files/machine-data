Light shot across the sky and shot into 3 directions

I was having a smoke outside my wife was out with me. We were looking at the stars when we saw an extremely bright light shoot across the sky and stop dead in its tracks. Then it appeared to split into3 sections and each section shot a different direction. About 20 second later 3 military looking planes came from the same direction the light did and they split up and followed there lights. The most crazy thing I've seen worth a witness.
