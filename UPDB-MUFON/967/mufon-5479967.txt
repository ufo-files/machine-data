Object 3 Simply looks like came from behind the trees and changed direction and rolled past my drone

Those objects were recorded with my Phantom3P drone while recording fall colors, my plan was to fly above the trees and record the colors of leaves and compose a nice fall event video..
When I examined the video at home I noticed all those objects coming from one location and it looks like from the trees , I examined the footage and enlarged it and also slowed it down and to my surprise those were white spheres and also this one object that looks like a disk shape and it appears dark in color, this object zoomed past my drone to the left of it and vanished... it almost looked to me like the reported orbs or rods..
Those objects were moving at incredible speed as my camera was recording at 60 frames per second and all you can see is blur when you pause a frame, except for the one larger object.
When I noticed the object I originally thought they were birds, but I could see birds flying below and they look completely different.
I never physically seen those objects as I was operating my drone about 500 meters away looking at my device control screen.
This is by far the weirdest thing I have ever recorded , I have over 100 drone flight and I am very detail oriented and I have never seen this in any other videos that I recorded.
