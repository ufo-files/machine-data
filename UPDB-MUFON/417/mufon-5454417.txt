{rjl} 30 objects traveling in straight-line path with a few in formation

On Wednesday, March 25, 2020 my husband and I were heading out to our car in Colorado Springs at 8:40 PM and I saw a bright object in the sky. Initially I thought it might be the International Space Station, and to double check that I took out the Sky Guide app on my iPhone. This confirmed that this was NOT the space station. My husband was there with me and started saying, "there's another one...and another one". Then we started counting. In total we saw 30 objects that looked like small magnitude stars. Most of them stayed the same brightness but a couple of them flared up or brightened for about 2-3 seconds. The objects came up from the southwest and headed northeast across the sky. The most stunning thing was that a few of objects were very close together in a formation. I was able to take three (3) digital photos with my iPhone and one (1) video. The entire event lasted for 15-20 minutes. When reviewing the photos and video, the objects appeared blue. To the naked eye however, the objects were white light.
