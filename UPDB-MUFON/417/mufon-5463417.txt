rocket, pointed both ends, three fins no sign of propulsion

Around about 1950 I came across two non-fiction books from the Sunderland library in Durham, England, I was sixteen at the time. The first book was called " A Short History of the Royal Navy " it described a sailing ship becalmed in the south Atlantic and picking up a (rocket shaped objected) my words! from the sea. The details were entered in the plain white logbook. The object was shown standing on three fins, very fat in the belly and pointed at both ends. no signs of propulsion and no portholes, the only word I recall is pewter. the research I have done suggest in was designed for one man. Unfortunately the ship ran into a storm it had to be dumped over the side. The ship docked in Bristol. If I hear back from you I will describe the second book and it will take us to the Himalayas.
