I was a trucker,loaded in Nogales,Az.at 2:39p their time for Houston,Tx.,while taking a short cut down HWY-82 no more than 45mins after loading I became real sleepy so I pull over in a little pull out and lay my head down.The next thing I remember was I was on the side of the road on Hwy-90 west of San Antonio, Tx. It was 10:pm. I do not know how I went 950 miles in 6 1/2 hours. This was in 1980, my bills were time stamp an and with other paperwork. My Mother lives in SATX and I call her to meet me at the coffee shop, she laughed at me till I show her my bills with the time stamp. This is when the speed limit was 55mph.i never took Hwy 90, always went I-10. AFTER YEARS of this and having people laugh about it, it bother me now. I do remember being inside,and they are not little grays.
