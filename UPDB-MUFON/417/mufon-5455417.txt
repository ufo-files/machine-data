{jpg} Hourglass-like object hovering motionlessly

Walking along East River betw. Houston St and Williamsburg bridge I saw a small hourglass - like shaped object of dull grey color hovering motionlessly above the river. Clear sky, no wind. Guesstimated altitude - 1000 ft. Did not move from its position by the time I walked away where I could not see it anymore. Did not have a camera with me. Because of this event I started carrying small camera with zoom with me at all times. No odd sightings since.
