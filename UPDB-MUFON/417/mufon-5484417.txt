{rjl} taking pics of the moon and saw object as a star. then it moved from the 5 o'clock position to 6 o'clock while other star stayed in position

I was sitting in my truck listening to LSU tigers playing baseball. It was a cool spring like night.. I got out of the truck to gaze upon the night sky and the stars. The moon was pretty bright sitting over head, so I decided to take a couple of pics of the moon and the star to the right of the moon. I didn't have my glasses on at the time I snapped the pics. After I zoomed in close enough to get the moon and star on my phone.. snapping several pics at once. The next day I reviewed the pictures I had taken the night before.. and was checking out the different pics.. when I saw the object around the 5 o'clock position. I zoomed closer to see what a star might look like on my phone.. and compared it to the next picture in the sequence when I noticed my star had changed positions from the 5 o'clock position to the 6 o'clock position. The star to the far right stayed pretty much in the same position.. but the one under the moon moved. This might be the ISS? I don't know.. that's why I'm sending the 2 pics to you guys.
