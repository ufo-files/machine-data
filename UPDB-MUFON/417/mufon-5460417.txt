{jpg} Circular object appeared with bright lights over cow pasture

Approximately 10 years ago in the winter time, I observed from my bedroom window a round/circular object that appeared behind the house I was living in at the time. The object was large and appeared to be an aircraft of some kind. It hovered into my field of vision over a cow pasture behind the house and my view was obstructed somewhat by trees, however, I was able to make out that it was a round aircraft with bright lights beaming from the sides and bottom. At first, I thought it was a helicopter but I would have been able to hear it due to how close it was. It stayed in position and hovered for roughly 5-10 minutes and then flew away.
