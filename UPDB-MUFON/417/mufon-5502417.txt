{rjl} What I thought was a very fast plane just stopped.

My wife and I were outside smoking at my sisters house out back after watching a movie.

I had noticed what I thought was a very fast plane just flying from east to west. I mean it was moving fast, very fast. I pointed out to my wife "Check out that plane hauling @55". She asked jokingly "Do you think it's a UFO?" I said "no, it's moving in a straight line like a plane" and as I finished saying that, like it heard me, it just stopped and froze there in the sky. We just stood there stunned for about 15 seconds staring at it. Then it did a rather large Z shaped zigzag, backwards from where it came, and shot out like a shooting star towards the southwest.

We were just stunned. No explanation what we saw.
