(GS) Bad lightning storm when many came flying in different directions close together

I was out in the middle of a lightning storm taking photos and I seen what looked to be a shooting star but there there were more coming. They were coming down almost then they would turn off the light and then turn it on again and take off so fast, I couldnt see them.

I was scared but confused and I wanted to take photos of it. I only have one photo that you can barely see the round light in.
I lost sight of it because of the lightning and it would fly so fast with the lights off. 

It DEFINITELY was NOT a helicopter, airplane, or anything like that because the lighting is HORRIBLE tonight. It is a thunderstorm with rain and very bad lightning.
