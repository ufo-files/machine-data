{nd} It was white, hundreds oval side by side windows on the bottom, no tail wings and I couldn't see both ends at the same time.

I was fishing in a pond on a beautiful summer day, when I saw this huge long thing slowly gliding through white puffy clouds. There was no sound, and it went on forever. It was larger round than a commercial airliner, and the amount of Windows on the bottom really got my attention. I thought I was seeing things at first, but the my angle was almost directly underneath it. When the end section became clear between the clouds, I knew it was not a prototype or anything from here. The end just tapered of without any tail wings or anything. It was as silent as night. I wasn't close to any highways or roads. If not for the reflection in the pond I would never had noticed it. It has bothered since that day over 30 years ago. When I tell someone what I experienced, they either laugh or tell me it was just a jet or I'm crazy. I started to doubt myself for a while, but I can't deny what saw. It was massive in leghth, no wings, hundreds of oval Windows on the belly and silent. You tell me.
