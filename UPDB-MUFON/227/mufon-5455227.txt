{rjl} 6 blue lights move horizontaly

While working on the railroad connecting railroad cars together west of Marion, Indiana I witnessed 6 blue lights moving horizontaly,. Then, the leading 3 blue lights spin and shoot off upwards and disappear. Next, the trailing 3 blue lights spin and shoot off upwards and disappear. On the radio, I asked if the locomotive engineer had seen what I saw. He replied no. I told some close friends about this but eventually quit sharing my sighting. Now, I share this sighting with my family , and I am happy to have had this sighting and wish I could see more. My father believed in aliens and so do I.
