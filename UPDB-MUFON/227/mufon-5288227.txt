5-6 red orange circle shaped lights in my neighborhood growing up

My dad and I were coming home from Awana Wednesday night and all of a sudden we see 5-6 red orange circle lights above us, right as soon as we got to our driveway. But dad continues to follow the lights but they kept going faster away from us but at the same altitude. We follow them for just a few blocks. But they were moving too fast and all of a sudden they were gone. My dad does not remember this event though. I was 9 and my dad was in his mid - late 30's. This was in Port Charlotte FL near Midway Blvd and Hayworth rd. The UFO flew north over the middle school and disappeared.
