First one bright light moving steadily across the sky, then noticed two more it appeared to be flying towards

This video was taken on Sydney's Northern Beaches, NSW on September 23rd, 2021, between 10.36am and 10.40am. Direction was approximately South to South-East. It was a very clear, very sunny day. At first I only saw the very bright object above the tree, but then I realised there were two others above the unit block, which I've left visible for the sake of scale. Sorry for the shaky iPhone camera footage.

I see planes go over all the time, these didn't look like that. I considered balloons and drones, but they were very bright, and seemed too high up. One other note; in the group of two objects, the one on the right looked cigar shaped to me, whereas the other two looked circular. The video doesn't really capture this.

As much as I'd love to think ET was out for a drive on a sunny day, I draw no conclusions about these, but would love to hear what people think they could be, or if anyone else saw them!
