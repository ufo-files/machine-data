While driving east toward Taylors Falls, saw a reddish-orange ball of light. Later a second one appeared.

While driving home, heading east toward Taylors Falls, I saw a reddish-orange ball of light. When I arrived near home on highway Co. Rd. 14, I turned the corner and stopped half a block from the highway, got out of the truck, called my wife on the cell phone to come look at it. She came to the front door and looked at it, then took a photo, then she walked to the truck. Would guess the light was about 1000 ft up and 1500 ft away, about the size of the full moon.. We watched for a bit and my wife took another photo, then a yellow object came out of the northeast sky and stopped under the first object. It was the same size as first object and the same brightness, like a twin. They hovered for a minute. The first one took off and moved off to the NE and disappeared in a split second. After about a minute, the second light did the same thing and disappeared in the distance..
