{rjl} Star like orb moving north followed by helicopter.

Was in the back yard with the dogs, i noticed a moving light, was thinking it was a plane, but the lights did not blink, i keep looking and can best describe it as a white orb, looked like a bright star to the east. Over mission espada area, was moving north and pulse brighter. Started to hear a helicopter, as the orb moved past my sight, the chopper came into view with the normal red plain type lights. The orb was moving to fast for the chopper to catch up to. I lost both objects when the went behind the roof line of the neighbors house. There was no sound from the orb. Should also say that i could make out the shape of the chopper but the orb was like a bright star. I see many things on the south side, much less light pollution here, i enjoy looking at stars and keep an eye to the sky.
