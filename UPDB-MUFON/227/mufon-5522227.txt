a brilliant white light, teardrop shaped

On Dec. 4, 2011

 I was on my way to the local store around 10:30 p.m. to get milk and cigarettes. As I came upon the intersection, I happened to glance over to the right, and see this bright white light moving fast across the sky at a 45 degree angle( at first I thought it was a falling star) then it stopped, hovered for a second, and then drop down into the trees out of sight. I froze for a few seconds, tried to put it together and then came home shaken my head like was that real...but I know what I saw!!
