{tjd} Seen covered ufo on freeway,escorted by state troopers

Driving southbound on I-75 I first seen a few state troopers ahead with their lights on escorting a flatbed truck with a oversized load taking up a lane and a half of the freeway, when getting close and riding past the flatbed was hauling a large saucer shaped object. Although it was covered with black plastic and I could see a blue tarp under that also, it was obviously a ufo. I can’t think of anything else this could of possibly been, I’ve seen many oversized loads on flatbeds but never escorted with several state troopers. And further down the freeway closer to the Ohio/Kentucky border most of the exits were blocked of by local cops. I’m kicking myself for not taking a picture or video but I was driving at the time and my passengers were sleeping.
