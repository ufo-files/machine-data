{rjl} Starlike with occasional flashes

My dog was acting restless so I got up to let her out at 12:15 am. When we got to the patio door I saw how brilliant the moonlight was so did not put on the outside lights. I went out also to enjoy the night and noticed what I thought was an airplane (it was in the general direction of the DFW airport) or bright star. But it was not moving and I noticed that I couldn’t see another single star in the sky. There were broken clouds that I should have been able to see stars between. I decided to go get my phone and take video if it was still there when I returned. It was not. I began to video at the place where I had seen it before anyway and after almost a minute it did reappear. I videoed the light for about another minute until it just faded away. Afterward, I was able to see other stars and photographed what I think was a planet low on the horizon.
