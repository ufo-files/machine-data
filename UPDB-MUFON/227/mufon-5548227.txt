bright flash, acceleration of a vessel that was lit up by the flash, fire trail/streak

i was chatting with a friend who was driving the opposite direction of me in our parking lot at the apartments. stopped for a few minutes to talk thru our respective drivers' windows. bright flash appeared in the sky above his car and in the distance by about a mile or so. lasted only for a brief instant. seemed like a trail of fire that was a perfectly straight streak in the sky... too fast to make out a shape of the vehicle, but definitely did not appear to be a shooting star. seemed as though the flash of fire boosted the speed of the craft. since both of our car motors were running, no sound was heard, if there was one.
