Very luminous green ball emerging from field then flashed then moved right in front of my house at about 40 feet away there was some sort of white pulsating lights when it stopped in front on m house it flashed again very bright

Monday night at about 9:55pm (10/08/2015), I went outside on my porch to smoke a cigarette and look at the sky and then I saw a very bright lime green ball emerge from the field across the road from my place at about an average of 75 feet from me...and a big flash occured and then it started to move ; I could see like pulsating white and green lights while it was hovering in the field beside the ditch near the road and then it stopped again right in front of my house in the field at about 40 feet away from my porch and another very bright flash occured. I was so excited that I ran inside the house to go get my spouse so he could see this, but when we went back outside it was gone. I told my spouse: We will see the state of the field in the morning and we saw circles and many patches where the barley was laid down in many places in the field and I'm not sure, but in some places it seemed burned. I took pictures of all these traces.
