I went outside to my backyard to throw away the garbage around 9:30pm. I suddenly saw a bright light out of the corner of my eye...at first I didn't think anything about it and was heading back to my home until I glanced in its direction. Coming from across the street in-between two of my neighbors homes and behind trees I see a bright light. I immediately called my mom.
She took witnessed this as the object changed direction and began heading North. While observing I noticed that the object was on its side and had a beam coming from the underside; the light was steady, but seemed to be scanning the ground. As if looking for something. We watched this craft for about 15min as it quietly vanished behind trees and buildings.
I immediately contacted the NC FAA, no word back.
