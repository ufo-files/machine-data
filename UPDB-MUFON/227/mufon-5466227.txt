Whilst stargazing, three 'stars' in triangle formation moved R-L apparent distance = length of outstretched horizontal finger and stopped.

Stargazing (naked eyes only) with my adult friend and daughter, we saw several lights moving in straight trajectories at a consistent pace across the sky that I assumed to be satellites but then I saw three what I thought were stars, in a triangle, move together in a straight line from my right to left almost immediately above me. The distance it moved was equal to viewing my finger's length held at arm's length.

There were no other lights visible in the middle of this triangle of 'stars', as if it were a solid, utterly black triangular object with a light at each corner. A moving light I assumed to be a satellite, moved across this triangular area as if underneath this unidentified object.

The angles were first like a scalene triangle then when it stopped, an isosceles triangle, as if it had tilted to be parallel with the ground.

I watched it for 15 minutes and it did not move again. I felt almost as if it knew I was looking at it. My friend and child did not see it move and were not sure if they saw it too.

I've never seen a UFO before and always assumed they'd be oval or rou and immediately looked up 'black triangle UFO' afterwards which I was very surprised to find.

The lights were steady. It was the way all three moved as one that shocked me, otherwise I would have easily mistaken them for stars.
