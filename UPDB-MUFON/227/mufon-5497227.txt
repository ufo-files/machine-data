It was a VERY LARGE green light with a smoke trail behind

This event was seen by three persons. I was the principal, who called out the strange light on the north coast of the Dominican Republic, a couple of hours ago. It seemed like a very large fireball, green, which fizzled and disappeared. No, it was not fireworks. Fireworks dont travel horizontally, nor are they so large. Besides, they are prohibited in this country. It should have been the size of an airplane and must have been at least three kilometers away. It is a clear night tonight. It was very strange, no noise and so large and bright green...There is an airport nearby, and no, it was not an airplane.
