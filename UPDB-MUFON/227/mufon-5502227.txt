{dr}Yellow-Orange ball of light silently flying NE Direction

On June 2, 2014, at approximately 21:30hrs In the City of Oneida, I was outside on my back deck smoking a cigarette. I looked up at the sky as I always do, and noticed a bright yellowish- orange orb flying in a North Eastern direction. I estimated the orbs height to be 200-300ft above the trees in my area (which are approx 75-100ft high). The orb continued in a straight line, and did not move up or down or side to side I used my video recorder on my Iphone, and I was able to take 2 short videos of the orb, before it was out of view. During the time that I was observing the orb, the sky was blanketed with clouds with a slight wind, but no stars were visible. This orb was the only bright object in the sky. The orb did not make any noise and was a solid steady color, however after I reviewed the video, I was able to see the orb, but the orb does appear to flicker in the video, and I am not sure if my Iphone was just having a difficult time focusing in on the orb.
