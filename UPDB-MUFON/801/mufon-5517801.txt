{nd} Four Orange Lights Over LaPush, Washington

My sister and I were sitting on the beach around 11:00 PM PDT at First Beeach near LaPush, Washington. We were facing the western sky. To the south along the shore there appeared an orange dot the size of an aspirin flying at the altitude that an airliner would fly. It flew faster than a plane. Then from the north two more orange dots flew southward -- again flying faster than a plane. Then a fourth orange dot came from the south along the shore. The four dots appeared to meet directly to the west of the beach, and then fly westward over the Pacific. They disappeared before flying far enogh to be out of sight. All four were the size of an aspirin, orange, and too large and fast to be airplanes
