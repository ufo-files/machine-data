My father (name withheld) was a [personal info removed/CMS/dr] when he died. One night ~ 3 am, he and another man were on a launch pad at Thiokol. They were surrounded by orbs of lights. I was told by my father's best friend at that time, it was my father who snapped the pics. The next morning they both were flown to DC to be debriefed. It looked like an ordinary rock or stone; however, it was way different. It could be transformed into a rope of metallic twine. Probably about ~12 inches. I held that stone for many years. My daughter remembers the rock. But, it is now gone.
