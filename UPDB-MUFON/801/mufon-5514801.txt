(GS) Heard noise strong wind forced blinds in

It was 0405 in the AM of 12 Oct 2012. I was in bed but had not fallen asleep yet this I am 100% positive. I was doing routine matinence on my PC when I heard what at first sounded like a car coming down the street from far away. The sound began to increase to a more high pitched hum. I am prior military and am familiar with most aircrafts we have in our military...this sounded nothing like any aircraft I had ever heard. It seemed to come from the West going SE at perhaps 30-40 mph. I never aw the craft however it produce a wind of about 50 mph that cause the house to make cracking noises and blew the blinds in as if it was a hurricane type wind. My dog was laying on the bed and she immediately went under it...SHE HAS NEVER DONE THAT IN HER LIFE.
I tried to look out as it passed directly over my house but I seen nothing. I could hear the craft as it got further away and finally out of ear shot...The whole thing start to finish was less than one minute.
I called the local police station this afternoon (10-19-12) and asked if there was any other reports. I was told no but if it happened again to call them...ummm ok.
That is all I can report. I have never filed a report before and am of sound mind.
