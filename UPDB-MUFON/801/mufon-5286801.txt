black shadow like entity or object flew in arc across sky with 2 direction changes

I go outside on my back deck almost every night to look at the sky and clear my head, meditate, that sort of thing. I turn off all the lights in and outside the house in order to see the sky better. As I was going out, I saw a flash of light, but I couldn't see the sky at that point because the top deck has a roof. I thought rain was on the weather forecast, so I assumed it was lightning and looked at the sky to see if we were going to have a thunderstorm, but it wasn't too cloudy at that point, although there were some present. (I could still see some stars). There was never any thunder. So, I assume it was heat lightning. I probably saw about 3 or 4 planes/jets fly over (I think we live in a flight path). Then all of the sudden I saw a black shadow fly across the sky in an arc from an eastward direction to a westward direction. I thought, well maybe that was just a bug (although it looked awfully large and nowhere close to me.) I waited to see if I saw anything else, and it quickly flew back to where it came from. Then it quickly flew back westward. I waited to see if anything else would happen, but nothing did. I went inside after waiting a few minutes because the clouds were starting to roll in (although we didn't get rain overnight). I forgot about it as I was falling asleep, and I only remembered just now and considered that reporting it could be helpful in case someone else saw anything similar.
