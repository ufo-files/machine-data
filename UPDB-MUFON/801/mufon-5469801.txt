{cak} Late nite brother woke family up to see object in sky

Brother woke family up late one night so we could see object hovering and moving up and down out of clouds. The object was round and looked like ceiling roof vent. we watched it for a few minutes then it shot off at an angle and was gone, then a few seconds later helicopters flew in the direction the object went.
