My boyfriend and I were sitting on my front porch when we saw a bright red-orange light flash and then the neighborhood lights went out. We walked to the yard for a better view of what was going on and directly above us we saw a dark disk shaped object with red-orange lights around the edge that were going off and on in a circular motion.At that time it started to move southward, weaving back and forth until it disapeared in the distance.I was very excited, I had never seen anything like it before and didn't know what to think, then when my brother came home and asked if we had seen it I was more excited because we shared this encounter.I have had 2 other sightings since then and each one is scarier than the previous one I will post those also.
