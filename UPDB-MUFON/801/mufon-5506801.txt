{kt}Discovered a bright orb over ocean in three different pictures I took. In two pictures, the orb was a firey orange color, and in another picture it is white.

I was traveling along the west coast and camped overnight near Crescent City, California. When the sun was about to set, I took a few pictures of the coast and ocean with my cellphone. I did not notice the orb when I was taking the pictures. When I looked at my pictures later, the orb was clearly visible in different parts of the pictures and were a firey orange in two pictures and white in one picture.
