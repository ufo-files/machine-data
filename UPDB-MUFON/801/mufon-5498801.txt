{cak} Single point of light moving in sky West to East Ddissappearing behind thin string of clouds

About 8:30 p.m. on Oct 4, 2014 in O'Fallon, IL. I was smoking out on my back porch of my apartment. It was a clear sky with few strings of clouds. The stars were clearly visible. To the Southwest I noticed a point of light miles away in the sky. It was moving to the East. At first I thought it to be a plane. But I noticed the light emitted, never flashed or blinked like on an airplane. It was just a steady white point of light. It didn't move particularly fast. But was odd that I saw no marker lights or beacon lights flashing. I watched the point of light move Easterly as it went past a thin line of clouds. After a waiting approximately 2 mins I saw no trace of the point of light. This is the second time I've noticed similar things in the past couple of weeks. I'm definitely going to keep watching the skies.
