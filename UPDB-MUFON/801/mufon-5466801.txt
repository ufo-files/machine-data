hunnting pigs with 2 friends west off coonabarabran nsw driving into the property 3 green lights shot strait up off the grouynd at ligh speed about 40m from us we hunted for 2 hours when about to leave we seen 3 lights abou150m away going above and then below large gum trees on the river.we stop and watched at one point a green light came bounceing across the field strait at us and vanished 40m from us.this spooked us and gave us the tip that its time to go .we move off in the car got 50 m away and our dogs jumped off the back .thay are trained to do this wwhen they smell pig scent .we stop the car thinking thay were on to pigs i got out and 2 red lights shot in and stopped dead 20m above use.i jumped back in the car and said go go there right on top off us.we tried to drive off but the engine was pulsing and lossing power.the pulsing became intense and was affecting us and the engine it only lasted a minute or so .a minute may not sound that long but it was unbelievable intense and very scary.the pulsing slowly backed off and the engine came back to life without having to turn the key. we took off about 5km down the road we stopped because we had gone without our dogs.we returned to the area and the dogs where standing huddled in the middle off the road. we commanded them to jump on but stay just stood there looking freaked out.i had to get out and lift them on by hand.this event scared the life out off us and our dogs and still makes my hair stand on end to this day it changed my life.contact me if you would like more detail
