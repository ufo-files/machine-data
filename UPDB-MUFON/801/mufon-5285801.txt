A famous speaker was a guest at our place when we were awakened by blinding lights coming in the windows

A famous UFO abductee spoke at our event center the night prior for Missouri MUFON, and was staying in our guest B&B in the house. At approximately 3:00 am the next morning I was awakened by a blinding blue/white light coming in the windows of my sun room on the south side of our house where I was sleeping. I was scared and woke up my husband and we both crouched down to look outside the windows to see what it was. All I remember is that I was frightened and that the light was so blinding that I could only make out that there were two huge lights (like searchlights) in the street about 30' off the ground, and that they lit up the entire yard and inside the house. What is strange is that we both just went back to sleep instead of getting up to go look outside to see what was going on. The next morning I got up early only to find that the speaker and his girlfriend were long gone. We had planned on making them breakfast in the morning and they were going to stay for that. The next day, a very large old oak tree in our backyard came crashing down into the street. I don't know if it is related, but I was just thinking about this incident and how odd it was, and spoke to my sister about this. She asked me to file a report to MUFON. Until now I thought it was the electric company with big lights for some reason, but now I'm not so sure. I think whatever it was it was there for the speaker.
