UFO Sighting on Hwy #655 near Driftwood, Northern Ontario Canada

I was hauling a transport load of wood chips to Smooth Rock Falls on October 21st, 1999 with a clear star evening. I spotted a red dot in the sky that looked like a star and stared at it while driving until it appeared above the transport instantly in a second. I was shocked and become afraid, I started to pray to God for protection fearing the large sized UFO would beam me up in the ship. At this time, it was 7:51pm. The UFO was approximately 200-300 feet above the transport and was hovering without sounds but many coloured lights surrounding the ship were bright and colourful, pulsing. As I kept on driving Northbound on Hwy #655, the UFO slowly started heading South thus loosing sight of the UFO and never seen it afterwards.
