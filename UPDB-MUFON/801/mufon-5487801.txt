{rjl} Observed Bright Silver Orb

I looked up and saw a very bright white object about 30 degrees above the horizon directly south. I thought it was a Border Patrol Helicopter, since they frequently fly in this area. I didn't see any tail section and it was a clear cloudless morning . I thought the sun was reflecting off the glass. As I watched, it looked like a big silver bubble. Then within seconds, it got smaller and smaller as it took off south  I would say that within 3 - 5 seconds it looked as big as a helicopter to a vanishing pinpoint of light.
