Glowing orange ball in the sky. Appeared flame-like.

I was driving home on 11/26/10 @ 2340 hours. When I turned northbound onto my street I saw what looked like a glowing orange ball of light in the sky. The Ball was moving North West slowly. It was Bright but not abrasive to look it. It some what had a flame-like quality on its upper half. I followed it for 1 to 2 minutes to keep it in my field of view. After about 2 min it began to pulse from bright to dim until it became a point of light and then it went completely dark. I watched the skies for about 10 minutes to see if it would return into my field of view. It did not. During and after I felt a strange calm. I had an oddly euphoric feeling about the object.
