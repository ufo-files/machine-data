We were having dinner with our friend Regius, and suddenly a strange entity appeared at the table. It was blinking, and extending something like an arm toward a sandwich. Our friend Peter was very upset, because it was his sandwich, and tried to struggle with the entity. However, Regio thowed an empty bottle of red wine, and the entity seemed to swallow it. At the end, a strange smell invaded all the house. We detected what seemed a kind of red jelly on the floor, and Regio tried to call the authorities, but the police did't show up (which is normal in our country). The next week, Regio tried to clean up the stain, but it was impossible, and the stain still remains there. The house is for rent, because Regio is very scared.
