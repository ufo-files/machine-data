Its the 4th of july and my friend and I went to BBQ at a friends in briggsdale. Around 8:00 is when we first seen it fell from the sky like a drop of rain fell down in a straight line then started to almost "dance like" above the ground and then continued in the sky all 3 of us seen it for about 20 to 30mins the longest I have ever seen one.
