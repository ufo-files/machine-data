Quiet UFO sighting

I had just got off work around 10:30pm-11:00pm and as I am getting to my car I noticed this triangular black looking looking craft approaching me. I thought it was some sort of drone but then I noticed it wasn't a drone and I looked and it was coming straight for me. I also noticed that there wasn't any sound coming from the craft and after 10 minutes of gliding it was directly over me. In the center of the craft there was a red light and as I am standing next to my car the red light was directly over me. After a while it begins to go east and it was just gliding and no sound was coming at all from the craft. I watched the craft disappear into the night sky about 20-30 minutes later and then it was completely gone.
