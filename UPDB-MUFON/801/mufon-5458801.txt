{cak} Boomerang shaped

At approx. 21:45 on 8/13 I went out on my patio with a pair of Gen 3, PVS-7 night vision goggles to look for anything left over from the meteor shower the night before. I had my goggles on for less than a minute and looked toward the northern sky and spotted a boomerang shaped object crossing the sky at a high rate of speed. It went from north to south, in a straight line and crossed the entire sky in 15 seconds or less. Unknown altitude, but appeared to be at least as high as most commercial airlines. No sound, no turns. I could clearly see the outline of a boomerang craft with multiple lights along the bottom. The lights did not blink or flash. I sat on the patio for approx. 30 minutes and never saw anything else.
