{rjl} over 47 lights in a uniform flight pattern. one after the other. Constant light w/o blinking lights.

On March 28th 2020, we were beginning to watch a movie outside with our projector and screen out front of the house. Upon glancing up to look at the twilight sky I noticed multiple lights in a straight line path. At first I though it was satellites but, Ive never seen satellites follow the exact same path let alone that many doing so. They just kept coming and coming. Over the last few weeks others that I'm friends with via Facebook have stated on their feed of seeing the same things unprovoked or approached by me or others to my knowledge. I called my parent who live approximately 75 miles away to go and see them as well. They were both astounded as we all gazed in amazement attempting to understand what we were staring at. With the sightings being so noticed I'm going to attempt to get better video tonight if they are present.
