2 slow moving lights and a 3rd appeared

I was dispatched to a house where the lady said the outside lights turned off. She was shaken and would not go outside to open toe locked gate to let me in. While on the phone I saw 2 light almost directly above me seemingly moving slow, or fast and really high up, but they seemed to be low. As they came into the clearing I saw a 3rd light that made it look like a triangle shape. I could not see anything between the lights as an object, but it was really dark out. The lights looked kinda like landing lights at first and I thought they were 2 planes when I fist saw them. There were no sounds at all and after I saw the 3rd light I watched as they slowly went past. All 3 lights faded off at the same time right above me. The object was either extremely big and really high or not high at all. It was hard judging because of how dark the night was even though it was a very clear night. I was on the phone with dispatch and said something to the fact about the lights. Once the lady unlocked the gate I changed out her lights on the porch that were indeed burnt out.

I was not going to say anything to fellow officers but ended up and 2 officers also said they had seen the object on several occasions prior in different parts of the city.
