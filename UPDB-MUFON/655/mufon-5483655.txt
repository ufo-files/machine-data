{nd} Taking pics of sun rays through dark clouds. Barely saw darker movement in clouds, checked pics and saw the object.

Picked my kids up from school around 2:30 pm, and decided to take longer route home to snap pics of odd dark clouds with several sun rays shooting through. I noticed the darker clouds shifting and moving in one area more so than the rest of the clouds. I saw darker shadows moving through the clouds, so I snapped a few pics. When I got home I examined the pics and saw the cigar/flat dark object and the odd white double lines that looked like white power lines, but they were not, they are too high. Not sure about the flat white spots in the clouds, but they did not look like regular cloud formation to me. There is no doubt in my mind that these objects wanted to be seen.
