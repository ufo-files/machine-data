2 disc shaped objects, glowing multicolor lighted, chase each other and following a massive fleet

My friend I and were driving on the road from Padova to Cittadella, around Limena. We spotted a couple of disc shaped objects hovering high in the clear night sky, a colored glowing one chasing a white one. The colored one's surface was glowing in different colors. The other one had a white color and a more rounded shape. Then we saw a massive group of lights in front of them: we still cant say if it was a massive fleet of single objects or a one bug object made of hundreds of colored lights. We could nit see any shape, too dark in the sky, no moon or other known objects as a reference. a couple of other guys stopped their car behind us and witnessed the entire phenomenon with us. No picture taken, i took a video with my crappy blackberry but nothing visible. The sight lasted about 20 mins, the the fleet all together moved up slowly and we believe it disappeared over the clouds.
