{nd} hovered over my platoon then casted a green light then left

My Marine platoon was on a bevac in the desert mountains of Camp Pendleton when we noticed a white light in the western sky about 30 miles miles away. The object seem to notice us at the same time a with in a second was over us. The object was as big a grade school and quite as it hovered over us. The a green beam came over us all for about 3 minutes and very quickly the object shot over the ocean and left into the sky. This was followed by military jets and helicopters. The next morning a General came out and told us what we saw was a misfired missle from Vanguard air base. We all known then that the government was in cover up mode.
