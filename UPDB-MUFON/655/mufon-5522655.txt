3 amber colored lights, one slightly obscured in a triangular form

I was driving to my mom's down a back road when I noticed a mosquito in my car. As I looked up to smack it I saw 2 amber colored lights. Driving a little further I saw a total of 3 above the tree line in a perfect triangular shape. I stopped and got out to get a good look and saw it was hovering and not a sound was coming from it. I could see it was metallic with 3 amber colored lights. The outline on the bottom and right side was very clear, the top was vague with the top light slightly obscured. It had an indentation in the middle from the bottom right corner up the right side. I became a little unnerved, got back into my car and drove past 4 trees and it was gone completely. I then heard and saw a couple of helicopters fly over head towards that direction. I heard helicopters for the next 1/2 hour or so flying around. I drew a picture but cannot send on my iPhone.
