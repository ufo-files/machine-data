Michelin Man Alien

Two months ago I awoke when a very bright light illuminated one of my bedroom windows--just the window -it did not radiate into the room. I sat up in bed and pulled off my c-pap mask. Not sure why just one window was so lit up. I than felt a presence in my bedroom and looked at the end of my bed and saw what looked like a michelin man. It looked like it was floating and its arms were rotating a little to steady itself. I was not afraid for some reason and felt that it meant me no harm. I looked again at the window and I knew I was definitely awake. When I looked at the being it communicated with me and said telepathicaly-I will come back and download information later. These were the exact words. My dog did not wake up or stir. The next thing I knew it was morning and I just kept thinking over and over what had happened. I did not make out any facial features.
