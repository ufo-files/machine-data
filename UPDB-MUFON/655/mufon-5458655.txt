{rjl} About 10 miles away about 500 ft above the water it sped from SW to NW at very high speed

This occured while on the USS Wiseman (DE667) when on 6 month cruise in the Pacific. Dont remember exact date but the Captain logged it in. I Was on the bridge with other crewmembers and we all saw this glowing object speeding from the SW to the NW at a very high rate of speed. It went from one side of the horizon to the other in about 15 seconds. It looked like it was about 500 feet off the water and probably 8-10 miles away from the ship. It stayed at the same altitude the whole distance.  The Capt logged it as a UFO. I believe we were between the Philippines and Guam and it was about 5pm, the sun was about to set. A meteor would not have stayed so close to the water for such a long distance.
