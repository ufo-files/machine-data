{cak} Triangle shaped object in the sky with three red glowing points on the edges

I was taking my trash out after dark. I like to look up at the night sky, the moon and the stars when I'm out. It's not uncommon to see planes or even a helicopter. What startled me was the shape which was triangular. There were three pulsating red lights on the edges. It just glided by, neither fast nor slow. It didn't travel a straight line but kind of went up a little and down a little. I craned by neck to watch it go by. I lost sight when it passed by my home. I did not follow because I was in my pijamas. I feel foolish now because I didn't run to get my phone but just watched confused, wondering what it was. I do not know what it was. It is unidentified in the truest sense. I know nothing about aircraft or astronomy. So, if this is something with a simple explanation I would really like to know. If this was something unique surely someone else saw it as well. I don't live in a large town but it's not exactly rual either. My neighborhood itself is fairly large.
