Triangle UFO

My dogs were barking and wanting out around 3:00am and they immediately took off out the door. I then heard them growling and barking which is very unusual (both dogs are deaf). Went outside immediately because of the possibility of neighbors getting upset of the dogs barking at 3:00am. Both dogs were staring at the sky and I could hear like a hum or buzz and the hair on my arms were like electrified…..I then saw this massive triangle craft slowly passing by. It had to be several football fields long/wide….I could see the craft was solid and had almost a carbon flat black color….it slowly passed by and continued on with no propulsion, no wings …nothing. Ran in and grabbed my phone and took a few pics with my iPhone 12 Pro Max. The lights showed up in the photo but the body itself didn’t. Came back inside and it was 4:00am…..45 minutes passed by and it felt like I was only outside 10 minutes.
