Two orbs checking out fireworks.

While vacationing at Eden Lake, my wife and I with our three boys were on the west shore of the lake watching people practice their fireworks (for July 4th the next night) all around the edge of the lake. Around the peak of the fireworks, my wife first noticed this strange light. It was a stationary and very bright red ball of light. It appeared to be at least 10 times larger than Venus would look from our vantage, and was hovering probably 100-200 feet above the eastern side of the lake. It was not blinking, but more like pulsating, continually changing its intensity between a dull red and a very bright red which also made it look bigger. After 2-3 minutes, it simply faded in intensity and disappeared completely without moving off in any direction. A couple of minutes later a second light appeared above the north end of the lake and behaved and disappeared in the exact manner, only the latter was more of an amber/orange rather than pure red. They were definately not part of the fireworks, more like they were just checking it out. Of course, they did not show up the next night for the larger display when I brought my camera.
