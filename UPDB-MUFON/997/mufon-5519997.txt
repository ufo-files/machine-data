Object with simultaneous flashing pink and blue lights over Zephyrhills at night

At approximately 8:10 pm EDT on March 14, 2012 I was walking home and noticed a large circular object directly overhead with pink and blue lights flashing simultaneously (2 lights total). The object made no sound and flew much faster than any commercial airliner, flying along a straight trajectory, apparently descending until it disappeared over the treetops.
