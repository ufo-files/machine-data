{jpg} Larger than star. Hovering. Shot straight with red laser emission. Super fast. Red emission.

I went outside in my back yard to walk my dog a little after 5 AM on August 4th. The sky was clear and the stars were bright. I looked up at the sky and immediately saw a bright object slightly bigger than the stars. It immediately shot in a straight line leaving a red laser-like trail. The object travelled at an astonishing speed and from my point of view a short distance. I’m sure it was a great distance though. The red laser-like trail never dissipated. Once the object got so far it all instantly vanished at the same time. It was not a shooting star or a meteor shower. It was near the star cluster that looks like a question mark. There was also a plane traveling just north of the object and looked to be at a higher altitude. It was all over working 1-2 seconds.
