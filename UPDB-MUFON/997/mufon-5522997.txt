Flash of light across sky.

On Oct. 29, 2011 in Bethlehem, PA 18015 during the freakish snow storm in october. We noticed 2 consecutive green light flash in the sky and once more an hour later. Light illuminated the whole sky, and only lasted 1-2 seconds each. After reporting to others miles away they too noticed a flash of light but wasn't green they noticed a blue light flash. The one reported the blue light flash in the sky in Fountain Hill, PA about 2 miles from where I saw the green flash. Then another person we know living in East Stroudsburg, PA about 35 miles from me, saw a blue light flashing in the sky too.
