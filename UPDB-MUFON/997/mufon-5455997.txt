{cak} Saw lights traveling in a straight line in unison.

I pulled into my drive way around 7:10 pm. I was checking my tire pressure when I noticed an unblinking white light in the sky slightly north of me moving at a steady pace. I then noticed 4 more lights behind the 1st moving in unison at an equal distance from each other. They did not look close enough to be connected. I did not notice any sound. I then went to my backyard and saw the exact same thing happening with 3 lights slightly to the northwest. The lights appeared faded out at the exact same point in the sky as if they were moving up and out of atmosphere. This repeated 2 more times with only 2 lights. This did not repeat the following night.
