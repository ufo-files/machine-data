Someone told me that there were lights in the sky that were strange. I went outside and looked around and started to go back inside and then I saw them. A glowing light shot down from the sky, stopped and, hovered and then split into about 5 pieces and then they shot out into different directions and disappeared. After a few seconds they came back together and rejoined themselves to one large glowing sphere and shot back up into the sky and disappeared again. I also saw other glowing spheres just hovering and then shoot out into the sky and disappear. This was not any kind of airplane or helicopter. I wasn't afraid but it was a strange feeling to see something like this.
