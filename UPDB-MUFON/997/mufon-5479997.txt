Beam of Light

Hot August Night. Couldnt sleep. Smoking a Cigarette and looking at the Stars.No clouds or Thunderstorm during this event. Clear sky.

No Thunder, no Storm, no Lightning in a Circle of 600km that Night. Clear Sky with about 30C1
No Airplanes or something else.
