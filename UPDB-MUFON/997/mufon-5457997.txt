{rjl} Saw 3 red-orange lights in the sky. Making a huge triangle Patten. Slowly one by one disappeared.

My wife and I have been noticing a red-orange light in the sky over the past 13 years. It's rare to see it sometimes. But we've tried chasing it down to see what it is. But it always seems to know when we try to get closer. It always does one of two things. Either fade out slowly after getting really bright or just vanish with in a blink of an eye. Tonight was the first time we saw 3 of them. Two parallel to each other and one below them as to make a huge triangle. Slowly one by one they just vanished. It took about 10 mins to watch them and another for them to disappear. Our kids were with and enjoyed watching them. I noticed about 30 seconds after each one disappeared that a commercial jet was starting to get close to where they were. The nearest airport is 2hrs away. Also we saw some faint shooting stars happening after they all disappeared. Not giving up on chasing it down or to get video evidence.
