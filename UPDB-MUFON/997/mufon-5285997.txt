Saw cigar object, had a weird experience, then object disappeared

On the evening of February 21 at approximately 6:30 pm I went outside for a smoke. I went out on the back porch and went to turn on my music on my phone. I noticed my phone was acting crazy, turning off then on, random apps opening, scrolling, weird screens i've never seen before. I felt a strange feeling about this so I tried to turn my phone off, and finally was able to. Then immediatly after I had this feeling to look up and there it was above the treeline. A cigar shaped UFO. It was dark grey with a hint of shimmer to it. Looked about 200-300 ft long. After seeing it and during I had this strange feeling like I wasn't in control. I was locked in on the object and next thing I know I was doing push ups against the railing and just staring at the object. Then it just vanished without a sound. I was in shock and went inside to tell my wife about what I just experienced. They asked me what took so long apparently I was gone outside for just over 35 minutes. No memory of that much time passing while I was outside. Then I began to explain to them what happened.
