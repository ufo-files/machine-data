{jpg} Ball of pure white light

Same object we had seen last year. A really bright white light came into view from the right, and continued to the left (south to north) in line with our roof until it was obstructed by the trees. The video is terrible. I couldn't steady the camera and its very pixally, but it did not blink. There was no sound. Just glided and didn't change shape and the light stayed the same the whole time. I looked through binoculars, saw no blinking lights.
