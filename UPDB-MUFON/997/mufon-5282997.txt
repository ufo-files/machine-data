I was driving north on Bruce county rd 1 and turned on con 6 heading to Underwood as I accelerated I noticed an orange light in my rear view mirror quite a distance away. As I drove the fireball followed behind me on the road and caught up to me very quickly and moved to beside me on the passenger side and followed me for a couple of Kilometres. I seemed to stay calm and never thought to take a picture, I stayed at about 60 kilometres an hour. It was about 10 feet from my van. It was orange with swirling yellow and was yellow in the centre. It was about twice the size of my van and the yellow centre was the size of the van side window. Then there seemed to be kind of a click vision wise and it swooped in front of the vehicle went over the van and headed back behind me to where it came from. As I went over the small hill in the road I could still see it in the distance. I continued home about 7 minutes down the road. There no sound coming from it and there was no heat emitting from it, it did light up the whole inside of the van.
