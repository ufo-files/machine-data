Bright moving light

A bright light larger than a planet moved methodically in a straight path. Very similar to the report submitted from Rice,, Mn and could help to have the video verify his account. Through a telescope the light was about 60 degrees above the horizon, moving along a straight path moving South to West. The lights on each side seem to rotate and move in tandem with the bright light (center) Other lights could be seen that form a triangular shape but I couldn't tell if they were moving at the same rate. The other photo is an entirely different light and independent of the lights to the south. This one light was in the East. It was moving too but I could not capture because the trees blocked my view. It was a very clear and still night and there was no sound. After viewing lights for 10 minutes, I went back inside only to return about 15 minutes later and I couldn't relocate the light in the video. In all there were more than 10 lights and the hexagonal shape light is the one I am most familiar with.
