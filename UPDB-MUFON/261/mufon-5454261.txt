{tjd} Multiple group of UFO sighting March 30th, 2020

My two girls and I were outside on their trampoline looking up at the night sky since the sky was clear last night. As we were laying there, maybe 10 minutes into us being out there, we saw a stream of 20 different, UFOs, go in a parallel line one after one another in a slow steady stream. They had an orange hue around them, in this time watching them come right after one another, we did see airplanes with their twinkling lights flying nearby but these did not have any twinkling lights ... 
We were spellbound as to what we were seeing. My oldest daughter noticed that when they got near the end of our view, some of them were circling around each other and disappeared. Talk about an amazing view. I can't believe that we actually witnessed what we saw, whatever it was! The thing that intrigued me was the fact that there were so many and all in a parallel line, one after another but with some distance as if they were flying after each other and the random circling at the end, that only a few did, just to disappear was also very bizarre. 

Unfortunately, I left my phone in the house, but even if I was to have brought it outside, my phone quality is not the best and it probably wouldn't have captured well of what we saw in the dark. 

Did anyone else witness this? I guess it would take you being outside and staring at the sky to have seen it like we were last night, but wow... I had to share! My kids were a little spooked but fascinated as well!
