{dr}Working at Moffett Field. Notice flashing object following commercial aircraft, then cross from west to east slowly

While working at Moffett field NASA Ames research. During construction, we have numerous aircraft we observe. Today I saw a typical commercial aircraft heading towards Oakland . I notice the round oval shape object in a very close profile following the commercial craft. Approximately 500 ft behind. It veered to the right heading towards Moffett field very slowly. Silver flashes as if it was rolling but looked as if it would just flash light a mirror or a bright light. It travels slowly to the east, seemed to stop a few times and then continue its path. Other people saw it but could not explain what they were looking at. I knew what it was.
