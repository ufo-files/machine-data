My Encounter as a 12 years old.

IN 1969 AS A 12 YEARS OLD IN JAMAICA,I WENT IN A BUSHY HILL AREA ONE AFTERNOON CLOSE TO MY HOUSE,I SAW TWO STRANGE CREATURES STARRING AT ME,THEY LOOKED LIKE HUMANS BUT THEY WERE NOT,THEY HAD UNBEARABLE BRIGHT EYES,ONE OF THEM WAS FIDGETING AROUND,I COVERED MY EYES AND SAID GOD HELP ME,WHEN I UNCOVERED MY EYES I SAW A BRIGHT LIGHT LIKE THE SUN DRIFT AWAY AND IT WAS A FLASH.
 I STUMBLED HOME,BUT I WAS AFRAID TO TELL MY PARENTS BECAUSE I HAVE BEEN WARNED NOT TO GO PLAY THERE,SO I HOPED THAT I GET OVER IT MYSELF. YEARS AFTER WHEN I MOVED TO THE UNITED STATES THAT WAS WHEN I KEPT SEEING THAT SAME IMAGES ON THE TV SO I TOLD FAMILY AND FRIENDS ABOUT MY ENCOUNTER WITH THEM.
  SEEING THE IMAGES AGAIN ON TV SPARKED MY INTEREST IN FINDING EXACTLY WHAT I SAW YEARS AGO,SO IN 2008 I WENT BACK TO THAT SAME SPOT I SAW THEM SINCE THE LAND IS CLOSE TO MY FATHER'S PROPERTY,INITIALLY I DID NOT KNOW WHAT TO LOOK FOR,BUT I PICKED UP SOME STRANGE LOOKING STONES AND SOIL FROM THE SPOT AND RARE FOOT PRINTS AND TOOK THEM TO THE LAB FOR TESTING IN THE USA,I ALSO SAW A BRIGHT AREA AT ONE SPOT WHICH I HAVE EVIDENCE TO SHOW.I WILL APPRECIATE IF SOMEBODY CONTACT ME AND I AM WILLING TO SHOW THE SITE WHERE THIS OCCURRED.
