@11:43 p-Tempe,AZ I witnessed 3 fireball orbs, hovering the eastern skies, in a triangular shape

My boyfriend and I were at a red light waiting to turn onto the 101 at 11:43pm when I saw 3 flare-like orbs hovering the eastern skies of Tempe AZ. They looked like flares, but the time of night and their proximity to the city didn't add up. The light turned green within a few seconds, leaving me no time to grab my camera phone, but as we were turning 1 orb appeared to slightly drop altitude& shift 20 degrees or so to the left. Unfortunantly, the freeway walls blocked the view until we got on the 60 2mins later, and by then whatever I saw was gone. I waited a few days looking for any news reports to clarify what I saw but, no luck. I would like to know if anyone else can relate to this or clear it up for me because I cant be the only person in AZ to have seen this!
