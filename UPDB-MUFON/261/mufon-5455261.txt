9 to 10 flying units following each other by 3 second delay travelling about 1.5 to 2x faster than satellite

Happened in early morning. Stars still very visible. Lights looked just as bright as stars. Maybe a bit more intense. 

9 to 10 flying lights following each other in line, directly behind the next light, travelling very fast in a straight line no evidence of noise( not even delayed like airplanes). The lights did not travel behind and to the left or right of the craft in front but traveled directly in line with each other. Very close to each other. The 9 craft spaced out equal distances visually. All 9 to 10 craft occupied about 1 third of thev180 degree ceiling view from below. 
The last light was travelling west (and heading south like the rest)of the first 8 or 9 and aligned with the 3rd last light at time of first sight. . After the roughly 3 minutes, the west light had slowed ( and acted as if it was not travelling straight or fast like the others)to be beside the last light, west of it. All lights flew faster than any known craft, airplane, or satellite or helicopters. About 1.5 to 2 times faster. 

After 3 minutes I lost sight of the line of craft heading south 558 am local time. The craft were almost directly above my location but slightly west.
