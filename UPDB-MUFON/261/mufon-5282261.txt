2 glowing cylinders with white blinking lights

I was traveling on highway 39 north by the EMPEPA building at 12:25-12:30 when I saw them. It looked like 2 glowing white cylinders hovering next to each other in the western sky over the city. Each one had a single light that would flash at the same time. As I was watching they moved to the NW and out of my sight. I though maybe they were blimps.
