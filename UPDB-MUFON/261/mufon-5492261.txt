{dr}Orb changing colors looked like movement of orb of the good witch of the wizard of oz

I was grilling when I saw a circular orb moving around the tree tops about a mile away. I happened to have such low battery on my cell phone that it wouldn't allow me to take a picture or video. I didn't want to go inside because I was afraid with my luck the object would disappear before I got out a camera. So with the small percentage of battery left on my phone, I made a last second call to my neighbor to come out quickly. She did, and for about ten minutes we both saw this object moved with no apparent flight plan. It hovered around changing from what looked like white red green and blue colors. It kind of looked liked the orb of the good witch of the wizard of oz. It never got closer or further. It would move diagonally then in a semi circle, then just vertically or horizontally. It finally dipped under the treetop line and thats when i decided to leave my grill and walk closer to where i had seen the object. By then it was gone and i never saw it again. We told her husband who thought we were nuts. I figured in this day in age it was probably a drone. I had forgot about it until a few weeks later i remembered the incident and decided to look it up online. It just so happened some guy who was driving that night, saw the object too, and decided to pull over and video tape the object. It is on youtube still and it was the same object I saw but my neighbor and I were a lot closer to the object then the video. Just look up ufo in Tamarac on youtube
