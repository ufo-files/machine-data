several balls of light with small explosions

I was driving back to reno from work in Spanish springs when I saw ten or so in the sky. They kept moving into different shapes. At first the lights made a square. When they moved into other shapes they made small explosions. I kept thinking I can't believe no one else is seeing this. When I picked up my wife I told her what I saw an pointed to where they were. She saw them too but this time there was only five or six and they were in a line. They were to far away for pictures to show up on my phone.
