{cak} Hovering Objects

Leaving gas station and saw 2 objects perfectly still directly to the south. Perfectly clear morning. Got in the car to drive closer. At first thought they were helicopters, but absolutely no movement or sound. One was below and left of the other. Sighted for about 5 minutes. Traffic was heavy, so had to watch the traffic. Kept looking up to make sure they were still there. After about 2 minutes, observed an extremely bright light to the left on the two objects, which then disappeared. After 4 or 5 minutes, looked up again and they were gone. If these were helicopters, I would have seen them leave.
