I noticed a bright orb in the sky while observing the full moon. I took pictures with iPad and shortly after orb disappeared.

I was at home watching tv when I noticed how bright and full the moon looked. I got up and looked out my patio doors to get a better look at the moon. I suddenly noticed a bright white orb to the right of the moon. It briefly hovered there then it seemed to move slowly beneath the moon. I ran and grabbed my iPad, threw the doors open and stepped outside onto my patio step, and started taking pictures as quickly as I could. The orb silently went from the right side of the moon to the left side but seemed like it was beneath the moon all the while it was moving as in an arc. It suddenly disappeared as though someone turned off a light. I previously witnessed a bunch of red glowing orbs pass over the park behind my home, but this was the first time I saw the white glowing orb. I am always amazed by brilliant stars and moving heavenly objects! Could it have been another planet? I suppose anything is possible, but I don't believe this was the case because it was in my view so briefly and moved right to left before disappearing from sight suddenly. I had forgotten about these pictures I took until recently when I was putting pictures into albums. The only reason I know the date I took these photos is because my iPad placed my photos under the date taken. I believe the timeline would be between 8:00 and 10:00 p.m. Hope this helps!
