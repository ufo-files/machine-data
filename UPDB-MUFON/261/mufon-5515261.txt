{nd} orb like. travling in formation then grouping together

I was outside smoking when I looked up and noticed time white orb like things in the sky. Then I noticed they were in a formation. After about 5 minutes they made a dog leg turn to the south. I counted 10 or 11 but there could have been more. Once I noticed the objects I felt like I couldnt break eye contact on them. I looked down fort a second because my neck hurt and when I looked back they were gone.
