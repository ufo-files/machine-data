Bright orange large sphere that travelled along the main road at about the same speed of our vehicle. My mother being terrified sped up and so did the sphere. It was NOT the MOON. My mother kept saying what is it what is it? I was very afraid myself being only ten years old. I don't know what happened to it. I think it just disappeared. This was my first contact but NOT my last.
