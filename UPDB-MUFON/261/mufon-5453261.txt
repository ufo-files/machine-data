The orb or oval object was almost see through accept for the oval edges. Could tell it was oval when it moved but could see clouds through it.

I was laying out at the pool with a few friends. I looked into the sky and realized there was something In the sky right above my head above the tree just outside pool gate. I thought it was my imagination at first so rubbed my eyes but it wouldn’t go away and it kept moving locations randomly but stayed in the same area. You could tell it was an oval shape but I could see the clouds through it! My first instinct and thought was that I was being watched by someone and that freaked me out. Not like watched by a camera, but by a person. As if it was intelligent or alive. I told myself I was seeing things so after watching it for like 20-30 minutes I pretended it wasn’t there. I felt dumb for being afraid of it and I had always been told there is no Such thing as aliens and “ufos”. All I know is that it is almost 15 years later and I still think about it and wonder what it was. I have only found one other sighting online so far that sounded sort of like what I saw. All I know is it was definitely something “camouflaged” somehow, but I have no idea what it was.
