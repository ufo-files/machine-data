TWO OF THEM CLOSE TOGETHER, SIDE BY SIDE, YELLOW ORANGE.

I WAS WALKING ON A DEAD-END ROAD (WHERE I LIVE). IT WAS PAST SUNSET. I NOTICED A CIGAR-SHAPED YELLOW ORANGE LIGHT VERY LOW ON THE HORIZON. I THOUGHT IT WAS AN INTERESTING TRICK OF THE MOON AND CLOUDS, BUT THEN I NOTICED ANOTHER DIRECTLY TO ITS RIGHT - IDENTICAL IN SIZE AND SHAPE, BUT A BIT DIMMER. WOW, I THOUGHT, THAT IS INDEED AN INTERESTING TRICK OF MOON AND CLOUDS, AS THE SPACE THE LIGHTS SPANNED WAS MUCH WIDER THAN THE MOON APPEARS. STILL THINKING IT WAS AN OPTICAL ILLUSION, I CONTINUED TO WHERE MY VISION OF THE LIGHTS WAS BLOCKED BY FOLIAGE. I WENT TO LOOK FOR THE "MOON" PHENOMENON LATER AND COULD NOT FIND IT. TODAY (AUGUST 2) I REALIZED THAT THE MOON SHOULD NOT BE SO LOW ON THE HORIZON DIRECTLY NORTH, UNLESS ONE IS IN ANTARCTICA.
