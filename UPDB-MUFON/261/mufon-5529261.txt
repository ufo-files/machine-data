Saw a perfect triangular shaped object fly directly overhead at a fairly rapid speed

It was approximately 11:07 PM EST when my husband and I were out on our porch star gazing, and I noticed something moving overhead (heading East), and my first inclination was to think it was a flock of geese in v formation, but realized it was a triangular shaped craft, dim, with lights on each side outlining the shape. It was moving pretty rapidly. I can't really give any specifics on how high up it really was, but it definitely did not seem too high up to me. I saw the object for a few seconds, then I lost sight of it. It also seemed to be a decent size. I apologize for not being able to be more specific, but I am bad at gauging these kinds of things. There is absolutely no way I could mistake this to be anything else. It stayed in a perfectly triangular formation, and it moved as one large unit.
