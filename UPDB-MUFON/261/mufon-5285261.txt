Pulsating light

Saw a pulsating light. Thought it was a star, but realized it was pulsating blue, red, green, white, possibly yellow light. Looked through binoculars, couldn’t focus  Looked like blurry sphere but the light pulsating was much clearer. Didn’t seem to move, but was gradually obscured by trees (much like stars “move”). Other stars visible and didn’t pulsate, but did twinkle. Many commercial aircraft visible during this time as is typical. In video it looks like it’s moving but that’s just my unsteady hand.
