(GS) I just witnessed a Fireworks Extravaganza. It was as big as a 747.

The fireworks extravaganza had just ended. As the smoke cleared, I heard
the shrill voice of a child "What is that?" A circular object was in the
southern sky and it looked like a golden yellow planet with black wisps
on it. The diameter was that of a 747. It was seen at a 35 degree angle and
it appeared to be 3.5 miles away. The object seemed to be moving away
at warp speed. It seem to disappear in quarters. It was like something out
of Star Trek." There was a huge crowd of people leaving Midway Stadium at
the time. There were media crews present. The witness expected to see
mention of it on T.V., but there was no coverage at all. The weather had
been perfect with no clouds in the sky and no humidity.
