UFO sighted in picture on phone after taking picture of a multi colored horizontal cloud.

May 25, 2017. Right about 12:00 high. One hour east of Bakersfield, CA. SW of Kernville on the Kern River just NEE of Wofford Heights, Highway158 to Cemetery Rd., follow the trail to the small beach at river side. Hanging with friends and cut the four Labs lose to play in the water. My buddy Des says, look at that cloud. Horizontal and thin with three distinctive colors. She grabs a picture of it on her cell phone. We look at the pic on the phone and the cloud looks awesome. The next day, Des shows me the same pic on her phone but, this time, she blew it up a click and there it was... A sphere with the top part looking somewhat reflective? You can see the angle of the phone looking up capturing the cloud and above that the sphere. Now judging by that angle, that cloud and that sphere are directly above Kernville Airport. So that's why I don't think it's a weather balloon. In a small town airports airspace next to Lake Isabella in the Kern River. It sure as hell looks like a UFO phenomena to me.
