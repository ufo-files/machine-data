White Light Moving, Pulsing to 20x Lumens, Slowly

At approximately 10:08 pm, on 1/11/22, I opened my back door to have a cigarette. As I looked up as I always do, I noticed a white light moving due west against the starlit sky. My initial thought was an airplane as always, as I always look up at the sky immediately and notice all planes and helicopters in the area. It’s a daily thing. But as I first glanced at it, it immediately seemed to glow brighter, like a slow pulse, not a blink, and then become dimmer. As I watched it, I started thinking it was a satellite. But yet again, it pulsed, and then it slowed to a stop for a duration of 15 seconds as if it was fixed in the starlit sky. After that, it pulsed again and slowly proceed due south until it disappeared over the horizon. It wasn’t a plane, it wasn’t a chopper, it wasn’t a drone, it wasn’t a starlink satellite or a falcon x launch. What was it? It literally was traveling west, slowly pulsing, comes to a full stop for 15 seconds, then proceeds due south and slowly and disappears over the horizon. It didn’t turn against the wind when it changed direction. It was literally heading west, then stops, then heads south.
 10 minutes after, I looked outside again and saw a ‘shooting star’ head the same exact direction. Not a fireball type of shooting star, but more of a streaker that doesn’t pop brighter. Maybe unrelated, maybe not.
