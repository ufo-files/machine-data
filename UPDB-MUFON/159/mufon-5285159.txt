White sphere 6-10' round

While flying My RC powered glider I observed a white sphere come over from the SW. Stopped directly above my Rc plane. I thought it was a big balloon that was floating by, As it came into the area about 60mph. When it Stopped and stayed stationary I ask my son if I should fly my plane up to it . He said yes and I flew towards it. As I approached it it moved out of the way and followed it for about 30 yards. I proceeded to fly around it and it interacted with me . Did not make a sound. As my plane flew near it I could get an Idea of its size. Then off in the distance I could see a corporate jet flying at around 500ft coming towards it. As it approach my rc airplane lost radio contact and went down. The jet was a Cessna citation (I am an A&P) kept fly towards it and as it was about 100 yards away the white sphere took off towards the north west over Folsom lake with the jet following at a slow speed. If I had to guess I would say it was some kind of drone controlled by the persons in the jet. It made no sound and moved any direction it pleased.
