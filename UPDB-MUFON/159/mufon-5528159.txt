Thin on the top thicker on bottom. All bright white light. Had 4 smaller multicolored lights circling around it.

The dog woke me up at approximately 4:45 am to use the bathroom. I saw what looked like a huge star hoovering in a southeasterly direction and had 4 little lights hoovering around it. I woke up my daughter to be sure I wasn't seeing things, and her and her friend sleeping over also saw the light. It was 4 times the size of the stars in the sky and my daughter took a picture of it with her cell phone. Didn't come out very clear. We watched it move slowly up and back for around an hour until we all got tired and went back to bed. I woke up again about ten minutes ago, and its still there. Smaller now, but very bright. I am watching the news, there is nothing about it. I am suprised, because it's in plain sight right now! 6:39am - it is now gone.
