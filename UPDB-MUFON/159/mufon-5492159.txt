{jpg} Cigar shaped about 100' with porthole type windows with a person like figure in one of the portholes

My wife and I were going back home. We had been over at one of my wife's sister house. As we were traveling back toward Englewood, Tennessee. As we got close to Englewood we spotted and object which was cigar shaped just above the tree tops just hovering. We stopped the car and got out to get a better look. You could see porthole type windows with which appeared to have a human type being at one of the windows. The object started to move slowly. We got back in our car and followed it. The object turned toward where my wife's mother and dad lived. We turned up and went behind my wife's family home. As soon as it got over her parents home it just disappeared. Our oldest son was less than two months old at the time. He cried all night and the next morning around 6 A.M. my mother came down to tell us that Charlotte's dad had died that night. We have told a lot of people about what we saw and happened that night. It is something my wife and I will never forget.
