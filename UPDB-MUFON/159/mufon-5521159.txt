Changed from triangle to orb, glowing, with flame coming from tips of triangle

We were outside trying to view the Northern Lights projected to be seen tonight. Suddenly, to our south, we noticed a light in the sky that appeared to be a triangle with flame coming from the corners and then it appeared to turn into an orb and was going all over the sky. Two others appeared from different directions.
