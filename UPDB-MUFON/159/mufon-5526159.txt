My friend Emils and i were taking some pics of the burn-off from a local factory

We were taking some pics of the burn-off at the local plastic factory, when I noticed that an object preceded a trail of smoke/vapors. Its trajectory remained the same throughout until it disappeared behind some cloud at the skyline. We took several pictures with a zoom camera, of which are of good quality. We have zoomed closer on our pc package for closer inspection and think that it could be studied in greater depth with better equipment. We have downloaded the pics to a file-share for anyone who wishes to study further.
