Husker Football Players see Unworldly Neon Green Orb

I use to have an apartment at [address moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] in Lincoln Nebraska and on one early morning driving down I believe 10th street in the direction of the stadium at 430 in the morning for a 5 am winter conditioning football practice I seen this oddest glowing neon green orb hovering in the direction right behind and above the Nebraska stadium from my viewpoint. It then went from hovering to instant incredible speed and out of sight. When I arrived at the locker room during taping I was elated and my friend who was on the same road and another player also seen it. I kept it to myself for many years because I thought it be some military new vehicle because I think there is a military base below the vicinity I seen it. It was to this day the most amazing thing I ever seen in the sky’s.
