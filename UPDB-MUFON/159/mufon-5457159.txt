{cak} Photographic anomaly

Leaving a restaurant, I took a picture of the lighted facade of the building across the street. When I reviewed the picture the next morning, I was stunned by the presence of an object or objects above the building. I had not seen/noticed anything in the sky when I took the photo. I went back during the day two days later, but did not see anything behind the building that might explain the object captured.
