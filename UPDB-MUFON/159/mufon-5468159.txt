Classic disc shaped UFO flew directly overhead. First noticed that there were no planes in sky, then bright red light heading towards us. The light dimmed and then re-lit,then rotated around the outside. No noise, passed over us and continued westward.

Went outside and noticed it was quiet. It was around at about 11:55pm. There were no planes in the sky and the stars were clear so we were looking up. Noticed a red light after 5 minutes. It was easy of us and headed almost directly towards us but with no sound to accompany. It was low in the sky so we assumed a helicopter. Then it drew closer and dimmed its red light. I commented on it dimming the light and it lit back up and flickered then stayed on. At this point I noticed that the light had begun to rotate around the object and that it was disc shaped and not plane shaped. The light went from the front to the right side and then to the back and around to the left side facing us. It paused then began to rotate around again. By this point it was directly above. There was still no noise and the outline was clearly circular. It passed over and continued on it a straight path to the west of us. Checked the clock and it was now 12:08.
