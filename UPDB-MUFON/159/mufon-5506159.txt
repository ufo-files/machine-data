{dr}looks like the ball of lights like on the Dallas sky line

There is a very bright light that shines each night. It is first visible in south west sky from my house and moves west each night until it out of site. Most people would think it is a star or planet but when looked at though binoculars you see what looks like the bright ball of light on the tower in the night sky line of Dallas Tx. with a dark line that travel top to bottom or sometimes right to left. I took a photo and blew it up and you can see a dark red sphere in the center of the lights. I first noticed it because it is super bright even more so than the moon and out shines a full moon.
