this is in the 70's late 60's me and my brother and our other friends were playing out in the tundra outside our village it was summer so the sun didn't go down till late at night, as we came up on a small hill we all felt the humming in our bodies and was curious as to what made us feel that within us then the other kids ran off scared and left me my brother and another friend and his brother we decided to look over the small hill and we saw 3 barrel shaped objects hovering exactly the same height above the ground in front of us there were lights twirling around them but the other craft remained stationary, even tho they looked to be separated it felt like one craft, after about 15 to 20 minutes they took off at an angle at the same time tilted and blinked out at an astonishing rate of speed and was gone. i was the first to go and observe the burnt marks it left i felt a wave of sickness coming over me and my brother grabbed me and took me away.
