{rjl} White and blue orb descended from sky to about 20 feet from ground, moved north from alley to 4th street then moved west 2 blocks then ascended back into sky.

I was walking to a friends house who lived 2 streets from me. I first noticed the object when I saw it coming straight down from the sky. I'm not sure what I first thought it was I was just kinda stunned at what I was looking at. The whole time that I witnessed it I did not move at all. The object was basically a large glowing white ball of light about 5 feet in diameter with some sort of flowing blue light also emanating from inside. The orb was moving slowly and hovered for a split second before making turns. when it came from the alley I was standing in the middle of the road and was only about 20 feet from it but the object took no notice in me and carried on as if it was doing some sort of surveilance, that's what I thought it was doing anyway. The ufo then traveled west at about 10 to 15 mph for a distance of 2 blocks and I say ufo because no one can convince me that it was ball lightning. Ball lightning is not going to make 3 90 degree turns and also descend and ascend and speed up and slow down. Well after the sighting my adrenaline was going pretty good and I was excited but when I told my friends they did not believe me, I was about 15 or 16 at the time. I lost sight of the object when it ascended back into the sky. after it ascended I went to where it descended and looked up to see if I could see something but there was nothing there. My memory of the incident may not be perfect cause it happened 25 years ago but it still sticks with me to this day. I remember that it was later in the day and there was not a cloud in sight. To put it simply, it was amazing.
