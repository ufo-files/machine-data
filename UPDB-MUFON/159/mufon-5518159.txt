Giant cone of light similar to jet wash but all the way across the sky

I & my brother had arrived in the Riverdale wynn dixie parking lot & I spotted a very large cone shaped trail or cone of light looking like it started on the East coast & continued across the sky into the clouds in the west. It had dark streaks beside each light edge delineated by another looks like residual light beams outside the cone. We took pics & a short movie of this incident. I know this was NOT light coming through the clouds due to the fact the sun was almost down & behind the stormclouds. To my knowledge light doesnt come from diffuse & focus to a point across the state.
