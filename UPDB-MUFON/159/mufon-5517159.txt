A huge white cigar coming down on a diagonal motion.

I was giving my friend a ride to his house with my car, and when we were close to the destination. 
The sky was really clean, without any clouds, suddenly we saw on the horizon a strange white (white cloud colored) object that looked like a cigar and it appeared to be real far away. At the first time we tought it was a cloud, but it ws too fast and clouds doesnt get that shape. Then we tought it was an airplane but it was not leaving any trae or making any noise. 
I stopped the car, so i could take a picture but it vanished behind the buildings on the horizon which were like 1 kilometers away from that position we were standing. It was descending on a diagonal motion.
I got kind of scared at the moment.
