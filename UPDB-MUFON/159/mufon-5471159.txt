On Tuesday, January 2, 2018, at approximately 2220 hrs, I was lying in ben reading when I heard the distinct sound of a military helicopter. The "chopping" sound of the helicopter rotor was very loud, I actually thought it had landed, or was at least hovering in the vicinity. I stepped outside and noticed the red flashing beacon approximately 1 mile in the distance and approaching my location very slowly. As I cntinued to observe the red light I noticed the helicopter was following a bright white light. The white light had no flashing beacons and I was unable to determine its axact shape, except for it being a white light. Within a minute or so the object and the helicopter passed almost directly overhead form the SW and continued in the NE direction until it was out of site.
