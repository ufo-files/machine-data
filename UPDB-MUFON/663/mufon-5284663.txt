It was a bright light. First thought it was a satellite but larger than usual

Saw a large white light in the sky but thought it was stationary and questioned whether it would be a satellite or not because it was much lower than I usually observe satellites and much bigger. It was in our southwest sky. I thought it was stationary but I enlarged the picture on my camera to 10x and realized it was moving in a circular direction very fast. As we watched it continued to drop below the tree line. We watched this for over a half hour. I went back out later and found the same thing in the direct south sky. Same thing. It was in the shape of a circle and it was moving and swirling as it was before. I took video of it in the southwest sky and just observed and took a picture of it in the South sky.
