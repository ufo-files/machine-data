{dr}three orange lights in southerly direction, stop and hover, then proceed north and disappeared

I went out side to have a cigarette and noticed three orange lights in a southerly direction stop and hover above my neighborhood. Two of these waited for the third to catch up and then went in a northerly direction and then upward and all three disappeared. I was in between two quad-plexes. At first I thought they were helicopters, but they don't shine orange. They traveled in a straight jagged line horizontally.
