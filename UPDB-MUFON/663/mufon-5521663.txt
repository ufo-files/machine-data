Went outside to check the mail. A starlike object brighter than polaris sighted next to the moon and I watched in awe as it steadily and silently descended down horzion until it was gone

I was going outside to get the mail. From the moment I opened the door I noticed a magnificently bright star-like object on the left side of the moon. It caught my attention with its brilliantly bright white star like features. Immediately, I thought it was a star until it began to move. The object began to descend down the sky towards the horizon, fast, without speeding up or slowing down. I was thrilled watching it because I was skeptical that I would ever see one again. About half a minute passed as I followed its flight path until the light dimmed and was gone.
