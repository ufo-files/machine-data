(GS) It appeared and flew behind a cirrus cloud. Then it disappeared.

I was sitting in my lawn chair outside and watching the clouds when all of the sudden I seen a reflective object appear out of nowhere. At first I thought it was high elevation plane, but then I noticed its disk-like shape. When I seen it, it came from west and went in a straight line to the north. I was only able to see for a few seconds before it sped over a small Cirrus cloud and instead of showing up on the other side of the cloud, it vanished. When I seen it I was both surprised and confused.
