Glowing ball with light wings parallel to ground

As I went out to walk the dog on 4/11 at 10:45pm I immediately saw a glowing light with light wings that were perpindicular to the ground. It was moving quickly, about as fast as a shooting star but somewhat slower. It was moving in a straight line which I could not tell was falling but more straight and level as it was close to the treetops and did not go behind the tree line before it disappeared. The wings were projecting out of the sides and were glowing and almost like reflections off of clouds, but there were no clouds in that direction as I could see stars as well. The ball was yellow with a fuzzy outline and round and not like any typical shooting star that I have ever observed. Strangely immediately after the ball disappeared two to three owls began hooting loudly, with their typical calls but also some strange calls that I have not heard from these owls before.
