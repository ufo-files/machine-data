{td} In a area of no cloud cover, orb hovered, then transformed into a disc-like object slowly moved then vanished

Me and my family were coming back from a vacation and had to drive through Portland. We were on our way crossing into Washington, when I looked up to look for cloud shapes, and animals and instead saw a orb sitting stationary somewhat over the Portland airport(near that area) in a area with no cloud cover. I sat dumbfounded when the glowing white orb all of a sudden changed. The bottom of the orb collapsed and the side came up and went into a lead/grey and chrome disc. The disc had a very slight dome on the top. The disc slowly moved south across the sky. Then it vanished. After I saw the object, I felt different inside, like a gut feeling, or something like that. I don't usually get that feeling unless I'm on a rollercoaster or something like that. It was a very strange moment. I'm not sure if a cloud got in the way, or if it flew off, or if I blinked, or what happened, but I can say that I have seen some strange things, but never this!
