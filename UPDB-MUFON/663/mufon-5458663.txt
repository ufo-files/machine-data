{rjl} It's started as large, rolling, thundering clouds moving easily 100 miles an hour...

I was a child probably around 10 or 11 years old. I was outside in our field hitting my baseball around as I usually did around 2:00 to 3:00 on an overcast day I believe it was sometime around Easter. Over the trees of our property came rolling loud long rolling and almost thundering lines of clouds that I stared at. and then the next thing I remember I ran into the house trying to get my mom dad grandma and grandpa to come outside and look and see what I was seeing. but it was now dark it had been many hours later and I have no idea what happened in that time.
