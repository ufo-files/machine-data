My wife and I were relaxing in our backyard around 10:30 pm on 6/25/16 when my wife suddenly said what is that, look, look as I looked up we observed a reddish orange orb approaching from the south moving north. very bright that wasn't descending maybe less than 1000 feet. No sound, moving faster than a plane or helicopter. We live near a approach pattern for the local airport. We see and hear aircraft all the time. We have never seen anything like this.
