Very bright green orb passed between my windows and trees in garden.

I was sitting in my home watching TV when the TV lost signal and a very bright green orb moved quite quickly between my windows and the trees in the garden. It was only visible for a few seconds and appeared from right to left. It was very bright and completely silent. I have been wondering if it was someone using a laser pointer, but, have now dismissed that thought as the light was far too intense and the orb shape being too large.
