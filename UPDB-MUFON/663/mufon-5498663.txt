A flying starlike light.

I was in my backyard looking at stars with my telescope. I spoted an airplane so i stared at it until i sighted a starlike flying object circling around the airplane,then the object started following the airplane. At first i had thought it was a falling star but it was flying and not falling. I stared at it for a very long time,then i started getting flashback's of my life like the object was tracking my memory. I lost sight of it after the flashback's and it blinked then disappeared. I told my dad about it,he had said to report it which i'm doing now. I got in my house and felt drowsy a weird.
