{rjl} 100 lights traveling in a perfect straight line appeared in the sky over New Richmond Wi . Bright solid white lights

I was in the yard walking my dog and was looking at the north sky. I noticed what first appeared as a light con trail that suddenly became a seemingly never ending stream of star like objects appearing from the darkness. I ran to the door and called my girlfriend outside to witness what I was seeing. She ran out and couldn't believe what we were seeing. The stream of lights traveling thru the sky in a perfect straight line. There were at least 100 star like objects. They were much brighter then the stars in the sky. It was like a string of led lights pulling thru the sky. We tried to use our phones to capture what we were seeing. The video only appears to capture our conversation about what we were experiencing. We both attempted to capture this phenomenon. Look8ng at the images and videos on our phones its unclear if we captured it. We will review our media and release the images if anything is viewable. I really hope someone besides the two of us observed this. It was something I've never seen or could explain. We are in shock and awe . This event lasted for 3 to 4 mins . The traveling trail of lights took up 20 percent of the sky in a perfect straight line. They moved North East till they disappeared from our sight . Speed was constant.
