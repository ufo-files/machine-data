{rjl} 3 moving lights in a triangle shape

Every night, weather permitting, i step outside to observe the night sky, Venus in particular this time of year has been spectacular!
I notice what looks like a star moving east across the sky so I assumed it was an airplane but I couldn't hear a sound so I kept observing then I noticed there were two more "stars" as part of this moving object (objects?), which formed a triangle.
