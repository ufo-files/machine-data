At8:30 on the evening of Jan 22, 2014I was walking back from the mailbox. I looked up at the stars and planets the way I usually like to do.As I noticed 3 bright what I thought were stars almost directly overhead, I stopped momentarily to look at them only to see that these stars were moving.At that point I noticed other star-like lights all moving to the northwest. At first I thought them to be satellites, but figured they could not be as they were flying much too close to each other.Upon looking around I also noticed in the south direction 3 lights lined up in a 45 degree angle all evenly spaced flying as if in unison, with other lights around them flying in the same direction. After a couple of minutes I got my wife to come and look at this to prove I was not seeing things. Shecame out in time to confirm what I had seen and she had no explanation of what it was we saw. I watched this event for 5 min, so I do not know how many others there might have been, but for me it was an awesome experience as I truly believe we are not and have not been alone in this universe for a long, long time
