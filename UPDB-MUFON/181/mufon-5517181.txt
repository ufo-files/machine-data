{dr}3 lights in a triangular shape that pulsated with slight color changes.

My wife, daughter and I were outside the back of our house with our pet hamster letting him run on our driveway. I pointed up in the trees and said whats that? It was a group of three lights moving slowly over the horizon still in the trees. I moved over in our yard to a clearing to see the lights better. At first it looked like the Chinese lanterns used during the fourth of July, but the stayed in a triangular shape with a slight variance to sphere or domed shape. It continued to move across the horizon and slowly ascended and moved back away from us and then dimmed out and was gone. This took about five minutes of time. My daughter was able to capture some of the lights on her iPad. My wife and daughter also saw the same lights hovering over the tree tops near the KC Royals stadium in November last year.
