I would go camping with a group of at least 50 boys of varying ages. We slept in tents. On this night it was early in the morning . I awoke to a strange orange glow outside the tent. I poked my head outside and saw this object hovering over a tree. It was like a crystal 10+ feet long and about 3 feet wide and faceted like a cut gem. It seemed to stay there for a few minutes i said 6 min but i don't think it could have been that long. I had the strange feeling that it was watching us. It was so captivating. I did not think of raising an alarm it moved off at an incredible speed and made no sound. It was so close to the tops of the trees that if i climbed it i could touch it . It made the top of the trees move like if it was pushing the tops aside. I kept this to myself for years. But shared it with my wife.
