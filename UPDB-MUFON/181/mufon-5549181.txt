SINGLE WHITE LIGHT TRAVELLING WEST TO EAST

IT WAS 6;30 PM WHEN I LEFT MY HOUSE TO GO FOR MY NIGHTLY WALK. THE EVENING WAS CLEAR, THE STARS WERE VISIBLE AND THE THREE QUARTER MOON WAS VERY BRIGHT OVER MY RIGHT SHOULDER. I HAD WALKED ABOUT 20 MINS. WHEN I NOTICED A SINGLE WHITE LIGHT TRAVELLING FROM WEST TO EAST.THE SPEED AND ALTITUDE OF THE OBJECT WAS NOT THAT OF A COMMERCIAL JET WHICH TRAVEL ON A SOUTH - NORTHERLY DIRECTION FROM WHERE I LIVE. IT WAS A SINGLE NON FLASHING WHITE LIGHT AS BRIGHT AS A STREET LIGHT.ALSO I SHOULD ADD THERE WERE NO FLASHING RED AND GREEN LIGHT LIKE AN AIRCRAFT OR HELICOPTER WOULD SHOW.AFTER ABOUT 10 SECONDS THE LIGHT JUST WENT OUT LIKE TURNING OFF A LIGHT SWITCH IN YOUR HOUSE.I CONTINUED TO LOOK , BUT SAW NOTHING NOT EVEN A SILHOUETTE.I MIGHT ADD THERE WAS NO SOUND DURING THE SIGHTING OR AFTER.I'VE SPENT 5 YRS. IN THE NAVY AS A SIGNALMAN, THE LIGHT WE USE TO SEND MORSE CODE TO OTHER SHIPS IS VERY BRIGHT AND THIS IS WHAT THE BRIGHTNESS OF THIS LIGHT REMINEDED MY OF.DURING AND AFTER THE SIGHTING I WAS A LITTLE EXCITED. I'M ALWAYS LOOKING UP AT THE NIGHT SKY HOPING TO SEE SOMETHING OUT OF THE ORDINARY.IT JUST SO HAPPENED THAT I FINISHED READING THE TRAVIS WALTON STORY "FIRE IN THE SKY" A FEW DAYS BEFORE THIS.EVEN THOUGH THIS IS MY FIRST EXPERIENCE WITH U.F.O'S , I'VE ALWAYS BEEN A BELIEVER THAT WE R NOT ALONE AND THAT WE ARE CONTINUOUSLY BEING VISITED BY E.T'S.
