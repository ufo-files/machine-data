{nd} There were 3 orbs spread out a good distance from each other.

I was headed north on my way home, I just stopped at our gravel road and was headed east when I looked to the north and had seen one light appear out of no where. I stopped in the middle of the road about to turn in my drive and watched, another lit up and then another. One went out and another went out and then they came back on. Then one at a time they all three went out and I didn't see any more activity. I noticed my wife was still in her car and my 16 year old was out and he asked me if I saw the lights and I told him yes that's why I was sitting in the middle of the road. My wife did not see them she was busy on the phone when she pulled up. My son also informed me he has seen these lights before and told us about them but by the time he try's to say anything they are already gone.
