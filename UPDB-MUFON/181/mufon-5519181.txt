4 possible UFOs were hovering over Matteson Illinois on April 22nd, 2012.

I was on my way home from having dinner with a friend of mine when I noticed that 4 bright lights suddenly appeared in the eastern sky. I was just making a left hand turn off of Harlem heading east on U.S Route 30 Lincoln Highway. I was just looking around the sky while driving when I noticed these bright light appearing very quickly. I new that these objects were nothing like I have ever seen in my life because of how sudden the appeared, and the formations that they were making, as well as how still they were. These objects first appeared in a straight line going from east to west, they stayed like that for at least 15 minutes. Then they all of a sudden started moving to form a triangular formation. I was very shocked and excited to see these unidentified flying objects, that pure adrenaline started flowing through my body. The flying objects suddenly just disappeared in the night sky.
