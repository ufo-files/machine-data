Observed silvery craft directly overhead, with 3 hooded uniform occupants looking down at me over a rail.

During an early hazy misty morning bike ride at the top of a bike trail hill ready to take a run, noticed a shadow pass over me, looked straight up, observed a silvery craft with bottom observation bubble. With 3 dark hooded occupants looking down at me over a rail. The craft slowly moved away. When i returned to the family picnicking, they asked where I had been so long, because the had finished eating a while ago, it seemed like I had about an hour of missing time. Following this childhood event I have had numerous dreams of laying on something and being paralyzed.
