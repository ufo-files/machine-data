{cak} me and my brother was leaving we look to the right at the end of the drive way and seen this ufo hovering

We stayed in a trailer surrounded by a corn field. When I tell ppl about this particular story I tell them that me and my brother was going to the store to get kerosene. Because that's what my dad did sometimes when he got off work. It only made sense to me. When reality is I don't really remember where we went. And don't remember how we got back. But we hop into the car pull down to the end of the driveway. I looked to the right and I said to him"you see that?" Let's go down there. He said "hell no" and turned the other way. And it seemed like when we had gotten we would of told someone but we didn't. Looking back its like me and him acted as if nothing ever happened. But before that I don't know how long before we seen the stationary UFO. We had seen a ball of light fall in that corn field. Don't know if it's any direct correlation. But what I did notice is how fast the military/air force, came in and bought everyone land in that area and how fast they put up a military base. As an adult my brother hates for me to bring it up in front of other friends.But he'll talk to me about it one on one. Truthfully I wish I never seen it. I question everything I feel like nothing matter. Besides my family of course. Sometimes I question if I really seen it. And always trying to put together that whole night. And nothing comes to mind it's frustrating.
