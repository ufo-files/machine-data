I was laying in bed, watching the stars, when the UFO came directly to my apartment building from a water tower far off in the distance. It remained hovering 30ft away from me for about 4 to 5 minutes. The three creatures inside were solid black- two were larger and one smaller. They made no noise and flew back in the exact same trail from which they came, and were last seen when a shroud of clouds swirled in, hiding them. The aliens inside had half watermelon shaped heads and stood 7 to 8 feet tall in height, save the smaller one which was the size of a 10 year old child. They were seated and their hands were stationed on a control table in front of them. I have reason to believe they're monitoring the earth, as well as an understanding that they choose not to land for a simple reason: death. "The monitors", as I've began calling them, see us killing animals, plant-life, even each other, and they know that landing would end in the same way for them. I am willing to take a lie detector test, as this event occurred two more times. I have paintings and sketches.
