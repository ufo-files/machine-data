I heard a jet behind me so I turned around. I didn't see anything so I turned back around. And out of the blue right in front of me was a weird looking, large triangular object. It was about 3-4 ft big moving from the E to NW. The weird thing was how slow it was moving and how low. And the object had a whitish blue light and nothing else. Thinking it must've been a weird looking jet I watched as it passed. It flew right over my head. As it did, I looked up to watch and I saw that the object was black or grey and had only one light. The Whitish blue light was coming out the bottom of the object and looked like fire or a flame or something. I'm pretty sure it wasn't a jet.
