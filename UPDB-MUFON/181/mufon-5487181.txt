{nd} Car left front dipped down and was slid parallel into next lane at 55 mph

I was in my car in left lane of two traveling at 55 mph when suddenly my left front fender dropped down and bottomed out then my car was pushed perfectly sideways into right lane as I reacted with a left turn of wheel whith sounds of tires screeching .When I realized I had been pushed I looked into rear view mirror and could not understand what I was looking at. I saw a clear or transparent 20' orb in median that was rippling and distorting scenery behind it with a 4' tall 3' wide egg shaped sphere inside just a few feet above median. Inside of inner sphere were large bright slow wiggling electrical bolts spagettied together. As soon as the clear orb stopped wiggling everything was transparent or invisible .
