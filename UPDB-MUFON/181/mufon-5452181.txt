On 20th May 2020 at 22.33 i was returning to my flat and turned to look at Pendle Hill it is part of the local senary. It is well known as witch country, My son has a good view of the hill from his bedroom. so I called him into his room to take a good look. He was stunned. We get a lot of Planes and helicopters going over our area. My youth was spent in the Air Training Corp and was one of the aircraft recognition team, I have been in Military aircraft and I am sure this was no civilian and military aircraft.
I took 3 pictures while I was outside. The light in the photo seems to have a core once I looked at it closer. There was no noise at all. It was very quiet due to the time of night and of course the Covid factor. In the phot you can see it just above a cloud bank.
