Approx 3 weeks ago there was a violent thunderstorm here in SW Fl. I was awoken by the thunder. I got dressed and went outside to check on our animals, it was raining as well. The time was approx 4-05 am. I started walking and there was a very large lighting strike in front of me, I stopped and another lighting strike struck, this time it lit up the sky in front of me and that is when I saw this large object approx 500 ft up about 90 degrees above the horizon. The object was glowing a blueish light when the lighting hit very close to this object. This object was visible for approx 1.5 to 2 seconds. Once the strike was over there was nothing to see but some clouds. This object has contours to it, and you could see the outlines in the hull. I was stunded at first to see something like this, but I know what I have witnessed. It has been several weeks and I did not tell any of my family members as of yet. This is the first time I am reporting this event to anyone,thank you.
