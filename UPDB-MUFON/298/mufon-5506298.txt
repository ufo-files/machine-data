{nd} Single pulsating white light, when it was off, pale blie light.

I was coming home from grocery shopping at Giant on Maple ave when I first saw it. It was a single medium brightness white light that was pulsating. When the white light was turned off it was a small bluish light, pale blue, then, the light would grow into a brighter larger white light, then the white light would shrink back to the small bluish white light and grow again to bright white light... I noticed it to the east and it was moving southeast to northeast. At first, I thought is was a plane or helicopter. Something was different about it, so it caught my attention and I kept looking at it. I also followed it. At this point, I no longer thought it was a plane or helicopter but a UFO. So, instead of going home, I followed it for a few minutes until I couldnt follow it anymore. I went to school road park and it flew over the trees and I lost it. I decided to go back home, so I kept driving straight on school road park to turn onto Orville road and head back home. To my amazement, there was the object again, now visible over a clearing... however, it was NOT going in that direction before I lost it. It would have had to have jumped to get to that new position in the sky from where I last saw it to be where I saw it now. Weird. Anyhow, it flew over route 309 and I couldnt follow it any longer. I wanted to get it on video with my phone, but as I was fumbling with my phone, I didn't want to take my eyes off of the object, I didn't want to miss anything. It happened rather quickly and I didn't have chance to catch it on video with my phone. For now on, I will have my phone ready for these things.
