Object observed was very large and cigar shaped, not shaped like a football or a blimp. I had gotten up from sleep to put the dog out to wee, as he was whining. I was standing on the front drop steps waiting for the dog when I saw a very large object flying towards us at low altitude. I grabbed the dog and put him inside. I watch the object coming closer. It had 3 lights. a small white light at the front and rear and a very large spot light pointed straight down a mid ship. The object was very large, maybe 200 ft or more. I could not make out any details, it was just a large dark object against the night sky, which was overcast. The spot light passed directly over me. It was blinding bright. I was not frightened, it was not a scary situation. the object was traveling North to south at about 7 mph at maybe150 200 feet up. I was trying very hard to see and remember details as I thought this was extraordinary as it move off several hundred yards I considered following it. But I was only dressed in underwear and thought it would be very odd if I was pulled over undressed and I did not think there was time to get dressed. 
Very few things I regret in life but deciding not to chase the object is one of them.
