{tjd} A grey metallic object hovering to the north moving southwest

Around 7:30 I went out to smoke a cigarette and saw a grey object in to the north. At the time I didn’t think much of it possibly balloons or planes etc. later in the evening I went out again after dark the object was in the same general area. At this time the sun has set and the object was only visible as a light. It was bobbing and pulsating but didn’t seem to move anymore than a few inches. I chalked it up to a satellite until looked over toward the constellation Orion and watched a satellite pass over. I came out an hour later and it was on the other side of the sky. Still bobbing and pulsating. My girlfriend and I both photographed the object multiple times. After the flash of the first camera the object seemed to come toward us. As we took more photos our cameras started to malfunction. As of filing this report It is 1am and I just checked for the object once more before bed. It’s still out there and now it’s in another spot still bobbing around.
