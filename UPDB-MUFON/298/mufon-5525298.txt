*Object changing colors comes down from sky then hovers and moves slowly.

I've recently seen several unexplainable objects in the sky when walking my dogs at night so I've been periodically checking out the sky. About an hour ago I watched as what appeared to be a twinkling star slowly descended over about 30 minutes time. The object got close enough for me to see it changing colors red, blue, white, yellow, & green.The lights appeared to reflect off of something above them but I couldn't see a definate structure. At first I thought it was a star but I've never seen star get this big or change colors like this. I originally seen the object to the west, then it started slowly moving north. The object is still out there RIGHT NOW! I've been seeing objects like this for about a month now in between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m. They don't show up every night but have been around 4-5 nights a week! It may be explainable but I've never seen any kind of aircraft move this slow and stay airborne as well as change colors like it is. Conventional aircraft blink a red and green light but these aren't blinking, they change from one to the other without stopping or dimming. Unfortunately the digital camera I own won't pick up the lights in the sky nor will my cellphone.
