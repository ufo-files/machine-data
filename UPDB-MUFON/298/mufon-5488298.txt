{cak} multiple objects on same flight path

My wife and I were sitting in our back yard. We always look at the sky as we have DFW airport about 8 miles away. My wife noticed a glowing object rising from the south. At first I thought it was a plane or helicopter but as it got closer it was moving way too fast for either. We watched it as it moved north and went to the front yard, where we saw 3 more rise from behind the house. We went to the back yard as it was less light and tried to film them with my ipad, which we did get a few good shots of them. They moved in basically a straight line but blinked the smaller ones faster than the larger ones. As we watched before they all passed over, what i would describe as a fleet rose from the south, about 8-10, some moving faster than others. We have seen many things in the night sky but glowing blueish green orbs are a first, we do have several on video.
