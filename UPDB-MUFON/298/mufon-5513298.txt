{dr}6 lights flying in V pattern over 191st headed E / SE towards tweeter and 57

Was driving on 191st street heading east at around 90th avenue when I first saw it. It appeared to be approximately around Harlem avenue at the time. It continued East over Tweeter and Carmax and eventually towards 57 where I lost sight. At first it looked like several airplane lights but they werent moving that fast and were in a distinct v pattern like geese migrating. It was also not moving fast enough to be planes. Possibly helicopters but I couldnt hear any noise and the lights were fairly large. Took pics but was driving at night when I did it. The quality is poor. You can see 3 of the lights in the bottom right hand corner. The snowy objects are my dirty windshield and the 3 white squares are my vehicle stickers. The object was about out of range when I took it.
