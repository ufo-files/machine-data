{rjl} A group of teenaged girls observed lights overhead which moved erratically. The number of lights did not change.

I have been wanting to report this for sometime.Members of a girls' camp, Sylvania of the Rockies outside of Bailey, CO, were returning to their cabins late one evening in 1962.Someone in the group saw the lights overhead, slightly to the south,and pointed them out to the rest. We all thought it was a UFO. The lights stayed in the same general area, but moved about erratically. All of us were convinced we we'd seen something from another world. We watched them for about 20 minutes. Scared, we reported it to the camp's highest ranked person. Said person,after about 20 minutes, said she had called the Air Force and was told the AF was doing exercises. Knowing we'd never be told anything else, and knowing what we saw was NOT US aircraft, which couldn't move like the lights did, we retired for the night.
Strangely, we did not talk about the event after it happened, but if I remember it happening, other girls must have, too. I had another UFO sighting the following year, in Casper, Wyoming, which I will report separately. After the camp leader told us about the AF and when we were walking to our cabins, the UFOs were not overhead any longer.
