July 26 and 27, 1977 Orange Triangular sighting at Campsite 7 of Camp Craig at Camp Boxwell.

On July, 26 and July 27, 1977, I was a Boyscout of Troop 158 attending a one week summer camp session at Camp Boxwell. We were at camp site 7 or Camp Craig. At the time, Camp Craig was the newest camp on the Boxwell Reservation. Camp site 7 was a short distance from the shore of Old Hickory Lake. Shortly before we were about to turn in, our Scoutmaster started to give us an impromptu lesson on astronomy. In the middle of his lesson, a large orange triangle came gliding from the North West direction over the opposite shore of Old Hickory lake. It was silent. Over our campsite, the object pivoted and took off in towards the east. None of the members of our troop got much sleep that night.

The next night was overcast. I was not quite dark yet. The small craft shop that ran below the cafeteria was still open. I was at campsite seven and our scoutmaster was with some of our troop at the craft shop working on a leather project. We noticed that the clouds over Hickory Lake lit up with an orange color. The same craft glided over Hickory Lake. We watched it do a right hand pivot and then descend in a zig-zag path. We did not see it land and do not know if it landed. Several members of our troop ran to the craft shop to inform our scoutmaster. My scoutmaster and I got into his car to see if we could observe anything from the Highway 109 bridge going North towards Gallatin, TN. There seemed to be a dock near the bridge that was lit by a string of lights. This was the only thing we saw from the bridge.
