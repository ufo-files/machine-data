Flashing white star-like light

I was sitting outside, it was very clear out I was watching the stars when I noticed a flashing white or very pale blue light that looked almost identical to the stars, but it was flashing. At first it moved in a horizontal line, then it would move up or down slightly before continuing horizontally. It hovered two separate times the first time was only for a second then it continued flying horizontally, the second time it stayed hovering for a couple minutes before the light started fading and ultimately disappeared right before my eyes. It was not moving further away, it was hovering while fading away.
