{cak} Object traveled a path in less than 2 seconds that most jets take 15 seconds or more to make

At about 3:30 am, I was sitting in a chair in front of my house. I had been scanning the night sky watching for shooting stars. I thought the object was a light from the ground. However, it moved through the sky much further and faster than any ground based light I'd every seen in the night sky. I noticed an oval shapped object about 20 degrees up in the eastern sky. I watched as the object, which looked like a misty red tear drop, raced across from the east to the south east at a high rate of speed. It cleared a distance it takes most jet liners 10-15 seconds to cross. It had no other markings or lights, just looked like a misty red oval moving at a high rate of speed through the sky. It continued to the south east very quickly and was gone.
