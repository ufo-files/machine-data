Saw 1

Was outside having a fire on property looking at the night sky and my wife and I were fixated on one "star" it was brighter than the rest but had a dimmer star almost directly below it. As we were looking at it, all of a sudden it became way brighter and start moving east as it was doing this 3 more fainter objects appeared and started circling the brighter object for roughly 5 minutes. The bright one went near its original position but would move periodically over the next couple hours and we also witnessed the smaller objects flying in straight paths over the same period.
