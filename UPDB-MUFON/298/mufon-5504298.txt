{cak} saw ufo form then leave

Nov. 2013, I was looking for "ison" around mars area. Had 10x50 binoculars, when a cloud like " poof" similar to someone puffing out cigar smoke caught my eye.I have seen these in the past through my lx 200 8", but thought it was just my eyes& the light playing tricks. This time it was a lasting cloud like dim bluish/ grey glow. Then it seemed to compact into a tighter area, & became brighter and more white. It all seemed to go slower now,& descended. It then became oval and took on an orange color, shape like the classic saucer.amber/orange sparks were falling/trailing from benieth it. A small tourquose ball formed next to it, went behind the oval, came around the left side of it and merged with the big shape. There were snaps of all different colors coming from this thing, mainly from the outer edges. No pattern, just all over, haphazard like. It shot to the right about 2 hand widths. Wiggled left to right and back for a bit, then the far side of it seemed to raise up, a black arrow shape appeared toward the center, it flashed brightly once, the next flash, it vanished, or took off! I thought about thisfor a long time. All I could figure out is it may have been meteoric powder( iron or rock?) Heated, polarized, drew together, and took off toward it's strongest pull from the earth. The whole thing lasted 2 minutes tops. Cool though!
