UFO comes without making a sound, and leaves waking up whole house

It was just after 12 midnight and I was laying in bed with my girlfriend, who was already asleep, as I was just about to go to sleep, I was incredibly startled by a defining vibration/rumbeling in the sky directly over the house. By the time I was able to get out of bed and to the open window, it had professed to making the sound of some sort of winding and whirling, sounded like as if I was standing on the runway when a double decker jet was taking off, it got faster sounding by the second, when I got to the window, all I could see was the area in front of my house slightly lit up, and was only able to hear the object ascending directly up into the sky as if it was moving at 1000s of miles per hour. My brother and his girlfriend were able to hear the object as well, but were both in 2 seprate rooms with no access to windows facing the direction of object. When we went outside to investigate, we had noticed that the tree branches closest to the ground were literaly pushed down anywhere from 3 to 10 feet each, some of them touching the roof of my brothers car. We called the local police and no had reported anything. I would have liked to believe this was an airplane or helicopter at least, but the way it didn't make a sound coming in, and sounded stationary and no more than 75 ft away as it "turned on" and ascended made me realize it couldn't have been, that as well as the bent branches. I was extremley disturbed at how incredibly close to the house it was, and was frustrated I couldn't get up sooner to cacth a better look.
