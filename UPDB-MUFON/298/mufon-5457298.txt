{tjd} Stationary Lights in the Sky, in 'Cross-like' Formation

On 10/31/19, between the hours of 11 pm and 12 midnight (PST), I couldn't sleep, so, as I usually do, went outside in front, near garage door to look at the night sky, stretch, etc. Looked upward, to my left and saw what *seemed*to be a cross-like formation of lights, the likes of which I've never seen. I thought they were stars or helicopters, but they were perfectly still. One could see three lights, vertically oriented in a near perfect column. On either side of this "column", was a single light a few hundred feet from the column of lights. Very odd. Took a picture, I must restate: I have never seen this 'arrangement' or orientation of lights before. Pic is a bit hazy, but one can see the 'column' of lights. On 11/1/19, will see if they show up again.
