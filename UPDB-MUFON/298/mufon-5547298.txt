Noticed a dull greyish object moving across very fast from S/E to N/W.

I was sitting in my backyard on Sunday the 14th, and looked up and noticed a dull greyish, sphere like object moving across the sky VERY FAST, almost as if it was falling, from S/E to N/W. At first I thought it was a shooting star but there was no trail behind it. Then I thought maybe it was a piece of an aircraft or shuttle, but like I said there was nothing behind it and it was moving quickly. I was looking up often because about 15 minutes earlier I saw what looked like a mylar silver balloon, but it was pretty much stationary, when it did move, it moved slowly, like no wind was moving it. The direction of that object was east to west.
