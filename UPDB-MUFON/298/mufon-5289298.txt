5 bright orange fiery orbs moved across sky

My husband and I saw a bright orange glowing orb that appeared like a radiating flame moving northbound over our into the distance. It was underneath commercial airplane height (as we saw one flying east from the object and much higher). It flew at about the local flight plane path and speed. It felt like it was very close and was extremely bright. After the first moved out into the distance another orb came over our , followed by 3 more about 30 seconds-1 minute apart. They followed identical trajectories and ended up together as a group in the distance. We viewed them with binoculars and could not identify anything recognizable about them. They could best be described as being similar to orange gas nebulas although it seemed they had a center that radiated fire/gas/light outwards into a circular shape. The weather was slightly smoggy/cloudy and appeared to completely clear up after they had passed.
