Triangle, a light on each point and 3 big lights underneath, on bottom.

I watched a large lit up aircraft flying NW to SE. as I drove closer, it went over me and the road. I stopped my car, rolled down the window and shut the car off. I looked up to the left out my window and identified a triangular craft with a light on each point and 3 big bright lights shining underneath on the bottom, shining down. Also it was silent, and it was a very quiet night.
The craft then changed direction and started coming back my way, towards me I thought, and I’m the only one around. It seemed rather close, 500 to 1000 ft. up. I estimate it was 30 or 40 ft. long. 
I was about to take a picture when I realized it was coming close to me. I got spooked and drove off.
