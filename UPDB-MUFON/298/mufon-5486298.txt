{dr}Red-orange orbs.

Red orange orb....very bright. Move slow,then popped up 10 or so feet from where it was. It was dropping smaller orbs from it. You could call them fire balls but, I prefer orbs. My son recorded it and saw more orbs on the video than what we saw with the naked eye. I have seen this a couple times in about three years. My other son and friend saw a show of orbs about three years ago. These orbs always appear in the same area. Very neat.
