Family of about 20 camping for the evening east of Yuma. A large object was sighted approaching Yuma. Object hovered over Yuma. Seemed to absorb all sound. Took off rapidly to the south to a higher altitude and stopped, turned on a dime, took off again to the west at a higher altitude stopped again, then took off out of the atmosphere. A family member was a US Marine and said the planes would be out after it in 10 seconds. When there was no military response, the family member had us all pack up and leave. The next day the family member returned from base after having reported the incident and indicated that we was "ordered" to not have sen anything.  The new paper reported that a docked ship in San Diego miss-fired a missle while in dock.
