I was 19 went to town over a bar fight my father got in at around 2 am town is 15 min. Away the bar was closed so went back home half way home I seen a bright light across a field about 800 ft across I stopped got out of my car and watched it for about 15 to 20 min. then got a bad feeling and drove off but when I turned on the road home the light falloed me it was just above the tree line I chanced it about a mile down the road then it took off at an angle and was gone in a second when I got home my dad said where the hell were you I said I went to town he said it 5:30 am I didn't tell him what happened and don't know where all that time went I am now 61 have never forgot this and have always wondered where that time went
