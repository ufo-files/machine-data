{rjl} Saw a straight line of lights at a 45 degree angle, in the clouds

I was outside my home when I happened to see a line of 4-5 white/ pale orange lights in the cloud cover in the south, somewhere between 9 and 10:30 pm. I assumed they must have been part of a craft, but I could not see it. It appeared to be about the length of a football field. The lights stayed the same shape, moved a short distance slowly and disappeared. After it disappeared I heard jets. I was telling my brother in law about it and he said that he had seen lights fitting the description at 9:30pm northeast of Circleville, he also said he saw a physical craft and it was moving.
