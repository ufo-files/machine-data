strange flash

I was outside and I just looked up looking at the stars, as I usually do. I stared at the area to the north about 60 Degrees for about 10-15 seconds and I saw what I guessed was a star and as I was staring at it started to get brighter and brighter till It was like headlight or flashlight bright. It held its brightness for about 3 or 4 seconds and just disappeared. In most counts I would call it a shooting star. The problem is it had to have been coming directly towards me, as it made no change in direction.
