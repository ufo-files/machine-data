UFO Sighting Over Brisbane from Coorparoo

We witnessed a glowing object that pulsated and fell from the sky in the NW. We were watching from the back of the apartment complex and the object that was a beautiful green then red and back to green colour moved from NW, to the north and fell like a leaf in the sky then started to shift to to the west. It passed in front of Jupiter and moved further west to the SW in an arc across the sky. It suddenly made a climb of some altitude then plummeted down and hovered, then a flash of 2 more orbs happened very suddenly off to the NW just past were Jupiter was shining. It appeared to be inside our atmosphere and completely unaffected by gravity. The sighting lasted 10 + minutes.
