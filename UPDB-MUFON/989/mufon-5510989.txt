3 orbs came down at 45 degree angle. white on the way down, orange or red on the way up.

i was looking out of my landing ( top of the stairs ) window with my son, feeling a bit anxious as i don`t like thunder storms.i live in a place called cwmbran (south wales), and the orbs seem to come down close to pontypool. there was a lightning strike at the same time as the 3 orbs came down, my son and i saw it, i shouted to my partner to come upstairs, but by the time she got to the top of the stairs the orbs had gone back up at exactley the same angle, but had changed colour. they were probably bigger than a car. it took about 45 seconds for them to come down and go back up i think. the thing that sticks in my mind is the change of colour from white to red/orange. i dwelled on calling the police for a while, but after thinking about trying to explain it, i changed my mind. not long after a police helicopter over newport ( very close to cwmbran ) chased a ufo. not much was said about it in the local papers. i`m only a normal person and cannot really guess because of the distance, but i would probably say that they were travelling over 500 mph (very unsure though) but they were very fast. hope this helps.
