{rjl} 8 orbs acended slowly from city then were chased by a helicopter

Myself and 3 other friends were bbqing in the back yard when one of my friends told us to come look at some strange lights slowly acending from what looked like the city scape.. Now it's important to note that the night was also being lit up by fireworks in celebration of Pioneer Day.. These were absolutely not fire works! The where hovering slowly then changing direction , and even some of them were moving in sync with each other. One by one they were acending up into the sky.. Then out of no where 2 helicopters headed straight in their direction , as the choppers approached the objects one by one they started to phase out.. As some phased out , more showed up shortly after and the helicopters would swing back around and then they would phase out again! This happened for about 15 mins before they all completely disappeared.. It was amazing to watch !
