At about 8:10PM I was looking in the sky from my location at Hammon Lane and Sands Drive and observed two very fast objects in the sky approaching from North to South the first one was way ahead of the second one and then the second one caught up to the first one then I observed they what looked like parallel to each other and hovering for about 40 seconds then the first object took and and abrupt turn East at an and angle and headed for the clouds above then immediately the second one soon followed up into the clouds, I have no pictures of this sighting because at first I thought they might have been helicopters but was soon to observe they were not from their actions. Then as I continued to look in the same direction of the first sighting North To South over the California 405 fwy, I observed 5 more objects in formation over the same area as the first sighting but later forming in a pattern like a constellation of which I immediately took pictures of, I noticed on my IPhone the location of this pattern sighting was over Rancho Street and the 405 Fwy . The objects seemed to hover for about 5 minutes slowly moving Eastly then one by one at an angle shot like a rocket into the cloud coverage then they were gone,I did try to take a video that didn’t come out but what what I did see in the clouds at that time of day was one of the objects just hovering and 2 hovering around it for unknown reasons.
