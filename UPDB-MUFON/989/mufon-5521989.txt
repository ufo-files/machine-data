Gray, rectangular craft hovering, perhaps moving slowly over Mesquite, TX

I was driving west bound on I-20, just south of Mesquite around noon when i saw an unknown size, grey, rectangular object to the North/ North West. My view of it was side-on. When I saw it, passengers in the car with me believed it was a blimp, but i knew that it was too big, and of the wrong shape. I know it was at a distance of 1-3 miles, as it was sort of hazy, about like the Dallas skyline was at that distance. The object appeared three-dimensional, but its width i am not sure of. It didn't seem to have any lights. The sighting ended as I moved toward my destination in a neighborhood with trees and two-story houses.
