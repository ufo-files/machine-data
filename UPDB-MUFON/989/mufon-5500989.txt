{cak} Two bright lights side by side . Then separated and disappeared.

We were getting ready to watch the super moon come over the eastern horizon. I noticed two bright white lights coming for the east heading west. They were traveling at a high speed . I thought it was a plane banking toward the south coming right towards us. Then all of a sudden the lights separated and one turned south and then disappeared .The other light turned to the north and disappeared. It was a clear evening and no other planes could be seen in the area.
