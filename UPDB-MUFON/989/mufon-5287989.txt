Lights flying above

Spotted what looked like a airplane. Started looking through binoculars to see it. After spotting in the binoculars the object turned right and circled back. It made a close pass by maybe a 100-200 yards away. The object looked like it would turn on a small white light and point it me. This happened almost constantly. Red lights visible constantly. At first the object flew in a very precision way. It made a move that reminded me of a music note. Than it seemed as if it was watching me and engaging with me. At one point the object came straight toward me and seemed to almost stop above me. It had a large white light on the bottom. It scared me . I could not believe what was happening. I could not figure out if it was a drone or something else. If it was a drone, we have extremely good pilots and the drones have improved 100 fold. I don't know what i witnessed tonight but it was like nothing i have ever seen. It made a noise up close it sounds like a putter. It had Red, white, green, amber lights. But most striking was the large white light on the bottom and how it felt very real that this thing was engaging me.
