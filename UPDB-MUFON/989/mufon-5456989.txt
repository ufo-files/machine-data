Two objects in sky during Northern Lights

I reviewed some footage that I had recorded on my GoPro of the Northern Lights and found 53 consecutive images with these objects. I would believe them to be satellites, but I had never seen a satellite so close to the ground. Furthermore, they seemed to meet each other and combine into one before they disappeared from my lens.

The first object is horizontal and is on the right side of the photo, traveling east. The second object is mostly vertical and is in the top left of the photo, traveling towards the horizon.

I have included the original photo followed by an enlarged photo of each, as well as a progression of their movement. Please let me know if these are indeed satellites! Thank you!
