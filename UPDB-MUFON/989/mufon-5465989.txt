My friend and I was riding around in the country and we stopped to check out road because there was recent flooding. We got out of vehicle and we felt we was being watched. I looked up and instant fear hit me. It was like a floating Hershey kiss shape. Next thing I know it is dark and late at night and was put back in my car. I remember taking off in my car but I don't remember starting car and I was driving as fast as I could. I dropped off my friend and I remember not talking. She states she don't remember anything.
