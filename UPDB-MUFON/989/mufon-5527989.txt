triangle shape moving very, very slowly over keenesburg, co

i was driving home from denver ( i am a truck driver) on I-76 eastbound,i was passing through keenesburg, it was already dark, when i noticed a triangle shaped object moving very very slow, no noise. The points of the triangle were gently rounded and there was 1 light at each point for a total of 3. It was not a plane, too slow moving, and i have never seen a helicopter that shaped. I only noticed it because of the light formation and it was not very high up in altitude. It was moving from the northeast to the southwest across I-76. I was a little scared and was trying to look up out of the window, when i realizied what it was and almost went of the road.
