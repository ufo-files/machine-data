(GS) Saw brilliant light that faded to star-like brilliance, then faded and made several maneuvers befor fading out.

Around seven-fifteem P.M.while in hot tub, noticed brilliant (maybe a flash?)of light out corner of eye. I turned to see it quickly fade to north star level of starlight, then quickly drop down and to the left of its position. It stopped abruptly, then shot over to a place beneath its original position. It began fading somewhat and was difficult to see until it re-appeared in itsoriginal position.It then drifted slowly southward until it was further down than it had been on its first drop, then faded out. Within about ten minutes of this event, a meteorite streaked across the relatively the same quadrant of sky.My husband noticed some of this as well. We were also not under any chemical, alcohol, or other influences at the time.
