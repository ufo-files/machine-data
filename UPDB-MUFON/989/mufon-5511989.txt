{dr}saw 5 fireballs in the sky at one time--then one by one they disappeared

Approx 9:15 on 3-20-13 while driving over I-70 going North bound on 291 highway, The driver of the car (not me), said, look at that! Off to his Left, out the window I could see 5 discs" looked orange, like I have heard fireballs described. They were moving from North to South very slowly and one by one would disappear. We drove to the corner of Hidden valley road and 291 highway at the Vets office and saw 2 more of these coming from the North. They staggered and didn't seem to have any exact time as to when the next one appeared. We saw the first 5, then 2 then 1 several times. I tried to get a picture but they were to far away. Bigger than the stars I was looking at. Ea one would kinda start after the other ones disappeared We watched for about 45 minutes and decided to see if we could tell where they started from but then they stopped.
(they did not shoot into the sky... they just appeared... it was weird
