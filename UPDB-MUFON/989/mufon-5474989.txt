{rjl} Fast moving orb at around 3000 ft in the sky

I was sitting in my office on the second floor in my home. I have a window in front of my computer screen, and I saw a fast moving orb moving towards the SE. There was no streak or comet tail, and it definitely wasn't a plane. It was moving way too fast for that. I noticed an airplane flying at roughly 32,000 ft further the South, and I know it wasn't anywhere near that high up. It had to be roughly around 3,000 ft up to be as small as it was, but shined brighter than any star in the sky. I witnessed another orb in my early 20s in Michigan, and that one dropped out of the sky straight down. That orb was at least 20 miles out, and dropped light someone dropped a flash light. This orb wasn't that far out, but reminded me of the same orb I witnessed back then. Its only been about 20 minutes or so, and there might be more witnesses coming forward soon.
