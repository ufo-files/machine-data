I was on Route 95 south around 6:30 am on 10/28/11. Just leaving Baltimore City I was on 95 south for about 8 minutes. I looked left out my window and saw a bright orangish-white orb making strange movements. It was moving in a "z" pattern. The orb then just stopped and hovered for about a minute. Then suddenly the orb just dropped out of the sky straight down in an amazing speed. It dropped all the way below the tree lines on the state park to my left. I could not see where it went next. Around a minute later I saw a strange "cigar" shape object cross directly in front of me on 95 south coming from the same direction the orb dropped. At first, I thought it was just a plane because BWI airport is near the location. But just when I thought it was just an airplane the object suddenly either turned into or turned-on light switch that lit up the whole object. It was so bright i had to turn away for a second. The crazy thing was when I looked up again, I no longer saw the cigar shaped object but saw a bright orange orb in its place. As I watched in awe as the object flew out of sight like a light switch was flicked again the orange orb went out and in its place was the cigar-plane like object again. I was speechless.
