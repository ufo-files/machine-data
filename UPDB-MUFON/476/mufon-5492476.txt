{rjl} Saw one then multiple objects in northern sky. they moved in right angles in burst of speed then stopped

Early 1960's in south Kansas City,mo. I was playing catch in a neighbors back yard. I looked up to catch the baseball,when I saw a silver object come out from the clouds and hoover. My best friend and I watch it for maybe 30 seconds. We then ran to front his house where our moms were talking on the driveway. We were excited and told them what we saw flying saucers and to come look. we ran to the back yard and pointed it out. It went into the clouds and now 4 objects re-emergered and hovered in the sky. The objects then proceeded to move in formation at right angles, darting and stopping. Playing in the sky for several moments. Then at once they zoomed out sight moving to the east in a blink of an eye! With in a few moments. A jet from then Richards-Gibaur AFB chugged across the sky in pursuit! Our moms were shaken and in disbelief! My mom never spoke of the incident and refused to acknowledge that it happened when I asked her about it. For years I kept to myself. I only found out 5 years ago that my brother was sitting on the front stoop with a female teenager of the home and both also saw the same thing! I knew what we saw was not manmade! The objects performed at a level that is still beyond our technology! I remember seeing an article in the KC Times or Star attributing the sightings to "reflections" off of buildings downtown! I never believed that was feasible. I saw something to this day i can not explain!
