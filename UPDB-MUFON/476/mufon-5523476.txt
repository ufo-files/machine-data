The UFO was made up of about 5 white lights in a circle with a red light in the middle.

I was at football practice in Raytown (Little Blue Trace ball fields) and there were these weird lights in the East that asended up from the tree line and flew North across the sky changing their path as they got farther away from the tree line. At the time, it was almost ignored until the coach noticed them and said, "Look, a UFO!" I was very suprised at the time but they disappeared behind the taller trees before I could tell what it was.
