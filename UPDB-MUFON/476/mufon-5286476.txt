Fast light

It started out as a slowly moving light that caught my attention - no larger than a star from my perspective. I wondered if it was an airplane or satellite, so I kept my eyes on it. It was creeping upward in a diagonal fashion.

Suddenly, it veered off to my right (I was facing west) and darted across the sky out of view. It was brighter as it performed this maneuver, so that my eyes could easily track it as it moved. It did not go in a perfectly straight line but changed its trajectory slightly about halfway through. It took but a fraction of a second to traverse half the sky.
