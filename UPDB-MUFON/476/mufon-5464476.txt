{cak} First seemed very bright and close like giant star then thru telescope looked like silhouette of disc glowing orange and blue light

I woke abruptly to terrible thoughts (very uncharacteristically), went outside and saw what looked to be a star of david type situation. It was so bright (white light) and seemed so close. It was stationary in the NE sky just above the tree-line for about ten minutes. Invigorated, I went and got the telescope and it had hoovered a bit higher and more toward the east. I saw through the lens what looked like the silhouette of a disc (kind of a crescent moon shape); underneath (lining the curve) it was all aglow with bright orange and bright white-blue light. It did not move abruptly but rather illusive gaining altitude and depth toward the SE over lake Erie. I watched it for about an hour as it grew smaller until it was just a spec in the far SE sky. When the sun fully rose, with blue sky all around I could no longer see it.
