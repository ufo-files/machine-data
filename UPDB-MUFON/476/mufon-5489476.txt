{dr}4 lights in the sky over Imperial Beach

My daughter and I where in the backyard, and out of thin air, a light came on, then another and another until 4 lights were visible. Once the 4th light came on, they all rotated counter clockwise. The 4th one moved into position to form a 3 light line, and the 1st was at a point to form a triangle. Once the 4th moved into position, they all took their turn turning off the light.
