{rjl} Flashing lights

About 7 pm I was called to the backyard by my son and his girlfriend they said hurry! They're pointing to the sky yelling UFO, UFO! Moving from left to right, south to north, over the golf course are three flashing light that seemed to be bouncing around the sky until the craft shifted or turned and you can see the shape was a triangle and the lights seemed to be connected. We followed it across the sky as it seemed to turn and head behind my neighbor's home. I ran to the front and out the RV gate to the edge of the driveway when my neighbor's sensor light when off in my face and I couldn't see as I was blinded by the light, the other witnesses still in the backyard watching from the corner of the northeast corner then joined me in the front where they said they could still it faintly until it faded from view, but I never saw it again.
