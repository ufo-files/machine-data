A rectangle box shaped craft

I was walking down the road and looked up. Right off the edge of the road just above the tree line about 75 to 100 feet above me were two rectangle amber lights about 4ft X 10ft, that were separated about 50ft apart and parallel to each other. The lights were not very bright and I tried to determine if it was just a reflection off a cloud but it became clear that these were the source of the light, and not a reflection. As I stared at it longer and my eyes were able to adjust there seemed to be a blacker than black outline of a rectangle box shape surrounded by a light mist that the lights were mounted on. I could not see the complete object because of the trees, only what I will refer to the back end of the object. The object appeared to be 50ft wide and 10 to 15ft thick. The amber lights were mounted in the bottom corners of the object with the short sides at the backside and the long sides pointed to what I believe the front of the object.

I starred at the object for about a minute trying to hear if there were any sounds or any static effects, there were none. After a moment or two, the object slowly but steadily pulled forward exposing a bit brighter sky and a slight fog roll as it moved. The tree tops did not respond to the movement of this object and the mist quickly dissipated.
