Slowly glided across the face of the Moon

Looking at a Full Moon, noticed something unusual, a dark spot at the top so I got the Binoculars for a closer view. A black unusual object! Strange in shape seemed to glide infront of the face from right to left then disappeared. I remember this black object so clear against the white face of the Moon.
