We watched 9-12 Flying Saucer's

I was 8 yrs old, when a friend of my father ([name removed/cms/rjl came to visit. When dad opened the door he told dad and I that were a bunch of flying saucer's flying around outside. The 3 of us went out side, and what I remember, is there was so many saucers, and I was trying to count how many there were. It was like they were putting on a performance for us, they would get in a straight line then they would drop straight down, then immediately shoot right straight up again, then they would start chasing each other like they were playing a game. Finally I got tired of watching them after about 5 mins.or so, and all of a sudden they shot off going towards the NW. After they left I can remember dads friend, saying to him that there was no way that our Air Force had that type of airplanes. Dads friend left, and dad an I went back inside. My dad was a Minister, and he and I never ever talked about what we had seen and had watched that day. It has taken me 73 yrs to be able to share this with anyone. Looking back on this experience, I have felt it was a blessing, and I can finally share with people that know that there are real.
