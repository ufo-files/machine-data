{nd} Glowing orb, first moving slowly, then multiple abrupt directions changes at incredible speed, then accelerated to unbelievable speed across horizon

I was sitting in my friends back yard, I saw what I thought was a very bright slow and low plane moving slowly, it then changes directions at an acute angle multiple times about 5 or 6 direction and speed changes traveling about 1/4 mile each time before accelerating at speeds I thought we're not possible, from almost close to me, all the way across the horizon instantly and it was gone, the whole thing lasted about 1.5-2 minutes. My friends I was with we're not there/not paying attention, and as far as I know I'm the only one who saw it. I don't know what it was, it seems impossible but it changed how I see our place in the universe.
