{jpg} Object chasing ULA Atlas V rocket

Living on Florida’s east coast we enjoy watching rocket launches from Cape Canaveral. Last night the Atlas V rocket flew south along the coast, glowing brightly in the clear night sky. We usually remain outside to hear the delayed rumble from liftoff which takes a few minutes to reach us 50 miles south of the Cape. While waiting, still watching the rocket, listening to the live feed, we noticed an object similar to a small plane, flashing the usual lights, chasing after the rocket. It was much too fast for an airplane. At this moment ULA announced the Atlas V speed went from 5000mph to 10,000mph still visible to us with this blinking object practically keeping up. We only had binoculars, no cameras, only our eyes.
