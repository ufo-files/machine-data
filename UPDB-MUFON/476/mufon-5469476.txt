{td}Small light (1ft diameter) appeared in living room, disappeared with a mechanical whirring sound witnessed by my wife and I. Hovered briefly a few inches away and disappeared

My wife and I were sitting in the living room one night after I got off of work. I was on the internet reading news and she was doing something on her phone. I was 6 feet away from her sitting on a chair facing north and she was sitting on the couch facing east. Suddenly out of nowhere, a light about 1 foot in diameter appeared within inches of her face, hovered for 5 seconds and seemed to dim and brighten slightly. It then disappeared into thin air, literally faded away as the light dimmed off and made a mechanical "whirring" or humming sound as it went back to whatever plane of existence it came from. It seemed mechanical in nature. We both witnessed the phenomenon and were left very perplexed and haven't talked to anyone about it, we have only discussed it with each other. Any ideas of what that could've been?
