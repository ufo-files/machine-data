{rjl} bright light seemed to be following us when we went to walk the dog

we went out to walk our dogs and heard what sounded like a weird hum or a jet-like noise. we looked up and saw this disc shaped light coming closer to us pretty fast. we thought a plane or helicopter may be in some kind of trouble and because our yard looks like an empty field it was probably trying to use our yard to land..... but then the sound stopped and the light disappeared and then reappeared with no noise. it was close enought hat you could see a rose gold/yellowish-ink color. very pretty. it seemed to be hovering over us or the field (we arent too sure). the light made movements that no man-made aircraft could make. it was gliding side to side and up and down very smoothly and way too fast and bright for it to be a drone. at times it was very still over us just pulsating and glowing really bright. after about what felt like the longest minute ever of this stare off between us and the light i decided to record. i was able to film about 9-10 seconds of the encounter before the light disappeared and a plane flew by almost immediately after. we were kind of confused and figured the noise and lights had to be from the plane. i didnt think much of it until literally this corona virus outbreak happened and something told me to look at the footage from that night.. i uploaded it to a YouTube video because its the easiest way to share it.
