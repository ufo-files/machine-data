LAST SUMMER, WHILE WE WERE DRIVING, WE SAW A TRIANGLE/OVAL SHAPED, DARK,SILENT, SMALL AIRPLANE SIZED, SILENT OBJECT TRAVELING PARALLEL TO US.
I'M NOT SURE WHAT MONTH IT WAS ANYWHERE FROM JUNE TO SEPTEMBER, A SUMMER MONTH. THE TIME WAS 10 PM AT NIGHT. WE WERE ON PATTERSON ROAD NEAR MOTOR AVENUE WHEN MY SON AND I SAW A LOW FLYING, SILENT, DARK, CRAFT FLYING ABOVE TREE LEVEL, PARALLEL TO US. WE WERE LOOKING OUT THE PASSENGER WINDOWS OF THE SUV, SO MY HUSBAND, WHO WAS DRIVING DIDN'T SEE IT, IT WAS HEADED TOWARDS PILOT MOUNTAIN, NORTH. IT CROSSED OVER THE TWO OF US AND WE LOST SIGHT OF IT.
IT HAD A DIM LIGHT ON THE BOTTOM OF IT, A REDDISH/YELLOW COLOR.
I DON'T KNOW IF THIS IS RELATED, BUT RIGHT AFTER THAT WE SAW, IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION, GREEN LIGHT SHOOTING DOWN FROM THE SKY. MY HUSBAND SAID WHAT IS THAT, FIREWORKS? WE HEARD OF NO CRASHES THAT NIGHT.
