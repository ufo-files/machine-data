{dr}Three glowing orbes of light traveling at a slow pace with a trail behind. Speed quickly sped up and disappeared into thin air.

The night of November 4th 2015 at approx. 8pm I looked up to see what was three illuminated lights traveling horizontally at a slow pace. The lights were so bright on the object that they were brighter than your average full moon. Its path left almost like a light trail behind it in the sky. The object quickly sped up and disappeared into thin air. Was A very eri experience and also frightening. If I were to have my phone to try to taken a picture the incident happened so fast that it would have been difficult to capture this event.
