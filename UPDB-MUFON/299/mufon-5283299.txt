I see them every night they are in my bedroom

I see holographic beings since 2014. Some walk as they are floating, some float in the air, while others fly through the air. This happens every night every day. Sometimes, I wake up with my bed vibrating or I feel something stepping on me. Sometime, I wake up and my head is vibrating like buzzing. I’ve tried to make contact and I’ve asked whatever it was to leave. I actually implored them to go away over the years. I thought I was schizophrenic. I don’t see anything else or have any other schizophrenic symptom. Now, there are a variety of different beings that come every night. I’ve seen insects, I’ve seen tall ones with the long necks, I’ve seen little ones, plasmas, weird shaped ones, and strong ones who look like warriors. They stare at me and sometimes I feel a tap or a touch. I can’t understand the plasma forming. I just can’t. My eyes burn when I look too long. I don’t know what to do. I am terrified of the grays. Thank God I don’t physically see them 3D only holographically. I think I would lose it. It’s been seven years of every day they show up at night time.
