Five bright lights in “V” formation that hovered then disappeared one by one.

Mid to late August 1969 in Westport Mass. looking east over the ocean. I looked up and saw five bright lights suspended in a “V” formation  against cloudless afternoon sky. There was no sound. I was stunned and stared at them for several seconds and watched as they disappeared one by one. There were no clouds, They vanished into thin air. 
To this day, I have wondered if anyone else saw this.
