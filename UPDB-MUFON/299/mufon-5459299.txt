{cak} two objects in glowing fog

While traveling south on I85, we observed a cloud/fog with a green glow a few miles ahead at approximately 1000 feet. The car had a sun roof which allowed viewing as we passed under. We were about 5 miles above South Hill.
As we approached. Several cars were pulled to the side of interstate on each side of the road. To include Virginia State troopers. I asked Tim to pull over, so we could see more. He refused.
As we passed under. There were two stationary objects. One over the other, facing 90 degrees in the other direction. It looked like two water towers overlapping. Both 100 to 150 feet in length. There were lights shining down. No movement from the objects.
I kept looking back, the scene remained the same until we traveled out of view.

I asked Tim, why he didn't pull over and he replied he had just been granted a security clearance after 7 months investigation period. I held the same clearance but have always wished we had pulled off the highway.
