bright clear xmas bulb silently clears the treeline and circles me once then shot off

for the past six months i have been going to said address to watch and care for my g-ma. at night before i go to bed, i usually go outside, walk the property, smoke a cig then go to bed. Well that night it was really clear out. so i took a few extra minutes to check out the stars and stuff. It was a gorgeous night that night. about halfway done with my cig, i looked southward because a bright light caught my attention. It looked like a clear xmas bulb. it came from the south, cleared the treeline behind my house. In my field of view( lookin south) it appeared in the south then kinda started to go from my right to my left. As it was doing so it dimmed then brightened 2 or 3 times very slowly. It was absolutely silent! If i went to grab a camera i would have missed it! i was fixated anyway. when it was about to float 100 ft in front of me and slightly to my left, I yelled HEY!!! 3 times and opened my arms up. when i did that, it circled me slowly one time then shot off so fast i couldnt believe it!!! I will admit that i already subscribe to the ancient astronaut theory and i do believe that life must exist elsewhere. This "light" was so silent and dimmed and brightened 3 times very slowly it was about 50 feet of the ground. Now i am a definite believer that we are being visited.
