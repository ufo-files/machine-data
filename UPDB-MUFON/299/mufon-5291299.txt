Blue Orb with glow - quickly ascended

I drove through Mansfield, Ohio on October 30th. I don’t have an exact location but it was somewhere near the jail. I tried to take a picture of the clouds just because they were pretty. I couldn’t get the lighting to work, so I quickly gave up after a few shots. After looking back on the pictures I took, I found a blue orb in each picture. The first picture shows the blue orb with a glow around it floating above a building. In the second picture (1 minute later. Time stamps for proof) it shows that the orb had ascended a great deal within a matter of a minute.
