{nd} blurry sphere, faint blue or white light like a streak ON object. flying on a path and repeated sightings throughout the hours

My friends and I were walking around at ventana ranch community park in Albuquerque, New Mexico. At about 8:40pm on September 9th, one of my friends says "Hey Is that a shooting star, what is that?" I looked and saw a disc shaped object with a blurry white light that looked to be in the shape of a streak. There was no noise, it was below the clouds. It curved right and disappeared. Then a few minutes later it came from right to left. Then it went over the tennis courts and ventana square. I saw it on that flight path twice. I last saw it around 9:50pm
