{rjl} The entirety of Liberty Lake (body of water, not the town named

I was sitting in a house overlooking Liberty Lake (body of water, not the town named "Liberty Lake"), facing three large, south-facing picture window. I was working on my computer, but the picture windows encompassed much of my periphery. Suddenly, the entirety of the lake lit up green for a second, then after a one second pause, lit up red for a second. As in, everything I could see out the window flashed green, then flashed red. The light was ubiquitous and did not appear to come from any direction. I have no idea the source. It was unnerving in how it covered everything I could see out of doors. I heard a slight electrical hum or whir in the background that corresponded with the second flash of light. 

I spoke with another person in the house, who said the power bumped/flickered twice with the same timing as the green and red light I described.
