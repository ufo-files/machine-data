{cak} Husband & Wife Observe Strange Silent Orange-Glowing UFO Light on Multiple Nights, Then at Bedtime Husband Sees 1 Sphere above Treeline Split into 3 Orange UFO Spheres that Rotate Clockwise and Counterclockwise and Form Triangle Before Disappearing.

I think I may have seen a UFO! My wife and I first noticed the odd light just before Thanksgiving 2013 after we moved into our new house in a community behind Pocahontas State Park in Chesterfield County, VA. We would see the light only in the early evening. It was always in the northwest (I checked with a good quality compass). At first I thought it must be a planet because it was so bright and we only would see it in the early evening. Initially I saw no other lights other than the single orange light that would become brighter and flicker. However, unlike a planet the light was not high in the sky, it was above the tree line, and the light was really odd. Then one night on or about January 3, 2014 I was lying in bed just staring at the light and it began to pulsate and grow brighter. Then it split and became three orange lights with each light being at the end of a triangle. The lights were in the same spot as always, the northwest. They didn't move across the sky. The lights pulsated in a clockwise manner for a few seconds and then changed direction for a second to counterclockwise. Planets don't do that! I could hear no noise. Then the three orange-glowing spheres just disappeared. I was just sort of stunned and excited after seeing the event. It's been about two weeks now and I haven't seen them since.
