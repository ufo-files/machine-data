{cak} Observed a stationary orange/amber orb hovering above expressway.

Witness (A) was traveling along I-94 between Mattawan and Kalamazoo when he noticed an orange/amber orb stationary and low to the ground, over the expressway ahead. Then he passed beneath the orb, no noise observed. Witness (A) exited the expressway at S 9th St and made some stationary observations but the orb was no longer visible anywhere in the western sky.
