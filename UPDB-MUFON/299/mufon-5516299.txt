Balloon Sized Shape-Shifting Orange Orbe

I think I saw a UFO going over the Wandsworth Shopping Centre earlier today at 11.40am! It was sort of round & wobbly looking, & going rather slowly (So at first I thought it was a balloon or carrier bag) but then it started to flash a light from it, a red light.

Then I suddenly saw about 4 smaller, bright, yellowish coloured bird-like shapes, flying around near it! They only flew around for a few seconds, then vanished, while the other thing continued to wobble about in shape (Sort of changing) & flashing a red light on it's edge, until I lost sight of it, going towards the Clapham Junction area.

That'd mean it was travelling in a North-Easterly direction over London. weird eh?

I was sitting outside a cafe having a drink when I just happened to look up & see it.
