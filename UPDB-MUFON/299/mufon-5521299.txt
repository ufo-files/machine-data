Very bright light I thought was Venus

While going to work at 6:30 AM, I noticed the moon and what I thought was Venus Close to it. I looked back at the road, then back at what I thought was venus. It dropped a short distance and then made a 45 degree turn and left at a high rate of speed to the southwest.
