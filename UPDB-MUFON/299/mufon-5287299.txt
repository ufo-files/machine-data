1990s Boomerang silent craft over Virginia Beach

I arrived home and walked from my car in the street to the door of my house when I looked up and saw a boomerang shaped craft with lights on the bottom that blacked out all of the stars moving silently overhead, headed east. I would estimate the size at just over one mile in length. This was in the early 1990s, I do not remember the exact year. I did live near the Naval Base but this item was bigger than any stealth aircraft and was very low and completely silent. Ialways wondered if anyone else ever saw the same thing.
