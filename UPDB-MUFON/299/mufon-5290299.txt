Yellow and blue lights with trail

Me and my family were driving around taking photos for painting landscapes as we have been doing almost daily since the start of the pandemic. I wanted to get a picture of this house so I took a series of quick shots out of the window. When I went into my photos to look for a photo to paint I saw 2 photos in a sequence of quick shots where a series of lights with a trail appeared. I considered the notion that it might be an artifact but as the car moved the camera picked up the objects coming behind the trees in one photo and one in the open sky. Also these objects did not appear in any other photos taken either right before or after and no where else in the photos I took.
