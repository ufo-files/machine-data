Photographing a Vertical Cloud - & Saw Outline of a Ship & Hieroglyphs

The day was lovely, blue almost cloudless sky, but then I first saw a strange vertical cloud, it looked like the trail of a rocket from the earth to the sky. Then there were two more looked like planes going across it, it looked crazy so I stopped and I photographed them then drove home, but almost home, again saw another vertical cloud in the distance I got out of my car and took a series of photographs it was only when I looked at the photographs on my phone I was blown away. In one photograph two white dots on the upper left hand side, when blown up, the higher one is clearly metallic! Also there is a distinct V shape pattern at the top of the cloud, when your eyes get used to looking at hte photo you can see the imprint clearly. In another photo you can see halfway up, strange shapes like hieroglyphics close to the vertical cloud, on examination of the other ship photo you can then just make out the same couple of distinct hieroglyphic patterns within it. In this same area near my home, I have seen unexplained circular lights cross the sky fast at night, too fast to photo. I have seen in Leighton Buzzard a huge stationary hovering UFO as I was driving with a friend but could not photo due to traffic etc. There were last year 4 earthquakes in Leighton Buzzard, strange for this area in central england. Something is going on...
