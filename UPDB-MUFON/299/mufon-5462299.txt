BRIGHT GOLD OBBS IN THE NIGHT SKY

I was in my home on Feb. 4 2019 when I saw these 3 orbs on the sky that were glowing. There was 3 when I first noticed them and by the time I got my laptop ready there was only 1. Soon there was 2. I have video of this event. They would fade then reappear for over 10 minutes. The shy had the weirdest shadows of which I have never seen. They faded away and left me dumb founded.
