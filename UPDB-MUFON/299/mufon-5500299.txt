Perfect Disc Observed by Two Sat on Infants Meadow. Acceleratedaway through clouds at great speed

Sitting On the Meadow located behind the Linton infants School,a friend pointed south up in the sky and said; "Look! whats that"?. I followed his finger as he pointed and saw a relatively large disk accelerate away through the clouds at a tremendous speed. My friend said it looked like a "ufo". I could only agree with him.

I only observed the object for a few seconds before it flew through the clouds and the object was far away, However i can recall thinking how perfect almost cliche it looked. A perfect circular disk illuminated my the afternoon sun. I could see it was a slight off-white color / dull grey.

I remember feeling very excited initially upon seeing the object, as it was almost too perfect in the way it looked, and the speed it traveled. i was quite stunned by its elegance as it moved away and through the clouds until it disappeared.
