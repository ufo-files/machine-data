32 objects that looked like satelites traveling NW to SE but disappeared above us.

At approximately 10:05 p.m. 5/3/2020 my granddaughter noticed something moving in the sky coming from the NW heading SE. My daughter and I looked up and told her it was a satelite. Then my daughter notice another one moving through the handle part of the Big Dipper. Then she saw another one following it. We kept looking up and kept seeing more all moving NW to SE and all disappearing basically above us. There were patchy high thin clouds also above us but you could see some stars through them. The Moon is fairly bright as the full Moon is four days away on 5/7/2020. As the "satelites" kept becoming visable in the NW, the flight path was shifting towards the SW but they were all still traveling NW towards SE. The high clouds were thickening up but the "satelites" kept showing up. At around 10:25 my daughter had counted 30 "satelites" so I went inside and pulled up Flight Radar 24 on my phone and it didn't show any planes in our area moving NW to SE. There was one commercial jet, American Airlines AAL 305, going from Miami to Seatle that was NE of us. I took my phone with the Flight Radar 24 outside to show my daughter and she said there was a plane over there and it was going in the opposite direction of the "satelites". She then pointed to another "satelite" and said that is number 32. She saw numbers 31 and 32 when I was getting my phone. I showed her my phone and said yes that the jet was coming from Miami going to Seattle. No satelites visible after they reached a point in the sky above use. We would look SE to see if we could see of them but we didn't. The "satelites" were all the same size and all traveling about the same speed at the same altitude. They all resembled satelites that you see. I have never seen that (32) "satelites" all traveling in the same direction in that short of time of approximately 20 to 25 minutes like this. The clouds were becoming thicker so we stopped looking for them.
