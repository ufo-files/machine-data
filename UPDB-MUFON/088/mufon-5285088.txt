Light flew over and disappeared

I was driving on 10 towards 610 going into Brooklyn park from Blaine. A light came from over my car very fast passing the cars ahead which actually caused them to slightly brake on the highway because it looked like it was going to hit the ground and then disappeared. I thought I was seeing things but then a guy who was behind me followed me off the highway just to ask if I seen what he saw as well.
