{jpg} UFO sighting over entire city of Plainview New York and then NE over rest of Long Island

Was with my mother and sister. First observed craft while driving home. Once home, watched from outside for a while. Sky was entirely dark. No Moon, no stars, no other aircraft. Observed with several neighbors coming out of house a disc like object but observed that there were two rows of lights on the side. Each row had red and white lights that travelled sequentially from left to right. Disc travelled slowly, was very large but totally silent. Disc remaining visable for over one hour. 911 and local radio and television stations were called by many people. First radio broascast advised it was a test craft from Grumman Co. of Bethpage, New York. They did work for government air force. Later this company worked on F-15, F-16 fighter planes and lunar landing craft. However that was much later. The interesting thing was that the next day there was not one report of the occurance in any newspaper, on any tv station or on any radio channel. It was hushed up very quickly and tightly. Still believe though that this was not a man made craft. In the sixities we had not aircraft that had pulsing lights and was dead silent. This was the first time of three subsequent times I became involved with a UFO.
