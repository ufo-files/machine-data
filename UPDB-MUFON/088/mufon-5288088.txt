I saw a mysterious object in a photo I took. I did not notice this at the time I took the photo.

I took a break from my bike ride to take a couple photos of my bike to attach to my workout upload on strava later. When I was looking at the photo later I was checking out the clouds and noticed a dark object. When I zoomed in on the photo it looked like a classic ufo shape to my untrained eye. This object is not present in the second photo I took a couple seconds after the first one.

This photo is located within about a half mile of an airport and within a mile of a military base. Conditions were very windy. For this reason I do not believe it’s a drone as it’s to close for civilian use due to the airport and if this were a military drone it’s flying awfully low in civilian airspace.
