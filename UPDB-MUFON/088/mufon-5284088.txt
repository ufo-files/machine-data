Metallic Disc- hovered then flew off at great rate of speed

Viewed on Easter Sunday approx 2000, give or take a year in Mayfair looking SW toward cemetery. Dull, darker metallic disc hovering for approx. minute or two, then almost instantaneously zipped off.
