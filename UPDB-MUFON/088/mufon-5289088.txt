Saw what initially thought was 2 parachuters.

Was fishing from bank on Mississippi River just North of Lowrey Ave Bridge. Saw 2 objects to the west of me initially thought were 2 parachutes. They were about that size. They didn’t gain or lose any elevation, however, and appeared to be moving same direction. One was higher up and behind the other. No sound was ever made, and they appeared to be following the river from south to north. No lights either and there were aircraft around commercial and a small plane a few minutes after sighting. I took out phone and took video and a few still shots. They eventually left my field of view behind the trees along river, so they were not very high up in the air, certainly lower than the small airplane is saw a few minutes after. After I have “reviewed” my photos/videos I’ve become more intrigued. In some frames a smaller object can be seen taxiing around the larger ones. No clue any information/identification is appreciated!
