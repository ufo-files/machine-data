Bright yellow orb

The 4 of us were sitting by our campfire near the lake in our yard. We were about to go inside because the fire was dying down. The fire was to my far right when I saw a flash of something bright yellow and glowing off to my left (no where near the fire). I turned to watch it. It seemed to hover about 10 -12 feet over the lake for about 2 seconds and about 20 feet off shore. Then it seemed to fly faster in almost straight line toward shore and then quickly vanished like it hit an invisible wall. At first I thought it was some sort of butterfly or something, but it was much faster and larger, and did not flutter like a butterfly would, not to mention that it was very dark out at this point. When I first saw it I wasn't scared at all, just really at peace, like a calmness came over me. After it vanished, I got really scared and asked if anyone else saw it. That's when my daughter (age 15) said she saw it too and described the same thing I had seen...glowing yellow-orange orb. It was the strangest thing I ever saw.
