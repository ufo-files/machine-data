{jpg} 4 witnessess observed a line of lights coming from East then headed NW. After the line they separated more and appeared to make various geometric forms. There were about 15 or 20. They eventually appeared to fly to a higher elevation and disappear.

My daughter was outside watering the tree when she noticed a string of lights coming over the house from the South East to North West. Possibly from Phoenix area to Coachella Valley area. They appeared to be about as high as a plane (approx. 35,000 ft). Neighbors also noticed and stopped. I came outside as well as my other daughter and observed them moving about and forming several geometric type shapes (4-6)then moving about and moving further up in elevation to where they were no longer visible. They moved around slowly and would disappear for a few seconds then reappear in a shape suchas a hexagon. It lasted about 15 minutes and they slowly disappeared. We tried taking cell phone pictures but not much luck. I've never seen anything like this before! It seemed unreal.
