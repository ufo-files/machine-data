I live in the hollywood hills and I was standing out side in my backyard looking north east which faces the 101 freeway when all of a sudden a bright white glowing object appeared out of no direction and zoomed away just as fast as it appeared leaving a sort of streak behind it. Only reason I didn't think it was a shooting star is because it was too low in the sky and was way too bright. I know its close to impossible to tell the distance of such an object but to me it looked as if it was lower than a normal flying plane. I see planes all the time going to and from Burbank Airpot and this was no plane.
