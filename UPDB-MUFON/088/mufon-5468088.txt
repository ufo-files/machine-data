Looked up thought I saw 2 stars till 2 more appeared then they started to move quickly. They were in a diamond formation, then they crisscrossed a couple of times & disappeared. Did not have time to get my phone out of pocket.

It was about 11:50pm on June 5th 2018 Smiths Falls Ontario Canada. I went lastnight to take my dog outside. I usually look up at the sky to see what's there. I tend to look up and know where the stars are at night. So when I looked up, I noticed what looked like 2 stars. But then 2 more appeared right beside them about the same distance apart. Looked like 4 really bright stars in a diamond formation. All of a sudden they started to move towards Ottawa Ontario Canada really fast. As they started to move at first they were going straight then crisscrossed a couple of times then back to the diamond formation then disappeared. There was no noise from them. Shortly after they disappeared a few airplanes flew by. I could hear those. It was an uneasy feeling I had as I was shaking so bad I couldn't get my phone out of my pocket fast enough to take a pic or video. But I did manage to take a pic just after. I know for sure I got a star in the photo but I did a close up after and saw an odd shape further past the star kind of blurry as a blew it up. But I'm not sure if it was another star further away or something else. I stayed outside for another 45 minutes and saw nothing. I went inside and looked out my windows for a while didn't see anything else. But I will definetly keep looking up!
