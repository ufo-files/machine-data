This object appears to be very much the same as the one reported by Shawnee State witness.

It was a very cold crystal clear night. Not late however traffic was very light. We were on a short a short drive home from our families place. The object appeared in our view directly in front of us. It looked just like the one in the Shawnee State picture! It had lights like stars on every tip. It turned in front of us an cut the lights. Thats it! Amazing!
