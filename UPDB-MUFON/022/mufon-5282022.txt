visual observation

I witnessed (2) oval shaped objects that looked to be apprx 1500-2000 ft. in altitude....apprx 30-50 ft in diameter......in formation about 500 ft apart.....looked like a row of windows or protusions around each object......there was a stream of some type of liquid flowing or spilling out the bottom of both objects.....based on the bend of flow the objects appeared to be moving approx 30-50 MPH.......the speed and direction was constant.....no sound or propulsion system observed. First impression when i saw the first object was a elliptical balloon that got loose from somewhere. Then i observed the liquid constinusously flowing out the bottom. No balloon could do this, since it must be lighter than air. Then i observed the second object, with exact same features, trailing the first. Both objects continued west till out of site. Liquid continued to flow until unobservable and gone. I will take a polygraph. I was in the USAF, so i know aircraft sightings.!
