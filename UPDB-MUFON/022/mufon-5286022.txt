In parking lot of hotel at night, i saw what I thought were floating lanterns. My phone would not keep a charge so I tried to record while charging

I saw and recorded floating... Fire balls is the best description I can think of. They were glowing and sometimes got very bright. They were silent. My phone wouldn't hold a charge so I was trying to record them while plugged into car charger. At one point i unplugged charger to try to get away from the glare of the stadium - type parking lot lights of the hotel, but as soon as i unplugged my phone died. So i have 2 videos because i had to plug back in and turn phone on again. My phone had been charged and fine until I tried to record what I was seeing then it was like I had no battery all of the sudden. I turned my phone a lot because I was trying to keep the glare from the flickering light out of the video. You have to use the trees that were in front of me in the parking lot as a reference for what is up and down. The orbs started from toward the ground across the road a ways away from me and just floated up in triangle formation (2 long triangles, 6 orbs) and they went up til I could not see them anymore. Very life changing experience because to this day I can't find an earthly or logical explanation of what i recorded (and i have tried to find one). I don't know what it was but it was kind of belief shattering for me that's all i know.
