{nd} Took a quick picture of sun set and after discovered the object at the top of the frame and zoomed in to find something I can't explain

It was nearing the end of my work shift in the evening and I noticed that the sky had a really nice sun set so I decided to walk out back and snap a quick photo. I proceeded out side and found a good position and focus on my camera so I could get the best possible resolution. I didn't really focus on the contents of the photo just that it was in focus so when I walked back in I looked at my camera roll to make sure it was a good picture. When I found the photo I noticed something that didn't seem to belong...it was a blueish green glow at the top of the photo and when I zoomed in I couldn't believe what I was seeing, or what I thought I was seeing. It appears to have two rings almost like two levels and under each ring is the blueish green glow as well as what appears to be a shaft that runs up the middle of both of the rings connecting them. I ran back out side after about 20 seconds of looking at it but nothing was visible that would match what I was seeing on my phone.
