{cak} watched bright light travel fast than hoovered as low flying plan past and it vanished

At 9:20 pm I went outside to smoke a cigarette, a bright light caught my eye to west over my neighbors house, not sure if it was a flash that caught my attention or just the size and brightness of the object. my fianc1 and I watched as a large round very bright object traveled across the sky. At first I thought it was a plane but it had no flashing lights and made no sound. We watched for a few minutes than a low flying plane that had just taken off from the airport went past between us and the object. The object hovered for a moment, the plane past and we watched the object just vanished. There were no clouds in the sky. This is not the first time I have seen such an object with this shape and brightness, but it is larger than the others in the past. those ones were small like a basketball and at ground level zigzagging between trees. This is the first time seeing one so big. I wish I would of had my phone or camera on me.
