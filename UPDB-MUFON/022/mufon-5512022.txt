Light blue light being chased by a jet at speed

I was star gazing as i do almost every night when i heard a jet so i looked in the SW direction when a light blue light appeared, i then told my mother to come out and see the light which i assumed was a jet afterburner but when i realized that the light was being followed by the jet that was making the noise i realized that the blue light was not an afterburner and asked my mum to hurry out to witness the event, the blue light made no noise and was traveling only a little faster then the jet which was traveling very quick until the jet caught up and maneuvered slightly away from the blue light when they both disappeared behind the clouds to the NE, the Williamtown RAAF base is nearby.
