{jpg} Walking home, filming video in selfie mode, watch video when we got back and noticed an object following girlfriend

My girlfriend was walking home and started filming a video to send to me via text. The camera was in selfie mode. When I got home and watched the video back, I noticed an object in the sky behind her. At one point the object seems to be right above her, then it follows her and then disappears. 

I am a pretty observant person and something about the object caught my eye. I watched it back and thought it was a leaf of the trees, but then the object in the sky starts moving away from her then disappears. It was incredibly close at one point and there was no noise. You could hear standard traffic sounds, but nothing out of the ordinary. 

I was shocked to see how close the object got to her with no noise. Then it started to follow her and eventually disappear. The sighting wasn't seen until later in the evening when I looked at the video again. I found it odd that you could see something in the sky when the image was blown out from the sun. 

I have the video, but I don't want it public for work purposes.
