{rjl} 2 orange glowing orbs hovering then one vanishing the other traveled SE

Witnessed 2 orange glowing objects. It was only one at first and it was just hovering in the sky visible above the tree tops. After several minutes a second orange glowing object appeared out of nowhere moving upwards from the tree tops and slowly circled the first hovering object. My friend saw this too and immediate started to video record from his phone. My phone was in the house charging so I ran in to get it as fast as I could and when I got back outside and started to record video from my phone the second orb had already vanished. I was able to record the still stationary hovering orb that was originally there and after several minutes it started to slowly elevate upwards a bit and travel SE. I stopped recording after I lost sight of it behind tree line. After watching the video I captured, I took a screen shot of one of the clearest frames. I magnified/zoomed in on the object in the screen shot and only enhanced the brightness/contrast and boosted color saturation only slightly to show it how it appeared in color to the naked eye as opposed to how it looked on the video that made it look more of a white light. Viewing it with the naked eye, the two objects looked very bright and very orange in color.
