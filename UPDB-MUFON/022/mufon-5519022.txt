At 1st there where four light asending all towards same elevation, leading up to six

Sitting on back patio I observed 4 live rise above the horizon to a elevation that appeared to be about the same. When they neared the same point the light all vanished. Full moon out with little to no clouds...shortly (2-min) following seeing the initial 4 there where 2 additional light moving in the same direction and vanishing at the same elevation. This occurred over the gulf off or near St Pete Bch / Maderia Bch.
