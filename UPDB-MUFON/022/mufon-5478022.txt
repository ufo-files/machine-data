{cak} I work next to Miramar Marine Corps air station

I was at work at MNC Miramar National cemetery and was taking a photo of the helicopters overhead for my nephew who works in the Marine Corps as a helicopter maintenance battalion. I took the photo and noticed the object in the same airspace as the choppers, I really didn't pay attention to it until I looked at the photo immediately after taking it. I then looked back up and noticed that it took off in a northerly direction. I don't know if it is a UFO or not I just thought it was extraordinary because it was in the same airspace as the choppers over Miramar air station.
