{rjl} red light appeared for four nights above a barn, stayed stationary. single light seen on ground in the corn field on first night. of red light.

One night when I was nine yrs old we noticed a red light above our barn. It didn't move, blink or change colors. It was there all night every night for four nights. On the fourth night my father saw a single light, he thought it was a headlight from a snowmobile, in the corn field. Being January that would have been possible. The next day my father and brother went to the area where the"headlight" was seen and could find NO TRACKS from any kind of vehicle. There had been no new snowfall or heavy winds to erase any tracks that might have been there. Around that same time there was a cigar shaped UFO spotted in the area of Black River Falls WI. My father saw this as he was driving home from work on Interstate 90. I believe this was reported in the media but since it was 40 plus yrs ago, and I was a child, I can not remember exact dates to do any kind of research on it. We never felt any fear from the lights and after the 4th night it vanished and nothing was seen again. I have never had another experience but am curious as to what these lights could have been.
