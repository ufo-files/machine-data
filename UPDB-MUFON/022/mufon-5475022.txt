{cak} While walking my dog a bright red/orange light came out from behind a tree heading towards the forest behind my house

I was taking my dog out to go to the bathroom when I noticed a bright white lite tracking Eastward in the night. It was exactly 10pm. After watching the light for about 10 minutes, I turned to go back inside and that's when I saw the red/orange glow behind my neighbors tree. At first I thought it was a plane on fire but realized that there was no sound at all. I ran inside and called for my wife who ran it on to the back deck. We both watched as this silent orb, which held a constant elevation and speed,traveled into the Huckleberry ridge conservation area behind our house. My wife and I see things all the time that don't usually affect me but this gave me the shakes. Whatever it was it was not something man made.
