{nd} Two reddish-orange spheres passed over us in an arc.

Two of us were in a tent on the deck that night. We were lying down getting ready to go to sleep. Out of the corner of my eye I saw a reddish-orange light. I turned my head and saw two reddish-orange orbs moving at a relatively steady medium rate in an arc across the sky. I had no idea what they were but I knew they were not planes. They made no sound and they did not blink. The event lasted approximately a minute or two. When I sat up to watch the orbs pass I alerted the person next to me. She saw the, midway through their arc and we both watched them until they passed over our roof. I was tired when I first spotted the objects but I immediately sat up in the tent. I was very curious and even though I didn't know what they were I was not scared. I was relieved that there was someone with me and that she saw the same thing.
