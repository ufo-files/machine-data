"On the evening of December the 5, 2017 at approximately 17:27 Pacific time, I was setting up for a trial
run with the Bahtinov Mask for focusing a Celestron 8 inch Schmidt Cassegrain telescope.
Towards the southeast and 20 degrees above the horizon, I noticed a bright object moving at an unusual
speed, difficult to determine as there was nothing to reference it to. The rate of speed, slower than an
aircraft, and the unusual shifting of colors, different than the chromatic aberration of stars led me to
frantically switch out the diagonal/eyepiece assembly for a prime focus shot with a camera.
The following is footage I managed to obtain.
I want to believe...the truth is out there."
