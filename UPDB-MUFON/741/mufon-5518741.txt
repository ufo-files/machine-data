I was on top of Beach House Watching Sun Set,when an object streeked over my head . I took a picture.

I like to get great sunset pictures,from the Florida coast,so every night at the beach house I would go up on top to the Viewing area to watch the Sunset,to see if it would be a memorable one or not . This particular evening,(I think it was Wed.evening,but it might have been another night (The date and time should be part of the digital info however) Any way as i was looking north west to watch for the sun set,an object which appeared to be a black orb,with a flaming bottom streaked over my head from out over the Gulf,headed inland . I pointed my camera at it,and zoomed the lens,and snapped a picture,before the object was beyond my sight. There was no sound at all . I have no idea how high the object was,but it appeared to be about airplane height .
