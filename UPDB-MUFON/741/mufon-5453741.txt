{cak} More than 30 smaller lights following a brighter light that looked the ISS all moving WSW to NE.

At approximately 9:30 pm under clear skies, I saw what I was a sure was the ISS. Then I noticed another less bright light following the exact same path a short distance behind it. And then another and another and another until I lost count at more than 30. Sometimes there were two lights at a time but they were all the same brightness, same speed and same path coming from the WSW to NE. I could see 4 or 5 in the sky at a time until they faded away in the NE and more would come in the same line from the WSW. I woke up my husband to look and he has never seen anything like this before either. I always thought those smaller lights were satellites but have never seen more than one at a time. A plane did fly into the local airport further North during this sighting but I could clearly see it was a plane and not part of strange string of lights. The lights came less frequently after 10pm and didn’t see any more one 10:30.
