Triangle shaped object with unwavering lights

It was around 7:30 pm when I was driving on the highway. My friend and I suddenly saw a huge craft, not very high in the sky. Clear sky. The lights at its base were double ringed and yellow. Must be around 5-6 huge rings of lights. 3 of them were in corners. 2-3 in the middle. The shape of the craft seemed like a triangle, but could also have been "A" shaped. 
Didn't seem like a regular plane. Too big to be any kind of drone.
