FILLED OUT AT MUFON HEADQUARTERS, WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER #18809, ON MAY 2, 2017
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

IT WAS ABOUT 9:00 TO 9:30 PM, AND I WENT TO TAKE THE GARBAGE OUT AND LOOKED UP AND SAW A VERY LARGE BOOMERANG SHAPED CRAFT, IT HAD A SOLID WHITE LIGHT ON THE NOSE AND SOLID RED/AMBER LIGHTS ON THE WINGS. I CALLED MY GIRLFRIEND AND HER DAUGHTER OUTSIDE AND THEY SAW IT AS WELL.
THE CRAFT WAS 500' TO 1000' IN ELEVATION AND I HEARD ONLY A SLIGHT HUM, LIKE A FREQUENCY. I TRIED TO TAKE A PICTURE WITH MY PHONE, BUT IT REFUSED TO TURN ON. WHEN I WENT BACK IN THE HOUSE LATER, THE PHONE TURNED ON. I HAVE NEVER HAD THAT PROBLEM WITH MY PHONE BEFORE.
THE CRAFT HOVERED FOR ABOUT 3 MINUTES, AND SLOWLY MOVED IN A STRAIGHT LINE PATH, STOPPED AND THEN HOVERED AGAIN. IT BLOCKED OUT THE STARS AND I WOULD GUESS IT WAS 2 TO 3 BLOCKS LARGE. WHEN I SAY IT MOVED IN A STRAIGHT LINE PATH, IT KIND OF FLUTTERED, IT MOVED, BUT NOT SMOOTHLY. IT CAME FROM THE WEST AND WENT EAST AND HAS WE WATCHED IT SLOWLY MOVE AWAY IT SUDDENLY DISAPPEARED. 
WE WERE DISCUSSING WHAT HAD HAPPENED WHEN WE HEARD A HELICOPTER OVERHEAD AND WENT OUTSIDE TO SEE A BLACK OPS HELICOPTER CIRCLING THE AREA. I TRIED TO CONTACT THE LOCAL AIRSTRIPS TO SEE IF THE CRAFT HAD REGISTERED ON RADAR, BUT THEY SAID IT WASN'T IN THEIR DISTRICT.
