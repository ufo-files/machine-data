(GSI took a photo and video taped via phone three lights, which were brighter than planes behind them. These lights did not move. When zooming in, the light altered between several colors. They hung in air for several minutes or longer. They were bright.

My oldest son and I were heading to his basketball practice in Waterford on the date above. As we entered ramp to M-59 from 75, I observed three lights toward SW at some distance. Having studied aircraft and air flight of various WWII, other planes, these lights were stationary, did not move, bright and immediately caught my attention. I have a video on my phone, and a photo. We pulled over and I filmed. My son was freaked out and we had to get to practice. When I zoomed in, it was clear the lights contained additional colors. I had the distinct feeling this was not of our planet simply because planes at Metro were behind these lights. I am still uncertain of what I saw and am hoping to upload my video and photo soon.
