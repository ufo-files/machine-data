{jd} Wife drives grandson home nightly to westside of town over exact same route. First sighting of any kind.

August 26, 2014 @ 2230Hrs my wife was driving west on Slate Branch Road taking our grandson home. She stated she had just rounded a curve and started downhill when she noticed a bright light in the sky directly ahead of her. She thought it was an airplane landing at the local airport which is located roughly a mile behind her to the northeast. She continued toward the object and stated she realized it was not moving, but hovering above the ground approximately 200 feet. She added the object was totally in the darkness but it appeared to be the shape of a hexagon. It had two small green lights at one end and two small white lights at the other end. In the center of what she thought to be the front of the object she saw a large white light which she perceived to be a landing light on an airplane. She stated she drove directly under object travelling about a quarter mile before she turned to come back and observe the object. She said when she made the turn and started back the object had disappeared with nor further sighting made. The only issue that defeats the argument of an airplane is the object made NO noise and was HOVERING.
