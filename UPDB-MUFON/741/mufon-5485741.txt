Me and my coworker were in the company box truck. We were in a rural part of St.Cloud Florida. He said he saw somthing, I assumed he meant a deer or racoon. I laughed at him, that's when he said look in the sky. The lights didn't move or make a sound. At first I thought it might be airplanes from the airport near by. When they didn't move and an airplane flew by (big bright light in the bottom right corner in one picture) I knew it wasn't an airplane. We went two streets over and I took another picture. After I got back in the truck, we wanted another picture facing another direction and the lights were gone. At one point the three Light merged into one light then went back into the triangle shape.
