One reflective disc and 3-4 translucent in proximity

I was out doing drone photography at a local gravel pit. Noticed a reflective disc at what appeared a very very high altitude. Took many photographs using a cannon t3i 75 to 200 lens and a binocular camera. The reflective disc was quite visible in all photo's. When I returned home later that day and viewed the photos on my monitor. It was then that I discovered the translucent discs. Initially i thought the translucent discs were lens imperfections or dust on my camera mirrors. However when the objects appeared on photographs from both cameras there had to be another explanation.
