UFO Seen In Spring Branch, TExas

About a week ago, on May 9, 2020, as Kay and I were coming home from a Bar-B-Q, at around 10:30 in the evening, Kay noticed a light in the sky and pointed it out to me. At first, I thought it was a plane, but the light was always pointed toward us even as the light traveled toward the east or west. Kay also noticed colored lights under the white light.

Yesterday, at our Beefy's meeting, a person who had been at our Skywatch in October, actually brought up the subject of that particular UFO. He said that his 82 year old, ex-military father had called him outside to point the UFO out to him. 

Apparently, we got confirmation of another UFO in the beautiful Spring Branch area.
