{rjl} No cars or lights of any kind for miles, then suddenly a huge beam of light shining in my car from behind

I was driving home from school and listening to the radio. I felt uneasy, but was nearly home. I was at a point in my drive where you can see cars coming from either direction miles before they appear, and none were around.
The sky was totally dark and there was no other cars on the road. Suddenly, a light that I can only describe as a stadium spotlight appeared directly behind me. It was eerie, I could see my own shadow in front of me as if the sun was shining behind me. The trees and fields were lit up on either side of the road as well. I grabbed my phone to take a video or photo, but was overcome with a feeling of terror... that I wasn’t supposed to record “it”. I was so afraid I literally hunkered down and blindly slammed on the gas pedal. I called my boyfriend and was so overcome with emotion that I couldn’t stop crying. I don’t know why I was so shaken or so emotional. This isn’t my first sighting of strange things in the sky in that’s area. Other members of my family have seen things as well, but nothing as close as this. As fast as it was there, it was just suddenly gone. There was no way it was a car, because whatever it was- was so big that I wasn’t even able to see where the light started or ended... it was extremely bright, close, and huge... like a spotlight directly on me.
