{dr}Very bright object moving, then hovering

I passed a woman who was filming the sky with her camera across the street from me. Once I moved up the block I that she was filming a very bright light that was moving. It started to slow down and hover, then it moved on and hovered again. I began to film the object in relation to the brightness of the quarter moon. Sky was very clear and it was a breezy very cold night.

I'm used to seeing helicopters and planes, but this was very far off but also extremely large and bright. There was also the occasional sound of thunder even though the sky was very clear. Thought it could be a star but it appeared to be two very bright lights close together. 

Similar object was spotted over Pittsburgh on August 4, 2016
