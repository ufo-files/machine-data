{dr}Back in the late 1960s, my father was plowing ground late one night and he said all of a sudden directly above him alight shot straight down and lite the old farmall and the ground around it in a circle ,then it was gone.

My father was out plowing late one night back in 1968 or 69, he was plowing with an old Farmall with no cab on it. He said it was after midnight when all of a sudden a very bright light out of nowhere, right over top shot straight down and lit the tractor and ground around it in a large circle. He said he looked up to see what it was but the light blinded him then the next thing it was gone. With the loud exhaust from the tractor he did not hear anything.It scared him so bad, he left the field and quit. I asked him what he thought it was and all he said was we will never know.
