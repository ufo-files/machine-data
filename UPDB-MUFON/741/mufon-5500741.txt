MY HUSBAND AND I WERE TRAVELING N. ON A COUNTY RD THE SUN WAS SETTING TO THE WEST AO IT WAS APPROX 5 PM OR SO AND AS WE LOOKED TOWARD THE HILLS ON OUR LEFT WE SAW THIS OBJECT COME OUT OF THE HILLS AND SLOWLY START TRAVELING W. BUT IT WAS VERY SHINNY AND AS WE GOT CLOSER WE PULLED OFF TO THE SIDE OF THE ROAD THERE WAS A COUPLE IN A CAR BEHIND US AND THEY PULLED OYER TO BUT WE NEVER TALKED TO THEM JUST AS THE OBJECT GOT REAL CLOSE THE OTHER COUPLE GOT IN THEIR CAR AND LEFT MY HUSBAND AND I STAYED AND WATCHED THE OBJECT CROSS IN FRONT OF US NOT MORE THAN MAYBE 5 FT. OR SO ABOVE THE TREES THIS OBJECT NEVER MADE A SOUND AND FORM THAT TIME ON WE REALLY DON'T REWMEMBER THE SAME THINGS OTHER THAN BEING IN OUR CAR HEADING N. AGAIN. WE ARE BOTH AIR FORCE RETIRED AND BOTH WORKED ON SEVERAL AIRCRAFT.
