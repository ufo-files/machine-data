{rjl} 3 red blinking lights in uniform triangle formation. went right over our house, hovered a little, then took off slowly. no noise made

My dad was taking out the trash, and it was already dark outside. Dad stopped to look at the stars, when he noticed 3 red blinking lights that were passing right above the house in a uniform triangle formation. Dad said he thought it was 3 separate planes at first, but they stayed in formation and were not making any noise. He ran in the house and called me to come see. I saw the 3 blinking lights, still in the uniform triangle formation. They looked pretty low, lower than a plane would be flying. Plus, they made no sound. They were moving very slowly away from our house, and even appeared to hover in place for a short time. Then they continued on its slow way across the sky. As they moved away, they ended up looking more like a straight line. They started out heading in a sort of north east direction, then turned and started heading sort of south east. We were able to see them until they went behind some trees. The whole incident lasted approximately 5 minutes.
