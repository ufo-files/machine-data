{rjl} Orange light paused twice smaller orange light fell from it and quickly disappeared twice large orange light changed direction twice

Two of my sons, my wife and I saw something on June 21st 2014 at approximately 9:45PM. The sky conditions were cloudy and there were no other lights in the sky, moon, stars, airplanes etc. This orange light was traveling slowly in the eastern sky heading west it then paused and a smaller orange light fell from it and quickly disappeared, The large orange light then changed direction and headed south once again it paused and a small orange light fell from it and quickly disappeared. It then continued to move southwest and apparently gaining altitude disappeared into the clouds. My wife claims to have heard a humming noise before it disappeared the rest of us did not hear it. My youngest son later found out that the space station would be visible at 9:45 near Cleveland, but we are 200 miles south of Cleveland and the sky was cloudy and I don 't believe the space station flies below the clouds, nor does it change course and jettison items. A couple years back on a very clear evening we saw the space station in the same area as this sighting but it moved quickly and did not change direction. I am a UFO skeptic I don't know why aliens that travel light years need lights, but I can't explain what we saw Saturday night.
