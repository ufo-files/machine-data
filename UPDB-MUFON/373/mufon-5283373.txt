Simular to Hard hat outline also I saw an outline of what looked like short wing because I could only see the side facing me.

We were going south on hwy 45- S and I noticed to my right a very snow white looking craft going same speed as my car. After verifying that it wasn't a reflection, I pointed at it and shouted look at that. At the same time it tilted back with front toward the clouds and it disappeared into the clouds in less than a second. I was left with my head against the windshield, I then asked my companions did they see it and only my sister-in-law said she only got a glimpse of it!
