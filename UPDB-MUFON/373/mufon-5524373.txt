Three lights, one bright and large, hover and two small lights absorbed and dissappears

I was sitting in my friends yard and looked up at the sky for no real reason and at the end of his yard, 25 feet from where I am sitting, he has a couple of very tall palm trees. Just above the palm trees I see 3 lights moving together in a straight line. The first light was large and had a kind of peach-colored aura. It stopped and the other two lights, smaller, the size and look of maybe the north star, zoomed into it and then it disappeared. I was too shocked to say anything the first moment I saw it, but when I did say something, my friends were mid conversation and ignored me and so they missed it. It was a bright summer evening and there was nothing else in the sky as far as I could remember. There was a small cloud formation a few inches away from the brightest light. I have the exact date marked in my diary, but I don't have that on me right now, I do know that it was a recent Sunday, however.
