{tjd} Jet black perfect triangle observed hovering

Walkiy home from middle school one day, perfectly clear blue sunny afternoon, I believe someone was looking at something in the sky and I took interest to what it might be and seen a completely black perfect triangle hovering in the sky, no movement, no discernable features because it was so far up. But the person who I initially witnessed must have walked away but several people walked past me in a span of what must've been over 15 minutes, looked up at what I was witnessing and thought nothing of it and kept going about their business.
For the time that I could stay the object never moved.
