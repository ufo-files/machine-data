{nd} Red light in sky going south

At 6:50PM on Saturday 23,2013 during a birthday for a 2 year old in a backyard in riverside California, I noticed a red light in the sky heading south in our direction.Three others I was with noticed it also, I said its a UFO. at that moment it changed direction turning west as it turned the light slowly went out as it descend and slowed to almost a stop, Then continued west without lights. It was still visible in the darkness it looked like a football shaped mass that seemed to blend in with the darkness of the night as it traveled on a path that seemed to descend west, We watched it till it was out of sight.
I felt like the UFO knew we saw it so it changed direction.
