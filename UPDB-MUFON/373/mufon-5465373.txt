{tjd} Two strange men visited my childhood home, was told to leave by relatives and lost hearing

Recently I've told some close friends about an instance that occurred as a child where myself, my mother and aunt were at my childhood home when someone knocked on the door. I was playing video games at the time and got up to see who it was. My mother and aunt answered the door first, I walked behind them when I saw two strange looking men in black suits and sunglasses. I remember them being very pale, bald and having very vague facial features. At that point my mom told me to go to another room while they talked to the men, this was out of the ordinary since my parents would usually just let me stand there or say hi at least. This is where the video games became important because the music was still playing when they answered the door but once I stepped into the next room I couldn't hear anything at all until they closed the door and the strange men left. I've been told by friends who are invested in the paranormal/extra terrestrial and have taken a guess that they were men in black.

I also know this seems like a faded memory but as far as I know I've remembered it like this for my entire life.

I've been strongly encouraged me to reach out and share my story with those who know more about the encounter, if it is an encounter at all. I hope this is something relevant to your research and studies.

Thank you for taking the time to read my experience and look forward to hearing back!
