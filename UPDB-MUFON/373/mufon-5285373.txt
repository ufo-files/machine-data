Trail of light moving upwards and SE direction. Disappeared a split second before colliding with commercial airplane.

My wife and I enjoy sitting in our back yard at night at our fire pit and star/airplane watch. On Aug 22 2021 we both witnessed a trail of light fly upwards and at an angle towards a commercial airplane. The light disappeared a split second before collision. I really believed that it was a surface to air missile that was bringing a plane down right over my house. My location is sw Detroit Michigan Wayne co.
