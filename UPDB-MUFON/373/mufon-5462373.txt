{rjl} Craft flew overhead, seemed to be in distress at first, then recovered and headed towards the rising sun.

I was at work with another worker just talking outside. An object appeared at approx. 90 degrees above us making a whirling noise. It was disk shaped with a small dome on top. It appeared to be in distress and making a noise , kinda like it was cutting out. It sputtered for about 4-5 miles (approx.) Then all of a sudden it seemed to kick in and shot off towards the sun at an incredible speed, disappearing into the rising sun light. We looked at each other like did this really happen and kinda freaked out. We never said anything to anyone (or at least I didn't). We were like did you see & hear that.
