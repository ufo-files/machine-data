white sphere rolls out of hole in sky

I have seen many unusual aerial objects beginning Wednesday, February 8, 2006. Therefore, I began to take up sky watching on a daily basis for about 10-15 each day. That's all it appears to take.

I was sky-watching about 3.15pm PST today. At 
3.22 pm, I saw a 'brush-stroke-like' part of the sky turn a slightly lighter color of blue, 
and then a white sphere appeared to 'fall out' 
of that brush-stroke. I 
can't explain it any other way. It just fell 
out, and hung there for seven 
minutes, until 3.29 pm. The brush-stroke was 
horizontal relative to the 
horizon, and the object fell out, or maybe 
'rolled out'would be a better term, like a marble, 
but very fast.

The 'brush-stroke' quickly faded after the 
white sphere fell out.

After 7 minutes, the white sphere appeared to get 
smaller and smaller until 
it vanished from view. The white sphere was 
about the size of Mars.
