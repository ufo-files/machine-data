have been seeing at night very bright lights in the sky east of my home when out on my deck with my dogs after dark. this one tonight was more due east of me and approximately one hour later has moved closer toward my home but slightly to the south, so it traveled in an hour south west. but the movement is gradual. It is very bright with bright lights shining off like one would draw a sunshine with the lines extending from the sun. But this one also has lights flashing as if around it's perimeter in green and red. It moves slightly up and down or seems to orbit around. Since late summer this is not the first time I have witnessed strange bright lights hovering. My husband has also seen them and being a navy man, says these lights are brighter than any stars he saw while out on the ocean. My property is rural, close to the area known as Chapel Hill bordering Lafayette, Johnson and Jackson county in Missouri.
