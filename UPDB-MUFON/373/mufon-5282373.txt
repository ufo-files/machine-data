Orbs travailing West above river

I notice one orb with a formation of 2 then one travailing West above the Chehalus River at noon and a few minutes later there was a power failure. That after noon I called the power company to see why the power failure as the weather was fair and there was no road that traveled parallel to the river and the orbs appeared 50 feet in altitude. The person at the power company said they could not find the problem
