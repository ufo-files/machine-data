I lost sight of it..this object was not above the trees..it passed below the high tree lines out there in the Cattle field..theres a huge road sign that it was slightly above It..It came from the west, an Headed Northward up along the west side.these are the actual photos from the film i shot..some are close ups others are distant..the film is on a sony 8 steady shot digital video Handycamcorder 990 X digital Zoom.
