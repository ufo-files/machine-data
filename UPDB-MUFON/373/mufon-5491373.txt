{cak} Basketball shaped bright orange tint of red moved silently like plane

At 10:04 pm me and my wife went onto our back deck while I lit up a cigarette. My wife noticed it first and told me to look to my left (south). That's when I saw it with amazement and awe. It was shaped like a basketball ball but kinda elongated just a tad (not oblong but not full circle either). It was very orange/red with no blinking lights like a plane (but it moved like a plane). After a couple seconds I reached for my phone to turn in video. I did record but nothing showed up (too dark). As quickly as I hit record, it vanished as if it went behind clouds but tonight there weren't any clouds. I can't say for certain what it was but I can tell you what it wasn't: plane, Chinese lanterns or a drone.
