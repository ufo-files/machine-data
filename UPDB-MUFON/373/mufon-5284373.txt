Brief sighting

Last night at about 10:20pm, I went out to my back yard to have a smoke. Near Campbell Airport so I am accustomed to aircraft circling the airport, they typically are small craft and the occasional helicopter. They usually circle at about 500ft counter-clockwise and normally do not operate at night. While observing the night sky I could see an aircraft high in the sky east of my position more than likely a commercial plane 
My sighting only lasted about 1-2 seconds as a ufo flew over my home at treetop level about 100-150 ft in a northwestern direction. It had 3 white lights in a triangular pattern, lite solid.
I could not discern any details of the craft other than the lights, the spacing between the lights was about 10-20ft, since it was dark and its speed makes it difficult to estimate, I would say approximately 40mph. there was no sound at all, it traveled about 80yrds before I lost sight because of the treeline. It happened so fast it was over before I realized what happened, I was like what the ? And it was over.
