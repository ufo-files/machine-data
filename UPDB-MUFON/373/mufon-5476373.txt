{cak} 6 glowing orange lights flew over house

Approximately 8:45 pm was standing in my driveway looking up to see if I saw any lightning from distant storms. Saw one glowing orange light fly overhead traveling from east to west. Immediately noticed there was no sound whatsoever coming from it. Was flying below the thick clouds. About a minute or so later saw 2 more lights trevaling same route side by side but staggered. Another few seconds later 3 more lights in triangle shape flew overhead in same direction. I ran inside to get my wife and son to witness it. As I was waiting for them to come out I saw 2 more flying in staggered formation. They were all completely silent and moving at a quick but steady pace. Were definitely moving nonstop. My heart was racing and it seemed really surreal. Like I was watching a movie. It was kind of disturbing. They didn't appear to fly like anything one would logically believe capable.
