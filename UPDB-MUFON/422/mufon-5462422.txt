{rjl} Seen passing at a distance under left wing of American Airlines (AA2436) SE of DFW airport

I was aboard American Airlines flight AA2436 about 20 minutes SE of Dallas Ft Worth (DFW) airport (exact location uncertain). While looking out the window (left side of plane behind the wing) I noticed a brightly reflective oval object traveling smoothly in the opposite direction, at a much lower altitude. At first I assumed it was another airline, but I could see no wings or other details. I'm guessing it was about the size of a small house or a jet plane, but I could not gauge its distance from our plane. I did not have time to pull out my phone to take a photo, so I kept watching the object, trying to distinguish any wings. Within a minute it had passed out of my field of view to the left. Over the next 15 minutes I saw two jet planes passing in a similar manner, but it was very easy to distinguish their wings and darker color. I realize this is a trivial observation, but hope that it might corroborate another sighting from the ground at about the same time and location.
