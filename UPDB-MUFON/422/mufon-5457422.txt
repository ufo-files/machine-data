UFO footage I videoed last night while my husband and I were fishing off our dock. We have planes fly over all the time - this was not a plane, private or commercial - this was not a drone, either. There was no sound and the lights were not typical of those we see on planes or drones. There were red lights on either side of a larger white light in the center. Lights were either on the bottom of the craft or on both, the front and back. The configuration of the lights didn't change from the time we saw it head on or when it was moving away from our vantage point. The movement was very erratic, to say the least - we watched it for a good ten minutes. A plane does not stay in our range of vision for that length of time. We are in the flight pattern for Gainesville and Orlando International, so we see planes all the time when we are outside at night and in the day. We have no outside lights and no light pollution that would affect what we see at night because we live in a very rural area, surrounded by lots of wooded acreage east of Gainesville and west of Palatka, Florida.
