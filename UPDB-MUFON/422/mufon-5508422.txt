I was taking a walk when I saw a black, circular object hovering in the distance. At first I thought it was a bird but then as I got closer I could see that It was hovering and then wobbling shortly afterwards. Since I had my camera with me, I took two photos. One while it was wobbling and another when it was stationary. I tried to get closer and take a third but then it took off at a very high speed. When I zoomed in on the stationary photo, I could see what I thought was a dome and windows, but I am not sure. This was the fourth UFO, I have observed.  Ever since I saw my first UFO, at the age of ten, I have been fascinated with the subject and have watched many documentaries on the Topic. I will continue to keep my eye on the sky.
