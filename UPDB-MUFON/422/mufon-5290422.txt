My wife & I had just backed out of our driveway and into the back lane when she observed what she thought were "planes" flying low over our house . She was able to look straight up from the passenger seat of our car and saw 4 triangle shaped objects flying above us. I stopped the car and got out. I saw the four craft flying in formation- 3 lined up diagonally and one to the side . they were silver- white in appearance with a dome on top , the middle part of the craft was a sleek triangle  and the bottom was slightly rounded. I heard no engine noise or saw no contrails. There is a small airport in Madison in which ( usually during the summer0 they will have antique plane air shows. These were NOT antique planes. They had no landing gear like the antique planes. I noticed a jet several thousand feet up with contrails coming from it. I was looking at it to compare it with what I was seeing. When I looked back to view the objects again, they were gone.
They were flying in a Southeasterly direction right toward the Ross Barnett Reservoir.
