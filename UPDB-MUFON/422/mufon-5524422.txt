I saw a doughnut shaped UFO floating overhead a couple years ago in the early fall.

I was laying out on the back slab of the house on a lounger looking upward. I watched a jet airliner fly over on it's way to Cleveland. We are about 60 miles from Hopkins Airport so the jets are pretty high here. As the jet passed over head it flew past a UFO shaped like a "Cheerio". The jet flew unward toward Cleveland but the UFO remained motionless. It appeared that the jet flew under the UFO and the UFO was about the size of a wing. I went into the house to get my binoculars but when I got back the UFO was gone.
