{nd} Star moving slowly

On 7/21/2014 around 10pm me and my wife took a walk on the beach outside our hotel (double tree) in Santa Barbara ca. We laid out on the beach to star gaze it was a super clear nigh, a few minutes later my wife pointed out a slow moving star it was so far away from us it took me a few seconds to spot it but once I did i didn't blink or turn away till it just disappeared. It was not an airplane, hellecopter, or anything else I could recognize this I'm sure of we had been there and saw various planes pass both near and far this light we saw did not blink did not have any other lights just a bright white light as high as the stars moving in a straight line from west to east directly over us I will never forget this!!
