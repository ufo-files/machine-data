Multiple lights in the sky, moving dimming and hiding behind clouds.

Friends and I were golfing when we first noticed the light in the sky near the direction of the sun. It's stable location and brightness in the sky is what got our attention and when we drove to look for our golf ball it disappeared. For the next 2 hours as the sun was setting we continued to observe several lights around the direction of the setting sun. The orb shaped lights were moving up, down, left and right, they also changed brightness throughout observation. At multiple times during the sighting the lights would move behind clouds or dim as soon as they were pointed out or captured on camera. I have never seen anything like this and have no possible explanation.
