{rjl} String of evenly spaced lights appearing low on the horizon, flying in a straight line and dissapearing when each one got to the top of the sky

My husband watched a light moving in the sky, a few seconds later, it was followed by another light, and then another, until there was a perfect line of lights all evenly spaced, moving slowly in a NE direction. As each light got to one high point in the sky, it would disappear completely. There were always about 5 lights in the flight path that he could see at one time. My husband yelled for me to come out and see. I witnessed the lights behaving in the way that I have specified. I watched for 5 minutes, and then ran inside for my camcorder. I also wanted to get a better view from out front with less trees. By then it was too late, they had all vanished. My husband thinks that he saw around 100 lights flying in this event. It was over within 15-20 minutes. I saw a clock that said 6:17PM, when I went to get my camera. I didn't capture it on film. My husband called his sister in a nearby town, to tell her about the sighting. She just sent me a photo that one of her friends on Facebook had posted Online, because it looked like our sighting, too. She said the photo was from Howell, MI. I saved the photo in my computer, but I do not have the rights to use it. It looks exactly like our sighting.
