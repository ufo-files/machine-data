Donut shaped object

The object was first observed almost over head and moved in a path over and away from us. The object appeared to change shape and was distinctly donut shaped at one point. Some flashes of light came from the center of the object. At one point it appeared to hover and spin then move in a different direction. The flight path was far too slow for an aircraft and did not resemble anything I have seen flying before. The video is shaky and I lose the object in the sky on camera but the are several good looks within the video of the object moving and possibly morphing.
