I had about eight children with me at the time with a 4-h club.
When they called my attention to look towards the mountains. Time was about 5-6 PM it was clear outside a sunny day. When I saw a Circular object hovering around some bamboo trees next to us the Item moved in a circular pattern around the bamboo trees towards us. 
I clearly distinguished a Circular object hovering above me me about some 15-20 feet away I could see a red ban around the upper windows of the craft but could not see anyone in the windows. 
I was concerned that the children could be abducted or hurt in any way so asked them to get into the covered area of the house. 
The object moved very slowly in front of me slowly elevated towards the mountains and suddenly moved like into an air pocket in the sky completely disappearing slowly as it went in. The sky was blue with no clouds in site. Or signs of the object...
