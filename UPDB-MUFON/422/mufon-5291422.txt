Saw 2 UFO's. One on right slowly decended then disolved. Then so did the one on the left

We were driving to the grocery store in 29 Palms California 10/20/20 @ 951pm on Amboy Road 29 palms CA. I looked to the right & saw 2 UFO's. They looked like 4 sets of round orange bright light each. Just 2 separate sets of 4 orange lights. We pulled over & started taking pictures & video with my cell phone. They were about 1/3 of a mile away from us. At first they just hung in the sky over the mountaing range side by side. Then they slowly started to move to the right and decend. Then they stopped and hung in the sky again for a couple minutes. Then the one on the right started to decend slowly by itself. It stopped right about the mountain range. Then its 2 orange lights on the bottom started to slowly disappear like it was falling behind the mountain range. It hung there with only the two orange lights on the top for a couple minutes then disappeared leaving only the other one on the left in the sky. It hung motionless in the sky for a minute or so. Then it started to travel to the right slowly. Then it stopped and did the same thing the other did. The two bottom lights slowly disappeared leaving only the top two then it slowly disentigrated until there was nothing left.
