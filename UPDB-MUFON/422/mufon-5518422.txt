around 30 lights seen by party

I was having a party in my back yard, someone pointed out a small yellow star-like dot moving at a steady pace slanting upwards. We all gathered and saw close to 30 of these star-like orbs coming up from behind the houses in front of us but it had to be at least 10-15 miles away from us, we did manage to catch pictures and a video of this event. It lasted around 10 minutes and we all saw the same thing.
