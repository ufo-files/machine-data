Late night swimming and saw orange and greenish lights!!

Just moved into a new home in beaumont texas and decided to do some late night swimming after unpacking all day. The wife and kid were sleeping and i was just floating in the pool. The wind was blowing very bad and i looked up at the neighbors palm tree when I noticed an orange light. I wanted to run inside and get my camera but things were still in boxes and I would never have found it. I got out of the pull and climbed on the roof. The triangular craft was hovering with 6 blinking greenish lights. The lights seemed to be going in some sort of sequence. After watching the craft for a few minutes it moved very quickly to the northeast and hovered some more. it was incredible! i would have sat there all night watching it. I hopped off the roof and grabbed my flashlight and climbed back up. I don't know what I was thinking, I just wanted to make some desperate attempt of communication, so I started flashing and waving my light. haha. I estimated the craft to hover for about 10 minutes before it vanished. I didn't move or anything, it just shined a little brighter then it was gone. This was truly amazing. I have always believed, but this just strengthens my belief.
