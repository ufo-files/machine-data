UFO observed in SE Indiana Sky.

My oldest son and I were on the third hole of a local golf course. We both spotted the object in the northern sky moving from west to east. The object was observed to move most the way accross the horizon before it was no longer visible. I've seen many military aircraft in the sky over Indiana, and none have ever flown that close to the hard deck...at that speed.
