Two Glowing Objects appeared stationary and rapidly ascended (later refuted)

Noticed two large glowing objects high in the sky that looked like a glowing ring. They were a good distance from each other and stayed stationary for a few minutes then rapidly ascended at the same time until they looked like a faint star and then disappeared completely.
