Dark spot in sky seen out window of my parked car. Reflected light at times.

I was sitting in the car at the post office (85299) while my partner took packages inside to mail. I looked out my driver's side window (toward the SE) and noticed a dark spot in the sky. It was very high in the sky and stationary. It may have been rotating but I am not sure. I only know that at times it reflected light and appeared metallic. It hovered there for approximately 5 minutes going in and out of thin clouds before disappearing behind more dense clouds that moved in. Believe it or not, I looked initially because I had a thought to to look specifically for a UFO. No idea why and I know that sounds crazy but within seconds of me looking out the window, I saw it. We went back with my Nikon DSLR camera and zoom lens but didn't see anything else as the clouds were still there and moving very slow. I guess it\s possible the object could have left as well.
