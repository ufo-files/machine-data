{rjl} Dark, clear night. 2 lights: 1 amber, 1 red, hovered above mountain silhouette for 20-25 minutes before disappearing. At one point, 2 planes passed relatively

Anthony, Texas.

Last night, I stepped into my backyard at about 8:00-8:05 PM. I was staring at the stars as I paced around and saw the usual air traffic with a beautiful display of stars. If there were clouds, I couldnt see them. It was a very clear night, as far as I could tell.

When I noticed 1 orange light to the East, I didn't make anything of it. I thought it was a really bright star because I knew that Jupiter was elsewhere, within my field of view. About 2 or 3 minutes after initially seeing this bright object, I noticed that a 2nd, similarly colored object was right next to it (bright white-orange). I continued to look at the stars while noting the position and curious nature of these 2 objects. Usually, I would see strobe lights flashing if it was a plane in the distance. At some point, when I looked back at the objects, the object to the right was very noticeably red. Thats when I ran inside to get my camera.

The lights stood in the same spot I had initially seen them in within the 25-30 minute span of the sighting. They would very slowly fade in and out (pulsating), and at one point, both had briefly disappeared for no more than maybe 2 minutes before I could see them again. I could tell based on light which was hitting a neighbors house from his backyard, that these lights were above the mountain silhouette. At one point, 2 airplanes with strobe lights went right "by" the object, as well. There was no sound coming from the objects, as far as I can tell. After recording video and taking photos, the lights were simply not visible anymore. As far as I can tell, they faded away (while stationary) and were no longer visible.
