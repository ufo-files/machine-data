Saw triangle object moving with no sound

I stepped out on the porch to have a smoke and looked to the southwest. I seen what first appeared to be a plane. I live close to the airport and see them regularly. This had a weird color of lights that made me watch it closer. Upon it moving closer to me, no sound was emitted. I seen it was no plane and had a triangle shape. Three rosey orange lights on all three corners with a bluish yellow light in the center. I watched it turn 189 degrees with no movement in direction. It was hovering. The size of this thing from what I could tell was at a minimum of 300 yards across. It made my hair on the back of my neck stand up.
