{rjl} gray tubular craft hovering over mt. in altoona pa.

in the summer of 1970 i was a junior in high school in altoona pa, i was near the altoona campus of penn state around 1100 pm when i noticed this tubular structure gray in color just hovering less than a mile away about two to three hundred feet in the air , the sky was very clear that evening no clouds at all. as i watched this craft 10 or so beams of light came out from the bottom side of it and seemed to circle the mt that it was hovering over , this went on for approximetley two to three miniutes, the lights were lazor type and bluish green in color, the lights swallowed up into the craft like they were turned of at the bottom end of them and swallowed their selfs up into the craft. then the craft took off with no sound at all at a speed to fast to comprehend and stoped on a dime. hovered a few seconds and with no sound at all it was gone in the blink of an eye. i never told very many people about this, who was going to beleive this ? this incident left me in awe and opened me up to all possibilities of believing that God made other forms of life out there and never forget that God created the heavens and that He is in control of everthing.i dont let things rent space in my head and keep myself open to all explenations of every situations.as a kid growing up in pa i wittnessed other ufo's along with my uncle in curwensvile pa on my grandfather farm.i've been living in los angeles for nearly 40 years and have seen two flying objects i could'nt explain. I know there is a God and that he protects me so i dont worrie about things i cant explain.
