{rjl} greenish white ball hovering then abruptly accelerated to a speed quicker than know aircraft

On the evening of December 10th, 2018 conversing with a group of friends outside I noticed directly to my south a ball of greenish light hovering stationary. There was some fog on this evening and you could not see stars. I observed it for approximately 30 seconds. Once I pointed the strange light out to my friends and they looked in the direction of the light and it instantly accelerated to a high rate of speed. We observed it traveling in a South East direction for 3 to 4 seconds prior to if disappearing.
