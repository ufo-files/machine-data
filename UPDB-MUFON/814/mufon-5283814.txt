white fuzzy sphere then changed to more aerodynamic shape

I was putting up a tv antenna on the south side of my house when the UPS delivered a package. We were both looking up at my antenna and I was explaining the antenna design to him when we both noticed a white fuzzy sphere approaching from the west. It was about the size of a penny at arms length. I would guess the altitude between 300 and 500 feet. I said "Do you see that?" His comment was yes and it looks like when you blow a dandelion. I agreed. As it got directly over us we both started pointing at the object and trying to make sense of what we are seeing. The object then started to change shape into a more aerodynamic shape. I would describe the shape as being somewhat oval similar to pictures of a hypersonic cruise missile with a blunt nose. As it changed shape it appeared to start rising in altitude and making a banking turn to the north. It was not a hard ninety degree turn but is was a tight banking turn not unlike something an F-18 is capable of achieving but probably a little tighter. It was also speeding up quickly. The object moved to where the house blocked our view. I ran to the back of the house. The object was gone. That took no more than a second. It was a clear day. Not a cloud in the sky. No jet trails. To disappear that quickly it had to accelerate to an extremely high speed.
