{cak} formation of approx. 20 lights moved across the distant sky

Wife was outside, noticed a pattern of lights moving across the distant sky from west to east at a high rate of speed. She yelled for her husband to come and see. He joined the viewing about 3 minutes into the sighting. The lights were mostly very bright white, some random red blinking. The lights were traveling in a straight line close but random altitude taking 5-6 minutes to cross to the east and out of view. There were approximately 20 lights seen crossing the sky in a straight line path. 

witness called Missouri MUFON State Director approx. 1 hour after sighting.
Report submitted by State Dir.
