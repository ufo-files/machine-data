Saw this in sky whilst taking photos of sun/chemtrails

We have been taking many photos of the sun using an UV/IR blocking lens on an Iphone camera. We are big believers in Planet X and have seen lots of unusual objects near the sun, but nothing like this!

Took these photos at 8.30pm GMT last night facing NW, lots of chemtrails in the sky, we noticed that there was this ring in the sky near the sun, no idea what it was, we thought at first it was something on our lens, but on checking, it was clean. We imagined it might be a hidden planet/spaceship? Or maybe a secret Government experiment/plane of some sort? Made us think of the old sci-fi TV series Stargate, it looked like a portal!

We lost sight of the object as the sun set, went away behind cloud and roofs of houses. Made us think we are not alone.
