{dr}2 same craft flying side by side, one wing larger than the other, rounded tips, something near center of each, like baggage area

I was driving north on the Ridge rd in Fairfield, Maine, at 8:45 a.m. July 25th, 2012, when I looked up into the sky to my right and 2 boomerang shaped air craft were flying slowly and silently side by side, but at a slight diagonal to one another. They were moving straight across the sky with no engine noises, no emissions. They were dark in color, brownish-black. They were moving from east to west over Ridge rd. One wing was shorter and smaller than the other, and both wings had round wing tips. They were identical to one another. I thought they seemed like some kind of ultralight gliders until I researched images on the web. There is nothing on the web that looks like these "planes". They were shaped just like boomerangs but there was a protruding "bundle" or baggage area between the wings, off toward the larger wing. I kept on going. Unfortunately I didn't have a camera with me.
