{cak} Saw craft with men like entity with black hair looking out window over trees near my neighbor house

Father was stationed in Jacksonville. My mother and I went to see a slide show at the neighbors,a neighbor knocked on door and we went outside. Over the neighbor's house we saw a craft with glass windows and human looking people. Three people had dark hair, very white skin, with black uniform, like spandex with fold over with silver trim. I only could see from the waist up as it appeared they were working at some kind of counter and only saw hands moving with light inside what looked like a globe over the saucer type. Neighbors looked up and saw also, my mother claims she didnt see anything.Saucer then moved slowly behind trees and then we went back to our home.
