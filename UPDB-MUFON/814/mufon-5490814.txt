{jpg} Starlike dull oval object to far to see details. Observed twice after military planes flew over.

Shy of a full moon tonight but a bright beautiful night. I was observing a plane flying over in East Fishkill location of NY. Around 10:30 p.m. I stood on the back deck and looked up at the few stars. I noticed an faint round/oval dull object in the far east sky moving from south to north facing east. As it moved, I seen it disappear. I thought, Ok, maybe a drone but it was so high up in the sky. I moved towards my porch to look up to the stars again; at 10:40 p.m. I noticed the same dull starlike round/oval object on the other side moving in a northernly direction. It hovered then moved again. It eventually disappeared upon my observation. I've noticed these occasionally but like my aunt in Puerto Rico says; it's just one of those wondrous things that is part of our universe.
