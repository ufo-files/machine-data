Reality Glitch

I was driving 275 E near Fairfield/Winton Woods exit. Within a blink of an eye, an airplane appeared out of nowhere. It was extremely low to the ground. Right in front of me. The airplane was not moving at all. No sound or lights. Other cars must have seen it too because of sudden braking and passengers pointing and looking up. I immediately opened my sunroof to watch as I drove under. As soon as I passed I looked back and just as fast as it appeared it disappeared. It didn’t move it was just gone like it was never there. It was fast. Less than a minute. As soon as it began I immediately felt fear and still very shaken by the whole event
