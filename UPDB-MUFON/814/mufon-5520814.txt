Triangle, 5 large white lights, one small green & one small red flashing light

I was driving east on 195 the object looked to be a few miles away to the east. First I thought it was a star, then I thought it was a plane, then I thought it was a helicopter. Once it got over 195 it followed the highway going west. As it was approaching the highway I could tell it was not a helicopter. As it got over me I could clearly see a large triangle shaped object with a metal surface and 5 large white lights.
