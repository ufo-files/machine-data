{rjl} Large blackish object.

I was coming home from work arriving at my complex around 11:45 PM on Sunday, March 10th, 2019. I made a right into my complex then a quick left. Through my front windshield at my one o’clock position I saw a very large object about 200 or 300 feet above the ground. It was moving slowly. I stopped my car and took in all in. It was large, blackish in color. Like a gunmetal black. It was saucer or oval shaped. It had a smaller upper level attached to it. I could make out the outline of the entire structure and it had these turret like raised joints sparadically on it on both layers. It had two red blinking lights one on each end of it. Do not know if I was looking at the front or the back, think the front...on the bottom lower half attached to it were three very large lights. The middle one was a very large oval orange red light. On each side was a smaller round orange red light. Regardless all three lights were very very large. I purposefully looked into the oval one and saw nothing but cloudy orange red light. It was making a low humming sound. I was scared. After about 20 seconds of looking at it I pulled my car under my overhang. I waited in the car until I no longer could hear the noise. About 40 seconds. The noise seemed to disappear in the north west direction. I wish I had grabbed my phone, but I did not want to make myself too obvious. I was just plain scared. I have seen TV shows about UFO’s. Have never seen a picture of one like this. I work in the airline industry and this was not a drone, plane, helicopter or a blimp. I have 20/20 vision. It was quite impressive and very glad I got to see it because I will never forget what it looked like.
