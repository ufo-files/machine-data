{dr}Second night of seeing orb shaped, reddish orange object

On July 4, 2014, myself and my 2 daughters reported seeing orb shaped objects (3) in night sky around 9 pm. Tonight, July 5th, 2015, in hopes of capturing object on film, myself and my daughter went outside and waited,hoping for another appearance of object, this time with binoculars and camera with telephoto lens. Around 9:15 pm, we observed one single reddish orange, orb shaped object travelling from East to North in a straight line trajectory. I began taking photos and tracked the object until it disappeared going north. Once again, it had a smooth, mechanical, straight line trajectory. If this is a satellite, it's almost as low in orbit as the ISS, only the ISS is not reddish orange in color. It is also as bright as a star.
