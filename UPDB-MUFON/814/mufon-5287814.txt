Came upon a silver missle-shaped object roughly 20-25' long hovering 6' above ground at 11:30 am Sat, May1, 2021 on motorcycle ride in rural area.

On May 1, 2021, my friend and I were riding our motorcycles South East along Route 79 toward Warner Springs. This is a rural, desert area and we had not seen any other traffic or vehicles in the area for approximately the prior 5-6 miles, which would have been at the junction of Route 371. At 11:30 am we came over a small hill. When we reached the top we both noticed a missle-shaped object, approximately 90' off the right side of the road. I looked to my friend beside me and asked, "do you see this?" He replied, "Yes, I do!" At that point, it was hovering approximately 6' above the surface when we first saw it. At this same elevation, it then moved forward slowly about 4-6', then the tail-end dipped downward, the nose came up slightly and the object ascended in this path for only a few seconds, before it pointed its nose straight up, accelerated upward and was out of sight within 10 seconds. 

It was a perfectly clear day with blue skies and no obstructions. This object vanished instantly before our very own eyes.

There was no sound, no combustion, no dust, no lights, no disturbance of any kind. Once it was gone, there was no visible evidence it had been there.

The two of us were amazed by what we had seen and the fact that we could no longer see it. It was gone within seconds, even though it was approximatly a 25' long object. The object was the shape of a a missle or a bullet with two very small fins (approx 2-3' high) on its rear top side. Otherwise, it was a solid looking dull grey color (not shiny, not reflective). We didn't notice or see anything resembling windows, doors, or other openings. We discussed it briefly and continued on our way. 3 days later, I, Ron, reported the sighting to Mufon.
