Bright light disappears, reappears, and then shoots off

I went outside at my work and decided to look up at the stars for a bit. Noticed a bright light moving in a straight line from west to east in the northern area of the sky. Watched the light for approximately 45 seconds. Assuming it was probably a satellite, I decided to mentally "ask" the craft to do something that would prove to myself it wasn't a satellite.(I only thought of doing after seeing groups demonstrate the same on "Close Encounters of the 5th Kind" months ago). Approximately 15-25 seconds later the light stopped in the sky and disappeared. I kept my eyes on that spot thinking it might just be a cloud that I hadn't noticed covering it up. Approximately 10 seconds after disappearing, the light appeared again for roughly 5 seconds and shot off along the horizon form north to south at an insane speed and vanished.
