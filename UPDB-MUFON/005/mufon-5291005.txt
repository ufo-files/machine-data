V shaped array of about 9 dull or reflected lights

Stargazing from our hot tub at about 1:30 AM Nov. 19, 2020. Observed a V shaped array of about 9 faint lights travel quickly across the sky, taking only 3-4 seconds to come into and go out of view. Similar in color to Mars, but faint. Very similar to a V shaped flock of birds, which it may have been, but appeared to be lights. Sorry no camera. Hoped for a repeat, but no other occurrence was observed. Reporting here in case anyone else saw this.
