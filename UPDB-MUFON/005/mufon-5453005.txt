Very bright ball of light

I am a retired Railroad Conductor. At approximately 0200 hours we pulled a train we had made up to the south end of Massena yard to be taken south by a road crew. Our switch engine was headed short hood north giving panoramic vision as the first car attached was short as a gondola. Looking North On the West side is the Taylorville power line From The Moses-Saunders Dam at Massena Approx 10 miles North-West A bright ball of light followed that line approx 1.5 miles South to a branch line then went East Approx One Quarter mile to the then new 765 KV line which runs South from Quebec Canada To eventually New York City Grid. It carries a tremendous amount of power. The ball of light then went North along the KV line maybe 2 miles to a transformer station.  There was a tremendous flash lighting the whole sky like Daylight Then everything went black. The only electricity was our locomotive. Our station operator turned on a portable and asked if we were all right as our high powered base station was out until power was restored.
I have nothing to relate the size of the ball of light to. I would guess between a softball and volley ball at the largest I would estimate the speed at less than 50 MPH the time duration was between 2 and 3 minutes. Railroads work 24-7s so we are out at all hours of the day and night
