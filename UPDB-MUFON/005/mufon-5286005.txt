Stationary object with short vapour trail

This is a remote part of Newfoundland - very little traffic/population, and beside the ocean. Weather was calm with full blue sky and very little wind.
As I drove south on The Viking Highway, I noticed what looked like a high level aircraft, high in the sky, with a short vapour trail. After around 10 minutes, I realised it wasn't actually moving - as a private pilot, I understand that in order to have a vapour trail, there has to be hot air created by thrust. I pulled over and got out to observe and take a photo. After a further 10 minutes, it moved upwards at a 45 degree angle until I couldn't see it any longer. The entire observation was about 20 minutes. Another motorist also pulled over and had been observing the same object. There was no noise or any other unusual occurrences.
When I checked the photo's I took, most didn't show anything, but one did. Unzoomed, you can clearly see the vapour trail. Zooming in shows a blurry, but bulky object that does not resemble any aircraft that I am aware of.  The thing that peaked my interest was the fact it appeared stationary for around 20 minutes unyet was producing a vapour trail?
This is the 2nd time I have witnessed and reported a UFO to MUFON. Several years ago, I reported a bright glowing orb that tracked my aircraft over Lake Abitibi in the Sub-Arctic.
