On 6/22 myself & 3 others were leaving an event in Orange county NY. What we thought was the North Star began moving erratically, up, down, circular, swinging, triangular, erratic and pulsating motions. It was very high in the sky but we could clearly see it. It pulsed then appeared to burst then come back together. The object is round, with either another smaller round object on top or an indent. It also appears flat and cylindrical & moving very fast.
