Large bright light rapidly dedcended from sky to surface/beyond tree line.

At approximately 7:30 pm on October 27 2016 I was driving down Joseph Howe drive going South. Upon passing under the 102 exit overpass, I witnessed a large bright white object decend at an incredible speed at a near perfect 901 angle. They incident only lasted about 2-3 seconds, and from my perspective the object appeared to be slightly larger than an asprin pill. It appeared to be quite far away, likely over the ocean. I lost sight of it beyond the tree line. The speed at which it fell was so rapid that it almost appeared to be artificially propelled. It did not have a tail and no debris or sparks were given off. It's light did not flicker or change in the slightest. I have witnessed many meteorites of various kinds and this did not display the typical characteristics. It appeared to be artificial. 

I felt as if it was not a natural phenomenon and it was quite shocking.
