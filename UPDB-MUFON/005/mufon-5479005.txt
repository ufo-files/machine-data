Seen a very bright light in the distance from Bedroom window. A while later it was gone

I was putting my son to bed and through his bedroom window which faces north east towards Lake Ontario when I saw this very bright light. At first I didn't pay attention to it but then after a few minutes I realized it was beside the apartment buildings at the lake front by Green Road. The sky was clear and it was very windy outside. I thought it was a helicopter but it stayed in one place and didn't move at all. It had no blinking lights just a solid bright light. after about 20 minutes I went downstairs to tell my wife and I could still see it from my back door. I checked before I showed my wife and she thought I was crazy but when I turned my head to tell her it is still there when I turned back it was gone. So I went back upstairs where I could see more clearly and much further. I saw nothing, if it was a helicopter or a plane I would have seen some light in the distance but it was as if it got turned off or disappeared. I have not seen anything since.
In the morning on the news I heard of a airplane that had to do an emergency landing at Pearson Airport and it dumped its fuel over Lake Ontario. But that happened around 6pm, the light I saw was after 10pm. The freaky thing was that it didn't move and then just gone.
