Travelleing N on Hwy 34 - saw bright orb like object to E in front of low clouds - Zipped hortizontally out of site

I was travelling North on Hwy 34 on my way to work when something caught my eye to the east. I saw a bright shiny orb-like object hover then zip very fast to the west horizontally into the clouds and gone. This happened at 7:55 am. It was fairly light out and noted the low clouds to the east. At first I thought it was a falling star but it flew horizontally out of site really fast into the clouds. I tried to rationalize what I saw but I'm not sure what it was. I also had another sighting on my way home that afternoon which I will report.
