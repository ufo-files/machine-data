Alligator Alley Florida Sighting

Me and a friend were driving home from Miami are to the west coast of florida. It was about 9:15 on 9/18/11. We were on alligator alley headed west, after about 15 mins of being on the alley we see a white light flashing way ahed of us. As were driving you can tell theres more than one light. We get about 30 feet away probably and it was about 70 feet above us. It was a perfect square. There were multiple lights areound the edges. The lights pulsated and thats how I was able to see the corners on the object. It was easy to tell that it was a square. It was grey in color. Bright white lights. It was so close to the ground it seemed like. There was absolutley no sound at all. We have no idea what else it could have been. It was moving very slowly in the median above our heads. Very big. Shocked at what we saw.
