{jpg} Looking for satalites that night like I have been doing all my life.

When I have time at work, I go up on my companies roof and watch for satalites or the space lab. While looking SE, I noticed an object moving at a very high rate of speed moving from the SE to the NW. I first saw it about 30 degrees above the horizon. It was goldish in color, and it's brightness was about half that of Mars. It traveled towards the NW very fast, and when it was overhead it made a unbelievable "S" shape maneuver and then it was gone. There was no noise like a jet would make, and it happened around the same time the cameras on the space lab showed a "UFO".
