{tjd} Two star-like objects moving in circles are if in a dance which suddenly shot off at very high speed

My ex-girlfriend and I witnessed something in the clear night sky that changed my perspective on the UFO question. We saw two lights that would have been easily mistaken for stars had they not been moving. They each moved in circles and figure eights as if they were dancing with each other. The stars in the background remained stationary, ruling out the auto kinetic effect. We curiously watched for a couple of minutes or so before they shot off in the same direction at what was clearly a very high speed. The skeptic in me was satisfied. Now, if only I could figure out what I saw.
