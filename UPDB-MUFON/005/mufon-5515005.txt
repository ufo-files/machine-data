{cak} Huge shooting star

This is a letter I just wrote to 2 friends in Chicago-we all travel a lot for work and keep having sightings lately, in my friends pictures from all over (Chile, Columbia, Mexico, Brasil especially for work) the things are almost in every damn pic and were feeling a little freaked. I hope you are the good reputable MUFON folks we see on TV, we don't know where else to turn and value our professional privacy. Dont get us started on the damn clouds either!


9p-out walking Dog and looking at the sky (I do a lot these days) to see the clear weather since storms just passed. 

I saw a huge shooting star! It was triangular and had no trail and OC has just as much light pollution as any metropolis-lived here half my life and NEVER saw a meteor. It didn't burn out yellow-red like meteors do but made an angled trajectory and disappeared...up. 

Heres where it gets fun...I started walking again, goin "huh" and seconds after, a huge unmarked aircraft with 4 engine pods passed over low, looked like a table was on top of it, no civilian insignia or lit tail, or dots of windows down the fuselage... flying where Ive never seen an aircraft cross before (again, within the Disneyland no-fly zone) and moving away from LGB or SNA approach/bank/landing space that I have watched for years and am aware of from my brief pilot training at LGB.

THEN very low and loud enough to shake car alarms on, 2 Huey Cobras go in tight formation whizzing over my head following the table top plane (er.. uh... the AWACS?) one was almost completely lights out with only red/greens and the follower had a huge bright searchlight forward-not on the ground.

What do you make of it?

Really weird: had the same sighting in same area (my 1st) 20 years ago.

Goin to MUFONs site now...
