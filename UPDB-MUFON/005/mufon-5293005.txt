Very large planet size craft

I was on a boat at Seeley Lake, aprox 10 pm, looking west. Spotted a large star/planet like craft in the sky, it morphed into a v-shape and jetted further out west. It then re-appeared and did the same thing, and then appeared to hit light speed to the west.
