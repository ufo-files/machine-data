My husband and I were returning home from the store. We were traveling east when we heard a humming noise. I looked behind us and saw a saucer type craft. It was at a 45 degree angle to our car. We were scared. The craft followed us for approximately 1 1/2 miles until we pulled up in my parents driveway. then ascended and disappeared.The craft ascended straight into the sky and vanished. We never saw it again.
