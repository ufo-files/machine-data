White and red spheres over Clearwater FL

My brother and I witnessed two spherical lights, one white and one red, swoop down across the sky in the south from right to left at a high rate of speed. We lost sight of them after they passed behind a tree. The red sphere was behind and to the left of the white one as if flying in formation. They moved from SW to NE and began flying upward just before our view was blocked by the tree. We both lost sight of them at that point. The spheres appeared to be about twice the size of the star Betelgeuse which was visible at the time. The sky was clear. We didn1t hear any sound.
