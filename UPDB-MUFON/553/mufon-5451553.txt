UFO pulsing light with a distinct aura. Moved in the sky erratically. Then vanished.

Shortly after midnight, on July 11, I was coming into my my house from parking my car in my driveway. I am not one to watch the sky regularly, but on this particular occasion, I kept "hearing" in my head, "Look up, look up!" Hesitantly, I looked up, and saw a UFO pulsating in the western sky.
