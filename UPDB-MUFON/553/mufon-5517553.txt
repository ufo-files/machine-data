{flg} look like a flame but not kept getting higher

4th july during fireworks in seattle. So many people saw it on my block everyone was outside. Also seen in other states. Very reddish orange and kept going higher and higher then faded out as it headed south. It was nothing from this world that I know of.
We just getting a fire out then we saw it in the sky coming from the south.
Someone saw it in the sky as a firework was going off. 
I thought it was a firework that explodes in the air, and then i noticed it was getting higher and higher so then i noticed it was not it.
It went south to north fast over the sky for about 3 minutes until it faded it first looked like a firework then the flame looking thing at end of it was gone and made more a circle shape.
I wish I seen more because I was in the house getting everyone outside. I thought was was being visited or just checking us out on the 4TH July. At first i thought it was a meteor or comet and I was like no because its going up in the sky.
The object finally faded away in the sky like it went out or it was just so far that it faded away and we could not see it anymore.

Now i see on YouTube and the internet. That it was not just in Washington state. Other people seen it more than once and the same sighting in other places and no one on TV talking bout it is strange to me.
