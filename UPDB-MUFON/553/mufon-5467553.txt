{dr}Red light in sky

My son was popping fireworks outside. I was watching television. He told me there was a red thing in the sky. I told him, "its a blood moon". He said its not the moon. I walked outside and he pointed to the sky. I then saw a red light blinking the sky. It looked like a bright red star. It was so bright. I first thought it was a star when I saw it. The circular blinking star blinked red. I ran inside to grab my camera, and when I came back, it was gone. It vanished out of the sky. It was the craziest thing I have ever seen. I live in Hilo, Hawaii.
