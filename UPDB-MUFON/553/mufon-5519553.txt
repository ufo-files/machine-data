2 Orange/Red glowing lights in succession over Huntington Station, NY

I saw these two Orange over Reddish lights flying one behind the other heading North to South crossing Jericho Turnpike at about 1 mile East of Route 110 on 4/7/12 at 8:45 pm, in Huntington Station, NY. They made no noise and I realized immediately that they were very strange. Misjudging their speed I attempted to run back in my house to get my camera. When I came back out about 15 seconds later the last of the two lights had entered the low ceiling clouds to the south of me, and disappeared before I could snap a picture. They were very bright and solid with an Orange light on top and a smaller Red light on the bottom. I am reporting this because I believe the there are many more people who witnessed this.
