On June 1st at 9:47 my friend "S", her 2 kids and myself were at their family cabin on a cliff over looking Cayuga Lake near King Ferry N.Y.
 We were all on the screened in porch that looks over the lake watching heat lightening on the other side of the lake. I happened to turn my head and saw a brilliant orange ball coming over the lake toward us. At times it seemed to be pulsing. It had come from the Interlaken area. I jumped up and said, "what the hell is that?" "S" turned and said, "oh my God, what is that?" 
 I grabbed my camera and took 1 picture inside through the screen. Then went to go outside and yelled, "light, I need a light so I don't fall!" "S" grabbed the light and we both went out. I took another picture, then remembered the zoom. So I zoomed in and took the 3rd picture. I couldnt get anymore as it turned south and it started to fade and was gone. We both could see it through the leaves off and on and it was just gone. Some where in their "S" told her daughter to be quiet...we then realized there was no sound at all from it. 
 It totally confused me..I remember thinking..a plane..no red and white blinking lights..no a fireball..no tail..no a meteor..no tail..no..What is it? 
 Later "S" and I came to the realization that we had seen something special and that it had to be a UFO. The whole thing was only 1 to 1 1/2 mins. long...yet seemed an eternity. 
 I sent the 3 pictures I took to Sam, head of Mufon here in N.Y. He said he will send them on to you. Thank you for your time. [Name removed/cms/tg]
