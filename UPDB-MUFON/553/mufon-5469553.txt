{rjl} Hovering triangle with red corner lights and a pink middle light

My partner and I were driving north on route 85 in Raymond, Maine. I noticed strange lights coming from the NW direction, and when we got closer, it became stationary and hovered over a house on the east side of the road.It was very foggy, and we didn't see any other lights in the sky. We pulled over to get a better look. It was a dark triangular aircraft with red lights on the corners and a larger pink light in the middle. The lights were solid, not pulsating. It was SILENT and hovering over a house at tree top level.There was no down-wash. My partner exclaimed, "That's a ufo!", and once he said that, the craft began to move away towards the north, and the lights began to pulse like an airplane. We were also driving north and it appeared like the craft was moving parallel to our vehicle. After about 10 minutes, we lost sight of the craft.
