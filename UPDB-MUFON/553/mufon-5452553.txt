treetop level round ship w/ windows 3 short grays and one tall standing behind the three

Was with a friend and walking down the street at night summertime,just before getting to my friends house we both stopped and looked up to see a ship about 45 feet round 30 feet thick at its center.Silver in color The middle was all windows divided into large sections a bar was seen on the inside at the lower part of the windows interior. we could see three grays lined up at the windows and behind them was a tall gray. The interior was all lit up white and all four just stood there motionless and seemed to just stair at us .there was no sound coming from the ship. As we stood there and stared at this I keep thinking This is awesome, After about 2 min or so I saw Mark my friend run off to go into his house. I continued to look at this ship with the aliens as they continued to stare at me. I was now thinking this is awesome and others had to see this to. I then went run into the house and remembered nothing after that for years.I will never forget it.
