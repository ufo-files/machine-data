Bright green emitting white light all around color moving

I first saw it at about 45* it was slowly descending on an angle. After it stopped it disappeared. It stoped about 5ft off the ground. It was beautiful. No windows just brightly colored a most beautiful greenish bluish. The colors were moving. It emitted light from all around. It was completely silent. It felt so positive like a fairytale almost. I got a happy feeling from it. It didn’t scare me at all. It was in a wooded area in my backyard. It stopped directly across from where I was standing. After it faded out and disappeared I waited a couple minutes then I walked over to see if I could touch it there. There was nothing there. It was small about 5 ft by 4 ft. I went back outside at the same time many more nights but never saw it again. It was only visible for about 10 seconds. I painted a picture of. It by memory. It’s not exactly the right shape. The shape was very unusual, triangular but not a triangle. It had more sides than three. No lights or windows visible.
