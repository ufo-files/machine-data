V Shaped UFO with lights moving low in the sky

I was sitting on my porch here in the tech center of Denver and looking at the stars with my Celestron Binoculars. I enjoy doing this on clear nights as I have recently received the binoculars as a gift. Completely clear night tonight. I got my wife out onto the porch to show her a star cluster above the positioning of Mars in the night sky. When she was lowering the binoculars down. I saw a noticeable smudge moving across the low sky with my eyes moving SW. I grabbed the binoculars and was able to see a v shaped craft maybe 25-40 yards across within a mile of sight. It had 15-30 yellow/orange lights on its underside that were visible but very dull in luminously. The craft moved southwest and shifted from a visible v position to a flatter position in which the lights were no longer visible. It appeared to pivot down to flat. I saw the craft for 2-3 seconds prior to having my binoculars up, and a good 6-7 seconds while using them. After I lost the sight of the lights, I no longer could distinguish the craft from the sky. Completely silent, and very slow moving compared to the planes I see constantly near the centennial airport very nearby. Had chills the next 30 minutes or so from the experience. I have seen satellites and many aircraft nearby. The size, speed of movement, shape and proximity of this craft was undeniable. It appeared to be more hovering and traveling not much more than 40 mph.
