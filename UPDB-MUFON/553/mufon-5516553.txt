(GS) Bright color changing stationary light

12:30am morning of August 8th, 2012. Going to sleep my eyes caught notice of a bright twinkling light in the sky. It changed colors and brightness similar to a diamond shines when you rotate it under a light. Sometimes it seemed that the lights followed a line like movie theater rope lighting, and other times the lights were more bundled together. Predominant colors were white and red/pink. Ive never seen such a light in the sky before. It was really like a diamond! There was no rhythm to the flashing.

It was quite a distance away, but I am unsure how to judge. It was bigger than any star in the sky, but small enough that I could cover it with an asprin.

What I found most striking was that it didn't go anywhere. There was no flight path... it just kind of bumbled around in an area I could cover with quarter. The movement was irregular and sometimes jarring. It mostly stayed still.

I attempted to record it with my camera. Unfortunately I don't have a tripod to counteract a slow shutter speed but I tried to keep the camera as still as possible. There are trees in the photos to use as a reference point.

I was mesmerized by the object for at least an hour but eventually I fell asleep. During the daylight today I don't see any object in the sky.
