Slowly moving luminous object

I first noticed the object above the tree line as an indistinct blob. I grabbeda pair of binoculars and when I returned the object had moved and was larger. Iobserved a distinct shape as if two opposing crescents had been joined togetherwith a large point in the center of one.The object continued to move slowly across the sky, growing larger and fainteruntil it was lost to view almost directly overhead.
