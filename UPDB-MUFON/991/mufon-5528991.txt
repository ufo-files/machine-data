triangel shape ufo with 3 lights hovers over the house and trees.

I was in my bedroom at home waiting for my wife and kids to get back home. My son ran inside and told me to come look at this thing in the sky. I stepped out onto the front porch steps and there was a black triangle floating over our house and the tree tops. It had 3 round white lights before each tip. It was massive. No noise. Just a vibration in my chest. I ran inside to get a video camera and when I returned outside my hands would not work the camera. I was just fixated on the triangle. It hovered and moved very slow. It slowly moved over the trees and toward the east out of sight. I really don't remember it leaving. just remember it wasnt there anymore. after my family went inside I stood outside with my digital camera in case it came back. two low flying black helicopters with flashing lights flew over my house followed by two planes. I flashed the planes with my camera and one turned around and flew over my house again. I remember feeling sad when the triangle was gone. I wanted to be close to it. like I missed it.
