Flying brilliant fuchsia light

This encounter was witnessed from Archangelos village Rhodes, Greece.

I felt a strong urge to go outside and look at the sky. When I stepped outside and I looked up (towards the east) I saw an extraordinarily brilliant and bright fuchsia light. The size of which I estimate to be about 1/6 the size of the moon.

The direction, course and velocity was constant. The distance may have been 30 to 50 km high.

I observed the object for approximately 10 seconds then I decided to run inside to get my camera. This took another 10 seconds.

When I got outside again the fuchsia light was further along on the horizon and was appearing smaller in size.

I managed to snap a couple of photos but...I had the flash on and the images are overexposed.

Ultimately the light vanished very high up in the horizon sky. This led me to believe that the object was very high in the sky.

The whole encounter took approximately 40 seconds.

This is not the first time I've seen unidentified flying objects in the sky. Members of my family have witnessed them as well. In fact, my mother and sister have seen a very similar object last summer (2013).
