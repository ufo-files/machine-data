{dr}4 -5 Flashing orbs

At about 9:30pm on June 19, and 20th, I was looking west and low on the horizon and I saw 4-5 bright, intermittently flashing lights moving in the sky above Rocklin or Lincoln, CA (I couldnt tell how far away they were). They looked similar to stars or planets from my vantage point but they moved in an nonrandom manner. They would move away from each other, then they would come back together getting very close. I thought I would see them merge together but that did not happen. Most interestingly, 3 of the orbs would form a flat, slightly slanted triangle, keep this shape, rotate the triangle a few times, and separate. This is a very clear event I have witnessed over the last two nights at the same time and I cant believe no one else has reported it as it is very obvious that these are anomalous. I first saw them on 6/19/12, was so amazed I thought I look into the sky again the next night. Sure enough, they were there again and doing the same things. I am going back out tonight to see if they are still there.
