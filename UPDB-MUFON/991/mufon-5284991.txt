At approximately 10:30 one evening, my up river neighbor called and asked if we saw hovering lights to the west of our property.

My husband and I both went outside and observed a hovering object less than one mile from our house hovering overhead. To the naked eye, it appeared to be triangular. We got out the binoculars and further observed the object. Upon looking through the binoculars, my husband said, "I've never seen anything like that before." I looked through and neither had I. We observed what appeared to be three separate triangular objects hovering above the farm next to ours. We watched for several minutes. Then I grabbed my camera and took a handheld photo using a 600 mm zoom. Then it was gone...appeared to have traveled to the west.

The next evening we all spotted the same image. Then I remembered the photo and posted it to Facebook and another neighbor reported seeing a similar object around the same time period. 

The photo is not very good because I was shooting the zoom handheld. I actually just grabbed and shot and never thought I had captured anything.
