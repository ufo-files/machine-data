Monday evening I was walking to my car during my lunch break. A storm had just past and the sun was setting. I decided I would snap a photo. The sun was blinding so I wasn't looking directly at it. I snapped the pic on my iPhone then finished walking to my car. I received a phone call and completely forgot about the photo until I was flipping through my photos the next day. I noticed a spot On the picture. I tapped to zoom in and activated the "live" feature. The orb moved and quickly darted off the screen. I never heard anything or saw anything. It was all captured in the photo. I don't know how to upload it in a way that it can be viewed "live"
