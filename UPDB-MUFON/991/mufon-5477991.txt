{cak} Got a Video of Orange lights seen over Wilmington, OH 2-13-2017 Clinton Memorial

We were sitting in our truck in the Clinton Memorial hospital parking lot when I looked up from the corner of my eye I seen a orange glow. Thinking it was lanterns I quickly shook my boyfriends arm so he would notice. We looked up and seen 4 orange lights appear out of no where and slowly disappear in sequence in a slight diamond shape. We were confused and joked it could be ufos so we kept looking up this time we had our phone and camera out. About 15-20 minutes later we seen more lights appear but this time in a triangle shape (4-5) of orange glows like lanterns that didn't blow or float away, and one appeared and lingered for a little. We started to get hyped up because we finally had a short video of them appearing and vanishing. We seen the same thing slightly scattered and just as we were about to call it quits he pointed out more to the left in the sky a cluster of them bundle close together about 7-9. Seen multiple reports and one matching our story in the same area same town same night.
