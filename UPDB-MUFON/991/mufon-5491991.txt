{jpg} Bright light woke up inside craft during surgery and extreme pain

I was nine years old living in a secluded small village lakeside. It was like any other night I went to bed. I suddenly woke up in the middle of the night I'm guessing around 1 am. A blinding light coming through blinds like there was nothing there. It kinda sounded like a jet but I was on a bunk bed and the other kid in the room did not wake up nor did anyone else. I tried to yell and hide but I was stuck in place only able to move my eyes. Next thing I remember I am face down on a bed with my face through a hole like a massage bed but made of metal. I couldn't move but there was an extreme pain at the back of my head. A cutting feeling just above where the spine is attached to the skull. I felt them put something in there it felt like the size of a piece of rice. I was then flipped over and saw grey extremely tall beings slightly larger heads, long fingers and large dark eyes. I then passed out. I then woke up in my bed naked and it was still night but hours later . My pajamas were never found and I even got in trouble for it. I never told anyone until recently when I had a few drinks I told my fiance to look at the back of my head I could still point out the spot where I felt the pain. There is a small scar exactly where I said and sometimes that area gets inflamed still. When I told her my story her face went white. It was nice to finally tell someone and confirm that there was a scar. It was also a terrifying realization for me. I also suffer frequent headaches because of the pain. I still have nightmares about it to this day.
