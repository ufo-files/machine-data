UFO sighted over Fayetteville/Springdale, Arkansas area on 4th of July.

While seated on the south side in the Arvest Ballpark stadium on the 4th of July, 2010, my family and I observed three lights together, coming from the south (the direction of Fayetteville) toward the north, clothed in flaming red and appearing to be on fire. As it continued on a steady path toward the north, it seemingly continued through the fireworks display at the ballpark. The object was moving slowly, as if observing the whole fireworks display, and after coming through the area disappeared to my vision. Though it began as individual lights, it appeared after about 10 minutes to glow flaming red. It seemed to be cylindrical in shape, and continued its journey northward until I could see it no more. I jokingly said to my granddaughter, I hope the pilot of that thing didn't catch on fire, going through all those fireworks! At first sighting of the object, I told my family to look at the ufo. They laughed at me, as usual.
