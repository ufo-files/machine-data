Orange Ball of Light Fading away

Stepped out of my house to have my nightly look at the stars. I was facing to the east from North Manchester. I noticed a bright star or some planets bundled together( I think it may have been Uranus and Jupiter) to the South East. It was left of the Moon. While watching that bright star. A light came on to the left of the star/planets. It came on for about 4 seconds and was orange and then faded off. It reminded me of the older car lights..orange colored and slowly fade off. I know it wasnt a plane because it never moved.(yes I know sometimes they turn there landing lights on for a bit and if they are headed straight at you ..it may look like its staying in place)After the light faded out there was a black spot in its place. I could see a round black spot because the moon was so bright and I saw it with my binoculars. The black spot stayed in position for a half an hour. I looked down for a second and looked back up and it was gone. I missed seeing the orange light with the binoculars.. When I went to reach for them the light went out.
