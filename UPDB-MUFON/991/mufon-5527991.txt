blue green light filled sky

I was sitting at the computer in the kitchen(which looks into my back yard) reading the news online when the most brilliant and beautiful blue/green light lit up my backyard. It only lasted a few seconds, but it made me feel warm and was the most gorgeous thing I had ever seen in my life. It reminded me of water you see on resort brochures. It was so pretty! Afterwards I just felt this longing to see it again. I still feel that way.
