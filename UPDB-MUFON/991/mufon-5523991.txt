5 orange pulsating lights hovering over Lake of the Ozarks

At approx. 9:45pm as I was sitting on my deck and I saw very large orange pulsating lights. They were rectangular shaped and there were 5 of them as I recall. I only saw them for 5-10 sec. before they simply went out. There was no noise at all and since I had lights on behind me I was unable to tell if there was any type of vehicle. I didn't have time to get any pictures. I recently spoke with another woman who saw something the same night. She said what she saw was out of "Close Encounters of the Third Kind" a large disc shaped object with beams of light coming from the bottom. She said this happened at about 8:30pm 8/15/2011. I called the local sheriff's office and they said they didn't have any reports of strange lights.
