{cak} I know this is going to sound crazy but I saw a ship emerge from a wormhole. not kidding

I have seen a number of UFOs starting with my first one on a navy base in Rota Spain with my brother and Mark & Eric McGinnis; but while living in Florida I say about a dozen, one was very close to the ground about a block from me, black triangle floating about the street about 65 feet. I am sending this report about the wormhole I saw. I kid you not, while walking my dog late night i looked up and saw a dull-white cylindrical light appear and then a yellowish/gold light came shooting out and then disappeared shortly after emerging. It took me a little while to convince myself what i just saw; I had a bit of a hard time telling myself "no, I didn't see a ship exiting a wormhole". After saying that to myself a few times i realized i really did see it. I am still stunned today when i think about it. wormholes are real. i still shake my head in amazement. i do feel lucky and privileged to have had this experience.
