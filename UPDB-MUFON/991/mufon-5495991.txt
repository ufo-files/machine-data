in 1976 i went out at mid night to change the water in a 100 acre field of grapes. about half way done around 12:30 i say 3 seprete strange light not far from me, over my neighbors house on bell rd. so as i was looking at the nearest one to me i didnt know what it was it would turn on a very bright light on shinging down to the ground then the light would go off. then moved about 50foot or so to the north then light would come back on again like a 16 year old kid that i was i wondered what could that be it didnt make any sound and had blinking lingts going around it. i had a kerosene lantern in my one hand and a shovel in the other hand. so like a dummy i started swinging the lantern around and around trying to get attention that is when it came my way .right over the top of me , scared me so bad i took of running and dropped my lantern in the water and my shovel too i made it about 30 feet when that bright light came on above me. ill never forget that bright light . then as this may sound odd, all the grape vines were going strate up, i grabbed the grape stump and post and wraped my leggs around it as well as my arms and hung on . it felt like as trying to pull me up. thats all i remember, the bright light went off and the grape vines stop going up and came back down i let go of that stump and ran as fast as i could from what ever that thing was, across the back side of that 100 acre field to my house, i woke up my mom and dad, but they didnt believe me. it was very hard for me to go back out in that grape field and finish changing the water that night , but what i cant figuer out, it was 1:00 am when i woke up my mom and dad?
