Looked like Venus but flying really fast in a straight line

Me and my girlfriends family where watching the lunar eclipse when suddenly her eight year old cousin noticed something flying, that made me first notice the object. I have no idea what I thought the object was when I first noticed it. The object looked like Venus flying faster than an airplane over us, in airplane height. But I can tell that it was not an airplane, I know a lot about astronomy and I can tell that this was not an airplane. When I noticed the object I thought this have to be a ufo, I have never witnessed it before. I lost the sight of the object when I couldn't see it in the sky anymore.
