8 ufo over livermore

My kids woke my husband and I up around 2:30am. We have a large window in our bedroom and happend to look out. We seen 8 bright flying objects in the sky. These were not planes or stars. They hovered around for hours. I was able to catch them on video. Three of them were lined up and pulsating. One on the side of it was an orange color of light, and the others were bright and white like star looking. I went back to sleep for awhile and around 5am woke up and caught to ufo still hovering in the sky these were so bright and white. This all took place in Livermore California on September 19,2009. This isnt the first time, this happend on August 14, 2009. I have a feeling we will have another good view tonight.
