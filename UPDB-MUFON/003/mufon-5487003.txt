Sighting of unknown object in the sky

I was out walking with my Mother in our gated community. I looked up and noticed a huge object in the sky. It was one straight vertical line with 4 antenna looking lines coming out of the top at angles.

I had no clue what this might be. It pulsated and glowed white with tinges of red. The antenna like lines at the top moved up and down.

Our neighbours came along about 30 minutes later and asked what we were looking at. They did not noticed it until I pointed at it. They thought it might be the space station. We stood there staring at it for another hour or more.

It was not physically moving in the sky, it just hovered. We finally left and went home. I took a picture on it with a small digital camera. Due to the low pixels, etc. the picture came out with it looking like a boomerang. We were not scared or nervous as this was so far away.

A couple of years ago while surfing the net, I ran across pictures of a UFO spotted closed to the sun. It looked exactly what we saw hovering in the sky in July 2007.
