{tjd} Saw a large glowing red orb

I took my dog out at 10pm. I was standing in an elevated area under a large tree when in the distance I saw a large red glowing orb appear above homes. It moved to the left slowly, would stop then move on towards the NE direction. The area it was in is also elevated, between where I was and that area there are large canyons, high powered electrical lines as well. Before this sighting I saw one of these red orbs outside our bedroom sliders at night. It was sometime after midnight, we were asleep and I was awakened by the red light in our room, it was very bright! . I turned over and it was a soccer ball sized red glowing orb outside our sliders, through the curtains. I have long dark curtains and it was so bright I could see the detail through the curtains! It stayed in one spot and appeared to be glowing inside almost like fire. It then moved to the other side of our sliding door then became smaller and disappeared. I didn't move, just watched it. I didn't wake my husband because he had work in the morning. It stayed there on our covered deck for at least 5 minutes. It appeared to have movement counter clock wise. I didn't have my phone on my nightstand or I would have taken photos. I did not have my phone with me when I saw it in the distance either. The sighting outside of our bedroom sliding door was early January. I felt afraid and that my privacy had been violated to have this orb looking into our bedroom in the middle of the night! I don't sleep as well as I used to since then! I will keep my phone on me at all times now!
