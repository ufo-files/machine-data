{nd} Round object white an blue with hole in center turning clock wise

On 11/28/2012 the moon is full and bright with some stars visable. At approximatly 1945 PM I witnessed 2 fast moving aircraft flying east. I was stairing at the moon at the time the aircraft went by. That night I noticed 2 bright stars. One large at the 11 oclock position and other at the 4 oclock around the moon. I brought out my telecope to get a closer look. The large object at 11:00 position what I could see was round, white an blue with a hole in the middle rotating clockwise. The other at 4:00 was just very bright white. I stayed out to observe for 1 hr an 15 mimutes I would have stayed longer but the temp outside was around 28 and falling. On the following night 11/29/2012 at 2000 hrs or 8 PM I observed the moon again but the objects are now gone. Im sure there were others that had to see this. I wrote in to see if I could get some feed back on this event.
