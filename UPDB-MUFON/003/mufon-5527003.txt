I saw 2 very bright lights moving towards me from the South East. It seemed to be moving slow1

Monday night about 11:45pm1 04/11/2011 Skiatook, Oklahoma.. 
I was out walking our dog and looking at the very clear night sky1 we were walking out in an open field away from a lot of light and I was thinking how clear the sky was1 

I saw 2 very bright lights moving towards me from the South East. It seemed to be moving slow1 it took about 5 min for the lights to get over head and that was when I could see this object was triangle shaped with 3 lights on the corners with dimmer lights on the leading edge. If I had to guess I would say that the object was moving about 50 to 70 miles an hour with no sound. Also I would guess that is was between 500 to 700 feet in the air and if you would hold a playing card out at arm1s length it was about that big looking at the card short side to short side.. I could not see the craft it1s self and could only see the lights. However as it went over head it blocked out the stars. As it moved to the North West1 the lights on the corners did not seem as bright and it only took a few min. for the object to move far enough away that I could not see the lights any more.  I could not hear any noise coming from the object at all and it stayed on the same course the whole time I saw it1 

The whole time my dog was running around at the end of her long leash exploring clumps of grass and did not seem aware of anything strange going on1 we were about 2 blocks from the highway and the whole time I could still hear the noise of the traffic. 

I did not have any strange feelings as the craft passed over head and had no lost time or dizzy feelings. To be honest, I did feel a little excited. Excited because I have read and listened to a lot of sighting stories, and at last I have had my second sighting, the first one being back in 1976.
