A co-worker was giving me a ride home. Above Geist I noticed 3 bright lights in a triangle formation in the sky. Mind you it is cloudy out...no stars and no moon. So I point it out to my co-work who proceeded to say "What the F... is that?" I say "I don't know but that's not an airplane." As we are slowly driving forward. Then we see two more lights side by side following behind. One would speed up then slow down. Then another and another light all fly in a speed I don't normally associate with any type of plane. And I know planes my partner is a aircraft mechanic / inspector. Anyway my friend turned on to the main road at which time the lights kept coming some would follow the others for a distance then shoot off in another direction. While some would seem disappear in their very spot. He and I were discussing what it could be and all I could keep saying is this isn't right this just isn't right. He's freaked out but we keep driving until we reach a round about at which time we turn north and pull over. We sit there and watch one, two, a pair, one, one, and so on. We sat there for at least 10 minutes. They just kept coming but my friend started stressing and kept saying "I've got to turn around now" and I kept saying just wait a minute but he turned around headed back to round about at which time we could still see some coming as we turned right for about 2 minutes until trees disrupted our view as he was driving. I think I was much more excited than my friend. I think it scared him a lot. By the way there was other people driving so maybe they saw something too.
