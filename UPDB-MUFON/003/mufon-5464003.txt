{cak} Bright white cylinder object moved slowly among starlights. Arizona University observatory.

I was searching for Internet streaming webcams from Arizona while monitoring 10 webcams of the ocean for hovering bright lights in the night skies.

I noticed at first I was looking at stars from a university webcam in real time from the time stamp displaying the seconds of the hour. I was happy at that instant. I continued looking for more webcams of the ocean from Europe and Canada. I glanced at Arizona University Observatory webcam and noticed a bright white cylinder or rectangle moving slowly among the stars. I verified the camera is from the University url. I thought it was aircraft lights blurring from the limitations of camera's CCD sensor. I watched the seconds from the time displaying on the camera's screen and realized it may be a blimp (at the time of this posting) moving slowly. The stars twinkle while the cylinder object is not moving. Not moving due to the CCD sensor. I made a screenshot of it's movements. The object change it's length when it appeared a few seconds away from the camera's field of view at the North west. Not a blimp. The object is out of view from the observatory camera's view.
