Descended to tree level stopped an hovered there silently for approximately 15-20mins

I was with my mum an dad at the milking shed,at between 6-15-6.30am as its milking time,
I was 6 years old at the time, we all saw this object, i clearly remember it was rectangular in shape approximately 1.5-2.0 meters high approx 6metres wide by 10 meters long 
was silver in color ,was silent had no engine noise, had bright pulsating lights which emitted unevenly
my dad grabbed his shot gun an we ,my dad an i walked over to it while my mum watched from the shed
my dad yelled out at it telling them to piss off ,get off my property before i shoot ya, ya bastards
but we couldn't see anyone 
my dad was upset because it had upset the cows they were uneasy not settled
I was more interested in what it was an who was driving it, how was it possible to sit above the trees an the trees weren't moving
like the lights was holding it up,
my dad an i stood there for at least 10minutes just looking at it
10-15 meters from it, as we were almost directly under it at this stage
we could hear a hum of some description like a speaker drone
the lights slowly increased speed rotating the object 
then started rising up with no engine noise
we walked backwards away from it as i think my dad was afraid of it as it was like nothing we had ever seen before, an something that made it even more strange was the object started to turn over like a wind mill,like walking up a flight of stairs that's how it moved it moved directly into the path of the sun rising, we watched for 5minutes or so till it was out of site, as we walked back to the shed my dad told me not to talk to anyone or tell people about it or they will think we are crazy/mad an telling stories about things we don't under stand,
Years later when i was in my teens i would speak to my mum about it an she would say it was definitely something not from our time an best we don't talk about it, as for my dad he don't want to talk about it, just told me to forget about it, its in the pass,
Im now a 52 year old grand father i have told my 2 daughters ,wife about it my grandsons are to young just yet

I strongly believe that day that something happened to me that Ive never got over like i never feel alone an i have experienced some really serious death accidents an health issues that can not be explained,
one that is still fresh in my mind is i was driving into work one morning on the fwy-way had the Cruz control set to 110kms/hr when out of no way a Deer jumped in front of my car smashed the whole area i was in an i remember climbing out of the car an looking at it saying to myself how could i possible survive this accident like its impossible i should be dead, Ive survived at least 10 maybe more, id really like to speak to someone, ill wait for a reply i think of said enough, thanks Marc
