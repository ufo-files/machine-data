{jpg} Cratt Ascending

I observed a white craft {Northern LA County}, it was leaving a white puffy trail like certain planes do but it was traveling completely vertically, ascending in a perfect strait line, I watched as it went through the cloud layer and continued until it disappeared into space... I watched for about a half hour to see if it would return but it did not. It was moving at a rate similar to a regular airplane, it was not going fast like the usual ufo. Plant 42 is about 10 miles from where I live.
