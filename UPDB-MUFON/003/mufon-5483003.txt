{nd} Glowing object changed direction and shape

On 6-7-2016 at approx. 7am, I was traveling east bound on Nevada approaching Sherwood, in Detroit Michigan. I noticed in the distance, directly east of me a shiny chrome/silver, round object in the sky. The object appeared to be approx. 1-2 miles away and about 1,000 ft in the air. At first I thought it was a plain landing with it reflecting the sun. The object was slowly getting closer and bigger, but did not change shape or color. The object was maintaining altitude, not losing or gaining. Suddenly, within a fraction of a second, the object was now going west, gaining altitude as if it was ascended into space. Shortly after the object went from west to east, it was gone. This event lasted around 20-30 seconds. The object never changed shape, color or reflectivity. It never gave any indication of a flight pattern to justify a human air craft. From Wes to east without stopping or turning. I have had several past experiences with similar circumstances but just found out about this site today. My last expierence was approximately 7 years ago in St Clair Shores Michigan.
