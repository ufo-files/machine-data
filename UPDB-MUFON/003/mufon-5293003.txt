Glowing snake like and moving.

I was walking home at around 10 P.M. I noticed a light source almost directly above me. What I saw was a moving, glowing snake-like thing that was moving like an earthworm moves when they are agitated. I would estimate that it was higher than 500 feet directly above me. It emitted a whitish-blue glowing light. I reached into my pocket to grab my phone to video this thing but unfortunately as I brought my phone up and started pointing it towards the sky the object literally just vanished in a matter of seconds. The whole experience lasted maybe 45 seconds. The experience really kind of blew me away because I'm a very rational science minded person but I am one hundred percent sure of what I saw although I can find no explanation for it.
