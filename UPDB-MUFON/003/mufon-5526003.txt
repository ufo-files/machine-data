Very bright light low in altitude 1500ft max, heading west to east over lake, dead silent.

I just saw this a half hour ago, I was at Longs Pond just outside city limits of Fort Collins. I was on the shore looking out across the water when I noticed a bright light low in the sky coming from the west end of the pond. I observed this light for no more than 3 or 4 min move in dead silence west to east, first it was moving in a straight line, but a little over half way across the pond it seemed to move up and down in a very odd manner as it continued to move east. It was at this point I knew that this wasn't any aircraft I have ever seen. The way it started moving up and down it seemed as though what ever was in control of this craft was not operating the way it should. I continued watching the craft until it disappeared behind the trees of the east shore on its way toward the Budweiser plant.
