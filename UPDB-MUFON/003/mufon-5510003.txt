{nd} A flashing red orb traveling West to East and deposits a mysterious red light on 2 different cellphone towers and when the light disappeared, so did the red lights on the cellphone towers.

I was in my friends house and at about 2:45 in the morning I left his room to look out the window. Before the UFO I spotted came into view the cellphone towers were completely dark. However when the UFO came it seemed to drop something on the 2 cellphone towers as it passed. The object was a flashing red light which flashed every second of the 5 seconds I saw the object. After it disappeared the red lights it deposited on the cellphone towers went out at the same time. At first I thought it was nothing until it started blinking red. I was going down stairs to get a drink but my sighting mentally disturbed me. I quickly returned to my room and have never forgotten those 5 seconds. I still am disturbed by my sighting and am wondering what was in the object and where it ended up. This was my first out of 2 UFO sightings. The second one was disturbing also. Now I am a believer!
