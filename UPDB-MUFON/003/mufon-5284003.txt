Four to Fie Doughnut Shaped Lights over Cary and Raleigh NC

My family and I were driving up Westlake, by Westlake High School. We first spotted five doughnut-shaped lights with a blue-green hue coming out of them, in between each doughnut was a line of white blinking lights, the lights covered an area of about 100 - 150 feet at a 45-degree angle less than a mile in front of us. The lights were hovering and moving northeast toward Cary, moving too slowly to be an aircraft sans a drone, and too big for a Helicopter. At this time we observed them as they ducked below the Treeline just as we passed the construction of the new 540 interstate. My Family witnessed them again when we got to the Westlake 10-10 intersection. We returned to our house and saw additional lights moving southeast at the intersection of the ten-ten and Fayetteville Rd. At this time my wife took a video from her cell phone camera until they moved out of view.
