Star-like mobile UFO

Walking at night, I saw what I first thought to be a bright lone star or planet. When zooming in with my phone, I noticed that the structure was moving in various directions and sporadically "blowing up" in a firework fashion and reappearing a small distance away. The event continued for nearly an hour until I went home after my walk.
