Triangle/boomerang shaped craft with 3 white outer lights and a red middle light

my husband and son (aged 12) witnessed 2 triangles either thursday or friday. 4th or 5th November as they were coming back from our local shop. One flew right over their heads, it had three, solid white lights, a red one in the middle which was flashing and a white light which appeared to be flashing on one of the corner lights. They said it made no noise as it flew overhead. The other one was off in the distance and flying in another direction. My son came running in to get me to see but by the time I got downstairs and outside it was a fair distance away but I could clearly see a row of lights from the back which seemed unusually long for so far away so it did not look like a plane,it had red and white flashing lights with the red light to the right side. My husband also refered to it as boomerang shaped as well as triangular.
He described it as going slightly slower than a plane would. As he watched it go over he says he was just in awe and amazed by it.
