Orbs in triangular formation approx. 500ft across

I was leaving a buddy's apartment on Cook Street. As I was putting the key in the car door, I noticed 9 bright red-orange objects hovering in the SW sky. They were orbs in triangular formation about 500ft across. 6 orbs were stationary and 3 were twisting while ascending and descending.
