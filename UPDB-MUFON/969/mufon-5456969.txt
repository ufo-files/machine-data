{rjl} While I was at work I noticed a strange silent featureless craft fly overhead, I took 3 photos of it.

When I was at work (GDC Technics) on November 16th 2019 at 3:35pm CST, I saw a strange, silent, featureless object fly directly over my post at an altitude of about 800 feet. I only noticed the object because I saw it reflecting sunlight off of its surface. I work as a security officer and was in the guard shack that day when I noticed the object, It was flying west directly over the north end of the alliance airport landing strip which was especially strange and dangerous considering it’s low altitude. I Began taking photos of it and as I zoomed in on my phones camera (IPhone 7s+) I realized the object had no discernible features, no wings, no tail, or any markings on the craft at all. I showed the images to a few coworkers of mine who have spent a considerable amount of time in the air force, one as a boom operator (30 years), and the other as an Air Force intelligence officer(13 years), neither they or myself could come to a definitive explanation as to what this object was.
