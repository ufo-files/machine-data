{cak} A string of lights hovered then moved very slowly - could have been one object

As reported to ASD in person: "My wife and I were getting out of the car when we noticed a string of about 12-15 reddish/white colored lights hovering in the sky above the trees. We watched, wondering what it could be when it started moving very slowly right over our heads. The lights all moved in unison, as if it was one large craft. We saw the stars disappear above the object so there must have been a craft involved although it must have been very dark because we could not see a structure. There was no sound or vibration associated with the object. We were both dumbfounded. We watched it for approximately 20 minutes, then could no longer see it. We wish to remain anonymous"
