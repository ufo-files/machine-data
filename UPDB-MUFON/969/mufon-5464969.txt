I was laying on my girlfriends deck to her house watching the stars late one night in little valley, at first we noticed there was what had seemed to be a shooting star that we saw from a distance and it was bright green which seemed odd to us but we paid no mind and continued on watching the stars and the event happened again not once not twice but three times we saw what had looked like a shooting star only everytime we saw It again in the course of an hour or two it had seemed as if it was getting closer to us and I only thought that because the streak it left in the sky as it passed by was getting longer and brighter in color. After awhile of talking about what we thought it may have been there was this massive glowing green disk/circular type ship about the size of the house and possibly the property we sat on slowly emerged just above the house and tree in the yard and came right over top of us, as you could imagine I couldn't keep my emotions together as I yelled and shouted with excitement my girlfriend held my mouth shut as she didn't want me being to loud. But she was very much excited as well and we couldn't believe what we were seeing. The ship slowly flew past us and out of our sight over the tree line at the back of her property leaving a light trail of greenish blue color behind it that faded quickly after it passed by. To this day I have been intrigued with UFO sightings and just everything to do with them really.
