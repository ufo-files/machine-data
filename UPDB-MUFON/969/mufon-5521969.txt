Flashes like lightning, Three huge balls of twinkling light

Driving from Kirkland Wa to Duvall Wa and observed flashes of light like a meteor or lightning. Turned onto Woodinville-Duvall Rd and observed more flashes and then three huge balls of light that were moving at tree top level. Lights then began to twinkle/flash as we drove farther down the road. Lost sight after about 1 mile.
