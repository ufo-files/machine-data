{jpg} Stationary light in sky looked like star but became incredibly bright before dimming and traveling west.

I was looking to the sky like I always do at night... looking for shooting stars and satellites or whatever else strange I might see. There was no visable moon and from my home there is little to no light pollution. I was smoking a cigarette and looked to the sky behind my home. Just behind and to the right of my home in the distant sky I spotted what looked like a star brighten INCREDIBLY!... It looked similar to what you would picture an incredibly bright meteor if it was heading directly towards you... (no tail or obvious movement). After several seconds, the light dimmed to the point of a dim point of light in the sky and then started moving thru the sky. No movement while it was lit up and as soon as it dulled it clearly started moving west until its light dimmed enough I couldn't see it anymore.
I see satellites every night as well as meteorites and this was like nothing I've ever seen or have a clue. Satellites nor meteorites stay stationary for any amout of time an then just start moving randomly and distinctly. I wish I could animate what I saw, or that I was an artist because I dont think my description can give this justice. I would have run indoors for my phone to snap a pic, but I knew the resolution would never pick up anything that far at night and I was afraid I would miss something if I looked away. I will never forget what I saw last night-
