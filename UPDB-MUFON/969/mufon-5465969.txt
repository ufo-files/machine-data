Jet-like object seemed to drift, then stopped moving and descended vertically behind the treetops.

I was travelling in a coach from Oxford to High Wycombe on the M40 and observed what at first appeared to be a low-flying white jet aircraft travelling over the hills at Aston Rowant. The movement was very odd and slow, and the nose of the craft appeared to be pointing at an angle away from the direction of travel. The craft stopped moving and descended behind the trees as if it was landing vertically, though since it went below the horizon it is impossible to say whether it actually landed.
