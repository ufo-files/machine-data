{nd} Observed lights travelling north to south at incredible speed.

I was with a friend talking on a rooftop patio after supper. I observed objects travelling south at incredible speed. My friend jumped out of his seat and we watched the objects go out of sight towards the horizon (south). We both counted 7 lights or reflected light objects unconnected in a curved geometry. Not a V, more like a half oval. The remarkable thing was that the distance they covered was miles in the period of just seconds. Of course not knowing the height of the object can change our perception of speed but even if the objects were only a few hundred feet above us, they were moving at thousands of miles per hour... without any sound. The only thing close to this was an SR71 about 25 years ago which the sonic boom afterwards damn near broke my eardrums.
