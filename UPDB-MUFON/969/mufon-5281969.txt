Lights

Lights seen wandering in sky around 11pm NE of Provo, UT area. Lights were very high up in appearance and travelled extremely quickly for the perceived distance. Star-link not visible at this time or location. 

One light traveled east to northeast, paused here and there and then seemed to exit the atmosphere at extreme speed. The second appeared right after as if arriving from the direction the other had gone to and continued its path west while wandering and pausing here and there. The second light stopped and flashed an orange color with an immediate response flash of the same color from another stationary light I thought was a star. 

After no more movement was seen I went inside. Entire witnessed time of movement is about 10-15 minutes. No scheduled launches found via internet search. Flight paths of commercial aircraft in the area make it easy to recognize conventional aircraft. These lights did not match standard commercial or private aircraft indicators or movements. Elevation appeared too high/distant for personal drones.
