The fast moving objects moving across the sky.

It was Father’s Day and our family went out to our backyard to roast S’mores. It was June 21, 2021, at 9:47 p.m. when we all saw the first big, orange firey object hovering bright in the sky so my husband video taped it on his phone until it just disappeared. Then just four minutes later, we saw two more big, firey objects at a diagonal to each other fly at high speeds in the sky and my daughter video taped this on her phone. We were amazed to see the spinning motion of the objects in the video and how they just eventually disappeared. The objects were flying at high speeds.
