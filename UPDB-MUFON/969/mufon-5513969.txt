{nd} saw ufo on the way to morehead

My wife and I were traveling to Morehead city, and were observing the moon. As we crossed the bridge leaving the town of swansboro, I looked to the left to look at the moon. At first I thought it was a tower of some sort, then realized that theres never been anything before, and to my knowledge no new structures have gone up. We arrived at Cape Carteret, and had been observing these lights for a few minutes. As we moved closer to Cape carteret, the object got bigger, like it was hovering over a forest in the distance. We pulled into McDonalds to grab a bite, and maybe 10 minutes had gone by when we left. I looked for the object and it was nowhere in sight. Im not sure what it was, but it wasnt an aircraft of any sort Id seen before. Im enlisted Marine corps, and theres 2 air statione in the area, so Ive been exposed to several different kind of aircraft, and would know if it were a conventional craft. I didn't have a chance to get any sort of picture of it, and my wife was eating, but she saw it as well. Not sure if anyone else saw it, but I think its a possibility.
