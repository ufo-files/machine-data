4 or 5 red dots in a perfect horizontal line, not outline of an object, moved at very strange pace

The dots/lights were in a perfect horizontal line, as if they were on the flat bottom of a triangle. They gave off a haze, almost like brake lights in the distance. They were moving synchronously, as if it was one object. It was moving fast across the night sky. Faster than an airplane, but slow enough to see it for about 10 seconds until it disappeared. The path was it took was odd and difficult to describe. It looked horizontal, but was gaining altitude almost like an upwards propulsion/hover while moving North. Could not see an outline of any objects, and there was no noise. I would best describe it as a square/rectangle, solely based on the positioning of the lights, but there could have been more than one.
