Silent, v.bright light, on curving path.

I was putting some kitchen waste bags into a dustbin, bottom of garden. Looking up, the sky was a tiny bit hazy, so stars not so bright. The bright light looked ball shaped, like a REALLY bright star and travelling leisurely on a curved path. Silent. No aircraft running lights. No beam down to earth.Coming from the South Downs and heading towards Eastbourne.(South coast of England) then presumably over the English channel. Definitely not a plane or chopper, just a ball of light, moving seemingly smoothly through the sky. Whole episode, about 6-7 minutes before nearby houses blocked view. Wishing I had a camera drone! 
I wasn't perturbed, just excited.I am 71 with a sound mind and body. Reported the event to local newspaper.
