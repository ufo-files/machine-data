{cak} Boomerang shaped ufo moving southeast without noise.

We stepped outside to look at stars. We had only been outside a few minutes when we saw a largish boomerang shaped object moving without sound southeast. We live in the city, so it was moving over the city. It was subtle and we would not of noticed it had we not been looking up. It had a faint orange fizzy glow to it. It was moving in a strait line very steadily. We observed it for about 10 seconds before trees and houses obscured it from view. This occurred at 2253 central time. We remained outside talking about it and watching the sky. It then made another appearance at 2301 central time again moving southeast but this time in a more southerly path. It appeared to be at the same altitude and again was moving silently in a strait path. We remained outside for about another half hour but did not see it again.
