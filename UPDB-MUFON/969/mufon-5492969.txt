{cak} Glowing green object in sky

I stepped out on the back porch one night. I believe it was in the late 90s, I was about 7-9 years old. I don't think it was cold outside, so probably around the summer. I saw a glowing green object above the treeline. It was what I would consider a classic shape- like a disc with a round top. It moved in a really strange way, like it would slide to the left and pause for maybe a second, then continue to slide and pause. I'm not sure how long I watched it or how I lost sight of it. I think my family was all asleep. I remember going into my grandma's room to go to bed and watched out the window, but I don't remember if I could still see it or not. I've never told anyone, I didn't think anyone would believe me.
