{jpg} Stationary

My friend and I were sitting in my backyard. I noticed a super bright "star" in the sky and pointed it out. Stars are hard to see with all the city lights in Chicago but this one stood out. So we both looked at this star for a while noting that it was super bright maybe 20 seconds or so. Then it started to slowly move in a straight line made a perfect 90 degree turn and accelerated away at high speed toward space. We turned to each other to be sure that the other had just witnessed the same thing and ran inside frightened. My mom thought we were on drugs. My neighbor was the only person who ever believed us.The glide path to land at O'hare airport is right beneath where we saw the object, but the planes travel east to west. The object after being stationary for a time traveled west to east, made a 90 degree turn and shot away out of sight at incredible speed.
