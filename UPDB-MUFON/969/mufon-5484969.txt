So me and my father was outside our balcony and witnessed 2 VERY bright lights.

Me and my father was at home and we went outside to get some fresh air, to look at the stars. Then we noticed 2 wierd orb lights moving insanely fast towards each other in a straight line, first we thought it was a satellite but it was to bright and it was moving to fast to be able to be a plane or satellite. So we waited and it looked like they were going to collide in each other and the lights began to look like 1. But they only went past each other and kept on flying towards east and the other light went west so we lost them. I was fascinated and me and my father was trying to figure out what it was. So i went here today 2 years later to write about it. I've never seen anything like it! This all occurred in Sweden Lekeryd.
