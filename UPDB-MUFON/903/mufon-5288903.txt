Saw a tic tac shaped object zipping across the sky flying horizontally broadside.

I was sitting outside as it was the first sunny and cloudless day in quite awhile. I was laying back in a lawn chair and saw what looked like a black object that looked like a pill or tube shaped object with the ends rounded like a pill capsule. It was flying broadside which was strange. No wings or any other distinguishing features, no contrail or any visible exhaust. No flapping or moving like a bird and it was much too high for that.
