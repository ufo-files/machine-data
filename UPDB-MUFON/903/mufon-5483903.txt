{rjl} 2 Low Flying, Dark in color Objects

Took dog out. Noticed 2 objects to my SSE. Low, moving steadily towards the East. Both were dark in color, possibly black. One flying in front of other maybe 500 to 800 ft up. The one in the front continued its flight path and the other banked left and was now heading NNE. No sound, just Blue and Red/Orange lights. While it banked left, I managed to get a picture on my cell phone. Once it completed its turn, I took another picture of it heading toward my house. It passed directly overhead. It looks bigger in the picture than it actually was because I had my phone camera zoomed in. The lights you see at the bottom of the picture is street lights and construction lights down a hill from house. The object is pictured making its turn before heading towards me. The second picture is after the turn, heading towards me. It continued its path and I watched it until I could see it no more. Have no idea what it was but it wasn't from around here.
