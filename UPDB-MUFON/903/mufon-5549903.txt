A horizontal shaft of darkness and a bright flash

12:55PT,Eureka,CA. While walking my son home from school26 the grocery store, he pointed out a contrail and jet. It was unusual in thatthere was a shaft of darknessthat the jet was on a parallel course with (bearingapprox.300 deg. over the ocean). He asked me why there was a dark line that thejet was following. As a NWS/FAA certified weather observer, I should have had ananswer, but I didn't. I say professionally that it was not smoke, but a shaft ofdiminished light, and the jet was following it exactly. Then, about 1000 yardsin front of the jet, at the terminus of the shaft, there was a very bright burstof white light, with a spherical object within. Altitude was approx.16-18Kft.The object then disappeared, and the shaft disippated after about 1 min. I haveworked aviation weather since 1993, and served on an aircraft carrier beforethat. I have never seen anything like this before. I know aircraft and airweapons. This was not a conventional aircraft.
