{dr}Cellphone photographing on coming thunderstom WNW. Eye in clouds with multiple objects in storm.

On Labor Day, 09/02/13, I, my wife, my sister and husband were visiting our mother in rural Elliott Co., Ky. Probably short of 5:00 PM, a strong dark thunderstorm was approaching from the West-NW direction. My sister loves taking pictures of storms and alerted me of an "eye" forming in the clouds in the southern portion of the storm. Me being an optometrist, was fascinated by this eye with iris, pupil, lids, and orbital area. We both were using our iPhones to photograph the cloud formation. Later that evening, when I returned home, I sat down to review the photos. To the northern portion of the storm, I noticed a tiny black speck in one photo. As I enlarged the photo, there were three objects. The nearest object was disc shaped, domed top with possible appendage on superior aspect. The bottom was smaller bowl shaped versus top dome. I called my sister to bring her attention to the objects. She had a picture with one object closer and larger and more circular in shape with dark gray coloration, possibly the bottom of object. After reviewing multiple enlarged photos, there appeared to be several objects within the clouds. The storm was moving straight line, not swirling. The objects seemed to move independently of the wind. There were five of us viewing the storm and not one noticed the objects, just the eye cloud formation. Those objects had to be someones technology, not birds, etc. I relayed this information to your representative, Allen Eppling; however, he cannot receive our photos by email.
