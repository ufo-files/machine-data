Strange lights over Chandler/Gilbert, AZ

Me and two of my friends were heading to a nearby city for some food. It was about 7PM, as we were driving down the road we can see a wide view of the sky, there were no stars out yet, but there was one really bright looking "star". I joked and said something like "hey look at that UFO" as a joke, and as we all looked again at the light, a second bright light appeared in the distance, and moved toward the other light. When the second light reached the first one, it stopped and the first light flew off and in a couple seconds faded out completely. After we ate at the fast food joint, we started driving down near an intersection at Power and Pecos and pulled over, when in the distance we saw 3 strange lights in the sky, which were stationary, then moved and made multiple formations (such as lines). Out of the three lights, the one on the right started drifting further away from the other two, as the other two drifted further way from each other, a forth light appeared in between those. These are the pictures I have, they are not that great, but at least its something. All three of us saw the same thing.
