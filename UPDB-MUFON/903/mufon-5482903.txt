{cak} My husband, myself, my 9 year old son, 5 year old son and one year old son were camping in Bostwick Inlet and we were abducted by a UFO

I didn't see any UFO but we were taken inside one.  I haven't had any hypnosis to recall what happened but I can tell you why I believe we were taken.  We went camping in a shack out at Bostwick and my spouse was drinking beer and the kids were playing games. It was just a one room house with 3 windows and 1 bunk bed. When you walk in you could see the stove and the beds were on the same wall as the door. One bed was regular height and there was another bed about three feet above the regular one. There was about 3 to 4 chairs there. The kids were playing games at the table and my spouse and I were sitting talking, I thought about my brother that had come there the day before when I noticed there was a bright light in front of the cabin. I thought my brother came back so I went to the door to greet him I opened the door and saw a bright light and the next thing I remember was sitting straight up in bed. I heard a whirring noise and I remember being afraid but I don't know why. I ask my spouse "Did you hear that? He was laying face down he said "Yeah" I asked him what did that sound like to you? He said "a space ship leaving" I don't know how the kids and my spouse and I got into bed. I don't remember. As soon as he said that we both scrambled out of bed grabbed the kids left all our food and bedding and ran to the boat and hightailed it back to town. The funny thing is we never talked about that for about ten years.
