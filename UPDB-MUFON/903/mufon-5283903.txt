Cigar shape

Saw at first what I thought was a satellite. I’m always looking for them when outside. It came from same direction as the other cigar shape ufo. This one was a dot that that seem to “shake” and speed up up momentarily, then stay on course. Thought my eyes were deceiving me, so I really tried to concentrate. Did the movement 2 more times and bleeped out. Head from west to east. 
That made me really wonder what I just saw. I continued looking up and about 30 seconds later this what I thought for sure was a blimp. So I pulled up flightradar24 and thought for sure I would see Goodyear blimp. No, nothing. I’ve seen the blimp many times and it always maintains it’s call sign. I then dropped my phone, pulled out my small scope, looked at it and about choked when was I expected to see lit letters only to see 6/7 huge panels of light that seemed to have something moving behind them. Not bodies of some kind, but pulsing movement. Hard to explain. Called a friend about 400 ft away, she saw it too. She said she heard a low sound of some kind, I heard nothing. I could see the outline, looked like a long shimmering soda can. I’m still looking through scope. Object made a turn towards due north, seemed to quickly dissolve while accelerating away within a fraction of a second. Could not believe what i just saw. No noise, no landing lights, just gone. Kept looking in same direction, no scope. About 7 or 8 minutes later I saw a long light north, satellite elevation. Lasted about 10 seconds, didn’t fade away but bleeped out. This happened over El Toro/Foothill Orange County.
