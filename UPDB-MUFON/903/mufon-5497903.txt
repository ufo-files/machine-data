{cak} Object flew very slowly overhead. I watched until it passed over a building and left my line of site.

It was between 6 and 6:30 AM, I parked my car in the uncovered parking lot of the building I worked in at that time. The parking lot is pretty large and I always parked in the same location about 200 ft from the building. I got out of my car, locked it, and started walking toward the rear of my car. At that point lights in the sky caught my attention. As I looked up, I saw a massive black triangle/boomerang shaped object with lights along the edge (all the way around the object). Even though it was dark outside, the object was darker. It was moving slowly over my head, as if just cruising. I stopped walking and just stood staring at it. I was mesmerized by the object and quickly came to realize that it didn't make any sound at all! It seemed so close as I could see the object and all the lights clearly. I stood and watched it for about 1 min until it passed over the building I worked in and out of my line of sight. Once it was gone I quickly went into the building and reported the sighting to a coworker. He just laughed at me and said I was having pregnancy induced hallucinations. This was actually the second UFO sighting I witnessed. I realize too much time has passed to investigate, but my thought is that perhaps my story might corroborate another sighting reported at the same time. I no knowledge that MUFON even existed until watching a UFO program on T.V.
