{nd} i just happened to look up at the right moment and 3 spheres silently past over

I was working on my truck and happened to look directly above and saw 2 spheres, one above the other, followed by a third at same altitude all in a perfect straight line traveling due north at approx 30mph maybe 200 ft high. It was 6:20 pm on march 30th 2014 and it was a crystal clear day. The objects I saw were crisp against the bright blue sky. They were perfectly round, were not attached to each other, silent, dull greyish color. They stood out against the sky. They were NOT balloons!!! I called a friend immediately to timestamp the event and drew a crude drawing. I viewed them only for a couple seconds until they went over the treeline.
