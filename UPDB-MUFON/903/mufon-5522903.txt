Happy B-day to me. I knew we weren't the only intelligient life! 30 witnesses

It was my b-day July 13th 1977 and I invited guest for Lasagne dinner.While I was cooking in the kitchen the evening, someone/one of the wilson children I think Sheldon) told me to come out and look. Something about, could I tell them what this is? I went out looked to the right on Summer Street down hill, and a disklike object with no sound was coming upwards(we lived on a hill)headed straight up the street. It's speed was possibly 25 to 30 mph the size of a baseball in distance. As it neared it got larger and larger until we all were able to see directly under it. On it's sides were, I guess a ring of light panels that blinked symotaneously according to the speed of the craft. It hovered over our heads and went over the hill higher than the one we lived by the children of the neighborhoods school #7 headed north towards New York. I say the craft's lights reacted tot he speed of the vehicle because of what happened on the news, shortly thereafter. Approxamately 7 or 8 pm(still light outside) on the news, Kennedy Air Force base picked it up and had five or six jets now flanking the craft, and the panels were now a glowing ring of light. Instantly caught on camera, it shot straight up in the air, leaving the jets scrambling not to smash each other because of pursuit directions during the flank. I thought ABC channel 7 news of New York did an excellent job of reporting this. Needless to say, I have no longer worn an egotistic veil of ignorance about other beings here on this planet. I have told my children and family that were there at the time that when the day comes for US to know it will be US that demand the truth.
