Line of glowing red lights

I was about to walk outside for my break and my manager was outside already seeing these things in the sky. I came out and saw what looked like red flashing stars flying over the building. I pulled out my phone and began to record. Some of the objects seemed to be flying to close together to be planes and they were flying slow. I immediately thought it had to be military planes because I knew it was not regular planes. I recorded 7 objects flying but there were more before I began recording.They sort of just disappeared into the night.
