(GS) 3 shooting star like objects followed each other, formed a triangle, hovered and spun, then flew off very rapidly

I was approx. 13 or 14 years old. It was a hot clear August night in Mohawk NY. A friend and I decided to spend the night at another friends house who had an in-ground pool. Around 11 PM we decided to go for a dip and cool off. We were swimming for about 20 minutes just messing around. I jumped off the diving board and swam all the way to the other end under water. When I stood up in the shallow end I wiped my eyes and opened them to focus. I saw what appeared to be a shooting star but wasnt moving extremely fast like a shooting star. I turned around to tell my friends and when I looked again, here comes another in the same path! I remember saying "I just saw 2 shooting stars in the same place!" that is when the third came! This one stopped on a dime, thats when I realized that all 3 of them had stopped and I also noticed that they looked a little different than the stars around them. The 3 of us watched in amazement as the traingular outline that they formed began to slowly spin, rotated about 3 times and took off just as fast as they came! We ran to the house and called Unsolved Mysteries lol (kids). Now, Being from upstate NY I saw alot of crazy things especially in the sky (which I will also eventually report when I get more time). This was the very most bizarre! If I am not mistaken I believe this happened right around the time of the Town of Columbia NY crop circle which was about 2 miles away on route 28.
