Glowing red triangle.

My wife and I were standing in our yard looking East when we spotted a glowing red object coming from the south flying in a Northeastern direction. We watched it fly silently through the sky above Auburn, IN at a slow pace. As it was becoming obstructed from view by my neighbors house I ran around to the other side to watch it. As it flew across the sky I happened to look straight up and I saw a large white object flying from the Northwest to the Southeast as it flew over head we were lucky enough to catch it on video. As it was flying off into the distance it faded away to nothing.
