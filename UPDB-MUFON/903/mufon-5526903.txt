Object off in distance, on top of me in sec's...then as quickly gone w/o a trace!

I was driving to work at 5:30 am west on Willis Rd, just past(west of) whittaker intersection in Milan Mi. This strange light appeared to the south west- far off. A triangle shape, with 3 lights- steady/not blinking. I have a HUGE back ground in avionics, and KNEW it was not a "plane". There for I watched it closely thinking... what the Heck IS THAT? It then moved SO quickly, all at once it was on top of my car. I locked up the breaks on my car, and jumped out in the street, it shot straight up,...and it was GONE! NO WHERE! I recently read that on the same day Selfrige Air force base had the same/similar sightings- confirmed w/washtenaw( sheriffs dept- officers calling in) reporting seeing the same type of things.
