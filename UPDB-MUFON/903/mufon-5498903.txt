{cak} Falling fireball that changed directions and took off.

A friend and i left his house headed to the store. While driving south i noticed what looked like a fireball falling into the atmosphere, But it was much larger than any other i've heard of. It continued to fall then stopped mid fall. At that point we pulled over and began recording it on the phone. Suddenly the object headed south at an amazing rate of speed.
