Burning orb, stationary, flames coming off

My friend and I were on a youth group ski retreat weekend in Hedley, BC. We went outside for a cigarette around 2am, and up above the trees was a burning sphere, about 300 feet in the air. From the ground it looked to be about the size of the moon on a clear night, but its actual size must have been 20-30 feet across. I could see flames burning off it quite clearly. We watched it for about 5-10 minutes, and it did not move at all. We decided to go back inside.It was very obvious in the clear winter sky.
