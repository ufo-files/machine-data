I was at 48-40-19N and 89-28-59.65W ---thought it was a satelite but it reversed course and came at me

I was at my cottage at Hawkeye Lake and let my dog out for a last piddle before going to bed. At 1:10 a.m. I thought I would see if I could find any passing satellites. Sure enough, one was passing almost straight overhead going from west to east. I was almost breaking my neck looking straight up with my body facing the north star, when from the top of my vision came another object from behind me (south) on what looked like a collision course with the first satellite. They crossed at almost a 90 degree angle. Both were the same luminosity. I watched both heading away from each other one going east, one going nearly due north. I wondered to myself if there were really UFOs up there when suddenly, the object going north came to a stop and seemed to hesitate in one spot for a few seconds. I thought that was pretty strange as I have seen countless satellites over the years and I know they don't do that. The object then reversed course 180 degrees without making a regular turn and it went from being a faint white to an almost eye hurting brilliant white/silver. It was getting larger very quickly and changed to a brilliant gold. When I last looked, it was about the size of a 81mg. low dose aspirin. I had the distinct feeling that I was being targeted, almost as though my thoughts about UFOs were being read. It appeared to be coming straight for me. I grabbed my dog, ran into the cottage and locked the doors. That is the most afraid I have been in the last 50 years. I got the distinct impression that I was being targeted. My wife doesn't have much belief in UFOs but she has never seen me so frightened in all our marriage.
