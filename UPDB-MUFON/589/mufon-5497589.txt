{dr}2 pulsating objects which separate and then one shoots a beam to some other object.

I was on the very crowded 118 and saw two large bright lights that slightly pulsated, one smaller than the other kind of hovering together and thought they were helicopters, but opened my window and didn't hear anything and they were very, very bright. They separated, the smaller one moved toward Simi Valley and the larger one remained still. I saw a broken line of light shooting out of the larger one horizontally to something smaller that would then light up. The freeway was packed so I'm sure there were many other witnesses to this sight. I was driving like a maniac trying to see this thing and could not take a photo.
