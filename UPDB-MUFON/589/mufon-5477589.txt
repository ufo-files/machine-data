I was on my way to get my husband from work at the gas company. I had to be around Feburary or March of 1975.It was around midnight. I had to drive through UCSB where I was at the top of a hill on a double sided road when I stopped. I couldn't believe what I was seeing. There was sgan enourmous cigar shaped object with a red/orange light that appeared to be speeding around the object. I was terrified. It made no sound at all. It flew over three miles in less than a second.Then it looked like it just lowered itself and vanished. I couldn't believe it. When I got home, I noticed that my neighbors were up. Needless to say, I was very upset. I needed to tell someone what I had seen. When I asked them what I thought I had seen, they said, a ufo. They had been on the other side of the road going in the opposite direction and had seen exactly what I had seen. I kept expecting that whoever was in that object would come and get me. Nothing happened. It should be noted that where it vanished was next to the ocean.
