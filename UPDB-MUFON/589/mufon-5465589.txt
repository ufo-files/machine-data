{dr}UFOs that disappeared while I was staring at them

I was driving north on I65 coming home from vacation and had just passed a sign for Cullman Alabama when I noticed objects in the sky. There were at least 10 maybe more. They were hovering and spherically shaped. It was a sunny day and I could see the suns reflection off the objects. The sighting only last about 15 seconds. My wife was in the seat next to me and I kept telling her 11 Look at that11. She was too busy with her phone to look up and I was getting pissed because I was serious. They were in a formation in a similar shape to that of the state of Minnesota. They were quite far away, possibly a mile or more. What has had me puzzled for so long and is disturbing to me is that the objects disappeared while I was looking at them. Not when I looked away but while they were in my sight. The best I can describe their disappearance is the sky was pulled tight like a sheet across a bed and then pulled in the center and when the sky went back flat like the sheet would do they were gone. 

I noticed the object because they were easy to see and not moving. The reflection made me think of metal or a windshield on whatever it was. I knew they weren11t birds because of the distance away and them not moving, etc. I felt I was in a state of like what the hell did I see. I felt lucky to have seen it when I did.
