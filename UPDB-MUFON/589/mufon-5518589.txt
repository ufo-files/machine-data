Suspected ufo photographed by Mt.baker wa

While hiking in the north cacades I and 3 others went to baker lake of of the Mt.Baker highway. We walked to just before the gate on the road thar goes over the dam and turned to the east to look at the lower lake and photograph it. This is one of those photos. This object did not show up in my photos. It is a photo taken by one of my group. He took several shots all within seconds of each other and the object is not in any of the other photos. None of us saw the object with the naked eye. We first saw it in the photo.
