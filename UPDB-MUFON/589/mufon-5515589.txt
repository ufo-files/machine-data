(GS) Orange Orbs about 25+ that arced across SW to SE direction

is it possible these were chinese laterns, maybe but they were very strong in intensity that varied in strength and brightness, even as some glided to east, some stayed very strong in color. in my 20 years plus of amateur astronomy i have never seen such a display of and array of lights that were consistently separated by about 10 arc degrees between each orb. they glowed in my binoculars and one solid source of light.

These lights drifted off higher up into the East.
