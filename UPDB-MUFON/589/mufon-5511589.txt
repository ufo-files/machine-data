(GS) Extremely fast moving triangle with the leading edges dimly lit moving at a high rate of speed over Knoxville.

My girlfriend and I were on our balcony around 945 pm on april 12 2013 located in west knoxville TN when I noticed a triangular object with the front edge dimly lit moving at an incredible rate of speed. I pointed it out to my girlfriend and she saw it before it disappeared to the north. The whole incident lasted maybe four seconds. It made no sound and the whole craft seemed semicloaked. The ligts on the leading edge of the craft looked like florescent tubes and were very dim. If there was a full moon I doubt I would have been able to see it. It looked like it was moving as fast as a shooting star would.
