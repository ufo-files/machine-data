Strange light descending on game camera.

Game camera on rural farmland caught three frames of an object moving from north to south descending. Three frames over 39 seconds catches the light in three positions from upper right to left of center, flaring brightly on the final frame and well below the treeline. (Note:The constant light on the center left is the light from a farmhouse about 3/4 mile away) The final frame convinces me that this light is not from a aircraft due to it being below the treeline either landing or hovering very close to the ground. The next picture is taken (tripped again by deer) 30 minutes later and the light is gone.
