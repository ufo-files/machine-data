{cak} UFO Jamesville to DC

I went outside for a minute and looked up to the Eastern sky, towards the Moon. For only a second, I saw a bright, glowing, circular object flying North to South at 8:05 pm, slightly more West than the Moon. This was about the same color as the moon and didn't make any sound. For an example, a satellite moving by in the distance would look like a spec, but this was maybe 15 to 20 times that size or around 3 times the size of viewing Jupiter in the night sky. I'm not a pilot, but being on many commercial flights at around 35k ft, I'm guessing it was roughly double that, way above the ceiling for many fighters. The travel speed wasn't at a blink of an eye, but rather too fast to be human. We're not that smart, at least, not yet. Picture SpaceX flying horizontally, which would be on the slower side. No exhaust plumes were visible.
