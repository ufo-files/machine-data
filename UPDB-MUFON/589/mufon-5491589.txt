Alien being not from earth

At the time of the incident, I lived in Otara, South Auckland with my (then) husband and 9 yr old daughter. The year was 1994. 
My husband had chosen a few videos to watch that night. I didn't really like his selection, so I retired for bed around 8-9pm on a Tuesday night. I think I might have fallen asleep around 10pm?
Not sure of time, but I woke to red/ blue flashing lights dancing/ moving around in my room, I figured it must be Police lights reflection beaming through my window...although I didn't hear anything outside, so I went back to sleep. 
I woke again...not sure how long after, to the room totally bathed in blue...but more of a white light, and I remeber thinking..."Geeze, haven't they (Police) finished yet! 
Anyway, I went back to sleep and woke up a third time. It was just coming on daybreak as I could clearly make out the furniture in my room...dressing table, clothes etc. 
It was at this stage as I was lying on my left side and I turned over onto my right side that I caught sight of 2...beings...1 1 short who had just dissapeared out of sight as it walked THROUGH THE WALL...and the other who was close behind the first being...who was very, very tall, and had an extremely large head! It's arms, legs and body were very thin...no shape at all to them like a human being, but very straight and narrow. It's shoulders almost came level with the height of the door. I noticed this because the wall which it (...they) had walked through was near the door entrance...NB: The door was closed!
As the second being proceeded to enter through the wall, it stopped, paused and looked back at me. Instantly I felt it had a 'smug' attitude on its face...although it showed no outward facial expression...I felt it was 'laughing' at me, almost as if it were saying..."what are you going to do about it/this?...you can't do anything!" 
It then turned its head...facing back towards the wall and continued to walk through it! 
I shared this with a close relative a few days later, but I don't think she believed me!
Through out my childhood, I have witnessed, heard and become physically sick from unusual sounds...feelings of sickness in the head...and No...these are not headaches or meigraines...but nouseous 'head feelings'
I am happy to undergo hypnosis. A freind once told me that she thinks I am being 'followed?' 

This isn't something I made up, but it IS something I need cleared up!
Since the incident of 1994...I will be honest with you...I have read a lot of information on UFO...alien abduction because I'm seeking answers and want to bring a 'closure' to why these things are happening to me. 

I would appreciate any help you could give me.
