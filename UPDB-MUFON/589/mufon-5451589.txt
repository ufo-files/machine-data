Video of cigar shaped obeject hovering for 5 minutes

Initially saw 2 objects almost straight overhead... only one object remained once I retrieved camera. The one was a square object with 4 lights in each corner moving slowly SW to E. The other object was grey solid cigar shaped with a white light on the top end which fluttered several times. This object hovered for several minutes, turning slowly. My video was moving because I could not locate the object through a small viewer, and as I zoomed, I was hoping to get at least one good frame. There are several included.
