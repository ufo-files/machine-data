Moving flashing light. Centered over county jail but not stationary. Moved slowly up state. Flash could be seen for nearly 5 miles lighting up the sky.

We were driving from barrow county to gwinett county and saw a strange flash in the sky. At first we thought it was police lights. When we got closer we realized it was in the sky over the jail and moving toward tennesee. The light was flashing but not staying in the same place. There were as many as four at a time but never in the same arrangement. It looked like one large light and 3 smaller lights.
