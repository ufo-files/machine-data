{dr}fishing with a relative

While out fishing with a relative at about 1:30 am asked what is that. when I turned in the direction he was looking I seen nothing I was looking too high. then very low in the sky I seen six objects coming out of the north in a very straight line going very fast they where, glowing a very bright emerald, green color. But you could still tell they had a solid structure to them. Then as the first one hit a certain point, it shot straight up leaving a green streak or tail behind it. as each one hit that very same spot it did the same thing, all leaving a streak or tail behind them. And they where gone!! We live in a very active area plains and jets helicopters, And the person I was with is a very reliable person with air craft knowledge. We had no idea what they where, we where stunned, by what we had just seen.
