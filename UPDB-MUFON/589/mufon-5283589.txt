UFO sighted

I have been seeking therapy for the past 17 years since the event. Through regression therapy I remember the event in Arlington, Minnesota.

I was on the trampoline in my back yard with my brother and my friend Joseph. I was trying to steal my brother's bounce when I fell and landed on my back. That very moment I looked up in the sky and witnessed a disc shaped object sitting still above us. I stopped my brother and my friend and pointed up at the object. We sat on the trampoline and stared at the object as it appeared to stay still in the sky. 

After a few minutes (Felt like it was hours) my brother shouted to get the camera. Joseph and I could not move a muscle neither could my brother Matt. We watched it closely and sat cautiously. It then darted NE and stopped. Then sat for an additional amount of time. 

My brother, Joseph and I sat and couldn't move. We were stunned and scared. The object then vanished behind a cloud and was never spotted again. 

As I got older, these objects have appeared many times, I have only reported one other event. 

My therapist says that this is from blocking out memories of fear and pain, that I repressed it due to how much it has effected me.

Last event was in 2013. When 3 lights appeared upon the day of my niece's birth.
