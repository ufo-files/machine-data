Glowing blue light

I was sitting watching TV one night and I noticed a blue glow shinning through the window behind me. I walk outside and saw a round what looked like a huge blue led light about the size of a large house about 15 stories high over me going at a slow steady speed. A few seconds after looking up at it it stops and starts to shake/vibrate then dashes very fast a short distance to the right, stops in place then starts shaking again and dashed left. Then it started back at the slow steady speed as when I first saw it till it was out Of sight. was around 1am and I think I had missing time. It just lasted a few minutes but I came back inside shooked, I remember seeing the clock say 4am. 3 hours later. Maybe I was so worked up that much time had passed until I looked at the clock after I came in. This event had me obsessed with trying to find out what it was or if anyone else has seen the same.
