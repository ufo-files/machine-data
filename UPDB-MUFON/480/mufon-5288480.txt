Two objects traveling S-N 30 or so degrees above horizon.

I've never seen a comet before, but that's what the primary light looked like to me. It traveled a path smooth and consistent from S-N and was about 10 time brighter than Venus. It had three trailing auras right behind it -- one directly behind and one each at approx 30 degrees off the trajectory. The space station?? A comet? It was incredibly bright and lit up the entire night sky. This one probably is explainable, but the other not so quick to dismiss. The other was much less intense and looked like it was following the bright light. It moved smoothly but erratically across the flight path of the former and intermittently emitted "flakes" of light -- almost like snow flakes -- as it moved. It ultimately disappeared to the NE and left a cloud as it disappeared. That object was in the atmosphere because the contrail changed form with the upper level winds. I called the Beaufort County Sheriff's office, but they had no reports of anyone seeing same. The first does not so much concern me. The second sighting does.
