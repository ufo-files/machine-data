{rjl} looked like beautiful bright large star. it got brighter then took off. it was gone in a second.

Me & my daughter were taking the kids to wait for the bus, it was still dark out. We noticed what we thought was a large, beautiful bright star high in the sky. We stood there admiring it for few seconds then it grew brighter & shot off! It never made a sound. If my daughter hadn't seen it with me I never would have believed my own eyes! We were both in awe from the experience but we were never brave enough to report it. I can't remember the exact date but i'm certain of the time.
