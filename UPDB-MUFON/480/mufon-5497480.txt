{nd} SAW 2 AT SAME TIME NEAR GLIDE PATH TO LAGUARDIA AIRPORT

MY FIANCE & I WERE PARKED ON SIDE ROAD OFF HWY TO BX WHITESTONE BRIDGE. SUDDENLY, AT TREETOP LEVEL, A COOKIE-BOX SHAPED CIRCULAR UFO GLIDED OVER CAR & CRUISED OUT OVER HWY VERY SLOWLY, AS IF IT WAS ON SIGHT-SEEING TOUR. IT KEPT ON CASUALLY, WITH A LINE OF PLANES OVERHEAD LINING UP ON GLIDE PATH TO THE AIRPORT. I EXPECTED TO SEE THEM ALL MAKE A SUDDEN U-TURN & GET THE HECK OUT OF THERE, BUT THEY DIDN'T. IT WAS AS IF THEY COULDN'T SEE THIS THING. BUT HOW COULD THEY NOT?? IF I STOOD ON THE ROOF OF MY CAR & WAS STUPID ENOUGH, I COULD HAVE THROWN A ROCK & HIT IT!! I THEN STARTED LOOKING AROUND THE SKY, & WHAT DO I SEE ALMOST DIRECTLY OVERHEAD? ANOTHER UFO! THIS ONE WAS WAY UP THERE, MUCH LARGER, & SHAPED DIFFERENTLY. ALSO IT WAS GOING IN A DIFFERENT DIRECTION. IT WAS LONG & NAERROW, WITH LONG ROWS OF WHITE LIGHTS OUTLINING THE ENTIRE LENGTH. THIS THING WAS HUGE. IT HAD A SMALL SHORTER LIGHT ON EACH SIDE NEAR THE FRON AT AN ANGLE, LEADING ME TO CALL IT A GIANT SLED; BECAUSE THAT'S WHAT IT RESEMBLED. THESE 2 WEREN'T TOGETHER AT ALL, LEADING ME TO EXCLAIM "WHAT THE BLEEP! IS THERE A BLEEPING CONVENTION GOING ON??" THE 1ST UFO, BY THE WAY HAD 2 OR 3 ALTERNATING ROWS OF RED & WHITE LIGHTS RUNNING VERTIALLY ACROSS THE BACK. THEY PULSATED WITH A STRANGE RYTHYM SORT OF LIKE THE LIGHTS ON AN AMBULANCE.
