{jpg} An extremely fast light appeared across the sky and moved in a straight line and then in a hook that my friend and I both saw.

It only appeared briefly when my friend and I were walking outside at night. We stopped in a neighborhood to play basketball and we sat down on the ground to look at the sky and suddenly a really bright thing streaked across the sky and we both pointed upwards at it. It moved too quickly to make out any shape. It looked like a shooting star but the line it made was too bright and too wide to be one and it also moved like a hook without slowing down when it turned. It scared me because I don't want to be abducted ever and we were right near a farm's open field with nobody around.
