witnessed sphere-shaped, blinking object while out running in Regina

I was innocently running through a back road quite close to an open field, minding my own business, when I looked up at the sky and saw a green and flashing red thing flying in the sky. At first I thought it was a plane, but it was silent and way too bright to be anything. It made me a little confused because I couldn't explain what it was. I was considering taking a picture and calling my sister to come meet me, because I was quite scared. As a took a turn to get back onto the main road, it seemed to get closer. It seemed oddly close and very bright. Half was solid greed and the other half was flashing red. I was half expecting it to send down an orb to pick me up. Since I was alone, it seemed like the perfect time to abduct me, but I ran faster because of that. After a few minutes of fast running, I stopped and looked around. It was no where to be seen. After about a minute, it reappeared. I sprinted home after that. When I got to my door, I couldn't see it anymore. Like it never happened. But it did. And it was quite scary.
