{cak} memory of possible abduction

I may have been abducted way back in the mid to late 1970s. I had considered what little memory I had of it to be a false memory. Until recently when, on a lark, I googled the location where it occurred and added "ufo". I did not expect to find another incident at the EXACT same location as mine that happened 7-10 years after mine. That one was corroborated by three cops who saw a craft similar to what I had.

Me and a friend would walk the railroad tracks from our hometown Jeannette to the now gone Greengate Mall in Greensburg, PA.

We would climb a steep hill from the tracks that led to the back of the mall. And leave the same way. One time when leaving the mall I saw a pill-shaped craft hovering silently in the wooded area. I ran ahead of my friend and waived at it. My friend caught up and the craft drifted over us. My very next memory is of my friend asking why I'm laying in the tall grass by the trail.  It was confusing to me as I had no memory of laying down. I don't recall what my friend recalls of this but he asked what I was doing laying there and I didn't have an answer for it. I do know we were now both late by 40+ minutes or so as best I can recall. So we headed off home.

All these years later I am wondering if something did happen or not. I haven't spoken to my friend about it ever. I don't know what his memory of it is.
I don't have a memory of any details of the craft since its been so long ago but I recall it being cigar or pill shaped and I could see it reflecting light. I don't recall movement of it other than it drifted back our way when I was waving at it.
Is all this a false memory? I'm not sure.
