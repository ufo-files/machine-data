{rjl} One orange/yellow fire type orb appears and then another, hovered for 10 minutes.

Three of us were on the beach watching the moon rise in Melbourne Beach, Florida on Sept. 19th 2013. Looking over the ocean one light orb appears and hovers before joined by another similar orb just directly above and to the south. These two light orbs looked as big as a penny, maybe a little larger from where we were on the beach. They somewhat pulsated, like fire, but were perfect circles and hovered, not changing shape or size. After hovering for about 10 minutes, the orbs seemed to sink towards the ocean ever so slowly, and then move back up to their original spot. After a few minutes, they seemed to disperse as if perhaps traveling east away from the beach, over the ocean more until they disappeared. A week later, talked to a neighbor who described the same thing from the beach just a mile south of where we were. In the picture attached, the moon is the obvious light on the right hand side. The two smaller lights are the orbs we witnessed.
