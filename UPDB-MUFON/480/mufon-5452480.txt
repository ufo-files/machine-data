UFO Sighting over home

Appeared to be shaped like a rectangular pillow with small antennas on both sides, the object was first noticed well above tree top level and continued to rise while following a northernly direction. While the object was traveling it appeared to flip end over end, causeIng the one side of the object to reflect the sun or appearing to flash on one side or give off a bright light. The opposite side seemed to be dull grey or lead color, at altitude it appeared to turn black or subdued in color. It stayed under the clouds and remained visible even for the small size it was.
