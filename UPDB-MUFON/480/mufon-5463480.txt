{cak} Saw a fast flying object making no sound during a break at work outside smoking with coworkers.

I was smoking a cigarette on break at work outside with coworkers when I looked up at the night sky like usual and saw a big object flying fast at low altitude which was not making any noise. It was really strange and I yelled to my coworkers to look at that and they stood up and one person saw it with me in time since it was moving fast and the building made the viewing time short. The object was darkened and looked triangular. It seemed as if it had three lights that were reflective and dimmer but constant with no flickering or flashing. They were at each edge of the triangle and was a bluish,white, yellow type looking solid light very dim. The object looked big and it had a dome typ border around it like a big invisible barrier that reminded me of some type of camouflage like in the movie the predator. The dome type barrier was a view from underneath so it appeared circular but bordering the triangular shape. Absolutely no sound and fast and looked like it was halfway invisible.
