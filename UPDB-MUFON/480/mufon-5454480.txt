{rjl} Line of stars in perfectly spaced formation, led by three light triangle, moving faster than a plane.

At approximately 0605 on 03/25/2020 I was out with my dog. I looked up and saw a stationary three light triangle. I thought what in the world? There was no flashing or sound and it looked like three stars spaced evenly. Then it started moving in a NE direction. Out of my peripheral vision to my left I began to see single stars lined up perfectly following the triangle, like a string of lights. Sometimes there was one star and then two, and so forth. Must have been over 25 to 30 stars/lights, maybe more. After approximately 2 minutes of watching this I ran into the house and got my husband to come out and he, too, witnessed the line of stars moving.
Living out by Tenkiller lake I see a lot of unusual things in the sky but this has been by far the strangest. It was beautiful and thrilling to see.
