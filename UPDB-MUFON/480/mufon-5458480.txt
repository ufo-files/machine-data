Paratroopers glide UFO and plane in video

We were watching the USC UNCpregame at Bank of America stadium in Charlotte NC. I took a video straight up as the Paratroopers were gliding into the stadium. Upon reviewing the Live Photo I noticed that the plane was in the lower left frame but there was a shimmering silver cigar in the frame with no visible wings and standing on end. I then took a still shot of the live photo and zoomed in and there are no wings and it appears to be 3 separate portions.
