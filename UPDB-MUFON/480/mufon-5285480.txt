Bright light that resembled a low flying plane but suddenly zipped away

I had just walked outside from having a dinner at my mom’s house and I happened to glance at the sky as I was walking to my car. I noticed a bright light to the east that was unusual. I assumed it was a light on the front of an airplane that was flying a lot lower than normal. I stopped to watch it to see what kind of plane was flying so low that late at night. Sometimes we have military aircraft (planes and helicopters) that fly to and from Fayetteville, NC at different times of day and night. They usually make a good deal of noise and this light had no noise coming from it. After a few minutes the light suddenly zipped away in an arc to the north and was gone. There was no trail other than what was from persistence of vision of it moving away so quickly.
