{rjl} I felt that a light appeared directly west in front of the direction that we were heading stayed there for at least the length of the turn onto route 31 until route 260. There was heavy traffic so there may have been other witnesses.

My boyfriend was driving west to bowling when we both observed a bright light. It was like no light we had ever seen before it seemed to be pulsing like back and forth like the clouds were coming in front of it; only there were no clouds that low that night. So there should not have been anything to obstruct the view of the light. It seemed to get brighter and break up like it was maybe going to come towards us in three. Again we commented it was unnerving because there was no light like that we had ever seen before. It seemed to go back together into one then go away slightly then like if you were driving and the moon was going "away" then it just seemed to be gone. We just couldn't believe something like that happened but the light was so unusual and the movement was not like anything I had seen before. Not a star or a plane. Unexplained for sure- I'm just glad I wasn't alone!!!
