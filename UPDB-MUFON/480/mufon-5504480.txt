large fast moving object accompanied by two smaller lights

I was alone at home just gone into kitchen to make a drink when I noticed a very bright object moving in the sky very far up. It was like nothing I'd ever seen almost diamond shaped with a few colours in it I know the skies very well as I often use my binoculars to stopwatch and this was not any flight path I had ever seen. As it moved over my house I was about to run round to the front when I noticed movement. There were two bright lights six sagging in the sky to the left of where the large object was traveling I watched these move about for a minute or two and then went round the front of the house but the objects had all vanished from view. There was no one around on my estate not a sound The large object made a humming notice but no sound from the smaller lights.
