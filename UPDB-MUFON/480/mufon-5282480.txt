Translucent 30 foot wide UFO above canal.

Translucent 30 foot wide UFO above canal. I was walking back from a grocery store on a Sunday morning at 8 o’clock and I was walking along the dirt access of a canal in Phoenix Arizona. I was carrying two bags of groceries when I looked up and I seen it floating over the canal approximately 60 feet in front of me and 20 feet above the water. I instantly knew what it was. It was a craft that was hamburger shaped whose cloaking device was failing. It was phasing in and out and I could see it but I could see through it as well. I drop the groceries and then I started to hear somebody screaming on the other side of the canal. That’s when I realized that it was completely silent the craft was. The guy was screaming and pointing at it. Screaming do you see that? I work construction so I just pointed at my eyes and I pointed at the craft and I gave them a thumbs up . Then the craft tilted towards me and slowly started to traverse away from me. I took off running after it because I wanted to get on it because I wanted to make contact. It went down the canal about a quarter mile with me at top speed running it was slowly moving away from me. It got up ahead and turned a corner and I lost sight of it in the trees. When I got up to around the corner to where I should’ve been able to see it it was gone.
