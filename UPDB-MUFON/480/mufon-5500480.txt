Oak lake flat disc with lights on sides going all around 2008

Well this is hard for me because it has been awhile since this event happened to me it was early in the morning my parents and everyone in the house were laying down for the night and so was i i dont know why i woke up but i did i went out to the kitchen to get some water and took a look outside it was a beautiful night out so i put my shoes on and i went outside i was standing on the deck when this thing i cant explain what it looked like came shooting out of the sky and stopped right over the small lake it must have been 500 feet across all around with bright rainbow colored light on the sides but all around the craft it was probably 50 feet away from shore i looked and looked and then woke up in my bed the next morning i belive i saw something and i cannot forget this vision this memory i have and that make me feel like something more happened i think about that night everday and i wont forget it
