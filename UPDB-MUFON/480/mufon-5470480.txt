Line of 4 bright lights which was joined by another after 30 mins . Was not a star or aircraft - lights did not blink

Line of 4 lights close together (so that they appear as a short line in the photo), much brighter than stars. The lights were mainly white in colour but also had a green or blue tinge. They did not move over the hour they were observed. They were at 45 degrees from the horizon. I observed until I needed to go to bed. After observing for 40 minutes another set of lights were observed which were closer to the horizon and somewhat dimmer. I took photo with my phone but it does not show the lights as points but as a smear.
