Looked out of my back window and spotted a bright light moving across the sky

I happened to look out of my back window which faces west at about 3.15AM and I saw a bright light moving across the sky coming towards my house, I called my son to look at it and we opened the window and watched the object get closer we noticed there was no sound coming from the object and it went over the house, we went to the window at the front of the house which faces east and I opened the window and waited for the object to appear, it was at this time I noticed the shape of the object it was a triangle with 3 straight equal sides and a light in the middle and the colour was black, I thought the object might have been a military plane but I don't know any military planes that are this shape and move without making a sound, my partner and son also observed the object and we watched it fly east in a straight line until it disappeared, I know it was not a standard aeroplane and when I see planes in the sky they are normally flying in a North/south direction and they always make a noise, I do not know what the object I saw was and it flew straight over a major uk city and I imagine it was spotted by other I also have a flight tracker app on my phone and it did not show up on this.

Damien Field
Bradford City UK
