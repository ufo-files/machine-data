Silver UFO

My mom, aunt and I were talking in the yard when we saw a very bright light in the sky.
At first we thought it could be a helicopter, but we realized that the light was very intense.
When looking at the sky more closely, we noticed an object in the shape of a silver colored soccer ball, measuring approximately 70 meters.
The object accelerated and disappeared.
