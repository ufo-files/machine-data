(I had my grand daughter write this, because she witnessed the event.)
I was outside swinging with my cousin, when we heard a very loud sound in the sky.
I thought that it was an airplane.
It flew strait across the sky, making a very loud noise. It was light yellow, and had very small, pointy wings at the sides. The front was bulky and rounded perfectly. The back was mostly strait, but kind of curved in.
I was scared when I noticed that it wasn't an airplane or a helicopter, and my cousin and I ran inside after it went away.
The object kept flying, but the sound suddenly stopped, and it disappeared in thin air.
