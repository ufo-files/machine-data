At first I was looking into the sky towards the West from my backyard patio at 22:37 and saw a bright object moving SW with pulsating a bright white light. Then it disappeared and reappeared travelling in the opposite direction NE, and it moved upward vertically and disappeared while pulsating. I was very excited and asked it to show itself for my wife to see, but my wife left. I then re-entered the house and just a moment later, I went out on the backward patio again and there it was again showing up pulsating light like never before. I waved and got more response by light pulsation.
