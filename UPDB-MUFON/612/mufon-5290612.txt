Silver cigar shape, on fire

Me and my son was taking the garbage out. All of a sudden my neighbor said what is that in the sky on fire. Me and my son looked up and seen it. It was silver in color and cigar shaped. It flew in a straight line, then all of a sudden it stopped in mid air hovering for about 20 seconds. Then it went straight down and we spent the rest of the evening watching for it to take off again. It never did. So the next morning me and my son hiked in the woods using a compass to find it. We found it and it appeared to have crashed. We could see it was still smoking. My I told my son to not go near it, for fear of radiation. We didn't have a camera to take pictures. I had a real feeling of doom when we were there. The spacecraft is located in the middle of nowhere. I went back a couple years later and it was still there. I called the government and they didnt care, and the police department who said I was crazy. If anyone want to see it let me know. But here is a warning. When you get close to it you feel dizzy and sick for days. That's why I refuse to touch it, or attempt to get into it.
