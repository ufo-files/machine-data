{kt}Huge, Triangle, 3 lights, hovering,Low humming

I was sitting in my room and heard a low humming noise, but did not think much of it. My electricity went in and out slowly and I looked out the window to see street lights doing the same. I then heard honking and saw some cars pulled over and one car where the person had gotten out and was looking up. I went out side and looked and saw 3 lights in the shape of a triangle over the tree line. They were one yellow two green. Then it changed 1 green, 2 white. I heard everyone talking but it was difficult as the low humming noise was constant and got louder. Then we saw a flash of light come down from it that seemed to make light as day in the area it was shone. then the light disappeared and we thought we couldn't see it anymore, only to realize it was a lot closer all of a sudden. We could make out one solid object. the man got in the car and left. I started to go back in, I turned around to look one final time and it was gone.
