Bright blue oval shaped orb with halo moving near Sun.

I noticed a large halo around the sun so I decided to photograph it with my phone. When I came home I viewed the pics and noticed a bright blue orb with a halo near the sun. I had no idea it was there and obviously couldn't see it with naked eyes as sun was too bright. It seemed to be moving into or out of the Sun, or possibly behind it. I don't think it was Mercury or Venus.
