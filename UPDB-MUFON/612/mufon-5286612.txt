Saw two (2) black orbs. Flying at different heights but the same speed.

I was walking my dog in Cherry Creek State Park right off of Havana and Belleview. I looked up in the sky and to the east and saw two black orbs in the air going from north to south (in the video I mistakenly saw the opposite). They looked identical and were traveling at the exact same speed. There was no variation in their speed or direction despite the wind. The speed did seem slow for an airborne object. There were no lights and they made no sound. I asked three others walking the trail if they spotted the objects, but all said no. Huge skeptic, but seriously, what are these?
