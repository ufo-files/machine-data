Several Red/Orange Orbs appear to be rising from over Sweetwater Reservior, made several formations, formed a line then rearrange to triangles and diamond shapes. General movement from north to south and dissappearing into the night sky.

High School reunion at Bonita Golf course. Outdoor venue listening to band. Many people saw these lights. I tried to capture on my cell phone camera but they don't show well. There were literally 50 + of these things in the sky doing various formations: arc of maybe 10 or 12, then reformating into triangles, diamond shapes. As the moved south across the sky they seemed to just dissapear all in the exact same area of the sky.
