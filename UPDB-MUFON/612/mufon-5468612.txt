Witnessed a black boomerang shaped object for 8-10 mins slowly moving through the sky.

I had just picked my daughter up from school and had almost reached our house when i spotted something that resembled a crab shaped black object. I thought at first that it was a parachute but couldnt see anyone dangling from it. After observing it moving from in front on me travelling to the right i decided to get a video to capture it. It seems to change direction slightly midway through the video and you can see the shape by zooming into the travelling object. 
I was extremely excited after observing it coming towards me at first; it was like a black crab like an M shape. I couldnt make out what it was at all. We watched the object until it disappeared out of view going travelling south. 
I dismissed it as a balloon afterwards but it didnt behave like a balloon; it was steady and i think it changes direction after a minute or two. It even slowed down at one point. 
I observed a definate ufo back in dec 2015 in the same area. It hovered and looked like a large star, then zoomed off at incredible speed. This has been included in a seperate report.
