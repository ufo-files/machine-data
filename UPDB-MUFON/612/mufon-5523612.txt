everynight at 7:30pm a star directly overhead appears when all others are still out - then it abruptly goes out - you could probably see this light from a great distance. every day for a week now at the same time. multiple people seeing this. it moves

I tell the kids & they see it too. called their mother/my daughter & told her to look. she lives several blocks away. she see's it & says "you know dad, it's moving" - I agree. It goes out again by 7:40. 10-3-11 it's 7:30pm. I'm on the highway coming home from work. I stop the car & get out & look - it's there. within a few min. it simply goes out. like a lightbulb being turned off. this appears to be happening everyday. it appears as a star. satallite or balloon or marvin martian it is extremely odd.
