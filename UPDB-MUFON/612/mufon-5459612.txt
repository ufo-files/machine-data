{jpg} 6 pulsing very slow moving red fireballs in formation

While driving home at 4am from my friends house, I saw 6 pulsing/glowing red orbs in the sky in a formation. I stopped and got out of my car to look at them. They appeared to be stationary at first, but while I stood there I could see they were all moving very slowly relative to some buildings/trees in the skyline. They moved slowly for about 15 minutes toward the coast before one by one slowly fading out.
