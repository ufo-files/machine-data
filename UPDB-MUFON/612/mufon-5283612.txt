5 glowing red/iorange spheres

I live in the country and stepped out to enjoy the evening, Looking South I see a red glowing sphere coming from the South above the tree line and up to directly above me and vanish on a clear starry night. Astonished, I look to the South again, and here comes another one, same path and moving slow enough for me to run, get the wife and kid, and they observe it disappear above our heads...say, 5-10,000 ft. up?  So, I look South again, and here comes another one, but this one turns to head West, then disappears. So I look South again, and another one comes along...so I get my phone and binoculars, get some video and the kid sees what looks like infinity symbols around the sphere/disk shape through the binoculars! It disappears above us...for a total of 5. Crazy stuff...The last UFO I saw was in Cali in the 80's, that just looked like a satellite that stopped and shot straight up and out of sight.
