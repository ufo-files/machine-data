Stationary object in sky

I was pulling out of the Mc Donald's drive through. I was looking out at the traffic and noticed and news helicopter. I thought that they where reporting about traffic as always. Juts as I noticed the helicopter my eyes caught an other object in the sky to the left of the helicopter. My first thought was it was an airplane. Because I see them all the time seeming to not be moving because I'm also moving. Giving the appearance that its stationary. As soon as I focused on the object it suddenly shot off in a SE direction. It took me a minute to process what I seen as im turing out of the driveway to drive towards my home. Not more than a block away. Approximately a minute time has passed since I seen the object a black helicopter passed over towards the direction the objects path of flight. 
The object was larger than the helicopter but smaller that the commercial jet in the sky at the same time. It was silver tic-tac shap. No windows, nothing but a smooth shape. No sound or anything. Almost as if I was looking at a picture. It was stationary. But with in a fraction of a second it took off out of my sight. There was so much air traffic out at the time and also other air traffic was visible during the sitting. I drove home and I was so shaken I couldn't even park my car in our driveway. I parked in the alley and hurried in to my apartment and just sat there. Two nights later I woke up sleeping on my living room floor. I don't know why. And have been having trouble focusing on anything since. And have had a abrasion on my head/forehead.
