{tjd} Watched it close and personal when very young

Was a young child at the time and used to hearing jets from Hill Air Force Base flying over and breaking the sound barrier. I think laws stopped sonic booms at the time I saw this craft. Sonic booms were probably banned for a few years but its something old people remember. I was in the kitchen with my mother on a very hot July or August day around noonish. We heard a loud roar like the jets that always trained overhead at the time. My mom, told me to go outside and watch. She knew knew I was an aviation historian at that huge age of 7 or 8. I watched the craft for probaly 2-3 minutes pass over the town of Montpelier, I watched it approach at slow speed and depart in slow speed. Was probably more like a 1.5 min but seemed like all day. Got very good view and watched the old people across the street come outside and watch it. It was at about 500' in elevation and as a child  I guessed 1.5 foot ball fields long. When my father came home from work at 6pm. He drove our family to the local landing field (dirt). No other adults admitted to seeing it, I was young. Never understood why they would lie about it. Always wondered what that slow moving craft with no wings was?
