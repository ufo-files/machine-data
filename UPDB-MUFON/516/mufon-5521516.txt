As I drove up the hill to my house I saw a large object flying slowly about 300 feet above ground. I was diamond/trapezoidal shaped with several yellow lights. I pulled over and it flew over my car into the distance.

As I approached Moretown about 10:30 pm, heading north on Rte 100 I saw a yellowish light in the distance. At first I thought it was the top of a radio tower on top of a hill somewhere. As I got closer to town I realized there is no tower anywhere close by. I was getting closer to the object and I could make it out better as I kept driving. When I got into Moretown I could tell that the object was moving very slowly through the sky, only about 300 feet above the ground. I started driving up the hill to my home and when I got to the top and the view wasnt obscured by trees I pulled over and opened my window. The object was flying very low yet I could not hear any noise. The shape was almost diamond-like or trapezoidal. It almost looked like 2 helicopters were flying one in front of the other, really close, but I would have been able to hear them so close to the ground. It was a big dark object but it had several yellow lights underneath and also a red light. It kept flying and eventually flew over the area of my car. Soon I lost sight of it over the trees. I got home and searched online and someone reported a similar sighting about a couple days ago over interstate 91.
