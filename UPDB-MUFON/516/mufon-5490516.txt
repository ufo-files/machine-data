Google surfing underwater structures

This is not a normal sighting report, more of a request to investigate a strange structure. As the attachment will show, there is a large lava like tube running beneath the surface of Puerto Rico. What drew my attention were the 3 right angle turns in the tube that is otherwise very straight and uniform in construction. At the northern most point there is a very dark straight line across the entire tube. All of this appears to be man-made to look like a natural structure. Although there are no other tubes in the area and lava doesn't flow in strait lines, rather follows terrain. I you are looking for a candidate for an underwater base, I think is is a good start.
