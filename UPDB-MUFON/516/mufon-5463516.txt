{dr}2 spheres approximately 30 miles south of Salem in the vicinity of Albany.

My wife and I were smoking on the landing outside of our apartment when 2 reddish orbs appeared in the sky approximately over Albany. They hovered, flew close enough that their glows almost touched, and moved towards and away from each other for rough 10 minutes. At this point one or went due east and the other flew due south.
