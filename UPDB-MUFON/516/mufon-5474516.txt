It was in 2006, Managua Nicaragua, it was 10:30p.m. I was playing monopoly at my neighbors house with three other people, at that time I was 11 years old, I decided to go home, when I went out the front door I saw a UFO coming out of a forest that Was next to the house of my neighbors, in that time the forest was being cut down to make constructions, today the place is a gatted Community.

The UFO was about 5 meters high, was circle medium-sized, had lights of all colors that turned very fast, I could not see the complete form, literally the UFO was on me, I could only see the bottom , I saw it for about 6 seconds, I was going very slowly but then it went very fast, no sound was heard, I was totally paralyzed watching them, it was impossible to capture any image, I was immobilized.
I returned quickly where the people I was with To ask if they had seen that, they were very close to me because we were playing near the front door however they did not seem to know what was happening outside the house.

A few days after the sighting, very strange things happened to me, I will relate one of the strangest things that have happened to me, I can not say if this happened after the sighting was real or not since I have very few memories: I was inside A very large cabin, was sitting and screaming, the cabin was silver and empty, it was like a kind of cage, I could feel the pressure changes, I could feel the speed pressing my body, I could feel the change of gravity, then I saw A very bright white light and I do not remember anything else, I woke up in my bed.

Nowadays I live in the same place, could recreate as everything happened, I never speak of this with any means of communication or with the authorities, only with my family, is a subject that in Nicaragua does not have much importance, here never have been great sightings And it is something very unusual. I have no picture of what happened that night, everything lives in my memory as if it had been yesterday.
