{rjl} Basketball shaped object appeared over the ocean going up down side to side for approx. 1/2 hr.

We observed from our front window at Sandy Point, WA which is across form Lummi Island, on 3-18-2016 at approximately 2200 a basketball shape hovering above the ocean. To object was silent, moved very quickly from side to side, darted up and down, all while emitting an orange -yellow light.

It was not frightening just intriguing since it was the only thing in the sky, hovering over the ocean.

We were getting ready to retire, we checked out the front window looking over the ocean at Sandy Point, WA and the object was moving right in front of our window.
