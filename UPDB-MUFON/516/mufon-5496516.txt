{cak} Red Orb UFO that hovered then descended until it disappeared.

I first seen the object when I pulled up to my house after work. I noticed it in the sky, the thing that made me notice it was that it appeared red, at first I thought it was maybe that asteroid that was supposed to fly by close to earth. But I noticed the inside of the red sphere was yellow and pulsated, it was bizarre, I never have seen any plane or helicopter have lights like that. So I went to my backyard and decided to film it with my iPhone 5 camera. I noticed it began to descent, it eventually disappeared behind the small hill that is visible from my backyard. Whatever it was, it was closer than what I originally thought it was, I felt excitement because I believe I filmed a actual UFO. But with that being said, the only thing I think it could of been would of been some sort of globe or something that someone could of been flying around, that's the only explanation other than something extraterrestrial.
