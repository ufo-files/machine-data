My family and I observed multiple orange lights in the evening sky traveling from east from the southwest. I counted at least 16 of these bright orange lights/orbs high in the sky lined up like a conga line. Looking directly south, they started at my 2:00 position and traveled to my 11:00 position within a 5-8 minute timeframe before dissipating once each of them reached a specific location.
