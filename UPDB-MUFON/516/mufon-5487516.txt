UFO sighting in Chilliwack, BC Canada

I sat outside for a smoke this evening at 8:15 pm PST, in Chilliwack, BC Canada and I saw a rather large object in the sky. It was dark outside, partly cloudy. I could tell the object was fairly close above the earth, there was no noise. It seemed quite large, larger than a jet airplane. It was difficult to tell the exact shape because the outline blended in with the dark sky. What I could see was two large windows on either side and they were emitting glowing white lights. The windows is what I found to define the object that I saw. It soared through the air slow enough where I could take a look for almost a minute, then it was out of my line of sight.
