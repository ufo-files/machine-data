{cak} Bright white light, change in path, hovering, low above water

I had just left a small Winter Solstice show at my local coffee shop and decided to go walk down by the bay before driving home. It was probably around 10:30 pm and dark with very low cloud coverage. I stood on some large boulders overlooking the water. After a few minutes I looked up and and saw a large, slow, low flying, silent bright white light move out from the clouds and then travel just beneath them over the bay maybe 300 or 400 feet away from me.

I don't want to bog you down with geography, but the other side of the bay from where I was standing is maybe a mile and a half or two away, and this bright light had to have been close.

It's elevation remained stayed the same, but it continued to move over the bay in a straight line until it stopped directly parallel to me.

(I know it sounds totally whack, but it's what happened.)

It brightened up and began to change back and forth between a bright white and orange. It sat there flashing in front of me for maybe 30 to 45 seconds before dimming down again to a solid, but still bright white, and headed back in a similar, but not quite the same direction it had come from. I watched it start to move over some buildings, and knowing the town well (because I grew up there and it's very small), was MAYBE a quarter of a mile away. It hovered there for a few moments before being obscured by clouds. I kept watch for another few minutes but that was the last I saw of it.
