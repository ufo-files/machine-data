{ck} Entered atmosphere East to west glowing like an orange cigar.

Sitting in the hot tub, saw the object hit the atmosphere glowing brightly. It traveled from east to west. Stopped north about light house field. It hovered at a high altitude and stayed stationary. Three engines were clearly viewable. Planes started to come in from the west and the object exited the atmosphere in a straight vertical lift till the engines were no longer visible. The object was in the sky for approximately 5 minutes. No sound was heard and no alcohol was involved. I cannot believe that there werent other people who saw it as well.
