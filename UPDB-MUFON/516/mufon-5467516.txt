a long train like object silver and bright

At approximately 9:45 p.m. on June 19, 2018 I was sitting on my balcony on a cruise ship going from Labadee, Hait cruising to Jamaica..I was drinking a cup of hot tea looking out into the ocean when all of a sudden i saw a bright light going from left to right, at first i thought it was a shooting star but it was not traveling very fast..the object slowed down a lot and i could make out a very bright silvery object that appeared as a long train traveling horizontally, it slowed down almost to a stop and i could see very bright lights coming from the object..all of a sudden it stopped and a very bright star appeared and this object entered the star without appearing on the other side of the star..everything became bright and slowly fell into the ocean and everything went dark..i went into my cabin to check the time and watch the ship's position on the TV, we were south of the Bermuda Triangle..the next day i ask my neighbors if they were sitting on their balcony's and saw the light show, no one remembers the sighting..i will never forget what i saw, we are not alone....
