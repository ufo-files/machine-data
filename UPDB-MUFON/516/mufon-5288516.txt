Descending mountain from coffee plantation

I was in Costa Rica in 11/2016. Snapped some of the landscape as we left the Doka Estate Coffee Plantation. I did not see or hear anything at the time. When I went thru my photos, I noticed dark spots in the air. I zoomed in to get a better look, and I can see sphere like objects in the photos. In one photo, I believe there are 4-5 in the same photo. Another photo, I believe I caught one near the Poas Volcano over the trees behind the volcano. Last shot, between the two large trees in the foreground just by the cloud line, looks like another sphere type object. I was not looking for UFO, but came across them after the fact. Unfortunately, I do not have more information to submit and answer to the best I could.
