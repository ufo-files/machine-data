{nd} A cyndrilical shaped object that would lay on its side from 12 position to 2 position and shape would bend like a v

At approximately 2:45 am til 3:26 I monitored an object approximately 20,000 ft in the sky. The object had a star like quality but had an elongated shape to it. When I first noticed it it was upright and straight @ 12 oclock and after an unknown amount of time maybe 7-8 minutes it turned in its axis and lay at a two O clock position.the objects light patterns were extremely bright in comparison with anything else in the sky with a bright blue light at the top of the thin cyndrilical object to an alternating red and phosphoric white center. The colors were extreme bright.another characteristic of the object is that the lights would flash from one side of it to the other with most of the movement toward the center of the object and it seemed to bend into a Vshape as if an arc of electricity. I wanted to film it but my battery was almost dead ony phone. I observed this at first from my balcony after some time maybe ten minutes I got my phone's portable external battery and called my neighbor..he came out at approximately 3:15 30 minutes into my observations. At which time I had to go back in the house and tend to my cooking..I watched the object another ten minutes perhaps before my eyes got too tired to watch ..Somewhere around 3:15-3:20 the object seemed to move further away into the southeast sky.
