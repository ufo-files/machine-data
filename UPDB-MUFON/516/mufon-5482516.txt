{nd} Red light with occasional white strope. Stayed in same spot for 20 min then decended quickly disappearing

Was outside my house in more of a country area trying to see if any fireworks we're available in the neighborhood for my daughter to watch. I noticed a bright red light out in the distance out to the Sw. I noticed it strobe white a few times. We have a plane route in front if the house so I know what they look like. This was no plane. It stayed in the same spot. After 15 minutes I got my wife look with her telescope because no planet should have been in that spot at that time. When she tried to focus we noticed it move a little then descend strait down. That happened very fast. We we're not drinking or under the influence. My wife is a sceptic and even she did not understand the object.
