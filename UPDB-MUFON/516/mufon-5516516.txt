(GS) Looked like a star too far away for more detail; possibly reflecting light

I was at work, and just looked up at the sky. The sighting was in Corpus Christi, Texas at the Wilson Plaza builtin. It was August 9, 2012, about 7;30. I looked up and saw an object. At first
I thought it was stationary, but then I noticed it moving slowly to the West. The object was too far away to tell what it was. I thought it was a star, however, the sky was still blue. It moved so much I had to get another vantage point, as my view was obscured by a building. It eventually stopped, and it was stationary for a while. I went inside the building and brought out a co-worker. He came out and saw it too. We both went back inside. I came back outside, and the object was no longer visible. I didn't really know what the object was when I first looked at it. I was excited about seeing the object, and came close to calling the newspaper and/or television stations.
