saw a slow moving star go behind the moon and not come out the other side.

my parents and i were visiting friends who lived up the street from us in wewoka, oklahoma. the adults were setting on the front pourch talking and their daughter and i was setting on the steps of their sidewalk talking. it was a clear night and a bright moon was shining. at some point i looked up and told her to look at that star moving towards the moon. it was moving from right to left. the whole experience took about five minutes. when it was over, we went and told our parents. they brushed it off and told us to go play. this was in 1954. three years before sputnik. after watching that satalight and several more of ours, it reminded me what i had seen three years before. they looked the same to me. i have told members of my family this story several times over the years.
