{cak} Dropped strait down like you dropped a coin from your hand. Seemed to disappear as opposed to fizzle out. Almost like it flew away from me real fast.

I was driving NW from Bethalto IL entertaining Alton IL on IL140 next to the Alton Mental Health Center. There was not much traffic, but I was in a small bunch of cars, 2 in left lane, 2 in right lane which I was 2nd car in left lane. I was just randomly gazing NW in the sky admiring its beauty and randomly saw this sea green colored oval light seemingly surrounded with a white/yellowish glow. It dropped strait down and seemed to cover 1/4 the sky in its decent. Then from my vantage point, it seemed to stop and pull away from me (heading NW) and literally disappeared. It all happened so fast but I can replay it in my head over and over just like it was the first time I saw it. The two cars to my right pulled over and I just kept driving since my house was less than 2 miles away. Just telling my wife the story as I pulled out my phone to call her, I was shaking uncontrollably. I have never seen anything like this, ever, and I have seen shooting stars and meteor showers. There were no light trails behind it, like if you were to see a shooting star. Also, it was oval shaped but seemingly was sideways as the long parts were up and down as opposed to left and right.
