Family was set up at the edge of a lake to try to observe comet NEOWISE with a telescope. Just above high cloud cover, a cloud suddenly lit up but gently, as if from heat lightning. It drew my attention and I looked to the cloud just in time to see this u-shaped thing flash a bank of white lights which seemed to be around the entire "u". It lit up the entire sky, and everything at ground level could also be seen clearly due to the brightness. Almost as soon as it flashed, it was gone. However, the shape of it and its lights were perfectly discernible for the few seconds it appeared. There were six individual huge, round lights. The object made no sound, and when it disappeared it didn't take off, per se; it was simply gone.
