On an evening in the fall of 1989 I was watching movies at a friends house on the Whitebear Indian reservation in Saskatchewan Canada. When I was ready to leave it was around 11:30 pm. As I pulled out of the lane onto the main grid which would take me to my residence I noticed a light on hill very near my friends house. I thought nothing of this as people often went up there to sit and visit or party. As I continued my drive on the grid road I noticed the light was coming down the trail on the hill. Again I thought nothing of this at this time. Hindsight did tell me however that it did move a bit odd. However it may have mimiced vehicle traffic. A point I think of at times. The grid did at this time take me up a hill where at a point I lost sight of the orb or light again at this time I had no notion that the light was anything other than a vehicle. At the top of the hill myself and the light did almost meet in fact we were probably less than 30 feet from each other. This was only a split second. Then faster than I could have snapped my fingers it moved at almost 90* angle around 300 yards. I did lose where it went for a few seconds. As I continued my drive I did see where it was. As I pulled up perpendicular to it I stopped rolled down my window and did shout at it to leave me alone. I sat for maybe 30 seconds as it hovered approximately 30-35 feet and maybe 75-80 yards away. It didn1t move. I continued to drive. At this point it manoeuvred towards my vehicle to approximately 20-25 yards of my vehicle. It proceeded to travel perpendicular to myself for around 2 miles following me into my residence yard where it ascended almost 90* straight up into the sky. At this point I lost sight of the orb.
