While at the Fargo Blues Festival this object flew directly overhead. At first I thought it was a drone, but it rotated quite quickly and there was a reflective portion or a light on it that flashed or reflected sunlight. I took pictures with my iPhone and I absolutely disagree that it is either a drone or a balloon object. It seemed controlled and flew purposefully with intent.
