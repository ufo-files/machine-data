Driving home from Spokane and saw object criss-crossing over the road

Driving home from the Spokane Airport, heading South to Lewiston at 1230 am on March 9, 2022. Husband was driving, I noticed something above and forward of the car. It was an object that was dark and non-reflective. There were lights in a square pattern on the bottom of the object: red, green and white. They were not flashing in any pattern, but kind of pulsating randomly. The only outline I saw on this object, close to Fairchild AFB, was vaguely square. The object hovered above us, then began swooping to the left, going up, coming closer to the ground, crossing the road, going up again, then coming to the right. It varied height and distance and crossed the road multiple times, then was gone. 
I don't sleep well, so the next night I was out in our yard with the dogs at around midnight. I saw three of the same type objects up in the sky. They flew around each other quite closely. In my opinion, it seemed 'playful". Two of them came at each other from the east and west, almost "playing chicken' but didn't hit each other , There was another that zoomed off to the north. The other two zipped to the east and west, out of sight. In the sky I could see that They did leave a dual sort of exhaust that was very short and thin/wispy. 
Since moving here in 2018, I have seen multiple objects from our house, including a multicolored spinning orb. Seems to be a busy area.
