I think my house or neighborhood was being scanned.

In bed, sound asleep, I was awakened by a strange low buzzing and the sensation of a line of electrical-type tingling moving across my leg, as if I were in an MRI. No lights. The cat on my bed seemed unaware and he started purring which oddly imitated the sound I was hearing. The vibrations I felt contributed to the sound- I laid still and tried to focus on whether it was a slight earthquake temblor but nothing more occurred, and suddenly it was eerily quiet. I was too groggy to think quickly enough to jump up and run outside. I regret not having done so. I have seen two orange disc/orbs in my neighborhood in the past few months that have seemingly glided low over the rooftops. I strongly feel that this was another one and it was hovering over my house. It was not a fearful experience.
