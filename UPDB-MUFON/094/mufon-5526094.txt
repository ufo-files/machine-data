Taking pictures of storm coming in.

I was taking a series of pictures of a storm coming from the west of my apartment. I have a nice view and decided to start capturing some scenes of the sunsets and the storms that I get to see. So this was a great moment to catch the sun setting in the west as a storm was encroaching. The sun was bright and when I took the picture my eyes where having a hard time seeing ...because of the glare of the sun. So I took the picture. As I pulled my eyes away from the camera phone I began to see lights dancing in the clouds...a flash here and another there. I thought perhaps it was just my eyes acting up from the intense sun...I thought I was getting sun flashes in my eyes. I continued to watch for a period of perhaps 4 min, and kept seeing flashes...thinking . "boy I really screwed up my eyes with that picture." I went back into the apartment never thinking anything of it. Anyhow, I captured something interesting...and I have other pictures of interest.
