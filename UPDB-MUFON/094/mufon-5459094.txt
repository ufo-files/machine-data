Slow moving large ball of light, disappeared

I was outside of my house putting garbage to the curb when I noticed what I thought was a plane.

It was a large, slow moving ball of orange light moving from my NW towards the south. It took about a minute before it was almost directly overhead and then it disappeared. I thought maybe it was a shooting Star but it moved very slow. I’ve never seen a shooting star last more than a couple seconds. This was over a minute. I was able to get about 30 seconds of the object on video. 

I am aware their was a meteor shower. If this was that it behaved in a way I have not seen before. I grew up in an airforce town. I have attended countless air shows. I was a avid skydiver in my youth. I am comfortable spotting planes at distance. 

I have a video but it was from a phone. It does not do the object justice. It looks like a star but I was able to track it for about 30 seconds. I was able to record when it disappeared. A few minutes later a plane flew over and I recorded it for reference (2nd video)
