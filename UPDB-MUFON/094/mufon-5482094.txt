{rjl} small white object moving fast then sudden stop

After a visit to my cardiologist at the heart hospital.Power and Broadway Mesa Az. I was feeling pretty good about my doctors visit and might I say in a good mood as I was walking back out to the parking lot. The hospital parking is a second floor outside parking deck. Walking to the car I looked to the east toward the Superstition Mountains and noticed a fast moving white object heading my way. This thing was 50 - 60 degrees above the horizon my guess. Being curious I kept an eye on this object as I unlocked and opened the car door. It appeared to be sphere shaped, but then again it was hard to judge at that distance, I do know it was bright white and coming in fast. I had several ground references to judge movement and speed (a large tree, and a telephone pole with several lines). As I was looking through the telephone lines the object came closer, it slowed down and eventually stopped. No mistake, I had those lines as a reference. It moved upwards and to the right (south)then stopped. after a few seconds it moved left (north) for some short distance and stopped again. At that time I noticed there were not one but two reflecting spheres very close together. The movements were rapid and precise,,, this is what really captured my attention. Resting my cell phone on the door of my car I took several pictures, some zoomed in some at normal setting. The sky was clear and blue at 12:02 PM Friday 7/22/16, temperature was a hot 106 degrees. I watched this object for several minutes darting up/down/left/right! since I captured it on my camera I decided to get in the car and head home. As I pulled out onto the street I noticed the object was no longer in sight. A very odd object and very strange behavior indeed. I will share the original untouched photos with the assigned field investigator and chief investigator.
