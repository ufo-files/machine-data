In 1992 in the month of October I was traveling from Ponce to San Juan using the new highway that was built. It was approximately 9:00 PM. I had been expecting to see a flying saucer all that day as I had purposely gone to the Lake Cartagena area where there had been frequent sightings but the day went by and nothing happened. As it started getting dark I headed home to San Juan. I was still watching the sky on my way home. I saw a red light come out of the clouds to the west and it headed straight for me. I stopped the car on the side of the road to watch the light. The flying saucer vehicle descended and hovered right in front of my vehicle slowly moving from west to east making sure that I got a good look at it. I continued watching it travel slowly from west to east.When it got far enough away I started on my way home. The vehicle turned and followed me for about 5 minutes then disappeared. It was a larger craft than what I saw in 1973 and it had spinning blades on the bottom. It also had portholes on the dome and different colored lights coming out of the portholes as there is something spinning in the center of the craft. I have reported my previous sighting but I submitted diagrams with this report. The most important thing that I realized is that whomever was guiding this craft knew my thoughts and knew that I was looking to see a flying saucer that day.
