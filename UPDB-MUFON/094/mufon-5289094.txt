Boomerang like craft hovered above in open field clear day, then 6 airforce jets came circled and took towards the direction of the craft

In the middle of the afternoon in a field between Riley Oregon and Brothers on the north side of Hwy 20. My father and I were there to harvest wheat with a combine. I was the there to help with greasing and fueling the combine. I was sitting on a water trough waiting while my dad was out working, and I was listening to music. It was a very clear sunny day and something very shinny caught my eye so I looked up and saw a very shinny metallic craft in the shape of boomerang with a tail in the middle. It was at least a telephone pole and a half above me. No sound but maybe a humming sound. It hovered for what seemed like minutes, but probably seconds. It took off in the direction of Burns. It went up in elevation kinda paused then shot off so fast it disappeared in seconds. A few seconds later six Air force jets showed up from the direction of Bend, circled above me then flew towards Burns in groups of two. The jets where very loud.
