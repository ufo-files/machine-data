On January 13, 2013, in Windermere, the witness reported watching a silent, triangular-shaped object the size of a football field move overhead at approximately 500 feet.

I was taking out the trash @ 12.46 am, it was clear last night, when first I felt a strong magnetic presence on a large tree 20 ft in front of me, I look at the top of tree looking for something and saw nothing, six more steps on my way back home and felt again the same magnetic thing, look up in the sky right over my head and I see this Large Triangle Craft Flying over me in a straight line-path, going NE to the coast, may be @ 60-70 mph very low altitude aprox 500 ft and I could count 5 not very bright white lights and making NO SOUND, NO NOISE at all, total silent, this craft was like a translucent dark, mimic the color of the Sky night. The size of a football field, I watched for 7 seconds trying to follow the trajectory of the UFO when it went over the houses and before that, it vanished. Then I start runing in to my house.
