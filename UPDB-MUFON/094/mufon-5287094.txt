Something Crashed in the Phoenix Area

Around 10:33 p.m. 6/14/21 neighbors in Mesa, Az reported a flash of light and loud boom. They posted their Nest video and it shows an object that appears to be crashing to earth and a few seconds later there is a flash and loud boom. Another neighbor posted a video showing that they too captured the flash at the same time. Several more neighbors posted hearing the boom and where they were in the city. I myself and my dogs heard and felt it. There has been no report of this anywhere on the local news. Additionally, after calling a local TV station I was told there had been no other reports. We deserve to know what is going on.
