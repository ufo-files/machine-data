I SHOT SOMETHING VERY STRANGE

WE WENT FISHING AND SAW IT I WENT BACK NEXT NIGHT AND SHOT THE THING BUT IT WAS NOT OF FLESH BUT A STRONG LIGHT CAME FROM THE BULLET HOLE ON THE CHEST ABOVE THE RIGHT BREST THE THING RAN ALONG AN ENBANKMENT NOTHING COULD EVEN WALK ON FASTER THAN I COULD RUN IT WAS ABOUT SEVEN FEET TALL HAIRY BUT THE FACE LOOKED LIKE A APE.WHEN THE BULLET HIT IT THE THING LOOKED RIGHT AT ME.A STRONG BLUE LIGHT COME OUT.I NEVER TOLD ANYBODY THIS BUT I STILL HAVE BAD DREAMS AND I KNOW THIS THING AINT MADE OF FLESH NOTHING COULD TAKE A 30 CAL AT THAT RANGE OR MANOVER THE WAY IT DID
