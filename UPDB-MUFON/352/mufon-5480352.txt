On September 27, 2016 around 9:30 or 10:00 AM I was driving south of Bogalusa, LA on Louisiana Highway 21. When I was passing the Red Zone night club (Latitude 30144'48.95"N Longitude 89150'43.55"W) I noticed what I first thought was large advertising balloons for the bar. When I got closer I saw that they were about 100 feet past the tree line which is about 100 feet behind Red Zone. There were three of them in a triangular pattern. They looked like two woks together, with one turned upside down on top of the other. The top one was a little bit larger than the bottom one. They were glossy black with a silver band around the center. They were very quiet, absolutely no sound. When I was almost out of sight one started to turn slowly clockwise and dropped a hose or something out of the silver band. The hose had a blank space and a solid space alternating the whole length of it. When I returned thirty minutes later they were gone. It was a beautiful fall day, not a cloud in sight and no wind.
