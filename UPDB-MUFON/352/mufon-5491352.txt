{cak} White cylindrical object seen overhead in Northern Kentucky

My husband and I were on our way to daily Mass. We had just gotten out of the car when I looked up and saw a white cylindrical (could have been cigar-shaped) object flying from the South toward the Northeast. I said, "Look at that." He remarked that it was simply an airplane, but I disagreed as I could see no wings or trail, nor hear any sound emanating from the object. I have seen similar objects 3 or 4 times in the past. The object was white but had a darker section in the middle which could have been a bay of windows. I was slightly excited but disappointed that my husband did not concur with my viewpoint of the sighting. I lost sight of the object when I went into the church.
