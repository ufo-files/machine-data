{nd} green cylindar shaped ufo seen at stockdale elementary school at night

I walked over to Stockdale Elementary school between 9pm and 12 pm and entered the playground and sat down. My husband followed a few minutes after. Out of no where a cylindar shaped UFO appeared in the sky above the playground and hovered. It was a glowing green color almost like a glow in the dark stick. A few seconds later two black helicopters followed and the UFO darted off so fast that we could no longer see it. It was amazing. I have attached a photo that resembles it most.
