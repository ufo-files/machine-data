{nd} just a large glowing, silent light

Sitting on back deck, when a large, orange-colored object appeared in the east, traveling west. No noise. Looked very similar to a Chinese lantern at first, but traveled too fast and kept a constant, unwavering course.  Very similar in appearance to 2-such objects observed in July. However, those noted in July were bright red, rather than orange.
