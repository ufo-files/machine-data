I have been watching UFO'S OVER EARTH on tv and thought maybe my experience was reported to someone at the time it happened which could verify my story. Which I did not think to do! Several years ago I was driving home on hwy 42 from Prospect KY to La Grange KY from my twin sisters house in the evening hours and I noticed way ahead of me flashing lights. It appeared to be a patrol car as if someone had been pulled over. As I got closer I could see that it was not on the road, but up in the air near the Wave 3 television Tower. I rolled down my car window as I drove further and when I reached the Tower I stuck my head out of the window and tried to look upward at the object hovering right next to the tower about 100 ft above me right beside the road. There was a car behind me and I slowed way down but did not want to stop in the middle of the road. I turned right on hwy 53 about 1/4 mi or less up the road and drove toward La Grange so that I could turn around to go back. There is a street right off the hwy on the right I turned on, to turn around. I took the next immediate left and put my car in reverse and backed up. As I was backing up I was looking over my right shoulder and noticed that the object was now very low to the ground and moving slowly through the trees across the same road. It moved slowly toward La Grange. I panicked and thought O M G ! it noticed me looking at it and it descended to my level and was following me ! So I put my car in drive pretty fast and took off. I immediately turned right and drove toward La Grange in the same direction as the object. I could have driven about a mile up the road and turned right on Fendley Mill Rd. and driven about 500 ft and it would have been coming right at me. But I was to afraid and rushed home and called my sister to tell her about the incident. ***** NOTE ***** Her husband was an Oldham County Police Officer who along with a couple of other officers, have seen an object at the same Wave 3 Tower !!!!!!
