{cak} Observed multiple round objects in the sky.

I was playing with my 8 month old grandson in my driveway when I first observed the objects in the sky. I happened to look at the sky because it was so clear and beautiful blue in a northeast direction at around 3:45 pm across the rooftops of the homes in the neighborhood. About 13 or 14 round white objects were in the sky traveling in a formation of three or four together and one in the front. The objects were not moving very fast and I watched them for about 6 or 7 minutes until I could not see them any longer. The objects made no noise and created no vapor trail as a regular aircraft would. When I went back inside my house I realized at about 7:30 pm that my cellphone signal booster had experienced a signal spike and had cut off my cellphone service. I have had this booster for over five years and I have never had a problem with this equipment and today after this sighting it malfunctions??
