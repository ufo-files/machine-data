in my room

i was in about the 5th grade. i lived in a home near cullaby lake in warrenton or. i woke in the middle of the night and was face up in my bed and could not move. the room was filled with a grey/blue light and i saw the heads of 2 short figures raised up next to my bed. they had skinny necks and dark oval eyes and triangle shaped heads. i was very frightened and tried to move. my torso was immobile. but i could move my left arm so i tried to bang against my bedroom wall to wake my mother down the hall. she didn't hear me. thats all i remember. the next night the same thing happened but i was able to get my left arm free and punched one of the figures in the face. what also was strange was that i didn't remember these events right away. one morning i woke up and remembered these events had happened a week or so before. i morning i remembered i found i had a sore spot on the back left of my skull. the area was now a dent. i still have it to this day. and was tender to the touch almost like a soft spot for the next 8 years or so. years later i was on a date next to cullaby lake and my now wife and i witnessed a strange light going back and forth behing the mountains across from carnahan county park which is about 2 blocks from the house that i had this encounter in. i don't remember the color of the light. maybe blue.
