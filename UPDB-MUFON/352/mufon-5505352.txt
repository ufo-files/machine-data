{cak} 4 of us were going down santa cruz ,when we saw an orange/red orb it was following us down the mountain.

We were coming down SANTA CRUZ mountain at midnight. There were 4 of us,and I am the 1st one to see it. Its was on my right side,and I looked out the window from the back seat. it was a large red /orange orb. Very bright it was next to us going thought the trees as we went down the curved roads. I told my friends to look at it, and everyone turned there heads to look at it, and we all said what the hell is that. Its following us thru the trees like they werent there. I told my friend to stop the car,and he did so did the orb. We did it again,and the orb did the same. We decided to get down the mountain because we felt it wanted to get lots closer to us,and we got a bit scared. As we went down the mountain it was right in front of us, we stopped. Thats where I don't remember what happen because all we remember was we got to the bottom of the mountain and,no one was talking. We were like in a daze. We got home at 5:30am it should of been home at 2:30am so what happen to 3 hours we lost. We done this routine a bunch of times before so I know. Funny thing we didn't talk about at all, like it never happened.
