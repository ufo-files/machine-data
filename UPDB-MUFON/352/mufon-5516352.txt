{cak} Blue Springs Mo UFO spotted, then disappears

I was driving home from a late night from Indpendence Missouri to Lees Summit when I spotted a star like, bright star over Blue Springs that was changing colors from red to blue to green and white. It was very bright and much brighter than a normal star in the sky. It was also low on the horizon. I lost view and when I got to a point where I could see it again it was gone. This was the same thing that the news reported about a while back I think. I have seen it before but I didn't see it disappear. No doubt it was there and then it was gone.
