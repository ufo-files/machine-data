{cak} streaked across sky, stopped, enlarged, then vanished

Was driving home from doing some shopping. Was heading west on sparrow Ave, about 3/4ths of the way towards turn to head on crane St caught out of corner of eye a fast moving streak of white light. Lasting about 3 to 4 seconds. The streak had the blur effect but no tail. After 3 to 4 seconds the object stopped and hovered while doubling in size, which last 1/2 to 1 second then the object vanished. There was no sound or smoke noticed.
