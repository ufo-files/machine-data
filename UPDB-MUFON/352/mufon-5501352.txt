{nd} 4 orb orange-red event.

I witnessed 4 orange-red orb type objects moving in the same vector/trajectory that seemed to be over Blairsville, GA moving in a NNE direction. Each orb followed each other clearing my line of sight over a mountain ridge by about 30 seconds apart. After roughly 15 seconds in view they blinked out. Max I saw at one time was 2, other two were individual before they blinked out. I am constantly on my front porch and am well aware of helicopter traffic as well as commercial air liner traffic out of both Atlanta and Chattanooga, these were neither. Closest thing it MAY of resembled was ariel flares, but there were no (and are hardly ever)fighter jets in the area. No sound.
