A few weeks after hurricane Ivan in 2006 I had gone to Gulfshores Al.to work on the restoration of the area. I rented a place 11 miles west of Gulfshores on Veterans dr.approximately 1/8th mile north of the Gulf of Mexico.I awoke at about 02:15 and walked outside to get some air and look at the stars..the sky was clear with no clouds visable.I saw a very bright light moving slowly from east to west.as I watched the light it stopped and hovered.Out of nowhere a small cloud appeared and a smaller light came out of the larger one and went behind the cloud.I stepped inside to get my telescope to get a better look at the light.this took only 5 seconds and when I went back outside the light was gone..just vanished very quickly.l was a signalman in the Navy and trained to observe my surroundings with accuracy but had never seen any aerial vehicle behave the way this one did. It moved silently and hovered motionless for several minutes.I waited to talk about this incident because I was the only witness as far as I know.I know what I saw and do not believe the craft I witnessed was a military craft. I believe it was not of this world. I am and was of sound mind and body.
