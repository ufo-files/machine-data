(GS) Red-orange lit object which was changing directions and then vanished.

I was out walking one of my dogs when a bright orangey-red light caught my eye. At first, I thought that it was someone shooting off fireworks because it is that same sort of red and the path was slightly in an arc. It wasnt blinking, but rather pulsating. It flew in a path over my house. I ran in and grabbed my glasses and a witness, and went out the back of the house. It traveled for a while on the same path, then reversed its path and then hovered. Then it rapidly got smaller and smaller until it was gone. I cant believe no one else saw it!
