{rjl} Round, 9 lights, big as a football stadium

Object hovering above treetops. I was driving home when observed. I pulled over & stopped almost completely under it. My car window was down & heard no sound. It stayed there still hovering. After about 5 minutes it took off & was out of sight in about 3 seconds still not making any sound. My 2 daughters were asleep in the back seat never waking up. All this happened 40 years ago. After seeing Chronicle's show I decided to write.
