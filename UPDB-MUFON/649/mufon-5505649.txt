{rjl} Observed unexplained bright lights that went in formation twice,saw 1 large one appear to come up from ground,more appeared/lit up

Aurora, CO, New Years day 2014 at 12:12am, my backyard faces directly east, I let my dog out and noticed as soon as I opened the door 2 unusual yellowish lights in the NE, they were lined up, at 1st I thought it was airplanes or helecopters but they looked differant and were just hovering, then a 3rd one appeared and the 3 formed a triangle, I went inside to get my camera, when I came back outside the 3rd one was either gone or the light went out,this is the 1st picture I took,then I saw one large one was rising up beyond the trees in the NE distance,2nd picture,I wondered if these were japanese lanterns but it was a cloudy night-this was just 3 hours before it started to snow, so I wasnt able to see neighborhood fireworks since it was cloudy,I just heard them. After the big one rose in the sky,there were 3 smaller ones again and they lined up in a straight line about the larger one for a few seconds,this is the beginning of what I filmed. then this formation broke up and the smaller lights spread out in a zig zag with the largest one still hovering and moving around in the sky below, more smaller lights appeared, some blinked, the video doesnt show all that I saw since I was zooming in and trying to keep the camera steady, there were a total of 7 accross the sky hovering and blinking, at this point I was a little freaked out so I stopped filming and went inside, so I really don't know how long they were out there, but right before I stopped filming it seemed the lights were starting to go out or they took differant direction and were gone,so when I went inside there were only 2 small ones left in the sky and the large one still hovering below. I am pretty much a skeptic about UFOs until I saw this, later than morning I started looking for sightings online,it was so noticeable I wondered how many other people saw this. I had to wait until I used someone elses laptop to report this because mine wont take any pictures or videos right now due to some kind of problem with it. Since I waited until now though I have been checking online and noticed alot of other sightings in other areas on New Years morning not long after midnight.
