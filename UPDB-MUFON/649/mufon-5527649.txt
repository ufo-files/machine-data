Two bright lights disappeared behind cloud cover.

Report written by MUFON S.T.A.R. Field Investigator *(Wit's name deleted--CMS/sg) Virginia (witness).

At around 06:12 AM in the morning, I was sitting in my vehicle at a worksite located at a new high school building that was being built in Berryville, VA, a few miles off Route 7. I was parked facing east as I was watching the sky change color from the sunrise. Then my attention was drawn to a bright, white light that appeared right above a dark elongated cloud. Strangely, I also noticed that the light1s trajectory did not behave as to a plane1s takeoff light, the flight pattern was vertical.  The light soon disappeared behind the cloud ribbon but oddly enough, it did not emerge above it 1 [See A on diagram].  About a minute later, I saw what seems to be another white light moving upward but slightly to the left of the first sighting and it also vanished behind a cloud 1 [See B].  These lights were slightly larger and brighter in comparison to what I believe to be the planet Venus that was positioned E/SE above the horizon. Each sighting lasted about 5 to 6 seconds prior losing them behind cloud cover.

Investigator1s notes: Airliners' white takeoff lights should have been turned off at that altitude and even if these planes were facing me (westerly flight-path) they would have eventually flown at an angle or over my area. No other navigational strobes or beacons were detected. Dulles International Airport is approximately 30 miles E and a smaller airport, Leesburg Executive Airport is about 18 miles from where I was parked.
