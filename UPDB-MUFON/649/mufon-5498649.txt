{nd} There were (2) objects above as I going outside to see why dogs were barking, I observed one slightly ahead of the other heading north then then turned No. E very fast.

I was in my home, and at approx.7:50 p.m. on October 8th 2014. My Wife was in our back yard on phone w/our daughter. I then heard dogs barking and stepped out to see why. As I took about six steps to reach my drive-way which has a incline to my garage where my wife was, I looked up and immediately observed (2)bright objects, One a little bit ahead of the other heading directly north. At first I thought it was two shooting stars but then realized they were moving too slow. I then excitedly called out to my wife (who was in deep conversation with my daughter) to "LOOK_UP!! Can you see them"? She was closer to the garage than I was and did not have the open view that I had. The sky was clear! She looked at me and then moved closer to my position to try to see what I was seeing (her night eye sight is not what it used to be), and by the time she had the right direction these bright twin objects had turned to a NE direction at what I feel is a unbelievable speed. My total view time I est. to be 18 seconds. These objects were actually crossing the normal airline flight path that I feel was unusual in itself.
