I was in the balcony of my apartment with my friend when I noticed two bright lights hovering in the sky in South direction (South from Yonge and Finch area). I spend most evenings in the balcony and at this point I know the usual traffic routes for the commercial airplanes. I had never seen these lights before and that's why they caught my attention. A few seconds after I observed them, one of them (the lower of the two) vanished. It didn't move or zap away, just vanished. This is when I started recording the object. It was in the distance (a few kilometers away at least) and completely silent.  It emitted a golden glow. I couldn't make out its shape. Tried zooming in with my phone, but it still looked like an orb of light. For comparison, I also zoomed into an airplane, and I could clearly make out the airplane in the video. After about 6 minutes of recording, the object vanished.
