Single point of light, varied brightness, then dissappearing.

Was outside at Harrisburg East Campground in Harrisburg, Pa, and observed a bright point of light, about pea sized at arms length,due West about 40 degrees from horizon. It was 4:36PM, EDT, a very bright afternoon with a bright sun directly above the light. My initial thought was sun reflecting off of a plane. The light grew brighter, then back to original brightness twice over a period of about 15 - 20 seconds. Then it quickly disappeared. I waited a few minutes, expecting to see either more light or a plane appear, but it did not. Im very skepitcal about UFO sightings, but this just seemed odd to me. By the way, Harrisburg airport is nearby.
