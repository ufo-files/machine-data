Me and my girl friend were taking a walk around 9:15 P.M.. I saw a very bright flash high in the sky out of the corner of my eye. I immediately looked at the flash and only saw a shiny kind of object moving across the sky. It looked like a dim star on a clear night. I also felt that it was very high by the way it looked. After we watched it for about 40 seconds it almost completely dimmed out. I lost it but my girlfriend did not. I picked it back up within about 10 seconds. It continued across the sky for a couple minutes and then appeared to have stopped in the sky. We stood there for a minute to see if it was going to move, and then we went inside. I have seen things moving across the sky before in and around this same area but I have never seen that bright flash before.
