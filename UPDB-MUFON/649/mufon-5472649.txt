UFO caught on camera in broad daylight in Porto Alegre, state of Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil.

It was really hard to capture this object on tape, because it was to far away. It was dark and solid and kept hovering for a couple of minutes. Disappeared and reappeared in a different position. Started to move north in the end and vanished from my point of view.
