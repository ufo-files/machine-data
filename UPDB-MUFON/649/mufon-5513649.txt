{dr}3 objects overhead. 2 high up stationary, 1 low and manuvering. chased away by fighter jets.

I did not witness this event first hand, but wanted to share the photo and story. Mid summer 2007 July 30 / August 1 around 3 am 3 objects were spotted above my parents machine shop during a shift break. One of the employees got out his camera and took a few photos. Two of the objects stayed up high and stationary while the third was much lower and "moving" around. After a few minutes of this all 3 objects fled the area (i don't know the direction). Right behind them came what appeared to be 2 fighter jets. This occured within close proximity to downtown houston. Zip 77020. The description of the objects given to me was they appeared to be about 12ft in diameter, were spinning, and had a variety of colors, but remained mostly a light blue or turquiose color. The bright light on the right side of the picture is the moon.
