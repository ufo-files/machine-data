Light ascending straight up and then traveling due north.

I live a few miles north-east of the Detroit metro airport, I frequently see commercial aircraft taking off and landing, I’ve never seen one with such a bright, solid light.

Cold winter weather and intermittent winds make “Chinese lanterns” unlikely. 

No flashers in any direction, craft appeared to ascend straight up relatively quickly, and then travel due north over my house and out of view. 

Craft appeared dark gray as it passed overhead, with slight tail and wing features, wingspan appeared similar to a 737 but without gaps under the wings. Like a delta wing design, not a black triangle. 
Appeared to have orange downward facing lights on the body. 

I stopped filming because I was frustrated with the halo artifact on the video from the auto focus. 

I was looking out window observing Sirius and surrounding stars with my telescope when I saw this light to the right.
