{dr}Flying object over Indian reservation with directional change and sequential lights

As I was driving west to work on Riggs road coming from Hwy 347 the road began to turn becoming 51st ave. As I was driving I saw an object in the sky with and outline that is not that of an airplane. I noticed it was too low and that the lights on it were not of a plane. The lights were turning on and off and when they were on they were in a pattern of some sort. There must of have been 4-6 lights on this object. I didn't hear any noise coming from it. Then I took out my camera on my phone and started to video tape it and it turned directions. It was going from North to South but then it went started going East. It wasn't a sharp turn just gradually turned. As I drove I lost sight of the object behind a large bush. I cant confirm that it flew away or landed. 

As I drove away my radio selected a radio station on its own. The same thing happened to my radio when I was driving down Groom Lake Road in Nevada. 

I don't know how to upload my video from my phone.
