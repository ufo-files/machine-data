{rjl} I seen a bright shiny half a oval hovering by tree tops. Then it was gone in a split second.

I was traveling on Rainier road. Leaving Lacey, Washington. Going toward Rainier. A very bright object caught my eye. I tried to get a better look, it was reflective and very bright. It was flat on the bottom. And dome shaped on the top. It was by the tree tops. It was sunny out. It was far away. I blinked and it was gone. I tried looking when we passed the trees, but didn't see it anywhere. I even asked my husband if he saw it. And asked him what is that? He said where? But it was gone. It hovered, went toward the ground a little. Then was gone.
