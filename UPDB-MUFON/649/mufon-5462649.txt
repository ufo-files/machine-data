My husband, our two toddlers, and I were on an evening drive. We drove 20 or so minutes from Ellsworth to Lamoine. On the way to Lamoine we did see an airplane. Saw the lights and the wings. 
We parked at Lamoine Beach for another 10 minutes, or so, before deciding to turn around and come home. 
We drove a few minutes then just as we were about to pass a church and graveyard I looked up at the sky. And two large lights came into view. They were very large and fast. 
"What the f is that?" I said to my husband who did get a glance of it as we turned a corner. 
It flew over head, it was very large thr two white lights looked connected to a craft but it was too dark to make an ourline of a shape. 
I saw the lights faintly as we were driving away. 
On the way back he (my husband) confirmed he did see what I saw without me giving him information.
