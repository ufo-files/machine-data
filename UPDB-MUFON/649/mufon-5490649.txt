{nd} Glowing Orb appears over Picacho Peak only for a moment

Yesterday, 8_02_2015, at 10:33 am while driving north on Interstate 10 just south of Picacho Peak, Arizona, I took a series of pictures of Picacho Peak with my iPhone while driving. In one picture, there is a glowing round-like object over Picacho Peak that is only there in one picture. I took the pictures within seconds of one another because I was driving and wanted a good picture. I never noticed anything (the orb) while I was taking the pictures because I was driving. I have the originals on my iPhone and as god is my witness, they are unaltered. I looked at all of the pictures on my phone and there is no object like this on any picture in my photos. The object has such a prominent glow, it does not appear as though it could have been dust that blew away, rather a legitimate object that was momentarily observable.
