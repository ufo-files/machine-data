Spherical orb of light that would become right and completely fade at irregular intervals

Saw a spherical orb of light roughly the size of a bb moving silently to the Northeast between Holdenville and Spaulding Oklahoma. It made absolutely no noise and flew roughly 750 feet in altitude. It did not blink but rather pulsed. It would become very bright and then fade to black then pulse a bit and fade again and then it would suddenly become very bright again. The Interval between pulses was random as was the intensity. It was an absolutely clear night with no clouds and no aircraft of any kind in the area. It continued at the same altitude and speed flying in a northeasterly direction until eventually it faded from side.
