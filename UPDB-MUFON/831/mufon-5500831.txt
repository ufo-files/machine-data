Chrome oval shape witnessed by myself and friend in woods - ufo just vanished after decent

Myself and friend were walking from his house (in earlstown) to my house (in Haydock, st helens).
It was middle of the afternoon - summertime - nice sunny day.
The route we took was through an area known as grange valley, a wooded area with fields.
As it was hot we took a rest on a fallen down tree with a rope swing and both lit cigarettes.
My friend sat on the swing and myself on the actual tree - we were about 6ft away from each other.
We had been there a minute or two when we both saw a siver/chrome object - we were both asking each other if we were both seeing the same thing.
It was about 40ft above the tree tops, about 100 meters away from us.
The object was about 15-20ft in diameter, chrome/metallic coloured (very reflective) -
Oval shaped with small landing struts beneath and a small dome on top.
It was slowly descending, it took about 2minutes to get into the tree tops, when we started to loose sight of it because of all the leaves we both ran over the field towards the wooded area where it would be. When we both got there it had just vanished. No sign of it at all.
We both went to my house after that and after telling a few people and being called loonies by various people, we just didnt make a big thing of it afterwards.
