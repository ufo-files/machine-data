{tjd} One evening myself and my girlfriend witnessed something unexplainable

So in the summer of 2002 my girlfriend and I were sleeping in my room. I stayed with my parents at the time. Their home was in the country and had ceiling windows all throughout the home. Around 2AM, my girlfriend wakes up screaming and very scared. Immediately I had to cover my ears and my eyes. A beeping/buzzing sound was coming from above our home, however it was the light that made it unable to see. The light filled my parents 4000 square foot home like it was the middle of the day. I was blinded by the light and so was my girlfriend. We couldn1t see anything and heard a very very loud beeping sound and kinda buzzing as well. Now I was scared like never before. She was freaking out more, I told her to stay in the bedroom and I was going to check on my parents. She refused to leave me. The sound and the lights were very very very intense. As I made it to my parent bedroom on the first floor(they where to only room without a ceiling window) I yelled in and woke them up. I said mom dad are you hearing and seeing this. At about that time standing in the door the lights were gone and the sounds also. They were gone in a split second. I went back upstairs and we spoke about it for about an hour asking what in the world it could have been. One evening here recently I had dream that on that evening I floated out of my window and it was during that time that my girlfriend woke me up. I guess I never reported it because it was too strange to report. I am not sure if I was actually upducted or not but the image of me floating through my window seems pretty real. That is the only memory I have.
