{dr}Appeared as if a plane was going to crash. Slammed on brakes. Slid underneath. Was 8 feet above car. Scared to death.

Coming home from ice cream with girl friend. Nobody on road except us. I yelled "Oh my God it is going to crash". Bright light coming over tree tops from left. I slammed on the brakes and my car came to a stop in the middle of the road, at a slight angle. I noticed a bright orange glow off of all the chrome. I went to open the door, but was locked. Unlocked door, got out, and to my absolute astonishment there was an orange ball, about 15 feet in diameter about 15 feet above car. As I stood there after getting out of the car, I starred at the ball for about 8 to 12 seconds. I was not only in amazement, but frightened as well. Then it shot off over Lake Erie at a speed that was incomprehensible ! Or it moved one foot and got the size of a pin prick. However, I do not think that. No sound, no nothing. It was definitely observing me. Took girl friend home, was obviously shaken, drove home. Got home about midnight. Saw the ball about. 10 pm or so. It was a fifteen minute trip home. I lost about an hour and a half, give or take. I wish it would not have happened. FYI, I have never had any recollection of being abducted, even though I lost that time.
