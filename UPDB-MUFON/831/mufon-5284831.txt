It seemed to be a black/ dark triangle with 3 yellowish lights, each one in one of the triangle's angle, it was there and then disappeared

I was attending a wedding in the Apote, a suburb of the town of Tiquipaya in Cochabamba, Bolivia; the couple was from the Adventists church, so there was no alcohol and because it was in the country, there weren't any street lights. At about 7 at night, as I was walking and looking around the place, I looked up and there was a dark or black triangle with 3 yellowish lights. I don't know the rest of the shape because it was all dark. It was just floating there at an angle of 60 degrees from the floor; it stayed there for about a minute, then turned to my right (SW), advanced in an open curve and then the lights instead of facing me turned to face the ground and I couldn't see it anymore. I don't know if it landed, there was a wall and a few trees and since the lights were no longer visible, I don't know.
It wasn't a plane, planes don't have that perfect triangle shape, they are not silent or fly so low and so slow. In the pictures, the red dot is me, they yellow, the object and the last is a geographic reference of the area.
