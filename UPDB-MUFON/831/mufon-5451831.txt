Teo black doughnut shaped objects were seen from a car on way back from work. I was the witness and was a passenger in the car. These were rotating slowly and looked like car tyres high in the air. I also viewed these from home which was close to the initial siting location (Carmunnock Road, Castlemilk, Glasgow UK in the summer of 2008. A report was made by me at the time.
