"My sighting that took place in September of 2014 next door to the Coast Guard station in Traverse City Michigan. All I can say is it was a low semi flat cloud cover at about 1000 ft above ground level for as far as I could see. A helicopter came in as I was outside having a cigarette break and hovered as normal pryer to landing. I don't know the ceiling height of the clouds but was surprised that C.G. was flying. The rest is hard to explain but what I saw was an lite gray object come out of the clouds about 75 yards behind the helicopter slowly. My best guestimate was it was 40'×60' in size but I believe most was still in the clouds. The object rectangular in length with round corners and had three divided , I guess windows, only they showed a somewhat reverse cloud cover from the surrounding clouds. This happened over a period of about 45 seconds to a minute before the craft slowly back into the clouds. If it was something in my eye's it would have continued up in the clouds but it disappeared in the cloud layer!"
