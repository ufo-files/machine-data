{dr}Red lights in sky

I was driving down 490 East bound at about 10:10 PM when I noticed to my right some red lights in the sky. I thought that they were fireworks and I pointed them out to the other passenger in the car and he recognized them also. We kept driving down the road when I noticed that the "fireworks" were not dissolving. We came to a clearing where there we no trees and we noticed that these lights were hovering in air. I tried to take a picture while moving in the car but did not get a very good shot. I do have a photograph of these lights and you can see in the picture that they were moving at that time. I did not feel scared, I felt calm and just wanted to find out more about these objects. I lost sight of the lights when they appeared to have vanished.
