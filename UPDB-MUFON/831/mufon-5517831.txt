{nd} Late night, multiple objects witnessed at once, by three or more people.

I was out having a cigarette, after watching a movie with my girlfriend and friend. I saw a bright light that looked like a top, it began to grow brighter, so I called my girlfriend out to view it. Than the light began move erratically, thats when my neighbor was walking by and I pointed it out too her, and than it shot up above 500ft in the sky, than began hovering and than it looked like it shot two objects of blue light towards the surface. It appeared to have a tracer to it, leaving two streaks of blue temporally in the sky. Afterwards it began to move erratically again from the left to the right until it disappeared. After that I ran up to the apartments next door to grab my other neighbor, when we got back outside we began noticing that there were more of them appearing after the first one left, at least a 5 mile radius of the crafts hovering around. There were different groups of them, doing a multitude of patterns. The main pattern we saw were triangles and squares, approximately twenty to twenty five minutes after getting my neighbor, he pointed out a Red Orb that appeared from the South East towards the Oregon City/Carver area. The Orb began flickering a bright red light, as it appeared to "dance" in a zig zag pattern intercepting the various patterns as it went. After that Orb disappeared towards the N/NE, approximately ten minutes after that, the square patterns began rotating from square to line, square to line and than began glowing red. Afterwards we grew tired but they remained in the sky, all four of us were extremely nervous but not exactly frightened. As of right now at three of us are still a little rattled from what we saw, and can still picture the blue lights. Currently my adrenaline levels are still on high, and so is my neighbors.
