Observed 3 white orbs, caught on cam but lost tape.

I was with my girlfriend at the time in the backyard sunbathing. As I was looking at the sky there were several clouds in the area. While I was scanning the sky I notice 2 white orb like structure. They seemed to be traveling just above the clouds. I rushed inside to grab my sony camera at the time. As I returned I reacquired it. I was able to zoom in 20X and it looked like a sphere like object white and orb like. It traveled in a straight path. When the military recently released those objects, they themselves witnessed, i decided to report my story. Feel free to call me anytime. I did go to a local station and showed them my film but never published the story. I cannot find the tape But I have quite a few 8mm and other digital film. I just don't have a player.
