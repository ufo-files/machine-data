A streaming light Goes up into the and disapear right in front of my eyes

At approximately 10:15pm, 11 FEB 2011, I was driving on hwy 295 South in Virginia and as I approached the mile marker 33(approximately), I saw a stream of light go up into the air right in front of my eyes. At the peak of the streaming light, I noticed it was wider than the actual stream. Within approximately 1.5 seconds, it was all over, and the entire streaming light had disappeared. I immediately thought to myself, was that a flare? However, flares don1t disappear they burn as the light falls to the ground. This beaming light appeared to come from the ground up into the air a fast speed and disappeared rapidly. The area were the streak of light came from was partially wooded, however, these are the trees that separate 295 N and 295 S. 

Based on what I saw, it appeared that it may have been something moving so fast into the air, that it left a bright white streak and then it disapeared. This concludes what I saw at 10:15, 11 FEB 2011. To protect my identity, I have utilized dummy information; however, Im sure there are other means of figuring who I am. I wish to remain anonymous.
