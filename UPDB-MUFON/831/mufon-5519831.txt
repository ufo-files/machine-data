Two white lights leaving a white trail in the sky

I was driving to work (684 south in NY - near Katonah) at about 5 am (est) when I noticed two white balls of light surrounded by a white glow traveling downward very slowly. They were leaving a trail of light behind them. I thought I was seeing things. Then they did something really odd, they changed directions (it seemd like they were doing a dance.). Not just once but numerous times. They were very erratic. They fell for a while and then shot back upward then back down at another angle leaving a trail of white light behind. I pulled over to look and other cars started to pull over as well, so I knew I wasnt seeing things. They were making lightning shaped patterns in the sky. I have never seen anything like this. It was amazing yet terrifying at the same time. All I kept saying is "what the hell is that?" I wonder if anyone saw these things. Unfortunately I couldnt get a picture of it. The glow was too dim by the time I got pulled over to the side of the road. Has anyone out there seen this? It was freaky!!!
