sighting (?) before 1965 (West of Houston, Tx)

When I was a child I remember we were watching the news.. they reported that aUFO had been seen on our side of town.. so my parents loaded us kids into thecar and went out to see for ourselves.. we drove out far out of town (no moon,clear skies, very dark), where we came across what looked like a water towerthat was lite up with spot lights.. we pulled off the side of the road to lookat it (only a couple of 100 feet away)... we heard a roar, rumble.. like a verybig engine idleing ..We concluded it was a water tower and went home... no onein the car but me (the youngest) remember this.. As I got older I keptedthinking about it.. Who lights up a water tower at night with spotlights...also, water towers are objects that belong in cities, not out in the rural farmand ranch land... I know it is way too late to investigate.. but I thought Iwould add it to your database..
