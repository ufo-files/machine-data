I got out of my car and checked the mail. For some reason I noticed movement in the sky. Black birds were attacking a black box flying slowly above the neighborhood the object then flashed red and then black and ascended above the birds. The red and black were not lights but rather as though it was red on one side and black on the other. It was obviously a rectangular box and by the size of the birds Id say 5 feet long. It made no noise like a retail drone does (my neighbor has one its very annoying). This was silent. As it began to disappear behind the trees moving off to the SE I realized there is a bridge over a railroad a few streets away that looks over most of the city so I hopped in my car and began to follow it. Just as I reached the bridge it just disappeared and from the west came a helicopter (black). The show was over. This is the oddest thing Ive seen and in the middle of the day. I stood for about eight minutes watching it at its lowest stunned just stunned as I quickly ruled out all the possibilities. This was not a fuzzy lights in the dark UFO sighting. The red and black is perplexing and the distance it traveled across the cityscape makes me think radio controlled retail drone is out of the question. It really sped up after the bird business and covered a good distance when I last saw it as I was driving it was just a black dot in the distance...it was moving faster than before. I wanted to report it because it was so odd.
