{rjl} A light that would seem like a light bulb on in a House, or a dim search light.

I was driving along the river beside Gainsborough highway heading home from picking kids up from church. I always check out the sky on our way in or out of home. We had just came up the hill to the seven sisters,which is a natural formation of rocks the town named. I looked to my right in the sky an where normally would be a few bright stars was a glowing light stationary in the sky. I was driving at least 45-50. I then asked if my step sons noticed the light too,at first I argued that it wasn't a star because I look every night on my way from work an this was the first I had seen the object. I continued home and returned to find that the object had left the area. I think I knew it would not be there when I returned but I felt I had to go back to see. I thought the object mite have been a a military craft but the light was not like lights on air craft it seemed to completely covered in the light emitting from it.
