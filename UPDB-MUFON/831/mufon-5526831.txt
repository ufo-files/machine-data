Friend sighting of UFO in the sky

My friend was laying on the bed of histruck one summer night talking with his friends (who were standing up and chatting/smoking) then he said suddenly,above the treeline,he saw a very big object passing by (from East to West) in an amazing speed yet completely silent. He screamed to his friends "Did you see that?? Did you see that??" his friends asked "See what?" he replied "The UFO.Big,huge,silver color, like the underbelly of an airplane but very wide,oval shaped". His friends laughed at him saying "Oh,yeah,and ET stuck his hand out and waved goodbye". He told me "Nobody believes me.They think I imagined it,but I swear I saw it.I am not scared of anything,but that was a supernatural feeling that truly frightened me" He told me that back in 1995,so I said "Well...who knows" and in 2008 in the very same town (Stephenville,Texas) people claimed to have seen a UFO.
