Orb sighted over the river Forth, slow moving and desended to a level under the Forth rail bridge then took off at an amazing speed.

I was out with a girl on a first date that night. We were visiting North Queensferry by car, to hangout. We parked the car and got out for a walk down by the river under the Forth Rail Bridge, at the time before the sealife centre was built you could walk right round under the bridge and around the cove. There was a picnic spot on the hill to the left of the bridge looking towards South Queensferry. We were just chatting, getting to know each other , at the time we were looking out towards Edinburgh to the left of us, I was watching planes coming into land at the airport, not really paying them to much mind as we were chatting away to each other. I thought what i was watching was a plane (the Planes landing lights). "South Queensferry has a big hill which blocks the view partially of Edinburgh and the airport". So the Orb desended fairly slowly and was still in view in front of the hill instead of behind the hill which most planes that land at Edinburgh airport do! This caught my attention straight away and pointed this light out the girl i was with, we were quite stunned at this. The light was pretty much in the middle of the river Forth travelling west up the river past Rosyth Royal Dockyard. To our surprise the Light/Orb slowly desended to a level which would take its flight path under the Forth Rail Bridge and Forth Road Bridge and took off at an amazing speed, Wow, we were takin aback abit to say the least. I've spoken about this sighting plenty of times but everyone thinks I'm nuts!  My feelings on this matter makes me feel that who ever was piloting this craft did that manouver for fun, because it looked fun to my eyes. It still thrills me now seeing what i seen, yours sincerely [Name removed/cms/tg].
