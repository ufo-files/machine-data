Moving craft that remained stationary and then another craft zoomed by the first. They both looked metallic and reflected the sun.

We watched one metal-like craft for two days on 10/13-10/14 2021. We saw it through binoculars and it appeared to glide around and change shape occasionally. It reflected the sun and appeared metallic but an area on top of the object moved but we couldn’t see exactly what was moving. While we were watching it in 10/14 we took a video and a dreidel shaped craft zoomed by it! So we captured that footage. These objects were different in shape and behavior.
