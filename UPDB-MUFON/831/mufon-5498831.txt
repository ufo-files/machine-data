{cak} One large object hovering, one glowing white orb then either split into two or another emerged from the one

I was in my apartment and woke up around 5:20 a.m. or so and meditated from 5:30-6 a.m. At six a.m. I made morning coffee and by 6:15 a.m. I was sitting on my couch, sipping on coffee, looking out the window admiring the beautiful sunrise. 

As I was sitting on my couch, I noticed a light in the sky that wasn1t moving, it just hovered, and it was really bright. Naturally, I began staring at it. Then I grabbed my phone to take pictures, which I did. There was a large, bright, cylinder/sphere shaped object hovering in the sky for about 45 minutes, nearby it was another white round orb like glow, and out of this white glowing light another one emerged, or maybe the one split into two, I am not sure but where there was one there were now two. Then the third one disappeared. Throughout this entire ordeal there was no noise at all. 

Sometime within that 45 minute window I noticed a passenger plane fly toward the trajectory of the object and turn around headed back in the direction it came, but I do live close to the airport. I also saw another plane approach the general area, and this plane left those white trails behind then flew away. Then at 7 a.m. I got up to take a shower and the objects seemed to have vanished.  

After getting out of the shower I noticed marks on my chest. There were two small puncture wounds on my right ICS 3 area very close to my sternum. I have noticed these marks before but never thought anything of them. To me, I always thought they were insect bites of some sort. So, that morning I didn1t think any different.
