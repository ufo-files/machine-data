UFO sighting in Maricopa County

On my way back from work I witnessed 3 bright white lights. The one light in the middle was constant and did not blink, while the other two on the ends blinked alternately. There also was a constant red light and appeared to be in the middle. I noticed the object because it was really low, and obviously wasn't a plane coming from the west because it was off route. At first I thought it was a helicopter with the flashing lights, then I approached it while driving in my car and it seemed to be moving pretty slow. I turned around and as i did that it changed direction (Northeast) then suddenly it turned back toward the East and began to accelerate much quicker then when I was previously close to it. During the sighting I definitely knew it wasn't a regular plane. The only possible explanation was that it was a stealth, but i highly doubt that. After the sighting, I am convinced it was a UFO merely by its lights, speed, and sudden change of direction. I eventually lost sight when it accelerated towards the East. My view of this object lasted from about 3-5 minutes.
