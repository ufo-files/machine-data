Fireball

There has been at least 4 different kinds of crafts seen 3 by myself In Pleasant grove, A almost invisible boomerang shaped craft Which was huge, 2 white orbs flying fast in close proximity of each other During the month of May & June. 
On this particular video we we’re hanging out In The back of my friends house. When In the distance we saw the fireball rise up out of a house somewhere on a 45 degree angle to our left. And started coming towards our direction very slowly. It couldn’t have been any higher than 150 ft. I walked up to the fence and immediately started recording. After it was headed our direction I sort of turned slightly to the trajectory of 2 houses down as if to not fly directly over us. It was flying over So slow and was about to pass the front of the house. So we decided to run thru the house to the front. It could not have taken more than 4 sec to when we went to the front of the house and looked back up and it was gone. We were confused and began looking around when I could bearly spot it in the distance ahead of the house. It was miles ahead in The distance. Where you could bearly notice the red dot getting smaller and smaller until it vanished. It was only after I realized it didn’t make any noise whatsoever.
