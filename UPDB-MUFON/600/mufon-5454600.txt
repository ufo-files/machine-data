{cak} satellite like

Around 9 pm in western sky. Just above tree line a satellite like object rose up passing the Dog star moving in an arc to the south climbing and passing through Orion's belt then fade out of sight. These lights were continuous one after another, about maybe 20 to 30. Spaced out and at different intervals. Ending about 9:20 pm. Then about 5 minutes later a brighter satellite like object rose up from tree line passing to the left of the Dog star on a trajectory for the big dipper but fade and was gone.
