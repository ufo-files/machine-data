4 red lights in each corner. craft silent. As large as a jet.

Saw a bright red light come over the mountain near DeRuyter Lake from the direction of Cazenovia in upstate NY. As it climbed and bank slowly I could see 4 red lights in each corner. Lights were steady. No other lights. It made no noise. As big as a plane, but really difficult to see the craft. We hear and see planes all the time. This was not a plane. As it went aross the sky it suddenly disappeared.
