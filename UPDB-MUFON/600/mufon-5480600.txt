Orange balls on a very fast moving, seen more than once in around 40 minutes

As a skywatcher I normally look at the east side of my house and yesterday I was looking when suddenly one orange ball was moving in the sky very fast. I did not pay to much attention, but 5/10min later 3 more balls in a triangular formation moved again very fast from NE to SW in a straight path. These 3 balls appeared again after 10min in the same formation and direction. After that I did not see it again, so I moved to west side of my house and I saw 2 more around 9:05pm. Those were moving slower and they look like that they had wings, but no fuselage, with orange ball circling them. My daughter witnesssed this last sight. I could not take any picture as they were moving so fast. I ask anyone in the area, please let me know in the comments section if you saw the same.
