{jpg} Noticed an orange light in sky, then noticed several, flashing, then they merged into a triangle and faded out.

I was a passenger in a car traveling west on I-10 in Katy. It was around 8 pm and I was looking out the window. I saw an orange/amber light that was too large and bright to be a satellite or star and was not moving at all. It was too high to be a street light. I thought it may be a helicopter hovering but it didn't have the normal lights. I looked around and saw there were other amber lights like it in a grid, maybe 15 or 20 lights. That was very startling to see. The lights were evenly placed over south Katy between Fry Road and Grand Parkway. The lights were slowly fading in and out, but not in unison, they were not moving. We pulled over in a parking lot on the north side of katy at Westgreen Blvd. to get a better look and there was no noticeable noise or heat coming from the lights. After a few minutes they started to slowly move together and seemed to connect. The lights completely disappeared and a large grey triangle traveled slowly and quietly east/south east towards the city of Houston. It was difficult to see the craft because there were no lights on it. It was about 200 feet above the treeline It was a very large, equilateral triangle that covered most of the area between South Fry Road and South Mason Road. Both of us had the very distinct feeling that whatever was in there knew we were observing it so we decided not to follow it. It was gone from view in 2 minutes, maybe less at that point.
