{nd} Spotted UFO while travelling on I-96 outside Lansing, MI.

I was travelling with my family on I-96 headed westbound outside Lansing, MI when I observed two lights in the sky blinking. At first, I thought it may have been an aircraft, however as we got closer, I realized that the wingspan and outline of the UFO was larger than that of a jetliner. It was long and oval in shape and had only two lights. Its altitude was no more than 3,000 feet and there was no noise coming from it. There were no nearby airports (the nearest airport, Capital City International Airport in Lansing, was 15-20 miles away). The UFO hovered over the interstate for a few seconds and then quickly moved over a field on the side of the highway. After approximately 15 seconds, I looked back and it was gone. The entire sighting lasted only 30 seconds. It should be noted that the sky was clear and the weather was fair. Visibility was greater than 10 miles and traffic on the highway was light.
