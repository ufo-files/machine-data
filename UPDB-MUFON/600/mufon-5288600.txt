Moving slow in straight line. Changed colors.

It was about half a mile away. I noticed it because a glint from the sun, which was behind me, reflected off the craft catching my eye. I started to look at it and noticed that it was in front of a cloud and moving very slow and staying at exactly the same altitude heading towards Nashville from Gallatin. When it got on the other side of the cloud and into the blueness of the sky it’s color changed from a white silvery metallic to a dark black. I kept watching it and it never changed altitude nor direction and stayed on the same path. It was moving to slow to be an airplane and to be able to maintain its altitude like it did. I am currently enrolled at college getting my Aerospace degree with a Pro Pilot rating with it to become an airline pilot if I choose or any other type pilot I choose to be. I tried taking several pictures of the object but it wouldn’t show up on the camera. I was actually getting frustrated because I would look up and see the craft but when I snapped a picture it wouldn’t show up and I would then look up and see it especially when it turned to black and still wouldn’t show up on my camera.
