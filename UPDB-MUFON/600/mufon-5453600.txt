{rjl} Streak of Blue light shot out of sky into field at end of my road

I was sitting in my truck watching TV using my heated seats to help with back pain.the weather was cool and clear. I was leaned back and chewing gum i decided to spit it out and when I rolled down the window I observed a streak of blue light shoot out of the sky towards the ground. I put my truck into gear and drove down the road and parked across from the field. I observed a dense fog had immediately covered the area after the event. after a few minutes the fog disappeared and nothing was there. I have seen this blue streak once before in my neighborhood but never thought about reporting it. Also note nobody was outside at the time of incident. and all of the stray cats i usually see in my neighborhood weren't out at all. which is strange because they always roam around begging for food.
