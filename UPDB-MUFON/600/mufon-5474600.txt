I was taking photos of the full moon when I noticed a black object flying toward the moon and into my shot. It made no sound and had had no lights. It flew from left to right then down then hovered then back to the left. When I got home and dumped photos to computer I noticed that there were actually 3 objects in the photos and sometimes 2. They were flying saucer shaped. I kept shooting but the objects were no longer visible. They just vanished.
