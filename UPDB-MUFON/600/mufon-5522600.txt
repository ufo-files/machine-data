Appeared to be v-shaped 6-8 objects that moved in a straight line then formed a v

We were standing in the driveway observing stars at approximately 10:00-10:15 on November 17, 2011. When in a straight line there were 6-10 flying objects moving in a line on a slant. They then changed direction of the slant, then they formed a v - the entire time moving across the sky. There were not any sounds. At first, I was shocked then my friend saw them too and we watched them until they flew out of sight saying to one another what was that did we just see a UFO?
