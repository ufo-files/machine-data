{nd} I have been seeing lights in the sky, they change color, red, blue, and white

I have been seeing strange lights for 6 months. I live in the country and it is really dark, I love to go out in the evening and sit on our deck and look at the stars. I noticed the lights one night when I was coming home from town there were 3 lights in the form of a triangle, facing east. What got my attention is the fact that they change colors. I have seen them several times. Last night I had the kids out on the deck looking at them as well. At first I was a little scared but as long as they stay in the air I know Im fine. Some times I go to a conservation area not to far from my house and sit and watch them, because its completely dark there.
