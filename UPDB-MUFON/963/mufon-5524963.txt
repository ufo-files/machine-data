3/1/2011 7:46 pm huge rectangular object 4 unblinking green lights set squarely in center of underside.

I was alone, driving up Southington Mountain (Meriden Rd) toward Waterbury at 7:46 pm on March 1, 2011 I noticed 4 green unblinking lights through the treetops to the left of me. The area is very wooded and the trees are quite tall. The object passed above the treetops over the road almost directly above my car. It was descending. At first I thought it was a plane crashing. I heard no sound, but my radio was on. When it past over the road, I realized that the lights were in the center square on the underside of a huge object. I only saw it clearly for about 2-3 seconds. I believe the object was rectangular and was gray/black and completely non-reflective. If the lights were not there, I don't think I would have noticed it. It blended with the night sky. I could still glimpse the lights through the trees on the other side of the road for about 2 seconds more. I drove to the top of the hill and pulled into a parking lot but did not see it again. I did notice a plane in that direction while sitting in the parking lot. I checked ufo websites throughout March and noticed that there were two other sightings at the exact time, one in Waterbury and the other in Meridan.
