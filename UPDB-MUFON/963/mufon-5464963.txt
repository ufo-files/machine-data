{cak} Over water directly in one place

As my Wife and I were driving to Winona 7 movie theater we both saw a round object hovering over the Mississippi river. As I observed was a really bright white light beaming down with a tiny of red at the top of the object. I pointed it out, but Wife said it was just a plane, but was only hovering in one place over the Mississippi not moving. I did hear a bit of different voices and felt kinda a fuzz like sensation in my body while observing it. After the movie it was no longer there.
