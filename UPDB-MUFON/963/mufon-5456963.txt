Hovering blue light

Hovering blue light over Pine Island, NY. At first I thought it was a plane, but I've never seen blue (like a deep cyan blue) lights on a plane, and it also did not have white flashing lights like planes do. I'm not sure how long it stayed still, as I was driving and I thought that because I was moving it just appeared to be staying still, but eventually it started moving way to the north-east. I have no idea how near or far away it was, so can't guess as to it's size or speed.
