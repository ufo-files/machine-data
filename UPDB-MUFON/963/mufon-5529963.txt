My husband and I were riding the Harley,when a cylinder appeared above to the right of us

My husband and I were riding on our Harley making our way back home from Moncton,when I noticed ,what I thought at first was a plane in the sky above us but to the right of us.It stayed with us as we drove ,it almost looked like it was hovering,but it stayed in the same spot in the sky as we kept driving.So,I pointed it out to my husband and he gave me a thumbs up.I watched it,and realized that it didn't have a tail section,or wings for that matter and it was orange in color.So,I thought it might be because it was banking or was in a position in the sky where I couldn't see the wings,but where was the tail? So I thought it was orange because it was nearing sunset and the sun was bright orange,so I looked to my left to see if the sun was above the tree line( which would explain why the object was orange) or below the tree line,then I looked to my right and the object had vanished.I scanned the sky around us and it was gone.There were no clouds in the sky at that moment and it just disappeared.The sun was above the tree line,we were on the highway.It took maybe 4 or 5 seconds when I looked away from the object on my right side ,to look on my left side to see if the sun was above the tree line or not,and then look back to the right to look at the object again,but it had vanished.This was only the 4th daytime sighting I have had,but have had many nightime sightings over the years since 1991.I have reported every sighting to HBCC,but now they don't operate anymore and this is why I am using MUFON.Since that sighting ,my husband and I have had several sightings.We feel fortunate for all the "shows".
