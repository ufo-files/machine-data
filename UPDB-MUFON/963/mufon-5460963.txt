On May 4th 2019 while working (as a house painter) rolling some Fascia Board, I noticed a small Metallic Disc shaped craft, I have seen them several times in my life but hadn't seen one in a couple years, I always tell people. This time I thought I would capture it on my phone camera. I kept an eye on it got my phone ready but, couldn't see it in my phone screen, i retrieved a zoom lens and tried to focas, but couldn't. As I was shooting, it seemed like several crafts, were coming out of one craft I believe around 12 or 15 total. I managed to get a few bulury shots. I uploaded what happened to my Facebook.
