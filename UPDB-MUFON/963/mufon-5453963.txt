{cak} Looked like an arrow with 4-5 white-blue lights

Was simply stargazing one night with my Orion XT8 telescope, looking at Jupiter, stars, the usual during the night, and saw a bright white light cross the sky. Thinking it was an iridium flare, I continued watching it slowly drift. However, it never dimmed during its few minutes of travel and started to turn ever so slowly to a different direction. When I started to follow ot with my telescope, it looked to be in a shape of an arrow, it could be the speed of the thing and needing to also keep track of it while moving with my telescope, but from what I could see, there was ~4-5 white-bluish lights compact tightly together and creating a shape of an arrow. My mother who was with me at the time snagged a pic of it while I was tracking with my scope.
