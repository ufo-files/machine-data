{rjl} Light flashing & moving in night sky

Tonight 11.24.15, I saw flashing lights in the sky that lasted long enough for me to go inside, get my phone & make a video which I will attach. The light moved across tje sky in flashes. Generally, it was in the SE sky, but sometimes due south. On my way home tonight, my daughter, niece, nephew & I saw a UFO moving slowly from SW to NE. It had flashing red lights on the back & bottom & green lights around the body of the ship. I said it looked like the U.S.S. Enterprise, my daughter said it looked like a circle with a clear top & my niece said an ice cream cone. My nephew is 3, he didn't say much. Also, on 11.22.15 my son & I saw a baseball sized ball of light in the southern sky. The light changed from white to flashes of red, stayed stationary long enough for my son to get me up & have me look, then moved in a small figure 8 & disappeared.
