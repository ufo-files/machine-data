{rjl} Red light hovering, dropped suddenly, began blinking, moved like a plane behind tree. Got witness, another drop out of sight

I was sitting outside on my back porch, when I looked to the North and saw a red light, unwavering, and not moving. The light went out momentarily which made me watch it. There are no visible radio towers in that area, and suddenly the light drops in altitude very quickly, and then begins to move eastward slowly, similar to a plane. It also has a white light that starts to blink about twice a second. The object momentarily disappears behind a tree, and when I move to see it better I can see it turn clockwise on the horizontal axis, and can no longer see the white light. At this point I run inside to grab another witness. When we return outside it is at the maximum altitude I had seen it at. We both observe it hovering, and as we comment on it, it drops in altitude again, very quickly behind the tree and out of sight. It does not reappear.
