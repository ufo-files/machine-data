after lunar eclips we saw a verry big triangle shape flying whan it went past the moon it was transperent.it was also silent

My children and i were watching the lunar eclipse. since it was cold,we went to my dads house.(across street from my house)He has a big picture window where we could set up a telescope and stay warm.When the moon was 3/4 in veiw after the eclipse,we decided to go home.As we stood on the porch talking about the moon a triangle shaped thing flew across,down,and around in front of us, my first thought was a kite with a broken string floating in a tumbling way back to earth.That is the only way i can describe how it flew.It only took about one or two seconds to fly by.There was a commercial jet in the sky at the time and the object made the jet look like a speck because it was so big.As it went past the moon we noticed it was transperent and you could see the moon through it.It really frightened my son .My daughter and i thought that it was a stealth bomber.But the object was huge,transperent,and flew in a verry weird path.Later we realized it did not make a sound.At the moment the only thing we heard was the commercial jet wich was not verry loud.I don't know if i was afraid,confused,or amazed.It happened so fast.If what i saw was military,I really hope it was ours.I cant think how any man made thing could move like it did.No sudden sharp changes in direction,but a sudden flowing change of direction.It was not in a zig zag pattern,but more of a smoother pattern.It was verry hard to describe.At the time we were in Columbus Ohio faceing East.We lost track of it because it shot away so quickley. If i was to guess a size i would guess 1/3 to 1 whole mile. It was hard to tell because it moved so fast.That was also on a verry clear night.We waited so long because im not the one that would see things like this alot.Once i saw a show about you and knew i had to do something.
