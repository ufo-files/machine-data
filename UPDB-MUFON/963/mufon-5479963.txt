{dr}Five orbs moving together roughtly south to north along Hillsboro Rd near GH mall

I was at a Halloween party with ~25 people on 10/30/16. I looked east toward the Green Hills Mall and initially saw three orbs in a triangle formation with the lead by itself and the two trailing lights more or less stacked. Then two more lights appeared. I alerted my friends, all of whom were outside on what was a warm night. Roughly 1/3 were dismissive, another group thought the lights were Chinese lanterns and the balance were unsure. The lights moved together on a roughly south to north trajectory. There was aircraft traffic in the area (BNA is ~10 miles away); they were not airplanes. The lights were relatively large. They eventually faded away; two together, three separately within a minute or so. The picture does not do justice to the intensity of the light or the clarity of the horizontal movement. Pretty cool! At no time did I have any sense of time/space distortion. I estimate the orbs were about 1 mile away from our location moving roughly along Hillsboro Road near Green Hills Mall (or beyond it to the east).
