THE ZION LIGHTS

I was camping with some freinds in Utah, and me and another freind were in a large, general motors truck. I had two other freinds outside who were sleeping (it was dark). the first red orb slowly rose out of a nearby mountain and hovered for a short amount of time.I noticed the orb, becouse it was late, and when it got late that night in Utah, I was just enjoying the clear night sky and thinking(which is in contrast to the normal wheather we get here in Michigan).When I first saw it, I didn't really know what to make of it becouse it had moved up out of the mountain, (I often observe the skies here in Michigan with a telescope as an amature astronomer,and have seen many a space-like object, despite the fact that Michigan was ranked one of the worst states for doing so). Me and my freind both saw the first orb, then we both saw a second orb slowly rise out of another area near the mountain. Both orbs hovered for a few minutes, then the first one slowly faded out of eye-shot. While this was happening, my freind said he saw another, red orb rise out of the mountain, but I didn't see it. They made no sound (or at least none that could be heard from our distance, for we were probably several miles away). I didn't do much when I saw the object, just stared at it with a look that resembled awe at being confronted by the unkown, and a little fear. My freind reacted with sleepy awe. Then the second one that I saw slowly faded away. the whole event lasted around 10-15 minutes.
