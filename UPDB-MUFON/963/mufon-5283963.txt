Three gray, diamond shaped objects flew in a triangular flight pattern from West to East. Beautiful night with some high clouds.

Halloween night in West Salem, OR. Beautiful night with light cloud cover, no moon. Out trick or treating with my 9 year old. It was a night when the stars seemed extra bright outside the light clouds, and I am an avid star-watcher with the family, so I had been looking up a lot. We were on a dark side-street and I looked up and saw three diamond-shaped objects fly from west to east over our heads. From my perspective, less than an inch long each but clear enough to see the belly had four facets, like a diamond with a cross in the center. Zero light, no noise at all. They were below the cloud layer but I could not tell the elevation. We have had drones, we have seen the ISS fly over the house, National Guard with helicopters are here in town also. My wife and I are prior USMC and have seen military bases and gear. The whole sighting was less than 10 seconds. They were in a V formation, one in front with the other two flanking off the sides. I have no pictures, and a cell phone wouldnt have done anything.
