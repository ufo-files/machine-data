3 lights in a triangle shape appeared to hover

Back in March 2021, we were driving NW I believe on the Taconic in NY. We looked in the sky and noticed some odd lights and decided to record video footage of the lights which you could see me zoom in for a closer look. The lights appeared to be in a triangle pattern and the lights seemed to hover. We kept driving so eventually lost site. No one seems to know what is was, however some people speculate that it was a stealth bomber. I don’t know why it round just hover in the sky if that is what is was. We would like to just find out what exactly those lights were that night. Since we were driving while recording, it looks like it was moving but the lights hovered, we were moving.
