it was about twenty years ago my brother and I were stealing old communication copper wires along train tracks in the middle of farm country very clear night we were having a break looking up at the sky we saw a light way up that was shooting across the sky at a very high rate of speed but it would just change directions without slowing down all over the sky we watched it for at least 4-5 minutes I mean across the entire horizon crazy fast I have no pictures or anything but I swear to God we saw it.
