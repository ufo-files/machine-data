I saw a bizzare object in the night sky that appeared to change shape

My parents usually leave the blinds of the southern kitchen window open so our pet cats can look outside during the day. The last person to go to bed is supposed to lower them. As i was the last one up on the night in question, I proceeded to lower the blinds. I first noticed the object because I saw lights like that of a jumbo jet (note- Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport is only a few miles away). So my first impression was it was a plane ready to land. However, I notice there was something odd when I didn't hear any noise. In addition, it was moving too slow to be a plane. The object moved slowly for approx. 3 minutes and 35 seconds, before abruptly accelerating. I followed the object until it was outside my plane of vision. During the sighting, at first I was annoyed because I thought the noise might wake people up from sleep. After I realized there was no sound, I was bewildered until I fell asleep.
