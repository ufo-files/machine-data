Weird thin cloud edges unchanging on cloudless night

There were absolutely no clouds on this moonlit night. I tried debunking this as an aircraft but with no sound, a cloud surrounding 4 evenly spaced lights...I can't. The cloud was thin encasing all four lights, the front end and back end had a consistent jagged shape. The sides of the cloud were consistent and defined.There wasn't any exhaust trail. Truly stumped having spent many years in air force experiencing all sorts of aircraft flying thru the air. This cloud with lights moved much faster than the jets that usually fly overhead at high altitudes.
