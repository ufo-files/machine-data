{cak} Unknown bright white object

A friend and I walked outside for a smoke and as I usually do I stared up at the sky. After maybe 5 seconds of staring off a bright white light caught my attention. It was moving very slowly. Slow like a piece of space debris moving across the sky kind of slow. I then pointed it out to my friend and the minute I pointed at it the object shifted its path and did what I can only describe as a u turn. It stopped moving and just I guess hovered. I do not know how far above the ground it actually was. The entire situation was exciting and a bit freaky at the same time. We did not loose sight of the object. When the object came to a stop we waited for maybe a minute then noticed that all sound around us had ceased and thats when we walked quickly back indoors.
