{dr}On my way home from work,on busy highway with other cars.

I was on my way home from work, on the north east side of Beltway 8 going towards Highway 59. I live in Kingwood, TX which is north east of Houston, TX about 26 miles. I was traveling along the road with several other cars around me, when I saw about 20 flying objects all flying very low, about highway streetlight level. The objects appeared to be hovering, but then started moving very slowly towards the south east. I started slowing down and noticed all the cars around me were also doing the same thing. It appeared that everyone was trying to take a closer look at what these objects were. I did not stop but kept traveling west until I passed under these objects. I did not hear or feel anything, but was a bit scared. I was listening to music off my iPhone when all a sudden, as I was passing under these objects, my music and iPhone appeared to stop working and after I went past the objects they both came on again. This freaked me out and I immediately called my parents to let them know what I had just seen. I have never in my life ever seen anything like a UFO and this event caught me totally by surprise. I hope someone else reports this so that I can confirm that it wasnt just my imagination.
