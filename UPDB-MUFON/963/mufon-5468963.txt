I only wanted you to know what we saw! Since I keep reading about UFO's, it keeps bringing back what my husband and I saw. It just drives me crazy to this day. It was July, 2013 about 9pm. I'm very upset that we did not have our cell phone or camera out there!

We live in Milwaukee, WI. We were outside with our dogs. I was in the backyard and he was in the front and called me to come. I knew it wasn't something that would normally be in the sky. To me it was amazing to watch!

I know it can't help you, but I thought you would want to know this weird sighting we experienced. There were 7 orange circles following in a line going NW. There was no noise like an airplane or helicopter which freaked us out. Today I still can't get it out of my mind. They just kept going NW and then lost sight of them.
