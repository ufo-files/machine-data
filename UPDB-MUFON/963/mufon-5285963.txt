I observed a very fast craft above east white hills

Whole outside early this morning I witnessed a very fast craft, no lights on it whatsoever, come across the sky in front of me going silently over the city and disappeared behind trees and I lost sight of it. I live by an airport and I’m very used to seeing planes and I’m familiar with planes and their behaviour, speed, colours and lights. This had no commercial back wing: just the blue wings and white shaft. Headed towards the ocean.
