{nd} Three crafts observed - glowing and blinking rainbow

After co-facilitating a shamanic dance journey, my partner and I were standing in a field in upstate NY & staring at the stars when we observed a strange orb of light in the distance. It was traveling in dance-like patterns and seemed to be changing to all different glowing colors. We then spotted another, in a different part of the sky, that appeared stationary but also appeared to be shining in all different colors. Half joking, half serious, I said to the orb - "if you're a UFO, do something and show me." Immediately after, a third craft flew directly overhead, so close that we could see its underside in some detail. It had four lights around its perimeter, and one in the center. The ones around the perimeter were blinking rainbow in a circular, sequential pattern. It was difficult to see the shape of the actual craft (the lights were the most visible) but it appeared to have an oval, kind of saucer like shape. It made no noise at all, which surprised us, as it was so close. It disappeared behind some trees in a matter of seconds. Afterwards I felt intense excitement and a sense of awe, as well as a tiny bit of fear mixed with a lot of curiosity about whatever entities we were visited by.
