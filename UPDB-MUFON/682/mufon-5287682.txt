i was driving my taxi at that time and in communication with a cb radio to another taxi driver . i thought it was a projection from the ground

i thought it was a projection from the ground pointing up, however i saw no light source and it moved fast and random from left to right up and down. it spun anti-clockwise, then off and on (vanished and reappeared) in the same place. i was driving about 20mph on a quiet straight road and saw it clearly shoot off in the NW direction in approx. 4 sec. heading towards Warrington Cheshire. getting smaller until i could no longer see it.(Approx. 40 miles) the sky was bright with high thin cloud ripples above it (possible a full moon).it had triangular shaped ambient lights underneath (like a lightbox) . i was laughed at when i returned to the office. but i know i saw something i could not explain.
