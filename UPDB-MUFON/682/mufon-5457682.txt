{jpg} Outside teaching when students pointed upwards and said “what that?!”

Students alerted me to a formation of white diamond shaped objects in two separate triangle formations with one flying out on its own. 7 in each triangle for a total of 15 objects total. Formations slowly but very steady and ridged in their formation flew almost directly overhead before slowly banking left towards the East. They arrived from due North. No sound fairly low and clear with many commercial and other jets in view way higher in their usual patterns seen through the day following the mountain pass to our North. We all watched as they flew steady towards the east.
