{td} Fast moving zigzag light.

I was in my yard with my dogs when I noticed a light, which at first I thought was a falling star. Upon watching it I realized it wasn't falling but moving extremely fast. Witnessed a light going extremely fast from West to East, but not a straight line. It zigzaged from South to North before heading back towards the east. It literally took seconds. I have observed planes hundreds of times, this was unlike anything I've ever seen.
