I saw this large object with yellow, blue, green, and red lights going around it. We stood there me, mom and my three sisters watching this big football field size object and asked my mom what do you think they are doing? She said, "Probably what we were, watching us". Then after what seemed like forever, maybe 10 minutes it lifted straight up and then, zip it was gone. I noticed that there were no sounds, no night sounds, chirps from crickets and frogs, it was as if all nature had stopped to see such a strange and beautiful UFO. Slowly the night sounds returned, and we went back into the house, it was my sister's thirteenth birthday, and had cake. The next day mom sent us to see if anyone else had seen it, one person said they did. My dad was not into saying anything, people might think we were crazy. We never talked about it for years. Then I wrote a children's book and want to get it published but who would, everyone will think I'm nuts. Then I heard of you all and thought maybe you could help me get my story out there.
