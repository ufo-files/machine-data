{jpg} Circular burst of light then a bright beam of light

Around midnight in the summer of 1998 myself and a friend were riding our bikes home from another friends house on west 5th st rd heading towards my house in stanley ky. It was a very quite night with no wind or clouds. I remember we were talking and riding at a fast pace bc it was way past time for us to be home. We saw this bright flash of light in the sky straight above us. It was a huge blue hollow circle. The center was perfectly clear. Almost like the circles that appear around the moon but way bigger and a lot brighter. Then a bright whitish blue beam shot out of the middle and then it was over.it never made a sound. Complete silence. The whole thing lasted just seconds. We were about 8 miles from my house and it scared us so bad we found the nearest house and knocked on the door and had then take us home. They thought we were crazy and probably on drugs. I've searched and searched for anything close to this and have come up empty.
