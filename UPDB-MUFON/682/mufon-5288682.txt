Four of us watching smoke from the Fourmile wildfire. The sun was red, though the red shifted in the picture . My wife took four photographs in a period from 6:39 to 6:42. Two photos in the middle had a craft. It looks more like a classic disk than a lenticular cloud. None of us saw it with the naked eye. The craft was invisible as we were watching the sunset. It appeared and disappeared. lenticular clouds aren't invisible to the naked eye. Nor do they come and go in an instant. My wife was viewing her photo file in December and discovered the object from Sept. 6. The other two we were with that night showed disinterest at the concept, despite the fact of the photo. 1
