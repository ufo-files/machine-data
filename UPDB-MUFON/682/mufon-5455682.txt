Was on the sun porch smoking a cigarette, watching you tube videos, when the dog started growling. He never grows at anything. I look behind me to see whats bothering him. And this orange- red or yellow- orange object was hovering slowly drifting. ( thats confusing but it didn’t seem to actually be moving but at the same time was). It was bright enough to illuminate the road somewhat. After it disappeared beyond the tree line, about 10 minutes passed and I could hear the neighborhood dogs getting agitated. Another 5 minutes and Duke was upset again, two tall dark figures were walking into the woods behind the neighbors house, towards where the object went. They definitely were not human their arms were way too long. And the gait of their walk was long ( like they looked floaty with each step). Several more minutes passed and the object left from the woods. I’m not sure if it headed east or ascended. I wanted to take pictures but also didn’t want to be discovered as I was very scared after seeing the beings. Like some kind of supernatural fear paralyzed my body from moving.
