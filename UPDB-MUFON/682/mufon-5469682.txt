{cak} Very fast moving traveling in a straight line parallel to HWY 70E. Low to the ground as we watched it flew behind a small hill and disappeared. My wife and I both saw the object and were stunned we saw such a thing.

My wife and I were traveling from Thatcher Ariona on our way to San Antonio Texas in separate vehicles. We were traveling at 10:30 AM. East on Hwy 70 just a few miles East of Thatcher. The object was flying paralled to Hwy 70 along side our cars as my wife was following me in her car. We were chatting on our car phones when my wife shouted do you see that? I simultaneously saw the object traveling parallel to the road along side us. 

It was cigar shaped, metalic, traveling very fast and low to the ground at about 500 ft. It was completely silent, had no lights and disappeared behind a small hill beside the road. I said to my wife we should see it cross the road in front of usin a second as the road was curving to the right. When we rounded the curb the object had discppeared.

Neither of us had seen anything like this before. This object had no wings, exhaust, sound or anything that looked or flew like an airplane.
