Observed bright glowing orb from window. When I used binoculars I saw bright glowing windows

I was sitting in my lanai at 5 am looking northeast out the window thinking I was looking at the moon. I than realized it was too small and in the wrong direction. I used binoculars to observe it and saw a bank of very large, brightly glowing windows; or it could have been the surface of the craft. It had to be quite low.as my binoculars are not very powerful. It frightened me and I ran to get my husband. When we came back it had gone higher and I was not again able to see any details with the binocular. It remained up high until the sky got light and disappeared.
