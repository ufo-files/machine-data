Half mile wide triangle lands north of Nampa.

As I drove to work at 12:45 am, (I can't remember the exact date but it was sometime in the first half of September 2010), northbound on Midland rd. between Greenhurst and Locust roads, there is a hill on the road that overlooks the city of Nampa, ID. As soon as I reached the top of that hill my eyes were immediately drawn to a large triangle shaped object that had a large glowing -white- light at each corner of the triangle. The lights I immediately noticed as unusual because the whiteness of them contrasted with all the other artificial lights of the city. When I saw the object it's approximate altitude was 500 to 1000 feet steadily descending until it dropped below the ridgeline/treeline at the far end of town. I could still see the glow from behind the trees for a couple of seconds before it went out. From my vantage point it was just west of the Sugar Beet factory which also lies beyond this ridgeline so I am quite confident making an estimation of the size of the object at one-half mile wide.
