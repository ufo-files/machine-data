{nd} Three ufos over clear lake indiana

At 730 in the evening, my roommates and i observed three UFOs in the sky in the same general area of the last two sightings. They were over and near clear lake indiana. The first UFO i believe is the same one i saw the previous times. It was stationary in the same part of the sky where i have saw it before.
The other two UFOs were solid white unblinking objects that were more east of the first and traveled slowly towards the east.My roommate commented that one seemed to blink out when a plane came near and lit up when the planes were gone. They were well below the flight path of the planes we see coming out of the direction of Chicago. One hovered by my view by a tree at 
treetop level near the lake.
The two brighter lights winked out and disappeared but the shimmery red pulsating one stayed there for awhile. I called a friend of mine up to look over the lake.curiously he couldnt see it even though i had it in sight. I kept the object in sight but somehow i lost it on the way of two blocks.
When i got to my friends house we looked at the sky but couldnt see it. But what we saw eventually saw was a bright light coming towards us. It turned to the east and winked out.It was well below the flight path of any plane, and the sky was clear and filled with stars.
It was warm and slightly windy as well.All the objects were silent. The only noise i heard was the traffic and the wind.
