flying object in a dispersed chem trail

on the afternoon in question,I noticed a flying object in a dispersing chem trail. the sky was high and very few clouds if any were seen. i was interested in a series of chem trail grids which are very frequent on long island. This time i saw ,what at first thought was a helicopter. i dismissed it because i saw no blade and that i am not aware of any helicopter that flew at that extreme height. it appeared to navigate left to right never going beyond the the chem trail. i watched it a few minutes on a s/e direction. i saw it for approx. 2 to 3 minutes when a branch from a tree to my left started to obstruct my view. i stood up from a park bench i was sitting on and moved a step or two to my right and was not able to pick it up again. it was like it disappeared. this has been on my mind since and was wondering if there was any other sighting similar to mine. ty for any info ,.
