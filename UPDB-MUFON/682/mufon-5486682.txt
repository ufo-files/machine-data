well me and my friends all decided to go buxton,welty rocks,,the winking man...to just chill out and have a smoke well we pulled up near a large hill then all got out to climb up and look about anyway it was dark n we could see for miles it was great no wind but the view was great ,,all of a suden in the distance a few miles or so away i saw a light like a star shooting straight up 500 to 1000 feet or so then coming straight back down from were it started it did that two to three times and then went up stoped the light just blinked out gone then 30 seconds later a strong wind came straight to us like was mental this so me and all of us got to ingie my mate to drive up that way to see what it was he was realy not wanting to go but we went anyway we were driving for a bout 5 - 10 minutes trying find were it was then we come driving around a hill top with a telegraf pole on the top then we saw it. it was like a diamond white light just sitting their then i said open the door i got out the jeep and went over the road to get a better look i looked up and waved like come here ..all of a suden the light started to move towards us i creped back towards the jeep every one was shouting get in get in and the light split into two lights like a car it came straight forward it was like a car at the front but it was triangle shaped no noise no sound just floated over us like a balloon but was defo not one, i went over us i felt like i could touch it then it went round this hill top out of sight then it was gone it was well strange the time went fast the earie presence was around i remember sheep in a huddle next to a gate close to the road and we all spoke about it then but never said anything to any one only my mum this i swer happened i have had a hard time remembering the date time and where this happened but it did
