{cak} Driving along I 94 West bound we saw a light that appeared to be rotating and hovering it then slowly move north west

Driving along , we saw a light that appeared to be rotating and hovering it then slowly move north west. It was low in the sky and was about a mile away from us. We noticed it because I had pointed out venus to my wife earlier in the night which was much higher in the sky. It appeared to be rotating and this caused its light to pulsate, the light it emitted was white then changed to reddish orange. It was around 500 ft off the ground and began slowing moving North West until it was out of site. This was between 11 pm and 11:30 pm. We were curious about the object and both noticed it wasn't anything we had recognized before. We live near a military base and have seen helicopters on many occasions, this was not a helicopter. My wife states she saw a halo of light around the object.
