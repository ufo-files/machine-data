The disc shaped object about the size of a basketball seemed to zoom cross the sky, and then hovered for approximately 6 minutes.The disc then appeared to descend while mkin a faint buzzing sound that I seemed to be able to feel more than hear. There was a blinding bright light, so bright it made your eyes hurt to look and just like that, it was gone. I fist noticed the object when I was sitting outside, watching the stars as I often do. At first I thought the object was a shooting star, but it began to hover. it was also much brighter and much lower than the other stars. I was at first terribly afraid that they were coming for me again. I relaxed when I saw that the object had stopped and was not very close to me. After the bright light and the objects disappearance, I felt extremely uneasy. I hope no one was taken.
