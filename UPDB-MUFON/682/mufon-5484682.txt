{nd} Bright light, extermely fast, zig zagged in multiple directions then zipped out of sight to the West.

I was in my backyard walking my dog when I saw the object, a bright glowing white light, just above the horizon to the north. It suddenly began to ascend and descend, zig-zagging at an extreme rate of speed. This lasted for about 1.5 minutes. Then it hovered overhead and zipped off to the West at an unbelievable speed and disappeared. I ran inside to get my husband. We returned and waited to see if it would reappear but it did not. I was not scared but very excited.
