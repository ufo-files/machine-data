3 fireballs, lowering the height of approximately 7miles to behind the sea horizon, duration of approximately 6-8 minutes.

This was the route from South Africa to Tenerife.I was standing on the ship's bridge, as 2nd officer, together with cadet.
I thought that it was the light from the plane.Then the three lights in the shape of balls began its descent toward the horizon.The balls are becoming larger, colors: yellow, red, green, blue, orange.I was surprised, I have never in 15 years of my navigation seen anything like it.I used binoculars, and made it clear cadet to watch carefully as further movement of objects, as they approached the horizon if they will change direction or enough to venture beyond the horizon. My opinion is that they dive into the sea.
The light have been far more than 12 NM, the radar was set to 12 nm, so that it was not possible to switch to 24 NM, to try to catch on the horizon.
There was no time, everything happened quickly, in the very end, even though the whole descent took about 6 minutes.
It was a full moon, cloudless, visibility excellent, as never experiencing, as it is daylight and there were two in the morning.I did not have a camera, unfortunately.
