{tjd} Many regularly spaced lights moving through the sky in a straight line for aprox. 2 minutes. Resembled a line of satellites

On 4/25/2020 at approx. 9:45 p.m. I was sitting on my patio after taking a photo of the Moon and Venus setting. Upon returning from putting my camera inside. I sat down and looked to the northwest and saw what I first thought was a satellite but they kept on coming in a perfectly straight single file line toward the southeast for at least two minutes. I went to the window and called for my wife and she came out to see the tail end.
They all looked like points of light like satellites do.
They where spaced evenly with an occasional gap which formed groups something like this:
four- space- two- space- four-space- three space etc... like a pattern.
then every few there was a single one with larger space in front and behind it.
All while moving in a straight single file line until they got to small to see.
Their speed was about the same as a satellite.
At one point they reached all the way across the sky.
I can only estimate how many I saw, I would say more than 50.
I am an amateur astronomer and have seen many satellites, if this was only one object I would not be reporting this but this was 50+.
