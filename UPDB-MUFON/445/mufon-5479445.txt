{jpg} Saw stationary ufo while getting morning paper

While getting the morning paper I saw a flash of red light. I looked up and spotted the ufo At first I thought it might be a helicopter, but I decided it wasn't, so I took four pictures of the craft with my Canon Elph. Strangely, although the craft was disc shaped, all of the pictures seem to edges. After taking the pictures, I returned to the house and didn't see when the ufo left.
