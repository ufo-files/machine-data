Saw a UFO in Berlin

I finished watching a World Cup football match on TV, in an open beer garden call BRLO, near the Gleisdreieck station. As the match finished, we went with 2 friends to the field next to the brewery, and were talking.
At some point I saw something in the sky with the corner of my eye, when I looked in the sky there was a big triangle/teardrop shaped, silver UFO hovering over us. It was just standing there one second, and the next second disappearing into the horizon leaving a bright line behind. The bright line disappeared almost immediately. I didn't even had time to tell my friends, as when i told them, it was all gone.
I was shocked and surprised at first, but then I felt good, because I always wondered if it was real. Now I know it is.
