Someone changed my story about the ufo next to Burger king.. My friend and I were standing at different angles during the duration of the encounter; directly underneath, to the side and about 50 feet away. The ufo was about 25 feet in the air. After throwing pieces of tar at it for about half an hour or so, it slowly went up another 10 feet or so, then it shot up to where the stars were in about a couple of seconds. I was with my co-worker/friend..My mom saw it in the light about a week apart from when I saw it and my sister witnessed the extremely large triangle ufo on I-84 crossing above the highway slowly.
