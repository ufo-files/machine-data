{dr}Witnessed UFO over Lake Norman, NC. It was a mesmerizing and exciting experience.

Witnessed UFO over Lake Norman/ Troutman, NC. First witnessed UFO looking out over the lake from back porch. At first glance thought it was a star or planet but after a few minutes it was something else. It was at a pretty far distance but had the shadow of a very large triangle or other shape. At one point it looked like it separated into different lights but came back together. The whole experience was amazing. Me and another person who also saw it could not believe it and watched it for almost an hour. The lights faded and got brighter a few times. Time seemed to change and it seemed to be sending out a signal that was effecting electricity. Also looked to be moving very fast beyond the UFO. It moved from side to side and up and down and toward the end seemed to just fade out but came back again. I could hear sounds in the woods. I was a little scared, excited, but calm and thankful to have seen it. After 45 min watching it intensely we went back inside because it was getting cold outside and it seemed to be fading out.
