Super bright at first, silent, somewhat stationary, round with median and small dome

A friend of mine showed me some footage that he was able to capture on digital video while working in his back yard. He did not know what to do with it until he brought to my attention about a year later. He said it was bright as a little star in broad daylight which caught his attention and because it remained for so long he went in the house and grabbed his video camera.  He has a very clean capture for more than a few second (hard to find through view finder / sun) but the image when centered and zoomed in is fairly focused is unmistakeable (non conventional aircraft).  We are going to make copies before talking to anyone and need to know who to consult with. I have personally deamed the recording authentic and consider this a major find (daytime - 2004 - So Cal). I plan to interview him myself as well. As compared to historial grainy photos or video, this is an eye opener). This is not a goodyear blimp, auto dealer ballon, or a toy of some sort.
