I walked out of my side door to go to my truck. The sky was dark as the moon was less than half.I first noticed it in the south west sky and thought it was a jet. It was very large (bigger than Venus or Jupiter look like) and moving steadily at a moderate speed. My wife came out with binoculars so I could look for aircraft lights, like the blinking green or red lights common to jets. None. Just a bright object on a steady path. After following it as it moved towards the northeast it got dim and faded out. The trajectory was steady and took about 2 or 3 minutes to go over the sky...a lot faster than a 5 mile high jet would have done the same path. I believe I saw my first UFO. It certainly was not a jet or airplane.
