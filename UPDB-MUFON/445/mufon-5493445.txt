{jpg} Object unknowingly caught in photo

While on a geology field trip, a couple of my students had their picture taken by another student. One was taken with the camera in landscape position, and nothing was in the photo. A second photo taken moments later captured a bell shaped object above the ridge in the background. No one witnessed the object. It was only noticed when the student enlarged the photo after the trip.
