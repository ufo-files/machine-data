I was in my driveway and noticed a lighted object moving southward above a neighboring street.

At approximately 8:00 pm, I was in my driveway near the corner of Lone Star Trail and Briarglen Drive having a cigarette, facing SE, and saw a craft just a couple of blocks away, approximately following Menlow Drive (which is the next street to the east) from north to south, just above the trees on the east side of the street. It looked as big as a 747! I watched in amazement as the object continued south-southwest and crossed Briarglen Drive. As it went over the houses and trees there it began to descent sort of to the southeast towards the lake, and I lost sight of it. It was elongated with 5 to 8 amber-colored lights located horizontally along the midline of the craft.
