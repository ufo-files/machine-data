{cak} Object was sighted moving between clouds at steady speed.

I was treating evergreen trees for bag worms with a truck mounted high pressure sprayer. I was treating about 30 trees in a straight line that ran about north to south. I was facing north, looking up while spraying the treetops. The sky was blue with a partial cloud cover of white puffy clouds. There was an opening between two sections of clouds that was about a foot distance wide at arm’s length. A bright white lens shaped object moved from behind the cloud on the left to behind the cloud on the right against the clear blue sky. It was visible for about 2 to 3 seconds and moved at a steady speed. The outline was sharp and there was no trail or sound. If there was a sound, I would not have heard it over the sound of the high-pressure pump. You could not have mistaken this for a plane or blimp or meteor. If you held a quarter at arm’s length at an angle you would get an idea of the size. I was amazed at seeing this as I am an amateur astronomer and have spent a lot of time looking up in the sky at night and never saw anything like this. This was visible in broad daylight.
