{jpg} Glowing giant cigar-shaped object with activity inside, semi-transparent

I was driving South on I-45, North of Huntsville on my way to Galveston at around 1:30 in the morning of September 20th, 1973. Out of the upper left corner of my peripheral vision. I saw a giant, glowing transluscent object come straight down to halfway between the earth and the moon. I turned my head and looked at it. 

In the one second it was there, I could see activity inside of it. It was a cigar-shaped, inter-dimensional looking object and it stood stationary. They looked at me, and I looked at them. Then from standing still, it instantly shot straight across the sky to the West at the speed of light and it was gone. The entire scenario lasted a little less than 3 seconds. 
I was stone-cold sober and 100% alert when it happened. I was shocked half out of my mind at what I had just seen. Its movement defied the earth's laws of physics. Not only that, but in the instant I looked at it, there were beings inside of it, moving around like they were entering data into computer-like machines. They resembled humans. 

I knew I had just witnessed something that was nearly beyond comprehension, but it was real and I saw it. This was the one time in my life that I had ever witnessed a UFO.
