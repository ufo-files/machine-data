A luminous sphere moving

In the morning, when I get out of bed, before exercising, I observe the sky and the stars. This time it is something bigger than a star, it is a sphere, a light that shines in all directions. I thought it couldn't be an airplane because it didn't have strobe lights and it wasn't a spotlight, but a ball with its own light.
I started recording with my mobile, after a few seconds because it was still and then it advanced slowly. It must have been over the sea, 1.5km (1 mile) away. No noise, continuous movement, but it flickered and changed direction. At first it seemed to float, then it moved and continued at the same speed until it disappeared.
