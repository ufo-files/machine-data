{tjd} While camping Orb/sphere passed almost directly overhead. Flew silent/intercepted my cellular data stopped working. Flew silent, hovered, then left

While camping over CA highway 89 summit (monitor pass 8341 ft-just W of topaz lake) in the Sierra Nevada NV triangle- I was sitting in my car around 10:30pm 7/15/19 listening to the radio & using cell data. Noticed data would not connect in a spot I always have service(& believe me, I tried everything to try & connect between powering cell off to changing servers on Speedtest to cycling airplane mode over 100x to resetting all network settings). Had service earlier, but nothing for the whole rest of the night from that moment just b4 I saw it. Then heard a family of coyotes howling... sounded like maybe 8 in total. Got out of car to pee in brush and I was cycling airplane mode on/off on my cell when I said to myself (very annoyed) “what’s happening?! is ET phoning home or something messing with my phone?” Then I look up to the sky right above Levethane peak & that VERY moment see a sphere/orb maybe 500-1500 feet away from me overhead. I estimate that it was no higher than maybe 9,500-10,000ft. It was maybe 20-25ft wide and completely silent. It was emitting a bright white light evenly around every surface of the sphere/orb craft. The light it emitted looked like the inside of a bright white light bulb- how a lightbulb interacts with the gas sealed within is how the exterior of the craft interacted with the air lighting up the environment. From far it would look exactly like a star. This is the 2nd time I’ve seen this type of craft, last 6/25/18 over Sonora pass (Bridgeport, CA hwy 108 also traveling NE) in the evening as it reflected the sunset-saw it for maybe 1.5 sec. between all the trees obstructing my view. That sphere orb looked to be translucent but also emitting/recflecting light. I’ve been camping several months the past couple years & ive been seeing a lot of activity recently (6 & 7/2019) while camping in this beautiful NV triangle. Late June early July 2019 I’ve seen -on 4 different occasions during 3 moonless light polluted free nights (usually between 10:30pm-3:00am) stars blinking at me & responding to my bright dewalt flashlight the very moment I get out of my car or tent. I never spend more than 5-7min a night looking up for UFO’s they just seem to happen within the moments I look up. Very strange, but defiantly an intelligent interaction, with who? Hopefully it was just some military drone/satellite tech or something, but all the blinks I received looked exactly like stars turning on/off. But back to my sighting last night... I tried snapping a photo with my iPhone but had the flash on, this is how I know the exact time @10:40pm I saw the craft. Hopefully others can corroborate along its path or review security footage during that time/place. My iPhone 6 optical lens could not get a pic. The glowing white sphere orb hovered for a moment b4 silently & fairly slowly (maybe no more than 40mph continuing NE towards the 395N & from my vantage point towards the mountains around Dayton, NV b4 disseapearing over Levathain peak. Lasted approximately 40 seconds mainly bcuz the slope of the mountain took up half of the sky. I can only approximate that the craft was coming from the south possibly SW but don’t know for sure as I only saw it when it was right above me. I wonder if the coyotes knew what was happening or if it interacted with them. & I believe that the sphere orb craft messed with my cellular data connection & I couldn’t reconnect until the next day. Made me mad bcuz I had a bill due that day! I did not fear anything during this encounter. I feel lucky to have seen it & witness it with my own 2 eyes, but next time I hope to have a camera with proper optics. Camping here is a hotspot for ufo activity.
