Bright fixated orb over mesa then turned red.

At approximately 1945 hours, we observed an intensely bright white light fixated in the sky to the west. It did not move or change. The clouds hid it for approximately 10 minutes. When the cloud cover moved the object was still in the same place but was a bright red color. The light did not change. The clouds returned and hid the object for approximately 20 minutes. When we checked the sky again, the object had moved to the southwest and was back to the intense bright white light, but this time the light would dim and return to bright. This continued for approximately 5 minutes before it disappeared.
