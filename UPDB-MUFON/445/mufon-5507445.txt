Sighting near the Legends

It was about 1:50 pm, Sunday September 30th, 2013. My Mother-in-Law and I were driving west on Parallel, in Kansas City, Ks. headed towards the Legends, shopping center. My GPS said in .2 of a mile exit right onto 435 south, which made me look upward towards the highway sign. The sky was blue and cloudless. I saw a glimmering silver narrow rectangular shaped object flying north, or to the right in the sky. It was high up. I said LOOK, to my Mother-in-law. As fast as I could blink twice it turned white. She saw it. Just that quickly a stream of white curled smoke came off the lower right side. Then it completely disappeared, even the smoke. I said, what in the world was that? 1She couldnt figure out what we saw either. The whole event was very fast, probably about a minute!
