{nd} hovering over highway

during thunderstorm saw this object approaching at low altitude over hwy in downtown area,I was driving east and it was coming toward me. It was a very large triangle and the lights on the outside seemed like they changed from green to white. There were no red lights at all on this craft. Then very slowly and silently hovered for a moment over a building near an intersection where I caught a red light. I nearly hit a parked car trying to observe it and cars coming at me seemed like they were veering to look it also. Then it changed direction to move SW and even though it was storming out I tried to listen for sound by rolling down my car window and heard nothing. It moved slowly at first and then like a shot it was gone.
