{rjl} Many Lights traveling in long line

This is second time I've seen this scenario at same time of morning.First time was two or three weeks ago. Didn't report that sighting. Saw same thing this time . Small bright round looking lights traveling at fast rate from the southwest passing directly overhead moving towards the northeast. Most were spaced evenly apart (not all). Even had three at once overhead, with two of them traveling side by side traveling northeast while a third moved in opposite direction and passed in between the other two. Looked like they were all at same altitude and same size(apparently). Sighting lasted approximately 10 minutes. And zero SOUNDS from objects. I WILL be setting up my camera next clear night in open area to catch on film.
