{cak} Mid day high altitude hover over Sioux Falls

I noticed a metallic object at about cloud level on a mostly sunny day. My location was just east of Sioux Falls. As I walked between my shop and shed, I noticed the craft was hovering in one place over NE Sioux Falls. The shape was disc or cigar shaped. I'm not sure how long it had been there before I notice it but I observed it for about 45 to 60 seconds. It flew up and away at very high speed. It only took 1-2 seconds for it to go out of view. 
 I know I should've noted the day and time but failed to do that. It was after Halloween and before Thanksgiving. I have no pictures or video.
