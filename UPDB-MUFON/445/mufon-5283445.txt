Definitely a believer...

I was walking to the store one evening when I happened to look up to see these two glowing orbs moving erratically above me in the clouds. The lights seemed to follow me down the street continuing with the same movements. I came out from the store to the orbs still moving erratically, that's when I began to record them on my way back down the street.
