{jpg} Object entered atmosphere

I work at night and was doing a routine check. On my way back to the building I looked up. That was when I observed a trail as if a meteorite was entering the atmosphere. The trail was bright red with a orange dot leading it. This lasted about 20 seconds. I was facing north and the object was coming towards me but still far away and way up.
After what I presumed was an object entering the atmosphere the red trail disappeared and the orange dot did too right after. A few seconds later a small dot reappered in the same spot where it had disapperead and after moving south towards me for a few seconds it changed direction to the northeast and continued until it disappeared behind the trees.
