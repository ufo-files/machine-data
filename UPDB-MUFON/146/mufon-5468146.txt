I ONLY WANTED TO TAKE PHOTO OF SUN UNTIL I SAW THE OBJECT IN PHOTO AND TOOK ANOTHER 2 SHOTS ONLY

I was waiting for my wife at the roadside and decided to take a photo of the morning sun. It was so bright and hurt my eyes. Then after the first shot I looked at the photo and saw a cigar shaped bright object near the Sun. Took another 2 quick shots and it showed the object getting in front of the Sun ie almost disappeared into the Sun and later when I got home, was surprised to see a huge orb, very bright like Sun in front of the building. If it was the Sun, it should be behind the building.This object must be huge.
