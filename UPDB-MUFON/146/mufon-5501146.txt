{nd} witnessed object flying across the sky during observation on lightning storm.

My best friend and I were atop my shed watching the lighting storm when I looked over and saw this strange object at first I thought it was an airplane then I relized that there was a lighting storm. And i didnt think an airplane would be flying in a storm like this. The object was flying in a strait line across the sky, it was hard to see its shape. My friend and I were amazed at this though we werent fully convinced it was extraterrestrial until I remembered the storm, there was blinding flashes of lighting but no thunder -strange-if you ask me. After watching the object for a couple minutes we went inside to share our story with our parents.
