{cak} Several people observed two orbs high in the sky moving West to East

Several people observed two orbs that resembled stars high in the sky. The obs were apart at the beginning and then started to move closer together. The orbs started to separate and moved apart. One of the orbs moved to the West, then the other orb moved to the East until high clouds moved in the area and suddenly the orbs were gone.
