Red-white-blue flashing lights of light in the sky

In the evening in Zielona G1ra, I looked up at the sky and noticed a strange flashing light. Ten minutes later, more lights appeared. Moments later, the lights of the plane appeared, which flew towards the lights. Five minutes later all the lights disappeared. A few days earlier the F-16 fighter was flying so low over the city for the first time in city history.
