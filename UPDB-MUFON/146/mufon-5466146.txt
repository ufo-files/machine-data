{td} 6 star-like lights, 2 each red, blue, white high in sky, were moving in different direction in unison. Suddenly, fighter jets came from south and they and UFO's disappeared over northern horizon

I am not sure about the date - sometime in mid 1970's. I am looking for anyone else who saw this event. I was outside a rural establishment on a high hill in Jones County, MS. The late night sky was clear. Suddenly I looked up to see six star-like lights/objects moving around the sky together, north of me. There were two each of red, white and blue, round bright lights, but their color was dim. They appeared very high in the sky, higher than you would see a jet stream but close enough to observe their color and movements and see that they were not nearly as high as the stars high above them. As I was watching them, suddenly two fighter jets appeared from the south, I assume from the base from the south, going in the direction of the lights. They flew almost over my head faster than I had ever seen them practice, and I could hear them. In comparison, the light objects made no noise. As I watched, the jet and the objects all disappeared over the northern horizon.
