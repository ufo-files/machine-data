30-35 foot Diameter fireball Crosley closely above the freeway

I was driving home from work approximately 3:30 PM on Highway 24 somewhere around the Orinda exit I notice to the right above me approximately 100 yards in front of me in approximately 100 yards in height A very large fireball coming down at a 45° angle. I was quite shocked because I wanted to slow down because I thought I would actually run into it at the angle it was coming down or it was going to hit the freeway. I had cars in front of me behind me and then the next right two lanes to my right.
I nearly missed passing immediately below it as crossed right in front of my windshield now at about 40 or 50 feet in the air. As it crossed I was expecting a huge explosion grateful that it didn’t hit any cars or the freeway. I heard no explosion. As he passed in front of me I noticed it was bright red like molten lava with a little bit of a trail but very little, outside edges were bright like it was on fire in the center mass of it was deep orange of varying Hughes. I feel very fortunate I avoided any accidents As I never took my eyes off of the object for approximately 5 to 7 seconds. I pulled next to another car to my right to see the reaction but the lady never looked over at my car and I just went home. There was no noise coming from the object whatsoever or heat I felt nothing. One thing I did notice that he sending out a 45° angle I noticed it was traveling slower than I would’ve expected something to be coming out of the sky like that. as I was able to get a really good look at it is it passed right in front of my car proximately 40 feet above me. I check the local news stations for the next few days and heard nothing further.
