crescent with the curved edge downwards

Going downstairs to my room and a bright red crescent light with the curved edge facing downwards caught my eye off in the distance. It looked like a rising moon after a couple minutes it started to dim and go down into a forest about a kilometer away. Roughly 3 hours before this event I saw a bright red light in the distance but had no distinct shape but was a lot brighter and smaller then the second "UFO" I witnessed. Have been seeing a few unexplainable things in the sky the past few months but nothing quite as clear as this.
