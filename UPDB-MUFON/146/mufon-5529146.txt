Bright orange ball showed up south of the 17 and 23 intersection. Appeared suddenly (was cloudy), moved then became stationary. Pulsed for first 2-3 minutes.

We were coming up on 17, heading southbound on 23 when a bright orange ball appeared in the sky south of us. It was cloudy but the object was visible behind the clouds, as it was bright. It appeared to be fairly low in altitude. The object was pulsing, though it wasn't that obvious. Object moved east towards culver for a short distance then became stationary for the next 5 minutes or so. Disappeared suddenly. Very bright and was definitely not a normal aircraft. At first I thought it was a large meteorite....but it was devoid of a tail and wasn't moving fast enough. The timescale and ability to remain stationary ended that idea. We were all calm but curious. Was the first object I've ever seen
