Two large disc shape objects. Moving S. Crossed over each other banked left and plinked out.

Was observing in back yard with friend when my friend said look up. Two large reddish disc's about have half the size of full moon moving fast towards the south crossed over each other twice and made large turn to left and plinked out. Sky was clear with no clouds. I have been an a amature astronomer for almost 50 years. This was not astronomical. Can sketch it if needed.
