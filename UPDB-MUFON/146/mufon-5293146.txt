Multiple black and white orbs after small air plane

Point and shoot. If you film any aircraft and slow down the movie frame by frame chances are you will see these black and white orbs. They move in incredible speed and cant be seen unless you search for them frame by frame. Like that you can see flight path and proximate speed and size.
