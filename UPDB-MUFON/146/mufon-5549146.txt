finding of 2 extraterrestrial bodies at river mouth in Panama

Somtime in 1976 2 bodies of extraterrestrials were found at the matahaogado rivermouth in the town of San Carlos in Panama.Allthough not present nor in anyway involved I read about it in the local newspaper "Critica".I have spoken to several older and very serious persons who not only saw but touched these beings. Both were very thin,with almond shaped eyes,of greyish-green color and with skin that felt very cold and smooth. They stated U.S. military troops arrived with men dreessed in white and the bodies were removed by these troops.The newspaper article I read was the only one published and the people of the town are very reluctant to discus this topic.
 On the previous night a group of local surfers were camping on Palmar beach in San Carlos adjacent to the above mentioned river.That night they saw a bright white light in the sky directly above them.It emitted two bright green beams in the direction of the rivermouth and than disapeared.
The following day the above mentioned newspaper item appeared.
 All the above I repeat as it was told to me.
