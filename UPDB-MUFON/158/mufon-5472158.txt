white glowing orange orb in straight path came SW above trees and disappeared NE above trees airplane like aircraft followed shortly after it the whole sky lit up with flash of white light then aircraft too disappeared above trees in horizon

Fawn township is a rural town next to Natrona Heights, pa and about 25 miles Northeast of Pittsburgh. One night myself and 4 other people were having a bonfire with last til about midnight and we decided to go inside around this time. We got everything together and 3 friends walked along up the hill infront of me and my best friend. They were talking to each other and paying attention to walking towards the house not looking at the nightsky. My best friend and I noticed a large white orb, glowing orange coming from the sky just above the trees. We stopped dead in our tracks to watch it without saying a word to each other. We watched it fly in a straight path NE and watch it disappear about the trees along the horizon. Next came a pretty low flying aircraft, it wasn't a helicopter, looked similar to a military fighter plane but smaller in size. This too, had no sound. It continued in the same path as the orb, when it got to the NE point above the trees in the horizon, the whole sky flashed white, and the aircraft also disappeared. I looked at my best friend and asked her if she just saw what I saw and she said yes, holy s**t, what was that? No one else that was with us saw it, and everyone told us we we were crazy.
