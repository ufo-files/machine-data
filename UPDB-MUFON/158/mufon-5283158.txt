Witnessed Lights Over Ocean

I was sitting on the beach (Myrtle Beach) when I looked up and noticed lights. One of them was a red light that moved a short distance completely sideways and extremely fast, faster than any jet I’ve ever seen. Not like a shooting star, but completely sideways, parallel with the ground. Then a few minutes later, a larger white light was more toward the south, then it turned into 4 lights, then back into one. Then the lights became somewhat of a “square” formation.
