Saw 3 lights traveling faster thans jets in sky

Witness did not want any of his information written up due to his job in the community, witness contacted State Chief Investigator after his shift and explained what he saw, gave me permission to tell what he saw but again no private information is to be used.

Witness states during his shift as a law enforcement officer, he exited his cruiser at 5:15 am on September 10, 2011. As he got out he looked up about 80 degrees he saw the first light, he noticed a jet aircraft in the area heading towards Rochester International Airport, the object was traveling faster than the jetliner, when the object got at 90 degree (directly overhead) the object disappeared, he states this could be due to the lights in the area and anything past the 90 degree would be affected by the night lights. about 30 seconds later another light appeared at about 75 degree, flew directly overhead and continued in a S/E Direction, the light was much brighter than the first, as this light flew overhead, a 3 light came into view, from the South heading in a northerly direction, they looked like they might collide but didn1t, there were 3 very bright stars out and the objects flew between these 3 stars.

Report written by Chief Field Investigator Stephen Cox.
