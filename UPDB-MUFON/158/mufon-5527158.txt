Cigar-shape ufo in Marfa, TX

Both my wife and I saw the ufo as we were driving to see my wife's family in Chihuahua, Mexico. As we approached Marfa, we saw it hovering for over 15 minutes in broad daylight. We stopped in Marfa and took 2 pics. In one you can see the ufo (once you zoom in) the other you can't tell anything. Once in Marfa we turned toward Presidio and we couldn't see it anymore because it was now behind us.
