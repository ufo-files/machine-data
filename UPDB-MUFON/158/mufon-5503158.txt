{kt}amber colored round object was observed moving from the NW towards the SSE and disappeared behind trees

My Dad and I were sitting on the patio on the south side of our home when we observed an amber colored round object that appeared to our NW (emerging from the west side of our house). Object moved slowly (approx. 30 mph) from our right front to our left front (towards the SE),dis-appearing behind the trees to the south. Object made no sound. We observed two white strobe lights at high altitude in the same area. Those strobe lights were from aircraft that commonly fly over this area. The object passed below both of the strobe lights. We could see the object below and in front of the higher flying aircraft. The altitude of the aircraft was between 1000 and 3000 feet. The object was approx. "pea-sized".
