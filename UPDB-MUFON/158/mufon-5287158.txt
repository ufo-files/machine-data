Flying object sped by airplane

While driving into the city of Chicago on I94 eastbound (Tower Road exit), I noticed a large drone looking object that was red and white flying from west to east. It was the same altitude as a plane that recently took off from O'Hare but this object was flying at least four times faster, in a straight line, for 30 seconds. It caught up and passed the plane and started to descend before going below the tree line and out of sight.
