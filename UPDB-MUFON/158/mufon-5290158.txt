My wife and I were driving WB on I94 from Wisconsin when I noticed an EXTREMELY bright round shaped light being closely followed by what seemed to be an aircraft. What seemed to be bizarre was that as both crafts slowly changed direction, only the aircraft stopped reflecting the light from the sun. The craft in the front that seemed to be "chased" by the aircraft behind never stopped emanating the extremely bright light. I'm a very intelligent person who can always seem to decipher what I'm really looking at and I'm convinced that there was an actual UFO being chased or escorted by a military aircraft.
