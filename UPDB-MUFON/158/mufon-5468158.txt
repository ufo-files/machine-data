{jpg} Pulsating light in what appeared to be a saucer like shape

Initially thought lights were dim stars or star covered with clouds. Realized lights where too close to ground. Lights pulsated and appeared to be on the outside of a saucer like flying object. Radio cut out as I was driving in the direction of object and got closer. Made me fearful thankfully I had to turn to get home which took me away from where the object was hovering. I turned and did not look back.
