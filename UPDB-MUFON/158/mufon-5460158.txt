{cak} I know what I saw wasn't a plane or a helicopter

I was taking my dog out to do her business and it was a cold crisp night where you could see a billion stars in the sky. I say what I thought was a plane or a helicopter at first as it had a red light blinking. I immediately realized the light would move to the right a little and disappear, the reappear to the left a little. This was repeating so I figured the light was circling the object. It stayed put for about 5 mins and then it moved to the left very fast a few inches in the sky from my perspective and then shot straight up in the stars and disappeared faster than I had ever seen anything move in the sky. I kept it to myself for a couple of nights because even though I was only 11 or 12 years old I knew it was something. So a couple of days later I told my dad, instead of the reaction I was expecting, he just stared at me and then looked at my step mother, he said that he had seen the exact same thing a couple of nights before I did. If you look at where St. John's NL is on a map, we are the most easterly point of North America. So it would have been a point of the planet where the first city lights of the continent would be. I'm 44 years old and I can still see this in my mind like it was yesterday. It wasn't a plane or a helicopter, it moved too abruptly and fast.
