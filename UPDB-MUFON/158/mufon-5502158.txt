I was sitting in the back yard talking on the phone looking into the sky towards the south when I saw an orange light ascend I thought at first it was a firework but it stopped and started to pulsate strangely. It then started to move slowly across the sky to the West until I could no longer see it glowing. I ran into the house and told my parents. When I went back out it was back in the same spot and moved around slowly from left to right, I called to my parents and they came out and watched it move around in the sky. Then it started moving towards the west again over the Everglades.
