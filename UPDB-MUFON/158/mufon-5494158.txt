I was outside playing with my son on the back deck. The sky was clear. I saw a jet fly by. It left behind a jet stream that appeared very close. With the sky the way it was and the jet stream looking so cool I decided to snap a picture. I noticed in the picture there was a white dot. my first thought was that it was a reflection from the sun. I took a slow motion video immediately after and noticed the object was moving. It moved like a fly would but looked nothing like an insect. It came out from the tree line and returned to the same spot. I also noticed that in the video the ball seems to be in front of the trees which are only a few hundred feet away. I decided to take more pictures and captured the object a few more times in the sky. Also noticing another jet further in the distance. There were also some pictures that the object did not appear in. After about 5-10 minutes the two jets and the object were gone. I have taken pictures from the same location two days in a row and no object appeared, however the sky was much more clear the day i got it. Also I could not see the object with my naked eye.
