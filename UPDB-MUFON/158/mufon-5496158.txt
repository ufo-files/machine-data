{nd} very bright glowing orb of red and orange that looked like on fire but yet shimmered like water does

My family and I were across the street at the golf course and had just finished watching the annual fireworks show. We were talking and walking out onto the street when we noticed the orb coming towards us. We only live across the street from the golf course so our walk home was going to be short. The orb was coming across the sky towards us when we noticed it. I took out my flip and videotaped it as it came almost over us and just past us. The orb was a very bright red/orange color that looked like it was on fire yet it shimmered and rippled as water does. There was no sound from the object as it went over either. As I was taping there was a man and his children standing not 5 ft from me yet they and no one else around us(my husband and I)would look up! So at the end of the taping you hear me on the phone to my sister and her granddaughter who are already across the street in front of the house. My sister was just across the street from where we were so she and Bella were watching the orb too. I was sooo excited to have seen it again but just when I thought it was gone there was another sighting just a half an hour later...but there were four more! I was in shock because I never thought we would see the orbs again. In total there was four separate sightings. The first was in March and we were maybe 50 ft from three orbs sitting in the sky forming an isosceles triangle above some houses. The second was this time that I just wrote about on the 4th of July. The third happened that same night on the 4th of July and the fourth sighting was on New Years Eve which there were three orbs. I am excited yet angry at the same time. I have had many experiences before but never like this. It is all I can think about and I am very conflicted about it.
