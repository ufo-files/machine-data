{jpg} saw a big ball of white light in the air just sitting there, then it flashed a beam of light on the ground

We were at Timber Ridge School in Cross Junction Virginia at the big lake with some of the students there. One of the kids called me and told me to look. I looked up in the sky and saw a white light shaped like a circle. It was just sitting in the sky and no sound. Then it shot a beam of light to the ground for a good period of time, I don't know how long. Then it just disappeared. The kids told everybody about it and my supervisor asked me what I saw and I just said I don't know. My reason for this was because when I got hired part of the paperwork I had to complete they asked questions about seeing ufo's. So I didn't want to get fired or get involved in anything. There were approximately ten kids out there and myself and another staff member.
