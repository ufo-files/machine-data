object leaving earth

I was driving to work. Upon turning onto Route 63 from US20, I observed a white beam of light, similar to the light emanating from an airplane. as I continued to watch the light, I noticed the light wasn't moving towards me, nor seemed to be coming from an airplane. The light turned from white to a white/blue and started to take a more cone shape. The shape of the trail then changed and appeared to have formed wings with a cone in the middle and became brighter in color. Just prior to the light dimming and fading out completely, I observed what appeared to be a jagged end to the light and a small white dot object moving away from the light, similar to what a satellite would look like in the night sky. I believe this to have been some type of object leaving earth's atmosphere.
