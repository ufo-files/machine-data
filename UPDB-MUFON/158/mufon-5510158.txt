{dr}UFO spotted over park

We were taking a bike ride through beaver creek, and stopped to observe the sky. Looking at the stars before leaving I spotted what looked like a star, but it was moving. It was moving in a straight path then would drop and change direction. The closer it got to us overhead it began glowing brighter. It looked like a moving star but when looking at it had a glow that made 3 beams of light look projected off the front of it. It also looked like a saturn shape. It did not change color just glowed white and bright like a star. After stopping overhead of us it continued on its way then sped up super quick and dimmed out to a star type of light-then completely vanished.
