{jpg} CIRCLE ZIGZAGGING IN ONE LARGE AREA IN THE SKY FOR HOURS

AROUND 4:00 A.M. WITH A COMPLETELY BLACK SKY WAS A BRIGHT CIRCLE THAT WAS ZIGZAGGING IN ONE AREA IN THE SKY. I LOOKED THROUGH BINOCULARS TO SEE IF IT WAS AN AIRPLANE OR WHAT. IT WAS SIMPLY A ROUND CIRCLE AND THROUGH THE BINOCULARS THERE SEEMED TO BE A TRAIL OF LIGHT AS IT SEEMED TO ZOOM DOWNWARD, THEN SIDEWAYS, THEN UPWARD AND AROUND. I WAS PUZZLED BY WHAT IT COULD POSSIBLY BE. I TRIED TO GO TO SLEEP BUT KEPT WONDERING WHAT IT WAS AND TRIED LOOKING AT IT AGAIN AND IT WAS STILL THERE. I COULDN'T SLEEP, KEPT LOOKING AT IT THRU THE BINOCULARS AND IT CONTINUED THIS IRREGULAR PATH, UP AND DOWN AND SIDEWAYS WITH NO CONSISTENT PATTERN. I BEGAN VIDEO'ING IT WITH MY CELL PHONE. I FINALLY WENT TO SLEEP AFTER A COUPLE OF HOURS AND WHEN THE SUN JUST BEGAN COMING UP AT AROUND 6:00 A.M. IT WAS GONE.
