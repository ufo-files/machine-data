(GS) Large Orb moving west and rising

My boyfriend and I were sitting on the back patio and I was taking pictures when I noticed a very large (aspirin-sized) bright object in the sky. I quickly took a picture of it and ran to get my camcorder.
We watched the object for 5-6 minutes as it ascended westward until it just vanished.
I also looked at the object through my binoculars. It was aqua colored,(perhaps because it was reflecting the sky which was aqua colored that eve?), it was glowing..no change in brilliance... no pulsating..., it had a darker center area. It didn't appear to be perfectly spherical but more diamond-ish in shape.
I have the film and the still photo.
