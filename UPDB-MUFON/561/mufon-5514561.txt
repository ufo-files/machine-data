{dr}Appeared from clouds then went back into them after hovering.

I was driving up to a stop sign at werkner rd. and north territorial rd. outside of chelsea michigan. Ahead I saw this large white sphere dip below the clouds then change direction while hovering then move back up into the clouds. Event lasted seconds as I came to a stop and watched. I thought at first it was apart of the cloud but it was bright white then moved upward leaving a dark shadow beneath it and I figured it may have been a drone. On a side note I looked out for drivers as I passed and saw a long caravan of trucks with black cloth covers and what looked to be mixed up barcodes all over them go past. And before them oddly enough I saw to be a person with sunglasses and maybe a wig with a alien shaped head and little mouth driving a new subaru suv. Ive never seen someones head to be shaped like this or it was an old lady with smooth pale skin. I still don't believe it but that is what I saw after all.
