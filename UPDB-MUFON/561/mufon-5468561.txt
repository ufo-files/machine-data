{cak} a bright white , egg shape that left a bright white tail , was moving at a high rate of speed

me and a few of my friends where up the north Umpqua river, roasting hotdogs over a fire, on the bank of the river. at about 12 midnight, a bright egg shaped object moved from horizon to horizon, in a westerly direction at a high rate of speed. it left a vivid white tail, about one quarter of the way across the sky.the speed and light was similar to a tracer round, but a lot brighter, and larger. approximately 3 or 4 minutes later the object passed over in a north, south direction, from horizon to horizon. one other person in the group observed the object.
