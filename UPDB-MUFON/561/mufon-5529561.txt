round sphere like object flying low over house in Hampton Georgia

On Saturday morning at about 6 am I was on my way to work, as I stepped out of my front door I heard a loud noise coming towards my direction, I looked up and down the street then I looked up but did not see anything, the noise got louder as if it was getting closer and I looked up again, to my surprise something was flying over my neighbor's house towards me,it was round tilted to one side and had lots of light, it was flying so low I could see the color which was like beige. I was so terrified because it was the weirdest thing I had ever seen. I ran back into my house and got my son, then I felt even more weird because when he came out all the lights were gone all he could see were three flickering lights in the distance and he could here the sound dying away, it was as if all the lights were now turned off.
