(GS) Driving to Bowling alley when overhad UFO hovered and cntacted ein car telepthically

While driving o a bowling alley I noted a UFO overhead. The car stopped and there was silence. As I attempted to look out the window a bright yellow/green light shone down forcing me to withdraw my attempts t look up. I could see an irregular edge which appeared to show the craft to be about the size of a city block as is blocked out the stars. I felt a sense that someone/something was contacting me telepathically. It was a conversation asking me if I wanted to go with "them". I responded no, that I was a poor choice as I had several medical issues and family psychological issues. This appeared to end the connection and just as suddenly as it appeared it disappeared.

I was going to arrive late to the bowling league so I was concerned that my team mates would require an explanation instead I arrived early by about 20 minutes. Somehow I had gained about 45 minutes.

I have no physical marks or indications of physical contact. I don't have any "lost time" from this incident.

One of my co-workers at the hospital remarked that he had seen in and was disturbed that I was uncomfortable talking about it when I explained that I didn't want him to think I was trying to "steal his thunder".

I have talked about some aspects of this with a couple of people but never told all of it.
