{jpg} Silver Diisc Dissappear over March AFP, two Fighters sortied in same direction.

I was driving south on CA HW 60. A flash of light caught my eye in the sky, which appeared to be an elongated silver object. At first glance I thought it was possibly an American Airlines 767, which were shiny silver with stripes down the side, but then I noticed there were no stripes or distinguishable objects protruding from the craft, e.g. tail fin, jet engines, etc. The object was flying south towards March Air Reserve Base at a steady altitude, and as soon as it was about to be over the base, it disappeared. I continued south onto CA HW 215 and as I drove past March Air Reserve Base about 5 to 10 minutes later I saw two F-15s take off and shoot off in the direction the craft appeared to be going before it vanished.
