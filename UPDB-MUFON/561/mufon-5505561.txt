At approximately 9:30 pm eastern standard time on January 6, 2013, I was at my uncles house when I noticed 2 red lights off in the distance. As it gets closer to us I realize that the 2 lights belonged to 1 craft based on how close the lights were to each other. The craft was heading towards a bright white object to the northeast of where i was. The craft that i was able to observe had 2 red synchronized lights, appeared to be under intelligent control, and was the size of at least 2 or 3 commercial airline planes in length. I couldnt determine the width of the object. The craft made no sound and it was a cigar or oblong shape. The sighting only lasted a few minutes. After the sighting, i saw what i assumed was a military aircraft flying in the same direction as the craft i saw.
