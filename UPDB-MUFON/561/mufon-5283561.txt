3 Comet-like Lights Zig-Zagging

I first noticed what I thought was a shooting star with a fairly long trail, moving straight in a line... Shortly after, the tail appeared to shorten up and it started diving and swerving back and forth. Eventually it faded after 30-45 seconds and I noticed what looked like at least another two that appeared and then disappeared, much lower to the horizon than the first.
