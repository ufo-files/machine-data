{dr}Through clouds

I was floating in the lake with a lifejacket. At one point I floated on my back and looked in the sky. I saw what I thought was a grey plane going from east to west at cloud level. I kept looking for the wings but never found any. It was a circular dome moving at a steady pace. The craft passed through the next cloud set and never came out the other side. I never saw it again.
