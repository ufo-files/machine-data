{rjl} Solid lights descending in the sky

I woke up to use the bathroom. I walked back into my room and looked out of the window like I always do. When I looked out of the window, movement caught my eye up in the sky. First, I thought it might be an airplane but then noticed it wasn’t pulsating. The bright white light kept moving and then started to descend in a diagonal motion from left to right. Then I noticed another light that looked like an airplane flying streight and was pulsating in the sky. As the plane moved past I noticed movement once again in approximately the same location as the first light, it was another solid white shinny light that started to descent once again from left to right. This one was followed by 5 more all doing the same thing one right after the next. It was like they were following each other going in for a landing but they looking to be extremely high in the sky. I could not see where they went or if they landed as buildings etc. were obstructing my view.
