{rjl} Looking straight up saw a black slow moving right angle going thru thin cloud back listed by stars.

Going out to retrieve the trash cans I always take a quick look for shooting starts. And there it was, a perfect right angle. Just like a carpenter's square. Black,no lights or sound. The clouds were very thin and light in color. I'm guessing about 500 ft.up. It Did not have any sort of tail fin or any fuselage just a perfect right angle cruising very slowly about 10 knots due north.
 I've seen Stealth Fighters and Bombers flying at air shows,and this was surely different.
And if it wasn't for the thin clouds being back lite from the stars I don't think that it would be possible to be seen. Because as it came out of a cloud it would disappear,then reappear as it went into the next.
