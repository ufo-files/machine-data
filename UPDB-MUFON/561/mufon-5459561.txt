{jpg} Crafts lights turned off and it then shot straight up

On this night me and my wife were in the driveway when I noticed a bright light heading toward us from the north east . Living in a populated area I was sure it was a search light on the front of a police helicopter that we witness unfortunately every night . As the light approached us I was expecting to be able to get a idea of the area in which they were searching due to the moving that type of light does when being operated . That’s when I realized something was strange about this craft . At its closest point to my house which was within 1500 ft and only 500 to a 1000 ft in the air I realized there was no engine noise . There were no red or green navigation lights blinking in fact the bright search light wasn’t a search light . The light that was seen at the front of the craft was as bright from the side and back of it as it passed , never dimming or flashing just constant and encasing the object . I watched the object heading south running parallel to apopka Vineland road for at least 1.5 to 2 miles when the bright white light shut off revealing a orange ball that slowly shot up in the air and disappeared . I’ve lived in the area for years and know the flight paths for the airports I also have seen plenty of helicopters along with attending the occasional air show and have never see anything like this .
