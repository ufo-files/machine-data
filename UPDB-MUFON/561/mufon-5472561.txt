At 5:43 am on 11/7/2017 I was taking my dogs into my backyard for morning break and I noticed a "star" move (southerly) abruptly for a short distance approx. 4-5 miles. This caught my attention and as I watched the object it changed direction (90 degree turn) and began to move easterly over my home at a high rate of speed transitioning over the entire horizon. I attempted to calculate the speed and I believe it was approx. 1250 mph. It took it only 35 seconds to make the journey before it was out of eye sight. The object was at a very high altitude giving it the appearance of a "star" like shine. It left no contrail and no sound. The purposeful movement and speed helped me rule out conventional reasons i.e. satellite, aircraft, space station etc. I felt this was significant enough event to report it but wish I had more detailed information to relay. Thank you for your time.
