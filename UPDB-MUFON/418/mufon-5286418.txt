Small disc shaped object over residential area

We were in the backyard when suddenly we both noticed this dark disc shaped object flying overhead. Really wobbly, almost over our yard you could tell it was a round shape from underneath. This was the closest it got, maybe about a 30-35 degree angle. Really wobbly when flying, unnatural. After the very first minute passed, a small airplane flys by. The object was way higher than a small airplane flying low. It kept flying higher and in a northern direction. Just coasting along not moving fast at all.
Afterwards we waited to see if it would return but it didn’t.
