{jpg} This was filmed in the Wichita Mountains Wildlife Refuge near Medicine Park Oklahoma !

I was sight seeing out in the Wichita Mountains Wildlife Refuge to spend time out there and take pictures and videos. I stayed out there until it got dark. When I was leaving the Wildlife Refuge. I noticed this bright light up in the sky near Mt. Scott and Lake Lawtonka. I could tell it was not a star, because it shone to bright. I parked in a parking-lot that is right by Mt. Scott and took my camera out, ( I used a Canon Powershot SX HS 40, with a 35X optical zoom. ) I zoomed in on the bright light and this is what I captured on camera. As you also can see stars in the video. I took this video on Nov. 10th, 2014. I left because it is completely dark out there. And being out there alone, it just gives you a creepy feeling. Plus the Wildlife Refuge HQ does not want people hanging out there after certain hours. Unless you have a camping or fishing permit being out there after the hours.
