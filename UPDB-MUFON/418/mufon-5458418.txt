{rjl} Star that slowly moved across the sky

Witnessed a small star like object traveling across the sky from east to west, in a straight line, to fast up to be in our atmosphere, no blinking lights or obvious shape. Looked like a moving star just flying across the sky with a background of stars. It moved slowly and took about 4 minutes to travel from one section of sky to the complete opposite then it was gone, no trace of it whatsoever where it would have seemingly been continuous, just like someone turned off the light! Very strange cause at first i thought i was looking at the ISS
