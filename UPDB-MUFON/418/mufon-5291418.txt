Sighting of aproximately 12 ufos

At approximately 8:45 p.m., I stepped out onto the front porch and saw a big yellowish orange light in the sky in front of me. I then saw another light come behind that. I had to read that there might be a collision of space debris and thought maybe that's what I was seeing, but then I saw a third light come and fourth. I then called into the house to other family members to come outside to see the lights. When my nephew came out he pulled out his phone and recorded a few of the lights. On the recording these do not look very big or show the color that we were seeing. They were about the size of an aspirin in sky. II observed approximately 12 objects fly across the sky in the north-west direction. One of the objects descended straight down toward earth. I could not see where it landed, only the approximate area it decended to. I don't know if there were any other lights in the sky before I came out onto the porch.
