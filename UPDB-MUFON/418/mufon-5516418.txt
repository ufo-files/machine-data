{cak} Observed light traveling from the east, looked like satelite / star heading in a south east direction. Took nearly an hour for it to travel toward us.

Sitting outside in hot tub watching the sun set waiting for stars to come out. I was facing east looking at Majic Mt. The stars were just beginning to appear when I noticed a bright one directly over Majic. It appeared to be a star but it was ever so slowly moving directly towards me. I called to my husband to get his opinion if it were moving or not. We watched this star like object move towards us (taking close to an hour) before it was directly above. No noise, just a solid white light and it seemed to be as high as the stars. I was judging its movement based on other stars and the direction was unlike any satelite a/o the ISS. This star like object stopped moving and seemed stationary for at least 15 minutes then the clouds rolled in but before the sky was completely cloud covered the star made a ninety degree turn to the north. We are unsure of what we saw but it was pretty interesting. I have been seeing odd things since a child, and I keep telling people if you look you will see something...eventually. Note: we were not freightened as it was a very queer sighting. In the past I have witnessed a boomerang object in the daylight, I have seen white lighting like flashes in the early morning, stars getting brighter then dim out completely, and this one sighting was the first of its kind for me. Just thought Id throw it out there to see if anyone else witnessed this phenomenon.
