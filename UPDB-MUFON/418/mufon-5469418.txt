{td} Very fluid object with light keeping a steady pace zoomed above the valley near my house

As I was laying on my couch downstairs, facing the windows that overlook the hills, I see an orange light zip across exactly 01 N away from my house. It was maybe 150ft above and it was uncharacteristically fast. I am quite the skeptic and am not one to believe stuff I see on the internet, but it's different when you have an experience with these flying objects. I ruled out that it was a plane because it was so low, I should have heard it right? No sound. I also thought it could be the overhead room playing a trick with the window, but I moved around in every possible position trying to see this "reflection" again but it wasn't happening. It was also very fast and traveled in a straight line, no wavering to be seen. I have never seen such a fast, smooth, and silent aircraft, it simply couldn't have been that. I didn't see / can't recall the exact shape of the object, mainly because it is late at night here. I only saw the light that looked like a flat disk but with a concentration in the center. Keep in mind this event lasted 2 or less seconds, as it entered my field of view to disappearing oner the mountains. Just now a plane flew by, heading from E to W quite a ways away and I can hear its typical whir as it passes. It also has its flashing white and red lights to signify it as a plane. The object I saw didn't have those lights, and didn't make a sound. I am pretty spooked to tell you the truth.
