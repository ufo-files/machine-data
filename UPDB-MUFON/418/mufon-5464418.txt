{cak} Three lights blinking in succession, wobbling and hovering, same as a recent sighting in England

I was leaving the grocery store in Kennewick, which may have been the biggest and most wide-open area in Tri-Cities. I saw to the South three lights blinking one to the next, I assume the fourth light was only visible from the other side. I couldn't see the ship itself, only the supposed shape of a wobbling disc. It hovered less than 500 feet for a few minutes, moving extremely slowly. They may have wanted to be seen. Then slowly they inched to the Southeast and eventually out of my sight. I saw a video clip very recently of a UFO sighting in England and it was the exact same story. The same explanation, the same details. I saw the same thing. So I decided to reach out and report it.
