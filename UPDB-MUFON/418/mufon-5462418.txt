Swarms of orbs and other objects.

Swarms of orbs, sometimes freely roaming and other times they appear as if blasted from a powerful source. They're always around, they also follow one around. There are also cloaked triangular objects, and funny looking clouds. Some of the objects emit light.

The attached videos are unedited recordings from an infrared camera pointed at the sky.
