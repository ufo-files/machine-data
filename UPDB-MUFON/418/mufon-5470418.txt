Coming home from a restaurant I looked I saw a hazy point of light in the night sky to the west. It did not appear to move at first but then it became clear that it was moving. Slowly. It got a bit brighter when it hit the southernmost point it got really bright. It looked like a missile test but it traveled across the sky really slowly. The conical trail it made vanished but the point of light that it the cone originated from kept going for a bit and faded out. I'm not really sure how long it was up there before I first spotted it.
