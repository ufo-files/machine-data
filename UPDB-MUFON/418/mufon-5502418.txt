{rjl} Driving east on the !-10 freeway. mid summer 2011.

I was driving home with my granddaughter mid-summer, mid-day, on the I-10 going east. I notice her looking out the top of the front windshield and she said, "what's that?" I tried to look out the front windshield but it was too dangerous because of the traffic, so I opened the driver side window and looked up, I didn't expect to see huge craft hovering just above my car, it was shaped like a triangle, the color was a gun metal black, it had a strange mat finish. There was something about the color and the finish of the craft that was like nothing I ever saw before. Rather than reflect light it seemed like it absorbed it. It didn't make a sound. There were these things that looked like rocket boosters on the under side, I know there were three and I think there were five. 

I was very confused, because it seemed as though no one else noticed or was able to see it except us. The freeway was busy this time of the day too. It was hovering so low I remember thinking it was impossible for no one else to see it. It couldn't have been even 200ft above the car. I don't remember more details very sharply because I was stunned, confused, torn between wanting to look, and trying not to because of the I-10 traffic...it just wasn't safe to not focus on driving. I was glancing at the cars around me, the craft, the road...We still talk about it, my granddaughters 12 now and says she still remembers.
