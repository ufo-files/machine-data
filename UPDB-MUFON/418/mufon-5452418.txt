Orange orbs hovering for over 5 minutes, then slowly moving, then rapidly moving

On July 4th 2019 at approximately 8:50 pm I witnessed 5 to 6 orange orbs hovering starting from behind Tussey Mountain in the Rothrock State Forest. I was looking south east from my apartment towards Musser Gap and the Centre county Hunington county line. I had walked outside to start my grill when I noticed them. I first took them for fireworks or flares, but it was just before twilight started and still bright outside. They stayed motion less for about 5 minutes while I prepared my grill. While I was preparing my grill I only periodically looked up to observe them. While I let my grill heat approximately another 5 minutes I watched them until the lead orb started to move north west. It started very slow and accelerated slowly and uniformly until it was out of my field of vision. By the time I lost the first orb, the second orb started to move in the same way only a little faster. At this time I made the decision to go inside and grab a pair of binoculars. By the time I came back outside, approximately 30 seconds, the rest of the orbs had vanished.
