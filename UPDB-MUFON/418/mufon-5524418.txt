MY WIFE WAS FLYING FROM JACKSONVILLE,FL. TO PHILADELPHIA,PA. THE FIGHT LEFTFL. SHORTLY AFTER SUNRISE ON THE 17TH OF JUNE, SHE NOTICED THE MOON WAS VISIBLE AND STARTED TAKING PICTURES. WHILE SHE WAS LOOKING OUT THE WINDOW OF THE PLANE SHE NOTICED A BRIGHT, SHINEY, OBJECT TO THE RIGHT AND BELOW THE MOON. SHE TOOK SEVERAL PICTURES OF THIS OBJECT BEFORE IT STREAKED OUT OF SIGHT. SHE SAID IT MOVED SO FASDT SHE DID NOT REALLY SEE IT AS A FLIGHT PATH. IT SIMPLY STREAKED OUT OF VIEW. TAKE A LOOK AT THE PICTURES.
