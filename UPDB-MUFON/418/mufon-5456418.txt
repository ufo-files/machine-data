{rjl} Multiple ships and mothership

At about 6:45pm I pulled onto the bush hwy turn off from hwy 87 pulling into the left side pull out. We were sitting there for a minute or so when I looked up to my left and saw a red light shoot across the sky and then disappear. I jumped out of the car and hollered omg asking my boyfriend if he saw that. He's clueless so he didn't. I grabbed my phone and started snapping pics and got the video you see below. I had to get him home so I only spent about 10 minutes on that site but I watched that mothership out of my drivers side window until we got up into the mountain range a couple miles north of there and I lost sight behind the mountain.
In the long video you can see a truck pass by at the bottom of the video which will give reference to the degree or distance.
