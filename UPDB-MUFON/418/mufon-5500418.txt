{nd} Large Fire cloud

On Aug 6th 2014 at around 12:30am my girlfriend and I where behind her place of business located downtown. We spotted two lights in the south western sky. The first light was white. It was slowly moving towards us then disappeared. The second light was yellow in color and did as the first. They where about 3min apart. We went in and talked about what we had just seen. We went out about an hour later and our jaws dropped when we saw in the same area as the lights was a fire in the sky. It changed shape and looked liked the moon behind a cloud. I grabbed my phone and looked at my star map app. It showed nothing in that part of the sky except a satellite. We looked at it as it was either going down slowly or moving off into the distance towards Jefferson county. I know people had to see this thing it was huge!!!!!
