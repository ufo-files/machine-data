{jpg} My wife and I witnessed 2 Glowing orange orbs moving rapidly over our neighbors house. I snapped 3 photos

July 09,2018
My wife and I were sitting out back of our house, and my wife exclaimed
"Wow Look at that glowing light" "Oh look there's another!"
I turned around and saw the second one clearly.It was an bright orange glowing orb moving rapidly from North West to North-North East
It was about the size of a softball, when we first spotted it
 We both ran into the house from
(the back door),I grabbed my camera, came out the front door and captured a picture of it.
( a blurred motion one, but the colors are evident).
My wife ran up ahead of me trying to follow the path of the first orb which flew over the trees and she lost site of it
I was further back from her, stopped and pointed my camera toward the path of the second orb. I captured a strange white streak, and also a translucent-colored orb, which I believe may have been the second orb 
that I was following, after changing colors from orange to translucent.
This was certainly very,very strange to see these bright orange glowing orbs.The entire viewing lasted approximately 5 - 6 minutes.
We would love to find out what they were. They were definitely not flares!Also this was not the moon, the moon was in the exact opposite
location from these orbs, and was not in the full moon phase
