UFO cluster following two other objects at high rate of speed

Our first night in Cancun, we randomly ended up having dinner outside. My wife (completely not a UFO believer) happened to notice something in the sky. Being a engineer and avid viewer of the sky, I suggested what we were seeing was a satellite or ISS. I noticed another light tracking a good distance behind which caused me to look directly overhead upon which we saw a cluster of lights in a completely random formation following the original two objects. The two objects and the cluster were only visible for at most a minute as they were traveling incredibly fast west to east to word Venus clearly visible in the sky. I quickly grabbed my phone and shot some video, but from my phone screen, only Venus is visible. When I get home, I will analyZe the video further. The other witnesses were resort employees.
