A light build shape, transparent white and a bright white object , trail behind it but! The trail did not stay longer than a second .

At 1.01am on 25/12/20 I saw a bright light in the sky near my home in Ipswich U.K. it was an old fashioned light bulb shape and was brilliant white and transparent in places , it looked to be quite large taking into account of distance. It was on a steep downward trajectory ( towards the sea ?) hard to tell as I lost it behind some roof tops. The trail was bright white but only lasted a split second, it did not linger like a vapour trail.I posted the sighting on my Facebook and had some replies regarding a sighting over Suffolk that day, that turned out to be a small meteorite; that was not what I saw, for one the meteorite was sighted around three in the afternoon of that day, my sighting was some 10 hours later and, it didn’t look like a meteorite to me it was craft like.
