{rjl} Fast, missle like object, light grey color flew directly in front of my car, hovered for about 1 second, then flew away.

Me and my Neice were in the McDonald's drive thru waiting on our food when all of a sudden I notice this missle type object fly directly in front of my car, hover for about 1 second, then fly away at a tremendous speed, maneuvering around the restaurant and several buildings before disappearing. All of this happened in the space of about 3 seconds. I asked my Neice if she saw it and she said yes. Apparently, the local Army reserve were flying drones at the airport not far from the restaurant, but I have never seen a drone that can maneuver like this one or go as fast. The object was going extremely fast. If I had not been looking forward, out the front windshield, as my Neice was doing also, we would have never noticed it. It made no sound. It had no lights. It had what looked like very short wings at the back of the object. If it were a drone, it was extremely fast, maneuverable and had the capability to hover, then take off at unbelievable speed.
