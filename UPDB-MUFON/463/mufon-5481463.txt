At first thought it was a small plane, but realized there was no sound. I watched it travel from the southwest to northeast. i then noted the blue lights slowed and craft slowed as well and came to a complete stop! No plane can do that! the blue lights went out, at least that what i thought, but realized the craft was turning around and the blue lights were in the back. as it turned i now saw 2 bright white and one red light. As it passed me I could make out the shape and could see the blue lights flashing again. I noted the the craft was not moving as fast and the lights we not flashing as fast as they were when i first saw it. I watched it travel in a straight line about 600 or 700 ft off the ground for about 25-35 secs before it was out of sight.
