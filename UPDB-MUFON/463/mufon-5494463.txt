I lived in central Missouri & my parents and younger siblings still living at home lived in southeast Missouri. every month or so I'd go down for the weekend. Its a touristy town with trail riders, rodeos, camping, canoeing, and swimming. If you like horses, woods,outdoors & no people to bother you, it's ideal. It was relaxing & a break from campus life in Columbia. On a weekend there in summer of 1983 (approximate), my family & I was watching a movie on Saturday night. There were six of us. During commercial break my brother went out onto front porch but never came back in. I went out to see what he was doing and he was looking at what we thought was a car with one headlight parked at the top of our driveway about 50 ft to the left of our house. We thought they were parked because we didn't hear the motor running. We decided the light was too big to be a headlight & we were looking through trees at the wooded area on the side of our yard too so it wasn't a clear shot view. We went back in to tell our dad there was a truck or something with 1 headlight "parked" on the hill of the driveway. He came to door & said it was probably Doc, who had a cabin at the far end of the driveway, like a mile up into the woods. We asked why would he be parked there just sitting. My brother & I went back onto the porch & noticed it had moved farther down the driveway closer to the yard. We watched it and we couldn't see it move, like clock hands it gained ground & was getting closer but we just couldn't see it moving. It got to edge of road & moved off road & thru tree until it got about 20 ft away. Now it was passed the trees & we had a clear view of it. It was about a 3 ft high & 3ft wide complete circle of white light. An all light globe? Circle? Orb? But it didn't shine a beam, it was all light and all we could see of it was the front. It was bright enough that all we could see beyond the edges of it was the background darkness of a small fence & the trees on the other side of driveway. It moved until it was 10 ft in front of us. It never did anything. It was so subtle. I guess if it shot up in the air and flew around the house, we would've been more scared, but it just didn't "seem" like it was moving or anything even though it was getting closer to our house. All the family came to the door & looked at it 2-3 times in the 45 min to an hour from the first time we saw it. We would go back in and then a few minutes later someone would go see how close it was. Then when we went to look to see if it was still there & how close it was. Thinking back on it, it terrifies me that it got that close. If it would have done something fantastic, it would have been so much more of an incident, but it was so subtle with the way it moved. I keep watching these "UFO" shows to see if anyone has ever seen anything like this & I would know they were truthful because I saw that same thing. Another important point needs mentioning about that is where our house was located. It was out in the country so far, our nearest neighbor was 3 miles away. Local people said that no one could live on that ridge. There was abandoned cabins up at the end of our driveway which was about 1 1/2 miles long. On the highway where our driveway started was an abandoned house & my family bought a cabin & house that had been abandoned and fixed them up & that's where they lived. People had bought property to build on & even attempted to build on that ridge, but for unknown reasons, they were always abandoned. This is true & there were 5 other people who witnessed this. In fact, I'm a little afraid bringing this up for fear of something happening. I've wanted to say something about it but worried about being labeled one of those people who think they saw a UFO. We, my family have never brought this up with all of us together but if asked confidentially, they would talk about it.
