That was last night. This is now:

Last night approx. 3:30AM I let my dog outside. While outside, I looked up to the North West of the Front Range in Boulder, CO. Specifically the Bald Mountain area. There was an extremely bright light in the sky that I assumed was an aircraft or some other type of night sky object (planet, star). I observed it for approx 2minutes and realized that it was approaching my city. It continued to come from the NW on a semi-curving line. It appeared to hit a significant hill that runs parallel down the mountains (start of redline on map image). When it hit that point, it drastically and suddenly increased speed and vanished from site going South.

After the object had left my sight, I immediately went into my home and pulled up radar flight trackers. There was a small aircraft relatively far away and two air liners that were significantly far away. Nothing else was registering on the radar. Those screen shots have been included.

I have seen enough aircraft of all sizes (including military) at night. The doppler effect should have kicked in at some point when it was moving sideways/away from me. The lights on the craft were not forward facing, they covered the entire object. There was no change in light depending on the viewable angle.

Please note, two factors: I live approx. [location removed]. They do training runs. Second factor is the video. I am having to move north / north east to keep the object in frame while avoiding tree cover.
