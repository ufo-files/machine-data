Fireball

My family and I were driving home on Ky I Princeton and turned onto Ky 139 and then observed a fireball burning bright white in the western sky. It descend from the sky falling toward a northern direction then leveled off and appeared to hover on the horizon. We lost sight of it as we traveled on the roadway. Not a aircraft of any sort there was no sound and it appeared a great distance away there were no lights flashing. I thought perhaps a meteor and it may have been although I expected to see a light flash from entry but never did. It should be noted this was at the beginning of dusk but was not the setting sun. It was either below cloud cover which was present -with a light snow, or perhaps shining through the cloud cover.
