{nd} Noticed what seemed like a star with varying degree of light intensity.

It was approximately 8:20p.m.on Sunday April 21st,2013 when I raised the blind on my south facing window,as I do most nights.My attention went almost immediately to a "star" as it was brighter than most and the light emitted was of varying intensity & appeared to almost vanish when it dimmed.Object was at roughly a 10 Oclock position in my field of vision when first seen.By 8:45p.m.,item was in a 2 Oclock position.The lateral movement was inperceptable.At 8:32p.m. a helicopter heading north on usual flight path,following US Interstate Route 93 passed below & to the east of object.At 8:34p.m.,an aircraft travelling east passed through my field of vision & appeared to be below the object.I watched intently,as I dressed and headed outside to continue watching object.I smoked a cigarette under the cover of some Evergreen trees,which blocked the light of the moon and the nearest streetlamp.At 8:43p.m.,I was back in my bedroom,and it was then that I became aware of the objects actual lateral movement,as object was now behind the branches of the Evergreens.I looked at the clock,it was 8:50p.m. & I could no longer see the object through the trees. I was hesitant to learn how and where to report this & were it not for the helicopter & aircraft in the sky as I watched this object,I most likely wouldnt be writing this note.I am curious as to whether anyone else saw this object.Were it not for points of reference,(my window frame & the trees)Im not sure that I would have continued watching object for a half hour.
