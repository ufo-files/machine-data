Nocturnal lights, Sudden direction change. Odd flash pattern.

I went to the end of my driveway to get the newspaper, the sky was still dark. I noticed a small triangle of lights that had a strange blinking pattern (blink, blink....blink)and were brighter than typical planes Ive seen. Object seemed to be located ESE of Salt Lake (North of me, in Sandy)and was headed North-to-south (towards me. Im unsure of its altitude, as the only reference I had were the clouds, which it was below.) The sky was too dark to allow me to see an outline beyond the lights, but the lights moved in unison and gave me the impression they were 3 lights on one vessel, as opposed to 3 vessels in formation. It did seem to be traveling about half-again (150%) as fast as typical planes. after almost 2 minites, the object paused in mid-blink and, when it resumed blinking, was moving at the same speed east-to-west. I saw it for another 30 seconds (appox) Then it stopped blinking. (remaining in the pattern.) It was still too dark to see any outline, and Id figure a plane wouldnt have turned thier lights off yet.)
About a minute after I first noticed the object I was still assuming it was a plane, but I started to video it on my camera phone (just in case.) Video is available, but I did not see anything on it.
An airplane did fly in a somewhat similar path about 15 minutes after the sighting. That was a commercial airplane, still flashing its lights (different color, pattern and sequence.) I could clearly see the fuselage outline by this time. It seemed to being flying higher than the object was, but still below the cloud cover.
