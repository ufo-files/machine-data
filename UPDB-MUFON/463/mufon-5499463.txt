{nd} Notice circle of lights far away in Northwest, North and Northeast sky, red and green.

We were standing outside Sunday night, just looking at sky. Noticed strange far away lights high in the sky, just hovering, red and green. Got my wife and we both watched and got binoculars and saw green, then red and white. They changed colors randomly and sometimes got brighter. The first was over the Northeast sky over Lake Erie and then we noticed 2 more sets of the same lights. These were in a line across from the first noticed lights. The one directly North and the third one, Northwest sky. We checked flight aware and there were maybe 2 commercial jets, but we saw those flying over. We did notice some other jets go by these lights, wondering if they were military due to speed and seeing their afterburners and they were quite high. (there is a Air National Guard base with jets 30 miles West of us). They went back and and forth a couple of times. When the jets went by the strange lights, they seemed to get brighter and more white. The jets went below and above the colored lights. This did scare us a bit, but we had to go in and go to bed, we both work early in morning. We watched this for about 1 1/2 hours or so. The Northwest lights eventually just disappeared, we never saw them leave. The lights directly North seemed to get higher in the sky, however, very slowly. The Northeast set of lights seemed to rise in the sky and move a little more West, but not very far. Both of these sets of lights were still in the sky when we went to bed. We were bothered and a little scared of what we saw. Anxious and excited to see these.
