my friend and I just parked in his driveway. Objects were very bright redish orange with distinctive formation. Witnessed from road side. at least 6 craft formed a large equilateral triangle shape formation before mingling with one another. I was very excited. They moved slowly. They then descended in a loose formation below the tree line which was less than a mile away from me and a fellow witness. after a couple minutes 3 of the craft arose back into view just above the tree line. One of the craft then headed west across the horizon and a moderate speed while the other two remained hovering then faded out. After a few more minutes two more arose from the same spot in the tree line. They hovered for 30 seconds or so then faded out of sight.
