When reviewing long exposure night sky photos, I found unusual lights in the sky that were not consistent with the movement of stars or satellites.

In the early hours of April 22nd 2020, I was in my back garden taking photos of the night sky. When reviewing them afterwards, I noticed coloured lights that were not in photos immediately before or after this one. I used a long exposure so I know they were not stars or satellites, as these lights left no trail which indicates they were perfectly still and not moving. There were 8 lights in total, 2 white, 1 blue, 1 yellow, 1 green-white, 1 red and then 2 white again, all in a row together. I did not see them by eye. Only when later checking my photos.
