{rjl} Two Silent High Altitude Lights Moving in Perfect Formation

Taking out trash, happened to look up and saw two white, constant intensity lights moving in perfect column formation, from east changing course to north east, at an apparently high altitude and high speed. No sound or contrails were observed. The whiteness of the lights did not fade in intensity. It is possible they were satellites, but not like one’s I’ve observed before. They appeared to change course slightly from east to north east. Interesting.
