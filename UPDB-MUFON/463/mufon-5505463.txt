I was taken through the wall or closet. was in a room that was very bright. it seemed like sheets or dividers. they where tall, had on what looked like robes. they had masks with big eyes.
this happened several times as a child. I was not afraid of them and do not remember ever being harmed.
later in life they came back. I could not move or speak. they came but I thought they looked like ghosts. I could hear but not understand. don't know if they took me or just looked at me but I was afraid.
Have had headaches and ringing in my ears. hard time concentrating. want to isolate.

This has gone on since I was about 5. they have not been here for a while but they will be back.

I have tried to talk to a few about this but they don't believe me.
