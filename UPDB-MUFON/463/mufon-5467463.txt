{jpg} Noticed an object as far as the stars flying stopping then short burst of light then moving backwards

Was talking on the phone on my front porch. Noticed an object as far away as the smallest stars moving East. It stopped, hovered in place, then let out an explosion or bright light. It then disappeared. I went inside got my wife and after spending five or less minutes looking it re-appeared briefly moving backwards in a Northeasterly manner. It was solid and had no blinking lights other than the explosion or light emission. It seemed to be moving slow because of the distance but it was covering vast distances in the sky after its re-appearance.
