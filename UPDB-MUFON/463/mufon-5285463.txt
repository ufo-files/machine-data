Two very bright white orbs

I was in East Vancouver looking South. Two bright white orbs appeared in the distance above the tree line. The second one was above and to the right of the first. They appeared out of no where and moved very slowly up and to the right. After about a minute they disappeared. Not flew away or fell. Just disappeared. I could see stars in the area so I know that they did not go behind clouds. There were no noises coming from them. They were definitely in our airspace (not outer space), lower than any plane or helicopter would fly over the city. Brighter & closer than any other stars, planets, satellites and planes that I saw that night.
