{nd} Three objects flying slowly overhead.

My wife, child and I were playing in the front yard when a glimmer in the sky caught my eye. I looked up and saw 3 silver, shiny, cigar shaped objects slowly flying overhead. Two were close together and the third was slightly farther away. The two closer objects continued on a straight path while the third slowly veered off to the left in a different direction. All three objects were completely silent and moving quite slowly at about cloud level. None of the objects had any apparent lighting. They werent close enough to see any details but it was obvious that they had no wings and were a reflective silver color. My wife ran inside and grabbed the tablet to take a picture. By the time she got back the objects were almost out of sight, bit we were able to get a couple of pictures. The objects aren't nearly as clear in the pictures as they were in person, but you can see one object in the images.
