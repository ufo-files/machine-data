{rjl} While taking photos of the sky, noticed an unknown green star or planet-like object.

I was taking pictures of the night sky in my front yard on the evening of November 14, 2015. Later, while reviewing the pictures, I noticed a green glowing object in two of the images. When I zoomed in, I noticed that the object appeared oval in shape. Also, the object had moved from the west side of Jupiter to the East side in only 15 minutes based on the time-stamp in my photos. 

The photos were taken with a Canon 60D and a 50mm f1.4 lens at 10 second exposures.
