twinkling lights,about 50 or so, over the ocean in a black mass.

my daughter and i were in myrtle beach, we were sitting on chairs a little after 10 at night. we looked up at the stars and noticed a bunch were twinkling. this was in may of 2011. so we walked closer to the beach looking at the twinkling lights and im thinking the ocean doesnt reflect in the sky, and in just a few minutes the lights started to move in this big black mass and then it started to slowly move out to sea. and then it was gone. it was quite a big black mass. and it was a very odd shape.
