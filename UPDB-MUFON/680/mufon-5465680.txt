{rjl} Large craft flew overhead then went behind trees - cow died

As reported to ASD in person (information only): "This happened in November of 2017 at my farm south of Grandview. I saw a large disc -shaped object come from the southwest headed northeast at a very slow speed. It was at about 100 feet above me when it flew over my head, then it went down in altitude behind some trees. It was very brightly lit with white lights coming from the bottom and illuminating the ground below. There was a low hum, but no other sounds and I could not see any exhaust. It as nearly silent. I was in my truck and got out of the truck to get a better look as it was just going over my head at 90 degrees. After it want out of sight, I got in the truck and headed for the area where it looked like the craft may have landed and got out. It took me about 10 minutes to get there. There in the stream was my best cow standing in the middle of the stream, but dead. I've never seen anything like it. This was a 1,200 lb animal, and she was dead and still standing up. The object was nowhere in sight. I am still bewildered by this event."
