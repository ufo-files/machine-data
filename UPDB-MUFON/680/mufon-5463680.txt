{tjd} Meteor Pictures with Blue Lights

There was much footage of this object -- supposedly a meteor -- on December 19, 2018 in the East Bay. But when I looked at the pictures I took, I had two blue lights in all the pictures. They were not seen by my eyes, but showed up in all my pictures. Are these artifacts or something else? Did anyone else capture blue lights when they took pictures of the light that streaked through the sky, and left a luminous cloud behind it?
