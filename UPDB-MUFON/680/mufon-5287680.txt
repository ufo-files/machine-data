I was staying a the Youth Hostile. My friends and I went out for a smoke and bright lights that flickered like a candle started moving across the tree line from left to right at a fast pace. Then one by one they stopped in a zig zag scatter plot. The changed shape, shifted several times. Everyone was in shock. Cars were pulling over in the middle of the street screaming "what is that". We assumed there must be an airforce base or something near by but there wasn't that we could find and there is no way planes could move that fast. One by one they went out like candles being blown out. I didn't ever tell anyone. it was a long time ago but I'm done not talking about it.
