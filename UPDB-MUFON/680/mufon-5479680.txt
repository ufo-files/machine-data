{cak} While looking at supermoon with starter telescope, saw black circular object make curved path across moon

Excited to see the supermoon and take pictures, I placed both my Celestron (starter) Telescope and new DSLR camera with tripod on my patio. At approximately 6:50 just after trying unsuccessfully to take good pictures with my DSLR (software too smart, i guess), I sat back at the telescope, adjusted it again, and looked through the eyepiece. I was astonished to see a small black circle moving across the moon's surface. It did not do a straight line across it, it did a curved trajectory on the upper part of the Moon (from the angle of view) until it reached the other side of the moon and could not longer be seen. I am sure there will be more reports of this as the perfect looking black circle was very distinguishable as it (very quickly) went across.
