{dr}Siliver barrel type object with a lens in the center sitting on a tree branch moving in a clockwise manner as if observing everything around it, like a camera

I was playing 'it' with my friends and hid underneath a neighbor's tree to evade them. Then I ran to the fire pump and turned around. That's when I saw this metallic object sitting on the branch that must have been above me the entire time I was hiding under the tree. It looked like the size of a keg of beer with the exception of a fish eye type of lens in the center that seemed to be like a camera. That's what I thought it was; some high tech camera. The object was moving extremely slow in a clockwise position as if taking in the scenery. I felt amazed that this thing would be interested in what a bunch of little kids would be doing. I remember thinking we're being watched for some reason I couldn't fathom. I ran along and when I turned around to look at it again it was gone, poof, as if it were a figment of my imagination. The first thing I thought was, "Gee, it must have decided we are really boring." I had one dream since that time about being taken away from earth and screaming as our planet has become smaller and smaller. I have no memory of being abducted though, and I am very glad that I don't.
