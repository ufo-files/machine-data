A break in time or teleportation

Back in 2015 in fresno ca me and my friends were outside around 3am. We were walking down a long road and there was a semi-truck on the side of the road and no other cars around. As we were walking towards the semi truck there was a bright blue flash of light coming from the back of the truck. This light almost looked like a burst of what i can only explain as energy, like the space x launch in 2018 but on a smaller scale. My friends and i were blinded for a second but we saw a glimpse of a car swerve out from behind the truck and the light flashed again a second later and the car disappeared. This car looked like an old maybe 80s style police car. The whole truck was wobbling side to side after this happened. It made the driver get out and check his truck. I turned my head to look at my friends and asked them if they saw the car and all they did was nod their heads yes. I still cant stop thinking about that night. Im 20 years old now and that memory will never leave me because i still cant understand what it was or who it was. All i lnow is it was a very weird night for me. I wish i can get some answers but still nothing about anything like this has been experienced by anyone else or at least i cant find it anywhere.
