3rd nighttime sighting of a slow flying pulsating object flying NE/E over Shamokin, PA

This report is my 3rd sighting of very low flying dusk or nighttime objects with extremely bright white/red or white/red/green softly pulsating orb-like lights observed over Shamokin, PA. The first sighting (with a video) was reported as MUFON case #109243.

My second sighting (no video) was a very short nightime sighting around 1 week before this sighting, approx 7:30-8pm, of a very low flying object over or just beyond the 500 block of N Shamokin St. I was at the corner of N Shamokin St and Independence St. I only spotted the object for around 5-10 seconds, flying east. It was silent and pulsating red/green/white, flying south east, just above buildings. It was so low, just over buildings, that I was unable to keep the object in site for very long or make a video.

For this third sighting, I was walking near the train tracks near the municipal building on N Franklin St. (located at around 512 Franklin St), looking north east/east towards the large medical/police/fire building. Low flying air traffic is very rare in Shamokin, and this object resembled my other two sightings (a pulsating bright globe-light object), so I immediately took a video with my iPhone. It was silent. As the object moved east it went into a bright glare from street lights and I tried to reposition myself while recording. My phone had a difficult time keeping the orb-like light in focus.
