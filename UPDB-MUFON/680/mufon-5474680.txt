{dr}3 orange globes 5 minutes apart traveled from south to north without sound

Wife upon going to bed, sitting on same saw outside a orange glowing orb traveling without sound from south to north. She came downstairs and related it to me but said it was gone. I shrugged it off when 5 minutes later she hollers down that the orb is back. I went outside and only caught the glow passing out of sight. Staying out on our deck approximately 5 minutes, I noticed a glowing object coming towards me from the south. I had a clear view of it as it traveled slowly without sound toward the north in a straight line. It looked the size of a large softball or small volleyball emitting an undulating orange light/glow not very high, about 500 to a 1000 ft up . Nothing spectacular, very light winds but off to the north there was lighting but no thunder heard. Stayed out another hour but nothing else was seen. I am a retired Division Chief for Ohio State Police and am very hesitant to state with any certainty that these were any thing other that orbs of unknown makeup and I remain skeptical.
