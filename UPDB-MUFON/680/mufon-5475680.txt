{dr}Huge object overhead. Heard and felt its size. Camoflauged to night sky.

We were 14yrs old and fishing on a sand bar between 2 small bodies of water. We were having an argument about our lines when a huge object came over us and hovered. We did not know what it was because it blended in perfectly with the night sky. We were terrified. It was very big but not loud and it seemed like the water got rough. It stayed directly over us for several minutes,lifted up and moved very fast when it flew off. Like a whirling rotating zing. We grab our stuff and ran back to the cabin woke everyone up. My dad thought we had an accident because we were shaking and unable to speak. We look for physical signs the next day but found none. The family cabin was on an undeveloped lake surround by bare pasture and no public road access. We have never heard the sound the object made ever again nor have we been able to recall anything further about our encounter but we both remember it like it was yesterday. It is strange because I can tell you how big it was as if I did see it and its exact flight path.
