saucer shaped

i was outside of the front door of where i lived at the time, i was pregnant at the time and think it was in the second half of the pregnancy so between the end of 1975 and march 1976 i don't know what made me look up i cant remember maybe just looking at stars but saw this thing hovering above the opposite houses with lights chasing round the edge 
it saucer shaped and i watched trying to see if it was a helicopter as it just sat still in the sky to my right. as i watched it shot across to my right side sat there a short while then returned to its first position and sat there then shot straight up in the sky and disappeared i don't remember the time but it was just getting dusk. as much as i wished to i have never seen any since, i told no one at the time i remember thinking that i wished i knew where to report it, but kept it to myself until my children were old enough to talk about it. i always wished i had a camera but loved the moment i had.
