{ck} Possible abduction

On either the 26th or 27th of June 2012 I awoke that morning with a burning sensation in my right sinus, like a couple of drops of some caustic fluid had been put there - as if I were laying on my back & it dripped in.. kinda hard to explain. Anyway, I rolled over to tell my partner about it & noticed two fresh marks on his back; circular & below the shoulder blade which later turned into bruises. I have photos of the bruises & will submit them if there is a place for it. There was nothing in the bed that would have caused this bruising, that was the 1st place I looked. I also scoured the house & found nothing that would make marks like that, so perfectly aligned. Later, when I stepped out of the shower, I noticed several small bruises & marks on my upper body [like I had been in a fight]. Neither of us remember dreaming that night nor any memory of how we got those marks on us. The dogs didn't alert us to anything that night [they didn't bark & wake us up]. Another odd thing is, the garage door was open that morning. Could all be coincidence & who knows about the garage thing but I thought I should share.
