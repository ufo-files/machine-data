{nd} Observed object hovering and moving north over Short Mountain by my house.

Well I went out to enjoy the sunrise. It was around 7 am because the sun hadnt reached over the top of Short Mountain yet. At first when I saw the object I thought it was a jet that was far, far off with chemicals or smoke trailing behind it. But then as I watched it I realized that it looked rather odd. It wasnt acting like smoke. And it wasnt moving like a cloud either. The sky was totally clear so it couldnt have been a cloud. It was too small and didn't move like a lenticular cloud either. It looked as if it was shimmering. I don't know if it was because the sunlight was reflecting off of it or it was spinning and had flashing white lights on it. It started over to the right, above the mountain, and started moving left/north bound over the top of the mountain. I am not exactly sure how fast it was moving, but it took it about 7 to 8 minutes to move from there over to the tree line and out of my sight. In the video its pretty hard to make out its best viewed full screen and in high quality. I am sorry for the poor video, but it was a spur of the moment filming and my camera isnt the best and I have no tripod to sit it on. I see star-like UFOs rather often at night here since I live out in the country where its easy to see the stars. But this is the first time I have ever seen anything like this. From what I can tell it was big.
