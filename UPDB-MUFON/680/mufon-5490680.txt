I was walking down a beach . 3 miles from the cook nuclear plant. Listening  to music . I don't  remember turning it off but it stopped  playing . I stopped to throw a rock and bam. Right to the left of my throwing path was this pyramid shaped craft hovering appeared out of nowhere I sat on the sand and blink it was gone . It did not take off it just disappeared . I stood  up talking  to myself  about what I saw. I picked up another rock. Threw it in the air again . And bam there it was in the same spot I tried to get a better look at it but I was almost right under it. No lights no sound. And blink it was gone again  but this time I felt dizzy when it disappeared . I looked around and I saw no one else. Just then a very small plane or drone flew straight  past where I was. I have seen  things after that day but never  that pyramid craft. And when I try to focus on it things get fuzzy. I waited till now to report it. I felt the need to get it out.
