{rjl} a portal opened up & a ball of light fell from it leaving a trail of plasma energy or light

On feb. 12th 2012 around 2 am I stepped out onto my balcony to check the weather before I go walk my dog. As I was looking into the clear night sky, a portal opened up & a glowing ball of light dropped from it & as it descended to earth it left a staggered trail of light.I live on the top floor(7th)in my hi-rise & I'm situated on good sized hill so I'd say this event happened over the china basin part of the s.f. bay.The portal happened right before my eyes & it looked like a slow-motion blast or explosion which consisted of about 10-12 rays or streams of light or plasma energy.The whole sighting only lasted about 4 seconds.I know it wasn't any rogue firework because I would've seen a trail of smoke coming from it as it was being shot up into the sky,but there was nothing like that.This thing happened right before my eyes out of nowhere & it was totally silent from start to finish.Also the streams of light coming from the portal were thicker and had a more solid look to them,compared to that of a big 4th of july firework.The sky wasn't lit up or anything though.The rays of light faded out while the glowing ball was falling and leaving this trail of staggered energy.And it fell straight down to either the ocean or land.I couldn't tell because it kind of faded into the horizon.I apologize for taking so long to report this.On the same day in 2013 & 2014 I tried to see if would happen again by setting up my camera to record the sky for 2 hours(at 2 am) but had no luck.I was awestruck when this happened & have only heard about something similar like this from the skinwalker ranch events.
