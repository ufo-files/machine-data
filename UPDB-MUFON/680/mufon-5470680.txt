{jpg} Drove down ordinarily secluded dark road, and witnessed amazingly bright lights omitted from hovering silent aircraft larger than commercial airliner.

Driving down State Route 5 at approx 9:20 pm, we noticed abnormally bright lights in an area free of homes or structures. What first drew our attention was how bright the lights were, and being very familiar with the area we knew there was no explanation within the realm of normalcy to explain the sighting. One witness felt particularly ill immediately before and following the event. Another factor that perplexed us was that the apparent aircraft failed to produce any sound. The craft was larger than any commercial airliner. I immediately came home to research and look for similar stories as to what we had just encountered. I came upon this website and had to share our experience. The witness other than myself was (prior to tonight), a die hard skeptic.
