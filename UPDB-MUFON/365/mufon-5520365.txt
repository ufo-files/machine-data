Triangle like Craft dropping Colored Orbs

Witness was awoken by a loud roar outside his home in the early morning. Sound described as a whirring roar with a crackle/static sound. He went outside to investigate the nose and noticed a large, triangle shaped craft hovering over a bay on the Wolf River just to the East of the residence. Craft was dark with no lights. At that time, craft dropped several colored orbs over the water which spun for a brief moment and then took off at a high rate of speed. The craft then hovered over the home in a westerly direction to a point directly over the wolf river. The craft hovered for a short time and again dropped several colored orbs which took off at a high rate of speed following the Wolf River upstream.(North) The craft then also followed the river upstream in the direction of the orbs at a slow speed until the witness lost sight of the object. Deputies from Winnebago and Waupaca Counties were contacted and dispatched to the scene. They were unable to witness any of the events.
