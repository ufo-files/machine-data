{rjl} redish orange orbs in the northwest sky

I was standing on my porch around 8:30 pm when I saw one redish orange orb appear low in the northwest sky. Soon after it appeared, another one appeared off to the right of it at a slight lower elevation. The first one went away followed by the second one. This occurred once more.
