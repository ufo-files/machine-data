Object appear on photographs during trip to the Grand Canyon

I traveled to the Grand Canyon with my family and while taking a burst of photographs of a Condor flying in the air I caught what appears to be a reflective round circle in the distance.

I did not notice the object in the air while I was taking the images. It was only after I had returned to my home and was reviewing my photographs that I was puzzled at what I had captured.

It could be nothing as the object appears very far, however I will include the original unedited images so it can be analyzed properly.
