large silent triangle with lights going from birmingham to burton

It was very low. Lower than any plane ive ever seen. It was a dark grey triangle with lights on the points and a red glow in the middle. The light at the front was red/orange and pulsated and lit the whole front of the craft up and you could see it was metallic. It was traveling very slowley. It was huge. Totally freaked me out. Ive seen many of ufos but nothing like this. Been trying to put a explanation to it but i cant. I hear planes that are ten times higher. But this never made a sound.
