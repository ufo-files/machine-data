{dr}Saw four red lights in night sky .

While watching Tiger base ball wrap up on TV, and watching local neighborhood fire works outside, I noticed what I thought was an airplane flying above the trees to the west of my location. It approached from the southwest and turned slowly to the north. I noticed it because the anti collision lights didn't look normal, not blinking any white light, just a constant red. The speed of the light was about that of a small plane. Then a second light from the same direction showed up. It too turned north in trail with first light. The first light exited at a very high rate of speed and gained altitude to the northwest. As I returned my attention to the second light, a third light approached from the direction as the first, and it too turned north. The second also exited to the northwest, but gained altitude faster than first as it penetrated some high-level clouds that the first went under. The third light did pretty much as the others, but it too penetrated the high level clouds. The fourth light approached from the south west, but I didn't observe the direction it exited as some neighbors distracted my gaze. And when I returned to look where I thought it should be it was gone. During this time, I did see a small airplane flying in an easterly direction, north of my location; there is an airport nearby.
