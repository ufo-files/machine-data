The most shiny silver looking object that I've ever seen....just sat there in the sunny sky.

I was driving on Tittabawasee Rd going east toward Saginaw from Freeland. As looked up to the east I noticed what looked like a commercial plane. It was different though...no wings. It was a cigar shape.  It just sat there from the whole duration from when I turned onto that road from M-47. It took me around 15 minutes because traffic was very busy that particual day. I am very into aviation and I know the difference from a plane and what I saw. After a while I had to watch the road because I was getting into the city...I lost sight of it.
