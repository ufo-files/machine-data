(GS) Red/orange/white randomly blinking object flying in a zig-zag formation

(6/3/13 at around 9-9:30pm) Myself and 2 others saw a diamond shaped object near steelton pa. At first it looked like a planet (that was low in the sky) we noticed it was moving across the sky slowly, when it got higher in the sky it started to move at a high rate of speed in a left to right zig-zag formation. At first it was not blinking then at started blinking randomly when it was above us (Red/orange/white) a few times the light went out completely and towards the end of our encounter it seemed like there were two objects (almost like one would break off or the object was in two places at one time) then we heard a loud bang sound (like a gunshot or sonic boom) then the object was gone
