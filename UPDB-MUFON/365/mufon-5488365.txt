Two disc like lights as one approached the other the other would move away, very fast movement cannot be a plane, strange phenomenon

At the time I saw this phenomenon I was getting ready to go to sleep. I looked out on the balcony and I notice a light in the sky this is moving. I could see a tractor where the farmer was working a few fields away, I then realised there were two lights in the sky. Both these lights were hovering in a circular manner where they were moving very fast, not even a plane would move this fast. One light would approach the other in a hover manner where the other light would then move away. When I first saw the lights I thought it would be a star or plane but nothing would move at this speed. This phenomenon lasted for around 9 minutes. I tried to make a video but it was too dark so I took some pictures on my phone to show the movements of the objects. My feelings once seeing what I've just seen has made me l rethink that extra terrestrials may exist..I know from reading this report somebody will say this phenomenon was due to a chemical reaction between cloud particles and weather conditions, but ask yourself what if it is not?
