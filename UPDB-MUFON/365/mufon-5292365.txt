Hovering Orb

An orb appeared at 11:00 am on 8/14/2020. I was outside building a shed and looked up and noticed that the moon appeared very clearly for the middle of the day so I went inside and told my wife that she should come take a picture of the moon. She snapped off a few pics at 10:54 am then went inside. Just after that the orb appeared. I thought it must be a balloon so I continued to work.  As I did so I occasionally looked up and noticed that the object stayed in the exact same spot for several minutes so I went back in to ask my wife to take pics to see if we could determine what the object was since it stayed in the same spot and did not appear to oscillate or move like a balloon normally would. She took a few pics and went inside. The object remained in that spot for approximately 15 minutes then started moving west very slowly. I looked up and saw that it appeared to stop again. I entered the shed and grabbed a tool that I needed and came back out and the object was gone.
