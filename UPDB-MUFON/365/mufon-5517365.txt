(gs) Red-Orange round glowing object in sky that lasted around 20-25 minutes

Around 8:50 pm est. standard time toward the W-NW. After just arriving home with my brother and exiting my vehicle, i looked to the sky to observe stars and/or planets and saw the object in the sky. I first thought it was a star or planet because it was not moving, after approximately two minutes i noticed a streak behind the object at which time i thought it was an airplane or similar object. Upon noticing that the object was moving to slow to be a plane i continued to watch it at which time i noticed it was glowing red, orange and yellow. I watched the object until 9:06 pm est. time at that time the object vanished beyond the horizon, during the time i observed the object it went from a hovering or still state in the sky to a slow moving state with a white streak behind it. The object moved toward the west in a straight line while veering slightly to the left. While watching this object move though the sky my initial reaction and feeling was astonishment.
