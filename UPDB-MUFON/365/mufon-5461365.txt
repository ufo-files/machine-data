{cak} Bright object seen during daylight hours

Just happened to walk out side. I was looking towards the Superstition Mountains and noticed an extremly bright object shining in the cloudless blue sky. The object was very bright similar to the colour of a welding flash (bright bluish white) and was very noticeable. It was larger and brighter than a star. I used my SkyView app and confirmed it was not a planet. There was a strong constant wind of out of the west around 25mph. Object looked as if it was stationary but after 10-15 minutes you could see it was very slowly moving in a west ward direction. The objects seemed to slowly turn red as the sun began to set. I could still see the object even after the sun had set and the sky was dark (faint reddish colour) I am not an expert on weather balloons, but I would figure if that is what I saw it would be moving in the same direction as the wind? I lost sight of it as the sky clouded over.
