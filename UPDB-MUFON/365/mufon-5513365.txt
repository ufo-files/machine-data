On January 1, 2013 I was heading home from work at midnight when I saw a red circular flash of Light that imprinted its image in my eyes for several seconds . The image was a perfect circle that flashed like a explosion and left behind lightning bolts within the circle . The flash was about 500 ft in front of my car . Then in about 10 seconds I witnessed the same exact thing happen again but was 3 miles farther up the road . I thought the first time was a lightning strike that was very close to my car and it left me seeing a circle in my vision .the second one being farther away I could see the red circle then the flash. Then what was like lightning bolts in the circle . The object was flying in a straight line and i had my windows up so i heard no noise. . It scared me at first then when I saw it again I grabbed my cell phone and called my wife to tell her what I saw . I am just wondering if anybody. Has seen anything like that .
