Saw metallic orb/circle in the sky (was reflecting the setting sun) traveling slowly before changing direction and disappearing.

Driving west on Ottawa Beach Road at approximately 2015 hours. Blue skies with minimal clouds. Noticed a shinny object in the sky in front of me at about 45 degrees. Round / orb shaped reflecting the sun. I scanned the sky because I had no idea what it was. when scanning, saw two airplanes at higher altitudes leaving visible contrails. The object was moving slowly from north to south, paused for a second or two and then appeared to change directions and travel west heading away from me before it totally disappeared. I scanned the sky and could not see it any more.  I was captivated by the object and didn't even think to get my phone out.
