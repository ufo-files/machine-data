2 x Blue Orbs that Change Direction Overhead of Camp Ground

2 x blue orbs come into view at varying east to west directions. As they get closer to the camp ground they each change direction by about 30 degrees and move in opposite directions to each other.

There is a possibility that each was some type of meteorite that produced a blue haze during vaporization as entering the atmosphere. Possible that the change in direction was the meteorites bouncing off the atmosphere resulting in change of direction.
