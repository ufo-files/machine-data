{jpg} Hovering Triangle in Goleta

I was working for a Large burger chain. In N Out. It was around the 4am early morning regular work start. I had to work on the roof that morning. It was very dark, no moon. I looked to the east towards Los Angels and seen a small plane flying towards the ocean, lights are blinking. I thought wow there flying early as well as me working early Then I turned away and looked straight up. There was another light and I looked away and thought the same thing, but then I looked back at it because the light I glanced at caught my attention to look back. I noticed the the light above me was like pulsating but in a weird way. As I looked at it, I was able to look at the plane flying to the east. No comparison in the lights. Then as I looked at it, it started to get smaller as if it was traveling upward. Then that when I realized that i was looking at the bottom of a triangle with 3 lights that weren’t blinking and the light that caught my attention was in the middle of the triangle, just pulsating. I could not see detail of the body but could make out the shape. There was cleaning staff down in the building, but I could not call them because I did not want to take my eyes off it. This lasted 3 to 4 minutes. Then as I was looking at it the whole triangle lights and all, started to get smaller as if it were going straight up. Then it just disappeared in a flash. I guessing straight up. I got off the roof and told no one. I just started the job and did not want to lose my job because they might think I’m a nut. To this day I stand by what I saw that morning.
