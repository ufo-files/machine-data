Saw bright light move slowly left to right, then descending

I was taking the dog out and having a smoke with my neighbor and brother. I noticed the light when I first got outside. It was in the west/south west direction. I figured it was Jupiter or a helicopter. -I had found Jupiter, so I asked to 2 with me to see what it may be. 

After observing it for a minute, it moved so slowly, everyone thought "UFO". I grabbed my camera and got about 6-7 minutes of the object. It slowly moved left to right and descended slowly. It had a pulsating red/green light, but the main light was white. We watched it until it dropped behind the building next to us. I am trying to upload the video, but have had issues.
