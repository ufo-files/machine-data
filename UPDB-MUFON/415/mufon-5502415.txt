{rjl} Driving down the highway, getting off exit, saw 5-6 orange glowing orbs moving slowly across sky

I was coming home from work and driving down the highway, I-75 Southbound, getting off at the Taft Road Exit into Cincinnati when I looked up to see about 5 or 6 glowing orange lights. They weren't too high up in the air, they looked like they could've been the top lights of those metal towers, but no such towers are near the highway exit, I've driven this highway many times for work and I've never seen anything like it before.

There were very few other cars on the road, due to it being late at night, so I slowed down significantly to try to get a good look at the orbs of light. They were moving somewhat slowly and deliberately away from me. I was turning right to get off the exit and they were moving right as well.

When I first saw them I didn't exactly know what to think. I've never seen a UFO in real life, only on videos on the Discovery Channel/History Channel or the internet, but about a second after thinking "What the heck is that!?" I thought to myself that they could very well be UFOs. I never thought they'd be so vivid, so clearly right there.

I looked down at the road to make sure I was still in my lane, because I was coming onto some stopped cars at the red light getting off on the exit of the highway. When I looked up the glowing orange orbs were gone. If they had maintained their previous speed I would've been able to see them from my stopped car at the red light, but they were gone.
