was after we my husband and i had gotten out of our truck and i don't know why but for some reason i looked up to sky and across the sky to the right of us came this disc- we watched as it came closer to us i could not believe my eyes- for it hovered above us for a few mins. with it lights circling on bottom -it was a gray color metal with a dome no sound i thought why doesn't it take me aboard i would like to see inside for i am a believer maybe that's why because i wasn't afraid it was about 20 feet above us in the center of our driveway so surprised we just stood still and watched the circling lights of many colors and as quick as it came it zoomed off to the right of us it left as fast as it had came no sound---i called the newspaper next day to see if anyone else had reported it and yes there were-it was in the library on record for awhile -i tried to open file again without any luck after all these years-my husband would never admit to it so i just let it go--for we know people look at you strange if you say anything about seeing it--but i know what i saw that night and it is not make believe it was a real saucer as i have described----
