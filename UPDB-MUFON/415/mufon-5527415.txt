( 1st ) I only want to report this to see if anyone else seen the same thing....At work, I walked out to check the weather at 4:10 A.M. it was a clear sky. I looked up and noticed a dull circular light traveling in a North westernly direction, light object traversed in a strait line with no blinking lights and no sound. From the moment it was over head until the time it traveled the whole distance of the sky was about 4 seconds. It was much faster than a satellite and to dull to be a meteor. I thought it could be a satellite at first but the object was larger than that but smaller than a low flying aircraft and it was definately the fastest moving object I have ever seen.
