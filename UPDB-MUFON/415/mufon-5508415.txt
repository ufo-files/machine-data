On my way to work, I took a picture of the sunrise. Later discovered the orb formation in my photo.

8/14/2013 5:19AM I was driving to work from Surprise Az, to central Phoenix. I stopped at a stop sign on the access road to Grand Ave. and saw there was a nice cloud formation backed by a colorful sunrise. I often takes pictures of scenes like this, when I can, and since there were no cars behind me this early, I decided to snap one of this view. Grand Ave runs diagonal (s/e - n/w).I held my phone/camera out of the drivers side window and took the shot facing east at the sunrise. I took two shots and never even reviewed them until a few days later (Monday August 19th 2013). I was at home using my phone to view my pics on my TV and thats when I noticed The glowing lights in the sky above the clouds. The TV doesnt have the function to zoom in so I had to look back at my phones screen to zoom in and was in aw at what i was looking at. 6 orbs of light in a circular or oval shape. Im not sure if they were separate objects or all a part of one object. I live near Luke Airforce Base and see fighter jets all the time but this was definitely NOT one of them.
