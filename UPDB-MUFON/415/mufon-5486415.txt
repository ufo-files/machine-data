I was driving at 6:06 pm where baseline road and watt meet. I saw an orange orb in the sky and instantly knew it wasn't a plane. I was thinking its a drone because Beale AFB is less then 20 miles away and i see them from time to time. It was heading in a straight direction South East away from Sacramento. Then when it got directly overhead I saw it had a vapor trail behind it. This was no drone. For a second I saw a red flicker come off end of it like when space shuttle or rockets boosters have those come off end of them. I got out of my car and didn't hear a noise. Then it disappeared. I really feel this was a man made object. If I had to make my best guess I would say it was a missle. Then it just burned out. The one thing I wish I could of seen was what shape it was. But the orange glow and the vapor trail behind it made that impossible. Another important detail was this looked like it was in atmosphere where jets would fly, not in space.
