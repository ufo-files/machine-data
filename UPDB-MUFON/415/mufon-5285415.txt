Daytime mental visualization of triangular shapes with eyes closed

Although I have had two Encounters of the first kind, one in the late 60's as a child and one while travelling at night in 2004, my experiences beginning in 2013 are more remarkable. I came upon this by accident. While looking at the sky and meditating, one day when I closed my eyes I saw the shadowed images of the trees, etc- i don't know what that's called when you stare at somethinng, close your eyes and the image is still there- but, unseen by my open eyes there was a equalateral triangular shape- 3 yellow globes connected by turquoise 'legs' - in my closed eye image.
As I experiment with this I now have more than one show up at a time- always the same coloration, the same trianglular shape. Some look smaller. Perhaps they are further away.
I am curious if anyone else experiences this. I've heard of people recording things with infra-red that are not visible to the naked eye.
What I have stumbled upon can be experienced without equipment.
Thank you for your time.

Maybe someone will benefit from this 'technique'.
