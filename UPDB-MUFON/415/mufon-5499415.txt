{nd} Around 20 lights over Guinea Road

Tonight when I was coming home from work I saw a few lights behind the trees when I was about to drive onto Guinea Road at the intersection of Braddock Road. I soon came to a red light, and curious, I rolled down my window just to see about 20 (maybe more) gold-colored lights moving above the traffic silently, all at the same, slow speed in one direction. They didn't seem to form a particular pattern, and I could not see any big objects. When I looked in front of me, I saw that the light had turned green, so I took off. When I came home and described the path of the objects to my family, they said that's the western direction. Since I am bad with directions, I checked both "east to west" and "unknown" in the questions about direction. I thought about them maybe being drones, however, I cannot think of why 20 drones would be hovering over traffic at that time. I hope other people have reported this because it was an amazing sight, it reminded me of the Phoenix lights. I hope you guys can shed some light onto this.
