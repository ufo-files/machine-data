{rjl} Encounter of the 4th Time.

Heard Police siren for several minutes in my neighborhood.
I went in my back yard to investigate what direction the sirens were coming in or out from, or if I could see possible police lights after the sirens go silent....nothing.

Taking one last look around my backyard, I noticed a helicopter height yellowish-orange orb type light in West sky traveling straight East maybe a few miles out or so but making no sound and with no blinking lights after observing for about 30-seconds.

I rushed back inside grabbing my cell phone to video tape just in case. After I video recorded the object for another 1 and 1/2 mins catching nothing on my cheap Andriod's camera, it's only luminating yellowish-orange orb light was steady along with it's East slow and silent course until finally out of view. 

I have seen man-made aircraft at twice this range and elevation which still gave off faint engine noise I could still make out, so I'm positive this was a UFO.

I seen same thing over head couple years ago except it flew direct over my head revealing at-close-distance a dead-silent Metal craft with antigravity propulsion of some type and web like haze like a shield, cloak or propulsion field itself.
