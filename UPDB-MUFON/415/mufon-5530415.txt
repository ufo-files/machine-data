On the beach when I noticed white circle in the sky.

I was on the beach in Watch Hill RI on July 26th at about 1pm.I was sitting in my beach chair the weather was beautiful not a cloud in the sky.I happen to look up in the sky when I notice a perfectly white circle shape object tracking from left to right in the sky. At first I thought it might be an airplane,and the sun was making some weird reflection of light on the planes body.As I looked on I noticed it was getting smaller.I still thought it might be an airplane that was now turning and I was going to get a look at maybe the planes tail or make out some wings,but it just disappeared.I was staring there in amazement when the object reappeared directly below from where on first saw it. It reappeared and disappeared in a split second.I told the wife and kids of what I just saw and they just laughed!! I thought it would be cool to share with you.
