ball of glowing light

Around 11:40 pm observed a white glowing ball of light, similar to a very large orb. The object appeared out of nowhere. The object was first stationary, then made a direct path towards the rocky mountains and dissappeared into a cloud. The object seemed to have an intelligence about. It was not a meteorite. Reminded me of the scene in Close encounters of the 3rd kind, when ufos come out of the clouds, only this object headed into a cloud but so much faster. The entire sighting was maybe 3 seconds.
