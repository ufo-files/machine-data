{tjd} At sea at night, no clouds, no other witnesses, just me and my binoculars.

Returning to Ketchikan from Metlakatla Air Station about 0130 hrs, calm seas (flat), no cloud cover. I am off watch and with the clear calm conditions I put on a Kapok life vest for comfort to lie down on the forward deck and with binoculars to star watch. Beautiful night and I look southward, our course was basically north south at that time, and I see "oh a sattelite" watched it for about a minute and then it took on new course westward via a 90 turn. A flat right angle turn. I thought it was then a meteor and watched for burn up. Altitude did not change, color did not change, course did not change til lost from sight by curve of earth and brightness of curve of earth westward. Both courses S to N and E to W were straight as a line. The turn was abrupt with no sign of flashing at all. Like cue ball of a bank, straight then going West. No, I did not fall asleep and imagine it. 
I also witnessed the Phoenix lights while they were cruising through Yavapai County north of Phoenix. Bright white (but not bright enough to hurt the eyes) a few thousand feet up. This was during the evening but still light out.
