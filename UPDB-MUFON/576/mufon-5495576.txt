{nd} Object in satellite orbit moving 4-5 times faster than ISS

I had just stepped outside tonight to observe the stars before getting ready for bed when I saw what appeared to be a satellite traveling south to north at a very high rate of speed. It was about 2.5 magnitude with a steady white light. I watch ISS every time it comes our way and this thing is at least 4-5 times faster. This is the third time I have witnessed this, my wife and I saw it first last summer and I've witnessed it twice my self. I've talked to people with knowledge of satellite technology and have been told there is no satellite that goes faster than ISS (that they know of) but there is no doubt that this was in a satellite orbit. There are no strobes and no sound, it simply zips through the sky. ISS usually takes 5-6 minutes to go from horizon to horizon but this is about a minute and a half. I'm hoping someone else will come forward as I can't be the only one seeing this.
