{cak} Very large triangle

While at work I stepped out to stretch my legs I observed the triangle just off to the south west of my post.At approximately 45deg above the horizon The object did not move,make a sound or do anything accept sit there.it was above the clouds with the moon 15 deg to the south east and above the top point of the triangle. Did not observe any stars inside the three points.Approximate length per side using a 12inch ruler at arms length each side was 11.5 inches from point to point.I was able to get a digital photo using a NV-100 night vision monocular and will include it after I download.
