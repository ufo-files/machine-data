dark silent triangle craft following us

coming home from a football game .
My step-dad, mom, best friend and I driving on a rural road. My mom says "hey something is flying above our car , pull over" so we eventually pull over and when we got out of the car. We see a silent black triangle ufo hovering just barely above the power lines . We sat there and observed it for maybe 5 minutes it had blue green dim lights around the edge of the bottom of the craft . I went to grab my phone out of the car and that's when it rose up maybe 100 more feet. Straight up and then zoomed out to the mountains in a matter of seconds..
