Orange orb spinning and flying through west London

I was walking down the Talgarth road in Kensington. It was snowing n so as you could imagine, i wasn't exactly looking up. I do have excellent peripheral vision though. Somehow, I caught a glimpse of bright orange from the top right side of the sky. As I focused, I noticed that it was a huge, fire - bright orange orb. It was moving forward but also spinning in very tight circles. I grabbed for my phone and was only able to record it as it was heading away. I do have almost 2 minutes of video. My camera definitely doesn't do the brightness of the orb any justice. But it was as bright as fire and definitely not something that we use for any type of flyte system.
