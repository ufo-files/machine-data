{td}White sphere of light fell from the sky. It seemed like a meteor except it did not shoot, it wafted down.

Driving south on Payne Rd from 86th St. Saw a bright, white orb fall from the sky. I thought it was a meteor, at first, but it did not come down with force in a straight line. It came down as if dropped from above and trailed some sparkles. It was disconcerting because I could not understand what I saw. I lost sight of it above the trees, houses, so I turned into the neighborhood on Quetico Dr. to see if I could see anything that had landed. I did not see anything. I went back the next day. I did not see any evidence of it then, either.
