small trialgle ufo with 3 round (really bright) lights underneath it

I have not reported this incident so as to not mar the memory of it. I stop in downtown Huntsville, AL at what is called the Big Spring every night on my bicycle. It's a park that I rest at 1/2 way back to where I live. I was sitting on the ground and looked up to see one round bright, white light moving slowly from the left heading right. As I watched, 2 more identical round tubes of light appeared forming a triangle. This thing moved slowly (30 mph)and had no sound whatsoever & was very close, about 50 ft up. I looked around for someone with one of those remote controllers, for I thought it was some sort of remote controlled toy. There was nobody with one & this craft was a little too big for that. I estimate it was 15ft from tail to front (the tip of the triangle). I have never looked for ufos & was not looking for one that night: I just looked up & there it was. Another person witnessed this down to my far right & I heard her exclaim "oh my God!"...& something else I could'nt make out. It blinked out after about 20 seconds of flight time. I'm 40 & never imagined I'd see something like this & it has messed with my mind a little. My curiosity is killing me. I don't believe this was a secret government craft from the Redstone Arsenal or anything...I believe I got to see an alien visitor. Thank You, (name deleted.)fg
