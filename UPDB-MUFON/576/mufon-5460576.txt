{tjd} Triangular object seen on Rt 6 east

My wife and I were on Rt 6 eastbound. About 30 minutes before this we had seen a UFO transform right before our eyes. It was something I've never seen before. Because of this we had already been keeping an eye on the sky. 

My wife said, "Hun, what is that?" At first it did appear to be a plane. One thing I noticed immediately was how low this object was and how bright the lights at where the wings would have been. I said, "That's no plane." As the object got almost over our car it was then that the blinking red light became a solid red light and a bright white light came on at the head of the object. As soon as this happened I noticed that we didn't hear any sound at all. A plane that low you would most definitely hear a sound. I took a hard right turn next to the FedEx plant and we could see that it appeared to have stopped in the distance. We went back up the street and we could figure out where it went.

As we started back down Rt 6 east. My wife told me, "Slow down!!" I told her that I was doing the speed limit and she said again, "Slow down!!" Instead of doing 50 mph I was down to 35 mph. I asked her, "Why do you want me to slow down?" She said, "It's following us." 😱😱😱 I slammed on the accelerator pedal and took off. As I was pulling away eventually the object disappeared. 

I will eventually be adding the story on hear shortly of the first UFO we saw last night.
