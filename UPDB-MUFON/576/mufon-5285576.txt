Really shiny chrome looking sphere was in the westward sky.

While my daughter and I were out on our deck cleaning out the birdcage, we both noticed a really shiny-looking ball/sphere type of object in the sky. We both stared at in shock. We watched it get bigger, then really small and it disappeared like a blink. Then we saw it appear again this time the same spot in the sky only to the left of the same area like at a 10:O'clock location and then it moved back to the original spot we saw it and it disappeared like an eyeball that blinks. After that, we saw glitter-like sparkles all around us for about fifteen seconds. We both did not know what to think after that. I went to get my cellphone to see if it would return so I could take pictures but it did not. Very cool and strange and the same time.
