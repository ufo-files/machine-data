{cak} Multiple discs flying in strange patterns as if they were playing or engaging eachother.

1.& 2. My girlfriend and myself were just arriving at her parents house one evening at lake chaffee in ashford connecticut up on the mountain. The night was clear so we decided to look at the stars before we went inside. 
3.& 4. Although the objects were far away we knew right away that they were multiple disks flying in patterns that we couldnt explain. Up down left right and even in circular patterns, much like a fly in your house, only much larger and very high in the sky. The discs seemed to be interacting with each other while flying like a swarm of bugs, somehow not colliding with each other while maintaining this strange flight pattern.
5.& 6. We were very excited as we knew these objects were out of the norm. After watching the spectacle for about 10 minutes we went inside for the night and discussed what we witnessed with her parents.
