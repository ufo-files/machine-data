{nd} Orange glowing object with boomerang wing shape drifting west to east

My attention was called to an orange glowing round object drifting slowly from west to east, approximately on a parallel course with nearby 14 mile road (which runs east-west). The object appeared to have a boomerang shaped set of wings or structure, also orange, on the other side of the, for lack of a better term, 1sphere1. It made no noise that I could detect, but there was considerable noise in the background as fireworks were going off in the surrounding area, as it was well after dark on July 4th. As I watched the object drift into the east it seemed to start glowing more reddish and seemed to fade in and out somewhat as it got further away. At this point I gave up looking and went inside. Whatever this thing was it just baffled me. It could possibly have been a drone, or maybe an elaborate Chinese lantern, although the wind was wrong for that- I checked weatherbug after I went inside and the wind was supposedly out of the south at 5 mph, although it seemed to be a very still night with no detectable wind. I1ve also seen Chinese lanterns and they are very unstable in their flight paths and ascend and descend abruptly depending on updrafts and downdrafts (and they don1t glow a solid orange either). This object was in a very stable flight path, although it seemed to be ascending slightly as it got further away. I can1t really say what height it was nor the size of the object. 
At the time I was in a condominium complex approx. a quarter mile south of 14 mile road in Novi just east of Walled Lake.
