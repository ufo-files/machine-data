I and another musician were on the way back to Little Rock, Arkansas from Hot Springs, Ar. and observed two big orbs hovering in the valley.

In the winter/spring of 1994 I, and another musician were driving back from a musical gig in Hot Springs, Ar. The races had started and we played in a club in town. Heading back on the main hwy to Little Rock we both observed a big light illuminating the mountains. I thought it might be a medical helicopter from a possible accident. it was about 1:30 am, cold clear night and I don't believe the moon was even out. When we cleared an opening in the valley there were two large hovering balls of light.  I could tell nothing was holding either one up, nothing was around them. They were obscuring the stars. Made no sound. Looked like two suns, maybe the size of 4 moons. We just slowed down and I thought for a second to get out of the car for a better look,but quickly changed my mind. This has changed my opinion of ever wanting to encounter anything like that again. I knew it was aware of us, and that was horrifying.
