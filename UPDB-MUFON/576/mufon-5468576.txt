{cak} Saw the ufo cloak itself as i watched it fade to invisible.

I was working for the shop at the auto auction, waiting for the van driver who was in the guardhouse to see if there were any vehicles to be picked up. I was outside the van smoking a cigarette. I was watching an airliner and thinking about how clear the sky was. The clear sky reminded me of September 11th 2001. The airplane passed & I saw something out of the corner of my eye. I looked to the right and saw something I could almost not believe. The second I focused information filled my head. The silver object never moved and it slowly faded to complete invisibility. The information in part was that the entire planets atomic weapons locations are know by this race & are ineffective for defense. The capabilities of this race are incomprehensible. After seeing this I was awestruck! I told the other drivers what I saw knowing they would not believe it. It was all so surreal!
