It was like looking at a star in the sky like in a flare form

The time was 12-31-2018 New Years Me and my husband where living in Nueva Tijuana Mx, that is a section of Tijuana Baja California. It was partly cloud and it had rained early in the evening. My husband and I had watched the ball drop in New York on TV. It was around 12:10-12:20 I was outside my back yard just listening to the fire works from the locals bottle rocks and cherry bombs. I saw what to me looked like a ball of light and to my husband like a flare style like light. I told my husband 1what is that1 and as we looked at it it move toward us . I would have to say it was 500 to 700 feet off the ground. It would move toward us and the stop for a few seconds and then move again. It was coming from a southern direction maybe 1 mile per hour as soon as it was over my neighbors yard it took a left hand turn. I ran up to our upstairs patio to see where it was going. As it move in a western direction toward the Tijuana airport it dimmed till it disappeared. When I woke this morning I hit the Internet to search if anyone else seen this orb and as of now nothing,
