{cak} Multiple bright red/orange orbs of light were traveling in loose formation in Chandler AZ Sky

This happened on Saturday night (6/1/13) at approximately 8:30 PM. We were traveling North on McQueen Road and stopped at the intersection of McQueen and Riggs Road in Chandler, AZ. I was sitting in the passenger seat and noticed multiple orbs flying low in the sky from the North to the Southeast. I quickly jumped out of the truck to capture a video of the event. The video does not do what we witnessed in person ANY justice. Its hard to tell from the video but these lights were orange/reddish in color and we could not hear any sound coming from them. As they flew by us towards the East more started to appear from the North.  I shot 1 minute and 40 seconds before we headed back to the truck to follow them. It took maybe 30-45 seconds to get back onto Riggs Road heading East and by then all of the lights had completely disappeared.
