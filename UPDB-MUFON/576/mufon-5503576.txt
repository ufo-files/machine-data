glowing object white blue colour, East of my location

I was stood at bus stop waiting for my lift to work at the time, it was about 5:30 in the morning and still very dark. I noticed a light in the sky that acted oddly it moved at varying speeds and changed direction suddenly at times it just seemed to stop suddenly and then continue, watched this for a few minutes in total amazement, all of a sudden 2 military jets flew over my head towards the object, as they covered half the distance between me and the object it rapidly changed direction and moved away from jets and then accelerated so quickly was gone in a flash, at that both jets turned round and headed back in a westerly direction.
