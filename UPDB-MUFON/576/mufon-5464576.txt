{dr}Observed strange glowing object in pictures

I was outside on the night of Nov. 3rd trying to figure out how to take long exposure shots and shots of the stars as well, so I was using the cars passing by my house for reference. I would periodically check the photos to see how they were coming along, eventually after taking a few round of photos I noticed a strange glowing shape in the sky in the shape of a contact lens. The day after I found a second photo that shows the strange object. In the moment I didn1t notice anything with my eyes nor my ears, I did see the plane flying in the sky and captured that in my long exposure as well. I thought it could be a lens flare from the passing cars but when I took the first exposure there were no cars passing by. The object does not appear to change shape or have an obvious flight path.
