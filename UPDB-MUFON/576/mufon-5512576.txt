(GS) Flash of light in the night sky that illuminated the ground around me

walking down the driveway facing south, a sudden flash of light in the south sky lit up the street ahead of me for a brief second, and the light moved swiftly - vanishing. I first thought it was a falling star, but I never saw one flash a light on the ground. Almost like a bright white flash bulb in the sky. It was so unusual that for some reason I merely started to laugh as I kept walking to my car at the end of the driveway. It quickly was sinking in that I saw something quite amazing especially how the ground was illuminated.
