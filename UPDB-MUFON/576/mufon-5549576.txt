Aberdeen Proving Ground UFO

We were driving back to Baltimore from the Aberdeen Proving Grounds Credit Union where we had gone to apply for another car loan. I was driving and the object was ahead of me in the direction I was facing, but slightly to my left... high in the air, but close enough to distinguish. I noticed it first. It was hanging in the air hovering... initially I thought it was a weather balloon because we were still in the Army Proving Ground area. When I saw it dart from left to right in a manner a conventional plane or other aircraft we know could not, we pulled the car over to the shoulder and watched. It was a dull gray...maybe like brushed aluminum... windows were not evident, though it seemed quite distance away so it would be difficult to honestly say. Not a cloud in the sky, a bright, early spring day. The object maneuvered back and forth in several directions, then sped forward and upward, disappearing in just seconds. We were flabbergasted... disbelieving what we knew we saw. We did a reality check with one another... Do you see what I’m seeing... YES And I can’t believe it Since that time, I have only spoken about this with people I was assured were receptive to such a story for fear of being labeled crazy. I am now a believer. I know this information is quite old and lacking specifics such as day in the month, but I thought you may be able to correlate it with another siting during that time and area. I hope this can be helpful.
