{dr}3 fire balls silently flying from north towards space

I was walking around my neighborhood with my family when I looked up and saw 3 bright fire balls all in a line 1 behind the other, the last moving just a little slower than the first 2. The kept one directional path from north to south but it seemed they were going higher towards space. They seemed to be followed by 4 air craft that looked more like extra high flying planes. 2 had blinking red lights and the others had bluish blinking lights. One by one they got to what looked to be our atmospheric line and disappeared. Shortly thereafter the trailing aircrafts changed course and went back to the north. I'm always looking into the night sky and this was a first for me and my family. None of us has ever encountered a sight like that. To add to the experience, just last night my mother and I saw another one, just one, coming in from the east and behaving the same as the other 3. There were no aircraft following this 1 and it looked to be at a much higher altitude, disappearing just the same.
