{rjl} A trail of stars in the sky

I came outside on a clear night and saw a trail of what looked like stars moving through the sky - deliberately in one line for two minutes before it disappeared into the sky. Three or so were trailing a little farther behind and moved around at one point and readjusted their position in the line.
