{dr}blue and green pulsating light covered object flying wildly in the sky

I was at home in the backyard looking at the sky, when I noticed extremely bright green and blue pulsating lights in the distance traveling west. The lights were so much brighter than anything, and was moving at a very fast speed, when it suddenly accelerated at a 45 degree angle down to the right then leveled out traveling east, then shot straight up at extreme speeds then down behind the mountain. It was mesmerizing to behold, I ran inside and grabbed my friend to come see it. We stood outside and it wasn't there, so we waited there for a minute looking at the mountain it had dropped down behind. It then came shooting up at mind boggling speed then darted left, right, shot down then up again and behind the mountain once more. We waited another minute or two, and it never came back. It was a life impacting experience. I have no doubt what we saw was nothing of this earth. I was a US Marine i know what aircraft looks like and what they can do, and that was nothing we have.
