I encountered what appeared to be a cloaked vessel

I was walking my dog over the fields, When she stopped and started shaking and her hair standing on end. about 30seconds later i heard a pulsating whizzing type of sound getting louder and louder, it seemed to be coming from directly above me getting very close, i then proceeded to feel the air rush down becoming very heavy to breath, as if the air around me had been compressed. As i looked around to see that i wasnt imagining this, i saw infront of me about 50yards away apart of the space in front of me was shimmering, i can only describe it as been like on star trek when part of a cloak fails. i walked over to the shimmering object and proceeded to try and touch it, as i did, for a split second i felt a cold hard surface, but before i could take a longer observation the whole thing whizzed up into the sky, the air that seemed to be compressed, whoosed up after the object as if the air was been sucked away. I felt very uneasy afterwards for a long time, and i still rarely go back over that field. I have never spoke about this before now, as i never realized that MUFON existed. I did try to take some images, but like i said that object seemed to be cloaked and impossible for my camera to pick up the shimmering. I would like to know if anyone else has seen anything similar?
