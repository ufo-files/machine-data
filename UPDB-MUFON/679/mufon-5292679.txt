Loss of memor

Myself and my daughter's dad was About to get on I-35 off of 23rd and Kelly and a bright light hovered over our car we both looked at each other and then looked up to see what it was then my next memory was we were about 20 miles from Richardson Texas moving to Dallas with him being in the U-Haul truck behind me, my mom and sister in my car. I have no memory from the time that the light beamed in our car up to the time that I remembered us being on the highway about 30 miles from Dallas Texas, when we was moving.. So with that being said there is a gap of at least 2 months of memory from the time that incident occurred till my memory of us being on the highway almost to Dallas which is about 2 months later.
