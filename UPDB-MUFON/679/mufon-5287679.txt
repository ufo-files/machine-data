Large bottom part of ? Sticking out of black cloud

I live on top of south mountain pa.,elevation approximately 900 ft.I was mowing grass when the sky started to turn black,I kept mowing for some time when it started to slightly rain,so I stopped mowing and drove the riding mower to my shed.I turned the riding mower around to back the mower into the barn.At that time it stopped raining,so I just sat on the mower thinking should I put the mower away or go back to mowing.I decided for some reason to look up at the sky to see if the clouds were breaking up.The strangest I have ever seen, directly above me was a very large shinny silver bottom part of a sphere sticking out of the bottom of the black clouds.It’s hard to explain,but the black cloud which looked to be about 3 or more miles round was being pulled in from all directions to this silver sphere.Which in turn was pulled up along the side the sphere into the inside the cloud.I watch this for 20 minutes or more,it memorized me watching it,never seen or probably never will again .anyway it(the sphere and black cloud together), ever so slowly mowed in a north west direction till it went out of sight,the sky cleared up again.I went inside the farmhouse and told my wife,who thought I was making up a story,so I went backing to finish mowing the lawn. I have told this over the years to people who think I made it up,so I stopped talking about till someone said to your website. So here it is,believe it or not. Thank you oh by the way while the black cloud was above me ,the sky was clear all around its perimeter.
