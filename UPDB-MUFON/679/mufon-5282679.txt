Star like objects traversed sky from SW to NE in one minute

Around October 20 at approximately 10pm went for a walk. I have lived in La Mesa, Ca for 22 years. Same house which I own. I usually don't look up but for some reason I did this night. I immediately saw two star like objects coming from the southwest. So if I was looking directly south the objects would be 50 degrees up and 45 degrees to the right. They seemed to be playing tag as they moved from right to left in the sky. It only took one minute for the objects to traverse from the SW to the NE. Crude approximation of 10 miles traveled across the sky in one minute equals 600 to 900 mph. Were not drones, jets, satellites based on speed and changes in direction. No sound, no blinking lights. Just two stars, UFOs having fun. They maintained their path toward the NE until they were out of sight. No video captured.
