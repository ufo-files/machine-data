{dr}White-silver sphere going at high speed with one jet beside it and another above it. Went straight down the road in front of us while we sat at red light.

My daughter and I were sitting at the red light on the way to school. We looked up and suddenly there was a bright round sphere going down the road right in front of us. Two jets followed it, one above and one beside it. It was going at a high speed. We looked at each other and continued on to school. We never really spoke about it and I don't know of anyone else who may have seen this. It has stayed in the back of my mind for yrs. I have always wondered what exactly it was.
We lost sight of it as it continued down the path of the road. 
She was in middle school, it had to have been around 2006-2008. The climate was warm. 
We have an air force Base in the area and seeing the jets following it helped to reinforce the idea this wasn't normal.
