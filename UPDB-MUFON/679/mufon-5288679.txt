Silver bullet craft

This sighting was observed by a family member who wishes to remain anonymous at this time. I was on the phone with them at the time of the sighting, and they were snap chatting me two odd black birds in a tree when the craft was observed. The craft was described as “a silver bullet” with fuzzy edges, moving quickly “but not as fast as some planes“. It had a “rounded front however the tail did not look rounded”. The craft continued on a horizontal path and then disappeared prior to exiting their line of vision. The encounter lasted approximately two minutes and a 20 second video was obtained via Snapchat. My family member did not save the video prior to sending it to me so I recorded it with a second phone, so this is a video of a video. The video I have is clear but when I try and share it, sometimes it is not as clear. I hope it posts well here. The craft appears to go up and down in the video but that was just my family member moving the phone, as it did fly in a straight line. It was a sunny day, with blue skies and no clouds.
