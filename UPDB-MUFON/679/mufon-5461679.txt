Circular disc UFO seen over Blanchardstown, Dublin

Whilst outside, I noticed a circular light moving between the stars. I first thought it was a star but it was brighter. Normally planes that come from SW go N/NW and a lot faster towards Dublin airport. This craft went East over towards Dublin City. First it was flashing randomly and then no flashes as it moved slowly with no noise. I got a short video and it’s clear it is a disc that has rotating lights or itself is rotating. If you zoom in on the video you can see this. I am constantly outside as a smoker so I know a plane when I see one. There were no red, orange or green lights and no noise. After seeing a UFO last year over a nuclear power plant in Germany and not realising it at the time I am constantly looking up to the stars to see if I can spot another one.
