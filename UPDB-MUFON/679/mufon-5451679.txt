UFO Sighting on the night of July 29, 2014 in the parking lot of a restaurant with multiple people present.

I wish to remain anonymous. I had a UFO experience with my family and a bunch of strangers after leaving the Mandarin restaurant in Ontario, Canada. It was about 9:15 pm on July 29, 2014. I know the exact date and time because luckily i still have a photo in my phone to prove it. These three bright orange lights appeared out of nowhere in the sky just hovering over us in a triangular formation. Then after about 20 seconds they started moving in synchrony all while keeping the exact same distance from each other. They slowly moved closer to where we were all standing and some people began to scream and shout in awe. After that they took off at a lightning speed and that was the last that we all saw of them. i have a photo to share for those who are interested in seeing what i saw that night. Never spoke about this with anyone except for my family who was with me that night out of a similar fear like those people had from the September 1, 1969 Berkshire UFO sightings. I just figured it was a waste of time as people wouldn’t believe me.
