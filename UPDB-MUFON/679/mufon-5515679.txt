(GS) observed crescent shaped object.thought it was a balloon or kite but did not move with the wind

i was driving and noticed a glint of reflection from the sun.observed a object at first believed to be a mylar balloon.silver reflective and banana or crecent moon shaped but had a round base attached to the bottom that was deep black colored.about 50 feet in the air.estimate about 3-4 feet tall.it hovered but bobbed up and down just slightly and also turned and twisted. it did not travel side to side for the 2 minutes i observed it.i thought who would tether a mylar ballon to the ground with 50 feet of string?this was over a house in a residential neighborhood.no car dealer etc nearby.object was still there in the same spot when i drove off.
