I was standing in my backyard as I always do after work every night looking up at the stars. I was enjoying the clear night and a sky with no regular aircraft in site. I was looking due west approximately what I thought was about 9 or 10 0clock. and I saw what I thought at first was a reflection of one of those spotlights that are used at special events or airports. then it moved closer and I could see a series of lights with equal amounts of separation. it had a slight bend in the formation, and it didn't move sqaurley from the apex of the curve, it moved at an angle.with all the lights following in line with the lead light.if I had to put a size on what the mass would be I would say it could have been a mile or two wide. though I couldnt see mass just a row of lights I felt it was one solid object.it moved from the northwest to the southeast. I felt in awe and completely convinced that it was a full blown ufo as I have spent a lot of time looking up at the night sky and I have definitely never seen anything that was as fantastic a site as this thing was. this isnt the first ufo I have seen so I didn't get overly exited but it was thrilling.
