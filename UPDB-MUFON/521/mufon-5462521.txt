{cak} Large round portal looking item in the sky. Heard low boom and ship came out and traveled N/E

On Sept. 26th. or 28th.I don't remember exact date, At 03:45 I was out on my porch when I looked up I saw a large red/orange round egg crate looking light appearing approx 45 degrees up from my location. I was looking S/W. I heard a low boom and a red light came out of the center. It proceeded N/E at a very high speed. This was the second time this has happened It's size was very large, if the moon would have been behind it, it would have covered 3/4th of it. The light or ship that came out of the center was about the size of an airliner that is flying as high as it can only much faster
