{tjd} UFO sighting while driving on the highway late at night

It was 12:55am on the night of March 14, 2018 when I was driving northbound on my way home from a friend’s house when I saw clear on the other side of town a very bright green and red light. It was not flashing, just stationary. Then all of the sudden I saw that the light was now about a couple hundred yards in front of me on the highway that I was traveling on. Then that is when I pulled out my phone and started to record. I stopped recording when I realized the unidentified flying object was coming down faster and faster that I thought I was going to collide with it. I had to swerve off to the shoulder of the road to prevent a collision. I remembered looking up out of my driver side window and seeing a large black disc hovering just 10-20 feet above my car and I remember seeing white lights all around the underside of the disc. Then after it had passed over me I turned once more to look behind me and I saw it hovering off to the side of the road just above the trees and buildings and on the road I saw a bright, defined circle light that was casted from the spacecraft onto the road. That was the last good look that I had of it. I know it was not a helicopter because I heard nothing but a quiet and kind of calming humming noise. I heard no propellors and there was no wind or anything being visibly blown around me as if a helicopter were landing.
