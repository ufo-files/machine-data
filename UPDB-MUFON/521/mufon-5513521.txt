{nd} bright orange lights hovering over PGA then moved in ways not humanly possible

i was in the car on turnpike heading north with a friend when i spotted 6-8 bright orange lights above PGA hovering when out of no where they start moving in ways no man made aircraft can move(up, down and side to side).
Later throughout the trip we spot about 27 more un-explainable flying objects(not orange like the first 6-8) moving at a fast speed and moving, again, in ways no mane made aircraft can move. 
My friend saw one that shot straight up and split into 3 changing its lights from red/blue to solid green. It was a 2 and a half hour trip and we saw activity for a solid 2 hours. Me and my friend were extremely freaked out and scared due to all the activity.
I know what aircrafts look like at night and those were like nothing i have ever seen in my life and nothing we could explain.
