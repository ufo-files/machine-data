{rjl} Viewed three glowing objects high in sky. watched as changed direction several times. When airforce planes came the object went straight up really fast and disappeared.

One evening in Tuscon my husband and I were sitting on the next door neighbors front steps talking when our attention was caught by these three white lights in the sky that were chasing each other around, they seemed to be glowing white and were round in shape and we called our neighbors outside to witness it. The objects were really high up so we could not hear if they made any sound. We all thought we were seeing UFOs. Two would hover and one would go straight up fast and come back down to the others. We watched for several minutes as they traveled over this one area, and then several Airforce jets came roaring through the sky towards the objects. When this happened the objects took off extremely fast straight up and disappeared and we did not see them come back. I searched the newspapers and the news reports for any mention of the sighting, but there was nothing.
