{dr}Cylinder shaped craft with three red lights on bottom in triangle shape about 20 feet across and 5-7 feet high

I was driving to work early in the morning. The sun wasnt up and I was the only one on the road. I travel this route every day. I noticed the object because of the blinding red lights that were shining from underneath. I first seen the lights, and while trying to understand where the lights were coming from, I seen the object they were attached to. As I got closer I began to slow down until I came to a complete stop in front of the object. It was hovering about 15 feet above an empty house. Was not spinning, just hovering there. I could hear a slight hum, but it was very low. the lights were in the form of a triangle on the bottom of the craft. They did not blink. The three red lights remained on at all times. The object they were attached to closely resembled the shape of a Skoll can. Measuring maybe 20 feet across and 5-7 feet high. I wanted to park and get out. But as I pulled forward to pull into the driveway at the very same house the craft was hovering over, I began to feel fear. So I continued forward on my way. The object and lights remained visible to me for another half mile or so until the trees and winding road caused me to lose visual.
