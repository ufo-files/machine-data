{jpg} Orange, glowing ball of light that hovered and then descended behind mountains.

I live on the 16th floor of a high-rise in downtown Denver. The sunset was beautiful this particular night and I noticed what I thought must be Venus above the mountains while looking S-SW. I thought the distortion from the atmosphere was extraordinary since the color was so orange. I actually thought to myself, "Now I see how people can mistake Venus for a UFO." I got my binoculars and realized the light was in front of some lingering clouds. At this point I thought, "must be an airplane coming toward me" because the light was so bright. I went to grab my phone to see what stars/planets would be in that part of the sky. I was away from the window less than a minute. When I got back, the light had dropped significantly closer to the mountains and appeared to have a short, fat tail on it - like it was moving away from my position at an angle. I watched it for the next minute or so and it dropped BEHIND the mountains. It did not reappear.

I have a good view of the Mount Lindo Cross from the apartment and that was visible that evening. I have no idea of the exact distance, but the object was north of the cross.
