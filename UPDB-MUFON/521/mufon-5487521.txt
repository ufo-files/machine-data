{dr}Three white orbs hovered in a triangle formation for about 45 seconds. Afterward they kept perfect formation and decended over the hillside. It was followed by about 6 aircraft

I walked outside and noticed something felt off. I looked in the sky and noticed the objects. I went inside and called my friend first on Facebook audio, then that software lost wifi connection so I ran into my house and called on a phone. He also noticed the same objects. They appeared to be three white orbs flying in a triangle formation the orbs had somewhat of a glow in the space between them. The three objects hovered for about 45 seconds to a minute then Steadily progressed over the hillside until out of sight. After noticing the three orbs it was noticed there were about 5-8 separate aircrafts following the objects. The reason there were assumed to be aircraft is because they had alternating white and red flashing lights. There was a noise almost like a huge rushing wind when the orbs went out of sight.
