{tjd} Ft. Worth UFO sighting

On February 7, 2020. Around 7pm, I noticed a really bright ball of light in the sky I had also noticed about 2 days before. I didn't think much of it at 1st until I noticed it had moved a little further west & had sat hovering again for a while. I couldn't tell how "big" or high it was, there were planes flying around as usual & appeared to be above the planes still. It got weirder when I looked at the object with the camera on my phone & noticed "something" was in the center of it & took some pictures. Definitely was not like I expected... It looked like a ball of energy with very bright light in the center & some kind of black or dark colored smoke or cloudy substance that constantly moved around the ball of light like it was a part of it. The pictures I took look like any ordinary star or ball of light until you zoom in & can see what I mean... This thing was constantly changing shape & pulsating or strobing light as the black stuff kept moving around it. My mind is officially blown now & am speechless really. It didn't look like anything I've ever seen before & definitely was NOT man-made... I don't know how else to describe it any better.
