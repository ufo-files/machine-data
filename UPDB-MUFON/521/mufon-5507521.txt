{dr}Driving by open field.

My wife was a passenger as my daughter was driving. In an open field on my wifes side,(North to Northeast), my wife noticed 3 bright white lights in triangle formation, 100 yards from her hovering taller than tree top. She saw no visible object because it was dark. One by one the lights turned off and one by one the lights came back on. They did not blink or flash on and off like a typical aircraft. Today, 9-29-2013, 1:30p.m. eastern time, my wife went back by the same area to pick up my daughter and told me there were no structures in the field. My wife was really awestruck while telling me what she saw.
