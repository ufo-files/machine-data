{dr}Streaking object with NO Jet Trail!!

From 2001 to 2004 i lived on the SC Ind.Res. NM which is 10 miles NE of Los Alamos Nat.Lab. im an Artist and love to take pics of wonderful cloud formations, so at sunset i sat outside w my camera, looking West. 1st i saw a jet(w the usual exhaust-trail) heading from N.to W., passing thru 2 Separate clouds, at about 25,000 feet. After it disappeared from view here comes another shiny object(from same direction) and it enters cloud #1, but it NEVER came out/passed thru the 1st cloud!! (it somehow stayed in/behind the cloud.) And it dawned on me: there was NO ex.trail accompanying it! i sat transfixed and in about 2 min. It suddenly re-appeared and sped-away much faster than it had arrived, in the same direction it had come from... sure wish id had a video camera instead of an SLR.
