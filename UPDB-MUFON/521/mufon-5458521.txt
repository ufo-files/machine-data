{tjd} Bright, Pulsating lights over Omaha, NE

While enjoying a fire outside my home, I looked up to the west and noticed a bright blinking light. At first I though it was a satellite, but it was not like any satellite I'd seen before. It pulsated weirdly, not according to any rhythm. I took out my phone and started recording it. After watching the video back, I noticed more lights appear towards the bottom of the screen, then at about 1:14 into it another light appears towards the top. After 1:30 there's not much to see. All the lights disappeared at the same time and nothing came back.
