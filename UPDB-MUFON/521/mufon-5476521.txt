{cak} Observed Illuminated Disk Fly Rapidly Overhead

I was outside with my dogs when I spotted an object moving quickly towards the East.I had my mini binoculars with me so I pulled them out and saw an illuminated disk style craft around 60-100 feet wide.It had several tiers of domes but moved too quick to see how tall it was. It was tilted at a 45 degree angle.There was no noise.It was glowing white like a star, but once I had the Binoculars on it for 15 seconds there was also a bright blue color to the outer rim of the craft.About one minute later I saw a much smaller disk fly directly overhead towards Canada at a much more rapid speed.In the next 30 minutes I must have seen 15 small "satellites" which looked normal but had all passed over me within a short amount of time which was very unusual. Around 40 minutes after the crafts had passed over,I observed a diamond shape formation of what appeared to be 4 US military aircraft flying in formation following the exact path of the first UFO,from West to East.
