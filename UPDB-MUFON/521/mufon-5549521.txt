Sighting Report:  13MAY05 AFTER 2230, RETURNING FROM POST OFFICE,
ON A RAINEY NIGHT THERE WAS A BREAK IN THE CLOUDS AND I OBSERVED AN
AIRLINNER APPROACHING KCI AIRPORT; IN THE CLOUD BREAK A LIGHTED
SPHERE APEARED AND CHANGED DIRECTIONS SUPER FAST SO IT WOULD NOT
IMPACT THE AIRLINNER. I HAVE NEVER SEEN ANYTHING LIKE IT BEFORE.
