Triangle lights.

Witnessed three lights in the shape of a triangle traveling from south to north off the coast of Rodanthe. We were staying on beachfront property. Object took about three seconds to travel horizon south to all most horizon north before abruptly banking east and appearing to go warp speed out of sight. Not sure of elevation as it looked like satellites but traveling together. I would guess lights were approximately 2 deg. apart.
