{cak} Observed pulsating orange spherical light source travelling east to west, split into 2 faded completely.

22:02 CST 13 May 2016.

Observed orange spherical light source traveling east to west. Pulsating gently. Intensity and size roughly same as Street Lamp at 1 block. Observed from the area of Coteau Holmes Louisiana, apparent location of observed object to be over or around Loreauville or New Iberia Louisiana. ~ 2 - 5 miles. Object had apparent altitude of 100 - 500 ft., apparent travel speed > 100 mph. < 300 mph. ~ Commercial Helicopter or light fixed wing speed. After < 2 minutes observation object began to dim noticeably, then appeared to eject another object of same size and intensity which seemed to fall straight down, both objects fading completely within 5 seconds.
