Bright UFO - Light Reached Me.

Looking up at the stars last night as it was so clear with almost no cloud cover. I saw a bright white light reflecting off the inside of my window which was open and has a white reflective frame, it caught my eye immediately , I turned my head to look up and saw a star like UFO moving very fast. 

This thing was so far up in the sky, it was nowhere near being able to reflect light off my window! it was as high as the stars! 

The UFO pulsated another bright white light which looked like it was a light which was being turned up and up until reaching it's limit. 

The light was the brightest white I have ever seen. 

The UFO then flashed a red light intermittently for a few seconds , I said out loud "Flash brightly again if you are what I think you are" , it flashed bright white for the third time, and vanished. 

I decided to stay looking out of the window for another 10 mins and then left to go to bed.
