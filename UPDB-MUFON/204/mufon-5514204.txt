{cak} 3 lights hovering in a triangular formation lit up the entire sky, then faded to nothing twice before returning to a normal luminosity and just hovering. small plane appeared to pass it heading west

I was driving back to DC from a friends house in Severna Park. I was headed south on route 50 and to my left were 3 lights hovering. They were in a triangular formation. There was a smaller red light closer to one of the 3 larger white ones. I could not tell if it was one entity or 3 separate vessels. The lights got very bright, lighting up the entire sky, then faded to nothing. Then came back to a normal luminosity, then again, got so bright that I could see the clouds behind them. Then they went back to a normal luminosity and just hovered. A small plane about the size of a cessna appeared to pass the lights. Getting very close to them, although the plane may have been flying at a much lower altitude than the lights and therefore further away from the UFO than it appeared from the ground. The object just continued to hover. It/they was/were at one point in the sky the entire time I was driving. I lost sight of the object as I kept driving. I didn't really look for it in my rearview mirror because it was so high in the sky. 

The lights had to have been visible from Lee Airport just outside annapolis. I wonder if any pilots or air traffic controllers saw the same thing I did.
