Football shaped object/bronze and brown - no sound

My wife and I walk our dog almost every day. As we were walking one evening I saw an object that looked like an elongated football with a bronze color on top and dark brown on the bottom. It was traveling north to south and made no sound and emitted no exhaust. It was traveling at approximately 15-20 mph and was out of sight before I was able to show it to my wife. There was a large hawk flying in the background and the object I saw was definitely not a bird, I have not seen it since. We live in an isolated area of NC approximately 6 miles from the Tennessee border and I am a retired Law Enforcement Officer with 28 years on the job. I believe my powers of observation are as good as they have ever been and the object I saw is like nothing I've ever seen.
