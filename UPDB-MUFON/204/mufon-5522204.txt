It was about 50 to 60 feet from my head. Bright orange sphere.

I seen a real alien space craft upclose. It's changed me forever. I was frozen in my tracks and I just stared in complete silence cause their was no sound. It was like magic. This is not a lie or a joke. I'm serious.
I got a weird feeling it was stalking me or observing me. My older brother was there with me and he was in shock. We kinda lost ourselves for that 15 seconds. I dream that I go back in time to see it happen again.
