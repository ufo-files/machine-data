4 lights moving across the sky very quickly. Made 1 semi sharp turn then we’re out of sight.

On deck having a smoke & coffee with husband. Very cold outside around -20C. Clear sky. Rural area, nearest neighbour with lights is approx. 400 yds away. Porch light out so stars are very visible. Husband commented and pointed out that 2 look like they are moving. I looked & said they are but probably satellites (very common to see). Then realized there were actually 3 moving in a line together. Then noticed a 4th fainter light ahead of the 3. The front one then turned to a more SE direction. The other 3 moved slightly out from one behind the other to almost beside each other and turned to same direction as the first. And back to a kind of line following each other then disappeared into the tree line where we could no longer see. This took approximately 20-30 seconds. This covered a reasonable distance of sky. If I were to hold up a ruler from the area we started seeing the movement to where we lost sight it would be approximately 12-14 inches. One light, the 3rd in line, was brighter than the others. 
*Question-As satellite sightings with movement are a common nightly sight, we wonder if there is/was a group of satellites moving in this area around that time.
