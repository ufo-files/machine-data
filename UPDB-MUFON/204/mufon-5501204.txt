At almost exactly 12:10am July 10, we were driving up highway 97 in kelowna to black mountain. My brother and I seen a disc shaped ufo that I first briefly thought was a shooting star. It was moving at least 5x the speed of an airplane and when we glanced at the ufo, it sped off even faster and disappeared into the dark clouds. I knew it wasn't a shooting star because shooting stars bounce off our atmosphere and this was beneath the clouds, also its speed increased to that of a shooting star quickly but gradually.
