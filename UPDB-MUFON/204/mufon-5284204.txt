Solid white object flying that turned and flew towards me as I watched then was back on it’s original path.

As I was walking my dog I noticed a solid cigar shaped white object flying. It was not as high as a plane would be flying and I saw a black helicopter flying towards it but pass behind it. It was flying NW in a straight path and then it turned and was flying in the opposite direction. I knew it wasn’t a drone because it felt like the object noticed me observing it. And suddenly it was flying in my direction, the front it it appeared to glow and radiate a light nothing like I’ve ever seen before and I looked down to my dog and when I looked back up it was back flying in it’s original path. Then it disappeared.
