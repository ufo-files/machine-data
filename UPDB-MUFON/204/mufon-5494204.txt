I was traveling south on Ravenna road in the evening around dusk when I came upon an area in which there were 6 or 7 red orange globes in the sky in a line directly above the road moving north. I travel this road daily and have never seen anything like this. the globes moved silently through the air in a direct line not as high as a helicopter or normal flying planes - much lower. the cars in front of me pulled over to the side of the road and I assumed they were perplexed by what they were seeing....I slowed down to continue to observe and try to understand. the objects continued moving north and I continues moving south. I never stopped but as I proceeded home I called my boyfriend within 1 min to say I thought I saw a ufo. I proceeded home which took about 10 min. I called Twinsburg police non emergency when home because I was so disturbed....I just asked if others reported anything similar and said I saw lights on Ravenna road/.... embarrassed I ended the call and said I have never reported anything like this before and felt silly but it was so disturbing I had to do something  No incidents since but saw online that others in this area have witnessed same objects. there is a military site close by so I keep telling myself it was them
