String of UFO's

At 10:12 pm on 05/06/21 I was on my back porch facing west when I noticed a string of lights Looking North flying slowly across the from East to West and they looked about the same color as a star and white in color. I do not know the exact number of craft because I was enjoying the show but if I had to guess it would 50-100 craft flying in a single file line. What was weird was the amount of craft and that the lights on each craft only came on in the sky at a certain point in the sky and the lights shut off at another point in the sky on each craft. It gave the impression in the dark that they materialized in a spot in the sky and dematerialized in another spot. Lights were not visible from horizon to horizon and it was extremely clear Sky's with no wind and 57 degrees. I have done small amounts of flying and have never seen anything like this. If I had to judge an altitude it would be around Three to Give thousand feet. 0 Sound! Perfectly quiet outside.
