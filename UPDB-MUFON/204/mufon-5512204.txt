(GS) Triangular object with lights at each corner seen on a cloudy night in southern Connecticut.

I was sitting on y trampoline with my sister and a couple friends and my friend told me to look up because there was something weird in the sky. It was cloudy out and I remember just seeing a black triangle against the clouds with a white light at each corner. The lights were reflecting off the clouds and it didn't look like anything I had ever seen before. It was traveling pretty slowly, probably 200 feet off the ground. We realized that it could possibly be a UFO so my friend told me to run inside and get a camera. We were so excited that we all ran inside and came out maybe 10 seconds later and it was gone.
