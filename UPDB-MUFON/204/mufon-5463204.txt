{rjl} Bright light in the sky

I Was stepping out my back door to have a smoke and read on my iPad. As soon as I opened the door I saw the large bright light, approximately the size of my pinky pad, in the sky just southwest of me. It was on the edge of a cloud bank. It only stayed for maybe 2 seconds and then just disappeared. As soon as it disappeared I looked at my iPad to note the time, 06:13, and then kept watching the sky for the light to reappear. In the 10 minutes that I stayed outside it didn't reappear. I have checked almost every night/morning since but have never seen it again. After seeing it I was in awe, but it made me smile. It was more of a "huh, I wonder what that was" moment for me.
