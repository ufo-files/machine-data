{jpg} Spotted it with night vision binoculars while looking at meteorite showere

In my front yard by myself laying in lawn chair watching Peresies meteorite shower. I had a pair o night vision binoculars. Was amazed at how much more stars able to see. Looked away for a few minute and saw a couple great big meteors fall the noticed a satellite moving west to east. Using night vision I followed satellite when I noticed triangle shape with round disk at each point. Seemed to be spinning. Watch it as it slowly moved across sky from the south traveling north northeast. It slowly went out of site like going deep space and disappear. I got scared cuz I knew I really saw what I saw and went in the house. Telling story next day made the hair on my arms and leg stand up! Went out next night same time looking in same place but saw nothing.
