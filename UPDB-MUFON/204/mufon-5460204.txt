{cak} Grey in colour, triangle shape with no sound

Object was grey, triangular and emitted no sound. Hovered above power line in back lane of my parents home. After approx. 20 minutes if suddenly departed,going straight up, again emitting no sound at an incredible speed.

My father at the time was a police officer, (detective) who every night unloaded his weapon and placed it under his hat in the front close. He placed the shells in a locked cabinet in his bedroom. At one point during the event he went into the house and returned with his weapon loaded. After the sighting he told all of us we didn't see anything and that we would no speak of it to anyone.

Dates and time are fuzzy with the passage of time.
