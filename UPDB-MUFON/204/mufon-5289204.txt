it was a bright blue light flying over head

It started when my dad and I went to his moms my grandmothers house to fix a water issue she was having. she lived in the outskirts of Bogalusa, La. on about 15+ acres and in the front was a junk yard her husband owned. So my dad sends me out to turn the water off from the well pump that was about 30 feet from the house. he tells me to stay out there til he hollers to turn it back on. while I'm out there waiting, I look up at the stars and it was a clear night and out there you can see stars for miles. while I'm looking i see a bright blue light that seems to be moving very fast. and as it makes its way over me it stops and comes back towards me. and at this time i notice its getting brighter and closer to me. it gets so bright that i have to shield my face because the light is so bright and blinding. the as fast as i saw it it was gone. after it left i ran inside the house to tell my dad what i saw. he looks at me with shock and asked me where have i been i have been gone for well over 35-40 minutes, i then explain to him what i saw and he told me that i was gone and that they went looking for me in the junk yard and thought i had just decided to wonder off. i told him no i was standing by the well pump the whole time and that this just happened and when he showed me the time of it being almost 10 pm i realized something wasn't right. I have never told anyone other than family about this. and it wasnt until recently i heard about this group, and wanted to seek out others who may have answers for me on what i had seen that night.
