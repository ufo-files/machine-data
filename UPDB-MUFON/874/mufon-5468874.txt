MASSIVE black triangle with 3 lights hovered over apartment building in downtown Brantford

A few years ago I think in the summer of 2014 my mom and I were both up late and wanting McDonald's so we went, after leaving McDonald's we drove toward the downtown area and we both saw this bright light just above the buildings. We were curious as to what it was thinking maybe it was just a weird star, or a plane and we drove closer, it wasn't until we got to the street across from it that we realized it was a MASSIVE triangular UFO with a round bright white light on each corner, hovering completely still over one of the apartment buildings. We rolled the windows down to see if we could hear anything, it was dead silent and also there were no cars or people anywhere, which isn't normal for downtown Brantford, there's always people around in this area, it was almost apocalyptic, like something had stopped time for everything except for us. We were so completely fixated on this thing that neither of us thought to pull our phones out to capture a picture or video it was like something had erased that thought from our minds (because it wasn't until we got home that we were like "WHY DIDN'T WE TAKE A PICTURE!?") But we drove around the block to get a closer look except because we were so close to the apartment building we lost sight of the spot for maybe 20 seconds, when we came back around to where we should have been able to see it again there was no trace of it. The thing I found incredibly strange was, how did an aircraft that massive hover so silently let alone take off so fast without making any noise what so ever? Anyway, that was probably the most eerie night of my life, sorry I have no proof but if you Google Image search "Black Triangle UFO" That's exactly what we saw. I never tell this story because of the fact I have no evidence so feel like nobody would believe me and think I was crazy.
