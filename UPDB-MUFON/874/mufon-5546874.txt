2 stationary white spheres, one small, one larger. small one fell, large dissolved in itself.

I was waiting for my morning ride, and was looking up to see if there were any stray Orionids, even though the shower was over.  I turned to my right and saw a white ball, not moving, not a plane. At arms length maybe the size of a quarter, as I watched it, it just suddenly 'fell' straight down, no trail of any kind. Then I turned to look behind me and there was a larger white ball, at arms length maybe like a 50 cent piece. Also very stationary, I kept watching it and it began to 'dissolve' slowly within itself, til a pinpoint, then was gone. As it dissolved, it's shape was hazy as the pinpoint dissolved. The sky was black again. Neither of these spheres moved and they were not planes or meteors. Not being good at distances, they were maybe, at ground level, a couple hundred yards apart,not real high and not real low. It was a beautiful experience, and I feel blessed to have seen this, like so many others have seen. I will keep looking up.
