Still Image and videos of a pulsing and spinning light source in Eastern sky that seemed to move closer and farther away.

I saw a post asking if anyone was watching the "space station" in the Eastern sky. I am in Central Illinois and decided I would go check it out. 

My husband followed me out. I had my phone and decided to take some pictures. We saw a gold/yellow light in the Eastern sky. We assumed it was a satellite because it was a different color than the stars and a little bigger. When I zoomed in on the light, it appeared to be spinning. I took a few still images and then it seemed to move away rapidly and got smaller. Then it moved closer again. I decided to take video at that time. I have a few videos and in one I was able to catch it moving away from us. My husband and I both commented on how weird it was that we couldn't see the "satellite" spinning until I zoomed in with my phone. I came inside, posted my photo/video and it was still in the sky when we came inside.
