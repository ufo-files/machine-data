{flg}It looked like a satellite heading SE. At first was very bright then disappeared.

I was about to walk in the back door to my house. I looked up and saw what looked like a satellite but was much brighter than a normal one. There wasnt any blinking lights or anything else like noise from an airplane or helicopter. I went inside to get my girlfriends 14 year old brother. When we went back outside it was still there on a direct path heading from NW to SE. Then the object slowly got dimmer and eventually disappeared. The entire event lasted for about one minute.
