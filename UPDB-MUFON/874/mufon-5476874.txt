{jpg} Personal observation of very short duration

I was walking my dog behind a small shopping area parking lot within 300' of my residence. I heard a sound above and to the right. Through a break in the overcast, I saw a large triangle shaped object with structural surface features and lights. It was moving slowly (forward I assume) at what could have been between 500' to 1500'. It suddenly began to disappear from the trailing end, the transparency revealing normal empty sky. My initial reaction was astonishment. I immediately began to question myself. Did I really just see this? I am a private pilot of 30 years and am very familiar with just about all general aviation as well as commercial and military aircraft. I also know the typical low altitude flight and approach/departure paths locally. What I saw was outside of design and behavioral norms. The entire event lasted between two to three seconds.
