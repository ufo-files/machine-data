{jpg} NUMEROUS RED AND ORANGE LIGHTS CROSSING THE SKY FROM LEFT TO RIGHT OR NORT6H ESAST TO SOUTHEAST

on aug 22 2015 i was out on my mothers BACK PORCH, AROUND 930 PM AND I SAW these orange colored lights with red glowing fuzzy lights around the orange circle these lights seemed to be traveling from left ne to south east.just crossing the sky in front of me. these lights just kept coming from the left to the right. there must have been 30 to forty of them although i didnt count them. they crossed in front of me and disappeared to the southeast. i called my brother and mother out of the house to watch and see if they knew what the lights were.we could not. the lights were in a formation which could have been in groups of threes but we do not really know, the lights just kept coming.as the last one appeared it also passed from left to right then disappeared. I did see an airliner pass above the lights. the plane was smaller then the lights. we checked all the local aqnd national news channels and nothing was mentioned.
