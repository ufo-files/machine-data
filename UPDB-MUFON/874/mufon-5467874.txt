In the spring of 2008 I was curling my friends hair next to my picture window. It was about 2 or 3 a.m. and we were sleepless. I saw a light out of the corner of my eye and looked up to see a round disk hovering over the top of my building. I was on the top floor. I yelled my friend's name and pointed up telling her to look and just then it quickly took off then stopped over the river next to the shipyard and it shut out it's lights. All this happened in the span of 25 seconds. I called the police station to ask if anyone reported a UFO and they said no. I hung up and left it at that. A few days later my friend, who was also my neighbor, saw 2 men (she called them Gmen) standing at my picture window..my door was open because the maintenance men were in there working and that's how she was able to see into my apartment. I moved out shortly after this episode (my move was planned and had nothing to do with the UFO). I then chose not to talk about this except to only a few people...Until Now.
