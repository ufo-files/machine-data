Experience was amazing! Rainbow lights illuminated the cloud and a silver sphere beside it disappeared!

I live in Tulsa Oklahoma. I was driving down 61st street in Tulsa between Sheridan and Yale. I looked up and was amazed to see a rainbow cloud that was glowing and beautiful. It was not a rainbow just part of the cloud. I told my son to look up and he saw it also. We were both looking at the cloud and saw a silver illuminated sphere floating upwards beside the cloud! The sphere was lit up then continued to get dull grey then just simply vanished and the rainbow light vanished also! This lasted for a couple of minutes. Right after the object disappeared a jet flew by. We saw something really special, my heart is racing and I feel so excited. I was driving so there was no time to snap a picture it all happened so fast.
