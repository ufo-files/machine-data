{dr}White Light Shooting Green Lazer it would disappear and return

On Sept. 8th 2011, I was out side my home in Tucson Arizona with my spouse we were watching the sky it was about 8:30 pm. A black hawk flys by my home daily. My spouse saw a very bright light that looked liked it was being rounded up by some flashing lights in the sky which we thought were black hawks the blinking lights went away but this bright light stayed for about 3 hours we watched this light fade and return I knew we needed to get pictures of the light. In the photo you see what looks like the light shooting a green beam into the sky......
