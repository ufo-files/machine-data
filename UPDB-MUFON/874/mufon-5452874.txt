Red glowing ball about 8 to 10 inches in diameter

Object flew along side car for 6 miles at 60 MPH at approx. elevation of 30 to 40 feet. Approx. 100 feet from vehicle. Followed vehicle on city street for 1/4 mile. At this time I stopped vehicle and exited vehicle to see what was following. At this time the object circled building and came to stop approx. 30 feet from me. It emitted a soft red glow, it hovered approx. 5 feet off of ground. It moved very little, I could tell it was moving up and down maybe a couple inches at most. I watched it with my vehicle shut off and it emitted no sound that I could hear. After observing object for approx. 10 minutes I proceeded to my apartment with the object following my vehicle for the remaining 1/4 mile. After parking my vehicle I observed the object floating around the parking lot and circling the school that was next door to apartment building. I watched object for approx 1 to 1 1/2 hours as it checked out everything in vicinity. It was still in area when I went to bed.
