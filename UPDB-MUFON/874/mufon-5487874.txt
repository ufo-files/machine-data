{rjl} Saw vertical rectangle with ocasional red dome light for about 10 minutes

Was at the dog park behind Southold Rec Center in New York (Long Island). It was a clear day 10/21/15 around 5pm. Noticed something in the air well above the trees but below aircraft flight path. It was moving slowly north from the Hamptons (south). Tried to get a friend to see it but he couldn't see it: "My eyes aren't good". Watched it for about 10 minutes move north, then turn east. Structure was metallic silver, reflective, with a red light (dome?) on top that would flash ocasionally, especially when turning. What caught my eye was it was a vertical rectangle, clearly without wings. No sound. But it probably was several miles away from me, moving away, slowly. Looked up vertical aircraft online and nothing fit. Looked up vertical ufo and saw one pictured on Youtube in Mexico City dropping orbs. That was what I saw, without the orbs. No pictures. Sorry! Will bring cellphone in the future to dog park.
