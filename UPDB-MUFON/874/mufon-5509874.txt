{rjl} White Star-like object moving rapidly, would blink on/off with long pauses. Object was heading Southwest.

While outside smoking a cigarette in North Houston, I noticed a really bright blinking white star like object moving very fast, going towards Southwest Houston. The light was blinking on/ off with a long pause in between. The object seemed to be pulsating. This was not an aircraft, nor a meteorite.
