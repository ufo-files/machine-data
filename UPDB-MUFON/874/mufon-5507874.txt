{rjl} Lights in the sky

I was driving home from Surprise, AZ heading west on bell near 185th st and witnessed multiple lights to the north west of us. Two of the lights were really close to each other as if they were sitting on top of each other and the other lights were scattered about but still close to each other. I mentioned it to my passenger who also noticed these red/orange lights and within 3 seconds they all disappeared at the same time. We noticed them around 9/11/13 8:30 pm but I am not sure how long they were there before that. I was driving so it was hard to tell but they did not appear to move. They were NW of us towards the city of Wickenburg. The whole event lasted 10 seconds or so. I have never seen anything like and I have seen flares fall from the sky in the past. Very strange.
