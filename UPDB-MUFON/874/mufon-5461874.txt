{cak} Black triangle with glowing lights at each corner

Sitting at the lake front (around 10:30PM) looking westward in the sky above Lake Michigan, me and a friend both observed what appeared to be a large black triangle (maybe a couple miles away). This triangle had glowing lights at each corner that seemed to randomly fluctuate between bright and dim. We both observed it move diagonally downwards (starting west of us and moving in the Southwest direction before vanishing) the object then seemed to slow down to the point where it appeared as if it was hovering. It then slowly rotated about 40 degrees away from us (as in the triangle began to appear flatter). After this hover/rotation the object seemed to completely vanish.
