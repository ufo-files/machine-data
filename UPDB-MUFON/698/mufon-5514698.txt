{nd} no object seen; but brilliant white flashes observed that filled the sky

I was in bed at the time and was awoken by the light
flashes. There was no traffic on our road (no headlights)
and no people outside with flash lights or something like
that.

Multiple bright white flashes were observed in the sky.
The first set (possibly 2 or more) were after 11 pm
and may have been as late as midnight. I did not
check the clock for the time of the first set.

The second set of brilliant flashes were observed
between 3 and 3:15 am Oct 24. Because of the night
conditions (eyes adjusted to dark conditions), the
flashes overwhelmed my eyesight for several seconds.
It was similar to lightning but no sound and no
clouds.

I went outside (3:12am) to try to spot the source of the
light. I could not find any object to associate
with the flashes. Each time the color was a
white flash and it filled the whole sky.

Sky was perfectly clear. All of the constellations
and planets were visible. There was one
plane traveling east in the distance (it looked
like a commercial flight from its height and
direction).

I could see two small meteor trails in the distance
(east) during the time outside.

Did any meteors breakup over the northern NH or
southern eastern Canada area? Was this simply
leftovers of the meteor showers from the weekend?
Or something else?

I checked all of the major news outlets on the Internet
and did not find anything that might match up with what
I saw.
