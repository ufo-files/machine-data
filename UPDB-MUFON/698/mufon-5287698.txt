Around 7 round lights erratically darting

Around 7 round lights erratically darting behind low clouds. I watched for at least 10 minutes.  This started moments before speaking to my husband at the time and described what I was seeing to him. The address is near Ft. Lauderdale airport, however, they were not like search lights. Estimated May/ June 1994. This has stuck with me for all the years.
