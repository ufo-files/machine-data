Looked out of the window towards the South-East and saw a stationary blinking light hovering over the horizon.

I was sitting in a classroom with several others when I looked out of the window and saw a star-like object hovering in the distance, appearing to reflect bright sunlight- the weather was completely clear and the sun was bright. After pointing it out to a friend and he also agreed with my observation. The light hovered and blinked erratically, dimming and brightening slightly with no discernible pattern, before fading and dimming out after approximately three minutes.

Had it moved I would have assumed that it was an aircraft reflecting the sun. Had it lasted less long and not been flickering, I would have guessed that I was seeing an Iridium flare.
