(GS) Two bright flashes, then glowing object flying erratically.

Around 10:30 Saturday night, my wife I were lying in the hammock. She and I both noticed a yellow/orange flash in the sky above us. We thought it was a meteor. Then it flashed again. Then we noticed a small ball of light travelling quickly from easterly to westerly. It was difficult to follow because it was so far away we kept losing visual contact with it. It accelerated and decelerated a couple times, switched to a more south-easterly course and sped off. It was silent and it seemed to glow bright then dim.
