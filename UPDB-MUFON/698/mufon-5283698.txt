My friend and I were on the roof top. We noticed a light moving in a strange way. It kind of bounced back and forth while still coming straight towards us. I was terrified. I felt like I was frozen. I could sense someone was coming. It slowed down before it made it over head. It moved in a few quick movements. I can’t even find a way to describe it besides simply, showing off it’s moving skills. Because it wasn’t looking for anything. It wasn’t running from anyone. I personally believe it was saying to my friend, yes we are real. Because after the movements it made, an overwhelming sense of peace and love came over me. And it was gone so fast.
