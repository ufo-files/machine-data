Huge orange fireball hovering over retirement home low in the sky behind low hanging clouds/mist.

I was 15 at the time. My mother received a call from her sister who was scared about UFO sightings she heard about at her restaurant from truckers. My aunt was having a hard time with her divorce so my mother decided to visit her. She asked me if I wanted to come along. It was too weird to refuse.

As we went outside, I noticed immediately the low hanging orange "moon" in front of our house. I remember saying to my mom "Wow the moon is weird tonight" and we watched it for a couple minutes. It was like it was on fire behind low hanging clouds, like mist above our heads, maybe 500ft up. 

After a while we got into the car and put it in reverse and saw the real moon behind our house. My mother stopped the car and we looked at each other in shock before going out. The ball of fire was nowhere to be seen.

My aunt said one of the truckers who stopped at the restaurant said he saw huge lights flying in the sky and other hears similar accounts from other truckers over the radio.
