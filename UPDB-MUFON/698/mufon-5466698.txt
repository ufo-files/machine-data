{jpg} There is video footage on FaceBook. Search for Eagle Pass lights.

I did not personally see this event but many people in Eagle Pass and and its neighboring boarder town of Piedras Negras did. If you go to Facebook and search up Eagle Pass lights or lights over Eagle Pass, you will most likely find the video footage that I'm talking about. Apparently many people saw it happen on both sides of the Border. I'm not too sure about the time but it was after Sunset because the video shows a very dark sky. The lights are very easy to see. At first it seems like there is one night here and one might there but then you can see them making a sort of circular pattern. I'm not too sure how many witnesses there were but apparently there were many. The pictures I am submitting were downloaded from Facebook. These are not my pictures.
