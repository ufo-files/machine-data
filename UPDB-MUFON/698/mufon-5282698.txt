Something I thought was a star flashed twice and never reappeared.

I was on the front patio looking at the stars and the planes flying around when what I thought was a star seemed to just appear. As soon as it grabbed my attention it flashed a brighter white and went out. The light came back in the same spot, flashed again and went out again. That was it. I’ve seen satellites fly over and they never flash. I’ve seen meteors and this wasn’t it. I know everything that it can’t be, just not what it was.
