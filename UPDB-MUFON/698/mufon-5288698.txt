Observed in sky while having lunch. Took a series of photographs over a 10minute period.

Having lunch at Berrima NSW April 10th at 12:03pm I noticed a tic tac object in the sky.
The object was stationary then moved in a southerly direction at a slow speed. The weather was clear and bright but very windy.
The object was white but I could not determine the altitude. I took pictures with my mobile phone for a period of 10 minutes while it moved away. I left the restaurant walked across the road to my car and the object seemed to be a little closer again so I took a couple more photos.
