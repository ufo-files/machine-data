{rjl} Noticed in photos after taken

I was at a public pool with my 2 sons. One of them said look at the rainbow around the sun. I had to shield my eyes from the noon sun but could see a halo like ring around the sun. I took several pictures with my cell,phone in rapid succession. After viewing them to make sure they came out I noticed the disc like object in all of them. The disc was in different positions even though they were taken quickly.
