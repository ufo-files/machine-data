{cak} We counted 25 planes (?) flying the same path, equally spaced.

At 10:00 pm, 04/18/2020, I saw a plane flying high above us to the north, coming from the west and heading east. Then I saw a second plane. That is rather unusual right now, as there is very little air travel due to the coronavirus. I then saw a third plane. The spacing between the planes was equal. A fourth plane then entered my field of vision, going at the same rate as the others and maintaining the same spacing. There is a bright planet in the sky where they were becoming visible to me. I counted thirteen planes, wondering if perhaps military planes were heading somewhere, then knocked on the window to have my husband come out to see them. We counted twenty-five. There was also another seemingly brighter, larger one not folllowing the same flight path, but above it. I lost track of that one due to watching for others to enter the field of vision.
