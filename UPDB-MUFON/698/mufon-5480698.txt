{rjl} White object travelling incredibly fast over Las Vegas

I'm on vacation staying in Las Vegas in a top floor of a high-rise and my room is probably about 500 ft up the building. My view is due east. I was watching the planes land at LAS airport from my room. The planes are landing in a southward direction. A white disk flew on a downward trajectory at maybe a 20 degree angle of descent at an incredible speed easily covering a mile in a second or two going in the opposite direction as the landing pattern so going due north and not far from the planes making their approach to the airport. The object was about a mile away. There are other towers I'm looking out on and the object flew well behind those towers and then disappeared at about 50 ft above tree level. So the object was about 300 ft above ground level when it came into my view south towards the airport and then as it flew by northward descended to about 50 ft and disappeared. Just filing this report in case anyone else saw this incredibly fast moving object. I wouldn't think a drone could possibly fly so fast.
