I was standing in the backyard of my moms house watching the dogs when I noticed a large star doing a serpentine motion. I went inside and grabbed a camera and got a clear picture of it. I do not remember when I lost sight of it. The object was amber in color and had a reflective surface. In the picture I took you can clearly see an oval shaped object in the same shot as the moon. I felt like I had seen an aircraft under intelligent control that I could not identify.
