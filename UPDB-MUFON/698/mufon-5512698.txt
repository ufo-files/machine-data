brown silent low flying fast moving diamond shaped object in night sky

Went outside with my wife to look at the stars just after 9pm , about 15 minutes later she tells me whats that and I see this diamond shaped brown copper color object moving silently about 40 meters high traveling in a linear direction and a fast speed, the object was quite dark and the only light on it were slightly glowing amber like hot coals sandwiched between the dark exterior. I am a skeptical person and was trying to figure out what it could be, all I know it was not a bird,bat airplane,helicopter satellite, meteor, flying rubbish. The weather was clear sky, no wind, no moon light.

As we were looking if we could see the object again, my wife spotted a dark shadowy human figure standing in the middle of the street staring in our direction, we quickly went in the house and locked the door, the streetlight was not functioning at that time.
