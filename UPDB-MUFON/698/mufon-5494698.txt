{cak} Turquoise fireball which flashed and vanished.

Driving east on highway 26, just past highway 217 on the way to work near 4am. From the south moving north was a bright turquoise color orb leaving a long tail. Initially I thought it was a meteorite or comet but it didn't fade, as it continued moving across the sky and reached directly ahead of me it flashed/exploded in a brilliant glow and was gone.
