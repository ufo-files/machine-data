(GS) First 4 silver/white orbs appeared coming from S. moving in different directions. Closer then farther apart lined up at one point total 6 orbs

We were in my daughters backyard by the pool when I noticed in the sky 4 light orbs moving toward us way high in the sky from the South to north. First four, then five then six. They moved in different directions most moving west but four moved into a straight line then apart pairing off two different pairs each moving close to each other then apart. Sometimes not moving. A plane flew by at the same time and appeared to be flying below the orbs. Finally only two visible after about 20-30 minutes. No sound, and moving with deliberation not blowing or drifting.  Completely baffled as to what they were, we were excited and a little frightened.
