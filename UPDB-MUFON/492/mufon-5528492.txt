ROUND OBJECT RED WHITE GREEN SPINNING WOOBLING ZIG ZAGGING

around 1966 iwas looking out the window in my room in the attic around 11or 12 when i saw this circular craft with raised top and bottom windows with lights shining thru there also were rotating red white green alternating on its rim this craft was spinning and falling when suddenly it righted itself spinning  as it did it started to hop like astone across water i could hear what sounded like glass or instruments breaking it then stopped losing altitude but still spinning like a top it started to move in a southern direction apparently it had incurred some damage because it spinning and woobling and zig zagging as it started to move away like someone drunk was driving it.that night ihad this nightmare when suddenly i realize im not in my room my heart is racing wildly im aware that i am not alone they are trying to calm me saying they mean no harm to me but they don't speak with a voice they are in my head i am very scared and they seem to be concerned about my heart racing they tell me to open my eyes but i don't iam petrified.i try to reason with myself that this isnt real when suddenly my left arm shoots straight up they are controlling my movements telling me evrything is okay islowly slide my right arm over tosee if my brothers are there because we sleep in the same double bed they know im doing this no one was there im on a table then suddenly its morning when i first saw the craft i raced down the stairs jumping and landing at the bottom of the stairs iran to my moms room woke her up she looked out her window she says she saw the craft but im not so sure it was still visible and woobling and spinning.ihave to many stories to tell in this report i need to talk to someone my nightmares are horrible I NEED ANSWERS I NEED TO TALK AM I CRAZY PLEASE CALL ME I WILL SUBMIT TO A POLYGRAPH I AM HAVING APOCOLYPTIC NIGHTMARES MY LAST ENCOUNTER WAS THREE YEARS AGO PLEASE CALL *(Wit's name and phone number deleted--CMS/sg).
