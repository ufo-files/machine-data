{dr}perfect circle with perfect double figure 8 of the circle

I looked up and saw a movement. It did a perfect circle down toward Earth then up towards the stars and stopped at the original starting point in an approx 5 miles diameter. I am guessing with the distance involved as it was I thought initially a star. The light then did a figure 8 going Southward in the sky and then up towards the stars and stopped again and then did a reverse figure 8 and stopped then did another complete circle all in the same diameter of the 1st circle. It then remained stationary for about 4 minutes and since it was cold I went back inside
