Dull white disc flying north to south

It was a windy day and i was walking towards the mountain due west when I saw the disc moving north to south just above the top of this mountain, or horizon as it appears to me. In light of ALL the sightings i have seen that were spectacular, this one was just another sighting albeit a dull one at that of which I see them constantly and are ALL around me. I happen to be close to the SETI arrays with this sighting.
