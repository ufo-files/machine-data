{cak} Witness observed what appeared to be the same object she reported in 4 prior reports. However, there were 3 of them this time and appeared to be doing acrobatic flying maneuvers to entertain whomever was watching.

Looking out at the sky tonight, not expecting to see anything--again, seeing the object in the general area as it was this morning (see prior report). There were 2 more objects to the left and right sides of the sighting. The formation of the sighting is like a "V" shape. These objects were very active moving sideways up and down the same colors, white and blue, red and green patterns just in a flashing pattern. At one point, the object on the right appeared to tilt on it's side a little bit. With this new appearance, it appeared to be a very large (huge)saucer-like shape. I don't like using that as an analogy, but I don't know what other way I can describe it. It looks like it has a wide rim around it where the colors were flashing through it or around it. The fog started overtaking my view of the object. The night was clear with lots of stars out.
