Orb over camp lake

While slowly paddling my canoe around 4am in Killarney Provincial Park, Ontario, to get a head start climbing a mountain on the other side of the lake. Incidentally, Killarney has some of the most spectacular (white) rock formations of any provincial park in Canada. I spotted this moon-starlike orb over Clearsilver Lake. The sky was pitch black except for a few distant stars. This singular orb was eerie; too big to be a moon, absolutely so silent it couldn't be an aircraft, and lacking the twinkle of a star. At first I was concerned the orb might be heading straight towards me, but became aware it was in a fixed position in the sky and never changed its size. I was mesmerized by it. The orb disappeared without a sound as soon as I lifted my head up from looking at the lake surface to check for a major reflection- there was none. The lake remained calm, unperturbed and mirrored the small distant stars. My fellow canoeists were all oblivious to what I had witnessed and the evidence was gone by the time I asked them if they had also seen this unusual light source.
