{ck} Bright orange light, slowly moving silently witnessed by my wife and I.

It was around 10 PM, Wednesday, July 4th. My wife and I were watching the July 4th celebration with Keith Lockhart performed on the Esplanade in Boston Massachusetts. We took our dog Charlotte outside, one last time for the evening when my wife said "Chris, look!" I said "What?" She said, "there, look at that light." I looked up and right over the tree line was a bright orang light that appeared to be the size of a softball moving slowly without making a sound. I could see it moving steadily right above the tree line as it did ascend and change direction slightly. We only witnessed it for a couple of minutes. I thought the light might be flashing. My wife thought that it wasnt. Perhaps looking at a moving object at night through branches of trees, caused it to look like lights were flashing. I couldnt smell, hear, feel any vibrations of any kind. It just quietly flew over the backyard leaving us to wonder what this was and why it would be flying over our backyard. I would love to hear if anyone else saw this. Just an FYI. My wife and I have never reported this kind of occurrence before to anyone or to any medium. This is the first time I have seen something like this. Neither of us were drinking or impaired in any way.
