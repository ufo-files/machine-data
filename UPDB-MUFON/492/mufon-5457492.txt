{cak} 3 beings... about 4 ft tall... grayish , big heads and eyes

Incident happened in the summer of 1976. I was a child. I was sleeping in my bed and I saw a bright light outside my window. The next thing I remember there were three short beings standing by my bed. I was worried that I was dreaming so I touched my eye with my finger. I wasn’t dreaming. Then they disappeared in a flash that went through the window to the bright light
