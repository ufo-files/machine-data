{rjl} Bright tube-like object flying in straight line

About 7:20pm I was about to point out a commercial jet (low, just overhead & going north) to my 3-year-old, when I saw a very bright, silverish tube-shaped thing higher up and to the southeast, heading southwest. I thought it might be a plane fuselage reflecting the sun (which had just set), but it was brighter than any plane Id seen.

I watched it closely and saw no wings or blinking nav lights. There was no sound (though a faint sound probably couldnt be distinguished from nearby traffic noise).

I thought it was strange looking compared to the aircraft Ive seen, and watched it closely. Afterward we went inside and I checked Wolfram Alpha for planes overhead, it listed two commercial jets and three cessna-type planes near the horizon, but this didn't look like any of those.

It didn't get closer to the horizon as it receded (as a plane keeping a consistent altitude will do). It just kept going in a straight line until it faded from view, at about a 70-degree angle in the southwest.
