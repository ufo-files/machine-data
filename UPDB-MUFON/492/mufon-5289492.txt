Cigar shap

On this day I parked my car at my friends drive way in East Los Angeles California. When I stepped out of the car, I looked around and saw what I thought was an airplane about to crash. There was fire coming from the back end of this flying object. It was very close, it was extremely low. I that it was a plane on fire ready to crash on this street. When it came directly in front of me I could actually feel as though people were looking at me, for a split second I thought I saw windows on this craft. As I looked I couldn’t see any windows nor any type of wings, nor tail fins. It looked exactly like a cigar, with fire coming out the back. It never changed curse, it kept traveling toward the ocean. I kept watching until it was out of sight. Didn’t hear anymore about this.in my opinion, some people are fortunate to see a window open to the universe, and see into another dimension. Some people know what to do with that information, others do not. Somehow I know what to do. I think it is pure egotistical to presume we are the only game in this entire universe. I strongly believe Mars is going to reveal mind blowing information that will propel everyone on earth toward academia.
