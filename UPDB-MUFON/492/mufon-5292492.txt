It looked like a white balloon until I cropped the photo

I had taken my grandsons fishing. We saw a jet fly over & when I looked up again I saw what looked like a white balloon. One grandson guessed it was a plastic bag. I would look up occasionally & it was still there. I remembered I had my camera in the car & went & took several pictures from there. When I got back to the river & looked up, it was gone. Was very surprised when I cropped the photo to see what it was.
