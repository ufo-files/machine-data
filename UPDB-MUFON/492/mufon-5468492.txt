Sitting on our back porch, my wife, son and I were smoking a cigarette and I said what's that? My son and wife turned and saw what I was seeing. The light was low to the ground but approximately a mile away. If I guessed, they were over the oil drums (very large petroleum towers?) To the south west of us. My son instantly got goose bumps then so did i. The lights went from right to left, in a kind of skipping sort of pattern. When the left light "skipped" to the "front" of the "ufo" a new light showed up from the trailing side. I likened this to an escalators last step disappearing into the floor then popped out at the bottom of the stairs. Then my wife said it was rotating, and I agreed to her statement. We all walked to where we could see it better, while telling my soon to record it. He did not get to the video portion of his phone before it just...dissappeared! Within a minute, 4 very high flying military planes came from the east, seemed to fly over the "site", then turned to the north. (We got terrible footage of these planes). My son got sort of pissed because he didn't get to see this thing disappear like my wife and I did. I didn't know it was gonna vanish like it did. I watch helicopters, and planes of all sorts living near DMAFB and this was nothing like that, nor a drone!
