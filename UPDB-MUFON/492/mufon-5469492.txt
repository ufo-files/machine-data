{td} Flying object very erratic for several minutes. Disappeared into horizon then reappeared over us (3) for a minute or 2 & then vanished. Called the local airport and no unreported aircraft in the area. We were left stunned and in shock.

Two of us were driving in my car. I saw an object in the evening sky flying very erratic. We watched it for approximately two minutes and then it flew in a straight line very fast into the horizon and disappeared. We arrived at our destination two minutes later and very determined, pulled my sister out of her bathroom while she was in the middle of washing her hair, outside to see the UFO. The huge craft then magically appeared above us hovering and had lights flashing across the bottom for approximately two to three minutes and then magically disappeared. I looked around the area to see if others were watching it as it was a heavily populated area but saw no one. We called the local airport to see if their radar had picked up any unusual aircraft in the area and their reply was "No". What disturbed us most as we felt it had mind control over us as after it initially disappeared into the horizon, why did we still pursue dragging my sister out of her home with a soaking wet and soapy head, throwing a towel on her? Also, how did the UFO know where we were as it had disappeared out of sight before we arrived at our destination? Unanswered questions we will never know. We have never forgotten this unexplained and bizarre occurrence to this day, which took place several years ago!
