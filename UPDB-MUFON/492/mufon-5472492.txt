{jpg} A bright flashing light that just disappeared.

I noticed a bright flashing light in front of us while driving tonight. I figured it was a helicopter, as I had seen one flying quite low around the area this afternoon. This light was flashing a bright white light similar to a normal aircraft. But I did not see any other lights on it. 

As we got closer it seemed to be just hovering in one spot. I pulled in to my buddies house and saw it moving very slowly towards us. Still flashing the same way the whole time. I got out of the car and started to walk up to the porch and turned back around and it was gone. I searched around for a couple minutes but it was gone. 

About a minute after I did notice what looked to be a satellite passing by. About the same size and brightness to that of a star but it was hailing tail across the sky in a straight path. 

The whole thing has had me scratching my head ever since. If it were a helicopter or drone, there would have been the noticeable noise they put off. But the only thing I could hear in the night, were the cars passing by. And there were no other lights on this 1aircraft1 other than the bright white light flashing. 

And I am kicking myself for not pulling out my phone and recording it. But I will be on alert for another look at this I hope and will be ready.
