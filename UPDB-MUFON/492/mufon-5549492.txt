round, shiney, rotating, aprox 40,000 ft, moving south, possible aluminum coated weather balloon, not sure

I went outside for a smoke and just happened to look up. There was a very shiney object spinning moving north to south. I am close to the Philadelphia airport and I could see several aircraft in the sky and could easily see that this object was much higher. I could tell it was spinning because the way the sun would bounce of it in an interupted way. It was spinning. The revolutions were uniform as in revolving about every 2 seconds. I thought it might be a weather balloon but I am not sure if weather balloons spin. I lost sight of the ogject after 30 or 40 seconds due to some large white clouds in the sky. I of course also thought that either its a ufo or I am seeing a satalite in broad daylight due to the sunshine. I would love to know what I saw.
