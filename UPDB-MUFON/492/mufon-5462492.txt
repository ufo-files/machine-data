{cak} bright light moving slowly across horizon.ascended very fast.

I was a passenger in a vehicle the car was parked while the driver went to the shop, as I was sat facing the horizon above the moor side which was approximately 4 miles away. I noticed a solid white light moving slowly across the horizon it appeared to be about an inch above in perspective from where I was. I initially thought it was a helicopter as it was moving so slowly, it followed a direct path from left to right and continued for approximately 2 minutes. I was intrigued as to what the helicopter was doing as it was moving very slowly and there was no search light or anything, suddenly the light ascended at a 45 degree angle and disappeared.I watched it ascend from start to finish which was within a second. I was shocked and excited and began to rationalize what I had seen . It suddenly dawned on me that I had witnessed a ufo, I was very excited and was in shock. I told my driver occupant of what I`d seen but they laughed. I was about 17 years old at the time and probably told lots of people and friends and have continued to tell people to this day, I am now 57 and the memory is still fresh and vivid in my mind and I have no doubts in my mind that it was real and was not in my opinion of this world due to the incredible acceleration and change of direction. I believe and it happened.
