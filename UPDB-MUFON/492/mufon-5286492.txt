3-4 slow moving square shaped objects

During work today, a colleague called me out to the back garden as she was observing 2 Square shaped objects moving strangely and silently high in the sky, with occasional sunlight glinting off panels.
Its a clear, sunny day with no clouds and very little breeze.
These 2 objects, then became 3 and shortly thereafter became 4, they moved languidly but not like balloons. 
We observed them for about 10 minutes when they appeared to increase in elevation and fade, the whole time we couldn't hear the noise of any engines.
This is in Dublin, postcode D20. I checked on my phone compass and we were facing a direction of 35° NE.
