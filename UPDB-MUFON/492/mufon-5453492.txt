{rjl} Intense bright light in the air

One night when I was driving home from work around midnight, I saw a bright light in an open field, I know that sounds cliche. But, it was about 20 to 25 feet off the ground with a cone shape light almost as if it was an intense spotlight. The fact that it was in an open field I thought that a farmer in the area was doing some late night work with a cherry picker and a spotlight. It was bright like a flood light so I thought it was from some guy. But, when I went to turn my head to the left to look out the window to see what the old man farmer was doing, something told me to just drive. I then said in my head "holy cow it could be a UFO," and I went turn my head even more fervently and it was almost as if somebody grabbed my head and cranked back and slammed on the gas for me. I freaked out and drove away. The next day, I was driving to work past the same area. I looked in that area and there were no tracks or anything on the ground that would indicate there was a cherry picker or a tractor or a truck or anything with tires. Now, I have worked on many film sets and I've worked outside often at all hours of the day. I know there is no way that you could get a light that far off the ground, that bright of intensity, without having tires on something. I never reported it because we lived out in the middle of nowhere in southern Utah. I thought I was just overreacting. As far as I could tell, I was the only person there. I was the only person on the road that night, I didn't see any other vehicles driving around, or any lights on in any of the buildings that it was near. It was about the ground in an open field near what I would surmise was a barn. It was about a good two hundred yards off the main road.
