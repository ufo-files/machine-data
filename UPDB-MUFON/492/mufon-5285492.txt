Appeared to be a star that flickered and changed colors, brightness, and directions.

We saw what appeared to be a star hovering high in the early morning sky. It began moving in different directions. It was flickering and pulsing between white, red, and blue-white (randomly.) It would stay still for 5 minutes then move. It sometimes moved like a kite in the sky. It fell like a leaf then would fly back up. It moved around at different angles. Basically, stayed in the same general area, however. We stopped watching after 15 minutes due to mosquitoes biting us. I tried to record with my phone, but was unable to pick it up.
