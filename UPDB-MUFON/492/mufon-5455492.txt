{jpg} It came over the treetops and was completely silent. It was huge with green lights all the way around it.

My husband and I had gone for a drive on a warm summer night. Windows were rolled down since it was a warm night. We were on the way home when an object glided over the treetops. It was huge and completely surrounded by green lights. It made no sound. After it disappeared, I asked him if he had just seen what I did. His answer was yes.
