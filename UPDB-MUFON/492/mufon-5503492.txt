{cak} Oblong object seen over wooded area

I was riding home on school bus. I happened to look over the woods to the East, almost to the junction of West Neck Rd. and Indian River Rd. I saw an oval shaped object hovering over the woods. It was a silvery white object. It hovered a few minutes and just disappeared off towards Dam Neck naval base to the NE. I just happened to be looking out the window of the bus at the time. I knew it was not of this world but knew I wouldn't be beloved if I said anything. Until the last few years, I had never discussed my sighting. A few years ago I read a book called Weird, Virginia. In it, there was a discussion of UFO sightings in the Virginia Beach area in 1965. Tonight I saw a show on the History channel about presidential knowledge of UFOs and MUFON, and decided to look at the website. 49 years later, I am reporting my sighting.
