{rjl} Fiery silent slowly spinning oval losing altitude moving south to north. Seen from back porch. Went to front porch to follow but could no longer see it.

My 18 year old son and I were closing house for the night and so put the dog out to go to the bathroom. I saw the large orange light through the trees then it moved into a clear area over the uncovered patio. I asked my son several times what it was. We agreed it was an unidentified object but he believed it was much higher in the atmosphere than what I believed I saw. As it went overhead, it appeared to me to be a slowly spinning oval shape with a bright orange light below. He believes it was space junk entering the atmosphere, but it was moving too slowly, almost gently gliding or floating, and there was no trail or tail. He said it burned out when I ran inside to go to the front porch. We looked around the house in the front and back finding no debris. It was a fairly clear night with stars visible and one or two clouds in the distance. There is a nearby air force base that flies drones. This object was about one tenth the size of a full moon.
