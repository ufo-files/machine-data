A hoovering red and shape changing object seen in Edremit. Multiple witnesses

Me and my friend noticed a red and dumbell shaped object hoovering above us and we started brainstorming about what it might be while we watched it slowly going ahaed north east. Also other witnesses were nearby. It changed its shape to a ball and to a thick cigar quickly. It was about 30-40 metres long and reflecting sunlight. Also the surface was metalic but the color was always red. I regret that I didn't take any photo or a video.
