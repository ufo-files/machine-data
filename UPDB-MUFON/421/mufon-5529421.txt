2 "stars" in the sky

I had just taken the trash out to the alley behind my home when I turned to go back to the house, in the south east sky was 2 bright stars about 35 degrees high in the sky. They were very bright with one slighty lower and to the right of the other. I thought wow I don't remember those 2 stars in the sky before. By the time I got to the side of my house, which is about a 100 foot walk, they were gone. I thought maybe a cloud had blocked them so I went back to the alley and they were still not in the sky. There are no clouds around to block them out.
