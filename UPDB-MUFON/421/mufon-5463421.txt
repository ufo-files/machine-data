{cak} Four stars into one

Riding in the truck, I always look at the stars at night. There were four stars that were making a box shape. I thought it was odd so kept looking at them. They then moved somewhat slowly into each other to make one star then it stayed like that. It stayed that way until we got home and I eventually just went inside because we live in am area where bears are spotted at night.
