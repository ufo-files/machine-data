{tjd} Glowing white light in the shape of a V pointing upward

The item was spotted shortly after midnight on Oct 1, 1973. It was a light in the shape of a V pointing upward. Shortl after seeing the light and following it while driving several miles, Larry returned home, not wanting to follow it further down dark roads, a jet passed over head, most likely from Truex Field in Madison WI. This information is from a letter found in a book bought at an estate auction in the spring of 2018. I found the letter interesting and felt I shoud share it with MUFON.
