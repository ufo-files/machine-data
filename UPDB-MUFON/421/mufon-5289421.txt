Green Fireball

I was driving westbound on I-88 just outside of Oneonta, NY. It was dark (the Sun had completely set) when a large green fireball flashed into view from out of nowhere. It was traveling primarily from North to South, maybe slightly NNE to SSW direction. It was brighter than a full moon but not as bright as the Sun. I was driving through some hills/mountains and the green fireball appeared to be heading in a gradual arc towards the Earth. My vision of the green fireball lasted about 10 seconds, maybe less, before it 'burned out' or possibly went behind a mountain obstructing my view. At that time I was staunchly against the idea of UFOs/UAPs so I immediately assumed it was a meteor based on its size and trajectory. It moved at a consistent speed and from my vantage point did not appear to change direction. When I first saw the object it was directly in front of me (in the Westerly direction my car was heading) at appx. 85 degrees and in the few seconds I saw it had fallen to about 60 degrees above the horizon. It was about 3/4 to a mile in front of me.
