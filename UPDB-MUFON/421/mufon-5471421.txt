Glowing angel over water

I went out of my house to start my car. coming back I looked over the water and seen an angel. Went in the house got my camera to take picture. Lasted about 3 mins. Did not see it go. It just hovered there. I felt very warm and happy. I went inside and then looked out the window it was gone. My brother suggested I send the picture to you.
