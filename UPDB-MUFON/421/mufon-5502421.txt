Multiple Sightings

I myself was at a drive-in movie and had observed throughout the course of the night several objects that were in the south, southeast,north. They were all stationary and blinked all different colors from red,green, blue, yellow.

My co-worker on the site I work at ended up seeing the same orange-reddish orb come from the southwest and move in the straight line path to the northeast at about 9:14 p.m. He didn't take pictures but did have another witness with him, it was a person that lives here at the site.
