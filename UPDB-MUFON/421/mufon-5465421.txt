About 9:30 pm, at about 50 degrees from eastern horizon, I observed egg- or teardrop-shaped mass of light in eastern sky. I watched it pulsating slowly on & off for several minutes. Then I called my husband to come outside to see it. While we stood together for minutes, I watched it slowly pulsating on-off BUT he said he was not sure he saw it! Even when I pointed & said "now it's on ... now it's off," he didn't see it. Eventually, I got tired of watching & went to bed.
