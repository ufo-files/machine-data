UFO being chased by two USAF Jets

This happened whilst I was in high school, I was awaiting the bus to travel to class in my sophomore year, clear skies at approximately 0630 in the morning I observed a rapidly moving silent oval/egg-shaped craft traveling from the south to the north. The craft emitted a soft green glow and appeared to be traveling at an estimated height of around 500 - 1500 feet. 

I remember thinking and questioning what the object was, when I heard additional craft approaching from the south, I turned to observe two military craft in pursuit, as they passed overhead I identified them as F-18s. They were low enough for me to clearly see the pilots as they passed over. 

I thought I had reported this sighting years ago however when checking the records of reports this was not listed so I am reporting it at this time.
