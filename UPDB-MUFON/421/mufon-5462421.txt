{rj} Irregular, rocky shape, like a cartoon asteroid. Overall dark colored with red/orange glowing areas

I was getting something from the car at about 9 PM. I looked up and noticed an object moving in a straight line path at about the speed and altitude of a low-flying commercial plane. The object was dark but had areas or spots of glowing red/orange. The shape was irregular like a rock, it was not geometric. The glowing spots were also irregularly oriented. The object looked like a cartoon or illustration of an asteroid burning up in the atmosphere, but I know that's not how they actually look from the ground. There was no trail, tail or exhaust. I was confused and curious and stood still to watch the object until it passed out of view over some nearby trees.
