{cak} Flashing light at high altitude II

I go out almost every night to retrieve my radar detector from my car between 12:00 AM and 1:00 AM, and I have been checking the sky every time I do to see if I can again see the light I saw and reported on 10/24/17. Although the skies over Albuquerque have been clear, as they usually are, I had not seen this light again until 1:00 AM on 11/12/17. It was in the same position as the first time I had observed it, SE of my location on Louisiana Blvd. NE, and was presenting exactly the same as it did the last time - stationary; blinking red, blue, green, and white; no sound; and elevation at about the same as commercial airliner.

I observed the light for 20 to 30 minutes and took some video footage of it before going back inside. It was still there when I left.

It was too high in the sky to be a helicopter and it was stationary, so it couldn't have been a fixed wing aircraft. It was too low in the sky to be a planet, a star, or some other celestial body. Moreover, I did not see it from 10/24 to 11/12, and if it was a planet, a star or some other celestial body, I would have seen it every night between 10/24/17 and 11/12/17 since planets, stars and other celestial bodies don't just appear, disappear, and reappear again at a later date; they are typically in the same place every night. And there has not been any significant overcast between the days I observed it that would have obscured this light if it was a planet, star or other celestial body.
