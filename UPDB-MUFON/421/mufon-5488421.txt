{nd} I was driving and felt euphoric while looking at the clouds so i snapped a photo of it expecting to see just clouds

I was driving towards Colonial Heights Virginia on Highway 144, coming to the streetlight at the intersection of 144 and Puddledock Rd. I felt the most welcoming feeling, which drew me to look at the clouds, euphoriic. So I snapped 4 photos of the cloud coverage, and went on my way, thinking nothing else of it until I got home and looked over the photos I had taken. In only one of the four photos, there are two orbs that appear, blue in color and almost perfectly round, I would say oval. For some reason, even though I didnt see the orbs yesterday when I snapped the photo, I can still tell you they were headed west.
