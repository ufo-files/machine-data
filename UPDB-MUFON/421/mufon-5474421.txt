{cak} Green orb moving fast and erratically. Dove down to horizon, then saw another white orb NW higher as well in video during filming lightening storm

I woke up because the thunder was very loud outside. I looked out the window and thought I saw something green in the sky, so I grabbed my cellphone camera at 6:20 AM and began filming the lightening storm. I first focused to my left where I thought I saw a green light in the sky and sure enough, though hard to make out with my naked eye, I could see the green orb moving about very quickly and erratically, definitely not an airplane. It zipped to the right then dove down almost like a meteor burning out, but then it reappeared again, zig-zagging around. As I panned across the sky during the storm I picked up another orb, which I didn't see until I reviewed my video and zoomed in to take a closer look at the green orb I had seen initially. It was more white and also made similar movements going from high in the sky, sideways, up and down, even visible during lightning flash. During the sighting, I couldn't believe what I was seeing, I even thought I was misinterpreting a speck of dust on my cell phone or after affects of lightning on my eye, but I had my fiance watch it when he got up and he saw them both as well without me pointing out what I saw. I was very excited as I have never seen something like this in my entire life, but have always believed ETs to be real and very present. After seeing it I feel truly blessed. I felt very happy and oddly enough "playful" is the only word I can think to use to describe what I saw and how it made me feel. Soon, I didn't see the green orb anymore, nor the white one so I stopped filming.
