September 25 about 10:15, my friend called me out on to the balcony facing the ocean. She pointed to an object in the sky. We were staying in an apartment in Hilton Head Island on South Forest Beach Dr. so the balcony faces SE. The object was shaped like a deflated football and was multicolored 1 red/orange and white. It was to our right but overhead traveling from East to West. We watched for probably 3 minutes until it disappeared hidden by roof tops and trees. There was no sound and the lights and moving were steady.
