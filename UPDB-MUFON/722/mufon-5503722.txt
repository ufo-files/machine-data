{dr}orbs descending toward greenway, became one and move toward bay plaza

About 6 am I look out the window, clear morning and I could see a couple of miles out toward Bay plaza shopping mall, I notice one orb of light decending toward the green way about 100 to 150 feet from my window. The orb was not producing a lot of light however it look like a solid mass. After a second I notice two more orbs moving toward the first one, one coming from the high school the other from the building across the greenway. All orbs became one this about half way in the green way and move toward the Bay plaza shopping mall, I lost contact about a 10 second later. This is not the first time I have notice orbs around areas where I lived. I have seen this type of orbs in, Puerto Rico, Virginia, California, Georgia and New York.
