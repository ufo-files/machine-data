There were two UFOs...flying in opposite directions from each other and nearly intersected at one point.

I was up in my terrace smoking a cigarette. Just as I was looking at the stars, I saw two lights moving . They also went very near each other since they were moving in opposite direction, but one from the right and one from the left. And both of them disappeared after some seconds. I have had UFO sightings plenty of times before both here in Nepal and back in 2012 when I was in the US. But this is the first time, I am reporting the sighting.
