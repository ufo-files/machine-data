Glowing orb pulsating/blinking red/white & disappeared/reappeared 3x

Was at a friend's house waiting for fireworks to start at 10pm. At 9:59 this glowing red orb appeared lower than the tree line to the east about a street away. It moved around, up and over the tree and then disappeared.  It reappeared (or a different one maybe did? ) looking west & then that one also disappeared. It reappeared one more time in the east and then disappeared again. My boyfriend, my 13 yr old stepdaughter & my friends sister all saw it. My friends sister is a skeptic so she kept suggesting maybe its a plane. You can hear me say "nope!" In the one video because it's clearly technology we don't have.
