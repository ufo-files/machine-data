Object appeared first as big orange orb in sky then morphed into about 6 balls of light before taking shape of some craft and flying

While at the pier with my girlfriend, right off the Melbourne causeway in florida looking towards the Eau Gallie causeway we both noticed a large orange orb looking object high in the sky. Once I started to record it it looked like it turned into about 6 different balls of light and then kept morphing while moving at the same time. It flew over close to us and all we saw was 3 big white lights at this time until it got closer and flew overhead us, it seemed to be some sort of craft, large in size but was making not a lot of noise like what you’d expect from a plane or helicopter but it sounded like it was just hovering over us almost making a humming sound. While I was trying to look up helicopters or rockets near by my girlfriend was watching the craft when she noticed it just disappeared along the water line
