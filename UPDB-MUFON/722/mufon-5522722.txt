Driving west near Pipestone, I90, looking towards homestake pass, sse of Butte

I was driving west just after sundown and witnessed a glowing, pulsating white light ball very very slowly ascending vertically, including in front of clouds that were less than 25 miles away. Once it reached the cloudline, it appeared to go out like a light. Camera review shows it rapidly accelerated westward, through the cloud, away from my position. This comes two days after a previous experience on the other end of town with a glowing/flashing red/orange orb, which I have seen in a pack of 10 before, but both stories are for separate reports. With regard to this incident, there were hundreds of potential witnesses traveling Westbound on interstate 90 between Cardwell and Pipestone. I do not know if object was visible from Butte on the other side of Homestake Pass, where this was ascending near.
