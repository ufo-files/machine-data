I was around 12-13 years old this was about 20 years ago. I was walking down a small side street with a another kid my age sadly I don't remember his name but it was not just us that saw we called to other friends playing and said to them look at what we were looking at. I don't remember who saw it first. But I remember exactly where I was. We saw what I feel now must have been fairly big a gold glowing cigar shape craft floating in the sky at first not seeming to be moving then slowly drifting to the left of where we were then out of sight. It seemed to have whitish circle windows also glowing like the rest of the craft which glowed all over. It was no blimp I've seen many. The time was afternoon I had finished school and was off to our local woods and park to play it was still day time but you could still clearly see this this glowing up like it was made of or powered by gold. It made no sudden movement's no speed or shooting off out of sight it just kind of sneaked out of view. I know what I saw I've never seen anything even remotely similar since id happily take any lie test hypnosis what ever.At the time I just remember thinking it was cool and unusual. But that was it I just carried on with my life but the last few years for some reason it's bothering me more and more I'm just glad I was not alone at the time and others saw this to.
