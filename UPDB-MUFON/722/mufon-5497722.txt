{rjl} meteor like object moving slowly across evening sky with flaming tail

I was in my driveway and glanced up at the evening sky while unlocking my car door and noticed an object similar to a meteor as it had a long flaming tail moving constantly but slightly left to right and back again as it crossed above but moving much slower than a meteor as it crossed the early evening sky. It would be hard to determine with certainty how high this object was above us but I would guess the object was at least a mile or above the ground it seemed to be arching slightly downwards as it crossed the sky. No sound was heard as the object passed overhead. I mentioned to my neighbors and their friends who were also outside if they are seeing what I do and all confirmed my sighting. Five others besides myself watched as the object crossed the sky. The object took 5 or six seconds to exit our field of view but just before doing so behind the rooftops of nearby houses the object made a sudden change of direction before disappearing beyond our field of view.
