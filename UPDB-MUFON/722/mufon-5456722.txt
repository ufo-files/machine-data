{rjl} Bright light came over the mountain range

Driving from Anchorage to Fairbanks is a trip I've done several times. I had just passed through Cantwell and was continuing up to Fairbanks about Mile 235. I see a yellow/white light come over the mountains to my left, stop and hover in front of the pickup, and shoot straight into the sky. Due to the darkness, I have no way of knowing anything about the craft except that it had a single light on underneath it, and it flew straight up at a high rate of speed. I drove the rest of the way to Fairbanks with my camera on and have done the same since.
