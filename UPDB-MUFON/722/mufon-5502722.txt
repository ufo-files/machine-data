I was a c141 flight engineer in the air force reserves working off duty at MacDonald Douglas aircraft I left work at 1210  to San Bernadino ca approaching grand terrace ca I saw two ht lights ahead above the freeway at first I thought it was two c141s out of Norton AFB. Flying in formation as I got closer I realized the lights were not moving I thought maybe two choppers as i crested the hill I thought somethings wrong so I pulled to the side of the road wound down my window the lights were almost above me I put my head out the window shut off my engine no sound at all. I got out of the car. That's when I saw a third light about a half mile to the west. The light where connected to a huge float triangle flat black 3 stories high it was hard to see against the dark sky and the bottom seamed to be morning the sky above I stood and watched it for about 5min. At fist I thought maybe air blimp as it floated to the west or maybe a hologram. I started to get worried what is this .1	2 years in the Air Force never saw an air craft like this as I pulled away I saw it shoot off to the south west .it seemed like the star ship enterprise
