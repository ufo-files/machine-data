{jpg} I was standing outside with my fiance and our neighbor when I saw a star-like object in the sky. It was very bright at first glance, but once I pointed it out and we all saw it, the lights dimmed and it seemed to vanish or evaporate.

I was standing outside our home with my fiance, visiting with our neighbor. It was a nice night, not too hot and there was some lightning coming from the eastern portion of the sky but it wasn't too cloudy, so I decided too star-gaze like I do from time-to-time. It was at this point that I noticed what looked much like a bright star, only it was moving at a fairly decent speed, so I knew it couldn't be a star. I was facing east, and it was moving right (not exactly in a perfectly straight pattern, but kinda wavy). When I pointed it out, my fiance and our neighbor then caught sight of it as well and we watched the bright, star-like object move until the light dimmed and then completely disintegrated/vanished from the sky. I was very surprised at the sight of it, as I have never seen any plane, jet, helicopter that looked anything like the object in the sky. If it weren't for the fact that it was obviously moving, it could have blended well with the rest of the stars in the sky. Also, I don't know if mentioning this is relevant at all, but my hair became very static-ridden at least an hour or so before the sighting. I feel very unfortunate that none of us had any type of recording device ready, but at least I wasn't the only one who saw it. I would never lie, let alone about something like this.
