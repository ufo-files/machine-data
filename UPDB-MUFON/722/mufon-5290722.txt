Injected with transmitting implant

Jan 10 2018
At the beginning of the night all i can remember is my friends asking if id like to accompany them to cap hill to hit some bars. I said no as i wanted to have a quiet night and had worked a hard day at a tile store in georgetown where i was employed at the time. After they left i remember standing by the island in the kitchen at which time i was somehow made unconscious. I have no reference of how long. I woke up and as soon as i stood i felt a feeling like pin needles all through my face. I laid back down and lost consciousness again. The next day i had i welt on the back of my head and a open cut. The only reason i came to realize i was implanted is the transmission of the implant which was voices and some type of brain stimulation in weeks following. The transmitted voices can translate from all sounds but seem to be adjusted by bandwidth of frequency. I understood this because i spent years working as a sound tech for multiple production companies. I have recorded the transmission and taken video that shows the very distinct articulation of speech.
