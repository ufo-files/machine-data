{rjl} star-like, blue white, circular or orbicular, steady moving in a straight line NE before gradually fading

On Friday evening, I was giving my dog her evening walk and enjoying the clear sky when I noticed what I thought was a slow moving star. I stopped walking so that I could gauge whether the star was in fact moving, or just playing a optical trick on me. Using another star as a reference, I observed the blue-whitish circular or orbicular light slowly move across the night sky away from my reference star. I have no clue how big or close the light was, as the only other objects in the sky were other stars, but it was definitely far enough away that it cast no light on the surrounding area or houses, and it made no audible sound. I observed the object move at a steady slow pace northeast before gradually fading as if it were on a dimmer, and eventually disappearing completely. I stuck around about twenty extra minutes waiting for something else to be seen but I did not observe anything else. I am not sure what I saw honestly.
