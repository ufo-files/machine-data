{cak} It was around dinner time, we had a very unexpected monsoon storm that rolled in. I saw the craft shoot straight up to 3500 ft and blast west just in the cloud base.

This event took place at the end of May 2012 or the beginning of June 2012. It was a very unexpected monsoon storm with violent lightning. It was Approximately 5:30pm. The sky was black. It was an incredible storm. My son and I were looking towards the east valley over towards the North Valley Mountain Preserve. Together we observed a hugh white ball rise up directly into the sky. We both exclaimed, what the hell is that? I knew, and he knew it was much too large to be a helicopter. It climbed just to the level of the clouds, so it could not be clearly seen, and immediately shot west, towards us. As it passed over me I could clearly see the light surrounded the object. It was extremely bright and moved very quickly. It was seconds from the I first time I saw it, until it passed my house. The sky was so black and the cloud cover was very heavy. Also there was a an abnormal amount of lightning and it had just started to rain. Once the object reached my house and passed, I lost sight of it because of the weather, its position in the clouds and its speed.
