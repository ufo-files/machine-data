(GS) Two objects hovered in the north part of the sky.

The two objects hovered in the northern sky shifting direction slightly, first to the east and then to the west. The two objects seemed to have merged and started rotating together slowly. They were watched until it became too dark to see them. They were observed with the naked eye and with a 20x monocular.
