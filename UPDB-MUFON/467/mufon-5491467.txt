Fast triangular object flys past star whiles tv show

I was watching the tv show "stargazing" by Brian Cox, when I was looking at the stars and then suddenly a fast object flew past at about 2/3 seconds and it caught my eye. So I rewinded the tape on SKY and then I put it in slow mo and recorded it on my phone and I managed to stop the tape perfectly in line with the star and then the object appeared as a triangle. perfect in size with the star also.
