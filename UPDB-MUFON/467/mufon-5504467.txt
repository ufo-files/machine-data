{nd} camping at boca lake next to Truckee an object as big as the mountain flew directly over

My friend and I were just getting in our sleeping bags in the back of a truck. There were about twenty of our parents friends drinking and partying around camp fire. When an ufo came over the mountain that was across from the river we were camped on. There were no trees on that side of river it was a bare hill. This object was enormous! Bigger then the mountain it came over. It proceeded very slow over mountain and directly over our camp., it had triangular shaped lights all over the bottom, as though there were smaller triangular shaped ships on bottom. The lights were red green and yellow triangular patterns. With white lightest around them. It simple made a humming sound and flew rite over us, there were pine trees on our side so rite after it went over us we could not see it anymore. No one remembered this object the next day in fact for two weeks , until I guess our memories came back. And we all started talking about it.
