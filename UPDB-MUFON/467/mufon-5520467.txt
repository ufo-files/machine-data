blue lights over san diego coast on december 31, 2010

MY HUSBAND A FRIEND AND I WERE DRIVING ON INTERSTATE 5 NORTHBOUND NEAR THE SEA WORLD EXIT ON 12/31/2010, IT WAS NEW YEARS EVE, AT AROUND SUNSET. MY HUSBAND POINTED OUT A LIGHT HOVERING ALMOST DIRECTLY IN FRONT OF US IN THE SKY, THOUGH IT WAS MANY MILES AWAY FURTHER DOWN THE FREEWAY. IT WAS A BLUE LIGHT, LIGHT BLUE LIKE THE. COLOR OF THOSE LED BLUE LIGHTS. I NOTICED RIGHT AWAY WHAT HE WAS TALKING ABOUT, BECAUSE IT WAS NOT NORMAL TO SEE THAT KIND IF LIGHT IN THE SKY. I KNEW IT WAS SOMETHING SPECIAL, NOTHING MANMADE THOUGH.I WAS HOLDING MY PHONE, BUT I DIDNT WANT TO TAKE A PIC CAUSE I DIDNT WANT TO TAKE MY EYES OFF OF IT. THE LIGHT, VERY QUICKLY MOVED FROM IN FRONT OF US TO WESTBOUND, WITHIN SECONDS IT WAS OVER BY WHAT I WOULD ASSUME WAS THE WATER OVER LA JOLLA, BUT IT REMAINED IN SIGHT. IT HOVERED THERE FOR A FEW SECONDS AND STARTED DOWN THE COASTLINE, AND AS IT DID IT CHANGED FROM ONE LIGHT, INTO TWO. THE TWO LIGHTS BOTH STILL THE SAME BLUE COLOR AND SIZE STARTED WEAVING OR WHAT I HAVE BEEN DESCRIBING AS FROLICKING WITH EACHOTHER, ALMOST AS IF PLAYING WITH OR RACING EACHOTHER TO THE LEFT OF US DOWN THE COAST. I HAD TO TURN AROUND TO SEE THEM BECAUSE THE VEHICLE I WAS IN WAS MOVING FREEWAY SPEEDS. I WATCHED THE LIGHTS UNTILL I COULDNT SEE THEM ANY FURTHER. I JUST WANT TO KNOW IF ANYONE ELSE HAS SEEN THIS OR REPORTED IT BECAUSE I CANT IMAGINE WE COULD HAVE BEEN THE ONLY ONES. I ALSO WONDER WHAT IT COULD HAVE BEEN, I MEAN OBVIOUSLY IT WAS A UFO TO ME BECAUSE I HAVE NEVER SEEN SUCH A THING, NOR CAN I IDENTIFY WHAT IT WAS. I NOW KNOW THERE ARE SUCH THINGS AS UFOs!
