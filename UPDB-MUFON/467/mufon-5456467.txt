Wobbling flat disc with modules arround the edge

At 5.20 pm Tuesday 4th June 2019 i was standing at the bus stop opposite Tesco supermarket, Mutton Lane, Potters Bar. Hertfordshire, EN6 2PB. 10miles north of London.weather dry with showers earlier. 100% light grey cloud cover very low altitude maybe 500 to 1000 ft. i could clearly see the top of the nearby Maple house office tower block which stands 200 ft and the cloud base was well above it
When i looked up about 45% south east and saw running along the bottom of the cloud a Silent dark grey flat disc around 100ft in diameter wobbling dramatically like a spun plate on a stick that had slowed and about to fall off. flying in an aerodynamically unstable way.
With what looked like around eight, half circle, square, rectangular and triangle modules around the edge. it travelled across the sky in a straight line from south east to north west horizon taking around one minute to do so, i would estimate the speed at a constant 40 to 80 mph. wobbling at about one rotation every 5 seconds. the wobble extending as much as 45% so giving a good view of its shape which was circular.
Although as much as 100 ft in diameter it was very thin at only 3 to 5 ft thick.
with the modules around the edge being around 5 to10ft wide, thickness also only that of the disc. My impression was whatever was piloting it was not aware it was flying just below cloud cover and could be seen, if it had flown just 10 feet higher it would have been hidden by cloud
