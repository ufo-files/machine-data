Bright, unwavering source of light, with 2, smaller trailing lights, eventually passing directly overhead

I'm an experienced astro-photographer, so the night was clear and moonless, as I completed my imaging set-up. Noticed object, low in east, northeast portion of the sky. Object, initially, seemed stationary, but slowly increased in size over a short period of time. I waited to detect a sound, but none was ever detected during the encounter. Out of curiosity I flashed my red-filtered pen-light in the direction of the object. Shortly afterward, I noticed the object increasing in size at a faster pace, moving toward me. As it moved closer, I noticed two, trailing lights, one to the left, the other to the right of the forward, bright light, approx. 40-60 ft. apart, giving the sense of a triangular shape. As the object passed directly overhead, I didn't notice any sound, or means of propulsion... silence. I turned to follow the objects trajectory toward the southwest...toward the town, but my field of vision is limited in that direction.
