two pin point lights, one seemed to be firing a tiny red dot at the other

I was on my patio staring up at the sky around 8:30-9:00 central on the night of August 9, 2020. I saw two ponts of light very high and moving at a very rapid pace. I initially thought they were satelites as we often see evenly spaced satelites moving across the night sky. However, these two points of light were moving toward each other. One point of light started to move eradically and moving back and forth in the same way a snake crawls. The second point of light was heading directly for the other and emmitted a tiny red dot that appeared to be "fired" or "shot" at the other. After the first shot, the "firing" aircraft moved in a spit second to a new position which allowed it to "fire" from a side angle. I could not believe my eyes, I went inside to get my wife and of course when we returned they were gone.
