Flashing 'star-like' object

On Friday night, June 27, 2014 we drove into our driveway and my wife pointed out some "beautiful stars." There was no visible moon. I looked up, and saw a white silver-dollar-sized flash up there among the stars. We then noticed a red-orange dot travelling from east to west. It looked like a star. Then it flashed again. As we watched, we noticed it would flash every ten seconds regularly. It went quite a distance horizontally. My wife exclaimed that it was like having someone photograph us with a flash camera. All in all we counted five such flashes. Then the object abruptly turned and headed south. It stopped flashing once it changed direction. The flashes we had witnessed were much bigger that the typical strobe on a airplane. We watched it travel south until it passed out of sight.
