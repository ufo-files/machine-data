Large, Very Bright, Orange Pulsating Ball (ORB?)

At 6:00 am I saw a Large, Very Bright, Pulsating Orange ball (ORB?) in the Western sky. It was pulsating very fast. and it moved several times from left to right, then back right to left. I felt unsettled looking at it.
 I watched it for about 15 minutes then it got smaller and smaller and disappeared. 
NOTE: There is something in the sky here many, many nights that is bright like a huge star. When it is close it looks like it could be rotating. The flashing lights are green and white or red and white. It is usually in the western sky, but I have also seen it in the southern sky. It does move sometimes up and down, back and forth. It is here several hours, but not all night. I do not have exact specifics to post this on its own page.
