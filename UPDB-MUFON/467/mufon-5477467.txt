I was in the backyard having a cigarette with my girlfriend looking up at the night sky.Suddenly a bright light appeared in the NE sky and quickly streaked to the north for a split second and then disappeared. We both took notice and wondered what it was. We continued to look in the same area of the sky discussing what it might have been. After about 15 seconds it appeared again in the same spot it had disappeared the first time. The light again streaked to the north, this time for a full second and again went dark.we both saw the same thing and it did not act like a falling star or comet. It was the brightest thing in the sky and moved very very fast. Many times faster than any plane. It was clearly the same object both times as it followed the exact same path after a brief moment of not moving/going dark and then picking up again. It gave me a bit of a chill because nothing is that bright or moves that fast. Especially with the two burst/streaks. Nothing like I've seen before and I am convinced it was not a falling star because I've seen them before, and not a meteor because they do not stop and start again from the same place in the sky or turn off an on.
