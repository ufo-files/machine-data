Slower than meteorbright blueish/white, exploded

I just laid down for bed, and have no curtains in my bedroom windows. My wife just closed her eyes and I was just staring out front window, which faces south east. A bright bluish whitish irregular shaped orb "slowly" moved its way across my view, then seemingly exploded lighting up the entire sky. Absolutely no noise could be heard. Object was traveling much slower than a meteorite, and seemed to be traveling horizontally. I'd put it about 45 degrees above horizon. I was sort of confused by what I was seeing and by the time my wife had opened her eyes, the eent was well over.
