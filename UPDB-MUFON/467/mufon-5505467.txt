{cak} very silver and very bright

I was in my room looking out the window when suddenly something appeared in the night. It was dimmer than all the rest of the stars. I watched it for about 10 minutes. It was in the some spot for a long time. Then suddenly it grew brighter and brighter. Soon (about 5 secs.) it was brighter than all the other stars! It looked like it gained a little elevation and zoomed off like a rocket. I felt really freaked out so I went to sleep and tried to get it out of my mind.
