{tjd} Large oval disc above Oak Ridge Marina...I was heading that direction when it vanished

I had just left Books A Million and had driven up to the Waffle House to turn onto Illinois Ave to go home, when looking across the street at a 45 degree angle to the right horizontally and above the Subway was this large oval shaped disc sitting motionless about an inch or so above the corner of the building in a cloudless sky reflecting the late afternoon sun and appearing to have a skirt across the bottom that was partially in shadow. Being ex-military and having a private pilots license, I estimated it to be 3 miles away over Oak Ridge Marina and a mile North of Bull Run steam Plant. I started driving in that direction but had to watch the traffic in front of me so that when I looked back after about 10 seconds it had vanished
