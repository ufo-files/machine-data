TWO DISKS SIDE BY SIDE COMING FROM THE EAST,THEN TURNED NNW .ONE REFECTED THE SUN,THE OTHER WAS A DULL GREY .

WHILE OUT SIDE I SAW COMING FROM THE EAST,TWO DISK SHAPED OBJECTS.THESE WERE ABOUT THE SIZE OF A PENCIL ERASER.THEY WERE CLOSE TOGETHER.IT SEEMED THAT THEY WERE TILTING IN THE SKY.ONE WAS REFLECTING THE SUN,BUT,THE OTHER WAS A DULL GREY THEY SEEMED FAST.BY THE TIME I GOT MY CAMERA I WAS ONLY ABLE TO GET A DOT IN THE SKY.I HAD TO DARKEN THE PICTURE SO AS TO SEE THE OBJECT.
