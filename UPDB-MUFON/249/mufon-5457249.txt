{jpg} A bright green light moving very fast over Richland

Around 8:50, Travis and another witness were traveling northbound on 240-W, about a half mile from George Washington Way. The first witness saw a bright green light within a mile away. It swooped down, then up and away. It was initially going Northwest, then changed turned West. The witness said "Shooting star," then Travis looked and saw the object going away from them, now about 5 miles away, headed West, very fast. They watched it for a few seconds, then it vanished.
