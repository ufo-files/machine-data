{dr}ORANGE RED FIREBALL ORB SILENTLY MAINTAINING ALTITUDE FROM SOUTH EAST THEN CHANGING DIRECTION TOWARD EAST

MY GIRLFRIEND AND I LOOKED OUTSIDE AT 12:03 AM 1-01-13 AND OBSERVED FROM PORCH A GLOWING FIREBALL ORB SILENTLY MAINTAINING DIRECTION FROM SOUTHERLY DIRECTION MAKING A HALF CIRCULAR RIGHT TURN EAST AND THEN SOUTHEAST OVER HALL ROAD (M-59). THE FIREBALL MAINTAINED ALTITUDE AND SLOW SPEED FOR AT LEAST 3 MINUTES. THIS WAS WITNESSED BY 2 PEOPLE. THERE WAS NO SOUND AND ORB LOOKED AS IF ON FIRE BUT DID NOT DISSIPATE, THERE WAS NO DEBRIS FALLING FROM ORB AS IT GLOWED NOR WERE THERE ANY SIGNS OF DEBRIS RISING FROM THE FIREBALL. IT CONTINUED ON A EASTERLY AND THEN AFTER 10-15 SECONDS A SOUTHEASTERLY COURSE AND CONTINUED ON THAT COURSE UNTIL DISAPPEARING FROM SIGHT. THERE WAS NO WIND AT THE TIME WHICH COULD ACCOUNT FOR THE CHANGE IN DIRECTION AND THE SKY WAS PARTLY CLOUDY TO CLEAR. THERE WERE NO AIRCRAFT IN THE VICINITY AS WELL. THE BEST WAS TO DESCRIBE WHAT WE SAW WAS A FLOATING CAMPFIRE IN FORM, 15-30 FT IN DIAMETER.
