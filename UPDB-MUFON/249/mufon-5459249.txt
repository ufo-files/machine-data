{cak} Huge object next to a building

I was on my vacation in Chicago and I was on a CTA bus from E to W in central ave passing Homer st there is a small bridge where you can see downtown buildings. I was window sitting when I saw a huge dark copper larger than an oval object just next to the tallest buildings it was 2012 I had no smartphone with me and anyway I wouldn't have time enough to take a picture. I was so surprised that I just turn around to see if anyone on the bus were looking the same as I was, they were just a few people listening to music or talking with someone else, what I can remember is me touching the window like a little girl just trying to figuring out what was that. At first I thought it could be a blimp but the size and materials I just can not describe. Next day I remember I looked in the news and on the web just to find out if anyone else saw it but I couldn't find anything related to this object.
