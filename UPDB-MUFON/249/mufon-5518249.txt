strange apparently 4 sided light with a dark center

I live about 10 miles from Fort Drum military base close to edwards,ny.I walked outside on my front porch at 10:30 pm on 6/4/12 and within about 30 seconds i noticed a light appear low in the sky.It was in the opposite direction of the military base.It started moving horizontally across the sky meandering up and down,then after about a minute it disappeared and reappeared at a much higher altitude (straight up)within a second or two.It repeated this process 4-5 times getting higher and higher until it disappeared all together.It was completely quiet,Within a few minutes after a jet flew from the base through the area.I have seen many jets,planes,helicopters,and drones and i instantly knew this was different.I pulled out my phone and took a picture (10:31 pm)and in the picture it appears to be 4 sided with a dark center.I did some research and i couldnt find anything i can explain that looks like this.
