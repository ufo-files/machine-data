{nd} Saw a pulsating orb the size of a full moon. Joined by two other orbs . Totally silent. Shot upward at great speed

I was returning home around 8 pm in December 1974. I got out of the car and noticed a pulsating orb about the size of a full moon. It was totally silent. Shortly a second orb joined the first and then a third. The three pulsating orbs just hovered in the sky in a triangular formation above me at about a 45 degree angle above the horizon.. in a short time one of the orbs shot straight upward at unbelievable speed. If it was piloted, no pilot could survive those g forces. The second and third orbs shot upward shortly after the first. I definitely felt these objects were under intelligent control as their movements were not random appearing coordinated. I was a school administrator in the county and told no one except my wife. I am retired now and wanted to make a record of this before I die. This sighting did not disturb me but filled me with wonderment and awe.
