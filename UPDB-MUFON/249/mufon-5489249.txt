I was in my garden when I heard a loud humming sound looked around then upwards I saw a diamond red object just above my tree with pulsating red lights hovering above my tree [about 60 ft] above the ground I ran indoors then saw something come out of the object and walk round my garden I stayed indoors till they went about 20 mins  the object then went up a little then hovered and slowly moved westward then sped off I went into the garden and found footprints going toward a bush and there I found my cat mutilated the object was still visible I went to get my camera but it had gone the footprint were like those of a lizard type animal as I was standing there looking at my poor cat it came back and I got a sort of picture just before It disappeared completely
