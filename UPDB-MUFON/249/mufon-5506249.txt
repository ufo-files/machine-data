{cak} Massive perfect white diamond in sky, opened up, bright flash, closed and gone

It was Sunday morning around 0345, i was sitting on front porch smoking cigarette. Front porch faces east. what made me notice this object: you could not miss it. It was cloudy out- very few stars and it was HUGE. It apppeared that the sky cracked open into an elongated, perfectly geometric diamond. It flashed very bright light for about 3-4 seconds and then closed up and i could see nothing where it had been. It was noiseless and stationary, no other color than white light

Initially, my thought was that Jesus was coming back- that thought quickly dissipated because it only lasted seconds. Next went into house to check web for other sightings of such things- saw alot of stories and videos - but none of the objects i saw on the web stayed stationary, or only emmitted one bright light or were as short in duration as what i saw.

I went back outside and heard at least 2 helicopters in the area. Then went to bed.
