(gs) horizontal lights (6-8) about 45 off ground over I-10

My wife and I were driving from Tucson to Phoenix on I-10 early Sunday morning July 8, 2012. We were about 60 miles from Phoenix when at 4:19am I observed an object with between 6-8 white lights horizontally aligned and about 45 feet above I-10 coming directly at me. It was about 100 yards ahead of me. I at first thought it was an overpass but quickly observed it moving directly toward me. I asked my wife "what is that" and when it was about 20 feet in front of me it made an almost 90 degree turn to my left and went toward a plowed field. I asked my wife to follow it but she said it had disapeared. For a few minutes I thought it was an airplane. Then I realized there was absolutely no sound.
