White orb or ball of light?

I was with my family driving from the eastern township Magog area back to Brossard (on highway 10 ) in a pick up truck.i was in the front passenger side looking at the stars and saw this one star that Was so bright like I never seen anything like it and I kept thinking about how beautiful the star was then I said hey dad (the driver) see that bright star and I was pointing at the same time at it as I did that it got even brighter and brighter then vanished I was confused asking my dad if he saw what I saw he agreed and told me about the times he seen stuff in the woods as a young man hunting in the b.c woods I asked everyone on else in the back seat if they seen it and they agreed my so was also with me he was only a lil baby though I also remembered seeing a light in the distance of the farm Fields that night a light kinda following us but would change direction and finally vanish( I seen the light more further up the highway we were travelling forgot to add)
