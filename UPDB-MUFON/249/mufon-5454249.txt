Light Flew into the Big Dipper's cup - abruptly changing direction and disapeared

The lights appear in the forest on a clear night on March 24th 2020 near the small town, Tatrocice. Czech Republic. We, 2 witnesses observed the lights descend down with no sound or echo on the lake. With Commercial Flights or Military Aircraft (German base not far over the boarder), you can hear the sound before the aircraft is seen as it resonates in the valley and is amplified by the lake. These lights were stealth and grouped in pairs with one light being the leader and all changed direction. From our point of view on the ground (1-2 km above) they all flew in a distinct path into the cup of the Big Dipper. Each pass, the 2 lights came down at a 70 degree angle to the earth's surface and suddenly change direction which looked like from our stand point again as horizontal line with the earth's surface and flew into the cup of the Big Dipper's. They all disappeared once they entered the middle of cup. The lights just vanished and each phase came every 1-2 minutes. Again, they made no noise and after the lights had stopped, we heard first the echo of the Military Aircraft's Engine and then watched it fly over the same path. The Military Aircraft didn't disappear, but flew over the tree line and out of sight. We could hear it was a Military Aircraft plus it didn't have flashing lights as Commercial and looked as it was in pursuit of the lights. We counted a total of 14, but there could have been more as we happened by chance to go outside at this time.
