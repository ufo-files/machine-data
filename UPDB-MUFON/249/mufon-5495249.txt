{rjl} Small white light disappearing into large white light.

I was driving East on Hwy 103 between Arlington, OH and Mount Blanchard, OH. when I notice a large white light 
high in the sky. It was not moving. Then I saw a small white light streaking in from the North heading to the large white light. It was moving much faster than any airplanes at that time. Just before it got to the large white light it almost stopped, than slowly disappeared into the large light. A couple of seconds after it disappeared The large white light went out.
After the large light went out I saw nothing else.
