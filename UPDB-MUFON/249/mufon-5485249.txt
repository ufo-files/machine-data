{cak} I observed 8-10 star like red objects,flying silently from east to west. They all vanished before reaching overhead.

While visiting relatives in Florida, I stepped outside to smoke a cigarette. I looked up into the sky to the east and observed three red lights in the distance moving ese to wnw. My initial thought was that it was a formation of airplanes. They moved toward.my location and two were then obstructed by a tree. I watched the third object just disappear at about 40 degrees I then looked back to the east and saw two more of these red star like lights moving in the same manner and direction. One disappeared behind the same tree, and the other just disappeared at about 55 degrees. At this time I went into the residence and called my wife and her mom to come outside to see these lights. We all observed 4 or 5 more of these lights moving the same way. Three or four disappeared behind the tree and never came out the other side. We observed one just vanish at about 75 degrees. I Was excited because it looked like something I had no explanation for. The lights were bright red and did not flash.
