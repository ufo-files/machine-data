I went outside to check the weather and to look at the full moon which was in front of my house. Directly behind me there was a flash which made me look up and initially see the stars so I thought I might have been observing a shooting star but quickly the object started to move up down left and right. As I got my phone out to film it my neighbor was pulling his car out of the driveway behind me and then it seemed to disappear but then reappeared when the car was out of earshot/site. 
At that point I tracked it moving down left right and almost in a circular motion at one point. It seem to be a triangle shape and also at the time they were airplanes in the sky further to the west so I knew quickly it wasn’t an airplane. At this point I actually started to direct it verbally. If I said move to the left and it would move to the left, etc. After about 30 minutes of this observation I actually said out loud I’m sorry I need to go in to get my son up for school would you please stay. Approximately 15 To 20 minutes later I went on my back deck and observe the object again for about 15 minutes. At that point each time I went to take my camera out it would stop but start again when I put it back in my pocket.
