I pulled in my in laws driveway. got out of my truck and looked down the hill in the rear of the house. coming up the hill, about a 1/4 mile was a round saucer coming up the hill straight to me. I ran inside the house and got my wife and her parents. We all came out into the drive way. By that time the saucer shaped what ever was all most up to the yard. Once it cleared the fence it stopped just above the trees. their where no leaves on the trees, so we had a clear view. All 4 of use where looking up at a sharp angle. I was excited And was repeating what is that. The others just stood and looked up and before it left my wife and her mother went back in the house like it was nothing. My father in law was laughing at me! I said " Red what was that". And this moron said it was a helicopter. A helicopter? It made no sound. The yard was about 2 acres and the saucer covered more than half. Not the first leave was disturbed . I served in the army and worked offshore, I have been around helicopters. One that big and you could not hear yourself scream ! It was a Quintessential, flying saucer. with the lights rotating. It never made a sound. This was in the late 80s.I'm the only one that remembers seeing anything. My EX wife and her mother remember me being excited, but don't remember seeing anything. This has bothered me for years, but now I'm 65+ nothing  bothers me now.
