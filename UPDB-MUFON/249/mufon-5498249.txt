me and my girlfreind have been watching a tri color stationary star like object in the night we have seen this object on a few other occasions this star like object is being seen all over the country..tonight 10/27/14 me and three other people were standing on 112 st in troy watching this object becouse it was so bright and changing colors from red to blue to white as we were standing there observing the colored object a bright white light suddenly flickered into veiw below the object and became very bright,we all said some explatives when it suddenly started moving off from a stationary position under the object we were watching..this light glided of going south until it disappeared in the night sky,,this however is not what made me decide to file a report, as we were walking back toward my apt still in awe of what we had seen and looking at the night sky as we walked i saw something that confused my sense of reality? so to speak...what we saw was a large group of very dull lights gliding over head in a loose v formation..i have seen many strange lights in the sky but NEVER have i seen anything like this,they looked about as bright as what a dying glow stick would look like, there were maybe ten to twelve of them. if you were not already looking at the sky it would not have been seen they were that dull but glided past very fast, about ten seconds later from the other direction we again saw three dull orb like lights silently glide over head in the other direction towards the adirondacks.. also as a side note,,the amount of low flying aircraft in the sky tonight was unbelievable and i would like to mention that if we did not know better it seemed like these aircraft were there to hide what ever is going on in the night sky, I live less than ten miles from the albany airport so aircraft in the sky is a normal sight but what got our attention was the strobing lights these planes and helicopters were flashing. after we got back as we were standing in the yard the last thing we saw before going inside was what i can only describe as a bright pinpoint of light extremley high up just smoothly gliding across the sky going from east to west.it did not look like a satalite this thing was to bright.
