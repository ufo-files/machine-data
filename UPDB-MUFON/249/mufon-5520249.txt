looked like a commercial airliner at first, but with a thinner middle section where the wings would be, like a space rocket

looked up to look at the moon, clear blue sky, school kids were standing around the bus station, 
thought i saw a commercial airliner out the corner of my eye, but noticed it had no wings. 
roughly same size, colour, altitude and speed as a regular airliner, most people wouldn't have noticed the difference.
no trail, no sound, and where the wings would be there was a 'scooped out' middle section, making it look a bit like a space rocket.
it traveled in a straight line, roughly north, although there was something slightly cock-eyed, like it was flying crooked and not quite pointing in the direction it was traveling. 
i watched it for about 30 seconds before it was out of view.
