Very bright light in the sky hovering and then started slow movement and vanished behind trees

Event took place at Lappeenranta (Kourula), Finland. I was walking and noticed very bright light at the sky hovering in place. I was just thinking thats strange and kept looking at the light and started walking faster to catch someone walking front of me and asked him "do you see that light on the" He replied "i see it and look, now its moving. What is that" And then the object went behind trees and we didnt see it anymore. Never seen anything like that on the sky before. I walked the direction where the light went but never saw it again. Sadly i had my phone with me but didnt think too much about the light before it started moving and then it was too late because i saw it moving maybe 5 secs before it went behind trees.
