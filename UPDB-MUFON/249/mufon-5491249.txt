{rjl} I observed a red-orange glowing ball or orb of light in the sky sometime around 10:45 PM

I was in my backyard facing north, when in my right field of vision I saw a red-orange glowing ball or orb of light in the distant northeastern sky. Although I was in northeastern NJ, I believe the orb was somewhere over Rockland County, New York. It moved slowly from right to left (east to west path) because I went into the house to tell my husband to come out to see it. When he came out it was more in our direct line of sight but hadn't moved as far as I would have expected. At that point we also observed an airplane flying in a southwesterly direction that appeared as if it had crossed the path of the orb. As it continued to move left, tall trees in the yard blocked our view of it. We waited to see it move where there was a break in the trees and the sky could be seen, but it did not appear again and seemed to have just vanished. I did not observe any structure to the orb. It just seemed like a glowing ball.
