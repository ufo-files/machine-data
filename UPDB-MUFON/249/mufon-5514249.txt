(GS) Moving Star Like Orb - Day Time - Tullahoma TN

I had pulled over on the side of the road at the end of Tullahoma Industrial Park (just at the edge of Tullahoma TN) when I noticed a white star like orb traveling between a split in the clouds. (the split was actually what caught my eye) 
As I was looking at the cloud, I noticed the object traveling slowly at first up through the split then it hovered a bit and started to move again and took off so fast it seem to disappear. I watched it for about 1 to 2 minutes. 

When I got home and was watching the video I slowed it down to a little more than half speed and I seen the object drop straight down very fast even on half speed and then come back up. The dropping straight down part was not visible to the eye when I seen it. 
It was really amazing, whatever it was!!!
Description: Bright light, star like orb, orb or sphere shaped, hovering and able to drop straight down and take off at a very high velocity beyond what the eye could see, no wings, no sound.
