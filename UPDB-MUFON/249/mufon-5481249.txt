2 red orbs and 1 star like orb moving in straight and then zigzag flight path

My girlfriend and I were sitting on the beach watching the waves as most couples like to do in the evening. When I noticed a red orb to our left moving from east to west. We first noticed it at abt 25 ft above the ground and it ascended to about 150ft. It disappeared behind the condo building and never reappeared. Within minutes a second red orb appeared at the location we saw the first, and traveled in the same direction and disappeared just as the first one did....the red orbs made no sound, nor did they change color or flash. We jokingly laughed it off as U.F.O.'s. Then within 5 minutes my girlfriend noticed something in the sky. She said look! It looks like a shooting star but it's moving in a zigzag. I've seen meteors before and this was definitely not a meteor. It never changed speed nor did it get brighter or dimmer in color. It stayed solid white and moved from South 401 off gulf Coast northward in a straight line but occasionally zigzaged from left to right at about 451 turns til we lost sight at about 401 off of the northern horizon due to city lights. Red orb sightings lasted about 1 minute on each orb and the last white star like object lasted about 7-10 minutes.
