{jpg} Witnessed an object I can't explain in Indiana.

We were traveling North on i65 between Crown Point, Indiana to Lake Station, Indiana. I noticed an object outside the driver side window to the west. My driver was going approximately 75MPH. The object was going much faster than the speed we were going. It seemed to be moving along the i65 corridor. I watched the object for approximately 45 seconds. It then made a sudden hard right turn, nothing that any conventional jet to maneuver, and disappeared into the clouds. As we made our way to Lake Station,I could see a fighter jet an f16? This was clearly a military jet that could only fly in a straight line. I'm still shaken by this experience.
