Low flying craft appeared with no sound and dissapeared

On new years eve 2017 between 10-11 o'clock , We witnessed a large low flying ship with three lights, the ship appeared to have a gold colour and was completely silent . The ship caught our attention due it's fast speed and bright lights , it was viewed for approximately 5 seconds than seemed to dissappear into thin air . The house we were at had cctv and when he tried to show his partner the footage of us reacting the next day the moment of the sighting was missing from the footage (approx 20 seconds ) and just skipped forward .
