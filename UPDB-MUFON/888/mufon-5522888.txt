2 glowing orbs changing different colors

Saw two glowing orbs- like energy balls- out in the night sky when I took dog outside. They were definitely not planes or helicopters. They were bright and moving around. The one over my neighbors house across the street was much lower than the one all the way to the left. They were in the air for about an hour and a half. No noise. The glowing orb seemed to have different colors coming off it - could be changing lights. We live near an army air field so maybe this is some experiment. They were directly east probably over the ocean. Both my mom and I witnessed them. They seemed to be staying in the same area - no huge moves or anything. My reaction was one of excitement and fear. Ive been reading a lot about UFOs lately because Ive seen these balls out here on the island before. Luckily, I was able to grab my video cam this time. I immediately went on youtube and saw there were orbs like the ones I saw on other peoples ufo videos. What is going on? Government experiment or is it aircraft? I never lost sight of the objects. I just closed the door and went to bed so I cant say how long they stayed out there. I viewed them for about 1.30 hrs.
