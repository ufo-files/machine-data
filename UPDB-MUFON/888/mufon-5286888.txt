Eratic moving object

Although it's been many years since I witnessed this, I finally feel that I won't be ridiculed discussing it. This happened in early September of 19993, my first week of college. 
It was around 10 at night and I was relaxing by a rail on the 3rd floor of my apartment building. Despite being in the city, the sky was clear enough to still still a good deal of stars. While staring into the starry night, I caught to be what was a satellite. To this day, I enjoy tracking satellites. As I was watching this light, it was moving in a linear fashion just like a typical satellite would. However, this object came to an abrupt stop. After a few seconds, it moved to another position quickly. I've never seen anything move that fast.
It proceeded to make a few more erratic moves just like that, and after a few seconds of it's last move, it shoots straight up into space and disappears. The entire ordeal lasted no more than 30 seconds (Because this was 1993, I cannot confirm exact timing). I estimate the object was about 15,000km in altitude. It was a white glowing object and stayed a solid white without any blinking. Getting this on record lifts a weight off my chest and I am glad to finally have it on record. The experience forever changed my perspective of the universe. I am absolutely confident this was not a satellite, asteroid, man-made aircraft, or space junk. I was completely sober and awake during this sighting.
