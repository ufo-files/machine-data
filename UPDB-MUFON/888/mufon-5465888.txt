Quick Soaring Light Orb

We just came out of Freshco in Brantford, got in the vehicle and I noticed a couple of strange lights in the sky. They were unusually bright so they caught my attention. 

One light was an airplane. The other was a yellowish orange/white orb of light. I thought maybe a satellite but it was much brighter and faster than a satellite. And much faster than the airplane. It soared past the plane and off to the west while the plane flew northward. It faded in the distance. 

I was surprised because I have never seen any man made objects fly that fast across the sky. Two of us, we watched the speeding light until it faded into the west. I checked the time. 9:04pm.
