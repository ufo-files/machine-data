Saw disc-like object in sky with lights flashing around base of object. It appeared to be following us. When we turned the car around to leave the area, the object disappeared into the sky.

I was in college and my friends and I were riding in a car up in the mountains near Greensburg, PA. There had been a sighting approximately 7 miles away and had been cordoned off. AS we were driving, I felt soothing strange. I was in the back seat and looked up through the back window when I saw an object in the sky. It appeared to be hovering above us and following us. The other 3 people saw the same object. We were afraid and the driver floored the car to return back to school. Shortly after that time, the object turned 180* and disappeared into the sky.
