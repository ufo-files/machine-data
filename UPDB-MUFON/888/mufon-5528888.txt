Blinking Object By Big Dipper Never seen Before

This seems very similar case 26638 in Flagstaff, AZ the same time period and day. The object was North of the big dipper when I was looking at it at 8pm in eastern sky. It looked like it was blinking red and blue but looked different than any other star that was blinking. I first saw this at about 8pm in the eastern sky. I went outside to look again at 2am and it was still in the same spot in relation to the big dipper and was now almost straight over my house. I saw a lot of lights (maybe 1 every 5 seconds) headed very fast toward the object they looked like smaller stars with a golden hue. These were not meteors.
