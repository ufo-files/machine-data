A V-shaped UFO passed overhead.

On May the 5th 2020 a UFO, "V" shaped similar to the one in the Phoenix video passed overhead, silently, from N to S flight path. My wife noticed first as she was looking up at star gazing. The object had fuzzy, round white lights in the shape of an uneven V. The right wing had 3 light and the left one had 4. We could not otherwise make out a shape. It was lower than 10000 feet and it was moving fairly fast. Not supersonic, perhaps a few hundred miles per hour. We had visual for about 9 seconds all together due to the buildings around us. Since we were sitting in a hot tub we had no electronic devices with us to record anything. I posted the event on a local neighborhood web page and another fellow also indicated that he saw the UFO and he was about 1 mile away from me. The event was unique and unnerving. Our first and most unusual experience. Had my wife not looked up at the right moment we would have missed it.
