Large triangle, Very bright white light.

I was heading North on I-89 coming down hill from Berlin to exit Interstate @ Montpelier. I noticed a very bright white light that seemed to be hovering about 200 ft. over trees just south-west of Exit 8 On/Off Ramp. From initial angle, approximately 1/2 mi. south; equal elevation of object looked like the shape of plane thought it was hovering or seemed to be motionless with "spotlight" on the front. I thought maybe search helicopter? I drove closer though I was unable to stop on the highway, the object remained in same place in the sky. As I exited the interstate I was approximately 1/4 mi. away, about 200 ft below and 600 ft North-East. From this angle the object was a perfect equilateral triangle maybe 30 - 50 feet from point to point. The point facing South had a blinding white light with the others having a mix of red and white only at the points, they were much more dull than the southern point as well. The middle of the UFO seemed to be nothing but the sky above, while the points seemed to only be connected by what I can only describe as stage lighting trusses/scaffold. 
Getting off of the exit turned me 180 degrees away from the object for about 1/2 mi. Though I continued to look back, it seemed to get lost behind the trees. I took the first road on the right now about 3/4mi. away knowing that Road would lead me closer to the triangle. At one point I thought I caught a glimpse of the white light through the trees, but when I came out where the object should have been directly above me it was gone. Even though I thought it was pointed south the only way I could have missed it was if its took off to the north. All of this happened within a 5-6 minute timeframe.
