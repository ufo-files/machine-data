{jpg} Observed hovering above the treetops with flashing lights for three consecutive nights.

The sighting took place on the Chuska Mountains within the FFA station on August 7, 8, 9, 2019 approximately around 7:30 pm in the evening. The lights were clearly visible from the flats of the Naschitti community, which is below the mountains. Two of the evenings , it was visible up to about midnight. The first evenings we watched it, it flashed real bright lights four times, like brightening your headlights. Then it slowly disappeared or faded away.
