{cak} Daughter and I witnessed glowing orb descend and disipate.

dissipate. A glowing translucent orb descend approximately over the river near the town of Eagle Rock, Va. My daughter and I were traveling on Salte Petre Cave Rd and both witnessed the orb descend into view and dissipate to black in the night sky in a measured speed. It was large roughly the size of a car and was not traveling at a velocity to suggest it was falling at a natural pace. But slowing down before it seemed to disappear.
