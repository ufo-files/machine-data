Red glowing globe of light, slowing rotating headed NE.

I was driving down my street and saw it, as soon as I got out of my car I ran across the street to watch it. I called my parents who were inside on my cell phone to come out and see it. They didn't make it out in time to see it, it went behind a tree and I lost sight. I later saw that the ISS was making fly overs that week so I figured it was that, however I watched 4-5 of the flyovers after that to "see it again" but even the brightest -4.0 magnitude was easily 100x smaller and not as bright as the UFO I saw, the ISS was also not red during any of its fly overs. I then googled "UFO July 4, 2010" and found one video that was the exact object I saw. unlike many of the UFOs that day mine was a single globe instead of the triangular formation of 3. There could have been more around but I was very focused on that one that I did not check the rest of the sky around me. It was not flashing or strobing. We have many planes that fly over our house so I know it was not a plane of any kind.
