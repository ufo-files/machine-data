Multi coloured lights at bottom the wings pointed up had like shark fin at back

It was on my brothers morning paper round me my brother and are friend Kevin we was walking to the paper shop Kevin on the far left Andy in the middle me on the curb of the road all of us looked up at the same time don't why we didn't hear anything and when we looked up there it was just gliding over us very slowly there was multi coloured lights underneath it the wings pointed upwards and it looked like a sharks fin at the back we looked down at the same time looked back up at the same time and it was gone this thing was huge it was a low as a house on top of house but no noise what so ever a few months after we saw this we saw it on tv there was a picture of the moon and this was flying across it me and my brother said that's what we saw omg I know it sounds far fetched but that's exactly what happened
