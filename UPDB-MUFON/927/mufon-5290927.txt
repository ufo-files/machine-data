UFO Observation in Sky

I was walking my dogs during their typical afternoon walk. We were standing on the pavement at the end of the road and I was just enjoying the sunshine on a clear day. There were very few clouds and the sky was a bright blue. I looked up and saw a V shaped craft that looked huge from my viewpoint. It was above the tree tops of some very tall pine trees. It was flying very slowly towards the NW. There was no sound at all. It was matte black in color with six round circles of a matte white color on the bottom surface. I was shocked and thought I was imagining things so I looked down at the ground and blinked a few times and then looked back up at the sky. It was still there. I watched it for a few seconds until it was gone over the tree tops and I couldn’t see it anymore. I have spent the last few weeks coming to terms with what I saw and then reported it when I was feeling more comfortable about it.
