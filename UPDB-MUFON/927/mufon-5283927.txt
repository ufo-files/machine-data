two crafts- light tubes- speeding orb

It was pitch black that night. Unusual. I was heading into the house about 8PM and noticed orbs of light- but not light- more like absence of black. They floated, kind of bobbed around until one arced up and disappeared to the west and the other floated east slowly. Beams of light flashed straight down to the ground right in front of me and I started taking pictures. A light ball of white light appeared like the flash of a camera from the south and lasted long enough to get a picture before it disappeared. The lights stopped as quickly as they started. That was when the two crafts could be seen. Barely. They had no sound and no lights. Hovering right over head in the pitch dark sky. What appeared to be light to me turned out quite different. Maybe a power source from somewhere else
