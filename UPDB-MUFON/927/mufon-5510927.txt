(GS) Slow, low flying, large, dark, triangle shaped object that made a curved turn.

I pulled into my driveway about 10:30 pm and as I get out of my car I look up and happen to notice this object in the sky. It is pretty low flying and moving quite slow. I couldnt make out much detail since it was dark but it was large and triangular and even darker against the sky. It made no noise and produced no light. I stood there watching it for at least a minute as it curved smoothly through the sky above my house until I could no longer see it. At first I thought it might be a kite because I thought I heard people in the field near my house but it kept moving. So, then I thought a bird but it made no motion to move its wings and was far too big.
