Observed hovering in western sky for 2-3 seconds. It blinked out and reappeared at 2 other locations within a few seconds.

It was a bright, sunny day with a narrow cloud band north-south, in the western sky at about 20 degrees above the horizon. I was driving west on 6th avenue in Tacoma, WA., going home after a routine medical appointment, Approaching the hwy 16 overpass over 6th avenue, stopped at the stoplight I noticed a round object, white on top, silver on bottom, hovering directly above 6th avenue at approx 30 degree elevation. It appeared about the size of a beebee, held at arms length. It was motionless and NOT an airplane. As I watched it, it just blinked out. I thought I was seeing a reaction of the sun at this time of day but then the object reappeared about 20 degrees horizontally towards the North, at its previous apparent altitude. The stop light changed and I proceeded under the overpass, just in time to observe the object for about a second and it blinked out again. I pulled over into a parking lot on the north side of 6th avenue and got my cell phone video ready to record it, if it reappeared. It did reappear, about 20 degrees further North but before I could get my video camera operating, it blinked out again. It just was there and then it was gone.
