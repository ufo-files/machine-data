{cak} When exiting highway 55

Traveling on 55 east I got off the Barnes ave exit. As I am exiting I see a Black object with a bright orange light traveling East getting ready to cross the Barnes ave bridge. Once I am on the bridge I stop to take pictures and video of the object. It crosses highway 52 still traveling East then passing about 20 feet from a water tower on the other side of the highway. Then traveling on 494 East Crossing the Wakota Bridge I see to my right the object flying over buildings now traveling more Northeast Crossing the Mississippi River. As I continued to travel on 694 North I lose track of the object and see a helicopter that is now running parallel to me as I travel 694 for about a mile and then I don't see it anymore
