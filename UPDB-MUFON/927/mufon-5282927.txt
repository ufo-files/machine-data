Saucer shape with dome, taken on Live Photo

My wife was taking photo of sunrise on morning of 1/19/22. She decided to take a few photos but she did not realize that her camera was on Live Photo. She can’t see very well but posted the photo on Facebook . When she did I saw the object in the sky it appeared to be bright and not normal. At this time I checked her photos on her phone. She had taken a Live Photo of the sunrise and when played by touching the photo it showed the object moving in all directions then plummeting toward earth..I then thought it was a reflection but my wife said she went outside on the porch with nothing in front of her but air. So at this time I zoomed in on the photo and saw a saucer like object clearly in the photo. The photo was taken on an IPhone 12. She did not notice the object due to it traveling at a high rate of speed.
