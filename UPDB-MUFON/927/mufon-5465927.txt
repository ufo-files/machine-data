{cak} Seen egg-like object letting of stream

1. I was heading home from school. I looked up at thick, white clouds and saw this super straight, thick stream letting off from behind the clouds. I supposed it was an airplane, and considering the airport was right around the corner from the school. But when I looked up, it looked nothing like an airplane. No wings nor was it at the height of an airplane. It seemed to be an egg-like orb going at an upward angle to the right. It was white and had greyish dots around the outsides. It stopped letting off jet stream, puffed once more, and disappeared behind some more clouds. I kept there for a second and never seen it come back from behind the clouds.
