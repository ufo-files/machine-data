Mother driving, I in rear of car, she noticed lights in trees to right, stopped after trees to clearly see strange

My older brother, myself and our mother (driving) returning home to Newton Stewart after a few hours shopping in Dumfries. We were on a less busy road we used often, heading west on the A712. Although not saying anything at the time, it transpired my mother was aware of flashing lights through branch gaps in a Sitka Spruce/Larch forest to the right of the car (North) apparently seeming to stay parallel with us. It was not until a break in the forest and green fields became visible that the then clear sight of this very strange "flying/hovering" thing became in plain view. It was a long time ago but I can still see in my minds eye what was there, silent! and if you can imagine five thin rugby balls all more pointed at each end, one in the middle at the top, two on either side sloping down slightly and the other two below each of those sloping down to an even greater degree. All of these shapes seemed to me to collectively make one unit as they flashed a slightly off white rosy tinged light at about one second intervals. I do remember being very scared and pleaded with my mother to get back in the car and drive to the nearest house for safety. I know that things in the sky, that close, and that don`t move and are silent, no more exist today than they did in 1978!
