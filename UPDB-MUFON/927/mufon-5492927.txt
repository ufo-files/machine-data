It was late at night my boyfriend /husband now were fighting and had made a mess broken dishes we took our trash to the west creek about 1/8 of a mile from the house it was just us two at home its an isolated place in the country the closest house is 1/2 mile away,anyway my husband and I were cleaning up our dishes we broke I stayed home finished straightening up and Jim drove our trash to w.creek to dump our trash I decided to go outside and wait for him to come back so I went outside and sat on the east side of the house onthe porch I was looking west and then I got this weird feeling I looked up a saw this huge black no sound object above me I thought to myself what am I seeing the stars were gone and somehing in my head said get up and go in the house out of the corner of my eye I saw something black walking towards me thinking it was my boyfriend at the time still feeling angry looked straight ahead but to me it walked just like jim I felt scared for a moment I just got to the door and pulled the screen door shut but barely closed the inside door thinking why is he walking back up here when he drove the pickup to the w. Creek,I proceeded to the living room and sat on the bed waiting on him to walk in the door any second. I thought to myself why isn't he coming in about that time I hear the truck coming from the creek,I was thinking what?! Who was just outside?!! Jim comes in and I aked him were you just up here which I thought was impossible, he said no I was taking our trash to the creek he said I thought I saw you down at the creek. I asked did you see anyone outside? Then after that I thought to myself my Eagle feather protected me from whatever didn't come through my door I still think about it to this day the only person I told was my uncle and he told me youre protected the house was a church. I know what I saw ,felt. The only thing I don't remember is going to bed or sleep,I cant remember going to bed that night or falling asleep.
