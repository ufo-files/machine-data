{rjl} At twilight, saw 1st star in cloudless sky then saw these 2 lights moving fast in echelon

My friend and I were standing outside his cabin at twilight and I noticed the first star of the night and immediately I noticed these 2 very large, very bright lights moving at about 50 degrees magnetic. The second was trailing the lead light at about 45 degrees to its right. We had an unobstructed view of the objects as they sped across the sky and suddenly vanished. There was no wind, no sound. The lights were bigger and brighter than the lights of any aircraft I have ever seen and they moved quickly then vanished. My friend had the same view as I and gave the same description. There was still a lot of illumination at the time we saw the lights.
