silverish white orb ascending near plane then disappeared within two minutes

My friends and I were at McDonalds Drive thru, I looked up, saw a plane with a trail coming from it and a few inches (because it was pretty far away from us in the sky) we saw a translucent orb ascending into the sky. After about one minute of viewing it disappeared. Looked nothing like the plane close by. We were facing east, we saw the sphere in about an ESE direction.
