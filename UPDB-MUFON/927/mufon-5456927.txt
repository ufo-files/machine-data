{rjl} 100 or more star like lights appearing and traveling east

Approximately 5:30 am I was starring at the Orion constellation when several lights began appearing from between the shoulders, one at a time in perfect succession, same distance apart and all traveling the same speed moving toward the east and spanning the whole sky. They were slow moving and in a perfectly straight line as if Orion was shooting his bow. This was definitely not a meteor shower. This went on for 30 minutes and I stopped counting them after 100 and they still continued.
