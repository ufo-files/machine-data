{rjl} I witnessed two lights one huge white light one small green light hovering and then watched the green light be absorbed by the white light

I was at Lake Lanier in Buford Georgia, I was going to start my grill but never did. I was looking up at the stars Capella and Gemini in the Northeast. There was a huge white light between Capella and Castor. My first thought was Jupiter was below Pollux and this was three times the size of Jupiter. So I watched to see if it was an oncoming plane headed directly toward me. It was not moving, or getting any closer. So I grabbed my cell phone and hit Goggle Sky map to see if it was a possible satellite. While I was waiting for cell to upload a bright green light appeared on the left side of this huge light that was not Jupiter. If using the white light as penny size out there than the bright green light was the size of Capella. Using the extended arm for degrees the green light was one degree from the huge white light. While changing my cell from Goggle Sky to camera the green light moved from left to right and disappeared into the white light. The white light absorbed the green light before I could take a picture both lights immediately disappeared, instantly all gone, no trails just turned off their lights gone! I watched the sky hoping to spot the lights again for fifteen minutes the lights were gone. I feel lucky to have witnessed this six minute happening and would truly like to know if anyone else has documented it.
