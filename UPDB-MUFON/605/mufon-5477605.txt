{nd} Fluorescent Aqua-Neon Green Chevron shape. flying low, incredibly fast, straight line, instant vanish

Standing in backyard, smoking cigarette, looking at sky right after sun set, approximately 7:10 pm Central Time in Santa Rosa Beach Florida. Saw a fluorescent aqua-neon green colored chevron shaped object flying very low at an incredibly fast speed. Observed for 3 solid seconds, enough to almost astonish, and watched its trajectory until I lost line of sight behind tall neighboring house. Waited a split second to recover my view but nothing was there. No disintegration, evaporation, or trail. This object was definitely a solid shape, not a laser, and was traveling fast enough to break the sound barrier, but I heard no sonic boom. I went back inside with a little crawly feeling in the spine and spoke to my parents about the sighting. Father is in aviation and having a lot of trouble explaining away what I saw. Pretty hard rational skeptic and found myself almost defensive about my observations to them. Never seen anything like this before.
