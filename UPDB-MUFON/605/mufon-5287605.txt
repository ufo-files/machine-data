Like 3 round lights stacked on top of one another

I was taking photographs of a sunset on a rainy evening, in which the clouds were very voluminous and colored. When I scrolled back through the photos I found the object. It only appears in one picture but it’s clearly there. There’s no glass to reflect anything, or cause any visual distortion. In the photo, a blue glowing set of 3 lights, seemingly stacked on top of one another in a vertical fashion are high in the air.
