{dr}Solid triangle sighting

I was driving home and saw 3 white lights out towards the hills to my left. My initial thought was it was a power line. As I drove along I could see no power lines in the distance. As I got closer the object began to appear large and began to raise in the sky. It was a triangle shape with a white light on each tip. It was about 70' across with a solid smooth bottom. I didn't notice any holes or markings on it. The object slowly approached the road as I got closer. At this point I pulled my vehicle over to get my phone out to take a picture. At that point the object flew over the road, then sped off into the sky going north. Never seen anything like it before. Occurred near Municipal Water District plant in Winchester.
