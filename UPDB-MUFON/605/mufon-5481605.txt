{cak} Starfish shaped object in the stars

Was on top 14,000f Mt Evans taking meteorite pics. Camera (Nikom D7100) set up to take a sequence of 9 13second exposures while on tripod. I just pointed camera to some part of sky for each 9pic sequence. I took over 500 pics. I didn't notice object until today when reviewing pics. I am submitting two pics. The first is #8 of a sequence which does not show the object (for comparison). The 2nd is #9. During the 13 second exposure you see a light from left to right and on the right, above a bright star, you see the object. You probably should zoom in. Because the line is not straight I ruled out a meteorite, satellite or airplane. I did crop the original photos and you see about 1/4th of each pic. The object looks like no other I have ever seen. NOTE - my ASA on camera was set to 1600. Please give me your evaluation in this. Thanks ahead of time.
