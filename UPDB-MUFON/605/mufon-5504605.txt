{kt}Boomerang craft witnessed by 2 flying above city - followed by a small plane.

Field Investigator:
I interviewed a witness that shared an UFO sighting that took place he believes back in the winter of 1986 over Manhattan, New York. At around 09:00 PM, Pedro C. and his friend Nino were exiting a building after delivering a package near the corner of 35th Street & 9th Ave., and they both noticed a large, dark gray, boomerang-shaped UFO slowly hovering above their location. Pedro: 1It flew very slowly westerly and slightly made a SW turned and hovered out of sight towards the Hudson River. The crazy thing is that there was a small propeller plane that circled this thing, it flew around and around it, clockwise.1 The boomerang craft did not make any noise and did not have lights - however the sound of the small general aviation plane1s propeller was heard. Pedro stated that the UFO flew rather closely over of the buildings; a few hundred feet from the rooftops, so that the cityscape lights were reflecting off the base of this craft. The sighting lasted about 5 minutes.  Report by Norman Gagnon 1 STAR Team FI.
