April 14, 2021 at approximately 10:15pm: While walking/facing south I saw two fireballs following each other at a static distance. They flickered, red and orange - unknown if they were reflecting sunlight or if they were literally on fire as they entered the atmosphere. The first was larger than the other and the second one appeared to go out before the first one was lost in my field of vision (due to the brightness of the street lights). Approximately three minutes later, I saw another identical object along the same flight path. Another three minutes later, another. Finally, after turning around and walking north, a fifth appeared directly over my house - I called to my family to come outside so they could see it (to prove to me I actually saw "something"). Again, similar "burning" appearance, same southwest trajectory. Due to their uniformity, I assumed they were satellites but checking sites I didn't immediately see any similar activity that should be viewable in my area at this time. My wife thought they resembled Chinese lanterns - she felt they were large, low and close - but their path was so straight and uniform, not "floating" like something riding the wind. Accompanying photo is of the fourth observed object - the arched shape is likely due to an unsteady hand and a cell phone.
