Thursday April 5 2012 approximately 3:00 pm I was traveling north bound on hwy 3 north of Brunkild Manitoba when I noticed the object in the sky, it was flying higher than any military or commercial aircraft and it left no vapor trail at that altitude. Object was a very large cigar shape and metallic in appearance. No other objects were visible at the time of the sighting. The sky was very clear and no clouds were visible. Object was ascending and disappeared into the atmosphere.
