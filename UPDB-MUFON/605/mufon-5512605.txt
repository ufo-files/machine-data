(GS) Lights in the sky in georgia

My wife and i where in our back yard taking our dog to the bathroom when we seen lights in the sky. At first i thought they where just lights from a plan since there is a airport not far from our home. As we watched more there where 4 different sets of lights. We noticed that 2 where flying north and 2 where flying south at a close range to each other. Out of now where one of the objects starting getting brighter then went from one light to 5 red bright lights then back to 1. We watched them make sharp turns then head south. after a few minutes they came back but heading north.
