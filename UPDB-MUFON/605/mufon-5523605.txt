Bright light moving across sky like a star then started glowing and disappeared

While I was standing in my front yard letting out my dog I observed a bright light that looked like a star moving across the sky in eastward direction. I then observed a white streak go across behind the object like a falling star. The next thing I saw was the object starting to glow very bright to a yellow/orange color and disappeared.
