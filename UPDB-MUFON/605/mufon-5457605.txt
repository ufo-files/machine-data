{cak} Looked like grasshoppers grey big head large eyes wearing a black jumpsuit with a badge or emblem on there left breast.

Two greys were observed by my brother and I. A saucer appeared from behind a hill to my right slowly moved across our path till it was in front of us they appeared shocked to see us and I think they were looking at the grass before we appeared in there path. The saucer stopped level with the ground in front of us and the beings, one tall the other smaller (thought they were father and son) just stared at us so I waved in a friendly way at them, the tall being turned his head to his left and must have told his son to wave back because the small one did he waved back in the same way mimicking my hand movements then slowly left tracing its path back behind the hill it appeared from. We left for home and 5 minutes later I saw it leave the area silently flying over the trees and up in the sky out of sight a grey dull disc.
