Incoming lights toward my apartment, middle of the night, both flashing/fluttering and static

Was scared, could not move as the lights were incoming and had been almost asleep at the time. Boyfriend was asleep, though it was as if I could not wake him / or myself to be able to move. Although, I do remember him hugging me though I could not return the gesture... so scared as the lights were all about my apartment, seemed to only be over within a few minutes also. I was wide awake and felt like I was shaking, a totally cold clammy feeling absorbed my whole body and then; nothing. (A sense that I would 'lose everything'.. no explanation.. just scared)
