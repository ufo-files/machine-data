In the NW sky, I witnessed a bright green/yellow inverted triangle burst of light.

On the 15th of December, 2021, in Carol Stream, Illinois my 11 year old son William witnessed a very bright but brief green/yellow inverted triangle burst of light in the North Western sky about 20 degrees above the horizon. The sighting was brief, maybe a second or two in duration. The object flashed brightly then went dark. It did not appear again.
