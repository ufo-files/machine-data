{cak} Glowing light that pulsated to a brighter light occassionally

1. I was walking home back to my house from a local Dominos with pizza, when it caught my eye.
2. It was so bright it caught my attention immediately.
3. I thought it was a helicopter at first, but if it was I would've heard it emitting some sort of a noise, which it lacked.
4. It was stationary and constantly glowed (My dumb Ipad2 wasn't able to catch this constant glow), yet rarely would glow brighter then ever, as if it was shining toward me (which is caught on film), then dim back down.
5. I quickly rushed into my house, looked for my camera only to discover it was out of battery, as was my Ipad2. But if this was the real thing, no way I wasn't filming it. So I charged the Ipad 2 quickly and recorded what I could in the cold night. I've seen planes and helicopters in the area my whole life, an airport is far away but close enough where you can see the plane lights in the distance in the sky. This was neither of those crafts, and something I haven't seen prior or since.
6. It was too cold and the battery was dying, so I rushed back inside to charge my Ipad 2 and upload the footage to youtube (so I have the date and time). I went back out but sadly it was gone, and never returned.
