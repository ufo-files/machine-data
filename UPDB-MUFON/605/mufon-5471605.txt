{rjl} High altitude above cruising height of a jet that was observed

I was walking my dog on a very clear night when I looked up like I often do and saw a star that appeared to be moving. When I looked out there appeared to be a pinkish star traveling at speeds I never knew could be obtained. It made The sharpest turns almost at right angles and at first I thought it was a drone, but when a jetliner cross paths and blocked the object out I knew it was way too high to be anything like that. I watched it for 15 minutes until it was like somebody flipped a switch And it was gone
