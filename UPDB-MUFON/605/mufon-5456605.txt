{jpg} Odd Blimp like object

Driving down the 405 we saw what looked like a blimp that was self lit. It wasn't reflecting light from the ground, but rather it lit it's self up (possibly glowing?), with a red blinking light on the bottom. It moved rather slowly and descended just above the treetops. Never saw it land, it looked like it was heading towards a residential neighborhood. The movement was fluid and slow often hovering for a moment then moving again. Normally I would write this off but it felt odd and I haven't seen a blimp in many years. It didn't look like a commercial blimp, more like a big floating poof. It was also a weird time of night for a blimp to be floating around especially with no type of advertising on it.
