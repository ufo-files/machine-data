On 10/24/18 at 6:30 pm I decided to take my Canon camera outside in order to photograph the full moon. While in my backyard waiting for moon rise I noticed what I thought were two bright stars in the southern sky. I thought it was strange that they were so bright already as the sun had just set. I soon began to realize that one of them was slowly moving away from the other. I watched intensely and even tried to snap a photograph however the camera was set up for a moon shot so I'm sure nothing will show. Suddenly one of the "stars" (the moving one) blinked out. It was there and then not there in a flash. The other one remained in the sky and so I'm not sure exactly what that one was.
I watch and photograph the sky quite a bit but have never seen anything quite like this. Does anyone know what I just saw? Do orbiting satellites suddenly blink out?
