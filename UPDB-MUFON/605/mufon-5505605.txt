{rjl} small fire ball that streak across, then hovered, started to blink lights, then went straight up at fast rate.

On New Years Eve, My cousins and I witness a red fireball streak above the roof tops off the canals of Royal Palm Beach as we were in a enclosed deck. The fireball came from the East, then turned North and stopped. It began to change shape from a ball to a triangle shape, but my cousin thought it was more rectangle shape. It started to pulse, white, red, then a orange color. The lights began to change position on the object. As I was videotaping the object, and the pulsating lights, it went straight up in an accelerated rate. It was like a blink and it was gone. I got 14 seconds of it on video after it changed from a fireball to a triangle shape. It was totally silent. No helicopter sound, no plane sound and no fire work sound. If this was a fire work, it must have been the most expensive one Ive ever seen, especially when it changed shape/morphed.
