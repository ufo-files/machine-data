{dr}It looked the same on top as on bottom a sauser shape all metallic with a black band aroun d it

I have filled a full report out and submitted it to this web site already,since then I have created this image of what I saw in the summer of 1965 along with my brother John and a friend Mike Kaufman. It was Mike's house we visited and when we got out of the car this is what we saw, just as you see it rotating counter clockwise and not making a sound, shortly after a few minutes from all the fuss we were making there were at least two dozen people came out of there homes to see what all this was over.We all remained in shock,after a ten minute period it started to move towards New York City to the west,at first very slowly gradually picking up speed at the same altitude as I tracked it down to a spec in the sky all at once it came right back to the same spot in seconds right over Mike's back yard never changing it's altitude. Then it flipped over and was inverted when it did this all the people had enough and there was panic in the street all the people ran back in there homes screaming in panic. Mike's mother called the Nassau County Police and while this was going on they told us it was a collage prank and didn't even bother to come out and fill out a report. It eventually headed back towards the city and we never saw it again.
