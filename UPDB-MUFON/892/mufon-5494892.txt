{nd} Diagonal line of lights. Turning white & red. Changing angles

Myself, my bf and our friend were driving in bend, or. At 2 am. And while doing so, we all witnessed a diagonal line of lights on the west horizon towards the cascade mountain range. The lights would all get brighter and then darker again. Some would break out of formation or dim out or go below the horizon and come back again. Those went away so we went back down Awbrey butte and then saw another set of lights on the se horizon. The lights would dim from white to red and then some of the lights in the line would become red except for one. That one light that was still white would get extremely bright!! All three of us have never seen anything like this before. We stopped our plans and persued this strange lights
