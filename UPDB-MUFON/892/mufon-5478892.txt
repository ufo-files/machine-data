{dr}Glowing, pulsing light, brighter than star above the tree and building line

Outside smoking a cigarette like I often do...See this bright light I haven't seen before. I live on the mouth of a canyon so I have a pretty clear view of the sky, airport flight path, and any helicopters that fly over...

Thought it was a plane at first but planes and copters were flying by as I was recording and it wasn't moving. It hovered for a long time, dimming and brightening, sometimes blinking and then it slowly descended below the building and tree line.
