{nd} Large object moves thru and casts shadows across the rooftops

While in my backyard I noticed something very large in size temporarily block out the moonlight and cast a shadow across the rooftops of the homes behind me. The large shadow moved in a west to east direction very fast completely blacking out the moonlight on the rooftop areas as it passed. It startled me and I scanned the sky but saw nothing that could cause the phenomenon. I became very nervous and uneasy and went back inside the house.
