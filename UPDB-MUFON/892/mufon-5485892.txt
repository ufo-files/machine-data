I was about 7 years old when I looked out the bedroom window. It was dark outside but the sky was clear. I saw a bright red orange, thin oval in shape. The object appeared suddenly out of the sky. It descended down to just over the tree line, hovered for awhile. It then moved in a straight line for approximately 100 yards hovered awhile there then descended slowly behind the trees. I remember being very perplexed as to what just happened.
