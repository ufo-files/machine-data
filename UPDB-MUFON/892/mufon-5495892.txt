This was a very large object like a ball with lights pulsating all aroud it with no noise

I was 11 years old at the time and right after dinner I went outside to walk along the alley ways that run adjacent at the bottom of our street and was cracking ice with my boots from puddles that had frozen over.My memory is fading with time but I'm not exactly the reason why I looked up to the sky but perhaps the lights from this craft had caught my attention. As I stared in complete awe and amazement at this thing that if I had to guess was as large as a football stadium that was just hovering directly over me and was maybe just a couple hundred feet above the large maple tree that was there at the time. It made no sound at all. I do know exactly how old I was at the time because I came across a old picture that was the only time that was taken at that address,and although I was only 11 years old no body can tell me anything other then what I seen was a ufo. I knew that what I had witnessed was unearthly and not from our planet and up until that point in my life I had never even knew what U.F.O's were. The next day I told some kids about what I saw and was immediately made fun of except for one kid who turned to me and said "I saw the exact same thing last night when I was inside my tree fort". To this day almost 50 years later it has never left my mind and often wonder if any reports had been made between Nov 1978 and around Feb 1979 because this thing,after hovering over me for around 3 to 5 minutes took off heading in a southwesterly direction and had traveled right across the busy QEW highway heading for the Burlington skyway bridge. I have never looked into it or reported it till now but would really appreciate it if anything has ever been put on file about this incident because this had to have been seen by lots of people. I often have wondered about under going hypnosis to jog my memory. [Name removed/cms/tg]
