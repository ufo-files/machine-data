I did, and in amazement I saw 4 bright orange lights in the sky, moving SE very slowly. At first, I thought it was lanterns, but these disld not move in a lantern manner. I ran back into the house for something to document it and ran back outside in only to discover my battery was dead! I hollered for my teen to come record for me and she caught a video which I have. We were all absolutely amazed and my youngest was terrified. I'm honestly still in amazement!
