An invisible space entity connected to the city of Portland

The entire city changed and I had felt the biggest fear I had ever felt. It was unlike anything I had ever experienced and it was awful. All sorts of humanoid figures appeared, the whole city filled up, as if they were monitoring me and trying to confuse me. An invisible spaceship--a loose force of chaotic energies, tied to the town, came down. I could feel it come from the sky. They looked human, but they weren't. They seemed to enjoy my fear. I hate them.
