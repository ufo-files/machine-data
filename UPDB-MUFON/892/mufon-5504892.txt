{cak} a cloud like fog very thick

I took pictures of what seemed to look like crop circles on top of a lake with snow on it. to my memory there was no ice or snow the night before. I could tell the ice was very thin for anyone to be on the ice. I saw a dense fog rolled in on top of the lake so dense that I cannot take pictures of the lake and minutes later I returned to see that all of the circles and snow melted in the fog was gone.I was in my apartment at the time. I first noticed when I looked out of my back patio window. When i first saw it I thought it was warm water coming from pipelines that was melting the snow in straight lines until I saw all that the lines did not coincide with the pipes going to the lake.they were also circles in various spots around the lines in it.because of the lake was so big I had to get a higher vantage point to take pictures and it took me several pictures to make a panoramic view of it. seeing the fog rolling in remind me of the movie the fog on how it came in and vanished. It was frightening. I even feel anxious about writing about it now.the first and last time I try to contact someone about these pictures and sent it to them I got no reply the pictures were erased from my computer.I did not erase these pictures. I still have these pictures . Value them very much and if you would like to see them contact me I don't want to take the risk of my smartphone mysteriously breaking down.Thank you for your time.
