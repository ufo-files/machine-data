It is important to note there wer no stars visible in the overcast sky. More and more orbs appeared so we pulled over to take picture. Unfortunatley other vehicles kept pulling over and their light made it very difficult to get a good pic. I talked with several other witness and none had seen anything like it, except for on tv, as i mentioned the orbs over mexico which was closest compareson i could come up with. The orbs began to break formation, all in seperate movement but in general heded east away from our position. As they became more sparse and further apart the lights began to simply fade out, as if either dimming or as i feel, moving away at a very high speed. When the last one caded we all looked at eachother, made a few puzzled comments and went on our ways.
