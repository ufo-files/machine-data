{cak} Random Green/ White light non sequential erratic path various speeds

We were walking into a movie theatre in Tomball Texas in the parking lot. Noticed what seemed to be a meteor streak across the night sky. The color was first white and then it disappeared, then reappeared as a green light meandering through the sky at various speeds and directions. The object stopped in mid flight, blinked, turned direction, blinked and then exited at an accelerated speed in the opposite direction. Then it disappeared and reappeared turned white then back to green and then took off in another direction. Trajectory: came in from the SE made a full right hand (parallelogram with 4 different sides)turn to the S and then quickly jumped from the S to the SW then stopped turn white for just a few seconds then disappeared. We thought it was a meteor until it changed directions then thought it was drone but was traveling too fast. There was NO sounds at all. It exited the area at a fast rate of speed and took off to the SW then disappeared. There was also a police officer with his lights on pointed in the direction of the object in the parking lot. We did not speak to him or her. We have no idea what it was and have never witnessed any object fly with that speed and maneuvering capability appearing and disappearing without any sound. We did not get any photo's. The object was too fast and kept disappearing.
