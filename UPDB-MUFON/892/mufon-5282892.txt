Bizarre illuminated objects on Google Earth with trail; Google later censors out image (Long Island)

The attached images portray 2 potential illuminated objects (or 1 object that moved) I randomly came across in 2016 on Google Earth when I was working as a realtor, which I discovered while researching a potential listing in Lloyd Harbor, NY. 

I had forgotten about it until I was cleaning up old photos in my phone and decided to take a look on my laptop today to see if they would still appear in historical satellite imagery or in any subsequent images. While one object was still present in the location where I had originally discovered it from that date in May 2016, I was absolutely shocked to discover that the more obvious/intriguing object with an apparent exhaust/trail emanating from the rear of the craft over a house has been since cropped out by Google. A large square area exactly where the object was pictured has now been superimposed over the original imagery. 

I felt compelled to send this over to you guys to see if you've ever seen anything like this before. The things that really intrigued me were; the colors illuminating from the top of the object (bluish white and violet), the apparent size (~35 feet), the extreme low altitude (~100 feet or less), the fact that the objects seem to be casting shadows, the location and apparent direction of travel (to Long Island Sound and the Atlantic Ocean), the interesting shape and outline of the objects, and the exhaust plume emanating from the rear of the object.

Please let me know your opinion on this and contact me with any questions or further information. I have attached images of the objects and the subsequent Google censored image. Thank you.
