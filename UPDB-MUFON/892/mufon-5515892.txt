{dr}13 straight nights

At this present time, I have spotted 4 craft with the red and green flashing lights. Appears to follow the same path nightly. North/Northeast to South/Southwest.
Added to this sighting was a triangular shaped star-like pattern in the Southeast sky. (the pointing-like to the North) This object was moving at a rather moderate pace towards the West. Saw this for about 40 minutes, then disappeared completely.
On the 11th night, there were 10 red and green flashing objects (various positions) and the one very large white lighted object.
The very large white object always has one red green lighted object near, to the Southeast of it. About 3am - 3:30am, the red green object is so close to the white object. But have not witnessed any joining together of these two.
Still early, but I am sure its going to be a real light show tonight.
