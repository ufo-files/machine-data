we were running our dogs in the woods and came to a field with mountains in background we saw a disc shaped object with 2 small objects coming out of it from the top rear

My friend and I were out training our dogs for hunting rabbits and we were walking through a stand of trees and at the end of the trees was a big open field with a backgrounds of mountains. We spotted a disc shaped object about a half mile away approxamately. As we both looked in awe, out of the top rear of the object came two smaller objects. When we saw this we picked up our dogs and ran to the car. There were no lights or windows that we could see and there was no sound. It was grayish in color. I wish to this day we had stayed long enough to see what they would do but we were afraid they were watching us or coming after us with the smaller objects. I am 72 years old now and have tried to tell this story to other websites with no luck, we never heard from anyone.
