{rjl} Plane ride to LAX from MKE. 30 min from landing.

Flight to LAX on Christmas day. Not sure if this is anything special. Saw this 30 min before we landed. My guess was we were near area 51. The lights grabbed the attention of everyone onboard. It was fairly cloudy and these lights would pierce the clouds even when we were in them deeply. I figured a reflection from solar panels at first. But the blinking was synchronized and penetrating. Shortly after we became parallel to the lights, which appeared to be below us or near the ground, the pilot turned away to put distance between us. He then re-altered course after awhile. Too me it seemed like a correction to resume. But we were 30 min from landing so I'm not sure. I didn't think to take video earlier. The video you see is towards the end of the sighting as the plane moves away after the course correction. Let me know if you know what it is. Thanks.
