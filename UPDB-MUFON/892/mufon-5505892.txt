[flg]SAW 7 EVENLY SPACED SILENT YELLOW/RED OBJECTS

On the evening of December 24 at just after 10 PM, my son, his GF and I saw 7 objects flying approximately 1 mile away over our area in Frisco. They were moving fast (speed unknown), glowing yellow and red. There looked to be blinking lights in the middle of the objects, which looked to be small bun-shaped craft.  They came in three sets of two objects followed by a single craft that looked to be spaced in the middle back, flying from the south (from Plano/Dallas, but not in the direction of DFW or Love Field), flying on a north/northwest path. All of the objects were silent; we listened for any noise but did not hear any coming from either the direction of the object or behind them.  As soon as they passed by, the yellow/red color faded immediately to the outline of the sky and they disappeared.  I immediately ran towards the back of the house and looked in the same direction for them but neither saw nor heard anything.  My son and his GF attempted to take pictures (with their cell phones) but nothing could be seen.
