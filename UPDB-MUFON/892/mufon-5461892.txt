{cak} Object Same Color Of Moon Spotted In Broad Daylight, Northern Colorado

I just looked up and saw the moon, I was riding my bike home from getting some cheezburgers. I saw up and to the right up the moon a small object, like the shape of a broom or something. I thought it was definitely not conventional so I jumped off the bike and watched intently as long as I could.

If I had to guess, I would say I saw some kind of spacecraft or drone that had hover abilities, very high in the sky, not sure how to determine the size or altitude of this one. It looked JUST like the moon in color, so I thought it might be an Iridium Flare, but it did not actually "flare" and get very bright. It was visible, and then after looking at my feet to set them right and look again, it was gone. Perhaps a 20 second sighting and I have never seen one like this in my previous sightings.

Thanks, and keep watching folks.
