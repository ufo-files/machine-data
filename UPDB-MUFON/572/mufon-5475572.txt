{dr}Orange Sphere appeared, lights beneathe and above appeared and flashed for a few moments, outline turned to dark cloud until fading away

A friend and i were outside talking at around 4:30 am, and i saw a Light grow into a orange fuzzy sphere that was growing. We watched for a couple of minutes as 4 lights forming a square shape appeared a slight distance underneath the sphere and a few more appeared above and/or to the side of the sphere. The lights disappeared and the orange sphere turned into a dark blueish color for a moment. The light turned back orange and the other lights re-appeared and all flashed. A cloud formed around all of the lights, looked as if it was the body of a rocket but built differently. We tried to record on our phones or take pictures but there wasn't enough light so we just watched hoping something crazier would happen. The cloud stayed its shape for another few minutes until fading away into the sky. We were by an airport but at the time there were no planes or helicopters and no other clouds in sight.
