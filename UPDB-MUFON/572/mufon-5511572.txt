(GS) Saw redish orange orb like light stationary in sky then moved east very fast

Took the dogs out back looked up at the stars since the experience my grandchildren and I had last summer, saw an orangish red glow in the northern sky thought it was a distant star or something. Went about our business then looked back that direction to see if any more rain clouds were out still seeing the object, after about five to ten seconds it move to the northeast in a straight line with a slight upward direction. It traveled extreamly fast and was out of sight in a matter of seconds. From where I live a plane is usually not that high up and would have taken minutes to travel that distance and be blinking.
