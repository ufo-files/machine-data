Teardrop Moving cloud with dark shape in center moving east at sunset

I walked out onto my deck in Harrisburg Pennsylvania as the sun was setting. As I looked at the sun setting, I noticed only one cloud could be seen in the sky just above and to the left of the sunset. However the cloud moved at a consistent speed from west to east. In the center of the teardrop shaped cloud was a dark shape. I believe the sunset caused the cloud and shape in the center to be visible. It could have been a man made vehicle, but it definetely was under control and was definetely enclosed with a teardropped shaped cloud.
