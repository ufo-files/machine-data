Noticed a long cigar shaped white object.

I live in Barnstable Ma. It is located on cape cod. On 8/5/2020 At around 19:55 I was driving home after picking up dinner for my family when I noticed a very abnormal object . It caught my eye and as I was driving I did not take my eyes off of it. It then disappeared even though it was not cloudy . I continued driving annoyed that I lost the object and did not attempt to record it . At this point it’s about 20:00 I am nearing the intersection near my house when the object becomes very visible again. Now the road I am on from the first time I saw it to the second time is very straight with a lot of visibility, with it being in the commercial area of Hyannis . At this point instead of going left to my house I continue straight which I am now driving toward the object . This object is long and cigar shaped, it has absolutely no distinct characteristics to its body other than looking like a white object rounded on either end. I then lost sight of it due to the tree line as I was moving out of the commercial area. I then tried to park At the exit 6 Burger King parking lot to look for it, but could not see it and lost it completely.
