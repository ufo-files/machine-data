{dr}large circle of white lights, pink/blue hint, hovered with a brighter light rotating counter clockwise over the stationary lights, visible thru the thin cloud cover. Very large circle of lights, craft not visible, Jetport & I95 nearby.

Intersection of Peverly Hill Road & Middle Road, Portsmouth, NH. I was stopped at traffic light and noticed a very large circle of white lights just to the NE. It was appx at the 10 o'clock position. Light cloud cover appx 15K ft above ground, lights very visible through the clouds. Lights did not rotate, however there was another light that rotated around the stationary lights counter clockwise that gave the appearance of movement, but the circle of lights did not move. The circle of lights at times had a pinkish/light blueish hue, but predominantly white (maybe the pink/blue color was the result of the cloud cover) - I watched the circle for almost a full minute. It stayed in the same place with the bigger light just rotating around the circle of stationary light. I kept trying to see if there was a craft there, but could not make out any shape of a craft as this was just above the cloud cover. My first thought is that whoever it was must have thought they were hiding above the cloud, but the lights were clearly visible. My light turned green and I was able to move forward. I pulled off Middle Road at the Portsmouth Plains Ball Field to continue watching the lights, but they were gone. - I drove slowly scanning the sky for the lights, but they were gone. The area is very very close to Pease Jet Port where there are a variety of aircraft, helicopters, passenger planes, Air Force Refuelers, etc.  I thought perhaps the circle was associated with a helicopter, but it was far far too large for a helicopter.  Planes take off and land all the time there and I would estimate a normal size passenger plane would fit into the circle of lights that I was, yes, it was very large. I did notice that the jet port did not have their runway lights flashing, just normal runway lights. The area it was hovering over was I-95. I did not feel afraid. I was in awe.
