Observed what appeared to be a meteor flash not trail. Watched an object track across the sky that resemble a satellite.

Letting my dogs out in my backyard. When I was looking up at a clear starry morning. I saw a satellite and tracked it across the sky when I saw a meteor flash, but it didn't have a tail. I saw an object emerge from the flash and it move and looked similar to a satellite. I observed it for approximately 2 minutes. I was under the impression that the object was descending because it began to fade as it stopped reflecting sunlight from its high attitude. Note the fash occurred many miles away from the satellite I was observing.
