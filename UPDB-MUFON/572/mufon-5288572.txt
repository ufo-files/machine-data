MOVED IN, HOVERED, LEFT IN DIRECTION IT CAME FROM

First spotted red light in sky. thought it was an airplane. then noticed a pulsing green light. 2 lights now, red and green. no sound. could not tell distance but was appearing through treetops so i had a point of reference, then it stopped moving and hovered for 3 or 4 minutes. lights changed to yellow and pulsated for a few seconds, then lights went back to red and green and object left area in the direction it came from. The lights were quite bright, brighter than the normal marker lights of local air traffic. It flew in, hovered in one position, then left in the same line it came in on.
