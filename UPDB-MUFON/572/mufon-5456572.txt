{jpg} Observed Oblong Object in Night Sky

On Dec 14, 2019 at 8:40pm, I noticed an oblong shaped object overhead, slowly moving from NE to West. The night was clear and dark, and I stepped outside to watch for meteors. It was barely visible with no illumination of its own, no lights or sound. It was high in the sky, probably a few thousand feet. The size compared to a ruler measurement was about 5mm. The color was a dull metallic color, that was only visible due to faint lighting from the ground. It was nearly invisible, and I would not have noticed it if I hadn't been specifically looking for meteors.
