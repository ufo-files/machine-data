4 witnesses observed 2 star-like objects perform right-angle hovering/path maneuvres, then disappear and reappear elsewhere over 45 minutes

It was a warm summer night, and my girlfriend at the time, her mother, her best friend and I were out on the balcony. I can't remember who saw it first, but we began observing a star-like object move slowly at very high altitude across the sky. Initially we thought it was just a satellite, but then it abruptly changed flight path at a 90 degree angle, followed by multiple further 90 degree changes, seemingly at random. After about 15 minutes, my girlfriend's best friend suddenly said (in German) "look, there's another one", and we noticed another star-like object performing the same kind of random-seeming 90-degree flight path maneuvres. Both were visible for about another 15-20 minutes. Then, suddenly, first one and then the other pulsed brightly for a short second and then disappeared. It look like the pulsed out of existence or flew away very fast.
