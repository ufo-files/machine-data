{jpg} saw an object with blinking lights and an outline of the craft hovering in the distance

My mom was outside and saw an object with blinking lights hovering in the distance. She came and got me to take a look and we continued to watch it and an outline of the object was slightly visible when the lights would blink. It was oval shaped and dark in color from what I could tell. The lights were red and silver/white at first, but then as we continued to watch the red light stopped leaving only the silver/white light and the object appeared to move further away until it was almost impossible to see.
