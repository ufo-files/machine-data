2 white lights, blinking rhythmically, not an A/C or tower.

I witnessed 2 white lights, side by side, blinking in sync with strong, bright, rhythmic flashes. There were no A/C in the area. It appeared 200-300 feet above the hill next to Candlestick Park (witnessed while driving north on Route 101). There is a radio tower I used as reference for color, height, etc - and this was clearly not another tower of such. The lights were larger and brighter than the lights on the radio tower. While observing the lights (and trying to figure out what they were) they vanished from the sky. No craft was observed. The lights just shut off and the sky was empty. The total time period was about 30 seconds.
