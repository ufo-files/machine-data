(GS) large triangle made up of 3 pulsating lights, 2 pulsating orange lights

Was driving home after getting food and gas in Monongahela. Noticed a large triangle made up of 3 pulsating lights hovering, Stopped my car to watch the lights pulsate and flash different shades of orange and red. I then went home, got ready for work. An hour later I left and the triangle was gone, in the opposite direction I saw 2 pulsating orange lights hovering parallel from each other.
