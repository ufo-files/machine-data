Object, orb-like and stationary glowing multiple colors unlike a star and somewhat bigger.

Finished watching a TV program, I had an impulse to go outside and look towards the east into a clear night sky while looking at the stars. This was highly unusual since I had not done so for quite a long time. I noticed one particular light that did not appear as a star in a usual way. Looking at it, it was pulsating multiple colors, orb-like and somewhat bigger than the surrounding stars. It did not move as I was looking at it. It was also lower in the sky that the other stars in that view of the sky. I then went inside for binoculars, 7x35mm, to get a better look and asked my girlfriend to come outside for her impression and view of it, as well. She saw it, looked through the binoculars, and observed the same as I did. The circular edges were fuzzy and it was an indistinct, orb shape but the colors kept changing in an unnatural way. These colors were brighter and deeper in color/range than a more natural, star-like manner. My reaction was that it was interesting, definitely a UFO to me and to her, but I was not able to spend more than about 10 minutes looking at it. We went back inside. About 30 minutes later, I went outside again to see if the object still appeared or had moved. Not to my surprise, the object was nowhere in the sky and had disappeared.
