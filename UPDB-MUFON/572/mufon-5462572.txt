{jpg} A UFO came into a forest clearing and hovered as if searching for something, then took off suddenly.

I was out hiking with some friends, and I broke off from them for a little bit to off the path. I was at the top of a ridge that overlooked a clearing at the bottom, and that where I saw the Unidentifiable Flying Object. The lights and the sound were what first caught my attention. I had no idea what the object was when I first saw it, but I thought the object could have been some kind of drone until it got closer and bigger. The UFO came into the sky above the clearing and hovered there for some time, I don't remember how long, I was disoriented. It was disc-like but it wasn't flat. The color was a dark grey, almost black exterior. The top was matte but the bottom was reflective. There was a ball of light on the bottom, almost like a large light bulb, but the light it emitted was not like a regular lamp It gave an all-around kind of glow, but it also shot out a straight beam at one point. The beam wasn't very bright, but it wasn't dim either. I lost sight of the object because it abruptly took off in another direction after hovering for a while. I was completely disoriented and mesmerized watching the object, I have no idea how much time had passed, it felt like a trance. When the object left, I was in a kind of shock and stood there for I don't know how long. No one has believed anything I have said about the event.
