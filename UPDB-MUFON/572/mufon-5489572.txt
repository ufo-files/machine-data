{dr}Bright light coming from ground

While on vacation with my family I was hanging outside about 1am while my husband went inside the hotel to print out Hershey tickets! Enjoying the view I turned on my phone camera and tried to take a picture of the moon. I noticed a light coming from the ground slowly going up to the sky! I started to take as much pictures as I could seeing the light stop half way up and disappeared! It was strange! Next day we walked to site to see if there was a spot light or anything on the ground and nothing was there!
