{rjl} Light following an airliner

On the night of August 8th, 2019 I was out about 11 p.m. when I looked up to watch an airliner flying overhead at a fairly low altitude, assuming it was on approach to land at the Boise airport. I t was a clear night, and stars were also visible as I first saw the airliner close to overhead but to my south. It was nothing out of the ordinary as I've seen many airliners fly over in my life but then I noticed one of the bright stars which at the time I guessed was Jupiter as it was a bright star and I was looking to the south but as I watched the plane going over I noticed the one I assumed was Jupiter was traveling alongside the jet. As that is nowhere near normal I watched the object which was to the south of the jet that was traveling east and trailing slightly behind it. I could only maintain sight for about 15 seconds before both the jet and the object went behind a treeline.
