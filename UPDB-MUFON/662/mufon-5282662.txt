2 UFOs within 12 hours of each other

Appeared as shape of a Christmas Tree in all red lights. Changed to an all green straight line with an arrow shape on top. Changed back and forth at equal intervals of about 3 seconds. The next morning (today as I write this), a bright light in the west appeared at around 6:30 a.m. The stars were still out and this was 20 times larger and brighter than the stars. Stationary for 45 minutes. As it grew light, all the stars disappeared but this light was as bright as ever. It eventually seemed to slowly move away until it disappeared around 7:30 a.m.
