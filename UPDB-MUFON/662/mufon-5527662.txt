two luminious objects pairing up one stationary and one that moved from east to west to meet up with the one that appeared to be stationary

On Feb. 10, 2011 between the hours of 11am - 11:30am I was relaxing in my hottub looking east.I observed a single luminous object which traveled from east to west then paired up with another luminous object which appeared to be almost stationary. The second object was noticed only because I was tracking the first object. Also, because it was slightly behind me @ 1:00 position.

Detailed version:
On a perfect clear day while relaxing in my hot tub at approximately 11:10AM I noticed a luminous object traveling west to southwest. I didn1t see any wings on it or a trail behind it like an airplane, this caught my attention. It was at a extreme altitude, and would of went un-noticed if I wasn1t wearing sunglasses. I tracked this object as it slowly started to change direction to northwest. With binoculars I tracked it over head,and I couldn't hear any type of engine noise. This object had no wings, and a very bright white light on the bottom.This light did blink, but not in a regular constant on-off pattern. As this object past overhead, and past the roof line I moved to get a better view. I was now looking towards west @1:00 position.I called my 22 year old daughter outside of the house to witness what I was seeing. The first object I was tracking paired up with another luminous object that went un-noticed until up to this point. The two objects I observed with binoculars seemed to be stationary with a short distance between them. They appeared like two stars in the daytime sky for about 5 to 10 minutes.
My daughter and I watched these two objects until they slowly went out of view.
