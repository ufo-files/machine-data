{tjd} Bright red object witnessed by me and 3 children for some time just after sundown.

. Some time near 7 or 8pm. The sun is setting well below the horizon. The reasoning of children. We were on the hunt for night crawlers. A large earth worm used for fishing. On most summer nights they could be found under leafs and other organic matter found in the gutters on the side of the streets. Fun stuff for a young boy.
The events of the evening took a sudden turn when several of the other children started excitedly yelling. Instructing the boys who had their hands in the gutters to turn and look over the trees at the end of the street. Down at the end of the block, at some distance over the tops of the trees was a very large bright red object that was as big as any of the homes near by. The object had the look of red hot molten metal. 
We ran to one of the kids homes but no one was home. We ran down the street to where my parents were and ran inside yelling for them to come out side to see. They came out and the object was gone.
