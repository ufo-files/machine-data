We were looking 139 degrees south East from the rear door of the trail side cabins in darlington wi. I noticed a bright light to the south East.

We were looking to the southeast from our cabin and noticed a bright light that was not a star. I saw coming Down under the light what looked like tentacles hanging down but nothing like a comet which I have seen before. So my wife and I took pictures to our astonishment it looked like a ufo with different features on it. We tooled several pictures and thought nothing of it. Until we watched this last Saturday’s episode of unidentified on the history channel and saw pictures of the exact same object from last year in Argentina from a pilot taken with his phone which made our hair stand up.
