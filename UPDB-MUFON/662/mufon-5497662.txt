{dr}observed aircraft ascending with a light tracking exactly with the plane

I was standing on my patio and observed a plane with a dull white light situated to the left of the aircraft just ahead of the aircraft. The light moved exactly with the aircraft from the moment I first saw the aircraft and went out of sight behind a tree. I immediately went on flightaware.com and saw only jet travelling at that time in that direction. SouthWest Airlines from Phoenix, Arizona to Dallas, Texas at approx. 6pm November 25th, 2014.
