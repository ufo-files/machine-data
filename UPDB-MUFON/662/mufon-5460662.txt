{rjl} I saw a straight line 20 to 30 star like lights traveling across the sky at a steady pace.

I saw a straight line of about 20 to 30 star like lights that stretched across the north sky about the distance from one end of the big dipper to the next. They were all moving at a constant pace heading north east / east. At the front of the line they were super condensed and then gradually spread out, not symmetrically towards the end of the line. None of them were flashing. They were almost like satellites but brighter and slower moving. Some clouds went in front of them and after they cleared all of them were gone.
