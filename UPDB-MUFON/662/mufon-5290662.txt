Looking to the West from our farm outside of Quanah Texas December 8, 2020 around 5:00 pm I observed a glowing light about a mile and what stuck me as being odd was it just sat in the sky. It’s not unusual to see lights and orbs near sundown but this one was different. I watched it for at least 15 minutes then it seemed to move slightly up then down and then it was gone , in a blink
