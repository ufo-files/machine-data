I Witnessed a Large Fireball Go Behind a Building in Dallas in Oct. 1973

Report Entered by OKSD/ FI #31522 - Report taken from a gentleman calling the OK MUFON telephone line. He describes " It was a fireball and it looked more horizontal then it was down. It flew from North to South behind a buiding. I never heard a noise and it disappeared from my sight on the horizon. The angle is what bothered me becase it didn;t seem steep enough to be a meteor. I am an AF Vet and have had 37 hours flight training. I am crazy about aircraft and can identify almost any of them."
