{dr}Huge fast-moving bright red light, flashing white lights darting, around sky

I was outside in my yard smoking and saw a bright red light, north of Grand Rapids. It quickly darted west, I estimated its speed at 1600 mph (moved about 5 miles in 6 seconds). Then it stopped completely and started flashing white bright lights and slowly moved back to the east, then picked up speed to very fast again. Then it slowly darted straight up in the air, then west again. My son was watching this too. We were astonished and amazed, the only reason why I noticed it is because I saw it before about 15 years ago. [The next night after this I saw another one in the same area at 8 PM, actually two of them which appeared out of nowhere and flew the same way as the first night.]
