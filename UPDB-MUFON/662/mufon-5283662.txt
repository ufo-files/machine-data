Building Sized Orb

At 12:35a on December 13th 2021, I was driving on Eastbound 44 heading to work, when I was about parallel to the White Castles off 44 I saw a very fast moving ball of light from the top left corner of my windshield zip past me and continue East toward the city. What stuck out to me is how big it was, how fast it was, how bright it illuminated, and how low it seemed to be. It was probably the size of 2-3 story bank building and it was fairly low to the ground, lower than commercial planes fly. When it first came to view over my car it was very bright, like I was looking at the moon. It didn't seem like artificial lighting with how it glowed so bright. My initial reaction was shooting star or comet but quickly realized this is too massive, grew way to brightly,, and low to be either as it was keeping a perfect horizontal line past me toward downtown. It was incredibly fast with no propulsion system. It was dead silent as it glided past me and it had to make from Highway 144/44 past 270/44 within a matter of seconds flying low, never losing altitude or changing direction like a shooting star or comet. There was nothing but open sky for miles and I could still clearly see it and would be able to track it's movements for some time but, the second my brain went from "this is a natural occurrence" to "wait, what is that thing?" The second I thought that to myself the light just went out while still keeping its same course, speed, and altitude. There was nothing around but open skies and really around for me to lose sight of it in. 

As a former Marine Corporal who served on an airbase. I had never seen anything like it before.
