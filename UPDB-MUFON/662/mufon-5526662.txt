3 bright lights in one place

I am a photographer by trade, and as we had some very interesting cloud formations this evening, i decided to take a few shots for background and scenery, on area of the sky caught my eye with a bright object moving very fast through the sky, i trained my camera and zoomed in to maximum zoom, and took the snap, it was only there for about 10 seconds in all, and then completely disappeared. I am using the Canon Rebel T1i, with an EF-S SS-250mm 1:4-5.6 IS Zoom Lens. Nothing much more to say only that it was very bright and interesting mainly the shape once i had loaded the image on to the PC and zoomed in even more. I have included 3 files with this note, the original, one with the object outlined, and a close up edited in Photoshop, though no other editing has been done just digital zoom. To add, i was facing west toward the City of Portsmouth UK.
