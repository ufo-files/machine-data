2 lights in the skyline

We witnessed these objects in the sky coming home from a late hike in the city. The first object appeared just above the treeline and I thought they were drones. I couldnt keep my eyes off of them because of the way they moved, the movements seemed too erratic to be a drone. I realized after a couple minutes that I should start to record them. One of the objects moved erratically for several minutes before flying away. The other remained stationary for a minute or two longer and then flew away at a very fast rate of speed. I am excited and confused with what I saw.
