Light in sky was following me then hovered over my car.

Coming home from work about 11pm heading east from Bayport Inn a bright light in the sky was following me for 5-6 miles.When I stopped at the stop sign at US 19 I noticed it getting darker then realized there was no more light but an object come from behind me and I watched it stop a few feet in front of my car.I looked up thru my windshield and this object was dark and smooth and flat. I looked out my rear and side windows and realized it was perfectly round.I was thinking if there was 1 car in front of me and 1 in the back and if I drew a cicle around them that was the size of this object that was just hovering over my car. If I stood on the roof of my car and stretched I could have touched the bottom of this thing.I then saw that my dashlights were slowly dimming and my headlights doing the same until my car stalled.I heard a woosh and this thing took off North on US19 and I watched it make a complete stop.Then it took off west towards the gulf in a matter of seconds.I had such a sense of calm even starting when the light in the sky was following me.In hindsight I should of been scared to death when it was hovering over my car but it just seemed fascinating. My old car started right up and I went home.I think about it every day and over the years told anyone who would listen. The next morning I reported it to the Sheriff office and they said it was probably the military testing aircraft off the coast.This was a flying saucer that I witnessed.About a year ago I googled flying saucer and car stalling and was amazed with reports like mine.To my amazement I come across abductee John Reeves which his experiences happened a couple miles from mine back in the 60's.
