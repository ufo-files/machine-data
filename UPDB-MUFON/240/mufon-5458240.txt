{jpg} Observed 4 white, brilliant lights in a horizontal line just above the tree tops. Appeared stationary.

Was on the way home with my son after a baseball game in Trenton, NJ. We take back country roads when possible to avoid traffic. At appx. 9:20 pm on Tuesday night, Sept. 10, 2019 we were heading home on Windsor-Perrineville Road in East Windsor, NJ. My son (age 27) said Dad, look at the lights. I looked to the right and observed 4 Snow White, brilliant lights....just above tree level at the end of a very large farmers field (location of lights were actually in Millstone, NJ). I only observed them for 3-4 seconds as our view became obstructed by closer woods and homes ever so often.  To me the lights appeared to be in a horizontal line with the front light slightly higher than the rear light. My son did tell me that when he first observed the lights there were only TWO of them, then the third and then the fourth..... I do not know what happened to the lights after that as our vision was blocked as stated above.... I returned to the area in daylight and drove around. It is all farm land and trees with some homes in the area. Not overpopulated... I saw NO light towers or anything unusual that would have been a source of this kind of light and shape......
