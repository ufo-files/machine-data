{nd} Bubble Orb Object in Seaside, CA

I was in my backyard walking my baby to sleep. I noticed the object glimmer above my neighbor's roof. When I first saw it, I thought it might be a giant bubble from a kid or something, but then it didn't pop. It was transparent but reflected light or glimmered as well. It came just over my head and hovered for what seemed like a long instant and I felt like it knew I saw it. I wasn't afraid, just very curious and mesmerized. I really was trying to think of a rational explanation for what it could be. Then, it took off and I couldn't see it because my house was in the way. I ran over to our deck area to see if I could see it from there, and I again caught a glimpse of it, now far away and higher, still glimmering. From there, it gradually faded into the distance. I'm wondering if anyone else saw something like this in the area.
