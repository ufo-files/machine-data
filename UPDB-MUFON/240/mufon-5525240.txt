Just an odd light which brighten up and grew before blinking out

I was on the sundeck above my garage at about 11:15 PM on July 28, 2011 when I saw a bright light. What made me report it was because it was odd. Normally, from my sundeck I can see aircraft making maneuvers for landings at LAX. Usually they head east towards my house an turn south; I can see their landing an marker lights. Occasionally, when the airport is fogged in, they fly over my house. On this night, I see this bright light in the west slightly below Arcturus? in Bootes. It moves slightly to the northwest and then brightens up and enlarges to a four-point diamond shape and then simply blinks out. No aviation marker lights were seen after the light went out; I couldn't tell what if any direction it continued to fly. I didn't hear any sound from the light. The attitude of the light was higher the planes that I see heading from LAX except when they come very close to my house; then the planes would be about the same attitude as the light. But, when they are that close, I can see that they are planes, not this light. I don't think that it was a police helicopter with its search light on; I should have seen aviation light still when the light went out; and I should have heard it.
