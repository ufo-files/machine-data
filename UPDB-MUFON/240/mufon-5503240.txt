{rjl} Pulsating Shape-Changing Light

On the morning of March 26, 2014, I was reviewing the captured events on my home security camera. The camera is designed to record video upon motion detection. One video from 01:30 that morning showed an odd light on the porch. At first I thought someone was playing with a flashlight, since I saw a companion light to the right of the main object, but the way the light behaved was inconsistent with that. The light appeared to pulsate, and it changed shape. There were also appendages that extended from the light. I showed this video to several people, but none of us could explain what it was.
