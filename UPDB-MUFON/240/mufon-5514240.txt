(GS) 8 objects in air; 3 separated and spiraled to the earth; sparky orange trails.

On November 17th... Driving west on Rt. 70 in Columbia, Mo about 7:30 pm, we viewed objects in the southern portion of the night sky which appeared to be clusters of white lights. There were 8 clusters of lights altogether and 3 separated from the original formation which spiraled downward leaving an orange trail behind it. The event lasted approxiately one minute then they all disappeared. No visual information or audio information was recorded. 
Our feelings were of shock, maybe awe. No physiological changes took place.
