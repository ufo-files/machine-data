Detected After Seeing Photograph Taken

I had been tracking the movement of the light blue round planet or object near the sun, which I believed to be Nibiru or Mercury. Not sure which, but was interested. This afternoon, I went to my backyard and took what I thought was a clear photograph of blue planet near the sun. When i looked at the photograph taken with my IPhone 4, I realized there was a shape that I had not seen when I took the picture.
