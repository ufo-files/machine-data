Long and shinny

I saw a long shinny cylinder in the north/west sky around 6:54am. as I was going to my car to go to work. The sun was out, and there were no stars in the sky that I could see. I went back into my house and got my camera, this took about 3 minutes; and to my surprise the object was still there. After snapping the first shot, it was gone. I watch the skies every time I go outside, and Ive seen a lot of strange crafts in the skies, but this was the first time I was able to get a photo of what Ive seen.
