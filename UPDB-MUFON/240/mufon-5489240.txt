I was taking a bike ride at night and was looking up at the stars, suddenly I had saw a bright orange orb, that was tens of thousands of feet in altitude,and moving thousands of mph.. It appeared out of nowhere, and shot across the sky like a shooting star, yet without loosing speed had taken a 901 right angle turn, and stopped on a dime... It hovered silently at 10,000 feet or more, then the orange orb dimmed, and dissapeared.. I had over 30 hours logged flying helicopter's, yet i had have never seen or know of any aircraft which could replicate the movements I saw.... The craft appeared as suddenly as it had disappeared, with the entire event lasting no more than 10 seconds... I would estimate from my distance the object around 30 feet in diameter.. Personally I had never seen anything like this in my life.... I had never mentioned what i saw too anyone, worrying what people would think, Yet can't stop wondering what it was that I saw.... It appeared in the direction of fort dix, which is less than 30 miles away South West. Also Lakehurst naval base is less than 10 miles away west.....
