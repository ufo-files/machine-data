Massive, silent low flying disc with rotating lights moving slowly

Just before dawn my fiancé an I saw a massive disc shaped object move across the sky at an extremely slow speed. It looked like it was flying very low and was completely silent. It had hundreds of lights around the perimeter that seemed to rotate. It looked to be about the size of a football stadium. It was enormous. We observed it for about 3 or 4 minutes floating above the tree line at a consistent speed. It was extremely slow moving. It never changed direction and it made absolutely no sound at all. It’s hard to describe how massive and slow it was.
