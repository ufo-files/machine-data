il y en avait 3 qui étaient assez hauts et un proche du sol.
ils étaient sous la couverture nuageuse.

We were in our car me (doctor) my husband (teacher) and our daughter (student).
We left Aix en Provence at midnight and 5 minutes after wishing our family the New Year.
We were driving on the A55 motorway towards Manosque when we saw on our left above the town of Venelles 4 high UFOs of complex shape reminiscent of suspension lamps from the old days, i.e. a small bulge on the top, a large central bulge and a small bulge downwards, orange-red or golden red, in fairly hovering flight.
It was midnight 15.
there were 3 which were quite high and one close to the ground.
they were under cloud cover.
As I was driving at a speed of 110 km / h I wanted to park on the side of the highway which was little used at this time but I could not do so because it seemed dangerous to me.
Our daughter was particularly moved and distressed at the idea that I wanted to stop the car when usually she is in control of herself.
The UFOs were moving slowly and then the bottom one disappeared.
We took the exit ramp to Pertuis and the relief hid the UFOs that were at the end of the sighting behind our backs.

Then we saw one again to the north and then it disappeared.
