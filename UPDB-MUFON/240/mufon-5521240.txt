UFO chasing birds

On the highway from Jerez de la Frontera to Medina Sidonia (county of C1diz, Andalucia, Spain) we saw an UFO chasing birds. We were driving at around 100 km/h, and birds and UFO were slightly faster. The birds would change abruptly their flight direction whenever the UFO came too close to them. The UFO changed twice its shape and colors, as to adjust to the clouded sky. It also seemed to glow, but coldly, with nearly no light emission.
