Very Fast Red Orange Orb

I was outside having a smoke, letting the dogs out and a light flew from the south to the north in under 3 seconds. I thought it was a flare at first and was waiting for it to go out. It continued to shine until i lost site of it due to obstruction. Still can't believe it happened, i checked a trusted flight radar site and there was nothing that flew that direction. There was nothing in my area at that time.
