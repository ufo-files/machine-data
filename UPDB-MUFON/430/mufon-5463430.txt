{cak} object was sphere that looked mirrored ,highly polished or transparent similar to a chrome christmas orniment

My at the time was on a company golf outing at a local golf course when the foursome [wife incld] noticed a mirror like sphere hovering above the ground near the tree line at the end of the fairway ,after a few seconds the sphere just flew away in NE direction without making a sound .
at approx the same time my son and i were deer hunting from a tree stand about 20 miles to the NE and noticed a jet aircraft flying off in the distant sky and then saw the glass like mirrored sphere as it passed by 
my son and i both saw it only because we just happened to be looking in the right place at the right time
Not knowing what my wife and friend seen had seen ,when my son and i got home i said to her Let me tell you what we saw today ! Her reply was Only after what i tell you what we saw !!
That's when we compared time and direction  SAME OBJECT !!
FYI to get a visual understanding of what we all saw is to look at the self portrait by M C ESHER in that drawing he is holding what appears to be mirrored ball . the visual effect he captured is so very near to what we saw . not sure if clear ,chrome or almost invisable
