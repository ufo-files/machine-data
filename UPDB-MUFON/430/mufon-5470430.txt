{jpg} Husband and I watched for ~5 min. similar to space x seen over arizona around christmas.

Husband and I had just came home from grocery shopping. We saw a strange light to the west of us in the sky that had a hazy white light coming from the top and bottom of the object. I noticed about 30 seconds later it had moved so I began watching as it moved from west to east. We walked across the street to get a clear view away from trees. We watched for about 2 min and then the object suddenly had a massive plume of mist like light coming from it that bloomed into a tear drop plume and 1 single orb like light in the very tip of the tear drop plume. The mist like light slowly vanished over about 2 minutes and all light vanished. Assuming this is a space x launch but the launch happened about 5 hours before this sighting and 2 launched in one day from 2 different areas seems a bit far fetched. We tried to get video with the cell phone but were not successful because we watched too long before thinking about the phone!
