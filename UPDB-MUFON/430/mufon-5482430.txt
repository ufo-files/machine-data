{nd} It looked like another sun but smaller

Well, I drive up and down the 528 daily. I live in Orlando and work in Merritt Island. Lately I have been seeing strange entities in the sky. I have not thought much of it, because the sky is clearer in Merritt than Orlando. But the other night I noticed what looked like a star, but it was red. So I just assumed that it was Mars. Then on the morning of the 4th, I noticed a bright star. It was around 5:40am(and driving on 528, close to the coast), so the sun was coming up, but I noticed what seemed to be a star, but much bigger. Then within 30 seconds it disappeared. I told one of the other managers about it, and thought nothing of it. But when I was on my way home on the 6th I noticed something even stranger. I was on the 528 heading west, must have been between 8/8:30pm, and was on the southbound ramp to the 417 where I can see towards the direction of our airport, I saw what looked like a sun, in the clouds, at first I thought it was our sun then quickly realized that it was just about sunset. What was most strange, it had another orb or body by it. Almost as if was a satellite of some sort orbiting it. It was less than a 10th of the main orb, but when I tried to stop, to take a photo it was gone. I know we have a lot of secret military instillations in florida, but this did not look man made. Hopefully someone else saw it. Thank you, Ric.
