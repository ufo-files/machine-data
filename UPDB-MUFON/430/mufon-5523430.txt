Star like in the middle of an clear sunny day in LA

Was lying on my lounger just looking up at the migrating condors catching the up-drafts (saw about 27 in various groups) when i caught a glimpse of an object moving behind the tree tops at what appeared to be a great altitude. It was drifting like a white party balloon - until it stopped and remained motionless for about 10 mins. It shone like a star in the sky - but this was mid afternoon on a sunny and clear day with a deep blue sky. I had time call the wife and kids to come out and see it. Looked at it thru binoculars - then I decided to get the camera; by the time i came back it was no longer visible/there.
