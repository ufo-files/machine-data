{rjl} Spherical silverish metallic object vanished in the sky

(Milwaukee, Wisconsin Dec.22nd)
I viewed something i couldn't explain today around 1:47pm in the sky. I was heading south on south 35th street and making a right hand turn on west Loomis road.

Something got my attention by shinning in the sky, a spherical ball, metallic object, it had no wings, no propeller, no lights, no sound. I also noticed "NO CLOUDS" in the sky. I asked my wife what was it?, I was able to see it from my windshield and driver-side window for about 20 seconds, about time i turned to her to tell her look, what is that? It was gone! I pulled over to step out my vehicle to look; to make sure my van frame wasn't blocking my view, the strange thing is, i would've seen it fly off into the distance...there were no clouds, i would've seen it, it disappeared!
