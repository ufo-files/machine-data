Diamond shaped spacecraft [making a humming and low roaring sound almost a whistling noise} - {djs}.

At 8:12 p.m. on October 20, 2020 I witnessed a diamond shaped spacecraft that seemed to have appeared out nowhere making a humming and low roaring sound almost a whistling noise I was pulling into my driveway. I had the windows open and as I pulled into the driveway I was startled by the noise, then looking around I looked up and it was just like it dropped out of the sky very slow moving it flew in a SW direction over the power lines and tree tops and continued to fly over my backyard slowly descending in a SW direction. about 1 full minute.This craft was Diamond shaped and at every point there was a light. a Red light, Yellow light, white light, and a green light. I have never ever seen this before. It was exciting. Hopefully I will be able to witness another one.
