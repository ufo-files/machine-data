{tjd} It was like a blue ball of light with a long tail it appeared just as quickly as it disappeared

Our friend was pointing out the star that was mars while we were getting Italian ices with our kids at about a little after 9pm, in Suffolk county NY, Long Island. As we were looking Suddenly a bright brilliant baby blue colored ball appeared to be coming from right near the mars star. Hovering for a second or two then It headed west across the horizon. it was bright and created a long tail as it moved, the tail was the same brightness and color as the circular object and it was moving fast across the horizon away from the mars star. Then all at the same time it disappeared long tail and all. There was nothing where it disappeared in the sky. It was almost like someone flipped a switch and the bright blue ball of light was gone leaving nothing in its space. The 3 of us saw it and immediately knew it was something weird. I’ve seen shooting stars, planes, and all means of aircraft including all types of handheld flying toys.. this was something completely unique to itself. I can still perfectly picture it.
