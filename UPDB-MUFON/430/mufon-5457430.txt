{rjl} Bright Green Light

I was on our land with my husband and we were having a fire around 10 pm. I got up to put more wood on and looked up at the sky. I thought I saw the small white lights of an airplane but then it began to fall down towards the ground. Just as I realized it was falling and I began to panic thinking it was a plane in trouble, the small white light became a very bright green circle. I yelled at my husband, "oh my God, look". He did not even have time to turn his head to look before the green circle took off and disappeared out of sight. It was almost like it had seen me looking at it and disappeared into thin air. I could hardly contain myself and went on for over an hour about it! I was so disappointed my husband didn't get to see it too.
