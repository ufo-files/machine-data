{nd} Saw what I thought was a star,but it was cloudy so knew it was not star,it then descended.

Was driving home from work last night..passed by a church on my right,then coming up by the convenience store(left side of road) right up from the church,saw what I thought was a star on left side above a hill.Remembering that it was cloudy,I said to myself,thats no star.When I first saw it,approximately a mile away,maybe less,it was about the size of a basketball maybe a little larger(as I got closer,it was about the size of like a large sized storage shed.)It then descended behind a hill.As I drove closer to where it was,saw it in the trees above a house.Unsure whether it landed as I was a little spooked,having saw the same thing where I work(10/25/2013)beside Lake Lure,and wanted to get home before the kids went to bed..wish I would've stopped in retrospect.It emitted a bright yellowish glow and then as got closer was more orangish,appeared to be circular but could have been a different shape as the light was so bright,the shape was undiscernable.Also,when I first saw it,I was near the county line for Rutherford/McDowell counties on Montford Cove Road.would attach a map of the area,but have not figured out how yet..
