This report corellates to report by [name removed/cms/rjl] Sept 2, 2019. Dennis was aware of my experience, so he immediately told me about his sighting. It was dark and he was heading home going west on Shelby Dr and almost at Coro Lake, E. Shore Dr entrance. He witnessed a very large bright light at tree top level descending from his left to right acroos the road in front of him. This would have been into a wooded undeveloped area. Based on the path, it aligned 100% with the direction and elevation of my sighting. Overtime, my I would hear a humming from the woods but dismissed it as a power box. Sometimes I heard but not always. My mom and I decided to drive to the back of a subdivion to get as close to sound as we could and see a power station or box, but couldnt see or hear anything. In recent years I have used Google arial maps to track path of both sightings but I dont see any evidence of power box, power lines or past disruption. I was too scared, cautious to try to walk into the dense brush to find out where intermittent humming was coming from.
