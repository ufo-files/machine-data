A very bright, small object

Unable to sleep, I got out of bed at approximately 4:00 a.m. and went downstairs to the window bench to sit and look out of the living room window for awhile. There was no wind or rain and it was quiet in the wee hours. Suddenly, a bright small light about the size of a softball or a bit larger appeared in my peripheral vision so I focussed my attention on it. In retrospect, it was as if my neighbour (to the right) tossed a bright ball from their balcony upstairs and into the trees. It travelled a bit slower than if it was a softball and judging by it's arc, it seemed weightless so I knew it wasn't anything like a baseball or softball. It was all very strange and I have never seen anything like that before. It all happened quickly but it left quite an impression on me.
