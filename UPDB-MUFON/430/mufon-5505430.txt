{cak} light went up slow turned left fast and vanished

At aprox. 2:28 am morning of jan 14 2014 in Virginia Beach VA I was looking over the north east waiting to see if I could spot the three rockets being launched from Wallops island flight facility, out of the corner of my eye i had spotted a light that looked like a star, but i had not seen any other stars all night due to cloud cover. The only lights in the sky were the moon and jupiter right directly above me. i continued to stare at the light as it appeared to move upward slowly, i thought it was the first of the three rockets but it was strange that there was no trail of purple like i have seen before. i attempted to get a video with my phone but it was too dark. the star moved upward for about 15 seconds and then seemed to move left quite fast then looked as if it went down and the just vanished. i would love to hear if anyone saw this or has any info on what it might be.
