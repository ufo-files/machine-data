{tjd} Large UAP Hovering 20 Minutes

This craft was not visible to the human eye. I would not have seen it but for the fact that I was using my IPad to record the sunset. At one point you can hear my son's gf say, "I can't see it." You can then hear me exclaim, "I know! It's cloaked!!" I was using my laser pointer in an attempt to interact with it. You can also see flashes of what appear to be green lights directly to the left, as though it may have been far larger than it appeared. At various points you can also see white orbs flying around it. Although it seemed to disappear at the end of this video, it did reappear seconds later and I was able to get additional videos of it. On the final video, it simply shot into the sky vertically at high speed.
