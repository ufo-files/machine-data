Orb planes helicopter white trails

I was outside taking trash to my dumpster. The weather was a beautiful day. No clouds, no wind, a lot of sun light. The skies were clear with nothing in the sky in all directions. Out of nowhere, several planes (or what looked like planes) just dropped out of the sky. They were flying in distinct patterns with very large white trails behind them. I saw a large basketball shaped flying object in the sky at the end of one of the large white trails. After seeing this object, I saw what looked like a helicopter drop out of the sky which was following the large white trails from the planes. This helicopter went over my house. It was reflecting sunlight and was 100% totally silent. This event lasted just a few minutes and then the planes, silent helicopter, and flying basketball object disappeared just as fast as they appeared. Poof they were gone. I took a picture of the flying basketball shaped object on my phone. I also recorded for about 2-3 seconds the silent helicopter on my phone. If you look at the right hand corner of my picture you can see the basketball shaped flying object.
