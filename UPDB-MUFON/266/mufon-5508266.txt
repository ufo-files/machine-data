Two redish round-like objects flew over my home going East to West. They were going too fast to be jets or any other man-made/normal mode of air transport we have. Also it was how they flew over my home and toward the West that was the most amazing. They were in unison for a few seconds then the object on left sped up (as if they were not already going faster than anything we have on this planet). When object on the left sped up, the object on right started darting back and forth (faster/slower) and then got closer to object on left. As I watched them, the object on the right then began to go around the object on the left in sort of a half-circular pattern from front to back over and over and then both objects sped up more, as if that is possible again and I lost sight of them as they headed out toward Huntington Beach and other Beach cities.
