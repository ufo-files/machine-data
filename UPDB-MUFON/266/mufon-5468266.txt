Sited at very high altitude reflecting gold. Possibly moved slowly by the wind. Very irregular shape with apparent protrusions

Noticed a very strange misshapen object moving slowly through the sky at very high altitude. It was gold reflective and appeared to either change shape as the the reflected light pattern changed irregularly. Also observed by my wife. We got out 10X magnification binoculars but difficult to make out the precise shape. Got out high quality digital compact camera and took several shots at maximum optical magnification hoping to see better images by zooming into the shots. Thought it may be the space station at first but moved too slowly through the sky. May have been a partially deflated weather balloon. Not sure. Height probably between 25,000 to 30,000 feet. Took some more photos of Trans-Atlantic planes between 35,000 and 40,000 feet as reference photos on the same magnification to compare with photos of the object.
