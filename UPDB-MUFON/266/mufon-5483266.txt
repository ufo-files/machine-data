{dr}A white orb flying through clouds.

I was in the living room of my apartment, laying on my couch. I caught the light/orb out of the corner of my eye. As soon as I noticed, it shot off in another direction then it seemed to be going. It was no white balloon or a bird. It made a deliberate change in it's path, fast. Like, it knew I seen it. I haven't told anyone. It happened in less than half a minute. But, I've started keeping my eyes on the sky again. It's path took it behind the building in front of us, but it didn't reappear as it should have from the other side of the building... It disappeared. That was that. There was no time, at all, for me to film it.
