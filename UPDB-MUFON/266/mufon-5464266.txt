I saw a white ring hovering silently and still above my street

I was at home when I saw an extremely bring light outside my home, I thought someone had their main beam headlights shining into my window, it was around 3 am, so I went outside to see what it was when I saw an extremely bright light hovering above the telephone lines in my street. it was completely silent and still, I looked at it for a matter of seconds and it went away, didn't fly off the light just stopped and then there was nothing there. during this time my dog was barking at the sky out of the window, and what felt like just a few seconds was over half an hour. the next day i felt very tired and weak and the leaves on the tree outside began to turn even though it was summer, a few days later they became green again. the light was very white in a ring about 15ft in diameter and didn't pulse or flicker just solid light. I was bright enough to illuminate the whole area, I doubt I was the only person who saw this as I live in a built-up area and the height of it meant it could be seen on more than just my own street.
