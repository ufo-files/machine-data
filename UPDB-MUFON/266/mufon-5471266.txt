{dr}triangle shaped, moved quickly, verified by another person

I went out to have a cigarette at approx. 2am. I witnessed a single light in the sky miles away that looked like a star. It then began moving in a figure 8 and 90 degree turns. I awoke my father to verify what I was seeing. I tried to film it but my camera on my phone would not pick it up. I watched for 20 minutes then went inside. An hour later I came out and same thing, still there. My last cigarette at approx. 430am it flew over my house and two more lights appeared making it triangle shaped. I lost track of it after about 2 minutes.
