{tjd} I WAS ON MY FATHERS FARM AND SAW WHAT I THOUGHT WAS A PTERODACTYL CRUISING ALONG

THIS CASE WAS TYPED AND FILED AT MUFON HQ FROM A WRITTEN REPORT SENT IN BY THE WITNESS. FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18809 SUBMITTED IT TO CMS ON OCTOBER 11TH, 2018. FI CAN REQUEST THE ORIGINAL FROM MUFON HQ TO BE SENT TO THEM.
---------------------------------------------------------------
THIS EVENT TOOK PLACE AT MY FATHER'S FARM SOUTH OF SAN BENITO. MY FRIENDS AND I WERE OUT ON MY ACRE WHEN WE HEARD BIRDS SHATTERING, WE LOOKED TOWARD THE SOUTH AND WITNESSED A PTERODACTYL COASTING ALONG A DITCH. IT HAD A WING SPAN OF ABOUT 10 FEET. IT SUDDENLY WENT STRAIGHT UP DID A COUPLE OF LOOPTY LOOPS FOLDED ITS WINGS INWARD AND LIKE A V SHAPE AND FLEW STRAIGHT INTO A WORM HOLE. THERE ARE MANY REFUGE'S IN THE RIO GRANDE VALLEY. I HAD HEARD OF BIG BIRD SIGHTINGS IN THE 1970'S. HELL THEY EVEN MADE A SONG ABOUT IT ON THE RADIO.
