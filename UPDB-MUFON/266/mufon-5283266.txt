Cigar shaped UFO sightings

Approximately, August/September 2014
Brownsville, Texas

At around 3:00-4:00 pm on a sunny afternoon, my husband and I were alone in our neighborhood pool.
As we looked up into the sky, we began to notice several groups of 4-5 white cigar shaped flying objects. Approximately twelve groups of these objects eventually glided over us in the course of about 8-10 minutes. They were grouped very closely together, floating in total silence but in a direct course from the South (Mexico). They were gliding horizontally across the sky. There were absolutely no observable windows, wings, tails, jet trails or means of propulsion. 
It was obviously very difficult to determine their size or how high they were flying. My husband’s very best guess is that each object was approximately 100 ft. across and that they were flying approximately 20,000 ft. above the ground. 
I had no phone or camera with me but, immediately, when we returned home, I called the police, Border Patrol, the local airport and even the local FBI. None of them would confirm that there had been any other reports of this unusual phenomenon.
