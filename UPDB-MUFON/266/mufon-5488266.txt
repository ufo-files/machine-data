{cak} Red circular object

My hubby and I drive to work together. We saw a red circular object that "glowed" red traveling slowly but smoothly NW to West. We both saw it, I had seen it before a year and a half prior. They seem to come this time of year...it appeared to be trying to disappear because it flashed a couple times slowly as I got out my camera and then disappeared! Of course I thought! Now when I say "flashed" it was more like when something electrical is shorting out. I know what a meteor, flashing airplane and helicopter looks like and this was none of those ! I know for a fact it was not any flying object known to man.
