Approx. 6-7 lights 2 red, one at each end. 4-5 blue green in the center between the red, making a straight line of lights.

I was walking my dog, to my left in the sky I saw a plane in the SW, going North. then off in the distance to the West, above some tree in the distance there were these odd lights. they were stationary and sitting at about an 35 degree angle. at first I thought it was a plane, but it look to not be moving. I kept walking and periodically checking on it, but it never changed. This was over about 15 min. before where I was walking and I started to loose sight of it behind the trees in the distance, from where I was walking. I walked about a 1/4 of a mile, then turned to make my way back. When I got back to where I started to loose sight of it, it was still there, and was till I walked out of sight of it again. I would say total time of the event was approx. 45 min. with no change in the object. It was a straight line of lights 1 red on each end, and 4-5 blue/green in the center. At the distance it would have been to big to be a drone. I would say, where I saw it above the trees, if it was right above them (but it was further in the distance) it would have been at least 6-8 ft long. using the span of the tree tops for a measurement.
