It was bright green shaped like tear drop

My friend and I were driving down Old Norcross and before we passed Oakland Road we saw an object colored bright green for like five second, it was shaped like a tear drop it seemed to be descending towards downtown Lawrenceville or towards that way. I have never seen any aircrafts that uses green light and moves that fast. I ride a bike and I have seen aircrafts move fast mach speed we were speeding enough to the point where the object would still be visible but it just suddenly disappeared, first time seeing anything like that, after posting it on facebook some other people also said that theyve seen it for a second. Could it just me a meteor? It was too green, large, and it was faster than a jet or any airplane the odd thing was that we didn't even hear a sound. It wasnt green lantern and this is not a joke. We tried to catch it on the iphone but it just disappeared.
