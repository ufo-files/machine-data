Fireball slowly descending.

An object resembling a large fireball with glowing edges and a transparent interior was witnessed and filmed at 6:43 am , February 22nd, 2020 in Port Arthur, Texas. The object slowly descended until it was out of sight. No noise was heard. This object was viewed from the ground, and was north-northeast of my position. It was witnessed for approximately 15 seconds before filming began with my cell phone; 30 seconds was captured on video before sight was lost behind a one-story structure.
