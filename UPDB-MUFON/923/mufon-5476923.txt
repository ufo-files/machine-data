{rjl} Light traveled right to left in big dipper/little dipper region

Outside facing north, looked up towards north star area, glanced rightward saw moving object. Object moving right to left, east to west. Object was between ursa major and ursa minor. Object traveled from last star in handle of ursa major toward north star in straight line and grew dimmer, eventualy faded from view. Object had luminosity of a common star, did not pulse, steady light.ISS search indicated no sightings in area at this time on this date. Object observed 4/21/2010 at appx 23:28 hrs (10:28 pm). Duration lasted appx one to two minutes. Object observed between last star of ursa major and north star. Object did not reach north star before fading out. Speculation- possible reflection of x-37 spacecraft.
