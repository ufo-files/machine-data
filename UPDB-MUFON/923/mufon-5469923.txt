{jpg} I remember seeing 3 ufos with 3 orbs of light each above my front yard as a child

I WAS 3 OR 4 YEARS OLD STANDING IN THE GRASS OF MY FRONT YARD IT WAS A VERY CLEAR NIGHT, I COULD SEE ALL THE STARS IN THE SKY. THEN ALL OF A SUDDEN 3 SHIPS APPEARED EIN A UPSIDE DOWN V FORMATION EACH HAD 3 LARGE ORBS OF LIGHT EMANATTING FROK THEM. THE IMAGE IN MY MIND IS STATIC I ONLY REMEMBER THEM BEING STATIONARY. SOON AFTER I HAD WEIRD FEELINGS IN MY LEG AND I HAVE A BLUE DOT WHERE I BELIEVE THEIR IS AN IMPLANT.
