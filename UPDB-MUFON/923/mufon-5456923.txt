{rjl} Dots flying under Space Station

I started to watch the You Tube live NASA feed from the International Space Station. I observed three dark gray dots keeping track with the ISS. At first, I thought it might be the actual shadow of the space station, but there were three. 

Then I thought they may be satellites, but they flew under and into cloud cover. Then I wondered if they could be planes, but as they were keeping pace with the ISS, they would have to have been travelling at over 17, 000 miles an hour.

I started to look up dots seen from the space station and ufos flying inder the space station, on my phone. I used the words "right now". Suddenly, the live feed was disconnected and an error from NASA came up saying the feed had "reached the end of it's life" and was no longer viable and may never be resumed.

I did not know whom to ask or report this to, so I am reporting it here.
