A large green ball of fire flew over an apartment building and overhead, continued past and beyond another building. I lost sight at that point. I tried to find a crash site as I assumed it was a meteor but nothing was found.

One night in July 2011 in the town of Stettler Alberta, close to 11 or midnight, I was leaving my friends apartment and as I was getting in my car, I looked up at the apartment building in front of me and witnessed what looked like a large green ball of fire streaking across the sky. The object looked quite low, couldn't have been much more than 100ft. From where I was, the object looked to be nearly the size of a snowplow, or other large piece of machinery. The object had no defined shape as it appeared like a ball of fire. At the time, I thought it was a meteor, so I tried to find where it landed. I found no evidence of where it landed, and there were no reports in the news of meteors afterwords. It wasn't until the spring of this year (2017) that I read several stories about green fireball UFO's dating back to the 1930's, it was at this time that I realized what I actually saw.
