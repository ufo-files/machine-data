Bright Blue Flash

I was outside of my house around 1:45am, letting my dog out before I went to bed. I was having a smoke and wasnt paying much attention to my surroundings, when a blue flash came out of the corner of my eye. I looked up, as it got my attention, about 5 seconds passed, and a very bright blue light/orb type event took place outside of a fast moving cloud. It seemed very odd and surprising to me, it was unlike anything ive ever seen, i thought it may have been lightning at first, but when i saw the second and brighter flash occur between clouds in mid air, i knew this was something out of the ordinary. The weather conditions at the time were partially cloudy skies, with wind gusts up to 35mph. Could this have been some type of rare weather phenomena, or was it something unexplainable?
