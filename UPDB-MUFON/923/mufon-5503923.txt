massive light beam descended from sky to the ground

On the night of October 31, 2007, I was with a group of friends and the plan was to have a small get-together. Before we settled in at my friend’s house to have some drinks, some errands needed to be ran in preparation. At the time I lived off Rt. 32 in Sykesville MD, as well as my friend’s house that this all was to take place. Our houses are considered in Howard County, but are on the rural outskirts next to the border of Carrol County MD. As our geographical location is more conveniently located to Eldersburg (commercial strip of Carrol County), we most often would travel to this area to purchase goods. Being a rural area with dense forests and farmland mixed in, many back-roads crossed through the area providing a more direct route away from the usual main roads. After going to the store, we were traveling one of these back roads to return to the residence of my friend. There were 3 people in the truck, a 94 Toyota Pickup- my friend and driver C.G., his girlfriend "H", and myself in the far-right seat. 

Traveling along Marriottsville Road 2, suddenly a bright flash occurred and completely saturated everything in thick intense blue - white light; even looking away from it as it flashed was very intense. Instinctively, we all turned to see what happened, my friend quickly pulled to the side of the road and stopped. The flash and extreme intensity light happened very quickly like lightening, but slower. And that seemed to be the initial phase or energy pulse required to trigger this. starting at the flash, a beam of light the color of lightning, shaped like a laser beam, slammed down from the sky to the ground at appx. a 15-degree angle. 

Adjacent to the road we were on is a few fields separated by a tree line, and it hit down 2 fields in. It hit within .75 miles of where we were and given the size of the beam to the distance, this beam had to be about 15-30 feet in diameter. Now this is where it gets weird given all I know about physics and light- the only rational name I can give this is a 'particle beam' due to the next set of actions this energy beam exhibits:

•	The initial flash and touchdown of this phenomenon took about 3 seconds. 
•	Afterwards, instead of shutting off and disappearing as one would expect light to behave, the beam started to dim. 
•	As the intensity faded, a smoke trail became visible spiraling down the entire outer circumference of the beam. The way the beam diminished its luminosity was also in contrast to normal light properties. 
•	The beam didn't dim as if it was still originating at the source, but it rather dissolved into the air- only suggesting that the light itself wasn't emitting from its origin but from the molecules of the air itself. The whole process took nearly 15-20 seconds.

I am a very intelligent man, and I learn all about science and technology that I can. I have a healthy level of skepticism, but I accept the outcomes that evidence suggests no matter how unlikely it may seem. And I know that publicly known science and technology can’t produce anything that behaves this way, although certainly possible. The energy itself required to produce a coherent beam of energy of this magnitude would be extraordinarily large and need to be capable of rapid discharge, which would most likely create an electromagnetic surge. 

To see a UFO (which I have) is something terrifying itself, but I can at least scientifically explain how it can fly and even rationalize ETs being on Earth if they aren't our UFOs. However, I can't explain how this light beam behaved the way it did. I haven't even heard anybody report anything remotely similar. The event itself baffled the three of us and terrified us that "what the @#$%!?" is the only thing worth saying for 5 minutes as we drove as fast as we could out of there.

Interestingly I have heard about government doing stuff around this area, and I also saw government driving white unmarked trucks bigger than I've ever seen down a highway close by at 3 AM, magically no other cars or civilian activity could be seen anywhere either....
