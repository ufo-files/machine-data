{rjl} Black Massive Triangle 3 red light craft on 90 west from Buffalo

Was driving at 01:50 in the morning after the Bills game and when I was on 90 west 68 miles from PA I noticed a massive red light coming at me. Then once the front light passed me I saw another light on my far right and another one slightly to my left going over me. I noticed from the base of the lights that the craft was a flat black and not making any noise. Also that the craft was going about 60-80 miles an hour 90 east over the tree tops. I first thought it was a Stealth Bomber but because it was so low and made no noise or that it could even fly at that low of speeds is crazy. Im just mad that I didn't just pull over a chased the thing and take pictures. Im just reporting this just incase anybody else saw the same thing that night.
