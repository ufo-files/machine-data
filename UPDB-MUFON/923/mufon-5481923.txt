I was looking for Ursa Major for my wifes IPhone App for finding stars and constellations. I had just found the constillation and from my left, a bright light...........looking like a flaming torch ball came down from on high, traveling south west to northwest. I watched for about 10 secs, noticing a black shape forming in front of the flames. It started out as a ballish form, then morphed into a geodesic form, kind of a parallelogram, then into total flame and disappeard behind the horizon and out of sight.
