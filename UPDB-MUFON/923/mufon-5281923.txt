Saw a white orb about the size of a full moon slowly and silently traveling North to South directly over my home.

Got home later than usual. Wife had made me a nice Corona with salt n lime. She had already gone to bed. Grabbed my beer and walked outside to check my greenhouse. Tilted up to take a drag aaaaand WTF?! What is that?! White moon sized orb silently just easing along about 5000ft? Right at the whispy almost not there cloud layer. No colored lights. No flashing. Just a white ball of light about the size of the full moon. Grabbed my phone… pos doesn’t even show anything up there. Ran back in to get my Aurora Pro… 10 seconds… gone. Nothing anywhere. Night vision shows not a thing in the sky but some stars. So I just hung out and watched for a few minutes. Then over my left shoulder (facing almost due East) flashing caught my eye. Boom!!! What is THAT?! Took video. Different object. This had three lights in triangular pattern with a flashing center light and what looked like lights shining off to the maybe 20-30 degrees each side. Have that video.
