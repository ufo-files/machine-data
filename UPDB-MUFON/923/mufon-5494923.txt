'V' shaped craft with extremely bight white lights flew straight over us.

I was watching TV at around 9:30pm on the 31st March 2015 when my wife spotted a very bright light through the window. We regularly see planes but this was very different and it grabbed her attention. As we got up to take a closer look it was heading straight for us is a south westerly direction.
Upon closer inspection it appeared to resemble the international space station, but about the size of the planet Venus and very bright. I instantly grabbed my camcorder and my wife and I went outside to get a closer look. When I zoomed in on the object (that was obviously getting bigger the closer it got) I noticed the camcorder actually diffused the brightness of the object enough to see two lights close together at the front of the craft and 1-2 lights either side at the aft end of the craft, but no tail or strobe lights. It was hard to tell if it was making any noise as there was a plane in the south east to where we were standing that my wife could see. But she seems to think the craft wasn't making any noise.
I filmed for around 50 seconds before the object disappeared over our house.
