Moved west to the east stopped and moved north, lowered to the tree level moved north again and stopped then dissapeared.

I was waiting on my front porch for a food delivery when I looked up and noticed a very large bright object moving slowly in the sky due west.
It stopped suddenly and did not move for several minutes as two commercial airplanes flew past it in opposite directions. When it moved again I ran into my house and retrieved my video camera and returned outside and filmed it moving closer as well as moving downward.It stopped and hovered for quite a while maybe 18 min or more above the house across the street from mine.

I took screen captures from the video and blew them up and saturated them with color, editing them into the video. I have posted it on my YouTube channel and on the disclosure.TV channel.
