Red lights moving in line, following southern ridge of valley between Saltville and Abingdon, VA.

Occurred between 10:14-10:20 PM (approx.) on evening of May 7, 2020, in Washington Co., VA, near Saltville. Saw what at first appeared to be five bright red (fire-engine shade) lights appear from southeast (one of us saw the lights appear as if they were gaining altitude and coming "up" over the hill from our perspective) and then move from southeast to southwest over several minutes. Regular blinking, regular patterns, appeared to move mostly in straight line until when, halfway down ridge, a larger solid white light appeared and the bright red lights appeared to be following it. When formation was at our far southwest, formation appeared to assume/present a wedge shape. After this, one of the red lights appeared to make a direct right turn towards the west. Videos have had audio removed, and filters applied for clarity of visual. (This report combines observations of two witnesses.)
