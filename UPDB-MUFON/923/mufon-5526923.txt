Green object in the sky

my wife saw a greenish object go southbound in the sky. My family was watching tv, and happened to see it from our living room window. They only saw it for a minute or so, until it disappeared out of view, behind a neighbor's house. It was oval-shaped, with a greenish glow. It kept a steady speed, and did not fluctuate in size. Also, it did not give off a tail or like meteorite would. There were no blinking lights. It's path was north to south. This was not a helicopter or a plane, but it could have been a meteorite, even though it did not have the normal signs of one.
