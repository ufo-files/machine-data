Unknown lights in the southeastern sky

On Wednesday, 7/22, I decided I was going to try to photograph the comet Neowise. I realized that I needed to do this somewhere outside of town to avoid the city's light pollution. I drove to a rural area only 5 miles outside of Rockford and parked on a little used gravel road. I proceeded to set my camera on a tripod and aimed it toward the Big Dipper in the northwestern sky..
I was not able to get a photograph of the comet. I couldn't locate it's exact position and there wasn't anything around to successfully focus my camera on. At some point as I was ready to leave I turned my gaze to the southeastern sky and saw what looked like a very bright star or possibly a planet. Since it was so bright I could easily focus my camera on it subsequently took several photos. I then viewed the photos on my camera and zoomed in on the object.  To my surprise, it was neither a star or a planet. The photos showed a bright object with a "trail" of several smaller objects beside it.
I should note that this object was not moving as far as I could detect.
As I drove back to town I kept my eye on the object the entire way. When I arrived home I was took several more photos from my back yard. It was still in the southeastern sky and had not apparently moved.
