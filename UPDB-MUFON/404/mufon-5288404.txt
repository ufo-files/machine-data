Glowing Orb observed from William Nicol Drive flying over Sandton

Took an Uber from 110 Culross back to 14 Winslow, Parkwood Rosebank

Driving in the Uber in a southern direction towards Sandton/Rosebank, on William Nicol Drive, approximately at the Dover Rd intersection, elevated viewpoint, overlooking towards Sandton, I observed an orange, glowing orb coasting at a constant speed of 250 + km per hour, 200 to 300 meters above the ground, over Sandton / Riverdale area. Approximate diameter of a large automobile / minibus. It travelled left to right, following  a curved trajectory, eventually turning towards Sandton. 

There was also a small aircraft with lights, flying over Sandton closeby, a little higher than the object, a few hundred meters back and to the left.
