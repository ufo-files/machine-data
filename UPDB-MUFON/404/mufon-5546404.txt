Big Light Ball moving

I don't know the exact date and time, however it must have been in October 2006. I was living in my parent's flat and was playing Counter-Strike or so in that moment. I was just looking out off the window for, let's say, the last time before I shutted the window shade.

I was looking north west when I saw a big white ball of light. First I thought of a meteoroid or a shooting star, but that thing moved way to slow for a shooting star. 

I'm not sure how far away it way, but I would estimated 15 to 20 kilometers. It moved away very slowly in the direction I looked and disappeared. I also saw a tail, however, I still think it was not a brock coming from outer space, because that day a few clouds were in the sky so I think it moved behind a cloud and lit through.

I went back to my computer and "back to business", and thought of some "natural" source. However I don't think so now. 

I don't know what it was, but I know what it was NOT: neither a plane, nor a helicopter, nor a shooting star or something else.
