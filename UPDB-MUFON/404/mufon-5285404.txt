A red Light is flying over the sky, droops bright white lights, then it fly away at a high speed while dropping sparks

(Sorry for the poor english, i'm german)
On October 11th 2012 at 4 p.m, a red Light appears on an cloudy day. I saw the Light in the corner of my eye in the west and watched it for ca. 5 minutes, then it disappeared. A Friend of mine was with me in the Room and saw it too, also the following. (He wants to remain Anonymus). After another 5 minutes the Light reappeard on the same position it vanished from, I was watching when it come back, the light simple was back on. It was still bright outside, we havent seen anything on the position. The light immediately flows upside, after a wile it flows ca. 45° upside right. The it stops, a very bright white light like a Flare is dropping from the Light, it falls very fast down, then stops midair, it looks like the white light stops and then is flowing upside a little. The red light flys on, then the same happens again. After that the red light is flying with a plane-like speed fast away, while it drops red-orange sparks within a dark-violet trail. Then it was gone. The white lights stayed for a few minutes, then they simple extinguished. I know from 2 more people that tehy saw the same Lights. During the Observation I was 14 Years old and neither of us had a mobile phone witch can take pictures.
