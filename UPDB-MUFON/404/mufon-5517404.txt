(gs) 4-5 red unblinking lights traveling north, loose formation, moved together ascended out of sight

Husband and I arrived home at 9 pm, he gets out of car and points "Look, look!" I turn and over the roof of our house we see 4 or 5 red lights, the lead light brighter than the rest and looks to be traveling at different altitudes. At first I thought they were flares or fireworks, but they traveled rather quickly with a slight back and forth or sinusoidal movement, no sound at all (and its very quiet at our house). There was some marine layer cover, not sure how low, but the lights were clearly visible and lit the clouds. They traveled from south to north, we watched them for about 2 minutes and when they were directly overhead they began to move together and ascend out of sight, one at a time, with the lead and brightest light disappearing last. 
Obviously throughout this sighting husband and I are asking "What are they?" Neither of us has ever seen anything quite like this before. They did not drop like flares and did not drop any phosphorous material. The movement just before they ascended and disappeared was in keeping with flare movement. 

The lights did not blink, as one would expect from a plane or other aeronautical device.
