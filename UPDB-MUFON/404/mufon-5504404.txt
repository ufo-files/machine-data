Downloaded JPL Mars Photo PIA16128

As a retiree, I've had time to examine JPL's Mars Photo Journal([Link moved to FI section/cms/tg] ) . After downloading PIA16128, I immediately noticed a white object flying above Mars surface. I also observed 4 streams of "jet wash" pushing downward - reminding me of a Harrier Jet in hover mode. I also see a faceless humanoid object sitting in an open cockpit. I am not certain of the distance scale for this photo but I suspect the craft and creature are "lilliputian" in size. I suspect that the creatures bone density is weak due to low gravity hence supporting only a small frame.
