{nd} UFO was like nothing I seen before

I was out side looking at the stars. It was a clear cool night. I was laying on the hood of my car facing the west. What made me look twice is a bright white light traveling northwest to the southwest just above and following the tree line that runs that direction. I thought it was a plane going to our local airport. I looked to see if any more aircraft was in the air and there was none. So I glued my eyes to it. Thinking it was a little to low. As soon as I was locked on it. It was like it knew I was watching it. The white light that was traveling southwest now turned strait north got above the power lines over our road that runs east to west, it made a 90 degree turn to our road the bright white light went out. But I could still see it. It had glowing light blue lights on each corner of the craft. It was just above the power lines. I watched in amazement. It flew right above me slowly. I was not worried I was at peace. I was thinking take me if you want me. It was a flat triangle. It was like a liquid carbon fiber it reflected light. No noise, no exhaust, no heat. I watched it fly slowly to the east as it flew until I could not see it any more.
