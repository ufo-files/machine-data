{cak} 2 orb-like objects slowly flying over treetops. No sound

I was on the phone, walking around outside my house when I saw two metallic orbs flying over the treetops across the street from my house around sunset. The objects were hovering a dozen feet or so above the trees. The objects made no sound what so ever. They had no exterior lights, but were very reflective. They were moving at a speed of maybe 5-10 mph. They seemed to just barely move. There were traveling a few feet apart, and every few seconds the distance between the two would vary slightly. They reflected the setting sun, but I couldnt tell exactly what the surface was made of. The surface looked like highly polished silver; completely spherical. 

I watched the objects for 3-4 minutes, then decided to get my telescope for a closer look. It only took about 30 seconds to grab my telescope and come back to the front of the house. But by the time I got back out, the objects were gone. I looked all around, but could find no trace of them anywhere.

I live in the direct flight path of four airports; Nashville International, Murfreesboro Airport, Shelbyville Airport, and Smyrna Airport. I am very familiar with comercial and military aircraft, as I see them everyday. But this was something altogether different. I cant honestly say "what" they were, but I know what they were not...
