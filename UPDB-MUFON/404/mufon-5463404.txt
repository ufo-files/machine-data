{cak} We were driving on 116th and we saw this craft on my right and he sped away

This event happened almost 50 years ago. My highschool boyfriend was driving us to a friend's house on 116th Ave in Kent, WA. I saw the craft hovering over the back yard area of this house to my right. He saw it, too and quickly drove to our friends house further down the road. We knew what it was. I don't remember being afraid, just curious. We picked up our friends and followed the craft down the hill toward the Green River in Kent. We pulled off the road we were on, and got out of the car. A policeman pulled up behind us, listened to our story, and told us all to go home. He said that what we were doing was dangerous, and he'd take it from there. So, we did. I didn't get his name, or follow up on the episode. We were a bunch of teenagers. I'd do things differently, if it happened today. If you showed me pictures, I'd point it out, and give a better description. We didn't have cell phones back then.
