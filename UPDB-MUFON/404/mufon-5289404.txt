Blue-green oblong orb hovered, then rapidly accelerated northwest while climbing and appeared to stretch/trail before disappearing.

I left work early that night due to COVID-19 symptoms, I can't remember the exact time, but it had to be between 11pm and 2am. I live in the Crestmont apartment complex right off of bells ferry on williams drive, and about halfway down bells ferry to where I would turn onto my apartment road, I caught something very bright out of my peripheral vision. I looked to my left (westward) while driving and witnessed an oblong oval shaped object that had a soft blueish green color. It was glowing brightly, didn't seem to be pulsing but just had a solid, uniform color. It also didn't seem to illuminate much around it, if that makes sense.. there was no glare. It was like a frosted light bulb almost.

It was only there for maybe a second or two before it almost instantaneously shot out what I'd estimate as northwest at an insane speed. It also climbed upwards during this in a shallow curve, the best way I can think to describe it is the star trails you see when a ship in Star Wars is jumping to hyperspace. It was like it was there, it accelerated from 0 to very fast in an instant, the light on it sort of "stretched" as it moved, and then it was gone.

Due to the distance and height, my first reaction was "weird street light I hadn't noticed over there before".. but then I realized it was too high for a street light if it was one. But then, that thought went out of my head when it moved. This whole encounter took place over maybe 5-7 seconds of time. It was the only time I've ever seen something in the sky that made me go "What the f***" out loud to myself. I haven't seen anything like that since.
