Still in disbelief it was shocking

I was sitting in my daughters home when she started yelling for me to come outside. "mom", she said. "I don't know what it is, I just don't know, hurry." As I got outside, I couldnt believe my eyes,there was these three bright lights and none of them were moving they also werent that far from us. She kept asking what is it? I said in disbelief, "It must be a UFO." I ran to get my husband who was asleep in the chair. I yelled at him to get up and come see this, we don't know what it is. While I was getting my husband, the UFO held still and never moved. Then once we got outside, One light went out and then the other light went out too. It almost looked as if it seperated, But then it started to move right in front of us when all the sudden the third light went out and we saw something like maybe vapor fall out from underneath. But when the lights left there was no noise or anything. We took a picture of the lights and what we think is the UFO. It shows four lights but we never saw more then three lights with our own eyes. The shape was in a saucer, as in one of the old toy tops that you spin. There was also two beams of light coming from underneath the UFO. I was astonished seeing this with my own eyes.
