{nd} stargazing. Star-like glow appeared from no where, bright bluish-white glow bigger than Venus, 3 seconds, then disappeared. Occurred three times.

I came home from concert. For some reason, as I never do this and that is what is strange to me is that I did so immediately when I closed my garage for some odd prompting, I stood there on the street and stared at the stars (a clear night!) Just looking for about a minute. Not moving, just standing there. Suddenly, I saw one "star “appear, turn very bright like Venus and perhaps a slight blue tint, then disappear. It was visible for about 3 seconds. About two minutes later it appeared again, very bright like before, then disappeared. Then I saw a very fast "shooting star" in the same area of the sky but not the same area, move northeast and disappear. I don't think it was a shooting star, though. Then the bright "star" light appeared again, very bright, then disappeared. It seemed to be slowly moving due east by its third appearance. I didn't see it again.it only was visible for about three seconds for each time. **I went inside my house and googled this. Many people report seeing a very bright star and disappear. Some are on video (you tube.) I'm not the only one who has seen this. People from across the world have been reporting this on forums and you tube. **
