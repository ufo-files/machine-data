{rjl} 2 objects thought to be two distant stars close to one another travel at rapid speed.

My son and I were throwing away the garbage, when gazing at the moon, we were also talking about the clouds covering the moon. Next to the moon were two bright stars, as we were walking we noticed the two stars moving at the same exact speed, but the distance was so high, at the level of the stars, they were traveling at the same speed in an exact movement, they finally traveled south and out of sight as the clouds covered our sight of them. They were definitely two rapidly traveling objects and too far up to be planes. Later that night, we heard of the Russian Missile on the news. These two light were not missiles.
