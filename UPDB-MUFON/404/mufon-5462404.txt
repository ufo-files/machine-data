{rjl} Brilliant light moved from west to east before shooting out circle much larger than itself

It was 11 o'clock at night I stepped out in the backyard to look at the stars. which I do a lot. I always see satellites that move from south to north that appear similar in size and brightness of the stars in the sky. The light that I seen was much brighter than the stars and much larger than the stars in the sky. As it traveled to the east just past my house I had to step out past my garage to keep it in site. It appeared to project a white ring in front of the object that reflected was as brightly as the moving sphere. The moving sphere flew into the center of the projected ring. When the sphere entered the glowing ring the ring then collapsed around the object and they both disappeared from the sky, leaving no trace. 
From my point of view it was high enough to reflect light from the sun, being as bright as the moon when it is in full stages. That night the moon was in the south sky. The objects I seen were in the northern sky. 
Before this event I held the opinion that wormholes didn't exist except in the minds of eggheads physicists. 
I believe what I witnessed was a flying craft create a wormhole of which it used to leave our star system, or our timeband.
