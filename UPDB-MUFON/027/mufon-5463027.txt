{jpg} At kings island parking lot. Seen object overhead. Silver. Looked like mercury bottom. Very hot.

I was in Cincinnati Ohio kings island parking lot with my kids and their mom. It's an unusually extremely hot day. I looked up to the sky and first thing I saw was an object. It was some thing I never seen before. It was shiny and bottom of the sphere looked like liquid mercury moving back and forth. The object was still and looked close up and far away at the same time. I felt excited and had static charge and so hot. I wanted to tell my kids to look at it. As soon as I spoke the words and turned our heads it was a quick pause them shot straight up out of sight in a split second and left a hole in a very thin cloud.
