On July 2, 2015 I went shopping for furniture. The store was closed until ten am. I decided to leave. I noticed a wiggly object to the South. It looked like the one I saw the night before, like an extremely bright star. The road was empty and I pulled over to see it. I had pictures from the night before but now I would have one in full daylight. The Orb started to make gigantic letters. It would start on a path and change its direction at extreme angles to form the letters. The bright Orb made the first set of letters "GOALIEN". Since I was able to understand the letters, I decided to take pictures. I took it one step further and videotaped it as it was occurring. Then I saw the Orb on a different path. On the next row the Orb wrote the word "KABOOM". Goosebumps went up and down my arms. The last row said "7/3". My body finally came to the conclusion that this was a hostile message. I was shaking to think what could happen to Dallas, Texas and I must act immediately. I was looking for other people as a witness. Nobody was looking at the skies. I saw a couple of airplanes fly above me but they didn't seem affected. I waited a few more seconds thinking that this was the end of the message and then I saw the Orb create a human face. I gasped and could hardly breathe. The message was intended for us. The Orb disappeared. I decided to leave and send my information to MUFON.
