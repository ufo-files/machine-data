I witnessed 2 blue lights around the outside with a yellow light in the middle and a beam of light coming out of the craft the beam seamed to bend at a 45 degree angle and panned the area like a search light. As the ufo vanished a helicopter followed it.

I was feeling over hot so I went to sit on my windowsill and as I looked over towards Canterbury university I saw 3 lights 2 blue and a yellow light inbetween the blue ones. It was completely silent. As it flew towards Canterbury cathedral a beam of white light came out of the craft and started scanning over Canterbury. The beam wasn't like anything I'd seen before after a few seconds of scanning the area the beam of light suddenly bent at a 45 degree angle halfway down the beam it almost looked like a tick of light. The beam turned off and the craft disappeared behind the tree's. About 3 minuets after the craft disappeared a helicopter appeared over Canterbury university with its search light on witch was completely different to the beam from the craft. You could hear the helicopter as it came over the university and you could clearly tell the original craft was nothing like the helicopter. The helicopter followed roughly the same path as the UFO and disappeared over the tree line although I could still hear the helicopter for a few minutes after it disappeared. The UFO was larger then the helicopter.
