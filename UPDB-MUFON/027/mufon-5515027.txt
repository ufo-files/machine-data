{cak} Glowing object pulsated in white with red-orange pulsating on bottom. Hovered for a few minutes and disappeared.

I was at home in my kitchen getting a glass of water to take to bed,it was sometime between 11pm and midnight. I walked by my sliding glass door in my kitchen and a light caught my eye. I looked out the window and noticed a bright white pulsating light hovering in the SE direction, probably close to a mile away. At first I thought is was a light on a tower, I checked this morning in daylight and there is no tower in that area. I did see planes pass by and could tell this object was not a plane. I watched the object for 10 minutes and it just continued to pulsate and was almost like a diamond shape the more I looked at it. I then went to my bedroom to get my phone so i could get a picture. When I came back it was gone. I looked in the sky for about 15 minutes hoping it would come back. I did see a shooting star and a small plane flying towards the direction the object was at.
I really do think that something was there. Whether its e.t or government.
