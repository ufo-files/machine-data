5am: Extremely bright diamond shaped blueish light. Thru binoculars I could see 2 pinpricks of even brighter light. I am not sure but they appeared to move occasionally. As it grew it the glowing got brighter and dimmer. 1/5 sz of moon.

I woke up exceptionally early at 4am. When I went outside I saw what appeared to be a huge star in the dark sky. It was way too big to be a star. Then I thought maybe it is that satelite that is supposed to fall. I realized it was getting bigger. It was in the eastern sky slightly to the south of the rising sun. I think it stayed rather stationary as it grew in size until it took off upward. Through binoculars it seemed transparent and then solid. I could see 2 or 3 pinpricks of light on it. These lights may have been moving. A small biplane entered this sector of the sky at about 5:30 am. It disappeared at about 6:30 to 6:45. Sorry wasn't watching the time when it left. It looked like an upside down teardrop or diamond. My Dad saw this too. I took a picture with my house in it for scale. It is not a good quality from my cell phone.
