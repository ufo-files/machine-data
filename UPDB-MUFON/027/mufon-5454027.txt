I noticed a plastic bag floating over my neighbor's house. I watched it thinking how odd that was a bag that high in the air. Then it started to rotate and move towards the mountain directly to the north of my house. It moved very smoothly and I watched it for 3 minutes as it passed above a raven and about 30 seconds it passed in front of an airliner. It then seemed to go up and disappeared. This was my second sighting of this kind in the last 3 years.
