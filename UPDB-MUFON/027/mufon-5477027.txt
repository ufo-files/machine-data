{cak} Boomerang Shape heading north at high speeds, takes 90 degree turn towards west.

I was outside last night walking my dog and around 10:30pm I observed a boomerang shaped craft silently shoot across the sky at tremendous speed that I can only compare to a meteor (shooting star). It headed north and then did a 90 degree turn toward the west. The object was completely dark with no visible lights but was very easy to see against the clear night sky. It was definitely a boomerang shape as opposed to triangle shaped. Sadly, I wasn't able to film or take any pictures and even if I had of been able to it happened so fast I think it would have been extremely difficult to have caught it on film at the speed it was moving. I felt a sense of excitement at having witnessed this while also feeling a bit of dread knowing that our government lies about this, which is what I have always suspected but am not 100% sure is true.
