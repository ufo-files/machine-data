About 5 cigar shaped crafts circling during daylight while sun was setting. Hovered and moved in NW sky 30 mins.

We had driven to an open place nearby to start looking for the Neowise comet. The sun was going down but there was still plenty of light. My niece had first seen the object in the sky and we dismissed it, not really paying attention. We were in an area at high tide and were more concerned with possible alligators. After awhile she asked again and we started to take notice. It looked like a long rounded craft that hovered and moved, It seemed like it might be circling and then other crafts about the same size were appearing from behind the main object we were watching. There were 5 that we saw in all. We did not start taking video immediately because we were just amazed at watching it with binoculars. The video on the iPhone was just not looking anything like the image in the sky. They were long and cylendrical like a cigar shape with a definite top and bottom that had shadow at the bottom. The video and photos just do not do it justice. I had to go home the next day (today) but my sister is going back there tonight to see if she see's it again. Please contact me if you have ANY questions, I'm dying to know if anyone else saw the same things.
