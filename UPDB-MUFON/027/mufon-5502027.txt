{cak} Walking my dog with a friend and both of us witnessed something in the sky

Walking my dogs with a friend. Clear skys.
A red light appeared low on the horizon and being a skywatcher my entire life it grabbed my attention as it appeared out of no where and then vanished at about 170degrees south west. At first before assessing the situation I thought it might be a plane but realized it had no blinking lights and there was no cloud coverage for it to disappear in. My friend was looking in the same direction also but had missed it the first time. I asked if he had seen it and he replied no. Then just about 10 sec. or so later it reappeared as I described to him and we both saw it this time at about 130degrees SW. Then we started noticing randomly blinking lights just above where we had seen the pill sized dull red light but at about 121degrees SW and slightly higher in elevation and much smaller lights like sparks, but not. Eventually the phenomena dissipated. I had the same sense of knowing that there was an intelligence present as I did when I saw one in 2012 a white blueish orb over my house in New Jersey around June 6-9 2012. This contact was almost exactly 2 years later.
