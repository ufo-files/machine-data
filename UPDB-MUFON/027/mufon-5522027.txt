Watch it in the sky for about 15 minute zig zag straight then do it again.

I know this happened, it zig zagged, then went straight for a bit, then suddenly switched directions. I watched all of the other stars in the sky and nothing else was moving. It looked just like a star. This is it, I know that they are out there. I am no longer a skeptic, as I know we aren't alone and haven't been for years. I was about 12 and my eyesight was perfect. I went into the house to get my mom and it was gone.
