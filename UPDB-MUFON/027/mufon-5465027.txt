{cak} PA Turnpike 76 floating lights

I was driving North on PA 76 between King of Prussia and Morgantown and saw several stationary objects in the sky. Probably about 7 of them. The first incidence were 2 next to each other, one higher than the other. As I continued driving toward Reading, I saw more of them. At first I though they were very bright stars but as I got closer I realized they defintiely were not. Then I thought maybe they were markers on top of a tower but there was no tower. I confirmed this when I did see a tower that was clearly visible, even in the dark. They had very bright white/yellowish lights that kind of twinkled. There may have been another greenish light on the right side but hard to say. They were mesmerizing and I couldn't stop staring at them. I wish I had taken a photo but was driving a rental car and didn't want to risk it. I found a youtube video of a sighting in King of Prussia that looked very similar to what I saw.
