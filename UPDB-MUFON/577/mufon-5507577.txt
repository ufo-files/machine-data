{nd} saw very bright light shaped like a cross in sky, it then went south very fast then shot straight into sky

I was in my little brothers room one night look out the window that faces west, I saw a very bright light that looked like a diamond or cross type shape. I was really involved in church at the time n called my little brother to come see. Being kids and going to catacism at the time had me believing and thinking it was a sign that jesus was giving a sign of belief. My brother and starred at the light for bout 5-10 mins just in aww trying to figure out what it was being so bright. Joking around we made comments that it was jesus because of the bright cross like shape. It then instantly took off south and appeared over a kmart, we ran to the next room following it and were tripping out, it sat in sky for a minute or two and then shot straight up into sky very fast and disappeared. We didn't know what it was and were were just tripping out. For years ive kept to myself and told very few people about the sighting and telling them that because what I saw and what took place I believe there are aliens and we did see something not normal and had to be ufo. I still wonder what it was in the sky that stood still and then instantly took off 1 direction and stopping once again instantly then vanishing straight up into the sky disappearing.. Did not know what to do and who to tell. I really believe nobody would believe and did not want to sound crazy or like I was just making up a story to scare people or make them think I was trying to play a trick on them to believe.
