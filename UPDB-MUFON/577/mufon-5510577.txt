{cak} Was looking at stars when this came out of nowhere and moved slow

My wife and I were in our backyard looking at stars toward the NE. When this object came out of nowhere by a star, it was a very bright blue/white glow. I thought at first that it was a satellite, but this was large and traveled slow compared to satellites that I have seen before that were small and fast. There was no blinking lights like a plane would have. This bright glow traveled NE until we could not see it anymore. My wife and I were surprised at what we just seen. I hope others seen this too, would like to know what it was.
