Reptilian & possible gray entity

I live in Evansville, Indiana, which is right by the Kentucky border…We have a state historic site called Angel Mounds, home to the Mississippian Indian culture(known for being Mound builders). Now, I do believe that you are more likely to have contact with an alien presence or more likely to have a sighting if you are “open” to new ideas, and not closed off. Okay let me start by describing the setting in which the sighting occurred…The room always had the tv on…To the left and to the right, there are small octagon windows…under the right window is a space heater that you can hang on the wall, it is supposed to look like a fireplace with wood burning and also has neon lights on it, which are always on..so me and my fiancée had gone to bed, and I had fallen asleep…typically, I will only wake up if a noise wakes me (I’m a very light sleeper) or if I have to use the bathroom…so I wake up, and the tv is off, and the room is quiet..nothing woke me up, not a noise nor did I need to use the bathroom…so upon waking, I happen to look to my right and I swear on my life, I see what can only be described as a Reptilian looking at me, along with a gray entity floating by the Reptilian’s head..now when I say gray, I’m not talking about your typical grays…this thing was shaped like I can only describe as Cartman from South Park, rounded, short, and a light gray color…My first impression is that this was a helper, assistant to the Reptilian…I thought to myself am I dreaming, so I consciously closed my eyes, reopened them, looked left, then looked right and it’s still there so I’m not dreaming…It appeared to me that the Reptilian was standing feet on the ground outside, and looking straight on at me, which the window is about ten feet off the ground. It appeared to be muscular, it was green in color, and obviously quite tall…I didn’t have a fearful feeling , but more of like an incredulous feeling…i definitely feel like the entity wanted me to see him/her, otherwise if it didn’t, then it had the power to hide itself had it wanted to…I know it wanted me to see it..Now it was probably that same day, much later in the day that I took my EMF meter to see if I could find an elevated reading…and I most definitely did! I will attach that result w/this post. I have not been able to replicate that high of a reading since that day…This is not the only experience I’ve had, but by far the best, and for the record I have no doubts in my mind to the validity of my sighting. I don’t know why I was chosen for this experience…I have an inkling that there is much more to this, other than my sighting of the Reptilian..I don’t know if there are benign Reptilians out there, just like there are good people, but as we all know there are also evil people too…I think it’s important to mention that I had been actively seeking to make contact with possible extraterrestrials by using an app called Ghost Radio when I go for walks, just to see if it’d work or not…
