{cak} Four objects that appear in photo.

When reviewing photos from highway trip through California, one photo revealed four diamond shape objects that appear to be flying from a southerly to northerly direction over the terrain and highway. The objects appear to be in a formation and leave a trail of some sort. The objects were not noticed at the time of the photo being taken. Photos from before and after the photo do not display the objects or any defect in the lens. The original photo is available for viewing and analysis on the telephone that made the photo. The photos taken on either side of the original photo on the original device are also available for review. The after photo is actually a very short film clip. This film clip taken about twelve minutes later has a glimpse of another object appears to be flying a similar path and is leaving a contrail. This object does not appear to be an airplane. 

The photo and the video clip were taken neat Tehachapi, California on CA 58, we think. The metadata on the original files should indicate the exact location.
