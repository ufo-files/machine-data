{jpg} Two lights single file 5 seconds from horizon to horizon no sound with two witnesses

I was at my son's cub scout camping trip in fenton michigan. Another parent and myself sat out after kids were sleeping and watched the stars. We saw two objects that only appeared to be lit by the moon traveling overhead single file making no noise. It only took 5 or 6 seconds for them to travel from horizon to horizon. I think at.around commercial air height. I have spent a lot of time staring at the sky. I saw the space shuttle leaving the space station one time. That was cool but this was different. It made me feel excited and in awe of what I just witnessed because it didn't look right like it didn't match anything that should be. Hard to explain.
