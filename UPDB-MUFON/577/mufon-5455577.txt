{tjd} Objects over Appleton WI

Did anyone else see strange lights in the sky over Appleton WI this morning? When outside having a cigarette and a cup of coffee before work this morning i saw a little flash out of the corner of my eye and thought "oh i must have seen a star twinkle". Sitting there looking up at the stars i thought that one star was moving. Probably the space station or satellite. But no. this "star" was moving back and forth. It would stop change direction even go in a small circle sometimes in an "s" maneuver. Go in a straight line stop go back stop move go left stop go right stop. i also saw two smaller "stars" travel very fast until i could no longer see them and then come back. Each from a opposite direction. They headed right for one another but passed each other by. these only traveled linearly. i also saw four or five bright flashes of light and two what i can only describe as a falling star. One created a wide trail the other did not. i watched the main object slowly ascend and travel SW starting and stopping until it was too far away to see. I was to be into work at 6am. but actually called into work to watch this unbelievable scene. i witnessed all the real stars fade away in the daylight but continued to see the bright light starting stopping and zigzagging away until i could no longer see it. When it was the closest to me I thought i could see one light on each side of the bright center one.
