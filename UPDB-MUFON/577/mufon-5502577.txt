An orb-like object primary red light with white glowl appeared silently crossing the sky then changed direction and went south until disappeared from site

It was 12:20 and i was sitting on my deck in Oakville when an orb-like object appeared with a primary red light with whitish trail in the west and moved southwest silently crossing the sky then changed direction and went south until disappeared from sight. Total viewing time was at least 5 min. It appeared to be floating and smooth movements. I have no idea what it was, but it certainly was not a firework (Victoria day weekend here), it was not an airplane or balloon or satellite, it was very close initially. One thought it may have been a remote control toy but how could it be silent, and then move so fast much higher and going south over the lake?  I have no explanation for what this object was and decided to report it here.
