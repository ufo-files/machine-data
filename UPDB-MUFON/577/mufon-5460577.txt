Three bright lights in sequential formation SW-NE

Straight line formation of three very bright, solid white lights-moving SW-NE at a realitively high rate of speed compared to previously observed aircraft and satellites. No sound and no change in brightness as they passed. Moved perfectly in sync but formation was not evenly spaced. Three witnesses in total. I believe that I possibly saw one other dim light traveling close in front of the last one in line for a small amount of time. Completely Silent. One witness viewed formation through a night vision monocular for a few seconds. No planes in area on flightradar24 aircraft tracking app. Not inside a mIlitary operation area. Not on a high-altitude military training route. At moment of this posting this report-our observation matches the flight path of at least three other sightings of same objects reported at same time last night stretching from Lake Michigan to Traverse City, to Mackinaw.
