My Best Friend called me out to see the bright objects in the sky. She handed me the binoculars, I looked at roughly 12 objects. One was dull orange oblong shaped, 6 were triangular-arrow shaped from what I can explain and the colors were blinking blue,red,white,purple. I also seen a white sphere shaped that had a white beam coming out of it. Seen a couple that had various colors blinking that was oval shaped and tilted. A few minutes after I seen one flying that had 3 sets of white/yellowish lights that were very bright (maybe the wings) flying very very fast, I followed it until it went behind trees and lost sight of it. Went inside to google about maybe possible drones and the little dipper seeing they were part of it and check on their diamond shape seeing they were the shape of one. Had internet problems, the pages I opened in another tab werent loading. Went back out about 25 minutes later to look at them with my binoculars, thinking maybe one of them was Jupiter. And observed another lit up circular shaped flying object that was smaller and flying way faster than the first one did. Told our next door neighbors about it who also came out and seen it as well.
