I saw Jupiter a little to my right...(west view) then another star next to that..sort of aligned, then what I thought was another star, which was the UFO, it just didn't seem to belong so I starred at it. 
The oldest kid outside was 14 yrs. old, as I starred at this so called star, it then started to move, sort of like an orb would kind of erratic, wobbly, as though my vision wasnt clear, but it was. Then 
I pointed at it to show my cousin (14 yr. old) and her friend who was 13yrs old, I believe. They watched with me as it then started to move northeast. We watched it till it faded away, three last things I noticed, there was no sound, as it turned off the star like light it had a reddish hue not light...so that may have been its surface color...also we live near Bush Airport, so planes are flying frequently through here, this object seemed to be below the planes level, but its light was equivalent with Jupiters brightness at this time. Sort of gave me a feeling of fear, because before I came out, the kids were the only ones out there, and they shouldnt of been out by themselves...it seemed to be just hovering...maybe observing...but it gave me an uneasy feeling.
