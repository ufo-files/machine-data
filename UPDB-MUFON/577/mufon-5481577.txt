Came out of movies around 8:30-9:00 pm on 13 August and noticed a very large dark cloud and what seemed like heat lightning as there wasn't any rain. At first tried to rationalize it as a plane until I noticed a plane to the right of the cloud which was significantly smaller. I immediately believed this to be a UFO. Posted it on Facebook to get others opinion. I didn't linger after the picture was shot, I lost sighting by leaving area.
