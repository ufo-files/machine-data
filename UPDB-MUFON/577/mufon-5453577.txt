High altitude Craft

Myself, my daughter & her boyfriend were standing outside our house looking up to the skies for the skylink & also Lyrids Meteor Shower, when my daughters boyfriend spotted a craft very high in the sky, it was basically just a white dot, no flashing lights. It was coming from the east I believe. However it was moving very strangely, it seemed to boost his speed every so often, but the rate it did this was really random, it was travelling very fast. It also dotted from my side to the other, it did not fly like a plane or helicopter, it was definitely controlled by something intelligent. Not the first time I have seen something strange in the sky, but it was very random & I just wanted to submit this to you incase someone else had witnessed it. It changed speed and directions very quickly.
