Two Saturn-like objects in the west/southwest sky

I believe this is the same object we (my dad and I) observed before. To the naked eye, it looks like an exceptionally bright star that seems to pulsate. With binoculars, I can see a Saturn-like object. The ring, around the center of the orb in the center, has five distinct lights 

The orb in the center is a bright white, almost luminescent light. The edges are a bit fuzzy, but that may be because of the distance from which we viewed the object. 

The second object appeared farther away but similar in shape/design to the first. 

We only observed the object for about 5 minutes. They were both still there when we went to bed.
