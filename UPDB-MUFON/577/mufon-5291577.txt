Looked like a white tic tac in the sky

I was eating lunch outside under a patio and afterwards, stretched out on my patio sofa with a pillow to rest for about 30 minutes and looked up at the tree line and immediately saw it. It was a real attention grabber. I release balloons every year on my mom and sisters date of passing and I know what balloons look like leaving the sky and at first, I thought this was a balloon, but I noticed it never moved and it was a moderately windy day. I called my wife to see if she saw it and she did and thought it looked out of place too. Over the next 15 minutes, it never moved up or down or anywhere and there was no way it was a balloon. I could not really tell since it was small, so I grabbed my binoculars and could see it was not round. Unfortunately, I have an KGK20 and not the best phone in the world and so the video I got was with it and I eventually held up my camera to one binocular and was able to get some closer video, but very choppy. I had to return to work and was not able to take the time with this event like I wanted to try and capture better video.
