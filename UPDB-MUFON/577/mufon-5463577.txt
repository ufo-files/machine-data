{dr}Saw red light move vertically, then down. When it was stationary would sometimes have blinking white light.

I was outside looking at New Year's fireworks. I notice a red light that would occasionally hover. When it would go up higher, it would hover, and the light would be blinking white, and not red. I thought the object might be a drone, because of its movement.
I was actually quite entertained by the object and its movement. It was fun watching it move around amongst the fireworks.
The object eventually dipped down, close to the earth, where I lost sight of it.
