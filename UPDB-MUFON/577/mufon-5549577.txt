I was sitting in my backyard at aprox 1100 p.m. saturday just lying back andlooking up at stars and the odd satalite passing over. The object just came intosight over the treeline in our backyard and my first impression was that it wasa flock of geese going over. The thing that got me moving ot of my chair was thespeed at witch it was travelling and if it was geese they wernt flapping thierwings , also they seemed to be giving off reflected light that looked like analuminum color. I ran to the side of my house to follow it and get a better lookand watched it go at a high rate of speed in a straight line towards thenorthshore mountains. I did get a better look at this time and saw that therewas an outer shape that was darker and I could make out a boomarang shape. Itmade no sound whatsoever and their were no bright lights on it at all. It tookonly a matter of about 6-8 seconds to cross over that all I said to my wife whenI sat back down was What the hell was that. She had seen nothing but was curiousas to my sudden trip to the side of the house. The only thing I can add is thatwhatever it was it appeared that it was trying not to be seen.
