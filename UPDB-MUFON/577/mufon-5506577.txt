{kt}Cluster of 5 very bright lights half circled moon then disappeared

4:45 PM El Cajon CA Nov 11 2013, saw a cluster of 5 very bright lights quarter circle the moon, then leave the moon and fairly fast, just dissolve. I was at my home and just happened to look up.The only thing it could have been is a cluster of balloons, but the 5 pieces stayed stationary within itself, they looked like bright lights, they were very far away, and the circled, then changed direction and disappeared.
