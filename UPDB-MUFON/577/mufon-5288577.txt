star-like object flashing and changing colors

Cloudless night, I stepped out the front door to stargaze. Saw what I initially thought was an approaching commercial jet, but after several minutes I realized it was not perceptibly moving. At a glance it had about the size and brightness of Venus or some other prominent star, but it was flashing and alternating colors, orange and blue. Called my daughter a few blocks away and she corroborated. It was probably by a small margin the brightest thing in the sky. It seemed to be stationary for at least 30 minutes and then moved gradually away (got dimmer/smaller, less flashy) and somewhat to the west and eventually out of view.
