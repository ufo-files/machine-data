Red/Orange triangle made up of lights, changed white.

I was walking my dog down Clyde road in Brighton England at around 8pm. Its a city so planes and lanterns and fireworks are common, however what made me notice this light was it was stationary yet a triangle shape of lights, hovering over the main train station.
At first i thought it was some gimmick or such but it was strangely high, i stood an observed it for around 30 seconds during which time its lights went off for around 3-5 seconds then came back on. and it made a sharp and perfect jolt to the right (around 200ft) and then slowly started drifting downwards in a perfect straight line. 
I pulled out my mobile phone and started to film but it was a clear but black night and city lights and houses were around. it only appeared as a circle looking object when i zoomed on my mobile but in real life it was a triangle.
i filmed it for 1mins 20secs ish, it came down between a tree and a house in my vision of the mobile phone, but these then gave clear reference points and the object can be seen flashing on and off again. drifting downwards and then taking off sharply to the left and out of view. 
its lights were randomly going on and off at intervals.
