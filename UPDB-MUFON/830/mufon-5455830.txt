{rjl} Giant disc with a row of windows in the middle as big or bigger than a football field

I didn’t even remember this event till I was about 30 years old My parents and I were coming back from a restaurant in Hudson Ohio we came up out of the valley into Montrose Ohio a suburb of Akron Ohio. My dad was driving my mom in passenger seat and I was in the back seat but sitting so I was pretty much in the middle. I was about 14 or 15 years old. About 1974-1975 not sure which. All of a sudden my dad stopped driving and I looked up thru to the front window and there was this giant disc about easy the size of a football field with windows going thru the middle. All I can remember is saying to my dad to follow it daddy follow it. He didn’t speak at all. My mom said no Tommy don’t, don’t! And I have no recollection of anything that happened after this. And my parents never spoke of it again. I am worried that we were taken and put back at home some how. I have no idea how we got back or anything else but what I have told you here. My parents have passed on. I only wish I had the money and way to be hyponatized to remember. I’m hoping MUFON someday could help me with this. No one believes me not even my spouse. I know it happened. It was barely over the road and much larger than any plane military or otherwise.
