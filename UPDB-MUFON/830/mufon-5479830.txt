{nd} Appr 16 small craft, steady red orange, flying in v formation, at high altitude, crossing night sky horizon in approx 5 seconds.

First saw these objects several nights ago while watching night sky. First appearance was 6 small orange points of light passing slowly overhead in triangular formation, crossing night sky horizon in about 60 seconds.Then few minutes later, two strings of 8 each travelling parallel flew back across sky at much higher rate of speed, passing over in the space of few seconds. Last night, saw approx 16 flying in close v formation, at high altitude and at high rate of speed, crossing sky in 4 or 5 seconds, till they were out of sight. These craft did not hold tightly to their v formation, those holding the outer edges of the formation spreading apart somewhat while in flight. These craft emmited steady pale orange glow, and were about the size and brightness of an average star in the night sky.
