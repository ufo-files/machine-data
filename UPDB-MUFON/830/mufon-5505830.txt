{nd} I saw it approaching and then hovering.  I tried to reach people on the phone but couldnt get anyone. It took off adn vanished in an instant, I think i hummed. There was a glow around th edges.

I was in a triler on our property in the country. When I first saw it it was at a considerable distance but was approaching and as it approached it seemd to speed up. Then it hovered right outside the trailer and covered almost the entire sky. I turned off all the lights and tried to call family and freinds but couldnt get anybody. At one point I went outside and thought it was humming. After a few minutes it took off so quickly I couldnt tell what direction it went. I did not see any structures or windows on it. I was extremely frightened and that was why I tried to call people. It came from a southwesterly direction but I don't know what direction it left in. I was afraid to mention this to anyone for fear of being ridiculed.
