{cak} Long bright red cone

Extremely bright pulsating red (long narrow) cone heading south, moving slowly, then fast, then slow, fast, slow, then stopping. It started dimming then shot off straight up at a tremendous speed. No sound, not a plane, blimp nor a helicopter. It may have been at 2000 feet and sure seemed very large, way larger than local military aircraft that are often seen. I've never seen or read about anything like it. It didn't have red lights, it seemed to be a big long triangle/cone shape that was very bright red. In the example image I created, the white lights were not observed, just offered as a comparison in size. One would be Sirius and the longer white light is the apparent size of an aircraft.
