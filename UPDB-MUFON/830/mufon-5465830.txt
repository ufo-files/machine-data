I was traveling home at about 12:30 or 1 in the morning I just gotten off of work. I was heading East on the Gene Snyder freeway. I notice to the left that there was a star-like object sitting in the sky not very high. Our airport was in the direction so I initially thought it was a helicopter. I rolled down the window but there was no noise. There were five or six cars all of us right there together and all the traffic slow down to look at it. As we move forward the object travel along with us everybody has slowed down talk to a crawl people are hanging out their Windows looking at it. We traveled maybe a hundred yards and then it changed Direction and went in front of us and moved along with us maybe a hundred yards ahead of us. All of a sudden there was a huge light in the middle of the expressway Lanes everybody stop nobody wanted to drive through the light. The funny thing about the light there was no beam just for a big circle on the ground and no Bean going back up to whatever that thing was. Everybody stopped. Some people were outside of their cars when the beam disappeared everybody got back in their cars and took off like a shot. The object change sides of the expressway to our right side follow this for about a half a mile or so and just disappeared. I wasn't able to sleep all night.
