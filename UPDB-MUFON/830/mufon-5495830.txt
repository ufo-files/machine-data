Ford KA

I was out for my daily 4am pancake run, where I run as fast as I can to the nearest pancake committee and make sure my views are heard. As I was careering round the corner, I saw it. At first I thought it was a normal Ford KA, nothing out of the ordinary. But when the "teenager" left the vehicle I knew the truth. Out of his head sprouted a bright orange straw-like material. A ginger. Then as he pressed the button to lock his car the headlights flashed white and as soon as they had, the mothership was before me. The monster called it "his mum's land rover". He transformed before my eyes, into a green leaf, with orange hair sprouting out of his armpits. Then he bit a nearby dog and his car began to fly and change shape. It let off an aura of animal mutilation. He checked his watch to make he was on time to report to the mothership. "MOTHER!" came the screeching cry, "I AM READY TO MUTILATE ANIMALS!"
