When I first noticed the UFO I was walking my neighbor's dog across the dam after visiting the lake. It was a little after 10:30 and it was a clear night. I was put on alert when the dog crouched down and started pulling me across the bridge. There were no one around and I hadnt seen a car all night. When I looked up I saw the ship and was trying to reason with myself as to what type of plane it could be. However, I quickly saw that it was too large of an aircraft to be flying so low and further more it had no visible propellants and an odd light formation. I hurried up the street and lost sight of it. A few blocks further I stopped to let the dog pee. When I turned I saw the UFO for the second time. As I watched it, it dipped down closer to the trees and emitted a beam of light. The outside of it was jagged looking, and it appeared to be a very solidly built ship. There were red blinking lights on the sides and unwavering white ones in the front and back. Scared out of my mind, and firmly convinced it was not a government airplane, I sprinted the rest of the way home.
