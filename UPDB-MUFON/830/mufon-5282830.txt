Vertical craft seen from Fulbourne Road in Walthamstow

Vertical craft seen from Fulbourne Road in Walthamstow while waiting for a bus. 
I looked up, while waiting for a bus and noticed a stationary object, which looked white, grey and shiny. The object then started to move, after noticing it for a few moment, I decided to open my phone camera because it didn't move like a plane or drone. The sky was quite clear, blue sky with a small breezy wind.
The object appear to have 3 parts to it, top, and enlongated middle and bottom. Reflective in the sky, it moved in a slow motion diagonally from left to right, and fortunately a plane came in to view.
The plane and road traffic helped to authenticate what I was viewing was real and not something else.
The craft was very reflected and although it was at a distance between 1 and 2 miles, I zoomed in to try and get a between view. Due to the low flight of the object, I believe people in the area beneath the object would have easily seen the object with more clarity. I took 2 videos.
