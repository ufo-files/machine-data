{rjl} Black Object spotted on Passe Grille Beach from Hurricane Restaurant

We were at the Hurricane Resturant on Passe Grill Sunday the 24th of November, 2019. We were taking selfies from the roof top bar. Took two with my phone then several with my girlfriends. After the pictures we were looking at them and noticed the strange black shapes i my two photos, taken directly at the sun. Then when we looked at hers, taken about 20-30 seconds later we see another strange object on the same path out of the sun (the object is located to the right of our heads in the clouds). I took my two pictures in dynamic camera mode, you can actually see the black shape moving and changing shape in both pictures.

That same day. I was taking pictures of our back yard, new fire pit, and just realized I captured something
hovering above the elctrical wires above the shed.
