{dr}It was bright like a star and was about 100 ft in front of military aircraft

I stepped into my from yard at approximately 9:15 in the evening to let my dogs out for a few minutes. I heard a helicopter coming from a north westerly trajectory. I looked up to see it. It was flying low- maybe 500 ft. I don't know for certain but I could make out the shape. It had its spotlight on and pointing down. It was then I noticed the star like white ball of light in a straight line in front of helicopter. It was matching speed and was heading south with helicopter following it. I stepped out farther into my yard and watched them both as the rapidly disappeared. I walked back inside my house and just thought- that did not look normal.
