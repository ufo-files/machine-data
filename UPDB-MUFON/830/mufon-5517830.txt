Metallic disc less then 10 miles from DC

I was riding my bicycle towards Potomac yards from crystal city. Which is weird after I thought about it because I wasnt going to even head over there. I planned on going back to my apartment, but something urged me to just go that way so I said [item moved/CMS/nd] Ill go real quick. As I was 3/4 the way there I was on a small overpass bridge and thats when I looked up and saw the usual planes in final. But this time I saw a metallic disc above the planes usual flight path and it just moved in several directions as to grasp my attention. Well it did and then it appeared to move away from me and suddenly it was gone. I don't know if it just zipped away or just disenegrated. I was excited to have seen something that I actually cant explain. I fly a lot and see a lot of traffic in the sky and this wasnt a plane or any other aircraft we know about.
