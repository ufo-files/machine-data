Very small black object moving slowly across the western sky.

I was looking out a west-facing window towards the just fully set sun and noticed an almost invisible black object moving across the sky near Venus. The size/distance was hard to gauge, but seemed fairly far, at least hundreds/thousands of feet if not miles. I continually kept my gaze on it having to sometimes re-focus using my peripheral vision as it moved out of sight. It seemed to disappear completely about halfway across my field of vision near to where the moon was in the Southern sky, but due to the difficulty in keeping track of it it could have simply faded from visual. It didn't move fast or erratically, just in a lazy/constant/straight NNW to SSE trajectory. There were no obvious lights, though at times I could see VERY faint, erratic and randomly placed white lights but it could have been a trick of the eyes since it was hard to see. Definitely didn't appear to be a jetliner or passenger plane. May have been a high-flying bird, stray balloon, or low-flying satellite. Identification was essentially impossible. The shape was difficult to make out, at times seeming like a circle/oval/sphere then a triangle/diamond, I attributed this difficulty once again to its size/distance and not necessarily any shape-shifting property.
