{dr}Watching Thunderstorm when we saw a bright moving light changing color & direction..

We were watching the first thunderstorm of the year when we noticed a light that was moving between us and the storm. At first we thought that it was an airplane but then noticed that it was to low and moving in a non ballistic state. It would hover, then change color, then move again. Were not sure if it was a drone with a bright color changing light or if it was actually a U.F.O. but weve never witnessed any drones in the area.
