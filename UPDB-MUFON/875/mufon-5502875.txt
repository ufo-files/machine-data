I looked at the sparse clouds while sitting in my back yard with my wife after I had "flooded the kitchen with water for the fish tank.We soaked up most of the "flood" And placed a fan to evaporated the rest of the mess in the kitchen and went outside for a rest.I looked at the sky and saw the object.It appeared to me to have a slender center fuselage with crecent moon shaped "wings".It was moving fast but I got a pretty clear image as it raced by.It appeared to be very high in the sky.There was no sound as I would normally hear from any jet or other airplane.It was very bright and I lost sight of the object as it moved away fairly rapidly.I could hardly take my eyes off of the sky almost expecting to see another one.Several minutes past when I saw what I thought was a "jet" heading in the same flight pattern.It did't appear as high as the object but I did hear the far off rumble of a jet.My neck is stiff after scanning the sky in a high level of emotion.I wasn't afraid in the least.I have never seen any plane remotely like this object.Don't think it was a "stealth airplane" from my pictures of one.WOW!!
