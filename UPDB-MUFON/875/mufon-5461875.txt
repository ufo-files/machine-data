{cak} Strange object moving directly upward

Driving north bound I-75 in Southeastern Michigan, early in the morning. The sky is still dark and there are scattered clouds. Imagine a blimp turned vertically, with a path going straight up. Object was illuminated, almost like a light bulb, as it ascended directly through a cloud. As it ascended before going through the canopy of clouds, it illuminated the underside of the cloud a perfect circular light and as it continued to pass through the cloud, upward. The cloud seemingly enveloped the object.
