I arrived home about 2 am from poker party, sober. Checked status of broiler chicken house and was headed inside when I saw a brightly lighted disc shaped object, about thirty feet across come over a ridge from the east and silently descend to or nearly to the ground behind some timber. I chose not to approach it and went to bed. The next morning I checked and there were no disturbances of foliage, ground that I could find.
