{cak} Saw what appeared to be a comet or meteor

It was Saturday evening (twilight)February 8th, 2014. I was Walking into church at around 5:30 with my 14 year old daughter. I looked up and and above the church, in the sky was what looked like an enormous meteor or comet with a tail. At first I thought it was one of those "wispy" like clouds but then I could see a red dot at the head of this thing. I continued to watch it after I got into church because I could see it through one of the windows. I watched it for a good 10-15 more minutes until it disappeared. I am sure it was moving because I watched it gradually descend over the horizon through the window. I thought that it might be a comet or meteor but when I goggled it, there were no reports of such events on that day.
