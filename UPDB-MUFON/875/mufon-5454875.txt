{tjd} Witnessed over 30, equidistant lights originating from NW sky and disappearing in NE sky

My family and I all witnessed over 30 flying objects this evening. They appeared in the NW sky directly beside or from a star/planet, each appearing a mile or so apart, all equally distanced apart, flew in an eastern direction and disappeared in the NE sky. These were definitely not airplane or satellites as they appeared and disappeared in the same spots and were exactly the same distance apart. They all flew at the same speed and same distance and all originated and disappeared from the same points.  My son stopped counting at 29 but we witnessed approximately 10 additional.  The entire episode lasted approximately 10 minutes.
