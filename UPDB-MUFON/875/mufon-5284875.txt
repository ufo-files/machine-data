Sought to craft black and white in color or above Palm Beach Shores

I was sitting on my boat at the east end of peanut Island in Palm Beach Shores Florida, when I looked up to the east and witnessed two craft close together they were dark and also white, and kind of looked like the craft in that Nimitz video. They were disc shaped with a long bulb underneath that was white. The disc part was dark almost black. They were traveling south, I saw them for maybe 20 to 30 seconds. I I then turned around to get the attention of my friend, and I looked back and they had disappeared. There were clear skies and it was quite unnatural. Then they were gone.
