{dr}Saw two orange spheres, size of a beach ball, slowly move from north to west

Sitting in my sun room watching TV. My house is under a flight path for Detroit Metro airport, and I can see planes flying low getting ready to land. Something told me to look outside. Something caught my eye coming from the north, I knew it wasn't a plane right away, so I got up and went outside to see it. The only thing I thought it could be was perhaps a drone. There were two orange spheres moving slowly, steadily, high where the planes fly. These were no planes. There was no noise. I went back to the sun room to get my phone camera and I came back outside. When I tried to take a picture, the objects darted to the west behind trees, where I lost sight of them. I believe in UFOs because this isn't the first time I have seen something.
