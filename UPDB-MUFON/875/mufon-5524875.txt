Top Secert Aircraft/UFO Violating FAA Regulations

I've been observing these things for awhile now. I noticed they often change their lighting to deceive people. I was expecting to catch the usual activity thinking a light would turn on or off like usual. Instead what happened turned out to be much more interesting. In this clip you'll see what appears to be a plane... watch closely as what we are lead to believe is a front head light or landing light changes from white to RED! Now it has 2 red lights! I believe this violates FAA lighting regulations because there should only be one solid red light on port side (the left wing) and depending on the aircraft maybe a blinking red anti collision light on the fuselage. This is to help other pilots determine who has the right away... you can look up aircraft navigation lighting if you're interested. Once it turns the front light red it slowly turns what should be the left wing tip from red to white. This is followed by the entire thing getting EXTREMELY bright.
