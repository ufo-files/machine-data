{kt}I asked for the appearance (with respect) to occur and I opened my eyes to it coming over my head.

The craft was dead silent. I only knew about it after I opened my eyes that had been closed for at least 15 minutes. I saw three lights. Two streaks of orange and the one red circular light in the middle was pulsating slowly. I was in my back yard. It passed directly over my head very slowly. It was about the speed of a helicopter but it made no sound. I'm used to seeing helicopters fly over and I know what they sound like. It passed over my head and cleared the trees. I was so dumbfounded that I began meditating again and asked for the craft to do another fly over my head if this was in fact real. After I did that for a few seconds, the craft turned around and headed directly over my head again. It came from the North and went South. Then it came from the South and went North. No sounds. I had a feeling of joy as if I had actually communicated to a being in the craft. It confirmed the reality to me when the craft reversed and came literally straight over my head. I at first thought it was a plane because of the red flashing light but I didn't hear any noise and I've never seen an airplane with two rectangular orange beams of light that were at least 6 times the size of the red flashing orb. I have extensive knowledge of military and civilian aircraft. The rectangle orange aligned North to South almost sandwiching the red light. There was distance between all the lights and I could not see anything behind the lights but I felt as thought there was a large unlit craft. It moved too slow to be any sort of modern air plane.
