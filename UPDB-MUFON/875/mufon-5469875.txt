Photographed over Great Barrier Reef

I was recently browsing photos on my device and cleaning out unwanted pictures when I came across something different in one image.

When the pictures were taken I was flying back from Japan to Brisbane around dawn. I was taking a series of sunrise photos with my ipad (clicking rapidly allowing for a constant stream of photos). 

I noticed in the twenty or so photos that there was one image that had two strange objects in the top left hand corner. These objects appear in only one photo, which means these objects are not a reflection as they only appear in a single frame.
