{rjl} Redish long skinny cylinder- like object in the night sky, moved slowly and took off suddenly

A friend and I were driving at night on my block on the west side of town in Wenatchee, WA when we saw something moving slowly in the sky that looked like a long red cylinder shaped object that was moving towards us. I turned the car lights off and with that the object continued moving toward us but when we realized that it was something highly unusual in the sky, my friend and I freaked out. We both began screaming, scared at what was coming towards us and I turned the car lights on and a split second later we watched the object that had been coming towards us turn to the side a bit and in the next second it vanished and we couldn't find it anymore. I can't say how close or how big it was but I have terrible eyesight and without glasses on I could clearly see it.
