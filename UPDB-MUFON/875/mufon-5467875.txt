On multiple nights in the last couple of weeks we have seen this same large bright light hovering just beyond the tree line behind our house. The light looks disc like and the only movement it makes is to ride and fall above and behind the tree line in the same spot. The movement is too slow to be a drone as you cannot see the movement only the changing distance from the top of the tree line. It does dip below the tree line and tonight it appeared to be moving closer towards our house at a very slow rate of speed. The object is also far too large to be a drone. It emits no sound and does not have any flashing lights. It is just a solid disc of white light. At first I thought it was a very large bright star or something of the sort but it's pattern of movement quickly ruled that out. While it has sparked a strong curiosity in me it also gave me a very uneasy feeling, especially when it seemed to start moving closer to us and our home, at this point my fiancee and I quickly made our way back inside. At this moment I can still see it out of our bedroom window and it appears to have now dipped well below the tree line however in the same exact area as it has hovered in the entire time.
