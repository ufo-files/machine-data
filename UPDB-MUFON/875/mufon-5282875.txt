{4} Orange lights, {appeared in sequence} over Rose City. - {djs}

Traveling by car east on County Highway F-28 between Rose City and Lupton, I observed three orange lights in the sky and possibly a fourth. 

Each one was an orange basketball sized light high in the sky. They never appeared at the same time, so it could be just one object moving at a very high rate of speed. 

The first light appeared more to the northeast and was visible for about 20 seconds. Shortly after the second light appeared due east for about the same duration. Then the third light appeared to the southeast and was visible for the same duration. 

About 2-3 minutes later a fourth light appeared due east and was visible for the same duration as the previous lights.
