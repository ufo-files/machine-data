The 6 lights moving silently from north to south on my field of view what I also noticed was a intermittent flashing white light that seemed to be following the main object about 5" back of it in my perspective. Note: it quit flashing when it made its turn but the 6 flame colored lights were visible until it faded from view to the right of the setting moon. There was absolutely no noise, vibration or sound of any kind being emitted that is what was so striking about the event.
