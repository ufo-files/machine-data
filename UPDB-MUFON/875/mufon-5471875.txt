Red hovering pulsating silent red light

In February 2001 I was driving a bus from Bawdsey to Ipswich in Suffolk during the trip between Hollesley Bay and the former RAF Woodbridge both my wife and myself observed a pulsating red light hovering about 10ft above a field to the right of the lane. As I drove towards it it started to gain a little bit of height and was moving towards the road at about 90 degrees to my path. As we got closer I opened my cab window to see if I could hear anything. The red light passed over the bus at between 20 and 30ft and was completely silent. I couldn't make out any structure to the light either. Bearing in mind that I am an aviation enthusiast and good at aircraft recognition I still don't know what this light was. We also get a lot of British army AH 64s practising around here and definitely wasn't one of them.
I appreciate that I have taken a long time to report this but it is only in recent years I have had access to programmes like ufo files unsealed and it was one of them on the Rendlesham incident which wasn't far from my sighting referring to a pulsating red light that maybe I put two and two together. As for the lighthouse at Orford Ness that has been used to try and debunk the Rendlesham incident in my case that was directly behind my bus and to the left of the red light.
Chris Beard
