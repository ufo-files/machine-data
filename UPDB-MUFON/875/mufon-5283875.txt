Two Orange Orbs

I live on the Columbia River on a island in between Portland Oregon and Vancouver Washington. I went outside as I do on clear nights to see what was going on outside and my tv started freezing on channels. I am only about 5 miles from Portland Airport as well as a small private airport in Vancouver. I noticed the typical drones patrolling for the Air Force that make their passes. I looked South from my deck and notice a very bright Orb flying south/southeast. It started to make quick movements and almost like a jerky motion and began to dim to like 80-90% of the original brightness. I ran and got my wife and grabbed my phone. It continue to move to the south and we watched it for a couple of mins. Couldn’t get any video of the first one. We waited for a few mins and went to the front of our house to see if we could see it still. We couldn’t and went back to the back of our house, when we looked up in the same general direction the other one was spotted one just appeared out of no where and was just as bright as before. I was able to get a video of the 2nd one which was moving to the East not following the same exact path as the first. We watched it and it also dimmed and I was able to get the video of that as well. It was moving very erratic and very jumpy. Making movements that can’t be done be any flying machines we have or that is publicly known. It was a circular orb that looked like plasma of some sorts. I almost felt like the like looked right at me at one point. This was for sure the real deal. First video is at its brightest and the second is when it dimmed but was still flying weird.
