Orb like objects with sharp change in flight

Around 2:20 pm on March 30th while walking down Washington blvd towards Hutchinson Rd in Robbinsville NJ, while walking witnessed several small orb like white objects in the sky. It was a clear day with minimal amounts of clouds and what made me initially look up was a plane probably coming from the local airport. To the left of the plane I saw several small white objects. At first I thought they were birds flying in formation but did not make a sound and seemed to be very high in the sky. After watching these objects for several seconds they stopped in the sky, made a strange geometric formation similar to a diamond and then started to move in a weaving fashion to make a second formation that resembled a figure 8. I took out my phone and took pictures and video but could not capture these objects. I was wearing my polarized sunglasses while viewing these objects and when I took my glasses off I could not seem them but as soon as I put my glasses back on saw them with great clarity. My lenses were clean, no smudges or streaks on them. I viewed these objects for about 3-5 minutes until they seemed to disappear in the sky. Again, what I saw did not make any sound and did not resemble birds but they looked like orbs. I can send over photos and video but I cannot locate any of these objects in the video or photos unfortunately
