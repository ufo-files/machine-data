Stepped out onto my porch at night and looked up into the sky as I always do just to admire the stars and look at the moon, breathe in the night air before retiring for the night. As I looked up I noticed what look like an illuminated spinning disc. As I watched I noticed it would hover for a few seconds the shoot extremely fast into the sky and then seconds later it came back. I ran into the house grappled my iPad and in the process of activating my camera and searching for the object.
