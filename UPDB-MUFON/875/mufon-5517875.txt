{nd} Starlike object moving and hovering in eastern sky

I was walking out to my garage at around 10:30 and thought I noticed a medium-brightness star moving in the eastern sky. I quit walking, stood still, and using a nearby power line as a reference, I observed that the object was stationary. I just about dismissed it when after about 20 seconds, the "star" started moving slowly to the south. I quickly ran into the house, retrieved some binoculars, and steadied them against the window pane looking due east. At first, I couldnt find the object, but I did eventually located it in the SE. It either moved quickly during the time I ran into the house, or I spotted a different object. When magnified, the object was a small orange/white sphere but had some amount of flickering blue/red colors. I quickly compared a nearby star of the same intensity but it didn't resemble what I was looking at. It moved northward and appeared to change directions slightly. To my surprise, I also noticed a faint white star-like object pass by the object I was watching at a significantly faster and different trajectory (similar to the speed of a satellite). Seconds later, this occurred again, except this object was travelling at yet another trajectory. Both smaller/dimmer objects passed quickly through my field of view, and I didn't follow them because I was concentrating on the larger object. I only saw the smaller objects on that one occasion, and they probably were barely visible to the naked eye. The larger object then passed behind a tree in my neighbors yard and reappeared about a minute later on the north side and stopped moving. After about 5 minutes of hovering, it began to lose intensity and a minute later it was no longer visible. (This may have been due to some small clouds moving in, but hard to tell in the faint dusk light.) Total time of observation was around 10-15 minutes. [Item moved/CMS/nd] and have a good understanding of physics, avionics, etc. I cannot come up with any rational explanation of what I saw. It must have been swamp gas :-) Ive checked the skies every night since then but have not seen a repeat performance.
