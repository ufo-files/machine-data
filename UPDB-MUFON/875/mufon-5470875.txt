{rjl} I saw a cigar object fly over our building. It had a light at both ends.

My husband and I were looking out our front kitchen window about 9:30 PM on the night of Jan. 3, 2018. Our window overlooks the covered deck, but we like to watch the planes coming and going into and out of the airports in Chicago. From the side of the window that I was standing I noticed a bright light and saw a cigar shaped object with bright lights on the front and back of the object. It moved so fast that by the time I told my husband to move over to my side and up look it had moved out of sight. It appeared to come from the northwest and headed southeast out of sight. It made no sound and was a dark metal color with no light between the front and back lights.
