{tjd} 3 Glowing Balls of Orange Light

First a flash of light appeared in the sky, with a small bright blinking light appearing just after. Then three orange glowing balls of light appeared just after in triangle formation. Balls of light lasted maybe 30 seconds then disappeared. There were then about 12 sparkling lights remaining. Very small, kind of star sized. 10 of the 12 lights then moved at a high rate of speed to the NW then we lost track of them. One of the remaining blinking lights then went much higher in altitude at a very high rate of speed and and moved to the east and north doing almost a circle coming back towards us... then it disappeared. We lost track of the other remaining blinking light.
