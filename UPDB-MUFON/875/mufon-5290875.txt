SPHERE SHAPE UFO

This happened back around December 1979 but could have been early 1980
I had just got off the bus at the bottom of the hill near the junction of A341 to Seend 
As walking up the road to my family home at the top of the hill called Brow Cottage, I noticed circle of light in the field to the left of me, as I walked up the hill the light seemed to shoot across the field close to the ground towards me. It stopped in the field behind the hedgerow and hovered there about 3ft off the ground and I'm guessing it was about 12/14ft height as it was taller than the hedgerow.
It looked like a silver ball with a light illuminating around it. There was no sound. 
I started to run up the hill and it followed me rising over the few houses before my house and as i ran around the back of the house it moved higher into the sky and away from the house, then hovered there. 
I stayed in the garden calling my father to come and have a look, but he didn't come. then suddenly it was gone
When explaining to him and his partner they just said it was probably a helicopter and it single light at the front. I know that wasn't possible, as there was no noise and I know what I saw was like a big shiny ball
I have spoken about this many times over the years and the majority don't believe. Seeing the UFO programmes on TV where some UK sightings are around the same time period as my sighting I felt its worth making it official before I can no longer tell the tale
