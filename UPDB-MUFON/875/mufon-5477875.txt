{rjl} Visual for @ 10 miles, flying super fast, no sound, a silver ball

Standing on my porch in the dark looking at the stars. The area in front of me was a forest which has been clear cut, offering great views for miles. I could see the prison complex about 7 miles away with ease.
I looked to the south and saw a shiny, star like object almost at tree top level about a mile away and moving fast in my direction. I started to tell the wife to come look but it was already directly in front of me (east) still at about 100' of elevation moving rapidly north. I continued to view it for maybe 3 seconds until it had passed the prison complex and past my visual horizon.
The object was star-like in appearance and moving fast, as I said. It covered maybe 10 miles in a couple of seconds. I'm prior Air Force and I haven't seen anything this fast. There was no sound
