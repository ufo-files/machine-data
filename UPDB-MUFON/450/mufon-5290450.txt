Black triangle hovered over me for a minute before returning in same direction at some speed

At about 21:00 a black triangle with lights at each end approached the field in front of me quite slowly. At first I thought it was a plane crashing or something because it was silent, large and heading to the ground. Then, it stopped and I looked up to see the large, black, rectangular object hovering almost directly above me a few hundred feet away. It then simply rotated slowly (horizontally, as a record would do on a record player) before disappearing in the direction it came from at some considerable speed. Absolutely silent. In hindsight, I'm surprised at how comfortable i was with the experience. It wasn't until the age of Google that I typed in "black triangle ufo" and felt vindicated after seeing many others had the same experience around the world. Reporting this now, thanks to the recent happenings with Pentagon, Nimitz, etc.
