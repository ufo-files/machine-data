{dr}Orange glow moving very slow

OK two people witnessed an orange glow appear out of no where moving really slow in the South West sky about 50 feet above a tree line, the object almost looked like it stopped once or twice, it then started to climb gradually to almost 300 feet above the tree line and then just disappeared. No clouds in the sky and before and after event they could see planes flying around, during event nothing was making any sounds even the animals were quiet. This location has reported other strange objects in the skies and I have one video of oval shaped grey objects changing directions, hovering and flying around during filming of a storm a little over a year ago. Starting to think this could be a hot spot..... This area is inline with the Greater Cincinnati Airport so I didn't think much of the first sightings but now that I have been watching and taking notes I think there might be something going on out here. I have been around planes all my life and so has the other witness and this sighting was not a plane and the video can not be planes.
