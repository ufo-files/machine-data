{cak} Thought it was a satellite. Then bursts of light from the back making it move faster.

I was in my backyard smoking a cigarette looking up at the sky like I usually do. I saw what I thought was a satellite. A burst of light came from the rear of it and made it move faster for a brief moment. This sequence happened about five times during which time the object slightly curved from northwest to southeast. I wasn1t particularly surprised because I have seen other objects before and look for them frequently, however, it has been a couple years since I have seen anything worth reporting to mufon. Lost sight of it due to foliage on the trees. This was a first for me seeing something that moved like this object. Who knows.
