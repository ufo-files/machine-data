{nd} Me and my friend were walking, then we were attacked.

My friend and I were taking a walk this morning when we heard a static-y sound to our right. I saw some sort of creature that was leaving fires while it was walking. At first, I didn't know what the creature was, but when I looked closer, it was some sort of entity made from magma, with a dull metallic plating over its chest.

The entity was followed by two others that were slowly walking southeast, but when they saw us, they charged with high speeds. One of them nearly hit me, but I was able to run to the side and avoid being killed. Another, though, got my friend - impaled her through with a burning hand... The entity dropped her, and came towards me. I was terrified for my life, so I ran as fast as I could to a location the fiery entities could not get me.

I went into an abandoned brick house, hiding from the creatures until they left. When they were gone, I went back outside and saw burned and scarred ground wherever the creatures moved, and the dead body of my friend...

I was, and still am, terrified about this whole thing. I'm afraid to even go outside the house now.
