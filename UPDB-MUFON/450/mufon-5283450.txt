Brilliant light in sky lasting approximately 30-45 seconds then disappearing instantly.

While driving south on Harrison Avenue in Butte, Montana, my family and I spotted the light at approximately 30 degrees above the horizon. The light at that point appeared smaller than an aspirin at arm-length. It was a brilliant, single, white light that appeared instantly, shone approximately 30-45 seconds, then disappeared immediately. The light didn't move. There were no aircraft in the area at the time (confirmed on FlightAware.com shortly after the sighting) nor were there any known astrological events occurring. Everything happened too quickly for us to get a photograph or video.
