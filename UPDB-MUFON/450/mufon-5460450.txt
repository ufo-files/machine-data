Green Fireball as of New Mexico in 1950's (Not a Meteor)

I was upstairs seeing the night sky through the window where I saw a green fireball that made no sound and no smoke, yet it was visibly burning.

I saw a small white then turned to green color fireball going across the sky in an horizontal fashion much like someone had thrown a firework sideways but never exploded. This sighting confirmed me that it was no meteor as for they travel vertically, and no lights illuminated the sky before the sighting. The total duration of the burning fireball was probably 5 seconds.

Regardless of it appearing to be burning identical to a flare no smoke was ever seen.

Original: "At 8:17pm I saw an unknown horizontal white then turned to green torched light (scorching green fire) like a flare, but when gone there was no smoke no trail left, thought while passing it had a tail like a comet, it lasted five seconds before vanishing into thin air, just as it appeared it disappeared, passing from right to left."

This is in fact of Extraterrestrial origin.
