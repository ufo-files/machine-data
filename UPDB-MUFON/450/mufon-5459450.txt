{tjd} Observed a bright orange light flying slowly west to east, light flickered our revealing black triangle flying east at constant course and speed

I was driving home from dinner traveling west on Cleves Warsaw when I noticed a bright orange light flying west towards me. It did not have conventional aircraft navigation lights or beacons, but was bright steady orange light. I pulled into a parking lot across the street on the corner of Cleves Warsaw and Ebeneezer Rds, and watched this object almost overfly my position. It passed overhead slightly south of where I was parked. It continued east towards Price Hill and Downtown Cincinnati at a constant speed and altitude, which I would only hazard a guess of being 1500 feet off the ground, I am not sure.  The other thing that was strange is that this is controlled airspace being in the final approach path of CVG. This object was flying across the path for final approach to three runways. 
Once past me, the orange light flickered off, and I saw a black triangle object that appeared to be large to me continuing east on the same course. I continued to watch it fly eastward until I could no longer see it. The daylight was getting less at that point, but still managed to watch it till it seemed to fade in the distance into the darkening sky. I did not unfortunately get any photos or video, as I was mesmerized and it didn't occur to me. Not really a believer in extraterrestrials, but I don't know if this was a drone, or something else. So I am reporting it here.
