{nd} 500 ft behind plane ( black object bright flash)

On 4/2013 @ 7:30pm myself and my kids were playing outside, we noticed a airline jet. Flying over head. My 4 year old son was asking about the the trail behind the jet. I turned to talk with him when my 8 year old little girl said " daddy whats that behind the plane?" I looked and didn't see anything there so I asked her what it looked like. She told me it was a black dot that flashed a bright light... So I looked again and I saw what looked like a beach ball size dark gray cloud about 100 yards behind the vapor trail of the plane. I explained to her it was probably just some clouds, then it flashed the brights light I have ever seen. The light was a blueish white light alomost like the glimmers a diamond gives off. The flash lasted 1-2 seconds and the object went back to the dark gray color. When the flash stopped it went instantly dark it didn't dim or anything just on then off. Soi grabbed my cell and by time I turned camera on it was gone.
