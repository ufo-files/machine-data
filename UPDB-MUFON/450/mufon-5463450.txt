{cak} SP said it looked like a satellite. Horizontal disc toward top. Saw same area for 3 days.

Shortly after moving to Texas, I was walking from kitchen to bedroom, saw flash of light out the front door. Walked out on the front porch, walked back in and had husband and daughter come out to look. My husband said it might be a satellite and then he and my daughter went back inside. It was around 500 feet from the ground in tree line about 500 or 600 feet from our front fence. It had a bubbled disk shape spinning around about 100 feet from the top and windows down and across the front. It had different colored lights along both sides, flashing like lights on a airplane, but colors not seen on aircraft; blue, red, orange, green and yellow. I sat on our front step for about a hour and and half, It never moved from the spot in the sky, so I went back inside. For the next two evenings, my daughter and we saw it hovering around the same area, but either a little higher or over to the right. I never saw it during the day or again since then. We never saw or heard that anyone else saw it or reported it. 
My husband thought it was strange, but didn't really acknowledged of what he thought it was. My daughter and I thought it was a craft of some kind, but we didn't talk about it anymore.

i don't want to to think we are insane, but my daughter, sisters and myself are sensitive to the paranormal world. Spirits both good and evil, and ESP. My nickname at work was spooky witch and my husband wonders if I'm a witch.
