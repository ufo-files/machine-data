UFO Followed straight path then disappeared

While driving north between Pisgah and Murfreesboro, Arkansas, I noticed orangish ball/sphere shaped object at least 1 mile high traveling south. My brother and I stopped the vehicle and got out and watched it continue south and then disappear within a blink of an eye. Sighting lasted aprox 2 mins.
