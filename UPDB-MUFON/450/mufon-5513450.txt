{nd} Witnessed 3 objects, where one moved horizontal and vertical with lights and a fire like emission.

While driving home from work and merging from the 15N to the 94E, I noticed what looked to be a plane on fire or a very large star or other object moving at a slight vertical incline headed East. It then changed directions headed West horizontally then dropped like a plane in distress all the time with a very large trail of flames. After dropping at a rapid pace, it then again changed direction shooting straight up with the flame trail and reached an altitude and then turned off the flame. There was also two other objects that I can only assume were like planes or helicopter that were with this object the whole time. It was almost like they were playing chase with each other. Its hard to explain how large the object must have been as I was miles away and the flame being emitted was very large to where I could not miss it in the sky when I looked over my shoulder to look for on coming traffic while merging on to the 94E. 
This lasted about a minute or so. As I was headed East, away from the activity, I continued to watch to only see objects with green/blue and red lights in the same area flying around.
