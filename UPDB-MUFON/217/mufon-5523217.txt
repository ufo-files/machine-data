Group Of UFOs Hovered Over Houses And Kept Disappearing And Reappearing

My brother was lying outside and he saw something in the sky. He ran in my house and got my Mom. She and my brother saw them. Then my brother ran and got me. I went out there and they had moved from the west of our house to the north side of our house. I saw them. There was at leasat 20 of them. They hovered and danced around each other. Sometimes they would pulsate a orangeish color. They kept disappearing and reappearing. Then they just left and we didn't see them again. We tried to take pictures of them, but they weren't in our pictures.
