saw disk shaped UFO over tree tops

I was visiting a friend's house in Grand Rapids, MI when I looked out the window and saw the typical disk shaped UFO hovering right over the tree tops about a half to a quarter mile from me out the window. It was silent and there were about 9 yellow lights on it. One of the lights was on the top. It was 4:35 PM. The sky was overcast. I watched it for about a minute and a half trying to figure out what it was and then I turned to my friend and said, "What is that?" When I turned back to the window, it was gone. I wrote everything down at the time so I wouldn't forget.
