{cak} 4 lights moving from north to south

On 10/12/2015 I observed 4 lights traveling from north to south along the florida coast from my location in Port st Lucie, this is the second time I have witnessed unusual lights in the same general location traveling in the same direction in just the last week . Unlike the previous lights which appeared as orbs, these lights looked like stars, except that there was no wavering in them , they did not blink or flash or twinkle, they were steady.They moved in formation 3 lights forming a triangle and a 4th light following behind at slightly greater distance giving them the appearance of a stingray, I believe that there is some serious U.F.O activity on the coast now, and I will be going down to the beach with camera in hand to attempt to video these events, as should you , my point and shoot camera will probably let me down .
