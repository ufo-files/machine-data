Pulling out of the mall exit off LP 1604 north and all of a sudden my Mom said what is that!

We were just leaving a resturant at the Mall of LP 1604 north when we got to the exit and my Mom said whatis that in the distance. I looked up it look like it was somewhere between Valero and UTSA. It was silver metallic object and at first we thought it was a big balloon like a small Good Year blinp but really shiny in the sky but then all at once it looked like it was morphing or turning. It also looked like it had a roundish body and tail. My Mom at first thought it was a helicopter but when it started to change and morph (this is the only way I can think to explain how it looked) she said she thought it looked like it was in 3-D and then started to look kind of flat and not in 3-D. It was very strange and not an airplane. So we pulled out onto the access road and looked at it for another minute. We decide to take the turn around and get a better look to see what it really was but after we went on the turn around 1604 South it was gone. So went back up the LP 1604 north back home we looked up in the sky and saw a black dot way up high and it could have been a bird except it was moving pretty darn fast. We turned in at Hills and Dales to look again and it was gone too and it had been heading toward the where the silver object was. My Mon and I have never seen anything like those objects and I have never reported anything like this before. I really have to say we did not know what either of those objects were.
