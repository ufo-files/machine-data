I was sleeping in my living room on my couch, when, all of a sudden I was awaken by my cat, Sally. She appeared to be in a lot of pain, using her paws to cover up her ears, as if she heard a noise I didn't. I thought about it, and convinced my self to go outside and investigate, due to a light that lit up the entire inside of my living room. It wasn't a normal light though. Almost like a laser beam, and orange and yellow one. Once I went outside, to my backyard, I saw three triangular shaped objects that appeared to be observing the field behind my home. At this point they had not noticed me standing there. I decided to get a closer look. I walked to the edge of my property line; as soon as I got there, I heard a very very loud buzzing/ humming noise. All three of them took turns making the noise. They where in a triangle formation. The one to the left descended and hovered closer to me. The hover was very smooth, like there wasn't any resistance. Once it got close to me (the size of my house) I was shocked. I could't believe what was happening. That was when the creek in between my backyard and the field began swooshing very hard. Water flew in the air, towards the craft. The craft then disappeared. The two other crafts above did the same thing. Went above the creek, and then disappeared. I was too scared to call the local authorities. I have heard what happens. About the men in black. I will never forget the day. I want to know what those crafts were and why they were behind my house, observing my field. And most of all, why did they disappear, and where to? I want an investigation. Since then, I had purchased a gun. Should I shoot at it? Please advise...
