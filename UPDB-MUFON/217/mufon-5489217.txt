{cak} Took a picture of the Blue Angels and this round orb appears in one picture, but was not visible to the eye.

I was taking pictures of the Blue Angels flight team at the Alliance Air Show in Fort Worth, Tx. There was nothing visible in the sky during their flight. After taking a few pictures, I was looking at them and found one picture where a bright orb appears to be following them. It is not the moon and no other airplanes were in the area at the time.
