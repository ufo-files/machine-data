Triangle craft seen coming home from work

Left work at a shop in Hemlock michigan around 6:00pm i was going south on Hemlock Rd. When I noticed a triangle craft flying over my car. I only seen it for maybe 3 to 5 seconds and could not stop due to the shoulder of the road. I tried to turn and look for it when it passed but the veiw was blocked due to a tree line. I was traveling about 55 mph. The craft had the classic lights in each corner and a red light in the middle. It had the same metalic color as a plane and was not black. I thought it was pretty cool to see. Hope to see another one.
