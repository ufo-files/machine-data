Looked like a star. Bright glimmering.

A friend and I where standing on the front steps of his house it must have been 1:00AM and I was looking up like usual. In the distance I seen a light moving in the sky coming in from the south. The object was moving North directly towards us. This object must have been pretty high, but still seemed within the atmosphere.

"Do you see that." I said. 

My friend focused in and was like "yeah.i do."

The object then stopped over us for maybe 3 or 4 seconds. And shot off east and the warped into space. From south to north like a shooting star.
