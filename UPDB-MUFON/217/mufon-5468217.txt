{td} Orange Orb hoovering for 10 minutes-Then blinked out.

Woke up to use the restroom in the middle of the night, 4:10 am 5/30/2018. I looked out the open window, through the screen as I came back to bed and notice a larger orange glowing orb hovering high above the mountains at apprx 15k feet. I'm a Pilot. this was not any normal aircraft, no FAA lighting of any kind. The object stayed put and did not move yet clouds did move in front of the object and according to the weather.com radar, those clouds were located about 2-3 miles in front of the object, hence were I approximate the coordinates of the actually UAP. After almost 10 minutes I finally thought to grab the my phone to snap a photo or video, at that moment the UAP just blinked out like someone hit a light switch.
