{jpg} White and Red Orbs spotted flying over West Hartford, CT from Newington.

Friday Nov 01, 2019 @ 6:50 am I saw one white orb with no navigational lights, markings or insignias traveling on a straight line in a North East direction over West Hartford and were viewed from Willard Ave and Francis Ave in Newington. The orb dissapeared from view and reappeared on the same line of travel, but much further away, it than abrutly turned back like in the shape of a knife blade and stopped. A second orb (red) materialized bellow it, but at a distance and both orbs turned on a white blinking strobe light bellow their crafts. The red orb wase flying in a circle pattern and made one turn before both continued in the same directon of travel / speed, towards the NE. Lost sight of them as I continued North on Willard Ave / Newington Rd.
