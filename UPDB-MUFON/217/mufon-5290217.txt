Matt black hockey puck shaped craft with 3 spikes pertruding, top and bottom, observed by 2 passing milliary jets.

On holiday, in Crete, lounging by the hotel pool with a friend soaking up the mid afternoon sun.
As we sat chatting to a lady and her daughter, out of nowhere came two low flying military jets, so low and loud, us and several other pool siders winced and ducked. Startled, I started to recall the time a military jet nearly took my head off when out sailing one time in Greece with my parents. Within a few seconds, the lady to our right suddenly blerted out 'what the hell is that?' As she looked and pointed at an object above the hotel, there I saw a matt black hockey puck shaped craft with 3 black spikes pertruding out of the centres, top and bottom. Not hovering in one position, but effortlessly just swaying back and forth and side to side. I instantly said 'that's a UFO!' and told my friend to take photos with his then iPhone 4. Within around 10 seconds the UFO that I and several others around the pool had been observing disappeared north over the hotel and across the Mediterranean Sea. The craft was matt black, pertruding spikes, no signs of any windows, propulsion, no sound and about the size of one of the jets that went by under a minute earlier. I'm guessing it was approximately 800ft away and under 1000ft in altitude. Unfortunately, the photos that my friend took didn't reveal any detail of the UFO, just black dots taking up just a few pixels. The illustrations given are as accurate as I can give, scale and UFO detail.
