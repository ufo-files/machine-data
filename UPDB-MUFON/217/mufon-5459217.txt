{cak} 3 red sometimes white orbs off the coast of Hampton

I was standing in front of the house having a cigarette looking at the sky to the east when 1glowing object appeared to be going from E to NE then stopped completely in place. A moment later a second object appeared brighter than the 1st but was glowing a very bright red orange glow. It stopped as if hovering there like a star for approximately 3min it then flashed it's lights as if it was a commercial aircraft. At little intervals it would come to a stop ascend then descend pulse and glow flash it's lights then hover there. After a few short moments my wife and mother came out and I showed them what I was watching I tried to get a video of it but my phone is not the best at taking video at night at great distances. We watched the object for about an additional 5min as it stopped in the air again my mom thought it my be a helicopter but quickly dismissed the idea after it did a decent with a zig zag type of motion to it very quickly. After crossing into my neighbors yard to get a better veiw cause it had started to go beneath the tree line me and the wife saw it stop several times blink and pulse with the red glow until disappearing completely out of sight as if heading back the way it came. Just to clarify I grew up in a military family and seen my fair share of helicopter s and planes of all shape s and sizes this was definitely unlike any aircraft I've seen. But considering the altitude and the way it moved I don't know. Hopefully someone else can verify what I've seen with my family.
