I was sitting in the back seat of our car with my sister driving and my mom in the passengers seat. I was looking out the window and something glimmered and caught my eye. I quickly thought it must be some balloon someone let go of ascending up in the sky. Or maybe it was some weather balloon. I thought whatever it was I may as well take my phone and start recording it. As I watched it, it kept on ascending higher in the sky at the same rate. As I looked more, I noticed it looked like two black orbs and once in a while they seemed to reflect sunlight, or it may have been making it's own light. They were vertical from each other getting higher in the sky in synchronization. I didn't think much of it because I thought it must be some balloon with something attached to the bottom, yet I did feel some doubt about what it could be. I then told my mom and sister to look out the window. My mom didn't see it as she was in the passenger seat busy talking on her phone. But my sister did see it and she thought it was cool.
