{cak} 3 red orbs above Kalama

My friend and I were in Kalama going to a friends house. We turned up the wrong street and decided to turn around. As we were doing this, above a tree I notice two red orb like lights heading in a north to south direction. They seemed to be equidistant from one another. They moved very slow in the sky. After about 1min-1 1/2min, the lead red sphere dissipated. It seemed like it just fizzled out. The second followed suit about 3 seconds later. Once they disappeared, my friend and I started to head towards our friends. I looked over to where I saw them first and sure enough, a third shows up. This one was the same color but started in more of a east to west direction. About 10 seconds of observing it going in that direction, it shifts its direction to the same as the other two. The change wasnt gradual, it was very abrupt. After the direction change, we watched the object for about another 30-45 seconds then the object fizzled out like the other two.
