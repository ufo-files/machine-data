I watched a bright non flashing red light in the southern sky. The light was moving from Southwest toward the Northeast.

At approximately 10:15 pm on the evening of May 2nd, 2020. I watched a bright red light moving from the Southwest toward the Northeast above neighboring homes. It was approximately 500 feet above me. There was no flashing lights and zero sound. The light was moving at a steady and fast pace until the light looked to phase away and disappear. I thought it might have been a drone, however it was completely silent and there were no flashing lights indicating this.
