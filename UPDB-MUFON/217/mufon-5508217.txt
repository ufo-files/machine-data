As we got back from shopping ,parked the car outside the house and then happened to look ahead at the very dark clouds on the horizon .There appeared a darting light backwards and forward behind the clouds.I pointed this out to my partner who said 'better get indoors' and so we did.
Five minutes later I had to head North to Harlow .
Once on the Motorway I glanced up and there appeared a moon like
Object to my left far ahead .
After a few miles the object appeared to grow larger.
By this time I was very curious.
Lost site of the object for a while while going down a hill.
As the car began to ascend up the next hill I happened to look up wondering why they were all driving at a snail's pace .
Then still looking up I saw the most beautiful ,diamond shaped object that seemed to hover above in the star lit sky.It looked almost like a massive chandellior with two beams of light reaching all the way to the ground.
Instinctively I drove the car into the hard shoulder , in order to get a better view.My actions frightened my twelve year daughter who had also witnessed everything .She screamed and literally forced me to drive on .
I feel that I Have missed a great opportunity of witnessing something very special on that night.Who knows what might have happened had she not been there with me.
