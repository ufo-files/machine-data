Caught trangular dark black craft while watching Blackhawks during what I thought was routine patrols

I was photographing two black Blackhawks that were flying in tight formation with additional equipment attached to the sides. (Looked like extended range fuel tanks because they had medivac badging so I don't think it would be pods for weapons). I caught a dark black triangular shaped craft behind them near the horizon. I included the photos to this report. The first is unedited. The second is the same photo zoomed and cropped. I took it with my smartphone. The helicopters did not appear to be aware of the craft, or at least they were not engaging it. The closest man-made thing to it would be an F117 or B2 but this is not a military operating area nor do those types of aircraft frequent the KOXC or KDXR airports. Blackhawks are seen routinely doing patrols/training, however are not based here.
