Stopped at road works took pictures just as I was ushered on.

While traveling home from work I was stopped at road works.I noticed as the sun was going down 2 orbs one above the setting sun and one to the Left in what looked like a another sunset.
The orb on the left is Green with 4 white dots on its surface and the one above the Sun was white.
I thought it was very strange to see another mini sunset to the left and then I could just make out the green orb. when I arrived home and enlarged the picture I noticed the 4 white dots on its surface and also the other orb.
