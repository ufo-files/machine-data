{flg} Orange ball of light ascended like a firework than traveled across sky from right to left-disappeared into a pinpoint of light

Was sitting outside in the backyard talking with friends. One friend commented-what is that? She pointed to an orange ball of light traveling parallel with the tree line from right to left. We watched it continue its path-conjecturing as to what it could be-no blinking lights like an airplane-perhaps a helicopter? Couldnt hear any sound. Once it reached our far left-it disappeared into a pinpoint of orange. The friend that spotted it at first explained how she thought it was a firework @ first because of the way it ascended & then she waited for it to explode but it didn't. It just kept traveling across the sky.
