Cigar shaped object moving slowly and silently overhead

Around 7:35pm this evening I went outside to get an item out of my car that was parked in the driveway; something compelled me to look up at the super bright full moon and when I turned around to shut the car door and then looked almost straight up at the moon I noticed a very dark cigar shaped object to the right of the moon from my vantage point moving slowly directly overhead; it gently reflected the moonlight off of itself so I could see the silhouette of it's outline and shape. It was moving very slowly with no audible noise or any visible lights. There were no contrails or exhaust gases or vapors. There were no other aircraft in the area at the time of this sighting. It proceeded to move slowly from the south to the north east and continued on until I could no longer see it. Unfortunately I left my cell phone in the house so I was not able to get any pictures or video of the object. And I was much too fixated and awestruck at what I was seeing to call for anyone in the house to come outside and be an extra witness. This sighting literally gave me goosebumps and made the hairs on the back of my neck stand straight up. However this was not my first sighting of unidentified aerial craft in this area. I've seen many other unexplained objects in the skies here over the years that I've lived here. But this was the first time I've ever seen the classic cigar shaped object that has been talked about in ufo folklore for decades.
