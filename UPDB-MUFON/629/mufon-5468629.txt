Strange appearance at the sky above a house

1. It was late night, I was jogging on a road with a river besides it. Then, I took a seat to rest for a while when I noticed something strange going on at the sky. 

2. The object was pretty much infront of me at the sky appearing above a house. It was shining and appeared out of nowhere. Because of the light I noticed it. 

3. First, I assumed that it may be an airplane but it didn't fit with the movements and the light etc..

4. The object appeared at the sky far away visible above the house which was also far away from me. It appeared on the top left corner of it and was like glowing. It was there for a while before it moved a little bit downward from my viewpoint on and then it disappeared. This was the first encounter and I didn't film it because it was unexpected. Next times, it appeared again but this time not on the top left corner, rather directly above and on top of the house. It was again resting at the spot for a while when it started to abruptly move in circles and very unnaturally. It disappeared and reappeared a few other times after that close to that spot but moved either more to the left or right. Then, it did similar stuff before it disappeared for the last time and wasn't visible anymore. 

5. First I was somewhat scared and shocked. Then I got more and more surprised. So, I decided to film it in the middle. I was astonished and it grabbed my attention. After the event, I felt happier and more releaved and better. 

6. The object disappeared in the sky.
