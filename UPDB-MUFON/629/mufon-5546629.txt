Black Triangle hovering, gliding in silence

IT WAS SUMMER 1985,,, ME, MY BROTHER MICHAEL, AND MY BEST FRIEND MIKE TONEY WERE THROWING A BASEBALL, PLAYING CATCH IN THE MIDDLE OF OUR STREET. IT GOT DARK REAL LATE. WE LIVED ON A MILITARY BASE..FT. HUACHUCA WHICH IS OUTSIDE OF SIERRA VISTA, AZ. I DON'T REMEMBER WHO SAW IT FIRST BUT WE LOOKED UP AND THERE WAS THIS LARGE BLACK TRIANGLE GLIDING SILENTLY BUT YOU COULD HEAR A LOW HUM....IT WENT OVER US AND CIRCLED AROUND THEN WENT BEHIND THE MOUNTAINS NEXT TO OUR BASE AND CAME OVER AGAIN AND WENT STRAIGHT....ON THE OBJECT THERE WERE 3 WHITE LIGHTS ON THE POINTS OF THE TRIANGLE OBJECT AND A RED LIGHT IN THE MIDDLE AND IT WOULD GO OFF/ON SLOWLY. IT WAS PROBABLY ABOUT 5000 FEET ABOVE US MAYBE... BUT IT GLIDED SLOWLY... AND I DIDN'T HEAR AN ENGINE ROARING...WE LIVED ON A MILITARY BASE...ALL I CAN SAY IT WAS DEFINITELY A UFO..I KEPT THIS WITH ME FOR 25 YEARS BUT THANKS TO TNMUFON SHE TOLD ME TO REPORT IT SO I DID. .THE PHOTO ATTACHED IS A GOOD DRAWING OF WHAT I WITNESSED ON THAT EVENING
