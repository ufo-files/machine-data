white or light color, distinct segment moving contrail ?

About 5:15 am out jogging on dirt trail away from street lights, behind a local University. A white color caught my eye against the dark night sky, looking up and east ward. In the span of 4-5 seconds I observed what I thought was a contrail. However this trail moved as one distinct segment or unit. It did not trail along the sky like a typical airline contrail. It zipped across the sky from above San Bernardino toward the direction of Palm Springs. Totally disappearing from sight in the span of 4-5 seconds. No lights or sound were observed. Possible it could be a meteor passing earths upper atmosphere ? I don't know. Never have had such a weird observation. I also stopped to ask a University Police officer, but he said he had not seen anything.
