observed triangle shaped object moving slowly, filmed objects flying near fighter jets

Jets had been flying in the area and went outside on Feb 13,2012 to watch them when I noticed a triangular shaped craft fling at lower altitude and very slow.It had big round white lights not to bright with a reddish orange light in center that were not blinking,no other lights were visible.I went in to get my camera, but when I returned it was gone. The jets were still flying around the next day, so decided to film them as they flew around. On Feb 16,2012 I was out in the early evening filming when I transfered my shots to check them out, I noticed that there were objects flying by these jets. I did not notice them while filming.I do not know what the triangular craft was that I saw, But it was not any kind of aircraft using jet engines, It was moving way slower than a small plane, and there was no sound from it at all. As for the objects in the film, I have know idea.
