{dr}Two different events caught on camera only few nights apart, only seen in pictures and not with my eye

On June 25th I was sitting in the parking lot of a gas station waiting to meet someone. I noticed one of the most brilliant sunsets behind a storm cloud, so I got out my new phone and started to take pictures. After looking the pictures, I noticed a white ball of light in them, all taken from the same position where I was standing behind my car. The first 2 were both taken back to back at 842pm and the other 2 at 844 and 845. There of course is the possibility of it being ball lightning or a lens glare, but it's a new camera and I never saw it with my eye. In fact, I wasn't going to even report them, but I have been reading other reports from parts of Georgia around the same time only 100 miles north, which made me change my mind. A few nights prior to this, I was on my porch looking at the moon, and I started to take photos, and noticed a white sphere in 2 of them. I tried to zoom in to get a better picture, but I have a cheap phone. It's possible it a reflection of a cloud, but I don't recall it with my eye and I would have noticed I think. Anyhow, I have the pictures, I'll label them with the time taken for clarity. I don't know if what i saw was anything at all, but because of the other similar reports on this latey, I decided i would atleast submit what I have
