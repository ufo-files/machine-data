2 lights( bright star or planet like) evenly spaced under the moon @ approximately 6:58. Disappeared, top one first then the bottom one.

Observed 2 bright lights (star/planet type)under the moon at exactly 6:56-6:58. Moon was on the rise in the SE at the time in a position of about 10:30. I did not see the objects appear. I noticed the objects and said to our group “look how bright those stars are. Look how symmetrically spaced they are. I didn’t notice them under the moon earlier”. While we were discussing them the top light disappeared. My wife said she could still make it out and then it was gone completely. She made the comment that it “faded away”. About 10-20 seconds after the first light disappeared the second one did also. We were still arguing about the first light disappearing when the second one did. The lights were not meteorites( stationary) and they were not planes or satellites. They were a little bit brighter( white) than Jupiter was this evening. The spacing under the moon was very weird. About 2 inches under the moon and they were about 1 inch apart
