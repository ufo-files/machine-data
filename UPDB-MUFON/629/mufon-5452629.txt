Observe 1 large bright light and 4 to 7 smaller lights

Just after 8 PM PT observed 1 bright light and a smaller one hovering for about 20minutes traveling westward from the san gabriel valley towards Pasadena and Duarte. Planes were seen flying in what looked very different from the hovering lights. I called a friend to come outside and watched what I was seeing through my bonocqulers. Other smaller lights that were observed were glowing or flashing red blue green lights and seemed to be placed in different places in the night sky. I light some what above me but very high in the sky seemed to wobble and through the lens had circular lights around the main circle. One woman saw this object also wobble before I told her it was wobbling or moving.
