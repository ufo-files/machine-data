Multiple UFO sightings

I was at 37th st on the northwest side of Indianapolis at work. I was doing security at a soccer game. The time was around 6:35p.m. and it was halftime. I just so happened to look up in the sky and noticed an object that looked like a white/gray balloon. I strained my eyes at first, I figured it might of been a plane or helicopter because the airport was about 6miles south of my location. They started to get lower and lower. The more I observed It hovered in one spot then it moved within the blink of an eye east. I was in shock I couldn't stop staring at it. I couldn't take my eyes of it them I felt like they new I was watching. It moved back in forth, then appeared 4 more of the same objects. They all got in formation, like the shape of a boomerang. There was a total of 5 UFO's. They drop almost down to cloud level then 1 by 1 they vanished straight up back into space with a blink of an eye.
