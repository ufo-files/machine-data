2 bright lights, too bright, too low and too close to be stars

2 bright lights seen between West and North west direction. One shot off quickly, the other remained for awhile. When I came back outside, the other light/ufo was gone. These were way too bright and low in the sky to be stars. Friends could see them from different areas in Houston, so it is doubtful that it was a spotlight or search light.
