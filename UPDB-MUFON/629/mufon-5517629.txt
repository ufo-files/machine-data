{ck} During city fireworks display first one then three bright orange orbs of size seen wide circling of fireworks display

Went out to look at Bartlett city fireworks display from my patio as looking east from my location @ approx .5 miles. All at once see one bright orange orb like UFO passing perhaps 400-? ft. above HIGHEST ongoing fireworks display in a large (perhaps mile) circle like pattern obscured at times by tree line from my vantange point. Going inside to retrieve camera I came out to see three same such orbs circling the display with two of them close to one another and the third following behind at some distance. Managed to take a horrible picture of the last orb prior to the fireworks ending and seeing no more of them after the event. These orbs exihibited no more than an orange glow and doubt that this would be a ruse of three private pilots performing a stunt as no sound could be heard during lapses in fireworks and only a orange glow was seen with no plane identifiers (green/red) seen.
