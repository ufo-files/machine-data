{rjl} twice my wife and I observered this object at two different times in the same night

I saw an object going south over Philadelphia with a red and green light slightly pulsing. I viewed this from Northeast Philadelphia at around 9:300pm and then again about 11pm. What caught my attention was this object was going the opposite direction of a flight path. then I noticed no strobe lights at all, just a red and green light. These lights were not lights on wing tips of an airplane. The lights were bigger and pulsing. The object stopped many times and went a little to the left then stopped and went North. As it went by I had binoculars on it and I saw a white light on the back end and the red and green also. The object then disappeared over the roof tops. The second event at 11pm I looked over my roof and saw the same object hovering just below the clouds. I had my binoculars on the object for about 15 minutes and cold see the red and green lights still pulsing. The object then began moving after 15 minutes of hovering a little left and right but staying in the same area then slowly started to move south again and I watched until it disappeared over the skyline. The second time I saw the object it seemed higher than the first sighting. I'm a sky watcher and this was not a plane or a helicopter. If anyone else saw this I would love to hear from you. Thanks
