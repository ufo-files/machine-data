We where driving home when the car started to stall a bit and when we got to the driveway I got out of the car when I heard a loud humming and aircraft noises, and when I looked up I saw a airplane flying very low to the ground and was fling in an eastern direction when I walked in to the street to get a better view I saw blimp like shape that appeared to be following the airplane. The airplane seemed to be following the airplane, and when the airplane disappeared over the tree line, the blimp appeared to rotate to face us and a red light turned on in the front and it started to blink and as the blinking occurred a buzzing sound pulsated in sequence. I started to get a throbbing headache that fell inline with the buzzing and then a spot light caught the craft and two jet airplanes flew down the road and the craft turned and took off with the jets in pursuit and soon a helicopter with a spot light followed the jets and that was all I saw. At first I was unsure of the blimp like craft until the jets and the helicopter chased it off so I figure that I should report it.
