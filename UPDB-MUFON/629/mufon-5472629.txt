{dr}flashing lights observed

I was standing outside on my back deck talking to my girlfriend on the phone when I started seeing these flashing lights through a tree. While I kept talking to her, they continued to flash heading south towards Wilmington. Several sets of lights all flashing randomly. After about 10 seconds I didn't see them anymore, and that's when I commented to my girlfriend what I had seen. The following Saturday night (10/22) I went to a halloween event going on at a park here in town when I overheard several people talking about the lights too while waiting to get in, exactly describing the same thing I had seen. I mentioned I saw them too, and they asked where, and I pointed in the NW direction, and they said thats where they saw them too.
