Mystery solved

The UFO mystery I reported on 7/12/12 has been solved. The lighted objects I reported on turned out to be some sort of small hot air balloons launched in memorial of a local high school girl who recently succumbed to leukemia. I had noticed there were a number of the candelaria setting on the baseball diamond across the street from the high school.
My deepest sympathies to the family for their tragic loss.
