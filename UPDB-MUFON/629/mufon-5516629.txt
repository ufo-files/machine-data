(GS) Strange Cloud Formation

By coincidence I noticed that there had been a sighting just a few miles from here this morning at aprox 7:30 a.m. When I came back from the grocery store at aprox 7:45 p.m. I noticed a strange cloud formation almost directly overhead: a large circular cloud formation with a line going straight through it with abrupt beginning and ending. Contrails usually don't begin and end abruptly like that so it was just a bit strange. By the time I got in the driveway and into the house to get my camera it had softened and dispersed somewhat. But when I saw it it was sharper and more defined than the photos depict. I got two clear photos of it. Dont know what it was or if it is a military attempt to divert attention away from whatever happened this morning, but just a bit strange.
