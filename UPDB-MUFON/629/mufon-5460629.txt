{rjl} Straight line of starlike dots traveling across the sky

My sister, Alexandra pointed out the long, straight line of star like features in the night sky to myself and my friend Nick. There was approximately 15 to 20 "stars" in a row and they would all appear brighter in the same areas across the sky. They traveled from the southwest to the northeast portion of the sky and it took approximately 30 to 60 seconds.
