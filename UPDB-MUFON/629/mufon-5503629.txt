{nd} Solid black object moving fast in a straight line without noise, apparent size made it look like a bird

I went out outside to enjoy the weather on my back porch. I noticed what looked like a bird at about 10 degrees above the horizon. It was no bird as it didn't have wings and seemed to move much faster than a bird. It didn't change direction and otherwise didn't move. I thought it may be a plane but It didn't seem to make any noise before, during, or after. I couldn't grab a camera on time. The fact that it made no noise suggests to me it wasn't a plane or helicopter. I could tell for sure it moved faster than a commercial jet but resembles the speed of a fighter jet. It felt astonishing to witness. It seemed to be over 5 miles away moving south to north as I looked west. After ten seconds of observing, I lost sight of it due to trees and immediately wanted to know what it can be so I filed a report.
