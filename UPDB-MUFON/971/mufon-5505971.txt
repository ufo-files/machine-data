{kt}3 of us observed over Southern horizon 2 light yellow diamond shaped objects moving faster than anything ver seen. Changed color to orange then when overhead to bright red. As they moved N they changed color again to orange then finally back to light

3 of us out looking at Hale Bopp Comet when we observed 2 light yellow, glowing, diamond shaped objects. As they moved closer overhead the color transitioned to orange then red overhead. We were in a valley running S to N which was the same path as the objects. We had a clear view from S horizon to N horizon. Based on planes we saw in the area I would say that they were about 1000 ft altitude. As they passed directly overhead there was no sound. As they passed further away they transitioned back to orange then to light yellow. It took approximately 5 seconds for the objects to pass from horizon to horizon. After the stun of what we saw wore off we asked each other if we all saw the same thing. We did. Have never reported before for fear of being labeled crazy.
