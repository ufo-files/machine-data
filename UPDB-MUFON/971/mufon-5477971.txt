8 pm, I was home. looked out my south facing window and saw 3 bright white lights to my south, horizontal and stationary about 30 degrees up. I went out on the deck and watched them for 5-10 seconds, and then they went out one by one, right to left. after the last one went out, I saw 2 fighter jets orbiting where the lights were. I could see the intermittent strobe lights of the planes. They flew around the area for about 10-15 minutes then disappeared to the west.

Two nights later (about 8pm?) I was at a location 5 miles to the east of my first sighting, and saw the same lights again towards my south, only they were bigger and farther apart than the first sighting. I thought they were closer this time. Again they all came on at once, then went out right to left. I did also see one fighter jet strobe in the immediate area. It left after 5-10 minutes. Very exciting, this isn't the first time I've seen strange lights and fighter jets in the general area of the nuclear plant outside Piketon, Ohio.
