Every single night

White and red blinking lights with no discernable pattern. Easily mistaken for typical aircraft, but upon close examination make sporadic fluttery movements, almost bouncing. Also change directions and velocity and/or stop entirely. Make no noise. Witnessed one red/white merge with larger white light as another red/white suddenly appeared to the left where first red/white originated. Have Witnessed red/white objects in all directions simultaneously. This is a nearly nighy occurrence. No regular flight pattern that coincides with commercial routes in the area.
