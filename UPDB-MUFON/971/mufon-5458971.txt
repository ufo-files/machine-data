8:06pm Thursday October 19th, 2017 outside Liberty, Indiana headed East towards the state line. It was already night time. As we approached the railroad tracks, we both immediately stopped our conversation as we both noticed a huge (football field sized) dark cloaked looking triangular solid object with 3 solid red lights, one at each triangle point. It was off the right passenger side of the vehicle. It was hovering on it's side over the cornfield completely silent. As we made our approach closer to it, it turned on its belly and quickly faded away. As if it went invisible or disintegrated. We captured our reactions to this object on our dashcam, but the object was too far right out of frame to see it in the film. We went over the railroad tracks and immediately turned right onto the gravel road (parallel the railroad tracks) to see if we could still see the object. We couldn't see it anymore and after about 5 minutes of waiting to catch another glimpse we headed out. So we turned around to head back towards the road we came off of. When we got back to the road we turned off of, our SUV shut off at the stop sign, but the lights were still on. We attempted to start the car a few times with no luck, it didn't even turn over but the car wasn't dead we still had interior and exterior lights working fully functional. After about 2 minutes we tried again and it started right up and we were off headed home once again.
