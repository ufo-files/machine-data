I noticed two objects moving in a North to South direction.

I was outside with my dog like I am every night around the same time, when I noticed two objects that looked like stars. One was ahead of the other and they were quite a distance apart from each other. The first I noticed was moving in a straight line, fast, then as I looked a little to the left of the sky & the first object, I saw another object same size, the color looked a little different then the first. The first one was more silvery, like a star color, the second was amberish. I watched them until they both went out of view. It was a clear night and I saw planes in the sky before, during and after the sightings. I even said out loud that there were no blinking lights on these two objects like there are on airplanes. I felt like, all right, something is going on because this is not the first time I have seen objects like this. But I have never seen two of them in the same sky and formation at the same time.
