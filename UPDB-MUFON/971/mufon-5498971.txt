{cak} Flashing lights, moved erratically, up, down, diagonal, but stayed in same area.

My sister first noticed the light while sitting out on our back deck as it was getting dark and the stars were becoming noticeable. She said that's too bright to be a star. It was first noticed in the east, over Lake Winnipesaukee, at quite a distance. I thought at first it was a star but it really was too bright to be a star and we could just barely notice colors flashing, white, red and green. We also thought it might be a plane, but it did not move like a plane. Went back out on deck about an hour later, around 9:00 pm, it had moved to a NE position over Lake Winnipesaukee. Watched the 'light' flashing colors, moving very erratically, side to side, up and down, diagonally, in circles. Checked back a few more times, still was there. When I went out to check at 11:00 pm it had disappeared. This happened for 2 consecutive nights, beginning Sept. 27, 2014 and also Sept. 28, 2014. I will check again tonight and report if it is still showing up. We both believe it was a UFO.
