Observed large white orb, thought it was a star. But it moved out of my view. Took pictures short video. of object.

Observed a large bright white orb from my back yard while sitting down with my dog. I noticed the object because it looked to a star, but a low star. Then I thought it was an airplane that was either taking off or landing at the Hobby Airport. I told my sister that I needed to get a better view of it. So I drove to the corner Service Station and parked facing the object. I took pictures and a video. An airplane headed towards Hobby Airport to land. It looked like it had passed in front of the object. After 20 minutes observation I came back home.
