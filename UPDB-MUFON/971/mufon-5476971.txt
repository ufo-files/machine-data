{rjl} Just a red pulsing light in the rain.

I was camping on my vacant lot. It was raining for hours. Water started coming in my tent. I decided to sleep in my CR-V. When I got in I put the drivers seat back. When I did I noticed this pulsing red light in the sky. I then thought it must be a light on a tower that I hadn't noticed before. The next day I saw there was no post or tower the light could have been on.
