{jpg} fireball with trail of sparks that left residue of blake substance in air

I was walking my dog as I do every night around the same time. I saw the light behind the trees and assumed it was a plane flying low until it came out from behind the trees that were approximately 70 feet tall. The object looked like a meteor with it's trail that struck me the most. The entire object looked like sparks. It maneuvered around trees, buildings, power lines, and homes. When I saw the movements I thought something was off so I tried to run after where I thought it would land or crash. Nothing was there so I continued to search the area for about an hour. I was in shock because I've seen strange things like this that seem to be getting stranger as time goes on, but nothing like this. I got tired when I was looking for it and became kind of dizzy. I thought it might have something to do with the black substance emitted from the object, which was not smoke so I went home. I lost sight of the object when it went below the tree line one street over and when I ran to the next street where it appeared to be descending, it was gone.
