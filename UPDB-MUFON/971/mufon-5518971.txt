Like a satellite but absolutely not a satellite!

I was outside looking at airplanes. I was looking due west at an approaching airplane when I saw what looked like a red satellite. I have been researching satellites recently due to the fact that I have been seeing strange lights for the past 2 weeks so I know what satellites look like now. It was heading west for about 5 seconds. This part looked normal enough, but then, in a very graceful way it turned South and traveled across half sky in about 5 seconds! It was very dim and reddish. I know this was not a satellite because: 1. It turned abruptly. 2. It shot across half the sky in around 5 seconds. This makes the 3rd sighting for me in 2 weeks and 4 UFOs total!
