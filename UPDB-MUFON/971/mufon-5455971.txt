APPROX. 5-6 RED LIGHTS...TRIANGULAR FORMATION

I OBSERVED 5-6 RED LIGHTS HOVERING AT FIRST IN SKY...THEY WOULD GO INTO A LARGE TRIANGULAR FORMATION....SOME WOULD CHANGE DIRECTION AND THEN COME BACK....FOR A FEW SECOND, AT RANDOM, SOME LIGHTS WOULD COMPLETELY DISAPPEAR, AND THEN REAPPEAR. WHILE WATCHING THIS GO ON, A COUPLE OF SMALLER AIRPLANES OR HELICOPTERS (NOT SURE AS IT WAS DARK) SEEMED TO FLY OVER THE FORMATION. I WATCHED FROM THE PLAZA PARKING LOT, FACING EAST, FOR 10 MINUTES....I COULD STILL THIS HAPPENING IN MY REAR-VIEW MIRROR, AS I DROVE BACK TO WORK, IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION (WEST BOUND ON TAUNTON RD TOWARDS ANDERSON ST.) UNFORTUNATELY I DID NOT HAVE MY CELL PHONE TO RECORD AS I WAS ON A QUICK COFFEE RUN FROM WORK.
