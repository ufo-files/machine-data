{tjd} Four orbs hovering in a formation. Two orbs separated like shooting stars and stopped again to hover.

I was walking home on Saturday, October 6th, at around 7:20pm in a residential neighborhood in Redwood City. It was dark enough that I was caught off guard by a distinct formation of lights about a mile south of where I was walking. I saw four (4) bright orbs hovering in a square or diamond like position in the sky. Moments later, two of the orbs fell like shooting stars from their original positioning...they started moving like shooting stars or fireworks. After they both moved they then stopped again, and began hovering again..at this point the 4 orbs made a 4 point linear position and started to move away, eventually dissapearing. 

I've lived in the silicon valley my whole life so I'm used to seeing a ton of activity in the sky whether its planes, drones, satellite launches, etc. This was certainly nothing I've ever seen before. I was only able to capture the last moments before the lights disappeared as I was running down the street to get away from the street lights that were too bright for my camera to work very well. For several days I tried to find out what it could have been, luckily some kids nearby caught the lights as well and posted the video on youtube. 

Space X had a launch the following night in Los Angeles. Apparently their launch was visible even from the Bay Area. This was definitely not that.
