Cigar-shape darker than moonless night sky

On 2022-01-21 at approximately 17:45, I was looking out a South-facing window in a completely darkened room of my house. Looking up to the left, my eyes noticed something moving in the sky, except I almost didn't see it. It was obscuring the stars behind it and I had to move my eyes slightly to the right to see that in fact it was moving, and surprisingly it was the shape of a cigar. There were no wings, no blinking or colored lights. On the left and right ends of this cigar shape where incredibly faint hitw lights, the same size, and intensity of faint stars (the type that you have to look in your peripheral vision to see). The cigar shape was traveling from the left of my window to the centre and seemed to be very high up, yet large. The event took no more than 6 seconds. The first thing that I though was, where are wings, and why aren't there any real lights to illuminate this thing. The moon was not visible in the sky at this hour, though it did rise a few hours later.
