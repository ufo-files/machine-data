{jpg} 2 Glowing Fireballs moved in my direction, stopped in the sky, and disappeared.

While sitting on my Coach, watching tv, I noticed a glowing fireball in be sky. Having seeing his object multiple times in the past, I rushed out to look. In the last, there was always one glowing object. This time, there were two, about 10-30 seconds after each other. The first time I witnessed this many months ago, I thought it was a plane on fire. I was able to predict what was going to happens based on passed sightings. Just as predicted, the first ball stopped it's slow motion, sat there for a moment, then slowly disappeared, still flickering as it did this. The second fireball did exactly the same 10-30 seconds later. This was the 4th time a sighting like his has occurred in the last year, and the first time I spotted two of the objects. Others who have seen it in my area have said its just the space station passing by, I have my doubts. Very curious as to what these objects might be.
