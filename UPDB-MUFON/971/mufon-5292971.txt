UFO flying from West to East

At approximately 00:10 am I witnessed a glowing object slowly flying due South from my location at around 20/25 degree's traveling from the West directly East, there was no sounds at all, I took several photos of the craft, but because of the distance and the fact I had to zoom in, the pictures are out of focus, I had to go from my back garden to the front to witness the craft again, but before I got to the front I woke up the second witness to this event, the craft by that time had stopped and seemed to be hovering around a mile or two away,.
As I was checking the camera to see if it had captured anything the craft had disappeared before I could get any more photos.
