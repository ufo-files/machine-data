Summer night in 1977, I was 16 years old and was dropped off at home at approx. 11:30 PM by girlfriend. Went inside to fix a sandwich and went to bedroom at about 12:00 midnight. Sat on bed and was drawn attention to a purple very bright light shining through the blinds and into my room. I got up and pulled the blinds open and saw a purple oval shaped object that looked like liquid metal flowing around the craft. It was hovering above the road and house across the street about 20 to 25 feet in the air I watched for a few seconds, got scared and went to wake up my sister and brother-in-law whom I was living with at the time. I was freaking out and by the time they got to my room and looked out the window, the object was gone! Till this day no one believed me but I know what I saw! I will take it to my grave. I was wondering if anyone else saw this thing also. This happened in Chesterfield County Virginia 1977 over Tammaway Drive in the Clarendon Subdivision. After all these years, I decided to report this thing because it really bugs me about what this was! Really need to know if anyone else saw this too!
