Saw orange orbs on beach

I was on the first night out of a 4 day trip to Puerto penasco Mexico.I was staying at a friends condo on the beach. Every one had gone to bed early but I was in a lounge chair looking to the west at fishing boats on the horizon. I got up and an glanced towards the east. There was 2 fairly large, baseball sized objects side by side that appeared as glowing orange orbs. I didn't have my camera so I was going inside to retrieve it. I was watching the orbs as I hurrying inside and tripped on a step. I was going to wake up my wife and friends but they were sound a sleep. Returning outside the 2 orbs appeared to be moving away until they disappeared about 10 seconds later. I didn't take a picture of the first two.I sat back down and within 10 minutes I saw one more from the same direction. Shortly thereafter 2 more orbs were heading in my direction. I was able to take pictures but they didn't come as close as the first two. Within a half hour I saw two more single orbs to the west above the boats. I took pictures of them. I have never seen anything like this before.
