Thought we saw 4 meteors was very excited to see them at our vacation cabin. Right after seeing what we thought were meteors about 4 objects were doing figure 8s in the sky. We shined a flash light 30 mins after watching them towards objects(they looked like stars moving at this point) one got very bright at one point and the perception that my father, friend and I it looked like it way heading strait for us. We ran away from the lake at that point. We got half way to the cabin and my friend turned and saw it was no longer coming towards us. These things kept moving very fast. about a hour into it we saw two very bright lights to the right of us over the lake. They seemed to meet together for about 10 mins (no notice) seemed small yet close to us at the time(half a mile out in the center of the lake). After 10 mins they took off at a great speed and nothing was seen after that.

weather:clear
sky:open no trees over a lake

I was about 14 years old at the time. Just learned of this website today. We have carried this event with us for many many years.
