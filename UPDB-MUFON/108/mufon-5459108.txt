{rjl} Busy street. No one else saw. Dull blue grey triangles stacked sideways. Large and close. hovered and floated behind trees. disappeared.

I was driving to the other side of town on a crowded street. I first thought the UFO was a movie prop and I thought how advanced it was to be able to do something like that. I looked around for the set. I looked back up and the UFO began to move behind trees. By the time I drove to where the tree was, it had disappeared. My van was covered by a shadow and I heard a whoosh. I couldn't see what cast the shadow. I did notice a helicopter in the area. The UFO was big. I would say the size of a big army helicopter. It was a dull light grey in color and looked like triangles stacked side by side...3 or 4 triangles. I noticed seams in the metal and smooth raised bulges in various places. What was crazy was that no one else saw it. No one else looked up. It was impossible to miss. This was middle of the day on a clear sunny day. I did hear my own voice but more gentle say either "we've always been here" or "they've always been here"
