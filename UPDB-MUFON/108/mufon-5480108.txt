{rjl} Object was hovering over 20 bypass near Jimtown museum

I was driving west on CR26 West of SR 19 just passed CR 1 when I noticed a really bright orange/gold lights over the 20 bypass. At first I thought it was a helecopter unntil it all of a sudden it just zoomed south into the clouds in a split second and all I could see was a bright dot in the clouds. As fast as it zipped into the clouds it came back again over the by pass. It was huge and very bright. There was another car about a mile east of me and we both were almost stopped watching it as it was looking like it was coming closer to us and thats when we both hurried up to get to Beech road and get away from there. After I got home I was looking up at the stars whenn I noticed that there was at least a dozen orbs cigar shaped crafts in the sky above Osceola & south western Elkhart county. I also observed orange orbs coming out from underneath the objects and coming down above the tree tops like they were looking for something or just filming what is here. The night before I was outside around 11 pm with my dog when a bright object zipped down from the western sky and was traveling very fast when it collided with a triangle shaped craft . The triangle shaped craft started spinning out of control for a few seconds then it was back under control and hovering for a minute then all of a sudden it vanished. The object that hit the triangle craft just kept lying very fast to the east. I don't know if anyone saw that happen other than myself. I don't have an I phone or I would've been able to take pictures. Every time that I've tried to take pictures my camera shuts off all by its self or they just don't show on film.
