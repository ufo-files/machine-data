I was looking at the ISS live feed and noticed an object just appear out of nowhere

I was at home checking my emails and decided to look up the ISS live feed to see what earth looks like from the astronauts poiht of view, during the feed an object appeared into view, to be honest I didn't think anything of it at the time as I thought it might be a reflection of one of the cameras or something to that effect however the object began to move while there were no apparent changes to the outer environment at the time, the object moved from side to side then moved back to its original position, since then I've tried to keep an eye out for the object on the live feeds, I got about a full 40 seconds before the transmission was cut by NASA.
