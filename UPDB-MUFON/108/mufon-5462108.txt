Between 4 and 6 years old at night I was woken up by an electric noise. I slept in the same room as my brother, I got up and went to sit at the foot of my brother's bed because there were shadows in 3 dimensions with coloured flashes coming. They were gathered in 6 to 8 shadows. I can't say the exact number. I don't think he was either male or female. He was talking to me but not in words, it was electric waves. With my adult brain I can explain this as if I were a computer and they were a flash drive. I understood what he was saying about the second one but then it was like when you look for a word you know it but can't say it. He was all talking to me at the same time and after a few minutes it became very oppressive and my head was buzzing. I tried to wake my brother who didn't react after I called my parents and just my mother heard me. That's basically it. I am sure I wasn't dreaming and my mother told me when I was older than that happened very often and she was trying to wake my father and that he too wasn't waking up. 
After that I didn't see them anymore but the electronic sound still reached around my 16th birthday. I am now 41 years old and it never happened to me again.
