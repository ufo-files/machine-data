I was outside having a cigarette and just looking up, thinking how bright the stars are tonight when out of the blackness of the sky I saw an object appear out of nowhere right where I was staring. It sort of looked disc like and had the appearance of a solid form, but the edges seemed a bit fuzzy. The color was a mixture - pinky orange, greyish red. Suddenly the edges looked as if they were folding inwards, it looked like it was folding into itself. It seemed to be moving straight up. In an instant it was gone. Seconds later a shooting star shot over my roof out of the north towards the south east. When I first saw the object I thought it was airplane lights, but they never look like that and it was too big and single.
