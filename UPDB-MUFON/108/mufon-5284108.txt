Very Large Circular in size

MUFON Sighting Report--I saw this Very Large Circular Disk in a USA Large Air Force Base HANGER. I saw if from above & then we went down to where it was located & I actually touch it, ran my hands across its surface. And I asked who owned the Disk & I did receive an answer. 
FYI- I worked for the Space Program at the time back in late 1978, therefore I was given the privileges of seeing, touching, asking questions etc.
