Star like object that zigzagged and changed color

I saw this near the end of the cities public fireworks display. Waiting for my wife outside Portable toilet when my eyes were attracted by what at first appeared to be a bright star. I was curious because it was glowed with a pale green glow that changed to a pale blue glow. This was extremely high in the sky and positioned nowhere near the fireworks so there was definitely no connection to the displays.  As I continued to look at the object it suddenly accelerated a short distance and just as suddenly stopped. My wife came out of the portable toilet and I directed her sight to the object. In again accelerated another short distance in a different direction and again stopped. This happened a third time and again stopped.  The fireworks had ended at this point.  Our last observation was seeing the object shoot very quickly in a straight line until it disappeared in the distance. This was the only time either of us had seen a UFO.
