Sphere like object, colored flame orange

Saw a sphere like object that had an orange glow to it. This object was moving on a semi straight line from what appeared to be west to east. Object would slow down shortly, then move back to its original speed. Was moving for approximately 2 minutes and then disappeared from the sky. The event was witnessed by myself and my friend/co-worker. We both agreed that it looked like nothing we had ever seen before.
