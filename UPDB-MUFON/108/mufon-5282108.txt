Bright light appears, hovers then takes off rapidly

My cousin and I were leaving my families ranch to walk home just down the street. As we were heading out the driveway a very bright whitish blue sphere appeared just over the front pasture to our right. Was maybe about 40 yards from us and about 30 ft off the ground. There was no sound and everything around us was still. It looked extremely bright but wasn't illuminating anything around it or us though the brightness seemed as a spotlight pointed directly at us. It stayed hovering with no movement for a little over a minute. Long enough for us to both verify we were witnessing the same thing. Eventually it took off in what I can best describe as an upside down nike swoosh west over the hills towards Richmond and the Pacific. It traversed it an a matter of a second with no sonic boom. Unfortunately my cousin passed away in 2017 so I am the only remaining witnesses.
