3 shiny round objects.

Early evening. Clear sky. I observed 3 shiny round objects flying in a straight line. They reached a point in the sky when the first object began flying in a circular formation followed by the second and third. They shimmered on the right side (sun). They were fast. They flew in a circular pattern in the sky and began flying away. 

I am near Joint Base McGuire-Fort Dix-Lakehurst. There are ALWAYS planes and occasional helicopters flying overhead. Planes were visible before, during, and after I witnessed them. These were MUCH MUCH FASTER. Apples and oranges. 

I ran indoors to get my phone to take a picture and lost them when I came back out. I spent the next several minutes in shock but surveying the sky.
