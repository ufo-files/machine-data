Spiral of fire

A spiral of fire spinning stationery over Mediterranean sea from Paris to Algiers airport 2 planes or more saw it with the passengers, including ours . everyone or so took pictures my colleagues and the cockpit crew the thing follow us since 11h00 or so am from Paris to Algiers we arrived at 8 or 9 pm the captain and the crew made a report but they said in the Algerians paper it was a balloon .i don't remember well the time the year etc...but believe me i saw it ..i was in my 20..now i am 54..it was stunning..when we landed it chow out of the sky at a great velocity..in the sky night it was orange red spinning..i just have my contract to show you that i was really a summer flight attendant
