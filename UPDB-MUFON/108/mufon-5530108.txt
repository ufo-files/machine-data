Saw a light moving and it got smaller and smaller until it disappeared.

i live west of toronto in ontario canada and last night (sept 10 2010) about 8:50 eastern time i saw a light in the sky moving towards the south, it was a circular light with no other lights flashing on it. i thought it must be a personal plane since we have an airfield not to far away. then i realized this light is actually getting smaller and smaller until it completely disappeared. the whole sighting lasted about 10 seconds. the light moved in a straight line. im not sure what the altitude of this object was but i got the impression it was rising.
