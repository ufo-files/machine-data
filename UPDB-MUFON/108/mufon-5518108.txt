Took a video of the Super Moon on 5-5-2012. After transfering it to my computer from my Panasonic video camera, I notice an object moving across the moon and then disappearing after leaving the moon on the left. After seeing this, I made a short video and uploaded to Youtube. Then I captured a still frame of the object as it left the moon (or what appeared to be leaving the moon. Then I enlarged the still frame 5000x and found 6 more objects, or what could be one circular objects with six orbs attached. I then made another video and uploaded to Youtube. Altogether I made 3 videos.
