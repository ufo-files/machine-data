{jpg} AM and PM Lights in Direction of Ravenna Armory

Glowing golden light appeared slightly northeast this am at approx 5:50. This morning marks the 6th or 7th time we have seen a light in this area. Our home faces east, we sit on the east facing porch both morning and evening. We have seen this light at this time in several occasions in the past 10 days. We have also seen this light or a similar one neatly every evening. When watching the sky and clouds, a streak of bright light will appear and then a glowing dot. The dot glows orange-ish in the am but white in the night. I’m imagining it reflects the sunrise. The lights general behavior is as follows: after a flash of light the glowing dot appears. The dot will hover for periods of 10-20 minutes.. then it may, or may not make an abrupt, right angle turn, before it just disappears.. the disappearance may be marked by another flash of light.. or not.
