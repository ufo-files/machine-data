Witnessed an object hovering and darting in and out of clouds.

On January 18th at approx 4pm local, I was on the balcony of my cruise ship docked in Barbados. I was approx 75ft above sea level and facing SSE. I noticed in the sky an object that was stationary but appeared to be moving slightly to the NNE. The object appeared to me to resemble an airplane on final wile looking head on with the landing lights on. After watching this object for approx 10 min i noticed the altitude and area where i first noticed the object had not changed, so i watched the object more intently. The object appeared to be White or silver in color and at an altitude that was the same as the base of the cloud deck. The object would go into clouds and come back out of the clouds either higher or lower than it went in but always in the same general area. While sillouetted against the darker clouds it looked bright white and when in the blue sky looked silver to white. I watched This behavior and Had my wife witness It as well for approx 1 hr, all the while in the same area and at the same altitude more or less. I Did take some digital photos while watching however i have not looked at them yet. The distance to me is unknown however the altitude would be whatever the cloud deck was on that date. I estimate between 2500 to 3500 ft. The object never appeared to get closer or farther away and there were no other air traffic witnessed in that location.
