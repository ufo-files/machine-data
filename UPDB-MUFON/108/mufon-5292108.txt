Hovering and oscillating bright UFO that seemed circular in shape

I was washing my hands in the bathroom sink just before midnight. There is a large window above it. I live in a wooded area down in a little valley in the hillsides of Santa Barbara. It was foggy last night. And so when I saw this bright light directly outside in the sky it really stood out. It took me a couple minutes to realize what I was seeing. It was hovering and almost looked like it was vibrating. And it seemed to oscillate. I got my camera and spent the next 40 + minutes on and off watching it slowly move to the right and up and then eventually move backward into the distance. During this time it would get brighter then less bright coming and going very slowly. Not fast in it's movements. It wasn't until it finally left that it seemed to move pretty quickly and back away from my sight. I want to upload some of the videos but I am struggling to do so via my iPhone due to the size of the videos. I think I did it right? The photos don't really do it justice. I would love someone to look at the video and help me understand what is there! I have seen other UFO's in my life but nothing I was ever able to capture via photos or video and so often questioned what I had really seen. Not with this. I appreciate your time and any help.
