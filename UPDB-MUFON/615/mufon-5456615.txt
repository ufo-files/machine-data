{jpg} Strobing light moved slowly across sky

My boyfriend and I were camping at Black Moshannon state park. At 1:45 am we walked to the bathrooms and were star gazing. That’s when we noticed an object high up in the sky. It appeared to be the same size as the brightest stars or slightly bigger. It was a single light strobing chaotically. It seemed to be a white light but it was so quick that it was actually hard to tell, I think there may have been a red light flashing intermittently as well. The object itself was moving from west to southwest at an extremely slow pace. When first observed I wasn’t sure if it was moving at all- that is how slow it was moving. This was the extent of the sighting, just a randomly pulsating light moving at a slow pace. We observed it for about 15 minutes before deciding to go to bed. I’ve never seen anything like this before and thought I should report it because it was very odd.
