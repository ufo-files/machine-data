I was outside my home filming the moon with my canon rebel t5 with a 650-2600mm HD telephoto zoom lens for canon Eos around 7:30pm.
I was trying to focus on the Moon and noticed something black shot over the bottom right corner of the shot.
At first I thought it was a bird but realized if it was the bird would have shown up bigger in the telescope.
The object was black and it looked like it was spinning almost like a boomerang..
I was very excited I1d been looking at the moon for months and had never captured an image like this.
