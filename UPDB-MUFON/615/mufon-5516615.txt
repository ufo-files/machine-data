(GS) Very bright light moving across the sky

Around 10pm we were out on our deck watching the sky when we noticed a very bright light seem to appear out of nowhere and travel across the sky in a north-east direction. It was much bigger than a star and much brighter. It was a white light with no blinking or flashes - just steady - and no other colors observed. It moved at a steady pace leaving no trail - was not a comet or satellite. It was completely silent but seemed to be close. Got on UFO tracker this evening and saw 2 other reports tonight of the same type of light.
