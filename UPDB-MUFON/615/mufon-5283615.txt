Hovering box shape

Seen what looked to be the shape of a box kite lighter colored on the ends with a darker midsection. Came out the bottom of a white low lying cloud. Big flash of light when it popped down. Bounced a little and hovered. Rotated to about the 2 o'clock position and moved off southeast. Another person at the same location seen it. Asked me what it was. I didn't get his name.. It Made a crackling static kind of noise. The altitude was about 1000 feet I'm guessing. And for the size of the object I'd have to say it was probably 100 feet tall and 40 feet wide? Rectangular box shaped.
