{dr}Saw three orb-like lights several miles high, in a triangle, moving simultaneouosly. I faded back and out of my sight after about 5 seconds.

Getting out of my car, approximately 10:30, around December 7 or 8th, I looked up to the sky, as I am wont to do in the evenings. I looked SE into the sky, from where I was standing relative to my house and saw three lights that all seemed to move counter clockwise from my vantage point. With each apparent revolution, it faded further and further away. Took all of five seconds. It was not the first time I have seen strange things up in the air. While I was excited, it did not alter my perception or view of reality. Theres life out there.
