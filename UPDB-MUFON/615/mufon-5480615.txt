{jpg} Object came across my line of sight while ascending at a 45 degree angle.

I was travelling southbound on I 5 at Fort Lewis Main Gate Exit. I was looking forward when this Sphere shaped object came across my field of view. I observed this object for about 5-6 full seconds. I came from over the Sound and crossed over Fort Lewis Main Post. The object ascended at a 45 degree angle and was out of sight in a flash. The object emitted a steady glow and was sort of Bullet Shaped like a 7.62 Boat Tail but no flat end it tapered across the length like a dinner plate being turned upside down. The weather was clear blue sky a few clouds. This happened at 1141hrs PST.
