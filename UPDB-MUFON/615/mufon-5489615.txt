my sister and I observed a silver UFO in our back garden 1962

My sister and I were playing in our back garden. I was 7 years old and my sister was 3 1/2 year old at that time. It was a beautiful sunny hot afternoon. Suddenly a silver object descending into our garden and it came down so low that we could see the occupants inside. 1 was male and the other was female. They both had blonde straight hair. My sister and I became very frightened because we had never seen anything like this before. We ran screaming into our back door looking back where the object hovered as low as our kitchen window. The occupants looked at each other with a confused expression and hovered for a few minutes before ascending into the sky! My sister still remembers the incident and I think about it almost everyday.
