Silver or white disk or oval seemingly stationary but it was actually moving very slowly to the northeast.

I saw the object from my back patio. My wife saw it too and said it was a plastic grocery bag. I kept observing and it didn't behave like a bag blowing in the wind. It didn't change shape or move like it was being blown in the wind. The wind was blowing at that time fairly hard. I kept observing and I discovered that it was moving extremely slowly across the sky to the northeast. I went to the neighbor and borrowed some binoculars. I was able to get a good look at it and it was disk shaped, either circular or oval depending on the angle. I continued to watch it move slowly to the northeast until it disappeared from sight. I have digital video and digital photos I took with my phone but I couldn't download it here.
