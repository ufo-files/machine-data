{dr}I saw a small white light swirling in the sky with rainbow beams coming out.

August 12th 2016 was probably one of the scariest and most fascinating days of my life. It was the day I saw something unexplainable, unnatural, and utterly amazing. It all started at around 11 o'clock that night, when I was laying in my bed, unable to sleep. I had been tossing and turning since 10, because my house was so hot I couldn't sleep. I had a fan on the right side of my bed, but thought I would be cooler if I had one in my right side, too. So I went into my sisters room to get her extra fan she keeps in between her bed and nightstand. I walked in her room and went to get the fan, but stopped when I saw a tiny white light out of her south-west facing window. Coming up from behind the horizon was a tiny white light, a little bit bigger than a star, but smaller than a planet. I knew from first glance that this was not a satellite or airplane. It was rising in an almost squiggly motion, and it was hitting each star on its way up. Almost like a connect the dots, although it left no trail. It kept getting closer, and as it came clearer into view, I saw two smaller white lights following the big white light on either side, and together the formed a triangle. The figures were now almost overhead at treetop level, and all of a sudden, two small rainbow rays of light shown out from either side of the main figure. It then sped up and disappeared above my house. I have absolutely no idea what this could have been. I was too astonished to remember to take any pictures/videos, but believe me, I am SO mad at myself that I didn't. I would do anything just to see it one more time. Whatever it was, it was very close to landing on earth.
