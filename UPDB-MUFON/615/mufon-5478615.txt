I went on a Russian airplane flight from Moscow to Athens and sat in window seat with my mother next to me. 30 minutes before landing in Greece I see strange looking land formations. Looks like mountains have been cut smooth like sword. Crop looking circle formations. Land looks beautiful but land being messed with like I have never seen. 5-10 minutes later I see a long thin like stick that is thin, and so long change directions when plane flies by. My mom also saw this and has never believed in UFOs. A minute or so later it looked like it was coming towards the plane. I was scared and my mother also but I tried to emit positive thoughts of we are ok and it won't hurt us. It stared to move into area airplane has passed and hovered over gigantic looking mountains that peaked far above the clouds for a minute or so and then I lost view. Mountains made me think of mount Olympus. Very scary, divine not sure, but we got to Athens safely.
