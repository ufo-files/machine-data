Large Black Triangle

I saw a huge Black Triangle heading West. Although there were planes in the sky this was at least 10 times the size. The speed was incredible but there was no sound. I only had about 5 seconds of sight before it disappeared over the horizon. There were no lights visible. I had no time to get a picture on my phone.
