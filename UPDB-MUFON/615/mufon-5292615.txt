Green colored lights moving vertically and horizontally

On July 22, 2020 at 11:30pm thunder storms move from north to south over Long NY. We observed what appeared to be a pulsation and rhythm to the lighting. We first observed this in our backyard and then to the front yard to film the lightning. While my daughter was filming lights were seen on the video that could not be seen with the naked eye. From 17-23 seconds into the video there appears to be some sort of orb light that emerges from the direction of the storm. This light was not visible until the video is slowed down by frame. My daughter then changes the angle of the video to a more westerly direction and immediately two greenish colored lights appear moving vertically and horizontally. A third light then appears. As she continued to video the third light becomes more visible and the three lights appear to be connected. Ascend descending moving vertically and horizontally together. The lights could not visually be seen by eye. The lights continued to be seen with lightning strikes in the background.
