Spotted and recorded a vessel in the sky flying very low it appeared invisible

Traveling on I45 at 1097 I spotted two very bright silver dashes in the sky. I turned on my phone to video and observed the two lines are in exact synchronization with each other. A third one appeared but angles in a differing direction. There were also spinning lights much higher but I later realized they were attached I just couldn’t see anything in the middle. It was invisible or blurred by clouds. After flying at a fast pace it ended up over lake Conroe and dead stopped on the sky over the lake and never moved. I lost sight while driving after this.
