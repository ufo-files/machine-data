{ck} Red Glowing Orb

I was leaving my cousins house after watching the fireworks when my cousin pointed an unusual object that glowed red/orange in the sky. It was nothing Ive ever seen, being in new York tons of planes and helicopters are in the sky at all times, but this was lower and was not showing any signs of man made human aircraft. The object moved in a northerly direction and became smaller and smaller. A few times it stopped and hovered. It became too far and too low to see where it moved to. Everyone was breathless. There were weird lightningstorms to to the east that were contained in the clouds. The object was slow and basketball size.
