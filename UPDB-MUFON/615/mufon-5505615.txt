I was not feeling well so I was in bed. My son "E" came up at 11:50 to see if I wanted to go down to watch the ball drop.

I told him no and started to look out the window to the east as two planes were coming towards us to go to Long Beach airport to land.

saw this red star. I thought to myself that I never saw mars look so red before. Then I said to myself "that can not be mars because

it is not that red!" I looked to the left for 5 seconds and then looked back and it was gone. That is when I realized that it was a moving

object. Even so, Unless it is very close to me, who the heck knows what it is.... But at least I

can finally say that I now have seen something strange after all these years of looking up in the sky :)
