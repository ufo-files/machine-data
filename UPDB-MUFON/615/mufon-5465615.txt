{dr}Large flying object with 2 large red tail lights pulsating rhythmically and a periodic small white flash on right side of object

At just before 10 pm, I had just gone to bed in my house in Bellevue when I noticed a lit object hovering high above my neighbors house to the west..it was lit up with yellowish lights and moved north out of the view from my bedroom window. I thought it was a drone and was annoyed. I left my room and went to front of my house to see it moving off toward northwest...I thought it was a plane except for the relatively large red double tail lights that appeared to pulsate in a steady manner and sequenced with a small white flashing light on right side of craft. There were no green lights. I was watching the back and right side of the object flying away from me. Again 2 huge red tail lights...pattern was glow brighter red L, then red R, then quick small bright, white flash on right side of flying object. It was very quiet and could have been a plane with strange light configuration but I've never observed anything like this before. It flew off beyond sight. It flew behind trees but could have been miles away. What planes have 2 huge red tail lights that pulsate?
