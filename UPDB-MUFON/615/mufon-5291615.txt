Lights dancing in sky then blinked out

On July 31, 2020 at approx 2230 the witness observed light come into view similar to a shooting star. With 30 seconds of stopping, the light then broke into several lights (see video) and "danced" in what appeared in the same spot. The lights scattered at one point but could still be seen through some trees in the area. The lights then formed back up and eventually blinked out after approx 30 minutes. The same thing happened on August 24, 2020 at about 2230 as well. The only difference is that during the Augusts 24th sighting the lights did not scatter.
