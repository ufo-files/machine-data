Thought it was a solid light but changed shape.

Were sat on a beach with my partner when we saw what we thought was a helicopter taking off and flying over the sky. It was moving a bit fast and then we noticed it wasn't making any sound so took a few photos,. When we got home and looked at the photos we had a bit of a shock as it seemed to be a line of fire that changed shape. The object just seemed to fly over us then off into the distant.
