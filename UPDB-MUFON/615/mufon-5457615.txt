I was driving back from dinner with my daughter. I noticed a very bright light in the sky. It looked huge compared to the few stars that were in the sky. It traveled from right to left in the sky then stopped. It stayed stationary for about 3 minutes then disappeared. I said what was that and my daughter said she was thinking the same thing what the hell was that. I had no idea what it was nothing I could explain away as a airplane it was way too big.
