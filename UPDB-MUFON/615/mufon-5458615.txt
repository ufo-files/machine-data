{tjd} Saw 6 white lights drop out of the sky, hover and flash lights for 3 or 4 rounds and then take off at a great speed

I saw what I believe to be a UFO at approx 0330 PST on Nov 17th 2018 in San Diego, CA. I was driving south on the I-15 near the Aero Drive exit, when I saw a triangular/Chevron shaped aircraft drop out of the sky to my left about a quarter or half mile to my left and definitely between 400 and 500 ft above the ground. It hovered for a couple seconds. It's 6 or 7 round bright white lights on its undercarriage flashed sequentially from front to back. I did a double take when I saw it drop and hover, but saw it long enough for the light pattern to repeat 3 or 4 times. And then it took off south with a speed that I don't think any jet could match. The lights trailed behind and then blurred together for a split second and then it was gone. My passengers were all sleeping, but I asked if anyone had seen what I had witnessed, but I was alone in my sighting.

I remember I had just checked my side mirror when a saw the light fall out of the sky at great speed. I thought it might have been a firework but then it just sat there and was too bright and the lights started to flash.
