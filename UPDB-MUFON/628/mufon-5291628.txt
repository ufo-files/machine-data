Looks like a pair of jet contrails

My friend and I were driving home from Hastings Michigan last month when we saw two pairs of what looked like contrails. This was an evening, probably 6:30. Still very bright outside. we thought they were jets, but resembled more of a comet trail not a contrail. 
Then, on another occasion, as I was returning from work one afternoon around 2:30 pm alone, I photographed another of these contrail/comet tail such events. I really thought nothing much of either event until I saw a Las Vegas area (I believe) report of something almost identical.
