3 UFO in vertical formation flashing orange yellow and green lights going up and down departed at a high speed.

In 1961 during June on a moonless and cloudless night me and my farther were hunting rabbits on a farm in die district of Molteno in the NE Cape South Africa. He informed me that he would like to show me something that he had observed on previous night. 

In a NW direction I observed 3 UFO's in formation flashing orange,yellow and green lights. The UFO's lights were moving up and down. This was observed for +- 5minutes. The at a high velocity speed the UFO's ascended straight up and disappeared. My father was a S.A. Police Station Commander at MOlteno. He reported the incident to the Bloemfontein Observatory. They informed him that he dad possibily observed US meteorlogical weather ballon.

Through the years I couldn't accept this explantion. The altitude height of the weather ballon is to low to allow sunlight refletion to be seen on a moonless night.
