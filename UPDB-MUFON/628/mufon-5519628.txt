UFO extremely bright stationary for 15 minutes next to communication towers

MY husband and I were getting ready for bed about 11:20 and he noticed this extremely bright object above the ridge between Amenia and Millerton NY, and just to the left of the communication. We got out binoculars and a camera and attempted to take photos. We are at 1200 feet above sea level and have a panoramic 180 degree sky view facing NW. We watched this object with pulsing colors of white, blue, yellow and red for 15 minutes. Judging by the stars that were out it was huge. We cant say how far it actually was as we can see a long dstance from our house. After 15 minutes it just suddenly disappeared.
