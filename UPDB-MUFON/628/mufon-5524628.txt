I was driving east bound on Telegraph Canyon road at approximately 8:00pm (August 26th 2011) and then turned left (NE) on Paseo Ladera when directly up in the sky I viewed two lights heading north east with no blinking lights only two constant white lights (side by side about 20 to 30 feet apart, and as I came up to park at home it had traveled another two to three miles north east and then I lost it as it went past my line of sight and dipped beyond the horizon line... It stood out because it was flying perpendicular to the normal flight paths going in and out of Lindbergh field...
