UFO observed in Eastern PA

My friend and I were traveling south on 512 from wind gap to morrestown and then to Upper Nazareth twp. While driving i had Bill take a photo of what looked like a Cresent shape in the sky, i proceeded to drop him off at residence and left. Approached rt 248 off of New England drive and took more photos at the intersection. I then proceeded to drive to my home and get a digital camera to take higher resolution etc. Before ending that I took one video which has the last timestamp of the sighting. The first photos are from an HTC inspire 8mp camera/cell phone and the photos after that are from a Kodak digital camera with 40x digital zoom. The last two are again from cell phone a day later on October 31st, 2011.
