We went to bed and were reading. we heard a strange humming sound. I asked my husband [a Vietnam Veteran] if it was a helicopter. He saidnone that I have ever heard Then a bright light lit up the room. I looked out the window and the yard was very bright. I then got up and looked out the other windows and realized that the yard all around the house was lit up brightly. I went to the front door and looked out,I looked up and it seemed the whole sky was pitch black, as if something were blocking it. I went back upstairs and looked out the window again,then the light went out abruptly,the noise stopped and it was as if it had never been there. I called the FFA the next day and asked if they had drug planes or any planes in the area. He said NO,then said Could it have been a UFO. I said pardon me and he said OH Never mind. Then he hung up!
