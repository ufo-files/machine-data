A red glowing orb that emits other orbs

On a midnight bicycle ride with husband. Traveling SW on our route home. I am on my bicycle behind my husband when, as we crossed a street, I observed a very bright glowing red light. I immediately slow and through the tree branches I notice 2-3 red orbs be emitted from the light. The main light is traveling horizontally and the orbs that are being emitted are coming out horizontally and flash for a second before disappearing. I quickly call for my husbands attention and pedal to the other side of the street for an unobstructed view. From this vantage point the light glows and pulsates red and emits one final orb. It continues to silently travel N and dim and brighten until it finally blinks out after approximately one minute and 30 seconds. During the observation of this object I also notice that when I squint my eyes it appears there are red lights going down the sides in a chevron style from where a main solid object should be.
