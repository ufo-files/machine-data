Looking at stars as I have for over 50 years with a perfectly clear sky. I noticed three white spheres heading rapidly deom NW to SE, Used telephoto lens to see lights and no navigation lights visible and no contrails. Objects copntinued with 14 more in trail an-t variable distances but in same line of travel. All were moving at speeds I have previously observed on satellites and looked as though they were illuminated by the soon to rise sun. After @ 15 minutes the entire goup had travelled SE and faded as satellities do. 

I have never, in 50 + years of plane and star watching, witnessed such a thing. It was very eerie. NO sounds or contrails at all and it was very quiet outside. I was unable to keep up with them to take a photo or movie.
