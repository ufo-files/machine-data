I witnessed a UFO appear one night driving in my car towards Smith Dr. approaching S Lee Hwy in Cleveland, TN leaving my apartment. A round/circular object with a bright green glow that looked like fire or flames burning around it appeared. It gave off ominous vibrations, along with every other sighting or encounter similarly that I have ever experienced. It triggered traumatic events from an abduction experience I had when I was younger relating to PTS from PAS (Post Abduction Syndrome) and caused me to feel startled and alarmed with a little bit of anxiety. It hovered and pulsated until it looked to have taken off into another dimension at hyper speed or faster than light speed (FTL speed) with a green trail still resembling flames around it. It took off heading west/northwest and it did not reappear.
