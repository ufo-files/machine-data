{dr}Stopped to take a picture of Whiskey Pete's sign. It appeared and I snapped the photo.

I was traveling northbound on I-15 towards Las Vegas. A friend told me about her family's connection to Whiskey Pete's Casino, so I stopped long enough to take a picture of the place and the sign. As I was getting ready to drive again, I saw the bright light moving quickly to the north (away from the sign), parallel to that mountain range. It appeared to descend for a split second, then went slightly back up, reversed its direction (back towards the sign)and flashed brightly and disappeared. I have no idea what it was, but it was amazing. Enough for me to want to report it to you. That is all the information I have. Thank you for taking time to consider what I have submitted.
