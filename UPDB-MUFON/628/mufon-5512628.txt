I missed an opportunity to film it front on!

It was a windless evening, I was taking my two sons, aged 2.5 and 5.5 to their grandparents house. The youngest was going slow, holding onto a fence with both hands imagining he was on some adventure; I guess. So I told him to look at the plane to distract him. I noticed the plane was quite unusual immediately but was more concerned about my sons well-being. I called to elder, as he was getting some distance from me to look at the plane too; so as to gather us together. He remarked that the plane was shooting. I laughed and said its not shooting, but that it was unusual. 
A few weeks earlier I had told him about the difference between the speed of light and sound like, as we frequently observe military jets, propelled craft and high altitude commercial aircraft. I told him to wait for the sound to come. We waited, and he said "Its shooting. I told him it wasnt. It was just the lights flashing. Thats when he said it was a UFO, and I suddenly realised it had passed, and there still wasnt any sound to account for it. I then proceeded to take out my phone, get the camera selected and take some video; resulting in the back end video which I have. 
Its from a Samsung Galaxy S 2LTzE, so it is in 1080 but I feel like a prize banana for not realizing sooner when I could have got a frontal and passing shot.
