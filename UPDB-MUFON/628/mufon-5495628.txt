{nd} steel ball that just got seviced, went over the house behind the one i was working at.

Was working in o`follin il . Was breaking for lunch about 11 am to beat the rush at 12. Got to the truck and turned around and seen this steel ball about 30 ft around coming from the north about 50 ft above the trees and went rite over the house behind the one I was working at. It was doing about 40 mph and heading toward Scott afb. I could of shot it with my old m16 I had in the army with iron sites easily. Blue skies no clouds. What made it even crazier was it was just serviced. 3 quarters of it was just wired wheeled. Shiny. The lower left of it was still rusty. No noise ,no intake. Then went out of sight to the south over the trees. I was in awe.
