Many Lights traveling across the sky

On Aug 1, 2020 at approximately 23:00 the sky was clear with no clouds in sight. Suddenly to the North West just above the tree tops. It was I don’t know what else to call it but an orb like object on a dimmer switch slowly started fading into the sky getting brighter and brighter until it was incredibly bright then slowly it began to get dimmer and dimmer and finally disappeared. 10 min later there were incredibly bright white lights moving fairly quickly across the sky starting from the northwest heading in the south east direction. It then slowed right down and stayed at this speed for about 10 seconds. It then just disappeared. It didn’t make any noise and was definitely not an aircraft. On Aug 28th at approximately 22:00 I noticed a light moving across the sky at a great spread. It was moving from the roughly 244 degrees south west heading 65 degrees North East. One would fly by ever 30-50 seconds and this lasted around an hour. The same thing happened on Aug 29th around the same time. I though it might be starllink satellites but it looks like there was not a launch on either of these nights.
