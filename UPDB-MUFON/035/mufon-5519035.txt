Bright star hovering near Big Dipper.

I was driving home south on I-435 in Kansas when i saw a bright star, that did not look familiar in the sky, near the Big Dipper. Ive been watching the night sky a lot recently for multiple reasons, The ancient alien theory intrigues me and i think i have figured out what it all means and i do believe that they will return in 2012. So ive been looking up since the new year. I observed the light as bright but distant, further south. and as i drove near home the light, though stationary, became negligibly bigger. i arrived home and went to my room and looked outside. it was still there i got on google and searched images of the big dipper to see if i could explain what id seen.. but i couldnt. So i turned back out the window and then the star was gone.
