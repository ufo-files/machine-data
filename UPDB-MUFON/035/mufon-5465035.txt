{cak} Orb approached jet on landing path to ft Myers airport followed for 1 mile and then backed off and flew off

I went outside to check the temp and heard a jet (a private jet) overhead. We are under the landing approach to Ft Myers international Airport. Planes are around 4,000 feet over our house. I just happened to glance up as I heard it directly overhead and then saw a small white object which I would guess the size of a beachball or basketball. It approached at about a 45 degree angle from the planes right rear at a speed faster than the plane and then took up a position in an area that would be considered a blind spot in a car. It maintained this position for about a mile and then fell back and passed behind the plane from right to left. I watched it for another 10 seconds and then it either winked out or flew beyond visible range. Its movements were very precise with instant directional changes.
