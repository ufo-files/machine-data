I was leaving home to pick up son across town and was running late. As I was pulling out of circular driveway I spot an object hovering over the rooftop of my neighbor across the street but far away. It caught my eye because it looked like it had blue rotating or pulsing lights and it was just kind of hanging up in the sky. I knew that it could not be an airplane because the lights were blueish. I almost immediately said to myself that it must be a UFO. I couldn’t move out of fascination and fear. I waited to see if it would take off. Then I pulled out my phone and videoed it for a few seconds and decided to call my husband Incase anything happened to me he would know. I could see it much better than what my phone was able to capture. I stayed watching it for 15-20 mins. I was late to pick up my son and had to go. I kept hoping it would go but it just stayed there. It was not a star. Disappointed, I had to go. As I pulled out of driveway I kept an eye on it as I was pulling away. All of the sudden it went straight down. Not like a helicopter but very fast.
