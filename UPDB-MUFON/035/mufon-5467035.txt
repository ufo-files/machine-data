Bright star above moon, moved from nw-s, 3 Other stars higher up, triangular shape Sw, FarLeftOne moved upwards.

Standing in back garden, 11.22pm, saw bight light in Nw direction getting closer, then moved leftwards towards South. No suden moves, steady movement dipping abit heading south over the roof tops.

The object was very bright, my initial thoughts where the brightness, oddness, not the usual flight path, shape, brightness of airplaines, or such, not just a star but with possible bits jutting out ever so slightly to the left and right, but overly bright.

At the same time I was watching the bright star disapear over the rooftops (heading south), I looked up and watched as a group of triangular shaped stars began to move (high up in the sky, W-S). The top left star moved upwards making the triangle a differenet shape.

I saw a similar star about a month ago that vanished into the night sky up past siris A. I went into the house, contacted mufon field investigator (email), took a pen, paper and went out to the back garden and drew the night sky drawing it as it was. When I did go outside again, (11.36PM)only one star remained from the triangle of stars in the night sky, the top right one, the sky was very clear, the moon bright, beautiful night.

Very clear sky, very warm, humid, bright moon.
