{cak} Hundreds of blue glowing star like orbs

My neighbor was yelling my name from across the street in a panic, first thoughts was that he needs help, so I ran outside and was in a complete shock as to what I saw. There were hundreds of moving bright bluish white lights spread over the entire sky, slowly moving as a whole. The spread was really hard to calculate but it was at least 40-50 city blocks long. After realizing that this was real I ran and got my wife and kids to come see by this time there were a few cars pulled over and the neighbors started to come out and look up in shock. We all just watched in disbelief as they moved away as a squadron like formation and got higher and harder to see until they were completely gone. My neighbor had his camera and showed me what he got, it was a bit blury, hand held sky photos are always blurred abit, but you can get an idea of what we saw. The photo is just a quarter or less of what we all saw spread across the sky., then I went in and grabbed my camera and tripod to try to get something cleaner but by this time the orbs were flying in every direction getting duller and harder to see, I managed to capture some very strange stuff in the pics attached.
