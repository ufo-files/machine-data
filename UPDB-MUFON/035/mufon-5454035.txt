Was stood at kitchen window 10 minutes past midnight saw a circular silver & pinky/orange Orb fallingor flying down from sky

It was the night of the Pink Supermoon,7th going into 8th April 2020.I had been taking photos of the Moon until about midnight.Went to the kitchen sink to get glass of water after putting camera away,it was a very bright night due to the Full Moon & the garden & sky were well lit.As I stood drinking looking out of the window some movement in the sky made me look up...I observed a Circular object falling or flying downwards from high up in the sky ,it came down straight to start with & then fell into a sweeping curve.It travelled very fast & as it curved it seemed to go below & behind the roof of a house that is behind my house & to the left.Next to the roof of that house are a lot of trees & I just could not see where the Circular shaped object went after that.It looked like it came down very low almost as if to land,but I cannot confirm that as I could not see clearly.It may well have gone back up out of my line of vision.I was a bit stunned & shocked because I could not work out what the object was.It looked to be silvery white around the perimeter but the middle part looked very orangey/pink & looked like it stood out ie was not flat,& looked translucent or semi transparent.How it looked to me was almost like an orange jewel/stone that was shaped & set into the silvery white outer edge.Sorry I know that this sounds a bit confusing,it was very bright & sparkly looking.It was unusual enough for me to be quite shocked & wondering what on earth it was.I did not have time to take a photo of it sadly as it happened so fast.I am wondering if the bright orange middle of the object could have been lit by lights on the inside,as it reminded me a bit of a car headlight.I did hear in the morning of a few other similar sightings via social media,in other parts of the UK around the same time.
