Bright ball of light coming west to east

I walked outside about ten minutes to seven, and observed a bright ball of light coming from the western sky towards my house. Very low flying, quiet with no noise, I grabbed my video camera. I taped it first from the west, from the back of my house and followed the ball to the east side of my house. It was moving very slow to the east for seven to ten minutes, heading towards Boise from Caldwell, Idaho.
