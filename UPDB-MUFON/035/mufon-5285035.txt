fast moving changing formation of lights

while putting recycling out at approx 10.30pm i looked up at the night sky ,within about 30 seconds a formation of what appeared to be approx 7 lights in a large wedge shape was travelling across the night sky at a steady pace,then rapidly changing speed and shape into a dance of up to 15 lights in a spiral shape revolving around a central axis ,i ran to get my phone and called my wife to come and see, by the time i got back they had gone but my wife assured me she saw them form into a tight wedge shape and move at an incredible speed over the eastern horizon
