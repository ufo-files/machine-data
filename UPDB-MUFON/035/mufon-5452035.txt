Dark craft with a very patterned underside no lights no sound

I was 16 taking out the trash and above my head was a dark craft slowly moving west not making a sound. I was close enough to hit it with a baseball. I lived about a mile south of Lambert Airport but I had never seen anything like it before or since. What amazed me the most is that It didn't make a sound. I only saw it for about 5-7 seconds as it flew out of site. It was really low like below radar low. I'm reporting it now with the encouragement of my mother at the time I didn't think anyone would believe me but she always did.
