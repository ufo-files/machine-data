My wife and friends and I went camping in a secluded state park in West Virginia called Blackwater falls.. It was on July 28 at around midnight. We were stargazIng Because there was a meteor shower that night. We noticed a small star like object it began to move to left to the right sporadically And then it darted off and disappeared. About a minute later we watched a triangle type object form in the sky and begin traveling horizontally. It had white lights on each corner of the triangle that we’re somewhat dim and seem to flash slowly. Our phones could not pick up The whites of the phenomena we were witnessing. We watched it move across the sky from west to north west and it disappeared. It passed by stars and the stars disappeared as it went in front of it. We were all astonished to say the least.
