I was sitting on my patio and heard a noise like an airplane but not like an airplane at the same time. It was like a big triangle and it had three lights, one on each point, then I called my brother outside and he agreed that it wasn't an airplane, so we watched it for almost an hour. It sort of looked like when police use helicopters to search for people and they try to stay in one area but it wasn't searching for anyone, I don't think. It would fly around for a few minutes and go in all different directions then it'd just stop then start flying again. At one point, we'd thought that it left because we didn't see it anymore so my brother went back in the house but I waited for it to show up again, it came back a few minutes later but it just flew around for a couple of minutes and flew away. When it first showed up, it was still pretty light out so I got a pretty good look at the shape of it, but it ended up getting dark and we were only able to see the lights the rest of the time. I tried getting a picture of it but it kept coming out blurry on my phone's camera.
