{nd} Lights of various colors flashing, looked blimp size, but moving too fast to be a blimp,

I was out in yard with my dogs, looked up at the sky as I often do to enjoy the night sky and was startled to see lights, could not make out an outline or shape of the object, but looked like lights on the side of a blimp, flashing different colors, but going too fast to be a blimp. I live near Angel stadium and I see blimps often, but this was not a blimp. These were not typical blimp advertising lights, they appeared to be navigation type lights. I watched as it moved east. to see if it looked more like an airplane at a different angle, but it did not ever look like an airplane. In fact there were several airplanes and a helicopter in the sky, and it looked nothing like them. As it got farther away, I must have been looking at the rear of the object, because I saw two inverted arrow shapes, points up, side by side, all white lights. I don't know what kind of craft has arrow shaped white lights. I watched it continue east until it went below the horizon and I could no longer see it. It never did anything strange, it was just unusual looking.
