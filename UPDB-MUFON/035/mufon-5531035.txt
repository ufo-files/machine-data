Bright light craft!

I parked here at home then got outta the car, and I immediately saw this bright white light, and it was maybe about four to five thousand feet above us (VERY close!).1 It was closer than a plane, and it was NOT moving like a plane; it was weaving a little bit, side to side, as it flew slowly over top of us.

I grabbed the binoculars and followed it for a few seconds, seeing a sphere with all this glowing light coming out of it.1 When I went for the camera, though, it slowly, slowly fizzled out to nothing - despite my pleading aloud for it to stay.1 (I guess it was somebody who didn't want their picture taken!1 ;))

Anyway, I saw a gray craft last September, and I just knew intuitively that it was Arcturians.1 This time, though, I didn't get a feeling about WHO it was, but I know they were good and loving.
