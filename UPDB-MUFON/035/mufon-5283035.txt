Pure bright white 40-50ft cylinder hovering for several minutes defined all know laws of physics

Swimming and surfing in the sea of the south west coast of Scotland (Luce Bay) with my daughter we spotted a pure brilliant white 40-50ft cylinder sitting in the sky. It was further out from our position. We watched for several minutes. It had no wings, no rotters and it did not move at all in wind or even in its position. We were in awe of whatever it was we were watching Like a flick of a switch after few minutes it was gone..my daughter's shouting it's gone in the sea repeatedly and pointing. We had been watching the large NATO exercise that was underway near RAF west freugh over the week so had been watching the sky or we probably wouldn't have seen it. There was no noise what so ever by what was powering this cylinder. It's white colour was so bright I will never forget it. Whiter than white. It was a blue sky day and great weather. Nothing else in the sky.
