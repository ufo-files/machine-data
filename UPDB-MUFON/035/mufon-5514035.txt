disc shape with dome on top with windows large enough to see head and shoulders

hapstead house,buckfastleigh,UK.In 1978 approx 8.30 pm it was dark and I had to check the grounds of the house.going out the back door of the house I took several steps and there right in front of me partly sheltered by a hedge approx 100 to 120 ft away was this disc shaped craft with a dome on top that had windows which clearly showed 2 types of human like figures inside,I saw approx 4 to 5 beings.the craft had already landed and was not making any noise and I stood frozen not understanding why they were'nt looking at me as I could see them so clearly.I remember running back inside being late to get to bed and not being able to talk for a couple of days.I was missing a period of time that at the time was not comprehendable and so never told anyone for about 10 years
