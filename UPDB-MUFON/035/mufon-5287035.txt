Bright white baseball sized object

I was sitting on front porch which faces West around 4:40 am, way above tree tops from the next street over, a baseball sized bright white object came down rather quickly to just above neighbors house peak, then dropped down between house and garage, leveling off to about 15 feet. It pulsated at regular intervals the entire time. It then traveled approximately 25 to 30 feet, then disappeared about 5 feet outside neighbor's home, in an area where I had witnessed a black shaped animal looking entity the year before. That incident scared me, the entity appeared from this spot, ran the length of the house, and then disappeared. The entity was like a tall cat, the back legs were way higher than the front,about 6 feet long, 4 feet high, with an elongated head. I saw no ears or eyes or mouth, just this long head. It was solid black. I never reported this for fear no one would believe me. Also, many years ago, the neighbor to the right of this report, had 2 beams of light appear on his house and garage. I Don't remember the year, but I did report this incident.
