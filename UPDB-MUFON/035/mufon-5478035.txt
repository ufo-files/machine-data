4 small craft joined large light then large light shot off into distance

I was camping on a lake 150 miles north of Schefferville, Quebec. It was perfectly clear starlit night. I noticed a very bright what I thought was a star and 4 smaller lights. One by one the smaller lights very quickly joined the bright light and then the bright object zoomed out of sight
