{cak} 8 still like objects in the sky

THERE WERE ABOUT 5 OF US OUTSIDE TALKING , FROM THE CORNER OF OUR EYES THE SKY APPEARED WITH 8 OBJECTS THAT LOOKED LIKE STARS. THE SUN WAS BARLEY GOING DOWN SO IT COULDN'T BE STARS. AND IF IT WAS AN AIRPLANE IT WOULD HAVE BLINKING LIGHTS ON THEM. THESE OBJECTS WERE BUNCHED UP AND ONE OF THEM HAPPENED TO TAKE OFF REALLY FAST. AS I LOOKED THROUGH THE BINOCULARS THE OBJECTS WERE BEAMING WITH LIGHT THAT TURNED REDDISH ORANGE.
