{rjl} Just after midnight saw a glowing orange light hovering then moving south west of me

Just after midnight in my San Diego neighborhood, I went outside to smoke. When I looked up, I noticed a glowing orange light, like a fireball. First I thought it might be a helicopter or plane, but the lights were all wrong. This was glowing orange and made no sound. It was first hovering then slowly floated from east to west. It wasn't a meteor or comet, it left no trail and moved too slow for that. I took a picture and some video of it. I've never seen anything like it before and I've seen plenty of aircraft and meteor showers to tell the difference. It freaked me out.
