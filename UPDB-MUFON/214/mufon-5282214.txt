Strange Tic Tac Shaped Glowing Object

I had just arrived home from a shift at work, and went out to check on our animals in the garden at 10pm. As I turned to go back inside the house, I heard a humking sound above me, and looking up watch a very strange tic tac shaped object moving slowly overhead. It was pulsating slightly and was approx 30ft to 40ft in length, but it had a kind of see through inner part. In this inner part I could make out a cube shape that was stationary. It proceeded to move NE to SW over my garden, and as it passed it made a slight right turn and dropped out of view behind some nearby trees. The speed of the object was quite slow.

I am ex military with over 10 years of fixed wing and rotary experience. I am now a commercial pilot flying fixed wing aircraft internationally. I am fully aware of all current military and civilian aircraft as well as UAVs and drones. I can categorically advise that I have never seen anything like this before, and I am quite clearly stumped as to what this was.

On doing some immediate research locally, it seems that I am not the only person in the area to have witnessed this UFO, as it has been seen on no more than 9 occasions since the start of 2022 in locality. I have spoken with local police who did advise that they have received some reports about this object.
