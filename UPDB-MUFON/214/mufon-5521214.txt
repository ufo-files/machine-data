26 objects appeared to put on a show for 25min then left without saying goodbye

Ok I and a friend had been exploring some mining sites in the ElPaso mtn in Kern county California, we had stopped at about 1630hrs to eat when I noticed a red light appear on hillside about 3/4 of a mile away but it went out and I did not think about. Once we finished eating I cleaned up the site put everything back into vehicle and prepared to leave, my friend wanted to stay awhile to see the stars, at about 20:15 the red light again appeared on the hillside but moving across the hill, when it went vertical over the crest of the hill top (5400) moved toward the center of a saddle and came to a hover.I new this was going to be interesting,a second object appeared from the direction of connecting hill and joined the first object in a hover. While my attention was on the two objects in front of me my friend told me to look at what was happening to my right, this is weird I am looking at 4 columns of 4 evenly spaced red lights that are blinking from top to bottom and from bottom to top and sequentially from my right to left with 2-6 bright white lights at the base moving in step with the columns. Now all this is going on there is no noise, and after 25min of this show everything closes down leaving just the first object which remained in a hover pulsed 3 times and disappeared!This not the end, This area is now part of the state park system and all trails are now marked like a freeway, my own experience in this area is 20years, but for some reason I could not find my way back to the highway which could be see from this location each time we tried we ended up back at the spot we had left, 5 times over 5 hours finally had to stop fuel was low and were not going anywhere! Next day we checked the area for any signs of foul play no sign of human playing games just the desert! How about that, this show was preformed for us at close range, like within a mile and no higher then 6000 ft. This was a first for me and I am impressed.
