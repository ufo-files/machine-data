{nd} It looked like a bright firefly flying in a straight line by the trees.

I was in the bathroom looking out the window. At first I thought a big bright firefly. But this is early April and just one. Then it went off and came back on very bright. This happened four times. Whatever it was it flew in straight path from right to left and it disappeared around the house.
