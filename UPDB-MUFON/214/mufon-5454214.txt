{tjd} It appeared as a spinning red thimble.

Approximately 11 pm March 31.
I spotted a red light behind a small aircraft. It first looked like the light of another aircraft close behind. I thought they would collide. The small plane flew off in a westerly direction leaving the red object clearly visible. It had what I would describe as a ' thimble' shape. It was bright red and appeared to spin.
After awhile it descended straight down. Just below rooftops. I thought it might have crashed but after 30 to 60 seconds it rose again to about its previous height. It then travelled in a northerly direction out of my range of sight.
I observed all this looking out my second floor window.
