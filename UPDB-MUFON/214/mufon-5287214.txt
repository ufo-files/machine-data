Several square glowing objects picked up by digital camera

I was riding as a car passenger with my brother and suddenly felt compelled to shoot a few pictures of the clouds ahead of us. I turned on my smart phone camera and took three pictures several seconds apart. When I looked at the pictures on my computer I could easily see several square objects with 4 lights each of their own. A dark circle is also in my pictures as well.
