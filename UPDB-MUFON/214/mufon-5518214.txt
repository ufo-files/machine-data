Glowing Hovering Object suddenly moves north and disappears

Around 8pm on 6/9/12 I noticed a hovering glowing light on the horizon towards the southwest. I thought it was Venus, but it was too bright, so I watched it. When it began to move I started filming it with iphone. When it moved out of sight I ran to my car and drove to a clearing. Within the two minutes it took me to drive to a clearing it was gone. I drove around the area, but saw zero indication of object. There were no flashing lights on object. Just a solid yellow orange glow. The sun was setting so it could have been reflecting off (maybe?) a metallic surface.
