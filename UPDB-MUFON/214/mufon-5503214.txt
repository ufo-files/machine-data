{rjl} Black triangle with many lights flew in a straight path over car I was in

I was with my friend at about 2 in the morning going from this other guys house back to my friend's place. We were on a dark road in a car I think somewhere in Amberly Village. I was just looking out of the windshield when I suddenly noticed the craft flying silently and quickly across the sky. My first thought was something along the lines of "Holy @#$% is that a UFO?" and I think I said that out loud haha. My friend noticed it next and was just as surprised and excited by it as I was. We watched as it flew across the sky before us at a pretty low altitude, not far above the trees. It was a black triangle with many lights on it. It happened too quickly to count how many lights there were but I remember that there were solid white lights at each tip. Not actually on the very tip, they were still under the craft.There were also a few white lights both solid and blinking in the center with a blinking red light in the center as well. I can't remember too well but I don't think the red dot was in the center, it was off to one side. We only saw the underside of the ship as it flew over us. It was totally silent from what I remember. There may have been a low hum (my memory isn't perfect) but if there was I don't remember it. It just flew right over us and disappeared behind the tree tops. It was a very exciting experience! But happened too quickly to get a picture unfortunately.
