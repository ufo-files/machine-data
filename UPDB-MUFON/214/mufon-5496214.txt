{nd} Three fast moving spheres

I was in my backyard looking for comet lovejoy. I saw what I first thought was a shooting star just west of the Pleiades moving north. It left a faint trail, but then I saw it continuing north. It was leaving more trail behind if but it was fading rapidly. The trail was thin and just barely visible. As I continued to watch, I could make out three greyish metallic spheres.They may have been flat instead of spherical, but because of the shading I got the impression of spheres. They were moving very quickly to the north but they arced to the northwest. I lost them as they passed just to the left of Cassiopeia. They were in a very tight group. The overall size was just smaller than the Pleiades. There may have been 4, but I was only certain of three. Their formation was not triangular...if there had been 4 it would have made a nice square. From start to finish they were visible about 4 seconds or so. They were absolutely silent and extremely high up. The time was plus or minus 2 minutes.
