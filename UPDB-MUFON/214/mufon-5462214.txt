{tjd} 2 self illuminated orbs hovering then ascending out of site

This isn't the first time I've seen this light. The first time happened approximately 9:00 pm Oct.14 2013. Understand that I smoke outside, not in the house. So I went out to light up a cig, when I saw a bright light coming from the east. Aircraft lights are not uncommon as I live half way between two military bases, Knox and Campbell, so I'm accustomed to seeing Blackhawks, Chinooks, and Apaches. This was different. The perfect sphere was traveling slower than other aircraft I usually see. The sphere was not emitting light but self illuminated with the whitest light I have ever seen. It got close enough that it was the size of an aspirin. There was no sound. Anything that size, I would have heard the whop of the rotors, or hum of the engine. It made a slow turn to the north. As it was moving away, a smaller red light dropped out of the bottom, hovered briefly and took off to the northeast. The larger light just maintained its course to the north until I lost sight of it behind the trees.
Three nights ago (Feb. 23, 2019), I went out for my final smoke of the day. I was facing east when I saw a very bright white/blue light slowly moving from north to south. As I watched it for about 5 minutes it came to a dead stop, For all intent and purpose, it was a star except I saw it move into place. Then it was just gone.
Last night(Feb. 25, 2019), I went out for my bedtime smoke and there it was. It wasn't moving, but had a partner. Very close together. From a distance it was just an oddly shaped white/blue light hovering. I put my cig down and went a got a pair of binoculars. All I could see was two perfect orbs of white/blue lights. They didn't move for several minutes, then they just started ascending straight up. I watched them till they were gone. 
I know people won't believe me, but I don't care. I know what I saw.
