{td} Me and two friends two lights very high left to right stopped faded out.

Me and two friends observed two white lights. In my estimation they where 70,000 feet high. About twice the height of a jet airplaine. The lights stayed the same distance apart as we watched them cross the sky for about 8 secounds. They then came to a complete stop and then in a second or maybe two they faded out as if they went up.
