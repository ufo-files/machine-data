{jpg} Bright light Descending down to trees

driving down Farmington road towards Beaverton in Aloha, Oregon. When my brother and i noticed a bright pink/white light descend from the clouds. At first we thought oh street light/ star but then it clicked that that was not a star! (there was low cloud cover due to rain) it got brighter and aligned with the road we where on. at that point my brother and i both where mesmerized on what we where seeing all at the same time being confused and scared on what we where looking at. deer in the head lights kind of way. i wanted to pull over but i kept driving . it seemed maybe about 1/2 mile ahead of us. when it slowly hovered toward our right and descended into the trees to where we lost vision of the object. at that point my brother and i looked at each other and looked around to see if anybody else had seen this bright object descend from the clouds?!?! nobody besides 1 car in front of us. as we approached the red light at 185th and Farmngton i rolled down he window and honked. Guy looked confused and looked so focused on the road all at the same time on what he saw. he quickly replied and said i saw that too and was so shocked that he didn't have time to film it like he wanted. whole night i was trying to piece the whole experience together. This was not a flare gun.getting shot up, satellite, fireworks or meteorite/fireball coming through the clouds if so we'd probably be all dead since it was huge/bright. Just stoked i can finally share my most recent 3rd experience after lurking on the website almost everyday.
