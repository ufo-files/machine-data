Multiple objects observed over Ada, OK

At 9:35PM, May 6, 2020, we first observed an armada of flying discs moving from the southwest to the northeast right over the town of Ada, Oklahoma. These objects generated no sound and flew in a straight lined pattern. The number of objects are unknown but by the time we viewed them, the number could be around 50. The night was clear, dark and no wind.
