negative object emptiness of light and form

7 people witnessed an event in Afghanistan near Bagram Air Base. It was a non military event involving an object from far away lasting about 15 minutes as it projected some type of energy onto a mountain side. It looked like the hand of god came out and touched the mountains but no sound. then in less than 30 minutes it was observed by 2 moving down the run way but not landing. Object appeared to be like a black hole. no noise or sound but was absorbing light. as the object went by it only had a minimal effect upon the air as it passed pallet wrap moved gently, but metal shipping containers jerked towards it as it passed by about less then ten feet from them. The base went to noise and light discipline just before the time of our original sighting by us. after the interaction on the runway 20 minutes or so, normal base lighting resumed. almost as if the control tower and base security knew and was aware of the object. there were no flights in the time period or sector south east of the runway. all aircraft were north west to north of Bagram. No TIC sit reps were filed for the sector all flight schedule operations after were normal with no sightings of the object.
