Star-like white light making semi-circles and 90 degree turns

I was going for a walk and looked at the sky. There was no cloud cover. I noticed what I initially thought was a star which I noticed was moving oddly. The light moved both in straight lines and semi circles and moved at 90 degree angles. I used my phone to record it, and I’ve attached the unedited video. In the video, the light moves in a semi circle. After that, it appears to disappear. However, when edited for clarity, you can see the light actually dims, as if going behind a cloud, although there were no visible or forecasted clouds. The light then continues, slightly dimmed. After I finished recording, the light returned to its previous brightness. I walked to a place I thought would provide a better view, but by that time I could no longer locate it in the sky.
