Glowing orange orb intelligently controlled

My friend and I were driving up Bully Choop mountain late at night around 2am, and stopped at a snow bank and continued walking. We were both remarking we could feel the mountain pulsing with this amazing energy. It felt amazing but is hard to describe. It just felt like we were on top of the world. All of a sudden we both stopped to look at the city lights of Redding far down in the valley below as we had done many times before (we often came up here after band practice). All of a sudden we saw what appeared to be a car headlight proceeding up a nearby ridge of Bully Choop to the SE of our location. It was bright orange/red in color, made no sound, did not pulsate, but appeared intelligently controlled. We were surprised because there was no road that we knew of there, but figured it could be an ATV or dirt bike. The light proceeded to take a turn directly toward us, thus leaving the ridge-line to hover in the open air. That's when we knew it wasn't a car or ATV because cars and atv's don't fly. It came right toward us. I freaked out and ran away, while my friend stayed and looked at it. After I ran out of sight, I ran back to see the bright orange/red spherical object slowly going away toward the west of Redding. It was crazy and to this day I have no idea what it was, but I know whatever it was wanted to chat, or at least say hi. 

Please keep in mind this was in 2010 and we didn't carry cell phones around everywhere, so didn't have them at the time. Even if we did the pictures would have been crap.
