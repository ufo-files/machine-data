A disc shape object was hovering over a cleared field

I was about 11 years old and staying at a friends house at her family’s farm and they had just cleared a field next to her house because they were building a new house (no construction had started yet). her bedroom faced the clearing and late one night her and I observed a disc shaped object hovering above the newly cleared field. It was spinning and had colored lights all around the sides. It was very large, probably a bit longer than a school bus but circular and flat. I do not remember anything after seeing it and I forgot that it happened for probably 20 years only remembering in my 30’s. When I remembered I reached out to the girl I had been with at the time and she said she also remembered it. That’s all. It was a farm, they may have had animals but I can’t remember for sure but it had been a dairy farm previously.
