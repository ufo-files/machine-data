Maneuverable Star like object in the sky

While surveying a closed off section of motorway on the M60 motorway Manchester, I was facing south when I noticed what I thought was a really bright star. I observed the object for a approx 1 minute and observed it making slight maneuvers. At first I thought it was trick of the eye however I noticed a star below the object and therefore I was able to track the movements from this stationary star. As I'am a Civil Engineer who was surveying at the time I used a dumpy level I had with me to zoom in on the object. I observed it ascend/descend and traverse slightly across the night sky. It did not appear to have any flashing lights as such but the shade of light did appear to dissipate as it made maneuvers. I continued to observe the object for approx 15 mins until I had to clear the motorway. When I left the object was still there. During this time I went through a process of elimination i.e it was not an aircraft,drone or star and concluded it was unidentifiable from my perspective.
