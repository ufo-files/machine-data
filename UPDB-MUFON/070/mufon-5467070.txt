{cak} the object was round and metallic. It was hovering just above the skyline on the Wellsville mountain.

In the early morning of April of 1997. I was sitting in my truck waiting to get in side building to do some sheet rock. I looked to the west of Logan towards the Northern end of the Wellsville mountain when i saw a metallic odject hovering about 300 ft above a ridgeline. Then the object slowly descended below the skyline. Which it disappeared for several minutes. Then it ascended very slowy to about 500 to 1000 ft above the ridgeline
The object just hovered there for several minutes. Then the object went straight up extremely fast and disappeared.
