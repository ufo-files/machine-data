{dr}my wife & I justwent to bed when we saw a Blue light

In 1989 we lived in a two story home with all bedrooms upstairs.we had just put our kids to bed. then we settled in our bed. I saw a Blue teardrop light on the wall of our bedroom, between the fireplace mantle & the bath door. I asked my wife what the heck and she saw it also. Now we lived on almost 2 acres with a partially paved/gravel street. At that time there were no houses to to south or east ofour house so I got up to look for the direction of the light . There no vechicles anywhere no noise infact silence. I turned to face my wife she was sound asleep.I walked to light and it wsa pulsating,I ran my finger across it, this is stupid but felt alive.THen I saw multi-colored lights from the top of the stairs.Then I woke it was daylight. For the nexttwo years the triangle object appeared above thetree line and smaller objects came from it and they circled the house. In all I the Big object and 3 small ones.
