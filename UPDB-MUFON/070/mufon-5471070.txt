Sat 24 images show fast moving object disturbing atmosphere on huge basis

I was checking weather conditions on Sat 24 on 8 Jan 2018 and was surprised to se a very fast and large moving object disturbing the atmosphere, causing large cloud formation and diaearing after object had passed. Disturbances covered a very large area and videoed the 15 min sequence update to the Sat 24 site..incredible to see this occurrence. I told all of my close friends to check out the occurrence while it was happening..so I have witnesses and footage. A must see!!!! As of writing the object still being seen over mid Atlantic still disturbing the atmosphere. Publication of video pending reproduction rights from Sat 24.
