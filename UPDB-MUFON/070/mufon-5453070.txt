A silent triangle shaped ufo flying above my car the changing direction multiple times

My wife and I were driving home when she asked me “what’s that?” I thought maybe a plane or helicopter or something but then once we got under it I stuck my head out the window and looked at it. It was less than 100 feet away. I could see it clearly. A dark gray or black triangle shaped vessel just cruising around. White red and blue lights alternately flashed.
