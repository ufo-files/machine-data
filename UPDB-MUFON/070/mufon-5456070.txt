{cak} Pulsating lights seen from commercial flight.

I was on flight VS7 which departed Heathrow on Friday 10th Jan. We flew over Canada and half way through the flight I saw some extremely weird lights down below. I found a picture online that resembles what I saw.

I was looking out of the left side of the plane when this happened. I looked down and saw long pulsating lights that kept turning on and off but it also looked like this was happening over water. Possibly a lake or a river.

During all of this I showed my wife the weird pulsating lights that looked like they were moving around a stream or lake turning on and off. The lights were very bright and was certainly not a reflection of anything. It was dark, almost day break when this all took place.

I know the approximate area where this took place as I had my sky map on the monitor. It took place somewhere around a town called Flin Flon/Thompson in Manitoba Canada.
