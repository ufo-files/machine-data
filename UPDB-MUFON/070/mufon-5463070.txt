{rjl} Boyfriend & I witnessed a triangle UFO fly almost directly over us about 50ft. slowly before it changed direction & asended to the sky.

My boyfriend & I were sitting out by the backyard firepit around 9:15pm when I saw what looked like a large swirling light or fireball slowly moving horizontally towards us over the eves of the house behind us. As I stood frozen & not wanting to take my eyes off of this thing, my boyfriend ran to the other side of our pool/firepit & away from any light source, that is where he saw the full outline of a triangle UFO. The "fireball" I was describing was actually the centerpiece underbelly light. He saw a whitish light on the points of the triangle also. The UFO was approx 50ft - 100ft above us. The UFO made zero sound! It moved slowly over us and when my boyfriend was in the house trying to find his phone to get a picture, that's when I watched the UFO go from a steady horizontal path to a sharp upward path and watched it fly away until it became a star in the sky.
