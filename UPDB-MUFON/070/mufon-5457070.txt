{cak} Saw a bright light in the sky. Didn’t move.

Family coming back from dinner at 5pm, got out of the car and caught a bright light in the sky about 40° East. (Same area where you would find Neptune) I was curious to see what it was but it never moved. Got out my kids small telescope to see if maybe it was a planet or star. It looked like a nebula, oval/cigar shaped orange/red color to a blue/white color. There was a long trail coming down from it. Reminded me of a jellyfish with one tentacle. As the sun went completely down the object disappeared but for the full 30-40 minutes it never moved from my point of view but it moved out of view in my telescope extremely slow. Slower than what stars and planets do in my scope.
