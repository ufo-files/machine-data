{cak} Flying from Chicago to Evansville, IN. Sitting at cruising altitude. I was taking a video of the clouds. Watching the film back I saw lights and flashing in the sky.

Left O'hare International airport flying to Evensville Indiana on 10/3/17. The weather was pretty clear. Once we were at cny cuising altitude I decided to take videos with my phone out the window of the plane. I assume we were anywhere between 25 to 35 thousand ft. I took the first video at at 6:24am. It was a 31 second video. I watched it and decided to take another. The second video was taken at 6:27am. A 24 second video. I was watching the clouds through my phone at the time. Once I watched the video back I noticed flashing lights coming from a light cloud. I showed my wife and we were kind of shocked and kept trying to justify the flashes. The other video showed no flashes. I watched the video frame by frame and did not see lightning or a reflection. They only lasted 4 or 5 seconds. I still don't really know what it was, but something was flashing and in the frame by frame shots seems to have a long flat look. 

The flashed bounced in one area moving right to left and downward. When looking closely I noticed a small object or point of light for a second once the flashing stopped heading downward then disappearing.
