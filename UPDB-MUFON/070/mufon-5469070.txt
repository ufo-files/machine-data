The object flew above us and disintegrated in a white silent explosion

Since 1998 and during a few years, one group of observers of aerial phenomena frequently visited the region of Agua Clara, located west at the city of Coro, Falcon State, Venezuela; in northern South America. This is a desertic zone with large deposits of marine fossils, crossed by the Mitare river and surrounded by mountains rich in minerals, mainly coal and quartz. In a first stage we made 30 field trips, utilizing notes, photo and video cameras, infrared and pixel films, binoculars, telescope, radio and very low frequency receptors (vlf), recorders and magnetometer.
Also we interviewed village inhabitants in normal state and during hypnosis sessions. We produced and e-book that includes text, photos and videos, that was reviewed by UFO Magazine (August-September 2005, pages 66 and 67).
On January 6, 2001 at 5:30 am we observed an object (sledge shape) flying at 100 meters above us, a few seconds later it disappeared in a great silent explosion of white light (see picture and sketch attached). The object left two black trails, resembling those that appeared later in Caracas (Venezuelan capital), reported by one of the largest circulation newspaper in Venezuela (El Universal, Zona Magazine, February 18, 2001, attached).
