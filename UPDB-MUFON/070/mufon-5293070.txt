Two metallic balls hovering close to each other lost eye contact of one but found one of the in a different direction hovered in one area for a while.

My husband kids and I saw two balls right next to each other, what looked like metallic balls that reflected a bright light when the light hit it. We lost eye contact of one but kept on with the other. It hovered for a long time in one area with slow movements in different directions. I started filming and couldn’t tell if I was capturing anything. Went inside for about 20 minutes came back out and found it on the other side of my house. Was able to take a picture. Once I zoomed into the video from earlier I can see multiple objects when I could only see one with the naked eye. Later once back inside I saw what I thought was a plane and thought ya see that’s clearly a plane. To my surprise it changed direction and went from right to left then forward out of sight. Couldn’t get that on camera it was gone before I could unlock my phone. The day before On the freeway around where I live. I saw a similar bright ball of light almost like a star but much bigger and brighter and that one zoomed out of sight. I was shocked to see things two days in a row.
