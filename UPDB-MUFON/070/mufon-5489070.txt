{cak} Extremely bright object hovering. Binoculars revealed the object to be a verticle shaped cylinder of light

Had arrived at my farm 10 miles west of [location removed] at 5:00 am. Got out of vehicle and immediately noticed a very bright light to the east. Went into shop and got my binoculars and the object appeared to be a solid object, long vertical shape like a tennis ball can. I took a few pics then tried to shoot pics through my binoculars, but couldn't. Got out a small telescope to try to take pictures through. Object had now changed shape to a bowl shape. Took a few pics of bowl shape, but they just look like a round object via this set up. Clouds obscured object for a couple of minutes. Cleared for a couple of minutes. Clouded again for a couple of minutes. Cleared again and the object was gone. Checked time at 5:47 am.

I'm on a hilltop with no lights on. Object was around 20-25 degree above the horizon. Could have been 10 - 20 miles away. Object remained stationary for the most part.

I felt excitement about the sighting. I'm not a UFO hunter, but am open to investigating something in the sky when I see it. This is my first sighting of something odd.
