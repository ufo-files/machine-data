Saucer shape with lights over Rt 60 in Virginia.

Is was about 1972, I remember not needing a jacket. My mom, my dad and my great grandma was n the car with me driving from West Virginia to Hopewell Virginia. We were stopped because of traffic near the toll booth. My mom and dad started getting excited and I remember my mom asked me do you see it? Look in the sky do you see it and I told her I couldn't. My dad asked where the camera was, it was in the trunk so we all got out and I asked my mom what she was looking at, she pointed in the sky and said do you see it right there, and when I looked i saw a saucer shape with a round collar at the bottom which had Square lights that I had actually took to be windows, along the edge of the saucer there were lights that were red, green, yellow and blue.Those lights were circular and when they would intersect it turned white and separated back to their original colors The Craft crossed over route 60 I would guess that it was roughly going North to South and then it crossed back over Rt 60 going South to North and then North to South once more before disappearing. My dad asked the toll booth attendant if they saw something in the sky he said no.
