orange flickering fireball going at steady pace

Was sat at my pc watching TV next to window with open curtains around 8pm when noticed orange ball of burning light, flickering and glowing moving across the sky, maybe 100ft up. It was travelling north/north west from the Camden/Kentish Town area up towards Tufnell Park tube area at a steady rather slow pace, slowly moving further away until my view was blocked by buildings. Have previously seen exact same thing on 25/9/11, 30/9/11 and 30/11/11, moving pretty much the exact same pace (one night was quite windy) and in the same direction. Once it burned out before it went out of view. Definitely not plane (too low, no sound) or Chinese lantern as constant pace, no height change and not affected by wind.
