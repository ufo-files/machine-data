Woke up on March 27 2018 with section of tissue removed from body

My wife and I were staying at the Holiday inn the dat before we departed N.H, to move to Kansas, My wife asked what happened to the side of my residual limb. I am an above knee amputee left leg. I felt no pain but there was a roughly 3X4 inch oval of flesh that appeared to be burned off. There was no blood, no serous fluid, no scabbing just a read oval around where a roughly 1/8 thick layer of tissue was missing. My wife took a photo of this wound. The next day a strange hand shaped bruise was on my left upper arm. She took two photos one without them highlighted the other with the bruises circled. 
I have no recollection of any thing out of the ordinary. However my wife said she had nightmares that something was in the room and held her down. Just a slightly over a year after that date she was diagnosed on with a extremely rare rapid growing uterine cancer that took her life in Oct 2019. I developed testicular issues that lead to a bilateral orchiectomy in April 2019.
