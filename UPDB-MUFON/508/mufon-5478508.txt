Brighter than a star. With bare eyes it seemed cylinder with binoculars, round, individual lights circling the outside and dark center.

I was standing on my balcony on the top floor of my apartment building. I work in aviation and am always looking to the sky. Near the moon seemed to be a very big and bright star about a thumbs length away from the moon (0735). At closer inspection it was obviously an aircraft but it was too far away to be a normal aircraft and still seemed much larger than looking at a Comercial aircraft at normal altitude. Also it was so bright. It seemed cylinder shapes with an almost triangle back directed towards the moon. It seemed to fly towards the moon before stopping and remaining stationary for a moment then change directions and almost orbit to another side of the moon about half a thumb size away from it. I went to get binoculars and returned (0745) and it seemed to have gotten much further away and through the binoculars it was round, disk shaped. Seemed like individual lights lined the outside and had a dark center. The lights seemed to almost dance around. I then stopped viewing it.
