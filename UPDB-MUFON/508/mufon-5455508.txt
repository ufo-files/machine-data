{tjd} UFO right above the powerlines

In the corner of my eye I noticed what seemed like 3 black balls connected by strings slowly hovering in the east direction right above the power line. At first I thought it was going to run into the power line as it was so close to it, but it slowly passed right over it. I grabbed my phone camera, took 3 pictures, 2 without flash and the last one where I put flash on you can already see the object disappearing. 

Since I had a reference point, I could tell the objects were very small, baseball sized at most, it seemed like two smaller objects were hanging from the top object by a thread, however there were no balloons or anything attached to them so I have no clue how they stayed airborne. It was no more than 20 feet from me.
