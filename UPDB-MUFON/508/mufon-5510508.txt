{cak} Solid illuminated object that passed across the sky in a straight line and steady speed

I was out during the late evening talking with two of my neighbors, while we outside we noticed a few aircraft flying over at what appeared to be at various altitudes and they had typical aircraft lights such as strobes and wing lights. We later noticed a solid, round, lit up object moving at a steady speed which seemed to be at normal cruising speed of a commercial jet except at a much lower altitude. The intensity of the light remained constant as it passed overhead in a straight line from west to east. At first I thought it may have been a satellite, but the altitude and the nature of the bright light, led me to think otherwise. The object remained in view for no more than 5 minutes, then was obscured from view by trees and houses to the east. (2 witnesses)
