Not a shooting star!

Initially I thought is was an astroid burning up as it was entering earths atmosphere. I was burning for the better portion of a minute than dissapeared. It then reappeared and appeared burning again. There was no overcast, it was a clear evening. When it reappeared it turn abruptly. At this point another lady beside me asked me what it was. The light went out again and the lady proceeded back to her business. It reappeared again and turned again. It initially was heading south changed direction west and then north.
