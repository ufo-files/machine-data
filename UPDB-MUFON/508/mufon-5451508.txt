Extremely bright light moving across my position then turned directly toward me

Sitting on a picnic table@ 230am. Sky was cloudless w/no atmospheric disturbances. Stars were plainly visible as the farm country surrounding me had only porch & yard lights from two houses. I viewed what I first believed was a commercial aircraft at 20k feet due to the apparently lazy speed. Flight path was from SW to NE. Upon closer approach it became obvious the light was far too bright for a commercial craft or even a helicopter search light, and was all consuming as the only identifiable "structure," if one can refer to a sphere of light that way. As there were no red/green wingtip beacons it confirmed this was no commercial or military craft.
This craft apparently saw me sitting in the nearly total darkness as it made a gentle left turn, flew directly for me as though to perform a "flyover" then slowed to a stop, now stationary in air, still roughly 27° up.
It was not a helicopter as there was no rotor sound. Having worked on rotary wing craft for 6 years I am positive this was no helicopter.
I watched the craft hover for as long as it takes for three drags on a cigarette. I slowly waved my arm over my head three times to communicate I was aware of its presence.
At this point the brightness increased as though a dimmer switch had been rotated a couple levels and a smaller light emerged to the right of the craft.
The smaller sphere had a green tint and remained stationary beside the white sphere.
It was at this point I went inside.
I checked out the window and only the white sphere could be seen. I watched for awhile longer... then I had to use the toilet.... It was not there after.
