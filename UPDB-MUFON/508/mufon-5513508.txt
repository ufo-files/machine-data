{nd} Bright pulsing orange star like light pattern shape crossing sphere, pointy edges

Noticed bright orange sphere outside bedroom transom window while lying in bed. Ran to living room to view more closely - object was just above neighbors house not high enough above to be a plane or other normal flying object. Reminded me of a Luminiara the way it lighted from the inside. It moved quickly yet silently from the position over the house toward the west then back eastward. It finally just went poof and disappeared in the Easterly direction. It maintained an orange color with yellow center the entire time.
