I came outside to put away the chickens and looked up. A bright glowing orb was high in the sky. Then I realized it was not so high. It looked like one of those battery operated closet lights you click on and off (puck-light.)The lights were in a pentagon configuration with one more in the middle. I thought, "Oh WOW look at that. It must be some kind of drone." Then I realized it was now only 20 feet up, just above the trees. It hovered, slowly rotating as I watched, trying to figure out what it was. Then the lights started flashing, changing beautiful blue- green and violet. I thought this was some kind of communication. The lights got lower, maybe 10, 5, 3 feet above the ground. I could hear a whirring noise. As I followed it maneuvered away from me and down the driveway. In my bare feet on sharp gravel and in pitch darkness, I was now afraid, and stopped following. As I watched the glowing flashing lights retreated and disappeared into the woods.
