large bright arc welder light object in morning sky

While heading off to Newcastle airport via taxi at 05.00 am observed the above object in the early morning night sky.This was witness by myself,fellow passenger and taxi driver.it was large about 10 times the size/brightness of Venus in the morning sky and really stood out from the other stars present at that time .Emitting a brilliant white light like a arc welder torch.Definitely not a plane or helicopter.Was still present at arrival at airport 10 mins later.A lot of people must of witnessed it as people were arriving for flights and others leaving at same time.
