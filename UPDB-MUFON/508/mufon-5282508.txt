Soft V shape

I glanced up at the cloudless cold sky to see the stars on my way inside my home from the parking lot. I quickly saw what I thought were ducks flying in a perfectly symmetrical "V" pattern. I looked away for 1 seconds when I realized that's unique. So I took a few steps closer looking for wings flapping, separation of birds and even quacks (since it was quiet outside with no wind). As I looked closer what I thought were ducks was actually a soft boomerang shape object slowly gliding about 5 football fields of the ground going no faster than ducks would fly. I could now see the outline of the edge and it was a gray, pearlescent, smoke/ cloudy shimmer. There was no clouds it was flying thru and no vapor trail of any kind. As I stood there following it with my eyes and patting my pockets feeling for my phone to record it shower angle up and to the right. As soon as it moved with the new direction the object disappeared from the right side of the boomerang to the left. There were no clouds, it had no sound and it felt like I waswatching a movie the way it cloaked from right to left (not all at once like any movie you see). Entire experience lasted 20 seconds. The vanishing part is starting but the silence of something that size being that close to my vantage point is shocking. I live by DFW Airport so planes are everywhere but this was not that. I feel like the only reason I saw it was from city lights below bouncing off of it but it wasn't like light reflecting off glass or metal.
