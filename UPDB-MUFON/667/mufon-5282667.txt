No trail, extremely fast. Disappeared then reappeared. Cloudy night

A very fast white light flew in a straight line for a few seconds and then it was gone. This was way too fast to be a plane, it was the fastest thing I’ve ever seen. It didn’t appear to be far away, and it was low in the sky. It seemed to be in atmosphere and not a meteor. There was no trail, no sound, no aura. This was a cloudy night which puts doubt in my mind I was seeing a meteor, especially at that altitude and angle. It did disappear and reappear (possibly behind a cloud) but was gone quickly. If you have ever seen the space station fly over it was that bright and that size but in atmosphere, and way way faster. Perspective was hard to judge because of the darkness and clouds but my natural reaction was that I witnessed something abnormal.
