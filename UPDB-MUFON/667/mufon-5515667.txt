UFO caught in high speed shots.

Firstly I must explain this is photographic evidence!
I was taking sunset photographs in HDR format (3 exposures of different aperture in approx 1 second). The camera a was at 30 times zoom intended to catch the sunset reflection on the old house windows.
Upon processing the three shots into an hdr format (1 hdr picture) I noticed a white spot in three places in the supper sky area. Investigating this I found that it was apparent on each of the hdr frames but in different positions. This pointed to the spot whatever it was must have been travelling at a very high speed to cover the distance indicated in the composite HDR picture. I ruled out camera shake as the other picture details are all correctly aligned.
