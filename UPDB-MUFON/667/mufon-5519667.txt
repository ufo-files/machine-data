Strange White Lights Next to Jupiter

Tonight as I was looking at Jupiter just after sunset I noticed a set of Six white lights just to right of it with the naked eye. I first thought these were Jupiters moons but upon investigation on the internet I soon realized youre not supposed to see Jupiters moons without aid. Also they were not in the correct formation.

All six of these lights were white, non blinking like stars and were sitting in a some what pyramid formation on the right side of Jupiter.

I looked at them with binoculars and got a much clearer view of them, I think they appeared much to large to be Jupiters moons. As far as I know there is no constalation and as I said they were all white and non blinking like stars. Not sure what to make of it. My parents were also observers of this event.
