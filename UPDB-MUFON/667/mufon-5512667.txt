{dr}was looking at some bright stars in the orion area when this very bright flame-like light appeared

Looking at bright stars in front of my house in the orions belt (due south direction) when out of the south-west came a slow moving, very bright orb. It stood out as the stars in the area are white/blue in color. Was looking for some clue as to its possible definition. ie regular strobe lights- we have 2 major airports within 5 miles. Nothing at all. Thought it was a hot air balloon flame at first. But was too high and too fast. Much larger and brighter than any plane and several planes were outbound from SFO on a northern flightpath. It appeared to be randomly glowing. Orange, yellow and red were the prominant colors. Approx. 4000 to 5000 feet up on a west to east (no change in direction) trajectory. Slowly went across the night sky before vanishing abruptly.
