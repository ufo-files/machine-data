{jpg} Viewer unexplained flying object from resort room deck/porch.

Mid October, 2018. approximately 12:30am, only on the upper deck of resort for 5 minutes (right outside my room) I looked up and saw a spectacular array of green to red and blue lights (they appeared to be 200 feet away but could of been alot farther considering I was viewing the object through many many evergreen trees, but it appeared somewhat close to scale. The lights were alternating in a triangular shape for approximately 1-2 minutes before it moved southeast slowly(these lights were completely mesmorizing) and it appeared that fireballs were shooting down to the ground after the 2 minutes into the sighting. I remember thinking "I should not be seeing this"
At that point I went into my room and went to sleep. The next day the resort had a reported "rooftop fire" and I went on with my life.
