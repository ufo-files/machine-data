Observed long gray tube with two thin brilliant bright white cylindrical objects.

On the morning of 23 April 2020, I was taking a walk with my wife and noticed what I thought was a helicopter drone. At first, I thought the lights on it were rotors, but they were not stationary. The body was a long gray tube with two thin brilliant bright white cylindrical objects, like a fluorescent ceiling light. I assumed I was looking at a KC-135 Tanker accompanied by two fighter jets. I attempted to put wings on it and count engines, it had none. I observed the “fighter jets” were moving very quickly and erratically all over and around this object. I was only able to observe it for approximately ten seconds as it was moving very fast; it had no contrail like all the other planes had flying that day.
