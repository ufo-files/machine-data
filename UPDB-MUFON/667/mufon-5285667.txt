Solid black car sized sphere ufo

My three cousins and I were leaving my uncles house one Saturday night. He lives out in Brighton Colorado. Brighton Colorado is known for its flat areas, farms, and ranches. There’s one highway you have to take to get to Denver which is south of Brighton. About 5 minutes into our drive, I noticed a solid black sphere in the distance hovering about 7ft above the median. Note* My male cousin was driving and my female cousin was sitting in the front seat on her phone. I was sitting in the back seat in the middle of the car looking at the road. I first spotted the object when it was about 60 ft away. As we were driving closer and closer to it, I realized that my eyes weren’t playing jokes on me. The object was so black, it absorbed all the light that went it’s direction. It was pitch black outside but yet, you can clearly see that this object was hovering and was a solid circle. As we were approaching closer, I was thinking to myself,” is this thing going to stay hovering or hit us.” We got about ten feet away from the object then it shot vertically up. It shot up so fast that it looked like it morphed into a tear drop shape then vanished. I was completely frozen in excitement and a little bit of fear. I waited about ten seconds to see if my cousins would say something. They said nothing so I said,” I’m I the only one who fu***** saw that sh**.” My cousin who was driving replied,” I saw that cuz, please don’t talk about it.” My other cousin didn’t see anything because she was on her phone. So we drove the whole back to town in silence. The object made no noise. Also, none of us recall of any missing time. Once we got home, my cousin explained to me that he’s seen those his whole life and to this day, he believes it’s demons following him. I personally think it was a craft.
