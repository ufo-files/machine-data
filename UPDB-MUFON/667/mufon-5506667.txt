{cak} glowing orbs

My wife and I had just moved into our new home in the country and I was watching the weather and there was a fast moving storm about on top of us coming out of the north west. I went outside and looked back over the house at the sky.I could see the storm front fast approaching and riding on the very front of the clouds were 3 orbs in the shape of a triangle .I yelled for my wife and she came out. I pointed to them and she seen them also just then it was like they knew we had seen them they just faded out of sight . Then I looked to the left a little and there were 4 more orbs floating in a slight arc.as I pointed at those they faded out also my wife ran into the house and wouldnt come back out . I was real close to those orbs and really thought they were alive like an organism . I have looked at the orbs on YouTube and have seen similar orbs there
