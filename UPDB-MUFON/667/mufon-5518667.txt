Was coming home late and saw a plane like light very very low with bright white and red lights.

On 421, coming home late and seen plane like lights (bright white light with smaller red light), thought it was a plane flying very very low. As turning down the road it was hidden near the trees, when got in drive way looked over to the field in front of my house and seen it very visible. I kept watching it and then the lights got lower to the ground and vanished.
