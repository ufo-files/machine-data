{rjl} Huge long moving object I the sky

My husband and I pulled into our driveway right around 0000 hundred on the 25th of may 2019. While getting out of the pick up, I was admiring the sky because the stars where all out and then as my husband and i were looking up, at first I thought I was seeing a shooting star. We quickly realized it was not, it was lights and they were beginning to fly over us. There were many lights all in one row flying on an exact straight line. We realized they were not separate objects. It was one huge object with many lights running along its body. The object was so large it was blocking out the stars around it and it lmade absolutely no noise as it traveled from west to east. We tried to get pictures and video on our phones but the quality it very poor.
