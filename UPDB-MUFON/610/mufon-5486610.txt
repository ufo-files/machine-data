{nd} 1 set of sequential lights moved in, hovered and then another same just appeared to the left.

Was sitting outside the bar and grill Yesterday's in support of a fundraiser event for a recently deceased friend. There were a number of people outside but I was sitting by myself, not engaged with anyone. Looking at night sky as I do and spotted to my left in the south western sky, a set of four white blinking lights in an alternating pattern. It was moving slowly then stopped and hovered. Another set of same lights just appeared on the same horizontal plane as the first with similar blinking sequential white lights. Both hovered for approximately five minutes. All potential witnesses were drinking and having loud conversations. No one else saw. Didn't think to pull out my cell phone for video.
