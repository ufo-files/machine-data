{rjl} Saw unusual bright light on way to work

I was on my way to work this morning on route 18 south, and i saw an unusual light in the sky around 6:35am. There is a tower at exit 15 that blinks red. I was about five minutes from that exit, and i saw a very bright white light off to the right of the tower. Then the tree line obstructed my vision, When I got to exit 15 I could clearly see the tower, but the bright white light was gone. I drive this route at this time every Tuesday morning, and i have never seen that light. At first, i thought it was a plane, but I could see it for at least a minute and it didn't move. Then a couple minutes later, when the tree line obstruction was no longer an issue, the light was completely gone.
