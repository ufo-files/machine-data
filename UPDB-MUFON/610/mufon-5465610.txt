{dr}Second time this happened.

This is the second time this happened. Several months ago three Chevron shaped craft showed up. They left when two military fighter jets showed up. I reported it to Mufon. Tonight there appeared to be just one object that had four red/orange lights. All perfectly spaced and all in a row. This thing was big! When the lights or engines were on it just hovered. The lights would go out and reappear a second later but the object had moved miles. This time I asked it to please come closer. It complied three times. Each time it moved closer the lights would go out and a second later they came back on but the object was closer to me. This is going to sound stupid but it was as if the object heard me. I am a retired electrical engineer who is not nuts. I know just about every craft we have both commercial and military. I have no idea what this thing was but God it was big. I live semi-remote making night sky viewing excellent. I just do not know what to say. I went inside to get my camera but it never returned.
