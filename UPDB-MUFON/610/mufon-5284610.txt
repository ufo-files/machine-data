Large Black Triangle

This was a long time ago but I remember it very clearly. I was taking my now wife to visit my grandmother in Hull. We arrived about 10pm and I happened to look up and thought I saw something moving. There were no lights or sound and the only reason I could see the outline was because it was a very clear night. The outline of the triangle was visible because it blocked out the stars as it moved. It appeared to be gliding silently with no noise or any type of lights. I pointed it out to my wife who asked what the hell it was. I had to tell her I don’t know. I had just spent 6 years in the Royal Air Force working on the flight line and knew every single kind of aircraft that was in the sky. This was definitely not any type of craft I had seen before.
