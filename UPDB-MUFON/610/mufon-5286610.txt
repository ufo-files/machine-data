8 or 9 white lights in a line

I first saw one, it was so small. Then a second or two later I noticed another one behind it. A few seconds later I saw 2 more very close together following the others. About 15 seconds later there was another one following the same path. 

Look, they were crazy small, but they never blinked and they all went in the same straight line. I just can't imagine anything being so high up. They looked like little stars, but that's not what they were. 

It was so out of the ordinary, I had to tell you.
