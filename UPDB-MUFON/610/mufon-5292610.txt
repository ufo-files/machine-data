Flew over house

I walked out my back door and there was a perfect silver sphere moving south to north of Dallas. I froze in place knowing this was not a balloon or anything. I snapped out of it and ran inside to grab my cam corder. I caught it just in time as it spead rapidly to the north. I filmed as long as I could see it. I broke up with my GF at the time and she kept the footage. She made a copy I shared with a friend that refused to return it. I have their names, they might share it. To be clear this was a perfect sphere. No tear drop, nothing hanging under it. I would say it was the size of a house at least. NOT a balloon!
