I came across the field into the yard and observed when exiting my car two large yellow blinking lights low in the sky which I thought were attached to a laarge oval object. I entered the house and reached for my binoculars and the object vanished. ON February 11th two lights were observed steady in the sky some distant away and one disappeared while the other stayed.
