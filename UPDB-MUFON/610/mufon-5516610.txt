(GS) Silent Bright White-Blue Object Turns, Hovers, Then Vanishes.

I was outside, it was a very warm summer evening when an object that at first glance appeared to be a star or airplane, turned in a very unusal way and began heading North. The object was a solid and BRIGHT white light that turned slightly blue and began to hover for a few seconds until the light dimmed and I noticed a black outline of what could have been a craft (it was an egg/soft triangular shape as far as I tell). And before I could get my phone out and film it, it just vanished. It never made sound, in fact the area/neighbor was dead silent except for dogs barking in the distance. Needless to say I was astonished and terrified.
