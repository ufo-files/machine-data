{jpg} Daylight sighting above the Black Canyon CO. Pics and video

Tue 6/11 10:10am. While driving N thru the Black Canyon (CO), a very remote area on Hwy 92 thru the West Elks surrounded by miles and miles of dense and remote National Forest, on a bluebird day w/out a cloud, I noticed a bright reflection of the sun off something in the sky. It was as if there was a giant mirror in the sky, it was that type of reflection. I knew immediately that was very unusual as I can’t recall ever seeing that happen before. I stopped to see if it would happen again and it did. I took a few pics followed by a video. It was hard to follow but after a few minutes it zoomed S incredibly fast.
While the pics and video are great, it wasn’t until I thought to zoom in the video and take screenshots and see if I could see more detail. My jaw dropped when I saw the still images from the video. What do you think? None of the pics or video have been altered in anyway other than to zoom in and screenshot/crop still images of the video. 
I see many anomalous objects in the sky all over this area of SW CO but this is the first daytime sighting I’ve had. 
Also, I took 3 photos in quick succession and then deiced to video. In two of the photos you can see the object however it doesn't appear in the other. Not sure why.
Also, FwIw, these were all shot on an iPhone 8
Would love to hear your thoughts.
