3 oval discs travelling in formation on outskirt of Westonzoyland village

Between 1979 and Sept. 1989, worked in Bridgwater Clarke's Shoe factory. Lived in Middlezoy village, so commuted 7 miles from Middlezoy, across Westonzoyland WW2 disused airfield, through Westonzoyland village, to Bridgwater. On way to work, 6:30'ish, halfway across airfield, as Westonzoyland came into view, saw three oval objects travelling, (left to right, trailing two at slightly lower heights).
Stopped my Honda C90 (small 90cc motorcycle), approx. 1000 feet from village border, took off crash helmet and turned off engine, to listen for noise from them, there was none. They continued to travel to my right until I couldn't distinguish them anymore, against the countryside.
This was 30 years ago, VERY approximately, could not pin it down any better than the period of time I worked for Clarke's Shoes. 
Also, I guestimated their speed at just 10-20 mph.
