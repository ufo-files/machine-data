{rjl} My boyfriend and I saw a very bright object move in a very fast fluid like movement. it would move downmard than up to the left then quickley back to the right it was fast and this happened multiple times then accellerated toward utica ny.

It was around 11 pm - 12 am on march 18th 2015. Both my boyfriend and I were out in the backyard watching the stars because it was so clear out no clouds in the sky. I looked to my left and saw a very bright object. It was very bright and large. It was moving oddly. Not like a plane or shooting star it was moving in very distinct fluid like movements. It moved down to the left then seemed to shift/tilt back and moved fast to the right. This seemed to happen multiple times. As it moved its shape seemed to be disc like but it was so far away. Its lights were a orange- yellow-red color to it. My boyfriend was watching with me. I was yelling "What the hell is that? Do you see what Im seeing?" This blew my mind. I have never seen anything like this before. It then seemed to just take off toward Utica very fast and it was gone. We remained outside for a couple of hours looking for it again but didn't see it. We did see a plane and the lights and size was tiny compared to what we had just saw and the plane was very slow compared to the object we had just seen. Absolutely mind blowing!
