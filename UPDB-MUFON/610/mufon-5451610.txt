Extremely bright red object that alternately changed color to bright green or whitish green, changed shape from round to odd shapes, no sound.

Fixed object but moving in a small area as it changed colors, alternating red to green plus slight changes in intensity of both colors, sometimes whitish green. It went from an object that looked like it had edges to a round sphere shape. It appeared to be rotating and I have photos to prove that movement. It appeared to rotate counterclockwise. I heard no sound. Although it was 4th of July night, this object was far above where any fireworks could have gone...this was not exploding in any way. It looked fixed but rotating.
