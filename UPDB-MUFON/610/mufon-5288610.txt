Saw a silhouette of an object as i was staring at the sky, this object seemingly descended from higher altitude and swept across the sky

I was out in Puttenham common doing some astrophotography, staring at the sky whilst taking some pictures, and saw what looked like a wide cigar shaped object with no lights, black with maybe a dark tinge of blue seemingly descend and streak across the sky very quickly, the whole sighting was less than 15 seconds, and as soon as i saw it, i was very fearful, packed up my gear as quickly as possible and left the area.
