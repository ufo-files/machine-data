Shining light beaming across the sky

Last night I was looking up at the night sky trying to catch a shooting star when I saw something smaller then the average star gliding across slowly then it disappeared only to reappear a bit further along the path it was going. Then all of a sudden it flashed a bright beam of light in the sky and continued on its way I followed it for over a minute as it kept disappearing and reappearing in the sky then it just vanished. I was on the phone with my mom describing my experience. Then a few minutes later i couldn't belive my eyes I saw it again but on the other side of the sky. But this time it didn't just flash a bright beam of light, it started to flash on and off on and off as I was watching it and just like before it was disappearing and reappearing til it vanished. It was amazing I know it wasn't a satellite because it was moving too fast. It was a very beautiful clear night and I know it wasn't a plane it was traveling much faster
