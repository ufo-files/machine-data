{dr}I took this picture with my Iphone from a city parking lot.

My husband and I were leaving to return back to our car which was parked in the parking lot. We were with another couple. We had just attended the Waterfire in Providence, RI. As were just about to get into our car, I decided to take a picture of the city skyline. I took several shots with my Iphone, and the next day posted it on FB. It was then, that it was noticed that there were lights in the picture. A row of lights...over 10..very tiny. But almost in a straight line. It was pretty high up in the sky. It's just a straight line of tiny lights. I looked at the other previous shots that I took and there were no lights. The other people that were with us also took the same shots, and he also had the lights show up in one of his shots. I can't think of any explanation for this. It is a single shot, over the city.
