round cloud like textured object with a trail

on my way to the port for mooring, i looked up to the sky to check on the sun, since it was around 7.30 int the evening i wanted to check how much time i had left to be able to moor the boat before it got dark. at first glance i spotted what i thought was the sun, than i looked again. the sun was already covered by land from my position, there was what looked like a bright round solid cloud textured object with a trail. it was far away maybe at the same height an airplane would fly. it appeared to be static but when i looked again it had moved. not sure if it was a UFO or a comet or something.
