{nd} white light seen moving across the sky

I witnessed a bright light directly above us. It then started to travel NE. There was a plane near it and the differences were startling. The plane had the normal blinking lights while this object had only a bright white light. We could see it for about 15 minutes and even after it moved very far away we could still see the light add if it was still pointed towards us. I have seen many airplanes with landing lights and this was different. Also planes do not travel with landing lights at altitude.
