Hovering then exited

Hovered stationary. Pointed out to me by resort staff. 3 objects. Very high spherical, round. No movement. Like a Venus size and shape but during the day. Much higher than the clouds. The next day observed object make a 45 degree angle change with no change in velocity. Then dropped 90 degrees down. Stopped then exited the opposite direction at unbelievable speed. It was not natural. Unlike anything I have ever seen. No known aircraft I have seen has those kind of aerodynamics.
