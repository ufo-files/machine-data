{cak} Saw lights in sky so we stopped.

We were out for a ride on the night mentioned. Coming back from the Milo area heading back home. It was around 9:15 or 9:20pm not sure exactly. I was getting ready to pull over to use the bathroom at the rest area in what my GPS said is Perkins, Maine. When I did stop I noticed a bluish green light coming from the direction of Millinocket. I'm not sure of the name of the body of water we were near. I watched the object get closer and closer. It traveled very slowly, when it got closer to us it hovered for quite a long time. Approximately an hour or so. Since that night I've had problems with my colon and also my stomach.
