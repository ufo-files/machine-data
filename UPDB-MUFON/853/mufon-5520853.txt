rectangle on bottom with a square on top, like a butter dish with a cover

I was driving on Route 1 South in Langhorne, PA yesterday, Monday, Feb. 6th, 2012, and it was a clear afternoon, no clouds in the sky. I just looked up and to my right in the sky there was a grey, big thing in the sky that didn't belong there. I estimated it to be over the Langhorne golf course area but I am not sure, but it was that area of what we local folks call old Langhore (town as opposed to the Langhorne gables area on the left side of Route 1 or the more outter areas near the Yardley bordlerline) area. It was rectangular across with a square on the top like a butter dish sort of shape. It was just there!! It was flat in the sky and then it turned sideways and just simply vanished. 
When I first saw it I thought to myself "what the hell is that?". It was a fast sighting for me because I was driving but I slowed down a little bit and watched and it turned sideways and disappeared. I said to myself in the car "No way that should be there!, No WAY that should be there!". I continued to drive and just thought about it and thought maybe someone else beside me saw it and I should tell someone. 
I lost sight of the object because it just turned sideways and disappeared.
I am not linked into you tube or flickr or facebook or twitter. 
I cant think of anything else. I just want to know if someone else saw it too.
