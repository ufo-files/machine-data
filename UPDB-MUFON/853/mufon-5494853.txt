{cak} Ufo darked out lit atmosphere and disappeared after flashing flashlight 3x's @ it.

I was walking on the beach with my girlfriend and dogs when we decided to turn and walk to the north back to where our car was parked. When we turned, we noticed a dark object darling the sky where the sun had went down 15 minutes before and thought it was maybe a really dark cloud, so I took my flashlight out, turned it on, and flashed it in a up and down motion 3x's and it just disappeared. Needless to say, we left quickly and am still dumbfounded. Had our second really big sighting just a week after that.
