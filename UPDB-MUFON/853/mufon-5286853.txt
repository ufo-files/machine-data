Orange glowing light bobbing up and down then travelling off in an easterly direction.

I'm recording this on the basis of two very credible witnesses that contacted me about this event.

Two witnesses reported seeing an orange ball of fire bobbing up and down in the sky north of their North Ajax location when getting into their vehicle after leaving a friend's house. 

Witness 2 estimates it was above Paulynn Park or Greenwood Conservation Area. Witness A called out to Witness B and when witness B looked up to the sky that witness saw it too. Witness B began filming with cell phone. The object then flew off in an eastward direction until it went out of sight. Witness B was 'Rubbed the wrong way" by the experience.

Video was taken but was lost because it was not saved. It is unknown if it can be recovered somehow from the cell phone camera. 

Neither witness wants to be identified but I can safely say that their account is entirely true. Witness B has always been a total sceptic about UFO's until now. Witness 2 is the one that contacted me shortly after the experience because witness two knows that I am someone that has a big interest in the subject of UFO's.
