{nd} Suddenly appeared - bright white light on 3/4 of elongated shape, bright green on back 1/4 heading down to ground - looked like it would crash - no sound

I was standing in the backyard with the dogs at about 10:30 p.m. the night of Thursday, May 8th, 2014. I often observe the night sky when I am out at night with the dogs. I was looking up. An elongated, fat, (not a thin line) shape that was a completely bright light, almost like it was on fire, appeared in the sky off to the south. It was huge so it looked like it was close. My house is two blocks north of a cemetery and it looked like it was going to crash in the cemetery. The actual size was very hard to determine as it looked very close because it was so big, but I could tell that it was farther away because the cemetery is so close. It was coming down straight to the ground. The front 3/4 of the object was a solid, bright fiery white light and the back 1/4 was a solid, bright green light. I did not see a trail of any kind. It was completely silent....no sound what so ever. It looked as though it must have gone straight down to the ground, but from my suburban backyard I would not be able to see off into the distance to see if it hit the ground or where it would have hit the ground. There were no planes in the sky. It looked nothing like a firecracker or anything like that. It looked nothing like anything I have ever seen. I have never seen anything that shape, color, that bright and fiery looking, that was completely silent coming straight to the ground. I have not seen anything in the news about it. Usually if it's a plane crash or something like that, it's reported in the news. But it looked nothing like a plane of any kind.
