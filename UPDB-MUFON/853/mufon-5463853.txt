A bright round light hovering at above eye level from the bedroom window, that accelerated into the ground in a flash

I am 61 years old, a retired engineer, it was difficult to come to terms with this event, but I wanted to share this experience, I went to bed at 11.45 after watching the News, I usually fall asleep on hitting the pillow, we have two dogs that sleep on the landing, if one of them starts the other one follows, so one starts growling and the other joins in, I tell them to be quite, but they continue, I get up to see what they are growling at, look out of the window to check on my car, and see this glowing light stationary above the Island across from us, it was very bright to look at, but it did not light up the trees or land below it, I have three cameras a Canon 7d with 100-400 lens, Sony A7r, Lumix g3, as a keen photographer, in the past I have always wondered why people who have "strange" experiences never produce viable photographic evidence, but now the thought of taking a photo never entered by thoughts, I was transfixed, I cant explain the feeling, then the light went straight down at an incredible speed, no bang, no explosion, nothing, I got dressed, and went to take a look. the tide was out, so I had access to the island, apart from some startled deer, there was no evidence of any impact, the following day at low water I looked all around the island and found nothing. Since this event, I have problems sleeping, and have woken up stood at the window, not remembering having got up to stand there, it has deeply disturbed me to the point of wanting to sell up and move, I am reporting this to see if anyone else as had a similar experience, and can offer advice or help.
