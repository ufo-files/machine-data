I was snapping pics of Mt. Shasta. I saw nothing with the nakey eye, but the photos revealed possible orbs over the peaks.

I arrived at a campground in the late afternoon in the town of Mt. Shasta California on Sunday May 10th 2020. Once I got set up, I walked to the edge of the campground and snapped a series of about 8 photos of the western peaks of Mt. Shasta. I saw nothing unusual with the naked eye (and I was looking), but later on after I uploaded the photos I noted some fuzzy orb like objects above the peaks that seemed to be spaced out approximately 10 miles from my position. I used a 70-300 mm Nikor Lens at 3/4 zoom on a Nikon D5600 camera body. I am a photographer, and as such, I am used to spotting bugs and birds etc., but these are not either in my estimation. Also, I did not download the pictures for two days because I noticed nothing unusual. I have attached the series of photos that are cropped a bit but otherwise no other adjustments to exposure etc. were performed. I used auto focus.
