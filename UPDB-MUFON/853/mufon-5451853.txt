Floating from over ocean, headed onshore, went dark and stopped

As I went outside to let my dog out, I happened to look up and see a glowing pink object in the sky with a brighter, almost yellowish center, coming on shore and slowly moving west. As it moved closer, roughly 500ft to a quarter mile to my north, I realized I had my phone in my pocket to video. 

When zooming in, you can clearly see an almost orange bullseye pattern, looks like conducting some sort of electricity. The light started to then dim, as I got my son inside the house and when I looked back up (end of video), you can see a dark object hovering and no longer glowing, then I went inside and did not see anything after that.
