Huge rectangular shape

My husband and I were on I-65 N, after going to see a movie, we were headed home from Rivergate. We were on Two Mile Pkwy (Rivergate Pkwy)entry ramp to the interstate and after we topped the hill that is where we observed a rectangular shaped object hovering almost directly above the Tyson plant. I can only assume it was rectangular in shape from the lights. The lights on 2 corners were white and blue/green flashing in color. The lights on the other 2 corners were a purplish/orange white. It was huge. My husband observed it, however he was driving. We drove past it and it sat there. Didn't move an inch. I turned in my seat and still watched it sit there, until we past Millersville exit ramp and topped the hill on the other side. I could not believe what I saw... It had to have been the length of a commercial jet and I know that other people had to have seen it as well. The lighting on the ground below it and from the cars on 65-S made it impossible to make out any more details. I just wish that we could have stopped and observed it better but we had to get home to my brother who was on the phone with me at the time. I had cellphone interference as well.
