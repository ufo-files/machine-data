I was driving home on towerline road, and as I stopped at the stop sign at towerline & toboggan road, I looked both way, but as I looked left, I noticed a few pulsating yellow-orange lights, that were kind of far away, but were still a little under the size of a baseball. as I started away from the stop sign, more lights appeared, seemingly out of nowhere. at this point in time, all of the 6 total lights were spread out. but as I continues driving, I decided to pull off on a side road, I believe it was hilst road off of towerline. and I slowly idled forward, and watched the 6 lights slowly get closer together, until it appeared that 3 were touching, making up 2 ships, with 3 lights each. then I noticed the road ahead was really snowy, so I started to turn around to head back the other way, and when I pulled into the field entrance on the right hand side of the road, I looked back up into the sky to my left, and all of the lights were gone, I looked all over the sky after that, but there was no trace of them, they had just disappeared. It was unlike any lights in the sky I had ever seen before, and I am 100% convinced that they were not human!
