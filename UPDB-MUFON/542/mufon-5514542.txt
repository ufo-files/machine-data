My friend and I were taking a walk on Siesta Beach with her dog after sunset about 7:30PM tonight, November 2, 2012. As we were leaving the beach, my friend turned around and said that it looked like someone had started a fire--I turned around and saw what looked like a glowing orange pot of fire. As we watched it, it lifted off with no sound, still looking like it had flames coming out of an ornage pot--it did have a white glow in the center after it lifted off--it ascended up and to the left until it dissappeared.

My friend acted matter of fact about it at first. I was excited about seeing it. After my friend got home, she started to feel more curious about it. I googled it when I got home as I had never heard of an orange fiery object. It did remind me of the image in the bible about seeing chariots of fire.
