I never actually saw the objects. I took a picture and noticed the objects hours later. 

My father and I were looking for arrowheads in a cotton field a few miles south of Marianna, Florida.

If the picture is zoomed, one object is visible just above my father. Three other objects, in a triangle formation can also be seen off in the distance to the right of the closest one about the middle of the picture.
