Orange Orb on 3 separate occassions

I was online trying to figure out what I've been seeing when I came across your article. I live near Summerlin and on at least three occasions during the summer of 2020, I witnessed an orangish orb flying over my neighborhood.. It does twist and twirl, it is gossamer and it does luminesce. It flies fairly low to the ground, but it is silent. As it traversed east to west, it made directional changes to fly more Northeast. It also gradually gained a much higher altitude as it moved along.

It has a dull amber glow at night. I notice that it travels from west to east, on a similar flight line as helicopters and small planes in the area. I thought it was an owl but its fluidity and speed are not the same.
