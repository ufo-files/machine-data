{nd} I was photographing the Sun and noticed an object in the photos that did not appear to the naked eye.

I was on my patio behind my house and was photographing the Sun because there was a haze in the sky that made a circle around the Sun. After taking the photograph I went inside to look at it. I noticed in the photograph a small bluish green colored dot. When I zoomed in on it I noticed that it was a ball-shaped object with bright green points of light in it. I went outside and looked up at the circle around the Sun and so nothing with my naked eye I took another photograph and the same object appeared only the points of light in the object were in a different configuration. I took several more photos and the object appeared in every one of them and the points of light change configuration every time.
