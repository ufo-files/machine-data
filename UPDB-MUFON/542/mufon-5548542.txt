I have been imaging these incredible objects for almost 7 weeks. They do, indeed, seem to have awareness and you can summon them with a flashlight and a good attitude. I don't really think about them as good or bad because they are in a class by themselves. Whatever they want to do they are going to do and we are just fortunate to witness them. If you live in Austin, Texas, just go outside at dusk and look to the ENE and you'll see them right between roof line and tree line -- hard to image but easy to see. And yes, some of them station right in front of stars and move right along with them -- I too have seen them suddenly come out of nowhere and form triangle-like formations in the night sky. After a while your shock will turn into astonishment but never less than amazement. If you want to see them you will. 

I was not going to post these pictures until I read Kim's report and viewed his/her images.

Your observations that these crafts might be covered in reflective tiles is a good one as I managed to image one of the ships with two of its three lights breaking down into some kind of geometric light pattern. This is the only time I've ever seen this and I haven't manipulated the image in any way. 

Also, well, make of the other image what you will. I thought it was a trick of the light until Kim's images made me think twice.

Both pictures were captured from digital video I took between 8 - 10pm the evening of 2/23.
