Object flying vertically

I was sitting in my room with my friend when he looked out the window and noticed something flying vertically up, at first he joked that it was an angel but we watched it fly all the way higher than we could see, completely vertical the whole time. It was a dark object and left thick trails behind it. I would assume it was a plane if it hadn't been flying completely vertically.
