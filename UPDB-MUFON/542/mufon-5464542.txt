I was sleeping next to my husband in our bedroom, I slept next to the wall on that night. At approximately 3:00/4:00 in the morning, a whirring sound woke me up, I leaned up on my right arm to prop myself up on my side and thought that I saw our youngest daughter (due to height similarity) standing in the doorway of our bedroom. Note: it wasn't unusual for her to ocassionally wake us up to ask if she could sleep with us. I spoke to her (thinking that it was my daughter) saying "What's the matter baby?". At that moment I noticed a foggy blue haze in the hallway behind the..presence. After speaking to what I thought was my daughter, and seeing the blue light-haze, I noticed the size of its head, being much larger than that of my daughters. When I realized that it could NOT possibly be my daughter, at that VERY moment that I had the thought 'that's not her'..I suddenly dropped back down to sleep again, no further thoughts, no response to my question that I spoke to who I thought was my daughter, I did not get out of bed, my husband did not wake up, nothing. I simply dropped back down on my bed and slept. Come morning, I had an uneasy feeling and was confused, wondering if it could have simply been a dream, but thinking it felt too real to be a dream.
