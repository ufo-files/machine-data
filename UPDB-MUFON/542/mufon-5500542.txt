Star moving erratically over oxford UK

I was in the garden smoking a cigarette looking at the sky and saw a dull star. The star moved across the sky stopped, changed direction and then again moved across the sky i watched it until it blended with the light pollution and went off into the distance. no sound was heard and was way higher than any plane or helicopter. left a trail at one point. was 90 degrees straight above me when it was moving most erratically
