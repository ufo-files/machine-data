Moving starlike light that changed directions and disappeared.

I was walking down the street with a witness, on a moonless and stary night. I looked directly up and told the witness to look up as I saw a light moving in a straight line. The light was about as bright as the brightest star in the sky at the time. I saw it moving in the Northeast direction. I immediately looked behind me to check if there were any cars coming because we were standing in the middle of the street. I looked back up at the light, and suddenly it was traveling in the exact opposite direction as it was previously. I looked behind me again for cars, and told the witness that we should probably get to the sidewalk. By the time I got to the sidewalk, which maybe took 3 seconds, I looked back up and it was gone. The whole sighting probably lasted 10 seconds, but I only say the UFO itself for about 5 seconds. There were visible planes in the area so we were pretty sure it wasn't a plane. Both the witness and I saw shooting stars not long after the sighting, but they didn't look like the light that we saw.
