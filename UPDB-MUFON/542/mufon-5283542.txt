Was first awoken by a humming noise that didn't choreate or sound similar to any known electrical emissions in my house. It was stronger. I opened my front door under the belief that it was originating from that direction and across the street between my neighbors two houses I witnessed 4-6 reddish orange lights that pulsated in sync with one another and swirled around what seemed to be a transparent disc like object (about 15 ft in diameter) in a clockwise fashion hovering for what I remember to be about 30 seconds before ascending upwards and vanishing. I could see through the craft itself, it was only due to the formation of the lights that I was able to depict it's size and shape.
