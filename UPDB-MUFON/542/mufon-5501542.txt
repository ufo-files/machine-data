{dr}Triangular object hovering

I was driving in Prince George’s County Maryland with about 4-5 people in the car and one of the occupants said look a UFO! We looked up and there was a huge triangular object hovering over a small building at about the height of a half mile up or so. The size of the object was about the size of a football field. Very big and hovered and made no sound at all and had a few red lights that would blink every few seconds. It was black in color and had no windows and a pattern on the bottom that looked the trails that worms leave in wood. We all saw it and were at a stop light and there were some cars behind us that must have seen it too. It was too big to miss but was silent so was very strange. We all just sat there looking in amazement of how such a huge object can just float in the air like that. Afterwards we drove away for some odd reason. I don't know why I didn't stay to watch more. We all still talk about the sighting to this day and wish we had a camera or something. It was truly remarkable how big it was and how low it was. This has been on my mind for many years now to find out what exactly I saw. All I know is it did not look like nothing I have seen on earth!
