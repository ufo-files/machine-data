2 orange orbs passed over with no sound

Noticed an orange light to the North. First thought was it was a planet or star, but then realized it was moving towards us. It didn't make any sound, and seemed pretty low in the sky. It continued past us, flying towards the South. After it passed, we could see the light was pointing ahead of it, pointing towards the direction it was flying. We could tell this because the light was not as bright when it was flying away from us as when it was flying towards us. Once it was out of sight, we looked back to the North and another one just like it was following the same path. It flew over us just like the first one, and was eventually out of sight as it flew further South. I got video of the 2nd one, but it's terrible quality.
