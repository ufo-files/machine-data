I JUST RECENTLY MOVED TO ANCHORAGE, ALASKA...I AM ORIGINALLY FROM SAN DIEGO, CALIFORNIA. I WAS HEADED TO WORK THE MORNING OF DECEMBER 1, 2015. I DIDN'T START WORK UNTIL 9:00AM, AND HAD ARRIVED SHORTLY BEFORE 8:30AM TO THE PARKING GARAGE...I HAD SOME EXTRA TIME...SO I FIGURED I'D GET MY PHONE OUT, SEND A SNAP CHAT TO MY FRIENDS BACK HOME, AND SHOW THEM JUST HOW DARK IT IS IN ALASKA AT 830 IN THE MORNING. THE STARS LOOKED UNUSUALLY BRIGHT AND STAND OUT-ISH TO ME FOR SOME REASON THAT MORNING, I FELT COMPELLED TO TAKE A PHOTO BEFORE STARTING THE SNAP CHAT. WHEN RECORDING THE SNAP CHAT, I AM PANNING FROM THE LEFT, WHERE YOU CAN SEE THE MOON IN THE TOP LEFT CORNER, AND MOVING TO THE RIGHT, AWAY FROM THE LARGER BUILDINGS, TOWARDS THE WATER. I WAS WATCHING MY PHONE SCREEN WHILE I WAS RECORDING, SO I ONLY SAW ONE UFO DART ACROSS INITIALLY. IT MOVED SO FAST, MY EYES COULD HARDLY PERCEIVE IT...IT ALMOST SEEMED LIKE A BUG THAT FLEW ACROSS MY SCREEN, AND MAYBE I WAS JUST REALLY TIRED. OH WELL, PUT THE PHONE AWAY AND LETS GO TO WORK! I DIDN'T CHECK MY PHONE AGAIN UNTIL I WAS AT WORK, AND I REPLAYED THE VIDEO AND IMMEDIATELY SAW MORE UFOS. I REPLAYED THE VIDEO OVER, AND OVER, AND OVER, TRYING TO MAKE SENSE OF WHAT I WAS SEEING...AND THEN I FOUND I COULD ZOOM IN WHILE THE VIDEO WAS PLAYING, AND IT CONFIRMED ANY DOUBTS I HAD. UPON ZOOMING INTO THE VIDEO, THE UFOS BECOME MORE CLEAR IN DEFINITION AND IN PATTERN. TO CONFIRM I WASN'T LOOKING AT LIGHT REFRACTION'S EITHER, ONE OF THE UFOS EVEN DARTS BEHIND THE TOP OF THE SHERATON BUILDING, DISAPPEARING MOMENTARILY BEHIND THE BUILDING, AND THEN REAPPEARING AGAIN. I WAS THE ONLY WITNESS TO THIS EVENT AND EVEN I DID NOT BELIEVE WHAT MY OWN EYES HAD SEEN, IT TOOK ME AROUND 12 HOURS OF WATCHING THE VIDEO TO FULLY ACCEPT THAT I HAD JUST WITNESSED UFOS, AND WEEKS OF RE-WATCHING IT TO COME FORTH WITH IT. WHEN I LOOKED AT THE VERY FIRST PHOTO I TOOK, THE PHOTO BEFORE I RECORDED VIDEO, YOU CAN SEE MULTIPLE UFOS HOVERING OVER DOWNTOWN. THE CLEAREST DEFINITION OF THE UFOS I FOUND WAS IN TAKING "SCREENSHOTS" OF THE 9 SECOND VIDEO CLIP AND ZOOMING IN. THE UFOS APPEAR TO HAVE CLEAR, DEFINITIVE PATTERNS, SHAPES, AND LINES. THEY ARE GLOWING BRIGHTER THAN ANYTHING I HAVE EVER SEEN IN THE SKY. I LOST SIGHT OF THE OBJECTS AS SOON AS THEY WERE OUT OF MY IPHONE CAMERA'S LINE-OF-SIGHT.
