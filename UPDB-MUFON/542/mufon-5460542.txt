Glowing Orange sphere following small single engine plane

Having a smoke on my back deck I notice a orange star (sphere) stationary in the sky over Lake Simcoe. When a small plane passed by, the orange sphere began to follow the plane north, up the lakes coastline, until it was out of my vision.

I have seen this orange glowing sphere almost daily for the last month. It appears to be a orange star, until it starts to move, usually in a eastern direction for a few minutes, then shoots straight up, and disappears. May 27th is when I observed the object following the small plane.
