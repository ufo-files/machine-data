Looking at Mercury during sunrise.

While looking at Mercury during the sunrise we saw a very bright light. It moved side to side, in a hovering manner. It was not vectoring as if on a flight path. It hovered back and forth a few times then went very dim. After a short moment it was completely out of sight. It never appeared to move closer or further. It simply dimmed down until there was nothing left.
