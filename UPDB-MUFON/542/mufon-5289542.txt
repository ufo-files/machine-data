Starlike light traveling from north to south. Then slowed and stopped. Then diminished to darkness.

Looking at the sky(Big Dipper) when what I thought was a satellite passed through the cup of the dipper. I had never watched a satellite cross the sky before, and figured I had nothing to do, so I’d watch it as far as I could. It was moving fast; faster than I thought it would. As I faced west watching this white light cross the sky it got to about 11 o’clock ( southwest sky) and the light slowed. That made me question if I was watching maybe a plane. But instead of banking and turning it stopped dead. It stayed motionless for 20 seconds or so. After that little bit of time questioning what I saw the light intensified a bit, then faded into the black early morning sky. Have been searching the sky intently ever since.
