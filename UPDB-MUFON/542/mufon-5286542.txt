Pulsating lights hidden in clouds over the Tijuana river

I was driving back home in Imperial Beach when I saw the clouds either near or over the Tijuana river silently pulsating in a series of red and blue lights. There were no noises either and I did not see exactly when it started, nor ended as it continued when I left. The colors seemed to be illuminating from the inside of the clouds rather then it being lit by a spotlight or something else from the ground as there were no apparent rays of light coming from the ground. Also, the area surrounding the Tijuana river is the wilderness and there is hardly anyone around there in the day, let alone at night. I managed to catch a clip of it as well. Check it out.
