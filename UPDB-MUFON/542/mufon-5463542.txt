{dr}Glowing disc, hovering then quickly took off, faster than any shooting star or plane.

At the time of the event I had stepped outside to get phone service, my friend had called. We were talking and the phone started cutting out so i looked up at the stars in agony. There i seen a large disc glowing a bright light blue, like LED lighting blue. a first I thought it was a plane, but it stayed still hovering. I had hung up the phone and started at it and then it took off. I had been weary to say anything to my father because I thought he would not believe me. After it left there was a black helicopter that put a spotlight on me and was only about 50 feet off the ground. I went inside freaked out and there was a red beam coming through the window so I hid.
