{cak} 3-4 Amber glowing orbs hovering over the ocean rotating around each other. Lasted 15-20 secs.

Oak Island Beach 9:40 pm 5/15/2018 4 wittnesses.Walking on the beach at night. One witness noticed unusual orb appearing and pointed out to others in the group.2 Amber goldish orbs appeared over the oceans horizon. 1 more appear rotating over the other 2 orbs. Lasted for 15-20 secs. At first the group thought it a distress boat or airplane. Waited for 10 min to ensure it was not flares from a distress boat. Everyone was in awe and confused as to what cause orbs to appear. 
We were concerned someone was in trouble. We had flashlights and try to beam them over the ocean to see if there were any boats. None were seen. As we were leaving the beach 1 appear again over the original sighting only lasted for 5 secs. The orbs were pulsating and dimed out sight.
