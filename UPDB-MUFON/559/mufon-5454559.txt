Glowing orbs

3 glowing, high in the sky approached each other from different directions, directionally as if each were approaching from the corner of an equilateral triangle toward the centre. The approach speed was close to fast moving aeroplane I’d guess. Once close to each other they paused for maybe 10 seconds then returned back toward the are they came from at extremely high velocity.
