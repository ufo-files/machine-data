{cak} I saw a white/orange sphere in the night sky on April 5, 2015

It was around 1:30 am on April 5, 2015. I was standing on my patio deck smoking. When I noticed this super bright light in the northwest sky. I knew that it was not a star due to its size compared to the stars around it. The object was also white and orange sphere shape like the color of an orange with a white mist surrounding it? This things hovered and moved up and down very slowly. I grabbed my iPhone and took photos and videos! When I looked at the object through the phone camera lens in video record it looked so different all white no orange color. However you could see movement and heat or energy surrounding the object. To get even stranger the object began to pulsate very intensely and ascend and descend at incredible speeds! I have numerous photos and videos of this object it was around for almost a week doing incredible things. I know I won't be the same since witnessing this awesome event.
