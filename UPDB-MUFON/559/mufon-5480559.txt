{jpg} Bright light fades slowly fading intensity

Exactly at 8:25pm I notice a light in the sky, similar to seeing a satellite except this brightened and faded significantly, and it's trajectory was exactly due north. 10 minutes later, in the very near vicinity of where the original object faded, a bright pulse/flash of light appeared, and this object faded very slowly, traveling very slowly and on a meandering path to the South Southeast. The object's path weaved almost on a sine.

This is the 5th time in the past month I've seen these phenomena.
