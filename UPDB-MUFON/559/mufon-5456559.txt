On Sunday the 29 of September 2019, my wife and I were driving west bound on Williams from ih35 towards fm3405 when wife pointed out a very bright light. First thing I saw tho, was a strange rectangular cloud with a Squared off hole in the middle. The light (orb) was just below the hole and just in front of the cloud. We watched it move back and forth, left to right and back again. We saw the orb disappear and reappear 3 times before disappearing for the last time. The huge "cloud " remained stationary until we turned onto FM 3405. It was still almost directly behind us. As it was still light out there was no mistaking what we saw.
