UFO came South over house. Stopped around 25 to 30 Feet in Air over me. Seen 3 human figures in Windows.

Seen UFO in distanced doing impossible Flight patterns. It disappeared then going North. Then a few minutes later the Disc came South over a House 30 to 40 Feet from the Ground.
