{fr}Light orbs moving in sky by the thousands, appearing as stars

Recently I began seeing the objects in groups of 7-10, at first appearing as stars, but then very obviously moving slowly in up and down and back and forth. Sometimes moving in sync with one another. Sometimes making short darting movements. They seem to hover in the same general space. They do not move over long distances. When they make an upward motion it is liken to the motion of a jellyfish. They seem to change glow from white to red, but not a consistent blink as an airplane would be. Some of them remain the same with only a white glow. It has gotten to the point that I cannot find a "star" that isn't making the same motion. I gave up on watching individual bodies of "stars" that wouldn't move in very obvious ways. Every heavenly body I focus on undeniably moves. I watch the sky every night. I have for years. I know how stars and the night sky should appear. Stars don't move. I took the approach to look for constellations. I found Pleiades. I was relieved until it literally rearranged itself and dispersed, almost as if intentionally. There are legions of them, all across the sky.  It's been going on for 2 nights now. I'm seeing it from my yard. I smoke and watch the sky. I don't believe it myself and have no explanation. If there is some other rational or logical explanation I would GLADLY accept it. I live in a rural wooded area. I had to put it out there somewhere, somehow, so I'm reporting it here. I hope and pray with sincerity that this information will be useful for some good cause, regardless if it offers me any explanation or not. I state the above with complete and total honesty and integrity.
