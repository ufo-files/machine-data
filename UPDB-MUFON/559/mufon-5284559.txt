I saw a tic tac shape hovering over the mountain tops.

On a clear, light afternoon with no cloud cover or rain, while driving on Interstate 90W between Superior and St. Regis, MT, I looked out the passenger window at the sky and saw a white tic tac shape above the mountain tops. It didn't seem to be moving and did not look like an airplane. I got my phone and opened the camera app, snapped a quick photo, then showed my husband - maybe 10 seconds total. I turned back to get another picture and the shape was gone!
