Large silent capital V shape headed south, had no lights and appeared cloaked so that underside looked like stars above it.

This sighting is being reported by Investigator Beverly Trout, as related to Trout by the witness to Des Moines, Iowa Case No. 35820. Sighting actually occurred in Taylorsville, Utah, probably about September 10, 2005, at around 9:00 p.m. with other witnesses also present and acknowledging what was seen. Object was seen at 80-85 degrees elevation. Itwas silent and appeared to be in shape of large capital V, as it headed south, at possibly 15,000-17,000 feet altitude. Witness indicates it was clear night and he could see stars in the sky, but his attention was drawn by his change in depth perception, and then he saw that the bottom of the object showed the stars that should have been beyond and above the object. His terminology was that he wondered if it was some kind of cloaking.
