Light was over Lake Simcoe Ontario coming from sw straight at us .seemed fairly low and very bright .was traveling towards us for about 5ish min

10 pm wed evening went out on my deck for a smoke before bed ..we live on the waters edge of Lake Simcoe, in Ontario, Oro Medonte. Looking over the water there was a object that was bouncing up and down, was large enough to see and was low as a small plane flying low over the water - this was not a plane or Chinese lantern. It was bright redish colour coming towards us. I got out my binocular and took a better look and appeared more clearish white colour. It moved fairly quickly and like a ball of fire, as it moved towards us a tree was obstructing the view, so ran down onto the grass to continuing watching it and it disappear completely.
