{nd} bonfire moving through the air quietly... but fire doesn't fly?

We saw a large yellowish/orange fire like a bonfire moving through the air slowly from a NE direction headed SE. It was silent and pulsating like a burning fire. It did not ascend or descend at all and went out of our sight (behind buildings and trees) after it had traveled approximately 1.5 miles. It was silent and had no other lights. We couldn't see the outline of the craft itself at all.
