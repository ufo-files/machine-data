Looks like a manta ray.

Madeleine and me (Andr1) were driving on Hwy10, going south. At kilometer 40 close to Farnham and the military base, less than 1000 ft on the right side of the road, in a field, we saw a very big object hovered very very slowly. The shape of it remains me a manta ray (the fish). I cannot tell the lenght of the object but it was wide ( including the wings) like the wingspan of a B737. Under we can see a turbine engine running slowly time clock wise. The shape of the wings were tapered, not large and had position lights like the planes. The color was metallic grey. In the front of it, I saw few windows like a plane cockpit. We think the object was analysing the soil. Less than a second, the object was gone, flying away at a very high speed. Madeleine and me had cameras on the dash but we didn1t have the time to take a picture.

Note: Andr1 is an active private pilot. 

My friend Andr1 was very impressed by the sighting.
