Three silver triangles in formation turn an exit horizon at incredible speed

The date listed is not exact but it it close. I saw a glint in the sky and looked up and saw what appeared to be a normal formation of delta wing jets except it looked more triangular and there were no aircraft lights. I watched as they moved slowly across the sky with no contrails. After about a minute, the formation abruptly changed direction (without changing orientation) and shot across the sky in an almost reverse direction. The speed was like watching a shooting star. They shot across the entire horizon and disappeared.
