{cak} Black triangle gliding LOW, SILENT, and FAST.

I had just arrived home on a perfectly clear night. As I walked to my apartment, I glanced up and saw the UFO. It was a black triangle with very dim, non-blinking lights in the corners (the lights looking like stars themselves). Because of the craft lights, and I guess lights on the ground, I could just barely make out the black shape. But it almost blended in with the night sky. I keep wanting to say it was gliding instead of flying. Because it was just moving so fast and in COMPLETE silence. It still seems so surreal just because the speed, altitude and (lack of) sound go against everything I've ever seen.

I live in an area with airplanes and some helicopters. I know what they look like and how they sound. This was not one of them. It was either a UFO in the popular sense, or some kind of highly advanced stealth aircraft. I honestly halfway feel like a crazy person. I mean, I have a science background and believe in other life out there. And I know governments have highly advanced tech they keep out of the public eye. But damn, this was one wild sight.
