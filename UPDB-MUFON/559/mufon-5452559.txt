Intelligent Orb

On 05/25/2020 at approximately 23:15 I witnessed a softball sized orb flashing in the backyard of my residence. At a distance I assumed the flashing was a lightning bug. As the orb approached the window I was observing it stopped moving and started glowing with a blueish white light. At this point it stayed stationary for a few seconds right in front of me. At this time there was a thunderstorm approximately twenty miles from my location so I assumed this was just ball lightning. Then a bright beam of white fast flickering light appeared and scanned from my six o clock to my twelve o clock. Once it reached the top of the window frame the flickering light stopped and the glowing object shot up and exited my field of vision.
