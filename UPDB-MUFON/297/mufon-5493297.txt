{nd} Orb appeared to rise from behind trees, Hovered, then moved from NNW to SW

On the evening of June 6th, 2015 at 9:05pm EST I stepped onto my fire escape to have a cigarette. I noticed what I thought was a Roman Candle ascending from behind the trees and buildings to the NNE. When the orb did not burst, or burn out, but instead slowed and seemed to hover for a few seconds I became curious. I pulled out my cellphone and started filming. The orb seemed to continue hovering for a few seconds then started moving from NNE to SW and seemed to accelerate as time progressed. The orb faded out after about a minute and 50 seconds from when I first saw it.
