{cak} 3 lights in formation suddenly descending in 3 separate paths.

Sometime around 1973 to 1974 myself and at least 3 other friends were in a field across the street from one friends house. I saw what looked like 3 lights of an airplane in the distance above the treetops but the colors didn't look right and couldn't hear any sound. I pointed it out to the others but they just disregarded it as a plane. I kept watching and suddenly each of the lights descended in different none linear paths, again no sounds heard. None of the others had seen the descent but they could no longer see what they thought was an airplane. After discussing the event for a few minutes we took a drive out to the direction I saw it. 
We ended up out in the area of Barre Falls Dam (which is probably a bit west of where we should have been). We stopped at a clearing out there near a dike. About 20 minutes went by and 1 maybe 2 helicopters flew over slightly to our east followed by a large plane with search flood lights shining down on the area.

There was never anything on the news or in the papers so I was wondering if there was any record from like Pease Air Force Base that sent out search planes or anyone else that may have reported something.
