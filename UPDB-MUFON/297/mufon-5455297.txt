{cak} Enormous structure dimmed to single light at treeline.

While driving I witnessed a massive quarry-like structure in the field with multiple orange lit arms and the such. I figured it was a group of cranes and equipment working overnight. But I never remember there being a site like that on the road. I drove by there many times before. As my vehicle came closer all of the lights dimmed to darkness. A single light, the height of a tree remained. It dimmed out and reappeared about a 100 feet away from where it was heading west. It continued this pattern about 4 more times until it disappeared. I drove back passed it hundreds of times afterwards on my way to work and found no quarry or massive lit structures in the day or night ever again.
