{cak} Flying blinking light

Today around 22:45, when I wanted to close the window, I noticed a bright white blinking light in the sky. Because of the darkness, I couldn't see the object itself, but the light was way to bright to be position lights of an airplane. It flew in a straight path below the clouds in northwestern direction and it blinked approximately in a 1-second-rhythm. I could observe the object for round about three minutes from different windows of my house until it got out of my sight behind a group of trees.
