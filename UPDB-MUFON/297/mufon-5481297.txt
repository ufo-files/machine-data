{rjl} We took pics of an ominous looking storm approaching from the West, didn't see the orbs until later when viewing the photos.

My wife and kids and I were standing outside a small shopping center and we were looking up at the sky towards the west because the winds had picked up and we noticed an ominous looking storm that was really intimidating looking. I grabbed my camera from nearby and snapped some photos of what I thought was a pretty cool looking storm front. After viewing the pictures though, I noticed two pictures showing orange colored orbs with definite shape. One picture even shows the orb with a tail like a comet as if it was rapidly traveling from right to left from the viewers position. When you zoom in on the pictures you can clearly see a perfectly round and solid object which defies traditional explanation.
