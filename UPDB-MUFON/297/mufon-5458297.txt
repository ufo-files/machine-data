{jpg} Strange light caught on doorbell camera.

Reviewing doorbell camera footage from the previous night, I noticed this light that resembled a meteor except that it changed direction. Initially, I was shocked but then looked for a logical explanation. First thing that came to mind was a bug. I get bugs on camera all the time and none have looked like this. When this first becomes visible, it looks almost like a meteor (which I have also caught on camera), but the sudden change in direction is what makes this incredible. It is almost like a skipping stone - I am not sure if a meteor could behave like that, I have only seen fireballs and quick, straight line meteors. Look between the flag and the pillar, closer to the left pillar. The object comes into frame from right to left - seems to be coming from further out, then down and left.
