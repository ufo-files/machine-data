{nd} 4th of July ufo

My husband and I were out on our back deck watching fireworks, when he noticed a light. I was video tapping the fireworks and put my iPad toward where he told me. In the video you can see the fireworks, but keep your eye to the right lower corner. It was really strange, I have a few videos from that night and the ufo was in most of them. It just appears and disappears. There were no FAA lights. My husband is the biggest sceptic, but he was amazed. We saw one light.
