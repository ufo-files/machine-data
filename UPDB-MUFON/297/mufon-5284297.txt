Dull black wide triangle with multiple dull lights

[personal descriptors moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] if that helps to validate my story. Got out of my car at around 7pm and looked up because I was looking at multiple helicopters in the sky. Right away a large black triangle that was wide, like an obtuse angle, flew over my head. I saw multiple dull lights, anywhere from 7 to 20 lights within the triangle. I immediately knew I was looking at a UFO. The triangle moved so quickly and was very low. By the time I said "oh my gosh what is that!" It was gone past my neighbors tree line. It appeared to be large, but could of also been smaller than perceived because of how low it was. I also was unable to tell if it was one solid object, or a large fleet flying in formation. It seemed like one object, but it was so quick and I didn't have time to process what I was seeing before it was gone. Whatever it was, I am extremely confident that it was not of this earth.
