Round or disc like following a jet airliner.

My wife and I walked outside to get some sun and I noticed a airliner just to the north of our house traveling west to east. This caught my eye because we have lived at this address for over 30 years and we have never seen an airplane with this flight path. At first I thought it was an AWACS because it had this large disc shape on the top but then the plane began a 360 degree turn to the west and that is when this object moved up and away from the plane and dropped back as it and the plane made the turn heading back to the west. The object followed the plane changing elevations several times either at the same elevation as the plane or at a higher elevation than the plane. When it made the 360 turn it seemed to get bigger like it was disc shape and banking with the plane. We watched it for a good 5 minutes or so until the plane and the object was out of our sight. I would be interested to know if the radar towers at Spokane International Airport or Fairchild Airforce base picked this up with the plane.
