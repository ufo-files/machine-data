Thought it was a satellite...I was wrong!

First, I know the difference between planes and satellites. I like to go out and watch the sky on clear nights, and this was a 4 or 5 day run of crystal clear nights, not a cloud anywhere, low humidity, clear atmosphere.

First saw it almost directly overhead going almost west. Immediately thought satellite, but it looked to be moving a little faster than normal, maybe 15% faster. Watched for about 8 to 10 seconds as it got to about 40 degrees to the horizon, took a 90 degree turn and accelerated so fast it left a small streak and my eyes and neck could not keep up. I could not even estimate how fast it was because I have never seen anything that fast. Altitude is unknown, it could have been 15 miles, or 150 miles, bit it was way up there.

It took me 3 weeks to even tell anyone, but I know what I saw and I can't explain it.
