I witnessed 6+ bright flying lights hovering and maneuvering over an area between Stamford CT and NYC

I witnessed six or more glowing lights from my apartment on the 13th floor of a high rise in Connecticut. These orbs were flying in seemingly random directions above an area facing the NYC metropolitan area. These lights then began to form what seemed to be flight formations. After approximately 25 minutes the lights disappeared one by one. I lost sight of if they took off higher or landed. I took several photos of the lights. I have not seen anything like this in my 27 years living in the NYC and CT area.
