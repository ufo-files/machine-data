pulsating or blinking lights in the sky with no sound.

while out capturing night shots for a photography course (at around 2.20am) i happened to see i pulsing or blinking light or lights out of the corner of my eye. i looked up at it and it carried on pulsing for approx 5 or so sec, then just vanished. as you can see from the pics, its a clear crisp night with not a cloud in sight.you can see multiple square orange objects that look nothing like the motion blurred stars behind. this shot was a 30 sec exposure taken with a canon eos 70D on a tripod using a 2sec timer shutter release. i have filed a report with Ufocusnz.org.nz and have sent them a copy of the Raw image also. i have had someone suggest it was the ISS or satelite, but it would have appeared as a streak or stripe if that was the case, looking closely it appears to be in our atmoshere, not orbiting. im guessing somewhere between 2 and 10km from my location?
