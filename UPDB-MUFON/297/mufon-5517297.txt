(gs) Three red lights one overtaking other, suddenly canting collor and disappearing

Cleanijng the driveway after fireworks observed three lights west of Wheaton. Initially two, then the third appeared. All were bright red, but not flashing like airplanes, there were no other lights (like white that airplanes have as well). Light number one was closest to me, light number two was slightly behind, then third light appeared in the distance. all the same steady red color. Light number two sped up and started gaining on light number one. They seemingly meted and then separated. All of a sudden one of the lights disappeared. I looked to see where the third light was but it disappeared as well. I went back to watching the remaining light. It started to change color from bright red to bright white. When it became bright white it seemingly turned up toward the sky and disappeared in the middle of my field of vision.Strange...
