I was driving my vespa on the SS Sorrentina towards Vico Equense and my son noticed a light still in the sky. I stopped and together kept watching the object that was still for few minutes and I took a couple of pictures. The shape of the object was like a V upside down, grey colored, with a couple of white trial below. Than I noticed the object started moving and than I took a videoclip during wich I noticed the object disappeared (see the attached videoclip).
