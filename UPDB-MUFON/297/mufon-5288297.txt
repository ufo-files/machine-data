Observed light to the east at sunrise

April 23d 2021, 0617hrs. 
I was taking morning cloud pictures and took 2 pics to the east, on the horizon. 

First was a portrait shot. I quickly turned the phone camera and took a landscape picture of the same unusual cloud formation. 

The first picture shows a light near the horizon on the bottom left of the cloud. The next picture, the orb seems to have ascended to a position above and to the right of the distant cloud formation. 

A couple seconds difference in pictures. I didn't notice the orbs until after I looked at the pictures. This cloud may have been engine exhaust from a rocket that launched before 6am that morning
