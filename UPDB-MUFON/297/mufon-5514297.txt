(GS) 2 dark glowing golden orange balls hovering to the North

1. Where were you and what were you doing at the time?
I was walking through the backyard with my husband. 

2. What made you first notice the object?
My husband, Jess Killion, yelled "Wait! Look!" and pointed up and to the North.

3. What did you think the object was when you first noticed it?
Very close bright stars hanging in the night sky - which was clear.

4. Describe the object and its actions and motions in detail.
Two dark glowing golden orange balls that hovered in the sky. After almost 15 seconds of observation, the bottom one disappeared, then the top one seconds after.

5. Describe your feelings, reactions and actions, during and after sighting the object.
Shock and confusion. I, nor my husband, do not know what they were.

6. How did you lose sight of the object?
The objects no longer glowed. They disappeared.

My husband had mentioned flares, but there were no jets in sight at the time and the skies were clear. He even admitted "Those things did not look like flares. I don't know what they were." Within several minutes while we stood there and discussed what we saw, a large jet (with a light on its tale), flew over our location and headed north to where we saw the orbs.
