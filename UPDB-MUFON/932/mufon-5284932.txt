Basketball sized fast moving orb

UFO PROBE/ORB
10-07-21: Today while traveling south on HWY 91. Just north of Brattleboro at 2:30PM. A basketball sized unknown flying object/probe thing came into view very close to the front of my vehicle. This was no human made drone. It’s movement was far beyond anything I have ever heard of or witnessed before.
Imagine the way a pinball moves when in rapid play and a basketball sized hummingbird but able to move 100 times faster. Omnidirectional changes in fractions of a second.

This, whatever it is. Came into view, paused for a moment, flew backwards for a second. As it seemed to be stationary for a moment as I was traveling at 60 miles an hour. Then flew directly at my windshield. As it got within a few feet of my windshield, it shot straight up and was gone.

This was the second time I had seen this thing within a span of 4 minutes. The first time was at a distance of 30 to 40 meters ahead of me. It basically did the same thing, but to another car in the other lane. When I seen this happen, I was totally puzzled as to what in the world it could be. Then I had my closeup experience with it a few minutes later.

Now I will do my best to describe the physical look of this object. It actually had the appearance like that of a water skipper bug. A really big one! About 12” to 14” in length. With a halo of blurry white that seemed to surround it. But it wasn’t flying flat, it flew upright. Like a hand being pushed into your face. Drones can’t fly that way, man made ones anyway.
