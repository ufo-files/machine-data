{rjl} Showed up in photo above remote lake , above treeline. Has vortices above and below.

Was with family at Lake above Creede, CO. No other visitors to lake. Elevation of Love Lake, approximately 11,00 ft. Family homestead in Creede. Standing next to son, taking photos of mountains and some white clouds (puffy) reflecting in lake. When finished shooting a few cell photos, looked at cell and discovered a craft in sky above treeline, over near mountain peak. The craft appears to be traveling vertically in the photo with what appear to be "vortices" above and below the disc. It was not visible to the naked eye, as the five of us were standing beside the lake looking at the sky/mountain/lake we were photographing.
