Dark object flying to the s/w towords JFK airport.

While in the back yard in the hopes of seeing a meteor shower I saw an objectthat looked identicle to a group, 6-9, of black helium balloons, strungtogether, and moving quickly acroos the sky. The only difference is instead ofthe balloons being vertical, one atop the other, as would be expected of heliumballoons - they were, and remained, in a fixed horizontal position as it movedto the s/w from the n/e, towards JFK Intl Airport. No wobble, no sound, nolights. Exactly like balloos but too perfectly a horizontal position26 flight. Balloons would have been rising26 the wind was calm which is why the speed26 horizontal travel was odd. Unfortunately I have only estimated the month - Ido not recall which meteor shower I hoped to see. May have been Leonids, notsure.
