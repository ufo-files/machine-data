{rjl} Saw an amber colored half-moon shaped object that matched the street lights exactly, but was huge!

My bedroom window faces due east.Because it faces a very busy street,I always keep my blinds drawn.One of the slats of the blinds got caught on the window sill when I made my bed which is next to it the prior day or I never would have seen what I did.At first, I thought I saw a street light where there never was one before. This was from a vantage point of sitting across from the window around 8 feet away.At first, the light seemed small and I questioned whether or not it was a reflection on my window.When I went to the window for a closer look,I could see that it was shaped vaguely like a half moon, but was tilted a bit to one side. It took up about 2 inches of space in the sky. The most notable thing about the silent, stock still object was that it was a solid amber color that perfectly matched the streetlights. It hovered much lower in the sky and unlike the moon, it had no variations in its color as the moon does. I then went into my dining room east facing window and watched it for about 3 minutes.When I left that room to go back to my bedroom to look at it, it was gone!There wasn't any cloud cover and when I scanned the expanse of sky entirely, it just disappeared.
