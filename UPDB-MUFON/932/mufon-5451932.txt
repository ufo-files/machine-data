Bright Light witnessed by brother’s family

My brothers account: Date was May 18th about 8:50 PM when I walked past my glass back door and notice an unusually bright light just above the trees to my Northwest. I walk outside to get a better look, then after a few minutes decide to start filming. No sound, minimal movement besides a tight circular hover. Took three different videos over about twenty minutes, then went to get my big telescope. When I came back outside after about a minute it was gone.
