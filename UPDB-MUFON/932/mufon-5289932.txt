Very bright light hubbering above my house

Got home after shift work at the sheriff's office 430pm to 430 am and set outside to unwind before going to sleep. While outside the back patio, I observed a very bright light approaching my home coming straight at me. I originally thought it was the JSO sheriff's helicopter maybe looking for something or someone, but the realized it wasn't making any noise at all. The light stopped well above the tree line, hubbered for at least 10 or so seconds then flew up and a very high speed and disappeared. It was a nice and clear night, no planes flying around and I asked my friends at JSO and the helicopter wasn't flying that morning.
