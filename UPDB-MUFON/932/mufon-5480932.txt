{cak} UFO discovered while viewing hiking photos after the fact

My wife and I were hiking and had reached Anderson Pass, the saddle next to King's Peak, which is the highest peak in Utah. The area is very remote.  I stood on the saddle and took pictures of the horizon in every direction.  When I got home and, three weeks later, was viewing the pictures on my large computer screen, I noticed this object in the sky.  I can't describe it as anything other than a UFO.
