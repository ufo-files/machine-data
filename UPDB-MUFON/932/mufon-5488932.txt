{rjl} lights flickered, path wobbled, abruptly disappeared or lights went out

Walked out front door of home to take dog outside. Noticed flickering lights that did not resemble a plane's lights or satellite or ISS. The path wobbled but not particularly fast or slow. Went back into house for binoculars. With binoculars, could see chevron or boomerang shape. Path started from SW and headed south to north. It seemed to go behind a cloud and then all lights [and shape] disappeared. Rather than thinking what if might be, it was more like what it was not. Feelings were "wow" and "I wish I could see more".
