Round shape looking like a plane but not right.

I was headed south on 25 towards Abq. I noticed I. The sky a white object that I figured was a plane and I thought that I should be able to see wings and blinking lights. But maybe not. Then I notes ti the east a very similar object that I couldn’t make out wings or lights. This one seemed closer. I looked back to see the first one but it was gone and I looked back to see the second and it was gone. 
I seriously felt like I was seeing something I was being shown and I knew when I looked back from the second to the first that it would be gone and I knew the second would be gone when I looked back as well. 
There were planes in the sky soon after with jet streams showing (very normal) the Abq airport is south the way I was looking at first so I didn’t think much when I saw the object at first but something was very odd about the whole event. 
I could never find the objects in the sky after that and that is the crazy part. The flight paths were right there and obvious with nowhere to hide or disappear behind anything. 
I’m making this report hoping someone else will to.
