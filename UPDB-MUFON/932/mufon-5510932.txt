(GS) Went out front of my house and saw five lights in a triangle shape with a flashing red/orange light in the center

I walked out front of my house at 10:30pm and the object immediately caught my eye, it was flying very low and had no sound, 5 lights in a triangle/ warped rectangle shape with a red pulsating light in the center. I watched the object fly from west to east over west Wichita. I saw other airplanes in the area and also saw and heard jet aircraft very high in altitude, all the planes I saw in the area had flashing red and blue lights with a strobe like characteristics. "Normal looking" this object had a very slow pulsating red and orange light in the center and looked very abnormal and different than any other aircraft Ive seen before.
