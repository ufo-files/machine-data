{cak} lights at satellite orbit in at regular intervals

At 5 Am Easter Sunday AM I let my dogs out and at 1st I thought oh cool at satellite. Then I saw another and another light. These lights just kept coming traveling NW to SE. About every 10th one a smaller light would "float" off and go east.The smaller lights were not as bright. I do not know how long these lights were happening before I spotted them at 5 AM. I watched them off and on until day light. I would say a total of approx 30 minutes. I racked my brain what could these lights be? Military, commercial planes..everything I thought could not fly as high as a satellite. I mean do we have that many satellites and if so why do I not see them more often. I am outside before daylight a lot.
