{kt}Very large craft hovering over a field as I was driving by

I was heading east north east on Highway 67 in Oaklandon, I had just left a restaurant after celebrating my godfather's birthday. The road curves north for a brief moment, and I noticed three really bright lights in a triangular pattern to my north east. I thought, "It's just a plane, but that's really low." I start looking at it more and more as I got closer, and as the road bends back to the north east, I was able to see several more, smaller lights (but not of any color, and no red or green light signaling any sort of wing) around the three large lights, which I could then tell were pulsating. I couldn't make out any sort of lines that would give off the shape of the object. I came to the stoplight between McCordsville and Oaklandon (it was red), so I turned the heater and radio off and rolled down my window to see if I could hear anything. By this point, the object was slightly behind and to the left of me. It couldn't have been more than a few hundred feet away from me, but it was absolutely silent. I know planes do not hover, and I know helicopters are audible from the distance I was from this object.
