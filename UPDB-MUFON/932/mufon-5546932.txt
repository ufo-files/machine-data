Bright star near moon just after sunset

I submitted Mufon report #18131 back in July - my third UFO sighting (1st in '75, 2nd in '98) and after attending a Labor Day gathering whereby various neighbors described having seen during the last 2-3 yrs in our area: a saucer, cigar, and orange orbs in addition to a large bright green ball descending over the Jemez, I have kept a vigilant eye on the sky. Additionally, there was Mufon report #19550 of a glowing orb which was about 5 miles away from me although I did not see it and it did not involve anyone I know. Anyway, on the eve of Sept. 28, I had to go to the Mini-Mart in the little village of Placitas located at the northern base of the Sandia mountains which run north-to-south on the east side of Albuquerque, before the store closed at 8pm. As I drove the 3 1/2 miles south to the mini-mart, I watched the moon and Jupiter which were very bright as they rose up and to the west over the Sandias. I recall looking at the car clock when I arrived at the mini-mart and it said 7:30, so I purchased what I needed and headed home. As the road is very winding, it takes about 15 mins to drive the short 3 1/2 miles back. Once I made the turn onto my street - which runs due west - I looked left to admire the moon again and this time I saw a very bright "star" located about 10-15 degrees below the moon and 10 degrees to the west/right of that point, as I looked south. Jupiter was to the left/east and a tad below the moon while this other "star" was almost at the same spot to the right/west of the moon. It was a tiny bit brighter than Jupiter, and I knew it wasn't a plane and as I was only a hundred yards from home, I slammed on the gas pedal so I could get to my binoculars ASAP. However, in the 15 seconds it took me to get home, unlock the house, grab my binoculars and run outside the "star" was gone - nor was there any trace of a plane or any other flying object in the area. I wondered at first if I should even bother filing such a quick sighting but after I read 2 other similar Mufon reports which also occurred on Sept. 28th, near the US east coast describing almost the same thing at the same hour (only EST), I thought it might have some significance. Lastly, I hope to get my neighbors to file their respective sightings w/ Mufon. Thanks.
