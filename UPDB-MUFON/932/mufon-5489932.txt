Saw a ufo hovering above treetops

I was on a field taking my dog for a walk, it was dusk and twilight. I looked around and saw a fighting thing similar to a size of a drone with two white-blueish lights above each other, and the object seemed metal. My mom saw other ufos in the nearby locations so I was on a lookout. It seemed like a drone with wing lights but it was in the middle of the filed with no people around. It flew smoothly and by the ease of motion appeared to be really light. The object seemed to hover and fly up and down and stared going my way but I stood and watched and it then started backing up and descended brow the treetops so I couldn't see it any more. I called my dog and started going the opposite the direction away from it. I felt sudden chill and paralysis, from fear I assume. I kept glancing back over my shoulder but I didn't see it again.
