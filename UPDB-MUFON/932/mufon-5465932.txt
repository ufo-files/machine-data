Outside with my dogs last trip out for the night. I caught sight of a very bright fast moving Object coming from the west heading due east at a very high Altitude. I have a bright flashlight I shined it at the Object knowing the light would not reach it. But it stopped, then it came back to the west straight overhead and it stopped lost Altitude came down to a much lower altitude maybe around 10000 feet hovered there for a few seconds then shot straight back up I lost sight of it for a few seconds. Then it went northeast made a very bright flash. stopped and went north out of sight moving Extremely fast.

When it came down to a lower altitude and hovered it was very large maybe a 1/4 acre wide and a half acre long kind of almond shaped. I could not believe what I was seeing then when it shot back up faster than anything I have ever seen I was shocked and confused never see anything move like it did. the flash was Extremely bright.
