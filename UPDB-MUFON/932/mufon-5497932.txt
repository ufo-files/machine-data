{cak} Saw a disk-shaped UFO a mile away; then disappeared; then an orb-shaped, smaller object came from same direction and flew over me

On Thursday, August 18, 2011, at approximately 9 PM, the witness was in front of his house pulling his garden hose around from the side of his house to water his front yard. As he entered the street beyond his yard he happened to look east down his street In the distance, he noticed a disk-shaped object moving just beyond a line of trees across the far fields at the end of his street. This was around a couple of miles away from him. The object had bright yellow lights around its perimeter and blue lights below those. The bottom displayed red lights.
