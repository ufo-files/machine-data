Two identically / separate dark silhouette diamond shaped UFOs -4 white lights on each extreme point- no sound.

In December 1978 I was serving in British Army on night infantry exercises in Pippingford Park Army Exercise area, Ashdown Forest, Sussex, England. One night between 1am & 4am we 
observed two brilliant white balls of light appear on the horizon hovering at about 500 to 1000ft about 3/4mile distant. The objects remained stationary - hovering (no sound) moving laterally away & towards each other. Each object then circled our position in opposite directions to each other. & flew directly over our covert position. Weather conditions were cold clear skies at time and situated in a very remote - isolated wooded area. We were laid up observing strict no noise/ blackout covert position & I saw one of objects fly directly above me. Object was dark silhouette diamond shape with one single white light at each point of diamond shape (no engine or flight noise heard). I saw these objects on three different occasions that night over period of about 3hrs & did not recognise them as a normal aircraft or helicopter. They seemed to be watching our operations & because they made zero noise I found this very strange & unnerving. The objects were observed by about 7 other colleagues and I personally reported it to senior NCO present. This is first time I have spoken about these events.
