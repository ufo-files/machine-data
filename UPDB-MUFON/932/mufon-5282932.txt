On Sunday 01/23/22 night I was outside the back patio having a cigarette around 9:30 pm . The night was pretty clear, stars were easily visible and I could see airplanes ( just the lights ) from a good distance. While gazing at the stars I noticed a faint object, it blended in perfectly with the night sky and only noticed it because it was a different shade..this only lasted for ten seconds then I lost sight of it, less than a minute later I was able to find it again, at this point it was just a strange shape, from a good distance away. It blended in almost perfectly with the night sky , just a few shades off especially when it passed in front of the stars..at this time I couldn’t recognize any particular shape, just a dark grey , almost black object moving north. There was no sound, no sudden change of direction, as I continued watching the object, now trying not to even blink, I was able to see clearly it was a solid object, a craft shaped like a boomerang, no visible light and no sound, no trail from propulsion, it was silently moving north and as it passed over the apartment building it was close enough for me to see its shape..
