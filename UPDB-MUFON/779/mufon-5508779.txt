{dr}Many starlike objects travelling low in sky going north, all slowly at same speed.

Went out my back door about 11:10 pm, looked to the left (east) and saw 10 - 15 reddish glowing twinkling starlike points of light traveling slowly at same speed to the north. I could see them pass between a house and a line of trees about 50 yards away. Impossible to tell how far away they were. They kept coming, passing behind the line of trees. I estimate 50 - 100 or more passed by in about ten minutes. They were not in a formation, just randomly passing by. After about ten minutes there were no more. I had no idea what they could be. I was not afraid, just amazed and curious.
