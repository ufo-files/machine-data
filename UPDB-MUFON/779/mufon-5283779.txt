Was a 2 encounter thing had letters come out of my skin

Me my roommate at the time had gone out side for a smoke and we’re sitting on a park bench looking at the mountains as the sun rose behind them, it was near 430am and the sun was hardly poking up behind the mountains there was a pink orangey and then a purple fading into the darkness of the night sky, the mountains were about 50km away, we both sat there and saw an object of about 5 inches come from the darkness to the purple and then pinky orange it was heading North to south and then on a sharp angle turned east towards the sun, as it did a second one appeared behind it and followed, that provoked us to spend the next two nights outside star watching, on the 2nd night after that we sat out all night and around 3-330am a star that had been over head but just to the west a bit became a ball of fire and made a sound almost like a jet but different and was around 100ft over us, as it came over head of us we could hear another sound like a deep woooowwoooowoooo(sorry i dont know how else to explain it) my friend asked “are we getting abducted?” I replied with an i dont know, as it flew over us and disappeared to the east for what ever reason we decided to go to bed I have no recollection If it flew over for 30 seconds or 3 hours, we then woke up at the exact same time the next morning and met at the bathroom at the same time, my friend had a scratch on his arm and I had the letters BARON protruding out of my forearm, at the time this kinda freaked me out and I went back to bed,when I woke up there was nothing on my arm and tried to recreate what was in my arm but nothing made any sense, I never really talked about it after but me and my old roommate talked about it for the first time since that day and it’s all came rushing back to me like it was yesterday
