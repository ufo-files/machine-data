Flashing, Morphing Disc moving N to S

Approximate time: Between 4:10 and 4:30 pm
Approximate coordinates: 
45.7833° N, 108.5007° W

Location: In the alley area that runs behind my apartment building in Billings, Montana. I was sitting facing due North. 

I saw what appeared to be a very shiny orb on the horizon, around -108.5 longitudinal coordinates, moving slowly up into the air, high and high, and further up into my view, heading slowly up into the atmosphere and heading due South. 

It looked like a larger than normal balloon at first, due to how it was quite shiny, almost a silver type of reflection. I was quick to note, however, that the object kept rising in a straight line into the air, oblivious to the wind or elements of the partly cloudy, yet pleasant Spring day, and was clearly far, far larger than a balloon.

As it became closer on the horizon I could see that the object appeared to be disc-like in shape, dark (grayish?) at times but also that it kept changing horizontal and vertical orientation, through morphs/flashes of warm/bright silver hues and very brilliant flashes. It felt quite light, quite floaty. 

The event took a few minutes, though, (as this was only Event 1 of Day 1 of what was to become a month long stretch of time of near daily sightings), I was ill-prepared without my camera on hand and do not recall the exact amount of time this entire thing took.

Throughout these few minutes, the object in the sky would occasionally pause, and then continued onwards, upwards, and into the clouds, still heading due South and out of sight.
