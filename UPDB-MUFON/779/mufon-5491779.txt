We were shooting off fireworks when we noticed 2 objects bright orange/red moving in random patterns. It had no strobe lights to indicate it was an aircraft. It moved in patterns an airplane could not do. It moved too fast for a helicopter to do the patterns it did. Plus they were glowing, almost a light pulse, but the color was red to orange. It also lit up some clouds it passed through. They eventually within 2-4 seconds just faded and were gone. After the two were gone a third was seen low in the tree line. We moved over to clear the trees and saw basically the same thing. It moved similar to the other two then climbed high and to our left, then looked like it moved away from us then 2-4 seconds it faded out quick. Total time that we observed these objects was 3-5 minutes. When reviewing a few pics we noticed a green bowtie looking thing in one of the pics. None of us saw this when observing the three glowing orb like objects.
