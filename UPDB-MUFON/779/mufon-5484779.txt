{nd} Object descended 1000 + ft. straight down very quickly within 3 seconds. Object had a Blue-ish green glow while coming down.

MY family and I were traveling North bound on State highway 249 looking for a place to stop and eat when we saw the event take place. We noticed that there was a car dealership in the distance about 1/2 mile up the road with a giant spot light revolving on the roof of the establishment. While exiting the interstate we noticed that the spot light had come across something in the sky and at that very moment the Object lit up as if it was on fire and descended straight down at a blistering speed. At first I thought it was a shooting star or fireworks but realized it couldn't be so due to the shear size, and trajectory of the object. I Wasn't really scared the moment I recognized it but I was very intrigued, and sort of in shock. The object must have traveled 1000 within 2-3 seconds.. which is ridiculously fast in my book. The object disappeared about 100 ft above the ground never to be seen again that night.
