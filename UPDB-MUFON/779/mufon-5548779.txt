Rotating object flashing red, white and blue/purple in rotation

I stepped out on my porch and my husband told me there was something odd in the sky that he was watching. It appeared to be flashing red, blue and white as if it was rotating. It did not appear to be moving in any direction, just sitting in one spot. I watched it for a while and there was no change. I came back out later to see if it could have been a star that had moved. I could not locate the object about 30 minutes after the initial sighting.
