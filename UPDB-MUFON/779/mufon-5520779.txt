Saw object with four lights dash across the sky. Driving home from work saw same object trail my vehicle while seeming to hover. In a dash the object fell like a shooting star and went into the water like it was absorbed. I thought it was a comet

I was driving home from work on the south end of Boca Grande Island when I saw what looked like a shooting star stop in the sky. It showed four lights once and then not again.I made a few assuming thoughts in my head thinking it was talking to me and continued driving. Almost the time I made it to the only bridge off the Island, the object appeared again in my 3 oclock position. as I was looking out the passenger side of my vehicle I felt as if it was communicating with me. I kept driving and the object, having four lights in the distance, began to appear as what seemed to be a comet heading right towards the road. I was afraid that it was going to cause an explosion and veered off the road. At this time the object looked as if it fell into the water. There were very small waves, like I threw a rock, but not much larger, and I thought "wow" it was trying to scare me. It did.
