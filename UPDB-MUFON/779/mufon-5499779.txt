{jd} Object with red light floating and rotating in the sky while moving East to South

Ok so I have been really skeptical about UFO's or anything of that nature, but today was different. 

I sit on the porch with my 13th month old at night. This is something just me and him do together. We were sitting there looking at the airplanes because we live down the road from Charleston, International Airport. I just so happened to be following one of the planes and an object caught my eye. My wife knows I am interested in things of this nature and I did not want to see this alone. I opened the door and called my wife over and she verified what I was seeing. I watched it for about 10 mins along the sky thinking it could be a star and light could be bouncing off the different levels of atmosphere, but the more I focused on it the more I could see a solid object the same color as the sky just a fraction of darker blue. At one time the object flashed one really bright white light while continuing on its path. I could have just witnessed a star or a balloon, but it was possibly 1 1/2 miles away and visible.
