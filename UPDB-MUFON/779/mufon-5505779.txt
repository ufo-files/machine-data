{rjl} Shape was unknown as it was too dark. Looked as if there was an orange flame in the center. Smaller than a hellicopter.

Just got back from eating dinner and when we pulled into the driveway, we both noticed a very low orange light. Got out of the car and it slowly flew over us in a straight path. You could see an obvious orange glow that looked almost like a flame. It was just before 8pm in a west end suburb of Richmond, VA. My first thought was that it possibly was a drone they were testing. No sound what-so-ever. It was probably between 100 - 500 ft. above us. It came from the southeastern sky and went in a straight path until we lost sight of it over the trees in the northwestern sky.
