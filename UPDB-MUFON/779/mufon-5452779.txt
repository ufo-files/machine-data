When I was ten years old (1965) some friends and I were playing out side on Green street between Cottonwood and Hickory about 22:00. I noticed these lights staying in formation in the sky moving from north to sough in a slow straight line over head. There was no sound and I thought that was strange. I at first thought it was an Ozark Turboprop passenger airplane which routinely came to Kirksville to pick up passengers. But, there was no sound that I was familiar with related to the Ozark airplane. I thought that was strange, plus the object moved fairly slow. I can't remember is my friends that I was playing with noticed the lights. I don't remember if I told my parents. I definitely knew what the Ozark airplane sounded and looked like because I've seen it fly over in the day and at night. My dad used to take me to the Kirksville Airport to watch it land and take off, plus I had an Aunt from Los Angeles use the Ozark Airline to come home to Kirksville to visit her mom and dad. I went on line this week to see if there were any UFO sightings in/around 1966 in Kirksville and there were none.
