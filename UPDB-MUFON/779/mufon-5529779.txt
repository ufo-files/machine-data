Orange/Red glowing object moving forward, hovering, and turning to exit

I was on my driveway in Commack,NY and saw and orange/red glowing object in the sky (far East of my home). I first thought is was a plane on fire, but realized it was too large for that. The color was astonishing. The object moved west in the sky and I immediately got my wife as a witness. the object then began to move north and then seemed to stop and hover for about 15 seconds. It then turned and began moving away in an easterly direction and quickly vanished. When it turned to move away, the color had stopped and the object appeared white (as a star in the sky)
