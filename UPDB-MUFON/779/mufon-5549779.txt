5/18/02 Atlanta,GA. Unidentified Silver Sphere Sig

This is a report of my "silver sphere",seen on the afternoon of May 18th.2002.Itwas seen at 14:20 EDT as I sat in my backyard for a rest.I was facingnortheast,when I saw a glint in the sky,to the northwest.Unfortunately,I had nothung my binoculars on my neck, as I usually do.Strangely,I would best describeit as a ball bearing, flying through the sky.Visibility was excellent and thesun was shining brightly,with a clear,blue sky.Its speed was tremendouslyfast.This proved to me,that it could not have been a balloon(or anything elseIm familiar with). My observation lasted through 50 degrees,horizontally inonly 7 seconds,at most.I estimated that the craft would have travelled fromhorizon to horizon in less than 20 seconds.I also estimated that probably,aBB,held at arms length,would cover it well.It flew on a straight course, with nostops,starts,zig-zags,or any other course changes.This sighting was my fourthand none of the other sightings were in any way similar to this one.
