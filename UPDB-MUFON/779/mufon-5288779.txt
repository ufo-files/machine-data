Star like object disappeared

I was out with my dogs around 5:30am MST. I'm a star grazer and I could see what I think was Venus in the Eastern sky. The moon was above me to the south and about 3 fingers south of the moon was a star about the size and brightness of Venus. I watched it while I was thinking what it could be, maybe Jupiter. It wasn't moving so I didn't think it was a plane in the pre-dawn light. I looked down at my dogs for about 5 seconds and when I looked up the object was gone. I could see that it was in the area of two faint stars, but as the sky was getting lighter I couldn't make out any other stars in the area.
