Red/Orange blimp shaped object hovered over field North of main road

Seeing case 111356 has prompted me to submit my own sighting that I believe was the same object.

My brother and I recall being driven home by our parents one night along the A6006 from the Rempstone direction back towards home in Stanford on Soar when we all stopped and saw this object. 

We saw a red/orange blimp shaped object hovering in the field on the North side of the road as we were traveling West; this part of the road was open with no trees just short hedgerows lining the road and with open fields on both sides. We stopped at the side of the road a short distance before the turning to Stanford Hall and looked North over to this object hovering.

There was no sound, no apparent movement and no obvious sign that it was a blimp/airship or any kind of dirigible craft. It moved slowly to the West towards East Midlands Airport and we raced home to watch it continue moving that way from our back garden (which faced West).

We believe there were other witnesses as later we were told anecdotally by people we knew in the area that it had been "an airship".

If this does indeed match up to case 111356 then it's wholly reasonable to assume that the object did in fact travel West towards the villages of Hathern and Shepshed and thus the M1.

In trying to apply logic, I find it difficult to believe it was an airship as that location is within the East-West corridor for flights landing at East Midlands Airport and so it wouldn't make sense for a blimp to be in a flight path like that, nor would it travel towards the airport and potentially along side the M1 (such as in case 111356) without causing disruption to the airport.
