"I KNOW WHAT I SAW"...My Dad was Project Office Commander of Minuteman Missile at Vandenberg A.F.B., California, 1965-1969. My girlfriend, her father and I were riding horseback on a nearby hillside at VAFB Saddle Club. We stopped to rest our horses...and then all of a sudden...I look up into a clear, blue sky and observed a silver, saucer-shaped UFO with rotating lights of every color around the center, hovering about 300 feet in elevation at a Northerly 30 degree position. I yelled for Melanie and her Dad to "Check that out!!" Within seconds...the UFO do a strange "G" shaped maneuver, paused and shot off out over the missile silos, over the Pacific ocean and disappeared in an instant. The object made NO sound, and left NO trail. We all saw what we saw and Melanie's father excitedly said, "we NEED to tell your father!!!" (being in the Air Force and all, I guess) We galloped back to the barn, put the horses up and he hauled [language removed/cms/rjl] to my parents home on base. We all ran inside my home to find find my Dad on the couch reading and excitedly told him..."We just saw a UFO!!!" He stoically responded..."Okay...can I talk to my son alone??" He then told me...DO NOT EVER SPEAK OF THIS NON-EVENT TO ME ...EVER, AGAIN!!! AND, surprisingly...I NEVER talked about it again, until 30 years later.
