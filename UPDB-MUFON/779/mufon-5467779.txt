{rjl} Jet black monolith laying flat w/4 yellowish lights, 1 on each corner

I went outside with my 2 dogs when I noticed what I thought was a planet, like mars, through the trees to the southwest. Then realized there was more than one "planet", and it was too big to be Mars. Then counted 4 yellowish lights, 1 on each corner. I could make out a monolith type, rectangular craft at an angle from my location. The lights would look like a skewed diamond pattern if it were drawn from my perspective. The moon had already set but you could make out the darker shape, like when the moon is dark. It was stationary and quiet. No way of knowing how long it had been there? But at least after midnight. That was the last time the dogs wanted to go out. I stared at it for a few minutes, didn't think to grab my phone. Went inside for a minute to wake up my Dad and by that minute we came out and it was gone. He thought I was crazy. Never thought I'd ever see anything.
 The area it was over is Stokes State Forest. It was south of Meyers Rd. and west of 206. Hovering over the middle of the woods. There's 5 miles of woods in that direction.
 Oddly enough, it was that rare full moon on the solstice. I have not found any other reports of a rectangle at any time. It literally looked like the monolith from 2001 on it's side. Similar proportions. Like a black ice cream sandwich, but 20% wider.
 Odd thing. We're on a flight path from Stewart Airport (NY) to Philadelphia. A 4 engine cargo plane would fly daily, directly over my house, I'm guessing 10,000 ft. Heading southwest. Loud enough to drown out the outside stereo; for 3 or 4 years. This past 2 years, almost none. And there used to be a lot of small plane activity. Even one for a few years, every night around 3am. Cessna. Same flight path heading northwest. Now, hardly any.
 I estimate it had to be around 10,000 ft up, so it was really big. If it was that same altitude, I'd have to say it was at least 8 times bigger than those cargo planes.
