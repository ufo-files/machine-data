amazing colored lights arose from the western sky,large slow moving objects with no noise,slowly ascended as they went east changed color, then vanished

8:30 pm on sept.27 2011 I was outside smoking when I seen this icredibly bright orange red object coming over my neighbors house, then suddenly another appeared following the first. They were identicle in appearance.Moving very slowly almost hovering. then started moving east,after about 30 seconds I ran into the house to grab my camera. Knowing that the batteries were dead I yelled at my wife to hurry outside. she followed me and I pointed but she had already seen them. the lead one started ascending and the light got dimmer,almost white. After about another 30 seconds they both were disappearing and then they vanished heading east. i looked at the clock to get the correct time,it was 8:32.
