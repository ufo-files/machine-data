{dr}A bright orb-like object was traveling toward witnesses. It vanished suddenly approximately 1 minute after the initial sighting.

I was sitting on my couch watching TV when I looked out the living room window and spotted a bright orb-like object that appeared to be traveling directly toward me. It seemed to be moving about the same speed as an airplane, but the light did not blink. There was no visible emissions trail or anything of the sort. I yelled for my fiance to come look at the object. We watched it together for at least 30 seconds and it seemed to still be traveling directly toward us, but then it vanished suddenly. My fiance grabbed our binoculars and went outside to try to find the object in the sky, but was unable to.
