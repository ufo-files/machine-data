{jpg} Glowing yellow orange circular shape

I was sitting on my patio and noticed a glowing yellow orange ball gliding across the sky. It disappeared behind a tree and a 2nd similar object appeared from the same direction the 1st one had come from.  It went behind the same tree and a 3rd one appeared from the same direction.  I went to the front yard and watched this 3rd ball just vanish. This repeated 7 or 8 times. These orbs were gliding across the sky making absolutely no noise. and they all followed the same path as the 1st one. I went into the house to grab my camera, but the last one disappeared just as I got back outside. I went out to the patio the next night, not expecting to see anything, but I actually did spot one of the same objects following the same path as the previous night. Unfortunately there was only that one this night.
