3-4 lights spinning in a circle pattern then joined together a few seconds then vanished

Then I noticed that there was no projected lights coming from ground level into the clouds and that the light was coming from above the clouds. When I noticed this was a possible UFO I took out my iPhone 13 pro max and shot a 40 second video. In the beginning of the video the multiple light circles were spinning around, towed the end of the video the multiple light circles combined together for a few seconds as one before vanishing.
