{dr}Flaming orb slowly flies across the sky.

July 4th 2014. We were in our back yard watching the neighbors fireworks. There were mulitple aircraft in the sky. Looking upward to see the arial displays, I noticed a bright orange orb that didn't fall to the ground with the other fireworks. Instead it became smaller and slowly travelled NE across the sky behind the trees. Minutes later another fiery orange orb appeared in the same location that we had seen the first orb. This time it was slightly larger and appeared to be pulsating or almost flaming. This object moved from its orignal location on an easterly path until it was out of sight. We thought the event was over and began discussing what we had seen. It was defintely not any conventional aircraft, there were no navigation light, no beacons. 5 minutes later another object appears in the southern sky. It again was bright red orange, it slowly made its way east. Before the third sighted object was out of sight another orb, same color and intensity, appeared and travelled in the same direction. This time the glowing lights turned off and you could see the objects moving through the sky. The were very pale but you could still see something moving. They eventually moved out of sight and that was the last we saw of them. I wasn't able to get pictures of the first 2 objects but had my phone when the last 1 appeared. When I took pictures my camera flash was activating and I remember thinking oh great, they know where I am, at that I see them. We felt very small watching these objects.
