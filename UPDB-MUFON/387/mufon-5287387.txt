Lights appeared to trace around the edge of the craft

This sighting took place in Portsmouth, NH in the summer of 1976. I was a young actor doing summer stock with Theatre By the Sea. We were performing plays in Prescott Park, an outdoor venue. The backstage area was behind platforms and set on the other side of which was the stage and the audience. During one performance, those of us gathered "backstage" - I'd say 6-8 individuals - looked up and saw hovering and moving slowly a round object with yellowish lights around its circumference that traced. Additionally, there were lights that appeared to be on the top of the object, and if memory serves they were red and blue. Of my three sightings this one could very possibly be terrestrial, as it appeared so "comic book".
