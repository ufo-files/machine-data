{dr}Line of lights turn into triangle then spin at proportional distance at great speed then separated in different directions

I was driving north thru the town of Ronan, MT when I noticed a row of three lights. They were too bright to be planets and too high to be power line reflectors (thats what I thought they were at first). I wasnt sure what the lights were and pointed them out to my passenger. Immediately the middle one began to rotate away from that line formation into a triangle. Thats when we felt shock because we knew wed seen something unexplainable. Thats when she started to tape and I could still see two of the lights as I was trying to find a place to park. The footage lost the third light as she was trying to turn on my video recorder. In the video you can clearly see the lights rotating at a high speed in relation to each other. They did this quickly and then then they split up and one shot off south, one went higher up into the air, and the third went into the clouds. I saw the documentary on the Tingley lights later that night, and its the same lights I saw in Ronan, same shape, except what I saw was moving faster.
