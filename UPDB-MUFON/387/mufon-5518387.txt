10 or so sequential orbs moved into view, following each other in arc

On 6/02/2012 I was standing on my back deck on a mostly clear night. To the south/ southwest, I saw what at first appeared to be a hotair balloon on fire rising up on the horizon. It passed by by head, heading north. Then I looked in the same area in which it had come, and there was another identical one arising. My husband, who is disabled and has a hard time walking, was inside. I called for him to come out and see. In the meantime I ran and got my own camera. I filmed what I saw, wishing I had grabbed a tripod, but didn't think it would last long, so kept filming. My husband was using his cell phone video/still shot camera.  We combined the videos today and posted them on YouTube. You could find them easily if you did a search under [name and title moved to FI section/cms/tg] which includes not just mine, but also my husbands videos. We have more videos, but there is only so long a video people are willing to look at on Youtube. I don't think it was aliens, my feeling is that there was a "jet trail" or rocket trail off of these, a flame that looked round and hot. I have read that others from Illinois, Kentucky and N. Carolina saw the same thing at around the same time as we did. Id like an explanation, one that does not involve "little green men" or nuts, but something more in the line of NASA or the US military.  Any reasonable expectation would be appreciated.
