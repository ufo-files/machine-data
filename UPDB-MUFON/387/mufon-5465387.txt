{tjd} Bright yellow-orange orb, hovered dimmed, then moved slowly northwest out of sight.

We were out on our friends balcony admiring the night-sky when we noticed a bright yellow-orange orb/light in the sky. At first we thought it must be a planet (Saturn, Jupiter or Mars). However, the object in question slowly became noticeably brighter(2-3 times original brightness). At full brightness it became clear that this object was within our atmosphere. We actually had the feeling that the object may have been observing us, observing it. After, watching it hover motionless in the sky for approximately 10-15 minutes the object slowly dimmed to a level lower than it's original brightness and silently began on a Northwesterly path away from our location. (The object seemed to have been traveling along the Eastern shoreline of Cayuga Lake before reaching the Northern-most point of the lake and turning Westward) It then made a second 45 degree turn further west and proceeded slowly on that path until out of sight... One of our friends who doesn't believe in UFO's insists that it was just A planet moving through the sky... but I can say with 100% certainty that it was NOT a natural planetary body.
