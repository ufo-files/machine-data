{jpg} Black Disc/Saucer shaped UFO sighting

This event/sighting took place 25 years ago when I was 12 years old, but it was profound enough that I still remember it like it happened today. My friend and I were playing in his back yard in the afternoon at approximately 2:00-3:00 pm. It was a sunny day with no clouds. His house/yard was in a small neighborhood on the north side of Richmond. His house sat adjacent to a large open grassy field with no trees. Across from that field was a large single story factory building. While we were playing in the back yard, I remember looking up suddenly across the field and spotting something moving slowly through the air that clearly wasn't normal. The object never changed speed or trajectory. It just traveled at a slow and steady speed until it was out of our sight. I would estimate the object was traveling at only 10-15 mph. We could see this object very clearly because it was only 100-150 yards from us and flying right above the tree tops. This object was disc shaped with the size and thickness of a medium sized car. It was all black with no shine what so ever, like it was painted with a flat black paint like what you see on a stealth fighter jet or bomber. There were no visible lights, windows, doors, antennas, or any other appendages on the object. I could however see some detailed seams on the body of the object. There also was no sound or emissions coming from the object as it traveled past us and out of view. It was as if it were floating on the air. My friend and I just stood there watching in amazement as it traveled past us. We were both speechless. This clearly was not any balloon, air plane, or any other object that I have ever seen to this day. It was definitely an "unidentifiable object". To my knowledge there were not any other reported sightings of the object that particular day. My friend and I were the only witnesses.
