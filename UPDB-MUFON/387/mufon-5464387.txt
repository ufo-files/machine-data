{rjl} Outside @0445 with dog. Looked to east, above Rincon mtn range, brillant white light hovering

@ 0445 hrs, 11/18/18, I took my dog into yard. A clear, black, starless sky, a brillant white object caught my peripheral sight. I turned, looking eastwardly towards the Rincon mtns, I watched a brillant light for a few seconds to confirm if what I thought I saw, was there. My first thought was, now winter months, more frequent events. It continued hovering, moving along the mtn line becoming brighter. There was silence, no aircraft in the sky, no visible contrail. I was outside 5-6 minutes. It continued subtle movements the entire time, and was slowly traveling slightly northeastwardly when I went into my house.
This is not the first event I have witnessed approx same hour and location.
