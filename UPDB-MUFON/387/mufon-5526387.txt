Arrow shaped, like a teathered kite but way up high.

Close to 35W and Hennepin Ave .East in Minneapolis, parking my car in company parking lot. I observed the alignment of four planets in a diagonal formation and then noticed an arrow shaped object directly above me, reflecting light from the city. I had no idea what it was but thought it might be a kite, tethered. It occurred to me that it would have to be an enormous kite. It did not move and looked like a giant orange arrow with the pointed shape and the shaft not connected. It did not change direction or move. It was orange in color and stayed there for about 45 minutes, until I had to go home. Too high to be a kite unless it was one the size of a mini van. The object had very straight edges that did not change shape. I just felt confused, like observing an optical illusion. It was definitely an object and not an illusion or cloud or smoke or reflection.
