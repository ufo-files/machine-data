Orange light In East Moving South/West

In the early morning hours of January 1st, 2011 my wife and I where driving(I was driving and she was in the passenger seat) home from a New Years party at my step daughters house. We where heading south on 39th ave just south of Bethany Home Rd when I noticed a ornage light in the east. It caught my attention because it was the brightes object in the sky. At first we thought it was an airplane but a I observed it I notice it didn't have and flashing red or green lights. When we got home the and down frome the car we notice that the orange light was moving slow at first towards south/west. As we watch it it seemed to speed up at times and than slow down. we obseved it for about 5 to 10 minutes as it moved towards the south/west. When it got almost to the south the light realy quickly got small and than went out. We keeped watching the sky to see if we could see it any more for 2 or 3 minutes but but no longer saw any thing. I don't know what it was but it was the second time I've seen that in the last 6 mounths.
