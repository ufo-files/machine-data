UFO sight in Brazil in the 90's

It was 1991, and I was a 10 years old kid, me, my family and 2 others witnessed an event on the sky in south Brazil (ITAPOA-SC) when the white lights appeared and start moving in different directions but following a path, it was moving up and down, sometimes it just stopped and kept unmovable for minutes, until start moving again, seems like it was pulsating or blinking (like a star but bigger than a star), it was far way from land, then was not possible to gather any details of the surface of this object, also the sky was clean, no clouds, also no moon as far I can remember. 
After all those years I am convinced that it was not an airplane neither a balloon. The place where we witnessed the event was very isolated that time in the 90' there was no airplane routes or airports in that area at that time and no military base near by, a truly isolated place used as a holiday escape for my family.
The only rational explanation I can deal with, it was an UFO. 
Unfortunately I have no videos or pictures of the event, only my memories.
This was not the first event that happen to me, I also had a physical encounter in another area in Brazil, all this information is hard to believe because I have no way to prove that, no records, it always happen all of sudden when you are unprepared to record.
