Me and some of my family members were sitting in our backyard at about 10:30 PM. We were just having fun/talking when I looked up and asked "is that a shooting star"? Because the object was moving and LOOKED like a star! It stopped mid flight very quickly for about 3 seconds (where my sister pointed out that it looked like a diamond) then turned very slightly and kept moving at a high rate of speed. We managed to see this again about 30 seconds later once it passed over our large tree, and it appeared that it was WAY higher in the air, then all of a sudden it seemed that it got so high we couldn't see it anymore.
