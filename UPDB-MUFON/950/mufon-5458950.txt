{jpg} Two bright lights appeared in storm cloud with a 3rd that appeared to chase it. They went up in a swoop and disappeared

Thunderstorm was arriving and we were looking out the window, watching the lightening. There were three layers of the storm clouds approaching us. The top cloud was white, the center was dark gray, and the lowest was blueish gray. As the dark part approaches us, I saw two large very bright balls of light appear and swoop up. They were going extremely fast. Few seconds later, a third one appeared and it seemed to be chasing it. It also disappeared very quickly. We didn’t see any more after that, and the rain came pouring down a few minutes after that.
