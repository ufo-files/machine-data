I was watching "Tornado Alley," on The Weather Channel, on 05/18/2015. It featured videos taken by people near the tornado that occurred in Washington, Illinois, on 11/17/2013. I noticed a gray, fat mushroom shaped blob inside the tornado, at about tree top level. It was pulsing, then it zipped out to the (from my viewing perspective), right side of the tornado.The shape changed from a fat mushroom shape, to a smooth oval shaped craft, medium gray with a dark gray center, similar to a blackeyed pea. What really made it "very clear," was when it flashed the white center horizontal light, that ran the entire length of the object. Then it hovered adjacent to the tornado, and emitted what appeared to be a white power beam into the tornado, and I heard the roar of the tornado increase. It was actually increasing the intensity of the tornado! It then zipped very quickly away from the tornado, hovered maybe 200 feet from it, and when the tornado changed direction, toward the UFO, it very quickly zipped farther to the right, and shot upwards, extremely fast, and with immediate change from horizontal to vertical direction. There's nothing man made that can make directional change that abruptly, and at that speed..at least that is known by the general public. We also have no aircraft, that can fly/hover inside or adjacent to a tornado, without being disintegrated.
