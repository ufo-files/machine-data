{nd} bright white lights 2 sets of three. the lower set were much brighter

It was around 5:30 in the morning. I walked out, it was just about to get light. I noticed two sets of bright white lights on the sky on SE. I watched them and tried to make sens of them. Then i decided to wake up my friend and her son to see if they see the same thing. We had a telescope that we tried to aim at it and see it better. It appeared like a very very bright half moon but in front of the clouds. The sky was totally covered with thick clouds. We watched them for a while and then i decided to come in and see if other people have seen it as well. not sure what to make of it but to report it.
