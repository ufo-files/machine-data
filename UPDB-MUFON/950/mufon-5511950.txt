{nd} Or- like object, entire object pulsed red to blue.

1. Driving on the I-78 in San Marcos, CA
2. Wife spotted it since it was flying very strangely
3. Thought maybe it was some kind of model, someone trying to hoax a UFO but I don't know of any models that could go that fast to cover the distance we observed.
4. Objects entire surface glowed, pulsing from red to blue. No individual lights - the whole object glowed. It moved fluidly, without any pattern. It would sometimes suddenly drop and come to a quick stop then change direction suddenly or ascend and continue.
5. Skeptic but perplexed on what it could possibly be
6. It kept dipping below the building/tree line and wed lose it sight of it for a few minutes. Then wed see it again up in the sky. After one dip below the treeline and we lost it again. We didn't see it again for a while and it was getting late so we continued on our way home.
