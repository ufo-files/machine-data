3 red lights moving in a wave-like path at incredible speed

I had just got off work and began walking home. The sunset was incredible, so I was watching the sky of course. Suddenly my eye caught a couple red lights in the sky. They looked like red stars and they moved unlike any airplane or helicopter could. The three lights were heading in the direction of south east, maybe south south east. As the sped along, they moved in wave-like motions. Now I thought about how you can notice that an airplane glides through the air, but these lights had too much control over where they were going. After I had processed what I was seeing, I began running in the same direction of these lights because I was standing too close to a building which had gotten in the way. After catching a glimpse of them once more, they disappeared behind another building and I never saw them after that. I was overwhelmed by this experience, so my eyes were watering, but I was astounded. I'd heard so many things about UFO sightings and I'd actually seen 3. It was intense.
