{tjd} line of lights like string of pearls crossing the sky

On 3-5-20 at 2015 hours myself and 2 other witnesses observed white lights traveling at a 45% degree angle coming from the west heading toward the North. There was a large bright star (? planet) somewhat low above the horizon at approximately 25%. These objects were less bright than the star mentioned earlier but were about the same size and color of the surrounding stars in the sky. There was no sound. The objects would come into sight below the larger brighter star and traveled toward the north. They seemed equal distances apart and there would be 4 or 5 visible at a time until they traveled a distance then the lead light would fade out but another one would become visible at the end of the line. It looked like a string of pearls traveling from left to right with the lead light fading out and a new one becoming visible to the left. Noted that the lights were all the same brightness until they reached the middle of the string and the middle one would increase in brightness for a second or two then would fade back to the original brightness. Each light did this as it reached the middle of the procession. this procession of light continued for approximately 15 minutes until they lead lights would fade out and no further lights would become visible.
