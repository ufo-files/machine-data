I snapped a picture of the moon, and noticed objects in the clouds

I snapped a picture of the full moon on October 24, 2018 at 6:34 a.m. while waiting for a bus. The bus came approximately 5 minutes later.

I looked at my picture in the bus and noticed three objects, two super shiny objects to the left of the moon and one below. These objects looked ribbed and curved mostly hidden by the clouds. I was shocked but it was too late to go back.
