{cak} 12-15 starlike lights that seemed to appear from nowhere, blinked at regular intervals, sometimes seeming to hover and sometimes moving straight (in unison)

I was camping with two friends at the Gravel Parking Area off highway 375 near Area 51. I was there to do research for a book I am writing. Soon after it got dark, we saw an exercise that involved flares--so orange lights would appear, seem to hover, and then disappear. Which was very cool to see, but we knew it was a military exercise.

The following unidentified sight is likely a military exercise, too, but we could not think of a solid explanation for it, and were interested in whether your investigators could help find one. 

Above the northwestern horizon, probably from 30-60 degrees up, were what I'll call a 'fleet' of about 12-15 objects (we saw them only as starlike lights). We did not seem them rise, or emerge from anywhere. They just seemed to appear, hover (this may have been an illusion), and move in a straight path in unison. They were visible for several minutes.

We saw the same thing the next night (November 1), around the same time (an hour or two after dark) from Campfire Hill, this time in the southeast direction: [link moved to FI section/dr/cms]

Our reaction was that we couldn't explain it, and that it was mysterious!

Thanks for the help!
