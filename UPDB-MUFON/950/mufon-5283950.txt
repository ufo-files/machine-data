Autumn Orange color, like a leaf.

11/29/21 18:05 SE of Collins Missouri I witnessed an Autumn orange object possibly starlike appearance. It caught my eye at about 30 to 40 degrees as it was descending straight down. It wavered or moved left to right as it fell. It had no trail or tail. It almost reminded me of a Maple leaf on fire falling straight down. The movement was like a Maple Leaf falling from a tree.
Possibly as close as 2 miles and far as 5 miles away. It was hard to not notice because of its color and movements. It disappeared within maybe 1000 feet of the ground. I woke up many times this morning thinking about what that was. I have been driving a truck for over 44 years and have seen 1 other object. This makes 2. The 1st object I have never told anyone about. If anyone saw this please respond.. Hwy 13 southbound was full of travelers. Some had to have seen this...
Thanks for your time.
