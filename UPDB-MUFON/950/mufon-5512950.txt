At 8:22pm my daughter , my wife and I were in our garage with the door open. We all saw this light rise above the trees and start irratically move and then hover. It was pulsing yellow, orange ,and red. It started to move higher and then to the north west and then it was gone from our sight.
We saw another one this Sunday the 20th at about the same time but it was faster and moved towards the southwest and became a red star like object before we lost sight of it. This cant be a lantern because it moves against the wind at times and its way too bright.
I saw another one above a plaza last year this time. It was up close and no lantern.
