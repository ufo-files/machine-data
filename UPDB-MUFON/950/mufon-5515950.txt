{nd} Three distinct sightings: Two bright flashes and one luminescent/incandescent ovoid object.

My mother and I were sitting on our side porch while I had a cigar and we had been observing full moon when I noticed two bright flashes in the sky with nothing appearing before or after the flashes. The third object appeared spontaneously in the northeast and seemed to be flying south to north in its trajectory. It was ovoid in shape with a luminescent/incandescent characteristic to its luminosity. After it had appeared from nowhere, about two seconds into our observation, we saw it shoot a pillar of flame or energy in a 90 degree from its body. It then disappeared and reappeared a moment later further northeast with the same luminosity it first had and then after about two seconds it disappeared.
