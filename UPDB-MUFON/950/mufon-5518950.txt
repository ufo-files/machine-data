3 green beams of light blinking over clouds

My boyfriend and I stayed at Whistler, BC for a week. We were out on the deck looking at the night stars when we saw what seems to be a green beam blinking in the sky. It was sometimes faint and sometimes more visible. It appeared transparent, but whenever a cloud passed by, we see it blinking. After looking at it for 10 or so minutes, we noticed a grey stroke right above the Ursa major. The stroke looked like it aligned with the green beam, although they were not attached and were a distance from each other. After looking at it for about 30-40 minutes, we saw 2 more green beams blinking - one above and one far below. Again, they were only visible when clouds passed by. They were all aligned together, and if you connect the beams together, it would form a straight line. After about an hour, they all disappeared, including the grey stroke.
