[flg] white ball w/ blue tint came to me and sat 2 1/2 ft. in front of me then left upward w/ 3 flashes toward 1 oclock

I left my grand mothers house after dinner to run across peanut field to go play w/ my cousin. As soon as I stepped over first terrace into field 5 or 10 feet a white ball appeared in front of me coming from nowhere and sat there. I don't know if it was scanning, reading, repairing or hampering me. I do not actually know how long I was there, it seemed like a minute or so. It left upward towards 1 oclock w/ 3 flashes so fast that by the time I looked up it was gone. the size @ shape of it would have allowed it to sit a mile or two above and it would have looked like a star. My mother carried me for 10 months and I was over developed causing the skin on the front of my neck to join and roll. Hair lip people do not join as we form from a flat plate in the womb. I am left handed and was forced to write right handed causing me to use both sides of my brain. If I had to guess it was scanning me for some reason. It was bigger than a golf ball and smaller than a baseball. There were no spheres of light coming off of it. It was solid and pleasant to look at. I know there is a GOD and he knows I speak the truth.
