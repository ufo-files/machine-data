3 Adults, 2 teenagers having dinner in back yard Adult observed glowing orange/red glowing object & pointed it out.

Close encounter in the backyard while having dinner (3 adults 2 teens): It was surreal. Brother in law went from under the canopy and walked into yard (to pass gas). He exclaimed "what is that" and pointed to the sky. He said to his daughter "that looks like what your mom called and told you about." I walked over and looked and said its the planet Mars - because of the reddish color - then I realized it was too big to be a planet and it was moving in a north westerly direction. 

Every one got up to look. I asked me son to grab the binoculars. Everybody took a turn looking. I couldnt get a good focus so I passed them on. My spouse and son saw red lights on the glowing orange disc.

The 1st one came from the south west, hovered then traveled towards our direction. It stopped, hovered (almost directly over us) and then began to ascend until it disappeared. As it was ascending my spouse noticed that our bedroom light switched on then off. The 2nd one also came from the south west & followed the same trajectory. It began to ascend but in an easterly direction until it was out of sight.

The first one was spherical & glowing orangish red. The second one was identical to the first one but it had a distinctly red segment along with the orange. There was no sound and there were aeroplanes in the sky - the glowing objects definitely didn't look like the aeroplanes!

It was surreal. When I realized it wasnt a planet or any aircraft that I was familiar with I felt both curious and "on guard". (The thought that it could be a developmental craft came to mind but then I thought who in their right mind would fly 2 prototypes at once over a densely populated area. We constantly see helicopters, private & commercial planes flying in the skies.) These objects were not like anything I/we had seen before.
