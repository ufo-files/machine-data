{nd} Helicopter following fast moving white light sphere of equal size

I was a front seat passenger coming home from work when I noticed a white light traveling at a high rate of speed just above tree tops. It was being followed by a helicopter which was traveling at a slower pace but on the exact path of the glowing light. They were traveling in a north easterly and were soon out of sight.
