Feb 14th Thursday around 7:00pm 2013, neighbor calls to report a odd light moving over our house now hovering above, I ran out with camera in hand to look and see a disc about 21 feet long less than 20 feet above my house, the disc blew apart before my eyes into four smaller orbs of light about 1 to 3 feet, two moved toward south first street, other two moved less than a second passed a neighors house then was gone. A bigger dark triangle was seen above my house neighbors said. I called them to look with me.
