Orange and Amber sphere/diamond

Around 11PM just south east of sacramento executive airport I observed an object just beyond sacramento executive airport. At first I thought it was a star and then it started to "crawl" across the sky and then stop and then it started to 'crawl' again in a more southerly direction. I thought it was an airplane coming in for a landing but the light became too piercing to be airplane landing lights. Living near the airport, I'm extremely familiar with what airplane landing lights and configurations look like. I watched this object for 15 minutes to make sure it wasn't an airplane. After 15 minutes I grabbed my telescope and saw the object up close. The light was brilliant and piercing, the object was a sphere but looked like a diamond at the same time, it had an amber and orange shimmer to it. I stopped watching after around 45 minutes because I was scared. I called the airport but I have not received a response yet. Please tell me someone else saw something of this sort on this same night because I really don't know what to think of what I saw, its something I have never seen before.
