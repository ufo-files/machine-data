Cone shaped cloud/mist coming from sky where there should have been clouds

My husband and I were having a bon-fire when I looked up at the sky as I often do when I saw two clouds that should have been linked together and thought that was strange. hen a mist/light shined down in a cone shape and I begged my husband to look up "For the love of GOD look up" I said. He saw it too and was amazed. 2 minutes of looking at this light and it disappeared as quickly as it came. I couldnt shake the feeling something/one was behind me. My dog wouldnt stop looking into the woods. I was scared. The following week I had a dream or what I thought was a dream, it was the cloud in reverse. I awoke at the back door looking up. I closed the door and locked it thinking I was safe. A year later I feel something on my neck, it feels like dried skin, I pick at it for days until I finally get twezzers and pull. Something, white plastic about 1/2 inch long, cone shaped, comes out of my neck! Before I can look at it close, I drop it on the floor never to be seen again. I believe, Ive always believed. I just believed that it would happen to other people, not me. So much for that.
