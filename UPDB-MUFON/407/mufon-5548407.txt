ABOUT 20"ROPE"EXHAUST TRAILS WERE SEEN OVERHEAD.

I TOOK DIGITAL PHOTOS OF ABOUT 20 ROPE EXHAUST TRAILS AND A COUPLE OF THE OBJECTS MAKING THEM.ALSO MANY GOV`T.PLANES WERE FLYING AROUND AT THE SAME TIME.WHERE COULD I SEND A SHOT OF THE THINGS MAKING THEM?I GOT A COUPLE.
THE NEIGHBORS SAW THEM TOO AND MY FAMILY.
WE WERE OUTSIDE WORKING ON MY SON`S NEW(TO HIM) CAR.
I LOOKED UP AND SAW THE TRAILS.A GOV`T PLANE IS WHAT I THOUGHT AND THINK IT IS.HOW THEY GOT IT ?WHO KNOWS.
THEY WERE FLYING REALLY HIGH AND REALLY FAST!I WAS EXCITED AND TALKED LOUDLY.WE REALLY COULD`NT SEE THE THINGS DOING IT.BUT THE CAMERA CUT THRU THE HAZE.THE PLANES WERE FLYING MUCH LOWER.TO ME THEY LOOK LIKE A SWEPT WING JET WITH WEIRD ENGINES THAT POINT OUT.MAYBE A B1?I BELIEVE THEY POINT OUTWARD TOO.AND THEY ARE SWINGWINGS.THE THINGS UP HIGH LOOKED MORE LIKE AN ARROWHEAD TO ME.LIKE A HUNTING ARROW ALMOST.WITH THE SWING OUT TIPS.
