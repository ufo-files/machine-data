Beings seen walking around

It was approx 11pm when I went outside on the deck of my girlfriends apartment and saw something on the basketball court adjacent from her building. The area is usually pretty secluded and dark due to surrounding trees. I had noticed a flash of light that was moving around but couldn't tell what it was, so I grabbed my phone to see if I could get a better visual. What I saw was an illuminated figure awkwardly walking around knees bent and it was slightly hunched over, along with 2 other figures dressed in black outfits. The encounter lasted approx 5 minutes and then suddenly they just vanished. I watched for a couple more minutes to see if anything else showed up or if I could see where they went. I finally worked up the courage to go down and investigate further, but found no additional evidence.
