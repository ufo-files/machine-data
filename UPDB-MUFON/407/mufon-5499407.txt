{nd} Saw a low flying object, whiteish blue, moving W to E, then the object ascended out of view.

While I was walking my dog I was looking up at all of the high flying airplanes, and then noticed a low flying whiteish blue light moving West to East on a no cloud, clear night. As I was watching it for a few seconds the object then began to flutter and get smaller, to what appeared to be the object ascending away from earth. After a few seconds of ascending the light was gone, and never came back. I will never leave my phone int he house again.
