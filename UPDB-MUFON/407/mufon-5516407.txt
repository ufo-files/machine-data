Manifestation of thought.

I believe in aliens and requested by thought that they make their presence known to me. With that a large star light object appeared near the brightest star in the sky... Then continued to move towards me over head very slowly, with no tail of any sort and no flashing lights. It was a solid light colour with no noise. Even the noise from th previous planes in the sky had gone. It then stopped dead over head, as I ran around to the balcony to my left to get anothe look it had vanished, with at it's prious speed couldn't have done, with no cloud present to block it from view. During the sighting I was excited and over joyed that they had responded to my request.
