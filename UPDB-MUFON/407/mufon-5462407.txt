{rjl} Triangular

My parents were sitting on front porch with landlady while I hung out with friends. I was 12 years old. They called me over to look. Triangular shaped yellow light moved east to west over treetops. Stopped in front of us, hovered for several minutes. Turned point up, hovered briefly then shot straight up and was gone. I don1t remember seeing an actual craft, just the light. My dad said that it was probably just a helicopter. But there was no sound and no helicopter could possibly move that fast straight up! In retrospect I think he must have said it to keep me from being afraid. I don1t remember feeling fear. I also don1t remember ever discussing the sighting with either of my parents afterwards. However, I never forgot it!
