My wife and went outside at dusk to have a smoke and check out the night sky,it had stopped raining / drizzling ,and I noticed a bright light in the east-se part of the sky.It was just sitting there,no wavering or bobbing like a craft,stationary like a star or planet,however there were clouds behind and wisps passing in front .I got the bnocs which I have strapped to a tripod and got a better look.Simply put it was a red-orange light ,all I could discern was ballish,perhaps spinning slowly.I went and got a camera phone but got discouraged by her and my inability to operate it.Soon it descended slowly out of sight in the direction of King road and Horseshoe Bar Rd.During the observation I did have amoment where I felt like I shouldn't be looking at it andshrugged it off.Got on the cumputer this morning and can see that someone else had seen a white object that the reddish object had descended from.I feel it neccesary to share and learn more.
