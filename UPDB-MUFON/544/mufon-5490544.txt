{nd} paperboy sky opened saw brushed steel object which was gray @ 1st

I obviously saw something ..After school I was visited by the adj. general of the local Nike Base.. Which He said I saw Northern Lights. Being a 13 y/o kid I listened to his line.I saw the sky open over a church...& A gray rectangular object fill the NE sky & paint itself a bluish brushed steel color.. Observed it as long as I could.. & Then delivered my papers.
