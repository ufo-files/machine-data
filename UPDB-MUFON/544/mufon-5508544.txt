{kt}Two objects gliding towards each other in sky come together and one revolves around the other.

I was standing outside my home around 3:00am looking up into sky and first noticed a white, star like object (object #1) gliding through the sky coming from the east. Soon afterwards, another object that was triangular (object #2) came gliding from the opposite direction and caught my eye even more so due to it`s brightness and solid yellow color. I then realized that both objects were heading directly for each other and I thought to myself that I was going to see them crash. The objects eventually met up, stopped in the sky, and object #2 began to revolve 360 degrees around object #1 with the upper point of its triangular shape facing object #1 the entire duration of this. After the revolution, object #2 began to glide away until out of sight behind the trees. Object #1 glided away for a moment, suddenly shot higher into the sky at a much faster speed, and stopped suddenly in place. I rushed inside to grab a camera to at least try and get a photo of object #1 while it was still in the sky but once I came back outside, the object had completely dissapeared. I believe that there`s no objects that can perform what these had done, whether man-made or cosmic phenomenon.
