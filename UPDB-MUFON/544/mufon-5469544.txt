{rjl} I saw the Phoenix lights UFO sitting above mountains behind Santa Fe,NM

On March 13, 1997 at 10:30pm I saw 5 very bright lights on top of the Sangre de Christo mountains behind Santa Fe,NM. The lights appeared to be the size of a car headlight but miles away. My first thought was "that thing is huge" Appeared to boomeranged shaped. My second thought, "was this some black opps project" I watched it for 15 minutes when it appeared to pivote, not turn, and headed north. Went on-line the next morning to see if anyone else saw this in Santa Fe. The only news was about the sighting in Phoenix. Three months later all network news casts aired the Phoenix Lights story. When I saw the pictures of the UFO over the mountains outside Phoenix, I knew I had seen the same draft over the mountains behind Santa Fe, NM, on the same day. I reported this event the day after it occurred but never heard from anyone.
