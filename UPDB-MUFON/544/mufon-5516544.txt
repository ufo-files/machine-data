(GS) lights pulsating over Mesa Colorado on three seperate occasions

The first sighting occurred when I was woken up at 4:17 am by my husband then I went outside to smoke a cigarette and witnessed a bright light in the sky too big to be a star and was yellow and pulsating. I pulled out my video camera and shot a video. I went inside and then it was gone. The second sighting occurred on July 30, 2012 between 2:00 am and lasted until the sun came up. I witnessed many lights ranging in size moving around some back and forth some up and down and others doing all of those. I sat for hours and watched them dance in the sky and went the sun came up they all slowly gathered and disappeared until there were only to left that seemed to back off into the sky for a hour then disappeared.
