Strange contrails

These contrails appeared over Morehead/clearfield ky, to the east, on 5-25-21 starting at 6:10 am
I decided to take a picture of the morning sky to show how beautiful it looked to my girlfriend. When I went back outside I was amazed. 
Look at the time it took to make these trails and the angle of one of them. I don’t know of any airports in that area. Also I don’t know of any airlines that travel that fast or can make turns at that speed.
