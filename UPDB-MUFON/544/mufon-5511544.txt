a huge cigar shaped mothership..sometimes shows smaller scouter ships coming out one at a time

for months i have seen this huge bright object in the sky right outside my bedroom window..in the beginning i was the only one that could see it..until april 15 2013 at 11pm that night we took a picture of it ..in the picture you see a huge bright light that looks much like a lightning flash..to the right if you zoom in ..you can clearly see that the object is cigar shape and very large..the person taking the picture crawled outside the window in my bedroom on the roof so there is no distortion from the glass in the window..i have been observing this object every night before i go to bed i take my binoculars to see it..although my binoculars are not strong enough to really get a good look at it you can see the outline...i knew all the time that is is a mothership because of its size and the fact once in awhile i am able to see small ships enter and leave ...this object moves slowly..i have watched it sit for some time then move slowly in the clearing so i can get a better look at it..my reactions are that of excitement and wonder..why isnt this object being monitored by anyone near my town ..why isnt anyone calling papers or tv to come and view what i see ..by the end of the night the ship slowly glides away from me ..thats it

note...if you blow or zoom in the picture you can see the ship to the right ..i just wish i had a better camera...thankyou "B"
