{cak} Pulsating orb hovering slowly

Looked out my window to the sports complex near my house. Saw a orb of light that appeared out of thin air, trail of material that looked like glowing particles streaming from the object. The object hovered slowly across the field around 40-50 feet in the air and vanished before I could get any closer.
