The object appeared to the naked eye to be a slowly descending white orb probably a mile or so away. When we looked through binoculars it became apparent that it was much further away than that, moving faster than we thought. Through binoculars it was clearly either emanating or reflecting reddish light. We tried to take pictures with our phones but couldn’t!
