I was heading north on Wadsworth Blvd. and turned east on 72nd. ave. in Arvada CO on 2/23/21 at approx. 6:25pm. That intersection is elevated with an unobstructed view east towards Denver International Airport. Immediately after turning I observed a series of bright light in an upside down "check mark" formation low on the horizon totaling approx. 5 lights at that time. There were another 3 lights to the left and I would guess a few thousand feet higher. This is overlooking Denver metro area with a lot of light pollution and these lights were by far the brightest thing in the sky. I also observed commercial air planes flying in front of these lights and they were much, much dimmer and flying in a predictable pattern. With no place to pull over I pulled out my phone and did the best I could to film them. When I reached the intersection of 72nd & Depew I pulled into the left hand turn lane and stopped to film them even though it was a green light. While filming them an Arvada police car stopped next to me, still a green light, and then sped away in the direction of the lights without pulling me over to see why I was stopped. I made 2 more passes down 72nd from Wadsworth trying to film the lights, but my phone would not record. The lights had changed formation by the time of the second pass to a series of lights in line and then into a "V" shape on the third pass.  The distance to the lights was to great for them to be helicopters or drones and still be that large and bright. By the time I got home they were still in the sky. I have not seen anything on the local news stations or online, but they were much to bright and obvious for me to be the only one to see them.  I have seen a posting on your website from Centennial CO at roughly the same time (not sure of the case number).
