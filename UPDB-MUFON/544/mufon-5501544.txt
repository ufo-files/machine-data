Three black objects/triangle photographed in Belgium

I was taking photographs of the Napoleonic battlefield at Waterloo, Belgium in May of 2006. Although I didn't see anything unusual at the time, I was surprised to discover three black objects hovering in the shape of a triangle in one of the pictures (they are visible in the center left). Knowing the reputation Belgium has for black triangle sightings, I decided to submit the photo here. There were no birds or aircraft in the area that day.
