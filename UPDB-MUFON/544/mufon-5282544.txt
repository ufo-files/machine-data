Bright shining orb (star like)

I was working with a friend near Oxfordshire, we were waiting for our payment. My friend went inside the house to get paid while I waited outside, it was a really clear evening so I looked up at the stars when I noticed to the right side of the sky a moving star, I watched it for a minute zooming across the night sky, it looked like it was actually moving out the way of some stars, I couldn't believe it so I grabbed my phone to record the next 2 mins of it before it disappeared, it would flash on and off sometimes too. In the 3mins or so I watched it it zoomed across the whole sky from my right to the left. I took a screenshot of the orb on the video and zoomed in, it looks like a bright white orb with a little black box (windscreen shape) inside.
