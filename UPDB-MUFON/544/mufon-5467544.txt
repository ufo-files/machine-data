{dr}It was red, went up, stopped and hovered for a few minutes, then vanished into a small green light, then disappeared.

I was with my fiance for the Fourth of July in a supermarket parking lot to see some fireworks. There were other people there as well, but I don't think any of them saw it because the fireworks were going off in the other direction. The firework show had begun at 9:30, and we were talking to the group next to us as we did so. Then I glanced around, and saw a red light in the sky. I thought it was a helicopter or plane, and kind of dismissed it. Then It moved to the side, which wasn't normal, and really fast. It flew up, stopped, and just sat hovering for a few minutes. I pointed it out to my fiance, and he thought maybe it was a drone or something, and then we ruled that out because drones didn't move like that and just hover. I was a little in awe, but a part of me was just thinking it was maybe a weird firework. But by the time I was really trying to see it and reaching for my phone, it just vanished. All the red lights on it just went dark, leaving only a small green light that was barely visible. My fiance was freaking out, and we just finished watching the firework show, forgetting about it. But it kept bugging at me, so I thought maybe others might have seen it. It had been circular, maybe disc-like. But it was hard to make anything else out, because it was so dark.
