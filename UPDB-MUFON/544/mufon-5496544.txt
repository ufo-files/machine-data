Ball of orange moving slowly in a straight line.

I was walking in a town of Zrenjanin, Serbia when I looked up at the sky. It was dusk but clear. I saw an orange ball of light hovering. Then, slowly, it started to move west. It did not blink, and it moved too slow to be plane. I observed it for ten minutes until it became too far to see by eyes only. I felt as if I knew it was not from this world.
