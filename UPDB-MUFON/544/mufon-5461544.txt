{jpg} Circular UFO

I awoke and looked at the clock which read 2:19 am. While still in bed, I looked out of the window. There is no blind at the window, our house backs to the desert and White Tank Mountains. I looked up to see the stars, but instead I saw a circular lighted object. I could only see the lights around the perimeter of the object. They were all different colors and they flashed. The object was going in many different directions. It shot straight up in the air and then would go very quickly left or right. I was stunned lying there in bed. It then disappeared before I could get a video.
