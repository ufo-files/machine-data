I observed and recorded a large bright sphere hovering over the neighbourhood (SE) from our first floor balcony. next to a military base. I took some photos and videos, it was just before dusk. The object was hovering for 2 hours.

I'm not sure, but I went out to the balcony and saw a large glowing sphere hovering over the houses. We stay next to a military base and about 6 F16's were taking off and they are always loud. To the SE the object was hovering. I took some video and photos of the object multiple times through the sighting as it was there for ages. When I had a look at the images I noticed another photo taken in the opposite direction (North) and it looks like a disc shape craft. I remember taking a picture in that direction but not seeing the object at all.

There were 3-4 other smaller objects moving in the sky during the same time which were more like orbs flying over the military base and directly overhead. They moved in multiple directions at he same altitude.

I felt very comfortable during the time of the sighting. There was some strange visual tunnelling happening when I was transfixed by it. It seemed like it was the main craft that was in control of the others.
