{tjd} Saw oval object at work

I was taking the trash out at my work looked up and saw this object flying in the sky. It was a white Oval shaped going fairly fast. It was a nice blue sky with some cloud clover and I didn’t see any VISIBLE wings attached to it. I observed it for about 2 minutes before it went out of sight and flew into the cloud clover. It didn’t slow down once, and had no sound, no lights, and no visible exhaust Coming out the back end. This is a fairly high traffic area for planes and we can usually hear them and see the exhaust coming out from the back. And since it was a nice clear blue sky I was shocked that I didn’t see any visible wings attached to it.
