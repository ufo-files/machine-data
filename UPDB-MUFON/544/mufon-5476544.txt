{nd} Appeared to have a halo around object, was travelling north and then abruptly switched to a western direction without banking

I was standing outside of my office located at South Kings Highway and Chippewa street in St Louis Missouri on an April Evening in 2008. I was facing west and noticed an object coming from the south heading north, at first I thought it was a helicopter, being in the military at the time I was looking at it to see if it was a military helicopter, as it got closer I noticed it was flying to high to be a helicopter and what at first I thought were its blades turned out to be some sort of halo or aura around it. I continued to watch it as it passed me and then it just changed direction without any sort of banking like a helicopter or airplane would do and headed west, I continued to watch it until I lost sight of it. Due to its high altituted I had a hard time making out the shape and color of the object, but it definitely was not a helicopter or airplane.
