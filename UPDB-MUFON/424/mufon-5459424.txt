Hovering crystal shape UFO

I was watching channel 9HD Melbourne on the 8/7/2019 at approximately 10:30am. Program was the Today Extra. I was watching a report on a Ninja Warrior contestant and noticed a object in the background. I walked up to the television and had a better look. There was a object in the sky just above the contestants head, out the window and in the distance. The best description I can give is that it looked like a vertical, long crystal shape. It was hovering and slowly rotating. My television is a large 4k screen. So the image is as clear as I can get it.
