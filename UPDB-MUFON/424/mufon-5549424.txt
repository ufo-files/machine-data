Silver disc in the sky that disappeared instantly!

I was in Lodi, Ohio working on a dairy farm while I was in high school. It was a warm summer day. I was spreading manure on a hay field. I looked up in the Eastern sky (that was the direction the tractor was traveling). I saw a silver disc shaped object. It was in the shape of a dish with a raise in the middle. It turned on it's side which made it very thin but you could also see the raised center more distinctly. It then disappeared out of site in an instant!!! I've never seen anything turn like that in the sky while remaining stationary and then bullet out of site in an instant!!!! The entire siting was about 2 minutes!
Once the dics disappeared, I looked back at the field and I was driving the trctor and manure speader across the farmers driveway through a fence and into a pasture!!! I had forgotten all about what I was doing when I saw the disc. The farmer was very upset with me and he did not believe my story about the flying disc! I have seen strange lights in the sky where I live now but they are at night. This sighting in 1974 was during the day! I am sure it was no optical illusion or airplane, etc. It was what I said it was... I do not know if it was a government craft.
It was a UFO to me!!!! I di not report it back then because I knew of nowhere to report it! I am thankful for you site and alloing me to get it of my chest!
