Was standing on balcony 15th floor and saw an object hovering with bright flashing lights on horizon over water. At first I thought it was a pretty large star because there were many stars in the background and then grabbed my DSLR with a 300mm lens. Thats when I was able to clearly see the multi- colored flashing lights and the object which was hovering. It did not look like a plane, blimp or helicopter. The lights were multicolored and were not flashing in any particular sequence. Took took approximately 8-10 photos but only one seems to show something similar to how it actually looked when I was looking into the camera. The rest of the photos came out with a light blur in the background.
