{rjl} Looked like giant airplane at treetops

I was taking a night walk around the block, when I reached top of hill after rounding curve, my eyes caught a huge object above tree tops in front of me. It was moving from my right to left in what appeared to be a descent. I saw a row of dim lights and immediately thought it was an airliner that was going to crash land. I looked quickly to my left to approximate where and how quickly it would make ground contact. I immediately determined that I didnt have time to run away, so I just dove onto my neighbor's front lawn (slight slope upward) curled into ball and covered my head with arms. I laid there for a minute but heard no crash. When I sat up and looked around, I prayed that no neighbor saw me!!! LOL
It was HUGE and it was there, but not there, kind of opaque. I went back home, not sure if I told my mom and her husband at that or later, but I did. We talked about it several times. We are all very open to this so it was always a matter of fact, casual and curious conversation. I will enter another report for my mom's husband's experience.
This is my 2nd report today. As I stated previously, I have no idea why I have waited all these years to report. Its not like I did not want to, I did. 
I live in Maine now, but wanted these reports to go to TN/MS investigators as this happened on border. You can reach me by phone or email if needed.
