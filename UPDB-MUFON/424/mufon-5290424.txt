Incredible. I didn’t see some far away little lights or shapes in the sky. I saw a full on alien battleship close enough for me to see into the window

I saw a cigar shaped alien battleship. It was at least a hundred yards long. It was massive. It had windows lights and I could see all the metal components on the outer surface of it like a spaceship in the movies. It made no noise. I saw it for maybe 3 or 4 seconds and as big as it was, it hit light speed and was gone in a steak of lights making a black whole where it disappeared into. The black whole and streaks of light stayed about a second after the spaceship went into it. All this with not a sound. It was as big as a skyscraper building, made of black metal. Hovered, turned, the back end glowed red and blasted off into hyper space. Nothing like any sighting I’ve read or heard about. Only thing that comes close to what I saw would be an abduction or a personal meeting with a space man. I can believe anything now. I will ask god what it was when I go to heaven.
