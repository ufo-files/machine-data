{cak} Observed two pearl-like spheres moving from north to south at incredible speed

It was about 2:00 am when I decided to go outside for a smoke break, after working on the computer for six hours. I stretched and looked straight up and saw two fast moving pearl-like spheres at the zenith, heading in fast formation to the south. They had an interior blue-white tone with no fuzz, sound or trail. They were moving so fast I decided to count down the observation time. They vanished over the south horizon in about eight seconds. I estimated they were at 40,000 to 50,000 feet altitude, and moving in excess of approximately 20,000 mph or more. They appeared to be about as big as a small aspirin held at arms length. They were about a finger width apart and in tight formation. They were so clear, it startled me. The speed was unnerving.
