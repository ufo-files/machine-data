{cak} Hovered then descended - sped up and turned and took off at high speed

I was driving towards Lytle Tx on FM 3175 .I saw stationary green light to the south of me . It started to descend so I thought falling star but it was speeding up . When it got closer towards the ground it took a curved 90 degree turn and took off at a speed that is faster than anything I had ever seen before. It took off so fast at a south west direction that it just went out of sight . My daughter was with me and she could tell that I had seen something abnormal . I did talk to another person that saw the same thing I did
