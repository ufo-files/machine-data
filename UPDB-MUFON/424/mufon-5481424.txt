{rjl} Sighting near Cleveland, Ohio

The witness was driving out of Cleveland, Ohio on his way home to Iowa. He had just got on Interstate 80 when he noticed a disk at 25-30 degrees elevation directly in front of him and about one mile away. The disk was traveling in a straight path from north to South and appeared to be silver on top and a dark grey on the bottom. The disk was no lower than 500 feet and traveling approximately 100 mph. The sighting lasted about 15 seconds as he grabbed his phone and got off one shot. After taking the picture he noticed that the object was gone.
