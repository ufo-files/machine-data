{dr}Moved across the sky at a very high rate of speed. Perfectly horizontal

I was standing on the front porch when I noticed a very bright light moving perfectly horizontal to the ground ant just above tree-top level. The abject was a very bright, white light. It did not deviate it's direction. Had no running lights. It did not pulse. Steady bright light. I at first thought it was a jet plane. It moved so fast I thought I was going to hear a sonic boom as it went by. But there was no sounds. Not even the sounds of an engine. As it moved away from me it just simply winked out as if someone turning out a light. It left no vapor trail or a trail of any kind. The whole event lasted only a few seconds. I estimate the size of the object to be about the size of a fighter jet. Could not see the object itself just a bright light.
