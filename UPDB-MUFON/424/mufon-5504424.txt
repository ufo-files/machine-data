{nd} I saw a bright white light with a red light that looked like it revolved around the object. It was in same place for ~2 hours.

I was visiting my grandmother at a nursing home in Killen, AL. I went outside to smoke. I was sitting in a porch swing under a tree and I noticed a bright white light to the East that had a red light that looked like it was revolving around the white light. I watched it until I finished my cigarette, then I went and got my brother. He saw it and he went and got my mother, father, sister and uncle and they all saw it. Some 40-50 miles away is Redstone Arsenal in Huntsville, AL. It looked like it could have been hovering over the arsenal. I had no idea what it was, but the red light wasn't blinking, so I knew it wasn't a known aircraft. When we left, it was still where it was when I first saw it, so I do not know how long it was there after we left.
