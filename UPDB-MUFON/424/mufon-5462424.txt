{rjl} Already submitted to MUFON, orange orb over park in Canton, new information

This was already submitted but I was unsure how to provide this additional information and correct the original submission as I've lost MUFON contact info from my original interview.

This refers to the orange orb and subsequent panther sighting in Canton, Michigan, August, 1992.

In my original submission I mention seeing the orb shape on my right toward the end of my encounter. It was actually on the left. I'm providing a Google map showing where I saw the orb, its path, the route of my car, and where I saw the panther.

I'm also providing a photoshop recreation of what I saw. The tree line of the park was closer to the parking lot at that time and the lot was slightly shorter.

I realize now that I would not have seen the light from the orb shine onto the treetops as it passed OVER the trees and I continued driving. I think I saw it again once I turned left onto Sheldon.

It continued to stay to the east of me as I went the entire route although it may have been south of me for a time.

I'm also including the newspaper article referencing a panther seen in the nearby town of Westland that came out within a week or so of when this sighting occurred.
