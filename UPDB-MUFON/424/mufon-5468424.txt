A UFO was seen over a forest West of Darmstadt

My fourteen friends and I were hiking in a forest West of Darmstadt (and East of Griesheim) at around 14h. We suddenly saw a UFO fly towards us from the West and then back the West. It was shaped like a typical flying saucer. It was black and made weird lights and noises. An alien poked its head out of the window and it looked roughly humanoid. When it saw us, it got back in and the UFO flew away. It was all over in about 10 minutes.
