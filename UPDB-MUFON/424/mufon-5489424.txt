I was by the pool at a friend's house in Silver Lake. Saw a strange shaped object in the sky. It's shape was like an inverted U, bright pink, lit from within, with fuzzy edges. It was falling down like a leaf, very slowly, floating around. I watched it together with 2 other people, for at least a half hour. The first thing I thought was that it was a bunch of gas balloons that got loose. But as I watched, and as it switched directions, floating around, I could see it from different angles and it became quite clear it was not balloons. The edges were fuzzy, like a a stuffed animal. It was lit from within and very bright pink. I was in awe and wondering what kind of object that was. It felt like a dream.
