{jpg}Bright, large, stationary light hovering over Sangre De Cristo Mountains. Very gradually headed straight east, then suddenly traveled east with extreme speed. Left an enormous, illuminated triangular cloud. Zoomed in photographs reveal three lights

My neighbor called to let us know there was something large and bright hovering over the mountain range by our home. At first, it was stationary, and we believed it was a large dust cloud in space. I went inside, and my dad called me back to say it was moving. It was moving very gradually at this time. I went back outside to see it had moved a great deal from when I had last seen it. My dad went back in the house to get his camera, and suddenly, it moved very quickly, directly east, and left an illuminated, massive, triangular cloud/plume. My dad photographed it in flight, and when zoomed in, it appears to be a huge arc with three distinct lights.
