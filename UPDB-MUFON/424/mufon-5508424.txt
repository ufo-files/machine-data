All black helicopter with no markings, lettering or insignia

I heard a strange muted helicopter sound which sparked my curiosity to go outside on my front deck, I witnessed a single low flying all black military style helicopter with a pole protroding from the front and its spotlight was on( it was around sunset time with lots of light still). The helicopter did a complete circle around my house with my house in the center. I couldnt help but feel an eerie feeling like I had a gun pointing at me. I noticed it was shorter in length than a Black hawk with similar windows on the side like the black hawk but all 4-5 were equal in size. I wanted to take my cell phone out of my pocket to shoot video but an eerie feeling telling me not to came over me.
