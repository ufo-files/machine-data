Strange Orb Over Chesterfield Va

This is the second time in the last few weeks that I’ve witnessed unexplainable lights over my home in Moseley, VA. Chesterfield co. 

After my first sighting where I didn’t have my phone with me, I swore to cary it with me every time I’m out walking my dog at night. 

On the night of 5/31/2021 I was walking my dog at approximately 10:20pm. It was an exceptionally clear night and the stars were bright. While facing north I noticed what I first thought was a low orbit satellite traveling from north to south toward me. I’ve seen them out here before. There were no blinking lights to indicate it was an aircraft. But the closer it got the more I realized it wasn’t a satellite, it was too low in the sky and it seemed to move, at times, in an inconsistent motion. It was clearly orb shaped. 

I grabbed my phone and recorded what would be the last 51 seconds before it vanishes. When I blow up the video it appears to be pulsating and seems to almost split into 2 orbs before coming back to a single object. The very last seconds of the video appear to capture the object changing to an elongated shape just before vanishing.
