My wife, 2 sons and I were exiting a retail store. We paused for a moment because it was raining very hard, I looked towards the sky to see the rain through the parking lot lights. I didn't hear any engine noise or helicopter sound. For some reason I decided to take these 2 photos of the rain and when I viewed the photos later I saw these 2 images that shouldnt be there. I do firmly believe in other life forms outside of our planet but they wouldnt be anything we could imagine. That being said I wasnt looking for a ufo in the sky that night. If there is another explanation of these lights besides a ufo please let me know because I didn't ask to photograph this.
