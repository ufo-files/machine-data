I was in my living room in Catalina Arizona hanging.out with a friend when my front gate security camera sent me a notification that it had been triggered. This happened at 3:19 p.m. in the middle of the day. We have Smart security cameras in my home so I opened the app and live streamed it and could see something appear in the sky get bigger and change directions. There were no clouds in the sky at the moment. The cameras have never been triggered by planes or clouds before which makes it a little weirder. But there was definitely an object coming from the West turning and heading north right above us and within seconds a second object or more could have been some kind of Fleet did the same thing came from the west and headed north and disappeared. The camera caught a whole 5 minutes of it. Check it out and let me know what you think. I've lived in Catalina Arizona for almost a year now and I've heard many stories from the older folks that I have lived here for a while about strange lights and such.
