{cak} saw 7 cigar shapes looked connected

we were camping along the Alcan highway Watson lake area. in the morning my older brother and I got up to start the morning fire when we saw something in the sky over the lake it was a flat black in color from where we were standing it looked like a bundle of logs on a logtruck each cigar shaped thing seemed connected and kind of staggered around the center one for a total of 7 we woke my dad and told him we were watching something in the sky we described it to him he said it was northern lights but after living in Alaska I never saw northern lights like that and in broad daylight. strange but I don't remmber what happened what happened to it this has bothered me for almost 40 years I have no clue what we saw.
