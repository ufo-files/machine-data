Star-like Object During Air Show Hovering Above B25

I was watching the 4th of July Thunder Over Cedar Creek Airshow at approx 5:15 pm July 4th 2020. I was sitting on the edge of the shorewall with my mother, my 14 year old daughter, and a 9 year old neighbor girl. We were facing westward.  I was watching a B25 Mitchell Bomber flying low across the lake. I saw what I could describe as a star during the daytime hovering over the plane. I thought at first it was a fighter jet that had gone to high altitude to come back down in a vertical descent, but as soon as the B25 passed underneath the "star", the star entirely disappared. I mentioned it to my mother right away, but she is didn't see it, nor did the kids. I did not see it or anything like it afterward. The sky was clearly and mostly cloudless.
