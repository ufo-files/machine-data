From the East at one minute to midnight New Years Eve one by one six orange flying orbs flew from the West to the East exact same path. Absolutely not airplanes or fireworks!

My husband and I were in a Hotel Room in Burlington Ontario, right beside Lake Ontario. We were waiting to celebrate New Years Eve from 2013 into 2014. I looked out the Hotel window and saw an orange orb slowly flying from the West in front of our hotel and to the East. I called over my husband to the window, and then over the course of perhaps one to two minute intervals five more exactly the same orange flying orbs came from the exact same location in the West and flew on the same path towards the East. I thought I had record on my phone to videotape but I guess I did not record. We were completely unable to determine what these six orange flying orbs were. Amazing!!!
