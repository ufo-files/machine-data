{td} On a flight to Los Angeles I looked out of my window to the north and bright lights in the distance.

On Saturday April 28th, 2018 I was flying to Los Angeles from Washington DC. When I was about 20-25 minutes away from LAX I opened my window and witnessed bright lights in the distance to the north. It was around 6pm pacific time and it was broad daylight. 

I was sitting on the right side of the plane at a window seat traveling west. Since we were flying over the desert I thought the lights could have been some type of military vehicles or maybe solar panels reflecting the sunlight. 

I was curious so I pulled out my Samsung Galaxy S9+ mobile phone and started recording a video. As I zoomed in on the lights I witnessed three stationary brightly lit orbs that were hovering in a line just above the ground. The lights were so bright that it was illuminating the ground beneath each respective orb. For the duration of the recording the orbs never moved other than hovering.

I recorded the video for about two minutes zooming in and out to show how bright they were from a distance and also what it was up close. I lost sight of the orbs as the flight continued and some mountains began obstructing my view. As long as I was watching the orbs they never moved but they were clearly hovering in place.

I posted a video to youtube just in case my attached file does not produce good quality.
