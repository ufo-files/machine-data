{dr}UFO sighted at Charles Mill Lake, OH after firework, Fourth of July 2015!

My husband, my daughter and her friend were all at Charles Mill Lake in a small country town in Ohio. It has a lake and covered forest camping area. It also has a beach tucked away down a long road for parking/fishing. We were walking back to our vehicles on the road through the woods and through an opening I had caught my eye to an object that was soft and dark oranges/red in color, star like object high in the sky. At first I thought it was a firework or star, but I had my glasses on and I could tell and know it was neither things. The object pulsated in a way and I told my husband and daughter to look, we all joked it was a UFO and then after a few moments of laughing, we looked back to the clearing of the trees to see it and it was gone. Reading all these other sightings all over the US, we wanted to share our story, we feel weird now! We're wondering if they could see us back!
