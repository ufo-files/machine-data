{rjl} Black aerial object viewed for 2-3 minutes

While waiting at an intersection traffic light I witnessed a black object against an overcast sky. At first glance I thought the object was a kite as it was mostly stationary but shifting shapes (as if being blown by the wind), going from a semi diamond shape to more of a parachute canopy (as if it was turning/rotating). While I was watching the object I was immediately in a semi trance like state and had visions of my brother and I as children flying kites together. As the light changed I realized I had been “zoning out” and I entered a shopping plaza driving east toward the direction of the object. The object became suddenly became visibly smaller and now more of a cylinder/rectangular/oval shape and started moving to the south at about the speed of a commercial airliner. I pulled over in disbelief/amazement and took 5 photos of the object before it disappeared behind a low lying wall of clouds.
