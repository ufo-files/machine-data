Vertical tic-tac shape hovered and flashed twice then began to move east while emitting a beam of light that illuminated the hillside underneath.

My girlfriend and I drove out to Pt Reyes to try CE5 for the second time. After meditating we exited the vehicle and there was a bright flash of light similar to lightening but there was no lightning or storms in the area. After about 40 minutes of observing the sky my girlfriend noticed a stationary light over a hill to the north. As we began observing the light it flashed twice and began to move eastward. The object was a vertical tic-tac shape and was glowing white/yellow light. A beam of light projected from the bottom of the object and lit the hillside brightly enough for us to see the surface of the hill. As the object moved east the beam scanned the surrounding landscape as if it was searching for something. The light emitted was a bright blue/green color. The object changed direction and moved westward while projecting the light then began to move east again, this time exiting our view behind a hill.
