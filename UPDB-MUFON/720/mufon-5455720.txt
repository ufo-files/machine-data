This description was written by the witness and sent to the MUFON ERT.

In the early hours of January 13,2020 I was parked on the side of my cousin house sitting in my car when I remembered opening my eyes and seeing myself levitated in my car in a fetal position.I was tilted to the left side just a little and that is when I saw something standing a few feet from my car wearing a white lab coat .I knew it had 2 arms from the coats design but I couldn't make out what it looked like because it was dark. I later realized that there was a mark on my left arm and I couldn't remember how I got it.I took a picture of it then zoomed in and it appeared to be a letter s.
