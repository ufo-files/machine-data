{nd} Bright light the size and brightness of Venus in the mornings sighted in northern sky flying ~ 1/4 speed of commercial planes in an easternly direction before disappearing behind a mountain.

Went outside to take my dog out for the evening and observed a very bright object in the northern sky from my house at approximately 45 degrees up from the horizon. It was moving at approximately 1/4 speed of a normal commercial aircraft in a straight line across the sky in a due east direction. There were three other commerical aircraft flying over the house, which is a normal track for them at normal speeds, sounds and lights. The unidentified object left no contrail, had no noise, had no blinking or other lights other than its on brightness. It was very similar to two other similar objects that have been seen from here. At least one of which was reported to MUFON at the time. This object was both brighter and bigger than the other two sightings. Was not able to get my binoculars to try and view it due to taking the dog out.
