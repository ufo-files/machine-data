{cak} I looked up, star gazing when I saw it. A bright light moving at a high rate of speed, much faster than an aircraft or satellite

I was standing in my driveway smoking a cigarette. Looked up at the sky to see how visibility was since we've had so much rain and overcast the past few weeks. Noticed the stars--as I was star gazing is when out of nowhere the bright object darted at an extremely high rate of speed directly above me. I was stunned, because I knew it was not the ISS and it was not a shooting star. It's path was vertical with no declination. I would guesstimate that it was moving well over 600 mph. It was a momentary event because of the speed.
