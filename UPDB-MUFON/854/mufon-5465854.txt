{cak} Driving up Santa Fe ski basin mountain, perfect blue sky, two identical white non-reflective discs with domed top.

My wife and I were driving up Santa Fe ski basin mountain on September 14, 2018 on a curvy mountain road but generally headed north east. My wife looked up through the windshield in about 451 and saw two identical white non-reflective discs of circular shape with a domed top.
It was a perfect blue sky with zero clouds. The disks ascended rapidly then stopped and hovered motionless for about five seconds and then simply just disappeared from view in an instant. The total sighting lasted approximately 10 seconds. There was no time to grab a cell phone or take a picture and my wife was so spellbound that she was motioning to me the driver but I did not look fast enough to see the disks.
