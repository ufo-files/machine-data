{rjl} On the porch when we observed a large black oval object 500 feet above us

While watching television, the picture froze up. We went outside on the porch, looking up we noticed a black oval shaped object 50 foot long, 500 ft. in altitude and about 100 ft. away. Then 3 red lights in a triangle shape began to slowly come on and grew in brightness. The object then sped off in a split second. Much faster than any known aircraft. The entire sighting lasted about 30 seconds.
