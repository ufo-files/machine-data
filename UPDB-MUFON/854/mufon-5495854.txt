Saw a steady red light followed by helicopter on the skyline. As it approached I noticed it was of a triangular shape as it passed silently overhead. The helicopter in pursuit was of Apache attack style. They were both moving rather quickly and as the UFO in question passed over us, the one viable light split into two giving the brief illumination of a triangular craft. Both were headed roughly West towards Long Beach in a straight line. Lost track when they passed over the house.
