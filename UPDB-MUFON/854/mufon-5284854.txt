Flaming disc fell then disappeared very close up.

I was driving about 10-14 miles west of Ozona, Texas at around 11:26 p.m. I saw to my right hand side a huge flaming disc falling rapidly, straight down from the sky. It was covered in a green plasma type looking flame, with white sparks flying off both the sides. The disc was shaped like a bowl, with the round side facing underneath. It was as close to me as a commercial flight airliner would be on a tarmac while seated in another plane waiting for takeoff. It was also easily just as large as a commercial airliner, if not larger. Just as it was about to make impact with the ground, it seemed to completely disappear in a split second. It would have made a huge impact, leaving debris and making a large sound if it had actually crashed. I almost stopped and walked out to the spot off the freeway where I saw it, but I was too afraid to do so.
