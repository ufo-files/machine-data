{dr}Strange lights coming from west. Followed by same non blinking or colored like aircraft. No noise.

I was sitting on my couch in my living room and had just finished dinner. Through a slight slit in my blinds on my window facing east I noticed a bright light unlike a commercial or any other air craft shining through. I first suspected it to be a different kind of craft like a helicopter. They flew from the direct west veered north all turned right and headed south. Some went around each other in a circle impossibly. I felt excited, slightly shocked,amazed and very interested. I have since been captivated. They went around the back of our house and headed south. They were all going the same direction in silence. Maybe a very slight hum.My girlfriend is a witness too.
