{cak} SAW A SILENT TRIANGLE FLY OVERHEAD

AT 10:50 PM ON JUNE 16, 2016 I WAS SETTING OUTSIDE LOOKING AT JUPITER AND THE MOON. ALSO LOOKING A THE BIG DIPPER. I LIVE ABOUT 5 MILES FROM LAKE MEAD. THERE WERE 3 TO 5 PLANES IN THE AREA, OVER THE LAKE. SUDDENLY WHEN I GAZED UP AND LOOKED AT THE BIG DIPPER I NOTICE A DARK TRIANGLE GLIDING QUICKLY AND SILENTLY FROM EAST TO WEST TOWARDS THE PLANET JUPITER. THE OBJECT HAD NO LIGHTS BUT AT EACH POINT OF THE TRIANGLE THERE WAS A OVAL SHAPE ON THE SURFACE. I BELIEVE THE OBJECT WAS LIT BY REFLECTED LIGHT FROM LAS VEGAS. I HAD TO LOOK TWICE BEFORE I REALIZED I JUST SAW A UFO. MY WIFE JUST WENT TO WORK AND I CALLED HERE TO TELL HER ABOUT. PLUS I WENT TO A NEIGHBOR AND TOLD THEM. AS THE OBJECT NEARED THE PLANET JUPITER IT FADED INTO THE DARK SKY.
