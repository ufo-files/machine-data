Street light sized light On horizon in 2 area's of town with in 15 min's of each other.

I left my house in Northern Rome, Ga to go get dinner. Heading south on MLK Blvd as i approached the hill at the Coosa Valley fair grounds (no fair in town they were completely empty) I noticed a light bright light bigger than the size of a streetlight directly above the hill as i got closer thinking it was just a star it streaked off for one second and disappeared. Thinking i was just seeing a shooting star i headed on to get dinner. Amazed and still looking to the sky i noticed that there were no other stars shining through. I shrugged it off and headed to my business then as i was approaching my home on Old Calhoun rd. right past maxway right before getting to Brinkley's auto i saw it again. The same exact light doing the same exact thing only heading in the opposite direction north this time. Each time i seen the light it only lasted for a few seconds but both scared the blank out of me.
