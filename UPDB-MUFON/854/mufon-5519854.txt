egg shaped thing made my skin feel like it was vibrating

I was coming out from statistics class from university of Nairobi,i was in an accelerated program so i had long classes that started at 5.30 and ended around 9pm.I had come out of class and headed into town that is very much nearby on foot to a restaurant to get some fries and chicken for dinner,it took longer than usual so i estimate i was on the catholic church hill at around 10.30.It was dark there was only one street light on and it was about half a soccer field away from the bottom of the hill,i slowly walked up talking on the phone to a guy i was dating at the time George,the call dropped and there was light comparable to high beam lights from a car,so i thought it was a car behind me and i quickly glanced back so i could gauge which side to move to away from the car,as i turned the light got brighter and it took me a couple of seconds before my eyes could adjust to light,and infront of me about 5 or 6 arms length was an egg shaped object a bit more elongated than a regular egg and with sort of appendages a couple of them it turned small point facing me and beside it stood a short silvery flowing person,it had red large eyes and the skin looked silvery and seemed like it had some sort of flow to it,it just stood there,the guard dog at the catholic church was barking so loud and growling and all of a sudden made a whine noise and quieted down.i felt very hot like i had just stepped too close too a fire and my skin felt weird like you would if a phone vibrated on you,I could not move,i literally could not move my feet,this was happening about half way up the hill,at the bottom of the hill i saw two figures and i thought it was some other student until they got closer and they looked exactly like the short one infront of me,she short one moved towards me sort of a floating fluid motion and one of the taller ones made a very loud shriek like a cat that just had its tail stepped on but louder.I saw a group of guys at the bottom of the hill they stopped for a couple of seconds and then proceeded up the hill such that i could see their faces one of them was a classmate the others i did not recognize.... the short one stopped and turned back and it is at that point that i was able to turn and run,in my mind i thought i had seen a demon or ghost of some sort.Until i saw this guy bledsoe i think was his name was on tv explain something close to what i saw i really don't know what to make of it,but i took a different longer route from that day.The guy from my class kept telling the story in class but since i think he had not recognized me i did not try to chip in the conversation.I was just glad he had seen it otherwise i would have thought i had gone crazy.
