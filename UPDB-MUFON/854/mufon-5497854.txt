{cak} Six Orbs Sighted.

My Sister sighted six orange yellow orbs after getting off of work at 10:00 pm Saturday the 1st here in Sheridan, WYO. . She said they were as bright as the street lights but were up in the sky . Three were to the west and two to the north. One more came up and joined the ones up north of town. Then One headed N/W the rest vanished. She took a cell phone photo but it was a poor shot and was erased .They were in formations. They were big enough my sister thought the moon was up but in the wrong spots. I myself seen a U.F.O. In the summer of 2012 on the south side of our K-Mart. It was cigar shaped. A dull white. About two inches in diameter and about one foot in length. It was flying about 20 feet off of the ground and flying from west to east at about 9:30 am. A cousin seen a U.F.O fly along the top of the hills south of Gillete ,Wyoming in mid 70s. All of the sightings were silent, No engine sounds. We are not alone. Seti does not need to listen to space communication. Get a chair and watch the night skies and take a camera with you. It is going to be my new hobby.
