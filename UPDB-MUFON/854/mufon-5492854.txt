{dr}my friend and I were outside and herd the ice breaking on the river. when the noise stopped, the UFO appeared

My friend was spending the night at my house. We would sneak outside at night a lot to walk around town. This is a small town in Maine so we would be the only people outside at 1 a.m.. we went out about 1 a.m. on this night and while we were still on my street, a small dead end road in the woods with no lights next to a river, we could hear the ice breaking on the river. At first we weren't alarmed by it. Then it got louder and louder. Finally to the point we got scared not knowing what it was so we turned around and started running back to my house. The loud noise of the ice breaking seemed to follow us as we ran. Then suddenly the noise stopped and just then we saw a disk shaped UFO come from that direction flying across the woods and the road and then right over my house at tree top level. It made no sound while flying. It looked like a disk with a dome. There were lights spinning all around the disk. The lights almost seemed like an aura. many different colors all meshed together. It was moving very slow and kept moving until it was out of site. I have seen 2 others near my house after this. One was a triangle shapped one that seemed to fly right for me. It hovered right over me. It was tje size of a huge building. My dad also saw a disk shapped one at night outside our house. Lots of strange things happened there at that house. My mom has seen lights or orbs in the house at night and I've seen figures outside the windows and doors at night. One was in the front door at about 3 a.m. . It was holding the screen door open and seemed like it was almost 8 feet tall. I couldn't see what it was. It just looked like a shadowy figure.
