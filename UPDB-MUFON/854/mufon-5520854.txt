Orange/red light slightly long on the sides, round on the top and bottom low flying change direction and ascend.

I went outside to move my car from the driveway to the street when I saw a low flying light between the 2 houses across the street. For the first second I thought it was going to be a plane on the runway pattern getting ready to land at Midway a few miles away, but I noticed that the light was an unusual orange/red color that I cant even recreate, as I never saw anything that color before. It went from a south to north direction, like the runway pattern was, for about 3 seconds, then it started to head in a east to west direction at an uneven hover and as it moved toward me, as I was looking at it from a west to east position, I noticed that it slowly started to ascend as it got closer, then it went directly upward, at the same time it was doing this, i notice a plane on the very runway pattern I described earlier getting ready to land. The oject first appeared below the treeline and now at this point it was above and moving higher, I backed up toward my car and unlocked it and opened the door. I second later I looked down for a second and when I looked up it was gone.
The object glowed with bright light and it was orange/red in color, but a shade I never seen in my life. it was round, but slightly elongated at the sides. It was quite low flying as I could see it between the two houses quite well.
