On March 3, 2020, at 9:06 p.m., my husband and I were driving from Lebanon, PA, towards Palmyra, PA. We were just outside of Campbelltown. On our left was a large, open field and we saw a long object in the sky with a bright white light on either side and multi-colored smaller lights in the middle that were either blinking or spinning, it was hard to tell which. It almost looked like a wedding ring, if you were to look straight down at one. We were driving, so it was difficult to determine how far away it was, but it was very high in the sky - well over the treetops. It was in our sights for about 10 minutes, but when we came up over a hill, the lights were suddenly gone, but a small ball of light shot up from the ground and into the sky to our left at a very rapid rate of speed - like that of a shooting star.
