{rjl} Hovering light above tree line just 5 miles north of NORAD in Colorado Springs.

I was sitting on my back porch facing west towards the Rampart Range in Colorado Springs. There was a bright orb in the sky that just hung out for quite a while. There has never been this light there before. After 5-10 minutes I realized my I phone was in my pocket and decided to record the event. I held the phone stationary and recorded crazy movements from the glowing orb. It moved fast and unlike anything I have ever seen. Very ironic due to the proximity of NORAD. Who knows?
