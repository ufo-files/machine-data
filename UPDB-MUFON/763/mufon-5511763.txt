{dr}A four light array, at times only 1 light and then growing to 4

My wife and I were driving north from Ajo, Arizona on route 85 in a Mazda convertible at about 8PM in the evening. We were returning to California from a business trip to Sierra Vista, Arizona. After dinner in Ajo we headed north. We first observed 4 lights off to the west, they were stationary and we were familiar with the area and knew they were no on a mountain of structure. They did gradually disappear and then reappear in the east, then disappear and re appear again in the west. I was able to get closer to the lights when they reappeared in the west. I stopped the car and watched closely. I believed I observed smoke coming from one of the end lights. I personally suspect that this is air force related and that what I was observing was an array of lights on an object; however I am an engineer and would expect the lights to be flashing, I would conclude that if this was for some military use that may not be the case. I am think this may be related to the 1Phoenix Lights1, but I guess that is what makes the a UFO.
