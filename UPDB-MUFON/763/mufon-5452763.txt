White light in broad daylight

I was sitting outside enjoying the nice weather when I saw this light in the sky. Maybe a kid lost their balloon? It certainly wasn't a plane, and it seemed very odd to me that it was dimming and brightening on such a clear day. It reminded me of orbs of light that I used to see in Las Vegas at night before moving to NC, so I decided to take a video of it. Same characteristic, dimming and brightening. Partially disappearing at times.
