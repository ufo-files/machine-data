Bright Anomaly

I had converted an old digital camera to take infra-red images by removing the sensor filter, the camera was a Nikon coolpix 995.

One Sunny afternoon I was testing the converted camera, taking shots of the trees and clouds and was pleased with the results. When I downloaded the images to my PC I noticed an anomaly; it was almost like a second Sun. I sent the image to a local ufo group, and they confirmed - as far as they could - that the object was visible between the cloud layers, but it might also be much further away.

Clouds show up really good in IR, and on my photo its easy to see the Sun shining above the clouds. I don't know if the object is above or below the clouds, or maybe even next to the Sun, because that was my first thought, as there is so much talk about Nibiru I wondered if this was the planet...?
