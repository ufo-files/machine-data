Niagara County UFO's

I was traveling East on Lockport Road into Pendelton when I saw the sky light up in a white flash. I turned south on East Canal and Just about pulled over immediately. I started filming with my cell phone from inside the car because I saw the one UFO disappear and reappear or a new one took its place. It hovered under a cloud deck of about 3500 feet over some trees for a while then slowly turned to the south. Suddenly a much brighter object came into view at the intersection of Fisk Road and East canal in a very light up area. The object out shined the street lights. It came in made a 90 degree turn north and started to very slowly fly away. meanwhile the other object I was rapidly losing site of.
