THE ALIENS TOOK ME INSIDE

I WAS RESTING IN BED AND I WAS BY A WINDOW. ME AND MY BROTHER HAD A BUNK BED, I SLEPT ON THE TOP BUNK. I COULD NOT SLEEP THAT GOOD THAT NIGHT, JUST HANGING THE BED. ALL OF A SUDDEN I HAD A LIFTED OUT THE BED AND (THROUGH) THE WINDOW. I COULD NOT FIGHT THE LIFTED JUST CARRYING ME TO A CERTAIN SPOT. I SAW (ALIENS) JUST THE LOOK OF A (PHOTO) WE ALL EXPERIENCE (CURIOUSLY). THEY STRAPPED ME TO A CHAIR IN THERE SHIP, THEN SETTLED THEM SELF (MISSION COMPLETE). SO THEN AS I SAT DOWN IN THE CHAIR AND ARGUED WITH THE ALIENS. ONE OR TWO OF THEM GRABBED A LONG SHARP OBJECT AND STUCK IT IN MY LEFT SHOULDER. AND PULLED OUT AFTER A FEW SECONDS. AFTER THAT THEY SEEM TO CELERBRATE LIKE (JOYFULLY) AND I FOUGHT WITH THEM TILL THEY FINISHED. I DID NOT KNOW IF THEY WERE GOING TO BRING ME BACK BUT THAT WAS MY WORRY ALSO. SUDDENLY I GOT THE STRAPS TAKING OFF ME THEY LEAD ME TO THE DOOR AND I PUNCHED ONE IN THE HEAD AS IT STOOD BY THE DOOR. (I WAS BACK IN BED AFTER) JESUS CHRIST
