Round ball like bright lights

I sometimes wake up during the night. I woke up grabbed a drink of water around 4:15 am. I closed my eyes to go back to sleep and could see bright light flashes through my eyelids. I opened my eyes and saw a large bright round ball of light hovering in the air toward the ocean. I mean really bright. Within seconds of seeing this light another appeared right next to it. I turned to my nightstand to get my phone to take a picture. As I turned towards the lights they quickly darted towards the SW. I have a sliding glass door without drapes that faces the direction of the ocean. Once the lights darted to the left they were out of my sight from where I was in bed looking out the slider door. I was a bit startled to get up and go outside to see where they were. The lights were so bright I figured that someone else that happened to be up that early had to see them. A couple of hours ago I posted an inquiry on the Nextdoor app to see if anyone else witnessed the lights or knew what it might have been. I received a response from a neighbor that Mufcon may be able to help with finding out what the lights were.
