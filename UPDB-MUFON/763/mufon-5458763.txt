{cak} Giant Fleet Over WV

On Aug 13th I was vacationing at a remote mountain cabin in Slatyfork, West Virginia. At 9:30 at night I started noticing unblinking lights that looked like stars slowly gliding across the sky. At first just a couple...then a few dozen at once...then literally hundreds...uncountable numbers...thousands...they were all going west and all traveling the same speed...some looked higher up than others. There was a swath of sky that looked 20 miles wide filled with these lights....it began to taper off around midnight but continued on until cloud cover completely blotted out the sky around 12:30....my entire family witnessed this 7 people in all. The objects made no sound. The next evening I watched the sky closely but saw nothing - BUT on the 16th at 12:30 AM my son and I saw the lights were back - but this time they were heading south and in smaller numbers than the previous sighting. I thought it might be ramping up to the huge numbers I saw a couple days before but shortly after they appeared the sky was blocked out by clouds and I was unable to see how long it lasted or how many ended up coming.
