Square/sphere shape with glowing yellow cross like structure in the middle of the square/sphere.

While I was going outside to my garage for a smoke I looked up and saw a square/ 3 dimensional object floating in the sky moving from the South towards the North/East. Within the square was a type of cross shape that was illuminated bright yellow and the rest of the square outline a visible dark colour. This was not like anything I have ever seen in my life. As I watched this object float across the sky I yelled at the kids to get outside and see this object. As the kids ran to come see the object, the cross lights within the square slowly started to go from bright yellow down to a faded redish/yellow hue until the lights and object completely disappeared in the night sky as it was travelling. This all took place within 1 minute. From the time the objects lights started fading to it disappearing took about 5-10 seconds.I can only reference this object against the stars in the clear night sky and it was many times larger than any object in the sky and I had a very clear and direct look at it. Neighbours dogs started barking and noise started happening when the object started its disappearing act. If I did not yell at the kids to come see I would have talen a video with my phone which would give much more validity to my claim. But either way I wanted to share this.
