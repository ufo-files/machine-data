{nd} Seen a solid rectangle blinking red light hovering next to radio tower blinking every 2 seconds

I worked the 2nd shift and got off work at 1 o'clock. But left work about 1:20. Was on my way to Hypermarket that was open 24 hrs.to get a couple things. When I noticed something odd hovering at the top of the radio tower. A solid rectangle that would blink every 2 seconds. At that time I was in awe what it could be. It would blink several times before it would take off towards the left up at a 45 degree angle. I saw it blink 2 more times from the original position before it was out of sight. It was really dark that night. Could not tell if anything else was attached to the red blinking rectangular light. I've seen a lot of shows and documentary and never seen anything like I've seen that night. I was so excited afterwards of what I witnessed. People who know me know I would never make something up. Especially something this wild. Has anybody seen, read, or heard anything like I've seen?
