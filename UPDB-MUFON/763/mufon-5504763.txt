{rjl} Silver Bluish Ball of light that changed into a Disc shape and shot off into outer space

Witness was on a camping trip as a young man (currently 58 years old) in Southern Ohio with two buddies. Noticed a Bluish gray light in the sky like the light of an aircraft way out there on the horizon, except that it moved faster. The object then turned into a Disc shape as it got closer and shot across the sky one way. Then turned and shot back the other way across the sky ten or twelve times. The craft would have been covering hundreds of miles per second before shooting off at tremendous speed into outer space. Witness is credible. (jch)
