{dr}Over 20 orbs appear, and vanish

A little after 10 pm, I was outside having a smoke when I saw a bright fire ball in the sky. After walking to my driveway, to try and get a closer look, I looked to my right (Eastward) and saw about 4 more objects. At first they were in a straight line, but then moved into a triangular shape and one by one disappeared. After about 6 minutes the last one appeared and I followed it on camera until that one to vanished! There had to be close to 20 different orbs that would appear out of nowhere, fly quickly. I was in awe, I couldn't move, barely able to breath because I had no idea what was going on. I couldn't even call my family to see what was happening. About 5 minutes after the last orb disappeared, a tremendous lighting/thunder storm came in. Sorry about the quality, I tried zooming in and out numerous times!
