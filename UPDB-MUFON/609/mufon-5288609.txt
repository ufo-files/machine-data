A clearly observed black Sphere travelled from west to east silently over Lyndhurst.

I was in my garden at approximately 19:10 BST, it was bright and a moderately clear sky of cloud formation when I observed a black sphere at approximately 2000 feet travel from over the rooftops to the north and travel silently east. I have recently retired from a career as a civil aviation AGA comms engineer and am well accustomed to seeing aircraft both military and civil at various strange angles as to provide a deceptive view. This was clearly a plain Black Ball travelling silently east without any apparent means of propulsion and had no lights or observable places for lights as the surface looked smooth. I was able to observe the motion of the object which neither deviated in height or direction until it went out of view.
