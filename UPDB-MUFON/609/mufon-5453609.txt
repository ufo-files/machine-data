A group of at least 4 orbs traveled together in a random formation. One orb quickly ascended and descended while still keeping close to the others and the orb that followed last within the group sped up (shot right through) to the front of the group. The orbs were gold and dim. I was driving south on hwy 6, towards hwy 90, and I saw a group of lights gliding through the night sky. The lights disappeared over the trees as I drove towards hwy 90, due to the trees being too high. Once I passed the trees where the front entrance to the Cullinan Park is, the small-like airport came after the park. I thought it was a plane, since there are air strips, but it seems like the orbs flew over the airport. The way the orbs traveled was unusual and not something a plane would do.
