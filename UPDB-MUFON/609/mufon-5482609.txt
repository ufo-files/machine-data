I felt like i could not breathe, had to get outside, huge red orb fillled the Western Sky.

On June 27 2016 I was at home in Northern Ontario at about 1:30 am. I was getting ready to take a bath. Suddenly I felt like I could not Breathe As if I was drowning under water and in order to reach the surface to get air I had to get outside. I told My boyfriend how I felt and together we went outside. immediately I saw a bright red sky it took a second for my eyes to focus I said what time is It (maybe the red sky was caused by sunset)No it is Red not pink and it was only 1:30 am hours before sunrise. I realised I was looking at a Gigantic Red ORB that consumed the entire western sky in every other direction the sky was darker than normal we had not been able to see many Stars at all that night It was vey quiet. There was an eerily calm feeling and a cool breeze. It seemed as if just slowly drifted away it only lasted about 3 minutes. We have no idea what it was. Please let me know if you do or if anyone else has shared our experience. I'm happy to help in any way I can I am very interested. Please Email me if you have any questions.
