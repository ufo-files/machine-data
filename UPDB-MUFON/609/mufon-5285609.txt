Very bright amber ball appeared at treetops, spotted again hovering above interstate 75S. Color had changed to a bright silvery light.

Was merging onto interstate when a very bright amber/yellow ball of light appeared at the treetops then disappeared from view as it traveled behind the trees. A moment later the object reappeared, seemingly hovering over the highway or moving alongside my car (it was difficult to tell because I was trying to drive and watch this thing at the same time). The color had changed to a luminous white/yellow. It was as brilliant as a planet in the sky. At a distance the object looked like a singular ball of light, but as I drove closer I noticed two sets of smaller, blinking lights on either side. The blinking didn’t seem to follow a sequence or pattern at all. At one point I passed directly under this thing and looked right up at it. I think I went into some sort of mental shock because I can’t remember exactly what the object looked like from underneath. I think the ball of light was the center of something triangular or boomerang shaped, grayish in color, with the blinking lights located on each side of the central ball of light. I checked my rear view mirror to see if it was still there after driving beneath it. My daughter in the back seat turned around to look for it as well, but it was gone.
