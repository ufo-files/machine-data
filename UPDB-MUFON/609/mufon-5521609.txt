grey amorphous mass

At approximately 3:30AM my son and I were outside our apartment trying to view the meteor show. I was looking northeast when something caught my eye moving to the south. I turned and viewed an amorphous mass of nothing for about 1 minute moving toward the southern horizon at a high rate of speed. It passed behind a group of trees and based on previous viewing of aircraft landing and taking off at a nearby airport, I would place the altitude at between 500 to 1000 meters. The object quickly accelerated and vanished toward the southern horizon. The object had no identifiable shape other than blob-like and an empty grey color. It was like looking into a hole that you cannot see the bottom of but as it moved it did block out the stars behind it. My son saw something but was looking in a slightly different direction from me so I cannot say he saw the same object. Having seen aircraft and helicopters in the same area during the day and night I can safely say this was not an aircraft of any design. In fact I think I would be safe in saying it was not a structured object at all.
