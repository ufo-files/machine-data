Triangular shaped object in sky outside my window.

I was laying in bed watching T.V. and I heard a noise that I thought was a jet getting ready to pass over my home. I made the statement "jets at this time of night?" and waited for them to get louder. The noise never got louder it stopped. I looked towards my window and saw lights coming through the shade. I was curious as to what it was so I pulled back the shade. I saw a triangular UFO one clear light at each tip perfectly spaced from each other with a large red light in the middle. The lights were very bright. The object traveled very slowly and stopped to hover for a around 30 secs. above my neighbors home. I jumped up to run to the door to go outside to get a better look but by the time I got there it was gone.It appeared to be so low that I actually woke my husband up because I thought the object was going to crash. As I was looking at the object through the window I could not hear any noise. There seemed to be no disruption of the trees around the object and my dogs weren't barking at it.
