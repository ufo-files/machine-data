{jpg} Saw series of 11-15 lights in triangular formation spaced equally apart

Was sitting with friends outside of restaurant at approximately 8pm and saw a series of faint, almost star like, lights in a triangular formation move overhead from north to south and quickly fade away over a period of several seconds. The lights appeared to be very high in altitude and my impression was that the associated object would have been very large in size based on the dimness of the lights, lack of outline defining the triangular formation, and scale with buildings in the background. I have made an attempt to re-create what I saw visually by showing lights in a triangular formation in a generic night sky image. The generic sky image does not reflect the actual location of the event, rather the intensity of the lights and formation they were in.
