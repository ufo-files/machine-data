{jpg} Sighting of unknown aerial object

I stepped outside one evening to observe a freak hailstorm in early October when I looked up and saw a super bright light shining through a break in the clouds.
By the time I ran inside to grab my camera phone, the light had moved further away and was obscured slightly by the clouds. 
I was able to snap a photo and record some video before it was gone from view.
