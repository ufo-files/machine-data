small in upper windows of the saucer shape

At approximately 2pm on a December afternoon we my brother and sister) were sliding down hill at our neighbor field when we heard a buzzing like the power lines do when its real cold out. My brother said look up! There was a saucer shaped disk right above us not more than a two story barn. My brother yelled get under your sleds. At the time it sounded like the best thing to do since we were in the middle of the field and no place to hide. When we got up the object was moving over our neighbors house and barn and just before my brother died we talked about it and realized it we were missing time. When we came out from our sleds is was almost dark and close to 5-6pm. We never talked about it after I think because we were more affected than expected. I was about 7 the twins were 9. I would love to have a regression therapy.
