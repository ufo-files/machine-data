{rjl} Spotted objects moving from southwest to southeast.There were at least 5 or 6 moving at a moderate speed.

I was at the Turkey Hill inForty-Fort Pennsylvania gassing up my wife's SUV when I happen to look up int the southwestern sky..I spotted 5 or 6 objects moving at a moderate speed In a southeastern direction. At first I taught they were aircraft heading for the Forty-Fort Airport.I then noticed they were a solid light with no blinking lights.I lived in the area for years and never saw aircraft flying In a group like that. I got my cell phone out and walked a few steps and snapped a picture.As I tried to take another picture a car pulled in front of me and hindered me from getting another picture. By then the objects were out of site. I knew I saw something strange .I know what I saw was not any type of conventional aircraft. There was no sound or blinking lights. The picture I took did not capture all of them.
