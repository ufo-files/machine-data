{rjl} Unknown Flying Object over Enfield NH

Tonight an object flew over Enfield NH from East to West.  At first it appeared to be a jet in sound and flight path, however, it seemed to be something else after observation. 

The object was in the shape of a triangle, with five small white lights. In the center was a large strobing red light.  I waited to see the green navigational lights and strobe lights appear but they never did. 

In addition, as the object passed, the jet sound never changed pitch ie doppler affect.  The sound did fade but never appeared to sound like it was moving away.

When the object reached about twenty miles away all lights extinguished in unison.
