Flat square flew silently and blinking over us

The engine of our car suddenly shutdown, we called help when. After about 5 minutes a flat matt black object appeared in the sky. It had blinking colorfully blinking lights. The object flew in a rectangle wave pattern, made no sound or emissions. We wanted to make a photo but the camera was black. The object was making me disturbed, but my heart rate did not change. After about 22 minutes the object disintegrated. After another 10 minutes a black helicopter appeared which was following the path the object followed.
