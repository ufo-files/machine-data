{dr}Materialized, then shot sideways, then straight down. Wasnt anything i have seen before.

I had just went outside around 11:40 pm on the night of the 19th of january, 2013. I lit up a cig, and looked up in the sky over my neighbors property. all of the sudden, I saw what I had at first believed to be the beginning of a falling star in the west, except it was brighter than i had ever seen a falling star be. It looked like a cluster of maybe 4-5 lights, very bright, that made up the aura of one larger light. Very bright. The object then took off sideways in the sky, to the nw maybe a dozen degrees, from my line of sight. Then it stopped in the sky for about 2 seconds, and descended very rapidly toward the ground. This was in no way close to my location, i project at least 20 miles away, and there were no clouds in the sky so i could see the object very clear and the path it took. I did not see the object after the descent, and i did not chase it or try to follow where it went. Looked in the sky for about 5 minutes after, did not see any helicopters, jets, airplanes, etc. 

I see falling stars just about every time they are forecast to be seen, and this was no falling star. I got pretty shaken up by the event that i witnessed, and i am not easily spooked. I enjoy reading about ufos and paranormal activities, but this was the first time i actually witnessed an unidentified craft.
