Saw a glowing luminous disc swaying back and forth along the horizon near the ocean, then it went up and repeated itself in diff. patterns, finally disappearing.

Looked out my bedroom window at dusk and saw a bright disc just over the horizon of trees along the ocean at the start of PCH it seemed. The disc was luminous and appeared to gleam and reflect and change in brightness as it swayed across the sky. Fell like a feather slowly, with control, bobbing up at times, and once to the treeline it slipped from sight for a second and then came back up and up and up swaying slightly and glowed. I watched it bob across the sky in the general vicinity of that area several times for a good minute before it disappeared. I knew it wasnt a plane because I could see other planes in the sky. There was no fog. I would be amazed if no one else reported it- Ive never seen anything like it.
