Orb Sighting

A bright, glowing orb appeared to the south around a cloud. It hovered for almost two minutes above the subdivision. Orb was witnessed by multiple family members from backyard deck at @ 9:45 pm on 6/19/2021. Orb seemed to be hiding or manipulating around a cloud. I grabbed my phone and started taking pictures. I tried to switch to video and my phone froze. Phone has never frozen like that before. I could not take any video. I was able to take 7 pictures. Then, out of nowhere, a military jet streaked across the sky. We never heard the jet coming. It was flying very low and appeared to be tracking or going after the orb. The jet was not very loud. We lost sight of the orb when the jet arrived.

The next day I examined the pictures. There was more in the video than just an orb. In the background, there may be multiple UFO's in the distance. You can see the shape and the distance of the objects change quickly. The photos were taken consecutively and the objects configuration and distance change significantly. One object is in the upper right distance. Another object is under the orb. Objects look differently in each picture.
