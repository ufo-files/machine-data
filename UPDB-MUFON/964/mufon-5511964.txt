{dr}4 objects hovering in red color, not flashing in any other color

My husband and I was driving home on Hand Rd. in Sarasota, Fla. around 9pm and my husband told me to look up and we saw 4 red objects in the sky just hovering. There was no other lights blinking like an airplane would have, then 1 of the red objects flew away going north and the other objects stayed then puff gone. I have never saw anything like it before ever. When I first saw the object I knew it was not an airplane or anything I have ever seen before.
