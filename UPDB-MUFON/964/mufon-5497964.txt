{cak}3 normal adults notice red glowing lights in sky, unlike anything ever seen Looked across sky saw a plane in SW, well away from lights, heading E. Each light changed positions from path, ascended one at a time, growing smaller, white, then vanished.

Three family members finished dinner at Allen Park restaurant around 7:30 or a bit later. No alcohol consumed. Pulled out of driveway onto Allen Rd, going East, and saw a path of bright, cherry red, glowing lights in the East, about 45 degrees. There were 7 or 8 small spheres lined up in a curved arch. Then four repositioned into an oval shape, all changed patterns a few more times, then one by one, ascended, growing smaller, turning white, then vanishing. When the last one vanished, the sky was dark, very clear, with no trace of any elements coming from where the lights were spotted. The lights created no audible sound. It was a clear night and these lights were very distinctive. A plane approached the direction of the lights from the SW, but was a good distance away -- had to turn around to see it. The direction of the lights were not near an airport, although it was not possible to discern just how far above they were. This experience was unique and not confused with any light event on the ground or coming from the ground. We were excited, but had no adverse or unusual feelings or reactions.
