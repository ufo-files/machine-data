star gazing

My sister and I and my 3 kids and my nephew all went to park outside the city, so that way we could witness the Bethlehem Star! because it had been almost 1000 years since it has appeared! unfortunately, it was so cloudy. My sister decided to stay in the car. I was the only one that got out because i find this stuff interesting. we were only out there because of me. I really wanted to see if i could find an opening in the clouds some magical way. And there was! right off to my left there was an opening. i looked up and the stars just seem to be moving so fast. i called out to my sister and tapped on the window. i ended up opening the door, I just wanted her opinion on how fast the stars were moving. but stars don't move. We joked around for a few seconds like, " what if it's a UFO." because i am a believer. then all of the sudden! 1.. 2..3... red triangles spin and take off! they were so high and far away. they made no sound. just in a few seconds. all 3 were gone. My sister and i couldn't believe our eyes! we went back to my house, and she sat on one end of the kitchen, and we drew the exact same thing. the kids in the car asked what was going on when we got in. so they witnessed us witnessing something.
