{tjd} Saw very large perfect triangle formation of three lights heading south on Big Run South.

Heading south on Big Run South after leaving the high school. Three very bright orange lights in the sky in my line of sight ahead caught my eye. They formed a perfect triangle with one light in the front and two in the back moving very slowly South. They blinked in and out randomly but never broke formation. All three lights then came on at once very bright and then vanished. The triangle formation was HUGE and flying very low. Not a plane- we are right around the corner from Bolton. My daughter and I both noticed and stopped our conversation to wonder what we were looking at. The lights did not come on again after they disappeared. There were quite a few stars in the sky at that time as well as airplanes much higher up. It was not dark- I would say dusk.
