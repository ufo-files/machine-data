Bright white orb hovered, then accelerated to great speed.

I was driving on a road I've traveled for years in the country. I saw a bright white orb hovering over a field about 30 ft above tree line height. It took off at incredible speed to the west and I lost sight over the tree line. 

I've told the story to family and friends since it happened. I can't remember the exact date and time, but this is a close estimate.
