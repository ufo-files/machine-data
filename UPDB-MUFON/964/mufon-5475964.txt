{nd} Myself and 2 friends observed a 200 to 300 ft diameter light decend and ascend in the fog

After an event that my girlfriend and I witnessed in 1974 two of my friends and I spent quite a bit of time searching the skies that summer. I do not know the month because it was so long ago. Beside an airport, to the left of the airport is a crop field, and again to the left a farm complex surrounded by trees, is the location where we stopped our car on Upper Valley Pk. Over the field, in heavy fog, we saw a ball of light dropping straight down through the fog. It may have come down as low as 300 ft or more off of the ground. We were parked in front of a lane which belongs to the house, which is to date, surrounded by trees. We exited the car and as the ball of light reached its lowest point in altitude we saw car lights come on at the end of the lane. Then the ball of light went straight back up and was going out of sight as an elderly man drove up to us from his farm lane and asked if we were having car trouble. The object made no noise and the one who asked us if we were having car trouble knew what we were looking at. We didn't. It went down and back up in heavy fog. It was not a helicopter and airplanes can't do what this did. It made no noise.The light was maybe 200 to 300 ft in diameter. It was between 1:30 and 3 a.m. The runway is to the right of the airport.
