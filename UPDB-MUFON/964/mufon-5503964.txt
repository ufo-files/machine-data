{kt}4 orange/red orbs observed above High Point monument in Hight Point Park.

Drivng home from Milford Pa. to be home in Westfall Township I observed at approximately 2100PM 4 orange/red disks information and hovering above High Point monument in NJ. 3 of them were about 1/4 mile apart in a straight horizontal line the 4th one was about 1/2 mile from the others but was on a different angle like at perhaps a 70 degree angle to the other 3. 3 of them were about 1000 ft in altitude the 4th on the right was substantially higher perhaps 2000ft. Appearance of the orbs suggest a flat appearance, the color never changed. No port/starboard collision warning lights. The just remained hovered then in a flash gone. Weather conditions were clear, no reflections of tail lights, no atmospheric anomalies as near as I can tell. I check my dashboard and traffic to determine if it was a possible reflection and was in the process of pulling over to take a picture with my phone. They were gone in the time it took to pull over. No other anomalies were observed or detected at that time. The event was about 4 mins when first observed to when I tried to pull over.
