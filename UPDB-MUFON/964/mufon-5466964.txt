{cak} very bright red/orange pulsating light in the middle and a cone with faint beams on both sides when it was descending

We were sitting on our deck overlooking the Ozark mountains. The bright light coming from the object made us notice the object. We thought the object was fireworks at first. The object was a red/orange in color sphere that had a pulsating light in the middle, the object appeared an then slowly flew toward us and then rapidly picked up speed and then very quickly descended behind a close ridge, while the object was landing we noticed after it had just eclipsed the ridge there was a faint red/orange upside down cone with two faint red/orange parallel beams of light next to it. We were in awe and were very scared and a little spooked. We lost sight of the object after it disappeared over the ridge.
