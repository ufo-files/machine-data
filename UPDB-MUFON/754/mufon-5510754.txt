Glowing orange balls of light three of them at different times

Happened near the harbor in Kincardine ontairo. At around 10:00 pm. Sitting on the patio at work and looked up and a orange ball of glowing light traveling across the sky. Pulled out my cell and started filming after it disappeared. A second one apered from the same as the first. It disappeared at about the same place. Then a 3rd one flew in al most same direction and disappeared a little quicker
