{rjl} Large amber object that vanished

I had just pulled into my apartment parking lot, it was about 9:45-10pm. I was sitting in my car smoking a cigarette, looking out the car window (the window was down). I saw up in the sky (at quite a distance) a very large amber colored orb. I couldn't look away, in my mind I was trying to figure out if it was a star; but it was much too large. It was stationary, it didn't appear to be moving in any direction. After maybe 30 seconds I noticed what looked like very faint blinking red lights on either side of this orb(?), shortly thereafter I remember this distinct feeling of "awe", I remember thinking "I have to tell my partner", who was literally sitting right next to me on his phone, but I couldn't say it out loud. While these thoughts were processing (and my eyes are locked on this object) it just vanished. It went out like a light. The sky was clear, I could see stars. As soon as it vanished, it was like I snapped out of this daze, and I jumped out of the car, yelling at my partner "it just vanished!"
I sat outside for a good 5 minutes hoping it would come back, but the only thing I saw after was a plane (it had red and green blinking lights and was moving in a straight direction).
