Bright sphere

A few years ago I was working in the Milpitas area doing some landscaping for a friend. It was a beautiful clear day. Suddenly I saw a bright globe passing by above me. Something like 150 feet above. As far away as maybe 50 feet west. Then I went and grab my camera from my truck. And try to take a picture of it . By the time I went and grab my camera the object was about a mile away. So I was able to take a picture of it. So I tried to zoom in but was not able to find out what the object was.
