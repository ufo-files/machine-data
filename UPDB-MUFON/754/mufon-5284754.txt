Saw a glowing ball with a cigar shape inside. Then a plane. Then a white object. Then another plane.

I went outside to smoke. When I stepped out I saw a helicopter. I was looking at the stars and saw a semi-transparent redish-orange light. It was roughly a slightly oblong circle but the edges were not crisp. It was 2 to 3 passenger planes wide and had a black cigar shape in the middle. It was going faster than a passenger plane and I saw no emissions or tail. It was also silent. It was headed NW from my position and disappeared before it was out of my eye line. I should have still been able to see it. It was about the same altitude I normally see passenger planes. There was plenty of sky left but it just disappeared. I then noticed a plane headed NW but it changed direction and flew NE crossing the flight path of the glowing object. A little while later I saw something glowing white streak across the sky. It was moving very quickly and was big, but not as big as the first object. It was also headed west. A few minutes later another ( or maybe the same one came back around) plane flew over following the same flight path as the first plane which crossed over the path of the two glowing objects.
I waited a bit but nothing else happened. I did not have my phone with me.
