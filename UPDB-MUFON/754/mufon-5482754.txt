{rjl} Black triangle hovering over TNT Ammunitions Plant

Near my home here in Chattanooga Tennessee there is vast expanse of land that was once used to manufacture military weapons and explosives. It is known by locals here as the TNT plant. The area was always fenced off until a few years ago when a road was opened up that went right through the middle of it. Though the road is still separated from the rest of the land which is covered with unusual looking bunker type structures and old train tracks. Trains still sit there at times and though it is supposed to be inactive I have witnessed things that would make one think otherwise. There is a dried out pond off the road with a bunker in the center of it. Once when driving past I witnessed a man standing by it in a radioactive suit. My cousin is big into photography and had stopped out there one time to take pictures of the trains. He was quickly approached by a man telling him the place was off limits and that there were high levels of radiation. None of this would seem too unusual except maybe to say that the place is definitely still in use. well one night I drove through there and had my first and only UFO experience. Near the trains there is an old water tower. Directly above it maybe 80 feet up the UFO was hovering at a slight angle. I could only see the underside of it. It was triangular in shape. There was a light in each corner. It lowered until I couldn't see it anymore because of the trees and trains. I drove on and to this day everyone is skeptical of what I had seen. Except for my friend that was with me. Well that is my story. Thanks for listening. I still drive through there quite frequently, but haven't seen anything since.
