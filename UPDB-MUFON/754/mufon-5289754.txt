I was a toddler. Was abducted from my bed

They had me on an examination table with a light above, several of them, it was very uncomfortable & I was terrified, though they seemed to try to calm me. They didn't verbally speak but I understood what was happening to me even though I was far too young to understand it. I had a near fatal asthma attack around that summer I believe, serious allergies, too. I had to ha e allergy shots at home just to go outside & be able to breathe. I seemed to outgrow it for the most part. There was a lot that I didn't accept. Wouldn't admit to myself that it was real until I was in my late 30’s. Never told a would. Not even my mother.
