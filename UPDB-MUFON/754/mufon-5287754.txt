Witnessed a light colored/transparent triangle with three soft glowing pastel yellow lights glowing in the corners traversing Edmonton in a NNW direction at 10:35 pm. Clear skies, late evening twilight with some stars out already. UFO moved quickly and was visible for only 4 or 5 seconds as it traversed about 30 degrees of arc aprox 45 degrees above the horizon going into the remaining twilight. It literally glided in total silence. Couldn't tell how large or how high it was but it was aprox the size of my thumbnail when fully extending my arm. The most impressive thing about it was how effortlessly it glided in silence. Like a dot on an oscilloscope.
