{dr}Fast moving blue object. Changed directions and hovered

Let my dogs out in backyard around 10:00pm. Looked up as it was a clear night and was observing Orion's Belt. The object streaked across my vision at unbelievable speed and as I attempted to follow it with my eyes it came to an abrupt stop. It then shot off 90 degrees to the right and stopped again. It hovered for approximately 25-30 seconds and then accelerated to the north so rapidly that it basically blinked out.
