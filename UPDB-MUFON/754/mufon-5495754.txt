{cak} Round metallic/chrome shape

I was in my backyard looking at a bird in my neighbor's yard with my binoculars when I noticed a very small reflection in the background. It appeared to me the sun was shining of the metallic object's surface. I focused in on the object and noticed that there were 3 objects traveling from right to left at the same speed. They were fling in a triangle formation but it was not a perfect triangle. There was definitely one leading the other two. I spend a lot of time in my back yard and have seen hundreds of airplanes at different altitudes. These metallic round objects did not appear to be aircraft. I believe the objects were at a very high altitude and they were moving faster than any object I have ever observed in the sky. I was puzzled when I first saw the objects and was disappointed I could not observe them for very long. I lost sight of them due to the high rate of speed. The whole event lasted approximately 3-4 seconds.
