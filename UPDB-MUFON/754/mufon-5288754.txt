ufo in sky froze my phone

I remember back in 2010-2012, I was in Palm Springs in a gas station with my parents and grandma. I looked up and I saw this dark disk (almost like a blimp) going as fast as a helicopter (and as low as a helicopter) passing me. It had blue and red lights going around the disk. I was in such amazement I asked everybody else around me "DOES ANYBODY SEE THIS" and people looked up in the air but did not think much of it or thought it was something else. My dad and I knew it was something different though. I took my phone out to take a video and my phone froze which I thought at the moment was VERY suspicious and it weirded me out for the rest of the day. It was going to fast for me to catch up with it.
