4 bright and blinking objects of various colors on Cusco

While the witness was with his family, the witness saw a bright object on the sky of Cusco. This happened on 23 November 2015 at 21:00. After a few moments, two more objects appeared. The three objects were in different locations, one of them was on the side of the Moon (the Moon height), one to the left side and another on the right side of the city of Cusco. After an hour appeared another object in the direction to the city of Cusco.

This happened to 30 or 35 Km of the Cusco, district Cachimayo, province of Anta, Department of Cusco.

Objects that were Cuzco were irregular movements (up, down, left and right).

At the end two of the objects that were on the city of the Cusco amounted at high speed, which was at the height of the Moon faded.
