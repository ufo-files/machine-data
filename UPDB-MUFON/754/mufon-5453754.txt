{cak} group of lights in night sky

As I was driving Home I thought I saw an airplane in the night sky and noticed there were no blinking lights. When I slowed my car in the middle of the road to look ( small two lane road outside of town) I noticed 15 to 20 starlike like objectsFlying through the sky in unison.. Different positions in the sky but same direction and same airspeed which was very fast as it went from left to right. As I got closer to home I noticed my friends were up and called them. Three of the family members then came outside and we witnessed Several star like objects (at least 6-7)Moving across the sky at approximately the same speed and elevation but at different times seeming to come out of the west and heading east to North east
