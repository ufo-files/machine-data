2-3 bright lights clustered, noticed smaller, dimmer redish light leading bright lights by about what seemed like atleast a mile,

I was out the back garden around 9:30pm, I went outside for a cup of tea and a cigarette. It was late but still lightish from the sunset. I could see about 5 stars almost directly above me and to the east where it was slightly darker, I kept looking up to spot more stars as it got darker. About half an hour had passed and quite allot of stars were out now, I looked up towards my neighbors tree and noticed a solid white light like a star only it was moving. As I observed it I seemed to notice a small hardly visible redish light leading it. at first I thought the bright light was getting closer to the smaller light but it must have been my perspective as they now seemed to keep the same distance and continued to keep that distance. the lights moved from east to west in a straight line but i heard no noises and no flashing lights like commercial aircraft, About 5 min after the lights went out of sight a plane with flashing lights came over which seemed to be higher than what I had just observed. No object was visible as the 2-3 lights that were close together was to bright to see through or past it. As for the small light it seemed to be to dim to make anything out and no other stars were blocked out as it past which makes me think the distance of the 2-3 lights was the actual size of the abject.
