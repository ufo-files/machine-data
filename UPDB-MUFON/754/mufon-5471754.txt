Saw lights outside of window so opened curtains. Then these orbs of light started shooting off in all different directions. Not sure how far away they were but coukd see them hovering above neighbors house/behind.

I was laying in bed on my phone at 12am. Then all of a sudden i noticed lights outside my window. I could see the lights shine through my blinds. I opened them and saw 3-5 different orb/shpere shapped objects giving off a very bright light, hovering above my neighbors house. They were the size of tennis balls but could have been much bigger as i could have been viewing them from a distance. I could not tell how far away they were. At first there was only 1 object but after about 5 minutes more came. There was about 3-5 in total. They then began to change direction very quickly and it seemed as if they were chasing eachother. They were moving to fast and changing direction so quickly and so frequently it could not have been a plane. This lasted for about an hour and then they flew off. I have seen nothing remotely the same since. I have 3 seperate videos of the event.
