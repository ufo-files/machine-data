{rjl} Witnessed multiple bright red orange lights flying in triangular formation and individually

At approximately 9:30pm on December 31, 2013 my wife and I were out watching the local fireworks at our home in Humble, TX. My wife pointed out a red orange circular light coming across the sky from the south east (OVER HOUSTON, TX) and suddenly (2) more appeared and the were flying in a triangular formation.. The lights appeared to me pulsating but NOT BLINKING.. constantly lighted. I began to video record the lights with my phone. we then noticed that more lights were flying in the same line of sight individually. They were all identical. We do have airplanes flying over our house non-stop as we are directly in the flight path of Intercontinental Airport. Most of the airliners that we see daily have red, green and white lights and they fly much lower and are very loud. The lights we saw made NO NOISE that we could hear. We also witnessed an airliner flying below one of the lights as they appeared in the South East. Total we witnessed 12 lights independently as they disappeared one by one. Then at about 12:15 am January 1, 2014, just a few hours later while I was talking with my neighbor Daniel, who had just arrived home, He an I both saw another identical light moving at the same pace coming from the northeast and flying northwest at a different angle but disappearing exactly where the others did. This one appeared closer and brighter.
