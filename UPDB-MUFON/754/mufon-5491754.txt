{dr}I saw cigar shaped object pass by while watching 1969 Apollo lift off

You probably have already seen this or was notified of this but while watching the 1969 Apollo lift off on the program called Nova "First Man on the Moon Show 42 Episode10 I saw a cigar shaped object that looked nearly as long as the rocket pass by it 5 to 15 sec or so after lift off..I believe that particular filming of the lift off was done by a family friend with the last name of Socoloff or something close to that..I've seen other films of the lift off in the past and do not remember ever seeing an object passing by the rocket while it was lifting off,it passed by at low altitude long before any stages separated from the rocket..I'm sure you must have seen this or was notified of this before or was explained it was just so noticeable I can't believe I could have been the only one to see this...Please let me know what this was if it was explained..
