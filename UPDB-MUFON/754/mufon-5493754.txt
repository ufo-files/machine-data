{rjl} cigar shaped hazy light

My brother was teaching me how to ride his bike. We looked up & saw this cigar shaped thing come up over the hill that was to the north of our house outside of Llano. It was a hazy bright light form. I was 6 years old but remember it well. We also saw 5 lights far in the sky one night all in a row move in the opposite direction immediately. Nothing at the time or now can manover like those lights did. I do not have video of this event. It accurd about 1962. I was 6 yes old.
