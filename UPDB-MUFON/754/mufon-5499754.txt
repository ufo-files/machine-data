{jd} Viewed three bright orbs in the southern side of interstate 4 while traveling west in Kissimmee area

Traveling with my teenage daughter westbound on interstate 4 approx. 3pm on a Sunday, both my daughter and I noticed three lights in the southwest sky as we were approaching the Kissimmee area. Traveling at an average speed limit of approx 70 mh, the further we went on I-4, the clearer these lights became and we noticed they weren't moving. And that they were actually orbs. They were the height of a plane in the sky and were very visible and clear to see. The sky was clear with no clouds and traffic actually slowed down as we were not the only travelers to spot these orbs. We continued to see them until we drove too far west to have it in our sight. They never moved.
