Object decended at a very controled rate... almost like a slow motion shooting star.

I was in my car waiting for my son at a friend's home on a dark winter evening with a clear sky when I observed a very bight and distinct light in the sky. It appeared to be larger than Venus and at least as bright. However, it was observed approximately 20 degrees north north east. I exited my vehicle to have a better look and it was then that it began a controlled decent that was not unlike that of a meteorite but much, much slower yet much faster than any aircraft I have observed. It's trajectory was almost vertical but not quite. It disappeared below the treeline and that is where my visual sighting ceased. I must emphasize that the trajectory was extremely unusual and controlled. It looked like a slow moving meteorite. Had it been a meteorite their certainly would have been an explosive event due to it's size. I grew up in city with a major NORAD base and have observed many types of military aircraft. This was unlike anything I have ever seen. Very bizarre. The object descended from approximately 30 to 35 degrees above the horizon and it's decent took approximately five seconds.
