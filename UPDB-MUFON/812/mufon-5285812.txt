Bright Odd-Shaped Object - Rapid MovementsnOver Small Area

Beginning at about 10 PM, I observed for approximately 14 minutes, a relatively small, bright, odd-shapped object which appeared to stay in a relatively small area of the night sky. (Its entire flight area could be hidden by my thumb at arm's length.) It moved irratically, sometimes slowly and at other times almost instantaneously in different directions, always returning to its home positon. It was at a great distance ... perhaps 10 miles or more away. Sometimes it was slightly obscured by high level cirrus clouds causing it to vary in brightness. (One dimmer pinpoint night star was in the sky, about 15 degrees away, which I sighted for reference purposes.)  The object was odd shaped and I would have to draw its apparent shape. At times there appeared t be a redish glow beind the "head" of the object.. At 10:15 PM I went inside my home to retrieve a pair of binoculars. When I returned outside, the object was gone. I searched for it again for almost an hour without luck.
