Bright, pulsating lights circling around craft - red, blue, green, yellow

I had come home from work 8pm and had been feeding my horses at the barn, went into the house, as I came back out of the house to go back to the barn a very bright light towards the south caught my eye- it was about the height planes fly but was much bigger and was moving slowly, lights not at all like a plane... I ran in the house to get binoculars--- as i focused it looked triangular, large, lights going around it red-green or blue- yellow.. it went straight up, then down and gradually further and further away--- as it vanished into the distance it appeared more oval shaped with the lights pulsating around the outside- very bright... there was no noise at all. I felt frightened because it really was not like anything I had ever ever seen before, and it just did not seem normal. I tried to justify what it was and could only think it was a satelite being repositioned or something...I have no idea what a satelite looks like....but the way the lights rotated around it, and seemed to pulsate, it just seemed to be not man made. I watched it for about 20minutes as it got smaller and smaller heading west.  about a month later another incident happened which I will submit another report about. I have since moved my bed away from my north facing windows, and get kinda frightened being outside at night. I am outside a lot since I have horses etc. there really is something unusual out there. maybe I live too close to area 54 (or whatever its called in Nevada...)... I had not really been a true believer in UFO's until these two happenings...
