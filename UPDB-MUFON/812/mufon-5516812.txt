{dr}Clear starry night, large bright light in northeast part of sky, peanut shaped, and remained stationery hovering in same spot . Still there after 30 mins.

It was around 9:30 pm and my husband and I let our dogs out to use the bathroom. It was a beautiful starry night with no clouds. We were admiring the stars when we saw something that looked out of place:
A very bright peanut shaped light in the northeast part of the sky. It was the only place in the sky that had no stars in it, just dark backdrop and this very bright, odd shaped light. When we first saw it we looked at each other and said, "Whats that?" We knew it wasnt a star. And we knew it wasnt a satellite. The more we looked at it, the more we were certain we were looking at something that was a UFO, either of secret high technology of this world, or something of the supernatual world. It didn't move at all during the whole time, just shone very brightly. When checking back 30 mins. later, it hadnt moved. That night we did some research and found out that the same description of this object was seen by someone else on 24th of July in Columbia, Mo, and on 28th of July in Bean Station, TN. That was confirmation to us that this thing was real.
