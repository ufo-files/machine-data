Lights in the shape of Triangle appeared over my house then disappeared in sync

I was out in my back garden with my dog just before bed-time around 11:30pm. The conditions were clear but not a lot of stars were visible due to the relative light at that time of night for this time of year. Noise pollution was at a minimum also.
I was sat in a garden chair I have, observing what is either a star or satellite that appears every night in the same spot at this time of year when from a westerly direction and moving relatively slowly, a triangular pattern of lights appeared from over the roof top of my house and seemed to hover. Three of the lights seemed to be at the points of the triangle shape with a further light in the centre of the shape. I couldn't gauge the altitude of the lights exactly but they looked quite high, I would say around 30,000ft and if so, the distance between the lights were huge. 
The lights seemed to hover for around a minute or so before in synchronisation, each light extinguished one after the other in an anti-clockwise pattern, with the central light being the final one to go out.
