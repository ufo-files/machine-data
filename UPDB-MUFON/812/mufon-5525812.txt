Round, red/orange glow, slow moving

I was standing on my deck around 10:30 PM looking at some fireworks to the east and saw what at first I thought was some kind of flare. It appeared just over the trees to the east of my house and looked to be climbing slowly. It started on a shallower trajectory before I lost it behind the trees to the south east. It was round with a reddish orange glow and moved about 30 degrees horizontally in a minute. Too slow to be a bottle rocket and went too far to be a flare. No lights showing it to be a plane. I would have been looking at it from it's right side where there should have been a green light on a wingtip. There were no anti-collision lights either. The weather report from the local airport in South Bend was showing broken clouds above 12,000ft and visibility over 10 miles. Whatever it was was below that.
