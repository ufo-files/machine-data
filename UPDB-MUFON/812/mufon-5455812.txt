{rjl} many lights in staggered line

At about 5:55 AM I was sitting in the back yard looking west enjoying the pre-dawn air and quiet. This is a normal thing for me, and I sky-watch while doing so. It was a mostly clear sky at the time.

I noticed what I at first thought might be a satellite moving WNW to ESE But about two extended thumb-widths behind and to the north was another one. Then another, and it repeated for the first 10 I saw. The eleventh light was close up to the 10th - nearly alongside. Then a wider gap and 4 more in the same pattern. Then another gap and a further group of 7 in the same staggered pattern.

I would estimate them to be about as bright as a magnitude 3 star. There was no blinking. The whole thing probably took about a minute.. i didn't see any flashes as from airplane strobes. I couldn't see more than about 7 at any one time. They were moving quickly.

I'm just 11 miles or so from Phx airport, and am used to seeing single planes flying very high over the airport, but I can usually see a strobe.

What I saw could have been a line of drones at 10000 feet or a line of jets at 40,000 feet, except there was no noise at all, and there were at least 22 of them. I have no way to know if I saw the first one in line.
