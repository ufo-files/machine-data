{rjl} sighting at night from santa fe university of art and design

I was at the santa fe university art and design. It was about 9 pm. There was a talent show and i got bored and laid down to the grass. Started to see the stars, suddenly one of the stars started moving, the another, then another and so on. At least I've seen 11 ufos moving in different directions and coming together. Two of them got away really fast at a speed of an asteroid or something like that. After 15 minutes, the clouds covered them.
