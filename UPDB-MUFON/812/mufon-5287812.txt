Craft with rotating colored lights appeared to be overhead matching our vehicle speed

Thursday, March 25, 1982. That is the right date, as I saved the ticket stub from the Beatlemania concert we had just attended at Welsh Auditorium. I was a passenger in a vehicle with two friends driving south on US-131 from Grand Rapids, MI toward Kalamazoo in a small sedan. About a half hour into our trip home we noticed an unusual object that was, for a lack of a better way to put, following us. It was more vertical cylindrical-shaped than disk-shaped. The top had different primary colored lights that slowly rotated. It was late, around 11 PM on a Thursday night and there was not much traffic. The craft appeared to be matching our speed of around 60 MPH and stayed overhead beside us a bit to the west for about 10 - 20 minutes on our southbound journey, likely between Wayland and Plainwell. There were trees that would block the view, then the object would still be there. At the end of the sighting we did not see it veer away, it just wasn't there anymore. I do not recall any poor weather conditions. Thank you.
