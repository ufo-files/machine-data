{rjl} Siting ocurred during a late August day in the year 1962

My friend and I were riding our bicycles on a Sunday cloud free early afternoon on the street where we lived when suddenly I heard a crashing sound. I stopped and turned around to see my friend had fallen off his bike and was looking upward. When I too looked up I saw what had caused him to be shaken up and consequently fall. Hovering no more than 30 feet above the parking lot was a flying saucer. There was no sound emanating from the object but it did have a bank of colored lights along the slightly tapered bottom. I cannot describe what the top looked like as I was so close to the object I was practically underneath it. Hence my ability to describe with 100% certainty the characteristics of this object. Within moments from our experience the object began to slowly move away in a straight path and then accelerated along the same path and disappeared within a blink of an eye. I remained motionless in awe rather than fear but my friend was profoundly affected and played a role in his failure to concentrate in school. To this day he refuses to speak of the incident.
