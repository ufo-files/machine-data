{dr}It was a perfect round orange orb

I was at my aunt’s house, we were watching TV we heard a loud boom, and the house shook. We all three went on the front porch and we all looked to the left because the orange glow got our attention coming from the lower field. There was a tree line between the house and lower field so we couldn’t tell what the glow was caused by. We saw an orange orb fly over the treetops from lower field and headed in our direction and descended 3 feet off ground we were on an opened porch 4 feet high and when it got to the it raised up about 2 feet off floor and came across the porch real slow. We were standing 3 wide my aunt in middle, she held her arms out and directed us back to the wall saying watch out let this thing come by, as it came by it being so close, we could have touched it. As it cleared porch floor it descended about 3 feet from the ground and continued though the yard and down into the woods. traveling not more than a slow walking speed. The next day my cousin and I went to the lower field and all the grass was flattened in a strange pattern. Since then, I have seen about 6 or more of these orbs a one-time flying in a chasing like pattern. My two sons, brother and cousin all saw these orbs at the same time.
