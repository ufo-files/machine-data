{jpg} 7-9 unknown orange globes descended from clouds and erraticly headed South above the houses across the street.

We were out on the front porch on a cloudy, cool nite, the 1st Sat in March, 2018. There were low clouds, and we were up in El Rito NM, (1/2 way between LANL & Dulce NM.)
 Suddenly 6 to 9 orange-ish rotating? spheres descended slowly from a bank of clouds, heading due South, about 10' above the houses across the street.    
  They glowed and seemingly "bounced" up & down as they moved, so it was hard to count them. They made no sound and had no streamers.
  It struck me that they were "controlled" by something, and were on a flight-path.
  We watched them for the 2 minutes they were visible, then they descended behind a house further South. 

  No time to get a camera even, as we just stared, since we'd never seen anything like them before. (we're both over 60 and ND/NDU). 

  Has anyone else seen them??
