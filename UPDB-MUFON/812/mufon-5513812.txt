(GS) Driving down 40 highway saw a solid metal sphere flying 100-500 ft above.

My girlfriend, best friend, and I were driving down 40 highway going West to Conoco Everyday on 40 highway next to the Tool Shed bar. As we were passing the Phillips 66 gas station by the Lake Tapawingo fire station I looked up in the sky as I normally do, sky watching. I along with my girlfriend who was driving, and my best friend in the back seat witnessed what appeared to be a SOLID METAL SPHERE of some kind... I have only seen something like this once before and I saw it above Blue Springs South High School about the same time frame around a month ago. The only way I can really describe this object is the Vaticans golden sphere statue that sits in the middle of the courtyard. I witnessed this object along with my girlfriend and best friend for approx. 32-35 seconds in broad daylight at 4:22 PM to 4:23 PM on December 10, 2012. It disappeared before I could pull out my camera phone.
