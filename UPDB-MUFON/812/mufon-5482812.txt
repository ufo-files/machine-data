{rjl} saw red glow coming from the NE and going West with no noise

At about 9:55 pm on 06/18/2016 my wife and I saw a red glowing object coming from the north east and almost right overhead and as it flew away it was going west. There was some white fluttering lights mixed in with the red,
it looked like a fire ball. The sighting lasted about 4 to 5 minutes and we heard no noise. We never thought to try and take any pictures.
