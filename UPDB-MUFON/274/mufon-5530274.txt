Bright white light shooting across under clouds then disappeared.

Driving North on 242nd a very bright light shot in a straight line from the East in a Northwest direction in front of my car well below the clouds. It was traveling very last and it only lasted a few seconds then just disappeared. It was not completely dark at this point and the moon was just starting to rise in the Northeast from my location. It was a very bright small ball of light, no craft of shape was seen, did not light up the entire sky or leave a trail. Meteor, shooting star or ufo?
