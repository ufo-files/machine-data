{cak} Light appeared straight overhead blinking approximately every 30-45 sec. Sometimes very bright, other times pulsating. After approx. 5 min, light moved slowly to SE from overhead position

I was outside on my back deck just looking at stars when I noticed, straight overhead, a bright flash. I kept my eyes on that position and a light slowly pulsed a yellow-orange color, starting very dim, then brightening up and dimming back out. After about 30-45 seconds, it flashed a bright strobe type light again, still in the same overhead position. As I continued to watch, it would slowly pulse every half minute or so. After a few slow pulses, it would flash in a strobe type manner again. I opened the back door and called to my wife who was actually asleep. She came outside and she too observed the light and it's behavior. After about 5-6 minutes of staying in one position straight overhead, it started to move slowly to the south east. I estimated that it took the object about 15 minutes to travel from about 90 degrees (straight up) to about 45-40 degrees South East of it's original location. After that period of time, I could not see it flash or pulse anymore. I still had a clear view of the sky, which was completely clear of cloud cover at the time. During the event, I tried to capture video with my iPhone video camera but there was not enough light to be picked up by the camera's capabilities. 

I would like to remain anonymous due to the fact that I am an active law enforcement officer. I have 30 years (in January) experience and currently serve as my department's Chief of Police.
