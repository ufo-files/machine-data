I saw a balloon-shaped craft but with a pointed top gliding across sky. It had a rod attached to the bottom:
I was in my back-garden enjoying the sun at around 2:10pm when I noticed a shimmering object (it was a clear day on 25/03/2020). The rod on the bottom of the object and the object itself did not seem to wobble like a normal balloon would. The object moved at a steady speed in the direction of the wind traveling west-wards and there was a plane in around the same direction but the plane was around 400ft-900ft higher than the UFO. I filmed the event with my Lumix Panasonic DMG-TZ6 camera. The object was a cream colour.
