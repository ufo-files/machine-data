{dr}7 yellowish orange objects were flying soundlessly over hollywood florida

January 1, 2013 appx 1230 we observed seven yellowish orange shapes traveling in a line but also appearing to be sig zagging as they came forward. The first two after traveling a distance apart came close together and then disappeared into the clouds. The last five just flew towards the first two and then disappeared. They appeared to be coming from the beach. They made no sound.  I do not believe that they were lanterns.
