{cak} I saw 2 of the same object in different directions before catching this one on film. I saw a shiny cigar shaped Ufo in descent through the clouds.

My girlfriend and I were laying on top of my suv enjoying the morning clouds after looking at the stars. My girlfriend spotted one and showed me then we saw two more. We thought it could be a jet but there was no emissions or sound. A cigar shaped wingless shiny object flew through the clouds. We have seen a number of sightings at this spot an others but it has always been at night and this was in the daylight so it seemed you could see more detail. We lost sight of the object in the clouds.
