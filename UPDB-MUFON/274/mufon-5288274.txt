Hovering metallic cigar shaped object

The corner of 40th and Nob Hill is a busy intersection and this was in the middle of the day sometime in the summer of 2004. I was in the Safeway parking lot looking up toward Eisenhower High School. I saw this vertically-ish positioned >40’long x >10’ diameter metallic object in the shape of a cigar hovering in the air. It looked and acted like a plain silver Mylar balloon with no seam, Windows etc. I looked around and no one else was paying attention to it, I asked a passerby and they just thought it was “weird”. So I went into the store to shop and when I came back out it was gone. It’s been so long I don’t remember every detail especially about timing but it was definitely mid-day in the clear summer sky.
