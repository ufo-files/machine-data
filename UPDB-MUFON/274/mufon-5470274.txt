Photos taken at sunset show a dark Trapezium shaped object with a massive bright and broad beam eminating from underneath it .t

My husband and I had visited remote lake Okataina for the day ( Bay Of Plenty , New Zealand ) . We had a picnic tea on the main shore while we waited for it to get dark ....we wanted to go and see the glow worms nearby . While we waited for darkness to descend I took 3 photos of a sunset . My photos show it was 10.1.18 at 7.53pm . My husband and I did not notice any UFO activity in the sky . However...over a month later while studying the photos I had taken...I noticed a dark Trapezium shaped object in the sky , with a very broad and intense light beaming down from underneath it onto the darker cloud below it . The lower cloud shows the beam shining on it very clearly . The beam of light eminating from the craft is the entire length of the crafts underside . It looks nothing like an aeroplanes light . The size of the beam it is eminating is considerable . It is not a normal shaped craft that we are used to seeing in our skies . I have darkened the photo slightly and zoomed on on the image
, so that the shape of the craft and beam of light can be seen in better detail . Original photos available to Mufon on request .
