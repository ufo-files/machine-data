{cak} i noticed bright, star like orbs in the sky S of Camano Island toward Whidbey Island. we witnessed them for over 2 hours.

My wife and I viewed 5-6 lights/orbs over Camano Island towards Whidbey Island. Most often, one would become extremely bright while others would dim out and you could faintly see them move both east to west and west to east throughout the two hours. Blinking red lights could be seen and were clearly commercial airliners. These lights were constant, bright, and obvious. Multiple symbols or geometric outlines were shown using the lights.
