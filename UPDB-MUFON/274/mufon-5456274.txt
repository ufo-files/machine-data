A glowing ball.

I was walking on the road,to visit a friend. And i only had one hour to be there.I was almost halfway,when i spot a glowing ball comming at me from left. At 11 years old,you did'nt have any way of beeing to late for dinner.But this glowing bal stopped in front of me,making me stop.And i did not try anything,it looked like it could burned me,looking like a sun.It was hoowering abouth one meter up in front of me. Almost making me do as they wan't me to do,but i wasn't sure of what. It than disseapered and stood in one meter from me on my back. I still had some time,so i took one step,and from there i remember only smal parts of what it wan't me to know,it seemed.I was looking at stars running against me,as if i had a car or a plane going wery fast during night.But i remember beeing several places around the world too.I suddenly was back where it took me from. I was not looking at the time,but my friend said i had to run back,because mye mother has called for me. Beeing 4 hours late,i finely got some food. But the strangest thing,was that there was 2 persons from the Aerforce talking englich and asked me what happened.That was 3-4 days later. That's my story,finely told to anyone else.And,not possible to get the date right. Date was like 21/08 1957.
