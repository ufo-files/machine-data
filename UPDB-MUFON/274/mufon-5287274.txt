Alien Handprint Left On Wall

After an abduction, the exiting grey left a hand print on my bedroom wall. It is still there. An analysis of the hand print reveals that it is much larger than a human handprint with distinctively different underlying anatomy. The hand print is still on my bedroom wall.

I have almost no conscious memory of the event. However, that morning, my girlfriend noticed the hand print. I have photos of the wall (moving in and setting up my bedroom, a new painting on the wall that I shared with a pen pal) and the hand print was not there previous to this morning.

I have been abducted hundreds of times and for a period from approximately 1985 to 2006 I would often wake up during these events and be conscious and aware. I've spent years observing. This handprint is a perfect match for one of the types of greys involved in the encounter.
