{rjl} Took a few nighttime photos. One photo revealed at least 7 possible UFOs.

I was at my computer. Around 9:45, I heard our dog barking outside. I put her in the house and then proceeded to observe a few "interesting" lights in the sky with very weak binoculars. I then decided to take some photos and went into the house to retrieve my camera. I took a few pictures and then had to change batteries. I went back outside and took a few more. Upon reviewing my photos, I discovered that one photo had at least 7 possible UFOs in it. One was a blue orb; one a red orb; 2 were aqua-colored unusual objects but ones I had seen recently in other individuals' UFO pictures; one was a white orb with an additional rectangular section; one was composed of 2 white orbs so it may have been a cigar-shaped UFO; and one was a white zigzagged-looking object that may have been a chevron-shaped UFO. during my observation, the objects all seemed to be hovering or stationary. I did not see all these with my naked eye; they all became evident when I reviewed my photos inside after the event. The objects may still be out there in the southeastern sky, but it is too cold to remain outside. I was a bit excited as the skies have been so dreary and cloudy lately, and I have not recently seen any unusual objects in the sky.
