{rjl} Silent orange and white pulsating light that dripped light from it

I observed a bright pulsating orange object in the north west sky. There were no clouds. It travelled in a straight trajectory directly over my head toward the SE. It was about the size of a small plane. It was pulsating orange and white and appeared to be triangular. On four occasions it "dripped" a white light that disappeared about a hundred feet down. The object appeared to be about 10,000 feet in the air and maintained a steady pace and altitude. The pace appeared about 200 mph. My husband is an amateur pilot. The object flickered a few times then disappeared almost directly overhead.
