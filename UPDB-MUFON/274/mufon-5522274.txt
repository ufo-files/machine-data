two orbs of cobalt blue attached together with white shaft(looked like a barbell)

Two witnesses obsserved at a 45 deg angle above the horizontal and above pine trees, two cobalt blue orbs connected together by what appeared to be a white shaft. The orbs were 2 times the diameter of the shaft. At first the orbs were closer and then moved further apart but still connected with a white shaft. After moving further apart, they were moving directly South toward Wedgefield at a high rate of speed, however, disappeared as if vanishing within seconds. Have never observed anything like this before. Both witnesses were shocked at the rapid speed and strange color with no sound whatsoever.
