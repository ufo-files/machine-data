MANY SIGHTINGS IN ONE NIGHT

two times earlier that night, between midnight and 3, I saw the same disc twice at least 30 minutes apart. I was with a group of friends who witnessed this bright light in the sky zig zagging up and down and back and forth, and then it zipped out of sight behind some trees. we continued doing our late night yard work and it showed up again, it moved the same way and left sight the same way.
