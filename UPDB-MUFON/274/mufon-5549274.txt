SMOOTH,METALLIC,NOT SHINY , BRUSHED METAL LOOK, NO NOISE,NO MARKINGS, APPX 30-40 FT ACROSS

I was on Life Guard duty at a Housing Development pool when I observed this object over the tree line from the pool . I am 22 years old , and this was somthing highly unusul . It was North of my location perhaps a mile away and about 400-500 feet high , on Hyw 29 outside of the city of Charlotte , very close the Hwy 485 by pass and close the the Charlotte Motor Speedway.At first I thought it was some type of Helicopter or Blimp , but I quickly realized it was something entirely different than anything I have ever seen before. It gave me an extremely eerie feeling and gave me quite a sense of anxiety . It moved steady forward with up and down movements in a sort circular pattern , without any sound . I then dissapeared below the tree line from my position. There were no other witnesses at my location at this time . This is in a very populated area and I would thing other in the area made this observation.
