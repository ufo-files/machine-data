UFO in Ontario

I was on my phone in my bedroom late Monday night. With my peripheral vision, to my left, I saw a set of three led looking lights. It was hovering in the air for almost a minute. Halfway through the colour changed from blue to red. Then from red it turned to burgandy. Finally it went from burgundy to the same colour as the sky.
