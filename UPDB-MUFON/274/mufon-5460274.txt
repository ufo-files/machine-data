{cak} Red orb in front of me on the road

Driving home at 10:45 p.m. in the distance ahead I saw a red glowing light. At first I thought it was the tail light of a motorcycle maybe a quarter of a mile in the distance. The closer I came to it the bigger it appeared. I thought I was seeing things and as I came about twelve feet from it I decided to slam on the breaks and swerve to the side of the road so I did not hit it. At this point the object the shape of a round ball started to crackle slightly like an old tv screen and the bottom of the ball morphed to the side thin and then like it folded on itself and was gone(disappeared). My daughter (16) was directly behind me a saw the entire thing but my husband who was riding side passenger did not see anything at all !?! This happened on M-37 South of Caledoina, MI and North of Middleville, MI.
