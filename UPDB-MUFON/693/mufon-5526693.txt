My Father worked at Todd Ship Yard.NASA would call him with the latitude and longitude of UFO's, he then would call home and give this info. to our Mother, and then we would go outside and take turns watching them. We did this as if we were watching a TV show each night.nothing to make a big deal over.Our family friends were Mr. Glen and also Mr.Carpenter.The ones we saw in Alvin, the guys in black would show up, they said they were salesmen and would go house to house and ask what they saw. They claimed it was gas burn off from the oil wells or swamp gas. As if we didn't know what that looked like. The men in black blocked off the road and would not let us go check on the lights coming from our friends home area.
