Strange bright lights for the past two weeks over Pevely

As reported to ASD by phone: I am seeing strange lights in the skies over Piedmont for the past two weeks. One is due west at approximately 2,000 ft. perhaps 5 miles away, it looked like bright airplane lights, some have a red light on top. Saw three last night. Then to the South I saw another one further away with two lights. THe objects move around slightly, and move very slowly across the sky for hours. Seeing them between 8:30 - 9:00 pm. Have seen these several times in the past two weeks. Last night two lights stayed in same location but raised up very slowly then stopped for 3-4 hours. I went to bed and did not see them leave. By 11:30 pm it was gone. Moved very slowly. I do not think they are planets because they are too close. Older people live in the area, other neighbors have seen them as well. Went to the police station and tried to file a report but they were not interested. South direction 60 degrees off horizon, one in the South 50 degrees. Heard no sound, no lights located on the bottom of the objects. I did not get a photo.I would like for an investigator close to me to come out and observe these objects.
