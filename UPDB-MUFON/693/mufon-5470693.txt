A bright light travelling horizontal at speed, before abruptly changing to a vertical course.

1. I was sat in the rear garden of my home enjoying the evening.

2. I noticed the object at approximately 9pm.

3. Uncertain as to what the object was? Initial thought was an aircraft.

4. Bright white illuminated object came into view, flying straight and level at extreme speed. The object was approximately 30 degs in relation to the horizon. The object changed immediate course, flying at 90 degs to the horizon, before disappearing out of view.

5. I was shocked at the speed and how quickly it changed direction. I am a qualified avionics engineer and have worked with/on aircraft, both civil and military for many years. Never have I seen something that moved at such speed and with that level of manoeuvrability.

6. Object disappeared out of view at speed at a 90 degree angle.
