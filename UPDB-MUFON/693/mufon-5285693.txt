Saucer shaped object at dusk

I was traveling west on highway 10 between Freemont and Weyauwega Wisconsin at approximately 8:45pm on September 1, 2021 when a series of bright lights appeared to the left front of my truck. They disappeared but then started up one by one around a circular object. They were extremely bright almost like exploding fireworks then rotated around an object. I couldn't make out the object but it covered the sky and the lights moved around it. 
I can only describe it as looking like the carnival ride that everyone stands in a circle.as the lights move. It appeared to be very large in size, maybe over 500 ft. In diameter. It was there for only seconds before the lights turned off.
I wish I could have stopped to film it now. I only made a sketch of what I saw the next day
