Black shape changing tic tac

Approx 3pm on Sunday 31/01/2022 a tic tac black shaped object was observed from the children's play area of Badger Park, Alsley Close chesterfield Derbyshire UK. The object made no noise was like a black tic tac standing on its end. It moved in a North Westerly direction perpendicular to the direction of the clouds which it was below. It was moving slowly and took 10 minutes to finally disappear. At some points during the observation the object looked to change from a 3d shape to 2d. It's altitude was apprx 500 ft increasing slightly as it travelled. My wife was a witness. Neither of us could identify it as an airplane (one flew past prior or a helicopter). It made no sound.
