{cak} Looked like the Phoenix Lights Stationary over Lake Michigan near Great Lakes Naval Station.

Was stationed at Great Lakes Naval Station 1976-77. Several times during this period I observed very large orange/yellow lights arranged in a round disc pattern completely stationary over Lake Michigan. This was observed at least 2-3 times over an eight month period, always in the same position, and always in the wee hours of the morning (2AM-3AM). Lights were spanning a distance of 2-3 feet at arms length, and were estimated to be 1-2 miles away. An uneducated guess of the actual size of the object was to be at least a mile across, if it was a solid object. However, only the lights were observed. No solid object could be seen between the disc shaped arrangement of stationary lights. I watched the lights for hours at a time from my fifth floor barracks window, looking downward from a bluff over looking Lake Michigan. No movement was ever observed. I never watched the lights until dawn, but no source of the light were observed during daylight hours. I had a commanding view of Lake Michigan from my barracks window and spent countless hours enjoying the view. But, in 8 months time the lights were observed only 2-3 times. I believe the lights to have been hovering completely stationary over the lake and not on the water. I serious doubt the lights to have been aircraft or watercraft due to the extreme cold and extreme cold that the sights occurred, while maintaining absolute stationary position over the rough fright water below for hours at at time. I actually thought that I was looking at a saucer shaped object over a mile across, but did not report it at the time due to the high security clearance that my naval career required.
