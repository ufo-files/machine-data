translucent rectangular pillar, hovering in place then flying away slowly south-southwest

I just gotten back from the store and was walking inside when I casually stopped outside the house and I saw something out the corner of my eye; I looked closer and I saw a rectangular pillar hovering in place probably half a mile in the air to the south-southwest. upon further investigation, it seemed to be translucent, with no propulsion source to be seen, simply floating in the air. I went around the corner of the house and called to my roommate to come look at it; by the time I came back the pillar was moving away, both ascending and going further away, in the direction of Mt. Shasta. My roommate could barely see it, and after about another minute and a half it was invisible from my point of view.
