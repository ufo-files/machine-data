{nd} at least 7 orange orbs traveled from southern horizon in a straight projectory

I was in church at a prayer meeting. A lady came in and got me to go outside and see something. Her husband had gone outside a few minutes earlier and had come in to get her to see this thing. Then she came to get me. It was approx. 7:30 pm eastern daylight time. The sky was dark and there were at least 2 airplanes visible with flashing lights. I witnessed an orange orb, looked kind of like a fireball traveling in a straight line across the sky from the southern horizon nearly straight north and disappear or fade out when it was about 75% across the sky. It was moving at a steady speed but not real fast. With in a minute of that one disappearing another one came up from the same area and traveled the same path. This was followed by another, that being the third one I witnessed. The gentleman who first saw the orbs had seen 4 of them before I came out, all doing the same thing. That would be a total of 7 that were witnessed. They made no detectable sound and seemed in some ways more of a natural event rather than an intelligent designed thing. It was however strange that 7 witnessed objects all did the same thing in a very ordered pattern. They seemed to not affect anyone or anything. I had no idea what they could be and they left no real impact on me other than scratching my head in wonder. This occured near Ohio state route 274 about 4 miles east of interstate 75 in shelby county Ohio.
