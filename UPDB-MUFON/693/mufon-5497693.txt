{dr}small red/orange lights that blinked like stars

While my wife and I were taking our dog for a walk she suddenly said, whats that? I looked up and saw about a dozen small red lights coming from the northwest traveling slow. I said I'm going to get a camera and ran back to the house and grabbed my camera and cell phone. By the time I got back they had all vanished. Within minutes another group of about nine came out of the north, by the time they got overhead they all disappeared at about the same spot. I tried to take pictures but could see nothing in the viewfinders. There was a third wave of 3 that did the same thing. I called 911 but heard no feedback after the report. They were all headed the same direction at the same speed and all disappeared in about the same place. My wife was visibly scared, I was mystified. What could that have been.
