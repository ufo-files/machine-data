Cylinder. White with black band. Short Appendages.

I was getting in my car and as I leaned to my right to reach for the seatbelt I looked up and saw the object through the skylight of my car. It was heading a little bit south of due East. It was a long cylinder with at least one black stripe. It had some apparatus at the front end and had small appendages in the middle. Perhaps They could be described as very stubby wings or panels. I ran toward the house to call my wife. But before I went in the house I stopped and took some pictures. In that short time the object had risen significantly and the pictures are blurry. It continue to travel easterly until it went out of sight occasionally being blocked by light gray clouds. My wife saw it as it was already passed our house. Flying to the east. It was still light out and the moon was relatively high in the sky and it reflected a lot of light.

Initially when I saw it it was much closer to the ground and the details were crisp and clear. It was silent without any noticeable wobble.
