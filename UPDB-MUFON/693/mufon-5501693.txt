{rjl} Light appeared around truck and surrounding area upto quarter mile

Location: M-43 between Bangor MI and Glendale.                               Approximately 3 miles west of Glendale 
Date: Sept 29, 2012 Saturday at 9:41pm
While driving home from Douglas MI. My girlfriend and I were traveling east on M-43. It was dark and no lights or cars on the highway. A bright light lit up the truck and the entire area around the truck. I could see the trees in the distance on the north side of the road. The light only lasted for a few seconds and then went out. I checked the clock on the radio to see if we gained or lost time. There were no planes or helicopters. There were no sounds-just a light from the sky. We stopped at our friends1 house and told them. They were skeptial of what we experienced.
