{rjl} Tube shaped item flew by going from my right to the left.

My neighbor and I were standing in my front yard talking and scanning the woods for movement. I noticed an object to my right following the ridge in front of us. At first, I saw a dot coming towards us from the west and as it got closer the shape became more defined. 
When I saw the shape I asked my neighbor if he saw what I was seeing. He saw it as well and we watched it go by. The object passed by in front of us and continued on to the left going southeast until we could not see it anymore due to the trees. 
The shape it took was tube like with rounded ends, like a pill capsule. It was white in color with the center had a black band that was less than 1/3 but more than 1/4 the lenght of the object wide.
After loosing sight we stared at each other trying to figure out what we saw. I asked him if he had heard anything as it went by and niether of us had heard any noise from it.
We were both excited because we had seen our first UFO. We realized we both had cell phones but neither of us thought to take a picture.
