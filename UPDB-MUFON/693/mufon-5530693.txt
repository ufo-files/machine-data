Two objects of short duration.

While cleaning up my patio I looked up and saw two very bright star-like objects moving very slowly in a South to North direction. I observed no navigation lights. I ran to my back door and grabbed my Binoculars for a closer look. By the time I returned I watched them dim very rapidly so that I COULD NOT SEEM THEM THROUGH MY 10X50 Binoculars. I watched for a possible re-sighting for about 50 minutes and came in to write this report. The objects were almost vertically overhead and had a luminosity about twice that of Venus. They trailed one behind the other. There were no nav lights or jet noise to be heard. I live near an airport so I am familiar with aircraft distinctions. wish there was more to tell but thats it.I was quite calm during the event as I had a feeling this was not ordinary aircraft I am accustomed to seeing in this area.
