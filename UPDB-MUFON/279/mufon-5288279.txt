Cigar shaped lights

I thought it was Starlink and then I did some research and Starlink was no where near me at time of sighting. I was on US 89 heading north towards North Ogden on Doordash delivery. I saw what I thought was a meteorite string breaking up. But it stayed the same shape. I pulled over and began to film. It moved very slow from NW to E above Ben Lomond. Not a powerline not Starlink, not anything conventional. No noise. No flickering. It was on a diagonal axis with the lights, I'd day about 10, all in perfect unison. Not even starlink can be that precise. Sorry for the quality of vid.
