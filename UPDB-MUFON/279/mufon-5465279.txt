{tjd} While driving north on Division my daughter and I observed what looked like a 747 hovering in the air to our left. After observing it for about 5 minutes it turned west and flew off.

Okay, my daughter and I saw the weirdest thing October 4th around 1:45 pm. We were in Spokane, Washington driving north on Division from downtown and she suddenly asked "Is that a balloon?" I looked out the driver's side window and saw a plane. It was the size of a 747, but it was close enough to see that it did not have all the windows one would have. It had a black stripe where the windows normally are. It had the normal pilot windows and one behind where the passenger windows are. We were looking at the complete side view of it and it was just hovering in the sky. Not moving forward at all and making no noise at all, We don't know how long it had been there before she saw it and, while we were watching it, it remained stationary for a quite few minutes, Then it suddenly made an abrupt right turn (heading west) and flew off. It only moved slightly forward to make the turn. I last saw it was heading west. I kept thinking 'Why isn't it falling out of the sky? Those kind of planes don't hover."
