{cak} Three large round reddish orange objects

I observed this incident on new years eve on 12-31-2013 at around 2340-2345 hrs. I was in my car traveling south on S.R.1001s in Natrona Heights Pa. and saw three large ball shaped objects in the sky hovering three in a row over a residential section of town and lined up side by side a few hundred feet apart. I was unable to make out what exactly they were but they definitely had my attention. I would guess they were 400-500 feet in the air. I then turned left on to Jefferson Ave and stopped my vehicle. I then shined a spot light on one of them but they were to high up to make out what they were. They then briefly formed a triangle formation for a short period of time and after that formed into one large reddish orange ball and left the area flying east. I could hear other people in the area outside talking, presumably to celebrate the new year asking "what is that?".
