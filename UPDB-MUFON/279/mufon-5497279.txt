{rjl} Like a fat bullet, bright saphire, appx 500' alt. no sound,appx100 mph.Appx.

Out on my deck having coffee when all of a sudden this bright blue object shaped like a giant bullet goes cruising by. It didn't make a sound, just went straight. I lost track of it as my view became obstructed by a neighbor's house. It then must have headed over the ocean. This object looked like it was out for an early morning cruise. It was the coolest thing I've ever seen.
