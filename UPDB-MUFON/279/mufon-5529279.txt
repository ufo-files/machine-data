very slow object moving across sky with glowing green discharge

We were driving back from nyc in november 1995. We were heading northeast on route 17. It was dusk like out....and while looking through the windshield......we saw an object that was hard to see its shape. But it was large and low. It was probably less than a thousand feet up. But what was so striking was that the object had a glowing green discharge coming out what would appear to be the back of the craft. It was going so slow that it didn't seem possible. Then it just kept moving across the sky and between the small hills.
