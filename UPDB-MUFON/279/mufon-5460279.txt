I stepped outside to get some air. Noticing a shooting star I gazed to the heavens. It was a extremely clear night. Low on the horizon I noticed an extremely bright light that was moving oddly. It skipped across the sky like a flat stone thrown on calm waters. The light was very bright emitting a bluish white color. It moved like nothing I've ever seen. It was not from this world.
