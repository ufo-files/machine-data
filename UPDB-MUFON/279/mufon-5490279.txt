{cak} Outside watching for meteor shower. Saw 4 stationary lights in pairs same distance apart. They all flashed different colors. One had a red light beside it. All 4 too low to be stars..

I was outside watching for the meteor shower, when I saw 4 lights in the sky near the horizon about a quarter up in the sky. They were in two pairs and the same distance apart. They did not move and were not stars. They were in the northeast sky. I got out my binoculars and could see that they were fluttering, wobbling and emitting different colors of light, blinking. The one on the very far right had a smaller red light that was close to it. I observed them for approximately 15 minutes and they did not move that whole time.
