{cak} 4 brilliant white lights, illuminated an edge top & bottom, top & bottom looked cigar shaped

I was traveling East on Early Mills Rd when I first saw it. It had four brilliant white light, no pulse, blink or color change. They were equally spaced apart. At arms length they appeared about the size of a shirt button. The lights illuminated an upper and lower edge above the lights. Above that edge, it appeared to curve into a cigar shaped. The lights didn't illuminate far enough to tell. It moved very slowly and smoothly in a constant direction, East. I heard no sound. I drove on Pheasant Road as fast as possible to show my wife, but till I got in the house to show show out back, it was no longer visible. In a news feed, UFO's have been seen in the area very recently, Dauphin County.
