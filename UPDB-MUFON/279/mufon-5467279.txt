{td} Bright orange looking like a planet. Got dim then disappeared. Reappeared again repeating same pattern then disappears slowly.

On the early hours of July 16,2018 my husband observed a bright orang star looking object in the sky. He continued to watch it as it faded in and out. There were no clouds in the sky.At approximately 12:20am my husband called for me to come to observe it as it had reappeared. I thought it was Mars at first then it began to get dimmer and then brighter. From bright orange to light orange.As it starts to fade away our outdoor street light went out for several seconds. We knew it wasn1t a planet and we were afraid. It disappeared at approximately 12:40 and didn1t return.
