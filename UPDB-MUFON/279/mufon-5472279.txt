Glowing ball of white loght

Was on vacation in Perth scotland, staying in my parents attic with my partner it was roughly 4am, partner had just returned from a town, got out of bed and passed the window noticed what looked like a really bright star observed it for a minute or so it was at least 10 times brighter than anything else it also appeard to be within the atmosphere and glowing like plasma it did not appear to move. I stopped watching after about 5 minutes so I could tend to my partner.I never spent any more time watching, simply went to sleep. I found it quite strange after the event I seemed to completely forget about it didn't show the video to anyone until at least a week later the only reason I'm reporting it now is because I recently seen a video of a similar object that seemed to disperse the clouds around it.
