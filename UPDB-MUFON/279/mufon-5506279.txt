{nd} Starlike object moving quickly from low elevation to high

November 24, about 5:30 PM: As my mom and I were driving home, south on I-5, very near 34, and I saw a WEIRD something. I was stargazing, hoping to see a UFO, and then I saw the fast ascending light. At first I thought it was a meteor, but this object was traveling up from the horizon. I was super-excited. We drove past where the object was ascending and I lost sight of it.
It was a REALLY light red, almost white, and it was going upwards at a slant. It looked like it was about 500 yards from a big-ish house/barn (I couldnt tell which) and it was bright and shiny. Well, not that bright. It had about the bright-and-shininess of one of those really far away stars. It looked bigger though. From where I was, it looked about the size of the tip of a dull pencil. IT WAS WEIRD.
