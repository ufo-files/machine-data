{dr}I thought it was a B-2 Bomber but it had winglets and a sharper triangle shape and was silent

My daughter and I were driving through town (Lawrence, Kansas) around 5 oclock in the evening and were sitting at a stop light facing North. I was watching Swallows flying and then noticed what I thought was a bird moving very slowly. I realized it was not a Bird and at first thought it was a B-2 Bomber. It was flying SW at an unknown altitude but either it was large enough or low enough that I could make out detail so as the light turned green we pulled into a parking lot and got out and watched it. It continued flying SW and then banked due south. As it turned I could make out it's shape better. It was a sharper point in the front than a B-2 bomber and it had winglets. It banked again due west and flew out of sight. It was completely silent. I took two pictures with my iphone but of course they don't show much. My daughter and I have both seen B-2s often as they are based in Missouri and have flown over Lawrence several times. They are loud and more rounded than this was.
