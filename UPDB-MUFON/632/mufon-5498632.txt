{dr}Flashing star-like object

I was going out to my vehicle on my lunch break ( I work 3rd shift ) when a flashing star-like object caught my eye. I continued to watch it for a few minutes, intrigued, expecting to see it move, assuming it was an airplane. However, the object stayed stationary and continued to blink in a strobe like pattern between blue, red and white. It was very far away so it is hard to say if it was completely spherical or if it was elongated. I called upon some people in a forum who dismissed it as ( possibly ) Sirius. IDK, This was the most vibrantly "twinkling" star I've ever seen and its colors were vet distinct, more so than what my iPhone was able to capture. It was located in the East/SE sky. I am in Kalamazoo, Michigan. There was no sound. It stayed stationary the whole time I observed it between 3:30am-4:30am.
