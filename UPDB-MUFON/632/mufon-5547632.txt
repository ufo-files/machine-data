Very large object with orange lights arranged in a V shape @ 1500' alt. and moving east to west.

I was traveling south on Watts Rd. and upon approaching the traffic light at Kingston Pike, I noticed in front of me a very large object with orange lights arranged in a V shape. The object was moving from east to west at about 1500' and decreasing in altitude. I watched the object for no more than 10 seconds until it went behind a 1000' hill that was about 2 miles from my vantage point. The object was moving at about the speed an aircraft would travel when landing. I never saw it go back up and there is absolutly no place to land in the area where I lost sight of the craft. I would estimate the object was 8 miles east of Lenior City, TN when I lost sight. Below is a link to a map of the area.
