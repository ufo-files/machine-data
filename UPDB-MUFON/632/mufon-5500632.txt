{cak} glowing orb-like object flying very high in the night sky, stopped and ejected something north of it, then continued north west direction until we lost it behind the clouds.

My husband and I were swimming last night between 10:30 and 11 PM. We were star gazing and he pointed out the object in question. We both witnessed a glowing orb-like object flying northwest in the night sky. About 5 minutes after we saw the object, it hovered and ejected something northward before continuing northwest again. It emitted a strange light that increased in intensity twice before dimming back. It was much higher than an airplane would be, as an airplane flew by about 10 minutes later and we compared what we had seen to the airplane flight. After about 10 minutes, we lost sight of the object behind cloud coverage. We were both in awe. I admit that for the hour or so following the sighting, I felt very strange in an almost melancholic mood followed by a headache before going to sleep.
