Went out with pet on the morning of 11-25-19 around 5:30am and, as always looked up to he stars. It was quite clear that morning. The Big Dipper was almost directly overhead, slightly to the east. At first I didn’t realize it was the dipper as there was a line of stars, I thought, in the handle. There were 3 and I chucked to myself thinking that Orion’s Belt moved. A fourth appeared and they started moving in a northerly direction in a straight line.

As I watched them go, a couple more lights caught my eye from the same area. The last few I saw appeared as I watched the same spot, not watching them as they moved off in the same direction as the others. When no more appeared I watched the ones I could still see until they were out of sight.

While I was watching, I did see 2 meteors enter the atmosphere, one from the west going east and then one traveling in a northwesterly direction. Neither came from the spot where the lights appeared.

I did look up my location and 46779 is at 41.57 latitude and 84.99 longitude.
