Two dark objects hovering in the sky.

I was taking pictures of St. Michael Church at Wychwood Avenue and St. Clair on Tuesday March 31st. I usually take two pictures to make sure the quality is good. Around Thursday of last week I was checking out the pictures when I realized that there were two dark objects in the picture. The two objects show up in both the pictures which were taken within 5 seconds of each other. The objects do not look like any birds, balloons or commercial aircraft that I know and everyone I show the picture to is unable to identify the objects.
