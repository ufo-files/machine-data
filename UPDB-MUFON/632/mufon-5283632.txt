This location, N.W. corner of Douglas County at its altitude would see nuclear power station to north (off line?) and the SAC Air Force Base in Bellevue; and was at the point SAC planes, Commercial cross-country, and those landing at Eppley Airfield separate. SAC goes south, Eppley traffic comes lower, and the cross-country traffic is very high. Note: military planes seem to go in and out on several paths; while the commercial stay on just two or three tracks to land N to S, or S to N. Military fighters vs. the large planes seem to use separate routes too. (Fighters seem to GO AS THEY WISH.)
I was focused on whether it was lower & Piper Cub sized, or up high and length of a Boeing 757(20' vs. 150'), and determined it was the latter. It did not appear to move - like it was observing - then just blinked out & gone. There were very few scattered clouds but my sight line was never obscured. A commercial jet was within a few miles at a higher altitude going East. OBJECT WAS NORTH OF Hwy 275 JUST EAST OF THE Hwy 36 EXIT. (Estimated 2 miles north and 6 miles high.
