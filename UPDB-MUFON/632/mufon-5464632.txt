{jpg} Central California nighttime bright light observation

Around 11 pm, on November 6, 2018, I was sitting on my couch playing a video game when I bright light caught the corner of my eye. It appeared unusually lower in the sky and was brighter than everything else in the sky. I ran out to my balcony and immediately turned on my iPhone camera. The best way I can describe what I saw was it was a bright amber-orange light and it moved horizontally across the sky. There was not tail behind it or port or starboard running lights that I could see. There was no noise being emitted that I could hear. I disappeared from my view behind a large tree that is front of my building. It did not re-emerge from the other side of the tree, so I assume that it either went dark or disappeared around the surrounding hills.
