{dr}disk with strobing lights in Cumming, Ga.

I was standing in front of my parents house in 1999, and I witnessed a disk shaped object in the Polo Fields Neighborhood hovering above the treetops, just outside the neighborhood above the woodline. Strangly, my parents do not recall seeing the craft. Its as if I saw it and they never remembered seeing it.
