metalic triangle

I was driving west on the 30 bypass between Exton and Downingtown,when I noticed a bright metalic light ahead of me in the sky. As I got closer to it and looked up in appeared to be a triangular shaped metalic object just spinning around stationary. It was not a plane or a helicopter thats for sure. I don't know what it was. I thought to myself other people have had to have seen this as well. I looked in the paper this morning, but did not see any mention of this. Maybe people saw it but only told people they knew about it. It was pretty weird.
