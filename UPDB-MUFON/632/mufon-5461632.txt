Observation of UFO in Tenerife Island, Spain

We go to Tenerife to play soccer tournament. This day is not permitted for the players to go out of the rooms. This night I can sleep and I go to the balcony to eat a sandwich. When I see this light stopped on the firmament. Very bright, with colors gold, orange and red.
The UFO make a little motion slow to one side and zigzag movements and disappear on the firmament.
In this moment I don’t know what’s really happening but in the others two balcony my team partners said “ do you see that light?” In this moment I understand that’s a real event.
I’m not scared but is possible if this teammate don’t assure that they see this light, we never talk with others.
Obviously the people never accept that was a UFO event.
