Silver with some glowing lights of blue and violet out one side. The other one was white.

I was sitting at the Bluewater Bridge customs lineup. I was going to work in Port Huron from my home in Sarnia at 0830 on Monday, April 2, 2012. I caught a glimpse of an object off the corner of the customs building which was hovering. It was silver and gave off a couple of coloured lights from one side. I tried to find my phone to take a picture and when I looked back up, there were 2. The other one seemed either smaller or farther away and was a white orb. I had to go through customs and when I came out, they were gone. I never saw them leave. They were just gone.
I was so excited, I couldnt wait to tell people.
