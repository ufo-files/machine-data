Huge green fireball moved in a downward motion over Scottdale.

Scottdale, Pennsylvania. My son and I stepped out our back door onto a deck. Time was 10:59 p.m. I looked up into the northeast sky and saw what appeared to be a huge, green fireball moving in a downward direction. I yelled for my son to look up - which he did - and we both watched it as it disappeared from our vantage point below a neighbor's roof line. I probably saw the object for no more than 3 seconds; and my son saw it for probably just 2 seconds. His description is the same as mine - about the size of a nickel held at arm's length. But he says there was fire coming from behind it. I quickly did a time check. I remained outside for an additional 30 minutes, but did not see it or anything like it again.
