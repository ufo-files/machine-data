{rjl} Large delta winged object. Dark except 2 white lights on end. Silent as flew over.

I was driving home between 10 and 10:15. At first I noticed just two white lights flying in paralell. I kept an eye on it as I drove. It was going roughly the same way I was. At one point I was able to pull over for a bit to watch, as something seemed off about it. This is when the object flew directly overhead. It was very large, and flew like a blimp, but was delta or wing shaped and made absolutely no noise. I could only see a dark outline against the sky, along with the two lights. Im used to seeing aircraft in the area as the area I was in is on the approach for the local airport. There was also other aircraft that I could see and identify by their lights in the area. This craft only had two small solid white lights on the wingtips. The craft was going very slow. Maybe about 20 kts/hr. As it flew overhead it took up quite a bit of the sky. The best thing I can relate it to in size is a 787 that I have seen on takeoff, but again, slower and no noise and maybe a bit bigger even. After watching it for around 10 minutes, it finally went beyond a hill and I did not bother chasing it. It took me so long to report it because I guess I just hadnt thought about doing so. Im one of the types that believes that there is tech the government doesnt like telling us about. Chalked it up to that.
