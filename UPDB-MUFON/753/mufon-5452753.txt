1 Red and Black Blinking Orb

We observed 1 red and black reflective blinking light traveling too fast to be a single balloon. The orb moved from west to east then we lost sight of it. I could not tell how large the orb was but what I could tell is that it was not a bird, plane, drone or helicopter. This thing was moving way too even to be a balloon. Anyway, my husband took video but nothing showed up. He took pictures and you can't see it except in one picture that barely shows it. I am upset that it was not captured in a photo or video. But if anyone in our area sees this I hope it is still in the area.
