{rjl} 4 red/white lights ascending moving west

Was at our office in San Leandro. Stepped outside for a smoke. Where our office is you can watch air traffic leaving and arriving and far overhead passing by.
As I stood there a red/white unblinking light ascended upwards in the west.It appeared that the light arose closer to the Peninsula, San Mateo area. From where I stood they appeared from behind a tree across from our office. So I can't be exact. And they all appeared to ascend in between other arrivals. Planes tend to be equally timed in their landing and takeoff. Typical departures in Oakland are either No. or So. SF flight departures I'm not sure of. After watching countless aircraft arrive and depart what I saw was NOT a normal flight path. Across the bay I am not aware of any military area's except further south of our location.The 2 busy airports are San Francisco and Oakland. The red/white light ascended and with a slight angular movement then disappeared in the clouds. Event lasted a couple minutes
Went back inside and about 8 or 9 minutes later I saw through the window 2 move of the same kind of lighting ascending from the same area. Stepped outside again and both light rose on the same path and disappeared in the same manner. No sooner than those 2 disappeared another rose and did the same. All movement as far as I can determine were all westward towards the Pacific.
