3 oval objects flying in formation over the ocean

In May, 1975, my wife and I were on the Hawaiian island of Maui, returning from Hana heading toward Kaanapali Beach. 
We were driving west on Hana Highway after dark, possibly around 9 PM, when we noticed 3 oval shaped objects flying in a 'V' formation over the ocean from the south. The ocean was very clear that night and the objects were very visible.
The objects emitted solid light like that of incandescent bulbs and they were rapidly approaching us. We were very concerned that we could be abducted by these alien craft since we were the only car on this remote road. We decided to pull off of the road facing the ocean, and turned our lights off to become less conspicuous.
The objects continued to approach our location, then they slowed and hovered off shore. We hoped that they might have lost sight of us.
After what seemed like a very long time, one by one, the objects left. Their departure was incredibly fast and they disappeared into the night sky. They made no sound; all we heard was the sound of the ocean.
We told our friends and family about our experience, but we never reported it to any official organization until now.
