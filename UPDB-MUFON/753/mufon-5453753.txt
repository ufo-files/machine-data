{cak} 5 triangles

We observed around 5 triangles with tails moving along the same path at high speeds and at very high altitude. Some trailing lights of the triangles would sporadically move up down left right while blinking to no particular pattern. We’ve seen many things before over the Minnesota river valley but never reported them. We are happy to report this. It was to far away so I wasn’t able to get video or photo evidence.
