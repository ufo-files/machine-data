{dr}WE saw a bright orange orb shining brightly then ascend and fade.

We were driving back from a 4th July pool party. We were pulling slowly into our house street and as we got out we noticed a bright orange glowing light in a starlight shape which ascended in straight line upwards. It shown brightly, remained stationary, not moving and then slowly moved up, then the light just faded. This took about 2 minutes. I can say this was not fireworks - we know the difference. My daughter took a picture from her phone (hope to download later). My older daughter and fianc1e were close behind in their car and also saw the light. We are mostly ex-military so we can confirm it didn't behave like a plane or helicopter. About 10 minutes later we heard a helicopter overhead. Did anyone else see this?
