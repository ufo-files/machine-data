It was though a motorbike head light was being turned on and off. It randomly changed position and stopped midair for a minimum of 30 sec.

I was sitting outside in the spa with my son and I noticed a light flicker on in the sky and it was as though someone had got a bright light and turned it on and the light just sat there and then it turned off. It then come on a little bit but not as bright but still bright enough to see and to notice that it was not a star or satelitte. Then when we were looking at it, it's slowly went to the left and it was not illuminating at that time. Then it started flashing in one spot, and kept on flashing in the same spot for about two minutes. I then went insi to grab a camera, the back light was turned on and I lost sight of it for about five minutes then it reappeared to the far left. It was a dull flash however it flashed for about thirty seconds in the same spot then it just disappeared.
