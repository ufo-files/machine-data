4th of July UFO

UFO sighting witnessed over Coeur d Alene, Idaho at 9:15pm during the 4th of July. 

It was a clear evening with a bright full moon. Firework were launching throughout the area and we were setting up our firework celebrating our nation's independence. A family member was looking up and saw a strange white glowing object hovering into position. The lighting of the object was very bright white. My wife and I looked up along with our daughter to take a better look at the object which started to defy the known laws of physics. It moved back and forth, shifted a few times. I have seen drones, but this didn't behave like a drone due to its speed. I shouted what I saw in the sky and which may have caused my neighbors to look to where I was pointing. It is unknown if they saw the object, but I watched the object suddenly accelerate away into the sky in a straight path out of sight. I immediately uploaded what we encountered to social media to get answers, but a few others may have witness the very same object. There are a lot of non believers, but I believe I know what I saw.
