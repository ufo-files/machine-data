{rjl} Orange lights, orb like, one in the center and 5 more surrounding in circle

Was stargazing while having a night swim. Clear night. I have 10 ft walls around the pool and saw an unusual light to the right of the moon, if you were using a ruler it would be like 4 inches to the right of the moon, but a little lower than the moon, I know, illusion, but I do not know how else to explain. I could only see orange-reddish lights. It was stationary when I was first observing it. Orange in color, round in shape, there was a center light and 5 lights, the same in size, circling the center. Suddenly the outer orbs separated from the center, and hovered, and a couple darted off in different directions. I jumped out of the pool and ran into the house to get my husband and a camera, when we returned in like 30 seconds, at the most, there was only a few orbs left. The center one and another went in the direction (further west) away from us till they were just pin point, like orange stars, they stayed parallel from one (one west, one northwest)till one left(the NW one) and one stayed behind. There was an aircraft that approached the area when there was three left and a few more aircrafts entered the same area after they had dispersed at different times. I did not get it on camera because it was happening so fast I did not want to take my eyes off it to operate the camera. A few minutes later the power went out, not sure if that had anything to do with it but it was a clear night.
