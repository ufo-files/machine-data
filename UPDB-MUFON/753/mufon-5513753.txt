(GS) Ball of light traveled over me

I was parking my truck and getting out, looking around as one does. I saw it come from behind my friends house and slowly travel over me before disappearing. I couldnt see anymore because of trees. I asked my friends a few mins later as it would have hovered right over them but they hasnt seen anything.
