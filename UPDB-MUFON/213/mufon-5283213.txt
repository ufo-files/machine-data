7 Oval Glowing Objects

I wake up in the middle of the night on 2016 June(exact date i don't remember) at 2:18AM and get outside to smoke a cigarette (i am a chain smoker), i sit down ,it was a clear sky no signs of humidity ,no clouds...beautiful clear sky with a lot of stars(noticed all these later after sighting),so i was standing there smoking my cigarette when i begin to hear a buzzing sound ,a high pitched sound like: Zink...zink...zink...zink when i instantly look up in the sky ,then i saw 7 oval shaped object in a formation of V moving slowly above my house they moved so slowly that i could observe them ,count them ...they had an orange color ,and at the bottom they had a blue circle when they passed above my house i begin to run outside of the garden to the streets to get a better view ...i saw them slowly passing away above the houses in the direction of Poggiardo from Minervino di Lecce. i was shocked by the sight i didn't even think of taking photos i was in awe...the sighting had a duration of 1 minute aprox.
