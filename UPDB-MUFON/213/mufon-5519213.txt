Just these lights of red and white and this object stayed in 1 place for 40 mins. I do have pictures. It may be a plane however, I never saw a plane stay stationary like that before?

I went out on my deck around 11:30 p.m. to have a cigarette. It was a clear night and a lot of stars were out. I noticed this twinkleing and thought ...that was a bright star. I came in the house and got my binoculars and realized that it wasnt a star because of the red lights so I figured seeing that we are close to an airport that it must be a plane. I sat down and just watched it and after 10 minutes it still didn't move. So I looked thru the binoculars again. The object seemed to be jerking almost like doing little circles?? This object stayed for about 45 minutes. It never went away it just seemed to go farther away up and then just disappeared.My wife did take some pictures which I have included for you. It was really freaky. 
Like I said this might have been a plane and I wouldnt have even filled this form out but my wife did see something about 5 months ago.

Like I said we live by the raleigh airport and we see the flight pattern of the planes all day long...the ones that went over our building just never ever happen.
