I witnessed an unknown object on the night 8/21/2020. It was around 10:00pm. I saw a bright orange light coming from behind the clouds. Once it was in front of clouds it stayed in place. The color reminded me of a forest fire at night. it must been atleast 5 miles away and high in the sky. It was round initially...like a shape of a ball. It had to been huge for me to see so distinctly from far away. It would change shapes. If you ever seen the movie Arrival... from 2016. There is a part in the movie where the aliens a using their own sign language...making different shapes...it was similar to that in different shapes.The color always staying the same ...forest fire color at night. I've never seen anything like it. There is interstate 20 nearby. Anybody traveling east or west on I-20 around 10pm on 8/21/20 would have seen it. This all lasted about 3 minutes before the changing shape became a ball again and then disappeared. I checked the news the next day and nothing was reported. These are not actual pics ...they are illustrations of what it would do when it would make different shapes. I did not go inside to get my cell phone because I did not want to miss anything.
