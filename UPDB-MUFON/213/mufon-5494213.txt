Back in 1972 we were coming from a High school outing with some friends. We were in the back of a truck, when one of my friend's saw an airplane above us. I looked and noticed it had no wing's and was very shiny! I told him "dude that's no plane it has no wing's" as I was telling my friend and pointing upwards it shot straight down in a V shape no sound what so ever! It's not shiny anymore since all we see is the underneath. Shot straight back up to the heavens! We just looked at each other speechless! Never mentioned it because I figured people would just laugh or jump to negative conclusions!Don't know or remember if the driver and other passengers in the cab of the truck saw it. Me and my two friend's in the back of the truck sure did!
