2 FLYING OBJECT SLIDING OVER THE SKY

AROUND 6.PM I WAS DROPPING MY SON OFF AT HIS FRIENDS. HE WAS JUST ABOUT TO GET OUT THE CAR WHEN I SAID "WHAT IS THAT". UP IN THE SKY THERE WAS A BLACK OBJECT THAT I THOUGHT MUST BE A HELICOPTER BUT THEN I REALISED IT WAS TOO SMALL. MY HEAD QUICKLY WENT THROUGH A BALOON? A BIRD? IT DID NOT MAKE SENSE AS IT WAS SLIDING OVR THE SKY. THEN JUST AFTER I SAW IT ANOTHER ONE CAME BEHIND IT. I OPENED MY CAR WINDOW MAYBE HEAR A SOUND BUT THERE WERE NONE. BOTH I SAID TO MY SO "GET YOUR PHONE" IT HAPPENED SO QUICKLY. HE GOT HIS PHONE, MEANWHILE I WAS WATCHING THESE 2 DARK OBJECT SLIDE OVER THE SKY. WE TRIED TO CAPTURE THEM BUT IN AN INSTANT THEY VANASHED. THIS IS THE TRUTH. WE DID SEE THEM. THEY WERE NOT OF THIS WORLD.
