{cak} 3 bright lights appeared and zoomed off FAST.

Was sitting outside stargazing on a beautiful clear skies night with a cool front in. Enjoying the weather. I go up to my second story perch and check out the skies. Stare at the stars and watch the planes come in and the usual helicopter. Saturn is at opposition still and Is super bright. I’ve been watching the stars since I was a kid and have seen meteors and comets and meteor showers. I know how long it takes planes to fly through my line of sight and the difference it takes them to do that at different altitudes. So I’m sitting up there and out of my peripheral vision I see a bright star come from east to west west. And vanish. And at first I was like maybe my eyes are tired from trying to nap the constellations. Then all of a sudden another. A super bright light that looked as bright as Saturn did popped out of nowhere and zipped fast I mean fast from east to west through the sky then vanished. Didn’t even go through my whole line of sight. Then a little to the right I saw another super bright starlike object zoom fast as hell from about ne to sw. and when I saw it pop up I stood out of my chair. And as soon as I had stood up it was gone. And nothing else for about 30 minutes of me being out there watching the sky. Just those three super fast lights. I could not explain them. Bright as the brightest star so it was far away. And went fast I mean fast two in one direction and another in a whole different direction. And wasn’t through my whole line of sight. Just appeared went fast and disappeared. Kinda freaked me out.
