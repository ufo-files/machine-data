{dr}Looked like an object traveling straight up with two thick trails underneath, only suspending and not moving.

Monday morning 10-27-14 approx between 7:00 and 8:00 a.m. headed east on I-75 about halfway between Naples and Ft Lauderdale. There were zero clouds in the sky. The sun was rising and still low on the horizon. I observed, well above and slightly to the right of the sun (and appeared to be about rocket height), some sort of an aircraft`traveling straight up with two thick airstreams beneath it; only it didn't look like it was moving. It just hung there and never changed direction, shape or location; like it was a permanent fixture. As I headed east, the roof of my auto eventually blocked my vision of it and I didn't pursue it any further. I just surmised that there was a Cape Canaveral rocket launch that I hadn't heard about, however, I was unable to find any information about a rocket launch on that morning.
