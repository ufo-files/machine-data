Medium sized Disc-Oval shaped Object with no Sound hoovering over the Taunus mountains in some distance.

Watched out of the window in the evening at 8:50 PM and saw some kind of a Disc-Oval shaped Object on the left side (North-West) of the building. I was excited and decided to record the event. When I came back with my mobile phone I spotted the object in some distance and took some pictures. On the pictures it looks like it could be a bird of some kind but it was a hoovering object - stationary - in about 1 km distance. I recorded the event and catched the object coming out of the thunderstorm clouds and fell about 300m in about half a second - than hoovering and remain stationary again. I recorded about 1:30 mins. The whole sighting was approximately 3 minutes.
