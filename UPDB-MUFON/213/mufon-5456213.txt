{jpg} Rough sphere

What I saw was a extremely large sphere very rough in appearance I could see the rough surface in both the reflection from the sun and shaded parts along with an antenna or something protruding out. I remember thinking that it look like if we had the technology it would be one of ours because it looked so primitive compared to other sightings people have of sleek shiney craft. It was a morning with fog and by the time i was driving to work the fog and haze had lifted well above the trees and was dissipating it looked as though t was trying to cloak within this haze which it did very well. It seemed as thou it accidentally found itself in a part of the fog that had opened up and revealed itself and immediately cloaked back in. It only took 4 or 5 sec but I was completely shocked at what I saw.
