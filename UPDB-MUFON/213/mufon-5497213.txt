{rjl} Lights moving toward southeast

Walked outside and saw approximately 15-20 orange lights moving in a southeasterly direction. They appeared to be spaced fairly evenly apart. I asked friend to take photo of them, she had to go inside to get camera so she only got 2 pics. They did not blink or flicker and made no sound. Everyone went back inside but me, I watched until they went out of view, while watching them another light appeared from the northwest. It seemed to be going faster than the others. It followed the same path as the other lights. Several people around this area saw them also. I felt I was looking at something very unusual and I watched u til they flew out of sight! "It" appeared to have a predetermined trajectory and no light chaned speeds.
