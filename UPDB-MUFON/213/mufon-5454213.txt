multiple objects captured in video

That afternoon, I was sitting on my apartment balcony when I noticed something flashing in the sky. Initially I thought it was a satellite, but it didn't move as such, it wasn't an airplane, nor a weather balloon. This object emitted a very intense and clean light. I took out my cell phone and started filming so I could analyze it later. There were approximately 5 minutes of film. When reviewing the videos, I started to identify several objects. All flying at an unimaginable speed (I struggled to lock the phone's time button), of different types, crossing the sky in a hundredth of a second, making 's' movements, parabolic, straight up and down, from the left to the right, down and up, appearing and disappearing. Every time I watch the videos, I discover more objects.
