{nd} cylinder shaped object with random flashing light

Im at an event called whaling days in Silverdale Washington and noticed a bright cylindrical object moving at an extremely high rate of speed with a flashing light spinning around it at random times. The light was white and occasionaly redish orange. It was at a further distance and traveling north and when I asked my wife to see it, I lost sight of it and it was directly above us. When I finally caught sight of it, my wife looked away and it vanished before my eyes. I didn't know what it was and I felt as if it was under intelligent control.
