{rjl} Massive Triangle non flashing lights that moved together

On 10/01/2018 I saw three non-flashing lights in the northern sky that formed a huge triangle. As they moved in unison, they gave the impression of a triangle craft tilting. Off to the left of the video, the flashing lights of an airplane can also be seen and are much smaller.
