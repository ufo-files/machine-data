{dr}UFO's over Los Lunas, NM on April 20, 2014 around 10pm

I was watching television around 10pm when a bright red light outside my window caught my eye. I anxiously rushed outside as I thought it was a UFO. I grabbed my phone and filmed what I could as the 3 lights slowly hovered, than ascended in the NE direction. The 3 lights were orb like and ranged from a variety of colors from red's to yellows, seemingly glowing as they traveled. Eventually the lights began hovering so far and high that they disappeared into the night sky. The closest thing they resembled were sky lanterns (also known as Chinese lanterns), though i've never seen these lanterns in person- only in film.
