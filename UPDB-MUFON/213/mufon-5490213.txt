{cak} After watching fireworks I noticed rectangular objects just hovering in the sky north of where I live

I was watching fireworks in my backyard, when I noticed 2 rectangular objects north of my location, I'm retired from the aviation side of the Navy, so I know what a aircraft and a helicopter look like, and these 2 objects were not either of these. They were just hovering above the mountains, they were not moving and had lights on the bottom of the objects. The first object after about 5 minutes started slowly descended down and out of site, the second object just stayed airborne for another 15 minute, before moving downward and out of site. I was excited to see these objects and I was not scared.
