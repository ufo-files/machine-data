{cak} Red, blue, green light changing directions rapidly

I initially thought it was an airplane, then I noticed the change in directions too fast to be far away and without any sound to be close enough in case of a drone or an unmanned small aircraft. I started recording after noticed the a blue light (among red and green) which is not common for navigation lights. Also called my attention that the intermitence of the strobe was in irregular periods of time. I was not able to capture faster movements that happened before I started recording.
