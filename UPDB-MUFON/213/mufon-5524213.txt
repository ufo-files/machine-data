If I would have blinked it would of disappeared, that is how fast the orb shot up into outerspace.

I went to smoke a ciggrette on my best friends back porch, which I had done thousands of times before. This night I was by myself and I noticed a light that I had never seen before. I thought it could be a new light pole. As I stared at it for about 5 minutes it looked to be pulsating. Then it shot straight up into space in the blink of an eye. It was truely amazing. I know that it was something out of this world, I believe it was an angel. I feel very blessed and lucky to have seen this. Every night I go to my top deck which is pretty close to the location of where I saw the orb, I have never seen anything like it since. But I am always looking so hopefully I will see something again one day. :)
