Two UFO’s

It was a clear night; no clouds in the sky. 
I was out taking pictures of the Christmas star and when I moved my cell phone camera directly over my neighbors house I could see two objects with blue lights underneath them moving slowly to the west.  I switched my camera to video but could not see them or perhaps did not have my phone aimed in the correct location. I then put my phone from video to photo and snapped a picture.  I was pretty stunned by what I saw.  They just seemed to disappear after that. These were directly south and the Christmas Star was much further Southwest.  I also got a pic of the moon, North Star and what looks like a blue orb.
