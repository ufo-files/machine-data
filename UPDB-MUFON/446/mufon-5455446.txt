While in the marine corps was up on a hill calling in fire missions for artillery. When I noticed two objects in the sky that appeared to be stars , when I seen two jets appear out of the NE headed in the direction of the two objects when I noticed the two objects move at undetermined speed toward my location where they hovered for a a few moments as the jets drew closer I had to call a check fire they were that close as the jets looked like they would engage the two objects suddenly moved straight up at undetermined speed into the atmosphere and disappeared.
