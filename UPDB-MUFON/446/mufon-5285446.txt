(TIC TAC Proj) Two tick tac shaped object flying in the sky

I was traveling to my daughters approximately a year ago to visit her in San Angelo Texas I was on highway 337 in between El Dorado and San Angelo. I looked up and noticed two TicTac shaped object flying from the left to the right so I pulled over and started filming them. They made absolutely no sound and just continued about their way. I have no idea what these were and this is not the first time I have seen these I actually have another video right over my daughters house of one single object that looks exactly like this.
