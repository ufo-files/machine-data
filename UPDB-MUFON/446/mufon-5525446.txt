six or seven bright orange lights on the sky moving same rotation , round

I was driving on marigold avenue going on my way to work when I notice in the sky six or seven orange bright lights moving on the same rotation i stop on the side and started to look. I thought it was star but then i told myself it can't be stars when they all are in the same section of the sky and moving at the same time. I'm not what exactly it is. I tried to take a picture but it doesn't show anything. So I decided to go back home and tell him to get out it started to fade away with the clouds. My husband got out when he call and stated he didn't see anything. Also it was raining at the time but had stop when I saw the sightings. There above the houses and trees.
