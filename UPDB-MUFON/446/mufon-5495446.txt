{cak} Large Pulsing light over horizon

Observed UFO heading West from Richmond County, GA. Appeared to be over Grovetown, which is located in Columbia County. UFO was quite bright, with large pulsing white light, with what appeared to be smaller different colored lights surrounding the larger light. UFO was observed for approximately five minutes, ultimately fading behind the tree line as it appeared to continue Westward. I attempted to capture a picture, but my cellphone camera was not able to detect the lights due to the distance.
