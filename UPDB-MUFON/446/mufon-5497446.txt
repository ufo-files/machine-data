{nd} Saw a number of lights in a vee or triangular pattern flying over the Country Club Plaza in Kansas City, MO.

On January 17, 2009, my wife and I were going home from a party at some friends house. I saw some strange looking lights traveling from the NW to the SE over the Country Club Plaza in Kansas City,MO. We pulled off the road as I wanted to get a better look. I observed 5 lights in a vee or triangular pattern. It seemed to be very big in size, moving very slow and not changing its direction. What was strange to me was, as an airline employee, I am well aware that all aircraft have to have certain directional lights to easily identify which direction it is traveling relative to your position of observation. There was none observed with any of these lights. I am curious if I am the only one to report this sighting. I know what I saw, and I hadn't drank a drop of alcohol for over 4 years.
