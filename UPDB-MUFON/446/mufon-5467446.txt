UFO FILES INFORMATION GATHERED FROM Art Stallings McKinney TX

INCIDENT #1
1963 approx
Art1s brother, Doug Stallings now deceased. the brother1s boss and a co-worker were headed to nearby wooded area at around 5:30 a.m. pre-dawn to hunt for deer. It was as they approached the wooded area they spotted lights that they thought was a vehicle travelling through the woods. However it became obvious the object was above the trees and moving fairly quickly and silently. The fuselage or tubes connecting the globes appeared to have portholes ot windows. The globes seemed to glow orange to white and the connecting tubes were luminescent.

What size or length would you say your brother estimated this was and what year was it?
Size would depend on distance, but he described it as large, and at low altitude. 150-200 feet in length maybe. My guess is 1963. It was during deer season, so that would be November-December I think. My brother and his boss got out of the car to look at this thing. The co-worker refused to get out of the car and was terrified.

My brother said the V shaped one was reported around the Houston area, so that may help to pin down the date. He said it was traveling due south
