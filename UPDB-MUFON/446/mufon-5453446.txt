{tjd} Watched a light make no sound, travel at a fast rate then disappear completely.

I have watched the night sky from my house almost every evening for the past 3 years. Seen satellites, shooting stars, planes, helicopters, drones in the evening sky with my naked eye, binoculars & telescopes. Tonight as I took out my trash around 10 pm, I saw a white light travel above the Olympic Mountains, Southwest heading North at a high speed and then disappear. I ran into my house to grab my binoculars (less than 30 seconds) to see if I could see it again following the direction of the flight and saw nothing. It was silent outside the entire time. You can hear planes & Helicopters from vast distances usually and the speed this was traveling made no sound and was far too low to be a satellites and it's trajectory was even with no accent or decent.
