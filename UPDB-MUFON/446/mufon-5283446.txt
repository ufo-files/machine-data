Witnessed what appeared to be a white color object with multiple pointed protrusions floating across the sky before disappearing

On 12/25/2021 we received a phone call from my father in law asking if we could see the white object in the sky. My wife and I and her grandfather went outside and after scanning the sky witnessed an object, white in color floating across the sky. I attempted to take a video of the object but unfortunately it was in the distance and does not do it justice. Looking at the object through binoculars you could clearly make out multiple triangle shaped protrusions on the object that appears round to the naked eye. It also appeared more silver in color through the binoculars. At least two airplanes flew over while the object was in the sky. There were zero clouds in the clear blue sky as the object cruised along occasionally moving between a north and northeast direction before suddenly disappearing.
