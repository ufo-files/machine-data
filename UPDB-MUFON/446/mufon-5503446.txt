A bright white glow shot in to the ground and left a ringing noise after it.

I was on my way to the 24 hour shop outside the forest when I noticed a very bright and noticeable white glow in the trees, I asked is anybody there as it's a forest and its unusual for somebody to be in there at 1am. Nobody answered so as I asked a second time "Is anyone there, are you ok?" this glow shot down into the ground at a fast speed and a few seconds after that there was a very painful ringing noise in my ear that only lasted a few seconds. 

I stood there in shock to think what just happened, I felt a mixture of scared but keen to know what just happened. 

When I finally got to the shop I forgot most of my shopping list and picked up random supply's I wouldn't of dreamt about buying.
