Star like bright light was seen flying along the clouds during Sunset. No blinking lights.

I was getting out of my car on the south side of Omaha and I saw a star like light flying around the clouds. It was very far away and it was between me and the clouds. It did not blink at all or change color. It was a solid yellowish white light. I had my camcorder with me and was trying to film it but by the time I got it out it flew into the clouds and I didn't see it. It would have taken me some time anyways to manually adjust my camcorder zoom just to get it focused at that distance. I did not see it again after it flew into the clouds.
