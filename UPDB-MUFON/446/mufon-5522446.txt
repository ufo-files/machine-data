Silent orange/red lighted object

My wife was letting our dog out and noticed a strang anomaly in the sky. She called for me to come quickly to see. I got outside in plenty of time to see the oddly orange/red lit completely noiseless object in the sky. I used a pair of low powered binoculars to see what appeared to have been something in a reverse teardrop shape lit with a noticebly orange light. Object was low enough to be a helicopter but moving too fast to be one. My wife and I had no idea what it was. She was glad I actually saw it with her. She couldnt believe what she was seeing.
