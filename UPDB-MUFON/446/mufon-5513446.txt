{nd} co-worker driving into work spots large object above vehicle very bright lights

co-worker comes into work at 11:30 at a new nuclear plant. He said he
had to talk to me. He was visiablly shaken. This guy hardly ever jokes
about anything. He told me when driving in to work, about three miles
from plant gate he looks up to bright lights. It was like the object
was following his vehicle. The object was shaped like a horse track with
all these brights non flashing. Couldnt see the top or sides since it
seemed to stay almost overhead. He was amazed at the overall size and
so many lights. The whole time he was talking he was shaking. His eyes
were wide open and talked faster than normal. He couldnt explain how
fast it left. Said it started to move ahead of his vehicle at a fast speed
and like that lights out. He told me not to tell anyone. He thought he
would be laughed at. This guy is a straight shooter and on the serious
side of life. I know he told me what he saw and was shaken up from the
event.
