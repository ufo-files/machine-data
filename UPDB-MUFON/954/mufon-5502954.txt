{nd} Driving on the highway and oberved a bright object moving that resembled the planet Mars.

On May 3rd, 2014, I was driving from Pleasant view TN, to Clarksville, as I entered Clarksville and got on 101st Airborne Parkway going towards Ft. Campbell, I was driving towards the intersection that crossed between 101st Airborne Parkway and Trenton Rd. I noticed seeing a reddish-orange star to my left hand side and noticed that this star looked like the planet Mars. I thought to myself that it looked much brighter in the sky that night, the second and third glance I made at this object it had gotten much bigger and brighter, as I came to a stop at the four way intersection. I continued observing it, which I had already assumed this object was not a star but something that was actually moving and coming towards my direction. It was moving from E to SW moving in a straight line path and slow enough to observe it while I made a stop at the red-light at the four way intersection. The object had a cigar-blimp like shape to it, the entire object has a reddish-orange color coming from it. It also had a shimmering Bright yellow with orange glow pulsating from it as it got closer in my direction. it looked large maybe around 100 ft. and was in the air probably cloud level. It continued to move and as it floated over the backside of the truck, no sound was made and eventually I lost sight of it. I believe I saw something that was very strange here in Clarksville. The main thing about this object was that the entire object glowed and no blinking lights were visible to indicate that it was a plane or helicopter. It did not make sound whatsoever as it floated over the vehicle I was in.
