{nd} On hwy heading south from Albany Airport to Kingston, NY. Sudden dense fog and craft appeared.

Was returning from Albany Airport heading south on hwy to Kingston, NY. on a clear night. Halfway in the Catskills and all of a sudden a dense fog appeared. I slowed from 60mph to about 5mph, couldn't see more than 20 feet ahead. Drove about ¹/⁴ mile. Suddenly a large disc shaped object with red blinking lights on the underside and what appeared like windows lit by a bright white light. The size was like patio door windows around the circumference. As it slowly passed overhead I concentrated on looking inside the windows, but they were too translucent or opaque. It continued over both lanes of hwy. I continued at 5mph in the fog and came upon a car stopped in the right lane with all 4 doors open and 4 people outside, I assumed it was because of the craft. I did not stop, but thought to myself how unsafe they were, given the thick fog, to stop like they did. I then drove about 500ft and saw the craft now on my right side slowly ascending to go over a small mtn. I thought to exit and try to follow it, but the next exit I came to was 25mi away. Told about 5 people a few years later. I don't think it is something they believe was real.
