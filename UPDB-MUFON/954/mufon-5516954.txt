{nd} saw two hovering lights in the sky at night.

I was just coming home from my job which I work in the city of Midland. I have to drive east on US-10 Business towards Auburn where I live. It was roughly 5 minutes after 10 pm and I looked in the upper left corner of my windshield and noticed a light above the tree line on my left. As I got closer I figured it was a radio tower or something, but then I quickly realized that where light appeared there was no tower because it was just off to my left of the freeway in a residential area. Driving ever closer I noticed two orange/white lights hovering one above another roughly 35 degrees off the of the horizon and Id say no higher than 1,000 ft. They were really close. They seemed to be pulsating and glowing brighter and dimmer, and it was almost if they were communicating with each other; I would guess that the two objects were no more than 100 ft away from each other. I couldnt believe what I was seeing...or I didn't know WHAT I was seeing. I thought at first that it may have been those candle balloon things but they were NOT MOVING at all, just hovering one above the other. Anyway I just watched it as I drove by and this only lasted for about 3 or so minutes until I could not look behind me WHILE I WAS DRIVING. I had thought to stop the truck along the side of the freeway but I was very tired from a long day at work. Im posting this sighting in the hopes that someone else may have seen this strange event in the area and maybe even have a logical explanation for it, or perhaps NOT.
