(GS) red strobing light

I was driving down the street with my wife and I began to look up at the renaissance building in downtown detroit. I had a clear view at the top of the building and I just saw a bright red lot strobing. We stopped and watched the object pulse for about 30 just sit above the building seperate from the buildings light. It then began to accend towards canada over the detroit river. It then disappeared. It was a bright red diamond kind of circular.
