balloon like, at first like a snake, then a question mark

Above the Orange Co. Fire Authority a thin "ballon like" snaked shape figure. It remained still even when the wind was blowing. If moved into the question mark figure before rising into the clouds/fog. We were looking North from out home bordered by Portolla Parkway, Jamboree and the 261 toll road.
