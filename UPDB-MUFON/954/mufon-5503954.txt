{kt}Noticed object out of the ordinary in my backyard. Watched it for a bit and then got my phone to try and record it.

I was in the back yard with my dog around 9pm. A pretty blue light in the sky caught my attention. At first, I thought it was a blimp - we sometimes have them flying in our area, but I couldn't hear any noise. As I watched this object I noticed it started moving around in many directions and seemed to be changing colors. I figured it would fly off, but it stayed pretty much in this one area so I took a chance and ran into the house to get my phone to try and record it. It was a strange sight and I wanted to try and figure out what it was. In the video you can hear the crickets in my backyard so at the time of the sighting I did not hear a plane or engine. I recorded it for 1 minute and 42 seconds. During that time it darted around up and down left and right and changed shapes and colors. At 1:12 seconds (I looked at the time on the recording) another object came down from the upper right sky and the two objects moved around wildly together for a bit (up and down and all around) before I lost sight of them. I've never witnessed anything like this before. I'm glad my video recorded what I saw because no one would believe me if I shared this story. It was not a blimp. At the end of the recording you will hear a plane. This plane route was behind me. It did not come from the objects I was looking at. If you watch this video in slow motion you can make out a circular object with lights surrounding it - two objects once the other one falls into the frame. I took stills from the video if you would like those too.
