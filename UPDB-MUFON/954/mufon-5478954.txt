{dr]Floating egg-shaped light.

Oustide on a clear night tonight, and noticed a large white egg-shaped object in the sky. Appeared to be moving or hovering, but not an airplane. We saw planes fly by, but this is big and brighter. A lot brighter than all other stars. We went to our upstairs bedroom and I attempted to record it on my phone. We looked at it with our binoculars and it appeared to have a holographic appearance. Very similar to what a virus drawing looks like in a biology book. Sort of appendage like but still round. It was very bright and after us observing it for about 20 minutes we noticed that is seemed to be slowly moving south. Still hovering and flickering, but getting smaller. Not sure how long it had been present when we first noticed it, but it was strange. Here is a link to my FB page where I saved the video clip.
