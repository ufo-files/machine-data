Elongated football shape. 100ft+/- Bottom half silver color. Upper half blue. No sound, steering mechanism, port holes, windows, propellers and flew slowly (30mph) & steadily from the South in N/NW direcrtion.

I had been taking a customary walk around my neighborhood and stopped at my driveway to try to spot a hawk that was making hawk sounds from a group of trees behind my neighbors house. His trees were around 80 to 100 ft high. Slowly this UFO appeared to rise up from behind the trees. My first thought was excitement that I was going to see my first UFO. Then I realized that it only appeared to rise up. It was moving in a straight line. I then thought it was a dirigible and I started looking for a gondola, windows, lettering or towing a sign. There were none and I became less excited. There were no engines, propellers, jets nor steering mechanisms. The bottom half was silver colored and the top blue of what I could see. It didn't appear to be over 200 ft high and about 100+/- ft long and 20/30 feet in diameter. It was moving in a straight line from the south in a North/North West direction at 30mph+/-. I disappeared out of site and I thought no more about it for a day or 2.
