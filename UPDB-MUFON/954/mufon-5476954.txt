Black silent triangle flew over pwer heads at night low and i could see shape of lights underneath

It was night in Klerksdorp northwest southafrica. We were busy setting up for stargazing when my eye caught the triangle silently flying over us. It took only seconds and we were too amazed that none of us could take a picture or really had time to do anything. You need to understand that for us to be able to hide from any lights to be able to stargaze we need to be between the house and property wall that sort of forms u shape barrier between us and all the lights of the city. We do however have a 100degree view of the sky, more or less. But the reason i bother to say anything about our view ( me my father in law and my mother in law) is that because the ufo was so close too the ground and was not traveling fast at all, i had a brief chance to see the lights at the bottom of the craft. They were not bright at all and were rectangle and looked very dim and and almost diluted orange in colour. Anyway what was also strange is that we saw it a second time but this time it was quicker and only 2 of the ligjts were visible. The craft was a very dark black colour and what was crazy and unnerving is that it made apsolutely no sound at all and it was just over our heads. Hope you get my message. Kond regards.
