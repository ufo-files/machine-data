White orb teardrop shaped with a tail, rainbow reflection off of it

I was in Omaha Nebraska. On the way home from a friends house, He was driving. I noticed the object in the sky because I seen a noticeable vertical rainbow reflecting off of it. The object was slowly moving towards the sun that was setting(just outside of the picture). I had my friend pull over and we both got out of the car and he took 2 pictures one normal picture and a zoomed up picture of it. I was astonished. It was so beautiful I could not take my eyes off of it. I had a calm feeling, I was not scared or frightened. It was purely gorgeous. We watched the object for about 15 minutes as it was still very slowly heading towards the sun. I watched it as we drove away.
