Flew over the house 5-7 times, large triangle 5 or 4 lights

Im in bed, and hear a loud roar. I look out a window and see a large and or very low aircraft with 4 or 5 lights. They were a single aircraft from what I saw. It was a triangle (3 lights) with a (possibly 2) strobing slowly in the middle. It was heading west directly over I465 at 10th street in warren township. This event actually repeated itself 4 or 5 more times starting around 11 pm on the 27th until about 2 am on the 28th, heading west every time.
