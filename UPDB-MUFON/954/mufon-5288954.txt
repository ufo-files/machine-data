Captured object by chance

I was driving north on I-5 in Ca on the evening of May 23, 2015 to my home in Washington. I stopped in Weed, CA for gas and to grab a burger at McDonalds. When I got of the car, I observed a beautiful light on Mt. Shasta and decided to take a photo. It was 8:08;19 when I snapped a photo. I didn't take the time to change to a telephoto lens because I didn't want to lose the light on the mountain. I had a 35mm lens on my Canon 6D. The ISO was 200,. f stop at f5.6, exposure was 10 seconds with the camera resting on the roof of my car. According to Google, Mt. Shasta is approximately 6 miles away from my shooting position. 
I did not take notice of any object over the mountain; my main thought was to capture the great light bathing Mt. Shasta.
I got around to try and edit my photo on April 6, 2020. Since I retired in 2014, I am a full time photographer with an emphasis on landscapes. When I zoomed in to edit, that's when I noticed the object above Mt. Shasta. Big surprise to be sure. I've asked several of my friends to come up with an idea what the object might be; most of them told me they did not know but that I should send the photo into one of the UFO organizations.
