{cak} UFO seen on Route 39 hovering then following erratically

Leaving my home around 5am, I noticed a very big and bright light in the sky that I assumed was a star or planet. As I moved closer to it heading N on Route 39 it got closer and I saw that there were blinking multicolored lights in a V formation. It hovered for 5-8 minutes in the sky as I approached it, but once I got close enough it started accelerating and followed me as I took one exit off the highway and onto another. It followed me for a good 5 min moving in a way I have never seen any other aircraft moved. I observed that it hovered as I slowed down to watch it. As I exited the highway to move into the city I watched as it continued hovering and moved on.
