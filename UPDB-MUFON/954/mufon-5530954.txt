UFO appeared in NNW sky while I was traveling east on Greenfield Ave. Looked 3 times it was there, gone on third.

I was traveling east bound on Greenfiled Ave around 100th street at 7:34 am on 7/14/2010, when I saw what looked like a balloon floating over the Wisconsin State fair grounds. I though at first it might be a car sale that had a huge balloon attached to it, but I did not see any string. The object looked like a clear blimp, and I could clearly see the sun glinting off of it. It was a clear day, not a cloud on the sky. I saw the object once, and then looked back at it again. On the third time, it appeared to shink in size, then the forth time, it disappeared.
