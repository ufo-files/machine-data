(GS) Bright orange lights formed a triangle v formation with other orange lights trailing behind

I was walking my dogs when I turned to head toward home when
the lights in the sky caught my eye. My first thought was fireworks but I did
not hear any booming sound prior. They formed a V shaped formation almost a triangle 
with other orange lights trailing behind. They separated and broke apart and almost
seemed to disappear but it is a cloudy overcast night and there is not a star to be seen in
the sky. They disappeared into the cloud coverage. They made no sound. There were a lot
of them. I unfortunately did not have my celll phone
on me to take any pictures.
