3 glowing Orbs in diamond formation over the ocean, disappeared after several different formations

Driving north on Scenic drive in Trinidad 6:15 pm saw 3 bright lights in a straight line cruising along above the coast line. I noticed that there didn't seem to be any flashing lights on the objects, just pulsating yellow light. When we pulled over to continue watchin the light went into a diamond formation and stayed like that for another 45 seconds and then each one simply disappeared. Wondering if anyone else saw this. It was pretty amazing.
