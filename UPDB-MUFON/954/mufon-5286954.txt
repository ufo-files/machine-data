Took pictures of solar eclipse and caught something odd

On the 10th June, the day of partial eclipse, I went out into my garden, in Somersham, UK, to try and capture it on my phone camera. At the time, because of the sun, I couldn't even see the screen of my phone. I went outside 3 times over a period of about 15 minutes (I believe the peak of the eclipse was about 11.14am UK time). On reviewing the pictures, I noticed, in 3 of them taken the second time I went outside, something odd,beneath the sun. My initial reaction was that it must be some kind of reflection. However, on studying the other pictures I found the same thing, in thick cloud, and seemingly not in the same position. I have attached 13 photos. If you blow them up and look beneath the sun, both to the left and right of the sun, you'll see something odd, and some with 'lights'. These pictures are genuine, I assure you, and I wondered what you make of them.
