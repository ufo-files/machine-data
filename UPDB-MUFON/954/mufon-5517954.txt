{dr}5 UFO Siting Springfield Louisiana

Went out for routine sky watching with my girlfriend, and we noticed two UFOS at once. One was NNE and the other was ENE, close in proximity. They glowed red/green/white/blue and hovered for some time before moving around a bit. They both moved in various paths, pulsated the different lights, and remained star-like after about 45mins activity. We next saw a craft very high in the sky with multiple lights, looked almost diamond/kite like, and it was very faint. We only noticed because some of the lights would flash randomly. One craft came from off the ground east of where we were at, glowing orange/red and took position underneath a pre-existing ENE UFO (which was in "stationary" mode - it looked like a star) it moved around and then stayed stationary like a star. Next we saw an orange light close to the ground north of our position, we ran to the treeline and used a flashlight to survey the location, and the orange light disappeared.
