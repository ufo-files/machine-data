On July 4, 1947, I looked up into twilight sky and saw a bright, oval object coming straight down from atmosphere that made a sharp right-angled turn to horizontal and flew south at high speed.

On July 4, 1947, I happened to be walking west through Woodland Park in Seattle, Washington. I think July 4, 1947, was the correct date because other witnesses (such as a Seattle city bus driver and U.S. Navy man *(Name deleted--CMS/sg) who took a photo of a white disc over Seattle on July 4, 1947)also saw UFOs over Seattle around the same time on July 4, 1947.  At the time, I was a 22-year-old student at the nearby University of Washington in Seattle. The time was around 5:30 PM in early twilight. Woodland Park overlooked Puget Sound and the Olympic Mountain Range to the west. I happened to look up and saw a bright, oval or sphere-shaped object descending straight down from the upper atmosphere. As I looked on in sudden surprise, the object made a very sharp and almost 90-degree turn to the horizontal and then sped south along Puget Sound at high speed. The object was silent and white in color. The total time of the sighting was probably ten or 15 seconds. At first, I thought the white object was an airplane but an airplane would not have come right down from the upper atmosphere and then make a very sharp right-angled turn to the horizontal with no noise of motors  The object seemed to be heading toward Mount Rainier (about 50 miles south of Seattle) and Mount Adams and Mount St. Helens. After ten seconds or so, the object disappeared from view to the south as it sped south along Puget Sound.
