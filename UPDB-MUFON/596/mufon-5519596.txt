2 bright glowing orange orbs

Sitting in our backyard I noticed one UFO flying at a distance through the trees in my neighbors yard. Thought it was too bright and orange to be a plane or helicopter. Walked around to to get a better view and then noticed 2nd ufo, exact same color and size, flying slightly behind the first at about 8 oclock. I freaked out and asked my wife, what the hell is that? They flew a little closer in a northwestern direction, slowed and then stopped completely. Very bright and very orange. At this point I knew we were seeing something strange. Both objects hovered over area of Chico, CA for about about 2 minutes. 2nd UFO faded and then disappeared. 1st UFO lasted about 10 seconds longer and then quickly disappeared. We were very shocked and amazed.
