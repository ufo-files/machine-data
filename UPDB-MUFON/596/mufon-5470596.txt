A red glowing disc or ball like thing moved through the sky silently, was visible approx 30 seconds and disappeared

I came down under my apartment to play with my 8 year old kid at 8 PM Indian time on 6/01/2018. My building care-taker also was presented in the location. We have seen a glowing red ball/disc like thing moving in the sky NW to SW without any noise. I am pretty sure it was not an airplane or chopper. It was moving faster than an airplane seems to be and it was also not far enough to the airplane altitude in our sky.My Building care taker asked me "Sir what is that..?" I was also have no Idea about the flying thing. He asked me whether i have a camera to shoot, Luckily my i-phone was in my pocket. I took a video(16 seconds) and took 2 photos of the same. You can clearly hear voices of my son and care-taker in the video when they witnessing it live. My son tells in my local language Malayalam "adu neengundallo" means " It's moving" . He also tells it may be a plane - but care taker from behind corrects him "No No it's not an airplane" . Can here my voice also telling that "soundoollya" means "There is no sound" . It remained and traveled up to 30 seconds max and disappeared in to the sky. After disappearing, after 10-15 second i have seen 2 or three blinks of light (not sure about the colors but i felt it like a police cars light - blue&Red) in the sky much higher than the spot where the earlier object disappeared. I never believed in the alien and flying saucers things... But now i strongly believes that i ve seen something in reality that i used to see in the NG or History TV only......
