{jpg} My family and I witnessed strange lights over Englewood Colorado

On September 24, 2015 my family and I had just moved to Colorado. We were staying in a hotel (Residence Inn) off of 
I-25 in Englewood Colorado. I was observing these amazingly odd, I was fixated, strange lights in the sky then my family began watching as well. The lights seemed to be hovering in the sky and cloning, we believed! I found my phone to record them and this is what I had captured. Only now did I finally find this video.
