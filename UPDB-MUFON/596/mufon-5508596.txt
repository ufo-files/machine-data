I was driving home from the convenient store I went to get my girlfriend a Gatorade and As I was driving south I notice four rectangular lights moving Over this factory called ridg u rak in north east pa. I pulled over and called my girlfriend to tell her to run out to the driveway and look east...I got out of my jeep and I was watching the formation one of the lights blinked out then came back on and then three of the lights blinked out and the one light started moving. I continued to go home after the sighting. when I got home we stood in the driveway and watched the one light. We got into my jeep and drove up to a secluded bridge where you can see for miles and also see over Lake Erie and there was nothing left to see.... I do not know what I saw that night but I do want to know if anyone has had any similar experience.
