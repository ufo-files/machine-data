{td} Saw three red and white lights in a horizontal lights traveling across the sky

After previous UFO sighting was driving home alone and talking to another friend on the phone about the first one. Was already watching the sky because I was thinking about the previous sighting. At first I assumed it was a plane, but it was going so fast that I got suspicious and started speeding my car to keep up with it. I was in disbelief that I was seeing another UFO. I lost sight of it after it disappeared behind the trees.
