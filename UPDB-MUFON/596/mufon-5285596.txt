Photo of Black Oblong Cylinder Type Object Gliding Across Horizon

Saturday Sept 4, 2021 6:06 pm
Daylight 6 pm. Standing outside in back driveway, talking on cell to friend. I noticed something in the sky a short distance away, to my left. I look over and see this very large completely black metallic, cylinder ish, oblong ish, rectangle ish shaped object with slightly pointed ends gliding in the sky at a somewhat fast speed. I did not notice any lights on it. It was holding itself at a steady upright position while gliding the horizon at a perfect & steady left to right position. No sound. 
It glided towards Seattle Boeing Field & also across the Seattle air flight area.
It was too heavy of an object to be caught in a possible airstream and traveled too quickly for an airstream. Also no wind that day. 
Within my sight for approx 3 minutes, until it was a black dot in the sky far beyond. 
I tried to take a video but learned that you cannot take video while talking on cell, but to my surprise later on that evening I noticed I accidentally took a photo of it! 
Approx size: in comparison to a large deuly truck. 
It WAS NOT a kite, a tarp, a box, balloon, paper. I cannot fathom what it could have been? Sadly, the photos are so much smaller than what I saw with my eyes.
