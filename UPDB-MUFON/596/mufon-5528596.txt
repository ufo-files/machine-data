Sighted V shape during Geminid observation

I had just taken my dog out for a walk, and decided to stay out a few more minutes to look at the meteor shower. I had counted 9 meteors as I stood there smoking a cigarette and looking skyward. Just as I finished my cigarette I took one last look skyward, and was about to head inside. Suddenly I noticed a silent shape moving directly through my field of vision traveling from NNW to SSE. The shape was in a V of solid mass at, what I assume was, a high altitude. At first I tried to dismiss the sighting as ducks or Geese but then realized that it was moving too fast. The object was unlighted and entered and left my field of vision within seconds. Moving at a speed that was faster than a commercial airliner could leave my field of vision seemed unlikely for birds. As soon as the object was out of sight, I came inside, told my wife what I had just seen, then grabbed my iPhone and began this report. I know geese generally fly and make noise as well so the fact there was nothing but silence and the speed combined slowly made me realize this had to be something else. What it was, I don't know!
