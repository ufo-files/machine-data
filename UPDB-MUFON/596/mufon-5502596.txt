{rjl} Watching two hawks above when i noticed something above hawks but below clouds.

Was watching 2 hawks fly above house when I noticed what appeared to be a bird way above the hawks but under the clouds. there was no wing movement which I thought was strange then I noticed another one come from the northwest and turn just in front of the other and both continued south in formation. they seemed to pick up speed once the were in formation. I noticed how fast they were moving and thought it was strange. s they passed overhead I could see the entire shape. When I first saw the object it almost looked like a W flying in the air that's why I immediately thought it was a bird. Once I could see the rest of the objects they appeared to be discs. I noticed no sound as passed overhead and flew in a perfectly straight line other than the 2nd one joining the first. The sky was cloudy with large clouds. The objects appeared to be just below the clouds and never disappeared into them. During the time i saw them it was sunny. I watched them until they disappeared out of sight. The entire time was 20 to 25 seconds. I have attached a rough sketch. the top one is how the object first appeared then bottom is how it looked after it went overhead
