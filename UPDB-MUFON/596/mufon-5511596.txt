(GS) 9 to 11 foot donut shaped circles

went to get my sheep on the 12th and everything was normal. On the 13th I went back to the same area to get the sheep and there were about 10 of these donut shaped circles between 9 and 11ft in diameter the outer circle all vegetation was gone for about 18" then in the inside the vegetation is normal. 4 of the donuts are in a straight line. I have lived here for 19 years and have never seen anything like this, and had 4 friends look at this and they have never seen anything like this.
