{nd} Orange fireball flew level across the sky from west to east.

Ramona California, Sept. 6th 2015. At approx 4:00AM I was heading to my car. I noticed movement in the sky, looked up and saw an orange fireball moving level, across the sky from west to east. It was sub-sonic, silent, a few hundred feet above the ground. The surface appeared to be on fire but it had no tail or left a trail. It continued level at a steady speed out of my view. I probably saw it for 5 to 7 seconds. I wasn't sure what I saw but I knew it wasn't a meteor.
