Spotted very bright light with ring around, that changed course and turned abruptly to avoid plane. Then pulsed and disappeared .

Went into my back garden with my partner and her little boy, and she noticed this really bright light coming from the south. As we watched it, it changed direction with a pulse of light and started travelling in a different direction. It travelled south-west for 4 minutes or so then, when a plane entered that air space it turned abruptly and carried on the same direction, the plane stayed on course, after travelling for a few more minutes it pulsed/flashed and disappeared. There was no sound and nothing like I have ever seen before, very bright and fast.
