cylinder shaped craft flew straight path without sound

I was walking a dog in a field outside of Greenville, NC, off of Doc Loftin Road. Clear blue sky overhead, you could see many planes on the horizon with contrails. Suddenly overhead I noticed a cigar/cylinder shaped plane without wings flying by. It looked to be divided in the middle or maybe segmented into two sections. It flew in a completely straight line just above the treeline, no arc or curve of ascent at all. I watched it fly directly over me and into the horizon. When it got just near out of my sight the sun hit it and the light reflecting off of it made it appear like a star. There were no clouds overhead and I had been watching buzzards flying circles just to my right when the motion of it caught my eye. Totally silent, no contrail or exhaust, nothing. Very fluid, straight line motion. I watched it for 10 minutes until it went too far away. No ascent or descent - it was about the distance that the crop-dusters fly overhead.
