round glowing object. sitting at tip of big dipper. started moving slowly across the sky. then shot up and disappear ed to a pin size then gone.

My husband and I were taking our dogs out to use the washroom. It was so clear we were standing and looking at the stars, my husband pointed out the big dipper. He then said what's that moving, it looked like something moving away from the dipper across the sky. It was bigger than the stars in the big dipper. it was a yellow orange color that glowed and it was round. It made no sound and it moved slowly across the sky. It moved to about the edge of the milky way and suddenly started appearing smaller and smaller suggesting to us that it had stopped and started traveling upwards and away from us. It was quickly gone to a pin size then completely gone from site. There was a plane traveling much higher than it and we could easily observe the differences between the two. I have 21 years Canadian Military Airforce/Navy and my husband was an emergency management coordinator. We both were left not knowing what we saw.
