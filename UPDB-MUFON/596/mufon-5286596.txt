UFO + Space Portal caught on Video Camera, July 2021 over the Colombian Andes. Filmed during a commercial flight at 7am in the morning.

The sight occurred during a commercial flight over the Colombian Andes last week. The co-pilot and myself were intrigued by the very unusual shape of the cloud we saw in front of us. A horizontal sand clock shape with an intense bright light in the middle above the cloud cover. It resembles like the space portals we all have watched in the movies. We took our phones and started recording and taking pictures. After landing and while revising the footage I spotted the UFO coming out of the portal and flying in front of the cockpit and then continuing south until disappearing and then leaving a halo of light in the horizon. The event all together lasted no more than 3 minutes. This was over a sunny and clear morning during the first schedule flight we had last Friday.
