{jpg} Stationary Star over Kendall

My husband and I were driving down 96th Street towards 127the Avenue in order to get on Kendall Drive to do some grocery shopping. It was around 8:00pm and we were driving slowly before we'd arrive to the stoplight at 127th avenue when we noticed a large oval/diamond shaped body of light suspended at a 45 degree angle from our position. There were no clouds in the sky so it was quite clear. What caught our attention is that a small plane was coming up to it from the Tamiami Airport which then descended. Two minutes later another small aircraft came out of the airport and flew in front of the stationary light without there being any reaction from it. By that time I decided to film the third aircraft which also approached the stationary light. From a distance it looked bigger than a star. At that time I observed that there was another automobile parked on the other side of the sidewalk also watching in that direction. The third aircraft also then descended. We then continued on our way. I took two pictures and a brief film. Upon our return at around 9:00pm the star-like light was still suspended in the same spot. There was no sound from the craft/light and no unusual phenomena surrounding the event although I remember that on Saturday night we had observed the same size light/craft in a stationary position (something that our commercial aircraft cannot do) over the same area of Kendall.
