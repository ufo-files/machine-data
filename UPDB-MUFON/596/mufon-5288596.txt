July 2020. I was floating in the evening of July 2020 in my pool beside my house. It was about 7pm. I watched several birds and bugs zip across my view. Everything was below the closeby trees. While looking straight up, I saw a fast moving object move directly from East to West, above the trees, so I knew it wasn't a bug or bird by the speed it moved across the sky. After the object moved toward the Western part of the sky, it arched toward the South, then stopped. At that point it appeared to stop, however it became less out of focus. I believe it went higher into the atmosphere until it was out of sight.
