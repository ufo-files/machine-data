4 hovering crafts in 4 different directions around a large pond with flashing red and green lights at night

Two of us went down to the pond from our cabin to look at the stars when we immediately noticed a large, bright white light with smaller red and green flashing lights directly ahead of us. It was stationary, with occasional hovering movements to either side and up and down, as well as hovering in small circles. We called the others at the cabin to come look, and they joined us. Binoculars showed the lights more clearly, but we were unable to tell the shape of the object at large. We watched for about 90 minutes, and in that time became aware of 3 other hovering objects with red and green lights in three different directions (behind us, and to each of our left and right). The first object we saw was the brightest and moved the most noticeably. We saw several planes flying at the same time (in the night) and they moved much quickly and smoothly, whereas the other lights alternated between hovering/ wobbling or stationary.
