This evening around 1940-2000 my daughter looked up and said look the first star of the night. My wife and I looked up because it is not a normal location for our usual bright stars. We looked at it for a minute to notice it was first moving, then notice it did not represent any kind of airplane. My wife then began filming the video I have attached to this email as I looked on with a pair of binoculars. Even with the binoculars it still did not look anything like a plane then in a blink it was gone from view.
