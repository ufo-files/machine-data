out of the corner of my eye i saw something like an explosion. it had a black ring inside of it. it flashed three times

We were stargazing with my class when I noticed a bright object in the night sky. Just when I focused on it it flashed real bright and then seamed to explode and then move across the night sky. I first thought it was a star. It moved in a linear fashion across the sky from east to west. I was very excited to see the object especially since it was so bright. I lost sight of the object when it faded away and the light got dimmer.
