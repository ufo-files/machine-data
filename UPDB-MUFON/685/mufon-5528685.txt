UFO over I 35 in Austin Tx

My Girlfriend and I were turning off of william cannon dr.onto the frontage road to I35 headed north towards downtown Austin and saw two lights hovering side by side and slowly moving down above the overpass to 290 and 35. It looked like a cars headlights in the air. We were about two miles away from it when we noticed it, it was pretty bright and it was not blinking but as we were going under the overpass it seemed the lights turned off and we lost sight. I thought it was a heli at first but saw no flashers or spotlights. My Gf just kept saying "thats an f'n UFO!" and of all nights not to have the flip camera with us!!!
