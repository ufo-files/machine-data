{nd} Stationary, vanishing, reappearing, diamond-shaped split into two bright white vertical triangles.

I was driving this morning and saw a stationary object in the sky. Where it was in the sky was very clear and blue. At first it appeared to be a solid and strangely shaped square, like a solid, white hole in the sky, but as I kept driving I noticed it was more of a diamond-shape and also appeared to have a vertical line going through the center of the shape. The shape was very solid and defined. I kept watching it to see if it was perhaps a plane flying but it remained stationary in the sky. After watching it for a little over one minute, it disappeared, almost dissolving into itself. Then after a few seconds it reappeared in a materializing way and was the exactly same size and shape as before it disappeared. I kept watching it but I didn't see it disappear again trees began to block my view.
