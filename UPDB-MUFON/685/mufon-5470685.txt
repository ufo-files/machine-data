{jpg} Witnessed event and drove to area and observed object and military and local police

The weekend before Labor Day in the summer of 1985 while staying at my families trailer home located in summitville NY a flashing light was observed above the tree line .. after a few hours my girlfriends dad decided to drive towards the object. Driving on NYS ROUTE 17 east we observed no one else on the highway.. but did observe state trooper vehicles blocking exits and entrances to the route.. we proceeded east and a military convoys was observed passing us fast.. we arrived at a spot near the pine bush exit of 119 to observe military vehicles in the clearling with a large pulsating object appearing to be malfunctioning or in distress.. with 2 smaller similar objets higher up above it.. we stayed to observe and shortly after were prompted by state police to leave area stating we didn1t see anything.. we were escorted back to the trailer park and compared stories with other witnesses
