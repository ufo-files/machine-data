{rjl} Underwater lights

On a plane that lifted off from LAX, I observed lights under the surface of the water in a V-shape pattern. The lights were round and appeared yellowish in color. I believe there were 9 lights. Hard to estimate the size of the object from the air, but had to have been rather large.
