{jpg} 4 lights in V formation S/SE & then all intermixed/disappeared - traveling out of sight in same direction

Standing in my backyard looking at the stars. Neck was hurting so I looked down and gave a quick prayer giving thanks for this peaceful time without stress. Looked up in south/SE above neighbors backyard and saw a V formation of 4 lights. My heart was pounding and I thought it was a large craft. As soon as I had that thought it broke apart and all lights intermixed while traveling further south/SW. It was very bright and I didn't have to question what I read seeing at all. I ran to the front yard and called out to my boyfriend who was in the garage. I grabbed him and we went back to where I was standing in the backyard but the lights were gone. We stood back there watching for them for an hour and a half but they didn't return.
