Six Moon like orbs or discs floated in sky haphazardly coming to center and segregating

Several moon shaped orbs of light appeared in sky as noticed from window first dancing in sky but no source could be traced as they were just in sky and dancing around unnaturally and on going down from flat we noticed they were altogether six in number and hazy due to rainy and cloudy weather and nighttime but clearly were bright lights and moved in every direction converging in middle of sky right above our head but high up in sky and then separated in every direction and then reorganized and repeated the dance. (note sound in video is the water flowing in hole below the ground and not from sky). Source of light could not be traced to any beam or anything on or from earth. After a few minutes we went down again after recoding and talking to people on the street who also wondered what they were and then we could not see them again as they had dissapeared.
