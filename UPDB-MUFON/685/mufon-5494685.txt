{cak} cigar shaped, very bright. looked almost like a figure 8, having appearance of a restricted center.I

I was leaving my house to go to work, heard a flock of geese & looked up to spot them. Instead, I saw this very bright object in the sky to the northwest. At first I thought it was a cylindrical shape, but as it neared, it looked more like two circles stuck together, or an oval that was clinched in the middle. It moved soundlessly to the southwest with no contrail. The size, altitude and speed were hard to determine, as the sky was totally clear and there was nothing to compare it to. I was able to watch it for about 20 seconds, then it was obscured by a tree, so I got in my car and went to work. I checked the open skies as I drove, but did not see it again. I did see several jets, including contrails, but nothing more out of the ordinary.
