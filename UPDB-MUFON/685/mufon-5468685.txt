Cigar like aeroplane without wings and tail, standing still in the air, disappear in about 2 seconds

I observed today an unidentified object in the sky - about 5:45 PM, returning from work by bicycle I noticed in the sky shimmering from the sun "cigar", something like a fuselage without wings and tail, which hung motionless in the air. I wanted to stop my bicycle to take a picture, but before I did it, I lost the object for about 2 seconds from my eyes - the roof of the passed house covered it - but I did not see it again in this place. It took me a few seconds to get to the open space behind the next building, but the sky was completely clean in the radius of many kilometers. He just disappeared. I yet looked around for it for a few minutes, but to no avail.
