My girlfriend and myself like to watch for meteors and on this particular night, we witnessed what we later concluded, was a battle in space between a massive, black, rectangular, keyhole shaped object and a much smaller craft. The small craft appeared as a varying point of light and moved across what had to be thousands of miles of space instantly, as though protecting a spot in space. It appeared to be firing some sort of guided type of light weapon at the larger object. This went on for many minutes and I recorded it on my cellphone camera for over 7 minutes until the battery died. I as of yet have not tried to have the video enhanced on a computer but plan on doing so in the near future.
