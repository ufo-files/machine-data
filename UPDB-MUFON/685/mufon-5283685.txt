Entity photographed in mirror

While conducting a paranormal investigation at a clients house in Ashland City Tennessee in 2017. I took a photo that captured the image of an entity that appeared to be a Grey in a mirror that appeared to have been using the mirror as a portal to enter and leave the residence. The first photo shows a clear image of the Grey raising it's left arm as if to block the flash of the camera as it appeared to be watching from the other side of the portal. The second photo shows a blurred image of the entity sitting or standing a facing away from the mirror. It almost looks like another entity in the background on the other side of the portal. I reported this to a friend named Barry Gaunt who was a member of Mufon in the Kentucky area. I am not sure if he filed a report of this incident or not, so I decided to report it now.
