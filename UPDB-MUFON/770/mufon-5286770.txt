I am an old Marine and semi-retired 27 year Police Officer. I'm not POSITIVE on the date. either Thursday, August 20, or 27, 2020 at 2251 hrs. I was walking South around my pool and a bright blue-green light appeared to the East low in the sky. Reminded me of a power transformer blowing, HOWEVER there was NO sound. The object was shaped like the ball projectile of a handgun round. After 2-3 seconds of just hanging there it accelerated, with NO noise, to the East-North East. Afterward I stood where I initially saw the object and held my hand up. The object would have been the size of my fist.
2 or 3 weeks later I told a nurse friend of this. She stated she had 2 friends driving home late on the same night in Rockwall TX who also told her they had seen the same object. I have not talked to them or know them.
