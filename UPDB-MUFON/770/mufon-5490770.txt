Big ball of light travelling from west to east stopped, hovered then went straight up. Bubble like thing fell

Me and the boyfriend were heading to bed early around 10:25, went in to lay down there's no curtains on the windows because we just moved in so something caught my eye up in the sky. It wasn't like any star I've ever seen so I got up and it appeared to be moving to the east but very slowly and it was a very bright color orange and bigger then a star. So I call my boyfriend over and we're both trying to figure out what this is when it stops and starts ascending and getting dimmer. But before it's gone this bubble looking thing floats down and looks to have landed somewhere in the field outside our house. We were trying to figure out if somebody parachuted from a plane but there was no noise just silence.
