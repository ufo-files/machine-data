I went out on my balcony to smoke a cigarette. My house is high up on a hill, and surrounded by trees. I am facing north west looking above the tree line towards NYC, when I see a bright light. I see other planes flying about, but this light is brighter than the planes. It could be a helicopters search lights I think. I stare at it, but it doesnt move. There is no noise, maybe its a meteor, I think. But again no movement, it is stationary, and the lights are so bright. I have seen meteors before and this doesnt seem like a meteor. I watch it some more, then I run for my cell phone to get a video. I record the bright light, in my phone it seems to be cigar shaped but with the naked eye it seems circular. it also seems to be pulsating. I am skeptical, I think its a star just very low and bright. I look at the other stars in the sky, and this is not a star it is just way to bright. I really don't know what I saw. It remained in the sky for over an hour. I had to go in my house, when I came back a half hour later it was gone.
