{dr}red ball traveling from the east, stopped, then shot straight up out of sight with a second

This was long ago, but while walking along this country lane with my date we stopped to look out at the starry sky above Pottstown to the NE. It was a perfectly clear, beautiful summer night. We noticed a red dot (ball?) in the distance that was moving in our direction at perhaps 5 miles (?) away. We watched it move slowly for a few minutes then it stopped and hung there still at a distance. Then it went straight up and out of sight in an instant. Never seen anything like it before or since. And we were not inebriated in any way, just two wholesome teens out for a walk. I believe it was reported in the Pottstown Mercury later as others had seen it too.
