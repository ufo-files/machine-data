distant star hovering then moving off at speed

i was in our garden having a drink of coffee, it was a clear night not many stars visible to the eye.i looked up at the viewable constellations (not many constellations visible due to location and street lights) and noticed a couple of stars which were not very bright, the stars appeared in a diagonal line in the sky fairly close together and the lower star was stationary then it shot off at great speed (ie: faster than any known air craft) and then it was gone.
