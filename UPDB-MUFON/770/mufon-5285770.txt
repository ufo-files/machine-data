A glowing, rectangular light passed through the sky as I was taking a video during a lightning storm

I was walking back from the gym and it began to get windy and lightning started. I was walking north and I turned to the east to take a video of the lightning and a glowing, rectangular object heading south cut across the screen as a truck was driving by.
