Observed through telescope. Portion of curved edge on a stationary disc shaped object surrounded by energy waves in the form of mist.

My wife and I were trying to look at some stars through my telescope. We could not see anything with our naked eye, but when looking through the lens we could make out the edge of some sort of structure hovering stationary in front of the star we were pointed at. It had a defined edge with strange patterns along the lateral, outside face of the ring. We could only see a small portion through the scope, like a slice of pie, so we could tell the object was super-massive. It stayed in sight for at least 10 minutes before it disappeared completely and the star became viable again.
