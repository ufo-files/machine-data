Point Isabel dog park, 4 objects in formation

I was walking my dog at Point Isabel Regional Shoreline next to San Francisco Bay on the afternoon of July 4. 
In bright sunshine, toward the east at about 60 degrees, a group of 4 objects appearing to be about 1-2 feet across hovered around 150 feet up, and moved in formation. A group of four strangers walking our dogs all stood and watched.
The objects sometimes flew in identical formation, and sometimes split up 10 feet apart.
I'm not sure whether drones can fly so close together and in such tight formation at speed, which is why I'm classifying this as a UAP.
There appeared to be a halo or different type of air around each one, sort of like the blur of blades, but again, they behaved differently than a typical drone.
After about 5 minutes of maneuvers, all 4 objects flew south, along I-580 toward Oakland.
I only took out my phone after a few minutes, unfortunately. So the still pics are not great.
In the still pics, look directly up from the group of people, at the top of the frame. You can see the 4 objects. This area is full of birds including pelicans and seagulls but these objects did not fly like any kind of bird.
