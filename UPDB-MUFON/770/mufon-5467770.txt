apparition d'un objet inconnu dans le ciel

La fluidité de son mouvement rotatif lui donnait , une impression de légèreté et de flottement dans l'air.

Installed on the terrace of my garden, my attention was suddenly drawn to an object, the shape and movement of which was completely unknown to me.
The fluidity of its rotating movement gave it an impression of lightness and floating in the air.
The shape of a dome which reflects a golden light, only on the top associated with a cubic shape of black color does not allow me to describe the top and the bottom of the object.
After a few seconds of amazement and incomprehension, I called my husband, screaming in my astonishment, and it was at that precise moment that I lost sight of the object in the sky, which instantly disappeared.
