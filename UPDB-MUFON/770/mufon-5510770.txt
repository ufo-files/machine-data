Buena Vista. Driving home around 8:30 we looked to the southeast and saw an orange spiral smoke trail, then noticed an orange orb a bit of distance above it that looked to be standing still. It started slowly moving north, stopped again, when a sudden burst of light and a double teardrop looking cloud appeared. The orb then appeared from behind the cloud, and quickly took off going due east.
