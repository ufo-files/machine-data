{rjl} Saw object in hover in sky, strange light patterns, fast movement then hovered again.

Me and a friend, outside of his house in Dickson City, about 9:30-9:45pm, saw an object in the sky. At first I thought it was a plane, but it hovered for approximately 10 minutes. So then I thought it could be a helicopter, but saw no apparent shape. It flashed red, blue and white lights, similar to a police helicopter, but the flashing of the lights were not in police light fashion, the patterns looked to be random. At one point, the lights seemed to go around the object in a circular/disc-like shape. There also looked to be another object, that illuminated no light, nor reflected any light from the object, fly right by it at high speeds. Because of light pollution it was very hard to see, and was much smaller than the object. The object also looked to of moved closer to us, slowly, then further away. Peaking our curiosity, we jumped in my car to drive closer to it. Walking to my car we briefly lost sight of it. We got in my car and started driving up the street, when we noticed it was at a new location, drastically north of it's previous location, leaving us to believe it traveled very fast (from when we lost sight to regaining sight of the object only took about 20 seconds). We drove toward it, keeping our eye on it, while noticing it looking to ascend and descend. Knowing we'll have no luck getting closer, and video or pictures would not be captured well due to light pollution, I took my friend back home. As I was driving away I could no longer see it, but he contacted me minutes later and said it was back in it's original spot, just hovering there.
