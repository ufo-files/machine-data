This is not like other unexplained sightings. It is more of an event that occurred in my home. My master bedroom has a master bath. This so embarrassing to talk about, but I feel the need to do so. I had been watching T.V. with my husband and waiting as long as I could to run to the
bathroom. I sat down, and mid-stream, I hear an odd noise outside my house. I didn't think much of it until it started moving along my roof. The closer it got to my bathroom window, the louder it became. It was a hovering sound, like you would hear in Sci-Fi movies. I couldn't move. I felt trapped. As the sound got increasingly louder, the sound moved into the bathroom. It came about 3 feet into the room. The sound was so loud now. I was so scared! I closed my eyes and started to pray " I am the righteousness of God in Christ, Jesus." I repeated it over and over. It suddenly stopped and just disappeared. I don't know what would have happened if it had reached where I was sitting. I get panic attacks now. And for several days, after this happened, my legs and arms felt weak. My jaw bones even felt weak.
