{td}In two nights in a row, while driving west in Abq, saw a hovering white light in the SW of town, (more west),. It stayed in that stationary position for a long time, maybe 20-40 mins and then it started moving but not like known flight behavior.

I was driving down Lomas, heading west, in Albuquerque NM. I saw a white light just hovering in the SW part of town. It was about 20-281 to the horizon. It was pretty low in orbit. When ever I tried to drive in the backstreets so I could pull over to see it better it disappeared from my sight, as buildings and trees were in the way. I thought it was odd that it wasnt moving. So it wasn't a plane or helicopter. This was at 5:40-6:10pm it was right before the sun went down but was still light in the sky. There were no visible stars in the sky, was still too bright for them, so it couldn't be a star. Plus I could tell it was closer to the ground. 
After I got home, I pulled over on a steep incline where I could see it better. By thid time it was around 6:10-6:20pm. It had gotten dark now. It started moving. But in a hovering way. Back n forth, left and right. Kinda aimlessly. It was weird. Then it turned towards me, and started heading east towards town, but i lost visual when it went behind some tall buildings to the south of me. 
This exact scenario happened twice. I saw it both days in a row and same time. No mention on local news what I could be. Im not sure if it was military craft ot something else, but it didn't move like any known aircraft Ive ever seen before. 

Oh yeah, also, there were some other aircraft around it sometimes. Mostly appeared to be helicopters. 
My girlfriend also witnessed this on one of the days.
I did take photos of it although was hard to tell what it was as was too far away. Just looked like a light mostly. But from the many pics u can tell how it moved.
