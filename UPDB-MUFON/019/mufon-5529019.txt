blinding sphere

Driving home after dropping my daughter off at dance class at 10:30 AM on 10/27/10 in Colleyville TX driving from southlake on Whites chapel back toward Colleyville I noticed a blinding bright round object dropping like a plane nosediving. I could not tell what it was but just asumed it was a plan nose diving and the sun was hitting it like when you hold foil up to the sun. Realized that did not make sense after recalling memories of other planes and them never looking like this object. It moved fast, I remember thinking I am so worried that plane looks like it is in trouble since it appeared to be falling straight down. It suddenly made a left (my Right) when I went through some tree and lost it. Got home and continued to look up but never saw anything else!?? Totally clear skies and sunny great day???
