{jpg} 3-4 Lights, Disappear and Reappear

My friend and I parked at a restaurant and I saw originally 4 yellow-orange lights hovering beyond a mall. They were in a formation, then the lights went away. A couple seconds later, they reappeared but in a slightly different pattern. There was a plane circling around them. They vanished and came back a few more times and then shot flames (similar to fireworks) out of the lights, this time there were three and they were in a triangle position. They then sunk below my view, going "behind" the mall.
