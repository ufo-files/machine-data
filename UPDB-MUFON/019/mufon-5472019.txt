Fishing at an abandoned road bridge on the Pike river between Sauble Beach and Wiarton on the Bruce Peninsula 
It abruptly appeared above our heads and lit up the surrounding area silencing the frog crickets and wild life( about 50 yards around us in a perfect circle)
Thought it was an Aurora beam but later found out no one has ever seen one

I did contact the government here a few years ago with negative responce. It did happen and if it was a natural thing I would like to talk to someone with the same experience.
