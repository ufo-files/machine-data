{rjl} Three different large Unidentified Objects flying close to each other with no sound

I was outside and noticed a bright white globe moving in the sky from East to West. It was very bright and was strobing. The object was very large - about the size of a commercial jet, and it was very close. I then saw another object that looked square and was a dark shape with two red lights and two green lights on either side on the bottom. These were not flashing, just stationary. I'd say the second object was about 1/2 mile from the first. Then yet another, rectangular-shaped dark object flew behind the second one on the same path and about 1/2 mile away. It had several white lights on either side on the bottom. These objects moved very slowly and surprisingly, made no sound. I would have expected to hear something since they were so close.
