I saw a transparent boomerang in the sky over marshall.

I walked to the store tonight as usual. However, on the way home when I looked up as I always do it was as if something yanked my head to the side to turn me backwards. It was then I saw the object. I followed its path for about 15-20 Sec. completely mesmerized by what i saw. My entire body tingled. It is colored gray because it was almost transparent.
