{cak} 3 orange/red fireballs hovering over Madison, WI.

Driving eastbound on the beltline at 9:55 pm. Saw 2 bright orange/red fireballs hovering over the beltline. Exited on Midvale, picked up my husband and parked on Midvale outside an elementary school. Then my husband and I witnessed 3 fireballs. They were large enough to be an aircraft and lower than the airplane towers nearby. The fireballs were significantly different than the red lights on the airplane towers. They were glowing intensely. They were moving very slowly southwest toward the Fitchburg/Verona area. Two of the fireballs were in very close proximity to each other, the 3rd was further away and moving a little faster. We got back in our car and tried to drive closer to them and then lost sight of them behind trees. They seemed to be moving together or in a formation.
