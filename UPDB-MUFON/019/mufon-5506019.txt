I was walking my dog,when I happened to look up in the sky and saw four or five lights moving slowly in the sky. They looked like stars,but they were in a pattern and moving. I immediately went into the house and called my wife to come and see this so she wouldnt think I was crazy. With luck the objects were still where she could see them. We both were amazed to say the least! I wanted to call someone at the time,but didn't know where to start,so I didn't until now,when I saw the article in the Charlotte sun News paper. Not that this should substantiate my sanity,Im a retired Police Lieutenant.
