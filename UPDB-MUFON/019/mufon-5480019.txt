{jpg} Like a strand of led Xmas lights that streamed colors back and forth the width of more than 2 football fields

I came out of my local bible study at approx 7:45, as I turn the corner perpendicular to coast hwy, I am already looking up do to my truck going up a slight incline. Then I see it. Stationany, silent, stuck at a canted 45 degree angle in the sky. Greens, reds, blues, and amber exterior lights that we part of a bigger object... they ran in succession streaming back and forth, left to right, kinda like the "knight rider" light... but in ALL colors and the width of 100s of feet. A group of 2nd 0 some odd kids in their 20's were standing on the street corner as I turned it... I asked them if the saw what I saw and they replied "yes." When I asked them how long they watched it...they said 10 min. I raced around the corner back to the church to grab my Pastor, he unfortunately just locked up and left. When I raced around to see it again... it was gone..
