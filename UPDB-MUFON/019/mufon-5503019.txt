I STOOD THERE FOR ABOUT TEN MINUTES AND OBSERVED THE OBJECT. IT CONTINUED TO PULSE CONSISTENTLY FOR THE ENTIRE ENCOUNTER. I WAS SO ASTONISHED BY WHAT I WAS WITNESSING THAT I CALLED A FRIEND ON THE PHONE WHO IS NOT A POLICE OFFICER, AND STOOD THERE TALKING WITH THEM ON THE PHONE AS I OBSERVED. THEY WERE EQUALLY AMAZED.
