{cak} On J train observed 1giant ball of light, then 2 then they switched positions then back then 3rd and formed a triangle

I was sitting on Manhattan bound J train and looking South East I saw a big light in the sky that was not normal, i saw planes flying in towards JFK airport and they were closer but much smaller then the light. Then a second appeared they zoomed past each other to where the other was then darted back to their original position. Now I jump up and start taking pictures but the train is moving. As this is happening there is at least 1 plane I see in the sky & a helicopter both much closer but much smaller with traditional blinking lights. A 3rd big bright ball of light appears & they form a traingle and stay stationary (what seemed like perfect exact distance) I get to my stop & run to the end of the platform to take pictures then they begin to simultaneously rotate counter counterclockwise, I think I can get a better shot from Jamaica bound side so I run there down then up the other stairs & take more pics.Then they stop & rotating, stay still & simultaneously then all dart up & right. I took 46 pictures on my Samsung s8+ & 9 of them have great pics. Just 2 balls of light then 3 & the position changes during rotation. Most amazing thing I have ever seen in my life
