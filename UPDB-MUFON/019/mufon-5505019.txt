{kt}Very bright object with various shaped objects moving in surrounding area

December 15,2013 approximate time 5:00P.M. I was driving back home to Alvin,Texas from visiting my daughter and grandchildren near Beaumont, Texas. I took I-10 to Beltway-8 south,then south on I-45 and exited Dixie Farm Rd. Driving southwest on Dixie Farm Rd I was coming up to Beamer Rd traffic light and seen this very bright spot above the clouds and reflections of objects moving and fading out.I looked to the right to see if the person in the car next to me was seeing this,she was busy texting.I looked at the van in front of me and could see in his side mirror he was talking on his phone. All during this time I was getting my work cell phone in camera mode. I took one photo with all three objects in view. Then as I was taking another photo,one of the objects faded out,the other object dropped down quickly and as I snapped the picture a greyish triangle object quickly appears and fades out of my view.I continually keep looking around to see if anyone else was seeing this.The traffic light turned green and the only thing still visible was this bright glowing spot.
