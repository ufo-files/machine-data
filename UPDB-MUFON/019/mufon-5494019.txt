{cak} saw a triangle shaped object with yellow/orange lights (unblinking) flying under clouds.

My husband and I were taking our dog for a walk at night, when we turned onto our street we noticed a flash of white light in the sky ahead that disappeared. I continued to look around and to the east I noticed two lines of orange/yellow lights, on a triangular craft. I asked him what it was and as I continued to look I noticed the object appeared solid, as the middle was all black. The object moved from south to north and disappeared behind houses and trees. We were both astonished, knowing immediately what we saw was a ufo. The object appeared to be below the cloud cover and at an arms length was the size of my hand.
