{rjl} Spring-Summer 1966, second hand account. {djs, classic saucer with a silver/chrome appearance and red, green, and white lights, Transcription from narrative}

On a warm, dry night sometime between March and August 1966, a large group of people containing both adults and children witnessed a nearby UFO. The shape was a classic saucer with a silver/chrome appearance and red, green, and white lights. It spun very quickly, like a top. It hovered over a schoolyard for about 5 minutes at a height of about 2 stories of a standard building. At that point, the witnesses heard a "Whoosh" sound, and the craft moved at incredible speed to the left, leaving a trail of lights behind it. At the time of the sighting children had been playing in the area, and the adults were watching them. Reportedly, dozens of witnesses saw the event. This event was relayed to me (who is reporting the event) by three members of my family: My mother, grandmother, and uncle, who each witnessed the event. 

Later that night, my mother (who was about five years old at the time) claims to have seen another, smaller craft. It hovered just above an approximately 10 foot tall tree. At the time the witness was hiding in a drain pipe, as she was playing hid and seek with other children. She reports that she was the only witness, and that she was paralyzed by fear. She also claims that she felt that the craft knew of her presence. It then zoomed off similar to the first, larger one. In appearance, the craft was similar to the first, only it was smaller, and the lights were not red, and green like the first, larger craft, they were only white or blue-white.
