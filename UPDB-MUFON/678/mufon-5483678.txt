3 lights in tandem randomly flashing

We were on a group sky watch in the New Forest, Hampshire, UK. There were 5 of us but 1 didn't witness the event as his car wouldn't start and he was talking with the breakdown service at the time. We were standing close to our cars when one of us pointed out the lights coming into view from behind some trees in front of us and travelling left to right (West to East). There were 3 objects in tandem moving fairly slowly and occasionally flashing. My initial thought was chinese lanterns but this was quickly dismissed through discussion with the group and one of us was using binoculars. It was at this point that I picked up my night vision scope and started recording. The video footage is in black and white but these objects appeared to be white when viewed with the naked eye or binoculars. We lost sight of these objects first with the naked eye and binoculars but they continued to be picked up on the night vision for a bit longer which I assume to mean they were travelling away from us, in a North East direction.

I lost sight of them after around 8 minutes as the video cable came loose from the night vision scope. When it came back on I found and continued to track 2 objects heading the other way. I think these weren't the same objects though as the original 3.

We since learned that these 3 objects were seen between Cornwall and Northampton that night by up to 5 separate groups of people apart from us
