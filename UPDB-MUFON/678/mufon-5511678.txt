{cak} Completely silent, very bright star like object moved from the west to the east very quickly.

Camping on the edge of the shawnee myself and two others observed a very bright, silent aircraft move across the sky. Very similar to the sighting others have reported of the "star like" craft that is spotted often in this area. It moved at a quick speed and was fairly close to the ground. Moved from west to east and vanished. No cloud cover. From what we observed, there were three very bright white lights and looked like a black triangle from a side angle.
