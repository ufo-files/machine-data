Bright light,

My wife and I went for a drive and stopped where the night before we witnessed lights in the sky which did not move for around 20 minutes they just blinked in the clouds and finally moved towards the west. Last night we returned about 2 hours earlier . I was using the bonoculars and had just passed them to my wife when as she put them to her eyes I noticed a bright light which grew larger quickly I asked her fo the bonoculars back and as she passed them to me she also saw the light, I focused on the light and as I was looking it turned at an angle and I could see the whole side of it. The sun was to the south west just going behind the hills in the distance. When I first saw the side of the object it looked alot like a torpedo in shape , I'm guessing about 75ft in length and I would estimate 10-15miles i'm not real good with distance. But I do know what we saw.In the time that I gave the bonoculars to my wife it went up at an angle into the cloud above. When she looked at the object all she saw was a black dot. It all lasted less than 5 seconds.
