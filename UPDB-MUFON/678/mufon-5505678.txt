{rjl} Large Black UFO affecting power to the neighborhood

My husband and myself were in our living-room watching a movie. All of a sudden my husband and I noticed a shadow that passed by the sun, obviously caused by a large object. At the time though, we thought nothing of it & then we heard a loud "bang" loud as a cannon, or as if a transformer had blown. At that moment power went out for a few seconds then right back on. This prompted my husband to look out the window & down the street where he noticed our neighbors were standing outside as if their power had went out also. (It did & they also heard the sound) Our neighbors went back inside. (Their power as well as ours was back on) When my husband walked away from the window, that is when I went over & looked out & up toward the sky. (I always was & still am a "sky-watcher")
When I looked out to the west, I saw a huge black object with many black spheres attached to it. It wasnt making a sound, but it was moving at a rapid pace yet, gracefully into the southwest where it disappeared into a sun-lit cloud. (I hollered for my husband to come back to the window to see it, but just as he got to the window is when it disappeared into the cloud leaving me the only one to see this) When I first saw it, I thought it must have been a group of birds, but I know that wasnt the case. This object didn't move like a flock of birds and was far too large because of the distance. I stood there straining my eyes because of the brightness of the white & well lit cloud, I kept watching anyway to see it come back out, as I was not wanting to believe my eyes. I stood there about three minutes just observing & reflecting on the occurrence. The object never did come back out. It was as if it vanished into nothingness.
