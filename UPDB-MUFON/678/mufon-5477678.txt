Event reported by FI in Qu1bec - Got in contact with entities and was told not to do it again.

One night in November, the withness was visited by entities. They asked him why did he want to get in contact with them and how was he able to do it. They told him not to try to communicate with them again. The visiting party was composed of 3 small grey aliens and a taller one dressed in black that looked more human. The following night, the witness said he travelled in time to the fifty's. He met with a young man and told him that he's from the future and (told the young man that) one day you will be famous. The young man became a well know artist in the USA.
