{dr}Black Cube hovers three feet above my bed

On August 30, 2015 at 4:45 am I was in my bedroom sitting at my computer
desk. I had the T.V. on and I was going back and forth watching it and
being on my computer. I heard a crackling noise that sounded electrical.
I turned around in my chair and jumped up very fast. Hovering, 3 feet
above my bed was a black cube. It was the size of a Rubik's cube. It 
appeared metallic because it was polished to a mirror finish. It was
hovering and not moving at all. I tried to grab it because I wanted proof.
When I went for it, it made 3 flashes of light and it was gone. The light
was light blue and it flashed like a strobe. Even though the cube looked
to be solid, the light came out from the center and lit the cube up. This
is the first time I've seen this cube. I find it amazing that after all
these years with experiences, they still have new things going on with me.
I will say this cube scared the s**t out of me. Anytime I have experiences
with black objects I get suspicious. Something is just not right with them.
One last thing, I want to go on record with the state, something big is
going to happen soon. What I mean is that disclosure is going to happen
very soon. All of our questions will be answered. The time is close.
