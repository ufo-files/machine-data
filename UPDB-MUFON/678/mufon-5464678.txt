Driving home from work late at night after picking up my dog. Dog started to get anxious so I pulled off the road to let her out to do her business but she started barking, thought she was barking at the traffic but then, there was no traffic. Waited for her to go to the bathroom but she stood still and was staring towards the road and then I later noticed lights hovering some distance from the road we came from. I quickly put the dog in the car and went to investigate but then the 5 lights rose higher and got smaller and smaller and zig zagged a little before disappearing. When I got back to the car my dog was in the backseat terrified. Tried to comfort her and but then noticed in the corner of my eye something move. I looked up just to see something running behind my car through the taillights. It had reddish fur, tail but a human face, very hard to discribe, I floored it. When home, my dog was too terrified to leave the car. I had a few nightmares after that.
