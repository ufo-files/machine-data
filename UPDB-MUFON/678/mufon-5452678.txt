Star like in color with green beams coming off it

It was around 10 PM and I was leaving the woods from four wheeler riding. I was getting ready to pull off on the road, from a bridge. That's when I looked up an seen a really bright star like object. Below the object on the left and right was glowing green in a straight line,which looked like it might of been the outline of the craft, which made it look a triangle. The object was seen coming from the west and lost it over the mountain heading east.
