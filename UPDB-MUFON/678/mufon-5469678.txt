{rjl} Spherical object that would light up and move instantly from one position in the sky to another. Hovered and pulsated a lot.

I was in my backyard, taking my Dogs out and having a smoke. I noticed a flash in my peripheral vision and it drew my attention to that area of the sky. A couple of seconds later I saw another flash of light very close to where I had noticed the first one. Maybe 10 seconds later a spherical shaped light slowly lit up, growing very bright, it seemed to shift colors slightly, like it took on a bluish tone. It then disappeared and another idententical sphere popped into view in a different part of the sky. I am unsure whether it was a another sphere or if it had just moved tens of miles in a split second. I see Airplanes flying at night and meteorites all the time and knew this was NOT either of those. So I yelled into the house to my family to come and see. My 15 year old Son came out and we watched the object hover, pulsate, grow very dim then intensely bright, change color and zip all over the Sky for about 10-15 minutes. It was quite a show. Sorry there is not Video, I thought about going in and getting my Phone but decided that it probably wouldn't have done any good because the object was so erratic and I had no idea how much longer it would be there. Didn't want to miss it :p. There was some Air Traffic during this time. It looked to be standard commercial Airliners. The last we saw of the object, it was "skipping" through the sky almost due West toward the Gulf of Mexico, near Crystal River, FL.
