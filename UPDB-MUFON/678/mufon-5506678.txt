{dr}This was my second time seeing the same formation of 4 lights circling, coming together, and spreading back out. I was followed both times.

I was standing at a bus stop with my son, whom I just got from daycare and we were on our way home. He said, "look daddy". I looked up and said, "oh my god, again". There were four lights in the form of a square that rotated in a circle. It seemed like every two rotations the lights would come together still holding the shape of the square until it appeared to be 1 point. At this time the lights expand back out into a square. It rotated the entire time directly above me. It was not off in the distance. I noticed that while these lights were very visible just above the clouds they were not visible in spaces where there were no clouds. My son and I get on the bus about 10 minutes later. We only have to travel about a mile down the road. Its about a 3 minute ride on the bus or a 30 minute walk with a 2 year old. We made it to our destination so I immediately decided to look back in the direction that we came from to see if the lights were still there but they werent there they were still directly above me. I live in the back of an apartment complex and it followed me there. It remained in the same spot after I had gone inside. I looked outside the window at it a couple times. The lights were gone within 10min of me being inside
