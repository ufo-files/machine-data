I have a glass window in my front door, which faces east. I was walking by the door and I noticed headlights in the sky. I backed up and stared. I moved my head around as I thought it might be a reflection, I wear glasses. No, it was still in the sky. I stared at this for awhile, thinking all kinds of things, but pretty sure what I was seeing was in fact a UFO. It started to turn south, and as it did so, I noticed something like fins or something on the side and as it completed the turn, there was a blinking red light on the bottom, near the end, but 10 or so feet from the end. There was a full moon that night or bright, because I noticed the reflection of the moon on the thing. After the turn was completed, it went a few 100 feet and disappeared. Poof, gone! I ran out of the door and could find, nor see anything. Called the 911, but no one else reported seeing anything. How could they not, it was 7:30 in the evening, people were driving on highway 41, less than a half mile east of my house. I remember I kept thinking, surely people are going to stop and look at this thing, no one did. So, was I crazy. I sat and thought this a bit and wrote down the day and time and drew a picture so I wouldnt forget, not that I will as I can still see it as clear as the night i saw it.
