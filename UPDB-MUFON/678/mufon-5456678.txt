{jpg} Dec 1995 witnessed object descend and submerge in LSC

Reported this sighting years ago to MUFON yet no trace of it now?? After reporting pushy investigator attempted to somehow verify when I had given all info in report. I will reiterate for the RECORD,

Winter 95 or 96. SCS tree lighting ceremony, afterwards parent stops at party store near laundromat 13/Jefferson. Waited in car with friend, clear night. Parked facing north and looking to our right out over lake (no houses blocking lakeview at that time) our attention was caught by a bright multi-colored light descending from high in southeast sky. It lined up due east of our position and descending completely vertically slower at which point I could make out a sort of dome on top that seemed larger than the disk structure of the bottom. Orange and green lights
flashed and sequenced randomly as it descended close to lake. It was maybe a mile out and we were 200yrds inland. I don't know if the lake was frozen at all at the time or out to what distance it could have been frozen but the craft appeared to submerge below the horizon or waterline and emanated a glow of its respective lightning on the surface of the lake for a short time before going dark. A friend with me at the time saw all of this, never said a word at the time as we were in disbelief. Asked friend about what we witnessed short time later, he said was told not to talk about it by parent.
