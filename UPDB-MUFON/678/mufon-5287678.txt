Single light sweeping around an apparent disk shaped object in the sky, periodically, seen for approximately 5 minutes

On the night of May 16th 2021 I went out into our garden for a cigar. I often look up to the stars, the sky was totally clear of clouds and there was a very bright half moon dominating to the SW. It wasn’t totally dark, I could see 2 other light sources across the southern sky, Denebola and Arcturus. I knew this because I was using an app on my iPad to see what the stars were called.
I noticed what I thought was a short tail of a shooting star to the left of Denebola. It was very short in time and length but I decided to keep focused on that portion of sky in case there were more.
I’m guessing within 10-20 seconds the same light happened again in exactly the same place. This time it appeared to move in a slight curve, as if a single light was travelling around the circumference of an invisible disk shaped object, once, with a gap ranging from 10-30 second between. Almost like a Nike logo tick. It happened maybe 5 more times before I remembered I had the iPad so I started to video the light. It was dark, my camera isn’t very good in low light but the footage was captured nevertheless, to my amazement.
The period between the lights increased gradually, then it ceased to appear again after 5 minutes. I tried filming again but it didn’t reappear.
