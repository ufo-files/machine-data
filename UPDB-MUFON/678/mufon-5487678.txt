Rotating shape flew overhead whilst I was lying back in my kayak, no noise?

I was kayaking solo on the river Weir somewhere between Bishop Auckland and Durham city. I was relaxing and laying back in my kayak. I had been trying to film sandmartins or swifts. I saw this shape above me and thought it was a black rubbish bag caught by the wind. I tried to get a better view with my camera so i started recording and zoomed in to maximum. It was a black shape rotating. Moving steadily in a straight line over my head. It was silent. It was sort of tumbling as it was rotating. I was relaxed throughout. I lost sight of it when it moved past the river bank i was resting under. After some refreshment I carried on my way.
