{cak} slow moving orange lights appear to get sucked into the clouds with no trail or normal flight pattern

My friend and I were out smoking a cigarette when we noticed two bright orange glowing lights through the trees. One of the lights was higher up in the sky, and after observing them for a while we deemed the lower light to be a plane. Then from the garage light behind us we noticed a shadow and we thought it was my friends mom, and she doesn't like us smoking so we got nervous for a second, but it turned out to be a leaf. We then brought our attention back to our cigarettes and conversations when I noticed that the light that was higher up in the sky was still there, but the lower one was not (because it was a plane). We were trying to figure out what it was when all of the sudden it completely vanished into the clouds that were way above it. It was unlike anything either of us have seen before, as it appeared to have been sucked into he clouds. It left no trail, made no noise, did nothing else besides move extremely slowly across the sky. It had orangish-reddish lights being emitted from all apparently spherical parts of the object. We were in complete amazement and were so excited to have seen something we can actually call an Unidentified Flying Object. During our excitement I move my eyes back to where we saw it, and noticed another slow moving object that was exactly identical to the other UFO in every way. Moved slowly, glowed, and about 5 seconds later it also vanished into the clouds the same way as the first.
