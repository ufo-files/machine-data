{rjl} Three discs hovered, sped off in different directions. Converged again and disappeared at high speed.

Three disc shaped objects appeared in night sky over Hickam AFB, Hawaii at approximately 2100 hours. They appeared in a triangular formation and hovered in position for approximately fifteen seconds, then sped off in different directions at a very high rate of speed. Around five minutes later the objects sped back into the triangular formation, hovered for a few seconds and then sped off at a very high rate of speed. They didn't reappear. I was not the only observer. Another person on the flight line observed the same event. I don't know the name of this other soldier, but we both discussed what we saw and we totally agreed with each other.
