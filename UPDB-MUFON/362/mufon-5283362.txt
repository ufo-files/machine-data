Sighting while riding in a car

While riding with some friends on a fall evening(almost dusk), I was located in the rear seat quietly looking up at the sky. I saw a lot of high altitude aircraft in the sky. I usually see those aircraft crisscrossing the sky leaving trails. On this particular day there were many and it looked as if they were converging! I then saw a disc very high up! It looked as if it were flipping like a coin…one side black the other side white. And as the other aircraft got closer…it blinked out! It was gone! 

I am also witness to the Kecksburg UFO and other sightings.
