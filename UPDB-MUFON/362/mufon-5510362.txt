(GS) I was out taking pictures & after uploading, noticed an object behind an airplane in the picture.

I take pictures of the weather & clouds all the time. On the 6th, I was out walking my dog and taking pictures. It was probably around 2 or 3 in the afternoon. My camera is a digital Nikon. When I uploaded the photos, I noticed a small cylindrical object behind an airplane. At the time I took the picture, I hadnt noticed either one. I take my pictures so that they upload very large & I reduce their size by 80 to 85%. When I went to reduce this picture, I noticed the object. There was a golf tournament in nearby Memphis and I observed a blimp in the sky the day after this happened, but it looked very obviously like a blimp. This object in my picture does not have the under carriage you see on a blimp and the size seems wrong. I played around with the picture & made it even larger and inverted the colors and the object is obviously there. Pictures taken in the same area of the sky within a few minutes of the same time this picture was taken do not show anything there, airplane or otherwise. I reduced the size of the picture to about what it was when I uploaded it and reversed the color inversion so that is back to normal. It is not a bird, or another airplane; I do not know what this is, which by my understanding makes it a UFO.
