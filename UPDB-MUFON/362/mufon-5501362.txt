Hovering green orb

I saw the object afterwards in photos taken on the beach at spanish point in county clare. It appeared in the photos and I did not see it in real time. It appears to travel from one end of the beach to the other and I noticed from the sequence of how the photos were taken it seemed to come closer to where I was standing on the beach very near the water. It does not appear in all the photos taken during the time therefore I don't believe it was a camera issue and I've never seen anything like this before.
