Egg shaped craft would be seen almost nightly for a month over the same woods

I would be repeatedly woken up at night from lights shining through the window. Once I was awake I could observe a craft hovering over the trees adjacent from my window for quite some time. The craft would move slowly in all directions before finally darting off into the sky and disappearing. The object being so close to and amongst the trees did not emit and kind of reaction force from the unknown means of propulsion because the none of the vegetation would so much as sway as if being hit by exhaust or anything being emitted by the object.
