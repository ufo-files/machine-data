Caught short video of very fast object while filming birds over Lake Erie

I was out on the beach at Cedar Point in July of 2020 taking videos with my Iphone. I was just kind of playing around with slow motion videos trying to get beautiful shots of the water and the birds flying in the sunrise. I didnt notice anything when I was taking the video but when I went back to watch it, I noticed an object, very quickly moving through my screen. I am unsure of what it is but it seems to be to low for anything that could be considered an orbiting object. However I heard no sound and as fast as this thing was moving there would have been a sonic boom. The object appears to fly straight and then slightly turn at the very end. I didnt realize MUFON was a place I could report the sighting until now. Maybe it could be explained by experts at the organization.
