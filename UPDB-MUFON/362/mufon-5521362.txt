3 groups of 4 small stars start moving and making formations at night

A friend was talking about how many more stars he would be able to see in Colorado, and we were all just looking at the sky, and suddenly some movement got our attention. Those tiny faded stars start moving in a formation with no sound. About 3 groups of 4 stars. Sometimes each group would be in line, sometimes form a triangle and suddenly they all moved like bees in a hive. There was no tail, like meteor shower, and they were very pale light blue, just like small stars. Im a skeptical person, and I was trying to compare to balloons, but it was dark and no wind, and these things were flying really fast. I don't know what this is, but I hadnt seen anything like that before.
