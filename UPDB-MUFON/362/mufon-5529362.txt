Bright Light

Was outside just walking around when I saw this bright white light going south to north towards Abilene Texas. I thought it might have been a satellite but it was way to low to be a satellite, theres no way this was a satellite. It just kept going then all of a sudden it dimmed out and was gone, as though it turned its lights off. I hope someone else here saw this. It was quite noticeable up in the sky.
