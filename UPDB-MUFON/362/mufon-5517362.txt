(gs) It was star-like, but there where 6 of them in a triangular shape/formation. No sound emitted from objects.

It was on the 4th of July. We where having a good time shooting off firecrackers when these lights appeared, in the sky, towards Tulsa. My younger brother thought it was nothing, but I know a ton about Astronomy. I knew it wasnt a star, because the lights where moving across the sky. Even though we have an airport and military base nearby, I knew it wasnt a plane, because there where 6 lights, in the formation or shape, of a triangle!! Also, they werent blinking red or green. They werent blinking at all, and there wasnt any noise. All I knew is that it wasnt a series of bright stars or planes. Then it hit me!!! They where UFOs!! This whole time my younger brother was shooting off firecrackers, but I was paying attention to the UFOs. I was trying to use my phones camera to take a picture, but I had to clear memory space for the lights. Unfortunately, by the time I got memory space, the UFOs where gone.
