This craft was at the time large to me as I was only ten to twenty feet from it on the atlantic ocean shore in Kitty Hawk, NC

The UFO was very pronounced and it seemed to radiate a kind of message to me that I was not familiar with as I was a young man who had just graduated from Manteo High School in Manteo , NC. The human like figure that stood on a hovering platform came from the craft and I looked at this creature who looked like a male with a flat white colored (like aviation flight suit). His eyes were dark and I think now that they might have had a amber color to the center of the eye. It looked as if it were glowing and looking right through me. I was held in some type of state of awe and could not move (I remember that like it was yesterday). There were no tourists in the area that I liked to swim at as during this time of the post summer season it was secluded area and it was just not congested like the full summer season. If you would like to speak with me about this I would be very happy to do so. By the way...I felt invaded that day and very awe struck. This event has put alot of stress on me during the years. I was afraid that the authorities would class me as a "nut of some kind". I only wanted to keep it in my mind and memory and I did for many years.
