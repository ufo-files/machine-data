{nd} bright star like object seen hovering and then abruptly descending

I was just getting home at about 1030pm when I noticed a very bright white star-like object which I thought nothing of. As I casually walked towards my house I saw the object abruptly and with incredible speed descend to below the tree line and out of my sight. The object was approximately 2-4 miles away from me and left no trail and had no sound.
