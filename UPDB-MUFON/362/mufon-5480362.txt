{nd} It was a large circle with glowing lights in it

A friend and I were coming back from Fishing when we were headed back to the house from the pasture. We noticed a bright light or orb to the right of us we first thought it was a planet but it was moving slowly and made no noise. We decided to go to the house and grab a few binoculars to see if we could get more detail of the object when I looked at it I could see this round object with bright lights on it. I snapped a few photos but it seemed like it was far off we went back in the house for a bit and come back out to see it was gone all the sudden we looked down across the tree line and we saw the same light only this time it was close to the ground stayed there a little bit and then vanished.. It was my first time I have ever seen anything like it..
