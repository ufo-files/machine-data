Observation of a large craft floating across the city

I was standing in my apartment when I was living in Melbourne between 2004-2009, on the 7th floor located in East Melbourne facing Richmond looking south east across the city.
As I looked out I noticed a large, and I mean large craft slowly floating across the city skyline. I felt as though I was above the object. At first I was not sure if it was a plane, a blimp, or some other type of craft. I noticed immediately that it was not a plane, helicopter or any other type of flying craft like a balloon.
It resembled a craft I had seen reported flying in Mexico about 1-2 months prior.
It was large, about the size of a soccer field.
It slowly moved across the city skyline. It did not make a sound.
I observed it for about 3-5 mins.
I told my partner to come to the window and look at the object. I asked them if I was seeing things and if they could see what I was looking at. They said yes.
It was during winter so there was low clouds which meant the object became obscured after a couple of minutes.
I was amazed to observe such a large object floating across the skyline in the heart of the city.
After about 30 sec to a minute I realised I should try to record it on a phone or camera. I went to get my phone and/or camera but it was dark and it was hard to capture the image.
