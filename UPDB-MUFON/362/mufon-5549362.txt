Four to five objects above my yard

I was taking some sky pictures at 10:00AM this morning (03/06/2006) and got these two pictures with the same objects in them. I took twelve shots and these were the only two with any objects in them. The second picture (the one with the objects behind a tree branch) was taken less than a minute after the first one was taken. The camera was pointed in an easterly direction and the objects were moving in NW direction. In the past six months I have captured objects of this kind and also white round ones and also white mixed with other colors. With the enlargements there are a total of four pictures. If I am unable to post all four I will make another report with the other photo in it.
