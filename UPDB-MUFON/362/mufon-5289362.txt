Green Sphere Glowing

I was camping In Wipourous Creek Campground in 1997 in the fall when my dog and i were camping in the woods across from the campground by the river and noticed a green light from a distance of about a mile but i had no visible sight of what it was at that time and it looked to turn direction and get brighter as it came in my direction and when i watched the light come closer i could see a sphere like object hovering my way and it looked kinda cool so i watched it come closer and as it did it stopped right at the edge of the cliff on the other side of the river just on top of the trees it stayed there for approximately 30 to 40 secs and then it kinda went down but didn't touch the ground and it just disappeared kinda like when you turn off a light and it was gone and the weird thing was as it was approaching my dog was quite and after it went dark she started to growl but nothing appeared.
