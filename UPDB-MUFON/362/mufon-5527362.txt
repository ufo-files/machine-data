Saw triangle shaped ufo going slow across the sky from east to west,no sound

Was in my backyard at 7;45 pm and looked in the sky at the stars when I saw a triangled shaped object go slowly across the sky from east to west. It had a center light with two other lights on either side forming a triangle.It was not blinking like an airplane would be. There was no noise at all! It lasted for about 3 minutes. I was shocked and could not move,very surprised.
