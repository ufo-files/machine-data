Planetike, low in West sky, appeared and disappeared 2x in 30 minutes

Was walking dogs and it just turned dark. I had parked my van and noticed a bright lite in the West sky. Low just above treeline. It was far enough away I thought it may be a star, but too bright. It was also the only star out if thats what it was. I thought it odd it being so low on horizon yet first star out?
Was walking dogs away from it and after about ten minutes I turned around and it was gone. We walked around a corner of wooed area and after another 15 minutes we walked back around corner and there it was again. I watched for a while being puzzled. Looked up again it wad gone. Continued walking and it appears again. This time I watch continuously and after about two minutes it literally turns off like a light switch. That's the last I saw.
