{rjl} when camping woke up to pee in middle of night...round glowing object a short distance from me about 12 feet up moving slowly

Was about 10 yr old...camping near my home with buddy. We ate a watermelon from his dad's store and I woke up about midnite to [verbiage removed/cms/rjl]. Opened the tent flap and about 15 ft away, about 12 feet up, was a round glowing object (kind of golden white color) that moved north in front of our tent. I climbed back in the tent and tried to wake my buddy to no avail. I got under the covers and dont remember anything more.  Since then I have had a small hard lump on the pad of my right thumb that I never had before. No scar or anything. Im now 81 and its still there. Dont know if it had anything to do with the object but a coincidence for sure. Ever since I have been afraid to camp outside or be alone at night for some reason.
