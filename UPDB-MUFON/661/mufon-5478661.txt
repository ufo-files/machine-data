Object that moved between trees and descended towards me

Whilst locking up the garage at our property I was inside the garage in total blackness checking the doors when I had an intense feeling that I was being watched by something - somehow I knew that this object was above me. I left the garage and feeling very scared looked upwards and saw an oval shaped bright orange light with clear distinct edges hovering between the branches of a very large sycamore tree about 25 feet above me between 3 to 4 foot in length. I stood there amazed and the object started to slowly descend and approach me through the tree branches at which point I panicked and ran down a long path and two flights of steps in a record time into the house.I did tell my parents but they did not believe me and I did not report this or another sighting due to feeling ridiculed. I felt like the object was watching me and observing my reactions.
