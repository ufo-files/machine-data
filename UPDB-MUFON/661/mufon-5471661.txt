{cak} Flat round whitish object stationary I sky

I was in my driveway hoping to catch a glimpse of the dragon rocket which was being launched from Cape Canaveral. Upon searching the blue sky I saw small whitish disc high in the sky. It was very still and made no noise. I thought at first it was the rocket but it didn11t appear to move for nearly five minutes. I have never seen a planet in the morning. I called out to my husband who was in the garage to look at this. I turned my head for a second and when I looked back it was gone.
