{jpg} Bright star-like light that traversed the pre-dawn sky.

My wife and I were taking an early morning walk. There were only a few stars still visible in the eastern sky as the sun was rising. We noticed a bright light to the northwest about 30 degrees above the horizon and at first did not notice movement. Then we saw that it was moving towards us rather quickly. I took my phone out and began videoing it. It turned slightly as it got closer and also slowed down. It seamed to change directions so that it would pass directly over us. There were no flashing strobes to indicate that it was one of our aircraft and it made no sound at all. A third person came walking along and we pointed the object out to them. They were also amazed at what we where witnessing. We watched it as it made another slight change in direction and faded out of site in a southeastern direction.
