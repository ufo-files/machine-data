(GS) Bright round Star Moving in straight lines zig zagging but staying in the same relative location

I was lying in bed around 1 am EDT, when I noticed a really bright star. I saw it move and I thought it was a plane so I ignored it. I continued to look out the window unable to sleep when I realized I had been watching this darting object for atleast an hour. The bright star was moving in a zig zag pattern, but always staying in the same circular zone. I knew I was looking at a UFO but I wanted to be sure, so I would close one eye and watch the object and the behavior was the same. I then decided to try and photograph it as well as video record the object but the camera on my phone was not able to record at that distance. I have the videos that are black screens if they are of use some how.

I spent roughly 2-3 hours watching this object. It never left the pane of glass it was watching it through but it moved all over the pane of glass.

I didn't really have any emotions or feelings at the time.

I lost site of the object when I fell asleep I guess. I cant recall how it ended.
