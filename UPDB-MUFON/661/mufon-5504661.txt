{kt}4 silver to white reflective circular objects seen circling due N and then moving very fast to east intermittently appearing and disappearing in a flight pattern not seen for commercial aircraft

While unloading our car , Sharon noticed 4 white to silver reflective circular objects , not birds about 2-5 miles move then eastward. Pointed out the objects to her husband what she was seeing since both of us are experienced birders, they were not seagulls. They were very reflective to silver and moving silently very fast due east as the same altitude, 3 objects disappeared and reappeared catching light from the sun and disappearing NE in a flight pattern not used by commercial aircraft. We were unable to get binoculars out in time to delineate their shape but about 10 minutes later a prop single engine plane out of Thomasville was seen heading east which was about 500 ft and moving about 140 miles per hour also seen due N of us and appeared 3 times larger.
