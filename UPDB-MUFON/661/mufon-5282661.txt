Early years. From 7 - 10

Hello. My name is Dawn. I have never spoken of these events before, but feel now I’m 47 that I should. 
I moved when I was about 7 to a new house. Obviously I was nervous about my change of primary school etc, leaving my friends, but this was entirely different and I remember it to this day.
On the second night in my new home with my family I woke to see a being at the end of my bed. 
The being was always during the night. I don’t remember anything but that being in my bedroom doorway. It was always open.
After this I noticed a mark on my upper right arm. It’s still there to this day. I wasn’t born with it, and it bothers me. 
I would be very interested to know if this is prevalent for an alien encounter. 
Many thanks 
Dawn Bartle
