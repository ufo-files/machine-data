I quickly realized it was too large to be a star so I assumed it must be an airplane. It appeared not to be moving at all so I assumed it must be headed directly my direction lights pointed this way. I continued to watch for the next 15 minutes bewildered since it never got closer, just hovered in place. Took great pics. called my nephew to make sure I wasn't dreaming and he took additional pics from his place. The object/ light/ drone? didn't move an inch for over an hour.
