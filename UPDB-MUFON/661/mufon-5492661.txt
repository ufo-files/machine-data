{rjl} Cone shaped object with One very very very bright light and 2 white lights on the side

Driving to work on Imhoff Rd north bound. I saw a very very bright light in front of me heading West bound. It looked cone shaped with 2 blinking lights on the sides. Main light in middle was extremely Bright. I watched it as it crossed over me as I turned right on Slater Rd. no noise.... looked like it could have come from the Lummi Reservation. I lost sight of it as I went down Slater Rd and turned in to casino drive way. Lived in this Area all my  life. I know what a airplane looks like and this wasn't it.....
