White Triangle Sighting in Hampton Roads

I was driving on 664, towards Western Branch in Chesapeake, when I noticed that there was a white triangular object in the sky. It just stayed there. I also noticed that there was a biplane towing a sign that was nearby. Since those only fly around 1000 ft up, and this triangular object was higher than that, I knew that it was far up in the sky. I watched it for about 4 minutes, and was on the phone with my friend, telling him about what I was seeing. There was a bluish gray dot at its center. It didn't move, it didn't turn, it was just hovering there, and then, suddenly disappeared.
