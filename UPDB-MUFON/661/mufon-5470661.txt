{jpg} Large star looking object. Bright white light, northeast corner of the sky headed north. Slowly got dimmer. Then headed east and slowly faded away into a red dot.

Setting up my telescope I was looking in the Northeast Direction in the sky and noticed a white star looking object slowly cruising across the sky, headed north and it was the brightest star in the sky. Moving it is similar speed to an airplane. But it was more hovering than flying, as it picked up speed the brightness of it slowly started to dim and got smaller. Then the object turned East and slowly disappeared leaving a red dot to slowly end the visual. It was extremely exciting. I wish I would have had my telescope sight it in 5 minutes sooner. It was so cool.
