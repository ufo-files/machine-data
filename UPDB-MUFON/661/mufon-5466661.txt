{jpg} I often look out of a window high in the house where I usually observe the Moon, planes in flight, stars, weather, etc. This window is about 15 feet off of ground. On Sunday 8-5-18 I noticed an Object in the sky about 2 a.m. Hovering about 5k ft up

On August 5,2018 at 2 a.m. I looked out an east facing window in my home, I noticed an object high in the Sky that was lit-up, it was very Bright, I figured it must some kind of plane and went about my business. At 3:00 a.m. I looked out the window again, the Object was STILL there ! I thought WHAT could hover for an Hour ? I grabbed my binoculars and looked at it. I could see lots of Bright lights on this Object, I counted over 25 lights, best I could figure. The Object was hovering over the next town. I decided to call that cities Police Dept to see if anyone else had called it in. I told the officer that answered the phone my story, he said NO one had called in yet, it was 3:30 a.m.

The object remained until sometime after 4 a.m.( I fell asleep, it was daylight when I got up, the object was gone. I estimate that the Object was between 5k and 10K up in the air, maybe more. It was VERY bright , it moved left to right off & on. I have looked in the same location for the last 5 days hoping to see it again, nothing came up. It hovered for over 2-hours.

I will keep looking for it.
