{td} My girlfriend and I were cloud watching when we both spotted different shaped anomalies

My girlfriend and I were enjoying the cool weather, I was laying on my back on top of the well house near, and she was watering her rosebushes when I spotted what looked like a capital letter I or a stick bug about the height of the clouds moving slowly south. Without losing visual I told my girlfriend to look, she said I see it. At that second the "giant stick bug" disappeared. When we were talking about what we'd seen, we realized we saw two different things in different parts of the sky. Her description was of a white ball of light heading south-southeast that also disappeared shortly after first seeing it.
