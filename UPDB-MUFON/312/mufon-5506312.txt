{nd} never saw anything like it before. very bright white light

I was in SW Denver near Evans and Federal at familys house for Thanksgiving dinner when i went out back to have a cig and as i smoked i gazed up at the night sky and saw a abnormally bright light to the SW of Denver. At first i thought it had to be the spot light off of a helicopter because it was very bright. the brightest object in the sky for 2 hrs. but i continued to watch it for like 15 mins and it didn't appear to be moving or have any flashing lights just a solid white light. it just stood there like a HUGE bright star. Every 15 mins after my smoke i would go outside to see if the object was still there. it appeared to have moved very slowly towards the west on a straight horizontal path. until about 2 hrs had passed and i no longer observed the object in the sky. i pointed the object out to several family members and 1 even got a still photo.
