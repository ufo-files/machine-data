{nd} Very fast bright blue object with a trail behind it flew through the clouds and lit them up very brightly.

I was driving to work this morning at 4:45 am. And I saw a very bright blue object streak across the sky. I thought it was a meteorite at first but the color didn't fit. The trail behind it was the same color. It flew into the clouds and lit them up very bright. It lasted about 5 seconds. Cloud cover obscured it or maybe it burned up in the atmosphere. It was an awesome experience and I wish I could have gotten a video of it.
