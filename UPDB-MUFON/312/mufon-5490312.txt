observing perseid shower coming from the jorth when 5 or six objects flew in a straight line past my field of view

I was in my front garden at midnight facing north to watch thr perseids meteor shower, I saw 6 in a half hour which lasted for a second or two...but these 5 or 6 objects flew horizontly right across my field of vision for about 5 or 6 seconds, at first I thought seagulls, but there was no flapping of wings and the flight path was very straight, they were close together but not in any obvious formation, and they stayed in the same position without moving away or closer to each other. They were a dull grey in coluor, slightly boomarang in shape and seemed to be at a slow speed. They went out of view past the trees in my garden.
