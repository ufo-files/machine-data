Just got to my cottage, 30 feet above river two ufo's flew by me,

Summer 2011, got to my cottage about 10;00pm, Half hour later 2 ufo's flew down Muskoka river in front of my cottage. 30 feet above the river. Both were about 30 feet long with lights on the side. Lasted about 10 seconds before they were down the river and out of sight.
I could not believe what I saw, My wife and step daughter come back from thier cottage, two doors over and told them. I was so freaked out, and they knew it.
Not ever before and never to this day, ever seen anything.
