{cak} Silver disk near Hanford nuclear facility

My wife and I were outside behind the best western hotel, viewing Mt. Adams when we observed a silver disk come up from a large hillside to north of SunnySide Wa. The disc went straight up then went west at fast speed. The object flashed several times as the sun reflected of it as it flew west. There was no wind at the time and temperature was 49. The disc made no noise and left no trail.
