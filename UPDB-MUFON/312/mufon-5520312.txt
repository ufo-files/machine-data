Glowing orange line, with small colored lights under for a short time. Moving west slowly toward Kirksey Ky.

Going to the store at 3:50am north on Dorin Rd looking west I saw an orange line in the sky above the tree line, about 3/4 mile of a mile away. Appearing odd, it tilted slightly, and I saw small colored lights under the glowing orange line. I pulled over at the intersection of I94 & Dorin Rd & watched the continuous orange shape fade slightly and re-illuminate as it moved westerly across I94 toward Kiksey Ky., for less than a minute. Normally I have my cell phone, bot this time I unfortunately was charging it. It would of probably been appeared as an orange line in a sea of black.
