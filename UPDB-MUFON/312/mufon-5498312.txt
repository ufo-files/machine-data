{rjl} Ball of fire

I was about 2 miles south of the Rickenbacker cause way. Me and 3 other people spotted the event. A ball of fire descended at a high rate of speed towards the horizon. The best description i can give is that it looked like a rocket burn. The object went behind a cloud and lit it up, so I know it was really far way and really bright.
