{cak} Upper Atmosphere similar to a spotlight through fog, with the beam decreasing til object/light vanished.

While stargazing, watching satellites pass overhead, an object appeared high in the atmosphere that at first looked remarkably like a Very Bright COMET, a tail extending many times the diameter of the light/object. The Light grew brighter and appeared larger as the the "Comet Tail" Fog-beam shrank til it was barely a glowing cloud around the object. The Bright Light at the centre faded, as the fog cloud around it also faded til it was all gone, total Time around 2 minutes. I would be curious to know if any other Cairns residents noticed this event. I have seen a similar thing many years ago, the evening of the Space Shuttle COLUMBIA Disaster, many hours prior to the disaster my mother and I saw a series of Bright Lights with projected beams that looked like a convoy of craft entering the atmosphere, change direction and continue on over the horizon, looking just like regular satellites as they faded out of view.
