{cak} Boarding school movie night, and my sister wanted to meet a boy, walked across football field and it was right above us 40 feet round with different light

Billy jack was the movie that was playing at the boarding school gymnasium. My sister and I lived across the school with our mother and two little brothers. This happened around summer 1986-87. On the reservation of kinuchee az, we went to movie night and sat in the back,my sister already had a boyfriend at the time which two off spring we eventually made. My niece and nephew. But that night she met another guy and was the whole reason we were there. My sister was the oldest of the family,so. I always tagged along where ever she went. We left the gym halfway through the movie to meet up with the guy she met. We walked across the football field and that's when we looked up and saw this huge , same size as the field we were crossing right on top of us, bout 30-40 feet up. I saw different size of lights , no sound , my sister seam to care more bout the guy she wanted to see. We hurried to the cattle gaurd and as I look around it was gone. So as she and the boy met up he had a brace on his leg with crutches. He was with another guy and said "this is my bodyguard ". Don't know how much time pass when "bodyguard " said look at that ufo. I looked and saw two more smaller ones that was round and had three legs slowly going across the teacher housings. It had windows, but the bigger one was not around. The two smaller ones slowly moved till we didn't see it anymore. If there's a way to get more memories of that night out. I'm all for it!!
