5-6 brightly colored objects appeared to be in an aerial dog fight

In August of 1987, my husband and I lived in a duplex in rural Northern California. We had a friend over and we were watching tv while playing games. I turned in about 9pm and when I laid down on the bed, I looked out the window (no curtains) and saw a bright red orb in the sky. I got up and moved to the foot of my bed, where I sat and watched. After a couple minutes, I saw 2 more orbs, glowing blue in color. Without taking my eyes off the window, I called my husband into the room. He came in and the called our friend in. We watched for about 5 minutes, before opting to go out into the backyard for an unobstructed view. We laid out in the field behind the house for close to 30 minutes. The number of orbs increased from the initial 2, to between 5 or 6. It appeared that they were in an aerial dog fight. One red orb and one blue orb zoomed towards each other, before quickly altering course. There were also green orbs that appeared to be working in conjunction with the blue orbs. At one point, a red orb appeared to crash into a blue orb and there was a very bright explosion like light. The red orb changed trajectory and the blue orb was gone. After that, the activity died down and, we waited for a bit to see if they would come back. They never did. 

My husband passed in 1989. I have no idea how to reach my friend, but I've only shared this story with my family members. 

It might be of note, that this happened near Mt. Shasta, California.
