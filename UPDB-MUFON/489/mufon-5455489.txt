Subject: Not alone
I’m originally from New Mexico located very near the White Sands missile Range. All my life I have seen and felt things that I cannot explain. I thought I was going crazy I was told I was going crazy by family and others. Except when it came to the aircraft. Where I’m from it’s an every day event to see something. The biggest thing for me is when I saw a big huge aircraft right above my house probably maybe 100 feet during the day I am not the only witness. My grandfather worked at White Sands he also took me to White Sands in a place that I know now people are not allowed. I got to meet a very high-ranking general there when I was a child but I do remember being frightened and confused. So this is short sweet explanation of what I’ve been going through for us I’ve seen orbs I’ve seen the blue ones a lot lately and I’m not the only one. If someone’s in my house They see the orbs or big flashes of light. Thank you for your story because now I know I’m not alone and I’m not as frightened.
