{cak} I witnessed many sightings from march 9- march 15. And possibly tonight(march 16)

Driving. Bright light and change of direction and hovering. It was unusual and nothing like I have ever seen. During; concerned, and watched them for hours. After, Concerned, worried, afraid. They went off into different directions and up into the sky farther than the ye can see.
