(GS) Bright rhombus, brighter than street lamps and no stars visible, hovered then left downward trajectory

I was driving eastward after work at 904 pm up northeast Halsey street in Portland and observed a very bright, vivid object hovering far in the sky, there were no stars or planets visible and the light was far too large and bright to be a plane (also too far away). The light seemed to come to a point in all directions of the rhombus and it stayed still until it began to suddenly drop with a south left trajectory. Being completely shocked at the brightness of the light and it being the only visible light in the sky I kept my eyes on the light to observe its actions. I could see through the trees that the light did come to a hault after its descending. I lost sight of the light by traveling towards the light and then it began to descend.
