Summer evening -1966

On a summer evening in 1966 between Marfa, Texas and Alpine, Texas, three other people (now all deceased) and I witnessed a bright light on a hillside. (This light was as bright as a lit football field. With this illumination, the terrain was easily discernable,) When the light began to descend down the hill, we left. We did not look up. There were no telephone lines or houses in this area.
