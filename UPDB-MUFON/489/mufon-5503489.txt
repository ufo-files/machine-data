{cak} Bright Blue Object Fluttering around like a butterfly then ascending, stationary then take off, then stationary blinking behind clouds

My daughter and I went outside in the back yard to observe the Blood Moon Eclipse which was not ready for viewing so my daughter went into the house. I then I noticed a bright blue light moving around like a butterfly, I thought to myself helicopters don't move like that. I called for my daughter a 13 year old skeptic to come see it and she saw the same thing. A bright blue object fluttering around up and down back and forth then up behind the clouds blinking rapidly then a slow blink. I ran to get my fianc1 but he didn't want to come and see, he'd rather not know what's out there. I went back into the backyard and could still see the object faintly blinking behind the clouds. It was like a blink pause 1,2,3,4,5...blink and continued until it disappeared completely. We tried to take a pic with our phone but nothing showed up.
