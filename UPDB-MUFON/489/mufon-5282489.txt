Beam of fixed light with a craft that appeared at the end of the beam.

On 2/4/22, while boating in Alamitos Bay in Long Beach, CA, I witnessed a beam of light over the ocean that looked like a plane contrail but did not dissipate. I continued to watch this beam stay stationary for 8 minutes. The beam would appear and reappear in the same spot for the 8 minutes. Towards the end of the 8 minutes, the beam looked like it was shooting down towards the earth and then shot back up to the sky. At one point two different beams appeared but in the same position shooting up and down towards the earth at what looked like 45 degrees. I paused my video and zoomed in and noticed there is some sort of craft at the end of the beam but was not visible to the naked eye. This could not have been a plane because it was stationary. It moved so rapidly, it couldn't be a helicopter. Also, the beam of lights I witnessed appeared and reappeared, did not dissipate like a contrail.
