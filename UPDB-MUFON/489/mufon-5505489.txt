{nd} Bright light over Austin that rapidly changed direction, hovered, captured on iPhone video

A fellow teacher and I were finishing up lesson planning at Cherrywood Coffee in Austin Texas (near downtown just east of IH 35)and were standing by his car. We were talking and about to leave. I looked up to see a bright light rapidly approaching from the direction of downtown (SW). It looked just like the police helicopter with its spotlight. Just before the light got over us it abruptly stopped, changed direction, and began performing maneuvers at right angles, stopping, ascending, descending, etc. We watched it for a minute or so, then I got out my iPhone and captured it on video. I have posted the video to YouTube. At times before it moved, the light would brighten and then make a sudden directional change. Eventually, it descended in what appeared to be a controlled fall, darted to the south and we lost it behind some trees. We felt like it was a high performance drone.
