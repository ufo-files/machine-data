{rjl} I was taking a picture of a cool full circle rainbow around the sun. I later checked out the picture and realized the orb.

I was skiing at Camelback Mountain Resort. While riding the chairlift up to the top, I noticed that there was a full circle rainbow around the sun. Never seeing this before, I took a few shots and went about my day. 

When I got home, I checked the pictures and observed something that I did not notice before. There was a white orb. In one picture it is white, in another it is white and emitting a red glow and in another it did not appear at all.

I do not know if the object was in flashing, changing shapes or making noise. I was not looking for anything like that because I did not even notice it until later. By looking at the pictures, it seems like it is motion because it gets closer to the sun in a matter of seconds. 

I ski at Camelback a lot and never noticed anything like that before. Many people witnessed the rainbow but I may be the only one that witnessed the orb.
