Sky clear noticed a bright round white light falling straight down at a medium speed .

Waiting for a Yoga class a mile outside of town. In the building next to the Union Restaurant. There was another truck there. I was waiting for the instructor to come back from calling from the Union when I looked south east and saw a bright round white light high in the sky. The light started to come straight down at a medium speed. As soon as I saw it coming down I felt like I was going to throw up. Turned the truck on to see the time it was 7:00pm. It took approximately 2 minutes for it too come down where the Union roof blocked the light. The sky was clear and was looking out of the truck window at it.
