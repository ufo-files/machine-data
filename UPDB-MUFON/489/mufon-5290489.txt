Strobe lights came out of the sky and dipped down towards my car and then abruptly disappeared

As I was driving home from work, approx. date March 15, 2019, traveling West on I 694 a huge light shaped in a circular cylinder shape descended down on my car. The shape appeared as an incredibly bright strobe. The strobe effect was very fast. The light came in front of me from the West and dipped down towards my car, lighting up the sky. As the strobed blinked down, it dipped down and then suddenly arched up and disappeared. It all happened in a matter of seconds.
