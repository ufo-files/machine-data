{dr}Glowing above clouds

My girl friend and I (we are both in our 50's) were walking the dog as we usually do at 7PM like we do every night at this time. What we took notice of was a large area that was glowing above the clouds tword the northern sky at a 45 degree angle. The area that was glowing was at least a mile wide. At times there was a faint beam that seemed to be rotating around at 30 sec intervals. We both watched it for 5 mins and commented how strange it was. The sighting started at 7:20 and lasted till 7:25 when the whole area above the clouds dimmed and then went out completely. There was no sound at all, and I have never witnessed anything like that in the 15 yrs I have lived here. We drove down the road to see if there was anything that could have caused it, but we found nothing. This is a rural area Zoned R10 so the houses are far apart.
