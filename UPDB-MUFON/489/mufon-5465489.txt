{jpg} Orange saucer like object moving in a zig-zag formation in the night sky.

Me and my two other friends were going for a night walk on a dirt road in the country. I was near the town of Miles,TX. When we noticed a orange light coming from the east. Then we were overcome with a since of confusion until the light started moving in the sky. It looked as if it was coming toward us while moving in a zig-zag motion. As it got closer it seemed to increase up in speed. As we noticed that we turned in ran inside. The morning after the sights, we all woke up with either a bump, indent, or cut on the head.
