Two light round objects

My parents knew that I smoked but I never smoked in front of them, so I went on outside the house and sat on the front steps to have a cigarette. While sitting there, I then saw two white round objects. They seemed to be just above the tree top level and a few hundred yards away from me. They looked just like .two full Moons, perfect white circles. They moved very slowly and in a straight line and kept the same distance from each other. They didn't stop or move up or down at all, just kept slowly moving in a straight line There was no sound at all coming from them. I then heard the noise of an engine and looked back to where I first saw them. This is where it gets interesting. There was a plane following them. I remember thinking to myself how could it be flying so slow and not crash. The plane was so low and so close that I could easily read the writing on the plane, which said, US Air Force. The plane was really odd. It was a one prop plane, not much bigger than a Cessna plane and it looked like it was really old, something that you would see in a WW 2 movie.

I watched the Air Force plane follow the two moving moons until they all disappeared from my sight. Plattsburg Air Force base was only about 30 miles away and I figured the plane must have come from there.
