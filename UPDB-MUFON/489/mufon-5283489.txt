Triangle & TicTac

Initially I noticed a large translucent triangle object appeared to be slowing very rapidly at 60 degrees heading S just above the tree line. 

Then I grabbed my phone out and started recording. It started to hover and raise its elevation and rotate counterclockwise. 

The triangle had a invisible translucent field but at the corners it had steady lights almost like It was mimicking the star sky. 

It began traveling SE and as it got further on the nose light was visible before diapering. At 1min 52 sec I lost sight of the Triangular craft and the Tic Tac appeared.

The Tic Tac came from 150 degrees SW hovering. Then it was holding its position at about 120 degrees S. It moved as if it was protecting the Triangular craft backside and watching me. 

I was very scared my camera light was on the Tic Tac started scanning and I lost sight of the Triangular craft. I stopped recording and hid inside my garage. My heart was racing. 

I’ve seen both of these objects in my other reports before. I have a 2min 40sec video and screen shot images zoomed in from the video uploaded.
