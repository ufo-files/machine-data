{nd} Two different UFO's witnessed within 60 minutes in the same area - one white light and one triangle

My daughter was driving down our road on 2/28/14 after picking me up from work and as we got a couple of houses away from ours we spotted a triangle hovering above some homes that are on the next street. It hovered there for a minute or so as we parked the car. We sat there and watched it for another 30-60 seconds then we got out of the car and just then it started to move with no noise over our house. I tried to take a picture of it with my cell phone camera but I lost it over the house before I could get the shot. It was a silvery metallic color with "ridged" sides and three bright LED type lights on the corners on the bottom and a small white light near the back end. I had to run errands with my 2 sons which took about 1 hour and when we got home I looked up to see if I saw anything again just not believing what I had seen earlier again. I saw Sirius and under it was an equally gig and bright white light. I thought it was strange and couldn't figure out what the light was when suddenly the bottom light started to move to the left/south. It was just a bright white light but lower than Sirius was. Three of my adult children also witnessed these two events with me.
