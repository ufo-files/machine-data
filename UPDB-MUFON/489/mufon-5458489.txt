{rjl} Oval object acended from 20 deg elevation to about 80 degree elevation then changed direction to the south and blinked out

While rafting in Bayou Grande near the Pensacola Naval Air Station the witnesses were watching aircraft fly out of the base when they noticed a round/oval white object ascend rapidly to about 85 degrees. The object "blinked out for a few seconds during the climb.and then remaind visible for about 2 minutes before disappearing for good. Both witnesses were familiar with aircraft and said the object was faster then aircraft. The object made no sound.
