Unknown flying object over mountains between Bergen to Oslo Norway

On the afternoon of May the 6th 2017 whilst driving over the mountains from Bergen to Oslo Norway I noticed a white flying object which at first I thought was a plane but noticed I could not see any wings or engines or any markings or names It looked a solid white cigar shape. It was a very sunny bright day so I took a photo on my mobile phone and once I got home I looked at the photo and when I zoomed into the photo I noticed other strange anomolys. The whole experience lasted a couple of minutes and then I lost sight of the flying object.
