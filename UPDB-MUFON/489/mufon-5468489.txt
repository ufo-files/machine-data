7 lights in three groups flying se at 10pm over Quesnel BC on 17 May 2018

On the 17th of May, 2018 my wife and I were stargazing in Quesnel BC. At 1050pm we decided to go in to bed when I saw two bright lights flying SE. They were very close to each other and flying erratically as they switching positions and almost chasing each other.Ten seconds later we observed a tight triangle formation of three lights flying in the same SE direction. I had my binoculars out at that time and tried to identify the lights but I could not determine any wings and did not see any navigation lights, beacons, or hazard lights.I called the Regional Joint Operation Centre at CFB Esquimalt and they said there was no military exercise at that time.As I was talking to them I saw two more lights flying parallel the same direction as the others but farther apart.There was no sound from any of them.
