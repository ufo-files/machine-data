Strange object flies westward from atlantic ocean leaves green trail.

I was driving on Parkways south from work around exit 98. In plain visible sight below cloud cover a missile shaped object with green trail (afterburner?) behind it. Sighting lasted only a few seconds. Was listening to music streaming from cell phone internet. Music cut out during sighting. Right after sighting a few second long down pour of rain occurred. Could not follow object beyond tree line. Object was gone before I could take a picture.
