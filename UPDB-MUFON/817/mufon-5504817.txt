{cak} It was unbelievable that we were both witnessing these crafts.

Husband was putting out trash and looked up and saw two red lights about 1/2 mile apart from each other coming from the west towards him.His first thought was maybe it was Mars, but they were moving so he dismissed that thought. He yelled for me to come see and when I got outside one red light had turned north and took off and just disappeared. The second, going slower,was coming at us but also turned north and seemed to hover. Then it followed the path of the first one going north at a a slower speed. We lost sight of it as it got farther away. The lights were orange/red at first and then solid red. No pulsing or blinking. The craft appeared to be round. Husband is the biggest skeptic on earth, but admitted these were not airplanes or anything he could identify. I was very happy to finally witness this. I am a member of Mufon.
