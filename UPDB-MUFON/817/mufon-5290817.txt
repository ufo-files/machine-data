Next to Mars two stars one smaller other larger faded away.

It was close to 6 pm on Wednesday 12/2/2020. I was walking my dog in San Pedro, CA. Looking at the stars and I was looking at Mars. There were two stars close to the planet. I had not seen them before. I was about to use my app to see what they were. When I thought I seen the smaller one move. I thought it was a satellite. But when I just stared at it didn’t move. As soon as I turned away and looked back again it was gone. Then the bigger star (about size of Saturn) I saw it with my own eyes move and just fade away. Towards the ocean. It all was only a couple of minutes time. A few minutes laterI did see a satellite go by in the other direction.
