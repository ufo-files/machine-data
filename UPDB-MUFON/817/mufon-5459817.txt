{rjl} Sometime during the 1960's my brother and I saw a sauce shaped craft hover a short distance away.

Sometime during the 1960's 2 juveniles were exploring a vacant house. Upon leaving the house a large saucer shaped craft descended and hovered about 150' away. It was huge, about 300'-400' across, silver/grey, silent, with no markings. It had some windows where two humanoids were seen.
