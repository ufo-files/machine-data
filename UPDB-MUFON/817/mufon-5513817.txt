(GS) huge solid white light hovered over the mountain

It was early Sunday morning around 3:40 am. My son woke up hungry so I went upstairs to the kitchen to make him a bottle. When I turned around to go back downstairs a light that I have never seen caught my attention in the window. It has been raining here since Friday so it was incredibly foggy with very very limited visibility to the point that weather alerts had been issued. This was the ONLY light that was visible, it didn't blink or move, it was solid white, very bright, and very large. It appeared to be just over the mountain line, I grew up in this area so I know where things are and where they should be. I thought I was seeing things so I moved to another window to have a look. It still didn't move and the trees that shouldve obstructed my view did not. It was as if the light had followed me to the next room while staying in the same location. It was amazing, as for how I felt, it was a mixture of curiosity and fear. I felt as if I were drawn to the light, at the same time I knew that my son was more important. When I went back downstairs I tried to wake my husband to show him but he wouldnt come look so I went back to bed after feeding my son. That night I looked out the window to see what the light couldve been but there was nothing there and hasnt been since.
