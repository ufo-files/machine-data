Orange/Red Pulsating Light

I was in my front yard and observed a bright orange red pulsating light low on the horizon, but stationary. Many planes pass above my house on their way to Milwaukee. Their bright white lights with red or green wing tip lights are easy to identify. This was not. I was looking in a south easterly direction when viewing the light. I went inside and continued to observe it as it seemed to ascend higher in the sky until lost in the clouds.
