Silent rectangular craft flew over my neighborhood

I was stargazing from my kitchen window at around 2:30am when I saw this dark thing in the sky that looked like it was moving. It was far away and it was dark and it was hard to see though, it was basically just what looked like a black object against a patch of dark sky so it was hard to tell what exactly I was looking at. I kept looking and it seemed to be moving in my direction, not super fast but also not super slowly, at a constant speed. In just a matter of seconds it was close enough for me to be able to make out its shape / color / details / etc. It was basically a black (maybe dark gray?) rectangular craft with a row of dim red lights that went all the way around it, and although I1m not entirely sure I think it also had some windows. It was flying pretty low so I could very clearly see it wasn't a plane/helicopter or anything like that, and it also made no sound as far as I can remember.
