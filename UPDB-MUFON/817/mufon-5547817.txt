Cluster of 6 lights moving E to W, changing positions as they progressed

Witness,while walking home from her place of employment at 7:30 p.m., saw an airliner high overhead at what looked to be cruising altitude. Directly below the airliner she saw a group of what she considered to be six (6) white blinking lights at about 80 degrees up from the horizon, moving east to west/northwest in a somewhat rectangular formation, equidistant from one another, but with the lights changing positions as they all continued moving forward within the somewhat rectangular cluster/group/formation. Witness had the lights in view for approximately 25 seconds, and there was no sound. Witness' estimate of speed is described simply as "very fast." Her perception is that the lights were lower than the airliner, but not as low as a light airplane might fly.
