Multiple Glowing Orange Orbs

I just saw this at 8:45 in eastern Pennsylvania- there were approximately seven glowing orbs, all in a perfectly straight row, flying against the wind. We were just talking and then looked up and saw three in the sky. We continued to observe and saw others appear as if from no where. Then they just disappeared into a cloud. They were moving only﻿slightly slower than a plane, but there was no sound at all. They did not appear to be floating lanterns due to the strange flight pattern. My friend and I attempted to get closer to them, and the appeared the same the closer we got.
