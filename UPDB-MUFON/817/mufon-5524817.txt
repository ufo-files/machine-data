Observed 2 circle type objects appear from nowhere and travelled and gone in less than 5 seconds

On Sunday August 14 approx 1230-100am we were outside hoping to catch a glimps of a remainder of a meteor showers but did not see any. Then all of a sudden I noticed a circle type objects appear from nowhere moving across the skys and all of a sudden just disappear. This was wierd because the objects were moving from west to east and the area they were in was clear skys . I live in a area were its not uncommon to see planes and their lights but this was no plane and for sure no meteor that I have seen it did not leave a trail of any type. it was clearly two orangish color cicle objects at a high rate of spee... I yelled to my friend and pointed in the direction and when they were trying to see what I pointed at they disappeared in a clear sky. weird. I will never see something like this again.
