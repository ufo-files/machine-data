{dr}Strange retangualar Object landed/disapeared

I was driving home from work from Timmonsville towards Lamar on Route 403. There were trees just on the left side and none on the right. 3/4 mile further there was a section with trees in a 90 degree angle to the road. 
Suddenly I see this huge >300ft dull gray rectangular object go down behind the trees. I didn't see it come down all the way from the sky.
It was more like 300 ft above the tree section and then going down. The shape was kind of square, like a huge almost box like object.

My first impression was that a big passenger plane is about to crash, so I hit the gas to get to the "crash site". But when the object disappeared behind the trees and I passed the tree section, there was nothing there. On the other hand I was looking for an explosion and fire. 
I kind of passed by and suddenly I realized that this was no airplane, too large and didn't even looked like anything remotely close.
