4 cojoined lights, clear night sky, pulsating, hovering, 30~ mi distant, over great salt lake desert, viewed x2 w/binoculars, stationary 1.5+hr's

Saturday night, 9/24/2011 - Utah, Davis County, fringe of subdivision meets desert and the great salt lake, clear star-lit night, north-northwest, observed at what I felt was at least a distance of 25 to 30 miles either still over the lake or over the desert - depending upon distance, with binoculars, an event which lasted longer than 1.5 hours (gave up and went to bed), a stationary object at elevation of at least 1-2,000 ft (guess), noticed initially at approximately 9pm and viewed w/o movement until 10:30pm, at the distance noted it was about the size of an eraser on the end of a pencil, center white light appeared to glow which was steady surrounded by 3 alternating triangular tiped or three cornered red blinking lights which were pulsating; submitting report because I just read an independent report on another web site where events were reported for Nevada on Sat 9/24 (same date as our event) and in Utah on Fri 9/23; object remained stationary the entire 90 min's my wife and I viewed it, both using binoculars w/unobstructed view; undoubtedly in the area before we noticed it and after we stopped to go to bed.
