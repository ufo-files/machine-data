{nd} 4 pulsating orbs in a line headed from sw to ne the made a 90 dgree turn at the same spot and faded out

I was in the street and looked up. I saw a big bright orange pulsating light followed by three more at about 10 second intervals. When I first saw it I could tell it was not an aircraft, and when the others appeared I knew they were not aircraft either. I thought it was really exciting. They headed NE and were visible for about 2 min total. They would slow down and then made a 90 degree turn at the same spot and then they vanished one by one as they reached the same spot.
