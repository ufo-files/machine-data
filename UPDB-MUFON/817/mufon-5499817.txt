In July of 1978 while on summer vacation in Minnesota at the age of 13, myself brother 14,sister 16,two cousins age 16 and 17 and one of their friends a boy unknown age were out for the evening. After bowling at the local bowling alley we were on our way home. While driving in this rural area we noticed an object in the sky moving back and forth up and down. We pulled over near a open field and the object came down directly over us about 15 feet hovering over us at a slight tilt. Bright lights it happened very fast. I was in front of the truck with my cousin driving and my sister. It was very windy and my brother was in the back and yelled "lets get the bleep out of here." We were in shock and just stared. Then it left as quickly as it came down. We drove off and tried to follow it. We were screaming out the Windows to get attention UFO. When we arrived back at my cousins house we were so flustered and were all trying to tell the story to my parents and aunt and uncle. I am 49 years old and have told this story many times. We still talk about when we get ttogether. I'm watching History channel now and felt it was time to tell someone who really knows something about it.
