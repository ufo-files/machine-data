White bubble/balloons shaped object

Ok so I was just out the front of my house in Balga, Perth Western Australia, taking the cat out for a walk. Looked up and out the corner of my eye seen a white dot come shooting out from the side of the clouds and then just stop like it hit a wall. Ran inside to grab the NIKON P900 with full 2000mm zoom, and caught this on video. It's a bit shaky but it looks as if it's spinning around and changing shape. 
It was above the clouds. I ran back in to get the tripod and came out and it was gone.

PS the plane you can hear is a cesna that was flying behind me.
