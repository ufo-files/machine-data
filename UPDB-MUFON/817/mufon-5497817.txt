{rjl} Mettalic reflective fluttering object above Bunker Hill Monument, tracing Ursa Major

I was finishing up class work when I felt the sudden urge to look up meteors. I was reminded of the shower, and ten minutes later went out to see the Leonids peak at 2 am. I was looking at the Northeast horizon when a glimmering, silvery object fluttering around the Big Dipper, or Ursa Major, turned in a loop above the Bunker Hill Monument before disappearing behind a thin wisp of low lying clouds. The experience lasted no longer than ten seconds.
