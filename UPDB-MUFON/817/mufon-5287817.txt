Objects resembling plastic bag in upper atmosphere

While sitting in the backyard in the bright, cloudless afternoon (I did not note the time so it could have been anywhere from 2:30-4ish pm?) I noticed what I thought was a plastic bag that got blown way too high. I followed it's path but a tree blocked my view. I made both my partner and myself go into the front yard to look at it. At that time we realized it was two objects. That ruled out a rogue plastic bag in my mind. It's hard to say how large they were because as low as they ever got, they were still incredibly high. Both were white with no markings and a perceived fluidity of shape but generally round-ish. I'm not sure if they actually fluctuated shape or it just appeared that way when they moved/reflected sunlight differently. 
  Initially they seemed to be drifting aimlessly then, after 2-5 minutes, one shot off to the north east and we lost sight of it about a minute later. The other object meandered a bit more then began ascending. It kept going straight up until it was no longer visible in a sky with no clouds. The entire incident probably took between ten and fifteen minutes. It may be from the top secret military base Camp Peary, which is in that general direction from me and is close enough for us to hear test explosions and the evening bugle if the wind is right.
