Fast moving red object in the sky

I took these pictures in quick succession while I was waiting for my wife to complete a training session. It was Sunday morning and I noticed the subject because the sky was clear. Initially it was a red speck but it got bigger. I happened to have the camera because I was taking some pictures of my wife. The object moved from the right to left. it seemed to be descending. After I took the pictures I could not trace the object again.
