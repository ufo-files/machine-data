{tjd} Saw a grey oval with 2 lights inside it, followed by at least 125 evenly spaced non blinking lights.

I was outside looking for the stars. I always do this every morning. This particular morning as I stepped off my porch I looked straight up and focused on what I thought were two stars, but quickly noticed they were in an oval of a grayish color moving slowly towards the NE. I knew this was a craft of some sort. All I could say was "thank you!" I then noticed a line of "moving lights" following behind the oval that were evenly spaced at about 4-5 seconds apart and seeming to be part of all one spacecraft. I followed it out into the yard and counted 25 of the lights before running back and forth from the yard to the house 3 or 4 times thinking I should get my camera or wake up my roommate, but I didn't want to miss anything! 
The whole ship was shifting NE and the lights were coming out of the clouds at that point and I thought it may be over, but they kept coming, so I did run and get my room mate, who saw the last 25 or so. I saw a total of 125-150 of the lights. We watched until it was out of sight and disappeared. it appeared to be below the stars.  
I was elated, in awe, and grateful. I felt validated and "not crazy" for believing something more was out there. I was in shock for a few days and was having highly emotional reactions and fear of talking about it. 
A few days before while out at 2 am to look at the moon, I saw it was behind clouds and coming into an opening in the sky so I waited, but as the moon reached the "opening" where you would expect to see the bright moonlight, it instead went totally black. the moon then was once again behind clouds.
