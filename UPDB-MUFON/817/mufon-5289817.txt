Orange hovering light dissipated then reappeared behind us.

Driving north on S 60th and Grange about to turn West into my subdivision on Greenway and noticed the hovering orange light over s 60th and I estimate Edgerton. Bright orange light that did not have any blinking lights to indicate an aircraft. My daughter and I watched it in my car for about a minute and it slowly moved West about over Edgerton Ave. we chased it in the car, it quickly dissipated and 30 seconds later it reappeared behind us. It dissipated again and was gone. Shortly after a plane flew in that direction. Just south of me on College avenue is a flight path to General Mitchell airport, this sighting was in the opposite direction. Since I’m close to a flight path I see planes descending to land every day and night. This was not a plane or helicopter. This was a solid large glowing orange ball not consistent with a drone. There’s over a foot of snow depth on the ground so not likely someone flying a drone and it was dark. When I stopped to pull out my phone for a picture it disappeared.
