Five very bright disc-shaped objects maybe several hundred feet up. One shot bright firework-like lights down.

Arrived home from work, around 6:00 p.m. Got out of my car and looked up over across the street. Five disc-shaped objects up maybe several hundred feet hovering in the sky. One shot a firework-like display towards the ground. As I can see more precisely with my glasses on (although certainly well enough to see what I did without them!), I turned around to grab them out of my car (took maybe 4 seconds). When I looked back up, they were all gone. I was stunned, wondering what they were. My thoughts were along the line of "What the ??!!@@@ were those?!"; although, I was also thinking that they looked exactly like 5 ufo's hovering. Decided to go for a walk down the street. Saw what looked like one more again towards the west (towards the mountains...I live on the front range with the mountains to the west)...It was a disc-looking shape, all bright (It is totallly dark here by 6:00 now, since the time change), moving very slowly, and then disappeared. I couldn't really tell if it just disappeared or went behind a house. Continued walking for about 10 minutes, and then went back home and took a drive towards the west where there aren't many houses/obstacles for night-sky viewing; but, didn't see anything else. Very intiguing, mysterious incident. I wish there had been a neighbor outside.
