WALKING IN THE DESERT HUNTING WITH MY DAD

FILLED OUT AND FILED FROM A PAPER SENT IN TO MUFON HQ. BY FI AT MUFON HQ ON 08/06/2020. HAD TO MAKE UP DAY AND TIME ALL THE WITNESS PUT DOWN WAS 1958.( WITNESS PUT DOWN TWO WITNESSES BUT HIS FATHER HAS PASSED AWAY SO WE HAVE NO INFORMATION ABOUT HIM)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IN 1958 MY DAD AND I WERE HUNTING IN THE DESERT WHEN MY DAD SAID LOOK UP THERE IS THAT THING WE HAVE BEEN CHASING, THERE WAS A BIG SILVER CIGAR HOVERING ABOVE OVER HEADS W/PORT HOLE RUN DOWN THE SIDES OF IT. IT HOVERED THERE FOR A FEW MINUTES LEFT WITH THE BLINK OF A EYE. MY DAD WORK FOR CONVAIR AIRCRAFT CO, HE WAS THE HEAD OF QUALITY CONTROL. AT PALMDALE CA. WE LIVED AT LANCATER AT THE TIME. 3 DAYS LATER BROUGHT HOME PICTURES OF THIS CIGAR UFO THAT HIS TEST PILOTS TOOK, THEY WERE FLYING F10Z'S. HE SAID HIS PILOTS TOLD HIM THEY WOULD GET ABOUT 1 MILE FROM IT AND WOULD TAKE OFF THEN STOP AND WHEN THEY COUT UP WITH IT, IT WOULD TAKE OFF AGAIN. THEY HAD PICTURES OF 10 WHITE DOTS GOING INSIDE OF THE UFO. WHAT MY DAD DID WITH PICTURE I DONT KNOW.
