{jpg} Black Triangle Observed SW of my position. It moved to hover of me.

Driving home from work, I noticed an unusual light along my usual path. It was to my front right about 1.5 miles away. It looked like two bright white unblinking lights directly next to each other. It looked unusual and it started to hover in an usual flight pattern to slowly start maneuvering over my position. 

The road winds slightly, so it swung slowly to my right all the while descending. Then it stopped directly overhead my vehicle about 500ft above and I could clearly see the outline on such a clear night. 

It was a large black triangle craft with white lights on the back two corners and a red light in between the white lights. So that only the back edge of the triangle had lights in the sequence of white-red-white. They didn't flash but were constant. It looked like a sad mimic of proper aviation lights. The craft was a matte black color. I last saw it right above my car and since it had slowed down so much in order to hover over me, I slowed down as well. (The cars behind me had to go around me since I slowed down from about 65 to 25 miles an hour.) 

The triangle had slowed down so much that even at 25 miles, that it passed out of sight as I was craning my neck to see it directly above me through the windshield. I couldn't safely slow down any further. The craft was directly above me as viewed through my windshield.

Once I passed the craft, I couldn't see it on my rear view mirror. I didn't feel nervous, scared or any other emotion except a sense of curiosity. Wanting to know if it's a human-controlled craft or other-controlled craft. Interesting.
