{tjd} Looked airplane like but the light were not of an airplane that I have ever seen

Day 3: Another warm April night, 4/25/2019. It was about 10:25. I walked out the front door, directly north like the first night and as I stood on the front walk I saw a green lighted, non-blinking air craft, about the level of a plane coming into Spokane Int'l Airport, maybe higher and my son made it out to see this one. We watched it thinking this is not a plane and as we watched it fly from east to west in the north sky we saw a bright white star-like light but it seemed odd and bigger than a normal star. As the craft with green light flew close to it the white star-like light suddenly and in a split second grew a bit and then disappeared giving us the impression that it shot off into the unknown. I did get something on my phone camera that night.
