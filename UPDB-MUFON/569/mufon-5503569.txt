My husband and I witnessed 4 bright red spherical objects over the Park Blvd bridge in Indian Shores.

While driving east over the Park Blvd bridge my husband and I saw four bright red lights to the north slowly flying southward. They travelled as a group but independently, at slightly different speeds and spreading out then closer together. Their altitude was about 1000 feet or more and there was no sound and their speed seemed about the same as a small private plane. After they passed overhead one disappeared, then two more went out of view. All of a sudden the last one darted off at a high speed, faster then I have ever seen something fly.
