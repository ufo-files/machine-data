refrigerator shaped with tube like object from top also had what looked like 3or 4 measurement dails across the upper part

4 children playing in front yard of new subdivision, in area that was once and army base .we were playing on the front steps of house when i looked up and saw this object over head' there were no street lights back then mid 60s   THIS THING was about the size of a ge refrigerator and had dials that looks like those on a natural gas meter in those days   it floated over the power lines and over the front yard  when i asked if the other saw it they just looked up and said "yeh its a satalite from huntsville'   they went on playing and i went to the back yard to tell my parents but it was gone . it disappeared as i went thru th house. no one was upset the adults didn,t see anything and thought i was making up a story.   but ive always wonder what it was this was 1964 or65 summer
