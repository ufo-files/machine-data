(GS) Several lights in dipper formation sighted over western sky in Greenwood, Indiana, September 15, 2012

My husband, sister, mother, and I witnessed 9 lights in a strange dipper-shape formation over the western sky. They hovered for about 3 minutes, moved slowly, then disappeared one-by-one. We verified that a few of our neighbors also witnessed the lights, and we reported the event to police. We tried to take pictures, but our cell phones and i-pad would not work.
