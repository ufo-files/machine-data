(GS) UFO was glowing, hovered then took off and disappeared

I was standing outside my house staring at 3 stars in a perfect triangle towards the bottom of the western hemisphere when a very bright glowing orb appeared about the size of 2 stars combined, the orb was so bright the center had a glow around it. The orb hovered for about 4-5 seconds to the left of the triangle of stars then took off until it was on the complete opposite side of the earth in the lower eastern hemisphere,it only took the orb about 1-2 minutes to complete its path half way around the earth, it then dimmed flashed once and disappeared. After the orb disappeared I felt very uneasy and almost scared.
