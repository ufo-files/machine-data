Encountered A patch of fog and a very bright light in the road while driving. We start missing time

I was 18 years old and going to a dance with a girl. We are driving through southern Connecticut. We were in a long flat piece of land but the road wound through the trees. We suddenly came upon a patch of fog in the road and whites came on out of the fog as if a truck had just turned on it’s high beams lights..
We drove straight into the lights. When I knew that I was between the two headlights of the truck and there’s no way that I was not going to be hurt I could see the international Grill and the red paint worn away from some of the lettering. We then woke up in her parking lot of her apartment. We laid there for unknown reason and tried to make sense of what happened she was completely out of it. We called her parents and they came they were so upset with me they asked her what drug she had taken. I told her none
