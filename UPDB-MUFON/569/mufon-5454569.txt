{cak} Series of approximately 6 objects very high in the sky traveling from north to south, Colorado Springs

I was outside looking at how bright venus was in the northwestern sky when I noticed in the northern sky what appeared like a star traveling from north to south. Too large to be a satellite, definitely not an airplane, no blinking or colored lights... followed by another star coming up out from the horizon, traveling the same path. Every 10-20 seconds another star-like object came up from the same direction, following the same path. I witnessed this event over the period of several minutes, as did a witness who I met in our apartment complex parking lot. He was definitely just as amazed as I was. These objects appeared from the horizon, brightest at first, flying in the same path, then they would fade until directly overhead we would lose sight of them. I have been looking up in the sky since I was a little boy... not every night but definitely a weekly event where I would look up into the sky.... never have seen anything like it.  I would have taken a photo, but I'm sure my phone would not have captured it. These objects were as faint as small stars and way way up in the sky.  JDS
