{cak} Photo taken of lake and mt's found object on download to computer

Was camping at Clear Lake, WA, was taking a picture of the lake and the afternoon sunlight reflection on the lake. Did not see the orb with the naked eye but only after downloading it to the computer did I see the Org clearly. I was using an Olympus Digital camera. Took several pictures only shifting the focus slightly, had the Org show in all of the pictures. Being at a great distance form where I was when taking the picture, did not hear or feel anything just the later discovery of what my camera captured. It does not appear to have any type of symmetrical lines but rather gaseous in nature. I have taken other photo's from this location without the same result.
