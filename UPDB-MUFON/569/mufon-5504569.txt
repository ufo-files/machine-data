{kt} 7 large row of lights

observed a row of 7 large lights that dwarfed streetlights far closer than object, they first apeared around 8pm than go out about 30 to 45 minuites later, they were in a straight line,very large, yellow orange, when observed through a rifle scope, the center of each light appeared red, they were just over treetop level, and remained stationary, walk to left or right one more would appear on that side as one would disappear on the other side. i, friends, family and neigbors witnessed lights, i watched them almost every night for a few months, a few days went by not observing, looked, but they never returned, we couldnt form any opinion as to what they were, didnt appear to be a game field, rows of lights used in farming, nothing but woods for 7 to 10 miles or so across jordon hill rd nearest hyway.
