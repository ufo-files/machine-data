{tjd} Rectangular object was hovering 10 feet above road.

While traveling west near the intersection of College Parkway and Notre Dame Street the two witnesses noticed a rectangular shaped object to their right. It was dark, around 2130 hours, and they could not make out the color of the object. The object was estimated to be 10 feet above the roadway with a base of 15ft X 15 ft and a height of 30 feet. It had a row of very bright red circular lights around the lower portion of the object and a smaller white round light under the center of the object. The car they were in had been habitually stopping when brought to a stop; the engine would stop running and would have to be started again. After the sighting the engine has been performing normally. The driver got out of the car in order to see better and "talk to the E.T.s. At this time, the object went up a few feet, assumed the horizontal position and went very slowly to the east at treetop level climbing to about 40 degrees, then started turning toward the north, getting smaller as it went away and going out of sight beyond the trees.
