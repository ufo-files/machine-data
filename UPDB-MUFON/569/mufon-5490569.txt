{nd} three sightings from 1972 to 1987

In 1972 I was standing in the front of my parents house in the front yard. Across the field app. 1/2 miles were 2 ships they were hovering about 30ft off the ground.There was another ship on the east in of the lake 'hovering and appeard to be sitting on the surface of the lake. after about 30 seconds it moved back to the other ships and they formed a triangle then shot into the air at a tremendous speed. they were gone out of site in about 10 seconds. the ships were box shaped and glowed in a very bright color but was not putting out visible light the second time I saw one was around 1984. On that occasion I was arrowhead hunting on the south side of the lake(iforgot to tell you the name of the lake,it was sheafer lake on the south side of parkin,cross co. hwy 75 south)thiswas a solatairy ship, it was round,a goldish color, it had a bottom that looked like the underside of a mushroom .it came over the trees coming toward me when about  half out it reversd and went backwards back over the trees and that was all I saw of him .My third sighting was in the summer of 1987.Same lake same time of year,the middle of summer. I was standing in the front of the house ,when when I saw three ships ,just like the first time. they were doing everything just like the first ones.The only diferance were the ships the were almost round with more of an oval shaped they were gun metal gray. they had a had a belt of lights circling them. they had orange and gray .they looked like a lava lamp but it was circling in a horizontal direction. when the other ship came back to them,the lava stuff started changing .there was a flat face that appeared on the part of the ships pointing toward me then they did the same thing the first group. approxment times
