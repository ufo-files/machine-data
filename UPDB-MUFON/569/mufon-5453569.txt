Seen a Triangular shaped ufo

I was looking out at the sky last night to see the meteor shower at about 10:25, which i couldn't see, so i went and got my binoculars just to see if it would help. i was looking in the sky over GCHQ when i seen this triangle shape come into view. my heart was pounding as i knew this was a strange object. It was just pure luck i was looking in the right place, it was a dark silver colour, i could clearly see it was a triangle, flew from right to left of my view and was changing direction as i lost sight of it. The sighting lasted about 5-7 seconds.
It was a silent night, no sound from traffic ( air or road) no lights emitting from it and did not move like a plane of ours, too fast Just a dull silver triangle moving very fast.
iv always been a skeptic about this sort of things but actually seeing this with my own eyes has convinced me there is something out there.
I will be looking at the sky a lot more now, i have too.
