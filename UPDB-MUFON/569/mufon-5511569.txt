(GS) 3 lights, sparkling orange/gold that made a triangle shape. 1 light moved inside the triangle and back out again to the edge. consistent speed across the sky

I was looking for shooting stars when I noticed 3 lights in the shape of a triangle heading N NW toward me from S SE and then over head to the west. Object passed directly over Hughes High School and University of Cincinnati Law College at 500ft-1500ft up. Object at least 40 feet wide or 3 separate crafts. Within first 5-7 seconds of sight at 30 degrees from horizon, 1 of the 3 lights moved inside the triangle shape 2 or 3 times and back out again. Stayed a consistent shape after that as a triangle. Kept straight-line flight path and a consistent speed as it crossed over head and then out of view. 3 lights observed the entire time of 30-40 seconds, sparkling orange/gold. I pulled out my flashlight and signaled SOS strobe at 400 lumens and the object did not respond. At this time the object had already passed over head and I was flashing from behind the craft.
