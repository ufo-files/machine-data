Looked like a meteor but lasted too long to be a meteor.

I was sitting in a field with my Boyfriend and his family at night (fireworks were going to start in like 20 min) i looked at the sky and saw a fireball going accross the sky. it lasted a long time. They saw it, but no one else in the field did. I first thought it was a meteor but most times they don't last longer than 20 seconds. it lasted between 20 and 30 seconds. no tail behind it. it soon went out of sight.
