Noticed glowing orange ball, travelling fast, quickly jumped, then disappeared

As I went out onto my south facing balcony at 10:58pm, I noticed a large (what appeared to be dime-sized), bright, glowing orange ball (with a fuzzy aura) moving very fast from south to north west across the sky. There was a plane about 50 miles to the west of it travelling in the same direction (flying parallel to each other). The orange ball seemed to be travelling about 3-4 times the speed of the plane. After about 2 minutes, it made an ekg-like "blip", while at the same time, the orange ball light instantly got brighter, then faded, then got bright again, (all the while travelling at about the same rate of speed) , then within a second or two, sped up immensely, then shot forward and disappeared.
