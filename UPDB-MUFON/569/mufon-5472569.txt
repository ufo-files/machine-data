{jpg} My girlfriend and I were driving west and noticed 7 to 9 orbs in the sky, we pulled over to look at them in a neighborhood and at least three other people saw it with us. They seemed to disappear one by one and pulsated into nothing.

My girlfriend and I were driving west bound on highway 90 in Cleveland. We got on the highway from East 55th, as we approached the W. 41st/44th St exit my girlfriend noticed at least 7 orbs in the sky. We both had no explanation for what we were seeing. They didn't seem to be moving quickly, they appeared to have a lantern like behavior. I initially thought they might have been lanterns. They were moving though. We pulled off the highway at West 41st street and immediately the orbs were obstructed by trees and houses. We took a left onto a street and decided to stop once we could see them through the treeline and houses. As we watched them we could see the orbs moving through the treeline and houses, there were at least three people on the street who witnessed it as well. We could only see one or two orbs at a time at this point because of the houses and trees but they were definitely moving in a linear motion very smoothly and slowly. I actually witnessed one of them disappear. The orb would pulsate once it was beginning to disappear and eventually fizzled out into nothing. I, nor my girlfriend have never seen anything like this in our lives. We were very shocked and could not explain what we had seen.
