Flew silently directly overhead across entire sky in less than 10 seconds

I was on the UNCW campus laying down on on the fountain talking on the phone when i saw the ufo. It was a clear night with many stars and I saw a gigantic black and gray object move incredibly fast from behind me to far out in front of me till i couldnt see it anymore in less than 5 seconds. I was stunned at how fast it went and it was pretty low but still higher than nearby airplanes. It was the color of the sky with a gray outline and was moving in a straight line. I quickly ran back to my dorm.
