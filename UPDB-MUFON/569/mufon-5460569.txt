{tjd} Brilliant Orange-Red Ball moving E-W over Palm Springs about 8:45pm on Sunday, May 26th

Around 8:45 pm Sunday, May 26th a brilliant orange-red ball at least twice the size of a small aircraft in diameter was seen moving about 45 degrees (above treeline - below clouds) heading east to west. It sped up a little, then slowed down and then stopped and hovered for a few seconds before turning south and flying quickly away until it was out of sight. No sound could be heard. The light was round (like a ball) and a vivid, rich color between orange & red.
