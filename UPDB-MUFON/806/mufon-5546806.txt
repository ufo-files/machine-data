Motionless and bright.

As my mother was driving me home from dinner, we stopped at a traffic light. As we stopped, I looked out of my window I saw a triangle-shaped object at about 500 feet away. It was probably about 200 feet from end to end. The lights were a redish color and were located at each end of the triangle. At first I thought it was an airplane but it stood still and didn't flash it's red and blue lights. I saw this object for about 2 minutes until my mother turned at the light and I lost sight of it behind some trees. When I returned home, I had a slight headache and stomach ache for the rest of the day. I believe I was the only witness in the area to the event.
