{rjl} Oval shaped object that remained stationary for 10 minutes and was colored gold and translucent.Gradually disappearing.

I was at work at the Mobile Infirmary.I was outside at 5:30-5:45 pm eating my supper.I was alone and the table was facing North-NorthEast.As I was eating I looked up and there it was at about 45 degrees hovering.It was oval in shape and translucent with gold reflective color.At first I thought I could see through it.It remained in one position for 5 or so minutes.In the background flying very high were 2 jets with exhaust streams.I keep looking at the object and it very slowly began to move toward the east and disappear.At this point I felt strange in my body.I felt slightly drugged.I immediately got up after it was gone and told a close friend what I had witnessed.I am a professional by vocation and am not on any medication.
