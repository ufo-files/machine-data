{rjl} Saw hovering light in sky, took off so fast left a trail of light like The Enterprise.

I was leaning out of my bedroom window smoking a cigarette and looking at the sky. There weren't many stars to look at but this one bright light directly in front of me but 500-1000ft in the sky. I watch it hover and move dartingly left, right, up and down for maybe 30 seconds. For a few moments it did this until streaking away faster than any jet or plane known by the general public. I compare it to watching the end of the beginning credits to Star Trek. Just like the Enterprise, this small ball of light in the sky left a white trail that was gone in the blink of an eye. I WISH I'd had my phone out.. I have no idea what I thought it was when I first noticed, but that event changed my perceptions and life.
