Multiple Star like lights from slightly diff trajectories

Observed approximately 6 instances of high, star like moving lights about a minute apart. All came roughly from SW or W headed slightly NE. Their lights would dim as they came closer to where the big dipper is above our house at this time. The lights went out or disappeared as they entered the big dipper sky area. Two flashes of bright white light appeared from the big dipper area of the sky as the first starlike entity got closer to it. Three more came from a similar direction into the big dipper area and lights went out. The fifth starlike entity seemed to light up and radiate a strong white light around itself like a halo as it got closer to the big dipper area of the sky. We watched 2 more come by on same route and also disappear into the big dipper area of the sky.
