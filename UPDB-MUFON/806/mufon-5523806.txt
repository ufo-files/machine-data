Very Bright Light Almost like a welding spark. Then faded to a red orange

In the Backyard just looking up at the sky. I noticed a blue light about the size of a penny or dime from about 4 feet away. The object was briefly still the faded to an orange or redish before slowly moving across the sky at about 30, 000 feet. It went for about 3 minutes then It just vanished. I believe it was to slow to be a shooting star or a meter of somesort. The object just popped into the air then vanished a few minutes later.
