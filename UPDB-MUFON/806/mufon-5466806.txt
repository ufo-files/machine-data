{jpg} Star like shape that ascended into sky

Me and my sister heard a loud plane or helicopter so we stepped outside to look. Once outside we noticed 2 planes (or helicopters, not sure since there were bright street lights) one behind the other. The one in the back had red flashing lights. We followed them until they were out of sight in the NW direction. We looked a little further down to see a starlike shape but as I noticed it was moving upward (rocket like) I asked her if she was seeing it move up as well. The whole thing took about 40 seconds from the time I noticed the starlike shape until it disappeared into the sky. I thought maybe it was a rocket but could not find anything about a rocket launch for tonight.
