Standing on south-side curb of Elvira Ave (street runs west-east) facing north during fireworks. Observed green light similar in appearance to a planet or star on a clear night but somehow obviously not as distant - initially appeared to be 5 miles away but seeing a wider radius of reported observers on this site. Unsure of degrees from horizon - I am 6ft tall and light was spotted between and beyond powerlines running parallel to, and behind Elvira houses across street.
