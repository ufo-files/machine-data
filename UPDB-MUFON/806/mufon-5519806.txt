Bright lights in the sky over DC

Witnessed it the night before while in bed, bright white pulsating light that moved like a firefly in every direction hovering over DC. Spotted again the next night doing the same thing then disappeared. There were also 3 white stationary bluish lights of equal distance from each other nearby.
