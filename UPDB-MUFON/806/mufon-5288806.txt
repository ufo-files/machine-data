Saw disc shaped object pass over my house.

This morning at 11.00 am I noticed a disc shaped object pass over my house. It appeared to be grey in colour and travelling at a great speed. It was travelling from the west to the east in a cloudless blue sky. There were no other aircraft or objects to be seen. I managed to get a photo of the object as it passed overhead. This is becoming a regular event in the skies over Adelaide.
