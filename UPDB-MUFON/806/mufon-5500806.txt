{rjl} Looking north from Daniels park , we observed 4 lights changing direction.

On July 4th 2014 , around 9pm , as we were watching the fireworks from Daniels park (Highlands Ranch) , I was filming with my video camera , and all of a sudden I saw these 4 lights way out in the distance floating around and changing direction for about 2 minutes , I filmed the whole event . The news channel later said that there was a parachute show , and that the parachute jumpers had flares attached to there feet , but I just don't know if that is exactly what we observed , because these light were changing formation and such .
