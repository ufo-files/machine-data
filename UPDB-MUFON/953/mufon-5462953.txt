{jpg} Spherical object with multi-color lights hovering about 30-40ft above ground. No sound, movements were smooth and quick.

I was about 11 or 12 years old when my family (mother,father,2 brothers) were in our driveway heading to a little league basketball game. As we came to the end of our driveway we saw a spherical object surrounded by multi-colored lights hovering roughly 30-40ft above ground about 50ft in front of us. I still have no idea what the object was, but I know that it was not natural. The object silently hovered in front of us slowly rotating. Then the object quickly moved about 1/4 mile to our right, and hovered there for a few seconds. The object then quickly moved past us to our left and over the hill out of sight, and then a bright light and lights seeming to go in many different directions. Nobody in the car said anything during the sighting until after the object disappeared. There was no fear only amazement at what we had seen. When we arrived at the basketball game many other people were talking about sightings and things like vehicles shutting down. In the news paper the next day there was an article about UFO sightings up and down the east coast the night before. The article explained the sightings as a meteorite. I don't know what that thing was, but I know that it was no meteorite!
