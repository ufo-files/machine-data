Triangle plus mothership in the sky

We were told of these strange objects that were in the night sky. So we started to watch and first saw what appears to be triangles in the sky. They moved from west to east. Then a few nights later we observed what appears to be a number of triangles in the sky, attached and motionless. I am not sure at what we are seeing and would appreciate some input.
