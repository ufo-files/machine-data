Bright flashing lights of non-aerospace colors (purple, green, pink, etc).

Over the course of living in Bath with family from 1990-1993 objects were witnessed both very high in the sky that flashed unusual colors that are not used by typical aircraft such as planes/helicopters (pinks, oranges, reds, purples, etc.) These appeared as spherical flashing lights with no discernable form that moved at very rapid rate in comparison to stationary objects such as stars. These flashing lights were seen stationary and within seconds would speed off in in unusual trajectories. Similar lights were also seen at ground-level and were flashing behind trees,indicating that they were not in the sky. Nearby neighbors appeared to have alleged crop circles burned into their back yard lawn around the same timeframe the lights were occurring. These occurances were witnessed by myself and my aunt.
