{cak} Very high up. One circular object and one illuminated object moving back and forth.

Got up at 4:15 and went outside on my patio. Saw orange light in sky to NNE (appeared star like - this was far away). I knew Mars didn't belong there so I went in and got my binoculars. I saw a perfectly round, unlighted object above the orange light. The only way I can describe it is when you see the moon during the day; and if the moon could be described as basketball size, this was tennis ball size. The orange light was below this and moved from side to side. Comparing to a clock face the motion was back and forth between 5 and 7 o'clock (but not quite). Using the minute hand it would be comparable to 27 to 33 after the hour. I watched this for almost 10 minutes and nothing happened except for this back and forth motion. It's possible that this array was moving away from me to the north as I could no longer see the orange object without the binoculars, making it impossible to video. I had water boiling on the stove for coffee and went in to pull this off the stove. When I came back out I could no longer pick up the objects through my binoculars. This was kind of scary because it was so far away I don't think it was some weird Defense Department project.
