{jpg} I think I am being abducted in my sleep and left with no memory afterwards.

For the past two years I have been waking up with burn marks on my arms, and hands in the shape of what looks like letters, dots, and lines. I have found large strange shaped bruises on my body with absolutely no recollection of how it got there. I have seen flashes of lights in the sky above my home, and I hear high pitched humming noises the day before I wake up with these strange marks on my body. I don't remember being taken, and I haven't seen them face to face. I leave all of the lights on in my home and have trouble going to sleep because I feel like I am being watched. I don't know what is going on and I was hoping some one could help me.
