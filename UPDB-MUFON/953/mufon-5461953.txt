{cak} Stationary bright lights in diamond formation

I was driving down Grove on my way to work and I saw four stationary lights in the sky. There was no cloud coverage and no other stars in the sky It is a heavily populated area so stars can rarely be seen, and certainly not this big or bright. There were many people in the commute with me, so there were certainly other witnesses. The lights did not move, except the bottom light got slightly closer to the other three and then stopped. This was seen around the local elementary school.
