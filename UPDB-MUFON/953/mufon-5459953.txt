{tjd} Bright orange orbs.

On three separate occasions I have seen this. At first i thought it was a blood moon. It appears as a large reddish orange orb that moves very slowly across the sky before disappearing. The first time I saw it was about 8 months ago at about 2:45am. I went out for a smoke and saw a large light coming through the trees, I thought it was the blood moon. But I realised it wasn't that time and it couldn't be the moon. It moved slow dipping down behind the trees in the horizon. The second time was on fathers day. Again I saw a large light through the trees, this time it was white light like the moon. It slowly faded to orange as the object moved horizontally across the skyline. I woke my girlfriend and we both watched it for about 20 minutes before it just disappeared. I saw it again the next night in the same spot. The first sighting took place in Cool, and the second two took place in a very rural burrow in north California about 20 miles away from Cool. Always between 2-5am.
