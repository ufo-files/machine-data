I was 5-10 years old and lived in a house on the side of the hill, in Morenci. I was woken up at night with a bright light. I thought it was a light from Jesus. I was taken up in the light and it felt like I was flying. Don't remember seeing anything inside or where I went. Just remember being scared to death. I was little and didn't know about flying saucers, I thought it was Jesus taking me up. It happened many times. I was scared to go to sleep. My Mom finally moved me close to her room. It didn't stop until we moved away. It was happening almost every night. I had heart palpitations when I was little and I was scared to death. I think that was the reason my parent moved away from Morenci. It felt like it was every night, but probably not. I would get this feeling they were coming after me, they were coming after me...I knew they were coming and I was dreading going to sleep. Nothing we can do about this, now, but I just want this recorded before I die, in case any other little girls were also getting taken. One time I was something hovering over but I can't remember, now what it looked like. I was little and had my eyes closed tight the enter time. Remember feeling rushing air in my face and then I remember waking up in my bed, in the morning. Not really sure what time it was. I used to go to bed when it got dark, I was little and then wake up early in the morning. I was a teenager when we finally moved away. Can't tell you how many times it happened, but it never happened after we left.
