Walking the dog, watching stars. Noticed one moving oddly.

My wife and I were walking our dog and talking about how clear it was out tonight. We were looking at the stars for maybe 5 minutes or so, then I noticed one started moving in our direction. It looked like a flickering star, the light never went out but it did severly dim on several occasions. It moved faster than any airplanes i have need move and I grew up seeing the Blue Angels fly. As it flew it seems to zig-zag and occasionally speed up and slow down. After about 15 minutes of watching said event our dog whimpered so we looked away and when we looked back it was gone. While watching my stomach was anxious, not because I was watching something I can't explain, But because it felt like seeing an old friend or relative for the first time in years.
