{cak} Two pumpkin colored orbs.

Time was about 7:50pm. Two orange colored orbs traveling north while we were looking east at about 25 to 30 degrees above horizon. The two objects appeared to be different object yet traveled in formation at slighly different heights to one another. 

The objects reached some trees, which made viewing a bit more difficult. They then made a sudden turn away from us over Lake Michigan. We then lost view. 

There was no sound and they were about 6 blocks away from us.
