{nd} Bright red shing object flying and hovering in the sky.

While sitting on the porch of my house with my friend, we happen to notice a bright red shining object in the sky. The object was flying from north east to south west towards us. This object then came to a stop and was hovering there in the sky. At one point a plane was flying towards it, and the plane had to change its course to avoid this object. Once the plane change its course and avoided this object, the plane then changed its course again to get back onto its original course. After about 4-5 minutes of seeing this object, it then began moving left and right and them started moving back north east from its original way it came from and eventually lost sight of it.
