IT WAS MOVING IN PLACE LIKE SOMETHING OUT OF A MOVIE.IT MADE NO SOUND IT WAS JUST THERE INSIDE THE REDISH WHAT LOOKED LIKE ENERGY WAS LIGHTS OF DIFFRENT COLORS. I WAS DEVISTATED AND A BIT CURIOUS BECAUSE IN THE BOOK OF Ezekiel IT TALKS ABOUT THINGS LIKE THIS. IVE HEARD LOTS OF STORIES BUT NEVER REALLY SEE THIS.
