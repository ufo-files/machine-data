{nd} Red glowing ball descended quickly in the southern sky

I was in my driveway with my 8 year old son looking at the full moon through his telescope. I stood up and moved to my right facing the south and a red glowing ball descended quickly (about 5 seconds) from about 45 degrees above me to down behind the horizon of my neighbors house. It looked as though it could have fallen into the ocean. It was approximately 7pm and looked too big and too fast to be a firework or a lantern. I was curious enough to check this site to file a report.
