{cak} 2 bright lights appeared in the sky

Sitting on the porch I observed 2 bright lights appear in the ne sky. The lights were moving nw. The objects separated and continued nw. The objects brightened and moved closer together and then separated and then disappeared. Witnessed by myself and granddaughter at approximately 9:05 pm. We were both in awe of what we were seeing. At first I thought it may be a meteor but the object did not behave in a manor that would be associated with a meteor.There was no trail and both lights blinked out within seconds of each other.
