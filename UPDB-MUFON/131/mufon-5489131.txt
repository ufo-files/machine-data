BRIGHT WHITE CIRCLE ORB WITH PULSATING LIGHTS FULLY AROUND IT

I was on night duty doing security from 12am-8am five nights a week starting Sunday midnight turning Monday to Friday morning 8am when my week of shifts were done..I spent all my nights outside watching over the property so my eyes an ears open to what ever went on..On Sept, 23rd. 2010 after the last of the workers left for home i set the alarm an went an did my rounds before parking sitting an waiting an watching like always when i stood outside the security car to have a cigerate to observe the night sky full of stars ..full moon above facing south of me i noticed to my right westward a bright white glowing circle slowly heading my way eastward...As it got closer lights started pulsating from all around it making no noise as it went overhead heading east...Like i looked at the full moon acouple times quick to try an guess the size of it an to me i'd say alittle over 3 times biggers then the full moon but maybe thats because it was low..I did light a cigerate just asecond or two before i seen an by the time it went by me my cigerate was done without taking a puff of it..Had to take alook see if i still had my shoes on after that..the maintance man who cuts grass showed up pushing a lawnmower along the buildings an yelled out to him DID YOU SEE THAT but he said no an asked what ..He just laughed an said it was prolly a airplane ...NOWAY I SPENT YEARS OUTSIDE I NO THIS WAS NO AIRPLANE.
