Multiple balls of light

I was star gazing in front of my home like always and noticed a bright yellowish light moving across the sky south to north at 10:50 and disappear after that about ten minutes later another one,a very small white ball of light moving right above me very high in the sky moving south to north at a very fast speed until i lost sight of it. Later around 11:20 another very bright and large ball of white light closer than other ones moved south to north this one was different because the white light turned on and off for about four or five seconds each time,I pointed a laser at it and every time it shined brighter and brighter it blinked about five times and stopped blinking and i lost sight of it since i didn't know where exactly it went after that it didn't emit any more light but also moved south to north. I couldnt get my iphone to take a picture or record since it was to dark.
