I saw what looked like a ship

I was in my yard hanging with my husband and looked up at the sky, which I often do. I noticed something that appeared out of the norm. It was about 3 X’s bigger and brighter than the stars around and looked as though it was “doing something”. 

My husband who is NOT a believer, noticed it too! 20 minutes later we were completely jaw-dropped. This entity clearly looked like a ship and was beaming red lights from the bottom of it very sporadically. It was motionless in the sky and completely silent. Suddenly it just disappeared as though it cloaked itself. We were stunned
