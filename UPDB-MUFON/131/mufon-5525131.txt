The shape flew across the sky, faster than an airplane does but it was quite far away, I was just able to grab my phone for a picture as it went behind a cloud.

I was looking out of my window in my house when I saw the disk-like object flying through the sky, it was obviously not a plane and moved faster than one, but still took a while to move across the sky because of its distance. After much thought the only conclusion I can come to is that it was a UFO, we have no testing grounds near by and even planes are not that common. I caught a snap of it just before it went behind a cloud.
