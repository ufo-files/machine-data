Red light streaked across sky disappeared then reappeared

Wife was hanging out the washing late at night,she called me outside and we both saw a red light streak across the sky at very high speed way to fast for any conventional craft i am aware of....appear to stop dead in the sky and change colour to first green and then white...then turned back to a solid red and finally disappeared completely....reappeared several minutes later so i grabbed my camera and recorded a video the object was then flashing white and disappeared again....suddenly reappeared bright red and took of in a northerly direction.
