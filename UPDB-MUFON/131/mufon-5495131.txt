On February 28,2015 I came home for my lunch hour at 4 am not noticing anything different, I relaxed and had something to eat. After about 40 minutes I decided to head back to work. As I was locking my back door I happen to look up at the morning sky in the west and I saw three streaks of light.They varied in color and also in intensity. I thought to myself why would we have search lights in the sky at 4 am? I continued to look in Eastern sky to see if there was anything there and there was also three streaks there too.This was starting to freak me out now.Then perused to look in the Southern Sky and there was a single streak in the sky.The lights didn't move or strobe, they just hung there in the morning sky.It was strange though because you couldn't see where the light source started from.I took out my cell phone and captured the lights from the west and south, the one I took in the east didn't show up.I had to get back to work, so I estimate the time I viewed this exciting sight was about 20 minutes.I'm just glad I was able to capture what I saw February 28,2015.
