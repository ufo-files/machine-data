{rjl} Objects in formation.

About three weeks ago at around 5:30 am my wife and I counted for the first time 14 lights in single-file going from south to north. I thought it
was unusual to see so many in single file but figured it to be a coincidence of some satellites to be traveling together along the same orbit. My previous "record"
of satellite sightings was 5 in a single sitting, all going in different directions and at different altitudes.
This morning was different, in that I counted 35 (!!!) in single-file traveling from the northwest to the southeast!! I originally was by myself in the back yard, but
called to my wife to come have a look and she and I counted a total of 35 lights in single file!!
