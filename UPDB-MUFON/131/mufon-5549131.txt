Very large Bright and fast moving sphere, possibly a meteor

My mother and i were on our way back from a family members house, and we were about 2-3 minutes away from my house. Just as we were about to turn onto our street my mother and i saw a very bright ball, white in color moving very fast but it was silent. I wasnt able to judge the distance or size of the object. we were pretty startled. i have never seens something like that before. I have seen meteor showers, but nothing ever like this. Meteors i have seen before were very small and i was only able to see them for a split second, but thats what struck me about this object, it didn't appear to be moving as fast as the meteors i have seen before, but it was moving very fast, and it was clearly visible for about 5-8 seconds before it went past the horizon.
