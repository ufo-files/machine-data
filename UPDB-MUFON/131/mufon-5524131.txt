Witnessed ascent of strange object. It looked like a rocket had launched from Western Michigan.

I was driving west along Ford Ave. in Wyandotte, MI on the night of September 11, 2011. At approximately 8:30 pm, I witnessed an orange light ascend quickly from WSW, heading E toward Windsor, ON. It seemed to change color only once, from orange to red, then back to orange. The object did not resemble a plane. I pulled off to the side of the road and got out of my car after it had passed overhead. I turned around and watched as the object appeared to slowly hover about 80 degrees above the horizon, due east, then changed to a north/north-east heading. The sky was clear and the object appeared to be very high up, probably more than a few miles. It did not seem to make any sound and it still appeared to be increasing in altitude, like a rocket. I watched it for about another minute and a half until it disappeared. There was one other witness who rode by on a bicycle. She said she though it was a plane that was on fire.
