{rjl} bright yellow orb with red scanning type lights on leading edge dropping bright fireballs occasionally

At about 2100 hrs est over a period of 15 minutes, my brother and I observed five flights of two crafts approaching our neighborhood in Union Kentucky. Initially ,they were low at an altitude of about five hundred feet.The crafts were bright yellow with a leading edge of red lights panning back and forth as if scanning for something. Occasionally the crafts would drop bright balls of fire like emissions from the rear. As the crafts came overhead, they ascended rapidly to an altitude of about 10,000 and climbing. One of the crafts would then go dark revealing it to be black and circular in shape. The crafts made no noise at all as they continued to climb northbound out of sight. As a retired police officer and an airline pilot of seven years, I can say that these were definitely not like anything I have seen before. I also have an extensive background in military aircraft.
