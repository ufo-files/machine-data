{nd} Driving home saw a bright white light in the distance that only slightly moved before disappearing about 30 minutes later

I was driving home and saw a bright white light in the distance. At first I thought it was a plane but after observing it for a few minutes I knew that it was definitely not an airplane. I pulled into my driveway and my daughter and I decided to go out to the road to take a closer look. It was still in the same place as when I first saw it and it looked like it was pulsating. I took a few pictures with my phone then went inside to tell the rest of my family. My wife was the first to come out then my daughters came out and went to the road to look at it and take pictures. We went in and out of the house a few times within the 30 minutes that we saw it. During that time it moved slightly to the right and finally just faded away. At one time I was a field investigator for MUFON back in the 90's,so it really peaked my interest. We saw several planes in the area immediately after it disappeared. We are only about 20 miles from Tinker AFB. Most of my family believes that it was something other than a plane or comet.
