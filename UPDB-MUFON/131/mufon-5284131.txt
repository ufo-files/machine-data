While driving home from college, (University of Connecticut), I noticed two triangle shaped objects in the sky. They were far away and came closer. Upon coming closer as I was driving, this object, (harder to see in the video but I have attached an image below), looked to be about the size of a tennis court. It was flying a bit higher than the building, which must have been 5-6 stories high. When further away, the other object looked to be about the size of a golf ball. This object is easier to see and is present in the beginning and end of the video. It has flashing white lights that randomly were red. Both objects continued traveling past the buildings and criss-crossed in paths.
