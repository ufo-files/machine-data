{cak} small sphere shaped craft, with light or something glinting periodically.

We were at my girlfriends grandparents house and I was waiting in the car. As I do lots of times I was looking up into the sky for planes and helicopters to observe. Then in front of me I saw the sphere coming towards my location. I got out of the car so I wouldn't lose it as it flew overhead. At first from a distance I thought it might be a helicopter but it had no tail or rotors and didn't make any noise, at all. When my girlfriend came outside I gave her my sunglasses so she could see it, which she did. From the time it took her to hand me back the glasses it had vanished. It also glinted periodically in the sun.
