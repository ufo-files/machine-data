{nd} There were 3 different objects, 2 disc and the other not sure. And a cloud that appeared to look like a big spaceship as well.

I was leaving Farmer's Market, grocery store on September 29, 2014, at 6:38 in the evening. And I seen that there was a very beautiful sunset, which made me want to take a few pictures of it. I did not notice the objects at that time, until I observed the pictures later that evening. That is when I noticed that there were 3 objects, and also what appeared to look like a larger cloud in the shape of a spaceship. At in one of the pictures there is only 2 objects, the disc looking one was coming out of the sunset where there were clouds. On another picture that object was no longer in the clouds, and then there appeared to be a 3rd object which was near the cloud that looked like a spaceship shape. The pictures I took show them there and then gone all in the same time, 6:38 P.M. And the last picture I took the 3 objects were gone, but the cloud still remains and never changed shape. I finally decided at this time that I should seen this to Mufon. I still have the pictures I took on my cellphone, but I am not sure how to attach these pictures.
