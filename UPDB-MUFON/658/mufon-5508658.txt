{kt}Large Triangle Craft, no lights, no sound.

My wife an I were at an intersection heading home when a guy on a bike and a car were on the side median pointing into the sky by the gas station. We looked up and I saw what looked to be a stealth bomber at first but did not have the staggered tail. Just straight across with a slot in the back. You can tell it was huge by the outline and as many stars it covered while it was moving. We were all asking each other what that was. No one knew. Didnt make any sound and didn't have any lights. We talked about it all night until my wife got sick of hearing about it.
