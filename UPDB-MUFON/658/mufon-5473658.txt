{rjl} Lights in the sky

My husband was driving home and it was around about or shortly after that people spotted some unusual lights in the sky. My husband saw them and pulled over to the side of the road , pulled out his phonoe and started recording. There were other people pointing up at them. So my husband got home rushed upstairs and told me to look out the window. He said there were lights outside in the sky.. by the time I went to go see them it was to late as they continued south and were no longer visible from my window but my son went out front to look and they continued on south which confirmed what my husband told me that they were headed south. My husband is retired Airforce and is a history buff and loves the Military Aircraft. This was something he didn't know how to describe. The thing that made my husband first notice it is that there were other people pointing to the sky so that is when he stopped ... got out and also looked.
