Multiple Orbs visible at airshow, larger dark object with smaller reflective and fast moving orbs

I was attending the Edmonton Airshow at Villeneuve Airport on 23 Aug 2015. It was a clear day to the North and East with little bit cloud cover to the West. I was West of the control tower facing North to North East with the sun behind me. I took 1186 photos during the airshow. I did not see any orbs in the sky during the show. I used a Canon 550D with a EF-S 55-250mm lens. Being a crop sensor, the 250mm is equivalent to 400mm. My camera settings were 1/500th sec, F9 and ISO 100 and i shoot in RAW. I edit my photos In Adobe Lightroom. 

Once i started editing, i noticed some bright spots in the photos. Not dust and no dead pixels and no hot spots from the sun on the lens. These spots were not visible on every photo, they are infrequent, even when i took shots in the same direction. Most of my photos were taken in Burst Mode @3.7 photos per second to capture the most possible action of the airplanes.

After i noticed some of the bright spots in different areas of the photos in the same burst (time stamp shows 3-4 photos in the same second), i imported the photos into Photoshop as layers. When i superimposed the photos and used a fixed point as reference, i noticed that the bright orbs moved in an extremely fast manner across the sky in a straight line. Some other photos show the darker and bigger orb with the sun reflecting from the top and shadows at bottom, consistent with the conditions of the day. 

Photos are unedited in RAW.
