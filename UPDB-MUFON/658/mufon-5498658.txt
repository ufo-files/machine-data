Observed as star formation, really bright, realised they should not be there, then vanished slowly

Walking home with kids, picked up my son to look at the bright moon then realised two star like objects which looked like a part of a constellation, massively brighter than they should have been, pointed out to my wife and they slowly started to fade away to nothing. Thinking back they seemed a lot closer in the sky than light years away where the stars would have been. Really very very unnerving as we all watched them.
