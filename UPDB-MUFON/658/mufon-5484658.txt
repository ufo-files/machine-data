{nd} Unexpected Visitor

Just one half hour ago, I watched a aqua blue disk hover in the sky near my house. It moved just like UFOs are said to, like a leaf in the wind. And a few times it jumped forward in the sky. It returned twice, and on last sight it pulsed a bloom of aqua color. There was no sound and no lights except the blue pulse. At first, I thought it could just be a bright star, but no star ever glowed such a color or moved. Of course, I was excited. I look at the sky night and day hoping to see a UFO, and have many times in the past, this was the most beautiful by far.
