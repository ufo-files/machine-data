An oval-shaped object flew across the sky quickly.

One day, my classmates and I went to a basketball court after tutoring. We were standing in the middle of the court, chatting. Suddenly, an oval-shaped object flew across the sky quickly, producing a flash of a blue light. In a split second, the object went behind the buildings and no one knew what happened afterwards.
