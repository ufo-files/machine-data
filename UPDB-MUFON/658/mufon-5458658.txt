The craft was hovering over the top of a house about thirty feet in the air. There was a white been coming down to the house.

It was after midnight when I was taking my dog out to go to the bathroom. As I was walking In the parking lot, I looked up at a house about thirty feet in the air from the ground. About ten feet over the house. It had a white been coming from the craft to the top of the house. It was still hovering over the house for at least ten minutes while I was watching and letting my dog go to the bathroom. It was still there as I was going in to the apartment. Then two nights after that, I saw another craft over the top of another house about seventy five yards to the South east from the parking lot again. Same things just a different house.
