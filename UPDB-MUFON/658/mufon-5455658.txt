{rjl} Lights in night sky

Drones had been scanning area for a few days. I went outside after I heard a sound. After watching sky for a couple minutes and not seeing anything close, I noticed 2 moving objects high in the NW sky coming my direction. I ran into the house to get phone, when I got back outside and had camera (phone) operating, the 2 objects were still traveling from the NW. 
I attempted to record both objects as quickly as possible. 2nd video I couldn't see drone(?), the triangular object.
After the 2nd video I lost sight of either object after they were straight overhead.
Today is the 31st of January, and today is the first day I looked at the videos. I was a bit surprised to see the ring of lights...when I videoed them they looked like 2 moving lights with no definition to either. 
The front of my house faces east, I saw these when I was facing north while on my patio. After I lost them through the view if my phone I couldn't see them anywhere.
I know the videos aren't anywhere near quality, but if you freeze the frame and zoom it's a bit clearer. 
I don't know if you've heard on the news as of late, but the drone sightings in eastern colorado to eastern Nebraska were quite the story the first to middle of Jan. The drones are still flying at night in Elmwood, but I haven't been out to view, I can hear them since they are low flying. 
I did show my husband and I sent a copy to my son, but other sharing with you no one else will see these.
