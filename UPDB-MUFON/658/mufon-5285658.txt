2ND SIGHTING THIS FLIGHT PATH CYLINDER OBJECT NO WINGS

As I was pressure washing my 2 story home, i noticed a silver cylinder flying thru the air over my house, about the size a plane would look on a higher altitude flight path. This is the 2nd sighting of this type object on the same flight path over my house, only in the opposite direction this time. The first time, 3 of us observed 2 traveling in a line in a southward direction over my house.  There was no sound or emission and there were no wings. It was cylindrical and no observable windows. I ran to get my wife but it had disappeared before we could photograph it. I have a prior report with pictures - the object was the same only this time there was only one.
