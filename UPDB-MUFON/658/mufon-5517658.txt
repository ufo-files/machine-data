{ck} Red fireball shape appeared in the clear sky as we were looking south from our porch and continued north until out of view.

My wife and I were looking south at the night sky when we noticed a red/orange object coming our direction, heading north. We could tell right away that it wasnt a plane because of the round shape. My wife went in the house to get the binoculors to get a better look. When we viewed the object through the binoculors it appeared to be on fire with red flames. It was very fast moving and was out of our sight in 3 minutes or less going north toward the horizon.
