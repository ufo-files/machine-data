Sighted upon approach to O'Hare Airport

We had started our descent on approach to Chicago from Boise, Idaho around 1:00pm on Monday, May 10, 2021. I happened to be looking out my window (I'm assuming North facing) and saw an small egg-shaped object flying in the opposite direction. It was moving REALLY FAST, heading in the exact opposite direction that we were flying. Based on the size, I thought helicopter. However, there was no rotor, no tail. It appeared completely smooth and I saw no type of jet stream. It looked as though there was some red at the very front and the rest was white or grey in color. I have never seen anything like it and have no idea what it could have been. I just know I was in a plane and this thing flew by incredibly fast. Unfortunately, I was not filming at the time.
