Glowing sphere only visable through camera, with Ora like ring which followed camera

It started out as a bright light blue thread like glow in mid air. It was only visable through the camer . I then decided to record it. When I moved the camera away from it it followed the camera as if it wanted to be filmed. So I filmed it as it flew around in a oval pattern until it flew up towards the sun and just stopped in mid air once again where I filmed it for the last time.
