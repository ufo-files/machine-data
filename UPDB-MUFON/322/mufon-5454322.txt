Looked like satellite's

I had gone outside to have a vape, and I always look at the stars. The night was incredibly clear, and we have almost no light pollution, so star gazing is good!
I saw what I thought was a satellite in the vicinity of Orion's belt, traveling from SW to SE. Within seconds I saw a second light traveling parallel to it. I very quickly spotted a 3rd light filtering the first two, making s large reverse triangle (base at the front and point at the back). However the lights appeared to be independent, and I could see the stars in between them. Add they went overhead towards the SE, I noticed a 4th light coming in to join the last light from a Southerly direction. I again thought this was a satellite as it passed the last light and traveled in a Northerly direction. This last light suddenly changed course and followed the other 3 lights towards the East and I lost sight of them as they headed towards Plymouth. I'm convinced they were not satellite's or aircraft as there were no navigation lights. I have no idea what they were, and the 4th light changed course to follow the other 3.
