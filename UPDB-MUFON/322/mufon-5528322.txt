Flickering star-like object "skipping" across the night sky

I was walking towards my car which is parked on a hill in front of our house; we live out in the suburbs away from the bright city lights so you can usually see the night sky very clearly; so when I looked up at the stars (which I frequently do in the evenings) I noticed what appeared to be a flickering star that was moving in a rather strange manner; it seemed to "skip" across the sky from left to right and then stop for a brief few seconds; then resume "skipping" and then stop again for a few seconds; then resumed "skipping" and then stopped; then after sitting still for several more seconds, it completely blinked-out and vanished altogether. The entire episode took only about 2 minutes or so but it made the hairs on the back of my neck stand up; I don't know of any man-made aircraft or even spacecraft that can move like that. What I witnessed totally defies the laws of physics as I know them to be.
