{rjl} Bright light High in atmosphere- lasted 6 min then disappeared. It was triangle in shape from my binoculars

I was in my backyard in Monrovia , California Monday May 22 at 5:30 pm. I noticed a very bright light very high in the atmosphere that was not moving. It went dull then bright. I got my binoculars and couldn't believe what I saw. It was a triangle shaped "craft". It was too high in the atmosphere to be a plane. I was visible shaken from the sighting.
