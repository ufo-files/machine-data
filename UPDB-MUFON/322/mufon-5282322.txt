8 bright yellow orbs stationary in a V shape. Then ascended separately.

Sunday March 6th, 22 at 8:30P, I noted 8 bright yellow lights(orbs I believe) in a V shape in the sky below the Big Dipper. I was able to find my IPad and take a few photo’s and videos but after the V formation had dispersed. 
The orbs didn’t zip across the sky in a straight line. At least 3 orbs ascended from the NE, while a few orbs disappeared from site. 
It was the V shaped bright yellow lights that I didn’t capture on film(my phone was updating, thus had to scramble for my IPad camera) that was soooo interesting, not so much what you see on the photo’s and video, I felt like it the bright V was close enough to make me question if I was actually seeing what I was seeing. Starlink is in question, but I can’t seem to find any info showing a perfect hovering/stationary V, or the like. The silly drawing is because I needed to try and document what I had just witnessed before I retrieved my camera. Interesting to say the least. I’m a nurse, not an artist. LOL
