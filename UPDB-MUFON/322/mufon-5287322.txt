Husband and Wife witness Two UFOs chasing each other up and down in the sky, two more separate follow

My wife and I were exiting our garage in the evening and we were walking towards our house when we noticed these two lights (one red and one green) flying across the sky but up and down almost like a dolphin in water. They were chasing are one another - playfully. We were blown away. I have had several UFO sightings over the years as well as my wife and this was the first time that we had seen something so spectacular. We know what planes look like flying overhead and these lights were not your typical orbs, they were spinning and almost seemingly changing shape, gaining intensity and colors that were not ordinary - glowing. They didn't seem like craft, they seemed like they were alive. They were completely silent. They moved through the sky so fast - faster than any plane! This was literally one of the if not the most amazing experience I have ever had. We live in an area known as Monsterland that has had UFO sightings, Bigfoot and Orb sightings. This is in central Massachusetts. I have had several sightings over my house in the past 17 years living here.
