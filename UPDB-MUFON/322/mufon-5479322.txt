{jpg} very large sphere over or on the water over the Rio Grand river about 100 + feet in diameter.

My wife then my girl friend and her sister and my self were on our way home at about 2:00 am we were crossing the Rio Grand river when I told them did you see that I stopped the car backed up and there it was A huge sphere red in color on the water or just hovering above the water we observed id for a few minutes after that my wife and her sister became frightened, and said lets get out of here. A few days later my wife told me that she had seen the object again in the sky after dark. this was in the summer of 1975. Albuquerque New Mexico.
