V-Shape over St. Louis Arch

I was sitting in my hotel room, on the 23rd floor, looking out of the open balcony door at about 3:15am. Towards the St. Louis Arch, I saw a V-Shaped craft moving at a speed that was too slow for an airplane to remain in the air. I saw seven lights in an amber color in a V-Shape on the bottom of the craft as it flew over the arch at about 2,000 feet. It made absolutely no sound. It was heading south along the MIssissippi River, then vanished into the night. I continued to watch to see if I could see airplanes, I could see the strobes from the airplanes from a distance. The craft had no strobe lights.
