Saw multiple orbs on numerous occasions in surrounding sky.

Starting two and half months ago, I saw a bright light at the end of my street. Every night since I have seen one or more lights in the sky in multiple directions. I observed it with binoculars and take cell phone pictures and videos. The objects are orb like, generally white but occasionally pulsing brighter and changing colors. Objects made no noise.

On one occasion I heard two knocks on my front wall. My two cats raced to the window started growling. I observed a six foot tall entity of some sort glided across the yard twelve feet away. It went through my fence, across the street, through the neighbors fence, up to their house and set off the security light. It then went behind a trailer and was not seen again.
