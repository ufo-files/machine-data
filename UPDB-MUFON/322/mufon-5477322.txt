{rjl} Merchants/I75. 6 lights total (4 white, 1 red, 1 blue). Hovered until we drove under it, then flew away.

Driving North on I75. At approximately 11:15 we saw a bright white light that was very still. As we got closer, we saw two additional lights on either side, one red and one blue. At first we thought it was a plane, but it was too bright. Then we thought it was a tower, but it was above buildings and too low to possibly be a tower, so we pulled over onto Merchant Drive. As we drove under the UFO, the one white light was actually four smaller lights in a square shape (with the red and blue lights on either side). As we got closer, it slowly moved away, and then rapidly flew away. The light eventually vanished on the horizon. We thought it could have been a drone watching for traffic, but it sped away entirely too quickly. We were awe struck and confused.
