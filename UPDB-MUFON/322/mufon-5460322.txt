{jpg} Four bright orange lights in a diagonal formation

As I was driving home along Denim Dr in Dunn NC at approximately 2030 on March 26 2019 I witnessed four bright orange lights in a diagonal formation for about 45 seconds. There were no red/green/white navigation lights visible. It's my impression that this was a single object. I don't know if it had any sound as I was driving with my radio on and my windows up.

When I first saw it I thought it was a C-130 (which is a commonly seen aircraft in this area) that was in the process of banking but after I lost sight of it for approximately 20 seconds due to trees they disappeared. I initially thought it was a C-130 because its size was comparable to one at the range I thought it was, slightly less than a mile. There's no way that a large cargo aircraft could have completed the turn and flew beyond my sight range in the short amount of time that I had lost sight of it. I continued to look for it in the area of the sky I'd last seen it as I was driving home but I never saw it or another aircraft.

Prior to the sighting I had noticed an unusual amount of aircraft flying but I couldn't tell if they were civilian or military (my guess is military due to the amount of them, their low altitude, and the fact that we don't get much low level civilian air traffic at night).
