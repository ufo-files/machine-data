{cak} white orb like object, saw with my son then disappeared

Out in my backyard to let the dogs out, and i was teasing my son and then i saw the object, i pointed to it and asked my son if he could see it, he said yes and i teased him that it was probably a ufo with aliens in it. He laughed but we noticed it did not move like a airplane nor did it have the lighting of a plane, it was solid white. We both thought it was a airplane at first and i asked him what he thought it was, he said he could not tell we watched it for about 30 seconds more and then it just blinked out, i grabbed his hand and he said he was scared and he was shaking. I got a little nervous, then we noticed a couple of airplanes so we watched them to see what they were going to do, but they did not seem to noticed the object. My son said he wanted to go in so i took him and the dogs in. Told my wife what we saw. I was in the military for 10 years and worked around aircraft all types, fighters, training aircraft, and saw them eject chaff and flare several times, this was nothing like that. Nor was it a chinese lantern. I do not know what it was, but id like to know. The object just disappeared. Wierd. My son said he saw a red and a blue dot right after it disappeared, however i did not see them. We could see jupiter, pleades, and several other stars, however this was not a star, nor was it a meteorite or meteor. First time i have ever seen this type of object. I do live right near a AFB, less than three miles away, they normally have C-130's and TDY planes, i also live less than 10 miles from Melbourne internatioal airport and we often see cargo and smaller passenger planes.
