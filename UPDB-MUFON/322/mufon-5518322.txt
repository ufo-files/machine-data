Leaving my house for my evening walh at about 8:35 I happened to look South West across the valley at the view and noticed a white spherical looking light. It was quite an unusual or out of place object that I trained my eyes on it. The light appeared to hover or move extremely slowly toward rhe east at an altitude I estimated as approximately 3,000 feet. After 30 or 40 seconds it began to perceptably increase its velocity toward the east, Still very slow but oviously moving. At this point it blinked out completely once, then reapeared, then blinked again, and again, appearing smaller each time until it completely disappeared.
