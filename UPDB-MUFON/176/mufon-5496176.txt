{nd} Bright orange-red starlike lights on CA coast.

The power was out over entire town due to storm. PGE had a few super bright lights, oddly some making sweeping motions skyward. Went outside to get some air, and saw 2 bright orange lights on the horizon. They moved upward in unison. Suddenly a third star-like object appeared next to them, but it was quite a bit larger than the original two. The hovered for a moment, then seemed to take off toward space, giving the visual impression of moving away from the earth...like a reverse shooting star. I Had a pretty decent telescope at the time, so I brought it out. Two more objects appeared about 3 mins. later. Watched them through the scope...appeared as super bright orange lights. They vanished the same way as the first 3, but this time an Airplane approached lights, didn't stop and passed under before they took off. I was with a friend and my husband.
