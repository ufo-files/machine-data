2 orange lights high in the sky size of stars moving closer together and then vanished.

While driving with a passenger my vehicle, at around 2245hrs travelling westbound, I noticed 2 orange lights that stood out to me high in the sky and they were moving closer together. I did not observe any flashing strobes or red/white lights indicating a plane. My passenger observed this as well. I pulled over the vehicle so we could safely watch from a stationary position. The lights were to the SW from our position. One of the lights seemed to be moving closer to the other and then they simply vanished without a trace. We waited for 5 more minutes and when they did not reappear we left and carried on our way. The lights were the size of the stars but they were orange and they were moving towards each other. They did not appear to be planes or planets due to the nature of their colour an movement.
