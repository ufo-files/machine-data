{cak} Icy blue glowing sphere with a white steam or smoke radiating from it.

I was having a light night talk in a fairly open soccer field with a friend at about 1:00 AM. I'm facing the tree line when to the right of my eye I watched a very quick, fairly large, glowing blue sphere fly downwards at about 30 degrees towards the ground before curving upwards within an extremely tight radius. Almost instantly, like instantly after it made the curve upwards it was like a light switched off inside and it was gone. The sphere was a very strong and very pretty blue. It was incredibly bright and appeared to light up the white steam or smoke that was coming off of it. Its almost comparable to the way dry ice smokes.
