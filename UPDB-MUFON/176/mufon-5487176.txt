{nd} I don't believe it was a craft. It was either coincidence that it kept appearing near my car, or it was some form of intelligence.

Date and Time: November 12, 2015. Some time between 11:30pm and 12:15am at night.
Location: Texas, Brown County. Three separate locations on Hwy 183. I was driving.
	First appearance: At roughly the following coordinates, 31.8792102,-98.918499
	Second appearance: I was still startled by what I had seen so I wasn't really paying attention to exactly where I was. But it was somewhere between the coordinates listed above and the coordinates of the third appearance.
	Third appearance: At roughly the following coordinates, 31.9639032,-98.920559
	What I saw: Two objects, possibly one, above the road. One long, oval shaped object with a shorter oval shaped object directly below it. The reason I say 'possibly one' object is because each time I saw it, it was identical in shape (though not in size) to the time before. Which leads me to consider the possibility that what I saw was a singular object with two oval shapes or projections that may have simply been more obvious than the remainder of the object which I may not have been able to see.
	On the first appearance, the object about 20 feet away from me. It was directly above the road. From the top of the larger oval shape, to the bottom of the smaller oval shape it was possibly 10 feet long. It was stationary as I drove directly under it. If I had got out of the car and approached it I might have been able to just reach the bottom of it and touch it. However, when I turned back to see what it was, the object(s) had gone. The first time I saw it, the object(s) was not particularly bright. That is, it did not seem to shine its own light, but was well illuminated by my headlights. There was another car behind me who had to have seen it as well.
	Oddly, I hardly remember the second appearance. I only remember that I saw it and it looked was smaller. As I sit here writing this, I'm trying to remember, but I just can't.
	On the third appearance the object(s) was to my left, about 5 feet from the ground and about 10 feet away. This time it seemed about 15 feet tall from top to bottom and seemed to be brightly illuminated from within. I went back to try to get a better look, but again, the object(s) had gone. During this drive I was traveling at about 55mph. When I turned off of Hwy 183 onto the road that leads to my house, I drove at about 30mph, hoping that I would see the object again and be able to stop before I passed it, but the object(s) did not reappear.
