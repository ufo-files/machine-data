A line of greater tnan 30 points of light moving from NW to SE

My 40 yr old son was on our front porch around 2115 hrs and saw a huge amount of the lights {over 100) and he called his mother outside to take a look. She came back in to get me up and when I got outside I first thought that they were looking at Venus in the western sky. Then I saw a line of lights that were moving just to the north side of Venus. They were heading to the Southeast toward Brooksville, FL. I observed approximately 30 of the objects. No noise, no flashing lights. I am an amateur Astronomer, so I'm used to looking at distant objects in the night sky and these were extremely high. I would guess well into outer space. I've never seen anything like this before.
