{cak} Flashing object in eastern sky

I was coming home from work around midnight 30 on Friday 8 Nov 19. As I was driving down the residential road in my neighborhood a flashing object caught my eye in the sky. I waited to get in my driveway to roll my window down and watch. After watching for a few minutes to distinguish whether it could be a satellite or aircraft, I decided to film it. It was flashing/pulsating red,green, blue, white, sequentially. It was not a strobe light, or the stbd and port lights on an aircraft. It was stationary and did not move its position. After about 5 minutes I decided it was enough and went inside. Prior to going to bed , around 0130, I went outside and did not see it any where in the sky. Weird.
