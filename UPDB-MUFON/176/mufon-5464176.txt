Stationary Bright light and later had bright red beam bellow it

My Mon and Brother saw this sighting and wish not to be named . They called me ( I am an active MUFON Member ) during the sighting and I told them to get some photos and video with their cell phone . This was seen by the both of them for over an hour . From the 5th floor of their condo they saw a very bright light huvering or stationary over the tree tops . This was seen just outside of their living room sliding door . This must have been seen by many as it was very bright and in a very populated area . Near the end of the sighting my Mon saw a bright red beam rectangular in shape bellow the bright white light/Orb and it was there for 30 mins or so . My brother sent me the photos and short video he made . The next day me and my wife did a bit of investigation in the approximate area of the bright light to see if it could have been just some bright construction lights . ( they have lived in the same condo for many years and had never seen such a bright light ever ) I made a short video with the photos and video provided along with my short investigation ( I am a regular guy and not a feild investigator ) Some of the photos I have zoomed in for more detail and the video recorded by my brother is the original untouched except the aspect ratio . The video says it all . Thank Tibor Gara a member of MUFON .
