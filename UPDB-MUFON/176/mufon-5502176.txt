{dr}Dancing star like objects, satellites don't do that. I was an Army satellite field engineer.

A friend texted me after 11pm from another state he was visiting and said some stars were dancing, following and moving strangely. So I went outside with my son and after a few minutes we spotted a star like object then two moving strangely in the sky. Then one was left and zipped around in sort bursts in all various directions. Sometimes moving  large distances then returning back to a position. I was an Army satellite field engineer and have never seen satellites move in such a manner. Cannot say what was moving up there, but was not normal. At times the color and shape appears to change a little bit. Wow.
