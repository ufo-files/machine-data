10-12 red lights in a row, no sound, flying in a straight line, all lights were right to left in a perfect line

I was sitting at Sifton Bog Natural Area located on Oxford Street West, in London, Ontario, Canada. I sit there at night and stargaze and meditate. I was looking up at the night sky approximately at 9:45 PM this evening and observed 10-12 red lights that were perfectly in a line, no sound as the pass overhead. The lights appeared solid with the occassional blinking left to right. I watched as the flew from Southwest to Southeast and then disappeared over the treeline. I stayed for a few moments to see if they would return or if it was from an outside source but didn't see them again. I got freaked out and left and came home as fast as possible.
