Bright moving lights, flashed then dimmed.

I was just outside. I have been watching lots of activity lately but most of it can be explained as planets or plains. Once in a while I see something really interesting. Tonight was one of those nights. I was just outside admiring the sky and listening to a concert in the distance. When my eyes fell on a star-like object that moved slowly from my left or westerly, to my right. Then suddenly as a plane passed near-by (probably at a different altitude) it flashed a bright white light and just sort of "powered up" as I like to say. It satyed bright for a few moments then went dim. It didn't flash off like someone turned off a light but rather dimmed slowly like a candle. It moved until it reach the Cassiopeia constellation and stopped. It hung there like an ordinary star. THEN from the same area where the I first noticed the other one, a new flash appeared. It flashed on quickly then off again. It moved at a different angle, slowly, toward the southwest. It just kept going until I couldn't follow it anymore. I was pretty excited and just had to come in and report it. There has been a lot more activity lately but as I said before this was really unique.
