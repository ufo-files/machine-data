Blurry white light

At approximately 11:25pm on June 11th, I went outside to my front yard to get some fresh air and look at the stars. A few bright stars and constellations caught my attention. While I was looking at the Big Dipper, I noticed this very faint light blinking, in irregular patterns. It would be lit and then disappear for a moment/go dark and reappear a second later. It was so faint that at times I thought I lost it. It was traveling from West to East at a quick pace—not lightning fast—but quite noticeable. The weird thing was that it didn’t travel forward in a straight path. I can only describe it as “curly cue”. It would make little half circular movements, wobbly like, the whole way. It looked like a very dim star that was moving. I know there was a piece of space junk—an old Russian satellite—that was also viewable—but that was traveling a different path than what I saw. There was no noise. I lost visual when it went E/NE, in the lights of the neighborhood. A few minutes after I noticed a helicopter traveling in the same direction, but it’s lights were green and white. I assumed it was a medical or news chopper, as we are in that flight path. I’m definitely not certain this was a UFO; but it did cause me to pause and wonder what it was.
