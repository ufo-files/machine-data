{nd} a ball of light that looked like the sun in color about 25ft in size

Well I was stepping out of the house to smoke because I didn't want my parents to catch me and a few steps out I heard the swooshing of helicopter blades and first saw a apache like helicopter then two Huey like helicopters then this ball of light the size of the helicopters and then two more Huey like helicopters with another apache helicopter and off to the side at a different altitude a shanook like helicopter in a supervisor like position following. During the whole thing I was in disbelief I lost sight because of the trees. I could see that the object produced it's own light because it shown on the helicopters and the tree tops with the same color as the object. Then my little brother called out my name and all I could say was did u see that. He said yes what was it I said I don't know maybe a ufo. Then after that the whole family seen strange lights hovering on a large hill not far from our home. Kinda like how the lights behave at that black mailbox I see in tv programs.
