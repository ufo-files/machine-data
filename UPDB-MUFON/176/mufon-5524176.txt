It was around 10 at night. We were inside and saw a giant fireball in the sky. We ran outside and saw it flicker. We saw it for about two minutes. It flew in a straight path and was very quiet. It was low enough to be a plane, but if it was we would have heard something. All I know is that I have never seen anything like it. It was really weird!
