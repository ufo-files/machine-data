They were not fire works. 

My father, mother and wife All saw the event. I researched and found other videos of the same activity occurring since 2013. 

All of the orbs just disappeared after 10-30 seconds. There was no sound. No aircraft of any kind. 

We are all professionals. We had no idea what this was. Just thought we would share.
