ULOS (Unidentified Light Objects)

I found a way to take photographs of light images that are not there.  The images are beautifully colored and amazing. I believe they are communication signals from another dimension, maybe trying to let us know that we are not alone.  At first I was afraid of what I was looking at. Then I was amazed, looking thru the beautiful rays of color and images that appeared.
