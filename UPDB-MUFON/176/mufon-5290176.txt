5 or 6 orange glowing lights at night under a heavily overcast sky in Titusville FL.

AT 8:14 I was traveling south on US-1 in Titusville. Passing the Hobby Lobby, I noticed 5 or 6 bright orange lights in the sky. The cloud cover was heavy and no stars were visible. A couple lights were in a tight cluster and the remaining were trialing below. They were all visible for about a minute, then disappeared, then two next to each other brightly re-appeared for a few seconds, then disappeared (quickly dimmed out). They were maybe a few miles to the south of my position at approximately 1000 to 2000 feet AGL.
