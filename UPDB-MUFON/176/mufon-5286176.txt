I thought it was a black, mylar balloon. When I turned my car around to take a second look it was gone.

Sort of strange. Driving home at 8:28pm and above the field near my house, about house height from the ground was a black, spiky looking orb. Looked like a sun shaped birthday balloon but it was black. No lights, no sound just hovering. I thought someone tethered a helium balloon just behind their house. There was a bicyclist going the same way as us. We noticed him turn around, it must have been unusual for him too. We turned down Orchard Creek and turned around on the first cul de sac. We we got back to spine we saw the biciclist standing and looking back where it was. There was no object. I rolled down the window and said "Was that a balloon?" he said "I don't know, it just disappeared. All of this happened in the span of 2 minutes.
