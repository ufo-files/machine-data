{rjl} Observed up 6 glowing amber orbs.

I was leaving my residence in Rancho Santa Margarita, CA around 7:45 pm to pick up my daughter when I looked SE and saw six amber/glowing orbs. I quickly grabbed my camera and started recording. Two of the orbs faded and four remained for quite some time. My fiance witnessed the event as well. When the orbs blinked out, I waited for them to return. After five minutes, four of the orbs reappeared and slowly moved left. I shot some video, but since it was on my cell phone it was not too clear. I have witnessed many sightings since the 80's, from Florida to California. Some in the day as floating metal discs, which shot away after a few minutes and an amazing flyover of a silent amber orb, which silently flew over the 5 freeway on El Toro road. It stopped over Rockfield Street for about two minutes and then changed trajectory to South and then stopped and then dropped out what looked like sparks from below. That was in 2001. What i witnessed tonight could have been flares from a military jet or an actual craft of unknown origin. Anyone else see this? Sorry for the blurry footage. Cell phone video could not focus properly.
