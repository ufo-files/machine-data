{rjl} Flying saucer followed us then we stop it stopped tree top high 40 t0 50 feet away

1977 me, my brother Rick and Gary Bumbock was going to the Loyal Wisconsin Fair we turned on Hwy 73 heading south I looked to the right and seen a flying saucer flying south tree top high across the field I pointed at it and said look at the flying saucer we turned right on Capital Rd going east I seen something to the right of us and I looked and it was flying tree top high right along side of the car we stopped about a mile from hwy 73 on the bridge over a creek it stopped over the field along side of the woods 40 to 50 feet away we got out of the car it was about 25 feet high and 30 to 40 feet wide with lights on it in a row different colors it was all silver we watched it for a long time flashing the tail lights at it we started to go and it followed tree top high by the time we reached hwy K it was gone.
