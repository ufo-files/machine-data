red star like hovering then changing directions and back again

it was in the south west area of the sky. i was standing outside and was looking up to take notice of the nights sky and then it appeared to look like a star but really red in color and it would move. at first slowly then i was not sure if i was seeing things so i grabbed my husband and we stood outside for about twenty minutes to half hour. at times it appeared what we were looking at also had something above it hovering. it would hover then move in one direction then stop and go back the other way. it would grow bright then dim.
