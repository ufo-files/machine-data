Smallish basketball-sized object gliding in straight line above rooftops

Looked up casually and saw what at first sight I thought was a floating, silver-grey helium balloon above the rooftop of a building, but as I tracked it, I noticed it wasn't behaving as I'd expect a helium balloon to behave. It never changed its aspect to me (i.e. it didn't wobble about as one would expect a helium balloon to do) and it flew in (as near as I could ascertain) an absolute straight line, at about the speed of a fast cyclist, say. It traveled at the same height across the street and disappeared behind the building on the other side of the street. Also, on looking closely at it, I was looking for "crinkles" around its circumference, as one would expect from a balloon, but I could see any. Yet I wouldn't say it was totally smooth either. 

It was either a freak of the wind with a helium balloon (i.e. it was a helium balloon that the wind just happened not to blow in the way one would expect - i.e. erratically - but in a straight line and without any wobble), or some new kind of toy (perhaps a balloon with a jet?) or it was what it started to look like by the time it went out of my sight - some kind of "scouting probe"-like device flying by some unusual, wingless means. By the end of the experience, I felt a bit strange, because I had at that point exhausted my stock of ordinary explanations of what it might be, and was starting to think of it as "wow, this is really a "UFO" UFO."
