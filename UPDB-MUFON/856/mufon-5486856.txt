{jpg} 6 orange orbs floated across field and ascended into space

I was watching TV and all of a sudden I had the impulse to go out and look up at the sky. I saw a twinkling orange star like object in the sky. As soon as I look at it, it stopped as though it was there just for me. All of a sudden, off to my right, just skimming the tops of the trees, was a line of beautiful bright orange orb shaped objects, in a line, floating across the field...They zoomed up into the sky and formed what looked like a constellation. They hung there for about 2 minutes. I took a picture of them. Then in like a second, they moved back away from me, and one by one, in a perfectly straight line, disappeared into space.
