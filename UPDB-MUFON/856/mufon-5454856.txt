At least 25 craft in a line evenly spaced

I have seen many reports over the past couple of weeks of multiple craft in a line, and last night I went outside to look for this line and it was already underway when I got out there. I live on the roof of a tall building so I have a great view, and I could see about 6 ufo's in a line and as one would fly over the roof and disappear, another would become visible in the distance and it was a constant line for about 7-8 minutes with at least 25 ufo's seen.
They were at a very high altitude and moving much faster than a plane in a NE direction.
I tried to record a video but none of the wide angle shows anything, but I was able to zoom in on the closest craft for about 10 seconds. I attached that part of the video and a couple of still frames.
I am going to look for this again tonight, there has been reports every night of the same thing.
