{cak} 31 starlike lights flew east

We were sitting outside in the hot tub when my wife saw the first 2 lights coming from the west. We could not hear any sounds but we could hear highway noise. We counted 31 objects flying west to east at a high altitude rather quickly. The objects seem to be in some sort of formation at staggered intervals. I thought they were jets but couldn't hear anything. Objects were flying in same direction on the same path and crossed between Mars and Orion's belt. Used for correlation in the sky. Unsure of identity of lights.
