Strange pure black rectangular blimp then changed shape...So strange...

I left LaGrange with my wife for her appointment, heading North on 301 towards Elyria., about 12:19 PM today. One mile outside the city way off in the distance, guessing 5-7 miles in the low sky, I saw this massive Black Blimp looking object but more rectangular. Pure black...It was huge and slowly moving to my left. I was thinking a rectangular Blimp-they don't exist? Caught my eye. About one minute later it slowed, turned and changed shape? Then I saw this smoke plume near it thinking there's a huge fire and some type of craft was viewing the fire? The thing turned, heading back toward the North, then moved to the right and disappeared!!! Lasted about one minute...I've gone to 30 plus airshows and know my aircraft, airships, whatever...As I got close to Elyria no fire, no nothing. I called the Lorain County Regional Airport and they had no helicopters, no blimps, no planes dragging banners behind them-only two Cessna's all day.. If anyone seen anything, knows anything let me know...Rough sketch below. Not an artist. Driving 55 so no photos should have stopped...
