{rjl} Bright light. Fast acceleration,

All of a sudden a very bright light flashed in the sky. The light went out and then came back. It stayed roughly in the same area not moving to far from it's original point. The light was very intense. It would dim down to the point that I wasn't sure it was still there, and then come back. It did this for about 10 minutes. An airliner flying east to west was flying towards it. It dimed down and accelerated to the south as the plane approached. That was the last I seen of it. It's acceleration was very fast.
