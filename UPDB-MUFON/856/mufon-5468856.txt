{jpg} Vivid green fireball (huge) descended across the sky. I smelled burning after that.

Driving home from work on a rural road. I felt like something was going to happen and a vivid green orb descended rapidly. At first I thought it was a meteor because it moved so swiftly. Much bigger than any meteor I'd ever seen and also extremely bright green blue. I felt like it had landed in a field close by. I called my spouse who was still at work and told them about it. I opened the car window and could smell burning but could not see anything. This happened at 1:11. I did not arrive home until 1:45. I should have been home at 1:30 at the latest. I remember driving very fast 75 mph on the road where the speed limit is 55. I think I lost time somewhere.
