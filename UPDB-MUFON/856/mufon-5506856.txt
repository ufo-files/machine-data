{kt}Meteor

Walking out onto my pier, I experienced a glowing meteor that exploded and disintegrated emitting a bright white and green light. It lasted for no more than 1 second. I am on the east coast United States facing the Atlantic ocean. It looked similar to the meteor that was filmed in Russia that actually made landfall. I stood around to see if any other meteors were around and didn't see any after a 10 minute period.
