Orb ufo's spotted ove Ruthin Castle wales

I was just in Wales and on the 23rd when I was takings some sky shots of the changing weather overhead, when I spotted and photographed what I though would just turn out to be a large bird, however after uploading the images they turn out to be round orbs. Once uploading I ran the images through filters (cropping and blowing the cropped image up then putting them through several filters using Photo Box Pro) 
I don't know what I captured but it was real and it was moving in the sky North to North East in what appeared to be a straight line, I lost sight of the object when it went out of view due to the castle. Although I photographed 2 object I actually only visually saw one with the naked eye, I only discovered the second one after it showed up on the uploaded photo which had been run through a filter. I have include the final image just to say the distance from where I was to the hills in the background was about 2 miles, just saying to indicate some scale. The siting lasted about 4 or 5 second from spotting the object to moving out of view.
