Star rotating around another star then takes off into space.

As the description states. At 3:30 am I went out for a smoke. I looked up into a clear sky as I do every night. As soon as I looked up there was a star? circling around another star? at an unimaginable speed. It was as bright as venus. Both stars were the exact same size and brightness. I thought both were moving at first but I believe that was because it was moving so fast. So that star circled around 8 times.(who knows how many times before I looked up). It then shot off into space and was gone. The light faded out so fast. It didnt fly accross the sky to the horizon. I was looking basically straight up. I changed my angle of sight maybe 11 from where it was circling to where it disappeared. It lasted all of 3 seconds. I am still blown away that I got to see it. Like if I didnt look up at that moment I would never have seen it.
