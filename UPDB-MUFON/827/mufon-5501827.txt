Looked out my window, saw lights, lights hovered, 5 minutes pass, lights zoom up.

At the time of the UFO sighting I was watching a movie. I heard a bang from outside and looked out my window to investigate. I noticed lights in the sky. At first I thought it was a plane. Then I noticed that the lights did not move for almost 5 minutes. It was too dark to know what the object was but I know the Canadian government does not openly have hovering, circle like aircraft. for about 4 and a half minutes, the craft hovered what appeared to be over my neighbours' house. For the last 30 seconds, the craft slowly lifted upwards, and then zoomed away. I lost sight when it zoomed away from my eyesight. I always believed that Extra-Terrestrials existed, now I've seen what I think is an Alien craft in my neighbourhood.
