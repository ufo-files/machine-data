{cak} triangle shaped object flew directly overhead

went out to walk the dog about 2240 pm and was looking at Orion trying to locate position of comet Lovejoy. A large silent triangle shaped object flew directly overhead. I could see the outline and there were lights covering each of the three tips which were rounded, not pointed. I didn't know what it was...possibly some experimental craft from Ft. Bragg. It came from that direction (North) and flew south/SE. It flew out of sight. no sound whatsoever.
