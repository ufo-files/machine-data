Looking at other planes in the sky, I saw a very fast moving squarelike object with lights making no sound fly past.

I was at home watching television, I had gone outside for a cigarette - into the courtyard outside my apartment, ground floor, faces east. Just before finishing my smoke I looked up at the sky as it was a clear night, dark, but no coulds and the stars were quite visible.

I was looking at a plane, which then made me notice another plane and a helicopter as I was watching the helicopter I noticed this object moving extremely fast, faster than anything thing else in the sky. It didn't look like any aircraft I've ever seen in the area, and unlike most of the jets that fly past, it made no sound and left no trail. The object appeared to be square/rectangular shape with slightly rounded edges and was black, I could only make out the front of it as on the leading edge there was a static red light directly in the middle - and a feint golden glow was coming from the opposite side of the object's front.

As soon as I realised this was not like any plane or aircraft I've ever seen before I got a rush of adrenaline/excitement said "F*** me" I dropped my still light cigarette and watched the object in awe - my jaw literally dropped.

I observed the object for approximately 8 to 10 seconds, it continued in a straight - horizontal direction at tremendous speed. I lost sight of the object when my field of view fell into the path of a streetlight, last sighted the object was heading directly north. 

I ran inside and checked the time 20:13 then proceeded to file this report. This is also the first time I have ever witnessed an event of this nature.

I am able to provide a sketch of the object I saw if I can email - I am not able upload files from my phone.
