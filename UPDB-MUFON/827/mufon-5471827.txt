{dr}Observed extremely fast contail moving E to W but no object visible. Abruptly stopped.

My wife and were walking our dog approximately 1300 in afternoon. Sky had no clouds, very clear. We both observed two contrails. One was a jet liner flying north, the aircraft completely visible, ~ 25K feet in altitude. The other was flying west and moving very, very fast, abnormally fast with no visible aircraft, no refection. It's altitude ~15K feet, lower that the jet. We watched it for approximately 2 minuets and the contrail abruptly stopped. We waited and watched but it never reappeared. We both have a military background and both agreed that whatever it was, that it's speed was remarkable and very odd the way the contrail abruptly stopped.
