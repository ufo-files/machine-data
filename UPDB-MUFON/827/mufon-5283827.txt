Bright turquoise light descending very quickly towards horizon

While driving westbound on State Route 82, about 2 miles before entering Aurora, we saw a very bright turquoise light appear from just behind cloud cover in the northern sky and descend quickly at about a 40 degree angle towards the horizon. The clouds obscured any fine details, but the object cast a circular light with a solid-appearing halo and was moving far too quickly to be a plane, helicopter, or drone. The object was visible for no more than 3 seconds before it disappeared from view. My teenage son and I were both in the car talking when we both spotted the anomaly, got very quiet, and were completely awe-struck and in shock with what we were seeing.
