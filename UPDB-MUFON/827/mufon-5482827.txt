{cak} satellite like, but got super bright

I was outside of our rehearsal studio with my band mates. I tried to get them to see the object but it was gone before they could see it. It looked like a satellite appears, but much lower in the sky. It was not flashing. There are many planes in the area, but it was not a plane. No blinking lights. Just a bright white light like a star/planet. It just seemed out of the ordinary. Not a plane or helicopter. And the reason for my report is that as it travelled from left to right, it become very bright, at last 3 times brighter, then disappeared. There were no clouds nearby. Just strange.
