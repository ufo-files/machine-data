Raises the hair on my body everytime I think about it.

It was 2007 opening day of rifle deer season. My girlfriend at the time and I went up to the ridge long before daylight. As we sat in the open ground blind I built we noticed a single orange/copper colored light moving in the sky. As we were watching it we noticed it was moving back and forth. Then in an instant it moves towards is and then turned into three lights in triangle formation. In between the lights was pitch black and blocked out the stars behind it. It slowly moved out the ridge straight towards us until it was almost directly above us. There was this ungodly Humm that was all I could hear. Then it was as if it seen us and in a split second streaked across the sky and disappeared. We jumped on my 4wheeler and went home to tell my mother who didn't believe me. I felt as though the encounter drained the energy from my body. The dreams I had for weeks after the sighting were as if it happened over and over. I couldn't get the humming out of my head. I was someone who never would've believed anything like that ever existed but I can now say there's something out there. Feel free to contact me at anytime. Thank you.
