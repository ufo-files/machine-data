{rjl} Glowing red-orange sphere

I went outside almost exactly at 12 am Jan. 1 2016 to watch the fireworks set off by the people in the neighborhood. I was on my bicycle a few houses away from my own. As the fireworks are finishing I notice a light from the South, moving North. Its a glowing reddish-orange sphere. It looks to be coming from the Tarpon Springs area and very low, not much higher than the trees. Its moving slow a little faster than walking speed. I am in it's path so I keep watching to make sure its not a plane or something. As it gets closer I still don't see any flashing lights or steady white lights. It looks to be 6-8 feet in diameter. As it gets near maybe a 500 feet or less in a blink of an eye its several hundred feet over my head. I move slightly to get directly under it. When I did it looked like the cage that holds the cork on a champagne bottle. Sort of jellyfish like without the tentacles. The underside looked like a big eye. Like the "CBS" logo. After taking a long look I rushed to the house to get my camera, knowing the thing would not be there when I got back. It took less than a minute but it was gone. When I went out with my cam I noticed my neighbors were outside, also watching the fireworks. They asked what was going on. I asked how long they had been outside and did they see the thing. They did. For a week or two before that night I kept having a thought that said to watch January 1-8. My neighbors and I saw basically the same thing up close the next night as well.
