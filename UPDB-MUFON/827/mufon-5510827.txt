{nd} orange orb appeared stationary, then sank into the horizon like a fast setting sun at 3:36am

AT 3:30 am, we saw what looked like a harvest orange moon out the formal living room. To get a better look, we went outside and saw an orange large orb with no clear defined edges. It was about the size of a 1/3 of a normal moon, very bright, made no sound. Seemed far away. At first it did not appear to move, but then it quickly sank into the horizon, like a setting sun. No zig zag movement, just straight down. We did not know what it was at first and the moon was above us, so it could not have been an eclipse. By 3:36 am, it had disappeared.
