The best part but, not part of the AIr & Sea Show.

May 11 1996 - Fort Lauderdale Air & Sea Show, A1A and Sunrise Blvd. 
Waiting for next air 'show,' watch two ‘aircraft’ many miles out to sea coming towards shore, i.e. Blue Angels, as they typically split up then come together at center. 

Now approximately half mile offshore, the two craft weren’t coming closer, nor joined by other planes. They were stationary, so now thinking these craft were of the Harrier jump jet category but there are no wings, no jets/plumes. These are white, oblong, perfectly still and silent. Still thinking they are far off and going to come closer but they don’t. 

For at least 3 minutes the two craft don’t move. When they do, they move, side by side and very fast, south parallel to water, approx. 500 ft, stop on a dime and hover. 

2 min later, both craft return to original center location, same speed, and stop/hover. 

1 min later, the two split – one north, one south – out of sight, visibly but in in less than half a second.
