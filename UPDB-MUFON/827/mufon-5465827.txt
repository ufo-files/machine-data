It was over Bay of Naples hovering, rapid side move then bullet fast ascent to space

Five of us on holiday accessed roof of hotel. Chatting, enjoying romantic view of Bay of Naples. Suddenly dark object appeared from nowhere hovering over the ocean about 100 feet 
Above the water maybe a mile from shore. Pulsating green, blue and red lights. Hovering for about 30 minutes. We thought it was a coast guard helicopter until it suddenly moved rapidly to laterally several hundred feet, stopped abruptly, then at high speed ascended into the sky until within seconds the lights became so faint we could no longer see it as it was so far above the Earth.
