{jpg} Lights/orbs hovering, changing direction and disappeared

Me and my girlfriend were leaving the bank heading westbound on Rockton Rd. Saw 3-5 red/orange lights in southern sky 3 in a perfect triangle formation, 1-2 in a straight line in parallel positioning. We thought it was maybe helicopter search party at first. Was about to make a northbound turn onto Dorr Rd and saw another 2-3 lights lower in between rooflines. As I turned north she was describing them to me as they were too my south, I turned right immediately into a neighborhood to get turned around to witness event. As I got turned back facing southbound there appeared to be upwards of 9 orbs, hovering, moving slowly in tree line/roofline, at one point two sets of three were in perfect triangle formation, at another time they were in a straight line. I drove down to Kelly Meyers park in Roscoe, Il and pulled over to get out, at this point all the hair on my body was sticking straight up in disbelief at what I was seeing. We pulled over next to a small SUV that appeared to be witnessing the same event but as I went to get out of ,y car they left. The lights started moving in different directions, a few went vertical and just seemed to turn off. A few went east and turned off. Two were heading northwest so I pulled out to go follow. As we were driving down the road we stopped for a stop light and tried to look back up and they were just gone. The lights were mainly a fiery orange color, but at time seemed more yellow and others almost apple red. No flashing or real pulsing. We saw several planes fly over head during the process, I can not explain what I just witnessed. By time the last two lights disappeared I noted the clock at 7:11 pm local time. Whole event lasted maybe 5-6 minutes. Too long for planes passing by, and I know it wasn1t a plane as we watch them fly overhead all the time.
