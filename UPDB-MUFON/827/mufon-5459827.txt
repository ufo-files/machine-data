{rjl} Object following airplane's contrail

At approximately 5:10 am on June 29th 2019 while sitting in my personal vehicle performing security services I observed what appeared to be a commercial aircraft flying in the sky from the west in a southeastern direction. After a minute or so of watching the plane, I began watching the contrail it left behind. While looking at said contrail, I observed a rather small, shiny, cigar shaped object trailing a distance behind the airplane following its contrail. The object looked silver in color and approximately half the size of the airplane. The object seemed to have been flying much slower then the airplane. The object at times also appeared to either glow, flash or reflect light off its surface. I watched and video recorded the object flying just below the airplane's contrail until it flew into some clouds and disappeared from my sight.
