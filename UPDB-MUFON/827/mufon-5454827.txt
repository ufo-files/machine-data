Red orb

I was around 6 or 7 years of age as was staying at my uncle & aunts farm in Mt Barker.
Me & my Uncle where in the farm shed where i was watching my uncle working on some machinery. I noticed a red orb circling the farm moving very slow but still covering quite some distance.
I asked my uncle what it was? he came out the shed & was looking at it with then it started to speed up moving very fast in an oval shaped path.
The next thing i remember is i was standing alone about 30m away from where me & my uncle where originally standing & my aunt arguing about where my uncle had been & as to my where about. she said she had come out to check on us & we had been gone for 1 hour & 20 minutes. the object was still present when my aunt went back inside but didn't notice it i remember it was stationary at that point. i then remember my uncle becoming very agitated & raising his voice several time telling me to stop looking at it by this time the object was traveling at tremendous speed!!! even at my young age i some how new it would have had to be traveling at at least 2000 kph
