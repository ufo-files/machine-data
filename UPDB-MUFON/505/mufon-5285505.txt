17 years after my only sighting , I went back to that spot and saw another

Day time around 5:50pm September 10th 2021, I went to the same location to show some people from my perspective of a sighting I saw 17 years earlier . When we got there I was on the phone and didn't get say say anything yet . I heard " look ! Look ! ". I looked up and right above me were I think I counted 6 U.FO.'s . We were there for a good 15 min. I believe. They had 3 looked like round balls or lights circling each other super fast underneath each one . They were in a circle or diamond like shape and moved in unison . They would glow a slime green color and at times they would disappear then reappear . Side note there were no clouds in the sky at the time but as I looked streight up and tried to really get a good look at it there was a fog they were hiding in .  One of them was separate from the others and it was not above but more so in front of us but still high in the sky . It at times would reflect the sky and other times I thought I could see into it . It would shift from looking more like a cloud and then blimp like and then like a balloon and then reflective again . It just drifted until it disappeared . I was told by one of the people I was with she waved to it when she first saw whatever it's is , it separated from the others and stood still for a while . I have pictures and a bad video which I believe if we saw them at night I could have picked up on the lights . The pictures don't show the colors or lights at all more like little white clouds and shapes .
