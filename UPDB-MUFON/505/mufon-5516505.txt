Bright orange light in the sky that disappeared then reappeared (could have been more than one)

I was walking home one night at about half 11-ish, when I was about 200 meters from my house I looked up at the night sky and saw a very bright orange light shoot off in an northern direction as it did so it faded out initially I thought it was a sodium enriched meteorite so I continued on down the road about 2 seconds after i first saw the shooting star I looked back in the rough area I saw the light this time I saw another orange light fly in a big Z shape, by this time I had walked behind a tree and lost sight of it. (the start of the Z was very close to the starting point of the first orange flash and ended way past the point where the first light faded out) thought it was a metiorite shower and I just saw 3 meteorites whos flight paths Had just crossed each other, but then I thought if it was that the Z shape wouldnt be continuous (which it was). there were no trails behind any of the flashes, it was just what looked like an separate orange light moving at a very fast speed, it didn't stop or even appear to slow down in the turns of the Z shape. 
I live near a flight path so Im used to seeing planes in the sky, this was definitely like no other I had ever seen, also it was travelling so fast I thought it was a shooting star so obviously moved a lot faster than any plane/helicopter/satellite 
Another strange thing about it was it appeared to have an effect of my phone, I was on it as I saw the lights and it froze and lost all signal about 5 seconds before and after both the lights, I don't know if the lights caused this but its a brand new phone that didn't do that before or since 
personally, am pretty sure it was some sort of E.T. ship.
