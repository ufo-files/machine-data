Orange star like object travelling at speed

I arrived home approximately about 8.40pm and as always looked up at the night sky to check out the stars and see if I can see any satellites passing over head. I saw what I thought for a second was a satellite (I see a lot of them and am fairly used to their size/speed and reflective light) but it was about 3 times the size (like how mars appears) and moving about 3 times the speed of any satellite I1ve seen. It was travelling in a roughly easterly direction. Shortly after it passed over head it appeared to go what I can only describe as slightly out of phase and a faint secondary image of it appeared to about 45 degrees behind it to its right looking at it from underneath.
Then both disappeared. It was not a meteorite or burning piece of satellite/space junk (bot of which I1ve seen before re presentation and speeds). There was no trail of any kind or deviation from its path or from what I could see its height
