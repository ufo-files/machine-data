Spotted dark triangular UFO

On or around September 7, 2007, my granddaughter and I spotted a dark triangular UFO from 11:00 p.m. for 22 minutes. This UFO just hovered motionless without sound above our black walnut trees for 22 minutes. There were red and blue lights emanating from the bottom of the craft. After watching it for 22 minutes; it just took off faster than your eye could register it; and again, without any sound whatsoever. When my husband returned from work; he found it hard to believe us; even though we were both in law enforcement at the time. We moved from (Location removed to Investigators Report Section/cms/LLL) in 2008 due to employment; and have not lived at the sighting location since. I have never witnessed anything like this since or before then. I know what I saw. It was a dark triangular craft. Where it was from, who knows?
