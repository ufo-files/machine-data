{cak} 2 hovered stationary for over 5 seconds

My wife and I were sitting out back of our home this evening enjoying the stars and relaxing. We heard a loud aircraft sound and my wife asked me if I thought it was an air tanker which we commonly hear in the area. As she was saying that I was fixed on what I initially thought was a single aircraft flying right to left (E to W). It looked like it had 1 steady light in the front, 1 steady light in the back and 2 smack light on the left and right of the back. I believed it was 1 bird until the aircraft turned north with both the front light and the back lights moving the same direction at the same time. At that point my wife was looking as well and said that was weird. I replied yes it must be a section of birds flying in tight formation, it looked like just 1 at first. The 2 lights than turned towards the N NE and each suddenly sprouted at least 4 flying aircraft of red and white. My wife said are they dropping flavors. I said yes they must be. some of the smaller crafts lights were red and some white. Before I finished my sentence those smaller light shed at least a total of 15 smaller crafts some of which were red, green and white. At that moment I knew these were not any type of craft or conventional aircraft. My wife asked if it was night skydivers but this could not be possible. They moved in all observable directions and at moments hovered stationary for over 5 seconds. The only way I can explain it is if there were 2 aircraft flying which dispensed drones or Utah's which then in turn dropped dozens of drones or UAV's. All of the lights seemed to get in to some sort of organized cluster type of formation and slowly went out of view towards the N NE.
