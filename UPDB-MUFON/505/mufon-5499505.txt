{nd} 6 orange/red glowing orbs being led by a cylindrical grey orb or saucer type vehicle.

I pulled into a parking lot and observed what I thought at first was an odd colored aircraft and then another and finally five orbs in a varied line from the north of Nansen Summit headed Southeast transecting an actual airplane flight path but below and to the west of the airplane. They were led by a "classic" saucer type vehicle, but from my perspective I could not tell whether it was an orb or a saucer shape. It was also unlit and illuminated by the rather large and bright moon this evening against the clouds that were above it. The orange/red orbs followed it to the SE and continued over PCC campus towards I5 and Tigard when I lost sight of them.
