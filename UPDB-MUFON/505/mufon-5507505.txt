{dr}While taking astro-photos observed 2 lights in formation moving together as if one object.

While a night of astro-photography in Mountain Ranch with my friend we observed what first looked to be stars in the sky above the mountain tree line in the north east. But being familiar with constellations I knew these were not stars. When then observed them moving towards us. They appeared as 2 lights in formation which could have been 2 miles apart from each other. But the moving lights were synchronized as if a single object with a solid white light on each end but no visible structure in the middle. The object then descended below the mountain line and dissapeared.
