10 bright redish orange ufos

I had just gotten back from the Movie Theater with my roomate , we went to the balcony of our apartmet to smoke a cigarrete.As usual I am always looking at the sky at night , I am a great admirer of stars and the moon. All of a sudden I saw this 6 bright redish orange flying objects , they were the size of the stars ,they were coming from down the horizon on the left size of the sky .They were flying close together but not in a linear ,ascendent and descendent directions ,there was also a helicopter flying at the same time on the sight!.I imediatelly told my roomate of what I was seing , he asked me what are those things as we stared at the objects.We started losing sight of them when the light went across our building , that's when 4 more appeared following them from the half of the sky , same colors , and were flying in the same direction of the previous 6 ones! I have never seen something like that in the sky but I knew right of way that they were UFOS! My roomate had always been skeptical about this subject but now he thinks that there's definately something out there! Unfortunately I didn't have a camera handy to record the sight ,I was so excited that I didn't even think about the camera!
