Saucer shaped disc dark metallic

I was travelling in my car approximately 3am in a westerly direction approaching the traffic lights on the corner of karrinyup and north beach road, when I saw a bright light in the sky above the traffic lights to my right. As I watched it the light disappeared and the UFO appears about 6ft by 20ft in size. I stopped my car at the traffic lights to get a better look, I got out of my car, The UFO starts to move slowly in a easterly direction 50ft above the houses with no sound, so I decided to follow it, as I did it moved across the freeway and started to speed up still flying at this altitude. So I gave up following it and decided to go to the nearest phone and ring the channel 7 TV station as it was further east in the flight path of the UFO, an engineer answered the phone at that time, so I said I saw a UFO and it was travelling in his direction, He asked if I would like to leave my name but I declined. I was still quite scared at that time.
