Green Ball

Green Ball of light observed in early morning darkness as I was leaving home......northern lights were not out and northern lights do not take the form of a round ball/oval. 

Object traveled in a straight line and was bright green, very brilliant. Seemed to be about 2000ft AGL and moving at a guess of 150 mph..........not a meteorite continued in a straight line until out of sight after 2-3 minutes.....no sound... i simply staired at it until it was no longer visible.

Born here on the north slope of alaska, never seen anything like this at all before.
