{cak} first a stationary light to WNW, then a triangle of 13 lights flying south from WNW, then another stationary light in WSW

Witnesses came home after picking up a pizza and noticed a very bright light outside about 10 degrees above horizon to WNW. Watched it about 20 minutes, even through binoculars, and it was changing colors. They live 2 blocks from Gulf of Mexico and it appeared over water. So placed a call to the US Coast Guard who gave them the local MUFON hotline number to call, called MUFON and reported and then noticed the triangular shaped object with 13 or so lights fly close by, estimated within 1000 feet, that made no sound or gave off any emissions and was moving from WNW southward rapidly. Viewed object about 25 seconds until it disappeared WSW. At that time noticed another very bright stationary object in the WSW, much brighter than a star and again changing colors. Provided sketch of triangular lights, lights resembled poker chips and were dull bluish-grey in color and some smaller lights were in center area of triangle.
