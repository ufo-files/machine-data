Caught this photo in between my attempt at rapidly taking photos of another strange object in the southern sky

I had noticed a unusually bright "star" for several nights. I felt like this is the same thing i had seen months before except it was in a different spot in the night sky. I noticed it once again in a different spot in the sky. I grabbed my phone and began taking photos. Oddly enough, I could not engaged the recorder to take video so I hit the shutter button repeatedly for a minute or so. This is the only photo that showed anything except the black sky. I played with the colors on the editor to see if i could enhance what i was seeing. The last photo is the original.
