{cak} Almost like a firework but without the explosion

I had been looking at the stars by my home when I noticed a light shoot up from behind some homes and trees than disappeared. It was extremely fast and looked like a firework shooting straight up but instead of any burn off or explosion it abruptly disappeared as it rose into the sky. It was a fast experience and the large light was an intense green. It was also strange because no one had been doing fireworks because it's spring when the event happened, it's also a week day as well as a school night, and the disappearing of the light was so fast that I almost thought that the light had gone into some sort of hyper speed after passing the houses and tree line. I am sure that the light had rose up from somewhere in the small town I am not sure from where.
