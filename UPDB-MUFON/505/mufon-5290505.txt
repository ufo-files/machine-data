Two very bright, short duration lights

We were watching the great conjunction of Saturn and Jupiter. After they set, we were looking at Mars. Between kids looking in the telescope, I looked into the eyepiece to verify that Mars was still centered in the view. I observed what looked like a satellite move through the field of view - not super unusual. As I'm thinking about this, the little girl asked "which one are we looking at?". I looked up to show her mars but there were two VERY bright star-like objects south of Mars, separated by about 1 arc minute. All seven people there remarked that those lights weren't there before. The two lights persisted for about 20 to 25 seconds and faded out of view. There was no observable radial motion to them. They appeared to be fixed against the surrounding stars. Nothing further was observed in that area of the sky. No planes or other artificial lights. I trained the telescope on that area of sky and searched but saw nothing other than stars.
