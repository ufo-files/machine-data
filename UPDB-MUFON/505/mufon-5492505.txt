{rjl} Cross referenced position of ISS and have confirmed it was neither Mars, Venus or the ISS. Object much too bright and moving much too fast to be any of these

Witnessed the orange orbs for the third time this year. It's as if it knows when I'm about to get a really good look at it, because it vanishes from sight each time I go inside my home to grab my telescope. The last 2 times I witnessed the orb, I was with other peers who can confirm the sighting and can confirm the orb disappeared from sight right before I got back outside to observe it with a telescope. All 3 sightings were at the same time of night and was moving in the same direction all 3 times. When I was alerted to come outside to "see this" by my parents, I instantly recognized it as the infamous orange orb. Object had no sound. Object seemed to be taking the path of a satellite in a low earth orbit, but satellites do not disappear or disintegrate right before your eyes. My father was the first to witness it on this occasion. Said he saw it rise from the ground to the sky and followed it home in his truck where he alerted my mother and I to come ouside and look at it (was still very visible at this point). Witnessed the object for about 60 seconds before I went inside to get telescope. My feelings were normal, that of amazement and curiosity. Object seems to have a mind of it's own sometimes, (as if it can think or if it is being piloted by someone who can think or be aware that I am about to get a very very good close look at it). Then again, maybe I am just over thinking. No pictures or videos available. It happens to suddenly for me to think of getting it on video. But I imagine this will not be the last time I see this thing, and next time I will be prepared.
