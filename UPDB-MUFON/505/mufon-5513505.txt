{nd} 2 fireball color objects following one another

viewed 2 orange fireball like glowing objects that were flying parallel to the earth.  The first object was a few seconds in front of the first object and had to small lights dangling below. Toward the end of the sighting it flew off vertically. The second object continued to fly parallel to the Earth until we lost sighting. No planes were visible during the sighting until a few minutes later we only saw one flying in the opposite direction. The two objects were seen together for about a total time of about 1 minute. We heard no noise at all from the objects, they were seen at 11:40 pm on Christmas Eve in Tampa, FL by 4 people. We viewed them until we lost visual sight.
