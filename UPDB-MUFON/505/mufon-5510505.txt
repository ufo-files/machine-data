{cak} Large, bright, white light, 500+ft above the tree line moving from South to East.

I walked outside to my front yard to take pictures of the stars. When I looked up I saw a bright, white light that was moving and appeared to be larger and lower than other stars. I ran inside and asked my partner to come and look at the object as witness. We watched the single,bright oval object steadily cross the sky with a slight wobble, no sound, just above the trees, street and river (approx.500+ft in altitude). We viewed the object for about a minute until we lost sight of it behind the trees. I did manage to capture it on video. (2 witnesses)
