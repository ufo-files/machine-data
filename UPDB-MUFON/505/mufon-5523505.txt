An unknown craft changing directions 3 times during the day(vapor trails, high altitude)

Last Saturday Oct. 1st. Coming back home after picking up my son from a friends house during the day, He and I saw what we assumed was an airplane because of its vapor trails, this is quite common of course but what happened next is something I have never seen before..Hwy. 94 driving East between the cities of Alexandria, Mn. and Osakis, Mn. this assumed airplane had changed course in a sharp south-western direction, then changed opposite direction in a south-eastern direction then moved northward. The vapor trails created the shape of a back-ward "S". Im thinking that the pilot of this craft lost his way, navigation problems. This is just something you don't see. This was the highlight of our conversation(my son and I) the rest of the way home. I told my husband when we got home, Now earlier tonight my husband mentioned he heard on the radio about several reports in Kansas and missouri areas past month now and I get on here and read almost identical reports of crafts making strange maneuvers, which prompted me to throw my 2 cents worth. Still curious about what it really was.
