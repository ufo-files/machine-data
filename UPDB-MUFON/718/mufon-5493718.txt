{rjl} Reddish star like object

Standing on porch looking towards the west at the moon. Clear skies. Noticed what I thought was an airplane since Metro airport is 16 miles awy. Noticed right away a distinctive, non-blinking red light on a north/nw trajectory; no sudden changes in path. After about 5 seconds noticed a discharge of reddish 'flare', just slightly smaller but still quite large, directly toward the ground. Lasted about 2 seconds and dissipated (reminsicent of a firework flare). Continued north. Seemed to change to a whitish red. Went behind a tree. I got off the porch to track it, and in that short 5 second time it was gone completely. After not seeing the object anymore I saw an aircraft moving directly where the unidentified object should have been but just slightly higher up, heading on a east trajectory. I thought that it was being tracked. It appeared it absolutely would have crossed paths.
