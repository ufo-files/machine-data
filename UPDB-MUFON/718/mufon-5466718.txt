Was hovering above large church cross lit up by blue lights then with 2 seconds took off. Gave chase but too fast

While taking two of my kids home to their mums house after dinner we noticed what we thought were red and green plane lights in the sky. We drove another 400 metres until we were beside the object which was only approximately 70 metres east and approximately 100 metres up. I was constantly asking the kids about the lights, the shape, visible things on the object. I wanted it to be a helicopter. My kids are 14 and 12. Both kids and I witnessed the lights didn't flash. The lights were on each corner and consisted of 1 green and 2 red. There was a white light also. As I was driving I couldn't make anything out except it was hovering above the church cross which was brightly lit up with a blue light. Both kids said the object was a triangle shape. I asked them again about the shape and asked them to stop messing with me. Both were very serious and repeated it was a triangle. My son 14 said there were three white lines underneath and what looked like a hatch. I told them both to keep an eye on it while I turned around as we were now approximately 200m past. It took 4 seconds to turn around. We drove back toward the objects position but it was now moving south and gaining altitude much much faster than I could drive. I turned again and I drove the kids home, constantly quizzing them, making sure they weren't making it up. Which they were adamant they weren't. I asked them draw what they saw.
