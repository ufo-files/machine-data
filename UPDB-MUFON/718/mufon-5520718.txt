For over three weeks a constant, abnormally bright starlike objects. One to SW one to NE.

On my front porch at approx 7/7:30 I noticed an abnormally bright star to the SW one night while smoking a cig. About two hours later when I went out again, the star had just vanished. Approx 1 1/2 later went to back porch and noticed the star was now out back. I took video after the third night, when I was sure this was no star. The same events just kept happening... It would appear out front, then about 2-5 hours later would disappear. Sometimes the one out back would be visible and the one out front was still there.  These lights are extremely bright and pulsate. The also seem to have little flares or sparkles that spurt from them from time to time. They also will have a reddish or amber like tinge to them.  I also noticed two of these at the same horizon line and a third, smaller light go straight up and disappearing just before you would think it would be connecting to the bright, unwavering light to the left, this was at 5:30am one morning shortly after my original sighting.
