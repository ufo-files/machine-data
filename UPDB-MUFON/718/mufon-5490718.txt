{jpg} 4 very bright red/orange lights in the night sky above the Salton Sea

It was Wednesday July 29th around 10:30pm and I was in my kitchen putting dinner away after gettin my kids to bed when I heard my dogs who are usually very quite start to bark and howl. I opened my sliding glass door to see why they were barking and witnessed them looking southeast at the sky and when I looked to see what they were looking at I witnessed 4 very large bright glowing red/orange balls of lights hovering high up in the sky. I watched them as the slowly shifted positions randomly. I ran inside to get my husband to see them and asked him what he thought they were and he could only describe them as very large balls of fire in the sky. We continued to watch them for about 5 mins as they continued to move randomly but staying in the same area close to eachother. I ran inside to get my camera and when I returned ther was only three left and they had formed a straight line with each other. I was able to get a few pictures before they vanished in what seemed like mid picture. I searched the sky's to all directions to see if I could relocate these lights but was never able to. While watching these lights I couldn't help but feel a sense of fear, dread, and depression like something was very wrong. I continued to look for the lights for the rest of the night and the next two nights following this event. I never seen the lights again.
