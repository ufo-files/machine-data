Spring 1978

Traveling at night with a friend west across New Mexico 1978. Pulled over to pee. Around 1am we were facing west and spotted 6 to 10 lights of different colors float about 10 miles away. Lights were large started moving jetting around, then one or two at a time shot start up or to the west and disappeared. It lasted about 10 minutes.
