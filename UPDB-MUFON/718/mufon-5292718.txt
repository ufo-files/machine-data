Triangle dark no lights large

Was on a night patrol in military. Was wearing night optics and felt a low rumble. Was in thick forest and couldnt see anything at ground level so I looked up. That is when I saw this triangle shape right above. It was large and blocked any moon light we had. I could see it clearly with the optics in a green tint. I removed the optics and still could see the dark shape above. It hovered for at least 10 minutes I think. Was keeping track of time for a meet up with another squad. We lost those 10 minutes I tracked when I looked at my watch after it left slowly. I asked others with me and they said they saw it too. I couldn't report it then because of looking like a fool in front of my commander. I have kept this quiet too many years because of that.
