Saw 3 bright red-orange lights in the sky. Saw them sw of Coral Springs Florida.

We went outside around 9:30 p.m December 31st, 2011, we looked up and saw 3 bright red-orange lights flying in a distance SW of Coral Springs Florida. They were very noticable, there was nothing else in the sky that bright. At first we were thinking they were airplanes but they were moving too slow, the lights were not blinking, and by how close they were together we knew they couldnt be airplanes. There were 2 on the top and one on the bottom. They were moving west plusating for about 10 minutes before they just dissapered.
