{rjl} I witnessed a white object in the sky to the west. It flew toward me, hovered over me, then flew away to the south.

Out walking my dog. Looked up and saw the white object over the hills to the west. It flew toward me descending down the hill and over the lake. It came to a stop near me above the tree tops. It hovered for five minutes. It then flew away to the south very fast, in the blink of an eye.

It was disk snapped 50-60 feet across, about two feet thick at the edge. The whole object glowed whitish-yellow, darker on the bottom. It made no noise at all.

It left me speechless.
