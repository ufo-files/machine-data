I saw what appeared to be a floating oil drum above a Lutheran church about five years ago in Sioux City Iowa. It was metallic, and reflective. This was hovering about 400-500 feet above the ground. I was walking my dog and I felt in awe of seeing it. I don't know why I didn't call the police, but I felt really calm and content while seeing it. I just looked at it for a couple minutes, and walked inside.........I don't know why I didn't make any phone calls! It was the damnedest thing I ever saw. Nothing was around.
