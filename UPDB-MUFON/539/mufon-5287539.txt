Mirror-like sighting

I have retained this memory perfectly for years without disclosing to anyone besides my husband (and the neighbour I witnessed it with,who has now passed away), what we saw that late spring day. I had just dropped my daughter off at her school bus stop, in which she was the only child to have to walk to a neighbours house to be picked up. I was walking home from the bus stop pushing my son Eli in a stroller as I saw a weird light on the pavement in front of me, like a cat who sees a laser pointer. I instinctively looked up, and I saw a perfect oval/ circle (depending on how it turned) in the sky. It looked like a mirror, I saw it one moment, then I saw it again. It swayed in the sky as a mirror and I called to my elderly neighbour across the road to look and see what I was seeing. He was an elderly man, he said "It's okay, they won't hurt us" and just left his driveway like it was nothing. I will NEVER forget this.
