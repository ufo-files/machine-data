On 12/4/2014 10:05pm I first noticed one item in the sky at about a 45 degree angle above the roof of the building across from me and my family. The object was changing colors from red, white, blue, green, and to purple. There were clouds below the item, you could see the item clearly. Approximately 10:15pm we noticed another in the sky to the left of it, then about 10:25pm, there were 2 more to the west of it to form a square then about 10:30pm the clouds began to move at a rapid pace to cover the 4 items by 10:35 all covered by clouds. At about 11 pm, the object moved from a 45 degree angle to approximately a 60 degree angle from the roof of the building across from us. On Halloween night 10/31/2014 we were in Lehigh Acres at a friends house and we were facing east as well at about 10pm we noticed a similar object that was moving around the sky going up, down, left, and right, at a rapid pace. Then about 10:30 it went from the northern part of the sky to the southern part of the sky it covered many miles in a split second. 4 days later 11/4/2014 as I was driving home from work at about 10:30 am I was pulled over by a black suv. A man walked up to my car dressed in a black suit with black sunglasses and talked to me his voice sounded like he was talking through a voice modifier. He knew my name and my information and he told me that the object i saw on Halloween night, that I never saw it and that I need to forget what i saw and not to tell anyone.
