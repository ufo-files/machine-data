Circled craft

I was talking on the phone and out of my corner of my right eye I noticed something yellowish orange and red. But then I seen the craft, it was circular and the top of it was black and the undercarriage was gray. And it had a 3 Circled light under the craft in the shape of a triangle. I only got about a four second look at it and then it went over the treetops. I tried to run down my property but then the lights phased out and it was gone.
