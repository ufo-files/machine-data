Large sphere hovering above treetops

Driving into work at hospital at 6:14am, noticed a very large sphere in the sky, approx 300-400 feet above tree tops on hwy15 Coming into kingston. Very bright white glow that faded into dull orange, then immediately flashed back to bright white. Remained stationary the entire time. I pulled over to look at it and it remained in the same spot for up to 3 minutes before shooting up into the sky and disappearing like it switched off all its lights. Much larger than a helicopter from that altitude.
