{cak} 2 fireballs seen over Kansas November 6 or 7, 2018

My husband was hunting in Kansas, Osage County. November 6 or 7, 2018. After hunting, around 6:00 pm., he and his friend are driving south and in the eastern sky they saw 2 fireballs (one right behind the first one), very bright white with a greenish tail, going North to South. They only saw it for a few seconds. Right after the lights were gone they saw a helicopter going in the same eastern direction they saw the fireballs.
