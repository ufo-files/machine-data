{cak} turning into the neighborhood when I saw a bright pulsating object in the sky ahead of me

I was turning left into the neighborhood and I noticed a bright star-like object a good distance away in the sky in front of me. It seemed to pulsate from a yellow-orange to a green color. It was too bright to be a plane or helicopter and it was too bright to be a star. It did not move. I thought about stopping to take a picture but someone was behind me plus I’m not sure it would’ve shown up well on my phone’s camera. When I went down a hill into the neighborhood I couldn’t see it anymore with the trees blocking my view. I attached where I was and where the object was in the sky from Google Earth.
