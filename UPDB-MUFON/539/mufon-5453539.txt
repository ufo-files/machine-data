{rjl} Spherical cloud surrounded by metallic moving objects

Good morning, on 4/20/20 me and terry we’re outside in the backyard when she noticed that a round cloud had appeared in the sky. We didn’t know how long it was there but when we photographed the cloud it began to dissipate rather quickly. When we went back to look at the pictures the next day we noticed small reflective objects all around the sky. The picture was viewed by others on reddit and I believe 16 were counted. The video at first didn’t alarm us but when we went back frame by frame we could seee that things were moving around up there. The video is a little late on the cloud but we do catch is dissipating rather fast. [video moved/cms/rjl]
