Sitting in my living room watching tv,which was positions under the south side window. Across the street from me was a large cattle pasture. Now this is the country where all sounds could be heard and any air craft flying at this altitude was never seen at all. While watching tv I saw a very bright light coming toward our home. My first thought was a plane was going to crash into our home. I ran to the window to see what it was. It was moving very slowly going north it was cylindrical and passing over the west side of my property line passing out of my view. I ran quickly to the north window the object was still moving slowly north at maybe 40 miles per our. It appears to be cigar shaped with 4 square Windows visible to me. It was close enough to me that I could see the silvery interior than it just disappeared. Wondering how it could have gone out of sight I ran outside and it was simply gone.
