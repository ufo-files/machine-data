{rjl} Observed glowing object in sky that stopped, brightened, dimmed and changed direction.

Was inside my home watching TV. Out of the blue, I asked for the star beings to come out so I could see them again. I went out to my front sidewalk and looked up in the sky to look for them. My wife came out about 3 minutes later and was also looking. I said "there they are". It was brighter than a star. Referring to a clock position, it stopped at approx. the 3:00 position. Then it moved downward in a SW "arc" direction to the 6:00 position and grew larger and brighter for approx. 3-5 seconds. It then dimmed and moved upward to the 9:00 position and then slowly disappeared.
