{cak} Two silent glowing orbs similar to ball lightning approach our vehicle, beaming twice like a camera, then disappear.

My boyfriend and I were in the parked car at 9PM, talking. I was looking one direction, he was facing the other. Suddenly I see two orbs coming at us fast, like a car, with an almost blinding ball-lightning-like light, connected by red in the center. My boyfriend sees three distinct flashes of light, which he says resemble a camera. I thought it was a cop car until it disappeared without a sound, never passing our car.
