{rjl} traveling in car with my friend on rt 299 toward New Paltz NY. Large object with lights moving toward us. We pulled over, got out of the car and watched the craft silently travel toward Poughkeepsie NY in a straight line never changing altitude.

This object had silent propulsion unlike any man made objects of this size. I new it was a UFO as did my friend who was with me. He passed away in the late 1980's
