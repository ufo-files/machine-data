{dr}Appeared to look like a bright light flying inside a baloon. Bright smoke trails surrounding it.

I was sitting in my patrol car facing east with my driver side window down. As I finished making entries into my laptop I happened to look north and noticed an unusually bright small cloud in totally dark surroundings. Soon after I observed a bright light flying a straight slow path from a west to east direction with bright white smoke billowing from its left and right position (not from behind like a rocket). It was so slow that it seemed like it was going into a hover. I could not identify what this was. I've never seen anything like it. I also noticed maybe two smaller flashing lights trailing from its left and right that seemed to be following it or were flying with it. I quickly grabbed my cell phone and began to record as soon as possible. The light continued its easterly direction, disappeared behind a small cloud, then reappeared at the other end when it produced a small ring of smoke similar to a pebble hitting a calm lake. Soon after the light was out of my view due to more clouds in its path. I served 11 yrs in Naval Aviation and never witnessed anything like this. Hope this helps you out.
