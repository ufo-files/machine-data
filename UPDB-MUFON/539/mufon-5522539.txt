bright orange fireball object traveled in straight line turned into small white object then dissapeared

I was on passenger side of car driving home on wig hill road when I noticed what appeared to be a fireball in the sky through the trees. We stopped the car at the next clearing so that we could observe. Got out of car and saw object fly in straight line, no noise, the object flew toward hills, stopped and turned white and shrunk in size, then disappeared. Reaction was shocked, we have never seen anything like it. i wish I had taken my phone out to record it.
