{cak} Bright white and star like moving North East, then abruptly turned east.

My friend and I were outside smoking when he said: "Why is that star moving?" - "What star?" He pointed up to the sky and when I saw it, it was just as high as any of the stars appeared to be. "That's not a plane" he said. Me: "No.. no that's not a plane and it's moving too fast to be a satellite." Then it abruptly turned east and ZOOMED across the sky and disappeared.
