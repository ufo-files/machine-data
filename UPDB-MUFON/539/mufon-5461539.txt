{jpg} Triangular craft passing perpendicularly over US 30

I was driving East on US 30 about 7 miles outside of Wooster when I saw a triangular craft with individual lights on the points. Each light changed colors randomly, and there was no obvious pattern to the changes. I saw the craft in my peripheral vision coming from about the South-East. It then moved slowly over the highway - almost gliding. After it had crossed the highway, it changed direction and went back over the highway before changing direction again to disappear over the tree line. A few seconds after I lost sight of this craft, I saw another one approaching the highway. The lights on this one also change colors randomly, but it did not double back after it had glided over the road. This craft also disappeared behind the tree line.
