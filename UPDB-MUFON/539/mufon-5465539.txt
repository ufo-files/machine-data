{dr}Both star-like objects appeared, then slowly got brighter and then slowly disappeared.

On September 19th 2018 at approximately 7:40pm about 2 miles east of Frederick, Oklahoma my wife and 3 kids picked me up for my lunch break. As we were pulling out of the parking lot we all were noticing how beautiful the sunset looked. As we were looking, my wife noticed a star-like object just west of Frederick at what seemed to be about 5,000 feet up appear close to the few clouds that existed, so I stopped the vehicle on the side of the road. It appeared dim lit at first and slowly got brighter as it moved southerly and for roughly 10 seconds it moved slowly but steadily in a straight path and its brightness fluctuated until it disappeared. About that time my wife was telling me to pull out my phone and start recording, so I did quickly. Just a few seconds after I started recording, right were it had disappeared, it re-appeared and did the same movement and brightness fluctuations again for about the same amount of time until it faded away. Then about 50 seconds later another one appeared slightly north of where the first one was and did the exact same thing until it dissapeared. In all I believe there were 2 "objects" that we could see. My wife and I really enjoyed this sighting because we have for about 2 years now been investigating the UFO subject and have been wanting to see what we thought was a legitimate sighting. Around the day of the filming we had been discussing in depth issues surrounding the secrecy of this topic so it was amazing that it happened when it did.
