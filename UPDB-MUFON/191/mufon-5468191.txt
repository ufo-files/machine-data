{td} More of a square shape with a soft white and sometimes reflective look.

I noticed this once hovering at a very high altitude when my friend and I were at the beach. An airliner was passing under this object. It hovered for a long while slowly drifting back n forth in a northerly to southerly path... There was no trail... No lights.. just like it was just hanging out like a kite. But at a very very high altitude. Then we got tired of watching it and went about our day at the beach. We couldn't take a picture cause it would not have come out in the picture.
