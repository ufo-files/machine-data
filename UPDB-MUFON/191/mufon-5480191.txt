{rjl} Orange orb larger than a harvest moon slowly flattened out and disappeared

Sitting on my back porch, a giant orange orb, larger than a harvest moon appeared in the southern sky. It was impossible to tell a distance. The light was brighter than that of a airplane light and just continued to glow in an orange/red color. I had no idea what it could possibly be. I became incredibly uneasy. The object then flattened out and widened, as if someone had "stepped on it" until it was a long orange line, and then it vanished. When it was over I noticed I was sweating profusely. It might have been moving SW a little but it was difficult to tell.
