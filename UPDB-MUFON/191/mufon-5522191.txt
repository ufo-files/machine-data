three orange orbs, moved together as if connected

Driving to Wingstop from my house at about 8:45 pm on 12/7/11 I saw three orange orbs in a triangle formation. They moved together as if connected but I couldnt see if they actually were or not. They were moving away from me and appeared to descend slightly and bank slightly. They were obviously not airplane lights or anything like that. After around 8-10 seconds of looking at them they kind of went out, almost like they entered a cloud, but there were no clouds and they would have been to low to have entered a cloud anyways. Almost full moon and no clouds at all. They just kind of shimmered for a second and then disappeared. Second "UFO" Ive seen this year and third one in my life.
