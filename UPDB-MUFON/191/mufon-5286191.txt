UFO silver disc object moving in a staight line and reasonable speed

Was driving home on the 05/08/2021 around 5:00pm(portugal time), and spotted, what I initially tought to be some of those dji drones; was driving in a straight line for some 500 meters only to realise that the object seemed to move parallel to my car and direction and was then I realised that the object was much far away and much higher; curiosity was building as I had already skipped the dji drone possibility, since it was for sure much bigger than a drone(by the distance it was I estimate that it was the size of a car); rapidly stoped the car, grabbed my phone and took 2 pictures on a moment that it was getting far away and even tought that I didnt make it to get on picture, only after zooming it cleary shows a circular metal looking object. This is also my first time to report something like this. This was spotted in Albufeira, Algarve, Portugal on a really bright day with no clouds whatsoever, and it really got me with goose bumps because its something there in the pictures that I cannot not explain. The movement seem to be about 70/80 km in a straight line till I lost it.
