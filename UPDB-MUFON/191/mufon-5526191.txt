bright white cylinder

At home,central NY, clear to partly cloudy sky, cool dry air, 65f. Looked out garage window, south, saw bight white cylinder shape, traveling from west to east. At first I thought it was airline traffic, went out for a better look, but no wings,strobes, or sound. It seemed to shift slightly to the south and gradually gain altitude, I lost it in the predusk sky not on the horizon. After it past it was a bright white dot, It faded as a point of white light. 
  Not a very spectacular sighting just wondering if anyone else saw it.
