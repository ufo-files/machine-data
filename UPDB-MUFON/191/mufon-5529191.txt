Close UFO Sighting!!!

I was outside on my porch of my apartment smoking a cigarette. I was looking straight up at the stars when it came into view. It was traveling south to north and gliding at a i would say 30 mph rate. I think it is safe to say that it was probably 200ft-300ft above me. It was a perfect triangle shape with 5 white protruding circles on the bottom ( one at the tip and 2 on each side of it. The underbelly was clearly visible from my postion. It glided by with NO SOUND what so ever! I lost it after 6-7seconds. When it disappeared i ran inside to report. I have never seen anything like that in my life!!! It scared me and shook me up pretty bad!!
