Giant craft, not an airliner, moving steadily, seen from airplane window.

Was returning home from Costa Rica, flying north possibly over Nicaragua at roughly 40,000 feet, when I saw what was a gigantic craft, not an airplane, as I had seen many airplanes on this trip and can positively identify an aircraft, moving slowly across the sky. It looked to be hundreds of feet across, about a mile long. I started taking photos of it as it moved across the sky. I kept trying to identify it, but the rate of speed and odd shape defied my identification ability. I have a series of 9 photos I took of the object. The pictures show it changing shapes. Not sure what it means.
