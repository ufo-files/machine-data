{rjl} 3 triangles flying together from east to west

I was on my patio deck looking east at approx. 1020 pm when I saw 3 red blinking lights heading towards my direction(west)... They were moving very slow but moving nonetheless... They came directly overhead and it was then that I could see that at least two of the objects were triangular in shape with 3 small red lights blinking on each corner and one single red light in the center. I couldn't make out what color the crafts bodies were but against the background sky I could see a definite triangular shape... The one furthest from me seemed to have the same configuration but I couldn't be quite sure... I estimated that the altitude of the one closest to me was probably between 500 to 1000 ft. If it was the size of a commercial airliner, then it would have been approx. 1000 ft. above me... The other two may have been higher in altitude, but not much... When the objects were approaching approx. a 45 degree angle headed towards me, I went in and grabbed my camcorder, however, my camcorder could not focus in on them. I watched the objects cross the entire sky from east to west until they were out of sight. I would suspect from the direction they were headed, they would have passed over the Sevierville/Pigeon Forge area. It took the objects about 4 minutes to cross the sky from one end to the other... They emitted no sound that I could detect...
