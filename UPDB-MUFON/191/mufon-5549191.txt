UFO Seen Over Wooded Area

It was late at night 10:03 pm. I guess it figured people were asleep. I had just walked out of my residence to remove my garbage. When I looked up right in front of me in the sky right above the tree line. We have a wooded area infront of my place. I saw three small red lights that resembled what stars look like far away. These were hovering, and moving over the tree line slowly the lights were in a triangle formation. Even though I saw for a moment a dark triangular craft within the lights when I focused on it more, the lights were just there but the craft itself was all of a sudden not visable. The lights proceeded to move out of my view over the tree line. Not long after that a group of jets was seen moving in the direction of the lights. I'am not much of an early night sleeper, and had most of my lights on, but it was as if it was watching me none the less. I usually get a bad sinking feeling when something is not right. I was stired up, but figured it could be a military craft, but then it does not make sense why it would be interested in that area. There is a lake below the trees. Nothing made me notice it I just happen to look up, and it was there.
