Driving South on Cole street. Noticed movement through top window of Jeep. Clear night, relatively no clouds at approx 8:15 pm. 4 translucent lights moved in a circle as if chasing each other about 2x then all 4 met in the middle one by one then dispersed back into circular chasing. Husband and I watched until we drove out of sight. Approx 6-8 months ago witnessed similar sighting facing towards Delphos area. That sighting was one light traveling in a circle for 2 hours. The lights in both were translucent and could see the clouds behind them and look projected from above the clouds but was not an advertising light. I am curious and kind of scared due to there being no explanation for either sighting.
