Donut shaped shinny silver object observed

My wife and I Saturday August 22, 2020 at approx 7 : 30 pm observed 2 shinny silver donut shaped objects in the sky with orange/red lights beaming. It was a clear day, blue skies, no clouds. It hovered slowly approx 5000ft in the sky approx half a mile away. We saw the objects slowly hovering in a direct path for approx 5 minutes, enough time to shoot these 5 photos with my point and shoot camera with zoom. Suddenly they were gone.
