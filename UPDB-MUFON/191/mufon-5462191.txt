{tjd} UFO Hovering Over Treeline and Buildings

I was pumping gas at a Kroger in Sandy Springs, Georgia off of Hammond Dr. by Sandy Springs Circle. I was surprised to see a UFO hovering over trees and buildings in the distance less than half a mile away. There was a black color to it, and it had a bulkier shape and outline to it than most other UFOs that I've seen before. It looked more like a German Foo Fighter from WWII than most other UFO's I've seen that have a slimmer and more aerodynamic profile to them. It only flashed there for a second it looked as if it's cloaking or what I've read about and studied online from Philip Schneider's talks of radar absorbent material painted on stealth aircraft and helicopters. It seemed as if that material was deactivated for a really small amount of time (approximately 3 seconds or less) and the UFO was visible for that time period. Nearly as quickly as it appeared, it vanished again into thin air. It was sunny and partly cloudy and was a pretty nice day weather wise. I looked around and didn't see any other UFOs. I was very astonished to see this and wondered what it was doing hovering in the near distance.
