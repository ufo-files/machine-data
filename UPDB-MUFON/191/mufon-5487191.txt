{nd} Taken from my bed

Tuesday night, I was laying in bed, watching YouTube videos. I couldn't sleep.
Suddenly, there was this bright light around me, and I couldn't move. The next thing I remember, I was in a room, with blindingly bright light. There were three beings. I could only see the tops of their heads through the blinding light. They were gray, and bald. One of them cut me open, and put his hand in my belly. He was either putting something in, or taking something out. The pain was absolutely excruciating. Every neuron was just screaming with agony. I've never felt anything so painful.
What I remember after that was being back on my bed, surrounded in the same light as when they took me. I couldn't move until the light faded. It wasn't that my body wouldn't respond, it was that there was some external force holding me still. Once the light faded, I realized that everything I had on me or in my hands was laid out next to me. Except for my pentacle necklace. That was still on my neck, hot to the touch. My blanket, which my OCD forces me to keep in a certain direction, was arranged the wrong way.
