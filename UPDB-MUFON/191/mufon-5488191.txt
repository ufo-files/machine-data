{cak} Followed us stopped when we stopped hovered outside building

We were leaving family members house when noticed object following us in sky. Stopped at party store to see if it would keep going. It stopped as well and hovered. When we got to our apartment building my husband went out to fields to look at it with binoculars. I went to neighbors and pounded on door. I could hear them inside but no one answered. The large light in foyer of apartment building fell to the floor breaking everywhere. No one came out it was if time was standing still. Went back to apartment and object was in front of balcony. It was light bulb shaped on its side with multi colored lights. My husband finally came back inside and to this day wont discuss what happened or what he saw in the binoculars. We had many other sightings in Michigan after that occurrence.
