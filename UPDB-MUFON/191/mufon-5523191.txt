I am a retired employee of the (F.A.A.), & have much knowledge of usual aircraft's altitude & speed. Of all my year's of looking up at the skies, this is the very first time I,ve ever seen anything like this.

At 7:30pm EST. I was standind outside in front of my door looking at the evening sky & star's when I witnessed an "OBJECT" cross the sky at an extremely high rate of speed from the north west---heading to the south east. Before it went out of site it made a 180 degree U-Turn & came back at me for about 3 to 4 sec's then disappeared/evaporated! I am a former (F.A.A.) employee, & this is the very first "OBJECT" I have ever seen after all the years I have been looking at the skies. The altitude was approximated at between 30,000 & 50,000 ft. It was hard to tell at the speed it was traveling at. (My front door face's due east.) Mike.
