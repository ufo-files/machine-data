{cak} Saw what appeared to be 3 stars that rotated clockwise in a triangle shape then move south parralel Ih35.

I was doing an account evening run about 20 miles south of Waco, Texas. At around 10:00 p.m. on June 28th, 2014 I looked up to the North Eastern sky and saw what appeared to be three stars rotating clockwise in the sky in a triangle formation. It hovered very slowly parralel Ih-35. I then saw it continue south following Ih-35. It then travelled out of site at a very fast rate. The object appeared to be at least a mile long though it could have been bigger. When I saw it passing it eclipsed the stars above it so I knew it was a solid object. It also didnt make any noise.
