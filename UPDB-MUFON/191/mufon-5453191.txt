Three orbs witnessed in and out of formation , then sped off in distance

Late October 2008, I got up for work just before 6 am. As we had small children and I was a smoker at the time, I went out to the back garden for a smoke with my cu of tea. Something white grabbed my attention overhead, I was a little confused at first, then realized these three orbs were flying between 500 and 1000 ft. overhead. they were in perfect equal lateral triangle so I thought it was a single craft with running lights. One orb then broke out of formation and then the three seemed to fly around at will. I watched this for a minute or two then the three sped off to the south I believe, at an accelerated speed and were out of sight shortly.  This has always played on my mind and I am just wondering if anyone else ever reported anything similar around that time. I have looked for videos on you tube over the years and have found a couple very similar to what I saw. One from an airplane with three orbs filmed out the window was almost exactly what I saw but I don't really know how to reference it for you. OK Thanks for your time.
