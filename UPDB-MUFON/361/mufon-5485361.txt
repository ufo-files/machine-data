{nd} 2 ball orbs flying at extreme speed with 1 zigzaging

I went out to the spa and watched 2 high speed orbs tracking each other heading se across the sky at high altitude. The orb to my right did 3 sharp zigzags in relation to the second and met back up with the other and they both kept going same direction. Then both curved south and sped up while curving to the south. They both then sped up and went out of our atmosphere. From watching high altitude jets I would say these where going probably 10 times there speed and all zigzagging was very precise and extremely fast with sharp turns. They went from horizon to horizon in about 15 seconds with a very definite change of direction.
