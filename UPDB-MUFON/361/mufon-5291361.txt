32 objects in tandem flight

At approximately 0620 this morning 10/25/20 the sky was very clear with numerous stars. The sun had not risen yet. I observe 32 objects in tandem, traveling in a straight line of flight from WNW to ENE. The fourth object made an abrupt left 15 degree turn and traveled in a more northerly direction. The remaining 28 objects were traveling, still in tandem, in a straight flight path. As they traveled further east I lost sight of them in the brighter sky. I observed these objects for 3 or 4 minutes.
