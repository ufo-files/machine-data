{jpg} 8 reddish lights, 2 horizontal rows of 4. Faded out on right, reappeared on left slowly. Then all gone and followed by 1 bright and much bigger red light that descended slowly.

While camping at Ocotillo Wells Vehicular Recreation Area, my wife, daughter, and I witnessed two horizontal rows of bright reddish-orange lights appear to the north, about 30 degrees above the horizon. We watched these lights for over an hour. During this time, the lights on the right would slowly fade completely out and then suddenly reappear on the left. Sometimes all of the lights would fade out and then reappear a few minutes later in a different spot to north. After over an hour, all of the lights disappeared. A few minutes later, a single, much larger and much brighter orange light appeared. This light hovered for at least 3 minutes,then descended very slowly. Just as the light looked like it was going to touch down, it faded completely out. During our sighting, none of the lights moved left or right at all. In the video I have attached, you can see the single orange light, although it is hard to make out. It is above and to the left of the headlights. It looks like it was blinking, but there was no blinking when I saw it. Sorry for the bad quality, but I tried to zoom in, but of course my phone wouldn't focus.
