Orange light crossing the sky and ascending observed during NYE fireworks

Soon after the NYE fireworks started, I noticed from the window of my 6th floor apartment a light well above the clouds and any other light, coming from north to south then apparently stopped for a few seconds and started to ascend into deep space. It took a minute and a half till I could not see it anymore.

I don't drink, don't use drugs and was well awake, so I'm sure that what I saw is indeed an UFO instead of a plane or rocket as planes don't behave like that and rockets are not allowed here. Fireworks don't behave like that either and can't go as high as that.
