{tjd} I witnessed a UFO on my way to work that evening

I don't remember the exact date/time of this occurrence since it was such a long time ago. But I will try my best. I left the house between 10pm-10:30pm that night. I was heading into town so I must have been on my way to work. I was an evening shift RN. Back then, there are no light poles from my residence until you are closer to town, so you are basically driving in the dark. I stopped at the red light (because I'm closer to town, I think there were light poles here) and was looking outside the window when I spotted a large object slowing coming towards me. I rolled down my window and stuck my head out and stared at it. It was a gigantic triangular shape object. It was approximately traveling about 5 mph. It was several feet above the light poles. I heard nothing at all. My car did not stop working. I can see bright white lights coming from the top of the object. It was so bright that it illuminated the shapes of the clouds in the night sky. I remember saying "wow" as it slowly passed me. I don't remember if I saw lights from the bottom of the object since this was so long ago, but I definitely remember the object and the lights shining upwards toward the sky. There was a truck driver who was also at the stopped light the same time as me, but I don't know for sure if he/she saw what I saw. But I'm pretty sure he/she saw it since I noticed the stop light turn green but neither of us drove off. I watched the object slowly fly from left to right. I have never seen anything like it before. I doubt I would have noticed the object if not for the bright light illuminating from the top of the object. I swear you can see the clouds like it was daylight as the object flew over me. I couldn't stay to watch it too long because I had to get to work. But it was amazing to see something like this object.
