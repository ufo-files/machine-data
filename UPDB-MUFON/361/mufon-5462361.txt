My daughter and I were sitting outside on a bright sunny day. She said mom what's that pointing in the sky. It looked like a very thick bright white distinct small cloud that was slowly gyrating and growing larger. I got my cell phone and took photos. It eventually morphed into what we both agreed looked like a fish or whale shape. We were both awestruck as neither of us had ever seen anything like that before. There were other clouds in the sky, normal puffy but Much less dense or firm looking as this one. The rest slowly moved with wind but this one stayed put. It really looked like marshmallow cream being shaped in the sky by invisible hands. It slowly dissipated or evaporated. We were both very puzzled and have yet to read of others who have experienced this. Lost the pics on my phone in the riv er unfortunately soon after..
