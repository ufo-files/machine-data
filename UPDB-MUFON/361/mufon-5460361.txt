Was out, like most nights, looking at the sky, and stars. Something caught my eye, 45* and NNE of Cleveland, Ohio. Couldn't tell if object was over northern Lake Erie, or southern Canada. I kept watching it travel on a perfect straight line moving west to east. It appeared to be not as bright as some stars, with a yellow/ white light that pulsed every 10 seconds or so. The light pulse only lasted 1 1/2 seconds. I watched it wearing my distance glasses for about 2 minutes, until it faded out of sight, into the NE part of my viewing area. Sky's clear, 49*. Many stars viewable. Never saw any object that propelled itself through the sky and only flashed a light every 10 seconds. A commercial airliner flashes red/green lights, has constant white lights visible.
