{nd} Riding in passenger seat of car on gravel road when to my right it was hovering above an old farm house.

I was riding in the passenger seat of a car on a gravel road outside of linden Michigan around 8pm I can't remember the date but it was between 1993 and 1997 when I had a strange feeling I looked out the passenger side window. Hovering over a farm house seemed maybe 50feet above the house a large black object shaped like a boomerang I would estimate it being between two and three football fields long and did not move or make any sounds. I asked my mother who was driving what that was. We stopped for a few moments and it never moved never made a sound. It seemed to have three round bubbles on the bottom of the object as if you were to take a ball cut it in half and stick it to a flat surface. I could see the object very clearly.Finally fear swept over us both and we drove off I watched as we drove away it never seemed to move. I had dreams about the object and aliens for the rest of my childhood. Weeks later my family was watching tv and they were talking about the exact same object being seen across Michigan and parts of Wisconsin.
