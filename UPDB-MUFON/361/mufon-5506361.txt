police chased it

I was in a car driving on a mountain road in wales uk with my 2 children and 2 other adults late one summers evening around 11pm in 1992/93/94 one of those years. the mountain was very big, with a huge drop into the valley on our right side, the left side was mountain cliffs,not a lot of space for trees or bushes to grow. it was hot and we had the windows open. we had passed no other traffic for quite awhile. its a very isolated road and it goes on forever and its very difficult to drive in the darkness. all of a sudden a craft with no noise came up from the valley just in front of us,lots of lights. it went over the top of our car and we lost sight of it as we went round the mountain. if someone was standing on the roof of the car im sure it could have been touched. i carry on driving round this mountain swearing as im doing so,scared silly its either going to come back for us or i will drive off the road into the valley below. in a matter of moments quite a few police cars go whizzing past with their lights flashing towards where we left this craft. they was after something for sure. we had no lost time that we know of,the engine didn't stop. we just carried on going and was very relieved to come across a village with street lights and we all went "phew". some days later i ask the other adults to draw this craft. we all drew the same, as it was so close all we could see was a formation of the lights in a diamond sort of pattern,all agreed as to how close it was and all agreed there was no noise and the sparse trees didn't move any ore than when a car went past.
