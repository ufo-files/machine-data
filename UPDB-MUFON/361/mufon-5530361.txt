There were about seven bright lights in the sky sitting still then one by one started moving away.

i was driving to the store and saw about seven bright lights in the north sky ranging in size. at first i thought they were airplanes but i know airplanes cant be that close together. they were all moving around but staying in the same general area of the sky and i was thinking maybe they were helicopters looking at an accident maybe, but they were too high in the sky. Then as i kept driving some went away. then i went to a hill to watch them and there were two, and they stood still for about ten minuets, and i thought they weere just telephone poles. then the one on the left got very big and bright, then the light got really small and started flying away to the rightand it really freaked me out. about three minuets after the first light started moving the other light started to follow it, and they both dissapeared in the distance.
