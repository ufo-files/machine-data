{tjd} Translucent wing like or boomerang object spotted at night headed straight line due south.

Was talking on the phone on my back deck looking up at the sky and just happened to make out a very faint object. I thought it was a gliding bird at first but did no flapping or moving in circle patterns as bird usually will do. Like I said I could barely make the shape out, a gray almost see through wing object headed south at a steady rate. No lights. I see plenty of airplanes cross this area of view and it definitely was not that. I then saw another one, or the same, come from the west going East, same rate of speed and looked the same. No sound. I waited around to try and explain it away but they didn’t come back.
