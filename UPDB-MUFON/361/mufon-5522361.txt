Orb slowly going dim and then a bright light flashed then disapeared then reapeared a little to the left

I was playing hide and seek (i know it's childish but we were bored) we ran to the end of the field we was playing it in whilst me and my 2 friends was looking for them i looked into the sky and quite far away there was a sort of orb in the sky it would start dim and them it would get brighter and brighter then flash and then go dim and disapear then it would reapear a bit to the left were it first disapeared and then reapeared and done the same thing as it first did it kept repeating. It done it roughly around 14 times before it disapeared behind some trees. When i first saw the object i thought it was a plane but then i thought to myslef "planes don't hover and they certainly don't pulsate like that" then i pointed it out to my friends and we tried to figure out what it was, the only thing we could describe it as was a lighthouse even though we do not live no where near the sea so that was ruled out.When i first saw the object i was scared but then i soon calmed down as i did not want to miss it in case it was something serious. I lost sight of the object as it moved to far to the left we moved a bit to the right to keep sight of it but it moved to far by then and it vanished behind trees.
