Disk-like. Sun reflected off the top. curved bottom. hovered and then diappeared

I was in the passenger seat of my father's car, heading east. I was looking up into the sky and that was when I saw this object. The sun was reflecting of the top of it and I saw a curved bottom. It hovered there, but not for very long. I covered my eyes but the glare was shining into them. At the time I thought it might have been a plane, but the object was too far south to heading for the airport. When I uncovered my eyes this whatever-it-was was gone. I never saw which way it went, but the sky seemed empty.
Later, talking about it, my father said he never saw it. Because, he said, he was looking at the road because he was driving.
