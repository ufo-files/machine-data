{nd} Bright object in sky dances for an hour over Castle Rock/ Sedalia

I saw the UFO out my window close to 11pm. It appeared as a star first then I realized it was not when it started zig zagging in the sky. I called a friend who also observed this activity with his girlfriend out their window in the city of Highlands Ranch.
