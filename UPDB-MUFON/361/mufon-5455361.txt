Multiple moving lights appearing then levelling off in easterly direction.

At approx 0515 am on 18/02/2020, I left home for work and stopped at my garden gate, facing south, and noticed a single white star like object moving from west to east at about 45degree elevation, there was no sound or lights associated with an aircraft. The morning was clear and cold with no cloud and a clear moon slightly above the object. As I followed the objects path east I notice another object to my left above my house to the north appear, less clear at first then as bright as the first object and of similar size. To my surprise, another object appeared to drop from the same area of the sky 5 or 6 seconds later, became as bright as the other 2 and followed the direction to the east. I then saw the same event occur from the same area of sky above my home and counted 25 objects all the same size at approx same distance apart move in almost the same path / /line / speed, in an easterly direction. I watched as they moved off for 5 more minutes, into the distance, no sound. I have no idea what the objects were but they just appear one by one changed slight direction and followed each other ? This is a genuine account of what I saw and would be very grateful for a logical explanation of what it was. I’m not sure if my phone would have picked up any image of this but I really regret not having the foresight to quickly try. Hope this helps in some way.
