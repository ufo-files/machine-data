{cak} Huge craft over Douglas Cty

On March 27, 2018, about 10;30 PM we were out in the front of our yard in Scottsburg, Oregon when we saw 3 lights coming towards us from the east and we could hear a humming. It was not moving fast enough to be a plane based on the size. It had 3 white lights and one red light. One white light was at the front and the other 2 were it seemed like 300 ft behind it in a triangular pattern. The red light was between the middle of the back 2 red. It flew right over our heads , looked black and had a low humming and roaring sound. It continued to go over us towards the Oregon coast about 20 miles from us. I have never seen any planes over in that area since there is no major airports and it was flying level which looked to be 1/2 miles or more up on the air. It was odd that it was going straight for the ocean

We lost it when it when over the western horizon. We took a video of it and you can see the red light and hear the noise it made.

We all looked at each other and no one knew what it was and that no one had ever seen anything like it before.
