large oval white object came from behind far treeline crossing north to south over western clayton county with flashing light on bottom, far too large to be airplane from that distance

I was sitting on the backporch, which faces east towards clayton county, when i saw a large bright light behind the far treeline.....it was much farther than the trees, and they have to be a couple miles away...when i first saw it behind the trees i thought it was the moon but it was moving wayyyy too fast for that...it looked to the eye at that distance to be about the size of a quarter moon as it looks to the eye......huge, brilliant white light, with a darker color light at bottom...it moved in a totally straight level line from north to south at a constant speed..im not sure if it was shifting colors or strobing, the one on the bottom, that is....it happened so fast.....because of the size it seemed to move slow, but the time it took to cross all the way out of sight was faster than the planes we see every night move.....it was just huge....i called my friend out to see, and she saw it as it continued to move towards the south until the sight was totally blocked....unfortunately i am the only one who saw it in the clearing, but she can testify as to the size of it moving beyond the treeline......i lost sight when it went too far south to see the light for the trees blocking the view....
