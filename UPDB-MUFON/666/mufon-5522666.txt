Chevron-shaped light near Jupiter

I was outside with my daughter, looking at the stars as we do most nights. We have noted Jupiter before and have never seen what we saw tonight. There was an object near Jupiter that was just as bright, but shaped almost like the arrow-style cursor on most computers. It sparkled and seemed to undulate back and forth. At times there would be a red light, then it would almost elongate to a straight line. There was occasional cloud cover, but the object still shined brightly through the clouds, almost like it was in the clouds. Other stars did not shine as brightly.
