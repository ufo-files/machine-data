150 foot diameter, silent soft blue glowing craft

Wife and I were sitting outside, her head in my lap looking up. She exclaimed “what’s that?” I looked up to my left and saw a silent disk shaped objects partially and faintly illuminated pale blue lower surface slightly protruding from the low level overcasting cloud cover. Cloud floor was @ 300-500 feet AGL. It transitioned from ESE to WNW @ approximately 30 MPH directly over our heads.
