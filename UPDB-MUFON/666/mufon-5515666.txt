(GS) 3 flashing bright various light colored discs flying in formation

was standing outside watching the beautiful nights sky. saw a helicopter, then a commercial plane. Then about 5 minutes later 3 objects that appeared disc shaped flew by from me heading south then appeared to change to SE direction. They flew in a wedge or V shaped formation with one in the front. They stopped then made a few passes over the tree line, then regained formation and flew away into the night. They came in FAST, then slowed big time. As they passed you could see them at an angle to where you could see the under-side and how the lights were emitting randomly in a sphere shape, then one brightly shaped light in the middle(like a bulls-eye) that was a white/pink that was really bright. I have been seeing a lot of things like this lately. Need to get a video camera set up... ASAP.
