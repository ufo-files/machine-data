Fast moving object accross daylight sky

I saw a bright white flashing light, Moving swiftly across the daylight sky as I was driving to work. I lost track of it because the car in front of me almost ran over a squirrel and I had to swerve. When I was on the 401 going eastbound towards Toronto, I briefly saw it flashing and then it disappeared into the sky. I work near Pearson airport and have never seen anything like that. When I 1st saw it in my mind I remember it being resembling a triangle but not 100% sure. It moved really swiftly across the sky with a pulsating white light.
