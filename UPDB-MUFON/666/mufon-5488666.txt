{jpg} 2 bright red lights, appearing to have shape of a sphere, flew overhead during lunar eclipse. East to Northwest.

This event was witnessed in our front yard, watching the lunar eclipse on 9/27/2015. Just as the moon was going dark, a bright red light appeared in the east and flew across the sky above us. I attempted to video this event, which was 10:07 pm EST, but failed. 5 minutes later at 10:11 pm EST, another one appeared, following the same path. This time I was able to record the entire event. It is shaky, but I was able to zoom in for part of it. The images are grainy but you can see something in the video. It was hard to tell just how big these objects were, or how far away they were, but the both were identical and followed the same path, from East to Northwest. They made no sound, and there were airplanes in the area at the time, so for reference against the airplanes they were brighter and appeared either larger or closer, it was hard to tell. They appeared to have a spherical shape, and the grainy video shows a pixelated sphere when paused at the point where it was in zoom. There were partial clouds so it is hard to determine if the objects were under the clouds or not. They were very bright and flew in a straight unwavering path. They made no sound, and didn't have any flashing lights. They passed the entire length of the visible sky in just over 1 minute. The video is 1 minute and 4 seconds long, and I probably missed about 10 or 15 seconds of this event while trying to get my camera working. It was fortunate that there were 2 of these, and I got the opportunity to record the second time. We waited around for a while to see if any more would appear, but none did. The objects both flew out of view towards the NW horizon, but became too small to see before they reached it. We temporarily lost visual when they were obstructed by a tree, but could then see them vanish in the distance.
