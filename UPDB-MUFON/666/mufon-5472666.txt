{dr}Looked like a dim star moving fast

I witnessed the object moving East to West. It was Halloween night and I was handing out candy. I looked up in the sky and saw it moving pretty fast. It wasn't a plane or shooting star because there were planes in the area. It was far high past the planes. It looked like a star moving. There were 7 witnesses. The first object went East to West then disappeared. It was witnessed for at least a minute. There was another one about 5 minutes later same shape and same height. This one moved South to North. At one point it shot to the right quickly and continued to move North. I was really excited witness this event.It lasted about 3 minutes. All witnesses saw the second object.
