A large star like object traveling from Northwest to Southeast no sound.

I was inside watching tv when my oldest son Josh who is eleven came running inside saying there was a strange light in the sky. Me and my wife and other two children went outside to see what he was talking about. And there in the Northwest was a star like object moving at a slow rate of speed from Northwest to Southeast in a straight path. The object had no blinking lights and no sound. We watched the object disappear over the tree tops.
