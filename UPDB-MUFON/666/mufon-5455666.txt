{rjl} I saw an unknown craft pass behind the sun.

I was at the park enjoying the setting. As i was walking towards the water, I happen to look up at the sky towards the sun. As i watched for a few seconds I noticed an aircraft about to pass my view. I was stunned and confused when I saw the aircraft pass behind the sun vs in front. I have never told anyone what I witnessed because I was afraid. The aircraft was large enough to see from the ground and it clearly went out of view until it passed to the other side.
