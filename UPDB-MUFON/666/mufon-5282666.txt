Tic Tac shape,white in color,moving at the speed of a small plane with no sound

I was outside cleaning the rain gutters on my garage. I was on the south side facing north, I was moving my ladder and looking up at gutters to see where I was going to place the ladder and that’s when I saw it going across the sky. The speed it was going and the lack of any sound is what caught my attention. I pulled out my phone and started recording, I was walking around to the front of my garage to keep it in sight so that I wouldn’t lose it in the tree that was behind my garage. I recorded it until it got out of sight. I recorded and reported an object the looked exactly like this one going in the same direction on February 29,2020. I also recorded and reported a similar object on May 7,2021 traveling in a north east direction about 2 1/2 miles south of my home. In my first recording I was standing only about 20-25 feet west from where I was when I recorded this one.
