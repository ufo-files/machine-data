As my wife and I were driving north on Interstate 35 toward Mason City, Iowa exit 190, I looked out the window to the east and saw this cylindrical silver object following us. I told my wife what I was seeing and kept my eye on it. It slowly turned to the east away from us and I watched it quickly disappear out of view. When I first saw it I wondered if it was an airplane, but when it turned I saw no wings. It was quite spectacular for me to say the least.
