hourglass shape

Driving home after broadcasting a basketball game on the evening of January 8th, I was was driving down my street heading east.. I saw thru the trees, what look like a very, very bright light. and this is what got my attention.. I drove past the trees and saw what looked like two very bright light, one on top of another, almost in an hourglass shape.. I did not notice any movement.. I do not think it was venus, or any planet, and have not seen this light or lights since. But, again, it was the vividness, brightness, that got my attention at first.. I am somewhat of a skeptic in outer-worldy beings, but this sure got me thinking.. I am 47 years old, work in radio, and have never seen anything like this before..
