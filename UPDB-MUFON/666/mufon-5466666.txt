flying in the sky of several objects

I received a phone call from my mother about the fact that she sees several flying points in the sky. When I looked at the sky, I saw that a group of about 10-15 objects like a satellite flying in the night sky was moving along the starry sky. A group of objects walked along the sky crossing the constellation Cygnus between the stars Deneb and Sandra. Next, she relayed the constellation of the Northern Crown.
1 It attracted attention that the group of objects moved rectilinearly rather quickly, the objects inside the group changed their location relative to each other. They then stretched in a line, then gathered in a swarm.
Without changing directions, the objects moved in a straight line and disappeared from sight, losing their brightness.
1 The objects were white-blue, had not very high brightness (less than the brightness of the main stars of the constellations) and there was a feeling that they were moving in some kind of gas cloud.
