Sighting above los banos d dam around 1995

Sighting above los banos d dam around 1995. approximately around midnight me and three of my friends were camping and noticed a strange light floating above the dam . as we got out of the tent to get a better look we noticed it was moving around the sky really goofy from north south east west. I was trying to make out the shape of it but the light coming from the bottom of it made it really hard to tell . after a few minutes of it bouncing around the sky all of a sudden it shot over the mountains to the west faster than the speed of light. no sound it was gone in a split second . we were probably in 8th or 9th grade at the time i didnt know much about ufos at that time but after seeing it move that fast with no sound i new that it was something weird .
