{td} Seen unusual light in sky after shuttle launch

1) I was on my back porch taking a photo of the shuttle Discovery launch. 
2) I didn't really notice it until I looked at the photos. 
3) At first I thought it was light reflection off of the shuttle but Discovery cannot be seen in the photos. Only the plume from the engines. 
4) The object is in the shape of an old fashioned door keyhole. I took 3 photos and it appears to take shape from a bright blob in the sky to keyhole shape. In the original photo there is an airplane heading west and this object dwarfed it.
5) I was just wondering what it was that I got a lucky photo of.
6) I didn't see the object until I looked at the photos on my camera. It was already gone when I went back outside. I darkened two of the photos to see if I could get a better idea of what I might have been looking at. Still don't know what it might be.
