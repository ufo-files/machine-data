Oddly behaving light

close to 930 or so i stepped outside to smoke and turned to see a peculiar lightin the north sky. the stars weret completely visible at that point. for severalseconds i watched a bright light descend slowly and then stop at a point asubstantial several degrees lower from where i first spotted it. while i staredat it, i thought for a moment to run inside and get somebody. in that instantthe light, as best i could tell, either turned off or sped away in the oppositedirection very fast. i lived in the clear-sky hill country for years. i knowwhat a shooting star looks like and this certainly wasnt one. it wasnt asatellite or a plane. i truly don't know what it was.
