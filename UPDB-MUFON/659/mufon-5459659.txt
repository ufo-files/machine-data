{jpg} Red light appeared out out nowhere i started video recording on my phone. It came closer to me, slowed, hovered and then sped away. 3 sep. times

I was standing in my backyard at 10 pm looking at the stars through the camera lens on my phone. Suddenly, I noticed a bright, red, round light appear out of the southeast. The light looked as though it was only a few hundred feet high and seemed about half a mile away. I began video recording it on my phone.  It moved quicker than I realized at the time, and within a few seconds it had made it’s way towards me and seemed to hover about 100 foot away and much lower. It flew right above the rooftop of the church that is aprox. 5 yards from my backdoor. It went behind a tree on it’s slow path and once it was observable again it moved incredibly far and fast away to the Northeast. About 10 minutes later, I went back outside and the same scenario happened again for a 2nd and even 3rd time! I was able to video all three occurrences but at this point I was FREAKED out. i wasn’t sure what it was, the actual size, or the actual distance away from me at the time or even to this day. I assumed it was a drone being flown by a hobbyist in the neighborhood or law enforcement. I told my dad about it today (a week and a half later) and showed him one of the videos and he encouraged me to share it. He’s a respected military vet and is pretty confident that WE ARE NOT ALONE!!! :). Hope this helps the study! ✌🏻💖
