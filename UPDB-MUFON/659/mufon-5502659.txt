Came out of neighbor's house about 11pm with 3 other people. We were facing South. The other lady said look up in the sky. There were 3 bright orange lights lined up horizontally. They looked like they were watching us. We had never seen anything like this. We watched a short time. Then I ran into the house to get other people to come and look. At this point the lights took off swiftly and without sound. It was a cloudy night and we could see an orange light behind the clouds moving up higher and higher toward the northeast. I was happy other people saw this as it felt unreal.
