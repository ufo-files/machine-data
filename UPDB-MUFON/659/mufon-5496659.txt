{rjl} Blimp / Oval Shaped Craft

I was walking my dog around 7 PM CST, it was a very clear starry night. I was observing some stars, and all of a sudden a white or beige oval shaped or blimp shaped object flew across the sky (straight line) at a very fast speed No sound, no trail, no other witnesses (or at least I don't think so). I have seen a UFO before, so it wasn't extremely shocking or anything. But this thing came in fast and left fast.
