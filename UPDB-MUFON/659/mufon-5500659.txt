UFO in Tbilisi, Georgia

I'm an American living in the Georgian capital of Tbilisi. This evening, I saw an extremely unusual object ascend from the trees on a hillside above the Saburtalo area. The object ascended slowly, in an extremely unusual manner, it then disappeared over a hillside.

I thought at first that the object could possibly be fireworks, but it hovered in the air and then began moving sideways. There was no flashing light - if I were to describe what it looked like, i'd say that it resembled a glowing snow globe. It could have been ball lightning - as the weather here has been extremely unusual lately. 

I have a clear view of this area of Tbilisi, and i've never seen anything like this. It couldn't have been a helicopter, because there's no where to land in the area. It didn't ascend like a firework, as it seemed to hover and then move horizontally, before gradually ascending over a hill. I watched as it pasted over a hill, and I watched it disappear as it seemed to descend the other side of the hill.

I watched this thing for maybe 10 seconds - I had my camera close by, but unfortunately I didn't think fast enough to grab it.
