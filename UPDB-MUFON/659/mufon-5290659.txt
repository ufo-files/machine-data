Observed large bright light fly/hover above road

I observed a very large bright light on Skyway Rd on my way home to Paradise from Chico California. It was raining really hard and there was patchy fog. I had just passed the Paradise Rod & Gun Club by approximately 1 mile. There was a vehicle about 5 to 6 car lengths behind me. I initially thought when I saw the light that there was a wrong way driver because the light appeared to be moving at a fast rate of speed toward my vehicle down Skyway Rd .

The light had a bluish tint kinda similar to blue headlights but brighter, larger, and appeared to only be one. I remember the light got really bright then turned sharply to my right (NE). I don't recall hearing anything, it appeared to be hovering like possibly a giant drone? I noticed right after the light turned there was no longer a vehicle behind me. I know for a fact I texted my partner at 6:49pm as I was leaving the Rayleys in Chico CA.  I arrived home after 8pm. I know because my partner was concerned about me driving in the bad weather.

I have searched for possible information/explanations. I feel it's very likely to be related to either a huge drone or something military. We have several AFB near us that fly aircraft over us frequently. 

I haven't been able to sleep much. I keep trying to go back in my mind to break it down but it happened so fast and the weather was so bad it's hard to say for sure what it could have been. I know it wasn't a car, helicopter, or flashlight. I know it wasn't the radio tower either since I saw the blinking tower light in the distance in the same direction the light went in. I don't know what it was, I hope the other person on the road that night (if they saw it too) reports it as well because maybe they know or saw more than I did. If I had to guess how close it came to my vehicle I would say no more than a 1/4 mile but visibility also was not great so I have no clue how accurate that is.
