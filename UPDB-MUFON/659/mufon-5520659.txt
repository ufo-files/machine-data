F-16 jets following or warning UFO

I live in Jesup Ga. 20 miles outside of Ft. Stewart military base. On Febuary 7, 2012 my son and i heard jets from inside our home around 2:00 P.M. They were very loud and i could tell by the sound unusually low. My son and i went outside to see the jets. We observed them flying in a circular formation. They stayed within about a three mile circular pattern of our home. We continued to watch them for appoximatly an hour and seventeen minutes. During this time my son and i took some pictures to show my wife. I didn't realize untill later i had captured on camera an object near the jet. Perhaps it was moving too fast for the naked eye to see. It is blurry compared to the F-16 but definitly was there. I only wish i would have had a better camera than my camera phone, however it is still easily seen due to flying so low.This is my first sighting of such an unusual event and thought that i should share it.
