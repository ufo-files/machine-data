Orange Orb over Pa

This dropped down and got huge. Then shot up and slowly disappeared. It then came back a few minutes later with 2 white lights. It made wild moves and hovered at various points. When you zoom in, it looks more like a disk but the light was so bright. We watched it for 15 min.
