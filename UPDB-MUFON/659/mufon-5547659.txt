I saw two alternating red lights that turned into a straight string on approx.15 red lights moving in the night sky.

As I was heading home from my work in Albany to just outside of Moran where I live, approx 5 miles outside of Moran I saw two red lights that I first thought looked like flares. I could distinctly see it was two lights, then they disappeared and then reappeared approx 2-3 minutes later. There were two red lights flashing on and off similar to a red light passing behind a picket fence. They were traveling in an easterly direction, moving slowly and steadily with the lights flashing on and off while traveling in a slow steady straight movement. I watched the lights trying to determine what they were and then approx 30 seconds to a minute later the two lights turned into a row of approx 15 non-blinking lights. I only saw this row of lights for approx 2-3 seconds and then the lights disappeared. I am not saying this was a space ship, it was just something I had never seen before. We do have many military planes fly across our home often and we are located approx 50 miles from Dyes Airforce Base in Abiline, but I have never seen anything like this before and was unsure of where to report this sighting. I wish I would have had my video camera with me but I did not so I have no pictures of what I witnessed. If you do find out what these lights were, could you please contact me as it has really peaked my curiosity.
