Hovering Cylinder w/ upright Nose Cone

The 1967 Solar Event off the Pacific was not a Solar Event.

I looked for years, but found the information in a book, I believe, from Leslie Kean.

It was a non-directional flash of light. Like an electrical spark.

Minutes later, an object entered our home in Silicon Valley. I became separated from my two family members that were present. 

It was a hovering cylinder (Stainless Steel w/ rectangular solar panels in a step pyramid layout) with a nose cone (bright dull anodized aluminum - platinum colored) that hovered about 4 feet from the ground and moved fast and in straight lines. It came up a short flight of stairs maintaining it’s distance from the ground and only turned in 45 degree angles. Could have reached the same spot faster going on the diagonal. 

When I turned to view it - I was rendered unconscious. When I awoke, I was some distance away and running to find my missing family members. When found, I rendered was unconscious again and awoke the following morning - as if nothing happened.

The device resembled a retrofitted last generation Mac Pro. Without it casing - the top is bevelled and ready to receive a nose cone.

Time was altered. I actually believe time has been being augmented for how long is unknown. As if in a loop.

I was three and a half years old.
