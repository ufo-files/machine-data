{dr}triangle event over madera ca. the heart of california

On the morning of Dec 14 between 1 & 1:30 A.M. I was outside observing the Geminid Meteor shower. I was facing a northward direction, when all of a sudden. a triangle craft came flying out of my right side. The craft had faint redish lights surrounding the craft, except for the rear. The speed of the craft, I would say was roughly around 50mph. travelling from an, east to north west direction. It only lasted for 6 sec or so. It was very low also.
