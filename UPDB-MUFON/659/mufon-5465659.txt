{dr}Continual rolling thunder sound.  Lasted for 20 minutes.  Heard similar soun before in about May. Thunderstorm in area. This was something else.

It was about 5:30 in the afternoon.  Staying in a house in a quiet neighborhood Denver area.  About I-25 & S Colorado Blvd. I was in the kitchen cleaning up.  Kept noticing low rumble, like thunder but this had been going on for over 5 minutes. Thunderstorm had moved into the area. 
  Curious, I went out on the patio.  Rumbling more noticable now.  Light rain.    Strange that the sound didn't let up.  I heard something similar back in about May this year. None of the people I mentioned it to had heard anything.  I put it in the back of my mind and forgot about it until hearing it again on this day.
    I set smart phone up to record sound. Doesn't do what I heard justice.  Sounds like wind blowing.  Which it was, but there was also the rumbling.  About 15 minutes into the sound the rain became torrential for about 5 minutes. I love the rain, but this was pretty intense. I could still hear the rumbling through the rain. 
 The rain let up and eventually (after about 20 minutes )the sound faded.  Strange.
