Firey pulsating lights over the hills

I work as a parachute rigger in casa grande Arizona. For a couple weeks. Almost every night. Bright lights appear and disappear on the horizon in front of my house. These lights look like fire from a distance. They are yellow orange. Usually one will appear then others up to a mile away . Somtimes they just hover. Somtimes one will hover while another travels horizontal while disappearing then reappearing almost instantly much farther ahead. I spend alot of time in planes and around aviation in general.
