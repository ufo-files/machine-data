(GS) The largest object with no sound with 3 sets of lights that I have never seen before!

I was standing on the side of my house having a cigarette and I was looking at stars as I do every night becuse I am bored. I see planes, helicopters, satilights(sp) and other craft all the time. I suddenly saw red/white sort of pulsating lights, then another set, but then another set and they were in a oval/triangle shape. It was so strange that after about 2 minutes I ran inside and grabbed my mother and she came out and at first thought it was a plane until the entire object came out from behind the treeline. She freaked out as well and ran across to get some neighbors. They came out a little too late as the object went futher off. They were only able to one or two sets of lights.

The object moved from the western side of my house all the way to the eastern side of my house in a half circle motion, not straight across the sky. I lost sight once it went too far east and the pine trees were in the way over the big lake in Boiling Sping Lakes.
