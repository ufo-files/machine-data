Red/white/green blinking stationary lights over Lake Michigan in triangle

For two nights in a row off the Lake Michigan shore we observed a stationary star like object blinking white, red and green lights. The first night there was one object, tonight three in a triangle shape. They were there several hours and seemed to just float in the air. Very unusual, brighter than a star and definite colors.
