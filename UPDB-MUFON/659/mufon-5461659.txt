{tjd} Observed a star looking object in daylight that would become very bright, then dim, then disappear, then repeating this pattern for about 5 minutes.

At Approximately 7:20PM I was in Johns Creek, GA looking at an airplane leaving a vapor trail as it passed by what looked like a star at about 120 degrees SE and about 50 degrees above the horizon. The sun was still up and shining brightly, there was no cloud cover apparent. It was so small I probably never would have noticed it had it not been for the plane leaving the vapor trail, but it was behaving strangely, in that it would get very bright for a second, then dim out and then disappear. A few seconds later, it would repeat that pattern. It appeared stationary in the sky for about 5 minutes repeating this pattern of extremely bright, to dim, and then disappearing.
