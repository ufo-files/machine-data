{rjl} In the north sky a long line of orbs all going the same direction. About 1000 orbs

We just got home from our religious place and I was exiting the car. In the north sky from Cavalier Dr I saw a long straight line of these orbs in the sky all moving in the same direction very close to each other. I called my dad over to see, he couldn’t believe his eyes it was about 200 orbs all moving across the sky. My mom came late when she saw only about 10 of them flying and then they disappeared in the night sky I guess. For a moment I thought maybe it was a blimp, like one of those “will you marry me” signs people get to fly across the sky but it was night. Almost about midnight. this was not a blimp or a sign. They were lights!! Probably about 200 lights all flying individually right by each other in a straight line. It looked like that one event back in 2001 I believe it’s called “the Phoenix Lights” where there were lights in the night sky all moving together and no one could explain what it was. What we saw was exactly that!! My father said that maybe it’s a huge mother ship and those lights are the lights from outside the ship like from the movies. But this was just so scary and there was no explanation for it!
