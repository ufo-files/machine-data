{dr}A quick sighting

I had just got done with my dogs walk and we were at the door on the west side of the house at the top of the ramp. I am about to put my key in the lock when I look to my left and see the UFO seemingly either over or just behind the Fort Wayne Museum of Art going in an easterly direction not very fast about 20 mph. I quickly grab my camera and start filming. It lasted for maybe 5 or so seconds as it eventually went out of sight behind the AT&T building on Berry st. behind where I live. It looked to be going in the direction of behind the downtown Fort Wayne Fire Dept. or more toward the Three Rivers Apt. .
