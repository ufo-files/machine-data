{jpg} Multiple objects in shape of boomerang

Standing outside in driveway looking at the sky when I noticed a boomerang shape group of lights in the sky. They where in the shape of the phoenix lights almost but they looked to be individual objects flying in formation. The objects where quite dim but flying at a high rate of speed (was only visible for less than 15 seconds as it flew directly overt) going south to north, and seemed to be at a very high altitude. There where probably around 30 of the lights, no noise was being made. At first I thought it was a shooting start but after noticing it's shape and the amount of objects, it was quite a shock to say the least. There was also a plain visible but they did not seem to be corralated, there was also a satellite visible that was flying from the north east to south west and this also did not seem to be corralated with the objects However the satellite flew close to the objects and they seemed to be at similar altitude. I lost sight of the objects as they flew over the northern horizon.
