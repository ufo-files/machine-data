unkown vortex

while flying from Pennsylvania to Florida last June, my wife took some photos of the wing of the plane. In one of the pictures there is an unexpected vortex that appears to be ahead of the wings leading edge and at some distance from the plane. the vortex appears to be heading into the distance at a ninty degree angle to the direction of the planes travel. this siting took place at some altitude and around three thirty in the afternoon. as is apparent in the photo the skies were clear (no clouds) and there doesn't seem to be any other craft in the area. She said she did not see the vortex herself until looking at the pictures later. the picture was taken with a cannon, powershot A720IS digital camera, 8.0 megapixel w/6x optical zoom. it is puzzling because i would expect to see a vortex behind the wind in the slip stream, espeasially at the wing tip.
