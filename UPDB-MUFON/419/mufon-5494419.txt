Appeared coming towards me but very high in sky, passing rapidly overhead.

I was walking towards Wilcox Road in Sheffield, UK when this object caught my eye. Luminescent lime green egg shaped, very fast and quiet. Appeared high in sky but lower than a passenger jet and much quicker. Made no sound. Saw the same thing again next day travelling in the same direction so could have been something on an orbit. Not saying it was alien but this sighting has played on my mind for years.
