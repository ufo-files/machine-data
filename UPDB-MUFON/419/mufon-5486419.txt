Orb like light

I was parking my car. The sky was dark and clear, full of stars, a place were i often can see starlights, so i was looking up in the sky waiting four a starlight. And then i saw this bright light from west 30 degrees above the horizon, like a plane but 3 times brighter and without positionlights and it was moving a bit faster then all planes that cross my house. So i lose sight in 35 degrees east above horizon. Now i watched it zoomed in before i wanted to delete the film. I can't explain what it was. Maybe somebody saw anything like this before.
