(gs) orange hovering light

At approx 10:15 pm I saw a yellowish /orange glowing light in the southwest sky above Emmett Idaho about 5-10 minutes before the fire works show started at first I thought it was a plane but the light was too large and did not appear to move whatsover after observing the light for 10 minutes I looked away for a about a minute and when I looked back up the light was gone! I searched the entire sky and saw no trace of it.
