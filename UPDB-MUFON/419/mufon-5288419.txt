Large Orb (not visible to naked eye) caught on camera when taking photos of the sunrise over Lake Michigan on April 17th

These photos were taken on April 17, 2021 approximately 15 seconds apart from one another with the first at 7:18am and the second at 7:19am. I was staying at Terrace Bay Hotel in Gladstone, Michigan which is located right on the Northern Shores of Lake Michigan. I walked out towards the water behind the hotel to see the sunrise, I was only outside for approx. 5 minutes during which time I snapped these two pictures of the sunrise, I did not see anything notable in the sky when taking these pictures. It wasn't until I looked at them later that I saw in the second photo what appeared to be an orb. I sent both pics to a friend who is a UFO enthusiast. He looked at the photos and told me that I had caught the orb in the first photo as well, I didn't see it at first as it was camouflaged by the glow of the sun. When zooming in on the second photo where the orb is clearly visible, you'll notice that it's surface is "sparkling" for lack of a better way to describe it, and the best way for me to describe it would be to say it looks like the huge sphere out front of Epcot in Disney World. Also note, the orbs location in photo 1 compared to it's location in photo 2 which was taken no less than 15 seconds later, it must have traveled rapidly to change positions so quickly. I would be most appreciative if someone would further investigate this event to either confirm or dispel my conclusion that this is a UFO.
