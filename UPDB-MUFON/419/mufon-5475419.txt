{nd} A light in the sky that appeared to be a satellite (no satellite could be identified) moved across the sky.

My wife and I were sitting on our deck at approx 10:20 pm. She pointed out a light moving near the horizon in the south west. As we observed the light move along a course towards the north east, it appeared to be a satellite. No airplane lights were observed, and it motion was very smooth, like a satellite. The light appeared to be above low lying clouds, but below higher clouds. After the light disappeared over the horizon to the north east, I performed an internet search to try to identify the object, but none could be found. The light traveled from my Southwest horizon to the northeast in approx 2 minutes, and obtained a maximum altitude of about 70 degrees.
