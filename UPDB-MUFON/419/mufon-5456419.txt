{rjl} Saucer-like disc

Grey disc, hovering 20 feet above 2nd storey apartment building. Felt close enough to touch. No sound. Some lights around edge of craft. We felt an intelligent presence was “inspecting “ us. Lights around perimeter of craft, possibly red, green and blue; pulsating. We both felt that we wanted to go with them, felt we weren’t interesting enough for the to take us. We lived very near to Dobbins AFB and Lockheed in Smyrna GA. Both felt desperate to leave with them. It hovered briefly, maybe 5-10 minutes, then sped away exceedingly fast, and out of view in a matter of 2-3 seconds. I was only 10 years old or so. The Miss Univers or amiss America pageant was on tv at the time, and I’d accompanied my mom to the balcony so she could smoke. The following morning we asked a neighbor in the apartment if they’d seen anything that night and she had no similar experience. I’ve very rarely told other people about the experience. Felt very embarrassed to have seen a UFO, knowing the stigma attached.
