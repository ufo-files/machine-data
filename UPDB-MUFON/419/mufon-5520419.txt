Back in about 1975 I witnessed the same type of ufo my friend captured a picture of the other day. I observed a flashing light in the sky witch at first I thought was a plane traveling to Newark airport. It was only after the flashing light came to a stop directly above us high in the sky that I determined it to be a helicopter hovering. As I watched the flashing light it suddenly took off at a high rate of speed then stopped on a dime in a different location, it then did the same thing again stopping in another location on a dime. After a couple of seconds it took off at what I would describe as warp speed and vanished into the night. Im sending the photo my friend snapped with his digital camera.
