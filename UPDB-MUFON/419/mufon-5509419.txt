(GS) they appeared on side of mtn and the moved to center of vally -lined up in rows of three then ascended to 3500 ft ten disappeared one at a time

not first sighting- at approximately 10:27 pm my neighbor called and ask me to look up at mtn and tell him what they were- when I got outside I saw orange lights appearing on side of mtn facing my house. as they appeared they moved out of the trees and headed out to center of valley where the seemed to wait for each other,as they got closer together they lined up in sets of three verticly with 20 or 30 feet between them, once each group had three lights they started gaining altitude and once they got around 3500 ft they started disappearing from the top light down. three groups of three did the exact same thing and when the third group started disappearing 2 more lights appeared on side of mtn and they headed in same direction as others but moving a lot faster and dissappeared at about same altitude. this whole process happened in less than 5 min. I live on adjacent mtn and they appeared halfway up mtn at about same elevation as my home and the mtn is less than a half mile away. my family also witnessed this as well as two other people. there wasnt any sound. I witnessed the same kind of lights about 6 or 7 years ago but they formed two triangles instead of vertical lines and seemed to have smaller objects going back and forth between the orange lights. cant explain but I will be definitely be watching that area. didn't get any pictures but after checking the internet there is a lot of pics and videos of what looks to be same things
