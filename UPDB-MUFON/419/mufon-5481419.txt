{rjl} Very large high polished silver disc hovering over marshland on the Satilla River by I95

Approx 430pm, driving northbound I95 just past Woodbine exit by Satilla river saw a hugh silver high polished disc hovering over the marsh next to treeline about 20-30ft off ground. object seemed to be as big as a minivan. I wanted to turn around but the next exit was 10 mins down and would be to long to get back it might be gone. I didn't turn around. I was freaked out wondering what the heck I saw and why was it in the marsh. I just so happened to look that way and saw it before I drove past. It was tucked back almost hiding behind the treeline on the highway.
