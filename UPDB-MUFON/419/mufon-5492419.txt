{rjl} I saw a star move, I began asking questions in my head and seemed to receive responses.

It was a nice night. I decided to enjoy some star gazing in the backyard. Within ten minutes of sitting down my attention was drawn to a star that seemed to flinch. I raised my hand and pointed my finger saying out loud that just moved. The light began rotating around the object disappearing behind it then reamerging. The first question was God? Out of the southern hemisphere there was an aluminescence. Two stars moved out of another constilation north of the first craft flying across the horizon above the house. When the third one took the same path I pulled my phone out and started recording. It came to a complete stop the entire time I was recording. I put the phone down still on record, it did not move. I turned the recorder off and it started to Bob up and down. I felt I was going crazy and didn't know what to do. I heard of you this morning. My experience is documented with NASA when they released a photo taken from the Mars rover of an orb on the same exact night. The video I recorded turned out all black.
