Observed a shiny object in the contrail of a military plane for several minutes, then it moved to one side.

While driving south on Rt. 1 in Wiscasset, Maine towards Bath, we saw a jet plane in the distance -about 11 o'clock. The plane had a short contrail. In the back of the contrail, towards the end, we observed a shiny object that seemed to follow the path of the plane. It was very bright and not part of the contrail. I passed my phone to my wife and asked her to 'look it up' on my flight app. (I should have asked her to film it). My thought was to contact the airline. The app did not have any information about that particular plane -making me believe it was a military one without their transponder on. After a few minutes we observed the shiny tic-tak object off to the side of the contrail but still following the plane. Soon after, we lost it in the trees and when we got to the bridge in Bath the plane was closer to Portland and was not being followed by the object. We did not see the tik-tak fly away. The interesting thing about this event is that the flight path was directly above old Maine Yankee Nuclear Plant, Bath Iron Works, and the retired Brunswick Air. I would like to know if there were any others that saw this event?
