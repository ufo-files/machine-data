I saw a large UFO disc with green lights very close above my house while I was walking towards my house. I felt a lot of love.

I was a Doctor in Veterinary Medicine and I wasn't into UFO's or ET's in 1997.
In the evening of February 27, 1997, I had parked my car and walked toward my house which was a path of about 50 meters.
I then heard a buzzing sound and I looked up.
I saw a large UFO that had the shape of a grey disc and it had big green lights all around, beaming from the UFO. The UFO was slowly rotating and moving towards my house.
The UFO was very close, just above my head, treetop high, so I could clearly see and feel it!

I felt an enormous love and joy and my heart opened.
I felt a deep communication with the beings that were in the UFO.
It felt like coming home. As if they wanted me to know that they love me and they opened me up to the cosmic truth.

The UFO hoovered slowly until it was above my house. Then I made the mistake to think: I will walk to the other side of my house, so I can see it better. Immediately, the UFO was gone. Without a trace, without a sound.

I have never met anyone who saw a UFO that close.
Now in 2019 I came across the website of MUFON and I decided to share my unique and loving encounter with this UFO.
Maybe it can help others to come forward.
