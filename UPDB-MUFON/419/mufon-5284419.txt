Stationary pulsating light

I was visiting my in-laws in Holladay Utah with my family. My wife and three children were sitting in their hot tub. My daughter had been staring up at the sky for a minute or two before she finally asked me, "Dad, what is that?" I moved over to see what she was pointing at, and my other two children moved to see as well. There was a bright light in the sky to the North East about 50 degrees in the skyline that appeared to be bigger and brighter than the handful of stars that were visible around 7:00 PM MST. Sunset was around 6:30 PM MST, so this was shortly after sunset. At first I wondered if it were a planet. But my daughter said the light had been blinking on and off randomly. Sure enough, as we sat there watching it blinked on and off several times It would remain lit for three or four seconds, and then disappear for two or three seconds before reappearing again. When off we could not see anything in its place, and when on, we could only see a bright light. It stayed in one position the whole time we watched, it was not moving, it was stationary in the sky. Then, the light slowly got brighter and brighter and then suddenly tripled in size and disappeared completely from view. We stayed and watched the same spot for a few more minutes but it did not reappear.
