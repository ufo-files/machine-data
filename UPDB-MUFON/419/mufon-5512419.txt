{nd} large formation of many white orbs travelling swiftly NE; lost sight from my window; could not find them outside in the sky afterwards

my partner was napping on our bed and i came in to plug my cellphone in, and happened to be down close to the floor to find the plug. i looked up out of the window and saw what looked like a BEAUTIFUL formation of white shimmering birds -- i yelled at my partner to sit up & put his glasses on to see. then i looked closer and saw that there were LIGHTS, glowing lights. they were moving NORTH over our building in victorian village, columbus. my partner couldnt see them so we RAN outside to try to find them as they headed over the building. ran all around the block, every which way, and no sign of any of the lights. they were neither in a V-formation or a straight line -- a sort of very intricate inter-woven pattern, diamonds maybe?, that id have to see again to describe. strangely enough wed BOTH seen the exact same thing with 2 other witnesses in a columbus-area grocery store on the day neil armstrong died. looked it up all over the internet & nothing ever explained. (august 25th, 2012). very, very moved by & curious about these lights. would love to know if anybody else has seen them today. (no chem/vapor trails in the sky, either ... )
