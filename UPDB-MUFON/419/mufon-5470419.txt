At approx. 6:30 pm, my boyfriend and I saw a large object flying across the southern sky. It was round or circle shaped and was glowing white. it had a huge wide white light trail behind it. The trail was more like a wide wedge of clouds that were lit up. It is difficult to explain because there is nothing Ive ever seen to compare it to. I dont have the words to describe it clearly. But it almost looked like a huge planet with a wedge shaped light trail. It was traveling extremely fast and straight without wavering. But it suddenly vanished from our field of vision after we watched it for about 10 minutes. It was up higher than any airplane can go. It was a clear cloudless night .
