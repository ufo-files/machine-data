{cak} metallic like craft projected beam down over taylor pony farm on us 127. heard horses panic.

I was out late at night watching persieds meteor shower on my parents back on water street in Hudson. Was between 2am-3am. Was sitting in recliner facing south. There is a little city light interference in the sky. Water street is only about 3 blocks north of downtown. I was scanning the sky south and west for meteors since this was best view of the sky at water street. Something large was descending rapidly at a 45degree angle. No lights just metallic looking because the city lights were reflecting of its surface. was heading south to north and looked like it was flying parallel to us 127 less than a mile west of my location. It was about tree top level and a bright beam of light came out of its surface pointing directly at the ground. From my location the line of trees is a block west of my location. The object look like it was 1/4 mile west if these trees still descending with beam of light. This object was very near the Taylor pony farm (veterinarian farm) on us 127 another name for this road is north meridian road
. I knew it was near this farm is because I could hear the horses from 1/2 mile away sounding like they were in a panic. When horse make the vocal sounds when they are scared. I could hear the horses for about 20 seconds. But the craft had went behind the trees so I lost sight of it from my location. Dont know if it continued north
Or hovered in the field over the horses at the veterinarian hospital. I wanted to get in my car and drive down to the area I seen it but frankly was a little excited and scared at what I heard (horses) and seen. Object had no lights except beam and no sound was heard from it.the next day I really wanted to stop or call the Taylor pony farm to see if anything the night before woke any of the residence up but never did.
