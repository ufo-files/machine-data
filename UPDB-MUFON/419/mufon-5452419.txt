I went outside to look up at the stars at approximately 9:15 pm and I counted 21 objects, but there may have been more as they were in sight when I sat down. They were all the size, shape, and about twice the luminesce of most stars in the night sky. They moved quickly--about 70% the speed of a passing airliner at cruising altitude--from S to N, and much, much faster than a Sattelite. The first several appeared from Zenith and moved from NE to the E horizon. The others joined the first lot and moved from the SW & W. It was very interesting because they followed a linear projection, equidistant from each other and all three groups behaved identically--in speed, separation, and brightness. I became convinced that they were not natural or orbiting objects when on occasion several seemed to have to "go around" something in its path; it would deviate from the straight line and make a half-circle, starting and ending in the linear projection. The three groups all disappeared on the Eastern Horizon, at the same point, around 9:25 pm EST.
