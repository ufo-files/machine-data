{cak} Observed blue pulsating orb traveling N/B then turned E/B and zig/zag as it gained altitude.

I had just gotten off work and returned home and took my dogs out for their walk at about 2200 hrs. We left the house and walked S/B to the corner which is about 700 ft. We do this walk every night the same way. We are close to Travis Air Base and there is a small local airport less than a mile away. There was a full moon and slight cloud cover. I see all kinds of planes and this was nothing like that. I am a retired police officer and I am trained to observe. As I stood on the corner facing east I saw a slow moving orb traveling north from my right. It was pulsating blue which is a color hardly ever seen. It appeared to be between 300 to 500 ft in the air and traveling at about 75 MPH. I would guess that it was about 10 feet in diameter and made no sound what so ever. I thought that someone may be playing with drone but at it got up near me it turned away and headed east. I watched it climb and travel beyond what a drone could do. It changed directions darting to the right then to the left as it disappeared into the cloud cover. The pulsating round blue light never changed shape, speed or brightness, it was very consistent. I thought it was strange because there were no other aircraft in the sky and usually I see several military aircraft during our walk or even small planes from the local airport. I told my family about my sighting and decided to report it in case someone else also observed it. It figures it was the only night I didn't have my cell phone with me to photograph it.
