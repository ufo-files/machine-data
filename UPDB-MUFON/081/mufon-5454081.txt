I was standing on my back deck 04/06/2020 around 11pm, I looked up and seen a full moon that was very bright and visible there were no clouds or stars , it was just the full bright moon against a black sky, I took out my cell phone (flip phone) doesn't have a great camera but I wanted to take a picture of the moon which I did, as soon as I snapped the first picture of the moon I noticed two cylinder shaped object 
just appeared out of no where! they seemed to be just stationary , at first I thought they were stars but then they started moving in unison they went in a straight line at first pretty much even with the moon , then they dropped down just guessing about 1,000 feet and continued to go straight , that's when I snapped another picture, it seemed after I snapped the second picture one of the objects looked like it turned towards me and got brighter (a reddish color) after that they seemed to start flying erratically really fast zigzagging and flickering and gained altitude has they kept flickering either they were getting further away , or the lights were just fading out, in any event they vanished at that point. and I snapped another picture of the moon in hopes of catching them in the photo I'm not sure if I did because like I said I was using a flip phone camera. I am attaching all the pictures I took that night . I know what I saw and it was pretty amazing , I will be looking in the sky again tonight at the same time I'm hoping they will return!
