in may of 1991  myself and many other people i was with saw a gigantic  craft  at least a mile long  and 500 ft wide from top to bottom,it came from the north east and heading south west , very low , no noise , slow .

I was at an outdoor concert in 1991  with many friends  in may of that year.The show had started and about  30 mins into it we noticed something in the sky coming from the north  east  it was low   just above the tree tops as it got closer it became bigger it must have been  a mile or so long and from top  to bottom 500 ft.We all just starred at it no  one really said anything we were kind of shocked at how big this thing was and somebody shouted out "UFO" then i realized what i was looking at.It was maybe a 1/4 mile from us to the south of us  heading west when we really got a good look at it.It was so low  i thought it was going to crash and kill every one in a  mile  area but it just flew right on by very slow did not move fast at all made  no noise.It looked ancient  very  very old  older than anything i"v ever seen  the only straight lines were the outer edges  and it kind had an arc  to it,the structure of it looked   woven together like a gigantic jigsaw puzzle as it passed by we could see the back of it and there was the power source it looked like the color of metal ore with a com trail  two miles long we could see the com trail for  20 mins  after it  flew out of sight to the south west.Two years after this i was at work talking about it and my boss pipes up and says he saw the same thing  he said"it flew right above  his house in Bonner springs K.S.  saying it was more v shaped and there was a smaller craft right behind it  to the right " then it occurred to me that we were only seeing one side of it which being  so big it  looked cigar shaped but it was actually v shaped he also said  there were  giant orbs at each corner as big as houses all pulsating and changing colors  with one in the middle.
