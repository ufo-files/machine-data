{cak} Bright white light/object in sky hovers and then vanishes as if using camouflage.

I was heading North on Hwy 97 and turned left on SW Veterans Way facing West where I was stopped at a traffic light. While waiting for the light to turn green I was looking at the pretty sunset and a very bright white light caught my attention. I saw this and thought it was a plane, but it was much smaller than a plane and so bright. It looked cigar shaped and seem to be staying still in the sky. I stared at it for about a minute and then it suddenly vanished as if it was using some kind of camouflage. It was at a great distance from me because it appeared like a dash in the sky, but the light was so bright and caught my attention in daylight. How it vanished is what was so shocking - It seemed to just disappear without any movement. I was very excited and couldn't stop looking for it on my way home, but it was gone. I have never seen anything like this before in real life. I ran in the door to tell my wife and she of course looked at me like I was crazy, but I had to share this amazing moment I had.
