7-8 blue-green orbs traveling in a straight SE course along coastline.

At 2000 hours on 3/19/21 I was standing at the base of the Newport Pier with five co-workers. There was thick fog (approx 300m surface visibility and a moderate NW wind. One co-worker pointed to the sky over the ocean (approx 30 deg elevation) and said, “Does anybody else see this?”

There were approximately 7-8 blue-green orbs in no apparent formation, traveling in a straight SE flight path at a relatively slow speed along the coastline and just offshore. Due to the heavy fog, I could not determine distance, size, elevation, or features. There was no accompanying aircraft noise. All five witnesses agreed that the objects were unlike any aircraft we had seen.

I was in the military for 10 years and worked closely with aircraft, mostly helicopters, during that time. This area is frequently travelled by military helicopters and I have seen helicopters at night using infrared formation lights. However, the lack of aircraft noise, apparent close proximity of the objects in the heavy fog, and lack of formation differed from the military aircraft I an accustomed to seeing.
