{dr}(2) Orange Pulsating Orbs Coming From South West To The North. Once above, one orb went west, the other continued north for about a minute and then Disappeared.

At 9:34pm on June 20th 2013, (2)Orange Pulsating Orbs/Saucer object appeared over the horizon and tree line looking south. The objects were approx. 40 feet apart with one following the other. Once above, the following object turned and went to the west and was lost behind a tree line. Lead Object was trailed for Approx another minute heading from strait above to North of my location. It then flickered twice and faded out and disappeared. I continue my investigation into this phenomena and have yet to pinpoint exactly what it was.
