{dr}3 Large Discs Surveying area Then disappears

I was at work outside in my car. I looked through my windshield and seen 3 Large Discs that looked like they were in almost a zip zag pattern like they were observing the area. They stopped and one bright light flashed from one (the center disc) my radio in the car cut out my cellphone froze and the walkie talkie I keep in the car had static coming through. I kept watching and then the radio came back in less than 30 seconds later and then the discs went into separate directions one going East the other West and the third came forward it happened so fast but I could see the one disc from far away for at least 20 minutes
