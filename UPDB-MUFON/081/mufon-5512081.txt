Red Triangle UFO

This happened when I was about 14 years old and I didn't really think much of it at the time or tell anyone about it but it still sticks in my mind to this day. Now I have the knowledge and know it was a UFO and couldn't be any normal craft. At the time I was going to bed and when I looked out of the window I notice a red triangle in the sky. The triangle wasn't red in whole but the edges were bright red, I'm not sure if the main body was transparent or black. It remained stationary in the air, no noise, motionless. When I lef tthe window and looked again it had vanished.
