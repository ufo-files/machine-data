A large orange reddish orb hovering over buildings for a short amount of time

I was walking out of my house not too long ago to go take my dog outside,i looked at the buildings southeast of my apartments an saw a huge orangeish/reddish orb hovering in place.Its altitude looked very low,hovering maybe only 300-400 feet off ground.I have never seen something so bright an something in the sky hovering that low.I watched it only for about 2 mins or so,then out of no where it was gone,the object didn't look to move or anything when it vanished,it just disappeared.
 I thought this was very out of this world,the huge size didn't really surprise me much,but it was how bright it was,i have never seen something so bright but yet not light up the sky or ground.The lighting had no affect on any surfaces,i could see as the building roof top was competly dark,an this object was not very far behind the buildings.
