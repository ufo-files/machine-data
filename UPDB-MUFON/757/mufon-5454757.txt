Husband and I saw over 25 star/satellite like objects zoom rather quickly as we faced S/SE. There would be a moment or two in between them appearing. Some would be traveling in groups of 3-5 some would be by themselves. They would sometimes stay on a clear path, others would swoop and go north or south.
