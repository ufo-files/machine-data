{rjl} Came from the same area ascending above the treeline, changing direction in a seemingly random pattern, then all headed to the same spot in the sky and vanished.

My partner and I were sitting on our third floor balcony facing towards the southeast, talking on speakerphone with one of my sons. I saw a series of four orange, orb shaped lights ascending one behind another from the treeline. I turned toward my partner and said "Look over there...what the heck are those?" I mentioned what we were looking at to my son. Then we stood at the railing and watched as three more orbs, then a fourth, joined the first four in what looked like a random dance in the sky. Some orbs went higher above the others, some moved back toward the direction they had appeared from. Then, one by one, the orbs moved higher and toward the east and as each one reached the same area of the sky they disappeared.
The event lasted only a few seconds.
