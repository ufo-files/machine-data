Streaking White Orb Passing High Above a Crop Duster Plane

Going over some old Super 8 film put onto DVD. Jogged my memory. When I was 10 years old we were vacationing in Mexico (1971). Driving in the State of Guanajuato, Mexico going to Mexico City sitting in the back of my parents Station Wagon. My dad would always have his Super 8 Camara to film us. He taught me how to use it and asked me if I saw something of interest to make sure to film it. You know, make a memory. This particular time as I was looking out the back window I noticed a Crop Duster flying close by. I decided to film it. So I started, at the same time I looked directly above it and noticed this White ORB streaking
East to West at a High rate of Speed. horizontally. Not really ascending od descending. I said to myself instinctively, that's strange, so I immediately turned the camara towards it and filmed it. Was able to catch just a few seconds of it. It was so fast it dissappeared behind a mountain and clouds. I totally forgot about it. Back in those days, you were lucky to get your film back in two-three weeks time. Plus in this case, it was about a month before we even sent all our Super 8 Film to be developed.
