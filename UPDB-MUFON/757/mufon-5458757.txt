I did not see the object at the time I took the photo, however upon looking at the photograph on my computer after our vacation, I saw the object and zoomed in on it on the photo. 

The weather was a dense cloudy and storming morning and we had ascended above the clouds. I was taking photo's of the sunrise and the clouds.

I do not think there was any breaks in the clouds that the object and the other small lights near it were something on the ground reflecting the sun because the cloud cover was dense and before take off it was quite a heavy storm. 

Our flight was Delta 1351 on July 15th, 2019.
