{rjl} My daughter, son and I saw a blue light that was stationary then moved and changed colors.

I was with my daughter and son on our back porch talking and enjoying the night air when we all saw this blue light appear in the NE over some trees, about 1/4 of a mile or less away. We watched and were wondering what it was, as it held a stationary position. All of a sudden it started moving, slowly bobbing back and forth then it changed color to a pinkish/purple then it would hover then move, then bob a bit then hover then it changed colors to orangish/reddish and it slowly descended into the tree line where we lost sight of it. A minute or two goes by then all of a sudden, further down (or east) the tree line a orange light ascends and hovers a a bit then has a flickering light that is red and then a silverish white. It looks as if it was maybe a plane to the everyday person but it was a glowing orange and it was such a strange hovering bobbing path it was going west on....and I've seen many many airplanes and they don't stop or bob or hover or glow orange. It was absolutely silent as well and a plane that close would of have some sort of noise. It continued slowly west until we were not able to see it from our place anymore. I took a few pictures when it first appeared and then my phone died. I attached what I have. I had just submitted a report last week of a pulsating orange light and this seems to be in the same vein. I have no idea. It was way to big to be a drone and went to far where a controller wouldn't be able to fly it....and I still don't believe either things were an airplane because I have been watching the airplanes for years. I feel excited and feel like my family is being watched in some way. We are all Rh-Negatives on both sides and have always had strange things happen. And I can't stop thinking the clouds are not what they seem and I watch them constantly as well. Sometimes they just don't seem like a real cloud but something trying hard to appear that way.
