{rjl} two bright balls of light fly over my house

on december 26, 2018, at 8:15 pm i stepped outside my house to have a cigarette. it was a partly cloudy night but also a bit windy pushing clouds across the sky occasionally showing the stars behind them as well as the commercial airliner planes that were flying in the sky at the same time. there were 2 bright orange round craft flying above my house. first one then the other. they seemed to be traveling together as they were maybe 50 feet apart. same speed, same level, as though they were on an invisible train in the sky. as i mentioned before, they were sharing this same space with actual airplanes which were dwarfed by these things. besides, the airplanes were above the clouds, whereas these things were very much below the clouds. my visual of these things were at no time obstructed by the clouds. their speed, which was hasty but not too fast, never wavered and their relative distance from each other and from me never changed. i watched them fly directly over my head making NO NOISE AT ALL. perfect silence. when they were directly above me i noticed the orangey haze they had seemed to split into several colors (like when you get real close to a tv set and it turns into a collection of blue, green, and red dots?) only these colored dots were yellow, purple, and pink. these lights never blinked or faded or pulsated or flashed. they stayed on. i watched them as long as i could before the treetops got in my line of sight. however, when i lost sight of them, they seemed to morph into the background disguised as stars...like camouflage. like the orange faded into white and their size shrank to fit in as stars.
