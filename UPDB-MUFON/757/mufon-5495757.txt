{dr}Objects appeared white on surface and some red coloring on another

In Logansport Indiana on a partly cloudy day the object appeared in the blue part of the cloudless sky around 3:00 or somewhere around. First noticed it at 3rd & Market intersection while stopped at a stoplight heading east. My honey took the picture, one picture straight on through the clean and clear windshield and then took two more pictures from out the side door (The window was rolled down). We didn't notice the additional objects until the pictures were developed. My honey and I seen the object several blocks ahead. The best view of it was when drove east a couple blocks from the right lane. I (Jimmy) was shocked and fascinated at the same time. I drove under the approximate sighting area and that's when I lost sight of it.
