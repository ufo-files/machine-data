{rjl} 1 solid white lite 3 different times and locations

June 12, 2016, 9:45 PM was on back deck when I saw a solid white light in NW, not blinking. Thought it might be space station but it continued in a straight path over my house to the SE. I watched it probably 2-3 minutes solid. I didn't get up to see it more the corner of my house blocked my view. That night there was a bright star on right side of the moon. About 5 mins later it was about 10:00 oclock in the SW sky moving to that star. Saw it maybe 3-4 seconds then it vanished. About 5 mins later it was NE, about 2:00 oclock in the sky by another bright star. This time I could tell it was traveling away from me, only saw it about 2-3 seconds, then out of sight, So I saw 1 white, round, solid light 3 different times and 3 different places. So I just figured it was a UFO.  I've had another sighting that was much more frightening that I reported to you. It was 9 illuminated copper ovals, 3 each set on a base of 3 steps, 3 to each step. That was right in my back yard. I hope I have included everything.
