Taking pictures of storm clouds and saw boomerang object in the sky.

I left work heading south on 99. I was trying to hurry home since there was a tornado spotted on 94 heading east to the Albion area, which was about 10 miles from where I work. The clouds were very scary and low. The rain slowly subsided by the time I got home but the tornando sirens were going off. I watched the news for a few minutes and decided it was clearing up enough to finally get some pictures of the clouds moving out of the area. In one of the pictures I notice a strange boomerang shaped object. It was strange and haven't seen anything like it before. I attached a picture to share...
