{kt}Bright orange lights making impossible manuevers through August and September

I work for Cherryfield Foods night shift for work on blueberry barrens in Columbia, Maine. Nearly every night we would observe an object low on the horizon, very bright make unbelievable moves. On one occasion we witnessed five of the objects perform a scattering of maneuvers. We have seen it go one direction slowly and then speed off and return to its original position. It normally takes place at about 9:30 to 3 in the morning. It is normally orange in color but has blue and red lights that seem to be spinning as it moves. I saw it again last night at 10 pm. I would guess that the object is between 10 to 14 miles away. It has gone over head of us but was really high at the time.
