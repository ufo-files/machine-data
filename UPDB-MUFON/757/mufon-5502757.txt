{cak} It flew over head like a plane but it was much too low to the ground to be one.

I was I'm my backyard, at night,with my dogs and listening to music on my phone when I heard something flying over head. I'm used to hearing planes at night so I didn't think anything of it . I looked to see it was just a plane flying by like normal. About 30 minutes later, I heard something that sounded like a jet. I looked up to see a very bright light coming towards my house. I sat completely still and watched it. When I First saw it I thought it was another plane but as it got closer, I realize it was too low to the ground to be a plane. I continued watching it as it flew over head not moving at all. As it came closer to me, my phone shut off as well as my music. I didn't know what was happening but I knew it wasn't normal. I watched as it made a turn in the air that was nothing like a plane turning. It turned towards the woods behind me instead of the straight path over my house. I continued watching it until it disappeared behind the trees of the woods. After I lost sight, my music turned back on like nothing happened. I didn't know what to think at the time other than it was no airplane or helicopter. I ran back inside not feeling safe outdoors for the rest of the night.
