Looked like a helium balloon hovering above, but then super fast departure out of sight (less than 1 second)

I was looking up to see airplanes (the location is a private pilot training area and aerobatic box) and there were none. Then I saw what I thought was a distant helium balloon. I stared at it for about 45 seconds before it "zipped" away at incredible speed (less than 1 second until out of sight) to the NorthEast. I could see no details or real color. It appeared to be light colored either white or reflective compared to the almost cloudless sky (some stratus clouds randomly in the sky). I would have thought little of it, if it hadn't moved so incredibly fast as it zipped away silently.
