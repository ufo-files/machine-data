Same as prev. reports. This time 3 objects. On this occasion also observed by husband. Some interference.

Approx 02:45 radio suddenly went quiet/dropped out for perhaps a minute. Went to usual window. Above tree-line, slightly more west than normal, saw large stationary object approx 8 o'clock to star. Two smaller objects to east of large one flying fast & erratically. Occasionally stopping, then zipping about (see also prev. reports). Woke husband. He was unable to see the two smaller, but could pick out the larger (less clear on this occasion as we have nautical twilight). Returned to bedroom. Radio functioning again, but battery dropped 30%.
