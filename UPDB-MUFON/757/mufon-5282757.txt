Dark blob object drifted overhead

My wife and I were in drive through line at a Chipotle. Low in sky due north saw a dark object coming towards us, like a balloon. As it neared us I jumped out of car and took digital photos with my smartphone (attached). The object moved horizontally and steadily from due north and passed us a few hundred feet east of us. Appearance was like a dark blob of balloons, with a reflective gray "dome" at top. Some parts had a rusty color, but mostly it was black. It did not tumble, rotate, wiggle, or otherwise act like a tight balloon cluster, although that is the closest known type of object that fits the description. The sighting was strange, but I figured it was likely balloons. However, after some time I checked wind direction (attached) for that date. Wind was out of the SSE at 13 MPH, and the object moved exactly opposite that direction. Also, we recently saw a UFO video made by a pilot on TV that looked very similar, so I decided to go ahead and report this in case there were other witnesses or similar reports on that date.
