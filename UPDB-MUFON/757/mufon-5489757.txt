{jpg} A pill-shaped craft was slowly heading north

I was sitting with my dog behind the glass door to the backyard, and I looked up at the sky by chance. A silvery, pill-shaped object that seemed to be very high up was traveling in a slow, straight line northward. I thought at first it was a jet, but I did a double-take and realized it had no visible wings and left no trail.
There was an airplane to the far left of the sky travelling in the same direction, and if the object was making any noise, it would have been drowned out by the plane. It was reflecting sunlight.
I didn't get my camera because I didn't want to lose sight of the object. I just sat there and watched it move across the sky for a few seconds, then it became blocked by trees. I didn't see it after that.
