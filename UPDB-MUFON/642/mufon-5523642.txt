The ufo was flying by a jet above the clouds, then another flew by 20 min. later that hovered and then vanished.

My 8 year old son and I were sky watching when he pointed out a small white shiny circular object flying by a commercial jet. It was much higher than the jet and moved a lot faster too. After about 3 minutes it was out of our sight. Then about 20 minutes later I seen a cigar shaped white ufo that was a lot closer to the ground than the other one. This one was flying in a straight path at first until it abruptly stopped, hovered for about 20 seconds and then completely vanished as if a bubble popping. It didn't fly away, it just disappeared completely!
