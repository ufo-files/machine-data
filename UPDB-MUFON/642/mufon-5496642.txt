{rjl} Me & my sisters playing ball in backyard that backs up to elementary school craft appeared as white glowing paper airplane almost luminous traveling back & forth from south to north and north in straight line before suddenly appearing in backyard

Me and my 2 sister were playing ball in the backyard in the evening,I was like 4 and my sisters were older, when I noticed a large white glowing paper airplane like object gliding through the air at a constant height approximately 50 feet with no variance in speed, and no noise. It was flying above the dirt field at the elementary school behind our house. It kept flying back and forth from south to north and north to south at a leisurely slow speed. I pointed it out to my sisters and they oohed and we went back to playing catch with a big bouncy ball, as we did not know what it was. I then noticed the object again appearing almost out of no where in our back yard as if it had landed magically just behind my sisters, I then pointed behind them and shouted look, and they screamed and we all ran into the garage via the back door, adjacent to the house. Me and my eldest sister ran more quickly and decided to mess with our other sister and shut the door and lock her out, she pounded on the door and screamed let me in, let me in for a good 30 seconds before we let her in. I remember immediately looking out the window to the back yard when inside the house, and the object was gone. We were all huffing and puffing telling our parents that we saw something. Me and my eldest sister did not remember the event until after a period of 3 to 4 years, but my other sister who we locked out has no memory of the event. The object had no windows, no insignia, no letters just a pasty white glowing almost luminous quality, the outline of the object was almost hard to define because of its glow but it was angular in appearance.
