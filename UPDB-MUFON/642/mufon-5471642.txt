{cak} Huge, classic disc shaped with dome, white lights sequentially moving around edge of disc

I was staying in a rough summer cabin type houseboat. There was a ladder linking the lower floor with the bedroom upstairs. I was asleep and got up to use the bathroom, descending the ladder. Beside the ladder was a large window which faced the Willamette slough, and beyond it, Sauvie's Island. I didn't look out the window on the way down that I recall. When I climbed the ladder to go back to bed, I looked across the water to the island and saw a huge craft hovering over the island. I stopped and watched. It was a classic "flying saucer" shaped craft and around the perimeter of the disc were white lights that appeared to be moving slowly and sequentially from right to left. I watched it, stunned, for maybe 30 seconds. Suddenly, it went straight up, in a second's time, and was gone. I never reported it or anything. I knew people would think I was crazy. I have told many people though, over the years. It remains a very clear picture in my mind. It was awhile before I got back to sleep.
