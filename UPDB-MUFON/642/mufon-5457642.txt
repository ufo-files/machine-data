{jpg} I saw an object that looked like a floating tire

I was driving north on Antioch approaching Johnson Drive when I observed what appeared to be a tire floating in the sky ahead of me. When I stopped at the light at Johnson preparing to turn right I was able to take a quick video through my open sunroof though this video didn't capture much of value. I continued east on Johnson Drive and stopped on Lowell Street to get out and take another video. The object was moving toward me but on the south side of Johnson, travelling southeast. It appeared to lay flat most of the time as it was gliding across the sky but occasionally tumbled or flipped over, at which time I could see it reflecting sunlight. I got two additional videos as it passed just to my south, heading to the south and east and out of sight.  I captured an additional unknown oblong object moving down in a smooth motion over several frames, nearby the tire-shaped object, but it was not seen at the time.
