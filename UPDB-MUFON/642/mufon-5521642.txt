3 arm star shape glowing brightly and moving low and silent 30 foot diameter

from albany ny: i was outside 6:15 to 6:30am looking for any meteor shower activity and taking my dog out. it was early dawn and the sun hadn't risen yet. i saw what appeared to be a plane in the distance. i am in the flight path of planes departing albany airport. the object was traveling north to south i'd say 30 degrees in the horizon. i thought it might have been a twin engine plane by the span of lights. i watched the object get closer and i noticed the object didn't climb but moved straight like a helicopter closer and closer. then i noticed no flashing light and no sound. just a smooth constant slow movement closer and closer. i realized it was something i've never seen before. i just observed and studied the object and it looked to me like 3 brightly colored arms attached in the center spread out equally like a 3 pointed star. the object was 500-1000 feet in the air i suppose. it was so close and i was ready for the object to pass overhead but a tree in my yard blocked my view and that's how i lost sight of it. it should have passed over me and i was sort of frustrated that it didn't. it just dissappeared from sight but it wasn't an illusion and this wasn't imaginary. i honestly did see this.
