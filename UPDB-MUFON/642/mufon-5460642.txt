Prior to this sighting I witnessed what I thought must be a satellite moving across the sky, looking like a star, moving slightly faster than a very high aeroplane. Then, from a different direction, I saw another, then another. This went on for around an hour. I think I saw one shooting star throughout this time. Then, around 0015, I saw this long line of what looked like a whole bunch of stars, like a train of them. There could have been 50 up to 100. Very close together at the front of the chain and less so at the end. It moved across the sky, much closer to the horizon than straight up, and it disappeared from my view and went behind some trees. I ran to where I thought I'd see it again but it didn't reappear. I sat outside for a while, maybe 20 minutes. During that time I saw another 3 shooting stars, the last of which appeared to shoot to the ground behind a nearby church and appeared to look like flame just before it disappeared.
