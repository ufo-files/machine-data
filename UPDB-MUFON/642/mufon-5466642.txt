NO UFO

IN 1993 I WOKE UP HOT SO I GOT UP TO THROW MY COMFORTER TO MY FEET AND I WAS FACE TO FACE WITH AN ALIEN. IT JUST STOOD INFRONT OF MY CLOSET STARING AT ME, MAYBE 3 FEET FROM MY FEET. I WAS TERRIFIED AND THREW MY COMFORTER OVER MY HEAD. I WAS SO HOT UNDER MY COMFORTER SO I PINCHED MY ARM AND LOOKED AGAIN AND IT WAS STILL THERE STARING AT ME. IT DIDNT MAKE A NOISE OR MOVE. FELT LIKE ETERNITY UNDER MY COMFORTER I LOOKED AGAIN AND IT WAS GONE. I RAN TO MY MOMS ROOM. I WILL NEVER FORGET THIS EXPERIENCE. AS A 35 YEAR OLD WOMAN TODAY I STILL SLEEP WITH THE COVERS OVER MY HEAD.
