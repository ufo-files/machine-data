{dr}Bright object moved slowly sometimes not at all then jolted up and down

My Dad and I were outside and I noticed this bright object in the sky unlike any star. Almost resembling the brightness of a planes light during take off. However, this was no plane with no path. It hovered stationary in the sky moving from time to time in a swaying mode. Observed a strong jolt of the object then remained stationary. When we watched the sighting there was not a cloud in the sky. During, I ran inside to get my phone and my compass. When I came back the sky was filled with clouds and the object remained unseen. Fog had abruptly swept to my doorstep. My Dad and I thought the whole thing was very strange and we decided it best to go back inside.
