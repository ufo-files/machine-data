{dr}rectangle of 6 lights seen against blue sky

My daughter and I were sitting on our deck talking, and I like tracking airplanes in the vast Central Oregon sky, so while looking for planes[a local forest fire was requiring forest service planes at the time] I spotted a spot in the sky. I told my daughter that" that one must be high because it don't seem to be moving". It was a partly cloudy day and we observed it between large clouds for about 30 minutes. It remained completely stationary. My wife was due home in a half an hour and we hoped it would stay to get a 3rd witness.my daughter decided to take a pic with her Samsung phone and when we looked at the pic it showed clearly a rectangle of six lights, two rows of three. Slowly the clouds covered it and we hoped it would be there when they passed, but another clear spot showed and it was gone. This sighting excited us and we spent the rest of the evening scanning the skies.
