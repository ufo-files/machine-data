{rjl} observed numerous UFOs over Camp Bullis and Camp Stanley,TX

My wife and I bought a home near Bulverde, Texas in May of 1995. Our home was located on [Address removed/CMS/rjl]. We lived about 100 yards down Rye from Blanco road, with Camp Bullis and Camp Stanley right across Blanco from us. Within a short time of moving to Rye, and usually between 2 and 3 a.m., I began to notice completely silent unidentified flying objects in the sky over Camp Bullis/Camp Stanley that resembled low lying twinkling stars. They would ascend from inside of/on top of? a large hill in Camp Bullis that featured a bizarre looking antennae array, would hover, sometimes alone and sometimes in formation, would slowly ascend and would then suddenly flit off in any/every direction, at very high speeds...The craft(s) featured very bright light(s), which would wax, wane and twinkle, running through the gamut of known colors....These objects could generally be seen on any clear night from midnight or later until shortly before the sun came up. I (my wife, and several invited friends) saw these lights on a frequent basis for a number of years, even parking my car directly adjacent to Blanco Road and watching them with my binoculars...I could never determine exactly what they were because the lights were so bright that they obscured the objects....We even saw then through our moon roof flying over our car as we drove south and north on Blanco Road...I also saw them descending and disappearing either inside of, on top of and/or behind the hill with the unusual antennae array in Camp Bullis1. Although I moved from Rye Drive in late 2007, and have not looked for or seen the objects since, I believe they are probably still visible in the same location at the same time(s).  As an aside, I note that the objects looked identical to footage I have seen of Area 511.
