{dr}They flew over my roof then circled about 30 ft. From there.

I thought at first they were fire Flys. But they were not flashing at all. They circled between my tree and garage after they flew over the roof of my house at about 15ft. Up above ground. I leaped up from my chair in my garage to see what I was seeing. My brother saw me jump up at that moment to see what I was disturbed about. He followed my position and saw them as well as I did but I saw the whole duration of the event. They circled perfectly for less than a minute. Then flew off in perfect linear formation just like they arrived then they flew off to the south. They circled in about a 4ft. Diameter circle when they were there but never blinking at all.
