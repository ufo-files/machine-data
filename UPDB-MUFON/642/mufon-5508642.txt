{kt}Flasing white lights in triangle formation

First of all, i am drug and alcohol free. I was smoking a cigarette on my way back home at around 9:30 PM. I looked up slowly when i noticed a bright light in the sky. At first i thought shooting star but it was moving far too slow. I squinted to see better and noticed it was 3 white lights flashing in a triangular formation. Traffic was really low at the time but i still couldnt hear the object emitting any noise at all. I couldnt see an outline either. It was moving from East to West in a perfect straight line. Had to be at least a mile up. Ive never been a firm believer of UFOs or aliens but this has made me want to look into it further. I hope others have experienced this as well
