{tjd} Disappearing light

I have seen this light 5 times over 2 years. Always around 10:00 p. m. Always in the sky above my home. It travels across the sky in a straight line. Sometimes it changes direction at a 90 degree turn instantly and then it just disappears by fading out rather quickly. It’s higher than the planes flying but not as high as a satellite might be. It’s always a solid white. Looks like a star almost. No sound. All I have is my phone for a camera and it never shows up on the video.
