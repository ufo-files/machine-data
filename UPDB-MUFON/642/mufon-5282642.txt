Two bright lights, left one was slightly above the right. Watched for about 10 seconds then the left one faded out and then the right one faded out.

Went outside to look at the moon and the stars at around 10:15 pm. The sky was clear, no clouds or fog. Observed two very bright lights and thought it was odd how bright they were given the moon was up and nearby. The left one was slightly above the right one. Observed them for about 10 seconds and assumed they were stars. They did not move. The left one then faded out slowly, taking about two seconds to disappear. Then the right one did the exact same thing. I have night vision goggles and went and grabbed them but did not see anything unusual. I am an avid sky watcher and don't remember ever seeing anything like it. 

Note: I guessed on the elevation in the sky because the field was required. My neighbor has a tall oak tree and it was above the tree line.
