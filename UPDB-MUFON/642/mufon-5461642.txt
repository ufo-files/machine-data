3 star-like lights in formation moving south in slow speed. The formation was like that of a triangle with one tip pointing down towards the ground. So that the light at the left and the one at the bottom were brighter and the third and last one was a bit dimmer. But then they all dimmed down simultaneously, (as if someone had turned the dimmer-switch for the livingroom lights) before disappearing completely.
