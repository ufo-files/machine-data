{td} Triangle shaped UFO traveled and hovered over buildings and tree-tops

I was driving along Swift Creek towards Strickland and noticed a triangle shaped object circling over the intersection. The object was traveling at approximately 5-10 mph and was black in color with two white lights on the bottom, both flashing sequentally, one red light on two corners, and one green light on the front corner. I pulled over to have a better look at the object. The object turned abruptly and sped off over the nearby trees. I followed the object back towards US 70 BUS. I turned right onto US 70 BUS and followed the object to a trucking company. As I got out of the truck, the object disappeared over a building and I lost sight of it.
