{dr}UFO seen and photographed while photographing clouds

I was merely photographing some cloud formations from inside my car at approximately 8 pm. I had seen some black specks in the sky but thought they were birds. After examining one particular photograph that was taken just seconds after another I saw an unusual black spot and when it was enlarged there seemed to be a domed object on an angle appearing through the clouds. There was also a dark center, possibly a domed area as well. There were no other objects in the sky such as planes or kites and the air was fairly still. It appeared as if it might rain though as there were some dark clouds in the area. This object was only seen for a brief moment as it seemed to just appear and disappear just as quickly and it really was too small to make out any details. The details are only seen when the picture was enlarged.
