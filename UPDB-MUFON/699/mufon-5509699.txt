{dr}circle of lights standing on end

I was watching the tv and saw a very bright red light out the window. At first I thought it might be some sort of firework but it didn't explode and it was traveling horizontaly. I saw two objects approximately 1 minute interval between them. When the third object appeared I grabbed my binoculars and went outside for a better look. The object was bright and moving slowly. There was no sound at all. It was low enough that Im sure I could hear a sound if it made one. It was lower than any airplanes I see around here. The object was made up of 7 lights in the form of a circle standing on end. I could see that the lights were separate but fairly close together. It went behind some trees but I could see it through them. It made a very abrupt turn to the east and seemed to lose altitude and I lost it in the trees as it got lower. I was very excited to see the object. I have never seen anything like it. I forgot to mention that the object was moving from south to north before it made a turn to the east. Also, I could not see a solid object that the lights could have been attached to.
