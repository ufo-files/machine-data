Two orbs in sky over South Jersey

Observed two objects in the sky in South Jersey at approximately 8:49 pm until approximately 9:15 pm. Objects remained in sky, but we were too tired to keep observing. As far as I know, they are still there. 

One was slightly larger than the other. The smaller one stayed to the left of the larger one. Both orbs. Originally when viewed, were changing colors in almost a web-like state (red, blue, green, yellow, orange) but then ended up as white orbs for the rest of the observation. The smaller one seemed to be pulsating and the larger one seemed to be rotating. Hovered mostly but did travel a little heading southwest. I have seen objects like this before in the skies over South Jersey. First time I am sending photos/video.
