A bright and shinny object flying in a straight path, very high speed, height almost like an aiplane in destination city

While was in a traffic jam in Koohsangi Street in Mashhad, Iran, I was looking at sky randomly and then I saw a shinny and bright object croosing a straight path in the sky with a very high speed, hieght certainly much less than an airplane's. The first thought was either an airplane or a shooting star. Then I realized it was way faster than a normal plane and I didn't hear any sound referring a plane and it didn't have any tail to be a shooting star. When I mentioned to others, they said they didn't pay any attention to sky. 
I lost sight of it the moment its path passed behind a building, the same way I noticed it as it appeared.
