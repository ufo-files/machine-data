{nd} 2 bright red orbs/lights that emitted no noise and faded out.

On July 28th, 2015 at 9:38pm I walked outside to let my dog out. Upon reaching the end of my driveway I looked up in the sky looking for stars. Immediately catching my attention were 2 bright red glowing orbs/lights in the north western sky. No stars were out/visible yet so the red orbs in the sky stuck out. After observing the objects for about 10 seconds I grabbed my cell phone out of my pocket and began to film the 2 lights. I filmed the lights for 34 seconds the entire encounter lasted about 2 minuets. The lights floated/flew across the sky at what seemed to be a speed faster then a plane. I see planes regularly over my house and know what they look/sound like during the day and night. These orbs emitted no sound. Over about 2 minuets the orbs floated/flew across the northern sky starting in the north west and ending in the north east. The bottom orb slightly lower and to the left of the top orb faded out first and the top orb faded out a few seconds after. During the sighting I was more focused on watching the objects in real time then watching through my phone I was also unsure my phone would even pick them up. I was in somewhat of an awe during the sighting. I was trying to rationalize what these lights could be. I stopped filming to focus on what I was looking at and maybe 30 seconds to a minuet after I stopped filming both objects had faded out. Upon reviewing the footage from my phone/camera I noticed the objects appear white not red on film. I thought that was strange my camera on my phone is high quality and films in 1080p. I don't know what the objects were but I am quite sure on what they were not. They were not bugs, swamp gas, conventional aircraft, ballons, stars, planets, or comets.
