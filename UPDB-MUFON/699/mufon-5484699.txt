visual contact with the entity out of the disc

In an afternoon my grandmother walked with her brothers for a mountain community when suddenly they heard a buzz and approached the bustling source and suddenly they found a disc on land, so bright and noisy, they have no idea what it was because they had never seen anything like, completely scared seeing the unknown object, a door opened object initially appeared a ladder and then a little man in a gray suit and big head, this entity motioned them to come over to the disc with a rod, but they ignored him and ran back this action very afraid, to get home they were perplexed and could not speak, they did not know what had happened to them, they saw an entity close out the disc and having no knowledge of the phenomenon they thought it was the devil and they did not know what was. They saw a lot of light, a disc in land and an entity out the disc.

They spent many years until they realized what the phenomenon is. Noticing similar cases on tv, newspaper and others, my grandmother tells me that it happened to her and her brothers on that occasion, because before she did not know what that was. 

This event occurred in San Isidro, Choluteca, Honduras in the 1950s
