Looked like birds at first.

Saw an object in sky as I was floating on my back in pool. Thought it was a bird at first but it was moving too quickly and there did not seem to be any wing movement. Noticed it disappeared into rain clouds and then reappeared seconds later. Then two more objects appeared. Thought maybe planes but one object completed a full cirlce in a very short radius. Judging by height and speed and path of travel this was niether bird nor plane. Watched objects move quickly and smoothly through sky in and out of clouds for about five minutes. Not thinking to get a camera.....duh. Then objects disappeared above clouds not to return. You tell me.
