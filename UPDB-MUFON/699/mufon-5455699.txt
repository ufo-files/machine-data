I witnessed a chain of 15 objects that flew in a line and were equally spaced and stretched from horizon to horizon E to W. appearing as satellites.

There was a chain of what initially appeared as satellites in orbit , flying in a linear formation spaced equidistant. They were in a near perfect line that stretched ultimately from the west horizon to the east horizon. As the first one disappeared to the east the last one appeared from the west. The actuall seperation of these objects is unknown given they're hieght but if you held your thumb and forefinger between them they'd measure about 4 inches in seperation. There was no noise . As the last object appeared , an actuall satellite ( as I know them) came into view in ' close' proximity but travelling tangent to the object. They were bright but the light appeared to be reflected vs generated. These were NOT satellites as I'm an avid sky watcher and I've never witnessed anything like this. There were no contrails so I'd suspect these were not high flying military aircraft... especially since there was zero noise and at 6:30 am on a cold morning I'd hear something.
