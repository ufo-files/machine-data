{cak} Triangle in Hooksett NH

I was at a stop sign and observed the triangle hovering over a field. Had blue/white lights at each point and a brighter white light in the middle of the underside. It appeared gray, sun was not up yet. Then the lights dimmed and it slowly started moving in a straight line. I was on a back road so lost sight of it with all the trees and turns of the road. I was not able to get a picture of it, but I have seen the triangle objects several times in the past.
