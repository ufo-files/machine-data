a light from sphere being divided into 4 beams going to 4 directions

1. Night walk at our terrace
2. A sudden sphere of bright light being noticed in to the clouds
3. Initially thought of some light beam being projected from the land but once noticed / observed no trace of projection being found
4. A light sphere in the sky was being divided into four parts like parts of saucers going in to four directions spreading in size & then those saucers were coming back to form a smaller sphere. It was repeated continously as we observed it for 30 minutes & captured the video.
5. I/we were amused to notice it. It was not something seen or observed before.
6. It kept on going but we came down to sleep after our night walk of 30 minutes.
