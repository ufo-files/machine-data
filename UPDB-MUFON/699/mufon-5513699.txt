(GS) 2 Objects (probably orbs) appeared at about 45 degrees above the horizon. After 5-15 seconds of observation they both faded out at the same time.

At the beginning of 2010 I began researching the UFO field. During the months of September and October I wrote a 27 page paper about the universe, flying disc propulsion, the ET disclosure process and in this paper I requested the confirmation of their presence. On October 16th 2010 at 6:45 pm I uploaded this paper to the internet. Approximately 45 minutes later, at 7:36 pm October 16th 2010, I witnessed 2 glowing hovering objects, maybe a mile up, to my east and about 15 degrees north of Jupiter. After 5 to 10 seconds I realized I was seeing something very unusual and after my eyes got really big they both faded out at the same time during a fade out duration of 1 to 2 seconds. During the next 7 weeks I had more than 10 other interactions with the paranormal, ET crafts and beings. The last sighting was a single blinking craft I witnessed with a friend in the early portion of December 2010.

My reaction that first night was that of incredible excitement and elation for about 15 minutes. Then I thought of Jodie Fosters character from the movie "Contact" and the scientist in me kicked in and I documented the event. Then, realizing that they went out of their way to say hello, I went into an incredible level of peace and humility followed by a lot of relief; we are not alone. It was the most purest time in my life. 

I have hand written documentation from that night and a youtube video of myself describing the experiences I had during my contact series. 

This is the second time I have filed this report as the first report seems to have been deleted.
