{dr}Basketball sized orbs of light seen off the back of USS Ronald Reagan (CVN 76)

During our transit from Hong Kong to Hawaii at the tail end of our 2011 deployment, I witnessed three basketball sized orbs appear one at a time and travel in the ships wake. They decended down into the water on a path that I can only decribe as a roller coaster. Each orb took the exact same drop. They were about 20 second in between. With all the countermeasures that ship has I though that maybe I was just seeing something I didn't know the ship had.
