{rjl} Circular object hovering up the street above the houses.

I could only see the bottom side of the object. It had multiple and different colored lights. Blue and orange lights. It gave off no sound, it did not spin, it did not land and, it was moving slowly at first, until I got my father and brother. By that time, less than a minute, it was already at the other end of the road. I do not remember if my father or brother could see it. I will never forget.
