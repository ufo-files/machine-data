{rjl} i watched it disappear. it reappeared in a different path and disappeared

I was about 7 yrs old and shared a room with my older brother.His bed was by the window.One night he told me to come look out the window and he sounded scared.What we both saw was something that was solid white and round.It moved slowly in a straight path and disappeared or jumped at light speed to another part of the sky.it moved a little faster in a different direction and this time it almost seemed to skip like a stone on water.It jumped again to another position,moved in a new direction and then it was just gone.its hard to say if it just disappeared or just traveled at the speed of light .I remember this moment like it was yesterday.When we tried to tell everyone of course it was gone and no one took us seriously.
