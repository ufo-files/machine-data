A bright light, looked like it was falling to the ground, but shortly before it did, it vanished.

I was driving east, heading home after work. Straight stretch of road, all open prairie on the Northside of the road. My coworker said "omg" and pointed slightly to the north. It was a bright light, looked like it was falling to the ground. It was traveling fairly fast. At first I thought it was a plane, but within a few seconds of seeing it, it vanished. I braced myself, expecting the ground to shake from an impact. Nothing. I realized then that there was nothing in that direction, if there was an impact, I would have seen it. At first we were confused. We were both expecting a crash, and nothing happened. My coworker and I had both had odd sense of fear.
