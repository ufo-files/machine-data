{cak} A very bright light appearing, moving, then vanishing.

I was in my room playing on my phone. Throughout the evening I had periodically looked out the window to see the stars, I was about to do this again and something in the night sky caught my eye. At first I thought it was a plane, because a flight path goes right next to my property, but the "plane" didn't have the same light patterns as this object did. The object had a constant bright warm whitish light coming from it. At first it seemed like it was hovering,but then it started to move. I opened my window and stuck my head out to see if it made a noise, it did not. I thought, as the bright object moved closer to the train tracks, my friends property, and my property to get it on video. It took me maybe ten seconds to take my phone off the charger and put it on video, but when I looked up again, the bright object had just vanished. I immediately told my parents, then for the rest of the night (till about 22:00) I stayed up hoping to see something that even resembled what I saw, nothing showed. My parents told me they saw the same thing two weeks ago, that was considerably lower in elevation and towards a different spot next to our property, with no explanation of what it was. Plus there has been more "UFO" sightings (approximately 10) in my area.
