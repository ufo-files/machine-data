{rjl} Two orbs in sky moving N to S. Smaller trailing larger.

Looking at the night sky eastward. Observed one very bright large silver orb moving north to south with a smaller one trailing closely behind. Can't estimate the speed but not moving very fast and flying in a straight line. I turned away just long enough to call for my wife to come see and turned around and they were gone. The larger one was extremely bright and the smaller one was clearly trailing the larger. Neither had time to just fly out of sight from the time I turned away, they just vanished.
