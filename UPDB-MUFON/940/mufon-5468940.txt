I am a street photographer in New York City. I was out on a foot walk shooting the Lower East Side last night for a couple of hours 7-9pm. I was heading north on Ludlow Street and as I was crossing Delancey, I turned facing east, to photograph the Williamsburg Bridge, and oncoming traffic at 8:08 pm. I saw nothing in the sky. It was cloudy and dark out other than city lights. When I got home and brought up my night's photos on my computer, there were lights in the sky. 9 that I can count from my photo. The lights had an organized pattern, sort of like a bird in flight.
