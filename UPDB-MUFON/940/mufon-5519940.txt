2 red-orange glowing lights staggered formation staying in form sped in horizontal path no trails

Standing out on the balcony having a smoke. I looked up because I always look up hoping to see something. And, wouldnt you know it, as soon as I did I finally saw something that amazed me. I actually said Whoa! out loud even being alone. The two objects came from out of sight from the South. Head due North. They gained size as they approached at a phenomenal speed. But, for some reason I feel slight slower than a meteor. when you see meteors they tend to burn up in the atmosphere and you can tell they are losing material and brightness. These objects did not do that they were the same brightness the whole duration of about 2 seconds. They were a strange orange red color unlike most of the shooting stars" Ive seen. That was weird and the fact that they stayed equidistantly apart the whole time with no trails behind them. I can only liken them to fighter jets staying in formation. The were single lights, no noise, way too high, but lower than a satellite, not pulsating and WAY too fast to be anything Ive heard of or seen. Thousands of miles an hour. The lights passed by at a completely horizontal level. Straight across the sky. This was no meteor, they were exactly the same size. When have you seen two meteors following each other that didn't seem to burn up, traveling in a completely horizontal line, with no trails, same color, in a fighter jet formation? I havent.
