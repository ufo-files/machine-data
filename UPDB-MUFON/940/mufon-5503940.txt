{kt}Large fireball fell rapidly from the sky, broke into several pieces & quickly disintegrated before it could hit the ground.

I was walking outside at approximately 11:15 PM south of Boston. I caught something bright streaking across the sky like a shooting star, but when I looked it was much larger than a star appears and it was much closer. It fell rapidly, unmistakably toward the Earth, broke into several pieces and disintegrated within a few seconds. As far as I could tell it never hit the ground. I believe I may have seen the same object that was reported as Case #54969 (from Jefferson, ME).
