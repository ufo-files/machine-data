So tonight my neighbor and I seen 5 star-like objects flying through the sky. 4 of them looked just like stars but moving swiftly across the sky. No flashing light, no sound, no nothing, they looked just like the stars but moving. The 5th object was super bright orange and was triple the size of the others. It went straight across the sky super fast, we thought it was a shooting star at first, then it turned and came streaking across the other way through the sky, this time its light got dimmer, just like it had hit a dimmer switch. It got lighter and lighter till it faded away. I looked up the ISS and there is NO WAY none of the objects could have been the ISS. The ISS was nowhere near my location. It was completely dark, stars out, clear night, airplanes flying in the sky with blinking lights on and sound. These lights looked just like stars no sound, super-fast, and that one dimmed its lights and turned around. We tried to record but the objects wouldn't show up on my ST66 Samsung camera. What could this be?
