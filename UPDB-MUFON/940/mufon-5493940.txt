{cak} Men in black

I woke up in bed, walked down stairs and saw two men in black suits approx. 6' tall close cut blackhair talking to my mother. As they saw me they turned to run towards me. I ran back up the stairs, into my room and jumped out my closed second story window, through the glass. As I hit the ground I would wake up again in bed and walk down the stairs to see the " men in black again. I would run up into my room and jump out the window again. This process reoccurred at least five times. Each time I would be more hesitant to walk down the stairs again. This happened over and over until I finally walked downstairs and they weren't there.
