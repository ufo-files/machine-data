Something with strange changing flashing lights came over the field and house from my left as it got closer it dropped and moved in erratic motions lowering and moving left and right up and down in no way a plane or helicopter could move. There were several different colored lights. It then started to hover and lower and hang in one spot. My brother and I felt like it was observing us so we ran back in the house and saw it shoot back up in the sky as we ran.
