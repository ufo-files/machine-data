Following me while driving

I was driving in the Nevada desert between jackpot Nevada and Wells Nevada. It was approximately 5 AM in the morning. As I was driving ice saw to my right a light that was approximately 100 feet up in the air. I was driving approximately 65 mph. The light kept up with me for approximately 35 miles. It was a very bright light. I actually looked around and was wondering where the light was coming from including turning my headlights off and on because I could not explain why this light was out there. As I drove there were some hillsides to the right of me which the light would disappear behind the hills and then reappear on the other side of the hills. As daylight began to break the object ascended to the skyline. It was a boomerang shaped object that had sky blue lights on it. As the sun rose, because the object had blue lights, it eventually disappeared. It was really strange because at no time did I think about pulling over and stopping to see what this light was. Today as I think about this incident, I wonder why the thought didn’t come through for me to stop and check this thing out. Because I am a very inquisitive person. This experience has affected me deeply and I know without a shadow of a doubt that it was a UFO
