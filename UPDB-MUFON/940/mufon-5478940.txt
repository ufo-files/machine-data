I was in my living room and happened to look out my window and seen a ball of light. Just after I noticed the ball of light and started to observe it there was a very loud BOOM! it shook my whole house and was heard and felt about 3 blocks over. There has been no official report but we did hear of increased police presents with out explanation a couple blocks from our house. No one will tell us why and everyone ignores all my questions. I an certain that what I seen was not part of this world or at least not part of any technology that has been released to the public.
