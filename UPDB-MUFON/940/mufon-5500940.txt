UFO Hovered Above Trees, Noticed while Driving

While driving home from work June 21st around 8am, I noticed an object hovering above the tree line about 1 mile to the south. At first I thought it may be a crop plane. I stopped and watched the object for about 5 min and it did not move in any direction. I wanted to drive closer to have a look but many of the roads were washed out due to the high overland flooding in the area so I was unable to drive for a closer look. After i took a picture of the object I continued my drive home.
