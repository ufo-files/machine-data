{cak} Observed a black triangle shaped object cross over the hwy then appear to hover over the trees beside the hwy before loosing sight of the object

My wife, daughter and I were traveling south bound on hwy 515 in Cherokee co. Ga when we observed two white lights above the trees and slightly to our left that appeared to be rather bright for a normal airplane and also lacking the typical strobe observable on small planes at night. As i drove, we watched the lights drift left to right over the hwy slowly. I began to decelerate (because there was almost no other traffic) to get a better look. As it reached the treeline to my right we could all see clearly a black triangle form above and to the right of us. It had two white lights on the rear of the triangle and one pale lt. green light on the front tip of the triangle. By the time i was able to completely stop we were slightly past where it was and we lost sight of it just beyond the treeline in front of us. I got out of our vehicle and could here no engine noise of any kind. My wife and daughter were able to keep a better i on it as i was trying to drive and they both stated that it was at a complete stop, hovering above the trees as we passed it.
