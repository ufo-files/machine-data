Looked up saw a lighted boomerang shape object

Was out side close up the chicken coup. Clear night without clouds, looking straight up and facing east , saw a strand of lights (4) in a V like formation traveling east, the right side tilted downward and the object started moving south east. It was silent. The shape was like a boomerang and appeared to have a dome, which I could see with the ambient light, the shape was much darker that the background and foreground. I watch it for 1 minute before it was out of site. I manage a picture that outlines it pretty well.
