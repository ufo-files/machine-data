{nd} orange object seen making very fast and unusual flight path

Myself along with four other witnesses observed a bright orange-yellow colored orb ascend from the foothills across the valley and the object began to take a very unusual flight path. It's accent was very fast until once it was high in the sky it began bouncing right and left at a weird hover. It did this for about two minutes before it moved extremely fast side to side and it's light flickered before disappearing. This event followed another sighting that occurred minutes before in roughly the same location as the detailed sighting. First sighting was witnessed by myself and one other person but the second sighting was observed by myself and four other people as well as the guys at the gas station down the road saw also.
