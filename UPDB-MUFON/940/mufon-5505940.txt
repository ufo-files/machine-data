Bright static light over Bracknell - Rapidly descended

I was in the kitchen, having a drink (approx 3.30am) looking out from the kitchen window.
I noticed a very bright white light (almost tear drop shaped) in the sky which appeared low and quite close, I opened the window to see if I could hear anything (rule out helicopter) and there was no noise, certainly wasnt a plane, nothing I could think of fitted its description.
Then the light/object just dropped at very high speed at angle behind the house at the back of my garden. This all happened in about 5-10 seconds.
It almost left a tail in its wake where it moved so quickly.
I was gobsmacked (never seen anything like it before) and kept trying to move my head to see if it was my eyes catching a light (it DEFINATELY wasnt).
