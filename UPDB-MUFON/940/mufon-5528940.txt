I was driving home from work and pulling into my neighborhood around O'Connor and 1604 (suburbs in north San Antonio) at about 9:20pm, I saw two white bright lights flying slowly ahead, they appeared to be a a relatively low altitude, as it flew closer and over my car a third bright white light turned on in the front of the craft revealing a triangular shape, as it passed overhead I could clearly make out the shape against the sky that was lit up from San Antonio city lights and I clearly saw the black triangular shape, it might had have some very dim lights in the center but not nearly as bright, it made no noise and I stared at it until it disappeared over the treeline still moving fairly slow going west/southwest. did anyone else see this!?
