{nd} White spherical shaped object located in the sky west of my home in Scottsdale AZ. Hovering for a short while

Just arrived home from Tucson a short time ago and when out on the patio noticed a white spherical object located above and to the west of my home. Got a binoculars. It appeared tear drop shaped, then became round. It had the appearance of a small moon. I noticed it around 6:08 then disappeared quickly about 10 minutes after that. No sound, no movement, just hovered. This is not the first time I have seen odd phenomena in the sky here. I am intrigued by the sighting. It slowly disappeared as if a cloud was dissipating in warm air, which is common in this climate this time of year. My first thought was it was a large balloon.
