Elongated object travelling extremely fast with no sound

I took a picture of sunset on smartphone and accidentally took it as a live shot. I looked through the frames later to choose the best one to keep and noticed what I thought was a mark on a few of the frames. I looked more closely and appeared to have caught something travelling at high speed from right to left and then disappearing vertically upwards behind a tree. If it was behind the tree it must have been some distance away and quite large. I created a video of the live shot and slowed the video down as well. I’ve also taken individual screen shots from the video.
