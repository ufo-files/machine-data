west central florida;

they are both at the same altitude no matter what position north or west.
my first instinct was to rush and get my camera however the fact is there is no reason to do that becaause this has gone on for a week and i can walk outside right now and they are there.
i only have a set of binoculars so defining a shape is difficult but i can see the changing of the lights clearly, also the white lights that are moving around them.
i have looked to see if there were any ufo sightings in my area and i could not find any
i would love to know what they are they definitly not a aircraft, if they are they have the ability to hoover for hours but the small white lights around them just does not make sense.
