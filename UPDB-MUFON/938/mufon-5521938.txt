Formation of about 7 orange crescent-shaped lights in the sky. They moved very slowly then faded away.

I was driving on Braddock Rd at almost midnight on 12/23/2011 when I noticed something irregular in the sky that caught my eye. Because these lights in the sky were almost the same orange color as the street lamps, I checked to make sure if I wasn't mistaken. I pulled over into a residential neighborhood to get a better look and the lights were indeed in the sky. About five lights were in a circular formation while two others just seemed to trail behind. There was no blinking, and movement was very slow to the point of non-existent. After no more than three minutes, these lights just seemed to dim, get smaller, and fade into the sky. While the first seven lights were fading out, an additional light appear in the sky behind everything, only to also fade out. I'm still feeling quite disturbed.
