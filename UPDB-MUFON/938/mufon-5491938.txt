{jpg} Weirdest lights in the sky, various colors

I was walking down the road,returning from a friend's house. Most people in my village are asleep by this time. My road dips down on a preettty steep angle, going to a beach (Hobart's Beach) on the Avon River. Out of nowhere, comes this light, pulsating between a deep red and pure white high in the sky, about a mile over where the water would be. Then, the thing exploded into 10 lights in a disc like hover, and flew right over me. then, they all combined and disappeared. I ran into my house, and called my sister, and she told me to come and fill out this form. Then. I heard this bomb-like noise followed by a loud deep humming noise, like a ship's horn but way deeper. Outside there was another red light, this time not pulsating, and it split into two, returned to one, and dived lightning fast into what would be the water. I then turned all the lights on in my house and hid in the bathroom. That is where I am as I write this.
