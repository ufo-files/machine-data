{nd} 3 bright orange orb shaped in triangular formation

In my sons room opening his window to see if it was cool enough to leave open tonight I happened to look up and saw 3 bright orange lights. The lights did not blink but glowed the whole time. I found it weird that they were in a perfect triangle. They didn't look to be moving. I called my husband and we ran outside but by then there was only 1 light left. It just hovered and then it just disappeared. I have never seen anything like it before and made me feel very uneasy.
