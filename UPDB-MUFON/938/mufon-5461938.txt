{cak} Unexplained light or lights

I live in a rural area on a farm. On the night in question I took my dog down the yard at the back of my house. I noticed an extremely bright light (to the north) which did not appear to be at a high altitude. I'm pretty useless with the camera on my phone but I took some photos and video footage. The light was stationary and then moved dramatically. I don't think it made any sound. To be honest I was a bit un-nerved by it as it was so unusual. I observed it for a while and then went back into my house - again I was a little bit un-nerved by it. I looked out of one of my windows later and could still see in around 10-15 minutes later but in a NW direction. In some of the footage you can see a row of lights in the bottom left corner - that is the local town (Cookstown) which is around 4 miles from my house. There is nothing else but fields in the direction of the unexplained light. Having looked at the footage again I think there may have been 3 lights when it was at a higher altitude. I will send some photos and footage but I may have more - I accidentally deleted some photos on my phone but they had been saved on my laptop. Please ask if you are interested. Also I have no idea about the elevation of the light - it was overhead. I could also forward a photo taken in daylight or at night of the exact same spot as it is now - without the light for comparison.
