Red glowing spheres flying slowly in triangular formation heading north. Dissapeared one at a time.

At about 10:25p.m. on 7-14-10 i walked out my front door facing south just to feel a cool breeze.As soon as i opened the door i saw approximatly 6 red spheres flying in a sort of triangular formation extremely slow north bound.Icalled my wife and children to the door and we watched this for about two minutes before they dissapeared one at a time.We were all convinced immediately that it was a U.F.O.not of this world.It was kind of a scary thing,the kind of thing that makes the hair on your neck stand on end.
