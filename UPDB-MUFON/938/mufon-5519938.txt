Thought it was a shooting star, but it changed direction and hovered.

I was on South Main Street in Brewer, Maine around 11:40pm-midnight. My fiance, and two friends of ours were pulling out of my friends apartment. I, who was sitting in the passenger seat, was looking up at the sky because I have a fascination with space and stars, etc. I got all excited because while I was watching the sky, I thought I saw a shooting star. However, it was going way too slow to be a shooting star. It was also orb like, with a bright blue & green color coming from it. It was going down path like, and then suddenly changed direction and did a couple zigzags before it hovered for a few moments. I tried getting everyones attention, but by the time they all looked it went below the treeline. I was confused, I really have never believed in aliens, but it definitely was not a shooting star and it really makes you think sometimes. When I got on facebook after, I was going down my news-feed, and somebody had posted a status seeing the same thing so I commented and asked him what he saw, and he described his sighting as the same thing!
