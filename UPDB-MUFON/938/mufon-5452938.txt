It was a dark cigar shaped object with flashing lights of red green orange and white going around it in the oblong direction.g

I am not sure if it was 2000 or 2001 when my son and I where returning home from Football practice. He was a player and i was an assistant coach. We had just passed the Boseman Farm Home when we both observed a cigar shaped object hovering at a slight angle over a water pumping station in the center of the field. We looked at each other and both said is that a Blimp. I think we both agreed at that same moment it was not a Blimp. We could see it was dark in color and we could see lights going around the outside of it. It appeared to be 100 to 150 ft long and 25 to 30 ft around. We had pulled off the side of the road to watch this object and we probably watched it for 10 minutes. It appeared to be tethered to the water pumping station and as we watched it the lights appeared to go around it faster and then it slowly rose up and started to move forward following Hwy 97 westward. We followed it for a few minutes as it continued to gain altitude and in a flash of light it was gone.We watched as the light got further and further away. My son and I both agreed it was strange and we did not know what we had just witnessed. We may have talked about it a few times and we have joked about it from time to time but we have never reported it to this day
