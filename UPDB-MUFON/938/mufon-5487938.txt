We were driving home from Waterford, saw that we needed gas and pulled off I75 to John R to get it. Wife noticed it in the sky and started filming it. It was really bright and we were facing west and it was traveling east and turned and went south then started coming back at us. Then we lost it when we started for the expressway after getting gas.
