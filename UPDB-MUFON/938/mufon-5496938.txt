Moving light in sky. Then moved at a speed like nothing on earth

The summer of 2012 in North East Edmonton about 2 blocks from Londondary Mall, A few friends and I where having a fire in a back yard when I looked up to the sky and saw a bluish white orb like light just floating across the sky like a satellite but was way to big to be a satellite. I pointed at it and asked my friend, "What is that?". Every one looked and said it was probably a plane or a satellite when all of a sudden I heard a plane and I pointed to it and said "Hey guys, that is a plane I hear and see and that orb is closer to us then the plane. So what is it?" At that moment, the orb went from moving in one direction horizontally to moving so fast and leaving our atmosphere to make it look to us like the orb flew away and hid behind a star. The most amazing thing I have ever seen.
