{cak} First thought it was a meteor. Didn't disintegrate then changed direction

I was outside having a cigarette before going to bed. I was looking up at the stars. It was a perfectly clear night. I saw what I assumed to be a meteor entering the atmosphere travelling from West to East. I watched and waited for it to burn out but it never did. Instead it split into 2 lights. Both lights were about as bright as medium stars and a bit fuzzy. I then thought I was seeing the white underside of the wings of a large bird. There is a large owl that visits from time to time. This seemed to make sense due to the two objects not moving in a straight line but slowly swerving in and out from each other. As the lights moved further East (NE from my perspective), they moved much further apart (too far apart for the bird theory) and then changed direction and both traveled together due south. They then disappeared behind some trees. The things that I found strangest were how fast they were moving and how silent it was.
