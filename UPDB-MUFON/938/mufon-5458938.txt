{jpg} 2 in the morning driving around with a friend. We spoted a low bright light in the middle of field just off the county road.

While out for a late night early morning drive. Myself and a friend spoted a bright white light hanging low in a field of an old county road in Central Texas. We stopped and watch this bright light for a second trying to figure out what it was. Both of us had no explanation. We started to chalk it up and head home as we started to pick up speed in the car the bright light held back in the field for a moment then began to follow us down the county road. In shock we stopped again this time we both got out of the car and watch as this huge dark triangle shaped object hovered us at about 300 ft above. it was huge silent and like nothing we ever have seen.
