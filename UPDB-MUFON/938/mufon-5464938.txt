{cak} Intense glowing orb viewed on nighttime flight from Iceland to Ohio

WOW Flight 113 on Oct 23, 2018 from KEF to CLE. Departure 21:10 GMT. After a nap I woke and looked out of the window, as I had the window seat. I witnessed an orb of intense glowing light well below the airplane's left wing. The glowing orb was sandwiched between the cloud layers. This phenomenon kept pace with the aircraft. It hid a little under a thin cloud cover below us at maybe 34,000 ft, but was situated above a thicker mass of clouds. We were flying above the Northern Atlantic Ocean. I guess we were either right before or just after the southern tip of Greenland. The orb's fiery glow could be seen permeating the thin cloud cover above it while reflecting its light off of the thicker layer of clouds below. Its color was hot white, with some hot orange (think molten metal). Occasionally the thin cloud layer opened up so I could see it clearly. I woke my husband to witness it too. I had to be sure it wasn't somehow moonlight reflecting off of the Labrador Sea/North Atlantic Ocean, as it was a full moon. I studied it for 10 minutes trying to come up with a reasonable explanation. Then it vanished for about 5 minutes, but I kept watch. It reappeared for another 5 minutes and then disappeared altogether. That proved to me it wasn't a natural occurring event because the clouds remained, the moon remained, the weather was the same - but the UFO was gone. Looking back I should have alerted the stewardess. I am putting this out there in case other people on the flight saw it too.
