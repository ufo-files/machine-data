{nd} Pictures taken, objects in pictures noticed later

My neighbor was with a friend at a lake behind my property. He took some pictures on his phone and only noticed the objects in the air in his pictures later. He saw and heard nothing when he took the pictures. He sent me a picture that I am submitting that has 2 objects. These are not raindrops on the camera or birds etc, as he sent me more pictures of objects that were taken later. At his home he expanded the picture on his phone and you can clearly see the upper object is a classical disc UFO. I submitted pictures to this site that I took on my property in 2006 of an object that looks like the lower object in this new photograph. My neighbor also mentioned that some friends he knows were in that area that day and they saw 4 objects in the sky. My neighbor has also witnessed strange light phenomena behind his place, in the direction of my property. I am extremely familiar with different aircraft, as I am a retired Air Force aircraft technician.
