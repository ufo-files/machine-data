My wife and I went to the PA UFO bigfoot conference saturday, day. That night we went on a Halloween Haunted Hayride at Bushy Run Battlfield. Our appointment was at 7:45 and we got there around 7:25. We checked in, got some coco and waited for the hayride to start. I was looking at all of the planes in the sky and noticed a white light coming from beyond the trees. I thought it was a satellite. It moved slowly then started darting and slowing, darting a slowing, and passed slightly over my head. Then it reversed direction of travel for a short distance and started to hover and kind of wobble, making very small circles and half circles. I was mesmerized and my wife then asked me if I '"saw that". I asked what she was referring to and she told me and it confirmed what I had seen. The ticket taker told us to board the tractor and we didn't pay any attention to it furthur.
