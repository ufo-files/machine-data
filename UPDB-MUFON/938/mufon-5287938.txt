Cigar shaped object with lights

At a party with some friends outside playing basketball. Then when someone pointed out some funny looking stars, we all noticed that they were much brighter than the rest. Then we saw the horizontal string of 8-10 lights start to move in unison. Upon further examination we noticed that it was a saucer/cigar shaped object that had exterior lights all in a row. it had hovered down below the cloud line. Then It moved slowly to the right, as if it were studying something from a distance. After around 10 seconds of it moving around, the object got smaller and the lights faded away as if it were flying away in the opposite direction of us.
