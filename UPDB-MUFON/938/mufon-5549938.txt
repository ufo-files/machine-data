Retracting beam of light along Colorado River in Utah

Four adults were camping in a canyone along the Colorado River in Utah. Thewitness and a second (nonreporting) woman were outside observing the night sky.The males were either in their tent or not present. A bright white light with abluish border appeared over a nearby mountain. It shone a white beam edged inblue angularly toward the ground. Momentarily the leading edge of the beamretracted into the light source. Duratio of the event was about 10 seconds.
