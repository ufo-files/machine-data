Double-colored hovering object over San Jos1, Costa Rica

Returning back from work I realized there was an unfamiliar luminous object hovering at middle altitude over the area of San Jos1, near to the Santa Ana county, which suddenly moved North towards the area of San Rafael de Alajuela, hovering again for 3 to 5 seconds. After that it began ascending, by the time I had started my cellphone camera. All details afterwards are in the video. It moved at constant speed, invariable from beginning to end of path, non forced stops and seemed to rotate from time to time, exposing what I believe might be two sides of a presumably circular, compact structure which faced red lights on one side and green ones on the other. The object disappeared behind the roofs of the neighborhood in direction to Santo Domingo de Heredia or possibly towards San Pedro de Montes de Oca. It was clear that this was NOT an airplane, helicopter or drone, I am used to see them all since childhood and I can tell the difference. It was NOT a meteor nor a balloon nor any kind of human device known so far. I wish it might have come closer or landed close to my position but it was not the case. I don't care if it was ET, all I'd like is to know if there's anything here they are looking for. I can be quite direct I did not forge this with any kind of digital graphics software, I am a call center agent with no intentions to lose my job for any fakes.
