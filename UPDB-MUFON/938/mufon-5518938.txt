Unidentified objects cylinder shape passes over Ipswich Queensland Australia.

I'm a firm ufo beliver and attend the local Ipswich ufo sociey meetings on a regular basis, for the last few months I've been setting up my video camera near a window on a tripod and letting it run unattended for about 1 hour 15 minites, I wasn't having much luck during the daytime so I switched to nighttime and on the night of the 7th of April 2012 I got lucky and this is the image I captured, the video was taken from my address in the heart of Ipswich, which is 22 miles west of Brisbane, I had the camera pointed North Northeast and angled up to about 40 degrees above the horizon, the video camera I used was a JVC GZ-HD520AG high definition, later when I stopped the camera and hooked it up to my recorder as I always do and as I viewed the part where the objects appeared I had at first thought it was a meteorite breaking-up but when I went back and checked later I was certainly surprised to find that they were in fact objects, two of the objects displayed flashes which I think are propulsion bursts as they passed over, I was hoping by sending you this short video clip that you would do an analysis and let me know what you find.
