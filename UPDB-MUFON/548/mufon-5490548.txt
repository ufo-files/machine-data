{nd} A flash in the morning sky

As I was leaving New Mexico I traveled on HWY 285 south towards Clines Corner; a truck and travel stop on I-40, from Santa Fe. 4 miles to the north of Clines Corner a very bright light appeared in the sky. A long curved craft appeared but almost instantly began cloaking itself. It was moving east to west and it disappeared little by little until gone. The rest of my journey was uneventful.
