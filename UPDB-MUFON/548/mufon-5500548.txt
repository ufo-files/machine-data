{cak} Came up from the west heading NE. Bright red and then would fade to white. Approximately 30 of them. I have video of the objects.

My husband and I just arrived home around 9:00 PM. My husband took our dog out for a short walk. A couple of minutes later he ran back in the house and said you need to see this!! I went out with him and we watched around 30 of these objects going up from the west. One at a time. They started off bright Red and slowly hovered and then would move a short distance and stop. Then they would move again and each time would start to change from red to white. Each one would rise and then hover and would slowly move towards the north from our home. One by one they woul rise, some times we could see five or six but then they would go away. I filmed the objects on my phone for about 20 minutes. Eventually, We could not see them, they just dissappeared. I have lived in NM all my life and have never seen such a thing. There was lightning going on at the time so it made it easy to see them.
