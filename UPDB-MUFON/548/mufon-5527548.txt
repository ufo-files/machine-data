I was looking at the full moon when a fast, very fast star-light object ---

In Feb. of 2010 I was looking at the full moon. I was standing outside on the pier were I live. The moon was about three hand widths above the horizon. Too orientate readers the top of the moon is north to the left is west. About 20 degrees to the left or west I watched a fast very fast star-light object shoot out from behind the moon. The object moved about 12 inches from the moon, slightly away, that is outward from the moons orbit. It then turned more outward and was gone in less then three seconds!
