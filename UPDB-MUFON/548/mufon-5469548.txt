{rjl} Sitting in living room. Looked up to high window saw 5 craft close. Jumped up craft moved back and up. Then changed positions while going higher.

I was watching tv. The house power started going on and off. I felt my skin prickeled and looked up at high window to see if weather was bad. I saw out side window 5 crafts that were hovering and as they did they were sorta dipping and had lights flashing. I jumped up and they then began moving back from my house and changed positions and rose up higher. I ran to my kitchen to get my charging cell phone and turned on camera video to see the last one about 500ft up moving SE of my house. It was pausing interminently and moving away. It was round and it had a spin and lights that flashed in a pattern. The skin of the craft was grey and smooth. I was shook up and realized I was sick to my stomach during and after.
