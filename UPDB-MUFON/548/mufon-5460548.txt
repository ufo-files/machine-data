{tjd} Bright lights putting on a show above the clouds at 3k feet elevation

While camping in spot 16 at the Juniper campground on Mount Diablo at 3,000 feet in elevation, the 3 of us spotted 2 (and near the end of viewing it for several hours, what appeared to be 3) bright lights in the sky. These lights would appear sometimes at the same time, sometimes one at a time and get very bright yellow then start to fade out and you could see flickers of light colors like red, orange yellow and green. These lights appeared above the clouds and there were no distant mountains or anything’s in view at the height we were at in that direction (we double checked in the morning, it was just sky as far as the eyes could see) We watched for long periods of time over the course of 3 hours starting around 9pm as the starts were all coming out and the night sky began to light up with stars. The lights stayed in the same general location the whole time while shifting back and forth over a small part of the distant sky and even up and down and then would disappear and reappear. For shorter periods of time they would glow so bright, especially when one crossed over top of the other one as they were hovering side to side on multiple occasions. An airplane or two appeared in the same area multiple times and even appeared to be checking out the strange lights. It was a neat experience to bear witness to. The video attached shows one of the lights when it was at its brightest but the camera skews what it actually looked like to the naked eye. The other video at sunset shows the general area in which we were viewing from all night.
