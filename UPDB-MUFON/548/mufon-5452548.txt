OBJECT Hovering !

Night before Dad bought me a new 22 rifle we mounted a scope & Dad said tomorrow morning going to the range. Next morning dad told me & my brother to put the 22’s in the Pinto. We lived by the beach. Surf City, NC. So we grab the guns and was on the deck. They were a lot of bird sing and flying. So we pretend to shoot the birds. After about a minute or so all the birds were gone and no singing anymore? We were puzzled we looked around and saw the UFO Hovering grey in color and no noise. We looked through the 4 x power scopes but only made closer no real details. So I told my brother to keep eye on them I was going to get my Dads spotting scope. I open the screen door and yelled Mom, Dad their is a UFO outside they laughted at me I ran to the back room to get the spotting scope and came outside it was no where to be seen? Brother said they left / Disappeared. My Mom & Dad said nothing was in the sky when they went outside? The drive to the range received along speech about you saw a plane or helicopter? My Dad we as in USMC both me & my Brother seen plenty of planes & Helicopters. This was a Disk hovering with the sun up with few clouds. No windows and only grey color. I have been looking in the sky ever since with no avail. I just want to share my encounter. Thank you.
