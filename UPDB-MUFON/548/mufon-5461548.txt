{jpg} Saw a craft circling around my neighborhood several times at various altitudes

Around 8:00 PM EST on April the 2nd I was in my upstairs bathroom. I happened to take a look out the window and saw a craft flying by with red and white flashing lights. At first, I just figured it was a commercial airplane or something, but then I noticed the craft all of a sudden take dip in altitude. Commercial airplanes don't do that. At this point, my curiosity was piqued, and I continued to watch the craft. I noticed it kept circling around my street and dipping up and down at various altitudes. Each time it would come from the north and make a clockwise circle. This went on for about 10-15 minutes before I eventually lost track of it. I don't know if this was an extraterrestrial craft or some sort of secret military craft, but it definitely wasn't a plane.
