(GS) Lights moved rapidly across the sky, changed direction and dissapeared

I was returning to the Riverside Resort Hotel, walking along the river walk (Colorado River) in the rear of the building. I happen to look up and see two lights moving rapid in a tandem formation (equivalent of an inch apart). At about the same time, the lights accelerated at a tremendous rate of speed. While the objects increased acceleration, their direction changed and the object on the right appeared to play catch-up, meaning his arch was wider during the course of the direction change.

I work at an airport and am very familiar with jet aircraft. They do not fly this fast. Also, with jet aircaft, the higher they are, the slower they appear to fly. These objects appeared to be high in the sky did not emit any sounds. I did not observed any blinking lights. The objects were solid white lights. A private aircraft flew North over the Colorado river and the resorts a few seconds later.

Its a little difficult to describe the feeling and its still with me as I describe this event. Its as if, you been told something didn't exist and now you see something tangible that contradicts what youve been told. Its not fear, not anxiety, I guess with me, its an exuberant sort of feeling.

I lost sight of them as they made a swooping turn and disappeared behind the building towards the mountains.
