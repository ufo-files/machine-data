Daytime white stationary light in sky that wasnt there about 20 minutes later

I had just finished my daily walk when I saw a probably white light in the sky (sunset was occurring around then). It was about 8:30 p.m. then. It must have been between 45 degrees and 90 degrees above the horizon; Id guess about 70 degrees above the horizon. Whenever I started looking around, I would lose sight of the object, and it would take a few seconds to find it again. The object was stationary, as far as I could tell, with sunset, planes, and clouds drawing my attention every couple of minutes. I thought it was just a balloon or a star, but it seemed to not move at all, meanwhile a cloud was changing its position every 30-seconds-to-a-minute, so I don't think it was a balloon. Then, sometime before 9:30 p.m., Id guess about 8:47 p.m., the object wasnt there anymore when I tried to look for it. I thought it had disappeared behind a cloud, but the object still wasnt there even after the cloud passed.
