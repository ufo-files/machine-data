Pulsating UAP observed outside my home showing abnormal aerial abilities and colors.

Was on my front porch having a smoke when I observed an object pulsating just above my treeline. I assumed it was a helicopter or small plane but when it started showing abnormal aerial maneuvers and having not observed any exterior or clearance lightning on the object I proceeded to grab my video camera and shot about 4 minutes of footage in which the video shows it hovering then move abruptly to the northwest and it was pulsating various colors towards the middle and a yellowish whitish bright glow around the exterior as it was a sphere like shape. Having seen a commercial airplane fly over my home maybe 10 minutes prior to witnessing this object I knew exactly what I was looking at when the jetliner flew by as I saw the blinking red, blue and white lights and obvious sound it made yet this other object I observed made no apparent sound which threw me off so definitely a first for me.
