{kt}Saw what appeared to be a large craft going slowly across sky

On February 22, 2014 at 7;20 am, I was on my way home from work when I noticed what appeared to be a very large cylinder in the sky. At first I thought it was stationary until I pulled over to get a better look.It seemed to move slowly in a North West direction. I could not see any wings or contrail and from my distance it seemed to be much bigger than a jet. To me it seemed 2 or 3 times bigger but I know it is hard to judge something that high up. I will say this definitely caught my attention when I saw it.
