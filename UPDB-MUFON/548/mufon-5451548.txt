UFO seen over my roof

I was on my roof with my son taking sunset and airplane photos (NIKON D3500 with a
70/300 Zoom. I noticed a tiny black object that did not look like anything I usually observe such as Planes, birds and Helium Balloons.
The object was flying from the West to the South, and I was able to take 5 photos.
It is only when I viewed the photos on my laptop that I realized this was no common object.
