{dr}Silver pulsating craft which was disc shaped.At least 100 ft.long

It was the last weekend of Aug in 1993.We were trying to get home to Duluth,MN.We were both teachers who had to get back before Labor Day.I told my family I was going to drive all night to accomplish our goal.We were somewhere South of Branson,MO.It was around 3:30 a.m. when a silver pulsating disc decended over the trees weaving in and out and up and down. This happened for what I thought was about 45 minutes.My daughter was very frightened.She was 16 yrs.old.She was sitting in the passenger's seat and saw everything with me.The next thing that happens is that we are awoken by the mooing of cows facing the pasture.The strange thing was my daughter was no longer next to me. It was my wife.We had purchased a brand new Dodge Conversion Van and I would never let my wife or daughter crawl over a new consol witha colored moniter and sratch it with their shoes especially while driving. It was about 7:30 a.m. My wife was up front and my daughter and son were in back.We missed approximately 4 hours of time.
