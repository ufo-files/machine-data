While driving West on the Main street of the town, noticed 2 odd-looking, very bright, "flaming" type objects. I assumed they were probably "Chinese lanterns" that many people "send up". I watched them for about 3 minutes, and BOTH of the objects STOPPED, and were hovering- and the colors changed from a flaming red, to orange, and then a bright white, then to green! Right then I knew that 1) Chinese lanterns do not "hover", nor 2) do they randomly flash solid white, then green. Both objects then began a slow course (MUCH slower than an airplane/helicopter), and while driving home, noticed that they had "changed course" from SW to due North. When I returned home, (a 5 minute drive) and got out of the car- they were no longer visible! I have seen "Chinese lanterns", planes, helicopters, etc.....and these 2 objects could have easily been mistaken for those lanterns- IF you were not "paying attention" to the randomly, bright flashing colors! These objects ALSO had no sequentially-flashing lights as an airplane would, and as far as I know- Chinese lanterns do NOT change from flame red, to orange-red, to bright white, to green!
