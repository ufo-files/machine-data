6-7 disks arranged in an arc, moved from overhead to behind trees in 5-6 seconds

The night was clear and dark, I noticed something right overhead and looked up. An arc of 5-6 discs were directly over my head and silently moved from SSW to NNE. I lost sight of them behind tall pines that are 150' from where I was standing. No sound and never changed speed. Camp LeJeune is 30 miles North and I could hear the helicopters but none were visible. This was nothing like any military craft that fly over. I would guess they were 500-1000' up but I'm not sure.
