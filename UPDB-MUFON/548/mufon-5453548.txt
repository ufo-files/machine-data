{rjl} Bright, round, stationary object

We were outside observing Venus when a bright, round object appeared for approximately three seconds. It was glowing, reddish across the center, rose-colored along the edges. It was at least a mile away and was the size of a penny in the sky. It made no sound, was constant (no blinking or moving), and simply appeared then disappeared.
