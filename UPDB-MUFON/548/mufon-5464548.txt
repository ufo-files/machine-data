{cak} Hidden in the clouds just west of Disneyland

I was leaving the garage, when I noticed some rays of light which seemed strangely short. The more I looked, I realized there seemed to be something in the clouds, but very slight and difficult to see with the naked eye. So, I captured the sky in a pic. Within a couple of days, I recalled that I needed to check the pic on my PC and enhance the lighting which sometimes reveals much more than meets the eye. I realized there was an object which is what I could barely see with my own eyes. I had to leave and couldn't stick around to wait for the clouds to dissipate. I looked up the weather around that time and July 10th is the only day stated as partial sun, even though I originally estimated July 6th as the day of the pic. We had been having lots of sunny days at that time. It was an unusual weather pattern with strange, short rays of light and something which appeared to be in the clouds.
