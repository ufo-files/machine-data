{kt}Two orange balls of light travelled towards me, making no noise. Then they altered paths and continued a path until they were out if my sight. They were intensely bright.

I was just leaving my job at 9 pm, Saturday, Oct. 19th. As I walked towards the driveway that led to the parking lot, I saw 2 orange balls of light traveling towards me, from over Geneva Lake, at Williams Bay. They were traveling slowly towards me, I kept listening for copter blade noise, but there was none. I then thought the glowing brilliant orange globes are larger then a helicopter would be, so I felt very perplexed what it was , I was seeing. The orange balls of light then made a 901 turn, and continued to move away from me until I could no longer see them . I was astonished at how blazing orange they were, and left wondering what I had just seen.
