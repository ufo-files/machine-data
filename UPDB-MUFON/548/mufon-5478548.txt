I saw a blue/white glowing orb appear almost directly above then moved in a straight line very fast.

I was standing in by backyard just past midnight when a blue/white glowing orb appeared almost directly above me.The size and proximity to the ground was absoloutly shocking.So was the bright light that appeared to be what it was made up of.There were no clouds at all in the sky it was a clear night.All of a sudden it was there above me and within a couple seconds it was moving west and out of sight as fast as anything I've ever seen in my life.This direction is commonplace for regular aircraft as it's the route most commercial airliners go towards the airport,however this was absoloutly NOT an aircraft.It made no sound whatsoever,appeared out of nowhere and moved at a speed I couldn't hope to explain.I have no physical evidence as it happened so quickly but like many others I've heard on interviews and television shows(which I would watch with interest but skepticism)all I can say is that I know what I saw with 100% conviction and something il never forget until the day I die.The physical makeup of the area is that of a small Forrest area with a creek bordering a large river a couple blocks away which leads to the ocean not far away.
