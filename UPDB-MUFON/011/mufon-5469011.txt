triangular formation of expanding and contracting glowing objects flying in a straight line

I was walking my dog along the street, and the sky was clear. Stars and a crescent moon clearly visible as I walked towards the SW along the street. I look up at the moon, and I notice a triangular formation of glowing objects flying in a straight path towards the NE. But the lights are moving in relation to each other, with the formation expanding slowly, and then contracting slowly, like a pulse. The whole formation behaved in an organic way, in terms of how the lights moved in relation to each other. The lights were oval shaped, and seemed to be tumbling or rolling over and over as they flew together in formation. I shouted out to the neighbours to come see as the formation passes overhead. One neighbour saw it as it went by towards the SW. Her other two friends did not arrive in time, but saw us witnessing it.
