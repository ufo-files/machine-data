Triangular Flying Object at Dawn

I was walking along the highway when I noticed something in the sky. There were a couple of dim lights on that flying thing in V-shape. At first, I thought it was just an airplane but airplanes do not have that kind of shape. I also thought it was just birds in formation, but it was still too dark for birds to fly. It flew right above me. It was huge so it seemed like it is flying low. It moved quietly- no sound at all. And when it went passed the trees, it's gone. I was out of words. I jogged so I could check the sky but it wasn't there anymore. I was really in shock and I felt weird. Itold my boyfriend about it but Inwas really disturbed and was so speechless. I just kept on scanning the sky.
