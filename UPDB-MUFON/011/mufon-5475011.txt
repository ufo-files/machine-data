{rjl} Saw a red orb rise up and take off.

My sister, Nephew and myself were just hanging around outside on a very nice summer night, When we noticed a red orb or shape rise up around a half mile away from a wooded area, Id have to say it was probably the length of a hot air balloon all the way around, (was not a balloon by no means) It rose up anywhere from 500 to 1,000 ft, Sat there for about 15 to 20 seconds, Then it took off, And best way for me to describe it, We were actuall looking straght ahead when it rose up from beginning, And when it took off after sitting there all we had to do was tilt our heads straight up and backwards, Right over our heads, Way faster than any man made object.
