{rjl} Small oval changing colors.

I stepped outside and started to look up as I always do. I saw a white flash from the corner of my eye, then nothing. As I walked out onto my porch I saw an oval shape coming from my left. I looked up and saw an oval shape a little larger than a basketball. I thought it was a meteor, but it was too perfect. It changed colors from blue to green to blue to green. There was a white stripe that went around it forming diamonds shapes. It wasn't very high up. It was coming down and it looked like it was going to hit somewhere close. It was really close to me. It went from my left to my right. There was no sound and had nothing that looked like an exhaust plume. I did look hazy around it. I don't know if it hit anywhere. I didn't hear anything. I was stunned because I have never seen anything like it, ever. Wasn't sure whether to report it. I did report it to a meteor site thinking that was what it was, but I heard someone on Coast to Coast from Mufon describing other sitings around the country similar to what I saw and thought I would report it here. I have no video or pictures. I could draw it if needed.
