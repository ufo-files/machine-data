This is a day I remember very clearly.. Single mom household.. I was four or five years old.. It was summer. Sunset was around 9 pm. This day was incredible.. My companion, female, four to five years old.. I remember that today was very different.. For the whole day, only way to describe it is we were channeling universal truths.. Knowledge flowed thru us.. I remember saying that we are God.. In the sense that we are connected.. We are one.. That all truths lie within us, and that we have the ability to access this information.. I've never felt as high as that day.. It was electric.. As dinner time came. An abusive mother forced me to check in and ask if I could still play.. We didn't want it to end.. Both our moms were out cold.. We rejoined, in apt complex. Light was just starting to fade.. We were holding so happy as we walked to the backside of complex.. One house only was there surrounded by grassy fields.. At that moment above the house fifty feet from us and fourth feet high hovered the craft.. Omg it's a ufo, I thought.. It was black. Twelve feet by twenty feet with alternating white light on the top and bottom of craft.. Random colored lights would blink on and off on the body of craft.. We couldn't move.. Very strong vibrations that buzzed through my body.. I remember watching craft lowering as if to land.. Then it go blank.. Our next memory was jerking awake , facing each other, holding hands.. It was very late early morning.. None of our mothers ever awoke.. Next day we went back, trying to piece together what happened.. We found a large circular brown spot in the lush green grass.. It changed me forever
