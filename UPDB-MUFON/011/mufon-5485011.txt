My Brother came in for a Family vacation and he was staying up at our Cabin in New Lisbon Wisconsin. For several evenings we would go out on the deck and catch up on times and watching Satellites for several evenings go by without incident. One of the evenings as we were talking and watching Satellites passing over for several hours watching them and looking at the stars, there were a Few Scattered Clouds but a Very Clear Night to be Star Gazing and watching Satellites . As we were looking toward the stars we thought we had noticed another Satellite and were following it across the Sky almost at a 10 O'clock position when it went behind a cloud. We never took our eyes off the object and Waited and Waited and Waited. As we watched the Cloud go by which was moving Slower then the object was the object Never Appeared again! We both looked at each other and said did you see that, where did it go. Fort McCoy is close to us in Monroe Wisconsin. We are in our 60s and thought people would think we were making this up but this did happen. Till this day we have told our Loved Ones but no one else as fear of being labeled Nuts or making this up.
