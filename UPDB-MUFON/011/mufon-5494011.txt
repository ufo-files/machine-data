On May 13 I was on my way home from my parents. I always take the same road home . The lay of the land in this area is pretty flat, farm land. As I was driving I observed a light to my north that was very strange. It had or it was shooting out a ball of light an sucking it back in. I looked to see if anyone was behind me an seen their wasn't so I stopped to get a better look. I watched it for a few minutes an decided to try an get closer so I drove up to the next road that I knew went in the direction of this thing. I could tell it was a few miles away an I could see it the whole time I was driving. I made a couple turns to get going toward the object. I am very familiar with this area, I have lived here for 35 years.. I came to area that I do a little hunting at so I stopped an started looking around . This area has those big tall power lines running through i,t so as I looked up that is when I saw a large object right over the lines , it sort of came on an went off then back on again, it done this for about 3 minutes. The object was about I guess 150 feet an about as big around as a 18 wheeler. I'm not a spooky person but to my surprised I did get a very strange feeling that I may be in danger so I got back in my truck an went home
