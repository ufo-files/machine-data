{rjl} triangle object with dull white lights flying fast from nw direction to sw

at 2230 hrs on 31 cot 2015..in McCamey Texas, standing outside my friends house I spotted a fast flying triangle object with 6 dull lights flying from the northwest to southwest. the night was clear with no wind, clam night. the object seem like it was flying very high and at a fast pace. Object had no tail lights of a aircraft or blinking lights of an aircraft, no noise, no vapor trail. object seemed transparent. From the time I spotted the object till it disappeared it was under 15 seconds. This was witnessed by my wife and a friend. This is the second time I have seen this type of object in the night sky. The first time was in Houston texas at my home, near Katy. This first sighting was the same type of object however it was in September, 2010, approxiately 2145 hrs. My friend and I spoteed the object flying at a fast speed from the southwest heading northwest. Very big flying approxiamately 20,000 to 30,000 feet because a commercial plane had just flown by heading east to west and by the objects comparison it seem like the same altitude. We first thought it was a flock of geese however, the object was flying faster than geese with no sound, no blinking tail lights of an aircraft, no vapor trail, with 6 dull white lights in the form of a large triangle. when it was over our heads it seemed like transparent. The night in Katy texas was clear , no wind, no moon, I decided to report this seconds event because I have seen this object twice....
