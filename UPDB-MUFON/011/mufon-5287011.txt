big 1-2 meters diameter plasma ball

This story happened a long time ago, I think it was at the end of spring or the beginning of summer 2007.
Back then I lived in Ukraine with my parents in my grandparent's two-floor house.
My room was on the second floor and had only one window approximately to the east.
It was around 2:20-2:40 AM. It was dark night. I was playing World of Warcraft on EU servers on my PC.
I don't remember which day it was but I think it was workday, everyone was sleeping. Our house was located on the hill in Pidriasne village. My parents were sleeping in another room. My grandparents were sleeping on the first floor. 
Suddenly electricity went down. My room became dark. I looked at the window and strangely it was looking almost like a day outside. Only instead of normal daylight, it was reddish-purplish-pink. I came closer to the window to see what is going on. The street was lit with pinkish light almost like during the day. Also, it was foggy outside and fog helped light to spread. 
But I couldn't see the source of light from my window. It was coming from my right. So I moved to another room which was between my room and my parent's room. There I saw my mom looking into the window. Her eyes were wide open and she was almost under hypnosis. I called her few times before she reacted. She said, "Do you see that?". And we both started to look into the window.
Around 10-15 meters from us, approximately at the same height as our second floor we saw a big 1-2 meter diameter plasma ball hovering 1-3 meters above our grandparent's utility building. We were staring at it for around 1 min I think, I vaguely remember but I also think we tried to record it on camera that my parents brought from the USA recently but it was not working. Then this object moved further away from us and to the right, like 50-100 meters away. Stayed there for a shorter time, not moving. And finally, it propelled itself to the horizon almost instantly. As soon as it was gone, electricity came back everywhere, as nothing happened.
My dad woke up too because we were so excited and loud. But he didn't see anything because it was already gone. For a week or two, we were telling everyone that we saw UFO but none really believed us or maybe they believed but soon everything went to normal life. At some point, I started to think maybe it was just ball-lightening. I really was not into UFO and knew almost nothing about this stuff. Until recent events related to the pentagon ufo report, I started to rewatch a lot of videos about this.
And I found this video about the Iranian incident: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qbH9OZupWOg
They describe there something very similar to what I saw and what appears to be a UFO drone.
