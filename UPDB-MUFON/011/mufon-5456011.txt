{cak} Fleet of orbs

January 8, 2020 a fleet of UFO'S flew from the north then moved diagonally to the left heading east. They stopped/hovered over and in between 2 tree's. Then they started to make shapes of triangles and spheres. They did that for a short time. All the while I thought I was recording from my phone. Then they let out of the shapes they were making and back to a "squadron" they seemed as they were scanning the area. I was flabbergasted!! My phone went onto the home screen and I thought it was because it could not record anymore but I was excited knowing that I taped it...I did take a photo just to have a still shot. The orbs left in the exact direction they came. I was on an adrenaline rush and was shaking wondering what I saw. In this time I forget that I have binoculars not 10 feet away from me. I go inside to view my video and NOTHING!! The photo is all I have!!
