{jpg} Orb did a C maneuver into clouds

Slept early Friday night, awoke around 2 am. Had a cigarette outside with my neighbor, something caught my eye in the northern sky, looked like a bright star, but it looked closer, based on the movement, and it was in front of the clouds, it moved in a "C" shape and disappeared behind the clouds (which were spotty). I thought it could be a drone, but I never saw it again, and it made a very smooth movement, appearing to gain elevation (the C turn went up, like a backwards C, from the bottom to the top). I could identify whatever it was, and it was a flying object, so there you go. I finally saw one lol. My neighbor was looking the other way and by the time I said, "wtf is that....!" it was gone behind the clouds. Minutes later we both saw what may have been a low orbit satellite traveling roughly from east to west across the whole sky, more rapid and larger than a regular satellite usually is, but in a straight line. So it was technically two seemingly separate UFOs within 5 minutes, the second one seen by us both, but was less strange. I figured I would report it, in case someone else saw it too.
