{cak} 2 small clouds formed and dispersed at least 4 times within 12 minutes and no other clouds were in the sky.

I was taking a break outside of my work and noticed a single cloud in a cloudless sky south of me. I watched this cloud quickly disperse within a minute or two. I then noticed a small cloud NE of me that I had not seen minutes before and thought it was odd that I missed it. After about a minute, I glanced at the area that I had seen the first cloud and was stunned to see another small cloud in the same spot as the first one. I watched it for a moment wondering how it formed so quickly and then I pulled my key chain video camera out and started recording. I took a little over 6 minutes of video that shows these clouds forming and then dispersing before I had to return to work. At one point it looked like these clouds were forming near small white dots but it was difficult to tell for certain. The videos show small white floating flying in front of the camera that are just cottonwood seed blowing in the breeze. I have not idea if this is a strange weather event or not. I hope someone can tell me after viewing the videos what it was. Please note that the date stamps on the files are incorrect.
