Caught video overhead while hiking

I was hiking in Tahoe and was trying to capture a sound trees were making. I caught this in a short video and didn’t notice until much later. I hike in this area a lot. Aircraft make a lot of noise and this made none. It was going much faster than any aircraft. It left no trail as almost all airplanes do. I’ve studied this a lot and honestly don’t know what it was. The speed at which it was moving with no sound or any sign of exhaust just doesn’t match anything I have seen in the area or anywhere else for that matter.
