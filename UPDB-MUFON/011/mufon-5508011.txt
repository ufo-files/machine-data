{cak} white light moving north first only lasted a few seconds, second orange burning light moved in multiple directions, lasted 3-5 mins

My daughter and I were sitting on my back porch discussing the passing of my mother and talking about the stars and where grandma might be. Then I saw a bright white light moving slowly towards the north, it lasted only a couple seconds and disappeared. At first I thought it was a shooting star but thought it was moving very slow (as I have viewed many shooting stars in my lifetime). so I told my little girl that it was grandma just checking on us and she gave me a hug and smiled. Then approximately ten minutes later we saw a large bright orange dot moving north well behind were the first dot was spotted. I ran inside and got my wife, son, and brother inlaw and we all walked out back to watch it. It moved about ten inches northward and made an abrupt turn to the west, went about two inches and turned again going south, then east, then south again. It proceeded to go, in a straight line, south untill it was out of sight dimming towards the end probably due to the earths atmosphere refracting the light.
