Starlike object flew from south to north at a 60 degree angle over Homestead, FL.

At approximately 8:34 PM (2034) on September 18, 2011 I came out of my home to take my 3 small dogs out at my front yard. While sitting atop my vehicles tail gate I observed a starlike object crossing at high velocity the sky from south to north on a straight trayectory at a 60 degree angle while facing west. It crossed the sky faster than an airliner airplane that at the same time was flying at aproximately same altitude but at a slower rate of speed. Photos were taken using a I-PHONE but photos are not clear.
