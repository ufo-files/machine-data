Rectangular silver to gray about the size of a large door

My wife and 3 year old grandson were out in the yard. My wife came in the back door and asked me to come outside. She then pointed up and said “Tim what is that? Looking up I saw about 10 objects. At first I thought it looked like giant pieces of ash, or construction paper. The objects were inconsistent and would go from looking like a rectangle then tumbling and almost disappear. We watched the objects for about a minute with no distinct pattern. Then as I was telling my wife that the objects were just floating debris. They all at the same time became rectangles and came together in a pyramid flight pattern. They stayed in that pattern and hovering for about 15 seconds and we’re only a few feet apart. Prior to this they all seemed random in their movements and at times were spread out over a couple hundred feet of space. In a split second they moved together in formation. Slow and to the Southwest in direction. Then they just disappeared. I have never seen anything like this, in person or in the media, tv or internet. This is the 3rd time my wife and I have seen a UAP. I am starting to think that part is strange. We tried to video them with an iPhone but the objects could not be seen on the video. My grandson is only 3, but he could also see them.
