Double star like object

On my way home from work I notice in the north east sky just above the tree line very bright lights, as bright as Venus would be in the sky. I only saw it shortly since i was driving, but it looked like a pair of lights almost on top of each other similar to what headlights look like from a distance. It/they did not appear to be moving at all. I lost it behind trees as I drove.
