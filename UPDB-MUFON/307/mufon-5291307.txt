White oval intercepted by another white oval near Dulce, NM

Clearly visible against a blue sky, I pulled off to watch it traverse the afternoon sky from a direction online with Dulce, NM to our southeast. As I was watching the craft a second craft identical to the first zoomed directly toward the first and stopped suddenly amidship having approached from the northwest heading sountheast. It remained for a few moments, then darted at a quicker pace than the approach on a line towards Dulce. The original craft continued on the the north at a constant rate of speed till out of site.
