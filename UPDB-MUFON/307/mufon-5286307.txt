Observation of ufo in clouds

First day of deer season , first Saturday in November 1994, approximately 6 am I was sitting on the edge of the middle of a clearcutt that had been replanted and the saplings were nearly 3 ft high. This clearcutt was about the size of a football field. On both long sides there was a fire lane about 8ft wide.
From the clouds to my right ( west) a bright light came down and shined the firelane from the far western end all the way to the far eastern end. The light went out. It came back on at the far western end and shone down the entire length of the firelane I was setting on. That light passed right over me. No heat was felt. When the light reached the far eastern end it went out. It's might be important to know that except for this cuttover and replanting of pine saplings , there were solid thick woods all around. 
At once , in the same clouds the white light came from , the clouds glowed pink, white and blue. They didn't flash,strobe or blink bit just a steady glow. Absolutely silent, no noise whatsoever. 
All of a sudden, lightning began to crash in all directions, the wind began to gust to the point my deer stand was coming apart. Thunder began to boom and the glowing lights disappeared at once. I immediately left.
