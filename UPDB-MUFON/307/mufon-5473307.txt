{dr}This object remained in the sky for 2 hours. It appeared to be reflecting sunlight and may have been close as it continually reflects the light as the sun set. I watched as it faded and ended as a point of white light that looked like a star!

The object remained in the sky for 2 hours. I shot phone video and took cel pics. It just sat there glowing and appeared to reflect the sunlight. Because it remained reflective from my view point for hours, it gave me the impression it was fairly close (miles away rather than many miles away). It continued to glow and then faded as the sun set. I watched as the sun set to see if it had internal illumination. It did not. However as I watched it fade, it became a white point that looked just like any star! That, too, faded. If it had moved at all, I would have said it was a weather ballon. The wind was blowing from the southwest at about 10 mph and it did not move.
