3 Craft, Cigar with small ones going in and out, folding wings, delta which kept fading.

Summer day ,clear blue sky,For some reson looked up to see 3 craft, one cigar shape with a funal at both ends, The next was a delta shape that kept fading in and out, the third appeard to fly in between the other 2 ships and had wings that folded in.I went into the house to tell some one and the whole thing went out of my mind till the next day. some 60yrs later i was taken from my bed by a tall man with long hair and asked if i would go with him as he wanted too show me something, i was taken in this field where there was a Greek like amphitheatre, i sat, and was joined by someone whos face i could not see however had a warm loving feeling about them. I was told to look up at the stares and focus on a black spot where there were know. Then a mother ship appeard and small craft came out in formation and carred out manoeuvres. My next encounter was years later comming home at night from a show in the city with my girle friend when a number of balls of light came past my car, i was doing 100k at the time, then all folowed the same projectory up into the night sky except one that traveled by the side of the car for a few seconds then followed the same path as the others which by this time had gone.
