{cak} Took an upward path from West and hovered directly above. Then turned northwest before ejecting another spark of light and disappearing.

I was outside in my backyard at around 10:40pm in Covina, CA on 1/8/19. I noticed a bright light in the sky towards the west. It was moving east toward my location. I first thought it was a plane, but it's movement made me think otherwsise. My second thought was it was a launch out of Vanderberg Air Force Base as there have been a lot of views in the LA area of launches. I later looked up any possible launches, and there were not any scheduled for that time.
The object was glowing a bright, reddish light. It slowly moved eastward before coming to a stop directly above my location. It seemed pretty high up, but still below the clouds. The object did not seem too large. There seemed to be a second, faint light above the main bright light, but it could've have been the main light reflecting off of the object. After hovering for about 5 minutes, the object turned towards the north before ejecting another, almost spark-like light that dissappeared almost immediately. About 30 seconds after this, the main light disappeared as well. This was the end of the event.
