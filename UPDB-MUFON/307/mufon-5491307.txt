{cak} I witnessed blinking lights to the left side of the moon.

I was out in my back yard with my dog Spanky, and I noticed an object to the left of the moon. I must have seen it while it was lit up (in the middle of blinking) because I thought it was a planet. I looked again in a few seconds and it was gone. I looked through my camera and noticed that it was not gone, but it was stationary and blinking. I don't know if it was communicating with anything on the moon cause it did seem like a pattern. I shot some videos and took pics. Then a few minutes later I noticed it moved under the moon and did the same thing. After reviewing my pics and videos I found out that there were 2 objects not just one.
