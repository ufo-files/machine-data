Appeared like a brilliant star at fairly low level and flashing while at hover.

I'm retired military (Marines and Army service) of 24 years, most of which as an aircrew member of UH-1, CH-47C&D, CH-54A and served as senior maintenance instructor. The pattern of the object I saw being performed would be impossible for any aircraft I know of and happened in fractions of a second covering an impossible distance. It started to pulsate brighter than anything I've ever seen, even a strobe light. There appeared to be a lateral line through the middle that made the white light appear bluish in color. It flew to the right then back again to the left; then zigzagged every which way, changing direction at right angles, ascending and descending in altitude (reminded me of Zorro TV show). It appeared to be showing off what it can do, then in a heartbeat, it flew towards my direction slightly turning to the NW direction and was hidden by my trees. It made a swooshing noise overhead and appeared almost as though it was a fireball. The speed was impossible to even begin to estimate. I was in a state of disbelief at what I had just witnessed. The noise it made could have just been air disturbance above me, I don't know. There was no audible noise emitted by it until then.
