The night started off with a dinner on my deck outside my home in Acworth, Ga.The night was winding down when a couple of friends kept pointing out a strange light in a north west area in the sky.At first i didn't look thinking they were confused on what they were looking at.After a few minutes they got me to look at the object.I noticed that it was pulsating and not stationary.The object moved slowly different directions.I found the object to be interesting so i went to get my 350mm telescope to see if i can get a closer look.I was able to get the object in scope and saw an amazing sphere pulsating as if it was ball of electricity.3 witnesses viewed the object via telescope and all had the same opinion on we saw .The object was moving making us change placement of the telescope multiple times.After roughly 20 mins the object accelerated north towards Tennessee and disappeared behind the tree line.
