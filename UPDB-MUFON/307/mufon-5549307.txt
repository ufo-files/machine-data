small dot with multiple colors

I was driving back home from a late night @ work. I was on Highway 510 in Puyallup when I saw a small dot in the southern sky. It was changing from red to blue. I thought it might be some kind of plain from Macord air force base in Tacoma, Washington. I pulled over to the side of the road, And As I stepped out of my truck a smaller red dot dropped from the bottom of the larger dot. The smaller dot took of to the East and I lost track of it. And Then I looked for the bigger dot and it was gone.
