{cak} Came down on a foggy morning, saw the lights with no sound, then shot straight up and made the fog follow underneath

A roommate and I worked at Will Rogers Airport at the time, I had to be clocked in at 4:30 am and as we drove to work, we usually listen to the radio but not that morning. We drove with the windows down and no radio so it completely silent during the trip. Once we reached Air Cargo Rd, just North of the F.A.A, we noticed a round object lower from the fog, the bottom had red and blue lights that flashed for a few minutes then it shot straight up, causing the fog to follow it from underneath. The incident took about 5-10 minutes, and afterwards, we looked at each other to make sure that we both witnessed the same thing and then drove as fast as we could to the Airport. Once there, we mentioned it a few co-workers but nobody else had seen it. I understand it was a long time ago but watching your show tonight made me realize, WE ARE NOT ALONE and I am very thankful neither my friend or myself was not harmed. We kept trying to pass it off as a helicopter or little plane but there was no sound, complete silence as it hovered down and lifted off. The year it happened is not certain but I do know it was between the years of 1989-1994, the years that we obtained employment there. I also understand this may not be much to work on but, at least our story(sighting) is known. Thank you for all your hard work and effort to prove, WE ARE NOT ALONE!
