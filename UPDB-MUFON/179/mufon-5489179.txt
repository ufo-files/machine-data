{cak} Horizon to horizon in less than 4 seconds.

I was at "The Gorge" entertainment center. I had just returned from the Foofighters concert and was laying on my recliner at my campsite.I don't smoke weed nor do I drink nor did I use any psychoactive drugs. There are no lights and the stars were beautiful, clear skys. At first I saw what looked like a shooting star almost overhead going North to South. Right where the shooting star stopped about 2 handwidths above the Southern horizon I see what looks like another shooting star moving from where the first object ended. At first I thought it was yet another shooting star but it traveled a bit slower. By slower I mean the shooting star took about 1/8th of a second to go from directly above to the 2 handwidths above horizon. This object took about 1 second to go the same distance. I could see white and red and thought at first it was a multicopter. The white and red seems to go out and appear at another area on the object. I literally fell out of the recliner to observe this(I watch for UFOs all the time)and reaquired visual after about 1.5 second not seeing it. Just as I saw it again it was near the Northern horizon and turning what looked like a 30 degree angle Northeast. I heard nothing. Like I said my first impression was a multicopter by the lights, a multicopter at about 500 feet. I have never in my life seen anything that traveled as fast as this. I saw it for maybe 3 or 4 seconds. Could have been an aircraft or a multicopter out of control maybe but by my estimation it traveled 10-15 miles while I saw it in about 3-4 seconds.
