Saw many neighbors outside looking up, went out to investigate saw 8-10 objects, moving from west to north east, took pictures, event had over a dozen total objects some swapped positioned in the sky two hovered for up to 8 minutes, long enough for me to grab my camera and tripod. I called them the twins they hovered almost stationary with the lower object pendulum like moving under the stationary upper. By this point had the camera set up managed to capture I think up to 7 objects in the frame and one very good close up. The whole street came out to watch with many optics including binoculars hunting optics. Amazing. Quite a show.
