{rjl} Was dark object with two small white lights on ends and a big white light in middle and behind it was glowing lights that changed from white,green, light blue, and red.

I was closing my dresser near my window. When I made my way back to the front of my bed I noticed out the window a diamond shape lights in the sky. The lights were changing colors from red to white to light blue, and green. I woke my wife up and asked her what do you think that is? She said a helicopter. I said it was to high up to be a helicopter. As soon as I said that a helicopter flew by and she could tell it was way higher than the helicopter. I asked her to get her dad's binoculars. Through the binoculars I could see a dark object with a small white light then a big white light in middle and another small white light on end. On the other side of the object you could see multiple lights changing colors like it was the exhaust from engine. My wife, son, and father in law saw the same thing and could not be leave what the were seeing. I notice the object at 1215 am and was still there when I went to sleep at 1 am. When we got up at 6 am the object was gone.
