Photograph taken at night which appears to show an UAP

I was on my apartment balcony which is on the 29th floor of a tower block located in central Birmingham. I was using my DSLR camera to take long exposure night photographs of the city and the neighbouring tower at around 12am (photography is a hobby of mine). A few weeks later I transferred the photographs to my tablet to look at them and noticed a set of 3 blueish green lights in a triangle shape in the distance. After examining the lights close up I find it difficult to explain what they could be. It is worth mentioning I took repeated photographs around the same time which did not show these lights, ruling out lens/sensor reflections or glare. At the time of taking the photograph I did not see these lights. The photograph was a 15 second exposure meaning the lights would have had too have been stationary as there is no light trail/blur. They could have also been a momentary blink which may account for why I did not see them. The surrounding glow suggests they were bright and at cloud altitude. The direction is near an airport, although the photographs certainly is not a airplane which would be moving and therefore streak or blur in the exposure. They are 3 distinct dots that make a triangle.
