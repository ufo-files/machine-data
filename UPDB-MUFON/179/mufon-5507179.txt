{nd} From the park I spotted a small orb fly over the clouds, soon followed by a larger, brighter, orb that moved irregular and would appear to hold stationery.

From Balboa Park in Van Nuys, Ca. I watched a white orb fly over sparse clouds at a steady speed from north to south. I was laying on my back on the grass in the park. Soon thereafter, a larger and brighter orb appeared, flew in an irregular path, would hover and change direction. I observed this object approach from an easterly direction move almost overhead and shift and stall in a zig zag fashion south/ southwesterly direction until it faded from view. As I was leaving the park I noticed a peculiar phenomena: as the sun was setting behind me in the west, I noticed what looked like a "false" sunrise happening in the east. What appeared as rays of light and shadow extending upwards from in front of the San Gabriel Mountain Range. From on top of a hill I took a short video of the phenomen with my camera on my phone. After reviewing the video, I caught something at 9seconds into the video flying from mid left ascending at high velocity. This object appears dark and is moving too fast to be conventional aircraft.
