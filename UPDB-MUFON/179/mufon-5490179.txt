It was like a see through large glass tube....there was a light inside. I was in bed watching the stars with Binnoculars about 4:20 in morning, and it appeared. It was just starting to get light out

I woke up in the morning at 4:00 am, as I have been doing for many years. This day in early July, I was looking at the sky with my binnoculars, at the stars. It was just starting to get light out. At 4:20, a large, see through glass test tube shaped object was moving over the neighbours house behind us. Behind the neighbours house is the lake. I could see what looked like a light inside, but nothing else. It moved at a slow pace. As it went over the neighbours house, behind us, it went in between our house and the neighbour beside us. It was about 30ft over the top of the houses. I jumped out of bed and ran to the back door onto the deck to look around the corner of the house and it disappeared over the trees. I them came back into the house and screamed to my husband to tell him about it. I think back daily about this encounter...I told some close family and friends, but there are only a few that believe.
