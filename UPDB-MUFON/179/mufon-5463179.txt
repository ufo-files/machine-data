I walked out the back door of 9 Old Orchard Road, Cherry Hill NJ which faces East.It was mid-afternoon, clear day, cumulous clouds. Happened to look up at clouds and scanning when I saw green object to the NE. Guessing it was over area between Mt Laurel and Hainesport NJ. I thought it might be a helium balloon that broke loose from a car dealership.
It was bright green in color, traveling leisurely in a straight line to the East.
No particular feelings, and my reaction was, "Hmm...that's interesting, thinking it was just a balloon, but when it didn't emerge from the clouds opn it's line of p0ath, I wondered, 'What happened to it? Did I just see a UFO?'
It never re-emerged from the bank of clouds as it should have given it's line of flight...
