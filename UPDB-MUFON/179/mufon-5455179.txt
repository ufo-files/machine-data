20 Bright Lights

I was standing out in the garden at 5:35am on the 25th Jan 2020 (and still dark) having a cigarette before going to work. At the corner of my eye I caught a moving, non flashing object at the corner of my eye moving from the west heading east which was definitely not a plane and I instantly thought it was probably a satellite. A second light then appeared on the same flight path and the distance apart remained the same until they were out of eye sight. Suddenly another appeared a little behind followed by another 17. All kept the same distance apart and same course. Impossible to say how far away or how fast they were moving although they were moving faster than a commercial aeroplane. There was no noise emitting from the orbs at all. I quite often look up at the night sky and can honestly say I have never seen anything like it before. I checked local media later that evening for any info or other reports but nothing at all. I haven't stopped looking or thinking about it since.
