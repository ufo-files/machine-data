triangle with dull circles under it no noise , aprox 7 circles .

Walking to the gas station with a friend around 11pm . my friend brought the object to my attention , I first thought geese flying in a V formation but as i watched , it was clear it was not geese . We watched the object we heard no noise it appeared to be moving slow the clouds were fairly low the object was moving in a strait direction , the triangle had approx 7 dull circles under it . I am not sure of the circles purpose . I was speechless . My friends first responce was alien , I disagreed . I don't know what I saw but I did see somthing . I lost focus as the object continued thru the clouds and darkness .
