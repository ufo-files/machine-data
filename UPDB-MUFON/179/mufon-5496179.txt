As my son and I were traveling down the road by Aliante casino heading towards Centennial hills, we noticed a very large fire ball trail that was hanging over the mountains. I first thought meteor, but it was hovering over the mountains. As I slowed down, it dissappeared for about 15 seconds, then reappeared. Very large almost as large as the mountain. It was like the fire ball was rotating. Then it disappeared.
