{cak} Bright orbish lights very vivid in broad daylight no clouds, disappeared

Me and my band mates were driving home to Spokane from Moscow Idaho on highway US 195, the whole drive is lots of farmland and fields. There was no cloud cover except for very low on the horizon and it was very little. My band mate and cousin, in the front seat noticed a light that was very bright for being day time. She thought it was a plane when she's pointed it out but I assured her wasn't. I've had UFO encounters a few times and am always extremely frightened when I see one, today was no different. After noticing the first we noticed the second. The second one shot straight up into the air. The first vanished for a while and then reappeared lower on the horizon. They moved very fast, my cousin tried taking a picture but it was far too bright outside to capture it. The light then disappeared again and moments later a black military helecopter began combing the sky it was in. It's been quite the wild road trip.
