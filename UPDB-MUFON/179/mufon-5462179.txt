{tjd} Triangle Shaped with dull gray lights on 2 sides. Not sure if it was solid or boomerang shaped.

I went out to look for Uranus. I had left my phone inside with my star map on it, so I decided to look for Gemini. I looked over my building to the southeast. I thought that I saw a fast moving low flying cloud. It was high in the sky. Not sure how high. It appeared to be V-shaped with approximately 6 to 8 grey dull lights on two sides pointing the direction that it was moving, which was north west. It was about a 5 to 10 second sighting. I don't know if it was solid or boomerang shaped. It made no noise and once it got past me a little ways, it disappeared. The sky was clear above me, but I could see clouds far off to the west. This took place at about 8:35 pm on Wednesday, February 27, 2019. I was wearing glasses at the time and it was about 38 degrees outside
