White hovering cylinder, first sighting was horizontal then 2nd time was diagonal

Was at a stoplight facing East about 5 miles from the coast and saw a white, horizontal object - not a cloud, not a plane, hovering in the sky. It wasn't moving at all - then it just disappeared! I then saw the contrails or 2 navy jets further north. Then about 2-3 minutes later I was on another street facing east again and looked for it - and saw it in the same area, but this time it was diagonal - then it disappeared again! I parked at home and got out to look for it again but couldn't find it. It was 100% not a cloud, not a bird, not a plane and it was stationary aside from changing it's orientation.
