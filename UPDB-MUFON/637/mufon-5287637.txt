Disc shaped no sound and different colored lights flashing around the middle

I ran my own food truck selling breakfast and lunch. We got down to a lot where we sell food at around 5-5:30am. I went to turn on my generator. Which is very loud, but before I could turn it on out of the corner of my eye I see lights in the sky over the river. I call out to my coworker who is also my girlfriend of 13 years. She came out, cause she can hear the fear in my throat so she comes out in a hurry. I point to the object in the sky. We both watch it flashing different colored lights around the rim of the object with no sound at all coming from the object. I noticed that morning was really quiet. We both watched in disbelief at what we were observing. I didn’t feel scared or threatened at all. It followed the los pinos river maybe 10 feet above the trees. We both watched as it went towards the next town. After it was out of sight i asked my partner if she seen it too. She got scared and is scared to this day, and don’t like to talk about it with others. I tell everyone I can most people don’t believe me. I have a feeling it was following the natural water system to navigate.
