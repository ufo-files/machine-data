Observed bright hovering light in sky that was stationary for several seconds then moved

On December 11, 2020 at 5:48 pm as I approached Hwy 53 west while driving on Jot Em Down road I observed a bight light while sitting at the intersection that was hovering in one spot. The light was pretty large at first I was thinking it could be a helicopter since it was stationary but as I turned right heading east on dawsonville hwy it appeared to be moving in my direction. As I was driving around 55 mph i could visually look out my drivers window as it stayed in my sites but when I got down around the dollar general, I observed the light start to go up very fast getting smaller and at the bottom I noticed flashing strobe lights as it disappeared into the sky.

Although, I initiated my cell phone video to record for some reason it did not record the video. I only show 3 seconds that only shows inside my vehicle but at least I can see the time it happened.
