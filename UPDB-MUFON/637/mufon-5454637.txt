{cak} Gold disc spun slowly above field for 10 minutes, then went straight back up into the sky

This was recorded by my late great grandmother, who saw the UFO in Menlo, Washington while her husband was operating the tractor in their hay field (they were dairy farmers). This sighting was in the 50s. 
"We saw a shiny gold colored saucer, about 30 feet across, hovering above the field. It was plain as could be. The sun was shining on it. It didn’t make any noise that I could hear, and there were no lights on it. It was spinning slowly, less than 100 feet away. There was no wind. 
We thought it was going to land, but eventually it went straight up and out of sight. 
We stopped the tractor and watched it for 5-10 minutes. Afterward, we ran in the house to see what time it was; it was a clear day in the afternoon. I've never seen any machinery or planes that looked like this. Others saw it too; we heard them talking about it later. The sighting was even mentioned in the local paper."
