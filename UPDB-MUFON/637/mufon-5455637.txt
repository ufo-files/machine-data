{rjl} 3 shinny spheres working together

this would require thousands of words. Observation of 20 minutes approx. start to finish. 3 shinny spheres i will never forget apparently. sphere 1 was parked low on western horizon 15 minutes. sphere number 2 performing a low altitude smooth radius turn then headed toward position of sphere number 1. this caused a con trail which evaporated in seconds. sphere 1 made an incredible vertical acceleration to miles high straight up. sphere 3 appears straight over head then travels toward sphere 2. Minutes latter all 3 disappear instantaneously. I told some people but then decided to remain silent. it frightened me. A highly analytical mind yet it took 20 years to finally consider the odds that the horizontal path from me were all 3 in a straight path !! That still bothers me plus the one straight above that after being observed headed on this straight path away from me. The entire activity was bizarre and left a memory which is still not comfortable. The situation of not logically resolving the unlikely straight line alignment to my physical location for 20 plus years is incomprehensible. How his never dawned on me is very unlikely, still disturbing.
