Visitation and possible abduction

July 17th I saw a ufo and a being appeared in front of me the same night. Approx 1 week later I came upon a floating greyish metallic-smoky colored orb at the entrance to my bedroom. It dissipated within seconds of my seeing it. I went to bed that night, slept well but awoke feeling extremely weak, very nauseous and there was a feeling of my heart being massaged in my chest. That lasted 3 solid days. I had my heart checked and all is ok. Panic attacks don’t last 3 days.
