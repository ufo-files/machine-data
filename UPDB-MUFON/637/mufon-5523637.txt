I was observing the morning stars in the country. I thought I was looking at a planet when the object began to move & get smaller until gone.

My hunting partner and I had arrived at the hunting lease at about 6am. We were parked at the gate entrance of the deer lease when we began to observe the stars. The morning was clear and the stars were very clear. I had turned to look toward the north when we saw what we thought was a very bright planet. The object began move and then disappeared. I confirmed with my hunting partner that he witnessed the same thing.
