{cak} Flying V formation

On Saturday night my husband, nephew and I were on our deck in Las Cruces stargazing. My husband was the first to notice a flurry of several dim objects that turned into a V formation. He stated they were at first moving beyond the parameters of any known human aerial craft. As soon as he pointed the flurry out my nephew and I looked to that direction in which we spotted a very large, dimly lit formation that appeared to be flying very high and very fast without any sound. The V formation started north of Las Cruces and flew towards El Paso. Again it seemed very high and it only took a few seconds for it to fly over our heads. My husband is the only witness to the flurry of the objects before they formed up into a V, but we all clearly witnessed the flying V. The lights were faint but visible, if I had to guess how many made up the V I would say between 8-12 total.
