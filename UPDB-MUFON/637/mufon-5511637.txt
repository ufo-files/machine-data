{cak} Blue halogen colored lights, progressive wave-like light, no blinkers such as a plane

I was walking my dog prior to bed as I do every night. I always have been intrigued by UFOs and have seen a couple before so I tend to glance at the sky and just observe in case there is something odd. When I first saw the light I thought it was a plane, it looked like a conventional aircraft at a glance. I don't know why I even kept looking, but I did. I noticed that the light had a flowing pattern to it. It would light and seemed to follow a curved, sphere like or circular pattern. It was a blue halogen light type color, and there were no strobe, or blinker lights, as u would see on a plane or conventional craft, so I kept looking. The craft appeared headed for a cloud bank and looked like it would disappear soon. Prior to reaching this bank, when there was still a visible space between the craft and the clouds, it disappeared completely. I continued watching as there was an opening in the path of the craft where it would soon be visible again, but I never did see it again. After seeing it and thinking about it, I realized that a conventional craft would have had some form of red and green blinking light associated with it. I found it exciting, the unknown is always something that has intrigued me. This may not be the most incredible sighting, but I wanted to report it as I had never completed this after my previous sightings.
