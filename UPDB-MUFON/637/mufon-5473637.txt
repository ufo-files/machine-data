Luz followed by helicopter, Argentina

I traveled on route 288 to route 40 and was at a distance of 30 km from Tres Lagos in the province of Santa Cruz.
I saw a light that had no flashes, was solid, was followed closely by a large white helicopter, both flying slowly but steadily.
I saw them for more than 11 minutes at a distance of between 500 and 700 meters at a point where the route rotates towards the SW.
I did not hear sounds and I did not see glows either, just the distant transit of the two objects that did not seem to fit the landscape.
The wind was blowing from the W so I did not hear the helicopter either, but I saw it clearly because the day was clear.
