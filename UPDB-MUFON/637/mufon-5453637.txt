{rjl} Observed 26 objects moving fast like the space station, some in line some in pairs. Observed for 20 minutes then came in to fill out this report.

At 9:10 PM i went outside to feed my horse and looked up at Venus. I observed a starlike object moving very fast from WSW to NNE, at first i thought it was the space station as i have watched that move across the sky several times. Then i saw a second object behind the first about two outstretched hand widths back. As i watched these two moving as fast as the space station would, a third object appeared the same distance behind. After the 7th object appeared i got my wife and son to come out and see them. All were in line except 3 pair which were side by side seperated by about three fingers on an outstretched hand. When i counted 26 of them i came into the house to report it.
