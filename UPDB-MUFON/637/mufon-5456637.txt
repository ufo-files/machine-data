{jpg} Gray smoke-like trails that moved erratically.

While driving North on SC 31 today, a partly cloudy day at the time, I noticed 2 small gray clouds. I thought how odd, all wispy white clouds and there are two little puff-balls of gray. My next thought was maybe they were from a fire. Then I noticed they weren't really clouds, they were trails. So I thougbt oh, just a plane. Still it bothered me the trails were smoke-like so it kept my attention. I was so captured by the odd movement that I pulled off the road to watch it. I'm glad I snapped a couple photos so someone could identify what this is. There was no sound but I could have been too far away. There were no lights that could see. I've drawn on the photo the approximate path it took while I was watching. If you zoom the photos, you can see 2 black circles, what I took to be engines but with the odd movements, I just can't be sure those are plane engines. 
Feed back on this sighting is greatly appreciated.
