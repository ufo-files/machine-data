{tjd} Three spheres

I was getting into my car and it was dark so I was having difficulty. I saw three green spheres, dark green on the edges and lighter in the center. They apeared to be in a V like a less than sign. < They had an orange color around each one and trailing behind a great distance from the edge of each so that it made six orange trails. I watched it for one seemed like a long time, about six seconds going from the north east to north west. Then everything was gone. I thought it might be an air burst and quickly got into my car.
