My friend and I witnessed a bright light above us. It moved very slowly at first. Then it took off at a slightly faster rate.

The video explains it better than I. The object was closer than it looks in the video. It was orb like. Brighter than any star. It flew slowly under the clouds above us. Then moved a little faster as it passed by. Then flying off into the northern horizon. No sound or trail emitted from it.
