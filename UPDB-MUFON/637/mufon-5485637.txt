{jpg} Appeared, shot across the sky, disappeared

I was driving down the highway and saw this green line in front of me to the right in the sky. It was a straight line that shot in a straight line then seemed to incline than disappear. It was similar color to a green traffic light, but I wasn't not near any traffic lights, so it was not a reflection. I was honestly incredibly happy almost to the point of crying because I know what I saw. I called my family and those who I know believe in extra-terrestrial beings for confirmation of my feelings and reaction.
