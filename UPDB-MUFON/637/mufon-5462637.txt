{jpg} Craft first spotted over Lee MA, then again in Westhampton MA

First spotted this as a very bright white light, stationary in sky over mass pike in Lee MA around 7:30 pm. Then around 8:15pm a very low flying craft flew right over my house, brightly lit, square, maybe diamond shape, did make a noise but not loud like a plane or jet. Was traveling north. Did have flashing lights and a bright white light. Was very noticeable in sky. Then saw it flying off in distance toward Northampton. Very bright white light in sky. I was outside with my horses watching for a while. Never seen this before. There is a nearby air force base in Westfield, 15 min away, Barnes air base. This did not look like any craft I've seen from our military but no military planes checked in on this. I assumed it was a new craft from our military base because if it was a real UFO from another planet, wouldn't our jets be quick to,check this out?
