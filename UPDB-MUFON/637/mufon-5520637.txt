Driving North 9:15 am observed from my car a rectangular solid dull gray object slowly gliding east to west

While driving to work this morning I observed a solid light grey rectangular object slowly gliding from east to west in front of my car. I was traveling at about 40 mph, going north on Gilbert Rd, Mesa AZ. At first, I thought it might be something like a sign being pulled by a plane for advertising purposes, but quickly realized there was nothing pulling it. I did not at first perceive it moving at all and since I was driving directly towards it, that I would be able to clearly see more detail as I approached. But as I got further along, I realized it was flying from my right to left in the sky and seemed to be gradually descending. I did manage to snap a picture with my phone, but detail is lacking as it was quite a distance away by the time I managed to take the shot from my moving car.
