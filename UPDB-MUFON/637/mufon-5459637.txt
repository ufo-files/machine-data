{jpg} Very fast vehicle on the 4th.

My wife and I were watching fireworks and aircraft in our backyard on the 4th while we were swimming. When we got out of the pool and were drying off, I noticed a red light several miles away to the southeast approximately 1000 ft in the air. It caught my attention because the light was red and was the only light I saw. Usually, starboard wing lights are red and the opposite is green. This had no other light but red.

However, I thought perhaps it would become clear to what it was as it approached, but it approached fast and as it went directly over our home, my wife and I could see the red light was circular with a black hole inside it.

It then climbed out of sight as it went northeast. It made no sound and traveled the entire valley in less than 30 seconds.
