{cak} Bright lights seen from Fayetteville NC

My wife and I were traveling on Skibo rd In Fayetteville NC, when we noticed an orb that appeared to be a meteor or a comet in the sky. I looked to my wife and said " look at that it, it cant be an airplane, it doesnt have any blinking lights" she said "yeah youre right". We observed it for about 15-30 seconds before the light in the sky appeared to vanish, but not a second later it re appeared in the sky along with two other bright orbs spaced a few hundred feet above it in a vertical formation. They began to decend and they eventually fell behind tree level. Were hoping that we are not the only ones to have noticed these three strange orbs in the sky tonight, but Im most certainly positive that they were not airplanes or helicopters.
