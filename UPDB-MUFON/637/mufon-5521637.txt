Looked like a plane turning to land. Light from White to Green then sparks out of back

While driving North on Hwy 97, almost to Hwy 31 Junction, a light which looked like a plane turning to land on an airstrip suddenly shot Easternly like a shooting star. It turned to a bright green light, then looked like it had sparks shooting out the back...only two sparks though. It slowed a bit, then disappeared behind the cover of trees blocking my view.
