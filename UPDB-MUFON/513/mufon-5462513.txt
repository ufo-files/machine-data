{cak} ORBs Sighting during the March 2016 Solar Eclipse in Taiwan

I was at work and took pictures of the Solar Eclipse. I didn't notice anything unusual. The weather was good except some low level clouds what actually made it possible to take pictures of the Solar Eclipse. Otherwise it would have been not possible because of the Sun's relatively high azimuth and the bright glare.

Once at home i noticed white, Star or Orb-like Objects close to the Sun on the pictures. I never saw this kind of things on any of my frontal "Sun" pictures i have ever made.

I think i can rule out lens flares and camera dirt. The dirt would be out of focus and the lens i have used creates a different type of flares but very seldom. So honestly i have no idea what it was. 

If requested i can provide the RAW pictures.

Camera Settings: F10-F12, 1/6000s, ISO 200
Equiment: Pentax Kx, Lens Asahi SMC Pentax 200mm
