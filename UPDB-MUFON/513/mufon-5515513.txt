{cak} Questionable light faded

My wife, neighbor and I were walking our dogs. The weather had been grey, some rain and clouds for a couple of consecutive days. It finally became clear that evening.

I had noticed Venus a couple of weeks prior and my first thought was to point it out to everyone, but it was almost 12 hours too early, let alone the wrong rotation in the sky! Its then I noticed that it was slowly moving N-NE! I thought, ok, maybe the ISS was flying over, everyone agreed. I watched it slowly fade into darkness, which made me think satellite or ISS makes sense. 

Today, I have looked up the flightpath of the ISS and many other satellites in the area at that time. NOTHING had flown over our area! No one thought to try to film it with our phones because we were convinced it was the ISS. 

It didn't appear to be anything else other than a satellite, but the brightness was brighter than every star in the sky. The entire sighting lasted maybe 30 seconds. It moved like a satellite, but it seemed to be slower than most Ive seen, you really had to watch it to notice its movement.

It didn't affect the dogs or any of us(my neighbor and wife werent too interested)! It slowly faded to the north-northeast into darkness. 

If I am wrong about the satellite and thats all it was please correct me.
