{rjl} Did not look like a plane at all.

I went outside of my apartment to smoke. Cigarette and I always like to look up at the stars above my building. At around 3:05 a.m. I observed a ball of light that look like a star but a lot bigger in size. I then realized this light was moving and I couldn't believe it because I've grown up near an Air Force base and there is an airport by my house and I see planes coming and going all of the time and have never seen a plane that was constantly bright like this object. There was no blinking red, blue or green lights just a ball of light that stayed light until it disappeared into the clouds.
