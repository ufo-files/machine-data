{nd} Star like object moves and stops

It appeared as if a star were moving in a SW direction. It suddenly stopped about 1/3 of the way across the horizon. Less than "literally" 2 seconds later, a smaller star like object shot from the main star like object and continued on SW. The original object remained stationary and was could be observed still stationary in the same spot the following night. It did not have a very intense glow when originally spotted. I only saw it because it was moving. I have never observed a conventional aircraft at altitude moving at the speeds of the objects....let alone come to a complete stop with seemingly no deceleration.
