Rotating balloon shaped craft over corn field.

Was driving with my 5 year old son @ 1045am when I observed what I first thought was a red and silver balloon floating over a corn field. Then I noticed it's movement seemed very strange and it was a bit large to be a balloon. It was rotating end over end and kept a level flight path it wasn't all over the place like a balloon would be. I was just kind of dumbfounded and even more so when it just dead stopped over the field and hovered for at least 30 seconds before continuing off. My son saw it too and we were both confused at to what we had just seen. I thought perhaps it maybe a funky drone but there was no one else around as it is a fairly rural area. Also when I got home that day I searched online and could not find any drones that looked even remotely close to what I saw. I would really like to know if anyone else has experienced something like this!
