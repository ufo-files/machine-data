Three stars in Pisces constellation appeared like a small version of handle of 'The Plough Constellation, revolved in a triangle.

I was outside letting my dogs out at around half nine at night. Three stars appeared in the Pisces constellation, which looked like a small version of the handle of 'The Plough' Constellation. I thought, 'That is strange, I have never seen those stars before'. Then they revolved in a triangle, uniformly and spreading apart. I rushed to get my phone from inside (I have no idea why as the video would not have picked it up) and when I returned fifteen seconds later the lights had gone.
