{rjl} Red fuzzy orb with amber orb in trail both slowly ascending then coming to a dead hover.

I was standing in my driveway facing west at exactly 8:50 PM and I see a red fuzzy orb flying at normal airline speed, heading from west to east, flying towards me over the Pacific Ocean at a distance of roughly 15 miles. The red orb steadily ascended at a airplane-like angle of attack to about 25,000 feet and slowly came to a dead stop while the amber light/orb caught up following the red orbs angle of attack. So I pulled out my cell phone and was getting the objects in frame and in focus. As soon as I had both in frame and in focus both orbs simultaneously flickered their lights off. I stood out outside for another 20 minutes trying to visually acquire them in a cloudless sky with unlimited visibility. Winds were calm. I did not reacquire them again. About 5-10 minutes later I started seeing commercial airliners flying along the same flight path and altitude as those orbs heading inland (from due west to east) from the Pacific.
