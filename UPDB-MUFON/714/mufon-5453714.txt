{cak} looked like the stars were moving

At around 9:45PM on 4/17/20 I walked outside to my backyard and looked up to see the stars appearing to move in a manner which totally disorientated me. I couldn't figure out if the earth or the sky was moving. After watching for a few seconds, i was able to see many "stars" moving in a NE direction. I ran back inside to get my mother who was in bed. She came out to the backyard with me and observed the same phenomenon that I was seeing. I thought I was recording the entire incident on my phone but there was no recording when I checked later. While watching the incident, I called 911 and reported what we were seeing. Evidently, someone else called 911 also about what we were seeing. The entire incident lasted at least 15 to 20 minutes. When the lights got down to only seeing one or two travel the same path, I went back inside. It was quite an experience I will never forget. I called the local police to see if they had seen anything. They reported that they did not. No surprise there.
