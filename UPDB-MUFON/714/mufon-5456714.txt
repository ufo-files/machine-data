{rjl} Around 10 Lights In The Sky

Around 10 lights were in the sky! Some were rotating around in a pattern but others were darting everywhere. They literally were everywhere and all over the sky. At first we thought it was spotlights but there’s no way it was. They seemed to be inside the cloud cover. They were bright enough to be seen clearly but no ones camera could pick them up because they were too dim for cameras. My husband saw the first ones and then ran inside to get me and we watched them with our neighbors who live in the same apartment complex. We couldn’t believe what we were seeing! I saw the same thing a month ago but it was only one light. This time there were 10! Then later I posted about it on Facebook and my friend Josh said that he also saw something similar tonight! He lives in the same zip code as me.
