(GS) Many Tiny Lights in the Sky

For the past several years and continuing to the present have observed tiny lights in the sky which move erratically. The lights are often very difficult to see unless they are pointed out to you by somebody with a trained eye, experienced in observing them. But once your eyes and brain sync they become increasingly easier to see.
