Four light points traveling in formation at high altitude and high constant speed

Diamond formation of four individual white lights (rear middle one being offset to the right, but not forming any uniform shape between them – only four traveling points) visually similar to orbiting satellites, but traveling at roughly 10-times faster constant speed, while looking to be at very similar or slightly lower height. 
First noticed directly above us while watching stars, at first we thought we're seeing four meteors falling at once, but then they did not dim down and continue flying in formation from east to west in hight altitude, then disappeared over the horizon after 20-30 seconds.
