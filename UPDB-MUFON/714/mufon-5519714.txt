Bright Pulsing light, moved from over ocean to far inland, faded then fell

Saw it outside my window, watched it for full duration, did not get any photos, had binoculars but it was just very bright and went from bright red to white. It climbed up then turned right and slowly flew inland , went more to white then faded and fell to earth inland from my location. No noise heard, but the surf was very loud.
