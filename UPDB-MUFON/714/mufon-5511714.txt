{dr}starlike object that changed shape and emitted flame

I looked out my kitchen window and first observed a star-like object high in the northeastern sky around 9PM EST on 4/5/2013. I continued to observe said object until around 3:30AM EST on 4/6/2013. During this time, the object changed shape and position several times. Sometimes, it looked like there was only one light, and other times, it looked like there were 3 or 4 lights across (from my perspective). At one point, near the end of my observation, I saw one red light. I could not tell if the red light was part of the same object or was a different object that had been emitted from the first object. The first object followed a gradual path from the northeast to the east and then gradually ascended. At one point, I saw a flame shoot out from the object. The first object reached a height that was nearly straight over my house. When I looked again at 5AM EST, it had either disappeared or was straight over my house. I live across from Lunken Airport in Cincinnati and am aware of the flight traffic. I did not observe any planes landing or taking off during the night of my observation as there was a very low cloud cover, and I could not see them. I observe the skies on a nightly basis and see the usual objects, but this one was a bit different. I did get excited when I saw the red light in addition to the white and when I saw the flame.
