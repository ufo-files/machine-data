Blinking/Pulsating light traveling at different speeds with stars seeming to blink in response

I was looking at the stars on a very clear night and noticed what I thought was a bright star seeming to blink brightly and in a regular pattern. It took my phone a few seconds to see it but you can follow the main blinking light and see what seems to be normal stars blinking in response to the main light. It started to travel at varying speeds through the videos, mostly maintaining a quicker pace or almost stopping. There was no noise other than the slight wind that night and with a small airport across the river I’m sure that was not a plane or drone. I am always watching the sky at night anytime I’m outside and see things of the sort almost nightly but this was truly awesome.
