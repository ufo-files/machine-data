{cak} Saw a brilliant white circular object moving above the trees making no sound. When it moved over the road and in front of me the light went out. Heard no sound.

I was walking my dog before going to work as usual. It was around 5am in the morning with a clear, dark sky. When I was on my way to my house, I noticed a very bright light moving above the trees in the forest. It was around 50 feet above the trees moving fron north to south.
