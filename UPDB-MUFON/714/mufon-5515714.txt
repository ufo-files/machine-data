(GS) Extreme Speed Supersonic Craft with 2 unwavering lights traveling due westward towards Texasabove 100,000 ft. elevation

Was sitting in car when noticed high altitude object moving at extreme rate of speed due westward. Object had 2 unwavering blue/green lights and was able to traverse entire 90* viewing angle in less than 1 full second. Objects most noteable feature was that it did not maintain linear motion. Object was neither observed accelerating, nor decelerating. Object appeared to travel through non-conventional means of displacement, perhaps gravitational lensing or area displacement. Object visually looked as a fishing lure might appear to a fish if it were sudenly jerked in rapid succession, making it appear as though it was rapidly accelerating and decelerating instantaneously due to friction caused by water density. Object did no appear to be powered by conventional propulsion means, even that of experimental pulsejet classification as such means would still appear to maintain linear motion and acceleration. Object could possibly be of experimental military classification, but unsure exactly why lights were visible on craft. Further investigation required for possible radar logs and sightings over Southwestern US and Northern Mexico for the same time frame suggested.
