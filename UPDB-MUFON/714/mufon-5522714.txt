(From my own emailed newsletter report)

This morning, about an hour before the break of dawn -- at 5:30 am., to be fairly exact -- I had the visitation of two UFOs in the eastern sky! They appeared, like that first one about a week ago, as two medium-bright stars, not paired but fairly close to one another -- and yet behaving quite differently from last weeks instance.

The one last week, if youll recall, was on a very slow but definite track southward -- too slow to be any aircraft or even a satellite. This mornings pair had no such consistent track. They moved slowly, but quite erratically and, as noted, independently from one another -- generally southward, but sometimes up or downward. One of them remained visible for more than a half-hour.

I was able to track them pretty well with binocs because I was looking through the filter of a leafless tree outside my window, a tree of many slim trunks that allowed me an easy measure of directional movement and relative speed. It was quite a display,and with two such sightings in a weeks time, I have to assume that they are out-and-about so as to be on display at this particular time.
