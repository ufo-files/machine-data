Spring of 1979

Was traviling at 9pm, sun was down, heading west out of Irwin, Iowa. It is very hilly there, about 75 miles ne of Omaha, NE. 
Came to the top of a hill and my car stop working and infront of me was a craft floating between the Hills going south. My hair was floating. I got out of the car and felt very light. There were alot of lights on the belly of the ship. Was moving soo slow.
