{cak} my two sons watched a glowing orange ball move west, then south and suddenly disappeared

On or about February 4th, 2013 at approximately 7:12-7:15 in the evening, my two sons jumped in their car to go to the local store. After leaving the driveway they came to a stop at a stop sign facing east. Both noticed a round orange glowing ball to the southeast of their sight. After driving east for one block they immediately turned around to keep watching and noticed that the ball began moving west turned south and disappeared. This happened in a few minutes after turning only a few seconds to disappear. This is the second sighting for one brother,and a third sighting for the older brother all in or near Anadarko,Ok.
