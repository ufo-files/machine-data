A Large MultiColored Silent Craft (Video)

When I lived in the City of Fowler back in 2014 I lived in the county so I had a large open night sky to see and surrounded by grapevines 
After midnight around 1 AM in the Summer time around August that night was very clear. I saw a flashing light off in the distance in the NE Direction maybe a mile or more away.
I video taped this object through a telescope to get a better view to my surprise it was a craft and looked to change shape it would move off and on. It lit up so bright I saw what looked like square windows in front and the front was split in half very odd. This object hovered and stayed a good hour or more. But i had enough video of it so I stopped filming. I have photo stills from this video in another MUFON case report # 114625 For this case I uploaded the video of what I saw that night. I also included a different video of a purple and green object. Thank you
