{rjl} Triangle. Broad daylight

I was sitting in my vehicle before work arrived 1230. I looked out towards the horizon and saw what I thought was a plane because of the closeness of the local airport. I like planes so I was watching it fly toward me. It then stopped. Dead stop. I thought maybe it was a helicopter but then it moved upward, to the left, back right and then hovered again while riding. It was then that could see it was triangular in shape. It hovered a couple more seconds and then moved off to the east very rapidly and out of my sight.
