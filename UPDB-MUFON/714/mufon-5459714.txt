{rjl} Solid red light, no sound, rapid acceleration then vanished

Was walking back from Fireworks over the Ohio in Evansville prior to ending of show. Observed an airliner flying over and saw flashing white light and red light. After finale of show was walking Northeast up Mulberry St within 10 yards of 3rd St. Saw an airplane flying in front of me, lights flashing (red and white). To my right just above the rooftop observed a solid red light moving from south to north. The light was solid red and moving rapidly across the sky. It appeared to be reflecting a small portion of red light onto a solid object just above the light that was facing my direction. My immediate thought was a plane with a red light shining from its side under a wing towards the river. However, unlike the previously observed aircraft there was zero sound. I told my fiancé to look at it and asked him if he thought it was a flare from a firework. He said he didn’t know. I said, it’s just so odd bc there is no sound, and no other observed lights and/or flashing lights. As I watched the light move in front of me, I stopped walking to get a better look, as it came directly in front of me the light vanished (not faded, not shimmered out, vanished). There was no audible sound or any visible aircraft. Immediately looked at my watch and recorded the time and the sighting.
