Changed colors like a fiber optic light, moved up and down, swayed side to side very rapidly

My husband saw a light while he was in the backyard and called for my mother and I to come out and view it. It moved rapidly and can be described as a fish swimming in water. It looked like a fiber optic light moving from one color to the next seamlessly. I told him to go get a camera and by the time he came back it looked as if it landed somewhere near the west mesa. I viewed it personally for about a minute. My husband viewed it for about 2 minutes. We will never forget what we saw this Christmas Eve night.
