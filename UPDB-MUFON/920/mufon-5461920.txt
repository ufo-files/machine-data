{cak} 2007 one day before Silvis, IL sighting..

March, 2007: I was on IL route 92 heading toward Milan, IL, which goes SE. At intersection of rt 92 and Andalusia RD I saw a black triangular object hovering above. I could not say the distant, but it was above wires of some sort (which were put there by electricians or other US company. The object was a little bit to my right. Upon seeing this I thought it to have3 something with Air Force testing. I turned left onto Andalusia Road, then left again on 67 to Just Kids Daycare. When we got out of the car a black triangular object was hovering above a pole and it appeared to be very close. Again, I assumed it to be Air Force testing. (I had seen a couple in California near military bases.)
I did not have a camera with me. I took my granddaughter into the day care and left for work I did not look up but it was still there before I left. No idea what it was for sure. However, a day or two after this, a woman in Silvis, IL, Theresa Sinclair, of Silvis, IL saw the same thing according to news articles.
