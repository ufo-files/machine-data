very bright light, during dusk, no stars out yet, no flashing but erratic movement

walking the dog at dusk, noticed in the sky directly in front of me a very bright object. The stars were not yet visible as the night sky was still dusky, not pitch black. There were no flashing lights of a plane, but there was a plane in the sky in the northern area. What alerted me was how bright the light was, it was larger than a star but not as large as the sun. It moved very fast and erratically up and down and backwards and forwards in a pattern I have never seen a plane or helicopter do. I immediately thought what is this? I grabbed my phone to video it but it dissapeared without a trace. I spoke to someone else the next day to tell them of this and they too saw this light, but in a different location perhaps more northerly. They described exactly the same manoueveres.
