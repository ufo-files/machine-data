Series of lights in the sky in Salem

Shortly after 10 PM April 2nd, 2022 a bright light was observed in the northwest sky. Initially the light was believed to be a helicopter until another appeared. After the first light faded away another appeared in a similar fashion and the process repeated several times. This happened repeatedly in the same area of the sky for longer than 10 minutes but less than 20 with up to five of them appearing at once. There was no observable sound but the objects appeared distant and inside the city there was too much other noise to notice.
We have watched a video with a similar type of sighting that was from a plane where they were looking down but have never seen such an event ourselves.
