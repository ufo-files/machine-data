{rjl} Saw a bright object ascending leaving a light trail and bending the clouds around it.

I was driving EAST on Lester rd in Apopka, FL. It was about 7:35pm the sun was setting behind me and you could see the sunlight on the tips of the clouds to my east. Out of nowhere a bright light was ascending upwards leaving a light trail and you could see the clouds bending around it. There wasnt a clear view because it never came out of the clouds. It was if it was on the edge of the clouds. I lost sight because was stopped at a stop sign and a car came behind me and once i turned i lost sight of it. I was shocked because i could see it very clearly the light and the light trail it was leaving. I have a video but its very blurry due to the auto focus would not lock in on it.
