{rjl} Just got a new phone and wanted to try out the camera

I had just got a new Droid Turbo 2 from the Verizon store in Silverdale, Wa. My wife and I decided to go get some lunch at a local pizza place. I was excited to try out the phone's camera because it was supposed to be a 21 megapixel camera. We were sitting down and I took picture of the surrounding area out the window. I looked at the picture briefly but really never looked at it closer until later that night. As I was looking at the picture I noticed what looked like bubbles in the sky. I zoomed on to get a closer look and noticed that there 8 of these things. They all basically have the same shape, but if look each of them closer the lighting is a little different on each. A couple of the even look like they are tilted to one side. If you look past them and zoom in to the left of them, there are three other objects in the clouds that look similar. Then I noticed one located to the right and up from the original eight and it appears to have a stream behind it. I was shocked and amazed when I saw all of them.My wife and I seen absolutely nothing when I took the picture not until later did I notice them. I showed my wife and son, and they also could not explain what they were.
