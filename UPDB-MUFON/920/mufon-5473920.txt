Observed from airplane on a flight from Spain to Ireland.

I was on a flight from Spain to Ireland. We were cruising at high altitude and high speed. We were over the sea. The sky was blue with very little cloud. I looked out the window, as you do on planes, and saw, clear as day, a sphere travelling in the opposite direction. Initially I thought, 'What's that?' I stared and realised it wasn't a plane. It wasn't anything identifiable. It was a large white ball speeding through the sky. I kept track of it for about 20 seconds.I took my eyes off it and lost track of it. I felt silly but told my travelling companion, 'I seriously think I just saw a UFO!' That was it. Unless you saw it you wouldn't believe it. I don't know what it was but it was there!
