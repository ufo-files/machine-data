{tjd} I saw a string of orb like lights

I was looking at Venus and saw these lights I thought were stars till I noticed them moving and there were so many. I started counting and first string I counted 8 then a break and something in front of next string it was two lights side by side with a gap between and there were 10 lights behind it in a row behind each other like a formation and another double light then 8 to 10 more. So approximately 28 lights in a row. They came from the west going NE. Now I've see these types of lights a lot here and elsewhere and have for years. I call them unknowns. And you can hit them or by them with a laser and they'll power up. But I've never seen that many flying across the sky at one time. I've seen 2 or 3 in a row before. I couldn't believe how many there was.
