CE5 session results

I had just done a CE5 session by myself right at noon time. After i finished the meditation i proceeded to walk outside and realized it was very cloudy with patches of blue sky. So i took a few pictures and went on inside to review them. In the picture you can see a egg/oval shape ufo just hovering there amidst the clouds. You can clearly see that the craft is not part of a cloud formation. I then went back outside and as i looked up i saw a cloud starting to fade away and it was like pulling back the veil. As that happened i saw a large black triangle shape ufo. As quickly as it appeared it vanished behind an upcoming cloud. At that moment i was so shocked and it was so quick i just had enough time to appreciate it with my own eyes.
