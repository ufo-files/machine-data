{rjl} Bar of Lights

I am on this site, since UFO means Unidentified Flying Object, and what I saw was something I could not quite identify, and in flight. Most likely, it was a man-made aircraft, since the airport in State College is roughly in the same direction as the object's origin.
At first, I thought I was looking at two stars, but then I noticed they were moving. I realized that they weren't two separate objects, but instead, one object with an anterior and posterior light. The object then began ascending, and I noticed what seemed to be smaller, faint-lights that spanned the distance between the anterior and posterior lights. These seemed to undulate in dimness.
Soon enough, the object seemed to fade away, most likely breaking unseen clouds in the night sky.
