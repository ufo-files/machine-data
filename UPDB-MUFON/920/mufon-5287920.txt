3 UFOs with military presence

Standing outside with dogs I witnessed a UFO fly from out of nowhere from the southwestern portion of the sky towards the east. Standing for a few more seconds I witnessed a military plane trailing it immediately. A second UFO in the same general direction Southwest to east path appeared. The military plane’s path followed a southern trajectory. Waiting a few more minutes I witnessed the two UFOs come back down as if they were stationary up in the atmosphere where they had initially disappeared. They took off at a high rate of speed. Joined together or on top of each other then crossed each other and took off in separate directions. One heading south towards the direction of the military plane and one heading in a more northerly path. About an hour and a half later letting my dogs out again. What seemed like a large star came out of a constellation and travelled at a high constant speed at a very low elevation. I felt if I had enough time I could have seen it with binoculars. It had an aura as it passed, with no noise. All UFOs were round and looked like a white star. None made any noise. Every time one flew over the house the animals around the country side would go nuts, barking, howling and mooing. When it got far enough away everything would stop. This happened every time.
