Witnessed set of yellow lights in the evening sky hovering and slowly moving without any sound.

A friend and I on June 28, 2011, were walking in San Diego about 8:30 pm and we both saw a yellow-gold set of lights that seemed to be hovering or moving very slowly at about 45 degrees. There was no motor sound even when the lights started moving faster. The lights would seem to go from four into two or one and then reappear. We both thought it could be our bad eyes or vapor in the air that made the lights seem to dance. We both knew we'd never seem something so odd in in the sky and it was eerie that there was no noise, especially after it seemed to accelerate and simply disappear before our very eyes.
