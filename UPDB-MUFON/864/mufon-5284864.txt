rectangular white light, not particularly bright (less bright than Jupiter), moving very fast North to South

I was sitting on my back patio at night, which I often do. It was a perfectly clear night; no clouds, but enough ambient light that only a few stars were visible. I was lying back in my Adirondack chair, looking straight up (90 degrees), when I saw this object/light come into view. It was rectangular, wider than it was high. From my vantage point, it appeared to be about a half inch wide and an eighth of an inch high. It was a steady white light, not as bright as Jupiter, which was also visible. No anti-collision strobes or navigation lights were visible. It made no sound. There were no aircraft visible at the time and the Flightradar24 app on my iPhone did not register anything in the air in my vicinity. It was moving in a straight line, far too fast to be an aircraft or a satellite. It traversed the sky from about 85 degrees to about 30 degrees, traveling north to south, in about one second, at which point it vanished. By its shape, I don't believe that it was a meteor. Although it was impossible to tell its altitude and exact speed or its distance from me, it was moving far faster than any aircraft or satellite I have ever seen.
