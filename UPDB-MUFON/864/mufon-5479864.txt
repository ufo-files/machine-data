Followed two objects as the flew over trees and open fields. They flew off following river Exe. Another couplle of people pulled into layby as they were baffled!

My wife and 3 children had been to a firework display. On the way home we noticed
Bright blue and white lights in the sky over the trees. Two objects were flying very
closely together, to close to be planes or helicopters. 
These things were being piloted by experts, close together then some distance apart, instantly changing direction & altitude!
They were in and out of site over the trees but eventually we saw them again, they were to our right flying parallel with our car. We pulled in to a rubbish tip and I started filming on my iPhone. Another car pulled in next to ours, the male and female didn't know what they'd witnessed either! We said goodbye and set off, catching up with the silent objects, as we entered Tiverton town we took a right, we looked to our left & saw the objects heading away over the town. That was the last time we saw them.
