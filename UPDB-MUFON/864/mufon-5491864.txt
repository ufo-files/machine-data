{dr}Orb with lights sighted in horizon in Arizona 6/23/15

We were traveling west on Interstate 40 around 8 p.m. with a beautiful sunset in view. I decided to snap a few photos of the sunset. I took three consecutive pictures and immediately looked at the photos to see if they were good ones. Upon looking at the photos I observed an orb to the right of the sunset with lights inside of it. I instantly observed the horizon again and the object was gone. I immediately took more photos in that direction but no orb was seen in any. We were driving so I could not identify any sound. In the photos the orb seems to move right and then left again. I researched the sighting to see if the image could relate and be explaind, but discovered no conclusion, therefore I am reporting it to you. The last photo uploaded was zoomed in from original photo.
