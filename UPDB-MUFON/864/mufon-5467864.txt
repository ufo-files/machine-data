{rjl} Flashing light in sky

I walked out of my house at approximately 11 am. As I was walking in my yard and into my driveway, I looked up into the sky and witnessed a flashing light. I observed it for like about 15 minutes and then decided to pull out my iphone to video record the flashing light.
