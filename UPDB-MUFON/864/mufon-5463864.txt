{rjl} Driving West bound on I76, saw 4 lights in the sky. First were in straight vertical line. Then became diamond formation, then descended heading West

I was heading Westbound on I76 on 12/15/18 at about 6:10pm. I looked out my driver's side window (South) and saw 4 white lights in a vertical line. I initially thought I had never noticed a radio tower there. Then I continued to watch. The 4 lights grouped together. 1 faded out then reappeared. Then 2 faded out and reappeared in a diamond formation. They started to separate as they descended on the horizon. That was when I was able to pull out my phone and capture them as they were low on the horizon.
