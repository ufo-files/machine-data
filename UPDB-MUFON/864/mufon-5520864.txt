bright lights hovering in sky for an hour then travels to west & disappears

On Monday night about 8:oo pm I stepped outside to smoke a cigarette and noticed the large, bright light in the western sky. An airplane flew above it and it appeared to rotate slowly, following the planes movement. My parents (ages 56 & 57)and my 2 children (ages 6 & 8) also observed the lights/object while standing outside and from inside the house. It hovered in the sky, with lights changing colors from white to red to orange to yellow, etc...randomly. Looking through binoculars, I could not identify a distinct object, although the light pattern looked like the shape of a cross. After an hour or so, the object slowly moved west and was out of sight. The following evening, the light/object appeared in the same spot as the night before, and about the same time. Again, after about an hour of hovering, it disappeared in a westerly direction. But, this time it re-appeared about 11pm and disappeared again about 15 minutes later.
