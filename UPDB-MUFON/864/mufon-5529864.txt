round amber lights silent

I went outside around 2am for a cigarette before going to bed. the sky was perfectly clear no clouds, i was outside about 3-4 minutes when i looked to the east and saw a disc shaped object no higher than 10-15 stories up coming from east heading west. it was lit up with amber or orange lights in a circular pattern. as soon as this object came off the ocean and over land its lights went out, i could still see it, it was like a shadow but still visable. as it passed directly over head it made no sound at all and headed west i realized there were 2 more, seemed like they were waiting for it with no lights and (dancing)around. the object moved very slowly. as soon as it met up with the others they all moved west and out of sight. I know what I saw and it was real.whatever it was.
