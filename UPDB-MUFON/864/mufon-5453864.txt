{cak} Observed over 30 lights moving in sky traveling from NW to SE

At 5:15 am it was still dark. I observed over 30 lights over a period of about 15 minutes traveling from the NW to the SE. They were not grouped together but if there were a couple lights near each other their position in relation to each other did not change. The lights were generally spaced apart - not grouped at all. This was nothing like the videos online showing more organic movements in a group or cluster. They all traveled at the same speed. To the north and south some were visable. As it got light and the stars disappeared, so did the lights. I suspect this fleet must have been up there before I saw it. Considering the 'organized' way they were traveling I had the thought that maybe the crafts were piloted by humans. I did not take a video, as I was in awe as I counted the white lights. I woke my husband and he came outside and saw them for a few minutes. They were all traveling the same direction, the same speed, and looked identical with no flickering. The seemed to be as high as a plane, traveling much faster.
