{rjl} UFO buzzed overhead while sitting on porch smoking cigarette

I went outside for a cigarette before retiring for the night. While sitting there this object came from behind me (N.W.) just over our apartment/house and continued on a Southeast direction. I still watched the object until I could no longer see it with my eyes.
