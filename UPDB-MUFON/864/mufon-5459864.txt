Looked the same as a satellite would but zigzagged instead of orbited

I saw what I thought was a satellite, which I would often see, but this white dot changed direction after about seconds, then again after two seconds, at about 45 degrees each time, each time it would travel about one hand span at arms length. Towards the southern horizon. It was a very uniformed pattern and lasted about 1 minute or less.
