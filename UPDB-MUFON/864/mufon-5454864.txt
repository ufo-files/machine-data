5 lights that looked like donuts

Partial cloud on the night , five lights that had shape of donuts spinning slowly clockwise observed this for roughly 5 to 6 minutes during which shouted to my wife who also seen them.
The strangest thing though , three to four months later was visiting friends roughly 250 miles south , went outside for a smoke and seen exactly the same lights which again my wife witnessed only this time the two friends we were visiting also witnessed the phenomenon
