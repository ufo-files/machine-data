{rjl} Extreme bright craft flying north to south quickly no flashing lights no sound obvious airplane travelled east to west at same time but had sound and flashing lights

While retrieving an item from my car I looked up into the sky and saw a very bright light or series of lights in the sky traveling north to south over my home. I called out to my nephew to come look at it. We then heard a plane and my nephew said it sounds like plane. Immediately a very obvious airplane passed over the house from east to west at a Lowe altitude than the unidentifiable craft. We continued to watch the lights until they disappeared from view. Very strange. We were both concerned at the size of the lights and the speed. We researched the possibility of another type of craft being present but could find no information and decided to make the report here
