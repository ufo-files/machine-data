{dr}Star like reflection that disappeared in a blink.

While driving to work on Route 1 express heading toward Morrisville, PA I saw a bright object in the northeast sky. I would have to estimate that it was about 3000ft above the ground and I was approx 1 mile from the object. The object didn't appear to be moving but it was hard to tell as I was driving in my car doing about 60 miles per hour. I never actually saw the object only a bright reflection. The time of day was approx 6:30am. Sun rise was at 6:19am on the morning of August 28, 2012. 
After about ten seconds the bright object disappeared. I didn't see what was causing the reflection. No plane. No helicopter. No blimp. 
About 20 minutes later I saw another bright reflection in the sky. This time I was on New Jersey Transit train and was just pulling out of the train station at 6:46am. Like before the object simply disappeared. I didn't see what was causing the reflection. There were no clouds in the sky.
