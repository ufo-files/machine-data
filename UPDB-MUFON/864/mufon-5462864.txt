{tjd} High altitude missle/like high performance high speed craft. It was above air traffic in Seattle Metro area.

Starting at 11:15 hours Pacific time on 1/26/2019. Traveling SE to NE was a missile type with little wings traveling at high altitude and high speed. This is just east of high traffic commercial aircraft control area for the Seattle Metro air space. This is the home of Boeing and I am very used to seeing all kinds of aircraft. I have never seen a vehicle of this kind of speed traveling this fast in a TCA. I have been a pilot and I would have reported this if I had been flying. This craft was traveling in a straight line with a cork screw contrail with a parallel smoke line. I am guessing this craft was traveling over the traffic 50 to 200 thousand feet at Mach 3 or 4 and gaining altitude. It went from horizon to horizon in 20 minutes.
