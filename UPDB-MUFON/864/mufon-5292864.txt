Vega was moving like a UFO

My son and I were stargazing at Joshua Tree, and for about an hour, we watched a star (or what looked like a star) bounce around like hummingbird. It would move up, then back, then left, then right, constantly zigging. Not sure if it was Vega or something near Vega, but it was seemingly at that distance.
