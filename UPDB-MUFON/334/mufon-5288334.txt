Orb like objects following Jet Aircraft

While photographing Airforce-1 flying across the black hills of South Dakota, I captured some objects that appeared to be following along with the aircraft. The mostly Orb like objects were too small to see in real time through my 70-300 Telephoto lens, but were instead noticed afterward while I was processing the photographs. There were several that appear perfectly round and likely metallic in nature, others were irregular or looked like a small glowing cross or plus sign. I have photographed similar objects throughout the western U.S.
