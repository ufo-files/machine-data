The short description pretty much summarizes it. We were out before midnight to watch fireworks for New Years Eve. We saw the object which was clearly NOT a firework. It travelled north then vanished. As I could tell was that it was a smooth red or orange light that travelled a direct trajectory until it reached a certain point and then vanished. Five minutes later, a white light showed up in almost the same spot that it vanished and travelled southbound at a slightly higher rate of speed until we could no longer see it beyond the treelined horizon. The white light resembled a slower moving meterorite. The original red/orange light did not.
