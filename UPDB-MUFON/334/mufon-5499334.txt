{nd} Very fast bright blue, descended through cloud cover, lit up surrounding sky and clouds

I was standing on my mother-in-law's porch, facing north, smoking a cigarette at approx. 22:20 EST. Bright blue light, super fast, no trail. Passed overhead in a northerly direction. When it first entered my line of sight from over and behind, it must have been around 55-60 degrees. I'm not sure how accurate that measurement is, as I am trying to improvise using my arms as a sort of impromptu sextant. From my point of view it was flying straight out. As it traveled at incredible speed, it seemed to passed through the cloud cover in descent, apparent because it lit them up (the clouds) in a beautiful blue glow. Lost sight behind the houses and trees across the street as it traveled away and down. The whole event last between 3-4 seconds but it was one of the most beautiful sights I've ever seen in my life. It was so beautiful in fact that it has left me with a slight feeling of euphoria. It was awesome in the truest sense of the word. It was so unexpected and so fast that I didn't have a chance to take out a camera of any sort. MUFON please feel free to contact me if I have left anything out. What an amazing sight. I can only hope I get to witness something like this again at some point in my life.
