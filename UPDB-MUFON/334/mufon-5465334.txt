{tjd} Abruptly moving object in astronomy photo

I was attempting to photograph an ongoing meteor shower with my Canon SL2 DSLR camera on a tripod from my driveway. I did not personally witness the object. It was only apparent in the photographs. 

I took several long exposures to try and catch meteor trails. In one photograph, an erratically moving light makes a squiggled trail through a large part of the sky. The object does not appear to be an artifact or result of operator error (such as motion blur, etc) as the stars in the background remain as single dots in focus. Its distance and rate of speed are unknown, however, it appears to be higher than the clouds that were passing by in the image. 

None of the other photographs (46 other exposures) exhibit the object and only one exhibits what appears to be meteorites. I have attached the two photographs, with the meteor photograph for reference. 

The photographs were taken with 20 second exposures with a 40mm lense at f/3.2.
