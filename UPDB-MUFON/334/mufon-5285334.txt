Bright light hovering, then quick burst and out of sight

I was driving to work on a military installation. As I turned into the street that goes to my work site, I see a light which appeared to be above my work site. I work at a classified site with flight restrictions, and although not unheard of, it is unusual to see any aircraft in the area. It was still dark and c=some cloud cover. At first I though it was a flood light from a helicopter and as I drover toward it (and work site). It moved at a pace that could not have been a helicopter and if it had been a search light of help, the beam would have veered off. This object stayed light and moved towards the west at rapid speed and either the light went out or was out of visual in about 1 second.
