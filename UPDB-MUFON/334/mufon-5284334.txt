Square object maneuvering in the sky

It was a fairly warm, mostly sunny afternoon. I was coming out of Lowes w some new tools when I spotted the object high in the afternoon sky. It appeared to be cube shaped & spinning & flashing wildly! It seemed to be both, emitting it's own light, but also reflecting the sunlight. It remained in the same general area in the sky, but moved rather erratically from side to side, up & down. At times it seemed to flutter as if falling freely, but then would make a sharp 90° movement. I couldn't begin to tell you how big or how far away it was, but my best guess is 30,000+ft. & HUGE!
The whole incident lasted about 3-4minutes, at which point I lost sight of the object.
