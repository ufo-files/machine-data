Object with shape/proportions of rectangle/cylinder moving sideways

Looking up at sky from University of Washington campus, laying on back and enjoying the weather and noticed black object about 60 degrees above horizon moving east-s. east in a large S-pattern. Object was at lower altitude than cumulus clouds. It appeared to accelerate as it approached 30 degrees above horizon. and became invisible a couple of times against blue sky. Initially thought it was a balloon, but edges were sharp and movements were those of a powered craft of some sort. Event lasted around 1 minute and weather was partly cloudy.
