{dr}Red Orange glowing lights hovering in random swirling patterns of movement, then fading out.

I noticed lights that looked like big Red-orange sparks in the SE sky. They were mostly hovering together, and had a slow swirling pattern of movement. they were larger and brighter than any star, but not large enough to determine a shape or distance. I thought they might have been glowing Chinese sky lanterns. There were about a dozen of them. I was on the phone at the time, and when I noticed them I tried to turn my phone onto camera mode, but by the time I fumbled with it, they were starting to fade. They didn't even show up on my phone's camera screen. Eventually there was just one left. The last one was still for a moment before if flickered and seem to either fade into distance, or burn out. They made no noise, and didn't perform any extraordinary aerobatics. It was impossible for me to tell how close they were without another frame of reference. I was surprised at first - never having seen anything like it, them intrigued, then frustrated because I couldn't get my phone to work, finally disappointed when they started to fade. When I looked on the internet news this morning I noticed that Red Orange lights in the sky have been seen before in NC.
