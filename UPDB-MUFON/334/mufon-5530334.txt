4 high flying objects appeared similar to how satelites in orbit, except speed was much too fast and non orbital motion was shown.

We were swimming down at Nelson's Landing which is on Lake Mojave. We were floating around looking at the stars. My sister noticed 4 objects moving in the sky. As we watched, 2 objects appeared to fly away from earth and while the one of the other objects sped up to join the other object and they flew away on an easterly trajectory. I watch the stars often, but have never seen a satellite exhibit any erratic behavior like I seen last night, nor have I ever seen one match the speed these objects had. Unfortunately we were swimming and didn't have a camera handy.
