{cak} triangular shape, large, bright lights and flat looking

I witnessed this while on my way home with my dad, we were just leaving my unlce's house in triple crown. it was a school night so i had to get home. i was looking out the window and i saw three very large lights hovering over a house not to far from my own. i told my dad we stopped the car. the lights were moving but were still keeping their triangular shape. we were not their very long, we were on frog town road which is a mainroad. but that night there were not many cars. there was one car though coming up behind us so we had to keep driving. once we got home we looked outside, we were not that far from the house the lights were hovering above, sadly the lights were gone by now but i'll never forget that night. i dont remember the day it was on but i do remember it was late at night on a school night around eleven or twelve it was very calm.
