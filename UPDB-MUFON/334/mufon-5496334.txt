{dr}Fireball

Upon exiting my mothers home at [Address removed/cms/tg]], I noticed a pair of fireballs in the southeast sky in the direction of Cincinnati Northern Kentucky Airport. They appeared to be two objects, possibly aircraft with fiery trails that extinguished and then reignited a few moments later. Their flight path appeared erratic and there were several other aircraft in the area. I opened the LiveATC.net application on my cellphone to hear if Cincinnati-Northern Kentucky Airport's tower or approach had any reports of possible aircraft in distress, there were none. I observed the objects descend quickly as if to extinguish an engine fire, the fire extinguished and the object regained altitude. Several other objects that, like the first two, had aircraft position lights illuminated were following these objects. The objects then re-ignited and again descended below the tree line to the southwest. The fires were extinguished and the objects appeared to fly possible 'split s' maneuvers and headed back to the east. As a student pilot, I was worried that I was witnessing a potential aircraft crash.
