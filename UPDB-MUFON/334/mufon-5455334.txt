Many ( at least 50 that we saw ) starlike objects travelling across the sky

Between 6:00am and 6:15am, on Feb 20/20, my spouse looked out the window, ( it was still dark out ) and noticed what looked like stars moving very fast in the sky towards the NE. We went out on our patio, and one by one, travelling at the same speed, very high up in the sky, travelling extremely fast, travelling in kind of an arc across the sky from one side of the horizon to the other, with no sound at all, we witnessed at least 50-75 starlike objects travelling across the sky one right after another quite uniformly. They were travelling so fast, I had a hard time keeping them in my binoculars vision. We have never seen anything like this before.  They were not as bright as the satellites I have seen going by, and there was just so many of them that we saw, and there may have been who knows how many more before we noticed them.  It was very strange. Could this have been army jets doing some kind of exercise? They were flying probably at least twice the height of regular jets, and at least twice as fast as jets. although, this is just a guess. We thought at first that they were shooting stars...they were that fast. Thank you, Mary
