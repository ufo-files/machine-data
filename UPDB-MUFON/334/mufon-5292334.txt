Huge cigar shaped objects (two) very high up in atmosphere.

We were in a friend's backyard on Father's Day afternoon, around 3:30PM when we noticed a somewhat shiny, long object way up in the atmosphere; probably at the altitude that military jets/bombers fly their patrols. It's surface looked metallic grayish/whitish, and the sun glinted off the exterior. The object was motionless. Within a few minutes a second object appeared from the south, moving quickly to the first one, and took up a perpendicular position adjacent to the first object. They both remained absolutely motionless for at least 15 minutes. At that point we had to leave the friend's home for another engagement and we do not know exactly how long afterwards the objects remained in the area.
