Went to fort Osborn to see fireworks..parked on street close by. Cloudless night bright and sunny all day half way through show rain came down heavy everybody ran to cars I looked up and saw UFO...watched it forv5minutes or so ran back to car ...

I was watching fireworks at night at fort Osborn barracks...had 3other people with me ...half way through the event it started raining really hard everyone ran to their cars..for unknown reason I stayed and looked up at the sky...that's when I saw this huge window like object just above the tree line...it looked like square windows of light about the size of a football field...the whole thing was lit up..at tree line I'd say about 30 to 40 feet high.when I looked around there was nobody else but I did see a girl run to a car behind mine and I heard her say did you see that ...very excited....when I got back to my car my wife wanted to go home right away as her hair had gotten wet and she wanted to go home and dry it off....forgot all about this thing till 20 or more years later......now I wonder if anyone else report this event was it a mother ship did it land etc etc I knew it was a UFO of some kind ..I was very excited in seeing this thing it was my first sighting......
