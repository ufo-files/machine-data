{cak} star like lights aligned in the shape of a triangle, flying approximately 500ft or so above me and very slowly, making almost no noise whatsoever

One night in 2012 during my freshman year of high school I was at my schools football field practicing kicking field goals. I was the only one there, as I was just dedicated to getting better at my sport. It was very dark, except for the one light my coach allowed me to turn on at any time at night if I decided to come practice kicking. Yes I had a cool coach. Anyways, i kicked one up and and I remember thinking wow what a nice kick, then something caught my eye. It seemed to be a triangular craft, dark as night, consisting of about 9, maybe 10 lights that looked like bright stars glowing a blueish white color around the perimeter of it. Or it could have been 9 or 10 crafts, in a triangular formation, flying very low at approximately 500 ft or so in the sky, and flying directly above me to my northwest. It or they, was making no noise whatsoever except for the air whooshing around the craft(s). Either way, I got a good look and took off running back home as fast as I could. I even left my phone, footballs, my tee, etc1 always that's my story, something I will remember for the rest of my life. Thanks for reading.
