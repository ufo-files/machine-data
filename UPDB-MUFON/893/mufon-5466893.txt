Well it was a summer Monsoon and I wanted to try and get some footage of the lightning. However the storm seemed to have died out a bit and I wasn't getting any good footage. I then started walking back home when I saw a light in the distance. I looked at it for a moment and realized it wasn't moving. Right then I new what it was because I had seen these lights before a couple years ago during another monsoon storm. I then ran back home and garbed my other camera because the one I was using for the lightning couldn't zoom in. I then ran down the street to get a better view of it with the least amount of trees and buildings in the way. Right when I started taking video thats when the second light showed up. They then started to light up real bright and then dim back and forth along from moving away from each other. Eventually they both dimmed out completely and were gone.  [link moved/cms/rjl]
