High altitude fly over

At 5:25 am central time, I stepped out to smoke a cigarette, a usual routine I do every morning, along with looking up at the sky to assess the weather (cloudy/clear) I notice a dull bluish light, giving off about the same luminance of a star traveling a high rate of speed, appearing to cross through the big dipper move from north to south. What made this different was a very bright flash of light it gave off as it traveled cross the sky. I witnessed about 5 flashes of light as it made it's way across the. No sound, no contrail, and no navigation lights were seen. As an avid aviation enthusiast, I know what to look for to rule out high altitude aircraft fly over, and usually confirm using Flightradar24 to map a flight path. No such aircraft in that quadrant of sky.
