{cak} Thought it was meteor bc it was white and descending fast then glowing pink/red when I lost it behind buildings

Waiting my turn to enter Brazos Crossing shopping center (the part that is located on south side of I-69) in Rosenberg Texas . Looked up and saw what looked like a meteor.or a very short contrail. Sky was completely clear of clouds ... a typical clear blue December day in South Texas.
When I noticed it was descending rapidly and starting to turn a pinkish red I pulled into the Walgreens parking lot to get some pix (also called my husband and asked him to go outside and look but the trees on our street obscured his line of sight).
Was able to get a couple of pix but was unable to capture any of the color change.
Shortly after, the object (still on a slanted course) passed from my view ....obscured by the rooftops of the stores in shopping center.
I then continued to The Home Store and parked.
Heard what sounded like helicopters after leaving my car but thought nothing of it until I filled out this report.
Have attached pix but really hard to see object unless you zoom in to area above (and to the left) the Home Goods rooftop.
