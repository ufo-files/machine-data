My boyfriend, our friend, and I were walking down the street around the border of Sunnyside and Woodside in Queens, NY, enjoying the strangely warm temperatures this winter has brought. 
As they are having a conversation about cars I am uninterested with, I look up to the night sky (I need to wear my glasses to see all the stars possible in a NYC sky) and for only 1.5 second, I saw two white, cigar shaped objects flying parallel to each other, with white auras at the ends. The top one was double in length, and right in between them was the star Regulus. They seemed to be moving outwards into space, going towards the left, as we were face the south east... Almost as if they were racing... I watched in awe and knew I couldn't turn away if I wanted to see it through, but before I could even blink... it seemed to have vanished into a dark area of space undetectable by naked human eyes.
