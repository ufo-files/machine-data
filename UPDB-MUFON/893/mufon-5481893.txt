{dr}Transparent triangle like craft, could see the stars through it but also could see definite shape of the object.

I was on the beach with my then girlfriend in late June, 2009, enjoying a nice walk around 21:00. We were talking and talking and just enjoying the atmosphere of the evening. We sat down for a few minutes and then got up, started walking parallel to the beach towards where I parked my car. I first noticed the object maybe 30 seconds after we started walking. I was looking at the stars to the west of me and noticed something moving. I could tell what it was at first but then when I focused on the area, I saw something moving that had a faint shape. The edges were visible but it was so strange because I could see the stars through the craft. It was moving when I first saw it from the north going south. It continued to move south after I stopped with my then girlfriend. There was no noise, no smell and appeared to be huge. 

It seemed like it cloaked itself and just glided across the sky, not moving really quickly, maybe around 30 miles per hour, but it was moving. It kept moving south until it started to reach the downtown area of Myrtle Beach SC. I lost visual of the craft when it appeared to have reached the tall hotels of the downtown area. 

I was first mystified and very curious of what I saw, and then I felt like I should fear the experience and not mention it to anyone, but I know what I saw.
