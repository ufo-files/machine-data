cigar shaped ufo, black surface with alternating flashing lights spaced less than a foot apart.

this interaction happened in 1986. I was 7 years old playing at the towns playground that was attached to the softball fields. There were 6 kids including myself playing around 7:30 P.M., no adults where on the playground. All of the adults where at the softball fields watching the older children play.

The ufo appeared after the sun had set. The ufo was cigar shaped, it actually looked almost like a submarine floating in the air. the ufo had a black surface with flashing lights less than a foot apart along the entire length of the ufo. It didn't make any sound and only the kids on the playground saw it. I remember trying to jump up in the air to touch it along with the other kids. We then decided to climb on top of the playground equipment so we could jump higher. After almost touching the ufo, the ufo then climbed in altitude until it could no longer be seen. Only the kids in the playground saw the ufo. All of the adults where at the softball fields about 150 yards away. To this day my father still doesn't believe i saw anything, my father was at the softball fields watching my sisters game. No Adults have ever admitted to seeing anything.
