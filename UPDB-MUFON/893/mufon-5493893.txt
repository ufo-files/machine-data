{rjl} Boomerang Shaped UFO

I just got out of my car, and I saw the sky, and I observed a dark colored moving object in the sky. At first, I thought it was a bird, but it was not moving fast like a bird. I asked my husband what he thought it was. At first, he thought it was balloon. As it got closer over head, we realized that it was in the shape of a boomerang or a pair of pants, and reflected lights. And because of its erratic course of travel, sometimes hovering, we knew it was something very unusual. It went far out into the sky and disappeared into the high clouds.
