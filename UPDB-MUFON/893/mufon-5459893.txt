{tjd} Spot in the sky flashed total of 4 times and what was thought to be a star shot into the light and disappeared

Driving home from work I stopped at a stop sign and off in the distance above a bunch of trees was a round spot in the sky that kept flashing an off white almost yellow light. it wasn't super bright but it slowly flashed three times in 2 second intervals. After the 2nd flash I got out of the car to watch. There was what appeared to be a star very close by to the flashing light. The round spot flashed for a fourth time and then the star moved into the light in a quick straight line and disappeared along with the light.
I sat on the road for 10 minutes waiting for that spot to flash again or the "star" to come back....it did not. 
I was experiencing lower left abdominal pain sporadically throughout the day today and my pain is now constant and more intense than before.
