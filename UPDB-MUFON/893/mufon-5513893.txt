{cak} Saw the craft over the edge of town (Annville), KY in 1989

Just before dark had stopped to use pay phone and while setting in car noticed the craft. The craft was moving extremely slow. No sounds no blinking lights and lights of town seemed to reflect off it. It was a bronze to gold color with exterior protrusons. I new this was no plane because it was so low. The object proceded on a straight line and was still visible when i decided to go home to get a camera, when i got back it was gone. I live about five miles from the sighting. Was some what excited at first but had seen many others so just watched.
