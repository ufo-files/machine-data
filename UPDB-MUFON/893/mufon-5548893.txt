a saucer-shaped object with white lights on base

I was at the Orange County fair on 7/21 and noticed a group of people looking at a strange object in the distant western sky at around 10:30 p.m. The object would hover and dart in no apparent pattern. It moved very quickly. It had bright lights on its base. The lights formed a circle. There were approximately twelve such lights. My feelings were that it was a strange sight and I confirmed those feelings with the handful of strangers that were observing the event with me. Eventually, the craft descended low on the horizon and disappeard. It seemed to be heading westward over the ocean. I have two very poor-quality pictures that I would be glad to append.
