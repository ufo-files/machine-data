{rjl} Cotton ball like object and cone shape shot out of it.

I stepped outside with my dog,then, I saw what was like a small cotton ball and there wasn't a cloud to be seen. As it caught my eye a passenger jet flew between my position and the object. It was stationary for a moment then moved a little then stopped. Then the object shot out a cone shape with some blue sky between the cone. Both the object and shape were white in color. Seconds after the cone shape shot out the cotton ball object seemed to implode in itself and disappeared. The event lasted a minute or so. I couldn't believe my eyes and have no idea what it could have been.
