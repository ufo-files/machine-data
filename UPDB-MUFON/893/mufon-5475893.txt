{cak} Seen 1 pulsating light in front, 2 next to each other in the middle and 1 in the rear. Then they faded away

I was sitting in my yard with my mom and happened to look up and seen 1 orangish pulsating light in the front, then two more side by side behind the first one and then one more in the rear. Kinda like a diamond shape. Watched them for a few minutes heading NE till they just disappeared. It was a clear night so I'm pretty sure they didn't go behind a cloud, they were just gone. The two in the middle were close to each other and there was no sound. I will swear to this till the day I die and wish I could take a lie detector test to prove it.
