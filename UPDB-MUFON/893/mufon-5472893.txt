{cak} unbelievable speed. Chevron, two rows of 6-8 lights each rapidly vibrating

Went outside at 8:45 to have a cigarette, have been seeing a lot of shooting stars so was looking skyward. The object caught my eye coming from NNE at what looked to be 1-2 miles high. The object was an obtuse chevron shape, approx. 160 degrees in shape. each of the four wings had 3 lights. The lights were not flashing but vibrating, making it appear fuzzy, almost out of focus. It was moving at an incredible speed,I witnessed it over approximately 90 degrees from my perspective and if my distance is accurate I calculate the object was moving at an estimated speed of 6000 mph. I estimate the object was 3 miles away when I first saw it, it flew nearly directly over head from NNE to SSE and I saw it for about 2-3 seconds. I see planes, helicopters and drones regularly and this was absolutely not one of those. It was not a meteorological event. The conditions are clear with unlimited visibility. I write this about 30 minutes after the event, I stayed out watching for a while. I've been a skeptic of earth being visited until tonight.
