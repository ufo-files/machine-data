{tjd} Looked like 15+ stars moving as a group

I thought I was dizzy at first because so many of the stars were moving. After bracing myself against a post I realized that I was witnessing a large group of, "satellites" moving in a loose formation. They were traveling from West to East and very near the North Star. Some of the ones at the back of the group seemed to alter their speed, faster and slowing, but the whole group generally moved together. They reminded me of leaves floating on a creek.
