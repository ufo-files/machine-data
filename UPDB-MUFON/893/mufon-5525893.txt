Bright white hovering light

We were having a fire in the backyard and sky watching, to the west we seen a bright white light coming towards us, I started to walk closer to get a better look and it was not a plane, or a satelite it was flying very low and it had no sound. I stood watching it for a few minutes with my daughter then it blinked twice, and it was like it shut its light off and we just couldn't see it, hard to explain, but its not like it flew off and just faded away, instead just shut its light off.
