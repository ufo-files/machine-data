UAP sighted above Gates of the Mountains Wilderness near Refrigerator Canyon

Strobing lights appeared in the sky on what appeared to be a large single aircraft. The lights ran pulsating forward and back. Additional, smaller strobing aircraft appeared and seemed to fly in semi-formation around the larger aircraft, some flying in opposite directions. 
The aircraft moved from W to E across the sky fairly slowly for its altitude and size until it disappeared from sight. 

Everyone is telling me that this is the SpaceX Starlink satellite formation, but didn't quite seem the same. Also, some are saying it may be the space station visible from Montana right now, but I believe this should have been a single, unblinking light. 

Two different angles were recorded on iPhones. I can provide a video from the next morning in the campground to put the shot into perspective.
