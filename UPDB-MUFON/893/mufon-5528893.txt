very big red flaming ball with a long tail

I was sitting in my hot tub with my husband facing north west when out of the corner of my eye to the right a large flaming ball with a long tail moving really fast, shot in front of us. It was a lot faster than a airplane, because we used their speed to compare. There were at least two planes in the area, because we have BWI nearby. It shot very straight towards the northwest. It was 20 times bigger than any star in the sky. Really amazing, not a shooting star.
