Huge V shaped, with red flashing lights.

Around 6:30 pm, on Friday, November 14th, 2008, my husband and I were having dinner out on our deck in Calabasas,Ca which overlooks the San Fernando Valley. We observed a very large, silent, V shaped craft that had red flashing lights around its perimeter. It was moving South to North. Since we live in a home which has a hilltop view, we are accustomed to watching various aircraft nightly. This was nothing like we have seen before. We called our 13 yr old son, and his friend, and together, we watched the craft as it flew over the valley.
