Two-Gray/silver and white objects- Aspirin size.

At exactly 11:40 A.M. Central Time January 26, I was at my sons house, seated at the kitchen table. The table is located next to a large picture window. I turned my head to the right to look out the window, and as my gaze looked up, I saw two gray/silver and white objects rapidly circling in tight formation, with one following the other. They were circling in an odd sort of oval path and continued circling for twenty to thirty seconds. Each had a bright non-blinking white light. One object headed west, then ten seconds or so after, the other followed and also headed west. They were traveling what I estimate was two times the normal cruising speed of a commercial jet. As the second object headed west, I noticed a twinkle of light below it, so it is possible there was a third object. (The sky was a clear blue, making for a good background to see the objects.) They were aspirin size in the sky, making it hard to determine exactly how high up or how big they were. My best guess is they were less than10,000 feet in altitude.
