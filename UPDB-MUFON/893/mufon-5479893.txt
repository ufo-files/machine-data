{dr}Helicopter flying northbound directly overhead along New York State Thruway trailing a small red steady burning red light up ahead flying in perfect formation to the front left.

On the evening of November 3rd, 2016, my wife and I were playing with our infant daughter on the living room floor when I heard a loud helicopter approaching outside. As I got up to observe the helicopter from our rear deck, I noticed that it was low flying (approximately 500 feet), and flying North along Interstate 87. I then noticed a small steady burning red light far out ahead and to the left of it. Both continued on in perfect formation North bound until they were out of sight. I was unable to hear anything but the helicopter, or make out the shape of the object with the red light being trailed. My wife also witnessed the incident when I called her to come see what I was observing.
