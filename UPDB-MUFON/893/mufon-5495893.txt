{rjl} Driving home New Years Eve shortly after midnight fireworks were heard but this sighting was unrelated to fireworks. Saw 2 red lights moving together in the midnight sky & approached 1 white lit object.

I was driving home New Years Eve 1-1-15 shortly after midnight. I heard and saw fireworks that were in a totally different direction and neighborhood as this sighting. As I turned left onto my street I saw red lights in the sky above moving from right to left across the street in a linear path. It struck me odd as they seemed round and glowed red and following a path in the sky but definitely not a plane or helicopter. They seemed to move much slower than an airplane. As I drove towards the end of my street to my home I saw more red lights above my house moving across the sky - there were 2 red lights/objects that seemed connected to each other as they moved simultaneously. I took out my iphone when I got out of my car and recorded the objects as they approached another white object in the sky above me. I got scared and turned off the video and ran into the house. You can hear the fireworks in the background but they were not coming from those objects as the fireworks were coming from a few streets over and I could no longer see those. You can also hear my dogs barking as they were in the house and heard the fireworks and also heard me pull up in my car. I am not sure what I saw above my house (on the video) were the same red lights/objects I saw when I first turned onto my street as I lost sight of those and they seemed to be heading in a different direction - they were moving across the sky above my street so it didn't make sense to me they would suddenly be at the end of my street above my house where I recorded them after getting out of the car. They were definitely the same color and type but I'm not sure if there were the same lights/objects I first saw turning onto my street.
