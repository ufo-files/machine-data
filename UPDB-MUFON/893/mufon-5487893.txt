{rjl} 7 orange light moving together the flickering out

I had heard a story from a friend of mine a year earlier about a street light like light in the sky at the end of his street when he was coming home from a New Years party (12/31/12) He told me about how slowly it moved through the sky, stopped at the end of his dead end street and flicker off. 

  I told him it was a flare or something and he had too much too drink that night. He swore otherwise but I didn't buy it. 

 Then next year (12/15/13) I was carring boxes to the Uhaul while moving out of my house I noticed a street light like light in the sky slowly moving from NE to SE. I couldn't believe my eyes, this was exactly what my friend had described. Then before I could get my phone out one by one six more appeared behind the first all moving in unison across the sky. I immediately called my friend that had told me about these very lights and told him "Go outside!!!" 

He asked "why?..." as he was walking out his back door (Which was only a few streets down in the same neighborhood I lived in) before I could answer back he said "Oh my god, that's what I was telling you about!!" 

"I know! Keep looking, there are seven of them!!"

 We watched all of the yellow orange orbs move together, in no particular shape, but all at the same speed and direction from NE to SE. They were in the sky for what felt like 20min but was more like 3-5min. Then one by one they flickered out. Not in any order, but randomly. 

 Now somewhere about 9/20/15-9/25/15 I was in my hot tub with some friends enjoying the night and a few glasses of wine, when I saw three rectangular orange yellow lights in a row very close together, moving in a cascading pattern, almost like a sea snake in the air. It was very low to the ground, it appeared to be only 50-60 feet high and following directly above a major road. It moved slow enough I could point it out to the others and we all had a chance to take a guess on what it was before it disappeared behind the trees. This wasn't the same shape as the lights I had seen in 2013, but it was the exact same color.
