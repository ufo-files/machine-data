{nd} at outside hakespear in the park

Me and my family was at an outside city park event- "Shakespeare in the Park" in the summer of 1994 and I was lying on my stomach watching the production and my arms got tired so I rolled over and onto my back and my eyes were directed upward and I saw a green neon triangle that appeared to be very high in the atmosphere- no cloud cover- dark night- but very clearly visible and size of quarter- I observed for few seconds then elbowed my 7 year old son- and pointed for him to look up- he craned his neck around then flipped over on his back and was able to observe with me for a second or two before object darted off in SW direction at incomprehensible speed. we didn't speak of it for over a decade- then one day I asked him if he remembered and he did and I asked him what he saw and he described exactly what I remembered- -then about another decade later I did the same thing and his response was the same, the second time he was around 27 yrs. old. It was very bright glowing green neon and was still when I first observed it and stayed that way for about 6-7 seconds total before it too off to southwest at a level of acceleration that is still not explicable based on what the normal person knows exists. It eventually sparked an interest in the subject for me...my son doesn't like to remember/think or talk about it. I have mentioned it only a few times during the past two decades...but a few years ago I read something about triangle green neon UFOs in Indian during that time period and I was so blown away- because at the time I had never heard anyone describe a triangle-shaped or neon- much less green neon triangle-shaped UFO- and wished I had reported it earlier. There is no reason for me to draw it- it was just as stated.
