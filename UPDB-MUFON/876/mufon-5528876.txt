Saw an object making very slow vertical decent/ nose dive had a trail much wider than any plane in cylinder shape lots of planes flying around

I was traveling South bound on the Garden State Parkway (GSP) at approximately 3:30 pm on Friday November 19, 2010, when I observed what appeared to be an extremely wide jet stream making a very slow vertical decent. My first observation was in the area of exit 145/ I-280 (East Orange, NJ). The jet stream was extremely wide, wider than any plane I've ever observed and it started out at the same width as it remained in the trail, unlike a plane where it starts thin and then widens. I watched this and noticed many planes, about a half dozen jets flying around the area in addition to the typical Newark airport traffic where I observed the usual passenger planes in the usual decent patterns. As I continued South bound on the GSP around exit 138 (Roselle Park, NJ 07204) the object was directly observable in the windshield and traffic came to a sudden halt then picked up again in about 30-45 seconds after the roadway turned and the object was not observable. I exited the GSP at exit 137 (Cranford, NJ 07016) and turned right onto North Street (probably West bound) no longer able to observe the decent of whatever it was, I continued to observe abnormal numbers of jet planes, nearing a dozen, flying around the sky. At this point I saw what appeared to be reflecting light from a metal object, the reflection would be from bear metal, I was unable to observe the shape as the reflection obscured direct view. The reflecting light then moved horizontal at a slow pace and disappeared, I also observed a plane making a decent for Newark airport at the same time in reasonably close proximity, the movements were nothing alike, they were clearly very different objects. After the horizontal movement of the object it disappeared, then reappeared. I then made a left turn onto Centennial Ave (Cranford, NJ 07016) losing sight, went one block, made a right onto South Ave (Cranford, NJ 07016). On South Ave I again observed the object reflecting light and still lots of planes in the sky. At the Cranford train station I made a left and lost site of the object, went about 3 blocks and made a right onto Lincoln Ave (Cranford, NJ 07016) where again I observed the object roughly when crossing Walnut Street. I lost sight of the object after making a left onto Denman Ave. I thought I may have seen it again about 10 minutes later from my house, but it was through trees and the observation is questionable.
