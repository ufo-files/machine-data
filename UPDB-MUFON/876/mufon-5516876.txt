{nd} A circular cake pan shaped ufo with lights & hatch landed in a small field near me. The hatch opened, no one came out it shut and flew away.

I was near my mothers house next to a small field about to light a cigarette, when I saw a large ufo land in the field near the ocean and some trees. I was looking in that direction anyway. I thought it was a ufo because it didn't look like a human air vehicle. The object I saw was about 15 feet in diameter & about 5feet high. It flew in quickly and landed in the snow. I remember seeing windows or lighted windows on the ufo. There was a hatch, like a door. It opened & no one came out. It shut and the uof ascended into the sky over the water and went right down the York River. I was so amazed, just leaving Hawaii where I had studied Astronomy. There was no one around, so I didn't have anyone to tell at first, but I eventually told my mother I had seen a ufo. It was really close to me so I felt lucky to see it. This is the second time I was at my mothers house that I had seen a ufo, but she has difficulty believing me. The ufo disappeared behind the trees as it made its way down the York River.
