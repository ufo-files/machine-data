[flg] star like light moving quickly in a race track formation then stopped and disappeared

I was conducting a training exercise when i saw the object. I was laying down on the ground I noticed the stars were out very bright and then saw the moving object among the stars. It was witnessed as a star like object moving in a quick motion and a race track formation. After 2 track formation movements the light stopped and disappeared. Approximately 5 minutes later it appeared again and completed a race track formation again and disappeared more quickly that the first time. Total time was approximately 10 minutes for the event. It did not appear again during that evening. I was not scared but curious. I have since been staring at the sky waiting to see it again.
