{cak} Light looked like a star, moved across the sky, ascended abruptly & blinked out.

I was in my back yard in my hot tub star gazing. I watched planes fly over head both flights coming in to land at Mccarren & planes flying really high leaving vapor trails. This was at night.

I noticed what looked like a star coming towards me. I watched it for about 10 seconds.
It suddenly flew upwards in what looked like a 90 deg turn. Within seconds of doing that it faded out from what was star light bright to orange then black. It literally went out!

I thought to myself "no way what the heck was that?" 

The light had no other blinking navigation lights like the planes that were flying around.
I have since seen similar lights silently moving around up in the sky just like this one. I have tried to look at them with binoculars but see no detail. Could they be satellites?

I still cant explain how or why the light I saw would go up the way it did & flame out within seconds.

Staying vigilant...
