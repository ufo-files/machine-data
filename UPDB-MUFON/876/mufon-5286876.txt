Glowing white ball in sky hovering {moving up slightly, come down go back up}.- {djs}

Around 2 am on Wednesday June 23rd a Glowing white ball shaped object in a South East direction from our home was spotted. Paul first noticed it hovering just above a roof line to a house. This object was much bigger than any star in the sky. Then he noticed it moved up slightly. I, Shannon, came outside to observe as well. The object was hovering but you could see it move slightly up and down or slightly to the south. It slowly did this for 30 plus minutes. We observed the object come down some and then go back up and hover. When it moved slightly you could see a trail behind it. The object was a glowing bright white with a glow around it. We looked to the North around 2:45 am Wednesday June 23rd and said we wondered if anything else was there. Just then we both witnessed a smaller white object coming from the east and heading north. As fast as it appeared and we both acknowledged the object, it disappeared. When we looked back at the larger object to the south east, it had moved up high and more to the south.
