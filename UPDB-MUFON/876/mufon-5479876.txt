{dr}Came from inside a residential home

I was letting my dog out and he wouldn't go outside so I looked out my back door and the neighbors house that was across the alley was emitting a extremely bright glow from inside the house it was so bright I had to squint and the noise was a cross between amplified broken words and screaming and high piercing whining as well as echoes. Shortly after about 1 week they moved out of the house. I've worked as a 911 dispatcher for 9 yrs and I have never seen anything like this before.
