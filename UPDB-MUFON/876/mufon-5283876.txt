Line of lights resembling Brail pattern

At approximately 6:40 PM on three December 2021 myself and to others were sitting in a hot tub in Brighton Colorado when we noticed a string of lights that were moving from south to north and the best way to describe it is they looked like braille. There were applied approximately 30 to 40 lights in a exact straight line. We followed the lights for approximately 30 seconds before they faded and went away. We first started it was a contrail from an airplane. The lights appear to be from the distance in the air to be approximately 1000 yards in length. Some media reports claimed that they are star link satellites In St. Louis Missouri.that were in line moving south to north but I call BS . it’s funny how these lights were seen in St. Louis Missouri as well as a suburb of Denver Colorado at approximately the same time.
