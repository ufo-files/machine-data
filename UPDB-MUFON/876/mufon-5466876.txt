Aliens running from museum

1.on my way back to my Airbnb in Kyoto japan. Walking through courtyard of shrine before neighborhood entrance. 20-30 minutes before midnight.
2.as I entered courtyard two 3-4 foot tall human looking creatures had jumped the wall of shrine and were running away.. they were about 30-50 feet ahead of my path. 
3. At first I though they were children but they were too small to be kids of any age. There bodies were proportional for their height. 
4. Creatures were about 3-4 feet tall. As white as snow, pointed like ears, arms and legs were skinny. Ran with arms behind them. Big black triangle eyes. Ran behind a corner of wall. As I walked towards the corner the had turned one of them looked back towards me and we made eye contact. Alarm of shrine was tripped, but no sound was going off. I was feeling terrified by what I saw. 
5. Terrorified, like I didn1t want to get to close to them. I had a shaking feeling/ sensation as I got a little closer before they ran off. 
6. I lost sight of them as they turned a corner. Probably jumped the wall of shrine as well.
