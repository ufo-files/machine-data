{cak} UFO HOVERED OVER MY WIFE AND I

IN 2008 MY WIFE AND I WERE WALKING THE BEACH IN PER DIDO KEY, FLORIDA. I NOTICED SOMETHING STRANGE. WHICH WAS A BLACK CLOUD OVER THE GULF OF MEXICO. THIS PARTICULAR CLOUD STOOD OUT BECAUSE ALL THE OTHER CLOUDS WERE WHITE OR NATURAL IN COLOR. THE BLACK CLOUD BEGAN MOVING TOWARDS MY WIFE AND I. I WAS NOT AWARE THAT MY WIFE WERE PAYING ATTENTION OR NOT. NOW THIS CLOUD WAS BLACK IN COLOR, A BLACK I'VE NEVER SEEN OR WITNESSED BEFORE. THIS IS WHAT MADE IT SO STRANGE. THE CLOUD HEADED STRAIGHT FOR US. WHEN IT REACHED US IT HOVERED ABOUT ONE HUNDRED TWENTY FIVE OR FIFTY FEET ABOVE AND IN FRONT OF US. THERE WAS NO SOUND TO IT AT ALL. THEN THERE WAS A FLASH OF LIGHT THAT LIT UP THE CLOUD. WHAT I SAW NEXT ASTOUNDED ME BEYOND BELIEF. THERE WAS A CRAFT WITHIN THE CLOUD WITH WINDOWS AND A BEING WAS LOOKING RIGHT AT ME AND MY WIFE. I TOLD MY WIFE THAT WE MUST GO BACK TO THE HOUSE. AS WE RUSHED AWAY. THE BLACK CLOUD WAS STILL THERE, THEN IT LEFT ON THE SAME PATH IT CAME. IT WENT BACK OUT IN THE GULF OF MEXICO AND VANISHED. I WASN'T SURE THAT MY WIFE HAD PAYED ATTENTION TO THIS WHOLE EVENT UNTIL SHE APPROACHED ME AND SAID, DID YOU SEE THAT AND I SAID WHAT? SHE SAID THAT CLOUD AND THAT THING IN IT. SHE SAID TO ME, WHAT WAS IT? AND I SAID I THINK WE SAW A UFO. I SHALL NEVER FORGET IT.
