{jpg} Around 9pm I noticed a bright blinking red stationary light in the sky between two buildings. It didn't move for awhile but did notice it would blink on and off between red and green. I used my kodak camera to take a video.

I was in the living room talking to my daughter when I noticed a red blinking stationary light in between two building and I interrupted my daughter to ask her to look at the light. We both wondered what it could be. I told her maybe it could be a satellite, a drone or helicopter? One of the buildings has a clock, so I noticed that the time was around 9pm when we first saw it. Then we noticed that it alternated blinking a green light. It did move slowly at times and once I thought it seemed to disappear and show up in another area? It was hard for me to hold the camera so the video might be kind of shaky in certain places. I had to turn it off and then rest my arm and then I would turn it on to record again. It lasted for about 45 minutes. A couple of times it would go behind the building and I thought it was gone but then in a few minutes it was back again. I have three videos that show this blinking light. This It seemed to stay within that area between the two buildings. The last time I saw it was when it disappeared behind one of the buildings. I always waited to see if I could see a path of the light traveling away from that building, but I never did. It just seemed to stay behind that one building and reappear between my viewing time of 9pm and 9:45pm. I stayed up till about 11:45pm but did not see it again.
