5 white lights appeared inside of a strip of Aurora Borealis. 10 seconds. April, 2019

On April 19th, 2019 I was standing in my backyard about to have a smoke. I as looking up at a clear night sky and I as admiring a faint strip of northern lights stretching across the sky. In the city this is very faint and you cant make out the clear definition of the "curtain" like appearance but it was there to my east and it stretched approximately north to south across the entire sky. 
I was suddenly confronted by the appearance of what looked initially like a shooting star inside of the strip of Aurora Borealis in the east sky. It appeared to come into the sky at a fast rate and then immediately slow down. at the same time as the slow down, 4 other lights of equal, unwavering intensity appeared in-line with the original. They did not blink or alter colour. They proceeded across inside of the Aurora, heading north. 
After about 5-10 seconds they vanished in the same fashion as a shooting star would. no speed change, no course change, no sound or colour. just five white lights disappearing. 

I almost forgot to smoke.
