We had just parked our car and we're walking through the parking lot towards the restaurant when all the power went out on the whole block. For some reason I turned around and looked up toward the sky when I saw a bright light shooting across the sky at an unusually high rate of speed. At first, I thought it was a jet of some kind, but then the object split into two smaller objects. The smaller objects continued to move across the sky in an upward direction before vanishing. I remember how eerily quite everything was. There was no engine noise as you would expect to hear from an aircraft, not even when the object split into two smaller objects, just silence. When I first noticed the object, I pointed towards it and instinctively yelled, "Look!" At which time, everyone in my group and in the parking lot with us turned around and looked up at the light. My whole family witnessed this event and remembers, in detail, what they saw; what we all saw.
