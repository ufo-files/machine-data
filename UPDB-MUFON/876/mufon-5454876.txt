{tjd} Lights moving in a straight line, western sky

First I saw a yellow-orange light in the western sky, then, maybe three minutes later I noticed just north of the first light smaller white lights moving together in a straight line perfectly spaced apart moving at the same speed, at first I thought of the phoenix lights, but there were many of them, they seemed to appear just north of the yellow-orange light then disappeared north of their path. They traveled slowly and shorty after the larger yellow orange light disappeared the smaller lights stopped as well. The event lasted maybe 15 minutes. Too high up to film on my phone.
