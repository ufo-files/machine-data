{nd} Two sightings in two nights

On June/23/2012 at about 10pm, I was standing outside of my house and saw what at first looked like a bright big star. As I was looking at it, a blue light about half the size of the main white light appeared then circled the light once clockwise then stopped and both lights disappeared. The next night on June/24/2012, my brother and I were in my backyard he saw a solid white light flying fairly quickly and pointed it out to me. We watched it fly for about 10 seconds then it disappeared. There were commercial planes flying lower than the light much slower than the light. It was a solid white light. It didn't have any blinking lights like airplanes and as fast as it was going, it wasnt like a shooting star. It didn't look like fire and it didn't have a trail behind it. Ive seen satalites before and it didn't look like any Ive seen. Im going to be out tonight with a video camera in case it happens again.
