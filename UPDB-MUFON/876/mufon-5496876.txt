{nd} driving with roomate on i40 saw very long row of bright lights hovering low over interstate.

My roommate and I were driving back from WV to Wilmington, we had just got on i40 at Winston Salem a little earlier and were around exit 105 heading east. I remember seeing the high point airport sign, looking up and see a very long row of very bright lights hovering over the interstate, slowly moving, for a second we thought it was a plane, no, too slow and planes look nothing like that, as we got closer we thought, maybe a helicopter, then again very quickly ruled that out (I was in Air Traffic Control School in 2013, I know that no plane or helicopter moved like that, looked like that, or would be over the interstate that low)As we continued to drive we kept staring at it and it continued to hover over the interstate slowly moving to the other side, by the time we got near it, it was already on the other side and descending slowly almost like it was landing somewhere over there, however we went under a bridge and lost sight of it because it was extremely low and behind us. A specific detail I do recall is that as I was staring at it, trying to distinguish it as an airplane or helicopter I realized the row of lights were so bright, it seemed like that was all that it was, was lights until I noticed a very deep dark black almost black haze as the color of whatever was emitting the lights. This was also seen by my roommates and I must add, neither of us drink or do drugs so this was not a hallucination, I have a lot of knowledge about most all aircrafts and drones and I can firmly say that this was not anything like that to my knowledge. This incident lasted maybe 2 minutes maximum and I remember being mesmerized by it, feeling like oh my god, does everyone see this? Can it really be a UFO just completely plain in the open, I remember feeling compelled to report this because I wanted so badly to know what it was, I so badly wanted to turn my car around and find it but didn't know what exit to take because I had already passed it. We can NOT have been the only ones to see this, this was clearly right in plain view on a clear, windy, freezing cold night. I wanted to take pictues but I was driving and by the time it occurred to film it, it had passed because I was so in awe and confused as to what was happening.
