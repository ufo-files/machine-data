Metallic objects in sky. Sun was reflected on objects.

Laying in new hammock in the backyard. Clear skies and very sunny.  The crescent of the moon was visible.  At first saw a shiny object thinking it was an airplane.  Saw a second one as well. Realized they weren’t a plane when one came into view.  The sun was reflecting on the objects. It was as if they were blinking or moving catching the suns rays. One of the objects disappeared.  My husband saw the one object.  I had my phone with me and took pictures as I was still in the hammock.  I just reviewed the pictures. Although you could not see the object looking at the pictures... once I zoomed in you can clearly see the object.
