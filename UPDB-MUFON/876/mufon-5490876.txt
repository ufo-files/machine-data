I was taking pictures of 5 attack helicopters that were flying north through the rural valley I live in. They changed direction and returned the way they came in. A few minutes later, they came back to the north, this time passing directly over my house. I managed to get a few more pictures with at least 2 frames containing the UFOs (20150630_154708 & 20150630_154718). I have included the raw pictures with the UFOs with the preceding and following frames along with highlighted copies and a rough overlay of 08 & 18 to compensate for the 10 foot difference in camera position between the two. More pictures are in the series but I haven't been able to see anything in the rest. The file names are the date_time stamp.
