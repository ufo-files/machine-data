This unidentified light was photographed at Caspersen Beach in Venice, FL on February 17, 2021 at 4:32 pm. At the time of the picture, the object was not observed. It was only when reviewing the pictures later that night that Jamie noticed the object. Some aspects we found puzzling were the dozen or so white dots of light contained within the aqua circle. There also seems to be a dark haze around the edges of the object. The cloud pattern We cannot provide any more info than the attached pictures as we did not observe it until much later. The picts were taken with an Iphone 8+. The picts were all taken from the same seated position and within seconds of each other. Only the angle of the camera to the horizon changed. The pict titled aqua circle 4 is my attempt at enlarging the image to highlight the white "lights" within the aqua circle.
