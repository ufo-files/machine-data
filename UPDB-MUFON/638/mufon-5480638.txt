{rjl} Gulf Breeze/Navarre stationairy object 2 minutes. Discovered in photos after occurrence

On the evening of September 9, 2016, I decided to go to Opal Beach between Pensacola Beach Bean Florida and Navarre Florida (near Gulf Breeze)to watch the sunset and take pictures for an art project. Several days later, when looking at the pictures I had taken, I noticed a dark stationary object in several of the photos. The object was in a single position from 7:02- 7:03 pm in a southerly direction- at a height that it cast a shadow on the cloud directly behind it. There was nothing in the photos at the spot 2 minutes prior to the even and no object one minute after the event. I know it was stationary because my IPhone SE takes short videos when the photo is taken. There are small clouds in the photo which remain in the sky throughout the event. This is the only object which appeared for 2 minutes and then was gone. It could have been an unusual momentary cloud, but there is no indication of formation and dissipation as a cloud would require. Moreover, the final picture does appear to show a dark disk-shaped object with what appears to be a pointed antenna-type object on its top portion and an apparent diamond-shaped field around the craft. It had some solidity as the object appeared to cast a shadow on the cloud behind it. I have 8 photos of the object, showing various shapes/forms, with the final photo being most distinctive. I also have photos of the area minutes before the event and minutes after with the object no longer there- which seems to me to indicate it was NOT a cloud. I am attaching the last (best) photo first. The last photo attached shows the sky one minute after the event, with no indication of the object. I have not included enhanced photos, for proper photo analysis by experts.
