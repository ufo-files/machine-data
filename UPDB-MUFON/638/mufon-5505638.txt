{rjl} Black triangle shaped ufo

I was 15 years old and out on our back porch feeding our cat and when I looked up to the sky I saw a huge triangle shaped ufo hovering above the tree, it made no sound, so I ran back in the house to get my parents and my siblings heard what was going on so they came also. We went back out to the deck and it was still there, so we were looking at it for a couple of minutes and then it began to move so my dad told all of us to get back in the house, so we did and I remember being in the kitchen and looking out the door window and seeing it coming towards the house, then it went right over our house so we then ran to the living room window to see if we could see it, it went across the street to the other house moving very slowly. Then was hovering there for a few minutes, then moved again very slowly until we could not see it anymore. I wish I could see it again.
