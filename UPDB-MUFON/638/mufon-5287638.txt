40-50 flying in similar path

I saw what looked like stars materializing in the west around 9:38pm 5/22. They were flying towards Las Vegas but tree line and homes blocked the final direction. I ran to the backyard to record in darkness as light from the upstairs would not allow the focus. I only got about 5 min of video but only :42 seconds seems good. 

I’ve read about the starlink satellites but the objects I saw flew much more of a military precision. Making me think they were space force. They seemed to drop in from space materializing from nothing to flying in twos then equalizing there distance. The time of night would make it near impossible for Sun reflection unless they were really high, but the viewing angle seems to lend objection to the elevation. 

I missed 20 easily, counted another 12 more before witnessing another 16-20 after that.
