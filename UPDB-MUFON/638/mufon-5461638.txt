{tjd} 3 Orbs in triangular formation

Witnessed 3 golden Orbs in Western sky hover in a
triangular formation, then they moved into a diagonal
line. Then one of the Orbs dimmed to a small red dot
that would blink red as it flew off to the south west.
The second Orb did exactly the same as the first one.
The third Orb did the same as the first two, and in the
same interval.
