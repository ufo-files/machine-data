{cak} Picture taken in Grand Teton Mountains.

Our family was on the way to Colter Bay RV park. My nephew was taking pictures of the beautiful Grand Teton Mountains. He noticed object on camera screen. He showed me the camera and I instructed him to continue taking pictures. We never heard or saw anything visual in the sky. All we had was the actual photograph of an object. On the way home, we stopeed in Roswell. We showed it to the museum. They asked when it was taking and stated they had sightings at that same time. They gave us Mufon in San Antonio paperwork. Time passed and we did not think much of the incident.
