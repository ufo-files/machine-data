ufo seen over 2 week period and on 10/13/2010 multiple ufos

ufo was a bright light, I couldn't make out any shape but looking at it with binoculars it was perfectly round and looked dim. The ufo started out being bright and over about 30 seconds it faded and stayed that way. It was visible all night for most of the days and on the days when I would see it leave it would fade to black without moving erraticly. I took me several days before I decided they couldn't be man made crafts. Two times on 10/13/2010 I tried filming the closest ufo but as soon as I would turn on the camera it would stop moving and just looked like a really close bright light so I didn't bother posting it on here.
