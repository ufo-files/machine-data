{ck} Driving down hwy object passed on horizon across the sky in front of us

Trangle shaped object with 3 bright lights, crossed over the hwy in front of us driving thru the arizona desert. My husband at the time and I drove team together. We saw it and I said, is that a plane..No it was no plane. By the time I got out of my seat to get the video camera it was gone. Ive seen many strange things in the sky in my travels. I will keep a better record as to where it took place. Many other truck drivers that night saw the same thing, they were all talking about it on the cb.
