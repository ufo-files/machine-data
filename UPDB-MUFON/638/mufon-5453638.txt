Looked like stars until they moved in a straight line

I saw over 10 UFO's that appeared out of nowhere and looked like stars to the average person and then you saw them move in a straight line with almost even distance apart travelling from the east to the west. As I was driving through a subdivision, I did not see where they went of if in fact they disappeared - I was just fascinated that there were so many travelling in a straight line at the same speed. One did a slight squiggle, but it looked like they were following each other going to somewhere in particular. 
They would be too far away to film as they were like small dots in the sky.
