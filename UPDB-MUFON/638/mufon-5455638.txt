{rjl} White glowing object with random red light going across bottom

3/9/19 Petaluma, California at around 11pm 
Driving home from the store and noticed this bright bar of light in the sky. I wish we would of stopped and got better photos 😐
This was blocks from my house and we were on Caulfield over pass headed home when I took this shot. Some neighbors said they saw it from underneath for about 15 seconds and then flash it was gone.
The thing was huge in the sky, the picture doesn’t do it justice.
I figured I would share it anyways.
It was reported by other people that night as well.
