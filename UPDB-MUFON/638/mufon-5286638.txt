Several white balls of light at treetop level moving around while headed east

I let my dog outside and noticed something moving just above the trees to my west then saw 8 balls of white light come over the trees and light up the trees and back yard. They looked to be about 50 feet or so above the ground, then they moved slowly around each other and off to the east. I watched them for about two minutes. I was so shocked that I didn't think to run back in the house to get my phone to get pictures. There was no sound at all. I could not tell if they were craft or just lights. It looked otherworldly to me, not like anything normal. I was very close to these objects, and my dog even noticed them. She just sat there staring. I have no idea what these things were. My neighbor told me to call the ASD so I did.
