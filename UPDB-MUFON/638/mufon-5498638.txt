{nd} Bright Orb with second Orb leaving it

On October 9,2014, early Thursday evening around 7:08 pm. I witnessed a Bright Orb directly above where I was sitting on my porch. I had my camcorder with me and was able to video this Bright White Orb moving slowly towards Owens Mountain. I recorded the object until it went out. When I went inside to review the recording, I saw a second Orb leave the first Orb. That right there shocked me! What object does that, that I know of, none!
