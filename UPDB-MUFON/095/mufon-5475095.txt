I had just laid down on my bed (it was still somewhat light out, it was dusk) and reached up to turn on my radio while looking out my open window. Something straight above the house caught my eye. It was disk shaped, had no wings, no running lights and had a strobe type light on it's domed top. Just as I realized it wasn't a conventional aircraft it took off in a flash heading due east and was gone in a manner of seconds. I spent 4 years in the Navy and stood many a lookout watch night & day reporting anything moving in the sky. I know it was a solid object (gray in color) shaped like the classic "flying saucer"..inverted pie tin with a domed top. I have witnessed abnormal lights high in the daytime sky in this area in the past, once also observed by 4 neighbors. I know what I saw, it was real and either secret U.S. aircraft, or something not of this world.  That's my story.
