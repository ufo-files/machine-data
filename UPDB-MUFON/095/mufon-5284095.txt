I saw a chevron shaped craft with no lights. It had a shimmery mirage-like appearance.

My sighting occurred on Nov. 19, 2021...the night of a partial Lunar eclipse. I had stepped out on my back porch to get a better look at the eclipse (at approx. 3:40 am) The eclipse was nearing the peak of coverage. I started looking around at the brilliant stars. It was very clear and dark and the stars were bright. I noticed movement out of the corner of my eye to the East. I saw a narrow chevron shaped object moving toward the West. Almost in the direction of the eclipse. It moved at approx. the speed of a airliner. It had what seemed to be some very slight hesitation in the speed and flight trajectory. Maybe 5-10 k ft high I'm guessing. I watched it for approx. 10 seconds. It moved behind some trees and out of my sight. It's surface was shades of black and gray with no lights. I shimmered like a mirror- mirage. It seemed to be reflecting light from the ground. I couldn't see any sharp edges, but it appeared to be solid and huge. I couldn't tell if it made and sound as there was a train passing in the distance. I quickly came inside (my heart pounding!) My eyes will be on the skies from now on!!
