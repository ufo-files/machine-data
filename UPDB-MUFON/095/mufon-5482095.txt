{rjl} Ball of light came from the sky. Everything stopped. Hovered over me, then shifted and went back into the dark. The ball of light felt so close and as if it stayed looking at me .

I was in Gainesville, FL leaving a friends with my boyfriend. He was walking in front of me and I was lagging behind. It was a windy night because I turned my head to see the trees move. At that moment that I turned, everything stopped. The trees were still and this ball of light just came out of the night sky. The ball of light stayed looking at me, then made a motion to the right and went back into the night sky-like a door or something was there. Once it had left, the trees started to move and my boyfriend was already in the car. For me it felt like 20 seconds went by but in reality it was more since I asked my boyfriend. It left no residue and didn't have any sound. Those are the only details that I remember from that night, but I can never forget how still everything became-till this day, it haunts me. Whatever I witnessed that night made an impression on me because no matter what, I can never forget what i saw or how I felt. That ball of light felt so close that it seemed that if I were to reach out my hand I would have been able to touch it..but I couldn't move. My boyfriend didn't notice anything and said he was just in the car waiting for me and didn't think to look back. ...It was strange.
