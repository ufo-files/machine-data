{cak} A classic disc like craft hovered about12-15 feet directly over my neighbor's house.

I was with 2 of my friends. My girlfriend and I were sitting on the curb with our backs to the houses on my side of the street while our male friend bounced a basketball in the street. We first noticed the object after our friend froze mid-bounce. I knew immediately it was a UFO. It hovered over my neighbor's roof, about 12 to 15 feet above. It made no noise or wind. It was a classic disc shape with colored lights around the outside. I believe the body of the craft was metallic. We were terrified. Our male friend took off running down the street, while my girlfriend and I ran into my home, next door to where this craft was hovering. I never saw it again, at least that I can remember.
