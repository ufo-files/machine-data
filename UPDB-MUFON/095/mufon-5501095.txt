{cak} Lighted object over Chautauqua lake N.Y at 10 pm July 11 2014

We were on our boat on Chautauqua lake when 4 of us seen a very large stationary lighted object.We observed this for several minutes watching this object at times brighten and dim. The object then made a very quick movement and hovered again and then another object came out of it.The other object was the same reddish orange color.Both objects then just disappeared.
