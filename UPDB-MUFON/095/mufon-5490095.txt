{rjl} Blinking lights

Me and my family were enjoying a nice night out in Chicago on our way to Buckingham Fountain. As we were waiting to cross the street we noticed something right away in the sky over the Lake. I grabbed my phone and started filming. We were surprised as I've never seen anything like this in Chicago having lived here my entire life. I continued to film and wondered why no one else seemed to notice or be concerned about it. We had the air and water show a few days after this sighting and wondered if it could have been smaller planes "practicing"? We also noticed a few helicopters flying near but not close to the object(s). I've sent it to a few news stations but have not received any reply or information.
