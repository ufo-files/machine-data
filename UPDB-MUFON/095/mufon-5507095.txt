{nd} 2 orange objects with red tail

10/17/2013 9:08pm West Seattle, WA
If you are familiar with West Seattle, I was traveling north on 63rd Ave SW just before the intersection with SW Admiral way, if not enter lat and long in google maps 47.57549 -122.41335.

I saw two objects over head glowing orange with a red light on their rear. I pulled car over to shoulder, rolled down driver window and took video with phone. Far to big to be satellites, looked more like small prop planes (size and height wise) but didn't have the typical plane beacons. the objects themselves may have been diamond-ish shaped.

I observed them to travel from the north or northeast, but slowly arc to the west. Direction is a bit difficult to determine if you watch the video, my car was pointed directly north, I was pointed up about 45 degrees probably over my side mirror. I tracked them until they faded and you will see the street lamp that was directly across the street to the west.

At first thought they might be the "GRACE" satellites, but their orbit is North and South. Second if they were satellites, they should have gotten brighter heading west not fade. Finally if you watch the video I uploaded, they fade at the same time, if satellites, I would expect one to fade first and the second to fade at the point where the first faded.

(sidenote, if you watch video, kill sound... had stereo on, and made a slight rude exclaimation)
