{rjl} Large red coloured H shaped too far away to see any screens or windows

I was travelling from Maimi Airport on a flight to Londons Heathrow, we had just ended a cruise and were travelling home, the light as we climbed above the clouds was very pretty and an unusaul cloud formation caught my eye. While looking at the clouds I noticed off to my left and a fair didtance below us an H shaped object which was stationary at the edge of the clouds. As I watched it slowly moved backwards as if retracting into the cover of the clouds.
The structure seemed to be tubular but in a H shape as if the H were lying on its back. Although the sighting was over very quickly I was taken aback, I knew it could not have been a plane and the shape did not look like any plane or helecopter I had ever seen. When I told my wife she thought I was kidding, and even now I dont think she believes me. I took some photos of the clouds that had vaught my eye in the first place on my wifes phone, unfortunately it was a case of closing the barn door after the horse had bolted as it is not on any of them. I watch then for as long as I could to see if it would reappear. Sadly it did not.
If required I can submit the few shots I took of the cloud formations, although there are no signs of the object/craft on any that I can see.
