Bright flickering lights that change shape and color

We see this thing a lot. It sparkles intensity and changes shape and color often. Often there is another one east of us but this one was west. It usually comes out around 12 and stays in roughly the same spot for hours. Last night it stayed for 1.5 hours then moved east very slowly. We bought a telescope so we could get a better view. Last night was the first clear night with the telescope. You’ll see in the two pictures the shapes and colors are very different. In the two attached photos select the pic then enlarge the tiny dot. Then you’ll see the shape and color.
