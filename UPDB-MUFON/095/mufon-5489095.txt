{cak} saw orange- yellow oval shaped light in sky heading north from the south

I was on my way to work at 9:50 pm maybe 50 feet from my house when i took a moment to stargaze something i do every night. as i looked towards the western skyline my vision instantly focused on the object. i stood amazed by the sight as i held back the urge to run inside with the intent to scream out loud that a u.f.o. was passing by in the sky over the next town over. Instead i made a visual scan of the skyline and as luck would have it a commercial airliner was also flying nearby thus allowing me to get a size estimate. the object appeared to be half the size of the airliner despite appearing to be farther away. the object was an oval shaped light that slowly faded in and out of view as if a child was playing with a dimmer switch. the light also seemed to flicker after a couple of pulses. this pattern never changed. The airliner was heading toward the south east likely on its way to one of the airports out near Boston can't say for certain.what i can say with certainty is that the object was traveling no less than twice the speed of the airliner as it traversed the sky. this was truly a life changing event as many members of my family have had encounters as well. i was excited to be able to add my name the list of family members with encounter tales.lost sight of the object as it started getting too far away to tell it apart from the light pollution coming from the city of Pittsfield.
