Large shear triangle object observed due south while out in garden, not an aircraft.

While shutting up my chickens in large south facing garden during darkness I felt static electricity and a very feint static noise. Looking up I observed a large full triangle object, around 50ft or more in size moving from a SE to SW direction, bloating out stars and some clouds. After around 2 seconds the object remained stationary for some seconds. Then immediately changed direction, not a slow progressive change but a right angle 90 degree then moved due south and then stopped after a few seconds. Object appeared (even though it was dark) to have a grey finish (seemed to reflect ground based lights etc) with white/blue haze on the leading edges, but no normal anti collision lights that I could see. Finally after a few more seconds the object ascended above cloud level (sat 300-500ft) and at the same time any lights on the object went out, however I could just make out it ascending cloud level as there was some moonlight visible. Static was still present for around 1 -2 mins. Bad headache and the chickens started making noise in there houses, which is unusual at night. Not sure what it was however there are some US air bases in the region, maybe black project, but not sure of course.

For sure this was not a helicopter, drone, UAV or ballon. What I witnessed was beyond my comprehension.
