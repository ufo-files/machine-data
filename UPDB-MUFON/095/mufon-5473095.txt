{cak} Witnessed a descending ball of light dart from the sky towards the ground, possibly off into the horizon.

We were in the car driving north around 9:15pm. It was totally dark. We noticed the light when it started to zoom downwards/towards the horizon. We noticed the light because it was so much brighter than the other lights around it such as street lamps or car headlights. It seemed to move extremely fast and we only saw it for a second. It moved much faster than a plane or helicopter when you see them flying in the sky. We were excited to see something like this, as it is different than anything we have ever seen before. We lost sight of the object when it seemingly zoomed off into thin air.
