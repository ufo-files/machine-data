My wife and I woke up from what we thought was hammering on our front wall and I got up to investigate. I walked around our house outside and didn't see anyone. I walked into the backyard to check back there, nothing. I looked up in the Northern sky and saw two stars side by side. The object then slowed to a stop and then moved up in a swooping manner and passed thru a small faint cloud above it.
