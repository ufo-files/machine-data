{cak} Two light sources during sunset

I went out to my backyard to grab something, when I noticed the sun behind the clouds, (one of my favorite ways to capture the sun), around 7pm on April 8th, 2018. After about 15mins of taking photos, I noticed a separate light source under, (what I thought was the sun),the first light source behind the clouds. I then decided to take a video of what I was witnessing, it is now 7:14pm mountain time. The light (again, what should be sun) that was behind the clouds was descending (setting) into/passing the second light source, that appeared, seemingly, out of nowhere. I was situated 2831W Latitude/Longitude: 43.634907, -116.24301 at 7:14pm. The sky that evening had so much activity going on and looking later at the photos I took, I captured quite a bit of strange sky phenomenon. The skies of Idaho have recently been very active. I have quite the archive of photos/videos of sightings I can't explain, and although it is not shocking, it's still shocking, if you can wrap your head around that statement.Both light sources in the end disappear below the mountains.
