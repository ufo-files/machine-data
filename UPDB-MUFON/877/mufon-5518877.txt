3 bright red lights in a triangle shape hovering over South San Jose last night.

My Dad saw three bright red lights that formed a triangle hovering South San Jose, CA last night around 8:50-9:00 PM while he was outside locking his truck up for the night. I couldnt see anything, but he was still watching it while I was on the phone with him. I would say the event lasted 5+ minutes until he lost sight of the lights.
