I saw an orb as I was looking out the window of the rear passenger, it moved swiftly upwards into a cloud and I blanked out for a moment before raising a brow then borrowing my girlfriend's glasses.

I was on the way back from a restaurant with my girlfriend and friends. I was looking out the left window of the rear passenger as I was sitting next to her. I noticed a strange orb of light move up into some "clouds" and I was shocked initially but decided to borrow my girlfriend's glasses to get a better look. I noticed another orb directly vertical to the orb I saw which didn't make any other appearance after I first saw it. The second orb remained there even after we passed by a semi truck. I've been questioning this same event ever since then till this day. I don't have any photographic proof of it. I would like to remain anonymous due to my concern for the safety of the people I care about.
