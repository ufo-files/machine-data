I was getting off of the Mechanicsburg Exit off of route 81 S when I saw directly ahead on the horizon 10 or so red/orange glowing lights hovering in the sky. They were in an odd pattern, almost looked like the big dipper, sort of like an arch, but in a weird pattern. They didn't appear to be moving, they didn't seem to be connected to each other. There were no light towers or airplanes. I stared at them while sitting at the stop light wondering what the heck they were, then continued down the road. I decided to turn back to try to get a picture, and when I came back 2-5 minutes later there was only one light left and it was on the opposite side of the highway, more above than towards the horizon.
