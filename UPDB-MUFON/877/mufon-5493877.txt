{rjl} Fuzzy pale orange sphere flying fast...

Traveling in my vehicle on a back road when I observed a light... Pale orange.. Traveling rapidly... From my right to my left... Directly in front of me... Steady illumination... No sound all of a sudden it stopped... Dead still... And hovered for 5-10 seconds... Then started descending... Picking up speed... Seemed to be in a free fall... I became very excited as I thought I was about to witness it crash into the ground... Then it suddenly stopped... Hovered again for several seconds, then continued again in the same direction... The instant stop, hover, free fall, then instant stop again blew my mind.
