{cak} Saw a hovering object then realized there were more...all similar all stationary

My wife and I were in our backyard just looking at the sky that night...we viewed aircraft coming and going since this is a busy air corridor but we noticed one object that just didn't move...at first we thought it was an aircraft because I could see red/green/white pulsating lights which are typical of most aircraft but after minutes had passed it just remained where it was. To the naked eye it seemed rectangular which divided into 4 square sections (see attached doc). I'd never seen a UFO before and we were puzzled as to why the object just stayed in one place in the sky...I then started scanning the sky and saw a few other similar objects, all stationary, in no particular formation...it just seemed like everywhere I looked I saw one...must have been at least 5...all the same...just sitting there, flickering, motionless. They were not flares that's for sure. Sorry for the crappy video quality...I have an old iPhone that's terrible at taking pictures or videos. I focused on the brightest one I could see that was to the west of us, in the vicinity of the Navy bases in Imperial Beach and Coronado Island. The objects were about 2-3 miles from each other...one that we watched faded away slowly...lights just gradually dimmed and it was gone. Wish I had a better camera!! ugh!
