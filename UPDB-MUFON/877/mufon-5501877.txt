{dr}Pitch Black UFO Hovering Over Truck

My grandma was driving me to school in October 2013, we were in Houston, TX, on the HOV lane. While we were driving, we saw a pitch black UFO hovering over the cars, moving slowly. The cars slowed down to look at the huge object. We thought it was like a plane or something, but it couldn't have been. It had no wings. My grandma and I were shocked, we didn't really have any feelings during the seen. We lost sight as we drove away.
