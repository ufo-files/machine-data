{cak} the lights flashed one lower than the other four

I was walking the dog, I was out for 2 or 3 min. when I looked to the east over the tree line, when I first saw the lower light. It flashed a dull but fairly large light, then about 2 seconds later above and to the left 4 lights flashed in line from right to left. Then it repeated. I ran in to the house almost dragging the dog, to get my wife to make sure I wasn't seeing something that wasn't there. When I returned to the door I did not see it again. There was no sound that I could hear, and from the distance that it was from me it had to be very large.
