{rjl} Huge object at least 200 or more yards long , light on front and rear only floated directly in front of our home, no sound or engine noise detectable . clear calm night with partial moon to our left and object came into view from our right.

My daughter and i had stepped outside to have a cigarette and were talking. I noticed a light to the right of me, thought it was a plane then noticed white lights front and rear, no blue , red or green. when it visually cleared our trees we saw that it was HUGE. This was not a airplane or a blimp, as far as i know there are no aircraft that we possess that are over 200 yards long and can fly with no noise. we watched it travel across our line of sight, it was a clear night with only minimal cloud cover to our west with a setting moon that shone light on the object. It was solid black, a large burning light on the front and a smaller glowing light at the rear. The strangest part was that there was a vapor trail coming from the front of the craft, not the rear. We watched it until it was out of sight, it traveled SE to NW. Members of my family are military and private sector pilots, I have been around aircraft almost my entire life and have seen other strange unexplained objects, but nothing of this clarity or size. This was most certainly, without a doubt , other-worldly.
