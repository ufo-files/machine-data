{jd} Never seen a UFO with my own eyes, But seemingly one appeared in a random photograph I took with my cell phone.

On August 9th at (around) 4pm, My mother, my daughter and myself were traveling on 77 north (about 30 miles south of Wytheville, Virginia), returning from a visit with my sister in Wilmington, North Carolina. Traffic was moving very slow and was continuously and frequently "jamming" up to a slow crawl. I was the passenger, my mother was driving & my 10 year old daughter was in the back seat. I took the opportunity to photograph views of the countryside, being that we are from Michigan & rarely get to see this part of the country. I seen a building that looked interesting to me, so I randomly photographed it. I continued to take photographs with my Iphone 4 until we decided to pull over in Wytheville, Virginia for the evening. After we were settled in for the evening, I decided to flip through some of my photos I had taken. When I came across this one, I must say I was pretty surprised. I did not see this object in the sky when I originally took the picture of the building (lower right corner). I thought maybe it was the rearview mirror of our rental car (a 2014 Toyota corolla) so I took several pictures (inside and out) of the rearview mirror. I could not duplicate it. There was no sun out, it was raining and foggy, so I discounted any type of sun reflection. Please let me know what you think of this picture. Thank you.
