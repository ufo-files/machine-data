Tracked extremely fast objects over Israel on US Navy Radar

Was on watch in combat onboard a US destroyer roughly in December 2011. Early that morning I tracked two extremely fast (Roughly mach 16) objects above Israel roughly at the height of 30k feet streaking from over the West Bank heading west until I lost track of them over the Mediterranean. After tracking them for about 10 seconds they jammed my radar and I lost track of them. It was so real that my CO turned the ship and prepared to fire countermeasures fearing attack. When the Rada rebooted from the jamming the sky was clear and we never tracked them again.
