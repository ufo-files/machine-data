Multiple lights that changed direction and speed

Was taking long exposure astrophotography photos at Maple Leaf Lake Conservation Area in Higginsville MO between midnight and 2am on 11th of August 2020. Shortly after 1am I witnessed what appeared to be several satellites traveling from west to east with naked eyes. Upon returning home and reviewing photos on desktop, the sequence of photos indicated that several of the assumed satellites changed directions, flight patterns and speed, while several seemed to "follow" the other lights.

Photos are 20 second exposures and were taken on Canon 1300d with 24mm lens at f/2.8, iso 1600. I also use a barn door type star tracker to minimize star trails, indicating there was even more movement present than shown in photos.
