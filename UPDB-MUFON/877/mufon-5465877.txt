Object in the sky of Lima

I was at home in the rooftop. The sky was nearly clear, I saw the Moon and one planet. Then I saw an object like a delta wing, gray at a very high speed, it passed all over the sky perhaps in one second from east to west. At at 90 degrees in the sky, no lights.

At the left side of the sky there was a normal plane, with it1s lights, normal speed and the normal sound that continued when the gray object disappeared in the horizon.
