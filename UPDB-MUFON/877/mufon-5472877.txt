{cak} White orb seen while observing passenger jet

I was enjoying the afternoon and playing with a flight radar app anticipating a flight from LGA to LAX to pass just north of where I was at. Just as the radar app said there was the flight, as I began to observe the passenger jet I noticed a second object in the sky not appearing on radar ,a white orb going the opposite direction (east) then slowing to almost stationary turned north slightly. I glanced back a the jet and when I went to look for the ord again it was gone.
