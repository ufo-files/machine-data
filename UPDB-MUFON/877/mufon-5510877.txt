Orange/Red Lights In The Sky

1. We were leaving our home to go to the convenience store.
2. My step-son was the first person outside and he yelled to me to come outside and look up at the lights in the sky.
3. What I saw was a "V" formation of orange/red lights, with lots of smaller lights trailing behind. They were very far away, solid orange/red light color and round and looked like they had flickering edges. My girlfriend and neighbors say it was helicopters, but I don't think so, the lights eventually flickered off and just disappeared.
4. Orange/red lights which looked like glowing orbs, the first I saw were in a "V" formation, the ones following were smaller and at random. They were up in the sky, very far away from us, but emitting enough bright light that we could see them. Also, at the same time a plane was flying past at a lower altitude, and I looked at both to make sure they werent planes or helicopters. I cant be sure, but I really don't think these objects were man made.
5. My first feeling was holy cow! I cant believe what Im seeing. So I ran in and told my girlfriend who is a total skeptic to come out and see. We followed the lights, I took some pictures but none of them came out, the objects were just too far and it was too dark. I followed the lights until they dissipated. 
6. I lost sight of the objects because they were moving a lot quicker than I could. I watched them move away and it looked like they disappeared in to the sky. One other thing that I noticed was that they were all headed in the direction of where their was some heat lightning flashing. Im not sure if this means anything, but they were all flying straight towards it.
