{jpg} Traveling in personal vehicle to see 3 seperate identical object fly over us very fast.

Traveling towards Naches WA on US-12 approx. 2:00am in my personal vehicle when my passenger and I both suddenly see three separate but identical objects fly over us very fast. They seemed to be in perfect alignment with the road but high in the air. Each object had four white lights in a diamond shape. It was too dark to see the surface of the objects. We both only saw them for about four seconds and tried to figure out what they were. Within 45 seconds after the sighting we saw several (5+) military vehicles traveling in the same direction as the objects. They appeared to be in a hurry as if in pursuit of the objects. We are in this area very often and have never seen military vehicles in this area. My passenger and I always talk about this incident to this day, although this sighting was in 2014 it is something we will never forget.
