{dr}saw 3 moving starlike travel 2 each other,hover,then return in direction they came from

two brothers,Johnny and bobby and I were night hunting from a vehicle when the driver,Johnny noticed a light in the sky.he stopped the car and we all got out.one star came from the west,one from the east and one from the north.they slowed as they approached each other then hovered.it was a clear and fogless night.it was like looking at 3 north stars as they were brighter than the rest.they were way up there and hovered in a triangle formation.i don't remember how long they hovered but it seemed a long time.finally,they travelled back in the direction they came from and out of sight in about 15 seconds.Sunset is a small community with hardly any exterior lighting so seeing all the stars was definitely not a problem.I don't know why I waited so long to share this.probably because back in 1973 in rural La there wasn't anyone to.My friends mother didn't believe us the next day,plus we were night hunting.My friends are still alive and we talked about it last year.They seemed like was just any other experience.
