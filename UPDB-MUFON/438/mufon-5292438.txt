White orb in the sky to SE

Saw a light in the sky to the south east. Thought it was a balloon. Looked at it with binoculars. Appeared to be a translucent balloon.
Took pictures with a 600mm lens. Object appeared to have a hole in the center. When I enlarged photo on the computer it looked more like an oval set of lights. Object was in the sky for over an hour. Gradually drifting further away to the east. Lost sight of object as it got smaller.
