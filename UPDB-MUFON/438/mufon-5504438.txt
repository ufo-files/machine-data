{nd} Took u-turn twice, moved down about 100 feet, then shot up into atmosphere.

We had just gotten into the car when I spotted a bright orange light in the sky. I was curious because of a previous unidentified object I had seen last year. The ball of light was not moving. It seemed to just hover, I would have guessed it was mars at first. We stopped the car and watched the light, which is when it began to move. It moved to the left and then took a slight turn down. Then it made a u-turn and was going the opposite direction in the sky. Then it moved to the right, down, and turned the other direction again. And then it turned back to the right and then shot up into the clouds and out of view at a very high speed. It looked like the size of a pencil eraser from where I was observing it, but the light was very bright.
