{tjd} Blinding White object floating at about 500 ft coming from the direction of Wright Patterson Air Force Base.

In June of 2007 I was a truck driver ,retired now. I was on I75 northbound 15 miles south of Dayton Oh on my way to Springfield Oh . It was 3am it had been raining low clouds Wright Patterson Air Force Base was about 10 miles to my East.. Then from that direction I saw a extremely bright white light. Floating slowly at about 500 ft as it came towards the interstate I ruled out planes or helicopters. No sound and it was so bright it was like trying to look at the sun it hurt your eyes. It floated over the interstate in front of me and.when it was over the median between the north and southbound lanes it stopped for a couple seconds then shot straight up through the clouds like a bullet from a gun. There were at least 20 to 25 other motorists around me who had to have witnessed it.I had my phone with me but was so enthralled with the event I never thought to get pictures or a video.
