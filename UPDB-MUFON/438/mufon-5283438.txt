dark colored, triangular, silent, red lights

We were two teen aged boys camping in my parents back yard on a summer night in San Angelo TX in approx 1967. It was well after dark and we were outside on the ground observing stars, meteors, and a few planes in the night sky; we were far from major cities so planes were not as common. Eventually we observed a dark colored, triangular object with a red light on each corner passing silently overhead from roughly north to south. We observed the object (or others) two more times that night, and they were following the same trajectory and were apparently at the same altitude. Altitude was difficult to determine because it was a dark sky and we were untrained teenagers, but the object(s) was low enough that we could clearly see the triangular outline. We were naive enough that we called the police and the nearby Good fellow AFB the next morning to report the sighting and try to determine what we had seen, but no one we called had any information to explain the sighting.
