flying drone and object appeared out of nowhere on camera crashing my drone

on the morning of sept 12, 2020 between 910-915 am, my girlfriend and i was out flying my drone, it was a beautiful clear morning with blue skies when i accended my drone off of a fallen tree. As the drone was elavating and started to move forward, I noticed that i started to lose signal to my drone and had no control over the steering of my drone. Not being able to see anything with the naked eye but my drone, i noticed it start to spin and float out of my control. upon trying to return the drone back to me, it seemed to keep going forward out of my control. apparently the drone went out of sight and crashed, i had no GPS to locate my drone and its location. Upon viewing video footage from the flight, as you can see, my drone was never recovered. The object viewed in the footage, seemed to have taken over my drone causing the crash.
