Only nightmares do I get description of entity

I was not aware at the time what happened however, since the incident and a few additional sightings, I found myself drawing a face which was causing me nightmares, when I drew the entity, some years later an image was shown on the cover of a newspaper and the image was exactly like the entity I was having nightmares and vague recollections of seeing, they called it a "Grey" - oval head with black large oval eyes, slit for a mouth and no nose or ears. Approximately 4 to 5 ft tall, thin and gangly with long fingers.
