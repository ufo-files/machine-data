{td}My son and I noticed an object in the sky that appeared stationary and then moved in and eastward direction and a relatively slow pace and then appear to move north once it got out over the ocean.

My son and I were beachside driving home and observed a cylinder shaped object in the sky that appeared to sit stationary and then slowly begin moving across the horizon in an EastWard direction. It did not appear to be traveling very fast and crossed over land to out over the ocean. There it appeared to take a more north/north east turn but that could have been a distortion because traffic has begun to move and her vehicle was also moving in in northern direction when we looked out over the ocean and saw the object1 It was just too difficult to say with certainty but it was certainly out over the water at this point in time and maintained roughly the same altitude . It did not appear to increase in speed or elevation1 There were little if any clouds in the sky and no other aircraft or objects in the area either.  I took two photos as best I could and just had them sitting on my phone until I saw an article showing a similar looking object that was seen over Vero Beach and filmed. This looks almost identical to what my son and I witnessed in Satellite Beach. I believe my sighting and the one posted were approximately 30 days apart from one another .
