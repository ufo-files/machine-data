{jpg} Four hovering objects in the sky

It was Wednesday, July 4, 2018 and we were sitting on the roof watching the fireworks in the Los Angeles area. We noticed a few drones flying around. When we turned to face west, we noticed 4 objects to be a larger than a drone off in the distance. Their movements also didn1t resemble that of a drone either. As shown in the video that I submitted, they were moving in a back and forth pattern with one of the objects staying stationary far above the other 3. They left a trailing flame as they were moving. After a few minutes of observing the objects, they just vanished. We looked further into it online to see if anyone else had posted regarding that sighting that same day and we found several. One other video from Los Angeles as well, with 2 other videos with the exact same patterns posted from Denver, CO and Bothell, WA.
