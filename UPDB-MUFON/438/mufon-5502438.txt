{rjl} about 15 red lights heading in a west direction

Driving north with my kids and we noticed lights low in the sky about fifth teen in total traveling west. At first I thought they were chinese lanterns but that night we had a strong wind about 20 knots from the west I don't think the lanterns can go against that strong of a wind field. The first row of lights about ten in total crossed our vision in about one minute in a v formation four or five more followed but in no formation. The traffic around us begun to slow down and looking at the objects. They were pulsating and red in color and did not change direction. The sighting lasted a total of two minutes and the objects were heading towards the everglades. I handed my son my phone and he was able to capture two photos of the last object we saw.
