{nd} bright pulsating red object that dissipated followed by a subtle white flash

makiki, oahu, hawaii. dec.28, 2012. 11:30pm (hst)

i was walking home from safeway on piikoi and kinau st. and something caught in my peripheral vision. my girlfriend and i saw a bright red orb hovering well over the normal airplane lines. we watched in awe for about 30sec then it dissipated. we felt something was still there so we watched for another 5-10 sec and saw a subtle white flash of light. it was a crystal clear nite, not one cloud in the sky. we thought, hmm, a flare, based off the altitude, this object would be over the pacific ocean. but no way wed see a flare that high and bright in the sky over 2 miles inland very high over buildings. so, maybe, we were lucky enough to see, what to us, was a ufo.
