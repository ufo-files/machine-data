USN Ship in Bermuda Triangle

Sometime in 1978-1979, possibly July, I was on the signal bridge for lookout on the USS (name of ship removed/cms/LLL). It was a midwatch meaning 12 to 4 am. While negotiating the Bermuda Triangle well east of Miami, I was told by (name removed/cms/LLL), the Officer of the Deck, to watch out for and report any fishing trawlers and any fishing nets. At one point I saw a fishing boat shining its spotlight erratically on the water and in sky. 

I advised the bridge and (name removed/cms/LLL) came out and was watching it from the port side. Within seconds, a bright circular light came out of the water and towards our ship. I again advised the bridge of an inbound aircraft. (name removed/cms/LLL) continued to watch it as it approached our position. 

Being on the signal bridge, the highest point on the ship and outside, I saw this bright object approach usbon the port side about 30 degrees upward. It made absolutely no sound as it circled our ship and it went slow around us the at an upward angle into the sky. As it went into the high atmosphere, it made a sudden direction change at a right angle and disappeared. 

(Name removed/cms/LLL) documented it it the ships log and we all were off duty and went to bed at about 4 am.

The following day, when I woke, I found the ship had lost electrical power and we were dead in the water. Our Gyroscope was just spinning around. Somehow the command had sent a message of our status and the Navy sent a ship out to us.
