The sudden flash-appearance of it was so large and close that it revealed its full visual characteristics of its spherical shape, colors, red ring of fire, and glowing trail smoke. The visual "tracer" effect it gave me was incredible and bold. A final white and yellow flash emitted from the object, immediately followed by a couple large-yellow halos directly behind it before disappearing. The way it disappeared and left a warped halo behind, looked similar to a sound barrier cloud formed at or more than a Tran-sonic speed. Amazing and memorable sight, and among the greatest astronomical observations of mine. I feel as if very few other people if any, saw this fireball of wonder as well, being it was 3am on the horizon of a well illuminated, big city.
