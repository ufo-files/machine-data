{rjl} Hovered above road in front of Lyman High School

My boyfriend was driving us from colchester to willimantic and when we were about to drive past Lyman high school there was a large triangular craft hovered treetop level above the road. The triangle had a light grey metal look to it and It had a light on each corner. The lights were white but with a blue tint that reminded me of some cars headlights color. My boyfriend kept driving and drove us right under it. He continued to drive without speeding up or slowing down. None of us spoke and I felt something similar to the shock experienced after being in a severe car crash. But without the fear felt during the car crash. There was No fear just a sense of going through the motions. Once we were up the road our friend sitting in the back seat broke the silence by saying, "that had to be a government craft" and my boyfriend replied, "does the government have the ability to make a craft that hovers that low and doesnt make a sound?" And I agreed with my boyfriend. Since then I have refused to go to Lebanon after dark. The exact date reported is a very hard estimate as it was winter as it was fully dark out before 6 pm. It was the day after our friends birthday if I'm remembering correctly so pinpoint his birthday and you got the date.
