{cak} Saw massive disc like ufo over a farm near Milan, MI

I was driving southwest down moon road with my daughter and my now ex wife, I saw some sort of huge disc object over a farm that is on a parallel road called stony creek rd. I pointed it out to here because honestly I thought I was hallucinating. She saw it as well and we were both stunned, we stared at it til we came to an intersection then made a left, watched it til we lost sight of it because of trees, then made another left on to stony creek road and where the disc was over the farm there was nothing there, it just vanished. I have no idea what it was or anything and at the time I hadn't really considered UFOs to be a thing.. It seemed to be rotating slowly in a clockwise fashion, it was chrome or silver and reflected the sunlight. The dome part had white or bright yellow looking windows and it just kind of hovered probably 300 ft above ground from my vantage point. It was massive, probably 1000 ft across I would guess. I had a mixture of excitement and fear, I was an infantryman in the army and I'm pretty good in stressful situation, I didn't even think about it out take a picture of it at the time I was just in awe of it. I still have no clue as to what the object was, all I know is it was like nothing I have ever seen before or since.
