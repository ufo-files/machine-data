Wife took the dog outside to her business. Wife asked me to look at an odd bright red light heading from north east toward northwest toward Bremerton Washington. After a fast glance I 
Got my 10 power Nikon binocular. Object appeared about 3 to 5 miles away, 1 to 2 thousand feet high. Very bright red, unblinking and silent. Sky was fair to clear. Slight overcast due to rainy weather. The time was 8 pm. As the first object headed toward Bremerton, two similar bright red objects followed, heading toward Bremerton. Although I didn't have a tripod, I had a good look and knew the object was not an aircraft. A few moments afterward, the 4th object Appeared. Traveling in the same direction. A few minutes later a commercial jet appeared, flying from seatac airport heading sw towards the Olympic mountains. No other aircraft sighted. Sighting lasted about 5 to 6 minutes.
