Very bright white light crossing the night sky.

Last Night(08/31/2011), here in Battle Creek, MI. about 9:20 PM (not really sure about the exact time) I was talking with a friend in front of his house, when suddenly a big (far bigger than an airplane) white light, on the sky got my attention. I asked him to watch the sky, He agreed with me 100% that wasn't a plane or chopter, no flashing lights or colors, solid glowing white light, flying from North to SW (Think so), I'm not sure about the altitude, but I would say about 3000 feet, speed? Faster than a comercial flight (relative, depending on real distance), maybe 1.5 times faster. I try to take a picture but was hard to find it and focus it. Then, before getting lost over the horizon (Line of houses across a parking lot) it dimmed slowly, till dissapeared. There was no engine or electrical noise, nothing. The whole thing took about 25-35 seconds. It was awsome!!
