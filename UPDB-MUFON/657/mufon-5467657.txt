{td} Silver/Chrome glowing craft. Was shaped like upside down pasta dish. Hovered then evaporated.

I was driving down a pretty popular road in my city at 10 o clock (roughly) in mid April when to my right, in a farm field, I saw a disc shaped glowing craft hovering only 20-30 feet above the ground. It was a silver/chrome glow that made absolutely no noise. It was BIG. I am pretty bad at estimates but it had to have been at least 100 sq. ft.. It was hovering at 30 ft when I noticed it and after about 45ish seconds, it just evaporated. It never moved. It never spun. Just gone. I loved it. I personally think there is something out there and to experience it that was awesome. I told my girlfriend and my parents but I don't think either one believed me.
