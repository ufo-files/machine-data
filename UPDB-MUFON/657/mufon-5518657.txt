bright orange/yellow/red orb moving low across sky, followed by plane

this sighting occurred 4 times. three nights in a row beginning May 7th at the same time and object moved along same exact path low in the sky. shortly after a plane was spotted flying at the same time and same path. May 19 2012 was the fourth sighting. Again same time and same path and shortly after a plane flew the same time and path as the other three times before that. I watched this orb for about 30 mins to an hour. At first I thought it was just an extremely bright star or maybe a low planet because it seemed like it was in one area not moving for about 30 mins maybe an hour and then i realized it was something else when the the orb started moving slowly and it would be out of my sight within 15 mins. An airplane with the blinking red light and white strobe light would be flying in the area as the orb began moving north north/west.
