{rjl} 2 starlike objects moving in different directions high altitude. airplane seen at lower altitude.

Playing disc golf at night (Buck Creek State Park with other witnesses. Walking uphill hole 11. The sky was very clear so we looked up to admire it's beauty when we noticed 1 starlike object moving overhead at a very high altitude(straight line movement). I thought maybe a satellite when we saw another moving in a different direction. Others in group thought airplane but then we saw an airplane with typical signal lights at a lower altitude. Clear sky no wind no sounds. We laughed about it and continued our game never looking up again.
