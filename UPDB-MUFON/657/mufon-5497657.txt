{dr}a bright solid luminescent, yellow, chevron shaped object the size of the moon

In 1954-55 witness (A) observed a bright solid luminescent, yellow, chevron shaped object the size of the moon which traveled on a straight line path from south to north. Witnesses were observing from the top of a tall building in downtown Kalamazoo on a group nightwatch for Russian bombers and UFOs. The object traveled from the SSE at an inclination angle of 60 degrees on a northerly straight-line path, disappearing behind cumulus clouds, NNE at an inclination angle of 75 degrees.
