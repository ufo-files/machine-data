50 bright lights in perfect line formation at high altitude and speed

In a clear sky with stars visible, we observed about 50 bright white, round lights traveling at high altitude, maybe over 10 miles high, at very fast speed. We observed for about 40 seconds. Perfect line formation as if a string of pearls. The last 10 had random spacing between them. Each was an individual white circle, as bright as a star. No noise. Absolutely not a plane or heli. We are very serious people, who have never seen anything strange like this. We are in awe!
