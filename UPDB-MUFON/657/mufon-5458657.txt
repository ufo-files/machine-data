{rjl} White cigar shape moving very slowly and disappeared within seconds!

My husband and I were on the 118 freeway heading towards the 23 and this is our second mysterious sighting in 2 weeks! Same object seems to be present. A bright white cigar shape (appears like a plane without wings) then suddenly disappears once we capture sight. 
This time the moon was out (seen during the day) and I was using this as a clear reference point to where it was. It was moving slowly away from us...too slow to be an airplane. Then suddenly, my husband and I couldn’t see it any longer and we were in shock! 2 weeks ago at 1:30pm we saw this same object together coming from the 23 freeway towards 118 direction!
