observed a moon like structure in hanging out of cloud bright like the moon with craters

at 1:45 pm on 12-10-2020, I went outside to take out my dog out. I happened to look up and noticed something that I thought was a full moon in the clouds.. it was glowing white with black craters on surface. I then ran to get my celestial binoculars. I realized while getting my binoculars that the moon would have been in the atmosphere, not in the clouds. when I returned in 1 minute I watched it for a minute. I then took phone out of pocket and zoomed in for pictures. the light started fading quickly . I then went inside and went back to work. I looked at photos , and could see a round black outline on the first picture that I took. by the end of pictures, 11 in total, it was gone. after 30 seconds, a large military plane, smaller that a c 130 flew from west to east low ,through the path and cloud. I am by macdill airbase.about 20 miles. I wish I would have taken photos first before using binoculars.
