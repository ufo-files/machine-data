{rjl} multiple lights appeared above horizon first in a straight line then describing an arc

We were visiting relatives in Foster KY along Western Hills Road. There was a variety of adults and children at a bonfire. The kids and one adult went inside because we were sitting under a tree full of June bugs and they started dive bombing us. This left 5 adults sitting by the fire. We were looking around and up, because of the large numbers of flying insects. Around 9 PM one of us spotted bright lights that came on and then off low above the horizon to the North East. It was a slow unpredictable process and at first we weren't sure what we were looking at. Then the lights moved a little more to the North and were more active. The were not moving fast and appeared to hover. During this time, there were many airplanes moving across the sky. The planes were much higher and smaller and less bright. The strange lights were bright and bigger than any I have seen attached to aircraft. At first, the lights were coming on and off in patterns of three in a straight line. There were at least 3 object described by 3 lights each. They took awhile to go through the process, and we were never sure they would start again. After we figured there were about 3 objects in total, they went out for a long while (5 minutes). When the lights re-appeared there were a series of about 9 lights that described the right half of an arc to the right (east) of the original sighting. I was immediately reminded of the Phoenix lights. We weren't scared, but we knew it was nothing ordinary or that we had seen before. Then clouds began to cover the sky and we couldn't see the lights anymore. The sighting ended about 9:30 pm.
