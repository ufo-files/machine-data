Happens in the same spot. I seen it to the north near a home where the family left and moved to town but still own the land. Also Seen it tonight 7/8

This has been going on for years. For the most part it is over the same old house less than a mile away. I went outside to smoke and noticed the light in the distance. It's always a orange/red color. Tonight I saw it to our east. It stayed stationary for about 15 minutes. Then went further east and just vanished. It happens more in the summer months and always before, after, or during a storm but, even when its not storming. On 7/6/20 we found most of the chickens were outside of the coop that morning and only 4 where inside. We counted them all to make sure they were all there and locked the door before going to bed at 9pm that previous night. The strange thing was the doors were still locked and shut. There was no gaps or holes etc for them to get out. We checked and checked and found nothing. This year is getting strange. I'll start documenting things this year. Maybe i'm just going crazy who knows.
