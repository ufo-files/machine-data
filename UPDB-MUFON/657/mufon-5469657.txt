{rjl} Four bright lights above Highview

I was driving home from work in Winchester on Rt. 259, on Highview just past Norman Road, when I saw four disk shaped craft flying in formation along the mountain ridge to the east of me. The disks staying in perfect formation and was a bright blueish green color. I would estimate that they were about 400 feet above the ridge. They were moving southward direction. I saw these disks stop in midair two times where they stayed stationary for about two minutes before continuing along the ridge
