Orb like craft that appeared

I was scanning the sky for planes or stars, while sitting on the roof outside my window.  None were visible and it was very dark, apparently thick clouds were hanging above. I was on the roof for several minutes and saw no lights. Upon climbing back inside, I noticed a bright light hanging in the sky. It was stationary but then seemed to either dim and dissappear or to move away rapidly within my line of site. Verbally, I repeated "come back" a few times while turning on my camera (in my phone). Almost on command, it came back. It then seemed to move away as if avoiding me, up, down, opposite my line of sight, diagonally, and even seemed to become elongated and tumble. It dissappeared several times. I was quite unnerved and went to get a larger camera while it was gone. When I came back to set up, I did not see it again for several minutes, but my DSLR (still) camera was on its tripod sitting out on the roof. I did take images with it but I do not believe anything was captured aside from the dark overcast sky. A few minutes later, I decided to go to bed, satisfied that it was gone. But I was compelled to look elsewhere in the sky, above my head, at which point I saw the object directly overhead. It was still below the clouds, and so was much closer. I moved back inside quickly as it seemed obvious that whatever I saw was fully aware of me. It began to rain immediately after and I was actually so unnnerved that I could not look out the window again.  The ceiling was reported as about 800 ft.
