a bright object in the sky on my way to work

At about 7 am in the morning, I along with a number of my co-workers observed this very bright light in the shape of an oval hovering in the sky close to the Nickel Rim South mine, which is behind the Sudbury Airport. A few described a second smaller light descending down into the larger oval light. I did not we that. I could just about make out a line running from the center top to bottom as it was motionless in the sky. I got dressed into my work cloths and went back out side to the parking lot with my smartphone and recorded the object for a minute or so. It was a clear sunny sky and the object was very white bright. It was approximately, a half mile from the Sudbury airport facing South West from my position. I knew it wasn't Venus or that it could be a weather balloon being so close to the Airport. After a while we went in for our morning briefing and then proceeded out to the head frame . It was at that time I noticed that the object had shifted position and was now more North west facing. This was witnessed by most of my crew
