{cak} Spaceship shone searchlight into bedroom windows several times

At around 2 am when I was in bed a large round light was shone into my velux window at an angle and lit up the entire room, it then moved to the opposite side of the house and shone the same light into the other vellum window again lighting up the room. I could hear it circle around the small village and came back and repeated this process 6 times. it sounded jetlike and was perfectly round with blue lights around the edge of the object and around the searchlight, it felt as though the experience lasted 10 minutes however the experience actually lasted an hour exactly.
