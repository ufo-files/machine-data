Object with lights falling like a crashing airplane

We were driving Southbound in I-25 in Ratón Pass, a few miles away from the New Mexico border when we saw an object calling from the sky. At first we thought it was a shooting star or a comet but quickly realized that it had a red and green light blinking like a plane. We were horrified because we thought it was an air plane that was going to crash into the mountain. But suddenly the object just vanished. No sound, no explosion, nothing. The velocity at which it was traveling would have made it impossible for it to be a plane. If it was a plane it wouldn’t have time to pull up before crashing. We looked for reports of downed planes in the area the next morning and didn’t see anything.
