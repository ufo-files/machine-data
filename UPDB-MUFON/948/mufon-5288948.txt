Came from behind the moon

On February 19, 2021, I was on a call with our human resource director and I had my 11 year-old son with me. As I was talking, my son and I happened to be staring at the moon. It was right at the time where it was about to get dark. As I was talking, sitting in my in the parking lot of a local , my son and I were both looking at the moon when an object appeared to come from behind the moon and make an orbital move around and then dart away from the moon at high speed. I asked our HR director to hold on while I watched and my son was going nuts asking me if I see it. It was very small, almost like a satellite you would see orbiting at night. However, this came from behind the moon, zipped around the front of the moon and increased in speed and disappeared. I described what I had just seen to the director and continued the conversation. Really cool sighting. Wish I could have taken a video.
