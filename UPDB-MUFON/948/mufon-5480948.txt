Observed fast travelling orange, star-like light in Adelaide, Australia.

I was sitting at my backyard table which faces east. In this position I have a pretty unimpeded view of the sky.

I was watching a Youtube video on my phone when I noticed an orange light in the sky at the top of my vision directly in front of me. I glanced up at it and waited to see the telltale flashing lights of an aircraft, but there were none. It was a solid orange light, star size and shape, but close, if that makes sense. The only detail I could make out was that it was flickering quite fast (the orange light).

I wondered if it was a chinese type lantern as that would explain the flickering, but it was moving way, way too fast.

I observed it for approximately one minute as it travelled east to west, until it blinked out and disappeared.

It approached from a distance, went passed my house and continued west until it blinked out. 60 seconds total. Too fast for a lantern, I assume.

Anyway, second orange light like that I have seen in this area, sightings 16-17 years apart roughly.
