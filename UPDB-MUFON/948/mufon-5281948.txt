I saw a saucer like ship hovering 20 to 25 ft in the air with 6 sets of lights blinking in sequence. Then a light came out underneath lot more

Me and my mom and sisters were driving north bound on 27th and or around 155st Miami Fl. We saw blinking lights hovering about 20 ft off the ground on the opposite side of the road. We pulled over and were about 75 to 100 feet away to our left. we watched for seems like 5 minutes. There were 6 light 3 on the big part and 3 on what looked like a smaller glass windowed drop down. this part of the ship was half the size and was directly in the middle of the bottom of the ship. that also had 3 lights on it blinking in tandem with the other lights. Red green yellow. Then a bright light I can only describe as the whitest light I have ever seen came out the bottom. This light was smaller at the top and got bigger at the bottom like a funnel. It was like a column of light. At that time the ship started to move around like it was searching for something. That went on for 2 minutes then the light went out, and the ship moved directly east 75 to 100 feet. Which put it right over our car 20 feet to our right. I could hit it with a rock and absolutely no sound. The ship stayed there for maybe 1 minute and move east maybe 150 yards. Then headed North on a straight path 50 miles per hour. The ship stayed on that path for 8 miles or so. I noticed a bright white light come out of the ship started out very close then the light got farther away. It looked like it dropped out of the back and was being towed by a rope or something. It got abought 30 feet behind it and stayed there until we lost the ship. We had to make a left turn to go home which made us go east and the ship went over our heads and keep the north path and we lost sight of it. We did go home grab my dad and went out to look for but it was gone. There is a very famous siting that took place at a school in the 70's that was almost on the path that would have taken it over that school yard. This is the first time I told anyone in a form fashion. I hope it helps.
