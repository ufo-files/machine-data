I hope I am sending this to the right place. My wife and I live in Selden, TX near Stephenville where there was the UFO uproar a couple of years ago. There was a really bright flash of light in the sky to the east of us, Towards Dallas/Fort Worth. It was so bright it drew my attention from where I had been looking. I never heard anything about it in the news but we watched it for about a minute or so. Long enough for my wife to call me downstairs and outside to see them. Thanks for any info on what this flash might have been.
