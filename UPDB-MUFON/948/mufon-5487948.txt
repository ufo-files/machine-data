{rjl} Bright object descending rapidly from sky

Driving N/NW along Strykers Road when a very bright light was observed rapidly descending some distance away, Lopatcong or possibly as far as Belvidere. At first seemed like a meteor, but noticed there were few other stars and it was descending very rapidly. The object was very bright and descended at an extreme speed, unlike anything else I have ever seen. It was bright white and did not leave a trail. I felt disoriented after this experience. I lost sight of the object at the tree line and would not have been able to pinpoint where it landed in the distance. Time from first sight to landing was likely less than a second.
