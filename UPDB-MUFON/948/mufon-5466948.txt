Taking video at random of night sky when object darts through frame and then changes direction.

July 27, 2018 @ 9:51 pm adt

A beautiful night observing and shooting video and photos of the night sky. One star in particular, located almost directly overhead, seemed to keep drawing my attention. As I was filming and zooming in, an object appeared shooting from lower right to upper left of frame. Once I edited video by adding a filter, I could see that the object changed trajectory and disappeared at high speed.
