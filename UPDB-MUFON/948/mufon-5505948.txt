{kt}Day time sighting

I was on my way to court to testify against someone who robbed my house. I was driving to hastings, mn. I crossed South Robert trail and as I puled through the light I saw the same triangular space ship I saw outside my house on January 6th 2010. this time it was during the day. It looked like it had three sides and a base. the side facing me was an equilateral triangle that was shining from the sun shimmering white/silver. I pulled out my cell phone and hit the camera button. my phone froze and instead went to the dial pad. I tried to exit it and when I finally got the camera to come up. 15-20 had gone by. when I looked up it was gone. and I was freaked out. I called Craig Lang and reported this to him a couple of days later.
