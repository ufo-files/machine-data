{cak} Beige color, wing shape soaring along the coast over head

I was sitting back enjoying life on the beach. Yes I had a four beers and listening to my music. As I looked up just in time to see a beige colored, wing shape object soar through the air overhead. It was higher than the building to my south as it flew over it. The span of it side to side was about 3/4 the width building. It flew maybe 20 ft higher than the building. I only see it for about 3, 4 secs. First thought was it's a bird but at 230am? I saw no wings flap. Approximately 1hr before this I saw a shooting star FAR off to the southeast and high in the sky. Most likely just a coincidence.
