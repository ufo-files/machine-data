{cak} Single Orange Orb Ascended then Hovered Over Clairemont Mesa

A group of 12 of us met on Saturday evening to meditate and call in extraterrestrial vehicles. We were doing this in conjunction with the worldwide CE-5 event (see [Link moved to FI section/cms/tg]). Immediately after our meditation session, a single orb of light appeared in the west, maybe 15 degrees up. Initially, a few of us thought the light could be the front landing light for a jet coming in for a landing at Lindbergh airfield, so we just watched it. The light continued to rise in the sky, appeared to be orange, and grew larger. It did this for one minute or so. By that time, all 12 in the group turned to look at the object. Then, the object stopped moving, and remained in place. By that time, the object was large and the color was clearly orange. Two folks stated that they saw the orange orb pulsing. The orange orb remained in place for 30 seconds or so, then began growing smaller and dimmer, as if it was moving directly away from us. The orange orb disappeared from view 15-30 seconds after it began dimming.

The orange orb was a great beginning to our CE-5 session. Over the next hour and a half, various members of our group saw other phenomena that were clearly unusual: two bright flashes of light high up in the sky; a star, directly overhead, that appeared then immediately disappeared; a large, bright orb of light, moving in a straight line from southwest to northeast that had the beams of light emanating from it. We also saw an ordinary satellite, moving north to south, high overhead, during our meditation, along with multiple planes and helicopters.

Interestingly, within 10 minutes of our sighting of the orange orb, a helicopter appeared in the west, in the vicinity of where we saw the orb.
