Shooting star that changed speed and directions multiple times

I was outside with a couple friends, about to head back inside, when I noticed what I initially thought to be a helicopter circling around.
It was a pulsating white light, moving slowly from east to west, then back east in a circular path at least a couple miles north of my location.
Suddenly, it ZOOMED west halfway across the sky, then stopped and hovered north..then ZOOMED east, stopped and hovered south..
I say "zoomed" because the closest speed comparison I know of is a shooting star.
There was no acceleration build-up, it just suddenly was going that fast. Same with the braking- it did not gradually slow, it just stopped.
My friends didn't share my curiosity and continued to head inside. I watched it do 3 or 4 circles in the same fashion before I followed them. 
I've not seen anything that fast before or since.
