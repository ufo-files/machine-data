Multiple orange objects flying across Overland Park, KS Sky.

June 9th 2012 Overland Park, KS @ 9:23pm CST. Looking due East. Saw multiple orange / golden objects. Traveling North Northwest, at altitude Im guessing @ 10 - 15 thousand feet. No sound heard, but traveling at high speed. At first it appeared to be a plane with a flaming engine due to the color, however it left no smoke or contrail. This object had a gradual arc in its course. Lost visibility when object was blocked by ground structures, Within 3 minutes another object looking exactly the same and following the same course flew by. This repeated 5 more times within an 8-10 minute time period. 7 objects total. The last two objects flew a slightly shallower course but exhibited the same characteristics. One of the objects before it was obscured by ground structure seemed to fade out. The object glowed constant, no flashing lights or tell-tail airplane lighting. I have multiple videos of it however it does not divulge any further identification.
I was on my parents deck talking with my sister and father after eating dinner. I had my back to the first object, my father noticed it first. That is when I turned and saw it. My father seemed to think it was a helicopter.
