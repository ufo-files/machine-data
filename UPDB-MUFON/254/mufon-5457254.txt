Orange light no bigger than a football

In outside restaurant in North Egypt with friends watching a film on an movie night, corner of my eye was a flicker of light, I ignored it for a good while thinking it was just a street light but after a few minutes it was frustrating me so I turned my head and could not believe what I was looking at, it was a orange glowing ball of light which was about 20-30 feet away from me and about 30 feet in the air, the object was stationery and I could see it very clearly, when I really focused on the light I could notice the light was swirling clockwise with its middle and there was very Suttle differences in the color, the light was solid and did not reflect it's light in the darkness, the swirling within its middle was like swirling some in a bottle so looked very strange. When I leaned forward as was in shock with what I was looking at and wanted to focus even more the ball of light shot over us in an instant. My friend next to said did you see that and I responded to him what did you see, he replied that ball of light. I just wanted to be sure we seen the same thing. This was not man made, it was like it was alive even tho I know how strange that sounds, it was so close to us I could see it very well and see an swirling energy within it, best I can discribe it, I really did try to explain it away but there is nothing on this earth that we know about that could explain this account. I have added a date of 2004 but would need to check my passport to check this is accurate. I have only ever spoke about this to a few people as people just think your mad or mistaken so what's the point, maybe here this story will at least be heard.
