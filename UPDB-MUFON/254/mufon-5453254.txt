I live in Port St Lucie and I was on a walk tonight and noticed a series of lights in the NNW sky. They were in a straight line, angled a little to west, almost straight up and down. They were spaced apart a little bit and moving upwards. Not at a fast rate. But, once they got almost directly above us they just disappeared. Watched them for about a minute, maybe a little longer. Probably 20 or so in a sequence. Wondered if it was a row of satellites or if we witnessed something extra terrestrial?
