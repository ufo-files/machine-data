seen white lights in the sky. object was flipping like a coin whilst spinning & pulsating

Myself & my colleagues were at work at stocks hall residential home in ormskirk uk. At 12am on the 26/11/13 myself & 1 of my colleagues nipped outside the front for a cigare When I had finished talking I turned around & seen white lights in the sky which I realized were moving. I said to both my colleagues "What is it". The three of us stepped forward to get a better look but there are tall trees on the homes ground. I asked 1 of my colleagues to come out the drive but before doing so I ran inside for my phone. When going out of the drive we had full view & I started to film on my mobile phone. This thing was the size of a football field if not bigger & it was flipping like a coin to one side of the sky to another & whilst doing this it was spinning & pulsating from the middle. Whilst this was happening I asked one of my colleagues to go & get our other colleague so she could see. None of us could believe what we was seeing. Myself & another colleague walked further & I started taking pics thener after made our way back inside. After a few seconds we both looked back out & it wasgone.p
