{dr}Glowing orange-red orb hovering just south of my home at close range. It headed west at a steady, very fast speed. I finally lost sight behind some clouds.

I was sitting in my yard on my iPad when something made me look up. Above our house was a very bright, orange red glowing orb. It startled me immediately because it was very large and not in a typical flight path. There were no other lights on it, just a single glowing orb. It then started to head west at a very rapid pace. I jumped up and started yelling for my wife to come outside. I grabbed my phone and started video taping. My wife witnessed it as well.
