{rjl} Right as a heavy rain began, I witness about 15-20 lights flashing on and off in a diagonal pattern very high in the sky to the NorthEast.

I was standing outside, around 10:12 pm, when it began to rain heavily. I walked out from my front porch and looked to the sky northeast of my location, when I saw a large group of star-like lights in a diagonal pattern, randomly begin blinking for approximately ten seconds. I went inside to get my gortex top and bottom on and when I returned, they were gone. I've seen many strange light phenomenon in several different places, mainly in the Southern California desert, but nothing like this before. A very mysterious and unique situation for sure.
