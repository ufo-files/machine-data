Looked like 2 dragonflies with different colours changing constantly but no sound and kept flying around the same area hovering.

I was upstairs on my computer when my mum told me to look out of the window, at first I thought it was an airplane but then I saw that the lights kept changing colour and that it was always moving around the same spot, I opened my window to see If I could hear any noise but there was nothing, the objects where flying at treetop level at a minimum to around 200ft in the air, my dad then pointed out that there was 2 of them and they were always emitting different colours but flying near each other, they looked like they had wings that rapidly moved as they flew, as I went to record it on my phone one the objects flew up really high and stayed there for around 45 seconds, it felt as though it was watching me recording it.
