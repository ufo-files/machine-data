At 12:30am my boyfriend an I was sitting on our deck facing the flooded Wabash River, when I noticed 2 objects that was hovering in the night sky. They was shaped like Christmas trees. A triangle shape with a small square under it. They both had white lights. One of the objects was huge compared to the other one. The larger one was shining brightly. The smaller object's lights was pulsating an it's lights was a lot dimmer than the bigger one I told my boyfriend, "That's not stars!" We watched them for 10 minutes and went back inside the cabin. A hour later we went back out and they were both gone. I've seen them before. They are just getting closer to my cabin because I can see their shapes more clearly. I've observed them moving in the past. They was moving at a snails pace. I just decided to report them this time because they are starting to freak me out.
