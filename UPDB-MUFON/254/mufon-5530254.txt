A dozen or more blimp/cylinder objects hanging in the sky.

This siteing was in the fall of 1959, on a clear sunny day in the fall. I was six years old at the time and lived in a house on Paradise road near the south western part of Orrville,Ohio. I was playing in the living room and decided to go to my bedroom for more toys. Once I got to the bedroom I felt compelled to look out the window, which faced the east. I couldn't believe what I saw,there were a dozen or more cylinder/blimp type objects in the south eastern sky. They looked to be just hanging there in the sky just as if they were painted without movement. I would estimate that they were all the size of a hotdog twenty feet away. I could see them clearly as our home was one of four on the street and a huge freshly cut corn field was behind our home. They were just above a black railroad tressel which was between one half to three quarter of a mile away. I ran to the kitchen to get my mom in here to see them. I tugged at my mothers arm and said there are blimps or balloons are in the sky, you gotta come see. She said your "imagineing things go play"! I looked at her and could see the wall clock was just before the three o'clock hour,I said come and look,and she came to my room and i said look,and she said where,I said right there as I looked out the window to the empty sky. She said,"go play" Now I never had much of a person to make up things and here I am in a position where it looks as if I'm creating a fib. I did go onto play with my toys afterward,but I still hate the thought that I may have been the only one to see this site.
