large, dark object stationary in sky

I was driving north on US-75 in Tulsa, between I-44 and the exit for Southwest Boulevard. The object appeared to my 11 o'clock off in the distance between 7:26 and 7:29 p.m. It was large, and long from east to west. It appeared stationary in the sky. It was perhaps in the vicinity of the Thomas O. Gilcrease Museum (they have some powerful pre-Columbian artifacts), or else it was farther away, perhaps in southern Osage County. My best guess is that this was a dirigible, or maybe a military C-7 cargo plane out of Tulsa International that only appeared to be stationary as I drove. However, if these two possibilities can be eliminated, the object bears investigation.
