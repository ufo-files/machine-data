(GS) 2 objects glowing orange and white, moving north east close to each other, then disappeared

driving home saturday evening on the north side of indianapolis, turned to head east and saw 2 objects glowing and moving toward the north. watched objects knowing that they were not airplanes, helicopters, balloons, etc. parked car and they were still close together. ran into house yelling for my husband and we watched the objects for a minute or so, flashing between white and an orange red color. they moved closely together. the one on our left was higher then the other one, and they moved consistently across the sky. my husband and i heard no sounds. we don't know what we were watching, but we know it was strange and pulsating with extremely bright light. the lower object disappeared first going northeast followed after about five to ten seconds by the other object. we were really moved by this enough to seek out a way to tell others about it. i will never forget this evening.
