Noticed a fireball object in the sky. It was bright and firey orange. We watched it fly by and completely disapear withing approxamately 30 to 45 seconds.(much faster than an airplane) It had No sound. It also did not have a long tail on it which may have made me think it was a meteor. As we were all talking about what we had just seen, We noticed another one that seemed slightly closer but repeated the same path as the first one. We then compared the speed at which it passed by with that of some airplanes that were also in the sky and it was much much faster. Not even comparable.
