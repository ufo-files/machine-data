12-15 extremely bright lights running n to s, 2 lights orbiting each other, and 1 of the lights changed direction

On 6/8/20 at 315 pm Harpers Ferry, WV Shannondale. Directly overhead at very high altitude, I witnessed 12-15 extremely bright lights traveling in a straight line, coming at approx 3 second intervals. The lights were round and approx the size of a medium star. They traveled North to South at approx 3 second intervals, and disappeared behind tree line. During this event I also witnessed 2 lights directly overhead, again at very high altitude, orbiting each other slowly, as if tethered by a rope. Then I witnessed 1 of the 'straight-line' lights stop suddenly and change direction to the East and disappear. The 2 orbiting lights stayed visible until the other lights were all gone, then it just faded out and was gone. The whole event lasted approx 7 minutes. The sky was crystal clear with absolutely no cloud cover, and no humidity, and no aircraft were in the sky. It was incredible!
