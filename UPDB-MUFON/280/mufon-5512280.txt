UFO sighting, bright lights, paralysed, two entities, memory loss

I woke up about 3am, very thirsty and needed a drink, I came down stairs to get a drink, I made one, drank half, saving the other half to take to bed with me, as I came up the stairs, I could see out of our landing window onto the horizon, I noticed a strange sparkler type lights in the distance, as they faded, a bright ball shaped orb descended from above the window, hovered above the ground for a few seconds, emitting a dim light on the ground but not as much as you would imagine from how bright it was, it then abruptly ascended out of view. At this moment I ran into my mothers bedroom, having to pass the foot of my parents bed to run to my mothers side, I shook her to try and wake her, she slightly came too, but I had left the door open slightly and a strong bright light was coming through the gap, I then immediately ran to try and fight whatever was coming, as I got to the foot of my parents bed, I collapsed, paralysed but conscious, as I lay there unable to move, two small creatures entered, I can only describe them as the typical greys, but with rough textured skin, large dark eyes, thin faces and bodies, wearing some form of clothing, with a strange turtle neck. I did not wake up till the following morning, I couldn't find my drink, and when I spoke to my mother about what had happened and she told me she had heard me go downstairs but nothing afterwards.
