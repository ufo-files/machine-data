White lights together in the sky pulsating and hovering

It was five Am and I was walking from a nearby shop to the trailer where I live. I look up East at about 30 degrees up from the horizon, roughly about a mile away, and I see this group of white orbs in the sky. I film them and watch them as they fade in and out slowly and once two orbs even seem to combine together. It was something I’ve never seen before, and it was odd to me how these lights or orbs would be so close together in the sky. I don’t think it was anything conventional, the orbs surely behave as such. No FAA lights whatsoever as well. I didn’t capture enough video to see The orbs disappear, which I regret, but I got some interesting footage regardless.
