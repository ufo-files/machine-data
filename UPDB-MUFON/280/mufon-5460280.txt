{cak} Round rotating object with multiple changing colored lights

When I was a young girl, (11 at the time) I had very long hair. My mother would take me outside every night to brush my hair so that it wouldn’t get all over the house. This particular summer night it was warm and the skies were clear. We had watched the sky most of the evening as the sunset and saw no planes or other UFOs. It was about 9 o’clock and you could just see the light from the horizon. It was mostly dark but light enough to see the farm land. We lived in the middle of nowhere in penncraft which is about 10 minutes from Brownsville in Fayette county. My mom began brushing my hair and we were talking about everyday stuff. I remember seeing whatever it was in the sky. It was dark and round and seemed to have a rainbow of lights around the outer edges. I remember seeing the lights changing color (all colors of the rainbow) and also spinning. My mother and I were astonished. We remember seeing it go from one end of the right side of the sky, all the way to the other in a split second. And planes are fast but this object seemed to travel MILES in a split second. I looked at my mother in shock and she looked at me. I was young and had NEVER experienced anything like that and Hardley ever entertained the Thought of aliens. I asked “MOM! WHAT WAS THAT!” Her face was white. She explained that she had no clue immediately stopped brushing my hair and rushed me inside. We talk about it once in a while but I have yet to experience any sightings like that. I have a few other encounters I’d like to share at a later time.
