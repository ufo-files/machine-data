5 bright white stationary lights blink in succession from right to left.

59 year old male, 52 year old female. Both Law Enforcement. Female went outside into the front yard about 9pm with house dog. Female returned inside about 2 minutes later. Male jokingly asked "did you see bigfoot"? Female replied "no but I did see a shooting star". Male said something like "did you make a wish". Female said "no but it's still out there". Male knowing that a "shooting star" only last about a second went out with female and looked in the sky to the west and observes 5 bright white lights in a horizontal line up at an approximate 45 degree from ground. Lights blinked in succession (one at a time) but remained illuminated from right to left. Male told female to go inside and get her phone to record what they were seeing. Female returned with phone just as the lights faded. The lights never moved horizontally or vertically and did not seam to move closer or farter away. Heard no sound. Cloudless night so lots of stars. It was either a new moon or moon was in the east. No obvious aircraft seen. Don't remember the date but was either late spring early summer 2021.
