{rjl} Bright light moving fast across sky

My partner and I were going to the grocery store when I spotted a remarkably bright star. Upon further observation it was not stationery but moving at an extremely high velocity. My partner quickly collected his aunt, mother and father from inside to witness this spectacle. As he was doing that I was getting my phone out to record it. As I was recording the object moved horizontally before disappearing into the distant horizon. I couldn't believe I was able to get it on video and have proof that I saw something unexplainable.
