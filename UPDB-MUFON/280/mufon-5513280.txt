{dr}While using my new digital Camera on a tripod to photo the moon rising it appeared in several photos

I went outside on my back patio to try my new digital camera and telephoto to shoot the Moon as it was coming out of the clouds. I set the tripod and camera on flash off mode to let it do its thing and set the 10 second timer on the tripod so it would be steady. I didn't see it when I focussed the camera on the Moon behind the clouds. I took a few photos and it appeared in a few of them as a pale blue green orb at first it was below where the Moon was and then it was above it in the next shot. Dont know if this is something for real or not but it was there. I have photos if there is a place to send them. 
I have attached the photos and the file 4 and 5 are of the Moon that night at the same time period.
