(GS) V-shaped object with blue, red and orange-red lights approached, hovered over car.

The witness phoned me on 6/13/2013 and left a message. I returned the call, and the witness recounted the following, as reconstructed from my sketchy notes:
1I was driving north in Hinckley at about 11:00 pm last Saturday [June 8, 2013] with my son (10) and daughter (8) in the car also, when I saw a bright light like a star to the north. It got brighter and dimmer and I could see the colors blue and red. I drove closer to it, then I handed my phone, which has a camcorder, to my son and turned the car left so my kids could see it. At that point I lost sight of it, but it must have approached the car very quickly, because my kids said it was over the car. They were frightened. [At this point] we were by the park in Hinckley. There1s also an elementary school park; it was not that park. [I stopped the car] and looked up and it was overhead very close. It was V-shaped and had an orange-red color like a fluorescent light in the corner.  It would have taken a 651 TV screen at arm1s length to blot it out. [The object then moved off and I followed it for four blocks before it was out of sight.] I later told my mom [and sisters]; they laughed at me; I1m often looking at the sky. I saw three more of the same thing last night [June 12], with blue and red on top. I don1t have a computer or Internet access. I asked a friend where I should report this and [s/he] got your name from MUFON. I have a question: Do satellites have colored lights that they can dim and brighten? 1
Later in trying to pin down the time more accurately, the witness said the time of the June 13th sighting was between 11:00 and 12:00 midnight.
1 W.I.M. Chief Investigator
