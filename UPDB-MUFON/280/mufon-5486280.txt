Around 8pm walking down driveway toward house, I stopped to look at stars as usual. Looking to West/Southwest about 45 degrees up I saw a bright star, but quickly revised my view to a planet, but knew there should not be a planet there(i am a space techie and former USAF weather observer)! As I continued to watch it got brighter and brighter and larger fast and i mean fast like less than a minute(approx.) and i began running down the driveway to front of house as its path seemed to be headed in that direction. I saw a neighbor walking (east to west)toward me.I asked her if she was seeing what i was seeing and she said yes and commented on how fast it was moving. It suddenly stopped just above our road just above tree height. It was all white like gleaming, somewhat egg shaped, but clearly over 6ft tall and approx.50ft long. It almost appeared to turn around and then shot back somewhat to the West, very very fast. Fast like faster than the F4's from my base! I was so astounded that I never thought about anything but seeing it for as long as i could. I ran inside and reported it on my computer to a sight supposedly there for reporting such sights. And i have been captivated by it ever since, hopping it would come back and trying to refine the shape of it.
