{cak} Glowing orb hovered then moved to the east before disappearing completely

I was at work in Snowmass, Co up on the mountain. I noticed a very bright reddish-orange light I thought might be a plane headlight but it almost looked like it was on fire. There was no discharge like a flare would leave so although it appeared to be burning, I knew it couldn't be. I figured it was a plane but it was stationary and there were no other lights. When it began to move I looked to see if it was a conventional aircraft obscured by clouds but it was a perfectly clear night. There were no flashing lights like a conventional aircraft would have. As it proceeded to the East, it no longer appeared to be burning. I reached in my pocket to take a picture and as I zoomed in on the object, it suddenly disappeared. At that point, I knew it wasn't a flare, it wasn't an aircraft, there are no flight paths over the mountain, and it definitely was moving too slowly to be a meteor. I don't know what else it could have been other than an advanced technology.
