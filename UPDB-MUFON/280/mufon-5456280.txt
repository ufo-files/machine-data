{tjd} extremely large approx 200 - 250 feet in length bluish-green lights on bottom of craft.

When working in Law while driving home from Yucca Valley, Ca. in my personal vehicle, something lit up my car including an area approx 50 to 75 yards around my vehicle and desert terrain by an strong light source from above. The lights appeared then disappeared as quickly as they appeared. I then observed what I thought was a huge dark grey cigar shaped craft approx 200 -250 feet in length and approx 30 feet tall hovering approx 20 to 30 feet above the ground off to my right or west of my vehicle along hwy 62 approx 5 miles north of the IS-10 freeway. I came to a stop and watched the craft as it hovered and then slowly moved westward. At approx 1/2 mile away the craft turned upward and showed its underside and that's when I realized it was an oval shaped craft with bluish-green lights around the exterior or bottom of the craft. Seconds later it sped into space.
