Appeared to be cylinder, silvery reflective, no wings, no contrail and no sound.

It was around 6 pm that I was next to my park vehicle and was looking in the direction of NE and gazing at the clear blue sky. That when I noticed the cylindrical elongated silvery shape object flying from east to west, I heard no sound , no wings, and flying in a straight line no more the 3000-5000 ft above land. I had my cell phone with me which has a 5 mega pixel and took several pictures. The sighting lasted for about two minutes. I took about four pictures an in two of them I noticed an oval shape dark object trailing below and to the right of the original cylinder shape object. ( of note this is not the first time I seen this object before, and flying in different directions).
