Observed V shaped light formation travelling silently across sky

I went outside my home to smoke a cigarette at about 10:30 p.m. on September 3rd. I was facing southeast and in my peripheral vision I noticed something at first I thought was a shooting star. When I focused my eyes on it I saw it was a V shaped formation of lights moving in the manner of an airplane towards the southeast. The lights were about the same size, color and brightness as the surrounding stars. I thought it odd that it made no sound. I observed it for about 2 seconds before it went out of my sight beyond some trees. Never having seen anything like this before, I have no reference as to the size, height or speed of the object. I would guess it was some type of aircraft but have never observed a plane that made no sound before. Initially I was unnerved by the sighting, but I wonder if it could have been a Stealth or some other type of military craft. I would like to know if there's any possibility of a Stealth flying over this area?
