{dr}light hovered and moved erratically, then disappeared

I am not the witness, but this is what he told me when I saw him looking out the window in my living room at 11:11 on April 27th 2013:

He and my roommate were sitting on the couch in front of our living room picture window. He happened to see a random orange/red light in the distance. He watched it hover and then move up and down erratically with no seeming path. He then told my roommate and they turned off the lights and began to watch. It continued to move erratically for a minute or so before I came upstairs and it dipped down before disappearing as I entered the room. They also mentioned that it changed from orange to more red as it moved. There was a quiet excitement tinged with concern/fear they both felt. I was very disappointed to have missed it. It was too far away for them to get a clear view of the object, distinguish a shape, or hear if it made any noise. We all discussed it for a few minutes afterwards, trying to find an explanation, but we couldnt think of anything that would move like that. It did not seem to be a reflection of any kind, the light seemed to be emitted from the object itself.

I have done a little research and have found nothing referring to this incident, nor have I found anyone else who saw what my roommate and friend saw.
