black object with jet black wispy tail

Observed the object while flying back to Texas. We were at about 35000 feet. The object was jet black - opaque. round solid end with wispy "tail" like a feather. Observing the clouds below it, while they were moving NW to SE, this object was stationary. After about two minutes it rotated 180 degrees in the same spot. It never dispersed like a cloud or wisp of smoke. And it did not have a "smoke trail" from an airplane or fire on ground. Plus it was too high for a ground fire. It appeared to be about 5 miles from the plane (north of the plane). It finally started moving north slowly....never dispersing but maintaining it's shape and consistency/density.
