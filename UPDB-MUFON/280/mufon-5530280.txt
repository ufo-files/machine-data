Object at high speed and altitude, making a swift 45 degree turn.

I went outside for acigarette, and looked up at the sky, as i always do.
Through a gap in the clouds, i suddenly saw a bright pulsating star come out from the clouds (allthough it was probably way higher up). At first i thought it might be a plane, but the altitude and speed rendered that notion impossible.

I made its way across the gap really fast (took about 10secs, traveled a distance of about "2meters"-as i saw it from the ground) Halfway trough, as i way moving for a better look, it made a sharp turn, to its right, and dissappeared in the clouds. There were no colored lights, although the object appeared to be pulsating.

I felt really surprised afterwards, and kept looking for about 20mins before I went back inside to report it. This is the first Unidentified Flying Object I have seen that I have not been able to identify, as I see sattelites and planes all the time.
