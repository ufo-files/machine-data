Bright orange tuning fork in the sky

It was a completely clear, blue sky day in June 1998. I was 15 years old and had just arrived at the community pool for a quick dip. I was about to jump in when I heard a boy scream, "Hey! What's that?" I looked at him and he was pointing up to the sky.

Though I now require prescription glasses, I did not at the time. I shielded my eyes as I looked in the direction that the boy was pointing: hovering about 25,000 feet above the ground was a bright orange object in a shape that I would describe as a tuning fork. I shielded my eyes and stared at it, mystified. It did not have any blinking lights, nor was it pulsating. It appeared to be a solid object, and I saw no exhaust. I would describe it as glowing but I think it was actually reflecting sunlight; I thought it kind of looked as if it was on fire. I looked away for a moment and then could not find it again.
