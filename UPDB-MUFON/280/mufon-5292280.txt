White Tic-Tac object observed during and after solar eclipse.

This event occurred during and after the solar eclipse on 8/21/2017 this object caught my eye as I was taking photographs of the eclipse. I captured several good images with use of the digital zoom and a tripod. An aircraft was passing the same area during the sighting but I was unable to capture both the airplane and object in the same frame due to the speed of the aircraft. The object appeared to move slowly in a southwesterly direction and the sighting was lost when the object went beyond the line of sight. Attempts to view from a different angle were not successful as I believe the object moved away at a greater speed, but this was not viewed or confirmed. There was no sound from the object, unlike the engine noise from the jet that passed. This was not a balloon, as the dimensions were rigid and there were no observable payloads.
