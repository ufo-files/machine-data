{rjl} Observed unknown object in the sky

My daughter was driving and noticed an object in the sky ahead of us. She asked me what it was, I said I did not know. She said, it has a round look like a drone but it does't have any wings. I said I did not see any wings either or any exhaust. I took a picture and then a few seconds later it went up and totally out of sight. We were driving North on 87 about a mile or so out of Pittsboro. The object came into view on the left side of the road, up in the sky, and traveled East to West across the road and then up and out of sight. I have seen thousands of aircraft, etc and have never seen anything like this object.
