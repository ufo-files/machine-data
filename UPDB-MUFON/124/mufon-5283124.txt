White light with one irregular white blink

I was looking out of my bedroom window which faces NE, and I observed a steady white light crossing my NE view going east toward the downtown Houston skyline with one irregular timed white blink. The east side of my apartment building obstructed it as it continued, so I looked on Flightradar24 and FlightAware which shows all air traffic in the vicinity flying, and there was not one aircraft near the area where I live. About 30 seconds after it went out of sight, it came back heading west in the same view of my window as it was before, and this time as it got to the western edge of my view back to where I first saw it appear, it abruptly zipped back east at a unfathomable speed, stopped for not even 1 second, and then zipped at another unearthly speed back to the west at a slight incline angle higher in the sky than it was before and disappeared permanently. The entire event was probably one minute.
