Two stars

I sat staring at the stars in the sky for a few minutes. Then, one star slipped off toward the West and disappeared. Shortly after that, another star which had been near the first one, also slipped off toward the West and disappeared. They both looked like any other bright white star that there was that night. They just looked like stars.
