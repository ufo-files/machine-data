{cak} A round disc with round portals rotating clock-wise above 60 foot pine trees

I was sitting on the side porch on a warm summer night with my parents talking and I glanced at neighbors lawn when I spotted a disc over the neighbor's pine trees. I yelled and pointed at the object and looked at my parents to see if they saw it. I raced into the house to retrieve my 35mm camera to film it. When I got back outside on the porch it was gone. I ran to the direction it was traveling but it was a wooded area behind the property. The object was gone.
