{jpg} Triangle in shape. Was around 12yro at time. Reminded me of something out of starwars.

This happened back in the 80's I was around 12yro at the time. I was walking home from a friends house with my sister one night it was on a weekend as I remember my parents had friends over. As me and my sister got onto the porch of our house a craft came up out of the woods across the street. I remember thinking this looks like something out of starwars lol, it was kind of triangular in shape. I told my sister to go get our parents. I stood and watched this craft turn and observed lights lighting up from the rear of the craft and then it just shot off. Of course my parents missed seeing it they were too busy with their friends. When they did come out I was to them just a kid with a big imagination. But I saw this and today I'm 42 yro. And I still tell about this event to some people. Whether they believe me or not I really don't care I know what I saw.
