Something seemed to be in the cloud

I was walking to my work place early in the morning as usual on Volga avenue. When I looked up at one point, I noticed something seemed to be in the clouds. There were partial outlines of an object through the clouds, which were completely different from the nearby cloud shapes. I stopped and took a few pictures. It did not move at all. I looked around - There were a few people walking but nobody else noticed it. Not wanting to look like a fool, I started to walk again. When I looked back a few minutes later, the shape disappeared.
