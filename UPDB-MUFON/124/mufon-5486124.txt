It was the spring or summer of 1967 or 68 myself and 3 other friends were hanging out in Exeter Missouri when someone noticed a light moving in the sky. It would move one direction then another then started moving to the south. We got in the car drove about a mile and got out to watch it when 2 jets one from the east the other from the west turned to line up side by side and was behind the light. They followed it for maybe 30 seconds then the light shot across the sky and left the jets like they were just setting there. The jets were low enough that we could tell they were not commercial but all we could tell about the light is that was just a light. I'm 65 years old now and since that time when I look at the sky I think about that night and long to see the light again but have not!
