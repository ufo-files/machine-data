Orange starlike light that crossed the sky from east to west in straight line. 2nd time I see it in 2 months. Probably not a ufo.

Ok, so that’s the second time I see this round, light orange light crossing Santo Domingo’s sky heading from east to west while looking south. Right over the center of the city. Thing is it was both times around 8/9pm. But first sighting I didn’t report, there was a plane who should taken off from la isabella airport, north of the city as it was still low but heading south. The red and light lights were clear and from what I saw the light crossed its path below it. 
That was about 2 months ago.
Then 2 days ago, I was at the Coldplay concert. Just arrived, about 7h30pm/8pm, I was in the east vip part of the stadium when I went to the bathroom, therefore looking south, the opposite direction of the scene, and the concert hadn’t started yet. I saw the same light, bigger than a star and with the same straight flight path. It disappeared behind a cloud and then construction didn’t allow me to see it anymore. 
I checked as I could if there are any satellites or other object that would have the same flight pattern without success. 
I will keep on looking at the sky and if I manage to capture this phenomenon or get an explanation, will come back to you.
