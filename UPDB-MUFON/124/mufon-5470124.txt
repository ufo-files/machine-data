{rjl} Looked out door window and saw object moving behind trees . Strobing red and green lights and a solid white light

Went into the utility room and happened to look out the window on the door. The object was behind the tree across the street , moving to the south . The window on the door faces west . Couldn't get a really good view ,because branches were in the way . Did notice that the object was saucer shaped and seemed to be rotating . Green and red lights were blinking and the white one was solid . Object was probably 30 to 35 deg. of angle , and under 500 ft. in height . Size was between a quarter and a golf ball . Distance about a 1/4 to 1/2 mile if that . No sound , and moving south . 
Went to get the binoculars but couldn't see it in them , and when I took them down the object was gone .
Its been about a year or too since I've seen one .
