Numerous Lights in Tandem Formation

While leaving work, I saw between 8 and 10 reddish-orange lights in tandem, traveling from the NW turning towards the SE towards Mt Rainier. Observed this with at least two other witnesses for a duration of approximately 2 minutes. The lights all blinked as a star would, but then went behind cloud cover, and disappeared. Called the FAA, and they had no reports of anything.
