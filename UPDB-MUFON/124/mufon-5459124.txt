{rjl} 2 Metallic Objects Seen in Sky

On my commute home from work in Grand Rapids, Michigan on March 15th, 2018 around 7pm I noticed 2 metallic objects that reflected the sun. It was a super bright sun that evening and then the sun was glinting off these objects that it caught my eye - literally. 

They did not look like planes or helicopters. There was no sound. I tried to take some photos while I was driving but the sun was too bright. I was about ten minutes from home at this point. 

As soon as I got home to my sisters house in Coopersville, Michigan I jumped out of the car never taking my eyes off the objects. They were heading west and getting farther away but I did get a few photos. My sister came out and then I went inside to grab my camera. I took photos with my camera and cell phone. 

There was a vertical cigar shaped object on the right and a disc like object on the left. The left object shifted positions to almost horizontal to more vertical. The object on the right stayed vertical the entire time. I stayed and watched for over half hour. 

I’ve never seen anything like that before or since that night. I was shocked by the lack of sound and by how slow and steady they were going. It almost seemed like they were just gliding in the sky.
