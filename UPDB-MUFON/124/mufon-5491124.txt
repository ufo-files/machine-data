{rjl} ufo appeared coming towards us and flew over head.lights,noise then flew away.

i was at a nieghbors house in brentwood ny.a suburb on longisland.we were at a bbq,it was either 4th of july or august block party.i was young about ten or so.as i was on the front lawn i started hearing a noise which became louder and louder like a roaring noise or something ,and lights.i remember people starting to point and say things like what is that what is that! thats not a plane people were saying as it got closer and closer it was coming towards us in a line ,more than one,it appeared there were a few.i did not know what it was i asked my dad who was a police officer and he told me and my friend to hide .so we went under a chair on the front lawn but could still see the lights and hear the rumble and feel as thought it suddenly got stormy out.it was the scariest ive ever felt in my life and i still remember that fear today and i am almost 50.there were alot of other people there that night and i always wonderd if anyone else ever reported anything from that night.i remember asking people about it later on and they would remember something strange but would never come out and say what.i know it had to be a ufo because of the fear and reactions of the adults that were there that night.it scared me good.after it past it was hard to go on with the night as it was normal things just seemed different.always wonderd if someone else talked about it.like i said it was the late 70's and i was a kid but i know what i saw and i know what i felt.ofcourse the adults were all laughing afterwards saying we seen a ufo but no one was laughing when it was overhead and going by us.anyway thats my sighting hope someone out there reports it to.it took me a long time but i never forgot.
