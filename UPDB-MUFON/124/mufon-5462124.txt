3 star-like lights in a triangle formation

There were 3 lights pulsating regularly in an equilateral triangle formation, they were hovering right over us blending into the stars.
Several seconds after we noticed it, it fleed at high speed ( as fast as a military plane) to the south-west, just like it was observing us.
It made absolutely no sound and left nothing behind it.
