{rjl} Small green orb appeared to fly in an unusual pattern

On December 22, 2018 at approximately 17:33, in Long Beach, California, a small green orb appeared in the sky and was observed from a west facing vantage point, to travel quickly on a southern trajectory, then change direction rapidly and travel slightly north in the opposite trajectory. The object appeared to "jitter" and move in sudden up and down, back and forth, trajectories with unpredictable motion.

This event was observed during a launch of an unknown payload by "SpaceX." The "SpaceX" vehicle is visible in the photos, but the unidentified object appears to be in front of it and not traveling in the same coordinated trajectory. 

The unidentified object appears to grow slightly larger and smaller in video frames. It also appears to vary in brightness. 

It was observed for approximately 5 minutes before the unidentified object vanished from view.
