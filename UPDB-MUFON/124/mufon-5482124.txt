{rjl} UFO appeared as a cloud in the blue sky

My sister was out on the East 2nd story patio and saw a disc shaped cloud hovering over a vacant field across the street at approximately 12:30 pm. She had me come and look at it and I took photos and a video. The cloud held the oval shape for over a half hour. It was the only cloud in the blue desert sky. The edges of the cloud object appeared to be circulating. The cloud then disentigrated after a half hour. Later that evening my sister received a News prompt on her telephone about UFO clouds that are appearing all over. We thought this was very coincidental since we just witnessed one.
