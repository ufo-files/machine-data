large round dark grey disc object, football field size looked like it had just risen from Lake Erie and was now ascending over road and then was gone

I was on my way to graduate school driving down Lakeshore Blvd, Euclid OH in spring 1972. Time was around 7 am. Suddenly in front of my car as I looked through the windshield, there was this huge, round,dark grey disc like object over the road. The back of the object was appeared to be at the edge of lake Erie and the front was over houses on the south side of the street. There was a small dome on top of the disc in the center. It was a different color and I can't remember the color. This object seemed to be ascending from the lake. One area was at treetop level at the lake edge and the other that was over the houses on the south side of the street was beginning to point upwards. It was a noticeable upward angle. In a few seconds this large object was gone. The was no sound or emission trail.
