{nd} 4 ufo's came together from n e s w then began to circle

When I was laying down on the roof of my family house boat admiring the stars when 4 lights coming from the north east south and west came together then began to circle each other until they became a seemingly one huge circle of light then a saw a massive flash of light next thing I remember is waking up 10 hrs later the weird thing was my brand new Casio watch stopped at 11:26 pm I tried everything the next day to get the watch to begin telling time again but it was frozen I finally had to take the battery out of the watch and put it back in after that night the watch worked perfectly never once had an issue with it
