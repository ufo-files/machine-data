{dr}I saw a green glowing orb went right over house

I was sitting on my lanai having coffee looking at the sky when out of nowhere seemingly saw a green glowing orb, I ran to get a closer look it went right over my house. I went inside looked out front door and window, but could not find where it went. The time was approximately between 5:30am and 6am. Frankly, I noticed because it was glowing and caught my attention with the color, never seen anything like before. I totally was in absolute awe, don't think I had a thought beyond what in the world is that. I did not know what to do afterwards, but finally decided if someone else saw I must be a witness to this. I really don't like having seen something unusual, but I cant come up with any rational answer for what it could be. I know pictures would be useful, but in a moment like that I cant think straight.
