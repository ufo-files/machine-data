{jpg} I was looking at stars saw it and got scared so I watched it then sketched it

I was at tall bull powwow but got sick so I decided to lay down. I was on my cot with all the other tents and saw it. It changed shape going for a drone like object to a saucer to a diamond. It seemed black but it's night so I couldn't tell. There were blue and red lights 4 of them 2 top red and 3 bottom blue. I was watching the bottom of it then it went toward the forest. I saw it start to go down like paper plate falling slowly. I didn't see if it had landed but it was behind trees so it appeared so. I got very chilly and uneasy right before I saw it. I was listening to music and then saw it and stood up on my cot. I was freed out so I started hyperventilating I wanted to tell my grandMA who I'm at the powwow with but I was scared they would think I was crazy.I lost sight when it hovered down behind the trees. Now because it's night every time I see someone in native American regalia I freak out and thino it's another being. Please help I feel like I'm insane.
