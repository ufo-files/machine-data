{dr}Yellowish green cigar shaped ufo with round portals with human shaped being in each portal. I could not make out features, just shapes.

Cigar shaped craft angular edges. Yellow to yellowish green. Round portals on side of craft facing me. Figures behind each portal sitting still but to dark to make out. I don't remember hearing any sounds. Busy traffic all around. I had one of my sons in a stroller with me. I was pointing to it and no one seemed to respond sort of like they didn't see me or maybe didn't care. It was like I was the only one seeing it, like they were all oblivious. It had some great size and mass to it. I forgot about it for years and some show I was watching about ufo blocking your memory brought it all back it just seemed to bring it back in a instant. Like my memory had been blocked somehow. I don't remember the ufo leaving. It was like it was floating there stationary. I don't remember walking away and I don't remember it leaving. This isn't something I'm making up and it's also not the only ufo I've seen, but the other time I was with three other people and we all saw it, but it was nothing like this. It was pink lights in the sky, near power lines and doing things in the sky that plains or helicopters could not do. The movements were very close and intertwined. We all had the feeling that they were drawing or gathering energy. I don't think we all could have been imagining them.
