5 fast glowing orange orbs over Round Rock, Texas.

I walked out to put my trash out at approximately 9:30 pm Sunday August 15, 2010 and immediately saw a string of 5 Bright orange glowing orbs in the sky moving to the North. I wondered if it was a meteor shower but they were traveling in a row and not burning out and lasted much longer than that. They were moving extremely quickly and in a line one behind the other.  As they got closer they were more brilliant in their glow and I ran inside to grab my camera.  As I emerged 15 seconds later they had already made significant progress and 2 were out of sight. The object appeared to have almost bug like wings that were also glowing orange. They were gone before I knew it so obviously not jets....too fast. I was able to get one picture as they were almost gone.
