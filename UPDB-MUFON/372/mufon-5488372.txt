I was at home taking the dogs outside in the backyard.It was a beautiful summer starlit night.I walked to the back of the property where my shed and the tree line are located,I felt like I was being watched.As I looked up just above the tree line I observed a silent, massive ,gun metal black ,triangle shaped craft hovering from just above the tree line. It was moving very slowly to the north its now over the top of me know it's blotting out the stars in my field of view above me. At that second a thought ran through my brain this is the real deal U.F.O. and just like that it shot straight up into the sky no sound, nor movement of the trees. This is when the lights came on green, white and red kinda like strobe or spin hard to tell as it climbed a mile or two perhaps into the sky had I blinked I would have lost track of it. It stopped this solid reddish orange fire ball dropped out from below the main object and just like that at atom splitting speed at the same exact fraction of a second the main craft shot north in a straight line and the fire ball shot straight opposite direction at this point I lost site of both objects. I was stunned. I noticed the dogs were already in the house I went in the house and told the family I saw a real life, live U.F.O. . My son, Brandon ,approx age 8 at the time,said I want to see it dad. I responded its long gone son there is nothing to see it split,literally .
