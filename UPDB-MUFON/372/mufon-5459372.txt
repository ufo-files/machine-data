{tjd} Florida light west to east---two objects shot out

The object appeared star like traveling west to east toward the ocean. I observed it for a few minutes and it had no sound. Much brighter than a star and larger. I watched ot go east to the ocean side and it had two small orbs come out and they went straight up and the light from each disappeared seconds later. The main object continued over the ocean in a easterly direction.
