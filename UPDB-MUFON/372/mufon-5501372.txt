{cak} Two shining discs overflew an eight year old boy in midafternoon on cloudless day in northwest Arkansas

I was about eight years old, in the family vegetable garden alone. My attention was attracted to two shining discs at about 3000-5000 feet altitude. They were in formation, side by side about 300 feet apart, approaching at high speed from the south. They made a sharp 90 degree turn to the west just before they got overhead. They left the area, still side by side at the same elevation, possibly descending slightly. The entire incident lasted about 15 seconds. At that time I had never heard the word "Flying Saucer", or UFO. I never reported because I did not think anyone was interested or that the data would be useful.
