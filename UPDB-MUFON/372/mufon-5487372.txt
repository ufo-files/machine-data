{nd} Was hovering in sky with no sound.

It was the fall of 93. My first child had just recently been born and I was over my girls house with the baby. I left around 11:30 driving home when I turned off of Brewster road on to summer avenue near minotola when I noticed what looked liked a street light. It was the size and same exact color of the street lights that are around here. ( a yellow ish light) as I was turning on to summer avenue I pulled over and got out of the truck and was leaning over the hood on the driver side watching the object. It was about over a friend of mine, his house. I watched the object witch I'm not really shure for how long ant then it started to rise. Not fast but kinda steady and slow that's when the light went out and couldn't see or didn't witness anything else. I know it wasn't a street light. I have told people I know about but this is the first time I have reported it.  Thank you for your service.
