My best analogy is this; imagine you're looking straight down on a toy top. You see the circular width, some edging, and a dot at center. It is a dirty yellow in color, with the outer dark edging and a center dark ring surrounding the blk. dot at center. I wasn't looking for anything in the sky- I'm standing outdoors on a warm January night, taking a cig break. Temp. approx. 45 degrees, no wind at dusk. Sky was overcast. I'm facing south. There is a 100 Ft. tall tree about 100ft. from where I'm standing. At the top of this tree, the right side near the top forms a 90-degree opening. At that point at the center of the opening is where I witnessed this object.

No sooner I really see it good, the yellow color instantly fades away, the dark outer edging becomes wider and the dark dot at the center increases in size, but not too large. In an instant, the entire object is gone, or transparent except for the black dot at center. This "turns off" like an CRT or old TV sea. All happened in about 5-6 seconds. How can forget this. I immediately recorded what I witnessed in a binder notebook. After a long time, I concluded it may have "cloaked" itself. Told my Sr. Electrical Engineer and CPA brother what I saw, and his comment was "I guess you were supposed to see it”. Gee, thanks a lot I thought.
