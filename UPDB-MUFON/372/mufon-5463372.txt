Walking home from my job at local diner observed three lights over the horizontal to the southeast. One light at top and two below forming a triangle. They came directly towards me. As it was over me only one large light in center and 6? lights either side of it. UFO made a humming sound. It was a dark night with no other lights close by. I had the sense the object was flying too low and might crash into hills to the west. I felt it was only 50 to 75 feet above me.No wing tip lights or tail lights observed, but had the idea it was triangular in shape. No dogs barking. There were sightings in town to my south newspaper reported the next day.
