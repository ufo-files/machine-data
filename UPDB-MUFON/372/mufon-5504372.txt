Saw apparent large flock of white birds oscillating in several groups within a larger formation at about 10,000 ft..

I had taken my neighbor to Wahlmart drug store on Grand Ave in Glendora it was 11:05AM. The neighbor had just gone into store and as I stepped from my car I glanced North on Grand Ave at the sky just above the foothills and saw what appeared to be a great flock of white cranes. Originally I believed they were flying at about 2,000 feet but then they had to be much higher. I watched the flock a few seconds and noted they were in several(more than 10 or 12) groups that oscillated around, rotating in and out, while intermittently vanishing and reappearing. All groups within the overall formation were slowly drifting West toward the ocean. While watching the formation one object broke away in a straight line heading East toward San Bernadino at a high velocity. Then I saw a two engine aircraft headed toward the formation from the West at what I believe was about 10,000 feet. The formation and the aircraft vanished at the same instant. I estimated that considering the number of groups, at least 10, with perhaps more than 12 objects in each group the formation had in excess of 100 flying objects.
I was surprised but not to excited since I have always believed that anything is possible. Previously (40+ years ago) I have seen green lights dive straight down into the ocean after midnight from the 405 Fwy near Huntington Beach.
