{rjl} Multiple white spheres hovering around moon on a crystal clear evening. They appeared and disappeared over seven minutes until they were gone.

My daughter and I were looking for the chickadee in our tree when three tiny spheres caught my eye. They were directly overhead, but they were so high, they appeared to be stars or planets. One was red and the rest were bright white. I called my husband over, and he immediately saw them, as well as my daughter. The red one disappeared, and I began taking my photos. They appeared to hover around the moon, varying their positions. At one point there were some at 7:00ish, 6:00ish, and 3:00ish. They were in clusters or pairs as they came in and out of view. Some stayed in mostly the same place. They appeared to be very bright white spheres either reflecting the sun1s light, or simply ultra white. Two ladies on a walk inquired about what we were looking at, and joined us. They, too, saw the objects. They noted that they looked like planets, but were then surprised to see them moving in and out of focus. They also guessed they may be weather balloons, but did not know. All of us were amazed and our conversation was lively. I commented that my neck was hurting from looking so high in the sky, so I sat on the sidewalk in a clearing and continued taking photos and videos. The women asked if I could see them better in my camera, and I replied that they were easier to see with the naked eye. We watched together for a good five minutes before all of them disappeared from view. My daughter thought they were moving toward the moon. At any given moment, there could have been as many as ten or as few as three.
