{rjl} Very large saucer shaped object with prominent white lights surrounding craft.

The object was caught on a game camera with before and after pictures of the horizon which gives perspective on the event. It was captured on film about 7.5 miles out from camera location and 1/2 mile in the sky (above the tree line ~ 8400 feet). In the distance are outlines of distant mountain ranges for perspective. 

Data was discovered upon review of all game cameras on the property. It is located ~ 15 miles NNW of Canon City on a mountain ridge line. The camera is facing SE over the valley.
