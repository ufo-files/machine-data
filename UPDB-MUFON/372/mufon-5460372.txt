Hey folks I would like to share an experience I had this weekend while camping and I'm not sure if this is the appropriate place or group,  If anyone knows more about UFO sightings or where to find more information I would really appreciate it. 
May 26 2019, approximately 11:45 pm my daughter, myself,a family friend and my daughter's boyfriend, all witnessed unusual activity in the skies. We were camping in the Beartooth mountains of Montana , sitting around shooting the breeze when lights caught my attention above the tree line, I pointed it out to everyone else and we were all stunned. These lights seemed to follow each other in a straight line sometimes fast sometimes slow, then suddenly they broke off , but they remained within our visual site. At first our thoughts were a possible meteor breaking apart but it was like slow motion to start out then they speed up. Disappeared and did it again, one broke away very quickly and as we called it began dancing just below the little Dipper. We watched them until approximately 3am.. It was so scary, exciting, puzzling and we were just dumb struck.
