Coming home from work and a ufo was over top of my car.

I finished work and left a little early. I took the short way home as we live out of town. I turned onto our grid going east. A car turned onto the grid from the highway and was about a mile behind me. My bluetooth rang and I answered it. It was my 19 year old daughter that was behind me. She said is that you on the grid in front of me. I said I guess I am in front of you. She said mom there is a ufo above your car. I can see it going back and forth over top of you! So I stopped and got out and she stopped behind me but it had gone up. The other one to the north was still sitting there. So we continued home went inside told my husband. Then my husband and me went outside to see if it was still there. The one to the north was still there. I went back inside cause it was -25 and shortly my husband came back in and said one was above our farm.
