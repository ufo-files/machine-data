{cak} Glowing light travelling from SE to W, then disappeared

I was on my front porch. I looked to the Southeast, and noticed a light in the sky. I thought it was an airplane. I watched for the blinking lights, but there was none. It was a glowing light that was reddish/yellow/orange. It traveled in a straight line to the east. It made no observable noise. It disappeared to the west of my location. Total duration - about 20 seconds.
