{jpg} An all black, slow moving (very wobbly) disc shaped ufo .. In broad daylight.

I was living in Green Valley Illinois years ago (Just now telling my story) One late afternoon I left. The sun was setting but it was still plenty bright outside. I drove just outside of the city limits and looked to my left. There was the craft. I can remember looking at passing cars like Do you see that?!? I kept driving but I clearly recall watching this thing wobble so badly I was sure it would crash. It was the cliche disc shape but it was all black. There was a very small airport down the road so I told myself it must be a new aircraft of some sort but in hindsight I know that was just too bizarre to be anything short of a ufo. My big question has always been, have you received any similar reports of this craft? I would sure love to know if someone else had to tell you too. Thanks for your time :) All the best.
