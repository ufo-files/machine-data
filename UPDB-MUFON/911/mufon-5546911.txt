Three equal-spaced lights (as one) at a 45 degree angle moving back and forth.

I was driving home, and at about 12:15 AM EST, I came to a stop at a red light in Easton, MA (Washington Street CVS) and the lights immediately caught my eye. I do live near a small airport, and thought it was a plane at first, until the lights started moving sideways, back and forth. This lasted about 14 seconds until the light turned green and I was too frightened and wanted to get home as quick as possible. I had to turn left, and then looked again for the lights as I turned again, but did not see them.
