A Light was above me

Well, it started with me being outside with my cat (He's an outdoors cat), i was in my coat, cuddling with him. Then a helicopter flew over me (probably a coincidence). It was night so I tried recognizing constelations, I found the big dipper but then.... A strange light caught my eye. I looked like a star, but it was moving, like not eradicly, but like shaking and i was also hearing some sort of... buzzing. I observed it for about 20 minutes and then it flew off into the distance. I tried recording it but my phone wasn't working. I then went home with my cat and I am now writing this. People of the Czech Republic be careful! They are out there!
