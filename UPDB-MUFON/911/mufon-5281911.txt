Hovering multi-dome shaped craft.

I practice taking photos of highflying aircraft as I exercise by walking near Silver Lake. I see how quick I can take my camera out of its case, turn on and zoom in, so I will be ready should anything worth shooting appear like animals, birds, planes. I spotted this highflying plane, and quickly took out my camera and as I was zooming in, I spotted the object in the view finder as I took the shot. It is reflecting the sun, so is a solid object. It does not appear to be a drone or any other man-made craft.
