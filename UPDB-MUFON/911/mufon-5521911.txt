Extremely huge, about a square mile. Moving slow in the east direction then disappeared

My brother and I were driving in Troy and saw a giant craft hovering. It was around 10 15 at night. We got out of the car to watch it. It was a triangle formation with three orange balls of light. It was there for about 3 to 7 minutes and very slowly started moving east. Then it was gone.
