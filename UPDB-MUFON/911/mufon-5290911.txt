I attended an overnight stay at Moundsville St. Penn on November 9th from 10pm to 4am . I have gone twice this year and the photos / interactions with apparations have been incredible . I rented out the whole place again for just my father and I . Several times throughout our exploring I tried to get a light on a small moving object to no success . About 12:30 am we entered the cell blocks . I had a feeling like many times that we were being observed and throughout the night there was inaudible mumbling in both our ears . I noticed something I couldnt explain standing very still on the second tier in front of a cell door . I noticed this was solid because I could not see through it or the cell bars behind where it stood . It was about 4ft tall , gray, giant long head , tiny body , and very long arms . I did not get a good look at its legs . I actually have photographs of it , however when I shown my light towards its direction it seemed to run down the 2nd tier grating , probably covering a few hundred feet in a second or two . If and only if this report is taken seriously wil I be comfortable enough to show my photographs . My life has been very full of abnormalities around me , however this experience I cannot shake .
