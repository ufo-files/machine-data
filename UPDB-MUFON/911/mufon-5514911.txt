From a single light came a second and third light

We were sitting on the back patio with no lights on and just happened to see a light in the sky moving. We first thought it was a plane or helicopter, but it made no sound and had no blinking lights. It kept moving towards the south at a constant speed. Just before it was almost over the tree tops a second light came out of the original first light. It kept on going and then turned around and a third light came out of the first light. The three lights then moved off in a triangular formation.
