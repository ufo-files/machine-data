Bright glowing light passing over Cannon County

I was leaving my house for work at about 6:45pm when my daughter says, "look mommy it's a shooting star!...it's still going... Mommy why is this taking so long to fall?" [side note it is an extremely clear night, the moon is shining bright and all the stars, satellites and airplanes are clearly visible] I look up from packing my things into the car and see this huge white ball of light moving across the sky quite fast. There were a couple planes in the sky, and helicopters fly over our house often so I immediately knew it wasn't them. It was going quite a bit faster than either of those, & too big to be a satellite.
My first thought was it's a drone, but the way it traveled and it's size there was no way it could be a. It came from behind my neighbors house and he has a mile long driveway and disappeared behind a treeline that is across the road and behind a creek. 
For size comparison if you look up and see an airplane and it looks to be dime sized, this thing was the size of a quarter. It was a blueish white color and had a white "light glow" around it, almost like an aura. ISS was not in the area, and there was absolutely no noise at the time. We live out in the country so usually there are dogs barking, and other various noises. Everything went silent except for a car passing. I tried taking a video on my cell phone, but the quality is bad; I had my hands full and couldn't zoom in and I believe because it was moving at a fast rate my phone couldn't focus on it. also please excuse my daughter in the video she was excited and being silly.
