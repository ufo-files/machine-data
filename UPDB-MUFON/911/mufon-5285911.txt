Silver, highly reflective, fast moving, very large object

I was in my backyard birdwatching at approximately 641 am CST when I noticed something in the sky coming from the SW. It was below the cloud line and from my perspective/distance from it, it was still about the size of an aspirin. It was very bright as though it was reflecting all the light around it. When I started recording it was like it knew it. Because suddenly it was as though it was gone. Upon reviewing the video however, it had been there the whole time only it had changed direction and shape. It went from a silver ball that was big to a small cloud like color. The object was initially traveling from the SW to the NE. But upon me seeing it the object went NW and UP.
