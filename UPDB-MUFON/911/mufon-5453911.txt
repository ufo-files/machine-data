Single Flashing light off in distance moving slowly

Viewed from a rear bedroom window the flashing light looked over five miles away . Colours were red and orange and blue and white . Moved too quickly to be a planet , definitely not a satellite as I have witnessed those whizzing by. Not a helicopter or airplane either as we saw other aircraft moving in the sky . Never seen anything like this before in the sense of aerial objects at night . Didn’t appear to be moving away or closer to our position by was definitely moving from S to SW across the night sky . Stood and observed for 30 mins , object still moving when we left .
