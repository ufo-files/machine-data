Flew parrell to me for about a minute

I was on the way back from a brass band rehearsal down a dark back road with fields either side, it was about 10pm. I don't remember anyone else on the road with me, it isn't a well used road, suddenly out of nowhere a triangle/cigar shaped object with red/orange lights on it appeared flying parallel with my car.  It completely messed up my what was well tuned radio with static (it was never the same after that!) There was no noise, it stayed with me until I was due to go under a railway bridge and before I went under it just vanished! No the layout of the road is different but I will always remember that night! My radio came back but not as well tuned as it was before! I didn't get a good look at it as I was driving I decided being a lone female down a dark road I wouldn't stop. I wasn't scared at all, which I have always found very strange, I always thought I would be if anything like that ever happened.  I went to work the next day and told my colleague, who suggested that I phone the aviation authority for our area, we live in a military County, in Wiltshire, England, to see if there was any exercises/activity in the area. I did so and they had nothing, they suggested I call the local paper and report it. I did so and the person I spoke to said it was strange because that very week they were going to include an article from a UFOlogist in the area who had seen one the week before, describing the same thing, he saw it in the town next door to where I was. They put the incident in the paper the following week along with a comment from the UFOlogist who said that it was good that someone else had seen something as well.  I can't remember the exact date but I know it was March 1998 sometime.
