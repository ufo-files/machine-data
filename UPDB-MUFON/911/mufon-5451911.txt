Circular disk

I went outside at 7:49 pm to adjust my antenna when I looked up & spotted the object in the Eastern sky... I went into my house to get my scope to look closer at the object..After settings my scope up to view the object, I seen that it was a very big circular object with a smaller object down below it swinging back & forth like a bottom handle on a grandfather clock... It travel from east direction to the west for a little over an hour before changing from a silver circular disk to a bright orange color before breaking up into 50-100 objects going into different directions at 9:11pm.. I have seen an object like this 3-4 weeks ago and before back in 2017...
