{rjl} Regular sighting

My wife and I have been seeing this object on and off for a couple of months. It will be there for several days and then be gone for a couple of days, then be back again for several days.
We see this object out of our house front window. It stays in position even when other objects have passed by. At one point it was gone for a couple of weeks and then returned. We have used high powered binoculars, but cannot get close enough to determine details.
