{tjd} Vibrating silver egg

My daughter and I had just passed through downtown Jacksonville Florida on our way home to South Carolina. We were a couple of miles north of the downtown area and approaching an overpass when I saw a silver shadowy egg shaped object about the size of a school bus traveling Just above the cars but extremely fast along the overpass. I yelled out to my daughter oh my gosh I just saw a UFO. I pointed to where I saw it and with both of us looking that direction another one the same shape and same speed passed by going in the same direction from west to east. We both Looked at each other with our mouths wide-open and couldn’t believe what we had seen.
