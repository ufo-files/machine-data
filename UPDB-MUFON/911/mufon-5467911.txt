{cak} We were traveling eastbound when we saw it in the sky. It didn't move. There were no balloons, no aircraft landing/taking off. Too far away from the airport.

We were on our way home
My son saw it first and asked me what it was because we are very familiar with all the aircraft in the area. This was definitely not any of them! I pulled over and he took the photo. It just hovered there. I looked at the photo and when I looked up again it was gone. It looked black on top and on the bottom. No lights, no wings. It looked like a black disk, then it tilted while hovering then it looked almost like a globe top.
