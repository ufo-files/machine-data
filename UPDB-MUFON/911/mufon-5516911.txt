{nd} saw a bright object drop, hover, and then shoot across the sky at a phenomenal rate of speed

On the morning of July 13 2012 @1 am. I was woken up by my dog having to go outside. I let her out and was standing on my porch looking south when i saw what appeared to be a shooting star. It dropped almost straight down, hovered for about a second, and then shoot across the sky at a phenomenal rate of speed. It covered a distance of probly 2 miles in a split second. I ran to my car, 10 feet away and grabbed my binoculars. It was a round pulsating/flashing white light with red and blue mixed in. It looked fuzzy/hazy. I only got to observe it for about a minute before it dropped out of sight behind some trees.
