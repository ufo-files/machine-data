{cak} This was the night TWA flight 800 crashed. I dreamt I saw a beam of light shoot down the plane. Then in the morning I went outside and the craft was parked in front of my house. Isolated rural setting.

I recently read UFOS OVER NEW YORK by Preston Dennett. It was the first time I became aware that TWA Flight 800 had been documented as being shot down by a UFO. As stated above, I dreamt that night about a jet to the south being shot down by a blaze of white light. I then dreamt I went downstairs and out the front door into the sunlight and encountered a craft that reminded me of a small space shuttle type vehicle standing on 'legs and feet'. It was translucent/transparent and glowed in a hazy way. It was phosphorescent with areas of lime green, yellow, orange, white. It just sat there. As I am terribly afraid of UFOS, I did not linger and went back inside. Though it was a dream, the sighting of the craft feels like a real event to me, while the witnessing of the jet being shot down was part of a dream. Obviously I have never forgotten this and reading the account in Dennett's book validates my experience. As I followed the news coverage of the event with great interest at the time, I was always certain the plane had been indeed shot down.
