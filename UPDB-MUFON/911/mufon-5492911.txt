{cak} Three stars were shooting at each other.

Three of us cousins were sleeping outdoors in an unused field on my dads farm in the 1950's, on [location removed].about August, when, about 10 or 11 at night we all looked up and saw three stars, very far away, apparently shooting at each other. The three stars were in a right angle triangle and a moving small light from each of the stars on the end originated a bit from the star and proceeded toward the corner star. The light, which seemed like another star would slowly move toward the corner star then go out and disappear about a 1/4 inch before it would have hit the corner star. The 1/4 inch is arrived at if you held your hand out at arms length and put your thumb where the three stars were. The stars were about two thumbs width apart. So both stars sent a small light ball toward the corner star and the corner star would send a ball of light or shoot ,randomly toward the other two stars. This lasted for at least 30 min. One of the older cousins is still living and quite a few years ago , when I asked said" Yes, I remember something happened that night" I never asked for any more confirmation. I know what we all saw. During this display of the stars all shooting at each other, it was about 10 to 15 sec between random shots maybe, all the other stars in a normal night sky were visible. I am saying this as I was told that at the distance stars are from the Earth that there is no way a light could go from one star to another as it would take light years so what I saw must have been really close to Earth. I don't believe that at all. I feel those three stars, which I feel are likely in the same place as they were in the 1950's were many light years from us and somehow defies physics as we now it to be shooting at each other.
