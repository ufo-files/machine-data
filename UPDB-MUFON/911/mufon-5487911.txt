{rjl} Silver / Chrome reflective, orb / circular / oval shaped.

Driving home from work. I looked up into the sky and noticed the size and altitude of a possible aircraft. BWI, Washington National and Andrews AFB all have potential flight patterns in this area. I noticed the vibrant reflective properties, and the size and speed made me think that something at that altitude the object would stall. I noticed what seemed to be a reflection from the sun at only and angle a circular shape could produce, and then total disappearance before my eyes. Nothing blocked my view, and the craft wasn't going fast enough to have sped away from my field of view (again, almost stall speed). 

I am in so much disbelief and play devil's advocate in any situation where a UFO may have been a potentiality. Disbelief plagues any thought I had previously of a potential UFO. This was no potentiality. ASAP or otherwise, this wasn't your normal aircraft.
