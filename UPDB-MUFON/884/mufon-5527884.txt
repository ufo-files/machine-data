Noticed the sphere while looking at clouds - thought it was a planet - took several digital photos.

While house sitting at my sisters in Elmira Oregon, I was standing on here back pourch faceing east, cloud gazing when I noticed the sphere at about 1'o'clock apprearing just in front of a small slow moving (NxNW)thin cloud. It was brighter, or void of, the surrounding light blue sky. I went in side to get my camera. I noticed that the sphere had not moved from it's location after taking about 8 minutes to get my camera, but the cloud had passed on. I lined the spere up with a top to make sure it was not moving - it did not, for the entire duration of the viewing. It left my sight by slowly fading out. I was excited that it might be a planet visabl in broad daylight. I also noticed a shadow on it's backside facing away from the sun.
