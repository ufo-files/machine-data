{jpg} Took short video before the lunar eclipse started, didn't know I was capturing some type of hovering orb

I was on my back porch getting ready to photograph the eclipse. I decided to take a short video clip with my cellphone. I was holding my phone in my hand with my arm extended downward so I could keep it steady. I was watching the cell phone and keeping the moon in the video. I did not know I had captured anything unusual until I played the video back. The blueish orb hovers and comes in and out of focus as if my phone was auto focusing on the orb, not the moon. The orb may have remained a long time, but I shut off the video after 57 seconds.
