UFO on 29th Sept.2019 in germany

Hello me and my girlfriend saw a light in the sky on the 29th.Sept.2019 in germany. We live in a small village near bamberg where you can see the stars at night really good.

It was dark and late in the evening.
The sky was clear we could see the stars.

It was around 8:30 pm. We had a walk with our dog outside on our yard and we witnessed an bright white light in the sky straight above us. 
It was brighter than a star like a comet and it was hoovering. The light was longer then usual comets i saw at clear nights. 
The light moved from North West to South East. Its was bright like 40 seconds or longer and then it disappeared. There was no sound it only moved slightly in a straight direction.
