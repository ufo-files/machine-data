{cak} 3 bright yellow lights forming triangle shape

Whilst travelling along Leicester Road, Hinckley near to Hinckley golf course I have noticed bright lights through the tree line hovering in the direction of Earl Shilton.
Upon the tree line breaking I have seen 3 bright yellow lights forming a perfect triangle pattern. This was hovering completely still and has been viewed for around 3 minutes. Upon turning into Station Road, Earl Shilton these three lights have just vanished. 
There was airplanes in the sky which were noticeably moving. I have never seen anything like this light cluster before.
The sky was clear, the weather was fine and visibility was perfect.
