Resubmission: UFO observed ascending change direction Santiago Chile

The attached video best illustrates everything.

Basically, I was vacationing in Santiago with my cousin, when I noticed a rocket-like light ascend into the sky. At first, I thought it was a rocket but then it became apparent that it was not (I don't think Chile has rockets). The light went vertical, high into the sky and then slowly changed directions. It was uneventful except for the fact that the light ascended, slowly moved and then appeared to come back to its original position in the sky.
