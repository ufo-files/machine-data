It just flew by in a straight line.

It was 6pm and me and my classmates were still at school. We were about to go home when my friend noticed something in the sky. We both checked it out and at first we thought it was just a plane flying by but it wasn't blinking and planes blink don't they? Also, for 4 years at this school I've never seen a plane pass by, only helicopters and they blink too. It was flying in a straight direction, didn't really change directions. I was definitely dumbfounded, and amazed. It's been a long since I last saw something like that. Also, it has been so long since the last time I captured something weird in the sky so I took a video of it. It went behind our school building so I couldn't see it anymore.
