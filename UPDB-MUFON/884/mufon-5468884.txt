{jpg} Object appeared flying east then then abruptly turning south until out of sight

My friend and I were outside trying to see the planets that are supposed to be in view tonight when my friend noticed a light that he said appeared to be shaped like a bird or plane that was low enough to clearly see the shape but it wasn1t like a bird or plane he had seen before. As he saw it he alerted me. By the time I saw it it was over trees flying east and disappeared. I jogged across the street to see if I could catch another glimpse. While looking south it reappeared apparently having made a sudden direction change and seemed to be several miles away and shaped like a circle with yellow, red, green lights underneath. It traveled south until out of site in about 5 seconds.
