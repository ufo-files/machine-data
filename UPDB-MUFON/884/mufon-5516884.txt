{nd} TRAVELED SOUTH NEXT TO MT MARQUETTE AT LOW ALTITUDE,SLOW SPEED AND DISAPPEARED BEHIND MT MARQUETTE

I WAS DRIVING HOME ON US-41FROM A FRIENDS CAMP EARLY IN THE MORNING AS IT WAS GETTING LIGHT. AS I TURNED SOUTH ON THE ROAD OVERLOOKING THE UPPER HARBOR I NOTICED THE OBJECT NEAR THE POWER PLANT AND A FEW DEGREES ABOVE MY LINE OF SIGHT. IR WAS LONGER THAN THE COAL SHIPS THAT BRING COAL TO TRE UPPER HARBOR POWER PLANT. THE NEXT DAY A CALLED K I SAWYER AIR BASE AND A OFFICER CAM TO MY OFFICE AND INTERVIEWED ME. LATER IN THE MORNING A TROPPER FROM THE STATE POLICE POST ALSO INTERVIEWED ME. HE TOLD ME THERE WERE OFFICERS IN THE KEWEENAW PENINSULA THAT SAW THE OBJECT AND HAD IT ON RADAR TRAVELING AT HIGH SPEEDS,ABOUT THIRTY MINUTES BEFORE I SAW IT. I WENT TO THE STATE POLICE POST AND READ THE REPORT BETWEEN THE KEWEENAW POST AND THE MARQUETTE POST AND THEY DESCRIBED IT SIMILER TO WHAT I SAW.I ASKED FOR AN EXPLANATION IF THEY HAD ANY IDEA WHAT IT WAS,THEY SAID THEY HAD NO IDEA AND THERE OFFICERS WHO HAD SEEN IT HAD NO IDEA AND COULD NOT EXPLAIN IT. BY THE WAY I AM A PRIVATE PILOT.
