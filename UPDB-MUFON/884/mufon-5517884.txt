{nd} A small round, silver object at about 2mile elevation

At about 2:52pm Sunday June 24, 2012 I was looking at the sky from backyard toward the south. I noticed a round object about 6-7 miles south possibly over Western Queen Creek/Southern Chandler coming toward me at about 2 miles elevation. First I thought its a Helicopter but it was flying too high for a helicopter & as it got closer to about 3-4 miles of me, possibly over Baseline & country-club I noticed its round shape kind of like a golden/silver ball. As it reached about 60 degree angle over my head it suddenly started ascending until I couldnt see it anymore, at the last moment it was over 7-8 miles above the ground. The whole time I could see it was no more than 25-35 seconds.
