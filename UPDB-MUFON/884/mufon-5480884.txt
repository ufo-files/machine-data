{cak} Rapid descent of obj from space.Yellow flash@crash.Choppers later.

In Astoria, Or; My brother & I were looking at the fading light through the trees. We saw a circular, non deteriorating object, come in from space. The object's apparent size was a penny to a golfball; however, including its surrounding fireball, the apparent size was basketball. It was intensely bright. Object seemed to emit an aura intense yellow light and was additionally in a teardrop ball of flame from air friction. It fell in from approx 25 deg going straight down. We saw it fall in about 1 to 1.5 seconds. I did not think it was possible to move so fast; faster than a shooting star. About 45 seconds to a minute later we saw the same yellow light; flash up from where the crash must have been. The thing landed between Warrenton & Hammond, Oregon. This would be the last place to crash on land; before Hawaii (thousands of miles away). The nearby Coast Guard had helicopters in the air, pretty much at all times, for the following 2-3 days. Yesterday, at 1:30 pm; a black, stealth helicopter flew over me (pretty much silently) as I drove a scenic route, near the likely crash spot. The stealthy (yet obvious) helicopter, came to a hover, approx 1.5-2 miles off the town's back roads. So, that is where the big wigs came to see the, whatever. Just an F.Y.I. :-) I didn't see much. But, the military did just get a prize out here!
