{dr}Light shoots downward in seconds

I was walking to Target around 11:09 pm admiring the stars and the Orion constellation. I see a plane moving slowly across the sky. Then in matter of 1 second I see what appeared to be. star like object appear at the same altitude of the plane and shoot straight down to the ground. Everything seemed to be 4-6 miles away. Speed of the object to shoot downward surprised me.
