{cak} Observed airliner east bound with vapor trail. Observed two star like objects west bound no vapor trails at about same altitude.

I was walking my dog at a park in Gilbert, AZ. near Cooper and Warner street. My dog stopped to rest and I looked at an airliner in the western sky with a vapor trail headed East bound when I noticed these two bright star like lights at about the same height the plane was at traveling WNW without any vapor trail. I noticed that the lights were one in front of the other separated by about 1/2 inch and a little less than that from the plane. The two lights would be bright and then disappear then be bright again as they passed the plane headed west. The two lights appear and disappear at different times, not synced at all. The event lasted only about 10 min. or less before it was all over. When it was over a neighbor came to me and asked if I saw the lights in the sky and he saw the same thing as me.
