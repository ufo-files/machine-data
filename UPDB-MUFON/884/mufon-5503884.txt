It looked like a regular sized retangular garbage can. I have pictures

April 16, 2012 approx 8 am I was driving to Hollister to work. As I was driving over Pacheco Pass (HWY 152) I noticed quite a few cars pulled over off the road and people out of their cars looking up at the sky. So I pulled over too. When I looked up I saw a very small object just hovering over a mountain peak. I had my Fuji camera with me with 30X zoom so I started shooting pictures. When I got home and downloaded pics I zoomed in again and saw what looked like a large rectangular garbage can. I have the regular picture and the zoomed in one. The Merced Sunstar ran an article on the siting which many people saw and reported moving at a high rate of speed. Except when I saw it, it was completely still in one spot.
