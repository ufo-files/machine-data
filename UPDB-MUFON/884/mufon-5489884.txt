{jpg} Strange slow moving red fire like object

I was standing in the back yard on the phone when I saw the bright red light slowly rising into the sky. It caught my eye from the bright color and the movement. I first thought it was a firework going up but realized it was moving too slowly and no explosion. The object slowly rose up to about the height of low flying planes usually spotted in the area and then moved west. It appeared to be like a candle flame in shape almost and it was a very intense light. I watched it until it approached the treeline and was gone from my sight. Appeared to be growing smaller as it moved.
