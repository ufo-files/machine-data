black cube

happened in January of 2010, in Brazil in S1o Paulo, the object was a black cube that followed the electric power transmission lines that go towards S1o Paulo's congonhas direction south region of the city, I was in my house putting clothes to dry on the clothesline when I looked up and saw the object above my house, was not very tall, I was watching I was standing in the air, I even waved, but I was afraid, a fear because the object seemed to me to approach, I entered the house quickly, and then I decided to go to the house of my mother who lives in a nearby street. on the street I could see the object slowly following over the towers heading south. on the street at that time was a car from Google maps, and by coincidence recently saw the object in the street view. attached to the photo print screen.
