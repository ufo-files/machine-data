{tjd} String of lights

Leaving to work at 5:30 a.m., I looked up to the sky towards the Big Dipper when I observed a string of white lights ( approximately 30-40) flying in formation with equal distances between each other. They were traveling NE and coming from SW direction. I observed that the lights from the SW were dropping from the upper sky (space) to the sky below one at a time and continuing the formation.
