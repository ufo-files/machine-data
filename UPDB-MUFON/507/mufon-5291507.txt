Followed driving home one night

I was visiting an ex girlfriend in CO at the time and we walked out on her deck in the front yard and we observed 3 objects in the sky and she had told me about them before never really believed her till than night. We were standing there looking at 3 circular objects hovering pretty high up all 3 looked identical had rotating lights in the center of the sphere spinning. Looked like maybe a pattern but they would flash every once in awhile.
All 3 of the objects suddenly disappeared straight up into the night sky. It just got really weird when I left her house one of the objects came out of no where I left at about 10:30pm got down the road driving south towards farmington new mexico between hesperus co and red mesa coobject stayed a consistent distant behind my truck and followed me for id say It felt like an eternity but maybe a 20 min span of time if that. I could see it bobbing in and out of my review mirror and that as abrut as it showed up it flew straight off on the horizon and was gone. And about 2 years after this happened to me I did see a big ball of light come down outta the sky in farmington hover above the mesa and it disappeared into the mesa and re emerged as 5 lights after 30 mins or so from the mesa and than all 5 flew straight up into the night sky.
I didn't know who to share with scared the crap outta me and still bugs me to this day I feel like I'm being followed at night all the time and I might still be.
