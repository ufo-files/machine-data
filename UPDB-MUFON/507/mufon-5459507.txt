{tjd} saucer shaped UFO that has one red light and flashing yellow lights in windows that moved in sequence

I was driving from my house on Clarendon Drive to Strawberry Park Drive to see a friend. As I came to Doyle Road I could see a UFO hovering over Easterbrook School above the Kindergarten section. I drove up Doyle and made a left onto Marilla Dr. stopped my car in front of a house across from the school and watched the craft hover. Then after about a minute it rose up slightly and SLOWLY cruised over and just above the houses towards Williams Rd. I followed it up Marilla Dr. and stopped at Williams Rd. A guy in a truck stopped at my left, rolled down his passenger window and asked me if I was seeing what he was seeing and I said "Yes, we are seeing a UFO "and he said "I'm getting the F**K Out of here" and sped off. I followed the craft over to my friend's house on Strawberry Park Drive..and ran in the house to get her..I don't even remember driving there since I watched it the whole time. My friend and I saw it and ran down to the corner of Strawberry Park Lane and Saratoga Ave. It put on a "display" for about an hour and a half. People driving up Saratoga Avenue stopped and saw it , as did some police that stopped to see what the commotion was about. I have never heard or seen any information out there about this night. This started at 10:00 pm on July 8, 1976. The Mercury News reported sightings on Lawrence Expressway at Doyle (police reported seeing this too) and also UFOs flying past the Air Traffic Control at San Jose Airport within a week (or two) later...but I can't find that news anywhere as well. Does anyone have ANY information or experience as I did during this time?
