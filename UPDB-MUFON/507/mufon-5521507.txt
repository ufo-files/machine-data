Driving home from town saw it over the Whites sands military range mountains

Was driving home from getting gas for my parents in their car when i turned west on Schoeller Pl when i noticed a very orange starlike object over the mountains between Northe Las Cruces and North Tularosa, I continued to my house as i watched it brighten in intensity and then decrease in brightnes. I pulled up to my house and alerted my family, my Mother,sister, niece and nephew, we all stood on the raised deck and watched the object brighten and dim then change colors before dimming out completey after 20 minutes of being stationary not to return the rest of the night. I experienced a feeling of seeing an ol friend.
