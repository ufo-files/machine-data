Very bright white light that was ball/star shaped

The only reason I am posting this is because I saw similar postings. My husband, sister along with myself were sitting on the back patio Monday night around 9:30PM and I saw a very bright white light coming from the west, heading east, shaped like a star. It appeared to be wobbling, almost traveling snakelike across the sky. I was looking straight up at it and it was probably between 500 and a 1000 feet up. I told my sister and husband to look at it and asked them if they thought it was a plane because I didn't. They didn't know what it was either and it made no noise. We studied it for about a minute, the my husband started to make fun of me about it being a UFO but I told him it looked like it was wobbling and he said,"yeah, you're right, it is." As it moved more east it suddenly dimmed then completely dissappeared. We had observed a plane traveling west at the same time and knew what that was immediately. We sat there for a few minutes discussing what we saw and we don't have a clue. It was such a brillant bright light and the experienced lasted around 4 or 5 minutes. We never saw it reappear. I wish I had got a photo of it but by the time I thought about it, it had disappeared.
