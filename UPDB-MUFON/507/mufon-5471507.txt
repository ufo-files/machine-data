{rjl} 4 glowing orbs seen over desert. 2 orbs disappeared and other 2 traveled 10 miles then met up with 2 more orbs

As I was driving home, I noticed four glowing orbs hovering over the desert near a store. 2 orbs disappeared, so I drove south approximately 10 miles and saw that the other two were parallel with me. I stopped my car (another car had already pulled over) and took some really bad pictures with my phone. They continued to move south so I followed then took a road heading west and they looked like were hovering over an abandoned mine. As I watched, two more orbs appeared. Just as quickly, they disappeared and the other 2 descended a little lower then disappeared. Screaming and excited, I waited then drove home approx. 4 miles away. The orbs I saw were in exactly the same place I had seen them a couple weeks after the Phoenix lights (which I also witnessed in Casa Grande, AZ). They were large, round and looked almost like they were on fire. They looked like they were slanted at times and also turned into just one orb a couple of times. I am a strong believer of UFOs and I was thrilled to witness this almost hour long event!!
