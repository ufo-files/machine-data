Bright shooting green light low in sky

Bright flare like object traveling east to west in vicinity of Rte 108/Rte 33 in Stratham NH. Low in sky (maybe 1000 ft) witnessed over a distance of maybe 2 miles before object appeared to burn-out. We were traveling at aprox 50 MPH, object estimated at aprox 100 MPH+. I've never seen a green flare that could travel such a distance, but that is what it looked like.
