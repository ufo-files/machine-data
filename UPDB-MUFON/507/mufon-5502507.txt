{rjl} Quick Brightening of star like object and then faded out

Was looking at Big Dipper and by the last star in the handle "ALKAID" an object appeared about 3 fingers width away at arms length and slowly brighten till it was brighter than Mars. The object then started to dim out and eventually disappeared with a slight flash. Tried looking for it for another 5 minutes but the object never reappeared. Thought it may have been a meteor flying straight at me due to the way it just suddenly appeared and disappeared in the same spot with a slight flash. The flash at the end seemed to seal the deal for me that this was indeed a meteor or space junk entering the atmosphere and burning up. Just figured I would report this in case someone saw the same thing.
