(GS) Unknown, light blue light in distance

It was 9pm on Thursday 8/9/12, and I had just stepped outside to enjoy the evening air. I looked south and saw a light blue light in the distance, probably about 10 miles south of my home, above the Santa Monica Mtns. 

Im pretty savvy when it comes to things in the sky. I know airplanes, stars, constellations, satellites. This was none of these. I knew right away it wasnt a plane. Then I thought it was a shooting star/meteorite, since the Perseid meteor shower is upon us. Then I thought it was remnants of a firework. I also thought maybe a flare. Those thoughts quickly vanished after what happened next.

The object was pulsating and hovering (I actually thought it was moving towards me, hence the lack of movement). It wasnt blinking like a plane, it didn't have different colored beacons like a plane has. It had a consistant color of light blue light that would get dim, then grow bright. Probably about as bright as Venus gets. It did this for about a minute and a half. All of a sudden, the light shifted to the left a bit, then did a quick loop up, then fell quickly down towards the left in what looked like a controlled movement. It didn't appear to crash or anything, it all looked purposeful. I lost eye contact with it as it went behind trees.

My thought was, maybe it is a radio controlled plane with lights on it. However, this light did not look close to me at all. As I said, it appeared to be about 10 miles south of my location in the west San Fernando Valley, above the Santa Monica Mtns. or so. 

Of course, being that Im pretty good with things I see in the night sky, this event has me completely nonplussed.
