{tjd} 15 or more lights following each other

On Feb 8th at about 5:45 in the morning I stepped out on my front porch to have a cigarette as I do not smoke in the house I looked up to the western sky and saw a row of lights moving west to east. They were moving in a formation following each other and spaced evenly apart. I counted 15 but there could have been more. As they pasted to the eastern sky they disappeared. It took they about 3 to 4 minutes to go from west to east.
