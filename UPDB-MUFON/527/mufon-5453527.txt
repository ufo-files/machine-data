{rjl} Lights over Harrington

While in my backyard with a friend I just happened to see something out of the corner of my eye. I turned my head and saw two solid lights aligned with each other but not the same object being followed by a third that had blinking lights like a plane. As they got closer we started to hear the noise of a jet that was following the other two lights which had no sound. I lost them in a tree and went around the tree to find them. All that could be seen when I came around the tree was the one blinking light from the trailing jet. Please excuse the language of my friend. He was a little excited.
