Flight Pattern - Very unusual pattern of objects high above

I usually am not up this early but I was looking at the night sky. I thought I was witnessing the space shuttle passing overhead because it was moving at a high rate of speed and it was pretty bright in the sky. Then I noticed another object in the same trajectory moving but not as bright. I thought it might be a satellite. I turned to the north and looked at the stars and noticed several of them moving. Then I realized a lot of them were moving, more than 20. I was shocked because they were much higher than the airplanes fly and if they are that high they don't have lights on. Every one of the objects could be seen with lights. If I had to say they had a shape, I would say it was triangular. I cannot be sure though. They were too far away. Some were brighter and larger than others. They were all traveling the same direction and at a high rate of speed. One moment they were there and the next they were gone.
