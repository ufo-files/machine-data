{cak} Five glowing pinpricks of light, alternating copper and silver in color, hovering, converging and then moving apart in the clear late-morning sky.

Three witnesses observed five glowing pinpricks of light, alternating copper and silver in color, hovering, converging and then moving apart in the clear late-morning sky. They were still there when we had to depart and return to work. We were in the Walk of Fame area of Hollywood, and the lights appeared in the north-eastern direction. We went inside and recruited several other coworkers, who also observed the lights, but thought they might be mylar balloons. They didn't strike me as being balloons - they were too bright to have resulted from only reflected sunlight, and they appeared to be quite far away.
