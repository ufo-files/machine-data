{nd} A bright light rejecting and accepting smaller bright lights that remained for several hours then suddenly at dawn disappeared into the clouds

Three of us were sitting on the front deck and saw this bright light, just blow the clouds. There was a moon that night. As we watched this very bright light just under the clouds, it started emitting and receiving smaller bright orbs of bright white objects. Upon dawn, it changed directions to the right and to the left, extremely fast, and suddenly went straight up into the clouds and disappeared when the Sun started to rise on the horizon. This was observed by all of us for 3-4 hours.
