Bright star moving in the sky

I was fishing with my flatmate and noticed a bright star, which seemed to be moving, I have pointed it out to my flatmate and asked,if it's in my eyes or really it's moving. My flatmate replayed: it's definitely moving, what is it? At that point I pulled out my phone and started to record and replayed that I don't know as was no eny signs of navigation lights and sound. The brightness and color of object was constant as it approaches, passed and disappeared in distance.
About an hour and a half later, the same looking and at similar altitude star like object was moving from west to east straight over us, I have pulled out my phone again and recorded video, took zoomed in photo and another video as it was disappearing into the east.
No navigation lights or sound in both instances, never before seen any thing like it. As a hobby I fly rc airplanes, helicopters and drones and I am sure that it was not any of these or any conventional aircraft I know.
