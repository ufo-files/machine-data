(GS) Bright white pulsating light witnessed by at least ten people,for about 5 minutes.

Witnessed by at least ten people on South Avenue, Rochester, NY. Originally thought to be an airplane but the consistently bright white lights pulsated and there was no landing gear or noise. The object was going faster than a commercial plane would. It went from one side of the horizon all the way to the other side in a mostly arc type path. The object was about the size of an aspirin in the sky but seemed FAR away. Me and my friend who were going to the store to get cigarettes, stopped to witness the event and it lasted for about 5 minutes. Others stopped and noticed the object. They were drunk and cited that it probably was a satelite. Albeit a very bright and fast satelite. My friend and I were sober and definitely disagree. This was in our opinion not a known object or satelite.
