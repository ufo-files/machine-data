{cak} Traveling fast and low emitted pulse of blue light for a second or two then was gone

Husband and I were in the hot tub. Sky was very clear. Then cloud cover came in from the north, just a few low mushroom shaped clouds came into view. My husband said the clouds looked odd, 30 seconds later I was looking up toward the west where the clouds rolled in and saw a blue light that suddenly appeared in the sky. It was not very large, but just above the tree line and the light was only there for a few seconds but long enough for me to observe that it was traveling very fast. It made no sound. It was traveling south and perhaps a little to the west. It was orb like pulse of blue light that was traveling to low and fast to be a plane. But it was visible for only a moment and my husband did not see it.
