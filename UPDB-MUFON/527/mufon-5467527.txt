{dr}200 orbs entered atmosphere, followed by moon sized craft & approx. 1000 smaller craft. Within 1 mi. of impact, all craft instantaneously vanished.

I was fishing at Lake Limestone , along with 2 other people, at approx. 1:30 AM. We observed the sky lighting up with the approach of moon shaped orbs entering our atmosphere. Approx. 200 of these entered our atmosphere, immediately followed by a moon sized craft, then 1000 smaller ones followed the largest. All craft appeared to be encased in a transparent globe. The craft had flames licking off of the sides. None of the flames left the encasement globe. When the lead craft were approx. 1 mile above the ground, they all instantaneously disappeared, in the blink of an eye.  They left a smoke trail behind.
