{nd} Witnessed a silver disk craft flying low over fields near Dixon

I was riding in a greyhound bus alone, on I-80 in 1976. I was 14 years old and was going to visit my Dad in Vallejo from Sacramento, Ca. I was sitting on the bus next to the window facing East looking out over the fields when I saw a disk shaped craft flying West over the farms toward the hills in the West, it was about house high off the ground, it was a bright day so I could see the shadow under it, it was flying slowly about 20 or 30 MPH. It slowly flew over the bus and the freeway and disappeared over the hills in the west. I looked around at the strangers on the bus to see if they noticed it but no one said anything, I think the bus was full. I never told anyone about it for many years, I figured they would not believe me. I have been interested in finding any of the many people who I know saw it that day either on the bus or on the highway.
