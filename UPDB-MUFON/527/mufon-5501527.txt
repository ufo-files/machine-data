{dr}Red Orb

I was at my aunt's house, and my mom sent me text telling me to come home because we were going to have a bonfire. As we (me, mom, dad, aunt) were sitting around the fire, I was looking up at the sky trying to find stars and constellations I learned about last year in my astronomy class. A red orb appeared out of nowhere and slowly drift to the north for about 6 minutes before it disappeared. We all watched in amazement as it just disappeared. It was a solid red color. I attempted to take a photo with my smartphone that takes good low light photos, but it would not show up in the pictures.
