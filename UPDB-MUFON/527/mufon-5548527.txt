Dull white oval moving at incredible speed, paused, then shot off to the west

GF and I are on vacation in Tucson, sitting outside at the Westward Look Resort looking up at the stars. we both observed a dull white oval object moving east to west at an incredible speed, it slowed, dipped slightly, then accelerated again to the west. No fireball observed, no tail..(i know what a falling star and space debris looks like). This object was moving way too fast for aircraft and no noise was heard. The speed of this object was incredible..gf and i looked at each other speechless and both of us said at the same time "what the hell was that"?
