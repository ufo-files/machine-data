reflective disk directly overhead observed through 10x50 binoculars

The sun was clearly reflected by the object with details of the sun with similar patterns as in recent sun pictures. When I first noticed the object it appeared white like a spotlight and reddened as the sun set. I watched it for several minutes through the binoculars (10x50) and it was crisp and clear with solar detail much like recent solar photographs I have seen. After about 5 minutes of observation We had to leave and it was gone 12 minutes later. The object was directly overhead (90deg.) and not moving as I proped the binoculars against a fixed object and it did not move.
