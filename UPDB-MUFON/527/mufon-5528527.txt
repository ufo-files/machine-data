Orb/Sphere Shaped UFO over Bloomginton

I was walking across a courtyard on the Indiana University campus with a friend and glanced up for no reason--it was cloudy out. I saw this orb-shaped object moving at a good 20-30mph across the sky. It appeared to have one light on the front (similar to an eyeball). The white was a white or beige color, but the craft itself glowed dimly a pinkish orange color. It moved in a very direct, straight path below the clouds, but higher than the treetops. I would estimate it to be about the size of a regular SUV. I would NEVER consider myself a UFO person, not even with an "open mind". But this UFO startled me and my friend and i followed it, running, for a couple yards trying to figure out whether or not it was a UFO. There was no way it could be a balloon--because of the front beam/light it had. It finally went out of sight over trees and into the far distance. We were able to watch it for about 2-3 minutes. It never occurred to me to report it until i was trying to tell my mother, who asked me to look it up, but no other sightings were reported, unfortunately.
