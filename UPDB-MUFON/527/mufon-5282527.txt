2 white tic tac objects observed, one travelling after the other. (video evidence of one)

I was driving up the road, heading to my parent's house, and an object caught my eye. It didn't look like a plane or helicopter. I saw no wings at all on it and I was confused as to what I was looking at. I thought about getting video of it, but it was too far away. As I was getting ready to get back into my car, I saw another object following at a distance behind the first one. I quickly took my cellphone out and took a few pictures of the object, then began recording video. After a short period, I stopped recording and took more pictures. The object stayed in view for about 2-3 minutes, then disappeared out of view, by getting too far away to see. (at the beginning of the video, you can hear me say "there's another one..." - it looked exactly like the first one I had missed getting footage of)
