{dr}USS Constellation UFO

Between the spring and summer in the year 1994, something showed up on radar on the USS Constellation at a rapid speed while in the waters west of San Diego. By what the radar was showing, the crew expected impact!
Then the island watchers station crew began screaming. A large light could be seen hovering over the island then shot straight up into the sky and out of sight. 
All watchers were immediately debriefed. Witness was asked what his thoughts were, he stated alien, and was asked to log it and forget it.
