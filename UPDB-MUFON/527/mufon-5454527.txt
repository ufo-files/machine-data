Glowing white object in Rotterdam sky

Yesterday evening around 7:54pm (GMT+1), bright sky, no clouds, I noticed from my window a bright white object in the sky moving from East to West over southern Rotterdam, with a brightness comparable to the brightest star in the sky, Sirius (pls. see attached MVI_9492 0036.jpg for comparison). Straightforward path W-E, constant speed, unwavering but very intense white light, no blinking position lights, absolutely no sound. Wind from SW at 15-20 km/h, ground temperature around 5 °C. As soon as the object disappeared from my sight beyond the nearest building, I checked on Flightradar24: no planes for miles and miles around the whole city of Rotterdam in all directions (NB: the local airport is currently closed due to the COVID-19 outbreak). Photos and videos were taken with a Canon PowerShot SX20 IS, only using the optical zoom.
