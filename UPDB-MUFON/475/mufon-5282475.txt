Lights over neighbours house ( reds, oranges , yellows, blues, whites )

I was about 12 years old , one night lots of colours of light lit up my bedroom , when I pulled back curtains ,I witnessed a huge ball of lights hovering over the roof of a neighbours house . My first thought was the house was on fire . I looked at the lights for 5-10 mins . I then closed my curtains . I decided to try and get to sleep , then some 5-10 mins later a pure white light appeared on the eve cupboard door opposite my bed.
The shape was of a triangle and on top was a circle ,joined as one , the light was solid and stayed for over 10 mins , the door was about 3 feet square ,the shape filled the door edges . I spoke to the light asking what it wanted ... I repeated this up to 5 times ... no reply from the light .
