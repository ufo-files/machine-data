We were driving West on Samaria Road, between Summerfield and White Ford roads when we noticed two brighter than normal stars. As we approached our driveway, the stars were shining brighter and brighter as if they were traveling straight toward us. They got so bright we decided to pursue, so we continued driving West. By the time we crossed Whiteford road and stopped the car, the lights were about 1000 feet from us and still coming closer. Lynda and myself got out of, but stayed close to the car. One of the light turned north and slowly moved away. The lights were so bright that there were no structural details. The other moved slowly toward us, came down at a sharper angle and hovered at treetop level almost directly above us. By this time, Kathy was begging us to leave, and then the light started to rise and move north. We drove back home, a quarter miles, told our family what happened, and my brother and I hopped on his motorcycle. The direction the light was moving when we left it, took it over an old quarry to the north of where we had our siting. We went back to the quarry on the motorcycle and just caught sight of it as it disappeared over the trees.
