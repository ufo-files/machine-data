{cak} Went to neighbors house to borrow sugar when I heard a fog horn that scared me. Then saw the ufo

When I was but a child of around 5 years old my sisters and I witnessed something amazing! My mother was in the kitchen baking a cake that evening when she realized we were out of sugar. She sent my sisters and me to our neighbor's house to borrow some sugar. We reached their home and had already knocked on their sliding glass side door when the ear splitting sound of a fog horn (at least that is what it sounded like to me) pierced the relative quiet of our rural street. The sound scared me so much and it made me, of course, turn my head to see where the sound had came from. When I looked my periferal caught the object so I looked up, straight up! There hovering only about 100' above my house was a golden egg shaped craft roughly the size of a mini bus standing vertically with a myriad of flashing very colorful lights all over it. The fear I felt disappeared and was replaced with absolute astonishment as I had never seen this type of craft before. It hovered there without a sound other than the initial fog horn alert. I got to see it for only about 3 seconds before my big sister plowed me over when she ran inside my neighbor's house dragging me and my other sister with her. By the time I picked myself off the floor and peered out of the glass door it had already gone without a sound. This event burned into my memory vividly. The craft looked very similar to my grandmother's fabrog1 eggs she collected. My sisters remember this but not as clearly as I do.
