{ck} Red glowing object in sky over Virginia Beach Va

I was sitting out on the porch of my home and at approx. 11.45pm on July 6th 2012 a red glowing object high in the sky caught my attention as it was very bright and at first I thought it was a plane on fire. The object was teardrop shape and looked as though it was on fire with something trailing beneath it. There was no noise apparent. It followed an arcing direction and I lost sight of it after approx. 2 mins. over the horizon. It appeared to be fairly large as it was very visible in the sky and looked very high up. I called my husband to come and see it as I was not sure what it was and wanted his opinion. He saw it very visibly and was not sure what we were looking at - whether it was a weather balloon, plane on fire or something else.
