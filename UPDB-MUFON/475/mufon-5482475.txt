{nd} Saw object, Bright light flashed, Object was gone

I was sitting on my deck at about 6:00 in the evening when I looked up and saw a silver object in the sky. I live on a flight path for planes landing at the airport so I figured it was a plane. It didn't appear to be moving so I thought it was just coming straight toward me. A white light flashed on the left side and then right in the center of the object a very very bright white light came on and grew until you couldn't see the object anymore and then went off. When it turned off the object was gone and there was nowhere it could go without being seen. The sky was crystal clear and there were no clouds. It just disappeared. Has anyone ever reported anything like this before? I'm really curious what it may have been and can't stop thinking about it. If you may have any idea I would like to here from you.
