{cak} The object was just hovering near downtown Buffal. Then stated moving toward the north stoping at times to hover changing color.

My son and I were coming home from a ride when I spotted something in the sky stationary, just hovering. I knew it was not a plane or helicopter because it was two small and quiet. It began to move at different speed from very slow to just appearing in another space. It moved from west to north. I was afraid at first but I had to stay there and record it until it disappeared.
