Immediately behind UFO Crash

We were a band driving from Phoenix, AZ to Denver, CO traveling up I-25 somewhere around Santa Fe or Albuquerque, NM during the siting. Unfortunately I was one of two people asleep in the back. I woke up to gasps from the driver and co-pilot passengers. They saw multiple white SUVs, with only red lights, escorting a cylindrical space ship, with no windows. It appeared very reflective, metallic, almost blueish looking because of its reflectivity.  it was being escorted in a flatbed by the convoy of SUVs southbound on 25. We later passed the apparent crash site where the guard rail was completely burnt and torn through.
