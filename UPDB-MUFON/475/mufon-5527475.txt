Two bright lights originating from north traveling southeast

At approximatly 7:00PM tonight i decided to step outside for a cigarette. As per usual i figured i go out back and see if it was clear enough to check out some stars, and bam crystal clear sky. So upon scanning the sky i noticed two stars that seemed to move from their location. At first i thought it was a visual perception trick, so i looked around got a fix on some other stars and looked back at the two original stars. Dont you know it they did the same thing. So i scanned around the sky again real quick and still none of the other stars were platying this trick on me. Now i decided i will focus all concentration on these two stars, and don't you know they were definatly moving. Both shown with the intensity of a very bright star, and there was no blinking as with an airplane, or redness as with a satellite. What made the whole thing even weirder was that they stayed the same distance apart the entire time they were visable, which was about 4 minutes moving from a north to southeast diection, and then just flickered out long before we should have been able to not see them. And yes i said we because once i realized what was happening i went and grabbed my roommates just to make sure there was something, and all agree that it was unlike anything they have seen before. This was probably the coolest thing i have seen to date in correlation with my want to beleive, hands down. One of my roomates tried to get a video on his phone but it didn't come out at all :(
