I was at home rolling up the widows in my jeep. I was sitting in the seat looking aout the sun roof at the moon. I noticed that the moon was out early and it was full. When I fist saw it I thought that maybe it was some sort of rocket. It was heading right toward the moon. I went from left to right in the sky. It moved very slow at first and I was wondering if it was possable to see a rocket or ship heading towards the moon with the naked eye. It got faster as it passed by the moon in the sky. I realized it was not a rocket or a ship of any kind. It stopped as it got passed the moon and stood still. I was still tring to figure out what it was. It stayed on one spot for a good 2-3 mins and the it shot off at a high rate of speed to the right.
