I was driving home when I seen what I thought was a helicopter, except the lights were too far apart and the back light wasnt blinking. The two solid lights were flying at a decent pace, I quickly rolled my windows down to see if I could hear anything. but it was silent, no noise at all. The lights passed over my house and there were gone. Ive never seen anything like that fly over before. I cant imagine it being a helicopter for a number of reasons. Like I said before the lights were too far apart for a helicopter, the objects seemed to be moving too fast for a helicopter, and it was too low for it to be a commercial airplane. The absent of noise also threw me off. I walked inside and on my computer I searched a short description of what I had seen. The Blue Springs, Missouri reportings seem to be something with similar characteristics. Except the two lights were moving.
