{cak} It was at night, I had to walk my dog and looked up in the sky and saw the ufo's

I went outside to let my dog do his business and I always like to look up at the sky. When I looked around I saw five orbs in the sky fairly far away. They were like a reddish color, also in a v shape but sideways and the point was facing in the direction they were moving. I followed them with my eyes until trees and house wouldn't let me see them. They were going way faster than any airplane I have ever seen. And there was not a sound coming from them.
