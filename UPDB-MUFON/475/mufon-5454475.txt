String of lights across the sky

We saw a string of lights moving across the sky at very high altitude at 6:15 am on 3/25/20.

I looked up and thought the stars were moving. Once I realized there was a straight line of lights across the sky I quickly traced to the start of the line where I saw more appearing one at a time to my Southeast. It seemed as if it was very close to my location that they were appearing. 
As soon as a light would appear it seemed as if it was already traveling along this path. 

This event went on for about 5-7 min. I could see them appear, travel the line and then disappear a long ways off. If I had to guess I would say the lights disappeared somewhere over Missouri.

After the event had been over for at least 5 min or more at the original point of the sky where the lights started 1 of the "stars" that was in that location went directly south. So what I thought the whole time was just a star right around the entry area ended up being another one of these lights and took a southern flight path but it wasn't constant. I seemed to slow down and speed up until i couldn't see it anymore. 

There were 3 eye witnesses to the original event at 6:15 but I am the only one who saw the last light move south.

I've seen a few UFOs in my time. This was the only multi-object event I have ever seen, and lasted longer than anything I've seen before. 

This event seems to be happening often. I say this because the most recent sighting on this website was in a town east of here this morning detailing the same thing I saw. And after looking at the UFO tracker on MUFON I see that along this same flight path on the 5th of March 2020 at least 2 folks from Missouri reported a very similar thing. 

Wow! The government is really up to something.
