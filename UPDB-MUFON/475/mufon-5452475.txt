Object changed speed and direction rapidly and multiple times near Chicago skyline

First noticed a white light rapidly accelerating by and heading toward city skyline. Object going much faster than a satellite and rapidly accelerating/decelerating unlike any plane. Approached city skyline (southern side), then suddenly stopped moving and hovered in the sky. Made several u-turn type changes of direction, and increased and decreased in brightness/light intensity as moved around skyline. It got pretty close to the Sears tower for some if it's final moments and did more directional changes/hovers before descending in the SE part of the sky near the lake and Indiana/Illinois border.
