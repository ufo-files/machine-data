Space ship appears above my home and does scientific experimentation

I was walking my zebra outside my house when my futuristic watch went off. I checked it and it told me that there were anomalies w/in the vicinity. I looked towards the stars and they weren't there. A bright light flashed in my eyes and I felt weightless. All went dark after that. I was scared. I awoke to find my body dismemebered and my head inside a jar. They had my body on a table and were prodding at it w/ their strange devices. One saw that I had regained consiousness and injected a dark fluid into my jar. I next awoke in my bed and had the strangest feeling to take over the human race.
