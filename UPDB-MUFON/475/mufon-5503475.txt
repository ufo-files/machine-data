{cak} Two rectangle like lights darting back and forth then straight up and gone

I was standing facing our lake to the north....the red moon was in the south sky not visible from where I was standing. I was smoking a cigarette when I saw a reflection on the melting ice on the lake. I looked up and immediatly grabbed my cell phone and snapped over 30 pictures of which only caught 5 images since they kept moving. It made my heart skip a beat, the last picture especially was when the two lights became one and shot straight up in the sky and gone.
