{tjd} Hovered over Mather Field AFB Rancho Cordova

"Summer 1969" My mother (now dead) and I were out in our backyard talking when I noticed a Diamond shaped object hovering about 4 miles NE of our position and I asked mom, do you see that ? she answered yes, what is that ? I answered I don't know. I asked her to watch it while I went in and got my 40 power Sears telescope. We both looked at it through the telescope. It hovered for about 10 min. with red pulsating lights on the top and bottom of a diamond shaped craft, hard to tell it's size as it was about 4 miles away, while it was hovering my mom went in the house and called the local weather station. They told her that they new nothing about it. They said they would call Mather AFB and ask them if there was some sort aircaft testing, they replied no, no tests going on. The weather station man called us back and asked my mom "do you want to report this as a UFO sighting", she replied YES, please do. After 5 more min. went by, we looked again through my telescope and it was gone. Interesting to note: Mather Field AFB was the home of a B-52 Atomic Bomb group at that time !
 I was watching a UFO program "Unidentifide" last week and a man described the same shape and size, and lights of a UFO on the program hovering over an AFB in the NE, USA. I had never heard a description of a UFO that matched the one we saw. It all came back to me, prompting me to report it.
