1st night camping at Horne Lake Park

Very clear skies!
Shortly after midnight, saw this bright shining intense light. It flashed very bright, and it did not seem to be moving, rather hovering. It flashed the bright light 8 times, not intermittently and not rhythmic such as a plane. It did not move on a trajectory as a satellite or plane. We were very curious. 
When we decided to go to bed, I looked at the sky again toward the location where we previously saw the sighting. Again, it flashed 4 more times, I called my husband and he was able to see 2 of the flashes, in the same location, and then it disappeared. We went to our tent, which has an open ceiling (with only a mesh for bugs) to allow us to see the stars from bed. I could not sleep, feeling as if the object was still there. At about 3:30 am, I asked in my mind if it was still there to show us, and again it flashed 10 more times, as we counted it, and asked for another one, and so on, until we were satisfied it was not a coincidence, and then it disappeared. We have been watching the skies for a while now, and we are familiar with the aps which assist us to identify satellites, meteor showers, commercial flights, international space station, planets/ stars, and so on.
This was very different.
