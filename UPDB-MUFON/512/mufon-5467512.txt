{dr}Total of five objects hovering, forming straight lines, circles, and both increasing and decreasing in brightness

We were sitting on our balcony, having some drinks and chatting. We looked out into the distance because we saw some fireworks. Then we saw 5 large bright objects far in the distance. For a period of like 20-25 minutes, they moved forming shapes (circles, lines) and changing in brightness from dim to incredibly bright. There were many planes that passed through the area with distinctive passenger craft red lighting, but these objects were blinking but never demonstrated any suggestion of passenger or military craft. The entire spectacle was sort of eerie, with these objects being so far but so bright. The entire event was a bit unnerving, sort of like seeing something out of a movie. It was far enough that we didn't worry, but it was certainly a strange occurance.
