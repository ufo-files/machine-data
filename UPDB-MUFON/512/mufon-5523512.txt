One bright orb follwed by Three bright Orbs...Followed by Saucer Shaped Object

Event took place during HS football game at Caserma Ederle, Vicenza, Italy at approximately 20:30 on 7 Oct 2011. Saw a very Bright Orange-Red Orb in the sky...Did not see it arrive or leave. It moved slowly..Initially thought it was a Plane to to a flashing red light. But was not characteristic of a plane because the flashing light was not apart from the bright orb...Almost undulating. Was not a flare. The Orb moved similar to a plane. Initially dismissed the object and returned to watching the football game. Approximately 25 minutes to 30 minutes later (21:00). The more orbs...the same description as the first were seen. The orbs appeared one after the other...one behind the other...Low on the horizon...South moving North towards our location...At first I thought possibly military jet aircraft with Afterburners...The moved slow but study...as the approached us...(maybe a mile) the objects rose straight up then slowly arched towards the West..But rising straight in tho the sky....One following the other but space apart...No noise was heard...However there was a football game going on...As the rose...Each one then dimmed to a pinpoint light in the sky...and disappeared...First one, then the other..and finally the third. Shortly after they disappeared...I noticed a dim object towards the west...About a 1/4 mile away drifting east at a study pace...Initially I thought this was a balloon...But is was study and moving in a straight line and a study speed...There was no bobbing as with balloons...It was Saucer shaped...Could not tell if it was emitting light or if light was being reflected off of it...The Silhouette was not black...But a very faint brown or dull orange..It was lighter than the background, night sky...It move steady but quickly passing from my right to my left and disappearing over the horizon at tree level...I saw it go behind the trees on the horizon approximately one mile away. the sized remained the same as it move towards the horizon. Total time of this was about 15-20 minutes. Weather was clear. Temp: 55 degrees. Slight or no breeze.
