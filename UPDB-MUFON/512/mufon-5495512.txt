At about 8:35 pm, 3/12/15, to the NE of La Luz, NM, at maybe 40 miles away was a large orange light that went out and then reappeared in a leftward direction...a series of giant orange lights...I counted eight...before they vanished...all of the lights observed were of the exact same size and shape...and as a US Army veteran of the Vietnam -era, I have seen aerial flares before...these were massive in comparison to what I have seen anywhere...duration of sighting: 12 seconds...I felt horrified at first...and then calmed down enough to file this report.
