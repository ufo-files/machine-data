At a 9/11 Memorial, Cape May Point N.J., B4 ceremony a large helicopter flew over area. During ceremony noticed object in front of a bright white cloud, no sound, no lights, no movement. A few other people noticed it in the sky, approx over Cape May Beach, west of Coast Guard base. A thunder storm rolled in over the bay as ceremony ended.
