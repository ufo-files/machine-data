{cak} Looked like fire balls. Thought they might be.  Until they changed elevation and slight directional change

Driving north bound on Zebulon Road. In front of me I saw 2 fire balls. Looked like possible meteors but tails of fire were intermittent. Objects zig zagged somewhat changing directions. Like going away from each other then heading towards each other. After 40-60 seconds on just blinked out and within another 10-15 seconds the second blinked out. They never seemed like they were getting closer or farther. Sort of moved like planes in an air show.  I’m 52 years old. I’ve seen military aircraft. I’ve seen planes and flares- THESE WERE NOT FLARES OR ANY TYPE OF CRAFT THAT IVE EVER SEEN. I WOULD HAVE TO SAY THIS IS FROM NOT OF THIS WORLD.  I would swear on a stack of bibles of what I’ve seen.
