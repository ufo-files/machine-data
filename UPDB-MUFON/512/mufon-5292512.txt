Round, black

A few days ago I saw what I thought to be a single black balloon drifting across the Toronto skyline and really didn't think much of it. Just a singular "balloon" all black, around 7 pm in the evening, and drifting eastward. Two days later, see something glimmer in the evening sky at 8 pm, and see several of these black balls. I got my binoculars and counted 7 of them. There were two individual pairs that distanced apart from one another in a sort of rectangle shape and drifting Westwards, while at the back of the four individuals there were 3 what looked ot be bunched together, hence why I thought they were balloons, but I could not see a string that could've been holding them together. As I continued to follow the ones up front, they began to take on a sort of boomerang shape. I continued to watch them up until I lost them in the setting sun.
