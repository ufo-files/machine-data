{nd} Not sure what it was, we noticed it when we were watching fireworks, we thought it could have been a drone.

There were 5 of us adults and 2 children watching the fireworks from our 7th floor balcony. One of us noticed a red stationary red ball in the sky just to the right of the fireworks somewhat higher in the sky. It just hovered and did not move,we thought it was a plane and continued to watch it. It suddenly went in a straight flight pattern and turned green and then red a few more times as it flew east and hovered again, then it swiftly flew out of sight behind some buildings and went southeast where we lost sight of it. Couldn't have been a helicopter or a plane. A couple of minutes later we did see a plane, could have been coast guard jet (going really fast) fly in the direction of where the object was headed when we last saw it.
