My wife and I were sitting in our garage talking about something and then we both stopped. We saw what we thought was a plan but it was moving way too fast to be a plane. Then it got bright and stopped, turn back around and then took off back where it came from. We watched it do that 3-4 times. We recorded a couple of them. Then we went upstairs and got our daughter and jumped into the roof. Then we watched it again. This time it started off as a bright green object covering the same path but as it got higher in altitude it changed colors to a dark red. It fly past it’s previous turnaround spot and kept going for about 5 more seconds before turning back around sharply. Then it flew back and hovered over a certain area before dropping below the tree line. About 15 mins after than we heard and saw a helicopter flying into that same area. And it’s still going now!
