{dr}floating glowing flame-like orb that crossed the night sky.

My husband and I were on our evening walk in our neighborhood. On our final leg of our walk in our development we saw this bright glowing light through he trees. As the trees cleared we could see clearly this oval shaped orb that appeared to be glowing red/orange/yellow, slightly fuzzy and flame like around the edges. It was high in the sky, below the cloud cover and as its direction was coming towards us it looked like it was descending then slowly rose and continued is gentle, slow, floating flight pattern from the north to the south east as it disappeared from view, all without any noise. We had no idea what it was and would really like to know if anyone else saw this.
