Light appeared didn't seem to be moving fast

On the way back from the Coorong along the 42 Mile Crossing, my wife and I noticed what appeared to be a low speed shooting star. As there was going to be shooting stars that night we thought is was pretty cool. To describe what we saw was a bright orange ball with a tail. As we had the dash cam we hoped it was captured so we could view it latter. The next day when looking through the videos I noticed that there were other videos that had a object in them that didn't appear to be moving very fast at all. Like this one that I'm uploading that looks as if the object just appears in the left of screen about halfway up and then goes into a cloud.
Be interesting to know what you think, meteor, ufo, reflection, swamp gas.
