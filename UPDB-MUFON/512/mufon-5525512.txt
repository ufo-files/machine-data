Silver saucer shaped object hovers just above the trees in Ft.Lewis Washington after Mt. St. Helen erupts

Im an army brat. I was living in North Ft Lewis, Washington state in a community called Beachwood. A couple weeks after Mt St Helen erupted I was fishing on a small pond called Sears Lake. Its located about one block just off American Lake and set deep in ravine/crater like area.My buddy Stephen OBriant and myself were fishing for trout. Suddenly what appeared above us was this enormous perfectly, disc shaped object. I would describe its color as brushed silver. It hovered about 10-20 seconds while moving ever so slightly, before it shot off towards Mt Rainier. We couldnt believe our eyes and were thankful to have each other for witnesses. It was so close above the tree line that I literally looked for "USAF" on it, but found nothing. 
Within the next couple of weeks I went back there with another friend, Peter Miranda. We had been followed by a group of local gal friends we hung out with. While Peter flirted with atop a sharp hill behind me it appeared again. Hung around for about the same length of time and shot off again, but this time in a more southeastern direction as opposed to the east/southeastern direction it went during the first encounter.
I will never forget this event.
