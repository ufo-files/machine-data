{cak} Moffet Blackhwak chasing the orb, 500-1000 feet from ground

I was in my living room when I heard a loud helicopter coming towards my house. It sounded different, like it was going all-out. So I ran outside to my back patio. When I got outside, coming from the North the was a Red-orange orb being chased by a Blackhawk helicopter, I thought that what i was seeing was in fact an Orb being chased, and was not happy about it. The helicopter sounded like it was straining to keep up. The Orb and the Helicopter were about 500-1000 feet from the ground, and the orb was about 15-20 to the front and the right of and below the Helicopter. This was not their Radar post that leads out from the front of most Blackhawks. The helicopter and the orb passed overhead with in about 15-20 seconds they were out of my view, and past the tree line. I ran to the front of my house, to see if it was coming back or if i could get a far off glimpse, when I heard what sounded like 2 very loud jets taking off from San Jose Airport. I live a bout 4 miles from there and only hear the commercial jets take off in the early morning. not mid afternoon. There is also a National guard station at San Jose airport. So they may have been fighter jets taking off after it as well.  The color the orb was, well it looked like it was a color of Mad!
