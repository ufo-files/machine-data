Two orb shaped pulsating lights

Returning from work on January 25 2022 and I noticed flickering lights that appeared to be close to my residence. Turning onto the road towards my residence they became noticeably closer and puzzling as none of the stars in the sky were flickering like these two particular lights. I gazed up and decided to just go inside and get together for bed and went out back to just determine what they may be. I took still photos and approximately 2 minutes worth of video and went inside due to extreme cold temps. The videos are surprisingly clear and the noise heard is the neighbors heat pump, and the cloudy mist is my breath. Nevertheless when you zoom the video in to see more clearly the lights and pulsation are phenomenal. It is incredibly active and multitude of colors and what some spots look to be lights. I’m fascinated and wanted to share as I have now become a member of Mufon and want answers just like many other people. Thank you for checking my videos and pics.
