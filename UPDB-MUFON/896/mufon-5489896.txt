{jpg} UFO Observed Two Nights in a Row

I was asleep on second floor and awoke suddenly. My window was open and I turned over on the bed. I looked out the window and noticed a triangular object which I thought was a helicopter. It had green, yellow, blue, red lights pulsing. The object was stationary and then abruptly dropped straight down rapidly, disappearing behind cloud cover. I was afraid and got up and shut my bedroom window, then went back to bed. The following night, I didn't open my window when going to bed. I woke again sometime in the night and saw the object which appeared to be closer than the previous night. In fact, it looked like it was at roof line. I was terrified; however, the next thing I remember was it was morning. Since these sightings I have been physically sick. I haven't seen this object since then.
