{rjl} Red light on N horizon that moved in a circle and back and forth over about where super fund site would be

My grandkids came screaming in the house there was a UFO. I reluctantly followed them outside and I am pretty darn sure this was nothing Ive ever seen before. It was a red egg shaped light that was traveling from E to W in a Northern direction. It traveled in an observablely large circular flight path and then turned around and went the opposite direction in the same circular flight path according to the children. I personally observed it moving in a circular direction. THIS WAS NOT A PLANE OR A HELICOPTER. BEYOND THAT I DON'T KNOW WHAT WE OBSERVED. I went inside to try and find my camera. When I got back outside a minute later, the kids had got in the car and locked the doors and were frightened. I ask them why they were scared as it was not very close to us. They said it just scared them because it was so weird. It was indeed, weird. And unfortunately gone by the time i located my camera. It appeared to be moving about where i would guess the superfund site is located. Over Pitcher, Oklahoma. Its very hard to judge size and distance but it seemed about 15 miles out on the horizen. The light was solid. It never wavered blinked or acted in any way like plane or helicopter lights. It was truly bizarre. I should have reported this sooner but surely other people saw this. It was so obvious. It was not late at night.
