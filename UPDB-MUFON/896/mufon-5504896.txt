{cak} Glowing pulsating green orb. Powerful aura and strong glow.

I was making a turn onto my houses street with my friend in his truck. We were about to make a left turn and I look up and there was this bright green glowing orb that appeared to be basketball size just hovering in the air. As soon as I had my eyes on it for about 20 seconds the craft sprang to life and proceeded south east from about zero to easily over 600 MPH leaving a glowing tail similar to that I have seen on a shooting star. It was like the light was being bent and stretched due to its acceleration. It made no noise during the acceleration at all. The orb itself was so bright that it was hard to miss. It almost made the sky seem to glow. It made my heart stop. I was terrified and awestruck at the same time. My friend who was in the truck with me and is a 911 operator sent me a message this morning telling me we were not the only ones who witnessed the lights last night and that the craft was also reportedly spotted in Pompton Lakes NJ roughly 8 Miles from my siting in Ramsey NJ.
