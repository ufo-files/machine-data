Light traveling faster than normal aircraft. no beacons. lower than satellite and bit quicker

Walking to shop. Fast moving cloud and the stars in the clear area of no cloud cover made me look up. I saw white dot moving quickly. Under the clouds and the light was viable when past through the clouds. at first I thought it might be a satellite but it was much bigger in comparison to others including the international space station plus the speed seemed really to fast. No trailer of any kind just a white light moving in a straight line. Too fast to be anything I've seen including missile but slow enough to track it. I stopped to watch and didn't speak to the gentleman on a bike who also stopped to look we both said nothing. When it went past the horizon and disappeared. We both looked at each other. He went on and I stopped for a minute more. I felt no fear I guess I felt happy for lack of a better word. I don't really believe but it was powerful emotion to warrant a report. The craft left behind the horizon of trees and buildings.
