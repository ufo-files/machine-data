BRIGHT Red-orange light moving south, then white moving north

On 8/14/11 I was in my backyard in NE Seattle (Lake City) at 9:10 PM PDT when I noticed a large very bright steady red light at about 30-40 degrees elevation to the east, moving slowly south. Just before it moved behind some trees it changed direction slightly toward the southeast. I walked to the street to try to see it again and saw a similarly sized bright white light now traveling north at the same elevation. It moved north for about 20 seconds, the veered to the NE and slowly got dimmer until I couldnt see it. Its apparent speed was about 1/2 degree (width of full moon) per second, but it might have sped up after it turned to the NE and moved away from me. No blinking lights, no sound that I could hear. I couldnt make out a shape. Its apparent size was at least 4 times larger than the ISS, like a small aspirin held at arms length. It was partly cloudy with the full moon below it in the east, but I didn't notice if the light was in front of the clouds or in clear sky.
