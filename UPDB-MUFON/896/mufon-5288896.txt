Dancing lights in shroud of fog

Parksville, Vancouver island. Just spotted a formation of lights that seemed a lot dimmer than the stars around it. The lights seemed to "dance" in that they would move around in a way that reminded me of fireflies or electricity. There were about six that I could see but they were quite dim. (Far away?) It sounds strange but it would seem that I could see the formation of lights CLEARER when I was looking at surrounding stars instead of directly at them. They would appear dim when looking directly at them but when looking slightly away, they would be more visible and they had a kind of... "fog" in between them that was also more visible when looking slightly away.
