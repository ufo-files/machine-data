{tjd} Straight line of orbs/ lights

At approximately 7:40pm on 3/6/2020, while in the backyard having a cigarette, I began looking up to the sky at Venus. I observed what I initially thought was a star appear under Venus. I then realized the light was moving. As I looked around more at the sky, I realized there were numerous orbs/ lights flying from west to northeast in a perfect line, one following the other, equal distance from each other. 
The lights/orbs appeared to be, from what I could tell, outside of the earth's atmosphere. The sky was perfectly clear, no clouds. 
These light's just seemed to materialize directly under the light of Venus. 
This continued on then a double orb/light appeared. The double light/ orb fly as if they were one unit but the orientation of the two lights were just a bit askew. 
I immediately ran into the house and asked my Mother to come outside to look at these objects. 
I believe we watched a parade of approximately 20 lights but I have no idea as to how many had passed overhead before I noticed them.
The second to last light appeared as if it was out of sync with the rest, was lagging behind, then sped up drastically to catch up with the others. 
The show ended with a final light, not with the rest but traveling from northwest to the southeast end of the light/orb line and the follow the rest. As this light flew over, I noticed an airplane flying south and was coming close to the final orb/ light. As the airplane continued in a southerly direction, the orb/ light just blinked out, disappeared all of the sudden.
