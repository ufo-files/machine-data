round, bright orange, flickering, constant light, moved slowly, but steady, then dissapeared

while on vacation in california, my girlfriend and myself witnessed a large orange orb shaped object in the night sky. The object traveled west along the coastline above carpinteria state beach and moved relatively slow and steady. We watched this for about five minutes and while watching the object turned out over the ocean and just dissapeared. We don't know what this was but we both saw it and wondered if anyone else witnessed anything similiar. The object was as clear as day and there was a lot of commercial airline traffic visible as well as stars in the night sky, so it didn't dissapear into a fog bank. We could only capture one photo since our camera was in our tent and we were ready to go to sleep. I also tried to email one of your members that night from the los angeles chapter , I used my phone to search for your site and found him, can't remember his name.
