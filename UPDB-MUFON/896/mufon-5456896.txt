{tjd} Purple Lights in the sky

Around 12:13 am on October 7, 2019, I was woken up by a video call from a friend in Quincy, Massachusetts. She and two of her friends were standing outside their apartment building at that time and saw bright circular-ish purple light in the sky. It seemed huge and stationary. 
The sky was a little hazy. Not cloudy. We couldn't estimate the size. It was visible for over 15 mins in the same direction (80ºE). 
We thought of possible explanations like stadium lights' reflection, bridge lights, some kind of chemical reaction, etc. None of that made sense. No beam of light seemed to be projected upwards. Not sure where this purple light came from. I wonder if such mysterious lights would even be considered as UFO event. Any explanation to it would be helpful.
