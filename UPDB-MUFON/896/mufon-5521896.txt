Red Lights over Chicago

We were in a movie theater parking lot 7:45 pm 12/25/11 parking the car to go see a movie saw several people looking to the eastern sky.We saw 5 red lights moving in the sky heading west southwest all were in a similar path but unorganized group they changed direction east southeast and started to disappear one after the other they seemed to be gaining altitude as they moved out of view also 2 more followed a similar path west southwest than east south east disappearing into the sky.
