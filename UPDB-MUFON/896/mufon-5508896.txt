{cak} Odd Light Dimming In and Out

Was out walking my dogs at night. For the past few weeks Ive been watching the sky for UFOs and usually can distinguish what is a plane or not by the blinking lights on the bottom of the aircraft, and thats what I have been seeing often that I thought was weird. But I was eyeing the sky tonight (this was coincidental since my phone was out of battery so I couldnt record or photograph.) But, as a skeptic, I was eyeing this object as a plane, but then the object light dims in for a brief second, and then out again. It looked like it was traveling due East. This went on for a short duration and then the dimming stopped and the object was gone.
I was sort of stunned and surprised by the whole sighting - I have been watching the skies for a while and seen weird things, that I quickly pointed them out as planes, so when I saw that there were no blinking lights emitting from the object, I continued to watch as it dimmed in and out and again and then stopped, with me losing track of it.
