Walking back to a friend's apartment with 2 friends saw a disc hovering. It started moving back and forth then shot off in the sky

For the life of me I can't remember the exact day, but I know it was 1990 something. There was a new TCBY near my friend Tom's apartment. We walked over before it closes to try some yogurt. On the walk back we are in an ally behind TCBY that leaf to his apartment building. We look up and see this disc just hovering above the buildings. Just as we are trying to figure out what the heck we are looking at it starts slowly going back and forth. Almost like it was trying to gain momentum. Then all of a sudden it shot off ( from 0-160mph) up in the sky
