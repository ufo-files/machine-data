{dr}Observed a single hovering light in the sky at a distance. Discovered 2 objects in pic later.

I helped my wife setup her business at the same location five days a week. From 6pm to about 10pm. On this Sunday after setting up the food truck. I sat down and watched the customers arrive and mill around the several other trucks in the lot. At 8:00pm I observed three lights (2 white,1 red,)near the Sprint sign to the west. I watched a few minutes. Not sure if they were a plane. I went back to watching the customers. After about 5 minutes I looked back towards the lights. The white lights had not moverd. The red one moved around the two white ones. The two white objects were in the same place where I had first observed them. I then then used my cell phone to take a picture and sent it to my daughter. The red one had vanished before I took the picture. I was about to get someone else to look, but the last two vanished. El Paso has a large airport. So, I was not sure if I had seen light reflecting off a plane. The moving red object made me doubtful.
