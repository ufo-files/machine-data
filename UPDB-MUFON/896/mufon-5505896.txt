{nd} A group of 3 , then 2 more yellow/orange bright lights appeared overhead travelling north to south,. Appeared to be very high altitude and no sound, travelling at a very high speed.

I was sitting in the back yard with my wife and daughter, Christmas Eve, three bright yellow-orange lights appeared from the north, travelling south. The third light was initially behind the first two , but was travelling faster and caught up, the three then flew together in a loose formation. I initially thought they were jet fighters but did not recognize the color as anything normally associated with an airplane, there were no white strobes, red or green nav lights, no rotating beacon, just the bright yellow glow. I expected to hear the jet noise any second but there was none, absolutely silent. The first three disappeared behind our house, then two more appeared in the north sky following the same path overhead. The last one appeared to accelerate and catch up with the fourth object, they remained close together after that. Those two flew overhead and disappeared to the south like the first three. Again no sound, and they traversed the sky overhead in 7-10 seconds. I could not make out any shape, I would estimate their altitude at greater than 10k ft. The speed and bright glowing color were unlike anything I have ever seen, I have been involved in aviation my entire life.
