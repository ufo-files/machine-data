Strange flashes of white light followed by a pulsing yellow light

This happened over a year ago but I’ve just decided to report it as it still bothers me to think about it. I was hanging out of my south facing bedroom window having a cigarette looking up at the sky as I often do, it was dark probably about 10pm at a guess, and I saw a flash of white light that almost seemed like a strike of lightning in space, it was in a west/north west direction, but it sparked my attention and I continued looking becasue it seemed to have a pattern to the light almost like a grid pattern. Then a few moments later it happened again and I definitely saw the strange pattern in the light flash again, almost like the light was in sections like a ladder, and then a moment later there was a third flash. As I continued looking up at the sky to see if there would be another flash and then I saw a large yellow light almost directly above like a large star but very bright, and it got brighter and brighter for a couple of seconds then dimmed, it repeated this 3 times then faded out to nothing and that was it. It was a very unusual and interesting sighting because it almost seemed to me like the flashes of white light correlated with the three blasts of the star like yellow light, as if the flashes were a weapon of some sort fired and the three pulses of yellow light were the weapon hitting something 3 times and destroying it. That’s obviously just my thoughts on what it seemed to be like. I know the flashes were not lightning as there was a definite shape to the light which was the same for all three flashes, and the star like yellow light was definitely not a star or a planet because it pulsed very brightly 3 times and then went out.
