About 12 or more bright arrow like objects seen flying across the sky and disappearing altogether within the same point in time

I was taking a landscape shot of the late evening sky, and suddenly about atleast a dozen of these hovering objects appeared in my frame while i was clicking the photo. The one closest and the brightest seemed to be leading the other objects. There was a circular aura around the object.. almost looks like a sonic boom when you're hitting higher speeds and within about 10-15 seconds i saw them disappearing into thin air at the same point in time. 

These objects were seen moving from top left of the frame all the way down to the right side, moving in a slanting direction, the objects too were positioned in a slanting way. These objects were spread across the sky, with atleast 800 mts gap between the left most and the right most object.

The right most object seems to be a bit distorted compared to the other objects, almost looks in a position where it is getting sucked in from somewhere in the sky. Probably from the point where they all disappeared..

Theres also only a single object that seems to be blue in colour among the whole group..

This photo was published on 31st December, 2014 by the Gulf Daily News, Bahrain. The Bahrain Meteorological Directorate confirmed that no meteors had been registered in the skies above Manama (place where I shot this photo) that evening.
