{dr}Subject and wife driving at midnight saw triangle light high up in sky.

IN THE MIDDLE OF AUGUST IN 1966, SUBJECTS WERE DRIVING HOME AFTER MIDNIGHT AND SAW A TRIANGLE LIGHT OVER 200 FEET IN THE AIR. THEY NOTICED IT WAS HIGHER THAN THE FOREST RANGER TOWER NEAR BY. THEY STOPPED THE CAR AND LOOKED AT IT WITH BINOCULARS AND SAW A DARK HUMAN LIKE FIGURE MOVING ABOUT IN SIDE THE TRIANGLE OF LIGHT, THE FIGURE SEEMED TO BE SITTING AT A TABLE AND MOVING LIKE IT WAS DOING SOMETHING. SUBJECTS HEARD NO SOUND AND SAW NO SHIP OR FORM AROUND THE TRIANGLE. LIKE IT WAS AN OPEN DOOR. THEY WATCHED FOR 20 MINUTES AND LEFT. THE NEXT DAY THEY CAME BACK AND NOTHING WAS THERE EXCEPT CATTLE IN A FIELD. SUBJECTS WANTED TO REPORT THIS SO IT WOULD BE ON FILE IN CASE OTHER PEOPLE WHO SAW THIS FINALLY WOULD REPORT IT TOO. THEY DON'T FEEL THE NEED TO BE CONTACTED ABOUT THIS EVENT. SUBJECTS NEVER REPORTED THIS EVENT AT THE TIME BECAUSE THEY DID NOT KNOW WHO TO REPORT IT TO.
