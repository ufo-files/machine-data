floating triangle shape with reddish flashing lights at the corners moving east. 2 small strobing lights travelled to the south at the same time

i was driving home - going east. there is much air traffic in our sky so i see flashing lights all the time and know the different airplane lights (we are on the flight path of midway airport & a local airport). i noticed these lights because they were strobing. then i saw the outline of the craft. it was large & triangular & looked almost like it was tipped to the side slightly. i pulled into my driveway about a minute after spotting the object. i ran inside to get my camera and when i got back outside i could still see the lights but it was too far away to the east to record. i then noticed a single strobing light not as far east. this light went almost due south. it met up with another light & they both continued southward. i watched until i couldn't see them anymore.
