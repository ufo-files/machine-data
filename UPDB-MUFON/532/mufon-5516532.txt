(GS) Stationary light vanishes

Im riding my bicycle to work, its some time around 3:45am CST. Im looking to the east, at Jupiter and Aldebaran. Im looking up at the sky every 5-10 seconds, and suddenly I see a new light "to the right" of Jupiter. Its almost twice the size of Jupiter and significantly brighter. It shines with a rough corona and sparkles, even with stratus clouds around (behind?) it. Its stationary and doesnt appear to move. I stop and observe the light, a few seconds later it uniformly fades away, over the course of 8 seconds or so. It piqued my interest as possibly being extra-dimensional, but had no adverse reaction on me. What made it so unusual is how it disappears so uniformly. I observed the same light, the same behavior, at or near the same point in the sky about a week ago during the same period of time.
