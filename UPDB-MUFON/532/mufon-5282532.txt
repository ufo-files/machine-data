flashing sphere, constant movement different directions, speed, colors

We were staying at the Sheldon Lodge in Denali, I was up about 5am hoping for some Northern lights when I noticed what initially looked like a normal star, very low over the mountain peak, until I noticed it was moving, thought maybe a Satellite. I watched for a minute or so then noticed it was moving a bit like a bouncing ball, looked like a pulsating ball of 'energy'. it would also change direction, speed, and colors, seen when zoomed it. I set up my( iPhone 12 MAX PRO.) in the track of the sliding window so the phone was stable while recording. I watched for at least 2 hours, there was also another one but was mostly behind the mountain peak Eventually they both went behind the mountain peak. It was fascinating and I feel compelled to watch the videos again and again. I am still processing the entire experience
