I was getting ready for bed and observing the night sky out of my bedroom window. There was a clear cresent moon and a very bright star that I believe was most likely the space station. As I looked to my left I noticed something moving rather slowly and it had very bright red and white flashing lights. It couldnt have been a plane or helicopter because it was travelling too slow and it appeared to be moving with a circular motion to the Southwest. When my vision became obsured by the trees in my backyard I quickly ran to the deck and opened the door to watch it until it disappeared. There was no noise of any kind.
