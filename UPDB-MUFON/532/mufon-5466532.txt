White light travelling W to E in southern sky. Flat trajectory towards airport (5 miles away) then up and out of sight in split second.

I have a 60 metre long south facing garden backing onto similar sized gardens.I was calling cats in late at night when I noticed a brilliant white light to the west. At first I thought it to be police helicopter but quickly realised it was travelling too fast and was not emitting a beam: it was more a glow, produced by friction.
I watched it cross about a quarter of the visible southern sky, at a flat trajectory when, just as it was about in line with my viewing position,about 5 miles from Luton airport, it just shot up and out of sight in a split second.
I have seen meteor showers and laser airshows but never anything like this.
I have thought about it many times overvthe years and considered it may have been an unmanned military proto-type, perhaps testing the airports and national radar.
I checked local and national news and could find no reports, which is odd as it crossed the M1 motorway. I have spoken toba number of people about it but just get odd, blank looks.
The worrying thing, if it was NOT a military craft, is that there were no signs of an interception.
And, if it WAS a military craft, what advancements have been made in the last 30 years?
