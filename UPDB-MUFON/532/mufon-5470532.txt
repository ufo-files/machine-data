{td}Cluster of lights flying in a changing formation

A pattern of lights appeared near the fullmoon on Jan 31, 2018 in the sky above Conway South Carolina. The lights appeared in the clear night sky as a kind of 1boomerang1 formation and started to move slowly. The lights changed formation and hovered for a while. Some of the lights went out and then came back on. The pulsating pattern of the craft also changed. I viewed the event with two friends. There was no sound and no trail that an airplane would leave. What I saw is something I never saw before. It was not flares. Please see attached video and please feel free to contact me if more information is requested.
