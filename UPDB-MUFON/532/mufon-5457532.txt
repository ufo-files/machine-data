{cak} A string of turquoise lights, near a 2 story bldg. that moved like rocking chair legs.

I was outside helping the drummer of my band load his drums in his trunk after we had finished a wedding reception gig.
I looked down the street and saw a string of lights that were moving in sync as though they were attached to an object. I could not see an object connected to the lights. However, when the lights moved, they didn't move as though they were being blown by the wind in a ripple effect. They moved in sync like curved rocking chair legs or a leaf slowly floating downward; even as a large 15th Century ship would move in high waves or a ball would move at the bottom of a curved container.
I asked the drummer, "Do you see that?" he said, "See what?" I said down the street by that building...the lights..do you see that?" He said, " Yeah I see it. Forget about that and help me get these drums in the trunk!"
I looked back to see what the lights were doing, and they disappeared.
There was nothing there but the building.
That's the best description I can give you.
I think I may have reported this a few years later, when the Internet became accessible to everyone; but I don't know when.
I've searched for any other people that could've seen this that year, but I've found nothing. No other reports of a UFO in Binghamton NY in September of 1989 exist as far as I know.
I'm certain that what I saw was not Christmas or advertising lights  blowing in the wind. A string of lights simply doesn't move like that in the wind. Moreover, there WAS NO WIND that night. 
It was a cool, very still evening outside of an American Legion Post 1645where we had played for a wedding reception that night.
