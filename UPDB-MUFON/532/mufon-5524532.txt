Observed huge pulsating red and blue light in night sky

While on patrol I observed a huge but stationary object. All I could see was red and blue maybe green flashing lights. It had a bright light or tail but it was shooting high above it and below it. It appeared out of no where. I watched for about an hour until first morning sun light and it disappeared simply as it had appeared. The tail shooting above and below must have been over a mile long as it sat high up in the night sky. I have seen this once before and it was about a month ago. This is facing due East but some degrees towards the south.
