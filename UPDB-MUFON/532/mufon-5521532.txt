Saw black triangular object w/yellow lights approx.50 above car behind me on highway

While traveling South in the slow lane on Interstae 91 at4:50 am on Tues.Jan.5th, I looked in my drivers side mirror and noticed a Black Triangular object with large yellow lights approximately 50ft. above and directly over the roof of a car, about 300-500ft. in the fast lane. After about 5 seconds I refocused my driving to the road in front of me as i was headed towards the breakdown lane.I manuevered my vehicle back into the slow lane and once again looked into the drivers side mirror and the object was gone. the vehicle behind me in the fast lane was still proceeding south behind me. the entire event lasted about 10 seconds. As I proceeded South as looked through my drivers side window and passenger side window into the sky looking for the object, it didn't reappear! After I proceeded approximately a mile South on the interstate I saw what looked like a red shooting star with a streak in the sky directly in front of me lasting about 2 seconds.
