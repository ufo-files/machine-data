{flg} 3 steady redish-orange round objects in Western sky slow travel N-S direction

I was standing on the street outside my home, watching the neighborhood fireworks. It was around 10:00 - 10:30 pm PST. I was looking to the west and I saw 3 redish-orange objects in the sky at about 50-60 degrees up. They were traveling together (sort of V-type formation) and moving in a north to south direction. By the time I got 3 pictures and tried to get video, they all just slowly faded out of sight before my eyes. They werent fireworks (that were going vertical), these were traveling horizontal. I got 3 pictures on my phone. They are a bit blurry, but you can see them in a sort of formation. I was sort of excited to see something like that. I didn't know if they were making sounds because of all the fireworks going off around me.
