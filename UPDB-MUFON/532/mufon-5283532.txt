Black object in sky not seen with the naked eye

While working on a "boom lift" about 50 feet up in the air near downtown Chattanooga on January 5th 2021, I took out my cell phone and took several pictures of the morning sky which was absolutely beautiful with the sunlight beaming through the clouds. When I got home that evening, I showed my wife and she noticed something in one of the pictures that I had not seen while taking the pictures. There in the middle of the sky was a black object similar to a wilted rose. It was pitch black and very clearly seen with a "stem" or possibly a "tail" on it. This object was not seen on the pictures I had taken right before and right after this one. I dismissed it but my wife did not. Almost a year after taking this picture, my wife finally convinced me to report it to the experts at Mufon. I hope someone can solve the mystery of the black rose.
