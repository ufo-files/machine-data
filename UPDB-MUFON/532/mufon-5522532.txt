like a helicopter but not a helicopter

Driving to work i saw a bright light on the horizon, thought it was a star, but then i never remembered one being in that spot in the sky.
i then concluded it was too bright to be a star, and it was changing colors like a twinkling star but so bright that no other star twinkled the same way.
as i headed east towards valley it disappeared, thinking yeah... its a star, the clouds must have covered it up.it then appeared a mile north of its original location in a matter of minute keep in mind was never moving just hovering. as i got closer to valley it began to look like a cars headlights, there were two white lights close together, i realized it was hanging over my work place Valmont Irrigation Inc. i grabbed my phone to get a pic, but right then it changed its colors to a pulsating red.
I pulled in to valmont with my eyes glued to it completly overhead. it started moving out slowly to the west, it resembled a helicopter in a way however the lights were spread apart like a square. i parked my car an watched it move out towards fremont, then i walked inside knowing this was strange.
