Bright red balls of light turn to rotating fire as they move above me.

I was at home. I have had a sighting recently in my town (case# 97337/ with video) and ever since i have been watching. I went out on my porch ti look around and when i looked up over top of my house there they were!!! Bright red. Solid and moving above my house. I yelled into the house for my fiancee to come outside quickly because i wanted her to see it. I ran in the house to get my phone. When i came back out they were moving right above me but turned from solid red to a spiralling orange/white light that was turning counter clockwise leaving fire as it rotated. All 3 objects did this. Each one moved above me and left one after another each disappearing as the other moves in to place. There was no cloud cover at all!! The stars were beautiful! I have a video! Send somebody to Cowansville. This is twice in less than a week. The ones from last night were unbelievable!!
