{nd} Daytime Sighting Of White Disk Object Flying Near Airplane

My boyfriend & I were working on our boat restoration in the backyard. I always have my camcorder with me as there are strange sightings in the skies here in Washington County. It was a bright sunny day today so it was difficult to see things on the view finder. I spotted a bright white object that looked like a disk or oval shape flying in the sky near an airplane i was watching. The white disk object was probably miles from the airplane but from my point of view it appeared to be above the airplane. I basically was using the airplane as a point of reference to keep an eye on this object. We watched it as it went out sight. I don't know what it was. I do know it wasn't any kind of airplane or helicopter.
