{cak} Diagonal line of gold lighted orbs flickering out one by one.

I was driving to my friend Dans house with him in the car. We looked up into the sky while driving down [location removed] In the sky was a diagonal line of orbs that were lit up a gold/red-orange color. There was 7 of them in a line. Starting with the first orb we watched the light slower flicker out like a candle and it disappeared. One by one in a line we watched each light slowly flicker out and disappear. We knew instantly it was a UFO because it was cloudy and rainy no stars were able to be seen. The perfect formation of the line and the gold orange color of the orbs made it impossible for it to have been anything else. No plane or balloon would or could be in that perfect formation and flicker out and disappear perfectly one after another. Everything was timed too well and executed perfectly. It was a strange feeling watching the whole incident and having that gut feeling that something was watching you from above.
