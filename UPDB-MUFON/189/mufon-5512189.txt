(GS) Very bright circular light observed while it moved in horizontal line, then went out

I was backing out of a driveway and noticed in peripheral vision a bright light to my left about 30 degrees above horizon. It moved for about 2 seconds in a straight line, I said do you see that? and the light was gone. This is an area near WrightPatt AFB. I didn't see any planes or other aircraft around. I thought it might have been a RC craft, but the light was so bright it didn't seem like a natural RC. It seemed circular although I couldnt really tell any object shape but to say the light itself was the shape. I would like to know if anyone else saw the same thing.
