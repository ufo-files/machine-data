{jpg} Moved quickly and changed direction

I was standing outside enjoying the evening in the cottage community where I was vacationing. A bring light moving at an extraordinary speed came along and did three 90 degree direction changes before continuing on its path. The object was brighter than a planet or star. I lost sight of it when it moved behind the treeline.
