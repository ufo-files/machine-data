{td} Saw this object in photo after photographing odd clouds over the area

I was outside my home in Port Angeles photographing odd clouds over the downtown/ harbor area. They seemed to be coming from the mountainous ridge south of town. I took pictures in many directions and over the course of about a half hour. Reviewing the photos later, several anomalies were discovered in the photos, perhaps most intriguingly was this domed flying object hovering in a mountain pass.
