{nd} Step brother and i both saw three bright blue lights (one object) hovering way up in the air directly above us then the ufo just flew away at a crazy speed in one direction

About three or four years ago, my step brother and i were camping at red river gorge in KY and we were just looking up in the sky at the thousands of stars because us city boys don't see that kind of sky very often. We had just cracked open our first beers so we were NOT drunk (we know what we saw!!!) We noticed that directly above us, but way way up in the air was three bright blue hovering lights in a perfect iscoceles (or however you spell it) triangle. It had to have been only one object because the lights were hovering around perfectly uniform and we were staring at the ufo for a couple minutes in shock and all of a sudden it took off at a supersonic speed in one direction and was gone in less than 2 seconds! I repeat we know what we saw and i will never forget this experience!!!
