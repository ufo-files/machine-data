{cak} I took a picture of a storm cloud because I am aware UFO's can be seen in clouds. When I magnified the photo a few days later, I noticed the neon green glow in the darkest part of the cloud.

We were driving thru New Mexico a few miles from the Texas border. We were on our way home from a trip to Show Low, Arizona where my brother lives. I have learned from shows on UFO sightings that UFOs are sometimes hidden in clouds. I took one photo of a storm cloud on 8/10/2014 around 6:00 pm. I did not see the object with a naked eye. A few days later, as I was looking over all the photos I took, I decided to magnify the storm cloud photo and noticed the neon green orb-like object. I magnified it as much as my camera would allow. I did not track any movement since I only took one photo and I was in a moving vehicle. In all honesty, I did not expect to find anything out of the ordinary in the cloud and was somewhat surprised when I did. I am not fearful of UFOs, I am fascinated by the mystery and this is not the first time I've seen one. I have another pic of clouds in Corpus Christ that has at least two UFOs hidden to the naked eye but visible as black objects when magnified. I am happy to submit that photo as well if it is requested. I also have many photos of orbs in different environments and events. As I said, I find this fascinating and am happy to share my experiences and photos.
