{dr}viewed 5 bright orange flying objects 2 in front, 3 following behind in triangular formation, then vanished one at a time with no trace into the night

My fiance and I were sittting on our back deck at 10pm on July 15, 2012, when we noticed 5 very bright orange lights traveling from the east heading west over Blue Springs. There were 2 in front side by side but with some space between them, and then they were 3 following behind in a triangular formation. After I first noticed them they were probally in the sky about 3 minutes. One by one starting in the front they flickered slightly and simply vanished into thin air one at a time until all 5 were not visible anywhere. Mind you they didn't fly further away so that they faded into the distance and I did not see any clouds in the sky just stars. It was extremely strange. I tried my hardest to see some shape or something in the sky after they vanished to explain it, you know like they were really still there, but they were not. I did see an airplane high up in the sky probally 5 minutes later, however that is the only other activity I saw in the sky for probally 5 minutes before and after.I simply lost sight of them when they vanished before my very eyes.
