UFO overLondon 14.08.2012

On 14/08/2012 I was enjoying London panorama from my balcony. Suddenly I saw 2 razor sharp clouds looking like perfect pyramids. I ran in to my room and grabbed my camera. By the time I set up my camera, those 2 clouds gradually changed its shape and beguine to look like the rest of the cloud (bubbly like). Took me a minute or so to observe them while filming, and I was about to switch off my camera. Suddenly I saw strange object (UFO) jumped out from behind one of the clouds, which previously looked like a pyramid. I beguine to follow this object which flew from South to the North.
