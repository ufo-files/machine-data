{dr}3 Dodecahedron/geometric flower shape UFOs in a staggard line formation bright orange, still for 2 to 3 minutes then disappeared; between utility lines and sky.

My husband called me outside looked up, on 900 block of Elm Ridge Avenue in Arbutus Maryland; saw 3 UFOs in a crooked line formation, first two, then a third appeared. All orange lights, each craft looked like an orange circle light in the middle and five to six circle lights around each. A dodecahedron/geometric flower shape is the closest I can find to describe shapes. they stayed bright, glowing for 2 to 3 minutes, and then disappeared, North sky, at 10 minutes to midnight 12/31/2015. At first, I was not scared, but then, since it felt and looked so close to us, like closer than an airplane in the sky; I started to get anxious that maybe it would zoom in on us, so I was just about to run inside when they disappeared. I still ran inside and looked on the internet to see if anyone else reported it.
