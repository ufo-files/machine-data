{rjl} Dome with lights. Lights were in center around structure

I was with a group of people who were in a rural area parked & talking. It was a pasture. The object came over the tree line in a streak. Traveling faster than anything I've ever seen. It stopped directly over us fora second or less. It was scary. It then streaked away. One other person in the group and I looked at each other and said in unison, "did you see that". No one believed us. I quit talking about it because I have been called crazy too many times. I'm a Christian and I 'swear to God" I saw it. The other person saw same thing I did. The newspaper reported other sightings as well. I can describe in greater detail. I have been ridiculed too many times to talk to a skeptic again. I am willing to describe in greater detail if anyone is honestly interested. It happened. Nothing is shaped like that. Nothing can travel that fast. It was absolutely silent.
