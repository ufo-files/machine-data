watched the object appear from nowhere while viewing stars at 421 Bridge and for over an hour, the object performed maneuvers, hovered, changed positions... never saw it leave.

Parked beside the 421 Bridge at approx 1:00 am 08/03/2011, walked to waters edge to view stars and an object appeared in the northwest, low near the mountain tops and hovered, wobbled and shot in various directions. It was an upright trianular shape and was an orangeish glow with bluish lights hinted at the center, or so it appeared. Watched for over an hour and left the site with it still in the sky. Appox distance: 2 - 3 miles. no blinking lights. The light would also vary in intensity. We watched until myself and another witness were convinced that it was not an aircraft or man-made object.
