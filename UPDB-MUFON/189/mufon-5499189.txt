{rjl} stationary ball of light that suddenly starting moving across the sky, emitting blue and red light.

My brother and I were driving on the highway when we suddenly saw a big ball of light which at first we thought was a light post but then realized it was too high to be a light post and there were no other lights in the area. As we got closer to this strange light it started moving in a upward diagonal direction, was moving very fast and had blue and red lights trailing behind it. It kept moving in that direction until it disappeared into the darkness.
