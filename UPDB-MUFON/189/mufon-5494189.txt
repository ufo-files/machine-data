I just got home from work with my young son and parked my car in front of my garage I found that my neighbor had cut some large trees in which one fell on my roof I got out of the car and was looking at the roof damage I was facing east and low in the sky I saw about 10 egg shaped very shiny objects flying counter clockwise as they were still flying one would fly out going east still shining bright until it was out of sight which took about three seconds the rest kept flying counter clockwise with the gap from the missing one and in about 10 seconds the one would fly back and fit in formation perfectly this happened several times then one would fly out and when it was out of sight another would fly out while they were still going counter clockwise then one by one they flew out going east until they were all gone I was amazed or maybe stunned I could not believe what I was seeing I kept looking at the sky and they never came back it was a bright sunny afternoon and I thought it may be shiny aircraft in an air show with the sun reflecting off them I ran in the house quick and called the airport to see if there was an air show in the area he said there was no air show and no aircraft in the area I asked him about maybe a military operation he said he did not have access to that information I never knew who else to contact about this I talked to friends and family about this they all look at me like im crazy im not this really did happen this has bothered me a lot all these years I wish there was someone I could talk to about this and maybe get some kind of answer or closure thank you
