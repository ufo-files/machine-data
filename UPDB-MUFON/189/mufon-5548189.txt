Wow im amazed!

I was getting home from my fianc1's house at about 11:22 PM It was a clear night with a few clouds. I got of my car and stretched, While stretching, I noticed there was a Multi-colored orb hovering about 5k feet north west of my house. It moved south very slowly and then banked east at an incredible rate of speed. At first I thought it was maybe a plane coming in for a landing because O'Hare is about 15 miles away from my house, but at rate of speed it was traveling it could of in no way possible been a plane. It was pulsing orange, red and green. I got out a pair of binoculars to confirm what I was seeing. I brought My brother outside to confirm what I was seeing and we snapped about 4 pictures of it. Easily the craziest thing I have ever seen. While snapping the photo's the object disappeared and reappeared. It moved in bursts to the north but would always hover back south west to the original north west position that I first saw it in. I watched it for approximately 22 minutes until it disappeared. I am going to get the film developed today.
