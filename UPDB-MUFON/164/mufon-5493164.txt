Star sized object moving in different directions.

In February 1986, I was 14 years old and living in Coffs Harbour, NSW Australia. I was interested in astronomy and decided to go and search for Halleys Comet which was nearest Earth. It was very early morning but due to it being almost 30 years ago, I can not remember the exact time (possibly 1am). I was at the top of our street looking in an Easterly direction searching for the comet. I do remember seeing it and being a little dissapointed expecting a better view. 
I remember seeing what I thought might be a satellite coming across the sky, but suddenly it slowed and came to a complete stop. It zig zagged in different directions (not great distances) and even went around in small circles. I could not believe what it was doing. I stayed to watch it for about one minute and then ran back to the house to wake my parents to come see it. I remember them telling me to go to bed. I can not recall going back out to see the object again.

You may be wondering why it has taken me so long to report this event and there are no excuses. I am doing it now because I have just finished watching a documentary on Travis Walton. Stanton Friedman said that not many people report their UFO sightings and gave your website to do so. After that event, I have alway believed there is more out there in the universe. 

I have seen satellites travel across the sky at night but this I can not explain and have never forgotten.
