{rjl} Orb like shape changing light remaining at speed and altitude of plane before 30 mins before landing

Location: on plane heading in Oakland CA, had to be 30 mins before landing.

It was level with the plane and we were still at a altitude that where no man made object was build as well as being over unoccupied land. It stayed generally the same distance for the 13 minuets it remaned with the plane and changed altatuded and shape once or twice. We went through a few clouds and it followed, we went through one big cloud and it was gone.

Could not ahve been a building bc there was none and it remained at a high altitude and stayed with a plane going at least 300 MPH. Was not light from the plane because it was white and did not blink. It was not a mechanical light.
