Pulsating glowing lights that appeared and disappeared

My husband and I were heading out of our townhome complex and as we waited on stop sign he noticed a glowing hovering light in the sky. He asked me to look and I began to record. As I zoomed in to see the light closer another light appeared making it two glowing lights in the sky. Then as one appeared the other disappeared. We were both surprised and astonished. We had to go cause another vehicle approached the stop sign and we left and stopped recording. As we returned no more glowing lights were in the sky.
