We 1st spotted the orb 30 miles south of Phoenix az. Traveling south to Roswell. The orb stayed on our left N NW for 45 minutes. Then it it began to move overhead. So we stopped and started taking pictures. At that point it seemed to stop moving. We began back on the road and it started moving again. We drove onto Vaughn NM and stopped at last store. I went inside a d ask the man working to see it. Him his wife and several others seen the orb. As we travelled on to Roswell we could see it as it became star-like in the distance. One hell of an exciting experience. We have many pictures and video.
