Large cylinder shape bright in the eastern sky

Bright cylinder shape I the eastern sky about 10 on a clock Direction in the sky bright light Shone in my face and woke me up it would be big and bright then get small it kept doing this I got dressed went out side about 240 am took two pictures when it was small then it got over cast I couldn’t see it anymore went back inside and went to bed at 300 am stayed awake until 600 am two weeks before this night saw the same thing in the same place around the same time it stayed lit up it had small white orbs coming from the top going around to the bottom and it looked like they were going inside of it watched for about 30 seconds wanted to take a picture but couldn’t stay awake fell asleep.
