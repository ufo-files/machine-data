A group of 6 to 10

Observed it from the Gallery of my house. Tried to record it first over my mobile and later on my handycam (yes i had this much time while the lights lasted). I first thought this was the beam of a searchlight but later noticed that it was made up of 6 to 10 smaller "<<" shaped lights moving one behind the other in a circular pattern and moving towards NE from E. Some of these "<<" lights jumped up from this trajectory and after rising a distance, dropped down again and realigned with the other lights. This was a bit unusual, so i started recording after observing this. I have two videos but both the videos failed to capture these lights convincingly. You will have to use your peripheral vision to observe these and this might take a number of attempts. I am hoping that you will have the technology to maybe put some filters on these videos to see these lights. I observed them for about 15 mins after which these lights disappeared. these lights were observed by me and my wife. they were not reported by anyone later. I came to know about your site through a youtube video and so decided to share it with you.
