On Dec 23, 2020, I saw two objects above the apartment complex where I live. One was stationary and the other one flew back and forth above the apts.

On Dec 23, 2020, at 6:27 am, I went outside my apartment with my cell phone to see if I could photograph the Star of Bethlehem (if it was still in the sky). I saw what I thought was a very bright star. It was stationary above the buildings across the street. I started recording it and when I saw the recording I noticed it was revolving and that there was another object flying at great speed, back and forth (from left to right), also in the same area. I watched for about five minutes. Then, I went inside my apartment.
