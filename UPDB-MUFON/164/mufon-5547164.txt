bright orb

We were sitting on our back porch. I noticed it was bright and moving. Bright object appeared from the west and arced across the sky to the south east. It was in airplane altitude. Clear and bright white light. Had no green or red blinking like an airplane and it was completely quiet. Seemed to move at a steady speed. Went over the horizon and couldn't see it anymore.
