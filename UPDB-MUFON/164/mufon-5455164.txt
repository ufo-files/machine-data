Sighted fast moving white solid light moving across my back garden at 4.30 am

Tuesday4th feb 2020, Winter here in the uk, mon-fri I get up at 4am to get ready for work, at 4.30 every morning I go into my back garden and load my company van up for the days work, I park my van in the back garden for security reasons, every morning as I walk into my garden I always look up into the sky to see the array of stars and I always try to pick out the moon, the I.S.S, the Orion belt , Venus etc etc, the reason I do this is I’m originally from London and because of light pollution I never really got to see the stars etc, I’ve lived in Cambridgeshire for the last 23 years and am awe struck at the millions of stars I see from where I live now,we call it big sky in East Anglia, this particular morning I was following my usual routine and at 4.30 a.m I entered my garden, I looked up at the millions of stars, it was a clear night sky with no cloud cover , there was intermittent wind blowing, no background noise like birds singing, commercial jets flying over at 38,000feet, no car or lorry traffic from the road at the front of my house, deathly silent as it is every morning when I noticed a solid white ball of light travelling from my right to my left very quickly for about 10 seconds before it looked to haze and then disappear , I didn’t have to strain my neck to look at it so it could have been at a height of a couple of thousand feet, at first I thought wow a shooting star but then realised that shooting stars travel vertically this object was travelling horizontally at an immense speed and NO SOUND, I’m a great fan of military air shows and often go to these ,I realised this was no fighter jet as no sound ,no flashing green, red or white safety lights and for a jet to travel at that speed they would have to have their after burners running, I stood in my back garden for a few minutes amazed at what i’d seen and after some deduction I’m convinced I’ve seen my first U.F.O,
