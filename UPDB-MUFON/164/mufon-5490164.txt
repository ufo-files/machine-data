Me and my family are staying at disney world's wilderness lodge. Staring at the sky, I noticed a red blip that was flickering slightly. I kept watching it thinking it was the planet mars or maybe venus with weird atmospheric effects. As I watched, it moved down. I wondered if it was a plane, but it stopped short and then did a sort of loop. There was a tree as a frame of reference so i know for sure it was moving. I was super skeptical and watched for some time. I considered the possibility that it might be a drone. Then, I looked away for a minute or less and it was just gone.
