Fast Walker UAP Captured by Drone Camera

I was filming with my drone above Lake Briarwood. When I reviewed the footage I discovered the object in question zoom by the drone at an incredible speed. Within a fraction of a second the object flies by. I zoomed in to see what it was and it is a spherical shaped object that is reflecting sunlight on its right side and the rest of the object is grayish in color or silver. In the footage I see the object start out at a really low altitude and then zoom upwards straight past the drone in a straight line path. The altitude of the drone was 120 feet and going 6m/s. I was filming in 4k 30fps. I know exactly where the object started out and the position of my drone. So someone if they wanted to could calculate the exact speed of this object. I have the flight log for the drone as well as the recorded video. The event happened on November 5th @ 3:41pm CST, above Lake Briarwood, Desoto, Missouri. I can provide the video if asked by an investigator.
