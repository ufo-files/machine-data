{dr}I observed 3 tall oblong objects suddenly appear side by side near the left side of hill which changed shape from 3 round spheres to 3 oblong structured objects.

I am reporting this because I saw the same bizarre looking objects recently on 12-27-17 in the exact same location. I was watching 3 sphere yellow orange lights hovering in a 10 o'clock to 5 o'clock position on the side of this hillside when suddenly these 3 spheres dropped down to in front of this hillside and totally changed shape. This configuration now looks like 3 bowling pins standing side by side.These objects are pulsating and changing colors. These objects look about as tall as a 10 floor building and are so bright at times and then suddenly just start fading away and disappear. A huge circular orange light was slightly to the left over the water and would brighten and dim and then turn off as if a switch just turned it off but you can still see this now dark object still hovering. This object appears in the general direction of San Quentin and I have seen it in the same low altitude spot on several different nights during "cluster of lights" sightings that I have witnessed.
