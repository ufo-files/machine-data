{dr}Two orbs rotating around each other moving across the sky

I was in my backyard and saw a light coming across the sky towards me but did not think much of it as a lot planes fly over heading to bergstrom airport. As the light came through the trees I saw that it was actually two lights, they came out in the open and slowed down and then began to go backwards and arch towards the west and then move south. When the lights switched direction they were rotating around each other making no noise. They were moving north to south then west and back to the south. They got behind the house so I ran in and told my wife to come with me and see what I had seen. We went to the front yard but did not see the lights. My wife thought I was crazy but I know what I saw and I have no explanation for it.
