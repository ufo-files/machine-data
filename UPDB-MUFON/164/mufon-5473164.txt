Went out for a walk saw a UFO. I was surprised.

I went for a walk to the bridge. Looked up at the sky and saw over the left bank this UFO. Then it started moving and I stopped looking at it. I also wanted to take a picture of him, but for some reason I could not. Probably I had a shock. It's not the first time I've seen a UFO. This object was already before I noticed it. He hung first, and then started to move, and I stopped looking. I wanted to ask him for help. I just stopped looking at him. There were still people there, I do not know if they saw it or not.
