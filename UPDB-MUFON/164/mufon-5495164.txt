{dr}two nights in a row clustered. coming and going

I was stationed in Bremerton, wa. I lived in the Bayview west apartments on kitsap way. I walked into my living room and saw 1 brilliant white light that was pulsating. Every now and then a red triangle appeared below it. I walked out on my balcony to look. As I watched a second appeared. It started out as a dull light as if distant. It instantly grew to the size of the other. Then another and another. As I watched I could see commercial air lines going to and from seatac. I noticed another bright light to the south coming from the general direction of Olympia. It just moved right in beside the others. As it did so a commercial flight (obviously an airplane) was traveling in the same direction. The questionable object moved so much faster than the plane that the plane seemed to not have moved. Then a few in the column vanished until only one was left. The last object was also the first object it stayed in the same place for approximately 45 minutes. This was the night of 15 nov2015 at approximately 2200. The night of the 16th or the following night at the same time the same thing happened exactly as before which is when I recorded what I did on my phone.
