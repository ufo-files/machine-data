Fast, zig zagging star lights { no flashing lights and the only color was that exactly of a really bright star/planet in the night} - {djs}

October 19, 2012. I got out from underneath truck after turning wrenches. Still in driveway I look at my spouse on the porch with the mountain range behind her. I noticed a bright light appear like a star, no flashing lights and the only color was that exactly of a really bright star/planet in the night sky. In my view it was traveling left, which is roughly east/northeast. As soon as I started to study it another one appeared above it, an inch in my sight. When it appeared it was not like a flash of light but more like an emerging light.

These two lights traveled at the exact same speed from my view point. From my viewpoint they zig zagged up and down. When I say zig zag I mean an abrupt and immediate change of direction. All while moving at a speed I’ve never seen something at that distance do. When they went out of sight they did it in the same fashion that the second one came into sight, light a light dimming, but not crazy slow. This whole incident last 20 maybe? 

I’ve grown up going to Oshkosh Airshow. I’ve watched numerous planes hit some serious speeds in my time. One breaking sound barrier. Even saw an sr71 landing in Milwaukee, Wi. Nothing I’ve witnessed comes close to the speeds I witnessed that night. I’m an avid night sky watcher since a small kid, nothing comes close to what I saw.

I tried to get my spouse to look, but she didn’t have her glasses on and couldn’t see much of anything beyond 20’. 

Might I note that I had no light pollution from street lights interfering with my view, it was a clear crisp night without any clouds in that direction. This memory is as vivid as when I witnessed it that night and something I will live with forever.
