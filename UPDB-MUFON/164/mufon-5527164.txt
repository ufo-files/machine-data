Large dark object moved smoothly and silently. Only saw what appeared to be the leading edge of the object.

Was trying to identify a satellite using an application on my phone and notices a shadowy object moving from north to south. It was dark, but appeared to reflect light on the leading edge of the object. It was either really low and moved really slowly, or it was extremely large and moving very fast at a high altitude. It appeared to be very thin since as soon as it never blocked any visible stars. I tried to show a friend and he did not see it. I questioned what I saw, thinking it may be a large flock of birds flying in formation, but there was no variation to the shape.
