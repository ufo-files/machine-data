Night Lights over Brighton

A little after midnight on Thursday Jun 18, 2020, I was sitting on my back porch. I noticed lights being diffused by a cloud, noting that it looked interesting. I followed those lights as they moved from behind the cloud, and noticed it was a strange shape. It moved out of my view behind a tree, then back into view. It then stopped and hovered. Over the course of a minute and half, it hovered, ascended, and descended at various times. It had white and red lights, which seemed to flash one after the other. I did not notice how it left. Shortly after I saw a faint red streak across the sky.
