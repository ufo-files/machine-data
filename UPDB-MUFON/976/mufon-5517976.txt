{dr}Thought it was a star at first glance but then it started to move

I was standing outside on my front porch having a smoke when I glanced up at the sky like I do most nights. I was facing the west horizon when I saw what looked a very bright star so I didn't think anything was out of the norm until it began to move. It made a sudden burst of movement east then move west of the horizon then banked south west until it was no longer visible. I was surprised and a little creeped out by it because like I said before, I thought it was a star!
