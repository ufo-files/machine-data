Green fluorescent sphere moving in an arc.

I saw this UFO one Summer evening in the SW of England in about 1974/5. No idea what it was but was a basketball sized sphere, a beautiful green colour and glowing, like traffic light green. It came up from ground level and moved in an arc, rising to about 20-30 feet and falling back down into waste ground. I was a about 50 yards from it but I could not see where it landed. It did make a faint pfsssh sound as it came from the ground, from the back of someone’s house, but silent after that. The garden of that house had a large radio-ham style ariel and, there was a large ‘gasometer’ near the arc of that green UFO object. I don’t know if that’s significant. I felt weird whilst watching it and did not investigate further.
