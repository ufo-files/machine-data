First 2 glowing orbs traveling at similar speeds, then a large object appeared nearby.

I was driving out of my driveway at around 7:30 when I noticed 2 objects, orange in color,flying slowly across my field of vision. It took about 2 minutes for these objects to move from 0 to 40 degrees across my FOV where they either completely disappeared or where hidden by cloud cover. It was at this point when I noticed a large egg or teardrop shaped object appearing to moved slowly towards and float above my neighbors house. It was at this point when I left in my vehicle, but the object still seemed to be floating in the area when I left. I would describe the objects as similar in color to fire or electrical sparks. The third object appeared to be made of plasma or some sort of non-solid substance.I was scared enough during and after that I wasnt in the right mind to record the event, even though I had a camera with me.
