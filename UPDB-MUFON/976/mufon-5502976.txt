{nd} Super white falling light

I was out walking one of my Dogs-this would have been around the 10:30pm range,Saturday evening..We live within a residential area that has man made lakes..as we were coming off the walkway behind one of the lakes, a super bright and large light,falling from the sky caught my attention. I saw it through the Pine tree's of some Homes across the street.It appeared to have come from behind the lower cloud cover,in the far SW and then disappear back into it.I would describe it as a falling effect. If it was a Meteor? It was super bright and fairly large from my distance of sight. I watched the area for a few seconds more to see if anything else appeared in the area-and it didn't.
