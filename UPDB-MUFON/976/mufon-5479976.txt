I was walking late at night & I was in route to my bank. I noticed a dull or that became bright, but I watched it through an old oak near a neighborhood park. As I got closer to the clearing I saw 3 off in the distance, just as bright as the first & two off in the distance were stationary, eventually disappearing & the first one moved from the left to the right & the other one off in the distance moved closer in my direction before disappearing. I knew it was an orb when I first saw them because I've been seeing an abundance in the past year. I took a photo & continued to walk to my bank because I was in a rush to get there & back home in a good amount of time.
