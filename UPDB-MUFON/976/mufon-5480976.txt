{nd} Object was not observed with naked eye, but was found when reviewing photo.

My girlfriend and I were walking out of the grocery store when I decided to take a scenic picture of the American flag with the open sky in the background. I took several pictures to be sure at least one of them turned out good. Nothing out of the ordinary happened at the time of taking the photos and nothing was noticed with the naked eye. After reviewing the photos taken, however, is when the object was discovered. The object was only seen in one of the several photos taken, indicating to me that it was not an airplane (since the pictures were taken within seconds of each other). The object almost seemed to have warped into its position or have been traveling extremely fast since it was not in any other pictures. The object is cigar-shaped and certainly looks like a solid object in the picture with no stabilizers, fins or wings. I have attached one photo with the same exact landscape and position just without the object. I was shocked when discovering the object since it was not in any other photos and seems to be above the clouds in the background. I truly believe this is a genuine unidentified flying object spotted in this photo.
