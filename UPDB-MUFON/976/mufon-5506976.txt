My daughter said that she and her friends were standing outside after doing fireworks and noticed a glowing object behind the trees across the street from where they were standing. She said it just floated there for a minute and then started to move. They decided to follow it and it just kept going at a constant speed(faster than they could follow it) and disappeared from sight about a mile and a half from where they first sighted it. This happened about 1 mile north of the town of Madera.
