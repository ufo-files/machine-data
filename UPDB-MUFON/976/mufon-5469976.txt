They looked like orbs

I looked outside on bonfire night and saw 4 orb's in a line from east to west, they were close together, the 2nd orb from the right started moving towards the orb on the far right it was glowing a golden colour at the bottom and looked like it was going to drip this golden colour as it was collecting at the bottom of the orb? This is when I went to get my camera, when I came back the orbs were interacting with each other and I took a few photos, I was really bamboozled by what I was looking at and just watched them for several minutes, I looked away several times in bewilderment and they disappeared!
