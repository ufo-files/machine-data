Cigar shaped object.1970s. very clear sighting .

My father worked 2nd shift in Crestline, he got home about midnight. He ran in the house and told my mother that something had followed him all the way home. They both went out and seen what my mother describes as a rust colored cigar shaped object moving down the road right on front of our house. It was flying very slowly,very low to ground.. she describes it as being below the powerlines. It was very large but made no sound. The only lights she remembers was green. . I've heard this description many many times over the years. The exact date she is not sure of but she thought it was the same time as the Coyne incident. We lived in the country surrounded by woods,corn and soybean. From our front porch to the road was probably 100ft. I've never doubted for a moment she seen what she has described so many times. She recently brought the subject up and wanted me to submit this report for her.
