Bright orange/red solid light moving silently south to north at speed of a plane, 2nd one approx 10 minutes later in the same path

My wife and I just spotted something out our west looking patio window. It was a bright orange/red solid light, moving like an airplane or helicopter. We often get helicopters flying over our house as the hospital is one block away. We both went outside and watched this thing fly silently over our house and continue northbound. Not a sound, completely silent, not a single blinking light, it just kept going north until we couldnt see it any more. (10 minutes later) A second one just went by. With the first one we were just dumbfounded and didn't think to grab a camera. After the second one showed we couldnt find the video camera. I found it now, but well see if theres a third, probably wont be. I contacted a local police scanner enthusiast and he said two other people had reported the event and that the police were called. He did mention (not sure how he knew this) that the FAA had no reports of flights in that direction.
