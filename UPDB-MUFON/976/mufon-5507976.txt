{rjl} Bright red and orange oblong object hovering over Houston for 20 minutes. Darted quickly towards Katy and hovered for 30 minutes.

Started watching TV outside when I noticed a large light in the distance. It was too large to be a planet or star. I watched it for 5 minutes and noticed it wasnt moving.  My son and I studied it closely with binoculars and telescope. It was hovering and throwing off red, orange, and blue lights. It quickly moved west ward and stopped to hover about 5 minutes. Then it moved from downtown Houston out towards Katy in about 2 seconds. It stayed hovering for 30 minutes . It disappeared several times then reappeared about 5 or 6 times. Then it quickly took off going west.
