Witnessed stars blink 2-3 times then shoot toward another star and

Grand Lake, Colorado Saturday July 18, 2020. Sitting outside on vacation and I was admiring the stars in the mountains. Perfectly clear night. All of a sudden I see what I believe to be a star blink 2 times then shoot off towards another star and what I would call be absorbed into the star in its path. Watched this same thing happen within my eyesight looking directly up into the sky for a few minutes. This behavior was coming from all different directions within my eyesight looking directly up. I observed this for a solid 20 minutes. The thing that sticks out most, happened within the first minute of my observation. One of the "stars" (as I would describe them) blinked 2-3 times just as the rest of them did before shooting off into a direction. This particular "star" shot towards another "star" across the sky, and about halfway through its destination to the "star" it linked up with (absorbed)....a stationary "star" shot a projectile at the moving "star." The projectile disappeared very soon after missing its target as how I interpreted it. The "projectile" looked like another star basically launched from another star. That's what I saw.
