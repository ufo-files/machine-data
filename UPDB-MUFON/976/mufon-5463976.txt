{cak} Bright white light descended straight down at constant velocity, stopped at ~800', then proceeded horizontally N at a high rate of speed.

I had this experience on the night of 12/9 as I was driving from El Paso Airport to Alamogordo on US highway 54, just as I was approaching Alamogordo. I was driving north on 54 at about 50 MPH in the slow-down zone approaching Alamogordo at approximate geo coordinates [32.863112, -105.968369]. The time was approximately 11:20 pm Mountain time. The sky was perfectly clear with the stars brilliantly visible (later, when I arrived at my destination in Alamogordo, I was appreciating the beautiful view of the stars). My wife was with me reclining in the passenger seat of the car. Directly ahead of me looking north, I saw a light that appeared to fall straight down from the sky at what seemed a controlled uniform velocity - not accelerating like a falling object. It was a white light (~4000-6000K) and got brighter and seemed larger as it got closer in coming down. I called it to Mary's attention and she saw it too. The controlled fall lasted about 5 seconds; and initially, it seemed as though it were going to continue to the ground right in front of me.  However, as it came farther down, it stopped abruptly at about 800' elevation and about 2000' to our east [estimate 32.877803, -105.960405]. The object's descent was straight with no flutter that would have been associated with a parachute for example. Even, at its closest to us, a shape could not be determined. There was no noise that we could hear, in part because we were in the car with the windows shut (it was pretty cold outside). After stopping its descent, the object immediately proceeded due north in straight line horizontal motion parallel to the mountain range, until it was out of sight in no more than 10 seconds. If it had gone 4 miles to be regarded as "out of sight", its speed would have been ~1400 MPH, but I did not hear any sonic boom. It was as though it was traveling right above White Sands Blvd in Alamogordo and then continuing north. As it proceeded away, we noticed a small red light above of it with a small dark gap between the bright white light and the much smaller red one. I have seen helicopters before over the Florida everglades in the dark - scouting for drug traffickers (but we were out for astronomy at the time). The helicopter used their search light and eventually found us in its beam. It was brilliant, but was only bright while it was pointed at us - the helicopter spotlight was well collimated - this was not the nature of the bright light on the object that seemed to radiate omnidirectionally.

Note that this is about 7.5 miles east northeast of Holloman AFB. It could have been an unknown type of military aircraft. However, why did this object emit so much bright white omnidirectional light? That was what caused me to categorize it as a UAP.
