Bright spherical lighted object overflies Salt Lake City

I was sitting outside on the porch at about 10:20 PM and was about to go inside when I decided to look up at the stars. While I did see stars I also saw a bright spherical object flying in a straight line overhead. I could only see a light. I could see no distinct object. It was not like an aircraft because there was no strobe light and there was only one large light. It was flying North the whole time towards the Great Salt Lake and appeared to be descending very slowly(Barely noticeable). I could hear no audible sounds during its appearance. After about a minute it flew too far for me to be able to see it. After seeing it I went inside feeling slightly nervous and apprehensive.
