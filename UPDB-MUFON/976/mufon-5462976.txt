a Meteor removed all my doubt

This is an odd report. But I feel compelled. On the face of it, On december 11th 2018 at around 11:30pm Barbados time. I saw a streak in the sky. And any other night, i would have called it a meteor. A shooting star. Even though i don't recall seeing any in the recent past since I've been here. What is odd, is that just a moment before the sighting, I was denying being of any use, to a God. If one even existed. I am agnostic. Extremely analytical, and i guess you can say, an ascetic. And the reason I was questioning my value to an infinite intelligence, was that i had started writing oddly weeks before. and finally found no way out, but to question this third possibility, if i wasn't just trying to write fancy things, and still able to question my sanity, then the writing could be a 'gift'. I submit, it was a craft, that created my shooting star. And have no evidence. Since then, all doubt in a creator has been removed from me. and i repeated this phrase, which I've never read before, I don't think. "In the name of he who has been refused. All I ask, is that you don't refuse me again!" And so, I have nowhere to go with all this. except to the most reasonable people on the planet. and even if my report isn't published, i would beg the moderator, to help me find out what i should, when one believes, that have achieved no less, than what the Buddha himself discovered. i need help. not fame. thank you.
