unknown flying object with unrealistic acceleration

On this date at around the mentioned time, I was on a break from work outside talking with a fellow coworker when I saw above us what I thought was just a commercial airline as we are close to TF Green airport in Warwick, RI. However, I noticed the lights on the object were not flashing as normal and object was moving slower than I normally observe an aircraft. 
All of a sudden the object increased immensely in speed, more so than I would think for a conventional aircraft. To me, it was like a military jet kicking in afterburners, but even faster and there was no sound of acceleration nor of an aircraft in general, and with the airport we always hear the planes flying overhead.
I was so startled I gave off a rather explicative exclamation at which my coworker, who did not see event, asked what was going on. It took me a moment to tell him at which time I went to see where the craft was heading to find it no where in sight, it was just gone from view.
This was an incredible sight and it could be a military aircraft but at the time, I feel there is no way one of our planes accelerates that quickly with no sound resulting.  
The object shot straight and almost curved a bit.
To say I was in a state of excitement the rest of the night is an understatement.
