I saw an object that looked like a profusely twinkling star. Upon looking through high-powered binoculars, it appeared to have three curved-tadpoles objects within the star, that circled around each other--one was light blue, one was pictures mish red, and one was light yellow. Very beautiful! I have seen this star, in the northern sky, on and off, for two years. Only once did I use the binoculars. As you stare at it, it begins to move up and down and or sideways. It's AMAZING!
