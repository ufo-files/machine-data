Star like object, randomly flashing cyan hovering in the sky for a few minutes then rapidly descended towards horizon/moved away

We were driving home along the A47 dual carriageway entering Peterborough from the west when I noticed a weird star flashing blue but stationary relative to us ahead, I initially suspected a helicopter but it was hovering way too high to be one. 
When I got home I could still see it from the garden over my gate.
After watching it for a few minutes it shifted back to cyan and rapidly moved away and down towards the horizon and vanished beyond it, similar in speed and appearance to how a ship from Star Trek warps from orbit.
Needless to say I was initially taken aback when it moved away but I quickly gained a degree of excitement.
