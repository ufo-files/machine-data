I am a former Police officer and the owner-operator of a security firm. At approximately 9 PM on date above I walked to my car to survey the community park located between Windmill Village South and Windmill Village North. My car was facing E. There was no traffic noise, no activity or ambient sound at all.Inexplicably in the middle of my survey, I looked straight up to my right (W) and saw an object at my 1 o'clock. I saw three dull red solid lights that when measured at arms length where 6 inches across using my thumb and forefinger as measuring guides.They were in a chevron or equilateral triangle shape with one light in the front facing E the other 2 were in the N and S position. No movement was observed.There were NO reference points. My second observation of object(there are NO time references,no sound,no markers)it had traveled to the E sky at my 11 o'clock. There was no apparent change in altitude or configuration. My only thought was "this is not an aircraft". It continued eastward. I broke visual contact and drove away with NO RECOLLECTION of the incident for 18 days. On 1/14/15 I had one brief thought-it surfaced like a bubble- about three red lights and it went away immediately. On 1/25/15 I was in general conversation with a business associate when the thought/memory again resurfaced, I shared what I experienced with my friend and then my wife. Several additional facts have eluded me until now. 1.) I had NO peripheral vision while observing the craft, no stars or sky.I was experiencing an extreme type of "tunnel vision". 2.)I turned to face the second point of observation without ever recalling that I changed my body position 3.)I drove away resuming patrol with no recall for almost a week. 4.) I carry binocs, cell/camera phone and a Samsung Sureshot camera for work purposes 5.)at no time was there a conscious thought to record this event-no emotion or curiosity which is in opposition to my professional observation and logic based thinking. Closing thought:it appears that my later recalled observations may be as or more significant thant the original object sighting over Buckeye.
