{cak} Silver cigar-shaped object with black patch on the side

I was letting the dogs out. It was a clear October morning with little to no wind. I looked up at the neighbor's house, and just hovering above it was a silver cigar-shaped object with a black patch on its side. It wasn't moving or making any noise. I watched it for a few seconds, then it moved ever so slowly in a straight line to the northwest, then just disappeared. Or, more accurately, it looked like it was sucked sideways out of the sky. No noise through any of this.

I went inside to get my camera, which was right inside the door. I was hoping whatever it was it would come back. Instead, it looked like a flock of white birds were flying NW. Again, there was no noise, and the birds looked large and all held the same position, like they were simply gliding with wings fully extended. I watched them for probably a minute--they just kept on coming, flying in the same direction. I took a few photos, which are too far away to see much. Enlarged they look like birds. In a few there is a straight item in the distance that looks like it has a black spot on it.
