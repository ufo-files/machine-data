(GS) While driving noticed a stable tube shaped object in sky, with some thing fluttering in front of it

I was driving down route 20 passing through Pingree Grove in Illinois, around 10:45 this morning, Im a cloud enthusiast, my eyes caught a straight white line in the sky about 2 inches long and there was something in front of it,or back, that seemed to be in the state of flux that I really couldnt make out the shape,I tried to take a picture as I was driving but could not, as I drove the to objects were still in the sky for several minutes.. I kept thinking stop and get a picture, but couldnt because construction was going on a bridge right as I was passing under I 90, on 20 going north towards the TA truck stop, as I started to pull over the object that was in front of the tube shaped (white) object pulled away then it seemed to then take form into a silver oval object, which could of been the sun reflection on the object, and took off, the other white tubular object seemed to take off the other direction north with in seconds it disappear. And all I could think of is did any of those guys working on the bridge see that.
