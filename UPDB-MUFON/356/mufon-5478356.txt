First seen orange object making very fast movements randomly. I flashed intense led lights at object which then blinked out. 10 minutes later an object circled our tent at 20 feet at high speed.

Sitting outside out hunting tent in a remote logging area south of Lodgepole Alberta.Observed two orange objects moving erratically. Having a close standoff encounter with a large craft 12 years earlier we both armed ourselves. I flashed my intense multiple led light in Morris code at the objects which eventually blinked out. Sitting around our fire about twenty minutes later my partner noticed a very fast red/orange ball moving past my right shoulder. I never seen it until it made a circle around our tent then disappeared over my left shoulder. The ball like object was about 20 feet above us. Never seen anything more after that night. We both worked in engineering all our lives and have never encountered this type of technology even after retirement.
