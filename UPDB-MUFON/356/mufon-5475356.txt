{nd} A large cigar shaped ship. Blinking lights but dark,

I was about 10 years old. I looked out my window and told my brother which slept in the same room I see a Lighting bug. I remember him saying to soon and he came over to look out the window. The blinking light kept getting bigger. He was 12, said a plane. And laid back down. I kept watching. No noise and getting closer. I went and woke up dad. My dad got up at that time could hear a low hum. Them my bother came in and said we need to go outside. There was a cigar shaped craft about 100 feet long going slow. No noise going straight north to Lake Michigan. We watched it going by. And in a few minutes it dives. Dad thought it crashed. Called someone. So now watching TV I thing it went into the lake.
