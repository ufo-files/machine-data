{tjd} Bright orange moving object that ejected two smaller object, then became larger with beams of light that looked like search lights that radiated 20 feet in all directions.

My husband and I were taking our dog for a walk. I first noticed the object as I stepped into the street in front of our house. When we first saw the object(s) I thought the bright orange object was a plane in the sky. It caught our attention when it ejected two smaller objects that remained dark orange for a moment before they became so small they seemed to disappear. The main object suddenly seemed larger, with a tail and became bright white with a great deal of what looked like mist/smoke all around it.The mist gradually dissipated then it became even brighter and seemed to shine beams of light in all directions that I can only describe as bright as search lights that radiated at least 20 feet in all directions. It slowly moved SW until it disappeared. It remained silent the approximately 10 minutes we observed it.
