{jpg} Video of slow moving very large 500 meter size object, gold to silver/white, rotating

At exactly 5:00AM, there were to objects moving due west to east, very slowly at what seemed high altitude. The first object looked extremely large and geometric, like a large solar sail. It rotated counter clockwise at a rate of about 9 secs. The rotation made the object appear flashing gold, then a dull grayish silver. The larger object was trailing a smaller pulsing white light similar to an aircraft strobe light. The flash rate was 3 seconds prior to the larger objects apparent gold flash. In fact, it wasn’t that much of a flash. It faded into and back out of magnitude ( -3 to 4 ) over the 9 second flash rate. Both objects traveled about 20 degrees of sky in exactly 3 minutes, the length of the video. Thatt would be pretty slow for an aircraft or a satellite. If I had to paint a picture, what I saw looked like a giant tumbling gold solar sail, counter rotating at a 9 second rate, being followed or being trailed by a large piece of silvery debris rotating at the same rate. The video is poor, but I wanted to document as much as I could despite the lack of resource.
