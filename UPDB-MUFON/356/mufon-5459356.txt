A bright light grabbed my attention when it appeared to fall almost like a "shooting star". It was not until it stopped in its path, and hovered, that my mouth was left ajar. The light then began to VERY slowly travel towards to the E until it was out of sight. 

I have worked in aviation while serving in the military, so I have knowledge of aircraft lighting and what they do, and do NOT, do. 

Since I also live in a small city in California's high desert, I am used to seeing "shooting stars". This was not what I witnessed.
