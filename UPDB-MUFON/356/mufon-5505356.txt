{cak} UFO in same location for 1 hour and changed shape

UFO sighting report
Received by phone by ASD on 1/16/2014
(Note: contact information is for ASD since witness wishes to remain anonymous)

The witness, called to report a sighting that she had last night. The witness described an octagonal shaped craft outlined in red and black lines along the edges, and a soft glowing hazy light illuminating the entire exterior of the craft. 
She observed the craft with the naked eye, through her glasses, and also through binoculars. The craft looked different while observing it through each different item. At first it appeared as an octagon, then as a disc, and finally as a cross with evenly placed lines. 
She observed the craft was located at the upper right side of the moon from her position. The moon is currently in the ENE sky and low in the sky at this time of night. The moon was full on 1/15/2014. 
The witness said that she could not tell how far away the object was but that it was the size of a pea or aspirin held at arm1s length.
The witnesses has a good high-quality camera on her cell phone and used it to snap some photos (she has not sent them to me yet) but afterwards her zoom quit working on the phone. She had no problems with it prior to this incident. 
She observed the object for approximately one hour, during which time it did not change position. She observed the stars moving, however. 
The witness then went inside the house and did not look for the object after that. She wanted to report the sighting to see if anyone else saw the same thing.
