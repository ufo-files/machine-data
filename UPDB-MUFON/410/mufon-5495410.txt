{cak} Three white lights in a triangular array moving above roadway.

I was leaving for work at my usual early hour (about 5:25AM). I live well away from city lights so at that hour everything is black. I made my usual right turn toward the highway when I noticed what I initially thought was a light aircraft moving well above the road and in a northerly direction. I am an aircraft enthusiast and when the MKE active is 7R, it is not unusual to see inbound traffic out of the southwest/west. This was not an airliner although I thought it might be a bizjet headed for Crites. The unknown had only three (3) white lights arranged in a triangle. Absolutely no nav lights or blinking strobes. My route has me turning right and then up a slight grade that gives you a good view (approx. 20 miles) of the airspace north to Waukesha. In the 20-30 seconds that the object was out of my sight, it had vanished. The usual array of TV news choppers can be seen easily at the same distance if they are out and about. Due to the darkness, no physical form was visible.
