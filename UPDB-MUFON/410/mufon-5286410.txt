Few Glowing lights that turned into many lights

So first thought I had witnessed maybe flares that had gone off nearby, but then viewed one main bright glowing light with a tail- thought was then a large comet perhaps. Was absolutely convinced for a few seconds it was something major entering our atmosphere with a trajectory looking to hit earth. But very soon after relaxed a little enough to notice it wasn’t careening toward earth perhaps, because it was too slow for that I believe. I continued to watch and noticed it seemed to level off and hover in same area of the sky, while simultaneously almost morphing/breaking into multiple glowing balls of light that over the course of the video seemed to join then separate , and configure in a most odd configuration. Couldn’t make heads/tails of it whatsoever! Lol. Just became awe inspired by it really,.. have never seen anything that bright in the sky with no seeming trajectory or pattern that I’ve ever come across. Just so strange , almost ethereal as best I can explain perhaps. Love to know what the heck it is?!
