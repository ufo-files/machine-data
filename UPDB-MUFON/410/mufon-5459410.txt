{tjd} red,blue,white pulsating lights; jagged motion; swooshing buzzing noise

In the middle of July 2014 at 11:00 p.m., I was outside relaxing when suddenly, in the Eastern sky, just over a nearby middle school's parking lot (Smithfield Middle School), there were pulsating red, blue, and white lights.  I couldn't see the craft's surface, only the pulsating lights.  The UFO stayed near the horizon as it kept somewhat stationary. Its stationary motion was very jagged: left (small increment), right (small increment), left (small increment), right (small increment), up (small increment), down (small increment), et cetera. After 45 seconds or so of staying somewhat stationary, it got increasingly closer to my home. It stayed 20 seconds hovering 300 feet above my home making a swooshing buzzing noise, then it went back over the middle school's parking lot. After one minute, it disappeared.
