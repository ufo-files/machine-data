[flg] silver colored cylinder dropped from the sky and flew west toward houston,tx.

at7pm on may 5 2013,me and my friend saw a silver colored object drop from the sky somewhat slowly and turned west toward houston. it was cylinder shaped craft with a polished silver finish. the craft flew west and about 8 -10 minutes later, two fighter jets appeared from the east and were in a south and west search pattern at the same alitude as the ufo was. in another 5 minutes later,two more fighter jets appeared from the northeast at the same alitude as the other jets and these two were in a side by side attack formation and headed due west toward houston. the object simply flew west toward the horizon. when we firt saw the object,we just looked with amazement at its strange vertical decent! My buddy is a former 
US army special forces and he said that it didn't look like any millitary aircraft that he had ever seen. the ufo made no noise and left no vapor trail at all.
