At 4 pm or just afterwards, my daughter and I observed at very bright orb directly overhead. This object appeared to be at high altitude and we saw the object between breaks in the high altitude clouds. Object appeared to be above the cloud cover. The very bright object which appeared as a very bright glowing orb moving to the northeast at a high velocity and had a high altitude. We had a clear view of the object for at least 10 seconds. It was not an jet or airplane. Just before and afterwards we saw jets that were stratospheric flying at about the same altitude. This object was at least twice the size of the the stratospheric jets.
