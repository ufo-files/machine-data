A 3/4 size of moon white maybe blueish orb in the sky.

I was playing a video game on the computer and happen to look out the window(which had blinds over the window). I saw whatever short amount of time I had until it was gone. I was calm for a little while but soon I felt like I was about to have an anxiety attack. It move straight across the sky maybe a little bit of turning. It just flew away the next half second.
