{nd} Looked in the sky and saw a triangle with 4 lights, one light going around and kept gliding over me

I was in the backyard on the phone with my neighbor and he told me to look up in the sky. He was the first one who saw and and then called me to look. He was outside tending to a fire. I thought it was some kind of spacecraft when I first saw it. It was a triangle shaped craft, from what I could see, that moved decently fast. It wasn't hovering, but gliding in a perfectly straight line in the same altitude the whole time. The UFO's lights showed the side of it just enough for me to tell that it was curved on the side. When it was flying right above me, I couldn't see the sky through it, but I couldn't exactly see it either. The fourth light erratically flew around the object and disappeared when it went on the top of it. During the sighting, i was in schock and disbelief in what I was seeing. i wanted someone else to see what I was seeing to make sure me and my neighbor weren't the only ones. After the sighting, I was still in disbelief. I couldnt believe if I actually saw it and I didn't want people to think I was crazy. It went over the trees by my house and I lost sight if each light as this happened until I couldn't see it anymore.
