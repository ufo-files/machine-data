Very large slow flying object with only red blinking lights visible over Napa County

On 03/04/2014 @ 2135 I went outside to secure my vehicle for the night. Standing in my darkened rural driveway, the sound overhead is what caught my attention and caused me to pause and look up. The sound was similar to a jet turbine but muffled. The sound came from the south. I was looking up and towards the south when at 2140, 4 red blinking lights in a horizontal line appeared from behind trees at 60-65 degrees above the horizon. My own words to myself at that time were "my God that is huge". The relative positions of the lights remained constant and did not variate and moved exactly the same relative to each other. The spacing between the lights was very large, larger than any aircraft lights I have seen before, estimating 100ft-300ft between each light. The lights appeared to be moving from south to north. At 65-75 degrees above the horizon, the lights then gently turned toward the east and continued traveling in that direction. After turning, I continued to see the red blinking lights but there appeared to be approximately 6 visible from that aspect. I continued to observe the lights until they disappeared from view. I could not make out any physical structure that the lights were attached to, perhaps due to the cloud cover blocking out background stars. I would estimate the altitude at 3000ft-5000ft, using a nearby foothill 1.5mi away, with a known elevation of 2000ft that the lights traveled towards. The sky had 100% cloud cover, estimated cloud ceiling at no more than 10000ft. Outside air temp was approximately 50*F. Travis AFB is approximately 15mi ESE from this location.
