Weird shadow like Boomarang disc

4/16/2012
Around 12:37(AM) my girlfriend and I were watching the sky, browsed an app on my mobile device to watch satellites, and then I looked straight up and even tho at night I started to make a shape out(12:40 AM). It was directly over me heading South/West. It looked pretty large, bigger than planes Ive seen before, made no sound whatsoever. It had an almost Stealth bomber look(the boomerang shape), it had blurry outlines like a haze almost. Very misty and black. 

Even with the night sky, due to the light pollution, I was able to see the blackness of this object. Id say it couldve been a few hundred feet from wingtip to wingtip. It was also flying very low especially for an aircraft and judging by the height I am positive I wouldve heard a sound. As I watched it fly for about 6 seconds it seemed to just vanish, almost ghost like(about 30 degrees South/West). My best guess it was flying at an altitude of around 300-550 feet. There was no lights visible to blinking and behind the whole wingspan seemed to look distorted almost as heat waves come up off a road on a hot day. Never seen anything like this in my life(even after living on various military bases).
