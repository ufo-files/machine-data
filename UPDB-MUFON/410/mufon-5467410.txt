{jpg} Observed object flying across sky at high altitude on straight line path

I was trimming a tree in my front lawn on Saturday afternoon, July 7th, at 1:30 pm, and while I was looking straight up in the clear blue sky I saw a white object fly by over head. At first I thought that it was a bird, but it was extremely high for a bird to be flying over. Not only that, but I did not see any flapping of wings or any wings in general. This object was flying on a direct straight line at an incredible rate of speed. I have seen jetliners very high in the sky fly over my home and you can make out the shape of a plane and see the contrails. This object had neither of these. It was moving faster than any jet, but not as fast as a shooting star. I have seen the International Space station fly over at night so I thought it may be that. When I checked the website for the Space station it listed the time for it to fly over my area at early in the morning at dawn and early in the evening at dusk. Again, I was struck by how fast it was going and how straight it1s path was.
