Appeared to be long contrail that stayed in front of my view of the moon for over 2 1/2 hours

Two nights ago I went outside and noticed what appeared to be a very dense contrail that stretched from one side of sky to other side. I was not able to notice any planes or anything flying. The trail was about the same diameter as the moon was from my perspective. The entire time the trail somehow maintained its position in front of the moon for the 2 1/2 hours I was watching. There weren't any other clouds however I later saw in my video there was possibly another trail much further away and not anything like what I had seen. I took about 4 videos which are low quality due to it being night time. I edited one video and you are able to see this trail much better.
