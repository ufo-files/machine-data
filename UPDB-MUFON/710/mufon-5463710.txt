Appeared at first like a shooting star but slower, but faster then a fighter jet. Seemed like it would drastically slow then speed up again.

I was outside looking for clear skys to possibly pull out my telescope and view the stars while my kids played. It was approximately past 6 pm when what I thought was a shooting star. I quickly told my son to look at the object that I just seen when all of a sudden it slowed down then took off quickly and then kind of dissappeared because of the clouds. I was following it in a straight line and I saw it again slow down again and once again took off. It then disappeared. There were planes in the area. This object was not a commercial jet or a fighter jet. At first I really thought it was a shooting star. But the way it slowed down then picked up speed really left me amazed, and surprised. My older son got a glimpse of it, but then confused it with a plane that was towards the emd of the site but traveling in opposite direction.
