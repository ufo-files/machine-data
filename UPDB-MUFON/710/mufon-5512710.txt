{cak} MANCHESTER CT - glowing streak and glowing ball of light hovering, than dropped

For the last several weeks I have seen 3 or 4 times seen something, very odd. I am driving down Hartford Road in Manchester, heading toward East Hartford. I look to my left over Route 384, and mid way in the sky I see this golden orange yellow streak across the sky. I think it is a plane, but it isnt. AT the end of the streak is this hovering ball of light. I pull over to watch it, and it is scary because at first you think, OMG is that a falling star,no, is that a comet? No. Is it a medeor?  I thought I would hear about a meteor on the news but never did.  So after waiting awhile watching this ball of light hover and glow a bright bright orange yellow, it drops.  It literally drops , bloop, gone.  I would say it was at least 50 miles from where I was on Hartford Road.  I saw this several times, and beleive so much now it is a UFO that I am going to go buy a video camera. If anyone else in the area has seen this please let me know. It happens around 3-5 pm around rush hour time.
