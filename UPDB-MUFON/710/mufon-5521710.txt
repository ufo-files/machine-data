Star-like Flashing Rainbow Lights (Not stars/planets) - Two videos (on youtube)

Last night I went into the bathroom (lights out) and happened to look out the window and saw a "star" that was flickering different colors. I figured it was an airplane, and since I love airplanes, I just watched. However, it did not move. I watched for several minutes and the colors kept changing, but it did not move. I started to realize this was something weird, so I went outside and noticed there were three other similar "stars" that were changing colors wildly, so I went back in and grabbed my camera. I was able to film off and on for several hours Saturday night. My camera could not fully focus on the brightest object, but was able to capture the color change. These objects were "fixed" along with real stars, so Im assuming they were outside earths atmosphere, just hovering. Sunday night, we looked and they were all in the exact same location. The close-up high resolution photos and video convince me that these are not stars. I have uploaded two files on youtube and have emailed news stations to be on the look-out tomorrow in hopes that they will be there again. Both videos are on youtube for faster viewing and sharing.
