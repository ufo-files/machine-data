{rjl} Camera trap photos taken in the Florida Keys (Lower Sugarloaf) show curving streaks and glowing objects in sky. One of these is detailed and square.

These photos were taken over a period of years by a camera trap aimed S from a beach on Lower Sugarloaf Key. This is a very small sample of many photos of streaks in the sky that were taken. A daytime photo is provided for reference. All photos were taken toward or over water (Straits of FL). The camera was set to be triggered by movement and had a 10 second delay between successive photos. None of the images were witnessed in real time, only when camera data was reviewed. There is no reason to believe the camera was tampered with as this was a remote area visited by few people.
