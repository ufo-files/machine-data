{dr}Silver, reflective, cigar shaped object hovering above Corridor X (I-22) as I was getting on the interstate at the Coalburg, AL. exit.

My daughter & I were approaching the on ramp to get on I-22 at the Coalburg exit. We both observed an object that was shaped like a cigar & was a very shiny silver color. It reflected the sun.
