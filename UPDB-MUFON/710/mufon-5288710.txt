'Strange Cloud'

While having coffee on the balcony, I typically set up my action camera, record until I leave for work, then filter through the data at another time. During the filter process I use the slider bar to speed up the video and noticed a 'cloud' that is not like any cloud I've ever seen before... It wiggles.

That is Tampa Bay, looking towards Tampa from Clearwater. In fact, the city is almost visible just to the left of the sunrise. That is the Bayside Bridge and its less than 1 mile away. The plane is landing at St. Pete International (PIE) which is less than 5 miles from the camera's location, due south and the plane is only a few hundreds feet from the bridge as the airport is directly where the bridge hits land again. Though it's hard to tell, the cloud looks closer than the bridge/plane in my opinion.

The cloud enters the frame at approximately 3:30 just above the plane as it exits the frame.

This video is not edited in any way, HOWEVER... in order to see the true nature of this cloud, I suggest you SPEED IT UP. What originally looks like a slow moving cloud, all of a sudden, seems to have a strange path. Even though I sat there drinking my coffee, I never noticed it until I filtered through the content.

I don't think it was a cloud, but I also don't think it was a craft.

Has anyone ever seen or recorded anything like this before?
