{nd} Star like object in the afternoon

I was patrolling south side area as a security officer. I looked up saw a cloud a bright object which I thought was a jet at first reflecting from the sun. Moving way too slow and about 30,000 ft up. Similar to a star but during the day. Stayed moving slow then hovered for about 5 minutes. I checked it with a tree to see if it's moving but it was not it then made a u turn and hovered again for a couple minutes the fmduration was about 22 minutes in a very small space of sky. Feelings were excitement I'm well aware of how jets and satellites move and this was not either. Since it was bright I lost the object once but managed to get a photo. It eventually very slowly flew off towards the west. Not a blimp way to high not balloons. In the video you'll have to zoom it will come out of the cloud around 19 seconds before the video ends.
