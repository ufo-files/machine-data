5 objects 4 of which moved around the larger fifth hovering then slowly moved north

I spotted a light in the sky and upon closer look determined there were 5 total. The 4 smaller lights look like stars, but there was too much ambient light for them to be stars. The 4 objects were moving erratically around the 5th object which was larger. I then had my wife come look. She is used to me pointing out things in the sky partly due to me stargazing and also from my interest in aircraft. She saw the items as well, which were now slowly moving in a N/NW direction. After about 5 minutes the smaller 4 objects faded in brightness to where I could no longer see them. As the larger object continued to move towards the N (almost in a direction that would intersect Polaris I’d visible at the time) it continued pulsing then suddenly disappeared from sight.
