Object moving slowly at what looked between 1000-2000ft.

Myself and a friend observed an object moving slowly at a height of about 1000ft on a sunny day.  It was a sunny day and the object reflected the sun every now and then as it looked like it was rotating in the sky.  The shape seemed to have straight edges. We obsereved it for about 10 minutes, during this time it moved slowly east, north-east and gradually climbed higher into the sky.
I could not wait any longer than the 10 minutes and had to head home. The object was still observable in the sky when I left. This all happened in the city of Munich, Germany.
