{nd} At boot camp at 5:30 am doing sit ups out side, saw shape flying in sky moving very fast than stopped mid air, moved slowly to the right stayed for 2 min than took off very fast to the SE and disappeared

I went to boot camp class that is held outside at Discovery park in Gilbert AZ and we started off at 5:30am doing planks than turned over to do sit ups and she was telling us to look up at the stars so we did not hurt our necks and I saw this dim light moving very fast towards us about the same distance as you would see a plane and than stopped to the left of me and moved slowly towards the right and stopped again and set there for about 2 min than shot off above our heads at a high rate of speed and disappeared in front of my eyes. I lost track of my class because I could not take my eyes off of the object.
