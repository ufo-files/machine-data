flash / explosion

Wednesday the 10th February at approx 11pm I was in the backyard looking at the stars as it was a moonless night and the sky was quite clear. I was looking north from my position, I noticed a flash of orange yellow in the sky, I thought at first it was an unusual comet burning up as it didn’t have trail, but as i continued to watch the sky i noticed what looked like a satellite travelling from the south to the north but slightly left of the area of the flash / explosion. it began to slow and its light began to fade until I could briefly see an outline of an object and then disappear totally as it approached the area of the flash / explosion but still to the left.  Then a few a seconds later a second satellite type object appeared flying the same path as the first one but went further north before fading out like the first object, after continuing to watch for a few minutes more I spotted a third satellite type object moving from the west to east and did the same it’s light faded out until it disappeared very close to the area of the explosion.  I have a military and aviation background they reminded me of military vehicles giving each other mutual support whilst moving in on a target / object.
