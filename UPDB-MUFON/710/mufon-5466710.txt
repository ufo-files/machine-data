I saw 3 triangular like vessels traveling south in a perfect half V formation from my balcony in Red Deer Alberta

I came out side on my balcony to have a smoke early morning. I looked up at the stars like I always do. I saw 3 perfect triangular objects flying in a perfect half V formation without any sound at all flying south towards Calgary. they were a very dull orange color an no sound at all. I have never seen anything like this ever and it scaired the hell out of me. I lost site when they passed the building next to mine.
