On August 2nd , 2013 , I spotted what looked like a classic "orange sphere" or "orb" just south , south east of me about 60 degrees above the horizon in the sky... It was moving ever slowly north bound... I stood and watched it for about a minute , then I yelled in the house for my wife to come and see it... When she opened the door and came outside , it blinked out... Then a few seconds later we saw it flash with a bright white light and shoot westward across the sky (90 degrees from it's original trajectory) very quickly at an unbelievable speed... Then , disappearing once again... I asked her if she saw that and she confirmed yes she did...
