{cak} large illuminated triangle. seen on my way home from work early morning. couldn't stop looking at it.

Im a musician. On my way home from a show my now ex wife and myself stopped the car and observed a huge triangular craft, lighted on all sides that we could see with an orange/gold light. Made no sound but you could feel it. We watched it for quite a while, what seemed like a half hour but must have been more like three hours (due to the time we actually got home) after a while I felt like we better get out of there, can't really explain why, but I knew it was time to leave. Has haunted me since that day. Hopefully sharing will give me some relief. Never lost sight of it, just kinda knew to leave.
