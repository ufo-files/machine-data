{rjl} observed a formation of lights two columns hovering then moving east then dimming

We were sitting in our living room on new years eve 2013-2014 waiting for our neighbors to start going crazy with celebration. At 23:59 PT many firecrackers, sky rockets and cherry bombs were heard along with a couple of loud muzzle-loaders and a couple of 9mm/22 cal sounds. My wife kept telling me to look out my north-facing window at the rockets that were exploding in our view from our second-flooor apartment. I looked several times noticing the bright stationary lights through the left corner of our window. Several more fireworks went off and about 2 minutes later at 00:01 my wife said "What is that?" I said "What?" I looked and saw two columns of lights glowing low red-orange hanging in the distance through the center of the north window (a sliding glass door actually). As I watched, the lights altered their formation and formed a wedge shape. Then straight columns and then I went to get a camera but my wife observed them changed to an inverted y formation and then flicker and fade out of site.
