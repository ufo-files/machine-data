{rjl} Clearly not helicopter, jet or blimp. No wings nor anything protruding from craft. Quiet and no trail

I just got off of the south Lake Tahoe ramp onto the highway at around 12:45 pm on Thursday Sept 20, 2018 and noticed something in the sky that looked weird. 4-6 cylinder or pill shaped objects closely together in a V formation with one a ways behind by itself. They were stationary or hovering. No noise detected nor any trail left behind. They looked to be silver or chrome. No wings detected and nothing seemed to protrude from craft. They were high above tree line. I was the only one in my car at the time and was driving on the highway so unfortunately I didn't get any footage or pictures.
