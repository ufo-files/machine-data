{rjl} I seen 3 lights in a triangular formation.

My girlfriend and her mother spotted an object flying over the house and called me while I was at home hanging out with her cousin. We went out looking for it. As we gave up and was driving home on East River Rd. I spotted 3 lights in a triangular like formation. They appeared to be stationary or moving very slowly at most. I immediately pulled over and took pictures before they vanished about 15 seconds later. We were excited but I was skeptical When I first saw it I figured it may have been flying lanterns but after watching my girlfriends video and talking with her and her mother made me think otherwise.
