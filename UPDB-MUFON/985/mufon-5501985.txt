{cak} seen one solo near 275 then seen three scattered slightly near Beechmont

My girlfriend and I were driving down 275 towards Beechmont-Amelia exit when i seen what appeared to be a glowing red light/object. i knew immediately that this was nothing i had ever seen before. She however was not very amazed by it. I took a picture of this light with the iPhone and when zoomed in on it, it was a perfect circle. We then ran an errand and on the way back through Beechmont, she pointed out three or so of these lights that appeared to be hovering in place as one of them was darting back and forth from the other two as if trying to be protective of them. We missed our exit and turned around less than a minute later and these lights were gone. Once in Eastgate, we seen a white light moving slowly and we pulled over to get a look at this one and it stayed in place for about thirty seconds then started moving again fairly fast while making no sound. There are many airports in our area and I for one have never seen a plane or anything else for that matter that looked like this did.  I would love any information i could get on what i seen.
