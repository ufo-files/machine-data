STARLIKE OBJECTS

I have seen these things on several ocasions on verious nights. They appear very bright mooving slowly with a slight zig zag motion as they travel in a certain general direction. They appear either dimm and change to bright and back to dim or disapearing all together Or they appear bright and then dimm or disappear and reappear,various senarios. This happens throught the night in different parts of the sky. My first thought is satalites or some other man made objects but the zig zaging and dimming and brightening is confusing.
