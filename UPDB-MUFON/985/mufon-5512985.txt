(GS) Bright glowing orb stayed in one spot for four hours, looked like a star.

I was at work when i noticed it around two oclock. I spotted it because it was very bright like a star in the daytime. I first thought it may have been a balloon but but it didn't move an inch for a good four hours. It was stationary, glowing, getting bright then dim. I was very interested and confused about what it was. It completely vanished after four hours, didn't see it go in any direction it was just gone.
