2 large red lights, with smaller sequential red lights trailing behind. Occurred twice within 1 minute

Sitting out on our beach house balcony looking South over the Oil Rigs in the ocean. 2 large red lights appeared with a series of smaller red lights sequentially lighting up to the right of it. The large red lights were staggered, the bottom light being slightly to the right of the top (our assumption was that it was one craft). The lights were lit up for approximately 10 seconds and all shut off at the exact same time. One minute later the same lights appeared and disappeared. My mom ([removed/CMS/fgc]) and I saw both events occur. After the first event, we notified my older sister who caught the second event happen. Immediately after the events (approx 5-8minutes after) 2 military helicopters were circling the island for over an hour, neither were visible on public radar.
