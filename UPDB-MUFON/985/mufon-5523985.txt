Decending light

This situation happened when I was around 19 driving with my brother. We were on a stretch of highway (20/131) leading north from Grand Rapids, Michigan to Cadillac. As I was driving with my brother in the passenger seat we noticed on the straight stretch of road that there was a really bright light to the left of a large hill top. The object looked like a really bright star that I thought was unusually bright. It was in my line of sight as we drove on the straight stretch for miles and hoovered. I didn't make much of it the light nor did I discuss the light with my brother as I drove. After awhile the bright light that was stationary dropped behind the hill top. I initially thought well it might be a car light behind me shinning on the windsheild so I didn't mention anything to my brother. My brother after about 10 seconds after seeing the light drop said did you see that light drop. Then we discussed the fact that it couldn't have been car lights since it dropped behind the hill. The object was a few miles out from the car and fell way too fast to be a plane. The object went straight down and did NOT wobble on the way down. The object was perfectly round in shape and extremely bright like a flash light in the sky.
