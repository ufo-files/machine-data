3 glowing peachy orange oval shaped ufo's decended quickley from the sky above my head and flew togehter in a intermingling formation, two disappeared and one hovered above my head and decended slowley down in an arch and above the building behind me.

I was rollerblading in a tennis/basketball court in Granville Island Vancouver BC around 10:00 Pm, the lights went out and it was time for me to take off my roller blades. So I sat down on a bench and proceeded to take them off in the dark. A low flying plane caught my attention and I looked up also noticing an interesting looking star just above it, so I watched the star for between 5 and 10 minutes.  As I was looking at the star A bright Orange light attracted my eyes off to the side and that's when I saw 3 illuminating objects intermingling together and around each other. Within a couple of seconds two UFO's disappeared leaving one on its own hovering what seemed to be as high as the low flying plane above me. It sat for a few seconds and then glided in a descending arch downward and over the building behind me with no sound. As the object was descending I perceived it to be larger than a plane (longer, wider, and thicker). It looked higher up than the plane to start with but descended to what I perceived to be closer than the plane and possibly just above the short building behind me. My perception of height and size may or may not be accurate due to my basic understanding of size and distance, but It looked close to me and large.
