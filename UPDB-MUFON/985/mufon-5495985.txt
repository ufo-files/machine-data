Stopped to pump gas and I looked up and saw an bright object hovering in the sky. I took a picture, looked to see if it came out and I took a few more. I finished pumping my gas and went to pick up a friend and came back to the gas station but the object was gone. The two jets flew by as we were looking up for the object.
