Thought this was a star at first but seemed to be blinking so got video camera and zoomed in

Was looking out of my window into the sky when i noticed a brighter than normal light in the sky that seemed to be blinking. at first i thought it may be a plane or helicopter but i decided to have a closer look with my camera. I set up my camera and using a 70/300mm lens with a 2x converter zoomed in on the subject. At first i took video just by hand and observed the orb shaped object. It looked like it was rotating and was constantly changing colour sometimes seeming brighter. I then noticed that the subject appeared to be moving slightly, a little bit up sometimes and down also from side to side. Just to make sure that it wasn't myself getting camera shake i then set my camera up on a tripod and recorded more footage.On seeing this object i felt very exited and after my initial thoughts on what it could be came to the conclusion that this sighting is either an electrical storm in the distance or something not of this planet.
