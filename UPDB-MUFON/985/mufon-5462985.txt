{jpg} Saw light on ground which moved upward then circled around to my right

In the summer of 1968 I arrived home at about 11:30 PM on a weekend night. As I turned in to the driveway I noticed a red light on the next hill to the south, approximately 1/4 mile from my house. At that time there was no garage there so I saw it immediately. When I first saw it I thought it was a Ford car (one with the large round taillights) with one light burned out and maybe the driver had his foot on the brake because it was brighter than just a taillight. As I was watching it began to rise up into the air slowly and with no noise or change of brightness.

After the object reached approximately 200-300 feet it stopped for a few moments and then began to move to the west, circling around over the woods. There is a factory located across what was a field at that time, about a tenth of a mile from my house. The object then hovered about 10 feet over the three phase transformers that powered the factory which was closed for the weekend. It remained there hovering over the transformers for between 2 and three minutes.

I felt no physical or psychological effects from the object, only a strong curiosity. I only stood there beside my car and watched it's actions.

After the time spent hovering above the transformers it suddenly shot straight up and was lost from sight in a second or less.

It appears from the Google Maps image that the transformer array is no longer there but at the time it was located straight out from the loading dock.
