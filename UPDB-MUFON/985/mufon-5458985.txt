{jpg} Stationary irregularly pulsing light with one dimmer light moving away from the brighter one

Was driving home on Hwy 6 and saw two lights in the sky and at first figured they were planes on a landing pattern for Houston HobbyAirport, located several miles to the north of us. I kept looking at them and the dimmer of the two started moving to the left (North, I assume) of the brighter one. The dimmer light isn't visible in the video from my dashcam. The brighter light was apparently motionless but gt brighter and dimmer with no regular rhythm. When I finally mentioned the light (the dimmer one had already faded), the brighter light started fading as well. 

Seemed too high to be a helicopter hovering, but that's the only "natural" explanation I could come up with. It's just that the light pulsed in a way I haven't personally seen before.
