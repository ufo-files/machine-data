{rjl} Golden sphere hovered for appr. 1.5 mins. then ascended to the stars.

My husband and I were watching television in our respective rooms when he started yelling, "Ma, look out the window, they're back". I ran to south facing window in my room and saw a brilliant golden globe hovering in the sky. I was ecxited because this is the first golden object we've seen. Usually they are white or red, blue, and green. I threw on my robe, grabbed my binoculars and ran outside to southwest corner of my building and viewed the object through binoculars. It was a golden sphere that when viewed through binoculars showed a reflective surface. If I held my arm out straight I could have covered it with an apirin tablet. I viewed it for about forty more seconds when it became extremely bright and ascended in a straight southwestly trajection until it was too small to see. I ran back to the house and asked my husband, "did you see that, too"? He confirmed that he did and that was it. Other than our adrenaline flowing from the recent event, the rest of the evening was uneventful.
