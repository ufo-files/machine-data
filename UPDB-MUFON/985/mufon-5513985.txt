UFO spotted off Highway 44 near by-pass
by Ofelia Garcia Hunter ofelia.hunter@aliceechonews.com | 
Posted: Thursday, November 22, 2012 10:36 pm 

About half a dozen people reported to police they saw an aircraft believed to be a UFO.

The reports were called in separately. The individuals described the UFO as being about 100-feet long in width and appeared round.

The reports said the UFO was beaming down a light near Highway 44 on the by-pass near Highway 281.

The calls came in about 1 a.m. on Thanksgiving day.
