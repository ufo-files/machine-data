4 spherical orbs in a chevron formation travelling southbound just south of Portland, Oregon

I was Operating a MAX train northbound along I205 and somewhere between Foster road and Powell Blvd I saw a woman with a stroller on the bike path that parallels the tracks holding up her phone, filming something in the sky. I looked and saw four spherical orbs in a chevron formation traveling southbound just south of Portland, Oregon. The orbs were much larger than aircraft warning lights and were perfectly spaced from one another. It looked like they were traveling south above the river valley. They were moving as fast as, if not faster than an airplane. Unfortunately I was only able to look for about five to ten seconds before I lost sight due to my direction of travel and my responsibilities involving the safe operation of the train.
