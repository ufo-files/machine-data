{cak} I saw a glowing object hovering.It split into two and both drifted slowly west.

Driving west on Cheyenne I saw a white glowing object above the Charlston mountain range.At first I thought it was an airplane with a short contrail coming from it.But it wasn't moving.It just hovered in one spot for a long while at a 45% angle.Then it split in half and both pieces were at an opposing 45% angles.Then one on the left stayed at that angle and slowly drifted west and over the mountains.The one on the right drifted slowly NNW, but did level to a horizontal position before vanishing into the distance.They were far away and glowed brightly,but it appeared I was looking at typical saucer shapes from a side view.
When I got to my destination I had urged several people come outside to look at the last remnants of this sighting.
