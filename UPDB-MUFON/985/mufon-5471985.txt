{jpg} Driving north bound looking east. noticed lights in the sky, pulled over and started filming. After filming the 18 sec video i watched the lights for 15 more sec then they disappeared.

I was talking to my brother on the phone while I was on my way home from work. He lives in Texas and I live in Colorado. I was driving Northbound and I turn my head to look East and I saw these bigger than a regular star type of light in the sky. As I watched, another one appears next to the one I was already looking at, then another one and then another one and then another one, making 5 total lights. I then said oh my gosh bro I'm seeing some crazy lights in the sky, he told me to film it and so I hung up and started filming, The lights were all doing the same thing. They were appearing and then disappearing as you can see in the video, the entire time I watched. Which was approx. 45 seconds. They then fluttered out and we're gone. I waited approx. 5 to 10 minutes to see if they would reapear and I never saw anything else after that.
