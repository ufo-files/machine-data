Orange fireball traveling south to north

This was an orange fire ball, traveling slowly from south to north over the west part of Mesa, AZ. Traveling too slow to be a meteorite or space debries. Did not appear to be any known type of aircraft. As it passed over head, it made no sound. As it passed to the northern sky, the color changed from orange to white, then disappeared.
