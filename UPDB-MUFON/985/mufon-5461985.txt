I was on my porch at home getting ready to go to friends house on the Rez. I watched a light appear and start moving southward. I jumped in my car and followed it to Marine park on Marine Dr near rez. I think it landed near there then rose from ground and headed south to Lummi Island and landed on a snowbank on North side. I left the park and headed to friends house and noticed a helicopter in the area and it headed toward the island also.
