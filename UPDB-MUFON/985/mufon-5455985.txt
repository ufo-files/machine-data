{cak} Lights rotating in complete unison spotted in sky, and were perfectly spaced apart

On January 16th, 2020 at 6:23 pm, my family and I were on our driveway. We were about to get into our car when my daughter yelled out “Why are all of those stars moving across the sky together?” We all looked up in amazement and shock as 12-15 starlike lights were moving through the sky, and were perfectly spaced apart. The row of lights appeared to cover a vast distance and appeared to be a string of lights at first. Then, we realized that after the lights would “disappear” on one end of the strand, they would reappear on the other side as if it were a rotating object. It slowly changed direction and then completely disappeared. What we saw of it lasted about three minutes and the video lasted about two. I am sending it to a professional to analyze. We will remember it as a family forever.
