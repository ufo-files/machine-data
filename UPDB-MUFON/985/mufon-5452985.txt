crash into water filled strip mine

on or around the above date, the object passed over my home and move to the northeast toward Carbondale Pa. The following day several people stated seeing the same object in the sky. It was said to have crashed into a water filled strip mine near Carbondale PA. That day military trucks arrived and the area was surrounded and cordoned off, no entry by anyone was allowed. Flashing red and green lights were seen under the water of the strip mine. Subsequently, there was a news release that it was a railroad lantern under the water. This could not be, the lantern did not flash and could n ot flash, but the lights under the water were flashing red and green in alternating sequence. A large flat bed truck was seen to travel down Rte 6, with military markings, with a large, seemingly oval or circular object covered by a dark green canvas tarp.
