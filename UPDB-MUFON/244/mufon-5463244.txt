{cak} The attached pictures were taken on my game camera over the last couple of years. I have actually seen similar lights at other locations.

Over the last couple of years, I have captured a variety of unexplainable lights on my game camera. I have attached several of the photos but I have more that are equally interesting and unique. My camera is on top of a hill a short distance south of town. It faces to the east and slightly upward to the sky. There are no houses or roads in the direction my camera is facing, only my deer feeder and oak trees beyond that. In some instances the lights seem to be as close as the animals also pictured.  Other times they appear to be in the sky well beyond the hill. I have found no evidence of any kind other than the pictures themselves. It is intriguing to me to have such a wide variety of lights and beams of light photographed from one location!
