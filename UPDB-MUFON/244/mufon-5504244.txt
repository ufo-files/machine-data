A bag like object floating and morphing in the sky with a light above & below it, viewed during daytime.

I was driving to work at about 11:33am on Monday morning the 10th of March 2014 , when I spotted something strange in the sky through the windshield of my car in front of me. It looked like a plastic bag floating high up in the sky constantly changing shape. I stared at the object thinking that its probably a plastic bag that has been carried up by the wind. A second later I noticed two small white shiny orbs(lights). One orb was above the object(bag) and the other orb was below it about 6 meters apart. While viewing the object ,the road that I was driving on at the time eventually came to a curve then after having to turn I could no longer view the object. When I got to work I did some research on the sighting and found a similar sighting that was reported on the 3rd of March 2014 in Scottsdale Arizona.
