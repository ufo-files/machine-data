Lights seen over Nottingham mid-late 80's

I recall seeing lights over my parents old house in Nottingham during the mid to late 1980's. I think it was around 1987. I used to attend Air Cadets in Stapleford during my early teens and one of the instructors lived nearby who used to give me a lift. When returning home which would have been between 9pm and 10pm I got out the car, said goodbye and begin to walk towards the front driveway. Something caught my eye and I looked up to see around 10 different coloured lights moving NW to SW above and beyond the roofline at a height of approximately 800-1000ft. I ran in to get the parents to come and view but they took what must have been 10 seconds to get to the front door by which time the lights had gone. I remember seeing a report in the local Nottingham Evening Post newspaper the following evening but looking through their archives I can find no mention of the event. I know more people witnessed this as it was in the newspaper but I can't find any information now that time has passed. I am hoping that Mufon has record of this event and if so confirm my failing memory but also add my sighting so you can track the movement of the objects based on other sightings.
