Caught UFO while filming weird planes by accident

I was filming these weird flights that have been going over my house. These flights have been equipped with strange lights that span from nose to tail and wing to wing, multicoloured. They sound like jets but a little louder. I was filming another flight (while I was clearly being watched from cars parked outside the field which happens every time I film these flights), and I uploaded it because it seemed weird. A week later, I watched it and realized that there could be a UFO tailing the flight. I, of course, did not see the UFO in person.
