South, SWest sky around 11:30pm, about 4 to 5 small, bright objects streaked across the sky in a formation. Much faster then a plane but slower then a shooting start

While in Lethbridge, Alberta around 11:30 pm on Sept 29, 2012 I observed 4 to 5 bright, round objects streaking across the Southern sky (200 degrees) in an organized formation. Much faster then a plane, slower then a satellite and not like a comet/meteor.

It was a full moon last night and these objects streaked across the South, South-west sky and disappearded into the bright moon light in 5-10 seconds.

I heard no sounds and objects were very far off on the horizon.
