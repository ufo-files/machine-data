{rjl} Fast moving object split into two and abruptly changed direction.

I was driving through the desert and witnessed a bright star like object moving southeast across the sky. The object then split into two and suddenly both objects changed direction, the more prominently lit object continued eastbound across the sky. Both objects eventually came back together and flew out of sight. The whole incident lasted about 2 minutes. I was completely surprised and I've never seen anything like that since.
