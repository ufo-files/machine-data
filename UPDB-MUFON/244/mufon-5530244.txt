Starlike UFO.

On August 29 2010 around 5pm I was outside with my family and I spotted what looked like a star in the sky and I pointed it out to my daughter, we both seen it. When we pointed it out to the rest of the family it shot off and disappeared. Then on August 30, 2010 I woke up at about 4 am and could see out the window that there were 6 star like objects but were moving very slowly and pulsating. They were gone by 4:50 am.
