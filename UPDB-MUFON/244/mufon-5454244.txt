For a week prior to sighting, what first appeared to be a satelite was being seen in the same general area (South Southwest) at roughly the same general time (10:00pm-10:30 PM). The object was silvery white and looked like a star, though it was was much closer and brighter. I tried to balance the phone on top of our mailbox to steady the shot.
What we we ended up with were 5 (1:00) videos of the object in its silve-white form. 2(1:0o) videos of it green and pulsing. And a single (1:00) video of the object seeming to strech and expand outward and reval spirals within its folds.
