Witness (A) and (B) on many occasions, while traveling together or at several locations around Jackson and Eaton Counties, have observed many unusual objects in the sky. The following nine events were related from a 23May15 interview with the witnesses; EVENT 2 of 9: While traveling south along M99 near the Eagle Rapids Medical Center, they observed a bright circular object at the tree-line to west, after turning down a side street to observe better, the object moved along slowly to the north, then shot up into the sky and disappeared.
