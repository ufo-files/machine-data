{td} Bright white light hovering over forest.

One evening from my front porch I saw a very bright round light hovering over the forest. It drifted around in a circular motion. It was there so long I had time to set up my spotting scope to see it better. Inside the bright light I could see two small red and blue lights inside the bright light, swimming around (like water bugs)in an erratic motion never touching each other. I watched it until I got tired of it and went inside. It has not returned.
