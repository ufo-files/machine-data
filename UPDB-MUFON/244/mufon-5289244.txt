Fireball with Aura

I am not sure of the exact date. The pictures I uploaded from my Google Drive have the date May 31, 2019 when I click for more details, but that may just be the date that they were backed up from my old phone to my Google Drive.

  That being said, one night, me and my girlfriend were staying up late hanging out at her house. I went to the back porch to smoke a cigarette and looked at the sky and in the southern sky I saw the biggest and strangest "star" I had ever seen. It was reddish orange so at first I thought maybe it was Mars or something but I soon had my mind changed.

  I will try to describe it as best as I could, but what it did next is very hard for me to put in words. It started like pulsing/emitting light, changing back and forth between red and orange and it had the craziest/most amazing "aura" I have ever seen. It was like solid but not. Almost like a cloud or liquid. The best analogy I can make is almost like how a squids ink changes and warps in water. It was mesmerizing. 

  After watching it for a while in awe, something in my head finally clicked and I said to myself I have to take a picture. I took out my phone and snapped a few quick shots, then turned around, opened the back door and yelled to my girlfriend to hurry up and come out because she has to see this thing. When I turned back around to look at the object it was gone. I didn't see how it left or disappeared but it was gone.
