{dr}Light hovering beneath clouds then ascends into space

Woke up at 4am to adjust my apartments thermostat. Then noticed a bright light through the patio slide door windows. I saw a big ball of light hovering. It was emitting randomly different colors of light from white to yellow to orange and red. It then ejected another small ball of white light which flew around for a few minutes and returned to the big ball of light. The big ball of light then slowly ascended off into space and disappeared.
