(GS) Sky watching at sunset. Spotted a short jet stream headed south. Within seconds the orb appeared and then vanished. Seconds later what looked like a jet appeared from the east heading SW criss crossing to the path of this

Since my initial sighting about 2months ago I look at the sky at sunset when it is clear to see if something will appear. About two weeks ago on 10/28/12 I was by Venice airport and saw a short jet stream fireball with no airplane causing this. All of a sudden an outline of a circular object or "orb" appeared then the stream and orb vanished. Within seconds in a different location another short jet stream appeared and nothing was visibly causing this. Keep in mind the sky was clear, no clouds. 

Today I witnessed another object heading South it changed shape and like the others made no noise. It was at about 15-20,000 feet high and moving at a very high rate. Seconds later what looked like a jet came from the east and headed across the path of this object. My guess was it was tracking or attempting to follow this object.

I researched on the web to see if anyone else had a similar experience or possibly witnessed this. To my surprise someone in Clearwater,FL about an hour north from where I am reported an incident just like mine. What is going on up there?! Each time I look up I sense that I may catch something. Almost like "they" want me to see them. Next mission I will break out the video camera and get some hard evidence. God bless.
