Bright lights over the high way with 3 beams up light down and one beam of light up

I was driving west on Rt 90 in Lake county. I am about a mile to the Rt 44 exit when I look up and see two bright lights over the tree line to the left of me. I notice three beams of light coming from each bright light pointing to the ground and one light beaming directly into the sky from its center. I knew there was three lights although I couldn't see the third because of the three beams of light pointing down. On my way back down the highway about 25 min later I looked for it again and it was gone.
