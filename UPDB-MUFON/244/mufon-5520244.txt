Stationary object. Did not appear to be standard aircraft.

I went outside to take out our garbage and get the mail when I noticed something that looked brighter than a star (which happened to be right nearby). I stared at it for a few minutes trying to determine if it was a conventional aircraft but could not identify any features to support this. After watching it change color and fade in and out, I went inside and grabbed my camera to take a few photos. Unfortunately they did not come out well, but it is apparent what I saw was larger and brighter than a regular star.

The object was stationary the entire time and did not appear to have any identifying features standard to conventional aircraft.
