ITHACA NEW YORK SIGHTING

RIGHT INMYSELF AND 4 FRIENDS STEPPED OUTSIDE IN THE BACKYARD TO HAVE A CIGARETTE. SHORTLY AFTER I NOTICE WHAT LOOKED LIKE THE BRIGHTEST AND CLOSEST STAR THAT ANY OF US HAD EVER SEEN BEFORE. WHILE OBSERVING THE BRIGHT SAR IT BEGAN TO DOODLE IN THE SKY. THEN IT MOVED UP AND DOWN AND GOT CLOSER AND THEN FURTHER AWAY. SOON AFTER THAT IT BECAME 2 OBJECTS IN THE SKT AND STARTED TO CHASE ONE ANOTHER . THEY SEEMED AS IF THE WERE PLAYING TAG. WE ALL WERE AMAZED AT HOW THEY ZIPPED ACROSS THE SKY INSTANTLY. THEY WENT FROM ONE SIDE OF THE SKY TO ANOTHER IN ONE SECOND. THEN THE 2 OBJECTS BECAME 4 OBJECTS. THEN THEY BEGAN TO MAKE SHAPES IN THE SKY. THEY FORMED A DIAMOND SHAPE AND STARTED TO .MOVE CLOSSER TO US AND THEN FARTHER AWAY...AS IF THEY COULD FEEL OR FEAR AND WHEN WE WOULD SETTLE DOWN...THEY REACTED TO OUR FEELINGS OF BEING FRIGHTENED AND OF CALMING AGAIN. SOON THEY BECAME 6 OBJECTS. I REMEMBER THEY MADE A STAR OF DAVID PATTERN IN THE SKY. AND SOON BECAME 10 0BJECTS THEN THEY BEGAN TO SWITCH BACK AND FORTH FROM HOVERING IN PLACE AND COMING CLOSER. AFTER A FEW MINUTES OF THIS THEY MANAGED TO KEEP GETTING CLOSER. AFTER A WHILE OF THIS I SUDDENLY REALIZED THAT HALF OF THE OBJECTS WHERE JUST ABOVE THE TREELINE AND THE REST OF THEM HAD MOVED BELOW THE TREES AS IF THEY WERE KEEPING GUARD. I BECAME VERY FRIGHTENED AND INSTANTLY BECAME FROZEN AND I KNEW THAT I WAS TRAPPED AND COULDNT ESCAPE IF I RAN I KNEW THERE WAS NO WAY I WOULD GET AWAY. I FELT THE FIGHT OR FLIGHT FEELING VERY INTENSELY. I REGRETED NOT RUNNING FOR MY LIFE. WE WERE ALL OUTSIDE NEAR A SMALL PND SURROUNDED BY TALL GRASS AND CATTAILS APPROXIMATELY 5 FEET IN HEIGHT. THERE WAS WHIPPING WINDS SO STRONG THAT THE GRASS AND THE CATTAILS WERE BLOWING WILDLY ALONG WITH OUR HAIR AND LOOSE CLOTHING (HOODIES AND JACKETS) AND I KNEW IN MY HEART THAT WE WERE SURROUNDED. I COULDNT SEE ANYONE BUT I COULD HEAR WHATSOUNDED LIKE MANY PEOPLEI IN THAT TALL GRASS ALL AROUND US. I MMEDIATELY REGRETTED NOT RUNNING WHEN I HAD THE CHANCE, I WAS CONSUMMED WITH FEAR AND THE THOUGHTS OF WHAT WAS GOING TO HAPPEN TO US AND I REMEMBER THINKING ABOUT HOW WONDERFUL IT WAS TO SEE THE LIGHTS AND THE SHAPES IN THE SKY FOR ALL THAT TIME AND HOW AMAZING IT ALL WAS. AND NOW I WAS FINDING MYSELF FEELING TOTALLY FOOLISH AND  SEEING HOW NOWIM TRAPPED AND WHO KNOWS WHATS ABOUT TO HAPPEN AND I KNEW ONE THING FOR CERTAIN....IT DIDNT FEEL GOOD ABOUT IT AT ALL AND I WAS COMPLETLY TERRORFIED!!! ....LOOKING BACK I CAN REMEMBER.THE AREA WE WE IN BEING LIT UP LIKE BEING UNDER A STREET LAMP..I COULD SEE MY FRIENDS ALL STANDING WITH ME WITH THEIRHAIR AND JACKETS BLOWING IN THE HIGH WINDS LOOKING TOTALLY FREAKED OUT...SO SUDDENLY WE ARE STANDING IN A WHIRLWIND AND THE AREA IS ALL LIT UP AND I FELT SURROUNDED AND COULD HEAR MOVEMENT IN THE TALL GRASS IN ALL DIRECTIONS...THEN THE WIND STARTED TO SETTLE DOWN OVER THE SPAN OF ABOUT WHAT SEEMED TO BE MAYBE 20 SECONDS.THEN I SAID "MAYBE WE SHOULD GO INSIDE" THEN MY ONE FRIEND SAYS " ID ONT KNOW MAN..MAYBE WE SHOULD STAY"...THEN ANOTHER SAYS."IM WITH YOU LETS GO IN"..NEXT WE ALL GO IN THE HOUSE AND THERE IS A CPLE OF PEOPLE INSIDE. NONE OF US SAY A WORD ABOUT ANYTHING WE JUST GO IN AND SIT DOWN...AFTER ABOUT 5 MINUTES THERES A LOUD KNOCK ON THE DOOR. ITS THE POLICE AND THEY JUST WALK .RIGHT IN THE HOUSE WHEN THE DOOR GETS ANSWERED AND THEY START WALKING AROUND SAYING "IS EVERYBODY ALRIGHT".."IS EVERYONE HERE AND ACCOUNTED FOR"?...THERE IS ABOUT 5 OR 6 OFFICERS AND I COULD SEE MULTIPLE POLICE CARS WITH THE LIGHTS ON IN THE DRIVEWAY THRU ONE OF THE WINDOWS....AS THE WALKED AROUND THE HOUSE ASKING IF WE WERE ALL OK AND ACCOUNTED FOR I REMEBER SAYING..."DO YOU GUYS HAVE A WARRENT"? THEY IGNORED THE QUESTION THE FIRST TIME SO I SAID IT AGAIN AND THEN THEY SAY "NO ...WE ARE JUST MAKING SURE YOU ALL ARE ALL RIGHT ..NOW YOU ARENT MISSING ANYONE. SO HAVE A GOOD NIGHT.." AND THEY LEFT.. WE ALL KIND OF LOOKED AT ONE ANOTHER ANDWERE IN SHOCK A BIT. BUT WE DIDNT DISCUSS IT AND JUST KINDOF LET IT GO. WE LATER WENT TO ANOTHER FRIENDS HOUSE AND AFTER BEING THERE FOR ABOUT AN HOUR ONE OF US SAYS THAT WE HAD A CLOSE ENCOUNTER. NONE OF THE REST OF THE GROUP SPOKE UP...IT WAS STRANGE. I REMEMBER FEELING LIKE IT WASNT A BIG DEAL. I RECALL THE FRIEND WHOS HOUSE WE WERE AT SAYING HOW HE THOUGHT IT WAS WEIRD HOW I WASNT SPEAKING ABOUT IT AND NEITHER WERE THE OTHERS AND HE POINTED OUT HOW HE FELT THAT WE HAD A MENTAL BLOCK IN OUR MINDS ON THE SUBJECT.. I DIDNT EVEN ACKNKOWLEDGE WHAT HE WAS SAYING. I NOW CAN REMEMBER TRYING TO IGNORE IT AND FOCUSING ON THE TELEVISION. IT WAS SURREAL TO SAY THE LEAST. I DIDNT SPEAK ABOUT IT TILL A COUPLE OF YEARS LATER. I HAVE SPOKEN TO 2 OF THE OTHERS WHO WERE PRESENT. ONE OF THEM REMEMBERS WHAT HAPPENED EXPLICITLY, THE OTHER HAS NO MEMORY OF THE OCCURANCE WHAT SO EVER. I PERSONLLY RECALL THE INDIVIDUAL WHO HAS NO RECOLECTION OF WHAT HAPPENED SAYING OUTLOUD WHILE WE WATCH THE FIRST OBJECT DOODLE...HE SAID "THOSE ARE ANTI GRAVITY MACHINES". HE HAS NO MEMORY OF THAT EITHER. IT WAS THE FIRST TIME I HAD HEARD THAT TERM "ANTI GRAVITY MACHINES". I KNOW IIN MY HEARY FOR CERTAIN THAT I HAVE REPRESSED MEMORIES OF THAT EXPERIENCE AND THAT I EXPERIENCED MISSING TIME AS WELL.
