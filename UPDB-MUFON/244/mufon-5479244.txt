{nd} Bright white round object shot across the sky

I was outside around 1am on Dec.3 2016 in pinconning mi. Taking my dog outside. I saw a bright white object shoot across the sky. It looked just like a huge meteor with a smoke trail really low. It lit up my back yard and trees. But this object made no sound. If a meteor was that big and low everyone should have heard it but it was completely silent.
