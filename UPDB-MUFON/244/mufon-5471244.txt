{dr}Three people observed a unidentifiable object floating across the sky then disapearing

New years 12:25ish am, Kaunakakai HI. Right after the neighborhood finished the 20 min long firework show in Ranch Camp. I was alerted by my father to "go look out in the sky what the f#@$ is that?" I was followed outside by my mother to the south side of the house. We witnessed a bright red-orange object floating east bound approx. 500-1000 ft off the ocean. It did not have any blinking lights and was just a steady brilliant glow. My father and I both agree it was probably doing less than 100mph. My mother and father are both skeptics when it comes to such things yet neither of them could process a skeptics response to what we were seeing. It continued on a eastern path for maybe 1min, then slowly turned south for 30 secs or so then slowly vanished. I was able to get some crappy cell video of it.. I'm sure dozens of people had to have seen it as everyone was still bringing in the new year.
