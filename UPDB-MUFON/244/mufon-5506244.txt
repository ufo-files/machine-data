{cak} 3 Greys in my room

My wife and I went to bed for the night in our master bedroom in the upstairs of our house. We had just sold our house and were getting ready to move so our bed was just a mattress on the floor. I like to fall asleep listening to something in my headphones, so I was lying in bed trying to fall asleep listening to Coast to Coast AM. I have trouble sleeping ever since coming back from Iraq. I know I had been lying there for at least an hour because of how far along in the show it was. I wasnt even sleepy yet so I was just listening intently to the show with my eyes closed when the audio started going in and out. It was then that the feeling that I wasnt alone came over me. I was lying on my ride side with my wife to my back, I opened my eyes and the room was lit in a pale orangish glow. I rolled over towards my wife and that was when I saw 3 typical Grey aliens standing in my master bathroom about 5 feet from my wifes side of the bed. I couldnt make out their faces. But I knew what they were from their big heads and how thin they were. I could see their outlines perfectly because of the orange glow that was emanating from the bathroom window that they were standing in front of. I could tell they were wearing some kind of tight fitting suit that was a darker color than their grey skin. I immediately started screaming, "What the F%$#, What the F%$#!" as I grabbed my wife in fear. She woke up and started screaming and then broke into prayer in here first language, Romanian. The 3 entities de-materialized into 3 orange glowing balls and floated through the walls of the bathroom. My wife was screaming," Whats going on." The orange glow faded quickly as soon as the last orb passed through the wall. About 3 seconds later there were 3 quick bright flashes of white light that came through all 4 of the windows in the bedroom and it was over. We had a room mate that shared a wall with our bedroom that never even woke up and I was screaming like I was in the middle of a firefight. My wife and I got up and looked out of the windows immediately but saw nothing. Needless to say I didn't sleep the rest of the night.
