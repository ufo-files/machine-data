smooth cylindrical slash too perfect to be natural

It was mid-day and I was standing in my backyard when I noticed a dark slash in the sky. The sky was cloudless but the haze left it looking more white than blue. Recently I emailed a photo of it to pilot friend of mnie who is a UFO buff and he said I should send a copy to MUFON. If you'd like I can send the raw photo (if you need it) for analysis. The trees in the photo are about 140 feet from my position and I'd estimate they are 30-40 feet tall. The object was south-south west of me and would appear to be over the Gulf of Mexico. I live just west of Pensacola NAS.
