object with lights

I was driving to get a coffee in the morning like I do nearly every morning. I noticed an object with lights in the air that wasn’t moving and I thought it was odd. As I continued to drive down the road it moved to the west but I noticed that there were no lights blinking. I started recorded at that time and it started moving north. When it went over top of where my car was it clearly had lights around it and had amber lights and it had no blinking lights. I couldn’t tell 100% but it looked disc shaped and the way it was moving was very odd. It was also flying extremely low.
