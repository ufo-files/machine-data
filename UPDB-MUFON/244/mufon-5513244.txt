{dr}Bright Gold light that came from the South went against the wind and headed south.

It was New Years eve and at the time I had not been drinking.It was very possible that I was going to have to go to work so I stayed sober.It was getting dark and I had seen people setting of fire works around the neigborhood around me.I was watching off and on and had to take my garbage out.I have to walk to a dumpster wich is located in the south of my place.As I walked I heard the fireworks around me and out of the due south I saw a bright gold light accendeing into the air.At first I though it was a firework but then it started getting bigger and going against the wind.It made no sound whatsoever then realised what I was looking at.I did not have my phone with me so I walked quickly back to my home and was watching this thing the entire time.Now also realise we had overcast skies and this thing stayed blow the clouds.It seemed to pace me as I went to my home.As I went in and grabbed my phone it was now heading due west.I was able to snap two photos and a realy bad video from my phone.There is an airport near by but I can tell you this was not a plane,a helcoptor,or a firework.It was a bright almost gold color and it was facinateing but a touch frightening.But I watch as long as I could until it dissappeared in the distance.
