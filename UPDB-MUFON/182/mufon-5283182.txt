Witnessed a flashing light (different colours) move from East to West quite high in the sky. Another light appeared then both disappeared.

I was standing out front of my townhome complex with my dogs. The dogs were enjoying eating snow so I took the opportunity to look at the moon which was quite bright. I then noticed coming from the East a flashing light which was high in the sky. It looked like a lit sparkler that you would put on a cake. As it flashed, it changed colours from yellow, orange to blue. Mostly it stayed yellow and orange. It was moving from East to West and as it did so I noticed it was moving slower than a usual aircraft. It also seemed to jump a bit forward and then back and twice it appeared as though it jumped to the left and then back to its original course. It kept an East to West trajectory though and moved mostly in a straight line. My initial feeling was that it was really, really high and also that it seemed to be in some distress because of its behaviour. I even mentally said to myself that I hope everything is okay with this thing whatever it may be. It then seemed to stay put very briefly, about ten seconds. Then, a new light appeared, this one was solid and yellow. This new light suddenly appeared in the West and close to the flashing object. The new, lit object stayed for about 20 seconds then both of them just disappeared. I got the impression the second object was offering some sort of assistance to the first flashing object.
