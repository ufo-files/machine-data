My husband and I saw what we first thought was a satellite as it was a small light that was travelling about as quick as a satellite would through the sky. It was very high up as a satellite would be. We followed it with our eyes as it moved S/SE coming from the N. Once the object crossed over our house it seemed to almost disappear. The night was clear, no clouds. We looked back in the direction that light/object had come from and there was another light coming, exactly the same as the last one. We followed this one with our eyes as well and it went and disappeared at the same location the last one had. Then we looked again, another one! Coming from the same spot the last two had. Same thing, disappeared in the same location in the sky as the the other ones had. This went on for over 20 mins. They were perfectly timed. When one disappeared another came. All in one big line, perfectly spaced. They all disappeared at the same spot except for one who took a quick right, right before it made it to that spot where they were all disappearing. That one light went West. We tried to take photos and video with our phones but our phones would not pick up the lights in the sky. This lasted for so long that our necks got tired of looking up and we went inside as we had no answers for what we saw. I have not heard anyone else in our town mentioning that they saw this phenomenon.
