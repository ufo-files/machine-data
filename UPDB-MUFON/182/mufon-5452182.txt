Only observed in high altitude.

We were camped in the Willows campground at Flaming Gorge Utah in campsite number three. We were sitting under a shade tree enjoying the view of the mountains just to the south of us.
At 4:50pm my brother Alan asked what is that bright white object in the sky, high over the mountains. We observed it for about 13 minutes in total amazement. It stayed absolutely motionless the entire time as near by clouds slowly drifted by. I then ran and got my Samsung Galaxy s20 Ultra 5G cell phone which has 108 megapixel camera and got three snap shots within two minutes just before it vanished instantly at 5:05pm.
One of the three photos has a small white object below and to the right seen by zooming in very close. That photo was the second one taken. The small object was gone in the third photo.
