{rjl} Three orbs interacting with each other.

My wife called me to the bedroom and pointed out two orange lights twirling around each other. After a few minutes, a third orb entered the airspace and they all left heading east toward camelback mountain. These lights were orange in color and seemed to move in a very strange fashion. They were about 100-200ft above the street. The speed and movement was very unusual and was unlike any aircraft I have ever seen. They also did not appear to have any structure to them, just glowing red/orange light.
