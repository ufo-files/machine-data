{nd} Observed red object traveling south to east in a straight line pulsating slowly

On the night of 08/30-08/31/2013 at 1210 AM and lasting for approximately 5 minutes my son and I observed a red colored object travel from about 15 degrees above the south the horizon to behind the tree line to the east. We were walking my dogs for their final outing of the day. We noticed a red light in the sky that at first I thought a an airplane navigation light, but it was to large and was not flashing and no other lights were visible. It appeared to be coming toward us and turn slightly to the east in a straight level path until it passed out of sight behind the trees to the east. We heard no noise. The sky was overcast no stars were visible. As the object moved it pulsated weakly at a slow rate.
