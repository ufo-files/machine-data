{cak} Bright lights flew left then right lights merged then gone

I was taking my son to school at 530 am on Sept 28 2015. We were on my deck when we noticed lights in sky about 50 to 100 feet away and about 100' in the air. It stood still for a few minutes..just hovered like then the lights split and started to circle each other then the lights joined and then just gone..no noise no wind just gone...it lasted about 5 minutes. No interference noticed on my cell phone or porch light...it just took off faster than I have ever seen anything take off and no noise at all..at that height we should have heard something....
