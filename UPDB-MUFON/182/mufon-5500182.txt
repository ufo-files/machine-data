I saw my first UFO few months back, i was grilling in the afternoon and looked up and spotted an airplane very high up, right on top of me, then another one going another direction but lower. Then few minutes after I saw a black dot flying by, it was lower then the high airplane, I thought it was a small bird or even one of those particles inside your eye that you can sometimes see going across your eye, but it wasn't, i closed my eyes and rubbed them, I still saw the dot still flying by. I was just amazed that I was seeing an object flying that i could not identify. I had laser eye surgery few years back and my vision is 20/15, so I know my vision was not playing tricks on me. The dot was too small and high up that my camera would never capture it. it was very small dot. I lost view of the object as it went to the horizon.
