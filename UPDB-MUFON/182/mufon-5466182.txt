3 circles of light that we1re within each other. Pulsating & spining in opposite directions

My wife and I were traveling in a car from the Forest of Dean to Gloucester after dropping of Christmas presents from family. Driving on the A40 through Huntley. My wife and I saw the objects (only two). She does not like talking about it as it makes her feel uncomfortable. We both looked up as we saw these two objects. Both had 3 circles of light inside each other. You could clearly see the circles moving in opposite directions within each other. One object was closer to us while the other object was further down the road to the right. The closest object was large. Approximately the size of a football pitch. The objects just hovered. My wife carried on driving and I kept my eyes on the objects until it disappeared. I had visual for about 5 minutes until we drove to Highnam roundabout (where the A48 meets the A40). I believe the street lights made it more difficult to see as there was more street lights at the roundabout. Causing the objects to disappear. The one thing know one can take away from me is that I saw these objects and they were as real as you and I. Only now I feel like reporting this I was unsure before.
