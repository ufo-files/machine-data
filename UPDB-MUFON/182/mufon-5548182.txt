GOLD/YELLOW OBJECTS

Approx 9:50 pm I walked out on my deck & saw two very bright objects side by side in the se sky.At the time they were both not moving. I immidiattly called my girlfriend to come out & see. She came out & the one object (further to the east) suddenly took off to the east & was gone in about 3-4 seconds. The other object stayed in roughly the same place for the next 1/2 hour or so.
