Portland bright cluster of white lights accompanied by a group of smaller but also very bright lights

Riding the bus into work this morning, I was listening to the radio,.   headphones on, eyes closed. We were on the Ross Island Bridge traveling west when I suddenly thought to open my eyes and look out over the Willamette River and check out the Portland skyline. It was about 6:45 a.m. and dawn was rapidly becoming full morning. What I immediately beheld was a cluster of bright lights over the river at ahout the same elevation as the bridge I was on, There were three bright white lights that seemed to flicker or flash rapidly, all very close together, and to their left was a lively little cluster of bright colored lights that blinked and seemed to tumble around each other. The whole group of lights hung in the air as I craned my neck around to keep them in sight as long as possible. This object was maybe a quarter of a mile away. About 15 seconds passed before the bus drove over the bridge and into the city innards. I discerned no identifiable shape and have no idea what it was. Sure would like to know if anybody else saw what I saw this morning. I've been watching the skies the last few months, especially since I viewed some YouTube videos of UFOs taken right here in Portland, just north of where I was, along the east side of the Willamette. Sometimes I shout st the heavens, urging the mystery to reveal itself. Maybe I was heard.
