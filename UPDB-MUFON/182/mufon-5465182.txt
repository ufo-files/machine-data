6 lights in total, in 2 collums with 3 lights in each collumn side by side.

I had left Morrisons supermarket and just as I was about to get in the car I noticed the 6 lights in a south-east direction. At first I thought it was the carpark lights hitting off the window then I remembered I was still outside the car. I spent a moment looking at them and tried to determine if it was the lights from a high-rise building but again remembered that there are no big rise buildings in that direction. I got in the car and continued to stare for a few moments and the lights didn't move but appeared glowing and reflective. I'm very familiar with the skyline in this part of town and have never before seen anything like this here.
