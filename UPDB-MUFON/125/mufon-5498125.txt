{dr}Saw lights in sky when cresting bridge while leaving town.

I was riding with a friend to go out of town to do some drinking. We had not begun to drink yet. As we crested the railroad viaduct leaving town I saw some lights in the sky across from the high school. Thinking it was a helicopter I joked to my friend how cool it would be to see a ufo some time and we laughed about it. When we got in front of the high school I could see that the object was hovering near the tractor dealership across from the school. I had the driver stop at the West bottom drive to the school across from tractor dealership and we got out of the car to observe. I observed that the lights were sequencing counterclockwise and were horizontal with the ground. I estimated them at the time to be about 50' off the ground at approz. a 1/4 to 1/2 mile distance. At no time did I see any structure or hear any sound. After observing for about 1 minute the lights began a slow circle of the dealership with a tilt of about 30 degrees to the inside of the circle. When one circle was complete the object again levelled and crossed the highway in front of us to circle a cattle lot in front of us, again with the tilt to the inner part of it's path. When complete it crossed in front of us again back to the dealership to begin another round of the dealership. My friend reached into the drivers window of his car and began to flash his lights. The object instantaneously was flying to the North following the horizon and in a split second was a speck in the sky bfore disappearing for good. The lights never stopped sequencing during this event and even when speeding off never made a sound. The entire event was less than 5 minutes in duration.
