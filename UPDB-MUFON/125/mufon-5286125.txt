Light which changed direction quickly and frequently

I was visiting with a group of friends in Criders VA, near the West Virginia border. It was a clear night and we went outside to observe the Milky Way and Perseids meteors. There were a number observed by all of us. At around 10:40, most of the group went inside leaving a friend and I outside. We were looking up and saw what initially looked like a star. But it began to move quickly from side to side and up and down. My friend first said it must be a satellite but no satellite moves that quickly and erratically. Our group had been discussing how big the universe is and the possibility of life on other planets. Ironically, when my friend and I saw the "light", he did not want to accept we were viewing a UFO/UAP. We watched it for about 5 minutes and went inside. I seem to have a Baader-Meinhof phenomenon with seeing UFOs. The last time I was down, I saw one. I frequently see them around my home in Baltimore.
