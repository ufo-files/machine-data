{jpg} UFO light straight line flight, came towards me.

Let the dogs out after we got home and it was dusk, I looked up to look at the stars and saw one of the "stars" was moving. My first thought was that it was probably a satellite or if I was lucky the ISS. Looked away and looked back and couldn't find it again. Not too concerned as it was so faint anyways it was easy to lose sight of, I figured. Just as I thought that, I caught glimpse of a similar one or the same one but traveling a slightly different direction and it seemed to be coming towards me. I assumed that because as it traveled it slowly got brighter and brighter. My jaw dropped, I turned to get my son's attention, turned back and it dimmed out and vanished.
