Fast white discs fly below perpendicular cigar shaped / reflective object hovering

Standing in the backyard, facing east I noticed to my left a pair of white discs fly (east to west). The sun was behind me so I had a clear view of them against the blue sky. No clouds. The discs were fast and kept flying in pairs about a minute apart. I watched them for about 10 or 15 minutes. (At least 30 pairs flew by). They were white, and made no sound whatsoever. Perfect formation. After there were no more discs observed, I happened to look straight up and noticed another Object just hovering above my neighborhood at about 5000 feet. It was not moving. I grabbed my binoculars and noticed the sun was reflecting off of it and it was giving off a bronze (or pale gold) color. It was shaped like a cigar, or a skinny rectangle, and was positioned perpendicularly like an I shape. Through my binoculars I could make out some type of riveting on the surface but since the sun was reflecting off of it, I couldn’t discern much else. After about 5 minutes it very slowly started to move toward my right (south) and maintained the same elevation, while moving slowly more south until it stopped. And then very slowly and deliberately started to ascend into the sky. I watched until it became a tiny dot. This was before I’d heard of Starlink satellites and so I have no idea if these objects are related to that.
