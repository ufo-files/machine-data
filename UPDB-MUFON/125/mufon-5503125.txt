{kt}triangle 3 light UFO chasing single light UFO starlike

I was at the Jimmy Buffet concert on Sat night, Apr 26, 2014 at Cruzan Amphitheater in West Palm Beach. It was around 10pm when I happened to look up in the sky and saw this triangle craft with a light at each point flying through the sky as well as a single light far ahead, travelling in the same direction. These were at an extremely high altitude, but the size of these craft were huge. All of a sudden, the triangle object sped up to a speed that is not of human design to catch the single light. The direction of both crafts was not a straight line, but rose up and then back down. When the triangle caught the single light, they both disappeared. Here in the city, you can't see many stars except the bright ones. This was no shooting stars and certainly not a man made vehicle. There were a few thousand people at this concert and I had looked around and saw various people looking up at the sky.
