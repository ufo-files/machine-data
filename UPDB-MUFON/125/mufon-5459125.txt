{rjl}large Metallic bullet or cigar shape object

I was driving home west bound on the San Mateo bridge on highway 92. There is considerable commercial air traffic in this area and I can easily watch the airplanes on their approach to SFO. In the distance, over the approximate area of Burlingame CA the larch cigar shaped object was hovering above the flight path of the air traffic. It was easily compared to the other air traffic. At first I thought the object was a blimp. But suddenly the object instantly accelerated to a speed I can only describe as a streak heading south toward San Jose. I lost sight of the object as it passed over Moffat Field. The weather was hot and clear. No clouds.
