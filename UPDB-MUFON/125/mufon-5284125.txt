3 small round objects and 1 tic tac shaped object

On Monday November 15th we observed a single cloud in the sky that stayed in one spot for over an hour. Finding it interesting, I viewed our home security cameras and noticed 3 small objects flying from the proximity of that cloud. Two of the round objects moved across the sky in a straight line and in the same direction without making a sound. The 3rd was a white tic tic shaped object that shot straight up into the sky. During that hour while the cloud was visible, we observed the same objects several times followed by a black military helicopter. After the helicopter flew in the area, the cloud disappeared and the objects were no longer seen. I am not sure what these are and would love a logical explanation.
