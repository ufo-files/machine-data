{dr}Orange Orb over Presque Isle bay

On the way back from an educational event, on Presque Isle State Park, a coworker mentioned an orange ball in the air. We were across the bay from the city of Erie, on the Park. We turned around and stopped in a parking lot to obeserve. There were four of us in the van. We all saw the object. I didn't observe it moving, but saw it stationary. The others said it was moving. Two of us were able to keep eyes on it as it simply disappered, and two of us noticed it was there, and a second later it was not there. At first I thought it was a satellite, but I didn't see it moving in a satellite like fashion. It was simply a large ( no scale to measure against ) orange orb. It was moving then to the North maybe slightly Northeast in relationship to the city of Erie, over the bay.I had it in sight up until I exited the vehicle and then looked up and it was gone. The others said they watched it and saw it disappear. It didn't blink as in aircraft lighting. We all thought it was really cool to be able to have our first "ufo" sighting. After all, we really didn't know what it was.
