{cak} cluster of lights flying in a straight line leaving a discolored trail

I was outside about 6 pm in the front yard facing west. Out of the corner of my eye, I saw what I thought was a meteor breaking apart in the sky northwest of me. except that the fragments didn't slow nor did they lose altitude. They continued silently in a straight line due south/southeast until I could no longer see them. When I did first see them, I ran inside to get my girlfriend so she witnessed the same thing I did. The whole sighting lasted about 2-3 minutes.
