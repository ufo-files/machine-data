Ufo light sphere over hill

The plasma ball flies uphill behind the Kuyalnik sanatorium, between the Khadzhibey estuary and the Kuyalnik after sunset on April 28, 2019. Appeared motionless 100 meters above the hill and disappeared suddenly over the mountain in 15 seconds. While the camera was turning, the plasmoid disappeared in sight, as a descending one disappeared behind building
