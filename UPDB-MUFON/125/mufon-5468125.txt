I was in a car with three other friends. We were heading to a bar. We came around a corner and I looked over the shipyard and there were 3 floating lights in a perfect triangle shape and I had never seen them over the shipyard before. I had my eyes on the lights for about 4 seconds before we drove down the hill and the buildings became too tall to see the lights. I asked my friends if they had seen the lights but they hadn't. I was very excited and didn't want to go to the bar anymore, I just wanted to find the lights again. But sadly, I did not.
