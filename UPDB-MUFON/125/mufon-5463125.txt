Observed two light silver disc shaped objects travelling N, N/E in a straight line around 2:30 pm..

1. Where were you and what were you doing at the time?

Came home from grocery shopping, placed bags on a table that is directly in front of a window that faces North. 

2. What made you first notice the object?

On my way home, I noticed the clouds in my area were all kinda odd, seemingly squared off at most angles. When I placed the bags on the table, that's when I noticed 2 disc shaped craft flying directly overhead. One caught sunlight and reflected it brightly.

3. What did you think the object was when you first noticed it?

A disc shaped UFO. Hard to confuse it for anything else during day time, and being under a regular flight path. Planes have wings. Helicopters have rotors and make sound. These two objects had none of the 3 major qualifying factors for being known aircraft. 

4. Describe the object and its actions and motions in detail.

- Light silver/ chrome colour, reflective. 

- Disc shape, flying parallel to each other.

- Moderate speed, straight path. About the height I see helicopters flying at, moving a little faster than a helicopter, too.

- No sound emitted

- No obvious mechanical means of propulsion

5. Describe your feelings, reactions and actions, during and after sighting the object.

I told my boyfriend what happened who was with me, however he is legally blind and would have extreme difficulty spotting the craft even if they remained still. Didn't think much of it, I've seen weirder things.
Decided may as well submit it for the sake of adding more data, didn't think it was really important at the time considering the objects didn't do anything major.

6. How did you lose sight of the object?

They flew out of view.
