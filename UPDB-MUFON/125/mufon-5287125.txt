Star-like but zig-zagging and extremely fast.

I was in the car on a fairly lit-up street, driving about 35 miles per hour, and I saw what I thought was a star in the sky, but it was moving extremely fast. It was not a plane, it was zig-zagging all over the place and was absolutely too fast to be a plane. It was a little brighter than if you were to look up and see a planet in the sky shining brightly, but not blinding or anything. The two biggest reasons I’m certain it wasn’t a star or a plane are the speed and rapid changes in direction.
