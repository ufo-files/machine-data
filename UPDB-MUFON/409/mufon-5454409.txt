{rjl} I saw at sateliite height eight objects traveling single file west southwest to east northeast.

I was taking my dog out about ten minutes before 9 pm, and I was looking at the Big Dipper to the northeast and Venus to the west, when I saw what I first thought was a communication satellite moving to the East Northeast. Then I noticed a fair distance behind it another identical light, closely followed by another similar light. I immediately called my wife to come to observe them. There was a slight lull and then there were 3 more objects equally spaced, on that same path. As I watched those three go. out of sight... I noticed two final lights appear, with the same spacing as the three, on that same thread-like path.
