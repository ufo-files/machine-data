{dr}Saw orange non-blinking light moving in linear path, it disappeared after 5-10 minutes

I was in the backyard having a smoke with my brother. We usually stargaze a bit and talk, specifically looking for UFOs and other anomalies in the sky. As I was just about to turn in for the night, before walking through the door to go inside, I notice a strange orange, non-blinking light and call my brother out. We follow it for 10 minutes. It was traveling a constant speed. At one moment (which would be what made it an awesome sighting for me), it passes over the moon at which point my brother and I could see its silhouette! It looked like an elongated pod with 3 thrusters at the bottom. 
Shortly after it passes over the moon, the light goes dim and it just disappears.
