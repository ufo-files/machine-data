{jpg} Boomerang Shaped. White cloudlike surface. Chaged shape to line, then to a ball, then back to boomerang.

I was at an informal gathering of about a dozen people in a parking lot of an industrial building, and we all saw it. I was the first to see it. I just happened to be looking at the sky, it caught my eye and I thought it may have been an ultralight aircraft. It had all the appearance of a delta-wing hang-glider style ultralight aircraft. But it was white on the leading edges of the wing. The rest of the delta was either translucent or not there. As I continued to observe it, it became apparent that it was a boomerang shape. It was very high in the sky, over the clouds. Way too high for an ultralight. over 5,000 feet at least. The object hung motionless in the air for a good 5 minutes. It eventually morphed into a straight line, then into a ball, then back into a boomerang shape. It drifted slightly to the east while changing shapes. After it changed back into a boomerang it started drifting faster and eventually was lost behind a cloud. I searched Google for an image and I found a story from 2012 that is the exact same thing I saw. I did not see orbs, and I did not get a paranormal feeling. I thought it may have been a weather balloon, but none of the images I found of weather balloons look like what I saw. Here is the article with a photo. [Link moved to FI Section/cms/tg]
