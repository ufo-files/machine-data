(gs) Glowing orangish red object sighted

In the house watching TV. Looked out the window and saw and unusual glowing orange object in the sky. First thought it wa an airplane.  Ran outside and noticed the object was totally silent. It was gracefully moving across th sky from northwest to southeast and glowing and flickering bright orangish red color. I felt elated and in awe. It simply disappeared.
