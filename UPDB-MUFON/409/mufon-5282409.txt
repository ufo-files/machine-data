Black profile of disc/sliver flying fast across sky

I was out in my backyard with my dog. I'm always looking up, observing the clouds, commercial planes, military helicopters and occasionally a crop duster during the day. On this day, I just happened to be looking up, when an object's motion caught my eye and saw a black disc or black sliver, just the profile, traverse the sky, from southwest to southeast until it past the treeline. It was traveling atleast 3-4 times faster than any commercial plane/jet, military jet or helicopter, that I'm used to seeing, in the sky everyday. Absolutely no sound as it went by and it was atleast 5 times larger than any commercial plane I've seen fly by. I've never seen anything like what I saw. It was completely out of place and flew by faster than I could get my phone out to record. It was a partly clouded sky, but the black profile of the disc/sliver stood out in the blue with puffy white clouded day, in the middle of the afternoon. It was something I've never seen, but I continue to look up, everytime I take my dog outside.
