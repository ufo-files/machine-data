2 separate crafts. Half round circle of pulsating blue lights on both craft at low altitude. One craft following and just a bit north of the first. Both craft on the same straight line SE course. No noise at all and no visible sign of propulsion. Pulsating lights looked like the were affected by heat, (like looking down a hot blacktop road and seeing heat waves from the surface). Could not determine size of craft, only diameter of lights. This was years ago and may be of little interest to you at this time.
