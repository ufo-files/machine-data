Triangular shape with 3 lights hoveringin front. followed our car in opposite direction

I was sat in my girlfriends car up Manmoel Mountain enjoying the clear evening watching the stars. After appprox 2 hours of us being there 3 bright lights in the shape of a triangle appeared from no where. You could see a solid shape to it but not clearly as the lights were so bright. At first we thought it was a police helicopter before realising there was no sound coming from the craft as it just hovered there. It made minor abrupt movements as if it was keeping itself stable by moving ever so slightly but extreamly fast if that makes sense. After observing for a few minutes I had a cold sensation come over me and I said to my girlfriend that I didn't feel safe all of a sudden and that we should go. We turned the car round and headed down the mountain road in the OPPOSITE direction with the lights behind. Suddenly the lights vanished from behind and appeared directly in front of us, this time they seemed lower as it lit the road ahead slightly. As we drove and changed direction in the lane the lights stayed exactly in front of us, as if they were watching us through the front windscreen. Then half way down the mountain the lights shot off towards the sky with incredible speed and vanished out of sight within a few seconds. Moments later 2 police cars arrived driving up the lane at a high speed. The police drove straight past us and up to the mountain.
