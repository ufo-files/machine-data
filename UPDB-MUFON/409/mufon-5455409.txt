{cak} Ball of fire like a mini sun was above one tree then magically above another tree next to it then just vanishes without any trail

My friend Adam and I were in back yard playing after getting back from movie theatre. It was about 10 at night and were tossing rocks at wooden fence at edge of the yard. The sky was completely clear no clouds no wind and no noise around. We looked at the top of the 2 pine trees in neighbors yard 2 houses over about 25 yards away and above the pine trees was a floating miniature sun like orb. It was a big circle ball of fire about the size of the water tank on top of a water tower. The orb looked just like a ball of fire a mini sun but it gave off no heat,sound, and did not hurt the eyes to look at didn't leave any dots or blurry vision like when you look at a light bulb. It just stayed about one pine tree then as if we blinked it was magically about the pine tree right nect to it about 10 yards from the other tree then it just disappeared. There was no trail behind it of any kind like you would see from a plane not even a streak of light was left behind it just vanished into thin air.
