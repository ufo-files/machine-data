{nd} Saw Object hovering and then man in black appeared in school yard

I have been observing these objects since over the summer and video taped a lot of them. They are a normal thing at later times of the night and I know they arent aircraft due to my experience in the aviation field. I was outside smoking a cig and saw a bright light chaging different colors hovering for about 15 minutes in the same spot. I know it wasnt an airplane as it was with out a doubt hovering and the lights were in no way regulation for civilian or military. Towards the end of the event I heard some rustling in the leaves in the school yard that is directly behind my house and a man stepped out dressing in all black. I couldnt see his face due to lack of light but he was just staring at me not moving. I went inside and locked the doors as this was very disturbing. As I went to close the blinds I looked out again and he was not longer there as I had my cell phone in my hand to call the cops. I don't know if this was coincidence or connected but it was pretty unnerving and sent a crazy chill down my spine.
