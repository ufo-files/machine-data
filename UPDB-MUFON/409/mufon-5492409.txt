{rjl} Hovering bright light/white saucer

I was 16 and walking to a friends house at night. I didn't have a car yet but walked across town. Small town of Northwood, Ia. I was almost to their house and on the sidewalk of their block. It was past 9 around 10 pm because it was dark and stars were out. I just had a weird feeling to stop walking and look up. And I saw a big bright white/silver glowing saucer/circular object just hovering directly over me above the tree line. There was no sound no wind, no nothing. I compared it to stars but it was much bigger and brighter. I looked around and no body else was near to see it. I wanted to run but I was on such awe of it I just starred at it till it left. As I starred about a minute later it did a bright flash like a camera flash and maybe 30 seconds later it took off straight west and was gone in 1/2 a second. I wiped my eyes and at that point I knew we are not alone and I told my friends and they didn't believe me. But I know what I saw and will never forget it.
