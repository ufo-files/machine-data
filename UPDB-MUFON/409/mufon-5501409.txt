{dr}4 lights. 1 blink off the a nother blink off then all 4 on. hovering. long oval like object from left to right.

I was walking back from the store watching firework. That is when I noticed 4 lights over the buildings in downtown Denver. At first I thought that it was a little weird. I kept walking west on Colfax looking over my shoulder. Then I saw them start blinking. One after the other. One would blink off and stay off for about 5 seconds then another would blink off. After about 10 total seconds it would turn back on. So I ran back home to show somebody because i did not have my phone to take a picture and I knew that nobody would believe me. I saw a pedestrian and showed him. He thought that it was weird. So I started running again. I turned the corner and looked back so I looked East, it was still there. When I got home and got my girlfriend to come outside to look. It was gone. It would have been about 10 minutes that I had seen this going on. It was about 9:30 in the evening. I know what I saw. I am not crazy. Thanks for listening. July 4th 2014 9:30 pm.
