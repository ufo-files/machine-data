{rjl} Multiple orange orbs turned into 5 UFOS with blinking lights flew overhead

On 3/26/2016 at 11:40 at night I witnessed several orange orbs on the horizon on my way home from work. I initially thought these objects were perhaps lantern fireworks or shooting stars but they were stationary and larger than any firework lantern I had seen before. There were 5 in total and as I was observing them in my car, the one I was looking at directly ahead of me changed into a disc shaped craft with four circular bright white lights. Two of the lights on either side blinked at a steady pace. The other orbs I saw began to change as well and had different colors but were essentially the same type of craft and took off in different directions so I lost sight of them. I was already driving in the same direction as the white UFO by coincidence so I kept an eye on it the whole time as I was driving home. One of my neighbors had stopped his car and was filming on the side of the road so I knew I wasnt the only one who saw them. When I got home I also decided to capture it on my phone as evidence. I wasnt necessarily scared or intimidated by the object but I was shaken because I had never seen anything as strange as this was in the sky before. It was about as high up as your average aircraft would fly but it was moving much faster. I recorded the UFO for about 3 minutes as it flew directly over my house and behind a treeline out of sight.
