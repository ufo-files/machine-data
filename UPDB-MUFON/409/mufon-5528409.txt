bright orange light

i was walking my dog, and as my dog took his time to sniff around i was looking around me, it was app 12 at night (dc 30th to dec 31st), and in the sky i saw an orange light, at first i thought it was a normal airplane, but it was soundless, flew BELOW the deck of clouds, clearly visible, and not three, but just one light! the light wasnt normal white or red, it was bright orange, and the object seemed shining. 
i was standing and looking in amazement. usually i occasionally see an airplane flying over when im walking the dog, but usually those make sound, and three lights are visible, and what i saw amazed me. someone in the street i was standing in turned her light on, then i realised i had to go home very soon to tell/show my dad (i was almost at home not even 30 secs walking away from home), 
my dad saw about the last few seconds the craft was visible, and from the back it looked orb-like, he also couldnt recognize it. so i saw a craft, bright orange, seemed shining (very shining), below the clouds (there were quite many clouds) one singular light, no sound, not far away, faster than an airplane, and from the back orb-like, does anyone know what ive seen?
