As per the request of Jan Harzan, this is about someplace I visited and was told to report it as a UFO. In 1975 I served as a Security Policeman at Kirtland AFB and was stationed on Manzano Mtn., a nuclear weapons storage dump. The nukes were stored on the outside of the mtn., a secret clearance being required to work there. On the inside of the mtn. was a concrete and steel roadway tunnel large enough for an 18 wheeler. One night on graveyard shift at about 3am I went for a walk up the roadway and at about 1/4 of a mile I came to an intersection where I could see forever in all directions. I continued on straight for another 1/4 mile then came to what seemed like the end. To the left of me was a narrow hallway about 20 yards long and at the end was an 8ftx8ft blast door with a series of locks on it. Covering the door were signs of keep-out,radioactive material, top-secret clearance only, etc. At this point a feeling of dread overcame me and I pulled my weapon and backed out slowly. I made my way back to the entrance of the roadway as quick as possible, never mentioned it again and never got caught. I only thought of recently when I saw a UFO story on the History Channel about Dulce,NM and a contractor who fell into a tunnel and saw a bunch of "Greys" and special ops guys. The story mentioned Kirtland AFB and I got to wondering what was behind that blast door I saw 39 years ago and maybe it linked up to Dulce. In any event I suddenly realized it may have been an elevator. Do what you want with the info but I never mentioned it before because I was in a restricted area with only a secret clearance. I'm 61 now and don't really give a s**t about it anymore.  Thanx Mike
