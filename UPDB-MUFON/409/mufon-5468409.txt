Orb like light from SE that slowly turned E.

I was out on my deck early this morning when I noticed a bright light heading my way from the south east on a clear night. 

I thought it was a plane at first, but something seemed different about this one. Considering its distance to me, I should have been able to see the flashing coloured plane lights, but all I saw was a fairly bright white light. 

There was absolutely no sound. Usually you hear the turbines or propellers. It was quiet. 

I quickly rushed inside to grab my phone and take the obligatory terrible UFO video
.If only I had a proper lens instead of my Galaxy S7. It's only 40 seconds long but it is some proof anyway. 

After the light veered east and beyond my sight line over the trees, I quickly used my browser to check Flightradar24.com to see if there were any planes in that direction. There was nothing to the south and east of me in that vicinity. There were planes to the north and northeast east of me. They were clearly not what I saw, but I am also very unsure of what that quiet light was...
