{nd} craft over centerville ohio 09/05/2015 hovering and moving in figure 8 motion

September 5, 2015 aprox. 9 pm an unidentified craft in a dim yellow light hovered over centerville ohio i was on alex-bell and clyo rd looking west towards centerville the craft moved north to south back and forth and so on for a period of 10 minutes last thing i observed it lowered below the tree line then it shot up about a footover tree line about a 30 degree angle from my view at ground level as it reappeared the light went from a dim yellow to verry briliant white or blue with red markers at ends and in middle it then began to expel or shoot flares with red lights & white trails as i viewd this i then herd multiple sirens and police vehicles heading in that direction (west) on clyo rd oddly as soon as sirens came on the light descended and i know of no military air field in that area as Wright Patterson Air Force base is at least 30-40 minutes away in the opposite direction north east
