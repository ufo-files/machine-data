It was large, quiet, was an object hover then accelerate at speed

I was walking my dog one evening .. it was still light out but very cloudy, hot and muggy...not even a breeze .. there was no one else around and there was a 'heavy' feeling in the air.
My dog noticed it first.. it was just hovering in the air in front of us .. we stopped and looked .. I was trying to figure out what it was when it started moving quite fast in a sw direction ... we ran up the road to keep it in sight while pulling out phone to get a picture ... managed to get one before it abruptly changed direction to the west and then seemed to disappear.. I had visual contact for approx 5 mins I think .. the whole sighting had a very surreal feeling to it .. I have seen lights and objects in the sky before but this was different
