7 yellow lights in triangular/boomerang formation

At around 10pm on June 12th 2010, me and my friend were sitting outside my house and were looking up at the night sky. The sky was clear and cloudless and stars were very visible. What we saw were 7 faint yellow lights in triangular or boomerang formation, flying in a straight line from South to North at constant speed. Its speed was very fast since we only saw it for about 15 seconds or less before we could not see it anymore. It was easily comparable to the airplane which was flying above us at that time. It was probably about 70x faster than the airplane since we saw that the plane took about a little less than a minute to go from one side of our visible sky to the other side and the UFO appeared to have a much higher altitude than the plane, though the impression that it was very far was probably caused by the faintness of its lights. It could have been a very big pitch-black craft larger than the size of two football fields, with 7 lights -- or it could have been 7 small crafts flying in formation. The craft did not emit any beams, sounds, vibration or anything. It just flew above us.
