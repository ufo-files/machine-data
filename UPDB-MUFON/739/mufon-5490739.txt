{jpg} Watched a bright object like the North Star traveling through the night sky

My wife and I were watching the full moon in the night sky to the south east and I was looking over towards Orion's Belt in the south west sky when i saw an object moving through the night sky. At first I tried to identify or rule out a plane or a satellite. It was not blinking nor have red or green lights like a plane, it it a clear sky so easily to identify. It was the size of the North Star and as it tracked across the sky it went through the only cloud in the sky which proved it was in the sky and not a satellite. I came in and grabbed my IPhone and took some pictures as it traveled to the south east sky. Knowing IPhones they do not pick up light well at night and this stands out nice and bright.
