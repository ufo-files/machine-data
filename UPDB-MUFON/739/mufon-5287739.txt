Three Pulsating Lights Over Moab

After a long day of Off Roading through the trails and what-have-you. Our group sat down around a camp fire. It was just towards the end of the night when I had noticed a weird object shooting from the horizon. It looked like a flare at first. It continued to travel through the sky in a straight line. I work with airplanes and am studying for flight school to get my licence. I could tell this wasn't a normal aircraft, as it didn't have standard position lights. It had also been traveling at enormous speeds. Probably at most Mach 1 or 2. Which was odd, because it made absolutely no sound at all. Any engine to produce that kind of speed (jet, rocket, or otherwise) would have made at least some sort of noise. I continued, flying in and out of the clouds before it finally vanished about two minutes later. 
About three minutes later, a second one had appeared. it was almost identical to the one before, only flying in a slightly different direction. It disappeared the same way.
Another three minutes go by and a third goes by. Only this one flew directly over my point of view. It was slightly egg-shaped and had some sort of dome sticking out the bottom of the aircraft. Three bright orange dots illuminated the craft.
