Seen on as photograph

Yesterday evening we were travelling on the N3 highway northbound between Warden and Villiers on our way back to Gauteng. I was taking photos of a beautiful sunset with golden clouds. The photos were being taken through the side window of our moving vehicle. There was a set of three that I took in quick succession using the camera motordrive (Nikon D5600) On the first one there is a tiny distant, indistinct object that to me looks like a UFO when I zoom in on that area on my computer. On the second and third photos there is no sign of the same black shape. When I zoomed in on that section of the photo, the object although very indistinct does seem to be saucer shaped. I am posting the first photo - you do need to zoom in to see the object on the photo which is to the right of the centre of the image (DSC_0244). I'd be interested to know what others think it might be. At the time of actually taking the photo I was totally focused on the beautiful clouds and I did not see any flying objects of any sort in the vicinity. I have not altered the photo in any way and I have the original on my memory card. Thank you for the opportunity to submit my photograph.
