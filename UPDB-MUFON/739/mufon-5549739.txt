1974 Appox Gilmer County, GA.

Evening after school, saw the Orb to the SW, was shaken so badly I ran intoglass in a sliding glass door did not break. WPPL radio in Blue Ridge GA said iswas a weather ballon the next morning, the weather ballon outran what appeard as2 F-111s that intercepted it from the South, probably from Dobbins AFB. I amhoping you have other data on this event because I remember 2 Counties Gilmer,Fannin, and maybe Pickens County saw this thing. please let me know if you haveand exact date on this, in the event of other reports have reported on this one.It is my intent to find more witnesses due to the fact that this has bugged meall these years. Now Im 40 now and feel that time is being lost to look at thisevent further.Douglas V.
