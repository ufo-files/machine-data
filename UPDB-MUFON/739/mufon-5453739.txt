{cak} A constant string of star-like objects flying about satellite speed, mostly in a straight line for at least 45 minutes...at least 35 objects

At about 2115 on Apr 17, 2020, myself, my wife Terry, my brother Mike & his wife Janet all observed a constant string of star or satellite like objects moving SW to NE across the sky. We witnessed at least 35 objects moving in a straight line at about the same speed as many of the visible satellites travel. Make no mistake, this was a procession of NUMEROUS objects that crossed our sky, which thankfully was almost completely clear at the time. Never seen anything like this long procession of distinct objects. They appeared way out but hard to tell. Most of them were in a very straight line of travel, single file but there were 5 objects that were moving at the same pace but above & below that level, and they were in a group.
