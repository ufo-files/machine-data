Followed home twice in one week by pulsating orb whilst driving approx 2.am in the morning driving from my partners house 10 mile home. 
It started high in the sky tgen ended up at tree level and stopped in the field next to my house in sutton. First night it stopped i got out of the car looked at it for a couple of minutes then it went back in the same direction it came from. Second night when i got to my house it stopped in the field again but this time didnt go away at that point got freaked out and ran indoors.
