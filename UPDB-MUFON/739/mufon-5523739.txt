Standing on patio 5 floors up at my residence and saw a group of yellow/orange, pulsating/flikering lights moving slowly in formation overhead at low altitude. As I have never seen anything comparable in my life, I had no idea what this was. At the time I saw it my reaction was confusion, curiosity, and shock. I saw the lights almost directly above my position for about 15 to 20 seconds and finally the passed out of view behind the building in which I LIVE.
