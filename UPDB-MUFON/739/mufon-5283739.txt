Large, slow moving UAP over Bessemer/Minnequa area

Three visible red lights moved slowly floating on a straight path north. As they moved, it became apparent that they were not separate but fixed to a larger shape that blocked light behind it and was appearing as the same color as the night sky. Estimating it was moving about 3-5 miles an hour. Hard to determine the overall shape, and eventually it was lost behind tree branches and rooftops closer to my location that obscured it at its distance. No sound, no erratic movements, just slow and steady and very low. Reminded me of a blimp or hot air balloon, but the shape and lights ruled both out, as did the speed and straight-line path and consistent elevation.
