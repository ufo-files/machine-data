8 or so Red/Orange Lights Shoot up and then vanish

My sister, husband and 2 kids took a walk around the neighborhood. We all of a sudden seen 8 or so Red/Orange odd shapped objects in the sky. This intrigued us so we tried to walk in the direction they were coming from. All of a sudden they were gone. Vanished in thin air. So we kept walking and then a few more poped up and were shooting up fast and then would just float down. They all came from the same directing. The ground! Not sure what they were but my husband has been in the military and says he has never seen anything like what we seen. Thought my eyes were playing tricks on me until my 2 year old nephew said " Look Guys" See and pointed up! Too bad none of us had our phones on us so no pictures.
