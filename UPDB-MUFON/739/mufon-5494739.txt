{cak} Did anybody else just see that...?

I was actually watching a video on Youtube involving the ex Prime Minister of Defence - Canada's statement that he believes in UFO's and suddenly out of the corner of my eye, I watched as an orange-ish light ascended into the sky from the West. At first it was somewhat dim and I couldn't really tell if it was just a star or an airplane or something, but eventually I began to realize that it was moving...and not flashing like airplanes always do. I kept my eyes glued on it as it soared over Venus's current position in the sky and it got brighter just after it passed. Definitely close to being outside of the atmosphere if it was even in it at all. In the last glimpse I caught of it, it looked like every vision of a "satellite" in the sky that I've ever seen. So...did anyone else in the area see that too?
