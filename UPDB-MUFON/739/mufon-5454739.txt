{jpg} Saw what I thought was a pulsating star, as I started to pay attention to figure out what exactly it was I was looking at, it just disappeared.

It was 5:00 a.m. Friday morning, not a cloud in the sky. I came outside to get something out of my truck and I looked up to the East and I saw what I thought was a pulsating green star. As I looked closer i said to myself wait a minute all the sudden it started moving to the right and then boom it was gone. I've seen plenty of shooting stars and various airplanes and this was definitely not either one. I stayed outside for 15 minutes trying to find it again with no avail.
