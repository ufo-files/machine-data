People in wingsuits????

I was driving on Country Club Drive in Aventura stopped at a red light by Turnberry Golf Course. I looked up and saw what I believed to be some balloons that had floated away from a party or something. I noticed that these objects were not drifting g as balloons would. I started taping on my phone and unfortunately the light changed and I started to drive away trying to follow them. I took the second video from another traffic light, but was honked by the car behind me. I finally parked somewhere but, by then the objects were way up in the sky and moving in a westerly direction, Are these people in wing suits? They were not ultralights, the whole thing is bizarre.
