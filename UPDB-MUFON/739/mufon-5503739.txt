{dr}Three green glowing orbs in the shape of a triangle hovered

As I sat "chilling" in my friends pickup truck with 2 other friends, 4 of us total. I noticed three green glowing orbs hovering above our townhouse community. The object or objects were very low to the ground, probably no more than 500 ft off of the ground. There was no noise from the craft and all we could make out were three distinct glowing orbs, each orb made up one corner of the TRIANGLE. It hovered for about 30 seconds and then took off into the clouds at incredible speed maintaining the same triangular shape. It illuminated a cloud as it passed through it. Also, a neighbors dog started barking before I had noticed the orbs. Very creepy
