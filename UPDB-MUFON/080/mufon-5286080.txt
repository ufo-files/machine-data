Orange Orb

We were outside with our telescope watching the sky. A orange orb came from the east and hovered in front of us. It looked like it was on a straight path but would sometime change speed and slow down just to gain more speed right after. It was not going fast. My husband videotaped it while I used the finder scope to look at it. At close range it looked like a spiral orb, ranging from orange/red/yellow in color. The colors were rotating and pulsating. I could see in the scope that is was slightly changing shape while always maintaining a spherical appearance. I was trying to communicate with them and as soon as I said that I wanted to call my sister to show her, it disappeared. The dogs were unsettled and scared after the event.
