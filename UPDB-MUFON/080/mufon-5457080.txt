{cak} Box dropped thru opening in sky

I was traveling along route 1 thru Frankfort maine. Railroad tracks are on left (as I headed towards Belfast) all the sudden, about 20 feet above the railroad tracks, I saw this 3D box that was some sort of metal but also black with raised writing like hieroglyphics on it. It dropped out of opening that appeared. It just hovered and a few seconds later, it disappeared the same way. I was shocked and immediately knew that I saw WAS A UFO.
