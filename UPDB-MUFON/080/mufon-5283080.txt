Large Red Glowing Light descending at a slow pace

When the Red Light was observed it was first seen when I was driving South on Gateway Drive approaching Oak Valley Parkway.  The Red Glowing Light could be seen South of Little San Gorgorino Creek. There is a ridge at the other end of the creek with a higher elevation. After I turned left on Oak Valley parkway I stopped my vehicle at the stop sign near Desert Lawn Drive. I got out to observe the glowing object and was going to take a photo. However, it appeared that the object could not be seen beyond the ridgeline. I went back to my vehicle and headed East on Interstate 10 then quickly exited Interstate 10 and headed West on the 60 freeway. I got off the freeway near the empty field where the light was hovering but was not able to see anything.
