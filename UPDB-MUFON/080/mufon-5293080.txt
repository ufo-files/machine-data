Large Round bright lights.

These round brightly lit orbs are here frequently. My first sighting was one in the North. 
Early this morning we had a power outage and it was pitch black outdoors. I was looking out the window at all the stars we don’t normally see and this time I saw the two bright lights in the south. I didn’t think to take a photo but will do so. They seem to be stationery, high, over our large field crops here, as if they are observing. Our farmers are out on the fields at night and the tractor lights can be seen in the fields in the dark. The farmers here have seen them many times and just seem to pass it off.
