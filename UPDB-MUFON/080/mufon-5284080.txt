10+ orbs hovering, gathering (patterns), dispersing, fast, sharp turns, one by one started to fade

We were at a community pool with my family and another family (unknown). My son first noticed these objects in the sky to the West. They were hovering and then grouping together to form some sort of circle or hub of communication and then disperse but still within site. They would occasionally float and then descend\ascend very quickly. Up, down, left, right, different directions but very consistent in speed. Looked very effortless. They made their way to the East of us and continued to hover. We all had phones but only 1 was able to get a solid image. Given it was day time, recording a video would have come out so well. Please see attached. 

We all gazed at them. It was really a show stopper. One by one they would either fade into nothing or take off so fast that we couldn't see where they went. It took about 60 seconds for all of them to disappear.
