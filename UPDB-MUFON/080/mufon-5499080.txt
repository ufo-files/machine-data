{rjl} Rainbow colored flashing/pulsating diamond in sky

My boyfriend had taken the dogs out for a walk before bed, and he came in and calmly told me to put my shoes on and come outside. When I walked out onto our front porch, we looked Southeast at 127 degrees, and there was a large "star-like" (I only say star because it wasn't a distinct shape, it seemed more like a diamond or just flashes of light) and rainbow colored. It was pulsating and flashing different colors in a precise order. At first I really did think it was just an airplane and even laughed about it because I thought my boyfriend had mistaken it, but it didn't move. Not at all. It was completely silent and literally perfectly still. I am an avid believer in extra-terrestrial intelligent life from other planets, and I actually receive emails from MUFON, but I have to be honest, I was racking my brain trying to think of anything else this might have been. I didn't want to get too excited about it and then find out it was just some military craft or maybe the international space station. But ive seen the space station in the sky before and this did not look anything like that. Not even close. The colors were so bright. After getting my fill of watching it, I got cold and my dogs were getting impatient so I went back inside. After about an hour or so I came back out to check on the object and it was gone.
