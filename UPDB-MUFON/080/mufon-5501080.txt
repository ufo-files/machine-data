{cak} high speed flaing object, going fast, then slowing down dramatically above head, then flickers twice, disappears

I was standing outside my house, south San Bernardino, California, approximately 10:45 pm enjoying the warm evening, facing a south direction, looking towards the moon, out of the corner of my eye, I see a yellowish, orange glow heading from the south- southwest horizon, very much out of the ordinary. It was traveling towards the northeast, I'm my direction. I continued to observe, and as it got closer overhead, I truly though it was an aircraft on fire, as you can see the presence of flames on it. Very scary. The fireball continued for another two minutes or so in a northeast direction, and I observed that it was drastically slowing down just about over my head but slightly to the east.it appeared as if it were going to change directions, completely stopped for about five seconds, just burning. Then it began to flicker, like a type of strobe, it flickered twice, then completely disappeared.i went online to see if an aircraft had disappeared or crashed, nothing reported, and I'm thinking I may have been the only one to see this object. In Australia, a few days ago, there was a report of space junk from a recent space launch. I don't know if its possible for this to be space junk from the launch, but it was very frightening and has me baffled to this very moment. I observe the night sky as often as possible, seen many other objects which have been unexplainable, but this was absolutely frightening. I sure wish I had photo or video proof.
