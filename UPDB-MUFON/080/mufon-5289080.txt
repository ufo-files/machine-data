I saw a round light in the sky. It moved from my right to the left. Then acclerated straight up at a rapid speed. Met with to other round lights then

On November 18 I went to retrieve my garbage can at the end of the drive way. It was a day after the Leonid meteor shower so I was looking up to see if there were any visible meteorite streaks in the sky. Mars was very defined in the sky that night and visible with the naked eye. Then I noticed in the southern sky an airplane flying away from me. I turned to my left toward the south easterly sky and noticed a bright round light. Being in naval aviation for 6 years I automatically thought it was landing lights on an incoming plane because the bright light was all I could see. Then the light got bigger as it approached in my direction. However as it started moving from my right to my left I thought this is not a landing light. I kept watching it and after moving slowly to my left for two or three minutes it accelerated straight up at a high speed until it was a small dot in the sky. I noticed it came to what appeared to be a stop and there were two more small dots. The three dots formed a triangle then all three accelerated away from me until I could not see them anymore.
