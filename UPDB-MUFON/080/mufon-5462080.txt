{jpg} Lights in the east sky seemingly just east of the town of Mayetta

I have noticed lights in the east sky usually when I get off work around 8 in the evening and sometimes just before sundown.  Usually one light and have seen as many as 5 at one time. Seeing to hover and move different directions and either disappearing or jetting straight up. Have had one sighting around sundown in the west sky just above the horizon seemingly near Jeffery energy plant. That time there were 3 lights yellow red and white. They hovered left and right for about 30 minutes then disappeared. Others reported seeing them that time
