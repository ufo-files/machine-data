{dr}Black diamond or triangular dimensional shape slowly rotating and travelling in a straight path in the sky above tree line. as it rotated I noticed the surface was shiny.

I was driving towards Pittsfield, Via Holmes Rd. Heading towards Deming Park.
I noticed the object directly ahead of me in my field of vision above Deming Park, It was a Black object and it appeared to be diamond like or triangular in shape. 
When I first saw the object I thought it was a kite, I stopped in Deming Park to watch the object, It was black and I noticed it rotating because one side would flash in the sunlight. The object was travelling in a straight path through the sky, over the tree line and houses.
I got back in my vehicle to track the object, By the time I had started driving I lost sight of the object.
