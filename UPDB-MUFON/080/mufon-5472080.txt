I went to have a cigarette around 9pm. I always look up at night as I am fascinated with space. About half way through my cigarette I seen very faint red and Amber colored lights moving fast. I thought it was a plane or something. As it got over head I noticed the pattern of the lights were strange and the craft itsself was big for the distance from the ground it was. And also the surface of the craft seemed to blend in with the night sky determined as it blocked out the stars as it passed them. Also as fast as it was going and being overhead I was expecting to hear the delayed sound of an aviation engine as it went out of sight behind the houses. There was never a sound and I will never forget the incident or the shape of the craft and lights as it took me some serious adjusting to the eyes to see the craft in the night sky. This isn't my first time seeing things that are unexplainable. Both in Iowa and Illinois. After the craft left my sight and I knew I had seen another unexplained aircraft. I quickly went inside and drew a picture and couldnt believe it as it was my first time seeing one of these that I was able to see the shape of it.
