Saw

I was camping at the Harrison River RV Park on August 16th with my husband. They show an outdoor movie in the evening at 9 pm. The movie was just starting when a woman in the crowd commented on a comet in the sky. My husband got up from his chair to see what she was looking at and stated that it wasn't a comet. I stood up immediately to see a comet-like object moving very fast in the sky to the south. I then saw it change direction and move in the opposite direction and then completely disappear. My husband saw it change direction at least four times.
