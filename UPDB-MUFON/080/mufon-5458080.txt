{cak} saw black unmarked helicopter then 5 min later a black triangle UFO

While driving home from Rose Medical Center after work, on Hale Parkway I first saw a black unmarked helicopter flying east at 3:46pm. I watched it through the trees and wanted to continue to follow it when I got to 8th Ave. but then lost site of it. I continued to my house via Holly Street when I then saw a black triangle UFO about a mile away in the sky under cloud level headed east. At first I thought it was an airplane but the more I looked, it knew it was not a plane. I pulled over to the side of the road by the shops at Holly and Cedar to take a picture of it but it disappeared when I looked down to get my phone. I don't know if I could no longer view it because the trees were in the way or if it accelerated.
