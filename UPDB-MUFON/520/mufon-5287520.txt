Multiple Starlike craft zigzaged all over sky

This is the 4th night in a row I have seen multiple craft all behaving in a similar manner.

Look like stars, although each night one was clearly visible at dusk when no stars were visible and stayed around for hours.

Last night there were at least 20 craft moving about at one time, some brighter than others.

All moved in all directions and hovered, often pausing.

Most stay in one general area, Although one was seen moving from horizon to directly above me over the course of 5-10 minutes.

Often seem to stop and go, often do little circles, sometimes dart ahead then a 90 degree turn.

Movement is much more than any satellite and seems to have no intended direction or destination.

All craft stayed in sky for hours, one could say they are somewhat "subtle" but clearly not any known aircraft or technology.

Each night has been extremely overcast and close to a full moon with very few stars visible, yet these have been all over the sky.

Other people must be seeing these, they were everywhere you looked.
