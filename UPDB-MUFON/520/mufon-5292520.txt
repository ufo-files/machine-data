Hovering craft in backyard

My son and I were in my room and the blinds were fully open. My son started screaming “mom look! What is that?” I turned to look out the window and we both were staring at a craft in my backyard. It hovered for a few seconds then sped off over our neighbors home rapidly. Our security camera in the area facing the craft was completely wiped clean and there was no history of anything for the past 24 hours on that camera. Every other camera around our home has at least 8-14 random security recordings. We both then slept very deeply and my son never sleeps past 7AM. We both woke up and drew the craft we saw. Our drawings are almost identical. My son saw lights but I didn’t by the time I turned it was not illuminating anything.
