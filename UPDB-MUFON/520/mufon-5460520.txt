{tjd} Looked like a train of hundreds of craft traveling across the sky. Extremely bright

While RV camping at Otter Creek Reservoir, my wife, adult and minor children and adult child’s girlfriend, we were sitting around the campfire commenting on the beauty and brightness of the stars. My son excitedly yelled, look there is a white line in the sky. We all turned our attention to where he was looking and witnessed what looked like a line of hundreds, possibly thousands of individual lights traveling in perfect single file at high speed from southwest to northeast. They were very far away. I would say in low orbit, and the line extended across the entire sky. Traveling from my right to left, the line went from very closely spaced lights, to a more stretched out spacing. This lasted for approximately 4-5 minutes. Toward the end, there were stragglers in groups of 2-4 that kept following the “train”. Several of these trailers made abrupt stops and direction changes before continuing on to follow the others. We were all absolutely aghast at the brightness, volume and coordination of these lights. They were too far to discern any shape or color other than white. Several of the kids tried to capture it on their phone cameras, but as dark as it was the cameras could not find anything to focus on.
