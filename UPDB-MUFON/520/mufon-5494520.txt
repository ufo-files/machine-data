{rjl} 6 craft traveling at 20mph then stopped and defended behind really small mountain

I used to live a half mile from the Papago Indian reservation in South Tucson. I went outside to feed the pigeons before walking to school. The pigeon cage was located on the south side of the back yard. As I finished up and closed the door I looked to the west and saw 6 slow moving objects. I studied them for a minute. I then realized they weren't conventional aircraft as they were moving at 10 to 20mph. They were flying in formation. One in front,two behind it and three behind those. They were 1/4 mile away when first I spotted them at 100' above the ground. They were shaped similar to a boomerang with a almost pointed end in the middle of them. They flew at that altitude and southerly direction for for another 15 minutes. As they came to a small mountain (1 mile X 1/2 mile X 100' high) they all stopped and began descending behind the mountain. Behind the mountain was the Santa Cruz river which is dry most of the year. The river banks were about 30' high at that location and the river a quarter mile wide.As this all began I hollered for my mother and two of my brothers. We were all watching the whole event unfold. Growing up near Davis Monthan Airforce Base I've seen plenty of aircraft. These were nothing I or my family had ever seen. I was truly excited to witness such an event. They were all about 30' from wing tip to wing tip. Front to back roughly 10'. After school that day my two brothers and I went to the river to see if we could find anything. After 2hrs searching we came up empty handed. After 48 years I can still remember every detail like it was that day.
