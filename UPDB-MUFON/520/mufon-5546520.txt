Dark Triangle with lights at vertices - two witnesses

was lookin toward south(mpls) and saw what appeared to be ufo.  was triangular and had white lights on all corners with a red flashing light in middle going from side to side and in the rear of craft there were two blue lights. i opened sliding glass door and looked closer and noticed that it was on same route as most passenger plane routes to msp airport, but the light configuration i thought was pretty wierd so, thats when i realized i couldnt hear the craft. i continued watching it but it did not follow the route but instead continued on a south western path rather than going to msp airport.  i did stand out on the patio and could hear the passenger planes at the same distance. all im saying it definately was a ufo  not that it was alien but.... what do we have that capability?...also it was not flying as fast as the regular planes   i really wish for the first time in my life that i had a video recorder then i could discern it for myself.... i just hope someone else saw this other than me and my girlfriend.  I did feel pretty freaked out cuz it was movin so slow and the gosh darn red lights goin back and forth i just cant understand why, and why would it have been following the path of passenger planes only to go off in a different direction it never zig zagged or anything just left out of my view thats all  very strange.
