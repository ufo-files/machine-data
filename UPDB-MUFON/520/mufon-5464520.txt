{cak} Many orbs flying just above the cloudcover, some merge

I was behind the Half Price books in Mason Ohio, and looked up. I saw a air plane (sounded like a propeller aircraft. Had to be 5 + miles away) and then saw 8 ish lights that flew almost right above me. They were just above the cloud cover, flying past (at like 4-5x a conventional aircraft). There was no apparent sound (at least over the other plane's noise) and appeared to fly with grace. They flew from the direction of pet's mart, to the direction of lifetime fitness on google maps. (from North East to South West) 

I didn't actually see the orbs, but saw their light of through the clouds. The light was like a electric yellow. They were in 2 or 3 rows the instant I saw them, but the outside orbs like jumped into the front of the pack or merged with the front ones. I'm not quite sure. I do know they almost seemed to act like a playful puppy would. And that it seems there were more orbs that entered my visual field when I first saw them than when I last saw them a few seconds later.
