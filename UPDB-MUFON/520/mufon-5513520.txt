{nd} Awakened at 3 am with electricity out, both I and my husband heard loud humming and bright green lights surrounding our entire house followed by an entity encounter by myself

I was awakened at 3:00 am with the electricity in my home off. Then my husband awoke shortly after myself, which is extremely rare as he is a very deep sleeper. I said to my husband that there mustve been a power failure in the night. At that moment, the entire house shook and vibrated and there was an extremly loud humming sound resonating throughout and all around us. I looked out my kitchen window and witnessed bright green light lighting up the yard encompassing our house. I was in shock to witness a humanoid figure beside the fence facing me about twenty feet from where I was standing. I could make the shape of what appeared to be a protective helmet over its head and observed a wand in his hand which appeared to be sparking. I could sense the being trying to telepathically compel me to go outside my home but even in my state of shock resisted it. My husband shouted from the other room, " What the hell was that???!!", hearing the loud humming sound continuing all around us. The whole incident lasted about five minutes before the lights dissipated and when I returned to the kitchen window the figure had disappeared.
