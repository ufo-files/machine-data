{rjl} Hovering glowing object in distance, then passed directly over us.

About 11 p.m. last night (9/22/17) two of us were sitting on the front porch. My friend noticed what she thought might be Mars in the distance, bright red and glowing. A few minutes later, she noticed Mars wasn't there any longer. Quickly we realized a bright red circular object was traveling toward us. The body of the object was bright red and well-demarcated by the lights. It eventually passed over our heads and continued on in a straight path. We noticed there was a single blinking light on either side of the object. My reaction to it was curiosity. I watched it for a little bit longer then sat back down on the front porch.
