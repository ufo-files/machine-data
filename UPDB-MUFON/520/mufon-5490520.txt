{nd} Sitting on the beach with boyfriend . Saw a very bright lightly that wasn't moving . Pretty far into the sky .. Dissapeared and than reappeared in another spot

My boyfriend and I decided to sit at the beach in one of the lifeguard chairs on the water just to enjoy the night . After talking for a while he pointed out a very bright basketball sized light in the sky . It wasn't moving ! It wasn't a plane . It just stood there . We ignored it and continued speaking when we randomly decided to check on it . It was gone. That was only in about a minute , it appeared like a blink in another spot much further from the original. It was to the left of us and than appeared right in front of us again, further away . How ever this time it was reflecting on the water . It would dissapear.... Completely and then reappear . Weird . We didn't think much about it .... Today I thought I should share . There weren't any noises . Looking at the pictures now it looked disfigured with other colored lights around it almost as squiggles . It dissapeared soon after . The first picture came out normal , as a circle .
