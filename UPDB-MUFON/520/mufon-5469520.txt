{rjl} Exiting hwy starring at funny cloud saw UFO near it over I35 Heritage Trace exit

I was exiting I35 South at the Heritage Trace exit in heavy traffic. I'm always looking at the clouds and a particular cloud caught my attention because it was by itself and looked like a flea but quickly morphing. To the left of the cloud I saw two black dots/spheres hovering higher than an airplane. I watched it while I drove and it began to fly west, it stopped in mid air. Flew, made a quick u turn, hovered. By now I was off the highway and trying to find a place to park so I could record it but I looked back up, like 2seconds later and it disappeared. In the blink of an eye it was gone. I don't know if it was 2 separate spheres flying in unison or 1 craft with 2 spheres attached but it was flying higher than a plane and I don't know if drones fly that high.
