Blinking yellow lights that disappeared

Was driving home from work and headed down Pearch Orchard Rd at Lumpkin Rd I noticed two bright yellow lights over the CVS on Peach Orchard and Lumpikn Rd. The lights were there breifly and then slowly faded out. I watched and the lights did not reappear at that location. Less than 30 seconds later, I saw 5-6 bright yellow lights from my location on Peach Orchard Rd. This time I was a little further down around the area of the Wells Fargo Bank. I looked down by the overpass of 520 and there were 5-6 bright yellow lights that came on (not in unison) and disappeared again. I watched again and the lights did not reappear. I turned right at Peach Orchard Rd and Windsor Spring Rd and as I was going down past the the automatic carwash I noticed another bright light right over the trees to my left hand side that appeared and disappeared. When I got home I informed my husband about what I had seen and we went out on the deck to watch the skies. We did not see anything else that nights.
