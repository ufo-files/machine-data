My son looked outside and observed a bright white light towards the west of where we live. We figured it was a plane but then the light never moved. It was stationary for 10 mins, then got bright and blinked out. Then a few minutes it reappeared and was gone within a matter of minutes. We have waited for it to reappear and it hasnt. We were excited and nervous and most of all curious to know what we had seen in the night sky.
