Bright object flying parallel to but just behind and to side of commercia jet

At about 6:30 AM On 1/18/22 I was alone, working outside when I noticed a single commercial jet approaching from the north, at ceiling (30000 feet I suppose), headed south, at about 50 degrees up. Only object in sky, so I noticed it. Maybe 15 seconds later I searched for it and easily found it. There was a bright white object almost beside it. It took me a moment to make sense of what I was seeing, which at first I thought was a meteor. This was an object other that a meteor for a few reasons. First it looked like a white glowing cigar, very bright with no trail. The object was moving exactly like the jet, only to one side and just behind the jet. Seemed slightly longer than jet, but very very bright. It mirrored jets speed and direction perfectly for good distance then I lost object when it entered clouds. Object was so bright I could see it through the clouds at first, till clouds got too thick I guess, then I lost sight of object. I feel jet passengers and crew had to have seen this, very close to jet.
