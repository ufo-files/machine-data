It was around 2am, while my girlfriend was driving home we noticed something in the sky. At first we didn't think much of it because there are sometimes helicopters flying in the area. But what caught our attention was that the object appeared to be low to the ground, right above tree tops and had no sound coming from it. Usually helicopters make a lot of noise so we were both very surprised and stunned at the same time. We looked right at it as it hovered slowly aboved us with a few lights blinking. I told my girlfriend to turn around so we can take a picture of it and as we were turning it just took off with an amazing speed, and it was gone just like that.
