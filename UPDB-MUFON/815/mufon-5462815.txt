{tjd} Saw giant triangular black craft fly very slowly over buolding, very very low

I was in the parking lot where I worked (for NASA) about 14 years ago, walking to my car at about 6pm. It was getting dark. Overhead appeared a VERY slow flying VERY large black triangular craft with extremely bright sets of lights on it at intervals, shining straight down. The thing seemed to be moving so slow it was a wonder it didnt fall out of the sky, and it was completely silent. It was so massive that my brain has kind of distorted my guess as to how big it was. I dont know if it was flying low and was just extremely slow and big, or if it was flying high, but was massive (just further away). The silence was also confusing. 

I marveled at the thing and watched as it flew over my building. I immediately decided it was a secret aircraft (as I worked in the aerospace industry). I thought it was funny that they were flying it when it wasn't completely dark, though, if it was secret. I figured the lights shining down were meant to obscure the craft itself. That's how bright they were.  Anyhow, I blew off the sighting for years.

I finally mentioned it to my friend recently. He was the head of Lockheed Martin at the time, and a couple other companies here. I told him about this secret craft sighting, and with surprisingly wide eyes he said we did not have any secret craft matching that description, and no craft he knew of could fly that low and slow. This guy is pretty much one of the top guys in town. He should know. It was only them that I realized it was a UFO.
