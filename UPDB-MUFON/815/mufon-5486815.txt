Since late last summer, my fiance and I have continued to see strange lights over the Palouse, Moscow, and Lewiston, ID. Most of them are seen at night, but we've also seen 3 cylinder shaped objects during the day now. During the day, the cylinders have appeared to be white, hovering, and then continuing on a path. No lights, no sound. We've witnessed several cylinders at night that have various strange lights, usually white/red/green that pulsate in an odd sequence. There are also still bright orbs that appear suddenly. Some stay stationary, other flicker and disappear or go on a straight path. Still seeing these nightly. I've taken several videos, and would like to know what they are. I'm hoping they're just some sort of surveillance (although that's not exactly a good thing), and not something unexplained. We have watched the stars a few times a month for years, and had never seen anything like this until recently. My mother and sister have also witnessed these a couple of times, too. A weird sphere has flown near the highway above my car a couple of times now. If I pull over, it seems to "disappear" or turn off it's lights and not be visible anymore.  I'm going to attach a few different videos of various types of things we've seen. I have several more, if interested. If anyone has answers or guesses as to what these things are, I'd love to know.
