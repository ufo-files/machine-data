multi-coloured, circular, very bright, moved fast and rotated

we were laying outside of the high school after about 1am and all of a sudden a very large (what appeared to be a star) came closer to us (it was about 2 miles up in the air) and it kept enlarging. next thing we know it is above our heads and it is moving in circles and it is flashing many colours and the photo i have of it looks like a red, yellow, white and green beam of light in a some-what triangular shape. it moved pretty fast and then just disappeared. at first we thought it was a plane but then after seeing various videos on youtube posted the same day of the same thing i knew it wasn't just a plane. we were all freaking out but weren't unusually effected.
