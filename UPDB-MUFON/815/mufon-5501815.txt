Travelling in vehilce taking randon pics, discovered object in pic later at home

I was travelling from Westlock Alberta to Edmonton on the morning of June 8, 2014. I was taking several random pics along the way of the flat landscape and farm land with my cell phone. My last photo turned out completely white and my phone would not work for about an hour after the incident. Upon reviewing the photos once I was at home, I noticed that in one photo there was a distinct Orb in the photo, another had a faint object in the air to the east, and one more photo had a distinct object either hovering or moving in the western sky above the Morinville Hutterite colony. I did not see these objects in the sky when I was travelling there but could only see them in the photos later.
