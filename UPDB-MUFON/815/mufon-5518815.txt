Having a ciggarette when something strange came down.

A long night of talking with an old friend and my wife at home in Melbourne.
I went outside for a cigarette when a light came down VERY quickly and hovered about 2km's away from us. It stayed for about an hour and a half then took off again straight up as quickly as it arrived.
It did not change shape or pulsate or anything, it just remained the same for the duration.
I've had previous experience with Ufo's so was quite calm. I don't find them frightening but am intrigued by them. I called out to my friend and wife to come out and have a look and we watched it for the entire duration. I also grabbed my camera and got a few snaps. As usual with most of these cases, blurry night time photos :(
