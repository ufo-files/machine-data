Star like UFO?

I was outside just after dark around 7:38 pm . I had went out for a smoke. I saw what looked like a start moving from west to East. At first I thought it was a star, then I thought it was a plane circling to land at airport. 

But then I noticed there was no airplane noise, nor were there blinking lights? I thought maybe I was at the wrong angle, so I moved Around my street trying to get a different angle. 

Then I started to think maybe the noise was just not here and maybe not ALL. Planes have flashing lights. Although I don’t remember ever seeing a plane with no blinking lights, and I hear planes all the time! I mean we’ve lived a couple miles from PDX for 20 years now. 

We’re used to hearing all types of plane sounds as there are commercial, private and military airports around here. But I swear they all have some sort of blinking lights. 

Today I asked hubby if all planes have flashing lights, and when he said yes, I decided to come here to report what I saw. 

No clue what it was! It did move mostly west to East, although at one point it moved a bit north. I’m just hoping someone else saw it or knows what it was?
