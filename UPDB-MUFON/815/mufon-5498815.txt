{cak} More then 20 slow moving orbs

A little after 8pm the wife went to put the trash out and directly over head was about 10 gold orbs with more coming from the horizon. We live in the woods so the horizon is about 45 degrees higher. She came running in and retrieved me from my recliner. I reluctantly walked out. WOW what a site. From over head to as far south into the trees as I could see were these small slow moving and very silent moving orbs. I asked the wife to get the phone. All moving in a northern direction very low and close to us. No pattern or formation just a random group. 2 or 3 broke off to the east over the river. A few appeared to pause or hover in the south. One stopped at what appeared to be over the neighbors house and ejected four tiny balls which fell in a circular or spiral action. At least 2 or 3 disappeared as they were moving towards us. There were to many to count when I first saw them and another 10 or more kept coming towards us. I got the phone and took 16 horrible pictures of the last one or two. When they finished coming I jumped in the truck and drove to the top of the mountain. We live in a deep valley but on top of the mountain I can see for ever. It was perfectly clear and I could see none of them. We live in a remote area but there was an unusually large number of trucks and cars evacuating the area. I believe there were a few other people that witnessed this.
