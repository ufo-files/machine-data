Witnessed a bright object moving across the sky from north to east.

Object was moving slowly and came to a stop. My wife and I were both witnesses. As we were watching the object it started moving again eastward at about the same pace. I ran inside to get my phone so that I could get a video. Within two minutes I came back with my video running. I asked my wife where it was. She said it moved west even further and pointed to the area where it was and said that it had gotten quite a bit smaller and looked as though it was disappearing. I kept my video pointed in that direction, but I could not see anything, and she told me that it was gone, that it had disappeared. Later, reviewing the video, I found that I had captured something moving very quickly, changed directions and appeared to come straight down at a very high rate of speed and appeared to disappear.
