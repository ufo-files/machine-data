{cak} Full sighting of UFO, right in front of me

Earlier that night I had been looking at stars and pondering ufo/extraterrestrials, then a large cloud moved over the general area. I was wide awake and lying at the foot of my bed. There is a large window cracked open on the side of me and a small square window above the head of my bed, so from where I am I can see out of the square window. It was 12:40 am and I witnessed an unidentifiable object with gold and red lights glide behind said cloud. I decided to stay up ten or so minutes to see if I anything else happened. At 12:50 ish I was slightly facing my large window lying down and I heard a sound as if something was descending from the sky, similar to an airplane but much quieter and less muffled. I turned a fraction of an inch towards my square window, thinking sarcastically "what if that is a ufo," and to my surprise I saw a cross section of a ufo directly in front of me through the window. I jolted out of bed to the head of my bed and was staring directly at a ufo. It had gold and red lights in a repeating order circling it about 1/3 up the top of it. Our streets have the houses facing inwards to each other so there is not much space for things to fit in between. It was a very small looking UFO, approximately 12 yards diameter, completely circular. I stared at it for about 15-30 seconds, completely horizontal from it as it was hovering, and then it tilted a bit, glided up over the houses slowly, made a right behind a few trees and was out of my vision.Mind you I was WIDE AWAKE the entire time, it could not have been a dream in the slightest, only other object I felt could possibly be it is a drone, but I feel that is far more unlikely, given it's appearance and1 everything.
