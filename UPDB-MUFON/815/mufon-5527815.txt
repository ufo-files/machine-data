Flashbulb and erratic movement of star-like object

I was standing outside my house observing the sky in a southeastern direction. Directly above my head was what looked like an old time flash bulb. A bright cirle with a dark center. This event lasted for a few seconds. After this flash I could see no object in the area where this event took place. I continued to watch the general vicinity and the event occured again in what I could describe as higher up or deeper into space. The events were exactly the same, a bright flashbulb like burst lasting about two seconds and no observable object in the place of this flash afterwards. After waiting about ten minutes I walked out away from the house into the street at that moment an object at about eleven o'clock in the sky blinked like a strobe on an aircraft however it was way out there and no color was eminating from it. This object moved or should I say "jumped" to a position southeast of me and was blinking and moving although I could'nt see the movement. It was changing it's position without any observable movement. The pattern of it's blinking was not steady. It seemed random. I watched it "jump" away and lost it on the southern horizon. As a sidenote: my neighbor saw an object streak across the sky way up there moving from west to northeast. He said it came to a complete stop and went straight up and disappeared. This event occured about a week before my event. The whole event really had me "creeped out" I could'nt help feeling like I was being watched somehow. It was hard to sleep that night.
