{cak} UFO flashed a bright light and then disappeared

This occurred over Torch Lake during a stargazing session. The UFO went in a straight SW direction with a single stationary light. It then appeared to stop in its tracks and flashed a really bright and intense light in our direction and promptly disappeared. I got the uneasy feeling that it knew I was there and that the flash of light was directed towards me. I know what satellites look like, and this was similar at first, but the flash of light and the disappearance rules that out. I studied some videos online of satellites glinting from the sun, and this event was completely different from anything of the sort. I can’t get it out of my head: that feeling that it knew we were there and was acknowledging us with that flash. I’ve never seen anything like it, and we’re in this area every year.
