Followed by a star floating above the water.

My partner & I were driving home from Tarbet , Lich Lomond, I observed a light in the water to the left of me while I was driving along the road, a short while later I noticed it was still there, I was curious as to what it could be, as it seemed to be following alongside my car, I came to a safe place that I knew & pulled in at a place called Hollyrood house, I stopped the car & started to obseve the object, I hadn't had time to mention it to my partner, before I knew it thr object shot up, taking at least 5 sharp angled, extremely fast manouveres & disappeared behind Ben Lomond, both myself & my partner shouted at each other, did you see that, we sat there for a while watching, then moved further along to Luss, we sat there for an hour or two, hoping to see it again, but we didnt.
