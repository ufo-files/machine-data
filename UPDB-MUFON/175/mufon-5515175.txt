Several objects in the sky

I was looking at the sky when suddenly a silver/white object called my attention. It was not moving. At first I thought it could be a satellite or something like that. I called my husband to show him the object. All of a sudden other similar objects were visible (we counted seven of them). We called some of our neighbors to witness the fact. Unfortunately we don't have a good camera so we got the cell phone in order to try to get a picture of what we were observing. My neighbors and us had the feeling the objects were observing our city or something like this. As the sun went down the objects could not be seen anymore. I think they were at that position all the time but they could only be seen due to the reflection of the sun.
