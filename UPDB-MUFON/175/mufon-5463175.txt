Saw a large flashing light in the night sky.

Went out for a quick walk with my dog.
While my dog found a place to do its business, i looked up in the night sky, and saw this "golfball" of a really flashing light.
It doesn't look like a regular star, so i started filming it, and took a few photos.
When i got back inside, i forgot all about it.
Last night i went into my gallery, and looked through all of my pictures.
When i got to the pictures i took that night, i zoomed in on the object, and it looked a lot more like something "alien".
So i am now reporting it here, as i am curious to find out if it is something, or just a very flashing star.
