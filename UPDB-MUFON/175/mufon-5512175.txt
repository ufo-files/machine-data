(GS) a bright glowing

Sept. 2012, cloudy night, went out on my deck to smoke, the bright light caught my eye, a glowing "bar" or edge of something. in seconds it took off leaving a light trail that lasted a second or two. No sound at all.
I kept questioning what I saw. Everyone I mentioned it to just laughed. I know what I saw. The light trail was not a single line. Thats why I thought it might have some dimension to it that wasnt visible because the sky was so overcast. It shot off over my row of townhomes without a sound
in a north/northeast direction.
