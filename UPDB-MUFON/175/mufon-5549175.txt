I was sitting on front porch looking east and suddenly noticed 3 green discs persumably from the same craft zoom across the sky at what seemed a fairly low altitude (under 5,000 ft.). The discs seemed only perceptable from the correct angle because after watching it fly a distance of about 10 miles in the matter of about 20 sec. it was no longer visible. My perception was that you had to be virtually directly under the craft in order to see the green discs which I presume had something to do with its perpulsion system. Keep in mind it was dark out with no overcast and it was defintely not a reflection on the clouds. It was totally silent and moved very smoothly and steadily. By the time I thought to take a picture with my camera phone it was no longer visible. Please let me know if you have had similiar reports especially in my area.
