Shiny silver cylinder, no wings, silent slow cruise by, below clouds

Aug 3rd, 2011 7:50pm. Just went outside to smoke cig and saw shiny silver cylinder with no wings glide by below clouds. From NNE to SSW. Going slower than an airplane would at that altitude and in a steady straight pace with no noise. Ran got binoculars and saw black marks from nose to midway, kinda looking like it had 2 levels? By time found camera, it was gone.
