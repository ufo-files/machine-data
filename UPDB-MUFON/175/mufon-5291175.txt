Small group turned into larger cluster

House faces north which has a huge mountainous area. 4 solid blue objects appeared over mountain headed south towards south (Tucson direction). Soon, several more objects appeared which made the cluster appear to be 7. They started heading south but were forming strange patterns. As they headed over the housing area a larger solid red object appeared and followed the cluster. They all headed into the lower clouds headed south but the colors were still discernible. The entire cluster headed south and disappeared into the clouds and darkness. I captured a portion of this on my phone but the phone did not accent the colors. I also was in a panic and filmed what I could. I am in the process of editing the footage so the good footage is viewable instead of the ground.
