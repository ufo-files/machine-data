{cak} red hovering fireball changing directions

Traveling north bound on the 101 freeway I noticed a red fireball with a tail and aura around it at my 2 oclock. I brought it to the attention of my passenger as the object moved horizontally across the sky to our 11 oclock, it appeared to slow down and hovered in place for about 45 sec. In that 45 sec the object lost its tail and seemed to diminish a bit resembling a red star.  The object then changed direction going out towards the atmosphere and gaining a tailing again as if picking up speed turned a bright red and disappeared.
