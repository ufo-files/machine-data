I was driving west, sun was setting it was about 8:20 pm on 4-26-12 and I thought it was a chem trail but it wasnt getting any longer, so I thought I must be getting glare of my windshield or vehicle so I moved my head around and what I thought was some kind of glare never moved or disappeared. I kept an eye on it and it suddenly tipped its side closest to me downward and that is when I could see it was an oblong saucer shape. It was glassy giving the appearance of being translucent with a dark tinted ring on top side that looked like a tinted window, a gray metallic edging outlined the outer edges. I had to curve to the south west and the sun was not shinning and I could still see it, then houses blocked my view for about a 1/4 of a mile and I turned west and it was no longer there, just nothing. This was over Saginaw Mi
