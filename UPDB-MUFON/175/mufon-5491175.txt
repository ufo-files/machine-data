{rjl} Silver object buzzed plane

I was on a Southwest Airlines flight from Baltimore to Ft.Lauderdale when over the ocean around the Fla/Ga border, a bright silver metallic looking object zipped past the plane at a very high rate of speed, leaving a trail.

It was on the west side of the plane, not far off the wing, maybe a half mile. And it moved from the front to back. (South to North)

It was moving so fast that all I could get with my phone was a bad pic of the trail.

I questioned the pilot on the way off the plane as there was no way he could have missed it since it came from in front of us, and he said he didn't see a thing, even after I showed him a pic of the trail left by the object.
