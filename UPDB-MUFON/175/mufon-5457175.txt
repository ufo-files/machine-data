{cak} Hovering bright light that disappeared

While traveling down Northampton Street on November 9th 2019 at approximately 5:25pm a bright white light was observed being stationary in the sky, it stayed in the same location for approximately 3 minutes and seemed to dim. The light then brightened and it appeared to have blinked out of existence.
