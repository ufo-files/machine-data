1990 motley crew dr feel good tour at LA forum

Very large dark gray and black Triangular multishapes, floating with no sound from east going overhead slowly with several course minor corrections. There were 9 people in my party, there were hundreds of people walking back to their cars when it was seen. Its shape was sharp degrees and angles like nothing Id seen before. It was huge, like maybe a couple of football fields long and 300 yard wide. Floating along at slow speed. The turned slightly northwest and without a sound took off at an accelerated speed and the poof! Gone... never told anyone until now. This thing wasnt built here... the edges and the faint glow of the edges just screamed at me this was not of our making. I dont think in the 90s we had the abilities the thing displayed, like the escape speed it left at!
