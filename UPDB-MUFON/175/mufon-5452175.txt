3 orange orbs

several years ago , I witnessed the 3 orange orbs in a triangle pattern. The sky was black with no stars. I remember it being chilly to cold out let Sept or October. I pulled over in a parking lot with my wife and watched for several minutes. Comment that there were no stars visible and the sky was unusual dark. Drove home. A few few blocks from were i first spotted this unknown, went back outside my home and watched for 30 to 45 mins. In a blink.of the the 3 objects were gone and the night sky returned to normal, i.e. stars and not fully dark. This took place around 9pm at night.
The next day I told a friend who did not see this but believes in "ufo's" and he found several videos of these and even found reports of thst nights incidents. 
It left.me a little shaken but not affected.
