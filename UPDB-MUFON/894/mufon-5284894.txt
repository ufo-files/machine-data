Oval ring hovered over the sky. Then zipped off when government aircraft were coming.

In 1999, before cell phones, I was leaving my house going back to work from having lunch at home. I was traveling west, and then started observing the cars around me were slowing and coming to a stop, with everyone looking up. I slowed and looked up too and saw an oval object just hovering in the air. It was an oval ring, so the center was hollowed out. It was some type of metal material and you can see that the sun was reflecting off of it. It hovered for a couple of minutes, barely moving. Then, you could hear loud planes and helicopters coming closer. This object then zipped off going farther and farther away, not left or right. It was so fast. And then in that same area in the sky, there were blackhawks and fighter jets. We all just sat around in our cars looking around like what just happened.
