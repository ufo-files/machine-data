{fr}diamond shaped UFO observed

I woke up at around 3 AM this morning at my parents house. Im visiting them for the holidays right now and i am currently staying in my old room at the far end of the house. I was awoken by the sound of their motion alarm going off for their shed outside the house where they keep the freezer and various other things.

i went out to check it out and found nothing out of the ordinary and went back inside

as i crawled back into bed, i looked out the window and saw a ufo, it appeared to be fairly large, diamond shaped and with a series of lights running along the outer edge of it, similar to this rendidition {link moved/CMS/dr] someone did, only with the lights going around the full edge of the craft and with a bright light in the very center of it.

this is where it starts getting weirder. i heard a very loud high pitched noise and it felt like my entire body was getting extremely heavy and i wanted nothing more than to close my eyes and fall asleep. i think i eventually did do just that and what i experienced was like a cross between dreaming and hallucinating, i don't remember most of it, but i do remember seeing something that showed aliens that looked like what we typically call The Greys, and following that, an image of me talking to my dad and him telling me this one sentence. "Aliens think chicken is a delicacy." 

after this, i seemed to have come to somewhat still in my bed, and still hearing that loud high pitched noise. for the most part i was still unable to move aside from wiggling my hand slightly. i felt as though i might fall asleep again to that sound and the thought of that happening made me scared to day the least, for what felt like hours i sat there trying my hardest to move and stay awake, then suddenly, it was like whatever it was stopped and i found myself able to move again. my limbs right now feel extremely sore right now, almost like i was doing heavy labor recently, and i found the entire length of the episode after i went back to my bed lasted just over two hours. the was no sight of the UFO outside afterwards either.
