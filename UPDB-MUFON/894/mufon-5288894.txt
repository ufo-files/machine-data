Observed UFO being chased by military helicopter over Puyallup Valley in Western Washington.

Sometime after 11PM on March 29th, 2021, after hearing an extremely low flying military helicopter (I believe it was a Blackhawk) over our home in Puyallup, WA, coming from South to North, I decided to look out a West facing window (looking North-North East following the path of the sound). I spotted the helicopter silhouette and its red strobe light slowly hovering North West over and just past the WA State Fair Grounds (Puyallup Valley). Suddenly, an object appeared about 1000 feet south of the military helicopter, hovering about the same altitude, which I estimate at between 1000 and 1500 ft, West of my vantage point. It remained stationary for about 1 second then began to move straight south. The helicopter immediately turned in the objects direction and began to chase it. The helicopter and object were moving at what I believe were speeds somewhere between 70 and 100 MPH. After about 5 or 6 seconds, the object disappeared (no clouds near the object and the helicopter was still visible on the same path), reappeared about a second later on the exact same spot, then began to move again in the same southerly direction for another 2 seconds. It finally made a sudden 90 degree turn do west. The helicopter immediately changed its heading toward the same direction and continued to follow the object until both disappeared in the horizon from my vantage point.

The object appeared as a self-illuminating sphere/orb and about 8 to 10 feet in diameter, if the object happened to be at the same distance as the helicopter was from my vantage point, which was likely the case, judging by the chase path.
