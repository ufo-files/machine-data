{cak} Saw lights appear/disappear in different locations over the ocean.

I walked out onto a deck facing east to look at the nearly full moon over the ocean on 28th street In Sea Isle City, NJ. I saw a large bright red light approximately 30 degrees above the horizon that caught my attention. At first I thought it was a plane, but then it disappeared, and re-appeared several times (while keeping it's general course north) but the light was dramatically changing locations while moving North. It then started bleeding or trailing into its appearances, and then it vanished. My feeling was pretty much "jaw-dropped." The whole ordeal lasted maybe 15-seconds.
