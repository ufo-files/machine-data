{nd} Looked Like a Fireball moving at the speed of an airplane.

My husband, my 4 year old daughter, and I had seen a reddish/orange fireball like object in the sky above the trees at my the apartment complex where I live, the night of New Years Eve. We were watching tv and I looked out the balcony of our third floor apartment and had notice this strange object. At first I blew it off as an air plane. The second time it came around I realized it wasn't. We had seen what we believe to be the same object three times within ten minutes. It must have been circling because we had seen it each time coming from the same place, flying in the same direction.(Unless it was three different fireball looking objects). There was no sound coming from it as we could tell. The object seemed to being at the speed of what an airplane would be. Two nights later my husband had seen the object again. I did take a short video of it on my phone. It did not come out to well and in the video it seems as thought the fireball like object is bouncing around a little bit but it was not.
