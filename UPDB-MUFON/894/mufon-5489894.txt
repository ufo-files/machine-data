{jpg} Witness a UFO during the month of August.

Returning home after an evening church service, I noticed a large cigar-shaped craft "gliding" through the night sky approximately 150 ft above the tree line. Initially I thought it was blimp-type object until I realized the intensity of its radiating red/orange lights. It emanated from within the craft. As it picked up speed, it eventually began to hover over the wooded area adjacent to my home. I was able to capture some of it on my iPhone. Although what I caltured on camera doesn't seem to fully capture the reality of what we were witnessing. I was left feeling totally overwhelmed by the incident.
