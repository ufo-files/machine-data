Unusual light- took photo - photo showed shape

At 23:15 EST on 02DEC2021 I went outside in my back yard to retrieve my dog as he was not coming to me when I called him. This is not unusual behavior for him. While outside I was calling my dog and he wasn’t listening. Out of frustration, I threw my head back and sighed. It is at this moment when I noticed a strange cluster of at least three, maybe four lights almost directly above me. They drew my attention because they were slowly dimming and then brightening. This pulsating happened sequentially and seemed to have a pattern. This group of lights was stationary in the sky above me and did not appear to move from that location. If I were to hold my thumb up to try to cover the lights, it would have covered most but not all of the lights. I decided to take some pictures and show my dad to see what he thinks of it. The first photo (in which you can see the tree branches) our backyard flood light was on. This light has a motion sensor and shuts off automatically after a certain amount of time. After the first photo, the backyard light shut off. I mention this only to provide the on ground conditions at the time. So all but the first photo were taken in a dark back yard. I did not see any shape on, around, or near the lights at the time when I observed them. When I reviewed the photos however, I noticed a silhouette that appear to be rounded and encompassing these lights. They looked to me to be lights on the bottom of this dark circular object.
