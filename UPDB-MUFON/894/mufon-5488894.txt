UFO in background of selfie pic

I was on a family vacation trip to Playa El Quemaito, on Barahona, Dominican Republic on July 22, 2015. I climbed to a lifeguard chair with my daughter and took a selfie picture with the front camera of my cellphone (Blackberry Z30). A couple of weeks later I was reviewing the pictures of that family trip and I noticed this UFO in the background.
