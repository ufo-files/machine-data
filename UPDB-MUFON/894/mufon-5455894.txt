I was 17 at the time. I didn't believe in UFO's for that matter. I went for a walk on the canal to my left of my parents house. I noticed the apple orchard at the end of the street was completely lit up and it was 9:30 at night. I thought it strange but kept walking. Then I noticed a aircraft coming in my direction. I figured at some point we would intersect. It made no sound other than a low hum. It hovered over me when it got to where I was walking. I stared at the lights since I could not make out the color or even see the aircraft. It was like it was invisible but I could see four bright white lights in the shape of a rectangle with four white lights not as bright or big as the first ones in between the rectangular ones...giving it more of an oval appearance....It hovered over me for a bout one minute then I waved it away with my right hand and told it to keep moving as I started walking backwards to my parent's house. The humming got louder and it streaked to the west in a arc of light then it hovered there for an instance then it arched upwards into the stars in a second streak of light. It just completely disappeared into the stars. It seemed to be parked in the apple orchard before it went up. My brothers have also had sightings near our parent's home. But this was my experience. I've never had one since.
