Color changing lights in the sky

I woke up to let my dog out to go to the bathroom and my brother came with me. We have sensor activated lights in the back yard and they went out, and that’s when we noticed the sky changing colors in one spot. None of us knew what could make that happen, or what it was. There were no planes, drones, helicopters, floating lanterns, search lights, or emergency vehicles. There were no sounds. It was brighter than a floating lantern, and bigger. It was cloudy, but you could see there was a darker shadow of cloudiness below the unknown object. The lights were also isolated above the ground, not reflected in the clouds from below it. It didn’t move, or appear in a specific shape. We’re not sure how long this happened as we were only outside for a handful of minutes. I recorded the sighting on my phone. I did go out to my front porch to check the sky, and it wasn’t happening anywhere else but in the area in the back.
