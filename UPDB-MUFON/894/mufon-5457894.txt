UFO landed changing formation 3 times

After returning home to a friends place we near the base of the mountains, we saw a group of five bright lights flying very slowly north over the mountains. The lights dipped below the mountain making those lights no further than 1.5-2kms away from where we were. 
There were 5 lights in a row. The lights changed formation the lights slowly rotated from a 5 lights in a row to diagonally. (Top left to bottom right) it then continued to change formation to 3 lights in a row on the bottom to 2 on top. The whole time it was slowly flying north and descending. Once it got to the last formation it past the tree line and landed. 
There were no flashing lights. No noise. 
Where we saw it was a rural area. We could hear approaching cars tyres rolling along the road but we could not hear this object in the sky.
We watched the lights the whole time from above the mountains to when it landed. 
We did talk about going looking for it, but we didn’t know where to start to look at 2am. 
The photos show what we saw. The lights were always moving and slowly changing into each photo. Drones were not invented. Mobile phones didn’t have cameras, and digital camcorders and cameras were expensive.
