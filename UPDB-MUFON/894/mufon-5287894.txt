It came in slowly and silently from the east to west

It was huge cigar shaped, with white lights in the middle running the length. What was odd was that it was tilted back like " the backspace" ( / ), Almost like it was scanning. It was silent, moving in a line east to west but it didnt behave like it was obeying any form of gravity. From my back porch i watched it moveing slowly it looked like it came over Hillsboro, Lake Oswego, and then over south east Portland continuing on like it was following the Colombia River on to Idaho.
