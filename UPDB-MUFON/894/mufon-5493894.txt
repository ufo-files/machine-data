{rjl} Noticed. A large glowing ball hovering and then flickering out

Went outside to smoke at 10pm and noticed a bright orange glowing ball come from behind the trees. No noise at all and it moved very slowly. It looked like a big fireball. The glow would occasionally flicker in and out, it then stopped and hovered until the glow would flicker out completely and I couldn't see it anymore. Then another one would come from the same place behind the trees, move slowly to the same spot hover and fade out again. This happened 5-6 times until I saw and heard a plane fly through the same location, and it never came back.
