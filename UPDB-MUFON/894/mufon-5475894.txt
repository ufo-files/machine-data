Night Fishing out of sheepshead bay Brooklyn and the boat goes out to the breezy point Atlantic side of the beach. 2 hours out and I happen to look up and see a light, rectangular light the size of a football field facing north. It caught my attention because this light was very long. Airliners have small round beams of light this was huge. The strangest thing is that it was a clear night and the stars were visible behind and around the object . Visible to the point that a object emitting this kind of light would have been seen, blocked out some of the stars, made some sort of noise but you could not see or hear anything. All I saw was the light facing away from the boat towards shore or north and the stars shining through it, whatever it was. A cloud of smoke started to cover the light completely and it was gone. No noise, no sound, nothing obscuring the stars. I will wait for your response and do not patronize what I saw. This was not ours.
