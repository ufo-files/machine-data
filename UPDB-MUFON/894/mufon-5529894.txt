Three orb's at Cloud level, moving in zig zag pattern

Sighting began at approximately 7:40 a.m.. I noticed a black sphere at cloud level moving in zig zag pattern. The sphere moved in and out of the surrounding cloud's, but staying withing close proximity of the near by cloud.After about 1 minute of viewing the sphere, it was joined by two other ors of the approximate size. The three spere's moved around one another in same movement and behavior. The three objects then moved in and out of the same cloud. After about four minutes of viewing this behaviour, the three objects dissappeared within the clouds and were lost in sight.
