{cak} Disc, spheres on bottom, shiny, metallic, dark dome on top, fast, instant change in direction.

In the summer of 2010 I was a passenger in a car making a trip from Fresno to Los Angeles for family purposes. It was a perfectly clear day with almost no clouds. I first noticed the object because it was hovering very low to the ground (500 ft or so) and moving in creditably fast. The object then suddenly stopped instantly quite close to the car. I could clearly see it without any doubt. There was no mistaking this object. I excitedly told everyone else in the car and pointed to the object. They also witnessed it. We were all absolutely amazed at what we were seeing. The object sat still in the sky dangling like it was tied to an invisible string. It glistened in the sunlight a bit. It had 3 black spherical bumps on the bottom and a black dome on top that did not reflect the sunlight.The object was clearly metallic in form and quite large. Allow me to emphasize that it was extremely close at this point, maybe 500 feet or so, and we knew exactly what it was. It looked like a very classical Hollywood depiction of a UFO. It simply could not have been mistaken for any other aircraft. After this period of dangling (which lasted around 45 seconds), it sped away in a perfectly straight line taking no time to get up to speed, then made a perfect 90 degree turn and began to gain altitude before disappearing. From the time it began moving again until it disappeared, no more than 10 seconds had passed. Everyone in the car was astonished beyond words, and to this day still agree on exactly what we saw.
