{nd} Orb ufo flies over LA

I was with friends having a picnic @ The Woodley Park Recreation Area on 6/24/12 When at around 6 pm. I was lying on my back on my friends picnic blanket looking at the clear sky and noticed a commercial airliner flying over head, but pretty high. I then noticed a white orb, very high overhead, bright or reflective, with a starlike quality about it- was stationary above the plane. The plane passes on and I watch as the orb moves along in a rather slow trajectory and then stops moving for nearly 5 mins. or more. It then proceeds to make a very wide arc, very high, pausing a few times, then heads back in the direction it came from.
