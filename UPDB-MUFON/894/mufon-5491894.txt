Observed large shiny saucer.

My friend was bicycling to my house when he met me across my street in (at the time) the Chevrolet car dealer parking lot. He was huffing and puffing pretty hard when he reached me and pointed towards the northeast in the air and told me to look at the silver shiny cloud. The fact of the matter was that there were no other "clouds" in the sky. We both said it was a ufo at the same time. The object looked like it was a huge saucer hovering over town to the northeast of my location that was reflecting the setting sun. I ran into my house on Scott Lane yelling at my parents to come and see this huge silvery shiny ufo. At first my parents were apprehensive, but as curious as I was, my parents reluctantly obliged. I grabbed my camera and took a picture that didn't show the object in it. My friend hurried home. My parents, neighbors, people out for an evening walk, and I on looked at the previously hovering ufo. It was slowly, and more importantly, and silently hovering on a straight line from east to west. The saucer then tilted and continued moving away from us onlookers. I would say it was about 45 minutes from start to finish. We were all in awe. Since then I have seen numerous white, star like lights (that clearly weren't planes, helicopters, or satellites) moving erratically in the night sky in various locations in the area I reside over the years. I believe!
