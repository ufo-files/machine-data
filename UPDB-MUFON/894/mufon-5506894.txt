{dr}10 orange glowing orbs flying in a straight line. The ninth one in the line floated down out of sight..

I was dropping my son off at his mothers house in Ashford, CT. His Mother met me in the driveway and we were chatting, standing outside of my vehicle. I noticed an orange glowing orb flying past, going from east to west. I mentioned to my sons Mother that it was a UFO. She then noticed a second orb of the same color and size about 30 secs later flying in the same direction at the same altitude. Six more orange orbs flew in the same direction consecutively. About 3 mins later, 2 more orange orbs appeared and were flying in the same direction. These ones were closer together. Orb number 9 seemed to float to the ground slowly while the tenth orb continued the same path as the previous ones.
