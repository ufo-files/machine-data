Bright light moving across the sky from west to east without any noise or flashing like aircraft

I was walking my three dogs outside by the side of our house and came towards the back of the patio when I noticed a bright light, that was much brighter than anything else in the sky. At first I figured it had to be a plane, but I didn't see any flashing wing lights. I put the dogs inside the house and told my sister to come out a look and she did and we both watched this light float across the sky almost at what a normal plane speed would be. However we saw and heard a plane way off to the south and could easily tell it was a plane, but this light made no noise at all and just floated to the east, towards St.john's. It was weird because when it was moving away from us to the east it was as bright as it had been when it were moving towards us. I was sorta shocked/excited because normally you see something and think it's a UFO or something but it turns out to be a plane/helicopter, but it was moving to fast to be a balloon and wasn't a aircraft cause it made no noise at all. 
We lost sight of it when I had went in the house for maybe 20 seconds, to come out and it had gone. My sister even is first to rule things out and said it was weird how fast it had moved with no noise. I would say it had to be about 2,000-3,000 feet in the air maybe, but it was a lot lower than any other plane that I have seen.
