{jpg} Ball of flame on slow path, then ascended out of sight very quickly

We were living at Fort Shafter, HI in late June 2013. We were sitting on our backyard patio after dark when we noticed a ball of flame slowly floating from the SE coming towards us. It looked like the flame you see on a hot air balloon. There was no sound from this ball of light and it lazily floated from the direction of Honolulu and over our house. We went out front to continue watching it and it suddenly ascended straight up very quickly - still without a sound. It was out of our sight within seconds. Until it made that sudden ascent, I thought it was a hot air balloon.
