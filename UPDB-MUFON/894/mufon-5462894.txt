{jpg} 2 jet flame shaped lights came from the north headed south then proceeded west over ocean pulsating multi-colored lights

As I and others were leaving a friend's house on January 24th, 2019 at approximately 2013 hours, my friend tells me to "check that out up there". As I look up in the night sky,(which was calm with no clouds and no wind), I see two lights that at first I thought could be an asteroid breaking up in the Earth's atmosphere. But upon further inspection, the lights were moving much too slow and appeared much closer to be an asteroid breaking up. These two lights were at first triangular-shaped similar to the exhaust of a fighter jet engine and were moving slowly in a Southerly direction from the North and were completely silent. It should be noted the airspace these objects occupied is directly over the Del Norte County Regional Airport which is restricted to only private and commercial flights (no drones allowed). As the 2 objects slowed down and changed direction to the West, they changed shape to a sperical configuration with multi-colored pulsating lights surrounding them. They proceeded in a Westerly direction and disappeared in the distance over the Pacific Ocean. Both objects seemed to move independently of each other although followed virtually the same paths with some variances in path of travel.
