{cak} Silver Ball Object Zig Zagging Over Our Heads

I am a school teacher and my dad is Retired US Army. We are both credible people. We were out at a park flying our drone. Because we were both looking up, we both immediately caught site of a silver ball-like object reflecting the Sun from the West. The object was Zig Zagging through the sky. It was moving quickly. We have lived on military bases most of our lives and know our military aircraft. What we witnessed today was NO aircraft we know about. Furthermore, there is no object we know of that can move effortlessly like the one we saw. We both yelled WTF is that. Do you see that object? It was present for a short time and then disappeared. The sky was cloudless. The object looked like a piece of solid Titanium. It was very reflective.
