{jpg} Extremely bright light flew over Phoenix heading east then turned north or northeast once it past airport

I was driving on I-10 eastbound from the 101 freeway when a large white light past me from behind and to the right of the freeway. It was dark and approximately 5am. First I thought it was an airplane coming in to land at sky harbor but quickly realized it appeared to large and had no red or green lights and no flashing red strobe. It almost appeared as if it were a police helicopter with its spotlight on me, but it continued flying east at a high speed and the brightness never dimmed. The moon and a planet were in the distance ahead of it. This object was larger and brighter than the large planet that was viewable at 5am. It went from the west valley over the airport and turned north or northeast once it hit the east valley. I think it was at about 1000 to 2000 feet so I lost it over the horizon shortly after it turned left. This object flew very quickly. Much faster than a commercial plane coming in on approach to sky harbor. I've never seen an aircraft this brightly lit or flying so fast at this low elevation.
