Fireball making erratic changes in flight at accelerated speed.

Sitting on porch with grandmother when we noticed to the northwest of our location in Midvale, which appeared to be to the west of Salt Lake City a fireball, glowing a red-orange and making some strange maneuvers at high speed. Not aircraft. Made right-angle changes in maneuvers without slowing down. We observed it for about 5 minutes. I don't know if the red-orange color was the craft itself or the sun reflecting off the object. The sun would have started going down when we saw it & no, it definitely wasnt the sun. At the time I was slightly familiar with UFO stories and believed that was what we observed. A number of years ago I had a paperback which had this sighting detailed and the author also stated that this UFO was probably the same one that came down close to an electrical substation somewhere in Nevada. Unfortunately, I no longer can locate the paperback and would dearly love to find it or own it again. This was the first time I observed a UFO. I have had a second sighting, in 1975 while flying in a commercial plane, but Im more interested in the one in 1962. Would appreciate whatever you can locate in your files.
