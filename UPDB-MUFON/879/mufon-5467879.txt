{rjl} An oval dark shape that moved slowly and smoothly until it went out of sight.

1. On the day of the sighting, I was at work, and walking between two buildings.

2. I looked up and saw an object over the building I was going towards. It was a nice day, and I was looking at the sky and clouds.

3. When I saw the object, the shape of it reminded me of a stunt I once saw, filling a trash bag with hydrogen, and letting it loose. This object however did not move like a bag filled with hydrogen or helium. A bag is basically a baloon, and from my observations of baloons, they will continue to rise, and tumble while doing so.

4. What I saw moved at a slow and steady pace, and at a constant altitude. It did not appear to be affected by the wind. I pulled out my phone and took five photos of the object. Later, upon review, I found an additional object that was in the frame, and close to the first object in two of the photos.

5. I didn't have any particular feelings at the time. I was very intent and in a rush to get a photo, as the object was getting further away by the second.

6. The objects continued on and I lost sight of them.
