Looked like a satellite but then started zig zagging.

My children and I (2 children) where out looking at the two bright stars in the night sky south of Selma Texas, I looked up after setting up the telescope and observed what at first I took as a satellite in low orbit, it was reflective because it got brighter, then it started to zig zag back and forth for about 40 seconds. Then it went straight again and faded out to nothing. When this was happening I pulled my daughter over to see it, she saw it just as it was zig zagging and said it was silver. It was heading East South East at the time and we observed it at about a 60 degree angle. I am a trained observer from the military so the behavior was not any aircraft or satellite that I know of.
