I noticed irregular pulsating flashing sharp and soft beams of light. 4 of us witnessed it

Was sitting in an open garden have a chat and i Saw a bright and dull irregular pulsating light travelling from south to south east at 830 pm on the 4th of sept. 17. At first i thought it was a plane but then watched it carefully. It looked like it was searching for something or maybe rotating. Suddenly it changes direction leaving a trail of light to the west. It was as if the object was playing around. I then showed it to my friends an they could figure out what was happening. The object traveled south east behing my building. The end.
