{dr}Bright white like object moveing at 60 to 70 miles an hour at a little over tree top level

Me and my friend were outside joking around on my parents driveway at about 8 o'clock at night. I remember he was trying to tell my something when he stops what he was trying to say points up to the sky an shouts "dude look". I turned my head around and saw a bright white light  object coming toward us from the north east. It was about 20 feet in diameter flew at an altitude of about 35 feet and went at about 60 to 70 miles an hour (and it didn't make any noise). It went fast so we only saw for about 7 seconds before it disappeared to the north west. At first we thought it was a search light from helicopter but we didn't hear anything and just before it disappeared over the houses we could tell that it was illuminating it's own light. We told one of our friends about this and he and him dad saw something similar just one week earlier at his uncles house just two blocks away.
