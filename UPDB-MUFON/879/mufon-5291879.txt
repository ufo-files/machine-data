Son sees UFO in upstate NY days before multiple UFO sightings in New Jersey

My stepson and his best friend observed a craft in his mother’s large tree filled back yard that faces the Catskill Mountains. The sighting happened at dusk while him and his friend were outside riding their dirt bikes.... they first noticed the craft out in the distance hovering in front of the mountains. Then it started coming towards them and they could see that this was a huge craft with no sound coming from it... the craft continued to move closer and closer to them when all of the sudden it zipped away in the blink of an eye and it was gone. Hearing about the UFO sightings in New Jersey on September 14th inspired us to reach out to MUFON and share what he had seen. Please feel free to contact me with any further questions. Attached is a picture of what they saw... I wish they thought to take a video but they were a couple of stunned 14 year old boys that couldn’t fully comprehend exactly what was happening.
