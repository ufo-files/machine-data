Saw a

Actual date would have been summer 1965 or 66. A friend and I were sleeping out. We went fishing in the Juniata River after midnight. We started home. I don't recall anything prior to looking up and seeing something huge and black, with lights around the edges like windows. There was no noise. I couldn't tell the shape. The back was over our heads. The bottom was curved so the front was not visible. We were outside the fence of a ball field and the front was somewhere past the fence on the opposite side. After a while it drifted slowly away towards downtown Tyrone. When it was gone, our rods and tackle boxes were on the ground beside us. I don't remember setting them down. We went back home. Neither of us ever said a word about what we had seen. That was over 50 years ago and this is the first time I've mentioned it.
