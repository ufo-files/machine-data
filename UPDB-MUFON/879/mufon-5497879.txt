{cak} Grey Drone?

I was looking into the dark night sky pointing out a few things about the stars to my friends son's when I noticed a grey object gliding by. It was about 50 yards above our heads. Both boys noticed it. It was in the shave of a V and made no noise.. The "wings" were about the length of a couch and had the width of a football. As it went by, it had no lights and we lost sight of it in the dark sky. This happened on Nov. 13th, 2014.
