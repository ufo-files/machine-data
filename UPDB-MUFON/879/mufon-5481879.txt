{dr}I watched the craft with orange pulsating panels move slowly over the corn field making no sound. Had another experience but not sure it was real.

I was standing on the back deck of my parents bedroom looking at stars when the object came into my view. It was traveling what seemed to be slow and the details of the craft were very visible. It was shaped like 2 bowls stacked together with a partial flat area on the top and bottom in the middle. It was roughly 300 feet above the corn field moving what seemed slowly. It had orange pulsating panels that were outlined in black trim. The object was maybe 200 yards away. There was no sound at all. I knew as soon as I saw the object it was an alien craft.I had a strange feeling I was being watched from something inside the craft. I watched it moving over the corn field for a few minutes. I ran in the house to get my parents. It was gone when we got back on the deck.There was an actual report right after my sighting in the next town. A farmer had a picture of a disc craft he had sighted but it did not look the same as the one I had seen.
