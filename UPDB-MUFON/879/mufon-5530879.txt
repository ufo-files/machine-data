Bright light floating at even altitude then rapidly climbed and slowly faded out.

Sitting on the porch having a cigarette and star gazing as I always do. There was a bright round light that was floating at an even altitude for quite some distance, then rapidly climbed upward and looked like it slowly burned out, the loss of light was like what you would see if it was a meteor but traveling up not down and at a much slower pace. I had a guest that I asked to step outside and take a look, neither of us could see anything that would identify this object as typical aircraft, such as blinking lights, and we both watched as it climbed and disappeared.
