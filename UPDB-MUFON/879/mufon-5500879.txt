{rjl} Observed one object hovering for several minutes. Another similar object descended from the North at a high rate of speed and met the hovering object.

I was floating in my pool when I noticed an object in the sky. It looked round in shape and seemed to be hovering. I pointed the object out to my wife. We watched for several minutes and the object never seemed to move. I got out of the pool, dried off and went into the house to get my phone. I recorded the object 2 separate times. I could not see the object while I was recording so I went into the house after recording to see if the object was visible in the camera. The 3rd time I recorded the object is the recording I will upload with my report. In the video you can see the object hovering just about center screen and to the left of the tree. At the 56 second mark you can see the 2nd object come down from the top of the screen at a high rate of speed and meet the 1st hovering object. Unfortunately I was so shocked by what I had witnessed I moved the camera right after this happened. When I looked back both objects were gone. There was no sound from either object.
