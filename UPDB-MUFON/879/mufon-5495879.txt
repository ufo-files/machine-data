{rjl} two main white lights with 3 small flashing lights. angle shape on top with multicolored edges

I was driving back home from school on rd 18 headed east toward Medina. I thought it was a bright star but then as I got closer it got bigger and I noticed a bright multicolored angled edge ontop of the light Like it was a rope of light or something. Still getting closer I noticed there were two main lights close together and tiny blinking lights beside the two big ones and abother light near the top toward the center space of the main lights. When I was under it I stuck my head out the car window and looked up at it and it still had all the lights and looked rectangular shaped. I could tell it wasn't moving. I saw another one when I pulled into my driveway on 301 and it made a circle. I looked up pics of drones and other lights in the sky including plane lights and helicopter lights but I can't explain what I saw.
