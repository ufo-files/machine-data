Light observed while traveling west

First saw what look like Jupiter but three times the size. Approximately 30 degrees from horizon. It looked to be about 5 to 7,000 feet moving at the speed of a jet not a small aircraft. It was primarily white but changed colors. There was not any running light la or strobe. It passed directly overhead. All three of us jumped out of the car as I tried to video the object. I could only keep it in the viewfinder for a short period. It accelerated or jumped about maybe two miles as I lost it. It appeared just seconds later in the eastern sky and moved quickly out of site. It appeared to be gaining altitude as it passed overhead. The sighting was 8:25 pm. Between Jonesboro and Limestone.
