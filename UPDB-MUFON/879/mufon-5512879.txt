{dr}Multiple crafts in the air above moonsville all going different directions

Around 6:00 PM on January 26 8 or more crafts were seen in the sky some going east some north with a trail behind them, i was on my way home in the car and i saw theses things in the sky. i don't think they are jets because they were pretty close to each other and there is no nearby military bases.I just looked up in the sky and saw them after i got home i went inside and came back out in about 5 or 10 minutes and they were gone all of them not even a trail left behind. i thought it was a jet at first until i saw all the rest of them and i thought now why would all these jets be in the same area, the objects just kept in a straight line i didn't see them make any sharp turns at all they were just going real fast in a straight line. i was really weirded out and curious so i searched to see if anyone else had ever seen any thing like this and i figured id better report it.
