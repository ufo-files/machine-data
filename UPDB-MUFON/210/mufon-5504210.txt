{cak} Appeared as a star, but made direction change unlike anything observed

My experience was about 18 years ago. I was 11. It was dusk, and I was fishing with my father and a friend of his and saw a bright light similar to a star moving in the horizon making maneuvers that modern aircraft are unable to make. At first I thought that it was a bright star, but the longer I watched it began to sway back and forth on the horizon. I pointed it out to my father who saw it sway from side to side. It then abruptly stopped and accelerated toward us. We then rowed the John boat to shore to get the binoculars in the truck. By the time we got the binoculars the object was overhead above the trees. It then disappeared before we were able to find it with the binoculars. The object made no noise as it approached. Throughout the entire experience I was excited because I was observing something that I had never seen before and was curious about what could be maneuvering like that.
