4 Round lights

My wife and I were driving home to Buckner, MO down Buckner Tarsney Rd. When above the trees in the cemetery I saw 4 perfectly round white lights around 1-2 foot circumference and around 2-3 feet apart in straight line just hovering above tree tops. I would say about 50 ft. above my truck. I said “wtf” paused my radio and told my wife to look up…she saw them too!! I wish I would’ve stopped and took a picture but I did not. We did not hear a sound except wind from driving. Only saw them for about 20-30 seconds then never saw them again. Did not see craft or shape just 4 bright white lights in a straight diagonal line. I drive by that cemetery almost every day and look up and have NEVER seen ANY lights in cemetery at night…usually pitch black!
