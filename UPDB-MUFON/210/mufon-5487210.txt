Husband and I were on a walk around 7:00 pm. We observer a large square/rectangular black object slowly traversing north from the WSW. No wind that day. We watched as the object continued north then it just stopped, in mid air. Held there about 1 minute, then its direction changed to traveling NW. No sound, no lights. As it changed direction the shape changed to a rectangle with a corner sliced off from one corner to center line. As this happened the higher side turned more metallic silver. Very strange. My husband and I went thru all things we knew about airial objects. No way it was a box kite, didn't move or behave like a kite. It wasn't a hovercraft, remote control, WAY to big, and maintained the same elevation the entire time. No wobbling like the remote control objects do. Plus there was no sound. Hubby and I were both in a state of amazement. We realized it was a UFO when it just stopped moving and just hung in the air not moving at all. It moved in a northwest direction until we lost site of it.
