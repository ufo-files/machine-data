{dr}Massive bright glowing orb slowly moving across the Alabama skyline.

My wife and I decided after dinner to go outside and have a cigarette when almost immediately after lighting up we noticed a huge bright object moving slowly and quiet across the skyline. Upon initial glance I thought it was just another Chem trail until I further examined what I thought we were seeing. While observing the object I noticed how slowly it was moving and compared to the airplanes in the sky at the time running Chem trails that it appeared to be emitting it's own light source. I live in a apartment complex surrounded by trees so we were unable to position ourselves to observe the object more than a few minutes before it disappeared behind the tree line.
