dark translucent semi- disc moving at constant speed under cloud cover moving from east to west

lat 17.7 long 83.2 around 6 30 pm i was on rooftop watching sky in supine position with head towards east. i was watching moving dark clouds to my left i was observing cloud margins after few secs i saw a dark translucent hemi disc like object with sharp margins over taking the irregular margin of an even dark cloud below it.i couldnt makeout whether the object was above or below the cloud but the other half of the [may be] disc was not clear.
First i thought it was a cloud but soon i was surprised.it had no lights,left no trail, was gliding in air at different velocity.I was oberserving its direction. moving clouds were easy to observe but not this one, it needed much more concentration to still vision to observe its path.
instatntly i knew it was not a cloud.it merged or disappeared along dark sky background. total duration of event approx.11 secs
