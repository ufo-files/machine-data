Very long string of unidentified lights

Clear skies over Potter County, Pennsylvania. Stepped outside of the cabin and noticed lights over the western horizon. Thought nothing of it at first, then more and more appeared to the west. Called out the other witnesses and started to count. There were at least forty-eight lights at uniform intervals. The sheer number of aircraft(??) is what confounded us. I notified the state police barracks. I'm a retired USAF weather forecaster.
