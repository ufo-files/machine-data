{cak} It flew under our plane

We were on approach to Birmingham Al airport my wife had opened her window shade I was looking out the window when out from under our plane a triangle flew on a line to the SW we were heading to the north for landing. It was not a jet or airplane it slowly rotated as it flew very strange it flew maybe a couple hundred feet below the plane and saw it very clear. It was like you would see on tv no kidding blew my mind. I only saw it for a couple seconds and it was gone.
