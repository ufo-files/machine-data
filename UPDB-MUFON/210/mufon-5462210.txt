Donut shape ring in clouds followed by an object that looked possibly like a sort of disc

Whilst traveling from kids school to home in a minibus I looked up and saw a donut shape in the clouds, made of what looked clouds only they did not disappear.
I made a photo of this donut shape which was very clear from a distance of 1-2km.
When I later zoomed in on the photo I saw directly above (100m) the donut shape another very weird hovering noiseless shape that resembled something between a disc and a super Mario turtle that had been flattened. Sounds weird but if you see the shape in the photo you will probably no what I mean.

Can1t say if it all flew away or whatever because I was traveling on a bus at the time...
Very strange..
