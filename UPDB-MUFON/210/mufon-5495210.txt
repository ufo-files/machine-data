{rjl} One white light and two green lights low to ground in triangle shape

I was driving south on I-25 in Colorado Springs and as I was about to go under an overpass I noticed 3 lights in the air. It caught by attention cause 2 were green, which I have never seen in the air. The other light was bright white at the front of the craft and was slowly blinking. The lights were configured in a triangle shape and it appeared to be very dark. It also seemed like the sky was darker in the area to hide the craft even though it was just passed dusk. When I was able to look back to see if it was there it had vanished.
