{dr}I saw an orange light, thought it was a plane. It went north for app. 5 seconds, then just vanished.

My husband and I came home from the library. We had gotten out of the car and while standing there, I happened to look up and saw an orange light overhead that I thought was a plane. It went north in a straight line for about 5 seconds, then just vanished. I checked online a couple of days later to see if anyone else may have reported it, to see if there was an explanation for it, but I didn't find anything. I don't know what it was. I guess I thought it was a plane because it seemed like it had lights to the sides at first, but when I looked at it more, it looked more like one big light, kind of like a plane. Then it just disappeared, which I thought was really strange.
