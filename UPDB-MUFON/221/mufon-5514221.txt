{cak} Bright spherical object descended and hovered

I was driving my car north to an intersection with Berryhill Rd. in Pace, FL when a bright ball of light descended straight down toward the ground. I was facing a northern direction and the time was approximately 6:15 p.m. The distance to the object was 100s of yards away and size was difficult to determine with any certainty but it appeared to be 10 to 20 feet in diameter at least. The object initially was a bright white but shifted gradually to a greenish color as it approached the ground. It then stopped and hovered above the tree line. I was momentarily confused as to what I was seeing and thought a large meteor was falling until it stopped above the tree line. At that point I had made my turn onto Berryhill Rd. when I suddenly realized what I was seeing. I turned the car around several hundred yards down the road to go back and see if the object was still there. I then drove north on Chumuckla Hwy and came around behind where I saw the object and later drove east down Willard Norris Rd. I did not see the object again. It was a very bright object and the road had several cars on it so I decided to report this in case anyone else saw what I did.
