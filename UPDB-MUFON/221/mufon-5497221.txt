{rjl} slow moving light pacing an odd path

I went outside to front porch to smoke a cigarette with my partner. We were observing the night sky because it hasnt been a clear night in a while. I saw a single light off to the east (where our porch faces) and it was considerably brighter than than stars and double a star size. I joked to my partner about it be is the same bouncing white light I saw at night a few weeks ago while outside smoking on the phone with her. She observed it and noticed it was moving. We both stared at it for over an hour. It appeared to have a small companion red light separate but always near it however it bounced around all sides of light. The main white light itself would move move very slowly towards us but shift side to side as appearing to be caught in a wind current. It kept its reserve ever slowly bouncing and weaving. It's main light was solid and but its companion would flick at random intervals. It appeared to be relatively low in the sky maybe 3000-5000 feet but not sure. Commercial airplanes would fly behind it. It moved opposite and faster than stars and appeared to be moving directly west towards our house. We watched for over 1.5 hours before we went to bed.
