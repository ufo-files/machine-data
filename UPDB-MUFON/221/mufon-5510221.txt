{dr}Solid amber orb. Rising north with a tight zig-zag flight pattern north. Slowed to a straight flight path and than stopped and faded out.

Walking north on Bowles avenue at 11:30pm. Was listening to a radio program via a wireless headset through my phone 3G on youtube. My phone went in and out and my headphones lost bluetooth connection. I pulled out my phone at eye level. My phone had froze. I noticed in the corner of my eye an Amber light at the treeline. A little smaller than a advil pill. It was ascending north and in a tight zig-zag flight pattern. Towards Ursa major. Immediately I knew it was not a plane or a sattalite. Nor was it the space station. This was by definition a Unidentified Flying Object. I tried to get to my camera on my HTC EVO 4G but it was frozen. The flight pattern continued north but straitened out. And it also slowed to almost a halt. I sensed it would fade out. And thats what the object did. I watched it come from behind the trees to were it disapeared at 60 degrees. When you encounter a sighting of this kind your mind has trouble understanding what it is actually seeing. I did my best to stay calm and observe. The color and size of the object does not fit any description of any known plane/sattalite/ or planet. I was a witness to a fireball/meteor sighting last month here in St.louis and made the report on a meteorlogical website. Many other reports were made of that fireball sighting and that is my hope with this sighting. It was not a meteor I saw tonite. The flight pattern was amazing. I have had 3 sightings in the last two years. I made a similar report last year in anahiem california of a similar amber teardropped shaped object that ejected another orb from beneath it. The colors of these two sightings are the same. But my sighting in california was at a lower altitude. This one was very far away. I hope this information is helpful. I will keep my eye on the sky.
