white light like a airplane but no red or green moving fast changed to red then gone

I walked out back and scanned the sky, from the north I saw a light like a white landing light of an airplane...Tyler Pounds regional Airport is about 3mi from my location...I have been watching air traffic for more than 20 years.This light was moving towards the East at a above normal pace.....at that point it was on my 12oclock....I entered my building on the west side grabbed my binoculars at the front door...maybe 10-15 seconds it was on my 3oclock....I tracked it for a few seconds and within a period of 2-3 seconds it shifted color gradually from white to red and gone....at no point did it exibit red or green clearance lights and it appeared to cross paths with an approching aircraft which had all of its required lights illuminated...this is what was so unusual....I don't recall seeing an aircraft in my area that did not have the red and green and beacons flashing....I have seen speeds comprable to this as I have been in the military...but then I really don't know how far away it was...it was'nt "oh my God" fast...but alot faster than normal air traffic.This object stayed about the same size the whole time.
