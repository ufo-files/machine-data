Beautiful Daytime Light Show at Abraham Lincoln National Cemetery

Walking with my wife at Abraham Lincoln National Cemetery we saw a bright flash high in the sky to the SW. After watching what we thought was an unusually bright sun reflection off a plane, the flashing stopped and we saw a white pill shaped object. Then there was a quick bright flash in the sky and the object was gone. The entire event was less than 30 seconds
