(gs) Red glowing bright/dim object over my house got my binoculars and watched as the light slowly dimmed then blinked out saw a black round object

I was watching tv in my family room, my daughter was upstairs and said she heard a weird humming sound, I heard it too but it was probably from washer in basement, I said I was going to check the perimeter which is a running joke in our family anytime anyone thinks they hear or see something outside, anyway I went out and looked up and right over my house there was a red object glowing brighter then dimmer. I watched for a long enough time to see there were no navigation lights blinking, so I ran into the house and grabbed my binoculars, went back outside which took about ten seconds, my wife was reading and asked me what I was doing and I said there was something over the house, I went back and was watching and I could slowly start to actually see the structure, and at the same time the light was dimming until it completely blinked out and I could clearly see the whole shape of the object, completely round and black, my wife came out and I told her where to look without putting down the binoculars, when she said she could see it I looked with my eyes and could see it too, I then kept looking with my binoculars until it disappeared over some houses it never made a sound that I could hear. I was amazed to actually be able to see the structure so clearly, mind blowing! Total time was about two minutes
