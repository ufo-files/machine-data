{rjl} Bright large light with smaller star-like light below to the right

Driving to work at approx 5:50AM 1/22/14. Heading South on Joslyn road just passing Canterbury Village on the right. See super bright ball of light in the sky to the South. Looks like a supernova exploding or something, thats what I thought it might be. Notice that there is a smaller light to the bottom right of the larger one. Looks like a star but brighter and slighly larger. I continue to watch this object as I drive and as I keep glancing at it, it is getting lower in the horizon. (this is a span of maybe 7 minutes). The last time I saw it was on Giddings road to the southeast in the sky slightly above the horizon. Kept wondering if the cars in front of me were seeing the same thing but no one was reacting. At first glance I thought nothing of it, but it was so bright yellow-orangish-white I kept thinking I was witnessing a supernova with a star to the bottom right. That star or whatever it was is not there in the morning and dissapeared along with the large object.
