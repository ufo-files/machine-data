{cak} greenish object descending while changing shape. Last photograph (not attached) revealed a sinister-looking face!

04 July 2019
9 pm (2100 hrs)

I saw the object while at the Park Terrace(?) cemetery near Hwy 169 and Shoal Creek Parkway (N 108th St.) …. where I had stopped to take photos of the beautiful arrangements left on many of the graves. 

While taking the cemetery photos, I noticed the greenish object in the air that was clearly not a balloon or a conventional aircraft. The object was descending while transforming its shape as it did so. My photos show each distinct shape. There were no fireworks in the vicinity at the time I took the photos.

The last photos were taken just as the object was dropping below the tree line situated between it and my location. It seemed that the object was landing, however I cannot assure you that it actually landed as I lost sight of it due to the tree line. IF it landed I would estimate it came down near the junction of I-435 and 169 Highway. My camera (a Nikon Coolpix P7700) was pointed in a NE to North direction for all six photos.

The last photo, taken just before the object went below the tree-line on the object's surface reveals a... face...that looks quite sinister.
