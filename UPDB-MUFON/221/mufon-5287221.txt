2 pink disc shaped objects appeared over the atlantic ocean and then the light vanished.

on 3 occasions over a 30 minute period, 2 disc shaped objects with smaller circular pink circles around the exterior appeared over the atlantic ocean at approximately 10:30 pm. they just appeared out of nowhere and sat still. After 10 or 15 seconds, they vanished. Several minutes later, they appeared in the exact same position and pulsated for another 10 seconds before vanishing a second time. After 10 minutes, they reappeared in the exact same position for a final time before vanishing and not returning.
