{jpg} First Observation.

I'd like to tell this story because after 30 years, I keep hearing other stories from local people that have similar experiences. Also, I am less embarrassed about it now.

I was trying to sleep in my room, I was about 12 or 13. It was my dad's house which was pretty new or just built. We lived on a farm at a 1/2 mile off of the highway on a dead end road. My room window faced the west. I loved leaving my curtains open so I could see the stars before I fade off to sleep (then anyway). I was used to living in [profanity removed/CMS/jpg], until now, this was a nice new house.
I'm laying there in bed looking out at the stars, I know them well at this point, clear night. They never move much, until that night. I see one in the NW sky I've never seen before. It happens to be above a machinery shed that is just outside a little north of my window. I see the light (just thought it was a star) above the roof line. I duck down so that the roof line blocks my view of it, it rises above it. I thought, [profanity removed/CMS/jpg] that was weird, its just sitting there. So I did it again, and it did the same damn thing. At that point, I got out of bed and shut the curtains, and went to sleep.

Sometime later, I have no idea what time, I was asleep in my room, facing away from the window I had covered with curtains. I opened my eyes, and my room was bright as anything I have ever witnessed. So bright, every piece of furniture was transparent, even the closet doors, dresser, and mirror. Everything was the same color ([profanity removed/CMS/jpg] bright and transparent, it looked like an x-ray). As soon as I realized this I sat up in bed to turn toward the curtained window, and everything went black (and normal). I screamed for my dad a few doors down, ran to his room, and pounded on the door. I said "a car is in the yard shining lights in my room".

He got up, looked out his windows facing south (the direction of the only access road where a car might be) and saw nothing, then sondered to the gun room and got a shot gun, and we went outside. There was nothing. He thought I was crazy then and now. He did stay in my room the rest of the night and nothing else happened.

There was corn planted all around that house and about 10ft tall at the time. No tracks in the yard after we looked in the morning.

I do not know what happened to me, I hope nothing. I have no missing time, or anything of the sort.

I have 4 other observations after this one, but this was the first and most personal and frightening.
