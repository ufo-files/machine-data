{dr}Looked like fireball, sudden directional change light slowly faded off until we couldnt see it.

My neighbors and I were having a neighborhood party and were sitting around a fire pit. Around 8pm on September 29, 2012 my mom pointed out to me that it looked like a fire ball was coming towards us. My neighbor said it was probably a helicopter or something, but helicopters usually arent on fire and coming that fast. I originally thought it was some sort of meteorite, until this fireball suddenly changed directions and went back up into the sky. I have NEVER believed in legitimate UFOs until last night. This fire ball which was coming at a slight curve towards the North coming from the South in the western sky stopped and headed in a straight path towards the sky seeming like it was going away from earth. We all watched it fade off until this fireball couldnt be seen at all. I came back to my dorm room and found out my roommate had seen the same exact fireball object thing. All of us who saw it after could not believe that NO ONE took any proof. Itll just have to be something we all keep with us personally for the rest of our lives.
