{nd} A cluster of objects was not far from two objects that were close to one another in a straight line

I was in my yard lying on my chaise lounge. I looked up and saw this cluster of objects and further up in the sky were two tiny objects in a straight line not far from one another. I went and got my camera and came back and took a picture, but I wasn't sure I could catch it being so far away. I have no idea what these objects were, but I only looked away for a minute after taking the pictures and they had vanished. I thought the big cluster might be a satellite but the two lights off to its side I hadn't a clue. They never moved until they vanished.
