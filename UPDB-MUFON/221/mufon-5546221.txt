Fireballs moving through sky but they had no trails behind them.

Me,my wife and kid were driving back from a picinic when I noticed ten people standing out of saucon valley grill pointing in the sky. Figuring they were lookin @ fireworks so I looked up to. I saw no fireworks but I saw 3 orb like fireballs in a triangle shape in the sky. I was curious(naturally) so I drove faster to a nearby parking lot were I pulled over so we could get out to figure out what they were.once we pulled over we saw two more flying overhead. They were about the size of a quarter and there was also a plane in the sky(alittle higher than the orbs).they seemed to flicker alittle but had no trails behind them. we watched them go over the mountain and out of sight. I then called my mother who lives kinda in the direction they appeared to be traveling to tell her to look for them. around 11pm when I got home I acually called the bar that I saw the people looking into the sky to ask them if they saw them too. The bartender told me that they saw the same thing I saw and one hovering over the houses too. I've always wanted to see something like this but everytime I think I see something I can disclaim it quickly. Not the case with this sighting. Definately something I can not explain.
