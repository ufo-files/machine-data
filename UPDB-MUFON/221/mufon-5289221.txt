At a little before 2130 as I was walking from my shop to my house I noticed a star that was out of place. 300 percent brighter than anything in the sky. Crystal clear cold night. No clouds. Upon watching it and heading out into an open field I noticed it moving. Grabbed my buddy and made him come watch this thing with me. We watched for over and hour, and every time I step out to see it its there it is. Currently 2312. 

Moves irradically. Almost like looking into a petri dish and seeing an organism moving under a microscope. Looked very far away. Movement up, down, left, right. Very bright almost a headlight that I could see moving like a serpent in the sky.
