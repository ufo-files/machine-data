My fourth sighting.

This is my fourth sighting. They are becoming commonplace already. I am expecting them to drop in for tea. I had just gotten to work and parked the car. As I was getting my lunch from the car trunk I looked up ans saw the space station flying over Edmonton from SW to NE. I thought I would take a few moments and watch it go over. Just as it was over top of me I saw another dark object streaking from West to East,travelling pretty well in a straight line with a slight downward arc. Just as it passed under the space station it gave off a brilliant flash of white light, then went dark anddissapeared in the eastern sky. As I have seen these before I was not startled or alarmed. I just thought, "Oh! There is another one."
