Light traveling very fast

I was walking in elm park, essex, east london with friends about midnight, there was a huge lightening storm in the south east of england, i was recording the lightening in my camera phone, while recording i noticed something flash across the camera, when i played it back a light in the sky travelling at a fantastic speed just before a flash of lightening, it was heading south east, at firsf i thought it was a shooting star but ive seen those before and this object was travelling much much faster and didnt look like it had a trail as shooting stars have.
