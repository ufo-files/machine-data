9 black dots in the form of a triangle across the face of a full moon.

I was looking through my telescope at the moon and witnessed what looked like 9 dots in the form of a triangle or arrow head almost like birds fly.
with perfect spacing. they appeared black and moving very fast as the sighting was over in a few seconds. when i looked without the aid of my telescope i saw nothing. The night sky was completly clear. It looked like they were flying across the surface of the moon though i think they were much closer to the earth.
