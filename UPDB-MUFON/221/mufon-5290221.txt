Ruby shaped object flying very slowly

I saw this object when I was in 5th grade, around sunset time I leave the classroom to the trash can, I look up and see an object that floats very slowly with a very strange and solid shape like a smooth gray stone from the size of a small car. He called my teacher "Mirian" and my classmates began to lean out to see what I pointed out, many began to ask what it was and the teacher with doubt tells us "it will be a space ship and aliens invade us." After a while the boys get bored of looking at the object and the teacher sits down again, the object flew very slowly and went away towards twilight in a straight line without changing course or ascending or descending, it had a lot of contrast of shadows and lights by the shape it had with very fine edges I was the only one who stared at it until it was lost in the distance.
