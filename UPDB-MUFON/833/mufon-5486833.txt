We where coming home and noticed this silver disc in sky with lights

We were coming home. Looked up in the sky and noticed that silver disc in sky. It had two lights on the front of it. The lights went shining. It was moving from west side of city coming towards us. We both stared at it and the it turned to right so I could notice more of it's formation and light structure. The two lights look more like old round ones. Then all of sudden it disappeared. There were no clouds in the area.
