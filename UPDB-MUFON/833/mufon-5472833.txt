2. Noticed 3 bright white triangle shaped lights with one red flashing light in the center of the white lights in the night sky.
3. A UFO of some type. I dont know what it was, but, I know what it wasnt... a known aircraft. Never seen anything like it before and I was a jet engine mechanic in the U.S. Navy.
4. Cant describe it but I have a picture that I've drawn of it that I could send if requested. It DID NOT have vertical stabilizers that I could see. It had what looked like an exhaust in the back of it, but, the sound it omitted was very muffled and it was super quiet. If by chance we hadnt been out on the back deck, we would have never known that it cruised overhead. We are under a major flight corridor, but, this was no plane or part of the flight traffic that flies overhead!
5. I personally was in awe of what I was witnessing. My wife and friend was nonchalant about the sighting.
6. It simple "floated"/ flew off to the North.
