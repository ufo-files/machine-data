{dr}Family and I heard loud sound. Looked outside and saw a helicopter (?) following two orange orbs in the sky almost directly over our house. Observed the orbs until they no longer became visible.

At approximately 11:50 PM on December 8, 2017, my family and I heard a loud sound coming from outside. I looked out the window and my husband and I observed a helicopter (?) following two large orange glowing orbs traveling almost directly over our house in a northeastern direction across the city of Salem, Oregon. The helicopter was loud and had flashing lights, but the orange orbs were silent and had no flashing lights.

We observed the orbs travel over the city until they disappeared from view likely due to the distance they were from us.

I would have liked to have recorded it, but it was so neat to watch and happened quickly enough that I was afraid I would miss my most of it if I tried to pull out my phone to record.
