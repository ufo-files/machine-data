4-5 bright lights flying in a line. Wavy, xhanged direction fast. No sound. Disappeared behind clouds.

I was walking from mt home, going to the school bus. About 1 kilometer. It was cold, almost no clouds. I was looking up to the right, and I suddenly saw 4-5 flying objects. Flying in line, like they were connected. Flyed to the left and right. Changing direction fast, without any sound. I was about 10-12 years then, and I instantly knew what this was. 
I was pretty scary then, and still is. Cause there is absolutely no doubt what it was. It was not a plane, not a helicopter. Drones were science fiction back then.

The lights was very bright. I guess it saw me since it disappeared behind the clouds after 10-15 seconds. 
The moves were not normal for any man made flying aircraft. If I had seen this today when I am much older, it would scare the shit out of me!!!
