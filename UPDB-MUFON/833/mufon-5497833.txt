{rjl} Triangle shaped, 4 dim yellow rectangle lights going down

I took my dog out around 1:00 am on November 17, 2014. I heard what sounded like a slow helicopter propellar. I looked at the sky and didn't see anything, then I saw a very large dark triangle shaped object with four dim yellow lights going down each side of the wings (8 lights total). It was moving to the west in a slow and steady pace. It was low enough I could barely see the outline against the night sky. Even my dog stopped and looked up and watched it. We then went back inside and I thought I heard the same noise again (slow propellar sound)about 15 minutes later, but wasn't able to see anything. My dog was very agitated and paced for sometime before being able to settle down, which is unusual for her.
