{rjl} Observed two separate lights in unison hovering, then zigzag in perfect sync and distance several times then hover. This happened over 1/2 hour.

My finance and I were leaving to go hunting at four in the morning,loading up the truck,when I looked into the early morning sky. Looking for cloud cover. There was not a cloud in the sky.I noticed a particularly bright star,then the star moved in a zigzag pattern. Suddenly there was a second light that moved in perfect sync and distance. I called to my fiance to hurry and come see this. I showed him the "dancing lights ". He said, "those are UFO's". He said what I was thinking to myself. My heart was racing. The lights were flashing yellow.,then orange then green. They changed continuously,then they blinked off and on four times. Then went back to original flashes. I told my fiance that those are no landing lights I had ever seen. We observed the lights for several minutes, he attempted to video them with his phone. I was excited and terrified at the same time. After we started driving we could still see them hovering for a few miles, then lost track of them in the trees.
