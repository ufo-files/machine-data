{rjl} Noticed a tear drop shaped, blue/white object above the clouds flying from right to left, no sound, no con trail.

It was around 1445 on 7/29/2017. I was behind my home using my water hose to was out my home air filters. At the same time I noticed several wasp nest on eve's of my home. I started knocking them down with the water hose when I saw a tear shaped object. The sky was clear with just a few white clouds. I would have never knew the object was flying by if I had not been looking up toward the eve's of my home. The object had to be pretty large because it was high above the clouds as I lost sight of it as it flew above the few clouds that were above me. The object made no sound and left no con trail as it flew. The object was outlined in white that matched the color of the clouds. Inside the tear drop shape was a second white colored tear drop shape. This appeared to be part of the object. In between the first and second tear drop the objects color was the same as the sky.The whole sighting lasted less than a seven seconds. I have never seen anything like this before. I have seen plenty of aircraft from military aircraft, C17, C130's, F16's, and helicopters to civilian aircraft along with balloons and blimps. I did not have my phone so I could not take a picture. I did not have time to call my wife to come out to see the object as it was moving so fast. I got the impression the objects color was on purpose to help conceal it. A person would have to be looking right at the object to notice it at all.
