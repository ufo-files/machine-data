{cak} Orange Orb came from west observed at tree line and the turned south over lake and ascended upward into cloud and disappeared

I was putting my son from one room on main floor to upstairs bedroom. I was looking west and saw at the tree lines what looked like an orange orb. At first thought it was an orange Japanese lantern. Then when I went upstairs looked out another window looking west and it moved at a faster rate than what I had originally thought and realized that this wasnt a lantern. Put son in his bed and looked out another window over Lake Lansing looking southward. And it went moving at a very fast motion and went upward into cloud and disappeared out of my sight.
