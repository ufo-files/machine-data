{cak} Saw at least 13 lights/objects in SE sky

I was walking my dog at on April 16, 2020 at 4:20am, saw the Moon, Mars, Saturn, and Jupiter all lined up. Began to star gaze, began to see lights VERY high up in the sky just to the right and above Jupiter. They just looked like a tiny light. I figured it was a satellite passing by, I’ve seen satellites pass over head before and that’s the best way I can describe what they looked like. I pulled up my star gazing app on my phone and it showed a satellite passing in that area. BUT, once that passed I kept seeing lights appearing from the same point in the sky, they followed the same path, each one was visible for about 30 secs I’d say. Living in Vegas you see a lot planes fly over head, there are tons of flight paths over Vegas. These weren’t on any flight path I’ve seen. The lights would appear way to quickly to be planes following a path. I observed this for 15 mins. Counted at least 15. Commercial planes don’t fly that close. It wasn’t planes. I know the Lyrid meteor shower is happening now, but these were not meteors. They were moving way to slow and they all followed the same path and appeared in the same area of the sky. I’m at a loss for what I just witnessed. I’ve never seen anything like that before. I tried to take a video but all I had was my phone and the lights were way to faint and high up the sky for it to catch anything.
