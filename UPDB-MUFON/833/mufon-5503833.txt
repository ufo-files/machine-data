{dr}I was flying a airplane at 21,000 feet from NJ to MI. A huge dark object flew straight over our heads and blocked out stars

I was co pilot in a 421 Cessna. The pilot and I were flying from Teterborough New Jersey to Pontiac Michigan and a Giant Black object flew right over us in the opposite direction. The pilot and I both witnessed it and were frozen in Awe. We saw the object for roughly five minutes as it passed over us.. It was a cloudless night and the object blocked the Stars from our view. After discussing it with each other we contacted the FAA center we were communicating with. We asked if we just had a near mis with another aircraft. They came back to say they do have an object on radar but no transponder report. We told them it just flew within 500 feet of us and scared us. They asked if we wanted to file a UFO report. We both looked at each other and said NO. I just wanted you to have a report on file due to the fact I just saw a show about PA residents seeing them in there area. One mans description of the craft and the lightning making it visible reminded me of it. I thought I should share the fact we seen it too really high in the air! The Pilot and I have talked about it several times over the years and I felt maybe it could help with something by letting you know how high we seen it. As the woman said on the show I was a non believer believer now I KNOW for a FACT they are real now. I do feel the FAA knows also but don't want any problems by being a witness to what they already know exist. I have NO reason to make this up. I just want to verify the facts that it was Huge and flying to slow to stay in the air as far as normal flying methods. Also it made NO sound.
