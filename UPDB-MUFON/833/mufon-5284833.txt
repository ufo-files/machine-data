Sitting on porch after dinner. Fellow resident did not see anything.

I was talking on the front porch with a fellow resident in Kent CT after dinner. I live in a retirement home. I looked up and saw a small silver shaped UFO moving south toward Danbury CT. It was moving at a high rate of speed. No sound. Ascended into a cloud. Thought it was gone, but it come out of the cloud. Suddenly it blended in with the sky and was gone. It really unsettled me. The resident could not see it. No sound. No contrails.
