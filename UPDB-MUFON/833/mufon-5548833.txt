2 star like objects. Twinkling brighter and faster than stars. doing barely noticable loops. they appeared to be bouncing off of the atmosphere.

At about 12am my boyfriend called me outside because he saw a star like object that didn't sem quite right to him. It appeared to have a tail and was moving in a small loop. It was brighter than a star and had a reddish tint to it, sometimes blue.. It appeared to be bouncing off of the atmosphere, we thought it could be a comet or something, possibly headed into the atmoshere. another thought was that it could be an alien-craft or perhaps an ICBM missile. About 45 min. later I spotted a second one. it was moving much slower but doing the same thing..
