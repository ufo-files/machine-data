{cak} Looked up to see a bright, greenish-white light/orb move across the sky, leaving a trail and disappearing in the clouds. Very strange.

I was stopped at a red light at the intersection, and looked up to see a bright, greenish-white orb of light move across the sky. It appeared to leave a white trail, maybe a fireball. It didn't look that far up in the sky, pretty close actually. After 3 or 4 seconds it just disappeared in the clouds. I was thinking it was a meteor, or space debris entering the atmosphere or something, but part of me feels it was extraterrestrial.
