Oval or round object

While taking movie photos of Mt. St. Hellen a streaking light observed from bottom right heading at 45 degree angle upwards . At time we were at the new observation area located facing St.Hellen. We were not aware of object until recently viewed movie (cell phone). That I slowed down image, made it larger in computer and saw what it was. Either a round or cylinder white object streaking towards the sky. That day there were no clouds to be seen, yet the object was surrounded by a small misty cloud. Did not see nor on the photo any trail of smoke or engine trail Have video but in wife’s cell phone. Will attempt to send..
