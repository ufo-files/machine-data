Pulsing Light in Western Sky between Cor Caroli and Alkaid

Tonight at 10:21pm CST my mother and I witnessed a stationary pulsating light in the western sky. This light would flash on slowly and blink out slowly several times over the course of 2-3 minutes before disappearing entirely. It would flash RED in color. The light was stationed mid way between Cor Caroli and Alkaid, almost as if you lined up Phecda and Merak of the Ursula Major constellation and stopped at the midpoint between Cor Caroli and Alkaid. I first noticed it trying to track satellites. We have very little light pollution in my area, which makes sky-gazing pretty fun. I have 20/20 vision thanks to LASIK surgery a few years ago. I also hold a MS in Geosciences from a top-5 research institution, so I have working knowledge of space-faring objects. The light would pulse red, almost like a light on a radio tower, then pulse out. It did this a dozen times. I ran inside to get my phone in an attempt to record it, but 30 seconds later it was gone. I was so shocked by what it was that I asked my mother to come out and take a look. I asked what she thought. She initially said "satellite" but told her that this was not moving. She confirmed and said, "UFO, I guess." It was maybe a little bigger than your average star in diameter, but was pretty bright. It was NOT a plane. A plane did track by just to the south of where this object was situated a few minutes later. That was flight "JetBlue 71" which flew just south of me at the time that the pulse appeared. JetBlue 71 was flying JFK to SLC. It would be interesting to know if anyone onboard noticed it.
