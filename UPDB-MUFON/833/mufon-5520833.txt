Long, quiet, hovered stationary sometimes ascending and descending

I went out to put something in the mail box when I saw a bright white light in the sky above my neighbors house off in the distance. I looked at it for a second and it changed to a bright orange color and started to pulsate. I went inside to get my mom and her boyfriend and they both saw it for at least 5 minutes then when back inside. I continued to look at the object taking several pictures on my cell phone. It wouldnt stay in one spot, but hover around some, ascending and descending and pulsating for at least 30 mins. A few times it completely flickered off and back on again. After about 30-45 minutes it went below my field of vision behind my neighbors house. When I looked at the pictures I had taken, I noticed, besides the UFO, another green light higher up in the sky, which was not visible to the naked eye but showed up in the pictures.
