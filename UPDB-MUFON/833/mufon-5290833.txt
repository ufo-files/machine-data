Soccer ball shaped with Blue/Red sequential flashing {filled the window view} - {djs}

Thinking way back on the event, when I was about 3 years old or 1947, possible on my way to bathroom, in middle of night, finding myself fixed, standing and starring at a bright/brilliant, soccer ball shaped round object, flashing with back and forth sequential red to blue in colors, with its separate/individual sections, of a soccer ball, not sure its distance from me, yet seemed to be possibly close, since it nearly filling the window view, looking at it through a 2' hex shaped window, 8' high from the floor, and about 8' from me. Unable to forget the sighting/ moment, thinking back on it often! Not sure how long the event lasted, seemed to last for some time, never remember seeing it leave, or my walking away, was I possible being observed and my thoughts erased while starring unable to look away? Wondering/mystified myself why I can't remember anything before/after the initial sighting! Photo taken from a soccer ball website to show something close to my description of the object, but what I saw was in much more of a crystal clear, and very brilliant glowing colors!
