{dr}Bright glowing orange/yellow lights hovering and seen descending, moving and disappearing

I was driving my daughter and her boyfriend when we noticed a large glowing light hovering above the foothills between Canyon Lake and Lake Elsinore..at first we thought it was a helicopter or flare but then realized it was to big and bright and it was changing direction including descending behind the hill and then reappearing..my daughter then saw 3 more objects appear in a diagonal shape and disappear ..I then saw a second object, same color - bright orange/yellow but it quickly disappeared. One remained hovering for about 10 minutes..I drove in the direction of the object to get a better look but then we lost sight of it, not sure if it disappeared or just descended..I turned back around and it was just gone..no sight of it ascending or flying away..a black helicopter then flew over to where it was hovering..at home we searched online for any eyewitness reports and found a post by a couple from orange county who filmed exactly what we saw on the same night (6/18/14)..orange county is about 40 minutes away. We were all pretty amazed at what we saw because this time it was at night when in the recent past we've only seen ufos out here during the day which are hard to see.
