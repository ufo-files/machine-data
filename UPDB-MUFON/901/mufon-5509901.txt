[flg] Dimmed in and out in a series of 10 minutes, changed directions.

We were leaving my moms home and my husband looked to the east to see a white light, that then disappeared. I told him it was a street lamp or an airplane, since theres always a lot of airplane activity. We took of driving east and the object seemed to have disappeared. Driving down a main street I looked up and the UFO appeared because it lit up again then dimmed out. 10 seconds later it appeared again in a different area, moved much to fast to be a man made object. I was terrified and wanted to cry. We lost track after we starting heading north again.
