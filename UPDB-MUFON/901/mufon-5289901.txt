Slow moving, silent, faint light V formation at cloud level in St. Louis City

I just went to my backyard to let my dog in about an hour ago and happened to look up and see a slow moving, very faint V formation of lights (between 5-7) pass overhead at cloud level. It’s very cloudy outside and the lights were very dim and subtle. If I hadn’t been looking up I would not have even noticed it because the lights were so faint and possibly obscured by the clouds. I couldn’t make out any defined edges but it was silent and moved overhead from east to west in a straight line at a consistent speed (est. 40 mph). The whole thing lasted about 15 seconds and I watched it until I lost sight of it behind some trees after it passed over. Not exactly sure of the size but it looked about the size of a tennis ball held at arms length overhead just at or below cloud level. Very interesting, cool experience. Never really seen anything quite like it.
