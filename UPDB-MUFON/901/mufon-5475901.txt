{cak} Triangle type aircraft flying over billboard lights all around edges. Followed for short time until it took off at a rapid speed

I was riding with a friend in his moms van. When we both glanced up and saw lights over the billboard in our friends property. I thought at first it was just new lights on the billboard. But it began to move at a very slow speed. We started to follow the vehicle and as it turned left we did as well. Still driving down a street we noticed that the object was quite large and shaped like a triangle. It had lights all around the edges. I could only make out the difference in darkness between the night sky and the object/craft because of the lights. Then not long after we headed down the street it began to speed up slowly. Then as if it was in a sling shot or fired out a gun it took off and was gone.
