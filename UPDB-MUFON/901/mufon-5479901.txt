{dr}Light with a an abnormally fast linear descent before glowing brighter and then going out completely.

Taking a break from some writing, I came outside my front porch to do some stargazing with my app and ended up taking a phone call. While on the phone around 12:10AM I saw a bright light appear very high up in the sky and at a distance. I'd say it was up as high as the stars. 

It passed in front of the stars and was quite large. It then descended in a line at a very rapid speed to maybe 30 or so degrees above the horizon, glowed brighter for a quick moment, and then it was gone like a light switch was turned off. 

Upon noticing it, I gasped and mentioned what I had seen on the phone. I hung up and immediately started looking around for meteor showers or any planes/helicopters in the area. After 20 minutes, I saw nothing.
