Strange object leaves bright trail in sky near military base

On December 20, 2019 at around 6:40 am I was driving on Fort Stewart military base on GA Hwy Route 144 East toward Richmond Hill, GA. The sky was completely dark outside with no clouds in the sky. I saw on bright light streaking across the sky leaving a very bright white trail across the dark sky. My car camera captured the object as if was traveling high in the sky leaving a long trail of light. The object suddenly seem to disconnect from the light trail, flash or blink in the sky then disappear into the sky darkness. I was able to track the object on my car dashboard camera for around 3 1/2 minutes.
