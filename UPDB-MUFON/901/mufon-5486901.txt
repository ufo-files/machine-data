{rjl} Orange lights in the sky

My son and I were driving south on Rt 95. All off a sudden traffic slowed down. My son asked me "what is that??" I looked out my window and saw something I have never seen before. Orange lights, in the formation of the Big Dipper. It then changed formation quickly. The lights broke off into a few other patterns, and I saw a bunch of the lights consolidate into one. They then went to a v-formation and disappeared immediately.
