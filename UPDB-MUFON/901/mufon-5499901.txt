Driving south on Bink,s Forest Dr. @ approx 10;15 pm 8-16-14 I observed what I thought were two lights coming towards from a plane (one on each wing tip) but they were the same color (yellowish red and orangeish) when i realized what I was possibly seeing,I immediately stopped the car in the middle left turn lane (my wife was with me and we both saw them) I jumped out of the car with my cell phone turned on the camera, my wife was looking out the passenger window(it was rolled down) they were almost directly overhead.The third light was trailing the front 2 (looked identical) the front 2 came together as 1 unit and turn to head west and third light was a little slower then it also turned to head west. The pictures I took show the lights and as usual not great detail, the 2 coming together with a beam joining them. The 3rd one is round, but has a more pronounced glow on the right(its left) side the whole experience lasted from 5-6 mins. There was no sound or any noise, the sky was clear and no moon.The pictures (in my opinion) are better on my cell phone-we down loaded them to my computer
