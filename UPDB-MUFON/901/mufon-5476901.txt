I was out for my nightly walk through the neighborhood at 9:30 pm. I saw three bright bluish white lights moving south to north and passing directly overhead. The lights moved steadily to the north, but as they did so they swarmed around each other like bees, and randomly pulsated. The neighborhood was very quiet with no traffic. The lights were totally silent, making absolutely no sound. I ran to my house and alerted my wife. We both stood on the driveway and watched the lights slowly fly off to the north. The lights continued to pulsate randomly. The night sky was clear with no clouds. Another neighbor also witnessed the event.
