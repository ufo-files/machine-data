{rjl} A bright light appeared north in the big dipper. A 2nd light to the northeast pulsed brightly. The 1st light then moved to the 2nd

It was about an hour past sunset. I went out to my truck to grab my wallet and on my way back into the house I looked up at the big dipper. I often look for the big dipper for navigation at times and in general just to observe its seasonal location as it rotates around the north pole. On clear nights I will briefly observe the night sky as the area I live offers a near horizon to horizon view. Right in the middle of the big dipper was a bright light. It was obviously out of place from what I would normally see. The light was stationary for about 20 seconds. Then to the northeast a second light caught my attention as it flashed very brightly toward the southwest. At this time the 1st object began to slowly move in the direction of the 2nd object. When I looked back toward the northeast I could no longer see the 2nd object. Once the first object reached the general area where the 2nd object flashed it disappeared from my view. I attempted to take video of the moving light, using another house as a reference point so the movement was visible. For some reason both videos cut out only a few seconds in.
