{rjl} brighter than usual plane appears to drop small object, object falls for short time and shoots off in opposite direction.

I was driving home from work when I noticed a very bright light in the sky. The light appeared much brighter than a typical light on an airplane, but after the light moved, and I had driven down the highway a little bit, I could tell it was just a plane. Then I noticed a small object that appeared to fall from the airplane for a short time, and after a few seconds that object abruptly flipped 180 degrees and shot off into the distance. The object appeared to glow as well, and was considerablly smaller than the plane. I admit it could have been a number of different things, but it just looked very weird. I lost sight of the object in a second or two, it had moved so far away, and so fast that it was almost gone in an instant.
