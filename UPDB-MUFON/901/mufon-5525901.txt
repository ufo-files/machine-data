Unexplained object captured in sunset

I was driving home from my boyfriend's house and the sky was a pretty pink color and it was close to sunset and so I took out my cellphone and took a picture of the horizon. Later as I looked at the picture, I realized there was an object in the upper right corner that I did not notice when I took the picture and so I zoomed the object in and could not come to any explanation as to what it might be.
