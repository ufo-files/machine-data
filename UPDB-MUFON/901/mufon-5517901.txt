{nd} Star-like object appears to turn into a bright fireball, descends and disappears

At approximately 1:45am last night/this morning (June 24th, I was taking my dogs outside for the last time and when I walked up onto my deck to come inside, I looked up at the sky (Northerly direction) and saw what I thought was a star. As I am used to seeing odd things in the sky I always watch the sky and as soon as I focused on this star it moved. it seemed to be letting me know it saw me too. It moved downward without blinking or any sound and suddenly changed into a very bright fireball-like object. This was definately not a shooting star or anything Ive ever seen before. It seemed to explode silently and begin descending very fast and before it reached the treeline it just disappeared. This sighting happened in less than 10 seconds Id say. This makes the 6th unexplained sighting since last summer.
