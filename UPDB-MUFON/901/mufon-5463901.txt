Black triangle spotted on summer early evening, stood still for a long time.

At the time of the event, I was outside in my backyard with my toddler son. We lived behind a big park that was very open, and I tend to watch the skies often. I happened to notice the object in the sky, which appeared like a black triangle. It was weird because it didn't move, just hovered there. There wasn't a cloud in the sky at the time. I asked my mom if she could see it and she did as well. Honestly I thought it was either a UFO or military. Since I have never seen anything like that in my life before, I went inside to get my camera to photograph it. I took the photo which I am submitting, which you can see it although it is far off. It never moved, never blinked or flashed lights. It was very far away from me and high up.
Other than seeing it, nothing happened. I eventually got bored of watching it and went about my business. Later when I checked it was gone. I later became annoyed that I had no answers to what I just witnessed. I also found it strange that nothing was reported on the news.
