{dr}6-8 Spheres flying erratically at Grand Prairie/Dallas border. Daylight. 1-23-13 Appeared to be above D.N.A.S.

In the backyard at 4pm lying in the grass enjoying a beautiful sunshiny day. I just opened my eyes and they were there. They appeared to be over the vacinity of D.N.A.S./ Henslee field. I immediately recognized them as sphere-type ufos. (I saw one once before in daylight in this same area in 87 or 88. That one was closer and the C-130s stopped their touch-n-goes until it passed.) I observed 6-8 Spheres flying erratically. They just looped around individually with no apparent destination. Kinda like a swarm of gnats. Observed over a minute. They gathered and ascended toward a particular point and disappeared. Then a straggler came from the south and rose to the same point and disappeared too. I don't know if they actually "disappeared" or just ascended out of sight very quickly. I suspect the latter. Videotaped on iphone but they were too faint to appear on camera. I just felt surprised and excited.
