{cak} Bright light at south end of Perrin AFB going N. stops overhead then goes N. over Base.

Around 1972-3 two other boys and I went to the south end of Perrin AFB not on base. There was a hill we and other friends would go to drink beer and watch planes land. My father was a fighter pilot who was a commander of a fighter squadron at Perrin 1963 to 1965 about. He retired 1969 to Denison. One of the other boys father was air force also. We just drove to top of the hill trees to the south open to north with clear view of runway north. Bright light comes N over trees we start hollering and waveing acting stupid. The light stopped about directly overhead. The light was so bright we I could not see outline of anything. We realized that there was no sound and no wind and we got scared. I got in car with friend other guy got in his and we hauled ass down the hill on to the blacktop and headed west with our lights off. I looked back and could see the light start to head N over base at slow speed but looked like light went out before it went over base. It was about 10 years after that night I remembered and brought it up to my friend I was with. The other person I didn't ever talk with. I bring the story to you before it gets never told. I ask my father if he ever saw UFO while flying he said he had when we lived in WA State. another story.
