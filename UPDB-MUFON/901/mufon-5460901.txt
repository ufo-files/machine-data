My husband and I were sitting outside enjoying a beautiful sunset and view from our cabin. I noticed 3 glowing round objects above the hills over Lake Roosevelt in WA

I was silent just watching thinking it was just the sun playing tricks on my mind. I kept closing my eyes and reopening them only to find the objects were still there.
Then I watched one of them (the largest) slowly disappear into itself. 
Almost like it was sucked into the air...leaving 2 remaining perfect round orbs.
That’s when I mentioned to my husband what I saw. 
He then said:
“I see them too...they look like small moons”
We both saw the 2 remaining orbs hover for a few minutes before they both just disappeared. Leaving no trace.
