My wife and I were driving north on Rt 29 from Silver Spring at about 9 PM on Christmas Eve. My wife was driving. We just reached the intersection with Rt 198 when I noticed a bright white light suddenly appear in the sky. We were heading in a North Easterly direction. Then a white but translucent light emanated from the white light for about 2 seconds and then the light moved rapidly and disappeared from sight. I joking said to my wife I just saw Santa. She asked what I was talking about and I explained what I just saw. I described what I observed without ascribing a reference to a UFO. The experience was somewhat surreal. We discussed the event. My wife is familiar with constellations and planetary movements and could not connect my observation with anything she knew. I have seen other UFOs, mostly hovering lights that then move rapidly but I have never seen anything like this episode. The most striking aspect was the "trail" that appeared before the rapid acceleration. The trail was not like a vapor trail and became almost triangular in shape before the object moved and disappeared. The object moved in a southerly direction for an instance before disappearing.
