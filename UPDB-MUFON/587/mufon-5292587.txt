Bright light hovering & silent for several minutes over the course of 3-4 days on subsequent nights.

The light was first observed hovering silently just East of Anchorage on the early morning of July 31st. Recorded for 15 minutes & observes w/naked eye for over an hour. Then sighted again early on August 4th in the same location. It moved to the South before it was observed again hovering at about the same elevation for several minutes. Eventually the light faded away & approximately 10-15 minutes later a bright light was observed flying directly over head before fading away again. The following day about 24 hrs later the, the light as again observed in the same location, just to the Sourh of Anchorage, AK. Simultaneously, another weaker/smaller light was observed in the Eastern sky at about the same elevation as previously viewed. The lights appear to hover motionless, but do appear to drift ever so slightly to the upper right or upper left as time goes on.
