Oval object seen partially through clouds lit with spotlights

On my way to work I thought the moon was strange before realizing it wasn't the moon. It was something else very large and oval extremely still not above or below the clouds, it was in the cloud level so I could clearly see it was there, only about 35% of it I could see bare. There were about three to 4 lights on the outside of it that gave off a lot of light enough for me to at first assume it was the moon.
Work is 10 minutes from my home, as I made each turn I was able to spot it still there and planned to get a better look once I reached my building. Once I got there and stepped out of my truck it was nowhere to be seen even though my building would have been a perfect position to see clearly.
I can't say exactly how far away it was because it was so dark, but my best guess is it would have had to be over finley .. Just beyond my workplace on Washington street in kennewick WA.
I've never seen a UFO before and remain a skeptic, however after much thought I'd like to report I saw a very large oval object hovering at a pretty low cloud cover level and was lit with 3 to 4 lights, made no sound that I know of And disappeared too quickly for me to see anything.
Once I got into my building there were staffs walkie talkies out of battery life. 
My building had no internet connection for atleast 30 minutes.
My phones been abnormally low of battery and I've been very tired and sleeping longer than usual.
