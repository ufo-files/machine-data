{cak} pulsing red and yellow light at high speed, vanishing instantly

While walking my dog I looked up at the stars and observed a slow pulsing red and yellow light moving from west to east at an unusually high rate of speed. it traveled about half way across the sky then instantly vanished. There were airplanes in the area and it looked nothing like any of those lights. I watched the sky for a short period to see if it would reappear. It did not.
