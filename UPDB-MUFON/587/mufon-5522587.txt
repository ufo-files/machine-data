It was about 11 at night. My best friend and I were riding on the highway in his truck just going south of ________ on highway __. The highway is two lane, rural, almost completely flat, and is surrounded by cornfields. We were going to go down to _______ to visit an abandoned house to see if we could witness anymore orbs as previously done there before. My friends had witnessed triangular craft fly right over them at this house previously also, so we had suspicions of what we might expect. It was nearly completely dark this night because of the moonless winter night. We had just departed from the outskirts of __________ when it rode up right next to us on the highway. It was 5 feet away from the drivers side of the truck, and was about 25 feet long.  It was only about 10 to 12 feet high with steep tetrahedral edges and it took up the entire other half of the road as it road next to us. It was completely silent keeping in stride with us on the highway for about a half mile. We just sat there completely silent smoking cigarettes as it watched us. For the half mile that it rode by us, its bottom was at about tire height to the truck so we could not see the undercarriage of it until if pulled away from us departing into a cornfield while raising its side nearest to us. The undercarriage had 3 small "orbs" located by its tips of the triangular bottom, and had on large "orb" centered in the middle of the craft. The "orbs" were spherical and emitted no color other than the small amounts of ionized oxygen and nitrogen. We were not afraid at all, surprisingly, but the radio that was quietly on in the car received only static while the craft was near the car. We now have reason to suspect that the beings inside the craft had/have something to do with the abandoned house because of the previous occurrences there(which i was not a part of), but that is purely based on speculation.
