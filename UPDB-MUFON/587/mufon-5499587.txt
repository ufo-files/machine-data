On Tuesday August 12 ,2014 at 05:45 My wife and I went to let the dog out. Once outside we immediately saw this bright light approx. one mile from our house and just above the tree tops drifting along in a northerly direction. The light was very bright in the center of the object with a white flashing light out on each side. There were no colored lights at all just white. The large center light was round and approx. 1/3 the distance from outer lights;I would estimate around 30 feet or so. The objects shape could not be seen. It was black. no other lights were seen. From where we were I would say the object was approx. 100 feet wide between the outer white lights maybe more. There was no noise at all it was perfectly quiet just drifting along. We observed it for less than 2 min. as it continued its northerly direction. It went over the trees where we could no longer see it. We have a lot of airplanes fly over our area and at times several military jets (none lately)so we know the difference between what we saw and commercial and private aircraft. This was not one of them. This was not landing lights or lights under such aircraft. There was no way a person could not notice this object. It was not light out yet. I can not say how high the object was flying but it was low.
