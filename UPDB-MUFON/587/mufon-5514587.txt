{dr}2 bright planet-like orbs, SE of full moon. 1 faded out, then 2 did same

I was walking my dog approx 05:30 MDT and was looking at the full moon, when I observed 2 bright white, planet-like objects located in the SE portion of the sky, near the moon.

The two objects appeared similar to the brightness of Jupiter-Venus during last summers alignment. They did not flicker and remained in a fixed location in the sky.

While I observed them, the object on the left faded out and moments later, the one on the right did the same.
