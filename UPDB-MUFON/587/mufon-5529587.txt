Flashing square

I was on my way to the movies, and I decided to take a back road because the movie theater was about an hour and a half away. There was no one else on the road at all. All of the sudden, a bright flashing square came out of no where. I pulled over and watched it for about 30 minutes. It eventually came down to about 20 feet from the ground. It changed colors rapidly. It zig-zagged left to right for a long time. Sort of putting on a show for me. It then zoomed off faster than anything I have ever seen. This was most certainly not a plane. It was very strange.
