{nd} we noticed an object hovering in the sky and decided to take pictures

we was standing in our driveway saying goodbye to my cousins when my cousin said "what is that in the sky?" we all looked and saw a balloon like object just hovering. It was at that point when my husband decided to go get his camera. Upon his return the object had moved to the east and these are the pictures he captured. We still are unsure of what we might have seen, as balloons do not hover. It moved at a rapid speed across the sky and then seemed to hover again in the east. Then the object disappeared.
