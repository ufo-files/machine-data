The UFO hovered over Raytown water tower then flew away.

I was on Raytown Road and 63rd Street sitting at a red light. I was heading to a friend's house. I noticed the object because a lady in the car next to me had out a video camera and was pointing it at the sky. When I first saw the object I knew it was a UFO because I have never seen anything like that before. I am familiar with commercial airplanes and military airplanes and this was neither. The object was hovering over the Raytown Water Tower. It hovered for five seconds from when I first saw it, then it rose up ten feet flipped over and slowly flew down Raytown Road toward the west. I chased after it and all of a sudden it emitted a black contrail and increased speed dramatically and disappeared. I thought it was the craziest thing I have ever seen and didn't think anyone would believe me. A friend of my sister posted on facebook that her son had spotted a UFO in the Lee's Summit Mo area. After I spotted the UFO I called KMBC news and they reported four or five spottings of the same aircraft.
