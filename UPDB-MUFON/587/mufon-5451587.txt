Two diamond shaped objects flying NE

I was in my backyard when I heard a hum. I looked up and eventually located a diamond shaped object flying overhead. It's hard to calculate the elevation but it was either very high or very tiny. At first I thought it. was a balloon and it appeared to tumble as light reflected off of it. The flashes were regular as if it was rotating. It traveled in a straight line and eventually disappeared from view in the distance. Less than five minutes later a second object, identical to the first, except it flashed red, followed in exactly the same flight path. I watched this one until it was out of sight.
