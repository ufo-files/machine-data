It started at 9:19pm eastern time above the ridge facing north from Red River Gorge Campground

Over 10 witnesses saw this event, which lasted from 9:19pm eastern to 6:43am. Numerous objects doing patterns and accelerating to speeds that are unheard of in my experience of growing up on AFB's and having also served in the AF. The scariest part was around 2:30 when one of the objects came over the ridge after punching through some cloud cover and shined a blinding bright beam straight down into the forest. I have contact info of the people that joined in on the viewing. We all tried taking video and it all came back black, although I was able to video a lightning bug at the same time. I had three field compasses that spun and couldn't get true north readings from 4:38pm until we were well out of the area.
