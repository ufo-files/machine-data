{cak} Light seen overhead, moving very fast

I was on the roof of an upper manhattan building with friends at about 2 AM on a summer evening sitting down and facing west. At one point a friend and I both looked up and saw a bright white light moving overhead towards New Jersey. It slowed down a bit halfway over the horizon and abruptly shifted towards the right. It shifted again toward New Jersey and sped off at incredible speed over the horizon. I have never seen anything move that fast. It was obviously very high in the sky, emitting its own light. It could not have been a conventional aircraft, as it shifted and moved west faster than should be possible.
