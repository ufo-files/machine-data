{dr}10 orange lights in northern sky

My wife and I were traveling east on M-59, having left from Utica, MI at 10:15 on July 28, 2012. A few miles before I-94 we observed a group of orange lights in the northern sky. The lights were arranged in two rows and were moving very slowly south. After about two minutes they formed into a single row and moved quickly south and out of sight. People in the car in the next lane also slowed down and were looking in the same direction as us.
