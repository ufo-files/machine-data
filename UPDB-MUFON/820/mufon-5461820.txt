{rjl} large object was floating above neighbors house at treetop level.

My son and I were driving home at 7 PM, it was very dark. We were nearing home and as we were passing the neighbor's house I noticed what looked like a new house on the hill behind the neighbor's house. I slowed to get a better view and I could see that it was not a house but some kind of large object that looked just like the front of some average house, no roof, just the front part. The object was hovering above the neighbor's house below the tops of the trees. It was about 4-5 times bigger than the house. When we drove past the object it just faded to black and and was gone.
