I was at my grandmothers house watching TV with my grandmother brother and wife and new born daughter.  The show we were watching on TV, (Before cable, just a TV antenna) the TV went to grey snow. My brother went out to see the stars. He came running in to say a UFO was outside. I came out and saw the UFO. I stared at it. I was in the USAF and I thought it might be some new type of quiet helicopter. Four of them. Three making a triangle and one in the middle. The UFO was making a small hissing noise. I was too scared to go take a photo. I went back into the house to tell my wife what I saw.  The TV went back on and I went outside and didn't see the UFO anymore. I haven't told many people about what I said because they would think I was joking or crazy. There is not very many days I don't think about this night.  Things would be different now. I am constantly looking in sky for another view. My friend also saw a triangle shadow in the night sky. It covered the stars.  That is why I am reporting. I know this is the truth.
