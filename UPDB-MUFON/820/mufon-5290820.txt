20+ lights traveling S/W to N/E in straight line.

While sitting on my deck at 5:45 pm, facing S/W, I noticed a light (similar to a star) coming from the S/W. That light was followed by another, then another, and so on. They were moving at a low speed. I thought of jets, planes, helicopters--- but there was no noise, and the lights just kept coming. I did not actually count every one, but the line of lights was like a sting of pearls. When the lead light passed me heading into the N/E sky, it faded out. Then the next one, and so on. The lights continued coming from the S/W. After about 4-5 minutes, the lights stopped coming from the S/W, continuing to fade out in the N/e sky. Minutes later, our (I live in a condo complex) power went out, lasting about 1 minute, then coming back on.
