Roundish orange/yellow silent object traveling fairly quick parallel to ground

My friend and I standing/sitting on the stairs to her house…noticed a roundish orange/yellow object….not at fire ball, not a rocket (no tail/trail) I live on the space coast and have seen hundreds of launches so I’m familiar with rocket launches….no sound, smooth travel, not an airplane, no flashing lights of any kind, going fairly quickly….I had enough time to take a picture, ration in my head what it could be, then decided to run up the stairs in and through the house to deck on the opposite side of the house…by then (unlocking and UN dogging (bungee cord) the door…it was gone. I did this because we were about to lose sight of it going behind the house. As soon as I realized it was gone and while going back out to the other side of the house where my friend stayed, I opened up my FlightAware App….there were NO aircraft seen in the area.
