{rjl} Large white hot ball. Turned to colors red green & blue then disappeared, came back then left to S.E. making no noise.

A friend and I were traveling south on 574N. in 1972 sometime in the month of June,about 5 or 6 miles north of Ruskin FL. when my friend asked me to look out his window and tell him what I thought the object he was looking at was. I appeared to be a very large rectangular object with no shape that we could see, but the lights were in a line. It looked to be about 300 to 500 feet off the ground off to the east. The object stayed with us while we were running about 60 MPH and as we got faster it stayed in the same spot, looking through the passenger side window so we slowed down to around 45 MPH and we could still see the object in the same spot as it moved with us. After about 5 minutes or so the object turned to a big ball that looked like white hot lava and left so fast all you could see was a streak that followed it and we thought it was gone and started to talk about what we were going to tell everybody and decided on not saying anything. About that time it came back to the same spot, looking through the window that it was in before it left the first time and turned to the three colors and followed us for about 5 or 6 more miles, just before we got to Tampa city limits and turned back to the light ball and left for good.Ruskin FL. is about 27 miles south of Tampa FL.
