{dr}Two red lights

While we were setting off fireworks just after the sun went below the horizon my kids noticed two red lights in the sky. I looked to where they were pointing. I thougth I was going to see an airplane, but there were two solid red lights that seemed to pulsate as they were moving slowly. They seemed to creep. I tried to take a picture of them, but they started to disappear then. I got one red dot in one of my pictures that you can barely see.
