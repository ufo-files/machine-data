glowing orange balls of light with bright white centers

Driving home from work, going south on my street I observed 2 bright glowing orange lights with brilliant white centers crossing west to east across the sky going very slowly. I stopped in front of my house and watched them move east and curve south west. The first one sped up and the light disappeared. The second was viewable for a longer time even though facing away from me. I backed into my driveway, got out of the car and saw 2 more coming. My dad was in the doorway watching for me. I yelled at him to come out. We watched those 2 lights follow the same path. Slower than the first 2. When I realized there were 2 more coming I ran into the house to grab my camera. ( I own a flip phone). When I came back outside the 3d pair were crossing the street. They were very hard for me to focus on. I saw one more coming and tried for it. I got shaky video of that 7th light but some of it may be clear enough if you can freeze frame it. You can see the very bright white light at the center and the big orange glow around that. There was a Bright white light I could hear was a helicopter coming then and I got film of that too for reference. I could hear the helicopter but nothing from the lights. The film is too long to upload and I don't know how to edit it. If I learn or get help I can send it to you.
