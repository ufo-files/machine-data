Silver Object Flew Above I-95 S. Near Walpole

I was driving on I-95 South from Boston to Rhode Island for work. It was approximately 6:56 AM. The sky was illuminated, but the sun hadn’t risen high enough yet to obscure vision. Other than the object itself, the sky was clear and visibility was excellent. I looked up and saw an object flying from left to right above the tree line off in the distance. I don’t know much about aviation so I can’t give you a great estimate. But it was about the same altitude as a low flying passenger plane. The object itself had an elongated oval shape with a reflective silver color. It gave off a bright silver aura type light with a long tail similar to a comet. It flew horizontally in a straight line across the highway for several seconds before disappearing. To be clear, it didn’t move behind any type of cover. It just sort of “blinked” out of existence. It was odd. There were multiple cars on the highway so it’s possible other people saw it as well. Unfortunately I was driving and it happened too quick to film or shoot a photo, but it’s very possible someone else did. I took a screenshot of my GPS to help pinpoint the location and a photo of the road to show conditions around the time it was seen. Hope it helps.
