Blue-green-silver object, no sound est altitude 1500 ft

While sitting in our hot tub bot Jamie and I observed a multi lobed object moving from NW to SE. We joke about seeing UFO's and she pointed it out to me saying, "Oh look a UFO!" Turning to look at it I noticed that it didn't appear to be a known type of aircraft. The object seemed to be made up of 3 or more lobes which were reflective and seemed to glow blue green and white/sliver. The object made no sound and appeared to be at an altitude of 1500ft. The object appeared to be moving at 250-350 mph. The object flew directly over our house with a viewing time of approximately 30-45 seconds from first notice to going out of sight over our roof line.
The object seemed to be rotating as it traveled from North West to the South East. While the object was in level flight it appeared to have a gentle floating effect showing no obvious form of propulsion. Both my wife and I agreed that this was not an aircraft type known to us. It did not appear to be a balloon or drone.
