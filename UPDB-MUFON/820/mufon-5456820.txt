{rjl} Yellow light stationary then turning orange to red and accelerated quickly west

I was approximately 10 years old at Mt. Zion Baptist Church evening service. The service was over at approximately 7:00 pm and about 6 of us boys went to a grassy area beside the church to play football while the adults stayed in front. While playing ball I looked straight ahead towards the horizon southward and saw the yellow light stationary appearing to be about the size of a grapefruit. I got the attention of the other children and we all stopped to observe it. At that moment it flickered orange then red and flew rapidly towards the west leaving a red tracer. We were all amazed and as far as I know it was never reported.
