{3 steady lights. 1 craft. Sharp turn up to the clouds and disappeared

I was heading North on 95 & just past Copans Rd and noticed a object coming from the NW and moving way faster than a plane. At first I thought it was a military jet but it had no blinking lights and was flying super smooth and fast. I remembered there was a private airport around the same area but it was to my East. This object came from the NW and as it past over the highway heading East it took a sharp turn, I'd say almost a 90 degree turn and went up at about a 75 degree angel in under a second and disappeared into the distance/clouds. This object must've been about 500-600ft from the ground flying at a high speed in which you would see a jet flying at a air sea show. It had 3 lights. 1 bright light on the side, 1 less brighter on the front/side, and a very very dim light on the front. If that's makes any sense but it makes me believe it must have been a triangle. The lights were not dimming. Not blinking. I watch planes come in all the time and always try to distinguish them. One thing that always gives planes away is low speed, blinking red, blue,white lights. This was not a plane. Especially the way it shoot off and a super super high speed.
