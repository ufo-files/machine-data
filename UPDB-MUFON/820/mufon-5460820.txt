{cak} Two brilliant objects orbiting each other

Camping in back yard sleeping in lawn chairs that adjusts to lay down position. I was awake looking at the stars on a perfect clear night. Peebles is a small town without light pollution. Two objects caught my attention because they were orbiting each other. They looked like stars at first glance except both were brighter than the stars. They moved in a circle as if joined together by an invisible centerpiece. Imagine a teeter totter going up and down except it is airborne and instead of up and down both ends keep going around in a circle. I was aware that Skylab was in space at that time but quickly realized it wasn't Skylab. After several minutes, maybe 20 minutes while my eyes were fixed on the objects they both suddenly stopped parallel from my perspective one on left and one on right level in the sky.i kept my eyes firmly fixed on the objects when the object on the left flashed for 3 or 4 seconds then moved across the night sky until it faded out of view,it was gone in 5 seconds. The remaining object didn't move and I watched it until the sun came up and it faded away just like a star. 
This event has puzzled me my entire life.i told everyone and it still comes up now and then.im sure they were craft. I believe was alien crafts or we have a secret space program. If another explanation exists I would love to hear it.
