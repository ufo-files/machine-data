{dr}Light hovers nightly in distance slowly elevating and then slowly drifts away as sun rises

Outside my house every morning around 430 am to about sunrise, there is a bright light that starts out drifting in one direction, then right before the sun rises, it slowly drifts away into the atmosphere.The light is so bright that it catches my eye every time and it noticeably moves across the sky. It first looked like a star but it moves too fast to be a star and too slow to be a plane. No other planes appear during this time and there is no loud sound. It puzzled me and made me question what it could possibly be but I could not find a logical explanation. It eventually drifts off into the atmosphere and disappears from sight.
