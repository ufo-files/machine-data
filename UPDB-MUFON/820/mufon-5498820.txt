{cak} WAS SEEN PASSING SILENTLY ACCROSS OPENING IN VERY LOW (100FT) CLOUD COVER.

I WAS FISHING FOR SMELT WITH MY FAMILY AT THE SINGING BRIDGE DRAIN. THE CLOUDS WERE VERY LOW THAT NIGHT AND I WAS SHINING MY FLASHLIGHT ON THEM. I COULD SEE THE MILKY WAY'S STARS THROUGH AN OPENING IN THE CLOUD COVER AND A DARK CIRCULAR OBJECT PASSED ACCROSS THE OPENING, BLOTTING OUT THE STARS. IT WAS SOLID AND APPEARED BLACK IN THE NIGHT SKY,THROUGH IN SUN LIGHT IT MAY HAVE BEEN ANY COLOR. IT ALSO HAD DIM, WHITE OR GRAY LIGHTS IN FROM THE PERIMITER ALL THE WAY AROUND IT. I WOULD GUESS THERE WERE ABOUT A TOTAL OF TEN LIGHTS. IT MADE NO SOUND AND WAS ONLY VISABLE FOR ABOUT TWO SECONDS OR LESS. I AM AN ARTIST AND RETAIN VISUAL IMAGES VERY WELL. I TOLD MY FAMILY WHAT I SAW AND WHILE DEMONSTRATING THE FLASHLIGHT ON THE CLOUDS, IT PASSED ACCROSS ANOTHER OPENING IN THE CLOUDS AND MY STEPMOTHER AND I BOTH SAW IT THAT TIME. THAT VIEW LASTED ABOUT HALF AS LONG AS THE FIRST SIGHTING. IT HAPPENED ON FRIDAY APRIL 16, 1971.
