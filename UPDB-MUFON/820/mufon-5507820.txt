{kt}Saw two objects flying, thought they were planes until I actually spotted two planes.

I was getting out of my truck to hunt on a clear sky early morning. I saw two white objects flying across the sky at a decent pace. I thought they might have been planes until I realized they didn't have blinking lights. After a couple minutes two planes were visible in the sky, so I ruled the plane possibility out of the question. I pulled my camera phone out and recorded a video, after ten minutes of watching these lights, they took off. The two objects were not very close together, but were heading in the same direction.
