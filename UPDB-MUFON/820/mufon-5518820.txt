Saw white cylinder shaped UFO

I was driving to work going north east from Lockport to Niagara Falls NY at 945am. I noticed a bright white object on my right side, perhaps a mile or more from me around 800-1000 feet up. The Sky was blue no clouds what so ever. I thought at first it was a small plane but realized it wasnt moving. I continued to watch the object that I would describe as a completely white cylinder with no visible lights windows or propellant devices. This sighting lasted a mere 5 minutes before completely vanishing from sight. Leaving no trace of direction... I would describe it as if it went invisible in a split second. This sighting is my second in this area and both times involved a white cylinder shaped object first in the sky then vanishing. Weird as hell to say the least.
