{tjd} Object observed in serious of photos of full moon

On August 2, 2012, the I was photographing the first of two full moons to occur in August, the "Blue Moon" to occur on August 31st.  I did not get the opportunity to take the 2nd full moon and left the first series of photos on my laptop. I came across them tonight (6 years later) and was about to delete them when I noticed something in the photos that I had not observed at the time. I don't know if it is a mechanical object; bird; or, insect, but it appears in six consecutive photos in different positions around the full moon taken over approximately one minute. In the first photo, there is an additional, small light that does not appear in the rest of the photos. The blue light in the lower right quadrant is probably lens flare.
