A huge red projection across a large area

I was flying out of the Denver airport, and I when I got into the air I saw a massive red projection of light across the ground. It was made up of thousands of small red dots. The dots were moving around and would sometimes blink. They were moving around each other. the overall projection was moving across the land scape. The size of it was probably about a mile. It almost looked like some kind of light show or something but I couldn’t find any such events happening in Denver.
