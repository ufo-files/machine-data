This took place when I was in college with my friend

We were going to a party out in the country at a farm house.As we were driving I noticed a bright star moving ,I told my friend that star was getting brighter & moving.As we approached the farmhouse,we drove up the dirt road,then we noticed the light was decending fast down to the corn field.As we entered,the cornfields lit up like football stadium lights,was so bright we could not see what it was,no noise,or anything from it,As we ran towards it,it shot straight up to the sky,& was moving up in the stars.We never said anything about this to anyone.To this day,since 1978,we talk about it whenever we call each other.I currently live in Florida,friend lives in Wisconsin.
