{dr}Black triangle shaped UFO with orange lights outside my bedroom window and also the next night at a bonfire with friends

I was in my bedroom laying down to go to sleep and I felt the urge to look outside my window. It was in between 9-10pm. When I looked out my window I saw a large, black, triangle-shaped UFO. The UFO had a pattern of light orange lights on the outside of it. It just sat above the trees and hovered for a good minute. After a blink of my eyes, I opened them to find the craft to have disappeared. It never made any noise, there was no trail left in the sky where it had been. It just vanished. the following night I went to my friends house for a bon fire. I hadn't told anyone of my encounter for fear of ridicule. My two friends who wish to remain nameless had been pointing in the sky. They screamed and stated that I just missed the UFO. I asked them what it looked like and they repeated the craft in which they saw to be identical to what I had seen the night before... without even knowing that I had seen the same craft prior to that night. I couldn't feel as if something was watching me. To this day, I'm not sure if it was just a coincidence or if the space craft was outside my window for a reason and then followed me to the bonfire the following night as well.. the space craft and I were the two variables in both events.
