{dr}Observered reddish-orange bright light near South Lake Leelanau

I was sitting on my deck, located on the east side of my home at about 8:50pm on the evening of Sept 2nd. I was hoping to see the moon rise. I could hear the boat traffic from the lake so the evening wasnt exactly quiet. I was looking SE and noticed a small blinking light from a small plane. There was enough light yet that the stars were not visible. I turned my head to look due east at the sky and then as I was turning my head back to look SE, I observed a bright reddish-orange light just pop on above a distant tree line.
It was headed towards my direction, NW. I don't believe it made any noise but I cant be positive because of all the boat traffic. It moved at a steady rate of speed for the most part.  I looked at it for about 10 seconds then went to get my camera, but it was too dark for my camera to pickup on it. About 30 seconds after I first saw it, it got dimmer and began ascending and I believe turned due east and then it disappeared.

Its almost like a super bright headlight that is coming toward you but this was reddish-orange. By the way, I saw the moon rise by 9:09pm and I laughed because it was the same reddish-orange color of the object I observed.
I am just amazed when I see something like this. Its fascinating and just a bit unsettling.  I have observed white lights such as this a few times over the past few years. Its my first time reporting such an incident.
