V Shaped Object with about a dozen light globes

Spouse and I were outside at about 11:16pm before bed. I was looking in the sky as usual. I saw a V shaped object with about a dozen white globes coming from the north. I yelled at my spouse to look at it. I was rather large. Maybe 2 or 3 times the size of a commercial aircraft. The lights and the surface of the craft appeared muted or dimmed if you will and almost like a grey in color. The craft did not emit any sound whatsoever and it was on a flight path just above our trees. As stated, it was coming from the north. As we watched it fly over our house, it started a turn to the south west. We were able to see it for just about 5 seconds more before obstructions blocked our view. My spouse is a very skeptical person and he even agreed that he could not explain what we had just seen. We found it to be very exciting and tried our best to explain it.
