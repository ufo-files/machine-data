(GS) While driving observed bright white glowing light descend.

At about 6PM while driving east on Shake Ridge Road between Volcano and Pioneer into the Sierra Nevada mountains, I briefly observed a large bright white glowing light descending rather slowly from the sky into the mountains. I quickly lost sight of it because the road I was driving cut off my view.
