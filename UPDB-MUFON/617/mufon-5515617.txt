bright light flew over me

I was taking pictures of the moon when I looked over my right shoulder and seen a very bright light moving in my direction. It was no higher than an average small plane but just a big ball of bright light. I then set up my camera to prepare to take photos of the object as it went over me. I managed to snap off 20 photos using my Canon Rebel XSi camera mounted on my Lowpro tripod.. I already had my Ultrasonic 300mm telescopic lens on it and the resulting photos proved quite interesting
