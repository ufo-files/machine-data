UFO above Exhibition centre (Carlton)

Sky was blue with a little cloud cover, noticed a round white orb circling and drifting in different directions. Interesting a commercial helicopter was in the same airspace. Originally thought it was a drone, because of how it moved, however after a few minutes it broke up into two units. A smaller orb, more metallic silver detached from the white orb. The white orb stayed stationary while the smaller orb circled it. After about 10 minutes it disappeared into the clouds. I'm sure I'm not the only one who saw it because it was obvious against the blue sky. Another reason why I don't think it was a drone was because it was far too high and large. Considering how high it was it would have been the size of a car. My brother suggested it was a weather ballon, but the odd movements would suggest otherwise. Also being so close to the city it seems strange to have a weather ballon there. Tried to take images or video but the zoom on my phone wasn't strong enough to capture it.
