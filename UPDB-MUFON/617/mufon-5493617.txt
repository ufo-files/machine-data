{dr}I WAS TAKING OUT THE TRASH AT 6:30 AM BEFORE WORK WHEN I SAW WHAT I THOUGHT WAS A METEOR

ON JANUARY 6TH, 2013, I WAS TAKING OUT THE TRASH AT 6:30 AM BEFORE WORK WHEN I SAW WHAT I THOUGHT WAS A METEOR SHOOTING DOWN FROM THE SKY. IT WAS RED AND TURNED ORANGE WITH A TRAIL BEHIND IT. IT COULDN'T HAVE BEEN A METEOR, BECAUSE IT CHANGED DIRECTION, IT ALSO HAD A KIND OF ROARING SOUND. THEN IT CHANGED SHAPE AND BECAME A SILVER SAUCER AND WENT VERY FAST INTO THE CLOUDS. I TOOK SOME PICTURES OF IT WITH MY PHONE AND AS I WAS GOING BACK INTO MY HOUSE A VERY BRIGHT LIGHT APPEARED OVER ME, I HEARD NO SOUND, IT WAS LIKE A VERY BRIGHT, ROUND BALL OF LIGHT. I SEEMED IN A TRANCE AND WHEN IT WAS GONE I WENT TO WORK. I MIGHT HAVE SUFFERED MISSING TIME, I'M NOT SURE. BECAUSE OF MY JOB WITH THE GOVERNMENT I COULDN'T SAY ANYTHING BEFORE NOW.
