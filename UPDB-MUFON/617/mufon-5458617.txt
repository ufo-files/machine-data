{tjd} I was camping in the mountains behind my house early evening a bright orb appeared

I went camping in the mountains behind my house . I knew this mountain . I camped by a spring went to bed around 9 with the fire out.
around 9pm a odd sound scared me . I got out of the tent looked behind me saw a bright light flying through the trees. zig zagging in and out . I don't remember what happened after the ball of light hovered above me . I was in the tent before day break . It was still dark . The tent was not zipped . I walked out to the fire ring . I had a small fire . There was stack of wood by the fire . I didn't stack the wood by the fire. I was in a daze . Daylight I packed my gear and left the mountain . I was 14 at the time . 
 Years later in my late 30's I had surgery on my left a elbow . X-Ray showed a small piece metal in my forearm in the muscle of my arm. I never worked with metal in my life .I am not sure what it may be. I have night mares of lights bright lights flashing some constantly on . Im blinded by the lights . I ear odd voices standing close to me . 
 Question is what happened to me ?
