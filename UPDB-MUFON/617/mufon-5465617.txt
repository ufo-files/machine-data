Sighting of boomerang shaped object with 7 orange lights late one night.

I was out walking my dogs around my local park late one night & when it's a clear night i'm usually looking up at the stars as it was on this particular night.I first noticed the object when my dogs stopped for a sniff in some bushes & as I stood waiting for them I was looking back towards the west.As I scanned the night sky I did notice a couple of lights & at first thought they were the red/white flashing anti collision lights of an aircraft,but then realized that A)-They were not flashing & B)-They were more orange than red. Curious,I kept looking back whilst walking on with the dogs & within about 5 or so seconds later the lights were now more visible & could now clearly see that there were not 2 lights,but 7 that were in a V-Shape with 1 at the front? & 3 down either side.As it got closer I could just about make out a dark outline sort of boomerang shape.As there was nothing to compare it against,I couldn't really tell how big the object was or it's altitude,i'm estimating my answers of size,distance & altitude on the small aircraft (Cessna size) that fly over the area during the day.It was only after it disappeared from sight that I realized it didn't make a sound as it flew past or just how quick it must have been traveling as from my first sighting of the lights to it's disappearance was in reality about 3-5 minutes,but seemed longer.As I continued on walking my dogs I tried not to quickly jump to a conclusion on what i'd seen,but there was nothing else I could think it was apart from a U.F.O. as there are no military bases or major airports(apart from Lt. 24miles away)
 Being a believer in UFO's etc. I was quite excited that in my mind I might have seen one.
