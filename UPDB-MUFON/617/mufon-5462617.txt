{jpg} UFO - Sighting

Summer 2015 (approx. June): 

I witnessed a UFO driving down Heards Ferry Rd from Mt. Vernon towards Riverside Dr. in Sandy Springs, Georgia one night. I saw this UFO from the beginning of Heards Ferry Rd past Riverwood International Charter School where I graduated High School. I chased this UFO in my car with a pocket New Testament Bible on my lap speeding down Heards Ferry Rd approximately 50-60 MPH (35 MPH) banging music in my car with subwoofers at full volume. This UFO was just above the tree line and seemed only a few hundred feet away with its lights all activated (white/yellow/amber/streetlight color) all around it. I followed this UFO all the way down Heards Ferry Rd (as it was backing further and further away from me as I was driving) until it started getting a few hundred feet above the tree line as I was passing my High School going towards the Northside Drive exit of 285. It got smaller and smaller until it started to blend in with the stars of the sky at a higher altitude. I just drove past the exit and kept on driving towards the end of Heards Ferry Rd.
