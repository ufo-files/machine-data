My dogs were barking inside the house so when I let them out around 10pm I saw all these Orange and red balls of light gliding across the sky. The first batch I saw were doing a formation kind of like and arrow head once they all cleared the roof and tree line across the street I couldnt see them. Then 4 or 5 came from the original direction the first ones came from...but this time they stay in a straight line and followed the same path as the first group. This continued for about 15 minutes...small groups would come from the right side of the sky and move slowly to the left side until I couldn't see them. They seem to take on a shape of a person like a man as they were going by. My husband at the time was in the shower...by time he got out there he did witness two of the objects go by but he thinks they are drones. I called the Police dept and reported what I saw. They did come to my house that night there were 4 officers. They looked at the video I had taken, but with the smirks I think they just thought I was crazy. The next morning an officer showed up at my home at 6:30am and wanted to see the video. That is when he advised me that I should make a report to your agency. I didn't know there was an agency for this. I am sorry I do not know how to attach video to this report. Its not a great video because half way through I realized that it wasn't recording and started it back up. Plus I said a swear word in it...I was pretty shaken up that evening. I know what I saw and I will never forget it. I just felt obligated to let the authorities know. Thank you for your Time!
