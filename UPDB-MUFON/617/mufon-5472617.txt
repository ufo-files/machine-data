I live on the 18 fl and look over a large unobstructed flight path of Southwest airlines and jets flying into Love Field as the pass near the city. 
Anything out of the ordinary catches our eye. On October 29 at 1830 hrs I observed a bright flash of movement in the southern quadrant of my view. At the moment I saw these objects about 10, they appeared to be falling burning pieces of debris from space at first, they glowed bright gold as if the sun was illuminating them but then more grey and red objects shaped like somewhat flattened globes appeared and some bright lights could be seen clustered high overhead barely visible. The objects continue to approach and descend together rapidly and I thought to grab the camera and start video and photographic record. The objects moved quickly to the north about 2 miles before they were almost overhead and they started to glow gold and all disappeared together. I have two pics that show a red object near a gold top shaped object and you see the red object disappearing in the next frame. The sighting lasted about 1 or 2 minutes. Then about 1 minute later another solitary object similar also appeared and disappeared in the same area but as I said a few minutes later however with no sparkles of light, it just vanished as it got to the same zone.
