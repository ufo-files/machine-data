{nd} Observed large glowing White domed UFO

I was on my porch looking at stars when I noticed a large glowing white craft appear out of nowhere.Within 5 seconds of me looking to the South,the Craft appeared to my North.I called for my girlfriend to "get out here" so I would not witness the craft alone like had happened just 10-15 days prior.

We both saw a glowing craft with a dome on top but due to the trees the lower part of the craft was not fully visible.The craft was at around 500' and was moving from West to North East very slowly(50 MPH). As it moved east it started to pick up altitude and the glow from it was enough to hurt the eyes.I shined my 15 million watt spotlight on it and then it sped up and turned to due North and quickly disappeared .The bright glow was still visible around the craft even after it left our direct line of sight.There was NO noise,no FAA strobe lights just an incredible super bright White glow that lit up the ground for hundreds of yards around it.The super White glow was identical to what I saw on another saucer shaped craft 10 to 15 days earlier in the same exact directions.Whatever this craft was,it was not from Earth as we know it!
