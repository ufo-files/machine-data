{dr}White sphere high in eastern sky - Tacoma, WA 6/27/17

At around 6:30 pm on 6/27/17 in Tacoma, WA, I saw what I thought was a jet very far off in the distance to the East. It was a bright pinpoint in the sky. I observed it hovering in one spot, appearing and disappearing. I assumed it was flying directly away from me fading out of sight as it travels further and further away. It reappeared and I watched it slowly climb upward then stop, descend back down and start moving south. It maneuvered in all directions in front of a cloud for several minutes. It then turned and began moving back north to the place I first spotted it. After slowly creeping north it rapidly moved upward only to immediately shoot back down. 
It was below the few clouds in the sky. I'm not sure how high it was or the size. It looked like it was nearly grazing the clouds but it was vey small and hard to get good perspective on.
At one point it appeared somewhat brighter and faintly glowed orange. Imagine seeing Mars glowing in the sky on a bright blue summer evening.
I observed it for about 10 minutes straight but lost sight of it. It stayed in a pretty small area of the eastern sky while I watched it.
