{rjl} 6-10 crafts dim to bright red, sometimes bright white, white flashes

Standing on the third floor of an apartment looking at the mountain south of Mica Mountain was 5-6 bright white lights in a semi circle not high above (and staying very near) the mountain... some dimmed and turned red, then some seemed to dim completely. Many times you could see 2-3 lights very close together dimly. There were many very bright white flashes of light where a few crafts seemed to disappeared. After tuning on and off, being bright white and bright red and dim red for nearly 30 minutes, they disappeared for 5-10 min and reappeared heading east, in a line at one point and thn finagle formation before disappearing from view.
