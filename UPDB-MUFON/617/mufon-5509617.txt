{kt}Orange Light

Sitting on my patio entertaining company. I noticed a bright orange light to my left. I thought is was a plane but there were no blinking lights and it was too high up. I thought it may be a firework but it continued to travel over the city of Mayfield Hts. I began to think it was a space station but it suddenly stopped and remained still for 15 secs & then began blinking erratically changing from orange to a white and acsended. forth.
