{dr}Single V-Shaped object over Phoenix, AZ on 10/31/2015 at 21:15 local time

Standing outside home in central Phoenix on 10/31/2015 around 21:15 local time with spouse. Looked up and saw a lights in a "V" formation moving from NE to SW. Elevation was difficult to discern. Best guess is 15,000 feet? Object was moving fast, I picked it up at a reasonably low angle looking up, it was directly overhead in less than three seconds, which is where my wife picked it up visually. We traversed our point of view to watch it continue moving southwest and after it passed overhead the lights grew faint and the object disappeared into the night sky. At one point, I actually did think it might be a flock of birds because the triangle shape seemed to flex like a bow recovering its shape, before it solidified into a solid V again, but the speed was way too fast and the elevation way too high, plus the lights were not reflective but appeared to be very faint sources of light. I've ruled out fighters flying in a "V" formation as well as there was no sound and fighters in formation at that elevation would have taken longer to traverse the sky. The craft moved from one edge of the horizon to within 30% of the opposite horizon in less than 10 seconds. Hoping others report seeing the same thing at the same time.
