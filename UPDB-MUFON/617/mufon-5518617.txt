Driving. Saw bright white light with green edge floating, then made a sharp arc downward, disappeared.

I was driving Northbound on Rt. 61, about 10 miles south of Blue Grass, IA. In the northeast sky, I saw a bright whitish light, rather large in size. There was almost a greenish tinge to a ring around the object. As soon as I saw it, I asked my husband if he saw it and he did. We only saw it for a few seconds and it made a downward arc and disappeared.

I had no idea what it was. Im hoping someone else saw it and has an explanation. I only saw it because it was so bright. It could not have been a plane, it was too bright, too big and moved too quickly in a downward arc to be a plane. There were also no light towers in the area, outside a red light in the north west sky that did not go off / flash/ blink at all.
