{cak} My friend and I were hiking in the snow by the river when a light appeared above us. We both turned and saw that a disk like object was releasing a large amount of light just above the tress. We had left our phones in the car.

1. By the Chattahoochee river hiking in the snow.
2. A bright light coming from the object.
3. We thought it was a helicopter but then the light dimmed and we saw the disk like shape. 
4. We didn't see the object come down but we watched it as it hovered over the trees and eventually went back into the sky until the light could no longer be seen.
5. We were frightened and nervous. We had left our phones in our car because we knew it was cold and we weren't going to spend very long in the snow. We got back to our car and called our other friend. We have been shaken up all day and decided to report it.
