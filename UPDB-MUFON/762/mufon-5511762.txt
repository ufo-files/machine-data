{dr}Hovering Sphere Viewed from landing plane

Hovering sphere viewed from a plane, flight DL6090 flying into JFK around 6:00pm, 16th October 2011. Close to landing but still fairly high, cars were visible but small on the ground. 

I saw, looking down from above, a spherical object in the air above the ground, it was a light gray or brown colour, and either had markings or structural elements in a different shade. There were no lights, maybe a faint glow. I cant be sure of the size, it was not large. I can1t be sure if it was moving itself as the plane flew past it. I only saw it for a few seconds.

I noticed it as it was fairly close, and realized in was not on the ground, the plane fly past it.
