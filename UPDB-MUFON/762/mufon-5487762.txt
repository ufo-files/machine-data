{jpg} 3 of us saw this very bright red ball shaped light over the ocean

3 of us where at the beach and saw the very bright ball shaped light very high up then all of a sudden it came straight down look like it almost land on the water.  It was a ways away from us though. It was about 7pm at dusk time.  Then after it was on the horizon for about a min it flow in several different directions then all of a sudden it was gone.  Just an FYI I was in the airforce as a aircraft mech and crew chief on fighter planes an I knew we had nothing like that!!
