Object rose up over the tree line & hovered for 15 mins, appeared to see me, tilted in my direction.

My fiance, My cousin & myself were driving through Pennsylvania for a couple hours enjoying the tranquility of the scenery & conversing about life when we made a wrong turn & ended up on [item moved/CMS/nd]. We were driving on a downward slope passing the hillside when I noticed off to my right that there were lights behind the treeline. At first I thought it was just lights from a house we were approaching but as we got closer the lights ascended up through the treeline & hovered just a few feet above the treeline about 200-300ft away from the road. The object was noticeably big considering it was about the size of the first floor of the house we had just passed. I opened my car window & had literally half of my body outside of the car as we were moving because I at first thought this cant be what I think it is. The object had no sound what so ever & the lights were the purest white I had ever seen & didn't light up the trees at all. 

It was a disc shape with three white lights on the bottom, I assumed it was the front of the object because I got the feeling that something in the craft was watching me from inside, & then the object tilted at about a 45 degree angle looking directly at me & I began to feel strange. It was emitting an energy that I had never felt before & I could feel the energy coursing through my body. This was not fear or an adrenaline rush. It was something different I was experiencing. I could hear my fiance in the background yelling "what is that?, What the ***** is that?" I could hear the fear in his voice. It was so close to us. I couldnt take my eyes off of it, not even for just a second so that I could grab my camera. Almost as if I was in a trance. As more cars began to catch up to our car (we were driving very slow since we were unfamiliar with the area we were in)& we began to pass the object it seemed to go into what I would describe as a decoy mode. The three main lights shut off & they switched on four much smaller lights. Two red(diagonal from one another) & parallel to the red lights were two green lights(also diagonal from one another). 

I broke out of the trance as we passed it, & I started to yell out in excitement "Turn your lights back on!, Please! Turn them back on." I guess I had hoped that they would listen to me. I then yelled out "I knew you were real!, Im so happy that I could see you even if this is the only time." It was so amazing. I kept watching out the window as we got farther & farther away from it & it stayed there until we were about a mile away from it. It then began to ascend into the sky & I lost sight of it. My cousin was in the backseat in complete shock. He remained quiet the entire time & just watched. He wasnt sure what to make of the object we were seeing. He was a skeptic prior to this experience & it took him a few weeks to admit what he saw. My fiance & I had discussed the encounter frequently after that & to this day still talk about it.
