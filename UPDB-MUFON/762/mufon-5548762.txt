Triangular white lights flying at a high rate of speed and then vanishing

I was walking on the boardwalk on Mission Beach and looked up to see what at first seemed like an unusual airplane. The lights were all white and in a strict V formation - very different than a typical airplane's lights. The object looked very large and moved at a high rate of speed. It was very high up and vanished after 6-8 seconds as it passed behind a street light and could no longer be seen. Very unusual and memorable event - I hope others that may have seen this will report their sitings here.
