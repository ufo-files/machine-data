Faint red disc followed by a bright white light flying in erratic directions, due to the speed and direction change this could not be Rota or fixed wing aircraft.

I could predict where the light would appear as I could make out the very faint red shape in the clear night sky, the light varied in brightness. The light remained close or connected to the faint red shape. It never looked like a flat shape only a full round shape 

The sighting was in the same area of sky for over an hour my wife also watched this with me. The sky was clear with no cloud. The light also gave off what I think was a static or electrical sound.1 arcing" but this was not a Flash like lighting but more constant. I did see aircraft in the sky that night but not close to the object. I would welcome any feed back or conformation of similar sightings.
