{cak} Came form the east and hovered over us for about 5min. than turned east and accellerated at high speed

We were driving east on a Highway 11 on Racine's south side when we noticed this triangle lighted object headed west towards us at a slow speed parallel to the highway and we noticed that it was not a plane. We stopped the car as the object had stopped almost directly over us and we adjacent to where a construction project was under way. The object was triangle shaped and I would guess it to be about 60-70 ft. on a side and about 500-600 ft.up. I can remember bluish light and reddish orange light along the outer edges of the triangle, It was like they were neon tubes along the side. As it hovered it rotated slowly back and forth clockwise and counter clockwise and seemed to be looking for something. After about 15 minutes of this it started to move to the east slowly and suddenly it picked up speed and accelerated to the east at a wow! speed until it disappeared out over the Lake. While we were observing this craft 2 other cars stopped and people got out of them and were watching it also. There was no conversation between any of us and after it was over everybody just got back in their cars and left. The next day there was no reports in the paper and we didn't report it either. This was approximately 13/4 miles west of Lake Michigan.
