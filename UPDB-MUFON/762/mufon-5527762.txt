The first one was bullet like. Second one was orb or star-like

Driving south on Washington St Denver. I caught sight of what looked like a jet at aproximately 30,000 ft. But as I looked at it, I realized this was not a jet because it had no wings. I watched it a few minutes before it vanished in the sky. This is the second time I have seen this type of UAP. It was shiny metallic with sunlight reflecting on it. Its body was long like a jet. There was no vapor trail. I was in my car driving so I could not detect any noise.
