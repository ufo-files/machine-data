(GS) Many lights starlike going from east to northwest.

Took our dog to the back yard. I was waiting and watched the sky, it was cloudy with large holes in the cloudcover. 

At first I thought I saw a satellite very bright, but they travel north to south or south to north. As I was watching there were more coming from the east, these were moving as fast or faster that a satellite, very high up and they just kept coming (must have been a couple/three dozen). In the middle of the procession, there was what looked like a equilateral triangle shape of lights, the only one I saw in the group. 

I watched them for about 5 - 10 minutes then went to tell my wife, not interested, so my brother-in-law went out and we watched them for another 5 - 10 minutes. They were nothing like wed ever seen. 

Brother-in-law saw a piece on channel 2 news this morning 12/12/12 and the event happened on 12/08/12. 

We had no idea what we were seeing but suspected something not from this neighborhood. 

It got too cloudy to see them, they were still coming, so we came back into the house.
