i have terrible insomnia and got up to go outside on our patio (we live in an upstairs apartment, with a small covered patio on the south side) to smoke a cigarette. the patio is located outside my husbands office. the closer i got to the door, the more i felt like something was wrong. i shrugged it off and turned the handle. the feeling of something is not right swept over me and i hesitated. i finally went outside and was so creeped out that instead of sitting i leaned against the door to smoke. i realized that i couldn't hear anything , it was absolutely quiet except for a strange bird making noise. there is a very busy highway that is north east of us that you can hear the cars etc. at all times. there was nothing, not a single car could be heard. on top of that , there was no wind. it was dead still and silent. i was so creeped out that i noticed something coming overhead and at first i thought it was a walmart shopping bag, then realized that was impossible. i stared at the thing until i couldn't see it anymore. our apartment buildings are so close that by the time i ran in to grab my phone (which was all the way in the bedroom on the other side of the apartment) it would have been gone. about a minute after this thing passed, i could hear the cars on the highway again and even a small breeze. the object went at a slow speed overhead and seeemed to change shape almost unnoticeabely. it wasn't very bright and i'm not sure if the light was emitted or was reflected. before it dissapeared it looked as if it had black surrounding it but the center was still a yellowish color.
