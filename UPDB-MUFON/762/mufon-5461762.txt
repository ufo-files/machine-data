{rjl} Saw a light similar to a plane or satellite but was not blinking.

The light I saw was similar to a plane but the light never blinked. It was of a reddish/orange color and decently bright. About as bright as a satellite. It moved from the N-E part of the night sky to the S-E part of the sky it seemed to move in a straight line at first at a very high speed. Similar to a meteor. (I actually thought it was a “shooting star” at first) but then the light got halfway across the sky and made a sharp zig zag motion across the sky towards the east coast and then back west and then straight south before it disappeared.
