White light surrounded by different colors

Diffused ball of lights with bright white star like light in center that moved in and out of the sphere. Occasionally flashed full spectrum of colors under white lights plus a beam occasionally shown down on surface. Started over Marine base, moved over city of Oceanside then slowly continued north along the coast. Observed through high powered binoculars.
