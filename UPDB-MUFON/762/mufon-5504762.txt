{rjl} Bright light that grew in shape and size. Moved slowly at first. Then sped up before disappearing

It was a little after 7 pm and I was outside walking my dog. I live on a mountain and have a very clear view of the sky. I often see airplanes but what I saw was nothing close to an airplane. As my dog walked around the yard, I looked to the sky. I saw what appeared to be a very bright star. It was brighter than any star I'd ever seen. As I continued to look at it, it grew in size and shape. It went from a round shape of a normal star, to a large oval shape. It had an aura around it. Almost like a fog. It moved slowly from my left to my right. As it sped up the shape got flat. So much so that during the last few seconds it was totally flat. After around 2 and a half minutes it just vanished. I was in total shock. Just after I lost sight of it, my dog started barking at seemingly nothing. I stayed outside for another 5 minutes or so and saw nothing after that.
