{jpg} A bright blue light going up from the ocean at high speed, then made a sharp angle turn south, speeded up and disapeared

I was sitting by the pool talking with my sister. We stayed there for a while. We were sitting facing east. Moments after my sister stepped inside, I saw a bright, round, blue light coming straight up from the ground above the tree line. I can't tell the distance of the object, but since we were only a block and a half from the intracoastal, it must have been on the ocean or on Palm Beach Island. At first I thought it was fireworks. I called to my sister while keeping my eyes on it. To my surprise, the light kept going up, and I realized it was going way too high and fast to be fireworks. I was calling my sister frantically to come and watch. After a few seconds going up, the light made a sharp angle turn south. It speeded up and disapeared. I was really shaken after and did some reseach. I have no idea what it was but it did not look like anything I have seen discribed anywhere.
