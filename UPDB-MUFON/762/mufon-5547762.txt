While driving in Brooklyn we spotted an object in the sky.

Nov.30,2007 at 4:00p.m. on a friday we saw a daylight ufo in brooklyn.It was triangular in shpae w/3 reddish orbs at each corner. the object was next to the rooftop of a building on the corner of Ave. Z and Nostrand Ave. My wife was driving us and she stopped at a stop light and told me to look at the weird thing in the sky and to my amazement I did and I was in shock I rolled down my window and stuck my head out and pointed at it. it stood there petrified, except the reddish, pulsating lights. The object was pointed at the building like the arrow on the computer. I looked at it really hard and it was pitch black no bolts, seams, or windows; just black, but the lights seem to be alive, the way they moved. When the stop light changed the object swished away. I told my wife "Let's follow it" and she said "Are you kidding, we don't want to be taken." We both confirmed what we saw to each other and now I can say I've seen a ufo. The following days I told people of what I saw to see if anybody else did too, but no.
