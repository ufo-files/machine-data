I live in Memphis Tn.In 1977 around 9 o'clock I was going to a neighbors house that lived five doors away. I got under the street light that is in our cove when i saw a shadow above my head as i looked up i saw an enormous triangle shaped object in the air.It got close to me and stopped.It hoovered in the air and then pointed it's nose down toward me.I noticed the lights on the bottom and when it pointed toward me i could see a frosted type window with figures behind the glass. I could see two sides of the UFO. I was a kid so i didn't know what it was but i knew i had never seen anything like it. It looked like it was made out of some type of metal,such as stainless steel without the shine of stainless steel.It had three lights on the underside. I don't know how long it was over me but i do know that i was petrified. It then leveled back off and started moving away from me heading East. I watched it start moving and the only sound coming from it was a hiss, like air being let out of a tire. I watched it until i could no longer see any part of object. I then ran home and went to my bedroom and sat and prayed that what i had seen was not real(but it was)7. I've told this story at least a hundred times and on one occasion i had a friend that worked as a Memphis Fireman come from across the room and told me he saw the same thing along with his wife ,kids,and a friend.I would love to be put under hypnosis and draw what i saw.
