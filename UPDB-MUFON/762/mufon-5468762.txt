{jpg} I was about 5years old sleeping upstairs. I was awoken by a large batlike entity with large glowing red eyes was hanging on the window. Making strange noises. I had night terrors ever since then.

I was sleeping upstairs in our farm house. It was on a dirt road, miles from neighbors. I was awaken by a noise and turned my head to see a large, dark winged, entity, with large glowing red eyes, hanging off of the screen in the window. It was watching me making noises. I screamed and my mother came up stairs and it was gone. She didn't see it. I had night terrors and was afraid of the dark after that. Years later I saw the Mothman stories and I couldn't believe what I was seeing and instantly knew it was what I saw. We only lived 10 miles from a TNT area. I realize that it was a few years before the bridge tragedy.
