A white orb was hovering in the sky. Over the course of an hour it moved west and disappeared.

It was about 7:40pm when a bright white orb was spotted in the sky above my home. It glowed quite brighter than the stars in sky and was the only object seen in that particular part of the sky. I watched the object for about five minutes and thought it might be spinning. I stopped watching it, thinking it might simply be a star. I checked on it later at about 8:20 and saw it had moved west. I checked again at 9:20 and it was gone. This would only prove to be one of the first sightings of the object that would continue to come back at about the same time almost every night since.
