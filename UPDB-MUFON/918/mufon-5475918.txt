Standing on my porch one evening and looked up, saw a dark triangle shaped object with three bright lights, one at each corner, hovering over my house at quite a distance but still distinguishable. Object did not move except when the two lower lights were drawn up or absorbed by the top light at a unnatural speed which then disappeared just as quickly. it has taken me years to come to terms with what I saw, I do not know what it was, but I know it's been unexplainable thusfar.
