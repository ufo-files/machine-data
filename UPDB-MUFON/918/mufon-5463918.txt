While observing the morning sky a bright light appeared out of no where and accelrated in a semi circle direction before dissapearing into the distance at great speed.

While at work I left the building I was in to go outside for a ciggarette. I am an avid astronomer and observer of the night sky so it is very natural for me to look up at every opportunity. I observed a bright light, around double the size and brightness of a star appear out of no where and fly in a semi circle pattern ( if imagined on a clock it appeared at 6 oclock and flew round to 3 oclock ) after the semi circle maneuver the light dissipated as if flying off into the distance at great speed.
