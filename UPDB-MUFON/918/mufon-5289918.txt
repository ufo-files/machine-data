At 1:15 AM New Years Eve, 2015, I witnessed a craft hovering over Townsite, Powell River, BC.

At 1:15 AM New Years Eve, 2015, I witnessed a craft appear to circumnavigate the moon then move to a stationary position and hover in the night sky over Townsite, Powell River, BC. I could clearly see the craft as it hovered there, so decided to try and snap a few photographs with a cell phone I had handy. The craft remained there for approx. an hour and a half, so allowed plenty of time to take photos. I obtained approx. between 150 and 200 images. Several nights before this occurred, I witnessed 4 or 5 similar craft hovering in formation over our residence in Townsite. There were two women present, both of whom became quite emotional when the objects began to shuffle formation and shift their position in the night sky. They both took refuge in the house. These craft remained for some time also before departing some time in the night. Because of the intense emotional reaction of my friends, I opted to keep the second occurence to myself. I didn't look at the photos until a year later, when I enlarged them and saw what appeared to be a strange object morphing from one unusual shape to another in what seemed to be deliberate fashion. On this evening I was looking after a young man with special needs and was staying on through the night. At around one AM, I went out of doors and soon after noticed the light that appeared to be circumambulating the moon. It then took stationary position some way up and began sequencing through a morphing pattern as evidenced in the photos. Since this evening on Jan 1, 2015, I have accumulated another few hundred shots of this and similar nature, in Lake Cowichan, BC., and Nanoose Bay, British Columbia.
