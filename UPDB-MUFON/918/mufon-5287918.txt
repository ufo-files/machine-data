Bright object moved across sky quickly and disappeared--not plane, comet, shooting star

Four of us stood in the dark back yard of our Sedona, AZ rental at 8:45pm on Thurs, May 13 to look at the night sky. From out of the SW sky, maybe 60 degrees high, appeared a very bright light that moved in a slight arc across the sky until disappearing in the East/ SE, dipping down towards the horizon until the light went dark, all in about 45 seconds or less. I'd never seen anything like it, so I told the other 3 to look carefully and remember what we were seeing. The object was bright enough that two of us saw two or three light streaks emanating from it (not "projected light rays" or anything like that, but just light streaks shining off of the object). It was not as high up as stars and definitely looked bigger. In the past, when I saw what I think was the ISS, it looked like a small dot (star) high in the sky, moving across the sky like a satellite---THIS WAS NOT LIKE THAT. Too large and much closer (tho I have NO idea how high; just it didn't look anything like any star or planet.. There were NO blinking lights, no aircraft red light. There was NO TAIL. Two of us thought we saw it move with a very slight wobble, not much, but noticeable with careful observation. It was a pure, bright white color. Not blinking.
