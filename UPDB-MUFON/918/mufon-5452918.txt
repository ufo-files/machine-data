Craft seemed to have come over Cheyenne Mountain

Was sleeping and I felt like something was telling me to get up, so I went to my back door, looked toward NORAD and saw something moving west to east at a very slow speed, as it got closer I was able to see three lights and triangle shape that was translucent in appearance, thought about grabbing binocular, but the craft just moved east and I was like a kid in a candy store. BTW this is the second time in forty years that I have seen something that I could not explain in detail, but like this incident it happened at 3:30 A.M..
