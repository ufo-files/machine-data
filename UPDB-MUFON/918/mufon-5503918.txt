{kt}Heard a loud helicopter. Looked out window and saw bright light being followed by helicopter.

Looked out window when I heard a loud helicopter not usually in the area. Saw a bright small single light being followed by a helicopter. Ran out the front door and called my wife to see it pass by the house and head directly towards the west coast. There was no sound from the light and the speed steady and constant. It wasn't going very fast. There were no flashing lights or colored lights. Very strange.
