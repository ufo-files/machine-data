{cak} Test Tube Shaped dull metallic stationary hovering craft over Albertsons grocery store

I was coming back from a Doctors appointment, and was heading west on University Drive, when I seen a Test Tube Shaped Craft hovering less than a hundred feet up from the ground surface, and over Albertsons Grocery Store on the corner of Val Vista Drive, and University Drive in Mesa, Arizona. I was sitting on the passenger side of my car while my roommate drove. I glanced over and knew that what I was seeing was a Craft of extraterrestrial origin. The craft was a dull metalic nickel plated color, and was a very large cigar shaped or test tube shaped. The craft stayed stationary and not moving for several minutes as I observed the most amazing thing I have ever seen. We turned south onto Val Vista Drive and as we drove to our home I continued to watch the craft until I could no longer see the craft anymore. The traffic was moderately moving along, and I know other people seen it besides myself.
