[nd} Triangle UFO

We were driving on highway 242, southbound, about about to exit on monument blvd / Gregory exit. Around 8-9 p.m.Our Speed was approx. going about 70 mph, when we both suddenly saw a huge triangular object in the sky, approx. 2000-3000 feet in the air. It was approx. above the old shopping mall which is now a mini strip mall. It was moving very slowly in a North direction (towards us). So it was above highway 680 concord / pleasant hill. When we saw it, we slowed down greatly to look at it, around 55 MPH. It was a triagle with lights bordering the exterior. Mostly red lights, some slowly pulsating. It had many lights, although the craft itself was hidden mostly by the sharp contrast of the night sky. The object made no noise, in fact we rolled down the windows and sunroof to see if we could hear anything. It was a massive object of which we had never seen before, we both grew up living around the an airport so we know what sounds airplanes and helicopters make. Also being around a major flight path of SFO/OAK airports we know what airliners look and sound like at low altitudes. As we examined the object driving slowly off the exit. It seemed to have not moved at all, hovering with no forward speed at all. When we were making our way down the exit, the freeway itself blocked our view of the craft, we waited for the light going eastbound on monument blvd. When we came out under the freeway overpass, it was completely gone, again, no noise was heard. We stopped in the empty parking lot for 15-20 mins looking at the sky to see where it had gone, but it was completely out of sight.
