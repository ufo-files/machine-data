Watched white speres pass by a mile or so in the air 3 days in a row at about the same time everyday, then at the same point every time would turn nearly straight up & disappear into space it looked like, & the last time we saw this happen a green glowing object pass right abouve us about 20ft & landed in someones back yard. We all felt like we were frozen for a few seconds or so. The objects that were flying over head did not seem to make any noise we could hear. It seems quite surreal because when the last sphere that went by turned up & disappeared into space is when the glowing whatever it was came zooming over our heads at fairly horizontal angle from the direction the spheres went. 3 of us witnessed this at the same time. Another friend of ours showed up not long talking about the green glowing object he had seen from across town. It is a fairly small town. i just have always wondered about this & wondered if there were other reported incidents of this in the Port Arthur, Texas area. We were actually in a suburb called Port Acres. This was sometime between 1972-73 We were all to afraid to go into the neighbors backyard & look for the abject that fell.
