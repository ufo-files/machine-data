Strange plane with green lights dropping balls of lights to ground

My boyfriend woke me up saying a strange flying object (he thought it was a plane) had woken him up because it was flying so low over the house. There was a pulsating rumbling (on and off like a heart beat) that woke him and he looked out the window. The plane had the strangest green lighting pattern on it - hes never seen before. It flew over the house and then over the trees across the road. He suddenly saw small glowing balls of light dropping out of the plane to the ground. When I woke up and looked out he pointed to a small glowing light across the road in a field. It glowed for a few minutes (not pulsing, a steady glowing) and then it suddenly faded out.
