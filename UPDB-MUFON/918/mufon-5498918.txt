{cak} Unusual cloud formations while looking west just before sunset

I was at home and all my doors and windows were open as it was a nice day I looked outside and noticed a straight line coming down from the sky. I watched and noticed another one coming down right next to it. At first I thought it was a airplane making a trail but I saw no plane. I got my binoculars out and could not see what was making the trail that was still in place. It reminded me of a meteor too because it also was glowing coming down but it was one after another following a straight path and stopping right above the tree line we witnessed 7 in all. The hole event lasted about an hour till the sun went down and we couldn't see anymore. I know that their were reports of meteors in a few states mine being one of them but this event was 2 days after the reports and I have never seen anything like this. I asked the local meteorologist about this and he said their were reports of meteors on Tuesday this event was on a Thursday. My camera died during the first video. the second video is after I plugged in.
