Unknown Flying Object between Hawai’i island and Maui

We were looking at the stars and could see across the channel to Maui. Could see the red blinking lights on top of the wind turbines. Noticed a red moving light that hovered in one location. At first we thought it was another wind turbine, but then turned white and slowly moved East. Was pulsating in and out (visible, not visible) and changed between red and white, becoming bright at times. Continued to move East and then went up and down before going behind a hill close to our home. Could not make out the shape of the object or vehicle - seemed distant 40-50 miles away and closer to Maui’s southern coast.
