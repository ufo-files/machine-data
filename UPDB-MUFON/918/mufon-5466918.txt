{rjl} Appeared above clouds at sunrise

On Jan 3, 2018 at 7:20 AM I was in my kitchen sitting at a table observing the sunrise. The object shown appeared in about 10-15 seconds as I was watching. It appeared to be about 4-5 miles away and was hundreds of feet across. The object looked to be circular in shape and had pointed v-shaped structures coming out from the outside edges that were similar to the crown on the Statue of Liberty, although not as exaggerated. The object was gleaming brightly in a silver color. I didn1t see the object move. It seemed drop in altitude to just above the clouds and then remain stationary. I routinely take photos of sunrises and wanted to capture it to show to my wife. I ran out onto my front deck in my underwear and only took two photos as it was very cold. I noticed that there were no birds calling which was unusual because I have neighbors with roosters and peacocks that call at that time. The most unusual thing about this is that where I would normally watch an event such as this very closely, I went back into my house and did not look at it again even though it was in clear view outside my kitchen window from where I sit at the table. My mind was very confused at what I had seen and I seemed to have an inability to focus on the event. I didn1t even show the photos to my wife and she happened to see them a few days later when we were reviewing my photos. Coincidentally, just after the incident we were returning home after dark and had just left the highway onto Windmill Rd when a UFO slowly passed over us from behind {west to east} and after it got about 150 feet past our car it quickly disappeared into the distance at a NE direction. I had stopped my car in order to watch the craft which was disc shaped and glowing orange and red on the under side.
FYI I am a retired Police Officer and am very inquisitive by nature.
