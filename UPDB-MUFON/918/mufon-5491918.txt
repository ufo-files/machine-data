{jpg] Nine Orange Orbs over New Castle

I was told they might have been Chinese lanterns, but I'm really doubtful of that theory. It is possible and would like to know if anyone in or close to my area had been releasing them. I had mentally asked for a sighting, two years ago larger objects appeared in the same location and only two days after July. When I looked out my window I saw this formation, an orb above and one below the other. They ascended slowly and then I noticed they continued to appear, one after the other, at times two together. They wobbled and even shot side to side at times. I was ecstatic when I noticed them. They glided off to the North East until they could no longer be seen and they all followed the same path.
