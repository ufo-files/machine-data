Sighting of a mid to dark grey oval object moving in a tight clockwise circular pattern above the west sid

This object was viewed from the east shoreline at the narrows on Baptiste Lake Alberta at 8:35pm August 11, 2021. I noticed the object in the sky while fishing for walleye along the shoreline. The object was a mid to dark gray color and it slowly hovered over the west hills traveling in a clockwise circular motion like circle staying above the treeline for about 3 minutes. I took the picture with my IPhone 11 to which it disappeared shortly there after by just phasing out. It did not zoom away at all, but only noticed the green illumination after viewing the picture afterwards. I am not really sure why it illuminated at that exact moment.
Steve Meredith Interviewed by Wayne Richey
