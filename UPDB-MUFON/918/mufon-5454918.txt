{tjd} 45+ lights ( satellite sized ) move in line

At or around 7:55 pm, on Friday March 6th 2020.

I’m a U.S Navy Veteran who has spent time at sea. I’ve seen planes and all sorts of objects in the sky. Friday March 6th 2020, 7:55pm I went out on to the deck at a friends house to let the dog outside and get fresh air. As I began to stretch I looked out to the night sky and noticed what I thought to be a satellite coming over the house. Then I noticed another one maybe 4-6inches following behind it. I called for my friend to come and see this too. As I continued to watch over the next 25min I noticed about 45 satellite sized objects move in a military like formation moving southwest to northeast. Not all of them would be evenly spaced apart. When I sat and focused on one at a time I could see that they would move side to side but only slightly. One of them, while flying in formation, moved the equivalent of a quarter inch from left to right. As I continued to watch, I could start to see bigger gaps in the line of flying objects. Panning the sky to look for more anomalies I noticed a plane come for out of nowhere in the distance, and cut across the path of the flying objects. As I looked closer I saw another satellite sized object fly parallel across the line of flying objects. At the end of the line of 45+ satellite sized objects I noticed 2 flying extremely close. Much closer than any of them flying together in line. These two were maybe less than a finger with apart with one trailing back and to the side, like a escort. While this is all happening I noticed a lot of dogs in the neighborhood were barking. I called a friend who lives about 12 miles south of me to go look and try and confirm what I was seeing. We were collaborating in real time over the phone of both of us the same objects flying the same path I described.
