{tjd} I saw an lighted object that looked like a star except motion to the south, from the north the object seemed to cloak and disappear

I saw an object that appeared to be a star except for the motion, it was in the north and was moving to the south. I was outside in my back yard. I was looking at the stars when I noticed that the craft had no red and green marker lights, it appeared to be internally lit, after maybe 4 minutes it just started to dim until it vanished. This happened around 4 o'clock am. I went back outside at 5:20 am and as I was looking at the sky I noticed an object at 90 degrees to the horizon going from the east to the west and as it moved to directly above my location it turned to the north and went towards the north, under the big dipper and eventually went out of sight. This object also appeared to be internally lit and had no strobe lights. I have been seeing these objects for awhile now. I wish I had a way to record these crafts or whatever they are.
