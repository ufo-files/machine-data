Scary

The date I gave was very approximate. I was driving southbound on highway99. The day was coming to an end, it was clear and I was listening to 1530 AM radio station . Commander Bill, our local traffic news tracker, was just on and was giving a traffic update. All of a sudden I see a bright streak of green light flash to my right . This light looks like a falling light and it is a straight bright green light. At the moment I see it, commander bill also sees it and he says something to the effect of " did you see that?! Oh my god! What was that?!" I called my boyfriend expecting the impact, to say goodbye and I love him. Commander bill is still on the air and he reports that whatever this light is, he is getting reports that people in Canada, Australia and New York saw the light. I have been searching for YEARS! to find out about this light and have been unsuccessful. I know I saw it and I know commander bill saw it as did hundreds of others. What was this light? The light "fell" towards the earth and I thought it was a nuclear attack. And the light vanished at the horizon
