{jpg} Taking night pictures of Mt. hamilton, waiting for lunar eclpse

We were waiting for the lunar eclipse to appear and I decided to take some pictures of the crowd gathered along the road. We never saw any lights, etc except for the occasional car lights coming down the mountain. The pictures were taken with a Canon T5, F-11, 1/125, timer was set for 2 sec. While going thru the pictures and sharpening them some, I saw these 2 strange shapes along the skyline. The 3rd picture does not show them anymore. I honestly did not see anything but obviously my camera did. My lense is brand new and there isn't any dust on it. I'm gobsmacked!
