{rjl} Green glowing disc over head

When I was about 6 years old, I decided to camp out in a tent with my two cousins during the summer right down below my house in a flat bottom area, with a clearing. The incident was up into the night, probably before midnight. My cousins and I were wrestling in the tent where the next thing I fell out of the opening of the tent on my back. The first thing that I saw was a green glowing disc, like a glow stick, without apparent form, kind of fuzzy, directly positioned over my head, as if it were observing me. I could not make out it’s dimensions, other than it was circular. I couldn’t tell how far over head it was but after three seconds of hovering above me then darting to the east, doing a sharp zig to the right, zag to the left, again, and again, going over the tree line, and out of sight, I figured that it was at least tree top high.
