{rjl} Very bright circular starlight object adjacent to commercial aviation vapor trail

Awaiting boarding at the Des Moines Airport and observed looking skyward from the terminal a very bright starlight object. Appeared stationary and larger then a typical evening star Three other witnesses observed object also. Orb or object was above the cloud layer as it would disappear from the passing clouds. From my limited vantage point the object appeared stationary.
