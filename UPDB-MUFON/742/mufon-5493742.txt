{rjl} Bright lights over Phoenix Arizona.

I was driving a tractor trailer west bound through Phoenix Az. All of the sudden the sky in front of me, behind the buildings and other structures, turned the most amazing bright blue color. It only lasted for a few seconds but it covered the entire sky in front of me. It almost looked as if an atomic bomb had been dropped on the city and this was this was the chemical aftermath of the bomb but it only lasted a very short time. I am positive that I am not the only one to have witnessed this.
