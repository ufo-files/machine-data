{rjl} Glowing orb witnessed on two separate occasions in one week, in roughly same location.

Just after 6pm on 1/22/20 I looked out an upstairs window and noticed a light in the sky to the west that appeared to be moving (bobbing up and down and swaying left and right. Aside from objects that were obviously airplanes, there was nothing else in the sky that looked like it. I videoed the object for over 18 minutes and observed it for over an hour until I lost sight of it farther to the west.

As I was returning home just after 7:45pm on 1/25/20, I noticed the light in the same part of the sky as before. I made multiple shorter videos this time because the object would disappear and reappear. The glow of the object fluctuated much more than it did on 1/22. It would get really faint to the point I couldn't see it, and then get incredibly bright out of nowhere. The last video I took was about 8:15, and that was the last time I saw it.

I'm attaching a 5 minute cropped, unfiltered video clip from the 18 minute video of the first sighting, as well as a cropped and filtered version. I'm also including the original 56 second and 2 minute video of the second sighting with a cropped, filtered and slow motion version. The rapid movements of the object in the cropped videos is due to me trying to stabilize my hands.
