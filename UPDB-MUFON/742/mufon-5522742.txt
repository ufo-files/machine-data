2 star like objects, one behind the other, from one end of the clear night sky to the other, the 2nd rear object moved slightly left to right , 3rd object was 4-5 mins after and moved south then east.

At my brothers house in Tampa. I was outside on my cell ph I looked up towards north Tampa, saw 2 starlike objects in line moving south. They stayed fairly close to one another, the rear object moved left to right as it followed. The 3rd object was less than 5 mins from the same north sky moved in a southerly direction then turned east. I called my sister in law out and she witnessed it as well. Our friend came out and he witnessed the 3rd object. We were in awe. It was not an airplane or satellite. We lost the objects in the horizon.
