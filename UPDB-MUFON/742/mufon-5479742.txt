I was driving home from work on 52nd Street towards Van Buren. Looked to the left in the sky and saw what looked like four flares spiraling down from the sky. Then they all stopped together. They hovered there for a couple seconds, moved into a square shape. Then they spiraled down again for a couple seconds, then lined up in a horizontal line. I lost sight of them when I pulled into my apartment complex for a second but when I looked back up, they were gone. Not sure what I saw but it was weird. This all happend around 7:30pm tonight.
