{nd} Several glittery objects hovering near rainbow in sky

A co-worker and I were looking at a beautiful rainbow in the sky. We were cleaning a room in the hospital on the sixth floor. My co-worker asked me if I saw the same thing that she saw....some blinking lights in the sky far away, but near the rainbow. She wanted to know if she was crazy. I saw them, and told her. There were 5 to 7 objects seemingly twinkling in the day sky, near the rainbow. It was very odd. Then within a minute or so, they were gone. Then they reappeared not too far from where they were. I remember that I told her they twinkled. They seemed to be reflecting the sun, like metal does. After watching the glittery lights for a minute or so, they were gone.
