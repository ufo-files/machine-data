{rjl} A blinking light came flying slowly into view then stopped moving. It hovered about making strange motions for 7 minutes, then disappeared.

While smoking a cigarette on my back porch I saw a blinking light fly in slowly from the north and into view. It was changing colors. Then it suddenly stopped. It did not slow down. It just stopped moving. Then for several minutes it hovered blinking between yellows greens reds and white. Then it blinked blue lights and white lights and some thing like a blue beam or a blue object moving very fast, shot out of the light to the north east. The light blinked green then went back to yellows. The whole time it had been sort of making weird motions while it blinked but when it fired that beam it came to a complete halt of movement. I stood up to get a better look and saw that it was immensely high above the valley and must be quite big to make this much light. A few more minutes went by of it changing to all sorts of colors then the lights changed rapidly and it was gone. It just disappeared.
