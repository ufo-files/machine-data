A primarily star- like shape that hovered over Lake Superior near Duluth, morphing into a pencil shape and larger translucent ball.

About 10:40 p.m. I looked at the Canal web cam to check on the weather (5F, clear). I noticed what looked like bright planet off in the distance over Lake Superior. I watched it for several minutes. It didn’t move, but it changed shape, dimming and elongating several times. I looked out the window from my house on the East Hillside, 600 feet above the lake and saw it. I checked Flight Aware, and there were no helicopters in flight in the area. No sound. The object seemed to be a bit lower than my vantage point.I went back to the webcam. From there, the object seemed at about a 15 degree elevation from the lake surface.The object pulsated, faded, and elongated, but did not move. I texted my son-in-law, but they couldn’t see it from their house because of trees. Shortly before 11, it morphed into a larger translucent balloon shape (not as bright) a few times. At 11:05 it broke into two small dots and vanished. I checked twice before I went to bed at 12:30, but it wasn’t there.
