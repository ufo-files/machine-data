Red sphere or oval in sky moving west to east, then vertically out of sight.

Laying in bed looking out of the skylight last night at 11:33 PM , I saw a roundish red object moving across the night sky from west to east. At first I thought it was a sattelite but it was too big and moving too fast. I pointed it out to my girlfriend and we watched it for a several tens of seconds. Before it went over the horizon, it appeared to take off vertically and I lost sight of it. I looked at a video on this sight from yesterday during fireworks and it looks to be the same object.
