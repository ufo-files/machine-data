{tjd} Collection of aircraft-like lights, forming a faint disc outline, flying north in a slow, steady path.

While trying to fall asleep in my bedroom, I looked out my window (which faces West) and saw the UFO. There were four (4) pairs of yellow-white lights, forming an octagon with a vaguely round outline, which seemed to glow a faint reddish-orange. UFO was flying around 1,500 to 2000 feet high and was over a mile away. The object was flying North at a speed comparable to a SH-60 Seahawk helicopter. Initially, I had assumed the object to be an aircraft coming in to land at the Mayport Naval Air station a few miles north, but the flight path for landing and take off is South-West to North East, not South to North. Whats more is that the lights did not match any aircraft I have ever seen, which is a lot since I have a keen interest in aviation. Said object did not make any noise either, which eliminates the possibility of an aircraft.
