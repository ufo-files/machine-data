{nd} Bright orange light, moved slowly for a minute W to E then vanished.

Appearing brighter than a planet or star, a large orange light moved slowly from west to east for approximately one minute, then vanished. I was standing outside smoking a cigarette, watching the lights of many airplanes (I live about two miles from the Charlotte airport), when I noticed the light. It did not pulse or waver, the light being bright and steady. When it vanished, the light simply went out. It was the speed of the object which attracted my attention, being slower than a satellite or airplane. The size was also interesting being so much larger than a star or planet, but seeming to be extremely high up. It moved in a straight line, and it's speed remained consistent.
