{rjl} White cigarette shaped object swiftly flying across the sky

A girl and I were loading funiture in the back of a pick-up truck when I happened to glance in the sky. I immediately noticed a small cigarette shaped craft with no lights or wings in the SE direction of the sky. I was so excited to see this because i have seen things in the sky here before but never had another witness or able to take a picture or record a video. I asked If she too was seeing the samething that I was seeing. She said yes. I quickly ran and grabbed my phone and started to record this cigarette shaped object in the SE direction in of the sky. It was ascending and descending a bit, not like any airplane. She noticed and stated that there were no lights, wings, or sound coming from this thing. I was so amazed to see what I was seeing. I could barely hold the phone still. I live by an airport and see airplanes every day fly at every altitude imaginable. This object that I seen didn't resemble any airplane or any type of craft that would normally be seen in our sky! The object then started to fly directly east and out of sight as though it just disappeared before my eyes. It was gone.  I do have a video but it is to large to be sent from my phone. I tried 3 times. I contacted a lady at MUFON by the name of Linda Flechtner. She told me to fill out the report and mention that I DO HAVE A VIDEO BUT THE FILE IS TO LARGE TO SEND FROM MY PHONE. 
I hope to hear back from you guys. I'm so thankful to have a place to report the sightings I've seen without being criticized. 
Thanx MUFON you guys are GREAT!!!
