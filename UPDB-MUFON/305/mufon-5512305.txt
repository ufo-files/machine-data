Saw a very bright white light, looked like a helicoper search light but this did not flicker at all, was perfect white light like it was not moving

Walking home from my garage when i came back from hospital (from visiting), on the way back, saw a very bright white light, appeared much brighter than a helicopter search light, however unlike a search light it did not flicker like it was attached to a flying machine. Just appeared in the sky to move quiet slowly, it didn't seem to emit any light on the ground or surrounding area, however wasn't close enough to tell.

I am not sure what the item was, if it was a flying machine it had a very good grasp of moving through the air as no machine vibration was showing in the light source, it wasn't a plane as it was way to large with no noise and to slow.

As an on looker i couldn't only watch, and to my surprise it was in the paper so others saw the same item.
