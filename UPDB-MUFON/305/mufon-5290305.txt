Spinning Lights from behind clouds close to lightening

Saw 3 light beams shining out from behind cloud from the western sky, towards the clouds where lightening was coming from southwest at the time. Couldn't see sources of lights as all behind a big lump of thick cloud. As I was watching, the light beams start spinning, forming a perfect circle. By then it was impossible to tell how many lights actually were there behind the cloud.. There were lots of lighting around that time of the night. those lights are much closer to the lightning than from where I was.
Then I heard airplane noise and looked to the source of the noise to my left, saw an aircraft with flickering lights flying from south towards the cloud where the spinning lights were. as I followed the aircraft flying past below the cloud. By that time those spinning lights had already disappeared. That's when I noticed the cloud was much higher than it appeared to be. In the meantime there was another aircraft flying from the opposite direction to the first one.
