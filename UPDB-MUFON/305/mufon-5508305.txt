Bright Object travelling very fast

My partner and I travelled to Massey Ontario, Canada were we were scouting film locations for a current project. On August 22nd, 2013, we were camping at my partner's mom's cabins by Toulouse Bay on the Sagamok First Nation. At 11:30 pm we were enjoying a fire and discussing the day's activities. When I looked up, I commented on how you can see the stars so clearly when your out of the city. It was then I saw a bright light moving at a high rate of speed. I quickly grabbed my Sony HDR-HC3 camera which had a wide angle lens on it already and began to film this object. I filmed for approximately 2 minutes and do not know what this object is. After showing the footage to various members of the community, there have been other reports of strange objects in the sky. What is this that we filmed?
