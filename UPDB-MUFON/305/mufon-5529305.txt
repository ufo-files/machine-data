Star like object over Cape Cod Bay

The star like shaped object appeared and then hovered in place for a minute or two over Cape Cod Bay at Paine's Creek Beach, then dropped straight down in a line and hovered in place for about another minute or two. The object got brighter. Then moved straight up and hovered awhile and then moved very fast to the Southeast and finally disappeared. The object made no noise and was not an airplane and or a helicopter. Myself and two freinds viewed it and other three people saw it and we all were saying Wow, how strange, it's definitely a UFO. Everyone agreed and was awestruck.
