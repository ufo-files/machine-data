Vertical reflective cylinder

About 7:05 a.m. on the morning of the 3rd of January , while I was getting ready to start my day, looking out my living room window I saw a cylinder in vertical position anywhere between 500 ft and up under the cloud cover , just about the crack of dawn. The sun periodically was in and out starting to come up and it showed a sort of reflective surface. It hovered for just a couple of seconds , then it descended about 50 feet for a few seconds then it ascended up through the clouds no longer seen . I don't know what this was, but I look for something above it to be suspending it and saw nothing . I opened up my front door and heard nothing and then it was gone. No vapor trail from propulsion, no sound , nothing. I couldn't explain it with any logical reasoning, therefore to me it was a UFO.
