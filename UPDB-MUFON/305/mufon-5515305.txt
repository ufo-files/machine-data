yesterday around 7:45pm it got hazy due to what appeared to be a cold front coming in but didn't hear about it in the news. Again it was a bit hazy, low clouds but visibility was good. This thing was huge. It looked like a golf ball size at arms length. Like a beam of light breaking the clouds coming at my direction, then it turned off, slowely, it began to disappear until it wasnt visible. The light was very near and very low Id say around 1000 ft or less. Im directly in the flight path of planes landing at our airport and I know the flight path on final is 1000 feet lowering to 500 ft so this thing was in that ball park and it was huge.
