{rjl} Very fast moving object very high up

I witnessed a small bundle of white and grey lights moving extremely fast and very high up. I was on my balcony 23 stories up and I see airplanes all night up here, but this thing was higher than a plane and moving very fast. I could make out 3 lights on it and it looked black. What got me was how fast it cleared my view! I have an unobstructed view of the entire valley and these lights cleared half of it in approximately 6 seconds. The valley is the Vegas valley and normal planes I see up near that height don1t get anywhere near the speed this thing did.
