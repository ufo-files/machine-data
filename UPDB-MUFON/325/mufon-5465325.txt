Metallic silver, rounded sides, boomerang shaped, silent, no emmisions

I was standing in front of my house. I do not remember where I came from or what I was doing. The experience starts with me looking up at the sky (facing North). There were 5 boomerang shaped objects overhead in the classic 'flying V' formation. All objects were the same shape, size and colour - metallic silver. The objects made no sound, nor did they leave a vapor trail or exhaust. There were no clouds in the sky.

The sighting took place in on a bright cloudless day. My first memory is seeing the Northern lights over my house - which is really odd as the northern lights do not come as far South a Lake Ontario. 
I stood watching the objects fly North and had the overwhelming feeling of saying goodbye to the objects.

I do not remember any previous contact or being abducted, only the feeling of watching them fly away and saying goodbye.

The objects were rounded on the edges and appeared to have no hard edges. There were no windows or lights; just the bright metallic silver over the entire object.

I watched the objects fly North for about 5 minutes.
