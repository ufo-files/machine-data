Globe shaped UFO descends, then ascends, then descends again and finally ascends and vanishes.

On January 15, 2012 1735 Hours, I was the passenger of a vehicle, driven by my General Manager. I was using night vision goggles to look at Mount Shasta as we were driving by this mountain. With the night vision goggles, I saw nothing spectacular. But, as I continued to look at the sky and the mountain, all of a sudden a globe shaped UFO descended from the sky, then it quickly ascended, then descended again and ascended one more time and blinked out. The UFO did a W maneuver in the night sky. My General Manager who was driving did not see the UFO. I would estimate that the UFO was right near Mt. Shasta and we were approximately 30 miles away from the mountain and UFO.
