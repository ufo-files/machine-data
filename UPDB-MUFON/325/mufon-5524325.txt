over sevral days multiple star like objects moving in various directions and speeds. a dark grey triangular object seen on one occasion.

My wife and i first noticed the lights at home on 8/31/2011. At first we just saw what we thought was a meteor until it suddenly made a change in direction. At that point we started watching more closely. That night we saw several more star like lights move quickly in one direction then move in various random directions. We have watched the skies all but one night since the initial sighting, seeing objects each time 4 days in a row. We have witnessed the lights in a v formation, flashing randomly, and on one occasion a dark triangular shaped object passed over head into the distance. Each time the objects fade out of view quickly but occur with regularity. In all we've seen more than a dozen different objects at different times and counting.
