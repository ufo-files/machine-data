On June 5, 2020 at approximately 10:30 pm as I was driving up the hill in my apartment complex when I observed a triangle shaped object traveling at a unusual low speed making no noise whatsoever. Due to my 4 year experience working as bomb loader in USAF I could not Identify the aircraft according to the current inventory of known US military aircraft it was traveling too slow to be a jet and no noise when flying at low speeds the object was about the size of 2 city blocks. I couldn’t get my phone out in time so I followed it for as far as I could before it flew out of my line of sight. Similar an appearance to a TR3B astra.
