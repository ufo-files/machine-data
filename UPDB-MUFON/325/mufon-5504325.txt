{cak} A black triangle with large circular lights on each point

I was with my friend heading home from the movies, and when we reached the road he lived on we saw a strange triangular object with large circular lights on each point. It was just off the road and hovering over the trees. There was a house about 30 feet away from the craft.My friend who was driving stopped the car about 100 feet before the craft. We both got out of the vehicle to get a better look. We were both stunned. I proceeded around the front of the car to get a better look. Then I decided to walk towards the craft. I'd say half way I turned around and asked my friend to turn the car off. At first he refused but gave in. I asked him if he could hear the noise the craft was making which sounded like a hum a transformer would make. I decide to get closer to the object I'd say i was ten feet away. I stood there and watched it hover over the trees for about 10 or so minutes. Then I decided to grab some gravel and throw stones at it cause I felt threatened. As soon as I lifted my arm to throw the rocks the craft shut it lights off and very slowly turned to the left. The road was curving to the right and then straightened. After turning left it started fly down the road but never changing altitude until about 1/4 mile down the road it accelerated at such high speed almost a blink of an eye kind of speed and it was heading north at a 45 degree angle. No exhaust was left behind and it made no sound when it accelerated. Changed my life and i never seen anything like it since and I was 16 teen at the time and nowI am 36.
