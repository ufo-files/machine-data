{cak} Witnessed a triangle over Dublin Ca.

Driving through Dublin on 680. My wife and I both saw three lights. She only saw the lights for a moment because she was driving and traffic was fairly heavy (there's no way we could have been the only ones to see it). At first the lights were horizontal just as any plane viewed from the side. It began to rotate side over side in place. I didn't see an actual craft because it was too dark out at the time but the lights stayed in perfect distance from each other the entire time. Hovering with no directional motion. The three lights were white and stayed on the entire time. No pulsating or flashing and no other lights of any color appeared. I took video of it on my phone that lasted probably about thirty or forty seconds. When I went to show the video to my wife it was no longer there (not the first time my phone has not saved video or photo though). It was HUGE. It was much, much larger than the buildings under it and was very low. This was near an airport and there were several planes in view at the time.
