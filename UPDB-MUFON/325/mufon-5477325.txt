{rjl} Looking like a larger drone, in a square shape with the lights in a square position.

My friend and I were walking towards a park at night. From my peripheral vision I saw what looked like a drone. I passed it off at first but then, my friend suddenly looked at the object and so did I. To my surprise this was no drone, this was too large and too fast to be one. The encounter was only a couple seconds because the speed of the "UFO" was fast. The "UFO" sped behind a building out of our sight then disappeared. We searched the area a bit to see if it could have landed, but there is no way. The "UFO" just vanished. Unfortunately I do not have any evidence of it happening, due to how quick it disappeared.
