Triangle w/3 lights

While traveling north on route 5 in Silvis, IL I looked out the windshield of the car, gf was driving, and saw what looked like bright light in the distance. As it came closer it became 3 lights (likely because when farther away the lights looked like one). I started paying more attention and rolled down the window cuz it was really low and figured I could hear something. It was completely silent as it "glided" over the vechicle i looked up and there was 3 lights on the bottom going across the triangle shaped object. The lights were horizontal in the middle of the object. As it went passed and I had no idea what it was I unbuckled my seat belt turned around sticking my upper half out of the window watching as it silently glided south almost directly above route 5. I'm half freaking out half excited, I look at the gf and she just said what was that? We turn right on 18th ave and turn around as fast as we can and start going south on route 5 trying to catch it. We get to when route 5 bends to the right and finally see it off to the left across the fields that are there. Then we lost it cuz we had trouble finding the road that would have taken us out that way. The whole time this happened not once did the lights on the object blink. I'm nowhere near an artist but I did a small sketch on a post-it note.
