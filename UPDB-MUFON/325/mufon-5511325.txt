It was dusk not dark enough for any stars in the sky and no clouds at all. I was driving down the road and through the trees lights caught my eye. At first I thought it was stars but when I could see it clearly I realized it was not dark enough for stars and there was nothing else in the sky. I could see five bright lights when I got a break through the trees I saw five bright lights three were in a perfect triangle shape. The other two were just above the bottom left point of the triangle pointing upward. After a minute or so the two pointing upward became level with the bottom left point of the triangle after a few minutes the two were gone. Now just the three in the triangle were still there. I watched them for a minute then went through the drive thru after about three minutes I looked back and they were all gone. They were pulsating a red and bluish color and looked like stars but were bigger and brighter. I was very freaked out because I have never seen anything like this ever in my life. After coming back to work from my break I overheard other people who had seen it also who had no explanation for what it could have been.
