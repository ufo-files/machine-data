{dr}I first thought it was a star but then it moved slowly and hovered with white lights and some red and orange.

At 2:00 a.m. Pacific standard time, I looked at the stars through my home window and one moved. I thought it was a plane but it stopped and hovered for a very long time, too long. It had no airplane or helicopter sound, it didn't have any sound. This was seen in San Francisco's inner Sunset District. One hour later it moved slowly towards the beach and hovered for another hour. It moved South and hovered, then moved East towards 19th Avenue and hovered. Two small planes flew by it at different times. It seemed to be higher than the planes altitude. It had white lights with some flashing red and orange. At 6:00 a.m. it was still there. It was too small to show up on my cell phone camera and video, it just came out dark. It could have been a drone, who knows.
