{nd} Slow and steady point of light no strobe lights observed like an airplane would have

I was standing in the east bound lanes of I-70 during a construction closure for replacement of Pecos St bridge around 4 am. I was scanning the sky getting ready to fire up Google Sky Map on my phone when I noticed this point of light moving in a NE direction towards DIA. My boss was with me and we watched it travel across the sky, I mentioned the lack of any flashing lights like you would see on an airplane wondering if I was just seeing airplane landing lights. The object was traveling slow and steady and I followed it until it was low on the horizon and blocked by buildings and trees. I studied it intensely to be sure it was not an airplane because this could be the third UFO I have seen in my lifetime.
