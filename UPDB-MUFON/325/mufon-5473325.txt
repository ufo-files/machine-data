{dr}Lights appeared, then traveled good distance then disappeared with no trace

Was standing on back porch smoking a cigarette and staring up at the stars like usual. Noticed a triangle shaped constellation at about the 3 o clock position away from the moon. I though that's a bright triangle, then the next thing I know I see a light manifest inside the middle of the triangle and head northwest. I yell for my wife when a second light formed in the triangle and began the exact same flight path. My wife came out and we both stared in disbelief at what we were seeing. We watched then head maybe 50 Miles northwest in less than 1 minute. At that time the lights both seemed to stop moving and just, go out. We both stood in disbelief of what we saw and kept saying "what did we just see, what the hell was that?"
