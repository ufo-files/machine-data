Sauser-like flying object with lights around it on the way back from Tuscaloosa one night.

Me and my family were on our way back from Tuscaloosa one night around ten, ten thirty P.M. when i looked out my window and saw what looked like a flying sauser with lights aluminating it. it was semi flat sort of like two plates stacked on top of one another. it was almost hovering almost right over the tree line when i noticed it. it took off fairly quickly for something so big. if it were to land, it would have taken up almost if not all and more so of my back yard. about a minute after I saw it, if not sooner, it was gone over the other tree line on the oposite side of the road and well out of sight. I was the only one who saw it because my dad was driving, my mom was talking on her phone and my both of my little brothers were asleep. I didn't tell my parents because it was late and my dad was irritated because not to much earlier, about an hour, my brothers got into a fight. I didn't tell my mother because she was talking to someone on her phone all the way home, and when we got home, I had to go to bed because it was pretty late.
