{nd} Square shaped objects with three red lights on bottom and white light on each corner

I was with my family driving south on 59 in New Caney and as we were nearing the exit we spotted a square shaped object just above the top of the trees that appeared stationary (hard to tell since we were moving). The object had three red lights in a row across the bottom that flashed one after another repeatedly. On each of the four corners there were solid white lights. My sister who was next to me was also able to get a good look, and admitted to not having an explanation, even though she is highly skeptical. My dad was driving and pulled over but at this point the trees were in the way.
