{cak} These lights were the last of more than 10 that passed over West Palm Beach FL. They appeared out of nowhere from the SE moving north and then veered gently to the east.

We were having dinner on our screened in patio just before 9pm when we noticed these bright yellow orange lights coming from the south. First one then another. It was only when we realized that they were coming continuously did we we go out to the front of the house so our view was unobstructed by the screen. Across the sky a series of lights nearly equally spaced and at varying heights and independent of one another were coming from the south toward us and then moving toward the east at a very slow pace. I began filming with my iPhone 5 and caught the last 5-6 lights as they disappeared in the east. They were at the height of where we would normally see helicopters or small planes in the area. We have seen aircraft in the area before as we have a community nearby with it's own landing strip. What we saw was very different and moved without any sound that we could discern. At first we thought that they may have come from the Morikami Japanese Gardens as on occasion they have a paper lantern lighting ceremony. These lights were much brighter and larger. I also checked with Morikami and they had no such event that evening. All told we saw more than 10 of these lights from the moment we noticed them at dinner. It was coincidentally very similar to what we had seen in the documentary SIRIUS on Netflix the night before. Very strange.
