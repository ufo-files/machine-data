Bright lights clusters

Witnessed bright lights in clusters over the ocean (East) from the beach in North Myrtle beach. First they’re was 4 in a in a trapezoid type shape then they disappeared in a flash and re appeared in a line of 6 ish. Then one by one disappeared until there was one left that seemed to hover for a while. Then disappears in a blink of the eye. One light came back but dimmer and seemed to move up and down and flew south faster then any craft I’ve ever seen. Then disappeared again. These lights were brighter then any star or airplane ever seen. There was 8 in my group and several others in the area watching these lights. Other people we have talked to state they saw them come back later on that night. I was not fast enough to film it all unfortunately and could not focus well on them.
