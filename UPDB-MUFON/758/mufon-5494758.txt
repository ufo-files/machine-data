{cak} ALL BLACK OBJECT, LOOKED LIKE BLIMP, BUT HUGE, MAYBE 300 FT LONG, AND A FEW HUNDRED FT. QIDE

MY MOM AND I WERE DRIVING IN A CAR, COMING DOWN THE HILL ON  IT WAS JULY, I REMEMBER IT WAS HOT OUT. WE WERE STUCK IN HEAVY TRAFFIC. I SAID TO MY MOM, WHAT THE HE__ IS THAT? ARE YOU SEEING WHAT I'M SEEING. SHE SAID YES. THERE WAS A SOLID BLACK OBJECT, THE SHAPE OF A BLIMP, BUT SO MUCH LARGER, SO LARGE THAT I HAVE A HARD TIME DESCRIBING IT'S LENGTH, AND WIDTH. MY BEST GUESS WOULD BE 200 FT.- 300 FT. LONG, AND A FEW HUNDRED FT. WIDE. NO WINDOWS. NO SOUND. THE OBJECT WAS GOING STRAIGHT UP IN THE AIR, UNTIL IT WAS MUCH HIGHER THAN THE EAST HILL OF RENTON, AT THAT TIME IT LEVELED OFF, AND WENT OUT OF VIEW ON THE OTHER SIDE OF THE HILL. A FEW MINUTES LATER, THE BLACK OBJECT CAME BACK OVER THE HILL, AND STARTED DESCENDING,IT APPEARED TO BE COMING STRAIGHT DOWN, AS IF IT WERE GOING TO LAND. I KNOW RENTON, WA., AND THERE WOULD NOT HAVE BEEN ANY PLACE FOR THAT LARGE OF AN OBJECT TO LAND, ESPECIALLY THE VICINITY OF WHERE IT WAS COMING DOWN. WHEN WE GOT HOME, I CALLED THE AIR TRAFFIC CONTROL AT THE RENTON AIRPORT, I TOLD THEM WHAT I HAD SEEN, I ASKED IF THERE WERE ANY OTHER REPORTS, AND THEY SAID NO. TO THIS DAY, I HAVE NO IDEA WHAT MY MOM AND I SAW.
