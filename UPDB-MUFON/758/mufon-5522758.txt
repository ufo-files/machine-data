I observed from my back porch 2 very bright stars in the sky that didn't look like the other stars at 6:37 am. on October 8, 2010 in McKinney, Texas. The 2 star like objects were set up at one being at 12 o'clock and the other at lets say the center of a clock. As I am looking at them the object at 12 oclock moved very fluidly as if on a clock to the 1,3, and then stopping at the 6 oclock position. As soon as the object stopped the center object got brighter and ascended up and backwards at what I can only describe as an unbelievable rate of speed and was gone in the blink of an eye. The object at the 6 oclock position stayed in the same position until the sun came up and I lost site of it. I was looking up at about 1/3 to 1/2 of the way up in the sky and was facing NNW. I sit on my back porch alot and I sit on a flight path to DFW airport so I see alot of planes and even helicopters on occasion but seeing what I saw that morning was the strangest thing I have ever seen in my life. What was so eery about it was the fluid clock like motion and how perfect the circular movement was. That morning was just over a year ago and its if it just happened yesterday, I believe its one of those instances that is seared in your memory and just can't be forgotten.
