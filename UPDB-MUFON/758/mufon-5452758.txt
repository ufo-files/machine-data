Entertaining atmosphere

In summer of 1976 I was 14, 3 friends and I were riding bikes up on a hill top. It was a new subdivision outside San Antonio, my father was in the Air Force at the time. The subdivision only had 4 streets at the time, it was out a moonless night and there were no streetlights , just the stars above. We weren’t really doing anything just hanging out, talking about the next school year, looking at the cloudless starry night. We were just about to head home because of the time when we saw something flying thru the night sky. We knew it wasn’t an airplane or a helicopter because it had pulsing lights along the bottom with a trail of light behind it, not a long trail of light but what when it hit the atmosphere it lit up the night sky like it was high noon. We freaked out, I told them since my dad was an officer maybe he could call someone at Lackland AFB. We raced down the hill to my house and when we got there all our parents were outside, we all lived within three houses of each other, they asked if we were ok and if we knew what had happened or saw anything. We told them what we saw so my dad made a call and he was told it was a weather ballon, yeah right. The next day it was all over the news but no one said anything about seeing lights in the sky, we talked about it like teenagers do and there was no other conclusion other than it wasn’t from this world and it darn sure wasn’t no weather balloon or meteor or airplane.
