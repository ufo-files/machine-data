{rjl} view from International Space Station-on camera

While watching a live feed from ther ISS a few weeks ago, I noted an object slowly become more visable and saved a few picture views from the live view. quickly, thereafter the live view camera was interupted and a signal loss message came up. I can't be sure what it is, and may be very normal for the ISS, but it appears as a type of craft to me, although somewhat dark and blurry, There are two photos and in one the object is more pronounced. There may also be an object faintly visable on the far left aspect of the view. The object in question is visable just under a solar panel attached to a Soyez vehicle I believe is docked to the station. I watched the live view for approximately 20 minutes before this became visable to me and saw nothing prior to it becomming visable. As stated, the live feed was interupted, consequently I was unable to see anything further.
