{jpg} Three circular white/silver objects hovering in place in the sky for 10 minutes after two planes fly by and vanished from exact same spot they appeared from.

I was lying in the backyard of my home and watched a plane fly across the horizon and turn to fly back the other way. I thought that was unusual. I kept watching it fly across and after the con trail dissipated, I noticed a shiny dot in the sky that just seemed to hover. My first thought was that the plane dropped something out of the sky. But the object just stayed in place while I was watching it. I called to my husband and he noticed another dot to the right of the one I spotted right along the path that the plane took. He ran in to grab the binoculars. We took a closer look with the binoculars and they both were circular shapes, white and silver in color and just hovered in the same spot in the sky for about 10 minutes. We grabbed a camera and caught a picture of one. Within the 10 minutes that transpired, another plane flew by and we spotted another object along that route of the same type. All three objects vanished from the spot they were at within the ten minutes that we first viewed them.
