{cak} I saw a reddish orb light up in the sky and then a 2nd a 3rd. All of the orbs appeared to be the same size, shape and color. The orbs all remained directly next to one another for approx. 25 seconds and then all 3 orbs disappeared

I was sitting on my back porch and out of the corner of my eye, right above the treetops to the east of my house I saw a reddish/orange orb light up in the sky. Not long after I saw a 2nd and 3rd light up one right after the other in a horizontal line. The three orbs stayed lit together for approx. 25 seconds and then at all once they disappeared.
