Triangular aircraft flying over apartments towards Tecumseh Stamping

This event occured back in early 90s [item moved/CMS/nd] I was walking across the apartment complex from my place to my girlfriends about 1 am. I don't know what made me look but when i looked back towards my apartment building i noticed 3 triangular lights moving very slowly and quietly moving towards local busniess [item moved/CMS/nd] ( i think that was its name).I stood there for what seemed like an hour just watching it. I could make out a silouette that the craft was triangular on the base with 3 triangular lights at each of the peaks of the shape. I could also see sillouette with the factory lights in the skyline that the craft also had a pyrimid shape on top but not straight, more of a flattened and slanted pyrimid, its hard to describe in words but i can see it as if i just saw it last night. I will include my best rendering of it as i can, im not the best artist but i can still pitcure it in my head any time. I saw no lights on it other then those i could see on the bottom. My first thought when i think about this craft is maybe was some kinda stealth fighter like the B2 Bomber or something but the slowness and silentness of it make me think otherwise. I don't claim this to be alien but judging from what tech we as humans have and are experimenting with i don't think it was man made.
