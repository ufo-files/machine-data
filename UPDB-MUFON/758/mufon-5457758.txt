{tjd} Saucer materialized out of thin air a few feet off the ground, hovered, then shot off to the right leaving a trail

In a nutshell, i saw a saucer shaped UFO materialize out of thin air about 25 feet in front of me. It was completely silent. I was up on a deck over a sloping back yard, so it was at eye level even though it seemed to be maybe 7 feet off the ground. It hovered for a what seemed like a few minutes, then shot off to the right leaving a trail. It must have dematerialized as it shot away, because it was below the tree line of the woods and it didn't crash.
