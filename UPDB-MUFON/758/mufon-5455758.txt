{rjl} Sphere shaped dark craft with a white light emitting from under center was hovering stationary for 2 and 1/2 hours also began pulsating color lights

I was walking home from a friends house late on a summer night. I was walking theough the park to go home when i was looking up at the moon when i noticed a light next to the moon. I made out a dark spherelike object that the light was coming from. Immediately i knew this was a stationary craft of some sort so, i began filming because i had my cannon powershot camera/video recorder in my backpack. I filmed as i walked down to my house at which time i went in and retrieved 2 family members to come out and look at this. They were as perplexed as i was here was this blatant craft hovering silently a couple hundred feet up next to a 65 to 75% illuminated waxing gibbeous moon. You could make out the outline of the dark craft because of the moonlight glistening off of it. At one point the craft began pulsating different colored lights. I filmed this for around 2and 1/2 hours at that time the craft shot directly up and vanished without.a sound.
