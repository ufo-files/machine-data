Bright light to the right of Venus begins to move fast and fades.

I was walking from my house to the barn to feed my horses. I stopped at my car to get my jacket. I was glancing to the west down the driveway looking at Venus and Jupiter that were very bright that night. I noticed another star to the right of Venus. I thought it was pretty so i stopped to look at it for a second. Star gazing is great to do in Arizona. Anyway as i was looking at them the star to the right of Venus started to move to the north. It went very fast and as it moved it faded until it couldnt be seen. It was very bright. Almost as bright as Venus. I was sorta wierded out by it.
