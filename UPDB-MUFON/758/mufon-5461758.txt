{rjl} 180 foot long cigar shaped UFO observed hovering over I-5 in Tangent, OR.

180 foot long cigar shaped UFO observed hovering over I-5. Object was very shiny, silvery, almost liquid-like, and very bright, reflective. I and the person I was with both observed this, asked each other, "Are you seeing this?!?". We stared at this beautiful thing hovering silent while you could hear the traffic on I-5 passing by for about 5 minutes. It will sound strange, but I had the sensation that the object was trying to fool my eyes that it was't there, but my brain saw it anyway. It looked partly transparent, like it was using some sort of visual cloaking technology. The brightness of the reflection made it hard to look at, had to squint. 
After about five minutes of watching the object, we both glanced at each other in amazement, and when we glanced back, it was gone. No trace, no sound, we didn't see it move away. It was like it had disintegrated. But maybe it was just cloaked and we couldn't see it. I was amazed, it's curious that such a large object could hover over the major roadway in the state without notice. Nobody on the highway seemed to notice it at all.
