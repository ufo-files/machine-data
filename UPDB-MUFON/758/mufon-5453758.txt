{cak} 3+ glowing orbs

Ever since February 11, 2020 my fiance' and I have been observing at least 3 or more glowing orbs prominently displaying very bright lights quite close to our house. I can't say how far or close exactly but I would say approximately 1/4 - 1/2 miles. They constantly change direction and elevation though so it's difficult to keep a solid standing eye on them for very long. Some of them will be stationary for a bit while the others move around randomly. Then they seem to switch and the others do the same at various times and directions. They change colors as well. Glowing from white to red to orange, yellow, blue and so on.
