I put this video on my youtube account ([Account name removed/cms/tg]) last night because I wanted to get it out as fast as I could. 

My son, wife and I were watching a movie and my wife left to let the dogs out. After a few minutes she came to the bottom of the stairs and told me to come outside quickly. I asked her if everything was OK, she said yes but hurry. On the way out, she said she saw something in the sky and didn't know what it was. When I got outside I saw the objects and I felt right away that they were UFOs. I recognized them right away as flying triangles and they were clear to see with the naked eye, more so than shows up on my video. She said that three crafts had passed while she was outside with the dogs and she came in right away to get me. My son got a camera and we started the video. We watched as the crafts came from the west in succession, equally spaced apart. They were silent and were quite low to the ground. As they travelled north, they climbed substantially higher in the sky very quickly, and very silent. All together about 6-8 crafts went by, spaced exactly apart. I am unsure how many came by when she was in the house getting me, but it was less than a minute. Eventually they all disappeared to the north. 

I felt very excited, even elated to see a UFO personally. I was not scared, and went outside later that night to the same spot and just watched the sky for some time to see if anything would come back, but it did not. My wife and son were more skeptical about what it was, but did say that they have no explanation for it. I have private pilot experience and have trained to recognize light configurations of airplanes coming and going and crossing my path. These lights didn't match color, configuration, or intensity of any aircraft that I have known. They were very bright, constantly changing color, and seemingly shape. It was an incredible experience.
