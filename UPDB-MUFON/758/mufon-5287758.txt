Meteor like descent that suddenly went still.

Driving home from work I noticed what I thought was a meteor. The object was bright and had a small trail of light as it descended toward the horizon. Suddenly it stopped descending and hovered. It was a ball of light at this point. I muttered to myself "What the fuck...," I continued to observe it while driving on Middle Line road heading towards Charing Cross at 12:41am. The light appeared very far away, I estimate its actual location was at Jeanettes Creek or over Lake St Clair approx. 500-800 ft in the air. After 2-3 minutes the light slowly fizzled out.

I checked my dash cam and unfortunately the light source wasn't bright enough to pick up though it did briefly show on film but appears to be a very faint star before fading away again.
