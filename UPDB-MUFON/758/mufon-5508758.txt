{dr}TWO SETS OF AMBER LIGHTS IN TRIANGLE SHAPE, MOVING SLOW AND DISSAPPEARED OVER CLOUD, NITE WAS CLEAR

ON MY PORCH TALKING TO MY FRIEND, AUG 10 2013 WHEN I SEEN AMBER LIGHTS MOVING SLOW ABOVE THE NEIGHBORHOOD. THEN A SECOND SET FOLLOWED THE FIRST TRIANGLE SPACED LIGHTS AND WENT INTO CLOUDS. I WENT TO GET MY CAMERA AND THEY WERE GONE.. I THOUGH THAT THE AMBER LIGHTS ODD FOR COMMERCIAL AIR CRAFT. AND THE I THOUGHT HOW ODD THEY WERE FLYING SO CLOSE TOGETHER. THE MOVEMENT WAS CALM AND DELIBERATE. I WAS IN AWE OF THE SIGHT AND KEPT REPORTING WHAT I WAS SEEING TO MY FRIEND.
