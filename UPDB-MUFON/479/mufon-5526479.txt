While taking a hike, I saw a triangle of 8 lights in the sky above Mt. Umunhum.

Me and my friends were hiking up a popular trail in our community, Mt. Umunhum. We sat down to take a drink and eat the 3 bagels I had packed, I updated my Facebook status from the spot using location, so my location at this time was (37.16373,-121.898511). I had finished my food when I looked up at our subsequent destination through a clearing of trees, that's when I saw a large shadow covering the monument on top of the Mountain, I wondered, "What on Earth could make such a big shadow?". So I sprinted a few yards ahead onto a rock to get a better view, initially I didn't see it, but I soon began to make out a triangle or square in the sky through connecting these hovering lights. There were around 9 lights that were arranged in a triangular or square way, the lights were yellow. They cast a shadow all the way across the peak, then shimmered away. By this time my friends had walked to me and seen it too.
