almost boomerang shaped craft with two lights on each side

i was looking out my window at around 3:50 am since it was a rather clear night and the stars were visible, nothing happened for a few minutes when as i was looking at a cluster of stars the craft just appeared and flew in a straight line above my house, it wasnt like anything ive seen before..i have seen lights in the sky of many variations but i have never seen something so large, it was either black or reflective (hard to tell since it was really dark and appeared out of nowhere) and was in a smooth boomerang shape from what i could tell, there were two white lights on each side and a faint glowing trail that quickly disappeared behind it.
