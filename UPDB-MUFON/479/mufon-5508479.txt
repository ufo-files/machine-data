Red And Blue light hovered over el cajon, ca

I was sitting in my car with a friend waiting for another friend on the corner of s anza st and east main st i opened the sunroof to the car to smoke a cigarette and first i saw a very dim white object moving slowly across the sky thats what caught my attention at first. i thought the first object was a plane but i don't know but then i saw a object at first it looked like a police helicopter with red and blue flashing lights then i noticed that it was not even close to a helicopter because of the movement the object was doing in the sky.. it started going super fast in a crazy line up and down left and right in a matter of seconds! i was stunned and i could not believe what i was witnessing and i asked my friend if he just saw the same thing i saw and he was shocked that he had seen what i had just seen.. the object keep flying around the el cajon sky and after 30 minutes the object started moving towards space and you could still see the red and blue light from it it just kept going further and further toward space... after an hour i left and i tried to go to the nearest hill/mountain to see if it was still visible we drove to mt helix in la mesa, ca but the object was too far in space but you could still see the red and blue light that was coming from the object
