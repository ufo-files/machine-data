Different ,,strange colours

I was was on the way to home with a good friend after a birthday party. On the way I saw different colors coming from the forest behind the medical centre. I thought its a laser show being reflected from the centre to the woods. The objects were round, changed their colors, moved in different sides, always staying on the top middle of a tree, which was over 20-25 m high on the top of a hill. I firstly tried to see if the lights are reflected from somewhere, tried to find an logical answer. I've never seen lights and objects like this before. We were staying for a long time just watching the movements and the changes of the colors. I then did a video, you can see the movements, but unfortunately the colors cant be identified.
