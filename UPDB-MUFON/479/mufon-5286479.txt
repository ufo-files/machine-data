Two unmoving white lights which had smaller craft with a white base and redish blinking lights on either side from main body eject.

I have seen numerous accounts of white, yellow, and red blinking lights in the sky ever since I first moved into my apartment complex in Lagrange. These lights are slow moving, seeming to follow a very specific flight pattern and towards a specific place. At certain times after seeing one, they will begin to blink in and out of existence and then simply vanish. However, I do have a good bit recorded and photographed from my phone from the time I first saw one until tonight.
