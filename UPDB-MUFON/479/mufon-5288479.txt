Solid red light, hover then zoom, stop, zoom, hover

We pulled over to take a nap on the way back home on our honeymoon. We had to take a nap. We pulled over on the side of the Parks Highway somewhere north of Denali. We were looking at the stars out the window and noticed a bright red light hovering in the air a ways up there. We watched it zoom one way for about a couple inches across the sky one way, then stop abruptly and hover, then zoom over at a different angle, then stop and hover, then zoom some more. I could see it clearly against the stars. I rested my head against the motor home (which was not running) so that I could hold my head still and watch the light. It kept moving in different directions though it stayed in a small section of sky. My husband and I kept watching it until we fell asleep. We both observed it flying, stopping, hovering, zooming an inch or two one direction then stop abruptly then switch directions and suddenly zoom another inch or two across the sky. It would start and stop moving abruptly. Never seen a lighted object fly around so abruptly like that. We watched it for approximately 30 mins or longer until we fell asleep. The date is approximate, though It was the 3rd or 4th week of Aug. 2019. It was dark out with lots of stars and no visible cloud cover. It was a bright red circular solid light that was larger than the size of the stars. It was over a mile away for sure. Different angles of travel in different directions though it stayed in that section of sky.
