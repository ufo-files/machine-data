{cak} blinking light came closer to another light and got brighter, large opened, small flew in, fireball.

I was sitting outside on my front porch swing smoking a cigarette listening to my kids play inside and looking at the stars. I noticed a very bright star which seemed to be getting closer/brighter. Farther off to the right, I saw what I thought at the time was an airplane with a blinking red light flying at a high rate of speed. The closer the plane came to the star, the brighter each light became. The flashing red light stopped flashing and became a brilliant white light when it seemed to be ready to crash into the other light. I started yelling at my children to get their dad; I thought I was going to witness a crash. It seemed as though this was directly over the field adjacent to my home. The larger light (bright star) was stationary throughout. When the two were almost ready to crash, what looked like a C-130 cargo door opened and the other craft flew in. There was no sound, but a large red/orange fireball. I ran into the house, looking throught the windows the whole way, to find my husband. By the time he looked out the window, the fireball was gone. After this incident, I sat out there every night looking at the stars until we moved. I have never before, or since, witnessed anything like this.
