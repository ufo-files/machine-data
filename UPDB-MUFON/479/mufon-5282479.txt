Videotaping a redtailed hawk and a dove photobombed my video. After reviewing the video I found a dot in between the two birds.

Upon reviewing the video of the birds, I noticed a dot in between them. I took a screen shot so I could expand the frame and found this dot to be tic tac in shape with nothing extruding from it. I further reviewed the screen shot and found I had 5 frames of this object in it. This object was not seen with the naked eye, only by freezing the frame. I found that tracking the 5 frames, the object was captured on video all within 13/100 ths of a second. I don't know of anything that travels that fast. The pictures have the red tail hawk and morning dove as reference points to show nothing was doctored up. I've never seen anything like it in real life.
