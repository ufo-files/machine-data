{nd} saw what appeared to be star, suddenly started moving e to se then joined by a 2nd starlike object. joined by 3rd trailing to the left. dissappeared suddenly.

My daughter and I were sitting in the backyard by a fire when I was noticing what appeared to be 2 stars below the moon. I then noticed that the lower star started moving left to right and quickly noticed another star-like light below it suddenly appear and seem to be moving in unison. At that point I called it to my daughters attention and said "look at those lights, they are moving" she immediately saw what I was referring to and right then a third light appeared to the left of the first two and the three moved together in formation for a few seconds before suddenly dissappearing one after the other. I was sure that they were too high to be airplanes and were not flashing or blinking. They had the appearance of stars but moved way too slow to be shooting stars. My daughter said right away "what the heck was that, that wasnt normal, did we just see ufos?" I am not sure we didn't.
