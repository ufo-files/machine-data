neon blue light with smaller red light on left side. Directional changes than hover

I was leaving work at 21:30 CST approx. 15 miles S-SW of Minneapolis, MN. I saw a large extremely luminous object hovering overhead and to the south. The object appeared rat an altitude of 2000-3000 ft. The object moved south in a straight line approx. 1-2 miles in 4-5 seconds stopping and hovering just above the tree line. I began filming the object at this time. The object disappeared then reappeared flashing 3 white lights one at a time for a few seconds. The object then changed back to the original blue/red. The object turned 90 degrees and moved approx 1/2 mile to the east. The object stopped and hovered again. At this time I alerted 4 of my coworkers who came out and witnessed the rest of the event. At this time I stopped filming as my iphone has a poor video quality and one of my coworkers began to film. The object hovers for about a minute before heading back west approx 1/2 mile and then made a 90 degree turn south and slowly headed south beyond the tree line and out of sight. I waited for 10 minutes but it did not return.
