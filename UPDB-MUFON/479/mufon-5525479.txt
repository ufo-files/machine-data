A tall thin black being

two tall thin black beings with a square shaped head. no visible arms. Standing by neighbors car in the street. Standing still. My 6 year old daughter went to get a glass of water from the kitchen at 2 a.m and the blinds were open. She looked outside and saw them standing by a car..they were not moving. She came in our room crying and my husband went to go see what she saw. nothing was there when he looked and my daughter told me it was gone. She said the creatures looked like halloween creatures. This happened in stansbury park utah
