Walking out of Walmart on Saturday night, I saw a burning ball of flames traveling at a high rate of speed!

My niece, my sons girlfriend, and I were walking out of Neighborhood Walmart on Feb. 25, 2012 (Saturday night). It was about 8:30pm and I looked up in the sky, towards the south and saw a red orange object traveling at a high rate of speed, heading north. It almost looked as if it was an airplane on fire as it shot across the sky. I was in awe looking at it, and said to my niece, "look at that fireball in the sky, what in the world is that?" She couldnt believe it either! We watched it race all the way across the black sky. My sons girlfriend was already in the truck, and she noticed us looking up at the sky and said, "what are yall looking at?" Then she looked up and saw it race by. We are not sure what it was? It could have been a meteor? Space debris? Space ship? We did not hear it, it wasnt making any noise but it was bright, looking like a fireball burning as it flew by. It burned out after watching it for about 20 seconds. It flew all the way across the sky flying at a high rate of speed coming from the south and burning out in the north towards Oklahoma. Really wild! Found a few posts on the internet of people that had also wittnessed it. Wittnesses from Houston all the way to Oklahoma City saw it!
