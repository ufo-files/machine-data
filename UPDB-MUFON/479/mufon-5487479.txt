{dr}flying chevron shape with dim white/yellowish lights

My girlfriend and I were out observing the Taurid Meteor shower when a slow chevron shape with 9-10 lights flew overhead. I say 9-10 because it appeared to be missing a light. It was not an even chevron. The right side of the object was longer. 5 lights on the right side, and four on the left. It slowly flew overhead at a distance over our heads, it appeared to be above 500 feet as we live in the flight path of Burbank Airport and was roughly the same height straight above our heads. It was dark however and I cannot be accurate with the height. It wasn't just silent, it was that eerie silent, lacking any noise whatsoever. A high frequency pitch was in my ears for almost an after witnessing the craft, it was weird because tensions rose between my girlfriend and I after witnessing the craft. We never fight, so this was out of place. After an hour or so, the pitch subsided and we came in to go to sleep.
