{dr}Very strange rotating, glowing lights

I was walking the trail that runs through Hendersonville, TN. when I noticed 4 glowing white lights hovering in the sky. After a few seconds, they began rotating slowly, almost fluid like, and then they picked up speed. The rotation was counter clockwise, for several minutes. After about 2-3 minutes, they lights/orbs morphed into just 2 larger lights, and then began rotation again. After a minute or so they began to almost "twinkle" and became 4 lights again.

I didn't feel scared at all, I felt more curious and thrilled than anything. Im a skeptic, and I immediately ran back to my house, grabbed my keys and got in my car to go find the location of the lights. Once I got to my car, they disappeared.
As I was driving down New Shackle Island, I saw the same light, only this time it was darting extremely fast, faster than any plane Id ever seen, across the sky. This was all taking place Northeast.
