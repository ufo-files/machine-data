{cak} Blue lighted object moved at a rapid pace across the sky from North to south

I was driving on 128 South getting onto route 3 South in Braintree MA when I first saw a blue object in the sky to my left at approximately 30 degrees. It moved very rapidly across my windshield from left to right decreasing slightly in elevation as it moved. At first I thought is was an airplane or meteor but it was moving way to fast and didn't have a defined trail to it. It dis-appeared at I was driving behind the tree line. It went in a strait line at a constant speed. I have seen numerous "shooting stars" and this wasn't one. I had a feeling of confusion as it was something very out of the ordinary, something I have never seen before. It had a deep blue color which made it all the more mysterious.
