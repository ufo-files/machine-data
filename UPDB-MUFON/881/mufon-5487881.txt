{rjl} Bright orb approached then made sudden 90 degree turn to my right and disappeared.

Was talking to mother on her porch when she told me to look behind me in the sky. We saw a orb that pulsed blue white light coming straight towards us. It slowed down and made a left turn towards the east at a speed and degree that was so fast it disappeared in an instant. As I asked what it could be the tv came on and not joke X files theme song began playing. The tv was off prior to us witnessing the unidentified flying object.
