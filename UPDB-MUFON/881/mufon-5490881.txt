My father and I observed a spinning Cylindrical diver coloured object that darted in and out of clouds over the Middlesbrough area in 2000 / 2001

Myself and my late Father where outside our bungalow enjoying the afternoon sunshine in Around 2000 - 2001, my father had some binoculars which he used to like viewing Aeroplanes passing overhead with when he noticed something to the north east direction some distance away over Middlesbrough and asked me to look through the binoculars to see if I knew what it was when I looked I saw a silver cylindrical spinning object that was close to some clouds I said to my father I thought it was a UFO and ran into the house to get my old Betamax video camera which I filmed the object for about 30 Seconds to a minute as it spun on the spot then darted into cloud then back to its first spot then behind the cloud again. Then my camera battery ran out but I still have the video but it's on a Betamax tape and I don't have a Betamax player no more.
