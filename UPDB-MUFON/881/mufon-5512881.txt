Disc topped cylinder with large rectangular windows internally illuminated by orange light. Aprossimative size 15/20 meters in diameter.

I am a lover of astronomy so often I go on the beaches in my area to observe the sky away from city lights. That evening as I watched the sky above me I noticed an object almost perpendicular on my head, moved slowly and without sound, disc-shaped, surmounted by a cylinder which opened the door lit with orange light extremely bright. I watched with binoculars 10 * 50. A few seconds of observation about 5, then I passed the binoculars to my girlfriend. The sighting was confirmed in the Cagliari area, same description.
