Approached by object in the sky

In 2011 me and two friends were walking in Ipswich late evening. Not very far from Rendlesham forest. As the sun was setting I looked into it over the horizon. I noticed straight away a odd characteristic in the setting sun which was an intense bring light sitting with the dyeing light of the sun itself. I stood out as you shouldn't be able to see anything other than the sun but sitting within its arch was an intense for if brilliant white light. I pointed it out to my friends but they brushed it off. We carried on walking and I kept looking to check this anomaly and to my surprise it was getting brighter which I worked out that it was coming toward us. The light was fading fast. I again pointed it out to me friends. We stopped and look at it for a few minutes idea and there was no sound but it was definitly head towards us. We carried on walking. I took my eye off of it for no more that 30 seconds and when I looked back it was almost over head. Moving slower. I stopped and looked up pointing I shouted look at that. My friends all looked up. The bright light above us. Stopped suddenly. The lights faded and as they did the object shot up into the sky. I tried to follow it with my line of sight but lost it. I scanned the dark sky for where it went and noticed a red dot apprea as it it was way up in the stars. My friends played the whole thing down but I know what I saw.
