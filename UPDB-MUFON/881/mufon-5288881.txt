Stationary implied corner of large craft/object suspended amidst turbulent clouds

It's taken me years to report this to MUFON; I'm not sure why. I remember it clearly though. I was sitting at a bus stop on Belmont near the cross street Leavitt. Heavy, dark, and massively churning clouds laid low over the city that day, but it wasn't raining. I was periodically looking down the street to check on the bus. I was staring at the clouds over the city and marveling at how angry they seemed when somewhere above the buildings in my eyeline, the clouds parted and the dark corner of some object was visible. I wasn't immediately amazed thanks to television and film, but I was eventually (within nanoseconds) struck that there was no known explanation for what I was seeing. By the time I'd realized I was looking at something that shouldn't be - what looked like the downward pointing corner of a craft or even floating metallic building - the clouds roiled back down to envelope it and no amount of staring afterwards gave any sign of something enormous suspended above the city.

I'm no nephologist, but I believe the clouds fell into the undulatus asperatus category. They were like watching the sea if it were terribly angry and upside down. 

The object appeared to be roughly a mile off, though that's difficult to gauge. It was somewhere between my position and the city, possibly over the lake (Michigan). It was to the south-southeast of where I sat and was visible to my left above the few low buildings on the south side of the street. The object was dark with no lights and no movement I could discern for the few moments I saw it. It somehow gave the impression of having much more bulk above it, but that may have just been an illusion of the clouds.

I shot video for several seconds afterward hoping it would reappear, but I only captured the clouds. My phone at the time was so crappy, the clouds don't even appear as impressive as they were in person.  [ Item moved to F I Report {tjd} ].
