Gold pulsating light stops in mid air, disappears.

At 10:05 pm central time, I observed at the MCI Airport a pulsating gold light shoot down from the sky similar to that of a shooting star. It stopped dead in it’s tracks, disappeared, reappeared briefly then disappeared again.Two days prior I saw a silver UFO at low altitude at the airport for just a few seconds. Two days after this event I had missing time of 1 hr 25 minutes while traveling to the airport, then found an unknown substance on the roof of my car that was not there before.
