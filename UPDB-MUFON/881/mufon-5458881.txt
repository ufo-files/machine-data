Was in driveway waiting for person to come out and watching sky. I was interested in aircraft so was always watching planes. It was a very clear night. UFO was very high in sky but was circling downward. What I noticed first was that there was no sound. As we drove away towards home ufo continued to descend in a circular pattern. As we got to an open clearing near Lincoln school ufo appeared to hover. We stopped car to confirm this and ufo was totally stopped and hovering right above
Large school complex. I saw windows in this saucer ufo. It was very bright but there were definite windows the length of the side I saw. We immediately left
Area and went a block to my home. I called Logan airport to see if airplanes were circling airport and they said no. I called Hanscom Air Force Base and they were not doing maneuvers. I then reported sighting to Air Force Base.
