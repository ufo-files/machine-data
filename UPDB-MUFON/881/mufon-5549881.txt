Humming, spinning saucer near ground in rural OH

Legacy case entered by MUFON of OH. A young mother was driving with her two sons and grandmother on Cranes Run Roadwhere it dead-ends into SR25 in rural southern Montgomery Co., OH nearMiamisburg in Aug. or Sept. of 1966 at 7:30 or 8:00 p.m. They stopped at a stopsign and looked across the field where the un was going down (W) and saw asaucer-shaped object in fron of the sun, approximately 1/2 mi. away. The objectwas bright, shiny and looked like it might be touching down on the ground orvery close to the ground. It was about the size of a truck."We heard a humming sound, quite distinct.... I couldnt tell a distinct colorbecause the sun was behint it, but it shimmered a lot and wavered. No wind wasblowing the ground, dirt, or grass. It had a clockwise right-to-left spin."The four occupants of the car watched it about 10 minutes. Other traffic pulledover (3 or 4 cars) and their occupants also saw the object. Soon the objectbegan to ascend."It tilted back and took off slow....I lost sight of it in the distance. Theobject was pretty looking but it still gave me goose bumps."
