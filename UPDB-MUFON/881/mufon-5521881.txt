Three large orange objects in sky seeming to take off from ground.

On Christmas night was waiting in a car with friend to go out. Noticed two large orange lights appear through the treeline to East. Both Ascending Rapidly and moving South. Watched objects for several minuts as they then slowly began a slow curve to the ESE at altitude. Approx two minutes after first two lights took off a third light ascended rapidly from the same location in the distance beyond tree line. Last light joined the other two and all moved off in a easterly direction. The area it seemed to rise from is suburban with many homes, businesses etc. However no airports are in the general direction to my knowledge (having checked Google Earth to be sure.)
