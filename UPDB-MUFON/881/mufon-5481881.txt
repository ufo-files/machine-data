{dr}Ascending in an arc from sw towards nw

Outside taking a walk when my wife spotted the first object. It was followed by five more objects on an identical flight path. Looked to be red in the center with orange/yellow extensions above and below. Pretty daunting. We have no idea of the size and scale, nor the distance from us. Definitely no marking lights or strobes. What was remarkable is how they made the ascent and turn so smoothly and precisely. If they were distant, they were moving quite rapidly.
