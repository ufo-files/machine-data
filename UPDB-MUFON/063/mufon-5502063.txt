I was on my way to the store with a group of friend in Charlotte,Nc on the night of June 6, 2014. As we were walking to the store when seen others looking into the sky towards the east of Charlotte. There they were in the sky, at first glance they looked like stars but they were moving and pulsating with a beautiful white light glow they were also flicking in sync and flying in a flock like formation.
