Round circle of light traveled across sky, size of basketball

Driving down Service Road in Sandwich toward Barnstable observed round yellow circle of light, about size of basketball, go from right to left and disappear behind trees. After a few seconds caught glimpse of it apparently descending in direction of Cape Cod Bay. Speed was consistent with aircraft, but the circle of light was bigger than any I have associated with airplane or helicopter in the past. Interested in seeing if anybody else saw it or can say what it was. It was not a Chinese lantern.
