Observed a bright light while camping with husband and children.

I was camping at Thunder Lake Provincial Park, which is approximately 21 kilometers west of the nearest town, Barrhead, with my husband and 4 children. My husband and I were sitting outside as it started to get dark and observed a bright light across the lake. It was an overcast night. It at first looked like a headlight. It started to come toward us and then suddenly zoomed to the right, then came back. We soon heard 2 jets come over and the light disappeared. After the jets left, the light came back. Animals and fowl were making a lot of noise. A boat went out on the lake toward the light. The light then disappeared. After the boat came back to shore, the light reappeared. Then it was joined by more lights - maybe 4-5. Some of the lights appeared to go into the bushes. The next morning we checked the bushes, but did not find anything unusual. When we walked down by the lake, there were dead fish everywhere. The people who run the campground told us the next morning that they also saw the lights and watched them all night long. We believe the jets that came over might have been military, because from the sounds, there were two of them. But it was overcast, so we don't know for sure.
