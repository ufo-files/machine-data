{jpg} Hovering craft, 3 bright lights, triangle pattern, turning slowly on it's axis, no sound. We stupidly shined a light at it.

A friend and I borrowed a family car to head out for a drive. Best I can recall was a time of about 9-11P.M. His home borderd on the last road of our small town. The only grocery store on that end is where we encountered it. Driving southbound, we both spotted the lights from the East, and thought it was a helicopter hovering. We stopped the truck to see what might be going on. It took us a moment to realize what we were looking at wasn't normal. Too afraid to get out and investigate, we lowered the window and cut the engine. It was a large black triangle shaped craft with 3 evenly spaced lights. It was rotating on it's axis with no noise. Speed of rotation approx 1 revolution per 30 seconds. It was canted with the underside open to the west. It hovered approx 150+ high, at about 100ft just left of the building. The lights appeared to be visually buzzing, or emitting an odd look,much like the heat of a car hood distorts your vision. After observing for a minute or two, my friend decided to shine a powerful flashlight at it. In that moment it stopped it's rotation, began to move South East at a slow pace. After 5-7 seconds, it instantly climbed South East and upward in a curved angle at a super high rate of speed til we lost visual. The surface was black, reflective and textured. Best description is "black cellophane". My friend unfortunately would not speak of our encounter for almost 15 years. He finally admitted to being afraid of acknowledging the event.
