Looked like a mini little dipper

Was looking at the clouds on my porch where I have for years. Noticed a small group of 'stars' that looked like a mini little dipper way off in the distance in the sky. I told my husband and kids that's not the little dipper I hadn't ever seen those stars there before. Then they slowly started moving upwards and formed two straight lines. After a few minutes they vanished!
