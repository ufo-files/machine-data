{cak} 3 ufo's in sky. They were lined up. Top middle and bottom. But they all appeared to be spread far apart.

Around 7:40-7:45 pm (it was dark outside) I was walking down my driveway taking my trash cans down to the bottom. When I got to the bottom I put the trash cans down and noticed a bright red orange light in the corner of my eye. I looked up and seen three round lights in the sky. They didn't make any noise, wasn't moving just hovering there, and had no other lights but the red orange solid color. They appeared to be far apart but in a pattern or line I suppose. One was at the top, then another was under it maybe 20-40 ft apart, and the last one was at the very bottom, 20-40 ft apart also. But they weren't in a straight line... the middle one was out farther right then the rest and the top and very bottom ones were lined up perfect. They lasted no longer than 4 seconds and then they just disappeared. I didn't take my eyes off of them, I blinked and they were all gone. I looked farther into the distance and seen two airplanes but their lights were so far off into the distance there was no way they were what I had just seen. Plus, their lights blinked and appeared to be blue or white.
