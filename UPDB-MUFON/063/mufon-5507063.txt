{dr}Bright

I was driving my vehicle on 67 North towards Mapleview exit at approximately 6:45 am on 10/17/13. The sun had not risen above the horizon yet and the sky was was getting light. There are two mountains directly east of there about 50 miles east with a "valley" in between. I saw a spot in the sky that was as bright as the sun but was only aspirin size in the sky. I didn't know what it was but knew it wasnt a star or plane as stars and planes are not that bright. It was like little pieces of the sun in the early morning sky. As I sat at the stoplight, I watched the light in the sky slowly move in a northerly direction. Then all of a sudden a second and third light appeared. One of the lights was elongated, almost like a shooting star but was kind of stationary and appeared like it was moving in a southerly direction. The third light was a little bigger than the first and seemed like it was moving in a northerly direction. As traffic started moving, I tried to keep watch. The lights stayed for a few moments and then disappeared. As I continued up the hill on 67N, I saw one of the lights had come back. I then traveled too far to see anymore. When I arrived at work in Poway, I kept watch from a second story window, but the sun had risen and it was too bright. I didn't see them again. As I drove to work the following morning I looked for the lights but they were not there.
