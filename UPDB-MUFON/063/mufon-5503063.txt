I awoke suddenly one night and turned my head to the left and looked out the window and saw bright lights in my front yard. I awoke my husband and told him somebody was outside (we thought it was a car) but when we looked again there was NO CAR just orbs of light right outside the window. My husband flew out of the bed ran downstairs and out the front door and the lights just disappeared into thin air.
