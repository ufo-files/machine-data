Randomly blinking green light that shot off towards air force base

My wife and I were outside in our front yard observing some nearby new year's fireworks, the ones they do early for the kids. It was 10pm. While watching them my wife noticed a blinking green light hovering in the sky. To us, it appeared as if it were observing the fireworks. I assumed it was a drone, though the blinking light was erratic. Once the fireworks were over, the light darted off to the east, towards the East Sale RAAF base. It flew at a speed faster than what the jets that fly out of the base fly over the town. We lost sight of it moments later as it disappeared out of view.
