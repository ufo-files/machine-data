{rjl} White object moving in the sky

We were installing solar on a rooftop in Santa Clarita . Coworker was on the ground talking to me while I was on the rooftop. He was away for a while and came back to talk when he sees a white orb in the sky behind my head but he doesn't describe it, he walks up the ladder while his eye is on the object. This whole time I cannot see the object, not until he gets on the roof and stands next to me do I see it. The object varied in altitudes very rabidly split second not only up and down buy moving side to side. After seeing it for a few moments the object hovered closely and stopped moving then ascended and the sky blinked white and black to where it was hard to see almost like a flash bang or something. Both our eyes watered and burned we got scared and carefully climbed down the ladder. We stopped work for about an hour before deciding to return to work.
