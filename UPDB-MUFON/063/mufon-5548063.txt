Star-like object in the Northeast sky over Philadelphia/Bucks County

I was just about to sit down in my backyard w/ a drink at 5pm. Before sitting down and drinking, I looked to the northeast sky and saw a star-like object in the sky. I watched it for a minute and it did not move. I quickly got my binoculars to look at it but it was too far away to see any details. Since it remained in the same position, I went and got my camera. I was able to take one picture of it. After taking a picture, I lost sight of it and it disappeared.
