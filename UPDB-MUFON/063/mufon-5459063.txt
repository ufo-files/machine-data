{rjl} Massive black triangle silently gliding overhead

I was driving home from grocery shopping one evening back in autumn 2013 in the Upper Marlboro area. While stopped at a traffic light I look up and I noticed that the sky appeared to be moving and part of it was pitch black... soon realized the pitch black part of the sky was a perfect triangle with lights on the 3 corners. This was in close proximity to Andrews Air Force Base and it was coming from that direction (south) headed northbound. If I had to guess at size it would be at least a football field, way to big to be a B2, plus the shape was different, more of an equilateral triangle and all three sides were straight. It moved very slowly for an aircraft and was silent making me wonder if it wasn’t some sort of “stealth blimp”. Curiously there were a lot of cars/potential witnesses out but given the color and silence of the object at night I probably would have missed it had I not randomly looked up.
