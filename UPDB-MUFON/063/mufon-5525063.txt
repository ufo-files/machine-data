a disc like shape with grey and black surface

On the afternoon of the 4th of august my girlfriend and i were fishing off a secluded jetty in the back creeks of noosa queensland australia i had just casted my rod out and turned around to put it in my rod holder when my girlfriend tapped my on the shoulder and said "omg what is that up there infront of that cloud" i looked up and could not believe my eyes as i looked up to see this disc like object in the sky i was amazed as it was like nothing i had seen before it was very unusual as it sat infront of big white clouds it had a metalic grey/black surface and looked like it was the size of a resort it also looked as if it was spinning but the weird thing was it only seemed like seconds i was watching it before it just vanished into thin air but my girlfriend said that we were looking at it for at least ten minutes i have absolutley no explination for what i saw but i would like to find out!!!!
