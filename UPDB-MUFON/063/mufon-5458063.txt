{cak} My roommate and I were walking in the park around 8pm when I saw what I thought at first was a star

My roommate and I were walking in the park when what looked like a bright star caught my eye. It looked like it was getting brighter, so I pointed it out to him. He agreed that it was getting significantly brighter. That's when it began moving to the left, As it moved, it got brighter and then dim and then bright again before it disappeared entirely and reappeared in a different spot. It did this once or twice more and then began moving away until it was out of sight behind a mountain. The light was a bright white, with a slight orange/red glow to it at times. At one point. the light appeared to briefly flatten out kind of, as opposed to a single point of light like a star. After about a minute of watching the craft, I attempted to capture footage of the event with my cell phone's camera. After realizing that the footage was going to be of negligible quality and content, I ended the video at 12 seconds.  Please, pardon any vulgar language used in the video. Needless to say, we were a little beside ourselves at what we were seeing.
