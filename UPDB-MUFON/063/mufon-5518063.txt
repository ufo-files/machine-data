Saw UFO traveling high rate of speed and fighter jet following

I was sitting on my back porch and noticed and orb traveling very fast, I pointed my video camera at it and noticed when I zoomed in it was a solid bluish white light that had no other lights located on it. I then heard a nosie and saw another aircraft with two blinking red lights following behind it about 15 seconds after I saw the object. The sound of the engines on the blinking red light plane was clear of that of a fighter jet. I saw the orb continue to the side of my house and it then vanished with the fighter jet continuing on a path to where it was last located. I also captured another aircraft shortly after on the same flight pattern with very odd green lights on it moving very fast.
