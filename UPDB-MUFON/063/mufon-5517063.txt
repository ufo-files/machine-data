{flg} Balloon-like

I saw what I thought was formation of 3 geese flying really high. As I watched them the "lead" object stopped and the other 2 continued their path. As they passed the stationary object they appeared to be flying at an extremely fast rate of speed. The stationary object remained in place until I looked away, when I looked back up I couldnt see it anymore. They were very hard to see to begin with due to their altitude. There was no noise, no contrail, no lights. As I stood there and observed them they seemed to resemble balloons.
