Fireball Ascends Into the Sky.

I was dropping my friend off at his house and it was about 6:20 PM. It was pretty dark outside and I was heading home. I was making a turn and when I looked both ways I did a double take because I saw something weird and I thought it was a streetlight. It was an orange-ish fireball ascending into the sky. It got dimmer and turned into a single ball of light. I was freaking out and I called my friend to tell her what I saw and while I was talking to her the object jumped to the other side of the sky in an instant. It was a fireball like before and when it dimmed, it looked like three horizontal lights and it shot off into the sky.
