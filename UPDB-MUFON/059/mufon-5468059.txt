{jpg} Noticed jetlike noise, looked up and saw object fly overhead and away.

It was evening, full dark. I was outside sitting in a lawn chair watching a movie on my laptop. I was aware of a noise like a plane but it wasn't until it was right overhead that I looked up to see a large grey object flying north to south. It seemed to be flying rather low for a jet liner, but still several thousand feet up. It had 2 white lights in the front and 2 red lights in the rear. It appeared to have no wings and a flat bottom. It's rectangular shape reminded me of a barge or aircraft carrier. It's corners did not appear to be rounded but more squared off, hence the resembalance to a barge. It continued from over my head south until it was out of sight. It was not quite as loud as a jet plane but made a noise very similar. I continued to watch it and noticed the light pattern did not change as if it was 2 separate planes flying together. It flew south over the nearby town of Dallas until it was out of sight.
