{nd} witnessed 3 glowing white orbs forming equilateral triangle. Objects moved extremely slowly, dimmed and brightened

I was picking my husband up from work at 10:30PM on Sunday, September 4, 2016. On the way home, we both noticed three glowing white orbs hovering over US31 near I-475. The objects were in a perfect equilateral triangle formation, with two objects on top, and one on bottom, which was significantly dimmer. We could tell immediately that the objects were not helicopters, planes, or anything we are familiar with, and in fact we saw a low-flying aircraft in the area at the time of the sighting. It was absolutely stunning. The objects moved extremely slowly, not breaking formation; hovering, but not fixed in the sky. We drove home, keeping the objects in sight, and called my mother outside to check them out. She was also amazed. We watched the orbs for approximately two hours before eventually retiring to bed. When I awoke at 4am, the objects were gone.
