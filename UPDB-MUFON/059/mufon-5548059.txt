I was driving my daughter home on Hwy 59 and we were about 3 miles east of Grove when my daughter noticed in the west above the tree line a flashing light about the size of a quarter moving in a zig-zag up and down then side to side. I slowed my car down so we could watch. While it was moving it would change colors from white,yellow, purple, red, and green. There is nothing that I know of that can move as fast as this light did across the sky. It came to a sudden stop and got smaller and smaller turned blue and disapeared quickly it took about 5 seconds to vanish. My daughter and I looked at each other with our mouths open and said what did we just see.
