Spot disembodied

While living in Amarillo, TX and working at a restaurant job during the time period, was assisting a co-worker/friend with an evening-meal catering assignment to Fritch, TX. After the catering event, was riding as passenger in work vehicle, traveling southward on TX Hwy. 136, with windows opened. The night was fairly dark, as the entire region was blanketed by a very low, thick/dense cloud layer down to approx. 1,000'-1,500' altitude, and was quite irregular-shaped/rough-looking/wafting-like on its underside. When nearing the general vicinity of the PANTEX Atomic Energy Commission ordnance plant to the general SE, the topic of conversation had eventually came around to UFOs. While casually looking out of the open passenger window, looking over the irregular nature to the underside of the cloud layer, I was surprised to observe a light source traveling through the irregular/wispy area of the cloud layer, initially proceeding in the same travel direction as our work vehicle, at a range of approx. 1/4-1/3 mile when closest, with an elevation of approx. 1,000' and approx. 40 degrees above horizon. When first noticed, the object was a little behind the work vehicle's positioning upon the roadway, to the general NW of our position, and appeared to be slowly, gradually overtaking our vehicle's speed of 55 mph, all without any discernible, associated noise/sound. The object was best observed/scrutinized when it had pulled even with the work vehicle, and appeared as a light source of amber/yellowish coloration, mildly-dim intensity with weak, steady, radiance nature, was of somewhat irregular shaping, was perhaps under 5' in both length/height, and had a somewhat "transparent" quality to it, seemingly allowing minor view of some type of "interior" details. The overall impression that it gave me, was of some kind of visible, disembodied/opened window/portal upon an otherwise invisible craft, allowing the escape/detection of some type of interior lighting, and notice of some minor detail beyond, similar to looking through the window(s) of a conventional vehicle with its dome/cabin light active. As the work vehicle reached a point in the roadway with the nearby PANTEX plant located to a more eastward direction, the object was observed to execute a slow, gradual turn until headed westward, and proceed away until lost to view in distance. Total observation time was approx. 1.5 minutes, and while turning away, the angle-of-view upon the object progressed through an appropriate similarity, like what expected from a side-located window on a vehicle/aircraft viewed at increasing angles. I did bring the object to the attention of the vehicle driver, but he did not observe the object, due to the sight-blocking nature of the vehicle's roof, and traveling at highway speeds, we did not stop the vehicle, as we figured the object would be long gone by the time we did.
