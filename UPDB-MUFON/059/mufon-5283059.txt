Two lights very bright. One descended vertically and light went out.

I was travelling west, joining the M20 motorway at Maidstone and saw two lights in the clear sky. They were very bright and quite close to each other horizontally. The light on the left went out out quite soon after noticing it, no object could be seen when the light went out. The second object on the right descended to approximately 30 degrees and the light went out. Again no object was seen after.
