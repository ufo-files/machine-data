Star-like object way brighter than everything else in the sky moving in odd manner

At about 6:30 I went outside to grab a cup out of my truck before dinner. I noticed a large, glowing object in the sky almost directly overhead moving Northeast at a fairly steady rate of speed. At first I thought it was a plane but noticed no blinking lights and no noise. It started moving in a slight zig-zag pattern but continued in the same general direction. As it moved away over a wooded area (but still well within line of sight) it seemed to stop and hover for a second before dimming to zero luminance within a matter of 5 seconds or so. It was the brightest object in the sky and appeared larger than stars but did not have the same characteristics of any satellite that I have ever seen. Before the sighting was over I tried to take a video but my cell phone camera malfunctioned so I was left with a 1 second video but you can clearly see the object as it's the only light in the sky that my camera picked up. The whole event lasted approximately one minute and I am left scratching my head as to what that could possibly have been.
