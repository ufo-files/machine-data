{jpg} Sometime in the early 90's I saw a bright light like a star come down from the sky.

In the early 90's I was watching a ballgame on tv with my grandfather around 8-10pm. He went to the door to spit then I noticed a bright star that got closer to us when it got above the field in the front yard it lit the whole place up like it was daylight there was no sound of engines no wind from exhaust then with a blink of an eye it ascended to space.
