I was walking across walk bridge at night when noticed light beam coming toward me it got to bridge behind me and followed me then hovered above me, object was silent, beam was bright and was slowly moving toward me. I felt hair stand up and I ran away.

I was out at local park watching playoff soft ball game. after game ended I went for a walk along walk path in wooded area,walk lasted approximately 45 min which brought time to about 11:45pm, the lights were out at park by this time. I was walking across the walk bridge that goes over a road when I noticed a bright beam of light shining on the road moving toward me.the light got to the bridge behind me and slowly moved toward me,I thought that it may be a helicopter but made no sound. I walked faster not knowing the source of the light. the light seemed to be appearing from nowhere at approximately 70 feet or less above the ground. when I reached the end of bridge the light was 10 feet away from me, I still heard no noise from the object it was completely silent.the light beam stopped moving and gave off an energy,I knew this as my hair on arms and neck stood up.As I stood there looking up to see what it was,I noticed the beam was unusually bright and started to move again toward where I was standing,hearing stories of when people ended up inside beams of light coming from ufo that didn't end well, I turned and ran a few hundred feet without looking back. I didn't see the light or object again.
