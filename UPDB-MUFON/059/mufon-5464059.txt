I was on my way into work this was in Canton, MI it was wround 5:45 a.m. I noticed three horizontal white bright lights kind of bobbing slightly, they then turned vertically. I was saying out loud11 what is that, what is that!!11 To myself slightly panicked. It then spread out to a triangle three rows of four bright white lights per line outer row, middle row, outer row four lights each with a red light in the middle. It looked as though it was having a bit of trouble and was going lower across the way . The truck next to me noticed it I think. It looked like he was trying to follow it. At first I thought it was a drone but the shape shifting changed my mind. I felt very nervous but intrigued. I had to keep driving into work I lost sight of it while driving. I have seen other things in the area a little further down. My mother and I witnessed a soundless black triangle no lights at night in the summer of 2016. On Michigan ave just past the high way. It was large and made feel very uneasy. We hurried inside and didn11t really talk about it again. I have seen other things as well.
