2 craft hovered building , 20 feet , above,10 seconds, scaled building then over GG Bridge

On September of 1978 , at approx. 7:20 PM , I happened to be walking my dog in a small church parking lot on Broadway at Franklin St in San Francisco. I happened to look up at the edge of a two story building and noticed the edge of something moving out over the roof of the building. Suddendly a small craft , row boat sized with a central rim about the center and two steep tail fins floated out from the roof. Then a second craft moved by its side and I realized it was the second fin I had observed. They were silent and the skin of the craft was sort of a soft reflective whiteish-silver with a faint orange glow. One craft rocked back and forth slightly. Both craft were less then 25 feet from me, I literally could have hit them with a stone. I of course was in some sort of shock but I felt both elation , a desire to go with them and a sense of panic, realizing that there was no mistaking what I was seeing. After about 10 seconds they took off and banked up and over a 10 story building then I observed them directly over the Golden Gate Bridge as two orange lights.
That month , there were numerious and varied UFO sightings in the SF region and several over SF.
