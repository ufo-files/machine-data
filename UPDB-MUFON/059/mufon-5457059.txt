{cak} Sightings of a bright light that appeared in the same position in the sky on 3 separate dates

My wife saw a bright light appear (in daylight) while we were driving west toward Blair, NE on Friday, September 27th, around 6pm. Before I looked, it had disappeared. I thought that it was possibly an airplane reflecting the sun but was unable to see any planes. On Thursday, October 3rd, at 9:45pm, I was standing on my front porch looking to the west and saw a bright light appear out of nowhere (at 45 degrees from the horizon). It did not move, and lasted only 3-5 seconds before disappearing. On Friday October 4th, I was telling my wife about it while we were both out on the front porch. I pointed to the sky where I had seen it, and as we were looking that direction, the light appeared again for both of us to see. Again, it lasted 3-5 seconds before disappearing. This occurred at 9:55pm in the same location. The light was high in the atmosphere, and was at least 20x brighter than any star. I watched the sky again on Saturday October 5th but did not see any activity from 9:30pm-10:30pm. However, the light appeared again on Sunday, October 6th in the same location, but much later (10:41pm). Each occurrence was at the same location in the sky, and with the same intense bright white light. I have continued to watch the skies at night around those time frames but have not seen it occur since Oct. 6th.
