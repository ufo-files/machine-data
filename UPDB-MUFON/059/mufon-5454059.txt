{jpg} Bright, irregular, with appearance of layered structure

I saw the object when going outside for a breath of air. It may have been there for some time, but I first noted it at 7:30 p.m. It first looked like a huge, bright star, but irregular in shape. It grew in size and then diminished, only to return even larger. It moved vertically in a series of convulsive steps and then settled down. Later, it slowly moved horizontally to the left and then slowly re-assumed its former position. I viewed it with image-stabilized, astronomy binoculars and saw something like a geometrical structure, with irregular sections and layers. It was longer vertically than horizontally. I watched for the entire time, but it never moved more than 10 degrees in any direction. At one point, the sky around it acquired a reddish hue. This is the third sighting at my place in as many months and it is troubling. I took a photo with my cell phone, but it did not show much detail.
