{rjl} Triangle Shaped Object, Low & Slow Flying

I was on my patio when the object flew low and very slowly over the apartment building. There were 3 very bright lights on the underside of the vessel, in triangular format. There were no other lights, ie: green or red lights like a plane would have. The vessel made a noise that was like a dull buzz or hum, but it was fairly quiet. I ran inside and out of my front door to get another look (because it already passed over my building) but by the time I got outside, maybe within 10 seconds, the vessel had completely vanished. Outside of my building, it's a very open area, so if the vessel had carried on that path, I would've unmistakably have seen it, but it just wasn't in the sky any more. I was awe struck more than anything. There was a pizza delivery man close by, but I never got a chance to speak to him to ask if he saw it.
