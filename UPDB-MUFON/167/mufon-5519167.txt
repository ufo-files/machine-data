Small Orange/Yellow Ball Floating East to West

Noticed small orange and yellow in color ball floating east to west on US Highway 50 between W. Sprinfield Road and County Line Road between Gerald and Rosebud, Missouri. Nearly the size of a basketball. Appeared to be similar to a luminary balloon for a memorial but did not share the same detail. Shined a spotlight on it and it appeared to drift through the light, almost transparent. Left area due to traffic, returned moments later and light was gone, checked fields near and nothing observed. 01:30hrs 4/27/12
