very long rocket with very long, skinny wings, tail fin and tail wings

i was in albuquerque out side walking when movement in the sky caught my eye. i looked higher up in the sky at about 45 degrees at 10000 ft. it was not going up. it was going horizontal. it was a rocket with 1 tail fin. it was easily, if not more, twice the lenght of a commercial aircraft. it then started a slow right turn. thats when i saw the very long skinny wings. they reminded me of the U2 but the wings were longer and skinny. i also then saw the tail wings. it made no sound and it left no trail. as i said earlier i thought it was a rocket until i saw the wings. it had no markings and no windows.
