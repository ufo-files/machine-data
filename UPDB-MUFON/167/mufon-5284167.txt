7 boomerang shapes traveling together

I left work on time at 7pm, took the 15 minute drive home, immediately took a shower. After that I went out to my back porch to sit outside and enjoy the beautiful night and weather. As soon as I walked out the door, I went to the back of my porch, lit a cigarette and that’s when they caught the corner of my eye. 7 distinct boomerang shaped (rounded nose and wing tips) aircraft traveling in Flying V formation headed due north. No sound, no blinking wing lights, just slightly glowing underside of the aircrafts. It took about 6-8 seconds to go from my approximate 8 o’clock to 11 o’clock when I lost sight behind trees. About half way through my sight, the craft in the rear left of the formation came out of line and circled counter clockwise around to the front of the formation at about the 2 o’clock position almost perfectly lateral to front #1 aircraft of the formation, in apprx 2.5-3 seconds. Not to say I was “privileged” but I was in the Marines and I never saw anything that shape nor move that quickly. It’s been a long time since but If it would be useful I can probably make a decent sketch or have someone visit to go over the account.
