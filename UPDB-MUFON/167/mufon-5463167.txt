I was camping at Big Creek Campground in Logan Valley, Grant County, Oregon, my three daughters were asleep in the tent. My friend and I were sleeping out under the stars. We had not gone to sleep and were listening to the radio at about 2:45 AM. Suddenly the radio cut out as a bright object shot very fast from South to Northwest approx 1/2 mile from us. It hovered above treeline for a minute, then went below the treeline for a minute, then hovered at treeline again, then shot extremely fast across the horizen to the South. The radio came back on. When I returned home, my step dad and another logger, told the story of seeing the same thing at the same time just as they were about to start their day. They were located on the back (south) side of the Strawberry Mt Range. The other person left the job site and quit his job and left the state. I am the only one still living that i know of that witnessed that sighting. Currently, that area is know for GPS not to work properly. It is about 25 miles from the town of Seneca. We were not frightend, do not know why. to this day it does not upset me to remember or to talk about it.

This was not my first sighting of a UFO. I will fill out another report for that one.

Until now, I did not know where to report this incident, but always wanted to.
