{cak} I was an airmen on South Vandenberg for the last Atlas E launch which was unusual

I was stationed at Vandenberg AFB, CA from 93-1996 and had some rather unique observations. However, one day I was watching t.v. and I could not ignore the show stated "Vandenberg A.F.B. CA 1995 or some date I cannot recollect at the moment. I worked directly on the pads and SLC's for the Titan II and IV, Atlas E, SLC 6, and SLC2E (Delta) on North Base...serviced the western test range and most facilities. 
The night they mention I had the closest proximity to the Atlas E that I believe the show talks about. I worked the missile operational support for many launches and can affirm some unusual observations. MY NCOIC and I were often speechless after some peculiar things we pieced together.
