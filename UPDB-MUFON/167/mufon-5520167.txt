orb gone from my sight in less than 4 sec

Last evening, March 11, 2012, at approximately 11:15 p.m. est, I witnessed a strange orb omit a light and move faster than anything that I have ever witnessed. I was standing in my backyard in River Rouge, MI facing North(the Dtroit River is to my East) when I saw a bright orb like object get extremely bright and in a matter of second shot upward at tremendous speeds without any sound. I tried to take out my cell phone and take a picture but by the time I grabbed it out of my pocket, the object was out of sight. Just estimating, I say the object had a diameter of about 15-20 m, the light streak omitted was at least five times the length of the object and was light blueish white in color. It was about 5-7,000 feet in the sky (much lower than a plane landing at detroit metropolitan airport), but when it left it was up in the stars and out of sight in a matter of at most 4 seconds. Weird!! This was my third sighting of a UFO over River Rouge in the past two years!! Any explanation to help me rationalize this?
