bright blue white glowing oval turning into comet

in nature preserve above bothell everett highway i saw what looked like jet trail or chem trail, a stripe of white cloud like matter directly above me, looked man made and is unusual...i followed this down to the bothell everett highway. at the crosswalk where mill creek blvd. meets the highway, i waited for the light, then looked up in the sky just at dusk there was a very large glowing blue white object hovering at roughly 301 which then slowly reformed shape into a comet/rocket and moved up and to the east, back toward the preserve.
