three white lights traveling together

I went out in backyard, looked at the stars and saw three white lights middle one offset front..listened for jet sound, did not hear any sound. went in got binocs and saw nothing but the three lights, then went behind trees..I thought it was just an aicraft, however, no red or green lights as would be on winged aircraft and no strobes..lights traveled in straight line ,no strobe, no sound. dissapeard behind trees where I could no longer see..This was around 11;05 pm on Monday night. sky was clear and stars were visible..altitude looked to be above 10,000ft. I am highly trained observer. This looked like a commercial aircraft, but as I said, it did not have strobes or wing lights of red, or green..and I heard no noise..I can hear jet noise from thousands of feet.this one had none.
