{td} Triangle shape appears near the surface of the moon

I was on my front porch capturing images of the full moon. I took about 6 images and called it a night. Several days later I downloaded my captures to the computer and noticed an anomaly in #3322. It was not in any of the other captures. I made several copies of it so not to disturb the original and used one of them to clean it up and clarify what I had. When I did this, it appeared to be casting a shadow on the surface of the moon. I use a Canon 5D MK2 DSLR with a Canon 500mm fixed 14.0 lens. The image I am sending you is not as clear as I would like, but it is not altered in any way. I am also sending the image taken just after, less than one minute, #3323, to show the image is not in that frame so it is moving. In #3322, look to the 8 O'clock position. You can check the metadata and see that this is an original. I sent this to Mr. Pope and he referred me to you. I don't think it is a satellite or other object from us.
