{dr}Traveling south in my car seen 12 or more pulsating orbs hovering over Breidgewater, PA.

As I was traveling south in my car on Route 51 I noticed strange lights hovering over Bridgewater, Pa. As I got closer to Bridgewater I seen these lights more clearly. It was about 7:45pm when I seen these lights appear. These lights were in formation. The objects were orb (round shaped) and glowed with a orange red light. Some of them even pulsated from white to red light. After about 5 minutes of hovering over Bridgewater they all of a sudden just disappeared.The way the formation looked gave me a sense of awe.
