{rjl} Spinning light that would disappear and reappear

We all thought it was a star but then it disappeared for a short time. After noticing this we all freaked out a little and when it came back we started recording. When we zoomed in we could see that it was spinning and it would get huge then disappear. At some moments after it disappeared it would reappear a distance away then to back.
