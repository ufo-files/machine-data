{nd} Large glowing object. Was seen by whole neighborhood for a very long time

This craft hovered over the Tn river for a very long time. I was a young boy approx 7-8. I remember my mom and dad and all our neighbors watching this. I'll never forget how calm, quiet and peaceful everything felt. It was a huge bright green object, with landing legs viable. Best way I can describe it is like the lunar lander but approx 5 times the size. I have tried for years to find any info on this, but have not been able to. I'm a retired Police officer now, and would just like any info on this available. I'm willing to speak with your investigators to help any way I can.
