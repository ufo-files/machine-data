{td} Noticed white dot in sky when visiting at a friends house, He also seen the object

Was visiting a friend at his house, as I was getting ready to leave I noticed a white spot in the sky, I asked my friend if he seen the object as well, and he said yes. my wife and I returned home where I picked up my binoculars and located the object again, it was still in the same location but appeared to be getting further away. I texted my neighbor and he came over, and also looked through the binoculars, and verified what I was seeing. The object appeared white in color and shaped like a donut, dark in the center but clearly white in color. using a compass I determined the object to be in an easterly direction of 100 degrees from my location. while viewing the object several aircraft where in the area.I was able to view the object for over an hour, and took a few photos of it with my camera phone. Not the best way to photograph it, but it was all I had at the time.The attached photo's can be enlarged and the object appears near and above the drooping tree limb on the left tree
