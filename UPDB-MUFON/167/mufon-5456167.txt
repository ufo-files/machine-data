It was a beautiful clear day. Bright & sunny. I just finished cutting my front lawn. When completed I pulled out my lawn chair and sat in my driveway having something cool to drink & some nuts to munch on. I was looking up & watching the hawks in our area floating on the O/H air currents. Then approaching from the East a orb crossed my eye sight. (The orb was traveling East to West in a straight line at a steady speed) At first I thought it was a bird but it never flapped it’s wings. As I stayed fixed on the orb I noticed it was all back. The orb did not change direction nor did it zigzag. It traveled in a straight line until the orb was out of sight. I do not have a photo of the orb. 
I know what I saw that day and I know it was NOT an aircraft. No wings or propellers. Too, I did NOT see any exhaust from the orb or any type of chem trail.
