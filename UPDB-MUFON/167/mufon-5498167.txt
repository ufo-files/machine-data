alien equivalent of mars rover

Whilst coming to the end of my dog walk we turned into Sunningdale Avenue in Irlam,I noticed something black crawling from the garden of number 64,Sunningdale Avenue which at first looked like a snake but I thought it can not be so I assumed it was a cat. We were about 35 yards away at this time but I could see it quite clearly as the street lighting has been upgraded to LED lighting. it was raining that night for the first time in a month.As my dog and I got nearer to it I noticed it huddled against the garden wall on the pavement, it looked just like a black ball of around 6 inches in diameter, my curiosity was aroused as its shape didn't look like a cat, as we got closer about 20 feet or so away from it I thought it will now run and hide under a nearby car which was parked nearby but it stayed where it was, until I got whithin 12 feet or so, at this point it started to roll towards us it then shot up in the air and became about 1 inch in diameter by about 3 feet in length it seemed to hang in the air and then started to spin at enormous speed and then dived down into the pavement and was gone. I was stunned and looked for a hole in the tarmac where it had disappeared but there was no hole there.Whilst it was spinning in front of me I saw coloured rings down its length which gave me the impression of lights on its underside.My dog made no attempt to attack this entity and seemed really quiet afterwards.I conclude that what I had seen was the alien equivalent of our Mars Rover but much more sophisticated. This sighting has been haunting me ever since invading my thoughts as I sleep.There is a good chance of fracking in this area soon so something my happen shortly.my questions remain whether it was being controlled remotely or are they here underground in this region.
