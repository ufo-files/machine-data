{tjd} A straight line of 18-20 objects which looked like satellites except were faster and more than 3-6 objects in a row.

I was walking my dog around the block and looking at the big dipper when the objects appeared to the left of the big dipper. At first there were 3 dots of light in a row equally spaced and travelling in a straight line just below the dipper and travelling faster than any high altitude planes and the lights were not blinking. There was no sound. They appeared like a satellite does except they travelled a little faster but on the same course. After the first 3 appeared , a small space occurred then a group of 5-6 lights appeared to follow the first 3 . They were grouped closer to each other. After this group came a smaller group of 4 and then 3 lights came after the other groups ahead all travelling the same speed and the same path. Again I noticed no sound or erratic behavior of these lights. When the last 3 objects which were spaced evenly apart from each other passed,no others were observed behind the line.
