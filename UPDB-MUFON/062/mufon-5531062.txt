Very bright intermitent flashes of light.

I stepped outside for a cigarette and seen a very bright flash of light above my head. When I looked up I expected to see a firefly as they are common this time of night/year. Instead I seen a very dim point of light almost unviewable traveling from south to north that within a minute traveled through the little dipper. The object appeared to be in the outer atmosphere. While viewing this point of light it flashed very brightly every few seconds (approximately 5 to 7 seconds). It flashed very brightly 2 or 3 times before it apparently quit flashing. It was at this time I lost sight of it. The object would not have been noticed if it did not flash. There were no other witnesses, photos.
