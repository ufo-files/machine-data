{cak} Odd sightings around I-79 in Bridgeport

I often hang around the area of the I79 rest area's, in the woods out back, on the weekends and I have spotted multiple objects on multiple occasions. I usually come in contact with these objects on weekends in the very late hours like 3-4am. I just discovered this website so my report is not really being made in a timely manner, but the date I used was the most recent. These objects are cylinder shaped and vary in size greatly. I have seen them appear in different colors, but the largest ones are very dark in color. Which is why I don't think the motorists on I79 ever see them, they do not have any lights. I fear people may find out what I am doing and look at me in a different way so i did not include my name in this report. These objects will appear often and the only stay a short time 2-3 minutes. When they are gone they usually leave behind a chem trail. I do not know what they are, we do have an airport in the area about 1 mile away in a strait line so some could be coming from there. It almost seems like they know the schedule of when I will be at the spot and know to come there. I almost feel like they could be stalking me. I know that sounds crazy. I have not ever lost time or felt strange/assaulted, so maybe they are harmless and just observing me. it does make me a little paranoid. I am not really sure if they could just be the alphabet boys... aka the FBI.... Have you heard any similar reports? Please reply to my by email only, I would be glad to hear similar reports, and maybe exchange some pictures I have collected of the objects with my phone, and some other guys have sent me.
