{cak} A glowing orange/red ball hovered, then split into 4 smaller orange/red balls, which then shot off in 4 separate directions and vanished.

I was driving at night on highway 98 in Gulf Breeze, FL, going through Naval Live Oaks, the National Seashore. All of a sudden there was this ball in the sky in front of me, floating above the highway, just above tree height. It was big and solid but glowing like it's a small star and getting near it would burn you, and orange with like a hint of red in it. It hovered there for a minute or so, it was hard to keep track of time while this was going on. Then it split into 4 balls of the same color and shape and shot into 4 equal directions, like from the corners of a square. They had small tails when they did this, but they were moving so crazy fast! They didn't go far from their start points and then at the same moment, they just vanished. I googled at the time to try and find a logical explination and all I found was lightning balls and I knew without a doubt that was NOT it. I watched your show and thought I should google it again and it was everywhere now! Then in the episode about the Texas sightings I decided to tell y'all. This is not my first experience. Starting from the age of 2, strange occurrences have happened.
