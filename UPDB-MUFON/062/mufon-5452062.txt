Star-like object moving across suddenly reversed course and shot across sky at a very high rate of speed

At approximately 3:20 am on 6/19/2020, I couldn't sleep, so I decided to look at my Heavens Above app on my phone to see if there was anything that I could go outside to view at that time. As I walked out of my front door and used the app to determine the location of the satellite that was passing over at approximately 60 degrees and heading from a southeast to northerly direction I fixed my eyes on a fairly dim blue, star like object that was not blinking and was trying to determine if it was moving, as that was location where the satellite should have been at that particular time. After staring at it for about 2 seconds, the object started to move towards the north a what appeared to be the speed of a satellite traveling at a very high altitude. After another 2 seconds of watching the object continue on it's path to the north, the object suddenly reversed direction, 180 degrees, without any visible turn and shot away to the Southeast at a very high rate of speed - I would estimate that it cross the 75% of the sky to the horizon in about 1.5 seconds. There was no sound, only complete silence which did not surprise me, because I no time did I feel that this object was in our atmosphere and any closer that your fairly typical satellite. The whole situation was pretty amazing and unfortunately I do not have any further details than that, but I know what satellites look like, I know what airplanes and other manned aircraft look like in the night sky, and I can assure you that this object was controlled by an intelligent being and the g forces that any human occupant would have been, with the sudden reversal of direction and extreme speed at which it crossed the sky, their is no living being that I know of that could have survived those forces.
