At ~3AM my friend and I were swimming in my pool. We laid down on lounge chairs to look at the stars. Off in the distance we saw what looked like a UFO. Having the phenomenal gift to be able to "call in" UFO's, I asked my friend to call in the UFO we were watching. She did so. Within a few short minutes we were standing directly beneath the UFO. It was so close it barely skimmed over my roof top. It glided through the air completely silent. It was triangle-shaped and had numerous large white lights. It glided by very slow, as if to give us a good look at it. We watched it as long as we could see it and then it was gone. The next day I looked up a picture of the Stealth Bomber, in anticipation of the debunkers' comments. What we saw was NOT a Stealth Bomber; the light configuration was very different and the UFO did not make a sound as it glided by overhead.
We were so awestruck we didn't think to get the cameras.
