Two moving objects in night sky

I looked into the night sky and observed what I thought to be a satelite moving quickly through the night sky, as satelites do. But then I noticed another object moving, hundreds of miles from the first object, both seemed to be mirroring one another, moving at the same speed and keeping a precise distance from each other. That in itself was strange, but when they both moved in a jagged, off balanced manner, both in complete and utter sinc with one another, that was really weird. It was as though both objects decided to trip at the same time and in the same manner with precisely the same off balanceness, even though they were hundreds of miles a part. They were two objects because the stars could be seen between them. I watched them both until they were out of site beyond the tree line.
