{jpg} Object Flashed and Disappeared

March 10, 2020 at 2125. Looking Northwest at 80° attitude in a dark section of the sky, a sudden and only momentary white to redish tinged flash of light appeared approximately the size and brightness of saturn. The flash to fade was momentary perhaps only a second in duration. It reminded my of the opening credits in Star Trek Enterprise when the Enterprise went warp and disappeared with a flash of light.
