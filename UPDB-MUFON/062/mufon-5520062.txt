No joking, i actually saw a large ufo in De Pere

My girlfriend and I went outside for a smoke, and overhead moved the most amazing craft I have ever seen in my life. It literally, appeared from directly over the roof at which point I was able to see the craft very clearly and at a very low altitude. It was triangular in shape with rounded edges and a holographic pattern lined the bottom of the craft emitting the view of the Crystal clear night sky. I pointed for my girlfriend to follow it however, by the time she could look it was obstructed by trees. Its heading was from the Southeast moving rapidly, and without sound towards the north west. It had to have flown directly over highway 41.

Im reporting this instantly, as Im utterly shocked at the magnitude and speed of this object.

When i first noticed the object I thought it was a satellite until I realized how low the object actually was. My girlfriend did not see this in time, but my reaction was awe and excitement.
