20+ unknown objects and at least 6 shooting stars

We are currently staying in Ballyconneely, Galway. Last night at 2am, we were watching the sky as it was a rare sight being entirely clear so you can see the stars perfectly. We were stunned by the beauty, we began to notice some of the stars had started to move. It began as one bright star following a straight path, then in changed direction and began zig zagging through the sky. Each one we saw would eventually burn out or would begin to go upward until the light faded to the point we couldn’t see it anymore. We were watching for around 45 minutes before we decided to go to bed. The activity never stopped it was amazing. We spotted over 20 and also during this time we saw at least 6 shooting stars. Two of these were not normal shooting stars, it was like a much brighter and faster flash that quickly disappeared. I seriously still can’t believe what we saw! There was also a bright orange orb hovering in the distance. We originally thought it was a satellite but then after all the other activity seen, I’m really not sure. Unforgettable night. I’ve been googling how and who to report this to and came across this group.
