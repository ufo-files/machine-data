{jpg} Saw what appeared as a red orb move very fast from the north to the southeast

While standing on my front porch last night, I observed what appeared to be a red orb come out of the north through the trees. It moved very fast; then slowed as it passed several trees. It moved above an apartment building so as to be seen in full view with no obstruction. This orb stopped and gave me the ominous feeling like it knew I was watching it. It was only seconds and then moved on quickly, heading southeast. It still kinda freaks me out that it stopped and gave me that feeling it knew I had seen it.
