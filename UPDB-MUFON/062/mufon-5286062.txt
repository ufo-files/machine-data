Aliens (2) seen

I have not seen an unidentified flying object; however I did have an alien encounter while out with a friend in the early morning hours of August 14th. My friend and I were laying on the Treasure Island shore on a girls trip watching the meteor shower. We had turned over and propped ourselves up on our knees to shake the sand out of our hair when we saw an abnormally tall and thin figure weaving between the beach and taller grasses far behind the coast. This thing was moving in such a way that it was hard to track, but my friend and I continued to see the same unexplained thing. We were afraid and decided to crawl from the shore into the ocean as to protect ourselves. Soon after, another very tall and thin figure seemed to float in front of us along the coastline. The arms were so long with large cupping-like hands, that the total appendages fell much below the knees. My friend muttered something below her breath, and the creature seemed to turn its head to watch us but continued to float backwards continuing to watch us the entire time. My friend and I were terrified as this creature seemed to travel a large distance still watching us as it went backwards and there came a point that we had been scared for what felt like so long that we got up and ran all the way from the water to where we were staying (small beach apt) leaving behind our belongings. The creature’s tall thin outline that had floated past us could still be seen to the right in the distance which was unexplainable as the sun hadn’t come out yet.

Both of us live in Indiana (even though this had happened in Florida) and feel our lives have been forever changed.
