{cak} Rocket type with two rows of yellow lights along the bottom

My 28 year old daughter and I were sitting outside at the end of our drive way as we often do . We had seen some lights that looked like camera flashes in the sky south and southeast of us . We kept watching for them and saw them a few times . At about 12:00 am (midnight)we were watching some other weird things in the sky that were not airplanes or helicopter as they were making no noise at all and they would appear and then disappear , We at first thought they were stars , But then we felt something overhead and we both looked up to see a craft that was shaped like a missile or rocket . It was long and had short wing looking things on both sides towards the back and the front of the craft , There was 2 rows of yellow lights going along the length of the craft . My daughter had her phone with her but we were so stunned that we didn't think to take pictures of the thing , We watched it as it went slowly over head .It made no noise at all it was totally silent .As it went passed we watched it as far as we could watch it and then it veered off to the right or to the west and then for a few seconds we lost site of it . We caught site of it again across the highway and over towards the college so we went down to the entrance of where we live and we saw it again heading west . It went higher and then just was gone , I don't know if it disappeared or it just went so fast away that we could not see it leaving , We have seen a lot of things in the sky over where we live and a lot of it we can not explain , We see airplanes and helicopters but then we see things that are not . We have seen a saucer shaped ufo fly over and when it turned lightly we saw a rounded top on it but could not see anyone inside . We have seen round one with strobing lights and flashing lights that are too bright to be planes .About a week after we had our siting of the rocket shaped ufo I was on face book and one of my face book friends had drawn a picture of a ufo that someone he knows saw in Fresno California I believe and it was the same thing that my daughter and I saw ,
