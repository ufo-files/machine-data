(GS) 3 bright orange/red glowing lights hovering and moving slowly in vary directions, then faded completely out of sight one by one.

We were traveling East on Route 460 in Lynchburg, Virginia. At the Candlers Mountain exit, we noticed 3 bright glowing light reddish/orange lights hovering and moving slowly on the North side of Route 460. First the 3 lights were aligned in a vertical formation, and were far from each other. Then they slowly moved into a horizontal alignment (one stayed in place). We turned the car around to get a closer look. When we turned around, one light was missing, then another faded out of sight, and finally the third one faded completetely out of sight. We didn't see them any more so we left. There were also many towers with darker red lights all over them, which were obviously staionary. These didn't look like them at all... they were very different in color, size, and distance. These lights were as high as airplanes, but bigger. Towers don't move or float around in the sky... or disappear.
