{rjl} Daytime: rectangular shining light craft with dome top and bottom

On June 7, 2019, during early evening, I was headed to my car parked in Trader Joe's (Creve Coeur, MO) parking lot. I normally don't park facing Olive Blvd., but this time I did, and since then, I do, more regularly. Facing southward, I looked up to the, basically, cloudless sky, and saw a whitish - glowing rectangular craft traveling slowly and quietly across the sky from West to East. I've seen and photographed them before, and decided to take a cell phone picture to examine when I got home.
