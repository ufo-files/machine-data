{jpg} V-shaped object wobbling as it flew over San Diego in NNE direction. Moderate/slow speed.

Was out smoking on the balcony. I like to look at the stars. I look up into the sky and see something moving almost above me. Could have been 3-5 miles above. The sky was dark gray. Not black. I saw a large V-shaped (not triangular) slightly lighter shade of gray (solid and dull) which appears to have 3 large dimmed lights on each side. A lighter shade of gray and not as well defined as the v-shaped. I looked down. Looked back up. Spotted it again and watched it flying NNE. I appeared wobbly, but only slight. I first saw it when I would have been just south of the Mexico border and watched it travel possibly 30 more miles (once it passed me) over 15-20 seconds to eventually blend in with the sky. No sound.
