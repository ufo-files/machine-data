Light in sky with abrupt changes in direction and velocity

My brother and I were watching satellites while camping (Satellites are dim whiteish lights that travel at a consistent path and speed). We noticed one light that was kind of yellow/orange and a bit brighter but traveling at a consistent speed across the sky from south to north. Then it abruptly turned left at a 60 degree angle at a higher consistent speed and a second later abruptly turned right at about a 30 degree angle again at a even faster consistent speed, accelerating out of sight with a small trail behind it.
