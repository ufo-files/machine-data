{cak} Very large craft ascension.

While trying to fall asleep, looking out my window, I saw three orbs that made a shape of a triangle. Bright red/orange lights. Total size was about larger than a 3,000sq house. I yelled at my girlfriend to wake her up. She woke up mad that I was loud and woke her. I told her look out the window!! We both sat there watching it wondering what it was. The craft hovered closer to our complex, then in a downward swooping motion, landed. The lights disappeared and I could not see any craft. Like as if it went inside the earth without breaking ground.. She saw it and she does not believe in ufo's nor et life. But she has changed her outlook since.
