Very bright flash in sky

Outside taking out trash and saw a very bright green/turquoise strobe above the northeast 
Horizon it was flashing like morse code rapidly and lasted about 3 seconds. 
I cannot describe the colour other than turquoise green and blue. The colour was the most incedible thing I have ever seen in the sky and also the brightest. There were no aircraft that I could see at the time.
