{rjl} Bright orb of light at end of a visible light beam from above

A friend of mine sent me a 30 second video from his security camera that was apparently triggered by a cat (shown in the video). About 10 seconds into the video, a blob of bright light appears in the lower right hand corner with a beam of light shining from above. It appears to move around on the ground a bit then appears to raise up into the light beam from above until it disappears from view at the end of the video.

Things considered:
1) Helicopter search light but not as wide and dispersed as that should be. No noise reported by home occupants.

2) Drone flying overhead but nature of the light not consistent with a known source.

3) "Pixelation" or other malfunction of digital camera. The equipment is reported to be in good working order.

It's uncertain whether this is an actual UFO in itself or a projection from something above.

My friend gave me permission to submit a report. Possibly some other reports similar around same time/area will coincide. We would like to hear of any others.
