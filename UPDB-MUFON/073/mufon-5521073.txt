UFOs over Budapest Liszt Ferenc International Airport

Three glowing objects fly with unusual motion above Budapest Liszt Ferenc International Airport. The video was made from P1cel, on 07. 31. 2011. 08:45 PM. One of the objects descends then again rises.

Landing aircrafts are visible on the video, but they look different. They move steadily and their navigational lights are flashing.
