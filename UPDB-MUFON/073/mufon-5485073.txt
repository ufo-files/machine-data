{rjl} Driving East in early morning. Saw stationary object above and in front of me. Took picture.

I volunteer with the local Humane Society and was on my way to care for the animals when I spotted a bright object in the sky. I watched it and noted that is was reflecting light and appeared to be stationary. No stars were observed at this time. I took a picture to show my family because I was thinking it could be a satellite. My car continued to function as usual. I did not have any sort of distress. The object stayed stationary till I arrived at my destination. When I got out of the car and looked up it was gone. No one was with me and I did not discuss this with anyone. My family said it would be a good idea to let MUFON see the pictures. Thank you for your time.
