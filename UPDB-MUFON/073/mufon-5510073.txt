{nd} Ball of orange and white light flying horizontally east. Was large when first sighted, then smaller when across the sky then fully disappeared.

Myself and two others were sitting outside and one person looked up and said "what is that?" We all got up and looked. One said is that a plane? No it kinda looks like a blimp on fire! It continued on a straight path going east and slowly got smaller as it got farther away. Two other people came outside just as it disappeared into the dark eastern sky. We were really confused and excited and are still talking about what it could have possibly when.
