{nd} Observed many and recorded some star like orbs flying / changing direction.

Family trip which included myself, my husband, my 4 daughters, and 1 granddaughter, to Great Wolf Lodge in Mason, Ohio on 5/18/16. The balcony of our room faced Kings Island. I recorded orbs flying around the Drop Zone ride (I think that is what it's called) at 22:16 for 1.43 seconds. And at 22:24 for 1.45 seconds. The ride was lit up, with 3 different colors very bright all night. It is very tall like a tower. I observed MANY with my 22 year old daughter. Recorded about 10, recorded 1 orb changing direction, a V path. We shouted to others in parking lot to look. We observed for at least an hour, then just went inside our room. I believe they were still out there.
