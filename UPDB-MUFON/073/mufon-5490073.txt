{rjl} Floating triangle with light orbs moving around it in same path over and over again kinda like how an atom looks with the orbs going around diagonally

I was camping at a camp ground in NE Pennsylvania USA, i was with my brother and nephew we were just playing around i was the second oldest there at age 22 my sister was the oldest there but for some odd reason refused to look/believe us. All 3 of us seen it. We just looked up in the sky and seen this strange triangle object floating and ever so slightly bouncing up and down and moving left and right while these orbs of different colors circled it. It was a clearish black triangle with clearly defined outline in a whiteish gold color. It just kept hovering in the same spot for about an hour. we eventually got scared and bored of it. Our necks started to hurt looking at it, it eventually started to rise up higher and then completely vanished but it seemed it moved upward extremely fast ever so slightly before it vanished. The orbs we're just balls of light very round that floated one after another in the same direction around the object. It is something I'll never forget neither will my brother or nephew. My sister had a phone but said her phone camera was broke which I'm very mad about because I think she was lying she showed no interest in looking at this amazing object its very strange how she acted that night. I think she didn't believe us and was scared to even look because it would shock her. I regret not having a camera and carry my camera everywhere I go just in case I ever see something like it again.
