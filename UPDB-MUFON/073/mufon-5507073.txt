strange objects caught on photos

I was on my deck at my house taking pictures of the sunset. Today, about 2 weeks later, I uploaded the pictures to my computer for editing. I noticed a strange image on one of the photos. I thought it was a small plane or maybe a dust particle on the camera lens but the shots I took immediately after and before did not have that image. I took the images consecutively, all within seconds of each other. The closer I zoomed into the other images I was able to see some unidentifiable object in each of the images, just not as clear as the first one I saw. I will upload the images. They have not been edited except for I think I cropped one of the images. The image with the most evident object is #4404, you can see it under the moon, kind of. The first shot (4403) only has the bright orb on the uppermost left hand side of the photo, looks like a star, but it is not int he other images. In the rest of the images the object is unclear, more to the right but appears to be further away.
