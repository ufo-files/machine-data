UFO SEEN IN THE EASTERN SKY

I WAS IN THE BACK YARD OF THE HOUSE.. I LOOKED UP IN THE EASTERN SKY, A BRIGHT CIGAR SHAPED OBJECT WAS RACING ACROSS THE SKY. I YELLED AT MY FATHER TO COME OUTSIDE. MY FATHER SAID "IT'S MOVING WAY TOO FAST TO BE A PLANE, AND THERE'S NO CONTRAIL". AFTER ABOUT 2 AND A HALF MINUTES.. THE OBJECT INSTANTLY VANISHED FROM SIGHT.
