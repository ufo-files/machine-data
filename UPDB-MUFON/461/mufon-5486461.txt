I live just outside of Baltimore, Maryland. I took my dogs out, glanced up and saw what first looked like a pulse wave or a ripple across the water, but in the sky. Then I realized it was not a wave but a string of birds flying, but the speed was ridiculously fast, faster than the planes that are constantly circling to land at BWI airport. They flew faster than a Blue Angel, and soundlessly. It was over in a few seconds. It moved like individual birds in a flying “V” formation, it had elasticity, but there was no wing flapping. The objects were very faintly illuminated, or reflective. They flew overhead and across the sky and out of sight in a matter of 5-6 seconds.
