{tjd} Lights riding and falling

Driving farm truck from farm to house. Engine sputtered them cut off. Tried to restart numerous times but would not start. Shortly there after I noticed a glow in the woods to the right of me. Them lights began to rise and hover for a few minutes then float back to ground. Numerous lights some alone, some with others continued to rise and fall. After the last one went down and the glow disappeared I tried to start truck and it started right up. I had tried to start truck during the incident but it wouldn't start. After it started I drove on to the house about 5 miles away. The farm was about 5 miles away from the house and surrounded by trees. This was just one of the sightings I have observed over the years.
