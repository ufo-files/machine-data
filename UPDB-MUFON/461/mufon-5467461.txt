{jpg} Yellow-orange light, observed with binoculars

It was 12:15 AM and I went out to the kitchen to get a glass of water. I checked outside the window to see if there were any UFOs in the sky. That's when I noticed a yellow-orange light in the sky, so I rushed to get a pair of binoculars to see what it was.

When I got back to the kitchen, the UFO was pulsating in brightness and moved around some. I used the binoculars and saw a row of lights on the spacecraft. It was hard to tell what shape it was, but I'm guessing it was triangular. At one point, the lights on the craft appeared and disappeared.

By 12:55 AM, I could no longer make out the object.
