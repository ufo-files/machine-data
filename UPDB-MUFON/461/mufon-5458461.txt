{rjl} Saw massive object in sky.

Had early AM delivery to college for my job. As I was leaving, I veered my head to the left and caught a massive object far up in the sky in the distance. It was high in the clouds. It sat still for minutes on end. It was shaped like a giant hornet then disappeared into the clouds for a few seconds then reappeared in a stretched out letter W shape. It sat a few more minutes then disappeared back into the clouds again. I drove in that direction to see if I could sight it again but found nothing. A cop stopped me and asked if I was okay. I said yes and just stated I left the college and he drove off. During the time I was watching the object, I took pictures.
