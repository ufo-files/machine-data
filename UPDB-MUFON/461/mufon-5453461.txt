{rjl} Bright red lights in Triangular form

My buddy and I were out on the golf course when all of a sudden we both jolt up with no words at the exact same time as if we both had the same shock feeling. No more than 2 minutes after we look up and see what looks like 3 bright red stars slowly gliding over the night sky. After following it in the sky for about 10 minutes it disappeared over the Rincon Mountains. Shortly after a giant military plane with searchlights slowly flys over the area in an unusually low manner over houses looking for something.
