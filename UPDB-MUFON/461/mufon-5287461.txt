Perfectly spherical black orb with smaller orbs of light around it.

I was walking my dog when I noticed a black orb flying in a straight path, and I initially thought it was reflecting sunlight. I got my phone out (XS MAX with the telescopic lense) and recorded it. I made 2 videos, the first being 1 minute and the second being 30 seconds. Upon viewing the footage, I zoomed in and noticed the light "reflecting" was not a reflection, but a separate orb/ orbs of light rotating around the craft, at one point detaching fully.

As the videos have been heavily compressed, I will upload zoomed in screen shots of uncompressed frames that show the strangest parts of the footage. I would like to add that the orbs of light in the freeze-frames were not in that position the frame before, it was as if, within a frame (60th of a second), the orb had moved across the craft to that position. I have no idea how the videos will appear to you (regarding compression), but on my phone the orbs are detailed enough to be sperate objects from the main craft.

I made a third video, zooming in and slowing down the crafts lights. Near the end of this video, I reverse it in slow-motion, allowing you to see the orb of light at the bottom right hand part of the craft (from my POV), proving it isn't a reflection from the sun.
