White cigar shape object over mountain from Bergen to Oslo

Whilst driving over the mountains from Bergen to Oslo No way I noticed what I thought was a white plane flying in the sky but I did not notice any wings or engines or markings on the plane so I decided to take a photo on my mobile phone. The object was white I could not see any windows and it was flying in a straight line and disappeared behind the top of the mountain and the horizon. It wasn't until I zoomed in to the photo on my phone that I noticed other strange anomolys and maybe other objects.
