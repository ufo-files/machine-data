{rjl} White orb hovers in broad daylight. then moves behind building and disappears

I just got off work and went to my apartment near I-10 and Elliot Rd. I was going to the mail boxes when I saw something in the sky. I went back and got my camera and started videoing. The object hovers in place for a minute. It at times appears to have a different shape than just an orb. Then the object starts to accelerate slowly behind the building. I zoom out and try to walk in front of the building but by the time I do the object is gone.
