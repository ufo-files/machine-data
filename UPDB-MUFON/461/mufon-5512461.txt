on the 28th november i kept popping out of my front door to look for visible colour changes to the moon as an eclipse was due that evening, it got to 2am and i thought i would have one last look before going to bed. i was out on my front path when i noticed a bright white star like object in the sky where there shouldnt have been one, i stargaze quite a bit so know what should be where in the night sky. as i watched it i realized it was moving towards me travelling from south to north and as it did it seemed to uncover another behind it which was lemon in colour, this was followed by five more white ones, one more lemon then another white, 9 in total all perfectly spaced,all solid round balls, bright but not glowing, by the time this last one appeard the first was overhead, i would say as high as a transatlantic flight but with no noise at all, im use to planes going over as we are on a flight path and we do faintly hear a rumble of the engines, these were silent. by the time the last was made visible the 1st object was overhead, the time it took them all in a long line to go diagonally over my house and disappear over the horizon to the north was less than a minute. we do live by a marine base but if this was aircraft on night training its like nothing ive seen before, the silence and speed made it quite scarey. i went in locked up and went to bed but its played on my mind since as it was so creepy, unusual,strange and so weird i felt i needed to inform somebody.
