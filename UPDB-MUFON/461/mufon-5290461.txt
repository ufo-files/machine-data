Brief visual of around 20 white/flashing objects moving across the eastern daylight sky in a direct path towards to ocean

A flash had caught my eye in the eastern sky while sitting on my porch talking to my neighbors. As I directed my attention to it, I saw two flashing objects and as I focused I could see a cluster of what looked like around 20 white/light objects moving across the sky towards the ocean. I quickly alerted my neighbors as we ran to the street to see if they were still visible but they fell behind the view of the houses along the street and couldn’t be seen anymore. The neighbors did not give a visual. The time I was able to visually see anything lasted maybe 15-20 seconds, and happened very quickly. The cluster was gathered almost like a flock of birds and maintained a constant distance apart, but they were visible long enough to know they weren’t birds and were flying way too low to be satellites. Some time around 15-20 minutes or so later, there was what appeared to be a smaller black/dark-colored plane flying around the same airspace that moved slowly across the sky from south towards east. It was high enough that I couldn’t get a good visual with my binoculars I quickly brought out after the sighting. A little over an hour has passed and it has since gone dark outside and no further activity has been observed. Was a very exciting experience and would love to know if there is a known explanation for it! While I couldn’t capture any footage the objects were extremely similar in appearance to the sighting recently reported in Washington DC upon looking up recent submissions, but did not zip across the sky as fast as those did and seemed more uniform in formation.
