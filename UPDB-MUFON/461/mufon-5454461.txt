{rjl} light in the sky changing direction and another light reacting

I saw a light traveling across the western sky that suddenly intensified and went dim. It intensified again in another location as if it were traveling in a straight line. Right after the light intensified the second time i witnessed a bright orange trail of light, that from my point of view would appear to make a "J" in the sky, as if it had dramatically changed its trajectory. The light intensified for a third time much much brighter and it was a very bright golden color for about 3 seconds. Adjacent to this light, another light flashed across the sky at an extremely high rate of speed and left an orange trail. It was as if it were fleeing from the first light. Then, the first light went dim and shot off in the opposite direction leaving a small orange trail that quickly faded away.
