2 bright white lights side by side blinking on and off at the same time being followed by aircraft

I was awaken by my son at approximately 11:30 pm on 8/30/11. He said there were a lot of strange lights in the sky... My wife went outside with him and I grabbed my binoculars and joined them on the front porch. My son pointed the lights out to me and I looked up then looked through the binoculars. I was surprised to see the amount of lights all in the same general area. The sky was clear. They appeared to me to be military aircraft, since I was always told that the military aircraft flew much higher than the civilian aircraft. I noticed that there were two bright white lights set side by side similar to an automobile. The lights were both blinking off and on at the same time. I have never noticed any aircraft doing this before. I noticed that the pair of lights were followed by what appeared to be a typical aircraft. My son pointed out to me that there more at another location. I looked in that direction then looked through the binoculars and noticed the same type of lights blinking off and on as the first set. This second set of lights was also being followed by what appeared to be a typical aircraft. We watched these lights for approximately 5-7 minutes until they went out of view due to the tree lines. There were also a few aircraft flying in other directions. This area is a normal flight path for civilian airlines; but what we saw definitely was not a civilian aircraft.
