I was sitting in the courtyard outside my apartment. I looked up at the half moon, saw a plane flying by and then noticed another object in the sky. I noticed the object was moving slower and was not a airplane or helicopter. I thought the object could have been another plane at first. The object cut across and down across the sky in a south-south east direction. It then stopped. I went in before it stopped and grabbed my camera. I realized the object was too far away for my camera to capture an image of it. Then I really felt like it was looking at me. Weird. I then thought to myself, if it doesn't want me to see it better than I don't want to see it. I thought this as I walked back into my apartment and put the camera down. I came back out and could not see it.I mainly wanted to report this just in case someone else saw it too. I did see another plane fly by where it had been a couple of minutes later and am almost sure it was a Southwest airline plane. The plane seemed higher than usual though for this area since I am not far from LAX.
