{cak} White Cigar-Shaped/Cylinder - very narrow, going east above clouds

On September 20, at approximately 6:60 PM, CST, I saw a white object shaped like a line going east. I saw it because I was watching a commercial jet directly overhead, and the object caught my eye in between cloud cover.

I wasnt going to file a report until I saw another report in your database from Bloomington, IL that reminds me of what I saw. The URL for that case is: [Case file 42603/cms/tg] http://mufoncms.com/cgi-bin/report_handler.pl?req=view_long_desc&id=42603&rnd=

I did not see as much detail as the other person reported. In George Filers most recent newsletter (Filers Files #38 2012), someone in Ripon, California reported seeing an object similar to the one I am reporting now.

This sighting lasted no more than 5-6 seconds. I noticed it because I was sitting on my balcony, facing east. A commercial jet passed over, under the clouds, and I saw the white object between the clouds. At first I thought it was another cloud moving above a bigger cloud, but it was moving in a different direction in a straight line. It looked white, not reflective or glowing. It was very narrow, like a swizzle stick, but not as long. From my POV the object was above commercial traffic and smaller than a Tic Tac, but again, very narrow and seemed solid. It seems like the pilot might not have been able to see it because it was basically right above him. I waited for the object to emerge on the other side of the cloud but it never did. I kept looking but it was gone and another jet flew over. It did seem to move slightly slower than the jet.

I have reported 3 different objects before, and I have spoken to field investigators from MUFON (really terrific guys, and very thorough). Those cases were marked as "unknown". Ive seen many objects in Forest Park, and I live between OHare and Midway, so Im familiar with traffic patterns and different types of conventional aircraft. When something looks different than what we expect to see in the sky near airports, it stands out. The object I saw yesterday wasnt something I typically see in the sky. It was kind of remarkable how it suddenly disappeared.

Last thing I wanted to mention is that the skies were pretty quiet here this summer. I have seen orbs and things (objects that seem "cloaked", for example) but noticed more of them over the winter and spring. In the past week, activity has picked up a bit.

Thanks ever so much for taking the information and I appreciate your hard work. Warm regards to all.
