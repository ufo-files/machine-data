The enormous object was moving vertically up along the cliffs off the Hudson River and then hovered above the trees. I watched it behind the trees as I was getting off exit 1 off the Palisades Parkway. My car was facing the Hudson River. I slowed down and saw this object rise up slowly behind the trees and hovered above my car and kept moving up and disappeared when I looked back. I was driving the whole time and didn't stop. I regret not stopping. It took me a couple of minutes to comprehend what I was seeing.
