{cak} Seen hovering over south st.louis just under clouds then pivoted and rose through clouds.

Driving to work at 5:20 am on hwy 64 westbound around Washington park. Spotted a large triangular object hovering just under the clouds. It was still twilight so the only thing I could see was the orange glow of the light being omitted from the craft which was three lights. One large bar and two orbs to the "rear". It was facing north east then pivoted east tilted 20 degrees and rose through the clouds. This lasted for about 5 min. Also judging by distance and height compared to the St. Louis arch it must have been a mile in diameter.
