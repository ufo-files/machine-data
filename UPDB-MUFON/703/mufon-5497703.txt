{dr}Baton shape white lights at each end square black windows on body

On Saturday Nov 22 at 6 pm to the south east, I saw something like I have never seen before. I saw a baton shaped craft that had white lights on each end and the middle was all black square windows. This thing turned end over end 3 times then came to a stop in the vertical position. While it was stopped it seemed to separate or get longer in length. At this point the lights went out and it dissappeared. There was no other air raft of any kind remotely near.
