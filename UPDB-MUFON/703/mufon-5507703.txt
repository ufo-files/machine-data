An nearly invisible light wave, like UV random light.

Date: 22-09-2013
Time: 20.00 hrs (GMT+5.30)

I was cleaning my camera in the veranda. When I turned it on after opening and cleaning it, I saw unusual violet light in the screen when I pointed it to outdoors. I thought it was lens error and the UV filter isnt working properly. Right then I realized this is night and there shouldnt be sunlight or UV rays. I turned my camera on and came out of the door . There was no visible light but a high intensity violet light was visible in camera . I zoomed to the light point and it looked like a small ball, or oval like light source and had two semi vertical lines (one at top and one at bottom) made of same light. The point that was source of the light had a big tree nearby and didn't had any street light nearby. 
I frightened and came back to house and then thought that I should record the light. I went out again with my camera this time with memory card, but the light was gone. I looked with torch but didn't see anything in the place.
