Driving home from work my video camera recorded a red cloud like UFO and then a silver cylinder UFO.

Back in November 2016 I was driving on Merrillvillel Road from work heading home and had my camera filming. My camera filmed a red cloud like UFO over a field. It instantly disappeared behind some foliage as seen in the video if you go frame by frame. Next the camera filmed a silver cylinder shaped UFO appear out of nowhere over the same field and fly straight towards and over me heading from North to South as seen in the video I'm posting here with this report. This all happened in approximately three seconds.
