Continuous trail of lights going in the same direction for several minutes

At five past six this morning I took my dog onto the back garden, it was still dark and there was quite a few stars out.
When I looked up I noticed a string of lights moving across the sky, it was like a conveyor belt of satellites. They were relatively equally spaced and I realised that there were lights already vanishing in the east and more coming from the west. I could see at least 10 in the sky at the same time, all going in the same direction, all bright and white, some brighter than others in what appeared to be an endless stream. There was no sound and they were at high altitude, they looked like satellites, but I've never seen a chain of them before, I counted over 20 but lost count when I went to get my wife to witness it. The last part of the procession was randomly grouped, about 5 of them in a cluster, but going in the same direction, west to east.
My wife had her mobile phone and tried to video it but we've not checked the video yet. I was too excited to go back to bed and needed to check the news to see if had been reported anywhere. So far, I haven't seen anything from anywhere else confirming this unusual sighting. So far I've not found any one else who saw it. It was awesome to see and I still feel quite excited to have seen it.
