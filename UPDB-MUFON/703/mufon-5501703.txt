Metallic sphere moving across the sky. Silent. Reflected the Sun.

Approx 6:30 pm, watching seagulls chase flying ants in a cloud free sky, noticed a silvery metallic object moving across the sky in a north westerly direction. The object maintained a steady pace and altitude. The object reflected the sun for a moment as it moved across the sky and disappeared from view. There were no planes in the sky at the time. The entire sighting took approx 10 from the time it entered my field of view until it moved too far away to be seen.
