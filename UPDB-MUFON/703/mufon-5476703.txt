{dr}3 gold balls darting around eachother, then went together to bigger gold ball which then zoomed up and gone

Gulf coast off Bradenton Florida, 1971ish. I was out in the gulf swimming, about 100 yds from the shore (the tree line was right on the beach, the beach wasn't wide there}, clear sunny day, flat water, not many people around. I looked back to the shore and saw 3 gold balls in the sky over the treeline. No real idea of their size, but they weren't tiny at all. They were shiny, not glowing with light, more like a gold christmas tree ball.The 3 balls were darting around, back and forth, up and down, for maybe 20 seconds. I watched them in wonder! Then as one, they came together and formed a slightly bigger gold ball, which then zoomed up at a sharp angle and was gone. This is the first time I have told this officially. Not much but I KNOW WHAT I SAW and feel really lucky to have seen them!!!
