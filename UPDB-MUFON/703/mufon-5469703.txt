I saw 3 ufos with red glowing and blinking lights in Pedregal Hills, Cabo San Lucas.

At about 11pm almost midnight on a beautiful clear night, about year 2013, I saw 3 disc-shaped ufos, about 160 feet (or 50 meters) away, in the west or north-west direction, when I started walking down from about middle of Pedregal Hills in Cabo San Lucas, Mexico. I realised they were ufos because they were hovering, and their flight paths were with quick directional changes (changing positions very fast, erratically, and making erratic patterns in the night sky). They were 3 bright glowing red lights, sometimes the lights were also blinking. They amazed/ perplexed me all through my 20-minute walk down the hill (Pedregal Hill). There was nobody around me, so I was quite scared, and I couldn't wait to ask someone what those 3 ufos were ! When I reached the guard-house of Pedregal Hills near the base, I asked the 3 security-guards what those 3 ufos were, as we could still see the 3 ufos there in the sky ! The security-guards said they were also observing the 3 ufos, and had never seen anything like that before ! The security-guards were also perplexed by those 3 ufos ! Thereafter, I continued walking to my apartment, into the streets with taller buildings, and I stopped looking into the sky. I talked to my friends once in a while about my sightings, but they did not have the same sightings ! I decided to report this now because recently I started watching youtube videos and realised that other people have reported their sightings, and some of the videos even showed the same 3 ufos that I saw (with the same red lights, same erratic changing patterns, and same blinking lights, hovering with flight paths of quick directional changes) !
