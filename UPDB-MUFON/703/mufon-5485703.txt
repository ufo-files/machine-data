Me my wife, my 2 nieces and 2 daughters were sitting in the car waiting on my sisters to come out this house from getting one of their hair done, I looked south in the sky about 3 blocks down and saw a round white light rising from the trees, I knew it was something out of the ordinary, the light blinking on and off slowly, after rising above the trees it started to move straight towards us moving very slowly quietly, light still blinking slowly, it flew over the car slowly it was a black triangular shape object with just that one light. I wanted to jump out of the car and get a better look until my wife grabbed my arm and told me not too, It flew until we couldn't see it anymore
