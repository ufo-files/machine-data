UNKNOWN SHAPED OBJECT WITH LARGE BLUE LIGHT ON ONE SIDE AND RED LIGHT ON OTHER SIDE

I WAS DRIVING NORTH ON I 205 AT AROUND 8PM. I NOTICED 2 LARGE LIGHTS ONE BLUE AND ONE RED SIDE BY SIDE THAT SEEMED TO BE HOVERING ABOVE AN OVERPASS. I LOST SIGHT OF IT AS I WENT UNDER THE OVERPASS. WHEN I PASSED UNDER THE OVERPASS, THE LIGHTS WERE VISABLE AGAIN. I THOUGHT IT MIGHT BE LIGHTS ON AN AIRPLANE, BUT THEN THE LIGHTS SUDDENLY MOVED WEST AT A HIGH RATE OF SPEED. IT THEN SEEMED TO TURN NORTH. THEN THE LIGHTS WENT BACK AND FORTH WEST TO EAST. I LITERALLY WAS DUMBFOUNDED AS I OBSERVED THE OBJECT FOR ABOUT ONE MINUTE. I PASSED THE OBJECT AT THIS POUNT, BUT I COULD SEE IT IN MY REARVIEW MIRROR FOR ANOTHER 30 SECOND. THEN IT WAS OUT OF SIGHT. THERE IS NO WAY IT WAS AN AIRPLANE OR A HELICOPTER. THERE WAS HEAVY TRAFFIC ON I 205 AT THE TIME. OTHER PEOPLE MUST HAVE SEEN IT ALSO.I WAS AWESTRUCK FOR ABOUT 30 MINUTES, AS I TRIED TO WRAP MY MIND AROUND WHAT IT COULD HAVE BEEN.
