{rjl} Three lights, two that were white and as bright as a star when viewed from miles away. One that was red. Flew in a triangular formation.

I was on my way to work at G.E I live by state street and 12th. I was heading east towards G.E when I noticed a bright light in the sky above the horizon. I debated on whether or not it was a star of some sort or craft. As I continued I decided that it was most likely a start due to the fact that it never changed direction or position in the sky and was very bright. However when I got to G.E I realized that it was a craft of some sort heading west in the direction from which I came. Two white bright lights were leading followed by a bright pulsating red light. They were traveling roughly 40 miles Mph. I stopped and stepped out of the car to view this craft(s). At that time they changed position from a horizontal triangular formation to a vertical triangular formation with the same light positions. Therefore I am not sure whether or not this was one craft with exterior lights or individual crafts shielded by bright lights. In the end they abruptly changed direction at a 90 degree turn from going west to going south and I then lost sight as they went over the buildings. Which allowed me to estimate the height to approximately 300-500 feet in the air.
