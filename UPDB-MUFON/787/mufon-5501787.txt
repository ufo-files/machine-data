{dr}pink/white orb of light flew E to W, viewed from window

Not certain what I saw as there is a storm going on with a lot of lightning. I was sitting on my couch with the shades open on the windows to my left. I saw a very bright light moving quickly out of the corner of my eye. It was bright pink and white combined. It was flying in an arc E to W, probably following Manchester Road as my home sits a few streets back from W Manchester and the windows are towards Manchester. By the time I turned to look directly at it, it was a little past the halfway mark in the windows. I did not see a solid surface, I am pretty sure it had a slight tail on it. I jumped up and ran to the window just as it got to the edge of the window, but it disappeared. I listened for cars, but heard none - the headlights of cars are orange anyway. My next thought was that it was a meteor, but with the size and low flight of it, it definitely would have crashed. I waited a few seconds and there was no crash...there still, almost 20 minutes later, has been no crash. Therefore I don't know what I've seen and have decided to report it here. It was higher than two stories high, but lower than the clouds. There was no sound and no interference experienced.
