{dr}Hovering cigar shaped object emitting green laser beam over house

Returning from a scheduled Ghost Hunt at Rolling Hills Asylum, I live in house behind asylum. I looked over at neighbors house which is about 250 feet away, west of the building. I saw a green laser beam shining over the house. I walked around the corner headed west on Linden road. about 150 feet west of the intersection when i had a clear line of sight of the field i then saw the cigar shaped craft hovering over the field emitting the green laser beam in an easterly direction. the cigar shaped craft was about 200-250 feet in length with orange glowing lights on either side. it had hovered for only 10-15 seconds then it disappeared in thin air, no sounds.
