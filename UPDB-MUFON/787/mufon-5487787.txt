We were just coming home and noticed two large very bright orbs one large and in front and one smaller behind. I could observe the stars and recognised the plough but stars did not even show up on my video. These objects were much closer and large.

Just returning from a nightclub and when getting out of the taxi I noticed a very bright and oddly love object in the sky. Then I noticed there was another light just behind it. I live in a high elevation and the view beyond is into a valley so there is literally nothing physical that could have been glowing whilst shrouded in blackness. I made a point of trying to compare it to the stars that I could see which were very dull considering it was a cloudless evening. We observed the lights for about ten mins and the large light did seem to have a pulse or movement or something. We speculated that it could have been one very large object and the two lights were related in a physical sense .. Front and back?!! I took what photos and vids I could with my iPhone but not great. Whatever it was it was an unusual sight. The fourth attached file is me trying to focus on the stars but my phone did not pick them up
