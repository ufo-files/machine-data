Me and my friend saw a flashing light in the sky.

When my school bus was about to leave the school my friend Rohan told me to look out side in the sky. I saw an object shinning in the sky just like a star and then it just disappeared. Just after two to three seconds I saw it again slightly away from the place where I saw it before and again I saw it third time far away from that place. I am sure it wasn't a plane. I think it was an U.F.O. If you observe a flying aero plane in the sky it moves at a low speed but that object randomly changed its position with a flash. A metal can only reflect light.
