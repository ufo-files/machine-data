{cak} noticed black disc ascending, jet in area reversed direction, it quietly appeared to come closer.

A friend & I were riding our bicycles at the local post office when we noticed a large black disc shaped object going directly up into the air. A Jet coming from the south reversed its direction, leaving a trail as it made a complete 180 degree turn. The object (unidentified flying object) appeared to be quietly hovering...then pulsing, appearing to get larger as if it was coming closer to my friend & myself. This was during a time when a lot of abduction reports were being made by people & we were so afraid that we left the area quickly. It was 8:30 p.m. & Cimarron Strip was on the television...as I waited for 'them' to abduct me, after getting home. I never looked back outside, out of fear & nobody else admitted seeing this happen. We never found out what it was, as everyone we told just laughed at us. I'm reporting this siting, in the case someone else did see this event & filed a report.
