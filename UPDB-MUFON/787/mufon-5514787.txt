star like object that suddenly disappears

I was smoking on my balcony. I saw a plane and something that looks like a bright star near it. At first I had a sense that it was moving but after watching it for more than a minute, it was standing still in the sky. It was strange because no planet or bright star was expected to be there. I went to my room to bring my telescope (30 seconds), when I was back it was gone.
