Flying east then turned south. Emitted a humming buzz sound that was loud.

I was getting gas at 9:45pm and as I was finishing up I heard a loud humming buzz sound, I wasn't sure what it was at first, I looked up as I got into car and seen a very large shaped object with 4 whitish yellow lights flying east. I was shocked to see it. The object would have been at least a half mile long. I got my phone out as I pulled up to the stop light and had taken a picture before it was too far to take. My picture came out a bit unfocused but I got it. I tried to follow it as it made a turn to the south and you could tell it was an extremely large object making it's turn, I couldn't keep up and it disappeared from sight as I couldn't keep up in a vehicle and had to obey traffic laws, but even then it was impossible to track. I knew I had just witnessed a UFO and went home and told my girlfriend and called my friend and told them what I had just seen. Well I was laughed at me until the next morning my girlfriend found a news report about the exact sighting by other witnesses, she want laughing then!
