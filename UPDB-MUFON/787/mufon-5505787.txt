{rjl} Very large bright light came overhead just above trees within 150 ft. of me.

Talking on the phone outside in the twilight of evening.  Very bright white light object larger than a bus and oval shaped flew over head and just above large 70 year old hardwood trees that are probably 60 feet tall.  There was no sound. Its speed was perhaps 30 miles per hour. It gradually climbed and increased in speed dramatically, then suddenly went straight up and disappeared.  The sighting lasted perhaps a minute and a half or less.  All I could say to the person on the phone was stutter "what, what, what????" for about a minute afterwards.  The size and closeness of this light should have made a lot of noise and should have caused some movement of the air.  It was absolutely silent.  The person on the phone heard no noise except me and could only giggle in awe when I started telling her what I had just witnessed.  This light was absolutely pure white and exceedingly bright.
