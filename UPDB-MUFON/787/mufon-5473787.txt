ON TOP OF THAT HILL THERE1S NOTHING, CLOSE TO THERE THERE IS A SITE THAT WORKS LIKE A QUARRY WHERE THEY EXTRACT CONSTRUCTION MATERIAL. ALSO THERE IS A LITTLE VILLAGE AT HILLS1S SKIRTS.
