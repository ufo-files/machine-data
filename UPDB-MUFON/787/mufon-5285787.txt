Black triangle UFO

So in Feb of this year, my husband and I were driving home. It was around 10pm on a Thursday night. At the corner of Bundchu and Susquehanna Ridge, we saw a black triangle shaped flying object. It was about the size of a small airplane. On the underside if the craft,, it had 2 white lights in the back corners, and a red light in the center. It was really low, like make the length of a football field off of the ground. No sound at all. As we approached and were under it, we turned off on a side street to take a picture. It pivoted 90 degrees and flew off at a moderate speed...still no sound.
