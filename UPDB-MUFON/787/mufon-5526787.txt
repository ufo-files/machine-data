Large Fireball breaks up dead overhead from East.

I was outside smoking a cig after walking the dog around the block and something caught my eye as a flash over the roof and looked up and saw a large fireball or large meteor the largest I've ever seen moving very slowly very very bright white a hundred times brighter than Sirius and break up into many fragments and disappear dead overhead. Wondering if anyone saw a better view.
