{dr}Triangle shaped lights in sky moved across sky rapidly then disappeared

I was driving north in Reading on Hampton Blvd around midnight, and saw in the sky in front above me the three circle lights in a perfect triangle, it was a very bright white, which caught my attention. I had no idea what it was I ruled out plane or helicopter instantly. It was small in the sky but bigger than any man made thing Ive seen. It seemed to be hovering in a way, and moving rapidly towards the northwest, descending. I lost sight of it through the tree line a few times, but still saw it. When I got past the tree line it was gone. I was in awe and kind of freaked out when i was viewing it. Afterwards, I got chills and had the feeling of being watched.
