Two subjects projected into mind of observer. Memory erased but recovered after meditation

Witnessed a spherical orb of large size north relative to the observer. Then a few minutes of seeing from the perspective of a camera in the ship of the two subjects. An attempt to communicate was made by both the observer and the subjects through movement of the limbs in a communicative way. After this violation of the observer's mind, the orb was spotted South of the observer until it moved out of vision.
