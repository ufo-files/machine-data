WITNESS SENT THIS REPORT IN BY MAIL AND I FILLED IT OUT FOR HER AND SUBMITTED IT. THE STATEMENT BELOW IS THE LETTER SHE ENCLOSED WITH THE REPORT. L.FLECHTNER 18809 AT MUFON HEADQUARTERS 5/3/2018

I WOULD LIKE TO SUBMIT A SIGHTING I HAD MANY YEARS AGO. I FIND IT TO BE NO LESS REMARKABLE NOW THAN IT WAS 59 YEARS AGO. DON'T LAUGH I DON'T KNOW WHY I HAVEN'T REPORTED IT MUCH, MUCH SOONER, OTHER THAN I FELT, SINCE PRESIDENT TRUMP HAS EVIDENTLY AUTHORIZED THE AIR FORCE TO RELEASE SOME INFORMATION, IT WOULD BE OKAY FOR ME TO REPORT IT. I HAVE NO IDEA WHY THAT SEEMED TO MATTER.
THIS HAPPENED WHEN OUR FIRST AMERICAN SATELLITE WENT INTO ORBIT AND WAS THE FIRST TIME THE ORBIT CARRIED IT OVER CHICAGO. MY FAMILY AND I WENT OUTSIDE TO WATCH FOR IT. I'M FAIRLY SURE THIS WAS IN OCTOBER, 1958. IT WAS IN THE EVENING, BUT THE WESTERN SKY STILL HAD THE RED AFTERGLOW OF SUNSET. MY FAMILY WENT INSIDE TO GET WARM, WE HAD ABOUT AN HOUR TO WAIT, BUT I STAYED OUTSIDE FOR A FEW MINUTES.. I TURNED AROUND TO GO IN AND I GASPED BECAUSE I THOUGHT A PLANE WAS ABOUT TO CRASH. IT WAS A WHITE CIRCULAR OBJECT WITH A BRIGHT RED GLOW ON THE LEADING EDGE. RATHER THAN CRASH, ITS COURSE CHANGED TO COME MY WAY. I THINK NOW IT WAS UP ABOUT 5,000 FEET OR SO QUITE HIGH BUT NOT AT AIRPLANE LEVELS. IT CAME DIRECTLY OVER OUR YARD THEN TOOK A TIGHT TURN BACK THE WAY IT CAME. BEING A VERY NAIVE 15 YEAR OLD , IT NEVER OCCURRED TO ME THAT THIS WAS STRANGE. I REMEMBER LAUGHING, ACTUALLY GIGGLING, AT WHAT TYPE OF FLIGHT PLAN WAS FILED THAT TOLD THE VEHICLE TO CHANGE COURSE EXACTLY AT EARL PESTOW'S WESTERN PROPERTY LINE, BECAUSE THAT IS PRECISELY WHAT IT DID.
AT THIS TIME I RAN IN TO GET MY FAMILY TO SEE IT TOO, BUT THEY REFUSED. I RAN BACK OUR TO SEE IT, BUT IT WAS NOT AT THE POSITION I THOUGHT IT SHOULD BE BUT WAS QUITE FAR TO THE RIGHT FROM MY ORIGINAL SIGHTING TO THE TIME I RELOCATED IT, ITS COURSE WOULD RESEMBLE A SORT OF NARROW SICKLE SHAPE.
MORE REMARKABLE, AS I WATCHED, IT DISAPPEARED THEN REAPPEARED A LITTLE FURTHER AWAY, THEN DISAPPEARED AGAIN, AND AGAIN REAPPEARED EVEN A LITTLE FURTHER AWAY. IT REAPPEARED ONCE MORE HEN DISAPPEARED. THAT WAS THE LAST I SAW OF IT.
ITS SHAPE WAS LIKE A VERY NARROW TUNA CAN IN HEIGHT VS LENGTH. IT WAS MAYBE THE SIZE OF A HALF DOLLAR HELD AT ARM'S LENGTH. THE BOTTOM WAS A SOLID BRIGHT WHITE LIGHT BUT DID NOT ILLUMINATE ANYTHING. THE LEADING EDGE WAS A BRIGHT RED FROM REFLECTED SUNLIGHT, ALTHOUGH IT DID APPEAR SOMEWHAT TO BE SPARKLING, LIKE 4TH OF JULY SPARKLERS, BUT RED, AT ITS NEAREST APPROACH I NOTICED THAT BESIDE THE RED GLOW, THE SIDE OF THE VEHICLE WAS A SHINNY SILVER/CHROME TYPE OF METAL. 
AT NO TIME DURING THIS EPISODE DID I FEEL ANY FEAR OF MISGIVING. I WAS SIMPLY IN WONDERMENT IF THAT IS A WORD AS TO WHAT THIS WAS. EVEN THEN FLYING SAUCER CAME TO MIND BUT, FOR SOME REASON, I DIDN'T THINK AN DEEPER INTO THAT. FOR ALL THESE YEARS, I'VE THOUGHT ABOUT THIS SIGHTING, BUT HAVE NEVER REPORTED IT, AND I HAVE NO IDEA WHY I DIDN'T.
IT TOOK MANY YEARS FOR IT TO REGISTER IN MY MIND THAT THE DARNED THING DEVIATED FROM ITS COURSE, CAME RIGHT OVER MY HEAD, THEN TURNED AWAY AGAIN  AND I HAVE NO IDEA WHY.
THANK YOU FOR YOUR TIME!
