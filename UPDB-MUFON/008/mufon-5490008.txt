{rjl} I observed it going from S. to N. very fast moving semi transparent, white light on all three corners. Approx 6 seconds viewed.

Approx. 12:40 am on the night of August 19th 2015 I went out in my backyard to sky watch as I often do after work. I was in my lounge chair always looking for anything moving. Airplanes, meteorites, satellites or whatever. I first noticed the "UFO" at approx. 12:50 am coming from the south at a high rate of speed heading north. It did not deviate in direction or speed. It took me approx. one second to calculate what I was witnessing. First thing that I noticed was that it was traveling at a very fast speed. If I had to estimate it I would say around 2000 mph. plus. The second thing that I noticed was that it was triangular in shape. The third thing that I noticed was that it seemed to have one white light at each corner. I also noticed that it seemed to be somewhat transparent. It was at a pretty high altitude, not sure how high as I had nothing to compare it to. I really can't say how big it was due to not knowing what altitude that it was. I have worked at our local airport in the maintenance dept. for the last 7 plus years. I know what an airplane flying through the sky looks like. This was not an airplane! As I watched it cross the sky I remember thinking... This is something that I can't explain. This is a triangle UFO. Shortly after it disappeared out of sight I remember thinking wow, that is the triangular shaped UFO that a lot of people see. I then thought, who or what was piloting this craft?
