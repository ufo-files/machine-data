Looked like a fluorescent tube flying from West to East, flying at an angle, one portion was higher than the other, not level. White light, steady,

I was walking my dog at 5Z:38 a.m. 24 March 2021 and was on top of a small hill of my property when I noticed a white light approaching from the West. Not just a light but it was shaped like a fluorescent tube, long and slender. It was not level, as I was looking at it the west end was higher than the east end, making it look like it was at an angle. I was on the top of the hill and the light was moving from West to East (left to right as I faced it). The light seemed to be brighter at two points and then fade as it got to what I assume was the end of the craft. I could not hear any sound, and it was probably only about 1/4 to 1/2 mile to the North of me. I could hear traffic on the Interstate which was about 4 miles away. Was a clear morning, about 23 degrees out, a slight breeze. 

I see helicopters and C130's fly around all the time in the daylight and at night but this looked nothing like those. My dog looked that direction but did not seem overly concerned or anxious. Did not take a picture...... just watched in awe.
