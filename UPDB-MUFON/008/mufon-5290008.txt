Radical contrails reappearing far away from each other

Observed contrails going in circles, then disappearing, then reappearing in straight lines far away from last; made sharp angle turns as well as broken trails suggesting ram-jet propulsion. These appeared to be 2 objects, or one object that moved at impossible speed. This was in military air space and over the Pacific off central CA coast during daylight.
