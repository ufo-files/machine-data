{cak} Walk outside & spotted a Red/Orange light moving south to north direction

It was 7:28pm on January 11th 2018 when my daughter, Kelly & I went outside to smoke a cigarette. As soon as I open the door & stepped out, directly in front of me about 3-500 ft in the south sky was this reddish/orange light was moving in our direction. I pointed this out to Kelly & she video the event, which we both watch this noiseless, pulsing light object move directly over us and continuous to the north & out of sight. We both where amazed & made sure we caught the event on video. As soon as the video was done, we both watch a commercial jet moving in a easterly direction & I commented about how you can clearly heard the jet making noise & which was much higher in the sky.
