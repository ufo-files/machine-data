Flashing lights in the sky.

My boyfriend and I observed two flashing lights in the sky last night. We viewed them for two hours.
My boyfriend was the first one to notice them from the church parking lot next to our house.
He texted me at 11:04pm to say "there are blinking lights in the sky. No engine noise."
So I went outside to look and saw them for myself.
I was nervous so asked him to come home. 
After watching for a few minutes I recorded the first of many videos with updates.
I texted our neighbor and told him about the lights and hot him outside. The 3 of us viewed them together all in agreement that we couldn't explain the lights away. Our neighbor watched for about 10 minutes then went back inside.
We stayed out on our deck for two hours watching and recording short videos.
We watched as the clouds rolled in to cover the stars behind but these lights were not covered. This told us they were below the cloud level. 
We do not know the degree, so I left it as "0".
But, they hovered above the tree line and kept an exact distance from one another and remained in one spot for the duration.
There was a couple airplanes seen in the area which had different lights flashing from these.
There was no sound, just these lights.
We had hoped we would witness them flying away or blinking out out but we didn't.
It was interesting to say the very least.
We aren't ready to say they were aliens but we aren't convinced that they were drones, kites or a weather balloon either.
We simply just do not know what the explanation is for this event.
