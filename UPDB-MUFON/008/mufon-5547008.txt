Sphere emitting bright star like light

On 11 September 2009 at 7:55 a friend and i took a walk with my dog. We walked one block north of my condo to an open field. I started to look up into the west sky and saw a sphere like object emitting light. the object moved from west to east over our location The object was very bright from all sides. The object was about 500 to 1000 feet in the air.The object appeared to be emitting a glowing smoke or cloudy mist. It looked to be about 10 ft. in diameter. We stood there in amazement and watched the object pass over. It took about 3 to 5 min. to move across the sky from west to east and then it disappeared. The object made no sound at all. The feelings we both had during the sighting was amazed and emotional.
