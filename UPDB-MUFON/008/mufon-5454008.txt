ON APRIL 9TH,2020 JUST AFTER 9PM CST I WAS OUTSIDE MY BUILDING OF RESIDENCE LOOKING AT THE SKY . I AM A AMATAUR STAR GAZER. LOOKING AT VENUS TO THE WEST ABOUT 30 DEGREES OF HORIZON. DARK SKY NO CLOUDS . I LOOKED AT VENUS THEN TURNED 15 DGREES TO THE NW AND OBSERVED A LIGHT IN THE SKY AS BRIGHT AS THE VISIBLE STARS BUT NOT AS BRIGHT AS VENUS WHEN I NOTICED A WHITE LITE TRAVELING FROM NW TO EAST SE. ALTITUDE WAS DIFFICULT TO DETERMINE NOT KNOWING SIZE OF CRAFT. THERE WAS NO NOISE. AS THE LITE WENT OVER HEAD I NOTICED ANOTHER LITE COMING FROM THE SAME DIRECTION AND PROCEDING IN THE SAME GENERAL FLIGHT PATH AS THE FIRST ONE. ONE LITE THEN WHILE THE OBJECT STILL VISIBLE HEADING EAST ANOTHER CRAFT FOLLOWED THE FIRST AND THEN ANOTHER FOLLOWED THAT ONE SO I COULD SEE THEM ALL MOVING ACROSS THE THE SKY IN FORMATION. SINGLE FILE NO MORE THAN THREE AT A TIME COULD I SEE THEY WERE SPACED ABOUT EACH CRAFT TAKING ONE TO TWO MINUTES TO CROSS THE SKY. THIS CONTINUED AS I COUNTED THEM GOING OVER HEAD 1-2-3-4-5-6-7-8-9 MY FINAL COUNT WAS 28 SIMILIAR OBJECTS ALL SINGLE LITES AND FOLLOWING EACH OTHER THEY MADE A SLIGHT CHANGE IN THEIR DIRECTION ABOUT HALF WAY THROUGH FROM A NW TO EAST THEN CHANGING ABOVE ME TO A MORE SOUTHERLY COURSE. I AM A RETIRED MANAGER. I GO OUT EVERY EVENING WEATHER PRERMITTING TO VIEW THE HEAVENS. I ESTIMATE A MINIMUM OF 10 THOUSAND FEET MAYBE A LITTLE HIGHER. THEY MATCHED THE INTENSITY OF THE STARS VISIBLE ALLMOST EXACTLY . I AM 100 PERCENT COFIDENT OF WHAT I OBSERVED I DO NOT DRINK!!!
