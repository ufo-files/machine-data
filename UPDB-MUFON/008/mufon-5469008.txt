{cak} Bright light making strange movements in the sky

@ 20:45 Walked outside and noticed what I thought were two bright stars next to each other in the sky, right away the one blinked out. I was a little shocked. It then blinked back on just below where it had been. 

Then it fired up like an Iridium flare, but it wasn1t moving, then blinked out again and moved to a lower position, blinked on again moved, then out, then on, moved again and back in the original position. 

I opted to run and get a camera when I got back out I only saw one faint light in the spot and a pale blue light dropping from it which I was unable to photograph in time. Then nothing there at all. 

Stayed a little while but they didn1t come back. I checked the same spot the next night at the same time and nothing there.
