{rjl} Ball of fire approximately the size of a helicopter, morphed between orange, red, yellow.

I was outside smoking a cigarette with a friend, I went inside, then my friend hollered at me to come outside. This was the second time he had seen this happen in a week. The fiery looking orb was traveling slowly at first about 100 feet above the buildings heading southeast. I ran inside to get my phone and take a video, once I was recording it seemed to be moving faster. I was totally dumbstruck as I have never seen anything like this before, but I wasn't scared, just startled. I maintained sight of it until it traveled beyond the trees.
