I was getting dinner and started driving off when I saw 6 orbs with bright white lights hovering off in the distance. There was a group of two orbs and a group of four. The light faded in and out slowly and were staggered. I observed them slowly change position from a line to an arc all while they were slowly drifting lower in the sky. I took a video.
