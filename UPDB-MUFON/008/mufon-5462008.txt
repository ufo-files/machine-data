{jpg} blimp-like object motionless over treeline

Current photo on Mufon website "Blimp" UFO Photographed Low Over Missouri is the only representation of what my wife and I had seen approximately ten years prior while leaving our rural subdivision on a gray overcast March day on our way to our business (Chevrolet dealership). Neither of us had cellphones or cameras at that time (only Blackberrys). Descending Elsah Hills Drive, the final stretch is perpendicular to Beltrees Road. Opposite that intersection is a treed embankment, over which was a gray, motionless, odd-shaped blimp-like object. We speculated as to what was the nature of the object for less than 10 minutes before driving away. I had hoped to be able to view the object from a few hundred yards to the east, but it was not visible from that location.
