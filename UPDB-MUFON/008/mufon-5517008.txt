{nd} Brilliant White Light in Dawn Sky, then again at Dusk

I was up at dawn on 7/22/2012, the only stars that were still slightly visible were the 2-3 that are super-bright at night. I then noticed in the NW sky a brilliantly white, BRIGHT, glowing, large ball of light in a stationary position. I watched it for 2-3 minutes, before looking away and then losing sight of it. That evening, at dusk, I was sitting outside with my boyfriend and saw a very similar brilliantly white ball of light streak overhead. It came from the NW and was heading SW, just above the tree-line. It then dissolved and disappeared in front of my eyes. I have witnessed a flurry of activity since Memorial Day weekend. It had been quiet for the past 2 weeks(approx.)until yesterday. I wish I had high-quality camera equipment to set up. Thanks.
