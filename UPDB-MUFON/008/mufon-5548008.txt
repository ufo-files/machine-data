I was outside of my house

I was letting my dog out to use the bathroom, and I saw something hovering over our house. I love to look up at the stars because of their beauty. Then I saw something hovering over my house. I was going to get a camer, but I was scared that when I come back it would be gone. I thought it was a ufo when I first saw it because, I love reading about ufo's and this was amazing and such a wonderful moment for me. Anyways, It stayed still for about one minute and then it started moving at a fast speed. It went side to side, up and down, I didn't know what it was doing. I was so excited and scared at the same time, when I was the ufo. It was amazimg and I enjoyed it very much. After about eight minutes, it zoomed out of sight.
