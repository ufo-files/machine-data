{kt}Witnessed 9 hovering orbs above a high-traffic road while driving south. 2 orbs sped away east bound. All lights eventually flickered out.

I was driving south on Randolf Rd in Charlotte, NC at 35 mph near a light tower with red lights. My passenger alerted me to nine orbs hovering over the road about 60 feet high by saying "What are those?'. I slowed down noticed the tower and looked at the floating obs. Each orb appeared to be a non-rhythmic flickering light in the center of a transparent sphere. The orbs were moving in a floating manner independently from each other. It was dark, but the lights did not appear to be part of the same entity and were far from the tower. From my vantage point, the left most two obs headed east at about 30 mph and eventually went dark. The other lights were still illuminated but the flickering seemed more desperate. I drove a quarter of mile and decided to turn back. I found a parking lot, turned in and drove to the edge closest to the three remaining orbs. I turned off the car and headlights and pulled out my camera phone. The obs were still in place and going dark one-by-one. I took one picture of the last orb before it went dark. I waited a bit longer but there was nothing to see. My passenger's account is similar, but she did not witness the two orbs heading east. Additionally, she experienced fear.
