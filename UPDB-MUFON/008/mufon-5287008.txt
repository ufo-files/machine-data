The UFO was triangular and diamond shaped

The UFO was triangular and diamond shaped at the same time sort of like a stealth fighter but more diamond shaped. It was metallic in color but had flashing rainbow colored lights that were built flush into the wings. It descended quickly and hovered over a small farm house close to the road. It made a slight humming sound than got a little louder as it rapidly increased speed. It made a 45 degree angle at about three treetop levels and zapped out of sight.
