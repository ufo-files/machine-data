Cigar shape object came out of nowhere filled with people easily seen through windows, lots of them.

I was sitting outside with my step daughter, it was about 9h00-10h00 at night, we were facing south, the ottawa river wich was right accross the street from where i lived. We saw comming out of nowhere, just above my neighborg's house accross the street a dark object shape like a cigar appear right in front of us, it had windows, over a hundred of them, and in each window it had someone sitting down by the window as if it were a bus, it moved like a snake, twisting towards the river and disappearing like by magic. The whole thing lasted maybe 10 seconds but there was no mistaking what we saw. There was no noise but a flash of light when it appeared. We were both in shock and amazed, we both saw the same thing and to this day i can't make sense of what i saw!
