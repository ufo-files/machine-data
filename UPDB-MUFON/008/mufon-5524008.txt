UFO SPOTTED IN SKY 1961

I WAS IN KINDERGARTEN IN 1961 I WAS PLAYING ON THE JUNGLE JIM WHEN THE BELL RANG TO GO BACK TO CLASS..I WAS RUNNING THROUGH THE TAN BARK WHEN IN A INSTANT I FELT SOMEONE WAS LOOKING DOWN AT ME...I STOPPED IN MY TRACKS AND LOOKED STRAIT UP AND SAW A ROUND SILVERY WHITISH OBJECT BLENDED INTO THE BLUE OF THE SKY..ABOUT THE SIZE OF A HALF MOON..IT WAS ROUND AND HAD A DARKER ROUND FEATURE IN THE CENTER..I WAS STANDING THERE HYPMOTIZED ON THIS OBJECT ..AND THE TEACHER HAD TO SCREAM AT ME TO COME TO CLASS..LATER THAT AFTERNOON WHEN I WALKED HOME BEFORE I WENT INSIDE THE HOUSE I LOOKD UP TO SEE IF IT WAS STILL THERE...IT WAS IN THE SAME POSITION ..IT HADN'T MOVED A INCH..IT WASN'T THE MOON.. THE MOON IS NEVER AT 12 OCLOCK HIGH IN SANTA CLARA ...AND IT WASN'T A BLIMP FROM MOFFETT FIELD..SEEN MANY OF THOSE WHEN I WAS YOUNGER IN THE 1950'S
