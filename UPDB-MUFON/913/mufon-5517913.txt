{nd} MP4 Video from VOB file sent to me by observer and documenter. Have original VOB.

I saw this video on Youtube in 2007 posted at the height of the "floating humanoid" sightings. I contacted the original poster and asked if he would send the camera file, which he did. I have posted various enhanced versions of the file on Youtube at different times, but never the whole thing, never with audio. This is the entire VOB file converted to MP4 using MPEG Streamclip, which reduced a bit of background noise, but still retains the original shape of the object quite well. The relevant sections are:

1:06-1:17
1:33-2:27
2:39-3:53

The object is totally silent, stationary, and judging by its apparent height is not a balloon. It appears to spin, wobble, and according to the person who filmed it moved from place to place. The early portion of the video has some glitches, and was filmed by the mother of the videographer. The later footage is of the videographers father filming Venus on the horizon. I do not speak spanish so I am not sure what is being said throughout. The videographer reported that the family was out changing a tire on their car when the event occurred, and was witnessed by the mother, father, and videographer.
