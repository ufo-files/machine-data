Brilliant bright white light observed in the sky that moved then disappeared into thin air.

While traveling northbound on I-5 I was about 2 miles from exit 119 near JBLM when I observed a distant but a very brilliant bright circular white light about 60 degrees in the sky. The daylight was still bright and visibility was 100 percent at the time of the sighting. The light looked as if it was distant, yet had an incredible clarity and brightness. It was a steady light leading me to think it may be Venus I was observing. I then verified that it wasn't a reflection on my windshield. The sky was clear on the horizon with no clouds visible at the time. I then observed the light quickly drop straight down to about an elevation of 50 degrees and pause. It was at this time I knew what I was seeing was not a planet or any conventional aircraft. I excitedly remarked to my two young children in the backseat that "Holy crap I see a UFO!". They attempted to look but could not see it from the rear cab of the truck. They shouted to me to "Take a picture QUICK!", however due to driving I was unable to do this at the time. The light then started traveling slowly east for about three to five seconds before suddenly vanishing into thin air. No light or craft was seen at this time. It was as if it just blinked out of existence. The entire length of the sighting was about 20 seconds in length.
