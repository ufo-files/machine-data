{cak} Observed disc-shaped object hovering over Canobie Lake, 5 miles NW. It seemed the size of 2 football fields. Movement observed in the windows.

My parents and I were watching television late one summer evening when I was about 9 years old. My father may have been the first to take note of the bright lights outside our back window, above the tree line. He noted it seemed to be hovering over in the vicinity of Canobie Lake Park. 

Soon after, he went about his business paying it no further attention. I, however, was repeatedly drawn back to the window to look at it as my father repeatedly yelled for me to get/stay away from the window. I'm not sure whether his reaction was out of fear of the unknown or something else. 

It was a massive disc-shape perhaps the length of two football fields across. It was metallic in appearance with bright exterior lighting. The outer edge did not come to a point as was so commonly portrayed in science fiction of the 1950s; it had a smooth outer edge that came down to a dome of lights below the disc. Windows partially encompassed the dome's circumference allowing for shadows and movement to be observed within the disc.

I have to wonder why it didn't cause a mass hysteria given it was so large.
