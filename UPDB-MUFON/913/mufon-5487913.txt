{rjl} was flying from Las Vegas to Baltimore

It was around 9:AM on August 6th as I was looking out the window I could see way off in the distance through the clouds red and white blinking lights of another plane flying in the distance. I watched it for several minutes as it flew towards my plane and eventually flew under us. As I was watching the other plane I saw a UFO. It was a dome shaped object yellow gold in color. Compared to the size of the lights on the plane it had to be 3 quarters the size of the plane. Whatever it was it was fling perpendicular to my plane and moving at an astonishing speed. It had to have been moving more than a thousand miles and hour because as I watched it move in less than 3 seconds it moved the distance of 3 windows of my plane. The sky was clear and sunny that day.
