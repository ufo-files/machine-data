{tjd} Spheres hovering

Saw what looked like a white star very bright and not much above treetops over Hwy 80 while I was traveling west into Portal. Then about 30 minutes later as my son and I were leaving grandmothers house we saw two very bright lights over portal appear and then disappear, in close proximity to each other. Then as we approached Hopulikit we saw two bright yellowish white spheres appear, then flicker and disappear. Then after we turned onto Hwy 25 towards Millen there were 4 of them at about 45 degrees up slightly to the right above the highway maybe about 500 to 1000 feet up in the air. They lit up and went back dark intermittently and moved around slightly, and then as the one on the far right began to shoot off towards the left they all went dark simultaneously.
