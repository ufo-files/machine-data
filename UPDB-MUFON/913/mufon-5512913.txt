I was with my church youth group in North Myrtle beach. A girl and i were alone on the beach looking toward the city of myrtle beach a layer of clouds was under a large circular object that seemed black or darker on the top, the light emitted by the bottom was extremely bright within a few seconds the ship ascended vertically a an extreme velocity many 1000s of feet per second. The girl I was with was frightened and 2 years younger we did run back to a rental residence she in no way should be affected by this experience please. She should in no way have been reminded because her mother wanted her to forget or make her feel as if this experience never happened. I also had forgot what i had seen and would not speak of as many things that have been done to me in my life by those around me.
