Electrical arc between jet and sperical object

I was driving West on 23rd Street in Independence just past Noland Road when I noticed a commercial jet flying at normal altitude from East to West and North of my location. There was a large sphere that was glinting in the sun just like the plane was, it was above the plane and that is what I was watching - when all of a sudden I saw what looked like an electrical arc shoot from the spehere to the plane and stay there for a few seconds, then dissapear. It is the strangest thing I've ever seen. The plane kept going as if it was unaffected by the event, then the sphere moved quickly up and away and out of my sight.
