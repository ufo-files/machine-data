{jpg} We were my wife and I in the park and we looked at the sky and there were the objects

My wife and I went for a bike ride to the park in the golden east reginal park and stopped for a while on the grass. After a while we turned to the sky and there were the objects. I took my camera and started recording. At the beginning you see how one object passes under the other that appear to be two spheres together red color but in a while it changes color and the two appear white. We did not realize when I changed color and changed to just being a white sphere side by side.
