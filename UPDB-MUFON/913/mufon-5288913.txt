Looking South out my home window while sitting talking on the phone, I immediately thought I was witnessing two pieces of a firework that had been shot into the sky but not exploded and that were on a downward trajectory. One was a green glowing, flickering round object and there was a smaller, possibly 1/3 the size, similar glowing green object slightly below it as they descended from the sky. I did not hear a bang, however, nor did I see any evidence of fireworks before or after this sighting. It is a very windy, gusty evening as a front is moving through, West to East, while the objects were moving earthward from East to West. The green lights appeared suddenly, fell for about three seconds, and then disappeared again well above the ground (about 70-80?). I'm comparing my two-story neighbor's house with the approximate height of the objects.
