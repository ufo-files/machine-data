{jpg} UFO flying formation unintentionally caught on cell phone picture of sunset

My wife and I were stopping in to our local grocery store called Shop Rite in Waretown, in New Jersey on September 18th 2018 around 7:25pm in the evening. We parked our vehicle and began to walk towards the store. My wife commented on how beautiful the sky looked and she wanted to take a picture on her cellphone to catch it. I looked over and seen the sky and decided to do the same and so I took my phone out as well. We snapped away on our phones. She took a picture and I took one too and we just put our phones back in our pockets and walked into the store without checking the pictures. It was only later on back at home when we looked at them that we noticed the UFO1s. 4 of them flying from right to left across the sky above and behind the apartments. My wife1s picture shows them a little faded like they are coming out of the clouds but mine is much clearer and shows that they have moved across the sky from where her her first picture caught them. We didn't see or notice them when taking the pictures but they showed up in the pictures. Almost like the camera was able to see them but we couldn't with the naked eye. It also might be worth mentioning that there is a recently decommissioned nuclear power plant called Oyster Creek which is very close to this sighting, its less than a mile away to the north.
