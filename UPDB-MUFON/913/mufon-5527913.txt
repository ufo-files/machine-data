The night sky was clear. No UFOs in sight. I asked for a sign. A flyby soon appeared.

I had decided to take the trash out to the bin in the backyard. Looked at the clear night sky a few times while I was out, to see if the starlike objects I see occasionally were present. There was no apparent activity I could see. I than decided to acknowledge from my personal experience that the starlike objects are known to appear no matter where I am, so I asked for a sign and went inside to warm up. Some minutes later I went outside on the back deck and looked up to see one starlike object flying over from East to West, approximately above 5,000 ft, no nav/strobe lights, just white/starlike. I held up my hand and waved. A separate quick starlike flash of light occurred some distance behind and aside from the westbound object. This sighting did not cause any distress, I always find it fascinating and exciting to see these flying objects.
