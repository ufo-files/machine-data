{rjl} Extremely bright white light hovering in sky then moving rapidly to the SE and disappearing in the night sky

I was traveling in a south to north direction when I looked to the east and saw this very bright pure white light hovering in the night sky. It then started to drift from my left to my right across the night sky and then moved very rapidly upward in a SE direction. It was not the moon, venus or any type of aircraft. It was a very clear cool fog less, rain less night. In addition there were no street lights on this road.
