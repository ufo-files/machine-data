I see two balls at the ends and a cylinder in the middle (halteres)

If the text was not good, sorry, I used Google Translator. I speak Portuguese.

I was about 10 years old, I do not know for sure. But I remember the scene to this day. I was playing with my uncle's binoculars, in his apartment, in the living room window. I was looking at the front building, the street ... and I looked at a cloud (up ahead) and saw inside (or behind) an object that seemed to be hidden. Almost stopped. There it was the landing strip of the Congonhas airport. There was always a plane there. Then he came out of that cloud and went to the other side. I do not know how close I was to the route. Do not remember. But it was close. I did not see any more. It was quick because the cloud was almost touching the other. The shape was an alteres (two balls at the ends and a cylinder in the middle - Halteres). I was agitated, kind of nervous about it. I knew it was a UFO.

No evidences, no photos... nothing.
