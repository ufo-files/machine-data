Grand sons and I were leaving the skate rink and to head home and noticed a large bright lite several miles toward our direction home. As we traveled down [location removed] out of the city of Bastrop it seemed as though the light was matching our line of movement. I turned off [location removed] and went east for a few miles and the light matched our line of travel until we turned right off [location removed]. The light hovered above the power poles and street light at the intersection of [location removed]. I stopped the car at the intersection ( [location removed]) and waited to see what the light was going to do but after sitting there a few minutes i decided to head home and drive under the light but my grand-kids were scared so I waited a few more minutes before heading to the intersection. I drove under the light and turned at the intersection to get home. As I turned I looked out the window and looked up to see why the sky got so dark in spite of the bright light and saw what it was. It was a large triangular craft about 20 feet above the car. It had what appeared to be navigation lights down the sides. It made no noise nor was there any wind blowing. We drove two blocks to the house and pulled into the driveway, jumped out of the car and watched the thing climb in altitude and continue out of sight. I am a veteran of the USAF and was trained on jet aircraft but I have never seen any such Air craft.
