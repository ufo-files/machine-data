UFO

1987 onboard USS John A Moore (FFG-19) a UFO was sighted on the Starboard
side Midship. I was on the bridge and heard yelling from 5 crew members. We all
turned green, like a lazor was turned on, then I heard UFO UFO. I turned and went
down the ladder to radio. Was later interviewed by NIS.
