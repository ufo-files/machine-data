As a child I was awakened in my bedroom by a small grey entity moving in circles. As I started to move I was paralyzed, and could not move. After approximately 30 seconds, the paralysis subsided, and the event became physical. I grabbed the entity, and bashed it about the room. After approximately 10 seconds the entity disappeared.
