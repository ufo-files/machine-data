unusual lights in the north sky between Evansburg and Drayton Valley Alberta

I was letting my dogs out for a pee.When I opened the door of my home,I noticed there were 2 objects in the north sky.It looked like there could have been more but the other ones were too far away to be sure.The reason I say this is because there were other lights that were spaced quite uniformly apart in longitude and seemed to be spaced out perfectly from the 2 closest to me.They stretched out east and west at about the same distance apart and at the same elevation as the two closest to me. they seemed to form a perfect line horizontally for as far as I could see from east to west It scared the hell out of me!.I'm guessing the 2 closest to me were half way between here and Evansburg(15 to 20 kilometers) Alberta and spaced about the same distance apart.I am 47 years old and was born and raised here so I know that they were not a normal part of our night sky.They held their position for all the probably 5 minutes I was outside They never swayed and remained in the same position when I went inside.They seemed to spin slowly judging from the movement of their white and blue lights.There was also another white light that seemed to be of a projected nature.They seemed to be observing something but I'm not sure as they were a long distance away from my position.I have also witnessed large,polished aluminum looking, almost zeppelin shaped objects in the same general area in the past(I have never seen any lights of any kind during daylight sightings). They always seem to show up in daylight hours when there's a huge electrical storm in the area.The more intense the storm,the more likely the chance of seeing one.And they seem to be playing in the storm front with crazy maneuvers and such.I have watched them simply evaporate from their seemingly playing in the clouds. None of the objects I have witnessed have made any sound that I could hear.Of the daylight sightings,what really intrigues me, is that they seem to be playing in storm fronts that are very fast moving and very violent.,The kind that makes us run for safety. When severe storm warnings are broadcasted for the area. When the clouds turn green and purple and really scary looking.And yet they seem to be playing in it like little kids.It is also interesting to note that there are 2 coal powered generating stations and large strip mines in close proximity(within 33 to 40 km from the area where I have seen them in daylight.I have reported one of the daytime sightings to the RCMP but they seemed more concerned about my sanity than about what I saw.Thanks Guys!Never again!I know what I saw and I know they're out there.Let's hope it's God,or at least that he's giving them their directions!
