I was driving on SE Mill Plain when I saw the cross flying object in the sky

I heading East while driving on Southeast Mill Plain coming up to SE 164th ave when I saw the cross shaped flying object in the sky. It appeared sort of flat, then turned sideways which is when I noticed it looked like a cross or plus sign. It had blinking lights on the shape. I was traveling about 40 MPH and was able to keep up with the object. I pulled into the Best Buy parking lot to get a better view. It continued moving through the air. I got back into my car and started to follow it again while heading towards my home. It started to turn off to the left (North) when that direction for a few minutes, then started to head West. I arrived home a few minutes later and asked my wife to come outside, but by the time she made it outside, it was pretty far away and she couldnt see the lights. This was around 9:45 pm tonight on March 13, 2012.
