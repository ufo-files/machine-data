{cak} Star object flying at constant quick speed

I was in my backyard a few minutes after midnight observing the night sky with my binoculars. After ten minutes of viewing the sky, I observed an object moving at a constant quick speed. It looked just like a distant star and it seemed to be beyond Earth's atmosphere, in the range of a satellite, but it moved at least 20 times faster than a satellite. I viewed it from the west and it headed towards Mt. Hood direction. I viewed the object for approximately 30 seconds until it faded from my binocular view. It never changed colors or size.
