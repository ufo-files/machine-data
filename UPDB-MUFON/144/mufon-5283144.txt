Cigar shaped white UFO with outline flying consistently

I was in a park in East Aurora, Colorado, a suburb of Denver. I noticed the UFO flying roughly a mile away and high, slightly under cloud cover. 

I pulled out my phone to video it, and the shape and flightpath mirrored other quite recent UFO encounters I have had. It is cigar-shaped, bright white, with a darker outline and a chevron down the middle, apparently bisecting it into two compartments. 

I have been visited by alien presences and have had encounters and abductions since I was a very young child. It apparently flashed in the middle of filming, though that could have been a function of zooming in. 

This may in fact be the way airplanes show up on video at a great distance, with natural light and video technology playing upon them in unknown ways. However with the surrounding UFO activity and alien contact I have experienced, it seemed to me to be an intelligent alien entity flying above me and communicating. I would be happy to discuss further with anyone who has additional questions.
