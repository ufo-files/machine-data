I was watching tv and all of a sudden it sounded like a jet just landed in my living room. I have never heard such a loud sound. I went towards the sound and it was coming from the back of my house at the time. When I opened the door, to my astonishment I saw this ufo with bright green fire coming from its exhaust ports which were shaped like a greater than symbol and a less than symbol . I only saw the rear of the craft as it looked to be having engine trouble as it nearly hit the top of the building next to my house. It slowly rose up and floated over the midtown street at the tree top level and in an instant I saw a green blur,, it was gone. This had to be seen my multiple people. I can't remember the exact date but it was definitely between July and September 2013.
