Standing on sidewalk outside of house. I saw 5 lights move in unison east.

At around 10:55 (ish) pm on 5/7/21, I received a call from my wife while she was at work. I could hear she was a little unnerved by the tone in her voice. She told me to go outside and look in the direction of the hospital. She wanted to know if I could see these lights in the sky. I went outside, looked north and to my surprise, I saw 5 lights moving in unison across the night sky. My oldest daughter heard me go outside and followed me to see what I was doing. I started to tell her. Before I could show her where in the sky to look, she spotted the lights herself. I had my phone out and camera app open. I tried to get video footage, but my phone would not start recording. I pressed that button repeatedly. Nothing. I couldn't even see them in the view finder. We watched them move across the sky at a steady pace. We lost them behind some trees. I walked east down the alley next to my house. By this time my youngest daughter had joined us outside. She ran to the crossway section of the alley. She stated she could see them too. By the time I got to where she was, I saw 1 light peak out (more faint than before) from behind the end of the tree line. I blinked and it was gone. There was a plane or helicopter flying higher than where we saw them, but it was flying west. It was also closer than the lights, as I could hear it but barely.
