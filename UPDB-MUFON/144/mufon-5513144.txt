dark object poked in and out of a cloud

With two of us in the auto coming out of Nashville headed East on I-24 - just before the Mufreesboro exit we both witnessed a dark object bobbing up and down - poking out of the top of a "cloud" (?). Moving side to side as well. After several minutes of this odd movement the object was gone.
As we traveled toward the area where the object had been spotted - there were NO clouds.
During the sighting we both noticed a white helicopter in the same vicinity.
As quickly as the object disappeared......the helicopter was gone as well.
