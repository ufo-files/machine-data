Saw white/red/yellowish lights flashing in a systematic pattern. Flying VERY fast, much faster than any plane. Seemed to wobble a little.

I was told to go outside by a friend because his mom had seen something at her house. I went into my parents' front yard and stood out there for about 20 min. Within 5 minutes I saw an oject moving through the sky. The object has white, yellow and red lights flashing sequentially and flying in a straight pattern in the NE sky. The object appeared to wobble a little moving side to side. It was moving much much faster than any plane I had ever seen at night. I then saw another object do the exact same thing about five minutes later, although this object was moving a little slower. I lost sight of both ojects as they moved through the sky and behind my parents house and were then masked by a hill.
