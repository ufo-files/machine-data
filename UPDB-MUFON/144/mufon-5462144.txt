UFO seen in early eve from garden, a large disc with markings, it faded as it moved away.

I was in my back garden looking for the rising moon but it was still behind a tree, I turned to look towards the town and saw the object, a disc shape, with markings, hovering in the sky, in a position above my tree/bushes. I had no doubt it was a UFO and couldn't believe my eyes. It then moved silently to the right, fading as it went, and it disappeared. I had called my husband but by the time he arrived from the house it was no longer visible. Recently there was an interview with Kim Wilde on ITV's, 'loose women' and she showed a picture of a UFO she had seen many years ago, I believe 2009, the UFO I saw was absolutely exactly the same. 
It was disc shaped which is not in your shape listing.
