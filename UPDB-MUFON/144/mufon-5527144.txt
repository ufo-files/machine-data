Visual Sighting while Driving

My Personel Computer is down so Im using the Library Computer to send this Ive already sent one E-mail but since I don't know if it went through Im sending a second E-mail. I was visiting the Dam and Recreation Area in Peru, Indiana in Miami County. On Mon.Mar. 28, between the hours of 7:00 and 8:00 am on Highway 12 coming into White Pigeon, Mich. I noticed a very Bright Illuminated Triangular Shaped, Stationary Object, as it was getting Light and there were no Stars Visible at all in the Sky and this Object was too close to Earth to be a Star. I observed the Object for 5 to 10 minutes and then noticed something streaking across the Sky, and what appeared to be a Jet traveling at a very High Rate of Speed as if Pursing it, left 2 white Plumes behind it. I asked Numerous People at Gas Stations and other Places if they Noticed it but since I was a Stranger they acted like they didn't want to Talk about it. My first Encounter with a U.F.O. sighting was at Fort Ord, California, I was pulling Guard Duty in a Isolated part of the Base at 2:00 am in the Morning, I mentioned it to the Sargent who picked me up after Guard Duty about 4:00am who told me to forget it and don't mention it. I was in the United States Army from 1971 to 1973. Sincerely *(Wit's name deleted--CMS/sg).
