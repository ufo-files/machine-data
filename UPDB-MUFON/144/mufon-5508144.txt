Glowing red square with orange light in centre

Out walking my dog with a friend I looked up as we came to the main road and saw a red light in the sky I asked my friend what she thought it was and we both stopped and watched as it slowly went over us and continued in a straight line till we could see it no longer..There was no noise and in the centre was a small orange light that seemed to flicker, whatever it was looked like a square and glowed red,there was only one we have never seen anything like that before..planes at the height it was would have made quite a noise this made no noise at all..
