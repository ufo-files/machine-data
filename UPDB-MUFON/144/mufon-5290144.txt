THERE WILL NEVER BE A SHORT DESCRIPTION TO ALL i HAVE SEEN

I LEFT THE POWELL RIVER HOSPITAL IN BRITISH COLUMBIA AFTER A BRIEF STAY AND LOOKOVER FROM ALL THAT NEEDED TO THAT DAY...ALL I CAN SAY IS THANK YOU FOR CARING AND OPENING MY EYES TO A GREATER WORLD. I AM STILL VERY LOST IN THIS RELM OF THINGS BUT ONE DAY I HOPE TO FIND PEACE AND LOVE FOR ALL ON THIS BEAUTIFUL WORLD YOU ARE. TRING TO SHOW ME... I LEFT THE HOSPITAL THAT LATE AFTERNOON DAY AND WALKED A DISTANCE UNTIL THE HEELS OF MY FEET BLED AND THE PAIN OF A FOOL SAW A LIGHT BUT WOULD STILL NOT BELIEVE WHAT MEN WOULD CLOSE THERE EYES TO FOR ALL THESE YEARS... A LARGE WHITE ORIB THE SIZE OF THE EVENING MOON FOLLOWED ME FOR MANY MILES THAT NIGHT...AND EACH TIME I LOOKED BACK AT IT... NO NOISE , NO MOVEMENT, JUST A SENSE OF PEACE I FELT. THE ONLY REAL CALM PEACE I HAVE EVER FELT IN MY ENTIRE LIFE... AND BELEIVE ME, READERS...IF YOU KNEW MY LIFE, YOU MIGHT KNOW WHY. TO THIS DAY AFTER SEEING THAT BRIGHT ROUND CIRCULAR OBJECT MY HEART AND MIND HAVE BEEN MORE COMPASSIONATE FOR PEOPLE AND THINGS AROUND ME...DONT GET ME WRONG I STILL HAVE MY DAYS BUT SOMETHING CHANGED MY OUTLOOK ON MANY THINGS... THERE IS A WORLD OUT THERE THAT MANY WILL NEVER SEE., OR BE APART OF... I AM STILL LOOKING FOR THAT PLACE FOR MYSELF BUT I KNOW MY WILL AND STRENGTH WILL SHOW ME THE WAY. THERE ARE MANY MORE EVENTS I HAVE SEEN IN THE PAST COUPLE OF YEARS BUT LET'S JUST LOOK AT ONE THING AT A TIME.
