UFO hovered over residential area

I was out taking photos of the moon and Venus when I spotted what I thought was a star cluster. I have poor eyesight, so with the naked eye and squinting through the camera, I thought I was photographing a star cluster. When I looked at the photos the next day, it became evident that this was a UFO that was changing colors from green to purple to what look like white panels, then became a structure with a bunch of light bulbs. I made a video and put it on YouTube [removed link] to share what I photographed and get feedback. I did calculations based on the EXIF data from the camera and what I know the camera to be capable of. I calculate that the object was 1.6 miles away, around 9,700 feet high, and 289 feet long.
