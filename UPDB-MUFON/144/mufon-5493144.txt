{nd} 2 hovering star lights pulse a bright light and proceeded to move northward

I was outside with husband observing the planets to the west and I happen to turn to the northeast and was looking at the stars when one of them pulsed a bright light and it began to move northward for a short distance and then disappeared. I told my husband what I saw and he was looking with me in the same direction when another star pulsed a bright light and it too moved to the north and then disappeared. Years ago when I lived in New Hampshire, I saw the same type of experience which made want report this.
