Large H Shape craft with white lights

my daughter was staying in my mother's house back in 2014 when she was 18 yrs old, her bed was right by her window. It was about 4pm she was on her phone lying on the bed when something caught her eye above the house across the street at said she saw a huge object hovering over the house.. She was astonished and tried to ring me because she got scared.. then she couldn't remember what happened after that.. She remembers phoning me later and telling me. She then drew me a picture. She still talks about it. She did tell me it was silent and didn't make any noise from the engine. The craft was a H shape with a flashing light at the front or back and lights along the side, she couldn't believe no one else didn't see this, she feels she had loss of time and as time has gone on she has started doubting herself. She did draw me a picture at the time. My daughter is not the type of person to make things up and is a sceptic but at the time she was adamant she saw this
