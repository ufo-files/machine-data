Zig zag star? Moved back and forth and almost in a circle

Wee we were watching the annual shooting star show -tonight and I saw this star about the same size as a star in the Big Dipper, moving. I compared it to the satellites I could see, not same pattern, to the plane that flew past, not same at all, the path was varied in speed, it changed direction quickly at times, other times it was still, it almost tracked back on itself sometimes, little circle movements… it’s all so strange. I’m not a UFO person, I’m a nurse- science based mind… my partner has very analytical mind- even he said it isn’t a satellite or plane- we considered the clouds but the distance it moved about doesn’t seem to make sense w the bit of thin cloud moving past…
