{rjl} Strange lights over Prescott, AZ

I went out on my front porch which faces west/NW and saw at first 6 lights like low hanging stars hovering over between Willow Creek Rd and Granite Mtn. As I watched, a 7th light popped into view. They began slowly moving around, with the light furthest to my left steadily pulsating, then went bright, dimmed, and the next one to the right of it brightened and began pulsating, and the rest would change positions. They slowly moved up and down and around each other in ways planes don't move. Eventually one by one they would each move off to my right and out of view. I observed them for approx 30 minutes.
