{rjl} 20-25 lights from launch and flight patterns to fade out and hovering

Approximately 9:25 pm January 24th 2014 I observed 2 reddish orange lights in the sky that moved in an ascending patter moving from south to north. After approximately 2 minutes, new lights appeared to be "launching" or rising rapidly from the south and travelling to the north. After reaching various altitudes, the lights would stop hover change direction and then eventually fade out and disappear at various intervals. This occurred approximately 20 -25 times. There was no sound.
