{dr}The object hovered over my uncle Dons new house. My Dad US Air Force pilot spoted it first,then my Mom and Aunt,then me an my two cusains.

I will give you the best description of the event as I can. It started when my Dad spotted what he thought was a reflection off the new power lines behind my uncle Dons new house (Fullerton,Ca) it was a object that Dad was looking at. He got Dons binoculars and started watching it close to see what it was, he could not id it. side note: Dad was in the AF for 28yrs and he flew bombers as well fighters, he flew his own plan until he was 81. He knew it was something other than a plane. Don work for the power company as electrical engineer. when dad ask for the binoculars Mom and my Aunt went out side to see what was going on and they watched it as well. what was over heard by our parents, got me and my cousins headed outside to see what was going on. AND this is where it got spooky.... we all looked up and there they were just hovering making no noise, what happened was this; Watching the Big ship, and then 3 smaller ones showed up out of no where, they hovered for about min then into the big ship, then in ( what I will say, 90 sec ) it had these round ball like things come out faster than you can blink, in a vee shape (6 to 8) then it just vanished leaving a small smoke trail, then it disappeared, like poof!! I can finish with this; I know my dad reported it and was told NOT to reveal anything about it, he never said any more about it.I can tell you I was scared after that, and I never said anything about it tell now. I would like to know what happened to that report that Dad turned in?? all this took place around 1950/1951. (I was born in 1942.)He made the report at a Military Base in San Pedro,Ca, it was an Army base.
