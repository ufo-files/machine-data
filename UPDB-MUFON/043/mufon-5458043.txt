{rjl} Very bright light with unusual movement before shot up into space

My girlfriend and I were out cooking dinner when I noticed an unusually bright and large light overhead. This light appeared not only unusually large compared to the many airplanes I've grown accustomed to seeing flying overhead, but also made multiple course corrections during the time I was observing it. I observed it for approximately 10 seconds before I grabbed my binoculars and observed it through those for another 10 seconds as it made a final course correction straight up at a much faster rate of speed. It vanished into space, and that was it.
