A spinning multicolored rotating upsidedown triangle.

I had left home to get something to eat at McDonald's,after I had ordered my food. I had looked up,for the spinning movement caught my attention. As I had looked right at it,it had sat motionless about 100 feet up,right directly above the BP gas sign. All of a sudden when the lady cashiere came to the window to ask for payment of the food; I had looked away from it for a second to pay for the food,it was gone. I fear GOD Almighty,GOD Allpowerful. I thank him for everything. P.S. I live close to Fairborn,Ohio. Home of Wright Patterson/AFB. Yours truley GregKPorter.
