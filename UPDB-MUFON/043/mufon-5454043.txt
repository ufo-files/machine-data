While fishing at approximately 630, I noticed a glowing starlike shape hovering on the horizon. It remained stationary for approximately 5 minutes and then just rocketed straight up leaving a large single vapor trail. I was able to get about 7 or 8 cell phone pictures of the sighting. My brother in law was in the front of the boat and saw the exact same thing. The sun was setting behind the craft and should have been a dark spot instead of glowing which is why it caught my attention.
