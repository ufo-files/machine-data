The craft was coin shaped silent 60 ft across 80-100 ft up & coverd in anti radar paint.

When living in brinnington on my way home to Middlesex rd.on my mountain bike I stopped on the foot bridge over the train track looking up i saw a coin shaped craft 60 ft.across 4-5 ft.vertical Matt black in colour totally silent & 80-100 ft.up appear into view over head & continued on strait out of view over the houses across Middlesex rd.at first I was shocked then excited thinking I had seen an extra terrestrial craft but then remembered the i could only conclude was anti radar paint then & since I have thought so I didn't report it because it struck me as being military possibly so didn't want to rock the boat so to speak till now.
