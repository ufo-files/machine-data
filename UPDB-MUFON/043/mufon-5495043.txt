{dr}1 object seen coming toward my position the stopping mid flight and hovering. 2nd object kept path

I was standing on my back porch letting my dog out around 4AM and noticed a bright gold/orange object in the sky heading in my direction. At first I thought nothing off it and figured it to be a plane or helicopter. While I was watching it, another object came into view N of the other object and was heading in the direction of the 1st object. That's when things got wierd because they slowed down and hovered. The 1st object descended then stopped mid flight. I noticed the second object had begun moving again and I lost it behind trees. When I noticed the main object again it got extremely bright, almost like a meteor entering the atmosphere and grew in size. I looked at it through binoculars and I noticed it was almost gyrating as it lite up and hovered in place above the tree line. As I was watching the object it started to slowly move to my left and stopped, then came back to the right in a very spooky way. After about 15-20 min I lost visual as it descended below a tree line. This was definitely a very shocking thing to witness but at the same time it was so spectacular to watch. I can only hope I see these objects again.
