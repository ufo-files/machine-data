Red yellow orange light pulsating

I was in the yard taking out the dogs. I was facing to the east. I saw a red light through the tree top to my right. I live and grew up near and currently live near Niagara air force base, so I tend to watch things that fly hoping to see something interesting. Once this light cleared the trees I immediately notice that it was different from anything I had ever seen: it appeared as a solid red light that was lighter I'm the middle. It seemed to pulsate randomly. There were no other indicator lights like I was used to seeing, no green or white, no wing lights, no sound. There is a local small aircraft airport nearby as well so I have seen a lot of single and double prop aircraft. It was moving along at a good clip. I watched it travel in a straight line for under 5 minutes. At one of the pulses it totally disappeared for a few seconds, maybe 3 to 5 seconds, then reappeared on the same trajectory for a few seconds, then pulsed out again. I stood there for an additional 5 minutes and didn't look away and it never reappeared. I have seen a multitude of private aircraft, c130s, refueling stratotankers, globe masters, f22s, f18s, predator drones, meteors, and a host of helicopters from medical transport to power line surveying to dual rotor military transport. I have never seen that type of light and never just saw something disappear like that and would like some closure on it.
