Two white lights moving in sync.

When driving around Arundel Maine we noticed two lights moving in the sky to the left of the road. The lights were white and brighter than what I would expect from a plane at that distance, and we only saw white lights, no red or green ones. One of the lights was seen blinking, then stopped and shone continuously for the rest of the sighting. The lights looked liked they were at the front and tail end of a craft. After moving slowly to the left, the craft stopped and hovered. After keeping an eye on the lights for a moment, we lost it in the trees, and when the trees parted again we could not find the lights. If the object kept moving at the pace it was when we originally saw it, there is no way that it could have escaped our line of sight in such a short period of time.
