a bright shooting star heading towards the sky that changed speed

i was outside my house in Glen Waverley when i noticed how clear the night is i always stare at the stars when i saw i what i thought was the brightest shooting star ascending from the ground level to the stars at rapid pace leaving a trail behind. It was moving very fast and appeared to have just vanished into the dark sky it happened so quickly i was truly amazed at the speed there was no sound my first thought was "do shooting stars fly in that direction" i wanted to inform somebody about this in the hope of some sort of information about this event and did anybody else see it. i really feel as though i may have witnessed something truly amazing , i really just want to know if anybody else saw this or even somehow managed to record it. i do have the greatest skyline view from my house and this is the first time ive seen anything like this! Rob.
