(GS) burial

My father told me that back in 1947 he and my uncle,who had driven for three days and nights and was working on a secret project for the Army at the time,buried an unknown object under the burn barrel in back of the garage on the right hand side. My father said he didn't know what it was,but my uncle seemed to be extremely anxious to get rid of it.I don't know what it was,but all these years I have wondered.Someday,someone may find out that it was part of the Roswell situation.I don't know,just spreading the knowledge.Could just be a manufacturing error he was trying to hide.
