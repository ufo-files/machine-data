{cak} giant snake/worm coiling through the sky moving fast but moving like a serpent

My Girlfriend and I saw what she called a "dragon worm" move through the night sky like a snake would, from far in the sky to close enough that it scared both of us into going back inside our apt immediately. after about 4 min I came back outside and could hear fast moving jets moving towards Canada in the direction we last saw the "dragon worm". I stood outside for another 10 or 15 min waiting for something else to happen when I saw an Enormous black boomerang pass right over my head with no sound. A few moments later I could hear fast moving jets coming back from the area I last heard them and could hear them fly right over my head towards the boomerang.. I never saw any jets but from how loud they were I assumed there was more than one and the time from when I could hear them to when they were loudest to when they were gone, I could assume they were very fast moving. This was over a year ago and it still has not left my mind. I have been looking online here and there for reports of things like what we saw and have found very few that looked real or authentic. And none that moved the way whatever we saw that night moved.
