Witnessed large practically invisible triangle with 3 dull lights traveling south to north approx. 2000-5000ft in altitude

I was returning from a walk with my dog around 830 pm looking up in the night sky noticing the brightness of all the stars, when 3 dull lights in the shape of a triangle caught my attention. The 3 lights where very dull, almost invisible. The 3 lights were moving from the south and heading north. I could tell that it was a triangle because the area between the 3 dull lights on each corner was slightly lighter in appearance than the sky around it. The lights on the 3 corners remained solid, and did not blink, flash, or pulse. The lights where all the same dull white color. The triangle did not make any sound as it traveled across the sky.
