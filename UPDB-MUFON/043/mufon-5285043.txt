Standing outside on our deck. I saw what I thought was a planet. It had flickering bright lights.

Standing outside on the deck, looking out over to the eastern sky, thought I saw a planet, after looking at it for a minute, I saw it had flickering bright lights. Got a pair of binoculars and took a closer look. It had blue and red, possibly white lights that were flickering and pulsating. I would have said it was a plane but it didn’t move at all. After fifteen minutes or so I went back in the house. Later, I let the dogs out, by that time the lights had disappeared.
