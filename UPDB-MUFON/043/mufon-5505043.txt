{kt}The UFO kept disappearing and then reappearing.

I was sleeping and I woke up around 4:45 AM. I had a weird feeling I should look out my bedroom window. I saw a light in the sky and at first I thought it was a plane or a star but then it disappeared and then reappeared and I knew it wasn't. I took video and pictures of it and it had blue and red blinking lights in the video when I zoomed in. It was just hovering there. I got video and picture of the UFO and noticed it had changed location when I looked at it again. I was scared. The UFO disappeared and I never saw it again.
