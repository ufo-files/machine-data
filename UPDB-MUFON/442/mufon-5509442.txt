basically i will break down the sighting into two parts, it happened around midnight, i spotted a large white sphere orbiting the 3/4 moon, after a minute the sphere blinked out and appeared probably 1000 miles from its original lunar orbit, a minute passed again and the sphere blinked out and reappear in its original lunar orbit,it did this several times, it was enough time to get corroborating witness, then it vanished, i work odd hours so im used to being out in the middle of the night, i like to stargaze, approximately an hour later i stepped outside again for a smoke and this is when the 2nd sighting happened,(no corroborating witness this time), only reason i looked up was i heard a small propjob plane go by with its classic light pattern, after the plane passed thats when i noticed a sphere flashing all colours of the spectrum above the tree line but right below the clouds so probably a few miles up from my south westerly line of sight, it was very odd, it was bright like a star but multicolored and definitely in earths lower atmosphere, weird thing about was as i was watching i noticed i could not keep my eyes focused on it even though it was stationary,by this time i was very intrigued about what this thing in the sky could be, I happened to have my 350 lumen led work light with me, i proceeded to shine my light at this hanging anomaly in different strobe patterns, as i did this the wavelength of colors started to change and the object started list left and right and up and down but still staying in its general original sighting area, when i could focus my eyes on it the the colors were amazing, this all took place within a 1 to 2 hour time frame, then at which the object slowly moved northwest gaining altitude till it disappeared behind the tree line from my line of sight. im pretty familiar with things in the sky and this was something very exotic!
