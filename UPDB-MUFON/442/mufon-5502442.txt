I was at the Erie Canal Locks at Lockport NY and I was at street level near Lock 35 near the lock door between lock 34 & 35. 

One of the disks did a loop and that caught my eye. Both were a bright white and after the look I noticed the second one and they kept their spacing and went to the North East toward Lake Ontario. There was no sound and they never changed altitude. They disappeared, I thought, before they should have been out of range. They seemed to be solid, no windows that I could see. They were not military or civilian aircraft. They seemed to be up at least where a small plane would fly or maybe higher. I had no way to tell how high they were because I didn't know their size. There is a coal fired electric power plant on Lake Ontario in the direction of the North East In the Town of Summerset NY. There is an Airport/NY Air Guard Base in Niagara Falls with Radar along with Buffalo-Niagara International Airport.

I am sending in 3 pictures JPG. 2 of what I tried to capture and one of the moon to show what the viewing conditions were. I am also sending the RAW photos (NEF). 

Photos taken with a Nikon D5100 with a Sigma 18-250mm Lens. Camera was set to Landscape. I had to switch to manual focus because there was nothing big enough to focus on
