Blue falling streaks followed by lights flying nearly unison rectangle formation

This occurred on a section of trail bridge over the Boca Ceiga Bay in the Bay Pines area of St. Petersburg. Was riding my bicycle on the trail at night and stopped on the bridge to take a picture of the unusual moon that night. I was aiming my camera into the sky when I saw shooting/falling blue streaks appear on the camera screen but when I looked at the sky they were not visible. I snapped a couple of shots and the streaks were captured on the pic. This was perplexing so I started to a video in case recording could give more clues on the streaks, however, instead of more blue streaks there now appeared rectangular formations of white lights that moved nearly (but not quite) in unison. The formations also appeared individually or in a line at times but always flew and changed direction at impossibly fast speeds. Further, I was only able to visualize any of these objects through the camera lens of my iPhone, they were never visible to my naked eye; when I looked into the sky for the objects I could see on my screen there appeared to be nothing there.
