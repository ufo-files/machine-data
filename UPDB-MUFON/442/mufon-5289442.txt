Circular or spheres, dull colored objects

At work yard at approx 7:20 pm Mar 01, I glanced up into the nite sky and observed what I first thought be be 2 balloons but quickly realized that they were not.
They objects were circular or spherical and appeared to be approx. 4-10 feet in diameter. One object followed the other in line. I immediately ran to my 2 co-workers who were also standing outside and pointed out the objects. They appeared to be several hundred yards above our position heading North in a straight line. 
I then called to my supervisor who was inside the work truck to observe these objects..
I then looked backed to the sky and all witnesses observed that the initial 2 objects were followed by 2 more objects trailing roughly side by side or one slightly to the side and behind.
The 4 objects continued in a straight approx. Northerly course.
I attempted to catch the objects on video but nothing was apparent on the screen focused on the nite sky.
As they disappeared from view, I also observed a pinpoint of light high, high above and to the West of the objects, which seemed to parallel the objects course. This pinpoint of light looked like a satellite when observed moving high in the night sky.
After the objects disappeared from view, I spoke to my co-workers and none were able to explain what we had observed nor offer any plausible explanation of the objects.
One later observed that the objects appeared to be in formation.
