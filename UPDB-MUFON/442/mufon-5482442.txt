{nd} Several orange orbs on the horizon.

Traveling home after spending the 4th of July in Oregon with family, I spotted what I first thought to be an airplane on the horizon while traveling east bound on I-84 just outside of Caldwell, Idaho. As I watched the single orange colored light split into two separate lights almost at the same time two more appeared within close proximity, I asked my wife in the passenger seat of our suv if she saw it and then all 4 were gone. She then started poking fun until after a few miles she pointed another orange orb out to me.this time when it disappeared we spotted another light but it was pulsating like a strobe light. We were able to keep it in sight until we got close to Boise and then lost it because of all the street lights.
