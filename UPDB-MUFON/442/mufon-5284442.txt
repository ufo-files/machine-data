Bright, glowing triangle, turning, phasing in and out.

My family and I were at Six Flags amusement park waiting in line for one of the coasters. I happened to just look up in the sky because there were a few airliner jets coming by, as is common in that area. Way further up into the sky I noticed a shining object. Initially I thought it was another jet just way, way up. However, it struck me as odd because it was almost flashing and I noticed it was not moving, and so I started watching it very intently. That's when my wife noticed it and then other people in line did as well. I got some video on my phone, and I was able to zoom in a little as well. If you have the capability to zoom in on the video, you can see that this object is either a triangle that is rotating or changing shape and that it is almost fading in and out of the visible spectum at times. Despite the glow, it also had sort of a mist or a dark aura around it. This was visible with the naked eye, and not just an artifact of the video. It got brighter as we started to watch it, and I got the feeling that it was putting on a show for us. Then is started to move and slowly faded until no longer visible. No one made the suggestion, but everyone on their own stated that it was a UFO or something from out of this world!
