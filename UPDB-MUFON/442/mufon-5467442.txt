INCIDENT #5
As described by Greg Fergus
Oahu Hawaii June 30th 1988

Greg Fergus was an E4 Army mechanic on Chinook helicopters at the P3 Squadron at Barbers Point Navel Air Station. Greg Fergus, his friend Phil Davis the Army Chaplain, and a Navy Commander decided to go diving pre-dawn that morning. It was dark but there were many stars out and there was a bright moon. They pushed their 16 ft small boat out from the shore near the town of Waianae. 

They were about 100,yards from shore and were preparing to begin diving when this very large cylindrical object came down silently from the sky and began emitting a cloud of green vapor or gas from the bottom of the object, The object was about 200 feet in vertical length and 70 feet wide, The cloud of gas spread about 200 YARDS in a horizontal direction from their observation point. The men themselves were within approx. 100 YARDS from the object. The men were also about 100 YARDS from the shore. The object stayed there for approximately 3 to 4 minutes and then accelerated vertically at an astonishing speed before disappearing .

The men were completely terrified and scrambled to get their boat back to shore.

The Navy Commander told Greg and the Chaplain that they must not say anything about the encounter as he could lose his command by reporting UFO stuff. The Commander was just as shaken as the other two.

Greg says attempts have been made in recent years to trace Phil Davis for corroboration of this report but have been unsuccessful. It is believed Phil is a Pastor somewhere in the USA. He was originally from Witchita Falls TX.

This author will also begin a tracing process.
