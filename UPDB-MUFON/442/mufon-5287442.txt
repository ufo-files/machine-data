I captured video on my home security camera of a small craft/light orb that appeared to come out of my garage (small golf ball size) with a sparkling trail behind it. It then seemed to shapeshift slightly and appeared larger, maybe the size of a grapefruit. It appeared to possibly have wings and tail fin as it flew past the camera. The orb then flew into my neighbors house and accelerate in speed. It had another sparkly trail behind it as it flew off. The flight into my neighbors house appeared to go out of earth dimension. The whole thing lasted about 7 seconds and was enough motion to set off the motion sensor on the camera to record it. There is sound on my camera and no apparent noise was immitted. There is no physical explanation for the light. There were no lights from my house or my neighbors, no cars driving by. The event happened about 3-4 feet off of the ground and within 5-7 feet from my front door. It was suggested to look like ash or smoldering debris. I do not have a fire pit and there were was no burning in my neighborhood that evening as far as I know. I am not in a location where forest fires occur. This occurred in a town of about 55,000 people and there is an airport and a military air force base within about 10 miles of my home.
