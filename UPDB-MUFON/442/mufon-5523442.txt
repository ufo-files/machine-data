two hovered in sky in the Ursa Major constellation, one white one red.

My wife, daughter, her friend and I were comming home from the store. When we started to turn into the neighborhood my wire noticed a bright object in the sky in the middle of the big dipper and asked me what it was. I seen the object and then a bright red one at the right edge of the big dipper. Both were stationairy and were not flashing, just solid light. Looked to be just north of town and they were not flashing like the normal aricraft that we see in the sky. There is a national guard helicopter unit outside of town and it looked nothing like what we see every day and night. The red one disappeared very quick and the white/yellow one stayed in one spot. After about 2-3 minutes it started getting smaller but never moved toward the horizion, it was as if it was moving out of the atmosphere and slowly got smaller and dimmer until it disapered. This was nothing like we see around here and it made my wife a little nervous
