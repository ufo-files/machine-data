{nd} 10-12 Orange orbs lights in a row flying all in one direction

ON 2/23/13 I LET MY dog outback to pee and from the corner of my eyes I noticed this bright orange orb or light flying low across the sky with a plane heading toward it from se to ne then another one . almost in pairs of 2-3 in a straight line one after another. in the photos If you look to the right side of pics you can see some through the trees.I yell to my fianc1 to grab every cell or camera and film it which she did. a few of the orbs looked like the merge together or crossed paths!!! the re aligned.The as they were passing us a strange DOWNPOOR OF RAIN HAERD RAIN CAME DOWN JUST IN OUR AREA!!!! AS SOME POLICE OR FIRETRUCKS STARTED FLYING BY THE STREET TOWARD THAT DIRECTION. WE DIDNT GET A GOOD LOOK SINCE WE WERE LOOKIN UP TRYING TO FIGURE OUT WHAT WAS GOING ON .. ABBY RAN IN THE HOUSE. THEN MY FIANCES CAMERA WITH NEW BATTERIES DIED/SHUT OFF SHORTLY AFTER WE STARTED FILMING! ABOUT 4 ORBS INTO FILMING. THEN SHE BRUNG HER CELL CAMERA OUT AND STARTED TAKING PHOTO AND SAID IT WAS BLURRING AND NOTHING SHOWED UP ON HER CELL. WE DO HAVE A VIDEO AND A FEW PICS ON HER CAMERA..one of the strangest things I have ever seen! the was viewable from Jensen beach to port saint lucie. I felt mesmerized at what we were witnessing... picture the color of a orange street light but huge and about 10-12 of them flying by  very odd night..
