{rjl} blue-white orb traveled straight with no sound

On january 17 2014 i was in mauriceville texas at my uncles home getting ready to go hunting in waco.i was at the edge of his house near the porch but not on it handling the rifles and ice chest, everybody was inside talking except for me. It was very clear that night and since mauriceville is kind of rural there wasnt light pollution so the stars were very beautiful, i live in the city so i took the opportunity to star gaze a little and it was as soon as i looked up that i saw this object. It was white in the middle with a blue tinge that gotta darker towards the edges. This thing was hauling ass and i mean it! I live near an airport so i know this wasnt a plane. It took about ten seconds to go through the entire sky on a straight path. After it was gone i had to stop and think and i just got this overwhelming feeling of awe and beauty. I know what i saw. I haven't told anyone else about it. Im 16 years old and i will take this to my grave. My grandfather saw a ufo back in the 80s in winnie texas, he never even talks about it. All i know is that life will be different because i will have to bear the burden of this forever and i don't want to talk because down here in the bible belt people are quick to judge. Please do your best to find more witnesses in the area because i have to know more about this and see if other people experienced what i did that night.
