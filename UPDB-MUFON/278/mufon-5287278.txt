Dim lights over San Francisco

I was awakened at 4:12 am by my dog when I looked out my bay window and noticed 8-10 faintly lit objects. Size and brightness of a dim star. They were traveling from east to west in a strong westerly wind around 30 KTS. There were approximately 8-10 objects in a random pattern but fixed in their separation to one another as they crossed the sky. Their altitude was 5,000 and traveling at least 500 KTS. I observed them for 15 to 20 seconds.
