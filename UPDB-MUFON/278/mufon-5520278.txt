Large completely silent triangular aircraft gliding slowly at low altitude a few miles from my location

As i was sitting in my car after pumping gas at shell station less than a mile from my home, I noticed a bright white fixed light hovering just below the ridge-line. It was low enough that at 1st glance, I thought maybe it was just a light from a home or building on the ridge. I then noticed the light wasnt fixed to part of any structure, & that it was moving very slowly towards my location; I sat in my car at the gas pump to watch the light as it sort of drifted slowly, & effortlessly closer to my position. It moved closer & closer very lazily, to reveal 2 flashing lights (slightly varying in color, sort of off-white & amber; [note] they were not apparent upon initially seeing the aircraft) on its right side. I couldnt make out a shape at that time, & i assumed that it was just a helicopter, as the altitude seemed too low for an airplane. I rolled down my windows (from cracked to fully open) to see if i could hear any engine noise, there was absolutely no sound; The gas station wasnt busy, so there was no obstruction in my ability to hear it if there were. As i said before, the craft was at very low altitude. As the craft flew (almost) directly over head, its shape & characteristics became obvious; it was distinctively triangular, & the wing span was at-least double the size of any airplane ive ever seen. There was a singular bright fixed lights on its nose, a bright amber light in the dead center, 2 flashing lights at the tip of each wing (or where the wings shouldve been). The craft drifted listlessly, moving as if it were underwater, & as i was observing, it sort of banked slightly to reveal its underside, & effortlessly flew turned vertically w/a wing pointed towards the sky, & a wing pointed to the ground. It maintained this maneuver for only a short period of time, however it was able to perform it w/an ease that seemed highly unusual. Each of these lights that formed this triangular shape were very vivid & not characteristic of any standard aircraft im familiar w/. I felt the urge to get out of the car & alert it to the attention the few people around outside, however no1 was paying any attention, nor did the seem like theyd be interested, & my shyness/fear of embarrassment wouldnt allow me to speak up to point it out. I noticed it was flying towards an area i was familiar w/, so i decided to follow it. I lost sight of it as i was making a U-turn in the parking lot to head in its direction, & as tried to relocate the object, It had completely disappeared. I don't know if i just lost track of it, or what, but the direction it was moving, along w/the apparent speed & size, plus the fact it only took 20 seconds or so to turn around & pull out of the parking lot, I felt like i shouldve been able to relocate it w/ease, but it was nowhere to be found. It was disappointing to lose track, however it was exhilarating to be eyewitness to such a marvel of aeronautics, whether it be 1 of ours, or from another world all-together. The area my small town occupies seems to be a magnet for UFO activity, as i see strange lights/craft fairly often...
