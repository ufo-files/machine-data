{tjd} Star like lights heading to each other

As I was walking my dog around 846pm, I saw what I thought might be the ISS above. It was a tiny glowing dot like a far away star moving directly across the sky at what I believe was north to north west. As I was tracking it through the trees of the sidewalk, over the street, I noticed that another dot just like it was heading the opposite direction, directly towards the first dot. As they got close I noticed a third dot also heading to the same exact point and they were making a perfect isosceles triangle and all heading for each other. Next I know there was just the one dot. I tried to film it with my phone but it was too dark. I noticed another 2 star like dots head toward the main one during the encounter, after the first three. After that I went home, but the main dot remained.
