Pitch black, darker than night sky, perfect sphere moving slowly at constant speed

My brother and I were having a bonfire one summer night, we were bother sober and clear of mind. The night sky was perfectly clear and as the fire died, I was looking up at the night sky and saw a perfectly shaped Circle, or sphere, pitch black against the bluish night sky. It was moving slowly, in a straight line, not deviating, from North to south, it was in my field of vision for approximately 2 minutes before it was not visible due to treetops blocking the horizon.

My date is not accurate as I am making this report years later...

When asking my brother about it now, he does not recall this incident.
