{tjd} Two glowing objects moving supersonic south to north at low altitude

At 22:18 on 31 Dec 19 I exited my house to the west to retrieve an item from my car. The weather was clear and visibility was unlimited. I saw a large glowing, oval shaped object approximately 1000 ft. in the air and about 2 miles west of my location traveling at well above supersonic speed. The object first appeared in the sky just above the moon and dissapeared to the NNW. The first object took about 1 to 2 seconds to travel from south to north. Just before the first object disapeared a second object, similar in all respects to the first, appeared on the same flight path and altitude chasing or following the first. There was no sound.

There were no searchlights in the area and no light reflecting off of dust in the air to indicate the presence of a searchlight. There was no light above the objects and nothing for llight to reflect off of. Winds at 190 degrees and 9 kts. Barometric pressure 30.05. Visibility greater than 10 miles. Ceiling unlimited.  In my professional opinion these were not searchights. An FAA weather briefing was obtained 1 hr and 40 minutes after the sighting. Other aircraft lights that could be seen were well above the unidentified objects.
