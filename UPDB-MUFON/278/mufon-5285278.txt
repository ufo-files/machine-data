silver oval shape approx size of football ascending

First day of school, driving home from teaching, as I approached high school, a power line crossing the road in front of me, was emitting stream of sparks, reaching all the way to the road from the line, and directly in the path in which I was driving. I slowed, but not enough to stop, and looked up just before passing under the line. From the point of the sparks on the powerline, I looked up just before passing under the sparks,and there was a silver metallic shape ascending upward and to the left. I've never forgotten this experience. I know this experience is insignificant compared to the more outstanding sightings, encounters, but I've never forgotten it, though it was many years ago.
