{jpg} Unknown object

I stepped outside at approximately 10:28 PM on 11/03/2019 and saw a fast moving object overhead. There were five somewhat dim lights, one central and the others surrounding it. It seemed to change shape slightly. I couldn't tell if it was a single object or five separate ones. It seemed to be flying a similar height to an airplane. It was moving very fast, I first saw it almost directly overhead and it was gone from my sight within 10 seconds. 

I'm one of those "I want to believe" people, but this was the first time in my life I've seen something inexplicable. I'm also an amateur astronomer and spend many nights under a dark sky, and I've never seen anything like this.
