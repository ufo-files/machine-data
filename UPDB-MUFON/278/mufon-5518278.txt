Star flew over Leavenworth

I was outside smoking and watching the sky for anything odd. I noticed a star like object coming out of the SE and flying NNE. Approx 1 min into watching it I checked the time 10:55 CST I continued to watch the "star" Around 2 min of flight it began to dim until it was no longer visible. It reappeared and grew brighter. A plane was traveling out of the west on an intersecting course. As the plane passed through the "stars" path you could tell the "star" was much higher than the plane which appeared to be flying around 20-30K+ ft. The "star" continued on its path where I lost track of it behind a tree to my north. I checked the spaceweather.com site for satellite flybys the time and direction did not match up with any of the satellites they have listed.
