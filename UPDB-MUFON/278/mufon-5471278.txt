this photo was taken by me, in May of 2017 in Michigan, in my back yard...my daughter had heard something in the tall grass, I walked over to see what it was, I saw two brief flashes of light as if from something metallic...it was dusk and the sun was below the horizon at this point. I took out my phone and began taking pictures. I noticed an image above the fire and only adjusted the light exposure on the photo to bring out the image, nothing else has been altered. The small dot of light to the right of the image is a light on a house, several hundred feet away on a neighboring property. We have frequent sightings of ufo's in the sky here, we see them about a third as often as we see aircraft here.
