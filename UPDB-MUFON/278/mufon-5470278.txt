{cak} I live about 5 miles or less from the Pentagon off of Route 395 in a condo on the 15th fl. Brownish object looked like an aquatic mammal with wings and it rotated from front to side. In pics I saw glowing objects.

Observed from my 15th floor condo 4 miles from Pentagon a large object in the West sky on February 12th at 6pm. It looked like a large aquatic like mammal with flippers or wings, dark in color slowly rotating in the sky. It rotated to the side facing in the direction of the Pentagon. When I took pictures I captured about 7 or 8 glowing white objects that took on different shapes and separated. Not sure how long show lasted, probably about two hours because I stopped watching.
