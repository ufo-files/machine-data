Orb over neighbors house

My husband and I were up very late and outside on Nov. 15th when I saw light appear over our neighbors house. My husband had his back to it so he didn't see it at first. The light kind of fazed in, it hovered for quite awhile then it started getting brighter and dimmed. It did this several times. We went inside our house for a minute, came back out and it was gone, then it reappeared over another neighbors house right next door to the house it had first appeared over. Please forgive the talking on the video, I was cursing out my phone camera because it looked very pixalated when I was capturing the orb.
