{cak} Ball of light hovered, increased in brightness to the point of appearing like a large white orb in sky for 4 minutes

Around 7pm i was walking back from our barn when i noticed an incredibly bright light in the sky, brighter than any i had seen before. I briefly recorded it until it disappeared into thin air. I ran inside to tell my family. We decided to walk back outside and i noticed the light about 15 feet to the left of its original position, brighter than it was before. The light dimmed slightly and then proceeded to get brighter and brighter to the point where the light appeared so brightly that it created a distinct orb shape with an aura around it. After staying in this state for a minute or so, the object disappeared.
