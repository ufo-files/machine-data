{rjl} Was alerted to event by small yellow lights.

I did not see a flying saucer. After seeing yellow alert lights which were sent specifically for me, I looked out of my glass door and saw many colored lights projected(?) on a grassy hill behind my apartment. They were all (perhaps 30 large disk shapes) moving back and forth. They were green, white and pale reddish. At the time I tired of watching them and went back to bed, but if they came again I would investigate them more thoroughly, though might get abducted. They had to have been projected from a craft above the apartment building, but they also appeared on the side of the exterior wall near me and I could see past that point and nothing was projecting those yellow lights. They went up that wall and disappeared.
