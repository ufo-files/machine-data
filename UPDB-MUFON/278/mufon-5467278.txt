{td} V shaped object one large light bottom front with small red light circleing it in pulses. Two other white lights on down the wings, after passing the object saw small green lights around the outline of the craft

My wife and I were riding our motorcycle home from Piqua coming up St Rt 66 when I saw the large yellowish light above the tree line. Asked my wife what she thought it could be a chinese lantern but it didn't float up.As we got closer she said it might be a helicopter because another light was visible. And could also see a small red light going around the light in front of the craft on the bottom of it. After we had passed the object I looked back and said no it looks a triangle and saw the green marker lights that showed the shape dimmly. I said triangle but it was actually v shaped. All the time we saw it, it never moved just hovered. I heard no sound but my Harley and never slowed down to investigate. My wife grabbed me tightly and said if they take me your going too lol.
