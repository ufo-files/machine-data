{rjl} Light in western sky. Seen from Dayton, Ohio on April 7, 1969.

A white gold ball sized object was observed in the western sky from Dayton Ohio on April 7, 1969. Object was traveling south to north from our point of observation in a relatively horizontal path for roughly 15 minutes. After 15 minutes the device began to immediately descend on a vertical path for about 5 minutes. 

As the object descended vertically for 5 minutes, near the end of this time, the device began to face out.

As it began to fade out, a seemingly "new" craft began to appear at the beginning point of its trajectory. This cycle continued for roughly 9 to 12 times.

My mother called Wright Patterson Air Force Base to report this sighting.
