1972 white disc black spots or windows hiding in and out of cloud no sound afternoon.

This is the first of 5 different sightings i have had since the age of 10 averaging 1 each decade or so. First as i have already have put in form above but a little more detail, I was playing in a friends back garden 1 summers day it was afternoon and i was swing upside down on playing frame and was looking up at the shy thinking how lovely it was and noticed a white disc shape craft with spots or windows no wings slowly heading towards a white cloud no sound it went behind and i waited for it to appear on the other side as cloud was small and one of only a few about instead it came back same way it went in it did this 3 times then stayed behind the cloud.
