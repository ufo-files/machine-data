{cak} 5 reddish/orange lights,inverted s formation, some lights pulsed bright-then-dimmed, traveled slow westward, then dimmed out

First sighting actually occured on August 4th, at intersection of M-53 & M-59, Shelby Township/Utica, MI. at approx. 9:40pm. Four (4) reddish/orange lights, facing southwest, approx. 80 degrees from horizon, curved pattern to right, steady glow, equally spaced.

Second sighting occured at my residence (corner of M-59 & Hayes, Macomb Township)between 9:40pm & 9:45pm. Same reddish/orange lights caught my attention, this time facing northeast, approx. 30 degrees from horizon. Five (5) lights appeared moving slowly westward in an inverted S shaped pattern. Some lights were pulsing bright-then-dim. Within a span of two-to-three minutes the lights all dimmed then disappeared.

Both occurances were near Selfridge Air National Guard Base. Perhaps some kind of military activity/training?
