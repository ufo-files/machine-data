I saw something very large with possibly a ramp hovering just above the ground.

Around midnight in August 2020 I saw outside my living room window a bright light (that didn’t reflect on everything around it). At first I thought it was a light in my neighbors field (100+feet away). I tried to focus in to see what they might be doing at that time of night. I felt like the light was floating/hovering. I put my elbows on the window seal and put my head in my hands to prevent any movement of my head. The light was hovering. 
The light being omitted was a pinkish yellow/orange. 
I believe I noticed a ramp that was a faint red glow. 
As I was watching this I noticed headlights coming towards it’s direction, I was very excited to see if whom ever was driving would see what I was seeing. I don’t know if they did or not, they kept driving and the way the road goes they had to turn away from it as they were going to see it. 
So I kept looking, I was feeling very scared when all of a sudden it seemed to come towards me. Not that it was actually coming towards me but like whom ever was controlling it wasn’t doing a very good job. 
I walked to another room to see if I could see it better. I saw the same thing. When I went back into the living room it was gone. 
I believe this lasted a few minutes but I was so frightened I’m just not sure.
