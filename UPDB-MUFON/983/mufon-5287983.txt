While Fishing, we observed a bright star-like object both hovering and moving in erratic patterns at a very high rate of speed.

On Thursday 05/13/2021 I was fishing at Ortley Beach with my girlfriend when I first noticed what looked like an extra bright star moving in a strange erratic motion. It would be hovering over the ocean and periodically zip all the way to the northeast, then the west behind us and back to the east over the ocean. It would be still for a minute or two and then repeat the pattern. About 20 minutes or so later the object seemed to descend rapidly. It was much larger and closer to us and began moving in a different erratic pattern. It would shoot up further into the sky and come back down every few minutes. There were a couple of airplanes flying at a lower altitude and it was obvious that whatever I was looking at was not an airplane or satellite. No jet or plane could come close to behaving this way. I pointed it out to my girlfriend who became concerned and wanted to leave but we stayed. There was another couple walking by and I pointed it out to the man and asked what he thought that could be. The couple were both just as confused and excited as I was. None of us had ever saw anything like it. I tried to take a cell phone video of it but it was very hard to make out in night sky. About 40-45 minutes after first observing the object it appeared to move to the southwest at an upward angle at the same high rate of speed until we couldn’t see it anymore. We stayed for another 20 minutes looking at the sky but didn’t see anything else strange.
