{jpg} Dozens ascending upward in a linear path

I was outside on my deck porch that faces North. I noticed dozens of star like objects spaced apart moving from North to East at first in a small distance then disappearing. Called my daughter to see. Then she pointed out they were coming from the lowest elevation I could see heading upwards from about NNW to N. They appeared to change path and head South until they vanished and I Could not see it's destination. They were silent and seemed to be very very high in the sky. I do have pictures on my phone. If I zoom in on the pictures on the phone, they can be seen.
