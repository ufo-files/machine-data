I WAS 8 YRS OLD AT THE TIME. MY OLDER BROTHER, LITTLE SISTER AND MOM ALL SAW THE SAME THING. AROUND 9PM, IT WAS DARK, WE WERE DRIVING IN OUR CAR AND WENT TO MAKE A TURN AROUND AT THE END OF THE STREET, LIKE WE DO OFTEN. HOWEVER THIS NIGHT AS I LOOKED OUT THE WINDOW, RIGHT ABOVE THE TREE TOPS THERE WAS A LARGE dark OBJECT Hovering over a tree and house. I SAID TO MY MOM WHAT IS THAT? SHE SAID "YOU SEE IT TOO" THEN MY SIBLINGS AGREED THEY SAW IT TOO. MY MOM PULLED UNDERNEATH THE TREE, THERE WAS A SPOT LIGHT/BEAM SHINING DOWN, MOVING AROUND, AS IF IT WAS LOOKING FOR SOMETHING.IT MADE NO SOUND. AFTER A FEW MINUTES MY MOM STARTED DRIVING BACK UP THE ROAD. AT THIS POINT THE LIGHT WENT AWAY AND IT STARTED FOLLOWING US UP THE STREET. IT WAS BEHIND A ROW OF HOUSES BUT WE COULD SEE IT IN THE GAPS, SLOWING FOLLOWING US UP THE STREET. WE MADE A TURN BEHIND A CHURCH AND BY THE TIME WE CAME AROUND THE OTHER SIDE IT HAD DISAPPEARED. DURING THE EXPERIENCE WE ALL FELT A SENSE OF CURIOSITY AND CALMNESS.
