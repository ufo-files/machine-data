{cak} 3 orbs in triangle pattern

I was visting my daughter and son in law at Fort Meade Md. I stepped out side to light a cigar. I was just standing there looking around. I noticed 3 white orbs in a perfect triangle, at an unknown altitude or speed. I opened the door and told my son in law to step out. After a minuter of trying to decide what they were we decided to film on phone. In the 5 minutes or so we observed them, they never broke formation, changed direction or speed. Having no clue of altitude, no way to determine speed. But, again, they never changed direction speed or formation
