{jpg} Solid white/yellowish light crossed over house then went dark with no lights

At 10:10 Pm I saw an "orb" or solid light moving similiar to an aircraft from West to East crossing over my neighbors house. It was on a straightline track with no other lights visible. Color seemed to be white slightly yellow.
what was odd was it had no navigation lights or position lights as all aircraft do. No flashing lights whatsoever.

As it crossed over my neighbors house it went dark and nothing else was lef to see. It dissapeared in the air and I could not see it after that time. Speed was maybe 150-200 knots similiar to an aircraft at 5000-10000 feet. From when I first saw it the total duration of sighting was only 3 minutes before it went dark. There was no sound at all. The main reason for my report is this was not a common aircraft due to no position lights or blinking lights at all, also it had no sound at all which should have been heard at the altitude it appeared to be.
It did not seem like a satellite since it was much brighter and lower than any satellite could be. My initial impression was it was an airplane but then with no blinking lights and no sound that does not add up.
