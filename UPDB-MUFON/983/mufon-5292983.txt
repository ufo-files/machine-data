Massive UFO.

Just saw a UFO! This thing was massive! With what looked like 1 huge extremely bright red light on either end blinking slowly and alternating. There was absolutely no noise. It appeared in the sky and slowly moved along until I lost sight of it behind some trees.
I was outside my flat In Ferndown when I saw it appear from out of nowhere (no clouds around) and slowly hovered in a straight line from south-east towards east. 
It looked like it could have been the size of 3 or maybe even 4 AN225.
I ran upstairs and got my mum to come down and see it too. We both watched it until losing sight behind the trees.
