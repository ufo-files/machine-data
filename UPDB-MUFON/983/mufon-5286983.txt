Bright flash in sky, not star, starlink, or satellite

I know this video contains starlink, but this report is not about starlink. For about four month’s my husband and I (and occasionally friends and neighbors) have noticed random flashes in the sky all above Salt Lake City, while sitting in our hottub at night. We just so happened to catch one of the flashes on video while filming starlink this night. We didn’t notice the flash while filming, but realized we caught the flash afterwards while reviewing the footage. Once noticing that we caught the flash on tape, I got on to the local Facebook page and another person in the salt lake valley also filmed starlink and when watching their footage I noticed that they also caught the flash on video from a different perspective as they were in a different part of the valley. We have two clips, ours is slightly out of focus as we were trying to zoom in, but you can see the flash off to the right side, very clearly not a part of starlink. The other clip is the video off the community Facebook page where starlink is very light and barely visible, but the flash is bright and off to the left side.
