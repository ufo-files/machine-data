I started to lie down in bed with the curtains open and decided to lay down and look at the stars when I noticed this thing flying high in the sky, as I paid more attention to it, the object got started moving in no way I've seen before. It was making moves like no aircraft made by man had made before. As I now watched this one it changed too and another then appeared and the same thing happened. I felt my heart race for a bit. but I then needed to get my camera so I could get a picture as I came back to get a picture the things had all gone.
