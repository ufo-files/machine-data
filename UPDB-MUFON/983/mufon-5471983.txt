{jpg} Eye shaped glowing object that disappeared

I was up early for work and at approximately 3:45am I was looking to the west/northwest and saw a eye shaped object that had a dull/faded blue light in the center and a white outline semi glow traveling at a great speed in a straight line towards the North. Before I could even relay what I saw to my coworker a thin white line opened up and the object disappeared into it. Both the object and white line vanished simultaneously. Never thought in my life I would see something so amazing. Will never forget that morning the rest of my life. Every time i go to work that early,I1m always looking to that point in the sky.
