{nd} Bright light that moved snakelike and then faded away

With my dad looking at stars. He said he can see Mars. I look at the bright redish star-like light and say no way that's Mars. We eventually realize it is moving. At first we think it is a plane, but a plane was right next to it with its blinking lights. The object then flew at a steady quick rate much faster than the planes and would swerve in a snake like pattern. It then faded once away from us. Not sure what it was, but definitely not a plane or satellite
