it was late at night in late summer in 1996, after I got off from work. At that time I got off work at 9pm , I would walk home which usually took about 25 - 30 minutes. However on this night it was as if I had awoken in my home , and the entire area around me was completely bath in in white light. I went outside to see where the light was coming from that was when I saw it was coming from above my trailer , it seemed to hover for a few minutes then it slowly went upwards into the night sky. it was a few days later that I noticed there where three or four oval shaped marks on my chest , a lot of people thought I had had heart surgery but I assure you I have never had an kind of heart condition or surgery to this date. also there is only two of these marks left on my chest. I still remember this to this date as if it were yesterday.
