(GS) 3 white objects flying close in the sky. 2 of the objects got so close to each other I thought they were going to crash.

On Saturday, January 19, 2013, I was driving north on Interstate 83 in Pennsylvania on my way to York, PA. At 10:36am I was around the Shrewsbury area when I noticed 3 white objects in the sky to my left. At first glance I thought they were birds by the way they were flying so close together in a row. I looked again and realized that they werent birds but some kind of aircraft. I kept watching them because they just looked kinda odd. No flashing lights or anything. As I was watching them, I noticed that the first 2 objects were getting extremely close to each other while the 3rd lagged behind slightly. All I kept thinking to myself was that they were flying too close together to be airplanes and I also thought they were going to crash into each other! My 8-year-old daughter was in the back seat and could also see them. She kept asking me what they were and I just told her that Im not sure. I got out my phone & tried taking 2 pictures of them, which didn't come out that great. We watched them go from West to East, crossing over I-83. As they were traveling East, some trees got in our way on the right. When we got to another big opening, the objects were gone. They were no where in sight. We watched them for about 10 to 12 seconds before we lost sight of them. I have absolutely no clue as to what we saw!
