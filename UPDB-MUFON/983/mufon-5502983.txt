{nd} A group of orbs moving across the sky in apparent formation

We were out walking the dogs. I saw a dense group of what I thought were stars until I realized how many there were clustered together and how quickly they were moving. They were pulsating a little and moving in some sort of formation (like a worm) across the sky. Eventually they moved so far east I couldn't see them.
