{cak} One bright object and another dimmer object that appeared to orbit the other.

I live in the Denver, CO area and was outside eating lunch on my patio around noon when I saw a bright object and a dimmer object move across the sky from the NE to the SE. The dimmer object appeared to orbit the brighter object. I thought it may be a passenger jet at first, however it appeared very high and wasn't giving off contrails like jets typically do that fly over the area. The objects moved slow and steady across the sky and appeared blurry and looked like they were reflecting white light from the sun. Since I thought they were strange I recorded them for a few minutes. They were very unusual to me, and I've never seen anything like that before.
