td}Noticed triangle then watched as it flattened & two lights flew away

I was having a cigarette in my backyard at around 730 at night on 2/2/18. What caught my attention was a perfect triangle, with two bright lights, one as the top and the second on the right side as u look at it, & the third, a much dimmer light on the left corner. It just stood out to me & before I had a chance to look away the triangle flattened out. The two brighter lights seemed to move into a parallel position with the star from the left corner of the triangle. The two lights kept their position as they appeared to start moving. I turned off my porchlight once I noticed the movement because the lights were dimming as they got further away, making them harder to distinguish. I had actually thought that the light(star) that remains was a plane originally, until it became clear that it was the only star in the bunch. The two lights in question did seem brighter than even the brightest star in the sky.  I was shocked and amazed. I woul say it lasted less than a minute. I didn't finish my smoke, I put it out and got my wife and brought her outside to explain what I saw.
