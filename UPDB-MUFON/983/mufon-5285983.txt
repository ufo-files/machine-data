Witnessed a bright red/pinkish, star-like orb over downtown Rochester, MN moving slowly from NW to SE and then it slowly faded away..

I was on my way to the gas station when I noticed what I've seen many times before, but never in Rochester. It was one of the orbs that often see when going to Treasure Island Casino along county road 6. This one didn't change colors like the others one often do. This orb was bright red in the middle and pinkish towards the outside. As soon as I saw it, I knew what it was, so I stopped and watched it until it faded away. The red color seemed to be emanating from within this orb as it moved slowly from NW to SE. It wasn't a straight line, it kind of wobbled and meandered its way across the sky. After about a minute of watching it, it changed direction and started going to straight east. It slowly faded away until all I could see was a black dot moving in the same direction and that too disappeared a couple of seconds later.

I wish I had a camera that would be able to capture these orbs that see quite regularly. However, I am too broke to afford anything nice.

I can't be the only person in this area noticing these things. I encourage anybody else that has seen these things to document what you see and share your experiences in the comment section or file a report with MUFON.
