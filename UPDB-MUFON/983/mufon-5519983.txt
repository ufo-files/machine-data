A dark red orb with a blue aura. Both colors seemed to pulsate and glow.

At about 8pm I got off the city bus on Thomas and 19th Avenue and I saw people standing around looking up at the sky towards the south. I looked up and saw a red orb with a bright blue aura or "glow" around it. 

It flew in a very odd pattern, with no real direction. Sometimes itd go right, then left, then lower, then higher, sometimes itd just stop.

After a while two helicopters flew towards it and it appeared to fly low and drop out of view behind South Mountain.

The whole event lasted about 7 minutes for me, but I have no clue how long the other people on the corner witnessed it.
