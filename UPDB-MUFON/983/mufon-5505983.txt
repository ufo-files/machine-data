{kt}Saw profile of large craft passing between clouds.

I was driving north on I-29 in NW Missouri I was just south of the Farris air strip in Faucett, MO. There was low cloud cover throughout the area but bright considering. I observed a large( football field size)craft heading in a SE direction move between two clouds the craft profile is all I saw. It was taller than say a 18 wheeler but it was a very short period of time so I can not be more specific on height. It was moving at roughly 60 mph maybe a bit more but less than 120 mph. It had no visible wings or slopes to the structure.It passed back into full cloud cover before I could even slow down to get a better look. I was dumbfounded and later dismissed it as nothing until I heard about another report in neighboring Illinois that appears to have taken place half a day later. So I thought I would share this little bit of info in case anybody else dismissed it as a trick of the eye.
