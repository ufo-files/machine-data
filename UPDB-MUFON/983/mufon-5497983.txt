It looked translucent - it emitted a triangle light from it & then the light started to disapate through the centre & the craft slowly flew away

We three were driving through rural Pyes Pa. 
I was driving as we came upon the crest of a hill the eye caught alight in the sky. 
We pulled into a side road, through the treeline we could see it. We tried to capture it on camera but were unsuccessful.
It was translucent or looked fuzzy, it was to my eye the shape of a builders square with the 'butt end' upright, we could see it was building a light structure from its 'butt end' one thick panel at a time, similar motion as if a person put one hand in front of the other and so on and so forth.
The craft built one panel of light again and again until a large white light in the shape of a triangle was completed, the light seemed you 'scan' in its 'motion' for a brief moment and then again from the butt end again a new 'empty line' of nothing began to come from the craft horizontally through the centre of the light triangle, the empty line grew larger and larger in thickness until the light triangle was gone by the empty line of nothing.

The craft then slowly moved on at which point it looked like a small star flying across the sky. It went on in a East-North-East direction.
