In digital photo taken at Grand Canyon a green orb darted in changing directions

A friend, listed as the contact above, took two digital photos at Grand Canyon with the 2 sec live feature. When the photo opens on the Apple phone the green orb darts quickly in multiple directions. She took a second photo and the same thing happened with minor changes to the position of the orb and its flight path. A family group was present and no one could see the orb; it only appeared in the photograph. I am a member of MUFON and asked her permission to submit this report; to which she agreed with the understanding that a MUFON investigator would call to get a full report and that she may have to send the photos to a MUFON cell phone to activate the 2 sec live feature.
