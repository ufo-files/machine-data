Very large ( compared to the space station ) brighter and faster heading NE towards the coast and seemed to be descending.

At 7am this morning while daylight was just breaking, my self and my partner both witnessed a very bright object which appeared to be descending towards the North East towards the coast. It just suddenly appeared in the middle of the sky and it's shape resembled the space station, which we see regularly. However, It was many times larger than the space station, faster and much brighter. There was no sound. After a few seconds the object 'merged' into a soft inverted triangular shape and then into a very bright globe shape. It seemed to remain still whilst this happened. The brightness softened as it moved away more slowly diagonally descending behind a mountain towards the coast. During this time a cloud gradually formed around the object and by the time it disappeared from view it looked just like a small cloud, except that it was moving. Can I just not that this was very much lower in the sky than the space station, which we have both seen many times. I did have the foresight to attempt to video this on my camera. The video did not register and the only audio is our voices. Also the cloud that formed appeared to be in front of the object with the object towards the rear of the cloud.
