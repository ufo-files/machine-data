Orange Blimp

MY Husband and I were standing on our deck and we saw a flash of orange.
I asked my husband if I was imaging it or not, he said NO i can see it too
it was changing color from orange to red, then gold to a white light. It was moving around erractily. We continured to watch it for five minutes then it started to fade to a white star like shape. We live on the second floor of an apartment building. Near the kennebec river. I was feeling scared because this is not the frist time I saw one. and yet fascinated, I wish it had of been closer because I don't know the definate shape. The color was what made us frist notice it and that it was moving around strangely. It stayed stationary for a little bit like 2 min. then wiggle around.... up down left to and right.. then the light was fading in and out.
