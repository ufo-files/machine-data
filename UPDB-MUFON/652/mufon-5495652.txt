I WAS OUTSIDE MY MOTHERS HOME AT 19:20 HRS. USING A LONG-RANGE BUG SPRAY TO KILL SPIDERS ALONG THE GUTTER OF MY MOM'S HOME. AS I SPRAYED, I NOTICED THAT SLIGHTLY ABOVE MY SIGHT PATH, A VERY LARGE DARK COLORED CRAFT WITH A TRIANGLE SHAPE, HAVING RED, ORANGE AND YELLOW LIGHTS ON IT'S PERIMETER MOVING AT VERY SLOW RATE ABOVE THE HOMES (APPROXIMATELY 100 YARDS OR LESS)
NEAR MY MOM'S HOUSE.

MY FIRST THOUGHT WAS, WHAT THE HELL IS THIS? IT WAS SO CLOSE THAT I FIRST THOUGHT IT WAS A VERY LARGE REMOTE CONTROL TOY. THEN I THOUGHT IT WAS TOO BIG TO BE A RC TOY, MADE NO AUDIBLE SOUNDS AND IT'S MOVEMENT WAS TOO SMOOTH AND LEVEL IN IT'S MOVEMENT TO BE RC TOY. I ACTUALLY OWN SEVERAL RC DRONES AND HELICOPTERS.

THE CRAFT WAS MOVING FORWARD VERY SLOW, THEN IT STOPPED, AND MOVED SLOWLY IN REVERSE, THEN THE REAR OF THIS TRIANGLE SHAPED CRAFT APPEARED BOBB UP AND DOWN 2 TO 3 TIMES (AS IF WERE ON WATER). THE CRAFT THEN SLOWLY MOVED FORWARD AGAIN, STOPPED ONCE MORE, THEN REPEATED THE BOUNCING ACTION. IT SEEMED AS THOUGH, WHOEVER WAS IN THE CRAFT, WASN'T FAMILIAR WITH THE CONTROLS.

THE SIZE OF THE CRAFT, FROM MY VIEW POINT, THE ENTIRE OBSERVATION LASTED NO MORE THAN 2-3 MINUTES.
