Was hovering over village then went north very slowly

I was at my front door having a fag (smoke) with my wife when I tuned to my right to go inside I saw red bright lights (two) and were blinking very slowly. I first though it was a plane but it just stayed there for 10 minutes then slowly moved in sequence , north bound.
I though this to be very unormal and was very interested to see what it would do I was going to get my phone to record it but by then the phone would not see it, I pointed to my wife and she could see them move off. They moved north over houses then I lost sight.
