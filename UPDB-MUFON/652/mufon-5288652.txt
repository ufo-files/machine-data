Wormhole shape tunnel

At 6:04 am Elon Musk rocket launched during a “new moon” cloud-free sky w/o winds. At 6:20am on March 14, 2021, my neighbor and I with my dog were walking due East (in line with Cape Canaveral, FL (as a crow flies)!
My home is perhaps 75 miles from Cape Canaveral due east!
My neighbor had shown me where 20+ minutes earlier she observed a perfect Musk Rocket launch in a clear sky. From that standpoint the rocket was pin needle sized.
We began our walk but returned to my home for a moment. She stood at driveway edge with my dog. As I walked only moments later to her I was shocked by the most intense blue triangle shape and I snapped iPhone 6 pic although my phone was not set for dark.
As we walked about 700’ down my street I continued snapping pics. As we reached a point away from trees we watched the rings and elongation transpire over a couple minutes. 
We were not only ones to report and I added a third person report from news indicating it is gasses from rocket. 
You decide. But I notice in the news report pic a weird green light object above the tunnel or wormhole shape; and, on far top left of one of my pics a sharp yellow dot, and a couple small white dots in tunnel center that show, and then don’t...
Perplexed that the gasses occurred over Winter Park, FL...
