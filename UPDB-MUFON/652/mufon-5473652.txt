{rjl} saw the craft below me, I was parked in a turnout and it appeared below me

I was working as a game warden in San Bernardino Mountains. At approx 02:00 hours I saw a light from an area called hunters flat,near Camp Angles,thinking it may be a spotlighter I pulled over rolled down the window and attached my spotting scope. As I looked at the light it went out over the canyon and at that point I knew it was not a spotlighter. At that time the larger craft appeared, football shaped with a line at the back, attached to the line were several ships like the one I was watching, at that point the smaller craft attached to the line and the lights in the large craft came on. I could see 5 or 6 objects standing in the craft looking out the window, they appeared very human like, as I said I was looking through a 20 power spotting scope. I did not see any type of uniform or clothing and am not sure just what they were wearing. The craft was a dull copper or orange in color. It started moving down the canyon at a slow speed, when it came to Mill Creek it sped up turned right and left at a very high speed. 

I should mention that the windows were in the middle of the craft, center, and toward the rear. I started to call it in to my dispatch, but thought !nope! don't need the heat, I have relayed this sighting to friends and the wife and was encouraged to report it.
