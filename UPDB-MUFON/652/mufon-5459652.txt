We were hunting and missed a big buck but thought we hit. It was rainy drizzle borking cloud. It was all most dark so we went to get our dogs to see if they could help find it if wounded, by the time we gotten back it was dark. There was a small draw we were checking I took the top and my partner to the bottom. I happened to look up and saw 3 or 4 drating light going everywhere in all direction changing course sharply. I screamed at my friend to get up the hill in a hurry. When he got there we watched about a minute to thirty seconds hard to tell. Then the clouds cleared a little bit and we saw a huge craft seemed as big as a battleship and realized it was a mother to the little ones. After a while it gotten in my head it was one of ours, not sure but yeasr latter I still think it had to be one of ours.
