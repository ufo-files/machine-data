{jpg} Round whit fuzzy ball.

I was sitting on my bench with my two dogs. I have binoculars and was observing aircraft. I live 50 miles east of the Columbus airport, in the country. I went out around 3pm and had seen 7 commercial jets and 2 small planes by 3:30. Even though it was a perfectly clear blue sky, it is sometimes hard to spot high flying jets especially without a vapor trail. I looked up and spotted one going directly west, very high, it was just to the east of me. I grabbed the binoculars to look at it when I saw this bright white fuzzy ball. It was 300ft or so high, traveling 50mph? S.W. in a straight line. At first I though it was a piece if material that had got caught in an up draft and was blown by the wind, But it had a steady speed and was moving parallel to the ground at a steady speed, and in a perfect line. It was not very windy yesterday, definately not enought to propel an object that fast. I did try to see it through the binoculars, it still in the few seconds I saw it, it looked round and fuzzy edged. Very unusual!
