I was doing a location scout for a music video shoot in the high Mesa wilderness areas of the dessert outside Joshua Tree and shot some pictures and took some slow motion video. Upon later review I noticed that there seemed to be objects flying through the frame, originating in the distance beyond the canyon ridges and flying over the top of my shot at a high rate of speed. At first I dismissed it as insects but then I realized I had been filming at 240 FPS and also saw no bugs in any of the other clips. I also noticed in the 1Live Photo1 taken just before the clip in question, a similar object (oval, metallic, reflective) can be seen traveling from behind the canyon ridge from left to right at an extremely high rate of speed.
