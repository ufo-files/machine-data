{cak} observed 3 disc type objects,one disappearing as it went straight after changing direction

I was talking on the phone,looking out at the soon-to-be sunset through a window. I noticed what I thought to be a plane with a vapor trail,but the object did not seem to be a plane,for it moved painfully slow and the object seemed to be one solid body;not a plane with a vapor trail. As I observed this object,I called some friends in the house to come look out at this object. The group of friends were quite perplexed, and about a minute or two went by and I observed another identical object without telling them, as my friends had gone back to watching t.v. As the second object went by, I began to feel fear in the pit of my stomach. The second object moved very much like the first.Then, a couple of minutes went by and a third object,moving vertical( the first two were moving horizontal) and very slow,changing direction abruptly,eventually. This direction change is impossible with my understanding of conventional aircraft,and the third object appeared more obvious as a disc than the first two,but seeing these objects in succession leads me to believe that they were all from the same origin or same shape. As the last observed object was within my perception,I called to one of my friends again and he and I saw this object fade and disappear, unlike the first two. We were watching this third object and it just vanished!! I felt a bit (and still) sick to my stomach about this sighting.
