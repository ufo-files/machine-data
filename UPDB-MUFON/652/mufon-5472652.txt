It was moving and just disappeared. I told my best friend to look up and see for herself. The light reappeared and my best friend and god daughter started watching it too. The light got really bright all of a sudden and disappeared agin. We all asked each other if we saw it and I lughed and couldn't believe what we had just seen. At the same time my best friend looked over the back fence and there was another object traveling under the power lines. My best friend said look at that what is that thing? It was about four feet high and in four layers on top of itself. It flew pretty close to us and went along the power lines and flew on by. We tried to get a pic of it but our cell phones wouldn't focus enough to capture it. It disappeared about the same time the light above us in the sky did.
