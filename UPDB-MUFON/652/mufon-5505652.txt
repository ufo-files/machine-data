{rjl} Numerous purple lights on tree limbs & garage. Not holiday lights.

On 31 DECEMBER 2013, at approx. 0120 hours, the following occurred. While walking my dog on the street in front of my residence, I observed numerous purple colored round lighted objects in a neighbors tree. The lights emitted more of a fuzzy aura than a glow. I observed a single matching single light on another neighbors garage, some 30 feet away from the others. I wear corrective lenses, so the first thing I did was look away and then back again. The lighted objects were still there. Thinking my rods and cones may not yet be acclimated to the darkness, I walked my dog about 80 feet down the street, watching the objects the entire time. These objects did not flash, change color, or move in any way. Nor did any sound emanate from them that I could hear. I swept them with the beam of a flashlight and there was no reaction. I continued watching them for another 4-5 minutes before returning inside my residence.

As a 62 year old retired police detective, I am not prone to flights of fancy. What I saw were not Christmas lights. The tree they were in had shed its leaves and there were no wires connecting these things to an unseen power source. The lights have not returned as of this writing.

Although these lights were not flying, I find them sufficiently unidentifiable to warrant making this notification to you.
