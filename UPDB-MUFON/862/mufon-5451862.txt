Black dot hovering in clouds during thunderstorm

I was lying in my back yard relaxing looking up at the sky and I noticed a very small pin prick black dot object high in the sky maybe 800 1000 feet up NNW while a thunderstorm was rolling in. This made absolutely no sense as no one would be flying in this weather and the object was stationary and not moving at all. We live next to an airport so I am well aware of traffic patterns and aircraft and I have flown airplanes and helicopters and this was neither. Lightning was going off sometimes close to it and all over the sky. The wind was blowing at least 10 miles an hour towards me and again the object was not Moving at all as I was trying to debunk it as a floating balloon. A balloon would be traveling at a high speed and moving with this weather and getting bigger if it moves toward me with the wind or smaller and higher if it was rising. I got my wife out back to look at it, pointed at the references and described it but she was unable to see it. The object was so small my iPhone couldn’t capture it so the video is mostly useless but I’m wondering if anyone else in the area saw it. I stood staring at it, and only lost it once the cloud cover got too dark and obscured it. I recorded the Exact direction and local winds at the airport. I tried other vantage points but the storm was too thick and it got too dark. Thanks
