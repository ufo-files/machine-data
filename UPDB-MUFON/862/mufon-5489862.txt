{jpg} Shape of triangle with white lights in each corner and a red light in center, no noise and didn't move

As I was driving west on Midlothian Blvd at about 5:40am I noticed a light above the entrance ramp to the freeway. From a distance it looked like a helicopter but as I drove closer I could see the lights better. The lights formed a triangle shape with a white light in each corner and one in the middle and a red light right below the middle light. It was stationary at about the height a helicopter would fly but it was close enough to me that I would definitely be able to hear it as a helicopter. It made no noise and as I drove past it never moved it stayed completely stationary until I made it down the road and it was out of my line of sight. I am familiar with this route as I take it to work everyday and I have never seen anything quite like it before. A coworker lives in the neighborhood in the distance behind the spot where I saw it and he stated that he heard a loud crash at about 3-330 am and he went outside and sat on his back porch and saw the lights about the same time I did.
