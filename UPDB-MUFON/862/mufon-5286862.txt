The Planet Ve

I’m a amateur astronomer from Texas and I’ve seen all the planets Mercury to Saturn I’ve even seen Andromeda through a 20-60x60mm Simmons spotting scope, and Venus is my favorite planet and woke up at 4am in the summer one April morning of 2020 to see the planet Venus. I’ve seen the moons of Jupiter before little dots round the huge planet, when I zoomed all the way into Venus I saw what I know aren’t two “moons” round the planet Venus. They were about the same size as such moons but the same size as the moons of Jupiter and the same brightness the longer I examined these false moons I noticed they were the shape of an oval with a cut out the top like a round V. Two of them . They were right next to each other and one of them moved away in a slow steady speed it scared me I couldn’t believe what I was seeing . I’ve never seen anything like it since round Venus. I wonder what they were doing there and where they came from.
