black hovering shapeshifting object daytime sighting

I went for a walk down my street and had just been looking up in the sky in the direction of West and all over the mostly clear sky, when I looked back again westwardly and saw a black object in the sky which at first I thought was a big bird. But when the object remained stationary, hovering and "shapeshifting" or maybe wobbling, I knew it was not a bird. It was not a plane, nor a helicopter, nor a balloon. There was no noise. At one point, during a duration of around 4 minutes, the sun reflected off the right side. It's shape at first was round, but then it was an odd shape. When it took off in a north west direction, it became flat, like a disc. It did not move off rapidly, but it didn't take long to be out of sight. I tried to take a picture with my phone camera, but I mistakenly forgot to save the shot. Don't know if I even got the object in my view finder. I believed it to be a UFO as I watched it with one other person. I was excited to have seen it, as I have seen two other UFO's in my lifetime, one with this same person.
