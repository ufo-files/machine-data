{nd} looked at first like a satellite, but then curved more NW, speeded up then another joined it

I just stepped outside to look at the stars, since its pretty night, with frogs singing, and noticed what I thought was a satellite moving north, which sped up and turned toward the northwest and while doing so, another point of light came in from the east, and then flew along with the first point of light to the W/NW while accelerating, and then disappearing in the distance. Ive watched hundreds of satellites, and these were not satellites, because they don't speed up or turn or change direction. They were also too high and too fast to be conventional planes.
