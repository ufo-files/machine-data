{rjl} Rare Aurora Borealis

I made my way out of work & drove to Target store, after I parked started walking & my co-worker & I both saw by the contrails a HUGE spot of aurora borealis super bright colors to the left side of the sun but quite far away from it. We appreciated it for about almost 2 minutes, I did not have my phone with me so went back to the car to get it & snap a photo and the event was gone, leaving the huge spot of gray cloud formation of where it was. At the time about 5 contrail planes were spraying in different directions. It was amazing, never have I experienced that aurora event in person, specially not in New Jersey!
