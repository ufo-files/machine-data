{rjl} Bright white disc like no noise object that hovered tree tops moving northeast from south west

Walked my dog that evening about 10pm in the Windsor Park area of Cherry Hill. I think it was in 1969 sometime in the summer. Went up the block on Orchid Lane to walk my dog which was odd that he did not usually go for walks that late. As I got to the top of the street, I seen a bright light all around me on the sidewalk. Thought it was the street light that just turned on. There was a light but was incandescent. This object was bright white. So turned around 360 and seen the object hover over the homes and road going north west in between my parents home. Watched it as it moved extreemely slow and with no noise at all. All I could see was on the turret there were square windows that turned slowly or it seemed. Then I ran down to my home say 500-1000 feet. At that point the object was higher in the sky about 2000 feet. I ran in house to call my dad and my brother and sister to come out. They did and watched it. It went in one direction then came back to the same spot. Then did the same in opposite direction. This is when it turned like a gyrocope turning and spinning in one driection than the opposte way to like showoff it seemed. Then the object wagged its tail like a dog (wavered) and slowly moved away from us till it was out of sight. We did not call it in. And was never talked about in the family. I finally had a regression done in 1993 and it seemed like I was taken for a ride. Like to the Pleiades, under the Atlantic ocean where I had sculpted a gold sculpture 6 feet high, and to Venus to another time zone to visit family there.
