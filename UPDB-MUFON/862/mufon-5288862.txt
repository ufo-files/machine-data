Extremely bright, white ball of light, huge, oval/teardrop shape object flew down

I was sitting outside on my porch late at night between 12 am and 2 pm Friday night/Saturday morning on March 26th. I was enjoying the full moon and stargazing for hours. During some time between 12:30 and 2 am, I was staring off into the direction NorthWest at a star just above the treetops, and off to the left of that star a huge extremely bright white ball of light, in the shape of a teardrop/oval shape object fastly flew by at an angle some 360-500 ft away in the sky in the NW direction just above the treetops and descended in a manner that was different from a falling star. From my perspective it was about the size of a golf ball It seemed to be heading down to land near the river. Also, the tail of the teardrop/oval shape was pointed in the direction it was heading, the opposite of what a shooting star would do. Additionally, I had an overwhelming feeling of knowing that this was an otherworldly craft and that they knew I was here. It was beautiful and exciting to see but a few minutes after the sighting I got an uneasy feeling and went inside. I have never seen anything like this before.
