Several anomalies seen in this location

I stayed in this location for around a year and would smoke outside while viewing the night sky. There were several sightings within this time frame. The most odd one would probably have to be seeing a few flashes of a red laser-type grid cover the night sky. There were several orb sightings as well as a couple of nights in a row where I saw a very bright orange sphere close to the surface hovering in a straight line, and going in the same direction the next night around the same time only this time it was a little further out, as if it was observing this location in a line-type pattern. There's no way to know if the occurrence with the lasers are connected to the sightings or not, however before all of this there was a time, right as the sun was setting that I saw a red orb over the mountaintops which a orange orb had split from and they were both hovering around that same area until they faded out. I'm not 100% on the colors but from my memory that's what I believed them to be.
