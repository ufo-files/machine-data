[cak} Multiple clusters moving away from airport and helicopters as if clusters were being observed

Filled out and filed at MUFON headquarters from a paper report (sent in by witness) by MUFON Field Investigator 18809 on 5/16/2019.
I called the witness to get her address and she told me that she saw about 100 clusters moving fast over the mountains toward Big Bear City. She thinks the date was August 30th, 2018 but her sister claims it was November 20th, 2018 {lf}
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I attended a birthday party BBQ outside. I heard helicopters so I looked up to see that they were in pursuit of something.    The 2 helicopters stopped right above the house real quick to observe and followed the clusters well past Big Bears mountains. Until they were out of sight. The clusters contained circular silver objects illuminating light. I was in shock I couldn't believe what I saw so I told my brother to look up. By the time he got his cell phone the cluster of lights went east and out of sight. I initially assumed the airport towers were in communication with the helicopters as the birthday party location is only a couple blocks away from the San Bernardine Airport. The objects in clusters did not look like our normal aircraft and moved incredibly fast. With in about 25 seconds the clusters disappeared.
