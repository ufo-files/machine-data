{nd} 2 then 3 orange orbs appeared

While watching TV I noticed two orange star like objects flying very close to each other when I looked out my bedroom window. I ran downstairs and grabbed my wife and we watched the two lights coming up from the south. The two objects appeared to make a stop then they disappeared. About 30 seconds later they reappeared in the same place (about 2 - 3 miles south) as I initially saw them. The objects had no sound as they flew and they repeated the same flight pattern then disappeared. Again they reappeared and this time there was three of them. It was a very weird experience.
