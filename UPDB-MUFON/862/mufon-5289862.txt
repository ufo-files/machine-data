Brown translucent triangle covers the moon briefly

Clear evening around 7:30pm, taking dogs out. I looked up, I always do, and I saw a large brown shadowy triangle enter the moons corona. Brown and transparent, the same color as our smog! It glided over the moon, covering it and disappeared as it left the corona. Exactly like a shadow would do. No noise, no lights and it lasted only seconds. If I'd had my phone there would have been no time to get a shot of it. I could see the moon through the triangle. There were fluffy white clouds framing the moon that night which made the corona very prominent. It was an unmistakable shadow going across the moon. WTH?
