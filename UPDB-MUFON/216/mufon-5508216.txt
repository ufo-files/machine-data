I was headed home from my brothers apartment. I decided to take back roads, because I thought it be would be a little faster than main roads, less cops. On my way I hit a traffic light that wouldnt turn green for me. After 3 light cycles I turned right and then a U-turn and another right to continue straight. I was blaring my music because I had recently upgraded.

About 5 minutes after the traffic light, I was on Kissel Road. I couldnt remember if there was a stop sign at Hunt-club and Kissel. When I approached the intersection I slowed expecting a stop sign. After I didn't see one I started accelerating again and then a light appeared above. **Very bright light that seemed to hover for a split second. It was already dark with little cloud coverage. The light appeared, like it spawned, then took off heading west with a slight curve in its trajectory (south). It was out of sight just as fast as it appeared.

I have friends that have seen UFOs but I have never seen one until now. Right when I saw it I thought, "thats it." I finally have seen one. I was flustered and got a little teary eyed. Not sure why but I know I didn't say anything. It went off into the sky and foliage surrounded my car once again. But I started to second guess myself, trying to pin other things, but NOTHING moves that fast.

I felt like it was almost destiny for me to have seen it. There was a little window in the tree coverage to have seen it, and i did. The street light and me taking the back roads would have all changed my location.
