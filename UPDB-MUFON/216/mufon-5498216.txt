{dr}UFO sighting on i-85 in Atlanta, GA

I was driving on the i-85 N at about 3am, heading to breakfast with a friend. We were having a conversation; laughing and talking, when suddenly I saw a huge bright white lighted object in the sky traveling in the same direction in which i was driving. It flew forward in a straight line just ahead of us then disappeared! It was a square or slightly rectangular shape and the light was extremely bright. My friend yelled out "Did you see that?!" as her eyes filled with tears and I confirmed that I did. She then asked what it was and if I thought it was a shooting star. She began to pray. I told her this could not have been a shooting star for a few reasons. First, it was far too bright. Second, it was too low in the sky. And last, because they do not move as fast as this object did. What we saw accelerated at what seemed to be the speed of light then disappeared in about 2 seconds. It happened to quickly to capture a picture, however I know what I saw and I'm glad someone else witnessed this intriguing event.
