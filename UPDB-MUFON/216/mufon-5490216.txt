{cak} Witnessed while outside smoking with 2 others. I watched for a hr before going to bed.

While outside smoking with 2 others a bright light was observed from the middle of the Big Dipper. It lasted for 1 second. Flashed on, then disappeared. 5 minutes later, the object was observed North East of us, over Liberty, Missouri. I observed it for about a hour before going to bed. This object changed colors in a sequential pattern. It hoveredmotionless. I noted star movement in relation to it due to the Earth's rotation, but it remained stationary. It was approximately 45 degrees off the horizon. I noted also, 3 shooting stars in the sky, none in that area. I tried to capture video with my phone, but could not find it in my lens/on my screen. I could only capture some still photos. The distance is over 5 miles away.
