My neighbor called me to come outside he was taking his dogs out. He said and I quote " You Gotta come outside and see this Sh*t". It was a black triangle moving east with no sound at a steady slow pace with no sound. I am a Retired AF Technical Sgt and I have been around Aircraft for over 22 years. This was very strange. I had seen the same thing 5 years ago in April except there were 2 of them translucent and could only be seen when the Lightening lit up the sky, a person in Atlanta reported the same thing I saw only 1 1/2 hours after I saw it.
