{tjd} flew across with 3 more objects, in a carousel like motion.

April 1st, 2020, around 10 am. I was looking out my window into the clear blue sky, then a bright, white sphere-like, aspirin sized (to my point of view) object flew across in a fast manner. Since it was very white, I thought it was a bird, but then it stopped mid-air, fixating its position. I jumped to the window, to see if it was really stopped midair without moving and 3 more objects, same shape, size and color, appeared into my view. They came from the same direction as the original object, and position themselves in a straight line, in front of the original object, and also fixated their location, mid-air. So for at least 1 or 2 seconds, you could see 4 objects in a straight line, mid-air. Then, the original object moved in front of them, in a circular motion, and the other 3 objects did the same. They all seemed to be moving in a circular motion, like a carousel. At this point, as they continued to move without stoping the carousel maneuver as I call it, they hid behind a cloud. I grabbed my camera and waited for them to come out of the cloud, but they never did.
