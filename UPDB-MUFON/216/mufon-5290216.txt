Orange Orbs, over 6, in formation

More than six orange orbs flew above the roof of the house opposite- then they flew up to several hundred feet above Brislington where 3 formed the corners of the triangle, just visible in the centre of the image.They flew extremely quickly, and changed direction many times, the nearest appeared as an orange lantern as well as an orb.Started 6pm above Knowle in Bristol then they went up to several hundred feet above Brislington in Bristol.
