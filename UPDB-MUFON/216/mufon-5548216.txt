A silent luminous coloured ball very light green

whilst walking home somthing caught my eye i looked up into a perfectly cloudless night it wasnt quite dark I saw an object round in shape silently moving quite fast from west to east the light was unusuall in that it didn't twinkle but was as if luminous no trail was noted and it moved very smothly the object first occured at my left and moved in a straight line i lost sight as it moved over roof tops its size was about the size of the erazer on the end of a pencil held at arms length.The colour is best described as between the colour of a tennis ball and the luminous paint on a watch face but pale not glowing it was very easy on the eye.
