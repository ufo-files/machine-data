{jpg} Weird orb spotted above family farm disappeared then reappeared with another. Followed but was evasive then disappeared. No clouds.

My mother was actually the first to spot it. She was outside smoking a cigarette, looking out at our farm which was across the road. Suddenly she spotted an odd hovering oval like ball of light that appeared out of nowhere. It had just turned 11 pm and she rushed inside to get me. I walked out into the garage and saw it too. At first I thought it was just a lost airplane but it wasn't moving, and there are no air ports for miles around. Not only that but we live in the middle of nowhere, hours away from any big city. We agreed we should check it out, because it was over private property and we had animals to look out for. As soon as we crossed the road in my mother's car it started to move. We followed it further toward's the back of the farm. Suddenly it disappeared. We stopped and waited. It was odd because we couldn't hear anything, not even crickets or cicadas and it was in the middle of summer. Suddenly it reappeared, but back towards the south. We reversed and followed. It would suddenly descend then ascend. The second time it took us towards the back (aka the North) it disappeared once more. Then suddenly it came back with a second weird white oval. They both hovered before shooting forward a small ways. Then one by one disappeared. We never saw either one again. Our small town has more cows than people, and most are over the age of 50. There's no way it could have been a normal plane or helicopter.
