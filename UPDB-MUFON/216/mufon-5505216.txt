{rjl} Viewed three orange glowing lights through cloud cover in shape of large triangle.

Driving to downtown Edmonds at approximately 7:30PM. As I descended down into the "bowl" of Edmonds (I was positioned on a hill so my view was partially towards the sky), I saw above me a glowing orange light that appeared to be moving very slowly--slower than my vehicle was driving, which was approximately 25-30 MPH. Upon further observation, I noticed two other lights, which together with the first, appeared to be making the shape of large triangle (a light at each point). All points moved together in the same shape. The light emitted looked like lanterns glowing in the sky. I saw didn't hear any noises from within my vehicle. Cloud cover prevented any further visual observation. Estimated elevation was approximately 10K feet. Direction W/NW.
