{cak} Large Sphere in upper Atmosphere Falling to Earth

Witnessed a Large Sphere in upper Atmosphere about the size of my Thumb Finger Nail. It was falling straight down at high speeds. It was Red in colour with some white around the right side edge. I didn't see any signs of a trailing light (like a tail on a meteor). Looking at it, felt like looking into an old style flash light.

It looked like a tiny sun falling straight to earth. I lost it in the tree line in front of my house but it was still really far away.
