{flg} Dull grey object 500-100 feet over house

My dogs kept barking erratically. I let them outside to use the restroom when they continued to bark. I looked around and noticed a grey colored oval object moving over my house at slow rate of speed 30 mph or less. There was not any wind and very little cloud cover.
