White flashing light from orange satellite sized sphere, appearing to break up visually speratically towards end of sighting

I was with my Dad at his house standing in the backyard enjoying a couple of beers looking at the night sky, when we both happened to witness what looked like an orange coloured satellite shaped object with a steady white blinking light around every 5 seconds or so.

It carried on in a north easterly direction for approximately 15 to 20 seconds before what appeared to be fragmenting or breaking up of the light visually before completely disappearing all together from sight.

We watched 2 satellites go over after the event, and are positive it was no aircraft or satellite like we had ever seen.

We both looked at each other and agreed that was rather strange, and not like anything we had ever witness.
