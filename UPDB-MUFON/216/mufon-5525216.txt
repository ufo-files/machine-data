I saw it and snapped a picture

I was pulling into the hospital parking lot and behind the hospital there is a river with a hill. I look up and i see this giant flying disk and i had no idea what it was at first but then i realized what it was. there was no doubt in my mind that what i saw was a definite u.f.o. This thing was at least the size of a football stadium. It was circular and it was black and grey and the sun was reflecting off of the top from what i could see. It had no shadow and it made no noise. The first time i saw it. It rose up from behind the hill and hovered there for about ten seconds then it shot up in the sky like a bullet and vanished. when i saw the thing i was in shock and i was very exited. I was driving home and i saw it the second time and it was doing the same thing but it was further away and that's when i snapped the picture.
