three straight light trails

I photographed strange three light trails from the balcony of ocean view hotel directly in front of Pacific Ocean (East coast of Chiba, which is about 2 hour's train-ride from Tokyo).

I showed the picture to the professional camera person as well as maker of camera (smartphone), and they could not figure out what it is. I learned that it is not a smear or blooming effect of the lens, and not a motion blurr of insect because photo was not taken by slow-shutters. 

Please see attached photos. First one is comparison of original photo (left) and exposure-enhanced one.

I took 8 more pictures but nothing strange was taken in other ones.

And to my surprise, when I extended these three lines, all of the crossed at one point where existed (at the time of appearance) a star called Formalhaut
