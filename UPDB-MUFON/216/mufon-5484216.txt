This was given as a status update by one of my friends: 
"Few minutes after my status update on earthquake some time ago, I was looking outside from my friend's window at Shillong for half an hour at the far-away massive lightning and the wild winds in action. A weird day with earthquake, cyclone and a total black out here and an unidentified flying object..... I need some break."
