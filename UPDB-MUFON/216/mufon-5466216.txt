Object travelling east to west following a jumbo jet on its edge, descending towards airport

One day in late July,2016... I went out on my balcony which faces north.I live in an area near Pearson airport in Toronto Canada.Air traffic is pretty much constant during the day.There is a Building in front of mine to which when outside, I sometimes watch aircraft coming in on approach ., flying out of view on one end of the building and of course re-appearing on the other end.On this day I was observing a four engine jumbo jet flying past the other end. A flat black opaque disc approx. 30 to 40 feet in diameter was flying directly behind the jet, on its edge.(I approximated the diameter by the size of the tail of the jet., (which is huge) assuming it to be 100 feet tall) I know I'm not too far off. The center of the disc was slowly spinning (like a barn roof vent when looking down on it) opaque and completely silent. The object mimicked every descending move of the jet until they both went out of sight past some trees in the foreground. The object stayed directly in the center, following behind the aircraft. I was shocked and didn't think for a second to grab my camera. Drones are not 30 or 40 feet in diameter. Just goes to show you, seeing is believing.
