In the late 80’s I was driving home from a class at the local college and something buzarre happened. It was after 9:00 pm and I had stopped at a stop sign on a road I always took to go home. It was on the north side of our town in more of a rural area. When I stopped I remember a dark object covered the sky above me and just hovered above my car. I looked out the windshield to see what had darkened the sky and there was a round object. I watched for a few minutes and it suddenly took off. There were lights on the bottom as it vanished. There was a car behind me so I got out and asked the man in that vehicle if he has seen what I saw. He saw it as well. I’ve never seen this again, but would love to know where that object came from.
