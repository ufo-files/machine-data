{rjl} In Copalis beach WA. White orb and one red orb ejecting 5 large red orbs. Left strange cloud behind. Made impossible movements

We pulled onto the beach late at night around 11:0O pm. We were going to camp for the night as we were heading to Port Angeles to camp with other friends and it was late. Around 1:00 to 1:30 am we saw a light rise over the beach from the north And move straight up in the sky to what looked like 10,000 feet in a few seconds. It left a cloud that seemed phorescent and looked like a DNA helix. Then a vary large red orb (seemed to be at about 1000 feet over the ocean, appeared. They began to move towards each other slowly and the red orb broke in to 5 other identical but smaller pieces. The white one seemed to just fall out of the sky as if someone dropped it and went to the ocean. It fell as fast as it rose, just a few seconds. The red ones slowly blinked out. That was it. About 10 min before this my phone cut off and would not turn back on until about 5 min after it happened and had 70% charge. One of My friends had the same thing happen with his phone, but my other friends phone was already dead for hours.
