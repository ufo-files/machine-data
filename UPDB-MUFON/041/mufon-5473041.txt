Bright orb in the sky

Driving down highway 15 South, southern direction towards US border in Lacolle. Appeared at top of car windshield and crossed visible sky at incredible speed before disappearing on the horizon. Dark sky, no other aerial traffic. Blue-green orb with well defined contour and intense brightness. Surprised, I tried to comprehend the phenomenon by mentally reviewing various possible explanations.
