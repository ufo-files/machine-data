{cak} Saw a Silver object flying about 200 MPH in a straight line North to South

I was cutting firewood on my property when I noticed a silvery object in the sky moving North to South at about 200 MPH and 500 feet above the ground. There was no noise coming from it. My best guess in size is 60 feet long and 30 feet high. Cylinder shaped was seen on 12-2-17 at 4:23 PM. I took photos of it with my cell phone so it's time stamped. I showed the photos to a MUFON Field Investigator and he said we should turn this in to MUFON.
