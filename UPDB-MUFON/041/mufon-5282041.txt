Lights over Winchester KY.

My husband, myself and our two children were on our way home from dinner and shopping when we noticed something hovering over around fox's general store. It had red, green and clear lights. The lights were blinking off and on in time. It hovered there for a bit then once it seemed to notice us it followed us around 2.5 miles. We went around a curve and it was gone. We searched and searched for it. No where to be found. This was at the intersection of KY 89 and KY 974, that is where we first saw it.
