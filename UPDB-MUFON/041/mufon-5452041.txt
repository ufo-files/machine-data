Floating basketball

We had just returned home and let the dog outside my wifey all to me to come outside she pointed up at the sky and the northeastern direction and we both saw a round object about the size of a basketball if you were to hold a basketball in your hand raise it up at an angle that's about the size of the object it hovered for a couple of minutes and then without any sound started to move Southeast and disappeared.
