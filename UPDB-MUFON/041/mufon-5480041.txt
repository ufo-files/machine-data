{jpg} Silver circle changing to oval with grey/white blinking light

I was stopped eastbound at the stop light at Cirque/56th St West. I was the second car in the right hand lane coming from Cirque/56th St W. Out of the corner of my eye I noticed a silver circle flying NE across the street above the AM/PM and continuing SE on above the Nursing home and the apartments on the opposite corner that I was stopped at. At first I thought it was a huge silver balloon but, It flew in a straight path against a breeze we had blowing at the time. the flags at the Texaco stations were flying to the North and the object was moving at a steady pace in a Southward direction. It is also noted that this was a clear, cool; crisp morning at approximately 10:10 am. The object was silver in color and changed from a circle to an oval shape. It was a solid object and you could see an outline, but at the same time could see the object change shape. There were lights that were grayish/white that blinked around the center half of the craft. When I held my finger up to the sky while I was sitting at the stoplight my index finger didn't cover the whole object. I watched it fly for sometime, but the blinking was random, meaning I saw no discerning pattern. When my light changed I made a right turn from Cirque/56th on to South Orchard St. I followed the object for about two miles and I lost of it in traffic. I have included a picture from Google Earth showing the intersection I was at.
