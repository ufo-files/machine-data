{jpg} Triangle ship

In 2018 on a Thursday night In June, about 9:45 I was sitting by our campfire, when I noticed a huge,satin black,thick,triangular, shape, moving slightly, across the sky. It had one huge, red glowing, light on its bottom in the middle of the craft. It was blinking very slowly. And three lights in two towers on the back of the craft. It was also being followed by a smaller ship, I couldn’t tell what it was. It was being dwarfed buy the triangular craft. I would say it was about 3 stores thick, and about two to three miles away from me. I can’t tell for sure how high it was. But I would say it was between 500 to 1000 feet high. I also seen it on the Following Thursday night. I never thought I woul see it again, or I would have had my IPad ready. I’ve also seen bright lights in the sky that seem to shoot straight up and and turn into a pin point and disappear.
