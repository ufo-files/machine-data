{cak} Strange multi-colored light seemed to be spinning and slightly bobbimg in night sky

Looking out of my bedroom window sky watching and observing the planets in the western sky this time of year noticed a light in long distance that should not have been there. Through 20X200 binoculars light was changing colors and appeared to be spinning. The colors were outrageous and the movement which appeared to be bobbing up and down blew me away. I had been keeping watch on this part of the sky because of the planets which as months pass will junction and pass one another. So I know this light was not supposed to be there. The light after watching and filming for some time simply faded away. Has not returned to area since. Another person saw it too and uploaded to You Tube. When ever I am rewarded with a sighting I always feel lucky enough to have been watching for any event in our skies.
