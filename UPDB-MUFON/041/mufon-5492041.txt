{jpg} I Was Standing in the midst of about 100 people, when the object appeared, banked iinto clouds sharply to the right.

I was at Saint Mary's Soup Kitchen amoung about 100 others, it was about fourPM. I was looking about me when I observed a silvery disk with a dome on top come directly out of the clouds. it stopped and hovered for 3 to 5 seconds. I just know that it was aware that I was observering and, then it sharply turned right and banked into the clouds where I could no longer see it. No one said anything and, I also remained silent about the occurrence. I felt that it was appearing to me personally and to this day, still do.
