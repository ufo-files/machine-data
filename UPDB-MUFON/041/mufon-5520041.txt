mechanical hovering rotating craft sighted.

My boyfriend was driving us down the road, when i noticed something hovering in the distance.  He mentioned that he thought it might be a kite, but as we moved closer i noticed that it was also rotating. It was rotating very slowly and hovering directly in front of a building (Baker Tilly on the corner of Lahser and 696) almost at the top. At this point, my boyfriend had nearly avoided an accident i almost caused just trying to get a good look.  And the best way i can describe it is, as two mechanical leaves swirled around eachother with the pointed tips facing down. I am currently working on an illustration since i was unable to get a photograph.
