Walking dog in backyard looked up and saw an object moving wobbly

I had taken my dog out around 8:00pm and while he was in the back yard I had looked up and noticed the big dipper. I was facing South/ east and saw what I thought at first was a star,because that was the shape and color, but it was moving in an easterly direction and like a wobbly line. Not side to side, but wobbly and going pretty quick. Now just prior to and after, I did see planes in the sky, with there different colored blinking lights. That is why I knew this was not an airplane or a helicopter. There was no noise. It moved toward the east until I could no longer see it. Like I stated, when I first saw this I said " NO FRIGGIN WAY" " THAT'S NO STAR" I felt excited a little because like I said earlier, I saw planes in the sky then this object, and this WAS NO PLANE.
