{nd} Odd shape changing direction changing fireball

Sitting at campfire at camp. Supermoon event had us looking at the sky more than normal this weekend. First thought it was a Big fireball shooting star. Bowie shaped fireball in east sky traveling very slowly northbound. Changed shape to circular and changed direction traveling east away from us and disappeared in the distance. Felt bewildered, not knowing what to think.
