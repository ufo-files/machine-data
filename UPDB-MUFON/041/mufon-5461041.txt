{jpg} Observed cuboid object tumbling through sky

I observed a cuboid shaped object tumbling through the sky. It was a very clear night, hot. There was no light being emitted from the object, but it's surfaces were highly reflective, reflecting the ambient light of the world. It was moving about as fast as a commercial jet and rotating end over end in the same direction it was travelling rather slowly. The only reason I was able to see it was because of the light reflecting off of it's faces. It didn't generate light of any kind.
