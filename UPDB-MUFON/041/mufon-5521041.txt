witnessed object 3 times while driving home after dark

driving home after dark, 1 to 1 and a half miles from home towards the West. Noticed bright light in the Western sky thought it was a falling star and realized it was moving and getting larger. It stopped ahead of me and fell down to tree level and hovered with different colored lights. It was cloudy around the object, the lights were bright with lights around the edges blinking from right to left with no pattern. Stopped vehicle and got out to look, called wife, then tried to take picture with cell phone. Approximately 4 mins, then it went to the South and then North East and was gone fast. Got back in vehicle and continued to drive home. Was about a quarter mile or less from home and saw it again in the West again, and was hovering over a field. Called wife again and talked while watching it. Lasted a couple minutes, and it disappeared. Continued driving home, pulled in driveway and as I was coming up driveway over trees behind home. It didn't hover, was moving slowly then streaked away to the North West.
