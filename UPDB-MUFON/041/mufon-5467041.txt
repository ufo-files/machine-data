{cak} Saw and took pictures of unusual patterns of contrails

I was sitting on the patio and noticed unusual cloud and contrail patterns and formations. I went back into the house and took cell phone pictures. 7 months later my wife and I noticed a black object adjacent to or in one of the contrails. There is also a very strange shaped cloud in the midst of the contrails.
