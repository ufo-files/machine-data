Witnessed matte, triangular shaped object with 3 orange lights.

I was on break around midnight, eating dinner with my wife on the tailgate of my truck. Randomly looked up and saw dark triangular shape with three, dull, orange lights slowly passing overhead before it silently accelerated north to south at an unprecedented rate. We looked at each other and couldn’t believe what we saw. Still think about it today. It appeared large but hard to judge as it was night time. The object itself appeared solid only because there was a noticeable lack of starlight inside the shape outlined by the three lights.
