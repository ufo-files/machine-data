Looked at first to be a very bright star then noticed pulsating lights and zigzag movements

I was on the deck of my house and looking at the stars. I noticed a very bright star over my roofline and continued to watch it trying to identify it. I realized that there were lights pulsating on it in red and blue and it was hovering and moving in somewhat of a zigzag motion on and off. I brought my son-in-law outside and he observed what I had and we watched the object for about 10 minutes longer when my 16 year old son (a genius but a real skeptic) joined us. At first, he tried to write it off as some type of star but then saw the pulsating lights and could no longer call it a star. We continued to watch and he noticed the movement and hovering of the object. The object was changing altitudes throughout. We went inside to see if there was anything in the area that may be causing this and when we returned outside a short time later, it was gone.
