{nd} Orb crossed in front of moon

I was traveling south on Lassiter Mill Road near Asheboro, NC at about 10:30 a.m. on Christmas morning 2013. Im sitting in the backseat of my fathers truck traveling south. I looked up in the sky to my right at about two oclock and at that time I seen a silver / whitish orb cross in front of the Moon traveling at a high rate of speed and then all the sudden it just disappeared. It left no constrail whatsoever and was at a very high altitude. There was a jet to the left of it in the sky leaving a contrail. The moon looked to be about the size of aspirin in sky and the orb was the size of a star as it crossed in front of it. The entire incident lasted only about 30 to 45 seconds. That is about as accurate description that I can give.
