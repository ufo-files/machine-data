Stationary line of unwavering lights

When driving home my son and I spotted what at first looked like a tear in the sky. Once home we got out of the car and could see a line of light or lights with no discernable spaces, about 8inches across the sky from our perspective. We had noticed it as we were driving and it didn't move, once we got out to look, I was able to take 1 picture before it appeared to enter into a pocket or something and the lights went off or disappeared down the line.
