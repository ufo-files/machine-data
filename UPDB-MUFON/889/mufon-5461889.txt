{cak} Family experience and viewing of UFOs

Please note: I was about 10 years old at the time. Only my younger brother is still living. He is able to articulate a very similar experience. His dates may be better than mine.  I do remember hearing my parents talking about seeing the event written up in the Oxnard Press Courier the following day. Briefly, these red glowing objects traveled smoothly and silently North to south. We were all in the back yard sitting stop a cement wall with a clear view. Notably, the objects, once in the general location of the See Bee base, hovered in formation apparently over the ocean. They suddenly underwent radical geometric position changes (up, down, side to side) in relation to each other. They then commenced to hovering once again. They simultaneously flew straight up with such rapid acceleration that they left trails of red light. I've look many times for any other mention of this sighting without any luck. This experience profoundly changed my dad: he was in a state of readiness for these crafts to return. He wanted to go with them.
