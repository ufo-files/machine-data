{kt}Observed sphere like object move at high rate of speed in a straight line.

Was on a ridge line in the mountains of CA where I used to live. At around 2-3 o'clock in March of 2008 I seen what looked like the reflection of a car or other vehicle in the distance. After observing this object for about 3-5 minutes it moved into a position where I could see a metallic white spherical shape. This object moved from the coast around Newport/Huntington beach,CA maybe further south. To the bottom of the mountain below Wrightwood,CA at extremely high rate of speed in a straight line. This was instantaneous. After doing this 4-5 times it flew off over the horizon in a instant. After seeing this object I was confused but calm. I didn't know what to think about what i had just seen. I had curiously about this subject but after seeing a object in person I want closure or know if anyone may have seen the same object that I saw that day.
