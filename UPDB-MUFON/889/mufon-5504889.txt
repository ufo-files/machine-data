{cak} A large light travelling north at reasonable speed

Outside of home about to get in car when myself and two other people witnessed a light travelling northbound across the sky of Milton Keynes, England. Our initial thought was star, but it was moving. Our second guess was an aircraft, which we all discounted as there was no sound, neither the common lights observed. As a keen aircraft spotter I quickly discounted this. Next we thought a shooting star, but it was too slow and there was a very dull light coming from the top of this bright red object. We all later agreed we were witnessing a UFO. The light was travelling too slow to be a shooting star or a plane; it was silent as well which discounts any type of aircraft at the proximity I was witnessing it at.
