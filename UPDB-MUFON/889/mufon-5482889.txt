{Cak} glowing, stationary orbs of light

On the 8th of June 2016, I was on my way home from dropping off my son with his grandmother in Suffern, NY. I was between exits 9 and 10 on the New Jersey Turnpike South when a little after 9 PM I saw 3 orbs of light to the west of me hovering over power lines. I was convinced they were either landing lights from planes (since Newark is north of the turnpike and Philadelphia airport south) or lights of an antenna ray but neither explanation worked (I am very familiar with landing lights for planes and these orbs had none). What surprised me most was that they were completely stationary forming a triangle. About a minute later, another orb appeared south of the others forming a kind of kite tail to the other three. There then appeared another light with clear lower landing lights which I am almost certain was a helicopter. As I continued to drive I eventually lost sight of the objects but was able to take 2 poor photographs of them.
