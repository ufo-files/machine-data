String of lights, about 15, some in pairs like some alone (EX: -  -  - -  -  -)    in strait line all same speed, nothing like plane or star

Was looking at sky towards the big dipper N direction almost over my head (about 5:55 AM), saw a light it looked like a satellite but one more appeared followed by a sting of about 15, some alone and some in paires one behind the other and appeared to be attached to each other, all at same speed, same distance between them except for the ones in pairs ( Ex: -   -   -   -  - -   -   -   -  - - and so on) No flashing lights, no noise, simply the color of any bright star, mostly blue. I did not have time to photograph, I wish I did!!!!
