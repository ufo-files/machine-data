{cak} Woke up on a hard table and slightly immobile surrounded by 3 gray like beings.

It was the end of another ordinary day and I went to my bed and fell asleep. I woke up and I was cold and noticed I wasn't on my soft bed anymore. I was on a cold table. The next thing I noticed is a very bright light above me on a silver background. And right after that I noticed 3 gray alien like beings one to my left and right. And another at my feet. I freaked out and immediately tried to move back but realized I could not move. I struggled really hard and was able to slightly move maybe an inch and then things got hazy and I don't remember much after that except waking up again still slightly immobile but I was able to move and speak again and I turned on all my lights and did not sleep for the rest of the night.
