Standing in the park on my break I observed a large, bright white glowing object decend & appear to land at an unknown distance

While standing in Fernhill park on my lunch break, talking on the phone, looking at the sky and stars, which is typical for me when the weather allows, I observed a large, glowing, bright white object appear to fall or decend from the sky in the east. It happened very quickly, about as fast as it takes to watch a shooting star and it reminded me of watching the same, although I know this was no shooting star or meteor, which I have seen plently of. I first noticed the object at about the level of the tree line, it quickly decended out of sight & I had the thought that it had landed somewhere. The object was roughly one and a half to two times the size of the airplanes that were flying nearby. I am unable to accuratly judge the distance that it was from me, but I am assuming it was a few miles or more, considering the size relevant to the nearby airplanes that I am sure were further away than it. I felt amazed and somewhat in shock at seeing this, so much so that I commented to the person I was talking to on the phone "I don't know what I just saw, but I know that wasn't normal" I then proceded to explain the phenomenon I had just witnessed while continuing to watch the sky for another appearance, which was not forthcoming. This occured on the evening of December 23rd between 5:15-5:20pm and I continued to watch the sky until 5:35 when I had to return to work.
