Two solid object UFOs with Auras flew by, disappeared, reappeared, then the next day reappeared

On Aug 8, 2021 at 10:29PM CST, I was sitting by the camp fire with my brother in law. He was facing west and I north. He spotted to large glowing objects moving at a north east direction. They were silent and glowed orange. I turned to take a photo, ran behind the cabin to get closure to take more photos, ran up the steep hill behind our cabins to get a better view. As I reached the top of the hill, both lights turned off. The top of the hill had a 360 degree view. I walked down the hill and 8 minutes later, they reappeared to the north and I took a brief video before the lights turned off again. As I got closer to the objects, I could see they we pancake disc shaped with an orange aura. My photos show this. They glided in the sky maybe 20-30 MPH without any sound. The objects were around 500ft in the air. The next morning at the pool/ lazy river, my sister in law says what's that shiny thing hovering in the sky. It's a shiny metallic disc hovering at cloud level in the same area as the objects the night before. This time it was at cloud level ~2,000 ft. It it was at cloud level, it had to be large given the distance. This was only one object and it hovered for about 15 mins in one spot as the clouds moved by. It just disappeared after a small cloud disappeared. Maybe 30 mins later, there was very loud fighter jets flying around the area for hours. It was either one or two circling for a few hours after. The glowing objects came in direction from Fort McCoy, where my brother in law was stationed years prior. There is also Camp Douglas with an airfield south. These weren't airplanes or helicopters as they were silent and you could see that they were no. Photos and video taken with an Iphone 13 pro. No photos of the second day disc since we were at the pool with no phone.
