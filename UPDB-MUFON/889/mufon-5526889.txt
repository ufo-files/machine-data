Small flying object caught on football practice film

The head football coach for our football team recently showed me a film from practice in 2009 where a small white object flies across the screen. After hearing about it i was skeptical but when i saw the film i was at a loss. The film was from the press box of our football stadium angled down to the south half of the field. During a play a fast white object similar in speed and appearance to a golf ball flies from the right of the screen and angles up to the top left. Obviuosly we would notice someone hitting golf balls over the football teams' heads, plus the location and angle would be impossible for it to be a golf ball. The image on freeze frame is blurry. But i cannot even make a suggestion as to what it may be.
