{rjl} Tumbleweed & branches with leaves, forming large circle in sky.

Was not able to get my camera, to record what I saw. About 1/2 mile from my front yard, saw what I initially thought were birds forming a large circle in the sky. A large, slow moving circle appeared in the sky. There were tumbleweed, rotating slowly in a clockwise motion. There were branches with leaves attached. I ran in my house, by the time I got my phone, to get video, these tumbleweeds & branches started to disappear. The branches looked like they were from an olive tree. This was not a typical "dust devil". It looked like a vortex, bouncing around in a circle. I know this makes no sense, but I will never forget what I saw.
