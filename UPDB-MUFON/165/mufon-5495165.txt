{dr}The UFO was hovering and the lights blinked.

I walked onto the porch and sat down. It was about 12:25 AM. About 5 minutes later I looked up and saw a black triangle shaped object with 3 white lights in the corner and a red light in the center that was hovering. At first I thought it was a helicopter but it started to gain altitude and then it hovered. It then disappeared for about 5 seconds and then it appeared again about 200 feet lower than before. Then the lights blinked about 10 times and just disappeared. It had no sound at all.
