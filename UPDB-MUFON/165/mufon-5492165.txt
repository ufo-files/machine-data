{jpg} I observed a fire red orb in the night sky under cloudy conditions. The object moved very slowly and ascended into the clouds.

I was driving home approximately 11 p.m. when I noticed a fire red ball in the night sky. I was heading west in my truck and the red orb was headed south. I came to an intersection and stop at the stop sign put my truck in park grabbed my cell phone took one photo and one short video of the object. I observed the object which was very high in the sky but still under the cloud cover, continue heading south very slowly. The object was a very bright fire red color that seemed to pulsate not really blink but go brighter than Dimmer brighter than Dimmer. The object then took an angular path upwards and ascended into the cloud cover where I observed the glow in the clouds slowly fade away to blackness. Even though the weather in my area has been raining of late I was still able to get a clear unobstructed view of the object .
