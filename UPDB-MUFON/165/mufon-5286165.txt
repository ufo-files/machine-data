Observed bright star like (bright as Venus) moving NW to SE at approx. 4am this morning, after 30 seconds simply disappeared.

Stargazing at about 4am 08/10/21 in Pt Pleasant Beach, NJ. Scanning the sky to the NW I saw what looked like an unusually bright star. I saw that it was moving from NW to SE, angle from me in the sky was approx. 80 degrees, bearing about 310 from true N. It was about as bright as Venus only slightly smaller. I observed it moving in a straight line for about 30 seconds when it suddenly shut off. At first I thought it could be a satellite but I have seen them on many occasions and this was bigger, brighter and moving more slowly. It wasn't an aircraft as I observed no running lights and it was moving faster then a typical jet. I ruled out that it was a satellite when it disappeared as it was heading SE towards the morning sun and would have maintained its reflection of the sunlight, plus I continued to watch based on the path it was on and it never reappeared. I am a trained observer from my Navy days and always watch the skies in the morning and at night whenever the stars are visible and have never seen an object act like this one did. Only conclusion was to report it to MUFON as I believe it was a UFO or UAP if you prefer.
