{dr}Three lights hovering, blinking and moving quickly. They changed size and brightness to naked eye.

Looking out my bedroom window over the Palisades on the Hudson River. Very clear night. Saw three lights kind of hovering that got brighter then dimmer and they seemed to sort of dart about. No noise, and no FAA airplane or helicopter lights. Watched for about 15 minutes and they were seen by my daughter and her boyfriend. They finally blinked out and disappeared. I thought, oh, more ufo's. I've seen many weird anomolies living in the Hudson Valley all my life.
