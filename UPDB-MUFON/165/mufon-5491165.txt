Triangular object with 3 flashing lights as well as 1-2 red constant center light(s)

It was 2:30 am that I went out to my garden to have a cigarette I looked up at the stars towards the east and there was a bright red light. As it got closer, heading north west I could clearly see it was a triangular object with a bright white flashing light in each corner of the triangle and 1 possibly 2 (I couldn't quite tell) red light(s) in the middle. It was heading north west and it seemed to hover but move very quickly and I saw it for about 3-5 minutes.
