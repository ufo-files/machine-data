A starlike light which moved in unexpected and unexplainable ways.

I first observed what I thought to be a satellite passing over. I am familiar with viewing them. They look like stars. This object moved towards the Plough formation and then abruptly stopped and moved to the left at a hard angle. Amazed, I had to look twice. I called my wife to come and see.

The object began moving again and darted around in a most peculiar fashion. It would move away from where it had stopped and then stop and turn abruptly sometimes almost doing right angle turns to return to where it had initially been hovering. At one point it took off with speed quite far away from it's hovering point and flew (for lack of a better term) in circles before heading back. At one point the object appeared to emit a flash of light which we perceived to illuminate the thin cloud cover around it. I was astounded to see it. We watched the object continue to move for around 20 minutes. It's movement was very erratic at times much like a small insect would dart back and forth.

I attempted to film this on my phone but the camera couldn't pick up the pin point light. We decided to go in after 20 mins of observing as the object showed no signs of stopping its behavior. It would always return to roughly the same spot.

After being in the house for around 30 mins the sky has clouded over and I can no longer see the object.
