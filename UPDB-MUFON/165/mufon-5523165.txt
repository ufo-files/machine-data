Orange flickering light moving to random spots.

It was late at night and I was over at my friends house everyone was inside hanging out and such. I stepped outside to get some fresh air since I didn't smoke. When I was outside I noticed an orange light flickering a good distance away, brighter than most of the stars up. I didn't pay it much attention at first because hell looking at the night sky you can see a lot of stars with an orange color to them and they flicker. What caught my attention was when it jolted in a random pattern I got my cell out and started snapping some pictures I managed to get two pics before it was gone. It didn't move to a distance till it was out of sight but, more or less it just vanished after about 5 mins.
