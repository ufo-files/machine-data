{nd} Many miles away, moving fast, lighting up four dots and one dash, then repeated several times

While moving north in vehicle in Henderson. Witnessed by three additional passengers. It was moving from the left to right directly in front of us many miles away. It appeared as a bright dot of light, then another, then another, then another, then a dash, all in a line. Then they disappeared and it repeated again several times, all the while moving from the left from my vantage to the right. Repeated three or four times until it became obstructed by local trees and buildings.
