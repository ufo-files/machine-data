{jpg} UFO over Astoria, NY

The glowing orb in this video has been appearing over Queens lately. It is very bright, and the only illuminated object in the sky. In this video I set up a stationary camera on a tripod and directed out an open window in my apartment here in Astoria Queens facing South. 

In the video the orb is visible just above the roof of the building next door to my apartment building. At first the orb begins to dip slowly until it is no longer visible from that perspective. That dip below the roof visibility took approximately 5 minutes. 

Next I moved the camera and tripod to extend out of the window to get a better view of the orb. From that perspective I captured the orb traveling across the sky in the direction of Manhattan from Queens. 

I added the soundtrack and minor glitch effect at the beginning and end for entertainment value.
