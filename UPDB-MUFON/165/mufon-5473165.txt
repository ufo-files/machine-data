I always watched the stars since I was little. I would take my children out at night and we would lay on blankets and watch the stars. One night I went outside with my 10yr old, we were Laying on car hood to look at stars as we looked up and it was above us, a big grey circular ship it was quite large with orange and yellow blinking lights around the edge of the circular ship. It must have only been 100 ft above us sticking out of a cloud right above our street in front of our house, when all of a sudden we seen 3 ships shaped like triangles shoot out real fast, across the sky and in 3 quick spurts across the sky they were gone each spurt was quit a bit of distance, they went spurt, spurt , spurt across the sky traveling a very long distance in a second flat and they were gone the large ship still above us. We screamed and ran in house, locked doors and closed all the curtains We were frightened. I gathered my 3 small children and we locked ourselves in my bedroom until the morning, There was no way I was going outside or looking out the window. I was scared to call anyone, I was afraid of being labeled crazy. Nothing else happened that I am aware of. I truly know, now that UFO's exist. They are real.
