Three enormous red/orange lights over Edmonton

Standing in Parkallen facing west at approximately 7:15pm MST. Perfectly clear gorgeous skies, looking to the west (I was observing Venus over the crescent moon admiring how bright Venus looked); I happened to scan over the horizon to my right (To the North) and three insanely bright, reddish-orange round lights in sequence were slowly but steadily heading eastward. They glowed, they were not blinking. I knew it wasn’t a plane or the space-ex satellites (I’d observed those heading overhead well over a month ago: these were NOTHING like overhead satellites, nor was it a plane or helicopter). I couldn’t tell if they were three separate lights, or one set of lights, but they were massive from where I stood. Then I thought to myself, ‘omg! A UFO!’ And in that exact moment they blinked sequentially once each inside of half a second and then returned to their solid, glowing state. They continued eastbound until they were out of my view from the buildings, tree line and clouds rolling in from the east. The entire sighting lasted approximately 4 minutes. Wonderful experience!
