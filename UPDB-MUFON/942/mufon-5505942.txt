{nd} Bright star like object that moved in quick random directions. Was viewed for over an hour.

I was standing outside having a cigarette with a few friends of mine. Looking up I witnessed what I thought at first was the International space station, as it was fairly bright and moving at a good clip. When suddenly it stopped. I have never in my life ever seen anything like this. It began, to move back and forth in quickly, changing direction every 30 to 45 seconds. It would then stop and hover. There is no doubt in my mind this object was at a very high altitude. I have been an avid stargazer for my whole life. I have seen a lot of amazing things. Nothing compares to what I just witnessed. It actually drove me to call the State police to report it. Someone has to have seen this. It was truely amazing.
