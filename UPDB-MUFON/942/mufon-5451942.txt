On Saturday June 13th at midnight was looking out my bedroom window and saw four to six round balls in the sky. At first I thought it was a drone, but noticed a purple color above the one ship. It was big and round and had lights under it. It had several ships that were bright in color. The one ship looked like a banana and the other ship had a ring around it.
