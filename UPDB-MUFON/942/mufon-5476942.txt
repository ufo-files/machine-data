Saw dark vertical/cigar shaped object hovering, then while slowly descending it changed shape to orb and colour to red/orange, disappeared in thin air.

I was moving my parked car in Mississauga (near Erin Mills Town Centre) when I saw a star in the clear cloudless western sky, which I've seen on other days too. The time was close to sunrise, so the horizon was orange-reddish. Below and to the right of the star I noticed what appeared to be a "hanging"/hovering dark gray/black object high in the sky. Unfortunately I didn't have the opportunity to take video of the sighting. It was thin vertical cigar/"I" shaped, with possibly slight curvatures along the object (it was too far to tell, I had to squint). After 2-3 minutes, it slowly started to descend a little to the bottom + to the right, and as it did, it changed shape to something more sphere/orb-like, it changed colour to an intense orange/red (like the horizon), and it seemed to be rotating/vibrating/pulsating. Within about a minute, it seemed to get smaller/disintegrate/disappear into thin air. There was no evident noise from the object throughout the sighting.

While it was in its original cigar state, I was confident that there would be some sort of explanation to the sighting - later I figured maybe it was a drone. However, the change in shape + colour left me a little baffled. I thought the colour change was due to a reflection of the horizon, but minutes after the sighting I saw a planes/birds near the horizon and none of them reflected the horizon's colour in a similar way as the object.
