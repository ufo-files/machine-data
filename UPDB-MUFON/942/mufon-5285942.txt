Was At Work between Bethersden & Smarden In The South East UK when saw A red Flash in the Sky so got my Nikon D3000 from my work shed & started taking pictures.
Couldn't believe what I saw when I zoomed in on the pictures & an internet search showed objects like this have been sighted in other county's as well including the USA,Canada & China.
No apparent sound or propulsion system & didn't exhibit non ballistic motion either & didn't seem to care if anyone could see it or not.
Just wish I had my Canon 250D there with its 24 megapixel sensor instead of my old Nikon with it's 10 megapixel sensor so I could have got more detail.
Couln't see any control sufaces & the way it tipped onto its side at apparently low velocity without falling out of the sky was surreal
