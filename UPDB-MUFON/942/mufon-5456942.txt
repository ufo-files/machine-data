I remember seeing a circular or triangle shaped craft when I was approximately 9 years old. The object just hovered above rotating slowly. I was with my brother at the time and when we saw it we thought at first what the heck is that, then we kind of freaked out and realized it was a UFO. We thought if we can see it (the craft) and if itt was occupied, they could probably see us too, and got that feeling too, and we as little kids panicked and ran home, not watching to see what happened later after just seeing it stationary rotating. This is relatively close by the Minneapolis St. Paul International airport, and we as kids loved to watch planes and jets fly overhead, as we lived several miles, just west north west of the airport on the path of a couple popular east/west runways. We were aviation fanatics, and knew for sure that when we saw this craft just floating in the sky it was not likle anything we had ever seen before, and was definately not any type of plane or jet that we were accustomed to seeing, and identifyting, since we had all kinds of models that we would build as kids. I am curious to find out if there is anyone else that has reported anything like this around this time frame plus or minus a few years. I would think the airport or the FAA traffic control center would have picked up this craft, since there is a large traffic control center nearly below where this thing was.
