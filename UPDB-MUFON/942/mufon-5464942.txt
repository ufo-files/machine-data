{cak} Bright large round object arced down very fast towards the ground but disappeared behind some houses and tree

My son and I were walking the dog and we both turned to walked down the road we live off of and when we looked ahead of us we both noticed this object at the same time. It was bright white and round. It was bigger than any star or airplane headlights or anything I have ever seen in the sky and it made no sound. As soon as we both saw it we both exclaimed to the other do you see that?" It was moving in kind of an arc trajectory down to the ground and I half expected to hear some kind of loud noise from a crash or explosion after it disappeared behind the tree tops and homes on the other side of the street but there was never any sound. I have seen star sized round shapes moving in the sky that I wondered what I was looking at but this was so big and it was in the atmosphere close to the ground and I have never seen anything lile it. It moved so fast and it had no blinking lights and looked like a glowing ball or something. We kept discussing how weird what we saw was for a few minutes when we noticed the lights of two helicopters heading in the same direction we saw the object go toward. The strange thing was the helicopters didn't even sound like helicopters. They were quiet but I could tell even in the dark evening sky that it was a helicopter and not an airplane. I wanted to go investigate but I wasn't able to tell if it landed and if so where it landed so we finished our walk home. I haven't been able to get it out of my mind and my son is a bit scared from it
