{rjl} Very bright strobe light moving below tree line;changed direction and location at an unimaginable speed.

Was driving in darkness down Hwy 292 from Inman towards Hwy 29. Open field bordered by tall pines (80-90ft tall?) ahead and towards my right. Observed EXTREMELY bright white strobe appearing to move between the trees ahead. One pulse/strobe=one movement to the right, about 100 ft a pulse/strobe. Moved equal distance for 5-6 strobes,then poof was gone. But almost in a blink, it was behind me perhaps half a mile back and moving on. It appeared to make an instantaneous direction (90 degrees) and location change. I saw it behind me from the bright glow of the strobe and because I was watching it. It was weird.... so low to the ground. No airports there; have no idea at all what it was.  Amazing intense light! I lost sight of it because I continued driving, and it was moving too fast to remain in my sight. I felt amazed and baffled.
