When I woke up, I looked out of my bedroom window and saw this entity standing near my barn. It looked like a 'gumby' figure about 11 feet tall. I could tell that because it was about a foot above the 10 foot fence it was standing by. It was around 3 AM. It stood there about 10 minutes or so and when finally I grabbed my cell phone and went outside to see a better view of the entity, it was gone. During my observation of it, I could feel inside my head that it wanted to talk to me.
