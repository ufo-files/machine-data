Formation egg-shaped objects

This happened in 1994 around june-july of that year at midmorning, at that time I was living with my family at my grandmother's house, I was at my room on the second floor working on drawings for prints I used to sell when shouts of people on the street atracted my attention, went to the window that points north and saw them pointing to the sky, so I went to the other window on the opposite side, the one that points to the south, that window you can slide the glass so thats what i did to have a better view and saw my neighbors in the parking lot next to the house watching to the sky, lift my eyes and saw a procession-like formation of hundreds of egg-shaped objects parading at slow pace moving west-east direction, at first I knew those were UFO's, went down the stairs to tell my grandmother's maid to come with me to the garden to have a better view and to share that experience, she was the only other person in the house at that moment besides my grandmother who was to old to take it out at that moment, was very exciting to see such a phenomenon, the whole event lasted around half and hour, there were no clouds, the sun shone on a blue sky, it was pretty clear, so it was easy to see them, they were far up in the sky but you can see them clearly these metalic egg-shaped things moving and they just kept parading until they were gone.
