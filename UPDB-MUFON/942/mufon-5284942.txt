Flashing lights in the sky

I first observed a rumbling while sitting in my cabin. I then asked my friend to check it out because it’d lasted far longer than normal for a jet. The rumbling became louder! My friend called me to run outside. He witnessed 5-6 in a row light up, they did not blink, they just hovered. I personally witnessed three light up and then head towards the southeast where they disappeared in the direction of Jupiter.
