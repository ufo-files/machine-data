UFO spotted that looked just like all the other stars in the sky.

One night after a friend had dropped me off, he was smoking a cigarette outside in the parking lot before we went to my apartment. We both noticed what looked like all the other stars in the sky. I believe what drew our attention to it was the blinking. We stared at it for a few moments, when it suddenly slowly started dropping. It reached a certain level and started to fly straight towards us with no deviation. We couldn't tell it was heading toward us until it got closer. I remember feeling like this thing was not only watching us but it could hear us. It flew right threw town, directly over us, so close I could have hit it with a rock. The whole time this thing had 4 or more blinking lights on it. The lights blinked almost how an airplanes lights would blink at night. The best way I can describe this this is like an old school roof tv antenna with a glass top. I believe it had two sky blue light on each side of it. maybe one underneath it and one behind it. I did not see or hear any propellers, nor was there any smoke or sound coming from it. At first I thought Satellite, but that made no sense. Then i thought drone, but the thing had no sound or propellers. Me and Justin just looked at each other like wtf was that. This was all around the time when [name redacted/cms/mmb] allegedly had a close encounter with a UFO in Pine Bush NY, so we were always looking in the sky and talking about UFO's. These days I still wonder if any of the stars in the sky are really stars or just some kind of UFO probe .
