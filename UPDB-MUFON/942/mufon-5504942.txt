{nd} saw a large cylinder shape hovering near the highway then disappeared

My sister was driving me to an appointment in Williamsport,pa. when she said look at that jet...its going so slow...I looked and saw what I thought was a big passenger jet but then realized it had no wings... it was as long as a 747 and the same diameter in the center...with a hump at each end like the 747 has..I realized as we got closer they it was only a few hundred ft. off the ground and was stationary. It was just north of I-80 and just east of I-180 a few hundred ft. We were very close and could see it was smooth.. no seams..white or very shine chrome color with what looked like a dark black window or door in the middle. I was so busy trying to figure out what it wasn't that by the time I went to get my cell for a pic it was gone. I don't know if it took off or disappeared.. just gone and no traces of it anywhere. Im very observant and really didn't believe what I saw at first. But I know it wasn't a plane..blimp..cloud..or anything I've ever seen in the sky..or the ground for that matter! Afterwards I was very upset about not getting a pic but can see now why many wouldn't. A friend later in the day described the exact same thing to me that she had seen an hour later in the selinsgrove,pa. are area bout ten miles or more south of where sis and I saw it.
