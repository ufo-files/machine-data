Rectangular object barely missed hitting a small airplane as it flew over my position, have had 8 sightings in this area since 1970,

Out of a fog bank running south to north a rectangular dark grey object with 4 bright medium yellow lights on bottom at corners flew 10-20ft over a small airplane coming out of the west, as the plane flew under fog bank the wash of the propeller moved the fog around the top of plane creating a tunnel showing this object coming out of the south, what I saw was the bottom of this craft most of top half hidden by fog bank, grey color blended with cloud color, slow speed, 4 light domes one in each corner of bottom of craft, light domes within a thin black frame that was like 6 orange slices around dome, no wings could be seen and it was at the edge of fog bank so if a plane I would have seen wings projecting out of cloud and no turbulence was seen from propeller if it had one, in fact I didn't see any disturbence in fog as it flew by, as the object approached the end of fog bank which turned to the right, it moved (floated) up to right into fog bank and was again hidden, speed roughly 30 mph maybe slower distance within 200 ft from me.
