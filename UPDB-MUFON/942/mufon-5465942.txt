driving late at night in a snow storm. Witnessed a glowing sphere on the north side of the road

I was driving to Paisley, Ontario late at night during a snow storm. While I was driving I was conversing with a woman on my CB radio. The trip I was taking was about 35 miles and with the snow, I estimate that I was driving at about 25 to 30 miles an hour.
When I was a few miles away from Paisley, I noticed an orangish ball of light in the air on the north side of the road. My thoughts were that it was the moon, as it looked like a large moon rise but the snow was steady and heavy and in after thought it was in the wrong location. 
The next thing I remember was a sudden change in the road conditions where I thought the snow was a lot deeper than it had been and the jeep was pushing harder to get through it. 
I went onto the CB radio and called to the woman who I had been talking to. She responded with a comment about me being back on line and that I had been gone for a while. I suggested that maybe I had been in a blanked-out area because there was a bit of a valley on the road that I was traveling on. She replied that I was away for a lot longer than it would take to go through that valley. 
When I got to Paisley I realized that it had taken me about two and a half hours to travel that 35 miles. Even with the storm conditions, that was excessive.
