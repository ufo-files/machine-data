My friend took a photo of the beach/ocean/sky and later noticed an object in the photo that looks like a disk in the sky.

My friend was on the beach of Dog Island, Florida (off shore from the coastal town of Carrabelle). It was a beautiful clear sunny afternoon with a normal ocean breeze. He and his friend took several photos with a digital camera of the beach/ocean/sky, looking south over the Gulf of Mexico. Later when looking closely at the photos, he noticed a strange white hazy streak in the sky, which he thought was an optical anomoly. Then he also noticed a tiny object near the streak in the sky. When zooming in on the object in the photo, it looks like a disk in the sky with the top shiney, apparently due to reflected sun light, and the bottom with a dark shadow. They had not noticed either the streak or the object when taking the photos. (see attached photo). To find the object, zoom in and pan down to the right at an ~45 deg angle.
