{rjl} First UFO heading southeast, intercepted by second heading southwest and shot down by same. Second UFO headed back to northeast

I watch the stars nightly during good weather and noticed a very bright light larger than a satellite right above me heading in a southeast direction from the northwest. All of a sudden another smaller light appeared to the northeast and headed toward the first one. A bright ray of some sort came from the second UFO and hit the first one. There was a very bright light and the first UFO disappeared. The second UFO returned on it's original path back from the northeast.
