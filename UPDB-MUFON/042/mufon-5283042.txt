Strange object

Me and my girlfriend were out making deliveries and had observed a ufo hovering and rotating about an hour prior to this event. The object seemed to be following us and keeping pace while we drove on a secluded back road trying to get back to the main highway, this lasted about 6 minutes before we lost sight of the object. During the encounter our phones started acting up with hers losing signal and mine showing glitches and having issues with the gps, the gps on my car also started having issues that seemed to clear up as soon as we lost sight of the object.
