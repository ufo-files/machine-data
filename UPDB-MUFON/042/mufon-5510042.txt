driving north on rt 65 south of lima,had just entered the city limits when i observed a very bright object, pulsating red and orange with a lot of intensity.it was bright enough to leave an image in my vision when i looked back to check for traffic.i was still driving at this time. the object ascended a few degrees and then while descending made a fairly sharp 90 degree turn to the east. at this point the shape of the object appeared to change, it became much less sphere like. again the intensity of the light made it to discern a shape it then started a slow ascent in a northeasterly direction. at this point i was finally to park my car. i got to listen for any engine noise. i heard none, but was a lot farther away than i would have liked. object then ascended at a fairly steep angle (75 degrees) until it became to small to see. the smoothness of the flight was remarkable. it looked like CGI. something else, there was a red light flashing on top. almost like and aircraft marker lights, but the brilliance of the object made the red light hard to see.
