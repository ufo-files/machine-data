{kt}Bright flashing light came rapidly from the NW , zig zagged around , the hovered and descended. Twice.

We were BBQing when Gus ( one of three witnesses ) noticed a bright light appear in the sky NW of us. We sat and watched it for approximately 1 minutes go from left to right a few times then stop and hover. I made a joke " wave at the aliens " and immediately after that it descended rapidly to the ground. We ran to get our phones and when we came back out it was doing some crazy zig zagging. The rest is on video. There are two other phones that recorded it but my phone got the best footage. 100% not a plane or helicopter or ballon. This was something else. Sorry about the commentary but we were excited and freaked out.
