{rjl} Bright Hovering UFO, THE UFO ZIZ AND ZAG MANY TIMES BEFOE TAKEN OFF DUE NORTH.

I WAS CAMPING OUT WHEN A TEENAGER BACK IN THE MIDS 60'S OUT SIDE MY TEN IN A SLEEPING BAG LOOKING UP AT THE SKY AND STARTS.  NOTICED ONE REAL BRIGHT, AND LARGER THAN ALL THE OTHER STARS. IT START TO ZIG AND ZAG AND DID SO AT LEAST 7 OR 8 TIMES, I FIGURED IT WAS OF THE AUGUSTA MAINE AIRPORT.  EACH TIME IT MOVED IT WAS IN AN INSTANT AND THEN IT STOPPED.  AFTER A GOOD FIFTEEN MINUTES IT TOOK OFF HEADED NORTHWEST AND DISAPPEARED OVER THE HORIZON. WITHIN A 4 SECONDS IT WENT OUT OF EYESITE OVER THE HORIZON.  I HAVE NEVER FORGOTTEN THAT OBJECT ALL THESE YEARS, IT WAS NO PLANE, HELICOPTER, SATELLITE OR METEOR, ABSOLUTELY NO NOISE AT ALL.  I AM 68 YEARS OF AGE NOW AND WILL NEVER FORGET WHAT I SAW AND NO ONE CAN TELL ME ANY DIFFERENT, WE ARE NOT ALONE IN THS UNIVERSE, HAVE A GREAT DAY.  I HAVE TOLD A FEW PEOPLE AND THEY ALL KINDA SHAKE THERE HEADS, AT LEAST I HAD THE PRIVILEGE TO SEE A UFO, THANK YOU. I HAD NO CAMERA BACK IN THEN, WISH I HAD ONE WITH A ZOOM LENS, MY FAMILY WASN'T THAT RICH TO HAVE A REAL GOOD CAMERA, JUST SO YOU KNOW.
