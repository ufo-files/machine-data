I have seen this phenomenon before years ago .. I was sitting on my front porch way before sun up and Venus was up due East .. 5:13AM and all of a sudden I realized something else was in view .. moving .. came out from behind a porch structure (post) .. was visible for five seconds and then turned its self off perhaps by rotating to a non reflective belly surface .. but it was still moving after it turned off because just a trace of the bright surface was still showing .. it reminded me of a manned space orbit coming directly over me .. fast, glorious and then gone .. but this should not have turned off as it was high in the East and the sun was not that far from the horizon as to shut anything off.
