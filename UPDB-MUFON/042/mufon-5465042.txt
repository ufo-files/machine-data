two hovered aligned one another and created squares and circles together in the sky

I was walking home when i first noticed them, there were about six and one ascended just above me about 20 feet, i realized they were so small about the size of a hummingbird and it seemed to have the same qualities of a hummingbird but they were silver and gold with reds and blues flashing randomly. As I walked home i kept my eyes on them the entire time and they stayed at the same pace as me, if I were to turn I motioned the direction and they seemed to understand, as the daylight came they disappeared before I made it home.
The next evening I went out for a cigarette and there were two hovering out front my house so they some how knew were I lived. The two hovered align with each other and I could see them making lines in the sky at first it was circles rectangles and squares and i motioned with my hands the shape of a circle and a rectangle to show I could see what they were trying to show me and it seemed like it excited them because they grew brighter. 
I went back inside my house but I could see they had moved directly in front of my window so I could still see them. This time instead of shapes it was like they were showing my a video but I couldnt really make it out, it looked like a bunch of people dancing but almost like on fast forward like they were going way too fast, and I'm not exactly sure thats what it was. I was really tired so I waved goodnight and they haven't been by since but when I go for night walks I usually see one facing the southwest and one facing the northeast.
I've made paintings of what I experienced and I've been keeping an eye on them for over a year now and have had a couple other experiences but this was my most recent and most interesting experience so far. I live in a small town with a native reserve which I've heard is a place that they like to go and feel safe...
