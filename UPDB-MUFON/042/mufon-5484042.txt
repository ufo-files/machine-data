{cak} Glowing blue orbs with attached white spheres captured in our photographs.

Rachel, Jeanne, and me (Tommi) (you may use our first names) were visiting the Echo Amphitheater in Abiquiu, NM.in the later afternoon on April 9, 2016.  We were all taking photographs. Rachel noticed a round, glowing blue light in one of her photos. At first we thought it was a reflection from the camera. It was curious so we examined all of the photos we took at the Amphitheater and saw the same or a similar glowing "blue orb" in four more of our pictures. We enlarged the images and found that the glowing blue orbs appear to have smaller white spheres clinging to them. We do not know if all of the photos are of one orb, or if we photographed more than one. We did not actually see the blue orbs, only captured it/them in our photographs. In some photos the orb appears solid while in others it appears partially or totally transparent.  We are attaching the photographs along with each orb enlarged for better viewing. Here are the pictures. We would love to know what MUFON thinks.
