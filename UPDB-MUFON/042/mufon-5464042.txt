{jpg} walking through the streets at night, we noticed a cluster of lights and an outline that we hadn't seen in the sky there before.

we were out walking in the street at around 9:30 in golden, colorado. a friend looked up and asked me what the cluster of lights in the sky was. initially we thought itya be a mountain house but the object was too high above the mountains and too brightly lit to possibly be a building or antenna (the pulsating lights also struck us as quite odd). it was an object with a triangular grey dull silhouette and triangular white light formation. the closer we got, the more the body outline seemed to fade in colour into the night sky. it hovered stationary until around 10:10 pm, when it tilted slightly and the lights were obscured a little from vision. we were very excited and also a little scared to see it. we could not stay out for longer than an hour in the cold, as one of us has an adverse reaction to extended cold exposure, so we lost sight when we had to go back inside.
