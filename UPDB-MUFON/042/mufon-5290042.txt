3 round objects seen overhead

As me and my girlfriend are watching a movie, she noticed humming so looked outside where she spotted a large round object with constant red lights and rotating white lights almost directly over the house and headed away. We went outside to get a better look and saw another 3 spread about 50 miles apart from each other, all traveling parallel and headed nw.

We get tons of air traffic over my house so I know what planes, helicopters, and jets look like; she points out things regularly but once I take a closer look I can typically identify them. I'm so used to it, I didn't bother to grab my camera. that's why these really caught our attention. I can honestly say I have never seen anything like them before.
