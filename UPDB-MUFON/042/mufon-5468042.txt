{jpg} Looked like a neighbor twin to Saturn, then it completed a semi-circle around Saturn.

Camping &fishing with a friend last night (June 7' 2018) turned really interesting and left us puzzled.

We were in Beach City, Ohio. Around 9pm, maybe a little after. No light pollution, and as I looked into the sky to look for Saturn, I found it, but it had a "twin" next to it. It looked identical to Saturn in size and brightness. The "twin" moved in a smooth, circular motion from to the right of Saturn, down under it, and ended up to the left of Saturn before disappearing.
