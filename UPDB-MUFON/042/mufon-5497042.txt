I do have a better example of what this video could be of or what i think it is photo attached.  I noticed in this video 2 prominent spheres (but there is a third sphere to the side if you look carefully) all alternate to the front as if it leap frogs its momentum, gliding through the atmosphere perhaps. Most times these transparent types do not appear to the human eye and appear only on video in particular during rainy periods or thunderstorms.
