Cloud shaped UFO hovering over MCClintock Highschool

I was on the MCClintock bus going northbound, when I glanced to my right outside my window I noticed what seemed to be two spherical blimps connected to each other, it nearly had the appearance of the side of a Jumbojet, but didn't have wings, it looked more like a metal-bodied cloud. I almost dismissed it as a commercial airliner but it didn't move for about 30 seconds just hovered over the MCClintock Highschool track. When my bus moved north it followed for a few seconds and then vanished in thin air! It moved to follow the bus! A gray mist was left behind that quickly vanished as well.
