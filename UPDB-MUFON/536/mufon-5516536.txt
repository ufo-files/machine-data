(GS) I saw 5 unknown objects in a line type formation which faded and disappeared after about 4-5 minutes with no sound at all.

I had just sat down on my couch at around 9:30 pm and looked out of my window and saw 5 yellowish-orange lights in sort of a line formation with no sound and no blinking lights.I was in the Richland/Elton area of johnstown and I see airport traffic all the time but this was nothing like any airport traffic that I had seen before in fact they were not heading anywhere near the airport. I watched them for about5 minutes until one by one they started to fade until they all disappeared. The sky was clear so they didn't disappear into the clouds.
