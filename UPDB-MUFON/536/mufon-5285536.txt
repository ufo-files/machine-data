fixed location, no wings, shiny light blue on morning light

i went outside at about 7:45 to unlock the gate for the weed-control guy when I noticed something shiny and light blue very high up, perhaps 10 miles above me. It had no wings and was not moving. It was shaped like a cylinder but seemed to split into 2 separate cylinders. and then just disappeared, leaving no trail and without sound. In order to see something that high up, it must have been massive.
