UFO over Sea if Cortez near Los Cabos

I saw a stationary UFO from my balcony at Le Blanc resort last night. It was NOT a star, satellite or aircraft. It was completely still and too clear to be above air cruising altitude; probably lower. It had one large lights with a few lights on what appeared to be a perimeter. I could see an outline/blur in the darkness that it was something big. It might have been cloaked, not sure. It was there over the course of the two or so hours I watched it. It is not there tonight.
