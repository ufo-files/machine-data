I WENT OUTSIDE AT 9:35 PM AND I SAW THE RED GLOBE IN THE EAST SKY. IT WAS ABOUT THE SIZE OF A FULL MOON. I CALLED MY WIFE OUT TO SEE IT. SHE SAW IT THEN WENT BACK IN TO GET THE CAMERA.
