(GS) Bright orange red orb flying

Upon driving to a New Years Eve party on 12/31/2012 I noticed a orange red fireball flying from the NE to the West. It wasnt going too fast but it was on a steady speed. I quickly grabbed the attention of my family to look up. I tried to pull over as fast I could because I wanted to witness this UFO but could not find a turn off lane. I then told my daughter to keep an eye on it from the back seat. She witnessed it flying west and disappeared into the horizon.
