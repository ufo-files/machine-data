Silver Rectangle

What I could see the object was large, approx. 300-500ft and rectangular in nature, shiny silver metallic looking with 2 large square bright white lights one on each end of the rectangle with 3- 5 smaller round bright white lights in between. It made absolutely no sound. I had gone outside to see the moon and planets never expecting to see a UAP. The UAP was sitting in the sky above the treeline and when I went to get my husband less than 60 secs...it was gone..we live near Mt Rainier and continue to watch the skies...now I know for sure...thank you
