{cak} A flash of white light, UFO appears.

My wife and I were traveling easterly on our way home from Green Bay on Hwy.54. I was looking at the sky ahead of us and thought nothing of the very few clouds that were in the sky.
In an instant, there was an instantaneous flash of light, not a flash like a camera flash, but a sphere-like ball. Then, a grayish, dark colored object appeared out of the light and traveled very slowly for an estimated 5-10 seconds and then it just vanished. 

There wasn't any air traffic near the sighting and no other objects near the vicinity. We had actually pulled over just to be certain that the sky was clear of anything but a couple of clouds. Clouds that were very light or almost transparent. 

I know exactly what we had seen and there is nothing that could, or will convince me, that it was just my imagination or it was just an anomaly. There was a instantaneous flash of round light and a UFO appeared from it.
