{cak} 5 military helicopters flying over, small glowing object fell slowly, then hovered

I was outside in my driveway having a cigarette at around 11:15 am facing south I heard loud helicopters, when the helicopters came into view I was surprised to see 5 of them flying together in formation, they were flying low and were all black, they had no markings that I could see. They were flying in a northerly direction. Just as they were almost overhead, a small glowing object fell slowly from them, I thought it might have been a flare or something, it fell until it went behind buildings and I couldn't see it anymore. I was on the phone with my daughter at the time who was on her lunch break at a restaurant that is about 2 miles away from my home, I asked her if she could see the helicopters and she said she could, then I asked if she could see the glowing ball that was falling and she exclaimed "yes!" I asked her where it was because I couldn't see it anymore. She exclaimed yes! I see it hovering over the mountains! From where I was I couldn't see the mountain tops because if the trees and buildings. So my daughter put her phone on video chat so I could see what she was seeing, and just then it disappeared. It hovered for around 3 to 5 minutes before disappearing. If it was a flare it wouldn't have hovered. I dont know what it was
