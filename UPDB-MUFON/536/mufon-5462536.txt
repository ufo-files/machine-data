{cak} Glowing ball of light appeared in sky moving from West to north. Stayed motionless then faded

While walking my dog along the B4209 (bulkington Rd/bedworth Rd) Warwickshire uk i stopped to get a dog poo sack from my pocket to pick up my dogs mess. (sorry to be graphic!) 

Approx Location looking North East:

[Removed link]
I noticed a very bright light move in a downward arc towards the north it slowed to a stationary position. at this point i removed my glove and grabbed my iphone from my back pocket and filmed the object. The time of this video was exactly 8:11pm. 

While filming the object i zoomed in with the cameras zoom feature which caused the object to be unfocused. it then faded to a very faint point and moved Northwards. 

The object was very bright and emitted orange/red/white light and made no sounds. I would describe it as a glowing bright star-like object. 

The weather at the time was partly cloud with a light breeze. i have a screen shot of the weather around that time from:
