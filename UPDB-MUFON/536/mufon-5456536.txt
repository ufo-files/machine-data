{cak} Orange diamond like object shining in the night sky

Immediately after seeing this object in the sky, I stopped my car and got out with my phone to see if I can capture what I saw. The sky was clear and dark. The object was bright orange and shaped like a diamond, but taller on the top and bottom (like a smudge lit up in the sky). I was able to video tape only a few seconds, as the object quickly disappeared. I had to stabilize my elbows on the car to keep still enough for the camera, but I can tell that my phone camera wasn't able to capture what I was seeing. What I saw on my phone looked totally different than what I saw in the sky. Nobody else was around and had I been there 2 minutes prior or after, I wouldn't have seen it. It didn't make any noise, and in a snap, it was gone.
