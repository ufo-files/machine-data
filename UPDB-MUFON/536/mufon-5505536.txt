{nd} Single orange light which disappeared, then reappeared but many more. One line of 6, one line of 3.

I was driving home from work on Tuesday, January 7, 2014 at appx 6 pm, northbound on County Road I just south of Arlington, WI . It was extremely cold with a clear, dark sky. I saw an orange round glow appear in the sky about 45 degrees from the horizon, to the north/northeast. It looked like it could be a slow flying airplane perhaps in trouble. The orangeness of it seemed like it could be an engine on fire or something. It faded and disappeared. I continued looking in that area. a few seconds later, the orange light appeared again but spread to the west as six identical lights, equally spaced, and in a straight line. To the right of that line and slightly above, one orange light spread to the north and west as three lights. There they stayed for about 10 seconds, then faded quickly and disappeared. It did not reappear for the remainder of my drive which was about 15 minutes. I did notice that within a few minutes there were about 3 or 4 regular appearing aircraft lights in that general area buzzing around and I wondered if craft had been dispatched to investigate. When I state the size as golf ball, I mean that each orange light would be covered by a golf ball at arms length. 

I was absolutely amazed by the sight of this. It was exhilarating and terrifying all at once. I only wish I had not been alone but I saw what I saw and it was very real. When I arrived home I called the Columbia County Sheriffs office to ask whether anyone else had reported this. I cant imagine I was the only one to see it! I remembered I had heard a report that there were unusual and similar sightings on New Years eve on the West coast so took to the internet to compare my experience. All report approximately the same thing I saw - orange lights in different sorts of configurations.
