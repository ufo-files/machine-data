Witnessed silver/chrome object to the North when coming back from bike ride. Son noticed it first.

Witnessed a shiny chrome/silver oval (pictures make it seem tic tac shaped) in the north sky after returning to my truck after a bike ride with my son near Bicentennial Park in Neosho. My son asked me what "that" was in the sky and I assumed it was drone, however when I saw it, the object was larger than any drone I've seen and was located in an area, that I would think, wouldn't be a good spot for flying due to the amount of trees and forest. It was in the sky just hovering for a min or so, then slowly moved towards the west slowly, there was no sound and no propulsion. It started moving behind some trees in my path of vision. Took two videos and a couple pictures. I told my son to hurry and we jumped in the truck and followed after it for about a mile, it gradually increased speed and headed NW and then was either lost from point of view or quickly left the area. We got to a clearing another half mile down the road, and did not see it in the sky anywhere. If it was a drone, it still would have been visible Id think.
