{nd} Usual sighting in Cleveland Texas in March 2012 of a V shaped air craft

On March 23, 2012, I was on my way home from Dayton Texas, when I spotted what looked like two airplanes in the sky. As I approached my left turn towards home, one of the air crafts seemed to be on a normal route in the night sky.

The other one however, seem to be getting bigger as I got closer which meant to me that this one was not moving in any direction, it looked like a V in the sky with weird lights.

Furthermore the lights on this whatever it was, were multi-colored and they seemed to be chasing each other towards the middle of the V shaped craft. By this point I was stopped on the side of bridge on a new stretch of highway in Cleveland Texas.

I was pretty shaken, but excited and decided to go home. If it was what I think I saw, then my only concern would I hope that they mean us, ( human kind) no harm.
