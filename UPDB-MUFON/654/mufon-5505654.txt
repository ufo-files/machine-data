{rjl} Two orbs were hovering north of 39th Ave. as we approached Union Hills they began moving SW. Then a third one appeared from the North and followed the other two.

We went for a walk in Glendale, Az. off of Union Hills Ave. and witnessed 2 orbs hovering. The lights were white at first, but then changed to a red-orange color and back to white. We didn't know what could be flying so low and so bright. We walked faster to try and reach the two orbs, but as we got closer they began moving southwest at about 15 to 20 mph. We walked about another 3 blocks north and a third orb appeared. It didn't look as big as the other two, and we didn't see where it came from. It also moved in the same direction as the other 2 orbs and at the same speed. We couldnt hear if they were making any noise because there were several cars passing on the main road. Frankly, I don't know what to make of it. It was an incredible sight. We lost sight of the three orbs as they flew over some apartments.
