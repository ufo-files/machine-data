{dr}White orb changed to a red/orange flying wing structure.

While standing in the driveway shortly after 8 PM on 09/23/18 I noticed a white orb travelling west to east. Living north of Albany, NY I always see a lot of air traffic. This first one was considerably lower than most commercial air traffic. I then scanned the sky again and noticed another white orb at the 200 degree mark. As it got closer it changed shape from round to what appeared to be a flying wing, glowing red/orange constantly and continuing on a course to the 20 degree mark. This second sighting was even lower in altitude than the first object. There was no sound at all, no trail. I was so fascinated by the second object I lost track of the first object. During this 20 to 25 second sighting I had called my daughter and her husband out to witness the event. This makes number 3 and 4 for personal sightings, one in 1979, another in the mid 90's which was even more spectacular than you could imagine. Simply amazing!!!

I did take a short video with my phone, it's kind of blurry and doesn't compare to seeing it with your own eyes.
