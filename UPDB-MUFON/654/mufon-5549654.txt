UFO reporting in Priest River, Idaho

I was getting into the car when I saw a bright beautiful green object.I thoughtit was maybe part of a rocket booster.I was facinated by the object. After itwas gone I became curious and wanted to know what it was. It was a lamp shaped,glowing, green object with four dripping balls of fire infront of the object. Iwas unable to see if the dripping balls of fire were connected to the objectbecause the sky was so dark. It went out of my vision behind mountians leavingtwo flashes of bright light occuring sometime shortly after.
