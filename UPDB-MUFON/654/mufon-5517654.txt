{ck} Orange glowing ball hovers then ascents

I was driving on 69 Hwy toward Excelsior Springs, the sighting took place approximately 2-3 minutes before reaching stoplight by WalMart, near wooded area with large grassy/wooded median.

Saw what I first thought was a non-disintegrating piece of a firework, then realized it wasnt going away and was hovering. It was an orange glowing ball that continued to hover not far from my car as I drove. I watched it as it hovered for a minute, then slowly move toward my left, hover for another minute, then slowly ascent. Within approximately 15 seconds it went from being relatively close to my car to looking like a star in the sky, then disappeared completely.
