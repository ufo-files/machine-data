Orange sphere ascending from the woods in the backyard hovering over the house and slowly ascending till out of sight

At around 8:30 in the evening just as Dusk was settling in what I thought to be a large balloon ascending from the woods in the back of the house. At first I thought it was a balloon candle lit with an orange glow in the middle. As a descendent I realized I did not have a Basket with occupants. I got off the phone immediately with my sister and called upstairs to my roommate to come out to his deck and look at it from above. He walked out with a friend of his that was over for the evening and saw it when he said that’s a effing UFO. at that point it had just come up from behind the trees in the back of the house and was over still a little to the south side and starting to ascend. It’s slowly ascended for about 4 more minutes until it became out of sight. We called the sunset police to see if anybody else reported the sighting. I think they thought we were drinking because they were making fun of us. Later on maybe a half hour afterwards it returned up in the sky for about a half hour hovering 3000 to 4000 feet. And finally disappeared out of sight. It is now 2021 and we still talk about it like it happened just yesterday.
