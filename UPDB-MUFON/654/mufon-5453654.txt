{rjl} Boomerang Shaped

On three separate occasions, I have seen this object always in the West. It makes no noise and appears to glow lite green, translucent nearly. It flys just below the cloud line. It is Boomerang shape, It has eight large round lights underneath the wings. The lights are dim. Another person who lives close by has seen it also on a different occasion.
