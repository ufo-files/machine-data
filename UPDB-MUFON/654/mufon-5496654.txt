{rjl} I was a kid and we all ran to the door to see a ship hovering about 300 ft. above ground at about a 500 ft. distance, flashing, and making a huge noise! It had to be the size of a city block at least. It hovered about 5 minutes then ascended!

I was around seven yrs old and we were at home one evening, when my sisters started screaming about a spaceship in the sky. I ran to the door to see, and saw one the most amazing things I've ever seen. A very large ship was hovering in the sky only a few hundred feet above the ground and approx. 500 feet across a small field and a city street. It reminded me of a huge Simon Says flashing in the sky! We had time to grab the camera and try to snap some pictures of it, but it was too dark outside and the pictures didn't take. We watched for several minutes as it hovered there directly across the street from our apartment. I have seen other U.F.Os since then, but from much farther away standpoints. That was an event I'll always remember! Awaesome!!!
