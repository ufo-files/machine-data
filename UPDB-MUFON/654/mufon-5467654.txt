{td} Observed objest appear, go slightly to the right and then zip off to the west at great speed.

I was up early to see if I could see the Northern lights. In focusing in on one area to the north of my location, I observed at about a 45 degree angle a small star sized light appear, go to the east for a short distance and then accelerate rapidly to the west. I continued watching this location and observed eight more lights that night. On the next night I observed between 15 and 20 and this morning, June 30, saw a couple of more.
