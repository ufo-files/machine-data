{rjl} A line of twenty to thirty lights all moving on the same course at same speed west to east at same speed and heading

At 2340 western time a friend and I were sharing a cigarette outside when I looked to the northern sky sky and saw a line line of lights on a perfectly similar trajectory and speed moving east to west. Upon further observation I noticed that there were 20 to 30 lights in this string and that the first ten to fifteen were all equally spaced moving at the exact same speed and course while the last 8 to 15 were at randomly spaced intervals with some groups of 3 to 5 but still at the same uniform speed and trajectory. The lights were of a star color and non blinking. As they went into the western sky they started to fade as though the sun was no longer making a reflection visible to us on the ground. I was unable to acquire any photos as all I had was a phone and the lens could not function well in low light conditions. I have attached what photos I got in hopes that they can be analyzed or enhanced to see what we saw, like I said the lens of an iPhone is not ideal for low light photos. On a side note, my dog was acting strange and circling about five minutes before and after we saw the unidentifiable object in the sky.
