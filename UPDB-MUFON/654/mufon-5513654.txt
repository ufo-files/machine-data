Glowing orb

I was in bed with my woman looking out of the top window when a glowing orb came rushing in an incredible speed and had a instant stop. It hovered for a second and went sideways with the same speed it appeared. Such a feat can't be done by human aircrafts.

I knew when I saw the UFO, it was an UFO.

I got spooked, so spooked I felt I was going insane. Cause such a thing could'nt be real. I believe in aliens, and thought that seeing an UFO was rare. So it felt like winning the lottery, like believing in God and seeing him.
