(GS) large cigar shaped object and smaller round object caught on film.8/5/2012

sitting at kitchen table,looking outside,looked like a 747 jet with NO tail and no wings.It was cigar shaped. It was travelling with another object smaller and roundish in a southesterly direction, small object vanished,large cigar shaped object stopped, then suddenly small object was on other side of cigar shaped object, and moving in a northwesterly direction with cigar shaped object following it. essentially going back the same way they had come from. all of this took about 5-6 mnutes. I yelled at my wife who grabbed a camera and got the pictures on the return path heading northwesterly. They just vanished then. Looked to be very far away. Felt it could not be planes-they would not travel so close together and in the same line.
