I was at my sons house in Mt. Arlington new .approximately 9:30 pm on july 10 2016 we were watching fire works when i notice a red light to my right . It was making sharp angle turns and then hovered over the water. It look like it was pulling water out of the lake i watched this for about 20 minutes. I thought it might be a helicopter. I was in the military for 15 years and never seen a aircraft do this ever.I was amazed and shocked . I recorded it on my phone and showed the family . the next morning the recording was missing . Then the object shot off in a high rate of speed from a stand still. and was gone so quick. My step son said he has seen unusual sightings before.
