Triangular craft with three white lights passed in front of my window. No colored lights, no sound.  The lights blinked on and off in a regular pattern and each light was on the corner of the craft. At first I thought it was an airplane but then realized there wasn't any green or red lights and the lights going on and off didn't add up. As it got closer I could see it was triangular in shape. Kind of shook me up for early in the morning!
