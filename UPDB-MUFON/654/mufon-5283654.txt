A very large fleet of yellow/white dots

I was looking for the pleiades with binoculars in hand, and this quick-moving white blurry line caught my eye,I thought it was a cloud whisp but it moved too quickly so I looked into the binoculars and saw a hundred or so white or yellow (it's hard to remember) dots moving together in a roughly semicircular or v like formation. They moved perpendicular to the formations shape, not in a straight line like starlink satellites. Most of them were clustered in the front and some were falling behind. They faded away dimmer as they moved towards the south and I could no longer find them again with the binoculars.
