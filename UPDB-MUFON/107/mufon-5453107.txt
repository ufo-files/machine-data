23 trail of satellites respond to my light signal

Looked like a satellite flying across. Then about 10-15 seconds another appeared traveling same trail. Then another and another. I grabbed my spot light and began signaling the objects. As if responding to my signal, the "satellite-like" object shined bigger and brighter. They traveled in a trail except for one or two traveled in a different direction than the others. Those went south while the rest traveled from west to east or northeast. I checked satellite tracker and there were no trail of 23 satellites traveling above or anywhere else in california. These objects flashed back at me and were not airplanes.
