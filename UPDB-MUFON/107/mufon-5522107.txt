Was sitting by the window in my living room when a light caught my eye then looked out the window, I assumed it was a plane or star at first..until I ran outside to see more clearly and realized it was flickering multi colored lights...I was about a mile away and I could only see the light through the trees but I could clearly see it was not an airplane nor helicopter..no noise I could hear coming from any direction. My husband and I both saw the object illuminating the multi colored light just hovering in the same spot..it proceeded to dim to nothing then lost site of the light..I waited a few seconds to see if it would pop up anywhere else,then my husband saw it pop up again and I saw it with my own eyes again in the same spot it was previously in before,but it only stayed lit for about another 30-45secs then it disappeared for good...it actually was the coolest thing I ever seen.
