Awoken by Invasive proceedure at 3:10am 19/3/2021

I was awoken at 3:10am this morning by intense pain in my torso (right side midway between right hip and right armpit). It felt like a knitting was being slowly pushed deeply into me. I said in a normal voice "What the F****" and immediately the pain vanished totally. 

Looking to my right I saw a number of very short figures scurrying into some sort of sphere shaped object carrying items, There were 2 open apertures through which they went, light from within shon out of the opening. When they were all inside the apertures closed like shutters coming down. 

I put the light on and examined my right side there was no marks of any kind, no soreness. there was nothing in the bedroom. I sensed fear from them, a sort of a sense of urgency to leave after being discovered. Eventually I went back to sleep (I sleep alone).

When I got up at 08:00am I felt very ill, it was hard to think clearly, I also felt very listless no energy and really didnt start to feel energised till 4pm.

NB: Over the past 3 months on a number of occasions I have been awoken by my bedroom being filled with light without a source which faded away to darkness when i looked around the room.
On another occasion I started to roll over to lay on my left side after visiting the bathroom, suddenly realised that my left arm was illuminated by light, looking further to the left, i saw a white glowing figure (approx 6ft tall) standing beside the bed, before I could speak it vanished. I didnt feel in danger.
