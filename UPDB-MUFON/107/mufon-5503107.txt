We went to the overlook at Muir Beach and were stargazing; my lady asked if we were looking at Orion because a star-cluster had the appearance of the belt on the constellation... when suddenly one of the 3 stars flew right and went dark, flashing back into view every 5-10 seconds all the way until it went out of sight to the north. Along the way it appeared to curve downwards and to change speed and location several times. It was kind of beautiful :-)
