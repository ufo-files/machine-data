At around 1:00am I was relaxing in my backyard alone laying by my pool. I remember thinking that the clouds looked rather odd that night and they, themselves appeared to be moving. The sky then cleared up and I began to notice multiple white orbs traveling through the sky. At one point I counted up to 6 orbs. The orbs were hovering and/or traveling through the night sky. At times, they seemed completely in sync. At other times, they appeared erratic. I pulled out my out-dated iPhone 6 and began taking video. While all the orbs that I witnessed do not appear present on the attached videos, two of the orbs appear incredibly clearly. Perhaps someone skilled at video enhancement can play with the footage to potentially reveal even more of the orbs that I witnessed that night.
