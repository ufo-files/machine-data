Hundreds of white lights flying in line formation

At 4:17 am woke up to a very long stream (contrail like) of white bright lights flying in line formation across the sky. The bright lights were perfectly in a line, but the distance between each bright light varied slightly; had a pattern that was consistent.; 2 closer, 1 further, 2 closer etc. they flew at same exact pace. No noise - quiet! I have never seen anything like it. Not sure if this could be “drones” but it was massive in length - 100’s of straight lights. I was in such awe I did not get my camera.
