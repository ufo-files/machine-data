Lights seen inside a cloud

From my rooftop I noticed a golden light inside or behind a cloud. It brightened and glowed for about 5 seconds and faded This happened about 3 times and then I started to take pictures of it. Picture is of a line of lights taken from my cellphone. Galaxy S7. At around the same time a friend saw a golden circular object streak across the sky make a sharp right turn and disappear. The line of lights was not visible to the naked eye. It has only shown up in the picture. I cannot give an estimate of distance but it was a cloudy night and this light was somehow in or behind a cloud. This entire episode lasted about 10 = 15 minutes then it was gone
