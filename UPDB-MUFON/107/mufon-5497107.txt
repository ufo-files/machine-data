{dr}While stargazing in the backyard, I looked up from my telescope to see two bright orange lights in the eastern sky.

While stargazing in my backyard on December 31, 2014, I looked up from my telescope at approximately 11:00 PM. In the eastern sky were two bright, orange star-like flares, hovering silently side by side roughly parrallell to the horizon. I initially thought it was a low-flying plane, but when I put on my glasses I could see that this clearly wasn't the case. I called through the back door to my wife to come see. As I called her, the objects appeared to drift apart, the left one (relative to my point of view going up and away while the right one appeared to go down and away. The objects dimmed as they drifted apart, until they disappeared from view.
