{rjl} Flashing/strobing/glowing light, moved erratically for a minute, strobed and moved.

I was in a car backing up out of the garage at the address when a bright light caught my attention. Pausing in the driveway, I looked up to see a bright object (unknown if spherical, extremely bright and starry) to the North. Pulling out of the driveway, the object crossed the street heading westward and began to flash/strobe. It paused momentarily before wobbling. Pulling into the street and then heading southbound, I stopped one block away (to prevent holding up traffic) and stepped out from the car to record the object. Fiance also stepped out of car to record as well. Recording it, it bobbed and weaved momentarily. After recording it briefly, traffic in area picked up so returned to vehicle. Looking into driver-side mirror, object them flashed brightly and moved behind trees after moving up slightly. After it got behind the treeline I lost sight of it and did not see it again.
