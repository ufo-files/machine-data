Flash of white light & orange orb followed us along the coast

I was driving with a friend of mine headed to Ellsworth, which is 15-20 minutes from where I lived in Sullivan. We left my house at 6:00pm. It was October so it was already dark out. The weather was clear, but chilly. It was around 45 degrees. The sky was clear, and the stars were out. We had driven about 2.5 miles when out of nowhere a very bright flash of white light blinded us. When it did it felt like a surge of electricity went through us at the same time. We were surprised and unsure of what had just happened, and a bit scared. We were still driving, and about a 1/4 of a mile up the road we are driving by the coast. We were about 15 feet from the ocean. The road is directly next to it. Sullivan is right on the coast of Maine. I looked to my left, and hovering over the ocean at about tree top level was a glowing orange orb. This orb was much bigger than a basketball. We couldn't believe what we were seeing!! There were no other cars on the road at that time. The orb seemed to follow us hovering over the treetops, and over the ocean for the next 3-4 miles. Once we were away from the coast a few miles, it was gone! We stopped the car to calm down. 

The next day we headed back to Ellsworth. In the exact same place, it happened again! The flash of white light blinded us. The feeling of electricity went through us again! This time we immediately stopped the car and turned around and went home. We did not want to find out if the orb was going to appear again. Now years later, I wish we had not turned around. I wonder if the orb would have appeared again.
