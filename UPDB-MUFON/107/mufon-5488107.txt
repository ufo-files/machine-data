{cak} Saw unusual lights from objects that made no sound.

8:32 pm I was at Carson and Main Streets at the time. Facing South at the stop light. Lights coming from the direction of the Torrance Airport (from my view) were a series of lights (8 in all), reddish orange in color, small in size (a little larger than a normal small plane1s light). No flickering at all in the lights. Their movements were in a ENE direction at about 200 1 250 knots. Altitude: Approximately 1,500 to 2,000 ft. I saw the lights for a period of 45 seconds then they all, one by one, looked like the lights were covered up. Other option on the lights were that they were forward facing and when they went overhead, they stopped showing because the objects were now flying away from my present location.
