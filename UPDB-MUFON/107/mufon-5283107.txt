went through pics taken of the skydiving team at Balloon Fiesta 2021 and found a triangular object

Had my Nikon camera with a hood and 24mm lens on a tripod for the balloon glow that night. At the end, the Team Fastrax Skydiving show started where 4 skydivers with parachutes fly down while releasing fireworks. I had the wrong lens on for taking decent photos and gave up after only 8 shots. I didn't really look close at them until now because I am getting some pictures together. I saw a video from last year where the plane that dropped them off kept flying back and forth. I think I possibly caught the plane in the first photo or possibly a satellite or 2. I had my exposure at 4 seconds for the first picture and changed it to 1/2 sec for the other 7 photos. My f stop was 3.5 on all the photos. The last picture I must have bumped the tripod because there are multiple layers of the subjects. Number 6 & 7 have some consistent red objects on the picture, but I think it is glare on my lens from the many lights that were illuminating multiple things...(sometimes cars would turn their headlights on). The center glob of lights are the skydivers with their fireworks. On the second picture there is an object/light on the left side of the picture. As I zoomed in the picture I noticed my 24 mm lens distorts the light on the edges of the pictures. It looks triangular, but I am not sure if it is a trick from my lens or not. Either way I know it is not a skydiver because they are all in the middle. And if it was the plane it would still be in the picture that I took 7 seconds later. It could not be the sparks that the divers were giving off because they were to the right of this object. Whatever it was was moving very fast because the streak it left behind it was just a 1/2 second exposure.  I am just a hobbyist photographer but I take everything manually and knew I wouldn't be able to photograph the divers like I wanted so I quit. I will include all 8 pictures...the 2nd and 3rd pics are at the same spot, the 4th & 5th are at the same spot, the 6th & 7th are at the same spot. The time stamp on each photo is as follows: 7:24:37, 7:25:13, 7:25:21, 7:25:29, 7:25,37, 7:25:46, 7:25:53. 7:26:46 The last picture I am submitting, I zoomed in (on the computer) on the unknown object for a close up. The only other logical explanation would be a drone. Although drones were not supposed to be on the field, some news stations had special permission. I am not sure what to think since I believe a drone cannot move that fast and would still be in the picture 7 seconds later.
