A sphere that changes direction and evaporates - army plane next to it

There was a soccer game on TV, one of my family member screamed.
I ran to her room and by the window we could see a sphere.
Then it started to move and did a "right left right" (45 degree) movement.
Then it accelerated and evaporated.

I could see a plane (army) next to it but it's speed was really low.
There is a military base next to the town in Mont Ventoux.
