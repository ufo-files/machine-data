Two objects fly close to each other, then away from each other, then close again circling each other.

My wife, me and the kids had just come home from my parents around 9:00 pm or so. When I exited are vehicle, I saw two flying objects right over the field in front of our house. I yelled over to my wife " What the hell is that". My wife and the kids quickly exited the vehicle and begin staring at the two objects. All four of us were left speechless staring into the sky. These two objects were flying around each other in circle formation. Then they would fly apart at the speed of light. They would start fling in there own circles and then straight up and down. Out of nowhere these two objects would fly at lighting speed back together. they would hover and go in circles. Shot up and down in the sky and apart again. This went on for along time. We were outside for at least an hour and this didn't stop. Finally they just went away. I walked inside with my kids for just a quick minute and when I walked back out they were gone. While we were outside watching the two objects, two planes moving very fast seemed to be heading towards the two objects. They might have been two commercial planes giving an illusion of moving fast and towards them. I thought they were government though. Not sure.
