Triangle craft with orange glowing tail viewed after bright flash in sky then immediately disappeared.

I was taking the trash can to the end of the driveway about 10PM on the evening of Aug 26. We live in a typical suburban Columbus subdivision, so the driveway is not very long. When I got to the end of the driveway, there was a sudden flash in the sky that lit up the whole sky like heat lightening. When I looked up, I noticed a triangle shaped object with a glowing orangish tail high in the sky. The object seemed to be only visible for about a second before it disappeared. The object didn’t seem to be moving quickly, but that could be because it was so high in the sky. It also didn’t make any noise. I’ve never seen anything like it before. It may have been a meteor burning up, but the object seemed too perfectly triangle shaped. I’ve seen plenty of shooting stars and it definitely wasn’t that. Color was very different and it didn’t streak across the sky. Also seemed much larger. Included is a crude mock-up.
