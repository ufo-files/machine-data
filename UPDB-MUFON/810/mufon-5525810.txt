Eight Triangular Bright Orange Lights Over Barrington, IL

Just before a July 3rd fireworks display in Barrington IL there were 8 triangular shaped bright orange lights up in the sky. They moved slowly across the sky and lasted about 8-10 minutes until they started to fade away. It was an unbelievable sight! At first we all throught they were airplanes in the distance flying together to see fireworks but as they grew closer we realized that they were just bright orange lights. It was kind of creepy.. looked as if they were a scene out of "Independece Day" as if they were getting ready to come say Hi from Outerspace!!! It was AWESOME!!! Upon our arrival home from the fireworks (approx 10:40pm)and the complete opposite direction where we first saw the 8 lights.. we saw 2 additional orange lights that where right behind one another and faded out together. It was quite the sight!!!! I did happen to get a picture but I have an old iphone and the pic is a little blurry but at least we have one!!!
