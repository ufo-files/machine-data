At the intersection of Valley Parkway and Hidden Trails, just before 7pm I saw in the distance to the south, two large bright lights, high in the sky. They looked unusual so I kept an eye on them as I drove to 1868 N. Broadway. The lights appeared to be moving north. There were no red lights near them like you would see on a helicopter or plane. By the time I got to the parking lot on corner of Vista & N. Broadway the lights were over my head. I got out of my car and looked almost straight up at them. They appeared to be hovering just in front and over me as if to look at me. I assumed it was a very large drone. All I could see and hear were two large bright lights and a slight swooshing sound. I tried to focus on seeing the blades of the drone or whatever was around the lights but it was just blurry and I could not make anything out. I thought that it was either the blades causing distortion or a cloaking of some sort but dismissed the cloaking idea to a wild imagination. I was late for a meeting so I turned my back on the lights and headed towards the buildings. As I turned away from the lights they also turned and started heading west. I looked back a few times before buildings blocked my view and could see the lights heading west. I made a mental note to tell my husband about the experience but we were busy and I didn1t think about telling him until a few days later. The experience was not scary, just very strange.

Called Escondido police and they said they have not flown their drones in a while so it was not them. Suggested it might be SDG&E checking lines. Called them too, they do not use drones. If it was a drone it had to be extremely large and bright to be seen from the initial distance I first saw it at. To my knowledge, no one else saw them.
