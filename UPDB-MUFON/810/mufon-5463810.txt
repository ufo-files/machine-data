I was filming the sky and planes in partwith a Panasonic 90x camcorder zoomicular. When I sensed something dark passed in front of the plane I was filming. Upon reviewing the footage I then discovered it was a flying fish or creature mimicking a fish

Herne Hill is located SE of London. There have been many observations there of anything from orbs to traditional UFOs through cryptic beings and creatures. On the 5th of October 2018 I was filming planes and other sky objects with my 90x zoom Panasonic camcorder when upon reviewing the footage I spotted this extraordinary fish looking creature flying or swimming at 1 mile altitude. It's a short film but I have managed to slow it down and extract pics. It was the third sighting of a flying fish in 2018 at this spot.
