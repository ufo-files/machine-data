Spiining object seen over Manchester, UK

The object I saw was quite high at cloud level and was slowly moving north. 30 seconds earlier I witnessed an airline in the circuit for Manchester airport traveling east at around 1000-3000ft. I live directly under the flight path for the airport so I see many aircraft during the day.

The object was rotating and the sunlight was reflecting off it every second as it rotated. The object was moving faster than the clouds and changed it's direction to the north east, then to the east before returning to a northerly direction. I immediately discounted a balloon as it changed direction and was moving faster than the cloud. I estimated it's speed at around 50 mph.

It had no wings or tail and although it was quite high I had a good look at it. The sighting lasted for 3-4 minutes before it went into the cloud and I lost sight of it.
