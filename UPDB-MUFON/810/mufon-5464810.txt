I was sitting on the second floor balcony of a condo in Sedona, Arizona around 10 pm. I saw a small orange orb about the size of a golf ball approach in the sky from my left and stop. I remember glancing away for just a second to reach for a potato chip. When I turned back, the orb was gone.
