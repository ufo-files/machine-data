Black strange shape

I was in Portsmouth I had just finished work and for some strange reason something caught my eye moving very slowly west from the north . I called two people that were there to look at the object, we were all saying what is it as did not move like a plane, helicopter or ballon it stopped and hoovered for a good 5 mins before going vertically up and disappear into the clouds . I took photos of object and a few weeks later was watching mufon tv program and freaked out when saw video clip of an object in chile flying over a volcano looked like it was the same object.
