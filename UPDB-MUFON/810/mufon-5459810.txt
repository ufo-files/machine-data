{rjl} Ground shaking boom after seeing bright lights

I was driving down Kiamesha Lake in Monticello, NY. When I looked up at the sky, I saw two lights flying and then stop. It was like hovering or wiggling around in place. Picture kids playing with two jelly beans. Then the two stopped and they flew off towards Broadway in Monticello. It didn’t leave a trail. It was like throwing two baseballs with one baseball further ahead. At first I thought it was a shooting star but I’ve never heard of a shooting star doing that. It just didn’t make sense. It was weird and I was scared. The next day there was an article about people feeling the ground shake and hearing a loud boom. I’m not sure if it made a noise because I was listening to music. I remember my boyfriend being in the car and I could have sworn he saw it too. I remember talking to him about it but when I bought it up today he said he doesn’t remember. He said he would remember something like that but he does remember me telling him about the article. Maybe I wasn’t with him. Maybe it was my sister but I could have sworn it was him.
