(GS) Appeared like a star in the sky just hovering above a school. Drove so I was directly under it and noted the 3 colors. Took off North changed colors to blinking Red.

Note: This is my 2nd report to Mufon regarding the area. Almost exactly a year apart of the first sighting. 

Was driving South towards home, looked up to see a star like object low to the ground. appeared to be close and couldnt possibly be a star. I came up to it as it hovered over a school on the road. When directly under it the white star like beam was diminished. Triangular in shape with a different color at each point. No sound. Maybe 100 or so feet directly above me before it disappeared while I attempted to get out my camera phone. Appeared approximately a half mile away, same colors before changing to a solid red before blinking red. I had turned around and was facing North at this time. I called my mom who lives in the area it had gone. 2nd witness. She acquired a grained camera phone photo. She watched it as it changed direction several times, I could no longer see the object. 3-4 more came and joined with the lone ufo. All hovering above the ground now at approx a mile above. They circled each other for a bit before moving off. Total observation time 1 hour. 

Can send photo upon request.
