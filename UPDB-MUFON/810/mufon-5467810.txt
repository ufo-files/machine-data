I was sleeping along with my parents on my roof top. We saw these two saucer like objects emitting white light and moving rapidly from one end of sky to another. It felt like they were moving in space with mountains below as boundaries. Villager too saw it and they came to me. They thought it was some sort of a flying ghost. They also told me that they have been regularly sighting this saucer like thing above the forests.
