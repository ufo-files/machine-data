{kt}Green blurred orb comming out of bay onto sand/ erratic movement

My husband and I moved to North Beach in Corpus Christi around four weeks ago.  I am retired and am outside 70% or more of a 24 hour day. We live right on the water front, and I must say, this place has some seriously odd things going on/around. First sighting witnesses in the air around the 4th. or 5th. of this month blew my mind, but this one is disturbing. 

A very bright green orb came out of the water on to the sand, then flattened itself. (im insane, right?) Yes, it flattened. This highly odd sight then ventured at speeds I can hardly explain, raced down the shoreline, around the sand, over a public beach restroom, back to it original spot, then disappeared back in the water. Did it again after about 3 minutes. Never witnessed it again after that, and I stayed on my waterfront patio quite some time after that, looking, as you can imagine. Two sightings in two weeks. The aerial was an unknown shape; quite distant, although noticeable purple, red, green, yellow, lets just say "rainbow" lights running around the shape of it. My husband actually witnessed that one. I know I am not going mad. Im beginning to feel this is a highly active area here by the bay. I just wish someone could explain the water Orb" to me. I know what I witnessed. I just cannot wrap my head around it. And my feelings on this?  Wish someone else could have witnessed this. Highly anxious to see it again.
