Last night at 8:45 pm, I saw a spherical object with lights that appeared to be spiraling cross my field of vision. When I first noticed, it was directly in front of me moving North east. I watched for 5-7 seconds and it had no wings, sound or trail. I estimate it was at 10,000 - 20,000 feet of altitude and travelling 500 mph or more. I ran for my camera and began taking a video about 15-20 seconds after I first saw it. By this time, it was to the extreme north east. The Camera was fully zoomed in and I tried to crop out parts of the video where nothing was visible and also zoomed in on my computer. This was not a plane, nor satellite. The sky was till very bright and I cannot explain what I saw. I have attached both the cropped and original video.
