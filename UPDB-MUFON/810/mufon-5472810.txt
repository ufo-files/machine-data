Something fired at obect, which ascended at great speed

I went out at around seven pm for a smoke, whilst standing looking east I saw an object moving slowly heading east. I did not take much notice as I thought it was an aircraft , then from my left I saw two objects going towards the first, then two fast what I would call bolts came from the two objects on the left towards the object heading east. the object heading east looked like it was going to be hit by the bolts? but it ascended with amazing speed straight up. The other two objects then proceeded on there course left to right, and disappeared. was I seeing things it has bugged me for two years.
