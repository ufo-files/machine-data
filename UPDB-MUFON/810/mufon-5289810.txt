I was 14 yrs old waiting for the bus to go to school with my friend when we saw a large sauce hovering over the bridge in Long Beach.

Took report over the phone from a very talkative witness who has no email at MUFON SoCal on 2/4/2021.He did not remember the date.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We were on the corner of Pacific and 7th street in Long Beach waiting for a bus to take us to school. We noticed a very large saucer shape hovering over the big
bridge near the naval base. The saucer shaped craft just hovered over the bridge to terminal island, and every now it would move in a different direction but basically staying in the same spot, just moving higher, lower or to side to side.
Then four military jets came in from over the water and whooshed in toward the craft, but the craft made no moves until the jets were almost on top of it. It then shot off out of sight with the jets chasing it.
Somebody must have see this happen, it was rush hour on a school day in Long Beach.
