Its a bright pulsating light, with light within light..a ring of fire.

I noticed what seemed to be a bright star in the daytime, I had been looking outside my door when I noticed the object. Almost daily this object can be seen from my front yard, it also comes at the same time every time. I decided to look at the object through a telescope. After viewing it from my telescope it was apparent to me and my family that it was no ordinary object. I feel this is a site that I have never experienced before. Every time I lose site of it, it descends below the tree line.
