{rjl} Bright light, moved quickly away

While driving east on W Columbia Ave in Battle Creek MI, approacing the airport I noticed and extremely large bright light in the wooded area across from the airport. I feared a plane was about to crash into me, but the light just stood in place for a moment before it quickly moved away as I watched. It was not high in the air, if anything just barely above the trees about 50 feet in the distance. The level of light was immense.
