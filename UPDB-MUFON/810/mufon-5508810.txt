{cak} 2 star-like objects (a llittle bigger) moving together mosthly in straight line N-NM at time they would vary speed and come closer to each other. Disappeared after about 15 minutes.

I was at my condos pool laying down soaking the sun about 10 am facing west. I noticed bright lights up in the sky and thought they were airplanes at first. There were 2 bright star-like objects against a dark blue sky ( I was wearing Polaroid glasses that darkened the sky). Objects were bigger than normal stars, almost like a bright morning star. The apparent distance between the 2 of them, was similar to the distance between Castor and Pollux (the 2 bright stars in the twin (Gemini) constellation). They were steadily moving in a NW path and would seem to vary speed slightly slow down or speed up) and come closer and then move apart from each other. I kept taking my glasses off to see if I was seeing things but they were still there. I watched them for about 15 minutes and then they disappeared in the distance. I don't know what it was.....but remember seeing something similar in the Gila Wilderness about 20 years ago. That time was 3 objects and were moving erratically then they disappeared....4 days later we heard a vibrating sound overhead, my wife was 5 months pregnant, after that, she started miscarrying and had to take her to the hospital. She almost died, doctor never found fetus....she thinks "they took it".
