6-8 orange lights in a line

I had just finished work and was talking with a co-worker outside when i first noticed a string of orange lights emerge from the clouds to the north. The lights maintained a north/south flight direction but changed their orientation and shape several times. Several of the lights traded places with each other before forming a chevron shape and passing over head. The lights maintained a constant speed and were only visible for 30 to 45 seconds due to cloud cover. The only time the objects appeared to change altitude was when the lights traded position with one another. My first inclination was that they were perhaps seagulls reflecting street light, but the chevron shape they formed flew the wrong direction for a flock of birds with the two points of the chevron as the leading flight edge. It was fairly quiet at the time and there was no noise to report. The lights did not strobe, and appeared to maintain the same brilliance, with no one light more brilliant then the next.
