At approximately 2130hrs I was walking from house to garage. I have always been a sky watcher.The blinking and rapid movements of three objects immediately was clearly apparent. All three moving,stopping, one to the west,one to the east, one north, also changing altitude. I was certain immediately these were ufos. not any aircraft,flare,etc.VERY fast movement. Each disappeared from its individual point in the sky. I experienced some type of Revelation,Certainty,Calming emotion.At one point I removed my glasses to confirm this wasn't a reflection or glaring of some sort. I'm not sure WHY I did that because I knew it wasn't.For a brief moment I got concerned about what I would do if this event was some type of attack. This is the first time I have witnessed an ufo (3). After they all were gone I remained outside to keep looking for them, but never did see anything odd again. Through-out the night I kept coming outside looking, but nothing. I heard no police sirens, no emergency TV alerts, nothing on the local late news. The next day, 5/2/2018 I called the command duty officer on MCAS CHERRY POINT and reported this event. I don't know why I didn't call immediately, except that after they were gone.. I felt calm, no sense of danger. I did report on 5/2 because it happens the base is having a large air show this weekend, 5/4-5/6/2018. I felt like that was a 'duty'.
