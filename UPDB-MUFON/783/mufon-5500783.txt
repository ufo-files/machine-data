{rjl} SUBJECT WAS OUTSIDE AT 3AM WHEN HE NOTICED SOME STARS DARTING AROUND IN THE NIGHT SKY

ON JULY 19, 2014 AT 3AM, SUBJECT WAS OUTSIDE WATCHING THE SKY, WHEN HE SAW STARS MOVING AROUND IN THE SKY. ON FURTHER OBSERVATION THEY WERE ORBS AND WERE YELLOW AND DARTING AROUND THE BIGGER SHIP. THE BIGGER SHIP WAS DARK AND DIDN'T SEEM TO HAVE A SHAPE, THE SMALLER ORBS WERE GLOWING ORANGE AND THEIR REFLECTION LITE UP THE BIGGER SHAPE, BUT IT HAD NO LIGHTS OF ITS OWN. THE SMALLER ORBS SEEMED TO BE "LEAKING LIGHT" ONE OF THE ORBS CAME WITHIN A 100 FEET OF THE SUBJECT AND HOVERED. SUBJECT COMPLAINED OF BEING DIZZY WHILE THE ORB WAS NEAR HIM, BUT OK ONCE IT LEFT. SUBJECT WATCHED FOR TWO HOURS UNTIL THE LARGER SHAPE AND THE ORBS SHOT STRAIGHT UP INTO THE SKY AND DISAPPEARED.
