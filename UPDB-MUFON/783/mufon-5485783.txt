{rjl} Im witnessing a blue/white flashing lights with yellow center. It seemed to be holding a line of 8-9 blue dots/orbs that descended to earth

From my kitchen window I observed a cross like/winged craft hovering about 800ft up NE of downtown Eugene Or. For approximately 24 minutes it hovered with a steady yellow center light and flashing blue and white lights on the left and right sides. There was a "line" that was holding 9-10 blue lights/orbs almost equally spaced in a straight line to the ground. Because of city noise, I was unable to tell if it made noise or any sounds. The small blue orbs didnt flash but shown like stars in an almost straight line the entire time. It seemed to be 3-5 miles from my location. I first observed this incident at 1:09 a. m. Monday morning 1-18-2016. It lasted approximately 24 minutes. I do not know if it appeared before 1:09 am. I cannot venture a guess as to whether is was a UFO or some kind of government vehicle/craft. I have never seen or heard of anything like it before.
