{rjl} Oval shape or sphere shape self illumination moving slowly across the sky

On the evening of September 16th 2018 I was in my back yard watching my chickens free range when an object in the southwest caught my eye. To the naked eye it looked like a very short light saber or illuminated frisbee flying very slowly from right to left. I used the half moon fully visible in the south as a reference in my video. I filmed the object for 1 minute and 30 seconds, and from another angle for about a minute or so. It was not blinking any light like a conventional aircraft would do. It did not move up or down. It stayed at the same level as it moved across the sky. There was no apparent contrail or wings. I have absolutely no idea what this object could have been.
