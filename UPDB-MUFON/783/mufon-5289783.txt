Saw an object travelling south over city of Calgary at night; object disappeared over centre of city

Objecti at first was assumed to be an airplane due to flashing of lights at regular interval; object disappeared over centre of city while traveling south; myself and one other witness experienced the sighting; first thought as an explanation was that it disappeared as it passed over clouds which blocked its view. Problem with that explanation was that neither I nor other witness thought were any clouds in that area of the sky meaning that we regarded the sky over the city to be clear
We did not have any explanation for why an airplane would suddenly turn its lights off while travelling over a major city. 
so we regard it as unexplained.
