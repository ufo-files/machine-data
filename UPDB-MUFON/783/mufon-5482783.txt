{rjl} Red light hovering. Flashing a blueish white light. Stayed about 5 minutes before filming

Was at a BBQ party. Around 9:50 pm on June 18 2016 when people observered red light flashing blueish white hovering to our east. Observed it for approximately 5 minutes before taking out my cell phone and recording it. Hovered for about 45 seconds after I started filming then it abruptly began to take off in a straight trajectory to the east from our location. Lost it when it passed the trees on the horizon. Very strange and 100% silent. Still pondering what I saw.
