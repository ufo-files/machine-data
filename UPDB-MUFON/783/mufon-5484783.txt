{nd} A long line of red/orange light with solid round spheres.

I was outside late at night with my dogs again, in my backyard. And I was looking at the stars on another clear sky. I looked up and east and saw a line of strange red/orange transparent light with what looked like three solid spheres. One on each end and one in the middle of the long line of light. The objects were so high they were either in orbit or a low orbit. They were moving slowly.
