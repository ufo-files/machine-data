An intense neon.like glowing red&gold object 40 ft. - 1500 ft.high- no noise

observed an intense glowing neon- red with gold object coming towards me from the west approx. 1500 ft. up. The glow was so intense it obscured most of the craft with exception of a peak-like top. It emitted no sound and passed directly overhead as i stood in my driveway. It was moving at approx. 200 - 250 miles an hour. It disappeared slowly in the east. Approx. 10 minutes later a police helicopter was observed travelling towards the direction the object was last seen.
