I was sitting in my living room and noticed a lot of lights in the distance. Looking out my window I thought it was a plane but noticed it was very rectangular and had too many lights to be a normal plane. I believe it had a combination of red and white lights. There seemed to be a pattern of sorts on the body of the aircraft. The first thought that came to mind was the visor on Lamar Burton's character from star trek. Except stack them making it a double. ..it's really hard to describe I will try to draw it. How it even got my attention is the fact that it was flying below the tree line. From where I normally sit I could see the ufo in a lower part of my window than normal olanes. I have been feeling very paranoid since I have seen it. There have been more airplanes and helicopters coming around my house. ..more than usual. I feel they may be coming for me or my son. This sighting is happening within a year of me being hospitalized for a random one time event of extreme virtigo. My 3 year old son last month was looking up in the sky during the day and started shouting "stars" and took off running as if chasing something. ...I could not see anything. So I am becoming very worried I feel like they searching for us.
