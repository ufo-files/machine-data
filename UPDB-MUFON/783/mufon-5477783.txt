A stationary black orb with brilliant silver crescent seen .

On Friday 24th did anyone else see a bright silver balloon about 1135 ?. I was waiting for a dellivery at work and was watching overflights with my binoculars , a B787 Nor , had just gone towards Gatwick and two westbound trails were almost overhead , has I focused on the larger aircraft , a B747 prob Lufthansa I saw a bright flash near to the jet , I was able to steady the binoculars against the door frame of the workshop , I saw a small black sphere or orb with a brilliant silver crescent like a new moom on the sunward side . The object was lost for a few min while I looked at the airliners but was in the same place when I looked back at the same place , I was able to get a clear sharp focus on the silver crecent but one of the many clouds coverd the object and I did not see it again when the clouds had passed . I have seen several orange weather balloons but never one like this despite the high windspeed on Friday the object appeared to be rock steady in position . My workmate could not see the object with the naked eye . Poss some sort of unmaned UAV observation system ?
