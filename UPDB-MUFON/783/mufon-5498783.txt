{nd} Observed ufo over ocean which disappeared over the horizon. Have video

Went out on deck of rental house at 12:15am which overlooks the ocean in N. Myrtle Beach, SC. Saw a large diffuse circle of light with a more defined center which was about 1/2 the diameter in width. I have never seen anything like this. It was a clear night with moonlight. No clouds. Object was about 15 degrees above the horizon. Went in house to get binoculars and then saw more individual lights as in a ring around the bottom of the object. Went back in house to get HD digital video camera. Object had moved higher in the sky and wasn't as large to see. Videoed the object for 47 sec. with close up shots. I was excited and amazed as I had never seen anything like this before. Stopped videoing to observe again with naked eye at which time it receded from view and disappeared over the horizon.
