{jd} Glowing multiple lights, was one object, going up and down fast

my wife and I stept out at 1050pm to get some air. We both noticed to the west a glowing sphere. It was producing white, green, red, and blue colors in a rapid way. It hovered for about a minute then started going up and down rather fast. It at times disappeared then reappeared fast. A aircraft moving to the north appeared and the sphere vanished. Once the aircraft left our view it reappeared . The sighting lasted about five minutes, and vanished. My wife and I were dumbfounded at what we saw, and felt lucky to see it, and talked about it for some time.
