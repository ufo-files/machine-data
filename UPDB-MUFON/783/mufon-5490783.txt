{jpg} Solid white light moving across the sky with no sound.

My wife and I walked out to our front porch because she thought she heard thunder but it turned out, she just heard my surround sound system. We walked down our walk and looked up into the sky to see if it was clear and it was. We then noticed a solid white light with no sound, moving across the sky in a direction that differs from the flight paths we have noticed in our area. The object traveled from the west to the east. We viewed the object through the trees in our front yard. I ran inside to get my phone to try to video. My wife stayed outside and watched the object until I got back. I could still see the object when I returned and lifted my phone but nothing came through on my video. We then lost track of the object behind the trees so I ran to the road where the sky is wide open but could not find the object again. My grandson said the internet had gone out at the same time we said we were seeing the object. We felt like we saw something out of the ordinary and it was not a plane in our minds.
