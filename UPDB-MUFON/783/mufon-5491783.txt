Orb flying around, when photo taken with infrared you clearly see not an orb but a disc

I was in my yard and I saw an orb flying around...it was mid day. It was zipping around the sky .....it was bright which was noticeable in the blue sky . I switched my camera to infrared because I was not able to see it with my phone.....only on infrared . I put my hand into a fist to block the sun , that's what that black blob is....I snapped the photo and there you have the small shaped disc . It hovered very low above me and just hung there . It was large like bigger than a city bus
