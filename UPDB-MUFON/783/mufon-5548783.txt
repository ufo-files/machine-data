Saw a silver disc hovering above the mt. just east of my location

I was heading south on Hubble Blvd from La Posada out In Spanish Springs when I saw a silver disc hovering east of my location. As I heading south on Hubble the object did not move, but it appeared to drop behind the Mountains as I got closer. So I turned around to go back and get onther look at it and it was gone. It was not a plane because it never moved from it's location. I went back the next day to see if there was something up on the Mt. that was reflecting the sun, and I did not see anything.
