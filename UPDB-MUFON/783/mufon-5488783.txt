Disc-shaped object, unlike anything ever reported.

One night, I was sitting on the steps of our front porch. There was a blackout, it was unexpected, it's not on the news or the paper. As I was looking out, I saw something totally unexpected, a shiny, possibly reflective, disc-shaped object, the color of silver. It appeared in a strange way, like a popup on a computer. It had two lights on the TOP of the object, but they were so bright, they could be seen on the underside. They illuminated the sky, and glowed like an angel. The one on the left was red, and the other was yellow. It had four cup-shaped landing gear on the bottom. While most reports of saucer-shaped craft have domes on top, mine has a cone on top, resting at the apex. It was red, with prison cell-like bars surround it. Then something unexpected happened: the red portion detached itself, revealing a separate, pyramid shaped object. It had a white insignia on it. It flipped over (it was upside-down) then positioned itself beside the main object. It shot numerous yellow rectangular-rod shaped missiles at the ground, then disappeared. The missiles seemed to disappear on impact. No effects were observed at the alleged landing sites.

The main object then rotated, slowly at first, then faster, faster than I could imagine. It glowed brighter, and brighter as well. It then shot itself at the sky at tremendous speed,leaving a golden fairy-like contrail in its wake. Seconds later, the power came back.

I have a question: did the UFO cut off the power just to reveal itself?
