{rjl} I first witnessed an object emitting or engulfed in flames free falling downward then simultaneously split into four smaller fiery pieces.

I was driving on I-75 S at around 10 pm on the night of 7-13-13 when I was nearing the Franklin exit, I noticed an object which was either engulfed in flames or emitting flames, free falling, it seemed, directly downward in a straight line. I would estimate it being 1 to 2 miles high. It was night time and I could not make out the outline of whatever the craft was but I could definitely see red flames. I continued slowly down the exit ramp while observing this object. When the object got down to approximately 1/2 to 1 mile above ground it simultaneously split into four smaller fiery "pieces" which continued downward and after 3 or 4 seconds all four fiery objects simultaneously disappeared. 
At this point, I had reached the end of the exit ramp, stopped and waiting for the light to change and I thought the event was over. Just then my passenger, whom I was taking home, said "look over there!" I looked to my right and what looked to me to be about 1 mile away and maybe half a mile above the ground and directly over the town of Franklin were four hovering white orbs in a group and they were moving up and down independently of each other. At this point my traffic light was green and I proceeded to make my left turn which then put the objects behind me so I was unable to continue observing.
I did a Google search shortly after the sighting and found one person whom reported seeing the same fiery object I witnessed. The person saw it from Kings Island in Mason, Ohio, on the same date and same time.
