Triangle of three solid black bubbles that floated and wobbled and took a path away out of visual range.

Watching kids play in front yard, looked up to see 3 triangle shapes made of 3 large bubbles. Floared like a leaf falling, different levels of altitudes. I open the front door and told the wife to come see, she did and didn't know what we were seeing. I thought maybe parachutes due to a small plane in area 5 mins or so prior and another small plane on a different path as well after that. I went and grabbed my binoculars I keep in the front closet and was able to "zoom" in. The was no strings or wires or any person hanging from the objects so no parachutes or gliders. At this point the 4 total objects now went away from us north toward Middletown or so and never lost altitude. They were out of visual range in less than 1 minute. Observed by two adults and two small under 6 year old children.
