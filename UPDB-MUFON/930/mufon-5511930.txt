My daughter called me from a baseball field to tell me that her and her friend had been watching these reddish orange lights hover and fly over them. They made no noise and pulsed. They traveled in different directions and hovered in the wind. I came to pick her up at her friends house after talking to her and we saw another one. 
These craft have been very frequently sighted in our town by many people.
