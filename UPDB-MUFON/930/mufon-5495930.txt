{cak} Driving to San Francisco I looked out of the sun roof of our car; I was reclining and I saw a silver disc shaped UFO.

My Husband and I were driving to San Francisco; I was reclining in my seat looking out of the sunroof when I saw a shiny silver disc with a dome on top and bottom of it. I immediately sat up and stuck my head out of the window and watched it for about two minutes. It seemed to hover over Coddingtown Shopping Center in Santa Rosa, CA then took off headed East. It went behind a cloud and that was all I saw. We were headed South on Hwy. 101 at the time. I just wondered if anyone else in the area saw it?
