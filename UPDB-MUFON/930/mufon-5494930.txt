observed triangular shaped object with obscured lights at each corner travelling very fast in a southerly direction at a low altitude

I was in the backyard about to get into the spa when I looked up to the sky and observed a triangular object with lights at each point and what seemed to be a shadowy body in the triangle. The object was moving as I looked up. It was moving very fast from north to south. There was no sound from the object. I lost sight of the object as it moved over the roof of my home. felt it was quite low down, not as high up as a plane but a fair height above the surrounding trees. The sky was clear and quite starry. I did not observe any other movement in the sky for the hour after the sighting. After the sighting I felt goosebumps on my skin and quite confused as to what I saw. I am still trying to justify what I saw.
