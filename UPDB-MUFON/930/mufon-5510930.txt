(GS) orange red glowing shape in sky

i stopped at the stop sign at moore rd and dean still rd , my truck was facing east . i looked right to turn on to moore rd to head south and i hit the breaks on my truck very fast i thought there was another truck right in front of me . i blinked my eyes and looked out the windshield and it was a bright reddish orange light hovering about 100 to 200 ft over moore rd . i put the truck in park and opened my door to get out and get a better look and when i got out it went up . to maybe 400 ft then the light or glow went almost out like it was now a dull glow then it went south and then east and dissapeard .when i say it was at 200 ft , i mean it appeared 200 ft from the road into the sky . im judging this because i know that the power polls on moore rd range from 40 to 50 ft in the air and it looked 4 times that distance from the ground . and as far as the distance from my truck it was about 3/4 to a mile .i have never seen any thing like it . what is strange to me is that how can something move and make no sound at all . how can something float in the air and make no sound and i be so close to it .
