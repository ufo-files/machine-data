{cak} Observed object while standing in window thru the trees. Hovered for about 5 mins then it vanished. No movement up down or sideways simply disapppeared.

I got up to have a cigarette before going to bed for the night. As I was standing there looking out the window I noticed an array of lights thru the trees. I live on top of Kelly Mountain, my nearest neighbor is 1/4 of a mile down the road and there are no street lights to interfere with my view of the sky. It struck me odd that the lights seemed to be in a pattern almost diamonded shaped and they glowed and seemed to pulsate. I watched it for a while to see if it was an airplane or something like that but it didn't move, my first thought was am I looking at an UFO? I don't know why I thought that, but it was something about the shape and the way it just sat there. It wasn't supposed to be there! While I was thinking this it(the UFO) just disappeared! It didn't go in any direction it just vanished. I do not drink or do drugs so I wasn't hallucinating. My sister asked me this morning why I didn't see the UFO last week and I told her actually I did see something strange and had told my brother in law about it but he just said "yeah right". She showed me the article about the other guy in Elkins who had also seen something so I figured I would write in too.
