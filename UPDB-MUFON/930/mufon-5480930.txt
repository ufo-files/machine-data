Laying down in my caravan and looking up through a sky light in the dark and a object flew past and then came back over our van

We were laying down on our seats in our caravan in the dark looking out of our sky light watching for shooting stars, my husband was laid oppistit side to me looking in a different direction when he first sat up saying what he had just witnessed , as he was telling me I was still looking and then that's when I saw a object that had a glow around it and looked like a oval spear !! That had a tall of light behind it , it seemed to be going straight across the sky over our window, it must have been a large object because it also seemed quite high up in the sky. The sky was very clear and lots of stars where out, also we saw several shooting stars that's what we were looking for at the time, we were very shocked and must say we have neither seen anything like this..! We have not told any family or friends what we saw and still question what we did see and have been watching the sky since. I hope this makes sence and I myself have had a vivid dream and could see the same object that I saw that night .
