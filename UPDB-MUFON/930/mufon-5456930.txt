{rjl} Saw a string of 40 lights traveling in a straight line

At 6 am this morning, I was out walking my dog. I saw a cluster of three objects fly in a straight line across the sky. They were all traveling at equal velocity. I began to watch as one after the other objects appeared from the same location over the ridgeline. Some of them would appear evenly spaced, others closer together, but all consistently flying in the same direct line of flight. I watched for five minutes as these objects traveled in a steady procession over the mountain valley that I live in. In all the objects totaled 40. When I saw them I was bewildered, amazed, fascinated, and frustrated that it was such low light and I couldn't get a good video.
