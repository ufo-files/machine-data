Bright light moving in sky that was NOT a plan or star.

I had walked out on the porch when my ex dropped off my son. I was making conversation as he hooked up my sons car seat and happened to look up at the moon. There was a bright light that I can only compare to the northern star that seemed like it was falling to the Left of the moon. The more I watched it the more I realized that it was not a star nor an airlplane. It moved fast and was not falling it was traveling southeast. It faded into a pin point withing a matter of 10-20 seconds. There was no sound. No blinking. No pattern of movement. This light was traveling fast and steady. I yelled at my sons father to look up over and over but by the time he looked up (after securing carseat) the light was already a pin point but still traveling. He also witnessed it but did not see the brightness that I did. I know we have alot of air traffic and I see planes and helicopters day and night... I have never noticed anything like this before. Was odd. I watched the light until it was gone. Lasted a total of maybe 2-3 minutes and that may be over estimated time. It happened quick.
