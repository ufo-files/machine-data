Three Objects Flying in a pattern

I was walking the dog on a bright clear day and noticed a drone fly over at several hundred feet in altitude and then hover in place. I live near a soccer complex, so drone activity is not uncommon as people practice their flying skills. As I observed the drone, I noted three whitish objects at a much higher altitude in my field of vision. I at first thought they were birds as the objects were circling in a tight pattern, but the altitude seemed too high for birds and I could not make out the shape of a bird. One of the objects seemed to take the lead while the other two followed. They continued to circle in a pattern as they drifted towards the Northwest. After a few minutes, the objects then flew away towards the Northwest in a diagonal formation at the speed you'd expect from a high flying aircraft. There was no jet noise and the circling patterns flown were too tight for fighter aircraft. I checked the Flightradar24 app and there were no aircraft in the area. The objects were too far away before I thought about trying to film them.
