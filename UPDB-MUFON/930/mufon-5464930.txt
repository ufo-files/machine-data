triangle of lights flew silently in formation

me and my three work colleagues stayed at chievley services near newbury in berkshire,uk. at roughly 8pm two of us saw a formation of lights in the shape of a triangle with three lights making the straight rear edge and one light at the tip of the triangle. it flew across our line of sight. it looked solid. it moved fast, but it was hard to tell if the object was close and small or far and large, but i feel it was close and small. it moved silently in a straight line and felt unnattural in movement and how silent it was. my friend and i were genuinely shocked and a bit confused. ive never seen anything like that in my life.
