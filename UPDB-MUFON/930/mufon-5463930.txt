{cak} orb traveled from behind cloud, outside cedarville ohio, hovered by cloud w/odd lightening effects

i was driving out towards cedarville, North on 72 from Jamestown, and just outside of town as i was aproaching the curve something caught my eye in the sky out my drivers side window. there was an object traveling out from behind a cloud, a bright orb, moving rather fast...though when i started watching it, the orb started to slow down and then came to a complete stop. the object was bright, shinning...and just sat there, unmoving. as the object sat there, the cloud started having what seemed to be electrical impulses from within the cloud. the cloud was blueish-gray in color. it was the only cloud in the area that was having these pulses. and at times, certain parts of the cloud appeared to open up like doors or windows when the pulses were flashing. i stood and watched this for at least fifteen or twenty minutes, and took several pictures of the object in the sky. the object is not the moon, or a star, as it was still pretty light out. i have a photo of the object taken at 8:23pm Est. after i left i went back the next evening to see if an object was stationary in the sky, perhaps a star, there was no object in the same area and have not seen anything similar to in that area since.
