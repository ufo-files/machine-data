Very large v-shaped craft flying north on I-90 in Massachusetts

Driving west on I-90 on a clear late afternoon I observed a v-shape aircraft flying from south to north. The object was very large. I would estimate 200 yards from end to end. After about 3-4 minutes of watching and taking pictures of the event, the object took an abrupt left turn heading west and then I lost sight as it flew over the hills in the distance.
