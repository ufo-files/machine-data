{jpg} Saw an extremely fast object in daylight overhead for less than 10 seconds

Around noon on Sunday July 26, 2015, I was reclining in a chaise lounge chair facing south in my front yard. I looked up, almost directly overhead, to see a very small reflective object traveling at a HIGH rate of speed from the south, in a direct line north. I got up quickly to get a better view, and lost sight to the north from my vantage point. All this occurred in less than 10 seconds. There were no clouds and no breeze, no other known aircraft or birds in the area. I certainly don't believe that it was a satellite. I do believe that I witnessed an unidentified flying object.
