At 10.00 am while at Kapunda in South Australia a strange black rectangular cloud formed and out of this cloud came six to ten flying objects. The objects crossed the sky from east to west at great speed and disappeared. This black rectangular cloud kept its shape for over an hour while the clouds around were constantly changing shape. Some of the objects were flying on their own, while others were flying in formation. They appeared to be disc shaped and metallic grey in colour, there were no flashing lights or sound and the objects flew in a straight flight path with no wobbling motion noted. This event was witnessed by five other people who are all willing to support this sighting. Each of the witnesses had cameras, and photographed the event. we have a large collection of photos of these objects and are only too happy for you to have copies of them if you wish.   Kind regards. Neil Kemp.
