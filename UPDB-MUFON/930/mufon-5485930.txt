{cak} Thought it to be maybe a drone or ultralight but it changed direction up and down.

I was outside in my backyard when I noticed what I thought was a bright star, it started moving toward me then changed altitude like it was coming down. I thought then, if it's an ultralight it better go up to avoid the power lines. It appeared to be circling around. Then I thought it could be somebodies drone until it got closer and it appeared to be a "V" shape with one light on it. I lost sight of it for awhile and went outside and talked to my neighbor across the street, he did not see anything. The sun was starting to come up and he told me about a half hour later that he saw the light in the area I had told him about. He said that it was in the sky then shot straight up and disappeared.
