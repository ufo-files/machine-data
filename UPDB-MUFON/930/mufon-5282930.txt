Bright light over my home zoomed out SW

I was outside my home in the back grabbing the trash cans. Something in the back of my head was telling me to look up and when I did I seen a bright white light hovering behind my home behind some trees over our creek. It creeped past and then I seen the large metallic shape, triangular with a bright white glow. It was so close I could even see the thickness and it was huge. It then flashed and zoomed off from over my home in Desoto to the Glenn Heights/Red Oak area. However this not being my first sighting at and near my home this is the first time I have seen the object in question up close over my own home as if it was watching me in particular.
