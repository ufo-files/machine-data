{cak} Very bright, round object seen in photo to left of the helicopter.

Pictures were taken of the helicopter ambulance on 02-25-19 at 12:49 pm. When going thru my photos on 03-12-19, I saw the object to the L of the helicopter. I enlarged the photo, so I could see what it was. It was NOT a weather balloon- it was round and very bright... so bright, that you could see it very well, even though there were no clouds in the sky. Looking at the object, it appeared to be slightly lower in elevation than than the sun, which was shining just to the right of the helicopter. The other photo was taken a few seconds from the other, but shows no bright object in the sky. I was not in the vehicle, so it was not a reflection off of any glass.
