Observation of a cigar and marble throw

In July 2017, I saw a burnt orange cigar coming from the west. The front of the 100-foot object was rounded, the tail was longer and there was a rod. At the end of the stem a ball of the same color. There was on the front, a kind of porthole like a smoked car window.
The object parked above the trees and the field in front of my house and threw some kind of small balls from the back. Then the object left again. He flew over a campsite that is by the water. A woman took a picture of her, but didn't want to give it to us. This woman told me and my brother that the object had disappeared above the sea like in a cloud. I had the opportunity to see a lot of things in my life. At forty years old, when I went hunting, my companion and I were dazzled by a green light. We don't know what it is. We wanted to go check the next day, but we didn't see anything.
