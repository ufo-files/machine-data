{nd} Extremely fast moving red and blue green light, changed directions and then dropped quickly to skyline

We were sitting in the back yard and my husband noticed something moving quickly over our neighbors house. We ran to the front yard and watched the lights zig zag quickly as it dropped rapidly toward the skyline. The descent was faster than any object we have ever witnessed falling from the sky. The lights were solid (not flashing)and were closer together than what would be seen on an airplane, almost right next to each other. The lights descended from about a 60 degree angle to the tree line in less than 15 seconds.
