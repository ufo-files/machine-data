{rjl} White circular light, with very defined perimeter about 1/8 the size or so of a full moon

On 4 separate occasions 8 people including myself have seen a white "orb" like object not far above the treeline, in the same 2 square mile area.
Myself and my brother saw it, clearly not a plane or aircraft I'm familiar with, seemingly far too large to be a drone, and it projected a beam much like a spotlight on a plane, but with sharp clearly defined edges.
My mother called me about 2 months after I saw it and, and was practically hysterical after her and a friend had seen 2 of them side by side. She didn't know that me or my brother had seen something similar.
After these events I mentioned it to a friend in the area, and he said he had seen it recently also. 
Its been the subject of conversation with a few people I know in the area, and just last week two of them apparently saw them. 2 of them they were going up and down from below the treeline to above.

The same night I witnessed the orb I and another brother had a sighting unlike the others. We were stargazing it was a very clear moonless night, and watching what we believed to be a satellite cross the sky when "it" made a very fast loop with another similar sized object, then disappeared. We both simultaneously stopped talking and yelled "whoa" as we were in awe.
I do alot of stargazing, I'm familiar with objects commonly mistaken for UFOs, I don't believe these are any of those
