WENT OUT SIDE AND LOOKED UP AT A HAWK FLYING OVER AND SAW A DARK CYLINDER GLOWING VERY RED IN THE SKY. IT WAS VERY HIGH IN THE SKY BECAUSE I SAW TWO JETS TRAVELING IN DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS NEAR TO IT IN THE SKY. I BELIEVE IT WAS AS LARGE AS THE JETS. WHILE I WAS WATCHING IT, IT TURNED 90 DEGREES AND I SAW THE SUN REFLECT OFF OF IT, IT WAS VERY RED IN THE CENTER AND MORE FADED TOWARDS THE EDGES. WHEN IT TURNED, IT WENT NORTH AND I LOST SIGHT OF IT. IT MADE NO SOUND AND IT WAS A CLEAR DAY.
