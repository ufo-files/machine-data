I was at the Ferry station in Visby on the island of Gotland where we live dropping off my daughter around 6:30 am. I took some pictures of the moon in the parking lot while I was waiting for my other younger daughter and her friend to come out of the terminal after she helped my older daughter with her bags. When we looked at the pictures of the moon there were some lights in them. We never saw any lights in the parking lot. So we didn't notice anything until we looked at the pictures. It could be a drone or maybe a helicopter but the lights look different than those normally look. I was curious if anybody else reported seeing the lights or there was some logical explanation.
