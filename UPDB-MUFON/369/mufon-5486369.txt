{rjl} 8 bright red lights were in a stationary formation in the sky hovering, one by one they were gone

I was driving to drop off a gift at a friends house, headed in the north east direction. I instantly noticed 8 bright red lights hovering in place in the sky in a weird diagnoal formation. I was extremely alarmed and pulled over into a church parking lot to take a picture. I got out my camera and the lights started to go away, I took a picture and got three of the lights photographed. Once I looked at my phone to send to others as proof, the picture was extremely distorted and skewed. I then got a very intense migraine that lasted over four hours.
