{cak} Star like light traveling in a straight line across the sky then meets another light

I was on my patio looking at the stars and observed a straight line traveling light the size and color of a star, that i quickly disregarded as a plane because it was higher in the sky as a plane would be. It was traveling at 5x the speed of a plane traveling to my left when i observed the same looking light traveling the same speed going right towards the original light that i saw. After coming very close to one another they spun around in a circle opposite of each other and beamed off the opposite direction they were originally headed or possibly kept traveling the same direction, i obviously couldn1t tell which was which after this occurred.
