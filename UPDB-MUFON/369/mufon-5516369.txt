{cak} light appeared high in sky as outline of teacup, then was infused with white, then green, then pink billowy/cloudlike stuff, then blinked off.

The three of us women were coming home from Lake Tahoe in a van. There was a terrible lightening/thunder storm a good portion of the way down the moutain, lit up the inside of the van several times. The storm passed and as we got closer to the valley floor I noticed how odd the sky looked. The sky to our left or south, their were a lot of threatening looking clouds, with pointed edges, and to our right, or north, there were beautiful, benign billowy clouds and I commented about it. About 20 min from grannys house, my friend who was driving was looking out her window toward the s/w and said "what is that?" I said "what is what?" as I couldnt see from the passengers side and through the trees on the side of the road. She kept saying as we continued to drive, "what is that light?", "what light?" "that light?" and so on. When we pulled into grannys driveway, we all got out and stared up into the distant sky toward the southwest and there amoung the weird looking clouds was a giant outline of a "teacup shape" light. We stared and all continued to repeat the phrase "what is that?". As we stood there in awe, suddenly as if from a corner of the outline an infusion of billowy white cloudy stuff filled the ouline, then changed to pink billowy stuff, then green, then the whole thing just blinked off. We all just stood there for several seconds trying to figure out what we saw. It was as if it had been a giant movie projection, or hologram. When I got home I called a few tv and radio stations and was told no one reported seeing anything. I made a drawing to the best of my recollection.
