3 orbs seen flying low in night sky.

Witness saw three glowing orbs in the eastern sky at 8:00 PM at Hickory Creek Dr and Harlem Ave (see first video). Objects appeared to flash red, green, and white and seemed to be flying in formation as well. Objects were noted to be slowly traveling south. Sighting lasted about 15 minutes and were recorded again from a barn at 24224 88th Ave at 8:13 PM (see second video). At this time, objects were no longer in formation.
