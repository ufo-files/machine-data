{dr}Red/Orange lights in servals areas at different times over 1 hour period before and after New Years

While on our way home at around 2330 on 12-31, we witnessed a red/orange ball hovering. We were in the area of southbound US 41 headed towards San Carlos park. We pulled over to look at it. It seemed to stop and start moving to the South. As we stared at it longer, it ascended and disappeared. Just a few minutes later, once we got home, we saw a similar anomaly in a similar area. We also saw several other red/orange balls to the East. These were grouped together. They also ascended and disappeared.
