{tjd} Was using binoculars at night. Something gray & translucent zipped past field of view. No light/sound

I have absolutely no clue what the hell I just witnessed. 
It’s a beautiful clear night. No moon so it’s there’s a nice dark sky.
I decided to break out the binoculars and kick the seat back. I was looking for Andromeda when something gray & translucent zipped past. I was like wtf and quickly found it again and followed it until the B pillar suddenly stopped me from tracking it. 
As I’m thinking back at what I seen I think there was 4 or 5 objects. These things were moving very fast. Seemed to be translucent, emitted no light, didn’t fly or glide like a bird and I didn’t hear any airplane engines. Although I did see a couple off to the north east.
This ranks up there with seeing meteors zip past your binocular view.
