I was outside my house around 9:30 p.m. having a cigarette. After a few minutes, I looked up and saw a huge, bright orange object. So bright, it appeared to be on fire. I called for my husband to look and he saw it too. He called for his nephew who was inside, to bring out his phone for video, at which point my husband ran around to the front of the house. His nephew gave him the phone and he (his nephew) had binoculars. By this time I came back around and thats when it took off south, within 20 seconds, it had dimmed to the size of a pinpoint and then disappeared all together. My husbands heart was beating so fast, you can hear him on the video out of breath.
We felt that we were witnessing a UFO. Our nephew, saw it the best through the binoculars and says it looked like a hazy, translucent sphere with a fire at the bottom of it. He was able to sketch it.
We were very very excited by what we saw. We lost sight of the object because it faded into nothing. I hope we were not the only ones who seen it.
