Two discs sighted in formation near NV test range.

On Aug 12, 2009 me and my mom were driving home from Jackpot NV heading S on the 93 to go to Laughlin NV starting at 6;00AM that morning. We then proceeded South on the 318 around 9:45-10:00 AM going 80 mph as there was nobody on the roadway at least 25 miles in either directions. At 11:15 AM I spotted 2 light grey colored objects coming from the east flying over the Irish Mt range and descending down into windshield view and stopped abruptly and hovered stationary for 6-8 seconds, one of the objects zipped behind me going North while the other one stayed in my view for 5 more seconds and came back into view with the other one as they both headed SW at a high rate of speed. When they both took off they looked like a blur speeding off and was beautiful the way they zipped around, unlike a conventional plane. The whole time this happened I tried to get my Mom to witness this but she has bad eyes so she was unable to see them. The reason I am posting this now is I was watching a video on Bob Lazar and he stated that used to test these objects on Wednesdays for whatever reason and saw what I wrote down on my map with date and time and saw it was a Wednesday. I am 100% sure these are experimental craft that fit the description of Lazars sport model as they were identical in shape and size and color.
