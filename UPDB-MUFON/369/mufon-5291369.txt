Night sky watching when I seen two lights turn on/appear very bright. Started taking pics as they began to move North(ish) until they disappeared.

It is a very clear night tonight so I decided to sky watch. Around 10:38 I seen two bright lights appear out of nowhere (as if someone turned on the lights). I began to take pictures of them. Both did not fit in the frame, so I had to take separate pics of each at first. As I took the first few pics I noticed they started to drop out of the frame. I was using a tripod tonight and had just snugged down the handle that locks it in place. So I had to loosen it to keep the lights in the frame from this point on. The lights only moved downward a slight bit and then began to travel towards the North for the most part. As they started to move they lights dimmed some. Moving North they also began to pick up speed then slowed. At this point they began, what looked like, a more upwardly flight path. As if to be going away from me (or the earth). One light faded/disappeared before the other. I kept taking pics until the other faded from sight also.
