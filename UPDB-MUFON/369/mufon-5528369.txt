Bright stationary light on cloudy night. No movement for 5 minutes.

While driving south on 127 between I-70 and Eaton, Ohio, I noticed an intensely bright object in the sky to the east. The time was 5:12 AM, with sky conditions being very cloudy with no stars or the moon visible. The object showed no running lights or anything that would make it appear to be a conventional aircraft. Object appeared to be stationary from my moving position. Once I arrived at work, the object was still visible and stationary from the parting lot. Total observation time was about 5 minutes. I lost sight of the object when I had to go inside.
