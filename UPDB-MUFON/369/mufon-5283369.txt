Glowing object flies straight and then abruptly makes a 90 degree turn.

As I approached the intersection of Marengo and Main, in Huntley, going west on Main Street, I noticed a kind of triangular shape - kind of like a child's drawing of a square with a triangle on top to represent a house - in the sky that seemed to glow rather than have lights. It was to my right, high in the sky (perhaps 40 -45 degrees) and ahead of me (northwest). I couldn't detect any movement at first but I had a red light so I looked up again and saw the object movlng south and getting nearer. It crossed my line of sight. While I followed it with my eyes I saw it make an abrupt 90 degree turn and go east. I could no longer see it and when I had the chance to turn south I couldn't find it in the sky.
