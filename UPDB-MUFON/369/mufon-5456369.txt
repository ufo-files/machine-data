{rjl} Cloaked Triangle with an orange glow on the leading edge of the craft

I was looking around the picture when I spotted the triangle craft in the sky. I moved the arrows left and right to move the google car frame pictures up and down the road
What I see is astonishing. Sort of resembles a black triangle photographed over texas
