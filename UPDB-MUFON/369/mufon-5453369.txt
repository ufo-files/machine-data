Orb of light, thought it was a star and it moved! Quick directional changes. Out of sight west in milliseconds. Had compass problems too.

I was using my astronomy binoculars to spot the recent star link train. Star walk will show you just when and where to look. Sighting to be at 10:36 - :39 pm CDT, rising in the west and setting in the south. I found them and was observing, naked eye and binoculars. When I got to the south I noticed a bight star in the view but paid no attention, the train was cool. As they were setting I thought, that’s not Sirius, Orion is setting now and it’s below that. Venus is over by the moon in the west, hum. And then IT MOVED! A quick dart to the east, not far but almost instantaneous. And with that it had my attention. A couple of quick, directional changes punctuated by stopping motionless for a short time 2-10 seconds. This went on forever (ha, seemed like it) but was only a couple of minutes. Then it took off to the west and was completely out of sight almost instantly. I waited but it didn’t come back.
Another strange thing occurred. When getting ready to observe the train I was using the compass on my iPhone to get exact bearings. The compass was wrong and it changed it’s wrongness by not being stable. I thought it was odd but I took an iOS update that day, so to recalibrate it by turning in a circle to reset the compass. I tried several times but it did not fix the problem. After the sighting, and it was gone, the compass still did not work - for more than 30 minutes! The next morning and the last two days the compass is working flawlessly. Things that make you go hum? This is my second and I feel lucky.
