My wife and I saw something in the sky while playing in the front yard with our son.

My wife and I were playing with our son in our driveway when I looked up and noticed a metallic object hovering in the sky. The object appeared to be stationary. I was able to capture it on video. It then disappeared for a minute or so and then reappeared. I was again able to locate and capture it on film. The object appeared to be metallic as it was very reflective. After a few minutes of observing the object it disappeared. However, today, June 4, 2021 I saw a similar object, or possibly the same object in the sky as I was driving into work. It disappeared before I was able to get any footage of it unfortunately.
