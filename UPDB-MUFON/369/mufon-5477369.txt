{rjl} Black, silent, angular craft went across the sky blocking out stars

So this happened last night. I had just saw a movie with a friend and didn't feel like going home so I headed to a local city overlook to enjoy the view and smoke a couple ciggs. I was only there for a few minutes before I started looking at the stars. It was a very clear night and even with the large amount of light pollution, they were quite bright. Just then, something blocked out one of the stars in my immediate view. I quickly caught sight of a black, boxey, angular craft moving completely silently across the sky. No lights, no exhaust, no noise. The only reason I was able to see it was because of the light pollution making the sky brighter than the black craft. It is difficult to speculate on the size and altitude as there were no markers, but if I had to guess, I would say it was cruising at about 250-350ft and was maybe 2-3 times the size of an average helicopter. Where I saw it wasn't straight above me, but instead out in front and up. The craft was moving pretty quickly and I only saw it for maybe 1.5 seconds.
