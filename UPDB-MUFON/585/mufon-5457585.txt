{cak} Grey disc with cloudy aura moves south

My wife and I were sitting on the back deck this night, and we both saw a dark grey disc shaped object appear from thin air over our neighborhood and began traveling south. 
There was no noise and no lights. It was dark grey and had a cloudy aura around the disc edges. The bottom of the disc was the only visible part of the craft and it was slightly parabolic in shape. And even though the disc was traveling south, the cloudy aura did not trail behind. There was no exhaust and it was traveling below the clouds. 
We watched it appear in front of us, and fly out of our field of vision past some trees.
