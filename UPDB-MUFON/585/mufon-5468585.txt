{cak} V-Formation, Seven circular star like solid lights

My buddy and I were out walking my dog around and doing some stargazing when all of a sudden we saw a V like formation of star like circular objects, approximately 6-7 of them just appeared out of nowhere and started coasting over our heads for about 7-8 seconds and just shot off towards the Andromeda constellation near the Betelgeuse star. We were a little shook up because this is the first time we have seen something like that personally, only on television. At first glance I honestly thought it was a meteor but then I noticed the formation and realized that it was in fact a U.F.O and it was incredible, definitely not something one stargazing would define as "Normal".
