{jpg} Thought I was watching satellites over Houston,TX

I'm a groundskeeper and clock in at 5am. This gives me a couple of hours to observe the night sky while working. I call my wife at 6am to wake her up every weekday and I always tell her how many satellites and meteors I've seen till that point. The morning of Dec 13, 2019 while on the phone with her I was following a satellite through the sky and realized that it was being followed by another one. The second one was about five seconds behind and a little below the first. Then five seconds behind the second was a third a little above the second. Then I got really excited while still on the phone with my wife when I counted thirteen in the same pattern. I counted and observed twenty before clouds covered the sky. They were traveling from NE to SE.
