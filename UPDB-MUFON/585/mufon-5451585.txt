Oklahoma Triangle UfO composed of three spheres

My adult son is visiting from L.A. So every night we are on the patio having a few drinks until about 3 am, catching up and watching for satellites. This morning at 2:40 am, he tapped me and said look, what he thought was a formation of birds approaching was actually a dull glowing triangle composed of three spheres.Two on the bottom and one on top. They were evenly spaced apart. It was travelling silently in a straight line from SE to NW . I state that it was glowing because there was a first quarter moon out and it could have been reflecting the moon, I am not sure but it also looked like an internal very dull light source. This was 300 to 500 feet in altitude by my estimate and if estimating size I would say no bigger than a full size pick up or van. My son watched this approach and I watched it from the mid way point until it disappeared. This was about 3 to 4 seconds , it was moving steady and fast. We both watched this event happen and this was not a drone , balloon or aircraft of any type. So I ask myself. How does a three sphered triangle fly without sound , stay on course and move almost undetected through the early morning hours ?  My answer is that it doesn't !!! because we witnessed this object and are amazed and excited. Now I can scratch UFO off my bucket list.
