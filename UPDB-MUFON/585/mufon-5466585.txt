{cak} Orange starlike light changed direction abruptly

I was observing Mars with my telescope, at about 80 magnification when I see an orange starlike light drift across my field of view, going from top left to bottom right, relative to my view. I followed it and it changed direction arcing up in about 1-2 seconds, it followed a natural curve in its turn. It was not visible to the naked eye, likely because it wasn't bright enough. With my telescope at 80x it appeared the size of a star, and its apparent magnitude was similar to Polaris'. The light wasn't blinking, and the whole observation was about 1 minute before cloud cover blocked my view.
