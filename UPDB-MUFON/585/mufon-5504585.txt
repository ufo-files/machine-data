I was working in the house and heard helicopters pass over close enough to rattle my walls and windows. After the second pass, I decided to investigate. As I came outside, I saw the helicopters just over my house from East to West and then disappear out of sight. I immediately noticed one hundred and one hundred and fifty white reflective round objects floating high in the sky over the mountain. There was no exact pattern but they never left a specific altitude range and traveled from East to West until I could no longer see them behind the building next door. These objects were very far off in the distance and not in any close proximity to the helicopters. This was witnessed by myself, my neighbor and my daughter.
