{cak} I went outside to watch the stars because I could not sleep.

I am 54 years old with 25-years aviation management experience. Last night I was having a difficult time sleeping and I went outside onto my breezeway porch for some fresh air. I often sit out there and watch the stars often looking at Venus. I thought I was looking at Venus as it was twinkling brightly as usual then I noticed it move drastically downward with red flashing lights and thought wow that must be an airplane or helicopter. Those possibilities seemed odd as I have never mistaken a star or planet for a commercial craft. I continue to watch as I seemed to hover much closer than I thought okay it’s a helicopter after 15 minutes I though no way this is a helicopter it shot straight up towards to high of elevation for a helicopter. I went inside for my eye glasses and camera phone to video. 

Whatever I was watching seemed to have bright lights swirling and flashing. I couldn’t make out the figure and finally thought it to be a drone of some sort. However, after an hour I no longer thought it to be a drone. I felt certain that I was seeing something I could not explain with my aviation experience and sent the video to a few other aviation and military friends. Most responded “that’s crazy” “what the heck is that”. Finally, one friend said he replied, “it’s a Reaper drone, they can stay airborne over 8 hours”. I felt this was the possible explanation.
