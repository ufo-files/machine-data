{dr}I looked out the window and thought I saw 2 shooting stars at first. Grabbed my cell and recorded and by that time they became 4! They looked like those 4th of July sparkler thingees at the end.

My mom had just moved into her new senior residences at St. Frances Heights yesterday and I went to visit. I originally was looking at the view from her 6th floor when I saw first 1 light and then these 2 lights that looked like they were fireworks going in different patterns high above that building. They were cone/triangle shaped at the head. At the end of each object they looked like those 4th of July sparklers continuously spraying off fire. They would be visible one second and disappear the next only to reappear. My mom and I watched them until they disappeared descending behind the nearby tall building. 

I felt very excited, shocked and fortunate watching these especially since I could not make sense of them. They seemed to go in a circular motion around one another like they were in some sort of dance. There was some sort of light noise but I can not recall what kind; it seemed pleasant. Unfortunately my cell did not capture the vibrancy of them; they were like stars or fire... The sky was dark and it was a beautiful balmy night with no clouds I could see at this time.
