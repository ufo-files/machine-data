I recorded a blue ufo using snapchat

Saw a blue disc coming towards me I pulled out snap chat and recorded it as it flew above my head silently going from right to left. It flew north on the Mississippi out of site towards saint paul and came back south with helicopters following it so I started recording again as helicopters followed it. I pieced together the snapchat 10sec in order I belive but not 100% sure each 10 second clip was in the exact same order and the put subtitles on a simple video. What was weird was the following morning there were black helicopters flying VERY low all over the town for the following weeks I saw helicopters all over the area constantly day and night and no one else ever paid attention to it.
