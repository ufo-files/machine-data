Looked similar to a star but bigger & pulsating

It was the only light visible in the sky at the time that I could see. It appeared similar to a star but bigger & brighter, & the light appeared to be pulsating or flickering, perhaps changing shape. It was difficult to see clearly with the naked eye. After I enlarged & brightened up some of the photos I was able to get a better look at the object. There appears to be some kind of aura or current running through or around it as seen in the photos. It was silent, & took about 8 minutes to travel from one side of the sky to the other.
