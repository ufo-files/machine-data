{jpg} I took a picture of the view from my driveway and noticed a strange object in the photo.

I was on my way home from taking my daughter to school when I noticed a beautiful rainbow colored cloud over the mountain range. So when I pulled into my driveway I decided to take a picture of it. I honestly did not see the object in question at that time. Two days later I was looking through my pictures and noticed a small blemish on the picture I had taken. I zoomed in on it and started freaking out at what I saw. So I did not actually witness the object but did get it in a photo. The object in question is about three quarters of the way from left to right and about center from top to bottom of the photo.
