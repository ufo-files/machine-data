Saw a large silent triangular craft with oddly distributed pulsating lights, then multiple star-like objects

Watched the skies for a number of hours. I saw multiple star-like objects traveling swiftly across the sky, changing direction — NOT shooting stars. The skies were VERY busy. I then saw a triangular/domed object I initially believed to be a plane; but, the lights were oddly distributed and it was silent. First, three yellow orange lights laid out side by side and atop, or on the side of the craft, pulsed, (looked a bit like windows perhaps) then a strange grouping of three red lights on the underside pulsed. It looked both triangular & disc-shaped.
