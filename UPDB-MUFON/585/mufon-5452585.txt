It

I was 17 years old and a senior in high school. We lived on a small rural farm with five clear acres of land behind our house. I went out at around 9:00 Pm, just before bed to check on my dog. It came out of nowhere, descending straight down out of the night sky and hovered ~ 50 - 100 yards above the center of the field. It then projected a perfect “beam”, that was ~ 5 -10 yards wide and spanned from the beginning of the tree line to the barbed wire fence. It looked like a big photocopier, projecting from the center of the hovering saucer. I was so stunned and shocked I could not move. Although I was outside of the “beam’s light” as it passed in front of me it stopped. Then suddenly the saucer darted over to me as if I had startled an animal. The beam focused on me like a flashlight, but it was perfectly round ~ 3 ft in diameter. The light was bright white, but you could see other colors in the light (like sunlight through a prism). The beam was over me for just a minute or two. It turned off and the object darted away at an approximate 60-degree angle. It never made a sound, complete silence the entire time. Scared me to death! When I went back in the house, I was so pail my mother asked me if I had seen a ghost. I did not tell a soul who would believe me anyway?
