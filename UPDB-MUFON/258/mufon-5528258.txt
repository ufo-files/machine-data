Circular shape. Three lights were visible on the sides as it rotated. Slow moving.

I was driving my three friends and myself home from Wal-Mart at around one o' clock am. My back window was fogged over, and my friend decided to wipe a bit of the fog away to look at the sky, when she noticed a circular craft above my car. She described the bottom as having lights. It followed directly above the vehicle until I pulled into the apartment complex my friend lived in, and the four of us exited the car to watch the UFO hover above the trees across the street. It was high up, about the normal altitude of an airplane, but it was clear that it wasn't ordinary. It just.. Stayed put, for the most part, and it seemed to gradually get smaller. I was startled to say the least, and we all went inside after a couple minutes of staring. We checked the window after we got inside, and it had dissapeared.
