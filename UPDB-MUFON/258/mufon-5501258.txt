{nd} Bright orange circular orb traveling slowly from S to N then disappearing

We were at the Mustang Park on the 4th of July when a glowing orange orb appeared and traveled slowly from the South to the North and then disappeared. About a minute later, we saw two more orbs, one on top of the other that took the same flight path and disappeared suddenly. We saw the exact same glowing orange orb at the exact same location and day last year as well. (Only one that time.)
