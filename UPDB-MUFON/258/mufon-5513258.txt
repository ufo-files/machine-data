{dr}Viewed a stationary red light as big as the planet Venus while looking NEat the moon.

I noticed how bright the moon was and like to take photos of it from time to time but this time next to the moon was one red glowing light as big as Venus. I thought it was a star for a few moments because it wasnt moving. I stood there for about 3 or 4 minutes and thought how odd. No flashing lights or sound. I then decided to grab my camera and take a photo of it and in less than a minute when I returned it was gone. I stepped away from the house and looked 360 degrees to see if it had moved to a different part of the sky. We get airplanes around our house all the time and I can always step outside and have time to follow them in the sky but not this time, it was just gone.
