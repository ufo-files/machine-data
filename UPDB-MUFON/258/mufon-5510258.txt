(GS) Standing on my deck

I had just finished watching the Baltimore Orioles defeat the Red Sox at about 10:30 pm. I went out to my back deck. I was looking in the direction what i believe to be NW  When I saw a red object in the distance passing over head. it did not make any noise and was quite larger as I look up it pass over and continued in what i believe is a southeast direction. I continued to watch this object for what seem like 3 or 4 more minutes as it traveled away from me and became a very small little red star like object out on the horizon. I turn around in look up and all most immediately another object of the same size shape and color was passing over head. I watched the second object for about the same amount of time. It looked like it came from the same direction as the first one.
