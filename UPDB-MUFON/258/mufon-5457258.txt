March 31st observed Alien craft

Roughly on around the 27th of March at around 18:30pm I noticed a very strange Helicopter circling around my house in Plaistow London, It was strange in that it was completely Blacked out, and it sounded like a piece of junk-yard crap , a real heavy motor, It was circling around 20 metres above my house in a radius that made apparent it was just circling around my house , It staid there the whole night . I could hear it as i slept, when Morning broke the next day at around 8:00am i could still hear it , I stepped out on to my balcony expecting to see it above me , but it was right there at eye-level, with me, at roughly around 15-20 metres away, it hovered there momentary (like it was waiting for me) then speed of directly to the west of me. March 31st, at around 19:00hrs at the same premises, I was settling down to watch a dvd when my whole studio flat lit up in a whoosh of Orange, Shocked i ran to the balcony , I stepped on to the balcony where i struck by the sight of a Bell shaped Craft , Difficult to judge the size but at a guess, the size of a 4x4 car, within the bell shape was a square orange grid of orange lights ,it hovered there like it was observing me, it looked flat (like 2 dimensional flat) i couldn't see around it it must have staid a full minute before calmly, very slowly started to ascend , I watched it float off right off into the stratosphere , it was was a fairly clear night, i could see it go right up , and then i lost sight as it exited the atmosphere, Ive given you the information about the helicopter because i believe the two events are connected, i wish to remain anonymous, but would be happy to co-operate with in any way i can.
