short unnerving experience with U.F.O sighting on my balcony

I was out of my front balcony having a cigarette and looked up at the sky.
The flashing lights caught my attention, I had a general feeling of uneasiness whilst I observed the object.

Initially I thought the object was a lightning strike but the weather was still and there were no other signs of a storm. The colors seemed a little odd and it all seemed very unnatural.

The object hovered in a sporadic manner while retaining velocity and steady pace.

I felt unnerved and that time stood still momentarily. It was a very surreal moment.

I lost sight of the object when the lights dimmed and I could no longer make the object out.
