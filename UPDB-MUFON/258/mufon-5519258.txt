chevron shaped flock of birds, too fast for birds

i was about 300 feet from my house at the barn. i heard a noise that sounded like a wind coming up, i looked up to see a v shape of white objects that at first i thought were flock of birds. they went very quickly from south to north and curved to the west, there was no sound other than a light wind sound, a swish. we have alot of geese and other birds that fly even at night but they always make bird noises and they don't move that fast. they were like white spots in the sky in the shape of a V, but 3-4 thick. geese fly in a V with 1 bird in front of the other , this was like 3-4 birds flying next to each other making a chevron shape, like seargent stripes, made me shiver when it went by. it was a very clear night with little wind and no noise. for the first few minutes i kept thinking what kind of bird could that have been , they made no noise other than a wind noise and they moved very quickly in a tight formation, and tuned to the west as they went overhead. we live north of tinker afb and i see lots of planes flying all the time. this was no plane and not a flock of birds. freaked me out!!
