{rjl} Seven strange lights either connected or synchronized in line equal distance, no noise, in the center light was white and all others were red. Middle light wasn't blinking, others were. A mile long.

January 15 my husband received a phone call at 8:21 pm from his brother that lives 20 miles away telling us to look outside in the sky. His brother is in local law enforcement and had never seen anything like this. My mother-in-law, husband, and I walked outside and saw the object heading our direction from the north east. It took the object about 1 minute to become completely visible above our house. It was a series of 7 lights following equal distance from one another heading west toward Shreveport. The lights were all blinking red except the center light, this one was a solid white light. These objects were very bright and we all could tell that it was something we couldn't explain. To me, it looked connected and it never flew by any other light source to see through or behind the objects. It never seemed threatening and my family seemed to be the only people outside to witness this experience. After the object was out of view, I called the local authorities and reported my experience. I was told that nothing of that nature had been reported that night and they would follow up on my report. We continued to watch the sky on and off throughout the hour and we watched a few helicopters make their way across the night sky. This was not my first encounter with a unidentified flying object, but my first time reporting an experience. I am an avid alien astronaut theorist, and all I can say is that I will continue to look up.
