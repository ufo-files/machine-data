Continually changing color of star-like size: red/teal/white while remaining stagnant.

I had just arrived home and was standing out in the darkness and my eyes adjusted to the night sky and the stars. When I looked over to the one area of the sky, I noticed what appeared to be a star that was blinking continually from red with white, to teal with white. It remained motionless. I went to a nearby location for a better point for viewing it and after a while, it began to lose its intensity of blinking red or green. Also, with it being after 12:30a.m. I was becoming very tired, so I gave up on watching it any longer. As far as my state of mind was at the point of seeing this object, I was wide awake and had not been under any influences of alcohol or drugs.
