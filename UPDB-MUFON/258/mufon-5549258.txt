Formation of 10-12 lights in the sky

Me and my friend ben were driving home from the movies and when you get off the highway you are on a hill overlooking in between gates and chili. What made us first notice the lights was an active helicopter with a searchlight searching a neighborhood to the right of the horizon. Behind the helicopter was a string of perhaps ten yellow lights that seemed to also be hovering. Me and my friend assumed the lights to be helicopters and commented on the unusual amount of helicopters in the sky. Once we reached lower land and began driving west we looked up in the sky to see the other "helicopters" and none were see in the area. The yellow lights were gone, but were definitely not flares. The yellow lights were illuminating the ground below that area very well, and the objects moved slowly, if at all. They appeared to be hovering and that's why we assumed to be helicopters. However helicopters could not leave the area as fast as we lost sight of the objects, and neither me nor my friend could explain what we saw. Later on I became excited when I realized the item could have been a UFO, but remain skeptical as to the real nature of the objects(s.)
