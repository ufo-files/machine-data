Bright Star-like Figure

I was out in the front of my house looking up at the sky and saw what looked to be a bright star that seemed to be gliding upwards and further away and then all of a sudden it disappeared. I know it was not an airplane because I saw a few of those in the sky too at the time and could tell they were airplanes by the way they were moving and how their lights were blinking. What I saw was a lot higher than the altitude that airplanes can get to but it was either big enough or close enough for me to have seen it. I first thought I was maybe seeing a "Shooting Star", but I have seen those in the past and what I saw did not have a "tail" and it was gliding.
