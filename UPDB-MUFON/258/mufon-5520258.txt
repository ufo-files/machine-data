Dandelion shaped UFO. Glows blueish-green from center.

I was driving from Indiana to Durham, North Carolina. I have made that trip a hundred times with nothing unusual happening. I always go through this area about the same time when I make this trip.

All I remember before the incident is that I was driving. THe next thing I know, I look over to my right and I say to myself, mmmph, I just saw a UFO. I say it in a matter of fact way and I just go on driving like nothing happened. For some reason I reach for my cell phone and send my brother a message stating that I just saw a UFO. I seem to forget the whole thing. I arrive at the motel and I look at my watch and I think, Wow, its 3 oclock in the morning?. I think, Boy, it took me alot longer to get here than I thought it would. I check in and go to bed.

I get up the next morning go to work. I work for a couple of hours and my brother calls and askes me what I saw. For a minute or two, I don't know what he is talking about. I forgot the whole thing! After he ask me that question, it all came back to me! I cant believe I forgot about the UFO! I have always wanted to see one. I finally see one and I forget about it? That doesnt seem right to me.
