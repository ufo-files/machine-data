My wife and I were walking our dogs just after dark on Pine Island Florida. All of a sudden we noticed a bright light in the sky, maybe a mile away from us. It was heading East to West at a fast rate but not super fast. It was moving in a straight line. It was a white ball of bright light and was leaving a green trail. We were not frightened and seeing it we were amazed and knew we were not seeing an airplane. Having never seen one we did not know if a meteor looks like that or not. We watched the local news that night and all we saw or heard was one broadcaster laughed and asked "did you see the UFO tonight?" We were sad there was not more about it.
