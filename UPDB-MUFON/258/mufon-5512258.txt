{cak} large round glow of white light above clouds. illuminating cloud cover from above. Huge

There was a bright white round light above the cloud cover.
It was there for about an hour and then faded away. It did not get smaller as it faded. There was no moon and the light was not being reflected onto the cloud from below. It did come from above the cloud cover.
