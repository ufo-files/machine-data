Observed three golden spheres

I was outside smoking when i noticed a soundless golden light travelling in the sky. It made no noise and it had no blinking lights. It appeared to be entirely glowing golden orange. I noticed a second, coming from a different direction, heading s to north. Then I noticed a third, but cannot fully recall its behaviour. I came inside and looked south out of my window and observed a star like object that was shooting out small objects.
