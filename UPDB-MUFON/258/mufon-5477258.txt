{jpg} Very bright white light wobbling and changing speed/direction

At around 8:00pm EST, I went outside to let my dogs out. I immediately looked up to see a very bright white light moving slowly across the sky. My first thought was that it was a satellite, but it was way too bright to be one. It also wobbled very slightly and would change speed and direction at various times during the 2 minutes it was in sight. It seemed to be banking to the northeast instead of north like it was when I first spotted it. I couldn't keep my eyes off the object until it disappeared over the treeline. I tried to get a decent picture with my Galaxy S6, but the low light made it difficult to snap anything substantial. About 10 minutes after the object was out of sight, a low flying helicopter unlike any I've seen around here flew along the same path as the white orb object.
