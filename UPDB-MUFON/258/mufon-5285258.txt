White stationary object

There was a white object hovering below the cloud cover in the direction of Pearl Harbor at 9:26pm on September 21, 2021. I was on the phone with my boyfriend after my class ended at 9pm. I was on the phone and walking in the neighborhood near my residence when I noticed the object. I watched it for a minute and decided to get off the phone to record the object. I wanted a better view so I ran to the neighborhood park and lost sight of the object. I ran back to the house and it was gone. I should have stayed put and kept recording. I did record another short video to show that the object was gone. The streetlights give off a yellow glow as where the object was white.
