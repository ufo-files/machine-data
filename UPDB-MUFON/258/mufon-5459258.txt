{cak} 2 bright orbs moving slowly, hovering, then speeding away

We were on the side step and we first saw them above the house across the street in between the tree and light pole. 
They were creeping real slow and weaving around each other. Then they started a little faster and started separating. They were going south and then they started splitting off at about 30 degree angle. That's when I got my phone and by time I got back they were far like the video shows. Before that they were not much beyond the school.
