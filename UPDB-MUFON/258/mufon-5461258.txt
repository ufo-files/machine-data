{rjl} Dome, numerous colors, 2 things hanging down, 100 feet or so.

I do not know what this was. No fast movements. Dome shaped object above two long parts hanging down. And the two parts hanging down did appear to be hanging, and independent of the dome but attached as they seemend to be swinging. The part on the my right was gray and the part on the left had numerous different colored lights. The craft went south, moving some side to side and some up and down and disappeared. That was at 9:20 to about 9:25 PM. Then when my friend Maria arrived I went out again and it came back. Was south by south west from where it was prior and traveled west to north west. Slow, moving side to side and some up and down. Can't say for sure it was from another world, just never seen anything like it and its shape is not something I have seen in the sky before. There were two airplanes I saw near when it came back. I did try and take some pictures but they all are black.
