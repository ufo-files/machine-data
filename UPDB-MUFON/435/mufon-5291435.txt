Light hovered and then sped off impossibly fast

I am a truck driver and was swapping trailers for a new load at the Sierra Nevada Brewery. I got back to the guard shack and found the guard standing outside looking out over the trailer yard at the sky. He asked if I had seen anything while switching trailers but I said I hadn't. He pulled up the security camera looking in the same direction and we found out that the object had been captured. It appeared to be a hovering light in the distance in the direction of the Asheville, NC airport that pulsed and then shot off to the NE at an impossible rate of speed for anything we have. He slowed the video down and we could see that the object jumped half the screen in a single frame. I recorded the video off the computer monitor at normal and slow speed on my phone as he was unable to export it. I have attached them with this report.
