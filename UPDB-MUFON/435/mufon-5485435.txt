Three lights moving around each other then fading to nothing

I had justs arrived home from work. It was a dark and starry night. Three lights caught my attention because it looked like a bright star constellation or planet alignment that i'd not seen before. Then the lights started to move around each other slowly and realign. I thought this was strange. It wasn't like Chinese lanterns, which seem to blow in one direction with the wind, these moved around almost purposely and not like they were blowing in the wind at all.
