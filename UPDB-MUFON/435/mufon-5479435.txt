On november 26th 2016 at approximately 7 am in the morning my wife and i decided to sit in the hot tub. we noticed a hovering object approxiamately 3 or 4 miles a way in the sky approximately 30 degrees from horizon. It really stuck out because there were no other stars visable that morning. we could not see the structure of the object but the red green and white flashing lites formed a triangle it hovered and flickered flashing lites for about 30 minutes. I got the binoculars out and thats when i noticed the triangle shape. While i was looking at it through the binoculars it disappeared on me. approximately one second later i then located it a good ways away from where it just was. It had suddenly moved very fast. It hovered for another 3 minutes in that spot then slowly faded away until i could see it no longer. I have always scoffed at the idea of ufo's but I really dont know of any man made object that could do what i just witnessed. We orignally tried to rationalize it away, perhaps a weather satellite, but it was to low, it seems gravity would pull it down. then we thought a drone but it couldnt possibly move that fast,could it? at any rate i kind of wish i wouldnt have seen it because i cant stop thinking about it. i did video it but it was too dark to see it good, however you can kind of see its white flashing lite
