Sighting of 4 disc like grey/black objects moving silently across a cloudy sky which appeared to be rotating and moving.

My daughter and I were playing outside in the garden. A passenger aircraft was flying over to the north of us and I looked up at it. I then notice four greyish black disc like shapes moving near the aircraft but moving south of it. They were almost directly overhead us at the nearest point. The four objects were moving at the same speed and appeared to be rotating and moving. I told my daughter to quickly run inside and get the camera as I felt they were something unusual. They were quite far by the time I took the photos and I had to fully zoom in. The sky was overcast and cloudy which made them more visible.
