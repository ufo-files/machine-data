High Speed Aircraft at over Mach 5 seen against a full moon

This is approximate date. While visiting a relative's house near President George W. Bush's summer house at Walker's Point, in Kennebunkport, Maine; I was sitting near by on our rear deck looking at the night sky, and I observed an ultra sonic aircraft flying against the full moon in the southeast sky. It was going faster than when I see the Space Shuttle fly by which would be over Mach 5. It appeared because it was against the full moon. Pease Air Force base was near by. I once met a pilot in Danbury, Ct. about thirty years ago from the Netherlands, and he told me he wanted to be the first ultrasonic pilot flying from Europe to Australia in an hour. It was around the people making the Hubble Telescope mirror at the time. It was real fast flying over the President's summer home while he was there. I lived near the Redstone Arsenal in the 1950s, so I am used to looking at the night sky. Lanzarote in the Canary Island has the most stars at night I have ever seen. I have seen a picture of my parents with Varner Von Brun.
