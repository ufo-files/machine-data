{td} Two lights high above downtown Minneapolis

I live in downtown Minneapolis. I am an avid plane spotter and track aircraft in and out of MSP frequently as a hobby. In my 15+ years of observing aircraft in my area I have become accustomed to both civil and military aircrafts and their movements. I also live across from a helipad.

What I saw tonight was not like anything I have ever seen in my many years of staring at the sky. There was initially one small bright dot hovering in circles over downtown Minneapolis and moving quickly at what appeared to be about 2-4 miles up. It spent about 1-2 minutes in a circular zig-zag pattern hovering over the city before it dimmed and then became brighter again. 

After this, a second light (craft?) was observed, and the first light moved closer to the second and then they both zoomed off.

It was brief, but absolutely not like anything I have ever seen, ever. I take looking at the sky so seriously that I have a tattoo of an airplane on my left wrist. 

I wasn't able to take a video or photo, unfortunately.

Two other people can confirm sighting of the first initial craft. I was the only one to observe the second as it was so quick to move along with the first.
