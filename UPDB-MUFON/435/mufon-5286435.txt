A triangular craft, classic TRB3 type. My first 'real' sighting of anything substantial.

Hello, i was in my garden in east London, Docklands area. UK
I'd gone into the garden to spot high altitude 'orbs' (probably satellites?)
A very warm July 17 night at 00:20 am. 100% clear skies.
I spotted 3 individual high alt 'Orbs' straight away.
Something in my head said, ''It's going tp be your night, tonight, Dave''
At 00:30 i see my first ever Skylink sats fly past SE to NW.
At 00:35 i got up to stretch my neck ache, still obviously excited by the starlink sighting. Scouring the sky in all directions as i did.
Then i gazed the north sky and i see a triangular craft going S to N in a straight line, with a slight straightening action before it disappeared beyond my view, 
There is a 2 story building in my way to view it for a slightly longer second or two.
The whole sighting was approx 3 secs long. But in those 3 seconds, i see the lights in each corner very clearly, probably a central light, but i was too amazed to remember, or even noticed?
I see at the same time, a clear outline of this craft, as it traveled very fast indeed.
Black/dark exterior around the lights of a perfect triangle outline shape.
UFO or man made? I don't know? But it moved with surreal speed and looked unlike anything terrestrial i'd ever seen.
It seemed to skim through the skies in a hologram ish, surreal manner.
I'm not suggesting a hologram at all. But if i'd seen a NASA rocket instead, it would be more tangible to describe compared with what i saw,
The silence of this helped with that feeling.
This is my truth.
God is my judge.
I'm amazed, and feeling privileged i saw.
Thank you.
