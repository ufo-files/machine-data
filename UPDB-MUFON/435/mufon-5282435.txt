Ball of light over head

My sister and I were walking home from our friend's house. We were in the road. I caught something out of the corner of my eye and looked up. There was a ball of light hovering over us and then it just disappeared. I said "Did you see that?" to my sister. She replied that she did. But after talking about it, we realized we each saw different things. I was looking up at the ball. She was looking down at our feet on the road. She saw a light encompassing us on the ground. I did not add her personal information to this report, but she does remember it. We both believe it was a UFO. But back then we didn't know what to do. We were only about 15-16 years old at the time. 
I will submit another sighting of a different object I observed in West Newfield, Maine with a different person about one year later.
