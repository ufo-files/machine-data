{nd} Green disc hovering approximately 100 ft

Me, my mom, my sister, my brother in law and my niece pulled into home at address above from going grocery shopping. My niece said "grandma what is that" we looked up and hovering about 100 ft to our left was a green disc. No windows, just plain green. No sound no smell no nothing. It just hung there. My mom went in the house scared. We just stated in disbelief. After I guess a min or two it took off going straight up and zig zagged at a sped I would say impossible and away. I have wanted to report this association but my brother in law was in military and didn't want any type of this attention. I am reporting it now cause I still don't know what I saw. I look to the skies every day I just want to understand what I know I saw.
