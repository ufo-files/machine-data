UFO Glittered Red, White and Blue

September 15th, 2013 - S1ance Session a success!

At 5:02am My girlfriend and I exited the east entrance of the house when yet again I witnessed the silent flight of an Unidentified Aerial Object.
The UFO flew from southwest to east until it became stationary in the eastern sky. The craft floated left, right, up, down and diagonally in a jittery rhythm as though adjusting to the earth's gravitational rotation (wobbled). The craft emitted beams of red, white and light blue lights at about 1500 to 2000 feet in altitude. Suddenly an aircraft jet, military or commercial flew over the UFO, causing the colorful glowing orb to descend in altitude as if seeking cover then disappearing behind the treeline of my neighbour's pines out of my sight. I then went back in the house to enter this experience in my Journal.

If you have seen UFO's twice already,
you are sure to see them a third time.
