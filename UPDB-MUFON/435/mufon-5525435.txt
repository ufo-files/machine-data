Flashing green laser lights accompanied by intense vibrations/humming

I was sitting at my desk and noticed suddenly loud vibrations/humming encompassing my house. I assumed it was a very low flying plane so I looked out my window. I saw in the forestry across my house several very bright laser-like green lights that flashed for a few seconds and disappeared. It was fluttery, green light particles that reminded me of a green laser. I could not believe what I saw and quickly rushed outside where the sound persisted; a very low frequency, humming, vibrations, that sounded like a kind of alien UFO. I did not see anything else after the light. The sound, however, went on for nearly half an hour but varied in intensity and eventually faded away.
