{rjl} 7 lights flying in a line the disappear

I first witnessed the lights while walking my dog tonight right outside my house while waiting for the Bears game to start. I notice the stars were oddly aligned in a straight line. Then I noticed there were seven of them all in a line. I look up at the stars constantly and I’m very familiar with the constellation patterns around my parents home. This was not a Constallation then upon staring for a moment more I noticed they were moving very slowly in a northern direction. That’s when I went inside and called for my mom. I am the only one who believes in UFOs in my family. And I didn’t even tell them what I wanted them to look at. I just needed my mom To verify the pattern was weird and also moving. We live near an airport so we have seen airplanes flying our entire lives and we know they do not fly in a straight line like that. When my mom noticed it she turned around went back inside to the rest of the family and said”.....she has a point.” That is when three more adult members of my family along with my two-year-old nephew all came outside to look at the flying objects. That was when my mom noticed that one of them had disappeared. As we stood there watching they slowly one by one disappeared at the same spot in the sky each time. The whole siding was about 10 minutes long. Being the nonbelievers that they are they researched and thought for sure It must be the new star link satellites. I found a website that was tracking them in real time, and they were nowhere near our location at that moment and the closest the trajectory even gets to us is four states away. Even my nonbelieving family cannot find an explanation.
