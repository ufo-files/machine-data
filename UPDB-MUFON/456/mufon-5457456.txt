{rjl} Ring of lights appeared high up and very dim, moving NE TO SW, something solid was inside the ring of lights darker than the moon lit night sky/

While observing the Satellites on this night, we were in awe at the large amount of satellites going in all directions, More in a few minutes than we ever saw before. Two were actually right behind each other going in the exact direction very close to each other. When i looked almost straight up i saw a dim ring of lights, The sky was bright and the inside of the ring of lights blocked out the sky beyond it. It was about the size of the moon as a reference. It only lasted a couple of seconds then disappeared. Now i am not too sure that the large amount of what we thought to be satellites were something else. We look at satellites every clear night, never had this many show up all at once.
