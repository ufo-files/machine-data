{rjl} Extremely high flyover

A buddy of mine, who wants nothing to do with this, & I were back in Desolation Valley east of Big Pine helping my uncle with his mine. Sleeping outside, we were laying on backs just talking. I noticed a triangular set of 3 lights moving rapidly above in sky. I pointed it out & we watched it move across the sky thinking it was a plane. Suddenly, the 3 lights separated & went every which way? The lights not only separated but in split seconds could change directions at 30 degree angles, maybe tighter? They played around like that for several minutes, then came back together into the original triangle shape & continued on out of sight. I know of no plane, even today, that can do that. I never said much about this because I did not know about this reporting service till just recently. No video, they didn't have that stuff then.
