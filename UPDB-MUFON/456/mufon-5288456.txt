Large fireball objects crossing sky over field.

My husband and I walked out our back porch door and immediately noticed two large glowing objects in the sky. They were bright orange-red, and gliding silently upwards and to the left of our field of vision, to the North. The objects were initially a few hundred feet from us, but moved further up and away quickly over a horse pasture behind our house. At this point I started recording the experience. 

The objects seemed to float or glide North together, one rotating a bit behind the other at a point ,with varying degrees of separation from one another until one and then the other eventually disappeared into the sky to the north. 

My husband is a teacher with a masters degree and I am a stay at home mom. We have never experienced anything like it. They moved fluidly like a drone but were far too large and quiet. As fast as a plane or helicopter, but looked only like a large fireball. The encounter last about two minutes.
