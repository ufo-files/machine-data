{rjl} Lights

My husband, our 24 y.o. son and I were standing outside in rural Ocean Shores, 9 pm, clear skies, and all of us saw lights that we first believed were satellites moving uniformly across the sky. The lights appeared to come from the direction of the ocean going SE and we counted 15 at least, following each other, one after the other and so on. We were shocked because there were so many, some seemed to detour slightly, then get back on path, following again in their trajectory. After 15 mins, two of the lights turned and came back the way they had come and appeared to come very close to and then go around the closest one that was still on the original first path. The two that had turned seemed to head back toward the direction of the ocean while all the rest continued. My family turned to go inside the house after watching these lights and saw that there were approximately six more behind us we didn't see at first! My son went inside, saying he was going to get on social media to share this experience and my husband and I stayed outside. We continued to see a few more lights, moving in the same direction, not as many as before but with similar spacing between them. I didn't think I had any device with the capability to have captured the lights, they simply looked like stars moving. I just have a basic cell phone. This was an amazing experience; whether military, ufos, I don't know, but what a great memory with my family to wonder about in the future.
