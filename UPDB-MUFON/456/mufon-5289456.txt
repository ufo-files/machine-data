Bright light traveling across the sky ine evening. Bright enough to draw my attention and caused me to tell my friend to look...

My friend and I were sharing oreo cookies one evening in my side yard when I noticed a very bright light traveling across the sky. It seemed to be quite far away, maybe a mile. When I noticed it I signaled my friend to look, and as soon as I did, it instantly zoomed directly toward us and closed the apparent mile long distance in a heartbeat. It hovered over the house across the street, and the lights blinked around the disc in a colorful pattern. We took a second to gather our thoughts to make sense of it, but ran off just shortly after it zoomed in on us. Terrified, to say the least. I tear up thinking of it every time.
