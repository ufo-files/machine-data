{cak} Bullet shaped object that slowly moved through the sky slowly, then flashed twice, disappeared, then flashed again while object was not visible.

While I was driving to work northbound on the 91 freeway at 1:23 pm here in Riverside California, I noticed in the sky what seemed to be a small silver aircraft not moving fast at all. I then saw two seperate witnesses in two different cars pointing in the sky at this object. I then noticed that this object didn't seem to have wings. The more I stared the more I realized it did not have wings and that this thing looked like a .50 caliber bullet. Within seconds of my observation this thing emitted two flashes back to back about a second apart then vanished. I'd say 5 seconds later another flash emitted from what looked like behind the blue sky! I have seen some crazy activity in the sky at night but never in the day and never so clearly. I am now sitting at work very confounded on what I was looking at. Unfortunately, I don't think I will ever know what it was I was looking at. I really hope others too will report this as this was over a high traffic area.
