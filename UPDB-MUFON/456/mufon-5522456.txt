I thought it was a plane until it stopped and hovered.

I saw a light in the westernsky that I thought was a plane. I had seen a plane fly through the cloud cover two nights earlier and that was a great image as the landing lights lit up the clouds around the plane. i thought this wouldbe the same until the lights just stopped. It was an orang/red glow with some bright spots. No regular running or strobe lights. I watched it for about 30 seconds, then tried to get a picture,with no luck. I watched it for about another 30 seconds,just hovering in place,no noise. It's glow faded and I could make out pulsing red/yellow lights.Then it suddenly shot straight up, moving through the cloud cover,which glowed with it's passing.
