Stepped out on the back porch with my dogs, and noticed what I thought were landing lights on a jet, but it made no turn for the runway at the nearby base. It continued on its south westerly path, Normally I would have heard the jet engine by this time, but I heard only the sound of a train passing by. Looking to the north I could see a commercial jet on approach to the International airport, I looked again at the craft now almost directly over the house, and flying at about ten thousand feet or a little below, the train had passed and I could no longer hear it, still no sound from the object, I ran out into the yard to continue watching the object, when it started to disappear into the clouds, it did not appear to ascend. the object appeared to be a ball or sphere about 30 to 40 feet in diameter and had a firery glow of orangeish redish and yellow color. and it continued this appearance throughout the sighting and appeared to be these colors all over..I would like to say I videoed the object and I did try but for some reason the camera Malfunctioned again!
