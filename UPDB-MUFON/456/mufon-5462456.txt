{cak} Family Story, Never reported

Well first off let me say this is a description/story/encounter from my mom and dad from 1967.You don't know my parents but I believe everything they have told me about this based on a life time of loving honest parents.My parents were heading home from church apx 6 to 8 pm sep to nov Wednesday night 1967. I was born in Dec 67 so this time period fits the dates/time. Wednesday was a typical church night service. They were heading home to Brace rd on Laird rd heading north on Laird road. The sighting took place just after the NID ditch close to Franklin ELE.My dad pulled off the road to the right just after the NID ditch. They were driving a Rambler??The story from them goes as.. as soon as they crossed over the NID DITCH they saw two circular objects the size of a house sitting on top of the tree tops to the north and the north west heading north on Laird road. My dad pulled off the side of the road to look at them because they were lighted up. My moms description over the years was that they were round, size of a large house and had windows that were lighted up? When my dad pulled over and got out of the Rambler, the one to his right started turning, my mom yelled at him get in the car because she saw the movement.He did and they sped off going home.
This IS pretty much the story I have been told by my parents over my lifetime.
YOU DONT KNOW THEM AND THEY ARE NOT CRAZY!!
