(GS) 3 Stars & 1 started moving

I was out front smoking a cigarette, looking up at the stars & a line of 3 stars caught my attention when the right one started to move. It started heading over my house but changed direction very quickly multiple times before heading off over the trees NW of me. I initially thought it was a plane, but noticed that the planes were flying overhead ALOT lower and you could hear them easily since the airport isnt too far away. The speed of this thing was incredible. There was another one that looked as if it were flashing brightly every once in a while as well. I ran inside to grab my camera and when I came back out, I saw a third object following close to the same path as the first one & the second one that was flashing brightly was gone. Unfortunately my camera wasnt able to pick it up as it looked like a moving star...
