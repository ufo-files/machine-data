orb appeared in front of me at head height and arms length. spun around me and stopped. took off and appeared seconds later and did the same again.

I was walking home alone in Belfast sept 1979. I was very alert to my surroundings as belfast was very dangerous in those days. As i was looking to my left i noticed a bright red orb shoot down from the sky and stop in front of me. It was in front of my face and about arms length. it was pulsating. At first i glanced around me looking for someone with a torch or if someone was attempting to distract me, but i quickly realized there is no end to a beam in mid air. The orb then started to move around me slowly and then sped up so quickly it was like a complete circle. after about ten seconds it stopped abruptly in front of my face. It remained for a few seconds before shooting off skywards from the direction it came. As i walked off trying to comprehend what had just happened i witnessed the object come from the same direction again. This time it descended toward the rear of a house and then rose over the roof and moved to the front of me again at face height and arms length. The orb did the same thing again. pulsating and then moving around me so fast that it appeared like a hula hoop. The object sat in front of me again pulsating before shooting off between two houses in the direction it came from. I cannot remember if i was froze to the spot but i do remember turning my head as the orb went around me.
