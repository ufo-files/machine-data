{jpg} Donut shape center of circumference spun continuously & had window like structures

Walking with with boyfriend after school through open brush field (tall dry grass) 1/2 block from his house. We suddenly observed object flying near us no sound S to N. We were walking W to E. We stopped didn't say anything only that maybe it was a toy. Then we noticed the center spinning and window like structures. I commented people couldn't fit inside. Then the object tilted East half down West side up and sped out of site. Doug and I never spoke about it again as if it never happened. Just lately the event reoccurred to me.
