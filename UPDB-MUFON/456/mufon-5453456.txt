Strange long formation of lights traveling NW to West across the sky

Sitting on the back deck with my girlfriend something strange happened. My girlfriend noticed something in the sky and I look. There is what appears to be a strand to light in the sky, my girlfriend says “its like a laser”. The idea of a laser made sense briefly, but then I realized that there is no way that it's laser. There isn't any way a laser was shooting at this angle, directly across the sky horizontally. 

I have seen the laser from the local observatory, and this was not a laser. It was bright, the movement of the separate light’s were in unison though. I had a view between the chimney and the tree in my yard from my seat. As the lights traveled across the sky from the chimney to the tree they disappeared. They disappeared after going behind the tree. If it was a craft I would have expected to see the lights continue on, but it disappeared into nothing gradually, one light at a time. Note that the night sky was crystal clear, the stars were bright, new moon in the sky. 

After the sighting I tried to gauge the size of the item in the sky. I put my arm out and it was about 8 to 9 inches at a arms length away from me. How high in the sky was the object? It could have been about the height of a airplane flying, or a satellite orbiting possibly.
