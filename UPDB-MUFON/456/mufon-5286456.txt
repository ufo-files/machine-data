UFO spotted in photo around downtown Los Angeles

On 12/21/2020 I went on the roof to take pictures of the "Bethlehem Star". After reviewing my photos later, to my surprise, I saw this "UFO" shape in one of my digital files. I did NOT see this with my own eyes that night. It was probably moving quite fast, because it was not in any other of my photos, taken during the same minute. My camera settings for this shot were: 1/20 sec at f/2.8, ISO 8000, 75mm. I'll include 3 photos; the original one, annotation, and a zoomed in copy. Quite perplexing. I've seen flare in photos before, but not like this.
