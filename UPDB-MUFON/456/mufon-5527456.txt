A whitish gray object moving in sky that disappered into clouds

It was about 12noon to 12:30 in Antelope, CA. I was in my backyard playing with my kids. My 3 year old son noticed a beautiful hawk circling above us. As we admired this majestic hawk my son had pointed to the right of it and asked "what's that dot?" I looked up and automatically thought it was a UFO and not a balloon as it was not moving upwards. It was whitish gray with no lights that moved from left to right in a perfect stright line. We watched/followed it for about a minute as it moved in the sky and disappered into a sparse cloud. My son was quite disappointed when he could no longer find/see this object of fascination. He had described it to dad so perfectly when he asked "where did the golfball in the sky go?" I was in total shock as my son and I just witnessed our first UFO!
