I was driving down old US 72 and saw 3 separate lights in an equilateral triangle. I pulled off to the side of the road and cut my engine off. I heard a low hum coming from them. They were about 500 feet in the air hovering, suddenly they took off. 1 went NW, the second went SE and the third shot straight up at speeds I've never seen before.
