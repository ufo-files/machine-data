Witness (A) and (B) on many occasions, while traveling together or at several locations around Jackson and Eaton Counties, have observed many unusual objects in the sky. The following nine events were related from a 23May15 interview with the witnesses; EVENT 1 of 9: While at their cabin location, while asleep, witness (A) was awaken by a bright circular object moving from side to side, shining into their bay windows, got up to get a better look and the object departed to the SW at 800 mph. 20 minutes later three aircraft flew over looking for the object.
