We were driving, then in our driveway, watching it, trying to get a picture, but it was too far away. We noticed because of the bright red-orange color. It pulsed, moved a bit, then vanished. We at first thought it might be a planet, or an aircraft, but it wasn't. It clearly stayed stationary at first, but then wavered, then moved irregularly toward the west, but rotated in a small circle, then disappeared. We were at first incredulous, and then hopeful. It definitely was not anything we have seen before, nor did it seem to be a planet or star.
