{jpg} Witnesses saw a very bright light in the sky

Three witnesses, at two locations, observed a big bright red/pink/white light in the sky that moved slowly from the east to WSW. The light emitted some smaller objects, quantity of 6 to 8, that descended toward the ground. A sound like a boom or a roar was briefly heard. The sound was similar to an F14 going into afterburner according to one of the witnesses who worked on the aircraft while stationed on the US Nimitz. Sighting lasted approximately 10 minutes
