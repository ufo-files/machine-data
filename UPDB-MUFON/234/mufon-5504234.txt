{cak} Fast Red Light and Slow Dark/Dull Grey Orb

While walking south on new street in Bethlehem PA at 9pm I observed a bright red light several miles high and moving fast. What caught my attention was how bright and unlike a plane the light was. There were no flashing lights. It was startling how different it was from anything you'd expect to see in the sky. After about 5 seconds the object veered south hard, but not angular, and several seconds later my view was about to be obscured by a building so I ran to get in the clear. When I did I saw the object to be further south and much higher in the southern sky then it disappeared. 

Coincidence 1: About 2 seconds after it disappeared, I saw a dark/grey orb at about the height of a 5 story building moving east, slowly descending across the skyline then out of view due to obstruction from buildings. 

Coincidence 2: The next day, a friend who lives 10 miles away put a video up online from the night before which depicted several red lights exactly like what I saw.
