white flashing lights

I was sitting on my porch looking at the sky when to my left. (south) over the water. I saw a white light flash like a strobe.
I ignored it figuring it was a plane.
Then ahead of it i saw two more lights the same. 
Again i thought there were a few planes up there.
So im still sitting there and out of the corner of my eye i see the lights flashing again. this time when i looked, i thought damn thats a lot of air traffic. then as i looked and ajusted focus. i saw there were over 10 flashing lights. and they were so close together and when i followed just one expecting to see a flash in the same path further along, there would be nothing! but they would flash in a different location.
It looked like fire flys moving around and turning on and off.
during the time i watched them i never saw any other light colors, only white like strobe. the sky to the south was filled with them comming on and off all in different locatins. 
Then, it stopped! nothing no more lights at all.
If they were planes you would think i would still see at least one strobe in the distance untill it got out of range. There was Nothing!!
How can so many air contacts just disapear?
Also might i add, i am pretty sure they where not flares. Flares don't strobe. I used to work on a ship for the USCG and i am used to making contacts.
