{rjl} Large glowing orb in a clear night sky

I was visiting family in Etna California, and we went out to feed their horses in fort jones. The night crept up fast and we were feeding them in the back field and someone said to look up. As we threw the hay off the truck we observed a bright white glowing ball moving across the sky in a strange pattern. It came from the east and headed north. Had it been still it would have look like a glowing planet or the North Star as the best way to describe it., but this object moved in a strange pattern across the sky heading in the direction of Mount Shasta. We observed it until it was no longer seen.
