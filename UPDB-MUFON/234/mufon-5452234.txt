Star like object

Object appeared to be star like and hovering in outer edge of atmosphere over Mt Ranier. I watched it for several minutes and could see a destination flatness to it. It had to be over5 miles long. The DSS would appear round compared to it. As I finally looked away it disappeared and a dim star was where it was. Could be a stellar event, but the glowing was very bright as if it was dipping into our atmosphere, perhaps to test it or to gather oxygen.
