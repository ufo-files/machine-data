{cak} bright light suddenly appeared over my car. no heat. no memory of subsequent ride home. no memory of event for over 10 yrs.

Appx. Dec. 1976 I was visiting relatives in Michigan. Driving home from a friends home with my toddler I suddenly, very suddenly had a bright white light over and around my car.the light covered my car. I remember it glaring into all the Windows. I saw no object. I heard no sound. There was no heat.The next thing I remember was walking downstairs at my in-laws home appx 9-10am. My family commented on me sleeping late and asked if I was ok. The oddest thing is that I had no memory of any of this for at least 10 years. My daughter, who by then was appx 13 yrs old and I were making the trip from Muskegon Michigan to the cedarville area. We were just chatting about an unrelated subject and the memory was just there. I had not been intoxicated or on meds when the experience happened. I'm reporting this as since that memory I have an interest in ufo phenomena. There is no more I can add as all I remember is the light and missing time. No marks on our bodies. No implants, I hope. Car worked ok. Cedarville was close to kincheloe afb so after I remembered this I assumed it was related to an aircraft. But all I ever saw was the light right above me. And the missing time is unusual.I do remember that I was terrified by the unexplainable light so it's not something I would have just forgotten.
