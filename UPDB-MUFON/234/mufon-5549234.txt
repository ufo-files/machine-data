25ft wide, 30ft long satin black 4-5 round windows yellow inside,100ft altitude

Between 5:30 pm and 6pm on the sidewalk of my house as I was picking up the newspaper I felt and heard a whoosh (no engine noise) approx. 100ft above me and 100ft to the north traveling about 40 miles an hour from east to west, a satin black rectangular craft with rounded sides,not angular. I decided not to try to run for the camera as I could not return in time. A little scared I looked in the windows to see if something was looking at me. There was not. I have not really thought about it since,though it is as clear as yesterday. I watched a UFO Tv show and decided to share my account. I remember that planes in 1962 all made noise, and were not black! At the time I thought it might be a gov. spy craft from Fort Riley near Juction City Ks. The craft was incredibly close, looked like a cigar box shape but all smoothly rounded no sharp corners anywhere and approx.12-15ft high (top to bottom of craft). So Quiet!
