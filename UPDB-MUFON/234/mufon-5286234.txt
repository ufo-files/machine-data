Headed NE on The Delaware River

Two days ago three friends and I went to the Delaware River. Three out of four of us saw something similar to what I have on film. The only exception for the first encounter is the flight pattern. On that day it was ascending vertically and after about 20 seconds it vanished into thin air.
Yesterday I went outside at 6 pm. It was super clear out. I looked up and all of a sudden almost materializing out of thin air was this white tic tac. Silent. I observed it travel NE before it faded from my vision. I kept looking up and saw about 4 or 5 more. All following a similar flight pattern. At one point two of them were parallel with each other following the same flight pattern as those witnessed previously.
