{rjl} Path of about 12 lights traveling in a straight path and interacting with each other.

I woke in the middle of the night and looked up to a cloudless sky. I was familiar with the stars and constellation patterns at this time of the night, and I was extremely surprised to find what seemed to be an entirely new constellation. At first the figure was just a line of lights in the sky, like stars in a row, but then the "star" on the farthest left side of the arrangement moved, touched the craft to its right, and then that craft began to move to the right and touch the next one. This sequence continued for all 12 objects observed until the last one was touched, moved right, and then the figure halted again just as it had been. It was almost like witnessing how a centipede's legs move, but on an astrological scale. Very strange. The pattern the faded away slowly, like a balloon does when it gets so far into the atmosphere that it becomes difficult to see, then disappears completely.
