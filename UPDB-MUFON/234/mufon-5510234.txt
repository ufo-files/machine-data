I go to a Chick Fil A for lunch and take it back to a open field by St. Pete/Clearwater Airport to eat and watch planes take off and land. It was a typical summer day with puffy clouds. I saw a cloud that was kinda vertical. A small shiny orb like object flew out and back into the cloud. It popped out many times popping out a little higher than before. I took off my sun glasses and looked again, I rubbed my eyes and looked again, and I finally accepted that something was popping in and out of the clouds.
