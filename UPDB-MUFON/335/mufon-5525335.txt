Previously dim gold orb, gets bright, moves, makes 90 degree turn then goes dim...

Could not sleep, went out side on my patio to observe stars and constellations. Was watching the area around Orion, when I saw what I thought was a star, suddenly get bright gold in color. This "star" then did something which defied gravity, logic and reality...it started to slowly move north northeast, then sped up, made a nearly 90 degree angle turn, then slowly got dim and i lost sight of it.
