unknown large craft that made no sound

several years ago a friend of mine told me about a huge craft he saw that went straight over his head, went across the road, over the plaza across the road and out of sight. it was the size of a football field and could be nothing but a space ship. about 3 years later, i had a similar sighting less than a mile from his sighting. i was visiting my parents one night and went outside for a smoke. i looked to my left and saw something very strange. it was very dark so i didn't really see the outline of the craft to know the actual shape, but there were 4 lights that i could see on the bottom which would be the nose and tail and wing tips if it were a plane. i observed it from about half a block away, so didn't actually see the shape since it was dark. i could see some windows and there was a very bright white light inside, like operating room bright. i saw nothing inside except the light. two strange things was that there was no strobe light which is required for all aircraft and it never made a sound. it was about the size of a medium sized commercial plane and moved from east to west. it went in a straight line and was out of sight within about 30 seconds.
