3 lights that formed a triangle that was hovering

I was driving with 2 of my friends. Al the sudden my friend that was in the back seat said " i think im looking at a ufo", then my other friend said " i've been looking at it for 2 minutes now! what is that?" i said where? where? where? i wanna see it. i finally seen it as i was swerving all over the road as i was looking for it.
