{tjd} observed the craft hovering, then become transparent, and travel across the sky

I was sitting outside on my patio stargazing like I do almost every night. I noticed a cigar shaped craft hovering in the sky, then it began moving and became almost transparent. As I followed it with my eyes, I could see the outline, but it looked like I was looking at a bubble.. I could see a blurry spot as it traveled across the sky in front of other stars. The air felt electrically charged - my hair stood on end. I couldn't take my eyes off of it, until it was too far away to see anymore. it was very eerie.. it was balmy and breezy outside My heart was racing, and I was in awe of what I was seeing!!!
