{dr}light on surface of road two of them as from above, passed one foot from car window

I was driving on back road at night saw lights on the road coming towards me. Two of them spaced eight to ten feet apart as if something above me was flying over and casting light down.It almost looked 3d so I stopped the car thinking I was going to hit something solid. As it passed one foot past my drivers side window it appeared as a living vibrating pulsating light like a helix or white electric looking eel. A very inense light in a. protoplasmic substance. It looked alive. I could not move to look behind me like I was frozen. Location was 10 miles from Scot air force base.
