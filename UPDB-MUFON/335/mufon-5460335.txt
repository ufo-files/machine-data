Was standing in my garden,looking for an owl in the trees. Noticed numerous "shooting stars" which looked like rockets on bonfire night.

As they passed overhead they disintegrated into many more parts which looked like more "shooting stars"

They were very slow. I'm used to seeing shooting stars which steak very quickly across the sky. These took about 20-30 seconds to cross the sky above me. From the time I saw the first one to the time I saw the last one was around 10 or so minutes. During that time there were 20-30 of these objects, each moving in the same direction.

I did check things like where the ISS was, but it was a few thousand miles away. Also checked my place app and there were no planes near either.
