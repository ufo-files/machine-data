Extremely bright object, like 'star of bethlehem' above medium level stratus cloud visible through gaps . Was stationary, few miles distant, moved closer. Vanished behind cloud..

It was late evening,and here,darkness had recently fallen. I decided to take my dog for a walk. We were walking along a short country lane, by the horse paddock.

The evening was warm with no wind. There was a broken stratus cloud layer,
which I estimated to be maybe 6000 feet. Suddenly, I saw what looked like an extremely bright white star like object through breaks in the cloud.

I continued to observe the object for around 15 minutes.It looked like how you would imagine the Star of Bethlehem, but even brighter. No other stars were visible. It was in the East, roughly over the areas of Boothstown and Worsley.

We live close to Manchester international airport,under one of the flight paths, so I'm used to observing plenty of aircraft.I am a retired dental and oral surgeon,I ride a motorcycle,and have piloted a private aircraft.
In the circumstances, I believe my observations are generally accurate.

The star like object remained stationary for approximately ten minutes,
then moved closer to my location, before finally becoming obscured by cloud.It was incredibly bright.

My dog, a German Shepherd/Collie, even looked up and observed it himself.

This is far from the first time I have observed an inexplicable phenomenon. Until recently, I didn't realise I could submit details of my experiences. I am a carer for my elderly Father, but when time permits, I will relate details of my several past experiences.
