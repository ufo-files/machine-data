{cak} Observed ufos in sky for well over 1 hr

While sitting on my porch drinking a cup of coffee around 5am I saw what I thought was comet and then another one dropping out of the sky. they then stopped and hovered and the tails disappeared.They moved right and left, up and back down and then slowly rose up and disappeared. Then it happened a 2nd time and a 3rd time with the 2 objects. Then four of them dropped and did their movements as the first one did. In total I saw them 9 times do this. The last 2 it was just light and they no longer glowed but were a blackish silver color.
