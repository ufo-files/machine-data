The light blinked off really quick to a very faint green light while simultaneously moving quickly, looping around, and back and forth turning back on the bright light several times. The aircraft was moving exceptionally quick with sharp turns with no meaning, almost like fighter jet pilots do tricks in the sky, like fooling around. It happened to be over a large vacant island with no human access whatsoever. It disappeared with no trace of the green light again.
