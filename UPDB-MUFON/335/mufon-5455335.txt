Flew from west to east but not in a straight line a bit wavy like flight path

I was walking to my car at 05:46am on feb 20th 2020 a light witch I thought was a satellite caught my eye so I watched it, it was solid white glow just like a star then I saw another then another. I counted up to 50 of them n then stopped counting it must have been at least 80 in my estimation for a duration of 6 mins. A couple of times one would fly in a higher flight path n slow down while the one behind it sped up until they were parallel n flew side by side. They didn’t all fly in a straight flight path. Another couple flew side by side from west to east from the time it came into my view then flew east together. I tried to film it, not sure if I even caught anything.
