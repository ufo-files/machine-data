I was at Po'olenalena Beach, Wailea, Maui photographing a couple after their wedding. I did not see the object during the photo session but after reviewing my files I saw a pyramidal shaped object in 2 frames. The object was brighter than the sunset sky and white in color while clouds showed warm hues of sunset. The frames show some sort of dark trail behind it, exhaust??
