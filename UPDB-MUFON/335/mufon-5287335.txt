Multiple objects moving across sky fading out before next one appeared.

Appeared like a satellite and faded out as it crossed the sky. Unusual was that several appeared one after another a few seconds apart as if coming through a doorway. Roughly a dozen or so appeared. I was able to track them with telescopic binoculars after fading out to the naked eye.  Unsure what they might be.  I have seen a lot of satellites and while they looked similar the amount of them coming one after another in exactly the same spot makes me believe it could possibly be something else.
