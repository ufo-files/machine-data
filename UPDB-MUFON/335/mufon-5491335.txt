{cak} Orange pulsating orb giving off heat hovered

I was in my living room watching tv when this glowing light caught my eye it came out of nowhere. I had no idea what it was I was scared it came towards me and just hovered there and then it was moving side to side pulsating in orange and white light. Then the object left the room and after a few minutes I got up and looked around my house and I couldn't find it. A few hours later I was in my back yard when I saw a black military helicoper fly over my house.
