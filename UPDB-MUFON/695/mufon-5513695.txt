(GS) Observed red pulsating light move across the sky.

Yesterday early morning (14 Dec12, 0200 hrs) my gf and I drove to a secluded area of local park near where we live (South Park, Pa) to watch the Geminids show. We saw many of meteors in the 30 or so min we sat in our vehicle. As we were about to leave I noticed a small red colored light at about 80 degrees horizon. Its altitude and flight characteristics were not unlike those of a typical sattelite-except this was red, and it slowly pulsated from bright to dim red every 3-4 seconds. I trained my 10x50 binoculars on this device and could see no other lights. It slowly dimmed from view at about 60-65 degrees. It flew a straight line and was heading due North for the entire 2 minutes or so that I could see it until it dimmed from view. I know this sounds like an unremarkable sighting but Im familiar w/the night sky and this was definetly something unusual or unconventional flying up there.
