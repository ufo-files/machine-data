White dots seems to be ejecting small concentrated clouds

The sighting happened after sundown. It was today, 12. feb 2021, sun going down 17:02↑ 244° SW. I guess it was between 16:30 and 17:00, at this point the sun was below/behind the mountains. Very clear skies, some faint clouds here and there, cold day and low/no wind.

Here is a Google street view of the direction of the sighting. It was above the mountains in the distance, maybe behind them.

I was taking a jog after work to the shop, before I went in I saw a commercial plane in the sky in great detail and after purchasing a sport drink and drinking it outside, I spotted a small banana shaped orange cloud in the sky and 2 white dots, the cloud was very bright due to the sundown. It was very far away, the dot on the left vanished, but the dot on the right seemed to exhaust slowly a new cloud, it started small, bright, kinda looked like a thin water drop and got almost as large as the banana cloud next to it. Shape is hard to describe, but it vaguely looked like parallelogram. After this the white dot moved a bit upwards and then vanished also.
Unfortunately, I didn't bring my phone since I like to jog light. On my way back home I could still see the clouds fainting, but I decided it was not worth it to run the the last kilometers home to grab my phone and video the disappearing clouds as I was already tired.
