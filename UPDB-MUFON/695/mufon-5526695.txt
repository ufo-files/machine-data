Very bright light in the distance, moved very little, could be nothing

Looking outside my window I noticed a very bright light that was just below the horizon. It reappeared every night for about a week. Showing up in three different places (over three different nights) showing up at the same level (just below the horizon) but moving about 15-20 degrees to the east each night. It was back in the third spot for multiple days. It was very bright and pulsated a little. I could not tell how far away it was. It would appear just as the lights of the city were becoming noticeable, stay in place then just shut off instantly. I though it was a construction light but it was way too bright. On looking at the photos later I realized that it had actually moved as well. The two attached photos show it at the very beginning of the second last night it was there and just a little later. Please note that I have not done anything to the photos. I leave it in your judgment whether you think you should post this sighting or not.
