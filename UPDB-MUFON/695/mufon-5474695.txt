I was standing alone at the bottom of my garden, heard a throbbing hum and when i looked for the source, saw a doughnut shaped, dirty, yellow-brown cloud, that bubbled & spun (in the direction of something i now know as an accretion disc), above our neighbour's house. After watching it for, I suppose above a minute, it instantly vanished with a single, deep thump-sound. I've hardly ever mentioned it to anyone, except in recent years, maybe cos I've had no decent explanation. But i do now remember feeling quite impartial at the time. There was no one else with me to wonder at at it.
