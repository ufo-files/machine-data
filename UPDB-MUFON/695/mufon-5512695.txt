{cak} Line of approx 5 lights in sky. Brightened, hovered, then slowly began moving northward. Then abruptly and quickly seemed to faded away.

I was driving eastbound on a rural secondary road in southern Herkimer county just prior to 6 PM Wed, Feb 6, 2013. It was about twilight, with the sky still appearing dark blue, not nighttime black yet. 

I first noticed a light in the sky in front of me, in the direction I was driving. My first thought was that it was the first star of the night. It really caught my attention, though, when it increased in brightness. 

I slowed my driving and looked at the sky, and saw not one "star" but approximately 5 brightening lights in the sky, in a line that was to me, on a slight diagonal. The lights were slowly sweeping northward, and the brightness grew then dimmed somewhat as they moved. 

The movement was much faster than planes move across the sky when observed from the ground. Also, the lights were close enough that I believed no airplanes could fly in formation that close together, that quickly. Yet the lights covered too much area to be on one airplane. The movement was also not "normal" to me - not anything I am used to seeing. The line of lights seemed to move irregularly, hovering then sliding northward. 

As I now gawked out of my windshield, hoping for a better look, the lights began to dim and faded away into nothing very quickly. I was left without any rational explanation for what Id seen. The whole thing lasted only seconds, maybe a minute.
