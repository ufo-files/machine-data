{tjd} 2 bright lights one above the other staying the exact distance and horizontal movement

I was taking my granddaughter and a friend to school early. The sun was just below the horizon and I noticed two stars that seemed to be directly above each other and the same brightness. I as the girls if they saw it and they said yes and it looked strange. As we came into town I stopped and watched them for about two minutes and the were moving from right to left and appeared to be connected by a thin line. I dropped the girls off and drove back to the highway about 3 minutes. They were still there and still moving west slowly. I watched them for about another minute and they slowly faded out and were gone. The lights did not appear to dim but more like a shutter closing. I spent 7 years working for two different airlines, some of that time at DFW it was not any type of running or landing lights I have ever seen. I would estimate the object was 5 or 6 miles away at about 5000 ft. But actually I have no frame of reference fo how far apart the lights were apart but it was pretty big.
