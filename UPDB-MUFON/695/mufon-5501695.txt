{rjl} pulsing light travelling southeast observed while camping

On our last night of camping near Bass lake CA., while sitting around our camp fire, my wife & I observed a pulsing light travelling in a southeastern direction but appeared to be zigzagging in flight. No colored lights observed as with standard aircraft. We had observed many aircraft during day with chem-trails at various altitudes. This light appeared from the north west and we tracked it's path as it appeared to be rising in flight. We lost sight of it after about 3 minutes as it flew over the tree line across Granite Creek east of Oakhurst CA. Our campsite was at 7100 feet. We spoke to each other agreeing that we did see a UFO and not a conventional aircraft. No sound was heard coming from it but we could hear jets during the day and saw them as well.
