On 10/26/2015, I was helping a friend change the battery in his car. At about 6 my friend and I noticed the sun shinny through the clouds but the beam was focused, like a magnifying glass would do with the Sun. We both took a few picture and went on with our day. Later the evening when I was at the dinner table with family I pulled out my phone to show them how cool the sun looked. That is when one of us noticed the round object in both pics.
