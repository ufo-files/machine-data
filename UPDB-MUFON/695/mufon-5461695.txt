{tjd} Horizontal Lights - sighting - Kansas - on Amtrak train - between Topeka and Hutchison heading west

The sighting - overview

  The UFO/lights sighting occurred while traveling on the Amtrak Southwest Chief train route. The train was headed west at the time approximately between the cities of Topeka and Hutchison Kansas. The approximate time of the light/ufo sighting/observance was between 1:00 to 4:00 AM. The date was approximately February 3, 1994 on a Thursday.

  The sighting

  Except for the train wheel and track noise of “click, click,” the train cabin was very/relatively quiet. Most passengers had fallen off to sleep by then, with the “late night” crowd eventually falling asleep around 12:00 to 1:00 AM. I was in a deep sleep sometime in the early morning/AM when I awoke and looked out of the train window to the North or Northwest to see some stationary lights. The train was out in the country somewhere. I saw almost no city or town lights of any kind. There might have been 2 to 3 home lights. The home/house lights were roughly 1 to 2 miles from each other. 
  - The unknown lights were roughly about a 1/2 mile/2000 feet away from the train. 
  - The light band was moving right to left or left to right and possibly alternating between the 2 patterns along a horizontal plane. 
  - The height of the lights looked to be around 200-300 feet in the air. 
  - The color of the lights were possibly red, green and yellow in hue. 
  - I could not make out any silhouette or shape to a potential physical craft or not, it does not mean it was not there though. 

  I thought about telling someone at the time of the sighting, but felt it was probably best not to and make a mental note of what I was seeing and record the data/information later. My “gut”/feeling was that this light/craft was intelligently controlled and most likely controlled by a humanoid species of sometime. Just a feeling, who knows.
  I watched this light/light pattern for about 5-8 minutes, then fell back to sleep. This sighting felt like I was supposed to see and feel this experience.
