UFO masquerading as a star or a planet. Expelled tiny object/light.

I went outside for a break to puff on my Vuse (Nicotine vape). I was looking up at the sky and I noticed a star like object in the sky. At first, I thought nothing of it as I dismissed it as a star. It did seem to be very close however. And it was orangish yellow, and very bright. I'm pretty familiar with the planets and the stars, so I was trying to identify what I was looking at, but I did not have my phone on me with my night sky app. But as I was looking at the star, I noticed that as I stared at it, I got a little dizzy and wobbly because the star was moving ever so slightly in a kind of jerky pattern. There were no clouds that were around that were moving, because that's usually my reference (you always think its the stars moving when there are clouds that are actually moving near them). 
As I caught myself staring up at this star in amazement (saying to myself "holy schnikes bat man! That star is moving in an unsteady pattern almost unnoticeable to the untrained eye."), I saw a much smaller white light come out of the star at the bottom, and it went down slowly in an S pattern. I watched it descend for about 10 seconds until I couldn't see it anymore. This whole bit took about 10 minutes, which was my break.
