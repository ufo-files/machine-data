{nd} Glowing purple orb performing extreme direction changes above North Las Vegas

My girlfriend was on her way home and noticed a light in the sky. At first she thought it was just a military plane due to our proximity to the Air Force base but then she noticed the object disappear and then reappear in another location. She started to observe the object and noticed that it wasn't actually disappearing but moving extremely fast to another location and in a different direction then it was heading. When she arrived to the entry of our apartment complex she decided to capture the object on video since she was not driving on the freeway anymore and the object was hovering conveniently above the apartment complex. The video was recorded on her iPhone for about 2 minutes and captured 2 orbs performing extreme maneuvers. The second orb was only noticed after watching the video several times. The second orb appears dimmer and seems to mimic the brighter orb's movements and is located near the brighter orb. The video ends abruptly due to another vehicle pulling in behind her and trying to enter the apartment complex. This sighting took place approximately mid November of 2015 at 9 pm in North Las Vegas.
