What was first seen was a red light and then that disappeared and a long rectangular orange glowing light appeared.

We were sitting on the patio at night, looking out at the sky and talking. I was seated. [name removed/CMS] was standing. We were both looking out toward the southwest toward the distant Native Reservation and Sierra Estrella Mountains. At first, I saw a round reddish light which was like a strange UFO object we had seen months prior. I mentioned it to [name removed/CMS] and then he turned his head and looked out that way, but the light stopped. (On the prior incident we had watched the red light it was overhead, got fuzzy, bigger and bigger and then suddenly shot off into the atmosphere less than the blink of an eye). Both of us stared as an orange light - I'd say the color of cantaloupe with a touch of red tone rectangle light was in the sky. It looked like if you saw toast from a side view, only seeing the long width/short height crust and it was a burning orange color. It suddenly disappeared. The whole time it sat in the sky in the direction of the Estrellas, it just stayed in place, but suddenly it's like it turned off or collapsed into darkness. I asked [name removed/CMS], "You saw that?" And he shook his head. He reported to me the comparison of seeing a slice of glowing orange toast from the side. Rectangular. Must have been fairly big given the distance away which was likely 15 miles away as the crow flies. It was at about 30-35 degrees height off the horizon. He reported seeing a brownish area in the center of the orange. From my vantage point, it seemed like solid orange with edges that had a bit of red-orange tinge. Oddly, I was looking in the direction where a friend and I years ago saw red-orange orbs chasing each other above us. I was ready to take a photo and reached on the table for my cell phone, but it had already gone. Entire incident lasted perhaps 8 seconds.
