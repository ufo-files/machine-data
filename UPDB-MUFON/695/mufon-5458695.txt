{rjl} Several Amber metallic pulsating orbs traveling in a V shape formation.

My son and I were driving up Jurupa Ave between Grand Ave. and Palm Ave. My son yelled for me to stop and look at something in the sky. I pulled over in front of the large church on Jurupa Ave. We got out of the car and looked at 5 or so Amber pulsating orbs traveling in a V formation above us. There was no sound and they seemed very low but taller than the large trees on the church grounds. I took out my cell phone to take a picture, but my phone crashed when I tried. They were flying in an Eastward direction and eventually disappeared.
I have no real evidence and the event happened several years ago. But the memory of it is still very clear. I will never forget what the orbs looked like.
