Cloud-like blimp shaped UFO/OBJECT

While driving north through Martinsville (as we exited off to detour due to construction) we witnessed what first appeared to be a blimp-like object ahead in the sky. It appeared to be moving towards us (southeast). We realized it was not a blimp and I grabbed my phone to take a picture. It seemingly disappeared right as I snapped the photo. I'm not certain if it's visible in the picture or not as it was gone that fast.
It first looked like a blimp but then sort of looked like a small cloud and had no real definitive features.
