{jpg} observed ufo hovering directly over muni airstrip, hidden by tumbling cloud 800 ft above the strip. motionless, no sound emitting pure white light under its bottom, all else hidden by cloud

Sleeping in my tent under the wing of my Cessna at Cordova municipal airstrip. Midnight saturday/Sunday am. Walked across the gravel strip to eyak lake to find what was making tremendous muffled booming sounds. Determined it was very large blocks of floating ice colliding with other blocks even though surface of lake appeared as one ice sheet. With mystery solved I looked directly overhead to see how bad wx was since I had to fly in the morning. Directly overhead embedded on the tumbling roiling cloud was a ufo soundless emitting pure white light from its underside. Rest of craft hidden in cloud. 800 feet above the ground directly above me. My plane was tied down in line with other planes, I was the only one there. Wx was very stirred up and clear below level of the churning cloud. Heard wind in nearby trees on the hill next to the strip but no sound from the object. I was tired and thought if they're watching me I can't deal with this and went back to my tent to sleep my name is [name removed/CMS/jpg]
