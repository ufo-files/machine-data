Bright Jupiter likeness (but not because Jupiter was also in sight). It moved say 10 azimuth and then just disappeared. It was not a plane--it did not blink or have other lights. It is in the opposite direction of the airport, which is to the east. The only possibility is a satellite, but the sun had set 3.5 hours earlier, and the light did not fade, it shut off/disappeared. I wish it had lasted longer so that I could have tried to video it. I was still in the "what the heck is that?" phase when it disappeared.
