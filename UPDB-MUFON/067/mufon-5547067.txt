Glowing bright white suspended high in the air

Me and my grandma were about to leave safeway when the object zoomed into view in front of my point of view. At that moment it seemed to move closer to us, but when we started to drive the object seemed to follow us. When we stopped, so would the object. During the experience the object appeared to be surrounded by a forcefield. The object appeared to be about a half mile away. At first I thought it was just another star in the sky. My reactions were same as most people: scared enough to be speechless. Suddenly 8 minutes later, it vanished into thin air...
