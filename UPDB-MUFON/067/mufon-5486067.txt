{nd} began as an amber color pulsating, turned to white and moved rapidly, slowed and turned red with 2 lights

Playing with child after dinner. Chased ball. Noticed a large orange/amber light in the north east sky, slowly pulsating. called daughter over. WE watchd for 15 to 20 minutes as it hovered, then increased altitude and pushed east towards the ocean. as it did so, the amber pulsating turned to a solid white light. moved rapidly across the southeast coast of NC, then hovered back over land south of my position. Turned Red, one light turned into two. flickered for 1 minute or so, then disappeared.
