{cak} Bright white light emitting spotlight towards ground then slowly faded away

I was driving to work down Antelope rd. in Citrus Heights Ca. I looked to my left and saw a bright white light with what looked like a cone shaped mist of fog. It then emitted a bright spotlight towards the ground. I thought it was a helicopter but as I kept looking I did not see any flashing lights. At one point I saw a plane fly by it and could clearly see the planes flashing lights. When the object turned off its spotlight I decided to try to catch a video of it. Unfortunately by the time I started recording the object was much dimmer. At its brightest it was as luminous as the moon. After I stopped recording I noticed a strange wispy looking cloud/contrail in a looping type of pattern.
