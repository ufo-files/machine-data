{cak} Orange glowing ball of light flying overhead without a trail behind flew overhead and headed off over Lake Huron

My wife and I were in our backyard walking through our backyard heading in after covering our bonfire for the night and we noticed a large orange ball of light in the sky streaming from the southwest. It flew approximately 50 miles per hour overhead. I had time to grab my cell phone and try to record it but to no avail. It headed out over Lake Huron towards Canada and we lost sight of it. I did not hear any sound from the object but my wife said she heard a hum or buzzing sound from the object.
