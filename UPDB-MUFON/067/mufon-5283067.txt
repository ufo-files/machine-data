First seen in the east, south east from our home.

It was moving back and forth then up and down, then back and forth as if it was looking for something. It was in the area for a good 45 mins, but would seem to fade out then come back. It would get bright the dim.
We do not have a camera, but my son has able to get a picture with his camera phone though our telescope. Why I am reporting this: is it is very strange that what ever this is came back the following night in same area doing the same thing. It did seem to be getting a bit closer though. I looked as if it might be getting closer to Hwy. 27 which was between the object and our location.
We then noticed a second object that was further north doing the same thing with movements and colors. We could not get it focused in the scope for a picture. I would be interested if any one else from our area saw this thing.
