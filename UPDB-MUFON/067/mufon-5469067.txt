{cak} Several UFOs in sky

I was taking my dog out around 10pm and began looking up at the sky when I noticed 4 objects all close to one another with quick red and white blinking lights. It then looked like a much larger ship came in range and the smaller ships began to disburse. I only saw 2 airplanes come within the airspace. I watched the ships move in all different directs, in an almost zig zag or snake like pattern across the sky. The larger one only appeared for a short time compared to the others. I walked to my front yard to follow them and noticed to the south one was very low, below the tree line just hovering. There was another to the west that looked as if something shot out from it. I was on the phone with my boyfriend the entire time. I stayed outside for about 45 minutes. When I went inside I checked the JAX flight log and there were NO departing flights during that time and only 1 scheduled to land. I tried taking pics but honestly it looks like a very faint just blinking red light for a couple of seconds.
