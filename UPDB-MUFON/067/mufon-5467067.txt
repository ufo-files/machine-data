Saw a triangle shaped object flying over with lights on each corner

I was just getting in to my room to get dressed and ready to sleep with my window open, the window was facing a small forest above, I was walking past the window when I saw a very big triangle shaped object with bright lights on the corners, I swiftly looked out the window to see it continue hovering over the forest away from my view. The strange thing is the lights on it didn't seem to light up the forest at all. The object did not rotate either, and I didn't hear ANYTHING as it passed by. The object was moving in a diagonal path South East as it slowly moved not to far from my apartment window.

I am writing this about an hour afterwards, and so I am still pretty shaken about it. I was only looking at this site the other day, and I've never seen a UFO in my entire life until now. It creeped me out after seeing it disappear over the horizon after.
