Even though the sky was dark, I saw a darker rolling/falling object very fast across part of the Eastern sky.

I love sitting out on my balcony on a clear starry night and look into the sky and just take in the beauty. I like to see falling stars or meteorites and I really like it when sometimes I observe what looks like moving stars. On the evening of Sept. 12th I was sitting on the balcony around 10:30PM and was doing my usual viewing of the sky. Then about 11PM I couldn't believe what I saw and am not exactly sure how to describe it. I live in the city and even though there on some lights around the area I can still get a very large view of the stars in the Eastern sky. So whatever this was showed up darker than the sky behind it. Tumbling is the best word that I can use to describe it, the event was very fast. I didn't see it land anywhere, just disappeared after about 15 seconds. Also noticed that it seemed to leave some sort of black trail behind it. My reactions was wow what did I just see. Everything that I have ever seen has always been illuminated against the dark sky, never have I seen a dark object.
