Solid bright white light with two trailing smaller lights fully crossed the Salt Lake valley while being directly trailed by a helicopter.

A solid, bright white light with a flat shape crossed the Salt Lake Valley from east to west in about three minutes. There were two much smaller, dimmer solid white lights following it in formation. These smaller lights were approx 1000 and 2000 feet behind the first light. The first trailing light was at the same altitude and the second trailing light was around 100 feet higher. This formation persisted for the entire crossing of the valley.

Directly following these lights was a helicopter with red and green flashing lights. It was a particularly loud helicopter, and having covered over twenty miles in three minutes, it was traveling several hundred MPH (suggesting a military helicopter). The helicopter was around 3000 feet behind the formation of white lights.

The only sound I heard was that of the helicopter.

The lights and helicopter first appeared in the East from over the Wasatch mountains near the Summit at Snowbird and traveled directly west until disappearing behind the Oquirrh Mountains (In the general direction of Dugway Proving Grounds).
