{rjl} Disc appeared while in motion over power lines and disappeared within a second

As my wife and I were heading north on Friendsville Rd. in Wooster Ohio, I was driving and actually looking through my windshield (it's a heck of an invention all these texters should look through once in awhile), and noticed the disc suddenly appear. It was heading in a downward (ish) trajectory and probably no further than 50 feet above some high tension power lines. As fast as it appeared it just vanished from view. My take on the siting was that it, for some reason, had to "uncloak" to absorb electrical energy from the power lines. It was dullish grey in color. Unfortunately, my wife only noticed the movement as she was not looking straight out the windshield. It's not too exciting, but it did, in fact, happen.
