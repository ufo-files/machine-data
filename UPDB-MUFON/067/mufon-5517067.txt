THE OBJECT HAD ALSO CHANGED SHAPE TO A CUBE AND THEN TO A CONE OF SORTS. THE OBJECT MADE NO SOUND DURING THE ENTIRE SIGHTING. I WAS ABLE TO GET A SHORT VIDEO CLIP OF THIS WHICH I WOULD LIKE YOU TO SEE.
