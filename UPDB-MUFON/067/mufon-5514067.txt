I witnessed a bright white Orb passing overhead that flew North eaat from Vancouver, passing a half kilomter in front of a smalll cessna airplane

Myself and 2 other people where standing on the outside garage patio when we all witness a bright white Orb passing 30 degrees overhead. The object made no sound and was travelling hundreds of miles per hour. It passed in front of a small cessna type airplane at perhaps the same altitude at a distance of 1/2 kilometre from the small aircraft that was flying at normal cruise speed. The Orb asended gradually towards the stars where we lost sight of it, amazing!
