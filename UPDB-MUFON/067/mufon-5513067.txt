(GS) Witnessed a boomerang shaped object overhead while driving east on Bake Parkway in Lake Forest. Red and white lights blinked and were brighter than an aircraft.

Was driving with my teen daughter on Bake Pkwy when I noticed strange lights in the sky. The lights resembled an airplane but were brighter and blinked faster. They did not look the same as an airplane or helicopter. The lights appeared to be red and white. I asked my daughter what she thought it was and she agreed that the lights looked strange. She said that it looked like a star. We kept driving and as we were talking I noticed that the object was overhead. I looked out of the top of the windshield and saw a boomerang shaped object. I only saw it for a few seconds and then I couldnt see it anymore. I told my daughter to watch it as I was driving and she thought she saw a boomerange shape momentarily as well. It continued on a SE path and then we lost sight of it.
