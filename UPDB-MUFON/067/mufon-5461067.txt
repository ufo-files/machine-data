{jpg} Several lights very high up we’re darting around and seemed to send or shoot blobs of light at a bigger object.

Several smaller orbs of light appeared very high in the western sky and darted around for a few minutes and then became significantly smaller. I assumed that they were ascending. Then I noticed another object much higher up that was stationary. lol of these objects were just little lights. I assumed that the bigger one was a star. 

Then the little ones darted around some more and began to shoot what looked like balls of light toward the bigger one. The balls of light became smaller and smaller as the moved toward the bigger one then disappeared. I would say that they each shot 5-7 at a time and they moved very fast. 

This is when I woke up my daughter to tell her to come outside to look. We watched as these things darted around the bigger one for about an hourl or more. At one point there was a very bright flash on light from the bigger one after one of those strings of balls of light reached it. . Looked like an explosion to us but it was still there afterwards. We watched the stars move behind the object as they made their way across the sky. The bigger object never moved. It stayed about 11 o’clock almost straight above us. There was at least one airliner that flew under them as they continued to dart around. The lights seemed way higher than the plane. At one point the bigger one just disappeared. Then it was back in the same spot a few minutes later. The smaller ones would dart away occasionally but didn’t do much other than what I’ve described. Clouds started moving in and we went inside. Nothing had changed up there the same behavior was still going on between the clouds when I last looked.
