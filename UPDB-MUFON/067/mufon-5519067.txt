Looked like a star moving slowly across sky then just stopped.

I have had 4 sightings over the years the one above was in the mid 60s. Was sleeping out w/friends,not really getting any sleep. Was laying on a sleeping bag on the ground looking up at the sky, it was a very clear night. All of a sudden I saw what looked like a star moving across the sky, it appeared to be as high up as the stars. It went a short distance across the sky and then just stopped. I watched the spot until I fell asleep. I didn't say anything to the other guys due to it happening so fast, and trying to pick out the light in the sky with all the stars would have been pointless. And they would have said your seeing things.

The other sightings I had were in the 80s while on patrol. (Policeman) These sightings were also bright lights but werent real high in the sky. There were on flashing lights or any noise. In two of these sightings the object seemed to be hovering in the sky until it seemed like it knew I was watching it and took off across the sky at very high rate of speed, again there was no sound or any other lights flashing or blinking, just one bright light. The other sighting was just a bright light moving across the sky from west to east, no sound or any other blinking or flashing lights.

I was in the military, on an aircraft carrier so I know what planes and helicopters sound and look like. And over the years as a police officer I have observed thousands of airplanes and helicopters while on the night shift. These objects I observed did not resemble any aircraft I know of.
