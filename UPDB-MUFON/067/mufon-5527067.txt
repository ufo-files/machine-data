Spotted UFO shaped like the body of a Dragonfly with no wings, glowing red like embers of fire. In Southeast sky. Hight of average jet. Moved westward.

I went out with my dog to see the supermoon. It was too low on the horizen or something and cloudy. Instead I saw a UFO shaped like the body of a Dragonfly without wings. It was glowing red like the embers of a fire and its light fluctuated like embers also. It was in the Southeast sky about the distance I would normally see a jet. It moved slowly westward till it got near the top of my big pine tree and got small and disapeared. It didn't really look like it just went behind a cloud so I moved over trying to see it but it was gone. I think I watched it for 1-5 minuets. I've checked the files on MUFON for awhile to see if anyone else saw it to no avail so I thought I'd better report it.
