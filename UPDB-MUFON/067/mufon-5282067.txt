Only seen flight

Description
2 self illuminating cylindrical tubes high in the night sky shined through curtains. Got up to see them hovering. Then lights shot back and forth between the two cylinders, I turned my head for just a moment and then I seen 7 round self illuminating balls in its place falling down to the west. They simultaneously stopped above the horizon and went back up in the sky to the east and started falling again. They stopped simultaneously again but this time when they went up they went into a V formation as they flew into the sky to the east.
