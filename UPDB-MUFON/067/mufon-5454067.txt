{jpg} lights above stratosphere

there was at least 15 lights approximately a mile apart flying from the west to east. a few broke off towards the north. they were above the stratosphere at first glance they looked like Satellites but they kept coming in a line evenly spaced apart. After the ten or so there was one that was heading North. It would flash then disappear for about a minute or so then flash again. The lights started changing direction at the end, instead of following the west/east line they were starting to shoot to the Northeast. I am not sure when they first appeared as I was not outside when it turned dark. There was even spacing as they appeared.
