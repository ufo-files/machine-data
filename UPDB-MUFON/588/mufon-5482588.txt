Two capsule like things came from different directions - became one - again became two - vanished in different directions

It happened exactly 16 years back. I was 16 years old. I was playing on roof top with my friend. We were playing a game of finding things. He asked me to show him a bird. I looked around but could not find. I looked everywhere in the sky but could not see a single bird. My friend asked me to keep looking and went downstairs on the nature's call. I kept looking in the sky.
After about 30-40 seconds, I felt I saw something. I looked hard and saw a capsule like object moving in the sky. I kept my eyes on it. It was hard to keep track of it. Nor because it was moving fast, but it was like transparent and far and high in the sky.

After around 5 seconds, it came to halt and there appeared another capsule joining it. Both the capsules became one and it remained so for around 2 and a half minutes.
I was very curious to see what happen next. So, I just kept looking.
Suddenly, I flash from somewhere on the ground dazzled me and I could just see the two objects flying away in different directions. 

I told my friends and they made fun of me. They called me a villager who doesn't know about a rocket. Since then, I never told anyone about it.
