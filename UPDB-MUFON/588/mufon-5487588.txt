{dr}Sighted hovering yellow light - white light while moving east - changed to northwest

my fianc1 was taking out the garbage and asked me to come look at the very bright star. When I saw it, I told her that it was a flare from Ft. Carson, but we should get her binoculars to observe it. She did, but I went looking for my camera and cell phone to call for additional witnesses. When I came back out, she told me that she watched it go very fast in a ENE direction, then turn toward the NW. 
When I looked, I thought it looked like an airplane flying toward the NW. There was another airplane flying in a NNE (normal flight path) direction.
When she described the movements and speed, I knew it was not a flare or an aircraft. It was still in the sky traveling away from us at what appeared to be aircraft speed so I decided to call 911.
Later, a policeman came out and interviewed us and we were able to point out the directions in the sky.
