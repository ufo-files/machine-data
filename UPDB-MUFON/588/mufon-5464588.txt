{dr}Black triangle silent large craft

Driving northwest from tri cities to Yakima observed an extremely bright light in the distance. We thought it was a really large plane that was flying really low. Stopped in Grandview, got out of the car and the object flew straight over us. We observed a very large low flying craft with three stationary white lights in a triangle formation with one red blinking light in the center. It appeared to be very large, low flying and made no noise, completely silent. It seemed to move smoothly in a straight line.
