I was in my room and saw strange lights in the sky. Green, yellow and red. Got my binoculars and it was a strange black object.

I was in room getting ready to celebrate new years when I suddenly noticed an object moving slowly about 400 feet away. It seemed to be flashing three different light:green, red and yellow. I first thought it was just a helicopter but at close inspection with my binaculars, I noticed that the object was bimp like and completly dark. The object continued to move at the same direction at a steady speed. At that point I got a bit nervous and pulled out my phone to record the strange incident. After around three minutes I lost the sight of the object due to my viewing angle. After around five minutes or so a Dutch fighter jet passed overhead. Quite scary.
