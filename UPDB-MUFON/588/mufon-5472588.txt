{jpg} Moved almost imperceptibly. Two lights of differing size; yellow. No sound. Seemed large.

At approximately 8:30pm EST, we were sitting on the back patio in the dark, talking and viewing the stars. My mother looked up and noticed two lights. She asked if they were moving or stationery because she was unable to tell. After a few seconds of observation, the UFO was noted to be moving slowly, steadily, almost imperceptibly in what I believe was an eastward direction. It seemed large. We could not discern it's shape since it was dark outside. There were two lights which were yellowish and appeared to differ in size from each other. They did not pulsate or flash. Although we are uncertain of the UFO's altitude at the time it was sighted, it seemed as though we would have been able to hear it, if it had been an airplane of any sort. We live close to a military base and frequently see large aircraft... always hear them. This made no sound. It was only visible for 7-10 seconds and seemed to be moving directly away from us and suddenly disappeared from sight. 
We were intrigued as this is not the first time we have seen "something" here in Havelock, but it has been a while.
