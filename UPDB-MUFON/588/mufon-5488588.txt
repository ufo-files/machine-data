{jpg} Was taking photos of the super moon eclipse while looking at the photos noticed something strange in the photo

At 10pm I was out front of my apartment getting ready for the moon to peak above the mountains that are to the ENE of my location so I could photograph the Super Moon, Blood moon, and Total Eclipse that was all taking place. I set up my camera as the moon was coming from very the mountain. As I was taking photos and looking at them I saw something strange in one of them. There was something that was captured in the photo that has some light to it and it appeared to move in one direction then chance direction and head back from where it came from. Not sure what it is at all. It was only on the one photo. I know there were aircraft in the area but to me it doesn't look like anything an aircraft would so. I think it could be a UFO. I have heard before that there has been one seen in the area earlier in the year but I don't remember who said it. I never did see the object with my own eyes. Just in the photo my camera took.
