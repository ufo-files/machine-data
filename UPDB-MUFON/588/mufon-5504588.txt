I was with my then girlfriend at the time and we had just got to the mall after eating lunch. I was waiting for my convertable top to close when I looked up to see why it was taking so long. I noticed something flying in the sky that at first I assumed was a bird because of she shape, but then I thought about it and realized it was way too high to be a bird and it's movement was too steady, not like a bird in flight. As I was trying to figure out what it was, it picked up speed and begain to do very tight figure eight's. In my amazement I tried to point it out to my girlfriend, but she wasn't wearing her glasses and couldn't see that far. All of the sudden it shot into the upper atmosphere and out of sight. There was no noise at all during the event. I couldn't believe what I had just witnessed. I tried to make since of what I saw and not just jump to the conclusion that it was an alien craft. I couldn't explain it. I won't pretend to know every craft we have in our employ in the USAF, but I'm sure we don't have anything that can bank like that.
