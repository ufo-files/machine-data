Two Objects Seen In Brewer, Maine 12/15/21

While at lunch at at 1:28 pm at work on 12/15/21, I Looked out of the break room window and saw two oddly-shaped objects hovering. They did not make any sound. I captured some video of them, but the top of the window was in the way. I went outside to get a better shot, and noticed that one of the objects was gone. The other just went straight up and appeared to leave the atmosphere. I watched it for about ten minutes until it was completely gone.
