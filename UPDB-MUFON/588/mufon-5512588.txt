bright red light seen in paddock

Daughter and myself were driving downn main rd and to the left of us in paddock was a VERY bright red orb? I always keep my eye on the rd but something made me look out my daughters window. It was very low in the paddock which is hilly, very grey overcast stormy day. My daughter was bewildered but made me feel excited, something so close! Had to look back to the rd and it just vanished.
