Red & orange blinking light, with a red orbe shooting out of it. Then a red/white flashing orbe, other red orbes hovering around main light, with a orange/whitish flash.

It was a Sunday evening around 11:55pm, I was sitting on my couch having a beer, I got up to use the washroom and grab another beer. Before I sat back down, I looked out my front living room window to check over the neighbourhood. That's when I noticed the big flashing orbe in the North Western sky. It was extremely bright and did not look like a plane. I grabbed my blackberry and decided to film it. After 3 mins of filming this orbe, I decided to shut my camera off and sit back down. It wasn't until 2 mins later I got up to see if it was still out there and it was gone. After I sat back down, I opened up my beer and decided to look at the video and was extremely shocked at what I seen. At the 1:55 mark on the video I noticed a smaller red solid orbe shoot out of the side of the big flashing orbe. A red & white light flashing can be seen coming back towards the big flashing orbe. I also observed at the 2:20 mark on the video 2 red orbes around the big flashing orbe. Also at the 2:45 mark there is a orange flash coming off the big flashing orbe. I noticed none of this while I was filming, only after when I reviewed the video I seen these other orbes. I woke up my family up to show them this video as attached.
