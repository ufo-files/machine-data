Saw 3 bright lights in the far distance while fishing with my family

At lake fishing. Sky was clear and full of stars. We noticed two very bright lights across the lake, above the tree line, but further away. One of them was higher than the other. They had some movement but not in unison. I thought they were getting closer for a second when the lower light went out and we couldn’t see it anymore. About 30 seconds later the other light went out and they were gone. We starred at the sky to see if we could see anything, but all we saw were airplanes that were very high in the sky traveling on a flight path. Then we noticed a third orb right over the tree line across that lake but it quickly turned off the light and we didn’t see anything else. I took a photo of the area we saw the ufos the following day.
