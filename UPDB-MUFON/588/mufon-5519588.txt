After seeing 3 ufos (no photo) at about 2pm.....later that night about 9pm

I saw (witness) 3 ufos about 10,000 ft at around 2pm, They were all 3 one on top of the other, maybe 800 to 1000 feet apart. Bright like an intense beam of light, like a Halogen lamp. The lowest one made a quick right turn and flew into a cloud. The next one up, moved down to the first ones position and then made a quick right turn and followed the first into the same cloud. The 3rd light moved down a bit slower (still very fast) and had a trail of light behind, until it moved into the position of the last light. Then it moved into the cloud (quick right) and dissappeared into it. 
About 2 minutes later, the cloud(the only cloud in the sky) dissappeared, and there was nothing.It was the only cloud in the sky also.....it was a beautiful and clear day!

Later that night I was looking in the sky after my curiosity had a hold of me, and observed the moon and a small round throbing and pulsating light near by the moon. I did take videos of this light and have those videos now. I didn't think about it until now, hoping to get some answers. 

I am an X US Navy veteran, and I know what I see to be a fact. I and have a pretty good knowledge of many types of aircraft. I have also worked very close to aircraft carriers in the Pacific Ocean.
