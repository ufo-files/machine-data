{jpg} On 7/26/2019, 7:27pm. Airborne gray cylinder seen through the cloud breaks over Hayward 880N.

On 7/26/2019, 7:27pm, we had just dropped off my 2 older daughters at dance practice in Fremont and had entered highway 880N. About 1 minute into freeway we witnessed an airborne gray cylinder through the cloud breaks. The object was traveling NE and the event lasted approximately 5 mins. I first noticed what appeared to be contrails coming from the object, but quickly realized that the perceived contrails were traveling with the object. The trails are not visible in digital pics or video and the object appears a bright glow in pics and video.
