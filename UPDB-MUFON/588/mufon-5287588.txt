I was letting my cat out of the back door into the garden & I noticed what I at first thought was a low flying aeroplane

It was Saturday night at just after 10pm & my cat wanted to be let out into the back garden of my home.I let her out & stayed with her as we have foxes in the garden & whilst I was waiting for her I looked at the sky.I could not help seeing,what I at first thought was a very low flying large aeroplane,but it looked a bit odd.I live on the direct Gatwick airport flight path,so am used to seeing planes fly over & what they look like...having spent a lot of my life attending airshows etc.This did not look right,it was very long & did not have the usual red,white & green navigation lights;it just kept on flashing bright red on & off constantly.Also when the red light flashed it was in a large trianguler shape,infact there appeared to be two red triangles flashing together one in front of the other & they were large triangles.There was absolutely no sound at all,this object was low enough that if it had been a plane I would have definitely heard it quite loudly.So I was just a bit puzzled,I wrote this sighting down when it happened & didn`t know wether to report it or not.I finally decided that I would report it,maybe someone else saw the same thing.I didn`t manage to get a photo or video,it all happened quite quickly & I didn`t have my camera or phone on me.Was also trying to get the cat back indoors as foxes around here,have started attacking pet cats recently,for some reason.
