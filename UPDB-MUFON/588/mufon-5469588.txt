{rjl} Multiple UFOs seen star like, strobing, and black triangle

My two friends and I were outside just hanging out for his birthday it was around midnight. I was the first one to notice the objects. It was the size of a star and just took off from place to place all over the sky. It did this multiple times and when I showed my two friends it grabbed their attention also. After about 20 minutes we started to see these saucer like shapes that were rotating with many different colored lights going around these UFOs. After about 20 minutes of observing these objects darting across the sky back and forth at a low altitude. We had the bright idea of flashing our flashlights at the objects. About 5 minutes later right above the tree tops right next to his house a black triangle appeared and was gliding towards us. It was massive atleast the size of a football field. It blocked out the star's and had a low hum to it. It was going very slow. There were not any lights on the craft. We were all very nervous asking each other what it was. It was lit up by the street lamps that is how low it was. We let it get right above of us before one of my friends said run and we followed suit. It was terrifying. I have 4 degrees and have no mental issues. I just wanted to finally tell my story in case anything ever happened to me.
