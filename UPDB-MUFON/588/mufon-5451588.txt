UFO fleet over Red Square, Moscow_December 2019

This event is somehow linked to my MUFON case #98548, dated February 21, 2018. In fact, the 3-spheres object I recorded last December 10, 2019 over Saint Basil’s Cathedral (see photo IMG_0237.JPG) appears to be identical to the 3-spheres object I recorded in the very same location 22 months earlier. The same can be said for the other "white lights" appearing in both sightings. In 2018 I did not notice anything in the sky, until I discovered the objects in my photos the day after. This time I thoroughly checked the sky, in the "hope" that "something" could be there again. Guess what: despite my best efforts I was not able to see anything in the overcast sky, nothing at all, just found the "objects" in my photos later that night. I also took a specific photo against the cloud coverage (IMG_0246.JPG) just to check if something was there or not: on the spot I saw nothing but clouds, on the picture there are two lights. I leave to you to define what those "objects" might be or might not be. No sounds, no noise, all invisible to the naked eye, but not to a smartphone camera.
