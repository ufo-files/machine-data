(GS) THREE OBJECTS OBSERVED IN A CLEAR NIGHT SKY PERFORMING AMAZING FLIGHT.

I WAS AN MP ATTACHED TO THE 24TH INFANTRY DIVISION AT FORT STEWART GEORGIA.SECURITY SPECIALIST.WE WERE TRAVELING BACK TO POST AFTER MANY DAYS IN THE FIELD PREPARING FOR DEPLOYMENT TO FORT IRWIN TO PARTICIPATE IN DESERT STING.IT WAS VERY LATE AND ALL WERE TIRED.WE RECIEVED AN AIR ATTACK ALERT AND DISMOUNTED OUR JEEPS AT ONCE.POINTING MY WEAPON AT THE SKY WHILE LAYING ON MY BACK I SAW NOTHING BUT A FANTASTIC NIGHT SKY.THINKING WHAT THE HELL ONE LAST EXERCISE I WAITED FOR THE ALL CLEAR.IT WAS GIVEN MINUTES LATER.AS I WAS GETTING BACK IN THE JEEP I LOOKED UP AND SAW THREE OBJECTS THAT LOOKED LIKE THE STARS AROUND THEM.THEY PERFORMED PERFECT CIRCLES IN THE SKY.SAME ALTITUDE,SAME SPEED,SAME SPACING.IT WAS PERFECTION DIFFICULT TO RELAY.GRAVITY BY NO MEANS CAME INTO PLAY.I FELT IN AWE.I REMEMBER HOLDING ONTO MY HOLSTERED 45 THAT CONTAINED LIVE AMMO.
