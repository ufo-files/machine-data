{kt}A dozen stars twinkle in a cloudy night sky Over Colorado Springs, then fly away!!!

July 4th Colorado Springs, CO -Getting out of a truck to go inside home, I see a dozen stars in the night sky over Colorado Springs. This is odd since there are no other visible stars in the sky due to a thick cloud cover that night. As I stare directly at this silent group of (12 or more) lights, wondering which constellation it was....the whole group of lights started to slowly drift away in a northern direction until they were out of my sight.
