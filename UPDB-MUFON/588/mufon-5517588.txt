Triangle Shaped UFO captured over Victoria Park, London UK - 25/05/2012

I was sitting in Victoria park London listening to music when I spotted a matt black coloured object floating in the sky. 

It appeared to be the size of a helicopter viewed at close quarters. 

I actually thought it was a helicopter, but as I carried on viewing this object, it quickly became clear to me that I was not looking at a helicopter. 

It was roughly a triangle shape.

I estimate it was about half a mile away.

It made no sound.. but just glided across the sky from the east in a north westerly direction.

The distance at its closest point from me was about 300-500 yards and flying at an estimated height of about 5000 ft or more

The sighting lasted 2 minutes .
