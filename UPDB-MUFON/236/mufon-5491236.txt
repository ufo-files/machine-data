My location was on the hill above my home at about 10:40 pm PST.I was taking pictures of Venus and Jupiter trying to keep a record of the planets while they moved toward their conjunction in July. We have so much unidentified craft activity up in this area that I was rather blase about the whole thing. I felt exctted about capturing a photo, but not too excited about the orb itself.
