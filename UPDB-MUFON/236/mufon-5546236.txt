Black sphere flying under clouds vanished once it hit the blue sky

After planting some germinated Morning Glory Seeds, I went out to the pipeline behind my house to smoke a cigarette. It was 4:00. I looked up at the sky where I saw yesterday's (6/18/2010) two UFOs, not expecting to see anything but still curious. To my amazement, I saw a black sphere flying under the clouds. I watched it for a minute until it made it's way to the blue sky. Once it reached the edge of the cloud, it vanished as if it could only be seen under a cloud. It flew in the same direction as my June 18th sightings, North East, though lower in altitude.
