A star like object, in an arc like pattern, moved acrossed our sky this evening.

This evening as we were sitting on our patio looking at the sky we observed a star like object that was moving rapidly through the sky. It moved in an arc pattern and came up over our garage area and went down and out of sight on the other side of the roof. It did not leave a trail. but it looked way to big and bright to be a falling star.
