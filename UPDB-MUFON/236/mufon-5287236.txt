Disk filmed in California that zooms out of the frame at high speeds

I was on a trip to LA with some buddies of mine when we passed through Morongo Valley and i spotted this object. It looked flat but was very far in the distance so it was hard to tell anything about it. It was so bright out in that direction that i remember not even being able to see right but i knew something was there so i grabbed my i phone and recorded it. My friends all saw it too but their used to me pointing out dots in the sky. I didn't even think anything of it myself until i looked at the footage when we came back from our trip and saw that the object had zoomed off at what looked like supersonic speeds. I'm no expert but i've never seen an aircraft move so fast besides my vehicle before. I've thought a-lot about what it could have been, maybe a glare or reflection from my lens? I don't think it's any sort of glare against the car window though because i had the window down while filming. I also don't think it's a glare because it looks as though it disappears behind the trees and trucks before re appearing from behind them; if it was a glare then it would remain on top of the trees and truck in the footage right? Anyways, like i said, i'm not an expert - just a curious kid that got some footage and wants some feedback on it if there's any professionals that are willing to take a look.
