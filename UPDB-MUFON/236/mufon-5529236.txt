Bright light appeared, not moving, then started shaking/moving and changing shapes

We were in our driveway when my husband noticed a very bright light in the distance. It was kind of low in the sky. We were wondering what it could possibly be. Too bright to be a planet/star or moon. Maybe a plane? We stared for about 4 min. and it did not move at all. I was then getting very curious. I went in the house to get my video camera. When I came out I started recording. It then started to move a little and turn? Not sure how to describe... the more I zoomed, the more it glared and then I started to notice it moving., so I would zoom out and just try to let the camera focus. It seemed to break into 2 lights then started changing colors. I noticed the colors red and green. The shape appeared to change, but now I'm wondering if maybe it was turning or something? It almost seemed to turn into a glowing ball at one point. It was the craziest thing we have ever seen. It kept getting out of our sight behing a rooftop, and we kept moving to see it. It finally got smaller and took off quickly. I have to say, I was a little concerned it might know I was video taping it. I have never really put much thought into ufo's before this.
