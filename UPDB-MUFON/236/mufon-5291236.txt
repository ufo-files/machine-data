Bright white star-like light then abruptly turned dim red

Noticed a very strangely bright light a little below the right star of Orion's belt in east and stopped to check it out. It abruptly then seemingly turned off the bright white light and became a dimmer red orb. Then began to fly gradually in front of the car to the north west I want to say and then eventually out of sight.
