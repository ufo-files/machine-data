{td} I WAS LAYING IN THE SUN AND LOOKED UP AND SAW THESE SILVER CHROME REFLECTIVE DOTS HIGH IN THE SKY

FILLED OUT AND FILED BY MUFON FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18809 AT MUFON HEADQUARTERS ON MAY 29TH, 2018
-----------------------------------------------------------------
IT WAS HOT AND I WAS LAYING OUT IN THE BACKYARD OF MY HOME TRYING TO GET A TAN IT WAS LATE JULY OR AUGUST IN 2006. I LOOKED UP AND SAW THESE CHROME REFLECTIVE DOTS ALL LINED UP HIGH IN THE SKY. THE ONE VERTICAL ROW WAS ABOUT THREE DOTS THEN A SPACE WHERE A DOT SHOULD HAVE BEEN AND THEN THREE MORE VERTICAL DOTS. THEN TO THE LEFT THERE WERE 5 MORE VERTICAL DOTS LINED UP TO MOVE IN WHERE THE SPACE WAS ON THE FIRST SET OF VERTICAL DOTS. I DIDN'T SEE THEM MOVE, BUT I HAD THE FEELING SOMEHOW THEY WERE ABOUT TO MOVE INTO THE SPACE. THE DOTS ALL HOVERED TOGETHER AND NEVER VARIED FROM THE FORMATION. THEY WERE VERY HIGH UP.
I LOOKED UP AT 90 DEGREES AND IT WAS ABOUT 10:30 IN THE SKY TO THE EAST OF ME.
MY GUESS WAS IT MIGHT HAVE BEEN THE SPACE STATION AND SOMETHING ELSE WITH IT.
WHEN THE FIELD INVESTIGATOR CONTACTS ME I WILL HAVE CONTACTED MY RELATIVE TO GET A BETTER DATE ON THE EVENT BECAUSE RIGHT AFTER THIS EVENT I WENT TO HELP HIM REMODEL A HOUSE.
