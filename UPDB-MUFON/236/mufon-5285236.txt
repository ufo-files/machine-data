Bright White Light with a Smaller Bright Blue Light

I was taking out the trash (why I didn't have my phone on me) and saw a plane on it's approach to SNA (John Wayne Airport). I noticed out of the corner of my eye a bright light that I initially thought was Venus or another bright planet. As I looked at it I saw it was a very bright white light with a smaller bright blue (not bluish-white -- bright blue) light above it and to the left about 33 deg. or so. I watched it cruise right across the sky from West to East. I tried to hear any engine noise or props or drone noises. The only thing I could hear (when the louder passenger jet passed) was a slight electric sound l(hum, buzz) like when you are near power lines. The smaller blue light eventually disappeared as it got closer to the 90 to the east.

I couldn't tell whether the lights were 1 or not. They moved together at the exact same speed.
