On the evening of Oct 27th 2014 650pm while standing out front of our home in Essington Delaware co pa. preparing to watch the launch of the Antares Rocket from East Coast of VA. we saw what we though was the rocket coming out of the S/E sky it was coming straight up and arching over head toward the north eastern part of the sky. it traveled at a very high speed at great height miles above the earth made no noise and appeared to have a tail like rocket exhaust, it was gone and out of sight with in minutes crossed the entire sky from s/e to n/e. we thought we had just witnessed the Antares rocket launch. only to later learn that the launch was postponed do to a sail boat in the area.   three pictures attached third one is zoomed in
