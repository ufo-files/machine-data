{tjd} Shiny metallic oval traveling eastward, then reversed course, accelerated, and disappeared

At approximately 2:39 PM on November 25, 2018, I witnessed a shiny, metallic oval traveling below the cloud layer from west to east in the airspace above Loma / I-70. I was traveling at 50+ MPH southbound. This craft traveled horizontally at a typical speed for approximately 45 seconds. The craft then changed shape from a thin oval to a fat oval and had a very bright reflection. At this point the craft reversed direction and climbed (10 degree) by either making a 180 degree turn with no forward progress or by maintaining its orientation and flying in the opposite direction. The acceleration of the craft made its trajectory to follow. It was so quick that the initial acceleration made the craft look like a straight line for a fraction of a second until its velocity was so great that it appeared to "disappear". 

The craft had no position lights and no contrails. The cloud layer was at least 30K feet. I later saw an airliner flying below the clouds and estimated it was at 24 to 30K in altitude. 

Without knowing about the craft's size, I thought it appeared to be around 5 to 15K feet in altitude.

I contacted the private Mack airport to ask about flight operations with no luck.

I am a retired Naval Flight Officer with over 1,000 hours of flight time.
