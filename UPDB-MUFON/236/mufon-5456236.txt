Very large UFO seem from British Airways plane

While travelling to the South of France on a British Airways flight at cruising altitude of 10,000 m (approximately) in early May 2018 I filmed in the distance with my iPhone a large UFO that appeared out of a cloud and was heading upwards leaving a white trail in the cloud. I reckon it was at least 80m in size considering its distance and the fact that it was the same size as a commercial plane.
