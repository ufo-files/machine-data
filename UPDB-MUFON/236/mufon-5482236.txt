{rjl} Three Glowing Orbs

While driving north on Market st in Johnsonburg a witness saw one light in the sky that they assumed was a planet. The car was pulled over once the light was observed to be moving. Once out of the car two more lights were seen around the same elevation. Tried to take a video or the orbs but the orbs were blinking in and out on the camera screen, but were solid when viewing without the camera. The orbs were observed for a few minutes and then one orb stayed stationary and the other two moved orbs moved to the stationary orb and the moment they all came together they just blinked out
