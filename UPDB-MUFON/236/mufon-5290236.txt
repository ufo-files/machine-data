witnessed entity in my bedroom

When I was very small I woke in the middle of the night to see two figures standing next to my bed. One figure was taller than the other. they both were not very tall . They had large round heads with oval shaped eyes. I don't remember being afraid. After a short time I went back to sleep. I never told ANYONE of this until I told my wife of 40 years just recently. I was born in 1952 and was living in Central Indiana at the time. That i can figure, this happened between 1956 and 1957. It has always bothered me that maybe I was dreaming, but I remember it as if it were yesterday. I have tried to research when the image of the GRAYS first became public , thinking that i may have seen the image and dreamed about it. If it was an encounter with an entity, I always asked myself, why me?
