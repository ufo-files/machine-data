I know this isn't a typical UFO report, because I didn't see anything, nor did my friend, Shane. But we sure got a lot of pictures. Here are the details of our event.
 The date was October 23, 2015. Our location was in south/east Hemet, California. I was driving my truck, heading west with Shane, my passenger. The sun was going down. 
 I had such a very strong urge to get pictures of the sunset. I mentioned it to Shane several times while driving down the mountain from Idyllwild. 
 We were at a red light on E. Florida Ave, waiting to turn left onto Fairview. I informed Shane that I was soon going to pull over somewhere, so we could photograph the sunset. With that, Shane pulled out his cell phone and took some quick pictures. The light turned green, I turned left and drove about a mile before making a right onto Stetson Ave.
 Now we were in the orange groves, where I pulled over onto the dirt, facing west as the sun was going down.
 It was pretty uneventful, as we made small talk while snapping off random pictures of the sunset with our phones. We were mellow; just enjoying the moment. After about 10 minutes, I started the truck, and took Shane home. 
 Sorry, but that's it. Nothing happened; we certainly didn't see any UFO's or even think of them. In fact, we didn't see any kind of aircraft as we sat in that orange grove. 
 I didn't even take the time to look at my pictures till about a month later, when I exported them to my computer. 
 I have studied these pictures almost every day for the last five months, and I have many opinions as well as questions. I would like you to come to your own conclusions; and I would appreciate any opinions or insight.
