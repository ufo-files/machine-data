{rjl} Seen through trees

Saw object through backyard trees. Appeared every night. Not at the same
time. Stayed in the sky for approximately 3 hours. Then just disappeared.
Was a stationary object not a plane or a star. On the 23rd of February
appeared a little lower than usual, just under the branches. On the 24th
of February did not appear at all. Will continue to observe and keep a record
of the time we see it appear/disappear and of course the day. The days of 
appearances was about five days in a row. All three of us saw the same
object and the same positioning. When we go to look for it we ask if our
friend is out there.
