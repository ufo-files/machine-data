Metallic sphere shape object

17th January 2019 in the village of Atlow near Ashbourne Derbyshire. I was walking uphill to the house pushing a roam with a baby in it after a walk in the village when something made me look up over the roof top of the house. It as a very sunny day and the sky was blue. I spotted what I thought was a light aircraft coming towards me on seeing it had no wings then thought it was a helicopter as there were many small white lights flashing on and off. I stood watching in amazement as it got closer and said what on earth is that. When it reached me it spun around over my head then proceeding in a straight line over the road and over a roof top of the house below as I was on an incline going up to the house. Once it went over Inthen lose sight of it but ran inside and told the child's mother of the baby I had been pushing up the slope. She immediately looked on the Internet to see if anyone had reported seeing this object in the area before.
