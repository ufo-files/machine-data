{cak} very loud, jet-like object flew through sky at high rate of speed

I was walking outside of my house, observing the nice weather. I started to hear the sound of a jet, and I thought nothing of it. Soon, all in under a second, I saw an object that looked oval- like fly at a high rate of speed above my head. It was traveling at, I estimate, around 550-600 miles per hour. All of this (the loud, roaring sound included) happened in under 5 seconds. The force from how low the object was flying, and the high rate of speed in which it was flying at, knocked me on my back. This event has made me feel scared, and I now wonder if this will happen again.
