July 4th 2019 BF & I watching fireworks over the city when a bright orb appeared in the sky/hovered & changed shape before slowly disappearing

On July 4th 2019 my bf and I were watching fireworks from a public parking lot in a higher altitude in the city so we were able to see many displays at once. All of a sudden there was a bright orb/flame looking thing just hovering in the sky about 600 feet above the ground. We thought it was the moon at first and then quickly were able to see it wasn't the moon but was something very odd when it was changing shape but still hovering in the sky without making any sound. It started out as a ball of light and then sort of elongated into a bright fuzzy line... then changed again into a tear drop shape followed by a crescent shape, got smaller and eventually disappeared.
