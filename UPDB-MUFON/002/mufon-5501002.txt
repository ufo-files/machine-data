woke up because of annoying humming. Saw 30ft black disc hovering above a tree 50 yards away

1. I was sleeping, and was awoken.
2. I was awoken by a humming noise.
3. It couldn't have been any more obvious as to what it was.
4. It was completely still hovering and humming loudly.
5. The humming sound made me feel physically sick and was gave me a headache. I was frozen on the spot for what seemed like forever, but must have only been a few minutes. I could not move, and I was trying to scream but couldn't.
I then had thoughts in my head that I MUST wake one of my brothers, so I had someone else that saw it. I physically grabbed him by the scruff of the neck, out of a top bed bunk bed and dragged him and shoved him to the window. He saw it thank god.
6. Then, it shot up at a 45% angle and dissapeared in front of both of us.

I was to petrified to sleep for months. It could have been July of June, but I thought I remembered it being a "4th", because I was trying to memorize everything.
