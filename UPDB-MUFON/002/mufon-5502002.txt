{cak} like a twisted ribbon & dots in an arc. Captured on game camera.

I set up a game camera at the wash behind my house to capture puma movement in the area. At 4:29 AM on April 29th it captured a diagonal object that appears to be a twisted ribbon and at the same time an arc of dots in the background. The camera uses infra red so objects close appear white. A bush in the foreground appears to be shaking violently as it is blurred. The camera detects movement and records at 15 second intervals. Another pic was taken 1 hr later, but nothing unusual.
