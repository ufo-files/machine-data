No movement looked like star then moved a little stopped then went fast

It was around 9:30pm I was laying in bed and heard my mom yelling for me. I ignored her like usual until she said UFO! She said look out the window there is a bright light just sitting still it looks like a star until it moves. I went out to the backyard and saw what looked like a huge bright star. At the time there was no movement. Then all of a sudden it started to move my way just a little then stopped again.At this time it still looked like a bright star and there was no noise. Then right before it started moving again a humming/jet like sound started coming from it.Then it started moving and it went from a bright ball of light to a triangular shape and you could see a few blue and green lights on it. It moved really fast after that and by the time I made it to the front yard it was gone but you could see the back of a helicopter going in the same direction it went. How was I feeling when I saw it? Excited and nervous
