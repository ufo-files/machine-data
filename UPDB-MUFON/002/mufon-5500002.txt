{rjl} Stationary lights

My daughter and I were driving east on Mahogany Dr. toward CR 305 at approximately 8:45P on 8/20/14. At the stop sign, looking straight ahead (east,)we saw 2 or 3 very bright lights hovering above the fields. We both thought initially that it was an small airplane, not uncommon in the area. As we watched it for a very short distance (heading north on CR 305) my daughter said that it is not an airplane. (We then turned east on CR 302) Then we thought a cell tower. Neither of us remembered a tower in this area, and the lights were bright white not red and not flashing. She tried several times to get a photo on her phone but trees and driving obstructed her view. She kept trying not sure what she was getting. We traveled a couple of miles and turned south onto CR 65 to see if we could get a picture. We drove to a clear area and looked west but the lights were no longer there. We did see an airplane at this time. When we were returning home approximately 1 hour later we searched the horizon over the field and did not see anything but a few red tower lights very low on the horizon. She checked her pictures at home and we were surprised by what she was actually able to get.
