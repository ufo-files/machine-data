Followed by round bright light.

When I was around 16 I was driving home from a friends house. It was approximately 9 pm. I was driving down a country road with absolutely no streetlights. As I was heading home at one point I noticed a very bright round light in my rear view mirror. It was alarming because it came from no where and just appeared. I just kept driving and I would slow down and it would slow down. At one point I actually stopped my truck to see what it would do. It also stopped and just sat there in the sky. That’s when I became scared and drove to the overpass over interstate 75. I stopped again and turned around in my seat to look out the back window. As I did it had stopped again. I watch it then move out over the interstate and hover again. Approximately 5 seconds passed and it took off straight up. It moved so fast it disappeared in about 2-3 seconds. I was completely taken by what I witnessed. I didn’t sit long before I drove home as fast as I could. I never told anyone about that because I figured they would think I was drunk or high. Of which I was neither. I have never seen anything like that and can still see it so clearly now. I eventually told my dad and a few others over the years. I really believe my dad was my only true believer. Everyone else just thinks I’m nuts. But it effectively changed my life. I know we have been visited from long ago. He believe they visit all the time now and are studying us. I also believe the government will never tell us it’s real since they wouldn’t ever want to share in any technology recovered.
