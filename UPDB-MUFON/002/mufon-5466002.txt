{jpg} Simultaneously observed several stationary orbs across vast sky distance

Both my wife and I observed many (approx. 7-10) orb like brightly lit structures in the sky where we live. We noticed them walking home in the Lively Apartments at approximately 8:10 until 8:25 PM. My wife pointed out the biggest one which was due southeast from our location and quickly noticed several others north and west of that original one. We got binoculars and noticed the surfaces of at least 5 of them which had separate lights making up the entire mass. The biggest one furthest south was pure white as was the closest one to it just north. The one furthest north which was the 2nd largest was of the same light configuration, however it appeared more triangular shaped and had light of red, green, yellow and white which made up its mass. At approximately 8:45 I returned outside to see if they were still there and noticed the biggest white one was gone but the multicolored triangle was still in the same location and a smaller one at the time which was next to the large white one was larger and glowing brighter than before and was a redish/orange color. I didn't have the binoculars this time so I couldn't see if the "golfball" like surface of lights was present on its surface. It is now 8:58 at the writing of this report. I am in the process now of downloading the pictures taken from my phone.
