{dr}Bright light in sky caught my sisters attention

My sister witnessed a UFO which was also seen by another witness at the same time. Both the other witness and my sister were on the multi use path behind my mothers house in Mill Valley. I have not interviewed my sister as to all the details but do believe she is a credible witness. She took a number of low resolution pictures on her phone camera, one of which is a classic boomerang shape. She knows about airplanes and helicopters, because my dad was in the military and both of us are avid sky watchers.
