{rjl} Bright light low in the northern sky

I finished my run on Sonoma Ranch Boulevard in Las Cruces, between Lohman and Dripping Springs Road. It's very dark out there in the desert. I saw a bright light low in the northern sky, below the Big Dipper and thought it must be a planet or star, so I would look it up later. I was standing next to my car and I looked down into it because I was syncing my sports watch with the app on my phone. When I looked back up to the north, the light was gone and that freaked me out a little.
