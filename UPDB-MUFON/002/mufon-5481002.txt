{nd} I observed the orb heading from East to the West flying over the cron field.

It was 7 PM. I had just left my sister`s house near Dresden, Mo. As i rounded a curve, I noticed a man pulled over working on his car. I stopped and ask him if he needed any help. He was working up under his car. He said he had thing under control. As we talked, I noticed a small red-orange orb flying over the corn field behind me on the North. I watched it heading West and wondered what it could be. I was going to tell the man what I was seeing but the orb was already gone. I never said anything to the man. I soon left the scene and never mentioned it to anyone for a long time.
