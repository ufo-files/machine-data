{cak} came behind a shooting star

This past July, I was on a boy scout trip at Shaver Lake, CA. About halfway through the trip on a Wednesday I believe, at approximately 12:00 midnight, I hopped into a Chevy truck bed, and decided to watch the stars. After about 15 minutes, my mind was completely at ease, but was re energized by what looked like a shooting star. Almost immediately behind this shooting star, was something I find extremely difficult to put into words, so please bear with me. This is the best description I can put forth: Immediately following the shooting star, there was a dark object that seemed to rotate 180 degrees, but as it was rotating, the side that was coming into visibility, was an bright white/orange orb that suddenly shot into hyper space like a star wars ship. This object made absolutely no noise. The first thought that came into my head was, "There was somebody/something operating that". Began to feel a little sick due to the fact of the possibility that I might have encountered a living being originating from somewhere else.
