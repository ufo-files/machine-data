{jd} Viewed hovering orange orbs near expressway

I was driving on I-69W right before exit 92 near Lake Lansing, MI. It was between 10:05 and 10:10pm. I noticed two glowing orange orbs in the sky. They weren't like any lights on planes or helicopters and they didn't appear to move. They were too close together to be cell towers. Additionally, the light omitted was about 3-5 times the size of any other lights in the sky. The two hovering objects did not move from what I could tell. When I first saw them they looked almost diagonal to each other, one slightly higher than the other, but as I continued to get next to them (looking south) they appeared to be in line with each other, not moving. Nothing that I could rationalize, as there was nothing attached to the ground. The road curved and I had to continue driving. I couldn't see them in my rear view mirror.
