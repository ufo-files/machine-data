I am 45 years old now but this happened when I was 12 years old. we use to live on a farm one mile out side of Chrisney Indiana. My father woke me up and ask me to get out my telescope he wanted me to set it up and show me something.we pointed the telescope out the west window and to my amazement we saw four cylinders in a row. each was higher in the air next to one another. they also had lights that went left to right in the center of the craft. the lights flashed up in the air but when one went down behind the trees it would turn its lights off and land the craft. My father told me that they were back at the stripper pit where their was water. all four was higher in succession from left to right. later the ship on the right came up out of the trees it would turn its lights back on. then the next ship on the left center went down and turn its light out. This went on for about an hour. We observed this in my telescope and got a good look at the crafts. there was still some light in the horizon so we could make out the shapes of the craft. they were cigar shape or saucer shape. later the craft that was high in the air just rose up and headed west at the same time until it was out of sight. later all four left in like manner. we never went to where they landed so I don't know it they left traces or not.
