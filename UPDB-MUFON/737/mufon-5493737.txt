{rjl} Dark & dense cylinder object appeared only in cell phone picture of a tall tree, Michigan City, IN.

I positioned my cell phone camera for about 5 secs to get the best frame of the top of the tree. Neither of us saw any planes, birds, nor sound in the surrounding sky. After I took the picture I quickly checked to see how it came out and noticed a rather prominent and bizarre object, I said to my cousin, "What is this??" We both we're a bit stunned, in shock , as we did not see any planes and had are eyes on the sky for a good 3- 4mins. My cousin pointed out that there was a strange "glowing aura" surrounding the object.
