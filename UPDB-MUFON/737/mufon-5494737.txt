{cak} the two of us observed two objects triangular in shape with no lights or sound traveling north at a fairly low speed less than 2000ft in sky. they were dark grey and one was following the other

My brother in law were standing in my driveway at about 10:20pm 4-15-15 talking when I leaned up against my car and looked up at the stars. I noticed what appeared to be maybe a satellite in space and told him to look. We watched it until it was no longer visible. At that point my brother in law said what is that and pointed. It was an object less than 2,000 ft. Triangular in shape with no lights greyish in color and completely silent. It was traveling in a straight line south to north. It was followed by another one exactly the same approximately 3-4 seconds later. It was the strangest thing I have ever seen
