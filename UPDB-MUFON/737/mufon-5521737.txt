It glowed orange and was round to teardrop shaped

We were sitting in the kitchen, just after midnite, my sister, her husband and my husband. My sister glanced out the window and saw something in the sky. She ran out the door telling us to come out. We went out onto the back patio and saw this glowing orange orb coming from the east pretty quickly. We thought maybe it was a New Years balloon, but it was moving too quickly. I ran back into the house and grabbed my camcorder, but by the time I got back out it had moved even further north. I still tried to film and zoom in. In the video, the light to the lower right is a cell phone tower. I tried to film the object as best as I could. It was such a brillant orange color! We watched til it faded from our view to the north.
