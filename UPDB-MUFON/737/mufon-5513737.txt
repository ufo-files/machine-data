(GS) I was in my yard looking south, chrystal clear night. Bright light appears to the south then three more to the south west.

I was standing in my front yard looking up at the stars on a chrystal clear quiet night. A bright light appeared from out of nowhere, like someone turned on a switch. Their were no flashing lights or sound, then it just disapeared. two seconds later three more of the same lights appeared in a diagonal line to the southwest exactly the same as the first light. they stayed lit for a couple of seconds then disapeared as well. Their was no noise and no flashing lights at all. Very strange.
