What i thought was a star began to move doward, then to its left

I was solo camping at 2 harbors on Catalina island. Looking toward the mainland I saw what appeared to be the brightest star in the sky. I Star gazed for about a hour while it was stationary. I noticed it began to move downward and then across the sky to the East. I started recording video when it first began moving. A palm tree obstructed my view for a moment, so i readjusted my camera twice to keep up, but then it was just gone. I walked around the palm tree to try and see it, but it was gone. I did see an airplane flying east , but it was lower, further down and was obviously an airplane. I don't think it was the ssme as what i just witnessed. 
Now, back up to when i was stargazing, before the star began moving. The star I've been referring to is straight ahead of me. But, to my right, i took a couple of pictures if the moon within 10 seconds or so of each other. The first shows what looks like a star between the moon and palm tree. It's gone in the second picture, but another (perhaps the same star like light had moved?) up higher in a different location. 
There was minimal clouds that night if any. I'm not exactly sure what i saw, but wanted to get the video and photos evaluated.
