Noticed Object after taking picture.

At 11:48 pm we stopped outside Roswell NM to take a picture of the UFO sign welcoming visitors to town. After leaving a couple of minutes later we noticed something above the sign. At first we thought it was just a reflection but we took 2 photos and the angle of the object is different and the colors don’t match. We returned to the exact spot approximately 6 minutes later to take more pictures to confirm we weren’t seeing a reflection and the new photos didn’t have the object above it. Also, if you enlarge the photos it looks like some sort of window with a being(s) inside. We did not see the object with our own eyes, the camera caught it. That’s why we returned to take additional photos. It’s very strange that we couldn’t see it. The pictures are very disturbing when expanded.
