Pulsating light above ocean being filmed by Surfline Ventura Overhead Cam one could see the ocean moving below this pulsating light.

On February 19 2022 at 05:21 on Surfline Ventura Point Overview Cam in the Rewind section I found this video of strange pulsating and if you watch this slow moving light would almost completely blink and out of view plus change direction in less than seconds. This craft had changing lights that would make it blend into the early morning twilight then a white like corona go around it until it moved out of the stationary Cam frame. All the while you can see a faint waves breaking below the constantly changing lights. I tried to contact Surfline but they said they would refer to their rewind tech support. The rewind time segments are good for five days so I recorded everything I could. Still have not heard from them regarding these teeing time segments and I wonder is this an advertisement or screen saver? All I know the way the pulsating blinked. off and on with you being able to see this craft become very small only to stretch back on it’s previous path. How did they do it in split seconds? Amazing!
