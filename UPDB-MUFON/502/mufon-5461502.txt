Lights in sky

Looked like several objects with a fiery trail coming down at an angle. I assumed it to be meteor activity at first. Suddenly there was a change and the objects began to hover and change direction slightly. At this point there was no fiery trail only white glowing lights moving independently. 2-3 began to drift away from my visibility and one seems to shoot something out that appeared like a reddish light but much smaller than the other objects. This small piece was dropping in an arch and was quickly lost in sight. I began to video record and captured 4 of the objects clearly. At some point in the video the lights simultaneously stop and a strange larger orb shape is seen on camera however with the eye the white lights were seen. I began to adjust the camera to see them again but only the orbs remained until they all faded out of site. A still photo was also taken immediately before filming
