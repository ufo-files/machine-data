Light traveling slowly in a straight line brightened and then disappeared.

I was outside smoking and looking at the stars as I often do at night. When my fiance saw an object no bigger than a speck traveling in a straight line overhead that he pointed out to me. There was no sound associated with the object that we could hear. It was hard to see at first but as it kept traveling it brighten quite a bit and then dimmed down again and disappeared.
