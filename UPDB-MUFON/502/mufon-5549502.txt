3 witness 30 ft.metalic ,mist covered egg shaped disk with red light land.

I was watching tv when Andre,my stepson,yelled;"look outside in the yard! shut up,I hollered,and go to sleep. "Its a U.F.O." he screams. my wife and I go running outside and at about 200 yds out and just above the trees was a 30 ft metalic object. On the bottom was a red dome that emitted light in sinc with a low frequency,moduation. Like a weak station at the lower end of the f.m. dial,but loud,at 5 sec. apart. We watched it go down slowly behind Davis pond. There was little doubt what we saw. I called the Montville police and told them it was a small aircraft that crashed. An officer came in 10 min. we drove down Turner rd. and heard the humming from behind the sandpit. We walked threw the pit and around the swamp only to see at 100 ft,the object spewing out sparks,like carbon arc welding. we decided to leave , frightened. The officer did not report the indicent. For all I know, its still there! I've never gone back!
