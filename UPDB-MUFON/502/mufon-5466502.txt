{cak} Saw object streak across sky, stop and hover appx 25 minutes, 2nd time I saw this event.

I was going fishing Friday morning and left at 5:40 AM. I saw what at first I thought was a falling star. It then stopped and hovered in the sky directly what appeared above the hospital only further up. I watched and was able to see it pulsating lights, a white, red, and a greenish blue color. I watched it as I walked toward downtown. It seemed to hover upward for a bit and I thought it might be an optical illusion so I stopped and rested my gaze with it just above a telephone pole. The distance did increase between the top of the pole and the object. At appx 6:05 AM the lights and object seemed to just go out, like turning off a light switch. Sunrise was a few minutes away and it seemed to disappear.
