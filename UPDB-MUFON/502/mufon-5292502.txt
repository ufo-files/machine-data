Red hovering lights

Came to close my bedroom curtains on the second floor. I saw a 2 red lights in the sky, thought it was a plane. But it didn't move at all. Another minute goes by and It move only slightly and then stopped again. A minutes later, it started moving very slowly over the house. The lights went to a red orange color. Shape was triangle, a buzzing noise. I am scared, beyond scared. I've never seen a ufo before. We were just outside with the telescope earlier too.
