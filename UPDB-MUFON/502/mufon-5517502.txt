{ck} thimble shaped silouette with flame discharge similar to after burner on jet. discharge went down from bottom of object.

My family and I were waiting to watch the fireworks show at the Speedway when we first noticed the object to the NW of us. It travelled steadily south and made no noticeable noise. There were no visible marker lights and it appeared to be a thimble shaped object emitting an afterburner-like flame straight down from the bottom. It travelled south and slowly disappeared. My young daught was the first to mention that it was a UFO. We were all awestruck and could not explain what it was. I grew up near an airbase and have seen all types of military aircraft. Nothing like this.
