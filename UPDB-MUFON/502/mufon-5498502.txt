{dr}Looked up at sunny sky, saw chrome ufo then dissappeared.

On October 17th, 2014 at aproximately 3:24pm. In Ridgewood, NY I was Driving SW on Myrtle ave on a clear sunny day I happen to look up and thought I saw a commercial plane traveling west. However upon further visual examination, I noticed that the "commercial aircraft" had no tail nose or wings. It was chrome and oval in shape. Gave off somewhat of a glare. I only saw this anomaly under a minute, before as it was reaching a cloud it vanished. I looked up as I drove before realizing it wasn't safe, I pulled over waiting to see the "plane" come out the other end of the cloud, but never did. I looked up and couldn't see any other clouds, planes birds, anything. It was clear and sunny about 721.
