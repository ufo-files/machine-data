{tjd} Rectangle with one main part and then tic tac shaped lights to the right

We stopped at a campsite to look at the stars. The sky was full of stars. The stars in the space disappeared and then we saw the distinct rectangle shape with two box like rectangles on one side and a bunch of smaller tic tac shaped lights in a row to the right. About 4 football fields in length. Took a video but it was black. After a minute the lights disappeared and there was a blank black spot again, and no stars for another minute, as if the thing turned off it’s lights. Then suddenly, the normal sky and stars were back. There were others who were camping that saw too, we heard them exclaiming about what we all saw.
