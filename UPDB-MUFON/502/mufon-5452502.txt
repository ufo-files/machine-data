On the top of Mt Provost, about 2km from me I can see one very large bright yellow light. I watched it, with others, for a couple minutes. It was pulsing randomly and sometimes disappeared entirely. After a couple minutes of it hovering it got very bright, the one yellow light turned into three very bright white lights in a triangle formation. It lifted slightly then looked like it moved in my opposite direction slightly before it disappeared.
