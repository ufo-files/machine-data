{rjl} White beam of light from invisible spaceship.

A white beam of light from an invisible spaceship was shined down on me from the sky outside on the shore of lake Minnetonka on a summer evening at my home. I also witnessed a cube shaped cloud over my house one night, a disc shaped cloud and square shaped clouds over me during the day, and triangular shaped clouds over the lake. And there appeared to be multiple types of translucent extraterrestrials inside of my house as if the extraterrestrials were using invisibility technology.
