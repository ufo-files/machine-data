i see them repeatedly (between 1 and 3) hovering over or near the local powerline electrical station

I received an email from someone in town stating there was a weird light in the sky which would hover and then dart from side to side. I jumped in my vehicle and went to the area that the email described. When i arrived there i was shocked to see a diamond shaped bright light which would change colours (from blue to green, white and red) almost like a consistent fusion of all these colours...no flashing. It was hovering over powerlines at the local town electrical station. Ever since then ive been back a few times and have seen them. What troubles me is that when i try to get close enough to take a picture they dart out with magnificent speed and disappear. Its borderline disturbing but amazing nonetheless.
