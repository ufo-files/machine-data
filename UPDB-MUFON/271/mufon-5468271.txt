{td} Very bright orange orb that would randomly dim down and turn blue.

I got up to go to the bathroom and passed by the window when something caught my eye, at first I thought it was a very bright star, but when I had a closer look I noticed it was considerably brighter than a star and looked like a fireball. The orb was a kind of bright orange, a color I wouldn't associate with a star. After a minute or so it dimmed down and turned blueish white, at this point if someone seen it they wouldn't be able to tell the difference between it and a star, but it flared back up and turned back to bright orange again after maybe twenty seconds. It did this approximately 5 more times at random(it was not doing it constantly or at regular intervals, it was bright orange the majority of the time) which led me to conclude I was not looking at a star or a planet. Eventually it dimmed out completely and disappeared.
