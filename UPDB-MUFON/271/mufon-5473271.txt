{dr}Aerial light display similar to a lazer light show

I would place this sighting at approximately 1999-2000, based on the location I was living at the time. I arrived home to my apartment complex at about 9 pm. As I exited my car, I heard a loud airplane droning noise. I was immediately curious, because that summer there were incidents where you would hear loud airplane noises during the day that would go on for a long time, but you would look in the sky and see nothing. On this occasion it was night time. I looked up in the sky and a large military seeming plane flew directly overhead flying due west. I watched the plane fly to the west, then turn south, then turn and head back east in my direction. The more interesting thing was now there was a UFO flying in front of it. I would say the UFO consisted of about 5 or 6 red lights which had a laser pen like quality. These lights were connected by lines of the same quality. But the odd thing was that these lights constantly moved in and out, changing and morphing their shape. It was like watching a laser light show flying through the air. This randomly changing light configuration flew by on my left not farther than a city block in distance from me. When it got to about parallel with my location, it changed direction and flew off to the northeast. I've search the internet and haven't found a report of a UFO of this type. I'd love to know if anyone has seem something similar.
