{dr}A silverish object in the sky

I WAS DRIVING WEST ON MELROSE AVE IN L.A. I STOPPED AT A SET OF LIGHTS. I LOOKED UP TO SEE A GLIMMERING OBJECT IN THE SKY THROUGH MY FRONT WINDOW AND NOTICED IT WAS FLYING VERY CLOSE TO AN AEROPLANE. I PICKED UP MY NIKON D700 WITH A 70-200MM LENS AND TOOK 2 PICS. THE LIGHTS CHANGED GREEN. I MADE A RIGHT TURN TO SEE IF I COULD GET A BETTER ANGLE THROUGH THE GAPS IN THE BUILDING BUT THE OBJECT HAD DISSAPEARED.
