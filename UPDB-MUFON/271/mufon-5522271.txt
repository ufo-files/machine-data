Three balls of fire fall from the sky and go dim, then reappear in linear form.

I was outside on the deck of my parents house smoking a cigarette with my brothers girlfriend. It was about 6:55pm. I noticed three lights in the sky that looked like red flares falling from the sky -- extremely bright with a tail. The lights fell in an almost free fall and moving away from each other for about 10 seconds until going dim at about 20 degrees above the horizon.

Seconds later three red burning lights reappeared in nearly the same area in the sky, and hovered in place while slowly pulsating on and off. These lights hovered there for about two minutes and were witnessed by myself, my mom and brother, and my brothers girlfriend.

After two minutes, the lights began to slowly move in towards each other to form a single point of light. This light then descended closer to the ground, approximately 300 ft. off the ground (about 10 degrees from the horizon). The light then simply faded out into the dark sky.
