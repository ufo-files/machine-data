{nd} Four large odd-colored steady light sources moving in formation

Observed four large, reddish/pinkish/orange lights in loose formation on a heading from NE to SW, slightly NE of my home in Frenchtown Twp. near Monroe, MI. The sky was not quite completely dark and I could see stars in the area between the lights. The lights were steady, no strobes, no other colors. The lights all moved in the same direction at the same speed- probably three times as fast as a commercial airliner would appear to be moving when very high. The light source was indeterminable, but appeared much larger than any known aircraft based on their apparent height. No sound was heard. They all disappeared/ went out at the same time when they were nearly overhead.
