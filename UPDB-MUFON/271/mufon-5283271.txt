Partially below cloud ceiling with huge rear lights

I was on my way too make my first student pick up. I was on U.S. 321 traveling north towards Maryville from Walland Tn. The morning was heavy overcast with slight drizzle. To the right of my bus i first saw a glow in the clouds. Very bright area of the cloud, then part of the craft came below the cloud. Very bright white lights on what i assumed was the rear of the craft because it was moving north west at an altitude of more than 500’ but less than 1000. There were four very large lights on the rear of this craft but i could tell that a very small portion of the craft was below the cloud because there was a tremendous amount of glow up in the clouds from the rest of the craft. These lights were inset in the shape of the rear, which was straight with each and turned down at about 22 degrees. The length of the rear was over 300’ and the diameter of each light looked to be at least 50’ maybe bigger. It kept a steady slow flight to the north west until i lost sight of it when i turned left off of 321 onto Grandview.
