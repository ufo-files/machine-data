{nd} Fireworks going on.UFO came hovered,and left,several times.At,end left quick.

On the 4th of July of 2014,I was at the Wilson county fairgrounds watching a fireworks show. I happened to look up and see a red object moving through the sky,with a blinking green light on it.Then it stopped and hovered for about 5 mins. before moving again.It left the area and came back about 10 mins. later,and it repeated the same sequence twice.The last time it came back,it stayed for about 10-15 mins. until the fireworks show ended.When everybody began to leave it left, but we saw it going too fast to be a helicopter.
