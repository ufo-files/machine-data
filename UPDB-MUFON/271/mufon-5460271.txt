{cak} Silver balloon type loo,kinda pointed top an bottom

It was just starting a thunderstorm but hadn’t started raining yet but very high wind gusts, i was standing in my driveway watching these crazy looking clouds rolling in from the Southwest I believe and noticed above tree top where the dark clouds were coming in that something was floating in the sky an appeared to be what I thought was a released balloon, it was silver an kind of reflective an watched it travel from my far right through the air perfectly horizontal with no changes in height or direction which I thought was so strange with the crazy wind an clouds way above it and behind it moving so fast but it stayed a consistent speed an height, I hollered to my wife who was about 20 yards away to look up at the sky above the house, she had difficulty hearing me with the wind an she only saw the rolling dark clouds to the right of where the objects flight path was, I stood there shocked an trying to justify everything it could possibly be an it’s been bothering me since that day, unfortunately she was looking way too high in the sky to see it travel over the thirty seconds or so it was within visible sight. I’ve never seen anything like this in the sky before an not since either, it floated at a consistent speed an height during a wicked windy beginning of a thunderstorm an didn’t once move up or down or twist or anything, I just can’t make sense of what I actually saw and like I said it has bothered me ever since because it’s just something I can’t explain or come up with a reasonable justification for what I saw. I was kind of hoping maybe someone else saw something similar so later on that same evening I checked local news an googled any recent strange sightings in my area to hopefully shed some light on this but found nothing an frankly was a little embarrassed to talk or ask about it so did no real further investigation for awhile, I was watching something on television an saw a commercial for some show and a woman had a book that had some ufo drawings in them from whatever source and one of the drawings looked exactly like the object I saw and it’s bothered me ever since which is why today I decided to do some research into weather balloons an how they travel and different shapes etc to try an figure out what it was an I’m more confident now than ever that it couldn’t have been a balloon that day and prompted me to finally make some sort of report with the hopes of possibly getting some answers or at least making a record of the sighting.
