Whitish object very high moving fast

Outside on sidewalk waiting for ride to work. Noticed a white faintly glowing object moving almost due north, way up in the sky immediately thought airplane, but it was moving too fast for a jet at that altitude. The object moved behind a cloud after watching for about 10 sec. About 3 seconds later the object reversed course and doubled back going due south, following same path. I watched it for about 15 sec. More and it passed behind clouds. I did not see it again.
