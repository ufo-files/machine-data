{dr}Cloud ship shines Heavenly Light on me

It was a very cloudy day today. I was on my back patio smoking, and I glanced at the sky and imagined what Nibiru will look like when it passes and how terrible that day will be for Earth. I glanced back up to the sky and prayed to God that me and my family be raptured before this happens, and i lowered my head. Immediately, I was overcome with a strong urge to cry tears of joy that was very hard to hold back. Then, I was told to look back up to where I had just prayed to God, and what i thought was the sun was shining directly down on me. I tried to look directly at it, but the light was full of love and overwhelmed my emotions and eyesight. I had to look back down and i felt the warm embrace of the light leave me. I immediately looked back up but the light was gone. There was a low roaring sound like that of a carpet cleaner that was fading away, and I noticed it was coming from a cloud that was surrounding the light i had just seen. The cloud, with the light inside flew away. The sun was not out. I watched it fly away and disappear in the clouds. I was overcome with hysterical laughter of joy and then sobbing tears of happiness. As it all happened, I had no time to think about what it was, I didn't even realize what really happened until it flew away. But after the fact I have no doubt that it was definately Heavenly and Angelic if not God Himself. Praise Him!!!!
