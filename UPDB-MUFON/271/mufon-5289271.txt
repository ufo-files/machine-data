6 lights

was driving home coming up to the throgs neck bridge was about a 3/4 of a mile from the bridge when I noticed 6 bright lights 3 in a straight line on the east side of the bridge and 3 in a triangle shape on the westside of the bridge, as I pulled off to the side of the road they flew past me. I lost sight of the lights then I saw them coming back only 4 of them flying south west I filmed them and lost sight of them being some trees.
