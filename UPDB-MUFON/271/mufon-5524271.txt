BURNING MAN RED UFO DROPPING ORBS

At aprox 8:45pm as I biked over to the temple burn I looked back towards the campgrounds and saw a red light moving slowly from 12'oclock to 3 o'clock, I noticed it was dropping red orbs, I thought it might be a helicopter but it was shining bright red and there were definately no pulsating flight lights. I watched as it slowly flew over the entire campground and eventually it took position right above the temple where every one was gathering, I pointed up to it with a laser and everyone around me saw the object pulse back. A lot of people saw this and a lot took video as well. It droped an orb right behind the temple and slowly moved away, then it hovered still for at least 5-8 minutes until eventually it just disintegrated or powered out at super fast speed.
