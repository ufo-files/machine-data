Two White Orbs Moving Fast. Located two additional objects in review of video

I just so happened to look up from my balcony patio and saw two white orbs flying in a straight line over the tops of the apartment complex. From what it looked like they were at a distance that was over the main road outside the complex. They flew straight and then jetted off at enormous speeds away from their original location and I lost sight of them as they went towards the downtown area of Asheville. They were flying very close to each other but were not of the same object. I have a quick video of them that I zoomed in so you can see them. They were way clearer to my eyes then in the video.
