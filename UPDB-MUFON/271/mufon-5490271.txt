{cak} 2am pacific time, lights, Vshape, Triangle, Aircraft

I woke up at 2 am, upset because I thought I had missed the perseid meteor shower. I went outside to smoke, my husband came out to join me after washing our babys bottle. We were really enjoying the view because we dont live around a lot of light pollution. All of a sudden in the middle of the sky I see these lights in a v shape or boomerang form almost like an aircraft.And I kept yelling at my husband, do you see it? he said yes I see it. I told him keep looking at it don't loose it! It was headed south east and became less visible when it went into the horizon. It flew or hovered right over us and it was extremely quiet. We became very uneasy and are absolutely shocked! We both have never witnessed anything of this kind.
