{cak} 2 bright lights

driving to work heading east. 5:40 am. I notice 2 lights in the sky. one brighter or bigger than the other. lights appeared to be hovering. I watched them hover for about 10 seconds. Got my phone out and took a pic. about 2 seconds later, the lights simultaneously moved west at a HIGH rate of speed. in less than a second, they were gone. Again, went from hovering to gone in less than a second. Got out of my car and looked behind me. They were nowhere to be found. I would chalk this up to stars or maybe even a planet reflecting except for the high rate of speed in which they took off. No way in the world were they airplanes or helicopters due to the speed in which they took off.
