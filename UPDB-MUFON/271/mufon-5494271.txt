{nd} Force hit car, blur lights appeared all inside/around the car, disappeared

I was driving home at around 9pm going north. The road I was driving on has sand dunes on either side, hardly any street lights, and a housing complex towards the of road. Driving, I was the only one on the road. I noticed some flashing lights in the distance, but thought at the time that it was just lightening or something. I continue driving north. While I was driving, I felt a force move my mini van into the other lane, going against traffic. I slam on the breaks because I was unsure whether I had hit something or had a tire blow out, etc. However, once I slam on the breaks, this blue, almost sparkly, light appears in my car and all around it. The light seems to have movement and is so vibrant that it looked like nothing I had seen here on earth. I'm freaking out and then all of a sudden the light is gone. Completely gone, in less than a second. So I hit the gas and try to run away from whatever the heck that was. At the end of the road, there is a stoplight in which you have to turn left or right at. The stoplight was red so I, needing to turn left, was stopped in the left lane. As I am stopped, my car is thrust into the other lane and the blue sparkly light appears again all in the car and around it. Too afraid, I drove away without waiting for the light to disappear again. But once I hit the gas, the light disappeared as quickly as it came. I then drove home and told my parents what I had seen. I never was able to see the source of the light, but I felt as if I was in some sort of field or beam. I felt like I was being watched as the light seemed very invasive. The light I had seen had movement, almost as if it was flowing. I wish I had more information but that is all I witnessed.
