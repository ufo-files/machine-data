The event happened not to me, but to a friend of mine. I was inside my apartment, when I heard my friend scream UFO UFO so I went outside to see what was going on. When I got outside, she was telling some other people about what she had seen. After she saw the whitish light pulsating, and not been able to move while she was observing the event, she saw the UFO just disappeared! 

After the UFO event, I made her write everything down, and made her do drawings of the UFO. She told me that she was feeling scared because she had never witness a UFO... After talking with her for a while, she still could not believed that she saw a strange object.
