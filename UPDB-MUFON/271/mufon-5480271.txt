It was new years going from 2010 to 2011 and I made a discussion to go home because I had work the next day. I only lived half a mile down the street so I drank my glass of wine and walked home. The time was around 2 o'clock when I made it home and I figured I have a cigarette before heading in. Growing up I always looked up into the night sky and tonight it was cold, very quiet and clear. As I looked up I noticed a odd orb of light and thought to myself maybe its mars or something but realized it changed many colors and was far though close. Then I noticed another one across the way and then the 3rd one each at a equal distance from one another. I thought to myself maybe its a ufo and laughed at the thought and considered maybe its little home made hot air balloons. Then I noticed the complete object was at a tilt and its center was dark negative space with nothing shining through it. Stars all around except in this dark triangle shape object which made no sound. This thing is extremely big and it was heading north west which happens to be the direction of the nuclear power plant. I looked at it for a few minutes external nervous and scared to even go inside my house to sleep. I was 24 at the time and really did some rational thinking as it happened. I'm 100% sure I saw something I shouldn't have seen and wish I never did.
