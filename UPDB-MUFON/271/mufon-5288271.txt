After originally being very confused at what it could be. I came to the conclusion it was starlink. It does look like a series of lights. However the more I inspect the video the stranger that idea becomes. 

Throughout the video you'll notice it's actually slowly spinning in the sky and changing orientation. It appeared to be moving closer to me, appearing larger and brighter in the sky. Heading south then veering to the east before disappearing. There are a few instances of a shadow starting at one end of the "beam" and going across the pattern to the other side. Specifically at the 38 second mark I noticed it very clearly. 

I'm an amateur astronomer and look at the sky alot, but never saw anything like this... it very well could be starlink with some weird atmospheric visuals. But Holy crap in person it appeared to get closer to me and then changed direction. Most starlink videos I see look like and average everyday train of satellites moving in a straight line... this looked nothing how I imagine starlink.. but light can do some weird things traveling through thousands of ft of atmosphere?

It did clearly appear larger and more overhead towards the end of the video. If you use the house as a reference. At the start I zoom in on it. By the end of the video I'm just about all the way zoomed out again.
