two objects appearing as close sattelites one pulsing other blinking. large lightening flash between them.

Just returned home with my partner and parked the van for the night. I looked into the south south easterly sky and noticed a very bright flash. I continued to watch and saw another sattelite like light quite close to the first. I observed this activity, one blinking very brightly every few seconds and the second one glowing and dimming at regular intervals. I mentioned to my partner that there were two sattelites close together and she watched and saw this. As we live in a "dark sky" area in the Scottish Borders, I regularly observe sattelites moving against the night sky and have counted up to 11 sightings in a 30 minute period so, although intrigued with spotting two so close together, I assumed this was just another sattelite sighting. However the blinking sattelite continued to blink and the second sattelite continued to glow and dim. Explaining what happened next is a little difficult as I cannot put into words the distance between the two objects as I had nothing to reference against, but a flash of light leapt between the two objects. It resembled a bolt of lightning and was extremely bright. I think (but cannot be certain)that the bolt of lightning eminated from the blinking sattelite. It lasted for a split second and my partner did not observe this event. I saw nothing extra after this and the two "sattelite" objects eventually stopped blinking and glowing / dimming after a few more seconds.
