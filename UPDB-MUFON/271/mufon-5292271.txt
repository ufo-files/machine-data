Light moving around incomprehensibly, high but under high clouds

I was looking straight upwards at the northern lights. At what first appeared to be a shooting star crossing through, abruptly changed directions and then did so several more times, quickly accelerating beyond any of my known capabilities of spacecraft. It did this for 10-15 seconds before disappearing. Then, about 10 minutes later, it appeared again, doing a very similar motion pattern, but for only about 5 seconds, and zoomed off in a different direction.
