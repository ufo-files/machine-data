{dr}Sphere-like shape in The sky that was crest-like red.

In Valley,NE roasting marsh-mellows, I look at the Sky and see a giant dull sphere that was red. It made a Crescent. I was with my Mother and Brother too. Later it faded away. We were thinking it was The Moon, Venus, or Mars. but we knew it wasn't the moon cause the moon was somewhere else, and Venus and Mars can't be that close to us. Later At lest two Planes came, one went by where it faded away, and the other had a search-light close to the spot where it faded away.
