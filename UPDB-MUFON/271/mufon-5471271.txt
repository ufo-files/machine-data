{jpg} Observed 2 stationary red globes in sky above Boise in general direction of airport.

I came outside of my home because I had heard several loud explosions in the neighborhood. These might have been New Year's early fireworks but they sounded like gunfire to me. As I was looking around I noticed two red globes in the sky in the general direction of the airport. One of them appeared farther away than the other. Neither was moving but remained stationary. I thought they might have been helicopters but I could see no movement at all and they were glowing red all over. More like a subdued fireball. I wanted to get a better view so I ran into the house to get a pair of binoculars. Unfortunately, by the time I got the binoculars unpacked and focused the objects had disappeared. So, this whole sighting only lasted a minute or two. I was disappointed that I couldn't see them with my binoculars, but the truth is that I wouldn't have been able to hold the 10x binoculars steady enough to see more detail anyway.
