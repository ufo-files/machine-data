I was driving on Hwy 49 and saw a bright, glowing cylinder shaped object hovering.

I was driving on Hwy 49 from Nevada City going toward Grass Valley about 2:30pm when I saw a big, bright white, glowing cylinder object that appeared to be hovering above the trees at a slant. This "rod" type object was about two inches at arms length in the southeast direction. As I continued driving and keeping an eye on it, I lost sight of it from the trees. I tried to locate it and then came around on the road and saw a very low, flat, "squished" cloud-like object that was about 8 inches to a foot long at arms length. It was the weirdest thing since there wasnt a cloud in the sky and the weather was gorgeous. I only saw that cloud-like thing for a minute and then lost sight of that from the trees. I tried to take pictures but it was difficult while driving.
