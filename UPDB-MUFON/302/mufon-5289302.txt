Round, grey UFO flat on top and bottom, no windows

In August, 1979, 6 other people & I were out in my friend's back yard by their dock. A large UFO came from the left, following the river and moved across to the right. It was below the treeline, maybe 250-300 feet from us. It was round and its base was wider that the top, so the sides tapered in. It was flat on the bottom and the top. It moved slowly but steadily, and made no noise.
