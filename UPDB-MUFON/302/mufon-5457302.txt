{tjd} Unidentified Wavering Light

Around 7PM on November 1, 2019 I was outback with my wife looking for shooting stars. On average we had heard 5-12 per hour and I had already seen a few when I was outside with my dogs. I noticed a very bright star that appeared to be bouncing or going back and forth but I thought my eyes were playing tricks as they adjusted to the dark again. I pointed at it and said to my wife, “Does that star or satellite look like it’s waving back and forth?” She agreed, it then followed along a wavy path, straightened out and then suddenly disappeared. I kept watching the sky thinking it was lost behind a cloud but nothing appeared again much less any clouds as it was a pretty clear night. I had downloaded the Night Sky app and ran to my phone to see if it was a satellite that was in orbit but none were on a similar trajectory. Would love to know if anyone saw something similar or can explain/debunk. Otherwise I’m claiming UFO. We have quite a bit of air traffic near here and this was not like what we are used to seeing. We usually can hear it too, this was silent.
