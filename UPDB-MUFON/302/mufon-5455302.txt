{cak} Stationary pulsating object, bright white swirling

Dogs were barking weird, I asked husband to check. He came in immediately and said get out here. We observed a bright flashing/pulsating object in the sky to the southwest. It remained in place and we could see a faint pinkish center. I began to record with my phone and could then see a distinct large pulsating halo of misty white. The object was clearly spinning at a high rate of speed with a distinct center ring of salmon/red. It pulsed often but randomly, with no discernible rhythm, occasionally expanding its halo. I became frightened when the object suddenly shanks to almost nothing, then emitted a huge halo of light. On seeing that, I felt as though the thing was trying to make sure that I knew it was looking at me. That it communicated with me. This freaked me out and I went into the house after recording a 4 minute video. My husband kept checking to see if it was still there. It remained in the same exact spot until approximately 11 pm, after which it appeared to slowly move away to the the southwest until it was no longer visible the last time he checked at around 11:30 pm.
