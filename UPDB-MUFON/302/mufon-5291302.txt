My son and I were driving down a dirt road to the local trash dump and we saw a silver cigar shaped object in the sky. The sky’s were completely clear today. It did not appear to be moving. We stopped and got out the truck to view it better. Then after approximately 3 minutes it just vanished. We live about 40 miles away from Shaw Air Force base, I don’t know if they have anything that could do that though. We waited around for at least 5 more minutes to see if it would reappear but we never saw it again. It did not look like a blimp or an airplane at all.
