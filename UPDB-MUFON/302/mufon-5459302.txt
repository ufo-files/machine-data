{cak} Little tic-tac shapes straight overhead. About 30 or so objects, bright white and glowing somewhat. Grouped but different headings.

Approximately 30 tic-tac shaped bright white glowing objects directly overhead. They were in a group about a foot or so in diameter from my perspective. The individual objects looked about a quarter-inch long pointing in different directions. I don't remember if they were stationary or moving - it was over 18 years ago. Looked somewhat like what oblong bacteria in a petri dish looks like. I don't know how long they were there - I noticed them and watched for a few minutes then drove away (I only remember leaving the house, not where I was going.)
