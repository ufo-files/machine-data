{nd} Watching fire works in one direction by the lake and then behind us seen this object in the sky

It was on July 5th, 2014, outside while watching fireworks in Laurel Maryland. The fireworks were done by a lake in one direction. I happen to look up and behind me and seen an object with 7 circles 6 going around and one in the middle in the shape of a flower. It moved backwards and forward slowly then eventually the lights went out and you would see a purple or blue light resemble an airplane light. then maybe about 15 seconds later you would see all 7 of the lights. 

Later after turning back around seen, it again just staying in one place and then it moved away.
My first thought was that it was an UFO. I was just concerned as to what that was In the hair and why would it be moving back and forth and over top of our heads. It moved away I kept my eyes on it for a long time then it just disappeared in the air.
