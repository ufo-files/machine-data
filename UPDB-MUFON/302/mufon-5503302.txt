{rjl} bright light hovering

Around 8:10pm 4/20/14 I was headed home to Moore,Ok. from Blanchard,Ok.I was traveling North and noticed a bright light in the sky northeast of me. The light didn't beam off of the object, it was more florescent and stayed with the object as the light was the object. I went over every possible idea of what it could have been. It was larger than any aircraft light. I know as big as this light was, I was positive I would have seen the actual aircraft. I did not see anything else, except that light. I looked for beams going up and or down off of this light and nothing was there. I can still see it in my mind. The bright light went a little right and then a zig up left and then right again and then it was gone. I have never doubted nor have I believed there are UFO's and other such things. I do believe this however was completely odd and not meant to be where it was and I still can't believe I saw I saw.
