Three bright objects-baseball size

My grandson Adam was visiting and told me the following: 
He remembers it was a Saturday about two weeks ago at 11:30 pm. He and his fiancee were out side when he looked up and in the east-north-east were these three bright , bright objects. He said they really stood out against the other stars. he asked Nichole, his Fiancee if she had seen them and she said yes. she wondered what they were. after a few minutes -about 5, the objects suddenly whooshed across the sky to the south-south-east and formaed a triangle there for another 4 or 5 minutes, thenthey suddenly zipped back NNE. He sais the speed was tremendous. I aske dthe size-like a softball held at arms length. Color: Very bright, like those new car headlights which are an intense blue-white light. Distance 40,000-50,000 ft may be closer.
