SINCE 1998 WE HAVE BEEN VISITING THE ZONE OF AGUACLARA IN THE WESTERN STATE OF FALCON,VENEZUELA.WE WENT THERE MORE THAN 50 TIMES AND PRODUCED AN E-BOOK, PHOTOS,VIDEOS,ETC IN WHICH WE DESCRIBE THE MULTIPLE SIGHTINS AND STRANGE PHENOMENA THAT WE OBSERVED DURING THIS PERIOD.ONE OF THE MOST INTERESTING EVENTS OCURRED THE 6TH DAY OF JANUARY 2001.WE STAYED IN A HUT OVER A HILL WHERE WE PERCEIVED THE DESERTIC VALLEY BELOW, SURROUNDED BY MUONTAINS RICH IN COAL AND QUARTZ.AT 5.30 AM TRHEE WITNESS AND MYSELF SAW AN OBJECT WITH A BULLET FORM AT LOW ALTITUDE (100 METERS) COMING FROM THE WEST THAT LEFT TWO DARK TRAILS AND AT ABOUT ONE KILOMETER TO THE EAST EXPLODED IN A HUGE WHITE CLOUD SILENTLY.THERE WERE NO REMAINS OF THE OBJECT AND WE IMAGINE THAT IT TRAVELED TO ANOTHER DIMENSION. A SIMILAR EVENT OCURRED ON DECEMBER 16, 2000 IN CARACAS THE CAPITAL OF VENEZUELA AND WAS REPORTED ON FEBRUARY 18,2001 IN THE MAGAZINE "ZONA"  OF THE NEWSPAPER "EL UNIVERSAL".NOTE:THE EVENT OF AGUACLARA WAS VERY RAPID AND BECAUSE OF THAT WE DID NOT HAVE TIME TO PHOTOGRAPH IT.
