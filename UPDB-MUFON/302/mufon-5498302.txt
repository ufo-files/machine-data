{rjl} Large Glowing light

Noticed the object from window in the living room so got up and looked to see what it was and noticed it wasn't a plane or helicopter so we grabbed the Ipad and started filming the object . It was moving from the North towards the Southeast at about the same speed as a small airplane , but was silent and very bright . It was a very bright orangish yellow color that seem to flicker and pulsate . I began to think it might be a chinese lantern , but it was way too large . It was pretty incredible . The object just disappeared like someone just turn the lights off .We had great visibility nice calm winds and clear sky at dusk .
