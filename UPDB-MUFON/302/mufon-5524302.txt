Witnessed 2 orange orbs flying quicly and errartically over Ottawa, Canada

I was in my sons bedroom just admiring the nice eveining when I happened to nice the objects. First sighting ever......2 orange, glowing orbs, raced around in and out of the clouds at about 1500ft. They moved as fast as a passenger jet but changed direction very quickly. They disappeared for about 30 seconds and then reappeared for less than 1 minute and repeated the same behavior. I felt I was witnessing intelligent live as these orbs seemed to "play' with each other as they darted in and out of the clouds.
