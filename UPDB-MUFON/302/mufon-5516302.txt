{cak} I was walking down country road w/ 2 brothers came to a 3 way intersection a bright light flashed my brother screamed ufo they ran I watched it crash into a swamp.

I think I was 13/14 y.o. We were staying for a few days up at Uncle S. somewhere between Midland, Farwell, and Clare. My Aunt and Uncle and S. went to a bar. So me and my two brothers walked about 1/8 mile to a road that intersected with the one S house was on. My brothers were on right side of the road we were on, I was in middle of road. When my older brother said what is that I looked up there was a young tree there and I saw a bright light behind the tree (I believe there were power lines there also.) My older brother screamed its a ufo, and they bolted running back to S. house, leaving me watching this great burst of light try to turn left to avoid either the lines or the tree, but it crashed and went into a swamp on the right hand side of the road that I was on. 
I stood there terrified and I was confused I was looking all around, (thats when I saw the big airplane way up) then I heard guys screaming coming up the crossroad in pick up trucks(2) I got scared and bent over and tried to sneak away, but someone saw me, so I ran all the way back to S. One of the trucks followed me, but I was afraid of them I kept walking all the way to the back of his yard. We werent supposed to leave the yard, and I didn't want to get caught.
When we got back to Detroit I read an article (The Detroit News or Free Press) about the mysterious red lights in Farwell/Clare and people talking about a crash. So I told my aunt and uncle what happened, and they told me not to talk about it again. They never took me up north again! It is possible that its still there, because it was so far in the country.
