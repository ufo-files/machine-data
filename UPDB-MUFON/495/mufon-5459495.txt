Stationary object in the sky at same level as clouds.

Driving through Karoo with family. Speck in the distance that didnt move at all for the entire duration we could see that section of sky. We all looked at it and laughed at the "ufo" as we werent taking it seriously, but on later research I found that this is a common type of sighting. I am sorry now that I didnt think to film it.
