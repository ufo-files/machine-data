Unknown object floating in Singapore sky

On 10 July 2013 at about 6:53pm, an unknown object was spotted "floating" in Singapore sky. The object was at a rather high attitude. It was a relatively clear day, with some clouds. The object can be seen easily as it seemed to reflect sunlight on its surface.

Please see the attached video. There seemed to be a smaller object around the bigger one. The smaller object could not be seen with naked eyes. I found the object after reviewing the video file. Video was shot with full optical zoom at 70x. 

The object disappear among the cloud after "floating" around and among the cloud. I waited for 5 minutes, but the object did not re-appear. However, an unknown craft was seen around the same area of the sky where the object was earlier. The craft was at a high altitude. I was not able to capture on my video camera, as both of my batteries were flat at that time.

The craft can be seen easily as it leaves a clear contrail. However, I could not see the shape/color/structure of the craft (with my naked eyes) as it was a long distance away. The craft disappeared after about 10 seconds.

If you need any clarification, please contact my email address. Thank you.
