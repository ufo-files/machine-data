While out jogging at night I observed a strange star-like object hovering at about 500 ft. (over Brownsville/South Padre Island International Airport presumably). The object was static for approximately 15 minutes until an incoming airplane force it to dive abruptly leaving a tail-like green glare. I then lost sight of the object for approximately 10 minutes (as I jogged pass trees and houses that blocked my view). I called my sister, who was at home, to see if she had a clear view but by the time she went outside the object was gone.
