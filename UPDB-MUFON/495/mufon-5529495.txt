saw a star like object blink on/off then disappear

on october 22 around 530 ish I was drinking coffee and star watching. I was sitting down and facing northeast towards greensboro. Last night I saw several shooting stars and I was absently scanning the sky looking for more while planning my day. I focused on a trio of bright stars and one winked out. I kept my eye on it thinking that a cloud had covered it and it would reappear. It did reappear but significantly to the left of where it was originally. After a few seconds it winked out again, then reappeared in a different position. I was awestruck and literally staring with my mouth hanging open. After this it stayed put and pulsed out a very bright white light. After about 30 seconds it winked off again. I started to scan the sky nearby to see if it would reappear and in my periphal vision to the left I glimpsed something moving at a great amount of speed. I sat waiting to see something else for about 10 minites, then returned to the house. I was shocked even though I've always hoped to see something anomalous. A weird mixture of elation and puzzlement.
